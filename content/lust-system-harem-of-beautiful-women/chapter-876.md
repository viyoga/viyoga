<p>Avery immediately leaned in and asked with concern, &quot;Godfather, have the disciplinary decisions come out yet?&quot;</p>

<p>The handprint on Leah’s face had faded, but Luis still felt some lingering anger when he thought of his sister-in-law crying.</p>

<p>&quot;They’ve come out. I don’t have the specifics yet. We’ll hear more when we get to school.&quot;</p>

<p>The entrance to City No. 1 High School was lined with luxury cars parked on both sides of the road, causing some congestion. They were all parents dropping off their students.</p>

<p>One by one, the precious children trudged into school with looks of despair. Parents weren’t allowed inside. Principal Ma was waiting at the school gate and said earnestly, &quot;Mr. Luis, this way.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sorry to keep you waiting!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing his cooperative attitude, Luis responded in kind.</p>

<p>After registering at the security desk, they entered together. Avery ran off to the restroom first, while Leah followed Luis to the principal’s office.</p>

<p>&quot;Please, have a seat!&quot;</p>

<p>Even though Principal Ma was quite friendly, Leah, being a high school student, naturally felt nervous. She sat stiffly, like a child who’d done something wrong.</p>

<p>Though he didn’t smoke, he’d still prepared an ashtray. Luis lit one without hesitation and waited with a smile.</p>

<p>Principal Ma sat down and said gently, &quot;First of all, regarding this incident and the disciplinary actions against Harvey Zhang, I want to sincerely apologize to Leah.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You were indeed wronged, and this was a failure on our part in management.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;At tomorrow morning’s school assembly, Harvey Zhang will make a public self-criticism and apologize to Leah.&quot;</p>

<p>This was already a significant concession. Every institution cares about its reputation, and keeping family scandals private is the first principle—especially for a school like City No. 1 High School, which prides itself on its reputation.</p>

<p>Luis asked casually, &quot;And what about that little bastard?&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma quickly replied, &quot;Zion’s mother has been in contact with Chief Gasle. She’ll be detained for about 30 days on charges of provoking trouble.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’ve spoken with Zion’s father. He deeply apologizes for his son’s actions. If you have any compensation claims, the school can mediate.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;After Zion makes his public apology tomorrow, he will transfer to another school.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had done some digging. Zion’s father worked in the same field as him—they were essentially colleagues.</p>

<p>Principal Ma watched Luis carefully, waiting for his reaction. The reason Zion agreed to a public apology was strategic. Aside from appeasing Leah, transferring voluntarily meant he wouldn’t have an expulsion on his record, which would have been detrimental to his future.</p>

<p>&quot;They’ve offered Leah ten thousand dollars in compensation for emotional distress.&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma quickly added.</p>

<p>Leah, being a young girl, couldn’t pick up on the nuances.</p>

<p>She was just relieved that Zion wouldn’t be at the school anymore. Deep down, she still had the ordinary person’s instinct to just let things go.</p>

<p>Besides, having such a disgusting person around every day would be nauseating. His departure was a good thing for her.</p>

<p>The obedient sister-in-law had grown used to her brother-in-law’s protection, looking to him as her pillar of support.</p>

<p>Luis nodded and said, &quot;Alright.&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma let out a long sigh of relief. In fact, he and Zion’s father knew each other and had a bit of a connection.</p>

<p>When the father realized he’d messed with someone formidable, he panicked. His first instinct was to smooth things over quickly. That’s why he’d beaten his own son and forced the prideful boy to make a public apology in front of the whole school.</p>

<p>&quot;As for Harvey Zhang—&quot;</p>

<p>Principal Ma paused, then continued, &quot;After the meeting, it was decided that he is no longer suitable for our teaching staff, nor for working at City No. 1 High School.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;With his consent, he will be reassigned by the municipal education bureau to a three-year teaching support position in White Horse Township.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Where is White Horse Township?&quot;</p>

<p>Leah asked instinctively.</p>

<p>Principal Ma gave a meaningful smile, stood up, walked to the map, and pointed to a location in the northwestern part of Inner Mongolia.</p>

<p>&quot;White Horse Township is here. The altitude is a bit high and the oxygen is thin. Female teachers can’t handle it, so they specifically require male teachers.&quot;</p>

<p>It was truly a remote and desolate place. The concept of &quot;gilding one’s resume&quot; varies—some rural areas are actually quite advanced, with ample supplies and decent living conditions.</p>

<p>But this godforsaken place was really a punishment posting. The fact that it had been vacant for so long meant no one wanted to go there.</p>

<p>&quot;Very well. Principal Ma has handled this quite properly. No. 1 High School truly lives up to its reputation as the best in the city.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was thoroughly satisfied.</p>

<p>Principal Ma wasn’t obsequious. He extended his hand and said with a smile, &quot;This is a problem within our teaching staff. We cannot tolerate such bad apples.&quot;</p>

<p>The outcome was naturally very satisfying. As darkness fell, Luis escorted his sister-in-law all the way to her dormitory before reluctantly leaving.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, even parents were only allowed in on the first day of orientation for new students. Normally, not even parents could enter, let alone the girls’ dormitory.</p>

<p>Leaving the quiet campus, Luis suddenly remembered his sister-in-law Nancy’s request.</p>

<p>He called Dominic Jr. The man was unexpectedly enthusiastic and readily agreed, &quot;No problem!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The sales work is outsourced anyway. The saleswomen are usually relatives of employees working part-time. Once the sales are done, the group naturally disbands.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’ll have someone arrange it right away. Just have your people contact me.&quot;</p>

<p>He agreed so readily because sales positions were always temporary. Once the project sold out, the team was dissolved—they weren’t internal staff.</p>

<p>Normally, with more units and slower sales, some positions would have a base salary but relied mainly on commissions. Good sales could mean a high income.</p>

<p>It’s no surprise that scandals often emerge—it’s understandable that saleswomen might engage in something with clients to close a deal.</p>

<p>The villa project currently had over twenty units, all fully renovated and ready for occupancy. Villas in the old city center were rare and would definitely be in high demand.</p>

<p>Generally, luxury villas are located in remote areas. No matter how expensive, they’re inconvenient. These centrally located villas had great prospects, and their limited numbers meant they’d sell quickly.</p>

<p>As for the commissions for the saleswomen, they were often a way for bosses and managers to support their mistresses.</p>
