<p>Luis found himself unable to sleep alone, so he took the opportunity to get some proper rest. He slept so deeply that day and night seemed to blur together, and he wouldn&#x27;t have woken up at all if his teacher&#x27;s wife, Winslet, hadn&#x27;t called.</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m heading back to the law firm to handle some work. Let&#x27;s arrange to meet for dinner tonight somewhere, and I&#x27;ll explain everything to you face to face.&quot;</p>

<p>The street right outside Winslet&#x27;s home was lined with late-night eateries. That evening, they had agreed to meet at a Korean barbecue restaurant.</p>

<p>As soon as Fatty parked his incredibly powerful manual-transmission car, Luis immediately popped open the trunk of the Mercedes. Together with Nancy, they started transferring the prepared imported liquor, tea leaves, and several cartons of cigarettes into his vehicle.</p>

<p>&quot;You do have a conscience after all.&quot;</p>

<p>Fatty smiled with satisfaction, then effortlessly greeted the restaurant staff like an old regular and began ordering.</p>

<p>Seeing the three stunning, gorgeous women, he couldn&#x27;t help but drool, probably wondering in his heart just what relationship his apprentice had with these ladies.</p>

<p>Inside the private room, plates of meat were quickly brought to the table. Luis first took out a red envelope containing 1,000 dollars and said, &quot;Master, this is to help settle your nerves.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, it was just one night. The smokes and booze are plenty. Why bother with money?&quot;</p>

<p>Fatty waved it off.</p>

<p>Hearing this, Fatty gave an awkward chuckle and quickly busied himself pouring drinks to hide his expression.</p>

<p>Winslet continued sarcastically, &quot;If it weren&#x27;t for you, he probably couldn&#x27;t even afford such high-end prostitutes.&quot;</p>

<p>Once the barbecue was on the grill and drinks were poured, Nancy couldn&#x27;t wait any longer and asked eagerly, &quot;Mistress, so what&#x27;s the situation now?&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet smiled, took a delicate sip of her wine, and said, &quot;Your master and his crew were only attempted offenders. They got a lecture, were held overnight, and then released. Because it was discovered a minor had proactively called the police, they didn&#x27;t even have to pay a fine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for other details, I&#x27;m not too clear. The case is still being handled, so it&#x27;s definitely confidential. Little Bin, you have connections in this area—wouldn&#x27;t it be better for you to inquire yourself?&quot;</p>

<p>To put it bluntly, this was a sting operation. Normally, just acting as a pimp isn&#x27;t a major crime, but involving a minor makes it much more serious.</p>

<p>Winslet said with a laugh, &quot;That little girl&#x27;s parents are both within the system. The moment they heard their daughter had been tricked by a man, they came rushing over like mad.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Afterward, they kept thanking your master, saying if he hadn&#x27;t realized she was underage and called the police, they&#x27;d still be completely in the dark.&quot;</p>

<p>The once-dominant industry leader, Nova Media, was swiftly and decisively taken down. Because higher-ups were paying close attention, the case was handled with remarkable speed and force.</p>

<p>It wasn&#x27;t until the end of the month that Gasle called Luis to arrange a meeting.</p>

<p>But he didn&#x27;t even have time for a meal. They just bumped into each other briefly at the entrance of the Municipal Bureau, where Gasle gave a rough rundown of the situation.</p>

<p>That bastard Anglo was finished. This well-prepared raid had yielded a full harvest—finance, operations, almost everything was swept up in one net.</p>

<p>Based on the progress of the urgent interrogations, everyone had confessed. Anglo, as the main perpetrator, definitely wouldn&#x27;t escape.</p>

<p>Coupled with the involvement of attempted underage prostitution, the nature of the crime naturally became even more severe.</p>

<p>Gasle said meaningfully, &quot;That girl still acted as if nothing had happened, saying she&#x27;d also had relations with Anglo, and it had happened that very night too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Forensics found a condom in the trash. The DNA comparison results are out. With multiple crimes combined, Anglo is looking at at least ten years or more.&quot;</p>

<p>But Deewa was utterly thrilled. Over the phone, her voice trembled with barely suppressed emotion, &quot;You jerk, I&#x27;ll talk to my mom first. I&#x27;ll find you later.&quot;</p>

<p>Thinking of that enchantingly seductive body, Luis felt a surge of excitement stir within him. After several days of abstinence, it was time to properly release some pent-up energy.</p>
