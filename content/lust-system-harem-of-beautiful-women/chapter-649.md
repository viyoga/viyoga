<p>&quot;What happened to her?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis grew even more curious. Was his sister-in-law&#x27;s reputation really that bad?</p>

<p>&quot;Her? She&#x27;s not some noble lady from a wealthy family, yet she acts so arrogant, looking down on everyone like she&#x27;s above it all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And the way she talks is so harsh, her temper is absolutely terrible. Unless you&#x27;re a masochist, you wouldn&#x27;t go after a woman like that.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe said with a serious expression, &quot;We all admit she&#x27;s beautiful, and her figure is outstanding, completely flawless, but just try actually getting along with her.&quot;</p>

<p>What a shock. He hadn&#x27;t realized that his ethereal sister-in-law&#x27;s lack of social awareness and emotional intelligence was actually a universally acknowledged flaw.</p>

<p>A low IQ didn&#x27;t really matter; she could just be a pretty vase.</p>

<p>But a low EQ was truly hopeless. At least she had enough self-awareness to stay home and avoid socializing, since she disliked it. The real problem was her mouth, which was genuinely kind of foul. Sometimes she offended people without even realizing it.</p>

<p>With that kind of cluelessness, combined with her good looks and great figure, she was naturally ostracized even more within women&#x27;s circles.</p>

<p>Luis looked astonished and said, &quot;Come on, it can&#x27;t be as bad as you&#x27;re making it out to be.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe pouted coquettishly, &quot;With that attitude of hers, honestly, she probably wouldn&#x27;t even know how to please a man in bed. We all think you must regret it, but you&#x27;re just swallowing the loss because you don&#x27;t want to lose face.&quot;</p>

<p>Then she added, &quot;We don&#x27;t say it openly, but privately, that&#x27;s what everyone thinks.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis didn&#x27;t know whether to laugh or cry. The judges in the audience were surprisingly professional to have reached such a conclusion.</p>

<p>Actually, those news stories about mistresses moving up in the world and getting a ton of money are mostly fake, or at least the chances of that happening are extremely low.</p>

<p>Because when you&#x27;re looking for a sugar daddy, the money they give usually isn&#x27;t that much. The sugar daddies aren&#x27;t just seeing you, either; they have to calculate the cost-effectiveness.</p>

<p>Besides, there&#x27;s a difference between just playing around and having real feelings. So that path isn&#x27;t as rosy as most people imagine.</p>

<p>Luis had the looks and the physique. Even if he were married, at least he wasn&#x27;t some middle-aged guy with a greasy belly.</p>

<p>Young, rich, and crucially, the first time he made a move, he paid for a fully loaded Mercedes-Benz G-Wagon in cash and registered it entirely under the woman&#x27;s name. That alone was an unprecedented first for everyone.</p>

<p>You see, many so-called sugar daddies are incredibly stingy. They calculate everything so clearly that sometimes you can&#x27;t get a single benefit out of them.</p>

<p>Even worse, if you&#x27;re not smart, you might get dumped and end up saddled with a mountain of debt. Stories like that are everywhere.</p>

<p>Now, Nancy wasn&#x27;t just driving around ostentatiously in the G-Wagon; she had also opened a bar or something. Compared to all those negative examples, Nancy&#x27;s situation was practically what people dream of.</p>

<p>Luis said cheerfully, &quot;It&#x27;s not as extreme as you all say. Nancy is actually pretty great.&quot;</p>

<p>If they calculated cost-effectiveness like that, spending over ten million dollars on one woman might not seem worth it.</p>

<p>But she was his sister-in-law! Setting aside the temptation of that specific relationship, the rewards he got from completing tasks with her were worth far more than that.</p>

<p>Add in the hidden benefits, and Luis wished he could spend even more. If he weren&#x27;t afraid of scaring her, he wouldn&#x27;t care about splurging at all.</p>

<p>For instance, the fully loaded Mercedes G-Wagon cost over two hundred thousand dollars. She was so moved she almost cried.</p>

<p>Luis also felt like crying, but for a different reason. There were plenty of expensive cars out there. Why didn&#x27;t she pick something like a Ferrari or a Lamborghini? She had such low aspirations.</p>

<p>As for their sex life, she had even suspected she might be frigid before.</p>

<p>But now, once in bed, she was absolutely a seductive goddess, lewd and wild, driving him to ecstasy, not at all inferior to any other woman.</p>

<p>Luis was completely satisfied. The main thing was the development and training he was doing with her, which brought him immense psychological fulfillment. How could he find any faults with that?</p>

<p>Chloe said reluctantly, &quot;Honestly, we think you should have gone after Hela or Deewa instead. They have much more femininity. Unlike Nancy, who makes people angry the moment she opens her mouth.&quot;</p>

<p>Just then, they were waiting at a red light. Luis lit a cigarette, then turned to look at her meaningfully, without saying a word.</p>

<p>Chloe felt a shiver run down her spine. The handsome guy who had previously seemed so kind and harmless, almost gentle, now had an indifferent look in his eyes as if he could kill.</p>

<p>The light turned green, and the car started moving again.</p>

<p>Chloe hesitated for a long time before saying, &quot;I, I didn&#x27;t mean to badmouth her on purpose. But right now, she, she seems to be butting heads with Bubbles.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who&#x27;s Bubbles?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked reflexively.</p>

<p>Chloe looked surprised, &quot;You don&#x27;t know? The biggest online streamer in Bathek City.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Never heard of her…&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said casually, &quot;Looks like you&#x27;re in the same circles. You sure know a lot.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe&#x27;s eyes sparkled mischievously, and she giggled, &quot;Yeah. If you want to find out, you&#x27;ll have to treat me to a big meal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No problem!&quot;</p>

<p>The car drove to another store. As soon as Chloe got out, she knew the place like the back of her hand, and the salesperson there was particularly enthusiastic.</p>

<p>Although the Mercedes-Benz C-Class was an older model and not considered a luxury car, people who drove it usually had some financial standing. And the fact that Luis was so young surprised them a bit.</p>

<p>Earlier, Maria had mentioned that his wife&#x27;s dream car was a Volkswagen Beetle.</p>

<p>Its cute appearance made it the car of her dreams. More importantly, it was easier to park near her workplace, and her novice driving skills would be more comfortable with it.</p>

<p>Luis made a mental note, planning to give his wife both the villa and the car together for her birthday.</p>

<p>It was meant to be a surprise, so he didn&#x27;t go through Nancy. He had her number and contacted her directly.</p>

<p>After some inquiries, the fully loaded price was just over three thousand dollars, with a deposit of about one hundred dollars being enough.</p>

<p>These salespeople in the industry could actually switch to other brands for business, and they still earned decent commissions.</p>

<p>Luis had started out as an auto mechanic, so he knew the ins and outs very well. He could have found his own channels, but it was more convenient to just let her handle it.</p>

<p>Luis transferred one hundred dollars directly. Chloe was overjoyed, but she remained cautious because he hadn&#x27;t responded to her previous advances.</p>

<p>Who knew what this one hundred dollars was really for? But as soon as he transferred one thousand dollars, she was completely convinced.</p>

<p>At least according to her understanding, this wasn&#x27;t how you spent money to pick up women. So she immediately contacted another store.</p>

<p>Based on his wife Lily&#x27;s preferences, he ordered a green Beetle.</p>

<p>The fully loaded version had just arrived and was undergoing inspection and interior customization. The best thing about Lily was that she wasn&#x27;t too picky. If Luis chose it, she would almost certainly love it.</p>

<p>Unlike other women, who were annoyingly critical about everything. So Luis came over right away.</p>

<p>&quot;Full payment? Why not take out a car loan? It would help with your cash flow.&quot;</p>

<p>The salesperson here was a bit frustrated when he heard that. A full payment would earn a commission, but not much, and most of it would go to Chloe.</p>

<p>A loan would bring in a much higher bonus. She batted her eyes at Luis, wondering how someone so young could afford full payment.</p>
