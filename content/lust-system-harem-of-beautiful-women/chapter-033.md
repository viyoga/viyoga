<p>Leah quickly waved her hands, pouting her little mouth in frustration and said, &quot;I didn’t mean that, don’t talk nonsense. I was just saying that brother in law is also quite good looking. If you dress up a little, you wouldn’t be worse than eldest brother in law.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis chuckled, deliberately teasing her with a helpless tone, &quot;Got it, in Leah’s heart I’m worse than Daniel.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, I didn’t mean that!&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was completely flustered upon hearing this, slamming the table fiercely and saying, &quot;Eldest brother in law is all talk, and eldest sister isn’t reliable either, how can they compare to you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I just meant that brother in law, it’s not like you don’t have money. If you bought some nicer clothes, you’d look even more handsome.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing her genuinely embarrassed and anxious, Luis quickly grabbed her hand, speaking with deep affection and gentleness,</p>

<p>&quot;Sweetheart, don’t get worked up. In brother in law’s heart, spending 10 dollars on myself is a waste, but if you spend a thousand, I’d think it’s totally worth it.&quot;</p>

<p>From the moment they met, Luis had only a few clothes that had been washed until they faded, and he was still wearing them now.</p>

<p>Looking at her own pretty clothes in contrast, Leah’s heart ached. It seemed brother in law was only stingy with himself but utterly generous with her.</p>

<p>&quot;Stupid brother in law...&quot;</p>

<p>Leah murmured, suddenly feeling a slight pang of sadness.</p>

<p>Luis had bought juice for her and her friend. There was sugar free ice water here for just 0.5 dollars, but Luis didn’t buy any. Now, as he spoke, he subtly swallowed his saliva.</p>

<p>She didn’t realize this saliva was because she had practically placed her chest on the table. She just felt brother in law was too harsh on himself, and for a moment, she felt guilty but didn’t know how to express it.</p>

<p>&quot;Bunny!&quot;</p>

<p>Suddenly, a girl sat down and hugged Leah tightly.</p>

<p>Her denim shorts accentuated her slender, beautiful legs, and her little black tank top exuded a youthful vibe. Most importantly, she was the same type as the sister in law.</p>

<p>Petite in stature but exceptionally full chested, the curves were visible even through her clothes. The round and plump contours were very clear, showing she was wearing a bra but also hinting at her hidden depths.</p>

<p>She carried a quirky little bag, and her outfit looked a bit old fashioned, clearly hand me downs.</p>

<p>Her features were delicate and pretty, almost on par with the sister in law, but she had a short bob cut, giving her a fresh and clean look.</p>

<p>&quot;Bunny??&quot;</p>

<p>Luis looked utterly confused.</p>

<p>Leah gasped and immediately covered her mouth, flustered and angry, &quot;Avery, shut your mouth.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Leah, behave.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah’s struggles were useless. This new little girl had her in a grip, practically groping her chest while laughing, &quot;Why are you overreacting so much?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Seriously, what do you think?!&quot;</p>

<p>Being manhandled like this in front of her brother in law, Leah was furious, her face burning with anger as she shoved her away.</p>

<p>She was almost at the point of losing her temper. The new petite, big chested girl let go of her and scoffed, &quot;Can’t even take a joke? We always do this normally.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was seething, her face red as she snapped, &quot;Are you insane? This is a public place.&quot;</p>

<p>Her little friend suddenly extended her hand, smiling sweetly, &quot;Brother in law, hello! I’m Avery!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hello!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was so confused his head hurt. He couldn’t help but ask curiously, &quot;Why is she called Bunny?&quot;</p>

<p>Avery was about to answer when she caught Leah’s murderous glare, her eyes practically spitting fire, which was genuinely intimidating.</p>

<p>She immediately stuck out her tongue and said playfully, &quot;Brother in law, if you’re interested, ask her yourself. If you really can’t get it out of her, I’ll secretly tell you later.&quot;</p>

<p>Then Avery suddenly looked suspicious and asked, &quot;Leah, is this really your brother in law? I’ve met your brother in law before.&quot;</p>

<p>If she’d met him, it was probably Daniel. Leah rolled her eyes and said, &quot;Do you have to know every single relative in my family?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’m just looking out for you!&quot;</p>

<p>Avery hugged her warmly, giving Luis a suggestive look, &quot;If he’s your boyfriend, no need to hide it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I already said he’s my brother in law. You’re so annoying, you little bitch.&quot;</p>

<p>Watching two petite, big chested girls playfully bicker was one of life’s greatest pleasures, especially since both had sweet, doll like voices. Their laughter was so sugary it felt like a sugar overdose.</p>

<p>Every man in the cold drink shop was swallowing hard as they watched, then shooting Luis looks of envy and resentment.</p>

<p>Luis basked in these hostile gazes, enjoying the sight of their unrestrained chest movements as they roughhoused. It was a visual feast.</p>

<p>After some discussion, the two girls settled on McDonald’s for lunch. It seemed the allure of fast food for young girls was undeniable.</p>

<p>After eating, they went shopping at the supermarket. Luis pushed the cart behind them, now clearly feeling like the third wheel with the arrival of the little friend.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother in law, this is a bit expensive!&quot;</p>

<p>After checking out, they went to another shop for ice cream. Avery ate while staring at Luis, then at Leah, her expression odd.</p>

<p>Leah, already feeling guilty, couldn’t take it anymore and snapped angrily, &quot;Avery, what are you looking at with those shifty eyes? You’ve been acting weird all day.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Me acting weird? Look who’s talking!&quot;</p>

<p>Avery said suspiciously, &quot;Is this really your brother in law??&quot;</p>

<p>Leah rolled her eyes and was about to retort when Luis asked, amused, &quot;Why is that so hard to believe?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It doesn’t add up. You pick expensive things you wouldn’t even buy for yourself, yet he puts them in the cart without hesitation, even complaining that what you picked isn’t expensive enough.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He’s being way too generous.&quot;</p>

<p>Avery said seriously, &quot;It’s not just that. I noticed something was off the moment I arrived, and now I’m even more convinced.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re insane. What’s off?&quot;</p>

<p>Leah rolled her eyes again.</p>

<p>Avery smirked and pointed at Leah, &quot;That dress is new. I’ve never seen it before. Those shoes are new too. Your mom is so stingy, there’s no way she’d buy you such expensive clothes.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re the same, pockets emptier than our faces. So, what’s the deal with these new clothes?&quot;</p>

<p>Leah rolled her eyes, exasperated, &quot;What do you mean ’what’s the deal’? Brother in law bought them for me. Avery, you’re seriously out of your mind.&quot;</p>

<p>Avery put on her detective face and snorted, &quot;I don’t buy it. Those sandals? My cousin bought a pair. What kind of proper brother in law buys his sister in law something that expensive?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I see what you’re doing. You’re insulting me!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was both amused and exasperated. What did she mean by a ’proper brother in law’? Was she implying he wasn’t proper?</p>

<p>&quot;Avery, you’re seriously delusional. A pair of shoes, and you’ve spun this whole conspiracy theory??&quot;</p>

<p>Leah was also at a loss for words, scolding, &quot;If he’s not my brother in law, who do you think he is?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hey, handsome, come clean. Are you trying to hit on my best friend?&quot;</p>

<p>Avery giggled, &quot;If you are, there’s no need to be shy. You’re clearly putting in a lot of effort, so you must be serious.&quot;</p>
