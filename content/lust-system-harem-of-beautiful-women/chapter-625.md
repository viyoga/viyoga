<p>&quot;Stay still!&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy focused intently on driving, swatting away Luis&#x27;s hand because the cheating bastard&#x27;s roving hand had gone too far. Groping her thigh was one thing, but trying to slip into her panties was far too dangerous.</p>

<p>With a tender, flirtatious gaze in her eyes, the celestial sister-in-law said, &quot;Brother-in-law, you&#x27;re not going back tonight, are you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I told Lily.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis nodded, speaking seriously. &quot;I originally had something to deal with, but your mother-in-law called me over on the spot.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You know how messy my schedule is, with my days and nights all mixed up. Lily&#x27;s used to it by now. Why, what&#x27;s up?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy&#x27;s expression turned seductively alluring, a hint of excitement in her voice as she said, &quot;Then let&#x27;s go get a room!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re not going home?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked, a note of surprise in his voice.</p>

<p>While a hotel room certainly offered a freer, more unconstrained environment, to be honest, from Luis&#x27;s perspective, home was the better choice.</p>

<p>His wife, his sister-in-law, his younger sister-in-law, his mother-in-law… Sometimes, when it came to cheating, the thrill wasn&#x27;t about unrestrained sex; it was far more appealing to have that sense of the household patriarch.</p>

<p>Money didn&#x27;t matter much to Luis; the best feeling was being the head of the family.</p>

<p>Nancy&#x27;s cheeks were flushed, her voice a little breathless as she said, &quot;It&#x27;s just too inconvenient at home. Mom and Lily are there, and now my little sister even stays over occasionally.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And the soundproofing is so bad. Every time, it&#x27;s just a nerve-wracking experience…&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, I&#x27;ve never stayed at a high-end hotel. I kind of want to give it a try…&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked with a lascivious grin, &quot;Just want to give it a try?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy shot him a coquettish glare and said, &quot;Fine, I didn&#x27;t like it the way it was before. Having to bite my lip to keep from crying out, not being able to enjoy it… it&#x27;s frustrating.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this sent a surge of primal lust coursing through Luis&#x27;s veins. It proved just how much his sister-in-law cared for him; she wanted to be able to fully embrace and enjoy the sheer pleasure of being with him.</p>

<p>Just as Luis was about to speak, her phone suddenly rang, the sound echoing harshly in the car.</p>

<p>He knew that Nancy&#x27;s social life was usually very simple. She barely had any friends; basically, only relatives called her, or else it was just telemarketers.</p>

<p>She might be strikingly beautiful, but socially, she was a complete homebody. She didn&#x27;t work and had no colleagues, making her life extremely simple.</p>

<p>So, Nancy immediately grabbed her phone, looking surprised. &quot;It&#x27;s Mom!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Huh?&quot;</p>

<p>Even Luis was taken aback.</p>

<p>A call from the matriarch naturally had to be answered. Nancy picked up. &quot;Hello, Mom, what&#x27;s up?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;&#x27;What&#x27;s up?&#x27; Do you even care about your mother? I was waiting for you to call me. But after all this time, not a single word. Are you acting like I&#x27;m dead or something?&quot;</p>

<p>The mother-in-law, who was now a docile lamb in Luis&#x27;s arms, a seductive and alluring beauty, was fundamentally a strict, harsh, and sharp-tongued shrew. The moment she spoke, it was like firecrackers going off.</p>

<p>Her words came out in a rapid, unbroken stream, and even the sound of mahjong tiles in the background couldn&#x27;t diminish the quality of her relentless tirade…</p>

<p>&quot;No, Mom, what did I do to upset you?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was completely bewildered.</p>

<p>She was racked with guilt, terrified that her secret trip to see her father&#x27;s mistress had been exposed. In her mother&#x27;s eyes, that was an unforgivable sin.</p>

<p>Ruth was fuming, snorting angrily. &quot;Still playing dumb with me!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your mother-in-law called me. She said you had another fight with Daniel, a really bad one.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She tried to break it up, but couldn&#x27;t manage it. That&#x27;s why she called your brother-in-law over.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Huh, she&#x27;s such a gossip. Why did she have to call you about it?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was getting a bit annoyed.</p>

<p>&quot;Of course she&#x27;d call! She&#x27;s your mother-in-law, she cares about you two! And she said it was over some trivial, insignificant little thing, yet you two could fight like that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Can&#x27;t you two be a little more mature? Now that you&#x27;ve got your business going, life is good. Are you bored, with nothing to do but fight?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fine, you fight. But you&#x27;re a married woman now. How did your mother-in-law even find out?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So what if she, the gossip, found out? Why did she call your brother-in-law over?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy said anxiously, &quot;Mom, it wasn&#x27;t me who called him!&quot;</p>

<p>As a woman who had clawed her way to the top of the corporate world, she was no pushover. Making this call to the mother-in-law first was a brilliant move.</p>

<p>By framing the issue as something trivial, she ensured Nancy couldn&#x27;t call her own mother crying about it.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, please stop. This conflict isn&#x27;t something that started yesterday.&quot;</p>
