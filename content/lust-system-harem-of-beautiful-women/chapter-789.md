<p>His sister-in-law was immediately incensed.</p>

<p>Luis lit a cigarette and said calmly, &quot;Isn’t this perfectly normal? The main reason your mom agreed to rebuild the ancestral home was that she didn’t have to pay for it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if we chip in for some furniture, it’s not much.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah’s personality couldn’t stand this. She gritted her teeth in anger. &quot;Brother-in-law, is this your excuse for sleeping with my mom???&quot;</p>

<p>That was definitely the soul-searching question. Luis smiled without panicking, simply speaking softly, &quot;Leah, I’m not trying to put you down, but do you understand the adult world?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What don’t I understand!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Young people always want to seem mature. Saying something like that to her was practically a challenge. In an instant, his sister-in-law was so mad her chest was heaving.</p>

<p>Seeing her temper flare, Luis knew he had her. He continued casually, &quot;Since you understand the adult world, then what do you think will eventually happen with your parents?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well...&quot;</p>

<p>That question caught Leah off guard.</p>

<p>She’d been about to blurt out, &quot;Then just get divorced—what else can they do?&quot;</p>

<p>Growing up, she had been mostly raised by Aunt Yana and Aunt Eloise. She had little emotional attachment to her father.</p>

<p>In her eyes, her older sister Nancy was practically a traitor—calling that mistress &quot;Auntie&quot; affectionately, even once calling her &quot;Little Mom.&quot; Unforgivable.</p>

<p>But when it came to the future, this was the most confusing age.</p>

<p>Luis leaned against the car and looked at her, smiling. &quot;Just treat this as me rambling, and then think about whether I’m right.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your mom raised the three of you on barely any money each month. Why didn’t she get a divorce? Because she’d get even less if she did.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Think about it from her perspective—if they went to court, how much child support would she really get? Would it be enough for three daughters?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if your mom went to work, would what she earned actually be enough?&quot;</p>

<p>While Leah listened in stunned silence, Luis put out his cigarette and lit another one.</p>

<p>&quot;Leah, right now life feels easy and happy for you. But have you ever thought about how your mom got through all those years???&quot;</p>

<p>Leah wavered for a moment, then said defiantly, &quot;Don’t give me that. No matter how Mom got through it, that’s no excuse for you two to cheat.&quot;</p>

<p>She was still clear-headed enough, but for Luis, this was manageable.</p>

<p>Luis just looked at her with a knowing smile. Leah couldn’t help feeling guilty, but she pressed on stubbornly, &quot;Am I wrong??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Aunt Yana and Aunt Eloise helped raise you, but only occasionally. Your mom was so busy she was practically losing her mind.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;According to her, when she was raising the three of you, she was so exhausted she didn’t even have the strength to hang herself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your dad went off to find a mistress, but the kids he left behind—she had to raise them. All things considered, it’s a miracle your mom never beat you.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah suddenly put herself in that situation—if her husband cheated and didn’t come home.</p>

<p>Then she had to raise three kids, looking like a disheveled madwoman. She couldn’t control her emotions at the thought.</p>

<p>Seeing her hesitating, Luis continued, &quot;But your mom kept going. That’s a determination no one can ignore.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;See the biggest problem? She’s been starved of affection for all those years.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said seriously, &quot;If she’d been willing to abandon you, her life would have been much easier. But you’ve seen it all these years.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She lived on that meager allowance, worked hard to raise you, and did odd jobs to make ends meet.&quot;</p>

<p>This time, Leah was visibly moved. But she still gritted her teeth and said, &quot;So what? That still doesn’t make it right for you to cheat with my mom.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis could tell her defenses were wavering, so he went straight in. &quot;Then let me ask you—if your parents divorced now and your mom found some old guy to marry, would you be happy with that???&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My mom wouldn’t make it that easy for that vixen.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah immediately scoffed.</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly. So why should that vixen get to live happily with your dad while your mom has to suffer alone all these years? That’s just not fair.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah looked conflicted. &quot;But... you still can’t be with my mom...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Leah, you’re grown up now. You should understand your mom’s pain.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;All these years, it’s just been a widow and her kids. You’ve all been bullied enough. Those old geezers—which one ever had good intentions toward your mom?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said earnestly, &quot;Your mom’s at this age now. She doesn’t want to find you a stepfather. And now that life’s finally getting better, is there really any need to stir up more chaos?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I admit we’re wrong—it’s all my fault, really.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with genuine sincerity. &quot;And don’t blame your mom. It’s on me. That night, on her birthday, she’d had too much to drink, and we were all so happy—I lost control for a moment, and it just happened.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;By the next morning, when we’d sobered up, it was already too late to take it back.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So that was the night...&quot;</p>

<p>Leah said dazedly. Of course she remembered her mother’s birthday.</p>

<p>Her mother had been moved to tears, and the three sisters had cried with joy too. They’d all seen how much their mom had suffered over the years.</p>

<p>Unlike the traitor Nancy, Leah was firmly on her mother’s side. She hated that vixen and had little affection for her father.</p>

<p>Leah sighed. &quot;Brother-in-law, but you’re with me... and with my mom... if my second sister ever finds out, our family will be ruined.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then we’ll just keep it from her!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis’s eyes lit up. In the end, her worry outweighed her anger—that was a good opening.</p>

<p>He immediately wrapped his arms around her from behind. Leah gave a few tsundere struggles, but when she couldn’t break free, she just let herself be held.</p>

<p>Breathing in the intoxicating scent of her hair, Luis said with a smile, &quot;Don’t worry, Leah...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your mom’s at this age now. If we keep this up for a few years, at least it’ll make up for all the years she’s suffered.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Everyone has desires. She’s been suppressing hers for so long—she deserves to enjoy herself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How could it be okay for her to get involved with some random guy out there? It’s better to be with someone we know, right?&quot;</p>

<p>It was pure, unadulterated nonsense—but for the moment, Leah actually found it convincing.</p>

<p>&quot;As for your second sister, don’t worry.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even before she got pregnant, she couldn’t satisfy me. And after she got pregnant, she was always worried I’d get too pent up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Men are all no good.&quot;</p>

<p>Leah mumbled with a blush.</p>

<p>&quot;Heh, your sister actually approves of me finding other women—just as long as I don’t pay for it and don’t break up the family.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Would you believe that if I went and told your second sister right now that we’re having an affair, she’d just scold me a bit and not really get that angry?&quot;</p>

<p>Leah shot him a seductive glare. &quot;Don’t be so sure. If my second sister finds out, she’ll definitely kill you.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis tempted her further. &quot;The thing is, your second sister has a secret—that’s why she’s so tolerant of me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What secret!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hehe, I’ll keep it a secret for now. I promise you’ll be pleasantly surprised.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hmph, I won’t say anything anyway. But you’d better be careful—if my second sister catches you, you’re dead.&quot;</p>
