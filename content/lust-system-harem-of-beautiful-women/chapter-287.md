<p>To put it bluntly, if negotiations broke down, Waller alone could crush both of their families. President Ye’s inspection was merely a demonstration of a firm and uncompromising stance.</p>

<p>Ye’s political career continued to soar smoothly. By joining forces with his powerful rivals, he naturally remained secure and unchallenged, which was why he did not want to see the Tiger Real Estate Group, an enterprise he had personally nurtured, become a laughingstock.</p>

<p>&quot;Him...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, yes.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic, an old fox, immediately responded obsequiously, &quot;Young Luis is a very good man, diligent and dedicated in his work. He is a bit rigid, but his loyalty and commitment have always set a fine example.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I am not speaking carelessly. This month he will also receive a special bonus equivalent to one month’s salary. It is already registered with the finance department. He can collect his salary and bonus tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, is that so? Take me to the finance department to have a look.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye listened with growing interest. Vincent quickly led the way upon hearing this.</p>

<p>Bella’s face instantly turned very ugly. She was no fool and immediately saw that the relationship between Ye and that accountant was not simple.</p>

<p>It looked like they were arguing, but for a mere accountant to dare to defy him... just how close was their relationship in private? Could this guy be some relative of his?</p>

<p>Impossible. Ye came from a standard, prestigious, and powerful family, a great clan with a terrifyingly profound background.</p>

<p>To put it bluntly, not a single member of the collateral branches failed to thrive; whether in business or politics, they were all exceptional talents.</p>

<p>But Bella had no time to dwell on it. She immediately quickened her steps to follow them.</p>

<p>At that moment, only one terrifying thought occupied her mind: if they truly were close, and if Ye found out that this reward was because she had injured him...</p>

<p>What if he flew into a thunderous rage... Bella dared not even imagine what the fate of the Leon family would be.</p>

<p>In the finance office, Ye looked through the records and accurately found the payslip and the 1,000 dollar bonus.</p>

<p>He seemed to be in a rather good mood and asked, &quot;Did he perform a heroic act? Why is he suddenly receiving such a large bonus for no particular reason or holiday?&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing the smile on Ye’s face, Dominic felt even more assured. &quot;President Ye, this is what he deserves. President Ye seems to know him. If you wish to know, why not ask him yourself?&quot;</p>

<p>As he said this, Dominic couldn’t help but glance at Bella.</p>

<p>After her elder brother entered the jade, Bella, who had single handedly supported the Leon family and then launched a counterattack, was certainly no fool. She was now one hundred percent certain that Ye and that little accountant shared a very close relationship.</p>

<p>The exact nature of the relationship was unknown, but it was definitely a positive one.</p>

<p>&quot;He goes by another surname now...&quot;</p>

<p>Ye muttered to himself, too quietly for anyone to hear. Seeing Dominic’s ingratiating expression, he did not press further.</p>

<p>Falsifying records in such a short time was impossible. After a moment’s thought, Ye said offhandedly, &quot;President Ben, have him come here. I have some matters I wish to discuss properly with him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic immediately went to arrange it. Luis, upon receiving the order, also went upstairs.</p>

<p>Everyone tactfully left the room, including Dominic and the visibly anxious Bella. The large finance office fell into an eerily quiet stillness.</p>

<p>Dominic, being in a position of authority, had now mastered concealing his emotions, but his eyes still lit up when he saw Luis enter.</p>

<p>Luis wore a reluctant expression. He sat down on the nearby sofa, directly crossed his legs, lit a cigarette, and began smoking, paying him no mind.</p>

<p>Luis now somewhat regretted using this cheat ability on him. Dealing with that Deputy Mayor Waller would have been much better, less troublesome. Now he had to worry about his own life, it was truly wretched.</p>

<p>An awkward and strange silence hung in the air. Ye was the first to speak, &quot;Do you hate me that much.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Between strangers, what is there to hate? I am not some abnormal person.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said dismissively, &quot;President Ye lost his composure earlier. Pulling and tugging at a mere accountant like that is beneath you.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye remained silent for a long while, then gritted his teeth and lifted his head, saying, &quot;Indeed. If they ask, you can say you are an external relative of our family. It will not cause you trouble.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you for that.&quot;</p>

<p>Following the plot implanted by the ’Dad’ cheat, Luis employed his acting skills, smiled lightly, and said, &quot;Rest assured, your surname is not as same as mine. We have absolutely nothing to do with each other, correct.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We are strangers. No matter what, I will not become a stain on your political career. You have my word, I will not cling to even the slightest benefit from the Ye family.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So, please, do not dwell on it any further. My life is quite good now, and I do not wish to be disturbed.&quot;</p>

<p>These words were filled with profound resentment. A seasoned person like Ye could not possibly miss the implication.</p>

<p>&quot;May I leave now?&quot;</p>
