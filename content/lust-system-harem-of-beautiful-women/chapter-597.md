<p>Luis continued to tease, &quot;What if, you know, there&#x27;s an accident and she gets pregnant?&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche shook her head and said, &quot;It&#x27;s because of my physical condition that I had to go through with having Avery back then. I got my tubes tied right after giving birth.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis kept teasing, &quot;But what if Avery doesn&#x27;t like using condoms? What if she prefers that direct contact of spirit and flesh? If I don&#x27;t finish inside, would I do it on her face, or in her mouth...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t care what you do!!&quot;</p>

<p>A faint blush crept onto Blanche&#x27;s face.</p>

<p>After all, that was her own daughter; discussing her intimate affairs with her lover was awkward, no matter how shameless she usually was.</p>

<p>&quot;Then you&#x27;ll have to tell her, you know, that high-protein is very nutritious and great for the skin~&quot;</p>

<p>After teasing Blanche for a bit, Luis kissed her goodbye. She had to head to the bar for a meeting in the afternoon, as she was now a key member of the bar&#x27;s management team.</p>

<p>Stretching lazily, feeling refreshed and spirited, Luis made his way to the front desk of the Ocean World.</p>

<p>&quot;Sir, are you sure about this?&quot;</p>

<p>The lobby manager, an elegant-looking young woman, looked at Luis with a hint of surprise.</p>

<p>&quot;Just swipe the card!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had already prepared his card.</p>

<p>The Ocean Dragon Palace Hotel operated on a points system. Accumulating points through spending could get discounts or complimentary gifts.</p>

<p>He had just recently learned something: all the properties under the Ocean Group, whether it was the Ocean World Spa, the Ocean Dragon Palace Hotel, or any other business, could be accessed with a single membership card.</p>

<p>The first thing he&#x27;d done was have his sister-in-law, Nancy, come and open a membership card for him. The card made accumulating points easier, and while the discounts weren&#x27;t huge, it was much more convenient for spending.</p>

<p>Nancy had spent ten thousand dollars to get the card and brought it over. Now Luis wanted to upgrade the card, and conveniently, he could also use this chance to add a bit more &quot;savings funds&quot; concerning his sister-in-law.</p>

<p>&quot;Sir, this… unless this is for business expenses, authorized by a company, maybe you should reconsider?&quot;</p>

<p>Holding the private membership card, the manager looked stunned.</p>

<p>This was her first time encountering such a situation. For business expenses, some companies might spend one or two million dollars a year on hosting, renting meeting rooms, and such.</p>

<p>But for a private individual to want to top up a million dollars was simply outrageous. She knew that the most she&#x27;d ever seen on a private membership card was around two hundred thousand dollars.</p>

<p>&quot;There sure are a lot of rules for spending money here.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis frowned and said, &quot;How about I give your President Leon a call?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, no, that won&#x27;t be necessary! I&#x27;ll upgrade it for you right away!!&quot;</p>

<p>The Ocean Group had VIP black cards. After checking the account, the manager&#x27;s demeanor shifted completely to one of obsequiousness. &quot;My apologies, sir, your card is already the highest-tier black card, personally signed off by President Leon!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This card allows you to enjoy all the benefits and discounts at any of our establishments, and you can even sign for purchases when the balance is insufficient.&quot;</p>

<p>She rattled off a long list of benefits, but Luis wasn&#x27;t really paying attention. He just urged her to finish the transaction and swipe the card.</p>

<p>&quot;*Ding... Hidden perk for the &#x27;Tall, Sexy Sister-in-Law&#x27; mission: Savings increased. Total savings: 1.52 million dollars.*&quot;</p>

<p>Having swiped the card incrementally over time, Luis felt a surge of happiness seeing the balance grow. He was more confident than ever that he could make her call him &quot;daddy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sir, here is your card. Please don&#x27;t hesitate to call the front desk if you need anything.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis took a shower, soaked in the bath, and had a scrub, feeling absolutely refreshed. He waited in the common area for a while, but the girls still hadn&#x27;t come out. He had to admit, young girls really took their time getting ready.</p>

<p>While waiting, Luis fiddled with his phone, browsing the introduction to the VIP area of Ocean World. He realized he had been quite naive when he&#x27;d come here with his family before.</p>

<p>Or rather, he just wasn&#x27;t used to the lifestyle of the rich. As part of a top-tier five-star hotel complex, the luxury and extravagant pleasures available here were beyond his imagination.</p>

<p>After finishing a cigarette, the girls&#x27; call came.</p>

<p>The sight of the two emerging from their bath was simply stunning. After their bath, they had changed into the resort&#x27;s casual spa wear—simple, ordinary shorts and short-sleeved tops that somehow looked exceptionally cute on them.</p>

<p>They had both tied their hair up in ponytails, making them look almost like twins. Their skin was fair with a healthy, flushed pink from the bath.</p>

<p>Even though they wore bras under their loose clothes, the way their chests bounced as they walked was enough to make anyone involuntarily swallow.</p>

<p>They held hands affectionately, a perfectly normal thing between girls, but knowing what his younger sister-in-law had been up to, Luis couldn&#x27;t help but let his imagination wander.</p>

<p>They were the ultimate, flawless combination: angelic faces with voluptuous figures, petite yet soft and cuddly. Just one such little girl was enough to drive a man crazy; seeing this pair together made his blood pressure spike.</p>

<p>His younger sister-in-law, Leah, pouted her little lips and grumbled sourly, &quot;Perverted, stingy brother-in-law! You said you were taking us for a feast, but you&#x27;re just bringing us here for the buffet.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Cheapskate.&quot;</p>

<p>In their eyes, Ocean World was already considered a high-end treat. But the meal offered was a buffet, a fairly standard one, similar to a hotel&#x27;s breakfast spread.</p>

<p>Only drinks and beverages cost extra. There was a large variety of food, but it was all quite average. It certainly didn&#x27;t qualify as a big feast.</p>

<p>Luis reached for her hand. She pulled away once, but he persisted and managed to grasp it, interlacing his fingers with hers.</p>

<p>Leah let out a shy, proud-sounding huff and turned her head away, but she let her brother-in-law hold her hand. After all, no one else was around.</p>

<p>Luis, not wanting to show favoritism, grinned and took Avery&#x27;s other hand, which earned him a sweet, happy smile from her.</p>

<p>&quot;How could I possibly be stingy? If my little Leah wanted the moon from the sky, I&#x27;d figure out a way to get it for her.&quot;</p>

<p>With a girl on each arm, Luis paraded the two perfect little girls through the place, drawing envious and angry stares.</p>

<p>They went to the VIP area, which he&#x27;d never been near before. As they entered, he swiped his card. A manager in professional attire approached them, her smile warm and welcoming. &quot;Good evening, sir. It&#x27;s a pleasure to be at your service.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had done his homework. He took the iPad she offered and said with a smile, &quot;We&#x27;ll be staying in a luxury ocean-view suite for the night. It has an attached massage room, which you two can enjoy.&quot;</p>

<p>The top floor of the Ocean Hotel was the exclusive VIP area of Ocean World, featuring over a dozen luxury suites, each with a different decor style, as well as a private restaurant offering a panoramic view of the city at night.</p>
