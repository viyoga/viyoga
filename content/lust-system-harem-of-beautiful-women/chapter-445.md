<p>They arrived at the lawyer’s office before two o’clock. Winslet greeted them warmly, while Nancy politely called out, &quot;Teacher Blanche!&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet nodded, checked the time, and said, &quot;Give that person a call. No need for them to come all the way here. We’ll bring the contract and notarization documents directly to the property center to get everything processed.&quot;</p>

<p>After the call to Nina was finished, Winslet took out two documents and said with a smile, &quot;Oh, right. Some good news came through just yesterday. I was so busy my head was spinning and I completely forgot about it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Anglo dropped the lawsuit?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy picked it up and looked, her face breaking into a delighted smile.</p>

<p>The constant worry she had been carrying finally settled. Even though Winslet had kept reassuring her it was just a nuisance lawsuit, any ordinary person would feel anxious getting tangled up in such legal trouble.</p>

<p>Winslet gathered her purse and said, &quot;Not dropped. The court simply refused to accept the case.&quot;</p>

<p>She decided not to drive, riding instead in Nancy’s car. On the way, she roughly outlined the current situation.</p>

<p>&quot;Based solely on a contract, with no payment records, no other insurance payment history, not even a proper probation period, and yet they claim the money was paid upfront.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That kind of situation is just arguing with empty words. Generally, for these labor and wage dispute cases, the courts are so swamped they mostly push for mediation.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And mediation would be scheduled who knows when. I was really startled when the result came out this week.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet asked directly, &quot;Did you pull some strings or something?&quot;</p>

<p>At the registration counter, Luis directly pushed Nancy forward. The fairy-like sister-in-law was completely confused. &quot;What are you doing?&quot;</p>

<p>She never expected Luis would so casually hand over a property worth millions to her.</p>
