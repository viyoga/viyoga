<p>&quot;Three thousand dollars, is it going to kill your family, or did you spend a fortune on it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If you can’t afford a wife, just say so. Why are you lecturing her as if my daughter stole your money?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth, this shrew, was eloquent to begin with, and now, having the moral high ground, her output was devastatingly effective:</p>

<p>&quot;Mother-in-law, that’s not how you should put it. What woman would sell her own jewelry unless she was truly desperate?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If Daniel were more capable, my daughter wouldn’t have to do this.&quot;</p>

<p>The words were deeply offensive, yet they weren’t baseless. Hela gritted her teeth and said, &quot;Mother-in-law, I didn’t mean it that way. You said yourself it was her nest egg. I just wanted to show I care about them.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth wasn’t having any of it, firing back immediately, &quot;Mother-in-law, parents show they care by spending money and effort, not just with empty words. If you truly cared, you’d have known where Daniel spent that over a thousand dollars long ago.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s for game equipment! It’s still worth that much if I sell it now!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing his mother losing ground repeatedly, Daniel’s anger flared, &quot;And her? Where did that over three thousand dollars go? All those bottles and jars of cosmetics?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Since when do you control how I spend my money? How amusing.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy smiled, raised her wrist, and said, &quot;Mom, how much is this worth? The receipt is here, see for yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>The Summer Cicada series bracelet worth over three thousand dollars perfectly explained it away.</p>

<p>Daniel’s face changed, and he immediately said, &quot;No, that’s your mother’s...&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth raised her own hand and took Lily’s hand, saying speechlessly, &quot;We’re wearing ours.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily even added, &quot;Brother-in-law, one bracelet is enough. It’s not like you’re practicing Iron Wire Fist, you don’t need to wear so many.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily’s follow-up strikes were exceptionally precise. Normally, one would only buy one bracelet; who would have thought Luis bought two specifically to suck up.</p>

<p>Hela’s face was now full of helplessness. She said softly, &quot;Nancy, what made you think of buying this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That old jewelry was too tacky, I didn’t want to wear it anymore. I thought I’d buy something nice.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy said as if it were the most natural thing in the world, &quot;Mother-in-law, gold holds its value. I just spent a little on the craftsmanship fee. What’s the problem?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well, you should have said so earlier, to avoid misunderstandings!&quot;</p>

<p>Hela was mortified, feeling she had lost all face.</p>

<p>At the very least, everyone now knew the Daniels family’s contemptuous attitude. Ruth naturally wouldn’t let the opportunity slip, immediately adding,</p>

<p>&quot;Mother-in-law, what exactly is wrong with your Daniel? He doesn’t earn money himself, yet he spends it on games.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I won’t comment on other things, but if it were me, I certainly wouldn’t agree to this. Besides, the over three thousand dollars came from selling her own jewelry. What’s with the endless interrogation? It’s not his money.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if your son mortgaged or sold the house, my daughter wouldn’t have the right to question it, would she?&quot;</p>

<p>After this barrage, Hela felt utterly miserable, glaring fiercely at her son. This was completely different from what he had said before they came.</p>

<p>Daniel was already furious with humiliation. He suddenly pulled out another envelope and said, &quot;Then what about this lawyer’s letter?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy did not even glance at it. She directly tossed the contract to her mother-in-law, Hela, with a clever, radiant smile. &quot;Mother, see for yourself!&quot;</p>

<p>Her attitude was so detached that Hela instead grew timid. Not daring to open the contract, she asked, &quot;What is the meaning of this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Your son does not earn money, and you support him. I earn my own money and encountered some trouble. I did not want to worry you all. Who knew he would make such a tremendous fuss over nothing.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As a husband, he is not only useless in helping, but now he is here causing more trouble. He is truly a piece of work.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy now spoke lightly, yet her presence was overwhelmingly powerful. She stated directly, &quot;All he knows is eating, drinking, and having fun. Mother-in-law, you ought to know that a lawyer’s letter is just a worthless piece of paper.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bullshit! It says right here that you have to pay tens of thousands!&quot; Daniel immediately spat out, exasperated.</p>

<p>Nancy looked at him with utter contempt. &quot;My apologies, but I have sued them as well. A few thousand is a conservative figure; you could write it as hundred of thousands. The real question is whether the court will support such a claim.&quot;</p>

<p>Hela felt a wave of weakness wash over her. Looking at her son, red-faced with shame and anger, she suddenly felt very angry herself.</p>

<p>Ruth suddenly let out a sigh and said, &quot;Mother-in-law, if you look down on us, you can say it directly. There is no need to find so many excuses.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mother-in-law, you misunderstand me. I am also a parent; I do not mean that at all,&quot; Hela quickly said, resorting to polite, formal words.</p>

<p>&quot;Last time, when the two children ended up at the police station, I thought you came to apologize as you said. Instead, you came to pick faults with our family.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth, now holding the upper hand, immediately adopted a tone of helplessness. &quot;Well then, you and Daniel should ponder what other faults you can possibly voice.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He hit his wife, and yet he comes here saying these things. Bullying our family is not done this way.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mother, please say no more about this.&quot; Lily’s follow-up strike was particularly sharp. &quot;Their family upbringing might be like gold and jade on the outside, but rotten within. Showing up empty-handed with New Year greetings for the mother-in-law’s family, only to express disdain upon arrival. After beating his wife, he fabricates stories and comes to our door to make trouble.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Look down on our family? But I have never seen our elder brother-in-law buy anything for you. Perhaps he is too poor and has spent all his money, so he can only come to our house to put on a show.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The elder son-in-law is less thoughtful than the second son-in-law who married into the family. Even a normal son-in-law from the countryside would not be that shabby.&quot; 2</p>

<p>&quot;At the very least, he should bring a couple of local chickens, right? How can he just buy two random bags of fruit downstairs?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And as it happens, we know the fruit seller. Those two bags together cost less than 3 dollars, and he still picked the cheapest ones. The humiliation is monumental.&quot;</p>

<p>This comment made Hela’s face completely lose its composure, her expression shifting between dark and stormy one moment and clear the next.</p>

<p>At that moment, Daniel suddenly stood up, slammed the table, and shouted, &quot;Lily, you ugly woman, shut your mouth for me! You have no sense of respect, who gave you the right to speak?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Shut up!&quot; Hela spun around abruptly and slapped Daniel across the face.</p>

<p>The sound was exceptionally sharp and clear. Daniel covered his mouth as he sat down, his face a picture of disbelief. After a moment’s thought, he lowered his head again.</p>

<p>But he still roared unwillingly, &quot;Mom, don’t listen to her nonsense! She sold all of her own bags! She must have...&quot;</p>

<p>Before he could finish, Nancy sneered, took a newly bought bag from the coat rack beside her, and first placed an invoice in front of her mother-in-law.</p>

<p>&quot;Mother-in-law, even if I sold three bags, they wouldn’t be worth much. The ones your son bought for me... well, it seems none are as good as the one you carry, are they?&quot;</p>

<p>Not waiting for Hela to formulate a response, Nancy pointed at the new bag she had bought that day and said, &quot;After selling them, I added my own money to buy a new one. That shouldn’t be considered excessive, should it? There’s really no need to come here and make an issue out of this.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This... It’s not excessive!&quot; Hela’s voice trembled slightly as she spoke.</p>

<p>&quot;Your family spends money on your son. How stressful buying a house is has nothing to do with me. My name isn’t on that deed at all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Every day he nags at me about how stressful buying a house is. Is he insane? What does that have to do with me? If I had known about Daniel’s pile of gambling debts back when we got married, I would never have married him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And what’s the deal with your son’s living expenses? If he gets all the money, then why should I have to beg him for it like a beggar? Am I just a haggard housewife who only needs to avoid starvation each month?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy completely erupted now. She slammed the table and stood up, saying, &quot;Mother-in-law, are these matters clear now?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Clear, clear!&quot; Hela was also startled.</p>

<p>This was the first time Nancy had lost her temper in front of her.</p>

<p>&quot;If it’s not clear, it doesn’t matter. You didn’t contribute a single cent. What does it matter to you whether it’s clear or not?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Understood. If she really owed 20,000 dollars, how much would you contribute? Have you ever treated my elder sister as part of your family?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Changing a bag, changing a piece of gold jewelry, and you all stare like thieves. How poor must you be to be so sensitive?&quot;</p>

<p>With this stab from Lily, Daniel and his mother were suffocated, while the Wood family members were mentally exhilarated.</p>

<p>Luis immediately brought a bowl of clear conch and fish maw soup from the kitchen and scolded mockingly, &quot;Wife, the adults are talking. Don’t interrupt.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, I know~&quot; The couple exchanged a secret chuckle.</p>

<p>Luis appeared to be stopping her, but in reality, he was rewarding her!</p>

<p>The atmosphere fell into a momentary silence. Hela simply did not know what to say.</p>

<p>She had originally intended to speak from a position of moral superiority, but instead found herself rebutted at every turn and was nearly driven mad.</p>

<p>&quot;Daniel, how are we going to account for that slap you gave me?&quot; Nancy asked, her face flushed with anger.</p>

<p>Hela immediately started, &quot;Nancy, husband and wife fight at the head of the bed and make up at the foot. It’s normal.&quot;</p>

<p>Her words immediately drew dissatisfaction from Ruth.</p>

<p>&quot;That was in the past. Who can tolerate such treatment nowadays? Mother-in-law, look at what happened today. Your son deliberately created all this messy trouble to pick on Nancy, isn’t that right?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What, if your husband hit you, would you endure it? If it were me, I definitely wouldn’t.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The hitting is done. You came here not to apologize but to say all this. Think for yourself whether there is any sincerity in that.&quot;</p>

<p>Hela was also losing patience. &quot;Mother-in-law, then what do you suggest we do?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What to do?!&quot; Nancy was a fiery character too. The moment her mother-in-law’s words fell, she suddenly stepped forward and slapped Daniel hard across the face.</p>
