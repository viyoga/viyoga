<p>Elara Ye’s tone was unusually serious for the first time.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m in Bathek City!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis took a drag and asked with a chuckle, &quot;Sis, did you get news about Xeno?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, what exactly did you do???&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye’s voice was hoarse and worried. &quot;I only got word this morning when I arrived at work. Xeno was hit by a car last night.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I didn’t do anything. Last night, I had a threesome with two women, a mother-daughter pair. It was fucking amazing.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis laughed lewdly.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t give me that bullshit and try to brush me off with this nonsense.&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye clearly didn’t believe him, her voice full of concern. &quot;You need to trust me. I’m your sister. You have to tell me everything about this, so we don’t leave any loose ends.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll talk when you get to the provincial capital. Sis, first tell me what’s the situation with Xeno.&quot;</p>

<p>Once Xeno arrived, as a TV station leader involved in the production, he was warmly received by the crew. He was an old acquaintance of the show’s producer. Naturally, he stayed at a luxurious hotel. Last night, the crew, including the director and lead actors, had wined and dined him.</p>

<p>The dinner was at a seafood restaurant near the hotel, accompanied by some of the young actresses looking to climb the ladder. The casting couch was an open secret in the entertainment industry.</p>

<p>Directors held absolute power over actors like feudal lords. Even some assistant directors in charge of external relations had slept with countless women.</p>

<p>But in front of producers, they bowed and scraped obsequiously. Above producers in the food chain were people like Xeno. So for young actresses, latching onto a real power player like him meant more opportunities.</p>

<p>Amidst the clinking glasses, Xeno was flattered into a state of bliss.</p>

<p>After the meal, the director and producer sent the others back first. They personally took the leisurely-walking Xeno and the young actress he’d taken a liking to back to the hotel.</p>

<p>That should have been the end of it. But after they left, Xeno, buying cigarettes at a convenience store near the hotel, spotted a drunk-driving checkpoint at the intersection and became intrigued.</p>

<p>Even though the young actress was coaxing him to go back and shower, the Chinese love for watching a spectacle took over. It was also one of Xeno’s favorite pastimes.</p>

<p>The night was long, and he wasn’t in a rush. Curiosity piqued, Xeno took the young actress to watch the commotion.</p>

<p>Initially, everything was normal; they hadn’t caught anyone. They watched for a while, somewhat disappointed.</p>

<p>He was just about to take the girl back to the room when suddenly, the crowd erupted in excited cheers. He hurried back to see a group of police surrounding a white BMW.</p>

<p>The white BMW went berserk, flooring the gas and swerving through traffic, sending pedestrians scattering in panic. Most alarmingly, a police officer was clinging to the hood, and another was being dragged behind.</p>

<p>Hit-and-runs happened, but this brazen and violent was rare. The BMW wildly sought an escape route.</p>

<p>It rammed through other cars to flee. Just as Xeno was stunned for a moment, screams erupted around him. He happened to be blocked from the view by the crowd.</p>

<p>Already slow to react due to the alcohol, by the time he realized what was happening, the BMW’s glaring headlights were right in front of him.</p>

<p>Over a dozen onlookers were knocked down. He and the young actress were among them, knocked unconscious immediately.</p>

<p>The BMW sped off, flinging the officer from the hood. The police on scene were furious. While reporting to headquarters for tracking and interception, they quickly directed the ambulances, originally there for blood tests, to rush the injured to the hospital.</p>

<p>Elara Ye said, &quot;Our leadership found out in the middle of the night. The young actress called someone from the crew, and they got to the hospital first to look after him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Our leaders are taking an early morning flight over. Xeno’s family also has strong connections. They’re going to check on his condition.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Our leaders are taking an early morning flight over. Xeno’s family also has strong connections. They’re going to check on his condition.&quot;</p>

<p>She paused, then said with a hint of reproach, &quot;Luis, you probably know more details than I do.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How would I know? I wasn’t there. This was an accident, and it has nothing to do with me.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said shamelessly, &quot;Sis, I told you, I was having a threesome with a mother and daughter. What does that bastard Xeno getting hit by a car have to do with me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t go blaming an innocent man. I’m a law-abiding citizen.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I usually feel guilty for a long time if I step on an ant. There’s no way I’d do something so heinous.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a long silence on the other end. Finally, Elara Ye let out a relieved laugh and said, &quot;Luis, that attitude is actually right. You can’t admit anything to anyone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Including me. The fewer people who know, the safer you are.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sis understands. Your caution is very prudent and mature. Don’t worry, this stays between us siblings.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Whether it’s your dad or your brother-in-law, I won’t breathe a word of it.&quot;</p>

<p>Finally, she said with lingering worry, &quot;Sis is just worried you might have been careless and left some unnecessary loose ends.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Since it’s said to be a big mess over there, and that’s not our family’s traditional territory, it’s better to be careful. I’d still like to go over some details with you.&quot;</p>

<p>After his bit of dramatic deception, Luis chuckled, &quot;Don’t worry, sis. You need to have faith in my abilities.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You can rest one hundred percent assured about this. It’s just an accident. No matter how the authorities investigate, that’s what they’ll find.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing Luis speak so confidently, Elara Ye inexplicably felt more at ease. She thought to herself that she couldn’t be the one to panic now.</p>

<p>Her little brother was right. In her attitude, she had to treat this as a pure accident. Otherwise, she’d give herself away.</p>

<p>With that thought, she felt a pang of guilt. She believed that Luis had done all this.</p>

<p>This harmless, handsome young brother had meticulously planned such an accident just to help her, a sister he’d only just met. The enormous risk he’d taken made her uneasy.</p>

<p>For anyone else, only parents would go this far for their children. Even in a typical marriage, couples wouldn’t do this for each other, unless they were foolishly love-struck.</p>

<p>It was indeed the most brutal and effective way to solve the problem permanently, but the risk was immense.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, Sis believes you!!!&quot;</p>

<p>She was still a bit disappointed that her brother only vaguely acknowledged it without sharing any details.</p>

<p>She couldn’t help but worry, but now she was mostly moved. She never expected Luis to be so good to her.</p>

<p>From what she’d gathered, this accident had created quite a stir. Though confirmed no one had died, the large number of injuries made it a major incident.</p>

<p>The top leadership was personally handling it, and the station’s leaders were rushing over to assess the situation.</p>
