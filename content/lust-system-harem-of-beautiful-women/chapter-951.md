<p>Cilantro, chopped scallions, sesame paste, chive flower sauce, coarse chili oil... all kinds of bottled chili sauces were fully available. You could mix whatever you liked however you wanted to eat it.</p>

<p>&quot;Lily, why are you eating so spicy!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth furrowed her delicate brows.</p>

<p>Lily had made a fairly standard dipping sauce, just sesame paste, red fermented bean curd, chive flowers, and chili oil. But the proportion of chili oil was indeed more than normal.</p>

<p>It looked like Eloise was about to speak, so Luis immediately defended her, &quot;Mom, what suits the taste is precious. Having an appetite is a good thing. If you cannot eat anything, and whatever you eat you throw up, then no matter how nutritious the food is, it is useless.&quot;</p>

<p>With that said, the elders’ desire to lecture was completely suppressed.</p>

<p>Lily also had a face full of happy smiles. At this moment Avery said with a grin, &quot;In the future when looking for a husband, you have to find someone like brother-in-law. Dominating and protecting your wife is really too awesome.&quot;</p>

<p>This sentence was taken as a child’s joke, but her mother Blanche knew that her daughter and Luis had long since had an affair.</p>

<p>Blanche rolled her eyes and said, &quot;That depends on whether he would even take a fancy to you. Besides, your brother-in-law is already married. What are you farting out this nonsense for?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I want to marry her but her family does not agree. Why do they not agree? Her husband does not agree!!&quot;</p>

<p>Avery’s mindset had long since become calm. She said with a grin, &quot;Mom, it is this kind of TikTok skit, right?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Get out of here. Not studying properly and playing on your phone all day.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche rolled her eyes at her.</p>

<p>The meal could be considered harmonious and happy. After eating, some went for a walk, and the mother-in-law and the others continued playing cards.</p>

<p>Brook took out the invitation. Luis took one look and said, &quot;I was wondering why you suddenly came over. There is no need to be so formal, right?&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel’s grandfather was celebrating his 85th birthday and had sent out invitations. All the branches of the family had to attend, and this time it absolutely had to be held lively and bustling, even more lively than a so-called 60th birthday.</p>

<p>There is a Chinese saying: at 73 and 84, the King of Hell does not call and you go yourself.</p>

<p>So an 85th birthday was an especially grand matter. If you crossed this hurdle, it meant heading toward a long life of a hundred years.</p>

<p>In fact, father-in-law Arthur had mentioned this matter before, but Luis had not taken it to heart. In his mind, this was the older generation’s business, what the hell did it have to do with him?</p>

<p>But then he thought of Darlan’s face-loving personality. The invitations would be sent out early. The three brothers of this family were none of them easy to deal with.</p>

<p>In the countryside, face was paramount. This time it would naturally be done on a grand scale. If you did not go, it might reach the extent that they would hold a grudge against you.</p>

<p>With so many people, Brook smiled gracefully, &quot;Of course. The old master has a good reputation, but at this age those old comrades in arms will not travel so far to come.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Moreover, the descendants want that kind of spectacle. This time it will definitely be held on a grand scale.&quot;</p>

<p>The time was next Wednesday. Luis took a look and, with a flash of inspiration, said, &quot;Then we will definitely go. But my wife is pregnant, so she will not participate in any red or white events. The old man should be able to understand this point, right?&quot;</p>

<p>Brook nodded and smiled, &quot;That is normal. My husband’s grandfather is still reasonable.&quot;</p>

<p>After chatting for a while, she left. Chloe also went back with the child and the nanny.</p>

<p>Her daughter Aisha was even reluctant to leave. Coming here there was a yard to run around and play in. The whole time everyone was overflowing with maternal love, carrying her around constantly. For a small child that was truly heaven.</p>

<p>On Sunday evening, Avery rarely asked for leave.</p>

<p>They had formally moved over here to live. The two-bedroom, two-living-room apartment was not considered high-end, but it was arranged very warmly.</p>

<p>Excluding the so-called public area, pieced together from two one-bedroom units, this place was actually much bigger than the apartment they used to live in. The most important thing was that it was quiet. There was no highway outside the window, so the nights here were very peaceful.</p>

<p>It was not the kind of so-called new community built for real estate development. This was an old urban area, a place where every inch of land was precious.</p>

<p>It was not necessarily bustling, and the roads were not that wide, but all the breath of life was preserved in this kind of place.</p>

<p>This kind of ordinary little home itself had nothing astonishing about it. The name on the deed of this two-bedroom apartment pieced together from two one-bedroom units was under Lily and Leah.</p>

<p>The transfer procedures had already been completed. To put it simply, they would not ask much either. Now it was already under the names of the mother and daughter.</p>

<p>And next, after the sales office’s performance was completed, those two storefronts over there would also be transferred to their names.</p>

<p>Some relationships seemed absurd, but only people who had experienced the beatings of reality knew that this kind of absurd answer was a more direct expression of love than so-called true love and effort.</p>

<p>Some people, even if they had money, would not necessarily be willing to spend it on you. After living a life like rootless duckweed for too long, actually no one was willing to do that.</p>

<p>This community was not considered new, but the breath of life here was abundant. Even if the neighbors did not know each other yet, after coming here they had already gotten to know so many people through Ruth, and there was stable work too.</p>

<p>Actually, both the mother and daughter were very happy. In the ordinary living room, Luis smoked and watched TV programs.</p>

<p>When he glanced over, the mother and daughter were busily working in the kitchen. To be precise, Blanche was the one leading, and Avery was trying to learn how to become a good wife.</p>
