<p>&quot;My dear brother-in-law!&quot;</p>

<p>With a cheerful shout, Leah and Avery, both dressed in school uniforms, emerged from seemingly nowhere and squeezed into the car, though they seemed to share some unspoken secrets and settled together into the back seat.</p>

<p>Looking at the two girls, each with twin tails and hair of almost identical length, Luis couldn’t control the saliva gathering in his mouth.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law, let’s go home and change clothes first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, yes, brother-in-law, we want to have a big meal for lunch.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No problem!&quot;</p>

<p>With a doting smile, Luis took them home first. The moment they arrived, the two girls scampered upstairs. Avery was now completely familiar with the place and hardly considered herself an outsider anymore.</p>

<p>When they came back down, Luis’s eyes lit up. They had changed into matching blue JK skirts, radiating a full, adorable Japanese schoolgirl style that was utterly lovable no matter how you looked at them.</p>

<p>The key points were their nearly identical height and similarly sized chests. Each was pretty in her own way, and even though the younger sister-in-law might have a slight edge, standing together they looked like enchanting twins.</p>

<p>&quot;Pretty, right!&quot;</p>

<p>Leah laughed, twirling around.</p>

<p>The tantalizing sway of her chest was enough to make anyone drool. Avery, meanwhile, made a funny face and said, &quot;Brother-in-law is practically drooling.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, your brother-in-law wishes he could just gobble you both up.&quot;</p>

<p>At noon, they ate at the Haidilao hot pot restaurant the girls had been craving. Afterwards, he bought movie tickets for them all to watch a film together. Luis had initially thought he might get a little mischievous in the dark theater, but the showing was packed, with people all around them.</p>

<p>Added to that, the movie’s visual effects were so bright. When he tried stroking his sister-in-law’s thigh, his hand was swatted away. When he tried the same with Avery’s thigh, she shyly but firmly stopped him too.</p>

<p>Left with no other choice, Luis had no option but to behave himself.</p>

<p>After the movie ended, they went shopping at the mall, buying some clothes and daily necessities. Honestly, walking around with these two girls definitely boosted his ego, at the very least drawing envious and jealous glances from others.</p>

<p>By evening, once they were back home, Leah started to seem a little fidgety.</p>

<p>Luis noticed her unusual expression but didn’t press for details, pretending not to see as he lit a cigarette on the sofa. He had no idea what the two girls had been whispering about together.</p>

<p>It was Avery who finally came over, lowering her voice to say, &quot;Brother-in-law, can we discuss something?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What is it?&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing her awkward demeanor, Luis wondered what kind of trouble she was brewing.</p>

<p>Avery whispered, &quot;A classmate is treating us for her birthday tonight. We’ll have dinner and then go to karaoke afterwards. Is that okay?&quot;</p>

<p>This statement took Luis by surprise. He had thought it was something serious, but it turned out to be such a minor request.</p>

<p>Leah immediately rushed over, clinging to Luis’s arm and wheedling, &quot;Brother-in-law, please let us go. We promise we’ll just eat, maybe have a little drink, and then come back straight away, on our best behavior.&quot;</p>

<p>Her mother’s parenting probably wasn’t that strict. It was clear Avery wasn’t particularly close to her mother, and she had a somewhat rebellious streak.</p>

<p>But Leah was different. Even though she was practically from a single-parent household, her mother, Ruth, was a veritable tigress. The idea of Leah going out at night was almost unthinkable.</p>

<p>Leah had been raised under very strict discipline. Not to mention her older sister, Nancy, who was basically a carbon copy of the maternal tigress, never holding back when scolding her younger sister.</p>

<p>Then there was the second sister, Lily. Her temper was good and she was gentle enough, but Lily’s nature was extremely conservative and traditional.</p>

<p>Don’t be fooled by her poor academic performance—it wasn’t for lack of effort; her natural aptitude just wasn’t there. She studied with remarkable diligence, the very model of a good student whom even teachers could find no fault with.</p>

<p>In her traditional worldview, it was normal to go out for meals with colleagues after starting work, but for a student still in school? What business did she have going out for fancy meals?</p>

<p>As for places like bars and KTVs, in her eyes they were utter dens of iniquity. What decent girl from a good family would ever go to such places? One could say Lily had never set foot in any nightclub and possessed no curiosity about them whatsoever.</p>

<p>In her view, it was acceptable for men to drink socially outside, but for women to do so was simply improper. Luis actually found his lovely wife’s peculiar mindset quite appealing.</p>

<p>&quot;You want me to keep your secret again??&quot; Luis teased.</p>

<p>&quot;Please, brother-in-law!&quot;</p>

<p>Leah brought her hands together in a pleading gesture. Honestly, with that adorable look of hers, it was impossible for anyone to refuse.</p>

<p>She was just too cute, especially when she got close, carrying that milky fragrance that was simply irresistible. But after a moment’s thought, Luis still gave a lecherous grin. &quot;You want your brother-in-law to be your partner in crime? Well then, shouldn’t you give your brother-in-law a little... benefit?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You lecherous brother-in-law! What... what are you trying to do?&quot;</p>

<p>Leah pouted coquettishly.</p>

<p>Avery looked somewhat embarrassed and said, &quot;Brother-in-law, we’re running out of time. Please don’t play any tricks on us, okay?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright. Then your brother-in-law will drive you over!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Long live brother-in-law!&quot;</p>

<p>The two girls cheered in unison. Surprisingly, the classmate hosting the party was quite well-off. For a high school student’s birthday, the venue wasn’t some small restaurant but the Ocean Hotel.</p>

<p>The two girls had dressed up carefully and were carrying their branded bags. They got out of the car at the hotel entrance, overjoyed, and headed upstairs together.</p>

<p>Luis parked the car in the parking lot and went upstairs directly to book a room for himself to rest in, wondering internally if he should try opening a blind box.</p>

<p>He now had three blind boxes. Keeping two for emergencies was enough; he could afford to open one and see what was inside.</p>

<p>But Luis didn’t have much confidence in his luck. What if he ended up with some useless, random item? That would be truly frustrating.</p>

<p>While Luis was hesitating, the room door suddenly rattled with a knock.</p>

<p>Luis was startled, quickly getting out of bed. He walked to the door and peered through the peephole, his eyes widening in astonishment. Standing outside was his boss’s wife—or rather, the boss’s wife from his time working as a accountant at Tiger Real Estate.</p>

<p>Bella stood there, dressed in a dignified gray suit dress, holding herself as proudly as a peacock. Her hair was styled up in a bun, further accentuating her elegant and captivating beauty. The powerful aura she exuded was almost too intense to look at directly.</p>

<p>&quot;Boss lady, is there something you need?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis took a deep breath before finally opening the door.</p>
