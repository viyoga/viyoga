<p>Deewa said softly, &quot;Being an influencer isn’t as easy as it used to be. People are getting tired of the softcore stuff.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Those videos of women in tight clothes and masks wiggling around, almost no one watches them anymore unless you actually have some dance skills.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy cursed with a laugh, &quot;Filters, beauty modes, skin-smoothing, all that stuff.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;With enough Photoshop, even a sow can look pretty. Those stupid men have been fooled too many times, they’re not falling for it anymore.&quot;</p>

<p>Women’s jealousy was a given. They’d always had a score to settle with Bubbles, even if they’d never openly clashed. They remembered every little grudge.</p>

<p>It’s like the saying goes, sugar doesn’t always sweeten, but vinegar always sours.</p>

<p>Deewa had been doing influencer work herself, but without a man backing her or investors, she’d remained small-time, working solo.</p>

<p>Nancy, who’d always been vain, had no interest in the bar business, but she was genuinely passionate about this.</p>

<p>The two hit it off immediately, and then they decided to bring Chloe into the fold.</p>

<p>Chloe was currently at home with the baby, and with the nanny’s help, she often found herself with plenty of free time.</p>

<p>Plus, she was on maternity leave, earning only the minimum wage, barely enough to cover the nanny’s salary. She needed to find a way to make some extra money.</p>

<p>Between starting a business to earn and getting back at that &quot;green tea bitch,&quot; the three campus beauties hit it off immediately.</p>

<p>With a common enemy, their petty squabbles could be set aside. So they pooled their funds and set up this company.</p>

<p>Nancy invested three hundred thousand dollars and held 50% of the shares. Deewa had limited savings, and Chloe wasn’t exactly rolling in cash either.</p>

<p>Each of them put in eighty thousand dollars for 25%, with Deewa handling the main operations and Chloe occasionally helping with shoots.</p>

<p>&quot;So does that mean I paid for this dinner for nothing?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis joked with a wry smile.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be so stingy.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy shot him a seductive glare and said, &quot;Do you know how Deewa planned this out??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I’d love to see it.&quot;</p>

<p>Since they were all young and comfortable with each other, the atmosphere was relaxed. Luis generously picked up the tab for the new company’s first team-building dinner.</p>

<p>Afterward, the group headed to the neighboring Hans’s Bar, continuing the night in a stylishly decorated private room.</p>

<p>Drinking, singing, playing dice, Deewa sat at the corner bar with her laptop, explaining:</p>

<p>&quot;Based on the current best model, the three of us will be the main talent. No more cheap softcore stuff.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’ll build personas and do restaurant and shop reviews, that kind of thing. The old ’paid reviews with over-the-top praise’ formula doesn’t work anymore. And selling group deals or anything like that isn’t necessary yet.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;In the early stage, we just need to build a following and do everything we can to block Bubbles before the ’One City, One IP’ initiative kicks in.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis glanced at the plan. Their first phase was clear, you could tell what personas they were going for just by the names:</p>

<p>Boss’s Wife Deewa, Boss’s Wife Chloe, Boss’s Wife Nancy...</p>

<p>Besides the three main accounts, they’d also run over a dozen smaller ones, a common strategy these days.</p>

<p>The persona they were building was that of Hans’s Bar’s owner’s wife. Luis laughed. &quot;My sister-in-law actually qualifies, but doesn’t it feel strange for all three of you to use the same persona?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be so jealous!&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy gave him an ambiguous smile. &quot;What does Daniel have to do with it? You’re the real owner of Hans’s Bar, aren’t you?&quot;</p>

<p>She’d had a few drinks, which made her more direct.</p>

<p>This was basically laying all cards on the table, making it clear that all three of them were involved with Luis, so there was no point hiding it anymore.</p>

<p>Chloe looked a bit shy at that, while Deewa’s already flushed face grew even more alluringly rosy.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was thick with tension, but Luis couldn’t exactly make a move in front of the employees.</p>

<p>The Campus Beauties’ Feast mission was actually quite difficult. There were three of them and he only had two hands, even subtle moves wouldn’t be enough.</p>

<p>Even if they were willing, if he couldn’t give each of them enough attention, jealousy would be a real problem.</p>

<p>Luis realized this mission was genuinely challenging, he’d need to get them all willing and comfortable with a threesome, and that foundation wasn’t quite there yet.</p>

<p>Deewa cleared her throat and quickly changed the subject. &quot;Here’s the operational plan.&quot;</p>

<p>First, all three would maintain the &quot;boss’s wife&quot; persona, documenting daily life and bar operations.</p>

<p>Then they’d branch into travel content, exploring local spots, and presenting an image of financial freedom. Only after building a following would they start live-streaming.</p>

<p>Of course, they’d have smaller accounts leaving comments to pique curiosity.</p>

<p>For example, they could highlight Hans’s Bar’s background, the largest LGBTQ+ hub in the surrounding cities, which was unique enough.</p>

<p>Then, with all three being &quot;boss’s wives,&quot; viewers would speculate whether the boss was male or female, and who they really were.</p>

<p>Deewa was confident that with their looks and figures, no filters needed, they’d easily attract male attention.</p>

<p>And with smaller accounts stirring up comments like &quot;What a waste,&quot; &quot;Give me a chance to turn you straight,&quot; etc., they’d also draw in female followers. This was a strategy that appealed to both men and women, and they’d spent nearly a month refining it.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m a total outsider to all this.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis scratched his head. &quot;If you need any help or money, just let me know.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’ll need to make occasional appearances too. We haven’t signed any other talent besides us three.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy smiled. &quot;We’ll feature different attractive men and women from time to time. We know plenty of beautiful women, we can even bring in family members.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for handsome guys, Daniel and you can fill in. But his physique isn’t as good as yours, that’s a real drawback. And he’s gained weight lately.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa teased suggestively, &quot;Now that’s a qualified sister-in-law, she knows her brother-in-law’s body better than his own wife does.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe covered her mouth and laughed, clearly enjoying seeing them bicker even after making peace.</p>

<p>Nancy wrapped her arm around Luis’s and said with a huff, &quot;That’s right, and I enjoy using it very much too. What’s the matter, Deewa, has my brother-in-law not been giving you enough attention lately? You sound so sour.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You slut!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa was still unmarried and relatively inexperienced.</p>

<p>Nancy’s teasing left her speechless. In truth, among the three, Chloe, the mother, was the most open-minded.</p>

<p>When women get together, they can be just as dirty. Especially since they all knew about each other’s relationships, teasing no longer had any effect.</p>

<p>After getting a full picture of the company’s operations, Luis was still in the dark, so this was what they’d been so secretive about all this time.</p>

<p>Regardless, it was good that they’d buried the hatchet and come together. At least the Campus Beauties’ Feast mission still had potential.</p>

<p>Past ten, the employees gradually left, tired from drinking and partying.</p>

<p>Luis walked the three of them downstairs. In that instant, he realized this was definitely a love triangle from hell.</p>

<p>As soon as they stepped outside, the three tipsy women, who’d been laughing and chatting about work, suddenly stopped in unison at the curb.</p>

<p>They turned to look at him with flirtatious, eager eyes. Though none of them said a word,</p>

<p>Those beautiful eyes were speaking volumes, each asking the same question: who would Luis be leaving with tonight?</p>

<p>Deewa stretched. &quot;Same here. I’ve been working overtime like crazy. If I sleep in the office one more time, my back will give out. I’ll get a hotel room tonight and just crash.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I can’t breastfeed tonight since I’ve been drinking. Looks like I can sleep on my own, huh~&quot;</p>

<p>Since everything was out in the open, there was no point pretending anymore.</p>

<p>Though they’d reconciled, the three campus beauties were all proud women, this was a contest of charm and allure.</p>

<p>Given the current dynamic, a threesome was basically impossible, and even a twosome would be tough. Luis knew his limits.</p>

<p>Cold sweat dripped down his back. Just as his scalp started tingling, his phone rang, it was his wife Lily.</p>

<p>&quot;Honey!!&quot;</p>
