<p>&quot;At that time I was half-drunk and half-awake, my head was so dizzy I had no strength to resist, but I clearly knew he took off my clothes, and then raped me...&quot;</p>

<p>Brook spoke of this very calmly, as if it were not something that happened to herself.</p>

<p>&quot;I resisted but it was useless. He slapped me and I became dazed. The funny thing is, after that bastard coerced me, he actually got drunk and slept in my bed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The next day, this matter caused a huge uproar. My dad wanted to kill him. I just kept crying. At that time, after what he did to me, there was a huge amount of blood on the bed. I was afraid I was going to die.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy asked very innocently, &quot;Then you did not call the police??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course I thought about it.&quot;</p>

<p>Brook secretly gritted her silver teeth, and finally sighed, &quot;My dad was also relatively open-minded.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He told me, if I wanted to call the police, he would support it.&quot;</p>

<p>But Brook knew her father’s personality. Poor as they were, he had always stood upright between heaven and earth. If Brook really called the police, he would feel guilty for a lifetime, and might even do something drastic.</p>

<p>&quot;My father-in-law, in front of me, tied up Darian and threw everything at him, smashing until the last moment when there was blood all over the floor and he was taken away by ambulance.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That time he had multiple fractures all over his body. The scar on his face was also left from that time. When I saw he really went for the kill, I was also stunned and did not know what to do.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Later, my father-in-law even knelt down and kowtowed to me, saying he had failed to teach his son properly. It scared me.&quot;</p>

<p>Brook sighed, &quot;I had no way to hate him. From childhood to adulthood, how good he was to me, to my family, I know better than anyone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;From my high school memories, when going to good restaurants, it was always him who had my dad bring me. He often bought me backpacks and stationery that I could never use up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That time my dad had a car accident, he was the first to bring twenty thousand over.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When my mom got sick, he also without a word took out fifty thousand. That money has still not been paid back to this day.&quot;</p>

<p>The two of them fell silent upon hearing this. This absolutely counted as a life-and-death friendship. Many blood brothers could not even reach this level.</p>

<p>Brook said calmly with a smile, &quot;At that time I could not harden my heart. They only had this one precious son. If I really sent him to prison, I would be repaying kindness with enmity.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And at that time Darian also promised that he liked me, and would marry me once I graduated from university.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I really did not know what to do either, so I could only vaguely brush it off. As a result, he also pestered me from time to time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This matter was treated as if it had never happened, but my father-in-law and mother-in-law treated me even better. After I graduated from university, my dad really did discuss the marriage with them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;To be honest, I was a bit confused. I muddled my way into marrying Darian.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe it was also a case of giving up on myself. If I had to say the one thing I was satisfied with, of course it was my father-in-law and mother-in-law who doted on me like a daughter. I just could not bear to make them sad.&quot;</p>

<p>At that time Brook was also lost and had no opinions of her own. This marriage could be considered a muddled account.</p>

<p>But seeing her father-in-law and her own dad happy, as if they felt this sinful debt had reached a perfect ending, they were also comforted. Comrades in arms had become in-laws, so they could visit each other more.</p>

<p>As for Darian, Brook also had bitterness only she knew.</p>

<p>When she was studying, Darian had caused plenty of trouble outside, and had fooled around with plenty of women outside. Although the elders all helped hide it, she still heard some of it.</p>

<p>After marriage, his heavy drinking, gambling, and cheating were also done plenty of times, making Brook furious beyond measure.</p>

<p>She wanted to be a qualified wife and daughter-in-law. When Darian was selling off her jewelry, she spoke up to stop him.</p>

<p>As a result, Darian, who was used to freedom, beat her viciously. If her father-in-law had not stopped him in time, who knew what big thing might have happened.</p>

<p>There was no newlywed sweetness. Not long after marriage she moved to the city and went to work at City First Hospital. With Darian it was almost a state of never having anything to do with each other until death.</p>

<p>Brook put on her clothes while laughing at herself, &quot;Come to think of it, it is quite laughable. At my wedding with Darian, it was so grand that I even somewhat wanted to properly be his wife.&quot;</p>

<p>At that time Darian’s family had money, and jadeite was at its hottest speculation.</p>

<p>Normally in the countryside people held their own banquets. When they married, they set over forty tables, the most attended in the nearby villages.</p>

<p>After collecting the gift money, each person received a red envelope and a pack of premium cigarettes as a return gift.</p>

<p>The food cost was 5000 per table, also the current record holder. Luxurious seafood was like it cost nothing. The liquor was uniformly Martell Blue Swift, and the celebratory cigarettes were also premium ones.</p>

<p>Brook could also be considered to have married gloriously. The stone in the hearts of the two comrades in arms finally landed. This was absolutely family upon family.</p>

<p>So at that time Brook let it go. Mainly that nightmare-like memory had become a bit blurry because of being drunk, so the harm it caused her was not as intense as for an ordinary victim.</p>
