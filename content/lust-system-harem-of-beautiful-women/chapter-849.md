<p>Suzanne took a deep breath, looked directly at Luis, and said, &quot;Don’t worry, I know I’m just the mistress. When it comes to your family’s affairs, even your father-in-law can’t interfere, let alone me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I have no right to meddle in your family matters.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Aunt Suzanne, I respect and appreciate your open-mindedness!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was slightly taken aback, then said, &quot;But I’ve always maintained at least a basic level of respect for you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I know, and I thank you for that!&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne’s eyes softened as she said softly, &quot;At first, when your eldest sister married Darian, they did come to visit us.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if I’m not one to despise the poor and favor the rich, at least I was happy back then. At least I had your eldest sister’s approval.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But it was all fake. Darian’s family is indeed well-off, but that has nothing to do with him as a person. He actually showed me about as much respect as he showed your mother-in-law.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I could see your father-in-law was angry, and I could see your sister-in-law’s helplessness. But they only visit once or twice a year, so I didn’t want to ruin the mood.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Darian doesn’t have any ill intent. He’s just a pure idiot.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis never thought he’d one day be defending that brother-in-law.</p>

<p>But it wasn’t insincere. In the past, Darian really was an idiot. But now that he’d turned gay, he was truly unfathomable.</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, honestly, none of this is really my business.&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne sighed. &quot;I know I’ve wronged your mother-in-law, and I don’t dare to upset her.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;To be honest, at this age, talking about marriage and such is pointless. I made my mistake back then.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis pulled out a cigarette, lit it, took a puff, and pondered: &quot;Aunt Suzanne, so did you see me today?&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne said decisively, &quot;I didn’t see anything today. I’ll be heading back after I finish my shopping.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re still busy in the provincial capital, and I haven’t seen you today either.&quot;</p>

<p>Communicating with an intelligent person was truly refreshing. Suzanne lifted the bags in her hand and said with a smile, &quot;Alright, I need to take these things back. I won’t mention today’s incident to anyone.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you!&quot;</p>

<p>Watching her leave, Luis was lost in thought.</p>

<p>Given the system’s wicked nature, Suzanne was such a beautiful, gentle, intellectual, and refined young woman.</p>

<p>She was the type whose beauty grew on you. What was puzzling was that she was her father-in-law’s mistress, yet the evil system hadn’t issued a quest for her.</p>

<p>Luis rubbed his chin, pondering whether he should stir up some trouble to test if the system might be interested in adding another green hat to his father-in-law’s collection.</p>

<p>The provincial capital during evening rush hour was always horribly congested.</p>

<p>The sky was still tinged with dusk, and the fiery clouds were a precursor to the coming storm. The air was oppressively hot, making it unbearable the moment you stepped out of the TV station gates.</p>

<p>A wave of heat nearly knocked her over. Elara Ye, dressed in professional attire that looked sharp and alluring, greeted passing colleagues with a beaming smile.</p>

<p>Although the station hadn’t deliberately publicized Xeno’s situation, the news had still spread.</p>

<p>Anyone with any status at the station had long since heard the rumors. The exact details weren’t clear, but the station’s leadership had all flown over early that morning.</p>

<p>Things were probably not looking good for Xeno. Naturally, with his competitor out of the picture, the position of deputy station director was already in Elara Ye’s pocket.</p>

<p>&quot;Sis, I’m almost at the MRT station. It’s going to take a while to get there. The roads are fucking gridlocked.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No rush. I’ll wait for you to have dinner. Just come straight home.&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye’s eyes softened. She went home, changed, and put on a pair of athletic shorts and a plain T-shirt.</p>

<p>The formidable female leader now looked like a gentle, homely housewife, barefaced yet still radiantly beautiful.</p>

<p>The roads in the provincial capital were so clogged even a dog couldn’t get through. It was nearly nine o’clock by the time Luis finally arrived.</p>

<p>Elara Ye, wearing gold-rimmed glasses, was working on her computer. She instinctively glanced at the time when she heard the door open, surprised at how much time had passed.</p>

<p>&quot;Sis, I’m starving and so hot!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis set down his luggage as soon as he walked in, looking travel-weary.</p>

<p>Elara Ye gave a gentle smile, closed her laptop, and stretched. &quot;I’m hungry too. I guess I’m just destined to be a workhorse.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I thought, since I haven’t left work on time in ages, I’d come home and relax a bit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Maybe get a foot massage or something. But as soon as my phone rang, I naturally started working again. Born to be a beast of burden.&quot;</p>

<p>She chuckled wryly, stretching as she came over and grabbed Luis’s arm, laughing, &quot;Come on, little brother, I’m starving. I want to eat something good.&quot;</p>

<p>She was clearly braless. The feeling of her breasts pressing against his arm was incredibly soft.</p>

<p>Luis gave a wicked grin. &quot;Sis, you’re going commando under that?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Duh, it’s so hot out. At work, I was sweating through my clothes. If I wore a bra at home, I’d suffocate.&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye wasn’t shy at all. Instead, she deliberately rubbed her chest against him a few times, still laughing. &quot;Little brat, is your sister’s tofu good to eat?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Are my boobs bigger, or are your wife’s?&quot;</p>

<p>She was really close to Luis now, teasing him without restraint, with a hint of that &quot;no respect for age&quot; vibe.</p>

<p>Luis grinned wickedly and said, &quot;Based on feel, my wife’s are probably bigger, but neither of you are really that big.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, so you’re being picky now, are you?&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye said coquettishly, &quot;You little brat, I bet you have plenty of women on the side.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not bad. It’s tough being this handsome. Getting seduced all the time is my biggest problem.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis made a smug face.</p>

<p>Just as they reached the ground floor, they received a call from his brother-in-law, Carter Peng.</p>

<p>Elara Ye answered a few times, then said, &quot;I just came downstairs with Luis. We haven’t had dinner yet.&quot;</p>

<p>Carter Peng’s car was already parked at the entrance of the community. With a driver, it clearly indicated his status.</p>

<p>The two got into the car, and Luis dutifully sat in the passenger seat, saying obediently, &quot;Brother-in-law!!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, I’m taking you to try some good Northwestern cuisine.&quot;</p>

<p>Carter Peng looked equally pleased, smiling as he said, &quot;Once this meeting is over, you and your sister will probably be working overtime and eating takeout again.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Since we still have time, let’s have a good drink together. Things are going to get busy in a couple of days.&quot;</p>

<p>The car stopped at a nearby halal restaurant that wasn’t lavish but was very elegant. Carter Peng had already reserved a private room. As soon as they sat down, he picked up the menu and handed it over.</p>

<p>&quot;You order, brother-in-law. You definitely know what their specialties are.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis immediately pushed it back.</p>

<p>For this young man who was practically his brother-in-law, his understanding, humility, and sense of propriety made Carter Peng like him more and more.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s have some boiled tripe, a braised pork strip, yellow mustard tripe shreds, and a boiled beef in chili sauce.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ten beef skewers, ten beef tendon skewers, ten pieces of fatty brisket...&quot;</p>

<p>After Carter Peng finished ordering, the waiter immediately brought up a large pitcher of ice-cold draft beer. Carter Peng himself opened a bottle of baijiu he’d brought.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, let’s have a drink first!!!&quot;</p>

<p>They clinked glasses. Carter Peng said cheerfully, &quot;Honey, Luis is really your lucky star. As soon as he arrived, our biggest headache just solved itself.&quot;</p>

<p>Carter Peng teased, &quot;Not only did he solve it completely, he solved it by sending someone to the hospital.&quot;</p>

<p>That’s why he’d said they’d be busy soon—it was already certain that Elara Ye was getting promoted.</p>

<p>With the support of his own family’s influence and the Ye family’s foundation, she would be a truly powerful deputy director.</p>

<p>Luis just smiled and said nothing, acting as if he knew nothing. Elara Ye, a bit excited, raised her glass and said, &quot;Right, Luis, let us siblings have another drink.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Congratulations on your promotion, sis.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis clinked glasses with her.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s all thanks to you, little brother!&quot;</p>

<p>Elara Ye was overjoyed and gave Luis a flirtatious glance.</p>

<p>They shared a knowing smile, feeling a secret thrill right in front of her husband, like two kids who’d gotten away with something.</p>
