<p>Even though Arthur was a small-scale contractor, in Logan’s eyes, he was still just a peasant.</p>

<p>Arthur similarly looked down on him. A poor teacher constantly proclaiming his literary refinement, dirt poor yet still putting on airs. What kind of virtue did he possess to be worthy of the gentle, wise, and capable Eloise?</p>

<p>Frankly, on this point, Luis strongly agreed with his cheap Father-in-law’s viewpoint.</p>

<p>Just based on the remark he made upon entering, he was lucky not to get slapped. A literati? A rural teacher, desperately poor, yet finding fault with everything. One could only say that civilized society had saved his wretched life.</p>

<p>Logan looked around and snorted, saying, &quot;What’s the purpose of coming to a place like this? You have money to burn, is that it?&quot;</p>

<p>Arriving and immediately being such a killjoy, Ruth’s temper flared up instantly. Just as she was about to erupt, her sister Eloise held her back, offering a subtle, pained smile and shaking her head slightly.</p>

<p>Although some estrangement existed between the two sisters, seeing that bitter smile made Ruth sigh and suppress her anger.</p>

<p>Logan stepped forward to inspect the table, tapped it, and said, &quot;Passable. It’s a solid marble table.&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing his pretentious demeanor, the three Wood sisters were furious. All three were seething, but seeing their aunt’s pleading look, they dared not speak up.</p>

<p>&quot;Uncle!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis followed behind him, offering a smile that didn’t reach his eyes, and said, &quot;The cultural circles are quite lofty, something we simply cannot access.</p>

<p>But if the Gestor Palace’s standard isn’t high enough, does Uncle have any better places to recommend?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Everything you say reeks of money, it’s all filthy lucre. Your awareness is far too low.&quot;</p>

<p>Logan snorted, brimming with pride, and said, &quot;I know a place nestled among mountains, waters, and bamboo forests, where every detail holds subtle delight. Spending money here just makes you seem like a vulgar, money-grubbing philistine. You’d be better off going there instead.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, next time I’ll go and buy it.&quot;</p>

<p>Logan turned back, startled, and asked, &quot;What did you say?!&quot;</p>

<p>Logan’s face instantly flushed a deep red.</p>

<p>Luis said with a laugh, &quot;I’m a vulgar man. To be perfectly honest, I rather enjoy the idea of using filthy money to insult civility.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Enough from you! Haven’t you said enough?!&quot;</p>

<p>Eloise could no longer maintain her composure. She glared angrily at Logan and said, &quot;We’re here for a meal today, and you’re picking faults and finding flaws. What exactly is your problem?&quot;</p>

<p>Logan settled down a little, sat down, snorted, and asked with a sullen expression, &quot;Where is Arthur? Why hasn’t he come?&quot;</p>

<p>Forgetting that it was his own brother-in-law, even addressing a stranger older than himself by his full name was disrespectful.</p>

<p>&quot;Auntie, why didn’t Aisha come?&quot;</p>

<p>Her aunt’s only daughter was her best playmate. A year younger than her, they always got along famously. Whenever they got together, they were like a pair of wild girls.</p>

<p>&quot;She...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Studying at home, of course. Her exam results were a complete mess. I don’t know who she inherited that pig-like brain from.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If she took after me, she would have mastered her studies long ago.&quot;</p>

<p>This sarcastic, veiled criticism was aimed at Eloise. Ruth could no longer listen quietly and lashed out,</p>

<p>&quot;less of your insinuations! With your character, you possess not a shred of true culture. You are a complete and utter failure.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Who are you calling a failure?&quot;</p>

<p>Logan’s expression changed instantly.</p>

<p>&quot;You. I’m talking about you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What a pathetic specimen! For thirty or forty years, stuck in a township school, you couldn’t even manage to become a head teacher. Those who graduated with you are probably all school principals by now.&quot;</p>

<p>This remark struck directly at his most vulnerable spot. Logan, with his extreme, literary-minded personality, was usually taciturn, but when he did speak, his mouth was exceptionally foul.</p>

<p>His cynical, misanthropic attitude hadn’t softened even at his age. While others used connections and sought opportunities to move to cities with better conditions, or at least secured minor official posts to earn higher salaries...</p>

<p>If he were truly content, genuinely principled and willing to dedicate his life to teaching and nurturing others, that would be one thing, and at least somewhat admirable.</p>

<p>In reality, he was self-righteous, loved meddling in others’ affairs, and was consistently sidelined at school. He was always left holding the bag when things went wrong, while benefits never came his way.</p>

<p>Forget about interpersonal savvy, he was too proud to lower himself and build good relationships with colleagues. He acted superior all day long, fond of lecturing others, and couldn’t even manage relationships with his own relatives, let alone anyone else.</p>

<p>&quot;That is because I am unwilling to associate with those corrupt people.&quot;</p>

<p>Logan said, his face flushing red.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, oh, it is because others look down on you. You would need their willingness even if you wanted to associate with them.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth continued with her sharp tongue, &quot;Brother in law, you really need to start saving that meager salary of yours now. Otherwise, if Aisha tests into a high school in the city, you will not even be able to afford the tuition fees.&quot;</p>

<p>Eloise quickly grabbed her sister’s hand and said, &quot;Elder Sister, I am working too, that will not happen.&quot;</p>

<p>Eloise was introverted and completely socially anxious. Finding a stable job was difficult for her, and she was currently working as a part time domestic cleaner. It was said she was already studying, preparing to take the exam to become a certified postpartum nanny.</p>

<p>&quot;That is still better than that Arthur.&quot;</p>
