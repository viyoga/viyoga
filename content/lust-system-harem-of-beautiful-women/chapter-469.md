<p>She had long harbored a preferred location for the new office. This small building housed the Bar Association, a hallowed ground within the industry, where merely hanging one’s shingle was a testament to one’s capability.</p>

<p>Right next door stood the city and district procuratorates, while across the street were the Municipal Public Security Bureau and the District Public Security Bureau... with the courthouse situated right at the intersection.</p>

<p>For opening a law firm, this location offered unparalleled geographical advantage, the absolute prime choice. No other place, no matter how cheap the rent, could compare.</p>

<p>However, apart from members of the Bar Association, the building was not open for public lease. They would rather leave floors empty than rent them out.</p>
