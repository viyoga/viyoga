<p>In the evening, Luis was woken up by a phone call and answered it somewhat puzzled.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, I dont have time to have dinner with you tonight. I have a case and need to go to the capital. You live in City Garden, right? I will be there soon. Treat me to a cup of coffee.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, Master’s Wife.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis didn’t dare delay. He immediately put on his clothes and ran downstairs.</p>

<p>At the Starbucks downstairs, Luis’s eyes lit up when Winslet walked in.</p>

<p>A gray suit skirt outlined her mature and charming curves, and her beautiful legs under the short skirt were wrapped in black stockings and stepped into mid-heels, full of temptation. Just her figure alone attracted all men’s attention as soon as she appeared.</p>

<p>Winslet wore a pair of gold-rimmed glasses, her hair naturally tied up, giving her a dignified charm. She wasn’t a peerless beauty, but she was definitely a beautiful young woman.</p>

<p>&quot;Little Luis has become so handsome.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet was also stunned when she saw Luis, but she smiled after sitting down.</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all, Master’s Wife is the most stunning and charming. I hardly dare recognize you.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis’s words weren’t just flattery. Although he had contact with his master’s wife when he was apprenticed to Fatty, Winslet was busy studying for exams at the time.</p>

<p>She wore men’s clothes every day. To say she looked unkempt might be a bit exaggerated, but she definitely didn’t wear makeup and was somewhat disheveled.</p>

<p>Back then, she wore black-framed glasses with lenses as thick as the bottom of a beer bottle, slightly twisted. Although it was obvious she had good features, she looked like a nerdy bookworm gone mad.</p>

<p>And now Winslet had transformed. With a little makeup, she had become a Legally Blonde. With a short skirt, black stockings, and gold-rimmed glasses, she was full of charm.</p>

<p>&quot;So you’re saying I was ugly before? Do you suspect I had plastic surgery?&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet looked displeased.</p>

<p>&quot;My master’s wife is naturally beautiful. How could she possibly need plastic surgery?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis joked, &quot;I was just thinking how someone like my master could possibly deserve you. Other people’s ancestral graves emit smoke, but his family’s grave must have exploded.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet’s smile stiffened slightly, as if she didn’t want to mention Fatty.</p>

<p>After taking a sip of coffee, she said softly, &quot;You little brat, you definitely wouldn’t think of me if nothing was wrong. Tell me, what difficulties have you run into?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As long as you don’t borrow money, our mother-son relationship will remain strong.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis knew she was very busy today, so he didn’t beat around the bush and explained his sister-in-law’s situation. Actually, Chloe’s situation was similar, so there was no need to go into too much detail.</p>

<p>&quot;That’s it? I know about Nova Media. Another lawyer has taken on the case.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet crossed her legs, sipped her coffee, and said with a disdainful smile, &quot;The so-called lawyer in their company is just a junior lawyer who just got his license. There are problems with how the contract was drafted.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If you run into someone stubborn, just hire a lawyer to fight them. It’ll drag on endlessly, and they won’t end up paying a dime.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis’s eyes lit up when he heard this: &quot;So this lawsuit isn’t difficult at all?&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet laughed and said, &quot;What do you think? I can just find an assistant to beat him half to death. He can only scare those who know nothing.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A lawyer’s letter is just a piece of trash. I can’t guarantee I’ll win if I sue you. There are always so many bizarre lawsuits I can’t even begin to describe them all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Master’s Wife, do you mean that if you step in, this matter can be resolved?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s not a big deal. Generally, a junior lawyer would be willing to take it. There’s no need for me to get involved.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet asked with concern, &quot;Was your wife cheated? If so, I will step in personally, and we can sue to annoy them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, no, it’s my sister-in-law and a friend of mine.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Well, well... you kid.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet narrowed her eyes and said with an ambiguous smile, &quot;I admire your maturity at such a young age the most. You’re even steadier than your master and never meddle in other people’s business.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Forget about my sister-in-law, there’s another woman. Be honest, what’s the relationship?&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing her smile like that, Luis didn’t feel comfortable hiding it, so he scratched his head and said, &quot;There’s some emotional involvement.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My precious son has grown up, gotten married, and even has a lover. He’s learned bad things too.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet frowned slightly at first, seeming very dissatisfied with Luis’s behavior. After all, she knew Luis was married and was acquainted with Lily.</p>

<p>Winslet also admired Lily very much, saying she was a model of marrying a virtuous wife. She kept praising Luis for his luck, saying even as a live-in son-in-law, having such a wife was a blessing.</p>

<p>Luis noticed she was a bit upset and immediately said timidly, &quot;Master’s wife, I wouldn’t want to deceive anyone else, but especially not you. Please don’t be angry, alright?&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Winslet’s expression softened considerably. Upon closer thought, it made sense—mainly, she was just surprised that Luis had admitted everything so readily.</p>

<p>Her husband’s apprentice wasn’t related to her by blood, yet he had always shown her immense respect. Whenever there was a holiday, if he had time, he would definitely bring gifts to visit; if not, he would at least call.</p>

<p>Thinking this, Winslet’s tone returned to its gentle warmth: &quot;I just didn’t expect you, you little rascal, to be so charming.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not really!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis scratched his head sheepishly and said, &quot;It’s mainly because Master’s Wife asked—I didn’t dare lie!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re a sensible boy after all.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet checked the time, thought for a moment, and said, &quot;Alright, tonight, bring them and the contract to my office and explain the situation clearly to my assistant.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Keep a copy of the contract. I’ll handle the specifics.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then don’t worry about this lawsuit anymore. These kinds of nuisance lawsuits are just about who can outlast whom. Once the documents are organized, I’ll send him a lawyer’s letter first.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet let out a cold, authoritative laugh: &quot;Whether the court will even accept such a sham contract is another matter. Who does he think he’s trying to intimidate?&quot;</p>
