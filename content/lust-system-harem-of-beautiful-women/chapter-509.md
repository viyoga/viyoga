<p>After washing up and changing her clothes, she went out. Although she was heading to the teahouse to play cards, it was also a gathering spot for the residents of this community, a suitable place for underground work.</p>

<p>Luis, on the other hand, waited at home for a while until the repairman arrived and replaced the door, and another group of people scraped off the paint from the entrance and repainted it white before he left.</p>

<p>He first went to the teahouse and left a few new keys with his mother-in-law, instructing the other women in the family to just get their new keys from her.</p>

<p>His mother-in-law thought for a moment and then said softly, &quot;Give one to your sister-in-law too. That way, when she comes up to help with the cleaning sometimes, it&#x27;ll be more convenient.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sure!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis revealed a wicked, lecherous smile.</p>

<p>After finishing his tasks, Luis received a call from Jian and arrived at the municipal bureau ahead of time.</p>

<p>In the reception area of the main hall, Jian brewed some tea, squinted his eyes, and smiled, &quot;The property management agreed to pay compensation. You can just tell me directly how much your loss is.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;As for this matter, it&#x27;s not a big deal. A few days in detention is just a token gesture.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had an indifferent attitude, even a bit puzzled, &quot;I just finished dealing with it, and it only cost me a little over ten thousand dollars. The money isn&#x27;t necessary. I&#x27;d be more satisfied if they were detained for a few more days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The person has also arrived. You can discuss it yourselves.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian said very directly, &quot;Luis, to be honest with you, we&#x27;re under a lot of pressure. Someone&#x27;s watching this case, so we can&#x27;t make a big deal out of it.&quot;</p>

<p>What he said was blunt enough. After all, Leok was the eldest son of the Leon family and a prominent figure in the area, so naturally, they couldn&#x27;t just mess with his wife casually.</p>

<p>At that moment, Luis looked towards the surveillance monitor. A white BMW was parked in the parking lot behind the building. Nina had mentioned that Leok drove this model.</p>

<p>Remembering how his wife had been scared into trembling, Luis felt his anger surge uncontrollably; his rage was burning, and he could barely contain himself.</p>

<p>Luis had never been a kind-hearted, pious believer. With the system&#x27;s empowerment, even more so. How could he tolerate this humiliation?</p>

<p>Just then, it was bustling outside. A group of police officers escorted in several bruised and battered guys, loudly boasting to Jian about their achievement:</p>

<p>&quot;Chief, we caught a whole nest. The quantity isn&#x27;t large, all of them are ecstasy users.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian immediately asked, &quot;How much did you find?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s loose, all on their persons. We&#x27;re going to weigh it right away. The scale got broken earlier when these guys were resisting.&quot;</p>

<p>Looking at those drug addicts, Luis&#x27;s eyes lit up with a brilliant idea—a malicious plan to make Leok&#x27;s life difficult.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Does the host wish to use the item (Time Stopper)?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Yes!&quot;</p>

<p>For an instant, the entire world seemed frozen. Everyone&#x27;s expressions, every movement, all came to a halt.</p>

<p>Not a single sound could be heard in the air. That eerie silence was more terrifying than death itself, instantly sending a chill down Luis&#x27;s spine.</p>

<p>He stood up and looked around curiously, feeling as though these flesh-and-blood people had suddenly become puppets. The effect of time stop in an instant was truly shocking.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... System reminder: This item is different from the Clone Technique.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When the Clone Technique&#x27;s effect ends, all traces disappear with it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When the Time Stopper&#x27;s effect ends, the traces left behind remain unchanged. Host, please proceed with caution.&quot;</p>

<p>Just as the system&#x27;s prompt sounded, Luis was about to reach out and pinch a policewoman&#x27;s face. The reminder startled him so much he quickly withdrew his hand.</p>

<p>That meant if he touched someone, he&#x27;d definitely leave fingerprints. If he actually acted on any wicked thoughts, he&#x27;d also leave DNA evidence. So, this was also a very dangerous thing.</p>

<p>However, things like cameras wouldn&#x27;t leave any clues, which was a slight relief. Luis thought for a moment, then ran to the nearby cleaning supply room. Pinching his clothes to avoid touching anything, he opened a drawer and sure enough, there were rubber gloves inside.</p>

<p>There was a brand new box. Surely no one would keep track of how many pairs there were or whether any had been used.</p>

<p>Luis took out a pair and put them on before returning to the hall. He went over to the group of triumphant police officers, looked back and forth among the few addicts, and picked the one whose appearance he found least objectionable.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;re in luck, kid!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis slipped his hand into the guy&#x27;s pocket and pulled out a bag containing several small red pills.</p>

<p>Thinking for a moment, Luis took a tissue, dampened it slightly, and wiped down the outside of the resealable bag. This way, his fingerprints would definitely be gone.</p>

<p>Then, based on the camera positions, he went to the back of the building. Leok&#x27;s car had just turned the corner and hadn&#x27;t found a parking spot yet.</p>

<p>The driver&#x27;s side window was open. Luis saw he didn&#x27;t even need to pick the lock. He glanced in to confirm it was Leok and opened the car door.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... System prompt: The Time Stopper&#x27;s bonus feature can only be used once, limited to one target.&quot;</p>

<p>This damn otaku&#x27;s perverted setting made Luis hesitate a bit, unsure if he should stir up extra trouble.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... This feature can only be used once. The host can decide when to activate it and when to stop it. Once stopped, it cannot be reactivated.&quot;</p>

<p>Prompted to this extent, Luis couldn&#x27;t resist anymore.</p>

<p>With a sinister smile, he took Leok&#x27;s hand, pressed it onto the resealable bag to leave fingerprints, then stuffed it into his coat pocket. Then he pinched Leok&#x27;s mouth open and tossed two of the pills inside.</p>

<p>He grabbed a bottle of water from the car and forcefully poured it down Leok&#x27;s throat. Leok was like a corpse, completely unresponsive; his eyes didn&#x27;t even blink throughout the whole process.</p>

<p>Watching movies, he always thought time stop was an awesome and cool thing. But now, Luis found it too eerie, so eerie it made his bones numb and his whole body feel uncomfortable.</p>

<p>After forcing the pills down, Luis took out a hammer he had grabbed along the way, took a deep breath to steady himself.</p>

<p>&quot;Bastard, you brought this on yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You dare mess with me? You&#x27;re going to have a long, hard time ahead.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis wasn&#x27;t soft-hearted. He raised the hammer and aimed a vicious blow at Leok&#x27;s foot.</p>

<p>His most uneasy feeling at this moment was because this blow landed accurately. Leok&#x27;s lower leg visibly fractured, the shape slightly twisted, looking horrifying.</p>

<p>But even more horrifying was that he showed no reaction at all. His facial expression didn&#x27;t change a bit, as if he were an inanimate object.</p>

<p>&quot;Damn it!&quot;</p>

<p>Enduring this atmosphere that felt worse than death, Luis gritted his teeth and broke both his legs, not even sparing his arms.</p>

<p>After finishing, he finally took a deep breath. Then, gritting his teeth, he activated the item&#x27;s bonus feature, designating Leok to experience the flow of time just like himself.</p>

<p>Leok convulsed once, then suddenly stared at the ferocious-faced Luis as if he&#x27;d seen a ghost. But before he could even speak, the intense pain shooting through his limbs made him scream uncontrollably.</p>
