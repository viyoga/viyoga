<p>&quot;At least show some consideration, even if you run over a stray cat or dog, you have to pay some compensation.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel said nonchalantly, &quot;But I said minor injuries don’t matter, what’s the point of 1000 dollars.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Right, you handled this with such class.&quot;, Ruth praised with a smile.</p>

<p>As the person involved, Luis’s face turned dark. Daniel continued in a condescending tone, &quot;Luis, get some ambition, stop loafing around all day. Do you know how hard it is to find you a job? I even had to treat people to meals and drinks.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Exactly, instead of thanking your brother-in-law properly, all you do is cause trouble.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth joined in scolding him.</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright, let’s just drop it.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy played the peacemaker, though her words were still mocking, &quot;Luis, you’re all alone with no one to rely on. Our family didn’t ask for anything and let you be a live-in son-in-law. We don’t expect you to be ambitious, but at least don’t embarrass us, understand?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Got it, Sister!&quot;</p>

<p>After enduring so many verbal blows, one more didn’t matter. Luis nodded.</p>

<p>Lily brought out some cut fruit, and Nancy immediately pulled her into the room. As the eldest sister, she probably lectured her again, acting like a mother figure.</p>

<p>&quot;Make tea for your brother-in-law. Look how much he’s worried about you, even coming to see you specially. You don’t even know how to appreciate it.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth grumbled.</p>

<p>Luis brewed the tea silently, cursing inwardly about how shameless it was to visit empty-handed.</p>

<p>Daniel was a playboy, the real idle good-for-nothing in the family.</p>

<p>His father was a school principal, and his mother was a department head at a major hospital. He was born into privilege, worked sporadically, spent money recklessly, and was the epitome of a spoiled rich kid.</p>

<p>He had pursued Nancy lavishly, and their wedding was reportedly grand. Arthur and Ruth felt their daughter had married exceptionally well.</p>

<p>Official status was unavoidable. Even though Daniel was a playboy with no real job, his family’s connections made him respectable, so the in-laws adored him.</p>

<p>After one cup of tea, Daniel got restless, &quot;Mom, let me see your hand!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth extended her hand, complaining, &quot;What’s there to see? It’s all old and wrinkly, dried up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nonsense, Mom’s hand is softer than a young girl’s, smooth as silk.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel flirtatiously held her hand, stroking it while saying, &quot;Mom, it’s a shame you don’t wear any jewelry on these hands.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth sighed dramatically, &quot;Just a few old, broken pieces. Wearing them would be embarrassing. I don’t have the money for new ones now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, I saw a beautiful bracelet last time, but someone already reserved it.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel kept fondling his mother-in-law’s hand, smiling ingratiatingly, &quot;Store-bought jewelry is mass-produced. Next time my friend’s high-end boutique has custom pieces, I’ll pick something beautiful for you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Really?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth was overjoyed but quickly feigned restraint, &quot;You just got married and have expenses. Raising kids isn’t cheap, don’t waste money.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Spending on Mom isn’t a waste.&quot;</p>

<p>Daniel fondled even more eagerly.</p>

<p>Ruth giggled like a young girl, right in front of Luis, as if they were a pair of adulterers, completely ignoring him.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Basic function unlocked successfully, displaying now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Only applicable to people you know, ineffective on strangers.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis stared dumbfounded, rubbing his eyes to confirm he wasn’t hallucinating.</p>

<p>Above Daniel’s head, a line of text appeared: Favorability 0, Contempt, Disdain.</p>

<p>Above Ruth’s head: Favorability 20% (Worse than a stranger, looks down on him from the heart.)</p>

<p>Footsteps sounded on the stairs, and the two quickly separated. Listening to the sisters’ indistinct chatter, Luis turned to look.</p>

<p>Their heads also displayed text. Nancy’s read: Favorability 50% (Treats him like a hired housekeeper, stranger-level.)</p>

<p>Lily blushed, her text reading: Favorability 75% (A stable but dull marriage, built on first love and long-term relationship, boring sexual partner.)</p>

<p>Suddenly, the text disappeared, and Luis felt slightly fatigued.</p>

<p>&quot;Display mode consumes energy. Normally hidden, but notifications will appear when favorability changes.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis thought this was fine. Otherwise, constantly staring at the numbers above their heads would make him look insane.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, what are you talking about?&quot;</p>

<p>As the eldest sister, Nancy was worldly and sweet-tongued, not only charming Ruth but also reportedly winning over her in-laws.</p>

<p>Daniel and Nancy claimed they came to see Luis but left empty-handed after eating. Ruth went out to play Cards.</p>

<p>The house fell silent. Lily finally relaxed and nestled into her husband’s arms, whispering, &quot;Honey, Daniel said Accountant jobs at Tiger Real Estate pay well after probation, and his uncle is a senior executive there who can help.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A damn Accountant made to sound like gold.&quot; Thinking of how Ruth had beamed while Daniel fondled her hand, Luis felt bitter, &quot;He’s the one loafing around, where does he get off lecturing me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Always showing off, making empty promises, and Mom’s dumb enough to keep falling for it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just ignore her, honey. She’s always been like that.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily snuggled closer, saying gently, &quot;Maybe we can talk to Mom again about moving out to rent our own place.&quot;</p>

<p>Her eyes sparkled with hope for a couple’s private life.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t bring it up. She’ll just scold us for wasting money.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis knew Ruth’s double standards too well. Besides, with the system’s tasks, staying close was the smarter move. Only a fool would move out now.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m used to it, but I feel bad for you.&quot;, Lily touched Luis’s face.</p>

<p>Luis felt dazed. Her body felt soft and full, no longer the bony frame from before.</p>

<p>After a day of adjustment, seeing her lovely face and smelling her faint fragrance, Luis’s desire flared. He gazed at her intently.</p>

<p>Lily lowered her head shyly, &quot;What? Is there something on my face?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Just a bit of beauty.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis lifted her chin gently and kissed her.</p>
