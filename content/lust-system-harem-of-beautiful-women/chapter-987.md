<p>Today this table’s food cost was 2000, and twenty tables was forty thousand. According to the rules here, you first gave half as a deposit, and after confirming the dishes were fine you gave the other half.</p>

<p>The village chef expressed that he was in a difficult position, because Arthur had fallen asleep on the sofa. In this situation, generally you would ask for it later.</p>

<p>Luis directly gave the remaining twenty thousand and then said, &quot;Big brother, please clean up a bit more thoroughly.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Definitely, definitely!&quot;</p>

<p>The host family being so straightforward also made him happy. He immediately called people to clean up more efficiently.</p>

<p>They were busy packing up their things. Luis walked back to the living room and saw that the tables and chairs had already been removed. The floor had clearly been swept and even mopped, very clean.</p>

<p>Grandma Wood was originally used to sleeping and rising early. Today she had drunk with her old best friends and had long since gone back to her room to rest.</p>

<p>Tonight the only one staying behind was Ruth. What thoughts she was holding, Luis was very clear about. So Luis also naturally stayed behind.</p>

<p>After all, his father-in-law was drunk. There always had to be a man staying behind to handle things, right? This excuse was incomparably proper and aboveboard.</p>

<p>Aside from that, Wesley had asked for a relatively long leave. Cecilia was three months younger than Leah, and this year she had also just finished exams and was on vacation. They had both stayed behind as well.</p>

<p>But this pair of siblings was relatively reserved. They had tucked themselves back into their room before the banquet even ended. Who knew whether they were asleep or not.</p>

<p>Luis had been in the courtyard the whole time, watching them put the tables and chairs into the car and, after cleaning up, rinse the courtyard with water.</p>

<p>Only after the village banquet team left did he have the designated driver bring the Benz C and the pickup truck into the courtyard and park them properly.</p>

<p>The lively day had completely ended. At this time it was about eleven o’clock.</p>

<p>Only then did Luis walk back to the living room. Inside the living room, his father-in-law Arthur was lying on the sofa, snoring loud enough to shake the heavens, just like a tractor running.</p>

<p>&quot;Arthur, damn, drunk like a dead pig.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth pushed him a few times and could not help cursing, &quot;How exactly do you two sleep? This snoring, even a ghost would not be able to rest in peace.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He does not snore when he has not been drinking. Generally after drinking he just sleeps in the living room by himself.&quot;</p>

<p>When Suzanne spoke, her tongue was also a bit thick.</p>

<p>Any normal person could tell that this was being drunk but not completely passed out. She was in a state of relatively slow reactions, just a red line away from completely collapsing drunk.</p>

<p>The door was already locked. The old rural house was very quiet. Ruth said impatiently, &quot;Get a blanket and pillow and just toss him here. He has no sense of shame at all, drinking himself into this state.&quot;</p>

<p>When Suzanne heard this she hesitated slightly. Luis walked forward and said softly, &quot;Mom, sleeping outside is uncomfortable. I will just get him back to the room.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth asked with concern, &quot;You also drank quite a bit tonight. Are you okay?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with a laugh, &quot;It is fine, at least he is not drunk yet!!!&quot;</p>

<p>As he spoke, Luis stretched lazily and loosened up his muscles and bones. He stepped forward and looked at his father-in-law, who was already drunk like a dead pig. With one scoop of his arm and a burst of strength, he steadily hoisted him onto his shoulder.</p>

<p>Why hoist him? Because carrying a man in a princess hold is pretty disgusting. At least that was how it was for Luis.</p>

<p>On the right side was the hallway. At the end of the hallway was the elderly room where Grandma Wood stayed. Although there was only one master bedroom, the first room they stayed in was also not bad.</p>

<p>After putting Arthur on the bed, Luis panted from exhaustion. Under normal circumstances it was fine, but after drinking so much he was a bit strained.</p>

<p>Just after putting him down, he took a few more bottles of mineral water and placed them on the bedside table. Suzanne said, moved, &quot;No wonder people always say your wife is blessed. Among men there are not many as careful as you.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis only gave a slow, gentle smile and said nothing. Ruth stood at the doorway of the room and said, &quot;I have no interest in serving him.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Suzanne, after you finish washing up, come over for a bit. I still have some things I want to talk to you about.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying this, his mother-in-law turned and left without looking back.</p>

<p>Suzanne was stunned for a moment. By the time she came back to her senses, Ruth had already left. Luis immediately stepped forward to help take off Arthur’s clothes.</p>

<p>For someone dead drunk like mud, generally when you move him around his body feels very heavy. If Luis had not been there, she definitely could not have managed alone.</p>

<p>Suzanne revealed a grateful expression and said, &quot;Thank you!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing that her drunkenness was already very deep, Luis said with concern, &quot;Auntie Suzanne, you are also very tired. I do not know what my mom wants to talk to you about. How about I go tell her to wait until tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, no. Ruth already spoke up, I definitely have to go.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My head is a bit dizzy. I will wash up a little and then go over.&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne thanked him warmly again. Luis smiled and turned to leave. She also closed the door with a face full of wine flush.</p>

<p>Luis went straight over to the master bedroom. His mother-in-law Ruth was also getting clothes to change into. Being covered in sweat and the smell of alcohol was very uncomfortable.</p>
