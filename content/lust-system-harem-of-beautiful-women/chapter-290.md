<p>Ye gave him an approving look, thought for a moment, placed a hand on his shoulder, and said very seriously, &quot;That boy is called Luis Bartolome.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, his name is Luis Bartolome!&quot;</p>

<p>Waller responded immediately.</p>

<p>Ye had no intention of hiding anything from this trusted confidant of his from the moment he called him in to listen to the call.</p>

<p>He patted Waller on the shoulder, walked back to his original spot, picked up the cheap cigarettes Luis had left behind, took one out, lit it, and slowly smoked it.</p>

<p>Waller was somewhat taken aback. His boss had quit smoking for nearly ten years, but at this moment, he truly did not dare to speak up and stop him.</p>

<p>&quot;So cheap, he’ll inevitably develop problems from this sooner or later.&quot;</p>

<p>After coughing a few times, Ye stubbed out the cigarette. After pondering for a moment, he spoke unusually frankly, &quot;Waller, you served as my secretary for over ten years previously, correct.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes!&quot;</p>

<p>Waller replied calmly, without any emotional fluctuation.</p>

<p>But he understood clearly in his heart that as a deputy mayor, one of over a dozen deputy mayors, he was one who wielded substantial real power.</p>

<p>It was not because his abilities were exceptional. In fact, every one of them was exceptional. The primary reason was that he came from the Sebastian Family and had once been Ye’s trusted key general.</p>

<p>&quot;Remember that boy from earlier.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I have remembered him!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;His name is Luis...&quot;</p>

<p>After hesitating for at least several seconds, Ye still said lightly, &quot;But in his blood, he carries the surname Sebastian.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, I understand.&quot;</p>

<p>As an absolute elite, Waller would not engage in superfluous words or gossip. For him, it was completely unnecessary.</p>

<p>Ye felt so helpless. The old man had instructed him to take care of everything. This matter had now escalated completely to the highest level.</p>

<p>To put it bluntly, if anything were to happen, it wouldn’t just be Ye who would have his hide.</p>

<p>&quot;A young man, harboring heavy resentment, his personality might also be somewhat distorted.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye thought for a long time, then suddenly turned and grabbed Waller by the collar. Given Waller’s current status, this action was somewhat abrupt, but Waller was particularly pleased, knowing this was a behavior that only occurred with great intimacy.</p>

<p>&quot;I will be leaving Bathek City this afternoon. Work arrangements cannot be delayed. My father was right. Until the appointment is officially announced, even if it’s ninety percent certain, we must not act frivolously.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye’s face was utterly serious as he said hoarsely, &quot;This child, I am entrusting him to you. If there is any need, inform me at the first moment.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;President Ye, please rest assured!&quot;</p>

<p>Waller’s expression was serious, utterly solemn.</p>

<p>Now that he was in a position of authority, he would naturally handle official matters with strict seriousness, but he understood even more clearly that a private entrustment like this carried even greater weight. One could say this was the first time Ye had ever asked him to handle a private matter.</p>

<p>Wiping away his tears, Luis ran extremely fast, thinking to himself that his acting skills should pass muster.</p>

<p>Having lost four blind boxes all at once, Luis felt his heart bleeding with pain, wondering why he had to act on such a sudden impulse.</p>

<p>He had just arrived home when he received a call from an unfamiliar number.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, hello, I am Waller!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don’t know you!&quot;</p>

<p>Was some vengeful spirit relentlessly hounding him?</p>

<p>Luis broke into a cold sweat upon hearing the voice. After a moment’s thought, he realized it was that deputy mayor.</p>

<p>The person on the other end spoke succinctly and efficiently, &quot;President Ye’s work is hectic. He will return to the capital this afternoon and won’t have time to come over during this period.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Remember my number. You can tell me about anything. I have already instructed the Taiga and Leon families. No one is allowed to contact you privately or inquire about anything.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If they harass you, remember to tell me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay!&quot;</p>

<p>After hanging up, Luis let out a long sigh of relief. He lay down on the sofa to rest his mind, when suddenly the System notification sound indicated an update.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Dungeon quest reset complete.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Based on big data detection, this dungeon’s difficulty is extremely high, classified as Hell level. It is the first task issued by the System.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The quest has been upgraded from a secondary storyline dungeon to a main quest.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Luis was completely stunned. He could imagine the quest resetting successfully, but why had a dungeon suddenly been upgraded to a main quest?</p>

<p>It was important to know that currently only the Wood family mother and her four daughters were the main quest. Other quest dungeons were basically extensions stemming from these main storylines, like Avery.</p>

<p>Yana’s &quot;Charming Widow’s Spring&quot; was derived from the Mother In Law storyline. Later, through a strange twist of fate, he also ended up with her daughter, Maria. This was an absolutely top tier mother daughter pair.</p>

<p>Even such a top tier pair remained merely a dungeon. The fact that no quest had been issued yet showed the System did not prioritize it.</p>

<p>Bella was indeed an enchanting and beautiful young woman, absolutely the kind of stunner men dream of. But judging by the relationship, why should her dungeon quest be directly promoted to the main quest?</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Main quest upgraded successfully.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Quest 1, Human Wrench. Please provide training for Bella’s first kiss with a man. Quest reward, 1 attribute point.&quot;</p>

<p>Upon the release of this quest, Luis was utterly dumbfounded. Human Wrench, what did that mean.</p>

<p>The System quickly provided a prompt, &quot;Based on big data analysis, Bella and Dominic’s marriage is only a superficial alliance, a false marital relationship.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;During her time studying abroad, Bella was a famous promoter and supporter of the legalization of homosexuality, and moreover a radical one. She herself was also promiscuous and fickle, changing many girlfriends. Currently, she is in an improper relationship with her secretary.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Rococo is her daughter???&quot;</p>

<p>Luis’s mind instantly became chaotic. He couldn’t help but ask, &quot;How come she doesn’t have the Taiga surname? I mean, she is married to Dominic after all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;According to data investigation, Rococo is a test tube baby born to Bella abroad at high cost. She is of mixed race and has no blood relationship with Dominic.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis cursed inwardly. According to the other information provided by the System, this was indeed a tough nut to crack.</p>

<p>She wasn’t bisexual like his wife. Bella was a thoroughgoing homosexual, a man hater.</p>

<p>And after all, she was among the radicals.</p>

<p>No wonder the difficulty shot straight up, directly turning the dungeon into a main quest. Daringly, the System had gathered sufficient data and, upon analysis, also found this woman particularly difficult to handle.</p>

<p>Ambitious, radical, and also irritable, she was simply a hysterical freak.</p>

<p>Luis wanted to put this task aside for now, because he felt the chances of success were far too low.</p>

<p>As evening fell, his wife called. She and her Mother In Law, Ruth, were going to the capital to participate in a medical and nursing training program.</p>
