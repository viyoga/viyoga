<p>&quot;Just give Big Sister’s name and phone number, and that’s it??&quot;</p>

<p>Ariana asked with a hint of confusion.</p>

<p>Luis had given each of them a full five thousand dollars in pocket money. Even if they spent lavishly, there should be some left over, so actually getting a room to rest by herself would have been no problem at all.</p>

<p>However, this little brat felt too embarrassed to abandon her friends, and with so many people, renting just one room would be way too humiliating. So, while she was thinking it over, she decided to cater to their preferences.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, just go straight there!&quot;</p>

<p>The rain was pouring heavily, showing no sign of stopping at all. Only after checking the news did they realize there was an orange alert. The whole way there, Luis drove with intense concentration, not daring to get distracted and take any liberties.</p>

<p>Following the address given by his little cousin, they arrived at the Ocean Sunshine Hotel. Honestly, it looked rather ordinary and had nothing to do with being luxurious.</p>

<p>It was not some four or five star place, just a ferryman’s hotel built by the seaside, pretty much on the same level as a budget hotel.</p>

<p>&quot;Here already!&quot;</p>

<p>He knocked on the door, and his cousin Ariana opened it wearing the hotel’s bathrobe.</p>

<p>With her petite and adorable figure, the robe wrapped her up especially tightly and completely. A woman with mature charm or a tall, statuesque beauty might have been able to pull off a sexy look in it.</p>

<p>When a cute maiden girl like her wore it, it did look playful and charming, but because it covered her up so thoroughly, there was not even a trace of alluring temptation or any hint of exposed spring scenery.</p>

<p>Seeing Luis, she revealed a sweet and charming smile, but when she noticed Leah behind him, her expression turned a bit panicked.</p>

<p>Under Luis’s mediation, the relationship between the two sisters had already been repaired, and she no longer brought up the little things Leah had done to her before.</p>

<p>What panicked her now was that, in front of her friends, she had been saying Luis was her boyfriend. If this older cousin came along and exposed the truth, she would lose face completely.</p>

<p>Luis saw through her thoughts at a glance. Leah giggled teasingly, &quot;Guoguo, don’t worry, Brother in law already gave me instructions, so of course I won’t ruin your act.&quot;</p>

<p>After saying this, she gave Luis a push that was soft and lacked any real strength.</p>

<p>However, Luis took the opportunity to wrap his arms around Ariana. His little cousin, who had just finished bathing, gave off a milky fragrance, radiating an overwhelmingly full allure.</p>

<p>&quot;Mmm... you’d better keep your mouth shut tight.&quot;</p>

<p>Ariana’s face flushed a deep red. She did not struggle, but at that moment she still felt very guilty.</p>

<p>After all, Leah and Lily were biological sisters, so of course she was worried that this little girl might let something slip accidentally.</p>

<p>Losing face in front of her friends was one thing, but if the elders found out, she would definitely be scolded to death. That would be a real disaster.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t worry!&quot;</p>

<p>Closing the door, the room was a sea view suite with huge floor to ceiling glass windows, a living room, and a bedroom with a large bed.</p>

<p>Inside the bathroom, a young girl was taking a shower. She was the friend whose birthday they were celebrating, called Koeh. Luis had seen her before, and her face was entirely covered with acne, making her completely uninteresting.</p>

<p>These hotel rooms were usually very quiet during weekdays, but became extremely expensive during weekends and holidays. The young girls were actually quite unfamiliar with this kind of environment.</p>

<p>As soon as Luis arrived, he considerately told them to gather up all their wet clothes and then called hotel service to have them taken away for dry cleaning.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother Bin, here already!&quot;</p>

<p>Not long after he sat down, the girl called Koeh came out and gave an ambiguous smile, &quot;Guoguo, your boyfriend’s come to pick you up. Are you staying here tonight or leaving with him?&quot;</p>

<p>Ariana’s face turned red as she said, &quot;You nosy little bitches, why do you care so much? Having a free room to stay in should be enough to make you secretly happy.&quot;</p>

<p>In their group, there were two guys and eight girls... the two guys braved the rain and went back, and one of the girls, whose family was very strict, was picked up by her parents braving the rain as well.</p>

<p>At that moment, Ariana was also very generous. In one go, she reserved all six of the hotel’s best sea view rooms.</p>

<p>Ariana felt a little guilty after saying that... after all, it was Saturday, and the sea view rooms were eight hundred and eighty dollars a night...</p>

<p>She had suddenly spent over five thousand dollars in one go. For their family backgrounds and ages, this was excessively luxurious, bordering on sheer wastefulness.</p>

<p>Leah didn’t say anything and pretended not to hear, even secretly telling her she would keep it a secret from her sister.</p>

<p>Hearing this, Luis smiled gently, &quot;As expected, my Guoguo has good taste. Sea view rooms at least make the stay more comfortable.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The other small rooms are definitely not great, and the air circulation isn’t good either. If you’ve already gone for it, you shouldn’t save money on something like this.&quot;</p>

<p>These words made Ariana feel incredibly proud in front of her friends, and she cuddled up intimately in Luis’s arms.</p>

<p>&quot;My dad said my little aunt is in the hospital, so tonight they’re staying there to look after her.&quot;</p>

<p>Ariana said somewhat sheepishly, &quot;I told them I’m with Leah, so tonight I’ll stay at your place.&quot;</p>

<p>After sitting for just a brief while, they all picked up their phones and started chatting on the Whatsapp group.</p>

<p>The other girls had finished showering and came over, wearing their bathrobes with a sense of novelty.</p>

<p>They chatted excitedly and curiously, all mentioning that the baths were still filling with water in their rooms, just waiting for the tubs to be full so they could take bubble baths and things like that. Sure enough, young girls really love this kind of thing.</p>

<p>A crowd of young girls wearing bathrobes, Cedric stood out as completely out of place, and Leah, who was wearing a dress, was the same.</p>

<p>Among this group of young girls, the prettiest was still Leah, followed closely by Ariana...</p>

<p>As for the others, they were just average looking. Some were dark and skinny... to be honest, Luis would not even glance at them, acting like a perfect gentleman.</p>

<p>Leah quietly reminded him, &quot;Brother in law hasn’t eaten yet!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We haven’t either... we had just gotten the fire going when it started raining, and now my stomach is growling too.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis immediately called the front desk to ask. Of course, the hotel itself had no dining services, but since many tourists came here, there were plenty of seafood restaurants nearby. Right next door was a famously good seafood food stall.</p>

<p>The ingredients were plentiful and fresh, and the cooking was delicious. The downside was the high price, but the wealthy flocked to it eagerly.</p>

<p>The location was a bit remote, so being able to eat after ten o’clock was already great.</p>

<p>Luis then led them all downstairs. Walking out in the hotel bathrobes, for some reason, they still felt a sense of novelty and vanity.</p>

<p>From the hotel’s rear fire escape, they could walk straight over without getting rained on at all...</p>

<p>The seafood food stall never had a menu, and the ordering area was right next to the holding tanks.</p>

<p>As soon as they walked up, the young girls all became nervous because there were many kinds of seafood that, even living by the sea, they had never tasted.</p>

<p>Black gold abalone, mantis shrimp, king crab... just looking at the prices was suffocating. Many were things they had only heard of but never eaten.</p>

<p>The good thing was that prices were clearly marked. What frightened them was that the prices were indeed twenty to thirty percent higher than what they usually saw.</p>

<p>The upside was that the food could be delivered to the hotel, and the staff would come back to collect the dishes the next day...</p>

<p>The place itself was spacious, but the environment was very simple and rustic. Hearing this, Ariana’s eyes lit up, &quot;In Mina’s room, there’s a big dining table right in front of the sea view window. Then we can just go eat there.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, we’ll reassign the rooms in a bit!&quot;</p>

<p>Koeh held her hand and smiled ambiguously, &quot;Don’t worry, we won’t disturb you and your boyfriend getting close tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Get lost, do I need you worrying about that!!&quot;</p>

<p>In front of them, Ariana indulged her nature wildly and without restraint, acting especially outgoing.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s do the king crab stir fried with typhoon shelter style. For this green mud crab, just stir fry it directly with scallions and ginger...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;One sashimi platter... lobster... make it with cheese, girls tend to like it better.&quot;</p>

<p>Actually, these are all pretty common selections. The truly good stuff are the wild types of groupers, the stonefish...</p>

<p>Especially the species that live around coral reefs, ones that cannot be caught by net but only by hand line. Those kinds of seafood are the true delicacies when eaten.</p>

<p>But well, the young girls don’t understand how to appreciate those. Simply satisfying their vanity is enough.</p>

<p>&quot;Grill one order of oysters, one of green mussels... one order of eel kabayaki as well...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;One order of spicy stir fried snowflake beef tenderloin, fragrant fried pork ribs... and one order of chicken knee cartilage!!&quot;</p>

<p>After ordering over ten dishes, Ariana felt immensely proud and quickly tried to stop him, &quot;That’s enough, Sis... Brother Bin, too much will be wasted if we can’t finish it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, how wasteful would it be if we can’t finish it all!&quot;</p>

<p>The others chattered noisily as well, clearly all very hungry and full of anticipation.</p>
