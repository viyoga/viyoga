<p>After a good heart-to-heart with his sister-in-law, he’d managed to temporarily defuse what could have been a major storm.</p>

<p>Mainly because she was young and easy to appease. Plus, she herself had an ambiguous relationship with her brother-in-law, she was in no position to judge her mother.</p>

<p>And her biggest fear was getting caught by her sister. Aside from that, there wasn’t much to be angry about.</p>

<p>After smoothing things over with her, Luis drove her back to school and let out a long sigh of relief.</p>

<p>His sister-in-law’s ultimate mission hadn’t generated yet, so for now he couldn’t claim her, which was a bit frustrating.</p>

<p>After dropping her off, Luis drove straight to City First Hospital. In the ground-floor annex, the cafeteria was being painted and fitted with air conditioning. It would be ready for use soon.</p>

<p>&quot;Dad, Aunt Lin, Big Sister!&quot;</p>

<p>In the office, the atmosphere was warm and lively. Hela and Arthur, the odd in-laws, were chatting amicably, with Nancy flitting around like a social butterfly. The mood was exceptionally harmonious.</p>

<p>Brook stood up and said gently, &quot;Senior, here’s the draft contract. The hospital’s legal department has reviewed it, no issues.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If there are no problems, let’s sign it quickly and go eat.&quot;</p>

<p>At Hela’s urging, Luis’s food company was officially registered. But thinking it over, when would he have time to manage it?</p>

<p>His father-in-law Arthur had just retired, so Luis figured he could take over. Earlier, he’d given Nancy five million dollars to top up their savings to twenty million.</p>

<p>The nearly five-hundred-square-meter cafeteria at City First Hospital, including the small shop, had an annual contract fee of one hundred twenty thousand dollars, quite cheap.</p>

<p>Nancy had put in two million dollars, covering renovations and utilities as start-up capital.</p>

<p>In the company’s share distribution, Luis gave Arthur 20%, with him responsible for management and operations, plus a fixed monthly salary. This was definitely a way to honor him.</p>

<p>After Brook and Chloe confirmed the contract details, signing made it official.</p>

<p>In fact, even before the contract was signed, Arthur had already sent workers in. This was essentially a done deal, the signing was just a formality.</p>

<p>After the formalities, Hela shook her head. &quot;I won’t join for dinner. I’ve got several important surgeries tonight, and I need to wait for the results.&quot;</p>

<p>Since she wasn’t going, Chloe naturally declined too. Brook said sweetly, &quot;Senior, I have to get back and file my report. I’ve got documents to organize tonight, I’m busy today.&quot;</p>

<p>So much for dinner plans.</p>

<p>Arthur said a shipment of materials was arriving that he needed to receive. He wasn’t comfortable leaving, so he’d stay and watch over things.</p>

<p>Old Arthur was quite traditional and took his work responsibilities seriously.</p>

<p>What made him happiest was that after his construction company closed, he could use the useful materials from storage for the ancestral home renovation. No need to throw anything away.</p>

<p>Even the strange furniture and artistic decorations could be repurposed there, a complete and very cost-effective recycling.</p>

<p>&quot;Big Sister, what a coincidence today. Let’s find a nice place to relax tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was driving, with Luis in the passenger seat. His hand was already caressing her fair, round ass, unable to resist.</p>

<p>His fairy-like sister-in-law was dressed tantalizingly, a miniskirt paired with black stockings. The look was dangerously seductive, impossible not to react to.</p>

<p>She hadn’t used to dress like this, but ever since their footjob session, she’d assumed her brother-in-law liked it and had kept up the style.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t mess around, I’m taking you somewhere!!&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy slapped away Luis’s sneaky hand and said with a coquettish glare, &quot;Perfect timing, all your old flames are there. I’m going to drop you right into a hellish love triangle and see how you handle it tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Old flames???&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was a bit confused, though he had a vague idea who she meant.</p>

<p>The Benz G-Wagon drove all the way back to their community. Right next to Hans’s Bar stood a high-end office building.</p>

<p>Each floor was over two hundred square meters, housing an independent company.</p>

<p>Following her up to the 17th floor, the sign on the door read &quot;Three Beauties Media.&quot;</p>

<p>Three Beauties, one might think of three-colored cats, but it actually stood for three campus beauties.</p>

<p>In the office area, over a dozen people were busy at their computers. They glanced up to greet them briefly before returning to work.</p>

<p>Further down the hallway were fitting rooms, changing rooms, a prop room, and two or three separate offices.</p>

<p>&quot;Deewa, how’s the campaign plan coming along?&quot;</p>

<p>In the general manager’s office, the alluring beauty Deewa was hunched over her computer, looking tired.</p>

<p>&quot;No good, dammit. The drafts these people submitted treat everyone like idiots.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa stretched hard and said in frustration, &quot;A lot of it is just recycled content from the internet, I’ve seen several of them two years ago. Unbelievable.&quot;</p>

<p>Only then did she notice Luis standing behind her with a grin. She looked a bit guilty and said, &quot;Why did you bring him here???&quot;</p>

<p>After all, Luis was a married man and Nancy’s brother-in-law. That relationship always made things feel awkward.</p>

<p>&quot;The boss spent so much money starting this company, of course he should come see it.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy checked the time. &quot;Chloe’s wrapping up her shoot too. Perfect timing, we can all have dinner together and fill him in on the situation.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, then wait a moment...&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa stepped out, announced it was closing time, and told the staff to pack up and head downstairs for a team dinner.</p>

<p>The group went downstairs, where the impeccably dressed Chloe was already waiting.</p>

<p>She gave a soft, charming smile and approached, but none of the three women acted close to Luis. They all kept a slightly awkward distance.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Side mission: ’The Campus Beauties’ Feast.’&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Host is required to sleep with all three of them in the same night. Threesome format is not specified.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Each must be creampied once for mission completion. Reward: one random blind box.&quot;</p>

<p>The Campus Beauties’ Feast mission was now active.</p>

<p>The group made their way to a nearby Cantonese seafood restaurant. The four of them plus over a dozen staff filled two tables, packed together in one private room.</p>

<p>&quot;You order. Make both tables the same.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It’s our first team dinner, don’t be too cheap.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa put down her bag, stretched, and tossed the menu to Nancy.</p>

<p>Nancy rolled her eyes and tossed it to Luis. &quot;You order. You’re paying for this meal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Steamed Boston lobster, ginger and scallion stir-fried bread crab, black pepper snowflake beef cubes, braised chicken, and roast goose, one each.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A cold platter combo, braised beef brisket, steamed leopard coral grouper, garlic stir-fried water spinach, and snow peas with shrimp.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For soup, one double-boiled abalone with morel mushroom per person, and for the big pot, black chicken ginseng soup.&quot;</p>

<p>With eighteen people at the table, Luis didn’t hold back and ordered all the signature dishes.</p>

<p>As for drinks, they could have whatever they liked. Nancy, ever refined, had brought several bottles of red wine.</p>

<p>As soon as the wine was opened, everyone poured themselves a glass without being asked. It seemed work stress had been building up and they all needed to unwind.</p>

<p>Nancy raised her wine glass and said, &quot;Everyone, let me give a proper introduction, this is our big boss, Luis.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You should cherish this chance to meet him. This guy is a lazy good-for-nothing who never shows up. After tonight, you probably won’t see him again.&quot;</p>

<p>That got a round of laughter. With her egging them on, everyone downed their first drink before the food even arrived.</p>

<p>The dishes came out one after another. Seeing how easygoing Luis was, the tension eased and everyone started chatting about work.</p>

<p>&quot;Chloe, I never would have guessed you were so domestic.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe had been washing Luis’s utensils, pouring his wine, and even found an ashtray for him, which immediately drew teasing comments.</p>

<p>But she’d been a wife and mother before. Her skin wasn’t as thin as her delicate appearance suggested.</p>

<p>In response to Deewa’s teasing, Chloe shot her a seductive glare and said, &quot;Some people are just lazy and like to mock others for being diligent. That’s not a good habit, you know.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Fine, fine, you’re the diligent one, okay?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy laughed. &quot;Alright, let’s give him the rundown.&quot;</p>

<p>Over food and drinks, the three of them took turns explaining the company’s situation.</p>

<p>After Anglo was taken out, a gap had opened in the local self-media and marketing account market. And since Bathek City wasn’t exactly a hub for this sort of thing, it represented a promising opportunity.</p>

<p>Their arch-nemesis, the gossipy &quot;green tea bitch&quot; Bubbles, who was always spreading rumors, was herself an influencer.</p>

<p>Seizing the opportunity, she’d rounded up her connections, sold everything including her house, and started her own company, aiming to claim the top spot.</p>

<p>According to TIktok’s future &quot;One City, One IP&quot; plan, each city would support one signature influencer.</p>

<p>There would be not only traffic support but also tiered rewards, quite generous.</p>

<p>Her ambitions were huge; she was gunning for that top spot.</p>
