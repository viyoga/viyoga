<p>&quot;Mr. Leok, please stay in the hospital and behave yourself!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis grinned menacingly as he held the hammer, saying, &quot;People who come looking for my trouble never have a good ending.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You bastard... what did you do to me?!&quot;</p>

<p>Before Leok could continue screaming, Luis froze his time once again. At that moment, Leok&#x27;s face had turned a deathly pale, somewhat twisted in agony.</p>

<p>Luis didn&#x27;t have the mindset to overthink things; the feeling of absolute silence throughout the entire world was just too terrifying.</p>

<p>He immediately stepped forward, straightened Leok&#x27;s body, gripped the steering wheel with both hands and cranked it all the way to the right, then stomped on the accelerator pedal with his foot, flooring it.</p>

<p>The car was started, but even with the pedal floored, the vehicle didn&#x27;t move. Time hadn&#x27;t just stopped for living things; the motion of these inanimate objects was frozen too, which was especially frightening.</p>

<p>In a situation like this, who would have the heart to do anything mischievous? You couldn&#x27;t even make a woman wet no matter how you touched her, so this actually wasn&#x27;t evil at all.</p>

<p>Once he was sure everything was set, unable to bear the atmosphere any longer, Luis quickly washed the hammer, slipped back, and put everything away in the cleaning room.</p>

<p>Everything inside the building remained exactly as it was; when he checked the time, only fifteen minutes had passed.</p>

<p>There was still time left, but Luis couldn&#x27;t stand the eerie vibe. After checking to make sure he hadn&#x27;t left any traces, he immediately sat back down in his original spot across from Jian.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... 13 minutes and 44 seconds remaining. Do you wish to deactivate it early?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Deactivate!&quot;</p>

<p>Without a moment&#x27;s hesitation, Luis canceled the effect of the event stopper. Whoosh, the surrounding noise came flooding back, and at that moment, he felt as if he had been reborn.</p>

<p>This process had been utterly tormenting, each day feeling like a year, a living death. When you&#x27;re the only living person in the entire world, the suffocating sensation nearly drove Luis insane.</p>

<p>Luis had just breathed a huge sigh of relief when a loud bang came from behind him.</p>

<p>The people inside the building were terrified. Jian and the other police officers immediately got up and rushed toward the back door, and Luis followed along, eager to see the commotion.</p>

<p>The backyard of the building was the unit&#x27;s parking lot. At that moment, Leok&#x27;s BMW had crashed into two police cars parked by the side gate, the impact flipping the hood up and sending white smoke billowing out.</p>

<p>Not only was the entire front end of the car crumpled, but the wheels had twisted off and flown out, showing just how violent the collision had been.</p>

<p>Those two police cars were also smashed up. Luis had to admit that imported BMW was really something; the engine had plenty of power and started off so ferocious. In such a short distance, most cars wouldn&#x27;t have achieved this effect even with the pedal floored.</p>

<p>&quot;Help...&quot;</p>

<p>From the driver&#x27;s seat, Leok screamed in agony, his face covered in blood, the airbag having deployed.</p>

<p>The front driver&#x27;s area was twisted and deformed, in a state of compression. Judging by how his body was contorted, he had probably broken a few ribs too.</p>

<p>&quot;Save him!!&quot;</p>

<p>Under the direction of a leader, experienced police officers immediately rushed forward, pried open the deformed driver&#x27;s cab, and pulled the bloodied and mangled Leok out.</p>

<p>Leok seemed to be out of immediate danger. After being carried out, he immediately pointed at Luis, his face full of terror, and shouted, &quot;Arrest him! Arrest him right now!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why??&quot;</p>

<p>Jian and a few people who looked like officials were completely baffled, and everyone else had no idea what Leok was talking about either.</p>

<p>Leok, his face twisted in horror, said, &quot;It was him... he used some kind of black magic to secretly break my hands and legs! As soon as I woke up, the car crashed.&quot;</p>

<p>This statement was somewhat incoherent, sounding particularly strange, especially the mention of &quot;black magic,&quot; which just didn&#x27;t seem right.</p>

<p>An ambulance arrived quickly, and two police officers accompanied him to the hospital. Since the accident happened within the bureau and was quite serious, they naturally had to investigate the cause.</p>

<p>Luis was also taken to the meeting room. Jian, standing by, said with a helpless chuckle, &quot;Leaders, that guy is talking nonsense. If you don&#x27;t believe me, ask the people in the lobby.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We were both sitting right there drinking tea the whole time and only went out when we heard the crash.&quot;</p>

<p>The constant flow of people in the lobby could all testify that Luis had never left, and the surveillance footage showed him sitting there the entire time.</p>

<p>So why would Leok say such crazy things? Everyone was utterly confused.</p>

<p>Soon, the police officers who had accompanied him brought news: &quot;The doctor said his state is too agitated, it&#x27;s abnormal!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Furthermore, with injuries that severe, he should be in excruciating pain. The painkillers had very little effect, so they suggested we run tests.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Suspected prohibited substances were found on him. We&#x27;ve already drawn his blood for analysis.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The person involved... was driving under the influence!!&quot;</p>

<p>Upon hearing this, Jian cursed, &quot;That drug addict! He was so high he was hallucinating.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No wonder, talking about black magic and all. Sigh, these people really are a destabilizing factor in society.&quot;</p>

<p>His innocence proven, Luis smiled wryly and said, &quot;Looks like I made a wasted trip.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian said, &quot;I&#x27;ll handle the follow-up. If anything comes up, I&#x27;ll let you know.&quot;</p>

<p>The circumstances of Leok&#x27;s car accident were so bizarre that the news naturally couldn&#x27;t be concealed. At this critical moment during the asset liquidation of Tiger Real Estate, the eldest son of the Leon family meeting with such an accident definitely brought mixed feelings of joy and sorrow to different parties.</p>

<p>Bella was definitely one of the joyful ones; upon hearing the news, she was so happy she could hardly sit still.</p>

<p>At her earnest invitation, Luis agreed to meet with Dominic.</p>

<p>The meeting place was at Dominic&#x27;s home. The villa area on Haiwan Avenue was a traditional wealthy district, home to the city&#x27;s most luxurious villa complex.</p>

<p>This was also the renowned project that launched Tiger Real Estate. Dominic lived here, Leok lived here, and even Bella lived here.</p>

<p>At the entrance of Villa No. 1, a somewhat frail-looking Dominic was being supported by a beautiful young woman. The woman wore gold-rimmed glasses, appearing very intellectual and elegant.</p>

<p>At first glance, she wasn&#x27;t strikingly beautiful, but the longer you looked, the more her charm unfolded—a type with a naturally seductive quality that made one suspect Old Dominic had been drained dry by this enchantress.</p>

<p>&quot;Welcome to my humble home!&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic came forward, shook Luis&#x27;s hand, and introduced him with a warm smile: &quot;This is Minda, the property owner of City Garden Phase Two.&quot;</p>

<p>Why not just say she&#x27;s your concubine? Minda also extended her hand, saying with a charming laugh, &quot;Hello, nice to meet you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, thank you for your help last time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all. The enemy of my enemy is naturally a friend. Besides, we can be good friends too, right?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Haha, let&#x27;s talk inside. Let&#x27;s have a drink and chat.&quot;</p>

<p>The luxurious solid wood decor exuded an old-money elegance. By the large round table, Bella, dressed in casual loungewear, was lazily and languidly playing with her phone.</p>

<p>Seeing her, Luis was momentarily stunned. Dressed like she was in her own home, it was very strange.</p>

<p>Bella seemed to read Luis&#x27;s mind and immediately gave a seductive smile, saying, &quot;What&#x27;s so strange about it? President Dominic and I are still legally husband and wife, so this is also my home.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, yes, we&#x27;re all one family.&quot;</p>

<p>Dominic immediately chimed in agreement.</p>

<p>Bella was doing it on purpose, winking seductively and saying, &quot;My room is upstairs. I&#x27;ll take you for a visit later~.&quot;</p>
