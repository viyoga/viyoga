<p>At the beginning of the month, Hail called again, reminding Luis not to forget about Ye&#x27;s birthday.</p>

<p>Luis thought it over for a long time, finally gritting his teeth and bringing the matter up with his wife Lily that evening.</p>

<p>That night, his mother-in-law Ruth had gone out to play cards. The young couple, after finishing their shopping, were snuggling on the sofa when his lustful, lovely wife heard the news and froze.</p>

<p>Her first reaction was to say, &quot;I thought we agreed to meet that girl??&quot;</p>

<p>Luis couldn&#x27;t help but laugh and cry at the same time. &quot;You little pervert, you remember that detail clearly enough. Don&#x27;t worry, I&#x27;m working on arranging that on my end too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My husband&#x27;s godfather... this is the first I&#x27;m hearing of him.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily felt a bit uneasy and said softly, &quot;I&#x27;ve never met him. What if he doesn&#x27;t like me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He wouldn&#x27;t dare. I&#x27;d knock all his teeth out.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with a cheerful laugh. &quot;We can go on a little trip first, then just go over to have a meal with him on his birthday.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Sounds good!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily had always been the type to follow her husband&#x27;s lead, a classic example of &#x27;the husband as the guiding principle for the wife,&#x27; absolutely a model of a qualified spouse.</p>

<p>Carried away by their conversation, Luis took her on a short trip for a few days. Since her pregnancy was still in the early stages, they visited places that weren&#x27;t physically demanding, beautiful scenic spots perfect for taking photos.</p>

<p>The day before the birthday, Luis finally called Ye.</p>

<p>This was a private line. Usually, calls had to go through his secretary first, but this number could reach him personally at any time.</p>

<p>It rarely rang, but when it did, it was usually for something important, the kind of call Ye would never miss.</p>

<p>&quot;Luis, my boy, what makes you think of calling me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Godfather, where are you??&quot;</p>

<p>That address of &#x27;Godfather&#x27; gave Ye a moment of surprise, but he quickly understood there must be someone nearby and immediately replied in a warm tone, &quot;I just returned to the capital a couple of days ago, still busy catching up today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Will you be free tomorrow then!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Tomorrow... I&#x27;ll have to check my schedule in a moment, why do you ask?&quot;</p>

<p>Ye asked, puzzled. It seemed Hail was right. For a man of his stature, something like a birthday was rather trivial and almost childish.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s your birthday. My wife and I were thinking of coming to see you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My birthday? You and your wife want to come? Alright, give me five minutes.&quot;</p>

<p>He hurriedly hung up. Lily asked curiously, &quot;Honey, your godfather seems incredibly busy. Is his work really that demanding?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Must be. He&#x27;s a typical workaholic.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye called back immediately, laughing happily. &quot;Work tomorrow isn&#x27;t too heavy, it can be pushed back. I&#x27;ll be free to wait for you all afternoon.&quot;</p>

<p>His tone was as delighted as a child&#x27;s. He asked eagerly, &quot;What does my daughter-in-law like to eat? Over here I&#x27;m always eating work meals and living in the dormitory. Tell me so I can make some arrangements ahead of time.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t prepare anything on your end. We&#x27;ll bring some dishes over. With just the three of us, we can keep it simple.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good, good. I&#x27;ll send you the address. When you arrive, just give your license plate number for registration.&quot;</p>

<p>Even though this &#x27;dad&#x27; upgrade had cost a fortune, he still had to assess its practical value. Maintaining the relationship was what mattered most.</p>

<p>Following the provided address, the Mercedes drove up to a quiet community beside a park in the capital city. The community didn&#x27;t look luxurious, but the security was exceptionally tight.</p>

<p>After registering their IDs, the guard said politely, &quot;The leadership has already notified us. Please proceed right in. It&#x27;s Courtyard Number Two.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily in the passenger seat grew nervous. &quot;Honey, what exactly does your godfather do? This place doesn&#x27;t seem accessible to ordinary people.&quot;</p>

<p>Her intuition was sharp. Land in the capital was always at a premium. Any plot that could be developed had long been turned into high-end real estate selling for astronomical prices.</p>

<p>Yet this prime location retained its original character, with rows of two-story villas with courtyards inside the community.</p>

<p>The key point was, it didn&#x27;t look like private residential villas. The patrols on the roads were also very strict. It was clearly unlike anywhere normal.</p>

<p>&quot;Him? He&#x27;s an official. Don&#x27;t worry about the details. My wife is so adorable, he&#x27;s bound to like you.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis comforted her, though he himself was incredibly nervous.</p>

<p>For Luis, someone of Hail&#x27;s rank was already the highest level an ordinary person could usually reach. Someone like Ye, with connections and influence everywhere, was on another plane entirely.</p>

<p>The gate to Courtyard Number Two stood open. As the Mercedes approached, the large door swung inward.</p>

<p>Ye Haidong walked out, dressed in casual home clothes. He wasn&#x27;t in formal attire, seemingly wanting to avoid appearing too stern. Even in ordinary clothes, his kind smile still carried an air of natural authority.</p>

<p>&quot;You arrived so early! I thought it would be at least evening.&quot;</p>

<p>Ye&#x27;s smile was also kindly.</p>

<p>&quot;Godfather!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily called out timidly. She picked up a box with both hands and said, &quot;We wish you many more happy returns of the day, year after year.&quot;</p>

<p>In the eyes of ordinary people, Lily might be considered plain, but to elders, she had a likable, prosperous appearance—the kind deemed perfectly suitable for a wife, for building a life together.</p>

<p>Ye squinted with a smile as he looked her over. He unceremoniously accepted the box and said with a chuckle, &quot;So you&#x27;re Lily. This brat really isn&#x27;t proper, only bringing you to see me now.&quot;</p>

<p>As Luis was hauling a foam cooler from the trunk, Ye already said warmly, &quot;Forget &#x27;Godfather,&#x27; it&#x27;s too tacky. Just call me Dad.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Is... is that really okay?&quot;</p>

<p>Lily asked weakly, but her gaze turned toward her husband.</p>

<p>That pitifully sweet and delicate manner was utterly endearing—gentle, virtuous, seeing her husband as her anchor. Seeing that his &#x27;son&#x27; wouldn&#x27;t be mistreated as a live-in son-in-law, Ye felt even happier in his heart.</p>
