<p>Luis was not deliberately trying to orchestrate anything, but he had at least created some precious time alone for his wife and her sister-in-law. The question was whether his wife would be able to seize that opportunity, and whether she even possessed the boldness, the sheer nerve, to do so.</p>

<p>Downstairs, the situation had devolved into complete pandemonium. By the time Luis arrived, his mother-in-law, Ruth, had also just reached the scene.</p>

<p>Kiara, the property manager, was a born shrew. The moment she spotted Ruth, she flew into a rage. &quot;You bitch, finally decided to show your face, huh? Your stunt today wrecked my car and blocked the community gate. How exactly are you planning to explain this?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis&#x27;s mother-in-law, Ruth, was every bit the formidable matriarch herself. Seeing Luis approaching with a dark, grim expression, her heart suddenly swelled with immense confidence.</p>

<p>Seeing that they were surrounded by familiar neighbors, she immediately raised her voice to a shrill pitch. &quot;You&#x27;ve got some nerve blaming me! You parked your stupid car sideways, hogging three of my parking spots. Is this how your precious property management bullies the homeowners around here?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That&#x27;s one matter entirely! I could have moved the car!&quot;</p>

<p>Kiara was so furious she ground her teeth. &quot;But you had my car towed out, and you completely destroyed the handbrake system and everything else! You are paying for the damages, and that&#x27;s final!&quot;</p>

<p>Yana, who lived in Phase Two, immediately began to mock her coldly. &quot;Oh my, you Phase One folks really have it rough, don&#x27;t you? Over in our section, we call it a &#x27;Property Service Center,&#x27; but you guys call yours a &#x27;Property Management Center.&#x27;&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;So it sounds like you pay through the nose just to hire a bunch of entitled overlords to come and push you around.&quot;</p>

<p>The residents immediately buzzed with discussion upon hearing this. Ruth&#x27;s confidence skyrocketed. &quot;Did you hear that, you rotten old hag? I pay my management fees on time, every single year! You bunch of bastards take our money and still think you can ride roughshod over us!&quot;</p>

<p>Someone nearby who loved stirring up trouble had already chimed in.</p>

<p>&quot;One car taking up three spaces? The hell else could that be if not deliberately looking for a fight?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, right! Auntie Ruth&#x27;s family has two cars and three spaces. One time I couldn&#x27;t find a spot and asked if I could park there; she was super polite and didn&#x27;t mind at all.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;They&#x27;re absolutely right! The property management is supposed to serve us. This crap about riding on our heads and bullying us, what the hell is that?&quot;</p>

<p>Kiara instantly realized she shouldn&#x27;t have admitted that. But she was far too used to lording it over others.</p>

<p>As the wife of Leok, she didn&#x27;t just run this property office; she also owned a real estate agency and had access to all the inside information. Usually, she just coasted through life, playing cards and idling her days away.</p>

<p>She had always looked down on people like Ruth, considering them beneath her league. Now Ruth dared to speak to her like this?</p>

<p>She was absolutely livid. Her face turned icy cold. &quot;You damaged my car! I&#x27;m telling you, you can talk until you&#x27;re blue in the face and it won&#x27;t change a thing! I have the evidence! You will pay for this, whether you like it or not!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We&#x27;ll take legal action!&quot;</p>

<p>At that moment, Luis strode over, his voice cold as steel. &quot;A lawsuit? Fine, we&#x27;ll fight it with you. Isn&#x27;t this your favorite trick? Whenever something goes wrong, you just make excuses and tell everyone to &#x27;follow legal procedures.&#x27;&quot;</p>

<p>This remark prompted many other homeowners to chime in agreement.</p>

<p>Phase One was mostly made up of relocated residents, and frankly, their general decorum was often questionable. There weren&#x27;t many who were particularly reasonable or courteous, but at this moment, that trait became an unexpected advantage.</p>

<p>Especially when it came to their other talents, they were absolutely world-class, fanning the flames of conflict and gleefully spectating from the sidelines.</p>

<p>Kiara&#x27;s eyes went wide with panic. &quot;You little bastard! Who the hell asked for your opinion?!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was already standing protectively in front of his mother-in-law, a cold sneer twisting his lips. &quot;Turning off our power, splashing paint on our door. You&#x27;re quite the scoundrel, aren&#x27;t you? We&#x27;ve already caught your man and sent him to the police station. Right now, I&#x27;d love to see how you plan to explain yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don&#x27;t you dare slander me!&quot;</p>

<p>Kiara wasn&#x27;t flustered; instead, she gave a nasty, mocking chuckle. &quot;Who the hell knows what goes on in your family? Those women in your house look like nothing but loose sluts. Maybe your mother-in-law or your wife was out seducing some other woman&#x27;s husband, and that wife came to your door to splash paint. That sounds about right.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The fuck did you just say? Shut your stinking mouth!&quot;</p>

<p>With her fiery temper, how could Ruth possibly tolerate that? She lunged forward, delivered a resounding slap across Kiara&#x27;s face, grabbed her by the hair, and began tearing into her.</p>

<p>Yana didn&#x27;t hesitate for even a second. As Ruth&#x27;s devoted best friend, she was incredibly reliable. She had been silently biding her time, but the moment Ruth made her move, she pounced without a moment&#x27;s hesitation.</p>

<p>&quot;Ow! What are you all standing around for, you useless bastards?! Beat them! Beat them to death!!&quot;</p>

<p>Kiara&#x27;s shrieks turned to howls of pain because it wasn&#x27;t just the two of them attacking her; two of Ruth&#x27;s close card partners had joined the fray, throwing themselves into the fight without a second thought.</p>

<p>Several middle-aged women launched a sudden, ferocious assault, and the usually arrogant Kiara was quickly pinned to the ground, screaming in agony. She desperately called for the security guards nearby.</p>

<p>However, there were only about a dozen guards in Phase One. Half of them were elderly men just going through the motions, and they all stood there dumbfounded. Only her few trusted cronies remained.</p>

<p>The first one to rush forward, rumored to be her cousin or some such relative, was immediately sent flying backward by a single kick from Luis.</p>

<p>Luis&#x27;s expression was cold and ruthless; his smile was dark and dangerous. &quot;You all seem to be misunderstanding something. I&#x27;m not here to play nice. If you want to throw down, I&#x27;m more than happy to oblige.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Virga raised his hand, his telescopic baton snapping out with a menacing click. &quot;Brothers! Let&#x27;s show these bastards what we&#x27;re made of!&quot;</p>

<p>Over a dozen security guards from Phase Two, already replaced with younger, stronger men, along with the combative hotheads Virga had brought from the main group, had been itching for a fight. Now, like wolves unleashed, they eagerly surged forward.</p>

<p>The scene dissolved into utter chaos, a full-blown brawl. The residents were thrilled, cheering wildly, and many even took the opportunity to join in and vent their own grievances.</p>

<p>In the early hours of the morning, everyone involved was taken away. But they weren&#x27;t brought to the local police station; they were taken directly to the municipal public security bureau.</p>

<p>While waiting for their turn to give statements, Luis focused all his attention on his mother-in-law and Yana.</p>

<p>His mother-in-law was brimming with manic energy. &quot;Don&#x27;t you worry! We didn&#x27;t lose a bit. That bitch Kiara, though, her face is completely scratched up!&quot;</p>

<p>She was in an almost feverish state of excitement, like an old woman suddenly gripped by a wild, youthful frenzy. Truth be told, at her age, she had long since settled into a quiet, docile life.</p>

<p>Her marriage to Arthur was devoid of any passion; it was a stagnant pond of daily trivialities. But this incident... this experience was far too thrilling.</p>

<p>To her, it felt absolutely insane. She never, ever imagined she would do something like this alongside her son-in-law. The sheer exhilaration was almost unhinged.</p>

<p>&quot;That fucking bitch Kiara! I&#x27;ve loathed her for years!&quot;</p>

<p>As she chattered on excitedly, a young auxiliary officer called Luis away. Everyone assumed it was just routine, a normal rotation for statements.</p>

<p>But inside the interview room sat Jian. He offered a faint smile. &quot;Conflicts between property management and residents are pretty normal. I just didn&#x27;t expect you to have such a temper, actually throwing punches yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>Before arriving, Luis had called Hail and briefly explained the situation. Although the guy hadn&#x27;t shown his face yet, he was definitely lurking in the background, watching everything.</p>

<p>&quot;Shutting off our power and splashing paint on our door terrified my wife. And on top of that, my mother-in-law was humiliated. I wasn&#x27;t about to just stand there and let those women take the brunt of it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I don&#x27;t blame you.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian nodded readily. &quot;But we caught the guy who splashed the paint. He&#x27;s not connected to the property company, just some jobless lowlife from outside. He also has full-blown AIDS and other severe STDs, the kind of case detention centers refuse to take.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He&#x27;s adamant that he holds a personal grudge against your family. He insists the property company didn&#x27;t put him up to it.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian lowered his voice. &quot;I&#x27;ve looked into this guy. He&#x27;s a real pain in the ass, a tough nut to crack. If he&#x27;s this stubborn, it means someone paid him off handsomely. Prying the real truth out of him won&#x27;t be easy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He hasn&#x27;t relapsed into drugs yet, as far as we can tell. But with money involved, guys like that usually can&#x27;t resist.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh? Can I see him?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You can look, but don&#x27;t make any contact.&quot;</p>

<p>Jian readily agreed and led Luis to the investigation area where the statements were being taken. As they walked down the corridor, Jian subtly tilted his head, a silent signal.</p>
