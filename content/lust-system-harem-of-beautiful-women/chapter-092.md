<p>After returning home, Luis directly pulled her into his mother-in-law’s room. Seeing her brother-in-law’s mysterious demeanor, Nancy, who was already burdened with worries, didn’t overthink it and followed him inside with a sigh.</p>

<p>Nancy shook her head, appearing too weary to say much more.</p>

<p>Perhaps she felt too ashamed, or perhaps she believed talking to Luis would be pointless.</p>

<p>&quot;I’m going back for a bit!&quot;</p>

<p>Through careful monitoring, he learned that Nancy’s credit cards were already maxed out, and she couldn’t borrow a cent from online loans.</p>
