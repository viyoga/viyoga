<p>Luis continued smoking, grinning as he said, &quot;Mom, get me a beer, make it icy cold.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay!&quot;</p>

<p>But the situation was completely different from what Lily or Leah had imagined. Ruth immediately agreed and went to fetch her son-in-law an icy beer, even asking, &quot;Son-in-law, do you want something to go with the drink?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No need!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis took a big gulp before sizing up his alluring, mature mother-in-law.</p>

<p>Ruth was still wearing the same outfit she had on when she went out, which clung to her voluptuous curves. Even though not a hint of cleavage was showing, her entire figure exuded a seductive, mature charm.</p>

<p>&quot;Mom, did you buy me underwear?&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth paused, then laughed. &quot;Of course, I got a pack. Not sure if the size fits, but you can try them on after Lily washes them. Don’t worry, Mom bought name-brand ones.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I trust you. It’s not like you haven’t seen my size before.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis couldn’t resist another jab.</p>

<p>The skill &quot;Entering a Woman’s Heart&quot; might have seemed useless or even awkward at first, but its effects were slowly becoming apparent.</p>

<p>If he had dared to say something like this before, Ruth, with her shrewish temper—even if she felt guilty about peeking—would have slapped him without hesitation.</p>

<p>But now, Ruth seemed unfazed. The growing number of secrets between her and her son-in-law had gradually made their relationship more intimate, crossing the usual boundaries between mother-in-law and son-in-law. Instead of taking offense, she treated his bluntness as normal.</p>

<p>&quot;Still joking around? Asking for a beating!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth stretched lazily and said, &quot;Alright, Mom’s going to shower and change.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Wait, Mom, I still want to chat.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis drank his beer, feeling restless and unwilling to let her go.</p>

<p>&quot;About what? I’ve been wearing this all day, it’s uncomfortable.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth laughed helplessly. &quot;If you have something to ask, hurry up.&quot;</p>

<p>Mother-in-law Ruth: Favorability meter 66% (close familial relationship, turning point)...</p>

<p>Seeing the updated favorability meter, Luis realized it wasn’t just his actions that influenced it—external factors played a role too. Still, he was relieved to see it had increased.</p>

<p>&quot;Never mind, Mom. If you don’t want to talk, forget it...&quot;</p>

<p>Luis stubbed out his cigarette and grabbed another beer.</p>

<p>&quot;What nonsense are you spouting? You didn’t even ask and you’re already saying crap like that. Asking for a slap.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth lightly smacked the back of Luis’s head—more of a playful, affectionate gesture, almost like flirting.</p>

<p>Of course, this was also thanks to Luis’s recent behavior. He had been teasing her with suggestive remarks, but by taking it step by step without crossing any lines, he had made her start to accept this kind of boundary-pushing banter.</p>

<p>This was a talent beyond the System’s influence. Luis himself found it surprising—he had never realized he had this skill before.</p>

<p>Upstairs, in the room, Lily and Leah were whispering about something, but they immediately fell silent when Luis entered.</p>

<p>&quot;Great, looks like I’m the outsider here.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law, we were talking about Sister Nancy,&quot; Leah quickly explained. &quot;Mom and Second Sister were arguing earlier because Sister Nancy has been spending recklessly. She wants to be a celebrity, doing livestreams and short videos.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She hasn’t earned any money yet but has already invested a lot. Brother-in-law Daniel’s family is unhappy, so they called Mom.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Mom called Sister Nancy, and they just kept arguing... Seems like there’s also a problem with Sister Nancy’s money.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis stretched and asked, &quot;What kind of problem?&quot;</p>

<p>The details weren’t clear, but apparently, it had blown up into something serious. Ruth hadn’t been able to get a straight answer either.</p>

<p>Originally, she had wanted to confront Leah about her allowance, but it seemed to have triggered something bigger. Luis couldn’t figure out what kind of mess those two freeloading spouses could have stirred up.</p>

<p>But with one idle and the other irresponsible, it was questionable whether the money they mooched off their parents was even enough.</p>

<p>&quot;Whatever. Whatever happens with Sister Nancy, we’ll deal with it later.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re all daughters, and they’re both sons-in-law. Mom and Dad look down on the poor and fawn over the rich. Just because Daniel’s family had money, they didn’t ask for a dowry or give a wedding gift—they just let Sister Nancy marry him.&quot;</p>
