<p>&quot;It’s the wedding wine his grandfather prepared for him. It’s been aging in the cellar for nearly twenty years. They didn’t have a wedding banquet, so it was never opened.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He guards it like gold—no one’s even allowed to touch it. The whole case is still sealed.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He wouldn’t even give a bottle to my dad. It’s not expensive, but with age, it tastes amazing.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy grew more animated as she spoke, looking at Luis with affectionate eyes—almost as if this mattered more to her than beating Mira.</p>

<p>Deewa was stunned. &quot;Your sister hasn’t even had any?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy nodded. &quot;She doesn’t like alcohol anyway, so she never tried it.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa blushed. This was clearly significant to Luis—a sacred, meaningful gesture.</p>

<p>What was he implying? That she and Nancy were his brides tonight?</p>

<p>After their semi-reluctant threesome, the two beauties’ thoughts aligned. They exchanged glances, a hint of embarrassment between them.</p>

<p>As he poured, Luis said casually, &quot;My father-in-law and Daniel’s father have both been eyeing this. If I didn’t hide it well, those two old drunks would’ve found it by now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Smooth.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa’s eyes softened. She could share in Nancy’s joy.</p>

<p>They clinked glasses and took a big sip.</p>

<p>Neither liked the harshness of baijiu, but this aged liquor was milder. They could taste a richness they’d never noticed before.</p>

<p>Maybe it was psychological, but what they once disliked now felt like nectar.</p>

<p>&quot;Brother-in-law, if your wife finds out, she’ll probably be jealous.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was in a great mood, a rare joke slipping out.</p>

<p>Since sharing secrets, their conversations had become much more casual.</p>

<p>Before, Nancy would only bring up such topics in private. Feeling guilty, she wouldn’t dare tease her own sister like this.</p>

<p>&quot;You two are my wives too.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis leered. &quot;If Lily found out, you could serve her during the day and me at night—wouldn’t that be perfect?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bullshit. She’d have you castrated in a heartbeat.&quot;</p>

<p>They joked and drank, the aged liquor warming their bellies. Seeing Nancy in such good spirits, Deewa—who’d already conspired with Luis—spoke up. &quot;Nancy, there’s something I need your input on.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What is it?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Bubbles contacted me. She’s fallen out with Vivid Media and wants to bring her studio under our company.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was stunned, then scoffed. &quot;Is she out of her mind? If you can’t beat them, join them? Since when is Mira so spineless?&quot;</p>

<p>Just as expected—Nancy saw it as surrender, while Mira saw it as business.</p>

<p>Nancy grew agitated. &quot;She’s definitely up to something. Now that I’ve surpassed her, she wants to play spy? How dare she even ask?&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa said bluntly, &quot;She’s out of options.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Why?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy asked, confused.</p>

<p>&quot;Under the ’Top Creator’ rules, only officially registered media companies can participate. Her studio doesn’t qualify.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;That’s why she joined Vivid Media. But our city’s self-media scene is underdeveloped—I know only two companies signed up.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if she registers now, it’s too late. Buying a shell company would also miss the deadline.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy narrowed her eyes. &quot;So you’re saying her only way into the competition is through us.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa nodded. &quot;I have contacts at Vivid Media. Bubbles and Iron Ox parted on bad terms.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And Bubbles doesn’t know her contract had a trap clause. Iron Ox’s legal team is already preparing to sue.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;From what I’ve heard, it’s a nuisance lawsuit. She could drag it out but she’d eventually lose—at best, she’d settle for around a hundred thousand.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis shook his head. &quot;These people are ruthless.&quot;</p>

<p>Some companies don’t make money from operations—they make it from termination fees, just like shady entertainment agencies.</p>

<p>Nancy perked up. &quot;So she has no choice but to beg me?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;For the competition, yes. It’s her only option.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa nodded. &quot;But she won’t grovel. If we cooperate, it’s a win-win.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Business-wise, Bubbles has a solid following. Her joining would bring more traffic to our company.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Especially since we’ve been playing up this rivalry narrative with Vivid Media—it’s boosted both sides.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Her switching sides now would be huge publicity. It’d improve our reputation and keep the momentum going.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa was a sharp operator, calmly analyzing the situation.</p>

<p>Nancy was annoyed. &quot;So she thinks she can just waltz in without showing me some respect?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Her team is experienced. They’d accelerate our growth.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa said earnestly, then subtly signaled Luis. It was clear Nancy was reluctant.</p>

<p>Luis knew his sister-in-law’s pride. On other matters, she’d compromise, but this one hit a nerve—she’d held a grudge for years.</p>

<p>Luis jumped in. &quot;Speaking of which, what exactly is your beef with Bubbles?&quot;</p>

<p>At that, Nancy’s face darkened. She took a big swig of the liquor, drinking sulkily.</p>

<p>&quot;Look on the bright side—if not for that, you might never have met Luis.&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa fanned the flames with a flattering remark.</p>

<p>Nancy hesitated, clearly weighing whether to share the old grudge.</p>

<p>&quot;Fine. I’ll spill it. You two already know anyway, so there’s no point hiding it from you.&quot;</p>

<p>After a moment, she finally made up her mind.</p>

<p>There was one thing in Iron Ox’s exposé video that was absolutely true: Mira was a notoriously promiscuous woman with a chaotic personal life.</p>

<p>In terms of being a player, she was even worse than Luis. She’d &quot;turn&quot; someone, and once the novelty wore off, she’d dump them without a second thought.</p>

<p>She was a year ahead of Nancy. When the freshmen Deewa, Chloe, and Nancy arrived, they were considered goddesses.</p>

<p>First, she sexually harassed Deewa, pestering her endlessly. Deewa, having a temper of her own, slapped her and put an end to it.</p>

<p>Next was Chloe. At that curious, impressionable age, Chloe was a total shut-in, obsessed with gaming and not interested in dating.</p>

<p>Mira wheedled her way into their dormitory. Chloe, annoyed by the harassment, was completely cold toward her.</p>

<p>She simply accepted Mira’s enthusiastic services, letting her go down on her a few times. Eventually, both lost interest and things fizzled out.</p>

<p>In Mira’s definition, she’d never really dated Chloe—Chloe had only given her a first kiss.</p>

<p>She’d accepted Mira’s caresses, teasing, and oral services without complaint, enjoying her devoted attention but never reciprocating.</p>

<p>She wouldn’t kiss, tease, or go down on Mira. Even touching her chest felt perfunctory. Mira grew bored and moved on.</p>

<p>All four were stunning, but Nancy in her youth was the icy beauty—aloof and unapproachable. Her proud demeanor was what attracted Mira most.</p>

<p>For a top-tier player like Mira, &quot;turning&quot; someone so proud and beautiful would be the ultimate conquest.</p>

<p>She shifted her attention to Nancy, pursuing her relentlessly, even making her sexuality known, doing candlelight confessions and grand gestures.</p>

<p>She did everything a male simp would do—and more, with more effort than she’d ever shown before.</p>

<p>Her devotion made the girls she’d dumped jealous of Nancy. Those who knew Mira thought she’d turned over a new leaf.</p>

<p>But when she confessed publicly, Nancy simply said &quot;Get lost&quot; and walked away without a second glance, leaving Mira humiliated.</p>

<p>She’d always been discreet before, but this time she’d been publicly shamed. To make matters worse, Nancy didn’t even look at her—just turned and walked off.</p>

<p>This made Nancy a hero among the lesbians, and even more admirers flocked to her.</p>

<p>Daniel wasn’t even Nancy’s first love. In school, the most handsome boy in the accounting class had pursued her relentlessly.</p>

<p>Nancy had been moved and agreed to date him. The boy was so thrilled he announced it to the whole school—rubbing salt in Mira’s wounds.</p>

<p>The bitter player, Mira, was scheming and ruthless.</p>

<p>Despite being forced to drop out, she secretly contacted the boy—somehow seducing him, using methods unknown.</p>

<p>This stone-cold lesbian had the boy utterly bewitched. Less than a week after he’d announced his relationship with Nancy, he publicly dumped her.</p>

<p>Her first relationship, without even holding hands, ended in humiliation.</p>

<p>Then the boy proudly declared he was dating Mira. Nancy became the laughingstock of the school.</p>

<p>Losing to Deewa or Chloe would’ve been one thing—but to a lesbian?</p>

<p>It was a huge blow to Nancy, who’d been naively dreaming of a beautiful first love. To be stabbed in the back like that?</p>

<p>Even worse, after Mira dumped the boy a few days later, he came crawling back to Nancy.</p>

<p>It became an even bigger mess. Nancy slapped him and became disgusted with men, keeping everyone at arm’s length thereafter.</p>

<p>&quot;No way!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis was stunned after hearing all this. &quot;Sis, was that guy brain-damaged or blind? I’d donate his medical bills.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy, who’d been fuming just moments before, burst out laughing. &quot;You’re so generous!&quot;</p>

<p>Deewa commented, &quot;That kind of thing would scar anyone at that age. Who’d expect Mira to pull such a backstabbing move?&quot;</p>

<p>The prouder and more sensitive you are, the deeper the wound.</p>

<p>Nancy couldn’t help but hold a grudge. Someone so proud could never forget such humiliation. Those taunts had become her nightmare.</p>

<p>Luis knew his sister-in-law’s personality well—this was worse than death for her.</p>

<p>He was deeply troubled. He started reconsidering the feasibility of the School Beauty’s Feast quest.</p>

<p>Both endings required Nancy and Mira to face each other. After all these years, Nancy still couldn’t let go—the trauma was clearly severe.</p>

<p>Deewa tactfully added, &quot;I’d hold a grudge too, if it were me.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy, finding a kindred spirit, happily clinked glasses with her.</p>

<p>After drinking, noticing Luis’s silence, Nancy asked carefully, &quot;Brother-in-law, are you thinking about how to handle this?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with a headache. &quot;I invested in this company to make you all happy—that was my main goal.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;By that logic, I should just refuse. Making you face your enemy every day would be painful even for me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But we’re adults. We have to be practical. In business, we can’t just...&quot;</p>

<p>Before he could finish, Nancy, visibly moved, interrupted, &quot;Brother-in-law, I know you’re torn. Don’t worry—I’m not saying I’ll definitely refuse.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’ve invested a lot of money. I can’t let my stubbornness waste it.&quot;</p>

<p>Her sudden maturity surprised even Deewa. Nancy had always been self-aware about her own, but still naive and untouched by the real world, lacking in maturity.</p>
