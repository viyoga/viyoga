<p>Now it was truly a case of waiting for the rabbit to run into the tree stump. It was just past 5,00 PM now. Given Eloise’s usual habits, she wouldn’t arrive until at least 8,00 PM.</p>

<p>Luis thought it over and called Teacher Blanche at the Store. Thinking he had nothing better to do anyway, he decided to take the car in for maintenance, or at least get it washed.</p>

<p>&quot;Sure, you haven’t picked up your after sales service card yet, have you? That damn Nancy mentioned it several times but never showed up. It’s really something, being responsible for driving but not for maintenance.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;See you soon then. It’s almost time, let me treat you to a meal.&quot;</p>

<p>Inside the Store, Teacher Blanche, dressed in a gray suit dress, was waiting early. The tight fitting OL outfit accentuated her mature and voluptuous figure.</p>

<p>The pencil skirt made the curves of her buttocks appear both perky and perfectly round, and her long, beautiful legs clad in black stockings were enough to make anyone swallow hard uncontrollably. Although she wasn’t an ethereal beauty like Nancy or the others.</p>

<p>Her smile carried a vixen like allure, and her demeanor was both dignified and deeply feminine. She was absolutely a stunning creature who could stir a man’s lustful desires.</p>

<p>&quot;Teacher Blanche, hello!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis extended his hand generously.</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t mention it, I’m not a teacher anymore,&quot; Blanche replied with a smile.</p>

<p>Blanche asked with a hint of teasing, &quot;Why didn’t you come with Nancy? You two are always inseparable.&quot;</p>

<p>There was a definite undertone of playful mockery in Blanche’s words.</p>

<p>After all, she knew Nancy was a married woman, and she also knew Luis wasn’t her husband. Her words carried a vague, seductive charm.</p>

<p>&quot;She had something to do and went out with her husband.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then you must be feeling quite lonely.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, what can I do? I’m just a side piece with no status.&quot;</p>

<p>As they bantered, a technician drove the car to the service bay at the back. A full interior and exterior cleaning, deodorizing, and maintenance package would take about an hour.</p>

<p>Luis chatted with Blanche while finalizing the after sales card. Seeing it was almost dinner time, and noticing they were already preparing to leave work and discussing dinner plans.</p>

<p>&quot;Sister Blanche, would you do me the honor of joining me for dinner tonight?&quot; Luis whispered, subtly moving closer to her.</p>

<p>Seeing there was no one else around, Blanche smiled with deep implication. &quot;Oh, not afraid your Nancy will get jealous?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;We’re just having a meal, why would she be jealous?&quot;</p>

<p>When she spoke, her breath was like orchids, carrying a fragrance unique to mature women, different from the homely scent of a mother in law.</p>

<p>Moving in such circles, her looks and figure were naturally impeccable. Unlike those tall, glamorous flight attendants, Blanche possessed a more mature and captivating charm.</p>

<p>She knew how to maintain herself, preserve her figure, and dress stylishly. She was undoubtedly a rare and captivating beauty.</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, if neither you nor I tell her, she’ll never know to be jealous.&quot;</p>

<p>It had to be admitted that Blanche was also extremely alluring. At the very least, the way her eyes looked at you seemed to hook one’s soul, making it impossible to control the flood of fantasies.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s go then. I won’t be holding back today.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t hold back. The more ferocious you are, the more I like it.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with a slightly teasing tone, feeling a bit puzzled as to why she was heading out the back door and deliberately taking a detour.</p>

<p>But watching her alluring back view in that OL outfit and high heels, it had to be said it was truly a feast for the eyes, irresistibly reminding one of those love action films from the island nation.</p>

<p>They chose a good restaurant behind the Store. It was actually more of a tea cafe style, not particularly expensive, with generally affordable prices.</p>

<p>Luis was slightly taken aback. He had originally thought this materialistic woman would take him for a costly meal, never expecting her to pick such a down to earth place.</p>

<p>Black pepper fillet, a mixed roast platter, braised beef brisket, and prosperous shrimp balls. All were fairly classic dishes.</p>

<p>They ordered two bowls of rice, a portion of Tralelo Tralala old stick soup, and a helping of Five Finger six stewed with seven bones, then began to eat and chat.</p>

<p>&quot;By the way, Sister Blanche, why did you say you stopped being a teacher?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked casually.</p>

<p>&quot;It’s a long story!&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche gave a bitter smile and said, &quot;The teachers there were originally just contract workers, without formal permanent positions. I worked there before, left for a while.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Then, due to a staff shortage near the end of last year, I went back, still as a contract worker. It was because of some personal matters.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche thought for a moment and said with a smile, &quot;Oh, right, there’s a car show in the capital early next month. I contacted Nancy, but she said she wasn’t interested in being a car model. But if you all have time, you could come and show your support.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No problem!&quot;</p>

<p>During the meal, her phone rang several times. Each time, Blanche would frown delicately and then hang up, looking both angry and helpless.</p>

<p>Their relationship wasn’t close enough for her to share, and since she didn’t offer an explanation, Luis felt it wasn’t his place to pry. After finishing the meal, he returned to the Store.</p>

<p>During the meal, Luis behaved very properly, mainly because he had a big dinner planned for the evening. Eloise certainly wasn’t as radiantly beautiful as Blanche was right now, but the temptation of bullying that traditional, reserved, and virtuous married woman was clearly far greater.</p>

<p>When he returned to the Store, the front hall was already closed for the day. Only the master mechanic in the back yard and a few apprentices were still working overtime.</p>

<p>&quot;Manager Blanche, a man came looking for you earlier.&quot;</p>

<p>An apprentice approached. As soon as he spoke, Blanche’s expression changed. She asked, trembling slightly, &quot;What did he look like?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Thin, looked very scrawny, like a monkey, his eyes darting around everywhere...&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this description, Blanche’s face turned even paler. She asked, somewhat nervously, &quot;Is he still here waiting for me??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No!&quot;</p>

<p>The apprentice shook his head and said, &quot;I told him you were off work. He said he’d come find you tomorrow.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche gritted her silver teeth, looking rather flustered. She glanced at Luis and said, &quot;Can I ride with you?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Of course.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis drove her away from the back entrance of the Store. Blanche was visibly uneasy and somewhat distracted, sitting in the passenger seat and frequently checking her phone, clearly very troubled.</p>

<p>&quot;Sister Blanche, where to?&quot;</p>

<p>Luis asked.</p>

<p>&quot;If you have time, could you just drive me around for a bit? It’s been so long since I’ve been to the seaside for a stroll.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche’s expression was filled with sorrow and helplessness.</p>

<p>Luis checked the time; it was still early, so he didn’t refuse. He drove her to the Hailton City coastal road. At night, this place was an absolute paradise for walkers, with several activity squares that could be described as bustling with crowds.</p>

<p>Blanche was absent-minded and silent the whole way, clearly preoccupied with worry. Luis didn’t know what she was thinking about, so he just drove aimlessly.</p>

<p>The end of the coastal road was relatively deserted, mostly wholesale markets that were almost empty at night. The pier was also quiet after dark, with only a few people fishing there at night.</p>

<p>He parked the car by the pier, where they could at least enjoy the night view of the inner sea.</p>

<p>Blanche’s phone rang again. This time, she let out a sigh, hesitated for a moment, and then answered, &quot;When will you ever stop?&quot;</p>

<p>Although she didn’t put it on speakerphone, sitting close, Luis could still clearly hear the hysterical, somewhat sinister male voice from the phone:</p>

<p>&quot;You bitch, you think hiding from me will work? You stopped going to the school, moved house, you think I can’t find you??&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You left early today, but if you’d left late, we would have met again.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche said very impatiently, &quot;What exactly do you want?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What do I want? Hehe, one night of marriage deserves a hundred days of grace, let alone how many times we’ve slept together. Don’t think you can just shake me off.&quot;</p>
