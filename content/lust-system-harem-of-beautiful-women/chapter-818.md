<p>Luis watched with a gratified smile. It seemed the persona they were building was starting to take successful shape. He then checked the video accounts of Deewa and Chloe.</p>

<p>Although the number of comments wasn’t huge, the tone had already formed.</p>

<p>&quot;Whoa, she’s a local beauty from Bathek City. Do we live in the same neighborhood or something?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I told you, this kind of wife vibe is the best. Wives are great, wives are wonderful...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;This is Hans Bar. Wake up, it’s known as a gay hangout. She’s into women.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Holy shit, that’s such a waste. Damn it...&quot;</p>

<p>The comment sections and livestream chats were all discussing these topics. Some horny netizens had followed all three accounts.</p>

<p>People’s curiosity was an unavoidable psychological quirk. When they discovered all three were called &quot;Boss Lady&quot; and all featured plenty of Hans Bar backgrounds, the topic instantly became even more twisted and bizarre, yet carried a tantalizing, suggestive allure.</p>

<p>&quot;Whoa, all three are like that...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Damn, who’s the actual owner of Hans Bar? Three such beautiful women all calling themselves the boss’s wife.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Don’t be envious just yet. Sure, they might all be sleeping under the same roof, but I bet they’re all lesbians...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;A dominant lesbian power move, huh. Damn, they really know how to play. I’m honestly jealous.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If I could spend one night with these three, I’d die happy right now...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I was curious and went to Hans Bar last night. Had a few drinks, it wasn’t as crazy as you all made it sound, but I didn’t run into the boss ladies.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis went through his daily routine of liking and commenting on the posts. After a moment’s thought, he changed his username, it had been a random string of numbers before, which he’d never bothered with.</p>

<p>This time, he changed it to Hans Bar, which looked like an official account and was perfect for stirring up more buzz.</p>

<p>On the livestream, Nancy took an unexpected turn. She pulled out an emerald bracelet she’d bought before and said, &quot;I got this a while ago. Not sure if I got ripped off.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Any experts in the chat who know their stuff...&quot;</p>

<p>The livestream immediately got lively. Some said it was fake, others said it was real, some mentioned A or B grades, and others claimed it was Guatemalan material.</p>

<p>Jade was always a fascinating topic, the more glassy it looked, the more valuable it was, yet glass itself was worthless.</p>

<p>&quot;Why don’t we queue up in a few appraisal streamers’ rooms and see if we can get connected when we have time...&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy was holding up well. She had a strong mental game and responded to comments while selectively ignoring others.</p>

<p>This was a golden rule Deewa had established early on: never respond to questions about sexual orientation or who the owner of Hans Bar was.</p>

<p>Maintaining that air of mystery was even more thrilling than softcore content, and that was how their persona would stay solid.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Would the host like to activate the hack: Glory of the Number One Supporter.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Activate!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis didn’t hesitate. He couldn’t keep pouring his own energy into this forever.</p>

<p>To complete the Campus Beauties Feast mission, he couldn’t neglect this matter. And this item was absolutely perfect for this situation.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Glory of the Number One Supporter hack activated. Binding target: Nancy.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... The hack is permanently online 24/7. Once bound to an account, it will permanently hold the Number One Supporter position, no matter how many gifts anyone else sends.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;If anyone threatens the Number One position, it will automatically top up and send gifts to reclaim the top spot.&quot;</p>

<p>This hack was ruthlessly effective. Whether you were online or not, it would always use AI to monitor and hold the Number One Supporter position, no need to manage it yourself.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow, thank you, hubby, for the carnival! This is my first time receiving such a gift. Thank you, hubby&quot;</p>

<p>On Nancy’s fan leaderboard, a few people were sporadically sending gifts. Most of the top spots were held by family members: her little sister, her wife, and her mother-in-law.</p>

<p>The number one spot had been held by Daniel’s personal account, which had sent several carnivals.</p>

<p>As soon as the hack was bound, it activated immediately, ten carnivals in a row. The stream’s popularity surged and a flood of new viewers poured in.</p>

<p>Her usual cool, aloof, mature-woman vibe was replaced by a look of sheer sweetness and happiness.</p>

<p>Nancy openly called him &quot;hubby&quot; in the stream. Although it wasn’t as thrilling as calling him &quot;brother-in-law,&quot; she was still happy to express her affection so openly.</p>

<p>If her sister Lily asked about it, she’d just say it was part of the script...</p>

<p>She’d always dreamed of being a star, and even settling for being an influencer was a good alternative. For his sister-in-law, who never hid her vanity, having such a womanizing husband who still invested so much care in her and supported her spending meant everything, that love had already won her over completely.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you, hubby. You’re on a business trip, make sure you get some rest.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy said with a seductive smile.</p>

<p>Instantly, Luis’s &quot;Hans Bar&quot; account took the Number One Supporter spot.</p>

<p>Even though he’d changed the name, she recognized the avatar immediately.</p>

<p>Luis was a Leo, and he’d always used Simba the Lion King as his avatar, ever since he first got online as a teenager, across both computer and mobile platforms, he’d used the same image for every account.</p>

<p>&quot;I’ve got to go now!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis exited the stream and checked who else was live tonight. Deewa was also streaming. His sister-in-law was showing off jewelry.</p>

<p>Deewa’s stream, meanwhile, was chatting with the audience, discussing which authentic local spots in Bathek City were worth recommending and which overpriced, bland influencer traps to avoid.</p>

<p>Since they were building a local account focused on one city, they didn’t aim to become national influencers. Their reach and promotional boosts were all targeted locally.</p>

<p>She was eloquent and articulate, far more lively than Nancy’s stream, though she also lacked big gift-senders.</p>

<p>Chloe was also streaming. Her side was relatively quieter since she wasn’t much of a talker, but she was also discussing where to find good food in Bathek City.</p>

<p>Luis quietly opened the system interface:</p>

<p>Inside his backpack were: Random Blind Box (7), Aphrodisiac Incense (Male), and Worker’s Curse.</p>

<p>He had already used the Glory of the Number One Supporter hack. Luis asked, &quot;How do I exchange for a specific item again?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... The host can spend two blind boxes to exchange for a previously used specific item.&quot;</p>

<p>The rules were the same as before the upgrade. Just hearing it made Luis’s head ache a little.</p>

<p>Tonight, his original plan was to exchange for the Leverage Hack he had used before to tame Hela, in order to complete Elara Ye’s mission. That item was the most practical. But now it seemed he’d have to think of another way, because Luis felt he had to distribute his resources evenly and not play favorites.</p>

<p>Even if the Campus Beauties Feast wasn’t a mission, any man would dream of sleeping with three such exquisite stunners all together. No one could even imagine how intoxicating that earthly paradise would be.</p>

<p>He couldn’t show favoritism. Luis thought it over carefully, this hack was permanent, so it shouldn’t be a loss.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Will the host spend 2 random blind boxes to exchange for the item: Glory of the Number One Supporter hack.&quot;</p>
