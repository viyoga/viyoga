<p>It was four in the afternoon, the time to return to school. Luis first drove Leah and Avery, who had changed into their school uniforms and were carrying backpacks, back to school together.</p>

<p>Originally, she thought she should hide a little longer.</p>

<p>However, Avery’s mother had given her drug addict father three hundred dollars, telling him not to cause trouble at the school.</p>

<p>A drug addict would definitely not keep his word, but this money would at least allow him to live lavishly for a few days. Plus, the security at City First High School was not just for show, so Avery’s studies would not be disrupted.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not worry, Avery, your brother in law will also help think of a solution,&quot; Luis said gently just before they got out of the car. &quot;Stay in contact with your mother, see what the situation is. If you need anything, call your brother in law immediately, okay?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Got it, thank you, brother in law!&quot;</p>

<p>Avery’s face was full of soft charm mixed with a touch of emotion. His sister in law, Leah, was also smiling very happily. Luis did not neglect her; even if there was no time for intimate kisses, he still sent her many bedtime messages every day.</p>

<p>Under her brother in law’s careful conditioning, the simple and adorable sister in law no felt much jealousy and had accepted his viewpoint that this was simply an extension of his love for her.</p>

<p>The reason brother in law seduced Avery was because Avery was her best friend and shared the same figure type.</p>

<p>&quot;You know how much your brother in law loves you. He also knows you feel guilty deep down because he is your own sister’s husband.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Even if your heart can accept me, your body would surely hesitate and resist.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Brother in law is also a man, with his own desires and needs, times when he simply cannot hold back. Do you know how many times your brother in law has already been intimate with you in his dreams?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When holding your sister asleep, in his dreams, your brother in law has already licked every single part of your body...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;When your brother in law can no longer bear it, let Avery be your substitute, alright? Do not let your brother in law suffer so much.&quot;</p>

<p>These words made Leah’s heart race, her face flush, and she secretly scoffed in her mind.</p>

<p>Yet, she also felt a little stirred, believing she had no right to stop her brother in law.</p>

<p>After all, Avery was also willing to be the caged canary that brother in law kept.</p>

<p>But she could not help the sour feeling in her heart. While it was true that being a substitute showed his respect and love for her, Leah felt it was unnecessary...</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, I have transferred your living expenses. You must focus on your studies.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis stopped the car, took out his phone, and transferred five hundred dollars to each of them.</p>

<p>&quot;Thank you, brother in law!&quot;</p>

<p>The two young girls immediately cheered. Leah now felt completely at ease, thinking how wonderfully blissful it was to be provided for by her brother in law.</p>

<p>She no longer had to ask her mother for living expenses every time, only to be lectured and receive very little money, or even have her mother take out her frustrations on her after losing at cards.</p>

<p>Compared to that, her brother in law was simply an angel, so gentle and considerate, genuinely afraid she might feel hard done by at school.</p>

<p>Avery also smiled with sweet emotion. By now, she felt somewhat ashamed; initially, the talk about being a kept woman had been somewhat joking.</p>

<p>After all, this was Leah’s brother in law.</p>

<p>But gradually, she had become addicted, empathizing with Leah’s joy of first love, and slowly began to look down on the immature boys at school.</p>

<p>Other sponsors were usually fat old men, but this sponsor was young and handsome. Honestly, Avery would be quite happy to have a proper relationship with him, let alone the fact that he gave her money, bought her bags, and was extremely generous.</p>

<p>Now Avery even felt a little guilty. He was spending a fortune, yet she had not fulfilled her duty to properly serve her sponsor daddy. Wasn’t she just freeloading?</p>

<p>Her big eyes blinked, already scheming about how to find an opportunity to properly repay her generous sponsor...</p>

<p>The two baby faced, big breasted girls reluctantly got out of the car. A kissing goodbye would have been lovely, but unfortunately, there was a completely opposite, flat chested girl sitting properly in the passenger seat.</p>

<p>The car headed towards the town. Luis said with a warm, slow smile, &quot;Ariana, let us go buy a cell phone first.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Buy a cell phone?&quot; Ariana asked, somewhat surprised.</p>

<p>The phone she used now was very old, with a cracked screen and insufficient memory, so laggy it was unbearable. Sometimes, trying to open school learning materials would cause it to freeze completely, making her feel utterly humiliated.</p>

<p>Logan, with his poor and rigid personality, believed that relying on a phone for studying was simply unorthodox.</p>

<p>After listening to other parents, Eloise also felt it unnecessary to buy a new phone. This old one, while broken, was still usable, and since it could not really play games, it was perfect for a student.</p>

<p>Like most parents, they did not consider how sensitive a child could be during adolescence, how this phone was enough to make her a laughingstock at school and breed insecurity.</p>

<p>&quot;Your second sister mentioned I had a decent second hand phone you could use. She said that before.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;But I checked it, and there is still some needed data on it...&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Ariana let out a disappointed &quot;Oh,&quot; and said, &quot;Then it is fine. I can make do with this one.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Absolutely not necessary!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis took her to the mall, straight to the Apple Store. Without a second thought, he directly got her a high end iPhone 15 and a matching smartwatch, spending a total of one thousand three hundred dollars.</p>

<p>Ariana was shocked; this was an astronomical figure to her. Her speech even stammered, &quot;Brother in law, this is too expensive, I cannot accept it. If my parents found out, I would be scolded to death.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis laughed heartily, shoved the bag directly into her hands, winked and said, &quot;You silly little girl!&quot;</p>

<p>As he spoke, he booped her pert little nose with his finger. It was an exceedingly intimate, doting gesture, and even the term of address carried a hint of ambiguity, causing Ariana to freeze uncontrollably.</p>

<p>Clearly, the number of times she and Luis had met could be counted on one hand. Their relationship was definitely not at that level; even as relatives, they were practically strangers.</p>

<p>Yet, holding the shopping bag, Ariana only blushed a little shyly and did not find the gesture particularly abrupt or excessive.</p>

<p>Luis carefully observed her reaction. Seeing she did not reject it, he proactively took hold of her hand, not daring to interlace their fingers properly, but instead grasping her by the wrist, and led her further into the mall.</p>

<p>&quot;Just tell them I gave it to you. How would your parents know if it is second hand or not?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, they use those one or two hundred dollar Android phones. What would they know about Apple phones, what they are or how much they cost?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Get yourself a pretty phone case, and no one will be able to tell what model it is anyway.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Rest assured, with your parents’ level of awareness, if you say it is a discarded second hand phone, they definitely will not doubt you.&quot;</p>

<p>Hearing this, Ariana’s heart settled. Her situation was the same as Leah’s.</p>

<p>Adolescence is a time of vanity, sensitivity, and easy insecurity. For her, this was also the most expensive gift she had ever received in her life.</p>

<p>She clutched the shopping bag’s handles tightly, her steps feeling a little light, even feeling as if she were dreaming. To her, this was a surprise falling straight from the heavens.</p>

<p>&quot;I do not know when your birthday is, so just consider this your brother in law’s birthday gift to you for this year!&quot;</p>

<p>Reaching the elevator, Luis released her, then reached out and ruffled her little head, his face full of tenderness.</p>
