<p>Thinking of the huge amount of money in Luis’s stock account and hearing from her sister that he had a lot of investments and had already achieved financial freedom, Nancy felt her heart pounding and began to have strange thoughts in her mind.</p>

<p>The sponsors that the students were trying their best to find were all middle-aged ugly men. If there was a young, rich, and somewhat handsome sponsor like this, she was afraid they would fight for him.</p>

<p>&quot;Let’s go look at cars together. You are nominally the customer, and the car will be in your name first. We can transfer it to her when it’s time to give it to her for her birthday.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis wanted to experiment. He nominally gave the car to his mother in law, but the money was registered under the name of his eldest sister in law Nancy. He wanted to see if the task would include the money in her account.</p>

<p>&quot;Why make it so much trouble?&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy felt a little guilty for a moment. She was now in debt and did not know what to do. The matter with Nova Media was probably not over yet. It might not be appropriate to have another car in her name at this time.</p>

<p>&quot;Sister, you forgot I used to work at a car repair shop. There are a lot of unspoken rules in our industry. There are kickbacks from the car repair shop and from acquaintances who bring them to 4S stores to buy cars. Of course, this money does not flow to outsiders.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Besides, I will pretend to be a car mechanic and take you there. I can check the interior and other configurations, and I can tell whether they are original car parts or not.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Kickbacks, a lot?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;2% is generally the industry rate. I just asked and it is still the same.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy wanted to refuse but she did not dare to tell the truth, and she could not think of any other excuse. After all, the car was to be given to her biological mother.</p>

<p>Luis handed her one of his bank cards and said, &quot;The password is six eights. You can use this one when you swipe the card later.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Good!&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy thought that it was just a car and there should not be any problems. Besides, Luis said that he would buy one to practice first, so he should buy a cheap second hand car.</p>

<p>Nancy was completely dumbfounded when Luis stopped a taxi and took her to the Porsche dealership.</p>

<p>Just as Luis was about to step in, she grabbed his hand and said in a panic, &quot;Brother in law, you want to buy a Porsche?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, let us do it right away, so that those gossipy women will not have to show off in front of Mom all day long.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis said with a smile, &quot;Do not worry, big sister, I can afford it if I can buy it. I will pay for the maintenance of the car, so you do not have to worry about it.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis pulled her into the store. sales manager greeted her warmly. Luis extended his hand and said with a smile, &quot;Hello, Manager Blanche, nice to see you again.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Hello, Luis!&quot;</p>

<p>They shook hands and said everything.</p>

<p>Luis had spoken to her on the phone before, and they discussed the situation and agreed on the amount of kickbacks. This was not groundless.</p>

<p>Many people like to bring a knowledgeable relative with them when buying a car, whether it is a new car or a used car, or directly ask a familiar mechanic to help them with the inspection or check the condition of the car.</p>

<p>Car dealerships are accustomed to this. Even if there is no prior communication before bringing a large number of professional technicians, they can still get it done with a brief private discussion when they meet.</p>

<p>After all, from the customer’s perspective, they will definitely trust their acquaintances or relatives more, and will listen to their opinions more if they do not understand something when buying a car. Therefore, this group of intermediary people are also valuable resources for stores.</p>

<p>They may even advise you to buy a used car instead of a new one.</p>

<p>When it comes to complaining, people say that this brand is not as good as that brand, and they always have a lot of say.</p>

<p>&quot;What would you two like to drink?&quot;</p>

<p>Manager Blanche asked with a smile on her face.</p>

<p>&quot;Coffee, thank you.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy just finished speaking when she suddenly grabbed her hand in surprise, &quot;Teacher Blanche, it is you!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Nancy!&quot;</p>

<p>Manager Blanche’s eyes lit up as well, and she said with a sweet smile, &quot;I did not recognize you. Come and sit down.&quot;</p>

<p>After that, they started chatting intimately. Luis was stunned watching from the side. He found out that she was the physical education teacher of their school, who was working part time here because of the bad job situation.</p>

<p>&quot;Nancy has made great progress and is now buying a Cayenne.&quot;</p>

<p>The mature and charming Blanche sighed.</p>

<p>She knew better than anyone how chaotic the school was, but this group of people always laughed at the poor but not at prostitutes, and she would be laughed at by everyone if she suffered with the poor boy.</p>

<p>If you find a big money sponsor, you will be worshipped by everyone. It does not matter whether this sponsor is old, ugly, or rich. It is okay. There are few people who look glamorous on the surface but actually live a good life.</p>

<p>As she spoke, she looked puzzled, because Nancy did not have any branded clothes on her, her bag was very ordinary, and she did not even wear any jewelry. She did not even have the most basic outfit.</p>

<p>Fortunately, Nancy had the conditions and temperament to do so. After a brief moment of panic, she smiled and said, &quot;Teacher, we have to buy a car to get around.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, it is a blessing for the teacher. I have not opened a shop for a long time.&quot;</p>

<p>Teacher Blanche pondered for a moment, then lowered her head and asked, &quot;Where did you find this boy?&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing Nancy’s hesitation, teacher Xiao opened her eyes wide and said, &quot;Is it your husband?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;No, teacher, that...&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy waved her hand hesitantly for a moment.</p>

<p>Teacher Blanche showed an understanding expression and said with a smile, &quot;We will talk about it later. Take a look at the car first.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche held the materials and followed along, also bringing over two cups of iced coffee.</p>

<p>Luis was chatting animatedly with the technician from the Store around a Cayenne, discussing everything from the configuration to potential issues with licensed vehicles in a highly professional manner.</p>

<p>Nancy looked somewhat puzzled at Teacher Blanche, who chuckled and pulled her to sit down in the nearby reception area.</p>

<p>&quot;Teacher, you don’t seem very professional,&quot; Nancy teased with a smile. &quot;This is the first time I’ve seen a salesperson who knows nothing about what they’re selling.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Salespeople don’t really understand these details. It’s mainly because the person you brought is too professional,&quot; Teacher Blanche lowered her voice and said.</p>

<p>&quot;Let me tell you, the salespeople here are just like those in real estate sales offices—they work on commission with no base salary, so very few of them are truly knowledgeable.&quot;</p>
