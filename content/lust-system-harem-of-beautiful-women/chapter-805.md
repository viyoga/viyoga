<p>&quot;Ding... Ultimate mission for ’Girl with Huge Boobs Little Sister-in-Law’ generated.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Deflower the huge-boobed little sister-in-law and complete the ’Intimate Family Threesome’ mission with her.&quot;</p>

<p>At first, Luis scoffed at this task, what was so hard about it?</p>

<p>Avery and Ariana were already ready and willing. He’d trained these two little lolis well.</p>

<p>They were at that age of first love, and Luis had fulfilled their prince fantasies while offering mature conquest and tenderness, no problem at all.</p>

<p>But then he read the next part of the mission, and his head started throbbing.</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Threesome options limited to: Nancy, Lily, Ruth.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ding... Mission reward: random blind box (1).&quot;</p>

<p>His sister-in-law’s ultimate mission had finally dropped. This should have been cause for celebration.</p>

<p>First, there was a new mission to complete. Second, that adorable, huge-boobed little loli was right in front of him every day.</p>

<p>Surrounded by her sweet, tempting presence, yet forced to hold back from taking her, it was torture for any man.</p>

<p>But reading the mission requirements, Luis felt his vision go dark and cursed inwardly.</p>

<p>He had to have a threesome to complete it, and the options were brutally restrictive, only one of his two sisters-in-law or his mother-in-law.</p>

<p>Keep in mind, his sister-in-law was still a tender high school student. If the Wood sisters or their mother ever found out he’d slept with her too, they’d skin him alive.</p>

<p>Luis realized that System 2.0, while making many improvements and adjustments, had also made missions far more demanding.</p>

<p>Compared to the gradual main missions at the start, the main missions hardly updated anymore.</p>

<p>And the new ones were brutally difficult, except for the first welfare mission, every task cranked up the intensity.</p>

<p>Compared to all that, &quot;Turn the Tables&quot; suddenly seemed like the easiest mission.</p>

<p>Luis immediately resolved to hold onto his remaining eight blind boxes and use them wisely.</p>

<p>With missions this hard to complete, even recycling side missions for income would take time, nothing would be quick anymore.</p>

<p>His blind box surge had come from easier pre-upgrade missions. The new ones were all hellish. He needed to stay sharp.</p>

<p>Without any clear solution, Luis was frustrated.</p>

<p>Just then, Blanche called. Luis realized he’d been neglecting her lately and felt a twinge of guilt.</p>

<p>On Friday nights, she’d normally be in class, but Blanche had picked her daughter up early and brought her home.</p>

<p>They finally had a real home of their own, something that gave both mother and daughter a sense of security.</p>

<p>As night fell, the curtains were drawn. With the summer heat, the living room AC was blasting cool air.</p>

<p>In the kitchen, the mature, alluring mother was busily cooking, speaking softly. &quot;Honey, it’s all settled now. Two months sounds like a long time, but it still feels rushed, I’m doing my best.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche had decided she was too old to keep working in nightlife, even if Hans’s Bar was a rare clean establishment.</p>

<p>She’d always been a drifter in this city. With her teaching degree, she still longed to return to education.</p>

<p>Unfortunately, she’d left her job and going back was unlikely, plus her major was being phased out.</p>

<p>Even finding another position would probably mean an unstable contract with low pay.</p>

<p>Still, Blanche wanted to try, she trusted her professional abilities.</p>

<p>But within days of starting a new job, her looks and figure attracted harassment and hints at &quot;casting couch&quot; arrangements. Furious, she walked out.</p>

<p>She’d tried to stay strong and not complain, but her daughter Avery noticed something was wrong.</p>

<p>Though they hadn’t been particularly close, Avery sensed it and immediately went to Luis.</p>

<p>At first, Blanche didn’t want to talk. But after Luis pinned her down and fucked her hard, she broke down crying, pouring out her frustrations.</p>

<p>Lying in a man’s arms, feeling protected and supported, that was a sweetness she couldn’t resist.</p>

<p>Luis asked what she wanted. Her wish was simple: stable work, not the precarious, uncertain existence she’d had before.</p>

<p>That was easy. Luis contacted Minda immediately.</p>

<p>The community kindergarten was set to open in September and was in a frantic prep phase. Minda was working to secure public funding and official status.</p>

<p>Luis had mentioned it to Hail, who’d agreed without a second thought.</p>

<p>So Luis arranged for Blanche to work there, letting her coordinate with Eloise, Serena, and Minda.</p>

<p>After several days of discussions, the plan was set: Blanche would serve as vice principal and director.</p>

<p>In practice, Eloise had been unemployed for years, and Serena was a bookworm with no experience.</p>

<p>Compared to them, Blanche was seasoned. Having struggled in the city for so long, her practical skills were probably the best.</p>

<p>From meetings to parent disputes, Blanche was the most capable and trustworthy.</p>

<p>&quot;You’re sleeping with that vixen, aren’t you?&quot;</p>

<p>That was Serena’s jealous comment. Unlike Eloise, who was nervous about being principal despite zero experience, Serena had immediately spotted the truth, and her jealousy was fierce.</p>

<p>Luis was shameless. Blanche’s looks, combined with her serious demeanor and obedient, submissive attitude toward him, made it obvious to anyone with a clue.</p>

<p>&quot;Yeah, next time I’ll bring you along for a threesome, hehe.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis had promptly pulled Serena into a quick round, making the young widow come so hard she tacitly accepted it.</p>

<p>They’d been working together for about a week, finalizing all the details. Blanche’s abilities had earned everyone’s respect.</p>

<p>Tonight, Blanche had taken the rare initiative to call Luis over for dinner.</p>

<p>He was still the man of this house. With the curtains drawn, wearing only boxers, he lounged on the sofa, watching her bustle in the kitchen.</p>

<p>&quot;Are you satisfied with this arrangement? If not, I can figure something else out.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis spoke gently. Blanche shuddered, then said, &quot;Oh, stop showing off your connections. Getting into that kindergarten is already good enough, I’m grateful.&quot;</p>

<p>But there was a hint of sourness in her tone. She was sharper than most and could tell Luis had unclear relationships with those women too.</p>

<p>The atmosphere in the room was strange, especially the mother and daughter’s outfits.</p>
