<p>Today, when facing Lily, her attitude was appropriately polite and measured, showing no sign of guilt at all.</p>

<p>But she still had a thin sheen of cold sweat on her forehead. After all, from a woman’s perspective, if anyone dared to seduce her wonderful husband, it wouldn’t be an overreaction to kill them.</p>

<p>There were already quite a few people inside. Minda greeted them warmly.</p>

<p>Chloe, Nancy, and Deewa were naturally present, as were Yana and her daughter Maria, whose swimming pool was about to open.</p>

<p>Eloise and Serena were on their way. In fact, it was pretty much his entire harem, all gathered together in harmony—a sight that was immensely satisfying.</p>

<p>They’d all brought gifts too. When they looked around, they admired the quality of the renovations. The quiet location in the heart of the community was a huge bonus.</p>

<p>As they toured the new home and reached the bedroom, Deewa exclaimed, &quot;Teacher Blanche, why is your bed so huge?&quot;</p>

<p>It was a standard 2.2 meters long, but a full 3 meters wide—nearly the size of two double beds put together.</p>

<p>Though the master bedroom was spacious, the bed was still somewhat excessive. Honestly, any longer and it might as well have been a kang.</p>

<p>Blanche was prepared for this question. She smiled and said, &quot;It’s fulfilling a childhood dream. I was always a restless sleeper, afraid of falling out of bed, so I got a super-sized one.&quot;</p>

<p>She said it without batting an eye. In truth, the bed was custom-made specifically to make mother-daughter threesomes more comfortable.</p>

<p>She’d ordered two of them—one for each bedroom.</p>

<p>Modern furniture factories had state-of-the-art equipment. As long as you paid enough, a bed could be made in a single day.</p>

<p>Luis paid generously without haggling and wasn’t picky about the design. The beds were made entirely of hardwood, sturdy enough to withstand any activity.</p>

<p>&quot;Bunny, Ariana, the three of us could sleep together on this bed without any problem.&quot;</p>

<p>Avery said shyly, with a hint of suggestive meaning.</p>

<p>Leah immediately widened her eyes, grabbed Luis’s arm, and whined, &quot;Brother-in-law, I want to change my bed to one like this too!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright. Pick out a style you like later.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis gave a wicked grin, naturally eager to fulfill her wish.</p>

<p>His little cousin was envious too, but she didn’t want one. First, her room was too small—this bed would leave no room for other furniture.</p>

<p>Second, she only came to the city on weekends. No one else would be sleeping over at her place. She was happy enough with her new bed and didn’t need to change it.</p>

<p>More people kept arriving. The new house was starting to feel a bit crowded, so Blanche invited everyone to head downstairs for dinner.</p>

<p>There were too many people to eat at home, so she’d booked a private room at a seafood restaurant across from the community, setting up three full tables.</p>

<p>Close friends and students, including Chloe—about ten people in total—along with Luis’s entire family.</p>

<p>The circle of beautiful young wives, colleagues from the bar, Daniel, Minda, and the property’s main managers all filled the room. It was lively and bustling.</p>

<p>Dishes were brought out one after another. Besides fruit juice, they opened a 6-pound bottle of Remy Martin that night.</p>

<p>Blanche was the first to raise her glass and said, &quot;Thank you all for taking the time to come today. Consider this a chance for everyone to get together and have some fun. Please, make yourselves at home.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Cheers!&quot;</p>

<p>Everyone raised their glasses.</p>

<p>Blanche was exceptionally intelligent, with high EQ and IQ, making her a delight to be around.</p>

<p>Her thoughtfulness and observational skills always made others feel comfortable. Now that her life was stable, she’d built up a strong network of goodwill.</p>

<p>&quot;This toast is definitely for you, Boss Yang. Thank you for parting with this place. My daughter and I absolutely love it.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche immediately refilled her glass and toasted Minda beside her.</p>

<p>Minda raised her glass with a flirtatious smile. &quot;You’re too kind. We’re all family here—no need to be so formal.&quot;</p>

<p>They exchanged knowing smiles, both clearly aware that they were sleeping with the same man. The understanding was unspoken.</p>

<p>After both beautiful women finished their drinks, Minda said generously, &quot;I should thank you too. To be honest, this house has been a bit of an issue for me lately.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What, an issue? You’re talking nonsense.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth, who got along well with her, rolled her eyes and said, &quot;You practically stole this place when you bought it. You could sell it and make a tidy profit.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You’re making it sound like you’ve been so wronged. Classic case of pretending to be modest. At your age, don’t be so fake.&quot;</p>

<p>Minda laughed helplessly. &quot;I’m not being fake. This house really did feel like a white elephant.&quot;</p>

<p>It was true that she’d gotten a good deal. She could easily resell it at market price and make at least two hundred thousand dollars—and it would sell fast.</p>

<p>But Minda couldn’t bear to part with it. The renovations were exquisite, much nicer than her own home. The design and materials were beautiful.</p>

<p>Having been Dominic’s mistress, she had high standards and taste. She loved this place too and had even considered moving in herself.</p>

<p>But she was used to her own home and didn’t really want to move.</p>

<p>She couldn’t bear to sell it, and the rent wasn’t worth her time. The troubles of the rich could be surprisingly simple.</p>

<p>The renovations she loved so much looked like a luxury mansion to Blanche and her daughter. Initially, Avery had been a bit resistant to moving, since she’d just gotten her own room.</p>

<p>But she knew the old place was too noisy. She’d hesitated until she saw this place, and all her doubts disappeared.</p>

<p>Dishes kept arriving: golden soup with shark’s fin, steamed lobster, geoduck with chicken oil, green crab stir-fried with ginger and scallions... one high-end dish after another.</p>

<p>The atmosphere was warm as glasses clinked. Since Blanche’s arrival, it felt like they’d become one big family, scattered across different corners of the community.</p>

<p>Deewa came over to toast and sat down, asking, &quot;Teacher Blanche, how are the sales going at the City Garden villa district?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The sales office is ready. They’ll be hiring in a couple of days.&quot;</p>

<p>Blanche was well-informed and said cheerfully, &quot;What, you big influencers are interested too? You should ask Manager Minda about that.&quot;</p>
