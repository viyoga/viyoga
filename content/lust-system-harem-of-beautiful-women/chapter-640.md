<p>The next day, he woke up bright and early, getting out of bed just as the sky was beginning to lighten. Chloe had wanted to stay in bed, but after Luis gave her a tug, she got up anyway.</p>

<p>Women are such strange creatures, always obsessed with losing weight no matter the time. It was somewhat understandable for those plump girls or those with a little extra meat.</p>

<p>But all three of the school beauties had this notion. Nancy loved playing badminton whenever she was free, and she also made time to exercise and go swimming.</p>

<p>Chloe also kept saying she felt a bit fat after giving birth, using running, dancing, and other forms of exercise like yoga to lose weight. Her methods were rich and varied.</p>

<p>In reality, they all had figures to die for, with perky bottoms, full breasts, and waists that most women envied—the kind of slim, narrow waist often called a &quot;wasp waist.&quot;</p>

<p>Especially Chloe, who had managed to maintain this figure even after childbirth. If anything, that little bit of extra softness only made her more alluring. If other women knew, they would absolutely want to kill her.</p>

<p>The sky had just begun to show the pale white of dawn, casting a faint, hazy light.</p>

<p>Luis changed into matching sportswear with her, and they headed to a nearby park for a short run and to play badminton to exercise their bodies.</p>

<p>They kept at it for two hours before returning home. After a quick wash, they prepared breakfast for the nanny. Once the child was fed, Luis took Chloe out again.</p>

<p>&quot;Honey, this feels so wonderful!&quot;</p>

<p>Sitting in the passenger seat, Chloe said with a slightly flushed face, &quot;Don&#x27;t worry, I&#x27;ll be careful to avoid any suspicion. When we go to your place and when I meet your wife, I&#x27;ll change into my own clothes.&quot;</p>

<p>Even though she was already a mother, wearing matching outfits with the man she loved still made her exceptionally happy. Her smile was especially sweet the entire morning.</p>

<p>&quot;Hehe, it&#x27;s no problem. I&#x27;ll just buy her the exact same set later.&quot;</p>

<p>They went to the best market in Bathek City, the one with the brightest, most high-end atmosphere and the highest prices. But because it had a wide variety of ingredients, including many types of seafood and a lot of organic food, people flocked to it nonetheless.</p>

<p>After shopping and buying a huge pile of things, they returned home around ten. Chloe took the child out to play, giving the nanny a bit of a break.</p>

<p>When she came back at eleven-thirty, Luis had already finished preparing today&#x27;s lunch.</p>

<p>&quot;Wow, so lavish! Honey, you&#x27;re amazing!&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe happily gave Luis a kiss.</p>

<p>When the nanny came out and saw the spread, she even praised them: &quot;It&#x27;s rare to see young people with such good cooking skills these days. Jia Jia, you are truly blessed.&quot;</p>

<p>The spare rib soup with dried scallops and bamboo fungus was absolutely delicious and naturally sweet, made with only the highest quality ingredients. The ribs were from black pigs, giving the meat a rich, fragrant flavor.</p>

<p>There were stir-fried abalone slices with shrimp and celery, lobster meat stir-fried with king oyster mushrooms, braised spare ribs, and a plate of blanched broccoli.</p>

<p>In the southern markets, if you&#x27;re willing to pay a bit more, the ingredients are prepared exceptionally well. In that regard, you truly get what you pay for.</p>

<p>&quot;Auntie, Chloe, you two go ahead and eat first!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis winked, then took the child into his arms and said playfully, &quot;I&#x27;ll pack some food to take and have lunch with Lily later. You two enjoy a quiet meal for now.&quot;</p>

<p>Chloe was not only enjoying the delicious food, but also feeling incredibly happy inside.</p>

<p>After the nanny finished eating first, Luis started packing the insulated lunch containers.</p>

<p>Seeing this, Chloe knew he was going to have lunch with his wife. He had bought so much food in the morning because he had made two portions of everything.</p>

<p>She no longer felt jealous. Instead, she gave him a tender and passionate kiss goodbye, her eyes soft and affectionate as she said, &quot;Honey, I&#x27;ll work hard. I&#x27;ll try my best to figure out a way to get your wife to agree as soon as possible.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I believe in you!&quot;</p>

<p>They had taken her car the night before, so Luis hailed a taxi to the community clinic.</p>

<p>It happened to be noon, the lunch break. At this usually idle and boring workplace, there was rarely much work to do.</p>

<p>Luis had called his wife in advance to tell her not to buy lunch. He had wanted to invite Yana to eat with them, but she was off today.</p>

<p>&quot;This is so delicious!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily was especially happy. Besides the fact that this was a loving meal specially prepared by her husband, the main thing was that Luis was always so thoughtful.</p>

<p>He knew she liked a little spice with her rice, so he had also bought a portion of poached sliced beef in hot chili oil on the way. For a couple to have five dishes and a soup, it felt a bit extravagant.</p>

<p>She invited some colleagues she got along well with to share. This meal was also a display of affection, and she enjoyed it immensely. For her, these little moments were enough to constitute a satisfying small happiness.</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, by the way, honey, Aunt Yana invited us to dinner tonight. She said Maria will be there too.&quot;</p>

<p>When they were finally alone, Lily said with a sly, teasing smile: &quot;Maybe she misses you.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis chuckled and said, &quot;You little minx. You didn&#x27;t get to take advantage of her before, and you&#x27;re still not satisfied?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;What do you mean? I wasn&#x27;t forcing anything&quot;</p>

<p>Lily giggled playfully, saying, &quot;After dinner tonight, I&#x27;ll send Maria off, and you&#x27;ll have some alone time with Aunt Yana.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ve probably been neglecting her lately, and she was too shy to ask me directly.&quot;</p>

<p>No need for your help with that, your husband has already played the mother-daughter duo...</p>

<p>As expected, his sister-in-law Nancy wasn&#x27;t home. With nothing much to do, Luis did a few sets of exercises and then took a nap, only getting up groggily when his wife called after work.</p>

<p>The reason Yana wanted to treat everyone to a meal was because it was a joyous occasion for her and her daughter.</p>

<p>The two shop spaces Luis had given them were finally rented out. It was to a single person, a boisterous couple from the northeast who planned to open a barbecue restaurant.</p>

<p>During the first phase of the property&#x27;s use, the units had already been combined, so they even saved on the cost of demolition, which was simply ideal. The upstairs office could be directly used as private rooms, making things even easier.</p>

<p>There wasn&#x27;t much clutter inside to clean up anyway, and the water and electrical lines were already in place with no need for modifications. After a simple cleanup, they were able to open just over a week later.</p>

<p>It was their first time being landlords, and they were both incredibly excited. After all, it represented a deep sense of security provided by their man.</p>

<p>The rent was set at a reasonable 400 dollars per unit. They signed a three-year contract with payment every six months, meaning the mother and daughter each received a lump sum of 2,400 dollars.</p>

<p>Maria made a special trip back. She invited over a dozen classmates from the city and some childhood friends from the neighborhood to show their support.</p>

<p>Yana wasn&#x27;t idle either. Before the barbecue place opened, she was busy promoting it.</p>

<p>As a local who knew the area well, she didn&#x27;t just spread the word at the teahouse; she also got Minda&#x27;s property management company to help with publicity.</p>

<p>That evening, she also invited her card-playing partners and colleagues, adding over twenty more people to the crowd.</p>

<p>With around ten tables set up outside, plus the indoor seating and the private rooms upstairs, the place was packed to the brim on its very first day without even needing discounts or promotions.</p>

<p>The funny thing was, the mother and daughter were running around like chickens with their heads cut off, buzzing back and forth like busy bees, helping to bring drinks and serve food. If it weren&#x27;t for them, the northeastern couple would have been worked to the bone.</p>

<p>The amusing part was that when Luis and his wife Lily arrived, the place was so full there wasn&#x27;t a single seat left.</p>

<p>The grills couldn&#x27;t keep up with the orders, and they were even starting to run short on the pre-ordered beer and drinks.</p>

<p>Yana actually ended up paying out of her own pocket to buy some small side dishes from the grocery store to supplement the supply. It just went to show how enthusiastic the mother-daughter duo were about their new venture.</p>

<p>&quot;Well, we can come back tomorrow then!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing how busy it was, Luis and Lily decided not to add to the chaos. They grabbed a quick bite of some beef pancakes from a street vendor and went home to enjoy some time together.</p>

<p>Aside from the sex, it was the warmth and tranquility of their quiet life together that Lily loved the most.</p>

<p>After their bath, Luis dried her hair, and just that simple act alone made her feel deeply satisfied.</p>
