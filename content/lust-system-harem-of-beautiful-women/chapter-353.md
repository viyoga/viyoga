<p>On the other end of the phone, his wife Lily’s voice remained coquettishly sweet and gentle:</p>

<p>&quot;She has eaten. My mother absolutely forbids me from going out to eat any random junk food. I secretly called Maria.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;She and Aunt Yana are staying in another section. Just now, the three of us secretly sneaked out to eat. We agreed to keep this matter hidden from my mother.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Husband, you mustn’t tell either, otherwise I will get scolded.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis chuckled softly and said, &quot;I am not stupid. If I said anything, your mother, with her temperament, would scold me too, saying I do not know how to properly discipline you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;How annoying, how can you speak about an elder like that.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Alright, alright, I will not say it like that then. So, what did you eat out there that is so mysterious?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Promise you will not get angry after I tell you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Silly, when has your husband ever gotten angry?&quot;</p>

<p>Strictly speaking, this conversation was not particularly sweet or nauseatingly lovey dovey, but it was still a display of affection, flaunting their relationship. Sitting to the side, Leah listened blankly and felt even more uncomfortable.</p>

<p>&quot;We went to eat barbecue. My mother has always been against us eating that, but at that moment I was just desperately craving it.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;It is fine. Being able to eat well is the most important thing. It is just a pity your husband is not there with you, otherwise I would have taken you to eat it.&quot;</p>

<p>Luis deliberately acted as if he noticed Leah was still there. While chatting with his wife, he simultaneously continued sending messages to Leah:</p>

<p>&quot;What is wrong, Leah. It is nothing, you should go back and rest early. It is your brother in law’s fault. Clearly, something like that happened today, yet I still had lustful thoughts. Your brother in law apologizes to you, okay.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Do not pay any attention to that thing. It will go down on its own in a little while.&quot;</p>

<p>These incredibly considerate words, combined with Luis’s gentle gaze, made the conflict in Leah’s eyes even more intense. She still held her phone without replying to any messages.</p>

<p>&quot;Do not be angry. Your brother in law did not do it on purpose. Be good... your brother in law promised not to make things difficult for you or force you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You need to rest properly today. Do not worry either. Your brother in law and you share so many secrets, and this is just one of them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I will absolutely not tell your sister. You rest properly now, my lovely baby. Please do not look so full of worry, okay.&quot;</p>

<p>Testing the boundaries slightly, then employing a strategic retreat to advance, this was a rather scummy but effective method.</p>

<p>He was gambling on the fact that the rise in her favorability meter was not without reason. It must have reached a certain level before it increased. The previous approach to winning her over needed appropriate adjustments.</p>

<p>Sure enough, the moment Luis stubbed out his cigarette, he lay down.</p>

<p>Then he gave her a gentle smile, an apologetic smile, but he lay back casually, making the tent in his trousers even more conspicuous.</p>

<p>&quot;I was just really craving it. I ate so much. Aunt Yana even said my appetite has gotten bigger, and that since I am thin, eating more is good for me.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Husband, we went to eat near the university district. That eastern barbecue place Maria recommended was absolutely delicious and so cheap too.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;The best were the grilled beef tendons and the grilled beef brisket. Next time you come, I will take you there.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily was chattering happily, but to Luis, it sounded like something both wicked and delightful.</p>

<p>Luis already knew exactly which shop she had gone to. When he went to the capital, he secretly met up with Maria and they also ate at that same eastern barbecue place.</p>

<p>Sometimes, after having fun at the hotel, they would go eat at that place together. Besides the beef, they also had things like ox penis soup, which was truly excellent.</p>

<p>It seemed Maria had a bit of a mischievous streak, specifically taking his wife Lily to eat at that place. It was probably somewhat intentional, with a hint of sexual suggestion.</p>

<p>Looking at Leah still sitting there foolishly, her expression shifting between dark and light, she was likely feeling a tumultuous mix of emotions inside.</p>

<p>This should be considered ineffective.</p>

<p>After all, he was her brother in law. Hoping that she, still a virgin, would take the initiative to seduce him had a low chance of success, even if she was already experiencing internal conflict.</p>

<p>Luis beckoned to her. Leah hesitated slightly, seemingly a display, but after just a second, she obediently lay down in the crook of Luis’s arm.</p>

<p>Once again, he held her soft, warm jade like body in his embrace. She pillowed her head on Luis’s free right arm. Although his arm was somewhat immobilized, Luis could still gently caress her snow white neck.</p>

<p>Clearly, Leah shuddered slightly, but she did not reject him. Lying there meekly, she quietly let out a long breath.</p>

<p>The feeling in her heart instantly vanished without a trace. Leah felt ashamed, wondering if she was being too cheap. But then, looking at it from another angle, she felt that it really was not her brother in law’s fault.</p>

<p>Your brother in law is so good to you, and he is still so considerate at a time like this.</p>

<p>Am I being too excessive, enjoying your brother in law’s kindness while still being so affected and reserved.</p>

<p>Young girls are always sentimental and often prone to melancholy. Now, lying in the warm embrace, feeling the skin to skin contact, Leah thought of what happened that evening, her heart softening, filled with a sense of guilt.</p>

<p>These subtle changes in expression could not escape the eyes of that old, lecherous fox, Luis. Therefore, Luis only caressed her face and hair without any further moves, still maintaining the call with his wife, Lily.</p>

<p>&quot;By the way, husband, how come you are not asleep yet.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily could not help but ask, because Luis’s lifestyle had been quite regular before.</p>

<p>He had deliberately lowered the air conditioner temperature ever since his sister in law entered the room. Luis directly pulled the quilt over both their bodies, holding the soft, warm, fragrant body in his arms even tighter.</p>

<p>At this question, Leah became particularly apprehensive, looking at Luis with a pitiful expression.</p>

<p>Luis only hesitated slightly before saying, &quot;Do not even ask. Today was a bit unlucky. The light on the balcony was a bit broken tonight. I fell down while checking it, and my head happened to hit the side of the cabinet where the glass is stored.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah...&quot;</p>

<p>This exclamation came not only from his wife, Lily, but clearly also from Yana, and even included Maria’s surprised cry.</p>

<p>Not only was Leah stunned upon hearing this, but even Luis himself was taken aback. Yana and her daughter were actually right there nearby. Could it be they wanted to hear some gossip?</p>

<p>&quot;Ah, husband, are you alright.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily’s voice suddenly rose several decibels.</p>

<p>Leah’s expression immediately became tense. Even though her brother in law had already told a lie, she knew her second sister would definitely have this kind of attitude.</p>

<p>In the eyes of her love blinded second sister, her husband was a precious darling. If he so much as scraped his head or had rough hands, her second sister would feel particularly upset. Last time, when her brother in law had a slight cold, her second sister was so worried she could not sleep.</p>

<p>Although it seemed like an overreaction before, the appearance Lily now possessed beauty that was something only Luis could enjoy.</p>

<p>In the eyes of others, Lily was still that woman with an ordinary figure and plain, unremarkable looks. One could say her good personality was completely overshadowed by her physical flaws.</p>

<p>This was everyone’s view, at least it was Luis’s view before. Preconceived notions could sometimes be harmful.</p>

<p>But regardless, in terms of appearance alone, they were indeed mismatched. Now that Luis had money, even his mother in law, Ruth, felt a sense of crisis.</p>

<p>&quot;It is nothing, just a little blood on my head.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Ah... husband, you hit your head and it is bleeding. What did the doctor say. Maybe you should come to our hospital.&quot;</p>

<p>Lily was truly terribly worried.</p>

<p>As her tone became tense, Yana and her daughter on the other end also fell silent.</p>

<p>The most nervous one was still Leah. Her breathing halted, her beautiful eyes wide open, worried about whether her brother in law would help keep the secret.</p>
