<p>&quot;Moving into a new house?!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth face was full of shock.</p>

<p>&quot;Yes, a few years ago, my father-in-law&#x27;s village was demolished and relocated. When the residential land was reallocated, they used some connections to piece the plots together and built a small estate. It took two years, but it&#x27;s finally finished.&quot;</p>

<p>The reason Darlan had been so tight with money before was because he had poured all his funds and savings into his hometown, starting to prepare for his retirement life.</p>

<p>Back home, his sister-in-law was very relaxed, lying lazily on the sofa and giggling, &quot;My father-in-law is a male chauvinist and also superstitious. He calculated the date for a long time before settling on it.&quot;</p>

<p>Not only was it superstitious, but it was also very particular. There was even a gold-embossed invitation card, with the content written in brush calligraphy, making it especially formal.</p>

<p>&quot;My father-in-law also called Dad, and Dad asked me to come over and tell you.&quot;</p>

<p>Her mother-in-law said &quot;Oh&quot; and ran out. His wife, Lily, happened to be taking a shower upstairs. Luis immediately pounced and hugged his sister-in-law&#x27;s body, kissing her.</p>

<p>&quot;Stop it... aren&#x27;t you afraid your wife will find out!&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy struggled a little at first, but then she closed her eyes in ecstasy, wrapped her arms around Luis&#x27;s neck, offered her small, delicate tongue, and passionately entangled with her brother-in-law.</p>

<p>Their kiss, born of adulterous passion, was very lingering. They kissed each other until they were both breathing heavily. Luis held her, groping her all over with a lecherous smile, and asked, &quot;Tell me honestly, what have you and Deewa been up to lately?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Pfft, it&#x27;s a secret. You&#x27;ll find out later.&quot;</p>

<p>Nancy asked a bit jealously, &quot;Why are you asking about this? Have you had your fill of her and now you&#x27;re reminiscing, wanting to go to bed with her again?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Heh heh, how could she ever compare to you!&quot;</p>

<p>Seeing that she was jealous, Luis couldn&#x27;t help but feel secretly pleased.</p>

<p>After the threesome with the two sisters last time, Chloe had developed a taste for it and had recently been deliberately trying to please his wife, Lily. With Luis as the pivot, her relationship with Nancy had also become closer.</p>

<p>According to her little tidbit of gossip, it seemed his sister-in-law Nancy and her arch-rival Deewa had reconciled.</p>

<p>And it looked like they had become quite good friends, even close girlfriends. They appeared everywhere together, often going for beauty treatments, exercising to lose weight, and shopping at the mall together.</p>

<p>There must be a reason for such an unusual turn of events. Luis was very curious about what they were up to and why their relationship had suddenly become so good.</p>

<p>His sister-in-law snorted and went off to find her younger sister. She was now on even better terms with her sister, often coming back to keep her pregnant sister company, showing a bit of that &quot;eldest sister is like a mother&quot; attitude.</p>

<p>The time for the Taiga family&#x27;s housewarming was set for Friday evening. His father-in-law, Arthur, specially came over to discuss it.</p>

<p>His in-law, Darlan, was a very face-conscious person, the typical patriarch type. Since he had invited them this time, he naturally wanted to make a big deal of it and invite all his relatively good friends and relatives.</p>

<p>His wife, Lily, was pregnant and couldn&#x27;t be involved in any happy or sad occasions. Fortunately, she was going to the capital with Yana this weekend, so he wasn&#x27;t worried about her being bored.</p>

<p>As for his younger sister-in-law, Leah, she happened to miss it, but at her age, she didn&#x27;t like joining such events anyway. She wanted to go to Avery&#x27;s place this week, which was easy to arrange.</p>

<p>On Friday afternoon, Luis drove, carrying his father-in-law and mother-in-law. Following the address his sister-in-law gave him, he drove to a small coastal town bordering the capital and Bathek City.</p>

<p>This was not a popular tourist spot. The scenery was very beautiful, not backward at all. In the past, it was known as a place of outstanding talent.</p>

<p>A small village nestled against the mountain looked very wealthy. After a brief inquiry by phone, they drove to a luxurious manor-style house in the village.</p>

<p>&quot;In-laws, over here!&quot;</p>

<p>Darlan, dressed very formally, tall and big, stood at the entrance waiting, his face beaming with spring. He directed Luis to park the car inside the manor, having specially reserved a parking space.</p>

<p>Many cars were parked in the open space outside. Darlan immediately opened the car door and enthusiastically pulled Arthur, laughing, &quot;In-law, you&#x27;re finally here! Tonight, we&#x27;re not leaving until we&#x27;re drunk!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;And you, Second Brother-in-law, stay here tonight. This is your own home.&quot;</p>

<p>He had an especially good impression of Luis. After all, a patriarch likes to see unity among relatives, giving a sense of family strength and upward momentum.</p>

<p>Daniel had originally been an idle good-for-nothing, living off his parents. It was Luis who put up the money and got him to partner in starting the bar business.</p>

<p>Working at a nightclub might be disreputable, but running the business itself was a legitimate enterprise. That&#x27;s why he particularly appreciated this second son-in-law and had specially emphasized that Luis absolutely had to come and join the fun.</p>

<p>&quot;Come on, come on, let me show you around.&quot;</p>

<p>This manor covered a full eight acres. Apart from the three acres of residential land the family members had been allocated and pieced together, they had also spent money to buy an adjacent five-acre plot of sandy, stony land from the village committee.</p>

<p>This area, with high salinity and alkalinity and all sandy, stony land unsuitable for farming, was considered a poor place in the past. The land was plentiful and the population sparse, so each person was allocated more residential land, and the price wasn&#x27;t high either.</p>

<p>Three brothers, each with a three-story villa-style self-built house. In the courtyard now, the village chefs were already preparing. With such a large courtyard, they could easily host the banquet right at home.</p>

<p>&quot;Dad, Mom, Luis, you&#x27;re here.&quot;</p>

<p>Inside the ground floor of the villa, friends and relatives were chatting and laughing. Daniel, now a boss himself, was sensibly making tea and entertaining the guests, exuding quite a bit of that entrepreneurial aura.</p>

<p>&quot;You go ahead with your work. I&#x27;ll take your parents-in-law upstairs to have a look first!!&quot;</p>

<p>The ground floor consisted of a spacious living room and dining room. The Chinese-style solid wood furniture looked steady, dignified, and stylish.</p>

<p>Darlan and his wife&#x27;s bedroom was on the ground floor. In rural areas, this was the spot for the elderly&#x27;s room. Since Darlan wasn&#x27;t the eldest among the three brothers, the long-lived elderly grandmother didn&#x27;t live in this building.</p>

<p>The second floor had two master bedrooms and a small living room, simply and brightly decorated. The third floor was a half-floor with two guest rooms and a large balcony for drying clothes.</p>

<p>&quot;You&#x27;ll stay here tonight!!&quot;</p>

<p>Darlan said very enthusiastically, &quot;We&#x27;re all related by marriage. You wouldn&#x27;t deny me this little bit of face, would you? The bed sheets and quilts are all new and have been washed!!&quot;</p>

<p>With him putting it that way, Ruth agreed to stay.</p>

<p>The courtyard was meticulously arranged. After nightfall, the lighting was particularly beautiful, and by the time the banquet started, it was buzzing with noise and lively.</p>

<p>Nowadays, seating at rural banquets is basically arranged by family or seniority, and another thing is that men and women usually prefer to sit separately. The main reason is that women don&#x27;t want to smell cigarette smoke, hence this custom.</p>

<p>As night fell, the courtyard appeared luxurious and full of ambiance.</p>

<p>Dishes from the village banquet chefs were brought to the tables one by one. They weren&#x27;t common fare. The main course was steamed small lobsters, and abalone, coral trout, and everything you could think of were available.</p>

<p>The liquor was XO, and there were all kinds of drinks. It could be said the preparations were exceptionally lavish.</p>

<p>The elders sat at one table. Seating was primarily determined by seniority, and secondly by whose connection they were. Luis was arranged to sit at his brother-in-law Daniel&#x27;s table.</p>
