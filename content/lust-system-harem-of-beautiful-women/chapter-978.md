<p>It was past nine at night when they drove into the courtyard. Arthur’s pickup truck was already parked steadily.</p>

<p>The family got out of the cars one after another. As the eldest son-in-law, Daniel also came, helping carry big and small bags of things.</p>

<p>The living room was brightly lit. Grandma Wood, who usually slept early, was now also perked up. Arthur was brewing tea.</p>

<p>Hearing the noise, he immediately walked over. You could tell he was clearly a bit nervous. Suzanne was also very uneasy and immediately came up to help carry things, calling out obediently, &quot;Sister Ruth!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You all are early!&quot;</p>

<p>Greeting each other politely like strangers, and asking after Grandma Wood, Arthur immediately led the way.</p>

<p>First they came to the master bedroom. The spacious and bright master bedroom was designed very modern and warm, simple and bright, clearly also trying to cater to her preferences.</p>

<p>Ruth nodded in satisfaction, then pulled over the luggage she had brought and said, &quot;You all go recognize your own rooms first. Put your things in order first!!&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne eagerly said, &quot;Sister Ruth, let me help you organize.&quot;</p>

<p>She was quite attentive and immediately brought over a box of clothes hangers, all kinds available. One look and they were clearly not cheap goods.</p>

<p>&quot;No need!!!&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth did not want to be so intimate with her. She shook her head and said, &quot;Go do your own things!&quot;</p>

<p>Coming to the second bedroom, the symbol of Luis and Lily’s strength and status. Actually it felt like this room was even better than the master bedroom.</p>

<p>Arthur took people to each choose their rooms. Anyway they would not come to stay often so it did not matter. On each door they pasted their favorite pattern as confirmation of ownership. As for the keys, each person held their own.</p>

<p>Aside from the master bedroom, the four rooms here were just right for Nancy and Daniel to still sleep in separate rooms. Simply perfect.</p>

<p>Carrying big and small bags, each organized in their own room. Lily’s virtuous energy directly drove Luis out, saying do not stay there causing trouble.</p>

<p>Unwilling to give up, Suzanne returned to the master bedroom, watching Ruth busy back and forth.</p>

<p>Although guilty, she immediately squeezed on a smiling face and came forward saying, &quot;Sister Ruth, under your wardrobe is a new safe. You set the password yourself.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;These things, you handle them all.&quot;</p>

<p>Ruth turned to look. It was the land deed and all the procedure certificates for this house, and besides that there were also spare keys for all the doors of the entire house.</p>

<p>This was undoubtedly proof of being the head of the household. She was using this way to show weakness. Her careful and cautious appearance even looked a bit pitiful.</p>

<p>Ruth was placing an ashtray by the bedside. She turned her head and glanced over, saying, &quot;We do not often come to stay here anyway. What are you giving me these things for?&quot;</p>

<p>Suzanne said with a flattering smile, &quot;You are the mistress of the house. These things definitely have to be in your hands to be justified.&quot;</p>

<p>This sentence was quite pleasing to Ruth, and she did not decline either. She directly called Luis over to set the safe password for her, then stored everything inside.</p>

<p>After everyone finished organizing their personal belongings and pasted their own labels on the doors, what remained were the guest rooms.</p>

<p>Once they finished tidying up, they came to the living room one after another. Ruth affectionately accompanied Grandma Wood in conversation. Seeing that Ruth had not gotten angry, Grandma Wood obviously also let out a big breath of relief.</p>

<p>It felt quite harmonious and happy. Who knew whether Arthur would have the happiness of a harmonious wife and concubines and a house full of children.</p>

<p>A boy who looked thin and small walked out from the other side. Suzanne carefully glanced at Ruth, then took the boy’s hand and walked over.</p>

<p>Solicitously she said, &quot;Wes, hurry up and greet them.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Big sister, second sister, third sister... big brother-in-law, second brother-in-law, Auntie Ruth.&quot;</p>

<p>The little boy called out timidly, his face full of shyness and unease. One look and you could tell he was the introverted and reserved type.</p>

<p>Suzanne’s son, Wesley, looked like he had just started middle school, very tender. Wearing a pair of glasses, he was refined and gentle. One look and you could tell he was the good baby type.</p>

<p>His face was round, absolutely belonging to the cute and dorky shota type, the milky tender kind.</p>

<p>Luis and Nancy subconsciously looked toward Daniel. Damn, although they did not know why, it was just subconscious.</p>

<p>Sure enough, Daniel was sipping tea with his eyes lighting up. He smiled and nodded, the first to speak, &quot;Good boy!!&quot;</p>

<p>Luis felt a chill all over. Damn, Arthur’s family was truly unfortunate. The old man was responsible for playing with your wife and your daughters.</p>

<p>Now there was also Daniel catching up from behind, who might bend your son and stage a drama of playing with the brother-in-law. It was truly too evil.</p>

<p>Thinking this way... Old Wood’s family, this kind of inviting a wolf into the house, and the wolves even came in complete varieties, could also be considered a family misfortune.</p>

<p>Before long, a little girl wearing short-sleeved cartoon pajamas, also wearing glasses, walked out.</p>

<p>Her brisk footsteps were full of tender vitality. Her cute ponytail swayed along with her cheerful steps, especially adorable.</p>

<p>Her height was also the petite type. Her face was round with a bit of baby fat. Her features were delicate, especially her big eyes, which were so cute. She exuded an innocent and dorky feeling that made one’s eyes light up.</p>

<p>Her perky nose, cherry little mouth, she inherited her mother’s beauty genes. She actually looked a bit like Leah, which was truly something.</p>

<p>She was also wearing a pair of glasses, but the prescription should not be high. One look and you could tell she was the sweet good baby type.</p>
