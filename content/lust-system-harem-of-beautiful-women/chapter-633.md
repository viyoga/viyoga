<p>When it came to cheating, she herself held a deep-seated hatred for it, which was why her feelings for her husband had become a mere formality, and she only maintained the marriage for the sake of her daughter.</p>

<p>As a lawyer, she had grown numb after seeing so much gossip, but she still maintained a sense of mental purity.</p>

<p>When people around her got caught up in such messy affairs, she found it utterly disgusting and repulsive.</p>

<p>But when Luis got involved in something like this, she accepted it with equanimity, feeling it was only human nature, after all, her apprentice was so handsome and rich, it was only natural.</p>

<p>Subconsciously, she shared the same thoughts as most people, feeling that Lily didn&#x27;t deserve her apprentice, and that her apprentice becoming a live-in son-in-law like that was too much of a grievance.</p>

<p>So, when it came to Luis&#x27;s flirtatiousness, she kept reminding him to be careful, since Lily was, after all, a good woman suited to be a wife.</p>

<p>&quot;Hehe, Master&#x27;s wife, wouldn&#x27;t it be better if you helped cover for me more?&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Oh, go on, you&#x27;re counting on me to cover for you?&quot;</p>

<p>The journey was filled with relaxed chatter and laughter. When the car arrived at the community hospital, Lily, who had received a call in advance, was already waiting at the entrance.</p>

<p>She had taken the afternoon off, and as soon as she got in the car, she sweetly called out, &quot;Hello, Master&#x27;s Wife!!&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Lily, you&#x27;ve gotten even prettier. Oh, look at that belly showing now. It feels like your skin has improved a lot since you got pregnant.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;You must have some secrets to share with your master&#x27;s wife, can&#x27;t keep them all to yourself...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Not at all, Master&#x27;s Wife, you&#x27;re seeing things...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;My eyes are sharp as a hawk. Your master&#x27;s wife is past her prime. If you&#x27;ve got any special tricks, you&#x27;d better tell me now.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Master&#x27;s Wife, don&#x27;t tease me. You look so young, even more like a young girl than I do...&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Haha, what a sweet tongue. No wonder my foolish apprentice is so utterly smitten.&quot;</p>

<p>Laughing and chatting, they arrived at the Real Estate Registration Center. Earlier, Lily had been told it was something to do with business, that a property needed to be placed under her name for tax avoidance.</p>

<p>His beloved wife Lily had always trusted her husband completely, obeying his words without any doubt whatsoever.</p>

<p>Winslet busied herself with the paperwork, while Lily obediently signed page after page of documents without asking a single extra question.</p>

<p>Regarding the Delphi Business Consulting Company they had acquired earlier, she didn&#x27;t know exactly how Nina had handled it, but the issue of the circular debt had been resolved.</p>

<p>The property rights of Villa No. 1 in City Garden were used to offset the debt, and now this house had been placed under Lily&#x27;s name.</p>

<p>Initially, the idea was to put it under his fairy-like sister-in-law&#x27;s name, to earn more reserve points.</p>

<p>But upon further thought, it seemed a bit too risky. He had already spent a considerable amount of money on her, and the portion that his wife and mother-in-law already knew about was abnormally high.</p>

<p>Now to give away another villa...</p>

<p>Not to mention his shrewd mother-in-law, even his wife, who was usually carefree, would surely realize something was off between them.</p>

<p>Although she now had a bisexual disposition and held a permissive, supportive attitude towards Luis fooling around with other women.</p>

<p>But if the target were her own sister, younger sister, mother... he had no idea how she would react.</p>

<p>Now that his wife was heavily pregnant, Luis dared not seek the answer to that, dared not provoke her like that, so he gritted his teeth and dismissed the thought.</p>

<p>It happened to be his wife&#x27;s birthday soon, so consider it a gift for her.</p>

<p>Although she was now living with her head held high, she had always had a knot in her heart before.</p>

<p>The fact that her husband had become a live-in son-in-law and endured humiliation and grievance had actually been very hard on her deep down.</p>

<p>She had always dreamed of having a home with her husband, their own little love nest where they could make love without restraint, in the living room, in any corner, anytime, anywhere.</p>

<p>Besides, living under the same roof with her mother, younger sister, and older sister was also inconvenient.</p>

<p>Finishing the procedures took about half an hour, and they also signed a vehicle purchase agreement in the meantime.</p>

<p>From start to finish, Lily was exceptionally obedient, signing wherever she was told without asking a single extra word.</p>

<p>Walking out of the hall, Winslet said with a smile, &quot;Lily, you really are something, so well-behaved. If my apprentice were up to no good, who knows how he might take advantage of you.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;He wouldn&#x27;t!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily&#x27;s smile was bright and full of happiness.</p>

<p>Winslet paused for a moment, vaguely feeling an inexplicable twinge of envy.</p>

<p>&quot;Master&#x27;s Wife, let&#x27;s have dinner together tonight.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;I&#x27;m not as free as you big bosses. I have clients coming over later, I need to rush back.&quot;</p>

<p>Winslet checked the time and smiled, &quot;Next time, it&#x27;ll be your master&#x27;s wife&#x27;s treat. After I get through these next few days.&quot;</p>

<p>&quot;Okay, I&#x27;ll wait for your call then.&quot;</p>

<p>After bidding farewell to Winslet, Luis took his wife Lily&#x27;s hand and went for a walk in the nearby park. The young couple truly enjoyed these sweet, simple moments.</p>

<p>Compared to strolling through high-end malls and supermarkets, Lily much preferred going to the vegetable market with her husband. It was full of life, a constant reminder that the man she loved most was now her husband.</p>

<p>Even though she was usually shy and reserved, she didn&#x27;t hold back when haggling, showing quite a bit of her mother-in-law&#x27;s style.</p>

<p>As dusk fell, the young couple were the first to return home. They changed clothes and busied themselves together in the kitchen.</p>

<p>A peck on the cheek occasionally, a peck on the lips sometimes, their happiness was palpable. This was the sweet, blissful little life Lily had always yearned for.</p>

<p>At six-thirty, the older sister-in-law Nancy was the first to return home. She leaned against the kitchen doorway and said casually, &quot;I&#x27;m so hungry! Stop showing off your love and tell me, what&#x27;s there to eat?&quot;</p>

<p>Lily&#x27;s face flushed with shyness as she said, &quot;Hubby is cooking today. We can eat as soon as Mom gets back.&quot;</p>

<p>Mother-in-law Ruth returned shortly after. Her younger sister-in-law Eloise had gone back and didn&#x27;t come along, which Luis felt was a bit of a pity.</p>

<p>But for now, completing the task with his older sister-in-law was the top priority. The task line involving his mother-in-law could be delayed a bit without much issue.</p>

<p>The table was soon filled with dishes. Although they could afford high-end restaurants, the comfort of a home-cooked meal was also very satisfying.</p>

<p>Sparerib and bitter melon soup, mushroom and chicken stew, steamed mandarin fish fillets, garlic stir-fried mussels, plus a roast chicken and a plate of celery stir-fried with king oyster mushrooms made for an exceptionally sumptuous feast.</p>

<p>They chatted about mundane things while eating, the warm, harmonious atmosphere feeling especially wonderful.</p>

<p>After the meal, the usually lazy Nancy surprisingly volunteered to wash the dishes.</p>

<p>Perhaps feeling a bit guilty, she kept urging Luis to take her younger sister for a walk to help with digestion.</p>

<p>By the time the young couple returned from their walk, it was around eight o&#x27;clock. Luis was feeling somewhat anxious, after all, the 24-hour deadline was approaching at ten o&#x27;clock, and failing the task wouldn&#x27;t be good.</p>

<p>Although his fairy-like sister-in-law had already turned her heart towards him, completing this task shouldn&#x27;t be difficult.</p>

<p>But he worried about unforeseen complications. Besides, Luis wanted to push the task forward quickly to get more rewards, so naturally, he was burning with impatience.</p>

<p>Suddenly, the doorbell rang...</p>

<p>Everyone was a bit surprised. The family rarely had visitors, and besides, these days guests usually called ahead.</p>

<p>&quot;It&#x27;s Chloe!!&quot;</p>

<p>Lily smiled and ran to open the door. Standing gracefully outside was none other than Chloe, dressed sexily and looking stunning.</p>
