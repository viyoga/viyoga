<p>The riot cops pressed up against the border of Rotwood Manor like men trying to push through a hurricane. The enchanted spikes went off, sending bolts of energy snapping through their ranks. Several fell, but the rest pushed on, goaded by Candy’s dark control.</p>

<p>I could feel them pressing against the boundary of my Lair. It was like a hundred dull blades were being jabbed into my skin. Lorelei’s form fluttered as she grimaced in pain.</p>

<p>Skills flashed through the air as Ishikana laid into them. She knocked the cops off their feet by coating the ground in ice or pushing them aside with gusts of wind. But they just kept coming. The last few stakes fell as they reached the gate. Someone pulled out a pair of massive bolt cutters and went to work ripping apart the lock, as the rest tried to either climb the fence or walk around it through the woods.</p>

<p>Rallying, Lorelei and I sent out gouts of Hearth Flame. Shields melted and body armor ignited. Cindy’s control over some of them must have broken because the cops screamed and fell to the ground, trying to douse the flames. But for every one that fell, someone else rushed forward to take up their shields.</p>

<p>Something snapped. The gate broke and swung open. But rather than pouring forward, the cops ducked to the ground, lowering their shields.</p>

<p>Behind them were rows upon rows of hunters.</p>

<p>The mob opened fire with rifles, shotguns, and handguns. Staccato bursts lit up my vision with muzzle flashes.</p>

<p>“Guardian’s Stormwall!” I roared, violently pulling on my Lair’s mana.</p>

<p>A barrier of churning clouds appeared. The salvo struck the shield. For a moment I swore I could feel each impact slamming into my conjured Skill. The MP drain was enormous.</p>

<p>I released the Skill. The remaining cops were blown back, crashing into the hunters and knocking apart their formation.</p>

<p>“Brent, we need to fall back!” Ayara yelled, tugging on my arm.</p>

<p>Ishikana leapt to the trees, still slinging spells, while Lorelei vanished. I could sense her reappearing along the property line bordering Herb’s farm, pushing back another wave of attackers.</p>

<p>The brainwashed townsfolks and the remaining cops rushed through the broken gate. The fence around it broke as it was crushed by the sheer number of people rushing toward us.</p>

<p>But the Invader’s Bane took a bite out of their numbers. The low-level people only managed to stagger a few meters before collapsing, overwhelmed by the debuff. The rest pushed on, even as the Bane sapped their strength.</p>

<p>I turned and ran toward the first barricade. I heard Candy’s voice, twisted and dark, call out from the back of the crowd.</p>

<p>“Go!” she screamed, her voice rippling with unnatural command.</p>

<p>The mob’s ranks broke, all order forgotten, as they rushed to obey the Mind Leech. More fell, but others trampled over them to take their place.</p>

<p>We ran as fast as we could, the mob hot on our heels. Those of higher level raced ahead while the weaker townsfolk fell behind.</p>

<p>Off in the woods, the first of the traps went off. People screamed as they were suddenly lifted off their feet and hurled into the air, the legs snagged in silken snares.</p>

<p>Ayara skidded to a stop and put her hand against a metal plate nailed to a tree, charging the enchantment she’d laid into it. A moment later, the enchanted plates we’d hidden under the driveway began to burst, throwing up geysers of gravel and dirt. More stakes, randomly scattered across the woods, went off, snapping off bolts of stunning lightning.</p>

<p>Pendulous traps swung out of trees, smashing people off their feet. Others were blasted with gas traps, blinding and disorienting them. Several poor folks fell into pits full of sticky webbing.</p>

<p>Lorelei had thought of those particular traps and had worked hard with Ishikana to set them up. They weren’t entirely practical, but if nothing else all the bizarre and explosive traps slowed the townsfolk down.</p>

<p>The mind control hadn’t completely robbed them of their survival instinct. They did their best to avoid the obvious traps, only to fall prey to the more insidious hazards we’d thrown in their way.</p>

<p>More explosions rang out, and the woods were full of screams. Ishikana leapt from tree to tree, snaring more victims or blasting attackers with her spells. I could tell she was pulling her punches, unwilling to outright kill the brainwashed attackers.</p>

<p>Candy pushed them harder, sending even more people after us.</p>

<p>Overhead, the moon had almost completely overcome the sun. The halo glowed even brighter; the few escaping rays of light were shaded red with spectral blood.</p>

<p>We reached the first barricade, but it was instantly overrun. Bodies pressed against the walls, ripping out their stakes. Ayara and I abandoned it and kept running.</p>

<p><em>Come on, come on! </em>I mentally pleaded, hoping the bastards would fall for our ultimate trap.</p>

<p>The Drow Blacksmith armed another row of enchanted plates as soon as we cleared them. The townsfolks overran the barricade and detonated them a moment later. Dirt and dust filled the air.</p>

<p>Someone lunged at me from a cloud of debris.</p>

<p>Decker, his riot armor ripped and burned, swung his truncheon at my head. I barely twisted and blocked with the side of my blade. Even with Invader’s Bane leeching his strength, he was a level thirteen Warrior. My arm ached from the power of his blow.</p>

<p>Roaring mindlessly, he gripped the club with both hands. He brought it down in an overhead swing, intending to crack my skull.</p>

<p>Ayara smashed her mace into his ribs. His face went white, and he crumpled, gripping his side in pain.</p>

<p>I didn’t have time to say thanks as Penkle and the remaining cops came at us. I barely pulled up a Guardian’s Stormwall as one of them unloaded his service pistol at us. Knocking him off his feet with the Skill’s dispersal, I slammed the flat of my blade into another man's face, knocking him out cold.</p>

<p>Someone clubbed me in the shoulder. I fought back-to-back with Ishikana as townsfolk joined the melee. All around us was a press of bodies.</p>

<p>“Brent, do it!” Ayara shouted.</p>

<p>“Not yet!” I yelled.</p>

<p>Cindy wasn’t on the property. I could feel her and Mitch, standing at the mouth of the driveway, not yet daring to cross my borders.</p>

<p>I threw out a wave of flame. A gap formed in the crowd, and we dashed toward the second row of barricades. Someone caught me across the leg with a knife, leaving a long, deep gash. I kept running, pulling Ayara behind me.</p>

<p>We ducked behind the makeshift barrier. Reaching for stashed weapons, I grabbed a spear. I had no Proficiency with the thing, but Ayara said the basics were easy.</p>

<p>I ignited it and jabbed it forward, sending out a bolt of Hearth Flame. I nailed a cultist, one of the old DIK members, in the chest. He screamed in pain, batting at his robes.</p>

<p>Ishikana swung out of the trees and grabbed him. They vanished, but I could hear the man screaming in pain and terror.</p>

<p>Ayara had picked up a long club and was beating people back from the wall. To our side, I could see more people pressing through the woods. They were tripping and falling, miring in traps and the persistent debuff of Invader’s Bane. Those that made it to the driveway were tripped up by the safety fence.</p>

<p>Amongst their ranks, I could see phantoms of the past. Shadows of sacrificial victims being chased by DIK. There were other ghosts as well, all offering bloody sacrifices. They were afterimages of those foolish enough to be swayed by the terror imprisoned under the manor.</p>

<p>An explosion went off somewhere behind the house. Through our bond, I could sense Lorelei’s excitement, a cord of fear running below the thrill.</p>

<p>I knocked another attacker down, then ducked behind the barricade and grabbed a Healing Potion from my belt. It tasted like warm cough syrup, but I could feel the wound on my leg mending.</p>

<p>Standing back up again, I held the line with Ayara and Ishikana. The sky grew darker and darker.</p>

<p>Finally, the moon took its place in front of the sun.</p>

<p>Darkness and Death mana exploded up from the ground as the cry of the damned echoed across my Lair. People stopped in their tracks, their eyes wide, as a bloody halo hung impossibly large overhead.</p>

<p>At that moment, I felt Mitch and Candy step onto the property. Hideous power surrounded them, protecting them from the caustic effects of Invader’s Bane.</p>

<p><em>Not yet</em>! I forced myself to wait.</p>

<p>Someone jumped over the barricade. I lost my spear and Ayara hurled away her club, taking out another Cultist. We fell back further, not even bothering to use the remaining barriers we’d set up. There was no time.</p>

<p>Lorelei appeared overhead, guarding our escape as we ran around the driveway and up the house’s front porch.</p>

<p>The townsfolk staggered back on the attack, driven by Candy’s mental commands. A few poor fools tried to trudge through the overgrown lawn and vanished, falling victim to pitfalls and other traps.</p>

<p>But there were still hundreds of brainwashed attackers coming after us. They seethed down the driveway, knocking down the safety fence and crushing the barricades, overwhelming our defenses. They pushed up to the bottom of the porch stairs.</p>

<p>To the right of the crowd, I saw Josh and Herb. They were waving banners as they goaded the mob. The surviving cultists rallied to their position, preparing for their final assault.</p>

<p>Ishikana landed above us, clinging to the wall of the house. Her hair was disheveled, and ichor oozed from a crack along her abdomen.</p>

<p>“Brent, I believe it’s about time,” she called.</p>

<p>Her dart snapped out, piercing a Cultist that had drawn too close. She whipped the cord, throwing the man off into the woods.</p>

<p>Mitch and Candy appeared, the crowd parting before them. They walked like a conquering king and his queen, standing above the melee.</p>

<p>Mitch cackled. “It’s over!”</p>

<p>I grinned “It certainly is.”</p>

<p>Reaching out to my Lair, I selected my next upgrade.</p>

<p><b>Lair Upgraded – Vile Will’s Bane!</b></p>

<p><b>You Have Received a New Lair Quest! – Defeat the Cultist, Protect the Prison!</b><br><b>Reward: ???</b></p>

<p>I looked toward the townsfolk. “Welcome to Rotwood Lair, invited guests! Mitch, Josh, Herb, and you DIK assholes, get the fuck out!”</p>

<p>Candy gripped her head as she began to scream, a cry of absolute agony. Mana, dark and clotted, churned around her as she fell to her knees. Blood began to run out of her eyes and ears as her voice went hoarse.</p>

<p>The townsfolk collapsed, like puppets with their strings cut. Black vapor rose from their stunned forms and recoiled back toward the Mind Leech. Each one hit her like a blow, rocking her shuddering form.</p>

<p>Mitch grabbed his wife by the shoulders. “Candy? Candy! What’s happening? Candy!”</p>

<p><b>Vile Will’s Bane</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with resilient flames! Residents and guests of your Lair receive buffs to their mental fortitude, making them more resilient against mental attack Skills like Brainwashing or Dominate Will. The foes casting these Skills will suffer severe backlash.</em></p>

<p>Merle, who was about to take an ax to my legs, tripped and fell. When he got up, he looked utterly confused.</p>

<p>“Brent?” He glanced around, then yelped in shock when he saw he was holding a weapon. “Brent, what’s going on?”</p>

<p>It had been a risk, holding the upgrade to the last minute, but I had been afraid that Mitch and Candy would have never walked into our trap if the Mind Leech’s control had slipped the second her puppets had stepped onto the property.</p>

<p>Candy gave one last, strangled scream before she fainted.</p>

<p>Mitch looked up at me, furious. All around us, the only people standing were the surviving DIK Cultists. Virtually everyone else was on the ground crying or yelling in confusion. There were countless wounded folks or those left strung from the trees.</p>

<p>Despite the chaos, I couldn’t help but grin at Mitch. “It’s over.”</p>

<p>“No!” He balled his fists. “No, no, no!”</p>

<p>Josh came over to stand by his leader’s side. “Mitch?”</p>

<p>Before we could stop him, Mitch ripped a knife from his belt and plunged it into the other man’s chest.</p>

<p>A blood-stained smile spread across Josh’s face.</p>

<p>“Baphomet feeds!” the Cultist croaked.</p>

<p>Mitch, one hand still gripping the knife, pointed the other at us. Murderous power gathered around him. Overhead, the eclipse paused in place, locked in a perpetual, gory halo.</p>

<p>“Brent!” Ayara yelled as I shoved her out of the way.</p>

<p>I only had time to jump off the porch, tackling Merle to the ground, as the front of my house was consumed by a crimson explosion.</p>
