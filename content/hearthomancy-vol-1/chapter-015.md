<p>My mind was perfectly blank. Underneath the makeshift bed I had made, I could feel the ritual circles I had carved in front of the fireplace. I made a mental note to sand and fix the floor.</p>

<p>Lorelei hovered centimeters above the blankets. “That was amazing.”</p>

<p>I could only grunt dumbly, still unable to think.</p>

<p>She giggled. Absently, I reached out and took her hand.</p>

<p>Sadly, we couldn’t stay like that the whole night. My wounds had been healed, but I was still sore and tired from the day’s events. Sleeping on the floor, even with a few blankets for comfort, was a terrible idea.</p>

<p>Groaning, I got up and tugged Lorelei toward my bed. A white, flimsy nightgown formed around her as I slipped on some pajamas. My little cot wasn’t large enough to hold both of us, but the Hearth Spirit hovered above me like she was lying across my chest.</p>

<p>“Good night, Brent,” she whispered, kissing me again.</p>

<p>Without another word, I fell into dark slumber.</p>

<p>In my dreams, I was roaming a gloomy realm. Stone passages and chambers rose around me as shadows danced across the walls. But rather than being scared, I felt like I was at home, every feature of the twisting maze was intimately familiar, like I was walking the halls of my own home.</p>

<p>I wandered through the maze, like a master surveying his domain. Ghostly creatures played at the edge of my vision, but they always retreated as I turned my head.</p>

<p>“Brent! Come on, Brent, it’s time to wake up!”</p>

<p>The main hall was illuminated by rays of late morning sunlight. Lorelei was floating in front of the hearth; she was practically bouncing with excitement.</p>

<p>“Come on, come on!” She grinned. “You need to see this!”</p>

<p>Groaning, I rolled to my feet. Joining her, I was relieved to see that the hearth was back to its normal size. My house was mystical enough as it is, I didn’t need a fireplace big enough to incinerate a city bus.</p>

<p>Lorelei kissed me on the cheek. “Good morning, sleepy head! I was afraid you’d never wake up!”</p>

<p>I grabbed my phone and realized I had failed to set any kind of alarm. Half the morning was gone; I had inadvertently slept in after the tough day before.</p>

<p>The Hearth Spirit grabbed my arm and pointed at the fireplace. “Hey, look, look!”</p>

<p>For a moment I wasn’t sure what she was talking about, all I saw was the roaring mana that filled the hearth. As I gazed into the flames, I realized there was a System menu floating before me.</p>

<p><b>New Lair Updates Are Available!</b><br><b>Weapon Master's Boon</b><br><b>Spellcaster’s Boon</b><br><b>Crafter’s Boon</b></p>

<p>“What the heck?” I muttered.</p>

<p><b>Weapon Master’s Boon</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with martial flames! Combat Classes that specialize in martial combat, like Warriors, gain bonus progress to Class and Skill progress while fighting, sparring, or exercising within the boundaries of your Lair. They are also more likely to discover Evolutions, Fusions, and Inspirations.</em></p>

<p><em>“Take up arms, defend your homes! Let my flames bolster your strength!”—Faz’Ledor of House Fazon</em></p>

<p><b>Spellcaster’s Boon</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with arcane flames! Combat Classes that specialize in mystical combat, like Wizards, gain bonus progress to Class and Skill progress while fighting, sparring, or exercising within the boundaries of your Lair. They are also more likely to discover Evolutions, Fusions, and Inspirations.</em></p>

<p><em>“Magic is just a matter of breaking reality to your will.”—Rodrick, The Burning Effigy</em></p>

<p><b>Crafter’s Boon</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with ingenious flames! Crafter Classes of all types gain a bonus to Class and Skill progress while practicing their art within the boundaries of your Lair. They are also more likely to discover Evolutions, Fusions, and Inspirations.</em></p>

<p><em>“Light the forges, there is work to be done!”—Tibalt the Merry</em></p>

<p>I carefully reread them, my mind spinning. “What is all this?”</p>

<p>Lorelei clapped her hands. “This is the reward for all our hard work so far!”</p>

<p>I glanced at her. “Got any idea what all of this means?”</p>

<p>She closed her eyes and focused. The flames in the hearth rippled, and I felt the house’s mana flow around us as the Hearth Spirit called upon her intrinsic connection with my Lair.</p>

<p>“Rotwood Manor is alive, just like I’m alive, and all living things grow,” she said finally, opening her eyes. “As the Lair grows, it gains new bonuses, new abilities that you’ll be able to choose from. Look deeper into the flames, you can actually see the progress!”</p>

<p>Taking her advice, I peered into the roaring mana flames. Another new menu appeared, replacing the basic one I had seen before that tracked the Lair’s mana level.</p>

<p><b>Rotwood Manor</b><br><b></b><b>Hearthomancer Lair Lvl 1</b><br><b>Hearth – 100%</b></p>

<p><b>Boons: </b>None<br><b>Banes: </b>None</p>

<p>My heart raced at the crazy implications before me. Did growing my Lair grant me and Lorelei new abilities? The three Boons I’d been offered were insane, granting the kind of bonuses that people would kill for. Could we unlock more? And what did Banes mean? It was all too much.</p>

<p>“Hurry, you need to pick one!” Lorelei encouraged me.</p>

<p>“Why the rush?” I answered.</p>

<p>“Because the Lair can’t level up further till you make a choice,” she said firmly.</p>

<p>I looked at the list again. “So, I can only pick one? What happens to the other two?”</p>

<p>She closed her eyes, thinking, before answering. “They’ll still be there the next time the Lair levels up, so you can always pick them later!”</p>

<p>I carefully reviewed our options. Weapon Master’s Boon didn’t do much for us, neither of us was a Warrior. Spellcaster’s Boon would help us level our Skills and unlock new options. We had already set up a targeting range in the backyard, and I could see Lorelei and me grinding out even more progress, maybe even unlocking some sweet Skills in the process.</p>

<p>“Evolutions and Fusions,” I mused, before looking up the terms using my phone.</p>

<p>As I had suspected, Evolutions were straightforward. Like my change from Wizard to Hearthomancer, they allowed someone to essentially upgrade their options. The System tended to reward these upgrades based on personal experience. The harder you worked, the more you put yourself out there, the more likely you were to get Evolutions.</p>

<p>Fusions were a bit trickier. Humanity hadn’t unlocked the option, but the Drow had. As Ayara had mentioned, Classes, Skills, and Proficiencies could merge if the circumstances were right, becoming a more powerful combination.</p>

<p>I also checked Inspirations, they matched the phenomena that Randy, my wannabe Alchemist friend, had mentioned. They were flashes of brilliance that allowed Crafters to create something new, or for martial Classes to perfect some Skill or technique. They in turn could unlock new Evolutions and Fusions.</p>

<p>“Damn, they’re all good,” I said, going back to the list.</p>

<p>I glanced at the chalkboard, thinking of our three big goals. We had leveled up and become more powerful, but money was still an issue. Bottling Mana was profitable, and I still had my renovation work, but even together they’d take years to pay for all the repairs the house needed. Though not pressing at that moment, the mortgage also loomed over our future.</p>

<p>Theoretically, we could use Weapon Master’s and Spellcaster’s Boons to make money, renting out access to people who wanted to power level. But I didn’t like the idea of a bunch of strangers trampling onto my property.</p>

<p>But Crafters were guaranteed money-makers with huge, long-term profits, especially under the influence of my Boon. We’d need to construct proper facilities, but we could then ensure we got a cut for any goods produced on my property.</p>

<p>Plus, If I was going to invite people to come and stay with me, I preferred it to be people we knew, people we could trust.</p>

<p>Like a certain, sexy Blacksmith.</p>

<p>I had no clue if it was the logical choice or not, but it felt right to me.</p>

<p>“Crafter’s Boon,” I declared, mentally affirming my selection.</p>

<p>I felt a rush of power as the mana that had been building in the hearth suddenly burst outward.</p>

<p><b>Lair Upgraded – Crafter’s Boon!</b></p>

<p>“Alright!” Lorelei cheered, hugging me. “Let’s do this! We’ll become the best crafter’s haven in existence.”</p>

<p>A new notification popped into my vision.</p>

<p><b>You Have Received a New Lair Quest! – Recruit a Crafter!</b><br><b>Rewards: Lair Progress</b></p>

<p>“You know what this means?” Lorelei asked excitedly.</p>

<p>I chuckled. “I think I do.”</p>

<p>The Hearth Spirit pumped her fist in the air. “Let’s go recruit ourselves a Drow Blacksmith!”</p>

<p>I paused, experiencing a flashback of the night before.</p>

<p>Before she could go in for a High Five, I grabbed the Hearth Spirit and hugged her close.</p>

<p>“Brent?”</p>

<p>“I just wanted to take a moment to say I love you,” I answered simply.</p>

<p>She melted into me. “I love you too!”</p>

<p>“My life has become so much richer, so much better with you in it,” I told her, releasing my hold so I could gaze at her.</p>

<p>She tilted her head and grinned. “But—?”</p>

<p>“But.” I sighed. “Sorry, I just felt weird. We just had our first night together—a fantastic night—and here I am already thinking about inviting another woman to live with us. And yeah, she’s hot, I’m attracted to her, but I’d never—”</p>

<p>“Shh—listen.” Lorelei put her finger over my lips. “I know how you feel. You and I are bound together, now and forever, and nothing will ever change that.”</p>

<p>She drifted out of my grasp. “But don’t mistake me for a mortal woman. I am a personification of this house, its history, and its potential. I want what the house wants, and I want to see this place continue to grow, to prosper.”</p>

<p>Currents of mana flowed around the room, circling around her form. “And like I said, I’m not the jealous type. If you end up filling the house with beautiful, capable women, I’ll be overjoyed, because their strength will become the house’s prosperity.”</p>

<p>She floated down, then gave me an impish grin. “Besides, even I have to admit, Ayara’s ass looked amazing in those leggings.”</p>

<p>With a snap of her finger, her outfit changed, mimicking the one the Drow had been wearing, except it was pure white. She gave me a little twirl, proving that she was no slouch in the appearance category either.</p>

<p>“I think I’ve died and gone to heaven,” I said, realizing just how damn lucky I was.</p>

<p>“Maybe you have? Maybe this house will become our little heaven on Earth.” The Hearth Spirit drifted back into my arms. “But don’t get ahead of yourself. The Blacksmith has the hots for you, no doubt, but you haven’t even invited her over yet. You’re better off taking one step at a time.”</p>

<p>Grinning, I kissed her again.</p>
