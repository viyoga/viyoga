<p>“This is so exciting!” Lorelei chirped the next morning as we gathered in front of the fireplace.</p>

<p><b>Rotwood Manor</b><br><b></b><b>Hearthomancer Lair Lvl 2</b><br><b>Hearth – 100%</b></p>

<p><b>Boons: </b>Crafter’s Boon<br><b>Banes: </b>None</p>

<p>Ayara smiled as we all stared into the hearth’s flames. “I’ve never been present for a Lair upgrade.”</p>

<p>I was glad Ayara could see the Lair’s stats. I wanted her input on what Boons or Banes I chose since my decision would affect us all.</p>

<p>The Blacksmith was documenting everything on her phone. Her Scholar friend had been surprisingly eager to help, especially once Ayara revealed I had unlocked a new Class for Humanity. Ishikana had requested that we document all our interactions and any strange happenings. In turn, she had promised to help us unravel the mysteries of Hearthomancy and Rotwood Manor.</p>

<p>I was a bit wary of sharing information, but Ayara swore again that her friend was trustworthy.</p>

<p>“Ishikana would never betray us. House Druxsellaic owes much to her, and I personally owe her even more,” the Drow Blacksmith repeated as I queued up our options. “It takes a bit to pique her interest, which says a lot considering how quickly she replied and agreed to help us.”</p>

<p>“Sounds like a good person to know,” Lorelei admitted.</p>

<p>“She’s strong too,” Ayara added. “I don’t know her exact level, but it’s got to be in the fifties or sixties for her primary Class, and her secondary can’t be that far behind.”</p>

<p>“Damn,” I muttered.</p>

<p>Even if she didn’t possess a combat-related Class, fifty-odd levels of stats would make the Scholar a force to be reckoned with. Ayara explained that she had a large collection of Skills as well, further expanding her prowess.</p>

<p>The first few emails we’d received from her had been pointed, full of questions concerning my stats, Skills, and the history of Rotwood Manor itself. There had been a slew of questions for Lorelei as well.</p>

<p>The Scholar had made promises of additional aid beyond answering questions about my Class and how the System worked. I decided to trust Ayara’s faith in the strange woman.</p>

<p>“Anyway, Boons and Banes!” Lorelei cheered.</p>

<p>Focusing back on the flames, I looked through our new options.</p>

<p><b>New Lair Boons and Banes Are Available!</b><br><b>Weapon Master’s Boon</b><br><b>Spellcaster’s Boon</b><br><b>Bountiful Grounds Boon</b><br><b>Invader's Bane</b><br><b>Vile Will’s Bane</b></p>

<p>I dutifully brought up Weapon Master’s Boon and Spellcaster’s Boon so Ayara could copy down their descriptions. When she was ready, I brought up the three new options.</p>

<p><b>Bountiful Grounds Boon</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with plentiful flames! When gathering resources via Mining, Farming, Foraging, or similar Proficiencies, all Classes are likely to discover additional resources. They are also more likely to find rare or valuable resources.</em></p>

<p><em>“All things came from Fire. The Fire provides all.”—Gospel of The Forgotten Sect: Book 1 Verse 1</em></p>

<p><b>Invader's Bane</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with vigilant flames! Intruders and uninvited foes suffer a crippling debuff that will slowly sap their vitality and lower their stats while within your Lair.</em></p>

<p><em>“You thought you could slip in, like a thief in the night? I shall take great joy in placing your head on the spikes above my gates!”—Cedric The Hated</em></p>

<p><b>Vile Will’s Bane</b></p>

<p><em>Your hearth burns with resilient flames! Residents and guests of your Lair receive buffs to their mental fortitude, making them more resilient against mental attack Skills like Brainwashing or Dominate Will. The foes casting these Skills will suffer severe backlash.</em></p>

<p><em>“My mind is as much of a fortress as the walls around us.”—Prescilla, Prefect of The Lonely Bastion</em></p>

<p>“Have you heard of Banes before?” I asked Ayara as we reviewed the options.</p>

<p>“No, but I can ask Ishikana, she might know more,” she said, dutifully taking notes. “I can ask her about those quotes too. I’ve never heard of the System offering them before, but we’ve now seen them several times when tied to your Class or Lair.”</p>

<p>I wondered if the people quoted were real. <em>Were they Hearthomancers like me, from other worlds, other realities?</em></p>

<p>Lorelei squinted as she reread our options. “Which one should we pick?”</p>

<p>Bountiful Grounds Boon seemed like another homerun if we wanted to make money, but I wasn’t sure about the whole Mining thing. <em>Were there seams of metal or coal under the property? How would we even get to them? Still, if we rebuilt the atrium—</em></p>

<p>My thoughts were interrupted by a disturbance in the flow of mana around the property.</p>

<p>“More company,” I growled. “Okay, I’m updating the chalkboard. We’re setting up a gate.”</p>

<p>Tromping outside, I was surprised to see it wasn’t an annoying neighbor or the cops approaching, but Merle the county inspector. He pulled up his car and gave me an apologetic look as he got out.</p>

<p>“Morning, Brent,” he called.</p>

<p>“Morning,” I answered. “What’s going on, Merle?”</p>

<p>He ran a hand through his thinning hair. “I’ve received reports of questionable activity on your property, so I decided to come check them out in person.”</p>

<p>I bit back a growl. <em>First the cops, now the county building inspector? These bastards don’t let up!</em></p>

<p>Taking a moment to rein in my anger, I asked, “Questionable activity? What does that mean?”</p>

<p>Merle grabbed a pile of paperwork and his tablet from the passenger seat of his car. “We received an anonymous tip that you were renting out rooms, and that you were making illegal modifications and repairs to the property.”</p>

<p>I could feel my anger getting the best of me. “Anonymous?”</p>

<p>“Someone sent them in via email. They raised such a stink that I’m out here on a Sunday.” Merle shrugged. “I’m legally obligated to check them out.”</p>

<p>I stepped aside and gestured toward the front door. “Let’s get this over with.”</p>

<p>“Sorry again,” he murmured as he entered the manor.</p>

<p>Lorelei and Ayara were already in the foyer. They’d overheard the conversation. Lorelei was fuming, clearly as upset as I was by the false accusations. Ayara wore a harsh expression.</p>

<p>Merle nodded to the Hearth Spirit, then turned to Ayara. “Hello, and who might you be?”</p>

<p>“Drax’Ayarellum of House Druxsellaic,” she responded stiffly.</p>

<p>“Relax, Ayara.” I put a hand on her shoulder, my words for her as much as myself. “This might all be bullshit, but Merle isn’t a bad guy. He’s just doing his job.”</p>

<p>The inspector cleared his throat. “Can I ask you if you’re renting a room here?”</p>

<p>“No. I’m staying here with Brent, he’s my boyfriend,” she answered, leaning down and giving me a quick kiss on the lips.</p>

<p>Merle panicked, giving a fleeting glance at Lorelei, but the Hearth Spirit just laughed at his discomfort.</p>

<p>“But I thought—” the inspector stammered, before regaining his self-control. “Yes, so you’re not paying rent, Ms.—Ayara?”</p>

<p>“No, Brent wouldn’t let me,” the Blacksmith answered.</p>

<p>“Okay, well that’s one thing ticked off,” Merle said, pulling out his tablet. “Brent, can you show me your most recent work?”</p>

<p>We took Merle on a full tour. He carefully inspected the wiring work Frank and I had done, along with the kitchen setup. Heading down to the basement, he then double-checked the electric service panel, the plumbing, and the water boilers.</p>

<p>“Yup, Frank was right, this would never pass inspection,” Merle said, tapping a pen against one of the water boilers. “It’s not a fire hazard, but you’ll need to replace these things before I’m able to fully sign off.”</p>

<p>“Got it,” I growled, my anger returning.</p>

<p>I knew exactly who had sent the anonymous tip. Hell, I wouldn’t have been surprised if Herb eventually reported me as the intruder on his farm. Considering the cops had just been there the day before, it all stank of my neighbors putting a coordinated effort to drive me out.</p>

<p>They were creating a paper trail of complaints. Even if the cops and Merle found nothing wrong, the fact that complaints had been made and investigated would be enough to use against me.</p>

<p>We gave Merle a tour of the backyard and Ayara’s new forge.</p>

<p>“All up to the fire codes,” I assured him as he checked the site.</p>

<p>Merle admitted to being more interested in her craft than checking for potential violations. He joined us as the Blacksmith resumed work on her sword, crafting a crossguard and hilt for her new weapon.</p>

<p>“Sorry about all of this, Brent,” Merle said as we escorted him back to his car an hour later. “I’ll go ahead and brush this off for the moment, but if I get another report I’ll have to come back out.”</p>

<p>“Got it.” I shook his hand. “Have a nice day, Merle.”</p>

<p>Lorelei and I watched as the inspector drove off.</p>

<p>“I think I know what option we should pick for our Lair upgrade,” she said darkly.</p>

<p>Heading back inside, I made a beeline for the hearth and then made my decision.</p>
