I headed to the Hitel School's Spatial Garden in Neo London's B District. Once you become a member of the Hitel School, it's a place you have to visit at least once.

,Since it's B District, even the atmosphere feels well-organized.'

Everyone walking around the streets is an intellectual equipped with neat attire and manners. Truly befitting of Neo London's top-tier district.

I wore a coat with the Hitel School's emblem and walked confidently down the street. Some people glanced at me, but no one stopped me. The London Guards working in B District bowed their heads when they saw me.

It didn't feel like they were greeting me out of genuine respect, but more like they were trying to avoid getting involved in troublesome matters.

,Not a bad feeling.'

I arrived at the Hitel Spatial Garden.

On the outside, it looked like an old mansion protected by high walls.

But in reality, it's a special space with hundreds of overlapping spaces. It's said that the entire size of the Spatial Garden is comparable to one district.

I headed to a small building like a ticket booth at the entrance of the Spatial Garden. It's where they sell admission tickets.

If an ordinary person enters the Spatial Garden without a ticket, they'll almost certainly get lost within 10 minutes. Once you get lost, it's impossible to escape without the help of a space wizard. The ticket also serves as a navigator, so ordinary people must purchase it.

,In the game, it was depicted like an infinitely looping backroom.'

I entered the ticket booth. The Hitel School wizard saw my clothes and stood up from his seat.

"You must be Eugene Meyer-ssi, who recently joined the Hitel School? I heard from Orsia-nim that you would be coming today. I have your school ID ready here."

I received the school ID he handed over.

"Does this school ID serve as an admission ticket?"

"That's right. It's not really necessary for us space wizards, but... Meyer-ssi is still a newbie, isn't he? You're probably not familiar with space magic yet. Be careful not to lose it. Ah, if you follow the direction the school ID points, you should be able to get to Orsia-nim's workshop. To receive an invitation from Orsia-nim... I'm personally very envious."

"Orsia-nim seems to be quite popular."

"There isn't a single wizard within the school who dislikes Orsia-nim. She possesses a talent that's on a different level, so it's hard to feel petty jealousy. Although she has a cold personality, she's calm and fair. I've never seen a woman as beautiful as Orsia-nim. Her intelligence shines as well, making her close to perfection...."

Praise for Orsia kept pouring out of the wizard's mouth. I gave a rough greeting and exited the building, entering the Spatial Garden.

"......."

In an instant, the space changed. To a vast space paved with sidewalk blocks. Green flowerpots were placed next to the sidewalk blocks. I checked the school ID. An arrow that hadn't been there until just now was pointing straight ahead. As I walked forward, more than ten forks in the road appeared.

,Come to think of it, you could capture Void Bugs in the Spatial Garden.'

Void Bugs weren't creatures I needed right now. In the first place, the utility of Void Bugs is limited. I brushed off the knowledge that came to mind and headed to my destination.

Orsia's workshop was a three-story detached house. It seemed too ordinary for a 7th-circle wizard's workshop.

,It's a space wizard's workshop. The really important place must be hidden with space magic. There's no way she'd invite another wizard to her workshop otherwise.'

Click.

The front door opened, and Orsia came out.

"Please come inside. I'll serve you some tea. Is black tea alright with you?"

"I like black tea."

In Neo London, you'll receive scornful looks if you say you dislike black tea. Orsia brewed the black tea quite well. I heard about the Hitel School's rules from her. There weren't any particularly troublesome rules. I also heard about the school's history and such.

"Ah, may I use the restroom for a moment?"

"It's at the end of the hallway."

I got up from my chair and headed to the hallway. In the process, Orsia moved her body as far to the side as possible to avoid me. I was a little heartbroken. She had been trying to maintain her distance from me all day.

,Am I dirty? Do I smell bad or something?'

I'm quite confident in my cleanliness.

I checked myself in the clean restroom. There was nothing wrong.

When I returned from the restroom, four thick books were placed on the table.

Hitel School's Basic Textbook, Introduction to Space Studies, History of Hitel, Storm of Space and Time.

"...Orsia-ssi. What are those books?"

"They're good books to read once. I'll lend them to you, so please read them."

"Um. I understand."

I took the books for now, but I couldn't guarantee when I would read them. I just don't reach for books that aren't magic books.

"I thought I would at least exchange greetings with the school head today, but it seems like the school head isn't here, just like at the Leison School."

"It seems like it's a time when academic conferences are frequently held overseas these days... It will be difficult to see the school head in person. I can give you their contact information if you wish, though."

"No. It's alright."

I then set the mood and said to her.

"Orsia-ssi. You said you would grant me three favors last time."

"Yes. If it's a favor I can grant, I will."

"My first favor is that I want to learn the basics of space magic from Orsia-ssi."

Learning the basics of space magic directly from the Hitel School's official successor. An opportunity that can't be obtained even with a fortune.

"I understand. However, the basics of space magic are vast. If you try to learn the basics without a goal, it could take years. Both you and I are short on time, so... it would be good to have a goal."

"The space magic I want to learn the most is subspace magic. Aiming for subspace magic... is that possible?"

"That's not a difficult goal. With your talent, it should be possible within a few months."

4th-circle space magic, subspace.

It's a tricky magic, but with the help of a space wizard, even an ordinary wizard can possess a subspace.

"I have a second favor as well. I would like you to help me explore an ancient ruin for about two weeks."

"...That's an appealing proposal as a wizard. But is there a reason why you need two weeks?"

"The ancient ruin is far away. I plan to take the underwater train. Of course. I'll cover all the expenses."

Orsia frowned and pondered. After a while, she opened her mouth.

"...What kind of ancient ruin is it?"

"It's an ancient ruin of Great Korea that has never been discovered before."

"...An ancient ruin that has never been discovered before.... That in itself is attractive."

"I'll give you 30% of the items obtained from the ancient ruin. In return, I would like you to give me priority."

"We're not going alone, are we? Ancient ruins have a high probability of having traps and monsters."

"Yes. I plan to hire an expert in that field. Since there's a huge profit at stake, three people will be exploring."

Ancient ruin exploration. If too many people gather, problems will definitely arise. Especially with a hastily formed exploration team? It would be strange if problems didn't arise.

Orsia remained silent for about 3 minutes before answering.

"I understand. I'll help you. I'll have to use the vacation I've been putting off this time."

"Thank you so much, Orsia-nim."

• • •

To hire the third member of the ancient ruin exploration team, I visited Rose, the head of X Manpower Agency.

Rose let out a hollow laugh after hearing my words.

"Ah, so. You want a mercenary of at least 5th-circle who has the insight to recognize ancient ruin traps, the skill to perform as a vanguard when monsters appear, and who will never betray you?"

The requirements are a bit shameless.

A mercenary who will never betray you. In the mercenary world, they're practically a phantom species.

Stories of mercenaries betraying their employers due to greed aren't even gossip in this world. From the mercenary's perspective, they can just take a big score and disappear. There's also the method of disguising the employer's death as an accident and stealing their assets. The dropped credibility will slowly recover, and it's profitable to swallow the employer's massive wealth in one go.

"I'm asking for a favor."

"I happen to have the talent you want. I have a contract with them, so they can't betray you. But the price is quite high."

"I can afford it."

"You can't afford it with the money you made from the chicken business."

"...I have funds whose source I can't reveal."

"Then there's an additional fee. I have to launder it too."

"It's alright. I saved the money to use when I needed it."

Not only hiring a mercenary, but the expenses going out for underwater train tickets and such weren't cheap. I had to spend more than 80% of the money I had saved so far.

It would be a lie if I said it wasn't painful. But you have to spend when you need to. It's not like I'm throwing money away on gambling. This is a perfect investment. I can get several times, several dozen times more profit.

"Come back tomorrow. If that mercenary accepts, I'll let you meet them. The money going in isn't small, so you want to see them in person and make a judgment, right?"

"I understand."

And the next day.

I faced the cyber ninja, Invisible Blade. Her face was covered with a fox mask, and her hair was tied in a black-brown ponytail. The silver cyber suit revealed her voluptuous figure without reservation.

A ninja who's supposed to act secretly, yet her attire definitely attracts people's attention.

"Long time no see?"

Invisible Blade shoots out a sharp gaze and waves her hand. I was nervous. I can't say we have a good relationship.

Instead, I asked Rose, who was next to me.

"Are there no other mercenaries? I can hardly trust that woman."

"I don't know what happened between you two, and I don't care, but she's the only mercenary who meets the conditions you require. If you want to find another mercenary, it'll take months."

"......."

I looked at Invisible Blade. Wouldn't she betray me at a very important moment?

Perhaps she noticed my worries. Rose continued.

"Even though her attire is like that, she's a capable ninja. She immediately catches most traps, and she's excellent in close combat. There's no need to worry about her betraying you because she's involved with me."

"......What's your opinion?"

I asked Invisible Blade.

"I haven't had any requests lately, so my body was getting rusty. Ancient ruins would be perfect as a warm-up. Ah, there's a condition. I get to keep some of the items obtained from the ancient ruin. In return, I'll cut the hiring fee by 30%."

"...I have priority over the items."

"Well, since you're the one who discovered the ancient dungeon and you're covering the expenses.... I'll concede that much."

Anyway, the members are assembled.
