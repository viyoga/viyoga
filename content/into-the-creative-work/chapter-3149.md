Doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo!

From Mr. Chwe's severed neck, the queue-helmet spun like rotor blades. Unlike the body, the head refused to fall, hovering in midair and homing in.

"Even for this sovereign, that was a blade at the very极限 of speed—hard to react to! Yet there was no weight behind that sword at all! You merely swing with brute force, following a predetermined form!"

"You talk an awful lot for a severed head."

"Such insolence toward your elder. A martial artist always keeps a trump card hidden!"

"Now you're invoking Confucian propriety? And you're a lich before you're a martial artist."

Mr. Chwe moved his dark mana as if my words meant nothing to him. Dark mana converged from all directions toward his head.

"Take this! Black Laser!"

A black magic circle drew itself before his mouth, and a black laser shot forth. He'd realized curses didn't work on me and was attempting a physical attack.

Sharp instincts for an old bastard. Absolute authority means nothing before pure physical force.

[Memorize: Death Ray]

A dark crimson laser pierced through the black laser and annihilated Mr. Chwe's head.

Death Ray is a 6th-rank Arcane spell. Even against incorporeal targets, it erases anything of lower rank on contact. Its power is enough to rival 7th-rank magic.

However, even Death Ray cannot completely annihilate Mr. Chwe. His life vessel—the very essence of a lich's existence—isn't here.

Dark mana and nanomachines converged to a single point in the air. They soon became Mr. Chwe's head. He'd pushed himself to regenerate his head as fast as possible, even at some cost.

"My servants! Come forth! Protect your sovereign, and kill my enemies!"

Jiangshi appeared from the sky, the ground, the walls. Along with summoning the majority of his jiangshi, he'd called together the ones scattered throughout the dungeon.

Dozens of jiangshi became energy and were absorbed into Mr. Chwe.

He was recovering by absorbing the jiangshi through his unique skill, Devour the Dead. In an instant, Mr. Chwe regenerated his entire body and extended a hand into the air. A magic nunchaku spun round and round into his grasp.

"What you face is infinity itself! You cannot defeat this sovereign here! Abyoooo!"

Mr. Chwe swung his nunchaku and closed in on me. Hundreds of jiangshi charged in from all sides as well.

"Chinese types really do love their bluster and exaggeration. Infinity? The moment I heard that word, I knew instinctively. You're running out of tricks."

I raised the hand not gripping the Vajra Divine Thunder. Golden lightning flickered at my fingertips.

[Memorize: Chain Lightning]

[Spell Dispersion]

[Attribute Grant: Holy]

[Trait Grant: Attack Transfer]

The lightning split into five branches, each extending in a different direction and reaching the jiangshi. The lightning chained, racing between the jiangshi. The charging jiangshi halted, then crumbled to ash.

Mr. Chwe was electrocuted as well, but unlike the lesser jiangshi, he didn't turn to ash.

"Spamming magic like that! I can hear your mana rod breaking from here! Abyoooo!"

I deflected the incoming nunchaku with my lightsaber.

"Worry about your own future before my mana rod. Got anything else to pull out?"

"How long do you think that golden sorcery will last!"

Gu-gu-gu-gu-gung.

The ground began to shake. The vibrations grew closer and closer to me. My gaze dropped downward, staring at the floor.

Kwaaaaaaaaah!

From beneath the earth, a massive corpse earth-dragon covered head to tail in talismans burst upward, jaws gaping wide to swallow me whole in one bite.

[Memorize: Frost Horn]

Jjeoooong!

The ultimate cold exploded outward around me.

The earth-dragon's massive maw froze solid, unable to close.

It wasn't over. From within the earth-dragon's throat, a massive skeletal hand shot out, trying to grab me.

Thunder Heaven Style, Lightning Flash.

I swung my sword.

A slash wreathed in golden lightning split both the skeletal arm and the earth-dragon in half simultaneously.

From beyond the earth-dragon's corpse, where my vision had been blocked, Mr. Chwe—who'd been watching for an opening—charged in, attempting close combat.

"Abyoooo! Hao! Hao-hao-hao!"

Mixing in feint kiais, he unleashed a flurry of nunchaku strikes, punches, and kicks.

Kkirik, kkiriririk.

My mind-controlled body evaded every one of his attacks. Feint kiais? When the attacks themselves look slow as a turtle, how could they possibly work?

My sword cut him, my fist struck him.

"That's it! Count! Fuck yes, that's right! Throw that uppercut! Turn that bastard into a bloody pulp—no, a nanomachine pulp!"

I ignored the bullshit being shouted from somewhere far off.

I cut and struck Mr. Chwe.

I focused solely on that simple, straightforward act. Mr. Chwe tried to create distance and break away partway through, but I didn't stop. In less than a minute, Mr. Chwe was destroyed and revived twelve times. The more he was destroyed, the slower his regeneration became.

"Krrrgh! Emergency escape!"

He abandoned his torso and activated his queue-helmet at maximum output, sending only his head flying to create distance.

[Lightning Spear]

The lightning I fired chased down the fleeing head and smashed it apart. His head fell for a moment, then recovered and flew back up again.

"…Your current self is overwhelmingly powerful, beyond what this sovereign can handle. However, you are not beyond defeat. Behold this sovereign's final masterpiece…! Myriad Phenomena Unification!"

Black mana and nanomachines spread in all directions from his head, then rained down toward the ground like droplets. The jiangshi and shattered house debris that made contact with them floated into the air, converging in one place to take the form of a giant.

'Using nanomachines as a medium to temporarily convert even inorganic matter into undead. And gathering it all in one place makes a rag golem. This is a pattern that didn't exist in the original game.'

It must be a form of vision magic. Yet its essence is not that of a corpse. It cannot be maintained for long. Even so, I cannot simply stand by and watch. That rag golem will last far longer than the puppet needle's effect.

"Merge!!"

Mr. Chwe's head flew off and embedded itself in the giant's forehead. At last, the colossal rag golem was complete.

Thunder Heaven Style — Lightning Flash.

I swing my sword and unleash a slash. It's useless. Against a giant over fifty meters tall, it amounts to nothing more than a scratch. Worse still, it absorbed its own corpse parts through Corpse Devouring, healing even that tiny wound.

'I need to destroy it in one strike.'

A memory surfaced.

The spellbook of German, court mage from 130 years ago, bestowed upon me by the Queen.

Recorded within that spellbook was an eighth-rank lightning-element spell.

'Normally I wouldn't dare attempt it, but with my current self, it's possible.'

I calculate the formula.

Kkiririk, kkiriririk!

Astrals, the spirits within me stimulated by the puppet needle, compute the formula, and hundreds of mana rods process it.

My brain and mana rods feel as though they're burning.

I glare at the rag golem's center. More precisely, I am perceiving what lies within it.

"What do you intend to do? Go on, try it if you can! Whatever you do, it's meaningless!"

Mr. Chwe shouts with confidence. I have no capacity to respond, focused as I am.

'…That rag golem is nothing but a hasty construct. Its mass has increased enormously, but its substance—magic resistance and the like—is at the lowest level. He couldn't have accounted for that far.'

Eighth-rank lightning-element magic.

[Plasma Burst]

A blue plasma explosion erupted from within the rag golem's body. Blue light leaked from every seam it had failed to conceal across its entire form. Immediately after, the rag golem's torso was blown to pieces amid a thunderous roar. Every fragment was ablaze.

'Plasma Burst at maximum power can obliterate an entire city, but… with my current self, this is the limit. The power right now is roughly comparable to Thunder Break.'

Plasma Burst's greatest advantage is that the explosion point can be designated. And it lacks Thunder Break's greatest weakness—being heavily influenced by the environment.

Mr. Chwe's burning head falls before me. Even as it burned, his head regenerated.

"Ho ho. That was magnificent magic! However, victory belongs to this sovereign. With that spell just now, your gold magic has reached its limit! Your soul and body—this sovereign shall put them to good use!"

Strength drains from me.

The puppet needle's effect is about to fade.

The puppet needle controls its target and squeezes their performance to the absolute limit—in exchange, it kills them.

It was never a spell meant to be used on oneself.

"I created the spell—do you think I wouldn't know that? To your eyes it may have looked like a desperate last gamble, but to me it was simply a tactic I could employ."

Merlin's Numbers. No.2 Avalonia—the right eye into which it was implanted activates. The rose blooming within my pupil exploded magnificently. Twelve petals vanish at once.

[Last Illusion]

The ability to turn reality into illusion.

Even this ability has its limits. It cannot turn the living into the dead, nor the dead into the living.

However, it could turn a fatal penalty into an illusion that does not exist.

The puppet needle lodged in my temple and the heat that had been melting my brain as a side effect vanished without a trace.

"Hoo. I won't be able to use Avalonia for a while."

"Impossible! You had a way to resolve the backlash of gold magic! I acknowledge it. Mage of Great Korea! You are a mage brilliant enough to be remembered in history! However, the result does not change! This sovereign is immortal!"

"You're wrong. This is our victory."

"Our?"

Mr. Chwe looks toward Wolfgang, far in the distance. As if I would mean that bastard by "our." I turn my head and look at Nora standing atop the ruined debris. In one hand, Nora held a crystal orb in the shape of a skull.

"Oh! My precious! So there was one more of you!!"

There are no jiangshi nearby, and regeneration takes time.

Before he could fly away with his queue-helicopter, I stomped down hard on his head with my foot, pinning it in place.

"Am I late?"

Part of Nora's clothing was torn, burns marked her arms, and blood ran from wounds on her face. There was no way a lich would have hidden its life vessel somewhere easy. She must have gone through her own ordeal.

"You arrived at just the right time. You didn't destroy it the moment you found it?"

"I had a feeling. This thing itself is a treasure."

"Truly, the industry's finest treasure hunter."

"You take it. I have no need for something so ominous."

Receiving the life vessel, I grinned and drew the grimoire Necromantic Heart.

Necromantic Heart is a necromantic spell that steals the soul of the dead and imprisons it within a core.

A soul core can hold all sorts of things, but at a glance, doesn't it resemble a life vessel?

In truth, Necromantic Heart reacted the moment it saw the lich's life vessel. It quivered like a beast that had discovered succulent prey.

"Bzzt bzzt! You bastard, surely you don't mean to—!"

I did not stop Necromantic Heart. A life vessel is the soul of a being, so it cannot enter my inventory. Had I not had Necromantic Heart, I would have destroyed the life vessel by whatever means necessary.

"Your name is Chwe-sik from now on."

The Death Command's Core yanked the Life Vessel in and swallowed it down with a wet gulp.
