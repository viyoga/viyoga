Bureureureu.

My smartphone vibrated. The caller was the Hunter Association. An emergency summons had been issued to me as well.

,'Probably a message popped up for A-rank or higher Hunters nearby. Since this is Gangnam, most of the top 10 guilds will be summoned.'

In my opinion, this was an overreaction. I don't even need to go. But if I don't go, I'll be branded a traitor. The chances of being witch-hunted if things go wrong are high. Excuses like, "I couldn't go because I had other business," won't fly.

,'Well, just responding to the emergency summons will build up my performance record.'

I got up from my seat.

Kang Seok-su was making calls here and there, moving busily. The guild members who remained at the Seventier headquarters also gathered their gear and followed Kang Seok-su.

Jung Ji-hak and I got into Kang Seok-su's car. Kang Seok-su stepped on the accelerator without hesitation.

"There's no detailed information. Representative Kang. Did you receive any separate information?"

"I don't know either. The Hunter Association is probably out of its mind too. We'll find out when we get there. Doesn't it show up if you search the news?"

"The news hasn't spread yet. It seems there are no casualties, except for the dungeon raid team."

"It's an S-rank dungeon. An evacuation order has been issued for the surrounding 500m, and Hunters from the Association are guarding the dungeon gate in preparation for any unforeseen circumstances. Even if there's property damage, there won't be any casualties."

An S-rank dungeon break. At first glance, it might seem like a very serious matter. But S-rank dungeon breaks happen from time to time. Of course, there are countermeasures for them.

Looking at the road, there are cars speeding by. Ambulances, police cars, luxury cars. The police are mainly controlling the roads, and luxury cars are speeding towards Yeouido. Hunters are probably riding in those luxury cars. Glancing at the sky, helicopters are also moving busily here and there.

"Doesn't our guild have a helicopter?"

"We're planning to get a helicopter later. We need to have various discussions with the Association and the government... It's difficult for now."

Seventier wasn't that great of a guild yet.

We arrived at our destination.

Hunters belonging to the Association were on guard with tense expressions. Monster corpses were scattered on one side. I checked the blood-soaked corpses. Reptiles with tiger stripes, about 2m in size.

,'Is that a Tiger Raptor, an A-rank monster? They're fast and move in groups, so they're annoying. Above all, the sharpness of their claws is at the level of Sword energy, so if you're careless, you're done for.'

We headed to the temporary camp set up by the Hunter Association.

The further inside I went, the heavier the air felt. The Hunters were each exuding their own presence. Dozens of A-rank Hunters or higher were gathered here.

"Who are you?"

One person inside, a middle-aged man, looked at us with profound eyes and asked. There was a sense of intimidation in his voice itself. He seemed to be in a bad mood.

"...We're the Seventier Guild, here to support after receiving the emergency summons."

"Seventier. I see. Thank you for coming. I'm Kwon Il-chan, the guild master of Bluebird. ...I received a report that he visited the Seventier Guild a few hours ago. I hope he didn't commit any rudeness?"

"It wasn't anything rude."

"That's a relief then. Thank you for coming all the way here, but I'd like Seventier to stand by. Bluebird will finish this incident."

It was then. I felt a presence behind us. I reflexively turned around. A group confidently entered the camp.

"Are you sure you can finish it?"

The one who entered was a middle-aged woman dressed extravagantly. Her impression itself was ordinary, but the atmosphere was extraordinary.

I knew her. No one in South Korea could not know her.

Like Kwon Il-chan, the master of the Ewha Guild, one of the top 10 guilds, was none other than her, Choi Seo-jeong.

"...Choi Seo-jeong. You didn't need to come here yourself. Did you come to laugh at us?"

"Ha, what do you take me for? I came to clean up the shit that Bluebird made."

"We'll finish our own work."

"You wouldn't be in this mess if you could finish it."

Sparks flew between the two. I watched with great interest. What would happen if they fought here? I was honestly a little curious. Even if they weren't S-rank Hunters, they were among the top powerhouses in South Korea.

But a new face appeared and stopped them. It was someone I knew too.

"Let's stop there. You two didn't come here to fight, did you? Reporters are gathering now. We need to avoid gossip. Especially Bluebird."

Baek Ji-eun.

It had been a while since I saw her in person. Our eyes met briefly. She smiled as if she recognized me and turned her head to speak to the Hunters gathered around.

"About 15 minutes ago, a dungeon break occurred, and a total of 74 A-rank monsters have come out of the gate so far. The monsters were killed by the Hunters who were waiting. The important thing is that only 74 monsters came out. Originally, there should have been at least three times that amount."

"There's only one thing we can think of. Bluebird's first raid team is still raiding inside the dungeon. Bluebird didn't fail the dungeon raid, but the dungeon break still happened."

Bluebird's guild master, Kwon Il-chan, emphasized his words, especially the part about not failing.

This was an anomaly.

The known dungeon break phenomenon occurs in two cases: when the dungeon raid completely fails, or when the dungeon is left unattended for a certain period of time.

"Didn't you completely fail the dungeon raid conditions?"

Choi Seo-jeong interjected. She was referring to the raid conditions that exist in some dungeons.

"Well, we don't know for sure yet. We'll have to investigate that later. What's important now is that we need to rescue the raid team that's still inside the dungeon. It would be great if Han Ah-young-ssi could step up... but she's currently raiding another dungeon."

"We'll go."

"That won't do. The Association can't trust the Bluebird Guild, which has already failed once. Besides, aren't all of Bluebird's main forces in the first raid team? It would be impossible to raid with Bluebird's remaining forces."

"I'm here. If I'm here, we can do it."

"Can you say that in front of the reporters gathered outside?"

"......"

Kwon Il-chan closed his mouth.

What the public wants isn't Bluebird's pride recovery, but a sure raid. Regardless of the reason, since a break phenomenon occurred in the dungeon that Bluebird was in charge of, Bluebird has lost credibility. Whatever the reason, Bluebird has failed.

And the public doesn't care much about the process. The best thing to do here is to follow the Association's words.

"We'll create a rescue team on the spot. The goal isn't to raid. A minimum number of people will move quickly to rescue the raid team. The list is as follows: Me, Baek Ji-eun of the Hunter Association, and...."

Baek Ji-eun called out the names of the Hunters. Those who were quite something even among the A-rank Hunters. Kwon Il-chan and Choi Seo-jeong were included as if it were obvious. Surprisingly, Kang Seok-su and Jung Ji-hak were also called.

"Lastly, Seong Yujin Hunter, for a total of 12 people."

"Wait a minute. I can't understand why Seong Yujin is going. I acknowledge that Seventier's Kang Seok-su and Jung Ji-hak are skilled individuals who have consistently raided A-rank or higher dungeons. But isn't this kid just a newbie who's been raiding B-rank or lower dungeons day and night? You're trying to take a guy like that?"

"Seong Yujin Hunter is an S-rank candidate recognized by the World Hunter Association."

"That's what I can't understand. He doesn't have any proper achievements. Did he bribe the World Hunter Association or something?"

I looked at Kwon Il-chan and smiled. I'm not the old me. I'm confident in my skills, and my background is the World Hunter Association. There was nothing for me to back down from.

"The newbie must be your son who failed the dungeon raid. Oh, since he failed the raid, he must be dead already. My condolences."

It's a well-known story in the Hunter world that Kwon Il-chan's son is the leader of Bluebird's first raid team.

"You little shit...."

Kwon Il-chan growled and exuded killing intent. At the same time, he naturally emitted mana and dominated the surroundings. His skill was incomparable to the A-rank Hunter I faced during the day.

But it wasn't overwhelming. I could do this much too.

I raised my killing intent in the same way and dominated the surroundings with mana. Each other's invisible mana collided as if fighting for territory. What's important now isn't power, but delicate mana control, the will contained in the mana. My mana pushed back Kwon Il-chan's mana and slowly began to apply pressure. Kwon Il-chan was flustered and hesitated.

"Let's stop."

The one who stopped us was surprisingly Choi Seo-jeong, the master of the Ewha Guild.

"As you know, the Bluebird Guild Master's situation isn't good. He's mentally cornered. Seong Yujin Hunter, who has a generous heart, please understand. This isn't the time for us to fight each other. Kwon Il-chan, you should also apologize for looking down on Seong Yujin Hunter."

"......I'm sorry. My judgment was clouded because the situation wasn't good."

Kwon Il-chan gritted his teeth and apologized. It was obvious to anyone that he was apologizing reluctantly.

I wanted to kill him, but... there were too many eyes watching. I decided to become a magnanimous hero for a while.

"I understand. You must be confused because your only son died. I understand you."

"...My son didn't die. I didn't raise him to be so weak that he would lose his life in such a low-level dungeon."

"No matter how much you prepare, the dungeon is a dangerous place. Is the master of a guild not even aware of that?"

"You...! A young punk has no respect for his elders...!"

"It must be nice to be old. Now that I think about it, isn't it about time for you to retire? It would be good to hand over the guild to your son and retire to the countryside to spend the rest of your life.... Oh, your son died. My bad."

"......"

Padeudeuk.

Kwon Il-chan gritted his teeth and clenched his fist. Woong woong. It was just that, but the space vibrated.

I spread my fingers in the opposite direction. Pajijik, lightning wriggled between my fingers.

Kwaaang!

An explosion occurred between me and Kwon Il-chan. Our gazes turned to Baek Ji-eun.

"Both of you, would you please leave if you're not going to cooperate?"

"......"

"......"

Me and Kwon Il-chan released our strength. Baek Ji-eun sighed and began a short briefing.

Kang Seok-su quietly said to me.

"Are you okay? Kwon Il-chan is famous for having a terrible temper. He's going to harbor resentment towards you because of this."

"Resentment isn't something only that bastard can harbor."

And we entered the S-rank dungeon in front of the Hunters and reporters.

,'Kwon Il-chan.... Seeing his temper, he's going to bother me later. If I get the chance, I'll have to send him to his dead son.'
