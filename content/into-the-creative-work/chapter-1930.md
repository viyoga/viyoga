A month has passed since Yuria entered the Maid Academy.

A month.

It was enough time for the freshmen to adapt to their new life. Most of the freshmen who didn't adapt were expelled. That's about 100 people. So, there are about 200 94th freshmen remaining in the Maid Academy. And this number will gradually decrease. The moment you get a failing grade, you're expelled, so you had to get up.

5 a.m.

Before the sun rises, an alarm sounds from the speakers installed on the ceiling of the dormitory.

It was part of a soft classical music. An alarm that makes you fall deeper asleep rather than wake up. If you don't get up here, you'll be late for the morning roll call that starts at 5:15. Then, a deduction of 2 points. If your score reaches 0, you'll be expelled, so you had to get up.

,....."

Yuria, as always, woke up as soon as the wake-up alarm rang. She leisurely gets out of bed, makes the bed, and changes from her pajamas to her maid uniform. After checking her clothes and cleaning the room simply, it was 10 minutes.

,Kyaaaaaaak!"

A scream echoes from one of the dormitory rooms. One of her classmates seemed to have overslept a little. Yuria decided to help her. It was never a pure good deed. There was a calculation that if she helped, it would come back to her someday.

Of course, she wasn't hoping for anything grand.

,Y-Yuria-ssi. Thank you. I would have been late for the morning roll call if it weren't for Yuria-ssi."

,It wasn't a difficult task. Let's go outside. We'll be late for the morning roll call at this rate."

Six people were late for the morning roll call today and were deducted points. It was because of the fatigue from yesterday's combat training. Yuria wanted to help them, but she couldn't. Because the dormitories were different.

After the morning roll call, about an hour of free time was given. Unfortunately, Yuria couldn't enjoy this free time. Because she was in charge of breakfast today.

Yuria and a few other maids headed to the cooking room.

Anna, the cooking instructor maid with a strict expression, said in front of the meal duty.

,The first thing in cooking is cleanliness, the second is taste, and the third is beauty. Now, what is cooking?"

The maid students said all at once.

,The first is cleanliness, the second is taste, and the third is beauty."

Anna nodded and divided the maids.

,Team 1 will cook bread, Team 2 will cook sausages and eggs, and Team 3 will prepare the setting. Now, move."

Clap clap!

Anna clapped her hands and the maids moved.

Yuria was in Team 2. She reached out to take the sausage out of the refrigerator.

Tuk.

Her shoulder bumped into the maid next to her. Yuria, who already knew, staggered and fell to the floor instead of dodging.

,Ah, shit. Watch out. I almost fell because of you."

The blue-haired maid smirks. Her sharp eyes belonged to Tine, who came from the streets. Tine sneered at the fallen Yuria.

,Didn't you come up to me first and push my shoulder?"

,What are you talking about? You were being slow like a slug, so we bumped into each other. Apologize."

Tine glares at Yuria. Her eyes were full of hostility. Yuria didn't do anything to her in particular. She just hated Yuria out of jealousy and envy.

Yuria, of course, didn't care about her.

,I'm the one who fell. I should be the one receiving the apology."

,Are you trying to start something?"

,Can you handle it if we do?"

,Is this bitch crazy?!"

Tine raised her voice. She didn't like Yuria, who answered back with a blank face. She was wondering if she should slap her to take the lead. Anna, the instructor, and the maids looked this way.

,What are you doing there?!"

,Tsk."

Tine, who clicked her tongue, turned to Anna. She took the venom out of her eyes and said with an embarrassed expression.

,I'm sorry. There was a commotion while I was trying to take out the ingredients."

Yuria also got up from her seat.

,I'm sorry. I was careless."

Anna looked at the two alternately and said.

,Accidents happen often in the kitchen. I'm glad you're both not hurt. It seems like a mistake, so I won't deduct points, but be careful in the future."

,Yes. Instructor-nim."

Tine turned around. She raised the corners of her mouth at an angle where only Yuria could see.

But does she know?

That the reputation Yuria has built up since the first day of enrollment is too solid to be ruined by something like this.

,Yuria. Are you okay?"

Juliet, with her light brown hair, approached Yuria. She was a classmate who she happened to get involved with.

,Yes. I'm okay. Tine-ssi seems to dislike me. What did I do wrong?"

,Yuria didn't do anything wrong! Tine is jealous of you!"

Like this, little by little, unknowingly, she chips away at Tine's reputation as if poison is seeping in. It was a kind of political maneuvering. The calculated kindness she has shown so far has created allies.

Originally, she would have gotten rid of annoying women like Tine in an instant by poisoning them or assassinating them, but this was the Maid Academy. If a murder case suddenly occurs, the damage will be greater.

Juliet has many friends because of her cool and innocent personality. The incident that just happened will probably spread as a rumor before lunchtime is over.

,Juliet. I think there's too little pepper in it."

,Oh, really? I guess it's better to add a little more?"

,What about the eggs?"

,I forgot to bring them. Wait a minute!"

Juliet is good at everything, but she's very clumsy.
