The place I moved to with the space-time scroll was a secluded spot in a village near Mount Fuji.

As I remember, this village wasn't heavily populated, but it was a lively place in its own way. That village is quiet now. Not a single sign of life is felt.

The house that looked fine from a distance had its doors and windows smashed when viewed up close.

I go inside to check. A messy room and bloodstains. A piece of cloth that appears to have been human clothing.

,Something came in through the doors and windows and devoured people. It must be the giant locust Madoka-ssi was talking about.'

While wandering around the village, I found a giant locust. The dark gray locust, the size of a large dog, had parts of its body bitten off and was dying on the floor. I don't know why, but it seemed to have been bitten by its own kind.

I split the locust's body by flying Sword energy with Hwaryeon Bido. Unpleasant goo splattered as the locust died. I kicked the locust's corpse with my foot and examined it intently.

,...There's no magic stone.'

Monsters created in dungeons have magic stones inside their bodies. There are no exceptions. Hunters earn money through these magic stones.

I killed a monster, but there's no magic stone? There was only one possibility. That it's not a monster created in a dungeon.

,The old wizards used to create monsters and use them as familiars... Is this a similar case?'

I walked towards the crater of Mount Fuji. Seeing the black smoke soaring into the sky from the crater, I gradually lose the desire to go.

,But I have to go. For confirmation.'

There were many locusts near Mount Fuji. At least 3,000 of them. Most of them were mating. I quietly passed by the locusts. The ghost cloak was helpful. It seems their detection abilities aren't very good.

I stood on the crater of Mount Fuji and confirmed that locusts were popping out of the boiling lava.

There's a dungeon inside the lava. I don't know how that's possible, though.

,Was it there from the beginning? Or did it suddenly appear? How can there be a dungeon in lava?'

Many questions arose, but I couldn't find the answers to them.

I took out a space-time scroll from my pocket and tore it.

Washington, D.C., in the United States, was experiencing a zombie apocalypse. Corpses move and attack the living.

At the center of the zombies was a black man.

,This world will overflow with death! Hahaha! Now, accept death! Death is more comfortable than you think! It's better than dying insane!'

The black man gestures with a smile. At that, the zombies rush to kill the living.

,Virus? Curse? Or is it that black man's ability?'

I don't know what it is, but it seems that black man is the culprit, so I took a picture of him and tore the space-time scroll.

I moved to Moscow, Russia. The guys claiming to be the new human race were white people with blonde hair and blue eyes.

,There's a yellow monkey here!'

,Come here, inferior livestock!'

They claimed to be the new human race, but they just seemed to be extreme racists. They didn't treat even white people as the same if they didn't have blonde hair and blue eyes.

Jjieeeeeeek.

Europe. Budapest.

,Haha! There are so many humans like trash!'

,Shall we fill our bellies? Humans. Offer us your blood!'

,Kyaaaaaaak!'

Blood and screams were rampant in the elegant and splendid city.

The humans with long ears that the World Hunter Association mentioned. At first, I thought they were elves. The characteristic of having long ears is famous for elves. But these guys I saw in person aren't elves.

Long ears, pale skin, protruding fangs.

A race that sinks its fangs into the necks of humans and sucks their blood. Vampires.

Ordinary humans are busy running away, and European Hunters are busy fighting vampires. In the meantime, a dungeon break phenomenon occurs, and monsters appear everywhere.

It was a mess.

Jjieeeeeeek.

Africa, which I checked just in case, was a kingdom of monsters. It seemed like thousands of dungeons had burst at the same time. I can't see people, only monsters are teeming. Those monsters are also busy fighting each other over territory.

Jjieeeeeeek.

I returned to Seoul. Park Gaini was running wild, so I thought it wouldn't be strange if Seoul was destroyed... but it was surprisingly quiet.

I go to where Park Gaini was. The neighborhood was devastated, and there were many corpses of Hunters.

I passed by those corpses and finally found Park Gaini. She was blackened as if she had been burned and was dead. Judging from the fact that the patient gown she was wearing was intact, it didn't seem like she was actually burned.

,It looks like she self-destructed?'

The situation began to be drawn in my head. No matter how unique and great Park Gaini's abilities are, there must be a limit. It's natural for destruction to come when the limit is exceeded.

The virgin ghost possessed by Park Gaini would have used her power without caring about self-destruction. Because that virgin ghost would have been an avatar, not the main body.

I looked at Park Gaini's corpse and thought of Han Ha-rin-yang. Since the world is in this state, Han Ha-rin-yang will die soon. I know I can't help it, but I want her to live... but there was no safe place in this world.

,Since I've decided to use the regression ticket, there's no need to consider this issue... Haa. What should I do now?'

I have to use my head at times like this.

What I want is information. But even if I travel around the world alone, the information I can get isn't that great. At best, I'd know how the world is going to shit.

,I need to know the situation in more detail. To do that, there's a limit to what I can do alone.'

A place that professionally collects information and is reliable.

A place that still has some power even though the world is in this state.

Only one place came to mind. The Hunter Association.

Sswaaaaeeeeeeeeeeek!

Fighter planes were flying in the sky above Seoul. I turned my back on the faint sound of bombs exploding in the distance and headed to the headquarters of the Korean Hunter Association.

,If I go to the association, they'll grab me and say something about doing something for Korea... Let's ignore it and just extract the information. If it's a high-ranking official of the association... I might be able to kidnap them if I get the chance.'

Among the executives of the Hunter Association, there are also ordinary people who are not Hunters. Just because someone is an employee of the association doesn't necessarily mean they have to be a Hunter.

,It would be convenient if Baek Ji-eun-ssi was here, but I can't get in touch with her.'

If the plan doesn't go as I want, I'll use points to use even the absolute hypnosis sticker.

When I arrived at the headquarters building of the Korean Hunter Association, I realized that the plan had failed. The association headquarters was in a state no different from ruins.

A single sheet of A4 paper was attached in front of the entrance.

-The Korean Hunter Association headquarters building has collapsed and been relocated. Those who have business at the association headquarters are asked to visit the Seocho branch or contact us at the contact information below.

,......'

I looked around. The other buildings were fine except for the Hunter Association headquarters. Only the headquarters was destroyed. It's a strange thing.

Brain Thunder Style Electromagnetic Wave.

I feel a presence from the basement of the association headquarters. I decided to go in for now. Fortunately, it wasn't so collapsed that I couldn't enter the headquarters.

The door that said "No Entry for Unauthorized Personnel" was smashed and couldn't stop me. I pass through the door and go inside.

I saw a chaotic hallway and office. There were quite a few corpses lying around. I walked to the innermost part where I felt a presence and stopped.

I found the corpse of Baek Ji-eun-ssi, half of her head missing. Looking at the corpses of other Hunters lying around, it seems like they had a battle.

I sighed. I didn't feel good at all.

I passed by Baek Ji-eun-ssi and went inside. The inside was more intact than other places.

In the center of the room was a table with a map of Korea spread out, and a woman was sitting in a chair in front of it. The bloody brown-haired woman was quite a beauty.

,I didn't expect a guest to come... You're not a rescue team. You're A-class Hunter Seong Yujin-gun, right?'

,...How do you know me? Who are you?'

,The association is watching you. To be exact, not just you, but all Hunters who have the potential to be S-class Hunters. Don't worry, I'm not going to play tricks. I don't even pry into your private life, no matter what.'

,If you did, I wouldn't have stood still.'

,I like your confident attitude. I'm Jeong Hye-gyeong. The manager of the Korean rift observatory here. I was dispatched from the World Hunter Association.'

,You're affiliated with the World Hunter Association, but you're the manager of the rift observatory in the Korean Association?'

,All the rift observatories in this world are the property of the World Hunter Association. The World Hunter Association lends the rift observatories to the Hunter Associations of each country and dispatches managers to manage them.'

I approached the rift observatory table. A table with a map of Korea drawn on it. The map was full of red dots.

,...Are these red dots dungeons?'

,Dimensional rifts. Yes. Dungeons. The red dots are dungeons where dungeon breaks have occurred, and the blue ones are dungeons before dungeon breaks occur.'

,There are hardly any blue dots?'

,That's why the world is ruined. Look at Jeju Island. It's full of red dots. It's already completely over there.'

,Are there any dungeons that the rift observatory can't observe?'

,Huh? That's an unexpected question. To answer, yes, there are. In cases that aren't dimensional rifts. The rift observatory can't observe spatial rifts like subspace. There's no reason to find them either. Spatial rifts are automatically repaired.'

,You're answering quite obediently.'

,The world is in this state... Who cares about secrets? And there's a reason I need to look good to you. Hey, can you do me a favor?'

,I refuse troublesome requests.'

,It's a simple request. Please kill me. I'm holding on thanks to my ability, multiple thinking... but I'm slowly reaching my limit.'

Jeong Hye-gyeong's eyes turned red and then returned.

,Are you possessed by a virgin ghost?'

,Yeah, that's right. A virgin ghost. That unknown monster ruined the Korean Hunter Association. The women are going crazy and running wild... If they're virgins, they get possessed and seduce men and kill them... The seduced men can't resist at all... What kind of existence is that?'

,If you were going to commit suicide, you could have done it alone.'

,My hands and feet don't move. The only things that can move in my body right now are my mouth and eyes.'

,What exactly is a dungeon? Why are they created, and why are they suddenly all causing dungeon breaks now?'

,I don't know either. If you want to know the answer, go to the World Hunter Association. All the secrets of this world will be there.'

,You're also affiliated with the World Hunter Association.'

,What organization would tell secrets to a low-level employee?'

,......What is the World Hunter Association? I thought it was just a higher-level organization of the Hunter Association?'

,As far as I know, the World Hunter Association has existed for a very long time. The name World Hunter Association is only used in today's era. Before that, it was called the Golden Dawn Society? Before that, the Illuminati. It was called by various other names, but... the first beginning was a religious group called Kabbalah, I heard.'

,You want me to believe such conspiracy theories?'

,I thought like you too. But think about it. If such an organization hadn't held the center of the world... would this world have been maintained until now? In a world where wizards and monsters exist?'

,......'

,I answered. If you have any questions, go to the World Hunter Association. The reason why the world is like this is probably there too. Ugh. It's getting hard to hold on. Kill me. I don't want to be possessed and played with by a ghost and die.'

,Okay. I'll kill you.'

Seogeok!

I cut off Jeong Hye-gyeong's neck with Hwaryeon Bido.
