,It seems you've underestimated this Honored One."

The Great Demon Bano spat out those words. The red light flowing from his eyes became even more violent.

Kwadeudeudeuk, kkadeudeuk.

The sound of bones colliding and bending echoed. The bone soldiers around him collided with each other, destroying themselves. Breaking and shattering. As a result, thousands, tens of thousands of sharp bone spikes were created. As Bano gestured, the bone spikes soared into the air.

,Won't you soon regret blocking this Honored One's path?"

I circulated the Demonic Energy. A dark Demonic Energy enveloped me. It was a protective aura.

,I told you, I came because I thought I could handle it."

,You're being deceived by that sorceress woman. Look, while you're fighting this Honored One on the front lines, she's dealing with the small fries in a safe place."

Sama-ryeong was destroying bone soldiers and keeping the monsters in check from the rear with her magic. Sama-ryeong was doing her part just by doing that. Thanks to her efforts, the battle situation was gradually tilting in our favor.

,Cut the crap with the divisive tactics. And shut your mouth while you're at it. It's unpleasant enough that a monster is even speaking human language."

,...Human language, is it? Yes. You humans have always been arrogant. You're the worst of them all. The type of human this Honored One hates the most. There will be no mercy for you. Die."

Bano gestured. The bone spikes floating in the air all rushed towards me.

The protective aura stood in their way. The bone spikes were not ordinary bone spikes. Each one was a bone imbued with a certain level of demonic energy. I didn't know for sure, but preparing these must have taken quite a bit of time.

However, the bone spikes could not penetrate the protective aura. Maybe an ordinary protective aura would have, but this was a protective aura using Demonic Energy. Furthermore, the amount of Demonic Energy was in no way inferior to Bano, who was at the middle stage of the Three Divine Peaks.

,The elixirs obtained through the Cheongangseong System and the elixirs enhanced with the Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo... The amount I've consumed is enormous. I'm more confident in my internal energy than anyone else."

Papapat!

The bone spikes were blocked by the protective aura and fell to the ground. No demonic power was felt from the fallen bone spikes. They had simply become bones with excellent hardness.

Bano, realizing that this attack was meaningless, waved his hand to stop the bone spike attack.

,So, you've got some tricks up your sleeve. But you still can't beat this Honored One."

The bone spikes disintegrated. Not only those floating in the air but also those that had lost their demonic power and fallen to the ground were finely broken down into powder. Bone powder gathered around Bano and began to be absorbed into him.

,He's pulling some trick. As if I'd just sit back and watch."

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 10]

I closed the distance with Acceleration and Instant. Bano's eyes did not leave me. Even though I used Instant, he could see my movements and react with his own eyes. It wasn't particularly surprising. Instant didn't work well against those stronger than a certain level.

I gathered Demonic Energy in my left fist. In an instant, the Demonic Energy was compressed. I punched Bano in the solar plexus.

Cheonma Shingong Dragon Fist.

Bone powder gathered and blocked the front of my fist. The compressed Demonic Energy stretched forward, trying to pierce through the bone powder, but it was not enough. The demonic power contained in the bone powder was extraordinary.

,If once isn't enough, I'll hit him twice, three times."

Cheonma Shingong Dragon Fist.

It didn't work any better this time. At least the bone powder rippled with the previous attack, but now it didn't move at all.

,It's useless. The bones connected to this Honored One build resistance to attacks they've already suffered. No matter how much you hit me..."

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Sword Ninefold Three Kill.

I lightly stepped on the Cheonma Gunrimbo. Instantly distorting space and slashing with my sword. The space was refracted, and the tip of the sword split into three, simultaneously targeting Bano's head, neck, and chest.

Again, the bone powder moved to block the front of the sword. But it couldn't block it completely. The sword had more penetrating power than a fist.

The sword pierced through the bone powder. The bone powder clung to the blade as if it couldn't just be pierced. But regardless, the tip of the sword, imbued with Demonic Energy, moved forward.

Twitch.

Bano, who had been standing arrogantly until now, was surprised and stepped back. Then, his right arm deflected my attack. His skin was made of bone. As if he were wearing bone armor directly on top of his skin. Thanks to that, I couldn't inflict significant damage. Instead, there was a scratch-like wound on the bone.

Bone powder flew in and instantly filled the wound.

,Impressive. Not only your skill, but the sword itself is also special."

,It's not over yet."

Cheonma Shingong Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Unlike before, I stepped on the Cheonma Gunrimbo with all my might. The bone soldiers, monsters, and even the Jiangshi around me faltered. Bano was no exception. The bone mask-like face of the bastard was distorted. The bone powder was also affected and rippled. All the bone powder attached to the blade flew away.

,The fuck? What the hell is this power that's messing with not just my insides, but even my mind?!"

A gap was revealed.

I wasn't going to miss it.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Sword Ninefold Seven Kill.

Seven slashes fell on Bano. The bone powder reacted and blocked the slashes. I couldn't penetrate the clumped bone powder.

,I told you, attacks I've already suffered build resistance."

,Stabbing and slashing are different."

,Isn't the essence the same? If you want to kill this Honored One, you'll have to attack more creatively."

,......"

My mind became complicated. The essence of Ninefold Three Kill and Ninefold Seven Kill was ultimately just wrapping Demonic Energy around the sword and attacking. If he had built resistance to that, then simple attacks would no longer work on him.

,Is a resistance once built permanent?"

That couldn't be. That would be a truly ridiculous ability. There must be limits and conditions to the resistance built into the bones.

,I can't figure it out right now. He's not stupid enough to tell me directly."

&gt;

It was the moment I was about to regain my stance.

Bano rushed in. A footwork that seemed to transcend space in an instant.

,He switched the position of the bone powder with his own...?!"

His hand reached out. The protective aura was pierced, and my collar was grabbed. Bano held a bone knife in his other hand. Ppadeudeuk, ppadeudeuk. The knife transformed like the teeth of a beast.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Palm.

Before the knife could fall, I swung my palm at Bano's torso. The Demonic Palm left a palm print on the bone armor protecting the bastard's torso and dug inside.

,Keok!"

Bano spat out blood and flew backward. The attack worked so well that I was slightly dumbfounded.

,You're spitting blood from an attack of this level? I see. You're nothing without the bones. There's a reason you're covering your body with bones. Resistance to attacks builds up in the bones? Then I just need to ignore the bones and attack. I'll fuck you up with my Inner Heavy Hand Technique based on my profound internal energy!"

,To be so arrogant just because one attack happened to work... This Honored One will personally separate your bones and flesh."

The remaining bone powder around him gathered around Bano. Bano absorbed the bone powder and increased his size. The bone armor became larger and harder, transforming into a threatening form with even thorns sprouting out. The bone knife he held in his hand was the same.

When all the bone powder was absorbed, his size had grown to nearly 3m.

Bano leaped and swung his knife. I hurriedly raised Hwaryeon Bido to block the attack.

A shockwave occurred that was enough to carve into the ground supporting my legs. Bano's ignorant attacks continued without stopping.

A chain of ignorant attacks continued like a storm. Even if I wanted to counterattack, there was no opening. I gritted my teeth and blocked the attacks.

,My armor and knife are compressed with over a thousand geun of bones. How long do you think you can withstand the weight of a thousand geun!"

,I think I can withstand it for the rest of my life?"

I bluffed, ignoring the trembling of my arms. A shallow provocation. But it definitely worked. Surprisingly, provocations sometimes worked better on experts than on noobs. Because it's even more infuriating when a weak bastard provokes you.

,The opportunity will come."

Bano was focused on killing me. He judged that if I fell, the rest of the work would go smoothly.

I endured. Even though my arms were shaking and my knees were screaming, I took the bastard's attacks. I conserved my internal energy while enduring with my body.

And finally, the moment of opportunity came.

The tide of battle had begun to tilt completely in our favor. There were fewer than five living monsters left, and the overwhelming number of bone soldiers was less than a hundred.

,Kuaaaaak! Bano-nim! I can't hold on any longer!"

,P-Please help me! Bano-nim!"

The monsters were asking for help. Nevertheless, Bano's eyes did not waver. He must have been thinking that he could just get more subordinate monsters later anyway.

It started with the wind.

A strong gust of wind blew, blocking the space between me and Bano. Then, a stream of water shot up from the ground. The water and wind mixed, becoming a dragon wind that trapped Bano.

,Sama-ryeong, who cleaned up the bone soldiers, helped me."

I took a stance. I didn't think I could kill Bano with just a dragon wind. The bastard was armed with thick bone armor.

Kieeeeeeeeeeeing!

With a sound like something grinding, the dragon wind split in half. The bones scattered around were powdered and absorbed into Bano. Bano's size increased even more.

I rotated the Demonic Energy of Hwaryeon Bido with my breath. Black wind gathered around me. The wind grew bigger and bigger. I kept the black wind, which should have become a storm, around me.

I felt damage to my blood vessels. Internal injuries were spreading little by little. It's okay. One hit is all I need. I just need to kill Bano's bone armor and everything else at once.

Bano, who had increased his size even more, was about to rush at me but stopped. He seemed to have sensed the danger.

,If you don't come, I'll go."

It was the moment I was about to run forward.

Kwack!

A jet-black Jiangshi that popped out of the ground grabbed my ankles with both hands. The Jiangshi's fingernails pierced my skin and dug into my flesh.

A surprise attack at the decisive moment when my mind was sold to Bano.

I distorted my face and swung my sword at the Jiangshi. The gathered black wind exploded, devouring everything around it. The ground was dug up, and more than 10 black Jiangshi hidden underground were ground to pieces.

I stomped on the head of one Jiangshi with my foot and burst into a roar.

,Guihon Heuksu!!!!"
