As soon as I gripped the Hwaryeon Bido, Chamsalgwi kicked off the ground and charged at me.

I had been focusing my concentration since Chamsalgwi appeared, so I didn't miss his movements and was able to follow him. His hands went to his waist, and he drew two swords simultaneously.

Even though it's a quick draw performed in a stance disrupted by running, it's clean. As expected, he completely controls his body.

Kaang!

I held the Hwaryeon Bido vertically to block Chamsalgwi's quick draw. It was Chamsalgwi who was impressed, seeing the blade bounce off.

,I heard you were wearing some weird armor, so I thought you'd be weak... But seeing you withstand my attack, it seems you've been diligently training your body."

"My strength has gotten a lot stronger recently. If you're just looking at physical stats, I'm above you."

I knew Chamsalgwi's real name, so I could check his status window.

『Name: Li Tao

Strength: A+ Stamina: S- Agility: S- Durability: A+ Mana: C

Trait: Ghostly Soul (S)

Skills: Swordsmanship (S+), Ghostly Energy (S), Possession (B)

Trust: 37』

Except for strength and mana, his stats are one level above mine.

'Seeing how high his agility is... there's a high probability that he specializes in speed battles.'

You can tell his fighting style just by looking at the clothes he's wearing. He's the type who fights by blocking or dodging the opponent's attacks with his sword. That's why he's wearing martial arts uniform instead of armor.

'A typical martial artist-type fighting style that suits the world of martial arts. But how is his trust so high when I just met him.'

I grip the hilt of the Hwaryeon Bido and counterattack.

'First, lightly.'

Pajik.

Red electricity sparked from the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

Drawing a red trajectory in the dark void, it aims for Chamsalgwi's neck. Chamsalgwi stepped back, using footwork. The tip of the Hwaryeon Bido barely grazed his neck.

Chamsalgwi grinned.

"Good. This is exactly the kind of battle I wanted."

"You dodge well. Could you see my attack?"

"Unfortunately, I couldn't see it. That slash was one that couldn't be followed with the eyes. But a sword is just a sword. The one wielding the sword is ultimately the swordsman's body."

Roughly, he's saying he predicted the slash by looking at my posture, not the sword.

'Textbook.'

It can be said to be the textbook way to deal with quick swords. But most people die to quick swords because they can't do this textbook method.

'Just dodging Lightning Flash makes him a skilled person above first-class.'

I acknowledged Chamsalgwi's skill. However.

『Li Tao's Trust: 45』

'Why the hell is this bastard's trust going up.'

I clicked my tongue.

It's a miscalculation if he thinks I'll go easy on him just because his trust is increasing. I'm going to kill this bastard here. It's already been decided.

As I prepared for the next attack, Chamsalgwi acted first. He wildly swings the swords in both hands. No, even if it looks wild, it's not wild.

He continues a series of attacks naturally, like flowing water, without being restricted by form. Thanks to the ghostly energy (鬼氣) on the blades, Chamsalgwi's attacks become even more chaotic.

Sparks start flying here and there as our swords collide.

'Not good. This bastard's sword is getting faster the more he swings it. It's getting hard to parry.'

I have one sword, he has two. It's not easy to shake him off with force.

I spread out my left hand, which wasn't holding a sword. Lightning gathers and rotates around the center of my palm, which is covered in steel armor.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Ten Thousand Lightnings (卍雷).

If I shoot it, he'll dodge it eight or nine times out of ten.

'Discharge.'

The lightning compressed in my palm explodes in all directions.

Chamsalgwi creates a protective aura to protect his body. This moment, when he's focusing his mind for the protective aura, is the gap I was aiming for.

Aura and lightning mix on the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido, forming a blade.

Lightning Stream Style (Lei Tian Liu) Lightning Gale Blade (Lei Gang Ren).

The sword tears through Chamsalgwi's protective aura and aims for his right arm. It was already too late when Chamsalgwi reacted. A posture that makes it difficult to move his weapon. However, Chamsalgwi's arm moved forcibly. His right arm is forcibly raised in a direction it can't move, and Chamsalgwi's sword blocks my sword.

Ttaenggang!

The sword, raised by the power of a third party, the Guardian Spirit, blocked the Hwaryeon Bido and shattered. His left-hand sword is swung. His blade was imbued with ghostly energy. That clearly has power comparable to aura.

Aura is the exclusive property of sword masters, but to put it bluntly, it's the emission of ki. Sword energy's density has become incredibly high, and that can be said to be aura. That's aura, just in a different form.

'Should I go in? Or not.'

If I go in, I can definitely inflict a critical hit. The problem is, I have to be prepared to take a critical hit too.

The worry was short.

I stepped back.

Even if I have complete recovery, it's not like I'm in a hurry. There's no need to push recklessly.

'I'm the one with the upper hand. There's no need to overdo it.'

Chamsalgwi didn't even care about his broken right arm, which he had forcibly moved, and stared at the Hwaryeon Bido with gleaming eyes.

"That sword. It's a very good sword, gun. I want it. I'll have to kill you and take it."

"Know your place. You think you can kill me when your right arm is broken? You should have realized by now. My skill is superior."

"...I admit it. Our physical abilities are similar... but your swordsmanship and judgment in battle are superior. I could tell you've been through struggles that can't be compared to mine. So, from now on, I'll give it my all."

A smile spreads across Chamsalgwi's face. The smile soon tore apart, becoming eerily ghostly.

The Guardian Spirit behind him quietly seeped into Chamsalgwi's body. He used the Possession (B) skill he had.

Two arms sprout from below Chamsalgwi's shoulders. The broken right arm also moved as he forcibly moved it. It's not that it's recovered. It's moving forcibly in its broken state, with the power of the Guardian Spirit.

Chamsalgwi threw away the sword he was holding in his left hand. And he drew the Executioner's Blade from behind.

"Four arms, but only holding one sword, gun."

"I realized that your tricks won't work on you. I'll focus on just one and cut you down."

Chamsalgwi moves his feet. His feet stopped before they touched the snow-covered ground. No, he stepped on the air. And at that moment, Chamsalgwi's body disappeared.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

Top right. Chamsalgwi, who appeared as if lying down, twisted his waist and swung the Executioner's Blade. I parried his attack with the Hwaryeon Bido.

Kaang!

Chamsalgwi's Executioner's Blade bounced off, revealing an opening.

[Using Instant (刹那). Remaining stacks: 10]

Reversing the blade, I rotate it and swing it downwards.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

Chamsalgwi stepped on the air and dodged upwards, falling to the ground.

"Amazing. You're the first to keep up with this speed. Even Gokju had difficulty responding to this speed."

I was the one who was surprised.

Just now, Chamsalgwi moved at a speed similar to Instant. It would have been difficult to respond if it wasn't for Instant.

"You can't produce that speed without any conditions, right? How many times do you have left?"

Blood started flowing from Chamsalgwi's eyes and mouth. Even so, Chamsalgwi is smiling.

"That's right. But your arm that parried my attack is also trembling. They say speed is power. How long can you endure?"

Chamsalgwi steps on the air again. By the time I heard the sonic boom, he was already behind me.

He's challenging me to a speed battle?

Fine. I'll play along.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 9]

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning (Swift Wind, Rapid Thunder).

Pajijijik!

The electricity that started from my spine spreads throughout my body, stimulating every corner.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 8]

I moved behind Chamsalgwi. Chamsalgwi was startled.

"...What are you doing."

His voice was filled with anger instead of joy.

"What are you talking about?"

"...You could have killed me just now. Why didn't you swing your sword?"

"To show you that I'm faster than you. Got it? Now die."

I swing the Hwaryeon Bido. I cut Chamsalgwi's body.

'An afterimage?!'

Chamsalgwi's body becomes blurry.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

'Chamsalgwi's location is on the left. No. He moved to the right.'

I raise the Hwaryeon Bido and block the Executioner's Blade flying from the right.

Kwaaaang!

My body flew backwards from the shockwave carried by the Executioner's Blade and crashed into the ground. Chamsalgwi hasn't just gotten faster.

I can't be dazed. Chamsalgwi is falling to the ground from the sky, with the tip of his sword aimed at me. Blood and ghostly energy were flowing from Chamsalgwi's entire body.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I dodged to the side and rushed at Chamsalgwi, who had fallen to the ground. Chamsalgwi reacted to my speed and swung the Executioner's Blade.

Our swords collided, creating a shockwave. We were knocked back in opposite directions, and craters formed on the ground.

"Keuheuhe. I feel like I'm going to die! But how can a swordsman resist such a fight! Give it your all, Jeokgwang!"

"You're the one who's dying. You give it your all."

Blood flows from between my lips. I suffered internal injuries from the shock just now. When you think about it, my durability stat was lower.

'Should I release the Hwaryeon Bido now?'

I immediately erased the thought. I didn't want to rely on the sword's power like a loser after coming this far.

Chamsalgwi and I simultaneously kicked off the air and rushed at each other.

The instantaneous acceleration broke the sound barrier.

Kwaaaang!

By the time I heard the collision sound, we were already clashing for the second time. Part of the suit was shattered.

The burden generated by breaking the sound barrier steadily accumulates in my body. If I wasn't wearing the Machina Suit, I might have passed out first.

'But that bastard is also possessed by a Guardian Spirit.'

Kwaaaang! Kwang! Kwaaaaaang!

We continue to collide in the air. And we're knocked back by the shockwave. Then we charge for the next one.

This was already a race.

If you stop, you die.

Blood splattered every time we collided. I couldn't tell if it was my blood or his.

At this point, the outcome was decided.

I had complete recovery, so I didn't care if I died. But Chamsalgwi didn't.

Chamsalgwi's speed started to slow down at some point. I didn't miss him and cut off both of Chamsalgwi's legs. Chamsalgwi, who had lost his legs, fell to the snow-covered ground.

Steam rose from his bloody body. The Guardian Spirit possessed by him struggles to rise. But Chamsalgwi, who was already at his limit, couldn't move.

"I lost. Even if I continued like this, I would have lost."

"You admit it quickly, gun."

"How can I not admit it when I'm in this state...? More than anything, how can I not know when we collided with each other? You were using swordsmanship even while colliding at that speed. You let the shock that could flow flow, and you endured the shock that you could endure. I... was struggling just to maintain my speed."

"...Was I?"

"You didn't even know it yourself? Haha. Right. Is there any other way to achieve unity of sword and self? Swordsmanship that has melted into the body even without intention. That's unity of sword and self."

"I'm going to kill you. Before that... why didn't you use the power of the devil? Your Gwirokgok should have made a contract with the devil."

"As you said, I made a contract with the devil. Gokju told me to do it. But what I pursue is not a devil or anything like that. I decided to live as a sword demon and die as a sword demon. Therefore, I have no resentment towards you either. I'm just dying because I lost."

"This guy's interesting. I'll give you a special choice. How should I kill you?"

"If it's called Chamsal, isn't it beheading? I've always beheaded the losers. So, please behead me."
