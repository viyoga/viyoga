Melissa guided Elena as Seong Yujin commanded.

Some of the decorations and sculptures were so good they would be better off in an art gallery.

,It could be the work of humans... but there's a high chance it's the work of dwarves.

In Elena's world, most masterpieces were made by dwarves, so she naturally thought that way.

,This kitchen is only for Master-nim. We have all kinds of ingredients, and the maids here are the best cooks in the mansion. Guest-nim, please have a taste.

Melissa brought a plate. It had shrimp on it. The shrimp, prepared for easy eating, had a faint garlic and savory butter smell. Elena was about to refuse, but she was drawn in by the smell and picked up a shrimp with a fork and put it in her mouth.

The plump shrimp meat burst in her teeth, and the taste and flavor spread throughout her mouth. Elena decided to fire all the chefs in her mansion and hire new ones.

,I'll bring it right away.
