The stiffness was only temporary.

They raised their guns, stood back-to-back, and scanned their surroundings.

,Who is it!

,Show yourself now!!

,Shibal....

Lieutenant Olok ignored his subordinates' commotion and quietly surveyed the area. He couldn't sense anyone nearby. The rhythmic sounds of insects hidden in the plants calmed his mind.

,Don't move recklessly. This is an air trap.

,Lieutenant-nim. I've never heard of such a thing. What kind of trap is it?

,It's a magic trap. I believe it's military-grade arcane magic. I don't have much experience with it either. Goddamn it. That woman, she said she was a combat mage, but she learned something troublesome.

He recalled the air trap and explained it to his subordinates.

An air trap is an invisible trap. Once activated, it generates a shockwave or wind cutter. Corporal Gralton's head was split open by a wind cutter, resulting in instant death.

,I heard the activation trigger for air traps is strict... She set it up damn well.

The important thing is how to deal with air traps.

The first method is mana detection. It's easier if you can use mana or have an item with mana detection capabilities.

,...Neither is possible for us.

To detect mana, you need to feel it.

In other words, you need the talent of mana affinity.

,If I had mana affinity, I wouldn't be here right now.

Most people can't feel mana. That's normal.

,The other option is... to wait until the air trap disappears. I heard air traps don't last long without a medium.

However, stopping the pursuit for an hour here would mean losing the target. This is the Lumel Forest. If they go deep enough, even someone familiar with this place would have to worry about survival.

Money and women flickered before his eyes. He was too hungry to give up either.

,...The activation trigger for air traps is contact. It activates the moment it touches the body. Unless magic power is infinite, there must be a limit to how many air traps can be set up.... If we don't pursue directly and change direction slightly while pursuing, we might be able to avoid the air traps.

Lieutenant Olok led his subordinates and resumed the pursuit. The smiles that had been on their faces were long gone.

One of the subordinates following Olok felt a strange sensation. He stepped on the ground and felt something soft squishing under his foot.

Puh-ung!

The ground exploded. There was no fire. The air burst, creating a shockwave.

,Aaaaaaagh!

The subordinate, whose legs had been blown off, screamed and rolled on the ground. Olok looked at the subordinate with cold eyes and pulled the trigger of his rifle. Bang. The subordinate died instantly.

,He was already a goner. I just relieved him of his pain.

,...But Lieutenant-nim.

,Think about Major-nim. Do you think he'd send us to the hospital if we got hurt?

,.......

No one didn't know what kind of person Major Bag was. Everyone shut their mouths.

,There's no time to dawdle. Just follow me closely. Air traps only activate if they touch your body. Watch carefully where I step and how I move, and copy me. Got it?

,Yes, Lieutenant-nim.

Lieutenant Olok felt his nerves on edge. Air traps could be set not only on the ground but also in the air. In other words, he could lose his head if he was unlucky.

,I'm avoiding places where traps might be set, but it's not perfect. Damn it. I wish I could put my subordinates in front of me.... But they have no loyalty whatsoever....

If he did that, there was a chance that the three subordinates would turn their guns on him. Kill him and run away.

Pee-ing.

With an eerie sound, the air trap activated. This time, it was a wind cutter. Olok realized he was safe and turned his head back.

The bodies of two subordinates were cut down and fell to the ground. Blood and guts poured out from the clean cross-sections.

,Hiiiiiiiiik!

Lieutenant Olok's last remaining subordinate screamed shrilly and collapsed. He was the most timid of the subordinates.

,...What happened?

,W-Well, Sergeant-nim staggered a bit, and then we heard a wind sound....

,Indeed. He was incredibly unlucky.

Lieutenant Olok wiped away the cold sweat and raised the corners of his mouth. He actually felt it was a good thing. He pointed his rifle at the subordinate.

,From now on, you're going first.

,L-Lieutenant-nim?

,Follow my orders if you don't want to die. Don't take it the wrong way. I've been taking the risks up until now. It's just your turn this time.

,P-Please, spare me.

,Don't be stupid. Move.

The subordinate moved forward with a tearful face. Lieutenant Olok looked at the subordinate's back and drew a big picture in his head.

,When I find those guys, I'll immediately unleash magic bullets to subdue them. I'll kill the guy and keep the woman's torso intact. And then I'll kill this guy too. I'll pick out the most expensive equipment and run. I'm sick of military life. Maybe defecting to the Republic wouldn't be so bad.

Kwaaaang!

Twelve hours after entering the Lumel Forest.

Special wavelengths flow through the Lumel Forest, rendering radios useless. It's impossible to communicate with the outside world through ordinary means. It wasn't particularly mysterious. There are many areas besides the Lumel Forest where radio waves don't work.

,Are we giving up the mission and going back?

,Usually, yes. In the case of this mission, it can be said to be a mistake by the higher-ups. You won't be reprimanded even if you give up the mission.

Will Major Bag back down like this? If he was going to, he wouldn't have started in the first place.

Ttchak!

,Let's take it easy. Let's sleep at night and move during the day.

Fortunately, Lexi Instructor was flexible in many ways.

On top of that, I set up wooden sticks and covered them with cloth. A simple tarp was created. Even if it rained, we wouldn't get soaked.

While I was preparing the sleeping area, Lexi Instructor set up alarm magic around us. It was a type of magic that would sound an alarm and notify us if a person or monster approached.

She and I laid down on the blanket at the same time. It was comfortable, perhaps because it was a magic blanket.
