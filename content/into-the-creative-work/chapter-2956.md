Despite the difference in numbers, the Baekhwadan prevailed in the battle.

There was a difference in the qualitative level of the soldiers.

The female Martial artists belonging to the Baekhwadan were elites, recognized for their Talent and beauty, trained daily, and even experienced in real combat. In contrast, most of Shahram’s soldiers guarding the castle were ordinary soldiers.

‘In this world, individual martial prowess is more important than the number of troops.’

One against a hundred.

One rider against a thousand.

Enemy of ten thousand.

Things that would appear in myths or legends were possible in this world. Of course, it was natural in a world where gods and monsters actually exist and intervene.

I slowly moved forward, following the path opened by the Baekhwadan. I didn’t let my guard down.

「Heavenly Eye activates.」

I spread my Sense and searched for Shahram.

If I only kill Shahram, the Leader of Ohel, this battle will end in victory. If, here, I can’t kill him and he escapes…

‘Things will get troublesome. Since the desert’s foremost Assassin would be aiming for my neck.’

Actually, him aiming for my life doesn’t bother me much. Being attacked by an Assassin isn’t new, and since I have complete recovery, assassination is as good as useless.

‘If he starts intensively targeting my women or subordinates, things will become quite troublesome. Shahram must die.’

From the sky above the castle, purple flames flew. Because I had the Jeheonseo, I immediately realized it was an attack made with Spells.

‘Even if it looks like flames, it’s actually a Curse. If physically shot down, the Curse will scatter.’

That Curse will cling to people, regardless of ally or enemy. It’s a ruthless attack that doesn’t care about allies at all.

‘The method is for me to counter with Spells, or…’

“Demon King-nim! I will block it!”

Enas stepped forward and raised a translucent, tower-shaped shield Barrier in the air. The purple flames were blocked by the Barrier. Enas smiled and puffed out her chest.

“Did you see it?! My Barrier blocked the attack aimed at Demon King-nim! Demon King-nim! I will protect you with my Barrier!”

A small Doll rushed towards Enas. It was an Assassin moving without sound or presence.

Cheonma Shingong Chamjeolmaryong.

As I, who had already grasped it, swung my Hand, black blades appeared and split the small Assassin’s Body in half. Through the gap in the torn mask, what was glimpsed was a boy’s Face. A boy Assassin. It wasn’t particularly surprising.

“Hiiik?!”

Enas, who had no idea about the Assassin’s presence, fell backward and landed on her butt.

“Enas. Don’t act recklessly for no reason and focus on protecting the Baekhwadan from Curses. I am not so weak as to need your help.”

“Y-Yes, yes. Demon King-nim!”

Enas tensed and turned her eyes to the battle. Spell attacks flew from various places, and Enas blocked them with Barrier magic. It looked like a battle of spear and shield, but… there were too many spears. And Enas was also tiring rapidly.

“Oh dear. The Spellcasters’ objective is obvious. They are persistently targeting you, trying to wear you down even a little, since you use Spells as a price.”

Even as Harmod spoke, an Assassin rushed at me.

The Assassin was torn apart by the forepaw of Hukrang (Black Wolf) I was riding. Hukrang, who had mastered Mapung Shingong, was stronger than most Martial artists.

“Harmod. You deal with the Spellcaster riff-raff. Surely you’re not going to whine that you can’t even do that, are you?”

“Haha. I’m just an ordinary merchant, so fighting really doesn’t suit me… but I’m confident when it comes to spending Money.”

Harmod took out colorful jewels from his pocket and threw them into the air. The jewels disappeared as if melting into the air, and over 10 Mercenaries appeared.

It was Harmod’s skill called ‘Jewel Mercenary’. A luxurious skill that temporarily summons Mercenaries by consuming jewels.

“Among the jewels I possess, these are selected high-quality jewels. Ah, it’s such a waste.”

Despite Harmod’s jesting, the Mercenaries didn’t change a single expression.

“Employer-nim. Please give your command.”

“Find and kill the hostile Spellcasters.”

“Yes.”

As soon as the command was given, the Mercenaries moved. Of course, the real human Mercenaries Harmod had originally brought remained and guarded him.

“With this much, don’t you think I’ve done the work of 20 people?”

Harmod said with a laugh.

Regardless of that, I dismounted Hukrang.

“Seeing that you’re about to move yourself, it seems you’ve found Shahram.”

“I found him. I’ll finish it and come back.”

I stepped forward. With just that, I drew the gazes of those around me.

「Deception kills presence.」

The gazes fell away.

I can’t hide everything with Deception. From the start, Deception isn’t like an illusion. Deception is very difficult to deceive if the opponent is openly watching or paying attention. Even if my Deception is SSS rank, that’s still the case.

‘But it works well on low-level guys. But it won’t work on Shahram. Since he’s already experienced it once, he’ll be extremely wary.’

Hidden on the ground, an Assassin shot up and thrust a dagger towards my neck. I dodged by performing a footwork technique, then struck the Assassin’s Head with my Fist. The Assassin’s Head exploded.

‘As expected, Assassins with good detection abilities see right through me. Is it natural since I was the target from the start?’

This guy is absolutely not a more skilled Assassin than Shahram.

It’s just a matter of how Deception is used, the environment, etc. The Deception I used just now only killed my presence; it doesn’t erase my existence itself. Those with a certain level of detection ability can perceive me. Still, over 90% of the guys here can’t perceive me.

After killing two more rushing Assassins, I reached the back of the castle.

The atmosphere changed in an instant. At the back of the castle, not a desert-style building, but an altar of an unfamiliar style was installed.

‘It’s a place hidden by Spells. Well, I understand, since it’s not a respectable place.’

The black building itself was heavily permeated with the smell of blood.

I approached the entrance. The heavy door opened by itself, without me needing to open it.

Kuung!

“So you knew I was coming? Well, everyone has a plan.”

I stepped inside.

A long, continuous dark corridor greeted me. The corridor was extremely long. The space had been expanded by Spells.

On both sides of the corridor, bound prisoners were kneeling. Behind the prisoners, Spellcasters with their Faces covered by black cloth stood like statues, holding obsidian daggers.

At the far end, Shahram was sitting on a large, ornate chair, as if he were the Master of a king.

“For an Assassin, that’s an undeserved chair.”

“On this chair’s Armrest, I will decorate it with your skull. Because you are superior to anyone else I have killed.”

Uwooong.

The Jeheonseo vibrated.

It was sending a warning that it was dangerous for me.

Of course, I also knew this place was dangerous. I knew as soon as I entered that this space itself was filled with Spells.

I raised my Hand and glared at the Jeheonseo. It was a cracked Jeheonseo, but after putting it in my Inventory for a while, it had recovered to some extent.

“Telling me to run away? If you’re a tool, act like a tool.”

The vibrating Jeheonseo stopped.

Taking a breath, I operated the Cheonma Shingong. The moment I drew up Demonic Energy from my Dantian.

“Begin.”

Shahram’s ruthless voice echoed in the corridor.

The Spellcasters plunged daggers into the prisoners’ necks. With Gags in their mouths, the bound prisoners died instantly without even uttering a death cry. Dozens of prisoners were executed simultaneously, becoming sacrifices.

Jjaeng-geurang!

The Jeheonseo wrapped around my wrist shattered and fell to the ground. I gazed at the fragmented skull-shaped Prayer Beads emotionlessly. The power and knowledge of Spells vanished. I didn’t particularly regret it.

“Is this the trick you prepared? If I can’t use Spells, did you judge that you could kill me?”

I scoffed at Shahram.

That I could dominate several desert regions and become the infamous Demon King was not thanks to power like Spells.

“It’s not over. Begin the next. Don’t forget the Promise that Mahir will resurrect you.”

How he will resurrect them, I’m a little curious, but I had no intention of just watching.

Cheonma Shingong Cheonma Gunrimbo.

My right foot stepped on the floor.

The floor trembled, and the Spellcasters received pressure, spitting blood; they should have floundered against the Inner Demon that came. Even if mental defenses were prepared, the pressure itself cannot be ignored. However, the Spellcasters were fine.

‘Is this space special?’

Dozens of Spellcasters simultaneously plunged obsidian daggers into their own Hearts.

I finished my Resolve to use complete recovery.

Because I know about Spells. That dozens of Spellcasters sacrificing themselves is a far greater feat than offering thousands of prisoners as human sacrifices.

‘I’ll have no choice but to die once.’

However, there was no death descending upon me.

“I know it’s hard to kill you directly with Spells. You are favored by the King of Macheon… and above all, I know you possess the Black Ankh.”

“You investigated very well. Do you want me to praise you?”

I had never openly revealed the existence of the Black Ankh. Nevertheless, the enemy knows. Even at that fact, I was indifferent. Gabriel must have given them a prophecy or something anyway.

‘They might know about complete recovery.’

I felt a very faint light in this place disappear.

Darkness fell.

Darkness that couldn’t be pierced even with the Heavenly Eye, a darkness that was by no means natural. Even if I took out a lantern from my Inventory, it didn’t brighten at all.

The smell of blood disappeared.

No sound was heard. I couldn’t even hear my Heart beating.

Sight, Olfaction, and hearing were blocked.

I don’t know about taste, but touch was fine.

A situation that could only be advantageous to an Assassin.

Moreover, Shahram possessed the power to ignore body-protecting Qi.

‘Defense is the worst. It’s meaningless. Offense is the best. I confirmed that Cheonma Gunrimbo had little effect in this space.’

My means of attack are not limited to Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Cheonma Shingong Sakpungmaryong Gwangpungnyeong.

Even though I couldn’t see, I felt it. A black wind, centered on me, swept through the surroundings. If Shahram was near me, his Body would be sliced apart by the black wind.

The cold tip of a Sword touched my forehead.

「Crisis Detection activates. Remaining stacks: 7」

Simultaneously pulling my Head back, I reached out my Hand and grabbed Shahram’s neck. I threw him to the floor and swung my Fist. Through touch, I knew I was definitely beating him. I also felt him regenerating.

‘Once I’ve caught him, I can feel him.’

I instinctively dodged Shahram swinging his dagger. I couldn’t dodge perfectly. My skin was cut a few times, and blood seeped out. I didn’t stop and continued to swing my Fist at him. Even after smashing his Head and piercing his Breast, his counterattacking movements gradually slowed.

The abnormal darkness gradually disappeared.

My Heartbeat began to be heard.

The smell of blood stung my nose.

Beneath me was Shahram, no different from a Corpse. His Eyeballs were burst, and his Breast was pierced with holes. He looked like he would die even if left alone.

“…How did you know?”

“It’s skill power. To say that is a bit Shabby.”

“To kill an opponent without resistance, you aim for the Head. It’s obvious.”

“…Jibril-isiyeo.”

Before Shahram died, I threw a Fist.

「You acquire 514 AP.」

“He’s a pauper bastard.”
