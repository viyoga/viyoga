The start of this Taiwan liberation operation began with the hunters representing each nation gathering on warships and moving together.

I decided to travel from Seoul by military helicopter. The original plan was to go with Elena, Yuria, and Seong Jigon, but I chose to go alone.

'It's to hide my abilities.'

If I moved together with Elena, Yuria, and Seong Jigon, I'd inevitably stand out. From their looks to their power, they were beyond ordinary people.

'If someone investigates seriously, it'll come out that they have no past. Yeonhee Lee, being ex-agent, can thoroughly fabricate her own background... but doing that for others is difficult. There's no time for it either.'

Why go this far to hide my abilities?

'Because once people know about my abilities, it becomes a pain in the ass to obtain items related to them.'

Just look at Taiwan.

Even though they knew Korea was searching for top-grade summoning stones, they pretended to the very end not to know they had one. Even though they had no particular use for a top-grade summoning stone.

'I do plan to summon in Taiwan... but if I get caught then, there's nothing I can do about it.'

Yeonhee Lee appeared through spatial teleportation. Ever since reaching Character Level 60, she'd been using spatial teleportation so naturally that she appeared and vanished like a ghost. It was also thanks to the Seonui Pearl I'd lent her. With the Seonui Pearl, even if she exhausted her stamina and mental energy, she recovered quickly.

"I shall escort you, Your Excellency."

Yeonhee Lee extended her hand toward a space.

A door-sized warp portal appeared. A military helicopter port at some base. At its center, a military helicopter waited with its blades spinning and its engine roaring. I naturally stepped through the portal and climbed aboard.

There was already a passenger in the helicopter.

A man with a sturdy build and short hair sat with his arms crossed, wearing a stern expression. His clothes were a military uniform. His rank was Colonel.

Seo Han-o, one of Korea's five S-rank hunters. The only S-rank hunter in Korea belonging to the military. He swept his upright eyes over me as I boarded.

"...Sex King, is it? You look more ordinary than I expected."

"Got a problem? If you don't like it, I can go a round with you."

The level difference was at least 40. But the overwhelming power I'd expect from a real-world S-rank hunter wasn't there.

'Combat isn't decided by physicality alone. He'll have no information about me, so chances to win will come a few times. As long as I don't miss those chances, I win.'

If it comes down to it, I can just use summoning.

"Why should I fight you? I respect you. Because of your existence, the Republic of Korea is currently reaping enormous benefits. I'm grateful to you."

His low, somber voice carried the heavy tone unique to soldiers, but there was no hostility toward me in it. I let the tension drain from my body and leaned back in my seat.

Soon the helicopter took off.

"Still, I really can't understand registering on the rankings under the name 'Sex King.' If it's to hide your identity, couldn't you register under some other wholesome name? And recently you registered on the rankings under another strange name too. Why not register under a phrase cheering for the Republic of Korea? Like 'We Can Do It' or something. That kind of slogan."

Despite his rock-heavy appearance, his mouth was incredibly light.

"By any chance, is your unit's loyalty slogan 'Pilseung I Can Do'?"

Seo Han-o's eyes widened in surprise.

"How did you know? Ah, did you figure it out from the unit mark on my shoulder? But weren't you someone who hadn't served?"

"Saw it on the internet. I thought it was bullshit, but to think you actually use that retarded slogan. Change the slogan."

"I've also always thought the slogan was a bit strange. We're the national army, so using English is a bit..."

"That was the problem? You're a real idiot too. Do you make nationalistic propaganda videos at home or something?"

"Nationalistic propaganda videos. People belittle them as nationalistic propaganda, but they're informative videos that let you objectively learn about Korea."

"Bullshit. Are you one of Yeonhee Lee's faction too?"

"I know that Director Yeonhee Lee is a patriot. However, her methods are too extreme. I wish she would exercise restraint. When war breaks out, the first to die are the soldiers of the national army..."

From the way he talked, he knew that Yeonhee Lee was the real power in Korea. It was a fact everyone in the know was aware of.

Though the true hidden power was me.

"Enough boring talk. Just change your unit's loyalty slogan. Want me to recommend one? How about 'Sex'? In Korean, 'mating'!"

Just imagining it made me burst out laughing. Even adult manhwa wouldn't do that. No, wait. I feel like I've seen something similar in adult manhwa.

"Sex!"

Carried away, I even did a two-handed salute.

Seo Han-o, despite being a soldier overflowing with pride, didn't get angry. Rather, he stared at me intently and marveled.

"Your form is sharp. That's not a salute angle a civilian could show. You said you hadn't served... a soldier of innate talent, then. No thoughts of enlisting?"

My eyes went round. It was a worse insult than anything I'd heard recently.

"You crazy bastard. How can you tell a person to enlist?"

"Haha. A soldier's life isn't as bad as you think."

"Forget it. Change the salute slogan instead. If you change it to 'Sex' or 'Mating,' I'll grant you one request."

"Hm? Does that mean you'll grant any request?"

"There are limits, obviously. Something like asking for 100 million coins, I'd grant easily."

If he makes a shitty request, I can just ignore it or kill him.

"What if I ask you to participate in a war?"

Seo Han-o asked with an unexpectedly serious face.

"A simple request. Japan? China? America? Russia? Heh heh. Just say the word. I'll take on Korea too. If you want, I can even cut off North Korea's head. Ah, the condition is that the salute slogan stays for at least 10 years."

He wouldn't be able to change the slogan anyway. Even if Seo Han-o is a hero of Korea, he's not the commander-in-chief of the armed forces. Just looking at his rank, he's a colonel, not even a general.

"Is there no time limit on that offer?"

"None. But you're seriously considering this?"

"To anyone else, that would itself be an insult. Not to you. You'll probably become an S-rank hunter within 100 days. Maybe even something beyond that... a Transcendent. By then, you might be able to single-handedly defeat North Korea... no, Japan, alone."

If I set my mind to it, I could fight and win against North Korea or Japan right now.

"You're sly, despite your looks."

"I hear that a lot."

It didn't feel bad. In the end, it meant Seo Han-o acknowledged me. Open flattery is nice, but this subtle kind of flattery is also pleasing.

"Seo Han-o. If you'd been born in the Joseon Dynasty, you'd have become an outstanding sycophant."

"...Is that a compliment?"

"It's a compliment."

I tend to like sycophants. Most of them have a good grasp of their position.

The military helicopter landed on an American warship. On the deck were US soldiers and hundreds of hunters gathered from all over the world.

I climbed out of the helicopter, looked around, and clicked my tongue.

'Out of hundreds, there aren't even 20 S-rank hunters. And more than half are Chinese hunters.'

It was proof that China considered this operation quite important. But even China didn't have Wang Suan, who was world-ranking level.

"Korea, the closest country, arrived last."

One of China's S-rank hunters sneered. Seo Han-o ignored him, and I held back.

'I can't reveal my hand just to kill that insolent bastard.'

The hunters' gazes turned not toward Seo Han-o but toward me.

"Wait. Who is that man? To call him an A-rank hunter, the power I sense is at best D-rank."

Hunters could gauge a target's power to some degree.

"D-rank would be around level 20. ...No way."

"Sex King? Sex King came in person? Why?"

"Has Korea gone mad?"

It was the A-rank hunters making a fuss. The S-rank hunters quietly observed me. They had already grasped my identity to some extent beforehand.

A man walked out from inside the warship.

He was a muscular man wearing a blue skintight suit with a gold rhombus on it. A square jaw and blond hair. Handsome, sure, but he looked sleazy. He was like the protagonist of a superhero movie.

Chris Nelson.

America's world-class S-rank hunter.

By current standards, the strongest man on Earth. In America, people call him Lightman rather than his real name.

The reality was that he was a hero manufactured by the US central government's investment, but his talent was genuine.

Chris's eyes lit up the moment he saw me.

"You've come. I didn't know Sex King would come along... but I can tell Korea is that serious about this operation. I'll put in a good word with the President."

I didn't particularly respond to him.

'Chris being here means... the US President genuinely wants to kill Garshin. America has staked everything on this operation.'

The aliens who invaded Earth must have been quite detestable.

Chris looked around once, then spoke.

"Since everyone happens to be gathered here, I'll explain this operation. Currently, Taiwan is surrounded and blockaded by the warships of America, China, Japan, and Korea. To prevent civilian casualties, artillery and bombing are prohibited. We'll split into teams and strike Taipei simultaneously. You just need to draw the fishmen's attention. The fishmen's leader, Garshin, I'll handle myself."

He spoke with a solemn face.

Swoosh. An Asian man with a sword at his hip stepped forward. A Japanese katana hung at his waist.

Japan's S-rank hunter Yoon Dae-jun. No, the traitor Nakamura Jun.

"I heard that fish's level is over 70. Are you confident you can win?"

"I represent America. To disregard me is the same as disregarding America. To doubt me is to doubt America. Shall I take it that Japan doubts America?"

"Ah, no. That's not it. I was just a bit concerned."

"There's no need to worry."

Chris said firmly.

I listened to that exchange briefly and was dumbfounded.

'Seo Han-o and that guy both have tremendous national loyalty.'

I know the reason. The original work has a lot of stories close to nationalistic propaganda.

Anyway, the warship began sailing toward Taiwan.

The gathered hunters scattered in their own directions. My back felt particularly prickly.

'That traitor bastard. Why does he keep glaring?'

It wasn't just glaring—there was killing intent in his gaze.

'Japan is famous for ninjas. I need to be careful.'

That bastard is a native Korean, but there's no telling if he changed class to ninja in Japan.

KWAOOOOONG!

The warship shook with an impact sound. A shaking clearly different from waves. It had taken a hit from outside.

Soon an announcement rang out from the speakers.

-We are under attack from the fishmen! All hunters and soldiers, prepare for combat!

Considering there were still hours left until arrival in Taiwan, it meant this operation had been leaked.

'There's a traitor among humanity. What a complete mess.'

Seo Han-o, who had been sitting, shot to his feet and gripped his weapon, a mace, tightly.

"Seong Yujin. You stay inside."

"You're telling me to hole up in here?"

"You are Korea's future. If you die, it's the same as Korea's future being destroyed. Honestly, Yeonhee Lee sending you here in the first place was incomprehensible to me. You should prioritize your life above all. Rather, entering the Tower right now might be an option."

『A dimensional war is in progress. During a dimensional war, you cannot enter the Tower.』

The notification window popped up the moment he finished speaking.

Seo Han-o clicked his tongue briefly and dashed outside.

"Anyway. You prioritize your own life above all!"

I watched him, dumbfounded, then leisurely walked outside. I had no reason to listen to Seo Han-o's words.

"So you were here, Seong Yujin."

In the corridor, I came face to face with the sword-carrying traitor.
