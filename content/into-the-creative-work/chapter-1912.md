I went into the tent with Cliff.

Inside, there were two more people besides Professor Hemmington. Neither of them looked like members of the expedition team. The atmosphere was rough, and they were armed. Probably mercenaries.

,Did she hire two more mercenaries? This is an unexpected variable.'

I scanned the mercenaries' levels. One was a gunner handling firearms, and the other was a warrior using a greatsword. Neither were superhuman.

"Welcome. Welcome to the Hemmington Expedition."

Professor Hemmington approached me. She was a middle-aged woman with gray hair. She had a sturdy build for a woman. Her square jaw exuded confidence and strength.

"I'm Eugene Meyer."

"I'm Nora Hemmington. Come and sit down. I was just explaining the upcoming exploration to the other mercenaries. Ah, you guys should introduce yourselves too. You're temporary colleagues, after all."

The first of the mercenaries to speak was the warrior in partial armor. He was a man with a short, sporty haircut and scruffy stubble on his lower face.

"I'm Jin Extum. Magic user."

"How uneducated. And you?"

I looked at the gunner. The tall man in a leather coat, who had been sitting quietly, slowly opened his mouth.

"Josh Winter."

"Eugene Meyer, a magician."

The mercenaries' reactions were lukewarm. But they were scrutinizing me with their eyes. Just as I was doing, they were probably assessing my level.

"If the introductions are over, come and sit down."

I sat down. Nora Hemmington handed me a cup of coffee. I took a sip. As expected. It was trash. It didn't seem like cheap beans, but it seemed like they made the coffee carelessly.

"What I need you guys to do is escort the expedition team and exterminate monsters. Since the ancient ruins are old, some monsters have come in and are living there. A place where people's footsteps don't reach. A place monsters love."

"That's strange. Doesn't the expedition team have any combat personnel? How did you get here?"

Jin asked Professor Hemmington. He seemed uneducated, but he was keeping the minimum level of courtesy towards his employer.

"The people who were originally in charge of escorting the expedition team left because their funding was cut off. The current expedition members can fight if they have to, but we didn't come here to fight monsters, we came to explore the ancient ruins."

"Are you saying you're continuing the exploration even though the funding has been cut off?"

The tall gunner, Josh, asked as if he couldn't understand.

"This is the ancient ruins that we found with difficulty, really with difficulty. We used most of the funding to unlock the hidden seal. Besides, our expedition members, including me, have invested our personal assets. If we give up here, we'll only be left with debt. We must get our hands on the treasure below."

Nora said with a determined expression. She meant that she couldn't back out until she made a profit since she had invested so much.

"...Do we get paid?"

Jin asked worriedly.

"I needed a statement of assets when I applied for the request. Don't worry, I have the money to pay the request fee. I'm a professor at Miskatonic University. Even though the expedition team's funding has been cut off, that status is still valid."

"Hoo. That's a relief."

The explanation continued after that.

After dinner, I and the mercenaries met separately. Jin arranged this meeting. He puffed on a cigarette and opened his mouth.

"If you guys have worked in this industry, you'll know. The atmosphere here is strange."

"Don't act unnecessarily weighty, just get to the point."

"For a magic user, you're awfully impatient. In short, I have a bad feeling. They're so strapped for cash that they're taking out loans, but they have the money to hire mercenaries? That's kind of weird, isn't it?"

"It is weird. So? What are you going to do? Quit if you don't want to do it."

I said with a hint of sarcasm. I already knew that the Hemmington Expedition was strange. From my perspective, these mercenaries were more of a variable. It would be better if there were no mercenaries.

"Oh, shit, you're so prickly, just like a magic user. What I'm saying is, let's stick together in case of the worst-case scenario. If we stick together, our chances of survival increase. That's obvious, isn't it?"

"The expedition team is one thing, but can I trust you?"

"Don't be like that. We're in the same boat. There's no harm in preparing, is there?"

"...He's right. There's no harm in preparing. I have a bad feeling too."

Josh, who had been quiet, said. It became difficult to object since he, who had been quiet, stepped up. I sighed and said.

"What are we going to do together? Are we going to stick together and stab the expedition team in the back?"

"I'm not sure that the expedition team will stab us in the back. I hope my feeling is just a hunch, but... let's stick together when I'm sure. After that, we'll decide whether to stab the expedition team in the back or run away, depending on the situation. Got it?"

"Insurance."

"As the gunner-hyung-ssi said, it's insurance for survival. How about it?"

"Not bad. Insurance is just insurance."

Josh said. I also nodded. It was literally insurance. I wasn't going to keep working with these guys anyway. There was no particular loss.

"Good. Let's share anything suspicious as soon as we find it."

The expedition team headed to the ancient ruins early in the morning.

Professor Hemmington directed us as the leader of the expedition team.

"Jin. I'd like you to scout. We've already explored halfway and found that there are no traps."

"You said you only explored halfway? What about the traps after that?"

"We'll have to go down carefully then. Check well to see if there are any monsters. There's a saying that you shouldn't show your back to a wild beast. That also applies to monsters. Of course, you, as a mercenary, know that better than I do."

"......"

"Eugene Meyer. I want you to continuously use search magic. If there are any monsters or anything unusual, tell me right away. Ah, and recharge the batteries when we go down. As an electric-type magician, that shouldn't be too difficult, right?"

"I understand."

I answered casually.

Professor Hemmington's intention was obvious. She was trying to exhaust the mercenaries' stamina and mana from the start. It's also what mercenaries do, so the other mercenaries didn't seem to realize it.

I went down below the ancient ruins. I looked around and admired it. Hemmington, who was near me, smiled at my reaction.

"It seems like you felt the mana lingering in every corner of the ancient ruins. You're a magician after all."

"I knew theoretically that ancient ruins naturally accumulate mana over time and transform into dungeons, but it's amazing to see it in person."

"Everyone is like that at first. When I first explored the ancient ruins, I walked while examining every single stone. It was full of so many amazing things."

"It doesn't seem like you do that now."

"I wish I could. But time is not eternal and is finite. The expedition team doesn't move alone, so... we have to use our time as efficiently as possible."

If she had checked every single stone, she might have discovered something new. Like the golden bracelet I found yesterday, or items like the Golden Gift.

Soon, we arrived at the middle zone. It was a kind of open space, and a huge stele was blocking our way. Ancient words were written in the center of the stele.

I don't know ancient languages either. But I know what it says. The original game kindly tells you with subtitles. It was a question asking those who wanted to move forward.

-Are you ready to sacrifice your life?

Professor Hemmington naturally interpreted the ancient words written on this stele. As much as she was called a professor, her skills were real.

She walked forward while receiving the gazes of the expedition members.

"We are fully prepared. Please open the way forward."

The ancient words written on the stele shone for a moment.

Deureureureureuk!

The stele split in half and opened like an automatic door. A dark path appeared in front of it.

"As expected of Professor Hemmington-nim."

"It's a pity that this is the Hemmington Expedition's last exploration."

"Professor-nim! What was written on the stele?"

"It's nothing much. It was asking about our resolve."

Professor Hemmington said with a smile.

"Ah, I see. It really wasn't much."

The expedition member also said with a smile. Jin, who was walking ahead, flinched. He felt awkward for a moment.

The expedition members were all in cahoots. They knew that this ancient ruins needed a sacrifice. They also knew that it was an ancient ruins that demanded human sacrifice. For reference, the more unusual the sacrifice, the better. The reason they hired mercenaries instead of ordinary people was also for that reason.

,But the expedition team doesn't know what's inside the ancient ruins. They couldn't have experienced it in advance.'

But I am different.

I experienced these ancient ruins through the game. Not only that, but I also know three different strategies.

Soon, we arrived at the first gate. The ancient words written on the stele were faintly shining.

"Step aside. I'll interpret the ancient words first."

Professor Hemmington stepped forward. She interpreted the ancient words with a serious expression. I saw the corners of her mouth twitching slightly.

"Professor-nim. What do the ancient words mean?"

"...Um. It's a slightly twisted ancient language. It will take some time to interpret. You guys should investigate to see if there's anything unusual here."

Professor Hemmington's instructions were given. The expedition members took out their tools and began their investigation. Some searched with magic, some examined every nook and cranny with special tools, and some lay on the floor and put their ears to the ground. They were all different.

I charged the batteries while watching Professor Hemmington.

Professor Hemmington must have interpreted the ancient words as soon as she saw them. And the content was.

-Offer a woman.

There are currently 24 people here in total. Among them, there are only 4 women, including Professor Hemmington. From the start, the professor's plan began to go awry.

Professor Hemmington was now wondering who to sacrifice and how.

Soon, she called Cliff, the vice-captain. It seemed like she had made a decision on what to do.

"Clara. Can you come over here?"

Cliff called Clara, the youngest and only other woman on the expedition team.

"Yes!"

They decided on a victim in just 3 minutes. I glanced back. The door leading out was still open.
