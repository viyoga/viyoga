「Entered Section 6,333, the Castle of the Obsessed King.」

I'm struck anew by how powerful information is.

Just knowing about Sections 6,331 and 6,332 allowed us to reach Section 6,333 without any harm.

Perfect information and a perfect strategy.

The Gods must think Kang Myungjin-nim is a true regressor.

'But there are no regressors in this timeline.'

Kang Myungjin-nim is a man who entered his younger sibling's novel. Even though he hasn't experienced these events himself, he's proceeding without a hitch. It can only be explained as a protagonist buff.

Kung!

As soon as we entered the castle, the gate slammed shut. We didn't panic and surveyed our surroundings. Old, broken chandeliers barely hung from the high ceiling. The interior of the castle was much larger than it appeared from the outside.

-Again.

A deep, male voice echoed. The A-Plan Legion's movements froze at the voice that filled the space.

-What are you here to steal again, invaders!

The voice, filled with resentment, carried intense animosity.

-I won't let you take anything more! Invaders! I will take your lives to protect us!

Everyone except Kang Myungjin-nim and I took a battle stance. But there was no attack.

Kang Myungjin-nim fidgeted with his halberd. The little Azure Dragon hidden in Kang Myungjin-nim's chest rubbed against his sideburns. He tensed up, unlike before we arrived here. Even knowing the strategy, he couldn't relax from here on out.

'Did Yuria-ssi successfully enter the hidden area? It's frustrating not knowing her situation.'

Kang Myungjin-nim, holding his spear, stepped forward.

"This is where the real deal starts. Just because everything has gone smoothly so far doesn't guarantee it will continue to go well."

He swung his spear at the old, torn black curtain covering the window on the right wall.

"Kkyaaaaaak!"

A scream erupted as the black curtain tore. The black curtain flew to wrap around Kang Myungjin-nim's body.

"Hmph!"

The spear was swung three times in quick succession. The fragmented black curtain fell to the floor, turning into wrinkled leather. Seeing the eyes and mouth areas in various places, it was definitely human skin.

"This castle itself is our enemy. Don't let your guard down."

Kang Myungjin-nim's dragon eyes shone brightly.

"Let's go."

•••

Clatter, clatter.

Five knights in black armor walked towards us from the hallway.

"Invaders... kill... invaders... kill...!"

"Kill...."

"Geeeeeeh...."

The faces visible inside the helmets looked like withered mummies. Blue ghost fire burned where their eyeballs should have been, and black energy crawled out of their mouths and bodies like smoke.

Death Knights.

Knights who abandoned the comfort of death for the sake of faith, more loyal than anyone else.

"The Death Knights' strength lies in their overwhelming physical abilities and defense, as well as the swordsmanship and experience they had in life. Never let your guard down."

Kang Myungjin-nim said. We all prepared for battle. We applied the holy water we had prepared in advance to our weapons. Since there was no one in the A-Plan Legion who could use the power of light or holy power, which are the natural enemies of the undead, we had no choice but to use items.

'Undead.... Since they're already corpses, they won't be electrocuted, so I would have thought they were annoying bastards in normal circumstances.'

But I had Hwaryeon Bido. Even if I didn't apply holy water to the blade, Hwaryeon Bido itself possessed great magic power, which could be said to be the nemesis of demons and the undead. The Red thunder manifested through Hwaryeon Bido also possessed great magic power.

"Astrape."

Red lightning surged from the blade.

I, along with Kang Myungjin-nim and Joo Seo-hyun-ssi, rushed towards the Death Knights and engaged in battle.

I ducked under the Death Knight's sword, which was swung in a circle, and dug inside. Red lightning burned the Death Knight's body. But it couldn't stop the death knight who didn't know pain.

'Kang Myungjin-nim is stirring things up well in the center, and Joo Seo-hyun-ssi... I don't need to worry. Even though her physical abilities are low, she's overcoming it with swordsmanship. She's doing well.'

It's not that we're perfectly in sync. We were fighting separately. But we were still overwhelming the Death Knights. That's because our skills were superior to the Death Knights.

I stabbed the Death Knight's body with my sword. But it didn't fall easily.

Booom!

I dodged by rotating my greatsword, and used the rotational force to hit the bastard's head. Kkaang! The helmet cracked, but I couldn't smash it. Just as I was about to retreat, feeling a tingling in my hand, the Death Knight's greatsword split into two and aimed for my neck and abdomen.

'An instant!'

The world slowed down. Looking closely, the greatsword wasn't split, but it was an illusion sword created by swordsmanship. The one aiming for my stomach is the real one.

'...Ha. Interesting.'

I deactivated Astrape. I wanted to defeat the bastard in front of me purely with swordsmanship, without using acceleration or an instant.

Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Heaven Style) Byeokgye (Blue Stream).

I swung my sword as I passed by the Death Knight. I aimed for the neck, but the Death Knight raised his gauntlet-clad arm like a shield to block it.

'Since it's a corpse without eyes, Byeokgye didn't work...? No, it's because of that bastard's skill.'

The Death Knight moved his heavy greatsword delicately and exquisitely. It's dangerous. But at the same time, it was also an opportunity.

Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Heaven Style) Paewol (Covering the Moon).

I dodged the bastard's greatsword by a hair's breadth and launched a counterattack. The Death Knight's helmet shattered.

I fell into the battle. The Death Knight trusted his armor and focused on defense, aiming for a heavy blow. On the other hand, I repeated hit-and-run tactics.

I was the victor of the battle.

After more than 30 attempts, my sword finally broke through the Death Knight's defense and severed his neck.

"I... lost...."

The Death Knight's inner light disappeared, and his body crumbled.

Looking around, the A-Plan Legion members were watching me. In particular, Joo Seo-hyun-ssi's gaze was unusual. She bit her lip tightly and glared at me. The fact that she was clenching her fists made it seem like she was frustrated.

"Ah, what? Was I the last one?"

"Seong Yujin-gun. I understand. The feeling that the surrounding time slows down and your consciousness becomes one with your weapon. It's a feeling that's not easy to experience. But restrain yourself now. It's dangerous in the current situation, and we're currently conquering the area."

Kang Myungjin-nim said seriously.

The feeling of becoming one with the weapon? It seems Kang Myungjin-nim is misunderstanding something.

I nodded vaguely. From experience, arguing would only result in more nagging directed at me.

"Okay."

•••

What stopped our steady progress was a broken statue. Four statues in the shape of demons were shattered and lying on the floor.

The others didn't think much of it. Having come this far, they had experienced that this castle was an old castle where the undead appeared. They wouldn't make a fuss just because a statue was broken.

But Kang Myungjin-nim, just one person, couldn't hide his agitation.

"No way.... Why is this.... Why are the gargoyles dead?!"

He was so agitated that a soliloquy popped out of his mouth. Kang Myungjin-nim's head must be working busily right now.

'Yuria-ssi passed through here. Did she get caught by the gargoyles? Well, gargoyles are originally monsters that guard the gate. Even for Yuria-ssi, it would have been difficult to deceive these guys with her reduced stats.'

Kang Myungjin-nim must be confused.

If someone had entered the castle before us, the initial voice shouldn't have been heard.

'She entered through a place other than the entrance. Without being caught by the owner of the voice, the ruler of this area.'

But due to the nature of this area, it was inevitable to be caught if there were many people. What Kang Myungjin-nim could think of was that all the enemies were skilled at hiding their appearances like assassins, or that there was only one target.

"Master? What's wrong?"

Ji Youngbin-gun called out to Kang Myungjin-nim. Kang Myungjin-nim suddenly came to his senses.

"...These statues are gargoyles. They pretend to be statues and attack people. But now the gargoyles are broken and dead. It means that someone came one step ahead of us and dealt with the gargoyles."

"Is that possible? Aside from anything else, it's impossible to conquer Section 6,331 without that mysterious compass. And until now, there hasn't been any trace of someone coming before us."

Yoo Inha-ssi, who had been silent until now, spoke. People nodded their heads. Thorough preparation was needed to get here, and there was no trace of anyone going ahead.

"Maybe they were tailing us and overtook us at some point."

"Master. Then shouldn't we move immediately? Before they gain control of the area."

"......I can't be sure that it's them. It could be alone, or it might not be a person. The purpose isn't to dominate the area either.... Hmm."

"If it's not domination, then what?"

"It could be something else. Either way, we need to move immediately."

Kang Myungjin-nim must have thought that their unknown purpose was the hidden area.

And later, he would find out that the hidden area had been looted by someone first. At that time, he wouldn't be able to suspect me or the others, even if he wanted to.

Because the information about the hidden area was only known to Kang Myungjin-nim.

A skull wearing a golden crown on its head was sitting on the throne, waiting for us.

"Invaders... you finally came this far. My soldiers, arise! Protect the kingdom from the invaders!"

"Yes, Your Majesty!"

70 elite soldiers appeared.

They are undead, but they are actually alive. They are merely temporarily manifested by the king's authority.

"Yoo Seohee-ssi. Yoo Inha-ssi! It's your turn!"

Kang Myungjin-nim shouted.

"Yes. We're finally stepping up. I've been itching to move since I've mostly been watching from behind."

"......."

Yoo Seohee-ssi used her skill, Seduction. All 70 soldiers were men. Their gazes were all fixed on Yoo Seohee-ssi, who was twisting her voluptuous body. The soldiers staggered towards Yoo Seohee-ssi, drooling.

"Seohee-ssi! I will protect you."

Ji Youngbin-gun shouted as if it was an opportunity.

"...Fire Blast."

"......Arise again! My soldiers! Even the flames of hell cannot burn our souls."

"...Yoo Seohee-ssi, Yoo Inha-ssi, Ji Youngbin-gun. You guys take care of the soldiers. The rest... kill the king."
