We walk the path to the summit with swelling expectations.

Just before reaching the peak of Domyung Mountain, an old man appeared and blocked our path. He was an ordinary-looking old man. There was no mystery or divinity about him. He looked like a neighborhood grandpa you could easily find nearby.

First Grand Master Myeonghon, and now this? Why are so many bastards suddenly appearing to block me? As I stirred up murderous intent, the old man waved his hand.

,Calm down. I have no intention of fighting you. I have only appeared to congratulate you on reaching the summit of Domyung Mountain."

,I'll gladly accept the congratulations. Now, why don't you fuck off?"

,You're quick-tempered. I can't say I don't understand. Grand Master Myeonghon attacked you, after all. I'll apologize on behalf of my old friend's rudeness."

,Why didn't you stop him instead of apologizing now?"

,Myeonghon was stubborn and pursued justice more than anyone else among us. He never let the wicked live in his lifetime. If he saw a criminal, he killed them; if he saw a rogue martial artist, he killed them; if he saw a Demon King, he killed them. Myeonghon sincerely believed that was what was best for the world."

,And that bastard died by my hand. Are you here to avenge that bald monk?"

,No. Myeonghon broke the rules first. I have no justification to retaliate against you. Myeonghon said you would bring a storm of blood to the world... but that's something the mortal realm must resolve. We, as the deceased, can only fulfill our destinies."

,So, what's your real purpose?"

,I told you. To congratulate you. Not only on reaching the summit. I congratulate you on your good fortune."

,Good fortune?"

,Domyung Mountain appears once every thousand years. Unfortunately, no one reached the summit in the last cycle. I think you understand what I'm saying, right?"

I didn't understand. Is he messing with me? Should I just beat him up first?

As I was contemplating, Milyeong opened her mouth.

,...The energy from the last cycle has accumulated and carried over to this ritual."

,That's right. In other words, you can gain even more than originally intended. So, how could I not personally congratulate lucky ones like you! Those who enjoy fortune that even we, who designed and conducted the Domyung Mountain ritual, could not! Congratulations! Heaven seems to be on your side! Now, go up and claim your sweet rewards!"

Clap, clap, clap.

The old man clapped and moved aside, clearing the path.

He really didn't do anything and just stepped aside. I was still suspicious of him. What if he backstabs me like Grand Master Myeonghon? There weren't many trustworthy bastards in this world.

,Should I just kill him first?"

The old man looked at me and gave a wry smile.

,I cannot defeat you. Also, we are merely the deceased and cannot receive the rewards of Domyung Mountain. Go on up. I will keep an eye on Guihon Heuksu."

The legend of Domyung Mountain says that the blessings of the sky and stars descend upon those who reach the highest point.

The sky is one thing, but the stars? Which star exactly is giving blessings?

Reaching the summit of Domyung Mountain, I felt the wind and looked around. I couldn't see Milyeong and Seong Jigone-ssi, who were on my left and right. Instead, I saw hazy clouds. Below me, I saw a massive mountain.

I was standing on the peak of that mountain. Looking up, I saw the sky. The higher it went, the darker it became. Countless stars twinkled there. Light fell from the most brilliant star at the center.

The starlight slowly descended and enveloped my body.

,It'll grant me what I desire? Then what do I desire?"

To save Milyeong from her coma? I already know how to save her. Milyeong-ssi said she would cooperate, so once this is over, I'll go straight to Milyeong and solve her problem.

Peerless martial arts and an increase in my cultivation realm? Peerless martial arts are already in my hands. An increase in my cultivation realm? I can do it with my own power. Because I have the Heavenly Gang Star System.

,I have the Hwaryeon Bido as a weapon. I have the Stormbreaker as armor. What I want is...."

Suddenly, my vision changes. I knew that Domyung Mountain was showing me something else.

There was a handsome man and a dying old man. The old man's eyes lacked strength, and his emaciated body was undergoing cell necrosis from his toes up.

The man asked the old man.

,Immortal Hae Yeong, why have you left the beautiful Immortal Realm behind and descended to the human realm again? I cannot forget the sight of you abandoning everything and ascending to the heavens that day. Is it even possible to descend from the Immortal Realm to the Lower Realm? If so, why don't the immortals intervene in the human realm?"

,The Immortal Realm is an illusion. There was no Peach Blossom Land. Ascension is just a trap. Immortals are not virtuous, and gods are not omnipotent. The Immortal Realm I saw was no different from the human realm. It has its own laws and power."

,...Is that so."

,You don't seem surprised. Did you already know?"

,I knew that immortals are not virtuous. There's no way a world filled with such beings could be a utopia. What do you mean by ascension being a trap?"

,Ascension is heading to the Immortal Realm through a path opened by the immortals. In exchange for using that path, you lose your power and become an immortal. Then you become a slave to the immortals and work for them. Even if you're lucky enough to accumulate power, you need to cultivate for hundreds of years to become a god."

,...So that's why ascension is a trap."

,No. Losing power and living as a slave to the immortals? We can do that anytime. We've already accumulated power and crossed worlds. What's the problem with cultivation? Rather, cultivation is more advantageous for us than for immortals born in the Immortal Realm. Ascension is just bait to hide true transcendence. They don't want transcendents to appear."

,Transcendence is breaking free from all limitations and becoming a primordial god. The Immortal Realm, the Underworld, and other worlds do not want the birth of a god."

,Can't you transcend in other worlds?"

,You can. But it's almost impossible."

,Is it possible in the human realm?"

,The human realm. It's also called the Middle Realm. Do you know why it's called the Middle Realm? Because it's at the center of all worlds. Therefore, the human realm has a higher probability of successful transcendence than other worlds."

,I understand that ascension is a trap. Then how can one transcend?"

,I don't know. They're hiding it thoroughly.... What's certain is that they will try to stop transcendence by any means. So, if you attempt transcendence, do it as quietly as possible. Just seeking information about transcendence can draw the attention of the Immortal Realm."

,Not only the Underworld... but the Immortal Realm can't be trusted either."

,The Middle Realm is the same. Don't trust the Guardian Gods. They can communicate with the Immortal Realm separately."

,Could they be the ones hiding information about transcendence?"

,That's what I suspect. Jin Hyeon-a, don't obsess over transcendence. The you I saw is a genius beyond compare. You will eventually transcend. Quietly wait for that time."

,I'm not interested in transcendence itself, but if transcendence is necessary for the order of this world, I will do it."

,More importantly, Jin Hyeon."

Jin Hyeon.

The Emperor.

The absolute ruler of this world. People dare not use his name.
