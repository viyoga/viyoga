「You have acquired 7,381 AP.」

AP obtained from killing Logride.

In the case of humans, you randomly acquire anywhere from 10 to 80% of the AP the dead target possessed.

'With my high Luck stat, I'd get at least 60% even in the worst case. Even accounting for that, this AP is pitifully low.'

There was something better than the AP that bastard had.

「You have killed the ruler of District 9,373, the Black Quarry. You usurp rulership.」

「You have become the ruler of District 9,373, the Black Quarry.」

「You will acquire 100,000 AP every 30 days.」

I rummaged through Logride's corpse. Not to desecrate it—just checking for anything useful.

'A demon of his caliber should be carrying at least one or two decent items, right?'

「Protective Footwear

Footwear enchanted with protective magic.

You can walk over fire without a scratch.

Rank: A」

「Helium Ore

A special mineral that only appears in Hell Zones.

Rank: S」

'Trash.'

Others might not care, but for a man with standards as high as mine, it was unsatisfying.

They were better than the shoes I was currently wearing, at least.

'Not good enough to justify wearing someone else's shoes. They even smell.'

Washing them right now was too much hassle, so I tossed them into my inventory.

Helium—I know that one too.

It's a mineral that only appears in Hell Zones. Helium is hard, heavy, and conducts mana exceptionally well. It also had the power to amplify curses.

'Helium ore is decent, but only one piece?'

For now, I put this in my inventory too.

'No, wait. Does helium ore even come from the Black Quarry district? Judging by the fact that this bastard was carrying it, seems like it does.'

I pulled out the Hell Zone map and unfolded it.

To confirm the exact location of the Black Quarry. Sure enough, it was nearby.

'It hasn't been updated yet. They said it updates periodically. Maybe it refreshes by the hour?'

Mammon hadn't told me exactly how many hours between updates.

'That son of a bitch. If you're taking my money, you should give me accurate information.'

I clicked my tongue briefly and moved to District 9,373, the Black Quarry.

District 9,373 had black soil as far as the eye could see. At its center was the quarry. Beside the quarry sat a small, hastily thrown-together village. I'd assumed a quarry meant stone mining, but they were digging coal.

I entered the village entrance. Scruffy demons dropped to their knees the moment they saw me. Judging by the pickaxes they carried, they looked like quarry laborers.

"New lord of this district! We follow the lord! Please, do not doubt our loyalty!"

An old demon pressed his forehead to the ground.

Naturally, I didn't trust them. Even if demons and demonic beasts obeyed a district ruler for the moment, they could betray you at any time. The system allowed anyone to become ruler by killing the current one—trusting demons 100% would make me a fool.

'They'll act obedient on the surface. Then the moment I look weak, or they're convinced they can kill me, they'll turn.'

No need to slaughter them preemptively out of fear of betrayal. Even if they're demons, these ones aren't worth half of the Logride I just dealt with.

"Does helium ore come out of this quarry?"

"I-it does. If we're lucky, we can mine about one or two pieces a week."

One or two a week? Who's supposed to survive on that?

"You mine coal instead of stone? What's the profit like?"

"We mine coal and sell it to hell merchants. We can earn about 3,000 AP a week. The lord takes seventy percent, and we split the remaining thirty."

12,000 AP a month.

Pocket change.

"Where are the hell merchants?"

"Hell merchants are wanderers. They come to the district periodically. Usually about once every ten days, so... they should arrive in four days."

"What do hell merchants sell?"

"All manner of goods. Water, food, equipment... They also sell mercenaries and slaves."

"Slaves? Keheh. Now that gets my blood pumping. Maybe I'll catch that bastard when he shows up in four days and shake him down."

The old demon was startled by my words.

"A-absolutely not!"

"What?"

Had this lowly grunt just told me no? My right hand had unconsciously gripped the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido. If not for the superhuman patience born of my Absolute Mind, I would have split his skull in half and checked how a demon's brain differed from a human's.

"Y-you must not antagonize the hell merchants. Each one's individual power rivals a Hell Count, and their influence extends across the entire Hell Zone! If you make enemies of the hell merchants, a bounty will be placed on your head and you'll become a public enemy of the Hell Zone!"

Hell merchants were that significant? I decided to change my plan. First, I'd meet one and see for myself.

'If they seem manageable, I'll kill them and take everything. I don't plan on staying in the Hell Zone long anyway.'

I was confident I could secure supplies without hell merchants.

"Until I give separate orders, keep doing what you've been doing."

I walked past them and entered the manor at the village center. Though it was only slightly better maintained than the other buildings.

I searched the manor's interior. Nothing particularly noteworthy. The warehouse, at least, was packed with war supplies. Looked like Logride had been buying war materials the moment he earned AP.

'The only real find is this orb.'

「Hell Merchant Emergency Summoning Orb.

Breaking the orb summons the nearest hell merchant.

Rank: B」

'Good thing I don't have to wait four days.'

I threw the orb to the floor.

The glass orb shattered and a wave rippled outward. The wave spread beyond the manor.

I sat on the sofa and waited for the hell merchant.

Ten minutes later.

Knock. Knock. Knock.

Someone rapped on the front door. A polite knock, pleasant to the ear.

"I am Prada, a hell merchant. May I come in?"

"Enter."

The door opened and the hell merchant stepped inside.

A red-skinned demon in a suit. Three horns protruded from his head, and a demon's tail was visible behind his back.

"Your appearance and bearing are similar to Mammon's."

"Oh. So you've met him. All of us hell merchants are his subordinates. May I sit across from you?"

"Sit."

"Thank you."

He sat on the sofa opposite me. He was neither arrogant nor servile.

"For a merchant, you don't carry any goods."

"Most of my wares are in subspace. A privilege of being a hell merchant. May I ask why you summoned me?"

"I was curious. Is there anything besides the Hell Shelter shop?"

"Truly rare items can be obtained from the Hell Shop. The Hell Shop is essentially our headquarters."

"Aren't you going to ask about Logride, the former master of this district?"

"We do not discriminate between customers. Whether the customer is human or demon, it doesn't matter. What matters is whether the customer can pay. And from what I can see, you are wealthy. You have that distinctive aura of a rich man, you might say. To put it more crudely... the smell of money radiating off you is enough to drive me insane."

The demon grinned, mouth splitting wide. When I furrowed my brow, he wiped the smile off immediately.

"One warning. Out of respect for Mammon, I'll let this slide exactly once. Cross the line one more time and I'll kill you."

"...Understood. I was rude. I apologize. We don't apologize empty-handed, so... please accept this demon mushroom."

He held out a red mushroom with horns growing from it.

「Demon Mushroom

Tastes so good it could kill you.

On first consumption, if your Mana stat is below 100, you die.

On first consumption, if your Mana stat is 100 or above, one of your stats increases.

Rank: A」

I popped it into my mouth immediately.

「You consume the Demon Mushroom.」

「Strength increases by 1.」

"You carry an interesting mushroom. Tastes like garbage, though."

"It's a mushroom our customers love. The taste is indeed the worst."

I pulled out a cola can and drank. The sweet taste of cola washed away the demon mushroom's aftertaste.

"Th-that's cola. A precious beverage rarely seen in Hell...! If you have more, would you please sell some to me? I'll buy at 30 AP each! If you could sell me just about 100..."

"I have no intention of selling right now."

If I price-gouged with modern items, the system would almost certainly throw a fit.

"I see. That's a shame."

"I heard you sell mercenaries and slaves."

"Yes. Mercenaries and slaves are among the goods we handle."

"If a mercenary or slave betrays me, do you compensate?"

"Even in the rarest of cases, that won't happen, but if it did, we would compensate you."

"How are you so certain?"

"Mercenaries and slaves have signed contracts with him. If they break the contract... they will meet a fate worse than death. Not a single being has ever broken a contract."

Prada spoke with a voice full of confidence.

"Good. I want to purchase mercenaries or slaves. Can I see the goods?"

"I apologize, but mercenaries and slaves can only be viewed through the catalog. The actual goods are delivered immediately upon purchase."

Prada pulled several catalogs from subspace. Each one was thick, looking like 300 pages apiece.

I picked up the catalog labeled Human Slaves.

The moment I turned the first page, I was greeted by a portrait of a middle-aged man.

「Name: Bali

Race: Human

Slave Career: 72 years.

Slave Traits: Grows potatoes with breathtaking skill.

Slave Price: 1,500 AP per month」

"1,500 AP a month? Don't tell me this is a subscription."

"As expected of an intellectual from modern society. You hit it exactly. It's a subscription."

"Can't I buy them permanently?"

"That is possible. However, their souls are already traded to him, so... the price is considerably higher. The human slave Bali would be about 1.5 million AP."

"The price jumps a hundredfold?!"

Prada whispered in a very small voice.

"A thousandfold."

"The price jumps a thousandfold?!"

"That's because the contract with him is being terminated. Only then does a slave gain the freedom to grow old and die."

"So slaves are immortal."

"To be precise, they die. The subscription is simply canceled. Of course, they can be re-subscribed through a hell merchant."

"Hmm."

I flipped through the catalog pages, examining them.

Most were men—farmers, blacksmiths, merchants—nothing combat-related.

「Name: Marie de Criana

Race: Human

Slave Career: 3 years.

Slave Traits: Of a viscountess's lineage; knows how to please a man.

Slave Price: 20,000 AP per month」

She was a woman with brown hair and downturned eyes. Dressed in a lavish gown, skin pale.

"A sex slave?"

"A slave is whatever you make of them. She can be used as a sex slave or a prostitute. Marie is a beautiful woman."

"She's passable. You didn't Photoshop the photo, did you?"

"Photo... shop?"

Prada echoed back. He clearly had no idea what Photoshop was.

"Ignorant demon. You don't even know Photoshop. I'm asking if you used a filter on the photo."

"Ah, I-I see. The slave's appearance is exactly as shown. We never manipulate our photos."

"Everyone says that. Modern people don't fall for that crap. I need to see the real thing—bring her here."

"A-as I mentioned earlier, the actual goods can only be viewed after purchasing the slave."

「You pay 20,000 AP.」

"Right now!"

"I'll bring her immediately, customer!"
