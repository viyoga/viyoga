Kwajik!

A spear pierced the chest of Killpaon, the 4th Legion Commander and Demon Dragon. It struck Killpaon's weak spot. In other words, a fatal wound.

Killpaon gritted his teeth and glared at the enemy before him.

A demon of human origin who recently became the new Legion Commander. The 8th Legion Commander, Baal. Her snow-white hair was exceedingly cold, and her red eyes were colder than the abyss.

,"That damn bitch...! She finally killed the 6th Legion and is now killing even this lord! Haven't you realized your identity yet? You're not human anymore, you're a demon...!"

"I know. The reason I'm killing you is simply to become stronger. The law of the jungle. I can't just ignore the prey in front of me, can I?"

Killpaon mustered his last strength for a final struggle. The land of hell shook violently.

"The outcome has already been decided."

Pajijijijijijik!

Purple lightning surged from Baal's spear, tearing Killpaon's body to shreds.

Baal used the Authority of Gluttony. Black smoke flowed from her body, engulfing the corpse that was once Killpaon.

An Authority that absorbs the power of those she kills. If the target is a demon, she can even absorb their Authority. However, she couldn't absorb it in a 100% perfect state. The absorbed abilities and Authorities are degraded. For reference, the Authority Killpaon just absorbed was Amplification.

,'That was a longer battle than I expected.'

Contrary to his reputation for being ferocious, Killpaon acted despicably until the very end. He was a snake, not a dragon. It was quite difficult because she also had to deal with the 6th Legion in the middle. But in the end, she was the one who won.

,"Baal-nim...!"

At the voice calling her, Baal turned around.

The 8th Legion. A legion of loser demons who weren't recognized by the other legions. Due to the war with other legions, its size had dropped to less than half.

The one who called Baal was a black cat with bat wings. Kihid. Uniquely weak among the demons of the 8th Legion. But he was the only demon in the 8th Legion who received Baal's favor.

The reason for the favor was nothing special. It was because he was cute for a demon.

"Yes. Kihid. What's wrong?"

Kihid was useless in battle because he was weak, but he had the ability to bring back good information.

"The 7th Legion, which invaded Earth, was annihilated in reverse, just like the 3rd Legion! 7th Legion Commander Germadon has also been extinguished...!"

"Too bad. I was going to kill him."

"What's worse is that the greatest hero of mankind appeared and massacred the 7th Legion!"

"...The greatest hero of mankind?"

"Kwancheon's Thunderclap, Seong Hari-ssi...!"

"......"

Another version of herself.

No, should I call her the original? Because she is just a fake.

Baal closed her eyes, feeling a surge of hatred. Anger and jealousy. She struggled to suppress those two emotions.

She is the demon Baal. Not the human Seong Hari. She cannot meet Seong Yujin. In a way, it was a good thing. If Seong Hari-ssi is that strong, then Earth, Seong Yujin-gun will be safe.

"And the 5th Legion has rebelled! The 1st Legion and the 5th Legion are currently at war!"

Baal's eyes widened at the completely unexpected information.

The remaining demon forces now are the 1st Legion, the 5th Legion, and the 8th Legion. The remaining two forces are suddenly at war.

"What's the situation? Who's winning?"

Even as she asked, Baal predicted the 1st Legion's victory. The 1st Legion had the opposite status of the 8th Legion. A legion of only the most elite demons.

"The 5th Legion is currently overwhelming the 1st Legion!"

"...The 5th Legion? How?"

"5th Legion Commander Mekin is dominating the battlefield! The impact of the 1st Legion Commander failing to subdue the 5th Legion Commander in the first battle and being defeated in reverse is significant!"

"That seemingly ignorant guy was hiding his strength. If this continues, the 1st Legion will lose?"

The real power of a demon legion is the Legion Commander. A demon with overwhelming power. Even if the demons of the 1st Legion are elite, they can't match the Legion Commander. From the moment the 1st Legion Commander was defeated by the 5th Legion Commander, the 1st Legion's chances of winning were low.

"Baal-nim! You're not going to get involved in this war, are you...?!"

"It's an opportunity to reap the benefits. Prepare."

"Yes, Baal-nim!"

The demons of the 8th Legion answered.

If they were to get involved in this war, the demons of the 8th Legion would likely be annihilated. But Baal didn't care. She wasn't very interested in the 8th Legion.

-I will not allow it.

The voice of a vast and overwhelming being echoed. The 8th Legion, which was heading to the battlefield, stopped. The demons of the 8th Legion, except for Baal, knelt on the ground, trembling.

"It's a Demon God!"

"The Demon God is speaking!"

"Ah, Demon God-isiyeo!"

Demon God.

The Outer God that demons worship.

The being who had been silent until now finally intervened. The tip of Baal's finger trembled. She didn't dare to confront him. The opponent was not someone she could confront. To the Demon God, she was no more than an insect.

,'There's only one reason why the Demon God intervened. If I participate in the war, the demon legions will collapse. As a Demon God, he wouldn't want to lose the demon legions themselves.'

The demon legions invade and destroy other dimensions. Most of the destroyed dimensions are offered to the Demon God. The Demon God grants the demons power, immortality, and the Authority to invade dimensions.

,'To the Demon God, the war between the 1st and 5th Legions is... a kind of thinning out? No. How would I know the intentions of an Outer God? Anyway, the 5th Legion will win the war.'

What would happen after that was difficult for Baal to know.

"The plan has changed. Let's rest."

The 8th Legion turned around.

And several dozen days later, the 1st Legion Commander died.

Korea was invaded by demon legions twice.

Once by the 3rd Legion. Once by the 7th Legion.

The 3rd Legion's invasion was prevented without any other damage thanks to Kang Ji-young's sacrifice. That Kang Ji-young's sacrifice was a bit big, but it was news that ordinary people couldn't really grasp. This is because the battle ended before ordinary people could even access the information.

But the 7th Legion's invasion was a different story. Machine demons rampaged throughout Seoul. Property damage was enormous, but casualties were low. This is because machine demons focused more on plundering machines than killing people. The top priority of the machine demons was to concentrate resources on Germadon.

In addition, it took less than 15 minutes for the machine demons to fully invade and end Seoul.

Germadon was ridiculously easily extinguished, and Seong Hari-ssi, who was freed from the Spirit King's curse, single-handedly massacred the machine demons.

Before the president could issue martial law, while the military was preparing to mobilize, and before the Hero Association could issue a summons to heroes nationwide, the situation was over.

The strongest had returned.

When Kang Ji-young died, Korea was uneasy. The KOSPI plummeted and the number of people immigrating overseas skyrocketed. But this time it was different. Even though demons invaded Seoul, the KOSPI rose instead of falling. Immigrants and foreigners who had gone abroad wanted to come to Korea.

There was only one reason. It was because Seong Hari-ssi showed overwhelming power. Consequently, the world was shaken by Seong Hari-ssi's official appearance.

So what happened to Seong Hari-ssi? She became the head of the Academy.

Behind that, there was a little bit of Seong Hari-ssi's involvement. Seong Hari-ssi's status is now above the president.

And the companies that sponsored Kwon Sang-hoon also went bankrupt. This is because the investigation revealed that the companies had joined hands with demons. The representatives of the companies were also sentenced to death without exception. The United States, which was watching Seong Hari-ssi, also liquidated companies within its own country.

Korea held a state funeral for those who died in this incident. After the state funeral, they returned to their daily lives.

Maruhan Academy also returned to its pre-reform state.

The world was peaceful.

Even now, they might be plotting some scheme to invade Earth.
