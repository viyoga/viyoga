The day the Dark Moon rose had arrived.

The air itself was different on the day the Dark Moon rose. From that morning, I moved with Marta and Deneb. Lena was Rank 5, so she decided to stay at the church.

I looked up at the sky. The sunlight was the same as usual.

,But the moon has already changed.

It was just that the moon hadn't risen yet in Divine France. However, Divine France was gradually being affected by the Dark Moon from the daytime.

,Even though Divine France has good air, it feels stuffy. The Dark Moons I've experienced so far weren't this bad.

Other people quietly ignored it. Because anything could happen on the day the Dark Moon rose.

I couldn't ignore it like them. Something came to mind.

,The Fallen, Calix of Corruption. It's not for nothing that his title is Corruption. He must have powers related to corruption... The stuffy air could be because he's using his power.

To have this much influence even before the Dark Moon has risen in the sky. It was clear that he was an existence that couldn't be ignored.

The place we headed to was a prairie so sparsely populated that it was practically deserted. Marta and Deneb tilted their heads, as it was just a plain prairie with nothing special.

"Is there something to do in this prairie?"

"Even if I examine it with magic, there's nothing here."

I also looked around. My senses didn't detect anything special either.

,In the original game, there are hidden dungeons and ancient ruins that only appear when the conditions are met.

The most common of those hidden conditions is 'It must be the day the Dark Moon rises.' This case is like that. Today is the day the Dark Moon rises, so the first condition is met.

,The second condition is that it must rain.

I looked up at the sky. Apart from it being the day the Dark Moon rose, the weather was clear itself.

"Did you know? It never rains in this area on the day the Dark Moon rises."

Deneb wasn't very interested in my words. She doesn't show interest in anything other than gambling or betting. Still, Marta reacted to my words.

"Really? The Dark Moon doesn't rise every day, so isn't it not surprising? It could just be a coincidence."

"You have to doubt everything on the day the Dark Moon rises. Even that coincidence."

[Dark Cloud]
