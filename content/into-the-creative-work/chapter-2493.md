Peng Cheon-su received a lengthy message. It was a report from his subordinate.

,The two thugs attached to Seong Yujin are dead. According to the police cooperating with us, they died fighting each other in a hotel room.'

That was plausible enough. They were thugs at heart, after all. But it wouldn't escalate to the point of killing each other.

,Seong Yujin killed them.'

And Seong Yujin returned victorious from the Devil's Game. The devil's power had manipulated reality. Even if they searched the room thoroughly or checked the surveillance cameras, no evidence related to Seong Yujin would remain.

The devil's power manipulates people's perceptions and reality. Those who don't know about the Devil's Game are bound to be toyed with.

,Those who know about the Devil's Game are not affected by the devil's power.'

That was why Peng Cheon-su could manage the Devil's Game participants in reality.

,Seong Yujin. This bastard killed three people. Since I didn't put a leash on him from the start, he's impossible to control. The tracking device was also discovered. I don't know where he's hiding, but I'll find him and kill him.'

However, there were many things to do right now. Seong Yujin was like a slippery loach, but he hadn't done anything to Peng Cheon-su, so there was no immediate need to kill him. Seong Yujin might die in the next Devil's Game.

,What's urgent now is...'

Peng Cheon-su thought of his subordinates. Those who had participated in the Devil's Game long ago and obtained magical tools and abilities as rewards. As they gained power, these bastards began to have other thoughts. It was expected, but he didn't like it at all.

,I should participate in the Devil's Game soon.'

Knock knock.

,Chairman-nim. I've brought the prepared item.'

,Come in.'

A well-built man in a suit came in carrying a silver briefcase. Peng Cheon-su received the briefcase and placed it on the desk. Opening the briefcase revealed a suit. Of course, it wasn't an ordinary suit. The inside of the suit vest was made of small chains, and the metal sleeves of the jacket could be made into knives.

,In the Devil's Game, you can only bring Devil's Game tools, except for clothing. The important thing is that it must belong to the clothing category.'

Chainmail armor is not allowed. But clothes filled inside with chains are allowed. It means loopholes are possible.

,The first game is about escaping from a murderer and surviving. In Korea's case, it's the serial killer Jang Hwi-chun.'

The Devil's Game occurs every midnight all over the world. When playing the Devil's Game, you also meet Americans or Europeans. They also start the game at midnight. Time difference? The devil's power makes things like time meaningless. The devil was omnipotent.

,If you kill Jang Hwi-chun in the first game, you can get additional rewards. You can also gain abilities that don't come out easily in the gacha.'

The most coveted are the truth discernment ability and the precognition ability. Truth discernment has considerable potential depending on how it's used, and precognition can see a short future. Moreover, if a bad future occurs, it even gives a direct warning.

,Of course, I know that neither is a panacea. But they are abilities that will definitely be helpful.'

In particular, Peng Cheon-su wanted precognition. When his subordinate had that ability, he almost died. As soon as the subordinate who obtained the ability saw the future where he died, he betrayed him and tried to kill Peng Cheon-su.

He opened a drawer and took out a black card. White letters were written on the front of the card.

-This is an invitation to the Devil's Game.

-If you put your fingerprint on the invitation, you will participate in the Devil's Game at midnight that day.

Peng Cheon-su had already made a decision. Once he made a decision, he never hesitated.

Kkuk.

He put his fingerprint on it. And he picked up his smartphone.

,It's me. Today is the day.'

And when midnight came, he participated in the Devil's Game and killed all four Jang Hwi-chuns, obtaining additional rewards. He was lucky. The place he started was a large supermarket, so there were many things that could be used as weapons. Moreover, he was with his subordinates.

As soon as Peng Cheon-su arrived, a notification window popped up.

&lt;Warning, give up everything and return to your hometown.&gt;

,What kind of ability is this? Is it telling me to commit suicide? Is it a curse rather than an ability?'

&lt;The user can only receive advice once a day.&gt;

,Is this supposed to be advice?'

Peng Cheon-su glared at the notification window that appeared in front of him and went down to the basement with his subordinates. In the underground prison, there were 20 debtors tied up.

They were piggy banks prepared for this day.

「Game Points Held: 0」

Peng Cheon-su, after checking his game points, took out a pistol from his pocket.

,S, save me.'

,I, I paid back the money. I'll give you more money...!'

,What I want is game points. Unfortunately, game points can only be obtained by killing.'

Tang, tang, tang.

Peng Cheon-su broke open 20 piggy banks.

「Game Points Held: 372」

The game points obtained were less than expected. It couldn't be helped. The things here were trash anyway. The rest should be prepared for later.

「Game Tool and Ability Enhancement - 50 GP」

One ability enhancement. 50 game points disappear.

&lt;Warning, if you cannot give up everything and return to your hometown, commit suicide.&gt;

,Are you kidding me?'

&lt;The user can only receive advice twice a day.&gt;

Just in case, he tried to enhance the ability, but it turned out like this. Peng Cheon-su gritted his teeth. The reason he had worked so hard to prepare until now was not to receive such an ability.

,I'm unlucky. It'll probably be the same even if I enhance the ability. I need to fix it right now.'

If you select a new ability while the ability is enhanced, the enhancement is reset. You have to enhance the ability from the beginning again.

,It's not too late now. I'll cut my losses quickly.'

Like doing business.

「Game Tool and Ability Gacha - 30 GP」

「Acquired a hot box cutter.」

「Acquired a game-exclusive recovery potion.」

「Only one game tool can be selected.」

「Choose one between the hot box cutter and the game-exclusive recovery potion.」

Peng Cheon-su's expression relaxed. The game-exclusive recovery potion was the game tool he had been aiming for. Luckily, he immediately obtained the game tool he wanted.

,Game-exclusive recovery potion. It heals most wounds on the spot. The effect gets better if you enhance it.'

Changing the ability is more important than enhancing the game tool. He continued to draw gachas.

「Acquired an electric fly swatter.」

「Drew the fast-eating ability.」

「Drew the diving ability.」

.......

「Drew the Voice of the Heart ability.」

When the points were running low, he stopped drawing.

「Drew the Voice of the Heart ability.」

「Only one ability can be selected.」

「Choose one between Advice and Voice of the Heart.」

,Of course, it's Voice of the Heart.'

Just by the name of the ability, he could guess what kind of ability it was. Compared to the advice that only spouts useless nonsense, Voice of the Heart is much better.

After changing the ability, he looked at the subordinate standing next to him. He was a smart guy who had studied abroad and was working as his secretary.

&lt;Using Voice of the Heart on the target. You can hear the target's Voice of the Heart for 3 seconds.&gt;

&lt;Thinking about the cleanup is terrible.&gt;

,Ji-chan-ah. Do you regret becoming my subordinate?

,No, Chairman-nim. Why are you asking that suddenly?

&lt;Using Voice of the Heart on the target. You can hear the target's Voice of the Heart for 3 seconds.&gt;

&lt;I do regret it sometimes. But I make a lot of money. If hyung-nim takes a position later, he'll give me a seat too, right?&gt;

&lt;Voice of the Heart can only be heard twice a day for 3 seconds.&gt;
