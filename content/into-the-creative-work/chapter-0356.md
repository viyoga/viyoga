356. Plum Blossom Sword Master Reincarnated in a Baronetcy

In the capital of Rapelli, four people were gathered in the room of Chancellor Makellos Helbrit.

The atmosphere was far from amicable.

Two were trembling in terror, one had resigned himself, and one stood there coldly.

Makellos shifted his gaze to the side. His wife and cousin, Katrin, was shedding tears instead of her usual ruthless face, clutching their child, Halios, while trembling. Halios's condition was the same as his mother's. However, he had already lost his right arm.

Makellos looked straight ahead.

In front of the corpses of 20 escort knights piled up like fallen leaves, a woman with the same hair and eye color as him stood expressionlessly.

Makellos remembered that it took less than 30 seconds for the 20 escort knights to become cold corpses. The woman in front of him was already a monster with powers beyond common sense.

He wiped the cold sweat flowing down his forehead with the back of his hand. No matter how much he thought about it, he couldn't think of a way to survive in this room.

"Is this by order of Baron Pruckus?"

Makellos recalled Baron Pruckus, who had maintained an impudent attitude in front of him. At the time, he thought that Pruckus must have a powerful backer whom he couldn't easily touch. He had planned to find out who the backer was after the banquet.

However, there was no backer. Baron Pruckus had intended to kill him and his family from the start.

"...A long time ago. On the day my mother died. I made a deal with my master."

"I see. Baron Pruckus saved you. I can guess what kind of deal it was. He wanted my head... no, the destruction of the Helbrit family."

"Yes. Instead of taking revenge for my mother with my own hands, I decided to dedicate everything to my master."

"The revenge was successful. Congratulations. But why are you hesitating? With your skills, you could kill us in an instant. What do you want from me?"

Yuria looked at Makellos. At first, she wanted to kill him as soon as she saw him. It was excruciating to suppress the surging murderous intent. But now, with the end of her revenge in sight, she was calm.

"I want information about the nobles who follow you. More precisely, I want their weaknesses that you hold in your hands."

"...Do you think you can control them just because you know their weaknesses?"

"Are they better than you, who are about to die now?"

"...That's not true. Alright. I'll control them."

"If you cooperate, I won't torture you. I'll cleanly cut off your head and kill you."

It was the moment Makellos was about to reveal information about the nobles. His son, who was next to him, shouted.

"Father! Are you giving up like this?! I don't want to die! I don't want to die!"

"There's no other way. It's better than dying from torture."

Halios gritted his teeth and glared. Fear made his body tremble, but he couldn't lose everything and die like this.

"I understand that you are my younger sister. I feel sorry for what happened to your mother. But it's not too late yet. You and I are family! Come back to your origin, your hometown, the Helbrit family! I will take you as my wife! We can rule this kingdom and live well, keoeok!"

A dagger flew and pierced Halios's chest. It was accurately lodged in his heart. Halios collapsed to the floor.

"Kyaaaaak! Halios!"

Yuria also stabbed Katrin in the neck with a dagger as she screamed.

The surroundings became quiet. Makellos took his eyes off his family's corpses. He had already accepted his death.

Yuria remembered every piece of information he spat out, without missing a single one.

Yuria approached Makellos right in front of his nose and asked.

"Do you regret it?"

"Yes. I regret it. I should have found Nell Rins and killed him for sure..."

She raised her dagger. A dark, shadowy black aura blade stretched out like a longsword.

"...Are you satisfied?"

"Yes. I am satisfied."

She smiled silently and swung her dagger without hesitation, beheading Makellos.

Yuria glanced at Makellos's head rolling on the floor and closed her eyes for a moment.

"Mother..."

This was because the heads of the Helbrit ducal family were hung on top of the royal castle.

The most powerful person after the king had been assassinated.

The royal castle was naturally in chaos. The nobles were not allowed to leave the capital, and knights and soldiers moved to surround the capital and conduct a search for the culprit. As time passed, the tragedy that occurred at the Helbrit's main family was also revealed.

Various conspiracy theories circulated.

Baseless rumors spread, such as a secret assassination squad from another kingdom invaded and erased the Helbrits, or an assassination guild that had a feud with the Helbrit Duke carried out the deed.

People said that perhaps there was only one assassin. The rumor, which no one knew where it started, quickly spread widely. People who liked legends and heroes even treated the assassin as a kind of hero and gave them a nickname.

Dark Emperor.

It was the nickname of the worst assassin whose existence was unclear.
