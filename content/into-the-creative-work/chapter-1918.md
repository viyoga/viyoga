,This is the outskirts of the Heist Empire.'

I had business at a facility here.

,Lumen Prison.'

A notorious prison where heinous criminals are transferred. One of the prisoners there was my target.

Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company.

The world's best pharmaceutical company that joined hands with the Freisel Kingdom army and conducted the Battle Mage Project with Unit 667.

I have to meet an affiliate of the Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company, which I have to eliminate, my target for revenge.

According to my own investigation, he worked as a team leader at the headquarters. In other words, he knows a certain amount of information about Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company.

He was an employee of Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company.

That alone is reason enough to take revenge. I will kill all the bastards related to Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company. I decided so. That's my revenge.

,No, no. Calm down. Don't get too excited.'

I forcibly calm my mind.

My plan is this.

I approach Lumen Prison, kidnap and kill a guard. Then, using the Absolute Polymorph of Lucid Dream, I transform into a guard and infiltrate the prison.

It's a bit dangerous, but if I act well, I won't get caught. And I was quite confident in my acting.

It's not human experimentation for black magic research, but black magic research for human experimentation... but what I get is the same, so it didn't matter.

Bang!!

,Did an accident happen nearby?'

The visibility is dark and it's raining heavily. It wouldn't be strange if an accident happened.

,It's not far. Should I check it out?'

I quietly headed towards the sound. I could hear shouts and the sound of cold weapons clashing.

,A battle. It's my unlucky day... why now? Is this going to be troublesome? Or...'

It might be an opportunity.

I approached the battle area, suppressing my presence as much as possible. The battle was over when I approached. Seeing how quickly the battle ended, it was highly likely to be an assassin's attack.

A black sedan was off the road and crashed into a rock. Judging from the completely crushed hood, there seemed to be no answer other than scrapping it.

When I lowered my gaze to the floor, I saw corpses. Several bodies of burly men were scattered everywhere. The corpses could be divided into two categories. Those wearing imperial military uniforms and those who weren't.

,Lumen Prison is run by the Imperial Army. Were they attacked on their way to the prison?'

The Heist Empire was a militaristic country. A country where everything revolves around the military. Resistance forces. There were many things called rebel forces. It's not strange for the Imperial Army to be attacked.

,This is the outskirts. And the Imperial Army here are people related to the prison... I can roughly get the picture. Did the inmates' backers attack?'

It's just a guess, so I'm not sure. There might be something else.

I walked past the car and under the shade of a tree.

A black-haired man, who looked to be in his 30s, was collapsed. His left arm was cut off and his abdomen was torn. It's a fatal wound.
