Wiiiiing.

Three drones flew over and collected the remains of the dog robot. Na Chae-young-ssi and other researchers will analyze the dog robot to figure out the technology that went into it. Of course, it won't happen in a short amount of time.

-"Are you okay?"

At Na Chae-young-ssi's worried question, I pondered for a moment. I didn't know what she was worried about.

"About what?"

-"About Jeong Du-jeong, really. That pilot said it earlier. He said he was a subordinate under Jeong Du-jeong."

"Jeong Du-jeong? That washed-up has-been? Jeong Du-jeong from when? If he comes at me with bullshit, I'll give him bullshit right back."

China was split up, and Jeong Du-jeong became an ordinary person.

He's in a situation where he should be extending a hand to Korea and asking for an alliance. To turn Korea into an enemy here? That's suicide.

Even if Jeong Du-jeong kneels before me and asks for an alliance, I have no intention of accepting.

We continued to move forward.

At a fork in the road, we found a human corpse. A corpse that was cut, torn, and crushed. Just by looking at it, it seemed like it had fought a dog robot.

'Looking at the equipment, it seems like a trained elite soldier... Did they get taken down by the dog robot without being able to do anything?'

He was a guy I could have crushed with one punch, but the dog robot's combat power seemed quite high. However, the dog robot is not at the Knight level.

'The thing about guiding me to the Magma Eater's location was bullshit after all.'

I probably would have been led into a trap.

"Since it's a fork in the road, we'll split into two. Squad 2, go to the left. Squad 1, go to the right with me."

"Understood, Your Excellency."

Recover the corpse?

I didn't. There was no time to do so, nor any reason to.

We continued to go inside. Another fork in the road appeared. After that, we didn't divide people and chose the right side.

'The cave is getting deeper. Should I have sent a reconnaissance drone first? No, drones alone can't handle even elite soldiers, let alone dog robots. It would have only raised their vigilance.'

There were at least four forces in this cave.

Me, Jeong Du-jeong, the Magma Eater, and the Jilin Province forces.

Other warlords from China may have participated as well. Magmanium is that coveted of a material.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

Just in case, I used electromagnetic waves. The electromagnetic waves were absorbed into the material supporting the cave and disappeared. Radar was useless in this cave.

'The Magma Eater's slime is this strong. That means Magmanium also has EMP resistance.'

That's why it's an even more desirable technology.

I, who was walking forward, stopped in place. When I stopped, the Knights also stopped and guarded the surroundings.

My gaze was fixed on the ground.

-It's a booby trap, ishiyeo. It must have been hard to see because the visibility is so dark....

"I can see it just fine."

It was a kind of experience.

As a Hunter, when exploring dungeons, you never know where monsters will pop out, so you had to look without missing even the smallest thing. That became a habit. No ordinary trap can screw me over.

"Your Excellency. I will disarm the trap."

"Forget it. I don't have time for that. A trap of this level won't cause much damage anyway. We'll break through. There are probably enemies nearby. Kill them if you see them. No need for prisoners."

I lift my foot and step on the booby trap.

Bang!

The booby trap exploded. Fragments from the fragmentation grenade hit the power armor. My power armor was fine. It would be a pain in the ass if fragments got into the joints, but I'm not an idiot, so I stepped at an angle where the joints wouldn't get hit.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

I continue to step on booby traps and advance.

Soon, Chinese soldiers appeared. They were wearing combat suits and exoskeleton equipment. They were nothing compared to the power armor we were wearing.

The Chinese soldiers and the Knights shot at each other. The enemies' bullets couldn't penetrate the power armor, and our bullets shattered even the enemies' combat suits. The level of equipment was different.

'That's how it should be. Look how much I invested in the Knights.'

Soldiers, equipment. Everything was top-notch. Only the best could become Knights.

It didn't take long for the Knights to annihilate the enemies.

"Your Excellency. We have secured the terminal attached to the enemy's combat suit. The terminal is intact."

"Jena. Hack it."

Son Tae-hyung-ssi put the hacking device on the terminal. The hacking was finished in less than 10 seconds.

-Hacking complete. Loading map information.

A 3D map is drawn.

Dots are marked all over the map. Green dots are scattered everywhere, and some dots are painted red.

"The green ones must mean allies... What are the red dots? It knows the location too well to be enemies."

-Disconnected allies. They seem to have been attacked by the enemy.

"Hmm. Were they attacked by Jeong Du-jeong's forces? Where is the Magma Eater's location?"

-There is no exact location information, but after analyzing the information, it is expected to be here.

Jena marked it in yellow on the map.

It's quite far.

And Chinese soldiers were waiting everywhere.

"Why are these bastards waiting without moving?"

-It seems they are waiting for reinforcements after already engaging the Magma Eater once.

"So, that means these guys couldn't kill the Magma Eater with their level."

While they were waiting, Jeong Du-jeong's remote-controlled robot ambushed them.

"Go straight. Kill anyone you see immediately."

-It seems our information has already been passed on to the enemy. Wouldn't it be better to change the route? We could be surrounded by the enemy.

"Break through before the enemies gather. And reinforce the Knights on this side as well."

I looked back at the Knights and said.

"We're running from now on. Any complaints?"

"No, sir."

Kung kung kung kung kung!

We started running, shaking the ground. The power armor was heavy, so it wasn't the speed of a person sprinting. It was more like jogging. As Knights who had already surpassed human limits in terms of physical strength, they continued to run without getting tired.

We broke through booby traps and killed enemies when we saw them.

That's how we arrived at the lowest cave.

The Magma Eater was visible, burying its head in the slowly flowing lava. Its long body was covered in carapace. Each of its more than 20 legs was sharp. Its overall appearance resembled a centipede. Its size was enough to fill more than half of the cave. At least this place was wide. It had widened the cave to eat lava comfortably.

The Magma Eater lifted its head and looked at us. Grinder-like teeth were visible between the jaws covered in carapace. It seemed to chew not only lava but even rocks.

'I can't understand it. What nutrients does it get from lava?'

I immediately dismissed the question that came to mind. There's nothing as meaningless as discussing common sense in this world.

I accept it as it is.

The Magma Eater moved its mouth and spat lava at us. We quickly dodged the lava that was being shot like a water gun. Getting hit by that wouldn't kill us immediately, but some parts of the power armor would definitely melt.

Taking advantage of the gap in our formation, it rushed at the Knight at the very end. Instead of dodging, the Knight grabbed a Mackerel Blade and activated the neural accelerator. He was prepared to die and finish off the Magma Eater.

Some of the Magma Eater's legs blocked the Mackerel Blade. The carapace was so hard that even an ultra-vibrating blade couldn't cut it in one go.

That's how a momentary gap occurred.

The Magma Eater wrapped its body around the Knight's power armor.

Kwaddeudeudeudeuk!

The power armor was crushed in an instant, and the Knight died.

Kwaaaaang!

The Knight self-destructed, but it only ended up leaving scratches on the Magma Eater's carapace.

"Dr. Na, was the Magma Eater this strong?"

-"I didn't know it would be this strong either. I don't think armor-piercing rounds will work either...."

The Knights scattered and surrounded the Magma Eater, firing their rifles. Heavy gunshots rang out in succession. Armor-piercing rounds did hit its carapace. But they couldn't penetrate the carapace. The firepower was insufficient.

I threw an ultra-vibrating throwing star from my waist. The throwing star hit the Magma Eater's head. Yellow blood flowed from its forehead. The wound was shallow. It didn't seem to be a fatal wound.

The Magma Eater glared at me as if enraged and rushed at me as if diving. Son Tae-hyung-ssi ran up from the side and stabbed the Mackerel Blade into its body with all his might.

"Kkieeeeeeeek!"

The Magma Eater writhed in pain with a scream. Son Tae-hyung-ssi couldn't hold on and fell, rolling on the floor. Instead of attacking again, it ran away at a rapid pace into one of the caves.

"Chase it."

I ordered.

The Knights quickly chased after the Magma Eater.

Instead of chasing them, I turned around.

There was a giant spider-shaped machine.

'I think they called it a multi-legged tank.'

Thick steel plates, long gun barrels and blades instead of cannons. Judging by the red blood and entrails plastered on its body, it must have killed Chinese soldiers on its way here. The multi-legged tank shone its red sensors.

"It's been a while. Unfortunately, I don't need your cooperation anymore."

"Are you the guy who was controlling that dog robot?"

"Yes. That's right."

I pulled out a pistol from my waist and fired at him. The special armor-piercing rounds, which only numbered three, hit the multi-legged tank's body. The steel plate was so thick that it didn't penetrate. Only a part of the steel plate was dented.

'Actually, I was aiming for the sensor... but I missed.'

This time, his gun barrel turned towards me and spat fire. I moved to the side to avoid the bullets that were pouring out like a Gatling gun. I took cover behind a suitable rock.

He immediately stopped firing.

'Did he run out of bullets? Or is he deliberately saving them?'

It's probably the latter. I have to assume so to account for variables.

I drew the Mackerel Blade.

Wiiiiiiiiing.

A silver-shining greatsword.

The generator mounted on the power armor also began to operate.

Pajijijijik.

Tatatatatatatatatang!

Bang!

A momentarily obscured vision.

Pajijijijijijik!
