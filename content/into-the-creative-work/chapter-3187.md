After exchanging greetings with Ha Heon-shin, the demonstration began immediately as we headed out to the training grounds.

An open lot several times larger than the training facility. The invitees, myself included, sat on prepared plastic chairs and watched the demonstration.

-This is a special-material round shield for F-rank Hunters. It has strength exceeding steel at half the weight, with resistance to fire, flame, acid, and more. Unless you're dealing with D-rank or higher monsters, this shield won't break during combat.

An employee explained the equipment into a microphone while a prepared Hunter demonstrated. They couldn't actually bring in monsters. In South Korea and most other countries, the principle is to kill monsters on sight. Unless the demonstration was held inside a dungeon, there was no way to use actual monsters.

Fwoooosh!

The mage spewed fire from his palm like a flamethrower. The F-rank Hunter blocked the flames with a single shield.

No one was surprised by the sight. Everyone here worked in the Hunter industry. That level of display was nothing special at all.

Ha Seung-hee, sitting in the seat next to me, tapped my shoulder. When I turned my gaze, she quietly asked.

"What do you think, sunbae? Does that shield seem usable?"

"For F-rank, it's over-specced. Honestly, even regular people can kill F-rank monsters. It's not like modern weapons don't work on them."

"If you were F-rank, sunbae, would you use that shield?"

"No. I don't know how to use a shield. Even if a shield is good, if you don't know how to use it, it's better not to."

"What about F-rank Hunters who do know how to use shields?"

"They'd want it. But that's all. F-rank Hunters only need to deal with F-rank monsters, so why would they need equipment that can handle E-rank or D-rank monsters? And F-rank Hunters don't earn as much as you'd think. That shield has poor cost-effectiveness from an F-rank Hunter's perspective."

"You don't think F-rank Hunters would buy it?"

"They'll buy it."

"But you said the cost-effectiveness is poor?"

"Other things aside, Hunters don't really weigh cost-effectiveness when it comes to equipment. You could lose your life while calculating cost-effectiveness. Even if it's a bit expensive, over-spec is better. You know that much, don't you?"

"I may have an E-rank Hunter license, but I don't know the Hunter industry in detail."

Ha Seung-hee had no intention of working as a Hunter. She knew well enough that she had no talent for fighting.

*She said cost-effectiveness, but that's still by Hunter standards. If that shield doesn't exceed a hundred million, it's on the reasonable side. Even if it does, people who'd take out loans to buy it will buy it anyway.*

The demonstration was quite entertaining to watch, so time passed quickly. Before I knew it, an hour had gone by.

I looked around and spotted a foreigner.

A large man with short brown hair. He had three silver piercings in his right ear. He was the man who hadn't greeted me earlier inside the building either, quietly standing in a corner.

Of course, there was no way I'd take interest in a man for no reason.

*He's an A-rank Hunter. And above average even among A-rank Hunters. In this room, excluding me and Ha Heon-shin, he'd be the strongest.*

I looked away immediately. I wasn't curious enough about his name to bother.

-The final product being introduced at this demonstration is the Blast Spear, a weapon specially crafted for A-rank Hunters. Usage is simple. Condense your power and throw it. It's single-use, but the effect is guaranteed. If the conditions are met, it can take down an A-rank boss monster in a single strike. It's also been proven to deal effective damage to S-rank boss monsters.

Ha Heon-shin approached my seat. His secretaries and his son Ha Jun-su came with him.

"Hunter Seong Yu-jin. Might I ask a favor of you?"

I tilted my head slightly and looked at Ha Heon-shin. Unlike Ha Heon-shin who showed no reaction, his secretaries flinched. They seemed to find my attitude rude. Since I kept my mouth shut and my eyes innocently open, I decided to let it slide.

"It depends on what the favor is."

If I were the old me, I might have been mindful of Ha Heon-shin's mood. But I'm not anymore. If anything, Ha Heon-shin should be mindful of mine. Unlike Ha Heon-shin who's weakened from his prime, I'm in my prime right now. Or maybe not even my prime yet.

"The Blast Spear. Originally an A-rank Hunter was supposed to throw it, but I'd like to see an S-rank Hunter like yourself use it. Would you oblige? You're free to refuse."

"I am an S-rank Hunter, so my price is rather high."

"I heard from Seung-hee that you plan to build a tower in Seoul. I'll personally oversee that project myself."

The president of Sejin Group's construction company was Ha Jun-su. Ha Jun-su's expression had stiffened slightly. It seemed this proposal hadn't been discussed with him.

*What's his goal? Does he want to gauge my power indirectly?*

I thought for a moment, then spoke.

"I have a condition. You do it too, Chairman."

"Well now. You want me to display my power next to an active S-rank Hunter? Are you trying to humiliate me?"

"You've already retired from being a Hunter, Chairman, so what humiliation is there? Show some credibility by stepping up yourself."

"Fine. But I'll go first. If I throw after you, wouldn't that be too pathetic an ending?"

"I trust you'll build my tower well."

"I am the Chairman of Sejin Group. Have you decided on the tower's name?"

Seong Yu-jin Harem Tower. I almost said it but held back.

"I'm still thinking about it."

• • •

The businessmen invited to the demonstration watched me and Ha Heon-shin with fascinated eyes. An opportunity to see the power of an active S-rank Hunter and a former S-rank Hunter was rare, so they could say they'd hit the jackpot today.

I looked at the Blast Spear in my hand.

On the surface, it appeared to be an ordinary spear. Its distinguishing feature was the complex patterns engraved both outside and inside the shaft.

"The Blast Spear. A spear made from a patented composite magic circle and specially refined monster byproducts. Ah, the identity of the byproducts is a secret. That wasn't disclosed even in the patent."

"You must be confident in it."

"Of course. The Blast Spear doesn't simply condense mana. That kind of function is common everywhere. The Blast Spear's greatest advantage is that it can partially contain the characteristics of a Hunter's ability."

Ha Heon-shin stepped forward. The target was a scarecrow placed about 300 meters away. It was enchanted. A scarecrow that wouldn't break even from a single full-power strike from an A-rank Hunter.

"I'll begin."

Ha Heon-shin took a javelin-throwing stance. He put strength into his entire body. In an instant, his muscles bulged and mana erupted explosively. Most of the mana gathered into the spear.

Above Ha Heon-shin's body, the form of a monstrous bird with jewel-like feathers appeared and vanished.

*Fairy Bird. An S-rank monster.*

He was using the ability of the Fairy Bird, which he had stolen in the past through his power of Monstrous Theft. The Fairy Bird had the power to control wind.

Wind gathered around Ha Heon-shin and soon began condensing into the Blast Spear. Ha Heon-shin wasn't controlling the wind. The Blast Spear was naturally drawing the power in.

"Hup!"

Ha Heon-shin threw the spear. It flew fast as a bullet. To an ordinary person's eyes, it wouldn't even be visible. At A-rank Hunter level, your thought processing naturally speeds up in combat situations without conscious effort, so I could see the spear flying.

The spear struck the scarecrow precisely in the head.

KWAOOOOOANG!

The spear exploded and a shockwave burst out.

Immediately after, wind gathered into one spot and a second shockwave erupted.

KWAOOOOOOOOOANG!

A gale blew all the way here, 300 meters away.

The scarecrow was obliterated, and the ground where it had stood was caved in as if it had been bombed.

-The Blast Spear's characteristics vary slightly depending on the Hunter's ability. Even with the same ability, the effect manifests slightly differently depending on the Hunter. The double explosion just now was influenced by the Chairman's ability. I'd like to inform you that the Blast Spear has no inherent double-explosion ability.

Ha Heon-shin rubbed his shoulder.

"Whew. My shoulder's stiff just from throwing this once... Times like this really make me feel my age. Ah, it's your turn. Can I look forward to it?"

"Aren't you already looking forward to it?"

I could feel the heated gazes of Ha Heon-shin and the other spectators.

"Witnessing the power of an active S-rank Hunter with your own eyes is something money can't buy. Please understand."

"I'll throw it toward the sky instead of the scarecrow. I don't want to cause an earthquake and make a mess for no reason."

"The Blast Spear has a guidance function, though not a precise one. To demonstrate that function, there needs to be a target point. I'll throw the scarecrow into the sky. How about that?"

"That works."

Ha Heon-shin threw the scarecrow skyward from a distance. The scarecrow shot up hundreds of meters in an instant. Its momentum had died compared to the initial throw, but it didn't fall immediately and continued rising higher.

I took a javelin-throwing stance and slammed mana into the Blast Spear in my hand.

Bzzzzt.

Lightning naturally flowed into the Blast Spear. I didn't block the flow.

I hurled the Blast Spear toward the scarecrow in the sky.

Blue lightning shot into the sky with a sonic boom. In the blink of an eye, the spear reached the scarecrow's head.

The power condensed in the spear exploded.

Clouds in the vicinity were pushed away by the shockwave, and a sphere of lightning dozens of meters in size that was released along with it shone in the sky for about seven seconds. A kind of discharge phenomenon. If any living creature had been inside it, their entire body would have burned to death.

"Hah. You. You didn't even go all out."

Ha Heon-shin, who had approached my side before I knew it, spoke with a complicated expression.

"I never said I'd go all out."

"...And yet this much. As someone who knew the old you, I'm at the point of suspecting you made a contract with a demon. If not that, did you meet a god?"

Ha Heon-shin seemed to be speaking half-seriously. As a former S-rank Hunter, he would know something of the truth of this world.

"The secret of my power is a secret. Because it's a secret."

"You do say the most pointless things sometimes. You seem to be getting along well with Seung-hee—may I ask what your relationship is?"

"Seung-hee is one of my women."

"...One of your women? If any other bastard had said that about Seung-hee, I'd have smacked him upside the head on the spot."

"Am I fine, then?"

"In my era, it was natural for S-rank Hunters to have multiple women. However, if you toss Seung-hee aside like a worn-out shoe... I won't stand for it."

Ha Heon-shin's eyes blazed. I shrugged.

"Even if Seung-hee tried to leave me, I wouldn't let her go, so don't worry."

"I'll trust that. If anything, you can start calling me father-in-law from now on."

"We're not even married, so that's a bit much. And isn't Seung-hee your granddaughter?"

"I look younger than my age, so people usually mistake me for her father. Say, would you be interested in taking on a company? It's a bit different from Hunter work, but it'd be interesting."

"Not interested."

After speaking firmly, I felt my smartphone vibrate and pulled it from my pocket to check. A message from Park Su-ho had arrived.

Park Su-ho: Hyung! Help me!

*Did this brat run out of money?*
