Even the air felt different, if I had to describe it.

Just a cursory glance around the warehouse interior revealed an array of extraordinary items on display.

Among them, a vajra caught my eye. Similar to the Vajra Trust yet distinct.

"What's this?"

"It is a vajra from an ancient esoteric sect. It harbors destructive power. A specialized tool for incantations, so it doesn't suit you, Eugene."

It was different from the Vajra Trust that Indra had personally forged and wielded. I wasn't disappointed. Not all vajras could be the same, after all.

"That sword over there? It's radiating killing intent."

"That is the cursed blade Muramasa. A sword steeped in the grudges and curses of the Japanese. It will attempt to seize its wielder's mind, but its edge is always sharp."

"Mind if I give it a try?"

"If it's you, Eugene, a mere cursed blade wouldn't get the better of you. Go ahead."

I gripped the hilt of Muramasa, displayed without a scabbard. Black energy surged from the blade. The dark aura tried to take control of my body.

Crackle.

Lightning energy stirred, keeping Muramasa's dark aura in check. The resistance was fierce. At this rate, it would take considerable time to tame the sword. Even if I did, I couldn't expect performance on par with the Hwaryeon Bido.

I set the sword down.

"Not to your liking?"

"No. What's that black one next to it? Unlike that raving cursed blade, it's quiet. Now I see—it's suppressing the cursed blade's energy so it doesn't grow stronger."

"That is the sword of Saint Catherine. The blade used by Joan of Arc, the holy maiden of France, during her lifetime. Unless it is she herself, it cannot unleash its true power."

The Association President's expression as he spoke of Joan of Arc was as serene as ever.

I looked around the exhibits again. This warehouse didn't just contain items related to famous myths and legends.

"Monster innards?"

"Yes. The lungs of the Fire Dragon that appeared in Korea. A portion of them. They are a panacea in themselves. Though it's been over ten years, there's no risk of decay while displayed in this warehouse."

I thought of the dragon hearts stored in my inventory.

"If I eat this, do I gain a dragon's abilities?"

"A dragon's abilities? Not quite that much. However, your physical capabilities will permanently increase. For S-rank hunters, it won't be of much help. If it were me, I'd use it as a magical ingredient rather than consuming it directly."

I considered bringing out the Hwaryeon Bido, then decided against it. The Hwaryeon Bido, forged from the Fire Dragon's scales, looked the same on the outside, but through repeated enhancements, its very essence had changed.

"There's even a plant."

"That is the Herb of Immortality."

"...If you eat it, you don't age? Why haven't you eaten it yourself?"

"The body doesn't age, but the soul rots rapidly. The consumer ends up dying young instead. And for those already old like myself, what meaning is there in not growing older?"

"Can you even call this a treasure?"

"Hoho. It is true that you don't age. And who knows what the future holds. Perhaps a way to overcome the Herb of Immortality's side effects will emerge."

The Fountain of Youth I possessed granted youth without side effects. By any measure, it was a superior compatibility.

The next thing to catch my eye was a slab of meat on a plate.

"What's that meat? Will I die if I eat it?"

"No. The meat itself is ordinary. The treasure is the plate. It infinitely produces beef every three hours. That is its only ability, but it defies the laws of the world... it may well be the most peculiar and precious treasure in this vault. Interested?"

"No. If I want beef, I can just buy some. Judging by the marbling, the quality doesn't look particularly good."

"It's not even Choice grade—at best Select grade meat. Edible, yes, but it wouldn't satisfy your palate, Eugene. Especially since you're quite the gourmet. Hoho."

The fact that my palate was particular wasn't widely known information.

Even if Yura knew something of my tastes, she wasn't the type to be so loyal to the Association as to report such trivial details to the higher-ups. There was a possibility he'd obtained the information through a different channel than Yura.

'Well, it's the kind of information anyone who cares to know would know.'

After that, I continued examining the displayed items and selected one.

The Potion of Foresight.

A potion that, when drunk, allowed one to see the future.

It couldn't show the distant future—only the near future, events related to oneself.

"Surprising. I thought you would choose a weapon or armor, Eugene."

"There were plenty of useful things, but nothing I felt I absolutely had to have."

The biggest reason I chose the Potion of Foresight wasn't curiosity about the future.

Because it could be duplicated with the Potion Copier.

Potions could be copied.

"Hmm. I will respect your choice, Eugene."

"But where did you get this potion?"

"It was a reward from clearing an S-rank dungeon seven years ago."

"Why didn't the Association use it directly?"

"The future it could show was limited. One's own future. And only the near future, not the distant one. That effect didn't align with the Association's pursuit of the greater cause."

"I see."

It was indeed an effect that didn't match what the Association pursued. Above all, the Association had other means of divining the future. With that in hand, they wouldn't have been fixated on the Potion of Foresight.

James and I exited the warehouse. I'd received what was owed, so there was no reason to remain at the World Hunter Association headquarters. With the help of a spatial teleportation ability user, I returned to Korea in an instant.

Back home, I duplicated the Potion of Foresight with the Potion Copier.

As expected, there were now two Potions of Foresight.

The volcanic island zone, which could have destroyed Atlantis, had been successfully conquered.

Thanks to that, Atlantis was at peace. Even if restoring the volcanic ash that had blanketed the entire continent and various other damages would cost money and time, no more ash would fall.

'If I set my mind to it, I could scatter volcanic ash anytime, though. Hehe.'

Of course, it wasn't free. Controlling the volcanic island consumed AP. Think of it as a kind of fuel.

Naturally, I had no intention of scattering ash for no reason. If Atlantis fell, both Elena and I would fall. Turning all of Atlantis into an enemy and winning was also difficult.

In any case, Atlantis was currently peaceful, mobilizing for recovery. The Emperor of the Justia Empire was also quiet, focusing on domestic affairs.

'This peace doesn't seem like it'll last long, but it's not bad. I've made a lot of money, after all.'

Atlantis was a playground and a gambling den for the gods.

Everlasting peace? The gods didn't want that. This peace was only temporary. If peace seemed like it would persist, incidents would erupt.

During this brief period of peace, I spent most of my time at the Valdert estate.

Part of the reason was that I was Elena Valdert's husband, but the main reason was that Elena was pregnant. A pregnant woman couldn't help but be sensitive. It was better to be by her side as much as possible.

However, Elena still worked in her office as usual. She was pregnant but it was early, so her belly wasn't showing. It hadn't even been ten weeks, so that was natural. Still, since she was pregnant, we were avoiding vigorous sex as much as possible.

'Once Elena falls asleep, I'll have a round with Arel.'

Arel, who worked as Elena's adjutant. Elena knew about my relationship with her and tacitly permitted it, so there was no reason to hold back.

"It's only early pregnancy. I'm just scribbling on documents, so the work isn't dangerous."

I watched Elena as she moved her fountain pen without hesitation. As always, her appearance was immaculate, and she worked with her back ramrod straight. Was it the pregnancy's influence? Her breasts had definitely grown fuller than before.

"Isn't that outfit uncomfortable?"

"It's fine for now."

Elena was wearing clothes that clung tightly to her body. Her figure was showing and it looked lovely, but it wasn't the kind of clothing a pregnant woman should wear.

'It won't affect the child... right? After all, we're a couple blessed by the goddess Hera.'

And with high probability, the child to be born wouldn't be ordinary.

"Ho. Our Emperor intends to stir up something interesting."

"The Emperor? Is he doing something strange?"

"He's planning a festival to commemorate the conquest of the volcanic island. He's requesting cooperation from noble houses, including mine. In short, he wants voluntary donations."

"If it's a festival hosted by the imperial family, couldn't he use the imperial treasury?"

"The Emperor considers it a virtue to use others' money when conducting public events. It's merely a request for donations, so it's not bad. What's interesting in the festival plans is the martial arts tournament."

"A tournament to determine the strongest under heaven? The Emperor had such tastes?"

"Hardly. With high probability, he's joined hands with the divine thrones. This martial arts tournament is perfect for alleviating the boredom of the divine thrones at this time."

"What's the prize?"

"The title of Strongest Under Heaven and control of Zone 7,414, the Valkyrie's Arena. Zone 7,414 isn't a zone the Emperor possesses. It's one of the unconquered zones."

"In other words, the System intervened in this tournament."

It was planned under the Emperor's name, but it was correct to view it as an event run by the System.

"There are no participation restrictions. Even non-imperials can participate. Will you join?"

I closed my mouth and pondered at Elena's question.

I didn't know exactly what kind of zone the Valkyrie's Arena was either. It was a zone that hadn't appeared in the original work.

But the title of Strongest Under Heaven tempted me even more than the zone.

'Just looking at it, it's clearly not an ordinary title. At the very least, all my stats would rise.'

The important thing was under what identity I would participate.

The Heavenly Demon and the Thunder God.

I had to choose one of the two.
