Choi Jeonghwa and Seong Hari took out cards from their pockets.

A plain white card. It was the invitation to the Baengnyeong Auction and a wallet containing the money to be used at the auction.

The golem scanned the cards and nodded. The sensor that had been red returned to its original color.

,Confirmed. I will guide you to the auction house. Please follow me.,
