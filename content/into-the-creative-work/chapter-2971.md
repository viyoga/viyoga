I recovered Mana with a Mana Potion. Of course, one Mana Recovery Potion couldn’t restore all my Mana. Only a portion was recovered.

‘This alone is enough to help.’

Feeling the surging Mana, I gripped the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido.

I couldn’t use a Domain.

‘That old hag isn’t as strong as Alterio, is she?’

Meld Norris was, from the start, a sniper. She could be called a ranged Dealer. If I could pinpoint her location and force close combat… I could defeat her much more easily than Alterio.

Tang!

A gunshot rang out. Gunshots from all directions, not just one specific one.

No bullets flew towards me.

But I didn’t let my guard down.

‘Since gunshots rang out, she must have fired. That old hag has the ability to slightly twist the direction of bullets and manipulate their speed. It’s a truly annoying ability.’

Tang! Tang! Tang!

Gunshots continued in a steady rhythm. No bullets flew towards me. And I still couldn’t pinpoint Meld Norris’s location. She was definitely hiding nearby.

‘Is she stacking attacks? To unleash them all at once?’

I spread my left hand.

Pa-ji-ji-ji-ji-jik!

Blue lightning flashed into existence, gathering and rotating above my left palm. I used the profound mystery of Myriad Lightning, but it formed into a sphere rather than the 卍 shape of Myriad Lightning.

The sphere, growing larger as the lightning gathered, slowly ascended and then stopped in the sky.

Lightning Heaven Flow (雷天流) Lightning Orb (雷球).

Kwar-reureureureung!

The Lightning Orb indiscriminately shot lightning in all directions with a thunderous roar. Naturally, its power was far greater than ordinary lightning.

I pointed my index finger in one direction. The Lightning Orb then slowly moved in that direction. Each time the Lightning Orb moved, lightning shot out in all directions, devastating the area. The Lightning Orb was, in itself, a small lightning storm.

To call it small would be inaccurate; it utterly devastated a 100m radius around its center.

The compressed lightning within the Lightning Orb resonated with each other, generating and maintaining energy for a time before disappearing. However, once the Lightning Orb was properly activated, it became uncontrollable until it vanished.

‘It could be called a downgraded version of Lightning Heaven’s Roar. Its power is less than half compared to Lightning Heaven’s Roar, though. Well, she’s not in this direction, it seems.’

I created another Lightning Orb and sent it in a different direction.

Tang!

A now familiar gunshot.

Simultaneously, a bullet aimed at my forehead flew towards me. The bullet couldn’t reach my forehead and was blocked.

Lightning Heaven Flow (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

Bing-geul-bing-geul.

The bullet rotated in front of my forehead.

Normally, a mere metal fragment like this would bounce off the Electromagnetic Barrier without issue, but it was resisting my power.

‘It’s not an ordinary bullet. Mana and will are imbued in the bullet.’

The bullet’s rotation gradually slowed, then it lost power and fell to the ground. The direction the bullet came from was the land I had devastated with the Lightning Orb. Trees were burned, and the ground was scorched. There were no living creatures.

Tang!

Over 30 bullets simultaneously assailed me.

‘Is she trying to break through the Electromagnetic Barrier with sheer quantity?’

It was an effective move.

The Electromagnetic Barrier, as if being gnawed away, couldn’t completely block the bullets’ movement and allowed them through.

‘I just need to move.’

I stepped on air, evading. I didn’t avoid a counterattack either. I threw the prepared Lightning Orb. A small storm swept through the surroundings and flew away.

‘With my current Mana, I can maintain the Electromagnetic Barrier and use the Lightning Orb twice more.’

Mana Recovery Potions also had their limits.

‘I could use Full Recovery, but… I want to save that as much as possible.’

I had to kill the Red Dragon. And the enemy wasn’t just the Tetrix Legion.

Something jumped out from the direction the Lightning Orb was flying. A middle-aged woman in leather armor, holding a rifle in both hands. It was Meld Norris.

‘Found her.’

Meld Norris, in a mid-air leap, pulled the trigger in a gap between the pouring blue lightning from the Lightning Orb.

Tang!

A single gunshot.

And instantly, thousands of bullets flew towards me, surrounding me.

Just before those bullets touched my body, I put strength into my entire body and stepped forward.

Uses Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 8

Lightning Heaven Flow Ultimate Skill: Heavenly Light.

I became light and charged.

Puk.

When the light faded, I had already plunged my knife into Meld Norris’s Breast. Hwaryeon Bido protruded from her back, piercing through. Blood burst out.

But I frowned.

I had clearly stabbed the center of her Breast, but it didn’t feel like I had pierced her heart.

“…It feels like I stabbed a tree, not a heart. Is your heart made of wood?”

Kul-leok.

Blood poured from Meld Norris’s mouth. She didn’t even wipe the blood from her lips as she spoke.

“I also have a trump card. …I think I know why Alterio lost. Where did such a monster suddenly appear from…? See you next time.”

Meld Norris’s body disappeared.

‘Meld Norris shouldn’t have a skill like that. Did she narrowly escape death with the ability of an item I don’t know about?’

There were items that allowed one to die and resurrect, so it wasn’t strange.

I roughly shook off the blood from the Hwaryeon Bido’s blade by swinging it in the air. I hadn’t killed her, but she was certainly damaged. Meld Norris was out of commission. At least until the Red Dragon was killed, I wouldn’t need to worry about her.

I saw the Red Dragon fighting other Legions in the center of Mount Hua Island. I had no intention of fighting immediately. The one to kill the Red Dragon had to be the Heavenly Demon.

「Uses Marriage’s Blessing (A).」

「Moves to spouse’s location.」

「You cannot use Marriage’s Blessing (A) spouse movement for the next 100 days.」

My vision suddenly shifted.

The Heavenly Demon was next to me. More precisely, it was Elena, who looked like the Heavenly Demon. She resembled me, but her appearance was slightly different. The Heavenly Demon’s appearance was slightly twisted due to using deception.

“You’ve arrived. A little later than expected.”

“It’s my voice. It feels stranger than when I faced the Doppelgänger.”

Ttak.

Elena snapped her fingers.

Elena, who had been in the Heavenly Demon’s form, returned to her original appearance. I was a bit flustered, not expecting her to suddenly revert to her original form. Fortunately, there was nothing around. Except for one thing.

A short distance from Elena, a man lay dead, kneeling on the ground.

A man with glasses and inverted eyes. He wore a white Gown and a black metal Mask covering his nose and mouth. There were no traces of battle around him.

“I paid quite a bit of attention to the Demonic Cult. There are injured individuals on Mount Hua Island, but no dead ones. Ah, they are currently fighting other Legions. The Demonic Cult’s skill is quite good. They aren’t dangerous opponents, so there won’t be any casualties.”

“That?”

I pointed at the dead man.

Madcurse Million, an executive of the Tetrix Legion.

A Crazy Bastard who had Remodeled his own body with curses and alchemy.

“He came to stop the Heavenly Demon, it seems. His goal was probably to stall for time against the Demonic Cult. He made various proposals, but… most of them were empty words, so I destroyed his Mind and brain.”

“An executive of Tetrix, a fairly well-known guy, at once? He’s a psycho bastard, so mental attacks shouldn’t work well on him. You really killed him at once.”

“I used 30% of my Lifespan. Well, it didn’t seem that necessary, but… I don’t seem to have much to do in the future, so I used it with the feeling of getting rid of troublesome things.”

30% of her Lifespan. It was by no means a small amount. Anyone would agree that using 30% of her Lifespan just to deal with a guy like this was excessive.

Still, I didn’t nag Elena. As long as she had Full Recovery, it wasn’t a big loss. Elena had a tendency to use her Lifespan recklessly ever since she gained Full Recovery.

“Since we’re meeting after a long time, I’d like to have many deep conversations, but… now is not the time. Did things go well?”

“The Tetrix Legion intervened, so I struggled a bit, but… there are no problems. It’s according to plan. I just need to finish it.”

“Then finish it quickly. I want to return to the Clan soon.”

Elena transformed into the appearance of Seong Yujin of the A-Plan Legion. Conversely, I used deception to return to the Heavenly Demon.

I raised my hand to Elena. Elena, in my appearance, chuckled and high-fived me. It felt strange because Elena was in my form.

“Let’s talk later. Husband.”

“…Uh, sure.”

Feeling an unknown chill, I moved towards the Red Dragon to finish things.

“It’s the Heavenly Demon!”

“Is the Heavenly Demon seriously trying to subjugate the Red Dragon!”

“The Heavenly Demon is alone! If the Heavenly Demon is alone, we can stop him! Stop him!”

The other Legions raised a commotion and showed hostility towards me. They were nothing compared to Tetrix’s executives. Moreover, these bastards were exhausted from facing the Red Dragon.

They stopped all their actions and tried to face me.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Heavenly Demon Divine Skill (天魔君臨步) Heavenly Demon Domination Step.

I stomped a true step with full power.

“Keo-heo-eok!”

The enemies simultaneously vomited blood, fell to the ground, and trembled. The mere fact that they weren’t dead meant they possessed at least a minimum level of skill. Instead of finishing off those who were neutralized, I took out the Dragon-Slaying Sword Gram, which I had prepared in my Inventory.

“Keu-reu-reu-reu-reu….”

The wounded Red Dragon growled.

‘Did the Heavenly Demon Domination Step not work on the Red Dragon?’

I suppose it might be due to the Red Dragon’s essence. The Apocalyptic Red Dragon is sometimes equated with Satan.

I walked forward to kill him.

“Keu-a-a-a-a-a!”

The Red Dragon’s two heads roared, gathering Mana in their mouths. It felt threatened by me and was trying to unleash the maximum attack it could.

Uses Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 7

‘Did it lose intelligence along with its power? Thanks to that, this is going easily.’

Big attacks always reveal big openings.

I used Instant to instantly approach the Red Dragon, which was preparing its attack, and plunged Gram into it.

Compared to the colossal Red Dragon, the black sword was not even a toothpick. However, this sword was Gram.

The Dragon-Slaying Sword.

‘A two-headed Red Dragon, not even the Apocalyptic Red Dragon, can do nothing against Gram.’

The Red Dragon, which had been preparing its Breath, stopped moving.

Not even a few seconds passed before the Red Dragon left behind its spoils, turned to ash, and disappeared.

「The Red Dragon has been subjugated.」

「Mount Hua Island has been conquered.」
