I transformed into a Karasu-Tengu and arrived at the heart of White Ogre Line, Blue Ogre. I wandered around, gathering information as needed.

I couldn't just waltz into the place where the massive blue ice mountain stood.

,Not only are there monster soldiers guarding it, but it's also protected by a barrier. Sneaking in is impossible. Using teleportation to get inside is also out of the question. I can't enter unless I break the barrier. The funny thing is, to break the barrier, I need to get inside.,

What's fascinating is that a ley line flows through this ice mountain. It's a place where massive spiritual energy gathers and flows. Usually, such places are called spiritual mountains. And spiritual herbs grow well in such places.

,But there's an ice mountain here. Not all spiritual energy is the same. I should consider the spiritual energy here to be closer to yin energy.,

Monsters stayed around Blue Ogre, forming a city. They were trying to benefit even a little from the immense ley line flowing from Blue Ogre. But in my opinion, it doesn't seem to have much significance.

I wandered around and found a rat monster running a small shop, so I approached it. It was a monster with a rat's head, standing at a small height of 130cm. What was unique was that black smoke was slowly flowing out of this monster's body.

Even though that looks unnatural, it's not unnatural. Just as a person breathes and emits heat from their body, this monster is simply emitting black smoke.

Through the Mind's Eye of Gold, which I was hiding with transformation magic, I could see the monster's name and realm.

Foundation Establishment Stage 3. Name is Gu Jae (九渽). 120 years old. Descendant of Chaos.

"Hey."

"Hic. Y-yes. Master-nim. Please, speak."

The rat monster Gu Jae quickly bowed and spoke. Right now, I'm only showing the realm of Ogre Stage 5. That alone was enough to make Gu Jae, a monster who was only at the Foundation Establishment stage, tremble.

"The black smoke coming from your body is peculiar."

"Yes, Master-nim. It's because the cultivation technique I practice is somewhat unique."

The Mind's Eye of Gold could distinguish truth from falsehood. The rat monster's words just now were a lie.

"Oh ho. Really? Tell me about what kind of cultivation technique it is."

"T-that is.... The key points of the cultivation technique are the sect's secrets. Even if I die, I cannot reveal the secrets of the cultivation technique!!"

"Trying to act all righteous. Tell me honestly. It's not because of the cultivation technique, but because it's a characteristic of your bloodline."

"......!!"

"Descendant of Chaos. Am I wrong?"

Gu Jae was startled and looked around. There were no other monsters nearby. He even used a small magic so that other monsters couldn't hear. Gu Jae seemed to realize that, as he looked at me with wary eyes.

"Master-nim. What do you want?"

"I need you to satisfy my curiosity. Are you a descendant of Chaos?"

"...Hoo. Since you already know, I can't deny it. Yes. I am a descendant of Chaos. This black smoke is also a characteristic of the descendants of Chaos."

"Why is a descendant of Chaos here? Shouldn't you be on that ice mountain?"

"My aptitude was poor, so I was kicked out. However, I received mercy and was able to live here, running a tonic shop."

"That's a lie. The bloodline of Chaos is too precious to be kicked out for lack of aptitude. They would rather kill you than kick you out... Ah. You were castrated?"

"......."

Gu Jae tightly closed his eyes. As I said, Gu Jae had been castrated. And perhaps he wasn't just not leaving this place, but unable to.

"Let's go inside the shop and talk."

"......Yes. Come in."

The inside of the building couldn't be called good, even as a polite remark. There was a musty smell, so I didn't even sit on the chair and spoke.

"What I want is information about the White Ogre Demon King, the descendants of Chaos, and Blue Ogre. First, how to meet the White Ogre Demon King. If possible, I'd like you to tell me how to become his disciple."

"His disciple? Haha. It's not my place to say this, but.... Master-nim is not qualified to become his disciple."

I fiddled with the hilt of the sword at my waist. The blatant threat worked well. Gu Jae sweated profusely and said.

"H-he doesn't accept ordinary monsters as disciples."

"I'm confident in my talent."

"It's not just talent, you also need bloodline. Becoming his disciple is the same as becoming part of the same clan. The Tengu clan's bloodline is excellent, but... it won't catch his eye."

"What if I were a Great Monster?"

"Of course, he would welcome you with open arms. But... there's no reason for a Great Monster to become his disciple."

That was true. A Great Monster could rule a region. There's no reason to become the White Ogre Demon King's disciple unless they had a special purpose.

"Hmm. That's a shame. What are the characteristics of the descendants of Chaos? It can't just be the black smoke flowing from their bodies."

"Master-nim. Please don't take offense. Sometimes, curiosity can be poisonous."

"Don't forget that there are beings who can risk their lives for curiosity. Not just their own lives, but the lives of others as well."

Gu Jae's whiskers trembled. He swallowed hard and said.

"The descendants of Chaos are born with yin energy from birth."

"It's unique, but it's not uncommon for monsters to be born with yin energy among monsters."

"But the descendants of Chaos are not harmed by yin energy. It's not that they don't take any damage at all, but they are almost 95% immune to yin energy attacks. Also, they have excellent compatibility with cultivation techniques related to yin energy."

"Was that why they set up their base on the ice mountain?"

Besides that, I obtained useless information such as the traditional ancestral rites of the descendants of Chaos and the White Ogre Demon King's favorite foods.

I couldn't find out about the White Ogre Demon King's martial arts or magic. That's because Gu Jae wasn't in a high enough position. Gu Jae had only met the White Ogre Demon King less than 5 times. Just because they were descendants of Chaos didn't mean they could easily approach the White Ogre Demon King.

"Thanks to you, I got some useful information."

"Please keep this a secret... Keo-eok?!"

My sword stabbed deeply into Gu Jae's neck.

"I'll definitely keep the secret."

Gu Jae trembled with both hands, trying to form a seal as if in a desperate struggle.

,Thunderbolt.,

Pajijijik!

The thunderbolt flowing along the tip of the sword paralyzed Gu Jae's body. Before long, Gu Jae died. I immediately put Gu Jae's corpse into my inventory.

It wasn't just because I was annoyed with Gu Jae that I killed him.

,If Gu Jae senses something strange and reports it to the descendants of Chaos, their vigilance will increase. If someone discovers Gu Jae's corpse, it could cause a commotion and raise their vigilance. It's safer to get rid of the corpse and handle it as a missing person case, so I can buy time.,

I quietly came out of the shop and scanned Blue Ogre with the Eye of Heaven and the Mind's Eye of Gold.

,Excluding the monster soldiers, there are seven Great Monsters, including the White Ogre Demon King. It's more than I expected, but... it's doable.,

The coordinate designation for the space-time scroll was completed in an instant.

With this, the preparations were complete. I could start the operation tonight.

,I'll start at noon tomorrow.,

The descendants of Chaos are strong against yin energy. And night is the time of yin energy. It's right to start at noon when yang energy is strong.

I tore the space-time scroll in a place where there were no eyes and returned to Gold Sand Island.

I spent the day being treated by Asuka and the other Onna-Tengus on Gold Sand Island.

We didn't hide our demonic energy and emitted it. The monsters around us were busy running away in panic from the demonic energy emitted by nine Great Monsters at the same time.

"The Great Monsters have invaded!!"

"Hiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiik!!"

"Run away!!!"

The two snake monsters of Mansadang waved their hands, focusing on killing the strong among the fleeing monsters.

The bone monsters of Black Bone Cave instantly turned the corpses of the dead monsters into zombies and used them.

I rode the clouds and soared into the sky. To a place high enough to look down on Blue Ogre, the ice mountain.

"What a great view. It's like a work of art that God made well."

I grinned, gripping the iron staff.

"There's nothing as thrilling as smashing someone's work of art."

I could see the descendants of Chaos in Blue Ogre panicking.

I lift the iron staff and use magic.

"Get bigger."

The iron staff grew in an instant.

The size of a small hill. No, to be a little more specific, should I say the size of a small apartment building? I couldn't be satisfied with this much.

"Get even bigger."

The iron staff grew to the size of a 63-story building.

The problem was the weight. The weight of the iron staff was about 10 geun. It only weighs 6kg. It was too light for its size. With this, I couldn't even make a scratch on the barrier surrounding Blue Ogre, let alone destroy it.

I swung the iron staff around, matching the timing.

"Get as heavy as possible."

『Using the light of the Heavenly Star.』

『Dark Star』

100 million times the mass.

A 10 geun iron staff instantly turns into 1 billion geun, 600 million kilograms.

I can't control this. I let go of the iron staff before my arms fell off from not being able to withstand the weight.

The iron staff falls towards Blue Ogre as it is. It shatters the barrier protecting Blue Ogre as if breaking a glass window, and crushes the beautiful ice mountain with its weight, collapsing it.

As the ice mountain collapsed, white smoke billowed up. When viewed from a distance, it was smoke, but in reality, it was closer to the snow that had been piled up on the ice mountain for tens of thousands of years being thrown into the air.

The magic cast on the iron staff and the power of the Dark Star disappear. My eyes pierced through the smoke and identified the Great Monsters inside.

,Two of the Great Monsters are seriously injured, and the other four are unharmed because they were quick-witted enough to avoid a direct hit. The White Ogre Demon King is unharmed because he protected his body with defensive magic items.,

The White Ogre Demon King had a similar appearance to a human. White hair and pale skin. His eyes were eerie purple. Thick black smoke was constantly flowing out from various parts of his body. The White Ogre Demon King's gaze turns to me.

He is holding a dark blue sword wrapped in black smoke in his right hand. A stronger power was felt from that sword than from the sword's owner, the White Ogre Demon King.

,That's the Chaos Gluttonous Wolf Sword that will be mine.,

The White Ogre Demon King swings his sword.

A huge dark blue aura flew towards me. I moved to the side to dodge it. The size was large, but the speed was incredibly fast. If I hadn't been watching him from the beginning, it would have been difficult to dodge.

I look at the sky where the slash passed.

The clouds flowing in the sky were cut in half. It was truly cutting the sky in half with a single sword. And that was only with the power of the sword.

"I am Son Yu-jin, a descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven, Sun Wukong! White Ogre Demon King! I will kill you and take my sword!"

The enraged White Ogre Demon King jumped into the sky.

"You crazy monkey monster!! How dare you covet my treasure without knowing your place! Today, I'll cut off your head and eat your brains! It's been a while since this old one has had a tonic!!"
