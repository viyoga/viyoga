Seeing the accumulated points puts me in a good mood.

I've got astronomical amounts of money in reality, but the points in the Pleasure Life app hit different. Comparing the two in the first place is just absurd.

'About time I leveled up Thunder Heaven Style.'

[Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Lv.5

You may use Thunder Heaven Style.]

[Would you like to spend 100,000 points to raise Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Lv.5?]

A hundred thousand points.

That's no small amount. It had better be worth it.

I raised the level of Thunder Heaven Style.

[Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Lv.6

You may use Thunder Heaven Style.]

Standing in the living room, my body trembled all over.

It felt like enlightenment was being driven straight into me. Before I could even savor it, it melted into my body and became part of me.

Like being forcibly upgraded, I suppose. It wasn't bad.

Crackle.

Red lightning blazed to life above my open palm.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Art (雷術) — Blood Lightning (血雷).

Lightning that uses the life force of blood.

As Thunder Heaven Style's level rose, I became able to use its techniques in reality.

'I can't use high-level techniques like Thunder Eye Golden Restraint or Paired Wings Entwined Branches.'

Even just this much was satisfying enough.

I flicked my hand through the air. The red lightning fell from my hand and vanished into empty space.

Blood Lightning draws on the life force contained in blood as its medium, so its destructive power is exceptional — but since it consumes life force in real time, it wasn't a technique meant for prolonged use.

'The effect when self-detonating is definitely something, though.'

[Available Points: 12,516]

I checked my remaining points.

I plan to save points for what comes next.

'Though that 516 is bothering me. Random draw, let's gooo!'

516 draws.

That's a number of pulls that could land something jackpot-tier.

The result. Something mediocre came out.

[Teleport Talisman

You can teleport to a place you've visited once before.

Single use.

Price: 30,000 points

※Warning

One-way trip.]

A talisman with a yellow background and red patterns drawn on it. The pattern looked like a few stick figures running like people. At a glance, it seemed like a talisman made as a joke.

'Even if it looks like a joke, the performance is probably legit. But is this really worth 30,000 points?'

I brought out a Spatial Teleportation Scroll from 'God's Atlantis' and used it.

'Like the Spatial Teleportation Scroll, it's single-use, but not needing to designate coordinates is convenient. Still, I don't think it's worth 30,000 points.'

Even so, being able to teleport to a place you've visited once is a big deal.

'Could I maybe even teleport into the Pleasure World?'

I picture my mansion in the Dark Moon world and stare at the talisman.

[That is a location you cannot teleport to.]

Teleportation impossible.

I expected as much. If I could get into the Pleasure World with just this, that'd be ridiculous.

'What about places that aren't the Pleasure World?'

Reality, but a different dimension.

Like, say, the place where Bramasen is.

[Would you like to teleport?]

It was possible.

I pictured the space where the God of Earth is bound.

[Would you like to teleport?]

I also pictured the dimension Bramasen had already devoured.

[Would you like to teleport?]

At this point, the Teleport Talisman looked different to me.

"…I see. So as long as it's not the Pleasure World, there are no restrictions. That's a hell of an ability."

The Spatial Teleportation Scroll also had coordinates, and could only move you if dimensions were connected. Prime examples being Park Suho's tattoo world, and Selbriar.

But if a place's dimensional connection was blocked, even the Spatial Teleportation Scroll couldn't get you there. Same for anywhere the space itself was completely sealed off, not just the dimension.

'The Teleport Talisman has no such restrictions. If it's a place you've visited once, you can move there even if it's a god's domain.'

It was a item worth its 30,000 points.

If I used this well, couldn't I plant a nuke in Bramasen's skull and run? Of course, in my current state, I'd probably get taken out the moment I got close to Bramasen.

'Not like he's the kind of bastard who'd die to a nuke anyway.'

If he were the kind to die to a nuke, he'd already be dead.

I put the Teleport Talisman into my inventory.

Even if not right now, there'll be a use for it someday.

Yura came to my house.

Yura, who's also my next-door neighbor, only ever came to see me for two reasons.

When she was hungry for sex, or when it had to do with the World Hunter Association. Seeing her standing in my doorway with a serious expression, it seemed this time it was the latter.

"The Association handed down orders again, I take it?"

"…Yeah. They want your cooperation."

I snorted.

"The World Hunter Association — do they see me as a convenient little tool just because I've cooperated a few times? Should I flip the table on them?"

"The Association isn't exactly forcing you. If you don't want to, you don't have to."

"What's the job?"

"Earth's orbit. They've predicted a dungeon will spawn on one of the satellites that's become space debris. The exact type and rank of the dungeon can't be confirmed right now."

"That's not a prediction, that's a prophecy."

"Maybe so."

Yura shrugged. She probably didn't know the details either. She doesn't work at headquarters.

"But dungeons spawn in orbit too?"

"Unofficially, one opened on the moon once. An S-rank hunter went and handled it."

"That's a headache. Do they send you up on a spaceship?"

"Not a spaceship, but the Association seems to have some means of getting to orbit or into space. Not science or tech — probably magic."

"Reality's science hasn't advanced enough to freely go back and forth to the moon."

"At this point, I'm curious what the World Hunter Association is offering as a reward. It's not just money, right?"

"They've agreed to give you one item of your choosing from the World Hunter Association's collection."

"Collection?"

"The treasures the World Hunter Association holds. I don't know the details either. Rumor has it there are legendary items — things like Durandal or Excalibur?"

"In a world where something like that could actually exist… it wouldn't even be strange."

For a request, isn't the reward way too good?

'No, wait. This is a job that takes you outside Earth. Space is a place where even S-rank hunters can't guarantee their safety. Space is the unknown. The dungeon that might emerge in orbit could be SS-rank. If things go wrong, you might not make it back to Earth.'

It's a burden in many ways, even for an S-rank hunter. Not that it matters for me — I can use a Spatial Teleportation Scroll to bail if things go south.

"You going to do it?"

"No."

Even if I'm curious about the World Hunter Association's collection, that's about the extent of it. With the Pleasure Life app, I can get broken-ass items whenever I feel like it.

"I just handled back-to-back jobs in China and the US. Why should I go all the way to space? I'm resting this time."

"Got it. I'll relay that to the World Hunter Association."

Even if it's not me, there'll be other S-rank hunters to take the job.

'I should tell them not to give it to Ayoung noona.'

If all the S-rank hunters refuse? Then the World Hunter Association will step in and handle it themselves.

'The Association President is someone even I can't ignore.'

I beckoned Yura over. She'd been about to head out, but she smirked and came to me.

It was around the time I was mulling over when to start the next round of Pleasure.

A meteor fell on Geoje Island, Korea. It landed on Garasan in the early hours, so there were no casualties. Police and the Association were mobilized and put Garasan under lockdown.

'A meteor suddenly falling, huh. Something's off.'

I could guess the cause.

The intel I'd heard from Yura two days ago — that a dungeon would open in orbit. Orbit and meteor. They share the commonality of space, don't they. Could this really be coincidence?

'And on top of that, the Hunter Association stepped in over a meteor.'

The Hunter Association is a public authority, so cooperating with police wasn't strange in itself. But when that happens, it's usually because something serious has occurred.

'A meteor falling isn't a serious situation. There were no casualties. Unless what fell wasn't actually a meteor?'

I don't easily trust South Korean media. South Korean media's credibility is below average.

'I'm a bit curious. Should I check?'

It's not like I need to go check myself. One phone call to Yura and it's done. Isn't this exactly what S-rank hunter authority is for?

—What is it? I'm busy right now.

"Busy? Where are you?"

—Korean Hunter Association headquarters. I came out for work.

"Because of the meteor?"

—…Yeah. How did you know?

"Just took a guess. But was it really just a meteor that fell?"

—The World Hunter Association sees it as an invasion by Xenorage, not an ordinary meteor. This is classified, but I'm telling you because it's you.

"Xenorage? What's that now?"

—An extraterrestrial lifeform. The World Hunter Association classifies it as an invasive species like Pyron. I only found out an hour ago myself.

"Well, well. So this world has aliens too."

—'Creatures from another dimension' is the more accurate way to put it.

"Same difference. How are you going to handle it?"

—It's not serious enough for S-rank hunters to step in. A-rank hunters. Among them, a top-10 guild should be able to handle it on their own. The World Hunter Association will issue separate orders for this kind of thing, so there's nothing I can do, but… it'll probably be the Jaun Guild. Jaun mainly operates in the Gyeongsangnam-do area.

Jaun (紫雲) Guild.

One of Korea's top-10 guilds. Even with its headquarters in Seoul, its main area of operation is Gyeongsangnam-do.

'If all ten top guilds concentrated in Seoul, there'd be nothing for them to do.'

It's not like dungeons only open in Seoul in Korea.

'Seyoung noona is in the Jaun Guild. It's been a while since I've seen her since she's been active in Gyeongsangnam-do. Maybe I'll go visit while I'm at it. I'm curious about this Xenorage thing too.'

Honestly, I'm also worried about Jin Seyoung. Pyron, the same kind of invasive species, is an opponent even A-rank hunters can't handle easily.

"Yura. I'm heading to Geoje now."

—Should I get the car ready?

"No time for that."

I rode a cloud made by the Cloud Command (制雲令) and flew through the sky. From the ground, it would only look like a cloud or smoke streaming past at high speed.
