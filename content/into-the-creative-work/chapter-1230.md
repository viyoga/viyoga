Having someone who knows how to use their head is definitely easier on the body. Avoiding traps was easier, and we persuaded the other initiates we ran into to part ways without fighting. They were also helpful when fighting monsters. I just wish they'd set up the barrier a little faster.

,123-beon.

,What is it?

We were walking on a path as narrow as a balance beam. The wind was blowing from the side, and the pits on either side were filled with sharp thorns.

But I and Jegal Mo-sun were relaxed. We are masters who have reached the realm of transcendence. For a master, walking on a balance beam is nothing. We could even run.

,I heard you swore allegiance to Number 88.

It was about two months ago.

I unofficially sparred with Cheon Yu-un and lost. I had been helped by Cheon Yu-un, and I was impressed by Cheon Yu-un's skills, so I swore allegiance while I was at it. That's the superficial reason.

,I deduced it from your attitude. Your attitude has subtly changed.

As expected of Jegal Mo-sun. He was worthy of being the brain of the Demonic Cult, later known as the Demon Brain.

,You're quite perceptive. Yes. That's right. I swore allegiance to Number 88.

,You're being too obvious. Ipmaso-jang-nim said they'd be watching us.

,It's okay. I'm not exactly betraying the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. Rather, swearing allegiance to Number 88 is itself an act of loyalty to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

,So you know Number 88's identity.

,Would I have sworn allegiance if I didn't know?

,...What are you curious about?

,The reason for your loyalty. I saw potential in Number 88. The potential for him to become the master of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

,...Number 88 is stronger than me. I acknowledged his strength. That's all.

,That's absurd. You expect me to believe that? There are plenty of people in this Divine Cult who are stronger than you. Ipmaso-jang-nim alone could kill you in ten moves.

,Ipmaso-jang-nim doesn't have the overwhelming something that Number 88 has.

,What is that something?

,...It's hard to explain in words. There's just something there.

,Well, fine.

Teop.

Jegal Mo-sun's hand grabs my shoulder from behind. Just by him putting strength into his hand and pushing my body, I would lose my balance and fall to the side.

,...What are you doing?

,123-beon. I'm warning you in advance. Don't hold me and Number 88 back. If I determine that you are not helpful, I will kill you without hesitation.

I turned my head slightly and looked at Jegal Mo-sun. He had an utterly serious face.

,That's what I should be saying, Jegal Mo-sun. Many people think you might be a spy from the Jegal Clan. Don't forget that.

,I will prove that in due course.

He took his hand off my shoulder.

,How long are you going to stay here? Move forward, 123-beon.

I frowned and walked forward.

•••

Two men and one woman were lying on the floor. I and Jegal Mo-sun approached them and examined their wounds. Unlike their blood-soaked clothes, their injuries were not serious.

,Sword wounds. They're still alive. The wounds aren't fatal.

,215-beon, 307-beon, 421-beon. Who did this to you?

Jegal Mo-sun asked them in a calm voice. The ones whose numbers were called trembled slightly. The one who answered was the biggest man. With unfocused eyes, he recalled the memory and said.

,A demon... I was attacked by a demon....

,A demon? You mean a monster?

,Not a monster...! He's human! The demon wears a mask and wields two swords... A Martial artist...!

,Tell me more! 215-beon! 215-beon!!

,......

215-beon lost consciousness. He wasn't dead, but seemed to have fallen asleep. Jegal Mo-sun looked back at 307-beon and 421-beon. They had also lost consciousness in the same way.

,It doesn't make sense that they suddenly fainted. No, this isn't fainting.

,It's a sleeping draught.

,Yes. They used a sleeping draught. It must be the work of the demon they mentioned.

Jegal Mo-sun glanced at my back. It's because the demon tattoo is engraved on my back.

,It wasn't me.

,I don't suspect you. You've been with me all this time, haven't you.

,I don't like the way you look at me.

Jegal Mo-sun didn't even scoff as he examined the fallen and stood up.

,We need to be more thoroughly vigilant.

,You seem strangely relaxed. Do you know about the demon?

,I don't know. However, I can make a guess. The demon knocked them down and put them to sleep. The demon is probably a Martial artist from the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. Ipmaso-jang-nim must have deployed them.

,Why?

,It's probably because the level of the initiates in this class is high. We just need to do what we have to do.

We left the fallen behind and moved forward.

A fork in the road appeared.

The right and left sides looked exactly the same. I closed my eyes and listened.

,I can hear the sound of water on the right. I can't hear anything on the left.

,Hmm. Let's go to the right.

,Why?

,It's a difference in information. It's better to know that water is flowing than to know nothing. Besides, it's a place where water flows. There might be an exit nearby.

We headed to the right.

Soon we encountered flowing water. The problem was that the water was flowing downwards, towards the underground. No matter how much I focused my eyes, I couldn't see the end of how deep the underground was.

,What? It's 123-beon and 366-beon.

Two women who had been hiding in the corner revealed themselves. 290-beon, Seo-mun So-ryeo, and 567-beon, Yeon Ye-ha. Seo-mun So-ryeo's clothes were stained with blood, but it didn't seem to be her blood.

,To think we'd meet you here.... You're lucky.

Jegal Mo-sun smiled.

,Did you find your Black Ogre Beads? We haven't found ours yet. We need to find the Black Ogre Beads first.

Seo-mun So-ryeo said. There was a slight anxiety in her voice. Yeon Ye-ha next to her was quiet. She was expressionless here too, so I couldn't tell what she was thinking.

,We found the Black Ogre Beads. It hasn't been long since we started the fourth test, so we can move slowly and find the exit and the Black Ogre Beads. First, let's drink some water and make a plan.

It was the moment Jegal Mo-sun finished speaking.

We simultaneously turned our heads and looked back the way we had come. There was a man wearing a demon mask. He was holding a sword in each hand.

We simultaneously gasped. We were overwhelmed for a moment by the aura emanating from him. That bastard is at least a master of the Five Energies or higher.

The first of us to move was Yeon Ye-ha. She drew her sword and charged towards the demon mask. I followed her closely behind, a step late, and said to Jegal Mo-sun.

,366-beon! Set up the barrier!

,Understood. Buy me some time!

Jegal Mo-sun began to plant steel rods into the ground, and Seo-mun So-ryeo followed behind me. The three of us worked together to face the demon mask. The demon mask leisurely swung his swords and dealt with us.

,Damn it. I can tell just by looking. Is he around the mid-stage of the Five Energies?

The fortunate thing is that he's just playing with us. If he really intended to kill us, I would have had to release the restraint.

White Sword energy soared from Yeon Ye-ha's sword. She swung her sword. The trajectory of the sword distorted and blurred the vision. It was an excellent Illusion Sword. But the demon mask easily deflected Yeon Ye-ha's attack.

Kaang!

With a loud explosion, Yeon Ye-ha flew backwards and rolled on the floor.

I and Seo-mun So-ryeo exchanged glances and simultaneously dug into the sides of the demon mask. The demon mask held his swords and spun in place like a top. The rotation was so intense that I and Seo-mun So-ryeo couldn't penetrate.

I kicked a protruding part of the floor with my foot. A stone flew towards the rotating demon mask. The moment the stone touched the rotating sword, it turned to dust and fell to the floor.

,It's done! Activating the barrier!

Huuuung.

Jegal Mo-sun's barrier activates. The air instantly becomes heavy. The demon mask stopped rotating.

We gathered in one place, wary of the demon mask. The demon mask remained still. It seemed he was giving us time to strategize.

,The barrier will slow down his movements. Can we beat him?

,It's impossible to win.

Yeon Ye-ha asserted. I and Seo-mun So-ryeo nodded and agreed with her opinion. To beat the demon mask, we would need at least five more masters of Yeon Ye-ha's level.

,Then running away would be the best option. First, you guys deal with him. Just buy me a little time.

,What are you going to do by buying time?

I asked.

,I'm going to forcibly shrink the barrier to seal his movements. Barriers and formations aren't my specialty, but... I can buy us time to escape from him.

,I see. I agree with your opinion.

Yeon Ye-ha and Seo-mun So-ryeo also nodded. At that moment, Sword energy from the demon mask cut through the air and flew towards us. We, who had been watching him, quickly scattered in all directions and avoided the Sword energy.

Except for Jegal Mo-sun, we immediately rushed towards the demon mask. Perhaps due to the influence of the barrier, the demon mask's response was slow. Even so, it was by no means possible to land a fatal blow on him.

Kaang! Kang! Kaaang!

We had trained together for the past few months. Thanks to that, the three of us were somewhat in sync.

The demon mask's attitude changed. His swordsmanship, which had been focused on defense, turned aggressive.

,Aaaagh!

Seo-mun So-ryeo screamed and flew towards the wall. Blood flowed from her left shoulder.

,You bastard!

I charged straight at the demon mask.

Slaying Demon Sword Technique (斬鬼刀法) Reversing Demon Chase (逆鬼追)

A blade rising from below.

The demon mask easily avoided it by taking a step back. But the real attack was behind me. Yeon Ye-ha, who had been hiding in the demon mask's blind spot using me as a shield, stepped on my shoulder and rushed towards the demon mask. Her white Sword energy momentarily increased to four, covering the air and attacking the demon mask.

The demon mask's eyes widened as if he was flustered. He crossed the two swords he held in both hands and swung them. A Ki wave erupted from the demon mask's body, blowing me and Yeon Ye-ha away. I and Yeon Ye-ha, who had rolled on the floor, simultaneously jumped to our feet.

Jegal Mo-sun began to run away backwards with Seo-mun So-ryeo.

,567-beon! We have to run away too!

,...No. It's a little late. We have to face him.

Kwang!

,Transience!

,Transience! Transience! Transience!

I prepared for his next attack, but he just stood still and looked at us.

Belatedly, I realized that the protective aura that had been protecting his body had disappeared, and Jegal Mo-sun's barrier, which I thought had disappeared, was restraining his body.

I and Yeon Ye-ha were swept away by the current and gradually descended.

Pungdeong!

,123-beon. It was you.
