A fist flies towards my face.

I immediately used Flash.

Eun Sang-min became a C-rank Hunter not long ago. Even if he didn't infuse mana into his fist, I can't deny that his physical abilities are higher than mine.

Besides, I never imagined Eun Sang-min would throw a punch right here. I can't avoid this fist without Flash.

,Should I just take one hit?'

It's more convenient to think about the aftermath. I can make the excuse that that bastard hit me first.

,But do I have to get hit by a punk like this? Fuck that.'

No matter how convenient it is for the aftermath, it's something that can't happen to my pride.

I clenched my fist and dodged Eun Sang-min's attack. Then, I summoned lightning and mana and swung my fist towards Eun Sang-min's face.

Kwaang!

Eun Sang-min flies away. The glass wall of the cafe shatters, and his body shoots out of the store, rolling on the floor several times.

,This, this crazy bastard is really... are you trying to do this for real?!'

Eun Sang-min got up, trembling. One side of his face is red and swollen, and blood is dripping from his mouth.

,He's getting up even after taking that? C-rank isn't just for show.'

Maybe it's because the opponent can also handle mana, but I couldn't even electrocute him with lightning. If my physical abilities were similar to Eun Sang-min's, I would have knocked him out already, even though my physical abilities are lower than his.

,You were the one who threw the first punch. I was just defending myself. You loser.'

I walked out through the broken glass wall. I don't care about the surroundings. This cafe was deserted anyway. There weren't many customers.

,Okay. You bastard. Let's have a funeral today.'

Eun Sang-min clenched both fists. Sharp thorns began to sprout from his fists.

That's Eun Sang-min's ability. He can make thorns grow on his body or objects. He can also create thorns in the air and throw them.

That guy brags so much about himself that there's no one in the department who doesn't know about his ability. And I've seen it myself before.

,Wipe the blood from your mouth. You already look like shit, and people will think you're a zombie.'

Pajijijik.

Lightning crackled on my right fist.

His physical abilities are higher, but I don't think I'll lose.

Eun Sang-min swung his fist at me from a distance. Stone thorns created in the air fly towards me. I stepped on the footwork to avoid the thorns and approached Eun Sang-min.

,Even if I don't have a sword, it doesn't mean I can't use the Yeongcheon style footwork.'

Eun Sang-min and I engaged in close combat. As expected of a C-rank Hunter, he showed signs of having learned martial arts. But it looked very clumsy to me. It was obvious that he didn't use his fists often.

I avoided his fists and kicks with the Yeongcheon style footwork and began to attack him unilaterally.

,Aaaaaaaaaah!'

Eun Sang-min lets out an angry scream. I noticed the ground nearby wriggling.

I immediately used Flash.

The ground turned into thorns and rose towards me, appearing slow. I jumped up to avoid the thorns and kicked Eun Sang-min.

,Keok!'

Eun Sang-min fell to the ground. It's the perfect timing to take a mount position, but I couldn't rush in because there's no answer if Eun Sang-min turns his whole body into thorns like a hedgehog.

,You're a C-rank Hunter. Why can't you fight like this? Are you really a C-rank? Did you bribe someone to become a Hunter?'

,You bastard!'

It was the moment Eun Sang-min stood up again.

,Stop!!'

Han Ha-rin activated her ability to stop us.

Gravity field.

A higher gravity is applied to my and Eun Sang-min's bodies. But it was far from enough to stop us.

Just as mana provides resistance to my lightning to prevent electrocution, it gives resistance to Han Ha-rin's gravity. Han Ha-rin's gravity cannot subdue me and Eun Sang-min. At best, it only slows down the speed of our bodies.

A long thorn extends from Eun Sang-min's grasp. Eun Sang-min wielded the thorn like a sword. It's a different level of skill than his awkward punches.

,This bastard... he crossed the line.'

Bare hands and weapons. The weight felt from the words alone is different.

I used Flash to create distance for now.

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 2]

I don't take out a weapon. I want to hide the existence of the inventory, and I thought it would be enough to use my bare hands since I have Flash.

,I've already beaten him enough. Let's knock him out and end this.'

He can't react to my Flash. If he were a proper C-rank, he would have reacted, but that guy was just a nobody who just became a C-rank.

I stepped on the footwork again and approached Eun Sang-min. I aim for the gap in his slowed movements.

I saw Eun Sang-min's eyes.

The moment I saw his eyes filled with hostility, old memories came to mind.

It was the voice of a college classmate I accidentally heard while passing by the hallway.

,Ah, seriously. What's with the ability to extend the body? I can only extend it by 30cm, what am I supposed to do with this ability? My life sucks. Should I just quit being a Hunter?'

,Hey. Hey. It's better than having no ability at all. You can become a Hunter. Look at Seong Yujin. He hasn't even awakened, but he still comes to the department. Honestly, isn't it a lie to say he has potential at that point?'

,Hehe. Well, it's better to have this kind of ability than having no ability like him.'

I knew that those guys were treating me like a retard behind my back, even though they pretended to care about me on the surface.

The subtle contempt felt in their eyes and the ridicule that took place where I couldn't see.

I knew it, but I couldn't do anything. I didn't have any power. I wondered several times if I should stop becoming a Hunter halfway and live as a normal person now.

,Hey. Are you the Hunter hopeful in our department? I heard you still haven't awakened after a year? Why is a normal person in the Hunter department?'

,Sang-min-ah. You've had too much to drink. Calm down.'

,Ah, come on. Let go. Did I say something wrong? Huh? If you're incompetent, you should be like, ah, I'm not cut out to be a Hunter. And just get the hell out. Why are you staying in the Hunter department and making a mess? It's so embarrassing to have a guy like you as my junior. Seriously.'

,Yujin-ah. Don't mind him. He's just a little drunk.'

,Hey. This is what ability is all about. Are you scared? Why are you trembling? It just grazed you. A guy who wants to be a Hunter is scared of just this... Man. Hunters always have to be prepared for death. You can't be scared like that.'

,Eun Sang-min! What are you doing?! He's still a normal person who hasn't awakened his ability! Are you crazy?!'

,Ah, geez... It's a joke. A joke. Why are you making such a fuss over just this...'

Pudeudeuk.

Eun Sang-min's arm was bent in an impossible direction and broken.

,Kuaaaaaaak!'

Hearing his scream, I took the strength out of my left hand and raised my right hand. A dark Demonic Energy was rising from my right hand.

I smiled at him. His eyes, trembling and sitting on the floor looking up at me, were filled with fear.

,A C-rank Hunter is trembling over just this... Weren't Hunters always supposed to be prepared for death?'

I used Demonic Asura (天魔修羅) to kill Eun Sang-min. Demonic Asura is a hand technique (手功) in Cheonma Shingong. He should feel honored to die by Demonic Asura.

,Kill... huh?'

Suddenly, my vision widened. I was filled with the thought of killing Eun Sang-min, but those thoughts suddenly disappeared, and the situation came into view.

It's like drinking very cold water when you're sweating profusely from the heat on a summer street.

I opened my eyes wide when I saw the Demonic Asura formed in my hand.

,Sh, shit. Demonic Energy? Why suddenly this?'

The black Demonic Energy in my right hand gradually decreased and disappeared without a trace.

,No. More than that. I didn't intend to kill this bastard!'

It's true that Eun Sang-min is an annoying guy. It's also true that I have a lot of pent-up feelings towards him. But it's not enough to kill Eun Sang-min.

If this were a game world, I would have killed him without a second thought, but this is reality. If I killed Eun Sang-min here, I wouldn't be able to handle the aftermath.

The reason I picked a fight with Eun Sang-min was because I judged that I could handle it.

,Why did I kill that guy...'

The question didn't continue. Suddenly, someone ran up from behind and grabbed both of my arms, subduing me in an instant.

,Eok!?'

,Don't move! Hunter Association!'

I was about to resist reflexively, but I took the strength out of my body when I heard those words. I shouldn't resist unnecessarily. The opponent has public power and can immediately execute Hunters.

,What are you doing in the middle of the street in broad daylight? Are you guys crazy? Huh?'

,......'

,Get up! If you use your abilities or move mana, I'll just kill you right away. Just get in the car quietly!'

•••

I was locked up in the Detention center of the Hunter Association's Seoul Southern Branch. I am inside a steel cage made for Hunters. I have already been investigated by the association staff and am waiting in the Detention center.

I looked at the Detention center across from me. Eun Sang-min, who I fought with, was also locked up. His body was fine. A Hunter with healing abilities among the association staff healed him.

,This will just end with a fine.'

Although the result is the opposite, Eun Sang-min is a C-rank Hunter, and I am an E-rank Hunter. Due to the nature of the Hunter Law, it is much more disadvantageous to Eun Sang-min, who is of a higher rank. Even though I beat him up.

Han Ha-rin wrote a statement as a witness and left. Judging by her expression, it seems that the person who reported it to the association was not her, but the owner of the cafe.

Eun Sang-min and I will end this with an agreement. The Hunter Law is fucked up, so whether you're the perpetrator or the victim, you're punished just for fighting in reality, not in a dungeon. In other words, it basically assumes mutual fault.

The best thing to do is to come to an agreement with each other and pay a fine to the Hunter Association to make this incident disappear.

I leaned against the wall of the Detention center and fell into thought.

,Putting aside the fact that I tried to kill Eun Sang-min... I used Demonic Asura in reality.'

I unconsciously used Cheonma Shingong.

That doesn't make sense no matter how I think about it. Except for the Demonic Asura just now, I have never succeeded in using Cheonma Shingong in reality even once.

But I unconsciously succeeded during combat? Considering my talent, it's absolutely impossible.

,There's no way this kind of thing would happen without a reason.'

It's a serious matter. There's no guarantee that this won't happen again.

,What's suspicious is... Cheonma Shingong.'

There's nothing but Cheonma Shingong. If you look at the Cheonma Shingong that often appears in novels, the essence of Cheonma Shingong is ultimately Demonic Art.

I went berserk and tried to kill Eun Sang-min using Cheonma Shingong. Using Cheonma Shingong, which I had never been able to use in reality, to do so.

,Cheonma Shingong is a martial art given to me by the King of the Demonic Sky. That guy's identity is probably a devil. Judging by his actions, he's definitely a devil.'

My conclusion is that the King of the Demonic Sky tampered with Cheonma Shingong.

Let's compare it to a computer.

I am an awesome computer. And Cheonma Shingong is an awesome porn video. The problem is that the porn video contains a virus that is infecting the computer.

,I had to replace my computer because of a virus in the past. I got scolded by my mom, but I was able to replace it with the latest computer.'

That method is impossible. I can't change myself into another me.

,It's impossible to remove the virus even with Complete Recovery.'

Because the subject of the virus is Cheonma Shingong. If I don't get rid of Cheonma Shingong, the virus will continue to appear.

,......A vaccine for the virus.'

I have one characteristic that can be called a vaccine.

Mental Resistance.

,I can't be sure, but... wouldn't it have some effect?'

It's not possible to raise the level of the Mental Resistance trait right now. I didn't have any points.

,And I need to go to the Atlantis of God world and find out about Cheonma Shingong. King of the Demonic Sky. That bastard is still untrustworthy.'

It was when I was internally cursing the King of the Demonic Sky.

A man approached the front of the Detention center. He looks to be in his mid-30s. He's a handsome man with a sharp jaw. He's wearing round glasses.

He looked at me in the Detention center. He is wearing a neat and luxurious black suit, but he doesn't seem to be an association employee. I also did not avoid his eyes.

,Brother-in-law! Over here!'

Eun Sang-min said. I realized the man's identity. There's only one person who could be Eun Sang-min's brother-in-law.

Han Su-ri.

He is the master of the Han Su-ri Guild, an A-rank Hunter, and a magician.

He didn't respond to his brother-in-law Eun Sang-min's call and opened his mouth towards me.

,I'm sorry. My brother-in-law caused trouble.'
