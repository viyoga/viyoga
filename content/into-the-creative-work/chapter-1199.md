I had to receive treatment from the doctor and have an interview with Ipmaso Chief Bae Taek-ju.

We faced each other alone in a narrow space. But we weren't equal. Unlike him, who was free, my hands and feet were tied. I felt the psychological pressure that Bae Taek-ju might kill me.

,Number 123. Why did you attack Instructor Jeong?

Bae Taek-ju's low voice echoed as it hit the walls and bounced back.

,It wasn't an attack, but an accident that happened during sparring.

,Instructor Jeong's dantian is shattered. A martial artist's dantian is shattered. I won't have you say you don't know what that means, will I?

,I know. It's as good as the end of a martial artist's life.

,That's right. You killed Instructor Jeong.

,I didn't kill him. He's still alive, isn't he?

,Why did you do it?

,I've said it several times, it was an accident. The instructor was the one who suggested sparring first. The instructor was the one who handed me the knife. If you're suspicious, ask Group 1. There are over 100 witnesses.

,.......

,And I was injured too. I got burned by the Yeolhyang Palm that instructor used.

I twitched my left arm at Bae Taek-ju as if to show off. It wasn't easy to lift my left arm with my hands tied.

I was wrapped in bandages because I had received treatment from the doctor.

,I saw it earlier, and it was very gruesome because of the pus and melted skin. I'll show you if you want.

,That's enough. The doctor said that since I applied medicine, there might be scars, but it won't be a problem.

,That's a relief.

,...Instructor Jeong has to leave Ipmaso because of you.

,He's not leaving the Shin-gyo (Divine Cult), is he? He'll live his life within the Shin-gyo.

In this world, martial artists are envied. Martial arts were power, and the higher the realm, the longer the lifespan. Ordinary people fear and admire martial artists.

Of course, that doesn't mean everyone becomes a martial artist. In terms of percentage, the number of martial artists wouldn't even be 5%. Among them, skilled martial artists are even fewer. Most live as farmers. This isn't much different even in the Cheonma Shin-gyo (Demonic Heavenly Cult).

,I'm getting hungry. How long do I have to stay here?

,...Number 123. I'm warning you. Behave yourself. Don't ignore the instructor's orders or talk back.

,Yes. I understand. But this time it was a real accident. And this is just my opinion... Didn't I make one of those bastards blind on the first day? I think Instructor Jeong was hired by that guy.

,I'll investigate that separately. Until the results come out, don't do anything and wait here.

,Here? What about food? I get a little edgy when I'm hungry. I want to take a bath too. Shouldn't I maintain cleanliness?

,Shut up and wait here.

Bae Taek-ju got up from his seat and went outside.

I was still restrained.

,Bae Taek-ju tried to push me mentally, but he didn't physically beat me. Things are going well.

I looked at my left arm wrapped in bandages. Pain rushed in. My arm hurts. I was dying to use Complete Recovery.

,No. If the burn disappears now, they'll think it's strange. I have to pretend to be hurt for a week... no, about three days.

And I was able to return to my accommodation around evening.

I heard that the instructor who fought with me left Ipmaso.

•••

,Number 123. Won't you spar with me?

Number 88, So Cheonma (Little Heavenly Demon) Cheon Yu-un approached me and spoke.

After the incident where I shattered the instructor's dantian, there were hardly any people who wanted to spar with me. The instructors were no exception. They were also subtly afraid of me. The fact that the difference between the instructor's skills and mine wasn't that great also played a part.

Outwardly, I'm at the 9th stage of Ipsik, and the average level of the Ipmaso instructors is around the 8th stage of Ipsik. The average for Group 1 instructors is the 2nd stage of Chulji.

,You want to spar with me? Number 88, are you out of your mind?

,Haha. Are you worried about the sparring that happened last time? That was just an accident. I know it, and others know it too.

Cheon Yu-un acted like a magnanimous person. His actual personality isn't magnanimous. He's just acting because it's easier to build rapport with others as a magnanimous person. Cheon Yu-un is the type of guy who smiles in front and does behind-the-scenes work behind your back.

The reason he's approaching me now is probably because he's sure that I'm Yeom Gu-seok. That's why I subtly took off my top yesterday and showed Cheon Yu-un the red demon tattoo on my back.

Cheon Yu-un, who has information from the original work, knows Yeom Gu-seok's talent. From now on, he'll try to make me his close aide no matter what.

I just need to oppose him at first and then swear allegiance later. Just like he uses information from the original work, I can use information from the original work too.

Cheon Yu-un took out a sword, and I grabbed a greatsword.

It's a slightly awkward weapon for me, but I couldn't help it. In the original work, Yeom Gu-seok uses this weapon to perform the Chamgwi Dobeop (Slaying Ghost Swordsmanship).

The instructors gathered. They watched Cheon Yu-un and me and announced the start of the sparring.

I rushed towards Cheon Yu-un aggressively. It was Yeom Gu-seok's fighting method in the early part of the original work.

,Fierce.

Cheon Yu-un put strength into his legs and deflected my knife. His expression was full of composure. Just like I'm hiding my strength with the Geumje (Forbidden System), he was also hiding his strength. It was difficult to grasp how much strength he was hiding, but I guessed that he had reached the realm of Ogi (Five Energies).

Kaang! Kang!

Sparks flew as the sword and knife collided. I was pushing with strength, but instead of overwhelming him, I was being pushed back.

,He's not using strength, but technique to fight me.

He's defending by deflecting strength appropriately. From my point of view, it's just petty tricks. If I get serious, I can break through Cheon Yu-un's technique and inflict a fatal wound.

,No. I have to lose here.

I forcibly suppressed the rising annoyance. I have to move according to the plan here.

Cheon Yu-un deliberately showed an opening. It's bait. A deliberately shown gap. The moment I dig into that, the sparring will end in my defeat.

,But I can't not go in. At my current level, I shouldn't have the skill to recognize that the gap is a fake.

I had to move according to Cheon Yu-un's intentions. I stepped towards him, using a slightly clumsy Chamgwi Dobeop footwork.

As if he had been waiting, Cheon Yu-un rotated his body and accurately deflected my knife. My wrist bends as it is.

,...Completely getting defeated is too much for my pride.

I forcibly grabbed the knife handle that was trying to slip out of my hand and swung it down. The tip of the knife grazed Cheon Yu-un's shoulder. A little blood splattered. Cheon Yu-un's eyes widened as if he was surprised.

Tteonggeurang!

The knife in my hand fell to the floor. I looked at my wrist and clicked my tongue. The price for forcibly swinging the knife was certain. My wrist was completely broken. I can't put strength into my right hand.

,I lost.

I looked at my broken wrist and said as if spitting it out. The instructor ran over and blocked between me and Cheon Yu-un.

,...To think you'd swing the knife in that state. I was surprised. You're really amazing.

,Amazing...? Are you mocking me? I'm just a loser.

,I was just genuinely impressed. Won't you spar again later?

,.......

I narrowed my eyes and looked at Cheon Yu-un. Cheon Yu-un was smirking. I could feel the greed towards me. With this, he's convinced of Yeom Gu-seok's talent.

,I won't be defeated so easily next time.

,Haha. I'll be looking forward to it.

Starting from this day, Cheon Yu-un and I started talking little by little and became closer. Cheon Yu-un was the one who started talking to me. Things were going well.

•••

Two months have passed since I entered Ipmaso. The training period at Ipmaso is one year, and exams are held every three months. Depending on their exam scores, trainees could be demoted to a lower group, or in the worst case, be expelled from Ipmaso.

Ipmaso Chief Bae Taek-ju gathered the trainees in the plaza and said.

,From now on, we will open the Ipmaso Seogo (Demonic Scripture Archive). You must learn one of the martial arts in the Ipmaso Seogo. In a month, we will test how much you have learned of the martial arts in the Ipmaso Seogo.

Ipmaso Seogo is a place where martial arts books are collected. But I don't particularly expect anything. This is because Ipmaso Seogo is only a place where basic martial arts are collected. The advanced martial arts that are commonly talked about in the world do not exist in Ipmaso Seogo.

Group 1, most of whom had learned advanced martial arts, were indifferent.

Group 2 was nervous. They could be promoted to Group 1 with the exam in a month, or be demoted and go down to Group 3.

Group 3's eyes sparkled with anticipation. Unlike Group 1 and Group 2, they were guys who had learned mediocre martial arts. For them, the martial arts in Ipmaso Seogo were an opportunity.

,Group 1 will enter first. Look around the Ipmaso Seogo and tell the instructor the martial art you have chosen. We will give you a handwritten copy of the martial art you have chosen in the evening. Please leave the martial arts books inside the Ipmaso Seogo as they are.

Group 1, to which I belonged, entered Ipmaso Seogo first. I was lukewarm. The martial arts here don't catch my eye. Hidden opportunity? Unfortunately, there's no such thing as a hidden opportunity in Ipmaso Seogo.

I wandered around inside Ipmaso Seogo alone.

A martial arts book catches my eye.

Chamgwi Dobeop (Slaying Ghost Swordsmanship).

It's the swordsmanship that Yeom Gu-seok learned. And it was also the swordsmanship that I learned to play Yeom Gu-seok.

Chamgwi Dobeop was a common martial art. If you have enough money, you can easily get it on the black market. Rather, it's more difficult to enter the black market than to get the martial art.

,One of the swordsmanships that the bandits near this area learn is Chamgwi Dobeop.

I passed by Chamgwi Dobeop. I couldn't choose Chamgwi Dobeop because I had already learned it. I have to choose something new that I haven't learned.

,I recommend this footwork to you.

I heard Cheon Yu-un's voice. I stopped walking and turned my gaze to the side where the voice came from. Cheon Yu-un was leaning against a bookshelf and talking to a beautiful woman with an expressionless face. Number 567, Yeon Ye-ha, the daughter of the Sword Demon. She is currently known as the most beautiful woman in the Demonic Cult, and in the future, she will be called Myeongin Mahu (Bright Blade Demon Queen) and become one of the top powerhouses in the Cheonma Shin-gyo (Demonic Heavenly Cult).

,What's the reason?

Yeon Ye-ha's voice didn't contain any emotion.

It's because of the effect of the Chamjeong Mashingeom (Severing Emotions Demon God Sword) that she learned. It's a demonic art that gains powerful power in exchange for abandoning emotions.

,Yuryeongbo (Ghost Step) is specialized in hiding traces. It may seem like a useless footwork right now, but it's not bad to learn it. There will be a moment when it's needed someday. Maybe that's in the near future.

,I see.

,It's just my recommendation. You don't have to learn it forcibly.

,No, I'll learn it. I've looked through the martial arts in Ipmaso Seogo, and they all seem to be the same.

,Okay. You won't regret it.

,Thank you for the recommendation.

Yeon Ye-ha left as she was.

Cheon Yu-un turned his head and looked at me. He smirks.
