,You, be my woman."

Poseidon was horrified. Was that really something you just blurt out to a woman? He already knew his brother liked women, but he didn't know he'd move so recklessly. Frankly, it was embarrassing as a brother.

,Look at Medusa. She doesn't know what to do.'

It's no wonder she's flustered when the chief god of Olympus suddenly appears and makes demands. Even if Medusa isn't human. Poseidon hurriedly followed after Zeus. The horse he was riding flew through the sky and arrived next to Zeus.

,Zeus! Is that how you treat a woman! I'm embarrassed as your own brother!"

Poseidon rebuked Zeus, his beard fluttering.

Honestly, he was a little scared shitless. He knew better than anyone how incredibly nasty Zeus's temper was.

But he was the second most powerful god in Olympus after Zeus. Even Zeus wouldn't be able to act recklessly. Though he realized immediately afterward that that thought was a delusion.

,Are you out of your mind, Poseidon? As the master of the sky, I have the right to have all the women in this world. Don't butt in and get lost."

,...."

Such abusive language that didn't respect his authority at all!

He might have shrunk back in normal times, but now his destiny, Medusa, was right before his eyes. Poseidon didn't back down.

,Zeus. No matter how much of a god of the sky you are, you don't have that right."

,Are you really out of your mind? Stop talking nonsense and go back to the sea. This isn't your territory."

,You bastard, Zeus...!"

The sea reacted to Poseidon's anger. The calm sea stirred up violent waves. It was like a roaring beast heading towards the land.

Zeus frowned deeply. The sky reacted to his discomfort. Dark clouds gathered. A cold yet intense wind swirled in the air.

Kureureureureung.

Blue lightning wriggled within the dark clouds. That alone made everyone on the ground tremble in fear.

Medusa was no exception. She was merely kneeling before the two gods, trembling as if sobbing.

,This son of a bitch is serious.'

Poseidon, with a solemn face, was inwardly flustered. There was no sign of him backing down at all. He felt bewildered and infuriated.

,Is he going to hit on every beautiful woman, goddess or human? Didn't he hit on Persephone as soon as she was born last time? Zeus. This bastard is truly insane. I must step in for the future of Olympus!'

He made an effort to erase from his mind the story of his nephew who was thrown into Tartarus for harboring ulterior motives towards Aphrodite.

,Zeus. Let's talk in private. She's too scared if we're growling at each other here."

,Wouldn't it be solved if you backed down? I'm not just acting on a whim. It's all to prepare for the future."

,Are you talking about the Gigantomachy...? Even so, you've gone too far."

,Hmm."

Zeus and Poseidon soared into the sky. After rising above the clouds, they confirmed that no one was watching. Poseidon sighed.

,Zeus. I fell in love with her at first sight. I felt an intense destiny as soon as I saw her. Acknowledge her and me."

,What a coincidence. I also felt destiny with her. Can't you tell by the fact that I came down directly from the sky?"

,Zeus-isiyeo, King of the Gods-isiyeo. You have many women, don't you? Give Medusa to me."

,There are countless women in this world. But no two women are the same. Medusa is my woman. If you try to take my woman, I won't forgive you, no matter who you are."

Pajijijijik.

Lightning crackled around Zeus. Zeus was serious right now. Poseidon let go of his competitive spirit. Fighting Zeus one-on-one? That was suicide. The moment he lost, Zeus would throw him into Tartarus without hesitation. Just like he always had.

,Zeus. Calm down. We're not ignorant Titans, but shining, intelligent gods of Olympus. If we fight, the world we've barely calmed down will become a mess. Let's resolve this peacefully."

,Peacefully? How so?"

,Let Medusa choose. Or are you perhaps not confident?"

,Not confident? Fine."

The compromise was easily reached. Poseidon and Zeus appeared before Medusa, who still hadn't come to her senses.

Poseidon stepped forward first.

,Medusa. My love. Your destiny is me. I have a gift prepared for you. Look at this horse. A horse that can run on the sea. Not only that, but over a million sea creatures will sacrifice their lives for you. If you choose me, all of this will be yours. Become the mother of the sea."

,Th-that is."

Medusa trembled. It was because Zeus, who was standing next to her, was radiating an ominous aura.

Zeus stepped forward and said.

,Medusa. You're my woman. If you don't choose me... I'll kill them all. All of them."

,Hieuk...!"

All of them.

At those words, Medusa thought of her sisters. Stheno, Euryale. Her ugly sisters, but they were her blood relatives.

And Zeus before her had massacred tens of thousands of people not long ago because he didn't like the memorial service.

,Who will you choose?"

,Y-Zeus-nim."

Medusa chose, trembling. Zeus smiled with satisfaction and approached Medusa, raising one arm to embrace her shoulder. She looked so pitiful as she trembled.

,I can't accept this!!"

Poseidon shouted. A violent wave rose and struck the land.

,What can't you accept now? Poseidon. Maintain your dignity as a god of Olympus."

,You're the one who should maintain your dignity!! Aren't you ashamed of threatening Medusa! I can't accept this!"

,What are you going to do if you don't accept it?"

Zeus's voice dropped coldly. Poseidon was startled, but he had already gained momentum.

,Destiny! I felt destiny from Medusa! Let's go to the three goddesses of destiny, the Moirai, and confirm my destiny with Medusa!"

,You're doing useless things. Medusa has already chosen me."

,Are you scared, Zeus!!"

,That's a petty provocation. I have time to spare, so I'll humor you. But you'll have to pay the price."

Zeus and Poseidon immediately went to find the Moirai.

Clotho of the past, Lachesis of the present, and Atropos of the future. The three old women spun, wound, and cut the threads of destiny, respectively.

The old women, who should have been working diligently, were not working. They were gathered in one place, chatting, and the threads of destiny were scattered messily on the floor. They were tangled to the point where it was impossible to tell what was what.

Zeus and Poseidon approached them, trampling on the threads of destiny that represented someone's fate. Poseidon was especially horrified.

,Moirai! What the hell is this mess? Why aren't you working?!"

,The noisy god of the sea has come. I don't know what purpose you've come for, but as you can see, all destinies have escaped from our hands."

,What in the world..."

Poseidon stared at the tangled threads with incomprehensible eyes. The threads of destiny appeared on their own, moved and intertwined on their own, and repeatedly broke on their own. That's right. Destiny was moving uncontrollably, without being controlled.

,Those who were supposed to become heroes are unable to bloom their brilliant stories, and those who shouldn't have been born are being born. Prophecies have lost their meaning, and even the gods cannot see an inch ahead... No one knows how this destiny will end."

,Moirai! Is that something you should be saying?! Isn't it because you've been negligent that things have turned out this way! It's not too late now! Correct destiny!"

,Ruler of the sea, the destiny you desire is not here. And do you truly not know the cause of why destiny has become like this?"

,The cause?"

The Moirai looked at Zeus.

,All destinies have become like this since you were born."

Zeus stared emotionlessly at the tangled threads of destiny.

,If there is no destiny, then you must respect Medusa's opinion, which she chose herself. Medusa is my woman."

Poseidon's face crumpled.

,Destiny is a mess right now! Medusa isn't what's important!"

,Medusa is more important than this incomprehensible destiny. I have decided Medusa's destiny, which has no destiny, so I have won the bet. Poseidon. Pay the price."

,...I'm going crazy. Did you know that destiny was in this state?"

,No. This is my first time coming here too."

,And yet you're so calm? Don't you know how serious the situation is?"

,It's already a finished destiny. I'm not interested in destiny now, no matter what."

,What are you talking about? What do you know?"

,Enough. Pay the price."

,...Are you going to throw me into Tartarus? Can you handle the sea without me?"

That was why Poseidon could confidently stand up to Zeus.

Right now, the sea was stabilized by Poseidon. There was an unknown monster god crouching deep in the sea, and as long as that monster god continued to create its subordinates, he calculated that Zeus wouldn't be able to do anything to him.

,Of course not. What I want are your daughters. You have 50 daughters, right? Hand them all over."

,This crazy bastard..."

,If you don't hand them over, I'll have no choice but to throw you into Tartarus.... This is a consideration I'm giving to my brother. I hope you'll understand my feelings."

Poseidon's hand, holding the trident, trembled.

,Are you really going to do this?! If I'm gone, the sea will be in chaos! Do you want the chaos of the sea to head towards the land?!"

,Poseidon. The sea is vast, but that's all it is. History is written on the land, and the sky encompasses everything. Don't become arrogant."

,Who is truly arrogant...!"

Poseidon gritted his teeth.

There was no choice. There was no possibility of winning even if he fought Zeus here.

At the same time, he realized the essence of Zeus.

King of the Gods.

He was a tyrant made of greed, with no intention of letting go of power.

Poseidon lowered his head.

He had no choice but to do so for now.

,The owner of 70% laments."

An unfamiliar voice penetrated Poseidon's ears.
