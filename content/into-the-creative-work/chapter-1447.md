I involuntarily gaped at the female ninja who vanished in an instant.

I guess you could say she was ninja-like, incredibly fast. I immediately scanned the surroundings with detection magic, but there was no sign of anyone. She had completely disappeared in that brief moment.

,I wanted to ask her about the power the ninja was using.'

I knew she wouldn't answer easily. Nevertheless, I couldn't resist the sudden surge of curiosity and opened my mouth.

,That's definitely a power related to magic.'

It was different from the magic I use, but it was definitely a power based on magic.

,Ninja characters are hard to find even in the original game, but here I am seeing one.'

Ninjas were one of the jobs that players couldn't choose. I remember there being rumors that the ninja would be added as DLC content later. I think so too.

I turned around. The corpses that the suddenly appeared ninja had massacred were scattered, filling the floor with blood. I smelled the fishy odor and felt a little relieved.

,I didn't even know that female ninja was nearby until she revealed herself.'

I would have noticed immediately if she had entered my sensory range, but it was quite shocking that I didn't notice her until right before she killed the gang members.

,I don't know her exact skills... but she's probably stronger than I am now.'

I checked the four transparent barriers surrounding my body. With the barriers, I would be able to block a few attacks.

,The problem is that I can't keep the barriers up all day because I'm afraid of assassination.'

I need a solid defense against assassination. I have something in mind, but it was difficult to implement right away. Even if my magical talent is genius-level, I haven't mastered all magic yet.

,I know far fewer spells than other 5th-grade wizards.'

I walked out of the building and stopped. I looked back at the corpses scattered behind me once again. I pondered for a moment. My original plan was to brutally kill the gang members and then display them. But the female ninja intervened. Is it okay to leave it like this without any repercussions?

,It's a warning, so it should be fine. But first... should I talk to Rose?'

She might know about ninjas.

•••

Instead of going home, I went to the X Manpower Agency in the X district.

It was still early dawn, before the sun had even risen properly. Laborers were lined up in front of the manpower agency, waiting. They weren't mercenaries like me, but laborers who were dispatched to factories and other places to earn money. They completely ignored me. I didn't bother talking to them either.

Soon, Rose appeared. She looked around once and then assigned jobs to the laborers.

,Don't cause any accidents on site.'

,Haha... I was drunk and out of my mind back then.'

,Don't secretly drink while working. I'm warning you, this is really the last time.'

Rose had a word with each of the laborers. Rough words were exchanged. After all the laborers disappeared, she beckoned me with her finger.

,Come in. What brings you here today? A request?'

,Personal business.'

I sat facing her in the office. Rose frowned as she listened to my explanation.

,You caused an accident... but you have justification on your side. The Fraxson Mercenary Corps won't get involved. They're not a stupid group that can't tell the difference between right and wrong in this kind of situation. The Harvers Gang... could be a problem. Those guys sometimes don't care about the consequences. It should be fine for now. As far as I know, Jupiter is quite busy right now.'

,Do you know anything about ninjas? I think she was called Invisible Blade.'

,What's with that ridiculous name? You got involved with a weirdo.'

,Not a weirdo, a weird woman.'

,...A really weird woman. I know a few ninjas, but I don't know the name Invisible Blade. You said she's highly skilled?'

,I don't know her exact skills. But it's certain that she's not ordinary.'

,For such a skilled person to massacre a small-fry gang like Harvers... there's no reason other than to build a reputation. She's probably a newbie who hasn't been in the city for long.'

,Do you know much about ninjas?'

,I know a few famous ninjas who are active in Neo London. I've heard that the roots of ninjas are in Unbelievable Japan, or something?'

Unbelievable Japan.

It was an ancient country that existed about 800 years ago. It doesn't exist now.

,Do you know anything about the ninjutsu that ninjas use?'

,I don't know much. Are you that curious about it?'

,From my shallow knowledge, ninjas use hand seals when using ninjutsu. But that female ninja used ninjutsu without using hand seals.'

,Wizards use hand seals too. And you even use no-chanting. Is that such a surprising thing?'

,Ninjas and wizards are different.'

,You must have been pretty surprised to see a ninja.'

,I was surprised. It was literally a ninja reality shock....'

,What's that again?'

,It's a thing.'

,Anyway, I'm sorry, but I don't know much about ninjas.'

,Okay.'

I got up from my seat.

,Aren't you going to take a request? There's a request that came in for you yesterday.'

,I'm too busy to do that. But what kind of request is it?'

,It's a bodyguard request. It's almost Dark Moon now. The pay is quite high, are you going to do it? There's still plenty of time until Dark Moon.'

,...Bodyguard requests are a bit much.'

,Why? Bodyguard requests are the kind of requests where you can get paid without doing anything if you're lucky.'

,I've done bodyguard-related work a few times... and it's rarely been a good experience.'

,Okay. Then I'll reject this request. Oh, thanks for coming and telling me about what happened today. Well, there's nothing I can do... but there are some guys who cause accidents and don't say anything.'

I said goodbye to her and went home.

•••

I closed the store earlier than usual and hid in the alleyway, watching the fish and chips shop across the street. I quietly followed the shop owner, John Clark. He was hunched over, expressing his anxiety with his body as he looked around.

,It seems like he heard the news about the Harvers Gang.'

He knows the inside story, so he'll guess that I'm involved. He's afraid of the blade of revenge.

John's house was in the F district. A so-called upper-class district. The edge of the upper-class district. A district for the nouveau riche who only have money.

,A guy who runs a fish and chips shop lives here? No way. Did he make a killing when he was in the gang and buy a house?'

I watched him open the front door and go inside.

[Detection]

I checked the surroundings with magic.

There were no guards. Instead, there were unmanned security robots in the garden and inside the house. They were hidden so that they weren't easily visible from the outside.

I grasped the level. I immediately took action. Back in the military, I had experienced breaking through security robots countless times.

Security robots are machines. Unless they're expensive magic engineering security robots, they're bound to be vulnerable to electricity.

[Electric Field]

Electricity flowed from my feet along the ground. Of course, I adjusted the current appropriately. Just enough output to disable the security robots.

I walked forward. The security robots, which were cleverly hidden in the bushes, were disabled and didn't react at all.

Having successfully infiltrated, I sat on the sofa and put my hand on John's shoulder as he watched TV and drank beer.

,You're watching an interesting movie. Planetarium. It's a fun movie. A bit racy, though.'

,Y-you are...!'

John Clark was horrified and got up from the sofa. The beer can rolled out of his hand and dirtied the floor yellow.

,Thanks to you, I couldn't sleep last night. It's been a long time since I stayed up all night, so I was in a bad mood all day while working at the store.'

,I, I was just trying to get the recipe! I swear!'

,Do you kidnap people for a chicken recipe? What would have happened if I didn't have any power? Would I have been alive?'

,I, I didn't plan to kill you! I was going to let you go once I found out the recipe!'

,You're spouting nonsense that even a dog wouldn't believe. A person kidnapped by a gang is already dead. That's common sense that everyone in this city knows.'

Woong.

Mana moved and a magic circle was drawn in the air. Seeing the magic circle, he immediately knelt down, bowed his head, and rubbed his hands together.

,I was wrong! I'll apologize like this... just once...! Just once... Die!'

He took out a pistol hidden under the sofa and fired a bullet directly at my head.

Ting!

The result didn't even scratch the barrier.

,Under the sofa, behind the TV, inside the drawer... you've hidden seven weapons just in the living room. You must have had a lot of things to feel guilty about?'

,S-security robot! Intruder! There's an intruder! Why aren't you moving?!'

He pulled the trigger. The pouring bullets couldn't even break one barrier.

Pajijijik.

The completed magic circle flashed and disappeared. A deep sense of despair settled on his face. It was worth showing him the magic circle on purpose.

,The movie ended with the heroine and the hero breaking up. And this is my original magic.'

[Microwave]

His body swelled up and then exploded with a bang.

Since his whole body exploded in an instant, I don't know if he felt any pain. But it was more gruesome than anything else on the outside. Blood and guts splattered everywhere. It would have splattered on my body too if I hadn't manipulated the barrier.

I left, being careful not to leave any traces as much as possible.

Five days later, I got a call from Rose.

-Are you busy these days?

,No. I'm fine. Is it a request?'

The store has stabilized.

I hired people to take over the kitchen for me, and now it's a matter of people who want a franchise. All other time is personal time. I was using it to study magic.

-That's right. It's a designated request specifically for you. The content is related to Jupiter. I don't know the details yet. They're demanding strict security, so they've made it a condition that they'll only reveal the details if you accept the request.

The keyword was thrown.

Jupiter.

The client knows the details of the relationship between me, Harvers, and Jupiter. The client could be a member of Jupiter. If not, they could be related to the female ninja.

,I'll do it.'
