Kwaang!

Kim Jaeji slammed his fist on the desk. The redness spreading across his face wasn't just from the throbbing in his fist.

It was because Seong Yujin had buggered off to Jeju Island right before the operation was supposed to kick off.

Which meant the operation was now on indefinite hold.

,Jeju Island Recovery Operation? Without even consulting me?"

It wasn't just Vice President Kim Jaeji who'd been bypassed. The members of the National Assembly hadn't known about the Jeju Island Recovery Operation until right before the announcement. The only ones in the loop were a handful of generals in the military.

,Is it even right for the President to go out and fight himself?'

As far as he was concerned, Seong Yujin was a dictator dressed up in the clothes of democracy. And dictators valued their own lives above all else. Even if that weren't the case, a leader was the heart of the nation, so going to a dangerous place like a battlefield was just plain crazy.

,What's even more absurd is that those South Korean pigs and dogs are taking it for granted.'

Seong Yujin wasn't your average president.

He was an Awakened, a soldier, a saint, and a hero.

If you turned on the TV, you'd see Seong Yujin's battle footage against the Mutants every time you turned around. Cunningly edited propaganda videos with the message: Our President is so awesome!

The people of South Korea were practically brainwashed.

,He knew about the plan and ran away... No. If he knew, he would have set a trap instead.'

Seong Yujin had that ridiculous personal guard unit called the Knights. A combat unit more powerful than any special forces. If nothing else, he was pretty envious of that.

,The plan can't proceed, but... with the President out of the picture, I'm the most powerful person in this country right now. I need to seize as much of Seong Yujin's influence as possible.'

It was impossible to take control of the South Korean military in a short amount of time. He was their former enemy. The South Korean military didn't have particularly warm feelings toward Kim Jaeji.

,Hmm. Maybe seizing Seoul at this opportunity... wouldn't be so bad.'

The quickest method, a coup, flickered in his mind. But there was no way the South Korean people would accept him. The Southern Military was also a problem.

,Persuading the National Assembly is the safest method, I guess.'

Just then, the TV broadcast an emergency breaking news report about the situation on Jeju Island. A military reporter was showing the situation on Jeju Island.

Mutants that bullets couldn't penetrate were shambling around like ordinary zombies. Bizarre creatures clung to the land, plants, and buildings of Jeju Island, wriggling. Horrific monsters had formed a disgusting ecosystem on Jeju Island.

,Seong Yujin. Don't die, and come back....'

Power aside, Seong Yujin's ability was too precious. Eternal youth and health. He could enjoy it if he only had Seong Yujin.

It was just as Kim Jaeji was about to make a political move.

He heard terrible news.

The Roachists. Those monstrous bugs had simultaneously attacked various strongholds of the Northern Military.

,Those crazy bugs! You're not helping at all! Is this the Chinese bastards' doing?! And what's with those guys getting taken down by bugs!"

Kim Jaeji exploded in anger. Civilian casualties were also significant. When things went wrong, people were quick to blame the leader. The people of North Korea regarded Kim Jaeji, not President Seong Yujin, as the leader of North Korea. It was the effect of continuous brainwashing.

Kim Jaeji ordered the North Korean generals.

,Kill all those bugs! Show those Southern bastards our strength so they can't ignore us!"

Becoming the leader of a unified Joseon Korea was Kim Jaeji's goal. To achieve that, he had to show achievements that the South Korean people couldn't ignore. The Northern Military being defeated by the Roachists was extremely bad for his future.

It was just as the North Korean generals were about to move, startled by his roar.

,Marshal Kim Jaeji-nim! There's a rebellion! Generals, including Lieutenant General Park Hahok, have started a rebellion!!"

,What?!"

The worst news had arrived.

And this news was broadcast throughout the Korean Peninsula a few minutes later. It was news that smeared Kim Jaeji's face, who held the title of Marshal of the North Korean military, beyond just making it dirty.

South Korean public opinion ridiculed Kim Jaeji, and support for Kim Jaeji among the North Korean people began to waver.

,Th, this son of a bitch...! Southern Military! Request support from the Southern Military!"

It was humiliating, but there was no choice.

The rebellion had to be put down immediately. The moment the civil war dragged on even a little, his influence over the Northern Military would disappear.

Jin Suhak, who had become the Minister of National Defense from the Chief of Staff of the Army by staging a coup in the previous Korea, scoffed at Kim Jaeji's request.

,The National Armed Forces are not your private soldiers. We may have unified, but North Korea and we were originally enemies. Military matters are sensitive, so we have agreed not to interfere with each other for now. North Korea should handle its own military affairs."

,Listen here, Minister! I am the Vice President of this country! Follow my orders!"

,The National Armed Forces only listen to the orders of His Excellency the President. You are not the President. Do not overstep your authority."

,Isn't this something that happened within the National Armed Forces?! The National Armed Forces should step in and handle it!!"

,This is His Excellency the President's order. South Korea and North Korea have unified, but it will take time for the sentiments and culture of the people to become one. The Southern Military is ordered not to interfere in the affairs of the Northern Military."

Minister of National Defense Jin Suhak hung up the phone just like that.

For a mere minister to act so rudely. Kim Jaeji, who was about to throw the phone in anger, paused.

There was an order from the President?

Putting aside how quickly he responded, was it right to ignore the rebellion of the Northern Military? The Northern Military was also part of the National Armed Forces, wasn't it? Wasn't the rebellion of the Northern Military also a rebellion of the National Armed Forces?

,...The Roachists' surprise attack after leaving for Jeju Island, followed by the rebellion of the Northern Military... There's no way this is all a coincidence.'

There was no evidence. But his gut, which had been the head of a country, was screaming vehemently.

This was all a trick by President Seong Yujin.

He drew his sword.

The tip of the sword was pointed at his own neck.

,.......

Kim Jaeji rubbed his neck.

,He didn't just get lucky and become president by awakening an ability. Did I underestimate him too much?'

He had dismissed him because he was young.

He thought he had taken the position of president with the ability of rejuvenation.

In reality, he was so ruthless and cold-blooded that he would swing a sword before the other side even acted.

Kim Jaeji gritted his teeth.

,It's difficult to quickly resolve the rebellion with the strength of the Northern Military alone. I can't focus solely on suppressing the rebellion because of those bugs.'

Seong Yujin was on Jeju Island. He wasn't dead. He could control the National Armed Forces through communication. He couldn't exercise his authority as acting president.

,...There's still a chance. When Seong Yujin returns. I'll aim for that time.'

He let go of the rebellion of the Northern Military.

The entire Northern Military hadn't rebelled. It wasn't a coup either. It was just a rebellion that had occurred within the military. It wouldn't be impossible to resolve. His influence would wane over time, but... he could accept that.

,I just need to get Seong Yujin.'

He would put a Seong Yujin look-alike in front and wield power. And he would gradually transfer Seong Yujin's influence to himself.

This plan would take a long time.

It would take at least five years to eliminate hostility from South Korea and accept him as a leader.

,It doesn't matter. If I can get Seong Yujin... I can do it for 10 years, 100 years.'

Kim Jaeji's eyes sparkled.

Jeju Island Temporary Base.

I was leisurely enjoying a vacation there. Even though gunshots rang out occasionally due to Gamyeo types or Mutants approaching the base, the stronghold was cozy, and supplies were plentiful. It could last at least a month.

,I have no intention of staying in a place like this for a month.'

With Na Chaeyoung and Choi Hyejin around, my lower body wasn't bored.

,I expected it to take a week at most, but... Kim Jaeji seems to have given up on responding. Then I can go back in tomorrow or the day after.'

All responsibility would be shifted to Kim Jaeji. Kim Jaeji, who had failed to suppress the rebellion of the Northern Military, would lose influence over the Northern Military. After that, he would slowly wither away. The day it was judged that his influence had disappeared. That would be the day I'd slit his throat.

The remaining variable was the nukes. Irina and the 3rd Knights would take care of that.

,Your Excellency. The damage to the Northern Military caused by the Roachists is greater than expected."

I had expected some damage from the start when executing the operation. There was no way Lee Yeonhee would say that without knowing it.

,Where was the variable?"

,The Roachists were faster and stronger than we had identified. Researchers say the Roachists have evolved."

,Evolved in that short amount of time?"

,Yes. The Roachists are more dangerous than we expected. According to reports, Roachists with wings have been discovered. They haven't been able to fly yet, probably because they haven't fully evolved."

I frowned.

It wasn't like I was afraid of those bugs.

Ordinary infantrymen might be afraid of bugs, but armored units could easily massacre them.

A Knight could easily kill more than 10 bugs alone.

,I'll have to exterminate the Roachists as soon as I get back. Have you found their colony?"

,We've found a few places. The real base of the Roachists is presumed to be in China."

,Tsk. I can't touch China right now. I'll only stir up a hornet's nest and suffer losses. It'll be impossible to completely exterminate those bugs."

,Yes. China is insidious as expected. They are untrustworthy people."

,Well, I don't like it, but if I leave them alone, they'll tear themselves apart. We just have to wait. Lee Yeonhee. Would you like a drink?"

I pointed to the beverage refrigerator. From cola to the finest wine. There was no drink missing.

,I'm on duty, so alcohol is a bit much. It's hot, so I'll have a cola."

I thought Lee Yeonhee would choose a domestic cola that you'd eat in the military.

I was wrong.

What she took out of the refrigerator was a cola made from barley. It was domestic, of course. I was slightly disgusted watching her gulp down the barley cola.

,Is everything good if it's domestic?"

,Of course not. I have my preferences too. I just prefer barley cola over regular cola."

Lee Yeonhee's taste seemed old-fashioned for her age.

I drank cola normally. It was good to drink as is, but this time I blended a little bit of drugs into it and drank it. It was a thrilling taste.

Ah. Aaaah~ Aaaah, aaaaaaah~

A singing voice came from outside the window. It was quite a sweet female voice. I didn't know who the singer was because it was the first time I'd heard the voice and melody.

,What big-balled bastard turned on the song in the base? I'm curious about his face."

No matter how good the song was, if it came into my room without permission, it was noise.

,Lee Yeonhee. Go find that bastard and drag him... Lee Yeonhee?"

Lee Yeonhee's face, which had been innocent yet somehow mysterious, had turned blank. There was no intelligence to be felt at all. She was staring blankly into the air, muttering something. I focused on her ears and could barely hear her voice.

,Long live the Republic of Korea. Korea must be great. Koreans should rightfully rule the world. The mandate of heaven is in Korea...."

,She suddenly went crazy. Is the cause the barley cola that Lee Yeonhee drank...."

I looked at the can of barley cola that Lee Yeonhee was holding with serious eyes. I didn't know that barley cola had such serious side effects.

-Master. The soldiers and knights in the stronghold have lost their minds. The song coming from outside the base is presumed to be the cause. Some soldiers have fallen into a state of mental confusion and have begun to riot.

Barley cola wasn't the cause.

Tang, tang, tang!

Gunshots rang out within the base. It seemed to be the work of the rioting soldier that Jena had mentioned.

,Is it a mental ability? If Lee Yeonhee couldn't resist at all and ended up like this... it's not ordinary. What about Doctor Na?"

-She has been injected with the anti-esper ampule that she usually carries. Doctor Na Chaeyoung is fine. Should I inject Chief Lee Yeonhee of the NIS with anti-esper?

Anti-esper was a drug that had the effect of resisting mental abilities. It was quite expensive because the material was the corpse of a special Mutant. It was a waste to use it on ordinary soldiers. It wasn't a waste to use it on Lee Yeonhee, but....

,It's okay. I'll do it."

I grabbed Lee Yeonhee's shoulder and used my ability. Intelligence returned to Lee Yeonhee's eyes.

,...I was attacked. I never thought I'd be attacked like this without being able to resist. Did I underestimate myself...."

Lee Yeonhee grabbed her head and shook it.

,It's not because you're lacking. This mental ability itself is ridiculously strong."

Lee Yeonhee was an Awakened with 5-star potential. She was attacked as soon as she heard a verse of the song? It wasn't that she was weak, but that this song was excessively strong.

,Jena. How are the Knights?"

-The armed Knights are fine. The unarmed Knights resisted by reciting prayers and armed themselves with combat suits. The symptoms of mental abnormality disappeared when the sound was blocked with helmets.

,It's less annoying than I thought. Have the Knights suppress the soldiers who have fallen into mental confusion."

-I have delivered the order.

,Where is the bitch singing this song?"

-She's outside the main gate of the base, 500m away. She's not human. She's presumed to be a Mutant.

,It's being heard all the way here from 500m away. She has an amazing vocal range. Prepare the combat suit. I'm going out myself. I need to kill her for sure before she evolves further."

-Yes. Master.

,Your Excellency. I will go with you."
