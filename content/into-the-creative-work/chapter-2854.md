Relations between Joseon Korea and China were not good.

China and Japan were the leading countries that opposed the reunification of South and North Korea. After reunification, they were practically in a state of severance. Of course, it was similar even before that, so I didn't care.

Jeong Du-jeong appeared on the large screen installed in my office.

Jeong Du-jeong, whom I hadn't seen in a while, looked utterly aged. His black hair had turned gray. His face also had quite a few wrinkles. He looked like he was in his sixties, not his fifties. That meant he had gone through a lot of hardship.

I leaned back deeply in the plush chair and watched him.

Jeong Du-jeong's eyebrows twitched. This was a childish battle of wills over who would speak first.

But the one who contacted first was at a disadvantage. Jeong Du-jeong opened his mouth.

,,...Nice to see you, President Seong. I saw the battle footage with the Roachists. It was impressive."

"You seem to have aged a lot. If you want, I can give you youth."

Jeong Du-jeong's eyes flashed sharply.

,,...How impertinent. Although this is a private meeting, show some respect. I am Jeong Du-jeong of China. If China sets its mind to it, it can wipe out a small country like Korea in three days."

"Then why don't you try it? Nuclear war? I can accept it anytime. Shall we do it until the land is soggy with radiation?"

,,...You're reacting too sharply. I admit that I spoke rashly."

Jeong Du-jeong lowered his momentum.

I swallowed the burst of laughter that was about to come out.

'Jeong Du-jeong. If I had said this a few months ago, he would have immediately gone on a rampage and taken military action. It seems the situation is worse than I thought.'

The fact that he's coming out so humble means he wants something.

Borrowing troops for internal suppression? Even so, this was impossible. The moment Jeong Du-jeong borrows the power of foreign forces for internal affairs, his downfall is confirmed. Not that I would send troops to China anyway.

'He wouldn't be swallowing his pride now to ask me for youth, would he.'

If bioengineering technology develops, he can rejuvenate without my abilities. China, which has a large number of technologists and bionics that can be called technological singularities in themselves, doesn't need to rush, which is Jeong Du-jeong's position.

"Koreans are said to be a quick-quick people. If you want something, say it quickly."

I expected that what Jeong Du-jeong wanted was power armor technology.

"Alright. I'm not one to enjoy small talk either. I want Korea's artificial intelligence technology and data."

,,...Not power armor?"

"Power armor? Ah, that technology is certainly impressive too. I'm really curious about what technology you used to overcome the power source problem. But right now, I'm not focusing on power armor. We already have similar technology."

At the heart of power armor are Galchinium and superconductors. It's hard to make without at least one of them.

'...I used Galchinium openly. China must know of its existence.'

Galchinium leakage is inevitable. It was impossible to manage 100% of the Galchinium used everywhere. There weren't enough people for that.

'Instead, I focused on managing superconductors.'

Even though technologists can't understand the superconductor I made, it's better if its existence itself isn't known.

"Artificial intelligence is unexpected. I did receive information that China is focusing on artificial intelligence... It doesn't seem to be going well?"

"We can create weak artificial intelligence as much as we want."

"What you want isn't weak artificial intelligence, but super artificial intelligence. But you'll have to create strong artificial intelligence before that."

,,...Tsk. So, you're saying you can't give us artificial intelligence technology and data?"

"Currently, Korea is the only country that has successfully created strong artificial intelligence. It's worth a thousand gold."

"Do you think we don't know that that artificial intelligence was created by chance and accident?"

"Isn't that why you're asking for my help because you can't even create that coincidence in China?"

,,......."

Jeong Du-jeong's expression soured.

The fact that he was continuing the conversation showed his desperation.

"What do you want? Technology? Or resources and food? If you want food, I can feed all Koreans to their fill."

When China encountered the players and realized its predetermined demise, the first thing it prepared was food-related technology. It was most afraid of starving people rioting. Thanks to that, no one was starving to death in China now.

"Korea is steadily trending upward. We don't need any technology right now. We have enough resources and food too. At least enough to be self-sufficient."

"So, what do you want?"

"The thirsty dig the well. What can you do for Korea?"

"Is that 'digging a well when thirsty'? I know that Korea has been suffering from the Roachists recently. I'll give you all the information about them. Who the Roachist monarch is, how they control and breed the bugs, and all the genetic information we have."

"There's been a rumor going around that China created the Roachists. Was that true?"

"The Roachists were first discovered in Korea. The Roachist monarch is Korean. That's why we call those bugs Roachists, the name Korea gave them."

,,...Korean?"

I was surprised by the unexpected information.

My interest is piqued. The reason the Roachists attacked Korea might not be simply because China ordered them to, but because of a grudge.

'If it's because of a grudge, it could get troublesome.'

But I put on an indifferent expression.

"What does that matter? Isn't that bug in China now? If you want artificial intelligence technology, put something more valuable on the scale."

,,...I'll give you Awakened research data. In addition, I'll give you the recipes for 40 kinds of drugs that China has."

"Strong artificial intelligence is Korea's present and future technology. You're going to trade it for mere drugs?"

Jeong Du-jeong said after much deliberation.

"I'll give you one of the three gene enhancers that China has. As the name suggests, it can enhance genes. It can only be used once on a living thing, and the downside is that you don't know how it will be enhanced. But if it succeeds, you can gain abilities comparable to an Awakened."

"I know about gene enhancers. An OOPArts. China has three of them? Where did you get them?"

"They appeared under the land and lakes."

I think about how vast China's land is. It's only natural that there are many OOPArts in that vast place.

"Deal. If you send the OOPArts to Seoul by plane, I'll hand over the hard drive containing the strong artificial intelligence technology and data."

"It will arrive by tomorrow morning."

The conversation ended without saying goodbye.

It was a beneficial time.

Lee Yeon-hee, who was outside the camera in the corner, approached.

"Your Excellency. I don't understand why you made a deal with Jeong Du-jeong. In my opinion, Jeong Du-jeong was cornered. Even if you refused the deal, there would have been no retaliation from China. Above all, I don't understand why you're handing over Korea's strong artificial intelligence technology at such a cheap price."

"It's because Jeong Du-jeong is cornered, as you said. If Jeong Du-jeong thought he could change the current situation with artificial intelligence technology... he would have dispatched troops to steal it. It's not time for Korea to fight China yet. And just because they have strong artificial intelligence technology and data, can those bastards create strong artificial intelligence?"

,,...That would be impossible. Jena is a product of chance. Even our technicians couldn't create strong artificial intelligence despite having the data."

"Exactly. There's no way the Chinese bastards can succeed in what we couldn't. Keu keu."

,,...The data we're handing over could be the key to opening China's future. I'm worried about that."

"China's future? I see a torn continent in my eyes."

"It's highly likely, but it's not certain. Is that a prediction based on evidence?"

"Evidence? It's my gut feeling. And the limitations of artificial intelligence are clear."

Unlike other people, I don't have much expectation for artificial intelligence.

Artificial intelligence is just a machine after all. A machine that dies if you just give it a little electric shock.

"Your Excellency is particularly harsh on machines, especially artificial intelligence. Jena would be sad to hear Your Excellency's words."

"Artificial intelligence is sad? Is that a joke?"

Whether it's super artificial intelligence or whatever, it's over with a hacking ttal-kkak as long as it's a machine.

'Ah, that's right. I can't use hacking in this world.'

But it didn't matter. I could easily kill it by letting lightning flow through its body.

"Trust me. The gene enhancer will definitely be helpful."

The gene enhancer was very compatible with my abilities. Even if the enhancement turns out negative, I can use my abilities to revert it and try again.

The next day.

The plane from China delivered the gene enhancer, drug recipes, and Roachist information, and left with the strong artificial intelligence data.

The gene enhancer looked like a cheap toy helmet with wires attached. It consumes 120 kWh per use and overloads, so it can only be used once every 10 hours.

'Was an electric car battery 60 kWh? Anyway, it's not a burden for me right now.'

Overload? It didn't matter to me, who has recovery abilities.

I decided to use it right away.

I connected the gene enhancer wires to the battery and put it on my head.

Na Chae-yeong, Yu Seo-jeong, and Lee Yeon-hee were near me.

Na Chae-yeong was glaring at me with dissatisfied eyes, Yu Seo-jeong was worried but had curious eyes, and Lee Yeon-hee had a smiling face as always.

Ttal-kkak.

I press the switch.

Something like electricity flowed from the head wearing the gene enhancer and stimulated my body with a tingling sensation.

The double helix structure of DNA is visible before my eyes. The DNA shines strongly.

When I came to my senses, I was lying on the floor.

An unpleasant feeling soared from my body.

"What the fuck...."

Flames came out of my mouth along with the curse I spat out.

My lungs were strengthened. I was able to breathe fire from my mouth.

Putting aside that surprise, I couldn't stand the unpleasantness. The feeling of all the strength draining from my body is really not good.

In the end, I couldn't stand it and used recovery.
