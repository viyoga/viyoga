"It's amazing, incomparable to something like a healing ointment! It will be more than a supreme medicine for martial artists!"

"Welcome. As promised yesterday, I brought the elixir and attribute measuring device."

He pointed to the table.

'I have a book on alchemy, but it's different from doing it myself. For now, I'll keep the elixirs....'

"Thank you. You brought them as promised."

"Our Ahyeon Family always keeps our promises. Unlike the Jikga Escort Agency, we value trust."

"I'd like to use the attribute measuring device right here. Is that okay?"

"Of course. May I be next to you? I'll tell you how to use the attribute measuring device."

"Yes. Please."

It's usually polite to leave the room. Because if your attributes are revealed to the enemy, it could become a weakness.

But if it's Shin Gongse, he'll try to be next to me no matter what excuse he makes.

'He probably has people planted everywhere anyway. And attributes aren't absolute.'

"Yujin-ssi, you must hold the attribute measuring device from the bottom up. So that the trigrams drawn in the center can be seen clearly. Ah, keep your head straight. It could be dangerous."

"Like this?"

"Yes. Now, pour your internal energy into the attribute measuring device."

I poured my energy in as he said.

Pajik.

A small lightning bolt flashed from the center of the attribute measuring device.

Immediately after, dozens of blue lightning streaks appeared simultaneously and wriggled.

Pajijijijijijijik!

The attribute measuring device cracked and broke.

"Oh! Yujin-ssi was born with lightning energy!"

"......Yes. There's nothing about other attributes at all."

Innate attributes were also seen as talents.

In other words, I had no talent for other attributes at all.

'Lightning energy must be because of the lightning attribute. If I didn't have lightning, I probably wouldn't have had any attributes.'

I feel bitter for no reason. I was hoping that I would have been born with some other attributes to some extent.

"Don't be disappointed. It's actually amazing. Lightning energy is rare, and it's better to be born with one attribute than to have several mediocre attributes. You can grow faster with a training method that suits your attribute."

"At the same time, the weakness is also clear, though."

Shin Gongse just smiled bitterly and didn't refute my words.

In this world, the weakness of lightning energy is earth energy. If the opponent is born with earth energy and uses martial arts or spells that use earth energy, my lightning will hardly work.

"O-okay."

The wooden board seemed to distort, then turned into undulating soil, and small hills of soil were created. The change didn't end there. The entire attribute measuring device glowed golden, and a flame flared up in the center. Soon everything disappeared, and the attribute measuring device broke.
