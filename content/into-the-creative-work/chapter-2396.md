I grabbed the A-rank dungeon raid reward. It was a small bottle made of bone.

Seeing the uniquely shaped bottle, I narrowed my eyes.

,Could it be the potion I know of?

Even in modern times, recovery potions exist and are considered essential items for Hunters, second only to weapons and armor. The top-tier recovery potions I mainly use are also made by alchemists in the real world.

,Recovery potions can also be obtained in the world of God's Atlantis, but the recovery potions from reality don't lose their effectiveness even when used in other worlds.

The potion-making technology in reality is quite advanced. Of course, recovery potions aren't a panacea. There aren't any significant side effects, but there are limitations.

The effect diminishes when overdosed in a short period. Even the highest-grade recovery potion can't regenerate a severed arm. And the medicinal effect for internal injuries is weaker compared to external wounds.

,Besides recovery potions, there are also doping potions. But they're not as popular as recovery potions.

The biggest reason is the side effects.

Useful doping potions had side effects. Those without side effects weren't very effective. And yet, they were quite expensive. So, most Hunters don't use doping potions.

But there are exceptions.

Doping potions made by alchemists in reality, but doping potions obtained as rewards for raiding dungeons.

The effects are excellent, and there are no side effects.

,This potion is it. The Power of the Earth Dragon.

A doping potion without side effects. Its effect is the best among existing strength-doping potions. The price is about 7 billion won, and it's hard to get even if you have the money.

The amount of strength increase varies depending on the user. When a C-rank Hunter takes it, their strength increases more than sevenfold. For an A-rank Hunter, it's more than double. The effect lasts for exactly one minute.

It's called a potion, but it's more like magic.

,Since I have a potion copier, I got one for free. It's a 1+1 deal that only applies to me.

I'm especially happy because strength-doping potions are very useful to me.

For combat? Of course, that's not bad either. That's what it's originally for.

But the first use that comes to my mind is for the inventory. The conditions for putting things in the inventory: it has to be mine, and I have to be able to lift it.

,Right now, if I use all my mana, I can somehow lift up to 2 tons.

I can easily lift a supercar that weighs a little over 1 ton, so I can store it in my inventory.

,I want to put fighter jets in there. But most of them weigh over 10 tons. Helicopters are better, though.

There are lightweight helicopters under 1 ton, but they don't look cool. What I want is an attack helicopter.

,That famous Apache helicopter weighs 5 tons, right? That's when it's just the frame.

It weighs more when you add weapons and fuel.

,If I take the Power of the Earth Dragon potion, I can put an attack helicopter in my inventory. One minute? I can put in dozens of them.

There were no conditions for taking things out of the inventory. You just have to put them in first.

Of course, buying an attack helicopter is difficult. It's impossible in Korea, at least. The United States is the same.

,Right now, Africa and the Middle East are at war, so it's easy to meet arms dealers or brokers.

Africa is called the kingdom of monsters because there are so many monsters.

In the Middle East, wars between people are bigger than monsters.

,Well, it's easier to get one in the Eternal Eden world than in reality. But... the problem is that you can't bring real-world items into the Eternal Eden world. The opposite is possible, though.

Besides, I was weak in the Eternal Eden world. I probably can't even lift 1 ton. A helicopter would be out of the question.

I was thinking about this and that when Han Ah-young and Ciel approached.

,You worked hard, Yujin. It didn't seem like you needed my help at all? The reward was the Power of the Earth Dragon, right? You're lucky.

,Hoo. It wasn't that difficult.

My hands and feet were trembling a little, but I still had some energy left. Besides, I didn't even use Complete Recovery.

,You worked hard. Seong Yujin-ssi's potential seems sufficient to become an S-rank candidate. I, Ciel Russell, guarantee it in my name and will report it to the World Hunter Association.

Ciel approached.

I paused for a moment, looking at her pink hair. She was flawless. Right now, I don't have a good sense of how to capture her. I also lacked information about her.

,I should postpone capturing her for now.

,Is the S-rank candidate announced immediately?

,No. It will take at least two days. The World Hunter Association Chairman's approval is required. Seong Yujin-ssi, I will guarantee it in my name, so becoming an S-rank candidate is certain.

,I'd prefer it not to be announced on a large scale, though.

,Why?

,It attracts too much attention. It's annoying if I attract too much attention every time I go to the convenience store.

Han Ah-young nodded next to me. She was reminded of her days as an S-rank candidate. Back then, her popularity was higher than that of a trending celebrity.

,That's right. I had a hard time because of that. Broadcasting stations contacted me almost every day, other large guilds made various offers, and special agents from other countries approached me. Even ordinary people recognized me, so it was hard to go to the convenience store.

I have to go on dates with Han Ha-rin and other women, but ordinary people stick to me asking for autographs? Just imagining it was annoying.

,I understand. I will suggest that to the World Hunter Association. Still, everyone who needs to know, such as the Korean Hunter Association, will know.

,Of course, they should.

That way, I can receive national support as an S-rank candidate.

,If you become an S-rank candidate, the country and the association will support you in various ways. I was able to get stronger quickly thanks to that. But... don't just accept it because they give it to you. All of that becomes debt.

Han Ah-young said with a bitter smile. The reason she works so hard is partly because of that. Because she received a lot of support from the association.

,Yeah. Nuna. Thanks for the advice.

Of course, I have no intention of blocking what's coming. Support? I'll take it all. And when I become an S-rank... I can pretend I don't know.

,I'm an S-rank, what are they gonna do?

I just need to suck up their support.

We left the dungeon. We parted ways with Ciel. She had to report to the World Hunter Association.

,Nuna. Is Ciel-ssi high-ranking in the World Hunter Association?

,Maybe? She doesn't tell me the details, but... she seemed to have quite a bit of authority within the World Hunter Association.

The Absolute Hypnosis Sticker came to mind. If I could hypnotize her with the Absolute Hypnosis Sticker like Baek Ji-eun, wouldn't it be easier in many ways? I could also extract internal information from the World Hunter Association.

,I don't have the 3,000 points to buy the Absolute Hypnosis Sticker right now.

Afterward, I went to Han Ha-rin's house with Han Ah-young. I enjoyed 3P with Black and White Double Treasure after a long time.

,Well, I just need to recharge it.

,Please. It's a big deal if the lab equipment stops working even once. There's an emergency battery, but it's better to prevent any possible accidents.

I headed to the electrical room on the third floor. I headed to the electrical room on the third floor. I put my hand on the battery and sent electricity through it. It was important to maintain a constant voltage. If I raised the voltage too high, the battery would explode.

One of the canine mutants. It has the form of a dog, but its skin is red, and it's faster and stronger than a normal dog. It's extremely aggressive and more dangerous than a wolf. Surprisingly, it doesn't have a good sense of smell, but it has excellent hearing.

-The leader seems to be a variant. The Holy Guard can handle normal Mew Hounds. But... the variant is still too much. Because we don't have enough weapons. I need your strength.
