Terius charged forward, holding his wooden shield in front of him.

A dull thud echoed with the impact against the shield. An arrow fired from the front had struck the wooden shield. Terius gritted his teeth. He didn't stop advancing.

"Keok!"

An arrow pierced the neck of an ally beside him. It was instant death. Even a potion couldn't save him. A quick glance revealed a face he remembered. They weren't close. They'd just crossed paths a few times while on guard duty or eating. He remembered him saying he wanted to survive and return home to his wife.

Terius tightened his grip on the shield. He advanced. His feet stepped on the ground. It was squishy, and he almost slipped. A glance down revealed hell. A bridge made of rocks and human corpses. The once-clean river was now red with blood, and human entrails and eyeballs floated on the surface.

'This is insane.'

He gritted his teeth and continued forward.

After crossing the bridge, a castle wall came into view. It was a towering wall. Allied soldiers placed ladders against the wall and climbed up.

Mabellote's soldiers responded by pouring hot water and sand. They stabbed soldiers with long spears. The enemy calmly responded without panicking. Allied soldiers were dying one by one.

"Get out of the way! I'm going up!"

"Don't get excited! You're done for if you lose your cool here!"

Knights, unable to watch any longer, moved. They climbed the wall and went over the top. The sound of swords clashing rang out. Mabellote's knights were also on the enemy's walls.

Terius grabbed a ladder and started climbing. Normally, he would have waited for the right moment before going up. Protecting his own life was the most important thing to him. He avoided dangerous actions as much as possible.

But from today, it was different.

'I have to kill the enemy and gain merit!'

He needed money. If he had 1 billion Ner, he could save Nephia, who was living as a slave.

Terius had been a slave until a few years ago, so he knew how harsh the life of a slave was. Many of the slaves he saw chose suicide.

'Nephia...! Nephia...!'

Terius, who had climbed up the ladder, swung his sword at an enemy holding a bow. The enemy screamed and fell. Terius climbed on top of him and cut off his nose with a dagger.

One nose.

It was proof that he had killed an enemy soldier. The value of one nose was 150,000 Ner. It was far from enough.

Terius rummaged through the soldier's body. There was a money pouch. There was a necklace with a small jewel embedded in it. As Terius was frantically collecting the corpse's possessions, he saw the words on the enemy soldier's white handkerchief.

To my beloved Daddy, Jenidep. Please come back safe!

"......"

Terius paused. His hands trembled.

"Terius! What the hell are you doing spacing out there?! Do you want to die?!"

A knight of Ansek shouted.

The knight blocked the enemy's sword that was attacking Terius. Terius snapped out of it and stood up with his sword and shield.

"I'm sorry! I'll help right now...."

"Kueok!"

A sword imbued with aura pierced the knight's chest. Terius gripped the sword hilt tightly. A blue aura bloomed on the blade. Terius's sword fell on the enemy's neck. Seogeok. With a chilling sensation, the enemy knight's head fell.

Terius grabbed the knight's head and hung it on his belt. Killing an enemy knight would earn him at least 5 million Ner.

He looked at the dead allied knight. His eyes were not properly closed, and he died with a tragic expression. Terius reached out and closed the knight's eyes. As Terius was about to return to the battlefield, the knight's sword caught his eye.

'...The knight's sword is worth money.'

He was the knight who saved him. It wasn't right to take and sell his sword. It was right to bury the sword with his body when he made a grave for him later.

'Does it even matter when he's already dead.'

After hesitating for a moment, Terius took the knight's sword and ran towards the enemies.

Kukukukukung.

The castle gate opened. Cavalrymen who had been waiting outside the castle charged in.

"Kyahahaha! We won! It's our victory!"

"Loot! It's time to loot!"

"Stop! Soldiers of Baron Prukus! Kyle-nim did not allow looting!"

"Our lord is Eugene Prukus-nim!"

"Keukeu. Do you think we don't know you're trying to stop us so you can loot yourselves? Ah. Don't worry, we won't touch the women. If you mess with the women carelessly.... Damn. Just thinking about it is scary."

"You goddamn bastards! Are you even proud soldiers of Prukus after doing that?!"

"Pride? Does pride feed you? Fuck off!"

Their argument didn't last until the end. They had seized the victory of the war, but the outcome of the war was not yet decided. The enemy was fighting without surrendering.

'Loot...! I have to get loot!'

Terius, who needed money, was enraged as he watched Eugene's soldiers breaking into houses and looting. He sneaked into a house when no one was watching. There was no one there. They had evacuated. He searched the house thoroughly, looking for anything that could be turned into money. There were no expensive items in sight.

'Damn.... I can't collect 1 billion Ner even after several years with this....'

Terius shook his legs anxiously. Nephia might be suffering at this very moment.

'Nephia....'

A method flashed through his mind.

He would kill the lord of this castle, Baron Mabellote, himself. Kyle is fair. He gives rewards that are certain as much as the merit he has accumulated.

'I don't know how much the commander will give... but he'll give at least 100 million? If not, he'll give a reward worth 100 million.'

Terius ran outside.

He ran breathlessly along the path where the cavalrymen had passed.

In the center of the castle, beyond the makeshift wooden fence, Baron Mabellote was making his last stand.

Kyle, unusually, was not stepping forward and was watching the battle from a distance. Terius felt puzzled. If it were Kyle normally, he would have been the first to step forward.

"Commander!"

"...Terius. You've come too."

"Commander. You don't look so good? Are you okay?"

"I'm a little tired, but there's no problem."

"That's a relief."

Terius was about to head to the battlefield, glaring at Baron Mabellote in the center of the enemy, but Kyle grabbed his shoulder.

"Wait. Terius. It's not a place for you to enter. Baron Mabellote will surrender soon."

"Commander.... I need to gain merit."

"...Why suddenly? Weren't you not interested in achievements?"

"I wasn't until now. I need money now.... I need money to save Nephia! I need money to save Nephia!"

"...Nephia. Is she Eugene's slave...."

"I will definitely save Nephia."

Terius, who spoke heavily as if swearing an oath, ran towards the enemy lines. Kyle couldn't stop Terius. His mind was complicated with thoughts about Eugene.

Terius approached Baron Mabellote along with the allies who were rushing at the enemy. The morale of the allies was high. Everyone was glaring at Baron Mabellote and was bloodshot over the achievements. Terius felt like his allies were competitors.

"Kill him!"

"Mabellote Castle will not fall."

"I... I will surrender! Please spare me!"

"This is hopeless...."

"Are you going to let Mabellote be trampled on! Get up and move!"

Shouts rang out from here and there. But Terius couldn't hear them. He focused only on one person, Baron Mabellote.

A gap opened. The knight who was next to the baron collapsed due to physical limitations, opening the way.

'This is dangerous. Baron Mabellote isn't that tired. A more certain opportunity....'

At that time, a piece of wood hanging on Baron Mabellote's waist caught his eye. A wooden carving of a knight riding a horse.

Information he had heard before came to mind. Baron Mabellote had a special artifact that allowed him to summon 100 wooden figurine knights every two days.

An artifact of that level is priceless. 1 billion Ner? It's a joke. He could earn more than 10 billion Ner.

"I won't be defeated like this...! I will definitely call for reinforcements and wipe out the Prukus family!"

Baron Mabellote, who was shouting in frustration, suddenly turned around. He threw himself into the sewer flowing on the ground.

"Baron Mabellote is running away!"

"Catch him! Catch him no matter what!"

"Do you think I'll let you escape after coming this far!"

The allies screamed and jumped into the sewer. But they couldn't move and struggled. It was because the armor they were wearing was heavy.

Terius, who was blinded by achievements, threw his armor on the ground and jumped into the sewer with only a dagger in his mouth. He was confident in his swimming skills. When he was a mercenary's slave, he had to go into lakes or rivers to catch fish to provide meals for the mercenaries.

Baron Mabellote ran away as fast as a fish. Was he hurt earlier? Blood was flowing from his side.

The allies who were chasing Baron Mabellote all gave up and went up to the surface. It was because they were not familiar with the water. Terius chased Baron Mabellote even as he felt his lungs aching.

'Money! I need money! Nephia! I'm coming to see you!'

He came out of Mabellote Castle through the waterway. Baron Mabellote and Terius went up to the surface and exhaled. Baron Mabellote pulled out his sword from the water.

"You chased me? You foolish brat. No, are you a brat because you're foolish."

"Baron Mabellote! I'll take that head!"

Aura formed on Baron Mabellote's sword and Terius's dagger.

"Are you so greedy for this body? But it will be you whose head falls."

Baron Mabellote used mana in the water to gain propulsion and quickly approached Terius. Terius gritted his teeth.

Plum Blossom Falling

Afterimages formed on the dagger, confusing Baron Mabellote. Baron Mabellote's sword narrowly missed, and Terius's dagger dug into Mabellote's neck.

"Geureureureuk!!"

Mabellote, sensing death, did not close his eyes easily. He squeezed out his last strength and swung his sword.

"Kkeuaaaak!"

Terius's right arm was cut off. Terius reached out towards his severed right arm, then paused. It was because Mabellote's corpse was about to be swept away by the current.

"No! Money!"

Terius gave up his right arm and grabbed Mabellote's corpse. His right arm had already been swept far away. Terius gave up on his right arm. Nephia was more important than his right arm.

He dragged Mabellote's corpse to the shore. He cauterized the severed shoulder area with an emergency potion and checked Mabellote's corpse.

'I, I killed Baron Mabellote! I've gained merit with this....'

He took out the wooden carving artifact of a knight riding a horse that was hanging on Mabellote's waist. On closer inspection, there was a crack in the horse part. Still, it wouldn't be a problem to use the power of the artifact.

'......I don't know how to use it, but if I sell this, I can earn a huge amount of money.'

He lay down on the ground with the artifact in his left hand. He was tired. His eyelids were too heavy.

'It's a relief that the severed arm wasn't my left arm. I won't be able to hold a shield anymore... but I can swing a sword with my left hand.'

He was ambidextrous.

Terius raised his left arm. The iron ring on his ring finger sparkled in the sunlight. It was fortunate that he was wearing the iron ring on his left hand.

'Nephia....'

Nephia's face came to mind. A young face. How has she changed now? She must have grown up beautifully.

Terius thought of Nephia every day. Even when he was being abused as a mercenary's slave, he looked at the iron ring and thought of Nephia, enduring day by day. He trained with the determination to meet Nephia, and he will soon meet Nephia.

'I'll save you. Nephia.'
