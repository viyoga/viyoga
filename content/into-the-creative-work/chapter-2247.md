[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts 1 minute.]

As soon as I activated Heavenly Heart, my body felt lighter. The curse that had been negatively affecting my body vanished instantly.

Rezo's pupils are shaking like crazy. He realized that the curse on me had disappeared.

,The curse is all gone? What the hell did you do? I've never heard of such an ability."

,The world is wider than you think."

I close the distance with Rezo. Rezo mutters something, moving his lips. It sounds like an African language, but I can't understand it. The moment his muttering ends, an ominous purple energy strikes my body.

Unlike the curses that secretly clung to my body, this curse was coming at me head-on. I briefly debated whether to dodge it or not. Just because I used Heavenly Heart doesn't mean I'm invincible. It only makes me immune to status ailments. If that's just a curse with the power to harm me, Heavenly Heart's effect won't work, and I'll have to take it.

I could use an Instant (刹那) to avoid the curse attack, but I'd lose the chance to strike. I hesitated, then chose to go forward. I have to press the attack now, while he hasn't fully realized my condition. The Heavenly Heart lasts exactly 60 seconds. I can't afford to waste a single second.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 10]

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 9]

Forward.

I break through the purple energy. The curse tries to cling to my body but slips past and disappears. It was a status ailment-type curse. Rezo quickly raises his weapon to block my attack.

,The curse doesn't work?!"

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 4]

Ignoring his surprise, I swing my sword and knife, pushing him relentlessly. Each swing of the knife flashes with lightning, illuminating the surroundings.

Rezo somehow manages to block my attacks... but his weaknesses are clear as day to me.

He said he learned shamanism? In the end, he's fighting by channeling spirits. Whether it's a god or a minor ghost, that's secondary... Just looking at his pure martial arts skills, he's not that strong.

While exchanging blows, I deliberately show a natural opening. As if waiting for it, a counterattack comes. I barely dodge the incoming sword and counterattack, slashing Hwaryeon Bido across Rezo's shoulder.

Hwaryeon Bido bounced off Rezo's shoulder.

I cut. Not Rezo's shoulder, but something else.

,He said he covered his body with curses? Dozens of curses are protecting him. I need to deal with that invisible curse armor first.'

There's no clever trick. The only way is to keep attacking and destroying it. Divine power doesn't work either. Rezo's curse is on a different level from being simply impure.

I continue the offensive. And finally. The tip of Hwaryeon Bido cuts Rezo's skin. Leaving a diagonal gash on his upper body. The wound is shallow, but the curse armor is breached.

Rezo, startled, loses his balance. It's his fatal mistake.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 3]

,This is the end.'

It was the moment I was about to slash his neck with Hwaryeon Bido. Rezo's body disappeared. All that was left in its place was a straw doll. I quickly turned my head. Because I had my senses spread out, I was able to find Rezo, who had appeared a short distance away.

,That's a high-level switcheroo. Why don't you use that ability to run far away?"

,Unfortunately, it's a trick that can only be done in this place..."

I was about to rush at the wounded Rezo but stopped. My legs won't move. Looking down, I saw a semi-transparent nail embedded in my instep.

,The curse is finally working. It seems that the previous state had its limits. About a minute, I guess. You can't use such a dangerous ability in succession."

Heavenly Heart's effect has ended. I inflicted a fatal wound on him, but I couldn't finish him off. How can I get rid of the nail curse embedded in my instep? I thought briefly and then unleashed lightning.

Pajijik.

Lightning flows through my leg, weakening the curse. It's effective, but it will take some time for the curse to completely burn away.

Blood is an essential element in curses. Especially the blood of a skilled curse caster and a 영매 (yeongmae, spirit medium) is even more valuable. In this case, even if I have to suffer a loss, I will crush you with curses.

Rezo smears the blood flowing from his upper body on his hands and scatters it on the floor. It seems like he's scattering it randomly, but strange patterns are drawn on the floor.

,I curse you. Your past, your present, your future. I curse your very existence."

I was suffocating. I felt a pressure as if my whole body was being squeezed. I felt like this world was trying to erase me.

,Arise."

From the east, west, south, and north, centered on me, apparitions appeared. Apparitions wearing voodoo masks. They shake skull-studded wooden staffs, performing a kind of ritual. The pressure on my body intensifies.

Just by looking at it, this is an instant kill. In the end, I'll die from his curse.

,I have Complete Recovery, so I can revive. But... I can't just die without resisting.'

Meteor Sword.

Five Meteor Swords are summoned from the sky and fall towards Rezo and the apparitions. Rezo flicks away the Meteor Swords with a gesture, and the apparitions melt away from the mana imbued in the Meteor Swords, but they continue the ritual.

,Meteor Sword.'

Swords wrapped in blue light continue to fall from the sky.

Kwakwakwakwang!

The surroundings are devastated by the countless falling Meteor Swords. The apparitions melt away and disappear. But the Meteor Swords don't reach Rezo.

,I was underestimating him because he's a curse caster... He's more troublesome than I thought. Are all S-ranks like this?'

Rezo's curse ritual continued. I kept pouring Meteor Swords that didn't reach him and thought about what to do next.

Die and revive with Complete Recovery.

Given Rezo's personality, he didn't seem like he would let his guard down. Pretending to be dead wouldn't work either. The only fortunate thing was that his complexion had been rapidly deteriorating since earlier. Rezo was also exhausted.

Kwang! Kwawaaaang! Kwangkwangkwang!

The Meteor Swords embedded in the ground explode, overturning the ground. I'm not just pouring Meteor Swords without thinking.

,This space itself is his domain, so it's imbued with curses. I need to mess it up even a little bit to make the next battle easier.'

,You seem curious."

,Huh?"

,Why your attacks don't reach my body. I'll tell you since it's the last time. This is also a curse. A curse that doesn't reach. A curse that wanders forever, the curse of the oasis... A curse made by mixing related curses. Unless it's a ranged attack with special power, it can't reach me."

,That's a damn cheat ability."

,Well. In the end, this is also a curse. There are limits, and a price is required. It's not an ability I can use often. I'll acknowledge you as my rival. So."

He stopped talking. Is he deliberately trying to piss me off? As I felt puzzled, Rezo turned his head sharply to the side. A shockwave erupted from one side and swept through. The aftereffects shattered and dropped Rezo's voodoo mask.

Rezo's face was distorted with anger.

,Lee Yeon-suk...!!"

,You said it's a curse that doesn't reach? It seems it doesn't work on wind."

A middle-aged woman in a suit walked over, rotating her wrist. Behind Lee Yeon-suk was Milyeong, who looked a little tired. As soon as Milyeong saw me, she waved her hand.

,Seobang-nim! Ah, it seems we came at a very dangerous time. You're thankful, right?"

Milyeong came trotting over. The closer she gets, the more the curse disappears, and my body feels more comfortable. Rezo is only glaring at Lee Yeon-suk, not paying attention to this side.

,I have never once forgotten you, the bitch who killed my son, Bian."

,The people who lost their families because of your terrorism probably feel the same way. Seeing you, who killed other people's families, trying to avenge your own family... I can't even laugh. I'll blow your head off and kill you like your son."

,Lee Yeon-suk! Even if it means giving up everything I have, I will kill you!!"

Blood burst from Rezo's body. The blood droplets didn't fall to the ground but stayed in the air, taking the shape of flies. I swallowed hard. I don't know much about curses, but if I get hit by that, I feel like I'll die within 10 seconds. That's how vicious and ominous the energy felt.

,Wow. That's a curse that completely grinds down his own life. It seems he never intended to return alive from the start."

The shapes of the flies seeped back into Rezo's body. In the meantime, dozens of apparitions appeared and attacked Lee Yeon-suk, but none of the apparitions inflicted any meaningful damage on Lee Yeon-suk.

,Barrier Armor. Apparitions can't break through that woman's barrier. Even if it's not in perfect condition, she's already like a walking fortress."

Rezo's body, standing in front of Lee Yeon-suk, swelled up greatly. I knew he was trying to self-destruct. Turning his own body into a living bomb.

,Let's fall into hell together. If it's to kill you... I'll gladly walk into hell!"

,Go to hell alone."

Lee Yeon-suk took a stance and threw a punch. The space in front of the punch seemed to distort, and then an enormous shockwave erupted, sweeping over Rezo's upper body. The shockwave spread out in a fan shape and completely erased the end of the mountain peak.

,A mountain peak flies away with one punch? Is this S-rank? No, it's not an ordinary punch, but it must have involved complex techniques...'

Seeing her casually smoking a cigarette even after blowing away a mountain peak, I definitely feel that she's not ordinary.

Milyeong looked elsewhere and clicked her tongue briefly.

,It seems I'll have to get the last hit after all. Please wait a moment. I'll be back within 5 minutes."

Milyeong jumped over a broken tree and headed somewhere.
