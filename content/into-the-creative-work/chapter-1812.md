,I am Baekran. First-Class Royal Guard."

I widened my eyes at the completely unexpected identity.

Perhaps she thought my reaction was one of disbelief, she took something out of her spatial storage.

A golden, shimmering cloth. It was written 'First-Class Royal Guard' and below it was the Emperor's royal seal. The Emperor's seal shone with a subtle rainbow light. I could feel an unassailable dignity from that seal.

,Royal Guard. The Emperor's right-hand man.'

The Royal Guard was the Emperor's personal guard and could be considered special agents. They were the Emperor's eyes, ears, and sword. It was said that even the officials working in the Imperial family trembled when they saw the Royal Guard because they had the right to inspect.

,First-Class.'

There were Special-Class, First-Class, and Second-Class Royal Guards. Special-Class was the highest, followed by First-Class. I didn't know exactly what position it was, but I could guess that having the right to inspect was an incredibly high position.

,...I didn't know you were a First-Class Royal Guard of the Imperial family."

,Just act as you normally would. That's more comfortable for me. And, as you probably know without me saying it, my identity is a secret."

,Why is a First-Class Royal Guard here?"

,It's because I'm a Royal Guard. Only Special-Class Royal Guards protect His Majesty the Emperor. First-Class and Second-Class work outside the palace when they receive missions."

,Received a mission? What mission?"

,Do you want to know?"

Baekran smiled faintly.

I nodded, feeling uneasy.

,There's been talk recently of impious and wicked martial arts circulating in the world, so I came to investigate. Of course, I'm not the only one who received this mission."

,Impious and wicked martial arts?"

,You probably know it too. The Reverse Demon Divine Art. A demonic art that transforms humans into monsters. I'm investigating that martial art, and the Demonic Cult is one of the subjects of investigation."

,Is the Reverse Demon Divine Art something that warrants His Majesty the Emperor's attention?"

,His Majesty the Emperor values the natural order. Humans should live as humans, and monsters should live as monsters. That's the natural order as His Majesty the Emperor sees it. But the Reverse Demon Divine Art is a demonic art that turns humans into monsters. His Majesty the Emperor is concerned about the Reverse Demon Divine Art."

It seemed like Muk Jilian was fucked. The only good thing was that the Emperor wasn't moving overtly. I could still see a way out.

,The Reverse Demon Divine Art has nothing to do with the Demonic Cult."

,You're not the one to make that judgment, I am."

Even the Demonic Cult would be fucked if it got involved in this. There was no force in this land that could be unscathed after incurring the Emperor's displeasure.

,The Demonic Cult is going to collapse before I even become the Demonic God.'

I was sighing inwardly when Baekran asked me.

,Now it's your turn. What's your identity?"

,I am Yeom Guseok, Lightning Blade of the Annihilation Squad of the Demonic Cult."

Baekran chuckled softly. Then, at some point, her expression completely changed. Her serious gaze was as cold and sharp as frost.

,Are you coming all the way here to spout nonsense? You're underestimating me too much. I know you're not Yeom Guseok. Why did you join the Demonic Cult pretending to be Yeom Guseok? What's your real purpose?"

,...How did you know I wasn't Yeom Guseok?"

,The Imperial family's intelligence network. Do you need more than that?"

,As you say, I am not Yeom Guseok."

I killed the real Yeom Guseok and was pretending to be him. The reason for pretending to be Yeom Guseok? Because of Cheon Yu-un. To receive Cheon Yu-un's support, and to avoid Cheon Yu-un's suspicion.

But I couldn't say that as it was. It would be strange for me to know about Cheon Yu-un. It would only arouse more suspicion if I said it.

,I'd rather tell the truth. Of course, I can't just say it. I have to make it so they can't dig any deeper into my identity.'

I thought for a moment and then opened my mouth.

,I am Seong Yujin, disciple of the Fallen Moon Divine Woman."

,The Fallen Moon Divine Woman?!"

,Do you know her?"

,She's one of the figures of interest that the Imperial family is paying attention to. She hasn't come down from Fallen Moon Mountain, so they're keeping it quiet... because she's a person who will cause a stir in the world the moment she comes down from Fallen Moon Mountain."

,You're watching her?"

,She's a person who is forbidden from being watched by order of His Majesty the Emperor. But news still trickles in. I see. So you're a disciple of that Fallen Moon Divine Woman."

She was nodding in agreement, but there was still suspicion in her eyes.

Still, she seemed more cautious than before, perhaps because she knew about the Fallen Moon Divine Woman.

,I still can't understand. Why did a disciple of the Fallen Moon Divine Woman join the Demonic Cult? Did the Fallen Moon Divine Woman tell you to?"

,My master is laissez-faire, so she doesn't care what I do. I told you before why I joined the Demonic Cult."

,Are you talking about how you joined the Demonic Cult to become the Demonic God? What's the reason for wanting to become the Demonic God?"

,Because the Demonic God is my place. Do you need more reason than that?"

,Hah...."

Baekran laughed as if she was dumbfounded. Then her mood relaxed.

,I understand. I'll believe what you say for now. I don't have time to keep doing this anyway."

She turned around and looked back. A group of people appeared in front of us. The martial artists who had been resting got up from their seats, grabbed their weapons, and waited for our reaction. I got rid of my disguise and went forward.

I examined the enemies. There were three masters of the Fifth Stage of Transcendence. The remaining 40 martial artists stood behind them.

,They're martial artists belonging to the Ma family and the Yun family, who have established themselves in Baeyeong City. Did they join hands with the Yu-un Commerce Guild?'

Thinking about it again, it would have been stranger if the Yu-un Commerce Guild hadn't joined hands with the Yun family, who could be said to be the rulers of Baeyeong City. They couldn't be doing this openly near the city without collusion.

I stepped forward and said.

,Yun family! Do you know what you're doing?!"

A middle-aged man with a beard slowly opened his mouth.

,Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok.... You're not using honorifics. I am Yun Gongseok, the family head of the Yun family, Blazing Moon Slicing Blade. I'm higher in seniority. Show some respect."

,Respect my ass. What you're doing right now is betraying the Divine Church. Do you know that?"

,The Divine Church will judge that you've gone missing."

,Very methodical, I see. By the way, you're the family head of the Yun family.... No wonder people say the Ma family is declining."

,How dare you mention the Ma family...!"

,I'm saying it because it's true."

,How dare you...!"

Yun Gongseok exuded killing intent and pointed the blade of his crescent blade at me. I also drew my sword. Not the broken sword from before, but the Hwaryeon Bido.

,They have the advantage. We only have me and Baekran as masters of the Fifth Stage of Transcendence... but they have three.'

It was fortunate that I killed the four masters of the Fifth Stage of Transcendence before they came. Otherwise, we would have been fighting 7 against 2 instead of 3 against 2.

,That would have been hopeless.'

The reason why Yun Gongseok moved late was obvious. He probably didn't want to reveal his identity as much as possible.

,It's surprising that Hong Ganghu revealed himself so late. Is he unexpectedly high-ranking? And is the guy next to him the head of the Yu-un Commerce Guild?'

He was standing there with a calm face even at this moment. It was very annoying.

,Their martial artists are of higher quality, and we have more than twice as many. We can barely hold out.'

I said to the martial artists following us in a determined voice.

,If we overcome this crisis, we can survive. So don't give up and fight. If you give up, they will kill you without hesitation."

This world values human life less than a medieval fantasy. But there was no one who valued their own life less.

Everyone took a stance in preparation for battle. I could feel the tension of the enemies. Their focus was directed at us. I grinned.

,Seize the initiative. Meteor Sword!'

A giant sword appeared in the sky. The giant sword plunged to the ground like a meteor. The formation of the gathered martial artists collapsed. Yun Gongseok and the three masters of the Fifth Stage of Transcendence noticed and jumped away, but they couldn't completely avoid the shockwave.

,Kill them!!"

I shouted and ran forward. My goal was Yun Gongseok. I would kill this bastard quickly and then deal with Hong Ganghu or the head of the Yu-un Commerce Guild.

,Traitor! I'll kill you in the name of the Demonic God!"

,How dare you utter the name of the Demonic God!"

The fallen Yun Gongseok jumped up from his seat and blocked my sword with his crescent blade. His knees were being crushed. I chuckled as I watched him.

,Your lower body is weak. Don't you know that the lower body is the most basic thing? You need to train more."

,The guy who ambushed me cowardly has a lot to say!"

,The guy who set up a strange trap is making excuses."

Yun Gongseok's face contorted. But he didn't retort. He had nothing to say about the trap himself.

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, Rakshasa Revolving Flash.

The Hwaryeon Bido spun around and around. I used that rotational force to swing my sword quickly. Yun Gongseok moved his long crescent blade slightly to block the sword. The crescent blade was so long that it could cover the gaps even with a slight movement.

,He's blocking very well. Damn it. I feel like he wouldn't have been able to block it if I had used Lightning Heaven Style....'

The Ghost Cutting Sword Technique is not a great martial art. Especially if you have money, you can easily come into contact with the Ghost Cutting Sword Technique. Because of this, battles often become prolonged when you meet a guy who knows the Ghost Cutting Sword Technique well.

,Wait. I revealed my identity to Baekran. That I'm a disciple of the Fallen Moon Divine Woman. Baekran isn't the type to blab... and I was planning to kill everyone else after this was over anyway. That's how I can get all the stuff here.'

I grinned.

Yun Gongseok said sharply, as if he didn't like my expression.

,What's so funny? You've probably realized it by now, but your Ghost Cutting Sword Technique doesn't work on me. You're still clinging to that crappy martial art."

,Really? Should I test whether you've really grasped the Ghost Cutting Sword Technique? Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, Evil Cut!"

Evil Cut is a large slash. When I raised my sword behind me, he grabbed his crescent blade with both hands and took a defensive stance. He would block it and then immediately counterattack.

But my sword was a stab, not a slash. The surprised Yun Gongseok turned his crescent blade to deflect the sword. It was the moment when the sword was about to hit the crescent blade.

Lightning Heaven Style, Lightning Serpent.

Pajijik! The red sword wriggled like a snake, avoiding the crescent blade and crawling towards the bastard's solar plexus.

,Not a chance!!!"

Yun Gongseok shouted, infusing his voice with demonic energy. Demonic King's Roar. It was the demonic version of Lion's Roar.

,Damn it. To be hit by this kind of cheap trick.'

I stepped back a long way. My ears were ringing and my head was dizzy. If I hadn't wrapped my body in internal energy, I would have suffered internal injuries.

Yun Gongseok judged this to be an opportunity and rushed at me. Blood was leaking from his mouth, probably from the aftereffects of the Demonic King's Roar.

It was the moment when I was about to raise the Hwaryeon Bido to prepare for a counterattack.

Kwaaaaaaang!

A huge explosion occurred, sweeping through the entire surrounding area.
