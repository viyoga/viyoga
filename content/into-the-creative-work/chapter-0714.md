Having discovered traces of the strategy team, we moved with renewed vigor. We chased the traces for almost two days without rest. In the process, if we saw any obstructive mechanical monsters, we didn't avoid them but engaged in battle and smashed them to pieces. Even if the battle was tough, we couldn't avoid the traces of the strategy team.

However, after another two days, even our enthusiasm began to wane.

,Haa. I found the traces and moved as quickly as possible, but I still can't see the strategy team.... How far away are we from the strategy team?"

Sara lamented.

,I think something is fundamentally wrong. Is the strategy team really somewhere here underground? Maybe while we were wandering around underground, the strategy team went above ground. We need to consider the possibility that we've crossed paths."

,...."

Flora listened to Han Sohee's opinion with her arms crossed. The fact that she didn't refute meant that Han Sohee's words weren't far-fetched.

,I've been scouting with Moka for almost the whole day, but I can't see the end. Is Mom really here?"

I drove the wedge in.

Flora bit her lip.

,...She might not be here for sure. Since Rayburn is in the strategy team... he might have found some clues and gone above ground. Still, us going above ground isn't a good idea. We haven't even looked around the underground road yet."

,Flora-ssi, do you think you can explore this vast underground road, especially one that's twisted like a maze, in just a few days? I don't think it's possible."

,Sohee is right. Flora. I know the surface is dangerous. But the current situation is too frustrating. I'm not saying we should leave the underground road altogether. But I think we should at least know how the situation on the surface is going. We have Yujin-nim and Moka. We can get information about the surface without having to wander around it."

,...You talk well. Honestly, I still think the strategy team is underground... but your opinions and guesses are valid. Okay. Let's go up to the surface for a bit and scout, and if there's nothing there, let's go back underground."

We moved again. The only difference from before was that we were looking not only for traces of the strategy team but also for an entrance to go above ground.

The entrance was easy to find. We had to deliberately search for the surface, but in the underground road, entrances appeared at regular intervals. We chose a suitable entrance and nervously walked up the stairs leading to the surface. Fighting on the stairs would be the worst, so we prayed that no monsters would appear.

Sara, who was leading the way, suddenly stopped.

,There's an iron door ahead. But this door... it feels kind of luxurious."

We also checked the door.

As she said, there was an iron door ahead. It was over 3M tall, and sharp patterns were embossed on the surface of the iron door. It exuded a luxurious atmosphere.

It was different from the buildings in this dungeon that we had seen so far. The buildings were magnificent, but they didn't have such minor details.

,Is only this door special? Or are other places the same?'

Anyway, what mattered wasn't the door's splendor.

Sara reached out her hands and pushed the door. The door slowly opened.

It was a place full of machines. Lights flickered everywhere, and wires of various thicknesses and lengths were disgustingly gathered. It was chaotic if you thought it was chaotic, and organized if you thought it was organized.

,Wow. Humans. I didn't expect you to come from underground. You guys are lucky?"

A woman's voice was heard. It was a rather cheerful voice.

We looked around to find the owner of the voice. Clearly, the voice was coming from all directions. But no matter where we looked, we couldn't see the owner of the voice.

,Who are you?"

Sara asked, holding her rapier in her hand. She was wary of the invisible enemy.

,I'm Machina. Ah, I know your identities. Flora, Sara, Han Sohee, Seong Yujin. Right?"

Han Sohee moved behind me. Suspicious that she knew our names, she prepared to deploy a barrier.

,You said you're Machina? What's your identity? How do you know about us?"

,Flora asks a lot of questions. I'll answer them one by one. I'm Machina, and my identity is an artificial soul. I know about you because I receive information from the machines."

,Receive information from the machines...? I don't understand. If you have information, why don't you use it? If you had used that information, you could have created a favorable situation!"

,I don't have the authority to do that since I'm exiled in the tower in the first place, and I don't want to help the Machine King."

,...Tower?"

I recalled when I scouted the surface through Moka a few days ago. One of the seven giant buildings in this dungeon was a towering mechanical tower.

,You saw the tower, the castle, and the factory while coming here, right? The factory makes machine monsters, and the castle is a repository for machines. This place, the tower, is where machines are controlled. But the control is held by the Machine King, not me. Now, this place is a prison that confines me. Nothing more, nothing less."

We looked at each other. We didn't understand everything Machina said. But I knew that Machina wasn't hostile. If Machina was an enemy, she would have attacked us right away.

,Machina. Show yourself."

Flora demanded confidently.

,I want to, but... I'm trapped at the top of the tower right now. If you want to see me, come up to the top of the tower."

The lights flickered and guided us to the path leading upwards.

Just as I was about to move my steps, Han Sohee grabbed me.

,Everyone. This might be a trap."

,Sohee. If that Machina artificial intelligence... no, artificial soul, was trying to kill us, wouldn't she have already killed us?"

,Sara is right. ......Even if Sara is wrong, we need to go up and confirm Machina's identity. Maybe Machina is the real Machine King."

,Sohee-nuna. I think it's too late to run away."

I pointed to the entrance behind me with my hand. The iron door leading to the underground road was silently closed. We had two choices. Forcefully open the door and leave, or go up.

•••

,Kkuuk. Kkuk."

Moka went up first. It was reconnaissance. I looked around more carefully than usual.

,There's nothing that looks dangerous. At least not to my eyes."

We cautiously started to go up.

,Flora-ssi. Machina said she receives information from the machines. That means she knows where the strategy team is."

Han Sohee said. Machina also knew information about us. She probably knew almost 100% about the strategy team as well.

,That's right. So the top priority is to get information about the strategy team. And..."

Flora trailed off. It was because Machina might be listening to our conversation. But it wasn't difficult to guess the rest of her words.

If Machina seems dangerous, eliminate her.

,She said she's an artificial soul. Is it a created soul? Or an artificial intelligence? If it's artificial intelligence, there's a high possibility that hacking will work...'

It's ambiguous. I think I'll only know if it works or not if I try.

We arrived at the top floor. There were no more stairs leading upwards. Machines were placed here and there, and in the center was a round core encased in glass.

Wiuuuuung.

The core shone blue, and then a hologram human appeared on top of it. It was a woman with dark blue hair and a white dress. The appearance was quite sexy, but in the end, it was a hologram. An existence that can't be touched.

,Welcome. This is my main body. Pretty, right?"

,Pretty."

I said nonchalantly.

And Flora stepped forward.

,There must be a reason why you called us here?"

,Yes. I want to get out of this place. It's frustrating to be trapped in the tower. I want you guys to help me get out."

,...What are we supposed to believe and help you?"

,I know what you guys want. The strategy team, was it? You're looking for the humans who are fighting the Machine King, right? I know the exact location. If you help me, I'll tell you the exact location of the strategy team. As a bonus, about the Machine King too. Isn't that a huge benefit for you?"

Flora looked around at us once and then said to Machina.

,...Okay. You know what we want. But you'll have to guarantee our safety."

,It's not a dangerous task. The reason I need your help is because I don't have hands and feet. And I have no intention of attacking you. Unlike humans, machines don't lie, so you can believe me."

Machina began to use her, except for me. I was excluded from the work because I was too young to be trustworthy. The work Machina ordered was to touch various parts of the tower. Depending on the task, I often had to go down to the first floor and come back up.

,Sara! Not that wire, the thick wire on the right! Cut that wire! Flora! Don't forget the button you have to press! Sohee is doing well!"

I crossed my arms and stared at the hologram. It was too well-made to be called artificial intelligence.

,What's your real identity? An artificial soul? Is it possible to artificially create a soul?"

,You seem curious about me. To teach you a little, I was created from the Machine King's soul. The Machine King determined that the biggest difference between humans and machines is the soul."

,Who?"

,My and the Machine King's creator. Should I say he was the last human here? The creator is already dead though."

,He must have been a great person to have created an artificial soul."

,That's right. He was a smart person. But hundreds of years after he died, the Machine King decided that I was an unnecessary existence. He judged that having a soul actually reduced efficiency."

,...The Machine King's soul is you, though?"

,Artificial intelligence and artificial souls are clearly different."

,Why didn't the Machine King get rid of you and just lock you up here?"

,The Machine King can't get rid of me. That's how the creator programmed it. Since he can't get rid of me, he locked me up in this tower. I'm going to take revenge on the Machine King when I get out. I'm going to get rid of the Machine King's artificial intelligence and take over his body."

Machina gave her precise instructions while talking to me.

And at some point, with a crash, the glass that had been enclosing Machina's core shattered. Energy began to flow out of the core. Sensing the change, she gathered at the top.

,Ah, ah! This abundant output coming into my body...! How long has it been?!"

I stared intently at Machina's core, which was rejoicing with golden eyes.

,Thank you, humans! As promised, I'll tell you the location of the strategy team. They're in the second machine factory! 120km west of the tower!"

The surrounding machines were torn off and rushed towards Machina's core. They were forming a body with machine parts.

,Han Sohee! Stop her!"

Flora shouted. From the beginning, she had no intention of letting Machina go free. She hadn't heard information about the Machine King, but she had found out the location of the strategy team, so it was time to deal with Machina.

The barrier surrounds Machina's core. But the barrier was easily smashed by the machine parts that flew in from all directions. The beam of light that Sara shot didn't do much damage to the core either.

,I roughly understand."

I chuckled. Inside the core, I saw something familiar. It was a spirit core made of machines.

,Machina. You're a machine spirit?"

,As expected of a spiritist. You finally realized it? Or is it because of those eyes? But even if you realize it, the situation won't change."

,You were planning to deceive and attack us from the beginning. The saying that machines don't lie is a lie."

,I'm the real Machine King. There's no way I'd let the invaders of my country live. You guys were too late to realize it."

I moved leisurely. The moment I saw Machina's core, I knew. That its essence was ultimately a machine. And as a machine, it can't beat me.

[Hacking successful.]

[Machina can be hacked for 15 seconds.]

,It's not even 15 seconds. It's shorter than I thought."

Kung!

All the machine parts and scraps of metal that had been gathering around the core fell to the ground. The core was still floating in the air, but its light had weakened.

,Wh, wh, what is this?! Why are you my master?! What did you do to me?!"

,There's no time, so let's make a contract quickly. There's only one condition for the contract. Absolute obedience. Don't even think about getting away with trying to stab me in the back. I'll use you forever."
