A week has passed since I returned from Japan to Korea.

Jin Se-yeong left Yeongcheon Sword Hall because of the A-class Hunter exam. I was sad that my sex partner disappeared, but it was okay because Han Ha-rin was there.

For a week, I carried out requests from the Hunter Association. To build up my performance faster.

My current skill is that of a C-class Hunter, but my actual Hunter rank is E. I worked hard to achieve at least a D rank.

,But, shit. There are no requests.'

Korea has many Hunters compared to its small land area, so requests are rare.

And of course, the requests I'm talking about are those that I can easily handle at my level. There were quite a few requests that weren't easy.

,Why are there so many D-rank requests that require me to go abroad?'

Korea, with its small land and high population density, is peaceful, but other countries overseas were different. Especially these days, China was going through the worst time.

Dungeons that the Chinese Hunter Association had failed to discover exploded all at once, releasing hundreds of thousands of monsters into China. Fortunately, the dungeons exploded in remote areas outside the city, so there were not many casualties.

Regarding this matter, rumors are circulating on the internet that the Chinese government caused an accident while experimenting with dungeons. The possibility that the Chinese government would do that is quite high. China had no credibility.

,Why does the world seem to be getting more and more fucked up?'

I had no intention of going abroad, so I just thought I'd achieve D-rank a little later.

Overseas travel?

I have the Amusement Life app. Instead of going on an overseas trip, it's better to go into the world of Amusement and enjoy it.

,Among the selectable creations, there are also travel documentaries.'

Well, I wasn't planning on entering the documentary world right now.

I turned on the Amusement Life app and selected a world.

[You have selected God's Atlantis.]

[Starting Amusement.]

•••

District 14, Sand Hell.

It's a desert near Today's City, District 2, which we are currently using as our base. I was walking alone in this place, where desert monsters attack if they get bored.

Unlike its brutal name, Sand Hell, the monsters that appear here are not very strong, so it is one of the routes frequently used by merchants and adventurers.

For reference, I am currently walking through the desert in a 'Cheonma (天魔)' state, wearing black clothes and a clown mask on my face.

I had two reasons for being here.

One of them is because of a request from Peshka, a necromancer who controls the back alleys of Today's City and is also a request broker.

The request was simple. To deal with the bandit group 'Dirt Hands' that appears in this desert.

Originally, Dirt Hands maintained a friendly relationship with the back alleys, but this time, they murdered a dark merchant crossing the desert and looted his goods. If they had returned the goods and compensated for the life of the dark merchant, saying they attacked without knowing, it would have been fine, but this time, Dirt Hands shut their mouths and disappeared into the desert. They crossed the line they had to protect.

,Damn it. You old hag. You should have told me specifically where they are.'

I took out cool bottled water from my inventory and gulped it down. All I can see around me is sand. I have no idea where the Dirt Hands bandits are.

The reason I accepted this annoying request was to also grant the request of Kang Myungjin, the protagonist of this world, so I took the request as a favor.

,If I handle Peshka's request, I might be able to get into Helten... Damn it. How long do I have to do these annoying requests?'

Helten.

Currently the most famous criminal organization in Atlantis. I'm trying to get in there. I can hide my identity using my unique trait, Deception (SS), and I can gain numerous benefits through Helten.

"Kkeu, kkeukkeueuk, kkeukkeuk."

A bizarre sound was heard. It sounded like someone was holding back tears, or like they were laughing.

I turned my body towards the sound.

Hyenas, similar in size to wolves, were approaching me, prowling. The hyenas with brown fur were a total of 5, and they were surrounding me. They were quite smart for beasts.

"So it was sand hyenas after all."

The expression on my face was boredom. This was the fourth time today I had to deal with these guys.

"Kkeueuk. Kkeuk. Kkeuk."

The sand hyenas made strange noises and rushed towards me. There's only one thing to be careful about when dealing with these guys.

Not getting bitten.

Sand hyenas have such powerful jaws that they can even chew through iron plates. Other than that, they are just fast. But that fast speed doesn't work for me.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Jeokgong (寂空).

A fist wearing a black glove, Bokma Armor, struck the chin of a charging hyena. The hyena's body floated into the air. And a beat later, the hyena's head explodes.

Bang!

Blood and flesh fell on the sand.

「Acquire 15 AP.」

It gave less than Heyder, the monster in the tutorial. These guys are stronger than Heyder, the monster in the tutorial. Nevertheless, the reason Heyder gives a lot of AP is a tutorial-only perk.

"Kkeueuk! Kkeok! Kkeuk!"

The hyenas all rush in at once. I moved my arms and legs, operating Cheonma Shingong numbly.

「Acquire 15 AP.」

「Acquire 15 AP.」

「Acquire 15 AP.」

The remaining one turns its body back instead of rushing at me and runs away as fast as it can.

,Change status window.'

「Changing to Noejeolsa status window.」

Pajijijik.

Lightning gathered in front of my hand and transformed into a swastika shape. I shot lightning at the fleeing hyena.

Kwareureureung!

The blue lightning accurately hit the hyena's body.

「Acquire 15 AP.」

"Where are you trying to run?"

I changed the status window again and started walking through the desert.

•••

While walking through the desert, I discovered an abandoned building. It was a stone building that wouldn't be strange if it collapsed right away, but it was quite large. It was spacious enough to accommodate over 100 people.

,This could be a place where the Dirt Hands bandits have set up their base.'

According to Peshka, the Dirt Hands bandits number about 30. If I fought them head-on, even I couldn't guarantee victory. So, I'm planning to ambush and kill the strong ones first.

,Deception.'

I use my unique trait, Deception (SS), to hide my appearance and presence.

However, since this is a desert, my footprints remained on the sand. I used Deception again to hide my footprints.

,...This is a bit inefficient, but I can't help it. There's no other way.'

It's better than getting caught.

I approached the building at a fast pace. Instead of entering through the front, I entered the interior through a small hole in the corner.

But there were no Dirt Hands bandits inside the building. I had overreacted.

,...I thought it was their base, but it wasn't.'

It did feel like the bandit's hideout was too obvious.

,Now that it's come to this, I'll rest here for a bit... Huh?'

I sensed a presence in the corner of the building. I tensed my body and hid behind a pillar, looking towards the corner.

There were 3 camels and 4 men. The 3 camels had luggage on their backs. The 4 men also had heavy bags hanging on them if you looked closely.

,They're merchants.'

「The King of Demonic Heavens shines his eyes.」

「The King of Demonic Heavens proposes a mission to you.」

「Mission Condition 1. Kill all the men and loot their luggage.」

「Mission Time Limit: 10 minutes」

「Mission Success Reward: 10,000 AP」

「Mission Failure Penalty: None」

「Would you like to accept the mission?」

I frowned.

The King of Demonic Heavens issues missions whenever he gets the chance. Most of them are missions to kill people. The reason for the mission? There's no special reason. The King of Demonic Heavens just likes me killing someone.

,I refuse.'

「Refusing the mission.」

I rejected most of the King of Demonic Heavens' missions.

It's a pretty good reward for just killing a few people, but I didn't want to be dragged around by the King of Demonic Heavens.

「The King of Demonic Heavens is disappointed.」

,I'll just leave those guys alone and stay here... No. Wait a minute.'

I looked at the merchants again.

Only 2 people are wearing swords on their waists. Their skills seem worse than mine. If I step in, I was confident I could subdue them in 30 seconds.

,They're perfect to use as bait, aren't they?'

3 camels. The luggage was a lot for just 4 people. Also, they don't seem to be in a dangerous position, so they are a target that the Dirt Hands bandits would drool over.

,Okay. Let's secretly follow these guys.'

It would be a better method than wandering around the desert aimlessly looking for the Dirt Hands bandits' hideout.

"But do you think that rumor is true?"

"That rumor?"

The merchants sitting on the floor were resting and talking. Listening to their conversation, it seemed they had met by chance while traveling and were moving together only this time.

"About the Eustia Empire. I heard they're opening up the Empire this time... Do you think it's true?"

"Ah... that rumor. Well, wouldn't it be true? That Eustia Empire is also feeling its limits now."

Eustia Empire.

The only empire that exists on the Atlantis continent.

The Eustia Empire controls over 700 districts.

"The reason they're opening up the Empire this time is... a plan by the princes to take the throne...? Or so I heard."

"A plan to gather forces and take the throne? Well, it's a pretty plausible rumor for a rumor. But what does that have to do with us?"

"No. No. Think about it. The fact that the Empire is open means that entry is freer than before. We shouldn't miss this opportunity and invest all our assets in trade. If that trade is successful, becoming rich is guaranteed!"

"Well... it would be like that if you succeeded about 7 times. If you succeed, that is. But do you think people like us can do it? Do you know how far the distance from here to the Empire is? You have to allow at least a month, and we don't know which roads are safe. The probability of failure is greater than success."

"Oh, come on. Why are you so negative? Of course, you won't be able to succeed alone. But what if it's not just one person but several? Gather dozens of merchants and move to the Empire together. Then the probability of success will naturally increase."

"Wouldn't the profits you can get be smaller if you do that?"

"Ehey. Of course, you have to think about what comes next. You attach yourself to one of the three princes. If not, you have to attach yourself to Empire Ogong."

Empire Ogong

The five pillars that support the Empire. Even the Emperor of the Empire cannot treat the five dukes carelessly. That's how powerful the Empire Ogong's power is.

"Well. What would they see in us to trust us?"

"You have to make them trust you."

"You're good at everything, but you seem to underestimate the world too much. My advice might sound presumptuous, but knowing your place is the secret to living a long life."

"Don't you have any ambition to become the best merchant?"

"Of course I want to be. But I know myself well. I'm not that kind of talent."

"...How boring."

"I quite like this boring life."

I was lost in thought while listening to the merchants' conversation.

The opening of the Eustia Empire.

That means actively interacting with people inside and outside the Empire. It also means giving people other than Empire citizens the opportunity to become nobles of the Empire.

,It's just like the original.'

But right now, it's an irrelevant story to me.

The merchants continued to talk afterwards, but soon closed their mouths and began to focus on resting. They checked the condition of the camels, examined the condition of the luggage, and took naps.

About 30 minutes passed like that.

"Hahaha. Who gave you permission to rest here?"

More than 10 bandits appeared. The bandits' clothes had a hand drawing with only 3 fingers. It is the emblem of the Dirt Hands bandits.

A smile spread across my face as I hid behind the pillar.

,I'm lucky! I can catch a few of these bastards and interrogate them to find out where their hideout is.'

And if I go to their hideout and deal with the remaining Dirt Hands bandits, the request will be over.

"B, bandits?!"

The merchants stood up. The two who were wearing swords took out their swords, and the other two took out daggers from their pockets and held them in their hands. The 3 camels stood there dumbfounded, not understanding the situation.

"Hahaha. Yes, we are the Dirt Hands bandits who rule this desert!"

My eyes lit up at the voice of the bandit who was acting like the leader. It was quite a hoarse voice, but it was clearly a woman's voice.
