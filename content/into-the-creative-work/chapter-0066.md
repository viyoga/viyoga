After the Halloween passenger ship incident, the influence of the Korean Black Cross Society plummeted.

Until then, the Korean Black Cross Society had received consideration and support from the South Korean government. This is because the Black Cross Society is supported by the United Nations under the cause of hunting vampires for humanity.

In fact, the Korean Black Cross Society had achieved considerable merit. They had been steadily hunting vampires in Korea for a long time, protecting the Korean nights. Until recently, that is.

,...This time, trust in the Korean Black Cross Society has collapsed.'

Director Lee Mun-hyun gnashed his teeth. Previously, he could contact the President directly. But now, he has to contact the President through the Chief of Staff of the Blue House. This alone made him realize the decline in the influence of the Korean Black Cross Society.

Currently, the Korean government is deeply concerned about Jeok-gwang. Jeok-gwang must be killed at all costs. Jeok-gwang, who disrupts the laws and order of existing society, is very dangerous.

The problem is how to kill Jeok-gwang. The Black Cross Society, which can be said to be a specialist in vampire hunting, has failed. Also, they realized this time that it is difficult to catch Jeok-gwang with the power of the police alone.

However, they couldn't mobilize the military to catch just one Jeok-gwang. The people of South Korea would definitely react sensitively to the movement of the armed forces. Furthermore, mobilizing the military because of just one Jeok-gwang is a humiliation and insult to South Korea.

,The Korean government can't move actively. All they can do is move the police.'

This is because public opinion in South Korea supports Jeok-gwang.

Jeok-gwang is a dark hero with superpowers.

This was the image of Jeok-gwang that the people think of these days. Of course, there are many people who criticize Jeok-gwang. It is also true that the vampire Hunter, who pretended to be a police special forces, was defeated by Jeok-gwang and annihilated. However, the fierce support of young people for Jeok-gwang buries their criticism.

At the same time, the incompetence of the police was highlighted in this Halloween passenger ship incident.

The fact that Chinese human traffickers are openly operating in Korea, but they didn't even notice, let alone investigate.

The distrust of the police, which had been built up little by little due to Kim Chun-seok's sex bribery, etc., has now exploded at once.

,The British and American Black Cross Societies refused the request for support.'

The superficial reason is to deal with the vampires in their own country.

,...They also know. That Jeok-gwang is different from ordinary vampire progenitors.'

They know the danger of Jeok-gwang. No matter how much they move the vampire Hunter clumsily, they are no match for Jeok-gwang.

,In the end, it's someone else's business. You goddamn bastards.'

Jeok-gwang is only active in Korea. He doesn't even show any signs of going to other countries. In the end, the foreign Black Cross Society decided to leave Jeok-gwang alone.

,It's the same even if I contact the Black Cross Society headquarters.'

All that came back was that they didn't have the resources to support him.

,Did they think that Jeok-gwang would stay in Korea forever? You morons.'

Lee Mun-hyun tried to maintain his composure as much as possible while walking along the mountain path. The person he is trying to meet now is one of the people he respects. No matter how bad the situation is, he didn't want to show him his ugly side.

At the end of the mountain path was a temple.

Hudan Temple.

It is a temple located deep in a mountain in Gangwon-do. However, there are no monks in this temple. He passed away 10 years ago. Currently, the man living in this temple is the monk's teacher and friend.

Lee Mun-hyun took a breath and stepped into Hudan Temple.

,...Every time I come here, it feels like I've come to another world.'

Hudan Temple was quiet. Even the sound of insects cannot be heard. Only the moonlight from the sky illuminates the inside of Hudan Temple. At first glance, it looks like an abandoned house with no one in it.

Lee Mun-hyun stopped for a moment and moved his steps towards the garden, not the house. It is where the gazebo is.

A man in his early to mid-20s was sitting quietly on an old gazebo. The man in the black Dopo was just staring silently into the air. Next to him was a Hwando in a scabbard.

Lee Mun-hyun deliberately made a sound and approached the gazebo where the man was sitting.

,It's been a while, Elder Baek.'

Baek Sang-gi slowly turned his head towards Lee Mun-hyun. Lee Mun-hyun quickly lowered his head to avoid his eyes.

Lee Mun-hyun had always felt a chill whenever he looked into Baek Sang-gi's eyes. His eyes seemed to be the eyes of something other than a human.

,It's Mun-hyun. It's been a while. Your face has aged. How many years has it been?"

,It's been 8 years, Elder Baek.'

,...Hmm. Has it already been 8 years? Since I'm not doing anything, I don't even know how time is passing."

Baek Sang-gi's tone was soft. However, if you listen closely, you can tell that his voice is very dry.

Baek Sang-gi.

A man who has lived for 300 years.

He accidentally ate an artifact called the Bead of Life 300 years ago, and he has lived to this day without aging. He is a legendary vampire Hunter who was called an exorcist or a Taoist hermit and hunted down vampires in Joseon as he saw them.

However, he retired 30 years ago and has been living in the mountains.

Lee Mun-hyun had no idea what he was thinking. No, before that, can a human who has lived for 300 years be called a human?

,...I need Elder's help.'

,My help? You guys are doing a good job of hunting vampires, so you don't necessarily need me to step in."

,...The vampire Hunters in Korea have been annihilated.'

,Annihilated? That's surprising news."

Baek Sang-gi's voice remained unchanged.

Is he really surprised? Lee Mun-hyun tried to erase the rising question.

,It's a vampire progenitor named Jeok-gwang. We couldn't handle one of them. I need Elder's help.'

,Wouldn't it be better to ask for help from the court... no, the government?"

,The relationship between the South Korean government and the Black Cross Society has broken down. The foreign Black Cross Society does not have the resources to support us. All we have left is Elder."

,...That Jeok-gwang guy is definitely not ordinary.'

,If Elder doesn't step in... he will dominate Seoul... no, the Korean night. We must hunt him down as soon as possible."

,...."

Baek Sang-gi looked at the night sky and pondered for a moment before opening his mouth.

,What kind of guy is Jeok-gwang?"

,I have a video. Please watch it and make a decision."

Lee Mun-hyun took out his smartphone, played the video, and handed it to him. These are the videos he has watched to death to study Jeok-gwang.

Baek Sang-gi picked up his smartphone with an awkward gesture. Baek Sang-gi also had a smartphone, but he had not gotten used to it even after several years. The smartphone was just a gift and he didn't use it often.

,Hoo."

Light flickered in Baek Sang-gi's eyes as he watched Jeok-gwang's battle video. It was different from the eyes that had been looking at everything in vain before. They are human-like eyes.

,This guy, his name is Jeok-gwang? He's an interesting guy. Putting aside the ridiculous clown mask... his swordsmanship is not ordinary."

,The superpowers he has are also strange. We suspect that he is the first progenitor to have more than two superpowers."

,Is that so?"

Baek Sang-gi answered roughly. He was engrossed in watching Jeok-gwang's battle video.

,...Are there any other videos?"

,Yes, there are.'

,Show them to me. I'm interested in him."

Lee Mun-hyun played the video as he wanted. It was a good thing for Lee Mun-hyun that Baek Sang-gi was interested.

,Using a sword instead of a gun in this day and age.... I want to share a sword with him once."

Baek Sang-gi asked Lee Mun-hyun to rewind Jeok-gwang's battle video several times.

Then, Baek Sang-gi asked Lee Mun-hyun with a question.

,Is this guy really a progenitor?"

,Pardon?"

,I have cut down thousands of vampires so far. Most of them prefer a combat method that utilizes the vampire's excellent physical abilities. But this guy is different. His physical abilities are excellent, but he relies on skill rather than strength. He fights like a human."

,Isn't that... his taste?"

,That may be the case. But listen. I have seen the swordsmanship that Jeok-gwang uses. No, to be exact, I should say it's similar swordsmanship."

,We also investigated the swordsmanship he uses, but we couldn't figure it out."

The Korean Black Cross Society thoroughly investigated Jeok-gwang. They tried to find out the source of Jeok-gwang's clown mask, the source of his clothes, and even the source of his swordsmanship.

None of them were successful. They don't even know who made the clown mask, and the brand of the clothes he wears doesn't exist. The same goes for swordsmanship. They asked experts in China and Japan beyond Korea, but no one knew.

,That's right. It may be a forgotten swordsmanship now. Mun-hyun-ah, do you know Geomgye?"

,...Isn't it an armed group from the Joseon Dynasty? I also know that it was a vampire hunting group behind it. In a way, they could be our seniors in the Korean Black Cross Society."

Geomgye (劍契) was an armed group from the Joseon Dynasty. According to records, it was not a very reputable group.

,That's right. I was also a member of Geomgye at one time. At that time, I had the opportunity to see the swordsmanship of some of the executives of Geomgye. I thought the lineage would be completely cut off... but it's still there."

,...Don't tell me. Is Jeok-gwang's swordsmanship from Geomgye?"

Baek Sang-gi nodded.

,It's not exactly the same swordsmanship. Maybe it has continued to evolve."

Then, a smile appeared on Baek Sang-gi's lips.

,Geomgye's Salgeom.... This is really, my blood is boiling for the first time in decades."

,Salgeom...."

,Originally, our swordsmanship contains the meaning of combat, self-defense, reason, and mental and physical training. However, Salgeom is a swordsmanship only for killing. The reason contained in that swordsmanship is to kill efficiently."

,...How does it compare to Elder's swordsmanship?"

,It is the opposite of my Seongrigeom (性理劍). Mun-hyun-ah. You can't know which swordsmanship is superior until they clash. I don't think I can win easily if we fight. My swordsmanship... has stalled at some point, just like my aging has stalled.

Lee Mun-hyun knelt down in front of him and lowered his head. The cold air of the dirt floor is felt through his knees.

,Please help me. Elder. Jeok-gwang cannot be stopped without Elder."

,Okay."

Baek Sang-gi said coolly. Lee Mun-hyun was embarrassed by the more neat answer than he thought.

,I was going to refuse until you showed me this guy's video. But, after seeing his swordsmanship, I thought I had to stop him. I won't be able to stop him unless it's me. For the sake of posterity... no, let me be honest. I will kill him for my end."

,The end, you say...."

,I will kill him, and I will also die. I'm glad I have a place to die."

Lee Mun-hyun and Baek Sang-gi's eyes met in the air.

Lee Mun-hyun felt goosebumps on his spine.

,Mun-hyun-ah. Immortality is not a blessing."
