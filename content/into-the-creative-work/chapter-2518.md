I stared blankly at Hyun Jichan. His limbs were severed, and a knife was lodged in his solar plexus. The floor was covered in blood flowing from Hyun Jichan's body.

No human could survive that. He shouldn't be alive. But Hyun Jichan was alive. He was breathing just fine, and his body was even twitching.

Hyun Jichan, who had closed his eyes as if accepting death, slowly opened them. And, flustered, he asked me.

,Why am I not dying? What did you do?

,Ask the little devil inside you.

Honestly, I was nervous. In game terms, it was the point where the devil possessing Hyun Jichan would reveal himself and start round two.

,A devil? You're saying there's a devil inside me?

,The reason you're not dying and are still alive is because of that.

That's when it happened. Hyun Jichan's left eye swirled around and turned gold. The pupil split vertically like that of a lizard.

,No. Participant Hyun Jichan died at this point. If he had committed suicide with a sacred weapon before dying... he might have become the victor.

A cracking voice. It wasn't Hyun Jichan's voice. I gripped the hilt of my sword for no reason.

,A devil?

,Yes. That's right. It's my first time seeing you like this, Participant Seong Yujin. Personally... I'm a fan of Participant Seong Yujin. Participant Seong Yujin doesn't seem human in many ways. As the game organizer, I was a little worried about the game balance... but that's how star players usually are, isn't it? I've been having fun thanks to Participant Seong Yujin.

I twisted the sword hilt with all my might.

,Kkeueueueueuk!

It was Hyun Jichan who was suffering. The devil's eyes were just looking at me as if enjoying it. The devil didn't seem to be doing anything at all.

,You're not going to fight?

,Haha. Why would I fight a participant? This game is about finding and killing the devil, not fighting and winning against the devil. From what I see, you don't seem like you're going to kill me right away... Do you have any questions? I'm in a pretty good mood right now, so I'll answer them.

I didn't really have any.

,Should I just ignore him and go help the other team members?

But I was worried about the devil. I didn't know what he might do while he was out of my sight. The devil bastard might use some strange power to escape.

,Why do you do the Devil's Game? Do you get any benefit from it?

,What is the purpose of a game? To have fun. Yes. The Devil's Game is purely organized for my enjoyment.

,You find this fun? I could understand if you stepped up yourself. All you do is... make rules and watch.

,There are people in the world who enjoy watching gamers play more than playing the game themselves. That's me. Although I'm not a person. To put it simply... it's a matter of taste. Please respect it.

I was secretly relieved. Because I didn't have to fight a super strong devil.

,I heard that the winner of the Grand Game gets a reasonable wish granted. What's the scope of that reasonableness?

,Ah. That's up to me. If I wanted to, I could make Participant Seong Yujin the master of the world. But I won't. That's no fun. The wishes of Participant Seong Yujin and his team members will be granted. If you kill me with Ascalon, that is.

,Hyun Jichan has been quiet since a while ago. Is he dead?

,No. He's alive. He's in a state where his mind is going back and forth from the pain. Well, it's Hyun Jichan's fate, so there's nothing I can do about it. If you kill me now, Mr. Hyun Jichan will be freed from his pain.

Of course, I had no intention of killing the devil right now. Whether Hyun Jichan was suffering or not was none of my business.

Soon, the team members returned.

The first one captured was Park Taesan. Jo Na-young and Bae Hyunsoo caught Park Taesan. Looking at Park Taesan's messed-up face, it seemed like he had been beaten up. When I asked how they caught him, Jo Na-young said she quietly approached and subdued him with her invisibility.

,Yu, Yujin-ah. We're friends. I think I... said some harsh things in the past. I'll apologize. You'll accept it, right?

,Of course, I have to accept it since we're friends.

Park Taesan smiled obsequiously. I didn't feel particularly disappointed. No matter how Park Taesan acted, my decision wouldn't change.

I approached Park Taesan and kicked him in the abdomen.

,Keoheok!

I stomped on the fallen bastard with all my might.

,Since we're friends, I won't kill you right away and will keep you alive as much as possible. And let's get beat up for a bit.

,Kkeuaaak!

While I was beating up Park Taesan, Yujinmyung returned carrying Peng Cheonsu. Peng Cheonsu was in worse shape than Park Taesan. He was dripping blood everywhere. There was a bullet hole in his right arm. He was barely clinging to life, but it looked like he was about to kick the bucket soon.

Shin Dohyun was visible in the back. He was sweating profusely and limping. I could see his thigh, which had been given first aid. The white bandage was stained with blood. There was no way Shin Dohyun would have harmed himself... I immediately understood the situation. In an instant, anger surged to the top of my head.

,This fucking... Peng Cheonsu, that bastard...!

I approached Peng Cheonsu and grabbed his limbs, twisting his joints to smithereens.

,Aaaaak!

Even hearing his screams didn't relieve my anger. I almost killed him, but I held back. Killing Peng Cheonsu like this would be too easy.

I tried to calm my anger and approached Shin Dohyun, who was sitting as if leaning against a pillar.

,Dohyun-ah. Were you shot?

,...I've had first aid, so I can endure it. It's nothing.

He was sweating too much for it to be nothing. I picked up Ascalon.

,Should I kill the devil bastard now?

&gt;

If I killed the devil now and ended the Grand Game, Shin Dohyun's wounds would also disappear.

,Don't. I told you it's nothing. Do it according to the original plan.

,......

,Yujin-ssi. Here's a potion that Peng Cheonsu had. Dohyun-ssi said she wouldn't drink it.

Yujinmyung handed me a small potion. A red potion that wasn't even a mouthful. I belatedly remembered that Peng Cheonsu's game tool was a potion that could only be used in the game.

,I don't need it. I can endure this much. It's not like a bullet is lodged in my thigh. Use the potion when it's really dangerous.

,Now is the time to use it.

,Eueup?!

I forcibly grabbed Shin Dohyun's cheeks and put the potion in her mouth.

,You know you'll only be wasting the potion if you spit it out, right? Drink it all.

,......

Shin Dohyun, whose cheeks were grabbed, glared at me as if she would kill me. It had been quite a while since I received this cold gaze from her. It felt strangely good.

Gulp.

Shin Dohyun, whose complexion had improved, got up from her seat without a care. Her wounds had healed in an instant.

,Peng Cheonsu. You fucking bastard. Don't you die.

,......

Peng Cheonsu tried to commit suicide by burying his only movable head in the ground, but Yujinmyung rushed in and stopped him. Yujinmyung carefully gave first aid to Peng Cheonsu's wounds.

And time passed.

We decided to hold out as much as possible as planned. That way, the other participants would die. The team members took turns standing guard, and if there were any approaching participants, they shot them to death with guns.

Yujinmyung interrogated the devil possessing Hyun Jichan. The devil never lied even once.

,Are you really a devil?

,Well. It depends on what you call me. Most people call me a devil.

,If I consider you a god, are you a god?

,If you want a god, I'll become a god. But I don't offer anything else.

In reality, they weren't getting any meaningful information from the devil.

Whenever I was bored, I beat up Park Taesan. And when I had time, I cuddled with Shin Dohyun in the corner. I could feel Jo Na-young's gaze, but... Shin Dohyun was my official girlfriend.

In the middle, Peng Cheonsu faced a near-death experience. This was despite having received first aid.

「Demonic Ion Drink

Completely restores stamina.

30 GP.」

I fed the ion drink to Peng Cheonsu. It seemed like there would be no use for it in the future anyway, and I didn't want to kill Peng Cheonsu easily. It didn't heal Peng Cheonsu's wounds, but it was enough to get him past the near-death experience.

Before midnight, I asked the Advisor.

,How many Grand Game participants are left?

&lt;There are 27 people left in total.&gt;

Excluding the 7 people here, there were 20 people. Quite a few were left. We decided to hold out for a few more days.

At midnight, a potion appeared from Peng Cheonsu's chest. I fed it to Peng Cheonsu. Since Yujinmyung and Shin Dohyun were pointing guns at him, Peng Cheonsu couldn't resist even though he had recovered. I smashed Peng Cheonsu's limbs again.

Since the punching bag Park Taesan looked like he was about to die, I finished him off myself. I kicked his stomach with all my might.

,I, I curse you. Seong Yujin...! You'll fall into hell! You fucking bastard!!

It was funny that Park Taesan mentioned hell. At the same time, I was also curious.

,Advisor. Does hell exist?

&lt;Hell does not exist.&gt;

,It doesn't exist. Just die.

Park Taesan died from the kick.

Next, I tormented Peng Cheonsu. Since Peng Cheonsu had a potion, I tormented him a little more severely. Was it called slow slicing? I used a knife to carve up Peng Cheonsu's limbs. I had suffered a bit because of this bastard, so I couldn't kill him easily.

Above all, what I couldn't forgive was that he shot Dohyun.

Yujinmyung interrogated Peng Cheonsu about that.

,I would like you to tell me about the business you have. Also, about the dark side of South Korea. You don't want to? Yujin-ssi. Please.

,I asked the devil, and he said that if you feed him a potion before his brain completely dies, he can live even if his heart is torn out. Shall we check out how Peng Cheonsu's organs are shaped?

,...I've said everything I know. Please kill me. I beg you.

,Uh. No.

Even Jo Na-young and Bae Hyunsoo, who could be said to have weak mentalities among the team members, didn't pity Peng Cheonsu. That's because Peng Cheonsu was a bastard who committed all sorts of heinous crimes, from drugs to human trafficking.

&lt;There are 6 people left in total.&gt;

3 more days passed, and 6 people were left.

Peng Cheonsu was executed. The only ones who survived were the five of us.

I picked up Ascalon in front of everyone and approached the devil. The devil smiled.

,You have won. You are truly cruel. Congratulations on your victory.

,Just make sure you grant our wishes well.

I struck the devil's neck with Ascalon.

The devil's head floated into the air.

,Yes. Your wishes will be fulfilled.

With the devil's last words, we returned to reality.

And the wishes were fulfilled.
