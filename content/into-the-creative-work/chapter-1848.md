[The game will now end.]

I'm back in reality.

Having become So Cheonma in the [Ascension to Radiance] world, I felt satisfied and went to sit on the sofa. My real goal wasn't some So Cheonma, but Cheonma, the master of the Cheonma Shin-gyo, but that too would naturally fall into my hands.

[Seong Yujin-ssi

Level: 90

Strength: 123 Stamina: 116 Agility: 113 Intelligence: 113 Virility: 123 Mana: 123]

[Available Points: 26,419]

I've gathered quite a lot of points.

Using points was always a matter of deliberation, but this time, I used them without hesitation.

Use 20,000 points to increase the level of Thunderclap (雷電) to Lv.11?

Thunderclap was my bread-and-butter skill. Honestly, Instant and Full Recovery felt more useful, but... those being Master level, I couldn't raise their levels any further.

,Actually, I want to raise the level of Thunderclap Style one more step... but that requires 50,000 points.'

After making Thunderclap level 11, precise control became possible. I could even control minute electromagnetism. What could I do if it went beyond that to level 12? I was looking forward to it.

20,000 points vanished in an instant.

I felt a pang of regret. 20,000 points, I could earn that quickly if I set my mind to it, but paradoxically, it didn't feel like a lot of points. At first, even 1,000 points felt like a lot, but now, even 10,000 points didn't feel like much.

[Thunderclap (雷電) Lv.12]

You can control Thunderclap as you wish. Mana and vitality are consumed when controlling Thunderclap.]

Use 30,000 points to upgrade Thunderclap (Thunder) to Lv.12?

The points required to raise Thunderclap to level 13 were 30,000. It required fewer points than I thought. I thought it would require 50,000 points.

I closed my eyes for a moment. Because I realized what had changed as Thunderclap's level rose.

,I can now give Thunderclap attributes.'

For example, giving Thunderclap a cold attribute, or making flames soar from Thunderclap, or poisoning the opponent who touched Thunderclap.

,...No, I don't think I can do it right now. To give an attribute, I need to know a lot about the attribute. The only thing I can do right now is give Thunderclap the attribute of fire.'

Perhaps because I often used the Black Flame Demonic Dragon of Cheonma Shingong, it was possible to give Thunderclap the attribute of fire. Even if that wasn't the case, it seemed that various things would be possible depending on practice.

,......The problem is that I don't have the talent, so it's difficult to use it right away. Maybe it would be better to train in earnest in the Ascension to Radiance world later.'

I opened my eyes. I didn't use any more points.

,I'm thinking of entering a new world this time, so I might need them right away.'

This time, if possible, modern times. However, I refuse a world that is the same as reality or worse than reality.

The SF world would basically have advanced science and technology, so it wouldn't be bad... but it could be a world that is too developed for me to dominate.

,I wish it was a world where I could feel fun if possible.'

I looked through the list of creations I could choose from. Because my level was 90, it seemed like there were thousands, tens of thousands of creations I could choose from.

,I don't remember what creation world it is just by looking at the title. That means I have to find them one by one.'

It's a bit much to choose a world recklessly, so it seems like it will take a few days to choose.

Now that [Vampire Detective] has failed, no additional information about [Eternal Eden] can be obtained. The important thing is that it is a creation that satisfies the conditions of being able to obtain modern items and satisfying my enjoyment.

[Eternal Eden] is a survival game with Awakeners. Awakeners can use superpowers. It was a kind of skill.

,I don't know if it's good or bad because I haven't played the original game much. It can't be better than Full Recovery, right?'

After much deliberation, I chose 3 traits and skills.

[Absolute Spirit, Thunderclap, Thunderclap Style.]

There were quite a few skills in the original game that manipulated the mind. From simply manipulating the target to stealing the target's memories to extract skills, or even skills that cause mental confusion.

,In the game, you have to control NPCs to create a force, but NPCs sometimes get unheard-of mental illnesses.'

[Eternal Eden] is a survival game, but it was a damn complicated game. At the same time, it's a game that takes a very long time to play, basically over 100 hours. It was a kind of minor game. A game for a small number of enthusiasts. I also got tired of it in the middle and only played it 2-3 times. It's a game that has a name but didn't become popular.

The reason I left out Full Recovery is because of the top-level ability, Recovery, and in the case of Acceleration, it was pushed back by the Thunderclap &amp; Thunderclap Style combo. I can't leave out Absolute Spirit. Just having Absolute Spirit eliminates the need to worry about hypnosis, brainwashing, mental destruction, etc.

I was sitting on a one-room bed. My body suddenly weakened as I came to the [Eternal Eden] world. It was at the level of an ordinary person. I didn't want to move my body for no reason because of that lethargy. And in front of me was a laptop showing quite interesting news.

-We have just received news that the 125th meteorite has been shot down! The United States, China, and Russia's nuclear missiles destroyed the meteorite and saved the world! The Earth is safe!

In this world, a total of 128 meteorites will fall from 2000 to 2020. And I know the timing.

,It's July 1, 2020 now. The 128th meteorite will fall in 30 days. The biggest meteorite in history will fall. Humanity succeeds in destroying the meteorite by using all nuclear missiles to survive the meteorite... but the meteorite falls to the ground. That's the trigger. The D virus will run rampant in 30 days.'

If the original game proceeds as it is, the world will briefly become a zombie apocalypse world and fall into chaos. Awakeners will also appear in 30 days, and there will probably be 300 players.

,I have to catch one of the players. That'll make things easier.'

The problem is that I have no idea who the players are. Besides, I didn't have much information about the original game.

,Let's start by preparing for survival. I'll have to bring food and generators. I can use Thunderclap, so I don't have to worry too much about electricity.'

I can't guarantee how long the zombie apocalypse period will last, but I'll have to endure it for at least a few months.

,Hmm. I want to eat Seoul... is that realistically possible right now?'

I thought about it for a moment. In Korea, Seoul was the best. The population alone is close to ten million. 20% of the population is in Seoul. As such, they will try to protect Seoul at all costs.

,First, I need to know who I am.'

I checked my smartphone, laptop, and email. There was my mother, father, and younger sister. There were a few family photos in the gallery. My mother and younger sister looked ordinary.

,They'll live well on their own.'

I deleted the family photos.

I decided to abandon my family because they didn't seem to be of much help.
