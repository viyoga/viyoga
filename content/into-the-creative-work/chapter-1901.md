The combat machine horse-drawn carriage was getting closer and closer. Things that weren't visible became visible. Not just the machine horses. The carriage itself was also modified for combat.

Neo London's gentlemen and nobles don't like excessive tuning. The purer, the more outstanding, and the closer to the root, the more elegant. That was their basic thought.

People who ride such carriages have many enemies. You can assume they amassed wealth through illegal activities. Like a gang, for example.

The carriage comes to a stop in front of us. The coachman glances around and opens the carriage door.

Cheolkeok!

The carriage's steel door opened, and the first to step out was a maid. A gray-haired maid with a cigarette hanging from her lips. Half of her face was covered in black tattoos. Even though her attitude was rough, the maid uniform she was wearing was neatly arranged. She had a spear strapped to her back.

,Come on out. There are no enemies. It's not a trap either."

The gray-haired maid said cynically, puffing on her cigarette.

Following her, a man with pale blonde hair and unusually long limbs stepped out of the carriage. As soon as his feet touched the ground, he emitted mana. He narrowed his brow, feeling the pulsating mana.

Suddenly probing with mana waves is extremely rude, but the mana wave itself is quite refined. Seeing him handle mana like that, there's a 90% or higher chance he's a wizard.

,I've been waiting for you, Hitech-nim!"

The broker Marick approached the man with a hearty laugh. The man spoke without acknowledging the greeting.

,What are those things?"

,Those things." He was referring to Yuria and me. I calmed the mana that was about to surge. It was obvious that this guy was a nouveau riche doing illegal things. There's no need to react to every single word. I glanced at Yuria. She was just standing behind me with a blank face. But her eyes were much colder than usual.

,Ah, those two are also interested in the factory that's up for sale this time. We decided to tour it together."

,That's the first I've heard of it?"

,I forgot to mention it beforehand."

,......."

The man's face contorted. Marick's actions must be embarrassing even for that man. The man glared at Marick as if he would kill him, but Marick just kept smiling shamelessly.

,There's no connection between that man and Marick. That's a relief, at least."

If there was a connection between those two, coming here would have been a wasted trip.

It was easy to figure out Marick's intentions. He wants the two of us to compete for the factory. That way, he can sell the factory for a higher price.

The man also seemed to have figured out that scheme, as he looked over here. He first looked at me and unleashed a mana wave once again. If the mana wave he unleashed earlier was to announce his presence, this one was for exploration.

,If I get ignored here, he'll come out annoyingly."

It wasn't always wise to endure.

I also unleashed a mana wave. The invisible mana waves collided in the air. My mana wave shattered the man's mana wave and swept past the bodies of the man and the maid.

The gray-haired maid's expression became subtle, and the man frowned deeply. He looked at me, then at Yuria, who was standing one step behind, and then at the carriage I had arrived in.

I could tell that the man's mind was racing. Soon, the man's face became calm. I don't know what he's thinking inside, but at least on the surface, there's no hostility.

He must have realized that I wasn't just some run-of-the-mill wizard, and judging by Yuria's appearance and the Unicorn Corporation's first-class carriage, he must have highly valued my wealth as well.

The man and the maid approached.

,Greetings. Since we've met like this, let's start with introductions. I'm Gillian Hitech."

Gillian extended his hand for a handshake. Frankly, I didn't like it, but in Neo London, those who ignore handshakes are called ruffians. For the sake of my reputation, I can't ignore the handshake. Of course, those who try to play tricks during handshakes are treated as the worst sons of bitches.

,Eugene Meyer. A somewhat unexpected meeting."

,Our Hitech family is in the transportation business. I'm thinking of expanding the business by buying the factory this time. What kind of business are you in?"

,I'm in the food service industry. Have you heard of YuYu Chicken?"

,Ah. That chicken franchise that's been doing well lately? I've met a rising star."

There was a subtle contempt mixed in Gillian's eyes. It was a familiar look. The food service industry wasn't a well-regarded field of business in Neo London.

,Alright, alright, gentlemen. Now that you've exchanged greetings, let's go inside the factory. Time is money. You're not exactly people with a lot of free time, are you?"

Marick interjected. We followed him towards the factory. I could hear the maids talking from behind.

,I've never seen you before. I'm Barbara. As you can see, I specialize in security."

,I'm Yuria Grace. I'm assisting my master."

,Ah, the secretary type? So, what's your grade? I'm a Double."

What she's asking about is the grade of maids and butlers. More precisely, the grade stamped on the certificate obtained after completing maid academy. There are a total of 7 grades, and Double is the second from the bottom. Just because it's the second from the bottom doesn't mean it's something to be ignored. Just having a maid certificate means you can be called an elite.

,...I don't have a certificate."

,Aha."

The gray-haired maid sneered.

,So you're a decorative maid hired for your looks. I bet your main job is moaning on the bed."

,Keuk."

Gillian, who was walking next to me, laughed. Judging by the twitching of his lips, it seems he's trying to hold back his laughter for now.

While feeling the urge to smack the gray-haired maid's head, I thought of Yuria moaning on the bed.

,Come to think of it, she's not wrong. Yuria moans on my bed every night."

I subtly checked Yuria's complexion. I was planning to argue with those guys right away if her expression was bad, but her complexion was actually calm.

,A maid is essentially a servant. A maid's dignity represents the master's dignity. In that sense, you need to cultivate your personality and appearance a bit more."

,What?"

,Kekeke."

I laughed. I laughed on purpose, but I also laughed because it was genuinely funny.

,A woman with nothing but looks."

,A maid is a subordinate. The maid's dignity represents the master's dignity. In that sense, you need to cultivate your personality and appearance a bit more."

I heard giggling. Gillian was laughing. Judging by the fact that he's laughing even though his maid is being insulted, I could tell what kind of personality he has.

,Oh, really? You talk big for someone who doesn't even have a certificate. You're obviously acting up because you trust your master, but you won't last long in this city acting like that."

,Barbara, that's enough. You were rude."

Gillian, having erased his laughter, stepped forward. Barbara frowned deeply.

,Yes, yes. I'm always the bad guy."

,Just because you're a maid doesn't mean you necessarily need a certificate. You didn't have a certificate until three years ago either."

,I was a mercenary back then."

,Right. You were a mercenary back then. What that maid said isn't wrong. If you do something vulgar, I, the master, will also become vulgar, so be careful with your attitude."

,Ah, yes."

Barbara answered as if she was annoyed. Gillian clicked his tongue but didn't say anything more. Just because you're the master doesn't mean you can insult or force the maid. In reality, it's an employment relationship. If you don't like the maid, you can fire her, and if the maid doesn't like the master, she can quit.

I was thinking about the maid certificate as I walked.

,I knew that maid certificates were important in the maid world... I didn't expect Yuria to be so openly ignored just because she doesn't have a certificate."

It wasn't something to just brush off as Barbara having a bad personality. I'm going to climb higher. Naturally, I'll meet the upper class and nobles. Every time, their maids and butlers will subtly ignore Yuria.

In other words, the same thing could keep happening.

,I should tell Yuria to get a certificate."

I was originally planning to send her to maid academy. It was also Yuria's goal. But I was trying to ignore the fact that I would have to part with Yuria. No, after graduating from maid academy, she could leave for another place.

,Hmm. What should I do to keep Yuria by my side? Should I raise her salary? Or...."

One method came to mind.

Pregnancy. Since we're sharing a body every night, if I deliberately try to get her pregnant....

,Is that too much?"

While I was absorbed in thought, we arrived at the factory's main gate. Marick pointed at the factory with one hand and said in an exaggerated voice.

,Now, look at the factory's exterior. Isn't it clean? This factory was built only 3 years ago. And 3 years is practically brand new."

Aside from a bit of accumulated dust, the factory itself was clean. The dust itself can be taken care of by cleaning the factory.

,I don't know what kind of factory you two are planning to run, but the site and area aren't bad. However, it's a bit small for mass production. You could say that it would be packed if you put in a few machines."

Marick spoke about the disadvantages without hesitation. He already knows. That those kinds of disadvantages aren't even disadvantages. Anyone can tell that mass production is difficult just by looking at the size of the factory.

,I can always expand the factory later. The important thing is to get it up and running and produce steadily."

I roughly looked around the outside of the factory. Even looking at it again, the space was a bit lacking.

,Now. The most important part is the inside. Let's go in."

We went inside the factory.

,Wow."

Gillian exclaimed softly. I was just as surprised. That's because there were machines left inside the factory. Moreover, they were in very clean condition.

,Why are the machines still here?"

I asked Marick.
