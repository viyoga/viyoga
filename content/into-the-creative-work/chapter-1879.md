A message came from Na Chae-young.

It said that the rebels-no, all those who started the riot-had been subdued.

,What a bunch of retards.

I had expected a riot to happen. It's not like I haven't experienced this before. I just treated them according to their abilities, but some assholes took it as discrimination. And those who want to get their hands on power by any means.

,Especially those who were already in power can't stand it. Because they think power is originally theirs.

The fact that this place is modern South Korea also plays a part. They already know the taste of freedom. If I oppress them, there will be protests or something.

,The internet and phone lines haven't been cut off yet. Some areas have lost power, but that's only a small part... I can't just execute them recklessly.

Execution is the most effective way to silence the rebels. The problem is that all eyes in the country will be on Seongak Elementary School if I do that.

,I'll have to quietly scold them in the special confinement room.

Also known as the special detention room. Those stupid assholes will regret what they did there. They'll only get out of the confinement room as corpses.

Uuung.

Then another message came from Na Chae-young. It had more detailed information than before. It was closer to a report.

,They tried to enter the main building without being armed?

According to the technicians who were caught and beaten by those bastards, their plan was to occupy the armory on the second floor of the main building first.

,What's going to change if they have weapons? Even if they have weapons, they're just ordinary people.

I think about the level of force in the main building where Na Chae-young is.

First of all, combat drones. They are highly mobile and covered in steel armor. Thanks to Galchinium, the battery performance is good and the armor is thick, so bullets won't penetrate. If the wings are broken, they lose mobility, but they are not easily broken either. The combat drones have combat AI, so Na Chae-young doesn't have to control them directly.

Na Chae-young just has to press a button. Then the combat AI will carry out the battle according to the set mode. This time, she used the suppression mode instead of the kill mode.

,If she had used the kill mode, they would have all been dead.

If they somehow break through the combat drones, they have to deal with the turrets hidden throughout the main building. Machine gun turrets, flame turrets, exploding mines, etc. The main building was perfectly armed in preparation for the worst-case scenario. Only those who live or work in the main building know this fact.

,The technicians knew this, so they didn't cooperate with the rioters.

If I had told people this in advance, the riot might not have happened, but it's better to hide important forces. If rumors spread that there's something valuable, bugs will gather for no reason. I want to hide the existence of technologist Na Chae-young as much as possible.

"Did something happen?"

Gu Tae-hee, who was pedaling his bicycle like me, asked me.

"It's nothing. There was a bit of a riot, but it's been resolved."

"A riot... and you say it's nothing?"

"Because we have Doctor Na."

"I guess she's more capable than I thought."

"She's not just more capable than you think, she's incredibly capable."

Gu Tae-hee was underestimating Na Chae-young. In my opinion, Na Chae-young is stronger than Gu Tae-hee right now. Of course, that's when Na Chae-young is armed with weapons, including combat drones.

"Are you going to use the evolution serum yourself?"

"Why do you think I'm going to use it?"

"Because you could become stronger if you used it."

"You know about the side effects, right?"

"...I don't think you, who overcame the side effects of Bister I, would die from the side effects of the evolution serum."

I felt the same way. Even though the blood vessels in my whole body burst, I was alive thanks to recovery. Even if I fail to evolve with a 50% probability, I don't think I'll die.

"I have no intention of using the evolution serum."

Even if I succeed in evolving, there's little chance it will benefit me. That's because of the two traits I have.

Dunce. A trait that makes it difficult to learn and hard to learn new things. Even if I successfully evolve, it will take quite a long time to use it properly.

And Purity. The efficiency of physical evolution drops by half. These two create synergy, so evolving could actually be a loss.

Need strength? In my case, consistent physical training is the answer. The Purity trait also has a 50% increase in physical training efficiency.

"Well, you're strong even without using it. If you don't plan to use it, sell it to me. I'll give you a good price."

"I might need it someday."

The evolution serum was a precious item. It wasn't something I could easily sell off.

"Have you ever used the evolution serum?"

"...Five of my subordinates used it. Only one of them succeeded in evolving. I don't think the 50% success rate is true."

"What about you?"

"I don't like taking life-threatening gambles."

I thought so.

You can evolve and become stronger with a 50% probability. But if you fail with a 50% probability, you die. Most ordinary people will reject the evolution serum. It's not like you become an invincible superhuman just by evolving.

,If you get a good weapon, you can become stronger even without evolving.

There was no need to risk your life. Unless it's a special situation.

I was about to close my mouth, but then something suddenly came to mind.

"You got at least six evolution serums? How did you get them?"

"Didn't I tell you before? There's Bionics on the government's side."

"You got it through a high-ranking official?"

Gu Tae-hee nodded. That high-ranking official seemed to be in a much higher position than I thought.

"We're almost there. Get ready."

Gu Tae-hee said. Gu Tae-hee's subordinates, who were going ahead, stopped first. They got off their bicycles and started unpacking. They were armor to protect their bodies. It was a bit crude and looked like medieval plate armor. But it was effective in blocking zombie attacks. Zombie teeth can't penetrate steel plates.

I'm impressed by their orderly movements. They felt more like a military unit than a gang.

"What are you doing? You guys get ready too."

I said to the security team. The security team members got off their bicycles, panting. I didn't like that. The gangsters' breathing was stable, but the security team, in name only, was panting except for a few. What's so hard?

The security team wore stab-proof vests and were armed with assault rifles. On the other hand, the gangsters had pistols and maces.

"A mace?"

"When dealing with zombies, blunt weapons are more effective than guns. Even monster spiders can withstand bullets to some extent. I can't be sure because I don't have enough information."

In the case of zombies, they move even if their heads are cut off as long as their hearts are beating. Monster spiders weren't that bad, but they could withstand being pierced by bullets without any problems.

,The security team armed with guns... well, they'll do well.

If they don't, they'll just die. It's not like I'm going to die, so I didn't care. Even if there's a vacancy in the security team, I can just hire more. There are many people at Seongak Elementary School who want to become security guards.

After finishing our preparations, we left our bicycles behind and moved slowly.

"Stop."

I said. Then Gu Tae-hee stopped. Then the gangsters and the security team stopped. They looked at me with puzzled eyes. Instead of answering, I stared at a building.

On the top floor of the building, I can see a monster spider in the window. A spider bigger than a person is staring at us. I knew it instinctively. That must be the queen spider.

Pajijijijijijik!

I created lightning in my right hand. Blue electricity gathered in my right hand. Conversely, my stamina was rapidly draining. Everyone looked at me with surprised eyes, but I ignored them.

I threw the gathered lightning at the queen spider watching from the top floor of the building.

The lightning flew in a straight line, drawing a blue beam, and landed right in front of the queen spider. It discharged while shattering the glass. Blue current spread throughout the upper part of the building and then disappeared.

It would have been easier if the queen spider had been electrocuted and died. But the queen spider was fine. It moved normally and stuck its hideous face out of the broken window and threatened us like a cat hissing.

"I used more than half of my stamina, but it didn't die... It's a tough one. Does it have resistance to electricity?"

I reflexively tried to take out my smartphone, but then I remembered that I had a walkie-talkie in my ear.

"Doctor Na. Are you watching?"

-I'm watching. The camera on your shoulder isn't just for show.

"Send me a present."

I gestured to Park Hae-gil. Park Hae-gil put the large box he was carrying on his back on the ground. When he opened the box, there was a drone inside. It was smaller than the combat drones at Seongak Elementary School, but it was a dangerous drone. That's because it's a drone with explosives built in. It's a so-called suicide drone.

Bbeep!

The suicide drone lit up. Na Chae-young, who wasn't here, started controlling the suicide drone.

Dudududududu!

The suicide drone's wings rotated and its body floated into the air.

Controlling a drone isn't easy, but it doesn't matter to Na Chae-young. The combat AI is doing the detailed operation of the drone, and Na Chae-young is just inputting commands to the AI and guiding the direction.

The drone flew to where the queen spider was. It went right through the window that was broken by the previous attack.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion occurred. Black smoke billowed up. But I clicked my tongue.

"It failed."

If it had succeeded, the explosive power wouldn't have been that low. It wouldn't have been strange if the entire floor had been blown away.

-According to the video shown through the suicide drone... the queen spider shot out silk before it exploded. The explosive power was reduced because of that. That queen spider's spider web seems to have excellent heat resistance. If possible, bring me that queen spider's spider web. I think it will be helpful for research.

"Is the heat resistance of the spider web a characteristic of spider monsters?"

-No, it must be a characteristic of that individual. Just like there are Awakened among people like you, it's not strange for special individuals to appear among other creatures.

I used up half of my stamina with that lightning attack, but I couldn't help it. I couldn't go back just because I was a little tired after coming all this way. That queen spider won't be completely fine either.

"Let's go in."

I said, checking the Bister II in my pocket. It seemed like I would be able to see how effective Bister II was today.
