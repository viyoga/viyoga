Hwang Chiseung had a plan.

Sung Yujin was just a greenhorn in her twenties who'd recently become an S-Rank, but the word "S-Rank" was never something to take lightly. Making an enemy of an S-Rank Hunter was a stupid move. Hwang Chiseung knew there was nothing to gain from it.

'Detailed information on Sung Yujin is managed by the World Hunter Association, so it's hard to come by, but I know the rough details.'

Until recently, Sung Yujin had been a college student. Regardless of that monstrous power and extraordinary talent, she had no social experience.

'First, I'll invoke the power and tradition of the Goryeo Guild to put a damper on Sung Yujin's spirit. Of course, that alone won't be easy. If I use the names of the retired elders to seize the upper hand, she won't be able to run wild.'

Any Hunter worth their salt couldn't help but know the S-Rank Hunters who had protected Korea. Without them, Korea would have fallen long ago.

On top of that, Sung Yujin had learned the Yeongcheon style. She would also know about the five great families that had safeguarded Korea.

'An S-Rank Hunter has the power to turn a city into scorched earth single-handedly, so their ego is equally inflated. It's only natural her pride would be wounded. Here, I take one step back. I apologize first and hand over a gift.'

The gift he'd prepared was a top-grade elixir worth tens of billions.

Tens of billions was just a number—in reality, top-grade elixirs were nearly impossible to obtain even with money. For Sung Yujin, who had just become an S-Rank Hunter, it would be enough to make her eyes roll back.

'Afterward, I maintain a good relationship with Sung Yujin... and at the right time, I propose she transfer guilds. Wasn't Sung Yujin affiliated with the Seventeer Guild? From what I've looked into, unlike Han Ahyoung, she isn't bound to the Seventeer Guild. The Seventeer Guild has no grounds to hold onto Sung Yujin. If she makes up her mind, she can leave the Seventeer Guild whenever she wants.'

The fact that Sung Yujin had only put her name on the Seventeer Guild roster and barely participated in guild activities was, in many ways, a good thing.

An S-Rank Hunter.

Just the title alone carried influence.

'A perfect plan.'

The car arrived at the underground parking lot of the Korean Hunter Association headquarters. Hwang Chiseung stepped out and entered the first-floor lobby with the gift he'd prepared.

The air was heavy.

The atmosphere inside the Association was tense. The Association staff were on edge like hedgehogs with their quills fully raised, wary of everything around them. The staff approached Hwang Chiseung with quick steps, as if they'd been waiting for him.

"Goryeo Guild Master. The Association President, the other Guild Masters, and Hunter Sung Yujin are waiting in the annex training room."

"It would be best if you hurried."

"Only the summoning order's recipient may enter. Your secretary will stay with us."

The staff rattled off their words rapid-fire.

Hwang Chiseung and his secretary furrowed their brows. The staff's attitude felt rude. The usual deference shown to him was absent.

"...I was a bit late to the summons. I had a reason I couldn't avoid being late. I'll discuss this matter directly with the Association President."

He had deliberately been late, all according to plan. But for the sake of his reputation, he had to offer an excuse.

"Secretary Kim. You stay here."

Despite being late, Hwang Chiseung walked leisurely. As the Master of the Goryeo Guild, he could not afford to forget his dignity.

Heading toward the annex, he briefly fell into melancholy.

'Old memories are surfacing. Back then, there was romance. Hunters fought each other at every opportunity, and they released their frustrations through sparring. The Association's annex training room was often used as that sparring ground. I fought a lot with Yoo Hyeongwook, the current Association President, too. The score was in my favor.'

Even after all these years, the Association's training room hadn't changed much.

'Choosing the training room instead of the conference room as the summoning location... It's obvious. To show off power as an S-Rank Hunter.'

Was it the Association President's doing? Or the impudent scheme of the new S-Rank Hunter?

Either way, things wouldn't go as planned. The masters of the top ten guilds were top-tier A-Ranks, even if not S-Ranks. Against a greenhorn who'd just become S-Rank, even if they couldn't win, they could hold out for a long time.

Hwang Chiseung confidently opened the door.

For a moment, he thought time had frozen solid.

The scene inside was not what he had expected.

At the center of the training room stood the fresh-faced young S-Rank Hunter, legs crossed. She wasn't hiding her power at all. The calamity-like aura of an S-Rank Hunter filled the training room.

His breath caught. It felt like being plunged to the depths of the deep sea in an instant.

Before her stood nine familiar faces, backs ramrod straight. They stood at attention like raw recruits drenched in military discipline, eyes cast downward. Some had palm prints on their cheeks and traces of blood at their mouths and noses.

Behind Sung Yujin, the Association President watched with cold sweat dripping down his face. In one corner, a short-haired woman covered in tattoos leaned against the wall, chewing gum and watching as a bystander.

Old memories surfaced.

Decades ago, when he had become a Hunter.

An era when corruption was the norm.

Memories that, with time, had been wrapped in the name of romance.

What lay beneath the peeled wrapping was savagery.

"Ha. Would you look at this? You show up thirty fucking minutes late and you're strutting in like you own the place?"

"Hunter Sung Yujin. This is..."

He couldn't finish his sentence. Sung Yujin moved. It was an incredibly fast speed, befitting an acceleration-ability user. She closed in on him and threw a punch.

Hwang Chiseung, who had once nearly become an S-Rank Hunter candidate, reacted. He extended his hand forward. The essence of martial arts he'd trained for decades was channeled into his palm. He traced a smooth arc, deflecting the fist.

KWAAAAAANG!

It failed.

With a thunderous boom, Hwang Chiseung's hand shattered. The force packed into that fist exceeded the power of any S-Rank Hunter he knew.

Before he could even reset his stance, he was grabbed by the scruff of the neck and slammed into the floor. It had been a long time since he'd kissed the Association training room floor so intimately. He tasted blood. Blood ran from his nose and mouth.

Hwang Chiseung hurriedly tried to raise himself, but Sung Yujin stepped on his back. Bzzt. Blue lightning coursed through him, temporarily electrocuting Hwang Chiseung.

"Why were you late?"

Sung Yujin asked.

Hwang Chiseung assessed the situation with his sharp mind.

He was fucked.

The youngest S-Rank Hunter, whom he'd assumed was a greenhorn due to her lack of experience, turned out to be a lunatic who summoned the top ten guild masters like they were schoolchildren. The problem was that there was no power to stop this lunatic. The retired S-Rank Hunters? This madwoman would throw punches at them without hesitation too.

"That is..."

"Don't give me that bullshit about traffic. You're all A-Rank Hunters too. If you ran here, it wouldn't even take ten minutes."

"There was... an important matter...! It was a critical agenda that required my decision!"

Polite speech spilled from Hwang Chiseung's mouth involuntarily. Because he instinctively knew that if he got cocky here, he'd be truly fucked.

"Ha, fuck."

That wasn't a sigh of being convinced by his excuse. It was a sigh filled with rage and irritation.

The foot pressing on Hwang Chiseung's back pressed down harder. Hwang Chiseung's eyes bulged from the pain of his spine about to snap, and he circulated his mana. If he couldn't hold out with mana, his back would burst.

"Hey. Do you know when a president's power is at its strongest? Right after inauguration. Guys with brains quietly keep their heads down then. Why? Because if you raise your head carelessly, you get cut down by the sword called power. And the one who first grips that sword—how badly do you think they want to swing it around?"

"H-Hunter Sung Yujin. Let's, let's talk this out...!"

"It hasn't been long since I became S-Rank. Honestly, I wanted to flip everything upside down and see just how far an S-Rank Hunter's authority reaches, but I held back. Because there's such a thing as an S-Rank Hunter's dignity. So I was going to have a little chat with the top ten guilds and wrap things up. But you fucking bastards. I call you and you crawl in thirty minutes late? This is fucking. This is you completely shitting on me. An S-Rank Hunter. Am I a joke to you?"

"I never once thought of disregarding you!"

Thwack!

Sung Yujin kicked Hwang Chiseung's leg out from under him. Hwang Chiseung rolled and flew across the training room floor, landing in front of the other top ten guild masters.

Hwang Chiseung's leg was broken. His knee bent the wrong way, and the bone, unable to just fracture, pierced through flesh and jutted out. For a normal person, it would have been a fatal wound that killed them before their mind could even break, but Hwang Chiseung was still cold and composed. As long as he didn't die, he could be healed.

Thud. Thud. Thud.

From far off, Sung Yujin approached, radiating killing intent. Hwang Chiseung looked at the other guild masters with eyes trembling in fear.

'If we combine our strength...!'

They averted their gazes from Hwang Chiseung.

Truthfully, Hwang Chiseung knew too.

That even if everyone here combined their strength, they couldn't stop Sung Yujin.

"W-Wait! Hunter Sung Yujin! I have a gift here! Actually, the reason I was late was to bring this gift!"

Hwang Chiseung frantically opened the box he'd been clutching in his hand and thrust it toward Sung Yujin.

It was a flower.

A flower glowing softly like a firefly.

Moonlight Grass.

A top-grade elixir that any Hunter couldn't help but know.

Crunch.

The elixir worth tens of billions was crushed under Sung Yujin's foot. Blue electricity even sparked, burning the Moonlight Grass pitch black. Tens of billions vanished.

"Ah."

At that sight, Hwang Chiseung let out a sigh without realizing it, then looked at Sung Yujin's face, contorted like a demon, and was horrified.

"You fucking bastard! Do I look like a beggar to you now? You thought bringing me something like this would make me go 'thank you!' and bow my head to you?"

Sung Yujin clenched her fist.

"You're getting the beating of your life today."

Hwang Chiseung was beaten for a full hour. There were quite a few moments where his life was in danger, but thanks to the A-Rank healing ability users permanently stationed at the Hunter Association, the catastrophe of the Goryeo Guild Master dying did not occur.

"It's about time for a meal."

Sung Yujin said brightly in the blood-soaked training room. But no one relaxed.

"From now on, when I call, you come running immediately. If you're even one minute late, I won't let it slide. Ah, I have no intention of joining your guilds, so don't contact me with annoying shit. If something comes up that requires contact, ask permission first before contacting me. Yura. What should we have for dinner?"

"Steak?"

"Not bad."

Sung Yujin left with Yura.

Those left in the training room were silent for a while. A sense of dread washed over them—that Sung Yujin, who had just left, might come back in and start shit again. When one minute passed, then three, then five. Only then did people let out sighs of relief. The tension eased.

"Sung Yujin... That one's a real lunatic."

"The fact that lunatic is Korea's S-Rank Hunter makes Korea's future look bleak."

"How does she compare to Han Ahyoung? If those two fought, who do you think would win?"

"It's hard to judge rashly. Neither of them has ever shown their full power..."

The guild masters left too.

The Association President looked at Hwang Chiseung, collapsed on the floor. He'd been healed, so he could move if he wanted to. The reason Hwang Chiseung didn't stir was likely because his pride had been thoroughly trampled.

The Association President shook his head and quietly departed.

Left alone, tears streamed down Hwang Chiseung's face.

'I can't just let it end like this, just take the beating and be done! That bitch insulted the Goryeo Guild! I'll show her the true might of the Goryeo Guild...!'
