With December approaching, the Academy got busy. The final exams were just around the corner. Plus, the third-year students would graduate at the end of December without a winter break. It was a busy time for everyone, students, teachers, and staff alike.

Of course, I was the exception.

,I'm already beyond the strongest in the grade, I'm the strongest student in the entire Academy.'

No student could keep up with me in the practical exam. As for the written exam, I could just cheat. It didn't matter even if the final exams started right now.

,What I need to prepare for isn't the final exams, but Christmas Eve.'

December 24th. The day the snow-white hell event unfolds.

I looked out the window at the still-falling snow and tore up a space-time scroll.

The place I teleported to was Jinryeong Seongga (眞靈成家).

A prestigious family in Korea that has consistently produced outstanding spiritists. It's a place that Seong Hari and I can call our main family.

I learned spiritism at Jinryeong Seongga as a child. For your information, in this world, spiritism isn't about how to handle spirits, but rather techniques that utilize the power of spirits.

Perhaps because I lacked talent, the only thing I could learn was Spirit Descent, a secret art of Jinryeong Seongga.

,I don't really need spiritism. Spiritism is ultimately just a technique to handle the power of spirits more efficiently and conveniently.'

Even with spiritism, the most important thing is still the power, the essence of the spirit.

I stepped into the territory of Jinryeong Seongga. As soon as I entered the barrier protecting Jinryeong Seongga, I felt the presence of numerous spirits. Most of them were low-level spirits.

As soon as the spirits sensed my presence, they flocked around me. Thanks to my Spirit Eye (S+), I had high spirit affinity. The spirits felt friendly towards me.

-It's Eugene! Eugene-gun is here!

-Long time no see! Long time!

-The Spirit Orb? The Spirit Orb? The Spirit Orb?

Only a few of the swarming low-level spirits spoke human language. These were spirits close to ascending to the intermediate level. For these guys, just one Spirit Orb would be enough to evolve.

I took out a Spirit Orb from my inventory and held it in my hand.

Some impatient spirits rushed towards the Spirit Orb. But they were blocked by other spirits.

-Are you crazy?

-Why are you only thinking about yourself?

-Wanna die?

Being low-level spirits, their desire for power was even stronger.

,Remember, only the strong get everything.'

I threw the Spirit Orb far away, where there were no spirits. The spirits were startled and flew or ran towards the Spirit Orb.

-Get out of the way!

-The Spirit Orb is mine! Touch it and you're dead!

Thanks to that, I was able to enter the family estate in a pleasant state, without any low-level spirits rushing at me.

,You have arrived, Young Master.'

,The Head of the Family will be pleased.'

,Young Master. The Head of the Family is currently away. He will be back before dinner.'

I walked, receiving greetings from the staff who worked at the estate and learned spiritism.

,You rascal. How long has it been since you've returned?!"

The one who scolded me was a small, elderly man in Hanbok with a Taoist air about him. Seong Myeong-saeng. The former Head of the Jinryeong Seongga. He's my great-grandfather. He was also my teacher who taught me Spirit Descent.

,I've been a bit busy. You're still going strong, Grandpa.'

,Look at this guy. Coming back after so long without even a hint of apology. What have you been doing running around outside?"

,You know I enrolled in the Academy.'

,You contracted with a top-tier spirit at the Academy? Not just one, but three?"

I looked at Seong Myeong-saeng with a surprised expression.

,How did you know?"

Even I, with my Spirit Eye, can't tell which spirit someone has contracted with.

,Heh heh. Spiritism.'

,Oh, there's spiritism like that?"

,You rascal! Of course not! And you call yourself the heir of Jinryeong Seongga, not even knowing that!"

Seong Myeong-saeng suddenly exploded and tried to deliver a knuckle sandwich to my head. He even used mana, so it was quite fast. But I'm not one to be caught off guard by such an attack. I stepped aside using footwork.

,...You take after your mother, you're damn good at using your body. But you have no manners. It's only right to humbly accept the teachings of the elders of the family, you rascal.'

,Waaah. I'm gonna tell Mom that Great-Grandpa almost hit me.'

Seong Myeong-saeng's complexion turned pale in an instant.

,Don't do that. I was wrong....'

The strongest in Jinryeong Seongga was Seong Hari. Seong Hari, who never loses when facing spirits, also has a temper that's no joke.

,So, how did you really know?"

,Cheol-woo told me.'

Cheol-woo is the top-tier earth spirit contracted with Seong Myeong-saeng. Of course, I've never met Cheol-woo directly. But I know that Cheol-woo is a spirit who resides in the spirit realm and travels to Earth. In the first place, top-tier spirits can travel to and from the spirit realm.

,...It seems the rumors have spread in the spirit realm.'

,It's unprecedented for a human to contract with three top-tier spirits. It would be stranger if rumors didn't spread.'

,...I guess so.'

,You wouldn't have come to the main family for no reason.... What do you want? Do you need some pocket money?"

,I have several purposes, but if I had to pick the most urgent and important one, it would be to meet the Spirit King.'

,Is it because of Hari?"

,Yes.'

I didn't hide my true feelings.

Currently, Seong Hari's stats are reduced due to the Spirit King's Curse (SS). If the Spirit King's curse is lifted, Seong Hari's power will become enormous. Maybe she could even fight a legion commander.

There's also the method of raising the Affinity Level and sharing Full Recovery or the Skill Heavenly Mind with Seong Hari. But that's only temporary. It only temporarily lifts the Spirit King's Curse (SS).

Above all, it was hard to predict how the Spirit King would react. It would be fortunate if he ignored it altogether, but he might cast a stronger curse out of spite, or even try to kill Seong Hari directly.

In the end, there's only one complete solution. To negotiate directly with the Spirit King. You might think that Seong Hari, as the person involved, should do it herself, but....

,To meet the Spirit King, you have to enter the spirit realm. But the moment Seong Hari enters, she'll be attacked and killed by the spirits. Even if it's not the Spirit King, the other top-tier spirits wouldn't leave her alone.'

In the spirit realm, which can be said to be the spirits' home ground, even Seong Hari can't guarantee victory.

Moreover, the Spirit King... is just an Outer God with the title of Spirit King. Humans without special power go insane just by looking at the Spirit King. Perhaps even Seong Hari would be no exception.

So, I have no choice but to negotiate in Seong Hari's place.

,That's insane. Does Hari know what you're trying to do?"

,She doesn't. If she knew what I was planning, Mom would try to stop me even if she had to beat me.'

,Do you know how I see you right now? I see you as a guy trying to end his life by jumping off the Han River.'

Suicidal behavior.

I think so too. Even if I have Full Recovery, it's over if I die again. Space-time scroll? There's a high chance it won't work. That's why I've been putting it off as much as possible until now.

But through future information, I've confirmed that the Demon King's army is invading Earth on a large scale. Moreover, it might happen in just a few months. There's no room to be picky about methods.

Seong Hari, who was called the strongest in the world during her prime. If she can unleash her power from her prime, it will be a great help. From the demons' perspective, it would be a huge disaster.

,I know it's insane. But I have to do it.'

,Why? What's driving you so hard?"

,If I don't, Earth will be destroyed. I need Mom's power.'

,Huh. That's absurd. Do you have any proof that Earth will be destroyed?"

,I don't. You wouldn't believe me even if I explained it. Just please help me.'

He looked at me intently and let out a deep sigh.

,Hoo.... Alright. I'll help you. But know this. It's not because I believe your words. Even if I refused, you would try to go to the spirit realm by other means. In that case, it's better for me, for the family, to help you. That way, there's a higher chance of you returning alive.'

,As expected of Great-Grandpa. You know me well.'

In reality, there was an alternative.

Gumokgyo. Those guys who worship Outer Gods also have some knowledge about the outer world. Borrowing the power of Gumokgyo to enter the spirit realm was the second alternative.

,You worthless rascal.... Hari and you, you're both making my insides rot.'

,Still, I'm better than Mom, right.'

,Don't talk back and prepare. We need to finish this before the Head of the Family.... Han-gu, that guy, returns. He would never allow it.'

,What do I need to do?"

,I'll prepare the gate. You prepare to enter with the spirits. Use Spirit Descent with either Mocha or Machina and enter in the state of a spirit.'

,Do I really have to do that? I didn't do that last time.'

,That last time was an accident. Shouldn't you go with a spirit so that the spirit can protect you? And it'll be much faster to adapt to the spirit realm by becoming a spirit through Spirit Descent. After all, that place is the world of spirits.'

,But why Mocha and Machina, and not a top-tier spirit?"

,Can you use Spirit Descent with a top-tier spirit and last long?"

,...I can't last long. I understand.'

,Top-tier spirits can travel to and from the spirit realm if they want to. They can help you even if they don't move with you. And it could be an opportunity for Mocha and Machina. Do your best.'

• • •

The gate was prepared in two hours. I used Spirit Descent (A) before the gate opened.

,Descend upon me, Machina.'

Spirit Descent is a spiritism that descends the contracted spirit into one's own body.

,Euaaaaaah!"

Machina enters me.

My body became a machine. My skin turned to steel, and thousands of metal gears meshed together inside my body instead of organs, constantly rotating and generating energy. It felt like becoming a perpetual motion machine.

It's a bit different from when I used Spirit Descent with Machina before. That's because Machina and I have grown since then.

My appearance has become that of a cyborg, but right now, I'm closer to a spirit.

,Mocha.'

,Kkuuung.'

I climbed onto the back of Mocha, who wasn't materialized. It was possible because I was in a state close to a spirit right now. I looked at the gate with my Spirit Eye.

,Great-Grandpa. I'm ready.'

,Hoo. Alright. Come back as soon as possible. Specifically, before Han-gu returns.'

,It won't take long if everything goes according to plan.'

,I hope that's the case.'

The closed gate opened.

Mocha flew through the gate without flapping her wings.
