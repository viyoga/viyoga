I headed to the detached house where Shibata had appeared. Since that bastard had come out of there, it didn't look like an ordinary detached house. Maybe there's a shrine maiden inside this detached house.

,Unlike the neat exterior, it's a mess.'

The inside of the detached house was chaotic. Broken pieces of decoration and trash were scattered everywhere. Occasionally, there were blood-stained spots. There's a big bed in the corner. It's clear that Shibata usually stayed here.

Looking around, I narrowed my eyes. My gut is telling me. There's something here.

,First, let's open all the doors here.'

The door next to the bed was the right answer. When I went inside, there was another door.

It's a steel door. There's no handle. Instead, you need a card key to open the door. It's a state-of-the-art facility that doesn't fit with a shrine. I tapped the steel door with the back of my hand and stepped back.

,I can't break it? I'll need a card key.'

I went back and started searching the detached house. It's where Shibata was guarding. Maybe there's a card key that Shibata used.

I was right.

I found a card key neatly placed in Shibata's desk. I took the card key and approached the steel door. BEEP. The steel door opened.

I went inside.

There were people. People wearing white gowns and masks on their faces.

,Who are you... Keoeoeok!'

I killed the ones who looked like researchers as soon as I saw them.

There weren't that many people. There were only four people here. I killed all the researchers and slowly looked around.

,I know this is a lab. I also know what they're researching...'

There were a lot of specimens in one room. Chopped human bodies, corpses with swollen skin.

It was clear that they had been doing human experiments.

,Shibata must be a successful product of human experimentation.'

I looked around the lab to the corner. There was no shrine maiden.

Coming out of the lab, I moved inside the shrine. I walked slowly, wary of enemies that might pop out from anywhere.

I found a corpse. It was a corpse wearing black clothes.

,...It's not the bastard I killed. In the first place, I didn't even come in here.... The cause of death is... a knife cut to the neck.'

And it's not a long sword like the Japanese sword I have. Looking at the wound, it's a knife-length knife.

,Hagawa Ryoko-gun. It's the bastard she killed.'

I go further inside. A few more corpses were seen. Like the corpse I saw earlier, it was neatly killed. Judging from the lack of signs of battle, it was definitely an assassination.

,As expected, a ninja.'

I walked forward, admiring Hagawa Ryoko's skills.

I saw a wooden door that felt somewhat elegant. I didn't approach it rashly and stared at the door. I focus my senses. I can feel a presence inside.

,It's too clumsy to say that the enemy is ambushing.'

If the purpose was a surprise attack through ambush, they would have thoroughly eliminated their presence.

The corpse outside and the presence heard inside.

,It seems the situation is already over. Nakagami Risa and Hagawa Ryoko did better than I thought.'

I confidently opened the wooden door.

It was a large space decorated with candles. The ceiling was high and there was an altar in the center. What was placed on the altar was a picture with complex patterns drawn on it. It probably symbolizes the mountain god that this shrine serves.

What's important is not the altar, but the three beautiful women in front of it.

Two of them are women who have already been in my arms. Nakagami Risa and Hagawa Ryoko.
