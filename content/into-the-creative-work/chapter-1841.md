Cheon Yu-un might have figured out Baekran and my plan to some extent. So, Cheon Yu-un could be preparing for something. I shouldn't just assume things will go smoothly.

,If I smash everything Cheon Yu-un has prepared and kill that bastard, I win.'

Conversely, if I can't kill Cheon Yu-un, I lose. I can't stay in the Cheonma Shin-gyo forever. Cheon Yu-un will try to kill me no matter what, and most of the Cheonma Shin-gyo is on Cheon Yu-un's side. Even with Cheonma Shingong, what I did is close to treason.

,Failure of treason means a purge.'

I looked around the Cheonma Shin-gyo. I saw a lot of people. Most of them are ordinary people. Cheonma Shin-gyo isn't just full of Martial artists who have mastered demonic arts. The economy can't run on Martial artists alone. The vast majority of Cheonma Shin-gyo were ordinary people.

,In the future, these are the bastards I'll be ruling.'

Where is Cheon Yu-un right now?

I recalled what I heard before parting ways with Baekran. Cheon Yu-un would probably be under house arrest. So, Cheon Yu-un wouldn't be in the Yeomma squad, which could be considered Cheon Yu-un's affiliation.

This is also Baekran's plan. The Yeomma squad is one of the top elite units in the Cheonma Shin-gyo. Even if the number of Yeomma squad members remaining now is small, it would be difficult for me to face them alone.

,It's fortunate he's under house arrest. Or, did I induce him to be under house arrest?'

Even if Baekran is a Regressor Cheonma, it's amazing to see her thoroughly understanding the current situation of the Cheonma Shin-gyo, making plans, and executing them.

I headed to the Socheonma Palace where Cheon Yu-un resides. Cheon Yu-un is under house arrest, so he should be there.

I'm a bit nervous since a lot is at stake. I adjusted my tension by fiddling with the sword hilt at my waist. It would be nice to have Hwaryeon Bido, but Hwaryeon Bido is currently cracked. If I swing it around carelessly, it might really break. Until I repair Hwaryeon Bido, I was planning to use another sword.

,Stormbreaker isn't bad, but the feel isn't quite right.'

In my opinion, Stormbreaker was closer to armor than a weapon. It's more efficient to use it as armor.

As I was walking while thinking about this and that, I arrived at the Socheonma Palace before I knew it. Four Martial artists were guarding the gate in front of the Socheonma Palace. All four were Martial artists who had reached the realm of Ogi.

,All four are Ogi First Stage. It hasn't been long since they reached Ogi.'

Martial artists of Ogi are rare. They are treated well even in the Cheonma Shin-gyo. They are considered both elite and main force. Setting up such Martial artists as gatekeepers?

,Even if he's Socheonma, does he have the authority to do that...?'

Suddenly, the contents of the original work came to mind. Cheon Yu-un was a sly bastard who built his foundation. He recognized talented Martial artists early on, supported them, instilled loyalty, and secretly cultivated Martial artists who followed him.

,These four must be the successful ones among the Martial artists Cheon Yu-un is cultivating.'

One bastard stood out. It was the one with his right arm wrapped in red cloth. It was definitely Narakgeom Ryeo-hak.

In the original work, he was the most loyal and most talented among the Martial artists Cheon Yu-un cultivated. The reason he is loyal to Cheon Yu-un is because Cheon Yu-un helped him avenge his brother.

I approached the Socheonma Palace without stopping. I felt the presence of a barrier. As expected, Cheon Yu-un had noticed the situation and was thoroughly prepared.

,Socheonma-kkeoseo is under house arrest. No one can enter the Socheonma Palace. If you have any words to convey to Socheonma-kkeoseo, please tell me. If you have a separate letter, I will deliver it to Socheonma-kkeoseo."

Ryeo-hak blocked my path and rattled off the words like a machine. Judging by the way he rattled them off without hesitation, it seems he's experienced this more than once or twice.

,I am Seomjeondo Yeom Gu-seok. I came to see my lord. It's an important report. I must report directly to my lord. Open the door."

,There are no exceptions. No one can enter the Socheonma Palace."

,Even Jegal Mo-sun?"

,......."

Ryeo-hak shut his mouth. Jegal Mo-sun is Cheon Yu-un's closest aide. He was likely inside with him.

,Tell your lord that I have come. Isn't that originally your role as gatekeepers? You're being presumptuous for gatekeepers. Presumptuous for gatekeepers."

I chewed them out. Having Ogi Martial artists act as gatekeepers was like using a butcher knife to kill a chicken. They knew that too, so they frowned at my sarcasm.

,...I will tell my lord. Please wait."

One of the four went into the palace. He came back out in just three minutes.

,Socheonma-kkeseo said that he will call you separately later. Please go back and wait until then."

,......."

My head started to spin. Cheon Yu-un collects talent and manages information through the original novel he read. The reason why the picky Cheon Yu-un trusts Yeom Gu-seok is because of that. The fact that that trust is wavering now means he's starting to suspect me.

,This can't be helped. The fact that the incident happened and I suddenly disappeared, and even the timing of my return matches perfectly.'

Even Jegal Mo-sun, who would later be known as the Demon Brain (魔腦), was by Cheon Yu-un's side.

,It would be difficult to get close under the guise of reporting and then *ssukssak*. I'll just go with the frontal assault.'

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 7]

Time slows down in a whoosh. I grabbed the sword hilt hanging at my waist with my right hand. Even in the slowed time, they reacted. Their eyes widened and they tried to draw their weapons. But they're slow.

Cheonma Divine Sword (天魔神劍) Hwanjeol (幻絶).

I used the only drawing technique of the Cheonma Shingeom. A black line extended from the scabbard and drew a horizontal semicircle. Three of them were cut by the sword as they were, but Ryeo-hak managed to get out of range. Judging by his flustered face, it seems he dodged it by chance with his intuition or something. I guess he really does have talent.

But the time acceleration hasn't ended yet. I rushed at Ryeo-hak. Ryeo-hak reacted to this. His reaction speed temporarily increased by several levels as he chased after me. You could call it a state of hyperfocus.

The sword Ryeo-hak raised falls downward. The reason he was given the nickname Narakgeom is because of that falling swordsmanship. Ryeo-hak was especially good at using falling swords.

,But his opponent is bad.'

Ogi First Stage and Samjeong First Stage. That difference can't be bridged with just concentration and talent. If I had been careless, maybe, but I was serious now. I had to kill Cheon Yu-un as quickly as possible.

Faster than what was falling from above, my sword grazed his neck. The black trajectory drawn by the demonic energy contained in the sword lingered in the air before disappearing.

[Time Acceleration has ended.]

Ryeo-hak's head fell with a thud. I passed by the four corpses and grabbed the door of the Socheonma Palace.

Pajik!

Electricity sparked and bounced my hand away. It was a barrier.

,Well, of course there would be.'

It's not a place where ordinary humans stay, it's a place where the son of Cheonma stays. It would be stranger if there wasn't a barrier.

I took out a talisman from my pocket. It was a talisman Milyeong made for me. It's impossible to break the barrier with just this talisman, but it was possible to enter the barrier. I held the talisman in my hand and opened the door. It opened without resistance. Instead, the talisman Milyeong gave me burned to ashes.

As soon as I went inside, a garden came into view. The garden with pine trees planted in it had its own charm.

,It's a garden that will soon be mine... hmm. Not bad.'

It was the moment I stepped into the garden. The ground under the pine trees rumbled, and blue-skinned ghouls sprang up like mushrooms after rain.

,Blue Ghouls (青僵尸), huh. It's Jegal Mo-sun's doing.

Jegal Mo-sun was a ghoul necromancer. The reason he was kicked out of the Jegal clan was because of his ghoul research. He came to the Cheonma Shin-gyo to do ghoul research to his heart's content.

The Blue Ghouls rushed at me. If you only compare speed, they were a little faster than Ryeo-hak, who I fought earlier.

,He must have poured all sorts of precious medicinal herbs into the ghouls while receiving Cheon Yu-un's support. Even when those corpses were alive, they wouldn't have been ordinary Martial artists.'

Cheonma Divine Sword (天魔神劍) Cheonchung Asura (天衝修羅).

I slightly lowered my upper body and pointed the tip of my sword forward. I wrapped my whole body in Cheonmagi and charged straight ahead.

Kwaang!

The Blue Ghouls that touched my body couldn't withstand the impact and were scattered in all directions. Still, the Blue Ghouls moved. Unlike humans, they have no pain and no thoughts. They just attack the enemy as their master tells them to.

I split the first bastard who rushed at me from head to crotch. Blue blood covered me. It smelled toxic.

,It's probably poison.'

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Mahura (摩睺羅).

I blocked the poison with a barrier that was stronger than a protective aura. Chiijijijik! The barrier that touched the poison melted.

,If I had unfolded a protective aura instead of Mahura, I would have been fucked.'

The bisected ghoul crawled towards me on the ground with its arms and legs. It was a bizarre movement.

Masin Sword (Heavenly Demon Divine Sword) Surananmu (Asura Chaotic Dance).

Dozens of black sword energies slashed the surrounding space. The surroundings were a mess of Blue Ghouls' blood and flesh. Even the charming pine trees were torn into thousands of pieces and lay scattered on the ground.

Feeling a kind of gaze, I raised my head.

The top floor of the palace. Cheon Yu-un and Jegal Mo-sun were looking down at me with seriously hardened expressions. I smiled and ran my thumb across my throat at them.

Cheon Yu-un distorted his face. A stream of blood could be seen leaking from his tightly closed lips. It seems he's incredibly annoyed by this situation. Or maybe it's because he was betrayed by me.

Now that I know the location of the bastard I have to kill, I just have to go and kill him. It was the moment I was about to jump up. A person fell from the sky with a thud, and the blue blood, flesh, and dust that had been piled up on the ground soared.

Even if I can't see, I can feel that bastard's aura. An aura that makes my skin tingle, an aura that is comparable to mine. No, maybe he's stronger than me.

,At least Samjeong. So, an elder.'
