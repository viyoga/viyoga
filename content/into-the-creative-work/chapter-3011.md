Sun Wukong used the Somersault Cloud technique.

In the original Journey to the West, it's described as a spell that lightens the body through a somersault, allowing travel by riding clouds. But in this world, the Somersault Cloud technique was a bit different.

When Sun Wukong used the technique, clouds materialized out of empty air. The clouds didn't scatter—instead they gathered and compressed together.

"So this is Sun Wukong's famous Somersault Cloud technique. It's a bit different from what I imagined."

"Kikiki. You know about the Somersault Cloud? Guess I'm famous in the future too, huh?"

"Probably more famous than the Jade Emperor."

"Now that's a fine thing to hear. Even better because it's not a lie. Kikiki. Alright, climb on."

I climbed onto the Somersault Cloud.

The Somersault Cloud was hard. It looked pure white with no gaps even from the outside. It felt as though every cloud in the region had been condensed into one place.

'At this density, there'd be enough left over to melt into water and flow away.'

A pointless thought. The Somersault Cloud was a technique. Something like magic. And the very word "magic" implies something that defies common sense, doesn't it.

The Somersault Cloud surged forward.

"Slower than I expected. About the same speed as me flying on the wind."

"I'm going slow on purpose. If I felt like it, we'd already be there and back."

"Why go slow?"

"Kikiki. Charging in blindly and killing everything isn't a bad option. But that doesn't seem to be what you want. It's also a request from the Bodhisattva, so I'll cooperate."

"I'll kill the Nine-Tailed Fox with the White Face and Golden Fur and Yamata no Orochi myself. The Nine-Tailed Fox first."

"And the rest?"

It's a bit of a waste of experience points, but you can't have everything. Some things you have to let go.

"Do whatever you want with them."

"Kikiki. Works for me. I heard there's a whole mess of yokai gathered there… Been a while since I got to stretch my hands. Not that I haven't been frustrated enough already, what with my uptight master and my idiot junior disciples."

The casual disrespect toward Tripitaka suggested he was still early in his pilgrimage to the Western Paradise.

'Surprisingly, he's not asking about future events at all.'

Sun Wukong himself surely knew the pilgrimage would succeed. There was no way he didn't know the extent of his own power, and his junior disciples Sha Wujing and Zhu Bajie were no ordinary beings either.

"We're here."

Maybe his pride had been slightly wounded by the comment about being slow. While I was lost in thought for a moment, we'd arrived at our destination. Below the Somersault Cloud lay the Japanese Imperial Palace.

"We're this close—why haven't they noticed us?"

"Because they're underneath the Somersault Cloud. From below, it just looks like an ordinary cloud. None of those bastards have the skill to see through my technique."

"The Nine-Tailed Fox with the White Face and Golden Fur should be no ordinary creature."

"The Nine-Tailed Fox? By my standards, she's nothing special."

Sun Wukong's skill in techniques was as renowned as his overwhelming martial power. Even in the Heavenly Realm, there were few sorcerers who could match his level.

I narrowed my eyes, gazing at the Imperial Palace. Black smoke rose from every corner of it. The black smoke didn't rise into the sky—it hung low and coiled around the palace grounds. That wasn't ordinary smoke. It was yoki—demonic energy.

"The yokai have increased in just this short time. The yoki pooled in one place is thick enough to see with the naked eye. Have all the yokai across the archipelago gathered at the Imperial Palace?"

"Including the ones outside the palace, about seventy thousand. What do you want to do? Want me to transform you with shapeshifting? Turn into a tiny bug, crawl into that fox bitch's head, and scramble her brains."

"That sounds like a shitty assassination technique just hearing about it. Not my style. I don't want to be a bug."

"Mine neither. If you'd said yes, I'd have been a little disappointed. So what'll it be?"

I raised my right hand upward. I focused my mind.

The air split open, and a single Meteor Sword I'd prepared in advance emerged. At minimum over 100 meters—the size of a building. The tip of the Meteor Sword aimed precisely at the Imperial Palace. Sssssssss. A chilling blue cold radiated from around the Meteor Sword, freezing nearby clouds into frost.

Giant Sword Fall.

The falling Meteor Sword was blocked by a barrier. A barrier powerful enough that even that colossal sword couldn't pierce it.

'This much I predicted. That Meteor Sword is just a disgustingly large and heavy blade that emits cold. A powerful barrier would naturally stop it.'

The Meteor Sword's true attack was the explosion.

'Detonate.'

The giant Meteor Sword exploded, unleashing a shockwave and cold. The barrier, shaken by the massive shockwave, froze in an instant and shattered.

"Kikikiki! Now this is exciting!"

Sun Wukong burst out laughing, clearly pleased.

Naturally, the yokai on the ground noticed our presence. After causing this much of a ruckus, there was no way the Somersault Cloud could hide our presence.

"SUSANOOOOO!!"

Yamata no Orochi, positioned some distance from the Imperial Palace, let out a roar of fury. The thunderous cry shook the grass and trees violently. Weak yokai collapsed, pissing themselves.

Sun Wukong and I remained unfazed.

"That's the one. An arrogant snake needs a hawk to teach it manners. This old man will tenderize that snake's flesh for you. If you're too late, I might just pickle the bastard in liquor."

Sun Wukong plucked a clump of hair from his head above the golden fillet and scattered it into the air. A powerful wind blew once, dispersing the tuft. Thousands of strands of hair became clones of Sun Wukong. The clones, each holding a Ruyi Jingu Bang in one hand, fell toward the ground with kikiki laughter.

Thousands of clones, yet each one was far stronger than any ordinary yokai.

'Even if each one can't match half of half of the original's power, he's summoning thousands of troops in an instant. What a broken-ass technique.'

The original Sun Wukong also leaped toward Yamata no Orochi.

"You backwater eight-headed snake! This old man will test your tricks!!"

Before Sun Wukong's body touched the ground, it grew enormous. Grown to rival the mountain-sized Yamata no Orochi, Sun Wukong raised that famous Ruyi Jingu Bang and swung it at the serpent. One of Yamata no Orochi's heads burst with a pop. But the destroyed head regenerated at incredible speed.

Yamata no Orochi spewed poison, and Sun Wukong retreated backward to dodge.

'So even Sun Wukong is wary of Yamata no Orochi's poison. Ah, this isn't the time to be spectating.'

If I dawdled, Sun Wukong might actually kill Yamata no Orochi himself.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

"S-Susanoo, sir…! I-I was merely bewitched by that cunning fox…! Cooperating with that yokai fox was not my own will!"

"Shut up."

'I can't sense the Nine-Tailed Fox with the White Face and Golden Fur. Is she hiding? Whatever. It's merely a stalling tactic.'

Taking the stairs down would be a hassle.

Warriors bewitched by the Nine-Tailed Fox and yokai serving as her subordinates rushed at me without warning.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Light (雷光).
