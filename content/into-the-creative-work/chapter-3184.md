Teleportation scrolls can't be used in Hell zones. The Mirror Tunnel is currently installed at the base and is in continuous use for moving troops and supplies. In other words, I had no choice but to run to the base on my own two legs.

'If I use the Flying Thunder God, it wouldn't even take an hour. Having to run there myself is a pain in the ass.'

Since I'm running through the sky, I just need to follow the path I came by. There's no way I'll get lost.

'Because I have the Hell's Aegis, I don't suffer the penalty effects from moving through Hell zones either. Aside from my legs being busy, this should be more comfortable than the trip here.'

After rising up into the sky, I looked in the direction opposite to where I'd come from.

'According to Mammon, the Hell Imperial Seal is at the eastern edge, was it?'

Since my position was close to the east, the eastern edge was visible from here too. What sat at the eastern edge was a massive castle.

A castle built in Eastern style.

Only that zone had a crimson sky. Perhaps it was because of the red river flowing nearby.

'The 9,566th zone, the Netherworld. It's a zone that appears in the original work too. Taking that place solo without an army is tough. I'll need to come back with troops later.'

Mammon said the other Regalia, the Hell Orb, was at the highest sky in Hell. I raised my head and looked up. Even though I was already in the sky, the sky stretched endlessly higher.

'It's not like I can just fly upward blindly and obtain the Hell Orb. This is Atlantis. Just as the moon is also a zone, if it's a zone, there must be an entry method.'

It would reveal itself eventually.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I became lightning and raced through the sky.

My base in the Hell zone is the 9,373rd zone, the Black Quarry. More precisely, it's a temporary base. The environment isn't as brutally dry as the Drought Hell, so I'll be staying here for the time being. After arriving at the base, I called the mercenaries together to catch up, and the next day I moved to the surface.

I met Elena at the Valdert estate. My gaze drifted to her lower belly. Maybe it was my imagination. It seemed like her lower belly was sticking out just a tiny bit.

"It's embarrassing when you stare like that."

"We've already seen each other's assholes. What's there to be embarrassed about."

"Fix that unrefined way of speaking. It's fortunate no one else is around to hear it."

"That's exactly why I'm saying it."

Smooch.

Since we were happy to see each other, I gave her a deep kiss first. Our lips glistened with each other's saliva.

"I heard the story. A great victory in the war in Hell, I'm told."

"Already? The rumors reached the surface in just a day?"

"Didn't you send the wounded to the surface first? They've been proudly boasting about the victory. Rumors like this always spread fast, but the nobles who sponsored you are actively spreading them. So they can claim credit for themselves. Thanks to that, my workload increased."

Treating the wounded in Hell required too much manpower, so all wounded who needed more than a week of recovery were sent back to the surface.

"I'm the one who won. Why did your workload increase?"

"People trying to get through me to meet you. Most of them want to support you. If they can get their name on your sponsor list, it counts as merit."

"Accept them. The more troops the better. It's not like I have to pay them anything in return anyway."

"You've already paid the price by achieving merit. Thanks to you, the Valdert name shines with honor."

"Good. The Valdert family is my home now, after all."

「You have killed the ruler of the 9,376th zone, the Centipede Den. Seizing control.」

「You have become the ruler of the 9,376th zone, the Centipede Den.」

「You will acquire 110,000 AP every 30 days.」

A notification window popped up out of nowhere.

'Looks like Helvis and Alexandra are doing well in Hell.'

That's the convenient part about Hell zones. Even if my subordinates kill another Hell zone's commander, the rulership comes to me as the monarch. In a word, auto-hunting.

On the surface, the one who lands the final blow takes most of the spoils, so it's an unthinkable system.

"I killed the Jester of Mischief who descended in his true body."

"…Ho-oh. That divine seat is the starting point of Ragnarok, isn't it? You've already prevented Ragnarok?"

"The original Ragnarok and Atlantis's Ragnarok are different. There seem to be more who want to trigger Ragnarok. One of the culprits he named is the Fire Giant."

"You mean the giant who burned the world in Ragnarok. If I had my way, I'd go to Hell and help you, but… things have come up on the surface too."

"What things?"

"Devil worshippers have appeared in large numbers. They've been causing incidents everywhere. Several nobles were killed by their hands. Most of them are demon contractors, so ordinary knights can't handle them — minor skirmishes keep breaking out."

"…Is this related to the Hell zones?"

"Probably. I suspect the boundary between the surface and Hell has weakened. Some ignorant fools blame you, but it's absolutely not your fault."

"Of course it's not my fault. Why would devil worshippers running rampant be my fault? Can't I just hunt down and kill the ones who blame me?"

"I'd like you to hold back on that. You don't know because you operate on your own terms. I'll handle it appropriately, so don't worry."

"Fine. But… those devil worshippers. You captured and interrogated them, right?"

If it's the Elena I know, the moment she heard devil worshippers had appeared, she would have gone out herself. And she would have interrogated them regardless of whether their minds were destroyed or not.

Elena smirked.

"You know me well. As you said, I captured and interrogated the devil worshippers."

"Was there a mastermind behind them?"

"Demons. Demons that crawled up from Hell seduced and incited them. Those who gained power ran wild as they pleased. There wasn't any particularly meaningful information, but… the demons' goal is obvious, isn't it. Just as you brought chaos to Hell, they want the surface to be chaotic too."

"Can demons come up to the surface easily?"

"I didn't interrogate a demon directly, so I don't know. However, there are loopholes everywhere."

There could be other items like my Mirror Tunnel.

Even if not, methods to travel between Hell and the surface exist.

'There are four entrances to Hell in total. One of them might be cooperating with the demons.'

It wasn't something I could resolve right now.

"It's hard for me to intervene in surface affairs."

"You just focus on Hell's affairs. As for the surface… the Angelus Legion is taking active measures. I don't particularly like it, but it's also true that thanks to them, the damage hasn't been severe."

"Their leader is an angel, after all. They couldn't just sit back and watch demons run wild. If they didn't step in, that would look strange in its own way."

After exchanging various conversations with Elena, I took a rest.

A new ability called Heaven's Response appeared. The moment I saw it, I tilted my head.

'Ignore usage conditions? I won't know for sure until I try it myself.'

Since it's a new ability, I should try using it at least once.

Even in reality, there were quite a few items with usage conditions attached. As an S-rank Hunter, I could obtain items with almost no restrictions.

An hour later, a Hunter Association employee dropped off an item at my house.

I carefully opened the metal box. Inside lay a pitch-black metal dagger.

I obviously knew what the dagger was. Still, I put it in my inventory and appraised it first.

[Gwigeom (鬼哭劍)

Forcibly uses the owner's vitality to unleash a slash.

When the owner swings Gwigeom, they suffer wounds.]

It's a demonic sword that can unleash slashes without any special ability, but inflicts damage on the user at the same time.

'Swinging it at home might be a bit much, but… I'm an S-rank Hunter. You think I can't handle a mere dagger like this?'

I placed a wooden log from my inventory on the dining table and gripped Gwigeom. I could feel Gwigeom sucking at my vitality. It wasn't to the point where I couldn't control it.

Shhheeeek!

With a sharp sound, a transparent slash flew out and gashed the log.

Sssk.

A cut opened on my right cheek and a drop of blood trickled down. It was a very shallow wound, the kind you'd get from a blade of grass.

'What happens if I swing hard enough to split the log?'

Shhk.

The slash flew out and cut the log.

In exchange, a wound opened on my left flank. The wound was bigger than before, and I bled considerably more.

'What a garbage item. Who the hell would use this.'

[Using Heaven's Response (天應).]

[30 points will be consumed. Will you use it?]

'It uses 30 points at once for this?'

The cost-effectiveness was absurdly terrible.

Still, I used it as a test.

The vitality that had been sucked into Gwigeom returned to me.

I swung the dagger and cut the log — already split in half — into smaller pieces.

There were no wounds I had to suffer in exchange.

By consuming 30 points, I had used this demonic sword without any conditions.

I tried to swing Gwigeom once more. Gwigeom took my vitality.

[Using Heaven's Response (天應).]

[30 points will be consumed. Will you use it?]

'It said temporary, but it's one-time use? Goddamn. This skill is complete garbage.'

Irritated, I tossed Gwigeom into my inventory in annoyance.
