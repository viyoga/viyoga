I frowned at Parkdal's words. It seemed the Mountain King, who could be called the king of bandits, was plotting something.

But soon, I realized it had nothing to do with me and relaxed my brow. I had no connection to the Mountain King. I didn't care what he was planning.

,It seems the Mountain King is deliberately trying to cause chaos."

Seong Jigone said. Unlike me, he seemed interested in this matter. Seong Jigone was sensitive to bandits causing a ruckus, being a general.

,That's right, Great Hero. I don't know why, but the Mountain King is trying to cause chaos by using bandits. His Imperial Majesty, the absolute ruler of the Empire...."

,He won't step in. There's no way he'd step in for something like this. Parkdal, don't mention His Imperial Majesty unless you really have to. I'm saying this for your own good."

,Thank you for the advice. I briefly forgot my place...."

,Well, even if His Imperial Majesty doesn't step in, those in charge of protecting the cities will find the bandits' uprising annoying. The Murim world will be buzzing for a while."

I couldn't disagree with Seong Jigone's words.

There were hundreds, even thousands of cities in the Empire. Not all of these cities were managed by the government troops; famous sects managed them. Their attention was bound to turn to the rising bandits. Those who were quick to notice would also realize that the Mountain King was planning something.

,Yujin-ah. What about you? You're active in the Murim world."

,I don't give a shit about those bandit bastards' antics."

I racked my brain for a moment. To be precise, I recalled the contents of the original works. I remembered a few episodes where bandits were causing trouble, but the scale wasn't this big. There was a reason I didn't know about it. And I didn't particularly care.

,I wish we could leave for the Sima Clan soon."

,Alright. It's about your woman, so I should cooperate as much as possible. But one last thing. Parkdal. Do you know anything about the Mountain King? What do you think his goal is?"

,...Currently, the position of the Leader of the Four Kings Alliance is vacant. The Mountain King is known as a rough and ambitious figure. I don't know the Mountain King's exact plan, but... I think he might be trying to take this opportunity to rise to the position of Leader."

That was a pretty plausible story. But this, too, had nothing to do with me or Seong Jigone.

We returned to the city first, then left for the Sima Clan.

•••

After traveling for fifteen days, we arrived at Jeongu City (井雨市), where the Sima Clan was based.

Jeongu City was a bit far from the capital. Still, it was a fairly large city that belonged to the so-called capital region. Seong Jigone, who had entered the city, gave an awkward smile.

,It seems like we came too early. I sent a messenger pigeon, but we arrived before the message."

,The message isn't that important, is it? We can just apologize if they say something. It's not like we came to fight the Sima Clan."

,That's true."

Seong Jigone and I headed to the Sima Clan. The Sima Clan's location was at the edge of the city.

,Doesn't the Sima Clan rule this city? Why is it at the edge of the city? It's usually in the city center."

,Who knows? Maybe it has some kind of magical meaning?"

Seong Jigone's words were correct. The closer we got to the Sima Clan, the stronger the sense of unease became. It felt like something unknown was constantly bothering me. I didn't want to stay here.

Looking around, I realized that only Seong Jigone and I were on the road.

,It seems like some kind of spell to repel people. I guess the Sima Clan doesn't want to welcome guests."

,They're certainly closed off. I wish they'd be more cooperative."

We somehow made it to the main gate. There were no gatekeepers in front of the magnificent house, as there should have been. All there was was a signboard that said 'Sima' and a gate with a red pattern drawn on it.

I was about to open the gate with force. But before my hand could even touch it, the gate opened on its own with a bang.

,...Was it an automatic door? That's unexpected."

,The fact that the door opened means they're welcoming us, right? It's not a bad sign."

I walked forward. Seeing things unfold so easily made me feel uneasy. It felt like walking towards a trap.

,In this world, you can get backstabbed at any moment. I need to be careful not to get backstabbed.'

Before long, I found a middle-aged man dressed neatly.

,I've been waiting for you two. So Cheonma (Little Heavenly Demon) Seong Yujin-nim, and the Little Military General of Hayang, Seong Jigone-nim."

,...They know about us. Seong Jigone said the message hadn't arrived yet."

,Yes. No messages have arrived at the clan recently."

,How did you know we were coming?"

,The Clan Head was once an Imperial Priest. One of the essential requirements for an Imperial Priest is to read the heavens."

They knew about our visit by reading the heavens? I was suspicious. Even Milyeong couldn't do that easily. She said it required other special talents. And even if you could read the heavens, matters related to the future were vague and couldn't be confirmed.

It was possible that Sima Ryeong, the Clan Head of the Sima Clan, was a sorceress with tremendous talent... but they might have predicted my and Seong Jigone's visit in another way.

,Well, at least they have some ability. That's a good sign.'

The guide stopped little by little as he walked. Each time, the direction he was going changed slightly. I felt frustrated and spoke to him.

,What are you doing?"

,Ah, there are dozens of formations installed near the main building of the clan. If you walk carelessly, you're bound to get lost. There weren't originally any formations like this, but... recently, the Clan Head seems to be experimenting with something.... So, for safety, we had no choice but to install formations."

It took 20 minutes to break through the formations and arrive at the main building.

The main building was also strange. The building itself was distorted and bent in every direction. It was as if energy was mixed into the building.

,...No. It's tangled."

,As expected of an absolute master who has reached the Three Peaks. You guessed it right away. All sorts of energies and formulas are tangled in the main building. It's the result of all the past Clan Heads of the Sima Clan training and researching here. We haven't been able to fully grasp exactly what power this main building has. It was created unintentionally and accidentally... Isn't it amazing? Other sorcerers outside the clan always widen their eyes and are shocked when they come to the main building. It was quite fun to see their reactions, but recently...."

The man suddenly became talkative. Seong Jigone and I followed behind him into the main building with tired expressions. He, who I thought would guide us to the end, stopped walking at some point.

,This is as far as I'm allowed to go. Just go straight inside."

,Thanks for guiding us this far."

I praised him and moved forward.

And at some point. We entered a room and faced a woman.

She was a composed woman. From a single strand of hair to a single wrinkle in her clothes, everything was neatly arranged. Her jade-like skin, crescent-shaped eyebrows, and firmly closed red lips exuded elegance.

It was difficult to see her figure. She was wearing flowing clothes. All I could see properly was her face.

,Are you the Clan Head of the Sima Clan?"

,Yes. I am Sima Ryeong, the Clan Head of the Sima Clan. I have been waiting for you two."

,How did you know we were coming?"

,I read the heavens. Before that, I was aware of your information. The Little Heavenly Demon of the Heavenly Demon Church, the disciple of Guwol Seonja (Immortal Guwol).... You have extraordinary identities, so I couldn't not know."

,The Sima Clan's intelligence network is truly amazing."

,I can't explain it in detail, but the Sima Clan's eyes and ears exist all over the continent."

,...Then, do you perhaps know the purpose of our visit?"

,I do not. All I read through the heavens was that you two would come to me and help me. The heavens did not allow me any more information than that."

We're going to help her?

Is this a roundabout way of saying it? That she won't help us unless we help her.

I wasn't particularly angry or annoyed. I had expected that she wouldn't help us without any conditions.

I stared at her intently. The energy I felt from her was stronger than mine. In other words, Sima Ryeong's level was at least in the middle stage of the Three Peaks.

Sima Ryeong invited us to sit down. She then used magic to serve us tea. After drinking the tea, I said.

,My woman has fallen into a coma. She tried to rise above the Three Peaks and reach the realm of Harmony, but she failed. My master says she's trapped in her mental world. She can't control her energy and keeps leaking it, so she only has a year left. How can I save her?"

,......Fortunately, I know the symptoms. Many Clan Heads of the Sima Clan have wanted to climb to higher places. More than 70% of them have failed. Among them, there were those who showed the same symptoms as the woman you mentioned."

,Do you know how to save her?!"

,I do. She probably moved her mind to her mental world to protect her spirit. However, the mental world would have been corrupted by the effects of the failure. That's why she's trapped in her mental world and can't get out. Because the corrupted mental world can no longer be called her own. You, Great Hero, must enter her mental world and bring her out. She can clean up the mental world later."

,How can I enter another person's mental world? Is there a magic spell?"

,Those who don't know much about magic think of magic as omnipotent. They mistakenly believe that everything is possible with magic. But magic is not omnipotent. As far as I know, there is no magic spell to enter another person's mental world."

,......."

I closed my mouth and listened carefully to Sima Ryeong's words. I sensed that Sima Ryeong hadn't finished speaking.

However, if it's a power other than magic, that's a different story. The Sima Clan possesses a treasure capable of performing immortal techniques. With immortal techniques, you can enter another person's mind. This is a proven fact.

There was a way. I felt a slight sense of relief and asked her.

,......Are magic and immortal techniques different?"

,Immortal techniques are a higher-dimensional power. You can do things with immortal techniques that you can't do with magic."

,What do I have to do? I hope it doesn't take too long."

Sima Ryeong asked me with a blank expression.

Do you happen to know the legend of Domyungsan (道明山)?
