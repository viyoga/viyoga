The Black Pharaoh disappeared. Even looking around, his presence couldn't be felt.

,Atlantis restoration complete.

The system notification popped up and disappeared.

I look around. Ordinary people were walking around as if nothing had happened. They didn't remember what had just happened at all.

But some people tilted their heads.

,Huh, uh? I feel a sense of déjà vu. Wasn't there something weird in the sky?

,What the hell are you talking about? Did you have a dream or something?

,A dream? Ah. Something like that.

,It's probably just déjà vu. You get that feeling sometimes, right?

,It felt much more vivid than déjà vu.

,Stop bullshitting and let's go earn more AP. God damn it. If we could just control one small area, we wouldn't have to go through this dogshit.

Some seemed to feel something, but in the end, they didn't think much of it.

,The strong ones wouldn't have forgotten like those bastards.

I crossed my arms and stared at the empty air.

,System.

No response. It's always like this. Unless it's something special, the system ignores me.

,The Black Pharaoh showed up earlier and tried to mess with me.

,Checking Atlantis records.

,As a result of the check, the Black Pharaoh's intervention has been confirmed.

,Sanctioning the Black Pharaoh.

,The Black Pharaoh is making a face. You tattletale!

It was just unnecessary drama. Even if the system sanctions him, it can't deal a big blow. All the Black Pharaoh did was have a little chat.

,System. I'm the victim. I deserve compensation.

,500,000 AP is provided as compensation.

,Taking it from my pocket! You damn robber!

After that, there was no further reaction. 500,000 AP was stuck in my pocket. I felt good about earning 500,000 AP for something so trivial.

,I won't let my guard down. That bastard leaves suggestions in people's minds just by having a few conversations... It's better to just forget the conversation we just had.

Since erasing memories is impossible, I decided to ignore it.

Pudeodeok.

Suddenly, a pigeon appeared in the air. The pigeon came to me and spread its wings wide.

Peong!

Like a magic show, the pigeon exploded and turned into a letter, slowly falling from the air.

It's a Lightweight Feather item. It has the effect of sending a letter to the target. The store price is 700,000 AP. It's a one-time use and has restrictions, so it's not a cost-effective item.

In short, it was an item close to a money-wasting item. Of course, there were advantages. It could accurately deliver items to the target, specifically a god.

I unfolded the letter and checked it. The sender was Samuel-ssi. The boss of Helten. The content was simple. A summons for the executives.

• • •

,Entered Sector 3,709, Star Judgment.

,This is a hidden sector.

,In Sector 3,709, truth and lies are judged.

,The penalty for lies has been lifted due to the dominator's settings.

,Due to the dominator's special settings, god seats other than the contract god seat cannot see the situation here.

,Due to the dominator's special settings, you cannot leave the sector by spatial movement, including scrolls.

,A secret curtain has been spread. All god seats cannot look into this place for a certain period of time.

I arrived at what could be called Helten's hideout. Looking around, all the executives except me were gathered. I made eye contact with Elena-ssi, who was wearing a mirror mask. We exchanged glances, but didn't acknowledge each other.

,The clown's here too. Now that everyone's here, shall we get started?

A raccoon sitting on the floor opened its mouth. Next to it was a wooden doll.

Besides them, there were quite a few others wearing masks. There were masks I was seeing for the first time.

,There are 8 in total. Last time I saw them, there were 3. 2 died and 1 new one came in.

Helten's executives are quite fluid. They didn't gather out of loyalty to Samuel-ssi, but for their own individual gains and purposes. If they wanted a close relationship, they wouldn't have used masks or code names.

Eight people gathered in a small space, but no one opened their mouths carelessly. This was also due to the special nature of the place. The moment they uttered a lie in this sector, they would be found out by the boss, Samuel-ssi.

,Thank you all for gathering. I didn't originally plan to summon you like this. We still have a lot of shortcomings.

Samuel-ssi's voice was gentle. A young man in his 20s with white skin and blonde hair. He had an appearance that inspired goodwill. At the same time, it was an appearance that could be ignored.

But none of the people gathered here ignored him. He overwhelmed the audience with a strange atmosphere. His presence dominated this place.

,He's a regressor, but not really a regressor.

This is the 8th Atlantis. Samuel-ssi has information from the past 1st to 7th iterations thanks to his Hunter ability, Record Viewing.

,Let's get straight to the point, shall we? I'm quite busy, so it was difficult to make time even now.

The mirror spoke. Samuel-ssi nodded.

,Everyone remembers the anomaly that happened an hour ago, right? Surely there's no one who's forgotten?

The executives didn't answer. It wasn't worth answering. Aren't they one of the most famous criminal organizations in Atlantis? Helten's executives basically possess outstanding combat power. Even if they were overwhelmed by the crack that happened an hour ago, they wouldn't have forgotten it.

,I'll ask just in case. Anyone gone crazy?

,Is it even possible not to go crazy after seeing that?

A lion mask holding a spear asked back.

,How much do you remember?

,...I know that something was observing us through the crack. I don't know exactly what it was. My head turned white and I committed suicide. ...Honestly, I don't want to think about it again even now.

The lion trembled slightly. I didn't expect the most belligerent of the executives to be like this.

,If the lion's like that... Am I the only one who hasn't gone crazy? Did Samuel-ssi go crazy after seeing the eyes too?

Samuel-ssi smiled brightly.

,That's why mental strength is important. Ordinary people receive mental damage as soon as they see the true form of a god. But the good news is that there are only two beings that can drive us crazy just by recognizing them.

,...Who are those two?

,The Key of All Doors and Eternal Abundance. The being that observed us in the crack is Eternal Abundance. Those two are gods that even the gods can't do anything about.

,You didn't go crazy after seeing that bastard? How did you endure it?

There was a kind of desperation in the lion's voice. Samuel-ssi opened a subspace and poured out items. A pointed hat, round glasses, an old coat, gloves with complex patterns, a necklace similar to a dreamcatcher, and so on.

,These are items with mental defense effects of at least A-rank or higher. Thanks to them, I was barely able to endure it. I should say I was lucky. It left around the time I reached my limit.

Samuel-ssi said it as if it was nothing. But no one could easily agree with that statement.

A sparkly person wearing a jewel mask sighed and scratched their neck.

,Boss. Isn't the boss's ultimate goal to kill a god? Ah, of course, the people here probably have similar goals. But how are you going to kill it? The thing that drives you crazy just by looking at it.

The other executives remained silent and agreed. They lost their will to fight after seeing Eternal Abundance. I understand. How are you supposed to deal with a monster that destroys stars just with the aftermath of its movements?

It's not like telling an ant to kill a human. Isn't it more like telling fine dust to kill a human?

,...If fine dust gathers, can it kill a human too? But even if it gathers, it won't even be a handful.

I understand why Samuel-ssi issued the summons. It's to comfort the executives. If the lion's like that, how can the others be okay? If he had left them alone, their will would have been broken.

,Lion. Think about it logically. That's not a god, it's a natural disaster. The god seats probably consider it a natural disaster too, right? It's a being that even the gods can't understand.

,...Isn't that our enemy?

,Shall I tell you some good news? You don't have to worry about Eternal Abundance ever again. It's gone outside the universe. To an unknown place that even the gods can't understand. You probably won't see it again.

I was surprised.

Isn't that the information I heard from the Black Pharaoh?

,Did that bastard pass on the information to Samuel-ssi too?

Did he find out with Samuel-ssi's ability, 'Record Viewing'? That makes even less sense. Eternal Abundance wouldn't have even moved in the past iterations.

,If it wasn't the Black Pharaoh... another god must have told him. He seems to have joined hands with a god of considerable rank.

I don't know who that god is.

,I'll tell you another piece of good news. The Key of All Doors has suddenly disappeared. But this is also bad news. Because the two cosmic beings that the gods were wary of have disappeared. Do you know what that means?

,......It means the gods can run wild. Even if that's not the case, they'll try to occupy the empty universe. To put it in perspective, it's like the owner of the throne has disappeared.

A guy wearing a letter mask said. I don't know his code name since he's a new recruit this time.

,He wasn't even in the original story.

I couldn't figure out his identity.

But this guy has been subtly watching me since earlier. I can feel it through my sensitive senses.

,Does he know me?

I have no idea who he is.

Maybe he's from the desert.

,That's right. The game is changing. It's a positive change for us.

,What do you mean?

I asked.

,Most of the gods' power is tied to Atlantis. And now it's too late to withdraw from Atlantis. Because the losses would be too severe. So what will the gods do?

They can't withdraw. But they've invested too much in Atlantis to just stand by.

,...Raise the stakes? Is Atlantis worth that much?

,It's up to the gods to give it value. The important thing is that our value has increased. We're getting an opportunity.

,......What kind of opportunity?

,A good opportunity.

Samuel-ssi smiles. He didn't seem to have any intention of telling us the detailed plan.
