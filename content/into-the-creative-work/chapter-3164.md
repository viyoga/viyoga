Before participating in the third and final preliminary round of the martial arts tournament, I used the power of foresight to check the future.

Countless futures were observed simultaneously. Even so, I hadn't checked every single future. That was physically impossible. The future kept changing, after all.

'When you look at futures bubbling up like foam, you notice something. Even among countless futures, there are things that don't easily change.'

With the extraordinary intuition awakened by the power of foresight, I guessed at the name of that phenomenon.

'Fate. A fixed future.'

The contents of the preliminary round, for example. The System knows I observed the future. Yet it didn't change the contents of the martial arts tournament. The fate that the martial arts tournament's framework doesn't change.

'It's not that it can't be changed at all. It's just that unless you touch the cause of that fate, it won't change.'

The third preliminary round and the martial arts tournament finals.

Having examined the future, I nodded.

'No notable variables. As long as I don't make a mistake, I win.'

While feeling a slightly premature sense of victory, a corner of my heart felt boredom.

'The biggest variable in the future I saw is me. Acting like a machine according to a set script doesn't suit my temperament.'

I could also see futures that accounted for the variables I myself might create. But I didn't. Knowing everything would be boring, but the biggest reason was one thing.

'The further the future I look at, and the more diverse the futures I observe, the more the power of foresight is consumed. I'll be actively using the power of foresight starting from the third preliminary round, so I need to conserve it now.'

The future I saw was one premised on me using the power of foresight.

In other words, if I exhaust all the power of foresight here, everything up to the future I knew would change entirely.

'To think that from the moment I see the future, I become subservient to it. How ironic.'

「The third preliminary round of the martial arts tournament begins. You will be transported to the preliminary round location in 5 minutes.」

「The third preliminary round is monster hunting. Find and hunt 16 monsters. Participants who kill a monster advance to the finals.」

「During the martial arts tournament, participants who would die are eliminated instead of dying.」

The participants, myself included, were dropped at the center of the island. The island was divided into separate zones radiating from the center. The east was lush and green, the south showed rivers and swamps. The west was wasteland and volcanic terrain, and the north was a winter with snow pouring down heavily.

The nearly 300 participants looked around and furrowed their brows. Finding 16 monsters in this vast place felt like a fool's errand.

Kiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiik!

An ear-splitting shriek rang out.

The participants turned their gazes north, toward the source of the sound. A single enormous bird was slowly flying across the northern sky. The participants' faces turned strange.

"Just from its size and presence, I can tell. It's a boss-class monster."

"Don't tell me all 16 monsters on this island are boss-class?"

"I don't know about the rest, but finding the monsters won't be hard. Boss-class monsters usually attack intruders who enter their territory first."

"...Take down a boss-class monster alone? That's brutal."

A war of nerves began among the participants.

Facing a boss monster alone is difficult. But joining hands with other participants is also difficult. They're competitors. Only the participant who kills the boss monster can advance to the finals. One monster per person. Killing one together doesn't mean advancing together.

'This place is about to turn into a shitshow.'

Some participants, myself included, quietly slipped away from there. They were the ones confident in their ability to face boss-class monsters alone without allies.

'Joo Seo-hyun and Kang Myung-jin, also from the same Legion, are each heading in different directions immediately.'

Kang Myung-jin already knew the information from the start. Joo Seo-hyun told me the location of a monster where I wouldn't be interfered with by other participants. If I don't create variables, the two of them will advance to the finals just as I saw in the future.

I moved quietly westward. I used Deception (SSS) to suppress my presence as much as possible.

While moving west, I stopped midway. And turned my body around.

"Did you think I wouldn't notice?"

Three participants who had been following me while taking cover behind trees and rocks revealed themselves without much reluctance. Those three had been watching and tailing me since before I used Deception.

"Damn. To think you'd catch onto our tail this quickly. The first-place finisher in the preliminaries really is different."

"I heard you received a prophecy in advance and knew the preliminary round information beforehand. Seems that was true."

"You're not the prophet yourself, obviously... Can't you introduce us to that useful prophet? And while you're at it, share the preliminary round info too."

They wore friendly smiles, but their essence was that of robbers. If I didn't cooperate, they'd draw their swords without hesitation and resort to force.

The viewers' opinion on this spectacle? They don't concern themselves with that much. This world isn't Earth in reality. In the end, it's a place ruled by the strong.

"If I knew information about the future, I would naturally have known you three would show up as well."

I summoned the Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory.

"You're going to go a round with us?"

"Aren't you overflowing with confidence."

"What a stupid choi—"

Before the bastard could even finish his sentence, I kicked off the ground.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Gale Thunderclap (疾風迅雷).

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

Full power from the start.

In an instant, I closed the distance to one of the enemies. The enemy couldn't react to my speed in time.

Schluk.

The tip of the Hwaryeon Bido pierced through the enemy's heart. Instead of screaming, the enemy grabbed my blade with both hands. Enemies on either side of me thrust their swords forward and charged in.

'The power of foresight.'

I see the future. There's no need to grasp every future. One second ahead, unfolding around me, is enough. Of course, this is closer to a confirmation check to eliminate variables in a future I already know.

I let go of the Hwaryeon Bido's hilt from my hand.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Lightning seized my body and yanked me backward. The blades coming in from both sides couldn't reach me. The two swords that had stabbed at empty air twisted their trajectories, trying to slash me as I retreated backward.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Art (雷術) — Violet Lightning (紫電).

A purple bolt of lightning flashed and seeped into the enemies' swords. Magnetism was imbued into the two blades that had tried to cut me, and they stuck fast to each other.

"What?!"

"Magic?!"

I grabbed the backs of both their heads with my hands.

"It's kiss time."

I yanked both hands together hard. The two heads collided in midair.

KWAAANG!

Their faces were smashed and brain matter splattered.

An unseen fourth enemy approached, aiming for my back. A transparent assassin using stealth. Aiming for my heart, caught off guard in the thrill of victory.

'Their skill at suppressing presence is excellent. If I hadn't known, I might have been done for.'

But I know.

An assassin who has lost the advantage of surprise is weaker than a warrior charging head-on.

「Using Lightning Speed (電光石火).」

My body turned into lightning. The assassin's dagger stabbed into my back, but ordinary physical force couldn't affect me in this state. Rather, the lightning transferred to the assassin. The assassin was electrocuted on the spot and couldn't move.

I grabbed the Hwaryeon Bido's hilt again and swung it at the assassin.

Shhk!

The assassin's head fell. The corpse vanished right after.

'With this, the tangled worms are dealt with.'

「Jangnan Sammaekgyeong tilts its head while looking at you.」

'It doesn't seem to have noticed the power of foresight... It's in a suspicious state, then.'

I had seen the future, but I hadn't seen countless futures.

'Even if it notices, it doesn't matter. There won't be enough time to pull any tricks now.'

The finals are one-on-one combat. There's almost no room for variables to intervene.

'It's proceeding exactly as the future I saw.'

I arrived at a plain in the western wasteland.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Void Path (虛道).

Basking in the pouring sunlight, I walk through empty air and ascend into the sky.

Unlike the first preliminary round, there was no penalty for flying through the sky.

I lower my head. At the center of the plain, I could see a single black boulder the size of a house. Looking closely, I could tell it wasn't a boulder but a monster.

A monster in the form of a giant cobra over 5 meters long. A monster whose body was wrapped in rock instead of scales.

I pulled Stormbreaker, in the form of a spear, from my inventory.

"Astrapé."

Bzzzzzzzzzt!

Lightning began to gather and condense around Stormbreaker.

「The Lord of the Skies shows interest in your spear.」

It seems curious about Stormbreaker withstanding Astrapé. Of course, I had no intention of explaining it to the Lord of the Skies.

Bzzzzzzzzzzzzzt!

The lightning coiling around the spear shone more brilliantly than sunlight.

I could feel mana draining out in massive quantities. But I didn't stop. I condensed the lightning to its limit.

The lazy monster crouched on the ground seemed to sense something was wrong, wriggling its body and raising its neck. Even if it was late, it was far too late.

"Die."

The spear became a line of blue light, piercing through the monster and burrowing into the wasteland ground.

Silence.

As if heralding the catastrophe about to arrive, all sound vanished.

KWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWANG!

The ground and the monster's body burst simultaneously.

Hundreds of thick lightning tendrils swept through the surroundings like kraken tentacles. As the lightning writhed, scars formed on the ground. The earth splitting open was a given. One of Astrapé's effects. Lightning gaining mass. Each single tendril of that lightning was a weapon.

'It's overkill, but... to finish it in one blow, there's no other way.'

The lightning subsided, and all that remained in its place were the scars carved into the earth.

「You advance to the martial arts tournament finals.」

「The title 'Finals Qualifier' is granted.」

「All stats increase by 1.」

「The Lord of the Skies gives a thumbs-up at your power.」

「Heavy Flashlight feels a sense of rivalry.」
