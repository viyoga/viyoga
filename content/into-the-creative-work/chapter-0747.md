747. Savior of the Academy

Ssaaaaaa.

The wind blew. The sound of the wind brushing past was as eerie as the laughter of a demon.

"Dodge."

I said. Kim Cheon-woo and I simultaneously created distance. Black flames soared from the ground where Kim Cheon-woo and I had been standing.

'That's no ordinary flame.'

I could feel the power of a demon in the black flames. A dangerous and unpleasant fire.

I immediately looked for Yoo Seung-jun. Yoo Seung-jun was dashing towards Kim Cheon-woo, not me. He wore a red tengu mask on his face, and black crow wings fluttered on his back.

'He targeted Kim Cheon-woo, not me. Kim Cheon-woo must have really pissed him off.'

I thought about helping Kim Cheon-woo, but decided to watch. I was curious about Kim Cheon-woo's true skills, and I could subdue Yoo Seung-jun anytime if I got serious.

'It wouldn't be bad to let Kim Cheon-woo gain some experience at this opportunity.'

Kim Cheon-woo activates his trait, Holy War (S). A golden, sacred aura settles on his greatsword. The spear and greatsword collided. It was Yoo Seung-jun, who wielded the spear, who was pushed back. It wasn't so much a difference in strength as a difference in compatibility.

Yoo Seung-jun gritted his teeth.

"That power! That goddamn holy power! The reason I lost last time was because of that power!"

Yoo Seung-jun flew into the sky. Not to escape, but to gain a favorable position. Kim Cheon-woo, who couldn't fly, had no way to attack Yoo Seung-jun.

Yoo Seung-jun wildly swung his spear in the sky. The wind and black flames generated from the tip of the spear became blades and fell towards Kim Cheon-woo.

Kim Cheon-woo moved with composure. Unlike last time, Kim Cheon-woo maintained his composure to the end. He didn't show any signs of going berserk.

"Do you think you can keep dodging? Die! Hahaha!"

On the other hand, Yoo Seung-jun was intoxicated by the power of the demon. Mana that seemed inexhaustible no matter how much he used it, a situation where he was unilaterally attacking. In a game, it would feel like he was using cheats.

He glanced. Kim Cheon-woo looked at me. I wondered if he was asking for help, but he wasn't. He ran towards the forest.

"Kim Cheon-woo! You're already in my grasp!"

The crazed Yoo Seung-jun chases after Kim Cheon-woo. It seems I've been pushed out of his interest.

'Thanks to that, it'll be easy to get evidence.'

I blatantly took out the camera I had been secretly holding and followed Kim Cheon-woo and Yoo Seung-jun.

The battle, which I thought would be one-sided, reversed in the forest. Kim Cheon-woo succeeded in cutting off the wings of Yoo Seung-jun, who was flying in the sky, by climbing a tree and jumping up.

"Kuaaaaaak!"

Yoo Seung-jun fell to the ground with a scream. If the opponent wasn't Yoo Seung-jun, the wings would have recovered, but he had bad compatibility.

'There's nothing to see. It's over.'

Yoo Seung-jun can't keep up with Kim Cheon-woo's skills. The power of the demon? Yoo Seung-jun's black flames and wind are being destroyed without being able to properly use their power in front of Kim Cheon-woo's golden light.

Kaang! Chang! Chaang!

The greatsword and spear repeatedly collided and bounced off each other. The golden light and black flames mixed and canceled each other out. The battle continued, and the damage to Yoo Seung-jun's body accumulated.

"Kuaaaaak! Why! Why! Why are you blocking my way!"

Yoo Seung-jun, having lost his reason, unleashed his mana. His body is gradually being eroded by mana. It was Yoo Seung-jun's mistake. The balance, which had been maintained fairly evenly, collapsed, and a large gap opened for Yoo Seung-jun. Kim Cheon-woo didn't miss the given opportunity.

Seogeok.

Yoo Seung-jun's right arm was cut off and fell to the ground. Yoo Seung-jun's face turned pale.

"I-It can't be. I...!"

Yoo Seung-jun took a step back. He watched Kim Cheon-woo, who was exhausted, and tried to escape. If I hadn't been there, he might have succeeded in escaping. I, who had been watching, intervened and kicked Yoo Seung-jun in the back.

With the sound of leather bursting, Yoo Seung-jun rolled on the floor. Dark red blood gushed from his severed shoulder.

"Seong Yujin...!"

Yoo Seung-jun raised his upper body. He gritted his teeth and glared at me as if he wanted to kill me. I stomped on his head. Kung! Yoo Seung-jun's head is trampled into the dirt floor.

"Aaaaagh! You!!"

Mana gushes out. Yoo Seung-jun flails his remaining left hand and swings it at me.

'Lightning.'

Pajijijijijik!

"Kkeuaaaaaaaa!"

Yoo Seung-jun's scream echoes. I didn't stop there, I swung my sword and cut off Yoo Seung-jun's left arm as well, and suppressed him by striking his Pressure point.

"Yujin. Aren't you going too far? What if he dies...."

Kim Cheon-woo approached. I let out a hollow laugh.

"There's no way a demon would die so easily with just this. More importantly, are you okay? You look pretty tired."

"I'm... okay."

Kim Cheon-woo's complexion wasn't good. If you look closely, his hands and feet are also trembling. The excitement of the battle hasn't left his body yet.

I pointed the blade at Yoo Seung-jun's neck. Yoo Seung-jun met my eyes with the cold metal. There was fear in his eyes.

"Don't tire each other out late at night, and answer the questions well."

•••

I interrogated Yoo Seung-jun for over two hours. But the information I found out was limited. At best, it was all about Yoo Seung-jun's identity and some information about the Japanese Magic Society.

"So. You don't know anything about the other demons in Maruhan Academy?"

"I don't know. How many times do I have to say it? I only receive and carry out missions alone."

"There's no way the Magic Society would trust a half-wit like you and proceed with the work."

"Whatever you say, I don't know what I don't know."

"This bastard. Can't you lower your eyes?"

I didn't like Yoo Seung-jun's glare, so I kicked his torso with all my might. Yoo Seung-jun let out screams and groans of pain.

"Kim Cheon-woo. Isn't there anything you want to ask this bastard?"

Kim Cheon-woo, who had been watching quietly, approached.

"Yoo Seung-jun. Why did you become a demon? With your talent and skills, you could have become a hero even without the power of a demon."

"...I'm an orphan. I entered the Magic Society and accepted the power of a demon to survive. If I didn't have the power of a demon, I would have become a cold corpse, abandoned in an alleyway and rotting away."

"......"

Kim Cheon-woo's complexion darkens. I put my hand on Kim Cheon-woo's shoulder.

"You don't believe what that bastard is saying, do you?"

"There's no need for him to lie. Yoo Seung-jun made a contract with a demon because he wanted to survive...."

"I'm going crazy."

Ppak!

I hit Kim Cheon-woo in the back of the head. Kim Cheon-woo rubbed the back of his head and glared at me.

"What are you doing?"

"It's because you're frustrating. Listen carefully. Yoo Seung-jun isn't an orphan. His parents are alive and well. And he made a contract with a demon to survive? There's no more novel bullshit than that. Considering the treatment demons receive in this world and the erosion of demons, it's more likely to survive by entering an orphanage."

"It was... a lie...?"

"That guy is a demon. You're an idiot for simply believing the words of a demon. Honestly, I don't even know why you believe Yoo Seung-jun's words."

Yoo Seung-jun, whose lies were exposed, sweated coldly and rolled his eyes. Just as he was about to open his mouth to explore the situation, he quietly closed his mouth and watched, as I held the knife and moved it around in the air.

"Even if that bastard's words are true, you shouldn't waver. He became a demon because of his unfortunate past? You can't be forgiven for becoming a demon for that reason. You know it too, Kim Cheon-woo, even if I don't say it. If we let this guy go here, more people will die in the future."

"......I was just shaken for a moment because I was immature."

Kim Cheon-woo grabbed his greatsword again as if to regain his composure.

"Seong Yujin, what are you going to do with Yoo Seung-jun?"

"I was originally going to kill him.... Can you kill him?"

"Leave it to me. I can kill him. Yoo Seung-jun must die here."

"I told you. I was originally going to kill him."

"......You're not going to kill him? We've extracted all the information we can."

"If this guy dies here and goes missing, there will be problems at the academy. If a student suddenly goes missing, it will be noisy inside and out."

Everyone knew that Kim Cheon-woo and I didn't have a good relationship with Yoo Seung-jun. If Yoo Seung-jun suddenly goes missing, it will be troublesome in many ways.

"Are you going to hand Yoo Seung-jun over to the Hero Association?"

"I told you I don't want it to be noisy. And I can't trust the Hero Association. It's easy for the Japanese Magic Society to interfere."

"Then what are you going to do?"

"I have to hand it over to someone who is capable, has power, and can be trusted."

"Is there such a person? I have no idea."

"There is."

I took out my smartphone and contacted someone. A calm voice was heard through the smartphone speaker.

-What is it?

It was the voice of Kang Ji-young, the head of Maruhan Academy.

"Aunt. A problem has occurred."

-If it's about the last incident, didn't I tell you to handle it yourself?

"It's not related to the Heroes Alliance. It's a more serious problem."

A sigh was heard.

-...Tell me.

I told her about Yoo Seung-jun. Yoo Seung-jun is a demon. Kang Ji-young, upon hearing those words, immediately changed her attitude to a serious one and asked for our location.

Kang Ji-young is the head of Maruhan Academy. She is a person who wants the daily life of the academy to be peaceful. She would want to end this quietly. I don't care what happens to Yoo Seung-jun in the process.

"Yoo Seung-jun. The Iron Lady is coming here. Shall we say our goodbyes in advance before we part ways? Farewell."

"Shit... fuck...."

Kang Ji-young's publicly known image was steel. She has no mercy and no flexibility. Of course, it's a little different from the actual Kang Ji-young, but it was true that she had no mercy for the enemy.

Yoo Seung-jun, who had no arms, looked at us and suddenly got up and tried to run away. I kicked a stone at my feet. The stone cut through the air and accurately hit Yoo Seung-jun in the back of the head. Yoo Seung-jun slumped to the ground.

"Where are you trying to run away to? Kim Cheon-woo. Cut off that bastard's legs."

"...Is it necessary to go that far? What if he dies...."

"There's no way a demon would die with just this. And if necessary, we can use a potion. You're not going to tell me you can't cut off his legs because you feel sorry for that bastard, are you?"

"...Mercy for demons is a luxury."

Kim Cheon-woo, with a blank face, approached Yoo Seung-jun with his greatsword. The greatsword fell from above, and a terrible scream echoed.

I support Kang Ji-young's actions. If Kang Ji-young, the head of the academy, wavers, the academy itself will waver. That's why I handed over Yoo Seung-jun's custody and evidence to Kang Ji-young. The academy runs properly when Kang Ji-young is there.

Dead. Or locked up somewhere.
