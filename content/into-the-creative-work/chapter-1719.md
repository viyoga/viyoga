The party is being dragged to the temple by those bastards.

I quietly tailed them. The important thing in tailing is distance control. Too close, and I'd be discovered; too far, and I wouldn't know what they're doing.

,If they were going to kill them, they would've done it right there. There's no reason to drag them to the temple.'

That means they're not just taking them to the temple to kill them. Makes me even more curious about what they're hiding.

,Wiping them all out alone and leisurely investigating the temple... that's probably impossible. These bastards are no joke.'

I'd win if we fought separately. Conversely, it's dangerous to fight them all head-on. I don't know exactly what they do, but they have some skill.

,I need to be more careful once I get inside the temple.'

I prepared the Illusiter. I have to use it without hesitation when it's dangerous. Saving the battery will only make things worse.

"Wh-where are you taking us?!"

"Shut up and walk."

The bearded man said in a low voice. He no longer had the relaxed demeanor he had outside the temple. He was clearly nervous.

The interior of the temple was simple, unlike its massive and grand exterior. A structure that led straight inside. Just walking forward would naturally lead to the center of the temple. I knew this wasn't a place made for people to live.

As I went further inside, I saw bloodstains on the floor and splattered blood on the pillars. I could tell that quite a few people had been dragged here.

,Judging by the reactions of the staff at the dungeon entrance... they probably didn't just capture and kill the Hunter party that came in.'

Dealing with Hunters has too many variables. If they needed to kill people here... it would be much better to kidnap people from outside and kill them. In the case of illegal immigrants, no one would care if they suddenly disappeared.

,This path is getting narrower.'

It's difficult to tail them.

It's better to give up tailing them in this case. It's much safer to go in and check after they've finished their business.

,But then I won't know exactly what they're doing. Let's use the Illusiter and go in.'

Seeing their level, I felt confident. If I made up my mind to run, they wouldn't be able to catch me.

Torches were hung on the inner walls of the temple, where sunlight didn't reach. The path continued further inside. The further I went, the more bloodstains there were.

And I came to the very end. A wide space appeared. A staircase and a table like a bed in the center. That was definitely an altar.

,Shibal. Is this another thing related to Brahmasen? I'm sick of Cults.'

The party seemed to be thinking the same thing as me, as they started struggling violently.

"Y-you crazy bastards! Were you guys a Cult?!"

"You're planning to sacrifice us!"

"S-save me! Please save me!!"

No matter how much they struggled, they couldn't break free from the restraints.

The bastards who introduced themselves as Berta didn't touch the party, unlike before. They didn't even scold them. They just stood there silently. Their attitude felt both solemn and alien. The party seemed to sense that too, as their struggles lessened and they soon became quiet.

A suffocatingly heavy silence descended.

It's quiet. So quiet that I wondered if the pressure had increased.

"Kanex-nim. We have brought the sacrifices."

My vision shakes.

At first, I thought I was seeing things. But I wasn't. It wasn't my vision, but the space that was shaking. The space continued to shake, and cracks appeared on the altar.

A head emerges from the cracks. The head, over 3m in size, was similar to that of a human. The skin was red, rotten, and sharp teeth were half-smashed between torn lips. The skin was peeled off around the cheekbones, revealing bone, the left eye was hollow, and the right pupil shone gold. A triangular tattoo was engraved on the forehead. The scalp was peeled off, and the skull was partially smashed, revealing the pink brain.

Judging by appearance alone, it certainly didn't look like a benevolent being.

"An unscheduled sacrifice, I see."

Kanex opened his mouth. Even though he had no neck, his voice echoed inside the temple.

"They came on their own. As the saying goes in our country, it's like a gourd rolling in with the vine."

Berta's leader, the bearded man, stepped forward and said. I could feel him being as careful as possible not to commit any disrespect.

"I have no interest in the sayings of your country."

"Are you not interested in the sacrifices either?"

"That's not true. Sacrifices are always welcome."

Kanex grinned eerily. Ten pink tentacles sprouted from the brain between his broken skull. The way they looked, like brain wrinkles made into tentacles, was quite disgusting. The tentacles moved in an instant and snatched the party. It was so fast that it would be difficult for me to react if I didn't concentrate.

"Aaaaaaah!"

"Save me, please!"

Berta remained silent, whether they screamed or not.

Kanex chewed and swallowed the party members one by one with his tentacles. In his large mouth, humans disappeared as mere chunks of meat. Even though he only had a head, what he swallowed didn't flow down to the floor but evaporated.

"Ooh.... Much more satisfying than the sacrifices I usually receive. This is the kind of sacrifice I wanted."

Crunch, crunch.

He chewed on the humans with satisfaction.

"I'm glad you're satisfied."

"Offer such humans in the future as well. I will surely give you a reward for the sacrifices. Don't you want that too?"

"I'm sorry, but today's sacrifices are special. If we weren't lucky, we wouldn't have been able to offer them to Kanex-nim."

"Hmm. I see."

Crunch, crunch.

Kanex devoured all the party members. Nevertheless, he seemed unsatisfied and rolled his eyes. Greed flashed in his eyes as he looked at the bearded man, then disappeared.

"Today's sacrifice was quite satisfying. I'll give you one more as a bonus."

Ptooey!

Kanex spat something out.

Two red walnuts. Or rather, it would be more accurate to say they looked like small brains. The bearded man bent down and picked up the small brains.

"Thank you, Kanex-nim."

"As you can see, these are more potent than the enhancers I usually give. The effects are also better. If you want to know about these things... offer high-quality sacrifices. Understand?"

"I will contact those above and convey Kanex-nim's words. Unfortunately, I don't have a choice."

"I expect it."

The bearded man, having taken the enhancer, looked at Kanex.

"How is your condition?"

"It's better than when I first saw you. But at this rate, it will take decades to restore my original power. I need more sacrifices."

"We are doing our best."

"I know. Let me tell you something. I give just rewards to those who follow me. If I regain my power... I will give you rewards that cannot be compared to enhancers."

"...I will convey those words to those above as well."

It was the moment the bearded man politely bowed and turned around.

"Is that all?"

"That's all for the sacrifices. The next sacrifice will come in tomorrow. I will visit you again then."

"Was that behind you not a sacrifice but your comrade? He looked quite delicious... too bad."

"Pardon?"

Kanex gestured towards me.

,Shibal. Did that monster bastard called Kanex notice from the beginning?'

The bearded man turns around. Because I was using the Illusiter, the bearded man didn't recognize me right away. Then he uses mana. A wave of mana sweeps past. The bearded man's face instantly contorted.

"Who the hell are you?!"

Cheok cheok cheok!

The bearded man and his comrades simultaneously drew their weapons. Hostility pours towards me. They also used mana to amplify their senses and detect me. The Illusiter became meaningless. I turned off the Illusiter to save the battery.

My appearance is revealed. Since I'm wearing a disguise mask, there's no chance they'll recognize me.

"Who are you? I know you're not with those guys from before. Reveal your identity."

"That's what I want to say. What are you guys who offer humans to monsters and get brain fragments in return? From what I hear, you seem to be subcontractors... who's your boss?"

Before the bearded man could open his mouth, Kanex exuded his presence.

"Dare... dare you call me a mere monster?"

"If you're in a dungeon, you're a monster. And look at your state right now. What else would you be if not a monster?"

"I am a god. Even if I have fallen and become like this due to defeat, there is no reason to be insulted by a mere human. The sin of blaspheming a god is never light... you will face a painful death."

"A god? Like Brahmasen or something."

"Brahmasen! You! What is your relationship with Brahmasen!"

Kanex shouted. As soon as he heard Brahmasen's name, he distorted his face and expressed anger. The air trembled in the aftermath. He was definitely not someone to be taken lightly.

One of the bastards picked up a bow and shot an arrow. Too obvious of a shot. This is to draw attention.

,As expected....'

There was a bastard approaching stealthily and quickly. He lowered his stance and lunged like a snake.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

I dodged the arrow and used Instant. Instead of Hwaryeon Bido, which could reveal my identity, I took out a plain-looking sword and swung it at the approaching bastard. The bastard changed direction. The sword cut off the bastard's right arm. Too bad. If the bastard's reaction had been a little slower, I could have split his head in half.

Three knives fly towards me. It was a woman named Do Hae-young. A woman with short hair. Looking closely, she's quite pretty. Her expression was as stiff as a soldier.

Ting! Ting ting!

I held the sword sideways and moved it slightly, deflecting the three knives. The deflected knives spun in the air and stopped. The blades rushed towards me.

,Psychokinesis.'

Just as I was about to deflect the knives with my sword again, my body wouldn't move. An invisible force tightly constricted my body. It felt like I was inside the ground.

,This is also psychokinesis.'

There's no need to use Cheonshim. I increased the output of mana and burst it out in an instant. The psychokinesis that had been constricting my body disappeared.
