,How dare you insult me?! I challenge you to a duel, you goblin-like bastard!

The sudden outburst drew the attention of everyone around us. Cotton flinched, but then shouted in an angry voice.

,Such impudence! Do you think I'd be afraid of a duel?!

Duels between knights were common in the Empire, so no one tried to stop us. Everyone watched with interest. Even Count Mork.

The duel arena was created in an instant. The center of the banquet hall was cleared in a circle.

Before the duel began, Count Mork spoke to both of us.

,War is just around the corner. I have no intention of disrespecting your duel... but I hope you'll keep it reasonable. It is forbidden to take the opponent's life or inflict serious injuries such as cutting off limbs.

I frowned. My plan to kill that Cotton bastard fair and square in a duel was thwarted from the start.

,Your Excellency. Isn't that just sparring, not a duel?

,Sir Daker, please understand me.

,My honor will be tarnished if rumors spread that I engaged in sparring, not a duel.

,That won't happen. I guarantee it with my name that this was a honorable duel. Don't you all think so too?

Count Mork asked the people gathered around. Of course, their answers were predetermined.

,As Your Excellency the Count says.

,This duel is honorable. We acknowledge it.

With the situation unfolding like this, there was no choice. I looked at Cotton. He seemed to know the value of his life, quietly preparing for the duel.

,No choice, huh.

I clicked my tongue inwardly and donned my armor with the help of the servants. I grabbed my longsword. Cotton held a large greatsword in both hands, disproportionate to his small stature.

,Let the duel between Sir Cotton and Sir Daker begin!

,Waaaaaaah!

Amidst the cheers of the crowd, Cotton and I simultaneously lunged at each other.

Kaaaaaang!

The clash of sword and greatsword echoed with a shockwave. It was heavier than I expected.

,He not only imbued his sword with mana... but his physical training is also considerable. I can't believe he's taking my strength head-on like this.

My physical abilities are slightly below the average of an A-rank Hunter. That means this small knight before me is on par with an A-rank Hunter in terms of strength alone.

He was much stronger than I thought.

,I wonder what his swordsmanship is like.

We walked forward, swords locked together. Distance was crucial in any battle. Especially with swords, being too close wasn't always advantageous. If you stuck too close, there wouldn't be room to swing the sword. This was even more true for greatswords.

As if he immediately grasped my intention, Cotton kicked off the ground and retreated, creating distance. His movement was quite strange. It felt like he moved his body using the greatsword as an axis.

I immediately closed in on him and swung my sword.

Cotton was barely able to defend against the continuous strikes. Even though his strength was somewhat on par with mine, his speed couldn't keep up.

Clang! Clang! Clang!

,To push Sir Cotton back with pure strength... impressive. Is that a greatsword disguised as a longsword?

,It's not just his strength, look at his swordsmanship. There's no opening. He's reached the pinnacle of swordsmanship alone. A knight with both strength and skill... remarkable. I don't know why he hasn't been granted a knighthood yet.

,Excuse me, sir. Hasn't the outcome of the duel already been decided? Sir Daker is completely overwhelming him, so why hasn't the duel ended?

,You fool. That's why you can't escape being a squire. Neither of them has used their full power yet. They haven't even used Sword energy. Oh, Sir Cotton's aura has changed. Is he planning to use his secret technique? Watch closely from now on. Sir Cotton's secret technique is a rare sight.

,Secret technique....

,Yes. A knight without a secret technique cannot be called a knight.

,Kuaaaaaaah!

Cotton screamed. Mana erupted from his body. I had no choice but to retreat, swept away by the mana. Red Sword energy rose like a mirage from Cotton's greatsword. But my gaze was fixed not on the greatsword, but on his left shoulder.

Because a long, slender third arm sprouted from his left shoulder. Cotton's third arm drew a longsword from his waist. That sword also shimmered with red Sword energy.

,Sir Daker! I acknowledge you! You are an outstanding knight! Try to block my secret technique!

Cotton charged. A greatsword in each hand and a longsword in his third hand. Looking at his stance, it was well-structured. He wasn't just charging recklessly. Instead of being surprised by Cotton's bull-like momentum and retreating, I lunged forward.

[Using Momentary (瞬間). Remaining stacks: 11]

Cotton couldn't react immediately to my accelerated movement.

I deflected Cotton's greatsword with my sword, which was covered in blue Sword energy. Cotton's greatsword and my sword rose above our heads simultaneously.

Cotton's third hand moved. I covered my left gauntlet with blue Sword energy and blocked the sword.

Then, using my left leg as a pivot, I put strength into my right leg. Pajijik. Lightning gathered on my right foot.

[Using Momentary (瞬間). Remaining stacks: 10]

A blasting roundhouse kick! My right foot struck Cotton's abdomen precisely.

,Keueok!

Cotton flew backward and crashed to the ground. He twitched for a moment, then stopped as if he had fainted. It was a force that could have killed him if he hadn't been wearing armor.

Count Mork immediately intervened and declared.

,The victor of the duel is Sir Daker!

Clap clap clap clap!

Everyone applauded.

,I heard Sir Daker was arrogant... there was a valid reason for his arrogance.

,Sir Daker suddenly became faster. It must have been a secret technique.

,Didn't sparks fly from his foot when he did the roundhouse kick? Could it be magic?

,You fool! Can't you distinguish between magic and a knight's secret technique?! There's no rule that a knight can only have one secret technique! Oh, and just in case, don't ask a knight about their secret technique. That's as incredibly rude as insulting their parents to their face!

The unconscious Cotton was taken to the infirmary, and the banquet continued. Far from being dampened by the duel, the atmosphere became even more lively.

Knights who couldn't fight directly had their squires fight instead.

,Don't lose. The moment you lose, you're no longer my squire!

,Offer me victory as my squire!

,Is that all you can do with a sword?! I didn't teach you that way!

,Fight! If you truly want to become a knight, don't fear death!

The banquet became more and more violent as time passed. The floor was stained crimson with the blood shed by the squires, and just as I was getting annoyed by the vibrating blood and alcohol fumes, Count Mork stepped forward.

,Let's end the banquet here. Keep in mind that the war is not far off, and get a good rest today.

And so, the banquet ended at dawn.
