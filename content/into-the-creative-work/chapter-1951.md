I continued to move in the direction the dowsing pointed.

Fortunately, it wasn't across the sea. The direction the dowsing pointed was somewhere on Palgong Mountain in Daegu. I had circled around Palgong Mountain, so it was certain.

To get to the exact location of the treasure, I had to get out of the car and move on foot. Driving a supercar around Palgong Mountain was inconvenient in many ways. And the area the dowsing was currently pointing to was restricted.

Police officers blocked the way to where the dowsing was pointing.

,I'm sorry. This area is currently restricted. Please turn back."

,What's the reason for the restriction? Did a monster appear or something?"

,Yes. I heard there was a dungeon break nearby. Hunters from the Association are currently taking care of it. It will probably be restricted until tomorrow afternoon."

,I'm a Hunter too, though."

,Is that so? If you tell me your name, I'll contact the Association to verify."

,A Hunter who's just here for fun."

,......."

I couldn't abuse my power or ignore them and go inside. This was reality, and I was just an ordinary Hunter. I backed up the supercar. I found a suitable motel nearby and settled in.

,Ha-rin-ah, what do you think?"

I asked Han Ha-rin, who was fiddling with her smartphone. My gut was telling me. This was clearly strange.

,I searched the internet. There are two articles about a dungeon break happening on Palgong Mountain."

Dungeon breaks weren't common.

And this was Daegu. It couldn't be compared to the Seoul metropolitan area, but it was a metropolitan city. A city with a population of over 2 million. Even though a dungeon break happened in such a city, there are only two articles?

,Something's up."

I took out my smartphone and sent a KakaoTalk message to Baek Ji-eun. She was an executive at the Korean Hunter Association. Maybe she knew something. The reply didn't take long.

Baek Ji-eun: Palgong Mountain? Hmm. I'm hearing about it for the first time.

Seong Yujin: You really don't know?

Baek Ji-eun: Just because I'm an executive at the Association doesn't mean I know everything that happens in Korea. Besides, I'm not in charge of Gyeongsang Province.

Seong Yujin: Okay.

Baek Ji-eun: Are you at Palgong Mountain now? When are you coming up?

Seong Yujin: I'll contact you later.

I put away my smartphone.

,The most likely possibility is that the Association is cracking down on a black guild. Otherwise, there's no reason for the Association to control it."

,I'm getting more and more curious."

,...About the situation now?"

,No. About the treasure."

Maybe the things happening now were related to the treasure. My curiosity about the treasure grew even more.

Han Ha-rin stared at me intently. Her gaze was somewhat dissatisfied.

,You're thinking of sneaking inside, aren't you?"

,I can't go back empty-handed after coming all this way. Ha-rin-ah, you stay here."

,You want me to let you go alone? Who knows what kind of trouble you'll cause? I'm going with you, so keep that in mind."

,A date in the mountains. Not bad."

And so, we started moving towards Palgong Mountain. It was easy to sneak inside, avoiding the police. No matter what, they couldn't completely surround and control the entire mountain.

I strode forward in the direction the dowsing was pointing. In my head, I was thinking about how to take the treasure.

,Once I get it in my hands, I can just go back with a space-time scroll."

I could give a space-time scroll to Han Ha-rin too. I knew she was tight-lipped, so she wouldn't tell anyone.

,......!!"

Our legs, which had been moving forward without hesitation, stopped.

I felt something that I couldn't explain in words. The air, temperature, and humidity hadn't changed. The only thing that had changed was the mana concentration.

I glanced to the side, and Han Ha-rin's expression was also stiff. Unlike me, she immediately blurted out the answer.

,An encroachment-type dungeon."

Open-type, and the most troublesome type of dungeon that appeared a few years ago.

I decided to take it a bit more seriously. Like exploring a dungeon. From my various experiences, I knew that letting my guard down would lead to a quick end.

Uuung.

The sound of air flowing hit my ears. I narrowed my brows at the unnatural flow of air.

The space in front of me distorted. It was like heat haze shimmering.

,This feeling is similar to when I use a space-time scroll."

I hugged Han Ha-rin, who was right next to me. Her eyes widened in surprise at the sudden action. Regardless, I held her tightly.

Uuuuuuung!

The distortion of space swept over me and Han Ha-rin.

Han Ha-rin and I appeared in a completely different place.

A tent.

Not a small camping tent, but a large tent used as a barracks. In the center of it, I was holding Han Ha-rin tightly.

,Shibal. What the hell is this all of a sudden?"

A man who was eating instant noodles cursed. I looked him up and down. He was wearing light armor and had a sword at his waist. A Hunter.

,You insensitive bastard. Can't you see the mood? Do you have to ruin it like that?"

,What the hell are you talking about, suddenly appearing like a crazy bastard!"

The man put the instant noodles on the table and jumped up from his seat. He reached for the sword at his waist, and I reached out into the air and summoned my sword.

Kwajik!

Suddenly, the man's body slammed into the floor. The table and instant noodles were also destroyed, dirtying the man's body.

Gravity. It was Han Ha-rin's power.

,Stop hugging me so suddenly."

Han Ha-rin grumbled softly and got out of my arms. She needlessly straightened her hair while looking at the man who was pinned down by gravity, unable to move.

I looked around and was slightly impressed. The applied gravity seemed to be applied only around the man.

,The ceiling and other furniture didn't collapse. Ha-rin has improved a lot too."

Before going to interrogate the man, I took out the dowsing and checked. The dowsing was pointing to the right. I had confirmed the direction of the treasure.

,I have a lot of questions. What are you?"

,...Can't you release this on that woman first before I talk?"

,Not that woman, she's my woman."

The man looked up at me as if asking what I wanted him to do. I shook the sword in front of his eyes, and he immediately lowered his gaze.

,First, answer my question. Where is this?"

I didn't know whose doing it was, or if it was a phenomenon of the encroachment dungeon, but I was forcibly teleported. Judging by the fact that the man was speaking Korean, it was certain that this was Korea.

,So you were caught up in the space distortion too? Goddamn it. Why did I have to appear inside the barracks?"

Kwak!

I stabbed the sword into the back of his hand. The man's eyes widened as he looked at his pierced right hand.

,I'm not playing around with you right now. Cut the crap and answer my questions. Next time, it won't end with your hand."

,......This is Palgong Mountain in Daegu. It's one of the barracks we use."

The man's voice lowered. He seemed to have realized that his life depended on his answers now.

I asked the man about various things.

The man's name was Seo Tae-oh. He was a member of the Black Guild Opal. I had heard of the Black Guild Opal. It was a mercenary guild that specialized in dirty work.

,What are you doing here?"

,We're just staying here as requested. We were preparing to withdraw because we confirmed that the Association's movements were intensifying."

,You weren't just staying here, were you?"

,The request was to stay and protect this place. Now that the Association is here, that request is over too. You don't seem to be affiliated with the Association... Let's just end it here. I'll pretend I don't know you two, so spare me. Then I'll tell you how to get out of here safely."

,Haven't you grasped the situation yet? You're the one whose life is in danger right now."

,Look around the barracks. There are six beds. Six people stay and live in this barracks alone. There are over 50 of my colleagues here. Do you think you two can handle that? Judging by the power I feel, you seem to be an A-rank... and your woman is not quite A-rank, maybe B-rank? Half of my colleagues are A-rank or higher. Can you two handle that?"

I glanced at Han Ha-rin's mood. I was worried that she might have some sympathy for this guy, but her eyes were much colder than usual. I didn't feel a shred of mercy towards the man.

Seogeok!

I swung my sword and cut off the man's arm.

,Keueueueup!"

The man desperately suppressed his scream. He understood that if he shouted here, his colleagues might come running, but his life would end.

,Tell me in detail who the client is, what kind of monsters come out of this encroachment-type dungeon... Tell me in detail."

I tortured Seo Tae-oh for 15 minutes and found out what I was curious about.

,...I've told you everything I know. I'll tell you how to get out of here too. So."

Puk.

Without listening to his words, I stabbed the sword into his neck. I had intended to kill him from the beginning. A way out? I had a space-time scroll, so it was fine. I took this opportunity to hand a space-time scroll to Han Ha-rin, who had a stiff expression.

,Tear the scroll when it's dangerous."

Han Ha-rin, who received the space-time scroll without a word, said after a moment of silence.

,The situation is much more serious. It's better to get out of here as soon as possible."

,I have to get the treasure."

,Didn't you hear that the client is Baekrim (White Forest)? Baekrim is behind this situation. Surely you know what Baekrim is?"

,There's no way I wouldn't know Baekrim."

The worst criminal organization operating in Japan. They openly commit violent crimes such as attacking Japanese guild Hunters and assassinating politicians. They are Japan's troublemakers, operating under the cause of changing the current corrupt Japan.

I had been caught up in their terrorism when I went to Japan before.

Those guys are suddenly operating on the Korean Peninsula?

,It's probably because of the treasure. According to Seo Tae-oh, there's an executive of Baekrim here. If an executive is moving directly...."

The treasure wouldn't be an ordinary treasure.

As I felt the rising greed, I saw Han Ha-rin's body trembling slightly.

Unlike me, who was used to this situation thanks to the pleasure life app, she had no experience at all.

I scratched my head. There was nothing I could do. Giving her clumsy advice would only hurt her pride.

,The only way is to get used to it."
