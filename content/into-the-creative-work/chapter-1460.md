,Bell Teier...'

I repeat the name, searching my memories. It doesn't ring a bell. I've never even heard of it in the original game.

'Is it just that she looks like the character I created?'

,You don't need to think about my name so seriously. I'm not even active in Neo London.'

,...Did you come from Hamburg Innovation?'

Hamburg Innovation

It's a city-state like Divine France and Neo London. However, its territory and population are smaller than other city-states. But Hamburg Innovation's mechanization technology is the best in the world.

If you want to turn your body into a machine, go to Hamburg Innovation. That's the saying going around.

,Oh. You're sharp? How did you know? It should be impossible to tell just by looking at me.'

,I guessed from those mechanical eyes. It would be hard to get something like that unless it's from Hamburg Innovation.'

Actually, I just threw it out there because the starting point of the character 'Jjapaguri' that I created was Hamburg Innovation.

Face, figure, voice, personality. She's a carbon copy of the character I set up. And even her place of origin is the same. She was definitely the 'Jjapaguri' I created.

Amazing.

It was very amazing to see my character, who I only saw through the monitor, looking at me. And even though it was the first time I'd met her, she felt familiar.

But I didn't bother to show it. I turned my body. She's the character I created. So what changes? I can't say, 'You're mine now because I made you.' In this world, she lives her own life.

I threw a coin into the fountain.

,Wait. You didn't tell me your name. Are you going to do this?'

,...Eugene Meyer.'

,Eugene Meyer. Hmm. I remembered it. It's a good name.'

,Didn't you input it?'

,I didn't replace my brain with a machine. I only replaced my arms, legs, and eyeballs with machines. Oh, should I include my heart too? No, that's.... Ha, I don't know why I'm saying this to you, who I'm meeting for the first time.'

,.......'

The 6th coin went plunk into the fountain.

,What did you come here for? Is it work?'

,It's personal. I got a piece of information that's been bothering me. I can't tell you the details. Do you understand?'

,Yeah.'

It's probably something related to her personal story.

The player characters in the original game have different stories depending on their origin and job. Her story is....

'She'll take care of it herself.'

She and I are nothing to each other. Reminding myself of that point again, I threw the last 7th coin. In the game, a message pops up saying that luck has temporarily increased if you throw 7 coins into the fountain. But nothing popped up in front of my eyes.

,Do you make a lot of money? If you're going to throw money in the fountain, give me some money too.'

,I didn't throw away money. I bought luck.'

,With 70 credits? That's enough money to have a meal at a decent restaurant.'

,If I have luck, I might be able to earn more than that.'

,Ha, I don't believe in that kind of talk. ...Are you a fortune teller?'

,I'm not a fortune teller, I'm a magician.'

,Suddenly I have a lot of faith in you. 70 credits... should I try it too? But does it have to be 70 credits?'

,Whether it's 1 credit coins or 10 credit coins, it just needs to be 7 coins.'

,Really? That's the best news I've heard all day.'

She threw a 1 credit coin into the fountain. I passed her and went out of the village. The sun was slowly setting and the dark moon began to rise.

The village was as quiet as a ghost town. Everyone must have gone home, locked their doors, and lay in bed, hoping that today would pass quickly.

[Levitation]

I used magic.

My body floats up. I flew towards the 4th largest mountain in the south.

Flying wasn't all that comfortable. That's because black carapace eagles, excited by the influence of the Dark Moon, were flying madly at me.

[Electric Whip]

I fended off the flying bastards with an electric whip. The crisply grilled black carapace eagles fell to the ground and became food for other animals and monsters.

Did I kill about 30 of them?

I arrived at my destination.

I look around to get my bearings.

'I saw a cliff before I came down. I have to go that way.'

Kuoong!

My heart stopped with a loud noise.

Sudden heart attack.

I contorted my face and forcibly moved my heart using mana.

'Fucking hell. A heart attack from the start....'

I glared at the Dark Moon in the sky.

Different abnormal conditions occur in each region on the day the Dark Moon rises. In the outer districts of Neo London, it's heart attacks, random diseases, and hemiplegia. Of course, the abnormal conditions disappear after a short time and return to normal. The problem is that you don't know when and where the abnormal conditions will strike again.

After about 5 minutes, my stopped heart started beating again. I no longer needed to forcibly make my heart beat using mana. That alone made me quite comfortable.

The cliff appeared.

Looking down, I can see a vertically cut cliff. The bottom of the cliff is dark and deep, so I can't see the end.

'It's okay because I don't have to go down.'

I approached the cliff. Suddenly, the flow of mana changed and went wild. In this state, it was difficult for even me to use magic. That's why I didn't fly straight towards the cliff.

'I came to the right place. Now, then....'

I look around. I easily found a faint blue flower blooming on the edge of the cliff.

In this world, there are plants that only bloom when the Dark Moon rises. One of them is the blue flower in front of me, Blue Distortion.

It's a flower with the ability to distort mana. You must harvest it only when the flower is in bloom. When the flower is not in bloom, it's no different from ordinary weeds.

I took out a box from my coat. I pluck the petals of the Blue Distortion and put them in the prepared box. This flower has the ability to distort mana, as its name suggests.

'It's one of the essential ingredients when repairing a broken mana rod.'

Because it only blooms when the Dark Moon rises, and even then, only deep in the mountains, the price is very expensive. If you sell it on the black market, you can make a good profit.

'One root isn't enough.'

I looked towards the cliff.

'The cliff in front of the triangular rock... that's the one.'

Hoo.

I shook off the tension with a sigh and headed towards the cliff.

I braced myself, lifted my right foot, and stretched it out towards the cliff. My foot didn't fall down the cliff, but stepped onto an invisible floor.

'...Thankfully, I succeeded. I guess I don't have to clear the pre-quest after all.'

I pulled myself together. Ignoring the black moon looking down at me from above and the cool breeze blowing, I walked towards the cliff.

'7366. 7366. 7366.'

I repeated the numbers and walked forward. I stopped my legs exactly 7 steps away. Looking down, I can see the pitch-black cliff below. If I fell there, I would definitely die instantly.

'3 steps to the right.'

Step, step, step.

'6 steps to the left. 6 steps to the right.'

The moment I safely took my last step, a door appeared in the air. Without hesitation, I grabbed the doorknob and pushed.

It was a 3-pyeong space.

Blue sky, fresh air, moist soil.

There, Blue Distortions were blooming in a cluster. It looks like there are about 100 flowers.

'Success.'

I squatted down with a smile on my face. I harvest the petals of the Blue Distortion one by one.

'A certain gardener's secret garden. A secret space that you can only enter when the Dark Moon rises after clearing the sub-quest.'

As you can see, you can get a large amount of Blue Distortion by coming here. You can come again next time, but there will be no Blue Distortion.

After collecting all the Blue Distortions, I came out of the door. I was still in the air. But the flow of mana returned to normal. That's because I collected all the Blue Distortions, which were the cause of the mana flow disruption.

[Levitation]

I flew through the air and landed on the mountain.

I fell into worry.

Should I go back right away, or should I risk it and go get something else?

'Let's go back. Today isn't the only opportunity. First, let's go back and repair all the mana rods, and then come back on the next day the Dark Moon rises.'

There was no need to move in a hurry. I put my regrets aside and floated my body into the air.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion was heard.

I stopped in the air and turned my gaze to the direction of the explosion. The location is the right side of the mountain where the vacant lot is. It's where the hidden ancient ruins are.

'......Telescope.'

I enhanced my eyesight with magic.

I look down at that distant place. Bell Teier, whom I met in front of the village fountain a few hours ago, was fighting a Droid.

Droid.

Robots who have turned against humans.

The nobles and citizens of Neo London hate Droids to the point of trauma. That's because Neo London was once on the verge of destruction due to a Droid rebellion. It can also be said that the reason why Neo London is now similar to the Victorian era culture is due to the influence of Droids.

'Bell looks like she's at a disadvantage.'

I decided to help her. There was something I was curious about about her. If my guess is correct, she should have been somewhere else, not there.

I flew towards where Bell was and prepared a magic formula.

Pajijijik.

The mana of lightning soared into the sky and gathered.

The Droid, with its steel body and steel skin, raised its head and looked at the sky. Two red lenses shone and captured me.

In front of it, Bell quickly moved and pulled the trigger of her pistol. The bullets accurately aim for the Droid's head, but don't do significant damage.

,Magician detected. Estimated to be level 5. Changing target priority. Eliminating the human.'

The Droid raised its arm towards me. Clunk, clunk. The arm turns into a gun barrel and is aimed at me. Energy gathers at the end of the barrel and is about to be fired.

[Thunder Bolt]

Ahead of that, a huge thunderbolt fell towards the Droid.

The surroundings became as bright as day. The Droid fell to the ground and didn't move.

'As expected, electric magic works well on those machine bastards.'

I landed on the ground. She, who was wiping sweat from the back of her hand and gasping for breath, stared intently at me.

,Was it unnecessary interference?'

,...No, thank you. I owe you my life. That Droid bastard was giving me trouble because armor-piercing bullets weren't working at all.'

I looked behind her.

The machine door leading underground was intact on the land that had been messed up by the battle.

Ah, these are ancient ruins that I found by chance. Don't worry, it has nothing to do with the Droid bastards. No matter how much I freak out, that door won't break. Do you know how to get in? You're a magician, after all.

,I know.'

,...Really? I was just asking.... Good. Then help me out a bit. Let's split what we get here in half. I think I can demand about half since I found this. What do you think?'

,Before we split it, we need to check what's inside.'

,That's true too.'

I walked towards the door.

I stood right in front of the door. I stared intently at the mark engraved on the door. It's a pattern that resembles a lotus flower.

,Do you know what that pattern is? I searched for it, but it didn't come up.'

,It's the pattern of the Transcendentalists.'

,Transcendentalists? What's that? Are you talking about superhumans?'

,No.'

I said, looking at the door.

,Only transcendence is our ultimate goal.'

Jiiiiiiing.

The door opened.
