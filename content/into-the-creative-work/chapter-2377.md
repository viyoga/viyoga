The executives of Luminous continued their meeting via video chat.

Ongoing fundraising, slightly more difficult recruitment of members, and the new leader of the Order Faction, Seong Yujin-ssi.

"......"

"......"

As soon as Seong Yujin-ssi's name came up, the executives clammed up like ghosts.

Even though it hadn't been many days, Seong Yujin-ssi's name had become incredibly heavy. The news and media were praising him day after day for his achievement of killing the Sage of Remegeton. Seeing the change in the attitude of the media, which had initially slandered Seong Yujin-ssi, it was clear that the state had applied pressure.

"Jeon Sehun, who went to scout Seong Yujin-ssi, has disappeared. He probably died fighting Seong Yujin-ssi. According to the information we've received, Seong Yujin-ssi won against the commander of the 3rd Division."

The expressions of the executives listening to Bright, the leader of Luminous, were not good. Rumors were circulating that Seong Yujin-ssi's martial prowess was on par with the director of the Order Faction. And since the attempt to scout Seong Yujin-ssi had failed, that Seong Yujin-ssi was now a definite enemy of Luminous.

"We'll postpone any retaliation against Seong Yujin-ssi."

"Even the executive Jeon Sehun was taken down. If we do nothing and just move on, the morale of the members will drop. There will be guys who try to leave the organization. And you're saying we should do nothing anyway?"

"Seong Yujin-ssi appeared suddenly. Before we make a plan to deal with him, it's better to execute the plans we've already prepared. Seong Yujin-ssi? Even if he's strong, nothing will change. We will break down the Order Faction's suppression and turn this Republic of Korea into a Magic Korea."

Bright spoke without wavering. The executives nodded at his steadfast appearance.

Bright continued.

"The Sage was an executive of Remegeton who had been active for a long time. He was a kind of spiritual pillar, and a symbol of Remegeton itself. Since that symbol has been damaged, they won't stay still. Remegeton will move in some way. We will focus on our plans and remain silent for the time being. We'll check Remegeton's reaction, and if there are no problems, we'll execute the plan."

"Isn't it possible that Remegeton will antagonize us?"

"That's the worst-case scenario. We and Remegeton have a common enemy called the Order Faction. If we don't provoke Remegeton first... they won't provoke us either. Now, move for the greater cause. If the plans we've been preparing for years succeed... we'll be the ones laughing in the end."

• • •

People gathered in an abandoned building.

They were homeless people of various ages. From teenagers to old men with canes. They weren't the ordinary homeless people you'd typically think of.

Illegal magic users.

They were people who had somehow obtained a grimoire, awakened, and used magic, only to become magic criminals, Harriers.

They sighed at their wretched, impoverished lives and lamented their fate.

"I heard there's a facility for homeless magicians. If you go there, you won't have to worry about food at least."

"That's a story for ordinary magicians. You burned down your house with magic when you awakened, didn't you? You're already a Harrier. As soon as you go there, you'll be caught by the Anti-Magic Force and dragged off to prison, you know?"

"Shibal. It's not like I wanted to become a magician, but I'm treated like a criminal...."

They swallowed their resentment.

Even if they had the power of magic, it was nothing compared to the Anti-Magic Force, a specialized combat unit.

Kwaang!

The entrance of the abandoned building opened with a rough sound, and a group of people entered. They were members of Luminous, who had been helping them for several months.

At the center of them was a man with dyed yellow hair and squinting eyes. The homeless people felt intimidated because the men around him, including him, were also wearing matching suits.

Won Bihyun. He introduced himself to the homeless people as a member of the magician organization called Luminous, and he drew people's attention with applause.

"Now, today we've gone a little overboard for you all. We have bulgogi lunch boxes. Of course, we'll distribute them to you for free. Ah, is there anyone here who's considering entrusting themselves to the government?"

Won Bihyun's tongue was smooth. As his voice continued, people stared at him with blank eyes.

"The government is just spouting sweet lies to deceive you. If you were ordinary magicians, you would be treated well enough. But you are magic criminals, Harriers, who have committed petty and serious crimes. The government does not show leniency to Harriers. You entrust yourselves to the government? The government will throw you in jail without a second thought."

The homeless people nodded as if they were possessed. No, in fact, they were being possessed. The magic of the man on Won Bihyun's right was brainwashing. Brainwashing magic worked well on the clumsy magicians who couldn't even handle their magic power properly yet.

Gu Seohwa, wearing a baggy black coat, a black baseball cap pulled low, and a mask covering her face, covered the ears of her younger brother, Gu Seojun, who was 10 years younger than her.

She had sensed the brainwashing magic in his words.

Won Bihyun approached Gu Seohwa. He handed the siblings two lunch boxes with a fake smile.

"Gu Seohwa-ssi. We've been keeping an eye on your talent. Stop being stubborn and join Luminous. You can't keep living as a homeless person forever, can you?"

"...I have no intention of joining a terrorist group."

"Then go to the government. Ah, but the government will send a murderer to prison, won't they? Haha. The siblings will be separated? That's terrible. I feel sorry for Seojun-gun, who will be separated from his older sister."

"......"

Gu Seohwa's eyes trembled.

Half a year ago, her house was burglarized, and her parents were murdered by the burglar. Gu Seohwa and Gu Seojun returned home late and discovered their parents just after they had been murdered by the burglar. At that time, a grimoire appeared, and Gu Seohwa awakened as a magician. She killed the burglar with magic and fled with her younger brother.

Self-defense?

She had once heard that the self-defense law in this Korea was a law that existed in name only. Moreover, she was a magician. In Korea, magicians are subject to stricter legal standards than ordinary people.

Now just 20 years old, she didn't want to be separated from her 10-year-old younger brother, so she ran away. The place she arrived at was this abandoned building. A brainwashing site of Luminous, untouched by the police.

By the time she realized it, it was already impossible to escape. Luminous members were quietly monitoring the area around the abandoned building, managing who came and went.

"Here, take it. Bulgogi lunch boxes are delicious. You have to feed your brother, even if you don't eat, right?"

Won Bihyun said with a sneer. Gu Seohwa gritted her teeth, enduring the humiliation, and received the lunch box. She couldn't let her brother starve because of her pride.

"Gu Seohwa. Luminous highly values your magic. If you put your mind to it, you could even rise to an executive position. Think about your brother's future."

"......"

"The situation has changed a lot since the commander of the 8th Division appeared in the Order Faction. We don't have much time left. I hope to hear an answer that benefits us all next time."

Won Bihyun turned around. It was the moment Gu Seohwa was about to open the lunch box with a sigh.

The wind blew. The wind was strange. Instead of escaping through the open window, the wind was swirling inside the building as if checking the interior.

Gu Seohwa, a wind magician, felt the will and magic power in the wind. Her gaze turned to the entrance where the wind had started.

There was a man.

It was the uniform of the Anti-Magic Force belonging to the National Order Faction. She had heard that it was a uniform made with magic, so it had more defensive power than most bulletproof vests.

On his left arm, which was so ordinary that it didn't seem to fit in with this place, was a red armband. A red armband that only the commanders of the Anti-Magic Force wore. There was a clear black number 8 on it.

The meaning was simple. He was Seong Yujin-ssi, the commander of the 8th Division who was making headlines in the news and on the internet these days.

"So the losers were gathered here. I'll give you guys a special favor. Bow your heads."

People didn't know what to do at Seong Yujin-ssi's declaration. Won Bihyun's face was distorted in an ugly way.

"I didn't expect a commander-level person to come to a place like this himself. The other members... you didn't bring them. I guess you didn't know we were here?"

"You Harrier bastard, you talk too much. Didn't you hear me tell you to bow your head?"

Seong Yujin-ssi flicked his fingers. An invisible blade of wind aimed for Won Bihyun's neck. Won Bihyun sensed the magic power and quickly ducked his head to avoid the attack.

Gu Seohwa, who was watching the scene from behind, opened her mouth. She could tell because she was a wind magician. How cleanly Seong Yujin-ssi was handling the wind right now.

"Damn it! Kill him! You guys are still civilians who haven't been put on the wanted list! You'll be subdued, but you won't die! Attack him quickly!"

Won Bihyun's subordinate gave brainwashing magic to his voice. The homeless people, who had been continuously receiving brainwashing magic, charged at Seong Yujin-ssi with dazed eyes.

And Seong Yujin-ssi killed everyone who attacked him equally.

Whether they were children, young people, or old people, if they attacked with hostility, he cut them down with Wind Cutter.

It was an overwhelming scene of slaughter. Even if the magicians tried to attack with magic, they were cut down before the magic was completed. The wind was invisible and fast, so it wasn't easy to avoid.

In less than a minute, the space was filled with the smell of blood. The floor had become grotesque due to the entrails and flesh. Seong Yujin-ssi was floating in the air. He had lifted his body with the wind.

"This is crazy.... Is it okay for the Anti-Magic Force, which protects the justice of Korea, to massacre people? They are magicians, but most of them are civilians who haven't been put on the wanted list. They're not Harriers! You're a murderer who massacred civilians! If this fact becomes known to society, something very interesting will happen!"

"Huh? What nonsense are you talking about? You're the one who killed those poor civilians."

"......Huh?"

"The Harrier group Luminous committed terrorism as usual and massacred innocent citizens. And the commander of the 8th Division punished the evil of Luminous and protected the justice of the Republic of Korea. Sounds plausible, right?"

"Y-you mean... you're going to distort the truth and put all the blame on Luminous?!"

"You understood correctly."

"Can you still call yourself a champion of justice after doing that?!"

"Since I'm strong, aren't I justice?"

"You crazy bastard!! Luminous is the new system that will rule this Korea in the future! I won't let the likes of you smear it with shit!"

Won Bihyun grabbed his grimoire, his tie.

"Ars Magna, Blood Chase!"

All the blood inside the abandoned building floated into the air and gathered around Won Bihyun. The large amount of blood transformed into dozens of weapons such as swords, spears, and axes, aiming at Seong Yujin-ssi.

"Die here!!"

Dozens of weapons rushed towards Seong Yujin-ssi.
