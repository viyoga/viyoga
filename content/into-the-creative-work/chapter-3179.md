"Kill just one Hell Count and I become a Hell Duke. This is way easier than I thought?"

The requirement to become a Hell Duke is to control ten Hell territories.

A Hell Count controls half that—five. Meaning, if I kill a fellow Hell Count and take those five territories, I immediately get promoted to Hell Duke.

Alexandra shook her head at my words.

"Normally, it's a difficult task. Usually, you seize an enemy's territories through war. War requires extensive preparation. Even if you win, losses are inevitable. Even after becoming a Hell Baron, defeating established Barons and Viscounts who've prepared for war for ages is nearly impossible. And even if you somehow win and become a Count, the other Counts will keep you in check. From what I've gathered, most new Hell Counts don't even last a month before dying."

"Breaking virgin ground with your bare hands is fucking brutal, sure. But I'm different in that regard. It's not just the money—I've got military support from the surface too."

"Yes. Master is special. However, the situation isn't exactly favorable. Master's territory borders enemies on all sides. East, west, north, south. The enemy can invade from any direction."

"Can't I just hand over a few territories? Even if those bastards occupy them, as long as they can't kill me, they'll only eat the penalty."

The only way to strip a ruler of their control over a Hell territory is to kill them. In other words, unless they kill me, occupying my territory doesn't mean they control it.

"That is true, but all the resources flowing from those territories will be plundered. It may seem trivial in the short term, but over time it becomes detrimental."

"...I'm supposed to get plundered? I'd only imagined it, but it feels like absolute shit. What if I concentrate more than half my forces on defense?"

"That would be giving the enemy time. As Master said, Master is an enemy of Hell. Before Hell unites to kill Master, we must secure at least the combat power of a Hell Duke. So what we need to do is... kill the Hell Ogre Count to the south and take control of the Hell Ogre's Forest."

"What's so good about controlling that place?"

"Hell Ogres spawn naturally in the Hell Ogre's Forest at regular intervals. In other words, we can acquire troops."

The natural spawning of Hell Ogres isn't surprising. Demons and magical beasts in Hell territories are treated as half-monsters, so they spawn naturally. Just like monsters keep appearing on the surface.

"The troops I can currently deploy are..."

"One knight order from the Valdert family, three hundred desert warriors, and three thousand soldiers. The soldiers are well-trained elite troops."

The rest haven't arrived yet.

"Is this enough to take on that Hell Ogre Count?"

"Engaging them is possible. However, at this rate, we'll suffer heavy casualties. But those casualties can be reduced with money. With defense-specialized equipment and healing potions, soldiers who would die won't."

"So in the end, I have to spend money."

Wouldn't it be better to just let the soldiers die? I thought that, then realized the war is only beginning. Not knowing how many more wars I'd have to fight, I needed to save every single soldier I could.

'This is just the start—can't afford to blow it all now.'

Moreover, if the first war incurred too many losses, support from the Emperor and nobles could dry up.

'They'll only keep supporting me if I produce results. Tch. Looks like I've got no choice but to spend my own money here.'

I summoned the Hell Merchant.

"Leave dealing with the Hell Merchant to me."

Helvis said, pushing up her glasses. I entrusted the transaction to her, the former Chief Hell Merchant.

I boldly spent twenty million AP. Even then, I couldn't arm everyone, and one potion per person was impossible too.

"Master, please wait here. This Alexandra will surely bring back victory."

"No. I stand at the front of the legion."

"...If Master dies, everything ends."

"Do I look like I'd die to some blind attack? There's no one in this legion stronger than me. Frankly, even if all you mercenaries came at me at once, I'd win. I won the Grand Martial Arts Tournament—I am the strongest under heaven."

"...Understood. But if it seems dangerous, retreat immediately. Even if we lose the war and our forces, as long as Master lives, it's not defeat. Conversely, even if we win the war, if Master dies, everything is over."

"I know damn well how precious my life is."

-How arrogant. Hell is not the pushover you think it is.

When I furrowed my brow, Alexandra looked flustered.

I immediately relaxed my expression. The voice belongs to a man. I know full well it wasn't Alexandra who spoke.

There's no way I, of sound mind, have gone insane, so it's not an auditory hallucination. But it's a voice only I can hear.

I looked down at the Ring of Solomon on my right middle finger.

"Agares. I have no interest in a common demon trapped in a ring, so don't speak without my permission from now on."

No answer came back.

The army prepares to advance.

The Hell Ogre's territory to the south is half a day's march away at best speed. That's how close it is.

"Master. I caught enemy scouts and assassins snooping around the army. ...My skills were insufficient—two escaped."

One of the seven mercenaries, the human assassin Pia, reported. Her eyes were sharp beneath her red short-cut hair.

"Good work. Let me meet them before execution."

I followed Pia.

They were kneeling on the ground with hands and feet bound. Eight in total. Their bodies were covered in wounds as if they'd been tortured, clothes soaked in blood. Helvis and Alexandra stood before them.

I scanned the subdued bastards. Pitch-black skin and elongated ears. Their faces were handsome.

"Dark Elves?"

"Evil Elves. Traitors who turned their backs on the World Tree and submitted to evil. Well, in a way you could call them a type of Dark Elf."

The Elf archer Pia said. Her voice was dripping with contempt for them. And by extension, for Dark Elves not present. Pia seems to be an Elf racist.

Evil Elves have extremely black skin. Dark Elves at least have a brownish tone, but these bastards' skin is darkness itself.

"Where are these bastards from?"

This time Alexandra answered.

"The Hell Ogre Count's chief of staff sent them. Their first objective was to scout Master's forces, the second was to assassinate Master if the opportunity arose. Additionally, we learned that the Hell Ogre Count is preparing for war against Master."

"They just told you all that? Weren't assassins supposed to have mouths heavier than locks?"

"Because they're Evil Elves. These creatures never treat anyone sincerely. The most important thing to them is themselves. They're trash."

Pia said.

I walked toward the Evil Elves. Alexandra tried to stop me but I ignored her and approached. Even bound, approaching an assassin is a bad choice. A skilled assassin would attempt the kill even in this situation.

However, the Evil Elves immediately bowed their heads to the ground when I approached.

"New Hell Count! Please take us as your subordinates! If you accept us, we will follow whatever command you give!"

"Your heads are light. Do you not even know the word loyalty? Trash, just as Pia said."

"We merely chose a worthy master to pledge our loyalty to! The Hell Ogre Count is a low-intelligence fool! He doesn't deserve our true loyalty! But you are different! Please accept our loyalty!"

There were many eyes watching.

The moment I accepted their loyalty—lighter than dust—my honor and reputation would surely be damaged. And that would affect army morale.

'If they were pretty girls I might've accepted them despite that... but they're all just dudes.'

They had Elven beauty, but I wasn't gay.

"Even the time to talk is wasted. Cut off all their heads and display them."

I turned away. Behind me, the Evil Elves' screams erupted.

"Kyaaaaaaah!"

They soon fell silent.

「Your subordinate has killed an Evil Elf. 311 AP acquired.」

Even without killing them myself, AP came in. Of course, the subordinate who did the deed also gets a portion of the AP.

'So I can earn a certain amount of AP through war. Though I won't be able to earn more than what I invested in the army.'

Atlantis's Hell territories run on war.

「Entering Territory #9,310, the Hell Ogre's Forest.」

「You are gradually losing your sanity.」

The Hell Ogre's Forest was too sparse to call a forest. The trees weren't ordinary—they're called ironwood. Processed a special way, they yield steel. Brown grass grew ankle-high on the ground. Nothing particularly special.

"Advance."

My army marched forward.

About three hours passed. Low-intelligence magical beasts suddenly wandered off, whined for food, or sat down and fell asleep. The worse ones bared their teeth at allies.

It was because of this territory's penalty—gradually losing sanity. As rationality dropped, instinct raised its head.

"Execute on the spot any magical beast that turns hostile to allies. I have no need for dogs that bare their teeth at their master."

Dozens of magical beasts died and became food for allied demons and beasts.

The army, having advanced further, stopped.

The enemy forces were encamped and waiting.

A force of ten thousand—massive five-meter Hell Ogres, Black Orcs snorting steam as they flexed their muscles, Evil Elves quietly biding their time, and common demons and magical beasts.

An enemy force twice the size of ours. That alone dampened our momentum. The penalty of gradually losing sanity made them more easily frightened than usual.

I stepped to the front of the army.

Far off, at the center of the enemy lines, I could see the five-headed Hell Ogre. The one incongruously dressed in noble's clothing. That's the Hell Ogre Count.

No need for words.

I summoned Stormbreaker, gripped it in my hand, and raised it high.

"Astrafe."

Chzzzzzzzt!

Blue-white lightning wrapped around Stormbreaker and spun.
