'...I couldn't kill her.'

Moon Ji-hyuk gritted his teeth. He had actually been trying to snipe Doris with his revolver. He aimed squarely at the back of her head, intending to kill her for sure, but instead, he mistakenly stepped on a fallen leaf on the ground, making a rustling sound.

'I missed a golden opportunity.'

Doris used teleportation to get out of his sight. Fortunately, she didn't move far, so he was able to locate Doris's position immediately. Moon Ji-hyuk aimed his revolver at Doris, who was behind a tree.

"A Vampire Hunter...? No, that reaction speed is closer to a vampire."

Doris judged whether Moon Ji-hyuk was human or vampire based solely on his reaction speed.

'She's a Kantra executive... she's no pushover.'

Moon Ji-hyuk tensed his body and held a knife in his left hand. It was a silver-plated knife he had received from Taker before.

"If you're a vampire, why are you interfering with me? I am Doris Ziegler, the 14th True Blood of Kantra. Who are you?"

"I have no reason to give you my name. I'll take the Bead of Life."

Moon Ji-hyuk assessed the situation. Even if he couldn't kill Doris here, he had to take the Bead of Life.

"You're displaying excessive greed, nameless vampire."

Doris used teleportation to get behind Moon Ji-hyuk. Moon Ji-hyuk reacted quickly and swung his knife behind him, but Doris easily dodged it and grabbed Moon Ji-hyuk's wrists, pinning him against a tree.

"Keueuk."

"This strength.... You're not a Progenitor. ...How did you find out about the Bead of Life? Who is behind you...."

Bang!

Moon Ji-hyuk's revolver spat fire. He twisted his wrist until it was about to break, forcibly creating an angle and pulling the trigger. A silver bullet grazed Doris's left shoulder.

"Kaaaaa...!"

Doris used teleportation to move away from Moon Ji-hyuk. Doris frowned as she looked at her left shoulder.

Silver bullets are too fatal to vampires. It was just a graze, but the silver poison is eating away at her flesh. At this rate, she won't be able to move her left shoulder for at least 10 minutes.

'Who is that bastard? Where did the information leak from? Did Kang Wook-sung betray me?'

Her mind becomes complicated in an instant. In the morning, she thought that she was being followed by a Vampire Hunter. But it's not a Vampire Hunter? Then who is it?

'There's no way a mere vampire would know information about the Bead of Life... or information about me. Whether it's a traitor or a new organization, I need to find out the information.'

An invisible organization. She needs to find out the information for the future of Kantra.

Doris's expression soon twisted. It was because that bastard was recklessly rushing towards the Flower of Life.

"Ah, no!"

Doris hurriedly used teleportation to appear in front of Moon Ji-hyuk. She kicks Moon Ji-hyuk in the abdomen, sending him flying far away.

Moon Ji-hyuk flew backward, hit a tree, and rolled on the ground. Doris is a Progenitor. Her superpowers are secondary; there was a difference from the start in physical abilities.

"Since you're also a vampire, you won't die even if you lose your arms and legs. After I retrieve the Flower of Life, I'll slowly extract the information from you!"

"Hmm. I am curious about that young man's background."

"Who are you!"

Doris turned her head sharply to the right. There was someone else.

Ba-seulak ba-seulak. He slowly revealed himself, stepping on fallen leaves. He was a man wearing a dopo. He had a sword at his waist. He had attire and weapons that didn't fit the current era.

"......Baek Sang-gi?"

Doris muttered as if she couldn't believe it when she saw him.

"Do you know me?"

"......I heard about you from the boss. That there was a monstrous vampire slayer in the Korean peninsula. However, the boss said that you would have committed suicide because you couldn't overcome the years. But... you're still alive."

"I'm about to die soon."

Baek Sang-gi approached him. Doris took out a knife from her pocket and threw it at Baek Sang-gi. It was a knife imbued with the superior power of a Progenitor.

Baek Sang-gi's sword was drawn from his waist and lightly deflected the knife. When Baek Sang-gi tried to rush at her, Doris clicked her tongue and stepped back. Baek Sang-gi kept his eyes on Doris and said to Moon Ji-hyuk behind him.

"Are you alright, young man?"

Moon Ji-hyuk leaned against a tree and forced himself to get up. His whole body ached from Doris's one kick. If he were human, he would have died instantly.

"Who are you?"

Moon Ji-hyuk asked politely. The man Doris called a Vampire Slayer earlier.

"I am Baek Sang-gi. I have many things I want to ask, but... let's put them all aside and ask just one thing. What are you trying to do with the Bead of Life?"

"......I'm trying to return to being human."

"It's been a long time since I've met a young man I like. What is your name?"

"I am Moon Ji-hyuk."

"A good name. You will become human today. Watch from there."

Baek Sang-gi moved. He, who had been walking leisurely, began to run, moving his legs quickly. Doris bit her lip and glanced at the Flower of Life.

'The second one hasn't even formed yet.'

She can use teleportation to take the Bead of Life and run away. Her ability is second to none when it comes to escaping.

But there's no way she can be satisfied with just one. How long has she been looking forward to this day? She has to take at least two.

Safety and greed.

Doris chose greed.

The blades of the sword and knife collide in the air. The sound of metal echoes repeatedly, and orange sparks fly.

The advantage in physical ability lies with Doris.

'But why am I being pushed back?!'

The combat experience was on a different level.

When Doris tries to push forward with strength and speed, Baek Sang-gi's sword moves exquisitely to deflect the force. And the sword, aiming for that gap, cuts Doris's body. She dealt with it in a hurry, so she wasn't cut deeply. But it is clear that the wounds will increase over time.

Doris was grateful that Baek Sang-gi's sword was not silver. If that sword had been silver-plated, there would have been no chance of winning long ago.

"Your left shoulder doesn't seem to move well."

"Why did a monster like you suddenly appear? Were you tempted by the Flower of Life?"

"I'm not interested in the Bead of Life, but as a human, I have to prevent the Bead of Life from falling into the hands of you True Bloods, wouldn't you agree?"

"A monster more monstrous than a vampire."

"I've heard that so many times that I don't feel anything anymore."

Doris swung her knife and immediately used teleportation.

Appearing behind her in a swung state, it could be said to be the perfect surprise attack. But this time too, the sword was blocked.

"Unbelievable, how...."

"True Blood. I saw your superpower earlier and know it. If I know it, I can prepare for it, and if I can prepare for it, blocking it is a piece of cake."

That's bullshit. This is an attack that you can't block even if you know it. The reason Baek Sang-gi blocked Doris's attack is because of the tremendous experience accumulated in his body.

At that time. Moon Ji-hyuk moved. He, who had been quietly approaching the Flower of Life, suddenly began to run with all his might.

"This bastard!"

Doris shouted at Moon Ji-hyuk.

"To take your eyes off me. You're too relaxed."

Baek Sang-gi's sword split Doris's left arm. Her left arm fell to the floor with a thud.

Doris used teleportation. Right now, her left arm is not important. Appearing above Moon Ji-hyuk, she grabbed Moon Ji-hyuk's head with her right arm and slammed it into the ground.

"Die! You who don't know your place!"

Just as Doris's right fist was about to fall on Moon Ji-hyuk's head to smash it, Baek Sang-gi jumped up and swung his sword. Doris hurriedly rolled on the floor and stepped back.

"Baek Sang-gi!"

"Did you think I was so easy to underestimate that you could ignore me, True Blood?"

Moon Ji-hyuk put his palms on the floor and raised his head. His face was a mess, with his skin torn and his nose sunken. He trembled, but he raised his upper body.

Ten steps.

The Flower of Life was only ten steps away.

"Yes, young man. That's the will. Go and take the Bead of Life."

"You! Stop! The Flower of Life can bear up to 4 Beads of Life! Don't you know that?!"

Doris shouted.

Baek Sang-gi clicked his tongue. He wanted to go and shut her mouth, but if he moved away from Moon Ji-hyuk, Doris would surely try to kill Moon Ji-hyuk with teleportation.

"......4?"

Moon Ji-hyuk paused.

He knew about the effects of the Bead of Life.

If there are 4, even if he eats 2, half will remain.

"Let me give you some advice as an old man. Greed only brings disaster."

Baek Sang-gi said.

"......I will become human."

Moon Ji-hyuk said, shaking off his greed, and Baek Sang-gi smiled.

"This foolish bastard...!"

Doris burst into anger, holding a knife in her only remaining right hand and watching Baek Sang-gi's movements.

Moon Ji-hyuk staggered but walked forward. He was getting closer and closer to the Flower of Life. The distance of ten steps became a distance of five steps, and five steps soon became a distance that could be reached with just an outstretched hand.

"Stop! Detective Moon!"

A familiar voice. It's Taker.

Moon Ji-hyuk felt a slight sense of joy and raised his head towards the direction of the voice.

"......!"

There, Taker, Seong Yujin, was gagging Yang Jeong-min with his left hand and holding a red blade to her neck.

"Taker...?!"

Moon Ji-hyuk collapsed to the ground. His legs gave way to the unbelievable sight before his eyes. His brain is unable to accept the information he sees.

Why is Taker with Yang Jeong-min? And that red sword he's holding.

"......That sword. Were you Jeok-gwang?"

Seong Yujin looked at Baek Sang-gi.

Seong Yujin then smiled bitterly and said.

"That's right. I am Jeok-gwang."

It was like a bolt from the blue for Moon Ji-hyuk.
