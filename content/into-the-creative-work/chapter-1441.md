10:30 AM.

Thirty minutes since the grand opening.

Not a single customer has visited my shop in the last thirty minutes.

My face hardened, and Karel, my part-timer, quietly watched me. I quietly sank into thought.

,What's gone wrong?'

Before opening the shop, I posted promotional messages on the Neo London community. I even created a YuYu Chicken SNS and posted there. I got about 30 likes.

It became 11 o'clock.

The number of customers who visited the store so far was 0.

What's the problem?
