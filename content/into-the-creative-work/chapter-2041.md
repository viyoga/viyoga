Kakemasa wreathed his entire body in demonic energy and blocked my attack.

The shockwave from the collision swept the surroundings, and the temple and ground shattered. I stepped back to regain my stance.

,I lack information about that bastard.'

My first full-power attack was blocked without a problem. Since I didn't have enough information about Kakemasa, I needed to be cautious.

Kakemasa, wreathed in demonic energy, grew to over 3m in size. Other parts of his body also changed. His eyes turned blood-red, and his teeth became as sharp as a shark's. Tattoo-like patterns appeared on his face and fists.

『The Demon Hunter (S) desires the death of the demon!!』

The Demon Hunter (S) trait reacts violently. Kill him. Kill him. It feels like it's whispering that.

"What kind of demon did you make a contract with?"

I could tell just from the energy I felt. Kakemasa had contracted with at least a Count-class demon.

"That's not information I'd give to a Demon Hunter."

Kakemasa kicked off the ground. The ground shattered, and a shockwave spread. Kakemasa's large body accelerated in an instant, despite its size. But I could see all of Kakemasa's movements.

,I overestimated that bastard too much.'

I had prepared this and that to face a cadre of Madojeong. I also strengthened the Demon Hunter (S) by killing demons and devils.

But when I opened the lid, what was inside was nothing special.

,I was too wary because I was once defeated by Madojeong.'

I dodged the fist coming for my head and swung my sword in retaliation.

Thunderclap Flash of the Thunder Sky Style.

The greatsword couldn't cut deeply into Kakemasa's body. His flesh was as hard as if it were made of another metal. Even with Sword energy, it only left a scratch.

Teop.

Kakemasa grabbed the greatsword.

"Your attack is fast. So fast that even my eyes can barely keep up. But I don't think I need to avoid it."

Meteor Sword.

A giant sword was created in the sky and fell towards Kakemasa.

Kakemasa reacted immediately. He punched the falling giant sword. The Meteor Sword shattered and scattered.

"Indeed, is your ability a strong body and somewhat special eyes?"

"They are the Eyes of the Immovable Lord."

"More like demon eyes."

I approached Kakemasa and created another greatsword, swinging it. The lightning and storm carried on the greatsword struck his right shoulder. Blood splattered from Kakemasa's shoulder. Exactly 2 drops.

Kakemasa responded by swinging his fist without any change in expression. I raised my greatsword to deflect the fist.

,He seems to have learned some martial arts, but it's sloppy.'

Or maybe my eye for martial arts has just gotten better.

Thunder Sky Style Thunderous Blade.

A power mixed with Sword energy and lightning swirled on the blade. Did he sense something unusual? Kakemasa widened his eyes and tried to create distance. But my sword was swung first.

Blood splattered.

A cut was drawn across his tough body as if a crack had formed.

But it wasn't a fatal wound. Even though I used Sword energy, it was just a cut like one made with a box cutter. Kakemasa's durability is insane.

[Wow. That body might be tougher than a suit?]

,To take that little damage even after being hit by Thunderous Blade... it's definitely tougher than a suit.'

[How about we retreat for now and come up with a plan?]

,All he has is a tough body, what's there to plan for.'

When I had a little leeway, I checked his status window.

『Name: Misaisu Kakemasa

Strength: S+ Stamina: A Agility: B- Durability: SS Mana: B

Trait: Indomitable Body (A)

Skills: Weak Fist (B), Third Eye (S), Combat Recovery (C+)

Trust: 13』

As expected, his strength and durability are bizarrely high. His trait is also a physical ability.

,Combat Recovery must be the ability to recover during combat.'

It didn't matter.

If I beat him until he reaches his limit, I'll be the one to win.

I stepped forward, dodging Kakemasa's attacks and unilaterally swinging my greatsword. Before I knew it, the battle had turned into a fight of martial arts against martial arts.

Weak Fist (B)? I don't know what kind of martial art it is, but its limitations were clear. Compared to the Thunder Sky Style, it's not even a third-rate martial art. The forms are somewhat systematic, but they're just things he seems to have seen and copied. The outside looks decent, but there's no substance.

The ensuing exchange turned one-sided. Because I had completely grasped Kakemasa's martial arts.

"Keuek, why! Why can't my fist reach you?!"

"Even if you can see all my movements, catching up is another matter."

A strong body and excellent eyes.

He would overwhelm most opponents. Because he probably hasn't met an opponent who has properly mastered martial arts in this world.

"As long as you only rely on your eyes, you won't be able to avoid my sword."

From my perspective, Kakemasa, who mainly uses his body, was easier to deal with than those who use all sorts of bizarre abilities.

40 minutes.

The time I spent relentlessly beating his incredibly tough body with my greatsword.

Kakemasa's body gradually became dull, and his knees fell to the ground as if his strength had given out. His giant body, which had been over 3m tall, shrank back to its original size.

"To think I would meet such an end. I never expected it. I couldn't even keep up with your skills...."

"I have something to ask. The remaining cadres of Madojeong, there are 2 of them. Where are they?"

"So that's what you're asking. To answer, I don't know either."

"You're a cadre. And you still don't know?"

"The tasks assigned to those two and me are different. Those two are focused on the ritual, and I am in charge of managing Madojeong. There must be no problems until the ritual takes place."

"You must have a place you're guessing they are? Tell me."

"Haha. Even though I've lost, I have no intention of begging for my life."

"I never said I'd spare you. Tell me if you want to die peacefully."

"I cannot say."

Interrogation time has come.

I interrogated Kakemasa using the torture techniques I knew. The result was my defeat. He didn't say anything until the end.

If I took him somewhere and tortured him all day, information might have come out... but unfortunately, he committed suicide. He blew up his own heart with mana in the brief moment I looked away.

,Even if I had a way to revive him, it wouldn't be worth using on this bastard.'

I killed a cadre who managed Madojeong. That alone was a definite achievement. Plus, my Karma went up by one.

[Even if you didn't get information about the other Madojeong cadres, you did hear about the demons under Madojeong. What are you going to do?]

,I still have time left, so I should take care of them.'

If they're subordinates of Madojeong, they must be demons. To grow the Demon Hunter (S) even a little, I have to kill them all without discrimination.

[Ew. I smell blood. Are you okay with killing so many people?]

,Of course not. The guys I killed come to my mind even in my dreams. But I have to act for justice.'

[At least wet your lips before you lie.]

I shrugged my shoulders and moved to kill the other bastards.

Whatever else, Arashi Academy was serious about the cultural festival. No, all of Japan was serious about the cultural festival.

I was dumbfounded to see people waiting at the academy entrance from the crack of dawn. Moreover, there were people with professional cameras, probably from broadcasting stations, here and there.

I understood why the academy students had been preparing for the cultural festival for so long. They had to prepare properly to avoid embarrassment.

So, what was I doing on the day of the cultural festival... I was wearing a butler's uniform. Originally, Class 1-4 had prepared something else, but due to an incident in the middle, they hastily changed it to a maid &amp; butler cafe. Because they could just get maid and butler uniforms.
