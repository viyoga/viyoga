I cleared the dungeon and handed over the dungeon's information to the Association. Information on newly appearing monsters and murals within the dungeon.

The Association accepted the information we provided with a cautious attitude, without making a fuss.

It might be a great discovery and a special experience for Han Ha-rin-ssi and me, but for the Association, our information is routine. Worldwide, dozens of dungeons are cleared every day, and new information obtained from dungeons is constantly increasing.

,Well, I got paid by the Association for providing the information. The rest is up to the Association.

More important to me than this was the B-rank Hunter promotion exam in three weeks. I was confident, but the promotion exam above C-rank was by no means easy. The success rate for the B-rank promotion exam is only about 20%.

,In the end, what matters in the promotion exam is specs. Most C-ranks can't get promoted unless they're Hunters who have awakened mana, and B-ranks need to have the power befitting a B-rank.

The main content of the B-rank promotion exam was clearing a dungeon with other examinees. If you pull a troll move here, or if you lack prior knowledge and cooperation between Hunters isn't smooth, you'll be mercilessly dropped from the exam.

,If I fail once, I can't try again for three months, so I should prepare a little, though.

And three weeks later.

I passed the promotion exam and became a B-rank Hunter.

•••

Having entered the world of God's Atlantis, I checked my status window.

「Name: Seong Yujin

Class: Lightning Severing Master

Title: Fate Destroyer

Godhood: Lord of the Sky

Affiliation: AL 401 Earth

Strength: 81 Agility: 70 Stamina: 76 Mana: 91 Luck: 32

Unique Trait: Deception (SS)

Trait: Lightning (S)

Skills: Astrape (B), Manroe (B), Lightning Speed (D)

(Applying status window)」

I worked as a member of A-Plan, clearing areas and raising my stats through training. Of course, I didn't do it myself; I made full use of auto-progression.

,Nice.

I felt it as soon as I came to this world. I am much stronger now than I am in reality. This body can aim for at least an A-rank Hunter.

,If I spin the Cheonma status window, it'll be even better.

Speaking of Cheonma, the error in Cheonma Shingong comes to mind.

The error in Cheonma Shingong discovered by the Cheongang Star System in the [Gwangmyeong Ascension] world.

,There's no way a mistake could have accidentally occurred in the gyeol of Cheonma Shingong.

Cheonma Shingong was created not by a human, but by a god.

And it's a martial art created by a god who, even if you only consider their status, belongs to the highest class.

,Martial arts don't produce the right effect if even a small part of the gyeol is different. Especially Cheonma Shingong. It's a martial art more precise than any complex machine. There's no way the King of Devil Heaven could have made a mistake.

No matter how I think about it, it wasn't a mistake, but he deliberately changed it and told me.

,The problem is, I don't know why.

The Cheonma Shingong that Cheongang Star modified became more efficient, but that was all. The demonic nature of Cheonma Shingong disappeared? No way. Cheonma Shingong isn't Cheonma Shingong for nothing. There's no way its demonic nature would disappear just by changing some of the gyeol.

Cheonma Shingong is a martial art that uses the devil. Without the devil (魔), Cheonma Shingong is no better than trash.

No matter how much I rack my brain, it's just speculation and I can't be sure.

,Let's use Cheonma Shingong directly. The King of Devil Heaven must still be watching me, so he'll react somehow.

「Name: Seong Yujin

Class: Cheonma (Heavenly Demon)

Title: Immortal

Godhood: King of Devil Heaven

Affiliation: AL 401 Earth.

Strength: 81 Agility: 70 Stamina: 76 Mana: 91 Luck: 32

Unique Trait: Deception (SS)

Trait: Cheonma Physique (A)

Skills: Cheonma Shingong (S) Subordinate (S) Mafung Shingong Transfer (SSS)

(Applying status window)」

,King of Devil Heaven. If you tampered with Cheonma Shingong to kill me.... I will never let you get away with it.

I can't just take it lying down, no matter what. If I get punched once, I have to stab them with a knife to feel better; that's my nature.

,No matter what it takes.

I began circulating my energy while reciting the gyeol of Cheonma Shingong, which the Cheongang Star System had corrected for errors. And as soon as I succeeded in circulating the Cheonma Shingong, a system notification window popped up.

「You have gained enlightenment.」

「You awaken the true Cheonma Shingong.」

「Cheonma Shingong (S) rises to SSS rank.」

「Cheonma Physique (A) rises to S rank.」

「Unbelievable achievement.」

「You have gained the qualifications of a true Cheonma by realizing Cheonma Shingong.」

The title 'Cheonma-jon (天魔尊)' is granted.

「All stats increase by 5.」

,...What?

I frowned. It wasn't just because of the unexpected notification window. Other uses of Cheonma Shingong were entering my head.

It's not a stance. It's something alien that can't be explained by stances. It's closer to magic or spells.

,At least it's not harmful to me.

「The King of Devil Heaven is shocked!」

「The system is shocked.」

「The system suspects the King of Devil Heaven!」

「The system is checking the King of Devil Heaven's logs!」

「The King of Devil Heaven is frustrated.」

「I swear on my name! I swear I didn't do anything!」

「The system still suspects the King of Devil Heaven.」

"What the hell is going on? Let me know too. Explain it to me."

I said to the system and the King of Devil Heaven. Originally, it is forbidden to give information to exiles or continentals according to the rules. But the current case may be an exception. I already got the reward. What I wanted was an explanation for the reward given.

「The King of Devil Heaven sponsors 1,000 AP.

"The Cheonma Shingong I originally gave you was in a degraded state."」

"So you deliberately tampered with some of the gyeol of Cheonma Shingong. Did you plant a trap or something?"

When I spoke with hostility and murderous intent, the King of Devil Heaven's message urgently popped up.

「The King of Devil Heaven shakes his head and denies it.」

「The King of Devil Heaven sponsors 1,000 AP.

"I had no choice. If I hadn't deliberately degraded Cheonma Shingong, I wouldn't have been able to give it to you. The system wouldn't have allowed it due to balance issues."」

"The Lord of the Sky gave me lightning without a second thought."

「The King of Devil Heaven sponsors 1,000 AP.

"He and I are different. Likewise, lightning and Cheonma Shingong are different."」

"......That's true."

I couldn't deny the King of Devil Heaven's words. The Lord of the Sky's status is higher than the King of Devil Heaven's.

The Lord of the Sky, Zeus, is a chief god and represents a mythology. He could be said to be one of the highest gods of Atlantis.

The lightning that Zeus gave me is SS rank. This alone is an epic and enormous sponsorship.

,Generally, the value of skills is higher than that of items. Considering the King of Devil Heaven's position.... It's not that I can't understand.

If he hadn't degraded Cheonma Shingong, the system wouldn't have allowed him to give me Cheonma Shingong.

,I should let this go. If I insist on this, my relationship with the King of Devil Heaven will be strained. I can't afford to have a strained relationship with him yet. There's so much I can squeeze out of him.

I closed my eyes and circulated Cheonma Shingong. Perhaps because it rose to SSS rank, circulating it became much easier than before.

「The system still suspects the King of Devil Heaven.」

「The King of Devil Heaven seems to be going crazy with frustration.」

「I never told him! If you investigated, you'd know too! System! You know that guy is weird!」

「The system requests an explanation from Seong Yujin.」

I was going to ignore it, but I opened my mouth.

"I was having sex and suddenly gained enlightenment."

「The system does not believe your lie.」

"Hey, System. Have you ever had sex?"

「The system says that sex is nothing more than a reproductive activity accompanied by pleasure.」

"Judging by the way you talk, you haven't. Sex is the truth. What's so strange about enlightenment suddenly coming while practicing the truth?"

「The Lord of the Sky nods.」

「The King of Devil Heaven chuckles.」

「The system sighs.」

The system didn't question me after that. It knows I won't give a proper answer.

I closed my eyes again and focused on Cheonma Shingong.

When I opened my eyes again, Elena was sitting on the sofa opposite me. She was quietly picking and eating grapes one by one. Looking at the bare grape branches on the table, it seems she's already eaten three.

"If you came, you should have said something."

"Would I bother you when I clearly know you're in mana training?"

Bothering someone or shouting to break their concentration during mana training is the same as attempted murder. If mana explodes or flows backward in the body, internal injuries are basic, and there is a very high possibility of becoming disabled or dying.

"How rude. To watch someone else's mana training while eating grapes. Is that okay because you're a Duchess?"

"That's right. If this wasn't my office, I would have immediately knelt down and begged you for forgiveness."

In conclusion, the fault was mine. I came into her office and recklessly circulated Cheonma Shingong.

I know it's a hasty act. But it can be said that my relationship with Elena has become that much closer.

,I'm not happy that we're not getting closer romantically, though.

Even if someone else had come instead of her, it wouldn't have mattered. It's not like Cheonma Shingong is a martial art that can be understood by looking at it from the outside, and even if someone touches me, I have Complete Recovery.

"The Decennial Festival starts in three months."

Elena, who swallowed a grape, got to the point.

Decennial Festival.

A grand festival held every 10 years in the capital of the Yustia Empire.

A festival to thank the past 10 years and pray for peace for the next 10 years.

This Decennial Festival is especially meaningful. This is the first Decennial Festival to be held in Atlantis.

"Unexpected. I didn't think you'd be interested in the Decennial Festival."

Elena didn't have much interest in the Decennial Festival. She didn't even attend the Decennial Festival in the original story. The Decennial Festival is held in the capital, and even if she attends, it will only make the Imperial Family, the closest aides of the Emperor, shine, and there's not much to gain.

"I'm not interested in the Decennial Festival itself. I want to ignore it if I could."

"Then ignore it."

"I've been up to a lot of things lately, so I can't. If I remain silent and don't move here, those who look down on me will run wild without knowing their place. I'm already busy, so I don't have the luxury to deal with those things one by one. And above all, the Imperial Family and all the other Empire Ogong are pressuring me to go up to the capital during the festival."

"It's not like you'll die if you go to the capital, so what's the big deal?"

Even if she goes to the capital, all she'll hear is a word or two from the Emperor or other Dukes.

The Crown Prince, her main enemy, is not at a level where he can say anything to Elena. In the Yustia Empire, the power of the Dukes is something even the Emperor can't ignore. It's not called the pillar of the Empire for nothing.

"There's something you need to do before the Decennial Festival takes place."

"As long as it's not something ridiculous."

"And I want to take the A-Plan Legion with me this time."

"What? Why?"

"Supporting Legions and forming close relationships is what's trending among the Imperial nobles these days. I can't be left behind by the trend. I've been sponsoring the A-Plan Legion since before. Don't I have the qualifications?"

Close relationship, my ass. There's almost no one who doesn't know that the nobles are trying to exert influence through the Legions.

"...The A-Plan Legion is far inferior to you."

"I know that the A-Plan Legion is gaining popularity. With your and the Legion Master's reputation, it's enough to decorate my name."

I pondered for a while and then nodded roughly.

There was nothing bad for the A-Plan Legion. Other Legions will be jealous, but that's all. The A-Plan Legion can handle it now.

"Tell the A-Plan Legion Master the details. I'm not the Legion Master."

"I intend to."

"What's the thing I have to do?"

"It's to deliver a letter to one of the Empire Ogong."

"An errand? Sounds easy."

"It won't necessarily be that easy."

"What's the reward?"

"What do you want?"

"I know there's a ball at the Decennial Festival. I want to attend that ball as your partner."

"...A blatant ploy. But well, I owe you a favor, so I'll grant it."

Before getting up from my seat, I asked Elena.

"Elena, how much do you trust me?"

"I trust you about 60%."

I grinned.

Perhaps the person Elena trusts the most in this world is me.
