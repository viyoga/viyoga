,The first method that comes to mind is... a trap.'

I recall the first day I entered the Lumel Forest.

I used air traps to deal with the pursuers who caught up to me then.

,...I'll give up on traps. The number of traps I can set is limited.'

I know how to make booby traps, but I don't even have bombs to use right now.

The magic, Air Trap, consumes a lot of mana.

,More than anything, there's a high possibility that they know about Air Traps.'

Judging by the number of people chasing us, it's certain that Major Vag of the 105th unit and the Resistance have joined forces. Then, of course, they would have shared information about Air Traps.

There are 15 pursuers chasing me. Two of them are Class 1 Mana Awakeners.

And I am a Class 3 Battle Mage.

The calculation is complete.

,I'll fight. I'll kill the enemies and rise from prey to hunter.'

I quietly checked my equipment.

After about 5 minutes, I vividly felt their presence. They revealed themselves between the trees. They aimed their rifles at me and quickly scattered. They advanced towards me, using the trees as cover.

Unlike the 105th unit, which was annihilated by the Air Trap on the first day, they were cautious.

,I didn't think you'd be standing still here. Did you run around all day and get so tired that your legs won't move?"

,You guys must be tired too, right?"

,We took turns chasing you. And unlike you guys, we adapted to the Lumel Forest early on. Stop the meaningless resistance and surrender."

,Surrender? I was going to say that first."

,I heard you're a Battle Mage, but you're quite arrogant. Are you trusting that paper-thin barrier? What are you doing, shoot! Let's show that arrogant bastard that magic or whatever is meaningless in the face of numbers!"

They pulled the triggers all at once from all directions.

Sensing a small explosion of mana, I immediately used Chrona. The world slows down. I can see the bullets fired from the muzzles.

,Mana Bullets. There are so many. At this rate, even a Class 3 barrier will be easily shattered. I'm glad I used Chrona as soon as I felt the mana.'

They made a mistake.

They approached within 10m to make sure they killed me.

,My telekinesis range is 15m. They've entered my range.'

It's difficult to twist their necks with telekinesis as they are.

Living things, including humans, innately possess a small amount of magic resistance. Telekinesis is a magic that is greatly affected by the target's mental strength and magic resistance. It's more efficient to grab objects than to directly grab the target's body with telekinesis.

,Mana Bullets are a bit tough, but... it's better than grabbing a person's body directly.'

I decided to use telekinesis to catch all the incoming Mana Bullets. A total of 72 bullets. It was a lot for 15 people to shoot.

,They were planning to kill me for sure, even considering the barrier.'

There are too many Mana Bullets. My ability is not enough to control them all.

,I'll give up on complete control and just change the direction of the Mana Bullets. As long as they don't come towards me.'

As soon as I activated the Mana Rod, Chrona was released. This means one thing. The movement of mana is also affected by Chrona. In other words, the Mana Rod is also accelerated. The formula was already calculated, so there was nothing to hold back.

The activation of telekinesis was faster than the speed of the Mana Bullets.

The flying Mana Bullets changed direction and began to rampage. Dozens of colorful trajectories of red, blue, green, yellow, and purple were drawn in the air. It was like watching a kind of artwork. However, the result was too terrible to be called art.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!"

,My arm!!!"

,H, help me. My legs are frozen!"

,Aaaaaaaak!"

One red Mana Bullet passed by me. It hit a tree and caused a small explosion. It's an exploding bullet.

,The tree is on fire. Another pursuer will come right away.'

Six people survived in the midst of the rampaging Mana Bullets. Of course, just because they survived doesn't mean they were unscathed.

I picked up my rifle and approached them to finish them off. I need to conserve mana. There's no need to use magic to kill those who have lost their combat power.

The gunshots, which felt even dry, rang out six times.

I left the scene before another pursuer arrived.

,I was nervous because there was a Mana Awakener, but... they're not much different from ordinary people. Is it because they're Class 1?'

•••

6 AM.

Around the time the sun began to rise, I found their temporary base and killed the 8 people who were left there. It was the 105th unit. The tents that made up the base belonged to the Freesel Kingdom army.

I burned the tents and took the supplies. The top priority is food, and then bullets. Unfortunately, there weren't many Mana Bullets.

,This is absurd."

A dignified, heavy voice was heard from behind.

I was startled. Even though I was tired, I didn't expect someone to approach me, piercing my senses. Cold sweat ran down my neck. I had prepared a barrier, but it wasn't good to be attacked first.

I shook off the emergency rations in my hand and turned around.

There was a tall, middle-aged man. He was wearing a black military coat. His mechanical right arm smelled of oil.

,To think that while I was away for a moment, the Ross unit would be annihilated by a brat who isn't even an Amur Leopard.... Brat. How should I accept this fact?"

,It's simple. Think about the will you'll leave behind."

I aimed my rifle at Major Vag.

,Of course, I have no intention of listening to the will of someone like you."

I firmly pulled the trigger.

The muzzle spat fire. The cartridge cases fell lightly to the ground. 12 exploding bullets, 4 electric bullets, and 5 impact bullets. I poured out Mana Bullets without holding back.

It was enough firepower to make Major Vag's coat tattered.

,That brat is audacious."

The problem is that only the coat became tattered. The Mana Bullets only managed to tear off Major Vag's artificial skin. Gray steel was visible through the torn artificial skin.

,...The machine wasn't just his right arm."

,I'm not as strong as I used to be because I'm getting old. So I remodeled myself with the money I've earned. I needed a performance test anyway.... A monster secretly raised by the higher-ups wouldn't be a bad opponent."

I took out a magazine loaded with Mana Bullets from my pocket. Major Vag is pretending to be calm, but the Mana Bullets can't be completely ineffective. I need to inflict even a little more damage.

Major Vag stretched out his right arm towards me. Part of his right arm glows blue.

Wiiiiing.

,Brat, that's a dangerous thing for you to play with, so it's confiscated."

,......!!"

The rifle in my hand flies into Major Vag's grasp. Not just the rifle. Even the pistol and attribute sword that I prepared as secondary weapons fly away. Magazines and knives were no exception. The worst part is that even the artifact bracelet I received from Lexi was taken away. In his grasp, metal items, including my belongings, are tightly attached.

Fortunately, Major Vag didn't recognize that the attribute sword and bracelet were artifacts.

,I'll praise you for not being attached to weapons. Usually, they would come along to avoid having their weapons taken away. But how are you going to fight without weapons? With that weak fist?"

I connected mana to the attribute sword. Major Vag, who is not a Mana Awakener, didn't even notice even if I openly moved mana.

,You're mistaken. My weapon is mana. A substance you can't even recognize."

Those who have not awakened mana have a sense of inferiority towards Mana Awakeners.

Even if you know how to handle a little of mana, the mysterious energy of this world, life becomes easier in many ways. Especially in special jobs like the military, Mana Awakeners are treated well and promoted faster than non-Awakeners just for being Mana Awakeners.

,Did you learn how to provoke from that woman?"

Major Vag's face contorted. His expression is vivid for a machine.

Click.

His left wrist bent and a muzzle popped out. White light gathered at the end of the muzzle and was fired at me. It's a laser. I ran right next to it and barely avoided the attack.

,Show me how far you can go with that great mana!"

The laser is fired again. This time I couldn't avoid it. It collided with the Class 3 barrier and was offset, but a hole was pierced in the barrier.

,...If I get hit one more time, the barrier itself will disappear. If I activate the barrier again... the mana I'll use for the counterattack will be precarious.'

I've used a considerable amount of mana coming this far.

,I can't survive if I focus on defense.'

The laser is fired. I couldn't avoid it. The barrier disappears.

,Haha! The shell is finally peeled off!"

He smiled brightly and aimed the muzzle at me. Light particles gather in front of the muzzle. The time it takes for the laser to fire is 3 seconds.

,He's finally let his guard down.'

The mind of the guy who is sure of his victory is focused on the laser. He's planning to hit me accurately and end it.

,Water.'

Water dripped from the attribute sword attached to his right hand.

,?!"

Major Vag reacted a beat late. His reaction is slow because he's a machine.

,Spark.'

The attribute changes to electricity. Even if it's waterproof by default, electricity will be different.

,Kueueueue...! That brat is using tricks!"

His right arm crackled incessantly, and a small explosion was heard as black smoke rose into the sky. The weapons that were held by magnetic force fell in droves. It's certain that he can't use his right arm.

,Damn it. Just thinking about fixing my arm makes me dizzy. Die!"

,Chrona.'

[Using Chrona. Remaining stacks: 1]

I saw the laser and avoided it. I don't know how to move my body, so I fell to the ground. Looking back, Major Vag is preparing the laser again.

The remaining number of uses of Chrona is exactly one.

,One time is enough.'

Feeling the sweat dripping down my chin, I stretched out my hand towards Major Vag and activated telekinesis. The artifact bracelet flew from among the fallen weapons. I immediately activate the Class 4 barrier. The laser that was fired at my head was blocked by the barrier.

,Tsk. That bracelet was a barrier artifact."

,It's a Class 4 barrier. You'll have to hit at least 3 more lasers. Well, that's if you have the power to do so."

Regaining my composure, I got up from my seat.

The laser didn't fly.

Even though there is an excellent optical weapon called a laser in this world, the reason why bullets are mainly used is because the cost-effectiveness of lasers is the worst. In the case of Major Vag, it looks like he doesn't have a battery, so he can't fire lasers indiscriminately.

Click.

His left arm changes. The muzzle widens and the sound of a cartridge being loaded is heard. Immediately after, the muzzle spat fire. Mana Bullets fly. I sprinted behind a tree. I didn't avoid it completely, but some of it was blocked by the Class 4 barrier.

,The quality of the Mana Bullets is good. The Class 4 barrier won't last long either. I have to settle it before the barrier disappears.'

I moved the attribute sword that had fallen on the ground with telekinesis. The attribute sword silently aims for Major Vag's nape.

,Do you think I'll be fooled twice?"

He kicked back. The attribute sword flew far away and was embedded in a tree. It's not the leg strength of an ordinary human.

,His lower body was also a machine. The attribute sword flew out of the telekinesis range.'

It was expected. The attribute sword did its job by drawing his attention.

,Shock Wave.'

I use the Class 3 magic Shock Wave. The shock wave shot from my fingertips flew towards Major Vag. The entangled tree breaks and the soil rises upwards.

Kwaaaang!

Major Vag, who was hit head-on by the Shock Wave, was pushed back. His mechanical body endured the Shock Wave, but it couldn't prevent cracks from forming on the surface.

,Should I hit him with another Shock Wave? My mana is precarious. Can I create an opening?'

Shock Wave has the best pure physical destructive power among Class 3 magic, but it consumes a lot of mana.

The enraged Major Vag fires Mana Bullets. The thick tree that was protecting me burns, freezes, and becomes fragments and falls. The Mana Bullets finally reached my barrier. The barrier shakes. It was when I was surprised and tried to run to the side. The Mana Bullets didn't fly.

Click click click.

An empty sound rang from Major Vag's left arm.

,Damn it. Did they all run out.... Well, that's fine. You can't fire magic indiscriminately either, so it seems like you don't have enough mana. I'll cut you down and kill you myself."

A knife popped out of his left arm.

,You're going to fight me in close combat? You must be confident in tag?"

I sneered at him. I'm not crazy enough to have a close combat fight with a cyborg. I had no intention of giving up the advantage of distance.

,Tag was my favorite game. I liked being It. I enjoyed catching the guys who were running away."

The back of Major Vag opened and a thruster appeared. I distorted my face. Major Vag laughed loudly. The thruster spits fire and flies while recklessly colliding with the trees and vines that block it.

,It's impossible to escape. I have to fight.'

High-speed combat using thrusters. It wasn't a good opponent for me, who is weak in close combat. But it wasn't like there was no way at all.

,Telekinesis.'

I control my body with telekinesis. Magic is 100 times more reliable than my hopeless athletic ability. I dodge Major Vag, who is flying towards me, to the side. It's surprisingly easy to avoid.

,He's also using thrusters for the first time.'

Major Vag rushes in from the front. His speed has slowed down a bit. I realized that the thruster's fuel was running out rapidly.

,If I endure a few more times like this, I'll have a chance to escape. But... I'm not confident in having a stamina battle against a cyborg. I'll settle it here.'

[Using Chrona. Remaining stacks: 0]

I calculated the formula and glared at Major Vag, who was flying using the thruster.

His knife was aiming for my shoulder. I can feel his intention to drag out the battle more surely by not aiming for a vital spot right away. He knows that I'm in a situation where it wouldn't be strange if I collapsed right now.

Instead of avoiding it, I dug forward. The blade grazed my shoulder. It's not a dangerous level.

,I'll endure the recoil with telekinesis...!'

I stretched out my palm to Major Vag's solar plexus. It touched. At the same time, my body was pushed back. My back hit a tree.

,Lightning Grab!!"

Using the pain felt in my back as a driving force, I shouted the incantation as if spitting it out.

The mana that stretched out from the astral plane ran through the Mana Rod and transformed into magic with the prepared formula.

Pajijijijijijijik!

Hundreds of lightning bolts burst out from my palm and ran through Major Vag's mechanical body. The surroundings were as bright as day with the light of the lightning.

,That brat!!"

Major Vag raised his left arm. But that knife never came down on me. The lightning destroyed his mechanical body. His expression also hardened. The only thing that moves is his left eyeball.

,Major Vag. I won."

My mana was already at the bottom, but I don't need mana to finish off a tin can.

I broke off a suitable branch next to me. I stuck the branch deep into his left eye, which couldn't even open its mouth.

A chilling sensation was felt through my hand.

,The limit of cyborgs is that they can't replace their brains with machines.'

If you replace the brain with a machine, it's not a human but a robot.

Having won, I sighed deeply and turned my back on Major Vag. I glanced back. Major Vag's mechanical body didn't fall even in death. It's an amazing body that deserves admiration.

,I don't think I'm envious.'

I sat down on the floor. As soon as my mana recovers, I'm going to smash that amazing body into small pieces. If there are cameras or something attached to that body and the data is intact, things won't end with just being troublesome.

Noon.

Dragging my tired body, I arrived at the meeting place.

Lexi was already there. Her appearance wasn't normal either. Her right arm was broken and her pink bobbed hair was slightly scorched.

,I found the Resistance's base and raided it. Lieutenant Olkor, who was the target, was alive with his arms and legs cut off."

,Did you kill him?"

,I had the time, but it was too much of a hassle."

She shrugged her shoulders. I nodded calmly. I would have made the same choice. I had no intention of taking such a troublesome burden with me.

Lexi came closer. Her head leaned on my shoulder.

,Ah. I want to have sex."

,...Is that what you have to say right now."

,I just said what I really felt. Let's go to the stream, wash up, and take a nap. And when we wake up... you know?"

I didn't mention that I killed Major Vag.

The time to return to the unit will be longer, but living like Tarzan with Lexi won't be bad.
