Cotton stepped forward towards the female barbarian, radiating confidence. However, it was clear that his hands holding the greatsword were trembling slightly. It was obvious that this was due to the axe he had just blocked.

,If we're just talking strength, it's similar to or greater than Cotton's.,

Even if the female barbarian had consistently trained her muscles, her strength was abnormally high. Cotton was at a similar level to me when it came to strength.

,Come to think of it... it doesn't make sense that two women, especially beauties, are wandering around in this empire.,

I don't know about the west of the empire where women rose up, but this area where I am is a typical male-dominated society. The only women who are treated well are noblewomen or female knights. Both are rare.

"Get out of the way! I have no interest in you!"

The female barbarian shouts. Her voice is so loud that my brow furrows even though I'm quite a distance away. Still, she didn't ignore Cotton and rush at me right away. Soon, her axe and Cotton's greatsword clashed in the air.

I glanced away for a moment, and a sword comes flying.

[Using Momentary (瞬間). Remaining stacks: 11]

I used Momentary to tilt my head to the side and avoid the sword. I'm wearing armor, but the flying sword was imbued with Sword energy. If I hadn't used Momentary, my reaction might have been delayed. In the worst case, I would have died instantly.

,It's a perfect time for a surprise attack... but does a knight throw their weapon, a sword?,

It wasn't the mindset of a normal knight. Traditionally, knights of the empire regarded their weapons as extensions of themselves.

I looked at the female knight with admiration, then paused.

She was holding a sword.

Did she have two swords from the beginning? That couldn't be. I looked back as if to check the sword that flew behind me and was stuck in the tree. The sword turned gray and disappeared.

,It doesn't seem like magic. Is it a secret skill the female knight possesses?,

It was similar to my skill, Meteor Sword. Of course, I can't jump to conclusions.

The female knight rushes towards me. She's fast. There's a sense of regularity in her movements. She's not moving randomly, but according to a standard. It seems she learned a swordsmanship passed down from her family.

Closing the distance, she positioned her arm as if to swing her sword at any moment.

,A feint with eye contact.,

But it won't work. I could feel the mana fluctuating in a different place with my heightened senses.

Three swords are created in the air and fly towards me. Each sword was a different shape. Just like before, they were imbued with Sword energy.

,Impressive. Most guys would get hit by this attack.,

Meteor Sword.

I created three Meteor Swords in the air and shot them towards the incoming swords. The Meteor Swords were clearly inferior in terms of durability. They shattered into fragments as soon as they collided. But it was enough to change the trajectory of the incoming swords.

The female knight's eyes widened for a moment, then returned to normal. She swung her sword, and I blocked it with my sword. The sword didn't stay still. It pushed against my sword as if scraping it away, and continued with the next sword strike. I focused and blocked the sword strikes.

Her swordsmanship was skilled.

"Is that your secret skill?"

"So?"

"It's similar to mine. It must be a top-tier secret skill. Not only that, but your physical abilities and swordsmanship are also outstanding. There are few knights as skilled as you in the empire. Why are you using that great skill to be a bandit?"

"Everyone has their own story. I'm more curious about your story."

Blue sparks flew every time the swords collided. Sword energy and Sword energy clashed and scattered. Of course, she didn't just fight. She created swords in the air and shot them out from time to time. I deflected the swords with Meteor Sword and responded.

,If we go for a long battle, I'll win easily.,

She's not just using a sword, but creating and launching swords imbued with Sword energy. It's a technique that clearly consumes a lot of mana.

Her sword became faster. The power in her sword also increased. She wants to end this battle quickly. In other words, she's feeling impatient.

Suddenly, her swordsmanship changed. The sword, which had been as rigid as a mountain, became as erratic as the wind.

,I've experienced similar swordsmanship in the Guangming Ascension World.,

I responded without panicking. Even if I don't have a talent for swordsmanship, I have experiences that others can't accumulate.

The swordsmanship changed again. This time, it was a quick sword focused solely on speed. It was a familiar type of swordsmanship, so I easily blocked it. The swordsmanship continues to change. The breathing, spacing, knee angle, and leg stance change in an instant.

,Is she a genius of swordsmanship comparable to Ju Seo-hyun?,

No, that's not the feeling. There was something lacking. It was as if she didn't have much substance compared to the swordsmanship she had learned. It seemed like she lacked experience.

,If it were Ju Seo-hyun, she would have picked only the advantages of the swordsmanship and made it her own.,

The female knight gritted her teeth.

The sword is heavy. It's difficult for an ordinary person to swing a sword 10 times in a row. Especially now that we're in battle. Because our lives are at stake, the tension in our bodies is at its peak. It was natural that our stamina was draining like crazy.

12th swordsmanship change. She and I swung our swords more than 200 times. I was also out of breath, but it was nothing compared to the female knight. The female knight was having a hard time even catching her breath.

"...Why? Why doesn't my swordsmanship work?!"

"Because it lacks substance? Your swordsmanship seems like something you learned by roughly estimating. It would have been better if you had focused on one swordsmanship."

"Kuh, shut up! Don't mock me! The victory hasn't been decided yet! You're tired just like I am! Do you think I don't know?"

The female knight rushes at me in excitement. Her concentration was shaken by my provocation. Her breathing was ruined and the momentum of her sword died.

,I've been waiting for this moment.,

I block her sword with my sword. As I turned my sword and led her, the female knight took a step towards me to avoid dropping her sword.

I thrust my mana-filled fist towards her abdomen.

Bang!

With a huge impact sound, her eyes rolled upwards. Then, the sword fell from her hand and her body collapsed. I caught her body with my hand.

"It's hard to capture them alive without injuries since they're moderately strong."

I turned my head to check on the female barbarian and Cotton.

"Huh."

Cotton was lying down. The female barbarian's right foot was on his head. Cotton lost even after using his secret skill. Blood was flowing from the female barbarian's right arm, but it wasn't serious.

"Cotton... isn't he dead? Why didn't she kill him? I heard that barbarians kill people mercilessly."

"That's just a rumor. We don't massacre humans for no reason. Well, we usually kill enemies... but you didn't kill that guy either."

Since I didn't kill the female knight, she didn't kill Cotton.

"Simple and clear."

"Is there any reason to be complicated?"

"No. In the end, the strong take everything."

I put the female knight down on the floor. The surrounding bandits were already terrified and couldn't come close. I advanced towards the female barbarian.

"Uryaaaaaaaa!!"

She charged while shouting a truly barbarian-like cry. Red Sword energy soared from the blades of the axes she held in both hands.

I pretended to block her axe with my sword, then dodged. Buuwoong. The axe cut through the air with a ferocious momentum. The aftermath was so strong that my body was blown backwards. Of course, it's not a normal attack.

,Wind? No, is it a secret skill related to strength? I could never have blocked that power. Good thing I dodged.,

Thanks to that, the counterattack continued smoothly. My left fist strikes her trained abdominal muscles. A mana-filled fist. The impact spread throughout her body, and it wouldn't be strange if she fainted like the female knight... but the barbarian endured.

She smiled and raised her axe.

"Your fist is a bit spicy. But it's not enough to knock me down."

"Lightning."

Pajijijijik!

Blue lightning that started from my left fist raced through her body.

The giant woman, taller than me at 190cm, couldn't withstand the electric jjimjil and fell backwards with her eyes rolled back.

,I was trying not to use abilities like Lightning or Momentary as a form of training.... It got tough in the middle.,

I rotated my left wrist. My wrist was stiff because her body was so solid.

I picked them up with both hands and ordered the dazed bandits.

"Take Cotton and come back. We're going back to the base."

"Yes, yes! Boss! W-what are you going to do with those women?"

"I have to make them pay the price for touching me."

"S-so, we'll get a chance too...?"

"Of course. I'll pass them on to you after I've had a taste. Well, I'm planning to have a good taste for about a week."

"A week? A-ah, no! I'll wait! Just give me a chance! Hehe...."

From now on, the bandits' lifespan was one week.

I brought the fainted women to the house where I was staying. It was a bandit den full of men, so I couldn't leave them to the other guys. I'd trust a passing dog more than those bandits.

I put them in separate rooms after putting special slave shackles on them and restraining them with handcuffs and chains. Just being imprisoned alone in a room would increase the psychological pressure.

"Master!"

Cotton woke up before the women. Cotton knelt before me with burning eyes.

"It was my mistake that I lost to that barbarian woman... I didn't know she could use such strong power. It must be a secret skill she stole after killing a knight. If I fight her again, I won't lose! Please let me fight that woman again!"

"Cotton. That woman lost to me and is restrained."

"Please leave the humiliation of that woman to me! I must repay that barbarian for this humiliation! If you give me just one week, I'll make her a bitch who obeys you, Master!"

I clicked my tongue as I looked at Cotton.

"You're crossing the line."

"...Yes?"

[Using Momentary (瞬間). Remaining stacks: 11]

I summoned a sword, held it in my hand, and stabbed it into Cotton's solar plexus.

"M-master? W-why... are you doing this?"

"Those women are mine. Can you covet the Master's women?"

"I-if you had said so, such a thing... would never have happened...!"

"You should have realized it even if I didn't say it. And you would have crossed the line even if I did. Consider it a mercy that I killed you in one go, considering our past relationship."

"You horny bastard...!"

Cotton cursed me right before he died. As a reward, I chopped up Cotton's body, stuck it on skewers, and displayed it in front of the house. The bandits trembled and didn't dare to approach my house.
