I almost died back then.'

If I hadn't had Complete Recovery, I would have had to live as a one-armed man. A one-armed swordsman? It sounds easy, but you can't do it without considerable talent. It would have been terrible if I didn't have Complete Recovery.

,What's a guy who collapsed during the battle talking about.'

,I couldn't help it because of my internal injuries! I don't want anything else!'

,We would have killed the sabok even without you.'

,...Just my son! Please, just save my son!'

,Ah, Father...!'

,Hong Gang-hu. Can you die for your son?'

,If you save my son... I will gladly give my life!'

Hong Gang-hu's eyes were sincere. He was genuinely prepared to die for Hong Je-yu.

,I can't understand it.'

He protects his son, who committed patricide because he was blinded by a woman, to the end? Even sacrificing his life? No matter how much paternal love he has, it was an incomprehensible choice.

Hong Je-yu only shows touched eyes because his father is willing to die for him. He doesn't stop him at all.

,What a crazy father and son.'

These guys were weird.

Whatever they do, my decision won't change. It's a little disappointing that I'm not backstabbing them in a dramatic situation, but I've decided to backstab them, so I will.

,Did you think you could survive after backstabbing me? You're both going to die.'

Hwaryeon Bido moves. Seogeok seogeok. He cut off Hong Je-yu's limbs. Hong Je-yu, with only his torso left, writhed, screaming in despair. He was like a bug.

,Je-yu-ya!! You son of a biiiiiitch!!'

Hong Gang-hu rushes in, bursting with anger.

Too early to be angry. Hong Je-yu is still alive, and the blade of Hwaryeon Bido hasn't dulled even after slicing his limbs.

When the tip of the sword pointed at Hong Je-yu's neck, Hong Gang-hu's legs stopped.

,S, stop...!'

Hong Gang-hu sobbed and pleaded.

,Cut off your right arm. Then I'll save your son.'

,I, I'll cut it off!'

Hong Gang-hu gritted his teeth and cut off his right arm. I was briefly impressed by his decision, which he made without hesitating for even five seconds.

Hwaryeon Bido went slightly into Hong Je-yu's chest.

,Aaaaaaaaaaagh!'

,You cut off your arm! Stop! Stop it!!'

Hong Gang-hu grabbed his sword with his remaining left hand. However, he couldn't rush at me while watching my reaction. Hong Je-yu wasn't dead yet, after all. He's holding onto his last hope.

She's definitely the protagonist of the Cheonma Revenge Story. The original protagonist was basically crazy about martial arts (武功).

That tendency was so severe that he was betrayed and died. And he regressed. After the regression, that tendency decreased, but as long as revenge was in his mind, love and romance wouldn't enter his eyes.

Thunderbolt Stream (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A streak of red light passed through Hong Gang-hu's neck. His head went up like a water rocket and then fell weakly to the floor.

,I would have given him more pain originally, but....'

I watched Baekran's reaction. Is she going to ask for this magical artifact? She was already sitting cross-legged on the floor, circulating her energy.

,She's openly circulating her energy in front of me? Does she trust me? Or is there something else?'

It was also proof that she had no interest in the Hong family father and son.

,How do you use the Light Force Pouch?'

I was lost in thought for a moment.

,Since the pouch (包) means to wrap something....'

I unfolded the cloth, put the heavenly jade inside, and wrapped it. Then the cloth glowed. When I unfolded the cloth again, the heavenly jade had disappeared without a trace. The cloth continued to glow.

,Ah, I see. So you charge it like this. How do you use it?'

I wrapped my body with protective ganggi and touched the Light Force Pouch here and there. I'm not too worried about an explosion. I only used one heavenly jade, so it won't be able to penetrate my protective ganggi.

I touched it here and there and found out how to use it. I rolled the cloth like I was rolling gimbap. Then the black cloth turned into a cylinder. A cylinder that's perfect for shooting something.

I hit the back of the cylinder with my palm. Light shot out from the front and hit the floor.

Kwang!

,I know how to use it, so that's enough.'

Three Peaks (三頂).
