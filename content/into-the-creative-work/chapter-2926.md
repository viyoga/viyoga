Marie Wong felt the moment the Gestalt attacked the ship. She couldn't get detailed information because of the radio, but she couldn't not know when the ship shook from the external impact and the emergency lights turned on.

,Yujin was right...!'

His prophecy came true.

If she hadn't become Yujin's woman... she would have had her head chewed off alive by the Gestalt.

Marie waited for Yujin in the A zone drop pod bay. This place was relatively safe from the Gestalt. Occasionally, lost soldier bugs would appear, but Marie calmly put bullets into the bugs' heads. Even though she was a medic, she was also a marine. Unless it was a surprise attack, she could easily handle one bug.

Kukukukukung.

,The ship has been shaking violently since earlier. It's entering the planet's atmosphere. No, it's crashing. When the hell is this bastard coming?!'

It was when she was stomping her feet in impatience.

She felt the presence of people in the hallway where the emergency lights were on. Not just one, but many.

Are the soldier bugs running in a group? She imagined the worst and picked up her pistol.

Fortunately, there were seven people running down the hallway. Their power suits were covered in blood. In the center was a man wearing a gold-decorated power suit.

"Captain Max?"

"You are... Lieutenant Marie Wong. I'm glad you're alive. The Forte ship is crashing. We need to escape in a drop pod. Come with us."

Marie's complexion wasn't good.

She remembered what Seong Yujin had said. That if he didn't come, she should commit suicide. That it would be the most comfortable end.

Should she escape the ship with Captain Max's group in a drop pod?

Marie shook her head. She rejected the instinct to live first. Seong Yujin is a prophet chosen by God. Without him, escaping to the ground would only bring a terrible future. That's Seong Yujin's prophecy.

"...I'm supposed to meet Private Seong Yujin here."

"We arrived here by the safest route possible. Even so, dozens died and only we survived. Thinking coldly, the possibility of Private Seong being alive is slim. It's best to escape with us. If we don't escape within the next three minutes... the Forte ship will explode. The sub-engine will cause a huge explosion."

"...I'm sorry. I have to wait for him."

Marie said in a firm tone, even as her body trembled slightly.

"Is that an order?"

"...Yes."

"I understand. I'll pretend I didn't hear that. I'm not stupid enough to bring up insubordination in this situation. I respect your choice. There's a spare drop pod, so escape within the next three minutes."

Captain Max's group crowded into one drop pod.

The gate opened and the drop pod was launched. The gate closed immediately, but she could see the surface of the planet Locke 3 getting closer. The land was desolate like a desert. It was because the Gestalt had eaten most of the animals and plants.

"Shit...! Should I have just escaped with Captain Max's group?"

She thought about the condition of Captain Max's group.

Some soldiers were carrying thick bags. They probably contained ammunition and food.

,...No matter how I think about it, I don't think I can last a week. That means I have to get to the outpost within a week... What if I'm unlucky and run into a Gestalt group? I'll just die.'

Marie picked up her pistol with trembling hands. The muzzle pointed at her own head.

,Not yet. There's still time....'

Committing suicide with a pistol versus being caught in a sub-engine explosion. Which is the more comfortable death?

,A pistol that can kill me instantly is better, right?'

It was when only such terrible thoughts filled her head.

She felt someone running down the hallway. Tensing up, she aimed her gun, and a marine in a power suit was running towards her. The helmet visor went up, revealing Seong Yujin's face.

"You bastard! Why are you so late?!"

"I stopped by the cafeteria on the way, and it was packed. But I got a lot of food!"

Look at him talking so shamelessly.

Marie was filled with annoyance, anger, and relief.

"You son of a bitch! Seriously! I thought you were dead!"
