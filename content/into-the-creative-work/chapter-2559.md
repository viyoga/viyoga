I looked around. It was full of corpses. Most of them were corpses of martial artists belonging to the Hwaryong Clan, but some were mixed with corpses of vagrants or ordinary people.

They were caught up in a battle. It was obvious because it wasn't just an ordinary battle, but one filled with poison. In addition, the Hwaryong Clan basically had poison in their bodies because they had mastered both poison and fire techniques at the same time. That poison spread as they became corpses.

,It's a relief I didn't detonate the Blood Thunder recklessly.'

If I hadn't limited the space of the Blood Thunder, not only the brothel but more than half of the city would have been blown away.

I headed to the Hwaryong Clan. I declared to the stragglers who remained in the Hwaryong Clan.

,From now on, the Hwaryong Clan is mine. You who belong to the Hwaryong Clan are also mine. If you run away, you die.'

I took over the best building in this city. They knelt before me and submitted. There were no complaints. They knew very well how strong an absolute master was.

,Bring all the courtesans in the city. Also, bring all the Hwaryong Clan's martial arts, elixirs, poisons, and other assets and offer them to me.'

I settled down in the Hwaryong Clan and started living the life of Riley. Of course, I didn't forget to train from time to time while doing so.

The Hwaryong Clan built an underground training ground rather than one above ground, perhaps to prevent the spread of poison. Maybe because they invested a lot of money, the training ground was quite impressive. Although small in size, a spiritual meridian also passed through it.

I took out an elixir from my inventory.

A round pill containing pure spiritual energy.

Palsandan (Eight Immortals Elixir).

This elixir did not have the effect of increasing internal energy. Instead, it temporarily amplifies one's perception. It is an elixir taken when overcoming walls, pursuing enlightenment, or learning and practicing important techniques.

I put the Palsandan in my mouth. My perception is amplified and my senses become more sensitive. Since it was a temporary phenomenon through the elixir, I immediately began training.

,...Actually, it's not even training. It's to find out the cause of the collision between the Demonic energy of the Cheonma Shingong and the lightning energy of the Lightning Sky Style.'

First, I operate the lightning energy with the Lightning Sky Resolution of the Lightning Sky Style. Flashing lightning energy races through the Eight Extraordinary Meridians.

Pajijijik.

Blue currents of electricity sparked around my body, which was sitting in a lotus position. I calmed the lightning energy. Most of the lightning energy disappeared, but some of it remained in the meridians.

I operate the Cheonma Shingong. Demonic energy wriggles and stirs in the dantian. And the remaining lightning energy and Demonic energy collided.

Kwadeudeudeuk!

The victim of that collision is my body. Qi and blood are twisted and internal organs are damaged.

,Keulkeuleok!'

I vomited blood. At that moment, I felt more refreshed than painful. Of course, it was also painful due to internal injuries.

The lightning energy collides with the Demonic energy as if it has a will of its own. The Demonic energy did not leave the rampaging lightning energy alone. The Demonic energy also began to rampage. Since I was operating the Cheonma Shingong, the Demonic energy was overwhelming. The lightning energy is quickly suppressed.

,Keulkeuleok.'

The problem is that I suffered internal injuries even in that short moment.

,But I roughly understand what happened. It's because the lightning energy has become stronger as the level of Lightning Flash has increased.'

When strength increases, shoulders rise. The lightning energy became fiercer at the same time as it became stronger. Of course, I can suppress it if I try. However, it consumes concentration. There's no room to focus on lightning energy during a battle where there are many things to pay attention to.

,The problem is that my body has become too accustomed to Lightning Flash.'

The immediate solution that comes to mind is to operate the Lightning Sky Style and Cheonma Shingong completely separately. So, in a way, it can be said that it is not a serious problem.

,It's become impossible to simply use the Lightning Sky Style and Cheonma Shingong like switching a switch, like in the Atlantis of God world.'

[Atlantis of God], which receives the support of the system, will have no problems.

,Can't I use Cheonma Shingong and Lightning Sky Style at the same time? At least, I hope they don't react against each other.'

Cheonma Shingong when Cheonma Shingong is needed. Lightning Sky Style when Lightning Sky Style is needed. If I'm going to use them, I should use them flexibly.

I pondered a solution, but nothing came to mind.

,...I don't know, so I should ask Wiyu-nim. It's good to have a teacher at times like this.'

I used the Cheongangseong System's space teleportation.

『A special barrier is interfering with space teleportation.』

My eyebrows twitched.

,A barrier? There is no barrier in the Hwaryong Clan. I've already confirmed it.'

Still, just in case, I spread out my spiritual sense. I stopped myself from emitting electromagnetic waves because the lightning energy and Demonic energy would react again and I would suffer internal injuries.

There is no barrier where my spiritual sense reaches. I try space teleportation again.

『A special barrier is interfering with space teleportation.』

,...There's a barrier in a place where my senses don't reach.'

There is a way to find out.

[I open the Eye of Heaven (天眼).]

I look further away through the Eye of Heaven. The further the distance, the more concentration is required, but with my current self, I can easily observe even a thousand li away.

I found the barrier.

A very large hemispherical barrier was installed centering on the city where I was. The barrier was in the form of a net and was gradually reducing its size.

,The Martial Alliance's Net of Heaven and Earth! Not only space teleportation, but transfer techniques won't work either.'

They were preparing to capture me for sure. It seemed that capturing me alive was their top priority.

I took out a space movement scroll from my inventory. Space movement performed with objects from another world using a different principle than magic.

Jjieeeeeeek.

,That may also be a method for the time being. But there is only one fundamental solution. Train hard.'

,......'
