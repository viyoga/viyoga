"Ugh..."

Clear, transparent tears streamed down her cheeks.

I quietly waited for her tears to stop. I thought her tears would stop soon, but unexpectedly, there was no sign of them stopping.

"I... I love you too, Master-nim."

She took a step towards me. I pulled Yuria into my arms.

I felt sorry for her in my heart.

The ring.

It was a ring that would disappear if I returned to my original world.

'I should find a ring that's as similar as possible in the real world and give it to her. It wouldn't be bad to torment the dwarves and make them create an even more awesome ring.'

Before long, Yuria's crying stopped. Now it was time to return to the room and confirm our love physically.

I grabbed Yuria's shoulders and was about to open my mouth.

Kwaang!

With a sound like a bomb exploding, the temple chapel doors burst open. Charging in like a storm were Duchess Filtania and her retainers, along with their guards. In total, more than 30 people barged in.

"Your Excellency Duchess Filtania! This is the sacred interior of the Temple of Love! The God of Love will not tolerate such violence!"

The temple priest tried to stop the duchess, but she didn't even glance at the priest, staring only at me and Yuria.

I spotted an old woman next to the duchess. An old woman wearing a robe. It was the old woman who took half of my entire fortune and gave me the ring. I could tell from the small part of her face that was visible that she was smiling.

'...Could it be? Did that old hag bring the duchess here?'

The back of my head felt numb. I didn't expect her to come at me like this. Was half of my entire fortune not enough?

"Yuria. Let's run."

I grabbed Yuria's shoulders and waist. If we fled with all our might through the window next to us, we might be able to shake off the knights.

"No. It's alright."

Yuria broke free from my arms. Her expression had returned to normal. There was no trace of her crying. She confidently stepped forward in front of the duchess.

"You've come faster than I expected."

"......"

The duchess glared fiercely at Yuria. But only for a moment. Soon, she let out a deep sigh and said.

"I've lost. Yuria. Come back to the main family. How long are you going to leave your seat empty?"

"It seems the King's answer has arrived."

"Yes. I never imagined you would go this far."

"Mother. Are you officially acknowledging our relationship?"

"......I acknowledge it. And don't forget, Yuria. As much as I've conceded, you must elevate the Filtania family to the highest position."

What's going on? I couldn't grasp the situation and just rolled my eyes around. I don't know why things are suddenly working out so well.

The duchess still glared at me as if she didn't like me.

"Let's go back. Eugene. No, Baron Eugene."

"......!!"

Now I think I understand.

Our biggest problem is status. Nobles must marry nobles. Nobles with low titles can marry wealthy merchants, but not the Filtania Dukedom.

'While I was locked in the dungeon for a month, Yuria was working to make me a noble!'

I later learned that Yuria had manipulated all sorts of documents to make me a noble, spread rumors by calling people, and made a deal close to blackmail with the King. In exchange for handing over evidence of the dirty deeds committed by Crown Prince Parein, I was granted a noble title.

I had become a noble in name and reality.

"When will the ceremony be held?"

Yuria smiled, looking at the ring on her left ring finger.

"As soon as possible. Yes. Wouldn't next week be good?"

"Don't say unreasonable things. You need at least three months of preparation time."

"Yes. I know."

After a short escape, Yuria and I returned to the Filtania Dukedom.

If status is the problem, then solve the status.

I felt anew that Yuria was incredibly capable.

Crown Prince Parein had already dropped out as well. From his perspective, he had already obtained Mary, and he wouldn't have time to spend at the academy in order to smoothly inherit the throne.

Mary had changed. The noblewoman who had been unpretentious, simple, and full of bright charm was gone. Her earrings, necklace, and even the dress she was wearing were all extravagant luxury items.

"Have you been well, Mary Komotopfs-yang?"

Yuria, with her arm linked with mine, smiled and said to Mary.

Mary didn't even try to hide her expression, frowning as if she was displeased.

"I would have been well if you hadn't come. What business do you have with me?"

"It's nothing much, we just came to deliver an invitation."

"An invitation?"

I handed Mary a luxurious invitation with gold leaf on the edges of the envelope. Mary opened the invitation as soon as she received it. Her face crumpled like paper.

"A wedding... invitation?"

"I will be marrying Eugene next year. In a way, it's thanks to Komotopfs-yang, so I came to deliver the invitation in person like this."

That was an excuse, and in reality, we came to provoke Mary. And that intention was successful.

Eudeuk.
