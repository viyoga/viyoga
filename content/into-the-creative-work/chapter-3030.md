Setting aside techniques and artifacts, when it comes down to pure raw power class, that bastard has the edge over me.

Since I'm outclassed in raw power, the longer this fight drags on, the faster my odds of winning will plummet.

The lightning deluge began from the dark clouds gathered over the earth. At its center, the massive white tree—that bastard's true body—was scorched by the lightning but refused to break. It wrapped its entire body in an orange wavelength, blocking the lightning as much as it could. But it wasn't blocking all of it.

'The important thing is that it's holding out.'

The Thunder Heaven Command surging up from the ground isn't a complete Thunder Heaven Command. Originally, Thunder Heaven Command draws its power from nature itself. The one on the ground is more than half powered by my own strength.

'My advantage is experience. That thing isn't a fully matured Phiron. It's been force-grown by feeding on the nutrients of its kin.'

It shared experience from another Phiron?

Whatever amount of experience it was, the time it had to share must have been short. Even if it started sharing experience the moment I arrived, it hasn't been a week.

'Even if it received that experience in the form of memories, we're talking decades. Maybe centuries to millennia of memories—it's impossible to fully internalize that in a matter of days. And even if it did fully internalize them, it's still just indirect experience.'

Compared to experience directly lived and felt, it couldn't help but fall short. On this point, I can confidently say I have the upper hand.

'Je-un-ryeong.'

Now that the bastard is focused on holding out, I gathered dark clouds in the sky once more.

Two choices were presented to the bastard.

One: abandon holding out and launch a full assault on me. The other: endure to the end.

I could see the wavelength protecting its entire body growing denser.

'So it chose the latter. If it were me, I'd have gone for the all-out attack. Still, it's not a bad choice.'

What it chose is the textbook move. As long as it knows it has the advantage in raw power, holding out gives it better odds of victory.

'I've already used more than half my mana. If I can't finish it with the next attack, I lose. I do have one more chance with Full Recovery, but... I won't use it. I'll end this without it.'

Thunder rumbles even from the dark clouds in the sky. Gathering power to the limit and casting Thunder God's Command is...

'Going to be tough. It takes too long. The longer it takes, the more additional options that bastard will have.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Ultimate Technique (極技) — Thunder Heaven Command (雷天命).

Lightning began striking down from the dark clouds in the sky as well. In truth, the sky's Thunder Heaven Command wasn't ready. There was no time to summon the dark clouds and gather the lightning—it was essentially dropping bolts almost immediately.

But the ground-level Thunder Heaven Command compensated for it. The lightning surging from the ground to the sky stimulated the dark clouds above, and the lightning falling from the sky stimulated the dark clouds below.

A circulation of lightning born from tens of thousands of bolts striking simultaneously. I stood at its center. I couldn't tell what was lightning falling from the sky and what was lightning surging up from the ground.

Lightning energy filled every direction. So thick that a single breath made my throat tingle. Of course, I wasn't electrocuted.

Thanks to the effect of the [Lightning Master Rune] in my inventory, I don't get electrocuted.

[Heaven's Heart (天心) activated. Duration: 1 minute.]

For the next minute, my mana became infinite.

I holstered the Hwaryeonbi I'd been holding at my waist and raised my right hand. The moment I generated lightning, all the lightning energy in every direction converged on me.

Bzzt, crackle.

Compressing the lightning in my hand, I never took my eyes off my target—the white tree. The bastard's orange wavelength grew so dense it began shifting into a shape like a turtle's shell. It was focusing entirely on defense.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder (卍雷).

Every bolt of lightning striking the surroundings spun and was sucked into my grasp, compressing.

'Using Thunder God's Command with my own power... that's impossible. Even my current body can't withstand the output of Thunder God's Command.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder Spear (卍雷槍).

The Myriad Thunder compressed in my palm elongated and transformed into the shape of a spear. Even in spear form, it continued rotating slowly.

'Domain Open.'

I'd only used it briefly when fighting Zhang Haran a few days ago, so the cooldown was fast enough that I could use it now.

As the domain imbued itself into the Myriad Thunder Spear, its blue color shifted to crimson.

The completed Myriad Thunder Spear stopped rotating. I gripped it and took a javelin-throwing stance.

'If it pierces, I win. If it doesn't, I lose.'

A simple spear-versus-shield showdown.

I hurled the Myriad Thunder Spear at the bastard. It flew with a thunderclap and struck the orange wavelength shield in the blink of an eye. Crimson lightning exploded in every direction from the point of impact. The compressed lightning was bleeding out from the sheer force of the collision.

The bastard's wavelength shield was far tougher than I'd expected.

'If I hadn't mixed in the domain, it would've been blocked.'

The Myriad Thunder Spear, which seemed to have been stopped, pierced through the shield and embedded itself in the white tree's trunk.

"This... this is power a mere human can possess...?!"

KWAAAAAAAH!

The Myriad Thunder Spear detonated.

The massive white tree shattered into countless pieces that scattered in every direction.

Watching the fragments of the white tree rain down from the sky, I savored my victory.

• • •

The three who had been watching Sung Yujin's battle—Ryu Gicheon, Ryu Cheongseol, and Zhang Haran—fell silent.

All three had predicted Sung Yujin would struggle and ultimately lose. Not because they underestimated him, but because the white tree's power was just that overwhelming.

As Ryu Gicheon had said, the white tree Phiron was at minimum an SS-Rank boss monster. A powerful foe that even an S-Rank Hunter couldn't guarantee victory against in solo combat. Predicting the defeat of Sung Yujin, who had only just become an S-Rank Hunter, was the sensible and common-sense conclusion.

And yet, what do you know.

A rookie who had just become an S-Rank Hunter had defeated a monster estimated to be an SS-Rank boss.

"Father. That power... is that S-Rank?"

Ryu Cheongseol was filled with awe.

Despite being over 30km away, the shockwave of immense power could be felt. Dark clouds gathering in the sky and on the earth, pouring lightning toward the heavens—a phenomenon beyond common sense.

Now I understood why S-Rank Hunters were called natural disasters. Anyone who saw this spectacle and still couldn't comprehend it would have their intelligence called into question.

"If it's S-Rank power, you should know it well. I'm S-Rank too. The Myriad Beast Empress beside me is also S-Rank."

"Hmph."

Zhang Haran snorted. Her gaze never left the front.

"If you went all out, Father... could you do something like that?"

"...I could split a mountain. But I couldn't erase one without leaving a trace like that bastard did. It's a difference in ability. No—I should acknowledge what deserves acknowledging. That bastard is strong. Among China's S-Rank Hunters, only two or three could even contend with him. And even then, victory wouldn't be guaranteed."

"If I become S-Rank... could I beat him?"

Ryu Cheongseol had no need to defeat Sung Yujin. But Hunters as a breed are sensitive to power. Without meaning to, they end up comparing their own strength to others'. Ryu Gicheon and Zhang Haran were also running combat simulations against Sung Yujin in their heads.

"In close combat, I'd win. I'd like to say that, but... his swordsmanship doesn't fall short even compared to mine. At range, it's only natural he has the advantage... Especially that crimson lightning spear he showed at the end—even I'd have no way to deal with that. The lightning in particular is troublesome. Without a means to counter that lightning storm, defeat is all but certain. Myriad Beast Empress. What do you think?"

"Hmph. Don't ask me."

Ryu Gicheon nodded at Zhang Haran's attitude, taking it in stride. Among S-Ranks, there were plenty of oddballs with personalities like hers.

Ryu Gicheon could roughly guess what Zhang Haran was feeling. It must be complicated. That there could be such a gap between people of the same S-Rank was hard to accept. Especially in Zhang Haran's case, since she hadn't been S-Rank for long.

'Cheongseol is still A-Rank. Since she hasn't crossed the wall, it must feel impossibly distant. That's why she can admire without envy. When someone is too far ahead, you don't even feel jealousy.'

Zhang Haran was different.

Same S-Rank. Sung Yujin wouldn't feel that far away to her.

'Zhang Haran is young. No telling what the future holds. People tend to think S-Rank is the end, the completion, but... even S-Ranks grow depending on effort.'

One thing was certain: starting today, the world's balance of power would shift, if only slightly. And at its center would be Sung Yujin. The standing of his home country, Korea, would rise as well.

"Setting all else aside, for a warrior of that caliber to appear—it is a blessing for humanity."

In the distance, a hero was returning.

When I got back, the atmosphere had shifted somewhat.

Ryu Gicheon, who'd been giving me dirty looks and muttering about my bad physiognomy, now stared at me with blazing eyes. It wasn't hostility, but it was a bit much.

In Ryu Cheongseol's case, her eyes were practically sparkling when she looked at me. Her goal is to become an S-Rank Hunter. Having witnessed my power, she seemed intensely curious.

Zhang Haran was still venomous as ever. Though when she looked at me, some of the venom was gone. Her attitude toward me had become slightly more cautious.

The rest of the Chinese Hunters were rigid with military discipline. Before, they couldn't even meet my eyes—now they were trying not to make a sound breathing.

"I apologize!"

Gwan Hacheon, the branch director of the Sichuan Hunter Association, suddenly bowed his head to me.

"What's this about all of a sudden. I'm tired, so get to the point."

"The combat footage was leaked! The Hunter journalists leaked the video before we could even contain it!"

"Ah, that? Don't care."

Normally, S-Rank Hunter combat footage is classified, so it almost never spreads. Because there are those who would analyze it to figure out an S-Rank Hunter's strengths and weaknesses.

I didn't care. You figured out my strengths and weaknesses? So what—are you stronger than me?

Besides, I hadn't shown everything in this fight anyway. And the Hunter journalists did exactly what I needed them to do.

'Now that my power is known, the riffraff will lower their heads on their own. The government and the Hunter Association will watch my every move too. Hiding your power is for when your power is ambiguous. When you have overwhelming strength, the story changes.'

My life in the real world was about to get a lot easier.

"Sung Yujin. Would you join us for a post-mission dinner? A friend of mine runs a restaurant. It's a bit out of the way, but I guarantee the food. The Myriad Beast Empress says she'll attend if you do."

My eyes met Zhang Haran's. Arms crossed, she didn't look away. If anything, she seemed to be asking what I was staring at.

"We did complete a mission together, even if it was brief. A post-mission dinner is the least we can do. I don't have any plans after this either."

I came all the way to China and met Ryu Cheongseol—I should at least get a taste before I leave.

'Heh heh. I'm looking forward to the after-party of the after-party.'
