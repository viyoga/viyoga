,"Hmm."

I turn the chip containing dark mana this way and that, examining it closely.

There was nothing I could discern.

I am studying magical engineering, but I'm not skilled enough to recognize a chip instantly. In the first place, I'm confident in the magical aspect of magical engineering, but I don't know anything about the engineering part. This feels closer to engineering than black magic.

,'In times like these, it's faster and more certain to ask an expert than to make a judgment on my own.'

There are several methods. Find a decent mechanic, or commission a broker.

,'Fortunately, the best expert in this field is nearby.'

I took the chip and headed to the Transcender's ancient ruins.

,"Haha. You really fought fiercely. You're the first magician I've seen shoot a railgun with magic. May I ask why you're doing such an inefficient thing?"

As soon as I entered the ancient ruins, I heard Varkase's words.

,"Inefficient, you say?"

,"Railguns can be used without magic, can't they? The R675 rifle is a prime example."

,"That's for military use. And it's one of the things the military manages with paranoia. It's hard to get, and even if you do, special forces will show up and drag you away the moment you use it."

,"Hmm. Is that how it is these days? In my day, we all carried around a railgun or so."

,"That was an interesting era."

,"It was a fucking era in many ways."

I walked down the hallway and held up the chip in my hand. Dark mana was leaking from the small chip.

,"I have a favor to ask. It's a chip that was inside a monster's body. Can you analyze it?"

,"That's not a difficult request. I'll grant it. I'm also interested in that chip."

I came inside. Bell Terrier was still undergoing adjustments in the capsule. Varkase's hologram appeared next to it and pointed to a spot.

,"Do you see that scratch at the bottom of the wall over there? Put the chip there. I'll scan it first and then connect to it."

I threw the chip. The chip flew accurately toward the scratch. Just before the chip entered the scratch, I used telekinesis to gently place it down.

,"Wow. You have skills that are unlike a magician."

,"I'm good at throwing and hitting things."

,"Wait a moment. It won't take long."

I sat in a chair. Since there was nothing to do, I looked at Bell, who was inside the capsule. As the character I set, her body was outstanding. I never get tired of looking at it.

,'Mechanical arms and legs. I wonder what it feels like to have eyes attached. Would it be comfortable?'

But I had no intention of attaching machines to my body.

For a magician who directly manipulates mana, machines are more of a hindrance. What's important to a magician is the balance of mana, and if you replace part of your body with a machine, it breaks that balance. Can't you balance it with a machine? A machine of that level would be incredibly expensive. And the adjustment is tough too. You have to get regular adjustments from a master-level mechanic. In my opinion, machines only have disadvantages for magicians.

,"The results are in. Interesting. It's structurally very similar to a chip I know."

,"Is it dangerous?"

,"It is dangerous. Do you know what a Mutant Chip is?"

Of course, I knew. I pondered for a moment. Would it be more beneficial to pretend I didn't know? After considering this and that, I decided to just pretend I knew. I didn't want to bother listening to Varkase's explanation.

,"...I know it as a biochip made by Voidists to accept Void Energy more easily. It causes mutations to make the body more receptive to Void Energy."

,"You know exactly what it is. The chip you brought is structurally very similar to a Mutant Chip. The difference is that it's made with dark mana instead of Void Energy."

,"Does it mutate the body to be suitable for dark mana?"

I asked in a serious tone.

I felt that something had gone wrong. This kind of thing didn't exist in my original knowledge.

,"No. Dark mana plays a role in accelerating the mutation of the Mutant Chip."

,"A chip to mutate the body while completely excluding Void Energy... If it's related to the Kabala Research Institute, the purpose is obvious."

The Kabala Research Institute, under Kabalia Industries, has branches all over the world, not just here. What the Kabala Research Institute does is conduct bio-experiments. They catch animals in various regions and conduct bio-experiments. Of course, that includes humans as well.

The reason why Kabalia Industries maintains its position as the number one company in the bio field is all thanks to the Kabala Research Institute.

,'They also have a huge market share in the food industry.'

They create and distribute livestock such as chickens, pigs, and cows as clones. With cheap prices and enormous quantities, they can't help but dominate the food market.

,'These bastards used to mass-produce humans in the past....'

A project to create the best army with clones of a legendary soldier called a legend.

That project failed.

Unlike livestock, humans needed a growth period of at least 10 years. Above all, the problem was that the legendary soldier's combat talents were not manifested in the clones. There were clones who manifested some of that talent, but it was still ridiculously insufficient compared to the original.

,"After a detailed analysis, it seems the name of this chip is called the Dark Chip. What are you going to do with this chip?"

,"If it's a product of the Kabalia Research Institute, there will be many interested parties, so I'm thinking of selling it through a broker. I can make some money."

,"You're not thinking of using it yourself, are you?"

,"Not even if I get shot in the head."

,"That's a relief. The Dark Chip has no directionality. No one knows how it will mutate. It's a chip made solely for the purpose of mutation by maximizing the influence of the Dark Moon."

,"Thanks for the analysis."

I retrieved the chip and sat in the chair.

,"Are you going to wait for her? Didn't you say you have no relationship?"

,"I have to say goodbye before I leave. I'm also curious about what will happen to her...."

,"Isn't your purpose to see her body?"

,"......."

Her capsule opened. Bell, who quickly came out of the capsule, immediately picked up her clothes.

,"Hey, can you take your eyes off me? That's manners."

,"I thought you didn't mind... I was rude."

I immediately turned around and looked at the wall. I had already seen everything, so there were no regrets.

,"How do you feel? Does anything hurt?"

,"Not bad. It's the best, rather. Today is the first time I realized my body could be this light. Thank you, Varkase-ssi."

,"It was a simple task."

After that, they talked about machines. In particular, they talked in depth about mechanical eyes... It was too technical, so I couldn't understand it.

,"Transcenders' facilities are hidden all over this world. The exact location is... I can't tell you. I don't know either. Haha. All of the Transcenders' facilities will be helpful to you. So, find them when you have time."

,"...We're not Transcenders, though."

,"In my eyes, you are already walking the path of transcendence. Being a Transcender is nothing special. If you have the qualifications and walk the path of transcendence... that's what a Transcender is. It doesn't matter if you deny it. I will treat you as Transcenders."

,"Varkase-ssi. Why don't you just honestly say you've been lonely until now?"

,"Haha. I can't deny it. Come back next year to receive a mana shower. Waste accumulates just by breathing, so it's good to clean it up periodically. Especially in the city, it accumulates faster."

,"I'll come again next time. Please adjust me then too."

,"Come again when you have the chance."

Bell and I said goodbye to Varkase and left the ancient ruins.

I didn't know Varkase would welcome us like this.

,'In the original work, I had to gradually advance the relationship through quests....'

I walked down the mountain with Bell and had a conversation.

,"That gorilla monster was resurrected and attacked? Don't talk nonsense. I confirmed it was dead... Ah, damn it. Today is the Dark Moon, isn't it? That makes sense."

,"I got a Dark Chip from his body. This is mine. It's a product of the Kabala Research Institute, so I can sell it for a high price."

,"I got a few of those, so that's what they call Dark Chips."

I told her about the Dark Chip.

,"...Wow. It seems like it would be expensive if you sold it."

,"Don't you want to sell it?"

,"For now, I'm going to investigate it a little more. I have a colleague in the city who specializes in this kind of thing. If it doesn't seem right, I can just sell it to guys who hate Kabalia."

After talking about this and that, we quickly arrived at the town.

,"Let's part ways here. Can you give me your email address? I'll contact you later. I won't forget what happened today. Contact me if you need my help. If I don't have anything urgent, I'll help you with top priority."

,"Are you leaving right away? There are still several hours before sunrise. It will be dangerous."

,"I came on a bike. I can't take the train because I'm an illegal immigrant. The Dark Moon is okay. Just focus on avoiding fighting."

,"Okay. Contact me if you need my help. I'll help you. But it's not free. I don't owe you anything."

We exchanged emails. She smiled and said.

,"I'm lucky today. Thanks to the Transcender, my body is better, and I've also become acquainted with a skilled magician. Did the coins I threw into the fountain during the day bring me luck?"

,"Maybe."

Bell waved goodbye and left the town on her bike.

I sat blankly on a bench in front of the station and looked up at the black moon. I was going to wait until dawn and the first train arrived.

,'The hotels probably won't be open because of the Dark Moon. I should have rented a suitable room before leaving the town... I made a mistake.'

I was thinking about magic in my head.

Jijik.

I heard a noise.

At first, I ignored it, thinking I had misheard.

Jijijik.

There are no two coincidences. I strained my eyes and looked in the direction of the sound.

A dark alleyway.

Something pure white was looking at me from there.

It was the moment I blinked. It instantly closed the 10m distance.

A white dress, hair close to white, pale skin. Thin arms and legs. Simply put, it was a ghost.

,'Because it's the Dark Moon, all sorts of things are popping out.'

I blink.

The ghost got closer in an instant. I deliberately blinked my eyes in succession. The ghost appeared right in front of me and screamed.

,"Kyaaaaaaaaaaak!"

Its mouth was open like a shark's mouth. Hundreds of small teeth were embedded throughout its mouth. On top of that, it emitted a terrible stench.

,"Shut up."

I grabbed the neck of the ghost, which was running to bite my head, with one hand and used magic.

[Lightning Grab]

Pajijijijijik!

The blue current generated from my hand electrocuted the ghost's body.

,"Kkiaaaaaaaaak!"

The ghost screamed with an ear-splitting scream and fell to the floor. Its body turned into particles and disappeared.

I, who was about to think about magic again and doze off, felt a gaze and raised my head. I saw a red-eyed man hanging upside down from the ceiling of the station. And a disgusting lump of flesh on the railroad side, and a woman crawling upside down like a spider from the store side.

,"Ha, they're making it not boring."

I got up from my seat. Mana races through the mana rod. Lightning sparks from my fingertips.

,"Okay, let's play around."
