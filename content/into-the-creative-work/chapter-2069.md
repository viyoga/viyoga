The demon's head splits in half, and its corpse collapses downwards, limp.

I didn't let my guard down and kept watching Anabes. Demons basically weren't normal creatures. They were bastards who pulled off things that defied common sense. Even with its head split in half, there was no telling what kind of bizarre shit it might pull.

Cheolkeok.

Die once and revive to inflict a fatal wound on an unsuspecting enemy. Isn't that a trick I use often? I didn't let my guard down and prepared for a finishing blow.

Rank: A

Reflects a portion of the damage received back to the target.』

Demon Hunter(SS)'s certainty and power absorption.

Maybe originally it could reflect attacks too.

,Demon Hunter(SS) doesn't fully absorb the power of the demon it kills. When absorbed, it's degraded from the original.

Maybe Anabes could reflect attacks too. If it wasn't for the surprise attack using the Ghost Mantle, I would have struggled quite a bit.

,Reflection won't be of much help.

Intentionally receive an enemy's attack, suffer a fatal wound, use the power of reflection, and then die. Revive using Complete Recovery and continue the battle again. That was the battle method that immediately came to mind upon seeing Reflection.

,It's more likely that it won't work against strong opponents.

I had no intention of relying on something like Reflection. Instead of intentionally getting attacked and using Reflection, it's better to charge in and swing my sword.

"I only lured him, but there was a possibility that a demon from another legion could have appeared. Luck is on our side. I feel good because it's going according to plan."

Then we moved on to the next stage.

Elena and Yuria worked on the magic circle, the demon summoning circle, drawn on the floor. They modified the summoning circle with the blood of the demon count Anabes and decorated the end with his chopped-up corpse.

I don't know much about magic circles, but they seemed to have easily understood the magic circles of this world. They said it wasn't much different from the magic circles of their world.

Elena checked the magic circle one last time and nodded.

"All preparations are complete. If the 2nd Legion Commander Samael is as we know him, he will come in person."

Usually, the legion commander doesn't move directly.

The stronger the demon, the more restrictions are placed on them. But this dungeon, which sealed the ancient demon, is different. Even a legion commander has almost no burden here.

Anabes, belonging to the 2nd Legion Commander, died. Not only that, but they are trying to summon a demon with Anabes' corpse.

A provocation and trap that anyone can see.

But Samael can't refuse. He was a great demon who ruled over thousands, tens of thousands of demons. The conditions to exert almost perfect power have been met, but we are afraid and refuse the summoning? Samael's leadership will be questioned by his subordinates. He must prove his dominance.

『Demon Hunter(SS) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『Demon Hunter(SS) warns you.』

Sssseuuuuuuuu.

"Oh, do you dare test my love? I am saddened."

The bastard shed tears of blood with a benevolent smile on his face.

I felt a sense of oppression. My whole body tensed up. A shiver ran down my spine. I gripped Hwaryeon Bido tightly and burned with the will to fight.
