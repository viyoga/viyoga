Bright, the leader of Luminosity who had seized the broadcasting station, thought.

,Things are going well.'

In fact, he thought that even if he seized the broadcasting station, it would only last 30 minutes at most. The bigwigs with a lot on their conscience would try to prevent their dirty secrets from being revealed at all costs.

Even if they couldn't order the military to come to Seoul, there was a high probability that they would forcibly send a few of the Great Magic Unit commanders dispatched elsewhere to the broadcasting station.

But 30 minutes passed, and an hour passed, but the Great Magic Unit did not appear at the broadcasting station.

Things were going too well.

,I even uploaded a video prepared in case the plan failed to overseas sites... the plan has already succeeded.'

The internet was already in chaos. The President of South Korea and the top corporations were involved. It would have been strange if it wasn't chaotic.

"Bright."

The man sitting at the desk to Bright's right, tapping on a laptop, called him. He was a man with a gaunt face and horn-rimmed glasses. It was So Dongwoon, a former reporter. He captured the truth in video with transparent camera magic.

"What is it? Did the Great Magic Unit finally show up?"

"No, I found out why the Great Magic Unit can't come to the broadcasting station. They seem to be fighting other Harriers."

He showed the laptop screen. The 3rd Battalion Commander Yoo Minha was fighting a Western magician on the road. There were also pictures of the 1st Battalion Commander fighting a foreign magician.

"Westerners? Could it be Lemegeton? Why did those bastards suddenly intervene?"

"They probably want chaos in South Korea. Or they're trying to eliminate the Great Magic Unit commanders while they have the chance. The important thing is that it helped us."

Bright frowned. Thanks to Lemegeton, things were going well, but this was their plan. Moreover, the intervention of Lemegeton, which has nothing to do with Korea? He didn't like it.

"How's the situation? Do you think the Great Magic Unit commanders will be pushed back?"

"Well, it's hard to say for sure in a battle. But the Great Magic Unit's skills have improved since Seong Yujin-ssi appeared. Besides, there's also the existence of potions."

"...That's right. Potions. Recovery potions are recovery potions, but mana potions are annoying."

Mana potions that restore magic power.

What's important when a magician uses magic is magic power. If you have enough magic power, you can spam magic. In particular, even Ars Magna, which consumes a tremendous amount of magic power, can be used continuously if you have enough magic power.

"I wish both Lemegeton and the Great Magic Unit would die."

So Dongwoon said with a sigh. Bright let out a dry laugh at that.

"We'll be the ones to die first, though."

In the end, that bastard will step in.

Bright was certain.

And a few minutes later, So Dongwoon said in a serious tone.

"That bastard appeared. On the roof. I heard he can even use space-time magic.... I should be glad he didn't appear inside the broadcasting station...."

"If it's the roof, that's where Wild is guarding. What about the camera?"

"I already sent it. Are you really going to broadcast it live?"

"Until now, the strongest of the Great Magic Unit and the symbol of the Order Country was the 1st Battalion Commander Hwang Seungmin. But Seong Yujin-ssi appeared and changed that. The Harriers fear Seong Yujin-ssi more than Hwang Seungmin. Even magicians who aren't Harriers fear Seong Yujin-ssi. And now, the people of Korea mention Seong Yujin-ssi more than Hwang Seungmin. Seong Yujin-ssi is by no means invincible, and it proves that the Great Magic Unit has clear limitations."

Seong Yujin-ssi began to be broadcast live on the channel. Seong Yujin-ssi glanced at the camera. As expected. He noticed the existence of the transparent camera. And he ignored the camera.

,Does he not care? He's so arrogant.'

The battle between Wild and Seong Yujin-ssi began.

And Seong Yujin-ssi won overwhelmingly.

,He uses not only fire, water, wind, space-time magic, and potion making, but also magnetic force magic? No, considering that electricity sparked, he must have used electromagnetic force. I should see it as electric magic.'

The situation was not good.

He has to defeat Seong Yujin-ssi to prove it, but he's rather making Seong Yujin-ssi stand out. Still, he watched for now. He still had a card left.

Seong Yujin-ssi, who came down from the roof, moved without ignoring the strong presence he felt.

,I'll take care of the remaining Luminosity bastards here.'

The Luminosity bastards can't escape outside the broadcasting station anyway. This is their last struggle. They probably don't have any plans to escape.

,Things have come to this, there's no way the government won't do anything. Even if the military can't move, the police special forces must have moved.'

There was a high probability that they were preparing to snipe from an unseen place.

,I feel magic power.'

He felt a strong presence of magic power. The hostility and murderous intent mixed in the magic power were certain. If he passed by even after feeling this, he would be called a coward. He let out a hollow laugh because it was such an obvious provocation.

,He's begging me to kill him. I'll have to kill him as he wishes.'

Seong Yujin-ssi headed to where the bastard was.

Oh Jungchul, a Luminosity executive, greeted Seong Yujin-ssi standing tall in the hallway. The young man wore an anachronistic Hanbok. He wore a navy blue dopo and a gat on his head. He even wore a hwandu around his waist.

He looked like a scholar from the Joseon Dynasty who had flown to modern times.

"Is cosplay your hobby?"

"My grimoire is this Hanbok. I wore the gat because it goes well with this Hanbok."

"What about the sword?"

"It's a weapon."

Oh Jungchul swung his sword.

He and Seong Yujin-ssi were more than 10m apart, but it didn't matter. His magic was a magic that fired a slash from the swung sword. An invisible slash flies towards Seong Yujin-ssi.

Seong Yujin-ssi moved slightly to the side to avoid the slash. Even if he couldn't see it, he could feel the magic power that made up the slash.

"Indeed, your calm attitude is amazing. But I'm not here in this hallway for nothing. It's impossible to avoid all the slashes flying continuously in this narrow space. Ars Magna, Slash Dance."

Oh Jungchul's Slash Dance fires three slashes at once. He even stopped breathing and fired the slashes.

Steam appeared in front of Seong Yujin-ssi. The steam soon became a large amount of water. The water containing magic power took the form of a dragon and rushed towards Oh Jungchul. Oh Jungchul thought he could cut the water dragon with his slash.

But he couldn't cut it. The water strengthened with magic power was much harder than Oh Jungchul thought. Moreover, it was impossible to avoid because the hallway was blocked by walls on both sides.

Kwakwakwakwa!

In the end, Oh Jungchul was pushed by the water dragon and fell to the floor. Seong Yujin-ssi looked at him with a pathetic look.

"It's not strong just because there are many slashes. It's strong if you can cut. Slashes that can't cut are meaningless."

Seong Yujin-ssi flicked his hand. A water blade cut his neck.

Seong Yujin-ssi ignored the camera still filming him and went downstairs.

"Don't move!!"

It was a hostage situation.

A man with a gun was pointing it at the head of a broadcasting station female employee. Seong Yujin-ssi felt that his magic power was terrible and knew that he was a low-level member of Luminosity.

Seong Yujin-ssi walked forward.

"I told you not to move! Do you not care if this woman dies?!"

Seong Yujin-ssi stopped and looked at the woman. She had ordinary beauty. Since it's a broadcasting station, there must be celebrities and beautiful employees if you look for them, so why did he take such an ordinary woman hostage?

,...This hostage situation is not accidental. Because the camera is filming me. If I ignore the hostage and attack, public opinion towards me will worsen. I'll be treated like a criminal. But if I don't do anything? They'll criticize me for being incompetent.'

Seong Yujin-ssi raised both hands. As the protagonist in this world, it wasn't good to just kill everyone.

"Haha, yes. Just stay still like that. My colleague will be here soon. I will never take the gun off this woman's head."

Of course, it wasn't like he was paying attention to public opinion. Magic power moved quietly. Wind blades cut off the hostage taker's hands and neck.

"You stupid bastard. What kind of hostage situation is it if you can't even sense magic power?"

"Kyaaaa! T, thank you!"

The woman sobbed and approached Seong Yujin-ssi. The moment the distance narrowed, the woman's eyes changed drastically. A small blade that popped out of her sleeve aimed for Seong Yujin-ssi's neck.

Seogeok!

"You knew... from the beginning? How...?"

"First. There was plenty of time to escape, so why are you holding a hostage situation here? Second. You checked my neck first when we first met, right? Third. Your acting was terrible."

Kwajik.

Cruel? Seong Yujin-ssi didn't care.

He just wore makeup like a woman and dressed like a woman. What's even more terrible is that he even wore hot pants and fishnet stockings.

The power of the Gayslayer, which kills even gods if they are gay, was still there.
