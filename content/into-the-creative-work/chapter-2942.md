Commander Kalpas exited the command center.

Gestag’s full-scale offensive had not yet begun. Thanks to this, the soldiers within the outpost were able to prepare for battle.

“Commander-nim. It’s dangerous. Please command from the headquarters.”

Kalpas’s adjutant said. Kalpas did not return to the command center.

“The strategy has already been issued. All that’s left is to hold out on the field. There’s nothing we can do from the command center. Is the General bug who came as a Death God still at the main gate?”

“Yes. As per Commander-nim’s orders, we are stalling for time without attacking or responding. But even that is slowly reaching its limit.”

“Tell them I’m coming. We can stall for at least 10 more minutes.”

Kalpas slowly walked towards the main gate.

There was no fear. From the moment he became the commander of this outpost, he had accepted his Resolve to die. However, he would not die without meaning.

Kalpas climbed onto the main gate. He saw a General bug standing a short distance from the main gate.

“Are you the leader of this swarm?”

The General bug spoke. The antennae on its head moved busily. It was sharing the situation with the General bugs on this battlefield.

Even if Kalpas killed that General bug, the Gestag forces under its command would not waver. The command authority would immediately transfer to another nearby General bug. He didn’t know the locations of those General bugs. Therefore, a strategy of only eliminating General bugs was meaningless.

“That’s right. I am the commander here.”

“I propose a truce. The condition is handing over Seong Yujin.”

There was only one reason Gestag wanted Seong Yujin.

Seong Yujin’s genes.

They wanted Seong Yujin’s abilities and Superpower. It wasn’t strange, as there were Gestag bugs that used Superpower.

Kalpas took a deep drag from his Cigarette and exhaled.

The population of the Lock 3 Planet Outpost was approximately 150,000. The Gestag forces besieging the outpost currently numbered around 100,000, but… if war broke out, reinforcements would come. It had to be at least 1,000,000 or more.

If Gestag was determined, holding out without fleet support was nearly impossible.

“Sometimes, one life is more important than hundreds of thousands of lives. He is the future of humanity, a hero. We cannot sell a hero. I refuse your proposal.”

Kalpas held a rifle in one Hand and aimed it at the General bug’s head. The General bug stood firm, only its antennae moving. The General bug had come here anticipating death.

Tang-tang-tang!

Three holes were pierced in the General bug’s head.

Gestag’s army, without any Shout, began to charge towards the outpost all at once. Turrets spat fire, and marines fired shells. Anti-aircraft missiles flew towards the flying objects coming from the sky.

Gunshots and cannon fire mixed in the once-silent desert. The ground shook, and black smoke billowed into the sky.

Kalpas glared at the battlefield and spoke as if spitting.

“For humanity.”

Swaaaaaaa!

Behind Kalpas, nuclear missiles were continuously launched from the silos, flying to various locations. They would strike the designated coordinates and block the incoming reinforcements.

‘And open the path for the hero.’

Kwaaaaaaaang!

In the distance, a nuclear explosion erupted, and a mushroom cloud rose. Afterward, mushroom clouds began to bloom one by one.

‘It would have been good if we could have dealt with the Queen bug with nuclear missiles….’

Gestag wasn’t that foolish. The hive where the Queen bug resided was exceptionally tough, making it impossible to penetrate even with nuclear weapons. An anti-matter bomb might have been a possibility, but… that would sweep away the outpost too, killing everyone.

After all 15 nukes had detonated, a single black craft soared towards the sky. It raced at a speed comparable to a fighter jet through the path temporarily cleared by the nuclear missiles.

The hero had begun the operation.

Now, only one Assignment remained for Kalpas.

‘Hold out until the hero captures the Queen bug alive.’

• • •

I wore a special forces power suit and equipped the Battle Exosuit Albatross Prototype.

Through the helmet’s UHD display, I could see Albatross’s equipment.

「Albatross Prototype connected.」

「Welcome, Colonel Seong-nim.」

「I am Albatross Prototype’s combat assistance AI. Please assign a name.」

“You’re Alba.”

「Confirmed.」

I slowly moved my Body. It felt like wearing another power suit over a power suit.

“The response is a bit sluggish. Reconfigure it.”

「Configuration complete.」

‘It’s certainly more comfortable than a standard marine power suit. The advanced AI assists, allowing me to conserve strength and stamina.’

This way, I could engage in combat for longer.

The 10 wing thrusters attached to my back also moved this way and that.

‘It’s a bit awkward, but I’ve got the hang of it. Once I get used to it, I’ll be able to maneuver much more freely than with a jetpack.’

With preparations complete, I looked up at the sky from the open ground.

Nuclear missiles were continuously launched from the silos, flying to the other side in an instant.

Commander Kalpas used all 15 nuclear missiles stored at the outpost for this operation. Thanks to this, the path to the great hive where the Queen bug resided was clearly opened.

「Engine readiness complete.」

“Launching.”

I leaped towards the sky. Energy burst from the wings, and my Body flew forward. It felt like being shot. Even with the power suit, I could feel the gravitational acceleration.

「Approximately 2 minutes remaining until the target, the great hive.」

Just 2 minutes. I couldn’t say that. As soon as I flew into the sky, flying Gestag bugs immediately began to follow. Fortunately, the path was opened by the nuclear explosion, so there were only 2-3 of them. If Kalpas hadn’t used nuclear missiles, at least dozens would have followed.

‘Three dragonfly-shaped ones, huh. Perfect for a warm-up.’

I flew head-on towards the bug circling and following me in the sky. The bug didn’t stop. It intended to block me, even if it died.

I narrowly passed by its side and slashed it with the Vibe Sword in my Hand. Thanks to the flying speed, I could cut it in half just by holding the Vibe Sword in my Hand.

The remaining two flew in from each side.

‘Eject.’

Six subspace ejection ports appeared in the air. Six Vibe Swords were simultaneously fired, embedding themselves in their Bodies. The bugs fell, and I reverse-summoned the Vibe Swords before flying towards the destination again.

Afterward, a few more flying Gestag bugs chased me, but I shot them down with the guided missiles equipped on Albatross.

While flying, I saw an army advancing towards the outpost.

It was an army of over 10,000, including large Giant bugs, Cannon bugs, Soldier bugs, and more. I slightly changed direction, flew over them, and dropped bombs, bombing them. My Inventory opened, and bombs rained down.

‘Right now, I’m a fighter jet that doesn’t need resupply.’

Attacks came from the ground, but they couldn’t shoot me down as I flew erratically through the sky.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwang!

I annihilated one army. Wiping out 10,000 bugs so spectacularly made me feel good too.

I had merely dropped the bombs from my Inventory, so it didn’t require any particular effort.

‘This is the power of Inventory.’

Flying at maximum output, Albatross’s battery quickly drained. It didn’t matter. Thanks to the Inventory’s effect, a new battery was automatically loaded.

「Arrived at destination.」

Dealing with bugs that popped out along the way, I arrived at the great hive over 3 minutes later than expected.

There was a colossal structure that looked like it was woven from dark red branches. Even after being directly hit by nuclear missiles, only a part of the great hive had collapsed, and the interior where the Queen bug resided was not visible from the outside.

‘They say the internal structure is so complex that even bombing it from the outside doesn’t transmit the shock to the interior.’

To capture the Queen bug alive, I had no choice but to enter the great hive directly.

I descended to the entrance of the great hive. On the scorching hot ground, I saw cooked bug Corpses. They were those that had died from the aftermath of the nuclear explosion.

Paaaat!

Soldier bugs hidden underground or behind walls rushed at me.

‘A few are still alive, huh.’

I gripped shotguns in both Hands and blasted the rushing bugs.

Kwang! Kwang! Kwang! Kwang!

The bugs’ Heads exploded cleanly.

Kieeeek!

Bugs hidden nearby rushed out one after another.

‘There are quite a few of them.’

There was no need to fight fairly.

‘Eject.’

Grenades dropped.

Peong!

After they died, I dropped back to the ground.

Kieeeek!

A Soldier bug hidden among the Corpses rushed at me. I subtly dodged to the side and threw a Fist. Its Head exploded.

‘This is the power of the Battle Exosuit. It’s incomparable to a power suit.’

The entrance to the great hive was extremely wide. It was spacious enough for a tank to enter. The reason for such a wide space was obvious. Bugs would be swarming inside.

To my eyes, the great hive looked like a colossal Trap.

‘If I didn’t have Inventory, I would have retreated. No, I wouldn’t have even launched an attack in the first place, just defended the outpost.’

I took out anti-Gestag poison gas canisters from my Inventory and threw them into the interior of the great hive.

Green gas filled the space and flowed inward.

Kyaaaaaaaak!

The Scream of bugs could be heard from inside the great hive. Kung-kung-kung! Impact sounds echoed from various places, as if they were slamming their Bodies against the walls.

Bugs rushed out towards the entrance.

I took out a flamethrower from my Inventory.

“I’ll burn them all to ashes.”

Puhaaaaaaa!

The bugs touched by the napalm-mixed flames began to not just burn, but melt completely. Unfortunately, the walls of the great hive did not burn.

Originally, flamethrowers were Weapons that consumed a lot of fuel, making them difficult to use for long. However, I had stockpiled plenty of fuel in my Inventory, so it was no problem.

I blasted flames and detonated poison gas canisters, making my Entry into the great hive’s interior.

Kuwoooong!

With a tremendous roar, a strong gust of wind blew from inside the great hive. As soon as I saw the poison gas being pushed back, I reverse-summoned the flamethrower. I threw a thermobaric bomb into the echoing passage and pulled out a shield to protect my Body.

Kwaaaang!

The explosion subsided, and I threw another poison gas canister. The wind was still blowing continuously. The poison gas couldn’t enter the interior.

‘That’s definitely not natural wind. What the hell is it?’

I entered with a shotgun and shield. The passage was too short and complex, like a maze, to fly through.

I went inside, following the bugs I saw, and came face to face with it.

It was 7 meters tall, bipedal like a human, with four arms equipped with bone Swords and bone spears. Its entire Body was covered in black carapace, like muscle. There were holes all over the carapace, from which wind was spewing.

「Named entity confirmed.」

「Giant bug variant.」

「Entity name: Slatter」

「Possesses Aerokinesis.」

“A Superpower bug, huh.”
