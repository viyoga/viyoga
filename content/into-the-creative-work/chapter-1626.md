-It can't be...!

Unlike the Demon King, who accepted death, Leosio thrashed and struggled until the very end. But the massive body of the magic dragon wouldn't budge. All it could manage was a feeble twitch.

Having its heart pierced was a big deal. No matter how much of a dragon it was, with a hole in its heart, it couldn't manipulate mana. It couldn't use magic anymore. The same went for dragon speech. After all, the source of dragon speech was the dragon's heart.

The magic dragon was dying. Unless someone saved it, the outcome wouldn't change. And there was no one here to save the magic dragon.

"Leosio. It's over."

Priscilla, in her human form, stood before the magic dragon. The magic dragon glared at Priscilla.

-Priscilla...! What's the reason? Why are you interfering with me! Aren't you and I the same dragon!

"Because we are the same dragon. How can I ignore your disgraceful behavior, which is a disgrace to all dragons?"

-Disgraceful? My plan was a great undertaking to overcome death! An immortal dragon! A plan to become the god of this world! Because of me, the honor of dragons will be elevated, and because of me, dragons will become gods!

"It is against the natural order. You should have learned it from your parents. Dragons are beings that uphold the natural order."

-That's just a bunch of old-fashioned bullshit!

"That old-fashioned bullshit is our identity."

-Priscilla!!!

Leosio roared. It opened its mouth towards Priscilla, but that was it. It couldn't move to swallow Priscilla, nor could it breathe fire.

"Leosio. Let go of your regrets. Death comes to everyone. Accept it."

-No, I can't accept it! It's not over yet, not yet...! Not yet!!

It was over.

The magic dragon's body began to collapse.

I narrowed my brows. It was a dragon's corpse. It would definitely be helpful. Especially Leosio's heart would be a great help in repairing the Hwaryeon Bido.

"Why is that thing collapsing?"

It was Priscilla who answered my question.

"It forcibly evolved its body while accepting both impure and pure mana. It didn't even succeed in that evolution. Leosio's body is collapsing due to the mana. There's no stopping it."

"What a waste."

"......."

Priscilla glared at me. Of course, I didn't look away. Priscilla had agreed to do as I told her. She was mine now.

-Do you think it will end like this...!

Leosio tried to struggle until the very end. But Leosio's words grew fainter and fainter. In less than a minute, Leosio's words became inaudible. The magic dragon's body also collapsed, leaving only its head. That head was also gradually disappearing.

I knew that the Demon King wasn't completely dead yet.

"Demon King. Do you have any last words?"

-No. I failed. That's the end of the story.

That composed attitude was the same as in the original story. Even in the original story, he calmly accepted his death when facing it. It was in stark contrast to Leosio, who struggled until the very end.

The magic dragon's head collapsed and disappeared.

With this, the matter was over. There was no one in this world who could threaten me.

"It's not over yet."

Priscilla said. I quickly snapped to attention and looked up at what Priscilla was pointing at. The dozens of magic stone gates in the sky were still pouring out impure mana, even though Leosio and the Demon King were dead.

"We have to close the magic stone gates. If all the pure mana here is contaminated... it will definitely affect the world."

"A cleanup, huh? Dealing with things like nuclear bombs is a pain in the ass."

"I'll take care of it. You guys go outside and block the aftermath."

Just as I was about to answer that I understood, a question arose. Can Priscilla really handle that many magic stone gates alone right now?

"Are you going to seal the magic stone gates one by one?"

"...You're being informal. Are you planning to act like my master?"

"Didn't you say you'd do anything? You're mine now. Answer my questions."

"Hmm. What if I don't want to answer?"

Priscilla retorted. I didn't feel any hostility towards me. I had no intention of playing push and pull with her, so I looked for an answer.

"Yuria. You answer."

"It's impossible to seal all the magic stone gates at once. Sealing is a delicate task, and Priscilla-nim doesn't have the capacity to do that task right now. Time is also running out. In half a day, all the mana will be contaminated. It seems Priscilla-nim is thinking of self-destructing."

"I will never allow self-destruction."

I said as if declaring it. And then I asked Yuria.

"What about taking our time and slowly sealing the magic stone gates?"

"The space is gradually closing. If the space closes completely... we may never be able to come to this other dimension again. But this space won't disappear."

Priscilla smiled weakly.

"This is the only way. If we leave those things here... they will definitely become poison. They will slowly eat away at the world, and the world will gradually become desolate and eventually perish."

"Yuria. Is there really no other way?"

"Even if I help, it's impossible to seal that many magic stone gates in half a day. But we have Elena-nim with us now."

Everyone's eyes turned to Elena. Elena calmly accepted the gathered gazes.

"So, I have to step up in the end."

"Elena. Is it possible?"

"It would be possible if I sacrificed myself."

"Is that so? Then don't do it."

"...Are you saying that sincerely? As you know, even if I sacrifice myself here, no harm will come to pass. It's more efficient for me to sacrifice myself here."

"Efficiency be damned, I won't allow you to sacrifice yourself like this. You can't even use Complete Recovery right now. If necessary, I'll step up."

"I can see in your eyes that you're serious. I don't know what you're planning to do, but give it up. I'll do it. Actually, I haven't even gotten a proper estimate yet."

Elena raised her head and looked around.

"Looking roughly, it doesn't seem like I need to use Limit Break. That's a relief. I came to this world, so I should enjoy my vacation."

Blue butterflies appeared around Elena. At a glance, there were over 300 butterflies flying in all directions.

I felt the space stabilizing. The mana, which had been as rough as the sea, became as calm as a lake.

"T, this is...."

Priscilla, who was sensitive to mana, looked quite flustered. Elena didn't even glance at Priscilla and said.

"There's no need to be surprised. I've only fixed the mana to prepare for any unforeseen aftermath. There may be variables if those magic stone gates suddenly disappear.... Now. Shall we erase them one by one?"

She smiled as if she was really looking forward to what was about to happen. She raised her right arm. Her straight index finger pointed to one of the magic stone gates.

"Should I say I was lucky? This space is isolated from the outside. Unlike the infinitely vast outside, the limits of the space are clear. If it were outside, I couldn't have guaranteed success even if I used Limit Break. But this limited space is different."

She moved her index finger slightly. The magic stone gate floating in space disappeared as it was.

"Have you ever had a lucid dream? I have them quite often. In my dreams, I can create whatever I want. I've created and enjoyed a happy world for myself. But I couldn't just purely enjoy lucid dreams. A world that I can control can be easily broken with just a small mistake."

Every time her finger moved, a magic stone gate in the sky was erased. She then spread out her palm and swept across the night sky. Every time her palm passed, the magic stone gates disappeared, and starlight appeared and twinkled.

Like that for 10 seconds. The magic stone gates in the sky disappeared without a trace. Only countless starlight twinkled there.

"It's over. Cough!"

With a cough, Elena's body collapsed. I quickly approached Elena and caught her body.

With a pale complexion, she leaned her body obediently against me.

"Haru. My lifespan is only one day left now. Eugene. Will you cry for me?"

Before I could answer, Yuria stepped forward.

"Elena-nim. That's enough with the jokes. You have Complete Recovery, so didn't you calculate one day and use your strength? If you hadn't created that starlight over there, you wouldn't have collapsed."

"Hehe. You're quick to notice."

Anyway, it was true that Elena had worked hard. If she hadn't been there, things might not have turned out so well.

I hugged Elena and said.

"Yuria. Pick up Floy. Sharnel. Can you walk? Priscilla. Take care of Kyle."

I prepared to leave. Yuria took off all of Floy's armor and carried him on her back. Priscilla grabbed Kyle by the scruff of his neck with one hand.

'Kyle. This bastard is a tough one.'

Maybe it was the power of the protagonist buff, but he survived until the end. He was quite impressive.

"Let's go."

We went out of the space.

"I do!"

"I... I do."
