I was about to pass by the bisected corpse of the death knight that wasn't moving, then stopped.

Magic stone.

The average market price for an A-rank monster's magic stone is 3 million won.

Normally, I'd ignore it and walk past—extracting a magic stone from a corpse is more of a hassle than a few million won is worth. I've already amassed enough money to spend for the rest of my life and then some. If I need more later, I can just earn it then.

'But things are different now. I need a lot more money to build the Harem Tower.'

I extract the death knight's magic stone. The 10cm stone was split clean in half.

'Damn. At this rate I won't even get half the original price. From now on I should think about the magic stones when I finish them off.'

Old memories surface.

Back when I was a fucking nobody, I had no money so I went around collecting every monster's magic stone… didn't I?

'Come to think of it, even back then I was too lazy and only took the ones in good condition.'

After I awakened my pleasure life, money became largely meaningless. Selling gold brought from other worlds was easier than hunting monsters.

'This time too, I could just sell Water of Youth…. No. If word gets out that something like that exists, it'll only get more troublesome.'

I wanted my life in reality to be as un-troublesome as possible.

I decided to work diligently. The fact that I, of all people, was working diligently gave me a vague sense of defeat.

Clank.

Death knights appeared. Two of them. The moment they saw me, they radiated blue eye-light and charged.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Is Sword (心卽劍).

In a single strike, both death knights' heads flew off simultaneously. Even though death knights are undead, they can't move without their heads. They're not dullahans. A dullahan requires destroying the head itself.

I split open the falling bodies and extract the magic stones. Pulling them from corpses doesn't feel great.

'Fitting for undead—the stench is awful.'

I left the death knights' armor and swords behind. They were laden with curses, making them difficult for humans to use. Remove the curse and their performance drops to less than a quarter. That curse is the weapon's power.

I practically sprinted through the castle.

'Running around like this damages the dignity of an S-rank hunter, but… Martial God's Path only lasts 10 minutes, so there's no helping it.'

I tried to use Flying Thunder God, but it was harder to control than I'd expected. This was actually worse than not using Martial God's Path at all.

'Martial God's Path lowers everything except martial talent. That's probably the cause. Flying Thunder God is a body-lightening technique, but… what matters is the ability to handle lightning.'

Flying Thunder God could be said to be closer to sorcery than martial arts.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

Electromagnetic Wave was the same. While using Martial God's Path, its range shrank. Still, I could grasp the entire structure of the castle.

'When using Martial God's Path, the effectiveness and efficiency of techniques that purely handle ki, or handle lightning, drop.'

There were exceptions.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Gale Thunder (疾風迅雷).

Lightning originating from the spine spreads through the entire body, stimulating it. Bzzt. Fine currents flashed with light, wrapping around my body.

'Using lightning ki to directly reinforce the body, like Gale Thunder, actually has better effectiveness and efficiency.'

Perhaps because Gale Thunder begins from the inside of the body rather than the outside. Or maybe it's because it's body reinforcement.

Another death knight appeared.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Is Sword (心卽劍).

This time too, I used Mind Is Sword to sever its head. The reason I only use Mind Is Sword against death knights is simple.

'I need to remember the feel of using Mind Is Sword, even a little. That way I can use it even without Martial God's Path.'

Talent gained through a skill disappears the moment the skill's effect ends. But the experience gained through that talent doesn't disappear. That's already been proven through Genius's Time, so there's no room for doubt.

Though since I lack talent, I can't say that experience remains 100%.

'Even if only 10% of that experience remains, it's a gain.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder (卍雷).

I fired Myriad Thunder at a death knight some distance away. The lightning failed to kill the death knight.

The power was weak, and it took longer to activate than expected. Myriad Thunder, being close to pure ki manipulation, had been nerfed by Martial God's Path's influence.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Is Sword (心卽劍).

I finish the death knight with Mind Is Sword.

I chew over the sensation left in my body as much as possible. Since I can't explain the principles of Mind Is Sword in my head, sensation is the best I have.

Eight minutes into using Martial God's Path. I succeeded in killing every death knight in the dungeon except the boss.

I'd already grasped the death knights' positions. I stomped a true-step into the floor.

Heaven Demon Divine Art (天魔神功) — Heaven Demon Lord's Step (天魔君臨步).

A shockwave spreads and the ground collapses. As I plummeted underground, I thought.

'Even Heaven Demon Lord's Step, which I avoid using in reality when possible, is affected by Martial God's Path.'

I landed on the floor and looked straight ahead.

A death knight one and a half times the size of a normal one sat on a throne. A half-shattered, faded crown rested atop its helmet.

Black armor covered its body without a single gap, and its right hand gripped the hilt of a sword planted beside the throne.

Blue eye-light blazed from within its helmet.

The Death Knight King's massive body slowly rose. As it lifted the sword embedded in the floor, pitch-black sword aura layered over the blade.

I tightened my grip on Hwaryeonbi Blade. As my will moved, mana moved too. The power otherwise called ki layered over the red blade. Blue sword aura (劍罡) glowed faintly like a star.

The Death Knight King stared at me for a moment, then took a stance.

A stance focused more on defense than offense. It had instantly grasped that I was stronger.

'Its instincts are better than most A-rank hunters.'

Perhaps thanks to Martial God's Path. How the Death Knight King would swing its sword was roughly drawn in my head. This was the same as usual. But clearer than usual. The experience I'd accumulated touched Martial God's Path's talent, creating synergy.

I could see the Death Knight King's reach. Minimum reach, maximum reach. If I stepped inside that reach, it would definitely swing.

I walked forward. The moment my foot entered its reach, the Death Knight King swung its sword.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Is Sword (心卽劍).

Later than the Death Knight King. But my blade fell on its head faster.

KWAANG!

The helmet shattered. My sword, wreathed in sword aura, was blocked by its helmet. Pitch-black aura was leaking from inside the cracked helmet.

Mind Is Sword is so fast that even I, its user, can't properly perceive it, but that alone didn't make its power absurdly strong.

"The armor itself is at the level of body-protecting aura. Two more hits and it'll be smashed."

The Death Knight King's sword swept diagonally. I parried with Hwaryeonbi Blade.

'…Hm? I parried out of habit, but… it feels a bit off. I used too much strength. Seems like I only need to move a little force.'

How?

Before my head could derive the answer, my body was already performing it. I deflect the Death Knight King's sword flying at me without stopping.

Minimal movement.

The direction of force drawing a circle.

The overall movement brings to mind the Taiji.

'I'm using the mysteries of Taiji? I've never even learned it?'

I've never learned it, but I know it from facing Taiji Heavenly Lord (太極天君) Gong Baeksung. I spent two years sparring in the Bright Ascension Island world, after all. Didn't I create a technique called Byeokpajin (霹波鎭)—a hard-overcomes-soft technique—to defeat Gong Baeksung?

But what I'm doing now is soft-overcomes-hard (柔能制剛). I'm perfectly deflecting its attacks with minimal force.

“…….”

I feel like I almost understand something. But I can't quite grasp it. My talent for martial arts is certain, but my head can't keep up with that talent.

KANG!

Suddenly the Death Knight King pushed my sword away and retreated. Pushing and striking are different, so my reaction was a bit late. Though it was more because I was lost in thought.

Pitch-black sword aura surged from the Death Knight King's blade. As it swung, a slash over 2 meters flew toward me.

I could dodge using Flash Step. But I didn't.

Holding my sword forward, I received the incoming slash using the mysteries of Taiji. The black slash returned toward the Death Knight King. Its force may have diminished slightly, but its sharpness was unchanged.

KWAAAAAANG!

Cracks spread across the Death Knight King's entire armor. As it tried to move and take stance again, one knee dropped to the floor. Even so, its hostility didn't fade. It emotionlessly checked its shattered knee, then wrapped it in black energy and forced itself upright.

'Not much time left.'

[Activating Genius's Time for 10 seconds.]

Would Martial God's Path and Genius's Time stack? If they stacked, would the talents add together? Would I get stronger? Unfortunately, I didn't feel my martial talent suddenly amplifying. What I did notice was my senses becoming sharper. Dozens of ways to kill the Death Knight King surfaced at once.

'As I am now, I can't use Thunder Light Calamity Wheel… far from it.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Ultimate Technique (極技) — Heaven's Light (天光).

The moment I stepped forward, I became light and appeared behind the Death Knight King, having passed through it.

The Death Knight King froze in the posture of rising. Immediately after, its head slid from its neck and fell to the floor.

'Normally, once Heaven's Light starts it's hard to stop… but now I stopped exactly where I wanted. I finished distributing the force precisely before using Heaven's Light. So the strain on my body is far less too.'

I felt that the principles of Mind Is Sword and Heaven's Light had similar corners.

Soon Genius's Time and Martial God's Path's effects ended.

The talent vanished.

The body sensations that had felt so vivid disappeared. Rather than uncomfortable, it was actually comfortable.

'Of course—it disappeared in an uncomfortable way.'

I looked down at the blade still layered with sword aura.

Recalling the sensation of succeeding at Mind Is Sword, I used it toward empty air.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Is Sword (心卽劍).

The blade cut through empty air faster than my perception. Traces of sword aura drew a trajectory in the air, then vanished.

"Agh! My arm!"

My arm hurt like hell, but I'd succeeded at Mind Is Sword.
