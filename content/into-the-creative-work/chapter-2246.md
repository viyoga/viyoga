A blue lightning bolt struck Rezo's head from the pitch-black night sky.

Rezo was fine. The lightning slid off the top of his head, seeped into the ground, and disappeared.

It wasn't a meaningless attack. Because I saw what that bastard's power was.

,"He keeps ghost-type monsters in his body and uses them. A curse caster who also learned necromancy."

"It's not necromancy, but a type of voodoo."

"Wasn't voodoo about handling zombies?"

"Zombies or spirits... They're all dead, so there's no difference."

Rezo took something out of his pocket. It was a voodoo mask carved from wood. Spirits appear around the bastard wearing the mask.

Ghosts.

Monsters that Hunters are reluctant to deal with.

Hunters can easily handle C-class or lower, but even B-class or higher become quite tricky, so there are even exorcist Hunters who specialize in dealing with ghosts.

,'But the ghosts around Rezo... seem to be A-class or higher.'

Rezo points at me with his finger. The spirits rushed in all at once.

,'I have just the right thing to deal with those bastards.'

I put on white leather gloves with wings and a flail drawn on the back of my hand.

It's the Inquisitor's Gloves that I obtained as a reward for attacking the Rainbow Fruit Dungeon in the past. I finished reinforcing it with the Light Ascension Path, but I rarely had a chance to use it. Its effect is to grant Selbrettina's divine power to an item for 10 minutes once every 24 hours. In other words, Holy Enchant.

Instead of reverse-summoning and re-summoning Hwaryeon Bido, I summoned Stormbreaker and transformed it into the form of a sword.

,'Hwaryeon Bido has the ability to strengthen attributes, but it resists divine power.'

It's absurd that it resists divine power even though it was strengthened by a god, but it couldn't be helped.

"Divine power, the nemesis of ghosts!"

I swung the sword, shining brightly with divine power, at the spirits. The effect was amazing. Just touching the blade distorted their form and they began to slowly disappear.

Perhaps because the spirits were being slaughtered too easily. Rezo called the spirits back into himself.

"Ghosts, huh? Do you consider these poor souls as mere monsters?"

"You're the one using those poor souls. What other bullshit are you going to say?"

"I made a contract with them through a legitimate ritual. You Koreans also perform similar rituals every year, right?"

"Similar ritual? What are you talking about?"

"Ancestral rites. I'm talking about the ritual where you worship your ancestral spirits."

"My family doesn't do that these days."

I jumped at the bastard with my sword. I fell right to the floor. Suddenly, my body became heavy. It wasn't the feeling of being pulled down by gravity. My body itself became heavy. This was a curse.

"Is that so? That's a shame. I went to the trouble of summoning your ancestor, but his descendant is an ungrateful bastard."

"Thisss bastarddd! Seong Yujin-ssi!!"

A shout rang out from above my head. I was startled and looked up. There was a stern-looking old man there.

I was shocked. It was my grandfather, who had passed away from high blood pressure when I was in middle school.

"G, grandfather?!"

"Rezo-nim is a precious person who will soothe our spirits! How dare you not show respect! I never taught you that way!"

I jumped up from my seat.

"You fucking old bastard! If you were going to die, you should have passed on some of your wealth! You died without any wealth and now you show up and act like this!"

"Y, you bastard! What kind of language is that to use to your grandfather! I am your grandfather! Is that how your mother taught you?!"

"You son of a bitch. How many ancestral rites has our family performed...! At this point, you should have appeared in my dream and subtly told me the lottery numbers! I'm glad I met you. If you want to eat ancestral rite food in the future, tell me the lottery numbers!"

"You don't know your place!!"

"Your place is my ass!"

I stabbed the sword, filled with divine power, into my grandfather's chest.

"Kkeuaaaaaaak!"

My grandfather's spirit disappeared as it was. I spat on the floor and glared at Rezo. Rezo had a somewhat dumbfounded expression on his face.

"Your tricks don't work. I don't know how you knew my grandfather's appearance and tried to deceive me... but there's no way my grandfather, who died a long time ago, would suddenly appear."

"...No, he is your grandfather."

"I told you your tricks don't work. There's no way you can summon the soul of someone who died a long time ago."

"I've been hiding in this land for 10 years. Did you think I was just living here without doing anything? This body was the son of a shaman, and I happened to be interested in the indigenous beliefs of this land. For the first few years, I also learned shamanism. Even someone who died a long time ago can temporarily summon their soul if the conditions are right. Just like your grandfather, who was just stabbed by your sword and disappeared."

"That was really my grandfather?!"

"Yes. You are a parricide who killed his own grandfather."

"You're trying to shake my mentality with nonsense. If that was really my grandfather... he would have told me the lottery numbers. At the very least, he would have told me about a stock that would skyrocket."

"Souls don't gain special powers. They become even more powerless because they don't have a body."

"Your tricks don't work. That old man didn't tell me the lottery numbers! No more ancestral rite food for you!"

"You crazy bastard. I feel sorry for him for having a grandson like you. Do you see your sword in front of me here?"

"Don't touch Hwaryeon Bido. It's more important than my grandfather."

"......The more attached you are to something, the easier it is to become a medium for curses. Especially since this sword has special powers. I can see your attachment to this sword. This sword will kill you."

Rezo reached out and grabbed the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido.

Pajijijik!

Red sparks flew and bounced Rezo's hand away. Rezo's expression hardened.

"Could it have some kind of will? It's more than I imagined."

"It's too precious for the likes of you to touch. Come, Hwaryeon Bido."

Reverse summon and then summon again. I held Hwaryeon Bido and Stormbreaker in both hands.

Pajijijijik.

Blue lightning sparked from Stormbreaker, and red lightning sparked from Hwaryeon Bido.

I resonated the two lightnings. The sound of thunder rang out and amplified the power.

Kwareureureung.

Lightning Style (Raitenryu) Thunderclap (Raimei).

"People think of curses as purely negative. But just as poison can be medicine, curses can be used in different ways."

Rezo threw off his top. Complex tattoos were drawn on his body. The tattoos wriggled as if they were alive. I knew those tattoos were curses.

"Lee Yeon-suk fights with dozens of barriers on her body. She uses the barriers as armor and weapons, so it's called barrier armament. There's no reason why I can't do it. It's not a barrier, but a curse... I'll become a curse armament."

Rezo, who has dozens of curses on his body, clenches his fist and approaches. The pressure is unusual.

,'A curse caster who fights in close combat? That's fucked up. Really.'

The only good news is that Rezo's footsteps and the way he clenches his fist are very clumsy. It was clear that he had almost no experience in close combat.

[Using Transience (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

I pretend to swing my sword and sword and detonate the lightning amplified by resonance. The lightning explodes and my vision is swallowed up for a moment. Relying on my senses, I swung Hwaryeon Bido and Stormbreaker in different directions.

Kaaaaaaang!

"What the hell is that?"

"He is a general-nim."

"Fuck. This is getting out of hand!"

The general entered Rezo's body as if he was seeping into it. Rezo's momentum changed drastically. He, who knew nothing about martial arts, turned into a warrior who had experienced all kinds of hardships. Moreover, into a warrior with skills beyond a master. Before I knew it, he held a sword and glaive in both hands and countered my attack.

Kaaaaaaang!

A shockwave occurred as the weapons crossed. My strength couldn't withstand it and I retreated. The opponent's physical abilities were slightly more overwhelming.

,'My physical abilities are inferior? Against a curse caster?'

It was shocking. So shocking that I wanted to give up everything.

But I pulled myself together after seeing blood flowing from the bastard's mouth and nose.

"There's no way a curse caster can fight like that without a price. He's eating away at his own life."

"......That's what curses are. A powerful and dangerous force at the same time. But it's okay. The result has been achieved. Can't you hear the screams of the dead?"

Kkiaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!

Hwareureureuk.
