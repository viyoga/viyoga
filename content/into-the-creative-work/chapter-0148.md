'She has a good figure, so she looks good in anything she wears.'

I was curious about what Han Ha-rin's house would be like, but it was neatly organized, and there was nothing particularly noticeable.

Surprisingly, she treated me properly.

It's just a cup of orange juice, but I'm satisfied just that she gave me something.

Pajijik.

"Look for specialized books related to lightning. You might find unexpected uses."

"......How did you awaken your mana?"

"Ah. Mana. This is actually a secret I haven't told anyone... I'll only tell seonbae."

"......"

Han Ha-rin's eyes intensified. I could see her determination to concentrate and not miss a single word I said.

"I received a special mana massage. You know about mana massages, right?"

"I know. It's a massage using mana."

And mana massage is still a massage that people look for.

Most people know it like Han Ha-rin. But in fact, it is very effective for physical health, including fatigue recovery. Because mana itself is beneficial energy. However, it is not very effective for Hunters.

"The one I received was a special mana massage. I received it steadily for about a month? ...About that long. Then one day, I suddenly awakened my mana along with my second awakening."

Of course, she won't believe it. She must have investigated mana awakening deeply, so she wouldn't have heard of a special mana massage.

"You don't have to believe me. But that's 100% true."

"......"

Han Ha-rin bit her lower lip.

I was calm. Because I know that I'm not the one in a hurry.

Han Ha-rin has been suffering because of mana awakening, so she will want to believe even a ridiculous story.

I tried to act as gloomy as possible. To be honest, I'm not confident in my acting, but I can't speak cheerfully when an acquaintance has died. It's all a lie, though.
