“Damn it…”

The Emperor groaned, looking at the sky.

The weather was good.

It felt like the first time seeing a clear blue sky since Mount Hua Island appeared. But damn it, that Mount Hua Island was currently floating above the Imperial family.

It blocked the warm sunlight, casting its colossal shadow and darkening the Imperial family.

‘Mount Hua Island has been conquered. The System displayed a notification window to everyone. The volcanic ash, which was the biggest problem, had cleanly disappeared. But why is that thing here?’

The Emperor racked his brain.

One of the hypotheses that emerged was this:

‘…Can the one who conquered Mount Hua Island move Mount Hua Island?’

No, what if they could not only move it but also control Mount Hua Island itself? What if it could spew ash again from Mount Hua Island, or if Mount Hua Island, which had flown over the Imperial Palace, could fall to the ground?

Cold sweat trickled down the Emperor’s cheeks.

He felt an eerie sensation, as if someone was holding a Sword to his throat.

The Emperor entered his Room and picked up the Tablet of Prophecy. And he used the Tablet of Prophecy, which could only be used once a month, now.

(Fate will be shattered.)

‘…There was no mention related to Mount Hua Island. Yet, this is also a significant prophecy.’

Fate is shattered.

What does Fate refer to? Does it not refer to what is predetermined?

‘Prophecy itself can be called Fate. Does this mean that a prophecy made by the Tablet of Prophecy is wrong? If so, my end might have changed.’

Should he be happy that his end had changed? Or should he be sad that the prophecy had suddenly become useless?

‘…Perhaps I might have guessed wrong.’

The possibility that Fate does not refer to prophecy cannot be ignored.

‘The Tablet of Prophecy only makes prophecies related to me. What’s certain is that it’s an event related to my Fate.’

He could not know what that Fate was.

As he was pondering, he felt a presence outside the door. The Emperor hid the Tablet of Prophecy in his Clothes.

“Your Majesty.”

“What is it?”

“Tetrix Legion Master Rain Brick has come to see you.”

“…I will meet him.”

The Tetrix Legion was a Legion secretly supported by the Emperor.

He met Rain Brick in the reception Room.

Rain Brick’s appearance was, to say the least, not good. He had Injuries everywhere, and his Clothes were partially torn.

The Emperor didn’t mind. What was important now was not mere formalities.

“Please forgive me for appearing before Your Majesty in such an unkempt state.”

“You speak words you don’t mean. I know you have no loyalty towards me. Let’s get straight to the point. What was the result of the Mount Hua Island conquest?”

“The Mount Hua Island conquest itself was successful.”

“Everyone knows that. You know that’s not what I’m asking, don’t you?”

“…The plan failed. The conqueror is not us. Nor are they Legions allied with us.”

“Is it the A-Plan Legion?”

He had been most wary of the A-Plan Legion.

The Mount Hua Island conquest was possible because of Kang Myungjin, the A-Plan Legion Master. He had an unusual amount of information. The Emperor guessed it was because he possessed abilities like seeing the future. Thus, he was most wary.

“No. The A-Plan Legion did not perform as expected in this Mount Hua Island conquest. However, the information they possessed was excellent, and the skills of their executives, including the A-Plan Legion Master, were extremely outstanding.”

“If not the A-Plan Legion… who seized victory?”

“It was the Heavenly Demon.”

“…Did you not guard against the Demonic Cult?”

“We did guard against them. We assigned two Legion forces to the Demonic Cult. However, the Demonic Cult’s power was beyond our expectations. Both Legions were annihilated. While we were checking the A-Plan Legion, the Heavenly Demon killed the Red Dragon and conquered Mount Hua Island. It was also partly due to our complacency, as the Demonic Cult had been passively involved in the Mount Hua Island conquest until then. We lost all Domination over the Mount Hua Island area to the Heavenly Demon and were expelled from Mount Hua Island.”

“…Lost Domination over the area?”

“Yes. From the beginning, Mount Hua Island was set up for the Last victor to gain everything.”

“Did the A-Plan Legion Master hide this information?”

“The A-Plan Legion gained no advantage from this Mount Hua Island conquest. He probably didn’t know this information either.”

“Is there a possibility that the Demonic Cult and the A-Plan Legion formed an alliance?”

“The A-Plan Legion gains nothing. On the contrary, they would become enemies of the Empire, so it would only be a loss.”

“…That’s right. I, too, spoke carelessly due to the frustrating situation. If your words are true… then the Heavenly Demon controls that Mount Hua Island.”

A sigh involuntarily escaped the Emperor’s lips.

The Heavenly Demon was a person the Emperor could not control.

Eliminating the Heavenly Demon was not easy either. Rumors subtly circulated that the Heavenly Demon’s military might was comparable to the Empire Ogong. What if he dispatched the Empire Ogong here… and lost them? The Empire could be shaken.

‘Even for the Atlantis conquest, the power of the Empire Ogong must be preserved as much as possible.’

The fact that few among the Empire Ogong obediently followed the Emperor’s words also played a part.

The Duke of a Thousand Years (千年公) was someone even the Emperor couldn’t afford to handle carelessly.

The Duke of Iron was known as a guardian of the Imperial family and held great loyalty within the Empire Ogong. However, because the Emperor was aware of the Duke of Iron’s background, he found it hard to fully trust him. As a result, he was hesitant to send him outside the Imperial household.

His relationship with the Duke of Foolish Sword had not been good lately. If he received an order, he would follow it, but there was a chance he was only pretending to comply.

The Duke of Water Clothes (水衣公) has had a lot of bad rumors circulating recently. He will undoubtedly use this as an excuse to make certain demands.

The Duke of Illusion (幻想公) was out of the question. Their relationship was already strained. Lately, even if he gave orders, they would refuse with various excuses, or if they complied, the results were far from satisfactory.

‘The strained relationship with the Duke of Illusion is much bigger than I thought. The Duke of Illusion would be perfect for a task like this.’

There had been similar incidents before. Whenever a person who could pose a danger to the Empire or the Imperial family appeared, he would order the Duke of Illusion.

And the Duke of Illusion would handle the matter perfectly, no matter who the opponent was.

‘The Tablet of Prophecy had foretold the Duke of Illusion’s death… To think the prophecy would be wrong.’

He still didn’t know why the prophecy was wrong.

‘If I had known this… Tsk.’

Regret always comes too late.

The Emperor cleared the distracting thoughts from his Mind and asked Rain Brick.

“Can you eliminate the Heavenly Demon with your power?”

“It’s impossible. Even if the Empire Ogong stepped forward instead of us, it would be impossible.”

“What do you mean by that?”

“We were expelled from Mount Hua Island without question or argument. If the Heavenly Demon establishes a foothold on Mount Hua Island…”

“Are you saying it’s difficult to even enter Mount Hua Island? Even if one successfully infiltrates Mount Hua Island, they would be expelled.”

“Yes. The System’s power is absolute. Killing the Heavenly Demon on Mount Hua Island is impossible. Furthermore, if we provoke the Heavenly Demon too much…”

“We cannot be sure how that bastard will react. The Heavenly Demon. That bastard is the Crazy Bastard I’ve seen in my life. He would surely ram Mount Hua Island into the Imperial Palace. How did that Crazy Bastard come to possess such power…? Heaven is truly indifferent.”

The Emperor felt his Head throb.

Kung!

The Imperial Palace shook.

“Up…!”

The Emperor sprang to his feet, then felt his Body, his very foundation, tremble.

Old memories, suppressed Sins, forcibly crawled up. The moment the Empress’s Last face, as she was being executed, tried to surface from the time he had ordered her execution for the sake of the Imperial family.

The crown the Emperor was wearing shone brightly.

"The Demon (魔) laughs."

"The Crown of Subjugation (逐從) purifies mental corruption."

The Emperor, regaining his senses, touched the crown on his Head.

A Treasure of the Imperial family that only the Emperor could wear.

Crown of Subjugation (Chikugō).

Protects and purifies the Mind.

Aids the dignity of the king.

Can only be worn by a rightful emperor.

Rank: SSS」

It was a Treasure that could boast of being the best among mental defense types. It could even block the Duke of Illusion’s illusions.

The Emperor looked at Rain Brick. He spat out blood once, then straightened his back.

“Are you alright?”

“…I am alright. My response was a bit late due to the unexpected attack. It’s an excellent mental attack. It seems an uninvited guest has come to the Imperial Palace.”

Kwa-aaaaa-aaaaa-aaang!

A loud explosion was heard from the Imperial Palace entrance.

Someone was fighting. It was probably the Duke of Iron, Guardian of the Imperial family, fighting the uninvited guest.

“I can Guess who the uninvited guest is. I should go myself before things escalate further.”

“…I will follow you.”

“You wait here. You and I have no public Relationship. It’s better not to reveal yourself as much as possible.”

“Understood.”

The Emperor left the reception Room alone. The Knights guarding the door at the entrance of the reception Room were trembling, slumped on the ground.

“Your Majesty…!”

“We will Guard Your Majesty!”

The Emperor swallowed his disappointment. To be Knights of the Imperial family, yet worse than Outsiders. Was it because these Knights were flowers grown in a Garden?

“Enough. You may rest for a moment. This is the Imperial Palace. No one will harm me.”

The Emperor walked down the corridor. Most of the Knights were incapacitated. In the case of the servants working in the royal palace, they couldn’t endure and fainted. Those who hadn’t fainted were cowering on the floor, Pissing themselves and weeping.

“No. No. I didn’t do it…”

“Save me, please….”

“Mother… Father….”

The Emperor walked past them and saw the Duke of Iron and the Heavenly Demon confronting each other at the Imperial Palace entrance. It seemed they had already had a skirmish, as the surroundings were shattered everywhere.

Knights were sprawled nearby, but there were only Injured, no fatalities.

“Heavenly Demon. What the hell is this? Do you take the Imperial family, the Empire, for a joke?”

“The Master has finally appeared. The hospitality is absolute Bullshit.”

“If you’re a guest, you should act like one…”

“Your Majesty. If you command it, I will kill that bastard here.”

The Duke of Iron, a 2.2-meter giant with short crimson Hair, growled. Every time he exhaled, the muscles all over his Body twitched.

The Emperor’s Mind wanted to order the Heavenly Demon killed immediately. How dare he cause trouble in the Imperial Palace.

However, the colossal Mount Hua Island floating above his Head suppressed the Emperor’s Mind. Mount Hua Island’s size was greater than the capital. If that fell, the capital itself would be annihilated.

“Enough. I will grant this barbarian one act of mercy. Heavenly Demon, for what purpose have you come to the Imperial Palace?”

“I came to receive desert aid funds.”

“……What?”

“Seeing the Empire citizens living well and prosperously made my guts twist. Since you are so prosperous, isn’t it right to bestow upon us, the people of the barren desert? So, hand over the desert aid funds. Ah, and I also need to see the face of Gabriel, who caused such Bullshit in our desert. Bring that pigeon bitch here.”

This Crazy Bastard, how dare he…!

In the Emperor’s eyes, trembling with rage, he saw Mount Hua Island floating in the sky.

Suddenly, his anger was controlled.

“I will remember this incident.”

“Aren’t you old enough to get dementia? Just in case, have a historian record it. Ah. Does the Imperial family not have historians?”
