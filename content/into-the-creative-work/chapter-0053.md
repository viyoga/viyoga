I sipped my coffee at a cafe with a good view of the Saebyeok Group headquarters. Through the window, I could see Moon Ji-hyeok running towards the headquarters and Yang Jeong-min belatedly running after him.

'Moon Ji-hyeok can't stop the bomb terror. He couldn't stop it in the original story, and the situation is even worse now than in the original.'

In the original drama, Ji An-seok is caught by Moon Ji-hyeok. He subdues him completely with the vampire's superior physical abilities before he can detonate himself.

The problem is the bomb that Ji An-seok had installed in advance at the Saebyeok Group headquarters. He can't stop that.

'And I unknowingly helped Ji An-seok a bit.'

I deliberately gave Moon Ji-hyeok, who was investigating Ji An-seok, misleading information. Moon Ji-hyeok believed me more than I thought he would.

The original Ji An-seok wanders around to avoid Moon Ji-hyeok's pursuit and arrives at the headquarters around evening when the sun begins to set.

'Since there was no pursuit from Moon Ji-hyeok, Ji An-seok arrived quickly. There are still two hours left until sunset.'

Vampires have lower physical abilities than ordinary people during the day when the sun is up.

He can't subdue Ji An-seok with the power of a vampire like in the original drama. On the contrary, there is a high possibility that he will be subdued by Ji An-seok.

'Hmm. This coffee sucks.'

Soon after, the police arrived and began forcibly evacuating citizens. The police special forces will arrive soon too.

'Should I go to Yeong Hye-jeong today?'

"Stop it, Ji An-seok!"

Ji An-seok had just finished checking the bomb he had installed in the bathroom in advance, when a breathless man who had appeared out of nowhere blocked his path.

Ji An-seok frowned. He was clearly proceeding with the plan without anyone knowing, but from some point on, police cars were dispatched to the Saebyeok Group headquarters and evacuating citizens. He couldn't figure out where he had made a mistake. Has the Korean police always been this competent?

"Are you a cop?"

"Yes. I'm a detective. Ji An-seok. What you're doing is wrong. It's not too late. Stop it."

"Well... I think it's way too late."

Ji An-seok took out a pistol from his pocket and aimed it at the man. Moon Ji-hyeok flinched and took a step back.

"You're a detective and you didn't even bring a gun. I'm not interested in you, so get out of the way."

"Are you going to involve countless people in your revenge!"

"It can't be helped. And if I kill the Saebyeok Group Chairman's family, I won't detonate the installed bombs. Get out of the way."

"......Installed bombs?"

"You didn't even know that far? I already infiltrated as a cleaner and installed bombs all over the building. As I said, if I kill the Chairman's family properly, I won't detonate the bombs. So get out of the way."

"Keuk...!"

Moon Ji-hyeok looked into Ji An-seok's eyes and groaned. Those were the eyes of someone who had already resigned himself to death. It was clear that no matter what he said, it wouldn't work.

'The bastard's goal is ultimately the Saebyeok Group's Chairman's family. If I get out of the way here... Ji An-seok won't detonate the bombs. Sacrificing the few for the sake of the many... Goddamn it. A detective like me has to pretend not to see a terrorist?'

The thing Moon Ji-hyeok hated the most. Sacrificing the few for the sake of the many. Moon Ji-hyeok wanted to save everything, big and small.

"......"

Ji An-seok walked forward while aiming his gun at Moon Ji-hyeok. He was wasting time confronting a single detective. And Ji An-seok had absolute confidence. He had a gun, and Moon Ji-hyeok was empty-handed.

When Ji An-seok got close, Moon Ji-hyeok rushed towards Ji An-seok. He aimed for the fact that Ji An-seok's left upper body was not moving well due to his injury. But now it's daytime. His physical abilities are currently below those of an ordinary person.

Tang!

A bullet pierced his thigh.

"......!"

Moon Ji-hyeok, who bit his lip tightly, wrapped his arm around Ji An-seok's neck. He was planning to knock him down to the ground and subdue him.

Ji An-seok responded nimbly. Although he had retired due to injury, he was a soldier from a special forces unit. Rather, he grabbed Moon Ji-hyeok by the collar and threw him against the wall.

"You're weak for a detective."

Ji An-seok glanced at Moon Ji-hyeok. Moon Ji-hyeok, who had hit the back of his head properly against the wall, was unconscious. Blood was flowing from the gunshot wound on his thigh.

Ji An-seok looked at him intently and headed to the Chairman's office, which was his original destination. He didn't care whether Moon Ji-hyeok died or not. His goal was to annihilate the Saebyeok Group's Chairman's family.

He entered the Chairman's office and frowned when he saw that no one was there. Originally, the Chairman's family should have been looking down at the event from this Chairman's office. But there was no one in the Chairman's office. The Chairman had already noticed him and ran away.

There was an emergency staircase at the entrance to the Chairman's office.

"Chairman Park Yun-su... you quick-witted bastard...!"

Ji An-seok took out a detonator from his pocket. If he pressed the button, the bombs installed throughout the building would explode all at once. Just as he was about to press the button without hesitation, he discovered traces of the Chairman heading to the upper floor.

"Helicopter! He's planning to take a helicopter from the rooftop!"

It will still take some time for the helicopter to arrive.

Ji An-seok smiled bitterly and began running towards the rooftop. He could detonate the bombs right away, but he wanted to see with his own eyes the death of his enemy, Chairman Park Yun-su.

"Wait for me! Chairman Park Yun-su! Let's go to hell together!"

It was around the time the elevator was almost reaching the first floor.

Kwaaaang! Kwaang!

Explosions rang out repeatedly. The elevator stopped and started going down to the basement.

"Kyaaaaaa!"

Kung!

The fluorescent light in the elevator went out and darkness fell.

'...Did he finally detonate the bomb.'

Moon Ji-hyeok bit his lip. His head became complicated. He clearly knew in advance. But he couldn't stop it. If he had acted a little faster, if it had been night instead of day....

'No. I shouldn't rely too much on the vampire's power.'

But that doesn't mean he can't use the vampire's power at all. It's not in his nature to let what he has rot. The only thing he has to be careful about is throwing it away without hesitation when he can throw it away.

"Ugh.... Ji-hyeok-ssi...! Are you okay? Are you hurt anywhere, like your head? It looks like the elevator stopped... Ah! Why does this have to happen at a time like this! Ji-hyeok-ssi needs to go to the hospital quickly!!"

Moon Ji-hyeok could see Yang Jeong-min grabbing the wall of the elevator and stamping her feet. Vampires could see through the darkness even without a single ray of light.

"I'm okay. Jeong-min-ssi."

Moon Ji-hyeok said calmly. It wasn't a lie or a bluff.

No sunlight entered the elevator, and the fluorescent light was broken by the impact just now. Because there is no light, the vampire's power is awakening despite it being daytime.

The gunshot wound on his thigh? It hurts, but it's already slowly regenerating. When night comes, it will be completely regenerated before the sun rises again.

In a way, it was a good thing for Moon Ji-hyeok that the elevator fell and stopped in the basement. Thanks to that, he didn't have to be taken to the hospital right away. If he had been taken to the hospital, the doctor would have found out that he was a vampire.

"Jeong-min-ssi. There's something I've been hiding from Jeong-min-ssi."

"...Yes? What are you talking about all of a sudden? More than that, take out your smartphone and contact the outside world quickly...."

"Please listen. Jeong-min-ssi. I'm not human, I'm a vampire."
