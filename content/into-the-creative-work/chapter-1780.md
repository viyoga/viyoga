I left the house and headed to a nearby dungeon.

To test out what I'd gained this time.

,A training ground isn't bad, but using it in a real fight is the best.'

I went to the Association website and scanned the dungeons in Gyeonggi Province.

,Going to A-rank or B-rank alone is tough and annoying. Since the purpose is to test my skills, C-rank is appropriate. D-rank monsters are too weak for a proper test.'

The best is a C-rank closed dungeon.

However, all C-rank closed dungeons in Korea were currently being raided.

,Those guild bastards have all the good spots.'

C-rank closed dungeons were popular because they were suitable for practical training for Hunters who had awakened their mana.

,Waiting for a new C-rank closed dungeon to appear is too much. I'll have to go with an open one.'

Even though other Hunters are a concern, I just need to be careful since I won't be sticking with them.

I quickly scanned the C-rank open dungeons.

There's a decent C-rank open dungeon near Incheon. It's called the 'Spirit Orc Legion' dungeon. True to its name, Spirit Orcs, which are C-rank monsters, appear.

,Orcs with the power of spirits are top-tier among C-rank monsters. C-rank Hunters avoid them, and B-rank Hunters have B-rank dungeons, so there's no reason to catch Spirit Orcs.'

The earnings aren't bad, so B-rank Hunters who are short on cash often visit the dungeon.

Having made my decision, I immediately drove into the dungeon.

"Kueeeeek!"

I encountered a Spirit Orc. Its skin is yellow. It's a Light Spirit Orc. This Orc's characteristic is speed.

As soon as the yellow Orc saw me, it screamed and charged. It's so fast that a normal C-rank Hunter would miss its movements if they weren't focused.

,This speed isn't enough to escape my sight.'

It's just fast.

I can follow it with my eyes, and it's not too late to react slowly.

I just watched blankly, and when it arrived at close range, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido, gripped it in my hand, and swung. The Light Spirit Orc's body was cut in two and fell. I collected the magic stone from the corpse and moved to find another Spirit Orc.

,Is this about the level of Spirit Orcs? I should use Meteor Sword on the next one I encounter.'

I encountered an Ice Spirit Orc with blue skin.

"Kueek, Kueeeeek!"

The Spirit Orc created an ice club in its hand. Then, it slowly approached me, revealing its murderous intent.

,Meteor Sword.'

[Activating Meteor Sword.]

Mana and stamina drained, and a sword was summoned in the sky. My face wasn't good. Because more mana was consumed than I thought.

The Meteor Sword fell precisely on the Spirit Orc's head.

"Kueek?"

The falling speed was also slow. Slow enough for the Spirit Orc to react casually.

The Spirit Orc hit the Meteor Sword falling on its head with its club as if hitting a home run. The Meteor Sword flew far away and disappeared without a trace.

It was a power that made me sigh.

Lightning Stream Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

The Myriad Lightning shot from my palm pierced the Spirit Orc's body. The Spirit Orc fell to the ground and died. I collected the Spirit Orc's magic stone with a complicated expression.

,The power of Meteor Sword is much worse than I thought.'

Its power was weaker than when used as a magical artifact, and its mana efficiency was also poor. It couldn't even properly kill a C-rank monster. It was difficult to use in battle as it was.

,Maybe it's because the skill level is low. I need to raise the level.'
