Pajijijik!

The Chain Lightning that killed all the mercenaries flickered several times in the air as if searching for prey, then disappeared leaving behind the smell of ozone.

I scanned the corpses and equipment of the mercenaries.

Mechanical equipment that looked expensive to anyone.

Emblems representing the Red Falcon and the Raven Knights were drawn in various places.

,Did opposing forces temporarily join hands? They didn't seem to get along very well inside the dungeon for that to be the case.'

They temporarily joined hands for their respective purposes. That purpose must be the reward from the dungeon.

,Spell Predator is worth it though.'

It erases or steals magic below level 7.

There's a condition that it's below level 7, but in reality, it can erase over 90% of the magic in this world. The value of Spell Predator is worth more than billions.

I looked around and staggered. Muscle pain swept through my whole body and I was out of breath. Because I was maintaining my Unique Ability, my whole body was under strain.

,Unique Ability release.'

The burden of the Unique Ability doesn't only affect the body. All 255 mana roads were overheated by the Unique Ability. It wasn't dangerous, but it seemed clear that problems would arise if I maintained the Unique Ability for a long time.

,Fortunately, there's no mental problem. It would be nice if it was just a problem with the mana roads... I felt a fatigue in my body as if I had sprinted 4-5 times.'

However, this level of fatigue will recover to some extent if I rest for a few minutes. Fortunately, the duration of the Unique Ability wasn't long.

I took out a potion from my inventory and drank it. My stamina recovers and my fatigue fades. Of course, potions aren't a panacea. For now, I've replenished my stamina with the potion's energy, but eventually, the price will come at night.

,I won't be able to fall asleep easily because of muscle pain.'

Well, I can endure muscle pain. If the pain is severe, I can take painkillers.

I put all the corpses and equipment into my inventory. The equipment can be sold for money. The corpses will be evidence to prove the justification of my actions.

,In this world, if you have power but don't wield it, you're treated like an idiot. Revenge is natural for my future reputation.'

Two targets to retaliate against.

Red Falcon and Raven Knights.

It's not easy. The one who dominates the battlefield is the same level 7 as me. Of course, not all level 7s have the same combat power.

,And I don't necessarily have to kill them. I just need to inflict fatal damage that makes them regret touching me.'

My victory condition isn't killing the enemy commander.

Mayhem.

Forcing unilateral damage on the enemy. And it's easy for a level 7 Archmage.

I smiled and headed to the Raven Knights fortress.

The Raven Knights fortress had its entrance closed. Armored forces were stationed at the fortress entrance, and soldiers were on guard on the fortress walls. An atmosphere of war was swirling throughout the fortress.

Their gazes and gun barrels are pointed at me. I didn't stop. Five magic circles appeared around me. A quintuple incantation squeezed out with 255 mana roads.

If the power of the magic manifested through incantation is 100%, the magic circle can show power of 120% or more. Of course, the disadvantage is that magic circles take longer than incantation. So, when a mage uses a magic circle, it's a declaration of intent to kill for sure.

,Stop there. The Raven Fortress is currently under martial law. We will attack if you come any closer!'

The one who seemed to be in charge among them shouted, sweating coldly. Of course, I didn't stop.

,You guys touched me first. This is just revenge. Accept it willingly.'

,...Just revenge? I don't know what nonsense you're talking about, but let's talk it out. I think there's some misunderstanding between us. No matter how great an Archmage you are, you won't be able to handle this fortress's barrier and power.'

,Barrier. Ah, right. That annoying thing.'

I took out a wand from my inventory. The antique-looking wand sparkled as it caught the light.

Spell Predator.

The effective range is about 100m, and to remove magic or steal control of magic, you must clearly recognize the magic with both eyes.

,It's hard to use even if I steal control of the barrier. The barrier doesn't have any special function other than protecting the fortress.'

Less than 100m left.

The person in charge of the Raven Knights bit his lip and pondered before shouting.

,Attack! Don't let the enemy get close!!'

Bullets, missiles, magic, and all kinds of attacks are concentrated on me.

I used my ability before the attacks reached me.

[Leap]

Teleportation of 10m. Avoiding the approaching attacks, I leap once again.

,Space movement...?!'

,Is he a space-type mage?! Damn it, why is such an Archmage suddenly appearing and attacking us?!'

,Shoot! Shoot! Even if he's a level 7 Archmage, we have the advantage as long as we have the barrier! The distance of space movement is short! Predict the distance of space movement and shoot!!'

Certainly, their response was quick and appropriate, as they had plenty of practical experience. But I had a moment.

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 11]

The Moment I used at level 6 was different from the Moment I used at level 7. If the Moment I used at level 6 felt like the bullets were moving very slowly, the Moment I used now felt like they had stopped altogether.

[Leap]

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 10]

[Leap]

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 9]

[Leap]

Repetition of Moment and Leap.

The distance to the barrier is within 100m. I achieved my goal.

I waved the wand towards the barrier. The wand sparkled, and the barrier that had been protecting the fortress disappeared without a trace.

,The barrier...!'

,Take cover behind the walls and tanks!!'

,No, taking cover is meaningless! Concentrate firepower on the mage and kill him! Fire all the missiles that can be launched from the fortress! Incinerate everything!!'

The person in charge shouted at the top of his lungs.

Too late. I was already prepared.

Five magic circles are activated.

[Blizzard]

[Air Blast]

[Fire Tornado]

[Thunder Bolt]

[Lightning Spear]

A festival of different magics.

The most powerful and efficient was area magic. The soldiers who were clustered together were swept away, and a path opened. Compressed lightning falling from the sky, Thunder Bolt, broke down the walls of the fortress, and Lightning Spear pierced through the center of the fortress.

The icicles falling from the sky were a disaster in themselves, and the Fire Tornado swept everything away.

It was then.

A huge slash occurred, cutting through the Fire Tornado. A man in black power armor walked out, striding through the scattering heat. A presence that dominated this battlefield in an instant.

He swung his black sword once more. The clouds that had been scattering snow, icicles, and cold air to the ground were struck by the slash and disappeared. Swordsmanship that grasps and cuts through the core of magic. He is a master of the sword.

,Let's stop here, Count Eugene Meyer.'

,Why should I? You guys touched me first.'

,...This must be about the dungeon that occurred this time. We apologize for this. However, didn't you also hide your identity, Count? We had no choice but to misunderstand.'

,That identity was an official identity of Neo-London. There was no reason to be attacked by the Raven Knights. You were blinded by greed and attacked.'

,...The Raven Knights supply Adamantium to Neo-Round Table Council and several companies.'

,Ah. That's great. I supply superconductors to the Neo-Round Table Council and several companies. The Adamantium you supply is ultimately just from this mine, and you're just miners. If the Round Table Council likes it, they can replace you at any time. On the other hand, the superconductor recipe only exists in my head.'

Me and the Raven Knights. If they had to choose one of us, the result was obvious.

,...Haa. I will bow my head and apologize for this incident. It's not just an apology in words. I will give 20% of the Raven Fortress's annual Adamantium production to LK Corporation for free. How is that?'

20% of the annual production. I don't know the exact number. But it's definitely not a small amount.

,I should stop here.'

I was a noble of Neo-London. I can't completely destroy the Raven Knights, who have Neo-London as their backing. If I did, my reputation and fame would be ruined. I can't let what I've built up so far collapse over such a thing.

,Is the contract ready?'

,It won't take long.'

We faced each other with the documents in the middle of the office.

,I am Orland, the leader of the Raven Knights. I'm late in introducing myself. My subordinates have been very rude.'

,Eugene Meyer. Wasn't it you who ultimately approved the subordinates' operation?'

,If I had known it was Count Meyer-nim, I wouldn't have approved it. The reason you sought out the battlefield while hiding your identity... was to become a level 7. Congratulations on reaching that level.'

,Thank you. But are you going to wear your helmet until the contract is signed?'

,I cannot take off this armor. This armor itself is both a curse and a power.'

,It seems you didn't reach level 7 in a normal way.'

,My talent is humble. I had to use unconventional methods.'

,That armor itself is your Unique Ability, isn't it?'

,...Where did you hear my information?'

,Everyone who needs to know knows.'

,That's true.'

We signed the contract and each took a copy. I put the contract in my inventory and got up from my seat. My business was over.

,Is Robert dead?'

,Robert? Who's that?'

,He's an old butler. He participated in this incident.'

,Ah. If you're talking about that old butler, I killed him. Is there a problem?'

,No. It's just a pity. If you're heading to the Red Falcon area, I'll have my subordinates guide you. I'll provide a helicopter.'

,...How did you know?'

,Now that you've just reached level 7, you must want to wield your power, right? I was the same.'

,I'd appreciate it if you could guide me.'

,Currently, the master of the Red Falcon is in another area. It will take about 30 minutes for the master to return to the Red Falcon area. Wreak havoc appropriately and come out.'

,Why? Wouldn't it be good for the Raven Knights, who are in an antagonistic relationship, if I destroyed the Red Falcon?'

,We're not just in an antagonistic relationship. We also cooperate in many ways. Ah, this is a secret.'

I wasn't very surprised. Didn't we see them cooperating to some extent over the dungeon this time as well?

After that, I headed to the Red Falcon on a helicopter provided by the Raven Knights.

The Red Falcon's fortress can be seen in the distance. Like the Raven Knights, it was protected by a barrier.

,The helicopter may be shot down if you approach any further.'

,This is enough.'

I went down to the ground.

There's no need to force my way into the fortress like when I attacked the Raven Knights.

,A barrier isn't invincible.'

Knock, and it will be opened.

As soon as I landed on the ground, I fired magic at the fortress.

[Railgun]

A metal bullet exceeding the speed of sound strikes the fortress barrier. The barrier fluctuated greatly once.

That day, the Red Falcon fortress was half destroyed by a mage.
