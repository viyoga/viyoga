Keolkeok keolkeok.

Each step made the armor clang with metallic sounds. I descended the rickety wooden stairs, each creak a testament to their imminent collapse, heading down to the first floor.

The first floor served as both a dining hall and a tavern, but it was devoid of customers. It was the middle of the day, after all. People were probably busy working.

,Sir Paladin-nim. You're up? I'll prepare your breakfast right away."

I was greeted by the landlady of the establishment.

She was a bright woman. Pretty face, great figure. I believe she had a husband, so she probably received quite a bit of envy from the men in this village. Or maybe she still does.

I walked towards the kitchen where she was.

,Sir Knight-nim?"

She was preparing bread and soup, looking at me with a puzzled expression.

,Oh? Your armor has changed? It's much cooler than the one you had yesterday. I'll get your breakfast ready quickly."

I didn't stop approaching her. She seemed to realize something, giving me a suggestive smile.

,...Did you change your mind today? I'm always ready."

She gave me a seductive eye-smile.

This woman had tried to seduce me last night as well.

This wasn't a brothel, but it was common to see innkeepers seducing men and selling their bodies. That's how tough this world was.

Kiiiiiiing.

I activated Holy Sight.

Her body and face distorted, revealing her true nature. A mantis-like head with arms made of blades.

,Wow. Those are some fascinating eyes. Seeing the Order's symbol... is that a special holy art unique to Paladins?"

,Shut up."

,Huh?"

My left hand moved. I grabbed the landlady's head tightly. Fingers encased in steel pierced through her skin. My fingers were stopped by her skull, but blood trickled down.

,Aaaaaaaaaaagh!"

,Die, you corrupted. That's the greatest atonement you can make."

I plunged the greatsword in my right hand into the woman's chest. Her body twitched. Her flesh writhed, shedding its human skin and revealing its true form. Without hesitation, I dragged the sword downwards. The woman spilled her entrails and collapsed to the floor.

I didn't let my guard down and struck the fallen woman's head with my sword.

Demons and the corrupted weren't human. Those with good regenerative abilities could regenerate even after spilling their guts. The most reliable way to kill them was to chop off their heads like this. An even more reliable method was to kill them using divine power.

[Level has increased.]

[Name: Eugene

Class: Paladin

Level: 2

Strength: 7 Agility: 5 Stamina: 5 Divine Power: 2

Available Points: 1

Skills: Holy Sight (Lv. Master), Holy Aura (Lv.1)]

I immediately used the point to increase Divine Power.

The most efficient stat was Strength, but the most important stat was Divine Power.

Before leaving the store, I glanced at the dead landlady's corpse. I only felt disgust.

,If you fall for this woman's seduction in the game... you get eaten and die right away."

This world was dangerous. Traps were everywhere. The reason I chose Holy Sight was to avoid these traps. There would definitely be traps I didn't know about in the future.

I walked out of the store and slowly surveyed the village. In this small village with less than 50 people, there was no sign of life.

I carefully examined the ground. I could see a dark aura. This was also one of Holy Sight's abilities. The ability to find traces of demons.

,I already know where it is."

I started walking. My destination was the forest behind the village.

•••

Kyaaaaaaak!

A monster jumped out from a tree. I calmly swung my greatsword, dealing with the charging monster.

The monster resembled a squirrel.

A Corrupted Beast.

Corruption doesn't only happen to humans. When animals are corrupted, they're called Corrupted Beasts.

Black smoke rose from the Corrupted Beast's corpse and rushed towards me. The souls of demons or the corrupted were sticking to me, cursing me.

,Simply put, it's experience."

[Level has increased.]

[Name: Eugene

Class: Paladin

Level: 3

Strength: 7 Agility: 5 Stamina: 5 Divine Power: 3

Available Points: 1

Skills: Holy Sight (Lv. Master), Holy Aura (Lv.1)]

I increased Divine Power again this time.

I had no choice.

If Divine Power was low, I would be quickly corrupted by the influence of demonic and corrupted souls.

,Just because I'm a Paladin doesn't mean I can't be corrupted."

Even with Full Recovery and Heavenly Heart, I had to prepare for corruption.

I continued forward. The frequency of Corrupted Beasts appearing increased.

,......."

I stopped walking and looked down at my body.

A dark aura was enveloping my body. After killing over 20 Corrupted Beasts, the corruption was progressing to a fairly serious level.

,I should use it soon."

I took out a bottle of Holy Water from my bag. I sprinkled the Holy Water on my body. The aura of corruption began to fade. The aura of corruption didn't completely disappear.

,There's a limit after all."

To completely eliminate the aura of corruption, I had to go to a cathedral or church. Holy Water's effects were stronger in sacred places.

,I've arrived."

A broken stone wall appeared. In the corner of the stone wall, which was over 5m high, was a giant spider. The spider's head, which was over 3m tall, was a carp's head. The completely mismatched combination caused an instinctive sense of disgust.

,...Arax. The gatekeeper of the tutorial. The newbie's nemesis."

It was said that half of the newbies who played Paladin for the first time gave up the game because they couldn't get past this monster.

,Let's do it."

I grabbed the greatsword with both hands and took a fighting stance.

The first pattern of Arax came to mind. It would leap. It would fly over in an instant and try to grab me. I would dodge and then hit its ass hard.

But my plan fell apart from the start. Arax didn't leap. It was circling around me as if it was exploring.

,...Right. This isn't a game, it's reality."

Arax's carp head gaped. It spat out oil from its mouth. I dodged the oil and charged at it.

,Holy Aura."

Divine Power moved. A faint golden aura surrounded the greatsword. The greatsword slashed Arax's front leg. Smoke rose from the severed leg.

,Kieeeeeeeeeeeek!!"

Arax screamed and rolled around on the floor. It was an opportunity. I climbed onto its body. I put strength into my legs, using the weight of my armor to press Arax down, and swung my sword.

,Die!"

It was the moment I was about to bring down the greatsword. The screaming monster maintained its composure and wrapped its spider legs around my body. Stormbreaker held up, so the legs didn't pierce my body. However, the pressure was getting stronger and stronger. The armor wouldn't last long either.

,Kikikikikik!"

,You tricked me with smoke! You cunning bastard...!"

Since it had come to this, it was all or nothing.

I let go of the sword. The sword was too big to swing in this close-quarters situation. Instead, I hit Arax's head with both hands and grabbed and pulled at parts of its body.

,Lightning!"

Pajijijijik!

The lightning manifested by Divine Power shone with a golden light. The lightning flowed through Arax's body. Arax screamed terribly. At the same time, its legs began to tighten even more.

The armor tore. Arax's sharp legs dug into my body. The feeling of its legs stirring up my insides was the worst.

,...Death is certain."

I gritted my teeth. I grabbed Arax's head with both hands and crushed it. The carp's head shattered. But its legs were still full of strength.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

Arax, having confirmed that I was dead, finally released its legs. It staggered, trying to regain its balance. Its shattered head slowly began to regenerate.

Arax had let its guard down. I didn't miss the opportunity and used Full Recovery. I grabbed the Paladin's sword that had fallen to the ground and immediately stood up. A startled Arax shot a spider web from its ass.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The spider web slid off to the side as soon as it touched my body.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 6]

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 5]

I closed the distance in an instant and frantically swung the Paladin's sword imbued with Holy Aura at Arax.

Wham wham wham!

I slashed its legs and split its torso. No, it was closer to hitting it.

,You disgusting spider bastard! Die quickly!"

Arax died.

Its soul stuck to my body, trying to corrupt me. Because it was a demon, the aura of corruption was incomparable to that of a Corrupted Beast.

[Level has increased.]

I hurriedly raised my Divine Power with the points I gained from leveling up. The aura of corruption weakened. I didn't stop there and took out Holy Water from my bag. The steel bottle containing the Holy Water was sturdy and didn't break even during combat.

I sprinkled Holy Water on my body. The aura of corruption weakened.

,I'll go back to the village, kill all the corrupted, and take the experience. And then let's go to the Valentier Cathedral."

After making plans for the future, I pulled the heart out of Arax's corpse. A demon's heart was a good item ingredient.

,I have to take everything I can."

•••

I returned to the village.

There was still plenty of time before sunset, but for some reason, all the villagers were gathered together. They quietly watched me. Their eyes were filled with fear as they looked at me.

,Did they find the corpse in the inn."

I looked at the villagers one by one. More than 10% of them were corrupted.

A man jumped out in front of me. A face I remembered. The landlady's husband.

,Sir Paladin-nim! Why, why did you kill my wife?!"

,Your wife was a disgusting corrupted."

,No! My wife was human! She was human! Why did you kill my wife?!"

,......."

I ignored his screams and passed him by. He wasn't corrupted, so there was no need to kill him. If he attacked, I could kill him then.

A boy jumped out in front of me. The boy, who looked about 10 years old, handed me a flower.

,Sir Paladin-nim! I heard you came to save the village. Thank you! Please accept this flower!"

,...Disgusting."

,Huh?"

My right foot moved. I stomped on the boy's chest as if pressing down on it. The boy's face, which had been bewildered, contorted with fear. The boy desperately tried to shake off my foot and leg, which were encased in thick steel. I put more strength into my foot. The boy vomited blood.

,It, it hurts, Sir Paladin-nim! Sa, save me!"

I ignored him and raised my greatsword.

The boy's eyes widened. The boy's face and body distorted. Human skin peeled away, revealing the skin of a gruesome monster.

,Corrupt."

I held the sword in reverse grip. The tip of the sword was aimed precisely at the corrupted's neck. The monster's tentacles surged and clung to my legs.

,Begone."

Kwack!

I stabbed the boy's neck.

Immediately after, the corrupted who had been hiding in all directions revealed their true nature and charged at me.
