That's South Korea's current official coin count. A simple calculation puts them at 262 days of survival starting today.

Comparable to the United States, a hunter superpower.

Of course, that's only counting official coins. The number of unofficial coins America holds is something only the U.S. president likely knows for certain.

Lee Yeonhee managed Baek Seonmo, the South Korean president whose mind had been dominated by Elena's illusion magic, and in practice she was handling the duties of the South Korean presidency.

Diplomatic documents piled up on her desk. Mostly proposals from hunter-weak nations. Screams that couldn't be hidden even in the cold formal language of the documents leaked through.

'Coin loan requests.'

Coin loan requests flying in from nations with less than 50 days of survival remaining.

As a head of state, it was a difficult request.

Coins were the lives of citizens. Those nations were essentially asking to borrow their people's lives.

'Considering future diplomacy and national prestige, I can't coldly refuse. But if I accept these requests, the citizens will be shaken.'

Currently, South Korea is the fastest-growing nation in the world. The coin acquisition rate boost from Seong Yujin holding the number one ranking was elevating Korea's standing.

If she refused the loan proposals here, the entire world would condemn South Korea.

'...The odds of getting those loaned coins back are slim.'

She had to think of it as throwing coins into the dirt. No, a donation for show. At least internationally, they wouldn't be condemned.

Georgia, El Salvador, Laos, Burundi, Mongolia, Algeria, Myanmar.

Split 3 billion coins among these seven nations.

For the time being, she'd be able to refuse further coin loan requests.

'I must uphold the moral duty of a developed nation. Korea is a developed nation, after all.'

There would be some public backlash, but not severe. And even if there was, it would be directed at President Baek Seonmo.

Nationalistic video scenarios rapidly assembled in Lee Yeonhee's mind. What needed emphasis this time was Korea's magnanimity and morality in helping the weak. If she led with morality, the citizens wouldn't be able to openly oppose it.

Some time passed, and a middle-aged man entered her office, tense.

"Director. Drugs are spreading through South Korea."

Drugs themselves weren't surprising. South Korea was no longer a drug-free nation. Narcotics were spreading through various routes. Especially lately, as life grew harder and crime rates rose, teenage drug offenders were becoming a common sight.

"There'd be no reason to report this to me face-to-face unless it's something unusual. This isn't an ordinary drug, is it?"

"Yes. It's a drug called Rou. It means 'meat' in Chinese. Unlike normal drugs, it's shaped like a small chunk of meat, which is how it got the name. I brought a sample with me."

He pulled a plastic bag from his briefcase. Inside was a chunk of meat smaller than a thumb. Raw flesh marbled with pink and white. But compared to real meat, there was something off about it. At a glance, it could have been mistaken for gum shaped like meat.

"What are the effects?"

"Just putting it in your mouth and chewing spreads the drug through your system instantly. As a stimulant, it eliminates fatigue, boosts concentration, and temporarily numbs pain. Its addictiveness exceeds cocaine or methamphetamine."

"...Judging by the effects alone, it could be used as a combat drug."

"It's actually spreading rapidly among hunters. They take Rou and attempt floor challenges while under its effects."

"What about side effects? It can't be just highly addictive."

"The brain and nervous system gradually deteriorate. Specifically, the brain slowly shrinks and develops infarctions. And when the drug's effects wear off, severe headaches occur. I'm told they're headaches that ordinary painkillers can't touch."

"So a vicious cycle is created where users take more drugs to forget the headaches?"

"Yes. The situation is serious. Experts have determined it's far more dangerous than other drugs. We need to take action as soon as possible."

"Is it confirmed that this drug was made in China?"

"According to our investigation, the source is China."

Currently, China was treading carefully around South Korea. Because of the 'True Blood' that Seong Yujin possessed. They were even trying to lift the Hallyu ban—there was no way they'd spread drugs into Korea.

Chinese criminal organizations operating outside the Chinese government's control? It defied common sense that such organizations could produce a drug like this.

"Form a special task force to respond to this drug. And... investigate the source of this drug more carefully and thoroughly."

"Understood."

Wang Jiezhou, the President of China, gazed at the small vial before him, his eyes gleaming.

『True Blood

Grade: Legend

Become a high-ranking vampire.』

He'd obtained it after a competition with the U.S. president. He'd had to shower Korea with concessions including lifting the Hallyu ban, plus 1 billion coins and a top-grade summoning stone—but seeing the True Blood with his own eyes, he felt no regret.

'Those damn Americans drove up the cost.'

He understood. The U.S. president was at an age where he had to worry about tomorrow. Of course he'd be desperate for the True Blood.

But Wang Jiezhou had employed a scheme, spreading rumors in America. The U.S. president becoming a vampire? The American people would never accept that. Before the rumors could fully spread, the U.S. president abandoned the True Blood.

"China has taken a slight loss, but in the long view, it ultimately benefits China. That China has become a superpower today is all thanks to my reign. As of today, my reign will continue for hundreds of years, and China will seize world hegemony."

He murmured as if steeling his resolve, then without hesitation tipped the elixir of immortality into his mouth.

"Urgh!"

Wang Jiezhou's eyes flew wide open.

Kkudeuk, kkadeuk, kwadeudeudeuk!

His body began to change. Fat burned away and bones were shaved down. Wrinkles vanished and his half-gray hair turned pitch black. His canines lengthened and his skin grew paler. Wang Jiezhou felt a surging vitality he'd never experienced even in his youth, and looked into the mirror.

His twenty-something self stared back from the mirror.

"Ha, haha. Hahaha! I've regained my youth!"

Sitting in his office, he swallowed dryly. He was thirsty. But what came to mind wasn't water—it was blood.

'So I really have become a vampire. It's not an uncontrollable urge to drink blood. This is weaker than a craving for a cigarette. Nothing to worry about.'

He put a cigarette in his mouth instead of blood. He pictured the future ahead.

'Leave South Korea alone for now. What I need to do now... is purge.'

The successors and close aides he'd prepared for his old age and retirement. He needed to reclaim the power he'd distributed to them and concentrate it on himself. That was the first step toward world hegemony.

"I will become greater than Qin Shi Huang."

Disassembler.

The basic skill of Technologists and Bionics in the 'Eternal Eden' world. It creates a large machine called a disassembler. Putting objects or biological corpses into the disassembler yields garbage and resources.
