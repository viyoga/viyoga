The Gentlemanly Demon (僞), whose true name is Mammon, the Demon of Greed.

I slowly walked over and sat down in the chair before him.

"It's been a while. Your atmosphere seems quite different from back then. You look far more at ease than you did."

"You remember me?"

"Of course. Few use this Hell Shop, and among them, humans are especially rare. I remember every human who has ever set foot in this shop."

"Other humans have made it here besides me? While still alive?"

"Hell's Rest Stop has an entrance in every Hell Zone. It can be opened as long as the conditions are met. Just as you fell into Hell, there are humans who fall into Hell while still alive. Most die in the Hell Zones, but if they're lucky, they make it into Hell's Rest Stop."

"Among them, has anyone returned to the surface?"

"Hmm. Not that I know of. Aside from you, that is."

Even Kang Myungjin was wary of the Hell Zones, so forget clearing them—just surviving to get out would be difficult.

"I need information on the Hell Zones."

"Haha. Of course, I have it prepared. However, most of the information you want will cost quite a bit. I only accept AP as payment. Bartering... unless it's an exceptionally special item, I won't accept it."

I curled up one corner of my mouth.

I'm not the man I used to be. The AP I currently hold is as follows.

「Held AP: 17,219,415」

17 million AP.

Even if I do nothing but breathe, over 43 million AP flows in every month. In all of Atlantis, when it comes to AP held by an individual, there's no one richer than me. The Emperor? He may have many treasures, but in terms of pure AP, he won't have that much. After all, the Emperor doesn't rule every zone of the Empire.

"I have plenty of AP. Don't worry about it."

"Very well. What are you curious about?"

"First, my zone. Zone 9,371. I left it vacant for years, yet nothing seems to have changed. If anything, the level of the demonic beasts feels weaker. No demons from other Hell Zones invaded my territory either. Why is my zone intact? Or is it only intact on the surface?"

I don't particularly like it, but Zone 9,371 is going to be my base. There may be dangers in my zone that I don't know about.

"Haha. That's not very difficult information. I'll tell you for free as a service. Zone 9,371, the Red Drought, is what you'd call a shithole. The demonic beasts roaming the zone are on the weak side, and there are no notable resources whatsoever. The monthly AP income would be on the low end too. Isn't that right?"

He's right. As the ruler of the zone, I receive 80,000 AP every 30 days. 80,000 AP is on the low end even among the zones I rule.

"True enough, it's a land where even water is hard to find. Before I planted trees, there weren't any trees either. And there was no demon ruling this zone."

"Correct. Other demons didn't know how to clear that zone. They didn't even bother looking into it."

The clearing method was to plant a tree in this drought. An ordinary tree wouldn't work, of course. Didn't I also receive help from the demon before me?

"Still, the 80,000 AP a month must have been tempting."

"Correct. Once you gain dominion, you can earn AP every month, so it's profitable. Therefore, the demons thought: once a ruler appears, kill the ruler and take the dominion."

I had been away from the Hell Zone, on the surface, all this time. Not once had I returned to the Hell Zone.

"...So the demon bastards were waiting for me to come back."

"Yes. It's possible word has already spread that the master of the Red Drought has returned."

I wasn't shaken.

As I am now, I'm confident I can handle most demons on my own. Of course, if demons attack in a pack, or a named demon comes at me, even I would have a hard time.

"I cleared the Red Drought and became a Hell Baron. There wasn't any particular benefit. Does this title even mean anything?"

"Information on Hell Nobility, is it? That'll be 300,000 AP."

"I'll pay."

「Paying 300,000 AP to the Gentlemanly Demon (僞).」

「Held AP: 16,919,415」

"A Hell Noble refers to a lord who rules a Hell Zone. You can lead the demonic beasts and demons within your zone as an army. Demonic beasts and demons with low power and low intelligence cannot carry out complex orders, but they will obey without fail."

"And if they're strong and intelligent, do they rebel?"

"Yes. Though the likelihood is low. In most cases, they leave the Hell Zone and enter the service of another lord."

"Are there any other benefits?"

"For lords of Count rank or higher, the Hell they rule itself becomes their power. Additionally, the Hell power they rule grows stronger under the lord's influence."

"What's the highest rank of Hell Nobility? King?"

"As of now, it's Duke. There are no demons holding the rank of King."

"...'As of now,' he says. What are the conditions for obtaining Hell Noble ranks?"

"Ruling Hell Zones. One zone is Baron, three is Viscount, five is Count, seven is Marquis, ten or more is Duke. The conditions for becoming King are unknown. There may be a demon who knows, but... at the very least, I don't."

He also told me the current number of known Hell Nobles.

Dukes: 12. Marquises: 20. Counts: 35. Viscounts: 73. Barons: 127.

There were more Hell Nobles than I'd expected.

"Not long ago, one of the Hell Dukes died."

"Hmm? No Hell Duke has died. Ah, perhaps you mean the 72 Demons? They ignore the laws of Atlantis and call themselves Hell Nobility. It's not exactly wrong. The Hell Nobles of Atlantis were influenced by them, after all."

"So the 72 Demons have nothing to do with the Hell Zones?"

"It's not that they have nothing to do with them at all. However, not all 72 Demons are connected to the Hell Zones."

That was the end of the information related to Hell Nobility.

300,000 AP felt like a waste, but I let it go. I had plenty of AP left anyway.

"I want a complete map of the Hell Zones. Preferably one with faction information included."

"A map without faction information is 1 million AP. A map with faction information that updates automatically is 5 million AP."

"Filthy expensive."

"The choice is the customer's."

"...I'll buy it. The 5 million one."

I had no detailed information on the Hell Zones. A map depicting the entirety of the Hell Zones was absolutely necessary.

"This map happens to have been updated today. Faction information is refreshed periodically."

"Updated today?"

"Yes. Since the Devourer of Forbidden Books was annihilated, the zone he ruled was updated. Currently, his zone has become ownerless and turned into a battlefield."

「Paying 5,000,000 AP to the Gentlemanly Demon (僞).」

「Held AP: 11,919,415」

「Complete Map of the Hell Zones

A map depicting the Hell Zones.

Faction information of the Hell Zones updates automatically.

Rank: S」

The map was colorful, with factions marked on it.

Using my zone, the Red Drought, as a reference, I roughly calculated the scale... It appears to be at least the size of the American continent.

"The blank spots here and there—are those uncleared Hell Zones?"

"Yes. Zones that require special clearing methods. Most are small and lack special resources. Some are zones that demons cannot clear."

"The zone ruled by the Devourer of Forbidden Books is... to the west. Quite far. Niflheim would actually be closer."

That could be called fortunate. The aftermath of the war won't reach me.

I checked the zones near the Red Drought. They were Viscount and Baron territories. Hell Counts and above were considerably farther away.

'So Counts and above are the ones ruling the honey pots.'

I checked the four cardinal ends of the continent. The entrances to the Hell Zones. Among them, the familiar one was Niflheim to the north. It was far closer than the other entrance zones.

"Niflheim's ruler only governs a single zone."

"Niflheim is one of the places that receives the dead first. That's why its influence is stronger than other zones. The power of the goddess who rules that Hell is also absurdly strong. Conquering Niflheim through direct confrontation... would be close to impossible."

I idly tapped Niflheim on the map with my fingertip.

If humanity is to clear the Hell Zones, they must conquer and claim Niflheim.

"Do you know about Ragnarok?"

"The destruction of the world."

"...I know there's a God of Deception somewhere in Hell. If I kill that God of Deception, can I stop Ragnarok?"

"I'll tell you as a service. You cannot stop it. The cause of this Ragnarok is not the God of Deception. However, it wouldn't be right to say he's unrelated. The children of the God of Deception are somewhere in the Hell Zones."

I stared at him silently. What reason did he have to specifically mention Loki's three children? The demon casually brushed off my suspicious gaze.

"I plan to clear the Hell Zones. Is there anything that would help?"

"There is one thing. It's an item coveted by many beings, Hell Dukes included, but it's so expensive no one has been able to purchase it."

"How much?"

"10 million AP. Since you've purchased a map from me, customer... I can show it to you."

"Well, let's see how impressive this item is. Show me."

「Solomon's Ring.

Mana stat increased by 10.

Can converse with animals.

Can see spirits.

Can seal demons and use the authority of sealed demons.

Currently sealed demon: Agares.

Usable authority: Word of Command.

Rank: SSS」

My deliberation wasn't long.

The Solomon I knew was a king with 1,000 wives. An item belonging to such a king suited me.

"I'll buy it."
