In modern times, a Chairman is no different from a noble. No, in some ways, they are much stronger than nobles. Even those in power bow their heads to the wealth they wield.

The reason I can act as I please, even while being called a total degenerate, is because I'm from a Chairman family.

With money, there's a lot you can do. You could throw a drug-fueled sex party right away, couldn't you? You can easily get most of what you want.

But this power wasn't mine.

I had to be mindful of others to wield this power. If I had to pay the price of responsibility to wield this power... it was no longer power or anything to me. Something that annoyed me. Nothing more, nothing less.

If that easy-to-use power was about to become shackles and a leash, I was willing to cut it off. After all, this is a quest world. It's good if I'm from a Chairman family. It doesn't really matter if I'm not. The quest condition isn't to become a Chairman.

I even have mind control abilities. When I need money, I can just control another Chairman.

I opened the door and went into the dining room.

The gazes of the people sitting at the table turn to me.

At the head of the table sat this body's grandfather, the Honorary Chairman.

Next to him, it's in order of rank. The current Chairman, this body's father, and his wife. Next to them sit the Chairman's siblings and their spouses. Then the Chairman's direct siblings. Then the cousins.

An empty seat. That was my seat. Even though I'm the Chairman's son, I'm the youngest, the third. Only cousins are below me. But those bastards below me are sending me contemptuous looks.

Even if I search my memories, it wasn't originally like this. They've judged that I've been completely pushed out of the successor competition due to recent events.

,Have you come? Why aren't you greeting? I told you firmly to greet your elders."

The Honorary Chairman clicks his tongue.

I was surprised. Contrary to that voice and attitude, the Honorary Chairman had no irritation or anger at all. Perhaps sensing that, the faces of the other family members stiffen. Even the Chairman, this body's father.

,I was late because the traffic was heavy."

,Is that so? That can happen. Come and sit down."

I didn't sit at the table but walked towards it. Just because the Honorary Chairman is showing goodwill? I've already decided to commit the deed.

Everyone's gaze turns to me at my actions.

,Seong Yujin! What are you doing?!"

,...I think he's acting a bit strange."

,Isn't he on drugs? He looks exactly like a drug addict."

,He came into the main house high on drugs? Is he out of his mind?"

,Father. It seems your nephew has gone crazy. Isn't it because you've been too lenient with him? Even if you usually emphasize strength, Father... this has crossed the line. You should punish him severely."

Everyone chattered as if they had gotten an opportunity.

I looked at the guy in the front. His information pops up in my head.

A cousin who's just entering middle school. The youngest son of my uncle. His name is Seong Yusaeng. Seong Yusaeng, who met my eyes, flinched. But he soon opened his mouth with an obnoxious smile.

,What are you looking at, you drug addict!"

He had no manners. Well, it was to be expected. I wasn't the only one who was spoiled and raised with love because I was the youngest. Besides, this family was born without manners.

,You said you were going to become the Chairman last time, right."

,Yeah!"

,You're my competitor then."

I grabbed Seong Yusaeng's hair and slammed him onto the table.

Peok!

Food splattered upwards. Rice and miso stew, broken pieces of dishes.

Peok! Peok! Peok! Peok!

I slammed Seong Yusaeng's head onto the table without stopping. I controlled my strength so he wouldn't die easily. But there was a limit to that. Soon, Seong Yusaeng's head burst with blood and flesh, and brain matter flowed onto the table.

A suffocating silence descended on the dining room. Someone's soul had left their body, someone rubbed their eyes as if they couldn't believe what they were seeing, and someone was terrified.

,Kyaaaaaaa!"

A sharp scream came from my uncle's wife. Seong Yusaeng's mother.

,Yusaeng! My son! What are you doing, Chief of Security! Kill that bastard!! I said kill him!!"

I turned my head slightly and looked at the Chief of Security. The Chief of Security didn't move. He was stiff. He was sweating profusely and met my eyes. He seemed to be contemplating something, then turned his head.

,Chairman-nim. I'm resigning. That, dying like a dog is a bit much even for me.... Yujin-doryeonim. I didn't see anything."

I was too annoyed to listen to his excuses, so I took out Hwaryeon Bido and swung it.

Lightning Flash of the Thunder Sky Style.

A red trajectory cut across the Chief of Security's neck.

In South Korea, a strongman who ranks within the top 100 in terms of strength died without doing anything. This was the power of doping drugs.

,What's with a servant flapping his mouth without the master's permission? And you, you fucking bitch. Who told you to scream so loudly?"

I raised my index finger and pointed at my aunt. A bolt of lightning shot out and hit the woman. The woman fell without even screaming, along with a smoky smell. She was dead.

,S, stop! Yujin! Stop it!"

Another woman stepped forward.

The woman next to the Chairman. My mother. I habitually looked at her head up and down. She got points for being my biological mother. She probably did pretty well when she was younger... but I wasn't interested in a 50-year-old hag.

,Did my words about being quiet sound like dogshit to you?"

I swung Hwaryeon Bido. Blue sword energy flew and cut my mother's upper body in half.

She's my mom? So what?

Fear descended.

I liked it.

Things proceeded smoothly. I thought the bodyguards would rush in, but there were no bodyguards inside. The bodyguards outside didn't seem to know anything about what was happening inside. If I had known this would happen, I wouldn't have injected the drugs to the limit.

,No, it was because I injected the drugs to the limit that the Chief of Security was scared. He realized how strong I was."

I swung my sword.

Seogeok.

One of my cousins' heads falls off.

,A, aaaaa!"

As I was about to swing my sword at another cousin, my aunt got up and hugged my cousin. I nicely cut them both in half.

I also killed the guy who was trying to escape when he saw an opportunity.

I also killed the guy who was begging for his life.

,Y, Yujin. I'm your father. Did you want to become the Chairman? I, I'll let you become the Chairman. So stop."

The terrified Chairman said. I killed my mother, so is there any reason to spare my father?

The Chairman position? It's good if I have it. The more money, the better. But it didn't matter if I didn't have it. If I had any regrets, I wouldn't have started this in the first place.

Seogeok!

I killed the Chairman.

I didn't think deeply about what would happen next. I'll probably become a fugitive. I know that I won't become the Chairman just because I killed the entire Chairman family. I plan to stay at Jeong Suyeon's place for the time being.

There's one left. The Honorary Chairman, the actual owner of Cheonil Group. I turned around to kill him, then stopped.

The Honorary Chairman wasn't trembling in fear. Even though his family was all dead, he had a smile on his face. His eyes looking at me were filled with pride and admiration.

,...Admiration?"

I looked at the Honorary Chairman.

,Why? Didn't you want to become the owner of Cheonil Group?"

,What's your purpose?"

,Did you want me to beg you for my life? I'm already eighty years old. I've already lived enough."

The Honorary Chairman is the founding Chairman who established the group. A natural Force Awakener like me. Thanks to that, he was so youthful that you could believe he was in his 50s. That was also the reason why he held real power as the Honorary Chairman.

,If you kill me, you'll be able to become the Chairman. I've already taken such measures."

,Such measures? Did you see the future or something?"

,How long do you think this grandpa has been watching you? I knew it would turn out like this someday. I knew that the nature of a ruler would come out!"

,I'm not the Seong Yujin you know."

,What are you talking about? You're the Seong Yujin I know. My grandson whom I know too well."

I racked my brain.

I searched through the information in my head. I looked through the memories I had already skimmed through once again. I recalled old memories. I've always been rude and disregarded life since I was young. The difference between the current me and the old me... there wasn't much of a difference. It's more like a difference in ability.

,What's your purpose?"

,I want Cheonil Group to become the owner of this South Korea. You can do it."

,Me? To be honest, I don't have any talent for making money."

The me in this world didn't have a knack for studying either. Even though I had a lot of money, I hated studying. My school grades had never been in the top 30.

,You have a talent for violence, don't you? Money and power are slowly losing their strength. What's emerging now is primal power. Violence. That power will rule the world. And you will become one of the rulers of the world."

,I ruined the family, does that matter?"

,You like women, don't you? You can increase the family as much as you want, can't you? As long as you're alive, the Seong family hasn't fallen. The angel is already watching you."

,......."

I stared at the Honorary Chairman. I don't feel any lies.

This old man was a madman. And there are two types of madmen in this world. Madmen who are helpful to me and madmen who are not. The Honorary Chairman was the former.

,The age of violence.... What, is the world going to end or something?"

,An angel has appeared. Do you know when the angel blows the trumpet?"

,......The end?"

,That's not all. The trumpet is the beginning of war. You will become a hero of that war. Hehehe."

Should I kill him? Should I use him?

I'm conflicted. There were pros and cons to each. The biggest benefit was, of course, using him. I could maintain my status as a third-generation Chairman.

Is this Honorary Chairman's appearance an act? Won't he stab me in the back later? Does mind control work on him?

,You're hesitating. Do you want to use me?"

,Even in the age of violence or whatever, money and power are important."

,Hmm. There's someone you don't like. What are you going to do?"

,I'll kill him."

,Even if it's the president?"

,There's no reason why I can't kill him."

,As expected! Good! Use me as much as you want! I'm just a useless old man waiting for the day I die anyway. Cleaning up after you would be perfect as a pastime."

,You can clean this up?"

,A monster came and killed my family and disappeared. Who would argue with me if I said that?"

I lowered my sword. I scattered the surging murderous intent and smiled.

,Grandpa. The moment the money runs out is Grandpa's memorial day."

,You're even going to take care of my memorial service? My grandson even has filial piety."

I laughed in disbelief.

Come to think of it, I remembered that Cheonil Group was conducting human experiments behind the scenes. The old man in front of me was the one who started and oversaw the human experiments.
