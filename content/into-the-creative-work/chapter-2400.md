The building collapses from the impact of the explosion. Fortunately, it didn't completely collapse, and it wasn't a high-rise building. The building's pillars and walls held up, probably because it was sturdily built.

,It's also fortunate that the missile didn't directly hit us.'

I check on Na Chaeyoung, whom I'm holding in my arms. Her eyes are tightly shut. The table blocked the falling stone fragments, so she doesn't seem to be hurt. Choi Hyejin and Irina are also fine. As Awakened ones, they have better physical abilities than ordinary humans, so there's no need to worry too much.

"What the hell is this? I shouldn't have come."

"Yujin-nim! Are you okay?!"

Choi Hyejin and Irina approached. Of course, I was fine. Na Chaeyoung also opened her eyes and sat up. Her lips were trembling, as if she was nervous. Well, it was natural for Na Chaeyoung to be the type to take action directly.

"What should we do? Anyone have any ideas?"

"Why don't we just smash everything?"

"Um, shouldn't we hijack a helicopter and run away? But missiles might fly our way, so maybe not...."

Choi Hyejin was ignorant, and Irina wanted to avoid fighting.

Na Chaeyoung brushed off the gray stone dust from her clothes and hair and said,

"We need to assess the situation first. Who do you think the enemy is?"

"Isn't it North Korea? This is North Korea, and... as soon as the South Korean president entered the building, a missile flew in as if they were waiting for it."

That was Irina's opinion. Come to think of it, most of the missiles that flew in seemed to have fallen towards the South Korean president. I didn't see it clearly, so I can't be sure, but that's how it felt.

"No. Yujin said it himself. North Korea wants a truce. Of course, South Korea doesn't want war either. So, this attack isn't by South Korea or North Korea, but by another force. Yujin, do you remember the Variant attack three days ago?"

"It would be strange if I didn't remember. Are you saying it's the work of the guy who was controlling them?"

"I think there's a high possibility of that. The timing is too perfect to be a coincidence. If that's the case, then there are probably two goals. One is to ruin the talks. The other is... you. Seong Yujin. The Awakened one Seong Yujin with healing abilities. If it's a force with some resources, they'd want to have you, even if it costs them a lot."

"...So it's not South Korea, at least. Doctor Na, where do you think it's from?"

"The United States. Most players will choose the United States. As such, it can be said to have more power than any other country. It's probably not the work of the US government. It's too extreme to say that. I think it's the work of some force within the United States."

It was then. Choi Hyejin pointed beyond the collapsed wall with one hand.

"What are those cobras crawling up here?!"

Hundreds of cobra Variants, appearing from who knows where, are crawling up. They are slightly larger than large dogs. Their shape is similar to cobras, but their backs are densely covered with spines like hedgehogs.

"...They're not cobras, they're hedgehog Variants. To be precise, they're artificially created Variants. Nazahog. They have almost no intelligence and can be easily controlled with a little training. They're weaker than naturally occurring Variants, but... their spines are poisonous, and they can shoot spines through their tongues."

"How potent is the poison? It's not like you die immediately if you get hit, right?"

"It's not that strong. In the case of humans, an adult can withstand it for about 2 hours even if poisoned. It will be a bit painful, though."

Tatatatatatang!

Gunshots ring out.

The sound was coming from inside, not outside.

"You bastard son of a bitch! Taste the spicy flavor of the Republic!!"

North Korean soldiers were firing their guns. The guns were effective. Bullets hit or penetrated the Nazahog's body. But the Nazahog didn't stop. They only collapsed and writhed on the ground after at least 30 bullets were embedded in their bodies.

Na Chaeyoung looked at him and explained with a groan.

"Nazahog are biological weapons. They were designed for combat. Nazahog don't feel pain. The best way to kill them is to aim for the head."

"Doctor Na, do you have the drone you brought? Can you activate it now?"

"No. The signal is jammed. It seems like there's a communication jammer. There's a combat AI, so I can run automatic combat mode, but... I have to go there and run it myself."

The plan to escape by flying away using the combat drone was scrapped.

"...I have no choice. If their goal is me, then I have to be the bait. In the meantime, you guys secure the helicopter and drones. The helicopter we came in landed on the opposite side, so it should be fine for now."

"That's crazy. You're going to be bait without knowing what the opponent has prepared?"

"Doctor Na, trust me. It's over if the helicopter gets destroyed here and we get isolated. We have to avoid the worst-case scenario."

"......The worst-case scenario here is you dying or being kidnapped."

"I know. Don't worry about that. There's no way I'd be taken down by those guys."

I made the best decision.

At least, that's what I thought.

,Are Kim Jaeji and the South Korean president... dead? It'll be fine if they're both alive, but... if either one of them is dead, a Korean war is almost certain. I'd rather they both be dead. If not... should I go and kill them myself?'

I grabbed the equipment in the corner. The ceiling collapsed and it was buried under stone fragments, but most of the equipment was fine.

I put on the combat helmet. It couldn't perform its proper functions because of the communication jamming, but it had a gas mask effect, so it filtered out dust and smoke. It was easier to breathe than being without it.

"I'm going. See you later. Choi Hyejin, Irina. You must protect Doctor Na."

I ran outside through the collapsed wall.

Pajijik.

Electricity flows through the pure white blade. The blade shone with a silver light, attracting attention from the surroundings.

The Variant Nazahogs around me all turned towards me at once. They reacted at the same time, as if their minds were connected.

,It's certain that some guy is controlling them.'

It's a type of psychic ability user. But an ability user specialized in controlling Variants, not people.

Since he's controlling them directly, he'll be hiding somewhere.

,Find that bastard and kill him. There's no need to be lenient. I can revive him with healing even if there are only pieces of his corpse. I can slowly extract the information later.'

Dozens of Nazahogs tilt their heads back slightly. It was a preparatory motion to shoot poisonous spines. I ran towards the back of the building. Poisonous spines flew and stuck into the snow-covered ground. None of them touched my body.

,I Yeonhee will be... safe, right? She's not an ordinary woman.'

Dozens of Nazahogs chase after me. As expected, their goal was me.

,It's inconvenient that the minimap isn't supported due to communication issues.'

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Wave.

Electromagnetic waves spread in all directions. I felt information within a 30m radius in real time. But the information was severely lacking. 30m was too short. Even in reality, the range of electromagnetic waves is over 500m. Feeling frustrated, I head towards a sparsely populated promenade.

Dududududu.

A North Korean military helicopter flies in from the sky. Communication is still jammed. They must have realized the anomaly of the talks on their own and dispatched. Armed soldiers descended from the military helicopter and began fighting the Variants.

I, who was running away to the promenade, stopped. I felt the presence of enemies surrounding me through electromagnetic waves.

,A total of 8 people. All are armed with bulletproof suits and assault rifles. They're even wearing combat helmets like me.'

They appeared, aiming their guns at me. I realized that their equipment was much better than the Holy Guard.

"Which force are you from?"

"Whoa, whoa. Calm down."

A man came forward. He was the only one among them who wasn't wearing a helmet. His equipment was weaker compared to the others. He didn't even have a gun.

He was a white man with orange hair.

"Are you an American?"

"Yes. I came from America to escort you."

"The US government is crazy."

"My name is Marcus. Unfortunately, I'm not from the government. You have two choices right now. One is to quietly follow us to America. We'll treat you as a VIP. Of course, we'll guarantee your life afterward. You'll be able to enjoy a comfortable life with privileges in a world that's collapsing. There's no such thing as a free lunch, so you just have to fulfill some obligations."

"You're piquing my curiosity. What's the second option?"

"To get beaten like a dog and dragged away. There's no comfortable life. You're confirmed as a slave. You know which one is the right answer without me telling you, right?"

"Yeah. The second one is the right answer. If you guys can drag me away, that is."

As soon as I raised the Cutlass Sword, Marcus shouted.

"Fire!"

Bullets poured in from all directions.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Barrier.

Even if I'm weaker in this world than in reality, I could still block bullets with an electromagnetic barrier. But not now. The bullets pierced through the electromagnetic barrier. The power was weakened, but they touched my body.

They didn't penetrate. It felt like getting punched. The problem was that there were dozens of bullets. Humans die if they get punched dozens of times. I fell onto the snowy field.

Marcus approached me. I quietly used Healing.

"Fortunately, you didn't die. Ah, looking at your eyes, you seem curious as to why the bullets are working. I knew you could manipulate electricity. So, I specially prepared special bullets that aren't affected by electricity. They're special bullets similar to rubber bullets."

Marcus took out a syringe from his pocket. It contained a green liquid. It was probably an anesthetic. Their main goal was to capture me alive.

"When you wake up from your nap, a new life will unfold. It'll be a somewhat terrible life, but... what can you do? Accept it, since it's the life you chose."

Marcus grabs my collar. I swung my sword at him. Aiming for his neck.

Kkang.

A translucent energy barrier appeared in front of Marcus's neck.

,So he's an ability user. I expected it since his equipment was weak.'

I knocked away the hand grabbing my collar and created some distance. They fired their guns at me again.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Barrier.

I reduced the power of the bullets with the electromagnetic barrier and avoided only the vital points with minimal movement. It was okay to have my arms, shoulders, and legs hit. I could withstand it.

"Evade! Create distance! Authorization to use grenades!"

Before the soldiers could react to his command, I approached them first. My footwork shone.

"You're too slow, you should die."

I swung the Cutlass Sword. The skintight suit the soldier was wearing seemed to block the blade, but when I applied force, the blade cut through the suit and dug in.

"Keueueueuk! There should be a stab-proof function...!"

"My sword is a bit special. In this state, it can even cut through steel."

Even if it can't cut through steel with some thickness, it can cut through stab-proof suits easily.

Seogeok!

,One down for now.'

I sense something flying towards me. It's too big and slow to be a bullet. It was a grenade. I lifted the corpse of the guy I just killed and used it as a shield.

Kwang!

I couldn't completely block the impact with the corpse shield. The combat helmet shattered, and my left arm was tattered. It was more powerful than a regular grenade.

I started running while using Healing. Bullets poured down. It was fortunate that it was a promenade. I could take cover behind the trees.

,If I stop, I'll die.'

I killed them one by one while running. I didn't care about getting hit by bullets. I could just heal.

Seogeok!

I kill another one.

"You monster bastard!!"

The soldier next to the dead soldier shouted and took out a grenade from his chest. It wasn't an ordinary grenade. It was a grenade with lights flashing on the machine.

The important thing is that it's mechanical.

Pajijik.

I used electromagnetic force to return that grenade flying towards me in reverse. The soldier flinched as if surprised. I couldn't see his face because of the helmet, but... he must have been horrified.

Kwaaaang!

The grenade exploded, and flames erupted. The flames that soared for a moment burned the surroundings and subsided.

,Did they make a flame bomb too.'

It wasn't a complete weapon, it seemed like a prototype.

Something flies towards me. A slash made of translucent energy. I kicked off the tree next to me and jumped up to avoid the slash.

,Marcus. Is that his ability? An ability to manipulate energy? Judging by how he's using it, he's nothing special.'

I killed all the soldiers, and only Marcus was left.

Jjak!

Marcus clapped his hands. Immediately after, invisible walls appeared on both sides of me and narrowed in at once. Before that, I kicked off the ground and ran forward, stabbing Marcus in the abdomen with my sword.

"How did you avoid it when you couldn't even see it? Was it a coincidence?"

"I can feel it even if I can't see it. And you're making it too obvious that you haven't used your powers before. You should get some training."

"...No one taught me how to train superpowers."

I twisted the sword. A groan of pain flowed from Marcus's mouth. I looked over the Nazahogs surrounding me and asked him.

"Where is the guy who's controlling the Variants?"

"Do you think I'll tell you? You're going to die here with me. Ah, I'll tell you something good before you die. We're going to recover your corpse and make a clone. You know why without me telling you, right? Now, let's go to hell together. I'm so glad I'm not alone."

Dozens of Nazahogs, with their mouths open, shot poisonous spines at once. There was nowhere to avoid. Pook pook pook pook! Dozens of poisonous spines were embedded in my and Marcus's bodies.

,Hmm. I didn't die instantly. Should I pretend to be dead for a bit? Maybe the guy who's going to collect my body will come.'

Seueueuk. Seueueuk.

The Nazahogs around me scattered. And about 3 minutes later, someone came out of the bushes, looked at the dead Marcus for a moment, and then slung me over his shoulder.

Pook.

"You idiot. You should have checked if I was really dead first. Or is it that my acting was that good?"

"To be alive after being hit by dozens of Nazahog's poisonous spines...!?"

"My healing ability is just a bit good. More than that, I'm looking forward to finding out what you guys who started this are."
