As corpses, flies were a cause for alarm.

Because these bastards will even devour rotten corpses. What's more, flies the size of 10cm? Putting aside their repulsive appearance, if I screw up, my undead army could become their meal.

"Black Sonata."

Black rain began to fall from the ceiling. The stats of the undead hit by the black rain increased.

"Kill those bugs."

Geueooooooo!

The undead respond. In reality, it's closer to an expression of hostility towards the flies than an actual response.

The speed of the incoming flies increased.

"Dark Lightning."

The black lightning emitted from my hand cut through the air and struck a fly. One fly was electrocuted to death.

'These bastards have excellent reaction speed.'

I wanted several of them to be electrocuted to death at once, but as soon as one fly was hit by the lightning, they scattered and evaded. Dark Lightning was ineffective against them.

'Should I change the status window to Lightning Blade and use firepower... No, I can handle these guys with my black magic.'

Changing the status window for mere flies was also a matter of pride.

The flies rushed at the alligator undead in the front. The alligator struggled and tried to shake off the flies, but it wasn't easy. The flies devoured the alligator in an instant. The alligator, now only bones, was emaciated.

"Move."

The alligator, now only bones, moves. It nervously swings its tail. The flies flew up as if mocking the alligator. The flies charged towards their next prey.

"Corpse Explosion!"

Kwaaaang!

The flies caught in the explosion rained down. Corpse Explosion was dozens of times more effective than Dark Lightning.

'It's a waste of undead consumed by Corpse Explosion, but... if I don't deal with them quickly, I'll lose even more undead.'

I don't want all the undead to have their flesh eaten by the flies and become skeletons. If there's only one type of troop, the army becomes weak. Moreover, skeletons are weak against blunt attacks.

"Corpse Explosion! Corpse Explosion!"

Kwaaaang! Kwaaaang!

Contrary to my worries, the flies were easily dealt with using Corpse Explosion because they swarmed together. I used the undead to gather the fly corpses in one place. They were insect corpses, but each fly was over 10cm in size. With hundreds of them piled up, they could be used as undead.

"I am your master!"

Black magic permeated the fly corpses.

The fly corpses clumped together and were reborn as undead over 2m tall. The undead flapped its wings and flew into the air. It was something resembling a fly. I simply named it Fly Bug.

I even used Undead Modification (SS). The Fly Bugs had the ability to spit acidic venom at enemies.

'Undead spitting acidic venom from the sky! That will be helpful!'

It can be used to throw other undead into the enemy ranks. If combined with Corpse Explosion, that's aerial bombardment.

Wiiiiiiiiiiiiing!

A swarm of flies appeared. Three times as many as before. The sixth disaster was not over yet.

'It doesn't matter. I can easily catch them using Corpse Explosion.'

I bring forward a plump zombie. It was bait. When the swarm of flies rushes at the zombie, I'll wipe them all out with Corpse Explosion. It was a very simple yet effective operation.

However, the situation did not go as expected.

The flies began to act separately instead of swarming together. They flew around in all directions, attacking the undead. Only two flies were interested in the zombie set up as bait.

'Do they have some level of intelligence? Or is a highly intelligent one controlling them?'

I can't use Corpse Explosion. It's the worst efficiency. I can't blow up an intermediate undead just to catch two or three flies. It's more efficient to have the undead attack them instead.

"God damn it! You're being such a pain in the ass!"

Wiiiiiiiiing!

The flies are also flying towards me. And there are quite a lot of them. It seems they think I look tastier than the others.

"Dark Flame!"

I create black flames. I lowered the firepower as much as possible. I could accidentally burn down the undead army.

Wiiiing! Wiiiiiiiiiiing!

Annoying.

The flies flying around me and attacking are very annoying.

"You goddamn useless undead bastards! Can't you see your master is being attacked?! Kill those flies quickly!"

I shouted at the undead out of frustration. It had an effect. The undead's attacks became slightly faster.

Whoosh.

A Fire Ball flew from behind and collided with a fly. The Fire Ball exploded, and its firepower spread in all directions.

"Keueok?!"

Flames clung to my body, caught in the explosion of the Fire Ball, and blazed fiercely. I fell from the kiln and rolled on the floor to put out the flames.

I glared at the culprit who shot the Fire Ball with fierce eyes.

Clack clack clack!

The Skeleton Mage rattles its jaw. It's as if it's saying it didn't do anything wrong. I felt really bad. I wanted to run over to it and smash its skull.

'I would have done that if it wasn't a Skeleton Mage.'

The Skeleton Mage was rare even among intermediate undead. Its body was weak, but it knew how to use magic, so it had the highest firepower among intermediate undead.

Whoosh!

Another Fire Ball flies towards me. Of course, the Fire Ball's destination is not me, but a fly. But the fly is in a position where I can't help but be harmed if the Fire Ball explodes. I strongly suspect that it's deliberately shooting Fire Balls in this way.

'First, I'll deal with the flies...!'

Black lightning shot out from the tip of my finger. Dark Lightning was more effective than Corpse Explosion against flies flying separately.

And 15 minutes later, all the flies were dead.

「You have overcome the sixth disaster.」

I looked around and checked the damage. 70 zombies and ghouls had their flesh torn off by the flies and became skeletons. There was no other damage.

I scraped the dead flies together in one place. I was a necromancer now. I couldn't just leave the dead corpses behind. I created 20 Fly Bugs. The Fly Bugs quickly moved around in the air.

And I arrived at the 3rd underground floor.

「The seventh disaster begins.」

Darkness came.

I couldn't see an inch ahead because of the pitch-black darkness.

'I can't even see the map.'

I didn't care. The structure of the 3rd underground floor would be the same as the structure of the 1st and 2nd underground floors. I just had to keep moving forward.

The undead are not frightened by the darkness. The undead army simply advanced according to my orders. If it were a person who wasn't undead, they would have been flustered by the sight of not being able to see anything.

「The eighth disaster begins.」

Thud. Thud thud thud!

Something fell from the ceiling to the ground. Judging by the sound, it seems to be something like a stone.

Thwack!

It seemed like something fell towards me too. I can't see anything in front of me, and I don't feel any pain, so I don't know where I was hit. I'm just guessing that I was hit.

"Skeleton Mage. Use Fire Ball on the ceiling. Light up the surroundings!"

Clack clack clack!

The Skeleton Mage uses Fire Ball.

It must have used it. Because I felt the mana. The problem is that it's still dark, so I can't see anything. The Fire Ball's flames didn't light up the surroundings.

"Barrier."

I gathered mana and created a barrier. No matter how much it's me, I couldn't cover the entire army. I have enough mana, but I can't see, so I don't know how far to extend the barrier.

'Let's save mana in preparation for the next disaster.'

Thwack thwack thwack thwack!

Something falling from the sky is blocked by the barrier. The sound was quite cheerful.

The undead army continued to move forward.

「You have overcome the seventh and eighth disasters.」

The disaster is over.

The world became brighter, and I could see what was falling from the ceiling.

'I thought it was a stone... it was hail.'

I check the damage situation of the undead.

About 5% of the total had decreased. Most of the undead had hail embedded in their bodies. Skeletons were better off, but zombies and ghouls with flesh had so much hail embedded in them that their bodies were barely visible.

One hailstone was embedded in my shoulder too.

I took out the hail embedded in the undead's bodies and went down to the 4th underground floor.

「The ninth disaster begins.」

The ninth disaster was locusts.

Locusts the size of large dogs were jumping towards me from the front. They have wings, but they don't use them.

"Shit."

They are incomparably more dangerous than the flies.

First of all, the locusts were excessively large. It wouldn't take them even a minute to devour a human. And they're incredibly fast. Also, there are over 2,000 of them.

"Fight together! Show those bastards how terrifying the army of death is!"

I use Black Sonata. Black rain falls. The strengthened undead prepare for battle.

"Skeleton Mages! Don't spare your mana!"

Dozens of Fire Balls flew overhead.

Boom! Bang!

The Fire Balls explode, massacring dozens of giant locusts. But the locusts didn't stop. Maybe because they have low intelligence, they didn't know fear.

Soon, the undead army and the locust swarm collided.

The locusts chew and swallow the undead, and the undead resist.

"Corpse Explosion! Dark Lightning! Dead Rise!"

I was the busiest. I fended off the charging locusts and resurrected the dead locusts as undead again. The locusts that became undead were helplessly defeated by the giant locusts.

The undead army was gradually being pushed back. More than anything else, the giant locusts were too fast.

"This won't do! Undead Modification!"

What are the natural enemies of locusts?

Horsehair worms!

I modified the undead and created dozens of long horsehair worms. The undead horsehair worms burrow into the locusts' bodies. It was quite effective.

After that, I focused on replenishing my forces.

"Dead Rise! Dead Rise! Dead Rise!"

The dead locusts rise as undead.

The war lasted for 2 hours. I was the one who barely managed to grasp victory. The existing undead legion was quite damaged, but I obtained 1,000 undead locusts.

'If I modify these guys properly, I can make them into Zealots with Speed Upgrade.'

It was a tiring but not bad battle.

「You have overcome the ninth disaster.」

「The tenth disaster begins.」
