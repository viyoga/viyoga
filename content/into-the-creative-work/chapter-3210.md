Seong Hari had been on edge lately.

Because things were too peaceful. The saying "calm before the storm" existed for a reason. She knew her son, Seong Yujin, was making his own preparations. She couldn't help. Yujin didn't want her to, and her stepping in could actually interfere with his plans.

Instead, she honed herself. Having lost her power to the Spirit King's curse, she kept her dulled senses sharper than a blade.

She had only lost her power—the experience accumulated from secretly continuing hero work just to make a living proved immensely helpful. One could say that losing her peak power had actually allowed her experience to accumulate well.

Having consumed the dragon heart of the Galaxy Heavenly Dragon, she was confident that she now surpassed her peak self. Yet she couldn't hide her tension, because the enemy she had to face was another version of herself who had become a demon.

How strong had that enemy become, wielding power similar to a spirit devourer without any hesitation? Even Seong Hari found it hard to imagine.

She had been inside the house when she suddenly snapped her head up. Seong Hari's eyes narrowed as she gazed out the window.

Just now, she had felt a disgusting demonic power.

It was an extremely faint magical energy.

A power that no one in the vicinity except someone of Seong Hari's caliber could detect.

Seong Hari grabbed her equipment and immediately went out the window.

On the rooftop of a building some distance away stood a white-haired woman in black armor holding a spear. She had the exact same face as Seong Hari, and she looked at her with indifferent eyes.

"Baal…!"

"Seong Hari. Let's settle this, just the two of us."

Baal's voice carried on magical energy. Seong Hari gripped her spear.

"Just what I wanted."

Baal waved her hand through the air. The sky seemed to crack, and a gate appeared. It was different from a dungeon gate.

Baal flew into the sky and entered the gate. Seong Hari created a force field beneath her feet and ran through the air into the gate.

It was a place with endlessly stretching white stone floors and a night sky filled with countless shining stars.

Seong Hari swallowed dryly. Beyond that night sky, she felt an incomprehensible gaze. She ignored it and erased it from her mind. Instinctively, she knew she must not dig deep into that gaze.

Baal, gripping her spear, took a combat stance. It was identical to Seong Hari's. It wasn't that she was copying Seong Hari. It was simply the most efficient combat stance in her estimation.

"Looking at you pisses me off. The same face and talent as me. But because you're the real one, you get to stand by Yujin's side."

"Is that jealousy? Yujin is the son I gave birth to. …The relationship between mother and child is a bit unusual, but there's no reason for a fake like you to interfere. And why are you so ill-mannered? I'm twice your age."

"I know. Ajumma."

"Ajumma, my ass."

"…Hag. Stop pretending to be young. The clothes you're wearing aren't something a woman in her forties would wear either. In modern slang, they'd call you a 'young-forty,' right? Looking at you makes me annoyed, then pitiful."

"Hah? I just wore this because it's comfortable and durable!"

Seong Hari lunged at Baal first. A strike accelerated by lightning aimed for Baal's head. Baal deflected it as if it were only natural.

KAAAAANG!

With the shockwave, Baal and Seong Hari were simultaneously pushed back. However, the distance Seong Hari was pushed was greater.

Seong Hari's expression hardened.

The difference in power.

She had realized that she was slightly outmatched in pure physical ability.

Baal's brow furrowed as well.

"Just as Mekin said. At this rate, I'd be pushed back by this damn hag…. If I hadn't gone all the way to the colony planet and struggled, a bad situation might have occurred."

"……Mekin. The Legion Commander of Destruction. That bastard predicted this situation?"

"Mekin looks like the king of ignorance at a glance, but… when it comes to sheer cunning, he's among the top of all demons. Mekin also knows that you, that Yujin, saw the future. He did blabber that after Yujin wiped out the Villain Alliance in America, he could no longer obtain information, though."

"…You were surveilling Yujin?"

"It wasn't direct surveillance. Among demons, there were those with the authority to read the past, see the future, or indirectly gather information. Of all things, they were under Mekin… I hope they died in this opportunity."

"……This opportunity. Bringing me here was a trap after all. …The outside must be in chaos."

"If it's not a trap, what did you think it was? Let me give you one piece of advice. The Demon Legion has bet everything on this invasion. If you stay here… humanity will lose and Earth will be ruled by the Demon Legion."

Dark red magical energy rose from Baal's body.

Seong Hari forced down her anxiety and impatience.

"Outside, my friends and Yujin are there. Humanity isn't weak just because I'm not there. From where I stand, you look more anxious than me. You want to kill me as fast as possible and go to Yujin, right? You don't want Yujin to die either."

“…….”

Baal gripped her spear tightly without a word. Seong Hari let out a laugh despite herself. Perhaps it was because she was still young. She couldn't fully hide her emotions. It felt strange, like looking at her own immature past self.

"Baal. Yujin wants to save you. I know that's impossible. Your essence is fake, and you're a Demon Legion Commander. Doing the things her son doesn't want to do is also a mother's job. Baal, I will kill you."

Behind Seong Hari's back, two pairs of silver wings appeared, shining like starlight.

"Try it if you can!"

The silver spear and the dark red spear clashed.

I narrowed my brow as I looked at Mekin standing there confidently. My mind raced.

'Baal isn't here.'

I had thought Mekin and Baal would be together. I assumed she would conserve her strength as much as possible and step in at the decisive moment. Just as Mekin had just done. If the Demon Legion had brought their full forces, Baal should naturally be included.

Baal isn't here. Is there a reason to hide herself by coming all this way now?

'If she was never here from the start?'

Thunder Heaven Style Electromagnetic Wave.

I scattered electromagnetic waves in all directions to detect. Baal was not on Demon Island. Instead, the electromagnetic waves detected heroes flying toward this side from the West Sea. The strange thing was that Seong Hari wasn't among them. In fact, if it were Seong Hari, it wouldn't have been strange for her to discover Demon Island before me and appear. Yet she wasn't there.

"Kuhuhu. Looking for Baal? It's futile. I ordered Baal to deal with the most troublesome one."

Seong Hari and Baal are fighting elsewhere.

Suddenly, the future I had seen before comes to mind. The result then was Baal's defeat. Baal dies at Seong Hari's hands.

'The future has changed. I can't guarantee Baal will win. Honestly, the current Seong Hari doesn't look like she'll lose.'

The problem was that neither outcome was the result I wanted.

'…Those two won't settle this easily. What I need to do now is simple. Kill Mekin as fast as possible and go to where those two are.'

Mekin grins. He speaks to the demons under his command.

"What are you doing, you rabble! Go to that city over there! Burn the city and devour the humans! Are you going to miss this chance to rampage without consequences?!"

"No, Demon King! We'll kill the humans right away! Kuhahahaha!"

The demons burst into laughter and sought ways to head to Incheon. Most demons flew through the sky, but some demons eyed the sea. They threw themselves into the water and began creating boats with their authority.

"Do you think I'll just stand and watch? Machina, Terra. Block the demons."

"Kuhuhu. Do you think two spirits can block all the demons?"

"I know it's difficult. That's why I prepared this without using it, for today. Spirit Gate."

『Summoning Spirit Gate (SS).』

A silver door appeared in the air. The door, over 10 meters tall, slowly opens according to my will.

『Opening the Spirit Gate.』

The air changes. As the connection to the Spirit Realm strengthens, its unique influence grows stronger in this location. As someone half in spirit state due to Spirit Necromancy, I felt vitality surge and my body grow comfortable.

[Ooooh, this feels quite nostalgic….]

I felt Arest's will rejoicing.

Countless spirits poured out from the Spirit Gate. From the lowest-grade spirits to the highest-grade spirits rarely seen in this world.

The various spirits automatically came out and formed temporary contracts. That didn't mean I was bearing the power of those spirits.

While the Spirit Gate is open, I don't need to bear the spirits' power.

'The Spirit Gate, strictly speaking, isn't my ability. It's a reward obtained when I contracted with the Spirit King.'

『Skill, Spirit Gate (SS) will be deleted.』

"The One commanded us to help humans!"

"Let's teach the demons a lesson!"

"Human! You have to give me that later!"

The spirits used their power. Waves rose where the demons were, winds howled, flames burst. The ground of Demon Island heaved, lightning fell, and darkness covered the demons. Screams erupted from all directions. The weakest demons began dying first.

'Is it because the power the spirits use isn't mine but the Spirit King's? A shame the Demon Hunter doesn't react.'

Mekin twisted his lips as he looked at the wide-open Spirit Gate.

"Spirit King. To think that outer god would help humans this much. This is unexpected. But that's all. They won't use their true power anyway. If you step in directly, the One will also move directly."

Mekin swung his greatsword. A high-grade spirit approaching him was struck and forcibly unsummoned, disappearing.

As demons of count-rank and above began stepping up in earnest, the spirits were increasingly forcibly unsummoned. Spirits were still pouring out through the Spirit Gate, but… more spirits were being unsummoned than emerging.

The tide of battle was unfavorable. However, I was relieved that the demons hadn't scattered to deal with the spirits.

"Finally coming."

From the direction of Incheon, an intense presence flew like a missile and landed on Demon Island. KWAAAAANG! The aftermath swept away demons.

Then, as the dust settled, the figure that appeared was Kang Jiyoung.

Korea's S-rank hero, the Pioneer of Earth and Heaven.

The wolf ears atop her dark brown hair twitched as she stood in a white coat and suit. Her eyes, using the Eye of Horus (S), glowed bright blue, and a magic circle rose around the greatsword in one hand. Behind her, Korea's heroes were arriving one after another.

"Seong Yujin. Don't leave the Academy on your own. You're getting demerits."

Kang Jiyoung was about to move when she stopped and looked at a spot in the air. My gaze and Mekin's also turned to the air. The air distorted, then tore open widely, and people emerged.

It was a shrine maiden with a hime cut and a voluptuous figure.

Japan's S-rank hero and the Principal of Arashi Academy. The Water Moon's Sky Princess.

She, who usually wore gorgeous kimonos and lived in the form of a child, had appeared dressed in full shrine maiden attire. She wasn't alone. Behind her were Japan's famous heroes.

"This humble servant has joined the battle to help my lord husband…. No, to help humanity!"

Tenrai Nagisa winked at me. The Japanese heroes around her looked back and forth between me and her with shocked faces. Kang Jiyoung's gaze was like looking at a senile old man.

That wasn't the end.

From the sky, a golden pillar fell onto Demon Island. The pillar of light, over 50 meters in diameter, had no physical force whatsoever. Soon the golden light disappeared like a mirage, revealing American heroes in splendid costumes within.

At the center was a handsome blond young man in a white suit.

The Chairman of the American Hunter Association, and the only SS-rank hero at present. Justice. Justin Haled.

Justin, with one hand shoved in his pants pocket, leisurely looked around.

"I've come to support as promised. Fortunately, it seems I'm not late."
