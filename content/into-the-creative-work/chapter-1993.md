,Kkeueoeoeok!'

Hades, grabbed by the collar by Zeus, involuntarily let out an ugly scream and flailed his limbs.

He was breathless. Not just because his collar was grabbed.

Divine power.

The overwhelming power of God was crushing Hades.

,When it comes to divine power, I'm no slouch either...!'

Hades was the king of the Underworld. And this is the center of the Underworld. It's only natural to exert more than 100% of his power in his own domain.

Yet he couldn't overcome Zeus's divine power.

,Does the worship of humans give power to Zeus?'

Humans living on the ground couldn't help but fear the sky. A place they can see just by looking up, an infinitely vast sky not allowed to humans, and a vague fear of the disasters that come from the sky.

,Even so, this level of power is unexpected. I can only say he was strong from the start...'

Hades gritted his teeth.

He couldn't give in so easily just because the opponent was strong. Even if he lost, he had to gain at least some advantage. Hades began to rack his brain.

,No answer. Do you really want to die?'

Kururureureung.

Thunder rumbled from Zeus. Blue lightning wriggled like a living snake along his arm. Astrape. Zeus's lightning. Hades couldn't help but tense up at the tremendous power.

,I resisted with divine power... but it's useless.'

Gods are immortal, so they don't die.

But that didn't mean they were invincible. They could be sufficiently neutralized. Like the Titan gods, including Cronus, who are currently trapped in Tartarus.

Hades gave up resisting.

,I lost.'

,You know your place well. I'll give you a chance to fix what you've done.'

There was a cold anger in Zeus's voice. It was too cold to be considered mercy for a traitor. This isn't mercy that Zeus wants to give.

That's why Hades could laugh even in the moment he was grabbed by the collar and overwhelmed.

,You seem to know that I'm the only one who can fix this mess. Well, who, what god, would want to take on this boring Underworld? The lower-ranked lesser gods would be eaten up by the Underworld... and the 12 Olympians have already settled in, so they wouldn't fall into the Underworld.'

,You talk too much. Do you really think I can't do anything to you?'

,Calm your anger, Zeus. Kidnapping your daughter and wife, Persephone... I'm also reflecting on it. I was too hasty.'

Thwack!

Zeus's fist landed squarely on Hades's jaw!

His jaw broke and he felt intense pain.

The fist didn't stop with just one blow.

Bang, Kwahang, Bang!

The fist wasn't fast. He slowly put his strength into it and heavily pummeled Hades.

,Kkeueueueueu....'

Hades gritted his teeth. Every time he was hit by a fist, it felt like the bones inside his body were being crushed. The bones of a god, which can't even be compared to those of a human? In front of Zeus's fist, that kind of thing was useless.

Kwahahang! Bang!

The fists continued.

Is there any point in enduring?

Kwahahahahaang!

His head exploded from one punch. As an immortal, Hades's exploded head regenerated. Hades, who had briefly fainted and woken up, couldn't avoid Zeus's fist that was flying in next.

Bang!

The fist falls again.

Hades knew that the fist was using pure brute force, not divine power.

,That monster bastard....'

Suddenly, a rumor that had been circulating among the gods came to mind.

Zeus is in his growth period.

The Titanomachy, which they thought was Zeus's prime, was only Zeus's childhood. When Zeus finishes growing completely, all the gods of Olympus combined can't beat Zeus. Such a ridiculous rumor had been circulating among the gods.

,Was it a prophecy, not a rumor...?'

Kwahang! Bang!

It was about the time he was getting used to his body exploding. A god descended through a hole in the ceiling. The god with winged shoes spoke to Zeus in a nervous tone.

,Zeus-nim! Please stop! Hades-nim is... no, Hades is already half-dead! He's lost his will to resist!'

,...Tsk.'

Zeus clicked his tongue and lowered his fist. The collar he held in his hand was still there.

,Hades. Listen carefully. The reason you're not thrown into Tartarus is thanks to the other gods of Olympus. They all said that you should rule the Underworld. They said to generously overlook one mistake. All the gods except me, that is.'

,...I see. It seems like no one wants to rule the Underworld. Fine. Do as you please.'

Hades slumped. He released the strength from his body. He had no will to resist. His divine power and charisma couldn't control the Underworld.

Zeus tossed Hades aside like throwing away trash.

,Hermes.'

,Yes. Zeus-nim.'

,Return to Olympus with Persephone.'

,Aren't you coming with us, Zeus-nim?'

,Since the sky isn't visible in the Underworld, there are many who don't know how to look up to the sky. I'm thinking of using this opportunity to show them the greatness of the sky.'

,I, I see!'

Sensing that the atmosphere wasn't good, Hermes acted quickly. He flew to Olympus with Persephone without looking back.

I knew the story of Hades and Persephone as well as it was famous.

But I didn't think much about it.

Because the current mythology is very different from the original mythology. The fact that Athena doesn't exist among the 12 Olympians says it all.

It's different from the original mythology. So, what should happen doesn't happen, and what shouldn't happen happens.

Poseidon falling in love with Medusa was something that should have happened originally. Originally, Medusa has sex with Poseidon in Athena's temple and becomes a monster after receiving Athena's wrath. But even if Athena isn't there, Poseidon is, so it was natural for them to love each other.

,I can't give a beauty like Medusa to Poseidon.'

So I made Medusa my woman. Poseidon's backlash? Who cares.

,The affair between Hades and Persephone is something that shouldn't happen.'

Because Persephone became my woman as soon as she was born.

Hades is gloomy, but he was the most sensible of the Olympian gods. So I thought he wouldn't touch Persephone. Persephone was my wife.

,That's what I thought.'

Hades was a madman.

He dared to touch my woman.

,I can't just let this go. I have to set a clear example. So that the Underworld can't crawl back to the surface again.'

As a bonus, there was also the purpose of venting my anger.

A little curiosity about the Underworld is a bonus.

,First of all....'

I saw the lesser gods looking this way. The gods who follow Hades in the Underworld.

,Zeus-isiyeo, please forgive us...!'

,We were wrong!'

,We will start working right away! We will try to reduce the chaos between the Underworld and the surface as soon as possible!'

Tsujeujeujeut.

Lightning strikes around me just by harboring a little murderous intent. This is both a power and a natural phenomenon. Zeus is lightning itself.

,If you have committed a sin, you must be punished.'

I grab the lightning and swing it around.

The lightning stretched out in all directions through the air and swept away the gods. The gods all fell to the floor. The lightning didn't disappear and remained in their bodies. Since they don't die, it will torment them for a long time.

,That motherfucker! Touching my woman?!'

I swung my fist.

Kwahahahahaang!

The wall breaks and the shockwave shatters the decorations. I destroyed everything that looked intact, whether it was a pillar or anything else. The more I rampaged, the more the Underworld wailed.

,Hmm?'

I realized something was off while punching.

My physical abilities have become a little stronger. I was able to notice it because I kept punching.

,Is Zeus's body growing even at this moment?'

I shook my head.

Even if it's Zeus, there's no way he can suddenly grow. There were several ways for the gods' power to become stronger. Innately, it's inheriting fate, and acquired, it's faith. It's not for nothing that the gods want human worship. They receive worship because it all helps.

,Worship is reverence.'

In conclusion, the number of those who revere me suddenly increased.

I glanced back. The gods of the Underworld, groaning in pain from the residual lightning. They were revering me.

,The fear of the gods makes me stronger... It must be possible because I am the king of the gods.'

Kwahang! Bang!

I smash everything and move forward.

Lava gushed out as the wall broke and covered me, but it didn't matter. I was too strong now to be fatally injured by lava. I wouldn't mind swimming in lava.

Kwahahahahaaaaang!

A huge door flies away and crashes somewhere.

,Keureureureu... Kking... Kkingkking....'

A huge black dog with three heads bared its teeth at me, then was startled and cowered.

Cerberus. The gatekeeper of hell buried his three heads on the floor and trembled pitifully.

I approached and punched one of its heads.

One of the huge heads exploded in an instant. The broken skull and brain poured onto the floor. Cerberus, horrified, tried to get up and run away. My fist was faster. I punched and shattered another head.

Cerberus, with only the middle head left, was in a panic and didn't know what to do.

,Since it also has the symbolism of being the gatekeeper of hell... I'll stop here. But this is the last time I'm letting you off.'

,Kkiing.... Kking....'

There is no next time.

This was also a warning to Hades.

After passing Cerberus, a river appeared and blocked my path.

,Is this the famous River of the Underworld?'
