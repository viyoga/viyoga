I could see a small city up ahead.

Trampling the grass as I moved forward, I stopped. I took out a scroll from my pocket and unfurled it, checking the map to confirm the city's features.

,It seems I've come to the right place, judging by the river flowing west of the city.'

The third test of the Demonic Cult's Induction Hall. The content was to carry out a personal mission. The inductees had to complete their assigned missions within a certain period and return to the Induction Hall. Failure to return within the time limit or failure to complete the mission would result in demotion or expulsion.

Missions were assigned according to the inductees' performance. The easiest missions were errands, like delivering items. These missions were less dangerous and more about being time-sensitive. They were relatively simple, but required diligent movement, even sacrificing sleep.

The mission I received was to kill a guest named Chu Jeo of the Seon Family Estate (先家莊) in Hagasi (河歌市), who could be considered the de facto owner of the city.

Chu Jeo, a guest who had been under the Seon Family's care for two years, was actually a traitor to the Demonic Cult. He abandoned his comrades during a battle with the Cheonui Alliance. He changed his face and went into hiding, but the Demonic Cult's professional trackers found him.

Chu Jeo's level was Stage 2 of Emerging Earth. It was a level too ambiguous for the Demonic Cult's combat unit to bother killing him, so it was assigned as my mission.

,The original story has changed a bit. Originally, Yeom Guseok was tasked with finding and killing an assassin operating near the Demonic Cult.'

It seemed my skills were better than Yeom Guseok's in the original story, so the mission difficulty was raised. For the same reason, Yeon Yeoha's mission was also changed from the original. She would probably be subjugating monsters near the Demonic Cult right now.

,The situation isn't bad. No, it's actually good.'

Because Hagasi is related to the missions of Cheon Yu-un, Jegal Mosun, and Seomun Soryeo.

After reconfirming the mission details written on the scroll, I summoned lightning. Pajijik. Blue currents flowed through the scroll. The currents soon turned into flames, burning the scroll. I shook off the ashes from my palm and headed towards the city.

Five martial artists were guarding the city entrance. The character Seon (先) was emblazoned on the chest of their navy blue martial arts uniforms, indicating they belonged to the Seon Family Estate.

They were making a list of people entering and leaving the city at the entrance. This also meant that the city was being thoroughly managed.

I had no intention of causing a disturbance, so I quietly lined up and waited for my turn. After about an hour, the merchant in front of me entered the city, and it was my turn.

,You're a martial artist, I see. Where are you from?'

A neatly dressed warrior approached. He had a friendly-looking impression. Judging by the energy I felt, he was around Stage 6 of Foundation Establishment. He seemed young, so he was quite skilled.

,Do I have to say?'

,Please understand. We've been keeping a register for 20 years.'

,Did something big happen 20 years ago?'

It's a shameful event for our Seon Family, but I'll tell you since it's already well known. Muyeong Shintu (無影神偸). I'm sure the young hero has heard of that name.

,Of course, I know it.'

I also knew that face.

,Our Seon Family was targeted by Muyeong Shintu 20 years ago. Since then, we've been thoroughly managing the entry and exit list.'

,I heard that Muyeong Shintu has retired.'

,A new Muyeong Shintu will appear soon. That's always been the case in the Jianghu.'

The Jianghu has a long history. To trace the origins of the Jianghu, you'd have to go back tens of thousands of years.

What's funny is that even after tens of thousands of years, there's been almost no development in civilization. Revolutions don't happen, and science and technology are still looked down upon. The Jianghu just keeps repeating itself.

The title Muyeong Shintu is given to the best thief of the era. If a thief who steals from the Emperor's vault appears again, he will become the new Muyeong Shintu.

,Do you really think you can stop Muyeong Shintu by checking these entry and exit lists?'

,It'll be hard to stop him. But this list is surprisingly helpful. Recently, we caught a merchant who embezzled from a trading company and a guy who tried to smuggle goods, thanks to this list. Besides that... well, it helps in various ways.'

,...I see.'

That's when it happened.

An dissatisfied voice popped out from behind.

,Hey, hurry up and go in! Can't you see people waiting behind you?!'

It was a middle-aged merchant. He was burly for an ordinary merchant, and there was a scar on his face. Stage 3 of Foundation Establishment. A merchant who had learned martial arts.

,Are you talking to me?'

The guy glared at me.

,Who else is here besides you?'

,I challenge you to a deathmatch.'

,.......'

Everything became quiet.

He probably didn't expect me to suddenly challenge him to a duel. The merchant scanned my behavior and frowned.

,Are you kidding me? I'm a merchant. I used to practice martial arts, but I've already quit.'

,So you're not a martial artist? If you've learned martial arts, you're a martial artist. The sword hanging on your waist is proof of that. Draw your sword.'

,Why should I duel with you?! I'm telling you again, I'm not a martial artist!'

,I refuse your refusal to duel.'

I reached for the sword hanging on my waist. The merchant's face twisted. He grabbed the hilt of his sword as if he had no choice.

,This crazy bastard! Alright! Let's dance with swords for the first time in a while!'

The merchant shouted and unleashed his energy.

No one could stop me and the merchant. Even the Seon Family Estate's martial artists guarding the city entrance were just watching the situation.

Because the merchant had accepted the duel. Duels in this world were similar to duels in the Middle Ages. And disputes between martial artists were common in the Jianghu.

That stupid merchant had misjudged my skills. He guessed that I was in the early stages of Foundation Establishment. Hiding my skills helped in times like this.

,Good! I'll go back to my old self today and feed your blood to my beloved sword!'

The merchant shouted. It was an outcry to be conscious of the surroundings. While everyone was watching with interest, I quietly drew my sword.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 6]

Slaying Demon Sword Art - One Wind Reversal.

I became the wind and brushed past the merchant. A beat later, a red line appeared on the merchant's neck, and his body and head fell to the ground.

A duel that ended absurdly easily.

The spectators didn't boo me. Instead, they looked at me with awe. If this were modern times, they would have screamed, called the police, and made a fuss, but this isn't modern times.

,...Amazing,'

The Seon Family Estate's martial artist said, seemingly genuinely impressed.

,It's nothing special.'

,I'm not just saying that. It's embarrassing to say, but I couldn't even see young hero's... no, great hero's movements. Great hero's slash was truly like lightning.'

,Thanks for the compliment. Can I ask you to take care of the cleanup? In return, you can take the wealth that guy had.'

,You've even transcended greed. We'll take care of the cleanup. Great hero. What's your name and where are you from?'

I paused for a moment.

Was it okay to reveal my name? I reconfirmed the mission. There were no conditions that I had to hide my identity.

,My name is Yeom Guseok. I came from the Demonic Cult.'

,...The Demonic Cult, you say. Indeed, I can't help but believe it after seeing that skill. Great hero, what's your purpose in coming to Hagasi?'

,Do I have to say that too?'

,I apologize if I've offended you. But this is also part of my job...'

,It's a trip. I heard there are many beautiful courtesans in Hagasi.'

,Yes. The courtesans of Hagasi are indeed outstanding. Especially the boat rides with the courtesans on the river... it's truly like playing with the immortals.'

Hearing that, I licked my lips.

Taking a boat ride with top-notch courtesans on the river? I can't miss this. Time for a change of plans. I'll enjoy the boat ride today and carry out the mission sometime tomorrow afternoon.

,Can I go in now?'

,Yes. Originally, we should collect a toll...'

He looked at the dead merchant. His colleagues were rummaging through the dead merchant's belongings. One colleague was holding a heavy money pouch and grinning.

,Since we've already received enough, you can just go in.'

,I see. Good work.'

,Ah, great hero. What's your title?'

,I don't have a title.'

,Then can I give you one? Many people have witnessed great hero's skills, so a title will naturally be given. That's why I want to give great hero a title first.'

,...Do you have a good title in mind?'

, Lightning Blade (閃電刀). The great hero will now be called Lightning Blade.

,That's a title I've heard a lot.'

If he had given me a shitty title, I would have challenged him to a duel, but it was a title I liked, so I quietly walked into the city.

He didn't just let me go. He ran up to me and said in a small voice.

Great hero. Go to the building over there by the river. It's Boyiru (甫伊樓), the finest courtesan house in Hagasi. It's so expensive that ordinary people can't even set foot inside, but the courtesans of Boyiru are truly exceptional. They are both beautiful and talented, and they know how to have a wild time.

,Thanks.'

That was very good information. My heart swelled with anticipation for Boyiru. I immediately headed towards the place he pointed to.

•••

I spent the whole night playing with Boyiru's top courtesans on the river and fell asleep at dawn. It was noon when I finally opened my eyes, looking around with drowsy eyes.

Food, alcohol, and naked courtesans were scattered around. My Semen was smeared all over the bodies of the five courtesans.

,It's indeed the best courtesan house in the city. It was a night without regrets.'

I had the boatman take me back to the city.

People related to Boyiru were waiting on land. Judging by their burly appearances, they seemed to be a black-hearted gang associated with Boyiru. As soon as they saw me, they immediately bent over and greeted me.

,Did you have a pleasant night?'

,I was satisfied.'

I threw them a money pouch. I had already paid in advance, and this was a kind of tip.

,Thank you, great hero! Please use our Boyiru again in the future!'

,Ah, I'm not sure if I should say this, but are you thinking of changing the name?'

,Yes?'

,Boyiru reminds me of homosexuality. It would be better to call it Girlru.'

,That's...'

,Don't you like the name? If not Girlru, then Mollu would be good too. Doesn't it sound nice?'

,There are some problems with changing the name right now... It's not that I'm ignoring great hero's opinion. I'll suggest great hero's opinion to the madam later.'

,One last question. What's the fastest way to get to the Seon Family Estate?'

,If you go to the city center through the street you see over there. The largest and most splendid place in Hagasi is the Seon Family Estate.'

I walked along the street he pointed to.
