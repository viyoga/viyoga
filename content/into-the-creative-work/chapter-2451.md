I smashed through everything blocking me and descended underground.

There were some slightly dangerous things. Like a giant spider doll with unusually high magic resistance, or a Death Knight made of dolls. Especially annoying was the Death Knight armed with magic armor and a magic sword.

,If I didn't have Vajra Trust and Astral Vision... I might have struggled quite a bit.'

Anyway, I was the one who succeeded, and I arrived at the very bottom floor.

What's blocking me now is a large iron door. I can feel magical power from the door.

[Electric Detection]

A search magic I used in front of the door. But I couldn't get any meaningful information. The iron door is blocking the search magic.

,Is it a trap? Should I smash it?'

Pajijik.

Lightning sparked from the Vajra Trust I was holding. The original 1-hour rental had already passed, and this is the 2nd hour. I rented Vajra Trust twice today alone.

,If I add up yesterday and today, I've spent a total of 450 million credits. If the Vision magic I get from this is trash, I'm going to be very angry.'

Just as I was about to throw Vajra Trust at the iron door.

Geureureureureuk.

The iron door opened on its own.

,Don't break the innocent door and come inside. There are no traps or anything.'

I went inside.

Magicians wearing black robes were lined up.

I was startled because I didn't feel any presence, but I soon realized that they were dolls, not people.

My gaze turned to the man sitting at the workbench at the end of the room, fiddling with a doll. He was a young man wearing work clothes and had blue glowing eyes.

,Are you Ilario Gomez?'

,That's right. To be exact, I'm the last doll that Ilario Gomez modeled after himself and put his copied thoughts into.'

,Are you going to attack?'

,No. Now that you've come this far, it's my defeat. The workshop is already more than 80% destroyed... and dolls aren't exactly infinite. Congratulations, intruder. You've won.'

,You're coming out cleaner than I thought. My purpose is Ilario's Vision magic. Where is it?'

,Don't rush so much. The Vision magic is here.'

He took a book out of the workbench drawer. It was a thick book with no particular decorations.

,You're being compliant. You should have done this sooner.'

,Haha. I can't just hand over Vision magic to anyone. You've proven your strength and talent by coming this far. That's why you're qualified to get this. Ilario wanted to leave behind his Vision magic, but he didn't want it to go to just anyone.'

[Telekinesis]

The Vision magic book on the desk floated into the air and slowly flew towards me. Before opening the magic book, I asked Ilario.

,How did you know my name when I never taught it to you?'

He raised his hand and pointed to his eyes. Blue glowing eyes.

,I can share my vision with the dolls. And these eyes are the eyes of a shaman who can see through names.'

,Is it a Mystic Eye?'

,No. It's a Spirit Eye. Black magicians, especially necromancers, prefer Spirit Eyes over Mystic Eyes. Because ordinary Mystic Eyes can't see souls.'

,Do those eyes let you see souls?'

,They let you see.'

,What does my soul look like?'

,Unfortunately, I can't see your soul. There's a difference in class. It's amazing that you haven't even transcended.'

Should I smash him and take his eyes?

While I was contemplating, I noticed cracks forming on Ilario's face. He was breaking down. At the same time, I can feel magical power from the book.

,Nothing in the world is permanent. Even dolls eventually break. That book is also the center of this workshop. Now that the core of the workshop is in someone else's hands, it's natural for it to collapse.'

,Too bad.'

,If you really think so... why don't you give it back?'

,.......'

There's no way I'd give it back. I didn't care if this workshop collapsed or if he died. My goal was Vision magic.

,It would be nice if I could take a few dolls to study... but it doesn't matter. If I have enough money, I can buy dolls from famous doll makers.'

To be honest, the dolls here weren't special. Rather, the dolls themselves are almost old-fashioned.

,The real important thing isn't the dolls.'

What makes the dolls move.

I opened the Vision magic book. It was over 500 pages long. Most of it was like an experiment log. I checked the really important magic formula.

,Necroanima... Is this magic really made by humans?'

,Haha. That's right. It's bound to be suspicious. It's such an evil and alien Vision magic to be made by humans.'

Necroanima is a magic that steals the soul of the person you killed and traps it in a core. From my point of view, it's closer to a technique.

The core is basically made from the target's bones, flesh, and organs. Additionally, using special metals can improve the performance of the core. The higher the level of the target, the higher the probability of failure.

,......Is this Vision magic really made by Ilario alone? If so, Ilario would have been a genius of the century.'

,Ilario made a contract with a demon. Necroanima was completed with the help of the demon. In other words, that Vision magic is something created in collaboration with a demon. Thanks to that, rumors even spread that he was using demons. How is it? Can you understand the formula?'

,It's difficult, but it's not something I can't understand. And... Ilario didn't just use Necroanima.'

,That's right. He used it with some modifications. If you make a soul core and put it in a doll... the doll becomes a moving undead. The problem is that the ego is too strong and doesn't listen to the master.'

,......So you go through the process of copying the soul core. You used the power of a doppelganger?'

,That's right. But the soul core copied with the power of a doppelganger is far from the real thing. In the end, Ilario failed. Even I, the last one Ilario made before he died, am far from the real Ilario.'

,Did Ilario try to gain immortality by becoming a doll? Even if he failed... he's a necromancer.'

,It's not for such a great reason. Ilario... is just a pervert. The only reason he reached out to necromancy was to create the perfect doll.'

,...That's why he became a necromancer?'

,You don't believe it, do you? But it's true. That's why he summoned a demon and made a contract. Rather, the demon liked Ilario like that.'

,Aren't you like Ilario's alter ego? Why don't you understand Ilario?'

,Because I'm a failure.'

,......Why were you stuck in the workshop? It doesn't seem like anything is binding you.'

,Some dolls, including me, left the workshop. I don't know if they're doing well.... I couldn't leave. The dolls that remain here will break and disappear without me, without that Vision magic book. Of course, I didn't make them, Ilario made them when he was alive, but... I'm Ilario.'

,...So you're a pervert too. Are the magician dolls here... unfinished?'

,I tried to make them into dolls by following Ilario. But I'm not the real Ilario, so it was impossible. All I can do is fix the dolls I've already made. Well, do you have any more questions?'

,How do I get out?'

,You need to get access to this workshop. Drop a drop of blood on the book. Then you'll get ownership of the book and naturally get access.'

,So it was a kind of grimoire.'

,It's a book containing Vision magic created by a demon and a black magician together. It would be stranger if it were ordinary.'

I hesitated for a moment.

What if this is a trap? What if a curse comes down when I drop blood on the book?

There's nothing more foolish than treating a grimoire carelessly. There's a reason why it's called a grimoire.

,I can handle it.'

I cut the tip of my finger with wind magic and dropped a drop of blood on the grimoire. The grimoire twitched. There was no other reaction. This grimoire became mine.

,Necroanima. You can turn souls into cores and use them as dolls. The essence isn't about using them as dolls. It's about storing souls in the form of cores.'

Soul storage.

That in itself is a great help to me.

,I can store the souls of those I'm not angry enough to kill and torment them forever. It will also be convenient when using human sacrifice magic.'

There's no immediate need to use human sacrifice magic, but you never know what the future holds.

,There are quite a few black magic spells that use souls.'

Necromancy itself is a magic that deals with the corpses and souls of the dead.

I put the Necroanima grimoire into subspace and looked at Ilario. Now that I'm the owner of the grimoire, I can tell. I can control him.

,I can take you outside if you want.'

,No, thanks. I'm getting tired of it. I'd rather break down here.'

,Is that so.'

I turned my back on him. The space distorted and an exit appeared.

,Goodbye.'

Ilario greets me casually.

I stopped as I was about to leave and asked him.

,But isn't there not much difference between dolls and machines? It seems like it would be even more perfect if you replaced the soul with AI.'

,You crazy bastard! How dare you compare machines to dolls?! Artificial intelligence can't replace the soul! You, don't tell me. Are you a dirty mechanophile?!'

Hating machines, especially AI, was a characteristic of Neo London citizens. In that sense, Ilario was definitely born in Neo London.

,.......'

,Hey, come here! Come here! You mechanophile bastard!!'

I ignored Ilario's outburst and went outside.
