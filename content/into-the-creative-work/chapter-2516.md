As the elevator began its descent to the first floor, we readied our guns, prepared to fire. We planned to blast our way out the moment the doors opened.

"How many are inside?"

I asked Yu Jinmyeong-ssi.

"There's a high chance there's no one. Or it might be carrying something not human. Either way, it's best not to let our guard down, Do-hyeon-ssi. If there are more than 10 people, blow up the elevator immediately."

"...Got it."

Shin Do-hyeon carefully fiddled with the detonator.

Ting.

The elevator doors opened on the first floor.

Inside was a man with a gun. My team and I reflexively pulled the triggers. The bullets passed through the man's body.

Not piercing, but passing through. The man was an illusion, a fake.

"Hmm. We've been had. If the elevator goes back up, the enemies will know we're armed with guns."

"Does it matter?"

"Paeng Cheonsu will be more cautious. The chances of the enemies coming down in the elevator all at once... are almost nonexistent. If they come down, they'll use the stairs. We should move some dynamite and set it up near the stairs."

"When do you think they'll come down?"

"Today or tomorrow, or maybe in 3-4 days. Food is one thing, but we're running out of water, making it hard to hold out."

"They played with us, so how about we send them a gift?"

We took the corpses, put them in the elevator, and sent it up. A little taunt, and we got rid of the bodies. Killing two birds with one stone.

• • •

The elevator we sent down to the first floor started to come back up.

Paeng Cheonsu, with his subordinates in front, glared at the elevator. He had sent down an illusionary clone in the elevator using a subordinate's ability, to see the reaction on the first floor. But the enemies, instead of trying to communicate via radio, quietly sent the elevator back up.

,So they have no intention of talking. Troublesome.'

He ignored Park Taesan, who was huffing and puffing on one side. Park Taesan was a Grand Game winner, but he wasn't actually helpful. Park Taesan was able to win because of his dice and intuition abilities. But for some reason, the dice weren't working.

,There's no one in the elevator. They wouldn't be crazy enough to come up in the elevator.'

Ting.

The elevator came all the way up, and the doors opened.

As Paeng Cheonsu expected, there was no one. Instead, there were three corpses. He also found bullet marks etched like scars on the elevator walls. Looking down, he could see bullets rolling around on the elevator floor.

Paeng Cheonsu gritted his teeth inwardly.

,Those bastards.... They're armed with guns. How they got the guns is secondary... what's important is the number of enemies. They must have unloaded as soon as they saw the illusionary clone.... Judging by those marks, there must be at least 3 or more of them. And the corpses are....'

The corpses weren't killed by guns, but by sharp blades. One was killed by a knife.

,The 6 guarding below have decent combat skills. The fact that they were wiped out without even reporting to me... means the enemies are quite strong.'

Paeng Cheonsu first assessed the situation. There were a total of 17 people here, including himself. 7 of them were his subordinates. There used to be more, but they hadn't joined. They were either dead, couldn't come due to circumstances, or, in the worst case, had betrayed him.

,The atmosphere isn't bad. Well, if they were going to freak out over corpses, they wouldn't have even participated in the Grand Game.'

Still, he was tense. Because of the bullets on the elevator floor. As modern people, everyone knew the terror of guns.

,The only ones with guns here are me, Jichan, and Park Taesan.'

Pistols. They were his subordinates' game tools, but they decided to use them. Park Taesan was being a pain in the ass, so he had no choice but to give him one. Even if nothing else, Park Taesan's intuition was helpful.

"Chairman-nim. What do we do with the elevator? Do we send it back down as is?"

"Send it down. I don't want to smell the corpses."

The elevator sent down came back up again. The elevator still contained the corpses. If they sent it down again, it would come back up again. Not good. His subordinates were getting tense every time the elevator came up. The corpses in the elevator were slowly but surely messing with his subordinates' mental state.

"Tsk. Get rid of the corpses. There are plenty of rooms anyway, so put them in an unused one. And wedge some furniture in the elevator so it doesn't go down."

"Yes, Chairman-nim."

"What about the food and water? You brought it 5 hours ago. How much is left?"

"...Only enough for today."

"If we ration it, can we last 3 days?"

"That's the rationed amount for today."

"Damn it."

At this rate, they might really have to survive by drinking piss, urine.

"But we have the scouting party that went outside. The scouting party might bring back other participants and kill those guys. The scouting party will also bring back the supply boxes."

"...That's right."

But if they did that, they were more likely to be ambushed. The scouting party was made up of disposable pawns. Of course, Paeng Cheonsu didn't say that.

Bbi-bbik.

The radar next to him reacted. He picked up the radar he had obtained in this tower.

「Supply Radar Tells you the location of supply boxes containing food and water.」

The radar showed the location of 7 supply boxes. It seemed like 3 new supply boxes had appeared this time. The problem was that they couldn't go outside because of the guys occupying the first floor.

He had to make a decision.

Go down the stairs or elevator and fight those guys, or hold out here.

,Fighting is a burden... and holding out relies too much on luck.'

If they were lucky, a monster might invade the tower and wipe out the guys on the first floor. If they were really lucky.

,In times like these... I need to look to Park Taesan.'

Park Taesan, with his intuition ability. His ability would definitely be helpful.

"Park Taesan."

"What."

The response was sharp. A sign that he was mentally cornered.

"What do you think? Should we go down and fight? Or should we hold out?"

"......"

Park Taesan was silent for a moment, then said.

"We hold out for now."

Paeng Cheonsu used his ability without hesitation. He could see Park Taesan's thoughts.

&lt;Shit. I have a bad feeling. Going down and holding out are both bad. Both are bad.... Damn it. What should I do? At this rate....&gt;

Going down and holding out. Both had similarly bad outcomes. Paeng Cheonsu narrowed his eyes.

,Park Taesan's ability only activates for things related to himself. For example, with stocks, his intuition only activates for stocks he's invested in. It doesn't activate for stocks he hasn't invested in.'

Paeng Cheonsu's mind started racing.

The representative on the first floor is Seong Yujin. Park Taesan's old friend. That bastard definitely holds a grudge against Park Taesan.

,Try to negotiate with Park Taesan.'

Paeng Cheonsu looked around and picked out the most useless guy he could find. A man from outside.

• • •

I killed 5 participants approaching the tower. I captured one alive and interrogated him, and he turned out to be Paeng Cheonsu's subordinate. He said they were a scouting party of 3. 2 were participants who had chosen to join. The scouting party had supply boxes with food and water, so we ate well.

Then we set up a night watch and went to sleep.

"I, I'll take the night watch. It's the least I can do."

Bae Hyeonsu said something sensible for once. Even if he was a little unreliable, it would be okay since he had the map. But Yu Jinmyeong-ssi shook his head.

"Night watch in pairs. Hyeonsu and I, Do-hyeon-ssi and Nayoung-ssi will take turns. Even with Hyeonsu's map, it's too difficult alone."

The team members accepted without complaint. The reason I was excluded from the night watch was because of combat. The team members wanted me to maintain my condition.

I lay down on the floor and slept.

"Yujin-ssi. Wake up for a moment. The elevator that was stopped upstairs has started moving. Use your Advice to see if those guys are coming down."

"...Do I really have to use Advice?"

"Yes. The elevator coming down now... might be to attack us. If it's Paeng Cheonsu, he'll split his people between the stairs and the elevator."

If they decided to attack, they would attack fiercely before it got any later. That was right. The more time passed, the more they would be exhausted from the lack of food and water.

I used Advice.

&lt;The person coming down in the elevator is Jo Sukyeong. He is Paeng Cheonsu's subordinate, and Paeng Cheonsu wants to negotiate.&gt;

Advice that tells me even what I didn't ask. I like it because it's convenient.

"Negotiation.... I see. Paeng Cheonsu will try to negotiate with Park Taesan."

"You thought of that right away? You're smart."

"Paeng Cheonsu has no one to negotiate with except Park Taesan. What are you going to do?"

"What's your opinion?"

"I would listen to the negotiation and then betray him. We are in an overwhelmingly advantageous position to do so. Another method would be... to announce the contents of the negotiation over the radio. We can drive a wedge between Paeng Cheonsu and Park Taesan. However, in that case, Park Taesan will be killed by Paeng Cheonsu."

"I'll kill Park Taesan with my own hands. Paeng Cheonsu too. No negotiations. As soon as Paeng Cheonsu's subordinate comes down to the first floor, kill him and send him back up."

"Yes. I understand."

"...You're not stopping me. I thought you would."

"Yujin-ssi is the leader. I will follow the leader's wishes. Honestly, with Yujin-ssi here, I don't think we'll lose even if we fight head-on."

Ting.

The doors of the elevator that came down opened.

A man stood in the blood-soaked elevator with his hands raised. I aimed my pistol at him.

"I don't want to fight. I am Jo Sukyeong, and I came down under the Chairman's orders. To negotiate."

Tang.

The fired bullet pierced Jo Sukyeong's forehead.

Yu Jinmyeong-ssi sent the elevator back up.
