Sosanhyeongeom Im Sugung's expression hardened as he watched Seong Yujin fall into a state of mushin during the battle.

,To be in mushin during a life-or-death struggle! There's only one explanation for this. Has he attained enlightenment at the crossroads of life and death...!'

Originally, mushin is like dancing on glass.

Even if someone just calls out his name, the mushin will break and the enlightenment will vanish. There's a reason why comrades and subordinates stand guard.

Being alone was the best way to fully grasp that enlightenment.

,That Little Heavenly Demon has unbelievable talent and abilities. He fought me on equal terms even without reaching the realm of harmony.'

It was obvious what would happen if he reached the Harmony Realm.

Ilhwijoje (One Stroke Creates the World).

Im Sugung swung his sword. The energy flowing from the tip of his sword became black ink, embodying the world. His sword energy became mountains and rivers, falling upon Seong Yujin.

Black thunder erupted from Seong Yujin's body, who was in a state of mushin. That mixture of lightning and demonic energy was incomparably faster and more destructive than before. As the spiritual energy of heaven and earth resonated with it, black lightning writhed in the sky and on the ground.

Seong Yujin stepped on the ground. The river evaporated in the black lightning.

Seong Yujin swung his sword. The mountain was torn apart by the black lightning.

Im Sugung realized it belatedly.

,So that's it. He had already attained enlightenment before falling into mushin. This is just the process of turning the spiritual energy of heaven and earth into a physical incarnation.'

His body was already prepared, but to gain enlightenment and break through to a higher realm during battle...

,Even looking at it again, his talent is unbelievable.'

Seong Yujin eliminated the distinction between the world and himself, becoming an incarnation.

Im Sugung knew that he would be defeated today. Instead of retreating, he raised his Hyeongeom. Ink flowed from his body.

,I thought I would be overflowing with power if I reached the realm of Harmony.'
