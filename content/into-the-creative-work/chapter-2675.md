Atlantis was peaceful as always today.

The curse of the volcano island covered Atlantis, but the damage was much less than in the original story.

This was because Seong Yujin's intervention had provided some preparation. Moreover, the conquest of the volcano island was slow, but it was progressing steadily.

Eventually, the volcano island would be conquered, and this curse would disappear. The residents of Atlantis believed this without a doubt.

However, an anomaly occurred throughout Atlantis.

Snow began to fall from the sky. At first, the Empire didn't realize this was an anomaly. The Empire was currently in winter, and it was natural for snow to fall in winter.

However, the snow didn't just fall on the Empire.

Snow fell even in the deserts where the sun's power was strong.

Snow began to fall in areas where it shouldn't have snowed.

When everyone realized the anomaly and was pondering the cause, a notification window popped up.

「Area 8,840, Winter Castle has awakened.」

「Until Winter Castle is conquered, snow will fall and the temperature will drop throughout Atlantis.」

It's natural for snow to fall in winter. The world won't end just because it snows a little. That's what the morons of the Empire thought.

But those with a bit of sense knew how serious this situation was.

,Your Majesty! We must conquer Winter Castle immediately! The snow is not the problem! The real problem is that the temperature is dropping rapidly!

,At this rate, the Great Farms will also suffer significant damage. Even if the mages are protecting the Great Farms from volcanic ash, maintaining the temperature of the vast Great Farms at an appropriate level would require at least four times more mages than we have now.

,If we do nothing, the future of Atlantis will be one of two things: either freeze to death or starve to death.

The Emperor swallowed hard at the words of his capable retainers.

Area 3,975, the Great Farms

This area, directly under the Imperial family, supplies 30% of the entire food supply for the Atlantis continent. A blessed land where crops can be harvested. A place that somehow managed to survive even during the curse of the volcano island. But due to this incident, the Great Farms were also about to take a hit.

,If the Great Farms are in this state, the other farms are beyond saving. The economy is going to completely collapse. No, survival is the problem, even before the economy.

The Emperor had a prophecy stone tablet that foretold the future, but it hadn't foreseen this incident. If he had known, he could have prepared more thoroughly.

The Emperor paused for a moment and then commanded.

,Information is needed before the attack. Dispatch a reconnaissance team to Winter Castle.

An elite reconnaissance team consisting of knights was dispatched to Winter Castle.

And the next day, the Emperor received a report of the reconnaissance team's annihilation.

The Emperor's eyes sank deeply.

,Inform the nobles and, first, use the exiles to conquer Winter Castle.

If even the exiles fail to conquer it, the Empire will have to step in.

Elena glared at the sky.

Snow was falling heavily from the gray clouds. At first glance, it might seem like a romantic sight. But those snowflakes contained a slight toxicity. This was because volcanic ash was mixed in the process of the snow condensing. Melting and drinking that snow would be insane, and even direct contact with the body was not good.

,Now even poison is falling from the sky.

The curse of Winter Castle was something that even Seong Yujin and Kang Myungjin hadn't anticipated. The curse of Winter Castle didn't even exist in the original story.

,I didn't expect the curse of Winter Castle at all. At this rate, my wedding will be ruined.

No matter what, that was something that absolutely could not happen.

If she waited for someone else to resolve the situation, she could suffer irreversible damage. Even now, crops were dying in real time.

The Emperor sent a reconnaissance team to Winter Castle, but news reached her ears that they had been annihilated.

It was obvious what kind of actions the Emperor would take afterward. He would try to mobilize the exiles in order to preserve the power of the Empire and the Imperial family as much as possible. Those exiles would also want to avoid a dog's death, so they would proceed with the conquest slowly.

Elena wanted to resolve this situation as quickly as possible.

,I'll have to step in directly this time.

Anything that interfered with the Valderth family's event was unacceptable.

Ttok, ttok.

Her feet headed somewhere. It was not the volcano island, at least. Rushing into enemy territory right away just because she was angry was not her style.

,What's important for the conquest is information. Winter Castle didn't appear in the original story, but the person who would know Winter Castle better than anyone else is....

There was only one person.

Kang Myungjin of the A-Plan Legion.

Elena visited the A-Plan Legion.

Kang Myungjin, the Legion Master, and I, the Sub-Master, met Elena in the reception room. Elena sat on the sofa chair and took a sip of black tea.

I had already heard her proposal.

The conquest of Winter Castle.

She wanted to conquer Winter Castle together with the A-Plan Legion.

I was surprised that she had come to me without even giving me a heads-up, and even more surprised that she was going to step in directly. Because if someone of Elena's position stepped in directly, it would often be more of a loss.

,The black tea tastes not bad.

I glanced at Kang Myungjin sitting next to me. Kang Myungjin didn't even touch the black tea and was lost in thought before speaking.

,It's black tea prepared for a precious guest. I'm glad you like it, Lady Illusion.

,So, will the A-Plan Legion accept my proposal? As I said, I will hand over the dominion of Winter Castle to the A-Plan Legion. I will also hand over the Winter Castle conquest reward promised by His Majesty the Emperor to the A-Plan Legion. All I want is to conquer Winter Castle as quickly as possible. That's all.

,Your Excellency. May I ask why you are in such a hurry to conquer Winter Castle?

,The Valderth family's event is about to be ruined. We must fix it before the damage to Atlantis gets any worse.

Both I and Kang Myungjin knew what that event was.

My and Elena's wedding. The remaining period until then was about 4 months.

The curse of Winter Castle could mess up the schedule. And if the schedule was messed up and the wedding was canceled... I didn't know how many months it would take to set the next date. Perhaps it would take years.

I predicted what kind of decision Kang Myungjin would make. The reward was a reward, but... Kang Myungjin wanted to conquer even one area for sure.

,If Lady Illusion is with us... the chances of success will be high enough. However, Winter Castle is a quite dangerous place. Individual strength is more important than the power of a group. We will select only a small number of people to conquer it.

,Do you know Winter Castle well?

,Yes. I have received a hint from the Contract God.

Elena and I knew that wasn't the case, but we didn't bother to dig into it.

,When will the attack begin?

,We will start tomorrow.

,...You're starting sooner than I thought. I thought you'd need at least 3 days.

,What else would we need when Lady Illusion, of all people, is with us?

I quietly nodded.

Empire Ogong. One of the five pillars of the Empire. Elena was one of the top powerhouses in Atlantis.

,Then let's meet tomorrow.

Before leaving, Elena stared intently into my eyes.

The next day.

There were a total of 5 of us.

Me, Kang Myungjin, Rilsne, Joo Seo-hyun, Elena.

To be exact, it could be said that there were 6 of us. Because the Azure Dragon Biya was clinging to Kang Myungjin. Even now, she was peeking out from Kang Myungjin's arms. She was not in human form, but in dragon form. She was small in size, so she only looked like a snake. Even though she looked like that, a dragon was still a dragon, so she was stronger than most guys.

,Your Excellency. We have finished preparing.

,Then let's move.

「You have entered Area 8,840, Winter Castle.」

A snowstorm raged across the snowy field. At the center of it was a frozen castle.

,I wanted to move directly into the castle, but... the system blocked it. What should we do now?

Elena looked at Kang Myungjin and asked. Kang Myungjin hadn't said anything about the strategy until now. The reason was that if he said the strategy in advance, it could change.

,Survive and find the mirror. The real thing is in the mirror. If even one person finds the mirror... we will meet again.

Brain Thunder Style Electromagnetic Waves.
