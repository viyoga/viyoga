I stared at the main gate of the Seon Clan.

Perhaps because it was the middle of the day with the sun shining, the main gate was wide open. I could peek inside through the opening. The garden was clean, and the buildings were large and extravagant.

The gate was grandly open, but that didn't mean I could just waltz inside.

Two gatekeepers guarding the main gate were glaring at me with fierce eyes.

Ignoring them, I took a step forward. They immediately raised their spears, aiming them at me.

When I stepped back, they returned to their original motionless stance. They were very well-trained.

"......."

I took a step forward. They aimed their spears. I stepped back. They lowered their spears.

It was after repeating this about eight times. One of the gatekeepers shouted in an annoyed voice.

"Stop! Are you messing around right now?! If you have business, speak up!!"

"Don't shout. Before I kill you."

"......!"

I released my killing intent. Overwhelmed, they swallowed hard and took a fighting stance. Killing the gatekeepers was easy. After all, they were just low-level pawns for show.

'The problem is, the guys inside are hard to handle without the restrictions.'

The Demonic Cult would be watching my actions from the shadows. It was impossible to break the restrictions and charge in. If I did, everything I'd done so far would be for nothing.

"I came from the Demonic Cult."

The gatekeepers looked at each other and lowered their weapons. A bead of cold sweat on their foreheads ran down their cheeks.

"...What brings a Martial artist of the Demonic Cult to the Seon Clan?"

"I'm on a mission for the Cult. A mission to eliminate a traitor of the Cult. The Seon Clan should cooperate with the Cult."

"......We don't have the authority to do that. I'll report to the Clan Head-nim. Please wait a moment."

"I'll give you an il-da-gyeong's time. After that, I'll enter the Seon Clan by force."

One of the gatekeepers hurriedly ran inside the Seon Clan.

The Seon Clan was one of the forces cooperating with the Demonic Cult. This was because Hagasi was a city near the Demonic Cult.

'The Seon Clan can't ignore the Demonic Cult. Things will work out. I just need to kill Chu Jeo. He's about the same skill level as me, so will he try to run? If he runs, things will get annoying.'

I spread out my senses. If I felt anyone trying to escape from the Seon Clan, I would move immediately.

I didn't have to wait long. The gatekeeper who had gone inside came running back, panting, and said to me.

"The Clan Head-nim has formally invited you, Sohyeop! I will guide you to the reception room!"

Things were going well.

I confidently stepped into the Seon Clan.

In the reception room, I faced the Clan Head of the Seon Clan. He was a man who looked to be in his forties. He was neatly and calmly dressed. With thick eyebrows and a beard on his chin. He was exactly like a great hero from a martial arts novel.

"It's a pleasure to meet you. I am Seon Hae-do, the Clan Head of the Seon Clan."

Hakryeo Seon Sword (鶴麗先劍) Seon Hae-do.

That was the epithet Seon Hae-do possessed.

I am Yeom Gu-seok, the Lightning Blade from the Demonic Cult.

"Let's sit down and talk."

I plopped down in the chair he pointed to. There was a teacup on the table. Clean green tea steamed gently. I took a sip of the tea and said.

"The reason I came to the Seon Clan is as I told the gatekeepers. Chu Jeo. I know that a traitor of the Demonic Cult is staying as a guest in the Seon Clan. Bring him to me."

I openly exuded hostility.

Seon Hae-do didn't bat an eye, even though I mentioned the Demonic Cult.

"Chu Jeo is a traitor of the Demonic Cult... I can't believe it."

"Are you doubting the Demonic Cult?"

"No, don't misunderstand. Our Seon Clan has an alliance with the Demonic Cult. Of course, I trust the allied Demonic Cult. What I can't trust is you."

"That Demonic Cult gave me a mission. An order to kill the traitor and bring back his head. Seon Hae-do. If the Seon Clan is an ally of the Demonic Cult, then cooperate with me right now."

"Haha. You speak as if you are a representative of the Demonic Cult. Are you the Cheonma of the Demonic Cult? ...No, that's too blasphemous. Let me rephrase. Are you an Elder of the Demonic Cult?"

"......."

The atmosphere was strange. Seon Hae-do didn't seem to have any intention of cooperating with me. It seemed things weren't going well.

"You don't easily turn away guests of the Seon Clan."

"You said he's a traitor of the Demonic Cult."

"The Demonic Cult isn't the issue. I just can't trust your words. Bring me a formal request for cooperation from the Demonic Cult with the name of an Elder on it. Then I'll cooperate without question."

"......."

Go back to the Demonic Cult at this point? Even if I ran quickly using lightness skill, it would take at least four days. There wasn't nearly enough time. I couldn't use a space-time warp scroll for official business either. Even if I went back to the Demonic Cult, there was no chance the Cult would write a request for cooperation from an Elder that Seon Hae-do wanted.

"Around this time, the Demonic Cult's Ipmaso is conducting its third test."

"......You knew from the beginning."

"I have no doubt that you are a Martial artist of the Demonic Cult. But there's no reason I have to cooperate with you unconditionally."

There was no support from the Demonic Cult for my mission. I had to complete the mission with my own strength somehow. Persuading the Seon Clan was also my responsibility. Getting Seon Hae-do's cooperation to kill Chu Jeo was part of the mission.

"You should have been polite and requested cooperation from me from the start. Well, it's not too late now. Be polite. Kneel down and bow your head to me in apology. Then I'll consider your request."

It might have been faster to request cooperation with money.

I sighed.

Annoyance surged. Seon Hae-do's level was only Fifth Stage. He was weaker than me. If I released the restrictions and used my full power, I was confident I could kill him within fifty moves.

When I got up from my seat, Seon Hae-do's eyes sparkled with anticipation.

I grabbed the teacup on the table and threw it at the wall. Crash! The teacup shattered, and the tea trickled down the wall. I felt killing intent from Seon Hae-do.

I opened my mouth and spat out my voice along with my killing intent. Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art) stirred. Demonic energy was infused in my voice.

"You piece of shit. I'll come back later and destroy your tiny Seon Clan without leaving a single cornerstone. I'll rape and kill your wife, tear your children apart, and burn all your family members to death. The name Seon Clan will disappear from the Jianghu forever. You just wait for that time in regret."

Crack!

The teacup Seon Hae-do was holding shattered. He trembled. Anger? No. He was clearly feeling fear towards me. He bit his lip. Desperately hiding his fear and feigning anger.

"...Are you threatening the Seon Clan right now? Someone like you? Even if I kill you right now, the Demonic Cult won't hold me accountable for any crime!"

"Kill me if you can."

"......."

He shut his mouth tightly.

Not cooperating with me was a different issue than killing me, who was affiliated with the Demonic Cult. He probably thought there might be some backing behind my confident attitude.

I quietly gathered my Demonic energy. I checked the restrictions. The restrictions were intact and hadn't been released. Seeing Seon Hae-do still stiff and overwhelmed, I realized the power of Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art).

I went outside. I ran into a Martial artist of the Seon Clan. I just passed by. Even if I didn't like it, I couldn't cause a scene here. A full-scale war with the Seon Clan would be disadvantageous.

'Coming back at night to assassinate Chu Jeo... that seems difficult. The level of security will be greatly increased. Chu Jeo must have noticed my presence too, so he won't try to leave the Seon Clan....'

Coming out of the main gate, I turned back and looked at the Seon Clan. The extravagant building was very annoying. Things that could have ended easily with Seon Hae-do's cooperation had become twisted. Killing intent rose.

"......!!"

The two gatekeepers grabbed their weapons and glared fiercely.

"What are you looking at? Lower your eyes."

"......."

As I growled, operating my Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art), the gatekeepers immediately lowered their eyes.

"What's this... Keok!!"
