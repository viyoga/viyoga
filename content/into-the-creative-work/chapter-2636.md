After half-destroying the Red Pulsion Fortress, I decided to leave the battlefield immediately.

The reason I came to the battlefield was to become a Level 7 through the dungeon. Now that I've achieved that goal, there was no reason to stay.

Especially, it's dangerous for me to stay in the battlefield. As the Chairman-nim of LK Corp, I'm still being targeted by countries and companies around the world. The fact that I'm in the battlefield will soon be known, and countless people will come drooling. That's why I came here hiding my identity.

,There are many Level 7 or higher powerhouses in the battlefield. I need to leave before they start moving in earnest.'

Once I arrive in Neo London, I'll be safe. Other Level 7 or higher superhumans can't do anything to me in Neo London. Unless they're completely insane.

,I'll probably come to the battlefield again someday.'

A place where all kinds of resources exist infinitely. A place overflowing with dungeons and monsters, where you can obtain high-quality artifacts. In the original game, it's famous as a high-level area. Even if it's hard to do anything right now, the battlefield is a place overflowing with things to gain.

I turned my back on the battlefield and returned to Neo London. I used magic to fly through the sky and return to Neo London.

After entering Neo London, I bought a newspaper at a convenience store before returning to my mansion. It smelled of ink, as it was a fresh newspaper that came out this morning. I stood on the street, waiting for a carriage, and read the newspaper.

The headline was about me.

-LK Corp's CEO, Count Eugene Meyer! Rampaging in the Battlefield and Becoming a Superhuman?!

The information was leaked in just one day. It wasn't surprising, since contact with the battlefield is ongoing.

I read the article. Maybe it's because the parliament had a say, but there was no content criticizing me. Rather, they're praising me as a young genius.

,I did reach Level 7 at my age, so I guess I am a genius. In that sense, Yuria, who reached Level 7 even faster than me, is even more amazing.'

I couldn't find any information about Yuria. That's because the Maid Academy manages information thoroughly. The only times Yuria's name comes up is as a winner in famous competitions.

Dag-dak. Dag-dak.

A carriage approached. It was pulled by two real, living horses, not mechanical ones. A unicorn emblem was drawn at the entrance of the carriage.

"I'm James, a first-class coachman from Unicorn Company. Are you Count Eugene Meyer-kakha?"

I dispelled the illusion that was hiding my face. The coachman, who recognized my real face, bowed and greeted me.

"I apologize. There have been quite a few incidents of people impersonating celebrities, so I needed to confirm."

"I understand. The destination is the mansion. Do you need the address?"

"I know the address. I will serve you comfortably."

I got into the carriage. I could feel the magic plastered all over the inside of the carriage.

,Air circulation, shock absorption, internal mosaic, etc. Magic for convenience and security.'

Wouldn't it be better to just drive a car if you're going to do this?

I felt it was inefficient, but I let it go. It's not like Neo London's obsession with tradition is a new thing. Although that tradition is twisted in a slightly strange direction.

I opened the window inside the carriage. Since the inside can't be seen from the outside anyway, thanks to magic, I didn't hesitate.

Neo London's unique gloomy sky, the lights of neon signs filling the streets, the acrid air, the sound of gunfire coming from afar. Sometimes, when I blankly look at Neo London like this, I realize that I've entered the world of the game.

,In my past life... Damn it. Now that I'm trying to remember, I can't recall it well.'

I arrived at the mansion.

The cherry blossom tree, which maintains its blooming state all year round, greeted me.

"Welcome, Master-nim. I heard you have reached the realm of superhuman. Congratulations."

The butler I recently hired said.

"Thank you. Was there anything while I was away?"

"It was the same as usual. However, a large number of letters arrived yesterday. Most of them were sent by nobles, inviting Master-nim to social parties. There were also some business-related matters."

"Reject the social parties as always. Business-related matters? If it's a proper business matter, they would send it to the company, not the mansion. Most of them are scammers."

"Hmm. There's also a letter from someone who claims to be Master-nim's cousin. How should I handle this person?"

"How absurd. I'm an orphan. I don't have any family, let alone relatives. It's offensive, but he's too petty for me to deal with directly. File a complaint with the London Guard in my name."

When you become famous, all sorts of crazy people cling to you.

Maybe this guy is a lunatic who really thinks he's my cousin.

"Understood. Should I also reject the party invitation from the royal family?"

"The royal family? It's not the royal party season. Give me the invitation first. If an invitation came from the royal family, I should check it out."

Soon, the butler brought the invitation. The splendid letter, decorated with gold leaf, had the royal crest stamped on it.

I let out a hollow laugh after reading the contents of the invitation.

It was about the royal family hosting a charity party to help the less fortunate.

Charity party. It sounds good, but it's just wasting money because they want to throw a party. Since there's the cause of charity, no one can object. Because the moment you object, you become an asshole.

,There's no way the royal family would sincerely hold a party for charitable purposes. Charity is just an excuse to throw a party. What's the purpose of throwing the party? To see me?'

Judging by the timing, that was right.

,I did become a Level 7 and can't help but attract attention. Are they trying to drag me into the battle for the throne?'

Victoria VI.

The current Queen of Neo London, she is old.

She had previously declared that she would abdicate the throne to one of her grandsons or granddaughters within the next few years. The reason why it's not her sons and daughters is because all of her children have refused to inherit the throne. They knew very well that even if they inherited the throne, they wouldn't be able to achieve as much as Victoria VI. They knew their place well.

,On the other hand, the grandsons and granddaughters are more capable than their parents, so they're full of greed.'

Frankly speaking, I don't want to pay much attention to the political world.

But economics and politics are inseparable.

,Even though Neo London is a constitutional monarchy, the influence of the royal family cannot be ignored. Most of Neo London's citizens are more loyal to the royal family than to the parliament. The royal family has been collecting all kinds of talents until now. If the royal family decides to take action, even the Neo Round Table Council can't stop them.'

As I became a Level 7, my influence on Neo London became unbelievably large. Even if I publicly announce that I have no interest in the royal family, people won't believe it.

,It would be better to see the princes and princesses this time and decide on a direction. It's not bad to get actively involved. It would be troublesome if I fail, but if I succeed... the possibility of becoming Neo London's best company opens up.'

The stakes are quite high. So it's too early to make a decision here.

"...Since it's a party hosted by the royal family, I should attend. The party is in fifteen days. Send a letter to the royal family saying I will attend."

"Yes, Master-nim. I will begin preparations for attending the party."

"Also, find a personal carriage and coachman. I can't keep using Unicorn Company's carriage forever."

"Understood."

• • •

The next day. The Raven Knights sent Adamantium. They didn't send the entire promised amount, just a portion of it.

,Did they send it to show that they will definitely keep the contract?'

Adamantium. I held the jet-black metal in my hand. It was much heavier than steel.

,It's basically more than five times harder than steel.'

Compared to Orichalcum or Mithril, its mana conductivity is low, but its durability is one of the best in the world.

Moreover, Adamantium also has shape-memory ability. Even if it's damaged or broken to some extent, it automatically restores itself without magic.

,It's surprisingly easier to handle than steel, and it can be processed into fabric by drawing threads. The color is a luxurious black, so it's popular among nobles.'

Lightweight magic is essential because it's heavy. If I process and sell it, I can make a lot of money.

My purpose wasn't processing and selling it.

,Can I mix it with a superconductor?'

LK-99 is made of copper, lead, and LK special material. What if I put LK special material into Adamantium instead of copper and lead and bake it?

,Like other metals, there may be no reaction at all, or a new metal may be created like a superconductor.'

It was worth experimenting with.

And I discovered a new metal that didn't even exist in the original game.

• • •

Time flew by like an arrow, and the day of the party arrived.

I took my personal carriage to Buckingham Palace in Neo London's A District.

I showed the invitation to the knight guarding the main gate and entered the palace.

Butlers and maids working at Buckingham Palace caught my eye.

The basics for servants working in the palace were Flower-class, one level above Triple. And occasionally, Superior-class servants were seen.

Lower-ranking nobles with insufficient power bowed to the servants. People couldn't treat Flower-class or higher carelessly, even if they were nobles. Flower-class was so high that they could even obtain a noble title if they wanted.

"Welcome to Buckingham Palace, Count Meyer-nim. I will guide you to the banquet hall."

I arrived at the banquet hall with the guidance of the maid.

-Count Eugene Meyer-kakha is entering!

My name echoed through the speakers. The gazes of the nobles who were enjoying the banquet all focused on me at once.
