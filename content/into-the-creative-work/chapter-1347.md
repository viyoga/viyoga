After confirming the ending of Ghost Romance, I was momentarily stunned. The ending was too unexpected.

,Could it be that I would end up doing exorcism activities until the end.'

If it were me, I would have quit exorcism activities long ago and lived lavishly gathering women. I would hunt down ghosts to fight if I got bored... but not to the extent of traveling around the world doing exorcism activities.

,...It seems like I did a lot of exorcism activities in this world.'

It must be because I actively killed ghosts to lower my ghostly energy in order to capture the heroines. Especially killing the ghosts of the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons must have been decisive.

,Still, it was fun in its own way.'

A life like the ending of Ghost Romance. I want to live it once myself. A life of traveling around the world, banging beautiful women and getting them pregnant....

After closing the Pleasure Life app, I showered and immediately lay down on the bed. My body isn't tired, but my mind is a bit tired because I've been in the Ghost Romance world without rest. No, it feels tiring.

,Come to think of it, I've hardly rested in the Ghost Romance world.'

I closed my eyes and fell asleep.

,I need to distinguish well between what I can do and what I can't.'

I in the Pleasure World have different abilities respectively.

I mustn't mistake what I can do in reality. Otherwise, problems will occur at important moments.

,My current physical ability is above the average of an A-class Hunter. It's a bit lacking to call it top-tier.'

An A-class Hunter's strength isn't all about physical ability. There are variables such as combat experience, awakened abilities, and equipment. My actual strength can be seen as top-tier A-class.

,But I don't have much experience meeting top-tier A-class Hunters, so it's just a guess.'

Uuuuuung. Uuuuuung.

A phone call came.

I took out my smartphone from my pocket and checked the caller.

Baek Ji-eun.

The woman who took a seat at the end of the association's executives. Under hypnosis, she thinks of me as her childhood friend and sex friend.

I narrowed my eyes and looked at the smartphone. It was rare for her, who was busy with her career, to call me directly.

,Let's answer it for now.'

-Seong Yujin! Are you free?!

Baek Ji-eun's voice rang out. As soon as I heard her energetic voice, I knew. She seemed like she was going to make me do something. Frankly speaking, I didn't feel like it.

,I'm busy.'

-Don't be ridiculous. I know you're free. You only go into B-class dungeons. When are you going to become a formal A-class at that rate?

My rank was still B-class compared to my A-class skills.

It couldn't be helped. To become an A-class, I had to have not only skills but also achievements. There was also a way to gain achievements through illegal activities... but my goal is S-class. I'm avoiding anything that could be held against me later as much as possible.

The reason I only go into B-class dungeons was simple. Unlike A-class dungeons, there are almost no variables. Moreover, it's not easy to attack an A-class dungeon alone.

,Do you have a job with good achievements, or something?'

-Hehe. I contacted you because there's a good job.

,What is it? I won't do it if it's troublesome.'

-You should do it even if it's troublesome.

I could feel the joy in Baek Ji-eun's voice. She seemed confident that I wouldn't refuse.

,She's underestimating me too much. I'm a man who can refuse any offer.'

-This time, it's a decision made by the higher-ups. It's a bit of a hassle, but there was talk of entrusting it to promising individuals.

,...Promising individuals?'

-Yeah. Promising individuals who have the potential to become S-class candidates.

,Does that mean if I solve that, they'll recognize me as an S-class candidate?'

-That's not it. The higher-ups are just checking the possibilities. It's something that's done once every quarter by gathering young A-class Hunters... it's not a very special event.

,I'm not A-class, I'm B-class?'

-Your skills are close to A-class. Plus, your growth rate is ridiculous. Even though you were late compared to others, you're already aiming for A-class. There's enough reason for the higher-ups to pay attention to you. It's late in my opinion.

-That's not it, but you'll be able to become an A-class Hunter quickly. And the Korean Association will focus on you. In a good way.

-It's related to the Thousand Hands Guild. Did you see the news?

,I don't have time to watch the news... but I know the Thousand Hands Guild. It's a fairly famous guild.'

The Thousand Hands Guild is one of the small and medium-sized guilds operating in Seoul. The treatment of Hunters is good, and they accept inexperienced rookie Hunters well. Therefore, it's highly regarded in the Hunter community for a small and medium-sized guild.

-That Thousand Hands Guild was designated as a Black Guild 3 hours ago. The association is occupying the Thousand Hands Guild's headquarters building.

,...The Thousand Hands Guild as a Black Guild? Did they evade taxes? No, they wouldn't be designated as a Black Guild for just tax evasion... What the hell did those bastards do?'

Black Guild. It was a word meaning a criminal guild. In other words, the Thousand Hands Guild is a criminal organization.

A well-known small and medium-sized guild suddenly plunging into hell. I was curious about what happened.

-They joined hands with a Chinese Black Guild and distributed drugs. The money earned from drugs is estimated to be 800 billion won.

,Drugs, huh? But isn't it over? You said the association occupied the headquarters.'

What you have to do is track down the remnants of the Thousand Hands Guild with other promising individuals. Capture is the best, but... it doesn't matter if you kill them.

,Haa. It's a troublesome job... but I can't refuse... Do you know where they are?'

-Don't underestimate the Korean Association. They've already finished tracking their location.

I didn't bother asking how they found the location. It's obvious. Magic, artifacts, the abilities of awakened ones, and so on. There are many ways. They might have borrowed the power of science and technology unexpectedly.

-I'll send you a text. Come to the location by 6 PM.

,6 PM means there's only an hour left. That means I have to leave right away.'

,I'm going, so why are you like this.'

The muscular man in the passenger seat is Jang Juseok. He's also an A-class Hunter, and his ability is Blood Control. It's the ability to control blood.

All three of them are A-class Hunters and promising individuals that the association is watching. At least, it can be said that they won't be a burden.

Thousand Hands Guild Master Oh Ganghu.
