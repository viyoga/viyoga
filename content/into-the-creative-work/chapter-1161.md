I blocked Sung Hari-ssi's path.

Sung Hari-ssi doesn't like using the Spirit Devourer trait. I didn't want to force my woman to do something she hates.

The blowing wind brushed past me.

[Are you going to interfere?]

Sung Hari-ssi and the wind spirit I'm seeing for the first time. It was obvious whose side I would take. It would be nice if we could talk it out... but the wind spirit had already gone too far. We won't be able to have a conversation.

I hold my sword and aim it at the spirit. The spirit's elephant face contorts as if wounded.

[So, you're just human after all.]

The wind becomes fierce. The tip of my sword shakes from side to side, affected by the wind.

"Oppa. I should be the one here...."

"Hari-ya. You don't like using that skill, right? I'll take care of it, so just stay put."

Even as I spoke, the wind was getting stronger. It felt like I was talking in front of a powerful electric fan.

'Approach and aim for the core. Let's end this in one go.'

I run towards the spirit.

The direction of the wind changed. Suddenly, an updraft lifted my body into the sky. At this rate, I'll end up like those three Chinese guys being toyed with over there.

'Stormbreaker!'

The Stormbreaker that appeared in front of me disassembled and clung to my body, becoming armor. As the weight increased, my body descended. The spirit's nose twitched. The wind intensified, lifting my body upwards.

'Sung Hari-ssi... thankfully, she's out of the wind's influence. She really has a good sense for these things.'

Kaang! Kang! Kaang!

Blades of wind strike the armor. The power and cutting force were insufficient to penetrate the Stormbreaker. Above all, the armor was dense. The wind couldn't reach my skin.

That doesn't mean I have the upper hand in the battle with the spirit. If the spirit changes the way it manipulates the wind, I'll be helpless.

'Machina.'

Machina, in her ethereal form, pops up next to me. Machina is unaffected by the wind in her ethereal state. At the same time, she can't use any power either.

[You're calling me already? I thought I was supposed to be watching that bald old man?]

I had assigned Machina the task of tailing and monitoring Yang Zixin. I had a feeling there might be something there.

'Can't you see we're in a desperate situation? Possess the Stormbreaker.'

[Alright.]

Machina answered coolly and possessed the Stormbreaker. It was the effect of having oiled her up with spirit stones recently.

'If only she were like this all the time.'

As Machina possessed it, the armor's design changed into a futuristic suit. In particular, the right hand transformed into a round, cylindrical cannon. The cannon's muzzle glowed and spun around. It sucked in the surrounding wind and used it as power.

Machina's abilities were versatile. However, the downside was that it consumed a lot of mana.

[Charging complete! Now all I have to do is shoot!]

The problem is that the wind is blowing so hard that aiming isn't easy.

Cheolkeok.

Steel wings emerge from my back. The wings helped me maintain balance in the swirling wind. With Machina's support, things were easier in many ways.

Pang!

An energy mass was fired from the cannon at the spirit. The spirit quickly twisted its body to avoid my attack. At that moment, the three Chinese guys who had appeared behind the spirit swung their swords at it. Like A-class heroes, they had escaped from the wind on their own and attacked the spirit.

[Aaaaaaack!]

The spirit screamed as its spirit core took a hit. I aimed the cannon at the spirit. It wasn't fully charged yet, but I used my mana to charge it instantly and fired an energy blast.

Just before the energy blast reached the spirit, a huge wind rose up, forming a barrier and soaring into the sky. The three Chinese guys who had approached the spirit were also pushed back.

[I won't forgive you! I won't forgive you! I won't forgive you!!!]

The spirit goes berserk. As if in response, the spirit core flashes brightly.

'It's trying to use its very existence to gain power. This is... really dangerous.'

The opponent is a high-level spirit. The power it unleashes in its rampage could obliterate this entire area.

I have to end this before the spirit uses its power.

'A way....'

[Auntie's moving?]

"What?"

I lowered my head and looked at Sung Hari-ssi.

Sung Hari-ssi was biting her lip and approaching the spirit. Sharp winds blocked her, but she advanced step by step. Sung Hari-ssi wasn't unscathed either. Each time she approached the spirit, the wind wounded her body. Blood droplets were carried upwards by the wind, and her black hair fluttered wildly.

"Sung Hari-ssi! Get back!!"

I shouted with all my might. But Sung Hari-ssi showed no reaction, perhaps because it was buried by the powerful wind.

'...If she can't hear me, I have no choice but to go myself.'

I used the wind to tilt the center of my floating body downwards. Normally, I should fall downwards, but... the falling speed was very slow due to the strong wind. On top of that, the wind's power was getting stronger.

"Machina! Is there no way?!"

[No way. It's too late. Even if we use the thrusters, the wind is stronger inside! You saw it block the energy blast!]

"......."

Wouldn't it be possible if I used Cheonshim?

[But how is Auntie holding up? Normally, she would have been blown away by the wind like those guys earlier.]

I belatedly felt a sense of doubt. How is Sung Hari-ssi advancing in that wind? Because of her outstanding physical abilities? That can't be it. Sung Hari-ssi's physical abilities aren't that great right now. She's at a similar level to the three Chinese guys who were blown away earlier.

Then is it because her will is strong? There are things that can be done with will, and things that can't. And there are more things that can't be done.

'It's the Spirit Devourer (S) trait.'

It's certain that Spirit Devourer (S) reduces the damage received from spirits.

[Yujin-ah. Are you just going to stand there?]

"......."

I looked at Sung Hari-ssi. Sung Hari-ssi was moving forward on her own will. Can I stop her? Does Sung Hari-ssi really want that?

"...I respect Sung Hari-ssi's choice."

Still, I watched Sung Hari-ssi. I'll run to her if anything happens.

[Auntie looks like she's in so much pain! What do we do!]

Machina's worried voice echoed in my head. Sung Hari-ssi's condition was so serious that she was worried. Her clothes were tattered by the wind, and every time she walked, bloody footprints were imprinted on the ground.

And Sung Hari-ssi finally reached the spirit.

[G-Get away!!]

The spirit's scream contained fear. Sung Hari-ssi silently moved forward.

The spirit didn't retreat, even while feeling fear. Instead, it attacked Sung Hari-ssi. A powerful whirlwind, strong enough to devastate the surrounding area in an instant, engulfed Sung Hari-ssi.

Sung Hari-ssi wielded her spear.

The wind was cut and disappeared as it was. Sung Hari-ssi then approached the spirit. Her spear accurately pierced the spirit core.

[A... Aaaaaaack! Aaaaaaaaaack!]

The spirit let out a desperate scream. I could see the spirit moving the wind in its last struggle. Seeing the wind gathering at one point in the air, it seemed like it was going to explode it like a bomb. But the wind disappeared before it could gather.

To be exact, the spirit's power was absorbed by Sung Hari-ssi.

[...Auntie ate the spirit.]

Machina said in a serious tone. It was understandable. From Machina's perspective, it was a scene of a member of her own kind being eaten.

'It doesn't look as grotesque as I thought.'

The wind disappears.

My body falls downwards. Thanks to Machina using the thrusters along the way, I landed safely on the ground.

I approached Sung Hari-ssi, who was standing there with a dazed expression.

"Hari-ya. Are you okay?"

Sung Hari-ssi nodded weakly.

"I'm okay."

Looking at her up close, her body was a mess. Torn clothes and a body soaked in blood. Fortunately, there were no fatal wounds. I took out a potion and poured it on her head.

The blood on her body was washed away by the potion. Her scarred skin was also restored to smoothness by the top-grade potion.

"Is it okay to use such expensive potions like this?"

She spoke playfully, as if to change the somber mood.

"You're more important than potions."

"...Thank you, Oppa."

I summoned new clothes and gave them to her. Sung Hari-ssi hesitated to change right away. Three Chinese guys were approaching from afar. They were covered in wounds.

"You had a potion."

"We would like you to give us a potion as well."

"We will repay you for the potion later."

They boldly demand a potion.

'Machina.'

[Okay!]

Wiiiiiiing.

A drone appeared in the sky and flew towards the Chinese guys. The Chinese guys widened their eyes. They hadn't seen a drone before, so they didn't immediately grasp what it was. Soon, they tried to avoid the drone, but the magnetic field emitted from the drone restricted their movements.

[Haha! Suicide attack!]

Machina shouted cheerfully.

The result was not cheerful. A huge explosion occurred, blowing away the entire surrounding area. It was a natural consequence that a crater formed as if a meteorite had fallen on the ground.

The three Chinese guys vanished without a trace.

"Hahaha! This is my power! You humans!"

Machina, materialized, laughed with her arms crossed.

Originally, it would be difficult for Machina to exert this much power on her own, but the spirit stones she had consumed over the past time and the special environment that had been transformed into a spirit realm gave Machina power.

Machina slowly flew towards us.

Sung Hari-ssi's expression wasn't good. It wasn't because the three Chinese guys had died, but because she seemed to be afraid of Machina's reaction.

Machina is a spirit, and Sung Hari-ssi literally ate a spirit in front of Machina. She must be afraid that Machina will fear or hate her.

"I-I'm okay."

A pink atmosphere surrounds us.

It might not be bad to spend some happy time like this.
