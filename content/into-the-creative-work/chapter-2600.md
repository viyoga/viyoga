Kwaaaang!

An explosion echoed in a certain research lab in Japan. The lab was half-destroyed, and the researchers' corpses were scattered haphazardly. Precious research materials were gone with the wind. The only saving grace was that some research data remained. But with this insufficient research data, there was only so much that could be done.

"......"

A long black-haired woman wearing a white cat mask and a suit watched a man rampaging through the lab after barging in. The guards, former special forces soldiers protecting the lab, moved to subdue him, but they were having a hard time.

"Oneko-nim! The terrorist's capabilities are too great! Please allow us to kill him!"

"No. We've suffered tremendous losses. We need to bring that man over to our side. Then we can extract information from him and strengthen our forces. More than anything, we need to find out who's behind him."

Oneko watched the terrorist. He had suddenly appeared at the lab and started fighting without saying a word. He was wearing a mask, so his face couldn't be seen, but his visible body was that of a soldier trained to the limit. On top of that, he seemed to have taken drugs, exhibiting physical abilities beyond that of an Awakened one.

But as a living being, he was bound to get tired. The man, who had been rampaging like crazy, eventually showed an opening as his stamina and the drug's effects wore off. The guards quickly rushed in and subdued him, putting him in a special restraint suit to immobilize him.

The guards felt a burning desire for revenge against him, but at the same time, they were terrified.

Tok-gak, tok-gak.

Oneko walked towards the subdued terrorist.

"Be careful. This bastard tried to blow himself up at the end. We took away the self-destruct switch, but... there were no bombs on his clothes."

"...You're saying he might have planted bombs somewhere else?"

"That's possible. In the worst-case scenario, there might be a bomb inside his body."

"A bomb inside his body? Even the most hardcore Cult wouldn't do that."

The terrorist remained stoic even after being captured. Did he bite off his tongue? Looking closer, that wasn't it either. He quietly watched Oneko approaching him. Madness and conviction could be felt in his eyes.

"This isn't good. Cut off about three of his fingers."

An order to shake the man's mind. The guard immediately took out a knife and cut off three fingers from the man's right hand. But the man's expression didn't change in the slightest.

'His eyes flickered slightly. It's not that he can't feel pain. But he's not afraid.'

Oneko moved closer to the man.

Less than 2 meters.

The condition for Oneko to use her ability.

She used mind control on the man. Only then did the man's face contort. He resisted, feeling a foreign force invading his mind.

'His Holiness will save me! I will not succumb to the power of the devil, and I will overcome any trial with faith!'

Oneko clicked her tongue.

"He's more hardcore than any Cult I know. This is going to take quite a while. Call the torture expert."

The torture of the man continued. Meanwhile, Oneko received a report.

"Oneko-nim! Terrorist attacks have occurred all over Japan! The Prime Minister's official residence has also been attacked by terrorists."

"Ha. A simultaneous terrorist attack, huh? What about the Prime Minister?"

"The Prime Minister and his wife are safe. However, the Prime Minister's children have died."

"That's a relief, at least. It would have been quite troublesome if the Prime Minister had died."

The torture of the man continued. They didn't hesitate to cut off all his fingers and toes, blind him, and burn his skin with a branding iron.

But they couldn't break through the man's mental resistance. The man didn't scream during any of the torture.

"Why did you attack this lab?"

"......"

"Where did you come from?"

"......"

"What's your name?"

"......"

"Hey, can you say something? Are you mute or something?"

"......"

The man didn't answer any questions.

Oneko felt herself getting increasingly tired of the man. Could it be that another Esper had brainwashed him first?

'No. There are no traces of brainwashing by another Esper. I would know, even if others don't.'

Even after an hour, Oneko hadn't managed to take control of his mind. But it wasn't possible to completely resist her ability with just a strong will. The man's mind was slowly wavering.

'Three hours. That should be enough. He's surprisingly resilient. Ordinary humans can't last even a minute, and even Awakened ones can't last more than five minutes in front of my ability.'

Oneko was genuinely curious about what this man's identity was.

It was then.

The man's mouth, which hadn't opened no matter what they did, opened. Fluent Japanese came out.

"The time has come."

"...?!"

Oneko and the guards were startled and looked at the man with wary eyes. So he wasn't mute.

The man's attitude hadn't changed. He turned his head towards Oneko and said.

"You wicked mortal bitch. I am His sword, His bullet. You will be judged by Him someday."

Immediately after, the man's body and head exploded. As his head burst, some of the blood splattered on Oneko's body.

There was no major damage.

The special restraint suit that had been binding the man's body absorbed even the explosive force. It was a restraint suit designed for mutant test subjects. The bomb planted in the man's head wasn't meant to kill the enemies in the first place, but rather to destroy the man's brain, so the explosive force wasn't strong.

"......"

There was no damage, but she couldn't shake off the shock.

To use the man who had single-handedly stormed into the lab and caused massive damage so futilely? It was incomprehensible to use such an elite combatant as a disposable tool.

"...Were there any Cults left in Japan? I would have dealt with them first, as they would become a nuisance if left alone...."

It was even stranger if they were a Cult. There was no reason for them to attack Japan. If it were them, they would quietly lie low and gather strength.

The purpose of the terrorism was also unclear. Usually, terrorism is meant to convey something or to threaten someone. But what about this terrorist? He simply destroyed without making any claims. Destruction itself was the goal.

'This is like revenge against the entire country of Japan. Has Japan done something recently?'

Something recently.

'...Could it be Korea?'

She suspected it, but she couldn't be sure. The Korea she knew wasn't this aggressive.

'I need to investigate.'

She thought of Origumo, a player remaining in Pohang, Korea, but dismissed it. Origumo was a disposable pawn anyway. It was more urgent to recover from the damage caused by the terrorism than to forcibly intervene, save him, and antagonize Korea.

'I need to talk to the Prime Minister.'

Unfortunately, she couldn't brainwash the Prime Minister. The Prime Minister had known about Oneko's abilities and had been wary of her for a long time. Now, the Prime Minister and Oneko were equal partners who had joined hands for their mutual benefit.

Seongsim Hall.

A place where Seongsim boxes containing the hearts of the Holy Knights are stored. Seongsim Hall is a very secret place known only to a small number of people within the Seongjin Church.

I took out a Seongsim box stored in this Seongsim Hall. A small freezer adorned with gold and silver. The name of Sohn Taehyung, the leader of the Holy Knights and a cardinal, was written on its surface.

I opened the box. There was a frozen heart. I took out the heart and placed it on the altar. And then I used my ability.

Blood, flesh, and bones were generated around the heart, taking the form of a person. It didn't take long for it to become a complete human form.

Sohn Taehyung opened his eyes.

"Your Holiness...!"

"Cardinal, congratulations on your resurrection."

"It is all thanks to Your Holiness's grace."

"Do you remember how you died?"

"Of course."

It had been quite a while since Sohn Taehyung's heart had been stored. Yet he remembers his death that happened just a few hours ago? This was proof that there was a soul in this world.

'Well, I knew it. There were quite a few things related to souls in the original game.'

I spoke to Sohn Taehyung, who was filled with emotion.

"I will hear your report later. First, I will extract your heart and store it again. Any objections?"

"It is according to the procedure. I am always prepared."

I used the Holy Sword, a small, pure white knife, to cut open Sohn Taehyung's chest, extracted his heart, and stored it in the Seongsim box. Then, I used recovery again to resurrect Sohn Taehyung. Sohn Taehyung's expression was peaceful even after facing death.

"I am sorry. I could not kill the Prime Minister and his wife in Japan. But I did kill the Prime Minister's children."

I didn't think that the Japanese Prime Minister was stupid enough to be killed by just one terrorist attack. That might have been possible in the peaceful days of the past. But now, in an era where survival is threatened by zombies, mutants, and Awakened ones.

He deserved praise just for killing the Japanese Prime Minister's children.

"Well done. The Japanese Prime Minister must be furious now. You weren't exposed, were you?"

"Yes. However, they may have found out my identity from my corpse."

"It doesn't really matter. We can just deny it. Relations with Japan are already strained beyond repair anyway. No, it's not just Japan...."

There are no perfect alliances in this world.

China, Japan, Russia.

Korea will eventually have to fight them. And Europe and America far away will be no exception.

I began to resurrect other Holy Knights, starting with him. I can't resurrect all 50 of them. Resurrecting people consumes quite a bit of stamina. Right now, my limit is about seven people a day.

"I met a woman named Oneko. She was a woman wearing a white cat mask."

I obtained quite interesting information from one of the Holy Knights.

"Hooh. Interesting. Tell me more."

"That wicked woman tried to take control of my mind."

A mental ability user.

She tried to break down his mind with torture and take control of it in the gap. In particular, that woman deliberately approached the knight.

'She restrained the enemy and then approached as if she had been waiting for it, narrowing the distance. Hmm. Is that a condition for using her mental ability? That she needs to be close, or that the closer she is to the target, the higher the success rate?'

I raised my guard against Oneko. She might be the person I need to be most careful of among the Japanese.

"How was that woman's mental interference? Could you withstand it?"

"I don't think I could have withstood it for long. At most, half a day, I think."

The Holy Knights are true lunatics even among fanatics. You can consider their minds completely gone. And those guys can only withstand it for half a day? Oneko's mental ability seemed more dangerous than I thought.

'Oneko is definitely an Esper with mental abilities. The best way to deal with that woman is for me, who is immune to mental abilities, to face her 1-on-1 and kill her, but....'

The opponent isn't an idiot. Oneko will hide herself well and won't try to move directly.

'...There's time. I should consider myself lucky that I found out about the woman named Oneko before it was too late. Should I put Oneko aside for now and clean up Pohang first?'
