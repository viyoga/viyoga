Young-hari's face contorted in pain. Her chest pierced by the spear, she tried to counterattack until the very last moment. But no matter how strong her will, there were things she could do, and things she couldn't.

Especially in this world created through artificial intelligence, which was ruthless. It didn't acknowledge will. Her attempts to cling to life with her will were crushed miserably.

Young-hari's head turned towards me. She seemed to want to say something as she looked at me, but her body dissolved into light and disappeared.

,Shit....'

I stopped running.

Young-hari died. But my head was arbitrarily finding hope. It was closer to wishful thinking.

,Young-hari is a player. This is virtual reality, and when a player dies, they revive nearby. She might have revived in a nearby village.'

「The source of the apocalypse has disappeared.」

「It will take 30 minutes for the apocalypse to completely disappear.」

「After 30 minutes, all functions of the Terra Simulator will return to normal.」

「You can log out after 30 minutes.」

,...30 minutes?'

I shouted to Seong-hari.

"Mom!"

Seong-hari flinched and looked at me. She looked like she was about to cry any second. She must have thought I was going to curse at her... I certainly wanted to curse. But cursing at her now wouldn't change the situation.

"30 minutes! We have to hold out for 30 minutes!"

"......"

Seong-hari snapped out of it and took a fighting stance.

Killing Young-hari didn't mean everything was over. To complete the quest and receive Susanoo's Magatama, I had to find Arinna NPC. And there was no way the enemies would just stand by and watch.

"Congratulations. You have saved the students trapped here."

The voice of the dead Hasegawa Isshin echoed from the sky. Hasegawa Isshin, who appeared by tearing through space, looked down at us with sunken eyes. His demeanor and voice were very cold.

"Please forget the disgraceful display you saw earlier. I'm not used to virtual reality, so I got too immersed."

A large-scale barrier spread across the sky. It wasn't like before, where he was deploying dozens of barriers simultaneously. He was focusing on deploying a single barrier. Naturally, the power of the barrier was much weaker. However, the durability of the barrier was much greater.

,I can't see the weak points of the barrier.'

Isolation due to isolation. That alone would be fine. The real problem was the effect of this barrier. It physically pressed down on the body and stole mana.

,The mana being taken is a small amount, so it's okay. The flow of mana within the barrier is connected to the students. Is this a barrier to support the students?'

It could be seen as a wide-area buff.

「Revival point has been changed. You will revive here even if you die.」

A sudden notification window.

The Terra AI, which had been silent until now, was intervening.

「Player burden rate is increasing.」

「Current added player burden rate: 120%」

「Warning! Burden rate is too high! There is a high possibility of post-traumatic stress! We recommend logging out!」

「Burden rate continues to rise!」

「Warning! We recommend logging out for your safety!」

,What the hell is going on all of a sudden.'

The AI Terra was intervening. We hadn't obtained Susanoo's Magatama yet. The AI Terra wanted us to log out as we were.

,Even if I don't know what the burden rate is, is there no further intervention?'

This was fortunate.

"Mom. I'll summon more spirits. You devour the spirits and get stronger."

Seong-hari shook her head.

"I'm already at my limit. Devouring more spirits won't make a difference. Rather, Yoo-jin-ah, I'll open a path. You find Arinna and receive Susanoo's Magatama. Fortunately, the village where Arinna is isn't far from here."

Seong-hari's suggestion was reasonable. If I ran now, I could arrive in 10 minutes.

"Can you fight alone, Mom?"

"Yoo-jin-ah. Do you know who your mom is? I was once called the strongest hero. Do you think I'd be defeated by something like this? Trust me."

"Okay. But it's hard to get out because there's a barrier."

"I'll break through it."

Seong-hari raised her spear. She took a stance to throw the spear in the direction of the village where Arinna was.

"What are you doing! Stop that woman immediately!"

Hasegawa Isshin shouted. The students around him rushed forward. Lightning erupted from Seong-hari's body, sweeping in all directions.

"Kkyaaaaaak!"

"It hurts! Why does it suddenly hurt so much?!"

"Burden rate! The burden rate has increased!"

The students fell back. They stopped trying to close the distance and prepared for ranged attacks.

Seong-hari threw the spear without paying any attention. A sonic boom occurred. The surrounding area was shattered, and the fragments scattered. With my spirit eyes, I could see the barrier, and I confirmed that a large hole had been created in it.

"Yoo-jin-ah! Go!"

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Sound Step (雷音步).

Kwarurureung! I began to sprint with the sound of thunder. I broke through the barrier, instantly covering 300 meters.

"Chase him! You must! You must stop him! If you fail to stop him, the academy...! Your futures will also become uncertain! So you must stop him!"

Hasegawa Isshin shouted. Some of the students closely pursued me. Glancing back, I recognized a familiar face.

A tall, sharp-looking male student with a pointed chin.

,What was his name again? Kiku... something, I think.'

He was chasing me with murderous intent, along with his companions. They were chasing me openly.

"I haven't forgotten the humiliation from back then! I'll kill you! Hasegawa Sota!!"

I stopped running towards the village. No matter what, I couldn't stand being ignored by these chumps. I'd deal with them lightly here and then enter the village.

"Spirit Summon."

Rocks rose from the ground. The rock spirit, resembling an armored soldier, waited silently for my command.

,High-grade. Top-grade would have been better, but this is enough.'

I commanded the high-grade rock spirit.

"Kill those bastards."

"As you wish."

The high-grade rock spirit ran towards them but stopped abruptly. Not of its own volition.

"Did you think we were chasing you without any countermeasures?"

The guy was holding something that looked like a shining stone. Lines extended from the stone, seizing the spirit and the space.

"A barrier?"

"The headmaster gave this to us! I heard you're not even related to the headmaster by blood? Just stay still! If we just stop you, we...!"

The guy grinned. The guys around him did the same.

"Did the headmaster offer his Butthole or something? Why are you so happy?"

A look of contempt was directed at me.

"You vulgar bastard! The headmaster promised us the right to use the Terra Simulator for 10 years. Do you understand? We can use the Terra Simulator for a whole 10 years!!"

"Sounds like you're happy to play a virtual reality game."

"You're not only vulgar but also stupid.... Don't you understand what it means to be able to use the Terra Simulator for 10 years?"

"In my opinion, this isn't very helpful."

I identified the barrier. I drew my sword and ran to the right. I stabbed the weak point of the barrier with my sword imbued with lightning.

Pajijik!

The barrier shattered. The fragments of the barrier fell beautifully but suddenly stuck to my body.

,What the fuck. Was this a trap?!'

Knowing that I could see the weak points of the barrier, they created this kind of barrier.

The barrier wrapped around my body. This was dangerous. Now that I couldn't use Heavenly Heart, there was no way to break free if my body was restrained.

"'A Desperate Struggle for Survival (死中求生)' is used."

"Desperate Struggle for Survival (Seeking Life in Death)"

Hope is everywhere.」

Desperate Struggle for Survival was a talisman that had been stashed away in my inventory. I had obtained it a while ago and completely forgotten about it. Thanks to this talisman, I was freed from the barrier's restraint.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

I swung my sword at the guy who hadn't yet grasped the situation. The blue sword light instantly severed the guy's neck. Before the guy's head fell, I twisted my waist. I swung my sword again at the guy's companions next to him.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A flash of light, and another head fell.

"Damn it!"

"Attack!!"

"Shit!"

Three out of the original five remained.

These guys were used to party hunting. But two of their party members were gone? They couldn't exert their full strength.

,If I only consider individual combat strength, they're weaker than me. And they're not receiving that old man's buff.'

I plunged into their midst. The blade shone with lightning.

Swish.

I severed another person's neck. The remaining two attacked me from both sides.

"Holy Barrier."

A faintly glowing barrier blocked their attacks. It didn't block them perfectly, and my shoulder was crushed. I continued my attack without paying attention.

Swish. Thwack.

I severed the woman's neck and stabbed the man's heart with my sword.

That was the end. The situation was resolved.

Turning around, I saw the high-grade rock spirit staring blankly.

"Useless bastard."

I killed the rock spirit with a slash. The aftermath struck my body, but it was bearable.

,It's easier without pursuers.'

As I ran towards the village, I glanced back. The area where Seong-hari and Hasegawa Isshin were fighting was quiet due to the barrier. It was impossible to know what was happening inside from the outside.

,I need to do what I have to do. Let's quickly obtain Susanoo's Magatama.'

I arrived at the village. I hurriedly looked for the flower shop. It was going too smoothly. There were no enemies chasing me. For some reason, I felt a creeping sense of unease that Hasegawa Isshin might have set something up.

It was unfounded.

The flower shop was tranquil. Arinna was watering the plants with a watering can. Unlike my urgency, she was so peaceful that it felt like she was in a different world.

"You've come."

"Did you think I wouldn't come?"

"I didn't expect you to come. I thought the woman next to you would come. After all, she was the one who killed the source of the apocalypse."

"...So you're saying you won't give me Susanoo's Magatama?"

I gripped my sword tightly. I'd kill her and take it.

"No. Aren't you and that woman a team? I have that much flexibility. Thank you for saving this world."

「Salvation

Overcome the apocalypse and save this world.

Reward: Susanoo's Magatama」

「Quest completed.」

"...You. You knew from the beginning that Hari was the source of the apocalypse, didn't you?"

"......"

Arinna didn't answer. But the corners of her mouth were smiling.

I raised my sword upwards. Arinna reached out her hand to me. A white, shining Magatama approached me.

I grabbed Susanoo's Magatama with one hand and brought down my sword.
