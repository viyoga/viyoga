We bought the slashing box cutter and the demonic ion drink. We scraped together every last GP we had, but it couldn't be helped. What if we'd left some behind and another participant bought the slashing box cutter?

,Rather than letting those bastards have it, it's better to use up all our points and take it ourselves.'

,I see exit 12 over there.'

It was the moment when the endless subway adventure seemed to be coming to an end.

I felt a cold wind from behind. I paused for a moment. Cold wind? What were we supposed to do when we felt a cold wind?

"Run right now!!"

Yujin-myung shouted and ran out. We started running after him. I shot ahead of Yujin-myung in an instant. My physical abilities were already superior, and now I was wearing wind sneakers.

,Wait a minute. If I take the lead, I won't be able to react if something happens to Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young.'

I deliberately slowed down to stay behind Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young. I was ready to support them if they made a mistake.

A cold wind brushed past my neck. I felt a chill down my spine. It felt like I was about to be bitten by a huge beast.

"Euaaaak?!"

Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young were running well, but Bae Hyun-soo was the problem. He fell while running.

,Should I save this bastard? Or not?'

My hesitation was short. I decided to save him. Bae Hyun-soo's mimicry ability was useless, but his game tool was very useful. If he had the map, he could accurately know the location of other participants within 500m.

I grabbed Bae Hyun-soo's body, which had fallen, and slung it roughly over my shoulder and started running.

"Keuheuek! Yujin hyung-nim...! Thank you! Thank you so much...!"

"Shut up."

It was a little tough, but it was okay. We quickly went up the exit stairs. I was curious about what was behind me before I went up the stairs.

,Should I take a look? Jo-eun is quiet, so it must not be dangerous.'

I turned my head to check behind me.

-You saw it?

There was nothing there. Only darkness. No, that darkness was something. I swallowed hard and went up the stairs. Fortunately, it didn't chase me to the stairs.

We safely got out of the subway. And I realized that this was the same entrance we used when we first entered the subway station.

"I don't think we should go in there twice. Is anyone hurt?"

"No."

Everyone was fine except for Bae Hyun-soo, who had fallen and was bleeding from his nose. Yoo Byung-jin handed Bae Hyun-soo a handkerchief and nodded.

"Good. Let's take a break here... and reconfirm our goals. You could say we're confirming our wishes here."

"I, I want to become the world's best professional gamer."

Bae Hyun-soo continued. I didn't care. Honestly, I didn't care what Bae Hyun-soo did.

It was Shin Do-hyun's wish. She was going to be my mother-in-law.

"My wish has changed. Originally, it was to become the world's best idol. But now I think I can achieve that on my own. My changed wish is to use my invisibility ability without restrictions. If I become invisible, I can act without worrying about what others think."

And she'll have an affair with me with that ability. I'm looking forward to the future.

"I'm... well, it's money. I'm going to get the maximum amount of money the devil can give me."

Money was a wish I came up with casually. I didn't have a proper wish right now. Revive my dead parents? You can only revive one person with a wish. I can't revive both of them.

,If it were in the past, maybe. But recently, I haven't even missed my parents.'

I was satisfied with my current life. A life with Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young. Honestly, I like the Devil's Game too. I can fully demonstrate my abilities, and I'm given fair compensation for it.

"The Grand Game is not our ultimate goal. We need to think about what comes after that. I want Yujin-ssi to become the king who rules the night and the dark side of Korea. The Devil's Game will be held again, and this is also a preparation for that. Of course, it will be difficult for Yujin-ssi alone. So I will assist Yujin-ssi."

The Devil's Game will be held again. The Devil's Game influences the world, for better or worse. We couldn't just sit back and get caught off guard.

"...You want me to achieve Pengcheon-soo's goal. Becoming the Night King Seong Yujin. Not bad. I'll do it."

"Thank you for making the decision. I pledge my loyalty to Yujin-ssi."

"Um. Let's talk about that later.... What's the next plan?"

What was urgent and important now wasn't after we made our wishes. What's more important now is how to win the Grand Game.

"We need to kill as many competitors as possible."

"Why?"

"If all the wishes of the Grand Game participants are fulfilled... there's a high possibility that the world itself will become a mess. Also, if the wishes of Pengcheon-soo and his gang are fulfilled, our situation will become difficult. They won't leave us alone."

I understood. For our peaceful future, we had to kill Pengcheon-soo and other enemies. And other participants who might make any kind of wish.

Everyone can achieve their wishes if they work together? Knowing what each individual's wish is. If a wish that would destroy Korea, if not the world, is fulfilled, wouldn't everyone be screwed?

Pengcheon-soo and his gang had at least 10 or more members, and all of them had a certain level of combat power.
