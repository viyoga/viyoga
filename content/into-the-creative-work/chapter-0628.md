I stared at Seira as if observing her while she slept.

Seira twitched in her sleep. I thought she was waking up, but she wasn't.

When the time came, I approached her. When I was about 3M away from her, she opened her eyes wide.

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim. It's time to switch shifts. Were you awake?"

,No.... I just woke up. I became fully alert the moment I sensed your presence."

,......You don't look too good. Shouldn't you sleep more?"

,There's no problem. My senses have just become a bit more sensitive."

Seira got up. A cold wind blew, hitting her body. She shivered and frowned.

,Gyeong. Did you feed me Yet powder while I was sleeping?"

My heart stung. Turning Yet powder into gas and feeding it to her was the same as forcing her to take it.

,If I had, Seira-gongnyeo-nim would have woken up."

,I was just saying. There's no reason for Gyeong to feed me Yet powder."

,Is this how you feel after eating Yet powder?"

,It's worse. My senses are so sensitive that the feeling of the air touching my body feels unpleasantly vivid. There's so much information coming in that it's giving me a headache. Did you put a large amount of Yet powder in the emergency rations I ate for dinner?"

,Do you see me as that kind of guy? You know very well that Yet powder takes effect immediately after consumption. If Yet powder was mixed in the food, there's no way Seira-gongnyeo-nim wouldn't have noticed."

,I'm sorry, Gyeong. My senses became overly sensitive, and my nerves became sensitive along with them. I don't know what's causing my body to be like this."

,If it's too hard to bear, go back to sleep. I'll stand guard."

,It's not that hard to bear. I'll return to normal after some time. I'll stand guard, so you sleep."

,From my position, I'm worried about Seira-gongnyeo-nim...."

,I won't go far, so sleep. That's an order. Are you going to refuse again this time?"

,I will sleep."

I lay down in the spot where Seira had been lying. Seira's warmth still remained.

,The stars are shining brightly."

,Yeah. Sleeping while looking at the stars isn't so bad."

I pretended to close my eyes but squinted. I focused only on her in the dimly visible world.

,Sleep."

Seira said. It seems she felt my gaze because her senses had become more sensitive. I need to be careful about this. Things could go wrong if I steal glances at her chest or butt.

,Seira's condition will probably remain the same even after a few hours. The effects and duration of Yet powder increase depending on the amount taken. The gas used on Seira is roughly equivalent to 300g of powder.... Even if most of the gas flew away in the wind, I have to consider that gas is more effective than Yet powder.'

I closed my eyes and tried to sleep, looking forward to how she would react tomorrow.

A cold drop of water fell on my cheek.

I opened my eyes.

The surroundings were already thick with dense fog, and the sky was full of dark clouds.

I looked around and searched for Seira. I found her without difficulty. She was sitting a little distance away, leaning her back against a rock. Her frowning face showed that she was very displeased. Her wavy ash-gray hair was damp and drooping.

,You're awake? I was just about to wake you up."

,How is your condition?"

,I'm fine. What's not fine is the weather. Raindrops are already starting to fall one by one. It's going to rain soon. And when it rains...."

,The Yet Slugs will run wild."

Humid environments are perfect weather for slugs to be active.

,There's no time. We need to find and eliminate the Yet Slug Queen as quickly as possible. That way, the burden on the city will be reduced."

,Yes. Let's move right away."

In less than 20 minutes, it started to drizzle. It was hard to find the way because of the fog. There were many obstacles in many ways.

And Seira's condition got worse and worse. The hood attached to her coat could block some of the rain, but not all of it. Especially since her senses are currently several times more sensitive than usual.

The sound of raindrops falling, the sensation of raindrops, the coldness of raindrops. She would be feeling all of those things. With information flooding in, her mind would become complicated, and she would become mentally exhausted quickly.

,The rain is actually a good thing for me. Yet Slug Queen. Please, I beg you. Stay hidden.'

I stared intently at Seira from one step behind her.

Sseueueueuk. Sseueueueuk. Sseueueueuk.

I could hear the sound of Yet Slugs crawling towards us from beyond the fog. If you only heard the invisible fog and sound, it was like a scene from a horror movie.

Seira held the Zweihander hanging on her back with both hands and swung it forward. The fog parted, and a large Sword energy cut through five Yet Slugs at once.

Seira put her sword back and started walking silently. I couldn't tell if the liquid flowing down her face was sweat or raindrops.

,She's overdoing it.'

She put too much force into unleashing the Sword energy earlier. If she were her usual self, she would have swung the greatsword more efficiently.

I didn't step forward even though I could have. I'm hoping she gets tired.

As time passed, her condition got worse and worse.

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim. I think it would be good to rest for a while."

,......I will."

We sat under a large tree. Thick drops of water dripped between the leaves, but we could avoid the rain to some extent.

,Your gaze has been fixed on me since earlier. Do you have something to say?"

,Gongnyeo-nim's breathing is not normal. Your breathing is also erratic. How about finding a suitable place and resting?"

,I'm fine. It's just that breathing feels a little awkward because my senses are sensitive. More than that, I've been thinking about the cause of why I'm like this."

Seira deliberately changed the subject. If her physical condition was really okay, she wouldn't have needed to do that.

,What is the cause you are thinking of?"

,I think the cause is killing Yet Slugs. I killed more Yet Slugs than usual. I stepped forward and killed them before Gyeong did. In the process, something from the Yet Slugs affected me."

,It makes sense that I'm fine too. Then I'll step forward and eliminate the Yet Slugs from now on."

,What if Gyeong becomes like me? I'll take charge of dealing with the Yet Slugs."

,Gongnyeo-nim might get hurt doing that."

,It's better than both of us getting hurt. Think about efficiency."

,......."

I nodded silently.

It's better for one person to be sick in preparation for the worst than for both of us to be sick. And I had to accept Seira's opinion for my plan.

Seira's condition got worse as time passed.

Now she couldn't unleash Sword energy and swung her greatsword close to the Yet Slugs. Still, her excellent swordsmanship skills were not going anywhere, so she easily dealt with the Yet Slugs.

In the afternoon, the dense fog cleared up a bit, though only slightly. And finally, we found the Yet Slug Queen.

The Yet Slug Queen existed among more than 100 Yet Slugs. True to her name, she was being guarded by the Yet Slugs like a queen.

The Queen's body was pink instead of yellow, so it immediately caught my eye. In addition, her body was at least twice as large as other Yet Slugs, and there was a hole like a volcanic crater on her smooth back.

A yellow liquid was flowing from her back. Only after concentrating a bit more did I realize that it was a small Yet Slug.

,......I see why she's called the Queen."

,The Yet Slug Queen lays more than 20 Yet Slugs a day. Yet Slugs also split more than once every ten days. When there is a Queen, the Yet Slugs become ferocious and the population increases to an unmanageable level. This is why the Queen must be dealt with quickly when she appears. Actually, it wasn't that big of a mission, but... Hooo. There have been too many variables coming all the way here."

I felt regret in my heart. If I killed the Yet Slug Queen, I would have to return to the city.

,I was looking for a chance to bang Seira.... Queen. This bitch should have stayed buried somewhere. Why did she crawl out....'

Seira had already finished preparing for battle and said to me.

,Gyeong. Can't you drop lightning on the Yet Slug Queen? If the Yet Slug Queen dies, the Yet Slugs around her will naturally scatter, so we can avoid unnecessary fights."

,I'll try. There are dark clouds in the sky, after all."

I had no intention of doing something clumsy in front of Seira.

Kwareureureureung.

Lightning wriggled in the dark clouds. As expected, I am stronger now than in reality.

I gathered the lightning in the clouds and dropped it on the Yet Slug Queen.

With a loud noise, the surroundings flashed as if a flashbang had been dropped.

Kwaaaaang!

The ground was dug up by the huge lightning.

But the Yet Slug Queen did not die. She wriggled her body and crawled out of the hole in the ground. Her antennae spun around, and she threateningly opened her large mouth towards us. Thousands of teeth moved like a chainsaw in her mouth.

,......She's fine. It was lightning that I dropped after consuming more than half of my mana... It's shocking."

The Yet Slug Queen, who was moving perfectly fine after being struck by lightning, didn't seem to have suffered any damage. It was truly shocking.

,There's no need to be discouraged. Now that I see it, the Queen is protecting her whole body with mucus. Not only your lightning, but most attacks won't work at all. More troublesome than that is that the Queen has figured out our location. Surprise attacks won't work anymore. There's no choice but to fight head-on."

,Shall we retreat and come up with a plan?"

,We can't let the Queen get away here. I'll fight the Queen, so Gyeong, support me and deal with the Yet Slugs."

,Yes."

Seira kicked off the ground and ran in a straight line. I followed her and dropped lightning on the Yet Slugs around her.

Kwaaang! Kwang! Kwang!

The sound of thunder echoed continuously. Seira, whose senses had become sensitive, didn't show any discomfort even though her ears must have been hurting.

Yet Slugs gathered in front of the Queen.

,I'll open the way."

Blue Sword energy and Red thunder crackled on the Hwaryeon Bido.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Noeseom (雷閃).

I unleashed blue Sword energy wrapped in Red thunder.

Numerous Yet Slugs were cut down and collapsed on the floor.

I aimed for the Queen too, but the Noeseom disappeared just before touching the Queen's body. Even if it had touched her, it was questionable whether it could have been dealt with for sure.

,Thank you."

Seira ran. I burned to death the Yet Slug bastards who were trying to rush at her with lightning.

When she arrived in front of the Yet Slug Queen, she gripped the Zweihander tightly with both hands. Blue Sword energy soared from the blade and turned into Sword strength.

Just before Seira swung her sword, the Yet Slug Queen opened her mouth wide and spat out mucus.

Seira moved to the side. After definitely avoiding the Yet Slug Queen's attack, she approached again and swung her sword. Her sword cut the Yet Slug Queen's body in half. The corpse of the Yet Slug Queen, split in two, fell to the floor.

,It's over.'

She seemed to feel the same way, as she staggered once and stuck her greatsword into the ground and leaned on it. She was trying to stand up even if she had to force herself.

That's when it happened. The antennae of the Yet Slug Queen, who was thought to have died after being split in half, twitched.

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim!"

Seira was surprised and gripped her sword tightly.

It was too late.

The Yet Slug Queen's body swelled up and exploded. Mucus covered her body.

,Aaaaaaaaaaah!"

A painful scream spread into the air. All the clothes she was wearing melted away in an instant, and she, now naked, staggered and fell to the floor.

I was startled and ran towards her. There were no Yet Slugs blocking me. From the moment the Yet Slug Queen self-destructed, they ignored us as if it wasn't their business and began to scatter as if looking for something to do.

I ran to Seira and checked on her. She's still breathing. She just lost consciousness.

,It's only natural that she fainted since she suffered extreme pain in her already sensitive state.'

The clothes she was wearing, including her underwear, had all melted away, but her skin had turned red.

,This is a full-body burn. I can't leave her like this!'

It was the moment I took out a potion.

Sseueueueuk. Sseueueueuk. Sseueueueuk.

The Yet Slugs were approaching me with hostility. The attitude of the guys who were scattering suddenly changed.

,Tsk.'

I put the Zweihander in my inventory and picked up Seira.

,Acceleration! Instant!'

And I ran away at full speed.
