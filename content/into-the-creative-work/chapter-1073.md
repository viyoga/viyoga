「Entered Sector 5,845, the Three Walls.」

I moved to Sector 5,845, the base of the Black Blood Alliance.

As the name of the sector suggested, this area had three walls. Three large walls rose in a circle as if guarding the center.

I didn't know much about Sector 5,845. It wasn't in the original [God's Atlantis] or even the setting book. I did some research, but I couldn't get any significant information.

Li Mei also didn't know much about this sector.

"According to the people who attacked this place with the Black Blood Alliance Lord in the early days... it wasn't a difficult conquest."

"In the early days of Atlantis, there were a few sectors that were easy pickings. It seems like this was one of those sectors."

The difficulty of conquering Atlantis sectors varied greatly. There were sectors where simply entering meant more than half the conquest was successful, and conversely, there were sectors with such high difficulty that even entering was difficult.

"Yes. The method to conquer this sector was... to get to the center by crossing the three walls."

"The height of the walls is about 50M. Are there any doors?"

"There's one each on the west and east sides. But when they conquered it, they didn't use the doors and just flew over. That was recognized as the conquest."

"They got a sector for free. I'm jealous."

Li Mei and I put the prepared item in our mouths and chewed.

「Wind Leaf

You can fly in the sky for 10 minutes.

Type: Consumable

Rank: B」

It was a green leaf. Unlike its tough appearance, it was crispy and savory like a nutty snack.

Our bodies floated into the sky. We crossed the 50M wall without any trouble and headed to the center. Glancing down, the inside of the first wall was empty.

"There's nothing here. I thought there would be a village or something."

"In the early days, there were monsters. But now they've all been subjugated, so it's safe. And the Black Blood Alliance Lord was going to build a village inside the wall, as Yujin-ssi said. To be exact, he was going to fortify it."

I looked down again. There was no trace of people at all.

"There's no one here?"

"The problem was supplies. The sector's location was in the middle of nowhere, it wasn't big enough to be self-sufficient, and there were no other merits."

We crossed the second wall. Inside the second wall, there were still traces of people living there. Crude-looking wooden houses and piles of garbage on one side.

"Still, they used it as a hideout in the early days. As the Black Blood Alliance's power grew, there was no reason to stay here."

We crossed the third wall. In the center of the sector, there was a large five-story building. It was a palace built in the Chinese style. The exterior was quite magnificent. Unlike the inside of the first and second walls, I could feel the presence of people.

"But why is the Black Blood Alliance so insistent on this sector? They could move their base to another place."

The Black Blood Alliance controlled many sectors besides this one.

"I don't know about that. Maybe the Black Blood Alliance Lord is attached to it because it's the first place he ever controlled?"

"Well, maybe."

Li Mei and I landed on the ground. People with weapons rushed in and surrounded us.

"It's Li Mei!"

"I heard she betrayed us, but I didn't expect her to show up so brazenly."

"Did she come back to apologize to the Alliance Lord and beg for forgiveness?"

"Her beauty is still the same even after all this time. No matter what, Li Mei will offer her body to the Alliance Lord this time."

"That guy wearing the jester mask... isn't that the Cheonma who's famous in the west recently?"

Chinese people rushed in. They were just chattering among themselves, but no one stepped forward. There was no one in charge.

I put my hand on Li Mei's shoulder.

"Li Mei. You're popular, huh?"

"It's not exactly something I want..."

Li Mei clicked her tongue. She wasn't intimidated by the gazes of countless men. I didn't take my hand off Li Mei's shoulder. I had to protect her in case something happened. And it was quite fun to show off Li Mei to the men. Some of the jealous gazes towards me even contained murderous intent.

The person in charge appeared. It was a man wearing black armor and equipped with a large glaive. The man, who was missing one ear, stood proudly in our way.

"It's Sal-on Daeju-nim. It's been a while. We've come to see the Alliance Lord. Would you mind stepping aside?"

Sal-on Dae. The Black Blood Alliance Lord's personal guard.

"That won't do. I haven't received any instructions from the Alliance Lord. I've just sent someone to the Alliance Lord, so please wait a moment. But who is the masked man next to you? Is he your lover?"

"What if he is my lover?"

"Don't be so sensitive, Li Mei. You're a smart woman. I don't think you betrayed the Black Blood Alliance."

"I appreciate you trusting me. If I didn't see the weapon in your hand, I would have trusted Sal-on Daeju-nim too. And the person next to me... I think you can guess, right? The masked ones. Aren't they famous enough?"

"Hmm..."

Sal-on Daeju groaned as he looked at me. Leaving aside the Cheonma's reputation, if he's an executive of the Black Blood Alliance, he must have heard about Helten. Helten's executives basically wear masks when they're active.

Sal-on Daeju closed his mouth. A silent confrontation ensued.

'If he doesn't move in 10 minutes, I'll have to force my way in. That Sal-on Daeju guy is no pushover, and there are a lot of small fries too... but I don't think I'll lose.'

Before 10 minutes passed, Sal-on Daeju's subordinate appeared. The subordinate whispered something in Sal-on Daeju's ear.

"The Alliance Lord has allowed you to enter the palace. I will guide you myself. Follow me."

Sal-on Daeju turned around. The ones who were surrounding us lowered their weapons. Li Mei and I followed Sal-on Daeju into the palace.

"Entering the palace... Does the Black Blood Alliance Lord think he's a king or something?"

"Even if he's not a king, he probably thinks he's a noble, right?"

I can't fool my eyes, at least, as an actual noble of the [Baekhwan] world. The interior of the palace was lacking.

'The exterior looks pretty good, but the interior is poorly managed. There are a few servants walking around, but they're clumsy. That's proof that they haven't been trained.'

This palace is just a facade.

We arrived at a large space. Judging by the atmosphere, it seemed like an audience chamber.

A man was sitting on a large chair. He was an obese man who looked like he easily weighed over 130kg. Short black hair drooped over his chubby cheeks. He had a smile on his face, so his impression was surprisingly quite good.

He was the Black Blood Alliance Lord, Wang Xuan.

"Welcome, Cheonma. I never thought the Cheonma of the west would be affiliated with Helten."

"You know me?"

"You're quite famous."

It had been quite some time since I had taken control of a part of the western desert, so it wasn't strange that the rumors had reached this place. Especially since the leader of an organization like the Black Blood Alliance Lord would be sensitive to information.

"I've prepared a feast for you."

"Do you think I came here to enjoy a feast?"

"...I wanted to show you the essence of Chinese cuisine... It's a shame."

"Let's get straight to the point. From now on, the position of Black Blood Alliance Lord belongs to Li Mei. You step down from that position and get out of here."

Sal-on Daeju's body twitched. I didn't let my guard down on him and waited for Wang Xuan's answer.

"Is there a reason why I should follow those words?"

"Let's make it easy. You don't want to die either, do you?"

"...To be honest, I doubt that you're really an executive of Helten."

"The one who was in charge of the Black Blood Alliance was originally Mirror."

"...That's right. Mirror Mask. Where did that woman whose inner thoughts you couldn't read go, and why did you come?"

"I've decided to take charge of the Black Blood Alliance. Don't doubt it. You know very well what the end is for those who impersonate Helten."

"......"

Wang Xuan sighed. He looked around once.

"...The Black Blood Alliance grew with the help of Helten. It was an unavoidable choice for the Black Blood Alliance's rapid growth and stabilization. Looking back now, it was the only blemish."

"So. What are you trying to say?"

"I will fix that blemish. The Black Blood Alliance will remain only as the Black Blood Alliance."

I let out a chuckle.

"You don't know anything about Helten."

Why is Helten even called a legend? It's because there's no force in this Atlantis that can handle Helten. Even the Ustia Empire tried to wipe out Helten but failed.

"I'll return those words to you. You don't know anything about the Black Blood Alliance."

Kkiiiiiiiiiiing!

"Kyaaaaaaak!"

Li Mei, who was next to me, grabbed her head and screamed, collapsing to the floor. Her face was distorted with pain.

"The Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation has been activated."

「Resisting some of the effects of the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation with superior mental strength.」

「All stats decreased by 20%.」

「Efficiency of skills and traits decreased by 15%.」

「Damage taken increased by 10%.」

It was a debuff party. Seeing Li Mei collapsed on the floor and complaining of pain, it seemed like it was even doing mental attacks.

Kkiiiiiiiiiiing. Kkiiiiiiiiiiing.

I looked around. Waves were emanating from something embedded in the ceiling, floor, and pillars. That was probably the identity of the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation.

"You're indeed amazing. Not moving an inch. But you're already in the center of the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation. Sal-on Daeju. Kill him. Leave Li Mei alone. She has to pay the price for her betrayal separately."

"Understood."

Sal-on Daeju rushed in. I summoned Stormbreaker in the form of a sword and blocked his glaive. Kaang. Unable to withstand his tremendous power, I was pushed back.

'...I'm being pushed back in pure strength?'

It was surprising. I'm in the Cheonma state right now. My physical abilities are buffed with Cheonma's Body and Cheonma Shingong. It doesn't make sense that I'm being pushed back so easily.

"You seem surprised. Let me tell you one thing, Sal-on Daeju's contracted divine seat is the Hegemon-King of Western Chu."

Hegemon-King of Western Chu refers to Xiang Yu. In Atlantis, Xiang Yu's contractors are quite famous. Xiang Yu only transfers one skill to his contractors.

'Lift Mountains and Overpower the World (力拔山氣蓋世). A cheat skill that doubles strength stats and further boosts certain strength attributes during combat.'

There are also drawbacks. Other stats decrease during combat.

'Even with those penalties, it's the best skill for warriors.'

I decided to give up on overwhelming Sal-on Daeju with strength and focus on technique.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma's Descent Footwork, Waveform.

I stepped on Jingak.

An invisible shockwave spread in all directions. The formation shook.

「The effect of the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation is temporarily reduced.」

Twitch.

Sal-on Daeju's momentum was slightly broken. Unlike giving me debuffs, the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation definitely gave him buffs.

I rushed at him and swung my sword with rotational force.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Sword, Return to Heaven.

Sal-on Daeju was pushed back. A drop of sweat ran down his cheek.

"Cough. Amazing power and sword. What's your strength stat?"

"Do you think I'll tell you?"

"There's no reason not to tell me, is there? In the end, a fight isn't about numbers, it's about the stronger guy winning. My strength is 135. It's the strength that's increased by the skill's effect."

"That's true. 106."

"...You're a monster."

He let out a sigh.

Stat 30.

The difference was almost absolute. It was close to the difference between a child and an adult. But despite the 30 stat difference, I wasn't being pushed back by him. Considering the influence of the Eight Gates Golden Lock Formation, it was natural for him to be shocked.

The glaive fell from above. I blocked it by holding the sword sideways and rotating it. The glaive was deflected to the side. A gap was created in Sal-on Daeju. I was about to swing my sword without missing that gap when I felt a presence falling from the ceiling.

'An assassin. The target is... not me, but Li Mei!'

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 4]

I cut off the head of the assassin targeting Li Mei.

"Foolish. What's so important about that woman?"

I felt the condensed killing intent and power from Sal-on Daeju, who had taken my rear.
