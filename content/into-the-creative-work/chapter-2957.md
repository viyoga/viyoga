I killed Shahram, the Leader of Ohel. This battle was over. It should have been.

‘Why isn’t the notification window saying I’ve become the Dominator of the sector appearing?’

When you kill a sector Dominator, the killer acquires that Domination.

That is common sense in Atlantis.

‘…Wasn’t Shahram the Dominator of the Black Sector?’

It’s not common, but it happens sometimes.

Putting forward a proxy for the sector Dominator.

Originally, sector Dominators are targeted.

I looked at Shahram’s Corpse, then surveyed the surrounding Corpses of prisoners and Spellcasters. The smell of blood permeated the air. And still, this place was dark, and the corridor was long.

‘What did Shahram say earlier? Mahir will resurrect you? Mahir. That name sounds familiar.’

As I repeated the name for a moment, it came to mind.

Mahir.

‘The Sage of the Desert. Mahir.’

A Grand Spellcaster among Grand Spellcasters.

‘In terms of reputation, he’s a more formidable bastard than the footless cripple Shahram. It wouldn’t be strange if he were the true Dominator of this sector.’

Looking at it this way, it was strange that the Spellcasters blindly followed Shahram. It was all because Mahir was there that the Spellcasters followed Shahram.

‘The fact that he’s my enemy doesn’t change anyway.’

Even if I don’t know his exact location, he must be nearby.

I went outside the building.

As soon as I stepped outside, I was greeted by a thick fog and a fishy smell that stung my nostrils.

It was a clearly abnormal situation. The fog hadn’t been there until just now. Also, the fishy smell hadn’t been this strong. The fishy smell was so intense it made me reluctant to even breathe.

More importantly, the Baekhwadan and the enemy soldiers, who had been fighting each other just moments ago, were now not fighting. Instead, they were moving around together, dealing with something. The enemy soldiers didn’t look like they had surrendered.

“Heavenly Demon-nim!”

Enas rushed towards me, panting. I scanned her Body up and down, checking her condition. She was drenched in sweat, but had no wounds.

“Enas. What’s going on? Why has the battle stopped?”

“Mermen have appeared from the sea and are attacking! We are temporarily cooperating with Ohel’s soldiers to respond!”

“So you judged the Mermen to be more dangerous.”

It was surprising how quickly the tide of battle had turned in such a short time. Enas and I moved towards the castle gate. I climbed onto the gate and checked outside. The thick fog couldn’t completely obscure my vision.

The residents of the port City were seen rushing towards the palace. They were running desperately for their lives, unable to even grab proper belongings. Behind them, Mermen with disgusting appearances, as if combining the forms of fish and humans, were giving chase. Though slow, their strength was powerful enough to tear a human Body apart.

The Baekhwadan and soldiers rushed out, confronting the Mermen and rescuing people.

“Heavenly Demon-nim! If you command, I will attack the enemy soldiers! If we attack now, while they’re distracted, we can inflict critical damage.”

“There’s no need for that. Shahram is dead. The residents living here are now mine. Don’t kill them recklessly. The taxes I need to collect will decrease. Moreover, each and every one of them is labor.”

“I almost dared to destroy Heavenly Demon-nim’s property. I apologize.”

“Even so, I’ll have to kill a few as an example. Especially Ohel’s Spellcasters, without exception, all of them will die.”

“All the Spellcasters currently here have been killed. Unlike the soldiers, they resisted to the very end. They seemed like blind Fanatics. Even in the Cheonma Shin-gyo, Fanatics are rare.”

“Well done.”

The Cheonma Shin-gyo of this world, despite its name, didn’t have a strong religious color. It was too large to be called a Legion, and too small to be called a nation.

‘The power of religion is weak in Atlantis. Even though there are Gods, there are so many of them, and Atlantis ended up in this state because of the Gods.’

Of course, it’s not entirely absent. Because Gods actually exist, there are also those who depend even more deeply on them.

From atop the castle gate, I looked towards the sea. I couldn’t clearly see the sea. It wasn’t a fog issue. Something else was blocking my vision.

‘This is similar to before. They’re blocking my vision with Spells.’

I looked towards the sea and swallowed dryly.

Within the thick fog, a colossal shadow was seen moving.

‘It’s getting closer. Whatever that is, I have no intention of avoiding it.’

Mahir must be by the sea. Even if he is a Grand Spellcaster known as the Sage of the Desert, he wouldn’t be able to do this remotely.

‘Should I go and scout it out first? I’ll set the coordinates here so I can escape with a spatial movement Document if necessary.’

「A special power envelops the sector. Spatial movement Documents cannot be used.」

The spatial movement Document was blocked.

Presumably, escaping outside the sector and entering the sector from outside were also blocked.

‘That bastard intends to finish this here.’

I pondered for a moment, looking at the foggy sea. Should I go alone? Or should I lead my troops?

‘This isn’t a war. Unless I’m taking them as Meat shields, it would be more convenient to go alone.’

Even the Baekhwadan could hinder me. Going alone was better.

As soon as I made my decision, I gave a command to Enas.

“Enas. Guard this palace until I kill Mahir.”

“Understood, Heavenly Demon-nim. I will definitely protect it with my Barrier.”

I squeezed Enas’s Breasts once, then leaped from atop the castle gate. Treading on air, I headed towards the seashore.

Upon arriving at the port, the first thing that greeted me was the fishy smell. It was an overwhelming fishy smell, so potent that anyone with a slightly weak stomach would have immediately vomited.

The ships that should have been docked in the port were all shattered, drifting on the sea. In their place, an enormous number of Mermen climbed onto the port. Each time they moved, the fishy smell wafted.

The Mermen, walking towards the palace, suddenly stopped, then glared at me in the air and screamed.

“Kyaaaaaaaak!”

Inside their gaping, massive Mouths, saw-like, pointed Teeth were visible.

They stretched their Hands towards me.

However, realizing they couldn’t reach with their Hands, they jumped. They leaped nearly 2 meters high. Still, they couldn’t reach me. So, they grabbed nearby stones, Items, and branches, and threw them at me.

Among them, some bastards even threw pitch-black seawater.

‘Is that a Spell? Whatever else it is, that black seawater looks a bit dangerous.’

Blocking the incoming attacks with my protective aura, I took a step in the air.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Gunrimbo, Wave Form.

From the foot that stepped on air, a wave of Energy spread to the ground.

Hundreds of Mermen, touched by the wave of Energy, dared not resist and exploded with a <em>pung</em> sound. Rotten, fishy-smelling blood and Intestines covered the port City.

「You acquire 1 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

「You acquire 1 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

「You acquire 1 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

「You acquire 15 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

「You acquire 1 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

「You acquire 6 AP by defeating a Deep-Sea Dweller.」

I casually checked the countless notification windows that popped up and dismissed them. Most were 1 AP. This meant there was almost no individual difference.

‘I absolutely shouldn’t go down to the ground.’

Cheol-peok! New Mermen crawled out of the sea. These bastards showed no hesitation even when stepping on the Corpses of their own kind.

It seemed fear was an emotion unknown to them.

“You’ve massacred them.”

An old voice was heard from the sea. I frowned and glared towards the sea. I could pierce through the fog, but the depths of the sea were impossible. A special power in the sea was obscuring my vision.

“They are all residents of the sea. Do you truly not fear the wrath of the sea? Can you not feel that he is watching you?”

“Is that Mahir? Stop spouting nonsense and show yourself. Or will you only come out if I bombard this sea?”

“Great Dominator of the desert, Heavenly Demon. You are as arrogant as I heard. But even that desert is less than a handful compared to the sea.”

An old man floated up onto the sea.

From the two eyes of the turban-wearing old man, a deep blue light emanated.

His white beard, reaching down to his neck, was curly, and in his left Hand, he held a wooden Staff taller than himself.

His Body, having risen from the sea, was not wet in the slightest.

Cheonma Shingong, Dragon Fist.

I thrust my Fist towards the bastard.

Compressed Demonic Energy/Qi shot out from my Fist, extending towards the enemy as if fired.

The seawater surged, blocking the Dragon Fist. I was a bit surprised. To think mere seawater could block my attack.

“Is that a Spell too?”

“Spell? Since there are Spells that borrow the power of other beings, it’s not wrong.”

“What have you done? Did the Emperor give you power?”

“What the Emperor promised us was not power, but peace. Ohel desired Gabriel’s revelation. I, too, had believed this to be the righteous thing without a doubt.”

The sea swirled around Mahir.

“‘Had believed’? Are you saying it’s different now?”

“It is different. Two hours ago, I was a Blind man who couldn’t open his eyes, and now I am a chosen person who has opened his eyes. How could they be the same?”

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Gunrimbo.

I stepped on air. The Energy of Cheonma Gunrimbo focused solely on Mahir. Yet, Mahir didn’t waver. He was composed. As if he had realized something, not even a hint of Enchantment was felt.

“Are you trying to shake my Mind/Spirit and heart? It’s useless. I have obtained forbidden knowledge from an ancient connection, and I am one who offered everything to the God of the sea and received. My Mind/Spirit is connected to the crouching sea, so how could the sea be swayed by mere demons?”

“That’s what you’ve ‘realized’?”

“I will tell you the end and the truth of this world. This world will sink into the sea.”

“That Atlantis will sink into the sea is a famous story. It’s nothing new.”

“I’m not talking about the continent. This world, this universe, countless stars will sink into the sea.”

I thought I could get some meaningful information, but it turned out to be a waste of time.

“Alright, you psycho.”

Pajijijijik!

I charged towards the bastard, emitting Black Lightning from my entire Body.
