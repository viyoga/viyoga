I stepped out of the crumbling Imperial Palace.

A one-sided battle was unfolding. Sun Wukong's clones were overwhelming the yokai. The gap in individual capability was vast, and even when the yokai banded together, they couldn't match the seamless coordination of Sun Wukong's clones, who shared the same essence.

"Uwaaaaaaah!"

Crack!

A kappa yokai's skull shattered under the Ruyi Jingu Bang. Blood and brain matter sprayed in every direction, its corpse sprawling like garbage. The stench of blood saturated the air.

'So human warriors are no different from yokai.'

Mercy toward humans?

There was no such thing.

Even when yokai dropped to their knees and begged for their lives, Sun Wukong's relentless bludgeoning never stopped.

At this point in time, Sun Wukong was still in the early stages of the Journey to the West, so it seemed he hadn't yet been tamed by Tripitaka. He freely unleashed his savage nature.

'No need for me to step in. Even if an infamous yokai occasionally overwhelms one of Sun Wukong's clones... the other clones swarm in unison, swinging their Ruyi Jingu Bangs, and the bastard dies without putting up any real fight.'

Sun Wukong's clone technique was, without question, a broken-ass skill.

I leaped toward the sky. My body didn't fall back to the ground. The wind buoyed me, pushing me upward.

Where mountains should have stood, everything had been pulverized by the aftermath of the battle between the real Sun Wukong and Yamata no Orochi.

Traces of a colossal being thrashing about, craters from the impact of massive, heavy objects crashing down, and rivers of Yamata no Orochi's lethal venom flowing in place of streams. As for the venom—just being near it, without even touching it, caused plants to rot instantly and humans and yokai alike to drop dead.

The battle between Sun Wukong and Yamata no Orochi had reached a stalemate. Yamata no Orochi was spewing venom like a madman from five of its eight heads, so even the great Sun Wukong couldn't approach easily.

The giant Sun Wukong spoke without taking his eyes off Yamata no Orochi for even a moment.

"Oh. You're here? What about that fox?"

"Killed her. Why the hell is that bastard throwing another tantrum?"

"Kehehe. He got smacked around a few times with the Ruyi Jingu Bang and ended up like that. Think you can win? Want this body to help you to the end?"

"I'll win. That bastard has to die by my hand."

"Alright. Then this body will just watch from the sky. One of my favorite things is watching a good fight!"

The giant Sun Wukong did a somersault. His body shrank back to its original size in an instant, and the Somersault Cloud appeared to catch him.

"My mouth is bored. Got anything to drink? Something like future food, maybe."

I couldn't deny that I'd received his help, so I decided to oblige. From my inventory, I ejected a bulk-sized bag of popcorn and a box of cold beer cans toward Sun Wukong. He caught them without batting an eye.

"Hooh. Popcorn and beer? Future food sure is fascinating!"

With a single sweep of his Fiery Golden Eyes, he'd instantly discerned the name and purpose of the items.

Leaving Sun Wukong to his reaction, I stepped in front of Yamata no Orochi. The half-mad Yamata no Orochi spotted me and bellowed.

"Susanoo! You cowardly piece of shit not even worth chewing on! You bring a monster like that from outside?! And you call yourself the god of this land after that?!"

A smirk slipped out before I could stop it. Even as it raged at me, it was keeping an eye on Sun Wukong. No matter how mountain-sized its bulk, a snake was still a snake.

"For a snake with no fangs worth chewing on, you sure do run your mouth. Seems the Great Sage's beating knocked some sense into you. Don't worry. You won't have to fear the Ruyi Jingu Bang anymore. Because you're dying here today."

"You? Me? You ran in fear from me and brought a monster, and now you run your mouth! Are you not fleeing because you're counting on that monster behind you?!"

Sun Wukong, who had drained a beer can to an empty husk in one gulp, shouted toward Yamata no Orochi.

"Eight-Headed Bastard! If you win, this old timer won't lift a finger and will head back to the continent! I swear it on the name of the Great Sage Equal to Heaven, Sun Wukong! So fight with everything you've got!!"

I shrugged.

"You heard him. All you have to do is win."

"That monster ape abandoned you!"

Three snake heads shot toward me.

I unleashed Susanoo's power. Having killed Japan's Three Great Evil Spirits and grown stronger, it was incomparable to before.

I gripped the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido tightly. Wind rose and spun around the blade.

Between the whirling winds, flecks of red light flickered intermittently. Hwaryeon Bido's attribute enhancement ability had activated.

The spinning wind compressed into the blade as if drawn into a black hole. Without hesitation, I swung.

A blade of wind flew out and sliced through three of Yamata no Orochi's heads. Yamata no Orochi instantly concentrated its yoki and regenerated the severed heads in the blink of an eye.

"You bastard...!"

Yamata no Orochi no longer charged at me recklessly. Having witnessed that power just now, it had begun to regard me with caution.

"Let me tell you in advance. That attack just now was a basic hit... no, more like a heavy attack. It wasn't a proper technique."

As I spoke, I thought.

Yamata no Orochi's regeneration was on the level of immortality. However, regenerating consumed an immense amount of yoki. The problem was Yamata no Orochi's sheer scale. I said immense, but from its perspective, it wasn't that much of a drain. Even the consumed yoki would replenish itself after a short time.

'Going for the single torso instead of the heads... hmm. Not a great choice. Most of its yoki is concentrated in the torso. It'd probably regenerate even faster than the heads.'

Whoooosh!

Yamata no Orochi opened its mouths wide toward the sky and spewed venom. Green liquid and fumes instantly blanketed the heavens. It intended to drown this entire space in poison.

'It used its head, at least.'

[You have absorbed poison. You resist the poison.]

With Poison Resistance (Master), I took the poison head-on instead of dodging. I staggered, poisoned. I nearly plummeted to the ground. A lethal venom potent enough to leave me momentarily stunned, even for an instant.

[You have absorbed poison. You resist the poison.]

But my body shook off the poisoned state at a rapid pace.

Normally, resistance wouldn't develop this quickly. It was possible because Susanoo's power was reinforcing my body.

[You have absorbed poison. Health and mana are recovering.]

The green liquid falling from the sky was now nothing short of a natural potion to me.

'Yamata no Orochi's yoki looks infinite at a glance... but it's not actually infinite. There's a limit. The problem is that my power will hit its limit faster than Yamata no Orochi reaches its own.'

I was still outmatched in sheer scale.

"My poison doesn't work on you...?!"

Yamata no Orochi clamped its mouths shut in bewilderment. The venom spray stopped. It wouldn't be using poison anymore. That was bad news for me.

'It still holds the advantage in scale. The longer this drags on, the more my odds of winning plummet. I go for a short decisive battle. With Susanoo's power at full capacity right now, I might be able to use that.'

Open Hwaryeon Bido's domain.

[Activating Genius's Time for 10 seconds.]

[Activating Heaven's Heart. Duration: 1 minute.]

Through Heaven's Heart's effect, my physical abilities were temporarily amplified and my mana became infinite.

Crackle...!

I used mana including the power of Susanoo, a god's power, along with the domain.

Kiiiiiiing.

A halo of lightning blazed behind my back. It became a brilliantly shining ring of blue light, rotating and growing sharper by the moment. In that instant, the ring's rotation halted and screeched.

'...Not enough.'

Even with my current power, I couldn't manifest it.

A god's mind, body, and karma.

Among these, the body and karma hadn't reached the threshold.

Kiiiiiiiiiiiiing!

The halo that had been about to scatter suddenly took shape and began rotating stably.

Thunder Heaven Style Divine Intent: Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel.

'It succeeded? Why?'

With Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel's success, my transcendent perception and senses allowed me to grasp the answer immediately.

'This world is helping me.'

Every current of the world was moving as if favoring me.

Then what was the cause of this favor?

I traced the world's currents, the power aiding me, backward.

And I found it.

Somewhere in the cosmos.

A scale, tilted to one side, came into view.

I understood that it was the source of the status window, and karma itself.

"You bastard, Susanoo...! What is that power?! What the hell have you done?!"

The voice was filled with rage, but I saw through it—fear lurked within. It too had realized that my current power made no sense.

"I don't have time to explain to you."

I could only maintain Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel while Heaven's Heart was active. In terms of time, less than fifty seconds remained.

Kiiiiiiing.

Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel burned karma, generating an immense amount of energy. Of course, this karma was not karmic merit. It was the karma of the being known as Sung Yujin.

'Even if I use it all, no regrets. With full recovery, even a destroyed soul returns to normal.'

I flicked my finger.

A colossal lightning bolt crashed down from the sky to the earth.

Lightning is originally a flash that begins in an instant and ends in an instant. But the bolt that struck now began in an instant and did not end in an instant—it remained.

The pillar of lightning connecting heaven and earth moved toward Yamata no Orochi at my gesture.

Yamata no Orochi's entire body burned within the pillar of lightning that would not disappear.

"Kraaaaaaah! You think I'd fall to a mere lightning bolt!!"

Yamata no Orochi endured within the lightning.

I lightly swung Hwaryeon Bido toward it.

Thunder Heaven Style Ultimate Technique: Heaven's Light.

A line of blue light was drawn through space. The space the line of light passed through was severed as it was. The lines of light that passed through Yamata no Orochi's body numbered twelve in total.

The twelve lines carved Yamata no Orochi to pieces. Its eight heads, torso, and tails were all chopped into segments. Immediately after, Yamata no Orochi's yoki exploded and the segmented flesh rewound like time reversing, regenerating.

"You think I'll go down like this?! Even if I die, I'll take you with me!!"

Yamata no Orochi opened its eight mouths wide toward me.

Thunder Heaven Style Ultimate Technique: Blast Quake Thunder.

The pillar of lightning exploded. Needless to say, Yamata no Orochi, caught within it, was swept up in the blast. Yamata no Orochi once again displayed that absurd regeneration.

"The source of your regeneration isn't your physical body—it's yoki. In other words, once I deal with that yoki, it's over."

Thunder Heaven Style Ultimate Technique: Seal Thunder.

Lightning generated from my fingertips pierced Yamata no Orochi's heart. Yamata no Orochi's yoki was temporarily sealed. That was the end. It couldn't regenerate and was annihilated.

And in the place where Yamata no Orochi had been, a single sword stood planted in the ground.
