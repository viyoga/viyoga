The Seongjin Church had a hierarchy.

Ordinary believers and the priestly class.

To become a priest, one had to dedicate everything to the Seongjin Church and Seong Yujin-nim. Their own property, and even their own families. They were fundamentally fanatics.

Originally, the priests were intended to be Seong Yujin-nim's bodyguards, but they were those who had failed due to a lack of physical talent. Seong Yujin-nim's bodyguards were the elite among the elite. Even if they armed their minds with faith, they could not become bodyguards without talent.

However, it would be a waste to abandon them just because they couldn't become bodyguards. Therefore, they became priests. Their main job was to become missionaries and spread the word about the Seongjin Church.

They didn't openly proselytize. Just because they were fanatics didn't mean they were stupid. Forcing people to believe in the Seongjin Church would only get them treated like a Cult.

The missionary priests first sought out the vulnerable. Even when the world went to shit, the impoverished still existed. They lured them in by providing them with necessities, including food.

At first, they pretended to believe in the Seongjin Church to get necessities. A religion that worships a human as a god? It looked like a Cult to anyone. But constant brainwashing and the kindness of the Seongjin Church gradually made them fall for the Seongjin Church.

They helped those in need and attracted the marginalized. They didn't hesitate to use honey traps. The truly admirable beauties were offered to Seong Yujin-nim... but the mediocre beauties focused on proselytizing. Even ordinary women could cover up their looks with makeup.

Armed with full makeup from experts, they seduced men. Even men with girlfriends, even men who knew it was a Cult, couldn't help but be drawn in. Men were sad creatures.

In this way, the Seongjin Church increased the number of its believers and expanded its area of activity through continuous proselytizing. But it was only in Korea. This couldn't be helped. Right now, South Korea was blocked from going abroad. Only cargo ships moved, and aircraft could only be used with the permission of the state. Overseas travel? Impossible in an age overrun by zombies. And no other country would accept foreigners.

Didn't the police crack down on Cult religions?

Not all religions are Cults. South Korea has freedom of religion, and religious faith doesn't have to be Christianity, Buddhism, or Catholicism. The Seongjin Church didn't openly commit illegal acts, so it wasn't branded as a Cult religion.

And as the world went to shit, Cult religions sprang up like mushrooms after the rain. The Seongjin Church was relatively moderate among the small and medium-sized religions.

The things that believers offered to the religion were, after all, voluntary donations. They didn't exactly threaten them. Even if they were ignored by other believers if they didn't donate. In any case, the Seongjin Church was slowly increasing its influence like a turtle.

Then something happened.

Seong Yujin-nim revealed some of her recovery ability to the public. The public shouted Seong Yujin-nim's name and went wild. The Seongjin Church was calm. Their god had simply shown her abilities to the ignorant. There was no reason to make a fuss.

However, proselytizing became incredibly easy. No, could this even be called proselytizing?

They didn't even proselytize, but people came to them wanting to become believers on their own.

Three days ago, there were 50 believers, two days ago there were 80, yesterday there were 110, and today there were 150.

They had something in common. They or their families were suffering from illnesses or were disabled. They knocked on the door of the Seongjin Church, which worshiped Seong Yujin-nim as a god, with the expectation that they could meet Seong Yujin-nim and receive treatment.

The Seongjin Church priests naturally said it was possible.

,In order to meet that person directly and receive her grace, you must actively participate as a believer.

The priest spoke in front of 150 believers. Behind him, a portrait of Seong Yujin-nim hung on the wall. The believers knelt in front of the portrait. They reverently clasped their hands together and listened to the priest's words.

,Min Hapseung-ssi. Please come forward.

,Yes. Priest Kim-nim.

Min Hapseung, a middle-aged man, went up to the pulpit. The priest pointed at him and continued.

,Min Hapseung-ssi donated 80% of his entire fortune and has been steadily carrying out proselytizing activities, informing 60 people of who the true savior is. He also sent his daughters to Seoul Seongak Elementary School to encourage them to volunteer. Seong-nim was so impressed by Min Hapseung-ssi's sincerity that she personally bestowed her grace upon him. Isn't that right?

,That's right. I met that person directly and received her grace. Some of you may know me. I was paralyzed in my lower body due to a traffic accident. I couldn't even go to the nearby convenience store without a wheelchair and someone else's help. But now I'm standing on my own two feet. It's all thanks to the grace bestowed by Seong-nim! Seong-nim is my savior and the savior of this world!

The believers watched Min Hapseung. Their eyes filled with fervor.

Seong Yujin-nim's volunteer hours were short. It wasn't even an hour a day, and no more than five patients benefited from it. Just going to Seoul didn't mean you could receive Seong Yujin-nim's treatment.

People looked for other ways, and that was the Seongjin Church. By donating money to the Seongjin Church and engaging in proselytizing activities, they could receive Seong Yujin-nim's treatment. At least it was more realistic than just going up to Seoul.

The priest sent Min Hapseung back and called a young child up to the pulpit. The child, who was not even 10 years old, stood there looking nervous. The priest smiled kindly and stroked the child's shoulder. The child's mother knelt next to him and prayed to Seong Yujin-nim.

,This child was a zombie until a month ago. This child's mother stole Satan's technology to save her child and turned in Satan's subordinates.

In the Seongjin Church, Satan refers to Kim Sukyeol, the Chairman of the Ilwoo Group, who is Seong Yujin-nim's enemy. More broadly, it referred to the Ilwoo Group itself.

The child's mother was a researcher at the Ilwoo Group. She stole the Ilwoo Group's technology and offered it to Seong Yujin-nim, and in return, she saved her zombie child as a normal person. From next week, she will go up to Seongak Elementary School with her child and work as a researcher.

,That sincerity moved Seong-nim. Everyone. Bring information about Satan and the demons. You can punish Satan directly. Killing demons is not a sin. Although the ignorant police will arrest you... your family will be taken care of by our Seongjin Church and Seong-nim. Satan deserves to die.

The believers' eyes shone. Especially the young believers. They had children. A young child who had become a zombie. A child they couldn't bear to kill and had locked up. That child could see the light again. By killing one Ilwoo Group employee.

In South Korea, murder is punishable by imprisonment for at least seven years. The Seongjin Church will provide a competent lawyer, so you only have to live in prison for about 10 years.

Wouldn't they kill a demon for their child?

Ilwoo Group employees were dying all over the country. Ilwoo Group's partners were no exception.

The believers deliberately approached them and started fights, or disguised it as an accident and killed them. And they went to the police station and confessed that it was an accidental crime. Of course, they didn't bring up the Seongjin Church's saint. They just said they killed them accidentally.

The police, prosecutors, and judges looked suspicious, but they glossed over it. Some of them were members of the Seongjin Church.

Stories related to the Seongjin Church were slowly leaking out on the internet, but that was it. Right now, South Korea's attention was focused on North Korea and the United States.

And Seong Yujin-nim became a member of the National Assembly by special law.

Equipped with a National Assembly badge, I thought of my next goal.

President.

But this isn't easy.

Unlike the National Assembly members, who have almost no real power in South Korea right now, the president has more power than ever before. It couldn't be helped, since it was close to a wartime system. The threat from North Korea and the big and small problems within Korea.

,If I kill the president, wouldn't I be able to become president within a year? ...Hmm. That's a bit difficult, isn't it.

I needed a support base. Fortunately, the Seongjin Church is expanding rapidly. On top of that, they're killing the Ilwoo Group vermin. Recently, Ilwoo Group technicians have been flocking to Seongak Elementary School. I needed technicians anyway, so I accepted most of them. Anyone suspected of being a spy was mercilessly killed and put into the disassembler to be used as resources.

,I heard there are problems with the Seongjin Church too.

Those who falsely join the church.

Those who only receive benefits after joining the church and then betray it.

,Where do they think they're going to run off to? Absolutely not. I need to pick out inquisitors separately and deal with them.

Kugugugugung.

The ground shakes. Suddenly, an earthquake occurred. An earthquake of about magnitude 1-2. It wasn't dangerous. Just enough for things placed on the table to shake and fall.

,There have been frequent earthquakes lately. I heard most of them occurred in Japan.

I heard that earthquakes of magnitude 5 or higher are occurring in Japan. The earthquakes felt in Korea are a kind of aftershock.

,As time goes on, the world will become more of a mess. I need to gather as much technology and resources as possible now.

The D virus isn't the only problem in this world. The 128 meteorites that fell on this world had a major and minor impact on the Earth. The D virus is just one of those effects. Natural disasters will continue to occur in the future. In the original game, natural disasters played a role in adding variables to the game. In other words, a matter of luck.

,For now, it looks like Japan is fucked since earthquakes keep happening. Mount Fuji could erupt... or a tsunami could sweep it away.

In the worst-case scenario, there would be a tectonic shift. The Japanese archipelago could move and stick right next to Korea. Would Korea and Japan get along well then? Absolutely not. Japan, which was already quietly preparing for an invasion, would gladly declare war.

,No. Or would they not declare war? Like Pearl Harbor.

And the variants and mutations are evolving. For now, the sea and air routes are open, but... who knows what will happen as time goes on. Giant predators will appear in the sea and capsize ships, and the sky will become the domain of a new ruler.

,Korea will be isolated. Korea with its sea routes blocked. Just imagining it is terrifying.

South Korea was a country with a trade dependency of nearly 80%. If trade is blocked, it's over. Even now, the economy was plummeting.

The earthquake stopped. I got up from my seat. I had an appointment.
