I saw a cave.

It wasn't a large cave. But a gloomy atmosphere emanated from it.

I narrowed my eyes and stared at the cave. The inside was dark. No matter how hard I strained my eyes, I couldn't see inside.

However, wind blew from the cave. The wind grabbed my bangs, shook them, and passed by.

I felt unpleasant.

It was as if the wind was measuring me.

,The wind carries killing intent.'

I drew my sword from my waist. The blade reflected the sunlight and sparkled.

,...Did you come from the cult?'

A man's voice was heard from the cave, along with the wind.

,Are you Sakpung Gwigyeom (Wind-Slicing Ghost Sword) Jeong Bae, by any chance?'

,That's right. It seems you're from the cult.'

A man walked out of the cave. He was dressed like a beggar. His clothes were old, and his beard was unkempt. His face was gaunt, as if he hadn't eaten properly. The strange thing was that, unlike his gaunt face, his body was muscular.

A physical condition that didn't make sense. It meant only one thing.

,He has reached the ranks of a master. In other words, he has definitely reached the realm of Origin Energy.'

I wasn't surprised because I had expected it.

,What is your name?'

I felt the direction of the wind subtly change. Instinctively, I spread my energy. The wind pushed back against my energy, interfering with it. Still, I found the starting point of the wind. The wind was blowing from the sword Jeong Bae held in his hand.

,I am Yeom Gu-seok of the Annihilation Squad's Lightning Flash Division.'

,Annihilation Squad... You've come to kill me from a terrifying place. Won't you just pretend you didn't see me? I'm leaving this place right now. We never ran into each other.'

,Why should I do that?'

,Aren't you also burdened by having a life-or-death battle with me? Even if I die, I don't plan on just dying. I'll struggle until the very end.'

,If you escape, where do you plan to go?'

,I will defect to the Martial Alliance.'

That's not a bad plan.

Even if he's from the Demonic Cult, they won't outright reject a martial artist in the Origin Energy realm who comes rolling in. Because he's valuable. They could also get information about the Demonic Cult through Jeong Bae.

,The reason to kill you has increased.'

,...Does it have to be like this?'

,The Divine Cult wants your head.'

I couldn't finish my sentence. Jeong Bae suddenly swung his sword and attacked.

The gently blowing wind turned into sharp blades of wind. It was too late to react by swinging my sword. I raised my left hand, lowered my head, and wrapped my body in the black tiger robe I was wearing. The wind blades battered my body. The black tiger robe was slightly torn, but I was unharmed, without shedding a single drop of blood.

However, I couldn't hold out for long. The black tiger robe was being torn more and more.

,You acted like a righteous warrior, but it's a surprise attack. You treacherous bastard.'

,I'll do anything to survive. I'll take that black robe you're wearing.'

,You're being greedy beyond your station.'

I moved my sword. From bottom to top, then from top to bottom.

,Like when I faced the Black Tiger, with the feeling of cutting through the Ganghwan.'

The wind blades that had been raging from all directions suddenly stopped. It was natural since I had cut through the center of the wind blades.

,You figured it out in an instant... You're no ordinary person. As expected of a member of the Annihilation Squad. Can you handle this too?!'

Jeong Bae's legs moved. Light as the wind. And as unpredictable as the wind.

,Is that the Swift Wind Steps?'

He came right in front of me. Wind revolved around his blade.

,I don't know if that's a sword or a chainsaw.'

I stepped back and raised my sword. If I just responded normally, the wind would ride up the sword and tear my arm apart.

Pajijijik.

Lightning flashed from the blade. The lightning canceled out the wind and flowed back into him. Jeong Bae was startled and kicked off the ground. He narrowly avoided the electricity right before it touched him. Even in the midst of that, he swung his sword and sent wind blades flying.

Unlike the previous wind blades, strong energy (罡氣) was infused into this wind. It meant this was an attack that couldn't be ignored.

I twisted my body to avoid the bubbles.

Right after, the wind gathered beneath me and then shot upwards. My body floated into the air. Before I was completely carried away, I added weight to myself using the secrets of the Thousand-Pound Drop (千斤錘). My floating body then fell back to the ground.

Jeong Bae had already taken a stance and was ready to swing his sword.

,Things won't go the way you want.'

Before my feet touched the ground, I extended my index finger towards Jeong Bae. Electricity gathered and rotated at the tip of my finger.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Ten Thousand Lightning (卍雷)

Lightning was fired from the tip of my finger. Jeong Bae was surprised and swung his sword. A wall of wind rose, but the lightning pierced through the wall and struck his thigh. Blue electricity flowed through Jeong Bae's body. Jeong Bae's body, electrocuted, stiffened.

,I had some expectations, but is this all you've got?'

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

Light flashed with the sword. The light passed exactly through Jeong Bae's neck.

,There's no feeling in it.'

Jeong Bae's body, which the Lightning Flash had passed through, became limp and disappeared.

,Body Double?!'

I felt the presence of a sword behind me. I desperately threw my body to the side. His sword grazed my side. The black tiger robe was torn beyond repair. If it hadn't been for the black tiger robe, I would have suffered a fatal wound.

I gritted my teeth.

Just now, I rolled on the ground to dodge the attack. In the Jianghu, this is called the Lazy Donkey Roll (懶驢打滾) and is a technique often mocked.

Looking at Jeong Bae, he was sneering at me with a crooked mouth.

,That was a very flashy movement. It's a technique that I couldn't dare to imitate. Not that I want to.'

,Shut up!'

I stepped on Lightning Sound Steps and rushed towards Jeong Bae.

Jeong Bae inhaled sharply. Wind rose. It kept rising. The wind was his sword.

Pajijijik!

I spread lightning in all directions. The electricity was canceled out and disappeared as soon as it encountered the wind.

Wind and lightning. Their essence is energy.

Kaang!

Sword and blade collided.

Jeong Bae and I swung our weapons without giving an inch. Sparks flew with each collision.

Even if Jeong Bae couldn't create wind, he swung his sword like the wind. It meant a free and ever-changing swordsmanship. A sword close to Illusion Sword.

On the other hand, my sword was simple. As fast as possible, as heavy as possible. Unnecessary skills were not needed.

From a slight distance, it would seem that Jeong Bae had the advantage. But I was the one in control of this battle. Jeong Bae's feet were gradually being pushed back. If I attacked 5 times, Jeong Bae would counterattack 2 times.

,Why are you holding back on me?'

Jeong Bae asked. Even amidst the entanglement of wind and lightning, sword and blade, his voice was clear.

,I'm holding back on you?'

,Did you think I wouldn't know? Even now, your lightning is perfectly controlling my wind. I keep creating wind... but it's being blocked every time. The difference in skill between you and me is clear. Lightning Flash Yeom Gu-seok! What do you want from me?!'

He was a perceptive bastard.

As he said, I was holding back on Jeong Bae. It wasn't just for the trivial reason of wanting to show off my skills.

,I thought I might be able to gain enlightenment by fighting like this.'

,Enlightenment? You're blocked by a wall. I know that feeling well.'

Jeong Bae's face contorted. I could feel his anger towards me.

,But let me tell you. I'm not a good training partner for you. Don't ignore the fact that my sword can take your life.'

,Are you angry? If you're angry, don't just talk shit and take my life.'

,I will!'

Kung!

His right foot stomped hard on the ground.

The sound disappeared. I realized that the area around me had become a vacuum.

Jeong Bae swung his sword as he leaped. The speed seemed slow. My concentration and Jeong Bae's concentration exploded for a moment, and our thinking speed increased.

I met Jeong Bae's eyes. He was only interested in killing me.

,He's confident of victory.'

There are times like that.

A premonition of success.

For example, in soccer. The feeling of scoring a goal as soon as you kick the soccer ball. Jeong Bae was convinced that he had killed me.

But that was just a delusion. You have to watch until the end to see if you really succeed.

Pajijik.

I heard the sound of electricity crackling. It wasn't coming from my ears. It was coming from my head.

The arm holding the sword moved. Even though I moved later than the enemy, I cut the enemy's waist faster than the enemy's sword.

Hwiiiiiiiing!

The wind that had been raging from Jeong Bae's sword grazed past me. My cheek was lightly cut. I wiped the wound on my cheek with the back of my hand and looked at the fallen Jeong Bae. His lower body was completely severed.

,One more time.'

,...What do you mean?'

,That feeling just now was good. Let's do it one more time. I felt like I was about to grasp something.'

,.......'

Jeong Bae was silent.

And at the end of the silence, he gnashed his teeth.

,Don't fucking bullshit me! You goddamn son of a bitch! Can't you see what condition I'm in?! You fucking cut my waist! Shit! So that's what my intestines look like! I'm glad I know something good before I die! You fucking son of a bitch!'

,Your arms are fine, aren't they? Swing your sword like you did before.'

,You son of a bitch!'

Jeong Bae raised the hand holding the sword upwards. The wind was about to rise, but it disappeared. His arm fell limply.

,Shit... I'll pray for your death in hell!'

Jeong Bae died like that.

I felt regret as I cut off his head.

,Something. Just a little more... I felt like something would have happened if I had just had a little more time.'

Of course, that was just my thought. In reality, everything might have been a delusion. I put Jeong Bae's head in my inventory and returned.

That evening, Heukmaeung (Black Demon Hawk) returned to the Cheonma (Heavenly Demon) Divine Cult with the head of Sakpung Gwigyeom (Wind-Slicing Ghost Sword) Jeong Bae.

Milyeong-ssi and I linked arms and headed towards Jongnam Mountain. It was a light atmosphere, like a date.

However, the conversation we were having was not as light as the atmosphere.

,The security is tighter than I thought. We haven't even entered the Jongnampai (Jongnam Sect) yet, but there are guards lined up everywhere.'

,They must have hired mercenaries. Because the crowd that has gathered is enormous.'

We were in a city below the Jongnampai (Jongnam Sect). It was called Jongnam City. As the name suggests, it was a city in Jongnam.

The city was in a festive mood.

Tourists who had gathered to receive the mystical energy of the Jongnampai (Jongnam Sect) were constantly gathering.

Milyeong-ssi and I whispered to each other. Of course, we put up a barrier around us so that others couldn't hear.

,What about the Jongnampai's (Jongnam Sect's) barrier?'

,It's more solid than usual. But it's still just at the level I expected.'

,When should we start the job?'

,It's best to avoid the first day. Because the security feels particularly tight on the first day. Let's start tomorrow or the day after. Today, let's just have a date together.'

,A date that doubles as a preliminary investigation. Not bad.'
