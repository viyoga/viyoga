"Merchant."

Rupil looked at the Transcendent who had called for him.

He was a golden-haired man. Even through a rat beastman's eyes, he was a beautiful human male. He wore robes of deep indigo cloth.

Rupil knew his name.

Ziv of the Hundred Sounds.

The only bard among the Tower's Transcendents. He could handle any instrument that produced sound, and that sound itself was power.

"Squeak... L-Lord Ziv. What business brings you here? All the goods have been sold..."

"I heard your wares come from Earth."

"Th-that's correct. Squeak. Earth is a world with advanced civilization. Not quite on the level of the Kite Empire, though."

"I have no interest in the Kite Empire. Isn't that a half-baked civilization that built itself by invading other worlds left and right, plundering their power and technology? For all its military might, its cultural strength doesn't even come close to half of that."

"I-is that so? Squeak. I don't know the details, so..."

"Earth, on the other hand, is different. Having been isolated from other worlds for so long, it possesses a cultural strength befitting its level of civilization. Merchant. I want Earth's music and instruments. Can you bring them? I'm willing to pay tens of millions of coins. If you don't need coins, my music would serve as payment as well."

Rupil was startled.

Ziv's music was special. It could strengthen allies or inflict status ailments on enemies. Rupil's eyes sparkled. He could smell the fragrant scent of money.

"S-squeak! Lord Ziv's music! What an honor! Is there a reason you want Earth's music? Any music would be like a firefly before the sun compared to Lord Ziv's music. Squeak."

"You flatter me too much. There is no superiority or inferiority in music. What I truly want is the stimulation and inspiration I'll receive from hearing Earth's music. Music from another world with an advanced civilization. As a musician, how could I not be curious? Will you grant my request?"

"Squeak squeak. It's not something I can decide on my own. I'm merely in the position of procuring goods, so..."

"Naturally. If I moved directly, there's no telling what effect it would have on the Tower, and that would be a burden on them as well. So I'll entrust it to you. Deliver my proposal for me."

"Squeak! Understood!"

China was quiet, as if nothing had happened.

From my standpoint, it was a relief, but also slightly disappointing.

'Wang Jie-Zhe probably has a rough idea that I'm the coin pickpocket. The fact that my affiliation temporarily changed to China is something the whole world knows.'

Yet there was no reaction. No criticism directed at me, no protest directed at Korea.

'They're covering it up.'

It stung to let 1.1 billion go, but there was no change whatsoever to China's officially held coins. Wang Jie-Zhe chose to preserve his own dignity.

'Kekek. He probably knows I entered Zhongnanhai too. Otherwise there'd be no reason for me to temporarily become Chinese. He might be trembling in fear, having realized he was nearly assassinated. Kekek.'

Next time I move to Zhongnanhai, I might need to be nervous too. Perhaps Wang Jie-Zhe could relocate somewhere other than Zhongnanhai.

And I heard news that China's S-rank Hunter Wang Shu'an failed his 66th-floor challenge. China didn't officially announce it. I heard it through Lee Yeon-Hee as well. If he'd succeeded, China would have shouted it from the rooftops.

'Wang Jie-Zhe will probably be lying low for a while.'

Japan is in chaos, less than a month after the Prime Minister's death. It's been reported as suicide, but those in the know, know. That the Prime Minister was made to kill himself.

I don't know who the next Prime Minister will be either. Whether they'll be hostile or friendly to Korea is also unknown. Lee Yeon-Hee wanted to use this opportunity to interfere in Japan, but lamented that she lacked the manpower and time.

I entered the Tower with Elena and Yuria and met Rupil.

"Squeak squeak squeak! Mr. Eugene! Here's this settlement payment!"

『You have acquired 3,715,967 coins.』

I received the coins.

Not bad. But I felt it was lacking.

'Wang Jie-Zhe is 1.1 billion coins from just one fleecing.'

Working diligently felt like a fool's errand. Though when you got down to it, I didn't really do anything.

Inventory, click.

The air split open and daily necessities came pouring out. Rupil squeaked as he began gathering the supplies. He sorted the frozen goods like ice cream first.

"The number of Transcendents was in the hundreds?"

"Squeak. There are Transcendents who don't officially operate, so even I don't know the exact number. But it's certain there are at least 700."

"And among them, the real elites would be the few dozen residing in the 80th floor and above zones."

"Y-you know your stuff. Squeak. Um, Mr. Eugene. A Transcendent called the Hundred Sounds made me a proposal..."

Rupil spoke while watching my reaction.

Ziv of the Hundred Sounds.

He was a Transcendent who appeared in the original work. The only bard among the Transcendents.

"I've heard of him. He wanted Earth's music?"

Rupil was startled. He was so surprised that his fur visibly stood on end.

"Squeek?! How did you know! That's exactly right! He said he's willing to pay tens of millions of coins or his own music in exchange for Ziv's music!"

"Starting with Ziv, more Transcendents will want Earth-made goods. Most will pay in coins, though."

"Squeak... W-what will you do?"

"The number of Transcendents is roughly 700. Even selling daily necessities at high prices to 700 people, the coins I can earn are limited. It's about time I started selling luxury goods."

Of course, I had no intention of just selling them freely. Luxury goods are luxury goods precisely because they're special. I'd control the supply. If I flooded the market with luxury goods for only 700 Transcendents, their value would inevitably drop.

'Even if the Transcendents know I'm controlling the supply, they can't do anything about it. No, most would actually welcome it. Who'd want limited editions otherwise?'

Even if a minority of Transcendents got pissed off, they couldn't do anything to me. Transcendents were bound by the Tower's restrictions. Without those, they would have reigned like kings in the Tower.

'In the original, did the protagonist sell radio sets?'

I pulled out top-of-the-line audio equipment from my inventory. I also uploaded the manual Yuria had written. With this manual, even I could roughly handle the high-end audio equipment.

"Squeek?! D-did you prepare in advance?"

"Kekek. Predicting the future is a piece of cake."

"Y-you're truly amazing! Squeak!"

For reference, I'd prepared classical music and short, intense modern tracks. I also included some songs I personally liked. There's no language barrier in the Tower, so everything should be enjoyable.

I could already picture the Transcendent of music being so shocked he'd fall over.

"Take it. I'll accept music as payment. Music that weakens enemies. I'll be waiting here, so go and come back quickly."

"Understood, squeak!"

Rupil hurriedly packed the goods into his bundle and left.

"Squeak squeak! Transcendents! I'm back again! Selling cup ramen!"

Rupil shouted loudly in the 82nd floor's public zone plaza.

"You're here!"

The Transcendents who had been waiting for Rupil rose and moved quickly.

While Rupil sold goods to the Transcendents as usual, he noticed less enthusiasm and fewer Transcendents than usual.

Since Rupil came at set times and sold the same goods every time, there was no reason to rush. On top of that, every Transcendent's dwelling now had daily necessities stocked.

"Cup ramen at 1,000 coins each. Isn't that too expensive?"

A Transcendent who had gladly bought cup ramen yesterday grumbled. He'd grown accustomed to cup ramen. Other Transcendents didn't exactly chime in, but they didn't object either.

"Today I'm selling a special additional food! Fried chicken! Not just any fried chicken—Korean-style fried chicken! Squeak squeak! Only 5 available! 200,000 coins each!!"

"Korean-style chicken? It's just chicken fried in oil."

"Hey, Rupil. You'd better not be underestimating us too much. Chickens can be found in the Tower too. Getting oil isn't difficult either."

"Just try it first, Transcendents! As a special service, I'll give you a free can of cola! Normally, cola is a paid add-on with Korean chicken! Squeak squeak!"

"Hmm. Even so, 200,000 coins for one fried chicken is a bit..."

Even Transcendents don't have infinite coins. They had to hunt to earn coins, and they had their own places to spend them. Let alone 200,000 coins for one fried chicken? Anyone could see this was a rip-off.

"Ha. A mere 200,000 coins. If you think of it as an otherworld premium, it's nothing. Beggars, get lost."

Among the Transcendents, Hent the Sage of Iron, famous for his wealth, stepped forward. His appearance was that of a man in his seventies wearing a grey robe with a snow-white beard.

He bought the chicken without a moment's hesitation, to show off his wealth to the other Transcendents.

"I'll take one bite, and if it's not good, I'll throw it away."

Hent lifted a golden-glowing chicken leg and bit into it with a crunch. His eyes grew so wide they seemed about to tear.

“……!!”

Hent's back arched like a bow. A highly stimulating food he'd never tasted in his life! The finest steak eaten by kings in his own world was nothing but tough rubber bands compared to this.

"Rupil! Sell me the remaining 4 chickens too!! I'll pay double the price!"

"S-Sage of Iron. I can't. It's one chicken per person! Squeak!"

"I'll give you four times!"

"I can't! Squeak!"

Rupil shook his head firmly.

Rules must be respected. Of course, it wasn't because Rupil was an upstanding rat beastman. It was because if he set minimum rules and didn't follow them, there was no telling how the other Transcendents would react. Making more money was good, but his life was more important than that.

"The Sage of Iron. That old man would throw away his dignity like that?"

"Rupil!! Just give me one chicken!!"

"Don't cut in line! I was here first!!"

Afterward, four more Transcendents' backs arched like bows.
