"......"

I was sitting blankly on the sofa.

I must have taken about an hour-long nap, but it felt like I had slept for dozens of hours and just woken up.

I checked the time and got up. I had something to do today. I put on my coat and went down to the first floor.

I ran into a silver-haired maid holding a laundry basket. She looked great in her maid outfit and smiled brightly when she saw me.

"You're going out, I see. When will you be back? I'll prepare dinner accordingly."

"I'll be back before 7."

Seeing the laundry basket she was holding made me feel awkward. My underwear and hers were tangled together in the basket. Especially her underwear, many of them had designs that made you wonder if they were really underwear.

"I will prepare dinner at that time. Have a good day."

I went outside, took a carriage, and headed to District R. After arriving in District R, I walked the streets on foot.

District R was a bustling area. It was a commercial district frequently used by the middle and lower classes. I had rented a store in an alleyway in this District R.

'It can't be helped. Commercial properties in District R are so popular that they hardly ever come on the market.'

The commercial buildings I happened to find were disgustingly expensive to the point where I couldn't even think about it. The only somewhat reasonable one was this store in the alleyway.

The store was under construction. The hired workers were changing the interior of the store.

The size of this store, located in the back of the alleyway, was small. It was only about 5 to 6 pyeong. If you separate the kitchen and the hall, it would feel even smaller.

'It's my store.'

I felt a surge of emotion as I looked at the store.

All the preparations were completed quickly. Thanks to commissioning a broker and greasing the palms of public officials, the business permit was issued immediately.

The store sign wasn't up yet, but I had already decided on the name.

'YuYu Chicken.'

Yu from Yujin. Yu from Yuria. Combined, it's YuYu Chicken.

'I wouldn't have been able to start a chicken business without Yuria.'

The most important thing in the chicken business is the taste of the chicken, but I didn't have any talent for cooking. When I made fried chicken with a recipe I vaguely knew, it tasted like nothing more than garbage. I tried several times after that, but it only resulted in wasting innocent chickens.

That's when Yuria stepped in.

'I was able to perfect the chicken thanks to Yuria's help.'

Not only fried chicken, but also seasoned chicken. I plan to push these two as my main dishes.

'All I have to do at the chicken restaurant is fry the chicken according to the method Yuria taught me.'

Roast the chicken at the oil temperature Yuria told me, for the amount of time Yuria told me. That's all I have to do well. Yuria has agreed to take care of the chicken's seasoning and the sauce for the time being.

Even if I knew how to do the seasoning and sauce, I wasn't good at it. I clearly watched her do it and did the same thing, but the taste was slightly different.

'I can't leave everything to Maid-in-Yuria. This business is mine.'

I don't intend to just thank her with words. I plan to pay her a portion of the profits from selling the chicken.

'Still, I need Yuria's help until the factory is running normally.'

I wasn't planning on being satisfied with this small store. My goal is a franchise.

'The taste has to be the same as other stores. To do that, a factory is essential. The factory has to season the chicken or make the sauce and provide it to the franchise stores.'

If the business is successful, I will make a lot of money. And a lot of money will make me a noble.

It felt like a rosy future was unfolding before my eyes.

"You're opening a chicken restaurant?"

Just as I was about to fall into thought, someone spoke to me.

It was a middle-aged man wearing a yellowish t-shirt. I knew the man.

John Clark. He was the man who ran the fish and chips shop across from my store. He smiled and approached me. I could smell the oil. It was unpleasant.

"Yes. It's a chicken restaurant."

"I don't like that you're frying things... Are you going to compete head-on with my store?"

"...You sell fish and chips, and I sell chicken. They don't overlap, so what's the problem?"

"It's the same fried food. Well, just kidding. I'm not too worried. You know why?"

"Why?"

"People in Neo-London don't like chicken."

John grinned. His teeth were made of steel. Not a cyborg, just reinforced his teeth with steel. In this world, that's nothing really.

"......"

"Well, good luck. It'll be three months at most."

He turned around and went into his store.

The citizens of Neo-London don't like chicken. He was right. Did you think I wouldn't do market research before opening a chicken restaurant?

'The chicken in this world is too greasy and tasteless. To the point where people say they'd rather eat fish and chips than chicken.'

But the chicken I'm going to make and sell is Korean-style chicken that hasn't existed in Neo-London until now.

'Korean-style chicken will be a hit. I'm 100% sure.'

I gave the construction workers drinks and looked around. There were no stores that could be a threat to my store.

After finishing the request to punish the thugs, I went to the employment agency and told them that I couldn't take any requests for the time being.

"Really? That's a shame. Your stock was rising. These days, there are a lot of people trying to find out about you to designate you."

Rose picked up a cigarette with her right mechanical arm and put it in her mouth. Her black hair fell smoothly down her shoulders. There was a black rose tattoo on the right side of her neck. Below that, the cleavage was faintly visible between the unbuttoned shirt. I tried to look away and focus on her face.

"I haven't been active for long, but I'm already known?"

"You're a 5th Circle mage who gets the job done. You haven't failed in any of the 12 missions. You're starting to make a name for yourself."

"They were all easy missions."

"It doesn't look that way to other people. And speak properly. You had a bit of a hard time dealing with the Palace Family, didn't you?"

"......"

I recalled the memories of the past. The Palace Family was a mafia organization from outside. It was a bit difficult to deal with them.

"You're becoming famous as a free mage. If you continue to take requests and make deals like this... you could become a big shot in 1-2 years."

"I'll say it again. I won't be taking any requests for the time being."

"Because of that chicken restaurant? Was it YuYu Chicken? You're doing something that doesn't suit you at all. I don't understand, what's a 5th Circle mage lacking that they're doing something like a chicken restaurant?"

"The chicken restaurant is just the beginning. I'm going to make the business more prosperous... and someday, I'll even advance to the center."

"You have big dreams. Do you want to become a noble that badly?"

"There are many things you can do if you become a noble."

"Then isn't it better to do this job? You can become a noble faster if you take requests. You're a 5th Circle mage, after all."

"Even if I become a noble that way, the other nobles will ignore me. In the end, I'll just be a noble from a mercenary background with nothing but magic."

"You just need to become stronger."

"To be properly treated in noble society, you'd have to be at least an 8th Circle. Do you think I can become an 8th Circle?"

"......"

Rose closed her mouth.

In this world, 7th Circle and above are called the realm of superhumans. That doesn't mean that 7th Circle is rare. If you look hard enough, you can easily see someone who is 7th Circle. But 8th Circle and above are really rare. No one can easily talk about the realm of 8th Circle.

"...Well, fine. I won't stop you since you're so determined. You're not saying you'll never take requests again, are you?"

"I'll take requests when I'm short on money or when I have free time. And if possible... I'd like the reward for the request to be related to magic. Elixirs aren't bad either."

"Okay. I'll call you if something comes up."

She put all the documents on the desk into the shredder. They were documents with requests written on them.

Since the story was over, I got up from my seat.

"Wait a minute. I'm really curious... Why the hell are you doing something like a chicken restaurant? You're a 5th Circle mage."

I know this world well. But that was about the game settings. I don't know the details of how this world works.

The same goes for mages. Mages in this world are more famous for research. There's no reason for mages to take on combat in a civilized city.

'But the only mages I know in this world are combat mages. Combat was the most important thing in the game, after all.'

Besides that, I don't know much about how people in this world live, what kind of food they eat, or what kind of clothes they mainly wear.

The number of things I can choose for a business with my lack of basic knowledge was limited. And among them, the only item that seemed most likely to succeed was chicken.

"Because I like chicken."

"Is that so? I'll bring chicken when you open the store. I'll give you a proper evaluation of how bad it tastes."

"I'll have one regular customer."

"Ha, I'll tell you in advance, I have a more picky palate than you think."

I know.

Her real identity is a noble.

But will she be more picky than my palate?

"I'll give you a coupon with every chicken. If you collect 10 coupons, you get one chicken for free. Make sure to collect the coupons well."

"I don't care about coupons, I'll spit it out on the spot if it doesn't taste good."

Grand Opening!

The day had finally come.

I came to the store early in the morning and looked around. It was my first time running a store, so my heart was pounding. After finishing the thorough cleaning yesterday and making sure the store was clean without a speck of dust, I put on a sanitary cap and apron and went inside.

Inside the refrigerator, there was pre-seasoned chicken and the secret sauce that Yuria had made. I poured golden cooking oil into the fryer and raised the temperature. I also checked the drinks like cola and beer.

No problem.

9:30 AM.

Someone opened the door and came in 30 minutes before opening. It was a young woman with brown hair.

Carell Orising.

She's the part-timer I hired for today. She will be in charge of the hall. I will hire a part-timer to take charge of the kitchen when the store is somewhat stable.

"Am I late?"

"You came right on time. Just put on the apron."

I handed her an apron with YuYu Chicken written on it. She put on the apron on the spot and waited blankly. There were still 30 minutes until opening.

"...Would you like to try some chicken?"

"Ah, no. Chicken from the morning is a bit...."

Her voice of disgust made me realize again that the perception of chicken is not good.

I smiled quietly. She came thinking it would be a sweet part-time job with no customers because it's a chicken restaurant... but she'll be busy as hell now.

'Do you think I'm giving you more than the minimum wage for nothing?'

I chatted with her appropriately and opened the store at exactly 10 o'clock.

I also held a 20% discount event as an opening event.
