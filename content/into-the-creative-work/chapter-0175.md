I came out of the village chief's house and looked around Nakil Village while waiting for Kang Myungjin-ssi to return.

It was boring. There wasn't much to see. The population was only 200, so I could see the whole village in about 15 minutes of walking.

'Nakil Mountain. The Adversary of the Sun is at the top of that.'

To be exact, it's the Adversary of the Sun (False). Area 406, Nakil Mountain, is completed by defeating the Adversary of the Sun. If I defeat the Adversary of the Sun (False), I can rule this area.

If I defeat the Adversary of the Sun (False), I can get one SS-rank item and one elixir.

'I have the Aegis Amulet. If I use this with an Instant... maybe it's possible.'

I looked at the high mountain behind Nakil Village, Nakil Mountain. Unusually, there was a path leading to the top. The path was straight, so it seemed like I could reach the top if I ran for an hour without resting.

'It would take a normal person several hours, but my current stats are better than my real body.'

It might not even take an hour.

'......Should I try it once? I want the elixir, if nothing else.'

I know how bottom-of-the-barrel my talent is. I have no talent. The reason I won in Dungeon Survival was thanks to 'Lightning' and 'Acceleration'. And I was also lucky.

I'm stronger than Kang Myungjin-ssi and Ju Seohyun-ssi right now, but I'm sure they'll overtake me over time.

'If I'm weaker than them, that means I have to be mindful of them.'

I thought about it carefully.

Kang Myungjin-ssi later defeats the False God of the Adversary of the Sun and acquires a weapon. That weapon is used at a pretty important moment.

But that's a story from several years from now.

'I exist, who doesn't exist in the original. The future will be different from the original from some point on.'

The heroine, Ju Seohyun-ssi, has become mine. The contents of the original are already creaking.

'If the future is going to change anyway, it doesn't matter if I change it now.'

Even if I defeat the Adversary of the Sun (False), the early contents of the original won't be greatly distorted. If it's Kang Myungjin-ssi, who knows the settings of 'God's Atlantis' inside and out, he'll grow without much trouble even if the elixir is snatched away.

'Since when have I been caring about the original? This is a game world anyway. I'm going to do whatever I want. Let me be a munchkin too!'

I decided to become a munchkin in this world.

Having made a firm decision, I headed to Nakil Mountain.

As soon as I got out of the village and entered the road to Nakil Mountain, a notification window popped up.

「The Adversary of the Sun (False) is looking at you.」

「The King of the Demonic Sky is looking forward to it.」

「The Owner of the Celestial Sphere strokes his beard.」

「The Golden Collector narrows his eyes.」

「A Fallen Star cheers for the Adversary of the Sun (False).」

「The Adversary of the Sun is looking at you.」

When I enhanced my eyesight using mana, I could see a man standing on the top of the mountain.

His black hair is tied in a topknot, and he is showing off his firm muscles with his upper body bare. He is holding a red bow in his hand and has a sword equipped on his waist.

The Adversary of the Sun (False) is a hero from Chinese mythology.

The hero who shot down 9 of the 10 suns with his bow.

Yi.

Even if he is a False God and not the real one, he is a much more powerful divine seat than Lu Bu.

Yi opened his mouth while looking at me.

"Exile, step back. I will not allow entry to Nakil Mountain."

His deep voice was heard. Even though he was far away, it felt like he was speaking right next to me.

"From today, I will rule Nakil Mountain."

I said boldly and started running towards the top of Nakil Mountain.

"Foolish. Just because you can see the sun doesn't mean it's close."

Yi aimed his bow at me and pulled the string. Then an arrow appeared on the string. I swallowed hard while looking at his arrow.

&gt;

'An arrow that shot down the sun. Even if he's a False God, I can't ignore it.'

Fortunately, I don't have the property of the sun. If I had the property of the sun, I wouldn't be able to avoid the arrow.

The arrow is fired. I used an Instant in response.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 4]

The scenery in my vision slowed down. Nevertheless, the arrow fired at me was flying at a rapid speed. There was no time to think deeply. I jumped to the right before the arrow hit.

The arrow passed by my side and stuck into the ground.

Kwaang!

A crater was created as if a meteorite had fallen. I wiped the cold sweat on my forehead with the back of my hand.

'Shibal. I'm suddenly regretting it. Should I just run away now?'

No. I can't back down now that I've come this far.

I started running forward again.

"So you have at least the minimum skill to be reckless...."

Yi started pulling the bowstring again. Fortunately, unlike the first time, it takes some time for the second arrow to be created.

'He can't shoot arrows in succession like in the original.'

Instead, the power of the arrow gradually increases each time he shoots an arrow. Yi can shoot a maximum of 10 times.

'Yi can't shoot the 10th arrow. That's because Yi can only shoot it once. Yi has already decided where to shoot the 10th arrow.'

In other words, Yi can only shoot 9 arrows. After that, he won't use the bow.

'Yi's close combat ability isn't great. If I endure 9 arrows, or arrive before that, I win.'

I can block 3 arrows with the Aegis Amulet. And if I use Instant 5 times, I can somehow avoid them.

But this leaves one problem.

'I have to avoid the remaining 1 arrow. I should avoid the arrows shot early on as much as possible.'

I had to avoid the first arrow with Instant. I needed to know how fast it was.

'At this rate... I can avoid it.'

The second arrow comes flying.

I tried to dodge to the left in time, but stopped. The arrow was faster than I thought. I can't keep up with the speed of the arrow with my movements.

'Instant!'

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 3]

I avoided it using Instant.

I had no choice. If I hadn't used Instant, I would have been hit 100%. I can't waste the Aegis Amulet's charges on just the second arrow.

The third arrow comes flying.

I tried to avoid it without using Instant this time, but I was horrified by the speed of the arrow, which was faster than before, and had no choice but to use Instant.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 2]

It was also impossible to put the Aegis Amulet in my inventory and dodge it. If I look at that power, I'll definitely die the moment it grazes me. I can only use the ultimate move, Complete Recovery, if I'm alive.

'Damn it. Now that it's come to this, I'm changing my strategy. I'm going to reach him before he shoots the ninth arrow.'

I started running with all my might.

I avoided the fourth and fifth arrows with Instant.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 0]

I couldn't use Instant anymore.

Yi pulled the string and started nocking the sixth arrow. The sixth arrow is different from the others. The arrow started to emit a pure white light.

"I admit it. You're no ordinary human."

The white shining arrow comes flying. The moment I felt it was flashing, the arrow was in front of me.

A translucent hexagonal shield appeared and blocked the shining arrow. A broken arrow fell to the ground with a huge sound as if space was being torn apart.

「The remaining number of Aegis Amulet is 2 times.」

I ran with gritted teeth.

Yi prepares the seventh arrow.

I reached out to Yi. I can reach him at this distance.

'Manroe (卍 Thunder)!'

With the sound of thunder, a blue lightning bolt rushed towards Yi.

Yi jumped up and avoided the lightning. He jumped over 5M and shot the seventh arrow in a perfect posture in the air.

A hexagonal shield appeared in front of me and blocked the arrow. The trees around me were all blown away by the aftermath alone.

'That bastard...! He's pulling the bowstring again without even resting!'

The eighth arrow took more than 10 minutes to prepare. That means it's that much more powerful.

'......Could the Aegis Amulet be pierced too?'

Unlike my worries, the Aegis Amulet blocked the eighth arrow.

Amazing. I never thought I would use nine Sunset Arrows on a single person.

I was sweating profusely. Judging from the pattern so far, the ninth arrow will probably take 13 minutes to prepare.

'I can't cover the remaining distance in 13 minutes. There's not enough time!'

He's a guy who dodges lightning without a care. There was a high possibility that taking out a gun and attacking from a distance would be meaningless.

'Should I beg for forgiveness? No, it's clear that it won't work. There must be a way. A way...!'

There is one way that comes to mind.

The setting book of 'God's Atlantis' contains detailed information about the Adversary of the Sun (False), Yi. I know what his purpose is.

'......If I use that method, there's a possibility....'

What's bothering me are the divine seats watching me. I think they'll react positively, but the Fallen Star might try to play tricks.

'Screw it. It's important for me to live now. I don't need to care about the divine seats.'

It might be a good thing if I think about the future.

'I have the Owner of the Celestial Sphere and the King of the Demonic Sky behind me. I don't have much faith in them, but they won't let the Fallen Star play tricks.'

Having made up my mind, I shouted at Yi.

"Yi! I know what your purpose is!"

Yi's posture remains unchanged. But his eyes looking at me were filled with curiosity.

"You know what my purpose is? I have never told anyone my purpose."

「The Adversary of the Sun is narrowing his brows and looking at you.」

Even the real Adversary of the Sun is showing interest.

It can't be helped because even if the Yi in front of me is fake, he is a fake based on the real one. Unless it's a special case, False Gods have similar personalities, values, and purposes as the real divine seats.

"I am a regressor!"

Yi stopped pulling the bowstring. He is staring at me intently, having stopped his actions.

"If you're a regressor... does that mean you've returned from the future?"

"That's right!"

「The Adversary of the Sun widens his eyes.」

「The Fallen Star rubs his ears, unable to believe it.」

「The Owner of the Celestial Sphere rests his chin on his hand.」

Regressor.

In Atlantis, it's special, but not a huge existence.

According to the setting book, regressors existed in the 2nd and 5th Atlantis. This is because even if humans go back to the past, they are still just humans in the end.

It's hard to use all the knowledge of the future. This is because the future is not absolute.

In fact, the regressors of the 2nd and 5th times became excellent exiles, but they did not become the best.

"......I can't believe it just from that. Tell me what my purpose is."

"Yi-nim is waiting for the day of the solar eclipse that will happen in a few years!"

"...That's right. But it's not enough. Do you know why I'm waiting for that day?"

"A solar eclipse is the day when the moon covers the sun. It's the day when the sun and moon are in a straight line. Yi-nim is waiting for the solar eclipse to kill that damn bitch on the moon with the 10th arrow!"

Yi's arrow exerts its proper power when it is shot at the sun. If you shoot an arrow at the sun during a solar eclipse, it's the same as shooting it at the moon.

Yi's goal is to fully unleash the power of the 10th arrow and kill the woman on the moon, Heng'e (姮娥).

"You know everything. Are you really a regressor...."

Yi lowered his bow.

One of the reasons why my lie worked was probably due to the influence of my unique characteristic, 'Deception (SS)'.

"You who have gone back in time, I ask you. Did I kill that woman?"

"You failed."

In fact, he couldn't even try in the original. This is because he died at the hands of Kang Myungjin-ssi before the solar eclipse occurred.

Even if he shot the tenth arrow at the solar eclipse as he intended, it was highly likely that he would have failed. There's no way Heng'e would just take the arrow. Above all, Heng'e is already watching Yi.

"......I see."

Yi nodded calmly. It seems he had predicted that he would fail.

"What did you come to me for?"

"To rule Area 406. But with my current self, I don't think I can ever beat Yi-nim. So I propose."

"A proposal?"

"I swear to make that damn bitch on the moon my sex toy! So please give me the right to rule Nakil Mountain!"
