The training grounds were quiet after the battle ended faster than expected.

Usually, sparring matches between Hunters of similar skill levels last about 3 to 5 minutes. Matches ending within 30 seconds usually indicate a clear difference in skill or one side being careless.

This time, it was clearly the former.

There was someone quietly laughing in the audience seats. It was Kang Seoksu, the representative of the Seventier Guild.

,Park Seowoon was also careless. Unlike his fame, detailed information about Seong Yujin is not well known. If you search the internet, you will find a few videos, but those are also from several years ago. The skills then and now are completely different.'

Seong Yujin mostly went to B-grade or lower gates alone. Was he afraid of A-grade gates? No. There's no way he would be like that with his personality.

,He must have been too lazy. Since he was alone, B-grade gates would have been enough for his performance and income.'

The puzzling thing is that you can't drastically improve your skills with just B-grade or lower gates. Skills increase through tough battles and accumulated experience.

,Looking at the battle just now, he doesn't seem to lack experience. Did he gain experience in a place I don't know about?'

Kang Seoksu imagined himself as Seong Yujin's opponent.

Closing the distance is suicide. He's not confident in winning a close-quarters battle against Seong Yujin.

,I'm a mage. Keeping my distance isn't a choice, it's a necessity. But even if I keep my distance, Seong Yujin won't be helpless. The lightning Seong Yujin just used is at the level of my attack magic.'

If it was ordinary lightning, an A-grade Hunter wouldn't have been knocked out so quickly. You can't incapacitate an A-grade Hunter with ordinary lightning. An A-grade Hunter's body is completely different from that of an ordinary person.

,Close-range is a sword. The acceleration ability is so fast that even I can miss it for a moment. The lightning seems to be usable at all distances. Moreover, due to the nature of lightning, even if you block it, you're not really blocking it.'

Even if you block it with a sword or shield, it flows through. To block lightning, you need to actively use mana or strengthen your body so that lightning doesn't affect you. Either way, it becomes tiring for the opponent.

,If he were an enemy, it would be a headache.'

He subtly turned his head. The faces of the two A-grade Hunters scheduled for the next sparring match weren't good. Seong Yujin's skills were much better than expected, and their plans had gone awry.

,But they can't back out now. They'll be called cowards. Moreover, there's the issue of the guild's reputation that sent them.'

The broadcasters? They seemed a little disappointed. The longer the battle, the better for them.

The ordinary people he brought along because he couldn't just ignore them were stiff and frozen. Sparring matches between Hunters. Watching it on video is completely different from seeing it in person. Hunters die with the slightest mistake. They are forced to recall the fact that they had been turning a blind eye to, blinded by the spectacle.

And the members of Seventier.

They were watching the sparring match with sparkling eyes. The more Seong Yujin excelled, the more Seventier's pride increased.

An S-grade candidate. In other words, someone who will become an S-grade Hunter someday. In the future, Seventier will have an S-grade Hunter. Just that alone will attract investors, and Hunters will envy the Seventier Guild.

,Show overwhelming power in the remaining sparring matches, Seong Yujin.'

That's when it happened.

A man approached him from behind.

It was a man wearing a pure white suit that felt almost obsessive. His tie, wristwatch, and even his shoes were pure white. Perhaps because of his sharp features, he had a cold impression.

Jeong Jihak. One of the guild masters of Seventier.

,It seems I'm a bit late."

,You are late. The battle just ended. It was an interesting sight."

,I think I can tell the result just by looking at the surrounding reactions."

Jeong Jihak looked at Park Seowoon, who was lying on the training ground floor.

,Do you need my help?"

,Forget it. Seong Yujin controlled his power. He only fainted, there's no serious damage to his body."

The next sparring match was with Kim Jio from the Bluebird Guild. Kim Jio stood in front with a nervous expression.

The sparring match ended in just 3 seconds.

He couldn't even react to Seong Yujin's attack using acceleration.

Seong Yujin's instantaneous acceleration was difficult for even A-grade Hunters to react to. If you try to react relying solely on physical abilities, that's what happens.

,Hmm. Impressive."

Jeong Jihak said with admiration. Kang Seoksu asked him, just in case.

,Did you see Seong Yujin's movements?"

,I didn't see them. That's why it's impressive."

,If you were Seong Yujin's opponent, how would you have responded?"

,I'm not suited for combat... but if I had to fight, I would have allowed the first attack and aimed for a counterattack. I don't know if it would work."

Kang Seoksu nodded. It was a tactic that only Jeong Jihak, a recovery ability user, could use.

Soon, the third sparring match began.

,This one will end quickly too."

,I don't know. I don't think it will be that quick."

Kang Dongwoo of the Ihwa Guild.

An A-grade Hunter whose name I've never heard of.

,But it's the Ihwa Guild. People ignore the Ihwa Guild, calling it the 10th among the top 10 guilds, but... I think the Ihwa Guild is hiding its claws. If they reveal their claws carelessly, they will only become a target for the other top 10 guilds, so they are hiding them.'

• • •

The sparring match began.

As always, I instantly closed the distance using an instant and swung my sword. The lightning swirling at the tip of the sword cuts through the air.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 7]

But the sword cut through the air. Suddenly, he disappeared. The place he appeared was at the end of the training ground, 10m away.

,...Teleport? Is it an artifact."

Teleporters are subject to special management. There was no way he would be here.

,You're quick to notice. Yes. It's an artifact. You're not going to disqualify me for using an artifact, are you?"

,I don't care."

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

He shoots the Myriad Lightning gathered in his palm at Kang Dongwoo. Kang Dongwoo draws his hand in the air. An invisible barrier rises and blocks the lightning.

I narrowed my eyes.

,An invisible wall. It doesn't feel like he's manipulating wind. Is it telekinesis? It's quite a troublesome ability.'

The biggest advantage of telekinetic abilities is that they are invisible.

,Even if it's invisible, I can feel it.'

Telekinesis also has its limits if you only use the ability itself, so you mix it with mana. That's how the power increases.

,I just need to sense the mana.'

Qi Sense.

I recently increased my mana stat. My current stat is the average for S-grade. When I focus a little, I can feel many things. Including the energy surrounding Kang Dongwoo.

,Well, I could use Thousand Eyes to directly see the mana, but.'

I didn't intend to show all of my abilities.

,I can feel it.'

Kang Dongwoo's mana fluctuates. The mana becomes an intangible form and flies towards me. In the form of a surface, not a point or a line.

I stepped back, using footwork. The telekinesis cut through the air where I had been.

,...You felt my telekinesis? You have amazing mana sensing ability."

,I just have a good sense."

,Haha. Then how about this?"

Kang Dongwoo spread his arms wide. At the same time, telekinesis spread in all directions.

,Is he planning to cover the entire training ground and then slowly narrow it down?'

The space is limited, so I can't avoid it.

,He's much more skilled than the two guys before him.'

But what he can do, I can do too.

,Lightning.'

Paji jik!

Blue lightning spreads in all directions through my feet. The floor, walls, and ceiling of the training ground flashed with lightning.

Peong!

The fluorescent lights on the ceiling burst and pieces fall to the floor.

Kang Dongwoo swallowed hard. He stopped the power he was deploying and rotated a telekinetic barrier around himself. That's how he wouldn't get electrocuted.

,...The range of your power is no joke. Honestly, isn't this cheating? I'm like this because it's me... In the case of other people, they wouldn't be able to attack or defend."

,Are you going to keep doing that?"

,I can't do that. It seems like I'm losing in terms of the amount of mana... I think this will be my last strike. It's a technique that I recently developed that combines offense and defense. I'll show you."

I thought he had been talking for a long time since earlier, but now that I see it, he was conscious of the camera, not me.

,He's going to try to salvage his image even a little bit because he thinks he's going to lose.'

It might be wise. The two guys before him couldn't even last 10 seconds. The public's attention will turn to Kang Dongwoo, who lasted relatively longer.

The telekinetic barrier protecting Kang Dongwoo's body began to rotate.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwa!

The rotating telekinesis grinds and smashes the floor and ceiling of the training ground with terrifying force.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

The lightning, which was made stronger by taking more time than before, was shot out and bounced to the side. Ordinary attacks don't work.

He walks towards me with a stiff expression. He's sweating, so it seems impossible for him to run.

,It seems like the mental strength and mana consumed are great. I feel like I could win just by maintaining this distance?'

But wouldn't that be too unimpressive?

This video will be talked about for a long time later, right?

I gripped my sword as I watched Kang Dongwoo approaching. The residual lightning in all directions gathers around the sword.

Kang Dongwoo had a surprised expression. He seemed to sense something strange, but he couldn't stop now that he had come this far. He took a firm step forward.

Lightning Heaven Style (Lei Tian Style) Lightning Gang Blade (Lei Gang Blade).

Ganggi and lightning are mixed together, forming a blade that shines with lightning.

As soon as he saw the lightning blade, his face stiffened with a serious expression, and I swung my sword at him.

His barrier splits and disappears as easily as tofu. The tip of the sword stopped in front of Kang Dongwoo's head.

,...I lost."

The sparring match was over.

After that, interviews followed.

The A-grade Hunters who fought me desperately managed their expressions and gave interviews, and I recited the script that Kang Seoksu gave me.

After the filming, Kang Seoksu approached. He had a satisfied smile on his face.

,It was perfect. You did well in the interview too."

,What's so difficult about reading a script? Who's the person behind you?"

The man in the pure white suit stepped forward in front of me.

,Nice to meet you, I'm Jeong Jihak. I'm a master of Seventier like you."

,He has recovery abilities. His ability as a rear support is the best. He can even recover severed arms."

,Yes. That's right. If you have any discomfort, please tell me. I'll treat you. Of course, the price is your share."

,Price? Can I just give you money?"

He smiled and shook his head.

,That's not it."

,Jeong Jihak's recovery ability is amazing, but... there's a condition that it's accompanied by tremendous pain."

,Yes. It's as the representative says. It's an ability that even anesthetics don't work on. That's why people avoid my ability. There are still people who seek me out because it's better than dying."

I had Complete Recovery. There would be no need to borrow Jeong Jihak's hands.

,By the way, this guy isn't coming until the end."

Kang Seoksu frowned.

,Was someone else supposed to come?"

,One of the masters was supposed to come. He's a belligerent guy. But seeing that he's not coming, he either forgot the promise... or he might have gotten caught up in an incident and couldn't come."

,Does he often get caught up in incidents?"

,I told you. He's belligerent."

It was when Kang Seoksu sighed. His smartphone vibrated in his pocket. Kang Seoksu took out his smartphone and checked the sender. His face hardened.

,...It's the association's emergency contact network. It seems like a problem has occurred."

Kang Seoksu answered the phone. His face became serious.

,Bluebird's attack team failed to capture the S-grade dungeon. As a result, a dungeon breaker phenomenon has occurred. Seong Yujin, Jeong Jihak. Your strength is needed. Prepare immediately."
