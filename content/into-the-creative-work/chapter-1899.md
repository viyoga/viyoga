I gained more points than I thought. But it felt like I didn't have enough points to use them right away.

,I want to raise Lightning Flash... I'm short by more than 10,000 points.'

Last time, when I raised the level of Lightning Flash to 12, I was able to give Lightning Flash characteristics. The problem was that it wasn't easy to control except for the characteristics I was familiar with. If I raise the level of Lightning Flash, I will be able to use Lightning Flash in more diverse ways.

,Should I raise my physical abilities at least?'

I pondered for a moment and shook my head. I didn't feel any lack in my physical abilities. Currently, I am a B-rank Hunter, but my physical abilities are at least A-rank.

,I probably don't meet the A-rank average.'

In fact, there is no exact standard for the A-rank average. This is because there is a big difference between individuals in A-rank. The physical abilities of physical-type ability users will be better than mine. On the other hand, magic-type or psychic-type ability users will have lower physical abilities than me.

,There are quite a few A-rank Hunters who are S-rank candidates. But they are not mentioned.'

The most important thing for a Hunter called an S-rank candidate is the talent to overcome the wall. Han Ah-young was called an S-rank candidate because she was recognized for that talent, and now she is recognized as an S-rank and is active on the world stage. For the sake of humanity.

,Becoming an S-rank seems like it would attract unnecessary attention and make me tired.'

As you can see from Han Ah-young's busy activities, the world was not so peaceful. South Korea is safe because it has an excessively large number of A-rank Hunters compared to its population. If you go abroad, you can easily see cities that have been destroyed by monsters. It's not easy to restore them because dungeons continue to appear all over the world.

,Becoming an S-rank isn't always a good thing.'

Still, I wanted to become an S-rank. The taste of power of an S-rank Hunter would be sweet.

I got up from my seat with enthusiasm. To become an S-rank, I had to become an A-rank first.

,Let's do two B-rank dungeons today!'

8 hours later. Exhausted, I regretted my decision and returned home.

,What bastard came up with a system like performance... Isn't there a way to get something for nothing?'

I met Ha Seunghee, the granddaughter of the Chairman of Sejin Group and a third-generation chaebol, at the research center after a long time.

The usually sharp Ha Seunghee looked disheveled for some reason. Her hair looked dry, and her expression revealed fatigue.

,You seem very busy these days?'

,Yes. There are many people who want HB-1.'

HB-1. A replica of Heville's stimulant. Sales started last time, and the media initially made a fuss about it, but now it's quiet. So I thought she wouldn't be busy, but that doesn't seem to be the case.

,Thanks to Yujin-seonbae, I'm making astronomical profits. Money must be piling up in Yujin-seonbae's account too, right?'

As she said, billions of won were piling up in my account. As long as HB-1 sells well, my account will not run dry.

,That's right. Even if I try to spend money, there aren't many places to spend it.'

I had the Game Life app. What do I want? I can just go into a suitable creative world and bring it back. Even if that wasn't the case, there weren't many things I really wanted. Even now, my house was filled with unusual items.

Just the [Space Movement Scroll] alone can be sold for hundreds of millions of won. There was also the [Fountain of Youth], which makes you younger just by drinking it. This is worth at least tens of billions of won. Even S-rank Hunters cannot avoid aging... I can get hundreds of billions of won. But money wouldn't be the only object of trade.

,Why don't you change your house? People usually buy a house when they get a lot of money. You could say it's a kind of real estate investment.'

,I like where I live now. I'm not interested in real estate investment.'

The officetel I live in is moderately spacious and enough for me to live alone. The location is not bad, and it is a state-of-the-art building, so there are no outdated parts. Above all, it had the greatest advantage of being able to meet Han Ha-rin anytime, anywhere.

,Investing in the company is also a way.'

,That's also annoying. And what if I invest in the company and the company goes bankrupt? Only I lose.'

Even if I had a lot of money, I didn't want to lose it.

,Then invest in yourself. Hunter equipment is expensive, but it's worth it.'

,Hmm.'

This didn't appeal to me either. Most of the equipment could be sorted out with Stormbreaker.

,Can't I buy Hunter performance with money?'

,The world doesn't revolve around money alone.'

,Even if I donate billions of won to the Hunter Association?'

,You'll just get a commendation and appear on the news once. That won't be a big achievement either. Large guilds donate tens of billions of won to the association every year.'

It's a kind of greasing the wheels. No matter how large a guild is, it cannot ignore the association.

,Yujin-seonbae. Let's get to the point. You didn't come here because of money, did you?'

,Oh, right. I heard there are some excellent alchemists under you?'

,Yes. It's thanks to them that I was able to create HB-1. They are still in charge of HB-1 production.'

,Is HB-1 selling well? I haven't heard any news about it lately.'

,It's selling well. Large guilds are placing a lot of orders. It's easy to get plants and crops that only grow in dungeons. So there's no harm to farmers.'

When HB-1 came out, there was a lot of talk. Some people liked it, while others didn't. They were farmers. They thought that if there was HB-1, the job of a farmer might disappear. They protested, and at some point, the protests disappeared.

,HB-1's weakness has worked as a strength. It sucks up the nutrients in the soil all at once, so it doesn't give the soil time to recover nutrients.'

So it's burdensome to use it for crops. The price of HB-1 also plays a part. Even if you use HB-1 and produce a large amount of crops, it's hard to break even.

,Hunters welcome HB-1. Thanks to this, the price of potions has dropped, and the mortality rate of Hunters is also decreasing.'

,That's a good thing.'

I put Bister I and Bister II on the desk. Ha Seunghee's eyes light up with interest when she sees the pills.

,Since Yujin-seonbae brought them, they must not be ordinary pills. What are the effects?'

,Bister I relieves pain and reduces suffering. It also slightly increases physical abilities. If you only take one pill, there are no side effects. Bister II drastically increases physical abilities. The problem is that it has some side effects, so I'm reluctant to use it.'

They are from the [Eternal Eden] world. The effects will be reduced. Most of the items brought from the game world have reduced effects when brought to reality. Pills are no exception.

,...Do you want to sell these too? I'd have to check how effective they are... but I don't think they'll make much money.'

,I'm not thinking of selling them. I brought them because I was wondering if mass production is possible.'

,I'll have to ask the alchemists. I think mass production is possible. But the problem is that we don't have the capacity. Where did you get these?'

,From the dungeon.'

,.......'

She didn't seem to believe me at all. But knowing that I had no intention of explaining, Ha Seunghee nodded and got up from her seat and brought the alchemists.

They looked even more exhausted than Ha Seunghee. Still, their eyes shone brightly because of the new drugs.

,I heard you're the one who brought HB-1. I heard about it from the director.'

,I'm Seong Yujin. We're all busy, so let's skip the detailed greetings and take a look at this. I got it from the dungeon.'

The alchemists approached with serious expressions and examined the pills. Their eyes sparkled and light emanated from their fingertips. It seems they are using abilities or alchemy. I was a complete layman who knew nothing about alchemy, so I quietly waited for them.

,It's quite interesting.'

,It seems to be a doping drug, but it has some side effects.'

,It's already a finished drug. If you try to eliminate or reduce the side effects, the efficacy of the drug will also decrease.'

,I'm curious about the main ingredients used to make this drug. I can't even tell using alchemy.'

The main ingredients the alchemists are talking about are creatures infected with the D virus. Bister I's main ingredient is a quadrupedal animal, and Bister II's main ingredient is a beast variant.

,If you take this, will you get a virus or something? The dungeon where I got this is a bit unusual.'

It would be a headache if the D virus broke out. Of course, even if you are infected with the D virus, not all humans immediately turn into zombies. Other substances are also needed for the D virus to be active. It's no coincidence that the zombie outbreak in [Eternal Eden] happened on the same day.

,No. The side effects are not of that kind. It's a blood pressure-related side effect.'

,There are no viral components. Even if you eat it, you won't get a virus.'

,Are you thinking of mass-producing and selling this too? I'm sorry to say this, but it's not worth it as a doping drug. There are already better doping potions and drugs being sold than these. The ingredients of the drug are interesting, but the effects are reduced.'

I nod. HB-1 is an irreplaceable substance. On the other hand, doping drugs that can replace Bister I and Bister II were everywhere and easily available. They are expensive but have almost no side effects.

The alchemists put down Bister I and Bister II as if they had lost interest.

I looked at them and thought of other things.

Antisper, which increases resistance to mental abilities for a certain period of time, and evolution serum, which evolves you with a 50/50 chance.

How would they react if they saw those?

,I don't even have the evolution serum. I have to go to the Eternal Eden world to get Antisper....'

I refuse to do anything annoying.

,Anyway, can you replicate it?'

,HB-1 is also a replica of the original. There's no reason why we can't do it with these drugs.'

The alchemists said with confident voices. I nodded and put the drugs in my pocket.
