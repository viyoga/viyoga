The chicken shop was a huge hit.

After the personal streamer named Fisher came and went, customers flocked in. Those customers also posted on social media, saying it was delicious. There were quite a few who raved about it, calling it the revolution of chicken.

,That Fisher guy's influence is incredible.'

I later found out that the man named Fisher was notorious enough to be called a store hunter. He would visit stores, taste the food, and give harsh reviews. That was Fisher's main content. However, very rarely, there were stores that Fisher gave favorable reviews to, and those stores all thrived.

Fisher targeted Yuyu Chicken for a harsh review, but instead, he praised it and left.

Thanks to that, all 300 chickens that I had prepared were sold out before evening.

,Neo London doesn't have a high population density compared to its area. All the people nearby who saw Fisher's broadcast must have come.'

Even though there was an opening boost, 300 chickens were sold in 8 hours. It was an unexpected mega-hit.

,This is a problem. I'm short on chicken.'

I do the curing at home with Yuria's help. Chicken meat is delivered automatically with just one order, so that's not a problem, but the problem was the curing and the sauce. With it selling so well, I couldn't rely on just one Yuria-ssi.

,It's too complicated to do it at the store. Frying alone is tough enough. I need to use a factory after all.'

I put the chicken in a box. Not forgetting to cast a warmth-preserving spell on the box, I packed some beer and headed to the X District Employment Agency.

Rose, who was preparing to leave work, smiled wryly at me.

,Who is this? What's a chicken shop owner doing here?

,I brought the chicken as I said I would.'

,I didn't think you'd actually bring it... Alright. That famous Korean chicken. Let's have a taste.'

,Has Korean chicken become that famous?

,It's a big deal on the internet. They say that disgusting chicken has been transformed into food from heaven. The fact that Neo London has been peaceful lately also played a part. Congratulations. Your shop has started on the road to success.'

She opened the chicken box and let out a small exclamation. The flavors were half and half.

,It definitely smells and looks different from the chicken I know.'

Chiik~ Ttak!

She popped open a can of beer, quickly took a sip, and bit into the chicken. Rose's usually calm eyes widened.

,...Wow. This is amazing? This is the best thing I've eaten lately. I see why that Fisher guy raved about Korean chicken.'

,I'm glad you like it.'

,It goes perfectly with beer. I think I'll eat it two or three times a month. So, what's the reason you came here? You didn't really come just to give me chicken, did you?

,I need money.'

,You're here to take on a request?

,No. I don't have time for that.'

,You've come to the wrong place if you need quick cash. Go to a bank. I don't play with my clients' money.'

,I can't go to a bank. It's dangerous with my current identity.'

My identity was meticulously crafted. But in the end, it was a fake ID. If the bank decided to investigate thoroughly, there was a chance I'd be caught. I had only just started on the road to success. I didn't want to take on that risk.

,I can't lend you money. If you need money, take on a request. There are a few simple requests that you could complete in a few hours.'

,Don't you want to invest in Yuyu Chicken?

,Hmm.'

,Actually, there were two tycoons among the customers who came to my chicken shop today who said they wanted to invest in Yuyu Chicken.'

She stared silently at the Korean chicken.

,...Indeed. It's worth investing in. If you've come to ask for investment, you must have a plan. Tell me the plan. I might be able to invest if I like it.'

,I'm aiming for a franchise. To do that, I need a factory.'

I tell her the plan that Yuria and I came up with together.

,...So, what you're saying is that the factory will receive the chicken, cure it, mass-produce the sauce, and provide it to the franchise stores every day?

,That's right. All the franchise stores have to do is fry the chicken at the exact time.'

,It's simple, which is good. You know the core of franchising well. But do you really need to build a factory? You could just commission an existing factory.'

,That's true in the short term. But I'm looking at the long term. And more than anything... the core of Yuyu Chicken is the curing and the sauce. I want to keep that secret as much as possible.'

,Hmm. Alright. How much investment do you want?

,20 million credits.'

,...That's not much? That'll only be enough to build the factory building? What about the machines?

,I'm a 5th-grade magician.'

,Ah. Magic engineering machines? I didn't know you were also skilled in magic engineering.'

,...I'm not that good. I'm planning to buy discarded machines cheaply and modify them. It's simple enough to cure the chicken and mix the sauce.'

,Alright. Let's write a contract. 20 million credits isn't a burdensome amount, so I can give it to you right away.'

,And I'd like to find about three trustworthy workers.'

,Workers to run the factory, you mean? What do you think this place is?

X Employment Agency.

It's a place that specializes in dealing with workers.

I built a factory in the T District.

I personally worked on the machines that went into the factory.

Magic engineering.

It sounds incredibly difficult at first glance, but the core is ultimately magic. It's about combining magic with machines to use them continuously.

,Complex magic engineering machines are impossible for me right now, but I can definitely handle simple ones.'

Washing the chicken, adding the specified ingredients to cure it, and adding the prepared ingredients to make the sauce in the specified proportions. These three processes were simple. It doesn't require complexity or precision.

The important thing is the proportions. It was a bit difficult to adjust that, so I decided to order it from a large corporation. They had made quite a few similar items, so the machine was delivered in just three days.

,As expected of a large corporation.'

The factory was built and started operating in ten days.

The factory cures 1,200 chickens a day. This is more than enough for the time being.

•••

The mega-hit chicken shop is very busy.

Customers are flooding in like waves. It was hard to even take a break. Karel, who was in charge of the hall, was groaning, so I hired one more part-timer. Karel brought his friend, so I was able to hire another part-timer in an instant.

,The kitchen is no problem.'

10 fryers. An additional 4 auxiliary fryers purchased. A total of 14 fryers can be run at full capacity.

Can I do that alone?

I could, even if no one else could.

Because I had the power of magic.

I use telekinesis. According to my will, the chicken comes out of the refrigerator and plunges into the hot fryer. At the same time, the alarm clock is pressed. When the alarm rings, I take the chicken out of the fryer and put it on a plate. I don't forget to mix in the sauce. I also take care of the dishes with telekinesis.

Using telekinesis, I easily did the work of 10 people.

Telekinesis consumed little mana, which made it possible. Instead, it was known to consume a lot of mental power, but that didn't really affect me.

,But it's not like I don't feel tired. I can do this for a while, but...'

If I keep doing this, I will reach my limit.

,I need to find someone to do the kitchen work.'

I need to make the chicken shop run well even without me. And gradually increase the number of franchise stores.

,Ultimately, I'll be sitting in a high place and just receiving reports. That's the goal, but... I still have a long way to go.'

There's no need to be impatient.

Business is going well. I just need to gradually climb up.

The problem broke out a few days later.

-Yuyu Chicken uses old oil.

The rumor started spreading, and customers started to decrease.

That was ridiculous. I thoroughly replace the cooking oil every time I fry 50 chickens. I don't just use cheap cooking oil, but also use oil that goes well with chicken.

As soon as I noticed the rumor, I posted rebuttals on the internet and inside the store. I even kindly explained that I change the oil tank every 50 chickens.

But the rumor was hard to catch.

,Here we go again.'

A man who had become a regular said while chewing on seasoned chicken. He was reading the explanation about the oil hanging on the wall. Since there were no other customers except him, I was able to ask him.

,Sir. Do you know something? I'll give you a can of beer as a service if you tell me in detail.'

,Huh? You don't know yet? It's that bastard, that bastard.'

He raised his hand and pointed to the fish and chips shop across the street.

,About three months ago, a fish and chips shop near here went bankrupt due to a similar rumor. That guy is a local here and has a lot of connections because he's from a gangster background. He's spreading the rumor through those connections.'

,I don't sell fish and chips either.'

,You sell fried food just the same. Who would want to eat fish and chips after eating this chicken? I don't know for sure, but that shop's sales must have been cut in half.'

,......'

I saw the owner of the shop across the street a while ago. He didn't seem to have a good personality at first glance.

,What should I do? It's obvious that he won't listen even if I go and say something... Should I secretly go to his place at night, disguise myself as a robber, and kill him?

,Boss. Just ignore it. This shop won't go bankrupt because of that rumor. People who know, know that the rumor is fake. Just because the number of customers has decreased a bit... is that enough to go bankrupt?

,Not enough to go bankrupt...'

Even though the number of customers had decreased, sales were booming.

People who are going to come will keep coming. This shop has become a well-known 'mat-jib' for those in the know. Oops. Customers arrived as soon as I finished speaking.

This customer was unique.

He was a butler wearing a black suit. With a neat atmosphere, he greeted as soon as he entered the store.

,Hello. I am Hyde, the butler from the Plat Baronetcy. Please pack one fried chicken and one seasoned chicken.'

The butler had dignity.

Not only that, but I felt power from the butler. The butler in front of me has at least 3rd grade power or higher.

I realize anew. Butlers and maids are by no means lower class citizens in Neo London.

,I'll prepare it right away.'

,I'm glad I came when there weren't many people. I was lucky.'

The butler looked at the explanation about the oil hanging on the wall. And he snorted as if he knew what had happened.

,People's jealousy and greed sometimes make them do stupid things. It seems that the boss is also in trouble.'

,I am in trouble.'

,Ignore it. There's no need for someone like the boss to step in. Rumors that are too far-fetched tend to die down.'

The butler's attitude was consistently polite.

I realized that the butler had noticed my level.
