,I'll kill them all...!'

I dashed towards the assassins.

The assassins were taken aback by the sudden charge. That's because I accurately saw their positions hidden in the darkness and moved accordingly.

However, the top-notch assassins quickly recovered from their bewilderment and scattered in all directions, surrounding me and throwing hidden weapons.

Even knowing the hidden weapons were coming from all directions, it was difficult to dodge them.

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, Evil Cut.

A large slash erupted from the tip of my sword, knocking away the hidden weapons flying from all directions. The assassins took out nets they had prepared instead of hidden weapons and threw them.

,A moment.'

My vision slowed down, and the nets fell slowly. The energy contained in the nets was not ordinary. Even using a moment, it seemed impossible to avoid them.

,They prepared this to deal with me. It's probably not an ordinary net.'

It's best not to ignore it.

Uuung.

I activated the Cheonma Shingong.

Kung!

My heart beat loudly. Accordingly, demonic energy coursed through my meridians. Vitality and power soared.

The blade seemed to hum, releasing black Sword energy. With the Geumje (restriction) lifted, I unleashed Ganggi (罡氣) without hesitation. If Ganggi was steel, Sword energy felt like plastic. I wasn't very satisfied.

,Still, it's enough to kill these bastards!'

I gritted my teeth and swung my sword.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Sword, Demonic Wind.

A black wind rose and swept through the front. The wind containing Sword energy tore the nets to shreds and mercilessly slaughtered even the assassins beyond them.

,Damn it. That's not the Ghost Cutting Sword Technique.'

,Don't panic. It's common for Martial artists to hide their skills. We just need to do our job.'

,...The hidden power should be moderate. That's... .'

The assassins fell silent. That's because I had leaped towards them. They moved nimbly, maintaining a proper distance from me, and mainly attacked by throwing hidden weapons.

,Annoying.'

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, One Wind Returns to Truth.

I used a moment to accelerate instantly. I beheaded one assassin who couldn't dodge. The assassin's head, soaring into the sky, had its eyes wide open. I grabbed the assassin's head with my left hand and threw it at another assassin like a baseball. The assassin gritted his teeth and punched away his comrade's head.

Two deaths. The assassins all retreated backward, creating distance. About 20m of distance. The battle paused briefly, entering a lull.

I focused on my breathing. Demonic energy leaked from my mouth.

,I won't use any more techniques or skills of the Cheonma Shingong.'

A tail that's too long gets stepped on. This place, Haga City, is where Chun Yu-un's eyes and ears reach. I must conserve the Cheonma Shingong as much as possible. Of course, since it's not the Cheonma Shingong of this world's Demonic Sect, they might not recognize it even if I show it off openly... but I should be careful just in case.

,Damn it. Because of the Geumje, using the Cheonma Shingong is sucking away my stamina and internal energy.'

The Geumje that Milyeong placed on me is sturdy and secretive. However, it had the characteristic of being able to be released immediately when I wanted it. The problem was that it was difficult to exert my full power because of that characteristic.

I'd rather use the Lightning Sky Style if possible. The Cheonma Shingong was too rough. If I'm careless, the Cheonma Shingong could break the Geumje.

I felt killing intent.

Four assassins prepared their next move.

,I'll attack first. A moment.'

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 4]

As the world slowed down, I realized what they were preparing. Two assassins turned their hands behind their backs and were spraying some kind of liquid on the floor. I sensed it was some kind of ritualistic thing. It wasn't unusual. These days, assassins do anything to kill their targets. Poison is natural, and they use curses without hesitation.

I lowered the tip of my sword. The tip of my sword touched the ground. I drew a line on the ground with the tip of my sword and raised it upward.

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, Reverse Ghost Chase.

A pile of dirt rose like a small wave, covering the assassins.

,Did he notice us using the spell formation...!'

I broke through the pile of dirt and killed the assassin in the front. I stabbed his neck with my sword and slashed downward. Blood and entrails spilled onto the dirt floor.

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique, Evil Ghost Ten Kill.

I squeezed out my internal energy and launched a cross-shaped Sword energy. The remaining three assassins flung their bodies to the side to avoid the Sword energy. I threw the sword in my hand. Thwack! It hit one assassin's forehead accurately. I didn't stop there. I ran and pulled out the sword stuck in the corpse.

,Two left.'

The assassins who had jumped up glanced at each other and then split in both directions, starting to run away. I chased after the one on the right first.

,I can't let them get away. Here... I have no choice but to use the Lightning Sky Style.'

Acceleration.

A moment.

Lightning Sky Style, Gale Lightning.

Wrapping my body in blue electricity, I stabbed the fleeing assassin in the back with my sword. It pierced his heart. Before he could even fall, I turned my body backward and ran.

The assassin left alone entered an alleyway. He seemed to intend to use the alleyway to shake me off.

,I have the Heavenly Eye.'

The Heavenly Eye could change my point of view. I could look down at the fleeing assassin from the sky. He didn't know that fact.

Pajijik.

I unleashed lightning energy through the Yongcheon blood point on the soles of my feet. I kicked off the dirt floor and moved forward. My legs became heavy, but my speed was getting faster. The scenery changed rapidly. I felt like someone was pulling me.

I used a moment for an even faster start.

,...!'

I opened my eyes wide. I saw electricity in front of me. No, was this electricity? It was a little different from electricity.

,It's connected to the direction I'm going. This is definitely... .'

Rails. There were rails laid out on the path I was going. I put my foot on the rails as if possessed by something. I became lightning and sped along the rails. In almost one second, I caught up to the fleeing assassin.

,...Did you use teleportation magic?!'

The horrified assassin dodged my stab and raised his left hand to counterattack. A small blade about a hand span long protruded from the back of his hand, piercing through his glove. A thin Sword energy coated the blade.

But I was faster. He had to swing his arm to kill me, and I could spread electricity from my body with just my will. The difference was significant.

,Kkeueueueueue!!'

The electrocuted assassin's eyes rolled back. His body collapsed powerlessly along with the smell of burning meat.

,I killed them all.'

The moment I sighed and released the strength from my body. The assassin's sword, which was falling, moved. I had used all my Moment stacks. I hastily stepped back and dodged the sword. The sword grazed my forearm. Blood splattered. Fortunately, the wound wasn't deep. It was just a slight cut.

,What the fuck.'

Annoyed by the unexpected attack, I kicked the assassin's head with my foot. Unfortunately, he was already dead. As an outlet for my anger, I chopped up all the assassins' corpses and returned to the inn.

Since assassins might attack again, I decided to save my complete recovery. Fortunately, there were no assassin attacks.

I woke up from a sound sleep at the inn and distorted my face.

,I feel pain in my right forearm. My right arm doesn't move well either.'

My right forearm was where the assassin's sword had cut me yesterday. It seemed like the sword I was hit with yesterday had poison on it. I took off my top and checked the wound. The skin on my right forearm had turned blackish-green.

It was impossible to expel the poison with energy circulation. It felt like the poison wasn't spreading throughout my body, but only altering my right forearm.

,Should I use complete recovery?'

After hesitating, I shook my head. This might be an opportunity. An opportunity to draw out Chu Jeo, who was hiding in the Seon Family and watching the situation!

,I'll go find him and formally request a duel. The guy who confirms that my arm isn't normal might accept the duel.'

And then, I'll use complete recovery at the decisive moment. The startled guy will lose his posture, and I won't miss that opportunity to kill him.

,I should think of a victory line. Go fuck your mom in hell. ...No. That's a bit cliché. What would be good?'

I went outside the inn.

Yesterday, I was tired and just entered an inn that was open, but when I saw it in the daytime, the inn wasn't good at all. There were stains everywhere on the floor as if they didn't clean properly, and the furniture was so old that it looked like it would break if you touched it. When I looked around, there weren't many guests either.

I went down to the first floor and sat on a chair. The waiter ran over with quick steps.

,Young hero! Are you here for lodging! What would you like for breakfast?!'

,...What do you have for breakfast?'

,Our inn's chef is very skilled. We can make almost anything.'

,You talk too much. What's the most popular dish?'

,Customers look for fish porridge the most in the morning.'

Haga City had a river flowing to the west, so there were many fish dishes.

,A bowl of fish porridge.'

,Yes! I'll bring it to you right away!'

The waiter really brought a bowl of fish porridge right away. It was the same system as soup and rice. They made a lot in advance and then scooped it out into bowls when customers came.

I looked at the fish porridge. Maybe it was because the visuals weren't good, but my appetite disappeared. I stared blankly at the bowl of fish porridge.

There was no need to force myself to eat it. I could take out the food I had put in my inventory and eat it. The inventory was full of Yuria's special lunch boxes that were no less impressive than a 7-star hotel.

But I usually eat at least one spoonful of local food. The fact that it's food from another world stimulates my curiosity. And if I find a food that suits my taste, I'm very happy and get the recipe from the chef.

,.......'

I raised my left arm. The waiter, who was leaning against the wall and resting, ran over right away.

,Yes, young hero. What can I help you with? A glass of water is 1 copper nyang.'

,Eat this.'

,Yes?'

,This fish porridge. You take a spoonful first.'

,The world of Martial artists is ruthless. Martial artists don't eat food carelessly. Being wary of poison is a virtue of Martial artists.'

,A virtue...?'

,Anyway, eat it. Prove that there's no poison in the fish porridge.'

,Oh my. I understand, young hero. You really are a Martial artist to the bone.'

The waiter took a spoon and plunged it into the fish porridge.

,Wait.'

,Yes?'

,Who told you to eat the fish meat? Eat the broth.'

,...Yes.'

The waiter ate the fish porridge. There was no poison, as he was fine even after eating it. His expression was also calm.

,Is it okay now? I've only been working at the inn for 5 years, and you're the first person like you.'

,.......'

I picked up the bowl of fish porridge and dropped it on the floor.

,H-huh! Young hero?! What are you doing! I told you there's no poison!'

,This was a mistake. Bring a new one.'

,A mistake?! I clearly saw it! You grabbed the bowl and dropped it on the floor! Damn it! Cleaning this up is my job!'

,I said it was a mistake.'

I fiddled with the sword hilt with my right hand, which didn't move well. The waiter turned pale and immediately smiled.

,M-mistakes happen to everyone. I'll bring a new one.'

The waiter ran to the kitchen. I watched the waiter. For reference, the Heavenly Eye could see through things.

The waiter took a ladle and poured fish porridge into a bowl. He looked at the cook's expression and then put something he took out of his pocket into the fish porridge.

,Hmph. He was an assassin after all. My intuition can't be wrong.'

I had some experience being assassinated in other worlds, so my instincts were good in this area.

,He ate the fish porridge without any problems earlier. Did he take an antidote in advance? Or is it a poison that activates later?'

Well, that didn't matter.

The waiter approached with a new fish porridge.

,I brought a new fish porridge, young hero.'

,Good work.'

I grabbed the back of the waiter's head with my left hand. I pulled my left hand as it was and plunged the waiter's face into the hot fish porridge that had been boiling in the pot until just now.

,Eueueueueueeup!'

,Fish porridge is your last supper. Eat the leftovers... in hell.'

I drew my sword like lightning and beheaded the waiter.

It was a clean beheading. Too clean. The severed body couldn't even accept death and flailed its arms.

,Kyaaaaaaak!'

,Heoeoeoeok!'

,M-murder!!'

The surroundings became a scene of chaos in an instant. I kicked the waiter's body and shouted.

,Boss, come out!!!'
