I went outside with Yuria.

A carriage I had called in advance was waiting at the entrance.

It was a fancy carriage for nobles. The horse was docile, and the coachman was dignified.

,"Your destination is the Priwood Marquisate, right? I will escort you safely."

,"Please do."

I said politely and stared in wonder at the real, living horse, not a mechanical one. A living horse was in no way superior to a mechanical horse. It dies when it gets old, you have to feed it constantly, it can get sick, and it doesn't run as long or as powerfully as a mechanical horse.

But the fact that it wasn't a machine captivated the nobles. Nobles favored carriages pulled by living horses unless it was absolutely necessary otherwise. That was the style of Neo London, and the noble culture.

I took Yuria's hand to help her get into the carriage easily.

,"Thank you, but I am merely a servant."

,"It's okay when no one's watching. And to me, you're a lady too."

As soon as we got in, the carriage started moving. The inside of the carriage was quiet. There was no rattling sound either. Neo London's advanced technology was in the carriage, not the horse.

I looked out the window. This city is amazing. It's futuristic yet old. It develops technology but clings to old culture. It talks about the freedom of citizens, but the class system continues to be maintained. Neo London was a city-state full of contradictions.

,"It's my first time meeting a Neo London noble... I'm quite nervous."

,"Don't worry too much. Master-nim will do well."

While I was recalling the etiquette of nobles in my head, the carriage arrived at the Priwood Marquisate. I looked out the window and exclaimed. A noble mansion that I would only see in history or fantasy was unfolding before my eyes.

,'It's incredibly big. The security is no joke either. There's even a barrier... If you look closely, it's all state-of-the-art.'

Neo London nobles hate machines. But that doesn't mean they reject machines. They use them if necessary. What they really hate and despise is artificial intelligence.

Yuria opened the carriage door and got out first. I slowly got out of the carriage after her. Manners and composure are the virtues of a gentleman. You shouldn't move hastily.

,"Eugene Meyer-nim. Welcome to the Priwood Marquisate. I am Herman, the head butler."

The butler standing in front of the carriage greeted me politely. Posture, attire, voice, etc., there was nothing to criticize. And I checked the power he possessed and quietly gasped. The energy I felt from the head butler was stronger than mine.

,"...I didn't expect the Priwood family's head butler to come out to greet me in person."

,"Meyer-nim is a guest invited by the Marchioness-nim. One of my duties is to personally attend to the Marchioness-nim's guests."

,"I'm glad you're treating me so well. I have one attendant. She will personally attend to me at the dinner banquet. Is that alright?"

,"Of course. Now, please come inside. The Marchioness-nim is waiting."

,"I can't keep the Marchioness waiting. Yuria."

,"Yes. Master-nim. I have the box."

I followed Head Butler Herman through the garden. I slowly turned my head and checked the surroundings. It was full of green. The flowerbeds were filled with gorgeous flowers in full bloom. The flowers planted in the flowerbeds were all different. It seems difficult to manage.

I found a gardener. The maid wearing a maid uniform was carefully cutting branches with large scissors over 1m long.

,'...Rank 3. No, is it Rank 4...? She's the kind of woman who wouldn't find it strange to cut off someone's head instead of a tree branch.'

I also found several other butlers and maids.

,'Each and every one of them is extraordinary. Even a decent military unit wouldn't stand a chance.'

The interior of the mansion was also amazing.

The splendor was dazzling. What was even more surprising was that items worth at least tens of millions of credits were casually displayed in the hallway.

I knew the Priwood Marquisate was wealthy, but... I didn't know it would be this much.

,'They must have decorated it like this on purpose to intimidate the guests who come.'

I was a little intimidated.

I arrived at the reception room.

Herman carefully knocked on the ornate door.

,"Marchioness-nim. Meyer-nim has arrived."

,"Come in."

I quickly scanned the reception room.

The room was naturally luxurious. I wasn't surprised because I expected it. What was surprising was the Priwood Marchioness-nim.

She was beautiful. Her blonde hair sparkled, and her skin was as white and fair as jade. The white dress decorated with jewels was beyond my estimation. She was covering her mouth with a wine-colored feather fan, but I could tell how outstandingly beautiful she was just from her exposed nose and green eyes.

,'I heard she wasn't young... but she looks like she's in her early to mid-20s.'

Her ears were pointed.

Elf.

To be exact, a human with elf genes. And a congenital factor.

Herman went and stood behind the Marchioness-nim. I approached her and greeted her according to etiquette.

,"I am Eugene Meyer. I work as a mercenary and run a small food store."

,"Yuyu Chicken, right? The chicken they sell there is delicious. Was it called Korean Chicken?"

,"Yes. I recreated the cuisine of Great Korea, a country that existed in ancient times."

,"I am Garmelia Priwood. My husband is the head of this family. Have a seat."

I slowly sat down opposite her.

,"Unexpected."

,"What is, may I ask?"

,"I've met countless mercenaries so far. But only 2-3 mercenaries knew how to show respect like you. They were all from butler backgrounds, but you're not one of them."

,"Yes. I am a stranger. I am very interested in Neo London's culture, so I learned this and that. I'm glad I wasn't rude."

,"You're more polite than my cousin's grandson. I don't know who taught you... but you learned very well."

She asked me this and that.

Starting with why a Rank 5 magician is working as a mercenary, the reason I settled in Neo London, what I did before coming to Neo London, etc.

There were several annoying questions, but I answered without showing it.

,"I heard about what happened a few hours ago. You killed the Gomez Clinic mother and daughter and the securities, and used my name to the London Guards, right?"

I immediately bowed my head.

,"I apologize if I offended you. At that time, I prioritized the Marchioness-nim's dress."

,"It was a little unpleasant, but it's just a trivial matter that I can overlook since the dress is intact. Explain the whole story. I smell something interesting from you."

Her green eyes sparkle, and her pointed ears perk up.

There is no hatred or fear of the murderer. There is only curiosity.

,'It's just as Yuria said.'

Yuria said while riding in the carriage. Most nobles, including the Priwood Marchioness-nim, don't care how many citizens die. The only time they care is when they suffer losses.

I summarized this incident from the beginning. She frowned when I mentioned taking the Blue Distortion and going to the Gomez Clinic, and that I subjugated the Prong gang as Lana Gomez's condition. She folded her feather fan and slammed the table irritably.

,"So that's why the Droid's fighter appeared in Neo London. Traitor to humanity...!"

,"I used my hidden card to deal with him."

,"Keep talking. Why did you make such a fuss at the Gomez Clinic?"

,"They were trying to sell me to Cavallia Industries. Have you heard of the Cavallia Research Institute?"

,"Hmm. I've heard of it... They say they're conducting heinous experiments. I thought the Gomez mother and daughter were good people... You can know the depth of ten fathoms of water, but you can't know a fathom of a person's heart..."

Gomez shook her head.

After the story ended, she looked at Yuria.

,"I want to see the dress."

I gestured to Yuria. Yuria came over and placed it on the table.

She exclaimed as she watched Yuria's movements.

,"An excellent maid. There is dignity in her actions. They say you can know the master by looking at the servant. I can feel your dignity."

,"Thank you, Marchioness-nim."

,"How did you hire the maid?"

,"I met her by chance in Neo London. I came to Neo London empty-handed, so I needed someone's help, and she needed experience."

,"What is her maid rank?"

The Marchioness-nim asked Yuria. Yuria answered calmly without panicking.

,"I don't have a qualification. I'm trying to get a certificate after gaining experience."

,"Is that so? Why don't you gain experience under our family?"

,"I'm sorry, Marchioness-nim. I only have one master-nim."

,"Your loyalty is admirable. Eugene Meyer, I'm sorry. As you can see, I sometimes lose my mind when I see good talent."

She didn't seem sorry at all. I suppressed the urge to slap this woman's cheek and answered.

,"No. I'm okay."

The box opened and the dress was revealed.

It was a gorgeous dress on the outside, but it became even more gorgeous when magic was activated. It was like a starry night transferred to a dress.

,'Honestly, I don't think this dress needs to be made with magic, but...'

How would I know the minds of nobles?

The Marchioness-nim, who was looking at the dress, frowned.

,"The light is weaker than I thought. Herman. Can you find a seamstress?"

,"...I will try my best to find one, but I can't guarantee that it will be repaired before the party."

,"I don't like it, but should I wear it as it is...?"

I stared intently at the magic dress and opened my mouth.

,"I can repair it on the spot. It's not difficult to make the light stronger."

,"...You? I'm not ignorant about magic. This dress may look simple, but it's intricately intertwined. Do you see the constellations here? You can tell that the brightness of the stars is different."

,"Yes. That's right. If you do it wrong, the balance will be completely broken. But I'm confident. I won't act like a child and throw a tantrum. The owner of this dress is the Marchioness-nim, so the Marchioness-nim should choose."

Chwaak!

She spread her fan and moved it gently, lost in thought.

,"...I'll entrust it to you once. I hope you don't disappoint me."

,"Yes. May I have the dress?"

I received the dress, opened Astral, and used an instant. I check the magic structure engraved on the dress. It was intricately intertwined. But that's closer to a lock placed for magic security. If you look at it one by one, it wasn't that complicated.

Mana moves. The mana containing my will instantly took control of the dress's magic.

I increased the brightness of the starlight.

,"Is this brightness enough?"

,"...Hoo. You understood and took control of the magic in an instant? Amazing. Increase the brightness a little more. Hmm. That's good. I'll pay you for this."

,"No, it's okay. This is my favor to the Marchioness-nim."

,"I don't usually accept favors, but... I'll remember this. Herman. Is the food ready?"

,"Yes. I will guide you to the banquet hall."

The dinner banquet began. I faced dishes that filled a table over 10m long. The taste was excellent. It was indeed a noble's meal.

I couldn't eat comfortably. There were many etiquette rules to follow.

,'I feel like I'm going to get indigestion trying to eat while paying attention to etiquette. I want to drink cola.'

After finishing the banquet and saying goodbye, I returned home.
