I arrived in Suwon. Looking down from above the city. No dungeon break or anything of the sort had occurred. The city was quiet and peaceful.

Thunder Heaven Style — Electromagnetic Wave.

Electromagnetic waves radiate outward from me. The city's situation paints itself inside my head. Even if Park Su-ho is concealing his presence, as long as he doesn't change his face, he can't escape my detection.

'Not in this direction.'

I shifted locations little by little, using the electromagnetic waves. It's not as if I can cover the entire city with them in one go.

To state the result — I couldn't find Park Su-ho.

'Is he not in Suwon? Or maybe it's a situation where electromagnetic waves can't detect him.'

Electromagnetic waves are near-perfect for physical detection. I can sense even an ant crawling on the ground.

'If it's non-physical, that's a different story. Electromagnetic waves can't detect mana either.'

Fortunately, I had a means of finding things that were abnormal.

Landing on the rooftop of a suitable building, I pulled Storm Breaker from my inventory.

It takes the form of a spear. Even in non-combat situations, it was inconvenient to carry around.

'Having to have Storm Breaker out to gain experience is also inconvenient. I've reinforced Thunder Eye Golden Pupil and have leftover experience points, so let's use them.'

『Growth (Lv.42 exp: 0/25,600)

‣Absorbs a portion of the life force and soul of slain targets to gain experience.

‣Experience can be used to strengthen abilities.

‣Currently absorbed experience (3,771/14,200)』

『Form Transformation (Lv.1 exp: 0/500)

‣Can transform into any desired form.

‣Currently available forms: Armor, Spear, Sword.』

I spent a total of 3,500 experience points to raise the level as high as possible.

『Form Transformation (Lv.4 exp: 0/4,000)

‣Can transform into any desired form.

‣Currently available forms: Armor, Spear, Sword, Shield, Club, Dagger.』

'All combat forms. At least the dagger is something I can carry around.'

I transformed it into a dagger. The massive spear compressed and became a dagger. Since the design can be altered somewhat within the fixed types, I made the dagger as small as possible. 10cm. It fits in a pocket. Not that I had any intention of actually putting it in a pocket.

I pulled a dagger sheath from my inventory and hung it at my waist. Honestly, I didn't like this either, but for now it was the best option.

『Using Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.』

Lightning settles into both pupils.

It should last about 15 minutes in real time. Opening my eyes, I immediately turned my head in one direction. The outskirts of Suwon. In a place without people, something heterogenous was visible.

'A barrier? Or a passage? Seeing as I didn't notice it until I used Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, it's no ordinary barrier.'

There's no way something like that would coincidentally be where Park Su-ho disappeared.

I flew through the sky using Flying Thunder God and moved. Seeing it up close, I could tell what it was called.

'Kamikakushi. A barrier that creates and pulls in a hidden world. It severs connection with the outside, but it's not completely blocked. Tracking the one who used that barrier — at the current level of Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, it's impossible.'

It was the moment I tried to get closer to the Kamikakushi.

A foreign man in a suit suddenly appeared before my eyes and swung his fist. A surprise attack using spatial teleportation. I'd caught the precursor phenomenon with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, so I responded calmly. My kick struck his torso before his fist could reach me.

KWANG!

The man was pushed back. Despite being kicked with considerable force, he didn't fall — meaning his physical abilities were quite exceptional.

"You're that bastard I saw at the demonstration earlier."

Short brown hair, a large foreigner. I remembered him as the third strongest among the hunters who attended the demonstration. The man with three piercings in his right ear had tattoos on his face that hadn't been there before. Tattoos that moved as if alive.

'Even with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, I can't fully grasp the tattoos. A tattoo ability like Park Su-ho's? To say that, though, the tattoos are concentrated only on his face. I should consider it a similar type but a different ability.'

Normally I would have killed anyone who attacked me on the spot, but the fact that his ability used tattoos similar to Park Su-ho's piqued my interest. More than anything, he hadn't targeted my woman.

'Let's keep him alive and squeeze out some information first. I'm also curious why he's after Park Su-ho.'

"...Thunder Heaven. Why are you here."

"I'm the one who's more curious. Drake. Why are you here? Even granting that you got here before me using spatial teleportation — inter-city teleportation should be difficult because of the barriers. How'd you do it?"

"......You know my name. I have no intention of harming you. Go back. I promise I won't harm Korea either."

"But you'll kill Park Su-ho? You've already done harm."

“…….”

Drake clamped his mouth shut and clenched his fists.

He vanished and reappeared behind my back. His fist aimed precisely at my head.

Already aware, I tilted my head to the side to dodge the fist and swung my left foot backward.

Drake, struck, was sent flying back. Then he scrambled to his feet and charged again.

That process repeated several times. I was fine; Drake was a bloody mess.

"I've got a rough idea of how tough you are now. Seems you need to focus depending on distance when using spatial teleportation, too."

"...How the hell do you know where I'll appear in advance? Can you see the future or something?"

"You want a lot. Do I look like I'd explain?"

I clenched my fist. Crackle. Lightning energy flashed once.

Drake used spatial teleportation. Right. It's a feint. He appeared then immediately vanished, revealing himself on my left.

'My reaction was a bit slow there.'

[Using Flash Moment. Remaining stacks: 11]

The moment he appeared on the left, I twisted my body and drove my fist into his face.

KWAAANG!

With a sound like a bomb exploding, his unconscious body crumpled. I grabbed his head with one hand.

'If I leave him and he wakes up and runs, it'll be annoying. Pressure points won't help either since he has comrades.'

I stood before the Kamikakushi and looked up at the sky.

That magical gaze I'd been feeling since earlier.

"I know you're watching. You're probably the one who used the Kamikakushi. Let's meet in a bit, mage."

I raised one hand.

BZZZZZZT!

I grabbed hundreds of strands of lightning and hurled them at the Kamikakushi.

The lightning smashed through the severed entrance of the Kamikakushi and burrowed inside.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God

Wrapping my entire body in lightning, I entered the forcibly opened Kamikakushi together with Drake.

I appeared in a sky seething with lightning.

Below, I sensed a powerful presence and Park Su-ho's presence. Glancing at the situation, it was a dangerous moment. I dropped straight in front of Park Su-ho.

"Eugene-hyung!!! I believed in you!!!"

This brat. He's been on the internet lately and his meme game has improved.

"Yeah. It's hyung. Hyung can come anywhere."

After a rough reply, I looked seriously at the fierce woman before my eyes. Semi-long silver hair with green, emerald-like eyes. On the upper left half of her body were tattoos that moved like Park Su-ho's.

I confirmed her name with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil. Ines Tereze. She was frozen stiff with tension. The aura blade surging from her raised greatsword didn't diminish, though.

'She's pretty. Could this be something Park Su-ho did wrong?'

If I hand over Park Su-ho, would she become my woman?

— is what I'd like to say, but the hostility she's directing at me is no joke.

"...Things have gone wrong. I had no plan to face Thunder Heaven, but... seeing what happened to Drake, fleeing is impossible too. In that case, rather...!"

Having made her resolve, she drew mana with all her might. The surrounding air currents shift from the aftershock of mana. It doesn't stop there — the asphalt road splits and small stones float into the air. The mana concentrates into her greatsword.

'She uses mana crudely. What's astonishing is that even using mana like that, there's no sign of it diminishing. No — even if it diminishes, it recovers immediately? Nearly infinite mana.'

However, just because mana is infinite doesn't mean she can use that infinite mana all at once. Her body rapidly accumulates strain. She's staked everything on a single strike.

I threw Drake, whom I'd been holding, to Park Su-ho behind me. It'd be a problem if he got caught up and died for no reason.

"That guy. Hold onto him well."

"Hyung! Just dodge it!"

"Too late."

Ines swings her greatsword.

A massive blue slash tears through the asphalt road and flies at me. I swung the Hwaryeon Bido summoned from my inventory. Red lightning burst from the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido.

Thunder Heaven Style — Ultimate Technique — Seal Lightning.

That slash is, in the end, compressed mass of mana.

If so, Seal Lightning — which has the property of pressing down on mana and breaking through — would be effective.

My prediction hit the mark.

The moment the slash touched my blade, it lost its momentum. Because the mana didn't disperse immediately, I had to withstand the slash with my own strength.

'If it were an ordinary sword, it would have broken.'

The Hwaryeon Bido won't break from just this much.

BZZZZZZZZT!

Red lightning surging from the blade wrapped around the slash and gnawed at it. Once cracked, the slash shattered like a broken vessel and vanished in an instant.

"Ah."

Ines let out a short gasp and staggered. Blood streamed from her nose. She'd taken internal damage.

I confirmed that my hand gripping the hilt was covered in blood. If it hadn't been for Seal Lightning, I might have lost an arm to that slash.

"That slash just now would have been threatening even to an S-rank hunter. I'll acknowledge it."

“…….”

Ines barely kept from collapsing, trying to raise her greatsword with trembling hands. The greatsword wouldn't rise.

I kicked off the ground. Appearing before Ines, I thrust my fist forward.

"Belly punch."

PFFFT!

"Keuk...!"

I moderated my strength appropriately for her, who was already internally injured. At least not enough to kill. She vomited blood and fainted as if falling into my arms.

'I hit her gently — she's not dying, right?'

She was still alive. I pulled out a top-grade recovery potion and sprayed and fed it to her body.

I didn't forget to subtly grope her chest and ass either. Since my body blocked the view, Park Su-ho behind me probably didn't see.
