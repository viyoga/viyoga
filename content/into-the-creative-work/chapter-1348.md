The car crossed the Military Demarcation Line through Gangwon Province and entered North Korea.

Just because we entered North Korea didn't mean anything special happened. The air was the same. It's just that the road was pretty awful.

,Here. Stop."

The van, which had been speeding along the bumpy road for a while, stopped in the middle of the road at Jang Juseok-ssi's command. And we got out of the car, fully armed.

Jang Juseok-ssi's ability is Blood Control. It wasn't just the ability to manipulate blood. He also had the ability to track a target using their blood.

,Which direction specifically?"

Kim Sieom-ssi asked. Jang Juseok-ssi raised his index finger and pointed to the forest.

,This forest. Oh Ganghu is in this forest."

,He probably didn't come here to rest... It seems he noticed we were tracking him and is trying to shake us off here. Jang Juseok-ssi, is it possible to know the exact location?"

,The margin of error is about 30m. I can't do any better than that, no matter what."

,There's a high probability that Oh Ganghu is lying in ambush. I think we need a plan."

Kim Sieom-ssi said, touching his baseball cap. He wanted a solid plan.

Shin Bora-ssi, who had been silent until now, stepped forward.

,The ambushes that a fugitive like Oh Ganghu and his group can choose are limited. Now that we've confirmed the possibility of an ambush, we won't be caught if we're careful. It's Oh Ganghu, exhausted from running away. The best thing to do is to attack right now without giving him time to recover his strength."

,Shin Bora-ssi. It only takes 5 minutes to make a plan. Let's be rational."

,If you want to make a plan, do it among yourselves. I'm going to catch Oh Ganghu."

Shin Bora-ssi confidently walked forward. I followed her and said.

,I agree with Shin Bora-ssi's opinion. What's the point of a plan when we've come all the way here? Let's finish this quickly and go back."

Kim Sieom-ssi's expression twisted in an unsightly way. Jang Juseok-ssi, with his arms crossed, followed behind us.

,I agree with Kim Sieom-ssi that we need a plan... but more than half of the four of us are refusing the plan, so there's nothing I can do."

,Tch, damn it. I'm going to report this to the Association."

Kim Sieom-ssi grumbled irritably and followed behind us. He had no choice. It was too far to turn back now.

We found Oh Ganghu and his four subordinates resting under a tree. They were a mess. Their clothes were covered in blood and dirt, and their faces were full of fatigue. Unlike us, who came here by car, they ran all the way here on foot.

Oh Ganghu smirked when he saw us.

,I thought the Association's professional tracking team was after me... but it was just a bunch of kids."

Before we could take out our weapons and rush at him, Oh Ganghu waved his hand first. His subordinates all put on gas masks. And black smoke poured out of Oh Ganghu's wrist and spread in all directions.

,It's poison gas. Be careful."

Jang Juseok-ssi said. It was unnecessary meddling. Because no one here doesn't know Oh Ganghu's ability.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷閃).

I swung Hwaryeon Bido faster than anyone else. Blue Sword energy wrapped in red lightning flew towards Oh Ganghu. Oh Ganghu didn't move. Instead, his subordinates wearing gas masks stepped forward. They all drew swords from their waists and lightly blocked the Sword energy.

Shin Bora-ssi stretched out her hand into the air. A long hammer was created in her hand. It looked similar to a sledgehammer. It felt luxurious because it was silver.

She held the hammer with both hands and slammed it into the ground with all her might.

Kwaang!

The shockwave occurred a beat late, in an unexpected place. The shockwave suddenly exploded under Oh Ganghu's feet. Oh Ganghu frowned and limped.

,Tsk... you're using such a boring ability. I was going to capture the bitch, but... I've changed my mind. Kill them all."

,Yes, Master."

His subordinates rushed towards us in perfect order.

Jang Juseok-ssi and Kim Sieom-ssi stepped forward. Jang Juseok-ssi tore open a blood pack with his hand and manipulated the blood, and Kim Sieom-ssi held his sword and indifferently aimed at the enemies.

,We'll take care of these guys."

,Shin Bora-ssi, Seong Yujin-ssi. You two take care of Oh Ganghu. We don't even expect you to capture him alive, so kill him as quickly as possible."

Jang Juseok-ssi and Kim Sieom-ssi began to fight the enemies. Shin Bora-ssi didn't even look back at them and headed towards Oh Ganghu with an indifferent expression. She approached, stepping on the gait, as she couldn't let the prey be taken away right in front of her eyes.

,Did you think you could do anything to me with just the two of you? Did I look easy to you?"

Oh Ganghu's body grew larger. His muscles swelled, and blood vessels burst in his eyes, causing bloody tears to flow. He had created a drug to strengthen his body and injected it into himself.

,Oh Ganghu. I will execute you."

Shin Bora-ssi's hammer changed. The size was about that of a toy hammer. But it was somehow threatening because of its flashy design made of red metal. She swung the small hammer at Oh Ganghu. Oh Ganghu spread out his large hand and grabbed the hammer. The moment the hammer touched Oh Ganghu's body, an explosion occurred.

,Kkeuaaaaaaak!"

Oh Ganghu staggered and screamed. His right hand had been caught in the explosion and blown away. Shin Bora-ssi still swung the hammer with an indifferent face. Oh Ganghu panicked and stepped back, avoiding the hammer.

Shin Bora-ssi, who was about to cling to Oh Ganghu, suddenly vomited blood. She was poisoned. Shin Bora-ssi calmly drank an antidote.

,Do you think you can be safe from my poison with that measly antidote? I promise you, who blew off my right hand, you won't die easily."

Oh Ganghu glared at Shin Bora-ssi, who was vomiting blood, and growled.

I seized the opportunity.

,Acceleration, Instant."

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 5]

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning (雷光).

I was aiming for Oh Ganghu's neck.

Oh Ganghu reacted to my attack. He hurriedly leaned his body back. The blade containing red lightning barely grazed his neck.

,This bastard...!"

Oh Ganghu fell backward and swung his only left hand. Black poison gas spewed out and clung to my body. I stopped breathing, but it was useless. The poison gas clung to my skin and began to be absorbed as it was.

I felt nauseous and my limbs became heavy.

,Hehe. How does it feel to have your limbs paralyzed? You'll be as scared as if you're dying alive. Hurry up and take out the antidote and eat it."

He was aiming for the moment I took the antidote. It was so obvious that I couldn't even laugh.

Instead of taking the antidote, I operated the Lightning Heaven Style.

Pajijik. Pajik.

Lightning flowed through my meridians and burned away all the poison. The nausea disappeared, and my limbs became light again. Oh Ganghu's face immediately crumpled.

,Damn it! Of all people, I can't believe there's a guy in the tracking team who's not susceptible to poison...!"

Oh Ganghu turned his body back as the situation became unfavorable. He was trying to escape by scattering poison gas like a skunk. Shin Bora-ssi and I moved at the same time. Shin Bora-ssi's hammer shattered Oh Ganghu's legs, and my sword cut off Oh Ganghu's neck.

Oh Ganghu died.

But the situation wasn't over. Shin Bora-ssi and I rushed towards Oh Ganghu's subordinates.

Shin Bora-ssi and I simultaneously distanced ourselves from Kim Sieom-ssi. We aimed our swords at Kim Sieom-ssi with a slight sense of confusion.

,Why did you kill Jang Juseok-ssi? Did you dislike him that much?"

,Jang Juseok-ssi's ability is annoying. An ability that can track someone's location just by having their blood... It's an ability that's too annoying."

Kim Sieom-ssi said leisurely. Shin Bora-ssi narrowed her already narrow eyes and glared at Kim Sieom-ssi.

,Who are you."

,You don't need to know."
