Thanks to the Vajra Divine Lightning, I quickly escaped from the hole in the Underworld. As soon as I came out, the hole in the Underworld closed and disappeared without a trace.

Thunder Sound Temple was a mess. The magnificent temple was crumbling from the aftermath of the battle, and the corpses of monks and martial artists were rolling around everywhere.

At the center of it all were Jeonjeong, the abbot of Thunder Sound Temple, and Pasanakgun, the leader of the Great Eight Evils Society. They were clashing fists at each other-fists packed with enough power to collapse a small hill in an instant.

Kwaaaang!

As fist met fist, the aftershock swept through the area with the sound of a bomb exploding.

,Looks like Jeonjeong has the upper hand. The difference in level can't be easily overcome after all.

Jeonjeong was in the Upper Third Stage. Pasanakgun was in the Middle Third Stage. Plus, this was Thunder Sound Temple-Jeonjeong's home turf, rich with thunderous energy.

Suddenly, Jeonjeong stepped back. Instead of following up, Pasanakgun spat out a mouthful of blood and caught his breath. Jeonjeong turned his head sharply and glared at me.

,I don't know how you managed to return from the Underworld... but it's good that the Vajra Divine Lightning has returned and the hole in the Underworld has disappeared. Blood Light Island and the Great Eight Evils Society... I'll kill all you vile bastards here! Vajra Divine Lightning! Come here!

,Did you think I'd let it go so easily?

I gripped the Vajra Divine Lightning tightly. I could feel it resisting me. It was impossible to put it in my inventory because I didn't have complete ownership of it.

,I am the master of the Vajra Divine Lightning! Do you think you can bind it with your power? Vajra Divine Lightning! Come to me!

The Vajra Divine Lightning slipped out of my grasp. In an instant, it was spinning around Jeonjeong.

,Finally, it's here!

Jeonjeong grinned. He completely erased my ownership and influence over the Vajra Divine Lightning and then threw it at Pasanakgun.

The Vajra Divine Lightning transformed into golden lightning and flew away. It was incredibly fast-naturally, since it was the speed of lightning. But nothing is unavoidable. There was a premonitory sign before it flew. If your senses were sharp and you reacted in time, you could avoid it.

Pasanakgun, who had been catching his breath, quickly rolled on the ground to avoid the Vajra Divine Lightning. Soon, his face turned red and blue. For a martial artist, falling on the ground was an utter disgrace.

,Hahaha! A so-called supreme master rolling around on the ground!

Jeonjeong sneered. Pasanakgun unleashed his killing intent towards him, but Jeonjeong didn't budge. The Vajra Divine Lightning, which had returned at some point, was floating beside his shoulder.

,Blood Light Island! Don't just stand there, help me! If we attack together, we can kill that damn monk!

,Team up? The fact that those words are coming out of your mouth is laughable. You and that damn monk betrayed me! Why should I help you?!

,This crazy bastard is arguing about that now.

Pasanakgun couldn't finish his sentence because of the golden lightning flying towards him.

,Even though I said that, I can't just stand by and watch Pasanakgun die.

It would be difficult for me to face Jeonjeong alone. Even if I killed Pasanakgun, it had to be after killing Jeonjeong.

Pajijijik!

Sparks flew from Jeonjeong's body. At the same time, his skin turned golden. It seemed that the Thunder Soul Arhat, a secret technique of the Thunder Sound Arhat Art, was influenced by the Vajra Divine Lightning. Lightning flickered wildly from Jeonjeong's right fist.

,That's the Thunderclap Strike of the Thunder Sound Arhat Art.

It was a simple-looking move, but its destructive power couldn't be ignored. I moved before Jeonjeong could finish off Pasanakgun, gripping the Hwaryeon Bido and rushing towards him.

Thunder Heaven Style, Flying Thunder God.

I took position behind Jeonjeong. He suddenly turned around and thrust his fist at me.

,I knew you'd come!

Thunder Heaven Style, Electromagnetic Barrier, maximum output.

I deployed a ki barrier mixed with protective armor head-on. His fist struck the barrier without hesitation.

Kwareureureung!

The barrier shattered with the sound of thunder from his fist. I quickly raised the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido in front of his fist. He didn't care and struck his fist against the blade.

Kwang!

Fist and blade collided, making a metallic sound. I was pushed back by the shockwave.

,Damn it. What kind of power is that? Even if he used the Thunder Soul Arhat, it's too much.

Even if the Thunder Soul Arhat temporarily boosted his physical abilities significantly, Jeonjeong was strangely strong right now.

,......!

I felt an alien sensation in my senses-a sensation like a line stretching towards me. It was a premonitory sign. I quickly twisted my body. Golden lightning brushed past me.

Jeonjeong gestured. The golden lightning changed direction and surged towards me. Sensing the premonitory sign as before, I quickly dodged.

,I can't just keep getting hit like this. Fall.

A bolt of lightning fell towards Jeonjeong. He must have sensed the lightning falling, but he didn't avoid it. It was lightning infused with my power-not ordinary lightning. But it had no effect at all.

,Barely tickles.

Jeonjeong provoked me with a smirk. I knew that he was born with a thunder spirit and that he had considerable compatibility with lightning. Still, I thought that my lightning, mixed with my power, would have some effect.

,It doesn't seem to be working at all... He must have trained in both thunder arts and external arts, so he's disgustingly tough.

The compatibility wasn't good. Moreover, the opponent was at a higher level than me.

,Should I use Cheonma Shingong?

No.

Cheonma Shingong has even worse compatibility. Even if he's a damn monk, the martial arts he's learned are Buddhist martial arts-extremely compatible. In that sense, Thunder Heaven Style would be better. And I don't think Thunder Heaven Style is weak.

,If pure lightning doesn't work, I'll cut him to death with a knife.

I gripped the hilt of the Hwaryeon Bido with both hands and deflected the golden lightning flying straight at me. Kkang! The Vajra Divine Lightning flew towards Jeonjeong but stopped right in front of him.

,A knife that's still intact after colliding head-on with the Vajra Divine Lightning... It's no ordinary knife, is it? Is it a Dharma treasure? Our Thunder Sound Temple doesn't use blades, but... I covet that knife! I'll kill you and take it!

,Now you're openly revealing your greed. Aren't you ashamed, you monk?

,I will transcend all worldly desires someday, so it's fine.

It was then. Pasanakgun secretly approached Jeonjeong from behind and thrust his fist.

,I don't know why Buddha is keeping you alive.

But it didn't work. A barrier had been deployed around the Vajra Divine Lightning.

Jeonjeong smiled confidently.

,Did you think I forgot about you? The Vajra Divine Lightning has excellent spell support abilities. It's nothing to deploy a defensive spell in an instant.

,That's amazing. Hmph!

Pasanakgun put strength into his fist that touched the barrier. There was no visible change.

,Keuheok!

Jeonjeong suddenly coughed up blood.

,You...! What did you do?!

,It's explosive force. Does a martial artist not even know that?

,Don't talk nonsense! Do you think I'd be affected by explosive force!

,Dharma artifacts aren't your exclusive property.

Pasanakgun's gauntlet sparkled. It seemed he had a hidden strike.

,You...!

Just as Jeonjeong was about to clench his fist and swing, I rushed towards him.

Thunder Heaven Style, Flying Thunder God.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 10]

I closed the distance in an instant and raised the Hwaryeon Bido.

,Domain Expansion.

Starlight poured out from the Hwaryeon Bido, illuminating the surroundings.

Thunder Heaven Style, Thunder Gang Blade.

The red lightning blade cut through Jeonjeong's barrier like tofu.

The Vajra Divine Lightning moved and blocked the Hwaryeon Bido. I almost messed up. The Vajra Divine Lightning was something I had to get, so I had no intention of destroying it. But, contrary to my expectations, there wasn't even a scratch on it.

,I even used the domain, and this is it? I knew it was Sakra Devanam Indra's Dharma artifact, but... I never thought it would be this powerful.

I deflected the Vajra Divine Lightning and swung my knife. But again, the Vajra Divine Lightning blocked the Hwaryeon Bido.

In the meantime, Jeonjeong and Pasanakgun were punching each other. As if they would die if they stepped back, both of them were swinging their fists without giving an inch.

,Not bad for a monk who only knows how to chant!

,This fist has been trained through long practice. Do you think a mere mortal can dare to stand against my fist!

Jeonjeong let out a battle cry and suddenly opened his fist, forming a palm and striking out. Jeonjeong's palm collided with Pasanakgun's fist. A huge bolt of lightning erupted from Jeonjeong's palm.

Kwakwakwakwang!

With the explosion, Pasanakgun was slammed into the ground and writhing. Electricity remained in his body, twitching. He didn't die, but he was electrocuted, and his right arm was twisted up to his shoulder. He should be grateful that it didn't explode.

,You're next!

Jeonjeong turned his body sharply and thrust his palm towards me. I tried to dodge with Flying Thunder God, but lightning chains stretched out from the Vajra Divine Lightning and bound my body. It was the spell Lightning Chain Binding.

,Shit. I can't do anything because of the Vajra Divine Lightning.

The Vajra Divine Lightning itself is amazing, but... Jeonjeong, who wields it, is no ordinary person either. Seeing him deal with Pasanakgun and me at the same time, it seems he's dividing his consciousness into two, controlling his body and the Vajra Divine Lightning.

Even when I try to move the Hwaryeon Bido to respond, the Vajra Divine Lightning clings to the blade and interferes.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 9]

,My innate energy was half drained by the Extinction Thunder Soul Technique... I should die once and look for an opportunity. Of course, I won't just die.

Before the Thunderbolt Palm could be fully cast, I extended my palm first to meet it. Unfortunately, I can't use the Thunderbolt Palm. It's a technique that can only be used after fully mastering the Thunder Sound Arhat Art.

,It doesn't matter. I have Thunder Heaven Style.

Pajijijik!

Golden lightning flashed from his palm. Blue lightning flashed from my palm. The two lightnings collided and canceled each other out.

,Hooh. You want to try? You'll regret it? Daring to think you can overcome my pure thunderous energy while harboring evil within you!

Jeonjeong increased his output. The golden lightning pushed back the blue lightning. I felt a pain like my left hand was being torn apart.

,I don't know what you mean by purity, but... I won't lose to you in a pure lightning fight.

Thunder Heaven Style, Evil Lightning.

The blue lightning devoured the golden lightning, which was mixed with demonic energy, and eroded it.

,Such vicious lightning! But borrowing the power of demonic energy was your fatal mistake!

The momentum of the golden lightning, which seemed to be pushed back, suddenly surged. The anti-demonic power unique to Buddhist martial arts was pushing back the Evil Lightning. A difference in compatibility. But I didn't care.

Because I can give different characteristics to lightning.

,Freeze.

Jjeojeojeong!

Jeonjeong's hand began to freeze. He widened his eyes. Since he had no pupils, only the light from his eyes became more intense.

,What the hell is that lightning...!

,Your concentration is wavering.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 8]

Not missing the gap where the Vajra Divine Lightning's reaction was dulled, I stabbed the Hwaryeon Bido into his side. The Vajra Divine Lightning was a step late and pierced my right shoulder, but it didn't matter. What mattered was that Jeonjeong couldn't move now.

,Yuyeoryeo!! Use the poison! Quickly!

Yuyeoryeo, who had been hiding in the shadows a little distance away, appeared. She seemed to hesitate for a moment as she looked at me, then bit her lip and threw a bottle of poison. The bottle collided with Jeonjeong's head and broke, scattering poison everywhere. He and I were covered in deadly poison.

Jeonjeong's face contorted. I chuckled.

,I know better than anyone how deadly the Extreme Poison of the Poisonous Evil is. Even if you circulate thunderous energy, it will take time to burn away the poison.

,Shut up. I'll kill you and that bitch and clean up the mess! Die!

The Vajra Divine Lightning, which had been stuck in my shoulder, came out and pierced my heart.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]
