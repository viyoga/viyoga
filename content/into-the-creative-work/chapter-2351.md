The Thunder Sound Temple looked different from the outside than it did from the inside. From the outside, it looked like an ordinary sect, with buildings towering up all over the place. But once you stepped inside, it was like a Buddhist temple full of stones, dirt, and grass. No matter where you looked, you could see statues of Buddha, big and small.

They were using a barrier to distort and isolate the space.

,Esteemed guest! Welcome to Thunder Sound Temple! I will guide you to the abbot-nim!

,Haha. To think an esteemed guest would arrive at this time! It's clear that Buddha is helping us!

,Om mani padme hum!

The bald monks rejoiced. I shook my head as I watched them.

,That's awkward. No matter how much you welcome me like this, I have no intention of converting to Thunder Sound Temple.

,Thoughts can change at any time. There's no need to make a decision so quickly. Come, let's go inside! Om mani padme hum.

I followed behind the old monk who seemed to be of high status. Then, I discovered a large Bodhi tree next to the temple. As soon as I saw the tree, which had been hidden from view at the entrance, I felt a churning in my stomach.

The usually docile Cheonma Shingong was wriggling.

,.......

I quietly gritted my teeth and suppressed the Cheonma Shingong. Like the Shaolin Temple, Thunder Sound Temple is also a Buddhist sect. If they knew I was a demon, they would try to beat me to death right away.

,Ooh. The esteemed guest recognizes it right away! That Bodhi tree is the sacred object of our Thunder Sound Temple! Shakyamuni attained enlightenment under that tree and became the Buddha!

,Impressive....

,Esteemed guest. You don't look well!

,Seeing that big Bodhi tree reminded me of something. Shakyamuni shook off Mara's temptations there and subdued him with his finger, right?

,That's right! No temptation reached him, and he subdued Mara in an instant with the earth-touching mudra!

That Mara is me, you son of a bitch.

I somehow suppressed the rising curses. I'm at a disadvantage here. If I had only reached the Grandmaster Realm, I would have immediately taken the heads of these monk bastards.

I had to endure it because I was weak now.

I was slightly surprised when I entered the temple. The inside of the temple was quite luxurious.

,These bastards... aren't they monks? There are gold and jewels embedded in the walls. The jars look damn expensive too... and the decorated cloths seem to be high-quality.

Yet the monk robes they wore were old and worn. It seems they were putting on a facade of being frugal monks.

,You seem surprised. These are all donations we've received. We couldn't carelessly throw away what we received, so we decorated with them.

You could just not accept them from the beginning.

I walked down the long corridor. Most of the monks who were guiding me stopped abruptly and put their hands together in respect.

,Why did they stop suddenly?

,It's because their cultivation is low and they can't enter further. The further you go in, the stronger the thunder energy becomes.

,Is that so?

The entire Thunder Sound Temple was filled with thunder energy. I didn't feel anything strange because it was a temple. It was all the same to me.

,Esteemed guest was born with a thunder spirit... so this level of thunder energy is nothing to you. Didn't it feel refreshing?

I checked my body for a moment. I definitely felt lighter than before.

,I guess so.

I went inside.

Now I could definitely feel it. Dense thunder energy was gathered in the center of the temple. The thunder energy was so intense that my lungs tingled just from breathing! Ordinary people would be electrocuted and die as soon as they stepped in here. Even those who have cultivated thunder energy would have a hard time enduring it for long.

,I'll stop here. The abbot is waiting inside.

I entered the abbot's room alone.

A bald old man in monk's robes was sitting quietly on a cushion. I felt the thunder energy swirling around him. He was born with a thunder spirit. It was different from me, who appeared to have a thunder spirit due to the Thunder skill in the game app.

,That's a real thunder spirit.

I didn't feel any jealousy. Rather, I was slightly disappointed. I could do that much with [Thunder] anyway. No, I had already surpassed him.

Even now, the thunder energy around him was starting to gather around me.

,Om mani padme hum! You are truly an esteemed guest! I am Jeonjeong, the abbot of Thunder Sound Temple.

,None of the monks at the entrance moved first. How did you know I was coming?

,The thunder energy told me, and I conveyed my will to the monks through the thunder energy.

Did he know in a way similar to electromagnetic waves?

It could have been a spell that gave the monks their orders.

Anyway, I sat quietly on the cushion in front of him. The power and energy I felt from him were extraordinary. He was at least in the Upper Realm of the Three Perfections. Moreover, this was the heart of Thunder Sound Temple. As soon as a battle broke out, the monks of Thunder Sound Temple would swarm in.

I had no chance of winning.

,What do you want?

,Haha. Isn't it the esteemed guest who opened the gates of Thunder Sound Temple? Esteemed guest, what business brings you to open the gates of Thunder Sound Temple?

,I don't like beating around the bush, and since you're probably expecting it, I'll be honest. I'm interested in Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts and spells.

,Thunder Sound Temple is the best in the world when it comes to thunder techniques. You've come to the right place.

I could feel deep pride and subtle arrogance in Jeonjeong's words. It didn't seem like the attitude of a high-ranking monk, but since he was a martial monk in the [Bright Light Ascension] world, I let it go.

,Will you let me see Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts and spells?

,Under normal circumstances, it would be absolutely impossible. Thunder Sound Temple's thunder techniques belong only to Thunder Sound Temple.

,So it's not normal circumstances now.

,There are conditions. If you accept those conditions, I will teach you Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts and spells. Of course, you must not reveal Thunder Sound Temple's secrets to others. You must swear to that.

,I swear.

I could make empty promises all I wanted. Once I became strong enough to destroy Thunder Sound Temple, there would be no oath or anything.

,And what are they going to do if I distribute them in another world?

,Haha. I will trust the esteemed guest's words.

,You believe that easily?

,For those of us who have achieved high levels of cultivation, like you and I, words are our will. Words spoken cannot be taken back, and reversing one's words is like wounding one's will.

Kwa-roo-roo-roong!

A thunderclap rang out. I narrowed my eyes and looked at the sky. The thunderclap echoed between me and Jeonjeong.

,What did you do?

,The lightning has heard your oath.

,Is it a spell?

,You could say that. I will state the other conditions. Esteemed guest, become a guest of Thunder Sound Temple and help us. In return, I will personally take the time to teach you some of Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts and spells. Of course, if the esteemed guest converts to Thunder Sound Temple... I will teach you everything about Thunder Sound Temple.

,I don't believe in anyone.

,I expected as much. How... will you accept?

,What kind of problem does Thunder Sound Temple have that makes you ask this?

,It's not something we can tell an outsider.

,It's not something that will harm me, is it?

,It's something that will help the world, beyond just Thunder Sound Temple. I can guarantee that.

,.......

I was closing my mouth and contemplating when Jeonjeong's atmosphere suddenly turned sharp.

,Esteemed guest. I know why you came to Thunder Sound Temple. Isn't your original purpose to shake off the Mara that constantly tempts you from within your body? Esteemed guest. I. Thunder Sound Temple can help you.

,...You knew?

Jeonjeong smiled faintly and raised his eyelids. He had no pupils. Only the whites of his eyes were visible, and a subtle yet mysterious light emanated from them.

,Haha. You can't deceive my eyes. I have mastered the Mind's Eye, so it's not difficult for me to see the evil spirit residing within the esteemed guest's body.

,You can see what I'm thinking?

,Hmm. No. The esteemed guest has an unusually strong mental barrier. I can't see anything.

So he could see it if my mental barrier was weak. It was an amazing and enviable ability.

,I could find traitors and rebels in an instant....

I carefully considered his offer. He wanted me to stay at Thunder Sound Temple as a guest and help them. He swore that whatever that help was, it wouldn't harm me.

,It's not like he's asking me to convert to Thunder Sound Temple or Buddhism.

In return, I could learn Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts and spells. It was beneficial in many ways.

,If it doesn't seem right in the middle, I can just escape with space movement.

Having finished calculating, I nodded.

,I accept.

,Om mani padme hum.... Welcome. First, unpack and rest. I will inform you of what to do next later. Thunder Sound Temple also needs time to prepare.

,It would be nice if you could tell me, even briefly, what problems Thunder Sound Temple is facing.

,Now that you have become a guest of Thunder Sound Temple, I will tell you. Decades ago, Thunder Sound Temple split into two factions due to differences of opinion and fought. At that time, a hole connected to the Underworld opened, and now Thunder Sound Temple is investing all its resources to destroy that hole. We had prepared a method, but we were short on strength.... That's when the esteemed guest appeared.

,A hole to the Underworld.... Are you crazy?

,It was an incident caused by Thunder Sound Temple's mistake.... Thunder Sound Temple will fix this.

Jeonjeong didn't show the slightest agitation. I stepped back after seeing his unsettling gaze.

I was about to be guided to my living quarters by another monk who was waiting when the Cheonma Shingong inside me started to rampage.

-You're doing something useless. Let's raid Thunder Sound Temple's treasury and run away. Don't you find this place unpleasant too?

I don't know if the Cheonma Shingong really said that. That's just how I felt.

,Shut your mouth.

The Cheonma Shingong became silent.
