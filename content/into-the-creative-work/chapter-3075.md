A coup d'état broke out in Japan.

The Deputy Prime Minister was killed, and Japan's S-rank Hunter, Tachibana Hayase, became the head of state.

He proclaimed himself the Shogun of Japan.

When I heard the news, I nodded.

'What a lunatic.'

I wasn't all that surprised. Even in the original work, Tachibana Hayase is a far-right imperialist. In a word, a madman.

'In the original, he didn't go this far. I never thought the snowball that started with killing the Prime Minister would roll all the way here.'

I had no idea he would kill the head of state and usurp the position.

'Normally, when a head of state dies, the next in the line of succession takes over.'

The one who determines that line of succession is also the head of state. Under normal circumstances, after the Deputy Prime Minister dies, the third-ranked Minister of State should inherit the position. If all the Ministers of State die, another politician takes over. If all the politicians die? Japan is a democratic nation, so the people vote.

'That's the procedure set by the Tower System. But that procedure was completely ignored? That means something else intervened.'

Here lies the question of whether the Japanese people would follow Tachibana Hayase. No matter how much of a hero he is to Japan, he's a madman who staged a coup and proclaimed himself Shogun.

To state the conclusion, they had no choice but to follow.

The head of state can banish citizens or approve naturalization requests from foreigners. This is merely a fraction of the authority the head of state holds. The true authority—and power—of the head of state is the ability to use the nation's official coins at will. There are procedures, but they aren't complicated.

'Tachibana Hayase has the Japanese people by the throat. Whether they like it or not, they have no choice but to follow.'

The head of state in the Tower System could be described as a system specialized for dictatorship rather than democracy.

'Lee Yeonhee is going to have a headache over this.'

A Korea-Japan war wouldn't suddenly break out. There was too much to lose for that. Assassinate Tachibana Hayase? He's an S-rank Hunter ranked among the world's best. That's no easy task. And he's not foolish enough to leave himself unprepared for that.

'Well, not my problem.'

As long as it didn't directly harm me, I couldn't care less.

Tachibana Hayase's coup sent massive shockwaves through every head of state in the world.

"A Hunter killed a head of state and usurped the position! Is this even possible?!"

Heads of state began to guard against their own nation's Hunters. No one wanted to end up like Japan. However, they couldn't openly be wary of Hunters. Hunters are heroes who save nations, after all.

Every nation in the world, including Korea, refrained from condemning Japan's Shogun.

"China has no intention of interfering in Japan's internal affairs."

"Korea has no intention of antagonizing Japan."

"The United States can only express its regret."

Tachibana Hayase is a madman. No nation wanted to provoke a madman and get into a brawl. Even the United States decided not to intervene in Japan. Right now, America's own interests came first. World peace was on the back burner.

Shogun Tachibana Hayase made a declaration in front of all the reporters on the very day of his usurpation.

"Korea must cease its illegal occupation of Takeshima and return it to Japan immediately."

Pffft.

Lee Yeonhee, who had been drinking water at that exact moment, spat it out the instant she saw Tachibana Hayase's statement. Her pupils shook like crazy.

"His first announcement as head of state is this garbage…."

Of course, there was a high chance it was just bluster. It could be a political move. He designated Korea as an external enemy to unify Japan internally. It was something successive Japanese Prime Ministers had done frequently.

"His next move will be to visit Yasukuni Shrine."

Lee Yeonhee's prediction came true.

The moment the press conference ended, Tachibana Hayase moved to Yasukuni Shrine with his followers. Half of Japan's politicians and a large number of famous Japanese Hunters attended.

Japan's Emperor? True to his self-proclamation as Shogun, he didn't even mention the Emperor's authority. The Emperor was just a totem.

Lee Yeonhee gave orders to the brainwashed Korean President.

"Deploy the navy to the East Sea. Especially—defend Dokdo at all costs. If Japan crosses the line… I will authorize firing."

There would be no concession.

Once you concede, you'll concede endlessly.

Lee Yeonhee could tolerate anything else, but she absolutely could not tolerate a second era of Japanese colonial rule.

Korea and Japan. Military tensions between the two nations spiked sharply.

"Your Excellency. Please pause your Tower challenges for the time being. The world right now cannot easily accept Your Excellency's movements. Especially with Japan on edge. It's not that I fear war, but there is no profit to be gained from war. If war can be avoided, it must be avoided."

Seong Yujin nodded coolly.

"Sure. There's no rush."

Korea's navy and Japan's Maritime Self-Defense Force met in the East Sea and faced off. The world's eyes were fixed on them. Time passed with guns pointed at each other.

War was imminent.

When Korean and Japanese stocks crashed and the entire East Asian economy began to tremble—

Out of nowhere, the incident erupted in Taiwan.

『The Novaltic Tribe of Planet Maphrax is invading Earth.』

A dimensional raid, the first in a long while.

The United States immediately tried to support Taiwan. However, China, which had its eyes on Taiwan, stepped in and blocked the US. Chinese and American fleets faced off in the Pacific.

"Helping Taiwan is for the sake of humanity! Is China shielding aliens?!"

"Taiwan is Chinese territory. Helping Taiwan is China's business. The United States should not meddle in this matter."

The United States could not ignore China's warning. The world is different from before. Now, one wrong move could trigger a real World War IV.

Korea versus Japan.

China versus the United States.

An alien force invading Taiwan.

The world turned into a shitshow in an instant.

Of course, both China and the United States had their own calculations. For one, neither nation believed for a second that Taiwan would fall to an alien invasion.

'Taiwan is a military power ranked 22nd. They have the strength that even the Chinese can't take lightly. There will be some damage, but they'll overcome it.'

America believed in Taiwan.

'If Taiwan weakens from this incident, the future war for Taiwan's reunification will become much easier.'

China also didn't think Taiwan would fall.

Several days passed just like that.

『The Novaltic Tribe has occupied Taiwan.』

『Taiwan enters captive status.』

『Garshin of the Novaltic Tribe becomes Governor-General of Taiwan.』

Taiwan had fallen.

"Huh?"

"Huh?"

China and the United States stared at each other with stupid faces, then hurriedly turned their ships around and moved to surround Taiwan.

"Huh?"

"Huh?"

Korea and Japan, who had been facing off against each other, also felt the news of Taiwan hit them like a slap to the back of the head. If they continued their standoff here, the whole world's condemnation would rain down on them. The fate of the Earth beyond nations was at stake. The military tension between Korea and Japan dissolved.

"Huh?"

The entire world was thrown into confusion by the news from Taiwan. The whole world began scrambling into action. The situation was more serious than China invading Taiwan.

Earth's humanity being occupied by humans—or races—from another world? That was absolutely unacceptable.

An external enemy is more dangerous than an internal one.

I looked at the TV monitor. On the screen was a man lounging arrogantly.

He resembled a human, but he was not human. His skin was hairless and glossy, dark in color, and thick. His eyes bulged like a fish's, and his mouth protruded slightly. On his neck were gills that looked like knife slits.

A fish-man.

"Hey. Does this work? Is it true that the whole world can see me right now?"

The camera director answered the fish-man while trembling violently.

"I-it's being broadcast live right now. If you look at this monitor, you can see it's on air!"

"Hooh. So my image really is showing. Good."

The fish-man stood before the camera. He flexed his entire body and radiated killing intent. Mana surged around him. Miscellaneous objects nearby were swept away.

"Earthlings, listen. I am Garshin, one of the great warriors of the Novaltic Tribe, one of the five tribes that rule Planet Maphrax.

『Garshin of the Novaltic Tribe becomes Governor-General of Taiwan.』

"Earthlings, listen. I am Garshin, one of the great warriors of the Novaltic Tribe, one of the five tribes that rule Planet Maphrax. I have conquered the nation called Taiwan on Earth and have been appointed Governor-General of Taiwan."

There was arrogance in his voice. Absolute confidence in himself.

"I have no intention of fighting other nations on Earth. We wish to obtain coins steadily. We have received the blessing of the sea, and 70% of this world is ocean. Do not provoke us."

Garshin moved.

At some point, his subordinate fish-men had brought forth an ornate chair. Garshin sat in it.

"There are quite a few foreigners here, excluding Taiwanese. I am generous. I will hand them over for just 1,000 coins per person."

With the world in this state, few people traveled abroad. However, there were still occasional business trips overseas. Not to mention diplomats working at embassies.

"And send food aid to Taiwan. Surely you don't want your own kin to starve to death. From what I hear, your Earth is materially very wealthy, so it shouldn't be difficult."

Garshin extended his hand to the side. A middle-aged Taiwanese man in a suit tremblingly offered a glass of liquor into his hand. Garshin downed it in one shot and laughed. The middle-aged Taiwanese man quickly refilled the glass.

"You Earthlings tend to be rude, but you have many fascinating things. Especially Earth's alcohol—it suits my taste perfectly. Earthlings, let us get along well together. Cheers."

Garshin drained his glass again and continued.

"I plan to hold a banquet soon to commemorate my inauguration as Governor-General. Every leader on Earth shall attend without exception."

Garshin waved his hand.

Click.

The broadcast ended.

“…….”

I let out a hollow laugh at the massive alien invasion event that only appeared in the mid-to-late portion of the original work.

Shortly after, through Lee Yeonhee, I heard a proposal the US President made to all nations, including China and Russia. To summarize, it went like this.

"Wanna go get some fish sashimi?"
