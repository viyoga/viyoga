Tadak, tadak.

The warmth from the bonfire began to fill the old inn. The sandman came calling at the perfect temperature.

Hong Gang-hu and his son Hong Je-yu were talking about something, but the content didn't register in my head. It wasn't important anyway. It was mostly about monsters they'd encountered while wandering the Jianghu.

Nod, nod.

It was when I was dozing off, my head bobbing back and forth.

Something is approaching. It wasn't a human presence. I lifted my head and circulated my qi. The sandman disappeared in an instant, and my head became clear.

Jeo-beok jeo-beok jeo-beok.

As my senses sharpened, I could hear something approaching through the rain, stepping on the mud.

,"There are too many trees to see clearly, but that's not the sound of human footsteps."

,"If it were a beast, it would have charged more recklessly. It's definitely a monster."

The Hongs jumped up from their seats and drew their swords. I was about to step in, but I stopped. There was no need for me to step in when these two were willing to kill the monster.

Jeo-beok jeo-beok jeo-beok jeo-beok jeo-beok.

The presence increased in an instant. I narrowed my eyes and looked outside the inn. The figures of the bastards, unseen due to the trees, darkness, and rain, began to appear.

They looked like flowing lumps of flesh. They had arms, legs, and a torso, but their size was several times larger than a human's. About 10 of them were approaching this way. Their bodies were covered in mud, making them look quite dirty.

,"Su-rak-gwi (Water Drop Demons), huh."

,"Aren't those usually found near rivers or lakes? Did they come out because of the rain?"

,"How would we humans know the actions of monsters? What's certain is that these bastards are trying to eat us. Yeom Dae-in-nim, please stay put. Since you gave us your spot, we'll take care of these things."

,"Are you sure you're okay?"

I asked in a monotonous voice. It was just a courtesy question. Hong Je-yu might not be, but Hong Gang-hu's skills should be enough to take care of Su-rak-gwi in the blink of an eye.

,"We're fine."

,"When you wander the Jianghu, you easily encounter those kinds of things. It's nothing, so don't worry."

,"Je-yu-ya. No matter who the opponent is, never let your guard down. The most dangerous thing in the Jianghu is carelessness."

,"Father. I've heard that saying over a hundred times, it's tiresome."

Hong Je-yu charged towards the enemies as if to avoid his father's nagging. I quietly watched the scene. Hong Je-yu's movements seemed simple at first glance. But there was depth within that simplicity.

,'Judging by the pure energy, it's a martial art from the orthodox sects.'

Hong Je-yu methodically sliced and killed the Su-rak-gwi one by one. When the Su-rak-gwi attacked as a group, he retreated without hesitation to create distance. He seemed more like a hunter than a Martial artist.

,"It contains the mysteries of both heaviness and fluidity. What sword technique is that?"

,"That's the Osan Sword. It's close to a patchwork of sword techniques from various places, but in other words, it can be said to have taken the advantages of other sword techniques."

,"Did you create that sword technique?"

,"I don't have that kind of ability. The Osan Sword is a sword technique created by my late father."

Hong Gang-hu said proudly.

This was different from the orthodox sects. The orthodox sects basically had a unique pride in their martial arts. They believed that the old ways were the best. That was why their martial arts didn't develop well over time. A patchwork martial art? The orthodox sect members would never say that about their own martial arts. It was the most shameful thing for them.

,'Is it because he's been a wanderer for too long? He doesn't seem like an orthodox sect member.'

Still, his tone and temperament seemed like that of an orthodox sect member.

I asked Hong Gang-hu, who was watching Hong Je-yu's battle.

,"Don't you need to help your son?"

,"I didn't raise him to be so weak that he couldn't even overcome this much."

I could feel pride in his voice. But his eyes were fixed on Hong Je-yu and didn't leave him.

The Su-rak-gwi used their abilities. Their bodies seemed to melt, turning into puddles of water and moving quickly towards Hong Je-yu. Hong Je-yu ran towards a tree. He stepped on the trunk with heavy steps and climbed up.

The Su-rak-gwi, who had turned into puddles of water, were left chasing after him like dogs after a chicken. Inevitably, the Su-rak-gwi returned to their original forms.

,"He's surrounded."

I said in a calm tone.

Hong Gang-hu placed his hand on the hilt of his sword. His leg was twitching, as if he was about to jump out at any moment.

,"...It's still okay. Since he's on a tree, he can just jump to another tree and escape the encirclement."

It seemed he had no intention of stepping in for just this much of a crisis. Since it was someone else's business anyway, I quietly watched.

Hong Je-yu jumped to another tree and escaped the Su-rak-gwi's encirclement. Even though the Su-rak-gwi were low-level monsters, they weren't as stupid as beasts. The Su-rak-gwi's encirclement was still solid. Then, the Su-rak-gwi charged at the tree Hong Je-yu had climbed.

The tree breaks. Hong Je-yu was surprised and jumped to another tree.

,"He's scared."

From the moment he got scared, his actions became passive. He kept glancing this way, looking for help.

,"That idiot...!"

It was the moment Hong Gang-hu clicked his tongue and was about to step in. A figure appeared a little further away. Hong Gang-hu tensed up at the presence. I also tightened my loosened awareness.

,'Who the hell is that now? No, is it a woman?'

As she got closer, I could see her better.

Like me, she wore a bamboo hat and black clothes. The lines of her body were slightly curved. It was definitely a woman. Her breasts were neither too big nor too small, just the right size. Her hips were also not excessive.

,'Should I call it sculpture-like? A beautiful figure that can be seen even through the clothes.'

Should I call it the golden ratio? The proportions, such as arm length, leg length, and body balance, felt perfect.

Her face was not visible because of the bamboo hat. But judging by the atmosphere, she must be a beauty.

,'She knows what the demeanor of a master is.'

She walked towards the Su-rak-gwi with calm steps. The Su-rak-gwi belatedly recognized her presence. Hong Gang-hu watched with uneasy eyes but did not step in. It must be because he didn't feel any murderous intent from the woman.

The excited Su-rak-gwi rushed at her as soon as they saw her. She calmly drew the sword from her waist. She walked lightly on the mud and swung her sword at the Su-rak-gwi. Her legs didn't stop, and her sword didn't break.

,'She's stepping on the mud, but there are no footsteps.'

In the blink of an eye, the Su-rak-gwi were being taken care of. I focused on her sword technique. It wasn't a sword technique from the orthodox sects, nor was it from the unorthodox sects. It was even less so a Demonic Arts sword technique. The sword technique was simple and too efficient. It was a sword that seemed to have deliberately excluded unnecessary elements and sharpened its sharpness. A sword technique that seemed to have realized the ideal of killing people. Ironically, there was no murderous intent at all.

She turned the Su-ran-gwi into corpses in an instant and sheathed her sword. She was showing that she had no intention of fighting us. Hong Gang-hu's tension eased.

,"Her skills seem similar to ours, but she doesn't seem like a bad person."

,"That swordsmanship, is it an assassin's swordsmanship?"

,"Huh? Ah, you could see it that way in a sense. Because it looks like a swordsmanship that is efficient to the point of being blatant. But it's not an assassin's sword. An assassin's sword is not that deep. That's the Imperial family's swordsmanship."

,"...The Imperial family's sword? A swordsman belonging to the Imperial family? Not the military, but the Imperial family?"

I asked back. The Imperial family of the Empire rarely operated openly. Moreover, this place was very far from the capital of the Empire where the Imperial family was located. There was no chance of encountering a swordsman of the Imperial family.

,"The military's swordsmanship is much more crude than that. She definitely belongs to the Imperial family."

,"...Why is that Imperial family swordsman here?"

,"Everyone has their own story. And if you're curious, ask her directly."

She and Hong Je-yu were approaching this way. Hong Je-yu was talking non-stop. When I enhanced my hearing, I could hear words like her swordsmanship was excellent, thank you for saving me, and so on. The female swordsman occasionally replied and just listened.

,'Her voice is clear and nice.'

Soon, the woman entered the old inn. She wore a bamboo hat, but that alone couldn't completely protect her from the rain. Her clothes and the bundle she carried on her back were soaked.

,"The rain is coming down too hard. May I rest here for a while?"

Her voice was beautiful. The timbre was that of someone in their 20s. The problem was that her tone was old-fashioned.

,'Her cultivation is at the Fifth Stage of Qi Gathering.... If she's at the Fifth Stage of Qi Gathering, there's no way she's in her 20s. She must be at least 200 years old.'

In this world, the strong ones are all over several hundred years old.

The Hongs' gazes turned to me. They seemed to think that I was the owner of this inn. It was only natural since I had arrived first and made the bonfire.

,"Do as you please."

,"Thank you."

The woman strode forward. She sat on my left. Her clothes were wet, making a squishing sound. She took off her dripping bamboo hat and carefully placed it next to her.

Her revealed face was more beautiful than expected. Her clean skin was like white jade, her eyes were big, and her features were soft. She had a slight smile on her lips.

A unique feature was that her hair was white. She tied up her long hair and secured it with a hairpin.

,'Did her hair turn white due to the influence of her martial arts? It's rare, but it's not strange.'

Hong Je-yu stared blankly at her appearance, and Hong Gang-hu became more wary.

,"I am Baek-ran. I was caught in a sudden downpour while passing by."

,"We, we came here to avoid the rain too. Ah, I'm Hong Je-yu."

,"Osan Nine Swords Hong Gang-hu. Your swordsmanship seems extraordinary... What's your alias?"

,"I haven't been in the Jianghu for long, so I don't have an alias. What's your name?"

Baek-ran looked at me and asked.

She smelled sweetly feminine. To the point where I wanted to pounce on her. But judging by her skills earlier, that wouldn't be easy.

,"I'm Lightning Flash Yeom Gu-seok."

,"Hmm. Is that so."

Baek-ran grabbed her wet clothes and squeezed them. Water dripped down.

I asked her directly.

,"Are you from the Imperial family?"

Her eyes widened, and the Hongs gasped. In this world, the emperor's authority was so immense that he was almost like a god. Therefore, the emperor and the Imperial family were not words that could be easily spoken.

Fortunately, Baek-ran didn't get angry.

,"That's right. Did you recognize it by looking at my swordsmanship? You have a good eye."
