[Great Fortune (Lv. Master) is activated.]

As soon as I entered the mountain, Great Fortune activated.

My body moved on its own, changing direction. I was taken aback, but I entrusted myself to the Great Fortune skill. Great Fortune is luck. It won't do anything bad to me.

,Seong! Where are you going?! Shouldn't we be heading deeper into the mountain?"

Mori Masahiro said. I replied irritably. It wasn't a pleasant feeling to have my body moving on its own.

,Just follow me for now."

I thought as I ran.

It was the earthquake that saved us in that moment of crisis. It was misfortune for the villagers, but luck for us.

,It's luck, but Great Fortune didn't activate then. That earthquake must be the one that happened in the original story."

Though I don't remember the details of the original story.

After running through the mountains for about 15 minutes, we arrived at a place with a large Japanese-style mansion. A little further away from this mansion was a hospital.

,This is... the Kaminoyama residence."

Mori Masahiro said, wiping his sweat.

,Kaminoyama. That's the shrine maiden's surname. Is this the shrine maiden's house?"

,It's the main family's residence. The shrine maiden, Kaminoyama Kagura, lives at the shrine. Looks like you didn't come here knowing that."

I just came as Great Fortune led me.

,...Shouldn't we just go down the mountain?"

Wada Kyoshiro said. It wasn't a bad suggestion. The original plan was to go down the mountain.

,I think I know what Yu-jin is thinking."

Nakagami Risa said with a smile. Everyone's eyes turned to her. Her openly smiling in this situation made her seem strange, and their gazes were full of wariness.

,Right now, we're in the worst situation. The people of this village are trying to find us and kill us, and we have nothing. We go down the mountain in this state? I think that's suicide. You felt how big this mountain is when we came, didn't you?"

,But Nakagami-senpai. If we go down the mountain, there will be a road, and if we walk along the road, we can get to a city."

,Wada. The problem is going down the mountain. This mountain is dangerous. There's a cliff on one side of the mountain, and I've heard that wild animals come out. Let's say we get down safely. What if the villagers are waiting for us at the bottom of the mountain?"

,...Senpai and Seong's opinion is that we should stay in this village?"

,That's not it. I agree that we should go down the mountain. The problem is that we don't have anything. We need tools. Food is essential. And since we might run into villagers... we need weapons too."

Nakagami Risa's gaze turned to the mansion. Everyone's gaze naturally turned to the mansion as well.

Tools, food, weapons.

The things we want will be in that mansion.

,...Wouldn't it be dangerous? The shrine maiden is behind the villagers, and since it's the shrine maiden's main family residence, the security must be tight...."

Wada Kyoshiro worried.

,It's just as dangerous now. If we run away from the mountain empty-handed, it will become dangerous. More than anything, we don't know the geography of the mountain at all. Even if we don't get anything else, we have to get food."

It was Mori Masahiro who said that.

,...I've been messing with my smartphone since earlier, but the signal is cut off. Not only the internet, but I can't even make calls. They've done something to interfere with phone calls in this village. We can't even ask for help from the outside. We have to survive on our own. To do that, we need weapons."

Fudo Jun said. He was cold-blooded. He wasn't intimidated by the villagers either. He didn't seem like an ordinary guy.

The circle members shared their opinions. And they decided in a democratic way. They decided to rob the mansion.

We climbed over the mansion's fence.

It was a somewhat high fence, but it wasn't a fence we couldn't climb over.

The mansion was quiet. We vaguely thought that there would be guards patrolling, but that prediction was wrong. There wasn't a single guard patrolling inside the mansion.

Tadadadak!

We heard the sound of hurried footsteps from outside the mansion.

,Wake everyone up! It's an emergency!"

,Yes, sir!"

,Search the mountain! Prepare the hunting dogs!"

,Understood!"

,Block the road below the village! We need people, so request manpower from the outside!"

The middle-aged man's shout echoed into the mansion. It was a voice I had heard somewhere before.

,...Shimada, the police box chief."

Mori Masahiro muttered, naming the owner of the voice.

We moved only after the sounds of people outside had disappeared.

The lights inside the mansion were turned on. They had woken up after hearing the commotion. The fortunate thing was that the mansion's main gate was still closed.

I, who was walking at the very front, said in a small voice.

,The people of this village are trying to kill us. I don't know why they're trying to kill us, but anyway, they're trying to kill us."

Did I kill the villagers first, so they're retaliating? Absolutely not. Those bastards would have tried to kill us even if I hadn't killed them first.

,So we kill those bastards too. If you're going to spout bullshit like 'You shouldn't kill people,' then don't do anything at all."

The door next to me opened. A person came out. It was a woman who looked to be in her 40s. Judging from the clothes she was wearing and her disheveled appearance, she seemed to be a servant working at the mansion. Before the woman could scream, I grabbed her neck and broke it.

Kkwadeudeudeuk.

The woman died instantly. I pushed the corpse into the room and closed the door.

My back feels prickly. I can see a slight bit of fear in the eyes of the circle members looking at me.

,...Was it really necessary to kill her?"

,Mori-senpai. What did I say earlier?"

,...Sorry."

,That woman was about to scream as soon as she saw us. What would have happened after that? Of course, the villagers would have rushed over, and we would have died."

,Yeah. We have to kill to avoid being killed.... This village is hell."

After that, we ran into several more people. I took the lead, but there were times when it was difficult for me to handle it alone. In those cases, Nakagami Risa or Hagawa Ryoko stepped up to help.

We killed the old man who seemed to be the owner of this mansion and successfully took over the mansion.

Without hesitation, we packed the equipment needed to escape into the mountains and the food in the kitchen. We took Japanese swords as weapons. Perhaps it was the mansion owner's hobby, but there were dozens of Japanese swords stored in one room. I quickly ran over and grabbed the sword in the most ornate spot.

,When you look at things like manga, there are famous swords hidden in places like this... but that's not the case in reality. The sword owner isn't stupid enough to put a good sword in a corner, right?"

Holding the sword, I looked at the detached building of the mansion. It was the only place in this mansion that we hadn't entered. There was no need to enter. We had already packed everything we needed.

,It's piquing my curiosity."

The detached building had no windows. Even when I looked around, it was blocked by concrete. The main gate was a heavy iron door.

I told the circle members, who were busy packing food and tools, that I would be back after going to the detached building for a moment, and stood alone in front of the detached building.

I grabbed the handle of the iron door. The iron door was open. It didn't seem necessary to use the key that was in the mansion owner's room.

White light leaked through the slightly open door. I was a little taken aback and opened the iron door wide.

Cheolkeongcheolkeongcheolkeong.

It was a small factory. Machines were running, making something. The ceiling was bright and clean. I could feel that they were very conscious of hygiene.

And in the center, a man wearing black clothes was sitting in a chair, looking at the machine. I couldn't see his face. A black cloth was covering his face.

He looked at me, panicked, and took something out of his pocket.

Red pills, Blood Pills.

And not just one. A whopping six.

He lifted the cloth covering his face and swallowed all the Blood Pills.

Drip. Drip, drip.

Bloody tears fall to the floor. And his body twitched.

,...No. Not his whole body... his muscles are twitching."

The bastard runs at me. The instantaneous acceleration was tremendous.

,But it's not at a level that transcends humans."

His veins bulging hand reaches out towards my head. It looks like he's trying to grab my head and crush it.

I bent my back and drew my sword. My sword was faster than his hand.

Seogeok.

His head is cut off and flies into the air. A blood fountain shoots up to the ceiling from his severed neck. His blood pressure was excessively high.

The body that lost its head didn't stop. I moved to the side and swung my sword. I cut off the bastard's shoulder. The tough, muscular mass was cut softly.

,It's indeed a famous sword."

I looked at him, who had fallen. Blood was gushing out like a fountain from his severed shoulder as well.

,There's a scar on his neck. He didn't say a word, so... did they remove his vocal cords and make him a mute?"

I turned my back on the corpse and looked at the machine. The machine continued to run even without a manager.

In the machine at the very front, things like powder were being put in and mixed. There were more than six types of powder in total, five of which were white powder, and one was bright red powder.

The well-mixed powder turned into pink powder and moved to the next machine.

Water is added and mixed. And it is boiled until the water evaporates. This process is repeated several more times.

,...I don't think it's water."

Next, the powder is rounded and hardened. This is how Blood Pills are made.

,It's made surprisingly easily?"

But that wasn't the end.

The completed Blood Pills are divided into two types at the inspection table.

I can't tell the difference on the outside.

,There are fewer Blood Pills on the right. Are they defective?"

I decided to eat them.

I have Complete Recovery. I won't die if I eat them right away.

First, I picked up the Blood Pill on the left, which I suspected was a success, and put it in my mouth.

Thump!

My heart beat violently. Vitality returns to my body, and my mind becomes clear. My senses become a little better. I feel confident. I felt like I could do anything.

,...It's a drug."

I ate the Blood Pill on the right.

Power surges in my body. My vision turns red. Drip, drip. Bloody tears flow down my cheeks.

,The one on the right was the Blood Pill."

I grabbed a handful of Blood Pills and stuffed them into my pocket. They might be helpful later.

I moved behind the machine. I saw stacked boxes. I cut the box with a knife. The Blood Pills were luxuriously packaged.

,Are they selling Blood Pills? They'll make some money, huh?"

There will be many who want this. The Yakuza, maybe even the Japanese government is secretly buying Blood Pills. The effect of Blood Pills, which temporarily strengthens the body, is something everyone would covet.

,There's a door. Is this where the ingredients come in?"

It was a door leading to the basement. I moved to the basement and let out a hollow laugh. The passage was full of powder.

,It's a drug."

There was another door at the end of the passage. When I opened the door and went inside, a terrible smell of blood stung my nose. But it wasn't a messy place. It was a cold and clean place. I turned my head to the side.

The corpses of Takana Hiroshi, Gonzo, and Sakurai were in the machine. In addition, I could see the corpses of the villagers I had killed. The machine seemed to be used to preserve the corpses so they wouldn't rot.

,Not only that, but they're also squeezing out blood. The machine next to it... seems to be separating flesh and bones from the corpses."

The separated flesh and internal organs are being boiled in a pot with blood. The bones are being crushed into powder.

,I don't know about the powder.... Are the bones, flesh, and internal organs one of the ingredients of the Blood Pills?"

I recalled the taste of the Blood Pill I had eaten earlier. It had no taste at all. I couldn't believe that a person had gone into it.

,...The Blood Pills had no taste at all. Did they not grind people up and put them in?"

,There's an intermediate process. The extract extracted from the corpses is mixed with the Kaminoyama family's secret medicine and then made into powder. After that, it is mixed with five kinds of drugs to make Blood Pills. However, the success rate of manufacturing Blood Pills is only 15%. 85% of defects occur in the process of clumping and hardening the powder. I've been trying to improve it for the past 5 years... but I've failed."

I turned my head to the side.

A 2m tall giant wearing a doctor's gown is looking at me with indifferent eyes.

,Who are you."

,Kaminoyama Kazuto. I'm the director of this hospital."
