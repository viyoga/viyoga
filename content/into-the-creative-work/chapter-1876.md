Park Hae-gil, having entered Seongak Elementary School, sat blankly in the first-floor infirmary of the main building. It was a simple infirmary with only a desk, medicine, and a simple bed, devoid of any medical equipment.

His right hand, chewed by the bug, was torn here and there. He received proper medical treatment, but it would be difficult to use his right hand properly for at least a month.

A month.

It wasn't a long time. However, in the current situation where the zombie virus had spread, a month was extremely important.

,If I had ignored him as that man said...'

His right hand wouldn't have been injured.

The man bitten by the bug would eventually be dragged away and become food for the bug, but even if he had saved him, wouldn't he have died from excessive bleeding? In the end, it was a meaningless act. All he lost was his hand and the self-satisfaction of trying to save him as a human. No, he didn't even have that self-satisfaction.

,If that man had stepped forward, I could have saved him.'

That man didn't step forward. He just watched the man being bitten and dragged away by the monster bug with cold, indifferent eyes.

,Why does that powerful strength belong to a man like that?'

If he had that kind of power, he could use it better. More meaningfully. More helpfully.

Park Hae-gil clenched his undamaged left fist tightly. He didn't have time to be pathetic like this. He had to prepare for the next thing. He couldn't stay here forever.

Deureureuk.

The door opened, and Yang Su-bin, wearing a white gown, came in. Yang Su-bin looked at him with tired eyes. He could smell soap on her body, as if she had just taken a shower.

"Su-bin-ah. Did you take a shower?"

"Yeah. I was all sweaty, maybe because it's hot."

"What's the occasion? You always showered at night, saying it was the most efficient."

"......That's not the case now. How's your right hand?"

"The painkiller must be working well. It doesn't hurt at all."

"I used a pretty strong painkiller."

A moment of uncomfortable silence passed.

Park Hae-gil carefully broke the silence.

"...Didn't you say you were going to talk to Yujin-ssi? What did Yujin-ssi say?"

"Fortunately, he said he'd sign a contract with PS Security. However, they can't pay money. Instead, they'll give food or snacks for the work they do. Besides that, if they do well, they'll give special privileges as a reward. The problem is that this is the last chance."

"The last chance. This must be all thanks to you. They need a doctor here. If it weren't for you, we might have died there. That muscle monster was someone we couldn't handle. Thank you, Su-bin-ah. Sincerely."

"If you're sincerely thankful, listen to my request."

"Request?"

"Please don't be so nosy anymore. I feel like I shouldn't be saying this, since I'm the one who received help from your meddling... I'm so anxious watching you from the side. You got discharged from the military because of that meddling, too. This time, you almost lost your right hand forever because of that meddling. I wish you would act for yourself, not for some stranger you don't even know. You know what the world is like now, right? Meddling doesn't help at all."

"......"

"Answer me."

"......If I had strength like Yujin-ssi, you wouldn't even say that."

"Hae-gil-ah!"

"You're right. My meddling was arrogance and a luxury for someone like me. It always ruined my life. This won't happen again. I promise."

"Thank you, Hae-gil-ah."

Yang Su-bin approached Park Hae-gil and hugged him. Park Hae-gil smiled faintly and hugged her shoulders back, then stopped.

The red spot remaining on her neck stood out. It wasn't strange. July 31st. It was the height of summer. It was common to get bitten by mosquitoes. Even now, if you let your guard down, mosquitoes would fly in.

,...A man who doesn't even bat an eye at witnessing a dying corpse is giving me another chance?'

Something was strange. He hadn't known Seong Yujin for long, but his personality was so unique and straightforward that it wasn't difficult to guess his actions. He decided to sign a contract with him, but he's forgiving him for disobeying orders? Why? Because he's short on manpower? A strongman who can fight and win against a muscle monster?

"......"

No. That couldn't be it. He just desperately needed a doctor. That must be it.

Park Hae-gil hugged her shoulders tightly.

Time passed even in anxiety.

Two weeks had passed.

PS Security's name was changed to Seongak Elementary School Security Team. Healthy men were added, and now there were about 30 people, divided into 3 teams.

Park Hae-gil was the security team leader of Team 1. The security guards belonging to PS Security, who could be called veterans, were all divided and took a position in each team. You could say they were the real power of the security team.

The people belonging to Seongak Elementary School basically don't receive a salary. Instead, they receive food or daily necessities as compensation for their work. The security team receives special privileges there. They can use some of the facilities as they please. They just have to stand and watch while others work. Zombies are basically stupid, so there's no dangerous work.

,More than anything, I received a weapon now.'

A gun. Even rifles and bulletproof vests were received. He wondered where they got the guns, but the research team leader said they made them themselves.

Research Team Leader Na Chae-young Baksa had only seen Park Hae-gil two or three times. That's because she was staying in the main building with Seong Yujin. Even the security team leader couldn't enter the main building carelessly. Only those who were allowed could enter the main building.

,Unknowingly, positions are being divided.'

Those with special skills could enter and live in the main building. The man in his 40s who came in last time lives luxuriously in the main building with his wife and children. His job was a machine repairman. A person who mainly repaired factory machines.

Everyone else lives in container boxes in the playground. Park Hae-gil also entered and lived in one of the container boxes. Thanks to his title as security team leader, he could use one box alone.

,This is another world. A place that is Korea but not Korea.'

Park Hae-gil headed towards the front entrance of the elementary school. People playing jokgu in the jokgu court next to the playground could be seen. Even though the outside was a mess due to the zombies, the people here were living a normal life. This was a special place.

"Have you arrived? Then please take care."

The Security Team 3 leader greeted him.

"You've worked hard."

The shift change was made in an instant without any handover. Security Team 3 leader, who used to be his subordinate, looked at him with a contemptuous gaze and passed by. It was because of his right hand that was injured by the bug back then. It was fatal for Park Hae-gil, who was right-handed.

Now he just had to spend time monitoring the outside with the monitor and occasionally patrolling. Park Hae-gil exchanged businesslike greetings with the Security Team 1 members and sat in the team leader's seat. The team member sitting next to him naturally took out his smartphone and entered a video site.

Park Hae-gil, who was about to frown and say something, soon widened his eyes. In the video, a man was fighting a variant while wielding a hammer. What was surprising was that lightning struck every time he swung the hammer. Just like how electricity sparks every time Seong Yujin swings his sword.

"...Who is that?"

That's Noeje (雷帝) Ban Ho-seong. Don't you know him? Ban Ho-seong, the guardian of Daejeon. He's been on the news a few times. Actually, people call him Daejeon Thor more often than Noeje.

If you watch the unedited version of that video, it's really no joke. What kind of zombies are swarming like ants... Ah, the military unit near there was also destroyed by the D virus."

Seong Yujin was famous for being fond of women. He doesn't act openly, but he subtly takes care of beautiful women. That must be true. If a beauty of unclear origin comes, he never sends her back. It's in stark contrast to when men come, he sends them out mercilessly.

No.
