Standing in the sky, I swept my gaze up and down the massive white tree. Seeing it up close made it feel even larger.

'Roughly 70 meters, maybe.'

The surface of the pure white tree, devoid of leaves, gleamed like polished marble. It didn't feel like a tree—more like a colossal structure shaped in the form of one.

Wuuuuung.

I move my mana. The mana within me and the mana in the atmosphere resonate with each other. Lightning flashes in the dry sky, gathering and spinning.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder (卍雷).

I reproduced the Thunder Break of the 'Dark Moon' world through the methods of the Thunder Heaven Style.

'I figured it would put up some resistance. But after releasing that wave once earlier, there's been no reaction at all. Did something go wrong? Or is it saying my attack isn't even worth acknowledging?'

Either way, the result would speak for itself.

I drop the lightning, amplified and condensed to its limit.

A bolt so thick it couldn't even be compared to ordinary lightning fell with enough force to split the massive white tree in two.

An orange wave blocked the lightning above the white tree. The bolt couldn't pierce through the wave. Blue electric sparks scattered in all directions like shattered glass.

“78.”

A voice emanates from the white tree. It's different from the method it used before, speaking to me through the wave. The sound is spreading outward. Not just me—anyone nearby would be able to hear it.

"That is the number of kin who sacrificed themselves for me."

"So what? What do you want me to do about it?"

"I wanted to let you know. The reason my kin willingly sacrificed themselves to force my growth—it was all because of you."

"Are you declaring revenge against me?"

"Revenge? Such emotions are a luxury for us. What matters most to us is survival. That said, it's not that we don't feel emotions. I am currently regretting things to the bone."

I sense the movement of mana from the white tree. From the branches down to the tips of the roots. The more finely it moves, the more stable it becomes.

The bastard was buying time with conversation while tuning its body. Optimization, you could call it.

I considered ignoring it and attacking, but decided to pry some information out first. There are three more Piron Forests besides this one in Sacheon.

"Is that regret for provoking me?"

"No. It's regret that my younger self, before receiving the sharing of experience and knowledge, let a human slip by mistake. If I hadn't missed that human back then, the World Hunter Association would never have known of my existence, and S-Rank hunters like you would never have come looking."

I stared at the tree with a bored expression. I had not the slightest interest in the sentiments of something that would be dead soon anyway.

"Piron's true body is a black tree. So why are you white? Do you control humans through waves? How is that even possible?"

"We can dominate lifeforms inferior to ourselves. I was born from that one's roots. To borrow a human word... you could call it a reincarnation. And I am also a descent-body prepared for that one. The reason my domination ability surpasses that of my kin is because my very birth was special."

"Oh. Then why can't you dominate the mind of an inferior human like me?"

"Humans vary too greatly as individuals. On top of that, compared to other organisms, their physical abilities are inferior, but the rank of their mind and soul is high. Dominating a human's mind over the long term is inefficient. I judged it better to dominate monsters instead."

"Is the red tree the one you call 'that one'?"

"...I don't know how you know that, but we also call that one the Great Life. Our beginning was that one, and through that one's sacrifice, we were able to escape a terrible existence and come to this place. And we learned that this world is also not safe. Do you know that the reason dungeons and dimensional rifts keep occurring is due to the absence of a god? Dungeons are attacks from a terrible existence. To defend against it, a World God must exist."

The terrible god this bastard speaks of is probably Bramasen. The World God would refer to Earth's god.

'So it's concluded that Earth has no god.'

Earth's god does exist. The World Hunter Association has it confined in a special space, so it's in proper condition.

"...Seeing that you're not surprised, it seems you already knew. Well, it wouldn't make sense for someone of your caliber not to know. I will make you a proposal here that benefits both you and humanity."

"A proposal? You want to negotiate again? After you stabbed me in the back first? You're shameless beyond belief."

"Aren't you the shameless one? I know you have no intention of negotiating with us in good faith. If we hadn't moved first, trusting you... we would have been helplessly destroyed. By the time you came looking, we would have had no preparations whatsoever."

"A creature without even a tongue sure talks a lot. Get to the point while I'm still listening."

"That one. Acknowledge and revere the Great Life. Cooperate with us and offer it divinity to make it the god of this world. Then that one will protect this world. Dungeons will disappear and humans will enjoy safe lives."

I chuckled.

"Heh. You call that a proposal? You're the one who said humans are inferior. And now you're begging that human for your life. That was funny enough to hear once."

"It is a rational proposal. You humans don't have much time left. Are you going to turn a blind eye even knowing that the number of dungeons appearing in this world is steadily increasing?"

"Do you know the saying 'wrong from the start'? You attacked humans from the moment you first appeared. And now you come asking for cooperation? Only fools would trust your words. Even if, as you say, the red tree becomes the new god of Earth and destruction disappears and everything becomes safe... that thing isn't humanity's god. It's your god. I can already picture humans becoming your slaves and livestock."

"...Words don't work with you. I will speak with the World Hunter Association Chairman."

"Even if the Chairman accepts your proposal, it's meaningless unless I allow it."

"As expected, you must be eliminated."

The moment the bastard's words ended, an orange wave wrapped around my surroundings.

My body, affected by the wave, trembles involuntarily, and my mana becomes unstable. I can't feel it properly thanks to Absolute Mind, but it's certainly affecting my mind as well.

'It attacks body, mind, and mana all at once, and since its essence is a wave, blocking it is nearly impossible. Even if I protect my body with a barrier, the wave would only weaken—it wouldn't be completely blocked.'

I already know the countermeasure.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

Against a wave, use a wave.

Amplified electromagnetic waves burst from my body, pushing back the wave.

With this, I've completely neutralized the bastard's attack! It's my victory! ...Is what I'd like to say, but the bastard instantly processed the wave. It gave physical form to the wave and created dozens of Piron incarnations in an instant.

'It feels similar to Jang Haran's spirit beasts rather than Piron's incarnations. Did it imitate them after being inspired by Jang Haran?'

I eject Meteor Swords from my inventory.

Dozens of Meteor Swords embed themselves in their bodies and explode. The struck incarnations lose their form and return to waves, disappearing.

'They don't have combat-specialized abilities like Jang Haran's spirit beasts. Roughly A-Rank monster level. They can be annoying, but they're not enough to land an effective hit on me. What's the bastard's real move?'

While staying wary of the bastard's next move, my hands were already moving.

I gripped the hilt of the summoned Hwaryeon Bido tightly and swung it at the bastard.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Flash (雷閃).

A blue slash wreathed in red lightning flies forward. The massive slash, over 5 meters long, was blocked by the wave protecting the white tree—then reflected straight back at me.

I was slightly surprised, but my hands calmly moved Hwaryeon Bido to strike down the Thunder Flash.

'Reflection. How far can it go?'

Meteor Sword.

Space splits open in the air and hundreds of Meteor Swords are ejected. Most are blocked by the wave and explode. It seems it can't reflect attacks indiscriminately.

A few Meteor Swords, deliberately ejected on twisted trajectories, reached the white tree.

Bang!

The explosive force of an ordinary Meteor Sword couldn't deal any meaningful damage.

'Still, I learned one thing. That wave isn't automatic defense.'

Attacks the bastard doesn't perceive can't be blocked by the wave.

'Should I try using Cheondun, the ability I gained when I raised the Cheonsim skill last time? If I use it openly it won't be effective, but... if I can escape the bastard's perception even for a moment, it could be effective... No, that's not it.'

The only thing watching me isn't just Piron in front of me.

Hunter reporters.

They're watching this battle from a distance.

'Today is my debut. Let's make it spectacular.'

Bzzzzzt!

Lightning bursts from my body.

Waves form at the tips of the white tree's thousands of branches. The waves compress, then become orange laser beams that fire at me.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕), maximum output.

At first, I blocked the bastard's lasers easily with the electromagnetic barrier, but the moment I confirmed the barrier was melting at a rapid rate, I changed my approach. From blocking to dodging.

[Using Acceleration. Will be maintained for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Gale Thunder (疾風迅雷).

I sprinted through the air, narrowly dodging the pouring lasers. But no matter how fast I was, dodging thousands of simultaneously fired lasers was impossible.

'Lightning.'

The ones I couldn't dodge, I canceled out by generating lightning.

The distance closes.

I see the massive wave blocking my path. The moment I drive my sword into the wave, the direction of force shifts and I'm flung up into the sky. As if waiting for this opportunity, thousands of lasers converge into one and fire.

I thrust my left hand forward.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder (卍雷).

Lightning gathers at the center of my palm in an instant and begins to spin. I poured my will into the lightning and focused to the limit.

Energy collides with energy.

The orange laser tried to pierce through Myriad Thunder, but Myriad Thunder held firm and kept spinning. The orange laser twisted in reverse, spinning through the air along with Myriad Thunder before vanishing.

The bastard stopped its attack as if catching its breath.

I pulled Jeunryeong from my inventory and used it. Dark clouds suddenly began gathering in the previously cloudless blue sky. No rain fell. Instead, thunder rumbled within the dark clouds.

"Do you think I'd let you create a favorable environment?!"

The bastard shouted.

A massive orange wave exploded at the center of the dark clouds. Power far too insufficient to kill me. But a wave explosion powerful enough to scatter the dark clouds gathered in the sky.

"Just as I thought. Compared to that massive body, your consciousness is limited. You're so focused on me that you have no capacity to watch the ground, are you?"

"What?!"

"Just because they're clouds doesn't mean they have to be in the sky. No, wait. Since they're on the ground, they're not clouds—they're fog."

I felt the bastard's consciousness drop to the ground instead of staying on me in the sky.

Dark clouds were spread thick across the ground, covering the earth. At their center stood the white tree, towering tall.

Kwaaaaarung.

Lightning flashes from the dark clouds on the ground.

Something impossible through ordinary natural phenomena. Lightning striking from fog is absurd. But those dark clouds were all under my dominion.

Lightning flashes between the dark clouds draped across the ground.

The resonated lightning is still insufficient, but I detonate it before the bastard can scatter the ground-level dark clouds with its waves.

"Howl, lightning."

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Ultimate Technique (極技) — Thunder Heaven's Command (雷天命).

Tens of thousands of lightning bolts shot up from the ground toward the sky.

The ground was bathed in lightning.
