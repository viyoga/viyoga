「Entered Sector 774, the Crossroads Shelter.」

Sector 774 was a place where a total of 108 roads intersected. There were roads in every direction. There were many wooden huts along the roads. They were huts that couldn't be called good condition even as a figure of speech. But some of the huts were inhabited. Because I could feel signs of life, like lights leaking out.

'I was told to find Road 23.'

I examine the roads in detail. If you look closely at the floor, the number 45 is engraved. This is Road 45. The road leading to the hut next to it also had a number on it. 81. These were addresses. Hut number 81 on Road 45.

'What I need to find is 23-106. Hut number 106 on Road 23.'

The road right next to it was number 44, so it wasn't difficult to find. Because the roads and huts are all numbered in order.

'Tch. What a pain in the ass. Why not just meet at the usual place.'

I know what Samuel is worried about. Because a spy was caught recently. He's probably trying to prevent information from leaking.

'Since when has he been so thorough...? No, is he being thorough because it's related to Tiamat's Heart?'

Anyway, after moving for 30 minutes, I found the meeting place.

'I can feel the presence of three people in the hut. If two are Helten and White, who is the remaining one? One of Helten's executives?'

I don't know. I'll find out when I go inside. I approached the hut and opened the old door.

「Entering hut 23-106. Direct combat is impossible in this hut for 10 hours, 41 minutes, and 23 seconds.」

It was a characteristic of Sector 774. Huts in this sector become immune to direct combat for 12 hours upon initial entry. However, you shouldn't be completely relieved because you can still do things like poisoning the water or setting traps at the entrance.

"You've arrived. Long time no see. Well. Is it only a one-sided long time no see from this side?"

Samuel, who was sitting in a chair in the center of the hut, waved his hand. White was standing next to him. Next to him, Lucas was kneeling with his hands and feet tied, a gag in his mouth, an eye patch on his eyes, and earplugs in his ears.

"I don't know why you insisted on meeting here. Did you think I would start a fight because I didn't want to give you Tiamat's Heart?"

"Haha. It's not like that. The safety guarantee of this hut is close to a joke. You can't engage in direct combat inside, but ranged attacks from outside work. The reason I chose this place... was because it's close."

"Close? Close to what?"

"Camelot Legion. It's an hour away from here. If you move a little faster, you can arrive within 10 minutes."

Samuel said, looking at the restrained Lucas.

I dug up Lucas's information and know it. Lucas Spencer. The master of the Camelot Legion. The bastard who directly targeted me in the Civilization Conquest.

"Are you trying to show off that you caught that bastard?"

"Don't be ridiculous. This is just a gift I brought along the way. If it weren't for you, I would have failed the challenge in Sector 7,500 and wouldn't have been able to challenge it again."

"I can give you Tiamat's Heart. But there's no such thing as a completely free lunch in this world."

I took Tiamat's Heart out of my inventory and placed it on my right hand. A heart that looked similar to a human's was beating on the palm of my hand.

"Of course, I know. I can't just receive something this big and shut my mouth. Tiamat's Heart is an SS-rank heart. Coincidentally, I have an item of the same rank. Choose one of the two."

Samuel opened a subspace and placed the items on the table.

One was a luxurious white glove with embroidery of a sword and shield, and the other was a chuunibyou-inducing purplish-black coat.

I checked the information of the two items.

「Sacred Duel Gauntlet.

Can be used on one target.

A 1-on-1 duel with the target is established.

No one can interfere in the duel.

Remaining uses: 3/3

Rank: SS」

「Wizard Killer.

Absorbs up to 3 spells depending on stats.

Stats increase for 10 minutes depending on the amount and level of magic absorbed.

Damage is reduced by 50% when hit by magic.

Rank: SS」

The purplish-black coat immediately caught my eye. Even if it's the same SS-rank item, this is one of the top-tier items.

Damage is reduced by 50% when hit by magic. This alone would be a terrible effect for wizards.

"Depending on stats? Exactly what kind of magic can it absorb?"

"It can absorb any kind of magic, regardless of the type. From simple and powerful elemental magic to complex illusions and barriers. There's a condition that your stats have to be high, though. If your total stats are 500 or higher, you can absorb magic below S-rank without taking any damage."

"And the absorbed magic becomes a buff and increases your stats."

I can't help but say it's fucking awesome.

But my hand reached for the glove, not the coat.

"I'll take the glove."

"That's unexpected. I thought you would choose the Wizard Killer. The glove even has a limited number of uses."

"The Wizard Killer isn't very effective unless the opponent is a wizard."

"It can also absorb magic traps or barriers installed in dungeons."

"Traps can be smashed, and barriers can be torn. I liked the glove."

"I'll respect your choice. Once the duel is established, nothing can interfere. Not gods, not the system. Maybe even the Illusion of the Mirror won't work."

"...Can the duel not be established?"

"There's nothing complete in this world. Nothing is perfect."

True. If I use Heavenly Heart when I'm affected by the effect of this glove, I'll be released immediately.

Contradiction.

If there is a shield that always blocks, there is also a spear that always pierces.

"Ah, tell me if you need my help. I'll help you as much as I can as a reward for this."

I succeeded in putting Samuel in my debt. Samuel is skilled, so he will be helpful later.

My gaze turned to Lucas. I felt good at the thought of taking this bastard to the living hell and tormenting him.

"You're not asking how I'm going to use Tiamat? Do you perhaps already know?"

"Samuel. Have you forgotten who I am?"

"...A regressor. So you knew White's identity."

"It's Pandora, you know."

Pandora. The first human created by the gods. And the starting point of all disasters and hope.

Samuel's goal is to turn her into a weapon to kill gods. Tiamat's Heart is just one of the ingredients for that.

I hadn't thought much about it until now. There wasn't much description of her in the original story either. But seeing her in person, she was a seriously beautiful woman. She had a doll-like feel to her, but... it can't be helped since she's not complete.

'I'll have to steal her later when she's complete.'

I haven't yet grasped the exact power of Samuel, the leader of Helten. I might even lose. And even if I steal her now, she's not complete, so it will only increase my work.

I'm going to take him to the living hell and find out everything from A to Z.
