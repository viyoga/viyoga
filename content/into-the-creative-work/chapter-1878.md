Strike while the iron is hot, Seong Yujin decided to move as soon as he stamped his seal on the contract. Of course, he didn't move alone. He took the entire 30-member security team with him. He was planning to test the security team this time. If he judged that they were of no help at all, he would replace them all.

It wasn't without a second thought that he took the entire security team. Even without the security team, the walls of Seongak Elementary School were high enough that zombies couldn't invade.

Besides, there weren't many zombies around Seongak Elementary School. He judged that there would be no problem even if he left the place empty for a few hours. Because the order inside Seongak Elementary School was already in place. And that was Seong Yujin's delusion and miscalculation.

Among the 200 people who had evacuated to Seongak Elementary School, there were those who were dissatisfied with the order of Seongak Elementary School. They had no choice but to suppress their dissatisfaction. Because Seong Yujin was there. And because there was a security team armed with guns.

But now the security team was gone. Seong Yujin, who had sliced through hundreds of zombies, was also gone. People had other thoughts. Representatively, it was Yoo Donghak, who was the head of the marketing department of a company that anyone would know just by hearing the name.

He was dissatisfied with everything.

Living in container boxes scattered and piled up in the playground, the tasteless basic meals provided in the cafeteria, the arrogant behavior of the security team, and the simple labor of only moving heavy objects. He didn't like any of it.

What he disliked the most was that the technicians he used to ignore were being treated preferentially here. They eat more delicious food. They even live in temporary accommodations built behind the school building. It was also a building made of containers, but the level was different from those scattered in the playground.

Yoo Donghak was always looking for opportunities. He was confident that he could do anything to climb up, whether it was politics, bribery, or threats, and he actually did.

He gathered those who were close to him, who were also dissatisfied with Seongak Elementary School. They were all people who had lived quite well before the D virus broke out. Like him, they were heads or managers of companies, landlords, phone sellers, and so on.

,Seong Yujin and the security team have gone out. I heard they'll be back in a few hours. This is an opportunity. How about we run Seongak Elementary School this time? Don't you all know how messed up he runs this place? Seongak Elementary School should be run for the benefit of many people. Without discrimination."

,Yoo Donghak-ssi is right. There is too much discrimination. In particular, Seong Yujin-gun doesn't hesitate to use his fists. Last time, my son went into the main building because he wanted to play some computer games, and Seong Yujin beat him up. He broke my kid's arm, his arm."

,It's a bit much that he went into a restricted area without permission, but the punishment is too severe. Even though the law isn't being properly used these days... tsk."

,I have to work for three days to eat one pack of ramen. This doesn't make sense."

,Do you know Park Seongshik, who used to work under me? That bastard is cutting steak with his wife and children every day. He even came to me to brag about it? A guy who used to be wary of me in society..."

They occupied one container and complained one by one. Time passed. But Yoo Donghak didn't stop them and encouraged them. Because that's how the dissatisfaction would build up.

And someone asked the question Yoo Donghak wanted.

,Yoo Donghak-ssi. You said it was an opportunity earlier. What are you going to do?"

,The owner of Seongak Elementary School is Seong Yujin. When he's not here, the one who manages it is his woman, Dr. Na Chaeyeong. I heard that she's the one who made the guns."

,Are you suggesting we kill her...?"

,Oh my! What a terrible thing to say! We have to catch her and persuade her. That's the only way she'll make weapons for us, right?"

,Will she follow us so easily?"

,Well, it might be okay to intimidate her a little. The important thing is that it's for everyone, right? Let's catch her first and persuade her slowly."

,What if Seong Yujin comes back? When I think about him cutting down hundreds of zombies a week ago, my legs are still..."

Shaky shaky. The middle-aged man shook his legs relentlessly. That ridiculous appearance was surprisingly not an exaggeration.

,He's also human. He'll die if he gets shot here. Is there anyone here who hasn't been to the military? All we have to do is get our hands on the guns in the armory. After that, we can manage Seongak Elementary School. Even if the security team comes back, we can respond with guns. You know how high the walls of the elementary school are, right? We have the advantage."

,...Can we do it ourselves? It seems like there are too few of us."

,We need to gather people we can trust. We just need to raid the armory on the second floor of the main building. Just the armory."

,It's not going to be that easy to get there..."

,Let's catch the technician Park Seongshik, who I mentioned earlier, and have him open the armory. He must have equipment. Once we get our hands on the guns in the armory, what can that Dr. Na do? Oh, do you know the people locked up in the detention room?"

,Are you talking about those troublemakers?"

If you cause big or small problems at Seongak Elementary School, you'll be caught by the security team and transferred to the detention room. In short, the detention room was a place for punishment. If you commit a crime that goes too far, you'll be exiled outside Seongak Elementary School with nothing but your body.

,Let's persuade those bastards. They must have a lot of complaints. If we gather people like that... the number of people who agree with us will increase. Who is satisfied with this life?"

If people gather, it'll work. People become stronger as they gather. That's common sense. A superhuman like Seong Yujin is an existence outside of common sense. And currently, that existence outside of common sense is not here.

Everyone's eyes lit up. Specific plans were exchanged and actions were taken. First, they decided to catch the technicians, including Park Seongshik. It's almost lunchtime.

Most people receive food at the cafeteria on the first floor, and those who are treated preferentially, including the technicians, eat at the restaurant on the second floor. It wasn't difficult to go up to the second floor. Yoo Donghak divided the people. One side was targeting the technicians, and the other was targeting the detention room.

They won people over. Including the people who were won over, there were more than 60 people in total. The rest were not won over on purpose. Because they didn't really want equality.

It wasn't difficult to surround and restrain the technicians. There were many people on the side of the rebellion, and the technicians didn't know anything about the attack.

,Please cooperate with us. This elementary school must be run without discrimination. Do you really think the current situation is right?"

,You ungrateful bastards. I actually felt sorry for you guys... but not anymore. Seong Yujin-nim was right when he said that humans reveal their true nature when they're released."

Yoo Donghak frowned. There were a total of 6 technicians caught. All of them were hostile. There was no sign of cooperation at all.

,I don't want to go violent. I know better than anyone that technicians like you are important in this era. Join us. We'll treat you without discrimination too."

,Why don't you put down the club in your hand first and then talk? You guys just want power. Money doesn't work here, and your career in society doesn't work either, so it must be killing you. Seong Yujin-nim will kill you all when he comes. I know his personality, he's really no joke. Oh, maybe you won't even be able to die. Do you know that there's a special detention room below the detention room? That place is real hell. If I were you, I'd just leave here. But I wouldn't have done that in the first place."

,...You've been thoroughly brainwashed."

Yoo Donghak was annoyed. He didn't like the fact that a nobody was looking at him with pity. In the end, he beat the technicians with a club. But the technicians were stubborn. Not a single one of them said they would cooperate. He had no choice but to stop hitting them before they died.

He couldn't kill them. Using violence was already crossing the line a bit. If he killed them, the justification would collapse. And the technicians were needed in the future.

Tti-ring!

A message came from his smartphone.

,Everyone. The people who were locked up in the detention room have agreed to cooperate with us!"

,Waaaaah!"

Some of the inmates were scared and didn't cooperate, but it didn't matter.

,Most of the inmates are strong guys... if we put them in front, they'll be helpful.'

That's when it happened.

The ceiling speaker turned on and a sigh was heard.

-Haa. A rebellion... then Yujin's words are right.

A woman's voice.

It was definitely Dr. Na Chaeyeong's. It seems like she was caught because there were cameras all over the elementary school. Yoo Donghak didn't care. Because it was something he had already expected.

-I'll give you one last chance. I'll give you some necessities, so I hope you'll leave the elementary school. There's a famous saying that when in Rome, do as the Romans do, right? There's also a saying that if you don't like the temple, the monk should leave. Let's end this on a good note. Please leave.

,That's ridiculous. If we leave this place, we'll just be eaten by zombies.'

The truth is, they don't hate this place. Who would hate this place, which is like an ark? What they hate is working at the bottom.

,That woman is just talking unilaterally! If we leave here, we'll just die! What we want is equality! Look! She doesn't even want to communicate! We need to go to that woman and tell her what we want!"

Yoo Donghak, who spoke powerfully, knew that it was time to move as quickly as possible. If he gave them time, the people in the main building would arm themselves with guns. He could become disadvantaged.

,Let's go!!"

As Yoo Donghak ran, people followed him in droves. It wasn't far from the main building, so they soon arrived. As expected, the steel shutter was closed at the entrance of the main building.

,Damn it. There are iron bars on the windows too. There's no time or equipment to cut the iron bars. There are no iron bars on the 4th and 5th floor windows, so should I quickly bring a ladder and climb up there?'

Bbang bbang!

A car horn sounded.

It was a truck used for work. The truck driver was an inmate. A middle-aged old man who fought with the kitchen ajumma over the food issue and was locked up in the detention room.

,Get out of the way! I'm going to break through with this!"

Enraged by his time in prison, he crashed into the entrance as it was.

Kwaaaang! Jjaenggeurang!

The shutter and glass door were shattered. The old man passed out with his head buried in the steering wheel.

,Crazy bastard.'

Yoo Donghak was slightly fed up, but this was an opportunity.

,The door is open! Let's go!! For freedom and equality!!"

,For freedom and equality!!"

People shouted and ran towards the main building.

-You fools.

Na Chaeyeong's monotonous voice echoed through the speaker.

And immediately after.

Dudududududududu.

,Helicopter sound?'

No. The sound was too cute to be a helicopter sound. It was a drone. Five combat drones the size of a human torso greeted them as soon as they entered the entrance.

-Yujin's answer just came in. He said don't kill them, just subdue them. It seems like he doesn't intend to just kill you.

The people, including Yoo Donghak, were too distracted to listen to Na Chaeyeong's words. The combat drones' guns spewed fire. But the gunshots weren't that loud. What flew from the guns were taser bullets.

Taser gun. Of course, it wasn't an ordinary taser gun. It was improved with galchinium, so the size of the taser bullets was reduced, and even if they missed, ordinary humans would pass out.

Tat tat tat tat tat!

The sound of the combat drones firing tasers was strangely addictive. But there were no people savoring that sound.

,Aaaaaaaack!"

,Son of a bitch!!!"

,G, get out of the way!!"

Pandemonium. The moment they saw the five combat drones, people lost their will to fight and started running away. Of course, no matter how hard they ran, they couldn't shake off the combat drones. Because combat drones can fly.

After 1 minute, there were no humans standing on two feet. Some even pissed and shat themselves.

-The combat AI's first real battle was successful. By the way... haa. When am I going to clean all this up?

Na Chaeyeong's sigh only continued to grow.
