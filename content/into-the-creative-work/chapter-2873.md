The Suwol Guild disappeared.

Less than a week after the Suwol Guild collapsed, the media and the internet stopped mentioning it.

No problems arose in Korea after the Suwol Guild disappeared. It would have been a different story if S-Class Hunter Han Ah-young-ssi had disappeared from Korea. Han Ah-young-ssi remained in Korea, and there were many large guilds to replace the Suwol Guild. Korea had an unusually large number of A-Class Hunters compared to its population.

,I'm realizing Han Ah-young-ssi's status anew. And the Goryeo Guild, which is maintaining its position as the number one guild in Korea despite Han Ah-young-ssi's absence, is also catching my eye again.'

Goryeo Guild.

They are currently ranked number one despite not having an S-Class Hunter. They overwhelm the Suwol Guild, which is ranked second in terms of size and performance. They clear A-Class dungeons as easily as eating a meal and also attack S-Class dungeons. They have three strike teams dedicated to S-Class dungeons and twelve strike teams for A-Class dungeons.

Of course, I know the true nature of the Goryeo Guild now. The Goryeo Guild includes Korea's five major families, as well as families known as prestigious clans.

Families that have been subtly influencing the Korean Peninsula since ancient times.

Hunters from these families are stronger than ordinary Hunters. It is generally known that one can only become a Hunter by Awakening... but they are different. They receive training from a young age without Awakening. In fact, most members of these families Awaken.

,Even though their numbers are very small compared to modern times, Awakeners have existed since ancient times. Awakeners were called magicians back then. Most prestigious families are descendants of ancient Awakeners.'

If a parent is an Awakener, there is a high probability that their child will also be an Awakener.

,Even if they are not Awakeners, prestigious families know their own methods of handling mana.'

Like mana cultivation techniques.

The Yeongcheon Style I learned when I was a nobody also had a mana cultivation technique called Yeongcheon Spiritual Energy Art. Of course, Yeongcheon Spiritual Energy Art could only be learned by those who had Awakened their mana. I guess it's fitting for the Yeongcheon Style, which requires talent to master.

,Hehe. Mana Awakening is equally difficult for both Awakeners and ordinary people. But I have a Mana Awakening Potion?'

This is a potion that can be steadily produced in the [Baekhwan] world. Moreover, it can be copied with a potion copier! There are no side effects either!

,I could sell it for at least several billion to Hunters who can't become C-Class because they haven't Awakened their mana.'

I've never actually sold it.

I didn't need the money enough to sell it. If I needed money, I could just go into an A-Class dungeon alone and wipe it out. That alone would earn me at least several hundred million.

,What I need now isn't money, but Amusement Life Points.'

[You have selected Dark Moon.]

[The Amusement begins.]

• • •

I, who had been spacing out, came to my senses.

I experience déjà vu about once a month. It's a sensation where familiar things feel strange. It's a sudden sensation, so I used to get flustered. But now I'm used to it.

I don't know the exact cause of the déjà vu. My physical condition is perfect, and there are no mental problems.

,Everyone experiences problems. Other espers are the same. Compared to espers who lose their minds due to the side effects of their powers, déjà vu is nothing.'

The déjà vu I felt today was quite strong.

,The déjà vu is probably caused by the ability I have. On days when the déjà vu is strong like today, there are slight changes in my ability. Usually for the better.'

The first thing I checked was my inventory.

,As expected. There are more things I don't recognize. The inventory itself has grown. Not only in size, but it also has an ejection ability. The problem is that I have to pay gold to use it. Meteor Sword? There are hundreds of thousands of black swords that explode on contact and inflict elemental damage... and there's an incredibly large one too.'

These things suddenly appeared? Just because of my ability?

,That can't be. No matter how much of an ability it is, it can't explain the items in the inventory. Before I possessed this world, the items that were originally in my Earth are in the inventory. And every time I experience déjà vu, items are added to the inside of the inventory.'

Looking at the items added to the inventory, there are quite a few useless things. But there were quite a few items that suited my taste.

,The most likely guess is that the one adding items to the inventory is me from a parallel world. Judging from the machines that cannot be manufactured with Earth-level civilization and the machines and magical tools that do not exist in this world... there may be more than one parallel world.'

What caught my attention was the Meteor Sword. Hundreds of thousands of magical tools are in the inventory? It's not easy to mass-produce magical tools in a factory. Not to mention the value of each one. And their sizes and attributes are all different.

,It's likely that they were made individually using a special method.'

Suddenly, I felt that I could also make a Meteor Sword.

So I tried it.

The mana inside me flowed out and gathered into one, taking the form of an oriental sword. What was created was a Meteor Sword with a subtle blue light.

,...Indeed. It was being realized with my ability. I can't maintain this for long. It will naturally disappear over time. I understand why it's kept in the inventory.'

I looked at the Meteor Sword from side to side and stroked the blade with my finger.

The tip of my finger shone blue, temporarily imbuing the Meteor Sword with magic.

Cold air flowed down the blade. Since it was an enchantment magic without any materials, the duration was less than a minute. I put the Meteor Sword into my inventory. And I waited for one minute.

,The enchanted Meteor Sword doesn't disappear.'

Uuung.

The space split and the enchanted Meteor Sword was ejected. The Meteor Sword was summoned back and disappeared before it touched the wall.

,I can eject the items I put in without additional cost. The mana efficiency consumed for ejection isn't bad. I can use it in an emergency. I should put in a Meteor Sword enhanced with enchantment. If I put in other ordinary weapons, I can use a variety of patterns.'

Don't they often say that magicians are those who prepare? I decided to grit my teeth and do it, even if it's annoying.

Afterwards, while practicing, I realized that the power of my lightning magic had increased and my leaping ability had been enhanced.

I wondered what the cause was and found the answer in the inventory. It was because of an item called the Lightning Master Rune.

,It exerts its power just by being in the inventory. It's an amazing item.'

• • •

I am a successful entrepreneur in Neo London, but as a noble, I don't have much influence.

What I can do as a businessman and what I can do as a noble are similar but different.

And in Neo London, the influence of nobles is strong. The way to increase influence as a noble is simple. Just attend noble parties and build connections. Especially since I have wealth and power, building connections is easy. It's just a bit annoying.

,LK Corporation is still going strong. But as time goes by, the buzz is fading. Even if they don't do it openly, there are those who subtly try to check me.'

The Neo London Assembly and the Magic Association are favorable to LK Corporation. It's natural since they own some of the shares. But the nobles were different. Companies that see LK Corporation as a thorn in their side will lobby the nobles to subtly check me.

,Even if they can't bring down LK Corporation, it's annoying. Even if the company's growth rate drops by just 1% due to that check, it's a loss.'

I reluctantly attend the nobles' social gatherings. The nobles seem to want to befriend me, sending me invitations several times a day.

Of course, not all nobles are the same. As old as Neo London is, the history of the nobles is also old. There are several factions among the nobles.

The main ones are the Royalists and the Assembly Faction. And below that, they are divided into hardliners, moderates, and so on.

,What leads Neo London is the Neo Round Table Assembly. The problem is that even there, factions are divided.'

The power of the royal family was not weak either. Most of the natives of Neo London support the royal family.

,I'm neither. The Royalists and the Assembly Faction want me but can't treat me carelessly. In other words, it's possible to play them.'

Maintain neutrality?

Well, it's better to join one side than to stick to a mediocre position.

,I lose more than I gain by maintaining neutrality.'

I looked at the invitations that came to me.

Most of the nobles of Neo London sent me invitations. From named nobles to fallen nobles. Social party invitations, ball invitations, concert invitations, exhibition invitations, charity party invitations, marriage proposal invitations, private yacht party invitations, and so on. There were all sorts of invitations.

Originally, I don't look at them myself. Most of them are cut off by the servants. Only invitations from authoritative nobles come to my desk.

[Telekinesis]

I used magic to float several invitations in the air and read them quickly.

,Most of them sent them without much expectation. Like a pie in the sky. They're just trying to poke at me once.'

In particular, there were quite a few tea time invitations from noble young ladies of similar age to me. Most of them were written directly.

,Young ladies I don't even know. I'm a coveted item. Successful at a young age and a 7th-circle magician... Well, most of them were ordered by their families.'

I classified the letters into three categories.

Good to attend.

Ambiguous in terms of time.

Trash.

Of course, there was overwhelmingly more trash.

,I only have one body and time is limited. I have to choose well.'

As I continued to classify, one letter caught my interest.

It was a letter written and sent directly by a man named Viscount Peeble.

The content, summarized without flowery language, was that his daughter was cursed, and he wanted me, a famous magician, to help.

,He didn't write down what kind of curse it is. Is it a curse that's difficult to talk about? If he's sending a letter to me... it's not an ordinary curse.'

I became interested as a magician.

I got on the carriage and headed to Viscount Peeble's mansion.
