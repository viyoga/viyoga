「Paying 10,000,000 AP to the Gentlemanly Demon (False).」

「AP Held: 1,919,415」

Solomon's Ring became mine. I eagerly tried to slip it onto my right index finger, then checked the ring already worn there—the seal of the Boci Magic Tower. I'd forgotten about it. I put the ring on my middle finger instead.

「Equipping Solomon's Ring.」

「Mana stat increases by 10.」

"Not bad. Just by wearing it, my mana stat jumps by a whole 10. And on top of that, that existence is sealed inside it. You're telling me you sold this for a mere 10 million AP?"

"Haha. I'm more astonished that you possessed that kind of wealth, honestly. Even an emperor couldn't have accumulated 10 million AP. And even if they did, they'd never have made it this far."

He wasn't wrong.

Just gathering a million AP is already an absurd amount.

If you ruled multiple zones, accumulating it would be possible in theory, but most never reach 10 million AP.

'Hoarding it is a loss in itself. AP is best invested immediately.'

There are plenty of places to spend AP. The shop being the prime example. You can buy elixirs that permanently raise stats, or equipment that helps in combat.

"Why don't you just wear this fine ring yourself? Can demons not use it?"

"I can use it. I'm the one who sealed Agares, after all. It's simply that I am, first and foremost, the shop's proprietor. No matter how magnificent an item may be, it's never more important than money."

The moment he mentioned money, his nostrils flared.

Looking at this demon, I had a gut feeling. This bastard would sell his own nation—hell, his own family—if it meant turning a profit. Assuming demons even have nations or families.

As I swallowed a scoff of disbelief, it occurred to me that he'd casually spoken a demon's true name. Normally, just hearing that name should have affected me.

"That name—Agares—is it alright to say it?"

"You said it yourself, didn't you."

"I said it as a test. And I'm fine."

"One who wears Solomon's Ring may speak or hear a demon's true name without consequence."

"I see. So it had that kind of hidden ability as well."

"Rather than a hidden ability, I'd call it a fundamental one."

"I'd like to know more about how to use the ring. And how to handle the sealed demon? Do I just extract its authority and nothing else?"

"That'll be 100,000 AP, customer."

"Your service is dogshit."

"Service costs money too."

I considered arguing, then dropped it. Arguing with a money-obsessed demon is an exercise in stupidity. It's only 100,000 AP.

「Paying 100,000 AP to the Gentlemanly Demon (False).」

「After 10 minutes, 1 AP is consumed.」

「AP Held: 1,819,414」

"I trust this will be worth the money."

"You cannot force commands upon the demon sealed within Solomon's Ring. You can summon the sealed demon, but if you call it forth unprepared, it may attack you."

"So I'm supposed to just use its authority while it's sealed?"

"You make a deal with the sealed demon. If you offer to release it from its seal, it will grant virtually any request. Including requests that could lead to its own annihilation."

"Even annihilation?"

"To a demon, being sealed in that ring is an utterly humiliating state of affairs."

"How do I seal another demon?"

"First, you must learn the demon's true name, and then you must subjugate it. More precisely, you need to make the demon feel defeat. In my case, I won a wager against Agares. Ah, and only one demon can be sealed in the ring at a time, so to seal a new demon, you must first release the existing one."

So that was the reason hearing a demon's true name had no effect. You need to know the true name in order to seal it.

"How do I use the authority?"

"You expend mana to use the authority. The amount of mana consumed will vary depending on how you use it."

Having obtained the information I needed, I rose from my seat.

"Oh my. Leaving already? I have some excellent products in stock, you know."

"There's nothing I need right now. I'll come again."

"Please drop by whenever it's convenient. VIPs are always welcome. Ah, this one's on the house."

He handed me a fortune cookie.

「Demon's Fortune Cookie.

One random stat increases or decreases.

Applies only once.

Rank: S」

"A random stat increase. You're giving away something precious as a freebie."

"The odds on that are fifty-fifty, you know?"

"For me, it's a guarantee."

I exited the demon's shop and ate the fortune cookie. It didn't taste very good.

「You have eaten the Demon's Fortune Cookie.」

「Luck stat increases by 1.」

• • •

I returned to the Red Drought.

I decided to spread out the map and formulate a plan. First, I'd conquer the nearest hell zone.

'In most territories where a ruler exists, you can gain dominion by killing the ruler.'

The problem was that I had no way of knowing the level of other hell territories.

'I could have found out by trading information with Mammon, but... I need to save my emergency AP. A hell baron shouldn't be that strong anyway.'

It was just as my thoughts were about to deepen. From my right, a force numbering in the dozens appeared.

It was a force composed of demonic beasts and demons. Their hostility toward me was blatant.

At the front of the force stood a demon with bat wings. Our eyes met. The bastard looked at me and grinned. His face already wore the look of victory, even though we hadn't even fought yet.

"So you really are human. The shopkeeper's information was correct."

"...Shopkeeper? Don't tell me Mammon sold my information?"

"You madman! To speak his true name with your mouth! How ignorant! ...No. Why are you fine?"

The demon furrowed his brow and glared at me. I could feel his wariness toward me.

I looked at him and asked.

"You're not a威信. Who are you, and where are you from?"

"I am Lograid, from the 9,373rd territory, the Black Quarry. A Baron of Hell. I will kill you and become the master of the Red Drought."

The demon licked his dry lips with his tongue. But his lips dried out again in an instant.

It was because the penalty of the Red Drought was being applied to the demon and his forces. Dehydration, and a random stat reduction every 24 hours.

The demon must be dying to kill me right now.

I pulled ice water from my inventory and took a sip. My throat wasn't particularly dry, but it was meant as a provocation.

"...Where did you get water? This is a hellish place where you can't easily find a single drop of water."

"Aah. This water tastes good. Does water come out in your territory?"

The demon's face twisted. Seeing how he couldn't answer right away, it seemed his place was just as hard to find water as here. The kind of place where you can only drink water when it rains. Though here, even that rain barely ever falls.

"Enough. I'll kill you and take my time exploring this territory."

The demon raised his right arm high. Black energy gathered above his hand.

Krrrrrrrrrrrrk.

A gale rose centered on the demon, and the space around him warped little by little.

'He looked like a piece of shit... but he's stronger than I thought. Any decent mid-to-small legion wouldn't even be able to touch that one.'

On top of that, the demonic beasts and demon soldiers he'd brought weren't low-level either.

'So that's what a hell baron is like...'

I raised only my middle finger on my right hand. The Ring of Solomon I'd just put on glinted in the sunlight.

"You son of a bitch..."

The demon seemed to know what the middle finger meant too.

I focused on the ring and used mana.

「Using the demon's authority sealed within the Ring of Solomon.」

「Authority: Word of Command.」

"Stop."

More than half my mana was consumed in an instant. And the entire surrounding space froze.

The wind stopped, the enemies' advance stopped, Lograid's technique stopped. Perhaps from the backlash of being forcibly halted, blood poured from Lograid's mouth. He'd suffered internal injuries.

However, Lograid's expression didn't stop.

"A mere human using the Word of Command...! Impressive, but this must be your best. The power of the Word will vanish soon! That will be your end!"

"Harm yourselves."

All my remaining mana disappeared.

Lograid's forces began to harm themselves. They bit their tongues, stabbed their own bodies with swords and claws. They began self-harming exactly as I willed.

「Demon slain. 52 AP acquired.」

「Demonic beast slain. 36 AP acquired.」

……

Lograid stabbed his own throat with a sharp black nail. Blood burst out, but he barely stopped just before death and clutched the wound. Demonic energy surged from his hand and was absorbed into the wound. The wound regenerated at a rapid pace.

"...I nearly died in the most absurd way. There must be a reason a mere human became a hell baron after all. No more letting my guard down. I'll kill you right now!"

[Using Acceleration. Lasts 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido and charged straight ahead.

Startled by my speed, which was faster than expected, Lograid flapped his wings and tried to flee into the sky.

[Using Setsuna. Remaining stacks: 10]

Riding the momentum of the instant acceleration, I jumped. The red blade flashed and sliced cleanly through Lograid's neck.

Landing softly on the ground, I shook the blood off my blade.

"Good to know, the level of a hell baron. Nothing special."

If a hell baron was at this level, I should be able to handle a hell count without much trouble.
