Leaving Ptelgar-nim's mansion, I took off the mask and put on a face made of artificial leather. A human skin mask. My face was now that of a slightly older man.

,This is daunting.'

I have to hunt down the hidden black magician. The only information I have is that the black magician is a tattoo artist.

,...Let's just do enough. Just enough. It's a mission I'll give up on in three days anyway.'

I fiddled with the mobile watch on my left wrist. The mobile watch was this world's smartphone. It can project holograms and connect to the internet. It's usually used with smart lenses.

Smart lenses are special contact lenses. The smart lens projects the screen of the mobile watch.

,Information keeps coming in.'

Number 212 was organizing the information the guards got from interrogating the hookers and sending it to me. Most of it was information about tattoo artists in this area.

,Couldn't find any information about the exploded hooker's friend. There are 21 tattoo artists in this area. I'll have to go and check them one by one.'

First, the closest ones.

I walked down the street, looking at the map projected by the smart lens. It was a gray city. The factories were running and there was a lot of black smoke in the sky. Most of the people walking down the street were workers in oil-stained clothes.

Still, it's a place where people live, so if you look around, you can find everything. Restaurants, bars, convenience stores, and so on. I looked around and wandered around the alleys.

I arrived at my first destination. It's a tattoo shop that operates with a worn-out sign in a deep alley.

There were a lot of worn-out machines, but surprisingly, it was packed with customers. I passed the waiting customers and headed to the studio.

,Hey, just a minute! Who are you?! Can't you see the people waiting here?!'

A red-haired woman with a short cut, who appeared to be an employee, popped out and stopped me. She had piercings in her ears and nose, and wore revealing clothes, with more than half of her body covered in tattoos.

I didn't want to bother explaining the situation. I took out a pistol from my pocket and aimed it at the woman's forehead.

,Move.'

The pale-faced woman quietly stepped back. The waiting customers also quietly left the tattoo shop. They were used to the commotion and knew how to deal with the situation.

I kicked open the studio door. A skinny man who was tattooing a man's back looked at me in horror.

,W-What are you! And what's with the gun?!'

Tang!

The bullet grazed the tattoo artist's temple. The tattoo artist's eyes widened.

,Don't do anything stupid and stay still.'

I approached the tattoo artist. He rolled his eyes. The customer lying on the bed watched and ran away.

,W-What the hell is this? Are you from a new mafia group that's settled in this area? Our shop is not an illegal tattoo shop. We are officially licensed to operate the shop. If you look over there, you can see the tattoo license.'

,Don't do anything stupid if you don't want to die.'

I grabbed the man's shoulder tightly.

Mana moves along the mana road. Mana easily invaded the man's body. There was no resistance. Unpaid. He's an ordinary person who doesn't even know how to feel or use mana.

,W-What do you want? Money?'

,I'm looking for a tattoo artist. Tell me everything you know about the tattoo artists in this area.'

,W-Why are you looking for a tattoo artist?'

,...I've been too much of a gentleman.'

Tang!

The bullet pierced his thigh.

,Kuaaaak!'

,I'm asking the questions. If you want to live, answer my questions honestly.'

,Kueueue.... A-Alright. The tattoo artist is....'

He told me about all the tattoo artists he knew. There were four tattoo artists that weren't on the list. Illegal tattoo artists.

After hearing the information, I left the tattoo shop.

,It's better to prioritize the illegal tattoo artists.'

,Stop!'

I heard a woman's voice from behind.

I slowly turned around. The female employee of the tattoo shop I saw earlier was pointing a gun at me.

,What the hell is this, suddenly showing up! You think you can get away with disrupting someone else's business?!'

,No one died. What's the problem?'

,Are you seriously saying that right now?! You shot Jack in the thigh and you're asking what's the problem?!'

,Do you want medical expenses? Sorry, but I don't have any money.'

She gritted her teeth.

,I swore two years ago that I wouldn't just take it anymore...!'

The woman pulled the trigger. The bullet flew towards my head. But the bullet didn't touch my body. The barrier magic I had used in advance deflected the bullet.

The woman's eyes widened for a moment. She trembled and took a step back.

,M-Magician...?! Why a magician?!'

I walked towards her. The woman gritted her teeth, pointed the gun at me, and pulled the trigger. It was a meaningless act. The caliber of the pistol couldn't penetrate my barrier. It might have worked if it was a magic bullet, but magic bullets are not something that ordinary people can easily obtain.

Cheolkeok cheolkeok.

The bullets ran out. The woman lowered her arm weakly. Her eyes were full of fear as she looked at me. Nevertheless, I could feel the unyielding fighting spirit in her clenched fist.

I grabbed the woman's neck. The woman choked.

,I'm not interested in your past circumstances. Even if I knew, it wouldn't change anything. I'm not going to kill you. You shot me, but it's not even a threat. Instead, I'll give you a piece of advice. Live quietly.'

I released the hand that was holding her neck. The woman sat down on the floor and glared at me.

,Live quietly?! You're the one who barged in when I was living quietly! Are we... are we supposed to just keep getting screwed over by guys like you?!'

,What's so new about that? Isn't that the way the world is? You don't want to get screwed over? Then get stronger.'

I turned my back on the woman. I hesitated a few times whether to kill her, but decided to let her live. She was quite pretty. If she dressed up properly, she would be a decent beauty.

•••

After striking out for the seventh time, I sighed and moved to find the eighth illegal tattoo artist. The sky was already dark. Perhaps because there were so many factories around, I couldn't see the stars or even the moon in the night sky.

,I'm getting tired. I'll check up to the eighth one today and check the ninth one tomorrow during the day.'

I walk down the alley. I was getting used to the smell of greasy oil. I came out of the alley. I headed towards the half-destroyed, ruined bridge. Instead of flowing river water, the bottom of the bridge was filled with sticky, congealed waste oil.

And next to the bridge. There was a small house made of scrap metal.

[Barrier]

I wrapped my body in a barrier and kicked the plank door. I pointed my gun and went inside.

In the center of the dark house, a man was sitting comfortably. He looked at me with eyes that shone fiercely, looking to be in his 50s.

I knew it instinctively. This guy is the black magician.

,I heard that some young magician is running around causing trouble.... Is this Ptelgar-nim's order?'

,You're the one who made the hooker into a living bomb and sent her to Ptelgar's mansion. My mission will be over soon, so I guess I should say you're lucky.'

I pulled the trigger.

The bullet flew in a straight line, but was blocked by the barrier and bounced off. I wasn't flustered. It was expected. A black magician is also a magician. Even if it's difficult to use elemental magic, they can easily use non-attribute magic like this.

,You. You have nothing to do with Clackson. Right?'

,What are you talking about? Clackson hired me.'

,I saw Clackson. He doesn't seem like the kind of guy who would be so reckless as to use a living bomb.'

,Do you judge people by their appearance? How stupid.'

,More than anything, Clackson doesn't have the money to hire a black magician like you.'

The black magician just chuckled.

While I was talking, I activated the magic I had prepared.

[Shock Wave]

A powerful shockwave blew away the black magician along with the surrounding objects. I took a step back and got out of the collapsing building.

I frowned. It was because I saw the black magician's body, swept away by the shockwave, turn into black smoke.

,Is it some kind of illusion? Where is the real one?'

[Detection]

I spread mana to search for the hidden black magician.

I found him. The black magician was hiding in the darkest place under the bridge.

[Ice Arrow]

A pointed ice stake appeared in front of me and was shot into the darkness. The black magician hurriedly jumped and avoided the Ice Arrow. The black magician, who landed on the ground, grinned.

,Your magic skills are pretty good.'

,Don't smile so creepily.'

It was the moment I was about to use the next magic.

Jjik, jjik jjik!

I heard the sound of a rat. When I looked down, I saw a rat rushing towards me. My face hardened. It was because a magic circle was drawn on the rat's body.

My head instantly figured out what the magic circle was. I understood quickly because it was a magic formula I had seen a few hours ago.

,A living bomb...!'

I accelerated my consciousness.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

I formed the formula. The accelerated consciousness returned to normal. I activated the three magic formulas I had prepared in a row.

[Barrier]

[Barrier]

[Barrier]

The rat exploded. One of the barriers was shattered by the explosion.

,The casting speed is ridiculously fast. Do you have an artifact that helps with casting?'

,This is purely my ability.'

,You're saying something that even a passing dog wouldn't believe. If you don't want to talk, fine. I'll kill you and take it myself.'

Jjik! Jjikjjikjjik! Jjikjjik!

Rats appeared from all directions. Each one was cursed with black magic to be a living bomb.

,I hope your artifact is sturdy enough to withstand the explosion.'

Even at a glance, more than 10 rats were running towards me.

[Air Strike]

I used Air Strike on the ground. One of the barriers surrounding me was destroyed. The shockwave spread in all directions, blowing away the rushing rats in an instant.

I bought some time. I reloaded the magazine I took out of my pocket into the pistol. I fired the pistol at the rats that were rushing at me again.

Kwang! Peoeong! Kwang kwang!

The rats hit by the bullets exploded. The scrap metal and old furniture scattered around were swept away by the explosion and floated into the air.

,One shot, one kill... You must have been a great marksman. But what a pity. I have as many bombs as I want.'

I don't know where they're coming from, but rats keep popping out. And finally, the bullets ran out. I threw away the pistol and used the magic I had prepared.

[Reverse Gravity]

Gravity is reversed.

Scraps of metal and pieces of wood floated into the air around me. There were over thousands of them.

,With the feeling of grabbing the entire space...!'

[Psychokinesis]

The pieces floating in the air stopped all at once. It was a fantastic sight as if time had stopped. But there was no time to enjoy the sight. I gritted my teeth and squeezed out all the remaining mana and concentration.

Thousands of pieces poured towards the black magician.

,Psychokinesis?! You crazy...!'

The black magician tried to run away, but it was already too late.

Thousands of pieces tore apart the barrier protecting the black magician and pierced deeply into his body. The black magician couldn't even scream and died.

The rats that were running towards me stopped abruptly. They looked around as if trying to figure out the situation, and then scattered in all directions.
