Seeing Seong Jigone, who had awakened with rage, I flinched. The presence and energy I felt from Seong Jigone's *gigan* were immense. If I were to fight the current Seong Jigone, I couldn't guarantee victory.

Ttang.

The bell rang.

The villagers, gathered in one place, flinched. To them, the sound of the bell was like a signal that the Jiangshi were appearing. Even if this situation is resolved well, they'll tremble at the sound of the bell.

"They're coming!"

Baek Mu-han said in a tense tone.

Kung, kung, kung!

Jiangshi appeared from all directions. The Jiangshi jumping from the front were the least of the problems. There were those jumping from the roofs, those rising from the ground like moles, and those suddenly falling from the sky.

Ttang. Ttang. Ttang.

Every time the bell rang, more Jiangshi appeared.

"I can't forgive this...!"

Seong Jigone moved. He swung his golden sword at the Jiangshi. Golden *geomgi* drew an arc and flew towards the Jiangshi. The Jiangshi couldn't even react properly and were helplessly defeated.

He didn't stop there. He slightly bent his knees and leaped towards the Jiangshi with an explosive jump, swinging his sword. The Jiangshi couldn't block the sword imbued with the light of twilight. The Jiangshi, harder than steel, were cut as softly as pudding.

Even while dealing with the Jiangshi, I kept an eye on Seong Jigone. It had been a long time since I saw Seong Jigone fighting with all his heart. I was also curious about the Guwolyeong Geombeop (Nine Moon Spirit Sword Method) that Seong Jigone had mastered.

'It's definitely a high-level sword technique.'

Sharp when it needs to be sharp, fast when it needs to be fast, and heavy when it needs to be heavy. The mysteries of rising martial arts were contained in the sword he swung casually. I could see it.

'Guwol Seonja taught Seong Jigone properly. But that's just swinging. It's not Guwolyeong Geombeop.'

According to Seong Jigone, Guwolyeong Geombeop is a sword technique consisting of nine forms (*sik*). Each form is said to have tremendous power.

Ttang. Ttang. Ttang.

The Jiangshi sorcerers, unable to stand by and watch Seong Jigone's performance, moved themselves. Every time they moved, the bell rang.

"Ugh."

Baek Mu-han suddenly groaned.

It's because of the bell sound. There was power in the sound. I don't know if it's a type of sorcery, but the bell sound was stimulating the semicircular canals and trying to shake the brain.

The Jiangshi sorcerers surrounded Seong Jigone. They held out the bells in their hands and shook them. They chanted evil spells with their mouths.

The space where Seong Jigone was located fluctuated. The laws twisted, and something from another dimension was about to appear.

"You crazy bastards!"

Baek Mu-han shouted. Unlike me, who didn't know what was happening, he understood what the Jiangshi sorcerers were doing. I grabbed Baek Mu-han's shoulder as he was about to rush out.

"What are those bastards trying to do?"

"It's the Netherworld! They're trying to summon beings from the Netherworld! We have to kill those Jiangshi sorcerers right now!"

Baek Mu-han said urgently. The Reforming the Wicked system probably told him. The Reforming the Wicked system is deeply related to the Netherworld.

"What. Is that all it was."

"It's not just that! The beings of the Netherworld are powerful! The moment even a Rakshasa of the Netherworld is summoned... the Middle World will fall into great chaos!"

"It's like you've actually seen a being from the Netherworld."

"That's...."

"You're overestimating the Netherworld too much. If the beings of the Netherworld were that great, they would have already dominated this world. And you're underestimating Jigone. Do you think Jigone will just stand still until a being from the Netherworld is summoned?"

As soon as I finished speaking, Seong Jigone swung his sword. The drawn golden *geomgi* didn't disappear immediately. It stayed around Seong Jigone, dancing as if it were alive. Every time he swung his sword, the dancing *geomgi* continued to increase.

The golden *geomgi* danced splendidly. The Jiangshi sorcerers around him were cut by the *geomgi*, bleeding and falling. The *geomgi* still danced.

"What the hell is that...."

Baek Mu-han stared at Seong Jigone with astonished eyes. He seemed very surprised to witness a martial art that was completely different from the martial arts he knew.

I didn't explain. To be honest, I don't know much about Guwolyeong Geombeop either. Even if I did know, there's no reason to explain it to Baek Mu-han.

"There are still Jiangshi and Jiangshi sorcerers left. Move."

"...Yes!"

Baek Mu-han and I moved like the wind. I took charge of the Jiangshi, and Baek Mu-han relentlessly targeted the Jiangshi sorcerers. Seong Jigone, still not relieved of his anger, rampaged like crazy, killing every enemy in sight.

The battle ended.

The Jiangshi were torn to pieces and fell to the ground, and only three Jiangshi sorcerers were still alive. Baek Mu-han had captured them alive.

Seong Jigone collapsed. There were no serious injuries to his body. It was just caused by overexertion. He'll return to normal after a few days of rest.

"......."

The Jiangshi sorcerers were silent.

They didn't say anything even when I spoke to them. I said to Baek Mu-han.

"I'll leave the disposal of these bastards to you. I don't care if you kill them to avenge your grandmother or torture them to get information."

"These guys are soldiers. There's someone who ordered them to do this. I want to know who's behind them."

"Is that so? So you've decided to extract information."

"...I can't torture them. *Daehyeop*. Please torture these guys and get the information."

Baek Mu-han gritted his teeth as if enduring pain. The Reforming the Wicked system must be sanctioning him.

The Reforming the Wicked system allows killing villains, but it doesn't allow torturing them. Commissioning torture is the same. Of course, the sanctions are weaker than torturing them directly.

"Since I decided to help, I'll help to the end."

"Thank you, *Daehyeop*!"

"It's not free. Repay this debt too."

"I will definitely repay it!"

I looked at the restrained Jiangshi sorcerers.

'I wonder how long these guys can endure *Bungeun Chakgol*.'

Before touching the *pressure points*, I put my hand in my pocket. I took out a talisman from the space pocket. The talisman written by Milyeong shone and swept through the Jiangshi sorcerers. A dark aura escaped from the bodies of the Jiangshi sorcerers. The Jiangshi sorcerers, who had been silent, were flustered.

"What is this...!"

"They forcibly dispelled the sorcery?"

"It's a high-level sorcery. How does that bastard have such a talisman...."

The Jiangshi sorcerers stirred.

"Did you think I wouldn't know your tricks? Guys who do shady things like this prepare for suicide in case things go wrong. It's famous that assassins hide poison in their mouths. What about sorcerers? They'll prepare for suicide with sorcery."

The expressions of the Jiangshi sorcerers turned dark. They had lost their means of suicide, as they had been searching for a way to escape until the very last moment. They had to activate the sorcery and commit suicide the moment they were subdued.

'It's not easy to cut off the attachment to life so easily. Especially for guys who have a lot to lose.'

These guys are at least sorcerers above the *Chulji* level. They must have gone through all sorts of hardships to reach that level, so there's no way they could easily give it up.

"It's *Bungeun Chakgol*. I wonder how long you can endure it."

I touch the *pressure points*.

The pain of the tendons and bones twisting covered the Jiangshi sorcerers. At first, they endured it. Gritting their teeth so hard that their gums burst.

But as time passed, their composure disappeared.

First, their mouths opened. Even if they had to scream, it would alleviate the pain even a little.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaak!"

The screams of the Jiangshi sorcerers echoed.

The bastards couldn't even last half an hour before opening their mouths.

"We are the Heosisa Squad of the Four Kings Alliance. We are an unofficial unit of the Four Kings Alliance, and we only move under the orders of the Alliance Lord."

"The Four Kings Alliance Lord sent you guys to our village? Why?"

Baek Mu-han asked.

"The Alliance Lord wanted the secret art of the Bloody Demon. The secret art was engraved on the Bloody Demon's body, so we were going to turn the Bloody Demon's corpse into a Jiangshi and take it...."

"If you found the corpse, you could have just taken it. Why did you attack our village?"

"The Alliance Lord didn't want this to be known. The villagers knew the Bloody Demon. We decided to erase this village."

"You bastards!"

Baek Mu-han shouted and swung his sword. The heads of the Jiangshi sorcerers fell. Baek Mu-han looked down at the corpses of the Jiangshi sorcerers.

"Are you satisfied?"

"...Revenge must be thorough. And my revenge isn't over. These guys are just swords wielded by the enemy. Just because you break the enemy's sword doesn't mean the enemy dies. They'll prepare a new sword."

"The opponent is the Four Kings Alliance. You're going to take revenge on the Four Kings Alliance?"

"I can't live under the same sky as my enemy. I'll kill him no matter what it takes."

"That sounds like you're going to commit suicide."

"Of course, I'm not going to charge into the Four Kings Alliance like a wild boar right now. A gentleman's revenge is never too late, even after ten years. I'm going to avoid the Four Kings Alliance and build up my strength."

"What about the villagers? The Four Kings Alliance might attack again."

"I'm going to take them to a city managed by a prestigious orthodox sect. Even the Four Kings Alliance can't recklessly cause a disturbance in a city where a prestigious orthodox sect is established...."

Even the Four Kings Alliance would be burdened by touching a prestigious orthodox sect. If they recklessly touched them, the blade of the Murim Alliance could be directed at the Four Kings Alliance.

There could be a worst-case scenario where the Four Kings Alliance and the Cheonma Shin-gyo join hands.... But the Cheonma Shin-gyo wasn't friendly with the Four Kings Alliance. Rather, they antagonized each other. The *sapa* martial artists and the *magyo* people were similar yet different.

"I'm worried about you two."

"You're worried about us?"

"The Four Kings Alliance will chase after you two. You two are skilled, but the Four Kings Alliance is...."

"Don't worry. We're not weak enough to receive your worries. Jigone belongs to the military. And he's a fairly noticed military officer. The Four Kings Alliance can't recklessly touch him. You should worry about your own future more than us. Do you have a plan?"

"I'm going to wander as a *ronin* and build up my strength."

I nodded roughly. Baek Mu-han is the protagonist and a reincarnator. He'll do well on his own.

'I'll use Baek Mu-han to check and eliminate the Four Kings Alliance. If it's like the original, this guy will also deal with the forces behind the Four Kings Alliance.'

I just need to not become enemies with Baek Mu-han.

Later, there will come a time when Baek Mu-han gains enlightenment and abandons Cheonma Shingong. At that time, I'll approach Baek Mu-han and obtain Cheonma Shingong. That was my plan.

The next day. Seong Jigone woke up.

We started to go our separate ways. The villagers followed Baek Mu-han and left for the city, and Seong Jigone used a space movement scroll to return to the military in Sichuan.

I also became a lightning stone and headed to the Cheonma Shin-gyo.
