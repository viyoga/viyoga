One stamp left to pass the preliminaries.

I looked at Ju Seohyun beside me. As it happened, our eyes met—she'd already been watching me. Her eyes narrowed.

"What."

"Nothing. I was just thinking about what happened yesterday."

"You…!"

Ju Seohyun's brow furrowed.

Yesterday, in the maze, I'd passed by Ju Seohyun and smacked her ass. Honestly, that wasn't my fault. Her ass had been begging for it.

Should I call it lucky for both of us? Nobody knows about it. It was being broadcast live, and plenty of people were watching me—how could they not know? Because when I ran the maze, I was using Deception (SSS) to fool the traps.

Even if someone was watching me in real time through the system, they couldn't see through Deception. No—watching through a screen actually makes it harder to see through.

I used Deception. So that no one else could hear our conversation.

"There's an easy fast way and an annoying fast way. Which do you want?"

"…What scheme are you cooking up now?"

"Scheme? You've benefited from me plenty so far. I'm in a good mood, so I'm giving you a choice."

"You think I don't know you're about to pull something weird? Explain it in detail."

"The annoying fast way is the circus. Do a few tricks, kill three clowns, and you get the mission stamp."

Originally, I'd planned to just do the circus mission without asking. It was a bit ridiculous and annoying, but it was faster than the alternatives.

Unlike me, Ju Seohyun didn't seem to like the idea of killing clowns at all.

"…What's the easy fast way?"

"Just take one couple photo. The problem is that even my Deception can't completely fool the viewers."

Unfortunately, Deception (SSS) isn't an ability that can perfectly deceive a situation. Maybe if the viewer started watching from the middle. But fooling people who've been watching in real time from start to finish with Deception is impossible.

Elena's illusion magic might have managed it, but my Deception isn't illusion magic. It can fool someone regardless of their mental fortitude, but if the target stays vigilant and keeps watching, fooling them is difficult. At best, it's just deceiving sound like right now.

Change my appearance with Deception? Possible. But it's meaningless against viewers who are constantly watching me.

"…A couple photo. Why is a mission like that in a martial arts tournament?"

"It's an amusement park."

"…I don't care. If I can pass the preliminaries with just one photo, it's a gain. And you'd be in more trouble than me, wouldn't you? You're married to that Phantom Duke."

"The Phantom Duke is broad-minded. Elena already knows about our relationship. Did you forget Fovitz?"

“…….”

Ju Seohyun's face turned red.

In District 6,700, the Kingdom of Entertainment, we'd done plenty of lewd things while active as the girl group Fovitz.

"And noble society isn't as noble as you think. If it looks elegant, that's just because you haven't seen what's underneath. It's common for nobles to keep mistresses. As long as it's not adultery between nobles, people let it slide."

The difference in standing between noble men and noble women is unavoidable. In most worlds, men are physically stronger, so it's male-dominated. If a male noble has a love scandal, people say 'it happens' and move on, but for a female noble, it gets harsher.

Of course, that didn't apply to Elena, the Phantom Duke. She was too strong to be dismissed by male-chauvinist thinking.

"…If there's no problem, fine. I just want to get out of this place before I lose my mind."

Ju Seohyun raised her head and glared at the sky. People were falling without parachutes. For those with means to fly, it was nothing, but two out of ten of them would be eliminated by falling to their deaths.

*Not exactly a normal sight.*

Ju Seohyun and I headed to the couple photo spot.

A photo zone by the glittering carousel.

People were swarming in front of it.

The photo zone manager was a beautiful young man with blond hair and wings. He wore a white tunic in the Greek style. Holding an instant camera, he frowned at the man and woman standing before the photo zone.

"You two are not a couple. I don't feel the love. Get lost right now."

The man and woman protested.

"We ARE a couple!"

"That's right! If we kiss right here, will you acknowledge it?!"

"If you have sex, I'll acknowledge it."

The young man's voice was firm. The man and woman looked at each other. The man asked the woman slyly.

"Should we?"

"Are you insane?!"

Smack!

The woman slapped the man's cheek. She had quite the temper—she stormed off from the photo zone. The man shook his head and headed in another direction.

Most people scattered after watching that little farce. But some stayed and stood before the photo zone. They didn't want to miss a chance to get a stamp for free.

"You two aren't a couple either."

"Ah, you two are a bit ambiguous…. Well, fine."

"Two men. I'll take it. Have a beautiful love."

"You love the character drawn on that pillow. Reminds me of old times. Hm? You want me to turn that pillow into a human? I can't do that."

The young man who'd seemed so strict had a surprisingly broad definition of love. To the point where it felt like he'd approve just about anything.

Eventually, it was our turn. Ju Seohyun seemed a bit nervous—her steps were stiff.

We stood in the photo zone. We hadn't decided on a pose. I put my arm around Ju Seohyun's shoulder and whispered in her ear.

"Smile if you want to look like a couple. At least do a simple pose."

“…….”

Ju Seohyun forced a smile, trembling as she did. And like me, she made a V with her fingers.

The young man holding the instant camera looked at us.

"Hmm."

「Irresistible Love (僞) is gazing upon you.」

The young man looked back and forth between me and Ju Seohyun.

The true name of Irresistible Love is Eros. Also called Cupid, he is the god of love. He was also the son of Aphrodite, goddess of love and beauty.

*In the future I saw, this scene didn't exist. I don't know what judgment Eros will make.*

If he didn't give us the stamp, we'd have to go elsewhere. That was fine. We had plenty of time.

"Very well."

Eros raised the instant camera.

Click.

Whirr.

Two photos developed from the camera.

"Wait!"

The man who'd been rejected by Eros earlier jumped out. He pointed at us and started arguing with Eros.

"Anyone can see those two aren't a couple! Look at the woman! Her body's stiff! Anyone can tell they're faking being a couple!"

I felt the urge to kill the man.

"Do you know love better than I do, when I am the god of love?"

"That's not it, but… by common sense, those two aren't a couple!"

"Common sense. Love sometimes transcends common sense. Love cannot be judged by appearances alone. If there are men and women acting out love, there are also couples acting out hatred. Love takes many forms. There is love that denies itself, and love that has yet to be realized. As the god of love, I can distinguish love. I swear on my name—those two love each other."

The man's face twisted. It wasn't a face that accepted Eros's words.

The god of love.

Even if he was a minor deity, a god is a god. If the man denied it here, it would be disrespect doubting the god of love. Disrespect is an insult to a god.

"No!! There's no way I—there's no way I could love this bastard!"

Suddenly Ju Seohyun exploded. Her face flushed crimson as she shouted her denial.

The god of love didn't get angry. Instead, he smirked as if he'd seen something amusing.

"I see. You're right. But I've already made my judgment. Here, take your photo."

"We're not a couple!"

"Yes. Yes. Understood."

No matter how much Ju Seohyun protested, Eros was unbothered.

I grabbed Ju Seohyun's shoulder and pulled her along as I approached Eros. Before taking the photo from Eros, I punched the protesting man in the head.

Thunder Heaven Style—Lightning Wave Strike.

My fist, concentrating all the strength in my body, burst the man's head. The headless body vanished without a trace just before it hit the ground.

"Fucking trash. Who the hell do you think you are to run your mouth."

A few people nearby were horrified, but neither Ju Seohyun nor Eros paid any attention. Ju Seohyun was glaring at the photo she'd received from Eros, her face still red. It was a bit unexpected. She'd screamed so violently that I thought she'd crumple it the moment she got it.

I took my photo too. It hadn't even been edited, but it came out well.

「You have collected all 8 mission stamps.」

「You have passed the second preliminary round. You may move outside the preliminary grounds at any time.」

Ju Seohyun and I sat on a nearby bench. She'd been about to leave the preliminaries, but I brought her to the bench. I didn't see the photo in her hands. She hadn't thrown it away, so she must have put it in her pocket.

"…Is there something else left to do?"

"No. I just want to eat rabbit today. I'll come eat rabbit in 30 minutes."

「Moving outside the preliminary grounds.」

Once outside, I went to Ju Seohyun's room exactly 30 minutes later. Ju Seohyun was sitting on the bed in a bunny girl outfit. Her face was bright red.

"Heh heh. You prepared well? Looks like you really do love me, just like the god of love said."

"Sh-shut up! You're the one who ordered this…!"

I smiled and walked toward her.

The rabbit was delicious.
