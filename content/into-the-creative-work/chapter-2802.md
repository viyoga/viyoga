I entered the tent alone, which was situated in the center between the Cheonma Shin-gyo (Demonic Cult) camp and the Cheonui-maeng (Heaven's Will Alliance) camp.

The Cheonui-maeng Lord, Sosan Hyeon-geom (Vanishing Mysterious Sword), was sitting at the table with his unique black sword placed next to his chair. He had the appearance of a stern man in his forties. His thick, dark eyebrows conveyed his stubbornness.

I didn't greet him and pulled out a chair to sit across from him at the table.

He didn't scold me. He tilted a bottle of liquor on the table and filled a glass.

,Would you like a drink? It's not poisoned. Even if it were, there's a saying that So-Cheonma (Little Demonic Master) has achieved immunity to all poisons, so wouldn't it be alright?

『Using the light of Cheon-gang-seong (Heavenly River Star).』

,Aeng-hwa-ju (Cherry Blossom Wine)? It's been aged for 157 years. The ingredients are ordinary, but it contains spiritual energy. Was it aged in a place where spiritual veins flow?

I held out my glass to him. Liquor containing spiritual energy was excellent in taste by itself.

He poured liquor into the glass and said,

,Such a Demonic stubbornness.
