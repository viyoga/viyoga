Yuria searched Mount Lurut for two days straight without sleep.

She found every cave and special place on the mountain, even going inside to check. But the artifact Seong Yujin-ssi spoke of was nowhere to be found.

At this point, Yuria began to have doubts.

Of course, it wasn't that she didn't trust Seong Yujin-ssi. It was just that there was a possibility Seong Yujin-ssi was mistaken.

"......"

Yuria pondered. She might have missed the artifact when she saw it. She didn't even know what the artifact looked like.

'I did pick up a few things that looked unusual, though.'

They didn't feel like artifacts at all. She tried using mana and magic, but there was no reaction.

'If I drag this out too long, it'll mess up the plan, so I should get back to the original mission....'

Yuria paused. About 500 meters away, she saw a peculiar monster. It was a bear with white fur and black stripes. The bear was lumbering around with a dagger stuck in its forehead.

From the blade to the hilt, the entire dagger was blue, embedded in the center of the bear's head.

Yuria's gaze shifted to the bear's tail. It looked exactly like a raccoon's tail.

'...A raccoon bear?'

A bear with a raccoon's tail. A raccoon bear, a monster. It wasn't a very strong monster, so it didn't make sense for it to be here, deep inside Mount Lurut. Other monsters wouldn't have left the raccoon bear alone.

'A mutation, then.'

Yuria made a quick decision. There was no other explanation for its mutated appearance and why it was here if it wasn't a mutation.

And the dagger stuck in the raccoon bear's forehead was likely an artifact. The fact that the raccoon bear was moving around without a care, even with a dagger stuck in its vital forehead, was the evidence.

'Grim Scythe.'

She used shadow magic.

A giant scythe rose from the raccoon bear's shadow. The shadow scythe was silent and made no sound, so the raccoon bear didn't notice at all. The scythe swung, and the raccoon bear's head rolled across the dirt floor.

Grim Scythe was a shadow magic spell specialized in surprise attacks and assassinations.

Yuria used shadow teleportation to appear instantly in front of the raccoon bear's corpse. She pulled out the blue dagger stuck in the raccoon bear's forehead.

Three small golden gems were embedded in the dagger's grip. Looking at the grooves carved into the hilt, it was estimated that there were originally seven gems embedded in it.

"......"

Yuria quietly frowned as she looked at the dagger. After checking with magic, she realized that the dagger was imbued with a mysterious power.

But the problem was that she didn't know how to use it.

Knowing how bizarre the artifacts of this world could be, she didn't want to take a suspicious artifact to Seong Yujin-ssi. What if this artifact harmed Seong Yujin-ssi? Yuria would never be able to forgive herself.

'It would be better to figure out how to use it and what its effects are.'

She had a guess as to how to use it. Stabbing a target with the dagger. After all, a dagger was meant to harm someone.

Yuria moved. There were plenty of monsters in Mount Lurut to be guinea pigs.

She didn't have any trouble finding a monster.

A bipedal, muscular cow.

A minotaur.

Yuria lightly threw the dagger. But the result was anything but light. The dagger pierced the minotaur's chest at near-supersonic speed.

The minotaur stumbled backward and spotted Yuria. The monster, which was about to charge at Yuria with a roar, stopped in its tracks.

"......"

This was clearly an anomaly. Yuria watched the minotaur with her arms crossed.

An hour passed in that state. Nothing changed. The minotaur was still frozen like a stone.

Another three hours passed. The minotaur began to change. Its brown fur turned red, and its size increased. It had become a mutated minotaur.

'...An artifact that turns monsters into mutations?'

Yuria moved. She activated Grim Scythe, which had instantly killed the raccoon bear. A giant shadow scythe rose from behind the minotaur.

The minotaur reacted. It turned its body and reached out to grab and shatter the shadow scythe.

"Kuaaaaaaaa!"

The minotaur roared. It grabbed a rock over 1 meter in size nearby and threw it at Yuria.

Yuria dodged the rock to the side and charged at the minotaur. The minotaur also charged without backing down. The minotaur's black claws glinted in the sunlight.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Amjeon (暗轉).

The area around Yuria and the minotaur was plunged into complete darkness. Yuria, the caster, saw through the darkness. On the other hand, the minotaur was flustered by the sudden darkness and looked around. Yuria didn't miss the opportunity.

Yuria swung the dagger. An aura blade flew out and split the minotaur in half, killing it. She used magic to retrieve the blue dagger stuck in the minotaur's chest.

The darkness disappeared.

'One of the three gems is broken.'

She guessed that the gems embedded in the hilt represented the number of uses. In other words, the remaining number of uses for this dagger artifact was two. She would have to use it carefully.

Yuria regretted it a little. She could have obtained more accurate experimental data if she had used it on a human instead of a minotaur.

But her regret was short-lived. She soon took out a walkie-talkie from her pocket.

"The personal mission is now complete. Start luring the monsters as planned."
