Hanahoe was formed.

Rowoon, the student council president and Chairman of Hanahoe, spoke to the students gathered outside.

,"Hanahoe has been formed to respond to the admission of commoners. Future activities will be decided through meetings. You guys, for now, disband and return to the dormitory."

,"We trust you, Chairman-nim!"

,"There's nothing we can't do if we stick together."

,"Commoner admission is absolutely not allowed! The Imperial Academy is for nobles! It has been, and it must continue to be!"

The gathered students disbanded. Rowoon looked at the Hanahoe members.

,"Let's continue with our future course of action. Shouldn't we first express our opinions to the academy?"

Everyone nodded at Rowoon's words.

Then, a student named Jin Corona stepped forward.

,"Chairman-nim. It's not something to be taken lightly. The academy might only verbally reject commoner admission and then accept commoners next year. Moreover, Chairman-nim, you'll be graduating next year. What will those who remain do if the academy changes its tune after you graduate? There must be a definite guarantee that commoner admission is prohibited."

,"That's not wrong. We should aim to have it specified in writing first. To do that, we'll need a plan. Let's continue the meeting. It's almost dinner time... I know a sandwich shop that's really good, so we can continue the meeting while eating. I'll pay."

,"Thank you, Chairman-nim!"

Even among nobles, not everyone was rich. Even if their family had a lot of money, it belonged to their parents, not to them. If they had a lot of money, they wouldn't have entered the Imperial Academy but would have hired private tutors.

Besides, the cost of maintaining dignity wasn't cheap. Someone's giving out free food? The noble children naturally welcomed it.

Hanahoe began to devise a plan while eating ham sandwiches at the sandwich shop.

Rowoon, as the student council president, wanted the situation to escalate since it had come to this. That way, his reputation could spread throughout the empire.

,'If I'm going to do it, I have to do it right. There's nothing to fear. All the academy students support me.'

The meeting started, but it didn't go well. They were still students without much social experience. For most of them, it was their first time even having a meeting.

Rowoon felt a sense of satisfaction as he led the meeting as Chairman. He was doing something. Everyone was listening to him. He was their leader. The sweet taste of power pleased him.

Whenever the meeting stalled, Jin Corona would appropriately step in and guide the direction of the meeting.

,"If the academy refuses, we must protest. We must express our opinions strongly."

,"A protest. Sounds unfamiliar. Is it effective?"

,"Of course, it's effective. Some students are already preparing for a protest."

,"...They're already preparing for a protest without us even telling them to?"

,"That's the atmosphere. Doesn't that mean the students are taking it seriously? We, as student representatives, only need to designate the direction of the protest."

,"I see. I've heard of protests before. Was it in the Entertainment Kingdom area? I heard there were several protests there. We can refer to that. Any good ideas?"

Representatives from the Knight Department, Magic Department, and Administration Department spoke with sparkling eyes.

,"Class boycott! If all the students refuse to attend the academy's classes, the academy will take the matter more seriously!"

A student from the Knight Department said. Class boycott. It sounded good, but in reality, they simply didn't want to attend classes. Exams were coming up soon, and they weren't well-prepared. They thought that if everyone boycotted classes, the exams might just be skipped, so they blurted out that opinion.

,"We must also control the laboratories. That way, the professors will listen to us, right?"

A student from the Magic Department said. They wanted to get out of the lab and take a break. Even if they weren't affiliated with the lab, they wanted to screw over a professor they didn't like.

,"First, we need to occupy the main building and find corruption. If we can find corruption... the academy will have no choice but to accept our opinions."

A student from the Administration Department said. They wanted to gain credit for uncovering administrative corruption. They had some confidence in themselves.

The students ate ham sandwiches and poured out their opinions.

,"Should we gather the students in front of the main building and hold a protest?"

,"Hold a rally in this weather? It's freezing, even if it's not snowing. What if the students catch a cold?"

,"We're nobles. We must protest in an elegant way. Ah, how about protesting by leaving our academy uniform jackets? Uniforms are the symbol that represents us, aren't they?"

,"Oh. That's a good idea."

,"There will be uncooperative students. What will we do with them? Student unity is important for this."

,"Our Knight Department will take care of it. If we go and talk to them nicely, won't they understand and cooperate?"

The meeting became more and more lively as it progressed. The students laughed heartily. The sandwich shop owner pretended not to hear and kept selling sandwiches.

,"But shouldn't we avoid tomorrow? Isn't there a government official briefing tomorrow?"

It was a day when government officials working in the field came to promote and explain their workplaces and families.

,"So, we should definitely proceed tomorrow. Won't our work be known to the outside world thanks to them? Above all, they are also nobles. So, won't they cooperate with us?"

,"I guess so."

The outcome of the meeting was already decided.

They firmly believed that the plan would succeed. Since it was representing thousands of students, they didn't think for a moment that they would fail.

Jin Corona kept his mouth shut from the middle and erased his presence. No one there paid attention to Jin Corona. It was as if he wasn't there.

The next day. The students of the Imperial Academy gathered at the school gate from the morning. At the forefront was student council president Rowoon. Behind him, a large number of students wearing masks appeared. Some of them were holding large flags.

(We will perish rather than open up.)

(Oppose commoner admission!)

(The Imperial Academy is for nobles!)

(Protect the tradition of the Imperial Academy!)

The guards, made up of retired knights, swallowed hard at the unusual atmosphere. If they were outsiders, they would have drawn their swords and stopped them. However, the opponents were not outsiders but students. It was impossible to point a sword at the people they had to protect.

The captain of the guard spoke to student council president Rowoon to find out the situation.

,"What's going on all of a sudden?"

,"It has nothing to do with you. We have demands to make to the academy. We're going to meet the president. This is an internal matter of the school. You just need to do your job."

,"......"

The captain of the guard stepped back with a troubled expression. As a guard, he had no justification to stop the students.

The students confidently stormed into the academy's main building. However, the president, vice president, dean of academic affairs, and other important figures were not there. The only people left were staff members without authority.

,"We've come to meet with the president. Where is he?"

,"The president has been summoned by Duke Valdert-gakha due to the government official briefing issue! First of all, students, please calm down! The president will be here in the afternoon!"

,"Ha, then you can do it instead. Our request is simple. Reject commoner admission. Guarantee it on behalf of the academy."

,"M-me? I don't have the authority to do that...! And commoner admission... as far as I know, it's not even an issue that's been discussed at the academy! There seems to be a misunderstanding, so let's talk calmly...."

,"You're not listening. No choice then. We'll occupy the main building until the president arrives. All staff members, please leave."

,"Y-yes?"

As the staff members panicked and didn't know what to do, the students shouted in unison.

,"The Imperial Academy must withdraw commoner admission!!"

,"Withdraw it!!!"
