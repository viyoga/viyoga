The day of the exam arrived.

Yuria's group 1 and Lydia's group 5 were to take the exam together.

First, group 5 would act as nobles, and group 1 would host a banquet as servants.

The given preparation time was one hour. Within that time, they had to clean the banquet hall, set the tables, and even serve the food. The most challenging part was, of course, the cooking.

Originally, maids would divide their roles. Cleaning maids would clean, and cooking maids would cook. That's because it's efficient. But now, because of the evaluation, they had to do the work of ten people.

,Group 1. Go up on stage and start preparing for the banquet party. You have exactly one hour to prepare."

There were five evaluation instructors and dozens of other classmates watching. Many eyes were on them. The group members were stiff with tension from the unexpected attention.

Clap clap!

Yuria clapped her hands lightly. All eyes turned to her.

,Let's get started. First, let's clean and decorate the dining hall. We'll finish within 20 minutes, and then focus on cooking for the remaining 40 minutes."

Following Yuria's instructions, they decided to move in an orderly fashion. Yuria, holding a vacuum cleaner, discovered stains on the white tablecloth and various spots on the floor. This was a prepared stage. If there were stains, they were also part of the preparation.

,Juliet. Please bring some sodium percarbonate. Rachel. Please use magic to create hot water."

They cleaned meticulously for 10 minutes. Items that couldn't be cleaned in a short amount of time were moved behind the stage.

,The concept is spring."

Decorating the dining hall was completed even faster than cleaning. It was just a matter of bringing out and displaying the things they had prepared in advance.

With the remaining 40 minutes, they headed to the kitchen right next door. Since they had already decided on their roles, they each proceeded with their assigned dishes. The most difficult task fell to Yuria. There were many things to keep an eye on in the kitchen. If even one person made a mistake, she had to intervene immediately. Of course, Yuria also had to cook. She was in charge of the main dish, steak.

,Preparation time is over. Group 5. Please enter the banquet hall."

The instructor said. They entered the dining hall wearing dresses typically worn by nobles, not maid uniforms. Group 1, the maids, had to serve them.

The five instructors watched them with hawk eyes, sharing their opinions.

,Juliet-yang is a bit clumsy. Didn't she almost spill the steak sauce earlier?"

,She seems nervous. But she hasn't made any major mistakes and is doing her part."

,Lydia-yang is definitely a noble. Her actions exude elegance and grace."

,Tine-yang is disappointing. Even wearing noble clothes, she doesn't feel like a noble. It's natural for a maid to have dignity... Hmm."

,Well, given her background. She'll get better once she gets used to it."

Then, the instructors turned their attention to Yuria, who was attending to Lydia.

,Her voice, tone, and posture. Perfect."

,She has a perfect understanding of etiquette. The details of bowing and raising her fingers... Above all, lowering her gaze slightly is perfect. It feels like she's completely serving the guest."

,She also seemed experienced when leading the maids. She reminds me of the Head maid from where I worked before coming to the academy."

,It's not that she doesn't have flaws. She's too noticeable."

,It's definitely a problem that she's more noticeable than the nobles."

,Even if we overlook her natural beauty, her presence overwhelms the nobles...."

The food was served to the instructors. Dishes made by the maids themselves. The instructors only took one bite each. They had to evaluate the other students as well, so they had to eat as little as possible. They took a bite of the steak and their eyes widened.

,My god. How can a steak be so perfect...."

,The doneness and sauce are perfect. It's one of the best steaks I've ever had."

,Yuria-yang was in charge of the steak, right? I've had the royal chef's steak before... and it's comparable to that."

The instructors stared at Yuria with astonished eyes.

The only flaw they could find was that she was too perfect. Other than that, they couldn't find any faults even if they wanted to. Frankly, even the instructors here were a bit inferior compared to Yuria. Especially Anna, the cooking instructor, whose face turned serious as soon as she tasted the steak.

,T-They want me to evaluate this? Do I even have the right...?"

Still, they did the evaluation. The score was, of course, perfect.

Next was the ballroom dance evaluation. There was nothing to criticize in the ballroom dance either. Both the guest service and the serving were excellent.

,Now, let's switch roles. Group 5 will be the servants, and Group 1 will be the nobles."

The roles were switched.

The instructors were as excited about Group 5 as they had been about Group 1. More precisely, they were excited about Lydia in Group 5. Lydia was also a talent recognized by the instructors.

,Her leadership is impeccable."

,Yes. However, she's overbearing. It's like an employer using employees, not fellow servants."

Everyone nodded in agreement.

,However, I like her work. I also like the Christmas concept."

,Yes. It feels warm. The cleaning was also thorough. Perhaps because she herself is a magician, she actively uses magic. Good. After all, it depends on how you use any power."

But the food that followed was disappointing.

,Lydia-yang was also in charge of the main dish, steak."

,Hmm. It's excellent. It's excellent, but...."

,The steak that Yuria-yang made earlier was just too perfect."

They only took one bite of the steak. The instructors headed to Yuria, who was on stage, dressed as a noble. Yuria, wearing a noble dress, was elegantly dining. Although she was young, she somehow felt seasoned. She felt more like a noble lady than a noble young lady.

,...Her etiquette is also perfect. She never makes a mistake in the order of using the cutlery."

,When our eyes met earlier, I almost bowed my head. For a moment, I thought she was a noble lady...."

,Wait. What's going on over there?"

Tine was attending to Yuria. Tine, feigning a mistake, spilled wine on Yuria's white dress. She seemed to be acting in her own way, but she couldn't deceive the eyes of the instructors who had gone through thick and thin.

,Let's watch for now. The exam isn't over yet. I want to see how Yuria-yang handles it."

One of the instructors said.

• • •

The wine spilled.

&gt;

Yuria's dress became stained as if it had absorbed blood. From her side to the hem of her skirt.

,I-I'm sorry!"

Tine immediately bowed her head. At the same time, she grinned so that Yuria could see it. As if saying that this suited her better.

It was ridiculous. To cause such a thing during the exam to embarrass her? She was too short-sighted. And she was looking down on the instructors too much.

,The instructors aren't stepping in.'

A maid making a mistake. It was something that could happen. As such, this would also be included in the evaluation.

So, what should I do?

,I should be faithful to my role. I'm a noble now.'

What would it be like in the [Baekhwan] world?

Her master, Eugene, would have brushed it off. He was lenient with beautiful maids. He would laugh it off on the spot, even taking off his clothes at this level of mistake.

But other nobles were different. It wouldn't be strange to slap her on the spot and kick her out.

,Even if there's a class society here, it's not strict. I can't treat her carelessly.'

But I didn't intend to just let it go. Because I was a noble.

,How unpleasant."

Yuria opened her mouth. The already bad atmosphere instantly cooled down with her cold voice.

,I-I'm sorry!"

,Don't just say you're sorry, you have to take responsibility. Your actions show the level of your master. I'm really disappointed."

Yuria got up from her chair and tried to leave the place as it was. That way, Tine would get the evaluation of being a maid who only made mistakes and couldn't fix them. Yuria thought that Tine would be expelled with this evaluation.

,I'm sorry, Yuria-yeongae-nim."

Lydia blocked Yuria's path. She bowed and offered a sincere apology. It was a special situation, but the real young lady of a count family bowed her head to a commoner.

,Please follow me for now. I'll prepare a change of clothes for you. You can't go back in that state, can you? Also, our master wants to greet the young lady in person. Please give us a chance to make up for the maid's mistake."

,...Alright."

Lydia had already taken control of the atmosphere. It would only be seen as abuse of power to insist here. Yuria followed Lydia behind the stage.

,I'm sorry."

As soon as they came backstage, Lydia apologized again. Bowing her head slightly.

,Our group member caused this because of her jealousy towards you."

,...You seem to be very close to her. Enough for you to bow your head like this."

,That's not it. I haven't even talked to her much."

,Then why is Lydia-yang apologizing instead?"

,Because I'm the group leader. It's natural for the group leader to take responsibility for the group member's mistake."

,......."

,There's still the ballroom dance evaluation left. The evaluation won't be good with that dress, so I'll lend you my dress."

,No, it's okay."

,You don't have to feel burdened."

,It's not that, it's just that I don't think it'll fit me."

Yuria looked at Lydia's chest. At best, a B cup. On the other hand, Yuria's chest was voluptuous. The weight of one side of her chest alone was about 2kg.

Lydia looked at Yuria's chest for a moment, frowned, and then sighed.

,...You're not going to go on the ballroom dance stage wearing that dirty dress, are you?"

,It's okay because I have a maid uniform. For a maid, a maid uniform is both a uniform and a dress uniform."

Yuria passed by Lydia.

Then, to the ballroom. Yuria really stood on the stage wearing a maid uniform. Juliet, wearing a pink dress, approached Yuria.

,Yuria-yang. Would you like to dance a song with me?"

,With pleasure."

The instructors nodded at Yuria.

,A noble in the ballroom wearing a maid uniform is grounds for deduction."

,It can't be helped. The only dress she had was dirtied.... But doesn't it look good on her since it's an outing dress?"

,It reminds me of the old days. I used to dance with a gentleman in a corner of the ballroom... with the faint music playing in the background."

,Yuria-yang was perfect again today. There was a mistake in the middle, but it wasn't a mistake caused by Yuria-yang."

,Tine-yang is very disappointing."

,If it was really a mistake, it would be one thing. Tine-yang disgustingly feigned a mistake."

,She didn't even clean up properly."

,You all seem to have evaluated everything. Hmm. Is Tine-yang failing?"

,She barely avoided failing. Except for that mistake, everything else was excellent."

,I don't think she'll last long. If she makes even one more mistake in the future, she'll be expelled immediately."
