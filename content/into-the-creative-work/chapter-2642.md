Yuria retrieved the alchemy blood from Jonas's corpse.

It wasn't for nothing that she butchered him to death. She planned to siphon off about half of the alchemy blood and offer it to Eugene. The remaining half would be left on the corpse. This was a kind of evaluation, so she couldn't openly steal it.

,Alfatier is interested in alchemy blood to the point that they're dispatching corporate agents to Neo London. If I offer it to Master-nim, he will surely be pleased.'

Yuria went outside the building. As she stood still at the building entrance, a maid and a butler appeared from the alleyway. A pair, a man and a woman. They were the eyes and ears dispatched by Maria, the headmaster of the Maid Academy.

,You've worked hard, Yuria-yang.'

,Did you take care of Jonas?'

Yuria nodded in response to their questions.

,Yes. The body is inside. The targets he was trying to trade with were Alfatier corporate agents. I took care of them as well.'

The butler let out a small sigh.

,It was a matter involving a giant corporation. However, we won't be able to pressure them with this. If they're corporate agents, they would have cleaned up the aftermath perfectly. Even if we investigate the body, there's a high chance that another fake identity will pop up.'

The butler seemed to have dealt with corporate agents a lot. Yuria didn't say anything, but she agreed. There was no way a giant corporation wouldn't have prepared for the possibility of failure. Easily catching some low-level grunt wouldn't inflict any damage on a giant corporation.

The maid hesitated for a moment before asking.

,Yuria-yang. I just saw darkness descending around this building. Is that perhaps Yuria-yang's unique ability? Ah, it's a personal question, so you don't have to answer.'

Did she not know that personal questions themselves were poison? Or was she asking with an intention? In any case, Maria Hilton would surely be disappointed in this maid if she knew this fact.

Yuria answered plainly.

,Yes. It's my unique ability. I don't think I can give a detailed explanation.'

Yuria used her unique ability partly to deceive their eyes. Though they wouldn't think it was simply for the purpose of obscuring their vision with darkness.

The butler, who had been watching with subtly curious eyes, nudged the maid's shoulder.

,Enough with the rudeness, let's go inside. Yuria-yang. Leave the cleanup to us and return to the academy. Maria-nim will be waiting.'

,Understood. I will return now.'

Yuria walked forward calmly. The voices of the maid and butler chattering behind her were audible to the ears of a Rank 7 transcendent.

,To think you'd ask such a question there. Should I say it's just like you?'

,I was curious. It's the first time I've seen the unique ability of a Rank 7 transcendent that I've only heard about. Don't make me the only bad guy when you were just watching from the side.'

,Well, I understand. Rank 7s don't use their unique abilities often unless it's a critical moment. Still, be careful with your words. Unique abilities are related to the mindscape, so most Rank 7s don't like others commenting on their unique abilities. It's fortunate that Yuria-yang has a good personality. If it were another Rank 7, you would have had to offer an arm?'

,Yuria-yang is famous for having a good personality. I thought nothing would happen. But Yuria-yang's unique ability is a bit... different from her personality. That darkness... it's excessively dark.'

,Yuria-yang's ability is shadow manipulation, so... it must be influenced by that. Besides, judging a unique ability by its appearance alone is foolish.'

,Ew. Jonas? The corpse is a mess.'

,The corporate agents' corpses are clean. Did she fail to subdue Jonas in one go?'

,If you look closely at the corporate agents' bodies, there are wounds. Unlike their seemingly intact appearance, the inside is a mess. Blood vessels, muscles, internal organs. All neatly twisted. She extracted information through torture. But what kind of monstrous skill is this....'

,Even a professional torturer wouldn't be this good.... She's a scary woman. Even if she looks normal, is her mind messed up? They say most Rank 7s aren't normal... maybe that's true.'

,More importantly, let's take care of Jonas's corpse. We need to extract some alchemy blood and hand it over to Maria-nim.'

,...Does Maria-nim also embezzle corporate assets when opportunities like this arise? I thought Maria-nim was on the righteous side.'

,Ha. You don't know because you haven't done this job many times? Maria-nim's justice is strictly for her master. Do you know what Maria-nim's nickname was back in the day?'

,...The Queen's Hound. I forgot for a moment because Maria-nim doesn't do much external activity these days. But doesn't she not have a master she serves anymore?'

,Officially, that's the case.'

,Are you saying she has one unofficially?'

,I don't know the details. But I know she has connections with the royal family. Maybe she's still the Queen's Hound. Hey, this conversation is a secret. Got it?'

,That's what I want to say. Your mouth is looser than mine. If this story leaks out somewhere, it's not just your reputation that will be fucked. So keep your mouth shut.'

Yuria stopped eavesdropping on their conversation. Unlike what she thought might be something, it was just ordinary gossip.

• • •

Maria welcomed Yuria, who had returned to the academy.

,You've worked hard, Yuria-yang. Yuria-yang's work was perfect. Swift, quiet, and certain.'

,I'm glad you were pleased with my work. May I hear the evaluation score?'

,A+. Perfect score.'

No evaluation form was given. In the first place, it wasn't a job that could be proudly documented.

,Then, may I go back and rest now? It was a busy outing after a long time, so I'm a little tired.'

,Yuria-yang. The 94th class will be graduating soon. Servants aiming for the Prime certification need to prove themselves for several more years, but Yuria-yang has proven her talent and strength by rising to Rank 7. After graduation, you will be working at the academy and leading a rather difficult daily life.'

,Yes. It's something I've been prepared for.'

,If you continue to receive the best evaluations like now... in half a year, you will be able to start the final exam. You will work as a direct maid of the royal family, more precisely, Her Majesty the Queen, for 100 days. Her Majesty the Queen will decide whether you pass or not.'

It meant it depended on the Queen's discretion.

The Queen could fail her if she didn't like her. Of course, that wouldn't happen. The current Queen, Victoria VI, was an existence without equal in fairness. She wouldn't make a foolish choice that would damage her own authority.

Even if the Queen opposed her for some reason, all she had to do was achieve accomplishments that couldn't be denied.

,Yes. I will continue to strive until that day comes.'

,I would like Yuria-yang to be the representative of the graduating class at this graduation ceremony. It's a formality, so there won't be much to do. And Yuria-yang will be receiving the Superior certification at the graduation ceremony, so please be aware of that.'

,...I didn't know I would be receiving the Superior certification at the graduation ceremony. Hasn't this never happened before in history?'

,Yes. Even Florence, the only Prime-level maid currently, graduated from the academy as a Flower-level. The Superior-level is the first in history, so you can be proud. All the instructors, including myself, have acknowledged you.'

,I'm more pleased by the fact that I've been acknowledged than anything else. Thank you.'

Yuria smiled and bowed her head.

Of course, she wasn't genuinely happy. It wasn't because she was used to the grandiose modifier of being the first in history. It's just that, for her, praise from anyone other than Master-nim meant nothing. That one insignificant word from Master-nim, ,Pretty,' made Yuria happier than 100 compliments and plaques.

,After the graduation ceremony, the Maid Academy will be closed for a month. You can consider it a vacation for Yuria-yang. Be sure to relieve the fatigue you've accumulated during that time.'

Yuria smiled sincerely this time.

,Yes. Headmaster-nim.'

• • •

Unlike the entrance ceremony, the graduation ceremony was held quietly in the morning.

The reason it wasn't held publicly like the entrance ceremony was because an enormous crowd would gather if it were held publicly. People from nobles and corporate personnel departments to reporters, soldiers, mercenaries, and civic groups gathered. The purpose was to scout the graduates of the Maid Academy. There had been problems due to this in the past, so from some point on, the graduation ceremony was held unofficially.

Isn't that a shame for the graduates whose goal is employment? Graduating from the Maid Academy meant being recognized as an elite. It meant being able to choose and get a job at a giant corporation in Neo London.

37 graduates of the Maid Academy's 94th class.

Their representative was Yuria.

The graduation ceremony itself wasn't much.

After listening to Headmaster Maria's address, Yuria gave a short speech as the representative of the graduating class. After that, they went up to the stage one by one, received their diplomas and maid certifications from Headmaster Maria, and pinned a badge proving their maid certification on their lapels.

Yuria pinned a rhombus badge with a cross drawn on it to her lapel. From today, she became a Superior-level maid, the fifth of the seven ranks of maids.

She exchanged greetings with Headmaster Maria and then said her final goodbyes to her classmates.

,It's a shame to part ways with Yuria-yang like this. But it's not the end forever, right? Yuria-yang. We're still friends in the future, right?'

A woman with slightly dark gray silver hair and red eyes said.

Lidia Metcalfe. The young lady of the Metcalfe County and Yuria's classmate who obtained the Triple certification this time.

,Of course, Linia-yang. I heard you'll be working at the royal family, Buckingham Palace at that? Lidia-yang has achieved her dream.'

,...I'm ashamed, but I received a little help from my family. Still, I plan to do my best since the royal family chose me. Yuria-yang. If you need my help, please feel free to contact me anytime.'

Yuria exchanged pleasantries with her classmates and parted ways amicably. Then, she boarded the carriage sent by Eugene with her classmate Juliet.

Juliet Bond. A Double-level maid who got a job at the Meyer Mansion this time.

She was a woman from outside Nero London, in the room next to Yuria's dormitory. She was quite lucky. If it weren't for Yuria, she definitely wouldn't have adapted to the academy and would have been kicked out.

,I'm going to be working at the Meyer County... Can I do well?'

Even a year ago, the name of the Meyer Count wasn't famous. But now, the Meyer Count was being touted as a rising force. He was immediately making the world buzz with superconductors, so there was no way he wouldn't be promising.

,It's okay, Juliet. I guarantee your skills.'

,If Yuria says so.... Ugh. I'm still nervous.'

Juliet was a Double-level maid. Her specialties were accounting and agricultural crops. Yuria directly taught her accounting. It was to use her, and it went according to plan. However, Juliet herself didn't yet know how competent she was in the field of accounting.

As the carriage got closer to the Meyer Mansion, the smile on Yuria's face gradually deepened.

,Master-nim is preparing to move urgently, right.'

The old mansion in District G. It was a mansion with a classic charm, but it was a shabby mansion compared to Eugene's current reputation.

For nobles, the mansion itself symbolized authority. Unless they were attached to their mansion, changing their residence was a natural thing to do.

The district Eugene was moving to this time was District D. The place where the real big shots of Neo London lived.

,It's the perfect timing for a thorough cleaning. From the mansion to the items, and even the people. Master-nim is rather indifferent to the inner workings, so I'll have to check everything.'

She had one month.

One month was enough time to clean the mansion for Master-nim.

Juliet trembled slightly as she looked at Yuria, who was silently smiling.

,Yuria. Is something bothering you? You seem a little different than usual.'

,How could I be bothered when I'm returning to Master-nim? I'm happier than ever.'

,I must have been mistaken. Hehe.'
