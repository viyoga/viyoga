There are 8 regions in this world.

Valentier is just one of the 8 regions, and each region is managed by a Paladin.

In other words, there are a total of 8 Paladins, including me.

,Compared to Earth in reality, it's a very small world. Maybe other continents exist.'

The existence of other continents is not very important.

What's important is that Valentier has become safe because of me.

Valentier was originally considered a dangerous area among the 8 regions due to the Great Demon Suten. Especially recently, it became more dangerous as the previous Paladin died. However, the situation changed when I appeared. Using the information I knew, I found and killed all the demons, the fallen, and the heretics. I even killed the Great Demon.

Valentier has become much safer than other regions. It was something for the people of Valentier to rejoice about.

Unlike the people, I am slightly anxious.

My growth has stopped.

I need to kill demons and the fallen, but they are nowhere to be seen.

There is one demon hanging on the iron cross in the city center, but this guy has to play a role in boosting people's faith.

,Repent! Repent! Repent!'

People's voices can be heard from here. They must be beating the demon as I ordered.

,Idleness is a sin. Should I ferret out heretics one more time...? It wouldn't be bad to test the faith of the residents.'

Someone might ask if I can't train or go to another region.

I train every day. I wake up early in the morning and train until the afternoon. But I don't feel like I'm getting stronger at all. It was a matter of efficiency. I was more efficient at catching 10 demons than training for a year.

,And moving to another region is not possible with my will alone.'

To put it a little exaggeratedly, the Paladin was no different from the king of that region. Such a Paladin enters another region at will and hunts demons? The Paladin and the religious order forces of that region will not like it. From their point of view, it's a territorial invasion, an invasion.

If a Paladin wants to operate in another region, they must go through the procedure, even if it's annoying.

,Paladin-isiyeo.'

A Holy Knight approached me. He bowed his head in respect.

,Speak.'

,A messenger from the Papal State has arrived. He is waiting for the Paladin in the reception room.'

,He's finally here.'

I knew what the messenger from the Papal State would say.

If the revival of the Great Demon Suten of Valentier was the content of Act 1, then Act 2 begins with the arrival of the messenger from the Papal State.

I headed to the reception room at a fast pace.

I opened the door to the reception room.

The messenger from the Papal State, who appeared to be in his mid-30s, jumped up from his seat as soon as he saw me and bowed his head in greeting.

,I am Francis, a bishop of the Papal State. I have brought a message from His Holiness the Pope.'

,I didn't expect a bishop from the Papal State to come in person. Let's get straight to the point. What is the message from His Holiness the Pope?'

,His Holiness the Pope is very pleased with the Paladin's achievements.'

,I just did what I had to do as a Paladin. You wouldn't have sent a messenger just to convey those words.'

,His Holiness the Pope has decided to bestow a blessing directly upon the Paladin of Valentier. In addition, in recognition of the merit of executing the Great Demon and restoring the stability of Valentier, he has decided to create a sacred object exclusively for the Paladin.'

,A sacred object exclusively for me....'

,What kind of sacred object would you like?'

,Holy Sword.'

I said without hesitation.

,There is a Holy Sword of Valentier, but one sword is not enough. The Holy Sword of Valentier has already been broken once, so it would be good to have another new Holy Sword.'

,I understand. However, Holy Blood is needed to create the Paladin's Holy Sword.'

,By Holy Blood, do you mean blood?'

,That is correct.'

,I understand.'

I nodded readily. This was also according to the original story.

The bishop of the Papal State took something out of his pocket. It was an object that looked like an ordinary stone on the outside.

,This is a Return Stone with the location of the Papal State stored. The Papal State is already prepared to welcome the Paladin-nim. Let's go to the Papal State together. If you have other busy things to do, I will gladly wait.'

,It won't take long anyway. Let's go now.'

I received the Return Stone.

I use Divine Power. The Return Stone began to shine brightly. The light of the Return Stone envelops my body. The bishop also used the Return Stone.

After a little time, the bishop and I disappeared from the reception room.

Pat.

The bishop and I appeared at the entrance of the Papal State.

A huge white wall over 20m high greeted me. At the open gate entrance, Holy Knights in white armor were lined up. As soon as they saw me, they drew their swords, stuck them in the ground, and paid their respects.

,Paladin of Valentier, I will guide you to the Papal State.'

The bishop walks ahead.

The Papal State was smaller than the city of Valentier. But it was much more developed than the city of Valentier. The faces of the citizens were bright, and the laughter of children echoed.

The Papal State is located in the center of the 8 regions and is thoroughly managed against evil.

All the citizens living in the Papal State have extraordinary origins. Only special people such as the collateral line of nobles, the blood relatives of priests, the descendants of Paladins, and natural-born technicians can reside in the Papal State.

In the center of the city was the Papal State building. It was a pure white castle as white as the walls. It is a white castle that is in no way inferior to the royal palace of the 'Baekhwan' world. Behind the castle was a huge statue reaching 30m.

A statue standing majestically in full body armor with a greatsword stuck in the ground.

,That statue. It's very eye-catching.'

,That is Diaros-nim. He is the first Paladin to receive the revelation of the Goddess Herianne.'

,Is he the one who killed two Great Demons?'

,Yes. That is also one of Diaros-nim's achievements. Diaros-nim's greatest achievement is laying the foundation for the religious order that would unite humanity. The current religious order exists because of Diaros-nim's dedication.'

,.......'

I closed my mouth. The statue was just so big that my eyes were drawn to it. I wasn't interested in the first Paladin or anything like that.

As I headed to the Papal State, citizens began to gather. The citizens cheered as they looked at me.

,People are welcoming me.'

,Didn't the Paladin execute the Great Demon Suten? No one is unaware of that great achievement.'

,Did the Papal State prepare this?'

,Everyone is voluntarily welcoming the Paladin-nim.'

Kiiiiiiing.

I used Holy Sight to look around. Then, cheers erupted even more.

The bishop smiled bitterly, but spoke confidently.

,I heard that you can distinguish demons, the fallen, and heretics. Are there any heretics among these people?'

,...There aren't.'

How could there be?

If the rumors about me have spread, they would know that I can recognize heretics, so there's no reason to appear before me while taking the risk.

,The Papal State is thoroughly managed. We protect the Papal State with the barrier of the Goddess Herianne's sacred objects, and our priests perform purification rituals with the citizens every 100 days. Demons don't even dare to think about targeting the Papal State. This place is a sacred place.'

The bishop boasted about the Papal State, spitting as he spoke.

I listened with one ear and let it out with the other.

The reason why the Papal State has not been attacked by demons so far is not because of the barrier. It's simply because there's no reason to attack the Papal State.

,Humans can be easily caught and eaten in other regions. Unless they're perverted demons, there's no reason to target the heavily guarded Papal State.'

I entered the Papal State.

Numerous Holy Knights, priests, and nuns welcomed me.

The bishop looked at me with a pleased face. This was an event prepared by the Papal State. An event to show off how much they welcomed me.

,I can't stand it.'

,Yes?'

I summon Stormbreaker. Chwaaaareureuk. Stormbreaker was disassembled into pieces in the form of a spear and stuck to my body, becoming armor. Then, I summoned the Holy Sword of Valentier and stabbed the bishop in front of me in the chest.

,Kkeuaaaak?! W-What are you doing?!'

,Shut up, Fallen. Did you think I wouldn't know while hiding your body with magic? My eyes can see through even demon magic.'

,I'm not a Fallen!'

,You're still talking even after your heart has been destroyed. The fact that you're still alive is proof that you're a Fallen.'

The bishop flails his arms and stumbles backward. I reach out my left hand and pull the bishop's neck. The bishop tries to break free from me by exerting superhuman strength. But my strength was stronger than his.

,The Paladin of Valentier has gone mad! The curse of the Great Demon Suten is dominating the Paladin! Holy Knights! What are you doing! Stop the Paladin! Priests! The Paladin of Valentier has fallen! Stop the Paladin with Holy Law! Nuns! Support the priests!'

The Holy Knights held up their greatswords, and the priests prepared to chant prayers. But the bewilderment in their eyes made them hesitate to act.

The bishop of the Papal State and the Paladin who executed the Great Demon. It goes without saying which one has a higher status and greater influence.

,Don't interfere. I am executing a Fallen right now.'

,...Paladin-isiyeo, Bishop Francis is one of His Holiness the Pope's disciples. What is the evidence that he is a Fallen...?'

One of the Holy Knights spoke calmly even in this situation. I looked at him with Holy Sight.

,My eyes are the evidence. Surely, you're not denying these sacred eyes given by the Goddess Herianne?'

,No! But, this is the Papal State!'

,Demons are like cockroaches. No matter how cleanly you manage it, they never get tired of crawling out. You've been safe so far because it's the Papal State? Throw away that complacent thought. Demons are everywhere. It's no different just because it's the Papal State.'

Before that Holy Knight could answer, another Holy Knight jumped out and swung his sword at me. I cut down the bishop and killed him, while simultaneously rotating my body to block the Holy Knight's sword.

,The Paladin of Valentier has fallen! Kill him! This is where His Holiness the Pope resides! We must execute the heretic for His Holiness the Pope!'

I coated the greatsword with Holy Power. The golden shining sword cut through the Holy Knight's sword and armor in an instant. The Holy Knight kneels down and glares at me as if to kill me. The Holy Knight's wound begins to squirm and heal. It is a regenerative ability that humans cannot have.

,Listen, Holy Knights. The place where your faith should be directed is not the Pope. Your faith belongs solely to the Goddess Herianne.'

The Holy Knight reveals his true nature. His skin turns red, and his snout protrudes like that of a beast.

I unleashed Holy Aura.

The sacred energy generated from me dominates the space. The Fallen became sluggish, suppressed by the sacred energy.

,In the name of the Paladin, I command you. Holy Knights, fulfill your duty.'

I swing the Holy Sword.

The Fallen's body was cut in half, spilling blood and entrails.

,Kill evil.'
