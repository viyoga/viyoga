I stepped on the accelerator of the military vehicle. The military vehicle shot forward.

"Where are we going?"

Shin Dohyun asked.

"Somewhere suitable to set up a base?"

I can't just keep driving around. The fuel in the vehicle is only half full. Besides, driving around like this means....

Tang.

A gunshot rang out from somewhere and hit the vehicle. To the right. I immediately turned the steering wheel and drove in the direction the bullet came from.

Tang, tang, tang!

Bullets rained down. But this car was bulletproof. Advice-nim didn't tell me about this car for nothing. As I steadily moved forward, a two-story abandoned building appeared. I could see a man shooting at us from the second-floor window.

I stopped the car right in front of the abandoned building and got out. I went inside the abandoned building. As expected, there was no one on the first floor. Stairs leading to the second floor. I pulled the pin on a grenade below and threw it.

Bang!

"Kuaaaaak!"

Judging by the guy's scream, he seemed to have been hit by the grenade. I immediately went up and finished him off with a pistol. I checked the items he had. There was nothing particularly useful other than bullets. Instead, I collected the game points I was aiming for.

「Game Points Held: 50」

There is only one place to use game points.

「Enhance Game Tools and Abilities - 50 GP」

'It would be reassuring to have an escape ticket worth 100 points... but I have to kill 10 more people here to collect 100 more points. When am I going to collect that?'

Since I didn't have any game tools, I could only enhance my abilities.

「Ability has been enhanced.」

'I wonder if I can use Advice-nim one more time.'

&lt;The user can use Advice one more time.&gt;

I was glad that it didn't take my question as a request for advice. I was a little worried because Advice-nim is kind of a moron.

'It seems to have gotten a little better as my ability was enhanced. It looks like it's not just the number of times that increases.'

I hesitated to use Advice-nim and decided to use it if the situation became urgent. I needed insurance in case the devil did something else.

• • •

"Kyaaaaaaak!"

Tang!

The woman who was screaming was shot and killed. Burly men gathered around the woman's corpse. Ten men all wearing suits. They were Peng Cheonsu's subordinates and the boss and members of the Manchun Faction.

"Hyung-nim. Isn't it a waste to kill a woman?"

Manchun frowned at his subordinate, who was licking his lips while looking at the woman's corpse.

"Tsk. You can hug women in reality later. Right now, just think about ending the game as quickly as possible."

"Hehe. Are you giving us pocket money?"

"Yeah, you punk. I'll take care of you. Chairman Peng-nim seems to be giving us a business this time. We just have to keep doing what we're doing. Just like this."

Manchun took out a small device from his pocket. It was a radar that showed people's locations. It was a supply that fell from the sky earlier.

'Let's see. There are 57 people left. We win if we kill 47 more people.'

It seems like a lot, but it's okay. These guys will fight among themselves.

'Most of them are traveling in groups of 2 or 3. They must be hastily formed groups. They don't have the same solidarity as our organization.'

Of course.

They had already anticipated what kind of game it would be before the game even started. Peng Cheonsu taught them.

'The Devil's Game Database, was it? He said he created something like that, even if it was rough? Peng Cheonsu. I have to admit that that ruthless bastard has a good ability.'

Looking at the radar, he decided on his next target. Two people who had been constantly moving around since earlier, probably having obtained a car, finally settled in one place.

'The more cars, the better. And it's not far from here, which is good.'

Manchun said to his subordinates.

"I've decided on the next target. Let's go, guys."

The Manchun Faction got into three cars and started moving. The boss, Manchun, naturally sat in the back seat of the sedan. He couldn't grab the steering wheel himself, he had his boss's dignity to consider.

The subordinate who was holding the steering wheel groaned and opened his mouth.

"Hyung-nim.... Wasn't I from the Special Forces? This terrain isn't good. There's no cover, and it's perfect for being sniped. It's hard to go around because there's a river next to it. Wouldn't it be better to go somewhere else first?"

"Huh. Gyeongshik-ah. There are only 2 people in that building. 2 people. On the other hand, there are 10 of us. What can those 2 do even if they try? Just step on it."

"Yes, Hyung-nim."

The car started running faster.

Taang!

A bullet flew and hit the car window. It was in the driver's seat direction. It was lucky. It barely grazed Gyeongshik's cheek. Starting with this, bullets began to rain down.

"This shitty!"

Gyeongshik cursed and turned the steering wheel this way and that. The car moved forward in a zigzag pattern. Manchun, who was in the back, was having a terrible time.

"Gyeongshik-ah! You're going to kill me! Drive properly!"

"I can't, Hyung-nim! The sniper's skills are good! If I drive calmly, I'll die right away!

The good thing was that there were two snipers and three cars in the Manchun Faction. The bullets weren't only shot at Gyeongshik and Manchun, but also at the other cars.

Kkiiiiiiik!

The car that was going well next to them suddenly swerved. The driver was sniped and killed. The car couldn't overcome its inertia and fell over. Smoke rose from the car. It didn't look like it would explode right away, but it looked dangerous.

The members of the organization who were in the car crawled out. As if waiting for them, the sniping continued. Four people died. The good thing was that the other two cars were able to reach the lower part of the building, where sniping was impossible.

"You fucking assholes!"

Manchun, who came out of the car, shouted. It was a shout for the two enemies hiding in the building to hear. That's how you establish dominance.

At that time, a tiresome voice was heard from the sky.

-Bombing will begin in this area in 10 minutes.

-We would like to inform you again.

-Bombing will begin in this area in 10 minutes.

Manchun looked around. He could see a red boundary line extending from the ground to the sky. This boundary line, which looked like a barrier, was the boundary indicating the bombing zone. The lucky thing was that this place was outside the boundary line.

"Shibal.... I'm really unlucky. Gyeongshik-ah! Can this building withstand the bombing if it falls here?"

"...This level of building cannot withstand the bombing. We have to get out of here. But since we have a car, we can get out in 1 minute."

Manchun looked around.

5 members of the organization.

Gyeongshik, who was from the special forces, was cold-blooded, and the other 4 had fierce eyes burning with anger. It was natural since their fellow members and friends had died. They wanted revenge. And that was the same for Manchun himself.

"Okay. We have to avenge our kids. We can kill them and run away in 5 minutes. Let's go!"

The morale of the subordinates rose.

"You fucking bastards!!"

"We'll make you pay the price for messing with us!!"

Two subordinates with hot tempers kicked down the door and went inside. Gyeongshik saw something and hurriedly closed the door.

Bang, bang, bang!

The bombs exploded, killing the two subordinates who had gone in first. The atmosphere drops sharply.

"...Gyeongshik-ah."

"I'm sorry, Hyung-nim. I couldn't save the two. He threw the bomb as if he had been waiting for it and went upstairs."

"What a crazy bastard. What should we do? I feel uneasy about going up the stairs. Should I go up trusting my ability?"

"Hyung-nim's ability can only be used once, so we have to save it. We also have grenades, so let's throw them from below the stairs to the second floor."

"Hyung-nim! Please leave it to us!"

"We will avenge our brothers!"

Two subordinates pulled the pins on the grenades and ran down the stairs to throw the grenades. Tang, tang! Two gunshots rang out in succession. The bullets pierced the two grenades exactly.

The grenades exploded, and the two subordinates were torn to pieces and died.

Only Manchun and Gyeongshik were left.

'These crazy.... All my subordinates died in less than 5 minutes?'

Now it was just absurd.

"Gyeongshik-ah. What should we do? Isn't it better to use smoke grenades?"

"No, Boss. If we use smoke grenades here, we will suffer more damage. And the opponent's shooting skills are not ordinary. I will use my ability."

Gyeongshik used his ability as he approached the stairs.

Clone.

To be exact, it was a fake that looked like a clone. It could be said to be a kind of hologram. As soon as the clone headed towards the stairs, bullets were fired. The bullets, aiming exactly for the clone's head, pierced the clone's body and hit the ground. The clone did not disappear and was fine.

Gyeongshik took advantage of the gap and shot at the top of the stairs. The opponent, who was hiding near the stairs, retreated without hesitation.

Gyeongshik stared at the top of the stairs and said.

"He's not an ordinary guy. I think Hyung-nim will have to step up."

• • •

I frowned as I entered the room.

'I didn't know he was a guy who used abilities like clones. It would have been dangerous if I hadn't been super focused.'

I was hiding near the entrance to the stairs going down, but he shot exactly at my head. Judging by his extremely calm expression, he was no ordinary guy.

'It's so fucking hard to deal with him since the opponent also has a gun.'

You one shot. Me one shot.

It would be better to fight each other with melee weapons. Then I'm confident I'll never lose.

"What happened? Did you take care of it?"

"How many are left? I don't know exactly if it's one person... but he's probably coming up. Get ready."

Shin Dohyun's eyes lit up as he picked up a rifle instead of a sniper rifle. He had already thrown away the ghillie suit. The ghillie suit is meaningless at this distance.

"Shall we jump out the window?"

The height of the second floor wasn't too overwhelming. But I shook my head.

"We have no reason to run away. Let's get rid of him here."

I'll use super focus to kill him the moment he opens the door and comes in. It's possible with my reaction speed and shooting skills.

Step, step.

I can hear footsteps. These guys are making footsteps openly and coming to the room we're in. There is only one set of footsteps.

Bang!

With a loud noise, the old door opened as if it were breaking.

Shin Dohyun and I pulled the triggers of our assault rifles. Bullets poured out of the barrels.

Ting, ting, ting, ting!

An invisible shield appeared in front of us and deflected the bullets. It was a magical shield that would only appear in cartoons or movies.

"Keep firing!!"

If it's a shield, it must have limited durability. As the bullets continued to pour in, cracks began to appear in the shield. And the magazines were empty. Unfortunately, both Shin Dohyun and I at the same time. We reflexively tried to replace the magazines.

&lt;Warning, use smoke grenades.&gt;

I pulled the pin on the smoke grenade without hesitation.

I stepped forward in front of Shin Dohyun and shouted.

"Dohyun-ah, run!"

I covered my face with my gun and arms. Bullets flew. Thanks to the smoke, the bullets grazed near me.

Tung, tung!

Bullets hit and slid off my bulletproof helmet and hit my bulletproof vest. I threw away my rifle as if throwing it away and took out my pistol and fired. I couldn't see because of the smoke, so I couldn't hit anything.

'Shibal. Let's get out of here for now!'

I was about to jump out the window when a bullet flew and hit my left wrist.

"......!!"

Ignoring the pain I felt, I jumped out. Shin Dohyun headed to where he had parked the military vehicle.

Tang, tang, tang!

Gunshots rang out from the first floor.

Shin Dohyun... was fortunately safe thanks to hiding behind the corner wall.

"He was on the first floor? There were two of them?"

I thought of the clone I saw on the stairs. The one who came upstairs was actually one person, not two. One person was guarding the military vehicle.

They exchange gunfire. I try to use super focus, but my head hurts.

'Ah, shit. Did I use super focus too much today?'

I had no choice but to exchange gunfire without using super focus. The guy was using the bulletproof military vehicle as cover, so it wasn't easy. In addition, the guy on the second floor came down and helped with the gunfight.

'I have the car keys, so he won't be able to use it.'

There was little time left. A bomb would be dropped here soon.

I closed my mouth tightly and made a decision.

'Let's do something crazy for once.'

I charged at them. Is it a problem that it's hard to concentrate when death is right in front of me? If I make even a slight mistake here, I'll go straight to hell. I have no choice but to concentrate even if I don't want to. I entered a state of super focus.

The places where the guys are hiding, the barrels of the pistols they're holding, and the bullets flying towards me look slow. I moved my body while roughly calculating those things.

'I'll take the hits I need to take.'

I'm wearing a bulletproof helmet, bulletproof vest, and bulletproof pants. I just have to avoid getting hit in the neck and face.

Bullets flew and hit my legs and abdomen. Instead, I put bullets in the guys' foreheads.

After killing two people, I immediately opened the door of the military vehicle.

"Dohyun-ah! Get in quickly!"

Shin Dohyun hurriedly got into the passenger seat. It was the moment I was about to insert the car key and step on the accelerator. My vision spun around. I lost consciousness, recalling the bullet that had hit and passed by my helmet earlier.
