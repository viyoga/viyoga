It was dawn, before the sun had risen.

I, standing guard on top of the mansion, narrowed my eyes and stared straight ahead. A cluster of lights was approaching this way from the distance at a considerable speed.

[Telescope]

I enhanced my eyesight with magic and identified the cluster of lights approaching at high speed.

It was bicycles.

More than 30 people were furiously pedaling bicycles and rushing over. The speed was nearly 300 km/h. It was clear that the bicycles had been illegally modified.

I tapped my mask and keyed the radio.

"Number 212! Number 31! Enemy attack!!"

-This is Number 212. What is the enemy's identity and location?!

"The enemy is about 30 people. They're all riding illegally modified bicycles. They'll arrive at the mansion's main gate in about 3 minutes. They seem to be armed with guns."

-Illegally modified bicycles...? Are they wearing angry-looking masks, by any chance?

"Let me check."

I focused mana in my eyes. My eyesight improved, and the enemies became clearer.

"Number 212. As you said, they're all wearing angry-looking masks."

-It's Angry Cycle.

"Angry Cycle?"

-They're a biker gang operating in this area. I heard on the internet that they lost a turf war and went into hiding... In my opinion, they must have been hired by the laborers. They're trying to pull off one last job and leave this city.

"Well, they can't keep living in this city after messing with a noble, so..."

The area below the mansion is bustling. I glanced down and saw the guards, realizing the appearance of Angry Cycle, preparing for battle.

"Number 212. Even if the opponents are just bikers, it's too noisy. It might be a deliberate attempt to draw attention. Check the barrier around the room where Baron Ptelgar is staying and prepare for the worst. Also, cast support magic on our guard friends."

-Leave it to me. That's my job, after all.

"Number 31. What about you? Do you want to get involved?"

-I'll follow orders.

Number 31's voice was calm. She has a battle maniac tendency, so I asked her once, but it seemed she wasn't interested in the biker gang.

"Protect Baron Ptelgar with Number 212."

-Understood.

With Number 31's reply, Angry Cycle crashed into the mansion's main gate. They sprayed bullets and threw bombs. The guards responded with the latest equipment provided by Baron Ptelgar.

'Even if they're just bikers, they're fighting too recklessly. They don't seem to fear death. I can even feel madness. There's only one explanation for this. Drugs.'

I open the Astral.

I can feel the mana around me, and the mana road is activated. I calculate the magic formulas, making sure not to use magic above the 4th circle, and even adjusting the power of 3rd circle magic. Mana rushes through the mana road, transforming into formulas and becoming magic.

[Fire Rain]

I activate 3rd circle fire magic.

Flames created in the air fall to the ground. The surprised enemies retreat. No matter how much their fighting spirit is heightened by drugs, they don't want to throw away their lives like moths to a flame.

'The power is weaker than Fireball, as expected.'

However, the purpose of Fire Rain was to control the combat area. I used Fire Rain to give the guards a chance to catch their breath.

-Thank you for the support. Thanks to you, those crazy bastards' morale has been dampened.

I received a radio message from guard captain Lakemak.

"I just did what I had to do."

-These guys... They took combat drugs. It's not easy to deal with them. Can you support us with magic?

"I told you. I do what I have to do."

Providing fire support is the role of a battle mage. There were few cases where I directly stepped forward to fight. That's because mages themselves are high-value personnel.

'...But in the military, I'm just an expensive consumable.'

I clicked my tongue and brushed away the distracting thoughts. Now is the time for battle.

"Iyah-ah-ah-ah-ho!"

One of the Angry Cycle bikers shouts. It spread around like an epidemic.

"Iyah-ah-ah-ho!"

"Iyah-ho!!!"

"Iyah-ho-oh-oh-oh!!"

The bikers furiously pedal their bicycles. But the bicycle wheels didn't move. They just waited, as if storing up power.

"Iyah-ah-ah-ah-ho-oh-oh!!"

The bikers shout. It's so loud that my ears hurt, standing on the mansion roof.

'Judging by the looks of it, they've decided to become kamikaze... no, Jaranis.'

The moment their bicycle wheels scratched the ground, trying to speed off, I also used magic.

[Stone Wall]

Several walls shot up from the ground. The walls were slightly tilted. The rushing Jaranis couldn't avoid the Stone Wall and ran up the walls, flying into the sky. They frantically pedaled in the sky. But the bicycles couldn't run in the sky and fell down.

The height was a whopping 10 meters. No matter how much their physical abilities were enhanced by combat drugs, they were bound to be injured if they fell.

Thwack! Thwack! Thwack!

The bikers fall futilely. The guards mercilessly shot the fallen guys, finishing them off.

'About half of them are dead with this. The guards' damage is minimal. This battle will end soon.'

I was contemplating the next course of action when I received a radio message from Number 212.

-Number 211! Number 31 is in battle!

"In battle? No one entered the mansion from the outside! Where did they appear from?!"

-When I asked Baron Ptelgar, it seems there was a secret passage he didn't tell us about!

"...Shit."

A curse word came out naturally.

We're risking our lives to protect Baron Ptelgar, but Baron Ptelgar, the person we're protecting, doesn't trust us at all.

I swallowed hard and calmed my mind. Didn't I already know that Baron Ptelgar didn't trust us?

"What's the opponent's level?"

-Looks like a 3rd circle warrior. Number 31 is fighting them. It doesn't feel dangerous.

"You're saying Number 31 is winning. Do you need my help?"

-No. It's okay. Take care of the outside.

"Okay."

I hung up the radio.

This kind of thing happens often. And in these situations, you have to act coldly. I can't abandon my job just because a colleague is in danger. If I did, it would lead to an irreversible catastrophe. What I need to do now is deal with the Jaranis in front of me.

Whoosh.

Mana stirs.

I drew a pure white magic circle in the air. It slows down the casting speed compared to manifesting the magic immediately, but it has the advantage of increasing the magic's power.

[Ice Spear]

An ice spear was fired from the center of the magic circle. The spearhead was aimed not at the biker gang, but at the guard captain, Lakemak. Lakemak, who had been wary of me, didn't panic and dodged the Ice Spear with a backflip.

The Ice Spear, embedded in the ground, exploded, and cold air swept around. The blue cold froze the surroundings. Lakemak's body was also frozen, but he shook his body and shook off the ice fragments. It's not that there was no damage. Lakemak's body became sluggish. It meant that the cold had penetrated deep into his body.

-...What are you doing? Is this betrayal?

"You're the traitor. I just received a report that the enemy entered the mansion through a secret passage. You already knew about the existence of the secret passage in the mansion. Didn't you?"

Lakemak is the guard captain, after all. Baron Ptelgar wouldn't have put just anyone in the position of guard captain. He must have worked under Baron Ptelgar for at least a few years, and he must have known about the existence of the secret passage in the mansion.

"Do you really believe I'm a traitor with just that? How absurd."

"So you're saying you're not?"

"No. You're right."

Lakemak kicked off the ground. He punched the heads of the guards next to him, smashing them, and rushed towards me.

I shouted to the panicking guards.

"I'll take care of the traitor Lakemak. You guys stop Angry Cycle."

The guards continued the battle, spraying bullets at Angry Cycle. The guards seemed to have been trained by soldiers.

I narrowed my eyes and looked at Lakemak.

'He's about a 3rd circle warrior. He was hiding his skills.'

He climbed onto the mansion roof in an instant, as if a monkey had possessed him. The way he used his body was even amazing. As someone who doesn't have a talent for exercise, I can't do that with pure physical ability alone.

"Are you from the Resistance Echoes?"

"Why do you think so? Shouldn't you suspect Bill Klaxon?"

"There are limits to bio-bomb magic. You can manipulate the actions of small animals like mice, but things change when it comes to humans. Humans have mana resistance and egos. It means that a black magician can't manipulate a human who has become a bio-bomb."

"So what's your point?"

"The important thing is the timing to detonate the bio-bomb. It's a plan that can only succeed perfectly if someone inside the mansion tells them the timing."

"...Well, you're right. It was a perfect plan. If only you hadn't suspected the whores for no reason."

"Did you hire Angry Cycle too?"

"Yes. Bill Klaxon doesn't have the money to hire them. I hired them as soon as the plan went wrong. Here's a suggestion, why don't you pretend you didn't see anything?"

"Why?"

"Baron Ptelgar must die here. That's what's best for world peace. As long as that bastard continues to live, countless commoners will be exploited and toyed with by him."

"That's none of my business. I'm carrying out my mission."

"...That's why I hate soldiers. They're not human, they're like machines."

Lakemak rushes at me. I take a step back to create distance. At the same time, I activate the magic I had prepared during the conversation.

[Torrent]

Streams of water shot upwards from under Lakemak's feet. The sharp, knife-like streams of water cut Lakemak's body. If he were an ordinary person, it wouldn't be strange for his body to be torn to pieces by the 3rd circle water attribute attack magic, but Lakemak only ended up with cuts on his skin.

[Ice]

The water freezes.

Of course, the water soaking his body was no exception. He was forced to wear an ice restraint.

"Haaah!"

He burst out with an aura along with a shout. A transparent heat haze rose from his body. He glared at me with bloodshot eyes and rushed at me.

I used telekinesis to attack him with the shattering ice shards. It was useless. The ice shards couldn't penetrate the aura surrounding his body.

I retreat. There was nowhere to step. My body fell helplessly from the roof.

"It's over!"

Lakemak, convinced of his victory, raised his fist and jumped towards me.

And we both stopped in mid-air.

I turned my eyes and looked at the mansion window. I met eyes with Number 212. She stuck out her tongue slightly and playfully squeezed her right breast with her hand. To be sexually harassed in this situation. I was a little dumbfounded.

"Wh-What kind of magic did you use?!"

"I didn't use it. My colleague did."
