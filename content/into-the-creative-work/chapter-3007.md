『The Storm God (A) is responding.』

The trait reacted.

Until now, the 'Storm God' trait had never responded unless I revealed my intention to use it.

'Just as the Demon Hunter trait reacts to demons, the fact that this trait reacted without my will being involved means... it can't simply be because the wind is blowing violently.'

A harsh wind and rain swept in.

Yet no wind actually touched my body. The wind was parting around me and only me. The torrential rain falling from the sky was the same. Rain refused to fall only where I stood. Splashing droplets were blocked by the wind.

I looked at the villagers groveling on the ground. They were all in wretched states, but I called out to the man who looked the least worse for wear, standing at their front.

"Hey."

"Hah! Takehayasusanoonomikoto!"

"Lift your head and answer my question. Do I look like Susanoo to you?"

The man raised his head. Gaunt, with hollow cheeks, he rolled his eyes as if bewildered.

"D-Did we ignorant folk do something wrong?"

"Was it you who called me—called Susanoo?"

"That is correct! We offered the village's rice and livestock in prayer! Did Takehayasusanoonomikoto not descend from Takamagahara in answer to our prayers?"

I had only entered a dungeon, yet suddenly I'd been dropped into this situation.

No need to panic. Even if it's sudden, I've been through similar situations several times before.

'The dungeon has probably been influenced by the Storm God trait. As for the clear condition, it seems to be killing Yamata no Orochi.'

I knew the myth of Susanoo to some extent. Compared to the original myth of Susanoo and Yamata no Orochi, the current situation was quite different.

I took out Susanoo's magatama, which I had shoved away in my inventory.

Susanoo's magatama, which contained Susanoo's power. Normally, Susanoo's power should have filled it very slowly, but now it surged in an instant.

'Is it the environmental influence? At this rate, I should be able to use Susanoo's power freely.'

I swung my hand through the air as a test.

A blade of wind formed and flew forward. It mowed down every tree in its path before vanishing.

"Hiiieeeek!"

The man who had raised his head was so terrified he slammed his face back into the dirt.

"Which direction is Yamata no Orochi in?"

"It has taken up residence in the east and runs rampant! It commanded every surrounding nation to offer up virgins, and any nation that refused was destroyed!"

"Virgin sacrifices? To eat them?"

"Pardon? Ah, yes—to devour them! Over two hundred virgins have already been eaten by that monstrous serpent! Our village also sent eight virgins!"

So Yamata no Orochi physically devours virgins. Not that a snake would have any reason to devour them sexually.

I surveyed the villagers. There were young women among them, but they were all ugly. Even accounting for their primitive living conditions.

"By any chance, did it demand only beautiful women among the virgins?"

"That is correct. That monstrous serpent said beautiful women taste better...!"

The man spoke as if swallowing his rage.

'A monster lusting after beautiful women. It's a creature that shouldn't exist.'

In the original myth, Susanoo got Yamata no Orochi drunk on sake before killing it. In other words, he didn't fight it head-on.

'Susanoo is one of the famous Three Great Deities and a god of war. For a Susanoo like that to avoid a head-on fight? That means Yamata no Orochi is disgustingly strong.'

Should I go about it the same way?

Where would I even get sake? I had a large amount of alcohol in my inventory, but whether it could intoxicate Yamata no Orochi—which could arguably be called a god—was uncertain.

'Hmm. This is inside a dungeon. It's not the real myth. Let's try fighting it first.'

I use the power of the Storm God (A). Wind gathered and lifted my body into the sky. I flew through the wind and rain toward where the creature would be.

It didn't take long to find Yamata no Orochi. It had settled where the river currents converged. A monster with eight heads, one body, and eight tails. Its size was absurdly massive. A single one of its heads looked to be at least 30 meters.

'Fuck. Why is it so damn big?'

What was even more absurd was that it wasn't alone.

There were humans and yokai around it. The humans, terrified out of their minds, were attending to Yamata no Orochi. They wiped down its body, which was constantly soaked by the wind and rain, and when Yamata no Orochi got bored, it snatched up and ate the attending humans.

'So it didn't only eat virgins after all.'

The yokai watched the scene with delight. And whenever the opportunity arose, they bowed their heads to Yamata no Orochi and flattered it. Yamata no Orochi casually tossed a single scale to whichever yokai pleased it. The yokai that swallowed the scale grew stronger in power.

'I should set up some insurance.'

I rode the wind and traveled all across Japan. It was to mark coordinates for spatial teleportation scrolls.

In doing so, I sensed the power of mighty beings throughout Japan.

They were yokai and spirits known as land gods.

I also confirmed the existence of the Three Great Evil Spirits of Japan: Otakemaru, Shuten-doji, and the Nine-Tailed Fox with white face and golden fur. They were weaker than Yamata no Orochi.

'They don't seem like beings that should exist in this era... The historical background might be all jumbled together.'

I hadn't come to a past era. I don't forget that I'm inside a dungeon.

'Just in case, I should set coordinates outside Japan too. While I'm at it, I can check how vast this dungeon is.'

I crossed the sea and arrived at the Korean Peninsula. I didn't enter the peninsula directly. How should I put it—it felt like a mighty being on the peninsula was rejecting me. The moment I tried to enter, I had a strong intuition that things would go wrong.

'It feels like some invisible force is protecting the Korean Peninsula.'

I set coordinates near the Korean Peninsula.

While I was at it, I went further west and checked China. Unlike the Korean Peninsula, I didn't feel rejected.

'A demonic realm.'

There were hundreds of beings on the continent comparable to Japan's Three Great Evil Spirits. They didn't even bother hiding their power.

'So gods and yokai are mixed together here. With my current strength, able to wield Susanoo's power, I could probably defeat a few of them...'

Even while using Susanoo's power, it hadn't diminished in the slightest. More precisely, even as I used Susanoo's power, strength was accumulating at an even faster rate.

'Is this the mythological era the dungeon has manifested?'

A sharp energy prickled against my senses.

Just as I had become aware of the continent's powerhouses, they too had noticed my presence, sending their gazes and power toward me.

"I have no intention of fighting. I'm leaving."

Chiiiiik.

I tore a spatial teleportation scroll and slipped away.

I returned to Japan and concealed my presence once more. Fortunately, Yamata no Orochi was completely off guard.

'One big shot.'

Bzzzzt.

Blue lightning flashed.

I poured my power and Susanoo's power out without restraint. Susanoo's storm referred to wind and lightning. The thick black clouds blanketing the sky began to rotate.

Kwaaaaarung.

Blue lightning flickered within the black clouds, resonating with each other's power and growing in size. Simultaneously, they gathered at the center of the clouds, refining into a single spear.

At this anomaly visible to anyone, Yamata no Orochi and the yokai who had been steeped in lethargy snapped to attention and raised their heads.

"Th-the sky...!"

"It's a god! I can feel a god's power!"

"This storm's power is...! Susanoo! Susanoo is trying to kill us!!"

The terrified yokai scrambled in panic, trying to flee.

"Stop your racket, you mongrels! I am here, and you fear a mere bolt of lightning? This is blasphemy against me! Anyone who tries to flee this place dies!"

Yamata no Orochi raised its colossal body and roared. Eight heads, but one voice. Unlike its multiple heads, it possessed a single ego.

Yamata no Orochi chewed and swallowed the fleeing yokai in one bite, letting out a roar. At that, the yokai trembled, fell to their knees, and bowed their heads.

Yamata no Orochi's eight heads all turned in unison toward where I stood.

"Susanoooooo!!!"

A bellow filled with rage. With just that, rivers reversed their flow and mountains crumbled. Even the black clouds in the sky quaked.

But my power did not scatter. I gazed at my target, Yamata no Orochi, with indifferent eyes and lowered my raised hand.

Thunder Heaven Style — Ultimate Technique — Thunder God's Decree.

A colossal spear of lightning, powerful enough to level mountains and still have force to spare, plunged down upon Yamata no Orochi. Humans and yokai caught in the aftermath were burned away without leaving even a trace.

But Yamata no Orochi endured. It withstood a destructive force great enough to reshape the terrain. Though five of its eight heads had fallen, it endured. The remaining three heads, driven mad with fury, opened their jaws wide.

A serpent's maw. Two fangs glinted. And at its center, a massive power gathering.

'Not even a dragon, and you're trying to use breath?'

That power was not something I could ignore. I'd realized it the moment it withstood the Thunder God's Decree. In a head-on fight, I was at a severe disadvantage.

I immediately pulled out a spatial teleportation scroll.

"Hmph. We'll finish this later."

Chiiiiik.

I tore the spatial teleportation scroll and fled.

I appeared at the southern tip of the archipelago.

"KRAAAAAAAHHHHHHH!"

Even from the southern tip, Yamata no Orochi's roar could be heard.

'Retreating using spatial teleportation scrolls... probably won't work twice. With power like that, it could isolate space itself even without erecting a separate barrier. To kill that thing...'

『You are becoming accustomed to the power of storms.』

『Your influence as the God of Storms grows.』

『The rank of God of Storms rises to A+.』

A notification window appeared.

Though karma hadn't increased, the rank of the God of Storms trait had risen.

'Until now there wasn't the slightest sign of the rank going up... Could this be because I used Susanoo's power to kill the yokai?'

Even if I hadn't managed to kill Yamata no Orochi, I'd slaughtered all the yokai and humans around it.

'I think I know the strategy now.'
