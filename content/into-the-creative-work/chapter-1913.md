Even Professor Hemmington wouldn't outright tell someone to go die. He wouldn't want to turn the entire expedition against him.

I focused mana in my ears, temporarily enhancing my hearing to eavesdrop on Professor Hemmington's conversation.

,According to my interpretation of the inscription on that stele... I need your help.,

,My help?

,Specifically, the help of a young woman. This ancient ruin desires a young and pure woman. It's not uncommon. Since ancient times, virgins have been regarded as sacred....,

,Well, yes, that's true. There are many stories involving virgins in folklore.... Even Divine France is obsessed with chaste maidens.,

,I'm grateful you understand.,

Clara's expression didn't brighten. She cautiously asked,

,Are you saying the ruin wants a human sacrifice? Pure virgins are the perfect offering for human sacrifices, after all.,

,No, don't worry, it's nothing like that. The ruin wants a young woman to dance for it. It would be much better if that person is a virgin. ...So, are you a virgin?

Clara looked around. After confirming that there was no one listening besides Professor Hemmington and Clive, she blushed and nodded.

,...Yes. I'm a virgin.,

,Don't be ashamed. Maintaining your purity is something to be proud of. Too many people disregard purity these days....,

Still, Clara's red cheeks didn't return to their original color.

,It's not like I wanted to keep my virginity.,

Clara wasn't exactly what you'd call a beautiful woman. Her hair was dry and her skin wasn't clear. It was obvious she'd been through a lot, wandering around as an explorer. And her physique was so sturdy, she wouldn't look like a woman at all if she were dressed like a man.

,What exactly do I need to do?

,Dance and sing in front of that stele.,

,Um, those are both things I'm not very good at....,

,It doesn't need to be perfect. What matters is the heart. Ah, but please pay attention to the singing. Even if the pitch is off, the pronunciation is important.,

Professor Hemmington started spouting a completely foreign language, claiming he would teach her the song.

,I've never heard that language before. Is it an ancient language?

,That's right. It's a pronunciation of an ancient language. It's a song related to blessings.,

It was all a lie.

That wasn't an ancient language, but the language of a small tribe somewhere at the end of the continent. The meaning was to offer oneself. The dance was also a dance for human sacrifice.

,It's flowing according to the original.,

Thirty minutes later, Clara stepped forward in front of the stele, under everyone's gaze.

Clara performed the dance that Professor Hemmington had taught her in a crash course. It was a dance that grotesquely bent the joints of her arms and legs. Her lack of skill made it look like a freestyle dance. Then, she sang with a cracked voice.

The dance was clumsy and the song was unpleasant. More than half of the expedition members frowned. But it worked.

The stele glowed faintly.

And.

Deureureureureuk!

A door opened in the floor, and tentacles popped out, grabbing Clara in an instant and pulling her down.

,Kyaaaaaaaak?!

Kwang!

The floor closed.

In terms of time, it was only about two seconds. None of those gathered here could react. The expedition belatedly shouted.

,Clara?!

,W-What the hell?!

,A trap? Why did the trap suddenly activate?!

Amidst the commotion, Professor Hemmington stared intently at the stele. The stele split in half and opened to the side. Inside the stele was a path leading downwards. Hemmington smiled momentarily, but returned to a blank expression.

,It was a trap in the stele. It can't be helped. Don't let your guard down, you all. Ancient ruins are full of the unknown, so the moment you let your guard down even a little, you'll face death.,

Professor Hemmington said in a calm voice. The expedition members grimaced but calmed down. They were used to the death of their colleagues. Ancient ruins are mostly dangerous, so there are quite a few deaths. Besides, this world itself was close to death. But that didn't mean they accepted death easily.

The expedition members whispered among themselves.

,To think Clara would die like this....,

,We confirmed there were no traps. Why did it suddenly activate?

,Could Professor Hemmington have made a mistake? Misinterpreted the ancient language or something....,

,That's not possible. Professor Hemmington is such an amazing person. If it weren't for the professor's knowledge at the last ancient ruin, we would all be dead. You know that too. The real problem is the mercenaries. We hired them for times like this, but... they're not doing anything.,

,That's right. If the mercenaries had reacted properly, Clara wouldn't have died so futilely.,

,I heard we spent nearly 100 million credits hiring those mercenaries... Isn't that just a waste of money?

The atmosphere quickly turned hostile towards the mercenaries. They were blaming the mercenaries.

Naturally, the mercenaries who were listening felt wronged. No matter how strong they were compared to ordinary humans, it was impossible to respond to something unexpected that happened in an instant. At least, not without more time.

,Well, I could have.,

I knew this would happen. If I had used psychokinesis, I could have saved Clara.

,It's a necessary thing to go down below.,

I tried to think of a reason why I should save the expedition member named Clara. There wasn't one.

To gain the expedition's favor? Professor Hemmington would hate it, saying things had gone wrong. And in the end, I was going to kill all these bastards anyway. I had to monopolize the treasures of the Edinburgh Ancient Ruins.

The hostility towards the mercenaries was palpable. Jin frowned and glared at the expedition members, while Josh and I remained expressionless.

,Enough with the chatter, pay attention.,

Professor Hemmington stood in front of the path leading down and looked around at the expedition.

,It will be more dangerous below. If there's anyone who wants to quit here, I won't force you to come.,

,Professor. How can you say that? We've come this far, how can we quit? That's an insult to our Hemmington Expedition.,

,We've already made all the preparations. Clara would feel the same.,

They couldn't turn back because it was dangerous. If they gave up here, they would only be waiting for a huge pile of debt.

,Indeed! I knew you'd say that! Our Hemmington Expedition has overcome all sorts of adversities and walked forward. Clara's death is unfortunate, but mourning can wait until after the exploration is over. We must move forward as we always have!

,That's right!

,The Hemmington Expedition cannot stop here!

,The more dangerous the ancient ruin, the sweeter the reward will be!

Professor Hemmington, having raised the expedition's morale, took the lead and moved forward. Jin followed right beside her.

I moved along with the expedition members. I was walking quietly when I felt a breeze in my ear.

-It's Josh. What just happened... I can't help but think it's strange. I was watching Professor Hemmington from the very back. Professor Hemmington didn't even panic once. As if she knew this would happen.

Josh had a good sense. But I didn't react much.

Rather, I was curious about the method Josh used to convey his voice to me.

,The mana is felt very faintly. It's not magic for sure. I don't feel any incantations at all.,

It felt like he carried his voice on the wind. In other words, he manipulated the wind. There's a technique called Jeoneum in martial arts, but... it didn't feel like that. I felt the wind in my ear.

,Is it an ability to control the wind?

That would make sense. Unlike magic, abilities could be controlled meticulously. Meaning, they had excellent applicability.

,If that wind entered my head through my ear...?

Wouldn't that be the perfect ability for assassination?

,It would be difficult because I have resistance... but it's not entirely impossible. I need to be careful.,

A stele appeared. And behind the stele, the path was divided.

Everyone looked at Professor Hemmington because she was the only one who could interpret the ancient language written on the stele. Hemmington crossed her arms and glared at the stele. She must have understood the ancient language as soon as she saw it. She was probably worrying about what to do next.

-On the right is life, and on the left is death. Life and death are one.

Simply put, if you go to the right, you live, and if you go to the left, you die. However, you can't choose to go to only one side. You have to go to both places at the same time.

,What a nasty ancient ruin.,

The reward would be just as sweet.

,It says that the path will only open if there are two destinies.,

Hemmington opened her mouth.

Frankly speaking, only one person needs to be sacrificed here. Sending one of the mercenaries to the left path is the most reasonable thing to do. The reason is that they're being sent to scout. If the mercenary doesn't want to go, send one of the expedition members with them. Then they'll have to go, even if they don't want to.

Professor Hemmington is planning to divide the group in half. She's going to kill half of the expedition members. Why? To monopolize the treasure at the end of the ancient ruin.

,I think we have no choice but to divide into two and advance separately.,

,It's a unique ancient ruin.,

,I'll give you a choice. Will you go to the right, or will you go to the left?

,Which way are you planning to go, Professor?

,My opinion doesn't seem to be very helpful here. I'll choose last.,

The expedition members' eyes became uneasy.

,Can't we all move together?

,You all know that it's safer to act according to the rules in this kind of ancient ruin. I think we'll rejoin later. Don't think too seriously about it.,

The expedition members divided in half after about 30 minutes of discussion. Professor Hemmington also spoke to the mercenaries.

,You guys should talk and divide the personnel.,

,.......

The mercenaries frowned. But they couldn't refuse. Guarding the expedition was the job of the mercenaries.

,I'm going to the right.,

I said immediately. The right path was the way to live.

,Right.,

,...Are you telling me to go to the left? Oh dear? My gut feeling is also to the right.,

Jin smiled.

I clicked my tongue inwardly and looked at Jin and Josh.

,It doesn't seem like opinions will be easily coordinated. What are you going to do?

Pajijik. Electricity crackled along my right hand. Perhaps my intention to fight was conveyed, as their eyes also became heavy.

,Magician. Your attitude has been a bit off since yesterday... Do you know something?
