District 20, the Plains of Tranquility, is a tranquil place as its name suggests. The district itself is quite large, but there's nothing there except grass and large rocks.

And it's a place where animals don't live.

,If you dig into the ground, you might find worms or something....'

Neither beasts nor monsters exist. And the same goes for people. There might be merchants passing through here, but no one makes it a base to build a house and live. That's because the ruler of this place doesn't want it.

,The identity of the ruler of District 20 isn't known... but they're probably a member of Helten. Because the one who commissioned me to come here is Helten.'

I walked aimlessly across the plains. They wouldn't have used Pesica to commission me just to screw me over.

,If I keep walking, something will come up.'

My thought was right on. But what came out was unexpected.

"This way."

It was a raccoon.

A raccoon with gray fur was waving one of its short front paws at me from the right, about 10 meters away.

I narrowed my eyes. Did that raccoon just talk to me?

,Even if this is a fantasy world, that's a bit much.... Is someone else hiding nearby?'

I looked around, but not even a shadow of a person was visible.

"This way, you clown."

The raccoon said, moving its mouth. Listening again, its voice was thicker than I thought. I had no choice but to admit that the raccoon had spoken.

"......What are you? I've never heard of a talking raccoon."

"I'm a raccoon, you moron."

I held back the urge to punch it and approached the raccoon.

,...Is it really a talking raccoon? Or is someone controlling it with some special skill?'

There were too many possibilities, making it difficult to guess.

As I approached, the raccoon moved away, keeping its distance. It seemed to be telling me to follow, so I silently followed behind it.

"Raccoon. Where are we going?"

"Shut up and follow me. You'll find out if you follow. And I'm a raccoon."

"......Are you a member of Helten?"

"You're probably guessing."

No. I'm asking because I can't guess.

The author who wrote [God's Atlantis] didn't set up everything about Helten. They only set up some of the executives who appear in the work, so there are Helten executives whose identities I don't know.

,Judging by this raccoon's arrogant attitude... it seems like it has a certain status even within Helten.'

As I was thinking this and that, I arrived in front of a rock. A flat rock over 4 meters wide was lying haphazardly on the plain.

"Why here?"

"You're so stupid. You have no taste."

The urge to kick the raccoon shot up, but I barely held it back.

The raccoon stood up on its two legs and swung its front paw at the rock. Its front paw didn't hit the rock but was buried inside.

I realized immediately. The rock in front of me is a kind of camouflage. It's camouflaging the entrance to another space with a rock, using magic or a skill.

"This is...."

"It's annoying to explain. There are no traps or anything, so just follow me. If I was going to kill you, I wouldn't have done something so cumbersome from the start."

The raccoon went into the rock. I followed the raccoon and stretched my foot into the rock. After confirming that my foot went in, I went inside.

「You have entered District 3,709, Star Judgment.」

「This is a hidden district.」

「In District 3,709, truth and lies are judged.」

「The penalty for lies has been lifted due to the ruler's settings.」

「Due to the ruler's special settings, Seats other than the Contract Seat cannot see the situation here.」

「Due to the ruler's special settings, you cannot leave the district by spatial movement, including scrolls.」

,A hidden district.... There was a place like this.'

The scenery didn't change much. A night sky, a plain full of grass. The cool air I felt. It was the same as before I came inside.

However, there was a rocky area a little further away.

"Don't space out and follow me!"

I follow the raccoon and walk towards the rocky area. The rocks were similar to the famous Stonehenge of modern times.

There were a total of 5 figures in the rocky area. It was difficult to identify their identities because they were each wearing masks and covering their bodies with cloaks.

"I brought him!"

The raccoon said that and approached a man sitting with his back against a rock and snuggled into his arms. The man was bowing his head as if he was asleep. He was wearing a black cloak, so his face wasn't visible.

"Good job, Raccoon."

It was a young man's voice. I turned my gaze to where the voice came from. A man was sitting on a rock in the center of this place. Wearing the leather clothes that exiles commonly wear, he was wearing an octagonal mask on his face.

It's a strange mask with no eyes, nose, or mouth. It's a mask that seems like you can't see anything in front of you, but he's looking straight at me.

"Nice to meet you, Cheonma. I'm the leader of Helten."

「It is the truth.」

The system notified me. It's because of the characteristics of this district. Judgment of truth and lies. That includes the words you say. In other words, if you lie here, you'll be caught right away.

I felt cold sweat flowing under my clown mask.

,...I never thought I'd meet the leader of Helten like this. I thought I'd only meet an executive at best....'

That's not all. The others are also unusual. Perhaps the people gathered here are Helten's executives.

"I am Cheonma."

I said.

Is speaking informally rude? These guys don't care much about that.

「It is the truth.」

The system popped up. It informed them that what I said was true.

"I heard from Pesica. You want to join Helten? What's your purpose?"

The notification window notified me again that it was the truth. But blindly believing the system's notification window is a hasty judgment. If the other person is the ruler of this district, they can change the settings of this district to some extent.

"I thought I could get more benefits by joining Helten."

"What kind of benefits? Money?"

"Power. My purpose is to get stronger by using Helten."

「It is the truth.」

It judges truth and lies. It can't find even the words that are deliberately hidden. If you pay attention to this point, you can use it enough.

"You're honest. Well, what we do is a bit illegal, but it's the best way to build power. But the purpose of our Helten is not to gather together and get stronger."

"I know. Even if I want power, I won't do anything like selling you guys out."

"Ah. You know. You know what our purpose is. How?"

In an instant, I felt the gazes towards me sharpen. Among them, only the leader of Helten was interested in me, but that changed with the statement just now. In addition to the leader of Helten, two executives are showing interest in me.

"Helten is the most famous criminal organization in Atlantis. I was intrigued, so I did some research."

「It is the truth.」

The research they understand and the research I'm talking about are different. I met the author who wrote [God's Atlantis] and researched various things. I also already know Helten's purpose through the original work.

"It's the truth.... You're more capable than I thought."

The man on the leader's left said.

He was a man wearing a mask studded with jewels and wearing the clothes of a wealthy merchant.

"Until just now, your value was only about 4 million Penny, but with that statement, it has risen to 80 million Penny."

"It's gone up 20 times. Still, it's quite stingy. Shouldn't I be worth at least 1 billion Penny?"

"That may be the case in the future. But your current value is 80 million Penny. There is a fatal problem with the judgment of truth and lies in this district. It's subjective. You may be mistaking our purpose for something completely trivial. So, on behalf of the boss, I'll ask again. What exactly is Helten's purpose?"

I pondered for a moment.

,Can I say this? ...No. The water's already spilled. If I don't say it, I'll be suspected in another way. Then rather....'

From their point of view, I'm weak. If I fought one of them right now, there's a high chance I'd die in less than 10 seconds. So I need to appeal to them about my specialness.

"Your purpose is....... To prevent the next Atlantis from opening. Isn't it?"

"...Oh."

The jewel mask let out a small exclamation.

"How did you know? Pesica doesn't even know. Only we know that, and the lower ranks don't know at all.... Is there a traitor among us?"

"I figured it out on my own. I can't tell you the details.... No one among you told me that. Of course, that includes Helten's executives who aren't here."

「It is the truth.」

"Indeed. It's understandable why the boss is interested in you. I'll raise your value to 1 billion. Do you know what level 1 billion is? It's a level of value that should be killed as quickly as possible."

「It is the truth.」

"That's a very kind assessment."

「It is a lie.」

"Cheonma."

This time, the person on the leader's right called me in a androgynous voice. It was a man wearing a cracked lion mask. He was holding a spear in his right hand.

"Are you going to betray us, Helten?"

"No. If you don't get in my way and don't treat me unfairly, I can cooperate. Even if that means killing a Seat."

「It is the truth.」

「The King of the Demonic Sky is giggling.」

「The Lord of the Sky is laughing at you.」

The Seats laughed at me rather than getting angry. It's the reaction I expected. From their point of view, a human saying they're going to kill them is not only rude but also looks like a comedy. That's because it's much closer to impossible than a human becoming a god.

"That word..., I like it."

The man wearing the lion mask said that and lay down on a nearby rock.

"Sparkles and Lion seem to be interested in you."

The leader said. Sparkles and Lion are a kind of nickname. You can think of it as similar to a code name. They're hiding their identities by wearing masks, so they can't use their real names.

"Then. Am I a member of Helten from now on?"

"That's right. But you can't become an executive. You're too weak for that, and you haven't done anything for Helten. If you want to become an executive of Helten, build up your strength from the bottom and work for Helten. If you reach a certain level... I and others will recognize you."

「It is the truth.」

I felt a question.

The reason why he, the leader of Helten, bothered to meet me in person. If he was going to make me a member of Helten's lower ranks, he wouldn't have met me directly like this. Because Helten's lower ranks are ultimately those who become disposable pawns.

"You seem to be wondering why we called you here directly. Why do you think we called you here? One, as a hint, I'm the only one who's interested in something about you. The others here are just here because they're interested in me calling you."

,...Is this also a kind of test?'

I thought for a moment.

What could the leader be interested in about me?

Successfully bringing the branch of the Seven-Branched Sword through Pesica's request? Acting with a clown mask, saying I wanted to join Helten? Moving under the name of Cheonma?

,There's no way Helten would be interested in that.'

Let's trace back my memories for a moment. There was one thing.

A reason why the leader would be interested in me.

I had definitely told Pesica before. And Pesica would have reported to Helten.

"...The Seat I contracted with. It's because of the King of the Demonic Sky."

"That's right."

「It is the truth.」

"The King of the Demonic Sky is a unique Seat even among the top Seats. The King of the Demonic Sky doesn't contract with just anyone."

「The King of the Demonic Sky is tilting his head.」

The King of the Demonic Sky seems puzzled, but it's not strange that the leader knows about the King of the Demonic Sky.

He knows what happened from the 1st to the 7th Atlantis through his Hunter ability.

"Do you know about the identity of the King of the Demonic Sky?"

"I know. Judging by that reaction, you don't seem to know."

「It is the truth.」

"What is the identity of the King of the Demonic Sky?"

"That's.... I can't tell you. I don't want to turn the King of the Demonic Sky into an enemy. And even if I don't tell you, you'll find out his identity soon enough."

「It is the truth.」

"......."

I frowned. In the end, I couldn't find out the identity of the King of the Demonic Sky. I thought about being stubborn, but I stopped.

"Then is our business over? What should I do now?"
