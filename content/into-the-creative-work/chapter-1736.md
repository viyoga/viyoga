,Eueung?"

Seong Hari was flustered upon entering the dungeon.

She wasn't surprised that the entrance disappeared as soon as she entered the dungeon. She knew she would naturally be able to exit once she cleared the dungeon. What perplexed her was the size of the dungeon.

She had entered countless dungeons before, but this was the first time she had seen one so vast. The sky was high, and the land and mountains before her stretched out endlessly.

,I heard it was a B-grade dungeon... Was I tricked?"

She sighed as if she had a headache. Recently, she had been tricked like this a few times. It was a ploy by those trying to take advantage of her current situation.

,Things have been quiet lately, so they must think I'm easy to take advantage of. I'll have to raise hell when I get out of this dungeon."

She slung her spear over her shoulder and walked forward. If she killed every monster she saw, she would eventually be able to exit the dungeon.

However, contrary to her expectations, what she encountered wasn't monsters.

People.

Five men and women wearing matching black uniforms surrounded Seong Hari in an instant. Seong Hari was taken aback. They looked like students in their black uniforms, but they were all armed with equipment. Their appearance wasn't entirely unfamiliar.

,An academy? It's not Maruhan Academy's uniform, though...."

The man in the center of them opened his mouth. Unfortunately, Seong Hari couldn't understand what he was saying. It was Japanese, not Korean. She had been to Japan a few times for work, but she had never formally studied Japanese.

,...Judging by his sharp voice, it's probably not good news."

The men were looking at her with lecherous eyes, and the women were staring at her with contempt. Well, she was used to that. She was currently wearing jeans, a tank top, and a coat. Her abs and exposed skin were probably attracting the men's attention.

Of course, just because she was used to it didn't mean she liked it.

,I'm sorry, but I don't speak Japanese. I don't know why you guys are in this dungeon, but... I need to do my job, so can you please move?"

Seong Hari said cautiously.

Just by looking at them, they seemed younger than her. Judging by their uniforms, they were likely academy students from Japan.

The problem was that the other party didn't understand Korean at all.

,Should I try English? ...What should I say in English? Ah. I wish I had a translator."

It was a truly difficult situation. Just as she let out a deep sigh, the man's voice sharpened. At the same time, hostility and murderous intent erupted from all directions.

,No, even so, you can't just attack right away?!"

Seong Hari kicked off the ground and jumped upwards. A sword fell where she had been standing. It didn't end there. A flame magic spell shaped like a noose closely pursued her. Seong Hari created a force field in the air to create distance and swung her spear to eliminate the magic.

,They don't hesitate at all when attacking people. They're nasty bastards."

Perhaps wearing uniforms was just a way to make people lower their guard.

She tightened her grip on the spear in her hand. She couldn't just let it go since she was attacked first.

She swung the spear once. The wind became a slash and swept through the surroundings. With a single strike, the area was devastated, and two of the men and women nearby were cut down and killed.

,W-What?"

Seong Hari, who had attacked, was flustered. She had only swung the spear for the purpose of checking them. But two people had died.

,You guys are too weak."

But why are they doing this?

Furthermore, there was something else she couldn't understand. Even after seeing her power, they weren't afraid. They didn't care at all about the death of their comrades and attacked her with coldness.

Had they finished their preparations for death? No, it wasn't that kind of feeling. It was more like they didn't care about death itself.

,Weirdos."

Seong Hari jumped high into the air and threw her spear at the ground. The spear fell like a meteor, causing a shockwave. The enemies, struck by the shockwave containing lightning, couldn't withstand it and were annihilated. And their corpses turned to ash and disappeared.

,...What the hell?"

She had won, but she felt uneasy. It wasn't just because the opponent was too weak.

,I think I've entered a strange dungeon...."

She felt strangely anxious.

It had already been 50 days since I came to this world.

That meant a day had passed in real time. Seong Hari and I had become quite accustomed to it. However, we still hadn't found Susanoo's Magatama. We were heading towards the place indicated by the mark on the back of my hand, but there was no end in sight.

Now I was starting to doubt if we were even going in the right direction. But there was no other way, so there was nothing I could do.

,There's a limit to how far I can go forward."

The place the mark was pointing to was beyond an area called the Forest of Bewilderment. The problem was that the condition for entering this Forest of Bewilderment was to be level 70 or higher. Thanks to that, Seong Hari and I were focusing on leveling up by repeating monster hunts and quests.

「Name: Hasegawa Sota

Affiliation: Caso Academy, 3rd Year, Class A

Level: 68

Job: Spirit User

Sub-Job Level: 41

Sub-Job: Priest」

,I only need to raise it by 2 levels."

It would take 2-3 days.

Seong Hari and I entered the cafe and sat in our usual corner. We ordered our usual coffee and desserts and started talking.

「The time of the apocalypse has been delayed.」

「Time remaining until the apocalypse: 94 days.」

,Looks like someone else cleared an apocalypse quest."

Seong Hari said, looking at the notification window.

It wasn't just Seong Hari and I who were trapped in the virtual reality. There were about 200 students who were connected to the virtual reality with us. They were more experienced in virtual reality than we were, and they were working hard to survive by going around and doing quests.

,They wouldn't want to die trapped in their capsules, so they're probably working hard."

,Shouldn't we help them too?"

,Do you think they'd accept us?"

The students had their own community. We couldn't enter that community. From their perspective, we were outsiders. Moreover, the Terra Simulator had become strange on the day we arrived, so we were even suspected of being the cause. The suspicion that we were the cause of all this.

In short, it was bullying.

A Japanese tradition.

Furthermore, they had heard Seong Hari and I talking in Korean, so the rumor that we were Zainichi Koreans had spread like wildfire. All the elements that made us easy targets for bullying had come together.

,Hasegawa!!"

Someone shouted as soon as they entered the cafe. Seong Hari and I looked at the entrance.

Kikugawa Keiichi, with his pointed chin and narrow eyes, glared at us as if he wanted to kill us. His eyes were full of anger towards us. Especially the way he looked at Seong Hari was unusual.

,I told you last time. Don't come to our hunting grounds! But not only did you come to our hunting grounds, but you also attacked us?!"

The bastards approached, huffing and puffing. They were full of hostility and murderous intent. Seong Hari and I stood up from our seats and prepared for battle.

I left Seong Hari, who didn't know Japanese, behind me and said to them.

,What nonsense are you talking about? We've never been to your area."

,That woman! Your sister killed us! She was showing off her power! I don't know where you got that item, but you guys broke the rules first!"

They drew their weapons. I lowered my voice and growled at them.

,I don't know what you saw, but we were doing a quest. We didn't kill you. And if we had killed you, would we have been here at this cafe before you? That doesn't make sense logically."

,Maybe you used a portal scroll!"

,I've never even heard of such an item! Don't go picking on innocent people and get lost!"

I pointed my sword at the bastard. When Seong Hari even drew her sword, the bastards flinched. They were especially wary of Seong Hari.

,You shitty Joseon bastard! Are you going to be so brazen about this?! "

,We said it wasn't us. I don't know who you were attacked by, but you shouldn't be doing such shitty propaganda. Just because we're staying quiet doesn't mean you can look down on us, got it? If we wanted to, you guys would be nothing."

,Your attitude right now is no different from insulting us. You should be groveling on the ground, but you dare threaten us? Do you think you'll be safe after this?!"

,What, are you going to attack? If you're going to attack, hurry up and do it."

I flicked my sword and provoked the bastard. But they didn't rush in right away. They were watching Seong Hari's reaction, wondering whether to fight or not. Then they retreated.

,...I'll remember this. Don't forget that just because we're retreating now doesn't mean it's over."

The bastard growled once and disappeared with his group.

Seong Hari and I sheathed our weapons.

,Why are those guys like that?"

,I don't know. They said we attacked them. Did you see how they were looking at mom earlier? It seems like they think they got properly beaten by mom. Did you do something?"

,I've been with you the whole time. What could I have done?"

Seong Hari shook her head as if she couldn't understand.

I couldn't understand it either. If they were lying to start a fight, I could understand it. Because that's just their usual bullying. But this time it was different.

,It didn't seem like they were lying."

That's why it was even stranger.

,I want to kill them all, but...."

Seong Hari wouldn't allow it. And even if I killed them, it wouldn't mean much. They would just revive in the village. Keep killing the guys I killed? I didn't have time for that. Seong Hari and I had to find Susanoo's Magatama.

,Mom. It's better to be careful of those guys for the time being."

,Yeah. I think so too. It seems like the kids are a bit on edge because they're trapped here."
