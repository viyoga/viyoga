Kugugugugugung.

Our steps, which had been trudging along the mountain range, halted. The ground was shaking. Not just shaking. Something was in the ground.

I lowered my gaze to my feet. Activating the Eye of Heaven, I peered into the earth. A massive monster resembling an earthworm was rising toward us.

"Yuria, Elena! It's coming from underground!!"

Elena used magic. The weight of our bodies suddenly lightened, and we floated into the air. A few seconds later, a huge earthworm-like monster surged upwards from underground. The creature, with skin that looked as hard as rock, opened its mouth wide to devour us. But its mouth never reached us. Its body plummeted downwards.

'Thunderbolt!'

Kwakwakwang!

Lightning fell from the sky, coursing through the monster's elongated body. It had an effect. The creature staggered.

Wiiiiiiiiing! Wiiiiiiiiiiing!

Something fell from its rocky body. Looking closely, it was a beetle-like bug. Not an ordinary bug, of course. It was a monster. Looking closer, I could see saw-like teeth rotating in its mouth.

"Bugs! Disgusting just to look at! I'll burn them all!"

Pajijijijijijik.

I scattered lightning towards the bugs. Struck by the current, the bugs fell to the ground with a slightly nutty smell.

Kugugugugugung!

The ground shook. I glanced sideways at the ground below. The giant rock earthworm was circling around before surging upwards. It was still targeting us.

"That is an impudent bug. It is only stirring up dust. I will take care of it."

Yuria descended. In a shuk moment, she had reached the ground. With my current skills, it was impossible to perfectly grasp Yuria's movements.

The rock earthworm opened its mouth and lunged at Yuria, who had landed on the ground. Yuria drew her dagger. The moment I felt her body stretch forward, it was already over. The huge earthworm was split in half, dyeing the ground with its grotesque entrails and red blood.

I dealt with the bugs and landed on the ground. I was about to tell Yuria she did a good job.

Kugugugugugung!

The ground began to rumble.

It seemed there wasn't just one earthworm.

After wandering around the Heversuite Mountains for three days, we found the remains of the Dragon Lord.

It was underground in a certain location. Looking around, the area was dug up here and there. It didn't feel artificial, but rather like the traces of the giant rock earthworms rampaging. It seemed it was originally on the ground but had fallen underground.

'Are these the remains of the Dragon Lord?'

The remains of the Dragon Lord that I saw directly looked like hard rock. You had to look closely to recognize it as a dragon.

'If it looked like real dragon bones, people would have recognized it long ago.'

I approached the rock, made a fist, and knocked on it. Tung-tung, the sound echoed back from the inside. It meant the inside was empty.

This time, I put strength into my fist and swung.

Kwaaang!

The rock shattered, revealing the Dragon Lord's bones hidden inside. I created lightning at my fingertips. The dark space was illuminated. Perhaps due to the long years, the Dragon Lord's bones were discolored to a yellowish hue.

'There's no heart here. Is it somewhere else?'

There were a total of seven rocks, including this one. The heart must be in the remaining six. I could move leisurely. Time was on my side.

I checked the second and third remains as well. There was no heart.

"Juin-nim. Uninvited guests have arrived."

Yuria said while I was searching for the fourth remains.

"Uninvited guests?"

Following Yuria's gaze, I looked outside. There were three men. Two of them, except for one, were people I knew.

"Kyle and Terius...."

Black hair, black eyes, a face like a nobleman. Kyle hadn't changed much from when I saw him before.

On the other hand, Terius had changed. His severed arm was reattached, and his entire body was muscular, with a height exceeding 2M. His muscles were so hard that he momentarily looked like a golem made of rock rather than a human.

'And the flamboyant man with blonde hair and blue eyes next to them... is that Olrond Geulbet Baron I heard about in the reports?'

Olrond Geulbet Baron. He became Kyle's subordinate a few months ago, and I received reports that Kyle almost always took Geulbet with him.

They also seemed to have noticed us, as they paused.

I raised my hand and waved to Kyle for now. Kyle and I are brothers. We are currently maintaining a not-so-bad relationship. Kyle also recognizes me as his younger brother, so he will face me and wave back.

"......."

Kyle didn't react. His atmosphere was different from usual. Kyle looked back and forth between me and Yuria before drawing his sword.

"Kyle hyung?!"

"......."

I called out to Kyle. Kyle remained silent.

I felt my head growing cold. This situation, wasn't it the perfect situation for an assassination?

'Kyle is trying to kill me here? Not Jent, but Kyle?'

Given Kyle's righteous personality, assassination was impossible. Unless I was his enemy. I am a competitor vying for the successor position, and a brother. I am by no means his enemy.

'Did Kyle change as the original story changed?'

It was entirely possible.

I clicked my tongue. My plan to use Kyle had hit a snag.

"Eugene Prukerseuueueueue!!!!"

There was a guy shouting my name. It was Terius. The arm cripple was rushing at me, shouting my name with a voice mixed with hatred and anger. In both of his hands, he held a large greatsword.

"Yuria, Elena. Don't kill them. We need to find out what happened."

"Understood."

"Is there a need for you and I to step in? Yuria alone seems sufficient."

I couldn't deny Elena's words. Yuria is currently a top-tier Aura Master. She is more than capable of sweeping away the enemies alone.

Yuria stepped forward. She was relaxed.

"Aaaaaaaaargh!! Eugene Prukerseu!! I'll kill you!! I'll kill you and save Nephia!!"

Terius, who was running, fell forward. His ankle was grabbed by a shadow hand. Terius, who had fallen spectacularly, rolled down the sloping ground. Terius, who had suffered minor injuries all over his body, jumped up from his spot.

Pak!

Yuria's turning back kick struck Terius's occiput. Terius lost consciousness and fell straight over. Then, shadow chains appeared and wrapped around Terius's body. Terius's suppression was complete.

"Yuria. You are strong. I thought you were not ordinary... I never knew you were hiding such power."

Kyle said as he approached Yuria. Kyle's gaze and atmosphere towards Yuria were wistful. Kyle seemed to be under some terrible delusion.

"There is no particular reason to hide my strength now. Kyle-nim seems to have changed quite a bit since I last saw you."

"Yuria. You are being deceived by Eugene."

Kyle said abruptly.

"...Yes?"

Yuria tilted her head. I was just as puzzled. Was there something I was hiding from Yuria? There were quite a few things I hadn't said, but there was nothing I was deliberately hiding. Yuria is the one I trust the most.

"Eugene is using you. Look even now. He is putting you in front to block me. He will surely harass you at night too. Step aside, Yuria. I will kill Eugene and save you."

"...Kyle-nim. You seem to be mistaken about many things. I have dedicated everything to my Ju-in-nim. It is just for Ju-in-nim to use me. Even if Ju-in-nim deceives me, it does not matter. I have already given my body and soul to Ju-in-nim."

"...As Geulbet Baron said, you seem to be thoroughly deceived by Eugene. It's okay, don't worry! I will save you!"

Kyle tried to pass by Yuria and target me. Red aura rose from his sword. The aura gathered on the sword, becoming an aura blade. His aura blade was as red as a plum blossom.

Yuria threw a dagger at Kyle. Kyle, startled, held his sword sideways. Kkaang! The dagger and his sword collided. The deflected dagger was embedded in the ground, and Kyle's body was pushed back.

"To think you would have such power...! Yuria! Step aside! I don't want to fight you!"

"Kyle-nim. Something seems strange. There are more than one or two things that don't add up. Did you perhaps injure your head? If not... did someone tell you something strange?"

"You are the strange one, Yuria! Why are you following Eugene? Eugene is just using you! Listen to me!"

Yuria sighed. Rarely, her expression was tinged with annoyance. It seemed she didn't like dealing with Kyle himself.

"Kyle-nim. There is no need to package your feelings with nonsensical words. Just say honestly that you want to have me. Your feelings for me and your jealousy towards Ju-in-nim are clearly visible."

"N-no. I am for you...!"

"I have never thought of Kyle-nim as special. Rather, you were annoying to deal with. Yes, if it weren't for Ju-in-nim's orders, I wouldn't have even wanted to deal with Kyle-nim."

Kyle faltered at Yuria's words. Then, he suddenly turned his head towards me. Kyle's murderous intent was directed at me. A chill ran down my spine from the murderous intent of an Aura Master.

"You are overstepping your bounds."

A cold voice rang out. Immediately after, Kyle's body trembled. Kyle looked at Yuria with eyes filled with a slight fear.

"Yuria. Don't you want freedom? I can kill Eugene and give you freedom."

"Freedom without Ju-in-nim... The thought alone is horrifying."

"Hoo.... Yuria. I'll confess honestly. I... Yuria, I love you. I want you to be happy. I will make you happy."

"I hate Kyle-nim. If it weren't for Ju-in-nim's orders, I would have killed you... How unfortunate."

"Y-you hate me? Th-that can't be. You, you have been waiting for me."

Kyle was agitated.

Geulbet Baron shouted from afar.

"Kyle-nim!! Get a grip!! Yuria is being brainwashed by Eugene! We must kill Eugene and undo Yuria's brainwashing! Kill Eugene!!"

"Geulbet Baron.... The Baron is right. I will kill Eugene and undo Yuria's brainwashing."

Kyle rushes towards me. And he was hit by Yuria's kick and flew backwards, rolling on the ground. Yuria looked at the unconscious Kyle emotionlessly before shifting her gaze to Geulbet Baron.

"I can feel the power of a demon in your voice. Did you bewitch people with the power of a demon?"

"Bewitch people? What a terrible insult. I have only given appropriate advice as Kyle-nim's military advisor!"

"Your voice is truly disgusting. First, I will deal with...."

Yuria couldn't finish her words and hastily swung her dagger. A plum blossom flying towards her shattered with a clang.

"Yuria.... If you continue to interfere, I will defeat you and kill Eugene. That would be the best course of action."

Kyle's skin turned purple. His pupils turned red. No matter how I looked at it, he was closer to a demon than a martial artist.

'Demonic Possession...? Kyle has fallen into a state of Demonic Possession?'

Purple plum blossoms bloomed around Kyle.

The plum blossoms made of spirit energy rotated, scattering their petals. Yuria blocked the plum blossoms one by one with her dagger and shadows. The scent of the plum blossoms was toxic.

"Yuria! I will! Kill Eugene and save you!!"

Yuria was expressionless. Her dagger shattered Kyle's sword, which was drawing plum blossoms. Shadow chains grabbed Kyle's legs, and small throwing knives thrown from Yuria's hand were embedded in Kyle's shoulder and arm. Kyle lost his balance and collapsed. Yuria stepped on Kyle's head. Kwang! Kyle fainted on the spot.

Yuria left the unconscious Kyle behind and ran towards Geulbet Baron. The terrified Geulbet Baron turned around and tried to run away, but he was caught before he could even go 10M.
