"We're here!"

Makina shouted cheerfully from the boat's cockpit. The boat stopped. Next to it, the tail of a giant great white shark, reaching 20m, smacked the surface of the water, pang, pang. Makina was remotely controlling the shark.

I looked around.

The open sea.

All I could see was the gently waving ocean.

'Is this right? ...Well, it must be. Makina may seem like this, but she's a machine spirit.'

Her personality is a pain in the ass, and she's disgustingly obsessed with food, but her calculation abilities rival a supercomputer. No, if she really put her mind to using her abilities properly, she'd be even better.

I moved to the boat's railing and prepared to dive.

"I'm going too!"

Makina dove right into the shark's head. A steel cover enveloped the shark's head.

"Please, just don't get in the way."

Splash!

I jumped into the sea and descended. A huge shark overtook me and went down.

[I'm faster!]

'I'm going crazy.'

Makina didn't have an ounce of seriousness. She was probably just having fun right now.

I went further down.

At some point, I couldn't see any light. It was pitch black. I couldn't see anything, but I could feel the coldness of the water and the sense of floating. It felt like being in the middle of space.

'Spirit Eye, Heaven's Eye.'

I pierced through the darkness. There were fish all around me. They were swimming in a clockwise direction around me, as if guarding me.

'It's the same as the first playthrough. I came to the right place.'

I descended even further.

[Ah, no! My Jaws! Help me! Seong Yujin!!]

Makina's telepathic thoughts scrambled my brain. I stopped descending and looked at the fish next to me, pretending to be distracted. It was a rather bizarre-looking fish, probably because it was so deep down.

[Nooooo!]

Ignoring Makina's repeated screams, I descended into the depths.

There was a pit. Blood and pieces of flesh spurted out from the dark pit. A shark eyeball, bigger than my head, passed by me.

Makina popped out of the pit. Finding me, Makina awkwardly swam towards me.

[Yujin-ah! Jaws is dead...! Avenge him!]

I nodded and grabbed Makina by the neck.

[Khek?! W-What are you doing...?!]

'You told me to avenge him. You're the one who killed the shark.'

[N-No! That guy killed Jaws! Let go!]

Makina bit my hand. She put quite a bit of force into it, so I had no choice but to let go. I clicked my tongue and looked towards the pit.

A king crab popped out of the pit. It was huge. Big enough to tear apart a monster shark that was 20m long. The monster crab with its sharp carapace blew bubbles from its mouth as it looked at me and Makina.

[Eek!]

Makina hid behind me.

I summoned Stormbreaker and transformed it into a giant harpoon.

'Makina.'

I sent my will to Makina.

[A-Alright. Is this how you do it?]

Makina's power was imbued into the harpoon. The shape of the harpoon gradually changed into a harpoon gun. I generated lightning on the harpoon gun. The electricity became the power source. I pulled the trigger and fired the harpoon gun. The harpoon shot out like a torpedo.

The harpoon accurately struck the crab's right eye. The harpoon exploded. The electric current spread in all directions, brightly illuminating the dark sea.

[Did we get him?!]

Makina's telepathic thought was conveyed to me as she rested her chin on my left shoulder. It was Makina, who was deeply immersed in the internet. There was no way she wouldn't know the meaning of that line, so she must have said it on purpose.

'Should I just bury it at the bottom of the deep sea this time?'

Of course, to do that, I had to get rid of the giant crab in front of me first.

The crab, having lost one eye, crawled out of the pit in anger. It was the size of a building.

[B-Be careful of those pincers! Jaws was done in by those pincers!]

The giant crab approached me. Each time its large legs touched the ground, dust billowed up and the currents surged.

The crab extended its pincers towards me. The speed was surprisingly slow.

[Dodge!!]

'Shut up. Your telepathic thoughts are too loud. Are you trying to screw me over on purpose?'

[No! That's really dangerous! That crab's ability is...!]

'I know.'

The currents generated by the giant crab's movement began to organize themselves. The currents blocked my surroundings like a prison. It wasn't easy to move my body because of the intensely moving currents.

[I got caught by that too! That's cheating!]

The currents tightened, restraining my body. The giant pincers slowly approached, trying to cut my waist.

'Heavenly Heart.'

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The currents couldn't bind me. I approached the giant crab. I reverse-summoned Stormbreaker and then summoned it back into my hand.

'Makina. Possession.'

[Okay!]

Makina possessed Stormbreaker. Stormbreaker's shape changed into a drill. The drill stuck to my right hand. The drill rotated using my mana as its power source.

The drill pierced through its belly.

[Drill through it!!]

The crab moved back and forth. The currents tried to grab me, but they couldn't because of the effect of Heavenly Heart.

'20 seconds left?'

I went into the hole made by the drill. I didn't like it, but I had no choice. This was the surest way. The drill stuck to my right hand came out.

'Stormbreaker, divide.'

Stormbreaker's unique ability activated, and the drill split into 6.

'Makina, can you control them?'

[It feels like my body has increased to 6! But I can control them all even if it increases to 100!]

'Do I have to explain what you need to do too?'

[No! I have some sense too! Here I go! This is for Jaws!!]

The 6 drills tore through the crab's insides. The moving crab stopped. I stood still, listening to the sound of the drills echoing in my head.

The crab's insides became a mess. The crab didn't move anymore.

I came out. It felt refreshing to come out after being inside the intestines. It would be even more refreshing to get out of the sea.

[How's the taste of the drill!! You enemy of Jaws!!]

I left Makina, who was mocking the giant crab's corpse, alone and entered the pit.

The pit, which was the giant crab's nest, was very large and dirty. The bones and flesh of monsters were scattered randomly on the floor. I looked around and moved towards one place.

A luxurious-looking blue spear was embedded in the center of a giant sea serpent's skeleton. I grabbed the spear and pulled it out of the skeleton.

『Blue Dragon Spear

Rank: C

Summons water.

Sturdy.』

It was similar to the Vermilion Bird Sword. You can enter the trial dungeon again using the Blue Dragon Spear.

'I can go in with Ryu Hana. The enemies will appear on their own in front of Ryu Hana, who has the Vermilion Bird's mark.'

The problem was that you could only open the dungeon if you were recognized by the Blue Dragon Spear or if you forcibly awakened the Blue Dragon Spear.

'Thinking about forcibly awakening it... Hooo. It's such a pain in the ass.'

I felt presences gathering around me. I shined my Spirit Eye and looked around. Horseshoe crab-like bugs were gathering around me. They were probably the ones stealing and eating the flesh of the corpses scattered here.

'They must think I'm easy. I guess I'm a lot smaller compared to the monster crab.'

I infused mana into the Blue Dragon Spear and swung it around. A giant current rose and crushed the bugs. The water quality got even worse because of the bugs' corpses.

'I want to get out of here quickly. ...Hmm?'

An unexpected notification window popped up in front of me.

『You are qualified to challenge the trial. Would you like to begin the Blue Dragon's trial?』

It was a notification window that I had never seen in the first playthrough. That's why I forcibly awakened it and used it.

'...It's popping up? What's different between the first playthrough and now?'

I frowned.

There were too many differences.

It probably wasn't the influence of the traits. Spirit Eye only has a big influence on spirits. The Blue Dragon Spear has nothing to do with spirits.

There were no skills or traits related to the Blue Dragon Spear.

『Name: Seong Yujin

Strength: B- Stamina: B Agility: B- Durability: C+ Mana: A

Traits: Spirit Eye (S) Demon Hunter (S)

Skills: Spirit Contract (A) Spirit Possession (A) Barrier (C+) Swordsmanship (B+)

Karma: Good 26』

'...Karma. This is the only thing I can guess. It must be that it didn't react because my karma was evil in the first playthrough.'

Having roughly understood, I got out of the pit.

Makina, who was venting her anger by making holes in the giant crab's corpse, released the possession and followed behind me.

[Did you come all the way here to get that blue spear? Ah! Is it a present for Auntie?!]

'It's not.'

[It's not? Auntie will be disappointed. You do know when Auntie's birthday is, right?]

'.......'

Makina was chattering noisily.

•••

Sreureung.

I drew my sword.

Ryu Hana, who was facing me, looked at me with cold eyes.

Two weeks had passed since that day, when Ryu Hana fought herself in the dungeon and lost. I had sparred with her more than 100 times so far, and I had won every time. This was because I was ahead in terms of physical ability and experience.

And today was the day before the trial. I thought I might lose this sparring match.

'...If I use special items or skills like Heavenly Heart... I'd win, but... if I only consider pure skill... I won't be able to win easily.'

I didn't expect Ryu Hana to grow this quickly.

"...I'll start."

Ryu Hana muttered. She, who was holding swords in both hands, rushed towards me. She wasn't fast. But there was a profoundness in her steps.

'There's no need to deliberately drag out the sparring. Let's end it quickly.'

Yeongcheon Style Lightning Flash.

A strike imbued with lightning aimed for Ryu Hana's arm. Ryu Hana didn't even twitch an eyebrow and crossed her twin swords to block the strike. Ryu Hana was pushed back by the force. Her profound footwork dissipated the force. She had realized the principle of flow while sparring with me over the past few days.

Yeongcheon Style Azure Stream.

I used the technique that had earned me more than 30 victories against Ryu Hana. I used footwork to deceive Ryu Hana's sense of distance.

Ryu Hana's method of breaking through Azure Stream was simple. She extended the swords in both hands and spun around like a top. I couldn't get close.

I quietly aimed for the moment when her rotation stopped and stabbed with my sword.

Yeongcheon Style Lightning Snake.

The sword curved like a snake and aimed for Ryu Hana's neck. Ryu Hana reacted as if she had been waiting for it. She knocked away my sword with her right sword and swung her left sword at my shoulder.

I used an instant to dodge the sword strike. Ryu Hana didn't panic and continued her next attack.

3 swords intertwined in the air. I narrowly blocked her swords while waiting for an opportunity.

The opportunity came. The moment she exhaled, exhausted, her arms opened. I didn't miss that short, golden timing when the space between the twin swords opened and pushed my sword in. The sword stabbed her solar plexus. However, the blade was blocked by the barrier training suit and didn't penetrate her body.

Ryu Hana sighed and lowered her swords.

"I lost again."

"I just got lucky this time."

"......When will I be able to beat you?"

"Who knows. You might beat me soon."

Ryu Hana glared at me with distrustful eyes, as if she thought I was making fun of her.

"Let's stop here for today."

"...Already? It's not even 10 o'clock yet."

"I have to adjust my condition because I'm going to challenge the trial tomorrow."

Ryu Hana's face instantly hardened.

I patted her on the shoulder.

"It's okay. You can win."

"......."

Ryu Hana quietly nodded.
