I gathered the villagers, now my slaves, in one place and executed ten villagers from the second village who had stabbed me in the back as an example. They were the village chief and his family and relatives who had led the betrayal.

Executing the family together was quite efficient.

Even those who easily throw away their own lives hesitate when their families' lives are at stake.

,There's a reason why they annihilate nine generations of traitors.'

Because it's efficient.

I gave the villagers a threat disguised as a speech and left the area.

The place I arrived at was District 99, the mercenary city of Lopua.

I met Norris, the broker. It was to keep Norris quiet.

,Wiping them out is difficult. There are too many mercenaries nearby.'

This wasn't my territory, and there were too many people. If I wiped them out here, it would only attract attention. Trying to handle it quietly could make it noisy.

,I don't necessarily have to kill him. Norris doesn't know much anyway.'

Besides, he's a mercenary broker, so he'll be tight-lipped. A broker with a loose tongue wouldn't last long in this business.

"Huu. You've come."

Norris greeted me. His mood wasn't good. He avoided my eyes. I came to pressure him, but he was overly conscious of me.

"You seem to know what happened."

"I am a witness to the Reaper's Contract. I didn't directly violate the contract, but I was somewhat affected. The damage is considerable. Of course, not as much as you, Eugene-nim, who was betrayed by the mercenary group on the spot...."

"It was a bit much. The most trusted mercenary group in this city acting like that."

"......I'm sorry. If you want a new mercenary group...."

"No, it's okay. I can't trust mercenaries anymore."

"I understand."

Norris nodded without arguing. A mercenary group that even made a Reaper's Contract betrayed me. It would be strange to easily trust other mercenaries.

"Then, the reason you came to me is for compensation?"

It was to keep him quiet. I hadn't really thought about compensation. But if he's offering compensation, that's different.

"Yes. You have to take responsibility as a broker. I trusted you and hired the Omikos Mercenary Group."

"Huu. I admit my mistake. Since I don't have any cash or AP at the moment... would it be okay to give you something in kind?"

"I don't care."

He took something out from inside the drawer.

It was a silver scissors. A luxurious scissors made entirely of silver from the handle to the blade.

「Scissors of Death

Can cut anything once.

Rank: SS」

My eyes widened. It was a much better item than I had expected.

,I thought he would only bring an A-rank item at best.'

It's an SS-rank item. It was such a good item that it was suspicious.

"You're giving away an item of this caliber for something like this?"

"It's not just something like this. Isn't it a matter of my reputation and Eugene-nim's reputation?"

Norris stared at me with serious eyes. He was afraid of me. It was similar to the face of a warrior facing death.

,He's afraid of me. Did he investigate me while I was moving to kill Fafnir?'

Maybe he investigated my actions in detail.

I've caused quite a few incidents. But those actions are roughly covered up under the name of the Illusionary Workshop, so they're not well known.

I held the scissors in my hand. It was cool and had an excellent grip.

"I like it."

"I'm glad you like it. By the way, you can use that scissors to cut contracts. It wouldn't be strange to cut even the Reaper's Contract."

"You're giving me such a great item. Aren't you wasting it?"

"It would be a lie if I said I wasn't. But it's an item that's useless to me. I got it three years ago, but I never had a situation where I could use it. It will be more helpful to you, Eugene-nim."

"I'll use it very usefully. And I hope you'll keep your mouth shut about this."

"Of course. If this gets out... it won't be good for either of our reputations."

A mercenary group that made a Reaper's Contract betrayed its employer. It was news that could damage the entire mercenary industry. In particular, Norris, the broker of the Omikos Mercenary Group, could have his career shattered.

,Ah, that's right. This guy is shitting his pants right now. If this gets out, he's done as a broker.'

Even if betrayals by mercenaries are common, the Omikos Mercenary Group was different. Aren't they a mercenary group famous for their loyalty? Their scale is completely different from ordinary mercenaries.

I put the Scissors of Death into my inventory and got up from my seat.

"Make sure to keep your mouth shut. Then we won't meet again."

"Yes. Of course. I'll take care of the Omikos Mercenary Group... I'll make sure they don't exist in this world, so it'll be relatively easy to handle."

"You don't think they're alive?"

"They would have been cursed and tracked by the Reaper."

I left Norris's office. As soon as I closed the door, I heard Norris's sigh in my sensitive ears. I pretended not to know and went outside.

Before going to Olympus, I visited the AP store in District 4.

AP could be used here except for special stores. It can also be exchanged for Atlantis's currency, Penny.

First, I bought a large number of space-time warp scrolls. I'm earning a tremendous amount of AP, but I'm also using a tremendous amount of AP, so it's hard to save AP.

,But I can't not buy space-time warp scrolls.'

Space-time warp scrolls are essential in Atlantis. It costs 60,000 AP per scroll, but it saves time.

Above all, it can be used in other worlds as well.

,Damn it. The price has gone up by 500 AP since last time.'

The price of space-time warp scrolls is increasing over time. Demand is greater than supply, so it can't be helped.

I bought other items as well.

「AP Shop Scroll

You can use the AP shop for 30 minutes when you tear the scroll.

Rank: B」

It's a scroll I bought for 200,000 AP. It's a scroll that allows you to use the AP shop in places other than the AP shop.

I had to buy it because I knew I wouldn't be able to leave the Olympus area for a while.

,I might need something in that area.'

The road to the Olympus area was not easy.

It's outside the Eustia Empire area, so no one ever goes there. There wasn't even a roughly paved road. It was literally nature in its natural state. In addition, there are quite dangerous monsters roaming near the Olympus area.

,Olympus is an area that opens in the later stages.'

Of course, I don't think I can conquer the entire Olympus area just because I'm going now. There are limits to the original information. The information I know is less than 10% of the entire Olympus area. I know less than I don't know.

,My goal is Hephaestus's forge. I mustn't forget.'

It was best to achieve my goal and leave Olympus.

I flapped my Valkyrie's wings and flew towards the Olympus area. It was several times, several dozen times faster than walking.

Mount Olympus can be seen in the distance. The mountain reaching to the sky was so enormous that it was intimidating just to look at it.

Gulp.

I swallowed my saliva and tensed up without realizing it. There seemed to be some other power existing in that Mount Olympus along with the intimidation.

,I've felt this before. It's divine power.'

And a tremendous divine power at that.

"Kkiwoo woo woo woo wook!"
