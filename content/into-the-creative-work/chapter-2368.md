I returned to Nakwol Mountain with the shell of a Mt. Tai Star Turtle.

Next to the mansion on Nakwol Mountain was Yang Hong-ye's smithy, with smoke constantly rising from the chimney. Thanks to the barrier Milyeong-ssi installed, no noise could be heard.

I entered the smithy. Yang Hong-ye was expressionlessly hammering Stormbreaker. Not forging it. Just hammering it.

"What are you doing?"

"I'm figuring out the metal's durability and recovery. I've got a grasp on the durability to some extent... but the recovery is erratic."

"Isn't Stormbreaker's recovery the same?"

"It looks that way at a glance. It varies slightly depending on the degree and cause of the damage. Being cut or broken is faster, but being crushed is a bit slower to recover."

Stormbreaker had that characteristic? That was new information to me.

"I brought the shell of a Mt. Tai Star Turtle."

"Just leave it around there."

"If I put it here, the smithy will explode, you know?"

Her hammer stopped.

"...How much did you bring? You didn't kill it, did you?"

"I killed it."

"...Ah, okay. You didn't really need to kill it. Whatever, just leave it outside or put the spatial storage on the table."

I casually placed the spatial storage on the table and asked,

"How long will it take to enhance Stormbreaker?"

"Hmm. Three years?"

"That's too long."

"Stormbreaker isn't just a magical artifact, it's more like a magical treasure. And not just any magical treasure. If it were crafting instead of enhancement, it would take at least ten more years. Just wait patiently. It's only three years anyway."

Only three years.

I briefly forgot the concept of time in this world. For me and her, who live for hundreds of years after reaching a certain realm, three years wasn't a long time.

'Should I use auto-progression?'

I decided to start auto-progression appropriately and train myself as well. I even had a spiritual medicine that would help with training. It was the inner core I obtained from killing the Mt. Tai Star Turtle.

I went under the Bodhi tree and sat cross-legged. It's quite helpful when circulating qi here. In terms of efficiency, it's more than doubled. Meditation also goes well. This probably isn't just my imagination.

'Martial arts from the demonic path are less efficient here... but it's fine when training the Lightning Sky Style.'

I took out the inner core of the Mt. Tai Star Turtle. Originally, it was larger than my body, but with Wiyu's help, I compressed its energy to the size of my thumb.

Fire, Water, Wood, Metal, Earth,

The five representative energies. Commonly known as the Five Elements Theory. Of course, these aren't the only five energies in this world. They're just the most common.

'The Mt. Tai Star Turtle has the energies of Metal and Earth. Among them, Earth accounts for more than 80%.'

Lightning qi wasn't compatible with Earth qi. Moreover, the inner core of a monster, not a spiritual beast, wasn't compatible with humans.

'At best, I can only absorb 20% of this inner core.'

It would be more beneficial to find a suitable monster to trade with. The problem is that most monsters are poor, and there are few monsters who trade with humans.

'Well, this is also an experience.'

I swallowed the inner core.

A bitter taste lingered in my mouth. A sticky energy burst inside my body.

I chanted the Lightning Sky Knot to purify the demonic energy. There was a limit. I didn't cling to it and exhaled the demonic energy through my breath. The Eight Extraordinary Meridians are activated. I gradually felt my consciousness rising.

There was a wall. Blocking me. It was impossible to go around or jump over it. I knew that I had to break this wall to move forward.

Bang!

I punched the wall with all my strength. The wall didn't budge. This time, I infused lightning qi into my fist and swung.

Kwaang!

The wall was still intact.

I wasn't discouraged. It didn't matter even if I failed. There would be plenty of opportunities in the future.

Some say that desperation is important... but desperation isn't the only answer.

Bang! Bang! Bang!

Hitting, hitting, and hitting again.

I just stubbornly hit the wall. How many thousands of times did I hit it? Finally, the wall cracked. Not much left now.

Bang! Bang! Bang! Kwajik.

A hole was made in the wall. I looked beyond the hole. There was a pure white space with nothing in it.

Urrr.

The broken wall collapsed, and my consciousness settled in its original position.

Opening my eyes, I took a short breath and stood up. By breaking down one wall, I became stronger than before.

I jumped from the 1st stage of the Three Flowers Gathering to the 3rd stage, skipping the 2nd stage.

'The other women are busy, and I've created a training routine... should I just run auto-progression?'

• • •

Three years of auto-progression.

The enhancement of Stormbreaker wasn't completed. While Yang Hong-ye and Milyeong-ssi were studying Stormbreaker, Milyeong-ssi had an epiphany and got absorbed in it. But unfortunately, she didn't reach the realm of harmony.

Instead, her tail increased by one, making it six in total.

"Seobang-nim! I've developed a new technique that will help Seobang-nim! You'll really like it."

"A technique that will help me?"

"Yes! It's a technique that appears in the Bright Light Ascension original work! It's called Silyuyeongin (Time Stream Spirit Guide)!"

I couldn't help but be surprised.

Silyuyeongin was a celestial art that appeared in the mid-to-late part of the original work, with the ability to accelerate time. To be precise, it created a small barrier and accelerated the time within it. The original protagonist learned this celestial art and used it frequently.

"If you really succeeded in Silyuyeongin... that's amazing. I could enhance a lot of things."

The biggest drawback of Bright Light Ascension, which enhances anything, is that it takes time. Since this point has been resolved... I can enhance various things.

'If I enhance cola or cider, the taste will get better. How delicious will the fried rice I make become if I enhance it?'

My mouth is already watering.

"Are you enhancing the Jeunryeong (Cloud Restraining Order) right now? I'll help you. But can you lend me the Bright Light Ascension?"

"The Bright Light Ascension? Is there something you want to enhance?"

"I want to put talismans and magical artifacts into the Bright Light Ascension to research enhanced techniques."

"Okay. I'll lend it to you."

I could trust Milyeong-ssi, so I could lend it to her.

The Bright Light Ascension produces different times and results even when the same items are put in. It wouldn't be different for techniques contained in talismans. It would probably be helpful to Milyeong-ssi in many ways.

"Okay. Shall we start?"

Milyeong-ssi took out a box from the spatial storage. A seemingly crude wooden box. But if you focused on it, it felt somewhat mysterious.

"It's a box to confine the barrier. A kind of auxiliary tool. Now, you can put the Bright Light Ascension in here."

I reverse-summoned the Bright Light Ascension, a painting depicting mountains, sky, clouds, and light, and summoned it in front of me. For reference, the Bright Light Ascension is stored in my room in Wiyu's mansion. It doesn't activate if it's in an inventory or spatial storage.

I put the Bright Light Ascension inside the wooden box.

Milyeong-ssi additionally put one piece of heavenly jade into the box, then closed the lid and began chanting a technique. The mysterious energy felt from the wooden box intensified.

"Okay, done. Unlike the Silyuyeongin in the original work, the efficiency isn't good. In the original work, one piece of heavenly jade could accelerate half a shichen, or one hour, into ten years... but this can only accelerate it by three years."

"Being able to accelerate it at all is something."

One hour later. I opened the box lid. The Bright Light Ascension was still enhancing the Jeunryeong. The heavenly jade that was put in had disappeared without a trace.

Milyeong-ssi put in one piece of heavenly jade and used Silyuyeongin again.

After another 12 hours, the enhancement was finally completed. The Bright Light Ascension accelerated for 39 years.

I took the enhanced Jeunryeong out of the Bright Light Ascension.

A dodecagonal azure metal plate. The appearance hadn't changed. But the felt mysteriousness was much greater.

Holding the metal plate in my hand and activating it, I curled up the corners of my lips. I could naturally know what I could do with the Jeunryeong.

'Not only can I control clouds to bring rain and lightning, but I can also bring snow or hail. I can control water and wind. The essence of clouds is water, so it's not surprising. The efficiency isn't good, but I can also freeze water. What's unexpected is that I can control wind.'

There's no need to hold it in my hand. I can use the Jeunryeong's abilities just by having it on my body.

I put the Jeunryeong in my pocket and spread out my palm. Wind gathered to form a sphere.

"Rasengan!!"

I threw the compressed wind in my hand at a nearby tree. Poong! The tree exploded.

"Ah, I'll borrow the Bright Light Ascension."

Milyeong-ssi disappeared with the Bright Light Ascension. Whether she did or not, I focused on the new power.

"Subdue Demons and Ward Off Evil Amulet!"

Hundreds of sword winds rampaged, cutting everything around. I was no exception. However, since I wrapped my body in protective qi, I wasn't injured. Sword winds without even sword energy couldn't penetrate the qi.

'Unlike lightning, controlling wind isn't easy. Hmm. What should I try this time? Should I make a tornado?'

Thanks to my experience handling lightning, I could handle wind plausibly, but it was quite lacking in detail. It seems like it will take quite a bit of time to get used to it.

"......"

Ooh.

I felt a gaze. I paused and turned around.

Wiyu was there. She was looking at me with cold, sunken eyes. She was holding a sword in her hand.

"S, Sifu...!"

"I told you to train in the training grounds... You must have wanted to train so much that you didn't even have time to go to the training grounds. I appreciate your enthusiasm, so I'll help you with your training for the first time in a while."

Wiyu swung her sword.

A sword wind flew. Confirming that it was a slash without qi, I stood still without dodging. Sword winds wouldn't be able to penetrate protective qi.

Seogeok.

My right arm was cut off. My right arm fell off with a large amount of blood.

"Attach your arm with a potion."

"No, wait. How did you penetrate the protective qi with a sword wind?!"

"Because it was sharp enough to penetrate protective qi."

What kind of bullshit is that? I couldn't say that. Because another sword wind flew. I picked up my right arm that had fallen on the floor and dodged the sword wind. I hurriedly drank a potion to attach my arm. Thanks to the clean cut, the recovery was fast.

'Lightning!'

Lightning fell from the sky towards Wiyu. Wiyu cut the lightning with her sword and sent a sword wind. The sword wind was more than twice as fast as before. I hurriedly summoned Hwaryeon Bido and cut the sword wind with lightning.

Wiyu swung her sword indifferently. She only swung it once, but the sword wind became three and flew towards me. Even in different directions.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 11]

I dodged the attacks with Instant and smiled bitterly.

'The start of hell training.'

I couldn't even pass it off with auto-progression. Wiyu recognizes auto-progression like a ghost when sparring. Of course, she doesn't know the details about the Leisure Life app.

'She said that the mind during auto-progression is similar yet subtly different. Ugh. Sword wind is flying. Is it five this time!'

I shook off my distracting thoughts and engaged in battle.

'I'm tired to death. Let's stop here. End game.'
