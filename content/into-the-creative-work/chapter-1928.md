I saw a wraith. That bewildered me. Especially since the wraith flying near me wasn't materialized with black mana. Unless you had a special ability like Niklaus's spirit sight, seeing wraiths was nearly impossible.

,Is it because I've seen a wraith once before?

If I hadn't known at all, maybe it wouldn't have happened. Perhaps I saw it because I had already observed a wraith. Or maybe there were other possibilities.

Anyway, I ignored the wraith. Wraiths that aren't materialized don't pose much of a threat. At most, they just cling to my body and cause fatigue.

Huuuwoooong.

I raise my mana.

I took out a tungsten bullet from my pocket and drew a magic circle in front of me. I threw the tungsten bullet into the center of the magic circle.

[Railgun]

The bullet is fired along the rails of electricity laid in the magic circle. It instantly breaks the sound barrier and falls in a straight line towards the Death Knight. The Death Knight swung its sword and knocked the bullet away. The aftermath sent the Death Knight's body flying backward, crashing down.

,Its swordsmanship is excellent. Was it true what they say, that Death Knights are created based on the souls of outstanding warriors from their past lives?

But it wasn't unscathed. The Death Knight's sword was shattered, and its right arm was gone. As befitting an undead, it didn't seem to feel any pain as it charged towards me.

I levitated myself with telekinesis. Necromancer Niklaus pointed his finger at me. The skeletons scattered around rushed towards me. As they did, the skeletons merged together, increasing in size.

In an instant, it transformed into a giant skeleton over 10m tall. Then, wraiths appeared from all directions, showering me with curses.

My body grew heavy.

The mana road became unstable.

My vision blurred.

If this were a game, it would be like receiving four or five debuffs at once.

But my mind was clear.

A heavy body? I can just move my body with telekinesis.

An unstable mana road? It's not exactly destroyed. I just need to control the flowing mana more carefully.

Blurred vision? My senses were already sharp. Even if the most important sense, sight, was blurry, I could vividly feel the surrounding situation.

The Astral expanded, and the mana within my body moved according to my will. The mana that burst out of my body became an invisible formula, rising into the sky. The formula and mana resonated, drawing a line emitting a blue-white light, forming a magic circle.

When the magic circle was perfectly drawn, I chanted as if pulling a trigger.

[Ice Strike]

A huge chunk of ice emerged through the magic circle and fell towards the Giant Skeleton. The Giant Skeleton, as clumsy as its large body, couldn't avoid it. The Giant Skeleton tried to receive the glacier with its hands outstretched.

However, the giant ice emerging from the magic circle hadn't fully revealed itself yet.

With a diameter of over 10m and a height of over 50m, the ice, worthy of being called a glacier, crushed the Giant Skeleton with its weight.

Kwajeek!

The Giant Skeleton's massive bony body collapsed. Cracks began to appear starting from the knees, then the shin bones, shoulder bones, and ribs.

,It's still holding on. Will it withstand this too?

[Gravity]

Gravity amplification.

It was a 1st class magic that I used moderately, so the increased gravity was only about 30%, but that meant the weight of the glacier increased by 30%. That alone was enough. The Giant Skeleton was crushed and shattered by the glacier.

I descended to the ground. I felt a cool chill. It wasn't bad, as it felt like my heated body was cooling down.

,...Who are you?! Leon Schneider can't use this level of elemental magic! Where is the real Leon Schneider?! Are you a spy from another country?!

I heard Niklaus's voice, laced with tension.

I raised the corners of my lips. I could feel Niklaus's tension. He had already revealed his hand. Curses using wraiths, Death Knights, Giant Skeletons.

His black mana must have run out already.

,Does it change anything if I'm a spy? You're going to die here anyway.

,Don't you know where I belong yet? Even if I die, the 6th Corps won't disappear. The 6th Corps will start an investigation, and they'll eventually find you.

,In short, you're asking me to spare you?

,...I'm proposing a deal.

,I don't need it.

[Ice Spear]

I held an ice spear in my hand and walked towards him.

,Death Knight!!

Niklaus shouted. The tattered Death Knight, missing an arm and a sword, charged forward.

I threw the ice spear.

Kwaaaang!

The ice spear pierced the Death Knight's chest and exploded. It instantly increased in volume, turning into snowflakes and shattering the Death Knight into pieces.

,It's not difficult to deal with a Death Knight that's no different from a tattered rag.

Niklaus looked at me with a face of grim determination.

,...I am a necromancer. I will become a wraith and curse you. You won't be able to sleep peacefully from now on.

,We'll see. I think I'll sleep just fine.

[Absolute Polymorph]

I transformed my right hand into a hand cannon and aimed it at Niklaus.

Kwaang!

The hand cannon spat fire. When the smoke rose and disappeared, Niklaus's upper body was gone.

Absolute Polymorph wasn't just limited to transforming into living things. It could also transform into non-living machines. The problem was that it was unknown what kind of problems that would cause to the mind.

Returning my hand to its original form, I levitated myself with telekinesis and flew into the air to finish things up. Passing by the glacier stuck in the center of the wasteland, I saw Kevin running away breathlessly.

Pajijijik!

Lightning surged from my fingertips and gathered in the center of my palm. I threw the lightning strike towards Kevin.

It hit. The blue lightning scanned his body and was absorbed into the ground, disappearing. I walked towards the electrocuted bastard.

He wasn't dead. Maybe if he was a regular person, but Kevin was also a soldier with the rank of Captain.

,You've been doing some fun things.

Kevin focused on his breathing and moved his mana. He spread mana throughout his body, awakening his entire body that had been paralyzed by the electric shock.

,Co, Commander-nim...!

,What is it.

,Please spare me! I'll tell you who's behind me!

,If you want to die nicely, tell me now. If you don't speak within 10 seconds, you'll become the mascot of Rumen Prison.

Kevin's face turned pale.

Matthias, tied to a cross in the prison yard. His arms and legs were cut off, and he was just withering away. He couldn't even die. Guards were assigned to manage him every day so he wouldn't die.

He wouldn't want to end up like Matthias, whose family was all executed before his eyes, unable to die, and spending decades in that state.

Kevin sighed. His resistance had completely disappeared. He said powerlessly.

,...It's Colonel Bergman from the 6th Corps's General Staff.

,The General Staff, huh. More troublesome than I thought. He's probably considered an elite, so why is he interested in a place like Rumen Prison?

,On the surface, it's because of money. Embezzling the budget is quite lucrative.

,On the surface? Does that mean there's an ulterior motive?

,Yes. I was suspicious, so I investigated separately. Colonel Bergman's family was running an artificial organ company.

A company that cultivates and sells artificial organs. It probably wasn't just cultivating ordinary organs. Otherwise, it wouldn't be competitive.

,I see. So, his goal was to acquire the prison to conduct human experiments.

Considering the special nature of Rumen Prison, it was the perfect place to conduct human experiments. Even I'm running the prison in a terrible state, and no one is really interfering.

,...Can't you spare me? I'll be sincerely loyal from now on. I've been running the prison until you took office, Commander-nim! I'm useful!

,Just be grateful that I'm giving you a clean death.

If I had my way, I wouldn't give him a clean death. He stabbed me in the back. But Kevin was the Deputy Warden. I couldn't treat him the same way as Matthias, the gang leader. It was better to kill him cleanly here and now. That way, Colonel Bergman would have to handle all the cleanup.

,Shit.... I was just trying to live a little better, but the result is this....

Listening to his pathetic last words, I used magic.

A bolt of lightning fell from the clear, cloudless sky, taking Kevin's life. I stepped over his corpse and headed towards the car. I had Flash in my pocket.

• • •

Three days had passed.

I was researching Flash, the potion that restores memories. It was too precious to just use once and be done with it. I wanted to figure out the recipe and mass-produce it if possible.

,I've tried everything I could for three days straight... and I don't understand anything.

Magic, maybe. But this was a potion. That meant the ingredients and their combination were more important than the method of manufacturing with magic.

,To find out the ingredients, I need an analyzer used by corporations. The problem is that those things are hard to get even if you have money....

There was no real way to do it for now. I had no choice but to set aside a portion and use it.

Knock knock knock!

,Commander-nim! It's a disaster!

A guard knocked urgently on the door. I shoved Flash into my pocket and said.

,Quiet down. Speak slowly. Did the prisoners start a riot or something?

Have I been letting the prisoners out too much lately?

The door opened, and a guard came in. He was sweating profusely.

,The 6th Corps Commander is visiting the prison in person!

,The Corps Commander? Why?

I was also taken aback and jumped up from my seat.

Corps Commander.

One of the most powerful figures in the militaristic Haist Empire. In the Empire, even high-ranking nobles can't hold their heads high in front of the Corps Commander. Moreover, the 6th Corps Commander was a member of the Imperial Family.

In terms of power, he was one of the top ten figures in the Empire.

Such a monstrous being is coming to a remote prison? That was no different from suddenly dropping a nuclear bomb.

,Damn it. Did Colonel Bergman pull some strings?

Clicking my tongue, I looked at the guard.

,Exactly how many days until he arrives?

,Th, that is, he's coming right now on a transport plane! He's scheduled to arrive at the prison in 30 minutes!

,...Why are you reporting that now?

,I just heard it from the 6th Corps Headquarters!

30 minutes.

Clean up the inside of the prison, silence the prisoners, forge the ledgers.... There was a hell of a lot to do. Of course, 30 minutes wasn't nearly enough. It would take at least two days.

I sat in my chair, crossed my arms, and calmly said to the guard, who was sweating profusely.

,Just do as you normally do. There's no need to make a fuss just because the Corps Commander is coming.

,As, as expected of you, Commander-nim.

The guard, who spoke as if he was truly impressed, saluted me and went outside. His footsteps seemed lighter each time he came in.

,Shit. What should I do? I haven't even properly milked them yet.

Feeling anxious, I put my hand in my pocket.

Feeling the rough shape of the key, the World Door, I calmed down a bit.

,Should I just run?
