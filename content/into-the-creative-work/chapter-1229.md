I managed to get two black wooden beads pretty quickly after starting. Considering that black wooden beads could be used as a means of trade, it was a very good start.

The problem was that I didn't know where the exit was. The later I found the exit, the worse it would be for me.

,It's also hard to join up with Squad 1, who you could call my comrades. Because I don't know where they are.'

There was no rendezvous point either. No, we couldn't decide on one. We knew about the existence of the Demon Incarceration Cave, but we had no information about its structure.

The Demon Incarceration Cave is every man for himself. You have to solve it alone.

,Still, I'm much better off than others. Because I have the Heavenly Eye.'

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

I didn't prefer opening the Heavenly Eye. The Heavenly Eye is good in many ways, but it consumes a lot of mana continuously. In this world's terms, internal energy is constantly consumed while activating the Heavenly Eye. It's nothing right now, but the burden increases over time.

,I have no choice in this situation.'

I slowly looked around and peered through the walls. Another wall was visible beyond the wall I peered through. I focused my eyes even more. More internal energy was consumed. I shook my head and deactivated the Heavenly Eye as I continued to peer through.

,...Damn it. It's too wide.'

I didn't find the exit. But I roughly figured out what was within a 100m radius of me.

I stroked my eyes and walked forward. If I overused the Heavenly Eye, my eyes would get strained like this.

I stopped after walking forward for a while.

A man was dead in the hallway. His body was rotting so badly that you couldn't think he had just died.

,When I looked around with the Heavenly Eye earlier, this guy was alive. He just died.'

Is it the work of a monster? No. There are no monsters near here. There was no one else either. I looked at the walls and floor of the hallway. Traces of attacks on the wall and faint green liquid remaining on the floor.

,It's a trap installed on the walls and floor.'

When I peered around with the Heavenly Eye earlier, I saw strange structures. I thought nothing of it then, but now that I think about it, they seemed to be traps.

,What should I do? Should I just go another way?'

By avoiding the traps, I could appeal my wisdom to the instructors who were watching me.

,If I go another way, there are monsters... I want to save my stamina as much as possible.'

I decided to break through the traps.

I briefly used the Heavenly Eye to scan the traps.

,As expected, it's a structure that spews poison from the wall.'

If you get hit by the poison, your body will rot like the guy who died here.

I walked forward.

At that moment, with a clanging sound, part of the wall opened and a round metal cylinder popped out. It looked similar to a gun barrel. It mercilessly spewed green liquid from the barrel. It sprayed so densely that even a Martial artist who had reached the peak of their training would have difficulty avoiding it.

I gathered my internal energy and deployed a Qi Shield. The poison spewed from the wall was blocked by the Qi Shield and fell with a patter.

,I wouldn't have been caught by this kind of trap if I didn't know anything at all. There's no way I'd fall for this trap when I already know about it.'

I passed through the trap with ease using the Qi Shield.

I kept moving forward without looking back. I decided to think about whether this was the right way later.

I frowned as I walked forward for a while.

,I don't know what's ahead. Should I use the Heavenly Eye again?'

I shook my head. I wanted to save as much internal energy as possible. And even if I used the Heavenly Eye, I had come too far to turn back and choose another path.

,If there are monsters, I'll kill them, and if it's a trap, I'll break through it like before.'

I advanced.

Whoong.

I felt a sensation of my body floating.

,It's a barrier. I've entered a barrier.'

I narrowed my eyes and raised my vigilance.

"You're number 123."

Number 366, Jegal Mosun, who had been hiding behind the wall, revealed himself. He had a tense face, but he let out a sigh of relief as soon as he saw me.

"You were hiding here with a barrier?"

"Yes. This is a perfect place to use a barrier. Ah, don't move rashly and move as I say. There's a trap in front of you."

"You're using the barrier to use the trap."

"It's efficient to use what's already there. One step forward, four steps to the right. Three steps forward again."

I moved as Jegal Mosun said. It seemed like he had set up not only a barrier but also a formation. I soon arrived in front of him.

"It's so nice to see you like this, number 123."

"What about the others?"

"There are none. I'm here alone. Ah, I found the black wooden bead. It was embedded in the center of the ceiling here. There were three traps around it, so it was quite difficult."

"But you still got the black wooden bead. I also killed a monster and got a black wooden bead."

"So, all we have to do is find the exit. That's a relief."

I nodded.

If it were someone else, I would have killed him and taken the black wooden bead, but I couldn't kill Jegal Mosun, who would become Cheon Yu-un's close aide.

Jegal Mosun and I shared information about the Demon Incarceration Cave. I hid the fact that I had one more black wooden bead. Just because we're comrades doesn't mean I have to tell him everything. And I didn't trust Jegal Mosun that much.

"I see."

Jegal Mosun drew his sword and started drawing something on the floor.

It was a map. A map based on the information I gave and the Demon Incarceration Cave that Jegal Mosun had experienced himself.

"I started here and moved about ilda-gyeong to arrive at this room with traps. On the other hand, number 123's starting point is here."

"...It seems roughly right. But does this even mean anything?"

"It does. If we compare it to the location of the buildings in the Demon Subjugation Agency right above the Demon Incarceration Cave, we can roughly know the size of the Demon Incarceration Cave."

Jegal Mosun drew another map on top of the map. It was a map of the Demon Subjugation Agency above the Demon Incarceration Cave. He had lived in the Demon Subjugation Agency for almost a year, so he drew it without hesitation. I was impressed by Jegal Mosun's drawing skills. He was a talented guy, as expected of someone who would become Cheon Yu-un's closest aide in the future.

"I started here."

"I started over there. I slid down through a hole in the floor. The passage was quite steep."

"Thanks to you, I came down comfortably. The depth is estimated to be between 5 and 7 jang. So...."

He drew a large outline on the map of the Demon Incarceration Cave.

"If we compare it to the size of the Demon Subjugation Agency, the size of the Demon Incarceration Cave would be about this much. The place that is thought to be the exit is here on the edge. We have to go in the opposite direction from where I came."

"I see."

I nodded.

To be honest, I didn't understand even half of what Jegal Mosun said. Why is he so sure that the exit is on the edge? How can he guess the size of the Demon Incarceration Cave?

I didn't think I could understand even if I heard the reasons, so I decided to just shut up and go along with it.

Sseuk sseuk.

Jegal Mosun kept moving his sword and drawing the map.

"What are you doing? Wasn't the conclusion already reached?"

"It's for the next person who comes here. I'm going to deliberately put in false information to confuse him."

"......"

The map creation was finished. He retrieved the steel rods that he had stuck in the ground for the barrier and formation and said to me.

"Let's go, number 123. If the two of us join forces, we'll be able to find the exit quickly."

•••

I walked forward with Jegal Mosun.

I had judged that Jegal Mosun was useful, but I seriously considered whether I should withdraw that judgment.

"Number 123. Don't step on the black platforms. The moment you step on them, the trap will be activated."

"Didn't you say not to step on the red platforms earlier?"

"That was a miscalculation. It's inevitable that miscalculations occur with traps that I'm seeing for the first time."

I stepped on the white platform next to the black platform. The platform went down with a swoosh, and ice picks fell from the ceiling. I, who had been tense, immediately stepped back and avoided the trap.

"...Hmm. It seems that the arrangement is more important than the color of the platforms."

"You fucking bastard. Tell me honestly. Are you planning to kill me using the traps?!"

"It's a mistake. And you wouldn't die from this much, would you? I believe in number 123."

"I don't trust you. You go first."

"You're good at joking. I have lower physical abilities than number 123. Not to mention martial arts skills. No matter how you look at it, it's more reasonable for number 123 to take the lead."

"Use magic! Magic! You went to the trouble of learning magic, so why aren't you using it?!"

"What kind of magic am I supposed to use in this situation?"

"Use magic to figure out the traps! At the very least, if you use magic that allows you to fly in the air...! You can pass through these platform traps without any problems!!"

"Hah. It's easy to say."

Jegal Mosun shook his head.

"Magic isn't omnipotent. There are types and compatibility in magic. In my case, it's necromancy. I specialize in activating magic through corpses and sacrifices. Finding out traps in advance with magic? That's the exploration type. Unfortunately, I don't know much about the exploration type. Magic to move by flying in the air? You have to calculate the spell formula to activate that magic. Precise spell control is essential."

"You're from the Jegal Clan, which is famous for magic!"

He glared at me as if he was displeased.

"Just because I inherited the blood of Jegal doesn't mean I'm proficient in all magic."

"The magician I know does everything without being picky."

I said, thinking of Milyeong-nim. She easily did any kind of magic without being picky. She used magic to build houses, summon large amounts of water with magic, and knew how to create barriers and even apply seals. She even cooks with magic.

Looking at Milyeong-nim, magic was close to omnipotent, with nothing it couldn't do.

"It seems you know a great magician. Anyway, those are things I can't do."

"Damn it. It would be much better to just run blindly."

"Number 123. Do you know the saying that if your head is bad, your body suffers? We need to save our stamina."

"There's also a saying that if your body is bad, your head suffers. Time is money. Should we waste time on these traps?"

"Time is money. Hmm. Good saying. But I prefer to be sure about everything. And I've now completely figured out the arrangement of these traps. Step on the platforms as I say. The order is black black white black white."

"If you fail this time too, I'll break through the traps my way."

"That won't happen."

It really didn't happen. Jegal Mosun and I safely passed through the traps.
