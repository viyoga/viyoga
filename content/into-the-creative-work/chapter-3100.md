Juan, slammed against the wall, slowly picked himself up.

Blood ran from his nose, but the mental shock hit harder than the physical pain.

'The ability applied successfully. But I didn't even see the bastard's fist. And he took my punch without a scratch?'

That punch just now hadn't been thrown at full power. Even so, it carried enough force to knock out an A-rank hunter if it landed clean.

"Keh keh. I can hear the gears grinding in your skull from here."

Juan ground his teeth at the mocking voice drifting from behind him—Seong Yujin's voice.

'Fine. He's definitely got the power to back up his confidence. My ability landed. What I didn't account for is that this bastard's physical capability is as monstrous as his awakened ability.'

He'd lost track of Seong Yujin's movement? Seong Yujin was famous as an acceleration-type awakener capable of burst speed.

'Even in a weakened state, he's like this.'

Juan rose to his feet. He turned to face Seong Yujin. That relaxed stance of his was infuriating. Juan reset his broken nose and blew it clear. Blood splattered violently onto the floor instead of mucus.

"Snff. You goddamn chink bastard. You've just made the mistake of your lifetime."

"A mistake? Smashing your nose?"

"Not killing me when you had the chance. On your first attack, you should've gone for my throat with a blade instead of your fist."

Juan drew up the mana inside his body and activated his ability.

He pushed his weakening ability to its maximum output. Not the half-assed effort he usually gave—this time he focused completely. The effect varied by target, but when concentrated on an S-rank hunter, he could weaken them by over 70%.

'And for an instant, I can weaken them by over 90%. Less than a second, and the recoil will tear up my insides… but who gives a damn. One moment—just one moment—is all I need to kill him.'

The moment he focused his ability, Seong Yujin's strength and aura visibly diminished. The brilliant radiance of lightning emanating from his body dimmed.

Juan dropped back into a boxing stance and began his footwork.

That wasn't the end of it. He didn't hold back his trump card either. Juan's heart began pounding faster, and the world started to slow.

Body acceleration.

'Spirit Pang. That's the shamanism I paid that damned Indian shaman two hundred million dollars to carve into my bones. Using it too long strains the heart, but… being able to move faster than your opponent can predict is a hell of an advantage.'

He closed the distance to Seong Yujin, stepping with footwork he'd modified and refined to suit his own body.

A straight-line movement bordering on teleportation.

Yet Juan confirmed that Seong Yujin's pupils never left him.

'He tracked me even in a weakened state…!'

Before throwing his punch, he sharpened his focus. Pit! Blood vessels burst in both of Juan's eyes. Seong Yujin weakened by over 90% for an instant.

'It landed…!'

He threw the punch.

Juan had no doubt—not a sliver—that Seong Yujin's head would burst like a watermelon.

'Huh.'

The instant he extended his fist, the weakening ability aimed at Seong Yujin suddenly released. While Juan's mind reeled in confusion, his fist drove toward Seong Yujin's head just as it had the first time.

But that fist never touched Seong Yujin's body.

Chwaaaaaaahk.

Dozens of lightning chains connecting to thin air coiled around Juan's entire body. He couldn't budge—forget throwing a punch.

Juan's judgment was quick. He used his weakening ability on the lightning chains. The weakening ability that hadn't worked on Seong Yujin worked on the chains. The chains' power diminished.

Puh-whack!

Seong Yujin's fist buried itself in Juan's face. Teeth scattered as he flew backward. The weakened lightning chains couldn't withstand the impact and snapped.

Chwaaaaaaahk!

The lightning chains re-secured his body faster than Juan could rise. Even through his swimming vision and surging pain, Juan tried to weaken the chains.

Seong Yujin crooked a finger. The lightning chains yanked taut, dragging Juan—bound tight—toward him.

Uppercut.

Juan's jaw caught the blow, spraying blood and teeth as his body rocketed toward the ceiling. He looked about to fly straight into the night sky, but the lightning chains binding him yanked him back down.

Then Seong Yujin's foot slammed into Juan's ribs. Juan crashed through walls and furniture, flew outside the building, and was dragged back by the lightning chains to become a human punching bag for Seong Yujin.

"Dempsey roll!"

Seong Yujin created afterimages as he battered Juan without pause.

Juan couldn't gather his senses. He was caught in a cycle—knocked unconscious by Seong Yujin's blows, then waking again. To properly activate his ability required focus, and even his passive abilities needed him conscious.

More precisely, whenever Juan lost consciousness, Seong Yujin manipulated electricity to jolt him awake. A fine current stimulating Juan's brain and nerves.

'F-fuck…! This—this bastard! He's playing with me…!'

A grin hung at the corner of Seong Yujin's mouth as he swung his fists.

How did it come to this?

Had he made a mistake during the fight?

No. There had been no mistake. Seong Yujin had come to kill him with absolute certainty.

'I can't just keep taking this…!'

Kwa-du-deuk!

Both of Juan's arms were torn off while he was still alive.

"Kraaaaaaagh!"

Juan collapsed to the floor, blood spraying from both shoulder sockets. His will to fight evaporated in an instant.

Seong Yujin raised his fist. Unlike before, he didn't swing immediately—he gathered power into it. Bzzzzk! The lightning concentrated in his fist spun, amplifying its force.

"Juan Perez. You've got good fortune. You didn't try anything with my woman. So let's end this cleanly now."

Death strode up to him and exhaled its breath in his face.

Juan remembered this sensation.

The first time in his life he'd stared down the barrel of a gun aimed at his head.

When he'd been in the thick of a war between criminal organizations.

When a rival organization's killer had pressed a blade to his throat.

An emotion he hadn't felt once since becoming an S-rank hunter.

"P-please, spare me."

Juan forced the words out desperately. Even he was startled to hear himself say it. Seong Yujin's fist paused.

"Keh keh. The Gold Master begging for his life. A rare sight indeed."

In that sneering face, Juan saw hope. His mouth moved faster. His pride had shattered with that single word he'd just uttered. Nothing left to hold him back.

"I—I have money. All the fortune I've amassed…! Every cent I'll ever earn—I swear I'll give it all to you! Enough to buy a city!"

"Gold Master. Sadly for you, I've got more money than you do. That Nectar you're so fond of? I'm the one who sells it. Keh keh."

"I-if you don't need money, I—I'll be your slave! Tell me to lick your feet and I'll lick them! Tell me to fight America and I'll fight! Please—please just let me live!"

"I don't need a slave like you. For an S-rank hunter, you're getting awfully pathetic. For the honor of S-rank hunters, I'll kill you now."

"N-no…!!"

Seong Yujin's fist struck Juan's head.

KWAAAAAANG!

The lightning in his fist exploded, lighting up the Las Vegas night.

"Spirit Pang."

Ahanu Senka—America's S-rank hunter and an Indian shaman—looked at the man who had summoned him.

A man in a suit.

An agent of the National Psychic Agency, America's unofficial state intelligence organization—the NPA.

The NPA primarily eliminated dangerous awakeners or coaxed powerful awakeners into state service before they could become hunters.

"What is it this time?"

"Korea's S-rank hunter, Thunder God, has murdered the Gold Master. He's still in America. Curse him."

"That greedy one was murdered… Good riddance. And I am not your hunting dog. These endless, baseless orders are displeasing."

"In return, we'll expand welfare policies benefiting the Indian community next year."

"…Tch. Do you know how difficult it is to curse an S-rank hunter? Let alone an S-rank hunter of another nation."

"He's currently in New York. He's on this land—so doesn't your ability reach him?"

"At best, I can deafen one ear. That's all."

"That alone is sufficient. In the world of S-rank hunters, that itself becomes a fatal weakness. Here—the materials on him."

The man produced documents. Seong Yujin's face and information were written on them.

As Ahanu turned his gaze, a photograph of Seong Yujin's face rose into the air on its own.

Ahanu focused his mind and extended his hand toward Seong Yujin's face.

Stop.

His hand rose—then halted.

Ahanu's eyes went wide.

"Spirit Pang. What's wrong?"

"The Great Earth Spirit has stayed my hand. It has stopped me. I will not do this. Leave."

"…Is the payment insufficient? We'll build a corporation for the Indian people to work at."

"Whatever payment you offer, I will not act against what the Earth Spirit forbids."

An uncomfortable silence fell.

The NPA agent let out a sigh and began gathering his documents.

"I'll report your refusal to my superiors."

"Tell your superiors this: do not try to wake a monster over one dead hunting dog."

"…Your counsel has always been right. I'll relay it clearly to my superiors."

A muscular blond man in his sixties read the report.

Hewst Locke.

The current President of the United States.

Originally, after Governor Boshiga's death, his rival Jack Taylor had been the frontrunner for the next presidency—but Jack Taylor abruptly retired from politics. That the World Hunter Association had a hand in his retirement was a secret known to only a very few. Even President Hewst didn't know why the World Hunter Association had intervened.

The World Hunter Association was beyond the reach of an American president's power.

"Spirit Pang refused, and warned me not to wake a monster. Ha. So I'm supposed to just swallow this humiliation."

He reviewed the report on Seong Yujin again.

His strength ranked among the very top even among S-rank hunters.

He'd killed the FBI Director and his family, then hunted down and killed agents too—the agents were terrified. They refused to take on anything related to Seong Yujin. The next batch of FBI agents felt the same.

'How the hell he got FBI intelligence is completely beyond me. Is there a traitor on the inside?'

Recent combat records.

Three days ago, encounter and battle with Juan Perez on the top floor of the Gold Dive Building in central Las Vegas.

Estimated time to resolution: under three minutes.

Afterward, the corpse of Juan Perez—limbs severed, head burst—and dozens of other bodies including the FBI Director were discovered. Appears to be an act of warning or provocation toward the U.S. government.

A powerful EMP blast occurred, and all electronic devices within a 300-meter radius of the building were destroyed without exception.

'A human being able to wield electricity of this magnitude. Modern cutting-edge weaponry would be utterly useless against him.'

Seong Yujin was currently staying in New York.

And over New York's sky, dark clouds had gathered and were slowly rotating. A bizarre supercell that neither released rain nor dispersed. The citizens of New York trembled with unease at the anomaly.

'The cause is definitely Seong Yujin. He's conducting a silent show of force right now. If a battle breaks out in New York….'

Like the combat footage captured in China, New York would be blanketed in lightning, and every human and electronic device within the city would die. The damage would be catastrophic beyond words.

'If I send America's ten S-rank hunters… damn it. No matter how I think about it, all I can picture is a nuclear bomb going off.'

The World Hunter Association wouldn't sit idle.

Above all, there was no guarantee America's S-rank hunters would follow his orders.

In free America, the most free-spirited people of all were the S-rank hunters. They moved at requests, not commands from an American president.

'Hoo. I need to cool my head. Maybe it's actually fortunate that Spirit Pang refused. A cursed Seong Yujin could have gone on a rampage in New York. I'm scrapping the plan to negotiate using the curse as leverage. Next….'

Only one option remained. The easiest—and the one he least wanted to take.

"…I'll have to meet Seong Yujin in person. Have my private jet prepared."
