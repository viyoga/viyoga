I used electromagnetic waves and walked across the pure white snowfield.

However, I couldn't find anything significant. Not even a common pebble existed in this space.

The floor beneath the accumulated snow? The floor wasn't land. What made up the floor was ice.

In this world, using electromagnetic waves with all my power allows me to detect roughly up to 3km. But other than ice and snow, there was nothing else I could feel. At least within 3km of me, there was nothing but ice and snow.

Time continued to pass. Walking and walking, nothing changed.

,The novelty wears off after the first few minutes. Looking at the same scenery over and over, I'm bored to death.'

It was so boring that I wished a monster would pop out and attack me.

,Kang Myungjin-nim told me to survive and find the mirror. But there isn't even a pebble, let alone a mirror.'

Being told to survive means there's something threatening my life. But even after wandering around for hours, nothing appeared.

"Oh, Lord of the Sky! Give me an answer, isiyeo!"

「The Lord of the Sky strokes his beard.」

「The King of the Demonic Sky is bored.」

The Divine Seats have no intention of helping. Or rather, they can't help. If the Divine Seats intervene recklessly, Atlantis will quickly become a mess. And for a god to intervene in Atlantis, they must pay a huge price.

,It's not an urgent situation where a god needs to intervene.'

The endless flow of time itself isn't a problem.

,I've been walking for hours, but the sun in the sky hasn't changed its position at all. That means, even though the system's power reaches it, it's a different space-time from Atlantis.'

Hadn't I already experienced something similar?

Hera's temple.

Perhaps this space and the space of Atlantis had different flows of time.

Maybe this is a mental world where my mind has been dragged in. I thought, but immediately erased it. There's no way my mind would be helplessly defeated while I'm still conscious.

,I'm hungry.'

I took out warm dumplings from my inventory and ate them. My stomach was filled. There was nothing strange about that sensation. I have a physical body.

I felt a little better after filling my stomach.

,I would have suffered if I didn't have the inventory. I might have been eating snow or ice.'

This makes me worried about Kang Myungjin-nim, Rilsner, and Joo Seohyun-ssi. They didn't have a subspace or inventory. Even if they packed emergency rations, it would be hard for them to last more than a week.

Elena? She not only has a subspace but can also create something from nothing. There's no way she'd starve to death.

,I'm starting to get annoyed... Should I use auto-pilot? ...I might end up wandering this endless snowfield forever. Auto-pilot is influenced by my past actions. No, would I starve to death before that? The avatar in auto-pilot doesn't use the inventory.'

It would be absurd if the avatar in auto-pilot used the items in my inventory arbitrarily.

I decided to just walk.

And so, time passed.

I got hungry again. I took out warm fish cake udon from my inventory and ate it. The feeling of the warm udon broth seeping into my body was amazing.

,Ah. Good. I should walk while watching my phone.'

The gazes of the Divine Seats watching me had almost disappeared. It seems even the Lord of the Sky and the King of the Demonic Sky aren't watching.

「The Olympian Goddess advises you to keep your body warm.」

My body isn't so weak that it would shiver from this level of cold.

I would have shivered if other gods had given me this kind of advice, but it was Hera's advice.

"Yes. I will keep warm."

I took out a thick winter coat from my inventory and put it on.

I left footprints in the snowfield again and moved forward.

Had about two hours passed? I started to get hungry again. This time, I took out a gas burner and cooked ramen. I don't usually like instant ramen, but cooking it in this situation made it taste like heaven.

,MSG is the best after all.'

「Endless Abundance says that moving your body makes you hungry.」

,...Come to think of it, I got hungry in just two hours this time?'

I felt something strange about Demeter's message. I wasn't usually the type to get hungry this often.

,I should stay still as Demeter-nim advised.'

I settled down and read a comic book, and four hours passed in an instant.

Kkulreureuk.

I got hungry. My body craved food. It was a clearly strange phenomenon. It didn't make sense that my body, which was statistically superior to a normal human, had such poor fuel efficiency.

,Something I don't know is at play. Is it some kind of curse?'

Looking at the goddesses' advice, it seems that staying still and keeping my body warm makes hunger come a little slower.

,But I can't just stay here forever doing nothing.'

It was when I was about to get up.

「Rainbow Bubble says that there's a reason why the saying 'it's dark under the lamp' exists.」

,Under the lamp? There's nothing here that could be called under the lamp.'

I lowered my gaze.

Pure white snow.

Underneath it, there's ice.

,Is there something under the ice?'

I was already sick of walking through the empty snowfield. There were enough reasons to try it.

I operated the Cheonma Shingong.

Jet-black demonic energy flowed out from my body. I concentrated my mind, lifted my right foot, and slammed it down.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Dominating Step (天魔君臨步).

Kukukung.

An intangible wave spread in all directions, sweeping across the snowfield. The snow piled on the ground shot into the air.

Jjeojeojeojeok!

A part of the massive ice under the snow shook and cracked. But it was an infinitely insufficient crack to break all the massive ice like land.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Black Flame Demonic Dragon.

Hwareureuk.

Black flames flickered around my right fist.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

I slammed my fist towards the ground. A part of the ice broke due to the shockwave, and black flames penetrated through the gap, melting the ice. The melted ice became water and instantly turned into steam, flying away.

,Black flames are more efficient than I thought.'

I saw a way.

I lost my mind and slammed my fist towards the ice.

Kwang! Kwang! Kwakwakwakwang!

The punching stopped when my mana ran out. The black flames that were melting the ice also disappeared. I was in the center of the ice. I felt coldness from all directions.

,It's dark.'

It was a waste to create lightning, so I took out an electric lantern from my inventory. Click. The surroundings brightened with a single button.

"...This is!"

I was surprised. The surface of the ice that received the light shone and reflected me.

There was no need for a separate mirror. The mirror I was looking for was this ice.

The lantern I was holding shook. The ice that was reflecting me started to reflect something else.

It was a modern city. But the city was covered in snow. There were no people. All that remained in the city were the traces of civilization. Only the cold wind swept through the city.

I moved the lantern. To be exact, I changed the angle of the light a little. With just that, the scenery that was visible changed.

Snow-covered volcanoes, frozen seas, hills piled with corpses, and so on. The common point of the scenery that was shown was that it was all snowing or a winter that had snowed, and there were no living people.

"The Winter After (Fake) recognizes you."

I felt a gaze. An icy cold gaze. It was definitely a gaze, but I could feel the coldness, and I shivered without realizing it.

Soon, the gaze disappeared.

,The Winter After, huh. It's a Divine Seat name I'm hearing for the first time, but... it's not hard to guess.'

The volcanic island area had a theme, or rather, a subject. The Knights of the Apocalypse and the Dragon of the Apocalypse were it.

,I felt hunger while walking through the snowfield. A Knight of the Apocalypse related to hunger is... the Black Knight of Famine.'

Then what are the landscapes that the ice surface is reflecting now? I roughly guess that they're probably other worlds. The 'God's Atlantis' world is a world where countless Earths exist. There are probably a huge number of destroyed Earths as well.

I flicked the lantern.

Instead of the scenery of the destroyed world that had been reflected until now, a familiar modern appearance appeared. Even the signs were familiar Korean. A river was visible at the end of the scenery.

Two men were walking in the center of the scenery. One was Kang Myungjin-nim.

"Hyung-nim. How was this week's God's Atlantis?"

"It wasn't bad. Except for the fact that there are unnecessarily many hidden elements."

"Hidden elements are the fun elements of God's Atlantis!"

I could hear the two men's conversation.

Judging from this, it seemed like it was a past memory of Kang Myungjin-nim. Kang Myungjin-nim's expression was gloomy, and his eyes were cold. Judging from that atmosphere, it seems he already knew what state he was in.

,Is he enjoying Seoul for the first time in a while in his own way?'

I moved the lantern. The angle of the light changed, and Rilsner appeared.

She was hunting beasts with other female elves in the forest.

"Rilsner. You're doing well for your first time?"

"Leila couldn't even hit an arrow at first because she was so surprised."

"This is nothing."

Rilsner bluffed. She was smiling brightly.

I shook the lantern again.

What was visible this time was Joo Seohyun-ssi wearing a dobok in an empty dojang. In front of her, holding a wooden sword, was a scarecrow.

Her hair was shorter than the Joo Seohyun-ssi I knew. Her height, chest, and butt were also smaller. There was still baby fat on her face, so she looked like a high school student.

"I'll kill you, Seong Yujin...!!"

Pakpakpak! Joo Seohyun-ssi relentlessly beat the scarecrow.

There's no need to worry about Joo Seohyun-ssi.

I was too lazy to hold the lantern, so I put it down on the floor. Another scene appeared.

This time it was Elena. A young Elena who looked about 7 years old. She was sitting crookedly in front of a desk, reading a thick book.

Then, she suddenly turned her head. Was it a coincidence? The direction was exactly the direction I was in.

Young Elena and I made eye contact.

"You've come. I was bored, so this is good. Come this way."

Elena gestured. And I was sucked into her world.
