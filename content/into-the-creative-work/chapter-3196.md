Time stopped.

What was slightly unexpected was that time had stopped even though I'd used my Domain. Even a Domain that twists the laws of reality can't stand against time stoppage.

'In battles between gods, the Domain is the power meant to prevent exactly this kind of phenomenon. And yet, if the Domain can't exert any power at all... Am I using the Domain wrong? Or is it a limitation of the Domain when channeled through Hwaryeon Bido?'

Either way, there didn't seem to be anything I could do right now.

"...To think you can stop time. As expected, it's His power."

Bruce approaches me slowly, his expression brimming with confidence. He's certain of victory and utterly off his guard.

'Yeah. If I just release Cheonsim on a whim, that's the end of it.'

The bastard probably doesn't have the faintest clue that I'm thinking perfectly clearly. Even if Brahma-sen's power stopped the world's time, it can't stop my mind.

'Right before he kills me, I use Cheonsim and kill him in return. If it works, I only need to kill him one more time.'

Thankfully, Bruce is approaching from the front. Right now, unable to move my pupils or use mana, my senses are inevitably limited. So Bruce striding straight toward me is almost something to be grateful for.

'I could probably use Cheonan to shift my viewpoint, but... he might sense something off, so I deliberately held back.'

Bruce raises his right fist. He looks ready to smash my head open just like that. He doesn't swing immediately—he starts gathering mana first.

'His experience may be lacking, but his reaction speed is S-rank level. I need to wait for a slightly more certain timing.'

Even if it's razor-thin, that's fine.

I have Flash, and in a pinch, Full Recovery. Even with options in hand, there's no worry about failure.

Just before Bruce's right fist moved, there was light.

From the sky of the time-frozen world, a pure white light rose and spread across the entire world. In that light, I felt a momentary warmth.

'...It's an energy I've felt somewhere before. The Goddess's power, perhaps.'

If Brahma-sen had directly intervened in his own world, then the Goddess must have intervened in hers as well. Though it was a bit surprising that she'd intervene to save me, when I'm not even her Hero.

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 8]

I can't let this opportunity slip.

The moment the world began moving again, I swung my sword at the flustered Bruce. Bruce belatedly snapped to his senses and threw the fist he'd prepared.

'The trajectory of that fist is obvious.'

A fist with no martial technique whatsoever, only raw power.

The instant I read his shoulder muscles, his gaze, his direction, I twisted my body. The fist missed, and my blade sliced across Bruce's neck.

Flames burst from his body. He blocked them with his Domain. In that gap, his body distanced itself from me. He'd used the flames as propulsion.

Krrzzzt!

Red lightning mixed with Domain erupted from my blade and scattered the flames.

In that brief moment. The head that had flown off returned and settled back onto his neck. Bruce rose with a shallow breath.

"...I don't understand. For someone who isn't even a Hero, the Goddess used her power to intervene."

"That just means the Goddess doesn't like you either."

The pure white light illuminating the night sky hadn't disappeared—it remained.

I could feel Bruce's momentum plummet.

The Goddess's power was pushing back Brahma-sen's.

"It's only temporary. He is within me! As long as He resides within me, I will not lose!"

Bruce bellowed. At that, I chuckled. The composure had vanished from his voice. In the end, even he knows. That this situation is the worst possible.

"Now you can't revive, and you can't use Brahma-sen's power to stop time like before either."

"...You noticed the time stop?"

"Did you really think I wouldn't?"

"How did you know? Did the Goddess tell you?!"

I didn't answer his question. If I plant false information that I have some kind of relationship with the Goddess, it might prove useful later. Even if not, I had no desire to satisfy his curiosity.

Thunder Heaven Style—Thunder Blade.

A Domain-enhanced slash flies forward.

Bruce couldn't respond with the same composure as before. With a frantic expression, he hurriedly moved his body to dodge.

'His physical abilities are S-rank level, so wide, telegraphed slashes won't connect.'

[Using Acceleration. Lasts 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 7]

Thunder Heaven Style—Swift Wind, Sudden Thunder.

I sprinted across the ground.

The distance closing in an instant.

Bruce, startled, raised a curtain of flames around himself. Since the flames were mixed with Brahma-sen's power, I didn't recklessly approach.

'This bastard. His way of using his abilities has turned defensive.'

In a word, he's scared shitless of dying.

'Lightning.'

Red lightning imbued with Domain tears through the curtain of flames. My eyes met the flustered bastard's. He waved his hand toward me.

Sand surged up from all directions and poured down at me.

The sheer blatancy of the ability almost made me laugh.

'You think you can catch me—someone whose combat style is openly speed-focused—with a sluggish attack like this?'

Setting aside time stop since it operates on a different plane entirely, the flames are threatening in their own right, so I can't ignore those. But sand? Did he really think sand attacks—far slower than flames—would actually work on me?

I stepped into my footwork. My body shot forward, evading the pouring sand. The sand writhed, trying to grab my ankles, but I was already out of that position and charging toward Bruce.

Thunder Heaven Style—Jade Stream.

I use the illusion technique.

I hadn't used Jade Stream recently. Against A-rank or higher enemies, Jade Stream is a technique that barely works. A-rank and above—whether Hunters or monsters—amplify their strength and senses with mana and don't rely solely on sight.

Conversely, against the rabble that Jade Stream works well on, there was no reason to bother using it. Against those, I could kill them with a casual wave of my hand without needing any technique.

“……!!”

Bruce's pupils darted wildly, seemingly thrown off by my movement.

'Thanks to his S-rank physical abilities, he's not completely fooled, but... he seems uncertain.'

Bruce reaches out to grab my shoulder. His hand passes through empty air, not even grazing my shoulder.

Schlck.

The blade pierces through Bruce's heart.

Bruce's expression contorts with shock, terror, and pain. But that too is brief. Soon, a smile spreads across his face. Both eyes gleamed with madness.

"He promised."

Squelch.

Bruce's right hand seizes the blade of Hwaryeon Bido. His palm is sliced open, blood pouring out, yet he doesn't let go—he holds it firm. With his other left hand, he grabs my shoulder. Just as disgust wells up and I'm about to kick him off—

Hands emerge from Bruce's shoulders, forearms, thighs, calves, and the tops of his feet, seizing my body. The power of Brahma-sen manifesting through those hands binds me in place.

"You son of a bitch... Are you trying to self-destruct?"

As a self-destruction expert myself, I immediately recognized his intent.

"When the time comes, He will descend upon Earth, and all of humanity will bow their heads before Him. You will die here with me, and I shall be called by Him when the time comes, resurrected to enjoy glory."

"This bastard... His eyes are completely gone. He's not even listening to me."

"Ah! O God! Ruler of all things! Lord of Madness! I shall devote myself to you!!"

Fwoooosh!

Bruce began burning, using his own body as kindling.

[Cannot use Leap.]

Brahma-sen's power interferes with Leap. In desperation, I wrapped my Domain around my body. The Domain is gradually being consumed by the flames.

'At this rate, I'll actually burn to death. Should I use Cheonsim to escape? Or should I just self-destruct too and use Full Recovery?'

Cheonsim and Full Recovery. If I have to play one of these two cards... it's Cheonsim over Full Recovery, after all.

[Activating Cheonsim. Lasts 1 minute.]

The power binding my body vanishes.

Even if it was a god's power, it couldn't hold me. Feeling the infinite mana granted by Cheonsim's effect, I focused not on mana but on moving my limbs.

I kicked Bruce's body and swung Hwaryeon Bido, slicing through his upper body. Bruce's burning body fell backward. The roaring flames gradually died down. By the time the flames vanished, Bruce's corpse had turned to ash and was carried away by the blowing wind.

Drip. Drip drip.

Raindrops fell from the sky. I tilted my head back and looked at the night sky. The Goddess's power became rain, swiftly suppressing the city's fires.

'Is it because this is the Murim world rather than Earth? The Goddess is exerting quite a bit of power.'

The Goddess's power countered Brahma-sen's quite effectively. Of course, that doesn't mean the Goddess is as strong as Brahma-sen.

This world is the Goddess's home ground, so there's probably some kind of correction applied.

'Unlike the God of Earth who abandoned Earth, the Goddess doesn't seem to have abandoned her own world. If we'd fought on Earth, she would've been even more of a nuisance.'

Though if it weren't for me, Brahma-sen probably wouldn't have intervened so openly in the first place.

'In the end, Brahma-sen has failed again.'

It wasn't as though there was no damage.

30% of Beroprin City had burned to the ground.

"Uwaaaaaaah!! My cityyyyyy!!!"

Park Su-ho's scream echoed thunderously from the city center. If the Goddess hadn't sent down rain, more than half the city would have vanished.

'There must be hundreds of casualties too. In monetary terms... well, it's not my city so I don't know the details, but it'll be astronomical losses. Park Su-ho poured his entire fortune into growing this city, even going into debt...'

Honestly speaking, Park Su-ho was almost pitiable.

'I should hold back on messing with Park Su-ho for a while.'

Two hours later. Park Su-ho, having handled urgent matters, bowed with his head pressed to the ground at my feet.

"Hyung-nim! This is the request of a lifetime! Please lend me 50 billion won!! I need that much budget to urgently restore the city!"

"Su-ho, Su-ho. Didn't you already fail to repay the 30 billion you borrowed from me last time?"

"I keep investing everything I earn into the city... Keuk. I'll definitely repay you this time, hyung!"

He doesn't say it outright, but he's asking for an interest-free loan. If he borrowed from the Association or a bank, interest would pile up. And Park Su-ho already had bank debt.

What an absurd bastard. I wondered if he saw me as a pushover, but then I remembered all the beauties I'd scored thanks to Park Su-ho—and not just one or two.

'50 billion or so should be fine. I do need money for the Harem Tower construction, but as an S-rank Hunter, I can earn 50 billion in no time if I put my mind to it.'

I put on a good-natured smile and personally lifted Park Su-ho up from where he was prostrate on the floor.

"No need to grovel over a little money, Park Su-ho! Someone once said—don't live on your knees, stand up and die! I'll lend you a measly 50 billion, as much as you need!"

"Hyung!! Thank you! Thank you! I'm overwhelmingly grateful!!"

"You little shit. Your quip game's improved. Oh, and Nella said she wants to be my woman. That's fine, right?"

"...Excuse me?"

"You two weren't even dating anyway."

"...That's true, but... Did you maybe get with Nella, hyung?"

"Why would I? You know she's not my type, honestly. I like women with curves, remember. But Nella says she'll die if she can't have me, so what am I supposed to do."

"Yes, Nella said that?"

"I have no reason to lie about something like this."

"Th-The atmosphere between me and Nella has been good lately. Nella smiled when I talked to her too."

"Did Nella say she likes you?"

"Well, no, but..."

"You're deluding yourself. A woman smiling at you doesn't mean she has feelings for you. If it really bothers you, ask Nella directly and settle it."

"...What did Nella say she likes about you, hyung?"

"That I'm rich and strong. I'm an S-rank Hunter. Women naturally like capable men."

"I'm capable too, though."

"More than me?"

“…….”

Park Su-ho hung his head with a gloomy expression. He seemed to have accepted the reasoning immediately.

But a bastard whose total debt—including what he borrowed from me and his bank loans—exceeds 100 billion wanting to date is just thief's logic, isn't it? Even for an A-rank Hunter, 100 billion in debt is pretty damn rough.

"Su-ho. If you want to date, you'd better pay off your debts first."

Looking at the state of the city, repaying his debt within a year seemed impossible.

Honestly, that bastard Park Su-ho probably won't repay his debt until he becomes an S-rank Hunter.
