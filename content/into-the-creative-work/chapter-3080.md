"Jigon! Jigon!"

Seong Jigon charged at Garshin, swinging his sword. Garshin planted his spear shaft to block. His body was shoved straight back.

"...A warrior of your caliber just appears out of nowhere. I see. That low-ranking warrior was a summoner, then? Among the tribe's shamans, there was one who summoned monsters. He was a weakling suited to his level, but..."

Garshin's gaze alternated between me and Seong Jigon.

A summoner's obvious weakness. No matter how strong the summon, killing the summoner themselves ends it. That said, it's not an easy task. The summon won't just stand there idle, after all.

*'That bastard has subordinates too. He'll try to use them to get at me.'*

Contrary to my expectations, Garshin pulled his gaze away from me. His eyes turned toward Seong Jigon.

"That's no ordinary fighting spirit. What is your name?"

"Seong Jigon. You said you're a merman, right? No wonder you feel similar to a yokai."

"Yokai? You mean monsters. I'll take that as a compliment. May I ask one thing?"

"Don't care. Can't promise I'll answer, though."

"Why does a strong warrior like you serve someone weaker than yourself? That is a dishonorable thing."

"I don't know why serving the weak is dishonorable... Eugene is stronger than me."

"...Ho? So that summoner has some hidden trick up his sleeve. Good. I'll defeat you and find out slowly."

Garshin, gripping his spear tight, radiated fighting spirit as he lunged at Seong Jigon. Seong Jigon responded without any change in expression.

KWAAAAANG!

When the two clashed, the one unexpectedly pushed back was Seong Jigon.

*'If we're talking pure physical ability alone, Garshin is above Seong Jigon.'*

However, Seong Jigon's true power doesn't lie in physical ability. A sword aura resembling twilight shimmered into being along his blade.

It was the September Spirit Sword Art, learned from his master, the September Fairy. Golden moonlight sword aura began to slowly fill the dark night sky.

"A powerful ability!"

Garshin's mana, brimming with admiration, spread in all directions. Seawater surged up in the shape of blades to counter Seong Jigon's sword aura.

The two, who had been fighting on the warship, gradually expanded their battle domain into the air. Seong Jigon using void steps, Garshin using the surging seawater as footholds.

*'That Seong Jigon. He's gotten much stronger. His swordsmanship especially has developed a lot.'*

Seong Jigon's current level was Samjeong 3rd stage. One could say there had been no progress in the meantime, but... for Seong Jigon's pace, that's above average. I'm the one who climbs levels at an absurdly fast rate.

*'For Seong Jigon, that's incredibly fast.'*

Squelch.

I sensed movement beside me. Turning my head, the merfolk who had been underwater were climbing onto the warship. They'd scrambled up, unable to wait any longer.

Even if they weren't as strong as Garshin, I could feel a presence of at least B-rank or above.

The merman looked at me and grinned.

"Kihihi. There you were."

The merman pulled a curved sword from his back, flicking his tongue. His body was a sleek pale purple, his face shaped like a loach — disgusting.

"You see, I can tell who's weaker than me. Thanks to this ability, I've profited plenty. Looks like today's another profitable day. Kihihihi."

I looked around. The other hunters were so caught up in combat they couldn't spare a glance this way.

"Give up. There's no one to help you. Kihi. I'll kill you, and then that great warrior beside you too. Since he's a great warrior recognized by Lord Garshin... I'll get plenty of coins too, right? Really, my luck is good."

"I wasn't looking for someone to help me — I was looking for someone to dump this on. Everyone seems busy."

Lightning.

Crackle.

I launched the blue lightning conjured in my right hand at him in a surprise attack. He deflected the lightning with his mana-infused sword.

*'Stronger than Earth's hunters. Whatever else, merfolk are definitely superior to humans in physical ability. And these ones have even accumulated experience.'*

It wasn't that I couldn't kill him in a one-on-one fight. The problem was that other merfolk were also climbing onto the warship. The sudden shift from waiting for the ship to sink to an aggressive stance was probably because Chris had collapsed.

*'I don't want to deal with the hassle of those bastards.'*

Therefore, my choice was one thing.

『Using 1,000 coins to summon Yuria Grace.』

『Using 1,000 coins to summon Elena Waldert.』

They appeared.

Yuria maintained perfect balance even on the tilted deck, gracefully grasping the hem of her skirt as she bowed.

"You called, Master."

Elena, who had nearly slipped for a moment, immediately used magic to lift herself into the air. Then she looked around once and let out a hollow laugh.

"I expected you'd call us sooner than expected, but this is even more of a shitshow than I imagined."

Before I could even open my mouth to give them orders, the merman cut in.

"Human females! Weaker than me! Die!"

The stupid loach merman seemed to judge strength by physical ability and mana alone.

"Tch. There's one fish here who can't read the room."

Elena clicked her tongue.

Immediately after, the merman who had been charging suddenly face-planted. Even as he fell, true to being a warrior, he didn't let go of the sword in his hand. That was his worst mistake. As he tumbled, his arm twisted and the blade's tip stabbed into his own throat.

"Kueok?!"

A futile end that looked like pure coincidence to any onlooker. The dying bastard stared at empty air. Eyes gripped by terror. Eyes like he'd seen an unconquerable monster.

*'Elena's illusion magic. If you're caught in an illusion without prior intel, it's hard to notice right away. Especially Elena's illusions — even knowing about them, they're hard to block.'*

The merfolk especially seemed ignorant. They appeared to have no countermeasures against illusion magic whatsoever. Though using illusion magic at all is a rare thing to begin with.

"Elena, Yuria. Kill all the enemies. Ah, leave that one Jigon's fighting alone. Jigon will win anyway."

"I shall gladly carry out your command."

"So the bastards who ruined my stocks were all here. I can't just let this slide."

"Elena. I'll handle the enemies in the sea."

"Hm? Will you be alright? Aren't enemies in the sea the trickiest?"

"The night sea is no different from being submerged in darkness."

Yuria's shadow, which seemed especially dense, writhed.

"That's true. Then I'll... start with normalization."

Blue butterflies appeared around Elena and scattered in all directions.

The first thing that changed was the sinking warship. In the blink of an eye, as if it were a lie, the warship was restored to normal. The sections destroyed by battle scars were repaired, and the tilting deck regained its balance.

The miracle didn't end there. The hunters' injuries sustained in battle were healed. Chris, who had been paralyzed by poison and unable to move, also slowly raised his body.

"This is... impossible. Did she turn back time or something?"

Chris stared at Elena with a look of shock.

Elena's body, which had been floating in the air, collapsed limply. I caught her with both hands. Elena's heart wasn't beating.

*'She used up all her lifespan.'*

Full Recovery.

Elena opened her eyes again.

"Death is hard to get used to. With this much, I shouldn't need to move directly anymore, right?"

"Good work. Hey, Chris. Get to work."

Chris looked at me with complicated eyes.

"...Now I understand why you keep rewriting world records. An ability that summons such impossible beings."

"I said get to work."

Chris nodded once and looked up at the sky. After watching Seong Jigon and Garshin's battle for a moment, he began attacking the other merfolk. Light flashed.

Yuria headed toward the deck railing. She looked down at the sea. As night seas are, it was dark even with lighting. At best, the surface of the water was visible to the naked eye. Dozens of shark fins circled, and the heads of panicked merfolk stared up at the restored ship.

"What was that just now? The ship was sinking! Why is it suddenly fine?!"

"I don't know either. Did we all dream the same thing? Those human warriors are fine too."

"We'll just attack again and punch holes in it! Humans are no match for us in the sea anyway! Move, move!"

"What's that human female? Something feels off?"

"Kill her! She can't even come down into the sea anyway!"

The merfolk drew their weapons. What looked like perforated metal rods were guns with triggers. Their appearance differed somewhat from modern guns. The metal wasn't clean either — it had a crude feel.

The merfolk pulled their triggers and streams of seawater shot out at high pressure. Yuria dodged the attacks by retreating backward.

Bang bang bang bang!

Even though it was seawater, it had more destructive power than ordinary bullets.

"Kel kel kel kel."

The merfolk were cackling among themselves, finding something amusing.

"W-wait. Something's wrong! In the sea! There's something in the sea!"

"Have you gone crazy? Did you eat pufferfish poison or something? Give me a little too."

"Kihi. Earth's pufferfish poison gives a nice buzz. Anemone poison is too weak though."

"I'm not joking, you idiots! There's something in the sea... AAAAAAAGH!"

The merman was torn apart. Part of the sea turned red with his blood. The surrounding merfolk froze. They saw it.

"It's a current...! A black current appeared and tore the ship's hull!"

"Don't touch the black current! Run!"

"AAAAAAGH!"

The terrified merfolk dove into the sea and swam. But the black current was wherever they fled. To begin with, the underwater was so dark that distinguishing the black current was difficult.

"Attacks work on the black current! Stop running and attack it!"

There were a few brave merfolk. However, their struggle didn't last beyond 10 seconds. Even if they attacked and destroyed a black current, another appeared. The black currents were silent and stealthy, so no matter how familiar with the sea a merman was, they couldn't sense them without focus.

When one minute passed, not a single merfolk or man-eating shark remained alive underwater.

*'So this is Yuria's shadow magic. Shadow is darkness. In dark places, its efficiency and power are tremendous.'*

I turned my head and looked at the sky.

Now only one merman remained — Garshin. Their battle was also reaching its final stage.

Golden sword aura resembling moonlight gathered on Seong Jigon's blade, and Garshin wrapped his entire body in seawater, swelling his size more than fivefold.

"Human! No — Seong Jigon! I will remember your name!"

"Garshin, was it? I'll remember your name too."

Seong Jigon swung his sword.

A golden slash like a crescent moon flew out. In response, Garshin, now a giant wrapped in seawater, thrust his spear forward to break through, but the slash cut through his entire body along with his spear, splitting him in half.

『The Governor-General of Taiwan has died. Taiwan is liberated.』
