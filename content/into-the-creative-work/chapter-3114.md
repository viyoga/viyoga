The slash connected with Gashadokuro's body.

But it failed to cut him. Not a scratch—just the faintest trace left behind. Even that trace faded in under a second.

I wasn't surprised or disappointed. I'd expected this.

'This bastard tanks nukes like they're nothing, same as Kujira. If this attack had worked, that'd be the real surprise.'

This was a show. A spectacle. At least it looked flashy.

"Keh keh. Is that your best? All flash, no substance."

"Yeah. And it's something you can't do. Even if you got off your ass, I'm still higher up. Your hands can't reach me."

Gashadokuro stood about thirty meters tall. I, on the other hand, was floating over a hundred meters above. Even if he jumped, he'd never touch me.

"You can't attack me either. Repeat that pathetic strike a hundred times and it still won't leave a mark on my body. All you can do is throw your army at me. Bring them! I'll crush every last one!"

He grabbed a nearby tree. Ripped it out of the ground like a weed and hurled it at me. I didn't move. The tree sailed past me and fell. Hitting a target from dozens of meters away isn't easy.

I aimed my left fist—the one not holding the Condemnation Sword—at Gashadokuro.

Clack!

A stake-shaped round jutted out from the top of my left gauntlet.

Bzzzzzzzt.

I command the lightning. With perfect control, I laid down an electromagnetic rail.

Thunder Heaven Style—Railgun.

The round fired at Mach 10. To push beyond that, I'd need more electricity.

Mach 10.

Ten times the speed of sound.

The projectile punched through his shoulder the instant it left the barrel.

"GRAAAAAAGH!"

Gashadokuro writhed in agony.

Inside my helmet, my face was twisted.

'Was aiming for the head. Missed.'

Only hit him thanks to the AI's targeting assist. Without it, I might've missed entirely—just like when he threw that tree.

'To tighten my accuracy, better to close the distance? I've got three railgun rounds left. The transport chopper's nearby, so I can resupply.'

The nuclear generator on my back was feeding me massive amounts of electricity in real time. Meaning I could kill him safely from the sky, just spamming the railgun.

'Only problem is, from the viewer's perspective I look like a coward. Doesn't matter for hunting footage, but… this is promotional and achievement footage.'

Should I go down there, accept a bit of risk? It's not like I'd lose to that thing anyway.

"I'LL KILL YOU!!!"

"Shut up. Besides screaming, what can you actually do?"

"You're not the only one who can wield lightning!!"

Gashadokuro roared. Blue lightning erupted from his body. Befitting his massive frame, the instantaneous output—brief as it was—was enough to overwhelm me.

'Reverse-controlling his lightning and using it myself… that won't work. Hard to seize control. Is it the mental resistance he has?'

Still, he can't do anything to me with lightning. Any current that touches my body directly, I can suppress instantly with my will.

Wuuuuuum!

My body, floating in the sky, lurched. Suddenly it felt heavy. More precisely, the power armor I was wearing—Eternity—grew heavy.

'Gravity. No, magnetic attraction.'

He wasn't using lightning directly—he was amplifying his magnetic force to pull me down.

'So he doesn't have the Dullard trait. Feels like being crushed by sheer output, but still—a guy not even half a year old operating lightning to this level is impressive.'

Doesn't matter.

He can't manipulate electromagnetic force with my level of precision. My descent slowed. I was blocking the magnetic pull from below using my own lightning.

That's when it happened.

A man appeared abruptly in front of me. Sharp-featured, wearing a military uniform.

An esper with spatial teleportation. Sagashiwa Shigeru.

'Hiding and waiting for his moment, then jumping in. Timing wasn't bad. But what can a worm like you do?'

The Eternity I'm wearing is a custom power armor. Point-blank rifle fire barely scratches it.

I was about to ignore him, but when I saw what he was holding, I reprioritized.

'This bastard! A Knight's ultra-vibration blade knife…!'

Looks like he looted it off a Knight. Knights aren't invincible—it happens from time to time.

Even so, an ultra-vibration blade is dangerous.

I discharged lightning to kill Sagashiwa Shigeru.

"Kuhk!"

Sagashiwa Shigeru gritted his teeth through the electrocution. No special equipment visible. Meaning he took a lethal-level current bare-bodied and survived.

'So he pre-loaded drugs.'

Gashadokuro's magnetic manipulation slammed my body down to the ground. No time to respond.

'Even if I hit the ground, I kill Sagashiwa Shigeru first. Leaving a teleporter alive makes the next fight more annoying.'

Thwack!

His ultra-vibration blade buried itself in my helmet. Even an ultra-vibration blade can't slice through solid metal instantly. Cutting takes time.

'Rather than teleporting away, he decided to kill me here. That resolve is admirable.'

I grabbed his head with my left hand and gripped the Condemnation Sword with my right. I tried pushing him off with my left—he gritted his teeth and held. His grip strength felt stronger than most mutants.

'Damn enhancement drugs.'

By now the helmet was pierced. Through the narrow gap, my eyes met Sagashiwa Shigeru's. The drugs had turned his eyes bloodshot, veins bulging against his skin.

Sagashiwa Shigeru's pupils gleamed with the ecstasy of victory, as if he'd already won.

"Die, monster!"

Schlck.

The ultra-vibration blade knife drove into my right eye. The blade pierced through in an instant and lodged in my brain. I activated my regeneration. Flesh shredded by the ultra-vibration knife regenerated in real time. The monstrous agony of that process—all I could do was endure it.

Combat drugs wouldn't help me anyway. And Jenna can't force-inject drugs into my body without my permission.

I drove the Condemnation Sword in my right hand through Sagashiwa Shigeru's torso. Impaled on the massive blade, he vomited blood, face contorted in horror.

"Why—why won't you die, you monster!"

"You're annoying, fly."

The moment I moved to twist the Condemnation Sword buried in his body—

KWAOOOOOONG.

My body crashed into the ground. The magnetic force had dragged me down after all. From high above, Gashadokuro's mocking laughter echoed.

"Keh keh. You were acting so tough—how's it feel to be planted in the dirt?"

The impact rattled my insides and damaged parts of the power armor. But through regeneration, my body and the armor were restored in an instant.

I stood up, pulled the ultra-vibration blade knife from my eye, and tossed it to the ground. The helmet repaired itself, and thermal imaging gave me a clear read of my surroundings.

At my feet, Sagashiwa Shigeru's corpse lay sprawled.

'Because of this bastard, I ended up on the ground.'

To vent, I stomped Sagashiwa Shigeru's head until it burst.

Fwooooom.

The swing of Gashadokuro's arm kicked up a massive gust, blasting the dust away in an instant. The hunched giant looked down at me with four eyes.

"You've fallen into my domain. You were so cocky up there… now before me, you're nothing but an insect!"

"Shut up. You don't even know your place."

I raised my clenched left fist and aimed it at Gashadokuro's head.

Thunder Heaven Style—Railgun.

The railgun round punched through Gashadokuro's head and shot into the sky. Just as cracks spread and his skull was about to shatter, time seemed to rewind—his head returned to normal.

Automatic activation of regeneration.

'I heard it from Oneko. Q's brain melted and he still regenerated back to life. So it's less about instant-death immunity and more about instinct-driven regeneration.'

Destroying the brain doesn't kill him. Probably even decapitation—he'd regenerate before the head hit the ground. Cauterize the wound like some myth? No. His regeneration heals the burn too.

'Keep it simple. Kill him until he dies. His stamina and mental fortitude aren't infinite.'

Same goes for me.

Becoming Gashadokuro boosted his stamina and mental reserves to absurd levels? The larger body would've increased the cost of regeneration proportionally.

"That hurts!!"

Gashadokuro, enraged, swung both arms at me.

Fast.

Thunder Heaven Style—Gale Thunderclap.

But I'm faster. Neural acceleration activates. I dodged his attack and moved to counter—then stopped.

Eternity, the power armor I'm wearing, couldn't keep up with my speed.

Thunder Heaven Style—Flying Thunder God.

Crackle.

Blue current sparked. The current moved by my will, grabbing and pulling my body and Eternity along. Movements impossible with Eternity's own output and speed alone became possible.

Shhhk!

The Condemnation Sword sliced through Gashadokuro's wrist.

Gashadokuro, with four arms, swung another. To me, moving in the accelerated world of Gale Thunderclap, it looked sluggish.

I cut through the other incoming arms and kicked off the air, leaping up.

THWAM!

I drove the Condemnation Sword into the top of his head and poured my full weight downward. My body dropped like an elevator, splitting his body open. The odd feeling of slicing through clone Q's upper torso attached to his chest—the face was identical to mine.

'He's a fake anyway. Thinking of it that way, this suddenly feels satisfying.'

Tap.

Landing on the ground, I used regeneration to restore the chipped edge of the Condemnation Sword. Gashadokuro regenerated his severed arms and split body in three seconds.

'Annoying. Is this what my enemies feel when they face me?'

No.

It's only "annoying" because it's me. Anyone else would've been in a hopeless situation.

"This is nothing!!"

He screamed and charged. I stepped forward.

Thirty minutes.

That's how long it took for Gashadokuro to collapse on the ground, his will to fight gone.

His limbs severed, he could no longer regenerate. Mental fortitude and stamina—both were scraping bottom.

"Damn it… if only I hadn't been born as just a clone…"

He didn't even have the energy to speak through his main body—he spoke through the clone attached to his chest.

"Pointless hypothetical."

"…Just kill me already. This shitty world. Should've just fucking ended. No—it's already ending, isn't it. Keh keh."

"Not going to beg for your life?"

"Original. Did you forget whose clone I am? If I were you… I wouldn't let anyone who looks exactly like me live. The very fact that something like that exists in the same world is disgusting."

"Agreed."

I drove the Condemnation Sword into his body. It pierced through both the upper torso and Gashadokuro's heart simultaneously.

But he didn't die immediately. His regeneration kept him alive until the very last moment.

"…Oneko. I only ever needed you…"

The subjugation of Gashadokuro was over.
