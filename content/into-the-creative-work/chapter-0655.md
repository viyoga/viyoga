Jerdin, who had been hiding in the luggage cart, no longer hid and confidently revealed himself. Thanks to that, we were able to easily enter the Nello Oman barony. But things didn't go as smoothly as I had expected.

The reason he doesn't approach his cousin Nello Oman seems to be because he's afraid of catching the disease.

"Nello Oman!"

Jerdin jumped up from his seat and approached the bed. He didn't hold Nello Oman's hand or anything.

"Jerdin... help.... Help me... Jerdin...."

Billmad. He was the head butler of the Nello Oman barony. We followed the butler into the reception room.

Jerdin's voice was irritable. He was scolding the head butler in front of him. The head butler said with a darkened complexion.

"The wizards were terrified and gave up on this matter. It was because the level of the curse was so high that it was beyond their common sense. 1 billion Ner could not hold the wizards back."

He came to get help, but now he has to give help. He can't ignore it because he desperately needs Nello Oman's help right now.

That meant it wasn't a curse manifested by magic. I became puzzled. Was it possible to cast a curse in this world in a way other than magic?

,It's possible if it's an ancient artifact.

The single word ancient artifact could explain everything. It was an almighty word.

"I think you are talking about the Elst's leaf."

That meant it actually existed.
