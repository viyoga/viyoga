Hephaestus kept swinging the hammer through my body. Every time he swung the hammer, pieces of stars burst and emitted a sparkling light. It was quite a fantastic sight.

But after a while, it got boring. I belatedly checked the notification window that had popped up earlier.

「Great Fortune (EX) is activated.」

「Great Fortune (EX) will be deactivated for the time being.」

'Hephaestus-nim. Great Fortune was activated earlier, but do meteor showers usually happen?'

If it's a skill that causes meteor showers, it's an amazing skill. Isn't it even better than the Astrape (EX) skill I just got?

'Abilities related to luck are not abilities that cause impossible things to happen. It's closer to confirming things with very small probabilities.'

'That alone seems amazing....'

'The meteor shower was something that was going to happen someday. Your luck just changed it to now. No matter how lucky you are, you can't twist the cause. Don't take luck for granted, and don't trust it either.'

'You can't twist the cause? Then what is the cause of the meteor shower?'

'Even a god doesn't know everything. But if I had to guess... somewhere in the universe must have broken.'

'......Is it okay to talk about such a huge disaster so calmly?'

'There are often gods who are crazy with boredom and run wild. As a result, natural disasters such as meteor showers and tsunamis can occur.'

'Isn't that dangerous?'

'The other gods nearby will take care of it, so don't worry. This is an important task, so don't talk to me.'

Hephaestus started to concentrate. The sound of hammering became louder and clearer. I don't know much, but seeing the space ripple and the lava swirling every time he hammered, it seems like it's no ordinary task.

I held back from asking how the star fragments would help the Hwaryeon Bido. If I asked a question and disturbed Hephaestus's concentration, I would be the only one who would lose out if he made a mistake.

'My hands are starting to hurt.... I hope he finishes quickly.'

Isn't he a blacksmith god? Shouldn't he just be able to complete it in a flash?

Kkang!

I suddenly came to my senses at the sound of the hammer, which was different from before. The Hwaryeon Bido was being finished. The heat decreased, the lava hardened, and the sparkling star fragments could no longer be seen.

The Hwaryeon Bido was completely repaired. On the outside, it didn't look much different. However, in my eyes, with Hephaestus possessed, I could see the energy lingering inside the Hwaryeon Bido.

This could no longer be called a simple knife. It was a kind of work of art in itself.

Unfortunately, even though I saw it through Hephaestus's eyes, I couldn't tell how powerful the Hwaryeon Bido was. Because I don't have Hephaestus's experience.

'Even if you use the knife roughly, it won't break. Even if it does break, it will recover on its own.'

'Are you telling me not to use it roughly?'

'Use it roughly. Tools exist to be used. What's the point of cherishing tools?'

'Is that all the enhanced Hwaryeon Bido's abilities are?'

'You can use its original abilities.'

'Are you talking about attribute enhancement? I'm glad it was quite useful.'

When you use lightning through the Hwaryeon Bido, the lightning turns red. It's not just the color that changes, but the power has also increased. I'm glad this ability didn't disappear.

'Because you used Fafnir's heart, you also gained the ability of Tam.'

'What kind of ability is it?'

I asked, full of anticipation. I didn't hunt Fafnir for nothing. I wanted a powerful ability, so I killed Fafnir and used his heart as a material.

'If you stain the blade with the enemy's presence... to put it more simply, if you stain it with blood, you can temporarily use the enemy's abilities. However, once you use it, it will take time to reuse it.'

'It's an ability that perfectly copies the opponent's abilities!'

'No. It depends on your capabilities as the owner.'

'Does that mean if I'm weak... I won't be able to use that ability properly?'

'You understood correctly.'

'.......'

In the end, it was an ability that depended on me. Should I like this? Suddenly, it doesn't feel like a very good ability.

'Didn't you put in other materials... meteors? Aren't there other abilities?'

'You can use a domain. You won't be able to use it often.'

'What is a domain?'

'It's about creating your own space around you and twisting the laws to your advantage.... Hmm. It's hard to explain in words. Domains are often used by gods when fighting for authority. They collide using this domain. Since you are human, there will be limits to maintaining the domain.'

'I think I understand roughly.'

'You understand?!'

'...Is it that surprising?'

'How can a human without divine power understand a domain?'

'The inside of the furnace. Wasn't that a domain?'

'...That's right.'

Hephaestus seemed genuinely surprised. How much did this bastard look down on me usually to show such a reaction? I barely held back from swearing.

'How did you know right away?'

'Have you forgotten what trials I overcame?'

'Ah. That's right. I forgot for a moment. You became a father in the trials and used divine power recklessly. Right. You used the domain several times. I think the explanation is over. I'll be going now.'

'No, wait a minute. Aren't there any other abilities?'

'There aren't.'

'Doesn't the Hwaryeon Bido sprout an ego... didn't you say it had a will last time?'

'Will and ego are different. The Hwaryeon Bido already has a will and recognizes you as its master. The possibility of that becoming an ego... it's not entirely impossible, but it's a very slim chance.'

'Is that so. But don't cursed swords and demon swords talk to their users?'

'That's not an ego, it's just evil spirits clinging to the weapon.'

'......Changing the question, will it not be corrupted even if I kill humans in the future?'

'It will remain unchanged even if you kill hundreds of millions, trillions of humans.'

'What about non-human beings?'

'I don't know.'

I checked the information of the Hwaryeon Bido.

「Hwaryeon Bido

???

Rank: ???」

I was embarrassed. Because originally, the information window of the Hwaryeon Bido was different. An SS-rank weapon based on Atlantis standards.

'Hephaestus-nim. Why is this information like this?'

'It must be because of the domain.'

Hephaestus answered as if he was annoyed and left. I've felt it since the trials, but Hephaestus doesn't seem interested in anything other than blacksmithing.

As Hephaestus disappeared, my body was naturally pushed out of the smithy.

I feel the heat.

"Hot!"

Startled, I ran towards the still-open portal.

「You have entered the 4,000th District, Mount Olympus.」

The heat I felt disappeared. I wiped my forehead with the back of my hand. I was drenched in sweat. Sweat was already dripping down.

'I can't go to that area without heat countermeasures.'

I had no regrets.

Unlike its nice-sounding name, the God's Smithy is very difficult to use.

Rather, I looked at the Hwaryeon Bido, which had been enhanced along with the repair. There were no cracked parts. The straight, red blade is even beautiful.

"You're back, Hwaryeon Bido!"

Uuung.

The blade trembled as if responding to my words. It couldn't be just my imagination.

I swung the Hwaryeon Bido in the air. The weight and center of the knife hadn't changed, but the sharpness of the blade had become more brutal.

I smiled with satisfaction after swinging the knife a few more times.

'This is the feel. I missed this feel.'

Lightning.

Pajijijik.

A red current flashed along the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido. You can tell just by looking at it. It's lightning that has been further enhanced through the Hwaryeon Bido.

'It would be difficult to use the Hwaryeon Bido's abilities here.'

I went outside the Temple of Olympus. If I used my power recklessly inside the temple, I might receive the curse of the gods. I knew very well that the gods of Olympus were surprisingly petty.

'After all, it would still be difficult to gain control of Mount Olympus.'

I flew out of Mount Olympus with the Valkyrie's wings. At the same time, I focused my eyes and scanned the ground. I needed a target to use the Hwaryeon Bido. A moderately strong monster would be fine.

'The guys who always show up and bother me are nowhere to be seen when I actually need them.'

I found a monster by the lake. It was a giant snake with nine heads.

'It's a Hydra.'

I knew it well because I had killed it in the trials.

'Can I handle it now?'

I compare the Hydra in the trials with the Hydra in front of me now.

I don't know. In the trials, it was like a bug crawling on the ground, but now it felt like a formidable enemy that couldn't be ignored.

'It must be Hydra's prestige. It will be weaker than the Hydra in the trials.'

The Hydra also felt my gaze and raised its body. After a moment of hesitation, I decided to fight it.

'If things go wrong, I'll run away.'

Complete recovery was still on cooldown, so I couldn't use it. I could use Cheonsim, so I wasn't afraid of poison.

The Hydra opened its mouth wide towards me. Poison was sprayed from all nine mouths and flew towards me.

'It reaches this far?!'

Surprised, I hurriedly dodged the poison and flew to the side.

'Damn it. If I were Zeus, I would have ignored whatever poison it had and torn all its heads off with my bare hands!'

Lamenting the lack of power, I moved my mana.

'Astrape!'

A thick bolt of lightning fell from the sky onto the Hydra. Two of the Hydra's heads were cut off and burned.

'Seeing it instantly finish off two Hydra heads, the power is outstanding as befitting an EX rank. It's also so fast that it would be difficult for most guys to react.'

The downside is that it consumes a lot of mana. I had to think of another way to use Astrape efficiently.

The Hydra screams and indiscriminately shoots out poison.

'Valkyrie's Shield.'

I approached it with the Valkyrie's Shield in front.

Chiiik!

The poison began to melt the surface of the shield. I was shocked. An S-rank shield is melting so easily!

'Isn't the Hwaryeon Bido going to melt too?! No, I trust Hephaestus's skills!'

If it's going to melt from Hydra's poison, wouldn't it be better to just melt? I cut through the Hydra's poison flying towards the tip of the knife. The Hydra's poison got on the blade, but it didn't melt.

I landed on the lake. Thanks to [Blessing of Water], I was able to stand on the water.

I stumbled as I ran towards the Hydra. Fortunately, I was able to regain my balance before falling.

'Have I become too accustomed to Zeus's body....'
