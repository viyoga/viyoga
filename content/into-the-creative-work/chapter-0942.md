I made a date with Kang Ji-young-ssi, who felt sorry for me because of the Maruhan Academy assassin incident. It was actually a dinner appointment, but after I whined appropriately, it became an official date.

Of course, I know what Kang Ji-young-ssi is thinking. She probably thinks it's just a date in name only and no different from dinner. And that's actually right. Dinner or a date, it's all the same.

,Still, the feeling is different because of the difference in words.'

The appointment time is 3 PM.

I arrived at the meeting place an hour early and was waiting for Kang Ji-young-ssi.

,Kang Ji-young-ssi doesn't see me as a man. She only sees me as a student and a friend's son.'

The age difference between her and me was the problem. About 20 years. Narrowing that gap isn't easy. But it wasn't like there was no hope at all.

"I thought I came early... I made you wait."

Kang Ji-young-ssi appeared at the meeting place. She stood out. A towering height of 180cm and long, silky, black-brown hair. Her outfit was casual. A gray blouse and navy blue slacks. It suited her well. Well, basically, Kang Ji-young-ssi, who is tall, busty, and has big hips, would look good in anything unless it was a complete disaster.

"You came 10 minutes early. Were you that excited about today's date?"

Kang Ji-young-ssi smirked as if she was dumbfounded.

"It's normal to arrive 10 minutes before the appointment time. When did you arrive?"

"2 o'clock."

"...You came an hour early? You?"

Kang Ji-young-ssi looked at me with suspicious eyes. It's already been a few years since she and I got to know each other. Even though we don't meet often, we've roughly figured out each other's personalities.

"I was just that excited for today."

"...Let me tell you in advance. It's a date for now, as you want, but there's nothing special. We'll wander around a bit, have dinner, and then part ways."

"Why are you like this? We agreed in advance. We're supposed to part at 10 o'clock. We have to hang out after dinner."

"Okay. The moment it hits exactly 10 PM, I'm going back without looking back. It doesn't matter what you say. Got it?"

"I got it. I won't whine. But please take the date seriously."

"A promise is a promise, so I'll take it seriously. What should I do?"

"Is this your first time on a date?"

"It's my first time. Strangely, men don't approach me very often."

Kang Ji-young-ssi said bluntly. I think I knew the reason why. When you look at her, most men are naturally overwhelmed. Her innate charisma and basically expressionless face. Above all, the strength exuded by an S-class hero.

She's not a woman that any man can easily covet.

"I'll call you by your first name during the date. Is that okay?"

"...Is there really a need to do that?"

Yes, there is.

Names are important between men and women. Especially between her and me. Try calling your date 'Auntie' during the date. Will that create a date atmosphere? Absolutely not. Rather, the opposite will happen, and it will ruin the subtle atmosphere of the date.

It's not bad to feel a sense of depravity, but right now, she and I weren't at the level of forming a sense of depravity.

"It's a date."

"...Alright. What should I call you?"

"Call me 'Oppa'."

"Haha."

Kang Ji-young-ssi laughed. And she grabbed my head. Her hand tightened.

"Kuaaaaaaak!"

It was a pain that felt like my head was breaking.

"S-Stop! It was a joke, a joke! Just call me whatever you want!"

"That was a boring joke."

Kang Ji-young-ssi let go of my head. My head, freed from her, still throbbed.

"...Instead, I'll call you whatever I want too. Ji-young-ah."

"......I'll only allow it until 10 o'clock."

Kang Ji-young-ssi didn't seem to like it, but she soon allowed it. Kang Ji-young-ssi isn't a cold-blooded person. She grants requests that don't cross the line if possible.

,For now, I've buttoned the first button well....'

Now all that's left is to create the atmosphere.

"By the way, Ji-young-ah."

I called Kang Ji-young-ssi. Kang Ji-young-ssi frowned and glared at me, then sighed and relaxed her expression.

"......It feels awkward."

She had a face that looked like she had attained enlightenment.

"Why aren't people paying attention to Ji-young-ah, to you?"

Kang Ji-young-ssi clenched her fist tightly. There was no sign of murderous intent or anger. She was just cringing from the awkwardness.

"...My face is known. Most people know that I'm the head of Maruhan Academy. If it becomes known that I'm on a date with a student, there will be problems in many ways. Especially your mother... Hooo. Just thinking about it is terrible."

With that, Kang Ji-young-ssi threw a ring at me. Glancing at her hand, I saw the same ring on her right index finger.

"A couple ring?"

"It's a ring with perception-lowering magic on it. It'll be a headache if our identities are revealed, so you wear it too."

"How long does the effect last?"

"It was made in a hurry, so the limit is about a day."

That's a shame. It didn't seem like I could take it to reality.

I put the ring on my right index finger, just like her. I felt all the gazes of the women who were glancing at me disappear.

,Experiencing the effect makes me feel even more regretful.'

Before starting the date in earnest, I checked Kang Ji-young-ssi's status window. To check her favorability.

『Name: Kang Ji-young

Strength: S+ Stamina: SS Agility: A+ Durability: S+ Mana: A+

Traits: Eye of Horus (SS), Earth Wolf (S)

Skills: Greatsword (S), Magic (A), Sixth Sense (S), Super Strength (A), Natural Recovery (S).

Favorability: 55』

Favorability 55.

Good. 50 or higher is a score that feels favorable. In other words, it's the level of feeling romantic emotions. Of course, that's only based on the game. Reality isn't a game, so you can't immediately start dating just because your favorability is over 50.

,The important thing is that there's a possibility.'

•••

The date course I prepared was unremarkable. It was considering Kang Ji-young-ssi's personality. Given her personality, she wouldn't like date locations that are too unique.

And there was no time. From 3 PM to 10 PM. It was a woefully inadequate time to plan a decent date course.

"Two iced Americanos, please."

"......Did you know that I like iced Americanos?"

"I saw you drinking iced Americanos even in winter and got the feeling. You're an 'eoljukah', right?"

"It's not that extreme. But... I've gotten so used to cold things that I can't eat warm things very well."

We sat in a secluded spot in the cafe without windows. A female employee brought the coffee. She brought a slice of cake that we didn't even order.

"We didn't order the cake."

Kang Ji-young politely failed. The female employee didn't back down and smiled brightly.

"We're currently running an event for couples who enter our store. We're giving away a complimentary strawberry shortcake and taking pictures with a Polaroid camera."

Kang Ji-young-ssi blinked her eyes.

"...They do strange events these days. We're okay."

"Ehey. Ji-young-ah. Aren't you going to ask for my opinion? Let's do it. They're giving us free cake and taking pictures, so of course we should do it. We're not losing anything."

"There's no need to do something like that...."

"We're on a date right now. A date."
