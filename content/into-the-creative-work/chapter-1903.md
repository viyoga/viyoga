I arrived in an unnamed outer district of Neo London. I'd been walking since halfway there for the sake of secrecy.

The time was already deep into the night. All I could hear was the sound of the wind rustling through the grass and the slightly irritating sound of insects.

Ttik! Ttikttikttik!

There was a cheerful sound. It was the sound of the pests rushing at me and Yuria being electrocuted to death. I didn't use magic. I just wrapped my body in an electromagnetic field with lightning. I can use lightning even without magic, after all. It was a kind of special ability.

"Juin-nim. It's that way."

It wasn't difficult to find the way with Yuria. If she hadn't been there, I would have wandered around for quite a while. I tend to get lost easily in places I've never been before.

Leaving the forest, I arrived at a ruined village. A village ruin with only one or two intact buildings. A place where people's footsteps had stopped, and the public officials, knowing it wouldn't make them any money, had abandoned it instead of cleaning it up. It was the perfect place for smugglers to make their home.

,There are no lights coming from anywhere. Is he hiding it with magic? Or did he set up shop underground?

I couldn't figure out which house Gillian was staying in right away. I didn't have that much detailed information. I had to find it myself.

,I can't use search magic. He's a magician too. I'd just be telling him I'm coming to attack.

This is his hideout, a magician's workshop. Everyone knows that magicians are at least 30% stronger when they're in their workshop.

So there are two effective ways to raid a magician's workshop. One is to somehow lure the magician out of the workshop, and the other is to attack the workshop itself with powerful firepower. I was aiming for the latter.

I was planning to drop the Thunder Break, the most powerful electric-type attack magic I could use right now, on his workshop and start the battle. It was certain that the workshop would be destroyed, and if I was lucky, there was a chance he would die instantly.

,If I use mana, I'll be found out, so I have no choice but to figure it out with my eyes. Let's see... If I were him, where would I set up my workshop?

First of all, not the center of the village. That's too obvious and open on all sides.

,If it were me, I'd set up my workshop at the edge of the village. But if there's something special that helps with the magic in the workshop, the story changes. First, let's slowly check the outside of the village.

But the plan went wrong. There were eyeballs embedded in the broken wall. The embedded eyeballs were watching me and Yuria exactly.

-I heard that lightning-type magicians as violent as lightning are becoming popular these days.... I didn't think you'd come right away. I'm glad I prepared just in case.

Gillian's voice echoes throughout the village.

,Could this entire village be his workshop?

It's obvious that the larger the workshop, the stronger the power the magician gets from it. I think I was mistaken for a moment. He didn't just set up his workshop here to make smuggling easier. If you set up a workshop of a certain size or larger, the council will impose sanctions. He set it up here to avoid those sanctions.

"Yuria. Prepare for battle. It wouldn't be strange if an enemy popped out from anywhere."

"Yes, Ju-in-nim."

Yuria took the sword she had brought with her in her hand. It's the first time I've seen her holding a sword, but she has a knight's license, so there's no need to worry.

,If I'm found out anyway, I'll use detection magic.

[Electric Detection]

It's a magic that I modified and strengthened the level 2 search magic, Detection, to suit my taste. A magic that searches for enemies by scattering electromagnetic waves in all directions. Right now, I can search up to a radius of 300m.

Electromagnetic waves spread in all directions, scanning everything around them. A huge amount of information came directly into my head.

"......"

"Juin-nim?"

Yuria tilted her head and asked, seeing my serious expression. I sighed.

"There are over 300 life signs detected. Even if he spread out decoys, that's an absurd number. He's already fully prepared. It looks like tonight will be tough."

As soon as I finished speaking, a person appeared on top of a particularly tall abandoned building. It was Barbara, a mercenary-turned-maid holding a long spear in one hand.

"I see you again after seeing you during the day. How lucky. Actually, I didn't like you two from the moment I saw you. Especially you, yes, the silver-haired maid. I wanted to tear apart that face of yours that thinks you're so high and mighty!"

She quickly took a stance and threw her spear. The spear flies straight at Yuria.

"It's okay."

Faster than I could use magic, Yuria stepped forward and swung her sword.

Kkang!

The spear that collided with the clean swordsmanship spun around in the sky and then stuck into the ground. Barbara took out a skewer smaller than her palm from her pocket. The skewer grew bigger and bigger and became a spear.

,It's a magic spear. If a spear can grow like that... it could get longer during battle too.

It was a habit I had when I was in the military. When I saw the opponent's weapon, I analyzed it first. That way, I could reduce the variables that occur during battle.

Keureureureureu.

With the sound of beasts growling, beasts reveal themselves from all directions. Coyotes, wolves, owls, eagles, squirrels, rabbits, etc., there were many different kinds. I wasn't surprised. I had already sensed that these guys were hiding everywhere.

,Of course, they're not ordinary beasts. Gillian is a magician who can also make chimeras. Even if they look ordinary on the outside, the inside will be completely changed. For example, muscles.

Chimeras are synthetic creatures. They're all alive. Gillian's ability to control 300 of them at the same time, even if they're just small beasts, is worth acknowledging.

"I'm going to kill that bitch! Do you understand?!"

-I heard that the maid over there is from the Knight Training Center?

"So what? She's just a half-baked knight who couldn't become a real knight, right? Logically speaking, if the path to becoming a knight was open, would she be working as a maid?"

In Neo London, maids were indeed an elite profession that was treated well. But it wasn't as good as being a knight. Knights were the ones who symbolized Neo London's military power.

-Okay. I'll take care of the magician. I didn't like that bastard either.

The beasts that were glaring at me and Yuria turned their heads and glared only at me.

"Yuria. Can I ask you for a favor?"

"Yes. That woman will not interfere with Ju-in-nim."

As a magician, a skilled vanguard was annoying. What if there's a magician behind them? The battle becomes twice as annoying. Just the fact that Yuria is taking care of Barbara makes things several times easier.

I opened the magician's spirit, the Astral. Mana fills the 52 mana roads connected to the Astral. I process the mana running through the mana roads into formulas and cast magic.

[Electric Detection]

It's different from before. This time, I added depth to the magic. The electromagnetic waves scan the surroundings a little more thickly. I carefully examine the life signs, excluding the beasts that have revealed themselves.

The center of this village.

I feel the presence of someone presumed to be Gillian.

I used telekinesis to float my body in the air and flew to the center. Over 300 beasts closely followed behind me. I ignored the beasts. There were no beasts that were dangerous enough to kill right away.

,It's easier to lure these beasts out and deal with them all at once. Or kill the magician who's controlling them.

There was an eerie building in the center of the village. A building so old and dilapidated that it wouldn't be strange if a ghost came out. I can feel Gillian's presence from the basement.

,Okay, then. Shall I say hello?

I reach out into the air.

A rock bigger than a human body appears in the air.

[Gravity]

The amplified gravity pulled the huge rock to the ground.

Barbara threw a spear at Yuria. Yuria easily deflected it this time as well. Throwing spears is meaningless. Even knowing that, Barbara continued to throw spears.

Kkaang! Kkang! Kkaang!

Spears were scattered around Yuria. Some were stuck in the ground, and others were bouncing far away and rolling on the floor. The moment all 30 spears fell to the ground, Barbara jumped and landed in front of Yuria. She held a long spear in her hand.

"I'll teach you, a well-bred young lady, what a mercenary's fighting style is."

"I don't think being raised roughly is a good thing."

"Yeah. You still have a mouth on you even in this situation, huh? I'm wondering if you can still talk like that even after your arm is cut off."

Barbara lightly swung her spear. A light blow as if testing the waters. Yuria stepped back slightly and dodged.

Kwang!

A shockwave strikes the back of Yuria's head. She staggered forward, and a spear came stabbing in from the front. Yuria twisted her falling body to the side and dodged the spear blade before standing up unharmed. Jureureuk. Blood flows from her forehead.

"Oh, what. You're not flustered? Judging by the fact that you were hit, it doesn't look like you expected it. Do you have any real combat experience?"

Barbara smirked. Yuria calmly scanned her surroundings once. There was no way the shockwave that suddenly struck the back of her head happened for no reason.

"...One of the spears behind me is gone. Did it turn into a shockwave?"

"Oh, that's right. My Ju-in-nim made it for me, and it's a formula that summons a shockwave instead of offering a spear. Or so it seems. To be honest, I can't understand even half of it, but it's surprisingly useful?"

"The key to summoning magic is a contract, so it's natural to pay a price. I guess you can't just summon shockwaves."

"Wow, you're smart. But smart guys don't survive long on the battlefield. No matter how smart you are, there are things you don't know. Even if you realize it, it's already too late. Your head must be fucking hurting, right? You're bleeding like crazy?"

Yuria is a genius. She can understand Barbara's fighting style just by seeing it once. But this world is full of things she doesn't know. The common sense and knowledge she has accumulated are basically from another world, so they're useless.

If you don't know, you have to get hit. That was also true for Yuria.

"Are you worried about me?"

"It's too easy this way. Why don't you do some first aid? I'll wait for you."

Barbara's words are 100% lies. Yuria touched the back of her head. Her hand was covered in blood. It's annoying, but it's not immediately life-threatening. Her thoughts are still working fine, so that means her brain is fine too. She could fight just fine without first aid.

Yuria took her sword and took a stance of the Yeongcheon style.
