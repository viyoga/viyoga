Deolkeongdeolkeongdeolkeong.

The armored vehicle headed towards the mission site.

They said it was the latest model armored vehicle, and the riding comfort was quite good, except for the noise.

There were a total of six people, including me, sitting in the armored vehicle's passenger seat. Except for Belhaga, the martial arts instructor, everyone was stiff with tension.

In a few hours, we'll be in a real battle with the bandit gang. A fight to the death awaits.

I was nervous, but not afraid. Facing humans instead of monsters was a first for me as a Hunter, but it was something I had prepared myself for even before becoming a Hunter.

"Don't be nervous, you brats. You're Class 1. On the other hand, the Neros Bandit Gang are all unranked except for their boss, Neros. They're just ordinary people with a bit of combat experience. You have an overwhelming advantage."

Unranked.

Ordinary people who can't use or even feel mana. But I can't completely ignore them. The guns they're holding are weapons that are equal to everyone.

"I assume you know how to use barrier magic? Even a Class 1 barrier can easily block dozens of ordinary bullets. But don't blindly trust your barriers. Sometimes, there are bastards who use magic bullets instead of ordinary ones."

Belhaga's words didn't change the atmosphere.

Barrier.

It's non-attribute magic.

The incantation itself isn't complicated, so any magician can easily learn and use it. Barrier is one of the first spells you learn when you start learning magic.

But ironically, it's one of the spells most affected by the incantation. The more complex and detailed the incantation, the stronger the barrier becomes. Also, because it's non-attribute, it's greatly affected by the Astral.

In simple terms, it means that if your mind is unstable, the strength of the barrier decreases.

"...Hoo. Looking at you guys, I don't have much faith. I'll brief you again."

Belhaga took something that looked like a camera out of his pocket, pointed it at the floor, and pressed the power button. A hologram of the mission area appeared.

"Razman City. A city ruined by the war 70 years ago. 95% of the city was destroyed by bombing, and only 5% of it remains now. It's about the size of a slightly large village."

The buildings shown in the hologram were two or three stories high. There were also buildings that looked like skyscrapers, but the upper parts were smashed or gone.

"Your mission is annihilation. Don't let a single person survive. Especially Neros, you must kill him."

Belhaga spoke like a beast growling. The kids swallowed hard at his ferocious voice.

He continued to brief us about Neros.

"This bastard had the nerve to steal the Dragon Factor that was being transported to the capital. There's no sign that he sold it on the black market, so he probably used it himself. Be careful. Well, even so, he probably hasn't finished adapting yet, so he's only about Class 2 at best."

Belhaga said it casually, but we, who were Class 1, could never ignore Class 2. If Neros really was Class 2, at least three of us would have to gang up on him. The best option would be for all five of us to do it together.

Belhaga finished his briefing, which he had repeated for the sixth time, and said to us.

"Check your equipment. If you whine about equipment problems during the mission, I'll kill you myself."

The kids, including me, checked our equipment. Unlike the kids, my equipment was a bit different. We wore the same uniform, but our weapons were different. Their guns were assault rifles, and mine was a sniper rifle.

I, who had the best shooting skills among us, was assigned the sniper mission. I threw away the idea that I would be safe just because I was providing sniper support from the rear. I know how dangerous this world is. I don't let my guard down.

'And I have to prove my worth here.'

That's the only way I'll be treated well when I return.

After checking all my equipment, I took a breath and looked at the kids who were assigned to the mission with me. They were about my age. All of them looked to be between 15 and 17 years old. All five of them were boys. I knew them all to some extent.

"We've arrived. The mission begins. I will not intervene in the mission until you are all annihilated. Keep that in mind and carry out the mission."

Kiiiiiiing.

The armored vehicle's door slowly opened.

"Just in case, don't get any foolish ideas just because you're outside. You can never escape from us."

I reflexively glanced at my right forearm. The slave seal under my uniform came to mind.

'Just because I'm outside doesn't mean I have a chance to escape. If I want to escape from this damn place, the first thing I need to do is figure out how to get rid of the slave seal.'

For now, escape is a distant dream. I put aside my lingering attachment to freedom and went outside the armored vehicle with the other kids.

It was a place where the traces of war remained even after 70 years. The yellowish-brown ground was hardened, and sand dust rose whenever the wind blew.

"Let's do as planned. Number 211. Please snipe well."

Number 14 said.

"I'll do my best."

I answered formally. After that, we parted ways. Number 72, Number 54, Number 105, and Number 14 went straight towards the ruined city, and I moved breathlessly to the sniping point.

The sniping point was on top of a pillar. It was a place that seemed easy to be sniped from in reverse because it was open on all sides, but the Neros Bandit Gang didn't have a sniper.

I squatted on top of the pillar and held the sniper rifle. The area on top of the pillar was too narrow to lie down.

I brought my right eye to the scope. The scope, which had telescope magic cast on it, clearly showed the ruined city about 1KM away. The first thing I did was find my four comrades. It wasn't difficult because I already knew their routes.

I tapped the radio.

"This is Number 211. I've reached the sniping point."

"This is Number 14. What about the enemies?"

"I'll check."

It was also my job to observe the enemies' movements using the scope.

"...Number 14. There are at least 3 people waiting in the building near 1 o'clock based on your position. They're chattering and playing cards. They don't seem to have noticed us at all yet."

"At least 3 people, huh? Can you snipe them?"

"I can't get an angle on them except for one."

"I see. We'll take care of it. Find the locations of the other enemies."

Number 14 and his group secretly infiltrated the building.

"Silence."

Number 14's voice could be heard through the radio.

Silence. It's a Class 1 non-attribute magic that eliminates the sound of the surrounding space. Along with Barrier, it's a magic that's particularly effective among Class 1 magic.

The downside is that you can't hear any sound even through the connected radio. I moved the scope to figure out the situation. The enemies were pierced by bullets and died. The battle time didn't take long. About 6 seconds.

"Clear. What's next?"

"7 o'clock based on your location. There are 2 people guarding the building entrance."

"Confirmed. The location isn't good for us to handle. Number 211. Can you handle it?"

"I'll handle it."

I stopped breathing and raised my concentration. The subtle shaking of the gun had disappeared. As soon as I was ready, I pulled the trigger and aimed the muzzle slightly to the side, then pulled the trigger again in quick succession. Two bullet casings fell to the ground. Flowers of blood and brain matter bloomed from the heads of the two enemies who were standing guard.

"......"

It was the first time I had ever killed someone, both in my life on Earth and in Number 211's life.

My fingertips trembled slightly. I let out a small sigh and shook off the trembling. That was it. I was surprised that I felt more indifferent than I thought I would.

I eliminated distracting thoughts and focused on the operation.

Number 14 and his group trampled on the corpses of the fallen enemies and jumped into the building.

The operation was almost perfect until we killed 10 people.

"...We've been discovered. We're going into engagement now. Number 211. Please provide cover."

"Understood."

I had fully anticipated this situation as well. There were plenty of bullets. I pulled the trigger to save my allies rather than to kill the enemy. Everyone, including Number 14, had to be alive in order to kill Neros, the boss of the bandit gang.

As I moved the scope here and there to cover my allies, I felt a strange presence and raised the scope upwards. A man was standing on top of the building, looking down at my allies.

He was a tall, slender man, reaching nearly 2M in height. He was wearing black clothes that were no different from rags, and a jade-colored branch-like horn protruded from his disheveled white hair.

Belhaga instructor was right. Neros had absorbed the Dragon Factor and become a Dragonkin.

"...Neros confirmed. I'm going into sniping now."

"We've confirmed him too. Maybe it's because he's a Dragonkin, but his presence is different. Can you snipe him?"

"I'll have to try."

In this world, there were Factors. Dragon Factors, Monster Factors, Hero Factors, Animal Factors, and so on. If there was a special human among your ancestors, you could be born with it congenitally due to their influence, and there were cases where you could awaken your abilities postnatally by injecting the Factor into your body.

Neros was the latter. And the Dragon Factor was one of the highest-ranking Factors.

The only fortunate thing was that there wasn't much time between injecting the Factor into his body and adapting to it. If we had come just one week later, the probability of success would have been close to 0%.

'I have to end it in one shot.'

I stop breathing.

It's not enough. I constricted my heart with mana for a more perfect snipe. My heartbeat stopped and the shaking completely disappeared.

At that moment, Neros suddenly raised his head. His eyes were looking directly at me. I suppressed my agitation with concentration and aimed at the space between his eyebrows. With a somewhat stiff index finger, I pulled the trigger.

Taang!

The bullet shot out. The sensation at that moment was perfect. I was certain that the bullet would pierce the space between his eyebrows accurately.

But my certainty shattered like a dropped glass bottle.

A blue current flashed on Neros's branch-like jade-colored horn, and the bullet stopped in front of the space between his eyebrows and flew back towards me. The bullet containing the current crackled.

I threw away the sniper rifle and rolled to the side. My reaction was quick and clean. But the bullet that he reflected was faster than expected. The bullet smashed the sniper rifle and grazed my shoulder. I fell below the pillar.

'If I had been 0.1 seconds late, my shoulder would have been shattered.'

I got up, sweating cold sweat.

"Number 211! What's wrong?!"

"...The bullet was reflected. He perfectly recognized me 1km away and reflected the snipe. Don't let your guard down. The sniper rifle is broken. Sniping is impossible. I'll join you there."

I grabbed my pistol and ran to the battlefield.

It's better to join Number 14's group and fight than to run away because I'm scared.

The sound of battle could be heard through the radio. Kwangkwang. Explosions continued to be heard, as if they had used grenades.

"Euaaaaaaaak!"

"He's supposed to be Class 2! This bastard is completely Class 3 or higher!"

"S, save me. My arm, my arm is cut off!"

"Keueoeoeo...."

Desperate screams could be heard.

But Number 14's group was still fighting. I was panting after sprinting 500M with all my might. This damn body had excessively low stamina compared to what I had trained.

Chijijik. Chijik.

Noise could be heard from the radio. There was a high possibility that Number 14's or the others' radios were broken. Or Neros had played a trick.

Kwakwakwang!

A blue lightning bolt fell from the cloudless, dry sky.

"......"

My legs stopped.

I had a strong feeling that heading to that battlefield right now would be a foolish thing to do. The disgrace of mission failure was better than dying. When you think about it, Neros wasn't someone that newbies like me should be dealing with.

"Goddammit. He's already Class 3? He must have a compatibility rate of at least 70%. Number 211, Number 14. Prepare to retreat with any surviving allies. I'll take care of him."

Instructor Belhaga's order was given. My plan to step back and watch the situation immediately turned to dust.

'Disobeying orders is serious... I have no choice.'

I started running again.
