<h2>Notice: Starting Thursday the 19th, it will change to one update per day.</h2>

This is Jeul.

It's been almost 4 years since I started serializing "Into the Creation."

To be honest, "Into the Creation" is a story I started without much thought. At the time, Joara was holding a contest, and the story I was originally writing was scrapped, and I was a mess.

Then the Joara contest caught my eye, and I wrote while watching porn. I wrote with my desires without restraint, thinking it would fail anyway. The response was much better than I expected. While hastily writing "Vampire Detective," I learned that if I used a female character illustration for the cover, the number of views would increase dramatically, so I used Yuria's illustration. Thanks to Yuria, I even got first place, and I felt good. Unfortunately, I was eliminated from the contest.

Since then, I've been doing two updates a day for 4 years. On Joara, two updates a day were the norm, and that's how you got good views and appeared on the best list.

For the first 1-2 years, it seemed okay. I could write a chapter in an average of 4 hours. There were typos and quality issues, but it was okay even if I did two updates a day all week.

But as "Into the Creation" accumulated, there were more things to consider. Story, balance, and so on. The more time I spent writing, the more tired I became.

I maintained two updates a day even on Novelpia. That's how it's always been, and it would be a bit much to slack off on updates just because the platform changed. Plus, the story of "Into the Creation" was bound to be sluggish.

While updating like that, my health deteriorated rapidly this year. Before going to bed, my hands would tingle, I couldn't fall asleep easily, and when I woke up, my head would ache as if it were breaking.

Maybe it's because my body wasn't feeling well, but my mind wasn't either. I became irritable over trivial things, and it became difficult to control my anger.

Novelpia reorganized the settlement payments. Looking at other works, there weren't many novels that did two daily updates as a standard.

I had an existential crisis. I felt like I was weird and stupid for insisting on two updates a day, and I was worried about my health when I saw my tingling hands. I sighed at the scattered cider cans and delivery food trash around me. When I sighed, the air felt stuffy, and my head started to hurt.

When I think about "Into the Creation," I keep thinking, "How am I going to finish this?" I even thought about just quitting and stopping the serialization.

Now I've reached my limit. I'm just going to update comfortably. I'm sorry to those who are disappointed.

Starting Thursday, I will do one update a day, 6 updates a week.
