Baekgeom (Hundred Swords), the A-class hero dispatched to the academy, Son Jeong-eon, was a demon.

He realized his limitations and made a deal with a devil to break through them. He was enjoying wealth and fame as an A-class hero, but he wasn't satisfied with that.

S-class.

That great rank flickers before his eyes.

He used to think he could reach S-class. But as time went by, he felt his skills were declining as his body aged, let alone reaching S-class.

Greed arose, and he made a deal with a devil.

Of course, Son Jeong-eon didn't stupidly make a contract just hoping for the devil's power.

The devil he contracted with, Noutroy, promised pure power in exchange for killing the devil hunter Seong Yujin. Power unrelated to the devil. If Son Jeong-eon killed Seong Yujin himself, the devil would take the devil's power and give only pure power.

Do you believe the devil's words? Even if I don't know anything else, the devil keeps his promises. And the devil Noutroy, who he contracted with, was fundamentally different from other ordinary devils.

,How did Seong Yujin get into Maruhan Academy? The devil said that there were other plans going on in the academy besides me... I don't know what's going on.'

Son Jeong-eon's purpose is to kill the devil hunter Seong Yujin.

But it wasn't easy.

From approaching Seong Yujin to figuring out Seong Yujin's capabilities. Nothing is working properly.

,It's definitely not normal. The devil said that fighting head-on is a stupid thing, so I have to aim for his carelessness and assassinate him.'

It was impossible to approach Seong Yujin and become friends because Seong Yujin was wary of him. Above all, he openly laughed at and cursed the demon in front of him during their first meeting. As if he knew that Son Jeong-eon was a demon.

,That can't be. Noutroy's power is disguise. Even a devil hunter can't see through it right away. If he actually realized I was a demon, he would have tried to kill me somehow.'

So there is a chance.

Son Jeong-eon changed his approach. He was going to isolate Seong Yujin first. Even if Seong Yujin is a devil hunter that even devils are wary of, he is still a student. Humans cannot be dealt with by force alone.

Fortunately, some of the second-year transfer students were influenced by him. To be exact, those who are sponsored by companies.

,Little by little, I isolate Seong Yujin. They are students who have already spent a year together, but they can't be more than family. Friendship can be easily broken by circumstances and wealth.'

At first, the plan seemed to be going well. But at some point, the atmosphere of the academy changed drastically.

The change started with the academy's first-year students. They woke up early in the morning and took over the way to school. They ignored the employees dispatched from the operating companies and bowed when Seong Yujin went to school.

"Seong Yujin-seonbaenim! Good morning!"

This was not the academy Son Jeong-eon knew. Even the military wouldn't do this.

The first graders treated the second graders politely. Even if it wasn't Seong Yujin, if there was a second grader passing by, they would immediately bow at a 90-degree angle. In addition, the second graders began to be aware of Seong Yujin.

The transfer students who received corporate sponsorship pretended to follow Son Jeong-eon's instructions and stayed quiet. Son Jeong-eon himself didn't know, but the transfer students had already told on Son Jeong-eon's work.

,Is it possible for the academy to change like this overnight...?'

The only ones who didn't change were the third graders. Unlike the first and second graders, the third graders were busy preparing for graduation. And among the third graders, there were many students who were aware of Seong Yujin.

Ko Young-jin, who ranked first in the first grade entrance exam, was admitted to the medical room. There is no danger to his life, but he will have to be hospitalized for a while. This was an unusual event considering the academy's medical level. The fact that he has to be hospitalized for a long time means that even recovery potions can't help it.

And A-class hero Taesan, who was dispatched to the academy, quietly canceled his dispatch and left the academy. Taesan did not say why he was leaving.

,Something is strange. What did Seong Yujin do?'

Son Jeong-eon met with corporate-sponsored transfer students. He was trying to avoid meeting them directly at the academy as much as possible, but he had no choice. Because the transfer students were avoiding his calls.

"The academy students are strange. You must know the reason. Tell me."

"...We don't know."

Son Jeong-eon read fear in the eyes of the transfer students. This makes things easier. Fear can be read as greater fear.

"You were able to transfer to the academy with corporate sponsorship. One of the conditions of the sponsorship is to follow my orders. Do you want to leave the academy that you worked so hard to transfer to? I didn't order you to die, nor did I order you to kill anyone. Just give me information."

The transfer students looked at each other. And he said firmly.

"We don't know. If the company cuts off sponsorship... we will accept it."

They were more afraid of Seong Yujin than the fear of losing sponsorship.

He was going to threaten them with force, but he stopped. Even though he has a contract with the devil now, he is not a real demon.

Son Jeong-eon looked for the faculty members. Among them, teachers who directly teach students.

"The atmosphere of the academy has changed a lot these days. I don't think it was like this when I went to the academy...."

"Director Kwon Sang-hoon is calling for reform. It's natural for the academy to change."

"That's not what I'm talking about. Haven't the attitudes of the first graders changed recently? In my opinion, second-year student Seong Yujin seems to be at the center of it. Do you know anything about it?"

Seong Yujin.

As soon as that name was mentioned, the teachers' expressions hardened.

"Well. I don't know."

"Students' affairs are for students to take care of. Teachers cannot interfere in every student's affairs."

"The academy is Son Jeong-eon-ssi's alma mater, but Son Jeong-eon-ssi is now a dispatched hero. It doesn't look good to be deeply involved in the academy's affairs."

Son Jeong-eon had no choice but to retreat without gaining anything.

As a last resort, he tried to shake the academy with the first-year students, but it didn't work at all.

Son Jeong-eon returned to his accommodation and turned on his laptop.

Chijijijik!

Noise appeared on the laptop screen and the silhouette of a horned human appeared. It was the mechanical devil Noutroy, who he had contracted with.

"The plan went wrong. It is impossible to assassinate Seong Yujin as of now."

Son Jeong-eon explained the academy's affairs to the devil. Noutroy did not get angry or sad even after hearing the report. He accepted it calmly.

-Is that so.

"What do we do now? Do we have to fight Seong Yujin head-on like this?"

-That's crazy. Seong Yujin is much stronger than you expected. You must remain silent until the time comes.

"Do you have another plan?"

-The plan is already in progress. When the world shakes, Korea will shake, and when Korea shakes, the academy will shake as well.

"Yes? More details...."

Tteok.

The laptop screen turned off.

It meant that he had no intention of telling Son Jeong-eon the plan at all. Son Jeong-eon distorted his expression. But there was nothing he could do.

"Fucking...."

Kwon Sang-hoon, the head of the academy, muttered profanities and threw his smartphone on the desk.

He had just been talking to Buster Man Ko Pil-doo, the father of Ko Young-jin and the clan leader of the Buster Clan. The conversation was actually pressure from Ko Pil-doo. Pressure to discipline Seong Yujin and expel him.

,I can understand Ko Pil-doo's anger. His son has become half a cripple. And he said he was even subjected to food torture. This isn't the Marine Corps, but it's a disgrace that hazing is happening here.'

He knew what had happened at the academy recently. This is because the victim, Ko Young-jin, spoke in front of the teachers. It was natural to tell his father, Ko Pil-doo. The only good thing is that he didn't speak directly to the police. Ko Young-jin knew that the police were incompetent.

,Expel Seong Yujin? I would have expelled him a hundred times if I could.'

I can't believe Seong Yujin would cause such an accident as soon as the new semester started. Didn't he stay quiet last year?

,Seong Yujin is Seong Hari's son. And the next S-class hero.'

The attack by the demons of the 3rd Corps. Those who know him in detail already regard Seong Yujin as an S-class hero. The Hero Association is the same.

,There's no way the Hero Association would just watch Seong Yujin's expulsion. Above all, Seong Hari is at the academy...!'

Seong Hari is an extremely enthusiastic parent that all academy staff members know. Do you call her a kangaroo mom in modern language? There was no way that extreme kangaroo mom would just watch the expulsion.

,Director Kang Ji-young was able to control Seong Hari to some extent, but... I... Damn it. Kang Ji-young is dead. I'm the director! I have to solve it!'

It doesn't make sense that he can't do what Kang Ji-young can do.

,Expulsion is not allowed. But a three-day suspension. No, two days would be fine. Anyway, Seong Yujin was disciplined. And I will return the academy to normal. There are already rumors coming out from outside the academy....'

Kwon Sang-hoon had his secretary call Seong Yujin to the director's office.

-This is a broadcast from the administrative office. Seong Yujin, a student in the second grade, first class, please come to the director's office right now. I'll broadcast it again. Seong Yujin, a student in the second grade, first class, please come to the director's office right now.

Kwon Sang-hoon sat in his chair and made a plan. First, he gently persuades Seong Yujin. He says it's a disciplinary action, but not really. He tells him that a two-day suspension is a normal break and that he will take care of the volunteer work.

Then, he gently explains the situation to Seong Hari with Seong Yujin, who has understood. After all, Seong Yujin was the one who made Ko Young-jin half a cripple. Seong Yujin wouldn't want things to get bigger either. Seong Hari will also understand.

However, Seong Yujin did not come to the director's office even after 5 minutes and 10 minutes had passed.

"It seems that Seong Yujin-haksaeng didn't hear the broadcast properly. Call him again."

The broadcast rang.

Seong Yujin did not come.

"...Secretary Jeong. Go and bring Seong Yujin-haksaeng yourself."

The 30-year-old male secretary left the director's office.

Ten minutes later, the secretary came in alone.

"That, Director. Seong Yujin-haksaeng doesn't want to come."

"......What? Seong Yujin-haksaeng said that? Why?"

"That's...."

Secretary Jeong hesitates. Something happened. Kwon Sang-hoon said with strength in his eyes.

"Tell me exactly what happened!"

"That, Seong Yujin-haksaeng said, 'Who does the director think he is, daring to order me around... If he has something to say, he should come and say it himself...'"

"......."

Kwon Sang-hoon felt like he was losing his mind. What did he just hear? Is that really what an academy student is saying to the academy director?

It was when Kwon Sang-hoon was trying to pull himself together and understand the situation.

Kwaaaaaang!

The director's office door exploded. Not metaphorically, but the door was shattered to pieces.

The culprit was Seong Hari. Her hair floated in the air due to her mana, and her eyes shone blue.

She looked like a crazy bitch.

"Are you crazy?!"

Seong Hari moved. Kung, kung. The expensive marble floor could not withstand her steps and cracked and broke.

"Seong Hari-ssi! C, calm down first... Keok?!"

He was grabbed by the collar. Kwon Sang-hoon was breathless.

"You send people to threaten a kid who is studying hard?! Are you even an educator? Huh?!"

Kwon Sang-hoon rolled his eyes and looked to the side. He asked with his eyes. Did you threaten him?

Secretary Jeong shook his head like crazy. The will that he had not threatened him was conveyed. Secretary Jeong was not stupid and would not have threatened that Seong Yujin. There was no way he could threaten him.

"Keueuk.... J, Yujin's mother, calm down first... I think there's a misunderstanding, let's talk... let's talk. I'll explain everything to you. Keueuk...."

"You better explain it well."

The disciplinary action against Seong Yujin was canceled.
