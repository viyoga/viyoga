This means it's an artifact that even D-rank or lower hunters who can't handle mana, or ordinary people, can use.

It could fire three consecutive fireballs capable of completely incinerating a car. Enough power to instantly kill a C-rank monster on a direct hit.

'An ordinary person can unleash anti-tank missile-level firepower with no conditions whatsoever.'

Dangerous.

And correspondingly expensive.

'Above all, this isn't mana-based energy. Meaning it can be used even in a world without mana.'

A world without mana.

Usually those worlds don't have superhumans like Awakened. Worlds with low power balance. But with this necklace? It could prove quite useful.

I put the pendant into my inventory and took a rest.

[You have selected Dark Moon.]

[Beginning Pleasure.]

Sitting in my office, I suddenly snapped to attention.

On the desk lay LK Group's quarterly performance report. Needless to say, we'd achieved record results. And next quarter would break that record again.

'Spaced out again, was I.'

The hand holding a fountain pen, mid-signature at the bottom of the report, stopped.

Something felt off.

Not in a bad way—in a good way. Even though I hadn't cast any magic, spaces my eyes couldn't reach felt vividly clear.

I extended my right hand toward empty air.

The magician's mind, the astral, responded, and a formula assembled itself in my mana load, completing the spell.

[Detection]

A detection spell that scatters mana formulas into the surroundings.

Information about the mansion's interior structure and the servants working within flowed into my mind, assembling into a spatial map.

2nd-rank spell Detection. Even used by me, a 7th-rank magician, its limits as a 2nd-rank spell are clear.

'It falls short in accuracy and precision. That's also why I use Electric Detection, which could be called a higher-tier spell.'

But right now, it's as vivid as when I've used Electric Detection. The range is one thing, but the precision is enough to sense even ants crawling on the garden ground.

'It's not just the information from Detection. Even without concentrating, my mana load feels as clear as if I were fully focused.'

I can sense things better.

That alone improves my magic efficiency.

'Efficiency suddenly improving without any kind of realization. That makes no sense.'

There's no effect without a cause.

Simply brushing it off as "I just feel good today" is something a magician cannot do.

Like a proper magician, I used magic to examine my body.

I inspected my astral and mana load, of course, and examined every part of my body without exception—down to my fingernails and every single strand of hair—using magic.

Result. Completely normal. No changes.

I checked three times just in case. The result was the same. My exceptional brain delivered the answer.

'It's an ability.'

Whether it's an otherworld perk or not, I possess a massive number of abilities alongside my magic talent. And it seemed this was an ability that had just awakened. I don't know exactly what kind of ability it is, though.

'My 13th ability? God loves me too much.'

Possessing 13 abilities, each one astonishing on its own. Unprecedented... it's not. In this world, there exist those with hundreds, even thousands of abilities.

'Still, I'm certainly a research subject. When an individual possesses multiple abilities, side effects develop with high probability—yet I have none at all.'

On top of that, my magic talent is exceptional.

'I'm definitely special.'

I have yet another identity.

Leon Schneider.

Warden of Lemen Prison in the Heist Empire, and a 5th-rank combat alchemist.

The illegal funds flowing in through Lemen Prison pile up quite nicely in my pockets. Unlike LK Group's corporate funds, this money is entirely mine to use as I please.

'Sure, LK Group earns far more than what Lemen Prison brings in now. But for building slush funds, nothing beats Lemen Prison. Above all, I can conduct stable magic experiments at Lemen Prison.'

Among magics, there have been several instances where humans served as materials. In black magic, using humans themselves as sacrifices is common, and in alchemy, human blood, bones, muscles, brains, and so on are used as ingredients. Of course, using ordinary humans as materials is almost unheard of.

'They're all criminals who'd die anyway. Since they're contributing to my magic research, you could call it an honor for them.'

Neo London and the Heist Empire.

Of the two, the Heist Empire is larger in scale, but Neo London is the better place to live. Neo London's public order is stable.

'On the other hand, Neo London has considerable regulations, so doing anything requires looking into this and that.'

The Heist Empire, by contrast, had no troublesome regulations. Even if they existed, they could be quickly bypassed or smoothed over.

'For magic experiments, the Heist Empire is more convenient than Neo London. On the other hand, procuring magic materials is tougher than in Neo London.'

Procuring materials isn't difficult for me.

I have my inventory and the No.9 World Door that can transport me to designated locations without distance limits.

'These are perfect abilities for smuggling. Maybe I should get serious and make a proper killing this time. ...No. I'd be better off spending that time training magic.'

I rose from my seat and pulled the No.9 World Door from my inventory.

I inserted the crude key set with five orbs into the door handle and turned it once.

Click.

The door opened. Beyond it was not a corridor but the warden's office of Lemen Prison in the Heist Empire.

I draped the black-blue cape coat, No.6 Phantom Dream, over my shoulders and stepped through the door. From that moment, my appearance had changed.

A black coat reaching below the knees, silver medals dangling densely across the chest. A formal cap pulled low. On the shoulders, silver pentagrams.

I had become Leon Schneider, Warden of Lemen Prison.
