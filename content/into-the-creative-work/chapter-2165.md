,Seong Yujin-ssi. Can I have a word?"

A man with a neat impression, dressed in a suit, spoke to me. He had such a kind impression that anyone, regardless of age or gender, would feel favorably towards him at first glance.

I knew who he was. He's been gaining fame recently, so there's no way I wouldn't know him. The reason he's famous is more than half due to his handsome face, but the other half is because he's a rising rookie Awakened.

,It's Han Duho-ssi, the Brown Bear of Gyeonggi Province."

,Oh. You know me?"

,I've seen you on the news so many times, how could I not know you? Of course, I was also impressed by your active performance. I heard you fought and won against a giant mutant in a one-on-one battle."

,It was a bit small to call it a giant mutant, but... I did win."

Han Duho is an Awakened with the ability to transform into a brown bear, befitting his nickname. From my perspective, it was clear that the brown bear wasn't just any ordinary brown bear, but one with enhanced physical abilities. There's no way an ordinary brown bear could take on a mutant.

,What brings you here to me?"

I didn't want to get involved with Han Duho if possible. If a handsome bastard is next to me, the spotlight gets stolen. The saying that you give one more rice cake to an ugly guy is bullshit. In reality, the handsome guy eats more and gets along better.

,You seem to be more impatient than you look."

,The bus is leaving soon. We need to prepare ourselves mentally as we have to face monsters. It's a life-or-death situation."

,You're not wrong. Then I'll get straight to the point. I'm trying to create the Korea Awakened Federation, an organization for the rights and interests of Awakened. Seong Yujin-ssi. Won't you join me? It would be a great help if Seong Yujin-ssi would join us."

Awakened Federation? Just hearing the name, I know what it is. He's trying to create an organization like the Hunter Association in reality.

But will it work well in this world? The biggest problem is that the number of Awakened is small. There probably aren't even 10,000 Awakened in Korea right now. And the power of the Awakened isn't that great.

In reality, there were Hunters with powers called natural disasters. Just being an A-class Hunter was stronger than a decent battalion-level unit. They were treated well because they had power. What about the Awakened in this world? Even I myself wouldn't measure up to a C-class Hunter.

Calculation result. The Awakened Federation has more disadvantages than advantages.

,I'm sorry, but I have an organization to be responsible for."

,I'm not saying to abandon Seongak Elementary School. You can just lend me your name for now."

,I'm sorry. The Awakened Federation isn't very attractive. I'll pass."

,...The Awakened Federation is an organization for the Awakened. The South Korean government might try to control the Awakened someday. We need to prepare, even for the worst."

,Haha. Han Duho-ssi, you worry too much. The Korean government is friendly to the Awakened. In that sense, the Awakened Federation could be seen as hostile to the government."

,The current government may be friendly to the Awakened, but we don't know how the next administration will change. So we need to unite in advance and prepare."

,I don't think it'll be too late to think about that then."

My will was firm. Eventually, Han Duho sighed. It was a sigh of giving up.

,Seong Yujin-ssi, you don't care about your fellow Awakened. I'm disappointed."

,I'm disappointed too. You're just recklessly trying to create an Awakened Federation, aren't you? Is it really an organization to protect the rights and interests of the Awakened?"

,...Hmm. You don't trust me."

,We met for the first time today. Han Duho-ssi, you suddenly discussed the creation of the Awakened Federation. How can I trust Han Duho-ssi?"

,...This is embarrassing. I'll admit my mistake. But I will definitely create the Awakened Federation. Please contact me if you're interested, even later."

Han Duho left his business card and left. He looked really embarrassed, his face was red.

I roughly shoved the business card I received into my pocket.

There will be no chance to join the Awakened Federation later. It looks like an organization with no power at all.

Soon, the bus arrived at the entrance of Seonwol General Hospital. As I got off the bus, I was greeted by reporters with cameras and citizens who came to watch. This was possible because Seonwol General Hospital was near the city center.

,Seeing this, it seems like the provinces are better than Seoul. Busan, which I saw last time, was also in pretty good condition.'

Seoul has not yet secured enough security for citizens to move freely. Because it was a place with a population of nearly ten million, zombies and mutants were hiding all over Seoul.

,Awakened, are you not afraid?!"

,What are your feelings as you support Gwangju?"

,Please say a word to the citizens of Gwangju who are watching!"

The reporters were making a fuss. It was amazing in a way. Even though the general hospital in front of them was teeming with mutants and variants.

,Don't they realize that mutants could pop out and attack them?'

Do they firmly believe that the military and police surrounding Seonwol General Hospital will protect them?

Either way, it seemed stupid.

Of course, I hide these thoughts. Because there's a camera in front of me. I put on a friendly smile and diligently responded to the interview.

,I rushed to Gwangju in one step for the citizens of Gwangju. I'm not afraid of mutants at all. I will protect the citizens of Gwangju!"

I shout confidently. The reporters and citizens gave me thunderous applause.

,Seong Yujin! Seong Yujin!"

,Zeus of Seoul!"

,Thunder God Seong Yujin!!"

Citizens chant my name. In my eyes, they looked like votes. Votes that will make me president.

,Okay, okay, the interview time is over."

The police appeared and cleared the area. They took us to a tent set up at the entrance.

• • •

,This is the mutant that has occupied Seonwol General Hospital."

A man in a police uniform played a video on the TV screen. It was a video taken by a reconnaissance drone.

A woman with long hair, reaching 2m in height, was filmed. She had four breasts on her back, and her hands were about 2-3 times larger than an adult male's hand. In terms of appearance, she was a terrifying female monster.

,We call this monster Catherine."

The monster had a name tag around her neck. The name written on the name tag is Catherine. It seems she was a foreigner when she was human.

,Catherine acts secretly without making a sound. Please be aware of that. Also, Catherine's main attack method is to grab and crush with her hands. A steel drone was crushed without being able to resist."

The human body is weaker than steel. The moment you get caught by Catherine, you should consider yourself dead.

,The variants inside the hospital are mutant rats that have mutated from rats. They are 50cm in size, move around at speeds similar to dogs, and are highly aggressive. Be careful as their saliva contains a paralyzing agent."

The police's short briefing ended. All the Awakened decided to move together. They judged that scattering would be more dangerous in the current situation.

Before starting the operation, Han Duho took out a camera from his bag. The police frowned and approached Han Duho.

,Why did you take out the camera?"

,I'm going to broadcast it live on the internet. Many people are curious about how our battle is going."

,...Please turn off the camera. It's not good for the operation to be leaked."

,Leak the operation? Are you kidding me? The opponents are mutants and variants. Are you seriously saying that mutants and variants watch broadcasts?"

,...It can have a negative emotional impact on viewers."

The police reached out to Han Duho's camera.

Slap!

Han Duho slapped the police's hand away.

The atmosphere cools down. Han Duho wore a cold smile.

,...What are you doing now? Are you obstructing official duties?"

,This camera is mine. Who are you to try to steal it? Does being a police officer allow you to steal citizens' belongings?"

,It's not stealing, I'm just trying to keep it for you."

,Isn't that stealing? And citizens have the right to know. It has a negative impact on citizens? Go on the internet. Videos of fighting zombies and mutants are easy to find. Or are you perhaps trying to control the media? If you insist on taking my camera... I will stop the operation here and inform the citizens of this fact. That the Korean police are ignoring the people's right to know and trying to control the media."

,......"

The police's expression changes colorfully, turning red and blue. He barely swallowed the anger that was bursting out and turned around and left the tent.

,Ha, it's ridiculous that the police who don't do anything are trying to control us.... They're out of their minds."

Those next to Han Duho nodded as if it were natural.

,That's right."

,Are all the police in Gwangju like that? They ignore us so much when we came to help."

,Let's make this issue public and socially bury that police officer."

,Whoa, whoa, everyone calm down. You're too excited. I don't like this either, but... we should treat it as a happening and move on. We have important things ahead of us."

The Awakened gathered around Han Duho. They seemed to be the Awakened that Han Duho had recruited. Han Duho seems to be genuinely trying to create the Awakened Federation.

Other Awakened also said a word to Han Duho, saying he did well.

,By the way, that police bastard was a bit strange.'

Even if I don't know anything else, controlling the camera was a bit too much. How many of the Awakened gathered here came to help with a pure heart? There won't be even one. It was certain that they all came here to gain fame. But prohibit cameras? By some mere police officer?

,Judging from how easily the police backed down, trying to confiscate the camera must have been a unilateral decision.'

If the government had issued an order, the story wouldn't have ended so easily.

,I have a strong feeling that something is wrong.'

Suddenly, I remembered that Seonwol General Hospital was related to Ilwoo Group.

I doubt they would openly interfere... but I quietly raised my guard.
