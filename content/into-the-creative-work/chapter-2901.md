There was still time before the Dark Moon rose. Of course, this was also time accounted for in my plan.

,If there are dungeons that only appear on the day the Dark Moon rises, there are also potions that can only be made on the day the Dark Moon rises.'

Usually, potions with such special conditions attached had ridiculously good performance.

,I already had a lot of money and had been steadily collecting rare materials for research purposes. More than anything, I got a lot from this Divine France, didn't I.'

The materials were sufficient.

The time was sufficient.

,I'm a 7th-circle mage. I also have a knack for alchemy.'

The pharmaceutical field was a little different, but it was within alchemy. And that alchemy also belonged to the category of magic.

,Even if mass production is difficult, if I know the recipe accurately, I can produce it successfully.'

First, I drew a cool magic circle on the floor. It was mercury paint that had absorbed mana for a certain amount of time every day for 1,000 days.

Next, I placed a magical cauldron that commoners could only dream of on top of the magic circle.

"Shall I help?"

Deneb, the Witch of Gambling, who was watching from the side, licked her lips and asked.

"Do you know alchemy?"

"I don't know about other things, but I know how to make potions. I'm a witch, you know."

Witches have a knack for making potions.

"Thanks, but I'll pass."

It wasn't that I didn't trust Deneb. Potions fail with even the slightest mistake. There was a possibility that things would go wrong if I tried to work with her when I didn't even know if we were on the same wavelength.

I put the ingredients into the magic cauldron.

The first thing I put in was a special holy water produced only 170 bottles a year by the Divine France's main temple. Putting in this special holy water increases stability. It means that it's okay to make small mistakes in the process.

After that, I threw in the pre-prepared magical ingredients. 30-year-old water parsley, very rare blue petals of mandragora, four-leaf clovers, dried baby flame frogs, super great red ginseng, and other expensive items went into the magic cauldron, plop, plop.

Deneb, who was watching from the side, clicked her tongue.

"How much did you just throw in? It must be at least 5 billion won just for the ingredients. Is this a definite recipe?"

"It is. It's a secret recipe passed down in secret."

I controlled the fire with magic, held the magic ladle with telekinesis, and stirred the cauldron.

,You mustn't boil it completely. Maintain a temperature where bubbles rise little by little and stir slowly. And continue to use alchemy magic.'

An hour passed. Sweat came to my face as I continued to maintain the magic. Mana is fine. It recovers more than it is consumed.

Two hours passed. Just as I was starting to taste sweetness in my mouth, the magic cauldron glowed. When the light disappeared, only some of the contents remained. A deep blue liquid. I put it in the prepared potion bottle.

"Hmm. It's a potion I've never seen before. What kind of potion is it?"

Deneb asked.

"It's a magic boost type. You know Overload, right? The effect is much better than that, and there are no side effects."

"A doping potion with no side effects. It doesn't seem like that's the only effect. What's the name of this potion?"

"Tears of the World Tree."

"...There aren't any ingredients related to the World Tree in there."

I shrugged my shoulders.

"I didn't make up the name."

I don't know why the name is Tears of the World Tree either.

• • •

A black moon rose in the night sky.

Marta, Deneb, Lena, and I watched the Dark Moon in a wide field. I chose this field because things would get annoying if there were cities or towns nearby.

Blackish-red smoke fell from the Dark Moon onto the field. Does it make sense for smoke to fall from the moon in an instant? It's the Dark Moon. You shouldn't push common sense. Even if space is torn in front of you, you have to assume that's how it is and run away.

,There's no good that can come from touching strange smoke like that.'

[Wind]

Even after blowing away the smoke with wind, I was uneasy, so I wrapped my body in a barrier magic. It was the right decision. The grass in the field that touched the smoke rapidly rotted. It was literally decay.

-A nasty thing is coming. Be careful.

Barakiel conveyed his will to me.

-That bastard has contacted another demon. Mephistopheles. And... oh dear.

It was Belial's will.

Mephistopheles contacting the Fallen was within expectations.

,Another transcendent demon has joined in. Probably one of the 72 demons.'

The 72 demons are capricious. Just because Belial shows me favor doesn't mean that the other 72 demons will have favor towards me.

,If the story of what happened on Walpurgis Night has spread... there will be even more 72 demons who are hostile to me. The fact that I am the Faceless Angel must have been revealed.'

On top of that, I used the power of Gabriel and Barakiel. If rumors spread among the 72 demons, the demons who are particularly hostile to angels would want to kill me before I get any bigger.

Dozens of skeletons wearing robes and holding staffs appeared in the sky. Each skeleton was adorned with jewels. They are not simple monsters. They are liches. Black mages of at least the 5th circle or higher. Mages who sold their souls to demons in their lifetime. Some of them are 7th circle.

As they waved their staffs at the same time, undead were summoned and landed on the ground. Blackish-red smoke sticks to the undead's bodies. Each piece of blackish-red smoke becomes a soul and burns in the undead's body.

,It wasn't ordinary decaying smoke.'

Calix of Corruption was a prince who failed even after offering his kingdom for transcendence. Considering that background, there was a high possibility that those souls were the people of his kingdom.

,Is he using the souls of his kingdom's people even after death? No, he's a prince, so he's not a tyrant. Well, judging by the looks of it, he probably killed his parents too.'

There was no way a guy who gave everything for transcendence would care about morality.

,The one who called those liches here is... Naberius.'

24th of the 72 demons. The demon of corpses.

Before I knew it, we were surrounded by thousands of undead. But it doesn't end there. The liches use warp magic. Through the opened warp, a large number of demon species such as imps, gargoyles, and demon knights appear.

,Even if they are liches affected by the Dark Moon, it doesn't make sense that they can succeed in warp magic so easily. The demons are blatantly using tricks.'

There is only one reason.

To join hands with the Fallen and kill me for sure. Because of the agreement, they can't directly step forward, so they're trying to kill me in this way.

,That damn smoke is blocking spatial interference. Long-distance space travel is impossible. Well, I can't use it anyway. ...I'm screwed.'

I never expected that another of the 72 demons would contact the Fallen and cooperate.

No matter how much 8th-circle Guardian Saintess Marta is here... there were too many enemies. There are more than 8 visible 7th-circle liches and demon species alone. Furthermore, they are still summoning undead and demon species... the four of us have to face thousands of them.

,This is comparable to the military power of a decent kingdom.'

Cold sweat runs down the back of my neck.

,If I had known this would happen, I wouldn't have thought of fighting head-on.'

It was too late to hide or run away.

,Shit. I shouldn't have transformed into an angel when fighting Mephistopheles.'

Then at least I wouldn't have had to fight an army.

"We gathered just to kill these humans?"

"Ah. A human, a witch, and an incubus. There's even a saintess. I'll tear the saintess apart and kill her."

"It's a waste to just kill them, let's play with them before we kill them."

The enemies look at us, convinced of their victory, and sneer. The reason they are not attacking is because they are waiting for orders.

But what about our side?

I looked to the side.

Marta's eyes, with a cigarette in her mouth, did not waver in the slightest. She was full of fighting spirit, so it wasn't that she had admitted defeat.

Deneb went even further. She was rolling two dice with her right hand.

Lena was touching her cheek with one hand and smiling seductively.

They weren't as flustered as I was.

Perhaps she felt my puzzled gaze. Marta said, exhaling cigarette smoke.

"During Walpurgis Night, we were the only two who could go because of its special nature. But today, there aren't any particular restrictions, are there?"

Bbuuuuuuuuuuu.

The sound of a horn rang out.

I turned my head and looked beyond that hill.

There were knights wearing white armor. All of them were riding warhorses with horse armor. Some of the knights held flags high.

A red cross.

The national flag of Divine France, and the symbol of the Knights Templar.

I listened with magic and heard the voices of hundreds of knights.

"The stench of the undead vibrates all the way here."

"It's the Saintess-nim's order. The witch and the incubus are allies for today only. Don't ask why. Don't doubt it. The Saintess-nim is right."

"There are imps. My specialty is imp hunting. I'll take care of the imps."

Divine mana rose from the bodies and weapons of the Knights Templar.

Marta grinned.

"Surprised? They're the guys who follow me. They won't attack you or the witch. Originally, about 1,500 people would have come... but because it's the Dark Moon, there are only 300 knights who can move right away."

Even so, it was an absurd number. I could see why Divine France, where science and technology had not developed like Neo London, could be on par with Neo London.

Kalkalkalkalkalkal.

Laughter echoes in the night sky. It was the laughter I had heard a few days ago.

The witches.

Witches riding brooms fly towards us through the sky. There were dozens of them, but they were elites of at least the 5th circle or higher.

"We came to help the Witch's ally under the orders of the Grand Witch-nim!"

"Witches! Aren't you the same demon species as us! Why are you taking the side of those things?! Aren't you afraid of the 72 demons!"

"Don't treat us the same as you guys. We don't serve any demons!"

I looked at Deneb. Deneb smiled.

"I didn't do anything. It's the Grand Witch-nim's order. It seems the Grand Witch-nim herself didn't step forward."

I understand that the 9th-circle Grand Witch cannot move rashly.

I looked at Lena. In response to my unspoken gaze asking if she had prepared anything, Lena shook her head.

"Incubi can't use their power much in battles like this. And I don't have enough influence to lead the incubi. But Belial-nim has decided to help me."

I turned my head and looked straight ahead.

The demon species who had been laughing at us, convinced of their victory, were no longer there. They began to tense up.

The numerical inferiority was still there, but the individual capabilities of the troops were superior on our side. In particular, the holy mana of the Knights Templar is the weakness of the demon species.

-Foolish mortal bastards.

A voice echoed from the black moon. Just hearing the voice made me feel like my ears were rotting.

-Do you think you can become something if you band together? Your flesh and souls will rot and become my nutrients.

A rotten corpse wearing a crown on its head slowly floated from the black moon and approached.

The Fallen.

Calix of Corruption.

I cast a spell of welcome towards him.

[Thunder Break]
