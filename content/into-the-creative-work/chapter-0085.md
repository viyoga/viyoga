The warhorses of this world are trained in a special way, boasting specs that can't even be compared to real-world horses. They're not only faster than regular horses, but their physique and strength are no joke either.

If a knight who wields aura rides a warhorse and charges across the battlefield, ordinary infantry can never stop them.

And currently, the warhorses are focused on running, wearing no equipment except for a single rein. The surrounding scenery changes rapidly. The sound of their hooves, tag-eul-tag-eul, is quite threatening.

The problem was that it wasn't a good environment to drive a motorcycle. The ground was studded with bumpy stones, and soon we'd have to cross a forest. In many ways, a horse is perfect.

'If I could, I'd like to bring a helicopter....'

It was impossible. Getting a helicopter would be easy enough if I stole it from the 'Vampire Prosecutor' world.

However, what goes into the inventory is limited to what I can hold. No matter how large the volume of an object is, if I can hold it, I can put it in the inventory. Conversely, no matter how small the volume is, if I can't hold it, I can't put it in the inventory.

Even if such a small village disappeared overnight, no one in the Fruckus territory would care.

Yuria Grace. Kyle's enemy who appears in the later part of the story.

The greatest genius and assassin in the world, officially recognized by the author in the original work.

Artel Village is a village located in the outer area of the 'Sea of Evil's Affliction'.

The Sea of Evil's Affliction is a forbidden place that everyone on the continent knows. Monsters pop out from time to time, and none of the adventurers who headed to its center have been able to reveal its secrets.

The Sea of Evil's Affliction is shunned by people, so it's the perfect place for fugitives.

Artel Village is a village formed by all kinds of fugitives. Criminals, debtors, wanted criminals, disabled people, slaves, etc.

Except for the occasional monster popping out of the forest, no one ever came to visit, so it was a very peaceful village.

Until just now. Until before the sun began to set.

Artel Village was in chaos. The sounds of battle and screams echoed from everywhere. Flames soared in various parts of the village.

The Pfrek Mercenary Corps. No, to be exact, a band of thieves disguised as a mercenary corps was attacking the village.

Although Artel Village is a village of criminals, the population is less than 100, and there are less than 20 people who can fight. Due to the nature of a poor village, the equipment they have is not very good either.

On the other hand, the Pfrek bandit group has more than 30 professional combatants. Artel Village is not something they can handle.

Yuria Grace, who was reading as Nell told her to, answered calmly. Her face was very inorganic.

Yuria then grabbed the backpack next to the bed. It was an emergency backpack that was always prepared. It contained food and daggers.
