I moved, following Kukuru.

Emerging from the squirming meat cave, I saw a lush forest. The weather was quite hot, around 30 degrees Celsius. The humidity was a bit high, though. Whatever else, I liked the fresh air coming in through my respiratory system.

,Surprised to see a forest? You're probably thinking, 'Why is there a forest inside a Transcender?'"

,I wasn't that surprised."

I learned about the origin of this dungeon from the original game. It was a pretty difficult dungeon, so I tried to tackle it several times. I even looked up tips online.

,It's inside a Transcender. Nothing is strange."

,That's right. Our common sense is useless garbage to a Transcender. Isn't a Transcender one who has surpassed everything, even common sense and laws? Don't try to understand it, just accept it. Well, to help you understand... this is also the Astral of the Transcender, Vision Devourer Belk."

,Astral is a wizard's mind. In other words, a mental image. Since he's a Transcender, the scale itself is different. But as far as I know, I heard the Vision Devourer died."

I asked, even though I knew the information.

If I knew too much, Kukuru would definitely be wary of me. There's no need to make him overly cautious. I'll induce a sense of superiority by making him think he knows a lot of information.

,Just in case."

I don't trust Kukuru. I'm seeing this guy for the first time today. What's more, he's a necromancer who wasn't even in the original game. It would be strange to trust Kukuru in the first place.

,He died. He devoured too much vision, too much arcane, and his stomach burst. It's proof that even a Transcender can't perfectly handle arcane."

,If he died, the fact that we're here wouldn't be established."

,Transcenders are those who have achieved immortality. Even if they die, they don't disappear. Right now, he's in a near-death state. He's been in a near-death state for about 7,000 years. The countless vision magic he devoured is slowing down his recovery."

,...Belk was a figure from 300 years ago, though."

,300 years ago? 50 years have passed since I was eaten by Belk. Don't dwell on the passage of time. After all, time is relative. The important thing is that Belk used his body, his astral, to create this dungeon. Even while dying, he couldn't resist his greed for vision magic. You've fallen nicely into Belk's trap."

,I walked into this dungeon on my own two feet."

,Yeah, yeah. The wizard who came in before you said the same thing. Oh, by the way, there are several entrances. And we periodically scatter information about this place like bait."

Quest clues.

The clue items and linked quests to enter this dungeon. I ignored them all and went straight to the dungeon.

But rest assured. What you can get here is real. This process itself is a ritual, so when the ritual is complete, the dungeon will disappear, I will be freed, and you will be able to become a 7th-grade superhuman.

I know.

I also know what I'll get at the end of this dungeon.

I was walking after him when I felt a sense of incongruity. A scenery I'd seen somewhere before. It's not déjà vu. I've actually seen this scenery before.

,Through the monitor."

A large tree divided into two branches, and a table carved out of a rock beneath it. And the sound of a stream coming from the right.

,Wait. Let's go right here."

,The opposite way. We have to go left. Is it because of the sound of the stream? Are you thirsty or something?"

,I can feel arcane."

It wasn't an empty word. I could feel arcane little by little throughout the forest. After experiencing vision magic once, I was able to sense arcane as well. The range is much narrower than mana detection, though.

,Arcane that I don't know...? No, you knew about arcane. Well, there's no need to rush. Lead the way."

I moved to the right. I forcibly suppressed my excited heart. I have to maintain my composure.

The place I arrived at was a stream with clear water flowing. What I paid attention to were the rocks in the stream. Rocks placed like stepping stones.

,You can feel arcane here? I've been coming and going here all the time. I haven't found anything special."

,It's noisy. I need to concentrate, so be quiet."

Kukuru looked at me with dissatisfied eyes. Regardless, I focused on the rocks. Rocks with slightly different shapes.

[Telekinesis]

Five rocks lifted into the air by an invisible magical force. There was a wedge-shaped mark on the invisible part of the rock. From 1 to 5 each. It was the order telling me where and how to place them.

Place the rock with 1 wedge at the top and arrange them in a pentagonal shape clockwise.

The moment the placement is finished, mysteriously shining lines extend from the rocks, heading towards each rock and forming a pentagram.

,This is fucking insane. Seriously, something like this was hidden in such a ridiculous place?"

Kukuru let out a hollow laugh.

Regardless, I focused on the pentagram. The hidden elements are the same as in the original game. So what I'll get will be what I know.

The center of the shining pentagram.

Space splits and a small hand mirror falls down. Having prepared in advance, I quickly reached out and received the hand mirror.

I don't know if it's arcane, but the mana and spell patterns felt from that hand mirror are not ordinary. Judging by the spell patterns alone, is it a defense-type artifact?

I briefly considered telling Kukuru the name and ability of the artifact.

If I tried to hide it forcibly, he would be suspicious. And I can't be sure that he'll betray me.

,...It's a Reflex Mirror. It reflects attacks below 7th grade once."

,Below 7th grade... it reflects even 7th grade attacks? What an absurd artifact. Is it completely disposable?"

,No. It needs a 10-day recharge time after one use."

In the original game, the cooldown was 10 days in game time. The important thing is that it reflects not only physical attacks but also incorporeal, formless attacks like curses.

Moreover, just putting it in a backpack or pocket will automatically activate it against fatal attacks.

,Putting it in the inventory won't activate it, right? The inventory is a kind of different space."

However, it doesn't reflect all attacks in a large-scale attack, but only the attacks directed at the owner.

,In the end, it depends on how you use an artifact."

I was confident that I could make good use of the artifact.

,Hmm. If you sell it outside, you can get a lot of money."

,Sell it? An artifact like this? I'm not selling it."

,I was just saying."

I moved, following Kukuru.

A moderately wide open space appeared, and there was a white-haired elf old woman. The old woman, wearing a black robe and holding a wooden staff, laughed at us.

,Kekekeke!! Haven't you heard that a bad witch lives in this forest?!"

,The witch of Garna Forest, Flora. A troublesome old hag has appeared. Don't think she'll communicate like me. That witch's ego has been eaten by the Transcender."

The witch pressed the end of her staff firmly into the ground. A magical wave spread in all directions and penetrated the surrounding trees.

The trees, imbued with magical power, move. They uproot themselves from the ground and move like legs, swinging their branches like fingernails. Their movements are slow, but that's why they have weight and power. Moreover, their large size itself is a physical advantage. Dozens of trees slowly advance towards us.

A scheduled encounter with the witch. The witch took the first strike. Perhaps the witch knew we were coming and prepared a large-scale magic in advance.

I said to him, who was standing still.

,What are you doing? You're not saying you brought me here and aren't going to do anything, are you?"

,This dungeon is also a ritual for you. This battle is part of the ritual. I can help you, but I can't fight in your place. Defeating that witch must be entirely up to you."

,You're good at talking. Stall for time. I'll finish it with one big hit."

,The elementalist's big hit, called the flower of the battlefield. Scary but also exciting. All right. I'll stall for time.

He stepped forward, holding a staff with a bird skull embedded in it.

,The forest is a repository of records. The information of countless lives is accumulated. And that record itself becomes a medium."

Kukuru's black mana seeped into the ground. Then, several wraiths with translucent entities appeared. Owl, jaguar, bear, deer, anaconda. The wraiths ran towards the tree soldiers, their eyes shining.

A power struggle between the wraiths and the tree soldiers. The battlefield began to stagnate.

Thanks to that, I leisurely prepared my magic.

Pajik, pajijijik....

An eerie sound heard from somewhere.

The changed nature of mana and the eerie sensation.

The witch and Kukuru looked at the sky at the same time. Lightning rotating in a swastika shape in the sky. The reactions of the two who confirmed it were different. Kukuru admired it.

,Did you modify the magic? It seems like you're perfectly controlling that power."

,Hiiiik! Is this crazy bastard trying to burn this area down?! Love the forest, you crazy wizard!"

The witch stomped her feet and used magic. Dozens of plant stems sprouted from the ground around her, forming a hemispherical barrier around her.

[Thunder Break]

A pillar of lightning fell to the ground. It burns and destroys everything, including tree soldiers and wraith animals. The residual current on the ground spreads in all directions, burning the plants. But that's only for a moment. The fire subsides quickly.

,This isn't an ordinary space, it's a dungeon. It's also inside a Transcender's body. It restores itself to its original form as if it were healing the body."

Still, flames are still burning in the place where Thunder Break hit directly. In the center was the witch, burning with the plant barrier. Ash scatters from the witch's body. It's ash generated from burning plants.

,She's constantly regenerating by turning her body into plants. It's plant regeneration vision magic. I knew it through the original game... but it's amazing to see it in person."

I reached out my hand. I knew the witch's hidden card through the knowledge of the original. So it's natural to prepare for it.

A dark red magic circle is drawn in front of my hand in an instant.

[Death Ray]

A 2m diameter dark red laser annihilates the witch and stretches out, creating a 50m long path.

,Heh. You were preparing Death Ray in case Thunder Break didn't work. That's a thoroughness rarely seen in arrogant wizards."

A golden aura sparkled in the place where the witch was. The aura slowly approached me.

,Don't avoid it. That's one of the rewards of the trial. You will gain one of the witch's magics. If you're lucky, you might even get the vision magic the witch has."

,I know. I said it from the beginning. Obtaining vision magic is also one of my goals."

The golden aura entered my head. I acquired one of the Garna Forest witch's magics. Learning it without any other process, as if devouring magic. It's a miracle that's only possible in this dungeon.

I, who had been closing my eyes for a moment, opened my eyes.

,Did you learn vision magic?"

Kukuru asked, as if curious. I checked my empty right hand and clenched my fist slightly.

,No. Unfortunately, I didn't get the witch's vision magic."

,Then what did you learn? If it's magic that creates tree soldiers, it'll be useful even if it's not vision magic."

,Summon Plant. It's plant summoning magic. It's magic that temporarily summons the plants you've grown. It's about 5th grade."

Temporary summoning magic? Is it a type of magic that imposes limits on plants and has a temporary effect on high-difficulty summoning spells? It's only useful for druids who like plants. It's too bad you failed the draw.

,The pick was a success. It was the magic I needed.

I unclenched my fist.

There were 3 cherry blossom petals in the palm of my hand.
