I faced Mir in the training grounds.

In the original story, Mir's weapon of choice is a greatsword. But the Mir before me is equipped with a great blade, not a greatsword. It's very similar to the weapon used by Do Hwang (Blade Emperor) Baek Jeong-su. He must have changed his weapon after becoming Baek Jeong-su's disciple.

I realize anew that the Mir in the original story and the Mir before my eyes are different.

"Gwangroe. I'm counting on you this time."

Mir bows his head.

"You're surprisingly good at bowing your head."

"...I bow when I need to. It's true that I don't personally get along with you, but I'm learning something from you, so I'm just showing the minimum courtesy. But don't think you can act like a teacher because of it."

"I wouldn't take a disciple like you even if you gave him to me. If it weren't for Hytera-nim's request... tsk."

"...What did you ask Hytera-nim for in return?"

Mir's atmosphere changes slightly. Unlike in the original story, he seems to already have an interest in Hytera-nim.

I couldn't honestly say that I asked to freeload at your clan house.

"I asked her to make me some magic items. Hytera-nim's skills related to magic are among the best in the world."

"Hytera-nim's skills are the best in the world."

Mir nods as if it's obvious. Looking closely, the corner of his mouth is twitching. It's as if he knows Hytera-nim better than anyone else in the world.

'Is he feeling a sense of superiority knowing that Hytera-nim's true identity is a Dragon King-level dragon?'

That Hytera-nim was making *aheung aheung* noises while I was giving it to her in the ass until an hour ago.

"Let's get this over with quickly. The technique I'm going to teach you is Gale Lightning Thrust."

Replacing training with a practical sparring session is a good method, but it takes too much time. It was annoying.

If I taught him some mediocre tricks, I wouldn't be able to show off to Hytera-nim. So I decided to teach him a technique at the level of a secret art. Hytera-nim will acknowledge that.

"Gale Lightning Thrust?"

"It's my ultimate technique. I'll show you once first."

Pajijijijik.

Blue electricity flashes and strengthens my body.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning Thrust (疾风迅雷).

"I can use that too."

Pajijijik! Bright yellow electricity sparked from Mir's entire body.

I took out Hwaryeon Bido from the air and pointed it at Mir. Mir tensed up and raised his great blade.

I move. Mir reacts and swings his great blade, but I had already moved to Mir's side and placed my knife against his neck.

"Keuk?!"

"All you did was cover your body with electricity and mana. Even if your physical abilities increase, your reaction speed and thinking speed don't accelerate at all. That might have been enough when dealing with other scrubs. But not against me."

"...No way. You can do that with electricity?"

"The human body ultimately moves with electrical signals. Being able to manipulate electricity at will means you can perfectly control your body. And I use not only electricity but also mana."

The Mir in the original story only uses the power of electricity physically. It was sickening to watch.

"...You're going to teach me such a valuable technique?"

"Doesn't Do Hwang (Blade Emperor) teach you this kind of technique?"

"......I can't tell you about my sect's techniques and information."

"I'm not particularly curious. He probably just taught you some mana enhancement methods and how to use a sword."

Mir frowned but couldn't refute it. My words hit the nail on the head. Mir changed the subject altogether.

"That technique called Gale Lightning Thrust. How are you going to teach it to me? The training itself seems difficult."

"I'll put my hand on your back and cast Gale Lightning Thrust. You can figure out the rest on your own."

"That's all?"

"Did you think I was going to teach you everything from the basics?"

"......"

"I'm wasting time. Let's hurry. You're not going to tell me you can't trust me now, are you?"

"I don't trust you. I trust Hytera-nim, who made the deal with you."

"That's what I should be saying."

I placed my right palm on Mir's back. I could feel the electricity and mana coursing through his body.

'He's a little different from ordinary people. Is it Do Hwang's secret mana enhancement method?'

I wasn't very interested, so I erased my interest and focused on the flowing electricity. Having eaten the Thunder Tiger Dragon's heart, my electricity is more active and faster than others.

"I'm starting. I don't want to do it twice or three times, so remember it well."

Pajijik!

I seize control of the electricity and mana flowing through Mir's body and use Gale Lightning Thrust. I spread the electricity gathered in the spine throughout the body, stimulating it. Yellow electricity leaked out of Mir's body, but unlike before, it was organized and not noisy.

"It'll last for about a minute. Try moving."

Mir moved. And he stumbled. He almost fell because he couldn't adapt to the accelerated body. But he seemed to adapt to the speed quickly, moving more smoothly than before.

Soon, the duration of Gale Lightning Thrust ended. Mir looked back and forth between his body and me with an unbelievable expression.

"I can't believe something like this is possible. How do you even know such a technique?"

"I have no reason to tell you. Can you use Gale Lightning Thrust without my help?"

"I can."

Pajijik!

Yellow electricity sparked from Mir's body. Mir confidently tried to move his body, but he fell right over.

"What is this...? I clearly tried to move my arm, not my leg. But why...?"

"The electrical signals are tangled. It means you're not perfectly controlling the electricity. Well, you'll have resistance to electricity, so you won't become crippled."

I didn't expect him to succeed in one try. Even though Mir has tremendous talent, he's definitely not on Yuria's level.

Still, after casting Gale Lightning Thrust about 30 times, he seemed to get the hang of it, so he was a genius indeed.

However, even if he succeeds, it won't be even half as effective as the real Gale Lightning Thrust.

It's only natural since he doesn't have the Thunder Sky Knot (雷天結), which is the heart of the Thunder Sky Style.

I didn't have even a speck of intention to teach him the Thunder Sky Knot.

'Anyway, this concludes my deal with Hytera-nim.'

I decided to return home, leaving Mir, who was immersed in training, behind.

To my clan house, where Hytera-nim and Jina are.

'If that guy masters electromagnetic waves, my parasite act might be exposed... But that's unlikely. Even though he's a genius, he's a simpleton, so he doesn't have much creativity.'

If he had creativity, he wouldn't use electricity so simply.

Mir finished his training and returned to the clan house around evening. Even though he opened the door and came in, no one greeted him.

"Seeing the shoes in the entrance, it seems like Hytera-nim and Jina are here too..."

It wasn't like this before.

When he came back after going out, Jina used to come out and greet him first. But after coming to Gigantria, Jina changed. Their relationship became awkward. These days, it feels like she doesn't even say hello unless I talk to her first.

'Hytera-nim also seems to have become less talkative lately.'

How should I put it? It feels like things aren't going well.

Mir headed to the kitchen first. He was thirsty. He opened the refrigerator and tried to drink a can of cola he bought from the Plack Merchant Guild.

'...There's no cola? Did Jina and Hytera-nim drink it? That's strange. They prefer cider to cola, so they don't usually drink it. Ah. There's no beef either.'

The highest-grade beef he bought from the Plack Merchant Guild when he bought the cola was also gone. For reference, beef and pork are more expensive than dragon meat in this world. Most of the other expensive ingredients were also gone.

'They must have eaten it for lunch.'

To eat the expensive beef he bought when he wasn't there. He couldn't help but feel a little sad. His gaze turned to the side.

There was a pile of dishes in the sink.

'They grilled it on an iron plate. They also made stew... And japchae (stir-fried glass noodles)? Those are my favorite dishes.'

Looking at the pile of dishes, he rolled up his sleeves. Hytera-nim and Jina usually clean up right after eating, but there were a lot of dishes today, as if they were busy.

'I can do as many dishes as it takes for Hytera-nim and Jina.'

Honestly, he also felt like he had been too indifferent until now. This was the first time he had done the dishes since meeting Hytera-nim and Jina.

After finishing the dishes and returning to the lobby, Mir was able to meet Hytera-nim and Jina.

It felt like it had been a long time since the three of them had gathered in one place. Mir felt strangely excited.

"What about dinner? Should we go out to eat? I saw that there weren't many ingredients earlier."

"Eating out is expensive, so it's better to save money. And... we ate the beef Mir bought for lunch. I'm sorry."

"Haha. I'm sorry too. I just really wanted to eat beef today."

"It's okay. It's okay. Then for dinner... Since there aren't any ingredients, should we make egg fried rice?"

"That's not bad! I like fried rice too! Hytera-nim too, right?"

"Yes. I like it. Because it's delicious."

"Oh. I guess I have to show off my skills a bit."

Mir smiled confidently. He often made egg fried rice when he lived alone. It didn't cost much and it was simple.

"I'll help you."

"I'll help too."

Mir didn't refuse their help. The atmosphere of the clan house was good today. It felt like going back to the old days.

It was when they were preparing the ingredients in the kitchen. Suddenly, Hytera-nim was gone.

"Jina. Where's Hytera-nim?"

"W-Well. Maybe she went to the bathroom?"

"Ah."

Jina looked at the corner of the kitchen with slightly awkward eyes. It was towards the side with the drawers. Mir also looked that way without realizing it. There was nothing there.

"Mir. I heard you trained with Gwangroe today? How was Gwangroe's training?"

"I was surprised because he taught me better than I thought. I learned a technique called Gale Lightning Thrust, but I haven't completely mastered it yet. But I've grasped the key, so it's okay."

"That's Mir for you. Amazing."

Mir gave an awkward smile at Jina's compliment. After that, Jina's gaze turned to the corner so strangely, but there was nothing there.

Hytera-nim suddenly appeared when they were frying the rice. Was it just his imagination? He felt heat when she approached.

"Is there anything I can help with?"

"I'll fry the rice. Set the table."

"Yes."

"But where's Jina? She was definitely next to me until just now."

"I think she went back to her room for a moment."

That's strange. He didn't feel her leaving. Mir tilted his head and focused on the fried rice. He couldn't serve them bad fried rice.

Jina appeared when the fried rice was finished. Her gray hair was disheveled, and her pink, plump lips were slightly parted. Was it because her lips were moist? Mir felt a strange sexiness.

"Jina. Are you okay?"

"...Ah. Sorry. I felt sleepy for some reason. I should go to bed early today."

She didn't seem sleepy at all until just now. Even though he was doubtful, he couldn't argue since Jina looked tired now.

Mealtime began. Mir inwardly expected exclamations to flow from their mouths. But the words that came out weren't exclamations. They ate the fried rice with expressionless faces, and then belatedly looked at Mir and said.

"This fried rice is delicious."

"It's delicious, Mir."

It felt like they were just saying it out of courtesy. Mir shoveled the fried rice into his mouth.

'It's much more delicious than what you can buy... Is my taste weird?'

"Eueut...."

Suddenly, a moan escaped from Hytera-nim's mouth. Mir, surprised, looked at her. Hytera-nim was frowning.

"H-Hytera-nim?!"

"I'm sorry. I bit my tongue for a moment."

"T-That can happen! Mir. I have a question about the fried rice. Can you look at me for a second?!"

Jina hurriedly called Mir and poured out questions. Mir answered them faithfully, but he felt a strange feeling.

Even though he was eating with them in the same space, he felt like he was far away.

'To feel lonely even when I'm with them. Am I crazy?'

We're having dinner together like we used to. We're looking at each other and talking.

It's harmonious. Our bond is still continuing. It definitely will.
