Zeus, sitting on the throne, closed his eyes tightly.

Even without directly facing that being, mental contamination and existential contamination were occurring. He suppressed and scattered the contamination with the power of a god.

,I didn't expect it to be this intense. It's a far more enormous being than I heard.'

That was definitely not a fake from within the trial. It appeared in its true form and confronted Seong Yujin.

,...I can't understand.'

-Don't try to understand. Because I can't understand either.

The voice of Crawling Chaos echoes in his ear. Zeus narrowed his brow.

,Can't Seong Yujin be saved?

-Unfortunately. It seems that that person is serious.... Even if I step in directly, it will be difficult.

,Can't I even see what they're doing now?

-Who would dare to spy on that person? That person is omniscience itself. Stay still if you don't want to be harmed. If they satisfy their curiosity, they will melt into nothingness as usual. It's not too late to move then.

,Seong Yujin could die.'

-They are a being that has captured that person's interest. If they satisfy that person, they will not only survive but also receive a reward.

,.......'

Zeus closed his mouth. Even if he wanted to refute, he didn't know much about the Outer Gods.

「■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■■」

A notification window popped up on the system that had been broken by the mere appearance of the great being. Judging by the broken letters, it seems that it has not been completely restored yet.

,...Wait. It's already restored enough to bring up a notification window?

The gods who created the system. Among those many gods, Zeus, who was in charge of the system's output, knew a little more about the system than others.

,Did they focus on restoring the output that goes into managing Atlantis? ...No. It's more reasonable to assume that other gods noticed and helped restore the system.'

He clicked his tongue. If the gods of other mythologies noticed, things would become troublesome in the future. Because they will ask all sorts of questions.

,I need to silence the other gods. Although I don't know if the traitor mixed in will secretly spread rumors. In the trial, Hermes' identity was Crawling Chaos. Maybe Hermes really is....'

The moment his head became complicated, an additional notification window popped up.

「Confirmed the disappearance of ■■■■■.」

「Completed the myth.」

「Ending the trial.」

,What?

Whose disappearance?

There is only one being whose name the system would express like that. A great being who can contaminate gods and humans with just their name!

,Is the system broken?

Then things will get a little troublesome. Atlantis might have to be temporarily suspended. If the system doesn't function properly in Atlantis, all sorts of gods will wreak havoc and it will be a mess. He would have ignored it normally, but he had invested a lot in this Atlantis.

Zeus suddenly felt something was wrong. Because Nyarlathotep, who would normally be nagging and provoking him, was quiet.

,You dirty tentacle. What kind of scheme are you plotting?

-...That person has disappeared.

He confessed the shocking fact in a voice without any playfulness.

,You're seriously saying they disappeared from the trial.'

Zeus didn't feel bad. Completing the myth meant that Seong Yujin had survived to the end. Seong Yujin deserved praise just for the fact that they faced that being, talked to them, and survived. In the old days, he would have invited them to Olympus and made them a god.

-That person's presence is not felt in the entire universe. The great nothingness has disappeared.

,...What kind of dog-like antics are you up to?

-I told you the truth.

,Did they move to another universe?

-I would have told you. I said that person's presence is not felt in the entire universe.

,Isn't it possible they hid themselves?

-Why?

,.......'

Zeus had nothing to say. Because he knew better than anyone that it didn't make sense for that being to hide themselves while being mindful of others. In that being's eyes, humans are worse than rolling stones, and most gods are worse than passing dogs.

,Is it possible to disappear from the entire universe? Could it be that everything became boring and they chose annihilation?

Zeus also sometimes wanted to choose annihilation because everything became boring.

-Annihilation? You moron. Don't say ridiculous things. You can never understand that person. Just know that they disappeared.

Zeus flared up.

,You're the moron, you tentacle bastard. Didn't the system just notify us? That being has been annihilated.

-Bullshit. The system....

Nyarlathotep stopped talking in the middle. Zeus also realized belatedly. He had forgotten for a moment because the system was so convenient. The system was just a system. No matter what, it didn't have the power to sense and be certain of the annihilation of a great being.

-Check the system. As the main god of mythology, you should have that authority.

Zeus reached out into the air. Electricity crackled from his five fingers. He accesses and inspects the essence of the system. It was an authority that only a few gods, including Zeus, possessed.

「Unauthorized access detected. Access is blocked.」

,What the hell?

Pajijik!

Zeus accessed it again.

「Unauthorized access detected. Access is blocked.」

「Unauthorized access detected. Access is blocked.」

「Unauthorized access detected. Access is blocked.」

The same notification window popped up no matter how many times he accessed it. Zeus looked at the system notification window with a dumbfounded face.

,System! You're watching, right! Come and explain!

「There is nothing to explain. As I said, it is unauthorized access.」

,You know I have that authority, right?

「You do not have any authority related to me.」

,You? Something must be seriously broken. I'll fix it myself.

Pajijik!

Lightning flashed around Zeus. He was going to use his authority to forcibly tear through the surface of the system, access the core, and repair the broken parts.

Ting!

Zeus's lightning bounced off.

,What is this? Am I dreaming right now?

「Forced interference with the system has been detected. The divine seat cannot forcibly interfere with the system under any circumstances.」

「A penalty is imposed on the owner of the sky for violating the Atlantis rules.」

「The owner of the sky is restricted for the next 100 days.」

Zeus felt his power rapidly decrease as if he had been placed under a ban.

,You crazy bastard! How dare you!

It was when the enraged Zeus was about to gather his strength. Nyarlathotep's sigh was heard.

-This is ridiculous. Did that person really choose annihilation.... What on earth did they realize through that human?

,Nyarlathotep! Did you mess with the system?!

-You stupid bastard. Even I can't do that. The power of that annihilated person has permeated the system.

,So that being really annihilated? Then why did their power permeate the system?

-It's something that that person decided.

,So why?

-How would I know?

,.......'

-Hehe. Anyway, the gods can't touch the system recklessly anymore. I didn't like that some gods, including you, were exploiting the system's loopholes... This is great.

「Detected unauthorized intervention by Crawling Chaos during the review and inspection of the trial.」

「Sanctions against Crawling Chaos and its avatars.」

-That's already in the past!

,Hmph. Serves you right.

Zeus laughed at Nyarlathotep to his heart's content.

But that was only for a moment. Soon, Zeus asked Nyarlathotep.

,If that being has been annihilated, what will be the aftermath? Such a great being has disappeared, so there must be an aftermath. The old ones aren't going to cause a ruckus, are they?

-I don't know. I've never even imagined such a thing. In the first place, that person wasn't very interested in such things... I don't think you need to worry too much about the aftermath. I have something urgent to do, so I'll be going.

Nyarlathotep's presence disappeared. Zeus clicked his tongue and got up from his seat. It was necessary to sort out the other gods who were in confusion.

However, contrary to expectations, the other gods of Olympus were not in confusion.

,I was wondering how things would turn out... It seems to have been resolved well. Thank you for your hard work, Zeus.

Poseidon said to Zeus while glaring at Seong Yujin on the screen.

,It was twisted at the end... But you sorted it out well.

Hades also nodded.

,The myth is complete. I never thought they would conquer even the north.... It should be said that there is no such perfect myth. It was a bit violent, but that's what myths are like in the first place.... I acknowledge the overcoming of the trial.

Hera said with her usual cold attitude.

The other gods of Olympus also expressed their opinions. Everyone acknowledged the clearing of the trial. Even if they didn't like Seong Yujin, they couldn't ignore their actions.

,The stories are all focused on the trial.'

Zeus carefully probed them.

,Can we say that the trial was overcome when that being appeared and ruined it?

,Who can handle that being? From the moment that being appeared, it was over. It actually ended, too.

Hermes answered in a bright voice. The other gods nodded as if they agreed.

,...They know that the trial ended after that being appeared. The other gods don't know what happened after that.'

It seems that they couldn't see Seong Yujin from when the system was cut off. The gods knew that as the end of the trial.

,I was able to see it because I was connected to Seong Yujin.'

It was possible because Seong Yujin was in Zeus's body. In Nyarlathotep's case, he was able to peek because he had a contract with Zeus.

Even then, he couldn't know the scene where Seong Yujin and that being were talking.

,Anyway, it's fine if they don't know.'

There was a reason why the saying "what you don't know won't hurt you" existed. The system also looks fine on the surface, doesn't it? Zeus decided to quietly shut his mouth.

,That human has completed the trial, so we must give them appropriate compensation.

Zeus solemnly declared.

,You're stating the obvious. Were you planning not to give it then?

Hera's taunt flew in. Zeus cleared his throat and tried to ignore it.

Zeus recalled Hera in the trial. Hera, who was cold yet subtly gentle. But the Hera in reality was glaring at him with an extremely cold gaze.
