I, who was seriously plotting betrayal, sighed. What do I do in times like these? After thinking for a moment, I slapped her on the cheek with the back of my hand.

Ttchak!

,Elena, stop talking nonsense and get your head on straight."

,......!"

Elena, who had been slapped, stared at me with wide eyes. The shock therapy seemed to work, as her depressed mood changed.

Elena's wavering eyes calmed down.

,Haa."

Elena, who sighed with multiple meanings, stood up straight on the floor without my help. She checked her surroundings, where explosions were erupting and screams were echoing. Elena grasped the situation and corrected her disheveled posture.

,You're the second person to hit me. It's been a while since I've been slapped, so it snapped me out of it. Couldn't you have used a little less force? My cheek is still tingling."

My handprint remained on Elena's cheek.

,The situation was urgent, so I put a little too much force into it. I'm curious who the first person was."

,My mother. I got hit a lot."

,...No way. Did you kill her for slapping you?"

,I can't say I didn't."

Elena chuckled. She used magic. The dress she was wearing disappeared, and she changed into her usual uniform. Unfortunately, it wasn't like a magical girl transformation, so I didn't get to see Elena naked.

She looked around once and nodded.

,What a mess."

,It's quite a mess."

The Emperor of Eustia and Vanrey, the master of the Barclay Legion Alliance, were fighting head-on. The Emperor wasn't being pushed back. It was incredible. Each time they clashed, the shockwaves were blowing away parts of the Imperial Palace.

,I didn't expect the Barclay Legion to interfere. Even less that their master, Vanrey, would aim for the Emperor's head."

,I can roughly guess what's going on. The Emperor has been in contact with the Barclay Legion for a while now. He probably weighed the pros and cons between the Angelus Legion and the Barclay Legion."

,The Emperor chose the Angelus Legion, and the Barclay Legion got pissed about it?"

,That's right. So the Barclay Legion committed this terror to weaken the Eustia Empire. The Eustia Empire's power is the greatest in Atlantis. For the Barclay Legion, they're willing to take a loss to reduce the Eustia Empire's power."

It wasn't a reckless attack.

Indeed, it's impossible for the Barclay Legion Alliance to raid the Imperial Palace just because they're angry.

,But why aren't you getting involved?"

,Unfortunately, I'm a criminal. If I, a criminal, were to rashly step forward, what would become of His Majesty the Emperor's position?"

Is she really doing nothing? I don't think so. Right now, she's scanning her surroundings with shining eyes.

,Why aren't the invaders attacking us? I'm one thing, but you're one of the Empire Ogong."

,Their target isn't us, but the Emperor and the Crown Prince. The Empire Ogong, except for me, are fighting to protect the Emperor, but they're being blocked by others and can't help the Emperor."

Gabriel was protecting the Crown Prince.

,...Was the Emperor always this strong?"

,The Emperor has a secret. Simply being born with royal blood isn't enough to protect the throne of Eustia."

,What's the secret?"

,I don't know, that's why it's a secret. What's certain is... it's related to Cheonnyeongong. Don't you know?"

,I don't know."

Building fragments fly towards Elena. I summoned Stormbreaker in front of Elena and knocked away the fragments.

,But why isn't that Cheonnyeongong actively moving?"

,That's because the Emperor hasn't given the order. Cheonnyeongong has always been like that. He doesn't actively help the Emperor, but he prevents the Emperor from dying as much as possible. Cheonnyeongong is even more mysterious than the Emperor."

At that moment, Kang Myungjin, who was focused on rescuing people, approached us.

,Duke Valderalt-kakha, Eugene and I will save people here, so it would be best if you could leave this place."

I glanced over. Seira, who was fighting the invaders on the 3rd floor, was visible. She didn't seem to be in any danger that required help.

,Not bad. I will."

Elena permitted. I roughly knew what Kang Myungjin was thinking. Kang Myungjin was looking at cutting ties with the Valderalt Dukedom. As the A-Plan Legion received support from the Valderalt Dukedom, it would be directly affected if the Duke fell.

However, I shook my head.

,Myungjin-ah. I am currently the escort knight of Duke Valderalt-kakha. I will carry out the mission I've been assigned to the end."

,...I understand. I will prioritize saving people."

Kang Myungjin answered and disappeared.

Elena looked at me as if I were a curious thing.

,Why did you do that? Is there still something to gain from me? Let me tell you in advance. I have become a criminal. The Emperor and the Crown Prince will try to kill me. They're probably thinking of using me as an example to solidify the imperial power. The reputation of the Empire Ogong was excessively high."

,Well. You won't fall. At most, you'll end up with a fine or a suspension order. I'll make sure of it."

,Like how you moved Cheolhyeongong and Gabriel? ...I'm afraid of you. It pains me that I'm starting to trust you more and more. I'm afraid that I won't be able to handle your betrayal."

,How many times do I have to say it for you to believe me? I won't betray you, okay?"

,I'm sorry. I can't trust you no matter what. And think about your usual behavior. Can you be trusted?"

I had nothing to say when she put it that way. I quietly shut my mouth.

,Duke Valderalt!!"

The Emperor, who was fighting Vanrey, shouted. Elena straightened her back.

,Yes, Your Majesty."

,Your punishment will be postponed to a later date! Your suspension is also revoked!"

,Give me your command."

,There is no need to help me! Evacuate the nobles in the ballroom! They are all capable administrators of the Empire! They must not be sacrificed here! Also, the capital is in chaos, so protect the citizens of the capital!"

,I will follow the Emperor's command."

Elena began to use magic in earnest. The atmosphere inside the ballroom changed as she intervened. The explosions decreased, and the nobles who were dying began to escape more easily. As the situation didn't go as planned, the invaders rushed towards Elena.

,Just a bunch of nobodies."

Elena flicked her fingers. The invaders, who were under an illusion, swung their swords at empty air or attacked their comrades.

I also moved. There were those who weren't affected by Elena's illusion, whether it was due to a skill or an item. I dealt with them.

,Instant.'

I swung Stormbreaker, which I had turned into a sword, and killed the invaders. I didn't actively try to save people. There wasn't much to gain, and there were quite a few guys stronger than me. I refused to get entangled with the Barclay Legion.

And more than anything, Elena was subtly killing the nobles. She was manipulating the nobles who would harm the Valderalt Dukedom just by being alive with illusions, creating a subtle situation where they would die at the hands of the invaders.

Ttokak ttogak.

Elena calmly walked out of the ballroom.

Coming outside, I could see the scenery of the capital. Flames were rising from various parts of the city. The sound of explosions and the screams of citizens reached even here.

,The Barclay Legion Alliance... they're serious. They must have been planning this for months at least.'

Elena moved. It wasn't towards the capital. The place she was heading to was where some nobles were gathered.

They were vassals of the Valderalt family, led by Baron Hyman Goldrex. Traitors who betrayed the head of the family, Elena, and sided with the Crown Prince.

Their faces turned pale as Elena approached.

,H-Head of the family!"

,W-What are you planning to do to us?!"

,Don't come near us! His Highness the Crown Prince won't stand idly by!"

,When you think about it, didn't the head of the family betray our trust first! To cast an illusion on us and manipulate our memories! That was crossing the line!"

,......."

The vassals, terrified, protested. Only Hyman remained silent. They tried to escape, but a magical barrier surrounded them, blocking their view of the outside.

,I was complacent. The illusion I cast on you was one that I, from 12 years ago, cast with all my might. I was confident that the illusion wouldn't be broken even if you died, but to think that the Crown Prince would have someone with the ability to break my illusion..."

,Head of the family."

,Yes. Hyman. Do you have anything to say?"

,I think that if the head of the family hadn't cast that illusion on me back then... this wouldn't have happened."

,Are you saying it's my fault? Hmm. I admit it. But my thoughts are a bit different. Even if I had trusted you and not cast the illusion, you would have sided with the Crown Prince."

,I-I'm sorry, Head of the family!"

,Please spare us, Head of the family!"

The vassals of Valderalt began to kneel and beg. Elena ignored them. Her gaze was only directed at Hyman.

,Stop with the same old stories about if I hadn't cast the illusion, if you hadn't listened to the Crown Prince, if I had trusted you. What's important now is the present. You betrayed me, the Valderalt family. And I do not forgive traitors."

,...Head of the family. Please have mercy. We did not reveal the Valderalt's secrets."

,I acknowledge that. And you have worked for the Valderalt family for a long time. I will show you the least bit of mercy. I will allow you to commit suicide."

,Thank you, Head of the family."

Elena then waved her hand. Cones of wind pierced their necks and hearts. They were all dead. Elena put their corpses into a magical space.

,Wouldn't it be faster to burn the bodies right away?"

,They betrayed us, but they are vassals of Valderalt. As they have worked for the family for a long time, I will treat their corpses with respect."

,I've been thinking about it, and the reason why that old man Hyman betrayed us is..."

,I know. Hyman didn't have much time left to live. He didn't betray me because he regained his manipulated memories and was blinded by wealth and revenge. He probably made a deal with the Crown Prince. Something along the lines of guaranteeing the safety of the Valderalt family in exchange for me losing my position."

,Eugene. I have a favor to ask of you."

,You know you can't get away with just words, right?"

,I will repay the debt someday."
