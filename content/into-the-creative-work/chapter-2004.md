「You have astonished the gods and overcome the trials of Olympus.」

「The gods of Olympus have acknowledged your trials.」

「Acquire the title of 'Honorary Olympian'!」

「All stats increase by 1.」

「The myth you have built is as false as a fleeting dream. But to some gods, your myth has resonated intensely. The false myth blesses you!」

「Luck stat increases by 10.」

「The remnants of ■■■■■ bless and extinguish you.」

「Cosmic luck imbues you.」

「Luck stat increases by 10.」

「Overcoming the trials of Olympus with overwhelming success grants you 1,300,000 AP.」

「As a reward for overcoming the trials, Astrape's rank increases to EX rank.」

「Astrape

Can wield the lightning of the gods.

Type: God Seat Skill

Rank: EX」

「To perfectly wield Astrape(EX), the rank of Lightning(SS) must be higher.」

「The power of Astrape(EX) is reduced due to the low rank of Lightning(SS).」

I quickly read the notification windows that popped up. I never get tired of reading them, no matter how many times I do. A smile crept onto my face.

'It didn't seem like a particularly difficult trial... but the rewards are so sweet.'

I resisted the urge to test Astrape. Because there were more rewards to receive.

「You have obtained dominion over the 4,010th District, Hera's Garden.」

「Acquire 500,000 AP.」

「As the ruler of Hera's Garden, acquire 200,000 AP every 30 days.」

「A golden apple tree grows in Hera's Garden. As the ruler, you obtain one golden apple each month.」

「You can receive the golden apple. Would you like to receive it?」

'I don't have to go there and pick the golden apple myself. How convenient.'

For reference, there was also a golden apple in the [Savior of the Academy] world. The effects were a bit different, though.

I received the golden apple. First, I was curious about what effects the golden apple would have.

The golden apple was, true to its name, an apple whose surface shone like gold.

「Golden Apple

Eating a golden apple randomly increases your stats once for the first time.

Eating a golden apple makes you one year younger.

Rank: S」

'Stat increase? Jackpot!'

On top of that, there was even an effect that made you one year younger. And it seemed like this was an effect that activated every time you ate it.

'The stat effect isn't the real deal, this is.'

Wealthy nobles came to mind. Time is equal for everyone. Nobles with more power and money than others would find that the most displeasing. They would want to live longer to enjoy their power for longer.

'If I sell it, I could make a lot of money.'

Even though I thought that, I didn't really need the money. It might be better to give it as a gift to my women. Juseo-hyeon worked hard last time, should I give it to her?

'...No. Juseo-hyeon is always plotting to take my life. There's no need to make her stronger.'

Even now, I wasn't confident in beating Juseo-hyeon if we only fought with swordsmanship. She was getting stronger at an alarming rate, as if she had gained some kind of enlightenment last time.

'I'll eat the first apple.'

Asak!

I shivered after eating the apple.

It was delicious. The refreshing taste quenched my thirst and invigorated my whole body.

'It's incredibly delicious! I don't want to give this to anyone else!'

「Luck stat increases by 2 due to the effect of the golden apple!」

「Luck stat has reached 100!」

「An achievement that cannot be accomplished unless the universe helps you!」

「Acquire Unsu Daetong(EX).」

"Unsu Daetong (Great Mastery of Fortune and Numbers)"

Activates randomly.

Type: Skill

Rank: EX」

'EX rank skill?!'

I was surprised. I didn't expect a skill like this to come out just because my luck stat reached 100.

'It's not like you can just raise your luck stat if you want to.'

Anyone with a proper mind wouldn't bother trying to raise their luck stat. Other stats are more helpful for survival.

'It's a bit underwhelming for an EX skill.'

Unsu Daetong. The ability is probably just making luck better, as the name suggests. The problem is that it can't be used actively.

'Still, it'll pay for itself someday, right?'

I came to a rough conclusion and continued what I was doing.

Asak! Asak! Asasasak!

I was mindlessly eating the golden apple when I realized I had eaten all the flesh and became depressed. All that was left was the golden apple seed.

'...Seed? Can I mass-produce golden trees with this?'

You never know. I took the golden apple seed just in case.

「You have obtained the right to use Hephaestus's smithy.」

「Would you like to use the right to use Hephaestus's smithy?」

Originally, I came all the way here because of Hephaestus's smithy.

I readily nodded.

"Use it."

Jiiiiing.

The space in front of me distorted and a portal appeared.

Inside the portal was a huge smithy with scarlet lava flowing. The volcano was constantly erupting, sending its heat to the smithy. More precisely, to the furnace behind the smithy.

I swallowed hard and tensed up.

If I were Zeus, I would have entered without hesitation... but I wasn't Zeus now. I protected my body with mana and entered the portal.

「Entering the 4,016th District, Flowing Steel.」

「Using the right to use Hephaestus's smithy.」

「You have temporarily become the ruler of the 4,016th District, Flowing Steel. Dominion will be maintained until you leave the district.」

It wasn't as hot as I expected. It was like early summer in Korea.

'It doesn't make sense with lava flowing next to me. Is it because I have dominion?'

I confidently walked towards the smithy in front of me.

I forcefully opened the smithy door.

There was no one inside the smithy. That didn't mean it was quiet. The huge furnace connected to the volcano was constantly burning.

'It's excessively huge.'

It was so huge that I didn't even want to get close to it.

"Hephaestus-nim. I have completed the mission."

The heat of the furnace leaked out and took the form of a person.

「You have completed the mission of the earth-shaking cripple.」

「Receive the mission rewards directly.」

Hephaestus opened his mouth while looking at me.

"You have done well. It must have been a difficult task, but I didn't expect you to accomplish it so quickly."

"I was lucky."

"I have been watching your myth. It's something my father would like. ...I also know what you think of me."

Oops.

In the trials, I exploited Hephaestus as much as I could. I exploited him like a slave. Without thinking about the consequences at all.

At that time, I was so engrossed in Zeus that I forgot that the other gods were watching.

"Well, I didn't originally intend to do that...."

"It's just something that happened in the trials anyway. The gods of Olympus aren't so narrow-minded as to scold you for something that happened in the trials. The trials are just trials. It was also quite fun to see another me building battleships."

"Do Hera-nim and Hestia-nim also think like Hephaestus-nim?"

"...Well. I don't know the hearts of the goddesses."

Hephaestus's voice was gloomy.

His relationship with women wasn't good in the original history either. He was abandoned by Hera and betrayed by Aphrodite, wasn't he?

"However, they didn't seem to dislike or hate you. Aphrodite seems to be completely smitten with you... I can't even begin to guess what my mother is thinking."

"Um. That, the thing with Aphrodite-nim...."

I sweated and fidgeted. Hephaestus has to repair the Hwaryeon Bido. He might hold a grudge and deliberately fail the repair. I would if I were him.

"The myths you know are very old stories. Nothing is eternal in this world, and even the immortal gods change. The relationship between the Olympian gods and what you know has already changed a lot. There have been many incidents and accidents not recorded in the myths."

"I said something unnecessary."

I took out the Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory. I stared at the red blade and paused. There were cracks all over the blade. Something was leaking from the cracked parts.

"...It shouldn't have been this badly damaged."

"My vision was temporarily shared. This knife has accumulated too much evil karma. Evil karma has accumulated even while I wasn't watching."

"Um. Do you think it would be better to just destroy it? Do you think so?"

"There's no need to read my mind. I am not a god who hides sin. And what sin does a tool have? The sin lies with the owner of the tool. Take out the prepared materials."

I took out Fafnir's heart. Hephaestus nodded.

Then I took out additional materials.

「Fate Destroyer

Can destroy fate exactly once.

Rank: EX」

"This is not something that can be used as a material."

Hephaestus said firmly. I clicked my tongue in disappointment. Since it was an EX rank, I thought something amazing would be created if I used it as a material.

「Black Heart

A perpetual motion machine that constantly moves and generates energy.

Rank: SSS」

I took out a black orb. Inside the orb, hundreds of gears were meshing and constantly turning.

"You're going to use this? As far as I know, this is...."

It was also the key to ruling the 57th District, the City of Lights, Etin.

There were many other Legions around the 57th District, so I couldn't recklessly rule the 57th District. And now, I didn't really have any lingering feelings for the 57th District.

"If a perpetual motion machine is put into a knife, wouldn't an amazing object be created? Like being able to use Sword energy indefinitely."

"Well. I can't guarantee the effect as much as it's used as a material. In the first place, a perpetual motion machine is not an object made to be used as a material. Are you serious about using this?"

"Yes."

There was no hesitation. In my head, I was picturing a Hwaryeon Bido that spewed out infinite energy.

Hephaestus looked at me with a strange face and nodded.

"I will respect your choice. Then let's begin. Entrust your body to me."

"Yes? What, what do you mean by that?"

"The knife you have does not recognize you. So I will go inside you and wield the hammer. I swear on the River Styx in my name that I will not have the slightest influence on your mind."

I didn't like it at all. But I couldn't refuse after coming this far. I have to fix and strengthen the Hwaryeon Bido.

"...Come in."

Hephaestus entered me. He naturally moved my body. He held a hammer in his right hand and the Hwaryeon Bido in his left hand. Fafnir's heart and the Black Heart floated in the air and followed behind me.

Surprisingly, Hephaestus entered the boiling furnace. Inside the furnace, molten metal and lava swirled and emitted heat. There was no ceiling, and the starry night sky was visible.

'The space itself has changed. Is this the real Hephaestus's smithy?'

Hephaestus heated the Hwaryeon Bido and began to hammer it with the hammer of the gods.

Kkaang!

A clear metallic sound rings out.

He repeated that several times. The Hwaryeon Bido was not being repaired, but rather being broken. Hephaestus didn't panic and hammered it until it was on the verge of breaking. Only after it had truly reached its limit did he melt Fafnir's heart and feed it into the Hwaryeon Bido.

The hammering begins again.

It was when Hephaestus turned his gaze to the Black Heart.

「Unsu Daetong(EX) is activated.」

'It's activating already?!'

Stars fell from the sky.

Over thousands of meteors drew silver tails across the night sky and fell to the ground.

Stars mixed with the volcano and lava. Sparkling stars were added to the scarlet furnace.

"The Black Heart cannot be used."

The blacksmith god refined the flames, lava, dragon's heart, and star fragments, and hammered the Hwaryeon Bido.
