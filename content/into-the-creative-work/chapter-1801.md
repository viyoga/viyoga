I was slightly surprised that Park Suho used divine power. The source of divine power is faith, and Park Suho is an atheist and a guy far from being devout.

,Is it the power of the tattoo?

I narrowed my eyes and looked at Park Suho's divine power. When I looked closely, I felt a sense of incongruity. It didn't feel like the divine power I knew. And the light of divine power looked weak. To the point where I wondered if it was effective.

,Suho-ya. What is that power?

,It's divine power. It's the power I received from the goddess Selbretina-nim.

Park Suho said proudly.

,...Did you even meet the goddess in person?

,I didn't meet her in person, but I met her in a dream. Thanks to that, I was able to use this power.

,What exactly is the effect of this power?

,I can gain an advantage when dealing with the power of the evil god and his followers. One of the reasons why a hero can stand against an evil god is thanks to this power.

Park Suho confidently raised his sword imbued with divine power. To me, it was a faint light of divinity, but it seemed to have an effect, as the power of the evil god seemed to weaken.

,Is there really a need to do that.

I wondered if overwhelming him with power wouldn't be a better method, but I kept my mouth shut. The elves wouldn't have brought Park Suho for no reason. There must be something I don't know.

,Yujin hyung-nim. The power of the evil god becomes strongest when it disappears, so be careful. If you let your guard down at that time, you can go crazy.

,Oh, okay. I'll keep a tight grip on my sanity. Start.

Park Suho struck down his sword towards the power of the evil god. The power of the evil god split in half, and that power scattered in all directions and disappeared. Just before the power of the evil god completely disappeared, the power of the evil god suddenly increased as if exploding.

Darkness.

I could only see darkness.

,Eugh.

I frowned as I received the power of the evil god with my whole body. It doesn't hurt. But I feel something trying to invade me, so it feels very unpleasant.

-Disgusting things were here.

A heavy and terrible voice was heard. It was the voice of the evil god, Brahmasen, whom I had heard several times.

,Not tired of it and came out again.

While grumbling inwardly, I tensed my body. In the worst case, that bastard could send me to another world again.

-Weak things. You will be eaten by dreams and succumb.

A small light appeared in the surroundings where nothing was visible. I reflexively focused on the light. I couldn't help but do so since light appeared after seeing nothing.

The small light gradually grew larger and flickered. And the space changes. I was standing in the classroom before I knew it.

,It's the high school I used to go to.

It was the moment I was about to look around with a strange sense of familiarity. Cracks appeared in the space, and the classroom began to collapse.

,What are you trying to do.

It seemed like it was trying to do something, but it just broke down and disappeared. I was rather annoyed.

I held Hwaryeon Bido in my hand and walked forward. I didn't even walk five steps before I saw something new.

Park Suho.

He was sitting on the floor, looking at something.

,Park Suho!

I raised my voice and called him. Park Suho, who was kneeling on the ground, didn't even flinch. He wouldn't be pretending not to hear on purpose. He wasn't that kind of guy.

I clicked my tongue and approached Park Suho. Park Suho had a blank look on his face. He was staring blankly ahead. It was obvious that he couldn't hear my voice.

,Is he watching something interesting?

I turned my eyes to the front of Park Suho. I couldn't see anything. Park Suho can see it, but I can't?

,This makes me more curious. I can't stand being curious.

[Opening the Celestial Eye].

I was going to give up if I couldn't see it even with the Celestial Eye. There was no other way except for the Celestial Eye. Fortunately, the power of the Celestial Eye worked. The darkness was pierced through, and a new sight appeared.

A family of four was sitting around a table, chatting and eating.

The gracefully aged middle-aged couple had soft smiles on their faces. A warm atmosphere was created from them. Park Suho and his younger sister, Park Gaini, were sitting next to them. Park Suho was engrossed in the bulgogi on the table, and Park Gaini was constantly chattering about something.

,Park Suho is a high school student... and Park Gaini looks like a middle school student.

It's a dream.

A dream about a time that can never be seen again.

The scene changed.

Broken cars were rolling around on the street, and corpses were scattered everywhere. The building was on fire, and acrid black smoke covered the sky.

Park Suho was hugging Park Gaini in his arms at the edge of the street and wailing. His parents were chillingly cold in a terrible state.

,Did Park Suho say he was from Changwon?

I know he's called the hero of Changwon. But I don't know exactly what happened.

,It's not common, but it's not a very rare occurrence either.

There are many Hunters in South Korea. But that doesn't necessarily mean it's safe. Most Hunters stay in the metropolitan area. If you go to the provinces, there are even claims that there is a shortage of Hunters.

Hunters are also people, so they prefer Seoul, the center of Korea. It's also more convenient for Hunters in Seoul.

The scene changed again. It's a scene of Park Suho when he was younger. A scene where the family came to the amusement park for a trip. They look down at the city from the Ferris wheel.

And the scene changed to the burning street from before.

,He's showing me good memories and shitty memories at the same time. That evil god bastard. He's determined to shake Park Suho's mind.

It's a useless act. It didn't seem like Park Suho would collapse with just this much.

-I will revive your parents and remove the curse from your sister. In return, swear eternal allegiance to me.

,That's a very sweet offer.

[Resurrection of the Dead] came to mind. I can also revive Park Suho's parents if I set my mind to it.

,But Park Suho and I aren't that close.

Park Suho is shedding tears. Considering Park Suho's usual self, it didn't seem like he would fall for Brahmasen. But I didn't want to wait for Park Suho to wake up on his own either.

I approached Park Suho and smacked him on the back of the head.

Ppahk!

With a cheerful sound, Park Suho's upper body collapses. I was willing to give him a soccer kick if he didn't get up. Fortunately, Park Suho trembled and raised his body. A gleam appeared in his dazed eyes.

,Keueuk! My head. Who the hell did that....

I made eye contact with Park Suho, who had raised his upper body.

,Did you finally wake up? I hit you because you didn't wake up.

,Ah, yes. I was caught in the evil god's scheme, but thank you, hyung-nim.

Park Suho, who quickly grasped the situation, raised his body.

-Is it you again.

Brahmasen's voice, sounding fed up, echoed. It was directed at me, not Park Suho.

,I was going to say that. Is it you again.

I'm just as sick of it.

I want to use the Power of Life and Death to kill him. But the opponent was a god. Let's say I killed him using the Power of Life and Death. Is that really the end?

,Jesus resurrected after 3 days. There's no guarantee that this bastard won't resurrect either.

That bastard confidently offered to revive Park Suho's dead parents. It wouldn't be strange even if he actually had such power. So, it wasn't a good idea to rashly use the Power of Life and Death.

-Insolent bug. I can't stand it anymore. I have to kill you and the hero even if I have to overdo it.

A sound was heard. It was the sound of something crawling. I and Park Suho raised our heads above where the sound was coming from.

Darkness.

In the darkness where nothing can be seen, it felt like it was wriggling and crawling. A huge presence presses down on us.

,Is he perhaps descending in his true form?

Drip.

Something fell.

It was blood. Red blood fell like a torrential rain from the sky. I and Park Suho were instantly covered in blood.

,Keu, keueueueueueue....

Park Suho trembled. His expression was distorted with pain.

,Is it not ordinary blood? But I'm fine.

Park Suho wrapped his arms around his body. His shoulders shrank, and his legs trembled.

Ttak ttak ttak.

Park Suho's teeth clashed like castanets.

And it revealed itself. Five red eyes look down on us. The huge tentacles were wounded, and blood droplets fell below. Why was it wounded? It seemed like the unreasonable price that bastard mentioned.

,Aaaaaaaaaaah! Kkeuaaaaaaaah! Beuaaaaaaaaah!

Park Suho goes berserk. He tears at his hair with both hands and folds and unfolds his upper body. Park Suho's legs are bent to the side, and blood flows down his nose. Even worse, he pissed and shat his pants.

I backed away from Park Suho.

,There was a time when I thought Park Suho was the main character.

That thought was wrong. What protagonist would piss and shit so disgracefully?

Park Suho staggered towards me. I was horrified and widened the distance even more.

-To think I would move myself to kill you bugs.

The culprit who drove Park Suho crazy said in a leisurely tone.

I felt an overwhelming difference in power. That bastard could easily kill us like killing bugs.

I clenched my fist tightly. If I fight that bastard now, how many seconds can I last? It would be fortunate if I lasted 10 seconds.

,Should I really use the Power of Life and Death.

If I use the Power of Life and Death to kill that bastard now, I will be able to live. I hardened my heart as I watched the bloody tentacles fall.

,I have to live first.

It was then.

Light burst in the air. This feeling was similar to the divine power that Park Suho showed, but different. It felt more intense.

There was some being in the burst of light. I can definitely feel it. The problem is that I can only feel it, but I don't know what kind of being it is. I can only guess at the existence.

-That damn bitch has revealed herself!

-You cannot harm them.

The woman replied calmly to the angry Brahmasen.

-Another interference! Now I feel more than anger, I feel hatred! This will never go smoothly!

Brahmasen swung his huge tentacles. The tentacles could not penetrate the light and were blocked. Brahmasen's five eyes glared at us before slowly closing.

-I'll see how long that insignificant life of yours will last....

Brahmasen's presence disappeared.

,I saved the Power of Life and Death.

I was satisfied.
