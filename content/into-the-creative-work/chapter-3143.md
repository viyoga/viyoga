"Merlin's Numbers. The artifacts Merlin used during his lifetime. For a high-ranking mage like me, or anyone with an interest in relics, it's an artifact you can't possibly not know about."

"Even among high-ranking mages, plenty don't know. Information related to Merlin's Numbers is kept under fairly strict control."

I was surprised by the unexpected revelation. Of course, I didn't let it show.

I'd never directly looked into information about Merlin's Numbers in this world. After all, I already knew about them through my knowledge of the source material. Though I didn't know everything.

"So Merlin's Numbers are the reward for this commission?"

"That's right. If you know about Merlin's Numbers, you know this is an enormous treasure, don't you?"

"That's exactly the problem—I know it too well. You're putting up a treasure like that as payment for this commission? Are you serious?"

No matter how I thought about it, the reward far outstripped the difficulty of the job. Merlin's Numbers—if offered to the Queen of Neo London, it was valuable enough to earn at least a count's title.

"It's a treasure, sure, but it's one only mages can use. And among mages, only a gifted few can use it. I may collect treasures, but I'm not the type to let them rot in a warehouse. A treasure is ultimately an object. It only has meaning when it's used."

"Can you show me what it is? I find it hard to believe you actually possess Merlin's Numbers."

"Here?"

"It doesn't have to be here. Do you happen to have it on you right now? That treasure?"

"Hm. Well, fine. There are no cameras here, and I've made my own preparations."

Nora raised her hand. The wavelength of mana rippled and the air split open. A subspace. There was no manifestation of a spell formula, so it wasn't magic. It seemed to be an ability, or some method of handling subspace I didn't know about.

What emerged from the subspace was a small glass orb. Inside the glass orb was a purple rose.

A mysterious aura emanated from that glass orb. In some ways, it felt similar to Arcane.

I swallowed dryly. Instinctively, I was certain this was indeed Merlin's Numbers.

*'A Number I've never seen before. Even I don't know all of Merlin's Numbers. At the very least, this is an artifact that never appeared in the game version I know.'*

The urge to snatch it and run was almost overwhelming. But I held back. There was no way Nora would have pulled out that treasure so openly without some kind of countermeasure in place.

"Which Number is it?"

"No.2 Avalonia. A Mystic Eye Merlin used during his lifetime."

"A Mystic Eye? This is Merlin's eye?"

"Surely you don't think it's an actual eyeball. It's an artifact. One that can be used by implanting it into your eye. Of course, it's not a surgical implant. If you have the aptitude, you can implant it just by holding it against your eye."

"…It's possible I don't have the aptitude."

A hollow statement, and I knew it.

I was instinctively drawn to that Mystic Eye. Aptitude? Of course I'd have it. With my talent, it would be abnormal not to.

"If you don't have the aptitude, will you give up?"

As if.

If I were any other mage, there was no way I could look at that treasure and give up.

"If that's a Mystic Eye, do you know its ability too?"

"One of my abilities is appraisal. I can appraise any object. If you implant Avalonia into your eye, you can use illusion magic."

"So I'd be able to use illusion magic. You're saying it grants aptitude for the illusion branch. Illusion magic is a rare branch. Just that alone is an outstanding ability, but… for one of Merlin's Numbers, it's a rather dull power."

"You. You know more about Merlin's Numbers than I expected."

“…….”

The one who possessed three of Merlin's Numbers was me.

"It's as you say. Avalonia's true ability is something else entirely. Avalonia can rewrite reality through illusion. Naturally, there are limits to its scale. And it can only be used with Avalonia's own energy."

"Its own energy?"

"Not mana. Vision energy. Would you understand if I said that?"

"You mean Arcane."

"Oh. You know. Most mages can't distinguish between Vision and Arcane. So? Will you take the commission?"

"I'll accept the commission. Can I receive it upfront, by any chance?"

"Hahaha."

Nora laughed heartily as if she'd heard a hilarious joke, then immediately straightened her face and said,

"Absolutely not."

• • •

The day I'd promised to meet Nora Hanger arrived. The day the Dark Moon would rise.

Nora and I met outside Neo London. The dungeon she'd mentioned would open somewhere in the mountains beyond Neo London. I didn't know the exact location because Nora had refused to tell me, citing security.

"You came right on time."

"It's a promise. What do you take me for?"

"The mages I've known usually showed up later than the agreed time. You're the strange one, for a mage."

"…A bunch of worthless bastards dragging down the average for all mages."

I grumbled as I began hiking after Nora.

The sky was darkening. The Dark Moon hadn't risen yet, but its influence was already spreading across the world. The mana density was far thicker than usual. Grass blades and leaves trembled on their own without any wind. Flowers that had long passed their blooming season were blossoming with lewd fullness. The legs of dead insect carcasses twitched.

My mana sense had grown sharper as well. Sharp enough that I could feel the presences trailing behind me without even casting a spell.

"Nora. Are those things behind us your colleagues, by any chance?"

"If they were colleagues, we'd have moved together. Ah, and it's not a trap to do something to you, either. This is apparently the third attempt, so… seems we've picked up a tail. Treasure hunters are treated like half treasure goblins, so they have plenty of enemies."

Her subspace likely contained all manner of treasures besides No.2 Avalonia.

*'And Nora has survived until now.'*

A Rank 7 Treasure Hunter.

She was by no means weak. The fact that she was still alive was proof enough of her strength. Even now, Nora was tense but unafraid.

"We're almost there. The dungeon is at the midpoint of the mountain."

"Is it alright to move so openly? You're not going to shake off the ones tailing us?"

"If they want to follow, let them follow. But they won't be able to get ahead of me."

Confidence flickered in Nora's smile.

Soon we arrived at the mountain's midpoint.

"There it is."

Where Nora pointed, there was a distorted space. The dungeon entrance. Under the Dark Moon's influence, the ground and vegetation around the space appeared warped.

"Wait. Let's deal with the ones behind us, even roughly, before we go in."

"Is it worth the effort? If they follow us into the dungeon anyway, they'll be done in by traps or monsters. There's no Rank 7 among them."

"I don't like being tailed."

"If that's how you feel, fine. Need help?"

"Don't need it."

I turned around.

Through the swaying vegetation, I could feel their presence.

"Come out."

“…….”

No answer came.

I raised my right hand. If they wouldn't come out, I intended to clean them all up with Thunder Break.

*'Thunder Break consumes a lot of mana, but it'll definitely sweep them all away.'*

Just as mana began to ripple centered on me, the ones in hiding revealed themselves.

One Rank 6 and five Rank 5s.

Judging by their clothing and armaments, they appeared to be mercenaries. In particular, the Rank 6 who seemed to be their team leader was a cyborg with more than half his body plated in machinery. The cyborg's right eye lens glowed red as he opened his mouth.

"Count Eugene Meyer. We have no intention of fighting you, Count. Our target is that treasure hunter and the dungeon before us."

"I've agreed to explore this dungeon with Nora. It's not for the likes of you to plunder."

"The likes of us…. You're certainly right. For us, opposing you and that treasure hunter is close to impossible. But we are merely a scouting party. Soon our commander and comrades will arrive. When they do, even you won't be able to ignore us, Count."

"Commander? So it was a mercenary company after all. What's the company's name?"

"We are the Wolfgang Mercenary Company. I'm sure you've heard of us."

A mercenary company with a Rank 7 commander named Wolfgang. The company was on the small side, but I'd heard of it because of the Rank 7 commander's reputation. It wasn't a company operating out of Neo London.

"A mercenary company that mainly handles assassinations and escort missions."

"Yes. Count Meyer. Join hands with us. We'll give you a portion of the treasures that treasure hunter possesses."

I let out a hollow laugh.

I didn't like a Rank 6 acting so boldly, and now they were offering me only a portion, not even the whole?

"You seem to think I'm quite the easy mark."

"Count Meyer. This isn't Neo London."

"So in your eyes, I'm just a mage who grew up coddled in a laboratory?"

My reputation should have spread considerably. Was it my arrogance? Or perhaps these bastards were treating my reputation as exaggerated.

It wasn't entirely incomprehensible. The combination of corporate chairman and combat mage was hard to imagine. They could assume the rumors were inflated for the chairman's prestige.

"Are you refusing our proposal?"

"I'm refusing."

The mercenaries instantly took combat stances. Their wariness spiked and gun barrels aimed at me.

"Even if we die, we'll take one of your arms, Count."

Ignoring the team leader's warning, I scanned him from head to toe.

"You. Your magic resistance is on the low side."

"…Magic is meaningless if it doesn't hit, in the end."

"True enough. Go ahead and try dodging, then."

[Using Leap Discharge.]

The moment I appeared behind his back, electric currents spread in all directions. Cyborgs were notoriously weak to electricity. Touched by the current, he was temporarily electrocuted and couldn't do a thing. In that gap, I raised my hand and calculated the spell formula.

Blue lightning appeared in my palm, compressed into a long, slender needle.

This was less pure magic and more immortal arts. Rank 7 Vision immortal arts.

Thunder Heaven Style, Lightning Art, Puppet Needle.

I grabbed the team leader's occiput with one hand and drove the Puppet Needle deep into his temple. Blue lightning burst from the needle that pierced through to his brain, seizing control of the team leader's body. Mana poured from his eyes as if exploding, and blue current raced across his entire body.

Kkiriik, kkiriririk….

Every time he moved his body, a terrible sound rang out like machinery grinding.

The Vision immortal art was a success.

I released his occiput from my hand and walked toward Nora standing at the dungeon entrance.

Kkiriririk!

The team leader aimed his gun at his own subordinates and pulled the trigger without hesitation.

"Aagh! Team leader?!"

"Why is he suddenly doing that?!"

"Fuck! What did that mage do to him?! Just shoot!"

The enemies began fighting among themselves.

Nora watched the spectacle with a look of disbelief, then asked me.

"What on earth was that horrible magic? It doesn't seem like black magic."

"Immortal arts. A type of magic."

"That thing is immortal arts…?"

"Let's go in."

We left the gunfire ringing behind us and entered the dungeon.
