I arrived in front of the old hotel.

For four days, I had Moka watch that old hotel. Since I exposed him, he might have stopped what he was doing and run away.

But my worries were unfounded. As if mocking my existence, that bastard stayed put and continued what he was doing.

,He must be confident.'

And there's also the possibility that he's not working alone.

Tensing my body, I gripped a dagger in my hand and got out of the car. Sara, sensing my mood, also got out of the car with a stiff face. In her hand was a rapier specialized for stabbing.

,Um, Yujin-nim. If it's a dangerous place, wouldn't it be better to request support from the Association?"

Sara asked, carefully watching my expression. She must have sensed the ominous atmosphere here.

,No. We can handle it ourselves."

,......"

For the first time, Sara looked at me as if I were a child. She seemed to think it was a childish remark. Knowing how stubborn I am, Sara gave up trying to persuade me, gripped her rapier again, and stood in front of me.

,But is there something in that hotel? What did you see with your Spirit Eyes?"

,Can't you tell? Don't you feel the wind?"

,Now that you mention it, the wind is unusually strong here."

,All the wind in Chicago is heading towards the hotel."

She became serious. If what I said was true, it meant that an easily dismissible anomaly was occurring in Chicago.

,Is it something related to the Wind Spirit?"

,That's right."

I briefly explained the current situation to Sara. Sara's face grew increasingly grim as she listened to me.

,Drawing mana with the wind to artificially create a dragon vein and corrupting the Spirit... This is no ordinary matter. I think it would be better to retreat and report to the Association."

,I'm telling you, we can handle it ourselves."

I walked towards the hotel. Sara, with her phone in hand, followed me in a fluster. She must have sent a message to the Association after all.

,Ignoring my wishes? I'll have to punish her with my dick later.'

Well, whatever.

It's not like the Association's heroes will appear immediately upon contact. I should be able to kill that bastard in about 10 minutes.

,First, I'll tear down the barrier.'

I had no intention of entering the barrier. I haven't fully grasped it yet, and I don't know what tricks he might have played with the barrier since then. It's better to eliminate his advantageous space early on.

,Moka."

,Kkuuuk!"

Summoned, Moka materialized and soared into the sky. Her body was already pure white, but it stood out even more against the dark sky.

I channeled mana into Moka through the connection. Pajijijik. Lightning crackled from Moka's body.

,Strike.'

A thick bolt of lightning, enhanced by Moka's power, struck towards the hotel. More precisely, at the barrier overlapping the hotel.

The hotel walls shattered, and part of the barrier was torn apart by the lightning.

Umjil.

Sara trembled. A chilling cold emanated from the torn part of the barrier, sending shivers down her spine.

,Yujin-nim! This is...!"

,...Was once not enough? Moka. One more time."

I couldn't finish my sentence and closed my mouth. That bastard came out directly from the torn barrier. An Asian man with black hair. He glanced around with cold eyes behind his glasses, then looked at us.

,Are you all there is?"

,I know what you're doing here. Surrender. Kneel there and put your hands above your head."

Sara glared at the man, pointing her rapier at him.

,Not the police, so the Association? How absurd. Just two. And one of them is a kid. You're far from enough to stop me."

,Whether we're enough or not remains to be seen."

Kiiiiiiing.

With a sharp sound, a cluster of light gathered on the blade of Sara's rapier. Sara repeatedly thrust her rapier in the direction of the man. Light was emitted from the rapier, flying towards the man.

The man took out a fan from his pocket, unfolded it, and waved it. A black barrier appeared, blocking all the light thrusts. It was as if darkness was devouring light.

,Insignificant."

The man folded the fan and swung it. Darkness stretched out from the end of the fan, turning into a whip and falling towards Sara.

,Evade it!"

Sara shouted. It was unnecessary worry. I had already finished preparing to dodge from the moment he folded the fan. The black whip struck the ground. Sara dodged it on her own.

,Too weak to be A-class, about B-class level similar to Sara.'

That's why the man's arrogance was strange. The fact that he could show such an attitude meant that he had something to believe in.

,Is it that fan? No. It seems like a somewhat special item, but it's just a weapon to use. Could the Wind Spirit have already been corrupted?'

Sara, after confirming that I was far away, attacked more aggressively. Slashes of light flew towards the man. The man unfolded his fan. The whip disappeared, and the barrier protected him.

,I roughly understand the ability of that fan.'

The battle was disadvantageous for Sara. Sara's ranged attacks were ineffective, and when she tried to approach, the whip's characteristics were displayed. It attacked a very wide range quickly, making it difficult to discern its trajectory.

,He's keeping Sara in check while also glancing at me.'

He probably roughly realized that I'm a Spirit Master.

,Moka. Lightning.'

I gave the order to Moka and pointed my index finger at the bastard, gesturing rudely. Pajijijijik. Blue lightning gathered at the tip of my index finger.

Moka and I shot lightning at the same time.

It was already too late to dodge. Sara had effectively restricted his movements. The man unfolded his fan towards the sky. He chose to block the lightning falling from the sky rather than my attack. He judged that the Spirit's attack was more dangerous.

,That's the right judgment. Moka's lightning, infused with the Spirit's power, is stronger than my lightning.'

But that doesn't mean that the lightning I shoot is negligible.

,Kuaaaaaaaak!"

The bastard, struck by the lightning I shot, screamed and knelt on the ground. White steam rose from his body.

,I should cancel my assessment of him as B-class. This bastard is C-class.'

If he were B-class, he wouldn't have knelt down from just one lightning strike. I approached the bastard with a dagger held in reverse grip. I was planning to slit his throat.

,...Who are you?"

The bastard looked straight at me and asked.

,Who are you then."

I am Wang Shi-en from Guiluogu (鬼落谷).

,What?!"

My steps, which had been approaching him, stopped.

Guiluogu.

It's a force located somewhere in China. It also appears in the original work and is considered one of the most dangerous villain groups in the world. Even the Gumokgyo can't take Guiluogu lightly.

,Guiluogu?! Oh my god! Why are you guys here?!"

Sara shouted desperately. She seemed to have lost her reason. It was understandable. There's a saying that Guiluogu is like a plague.

But Wang Shi-en didn't give her any attention and only glared at me.

,Those golden eyes are Spirit Eyes."

,You recognized it just by the color of my eyes?"

,Special eyes possessed by Spirit Masters. Only a fool wouldn't recognize them. I know what you are."

I didn't waver. Just because my identity was exposed doesn't mean Guiluogu can do anything to me right now. It's the same when I return to Korea. Guiluogu will be busy strengthening themselves, and they can't disregard Seong Hari and the Jinryeong Seongga (眞靈成家) supporting me.

,I envy you."

Wang Shi-en raised his hand and adjusted his glasses.

,Without these glasses, I'm just a half-wit who can't even see Spirits or Specters. But you're not. You can see not only Spirits but everything else. I envy you. I really envy your talent."

,...!!"

An ominous aura emanated from him. I was startled and stepped back. It was an aura too strong for me to handle.

,Yujin-nim!"

Sara jumped in front of me and raised her rapier.

Wang Shi-en looked at us, then took out a golden bell from his pocket. He grabbed the bell and shook it.

Ttang. A clear bell sound rang out. I was momentarily distracted by the sound, which was too pure to know what his intentions were.

,Come out. Come out and kill them all."

Corpses emerged from the barrier behind Wang Shi-en. With pale skin and gleaming whites of their eyes, they dashed in all directions. The number exceeded one hundred.

,Zombies... No, are they Jiangshi?'

Some of them rushed towards us. Sara blocked in front of me, facing four Jiangshi at the same time. They didn't seem like ordinary Jiangshi, but she, who used light, had good compatibility with them. I avoided the Jiangshi and stood quietly behind her. The Jiangshi's physical abilities were higher than what I could handle.

And above all, there's one bastard I need to kill.

,Wang Shi-en! That bastard is running away! Moka!'

Moka chased after Wang Shi-en, who was riding on the back of a Jiangshi. The Jiangshi was clearly running on two legs, but somehow it was leaving the place faster than a motorcycle.

,Moka, body slam.'

Moka spread her wings wide. Pajijijik. Lightning wrapped around Moka's body.

Moka flew towards Wang Shi-en like a fired cannonball. Just before colliding with Moka, Wang Shi-en threw his fan. The fan caused a black explosion, tearing off one of Moka's wings.

,Damn it. I should have killed him earlier, even if it meant overdoing it.'

I summoned Moka.

,Kkuuk kkuuk...."

Moka groaned. I fed Moka a Spirit Stone that I had prepared for emergencies. Moka's torn wing quickly recovered. Still, it seemed difficult for her to move right away.

,Yujin-nim! Are you hurt anywhere?"

Sara, who had dealt with the four Jiangshi, knelt on the ground and examined my body.

,I'm fine. More than that, don't you have to notify the Association?"

,Ah...."

Her face turned pale, and she hurriedly took out her phone. Nearly 100 Jiangshi had spread throughout Chicago. For the time being, Chicago would become hell.

,More than that, what happened to the Wind Spirit?'

I left Sara, who was passionately talking on the phone, alone and walked into the barrier. A chilling cold that made my body tremble washed over me. I glanced at the talismans attached to the wall and walked towards the center of the barrier.

The Wind Spirit was still bound to the barrier. Unlike when I saw it before, the Spirit's body was not unstable. Instead, black things were holding onto the Spirit's form.

,It's almost completely corrupted. What should I do?'

Even if I smashed the barrier and released it, it didn't seem like it would return to its original state. Rather, it would just be like releasing this bastard outside.

,Would killing it raise my good karma?'

I don't know since it's not 100% completely corrupted.

After pondering for a moment, I took out a Spirit Stone from my pocket. Maybe I could restore the Wind Spirit with the Spirit Stone.

Kaaaaaaaak!

The Wind Spirit's reaction to seeing the Spirit Stone became violent.

,So you want the Spirit Stone too. Here, I'll give you one. Eat it."

I threw the Spirit Stone. Then, something like a black mouth appeared and swallowed the Spirit Stone.

I watched the Wind Spirit with anticipation.

Wind began to blow fiercely around the Wind Spirit. I raised my hand to cover my face while watching the Wind Spirit. The Wind Spirit grew larger and larger.

,It's not returning to its original state, but rather...'

Rather, it's becoming completely corrupted. The talismans inside the barrier were blown away by the wind and torn apart. The barrier was gradually collapsing. Feeling the danger, I retreated from the barrier. I had no way to kill that Wind Spirit right now.

,Yujin-nim! What, what's going on?!"

,The corrupted Wind Spirit is starting to break free."

I looked at the hotel with a tense face. In the worst case, I might have to summon Yuria here.

Kwakwakwang!

The barrier shattered, the old hotel was destroyed, and the Wind Spirit emerged. No, since the Spirit was corrupted, it would be more accurate to call it a Demon Spirit.

The Demon Spirit revealed its form above the destroyed hotel. It was a form resembling a small black beast, and its entire body was constantly swirling.

,Oh my god...."

Sara was horrified. The power felt from the Demon Spirit was extraordinary. She raised her rapier in front of me, but her body was trembling.

The Demon Spirit looked at me. But it didn't speak or rush at me. The Demon Spirit soared into the sky.

-I'll kill you! Wang Shi-en!!

The Demon Spirit flew through the sky of Chicago, searching for Wang Shi-en.
