,Shouldn't we just wipe out the enemy like we've always done?"

We have the larger population. We hold the overwhelming advantage.

Arel unfolded a world map, drawing attention. The world map was far more detailed than the last time I saw it. Half the continent was under the control of the United States of America. The territory alone was enough to show that the United States of America was an overwhelmingly powerful nation.

,Your Excellency. This simple map suggests that the Soviets have two options: advance west or advance east. But in reality, the Soviets only have the option to advance west. The path to the east is blocked by a massive mountain range."

I opened the screen and checked the mountain range. As she said, a massive mountain range blocked the path to the east. Not only were several mountains in the way, but blizzards were raging. It would be insane to try and advance through that.

,I understand. Crossing that mountain range would be insane. They'd probably be annihilated before they even made it halfway."

,Yes. Since the land route is blocked, they'd have to go by sea. But we don't have suitable ships."

There were no ships big and sturdy enough to cross the continent.

,Right now, the United States of America has a small population compared to its vast territory. It has abundant resources, but it's not even developing them, let alone utilizing them. Now is the time for the United States of America to postpone war and focus on development for the future. Because the future will be the era of the United States of America."

,Isn't the International Coalition dangerous?"

,If we don't start a war, the International Coalition will be the one feeling the pressure. They won't send troops while taking losses. Even if they do send troops, the numbers will be limited. There's no way the United States of America will lose. The best they can do is impose a trade embargo."

,What impact will a trade embargo have on us?"

The corners of Arel's lips quirked up slightly before returning to normal. It was a sneer directed at the International Coalition.

,None. The United States of America has all the necessary resources, including manpower."

I stared at the world map.

The United States of America is a superpower that controls half the world. The other half is controlled by the International Coalition. And the International Coalition is not a single nation but an alliance of five nations. In terms of potential, no nation could match the United States of America.

The best option for the United States of America is to hunker down and wait for the future. Hunkering down wasn't particularly difficult either. I grinned.

If they want to do something, they have to do it before then. But all they can do is raid coastal villages through piracy. Even that wouldn't be easy if the United States of America decided to defend properly.

The island nation of Great Britain could have sucked all the sweet juice from behind the scenes.
