I found a corpse while walking down the street.

It was a middle-aged man. His solar plexus was red. Whether it was a knife or a spear, something had pierced his chest.

Just by looking at the corpse, it was hard to tell if it was a zombie or a human. I glanced at the corpse and passed by. Yang Subin flinched at the sight of the corpse but remained calm.

,Aren't you used to corpses?

,...I'm not used to them. I'm used to patients, though.

Before long, I found another corpse. The corpse was an ajumma. Her skull was dented.

,This is a human corpse.

,...What makes you so sure?

,Zombies don't die just because their heads are a little dented. They're already brain-dead, so do you think they'll die from brain damage?

,...You're describing zombies as living beings.

,They're not undead like in fantasy novels. These guys are just infected and turned into zombies. Their brains are dead, but their hearts are still beating, and they instinctively attack and devour humans... no, living things. Even if you somehow survive these guys, if you get bitten by a zombie, you'll just become another zombie. There are no exceptions.

Players and Awakeners are no exception either. If you get bitten by a zombie, you're screwed and become a zombie. However, it was possible to prevent zombification with a special vaccine.

,I've been wondering for the past few hours... how did you know this was going to happen?

,Since you're bored, should I tell you? It's something that will be known eventually anyway.

I told her about Players and Awakeners. She couldn't easily believe what I said. But she couldn't completely ignore it either. I didn't bother trying to convince her. She'll eventually understand that I'm right.

,If zombies are living creatures... that's a relief.

,What is?

,Living things eventually die if they don't eat. If people just hide in buildings and wait... the zombies will eventually starve to death.

,You're wrong. These guys eat other zombies if they're extremely hungry. And it doesn't end with just eating them. If they eat enough living things, they evolve. The tricky thing is that you can't know how they'll evolve.

,Evolve. There's no way they can do that so easily....

,The virus from outer space makes it possible. If you're lucky, you might evolve too.

Jjaenggeurang!

I heard the sound of glass breaking. The zombies that were wandering nearby heard the sound and ran like crazy. I looked towards the sound. It was a large mart that you'd find in any neighborhood. People were gathered there. There's no smoke without fire. It seemed like a fight had broken out among the people inside the mart.

,Save me!! Aaaaaaaah!

,Shut up! If you're going to die, die alone! Sibal!! The zombies are coming!!

The large mart was in chaos. I stopped and watched the scene. Zombies were chotbab to me, but not to ordinary people. The people attacked the zombies with whatever weapons they could find, but the zombies were superior in terms of physicality. If you're going to fight zombies, you have to use your head, but the situation was too chaotic to do that.

The people's screams only increased.

,...Aren't you going to help?

,Why would I?

,......,

Na Chaeyeong told me to bring people, but I didn't feel the need to bring the guys who caused trouble at the mart to Seongak Elementary School.

,If I take them, won't they just get cocky? They need to suffer a little to know how great the prepared shelter is.

Kyaaaaaaak!

A young woman in the mart screamed. I reflexively checked the woman's appearance. She was a pig nyeon with only a decent voice.

,Keukeu. Byungshin-deul.

,...The chaos is only temporary. There are many reservists in Korea. It will be sorted out after a little time passes.

,We'll see.

The mart eventually turned into a zombie's human flesh party. Blood splattered, but the entrails weren't scattered. The entrails and flesh all went into the monster's mouth.

I was about to pass by the mart with Yang Subin. Until I saw a zombie with a snout sticking out like an animal.

,I have to take care of it before we go.

,...Huh? Suddenly?

,Look at that snout-faced bastard over there.

,...What is that?

,A variant. It's starting to evolve. It looks like it'll be a pain in the ass if we leave it alone, so I have to get rid of it before we go.

It's said to nip it in the bud. I wouldn't have known if I didn't see it. But since it's right in front of me, I have to take care of it before I go. I pulled out my sword from my waist and walked towards the mart.

•••

I looked at the building that was falling apart. It was a building with a parking lot, and zombies were loitering around.

,Contact Park Haegil.

,Yes.

Park Haegil answered the phone before the first ring even ended. I snatched her smartphone as if stealing it.

,I'm here. What floor did you say it was?

-It's the 3rd floor. There are five zombies wandering around in the hallway. After experimenting in various ways, I think we can distract them for a moment. The problem is the parking lot. There are too many zombies. We have to go through the parking lot to get out of here... but it's impossible right now. It would have been possible if the zombies were as weak as the zombies in dramas... But where are you?

,Parking lot entrance. Can't you see me from there?

-Please wait a moment. It's on the far left of the building, so the viewing angle is... Ah, I see you. Two people?! Don't tell me only two people came?!

,Two people are enough.

I deliberately didn't contact Park Haegil on the way here. Because I knew he would nag me with useless things. I took out a grenade from my bag and handed it to Yang Subin.

,I don't think the zombies will come all the way here... If it's dangerous, use the grenade. You see that wall over there? Climb up there. Zombies are stupid, so you'll be safe if you're out of their reach.

,P, please be careful.

-What are you trying to do alone?!

,Shut up and answer my question. Did you say there were two injured people?

-...There are 8 of us in total. One has fractures in both legs, and one is unconscious after hitting his head against the wall while fighting a zombie.

,So it's not so urgent that you need a doctor right away. Get ready to leave. I'll clear the parking lot first.

-Did you really come alone? No matter what, you can't do it alone.

I hung up and headed to the parking lot. The zombies heard my footsteps and rushed at me. I also ran forward and swung my cutlass. The silver blade smoothly cut through the zombies' upper bodies.

Tududuk.

Blood splattered on my body. I didn't care. The dangerous thing is not getting the zombie's bodily fluids inside my body.

,I do have recovery, but there's no guarantee that it will prevent zombification. I have to be as careful as possible.

I rely on my senses to swing my sword so that blood doesn't splatter on my body as much as possible. It was a skill I naturally learned from killing so many people.

Seogeok!

The zombie's head flies off. I realized that and went, "Ah, shit." I was going to cut its upper body, but I unconsciously cut off its head out of habit. The zombie's hand tightly grabs my shoulder. Its fingers try to dig in but stop. The clothes I'm wearing are special clothes that are hard to penetrate even with bullets.

,This is skill.

I kicked the headless zombie's abdomen. The zombie flew backward and collapsed with a thud. Another zombie popped out as if it had been waiting.

Chwaaaaaak!

Red blood splattered coolly in the direction I swung my sword. After clearing all the zombies in the parking lot in 5 minutes, I entered the building. I sliced through zombies from the 1st floor to the 3rd floor.

On the 3rd floor, burly men were waiting for me. In the center of them was a man who exuded the aura of a leader. It was Park Haegil. He was a man with a short buzz cut. With his broad shoulders, he looked just like a soldier.

,Seong Yujin-ssi. I am Park Haegil, the president of PS Security. I saw you taking care of the zombies in the parking lot. Have you trained in kendo? No, before that, where did you get that sword?

,Why? Are you jealous?

,To be honest, I am jealous. If I had that sword, I would have been able to defeat the zombies more easily. But I don't have the confidence to wield the sword as well as Seong Yujin-ssi, so I'll give up.

,Are you ready? Let's go.

,There may still be survivors in the building. Wouldn't you like to take a look around?

,Are you sure there are survivors?

,...I don't know.

,I don't think there are any survivors.

It was the moment I was about to turn around. I saw a lump falling from above through the window.

Kung!

I quickly approached the window and looked down at the parking lot.

It was a muscle monster over 2m tall. The Hulk-like bastard had fallen into the parking lot and was eating the zombies I had killed. It was a variant. The bastard, who was tearing apart zombie corpses indiscriminately, slyly raised his head and looked at Yang Subin. The muscle monster grinned. It had recognized Yang Subin as food.

,Damn it.

I ran towards the window. I broke the window with my body and fell while tightly gripping my cutlass. Was it because of the sound of the window breaking? The monster raised its head and looked at me. The muscle monster gripped its large arm and took a stance to swing at me. Then, as the distance closed, the monster threw a punch.

Pajijik.

Electricity sparked from my whole body.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Biraeshin (Flying Thunder God).

As if something was pulling me, my body moved sideways in the air. The muscle monster's huge fist narrowly missed. I didn't miss the opportunity. I slashed the bastard from head to toe.

,Keugeugeugeugeugeuk!!

The monster screamed and swung its arm at me. I hurriedly stepped back.

,It doesn't have a heart? Is it on the right side instead of the left?

It's not strange since there are people whose hearts are on the right side. I was about to fix my sword and give strength to my legs to charge when it happened. The monster grabbed a car nearby and threw it at me.

I didn't stop running. I lowered my upper body sharply and lowered my sword.

Kakakakakakak!

The sword scraped against the asphalt and caused sparks. The car flew over my head. The muscle monster stretched out both hands towards me.

I avoided the large hands and passed by the monster's side. I ignored friction and inertia with Biraeshin and sharply turned my direction to take the monster's rear. The monster hurriedly tried to turn its body. It was too late. Before its body could completely turn, my sword was falling on its shoulder.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Noegwang (雷光).

The silver sword became a blue lightning flash and diagonally cut through the monster's upper body. The cut upper body slid down and fell to the floor. As expected, the heart was on the right side of its chest.
