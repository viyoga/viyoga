After a little time passed, the rotational force naturally disappeared.

I hurriedly swam and climbed out of the lake. The lake, which had been so transparently blue, was now contaminated with the Imoogi's blood and flesh. I didn't want to stay there even for another second.

,Nari!! You're amazing! Nari!!"

Okto hopped up and down in place, making a fuss. It wasn't admiration coming from the heart. It was acting to survive. I quite liked it. After all, flattery is acting. Okto knew his place pretty well.

"Imoogi. It wasn't a big deal."

"Aren't you going to take the Imoogi's corpse? Nari."

I turned my head. The messed-up Imoogi's corpse hadn't disappeared. That corpse was the reward.

"Too much trouble."

"They say Imoogi meat tastes excellent and is good for spirits."

"Is this you, you goddamn rabbit bastard, ignoring my virility?!"

"No! No! Nari!!"

Okto said urgently. I ignored the talking rabbit's nonsense and plopped down nearby.

'I was a little tempted by the idea of it being good for virility...'

I was too tired to move.

I was exhausted from using the power of the Rotation Dragon.

And this wouldn't be the only reward.

'It's a hidden dungeon. There's something here. I can't just catch the Imoogi and go back. Okto is good at finding paths, so if I use him well...'

A notification window popped up in front of me. Fortunately, it seemed I wouldn't need to put Okto in front and move to find the hidden rewards.

「The essence of Wolsan fills Moon Lake.」

「The energy suppressed by the Imoogi is released.」

「The barrier from the outside world is lifted.」

「The Rabbit Merchant reveals himself.」

The space cracked open, and a rabbit with a large backpack popped out.

The rabbit looked around, then looked back and forth between me and Okto.

"Wow. There was a fellow of my kind here. But you're not my customer. Human-nim over there. Nice to meet you, I'm the Rabbit Merchant, Roto!"

At this point, there's no way to not know what the reward for this hidden area is. The reward for this hidden area is using the Rabbit Merchant's shop.

'Not bad.'

I grumbled for no reason, but couldn't stop the corners of my mouth from lifting. It wasn't just "not bad," it was lucky. Special merchants you meet in hidden areas like this sell special items. If you meet one, you've hit the jackpot. Of course, only if you have a lot of AP.

"Lotto."

"It's Roto!"

"Yeah. Lotto. What kind of stuff do you sell? If you sell trash, I might be disappointed and kill you."

"Y-you're a scary person. But don't worry! I'm a merchant who sells items that are perfect for you, customer-nim!"

"How is that possible?"

"I'm a subcontractor of System-nim!"

I understood immediately.

'That means he's directly connected to the System. He's top-tier among special merchants. I hit the Lotto.'

I desperately tried to stop the corners of my mouth from lifting.

"Can I meet you here again if I come next time?"

"No. I'm a special merchant who only comes out a few times a year. You can't meet me unless you're lucky. You meeting me means you're lucky. It's not easy to meet me with ordinary luck! Hahaha!"

The Rabbit Merchant laughed cheerfully.

That meant my Luck stat had an effect. My Luck was a bit high.

I approached Lotto. If the System was behind the Rabbit Merchant, killing him and taking his stuff was impossible.

"Show me the goods first."

"Here they are."

Lotto wiggled his short feet, twitching his rabbit ears.

A notification window popped up in front of me. The items I could buy from the Rabbit Merchant were lined up in a row.

「Blood-Soaked Robe (S) - 800,000 AP

Demon Summoning Scroll (SS) - 2,000,000 AP

Lightning Dash (C) - 150,000 AP

Pretense (A) - 1,780,000 AP

Map with the Location of an Alchemist's Elixir (B) - 400,000 AP

Demon King's Fate (SSS) - 66,666,666 AP

.......」

Expensive items and skills popped up in a row. All of these items were related to me in one way or another. In the case of the Blood-Soaked Robe (S) at the top, it added a bonus when using Demonic martial art.

'S-rank is by no means a low grade.'

Ordinary Exiles would light up their eyes and rush in if they had the chance to get an A-rank item or skill.

'But is this helpful to me?'

I was already receiving a bonus for Cheonma Shingong.

That's what the trait Cheonma Physique (S) and the skill Cheonma Shingong (SSS) were for.

'Will this robe create as much synergy with Cheonma Shingong as Cheonma Physique does?'

Of course not. Cheonma Physique was a trait for Cheonma Shingong. And the robe and Cheonma Physique had different directions.

'My Cheonma Shingong has reached its limit. Even if I get an additional bonus from the robe's effect, will anything change significantly?'

It's not like getting a bonus would raise it to EX rank.

I passed on the Blood-Soaked Robe (S). It didn't appeal to me much.

'Since it's blood-soaked, it'll probably smell like blood.'

Next, what caught my eye was an SSS-rank trait.

Demon King's Fate (SSS) - 66,666,666 AP.

It was a trait that increased all stats. In addition, there was an explanation that you could become a Demon King.

'The explanation is short and vague compared to the rank. This is definitely a jackpot.'

I wanted to buy it, but I didn't have enough AP. I didn't have nearly enough.

「Current AP: 12,410,197」

'I have a lot of AP... but Demon King's Fate (SSS) is too damn expensive.'

There wouldn't be many humans who could buy that. I shook off my lingering feelings and checked out the other items.

While quickly scanning the items, a unique item caught my eye.

「Black Tortoise's Condom (A) - 50,000 AP」

There was a condom. And it was an A-rank condom.

「Black Tortoise's Condom

Wearing the condom grants extreme defense.

Rank: A」

"What kind of bullshit item is this?"

As I muttered in disbelief, the Rabbit Merchant's eyes sparkled.

"Ah, speaking of that product, just wearing the condom gives you defense comparable to the Black Tortoise! The downside is that it's disposable, but the advantage is that it can withstand most attacks! It can probably even withstand the Azure Dragon's breath!"

"I will never wear a condom."

「Rope of Sensuality (S) - 1,500,000 AP

Cleansing Liquid (A) - 50,000 AP

Succubus's Whip (A) - 700,000 AP

Rabbit Fur Restraints (B) - 120,000 AP

Rabbit Ears of Heat (SS) - 2,500,000 AP

.......」

Even the names were unusual. The Rabbit Merchant Lotto, who saw the list, widened his eyes. Lotto took a step back for no reason, slightly increasing the distance.

"T-this list isn't something I decide! The System sells these products tailored to you, customer-nim!"

"The System knows me well."

I slowly looked through the adult items.

There wasn't a single one I didn't like.

I especially liked two of them. One was the Rope of Sensuality (S). It could adjust the sensitivity of the restrained target from 2 to 100.

'And the Rabbit Ears of Heat.'

「Rabbit Ears of Heat.

Wearing the rabbit ears causes heat.

Rank: SS」

A weak effect for an SS rank. That's what you might think.

'The System sets the standards for the ranks. If the System gave it a high rank of SS, there must be a reason.'

And it wasn't difficult to guess the reason.

'Think of it as a kind of curse. Just because you put a curse on someone doesn't mean it will always work. It's affected by the target's resistance, mental strength, and the effects of special items.'

The fact that this item with a simple effect is SS rank means that it works even if the opponent is incredibly strong.

"I'll buy it."

"Oh? R-really? It's not a very helpful effect in battle..."

"I said I'll buy it."

"Thank you!"

I continued my shopping spree.

"Customer-nim! Here's a rabbit tail that goes well with the rabbit ears! Of course, we also have a bunny girl outfit!"

"Nari!! I'll make the bunny girl outfit...! I'll make it for you! Just find me a model! I'll make a masterpiece...! If I have enough AP, I can make something of better quality than the bunny girl outfit sold here!"

Two rabbits were shouting loudly. I ignored them and focused on shopping.

「Steel Rabbit Tail.

Wearing the rabbit tail increases physical durability.

Rank: S」

'The price isn't as high as I thought. I should buy it.'

I put the bunny girl outfit on hold. It was only B rank.

'I would have bought it if it was at least A rank.'

My eyes, which had been raised by seeing luxury goods, didn't come down easily.

The frenzy of shopping continued. The items I bought from Lotto were gradually piling up next to me.

「Current AP: 2,250,197」

Spending ten million AP was instantaneous.

I spent AP only on adult items that weren't directly helpful in battle. It was a satisfying shopping experience.

'I have several districts under my control right now. AP will be earned over time.'

Originally, the basic thing to do was to invest the earned AP in the district to maximize profits... but I didn't feel the need to do that.

'Let's see.... I've bought almost all the adult items.'

I found an interesting item at the end of the list.

「Moon Mirror.

You can see places where moonlight touches.

You can collect moonlight.

Rank: S」

Moon Mirror.

A treasure that Hang Ah possesses. The price is a whopping 2 million AP.

"You sell interesting things."

"It's the perfect item for voyeurism."

"What does it mean to collect moonlight? Do you collect moonlight and shoot it as a laser?"

"Of course, that's possible too. Moonlight is energy."

"So it has the ability to accumulate energy. That energy can be used in various ways?"

"Yes. Yes. The energy depends on how the user uses it."

Of course, the Moon Mirror (S) sold in the store is fake. Not only the Moon Mirror, but all items related to gods are fake. Compared to the original, its effects are significantly reduced.

"Interesting. I'll buy it."

"Thank you!"

The Moon Mirror appeared.

It was a round mirror. The size was similar to a watermelon. I was reflected in the Moon Mirror. I focused on the mirror. Then my appearance distorted and something else was visible.

It was Elena.

Elena, sitting in a chair, was sitting arrogantly with her legs crossed and her chin resting on her hand. In front of her, five men and women were sweating and watching her. Elena was looking through the documents on the table one by one.
