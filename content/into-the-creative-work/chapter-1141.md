,Mocha."

Pajijijijik.

The high-grade spirit Mocha spread her wings and appeared before me.

A large amount of mana drained away in an instant, and Mocha materialized.

,Kkuuuk."

Mocha spread her wings. Dozens of bundles of lightning flashed from her sparkling wings and flew towards the skeleton.

The skeleton staggered from the lightning strikes. Holes appeared all over the skeleton. But that was only for a moment. The curse gathered again on the skeleton. The skeleton's damaged areas regenerated.

I frowned.

,Aside from not being able to kill it in one shot with Mocha's firepower... the problem is that damn massive curse."

,Yujin. We need to find and eliminate the medium of the curse."

Shin Nari-sunbaenim said. I understood her words immediately. It meant we had to find Kim Jeo, who was hiding somewhere in this academy.

,Sunbaenim, do you know where that bastard is?"

,I don't know, but... Imo might know."

There was a good chance. The evil spirit would be more sensitive to the curse than the living Nana and Shin Nari-sunbaenim.

The energy emanating from the skeleton, which had finished regenerating, was ominous. It opened its mouth wide and spewed out a curse. Mocha stepped forward and blocked the curse that was crashing in like a wave.

,Kkuuuk...."

She couldn't hold out for long. That was Mocha's will conveyed to me. The curse that Mocha shed touched my body. My skin became rough.

,Even if I summon Makina, there won't be a good solution... I guess I have no choice but to do that."

I turned my head sharply and looked at Shin Nari-sunbaenim and Kales. Shin Nari-sunbaenim was enduring the curse even better than Mocha, and Kales was frowning behind her. Kales, whose contract with the high-grade spirit of darkness had been severed, was not compatible with that skeleton in his current state.

,Nari-sunbaenim. Can you block that thing for a moment? Five seconds is enough."

,I can do it."

,Please."

Shin Nari-sunbaenim released the curse that had been accumulated in the rope I had given her. For a moment, that curse seemed to overwhelm the skeleton's curse and attack it in reverse, but it began to be pushed back. That was because the skeleton's curse was almost infinite, like an ocean.

,Mocha. It's been a while since Spirit Possession."

,Kkuk!"

Mocha flew over and permeated my body. My body changed. My hair and skin turned snow-white, and wings of lightning formed on my back. It was a strange sensation, but I knew in my head what I had to do.

I kicked off the ground and jumped up. I accelerated by flapping my thunder wings. The skeleton reached out its white hand to me. I kicked the skeleton's large hand with my foot. The shattered hand scattered in all directions. I approached while avoiding the skeleton's swinging other hand.

Kwaak. I clenched my fist tightly. Pajujeujeujeut. Electricity gathered and clumped in my fist. I slammed my fist into the skeleton's head.

The skeleton shattered like glass falling on the floor. The curse gathered to regenerate the skeleton again. I continued to swing my fist at the curse.

Pajijijik, pajijijijik!

The electricity generated from my fist burned the curse. The curse, which had been endless like an ocean, seemed to give up on the skeleton and disappeared without a trace.

I landed on the floor and released Spirit Possession. Spirit Possession is all good, but it consumes too much stamina.

,...You possessed a spirit. I've never heard of such spirit magic. Is it a secret art of the Jinryeong Seongga?"

,That's right. Please keep it a secret if possible."

Heelkkeut.

,Sunbaenim. What do you see?"

,As the curse is leaving this space... Imo got swept away."

Come to think of it, the evil spirit that had been attached to Shin Nari-sunbaenim was nowhere to be seen. I used my Spirit Eye to examine the flow of the curse leaving this place. I could see it. The problem was that there wasn't just one or two flows of the curse.

,Sunbaenim. Do you know where the evil spirit is going?"

,Yeah. I can feel Imo's presence. It wasn't like this before... but now I feel like I'm deeply connected to Imo."

It was the evil spirit that was always attached to Shin Nari-sunbaenim. It didn't make sense that it was suddenly swept away by the flow of the curse.

,Maybe it moved to the source of the curse. Sunbaenim. Please guide me to where the evil spirit is."

,Okay."

Shin Nari-sunbaenim ran down the right hallway without hesitation. And she threw open the door at the end of the hallway that led outside and went out.

,W, what is this...?!"

,........"

Kales was shocked. I narrowed my eyes and looked around.

The sky was dyed red, and black hands were planted in the ground like flowers. The hands wriggled and struggled to grab something.

,The academy's barrier has been changed by the curse...? This is absurd. The academy's barrier is one of the best in South Korea...! No matter how rampant the curse is, there's no way the barrier could be corrupted. ...Could it be. Someone else...."

,Kales-sensei. It's already happened. We can figure out the cause later... let's chase after Shin Nari-sunbaenim first."

,Ah...? Na, Nari-ya! You have to come with us!"

Shin Nari-sunbaenim, who had been running ahead, slowed down. Kales and I followed behind her, looking around. As Kales said, the barrier was corrupted, so things looked distorted.

Teachers fighting tree monsters could be seen a little ways away. I deliberately made a sound, but the teachers couldn't see us. Kales also couldn't recognize the teachers.

,It must be distorted by the corrupted barrier. The reason I can see them is because of my Spirit Eye."

There was no need to help the teachers.

They were teachers at the academy. There were differences depending on their major, but their combat power was at least that of a B-class hero or higher.

Shin Nari-sunbaenim's legs didn't stop running. Anxiousness and impatience could be seen on her face, which was always expressionless and calm. She might lose the evil spirit. That ominous thought was making Shin Nari-sunbaenim anxious. The evil spirit was her family, after all.

The place Shin Nari-sunbaenim arrived at was a garbage dump. Its size was extraordinary. Garbage was scattered everywhere. The stench stung my nose. If you looked closely, there were also monster corpses and such.

This place was a garbage dump and an incinerator at the same time.

Once a month, magic students incinerated the garbage with magic under the pretext of practice. Magic students looked forward to the incineration day. Why? Because burning garbage with magic relieved stress. It was legal arson, legal destruction.

,Something... is creepy."

Kales said. It wasn't just his imagination. The curse was pooling in this garbage dump. The pooled curse was overflowing and spreading throughout the academy. In other words, this was the source of the curse.

,Kim Jeo... that bastard. He was hiding in the garbage dump. Hiding in a place like this... he's got a good stomach."

,What.... Kim Jeo is hiding here? Where is he?"

,I don't know the exact location yet... but I'm sure he's here. Think about it. There's no better place to hide than here, right?"

,That's true. There are no managers in the garbage dump, and it's not a place that people frequent unless it's incineration day. Even if someone comes, it's hard to find him if he's buried in the garbage...."

Kales was convinced.

Shin Nari-sunbaenim looked at the air. The evil spirit swayed and flew over, clinging to Shin Nari-sunbaenim's back. My and Shin Nari-sunbaenim's gazes were directed at one place that the evil spirit was pointing to.

I drew up mana and stepped on Jingak. The garbage soared into the air and flew away. When the garbage disappeared, Kim Jeo, who was lying on the floor hiding, could be seen.

Kim Jeo's condition was not good at a glance. His seaweed-like hair was dry, and his body was as thin as a scarecrow. Red spots were on the skin exposed outside his clothes. Strange objects were lined up around Kim Jeo. They were the charms that Kim Jeo had stolen.

,Let's kill him."

Pajijijik.

,Kim Jeo is dead. No, he's dead, but... he's alive."

Shin Nari-sunbaenim said.

I roughly understood what that contradictory statement meant. It meant that he had become a being similar to an evil spirit due to the curse. It wasn't strange since this was a world where the existence of souls had been proven.

Kim Jeo got up.

Kkaakkkaakkkaak.

Absorb the curse, strengthen it, and attack. It was simple, but it was fatal to Kim Jeo.
