People gathered in a spacious room within the Yun family's estate.

Representatives from the five families of the Ma clan, excluding the Cheon family, were all present. They waited for Baekran, seated at the head of the table, to speak. Their hearts were rotting with anxiety and unease, but none dared to step forward and take responsibility.

"......"

Baekran continued to remain silent. Glances were exchanged among the family representatives.

Their opponent was a mere woman. They didn't know her real age, but she was alone. Her power was estimated to be around the peak of the Fifth Realm. Everyone gathered here were powerhouses of the Fifth Realm. If they all joined forces, they could kill that bitch.

Baekran closed her eyes, as if deep in thought, touching her forehead.

It was an opportunity. The representatives moved their lips, sending each other telepathic messages.

-What do you think about killing her here? Even if she's a First-Class Imperial Guard, if the body disappears, even the Imperial family won't notice.

-Are you out of your mind? What idiot would commit such an act alone without any preparation? That bitch must have prepared something.

-What if the word gets to the Emperor that we killed a First-Class Imperial Guard? Do you think we can get away with it? The Emperor himself will move! It won't end with the destruction of our families!

-The moment the Emperor decides, we're done for! The Emperor is not human!

-Goddamn it. Then what are we supposed to do?

-We must respond calmly, especially in times like these. What is the purpose of that bitch summoning us all here?

-What is she trying to gain by pressuring us?

-What about asking the Shin-gyo for help?

-Don't you know why the leader is holed up? It happened after he had an audience with the Emperor! The Cheonma has yielded to the Emperor!

-The Shin-gyo probably already knows. All of us have moved; there's no way the Shin-gyo wouldn't know. This is Shin-gyo territory.

-Then why aren't they helping us?

-Because they're afraid. No matter how powerful the Shin-gyo is, it's just a sandcastle in front of the Emperor. We are grains of sand. We must never turn the Emperor into an enemy.

"His Imperial Majesty is merciful. Even when some ruffian pointed a sword at him in the past, or when a foolish old man insulted him, he did not lash out."

Baekran opened her mouth. The Ma clan representatives, who had been busily exchanging telepathic messages, focused their attention on her words. They had all heard what Baekran had said at some point. The current number one under the heavens, the greatest of all time, the Emperor, did not use his immense power recklessly.

"However, there are beings that His Imperial Majesty can never tolerate. Those who twist the natural order, those who desire a new heaven to open, the underworlders."

There was no one present who didn't know what an underworlder was. There were things one naturally learned upon gaining a certain level of power, and that was information about beings from outside this world. Of course, they didn't know the details. One thing was certain: they were beings whose presence in this world was of no benefit.

"Our primary mission as First-Class Imperial Guards is to investigate and deal with such beings. I was investigating the Reverse Demon Divine Art. I came across the Yuun Trading Company and learned that the owner of the Yuun Trading Company was offering sacrifices to an underworlder in exchange for elixirs and magical artifacts."

"That was something the Yun family did on their own!"

"The investigation is already complete. Didn't you receive elixirs from the Yun family?"

"It was a legitimate transaction! We didn't know the Yun family was trading with the Yuun Trading Company!"

"The previous destination of the Yuun Trading Company was Jae-yeom City, where the Jin family is located."

The head of the Jin family was startled. He tried to make excuses but quickly shut his mouth upon seeing Baekran's clear eyes.

Baekran picked up a brush from the desk. She moved the brush across the pure white paper. Their gazes were drawn to Baekran's brush. It was as elegant and graceful as a crane. Then, their gazes moved further down. The expressions on their faces turned pale in an instant upon seeing the ink on the white paper.

(To His Imperial Majesty. It has been confirmed that the five families, including the Yun family, have joined hands with the Yuun Trading Company, a minion of the underworld. The evil Reverse Demon Divine Art, which turns humans into monsters, belongs to the Little Cheonma of the Cheonma Shin-gyo)

"Wait! Wait! Please wait a moment!"

The five representatives were horrified. The brush that had been moving gracefully stopped. Baekran looked at them.

"Do you have something to say to me?"

"Imperial Guard-nim, you're mistaken!"

One representative shouted. If that letter reached the Imperial family, they were done for. The other family representatives desperately defended themselves. They moved their mouths. Their excuses gradually turned into pleas. Baekran silently listened to their words.

As they regained some composure, the contents of the letter caught their eyes. The Little Cheonma, mentioned out of the blue.

'Little Cheonma? Why the Little Cheonma...?'

'Yuun of the Yuun Trading Company has the same name as the Little Cheonma, but... can that be considered evidence that it's related to the Little Cheonma?'

'What if the real purpose that bitch is aiming for is the Little Cheonma...?'

They weren't stupid. Their minds raced.

Little Cheonma Cheon Yuun. If he dies, will the Cheonma Shin-gyo collapse? It won't. Rather, it could be an opportunity for the other five families.

'If one masters the Cheonma Shingong, they can become the Little Cheonma.'

'The current Cheonma is holed up... There's no reason why a new Little Cheonma can't emerge from our family.'

'Even if it's not our family, as long as it's not a direct descendant of the treacherous Cheon family....'

One of them mentioned the Little Cheonma.

"Little Cheonma! The Yuun Trading Company is definitely related to the Little Cheonma!"

"Hooh. I only have suspicions... Why do you think so?"

Baekran, who had only been listening until now, reacted for the first time. The family representatives were convinced.

'The real purpose was the Little Cheonma!'

'What did the Little Cheonma do to turn a First-Class Imperial Guard into an enemy?'

'I don't know the details, but this is an opportunity.'

The representatives competed to denounce the Little Cheonma and express their suspicions. Baekran responded to them and naturally led the conversation, explaining the plan.

"There is someone suitable to be the Little Cheonma. If you support him... he will be able to solidify his position as the Little Cheonma. What do you think? He is deeply loyal, so he will surely reward your support. And so will I."

"We cannot leave the position of Little Cheonma vacant for long. We will support him."

The representatives of the five families said with awkward smiles. They had been led around by the nose since the beginning of the meeting, and they had no choice.

• • •

Having secured the cooperation of the five families, Baekran leaned back in her chair and closed her eyes.

Distant memories surfaced.

Memories from before her regression.

Before her regression, she was not a First-Class Imperial Guard. She had not become the adopted son of a eunuch and entered the Imperial family.

She didn't know her parents, but she remembered the master who had raised her. Her master was an assassin. On the surface, she was an ordinary old woman, but she was an assassin who had assassinated countless martial artists. The old woman had no alias. That was how thoroughly she had concealed herself. She was also an assassin who handled the dirty work of the Imperial family.

'Ran-a. Live as you wish.'

Those were the words she had said when Baekran became the adopted son of a eunuch. And those were the last words her past life had uttered.

She had been killed by a Martial Alliance warrior. It wasn't during a mission. Before her regression, the old her was retired and had unfortunately gotten involved with a drunken Martial Alliance warrior. The cause was her beauty. After a series of twisted events, her master was killed by the Martial Alliance warrior.

To take revenge on the Martial Alliance, she joined the Cheonma Shin-gyo, the Martial Alliance's greatest enemy. The skills she had learned from her master were helpful. As an assassin of the Cheonma Shin-gyo, she killed enemies. Before her regression, she was the dagger of Little Cheonma Cheon Yuun. That was why she could calculate Cheon Yuun's actions.

'Cheon Yuun will become the Cheonma. Now, when he hasn't solidified his position as the Little Cheonma... is the best time to kill him.'

Little Cheonma Cheon Yuun would become the Cheonma decades later. Before her regression, she was Cheon Yuun's loyal assassin, assassinating all sorts of enemies. Cheon Yuun was generous to those who were loyal to him. Whether it was martial arts or elixirs, he provided solid support.

'Then, by chance.'

It was truly by chance. Following Cheon Yuun's orders, she entered the Cheonma Mausoleum to kill his cousin.

'I discovered the Cheonma Shingong.'

She felt a strong connection to the Cheonma Shingong. As an assassin who desperately craved martial arts even while assassinating enemies, she couldn't resist the temptation and laid her hands on the Cheonma Shingong.

And she mastered the Cheonma Shingong. Of course, she hid that fact from Cheon Yuun.

'I never thought he would have the ability to recognize someone who had mastered the same Cheonma Shingong.'

It was like a side effect of the Cheonma Shingong. Because of that, the moment she faced Cheon Yuun, she was exposed for having mastered the Cheonma Shingong.

Her relationship with Cheon Yuun immediately broke down. She ran away. The assassin skills she had mastered were a great help.

Baekran stroked her left cheek. The scar she had received from Cheon Yuun while escaping. The cheek where the scar should have been was warm and smooth.

'All my subordinates died.'

And in the end, Baekran killed Cheon Yuun and became the Cheonma, reigning over the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

'I already took revenge with my own hands before the regression. But I have no intention of letting you live, Cheon Yuun.'

The grudges from before her regression still remained, and Cheon Yuun was only a part of the revenge she had to take.

'There are many bastards to kill.'

She would not involve her subordinates from before her regression. This was entirely her own revenge. The fact that she didn't trust them was also one of the reasons.

'Yeom Guseok.... No, his real name was Seong Yujin, wasn't it.'

An existence that didn't exist before her regression.

A variable.

A martial artist born with the aura of a star, like the Emperor.

'His growth rate is fast. Almost on par with me, who has regressed. He has enough value to be used.'

She thought of her revenge targets. She couldn't face ordinary people. There were existences that were too much for her to handle alone. She needed at least a powerhouse at her level, a comrade who could become as strong as her.

'It doesn't necessarily have to be a comrade.'

A relationship where she used him, and he used her. That was enough. So, she would accumulate debts. He wants to become the Cheonma? She would let him become the Cheonma. Because what she needed now wasn't a fancy position.

She sighed. Hot breath went out, and cold air came in.

'Only revenge.'

She repeated the determination she had made when she died before her regression.
