No matter how much I tortured Paul, I couldn't get the information I wanted. The information he knew about Alphatier Pharmaceuticals was only very basic. Basic information that anyone could find anywhere.

,It's certain his memories were erased by some method. There's no trace of magic, so it must be science or drugs.'

Alphatier is a pharmaceutical company. It wouldn't be strange if they had a drug that could erase memories. A drug that erases only specific memories? Alphatier can do it, even if no one else can.

Alphatier handles not only drugs made with science and technology, but also magical potions made with magical ingredients. You can think of them as handling all substances that affect living things under the name of drugs. A drug that erases specific memories? Officially, it doesn't exist, but it wouldn't be strange if they were making and using it unofficially.

,I have to get my memories back.'

Magic to restore memories. Or technology to restore memories. A drug to restore memories.

,If there's a spear that can pierce anything, there's bound to be a shield that can block anything. There must be technology related to memory recovery. Humanity has developed that way.'

Click!

I tap on the laptop and search the internet.

It goes without saying, but it's hard to expect to get high-level information from the internet. The internet is basically censored in every country.

,But there are loopholes, and what I want is a small clue. If I dig deep into that small clue, I can get some meaningful information.'

If all else fails, I'll commission a company that specializes in information. This has the disadvantage of costing a lot of money and the information here can leak out in reverse, but you can easily get high-level information.

,...Nothing. Even if I search through the list of magic potions, there's nothing related to memory. At least there are some things related to memory among the magic ingredients, but it's not what I want. Should I look through the papers too?'

I click my tongue.

I've looked at papers related to magic before. The reliability of the papers was lower than I thought. Most of them were papers that seemed familiar, and among them, there was hardly one out of a thousand papers that had useful content. Most of them are not open to the public and are controlled. For military reasons, or for corporate secrets.

Knock knock knock

I raised my head at the knock. I turned off the laptop. I didn't get an answer.

,Warden-nim. This is Deputy Warden Kevin, Captain.'

,...Come in.'

Kevin comes in and salutes. Seeing his disciplined posture, I inwardly clicked my tongue. This bastard doesn't get intimidated even if I deliberately bully him. He's strong-willed... or rather, he believes in something else. It was certain that he had someone backing him up.

,What's going on?'

,I heard you're looking for information on memory recovery recently.'

,How did you know?'

,Rumors are circulating among the guards. Is your memory not intact?'

I asked a few guards just in case, and it seems like the rumors have already spread. It didn't matter. As long as rumors about Paul and Alphatier don't spread.

,It's a personal matter. If you're going to talk nonsense, go out and work.'

,I've heard that the Imperial Army has a magic potion that restores memories.'

,That's not nonsense. Tell me more.'

,There are spies who have their memories erased and are deployed. This is to prevent spies from being deprived of information when they are caught. I've heard that the Imperial Army developed a magic potion to restore erased memories in order to extract information from spies.'

If it were another country, I would have shouted nonsense. If they had made such a drug, Alphatier Pharmaceuticals would have already taken it over and sold it at a high price, or they would have used it only for their own corporate profits.

It made sense.

The only places where Alphatier Pharmaceuticals can't exert its power are city-states like Neo London and Divine France, and the Heist Empire. In particular, the Heist Empire is a place where even megacorps can't easily enter. Even if they do enter, they have no choice but to be aware of the military. The Heist Empire is Kaiser's militaristic dictatorship.

It was a country where companies and everything else would be blown away if Kaiser didn't like it.

,Can you get that drug?'

,I wouldn't have brought it up if I couldn't get it. I can get it if I mobilize all the connections I have. But...'

,But what?'

,It's designated as a special strategic material. It costs a lot of money to get it. There are many people watching in the prison, so we have to trade in a secret place. I think you'll need about 200 million credits.'

I'm suspicious.

Kevin is a guy who is hostile to me. A guy who is constantly aiming for my position. A guy who wouldn't be strange if he pointed a gun at me right now.

That kind of guy is suddenly trying to help me? I can only think that he has an ulterior motive.

,200 million credits. It's expensive, but it must be worth that much.'

,Of course. It's something you can't get even if you have money. 200 million credits is not expensive.'

,Okay. Proceed.'

,Understood!'

It's hard to believe that Kevin, who is only the deputy warden of Lhumen Prison, has such abilities. He's probably trying to use his backing.

,If it's a trap, I won't be able to get it. But it doesn't matter. It's not a bad idea to find out who's backing this guy and kill him on this occasion.'

200 million credits.

I can afford it with the budget embezzled from the prison. If I feel it's not enough, I can squeeze the prisoners.

,As expected of vicious gang members, they have quite a bit of hidden wealth.'

There were some prisoners who were babbling that they could live a life without envy if they were released from prison.

,I should let them know that they might die before they get out.'

The fear I planted in the prisoners is also fading. Recently, the criminals, like goldfish, are acting up because I've been quiet.

,I'll have to set a date and execute some of them so they can't act up. Criminals seem to cherish only their families... so I'll catch and execute all of their families.'

It didn't matter if they rioted. Then I can execute them for rioting.

,Someday, they won't even be able to think about standing up to me.'

Before that, I need to find a way to save the budget. That way I can embezzle more of the budget.

,I should get my hands on food. I've reduced it to two meals a day... but there are still a lot of prisoners, so it costs quite a bit of money. Hmm. I think I heard that they make livestock feed from food waste... Can't I adjust it so that humans can eat it? I feel like I'm getting some magical inspiration... I'll put magic on the food waste to keep it fresh... No, wait. Isn't it more efficient to modify the human body than food waste? If I modify the stomach with magic, I can easily digest food waste.'

Magical inspiration is flooding in.

,I killed the summoner Gillian and got the Chimera Research Book and the Summoning Magic Book. Should I practice human sacrifice magic on this occasion?'

It's all for the development of my magic.

I got up from the chair and tilted my head, loosening my neck. Ududuk! Ttuduk! The sound of bones echoes. My shoulders feel heavy for some reason.

,Have I been overdoing it lately? I feel tired.'

Business management and magic engineering factory management in Neo London, Lhumen Prison management, personal magic research, etc. There were so many things to do.

,If I get rid of Kevin and put Ance in the position of deputy warden, things will be a little easier. Let's just be patient until then.'

I massaged my shoulders and went out of the warden's office.

• • •

A week later, Kevin said to me.

,The transaction location has been decided. We've decided to trade in the wasteland area near the prison.'

,That wasteland 30 minutes away?'

,Yes. It's the perfect place for a transaction because there's nothing there. Are you ready with the money?'

,What do you think of me? Of course, I'm ready.'

,I'll tell them we'll trade. The time is... after lunch tomorrow. How is it?'

,Not bad.'

The next day came.

I had lunch at a restaurant in Neo London and came to Lhumen Prison, and I frowned slightly.

I didn't feel well today. I felt like the fatigue from working non-stop recently had exploded now.

,It reminds me of the old days. It was like this on the days I worked hard.'

It was the first time I had experienced such a terrible condition since reaching the 5th Circle.

The higher the Circle, the stronger the body naturally becomes. Since I'm a magician, my physical abilities don't become extremely strong, but I don't get sick easily.

,I should take a good rest for about two days after this is over.'

I went out of the prison.

Kevin prepared a car separately. It was a secret transaction, so I couldn't take my subordinates with me. Even if I didn't like it, I had to move with Kevin. I got into the back seat of the car with a money bag containing 200 million credits.

Kevin, who was sitting in the driver's seat, spoke in a calm voice.

,You don't look well. Are you okay?'

,I'm fine. Focus on driving.'

,I'll start.'

The car moves forward.

I used body scan magic on myself to check my condition. I suddenly thought that my poor condition was Kevin's doing.

,There's nothing wrong. It feels too bad to just say it's my mood. He wouldn't have poisoned me... What is it?'

I hadn't eaten anything at Lhumen Prison. There's no one to trust, guards or prisoners, so I can't eat food carelessly. I always ate food in Neo London. The same goes for drinks. When I was thirsty and drank water, I used magic. There was no way poison could have entered my body.

,...Was I too sensitive?'

No.

My senses were getting sharper and sharper. I was familiar with this feeling. When I was about to fight, when there were traps lurking, when assassins were trying to kill me, etc. It was just a hunch, but strangely, these kinds of hunches often came true.

,I heard that those who have experienced many dangerous things acquire it a posteriori, but I'm not that good, I was born with it.'

I admire my own talent and prepare for battle. So that I can deal with any surprise attack.

The car stopped in the center of the wasteland, at the agreed location.

Kevin got out first and opened the back door. I smelled the dust and looked at the man standing a little away.
