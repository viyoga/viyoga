I was in a car. There was a steering wheel in front of me. The car was clean and stylish. It looked like a recently purchased car, and it even had a navigation system. However, when I looked straight ahead, I saw an unfamiliar cityscape.

'......New York?'

New York is so often the setting for movies and dramas that I recognized it right away.

I examined myself.

I was wearing a black suit. I took out the items from my pockets and checked them. Smartphone, wallet, pistol, ID. I checked my smartphone first, and then my ID.

The ID was not the typical ID I knew. It was made of a special material and had numbers engraved on it.

'Mr. S, huh....'

It seemed I was a secret agent in this world.

'Hmm.... Should I go get something to eat first?'

I was about to start the car when someone walked over and sat in the passenger seat. I looked at the woman sitting in the passenger seat.

Wavy brown hair and a sexy figure. She was wearing a black suit like me, and it suited her so well.

Her skin was white, her eyes were brown, and her chest was full with an F cup.

I know her.

Miss J. Jenna Wiley. The female lead of 'Agent of Ople'. From the middle of the movie, she teams up with Mr. A, the male agent and protagonist.

"S. I brought a hamburger and cola."

"Ah. Thanks."

I took the hamburger Jenna threw at me and immediately unwrapped it. Looking to the side, Jenna was also unwrapping her hamburger and eating it. Because she was such a stunning beauty, even eating a hamburger looked beautiful.

I shoved the hamburger into my mouth and sucked on the cola.

In my head, I was thinking about how to bang Jenna.

The agents of Ople are all special people with physical abilities superior to ordinary people, but they probably aren't superior to me now.

It was a simple matter to overpower her and violate her right now.

'But that wouldn't be fun.'

And seducing Jenna didn't seem too difficult.

'In the movie, she kisses the male lead even when nothing happens. I guess I just need to overcome a few hardships and trials.'

"S, what are you doing! Move! Chase them!"

Jenna suddenly raised her voice. Looking straight ahead, a black van was moving busily. It seems the mission is to chase that car.

I sucked on the cola and stepped on the accelerator. The car shot forward.

"Too fast! Did you forget we're tailing them?"

"J, this is fine. But who are the guys we're chasing?"

"What?"

Jenna looked at me. It was a look that saw something strange.

"Just kidding. Just kidding. But it looks like those guys already noticed us? They're stepping on it way too much."

Jenna's head turned back to the front. She glared at the black van and held a pistol in her hand.

"Now that it's come to this, the best thing to do is to catch them and secure them. Don't let them get away, S."

"Leave it to me. I'll show you my amazing driving skills."

I stepped on the accelerator. I kept stepping on it.

The needle on the dashboard soon exceeded 200km/h.

"Kyaa! In front! Car!"

I leisurely turned the steering wheel and avoided it. I narrowly passed the side of the car. Horns blared from behind, but I ignored them. Jenna opened her mouth with a shocked expression. She unconsciously grabbed the handle on the ceiling.

"...S, I knew you were reckless, but I didn't know it would be this bad. Thanks to you, I'm going to get an earful from Cote!"

Cote was the direct superior of the agents. He hands down missions to the agents.

"Got them."

I pulled up close to the side of the black van and lowered the window. Through the window, I saw a skinhead man sitting in the passenger seat. He had a fierce face, his bald head was covered in tattoos, and he looked ferocious.

The guy also lowered the window and growled at me and Jenna.

"The fucking dogs of the country followed us well. Don't you value your lives?"

"Shut up and pull over, baldy. I'll prepare a cozy house with iron bars for you, so let's go nicely."

"Fuck You."

The guy took out a pistol. I immediately hit the brakes.

Tang! Tang! Tang!

Gunshots rang out. He was firing real bullets. The guy didn't stop and started shooting at us. I turned the steering wheel violently to avoid his attacks.

Glancing at Jenna in the passenger seat, she was ready to fire her pistol.

"What, we're allowed to shoot?"

"S, if we just leave those guys alone, the number of victims will increase exponentially. It's right to deal with them here. And did you forget that those guys aren't ordinary humans?"

"......Ah. They look exactly like humans, so I was confused for a moment."

Jenna looked at me as if she was dumbfounded, then stuck her gun out the window and started firing back. Her shooting skills were excellent.

The bullet Jenna fired burst one of the black van's rear tires. The black van shook and crashed into the bridge railing.

The guys in the van rushed out. Each of them holding a pistol, they started running away while shooting.

"Stop the car, S! They're getting away!"

"Okay."

Unlike the urgent Jenna, I leisurely stopped the car and got out. Then, I aimed my pistol at the guys and pulled the trigger.

Tang! Tang! Tang!

The bullets accurately hit the guys' legs. Every time I pulled the trigger, the guys fell, and in less than 10 seconds, I was able to subdue them all.

"At this rate... huh?"

The guys with pierced legs got up again unharmed. And they turned towards us. The guys' eyes had turned bright red.

As the guys ran towards us and aimed their guns, I pulled the trigger faster.

Five out of six people died instantly with bullet holes in their foreheads, and one person couldn't move with bullets in his arms and legs.

"S... that skill...."

Jenna was so surprised that she couldn't speak. It was too good to be the skill of an ordinary human. I didn't bother to answer and just smiled at her.

Afterwards, police cars and ambulances arrived noisily, and we went to headquarters with one guy alive.

At headquarters, I had to listen to nagging and scolding from my direct superior, called Cote.

Back in reality, I re-watched the 'Agent of Ople' movie and scraped together information and analysis about Agent of Ople from the internet. As it was a once-popular movie, there was a lot of information.

'This, this.... I see an easy way to go.'

I grinned and entered the Amusement World.

The agents of Ople basically work alone, but multiple agents are deployed for dangerous missions, and partners sometimes work together.

Me and Jenna weren't partners. We only worked together for this mission. In other words, if I do nothing and stay still, Jenna will work with 'Mr. A', the protagonist of the original work.

That's not what I wanted.

I waited for Jenna in the hallway of the headquarters. And when I saw Jenna coming out, I immediately approached her.

"Miss J, I want to talk."

"Talk? I don't know what you want to talk about, but I'm busy. I have to interrogate the guy."

Jenna didn't stop walking.

"It's about the Red Eye. I personally got my hands on a lot of information about those guys."

"...A lot of information?"

"The low-level guy who was caught won't open his mouth. You know how tough those bastards are, right?"

"......."

Jenna stopped walking. I took her to a place where there were no people, a cleaning supply room. The supply room smelled musty.

"Cote is in cahoots with those guys, the Red Eye."

"......!!"

Jenna's eyes widened.

Red Eye.

It's the evil organization in this world, and the guy I have to eliminate is the boss of this organization.

"...Lies. That can't be."

Unfortunately, what I said was true. It's revealed in the second half of the movie.

"It's true. If you look at Cote's personal computer, you'll know right away. And Cote is planning to kill the Red Eye grunt he captured soon. When that happens, you'll have no choice but to admit that Cote is a traitor."

"......If Cote is a traitor, why are you telling me this?"

"It will take time to report it to the higher-ups. And if Cote is caught, the Red Eye might stop their activities and go into hiding."

"......S, what do you want from me?"

Step, step. I heard someone passing by. Me and Jenna looked at each other in silence.

"J, be my partner. It's getting too much to move alone."

"I'm sorry, but I have no intention of becoming someone's partner. I'll bury the Cote betrayal... I have no intention of stealing someone else's credit."

Jenna turned around and tried to leave the supply room. I grabbed her shoulder.

"J, the story isn't over yet. I know one of the Red Eye's bases. It might be an opportunity to find the Red Eye's boss."

Jenna paused.

Jenna became an Agent of Ople because of the Red Eye. When she was young, her mother and father, who were police officers, were murdered by Red Eye members. She is chasing the Red Eye to avenge her parents, and her ultimate goal is to catch or kill the Red Eye's boss.

"...Where is it?"

"If I tell you now, you'll go alone. Let's go together. Be my partner."

After much deliberation, Jenna answered.

"Okay. I'll be your partner. But if your information is false, you'll be sorry."

"That won't happen. Ah, you know it's hard to go right now, right? And you shouldn't tell Cote. Because he's a traitor."

I grinned.

With this, Jenna will no longer be the protagonist's partner.

"An inspector has been dispatched from the higher-ups. Cote can't move recklessly. The opportunity has come, just like you said!"

As I had predicted, the Red Eye grunt we had caught recently died. It was ruled a suicide, but the higher-ups didn't believe it and dispatched an inspector.

In the movie, I wasn't there, but Jenna catching the Red Eye grunt was the same.

"It's our turn to move."
