Lee Chang-soo is a ghost.

I'm not talking about an evil spirit left with resentment after death. It's a kind of metaphor. He had no real traces. He had several identities, but none of them were his real identity.

Lee Chang-soo has no family.

His oldest memory was fighting with kids his age.

If he fought and won, he could eat a warm, delicious dinner, and if he lost, he had to eat tasteless, cold porridge.

Lee Chang-soo was of average build, but he had talent. He ate a warm dinner almost every day.

He grew up learning how to kill people.

Only later did he realize that he had been raised by Kim Jae-ji. He became a member of Sari, a special forces unit that only carried out Kim Jae-ji's orders.

An assignment came down.

It was an operation involving all members of Sari.

Lee Chang-soo learned today that there were a total of 37 Sari unit members.

They are participating in an operation that they must never fail. An operation that must succeed even if they die. It is an operation to kidnap Seong Yujin, the president of Joseon Korea.

Lee Chang-soo hid in the darkness and quietly recalled the details of the operation.

Seong Yujin returned from Jeju Island. He will arrive in Seoul in 15 minutes. He will land at a very large heliport near the Blue House. The transport helicopter is too big to land directly at the Blue House.

Seong Yujin will travel to the Blue House by car. As usual, the security personnel will be 2-3 drivers.

The distance between the Blue House and the heliport is close enough to be less than 10 minutes by car, but it feels like there are too few security personnel. This must be because he is confident in his own skills. If it were Kim Jae-ji, at least one unit would have joined the escort.

,My mission is to ambush and kidnap Seong Yujin, who is traveling to the Blue House, with all the unit members.'

He had never worked with all the unit members before. But he wasn't worried. They were experts who had received similar training. And the operation itself was not complicated and simple.

,The key is to neutralize Seong Yujin without killing him.'

You must never kill him.

-Get ready. Seong Yujin's car will appear soon. It's a top-of-the-line sedan made by a South Korean company. There's only one car running on this road at this time, so there's no chance of mistaking it.

He's talking too much unnecessarily. He knew it was a consideration to relieve tension.

Lee Chang-soo took out a pill from his pocket.

XT-2.

It's a drug made in China.

Taking it temporarily increases physical abilities and reduces pain. However, the next day, he would experience severe muscle pain and headaches that would require him to take painkillers, so he needed to be prepared when taking it.

There is a strange rumor that the ingredients of XT-2 are babies less than 100 days old. Lee Chang-soo didn't believe it. It's a practical reason. There's no way that such a small body of a newborn baby could have such useful ingredients. He wouldn't believe it even if adult men and women were used as ingredients. The reason was the same.

,They must have made it with a specific variant.'

Gulp.

He swallows the pill.

His whole body was overflowing with strength.

Awakener Lee Chang-soo took additional drugs.

Exceed.

Similarly, a drug made in China. The effect is a superpower booster. It temporarily enhances the abilities of the Awakened. The side effect is that the body becomes tired. The only thing is that you can see hallucinations if you take it often.

Lee Chang-soo shuddered. He could feel strength all over his body. He felt like he could do anything now.

-It's here.

A voice heard from the radio.

Lee Chang-soo looked at the road. A black luxury sedan was running without shaking.

Lee Chang-soo and the unit members moved. They ran onto the road and surrounded the car. They set up a barricade in the front.

The car stopped and people got out. Lee Chang-soo was a little surprised by this. Usually, they try to hold out in the car as much as possible.

It was Seong Yujin and three armed drivers. The drivers had pistols, and Seong Yujin looked around at the crowd once.

,Everyone's faces are black. You're skinny because you don't have enough to eat. Kim Jae-ji. Did that bastard tell you to do this?

Seong Yujin, the target, spoke as if he knew everything.

Lee Chang-soo and the unit members naturally remained silent. There was no reason to open their mouths unnecessarily and give information to the enemy. More than anything, there was no time to do so. They had to kidnap Seong Yujin before reinforcements arrived.

More than half of the unit members rushed at the drivers. Lee Chang-soo and the rest targeted Seong Yujin.

Tang! Tang! Tang!

Gunshots rang out. The drivers and unit members pulled the trigger at each other without hesitation.

The unit members targeting Seong Yujin rushed in with melee weapons instead of guns.

Pajijijik!

Blue lightning flashed from Seong Yujin's entire body. Seong Yujin stepped on the ground with all his might. Blue electricity flowed into the floor, trying to electrocute nearby unit members.

None of the unit members were affected. Knowing Seong Yujin's abilities in advance, they wore insulated uniforms.

,You've prepared thoroughly. Annoying.'

Seong Yujin swung a silver, shining super-vibrating blade, the Cutlass Blade, at the approaching unit members.

Seogeok!

The head of the unit member who was aiming for Seong Yujin's legs was cut off. It's not over. Every time Seong Yujin swung his sword, one person died.

Lee Chang-soo stopped.

This is not a difference in equipment. He could tell because he had been training for a long time. It's a difference in skill.

Even if Seong Yujin was holding an ordinary sword, the unit members would have been cut down.

The unit members knew it too. But they couldn't stop. They carry out the operation even if they die. That's how they were taught, and that's how they lived.

Lee Chang-soo was no exception. He just had different means than the unit members.

Lee Chang-soo focused on Seong Yujin's legs, who was killing the unit members. To be exact, it was Seong Yujin's shoes. He manipulates the shoes to prevent Seong Yujin from moving.

,Interesting trick.'

Seogeok!

Seong Yujin continued to kill the unit members. His lower body was not moving. Lee Chang-soo gritted his teeth. He tried to manipulate the sword Seong Yujin was holding. It was impossible. Seong Yujin's strength holding the sword was too strong.

,...That's not human strength. If he had tried to move his feet, he could have moved them. The reason he didn't... is he looking down on us...!'

Lee Chang-soo threw the military bayonet he was holding at Seong Yujin. The target was Seong Yujin's right arm. The military bayonet twisted its trajectory in the air. As if being wielded by an invisible force.

Pajijik!

The bayonet stopped in the air 30cm away from touching Seong Yujin's body. It was blocked because it could not penetrate the blue current.

Seong Yujin looked at Lee Chang-soo.

,Telekinesis? No. It's a lower ability. If it was unlimited telekinesis, he would have tried to burst my eyes. That would be more efficient.'

Seong Yujin speaks pretentiously.

Lee Chang-soo ignored Seong Yujin's words and focused his mind on the bayonet.

Drip.

Blood flowed from Lee Chang-soo's nose.

Shiver. The bayonet caught in the electricity was about to move. A stream of electricity flew through the air and hit Lee Chang-soo's face. It was a part that the insulated uniform could not cover.

Lee Chang-soo felt the pain of his entire body being torn and burned.

,......!

No scream came out. His body was electrocuted and he couldn't even make a sound.

The current that had entered once did not disappear immediately and ran through Lee Chang-soo's body.

When Lee Chang-soo came to his senses, Seong Yujin was standing in front of him. He was holding a black thorn like a tree branch in his hand.

,He's an Awakener. I wanted to experiment with him.'

Lee Chang-soo felt uneasy and looked around.

There were no unit members alive. Only the three drivers and Seong Yujin were standing. None of them were even fatally injured, let alone dead.

He misjudged from the beginning.

Their power has already surpassed the level of humans.

Puk.

Seong Yujin stabbed the black thorn into Lee Chang-soo's back, who had fallen. The black thorn turned white.

Lee Chang-soo felt something flowing into his body. It destroyed and recreated him. In the process, Lee Chang-soo felt a sense of awe towards someone. But he couldn't tell who it was.

Aside from the blurry vision, he could see very well. It's normal. He has five eyes, so he should be able to see better than when he had two.

,I'm hungry.'

He smells something.

A smell that stimulates the appetite.

He realizes that it's the smell of blood. But was the smell of blood this delicious?

,It's disposable, but the performance is certain.'

,...What did you do to me, what did you do?

,I made you a variant. The change happened in an instant. What's your name?

,A variant? I became a variant? I'm not a variant. Human. I'm human. My name is Lee... who was I?

What was my name?

I can't remember.

,Wow. I didn't know your name would be reset right away. Do you remember your childhood?

,.......

Childhood? I think I had one.

,Looking at your face, it seems like you don't remember that either. Do you remember Kim Jae-ji's orders?

,...Who is Kim Jae-ji? Is he the one who made me like this?

,What nonsense are you talking about? I'm the one who made you like this.'

,You're just food? Ah, I'm hungry. I need to eat you first.'

Lee Chang-soo, who had become a variant, used his ability. Of course, he had no memory of his ability. Of course, he only knew how to use it.

Objects around him floated into the air. The three drivers rushed like lightning and stabbed their swords into Lee Chang-soo's body.

,......!

Lee Chang-soo didn't feel much pain. But he couldn't use his strength properly. He instinctively realized that he had been killed instantly. The fear of death comes.

,He became a variant and his power exploded. Seeing him call him that person, is it a gene imprinting on the owner? Let's try it one more time.'

Seong Yujin used the power in the white thorn he was holding. The white thorn turns into a black thorn.

Lee Chang-soo trembled. The fear of death? No, it was fear of the being in front of him.

,Stop...!

The driver's sword was stuck in Lee Chang-soo's head.

Lee Chang-soo lost consciousness and then woke up. He remembered that he had become a variant and lost his memory.

,What the hell is going on...?

Puk!

Before he could understand the situation, the black thorn was stabbed into his heart. The black thorn turns white, and Lee Chang-soo turns into a variant with five eyes.

,He became a variant with the same appearance as before. Is there some kind of rule? Should I try it one more time?

Lee Chang-soo, who had become a variant, had lost his memories of his human days, but he was vividly feeling his instinctive fear.

,N, no, stooop...!

Of course, Seong Yujin didn't stop.
