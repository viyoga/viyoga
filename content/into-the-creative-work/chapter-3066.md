[03:27:51]

We'd pushed through the upper levels of Last Ark, installed modern weaponry, and stationed armed soldiers throughout. The soldiers looked at me and Daren with uneasy expressions, gauging our reactions. Judging by the looks on their faces, they wanted to kill me. The moment Daren gave the order, thousands of soldiers would attack me all at once.

'You think I didn't prepare for this? If something feels off, I'll just use Lightning Leap to escape. I've finished all the preparations for that. And other preparations too, for that matter.'

I walked around the upper levels of Last Ark, checking everything. There didn't seem to be any problems.

The preparations to face Origin were complete.

[Come On Baby

Summons the target before you.

Cost: 70,000 points

※Caution

You must know the target's face and name.]

[Come On Baby], the reward I'd obtained from completing the Gyeongseong 2033 quest.

'This will definitely let me kill Origin, right?'

I couldn't shake a lingering unease, but this was the best plan I could come up with.

[Using Come On Baby.]

[Please recall the target's face and name.]

'Origin.'

I knew the face and name from the original game, of course. I only knew the 3D graphics version, but that shouldn't be a problem. It wouldn't be much different from how it looked in reality.

[Target's existence confirmed.]

[Summoning target.]

There was no delay in the summoning. The moment the system notification ended, a massive lump of flesh over 10 meters tall appeared before my eyes.

At the center of the lumpy, crimson flesh was a giant red eye, and hundreds of thick, long tentacles protruded from its surface.

Before it could even assess the situation, Origin moved all hundreds of tentacles simultaneously. The thickest ones burrowed into the ceiling, floor, and walls to anchor itself. Then the remaining tentacles began attacking indiscriminately. It took less than 5 seconds after being summoned to react.

"What are you doing! Attack Origin right now!"

I shouted at the soldiers who were standing around dazed. The soldiers began fighting against Origin. The rockets and missiles I'd supplied poured toward Origin. Gunfire and explosions filled the interior of Last Ark.

Origin blocked the attacks with a telekinetic barrier. Its massive pupil turned toward me.

-Was it you? How did you summon me to this place?

Origin spoke with a voice materialized through psychic power. His voice reverberated through the air, booming and resonant.

'Cheonma Holy Sword, launch.'

A 6,000-ton sword shot forward. Origin didn't react. Compared to its size, the Cheonma Holy Sword would feel like nothing more than a toothpick.

KWADDEUDEUK!

The Cheonma Holy Sword shattered the telekinetic barrier and embedded itself in Origin's main body.

PWEEEEONG!

From the powerful physical impact, more than half of Origin's flesh burst apart, spraying a fountain of blood. The tentacles that had been flailing wildly and killing soldiers stopped moving.

"Origin is dead!"

"It's real... Origin, humanity's enemy, is dead...?!"

While the soldiers were overwhelmed with emotion and stopped pulling their triggers, I didn't let my guard down.

Origin's remaining flesh writhed and regenerated its form in an instant.

'An absurd level of regeneration. But it's not true immortality.'

Origin's regeneration is a psychic ability. One that devours mental energy at a monstrous rate. In other words, once its MP runs dry, it can't regenerate anymore.

I unsummoned the Cheonma Holy Sword and launched it again. Unlike before, Origin didn't ignore the Cheonma Holy Sword. It twisted the trajectory of the incoming sword with telekinesis. The fact that it didn't block it head-on told me something about its intelligence.

'...Just now, right before I opened the inventory, its gaze seemed to move. Did it actually react to that?'

I opened the inventory ejection port on the complete opposite side. I watched Origin's massive pupil shift ever so slightly.

'It can even detect the tiny spatial distortion from the inventory opening. At this rate, I have to assume it'll react 100% to spatial movement too.'

I should avoid using Leap as much as possible. Right after a spatial transfer, there's a high chance a tentacle would fly in and skewer my body.

[Crisis Detection activated. Remaining stacks: 7]

'Where?'

There was nothing nearby that posed a threat. Immediately after, recalling the attack pattern from the original game, I rolled to the side.

A tentacle erupted from the floor where I'd been standing. If I'd stayed still, I would have died for certain.

'It deceived my senses. No, rather—it was just too fast for me to react.'

My current physical abilities were at the level of an ordinary person. My reaction speed was inevitably far lower than it used to be.

'Origin is prioritizing killing me above all else.'

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 6]

I started running. Dozens of tentacles bent at right angles this way and that, trying to impale me.

'As long as it's not attacking stealthily like before, I can dodge.'

If I trusted my reaction speed and tried to dodge after seeing the tentacles fly in, it would be too late. Like dodging a bullet by watching the gun barrel, I had to predict the tentacles' movement and evade.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

I used electromagnetic waves to pinpoint the location of nearby tentacles. Even so, there were times I couldn't dodge the tentacles flying in from all directions.

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 5]

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 4]

In those moments, I used Flash to evade.

"What are you doing! Kill it quickly! If you can't kill it, all of you die too!"

I bellowed at the top of my lungs while dodging incoming tentacles. The soldiers gritted their teeth and fought more aggressively. Even if they hated taking orders from me, the soldiers knew that if Origin wasn't killed, humanity would ultimately perish.

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 3]

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 2]

'It's working. Origin's movements are getting slower.'

The problem was that the soldiers were dying too, and our firepower was dropping. The death toll among soldiers already looked to be over 2,000. At this rate, we might not be able to kill it.

'Should I force-detonate the timed bombs I secretly planted all over Last Ark? What if Origin protects itself with a telekinetic barrier? I might not be able to kill it.'

Only one method came to mind.

Self-destruction using the domain of the Hwaryeon Secret Blade. With my current stats, I can't utilize the domain at 100%, so self-destruction is the best option.

'One chance... I'll take it.'

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 1]

BZZZT.

I snatched the crackling lightning from my grasp and hurled it at Origin. It was blocked by Origin's telekinetic barrier, but some of the electricity traveled toward Origin's vicinity.

[Using Lightning Leap.]

I appeared right in front of Origin. Before Origin's telekinesis could push me away, I used my last Flash.

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 0]

I gripped the hilt of the summoned Hwaryeon Secret Blade.

'Domain Open.'

BZZZZZZZZT!

Along with crimson lightning, the domain burst forth from the blade and pushed back Origin's telekinesis.

Squelch.

I drove the sword into Origin's flesh.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Art (雷術) Blood Lightning (血雷).

I unleashed Blood Lightning using all the blood in my body.

KWAAAAAAAAANG!

Crimson lightning burst out, tearing my body apart, and swallowed Origin whole.

[You will automatically return to reality in 10 minutes.]

[You may view the ending.]

'Origin is dead too. Ten minutes left. Might as well fuck Vera one last time before I go.'

[Using Full Recovery.]

The moment my body returned, what I felt was an immense weight pressing down on me.

'Damn. Am I buried under Origin's corpse? It's not enough to crush me to death, but it's hard to get out on my own.'

It's fine.

I have an escape route prepared.

[Using Lightning Leap.]

I appeared deep underground in Last Ark, in the Market's room. The safest place in all of Last Ark.

"Heh heh. Vera. I came to hold you one last time."

I approached Vera, who sat in the chair like a puppet with its strings cut. I spread her legs with both hands and pulled her body into an embrace. That was the moment.

Squelch.

A blade embedded itself in my heart.

'Setting aside that Crisis Detection didn't trigger because I had no acceleration stacks—she deceived my senses even while I was using electromagnetic waves?'

Curiosity struck me stronger than pain or rage. I looked down and saw a folding knife clamped in her teeth.

My senses couldn't detect the blade.

"...A knife made of an insulator, huh."

Normally, I'd get suspicious the moment I felt a gap in my senses. But Vera had it hidden inside her mouth, so I let my guard down. I'm not the type to thoroughly suspect the inside of a woman's body, after all. And just moments ago, I'd been drunk on the feeling of victory.

"I figured out that the source of your ability is electrical manipulation. And you let your guard down in front of women."

"...I haven't handed over the final authority of Last Ark's main system to anyone yet."

"You never intended to hand it over in the first place. You never seriously planned to live in the future either. You have no lingering attachments. To you, this world is nothing. If everyone's going to die anyway... you, at least, I'll kill myself."

She's right.

In about 3 hours, the timed bombs I secretly planted all over Last Ark will detonate. There is no future for this world.

"Heh heh. You're the first woman to actually kill me. I'll remember you, Vera."

Strength drained from my body and my consciousness gradually faded.

Stab! Stab! Stab!

Vera kept stabbing my body with the knife, as if vomiting out her pent-up rage.

I mustered my last bit of strength and spoke to her.

"Log out."

Vera's expression twisted grotesquely.
