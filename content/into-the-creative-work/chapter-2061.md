I kept moving forward.

I thought I couldn't see the end, but I had already reached it.

A wide space. Debris of marble floors scattered everywhere. At the end of it, a being pierced by spears and swords.

『Demon Hunter (S) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

『Demon Hunter (S) discerns the demon.』

『Unable to obtain the demon's information.』

My whole body is overflowing with power.

Demon Hunter (S) wanted to kill the demon in front of me. I approached it.

Its jet-black body had cracks all over. The corners of its mouth were torn as if forming a gruesome smile. Blood flowed from the areas pierced by the sword and spear. However, only its eyes resembled those of a human.

Demon Hunter (S) didn't know the identity of this demon. That's because the demon in front of me was a very old, ancient demon.

,What a miserable sight."

,Keuheuheu. Even if I look like this, I'm at peace? Even if it looks painful, there's no pain. It's been so long that pain isn't even pain anymore."

,Are you pretending to be relaxed? I'm going to kill you now."

,The demons who threw me in here couldn't kill me. I was strong back then. But as time passed, I weakened. To the point where I could be killed by a mere human. Keuheuheuh."

I frowned. I didn't like the way this bastard was laughing so nonchalantly. I wanted to see him begging for his life.

,Since you're going to die anyway, at least beg for your life and entertain me. You never know. I might change my mind and spare you."

,A demon hunter sparing a demon? Haha. That's a funny joke."

,Don't you want to survive and take revenge?"

,Are you trying to tempt me? I don't know who the demon is. Revenge is fine. After all, isn't there an expert at killing demons right in front of me? Even if I die here, you won't stop and you'll kill those bastards. The scales are already tipping in your favor."

I don't like it.

That attitude, as if he had won even though he had lost.

Even if it's a coincidence, that I'm killing demons and acting as his proxy for revenge.

But I couldn't not kill this bastard, and I couldn't not kill the demons.

,What are you doing, you nemesis of demons? Kill me. Isn't there a demon in front of you? Ah, are you curious about my name? I am Apophis. The Demon of Truth. I see the truth, tell the truth, and pierce through the truth. Now, hurry up and kill me. Nemesis of demons. Or would you prefer to be called this? One who came from outside the world."

,......."

This bastard knew about me after all.

No, is it right to say he knows?

I narrowed my eyes and looked around. There was no one.

It was strange.

Yuria's and Elena's capabilities are by no means below mine. Especially Yuria, unless she doesn't want to, this kind of attack won't work on her. Because she has absolute mental strength. Elena might be more prone to mental breakdown, but she will eventually overcome it.

,Where are they?"

,Are you talking about those who came from outside like you? Their records are too insufficient, so they were sent to another space upon entry. But I probably won't be able to hold them for long."

Records.

The Demon of Truth doesn't just randomly scan my memories and judge the truth.

,Well, it's too subjective to judge the truth based on memories alone. People's memories can be distorted, and there's no guarantee that what a person directly saw is the truth."

I realized that Apophis's power was a bit more high-dimensional.

But instinctively, I knew that this bastard didn't know something.

I came from outside? That's right. But he didn't know exactly where I came from. If he knew, he wouldn't have spoken vaguely and would have shown off.

I recalled what I had seen coming here.

Everything was from the [Savior of the Academy] world. Events from other worlds didn't appear. The same goes for the first playthrough. Yuria and Elena went to another space because their records were insufficient?

The answer came out.

,You call yourself the Demon of Truth. You're not living up to your name at all."

,...Huh? Stop with the pointless provocation. I know you know much more truth."

,Then do you know about God?"

,Of course. They have already left this world. They moved to a world more suited to them. All that remains in this world are their traces. There are gods remaining in this world, but none of them are in a normal state."

,What about the god with nine eyes?"

,Are you talking about the Outer God who is on par with the Demon God? They basically have no interest in this world. Except for the Demon God."

,As expected of the Demon of Truth. You know a lot. So you must know the truth of this world too."

,The truth of this world? What are you talking about? That this world was created by the mercy of God? That this world has overcome the crisis of destruction seven times? You spoke so confidently, so I hope you don't disappoint me too much."

,This world is a creation."

,Are you trying to say it's a brain in a vat world? That it's a creation because it was made by the mercy of God? Don't try to fool me with such wordplay...."

The Demon of Truth couldn't finish his sentence.

One of the powers of the Demon of Truth is to discern lies from truth. He realized that what I just said was not a lie.

Apophis's composed body faltered. Confusion could be felt in his human-like eyes.

,This world is inside a creation? A creation made by God? Is that what you're trying to say?"

,Humans made it. To be exact, it can be called a company. Since the original is a game, there's a scenario writer, an illustrator, a programmer, capitalists, and so on... Everyone worked together to create a world for fun."

,......."

Thump. Clang.

Apophis moves his body. But it was impossible because the sword and spear were piercing through him.

,...If this world is the game you're talking about. How did you get into the game?"

,Because my ability is to enter creations. Novels, movies, dramas, comics, games. I can enter any world. This knife I'm holding, Hwaryeon Bido, was originally from reality. I asked a god in another creation called 'God's Atlantis' to strengthen it, and now it's like this."

,That's impossible. There's no way such an ability exists... no way!"

Is the Demon of Truth trying to deny the truth? What a joke. I smiled contentedly as I looked at him.

,Shall I tell you another truth? I'm in my second playthrough. In my first playthrough, I was called SSS-rank and played around in this world. If that bastard Meken hadn't backstabbed me at the end, this world would have been mine."

,...I can understand things I couldn't understand. If what you say is true, then surely.... But. But...!"

,Demon of Truth. Everything you know is a lie. How is it? Is the truth of the world you learned at the end beautiful and satisfying?"

,FUCK!!!"

Magical power flowed out from the Demon of Truth, sweeping in all directions. I held my sword forward and resisted the magical power.

,I know that ridiculous bullshit is the truth! Because when I look at your records, I can see that it's the truth! But why did you say it now? To make fun of me? To shake my mind? Did you degrade this world for such a reason!! Even if you hadn't said that truth... that truth wouldn't have existed! You have defined this world as a lie!"

,A lie? I didn't say a single word like that. This world is real. Even if it's a world inside a creation, it's real. Even though other ordinary guys feel like NPCs, some of them are my women who I wouldn't trade for anyone."

,......So that's it. This world becomes real because of you, not a fiction. Are you a god?"

,No, I'm not. If I were a god, would I be doing this? I would have taken care of all the annoying things a long time ago. Well, I might have used Absolute Authority if there was really no other option.... But it feels like it would be too futile to use it."

,......."

The Demon of Truth closed his mouth and closed his eyes.

He despaired. That was what I wanted. I chuckled and beheaded the Demon of Truth.

『Successfully hunted a demon. The effect of Demon Hunter (S) is enhanced.』

『The rank of Demon Hunter (S) rises to SS.』

『Demon Hunter (SS) obtains information about ancient demons.』

『Demon Hunter (SS) absorbs a portion of the power of the demon he killed.』

『Power of Truth

Rank: S+

Discerns the truth.

Finds out the truth.』

The rank of Demon Hunter rose and I also gained power.

The Power of Truth can be seen as a kind of lie detector.

Uuung.

The surrounding space is distorted. Apophis's corpse disappears and Elena and Yuria appear from the air.

,Being trapped isn't a very good feeling."

,I believed that Master would solve it."

It wasn't just them who appeared.

The three people from Guirak Valley also appeared, each collapsing or sitting down on the floor. The guys who thought they would be eaten and killed by the Demon of Truth didn't die and survived. The Demon of Truth died before they did.

,Yuria. Subdue them."

,Yes, Master."

Chwareureureureuk.

Shadow chains appeared and bound them. The bound guys still hadn't regained their senses.

,I don't know where we got caught."

Elena muttered. We used a space travel order to move. Because it's convenient. And because we can shake off any possible tail.

,Even if they're dying right now, they're not ordinary humans. There's no way these humans would have come here by chance. Shall we start by messing with their minds?"

Elena raised her staff. It was her specialty to mess with the target's mind and obtain information.

I raised my hand to stop her.

,I got a new power this time. Let me experiment."

To use the Power of Truth, I had to wake them up. I approached them and slapped their cheeks.

Slap! Ssaak! Slap!

Crisp sounds rang out in succession. I swung my palm repeatedly until they woke up.
