Kim Je-o, the professor of Joomul (Crafting) Department and representative teacher, sat in his office, clenching his fist. He had obtained some information from the students that morning.

The information was that some first-year students had attacked a contaminated area with Seong Hari. And at the center of it, as expected, was Seong Yujin.

,Suddenly went to the contaminated area with Seong Hari? That bastard must be planning something.'

And he's moving Seong Hari to warn me. To show that he has Seong Hari as his background.

,...Fucking bastard.'

The duel between the Joomul Department and the Spirit Department was less than a week away.

Kim Je-o was confident in the Joomul Department's victory. The Spirit Department had only one person, while the Joomul Department had seven. The numerical difference was seven times. Even if Seong Yujin was the best in the first year, the Joomul Department's characteristics allowed them to use external items within the rules of this duel.

The Joomul Department would bring carefully selected joomul (crafts) to use. Even if the opponent was an A-rank hero, they were confident they could overwhelm them with the power of the joomul. And the Joomul Department had Shin Nari. They had more than enough power. The Joomul Department's victory was certain.

That's what he thought. Until he heard Seong Hari's name.

,...What if Seong Hari intervened this time?'

Seong Hari.

One of Korea's Five Heavens, and Korea's only SS-class hero. She hadn't been active much in recent decades, but she was once recognized as the world's strongest hero. Even Academy Head Kang Ji-young was said to be a step... no, several steps below Seong Hari.

,What if Seong Hari's skills, connections, and items fell into Seong Yujin's hands?'

The Joomul Department might lose.

Kim Je-o nervously bit his nails.

,Mr. Kim Je-o. Are you there?"

The door opened without permission. Kim Je-o glared at the person who entered, his mouth tightly shut.

,What brings you here?"

,Don't be so cold. I've come to help you. I also find the existence of the Spirit Department annoying. I can help you."

He approached, carrying a suitcase in one hand.

,This is the Joomul Department's business."

,Are you going to say such disappointing things between us?"

,...The Joomul Department's power is enough."

,Why don't you take a look in the mirror? You look like the main character in a horror movie right now."

,....."

,If the Joomul Department loses this time, you'll lose a lot. The reputation of the already declining Joomul Department will fall to the ground, and the students will laugh at you. And you... you'll lose the Vice-Dean-nim's trust."

Vice-Dean-nim.

At those words, Kim Je-o's eyes widened.

,I know you. You're the kind of man who would shake hands with the devil to get what he wants."

,Are you saying you're the devil yourself?"

,Why are you saying such scary things? You know that what I said was just a metaphor."

He finally came to Kim Je-o's desk.

Kung.

The suitcase was placed on the desk. Mana vibrated from his palm. The magical lock was released, and the suitcase opened.

,......!!"

Kim Je-o's eyes widened like lanterns. A deep curse was felt from the opened suitcase. His eyes trembled.

,...They're all joomul (crafts)."

,As a teacher of the Joomul Department, you'd know. How amazing these things are."

,Even if you only count the money, it's worth tens of billions.... Where did you get them?"

,That's a secret. Anyway, with these, the duel will be no problem. I'll donate them to the Joomul Department. Use them as you see fit."

,...If we use these, the Academy might suspect us."

,Hahaha. Do you really think so?"

,....."

Kim Je-o's expression twisted.

He knew.

The Academy wasn't very interested in the Joomul Department. Maybe it was different in the past. Now, the Academy officials didn't accurately know what joomul the Joomul Department had. There were documents, but no one actually looked at them. Why? Because the Joomul Department was declining.

,What's at stake in this duel isn't just the future of the Joomul Department. Your future is also at stake."

,...I and the Joomul Department have nothing to give you in return."

,I only need one thing in return. The dissolution of the Spirit Department."

He turned around, leaving the suitcase behind.

,I'll be expecting it."

He disappeared.

Kim Je-o exhaled. His eyes flickered strangely. His hands reached for the suitcase as if possessed. A smile spread across his face as he checked the joomul one by one.

,I will definitely win and prove the value of the Joomul Department.'

•••

The day of the duel arrived.

I looked around before entering the artificial dungeon.

There were many spectators. Most of the Academy teachers attended, and even Academy students who had heard the rumors gathered. There were few second-year students. It was because it was the practical training season.

I calmly scanned the gathered first-year students. Most of them predicted that the Spirit Department would lose. No matter how good I am, it's impossible to face seven people alone. That was the reason.

,Lee Sieun is cheering for me... and even Ryu Hana came. This is unexpected.'

I just skipped over Kim Chun-woo and Ma Jin-bae. I'm not interested in guys like them.

Then my gaze stopped at Choi Da-yeon. I met Choi Da-yeon's eyes. Choi Da-yeon, with her arms crossed, was haughty. Even after what happened, she didn't avoid my gaze. That was very Choi Da-yeon-like.

I looked away. I looked at the seven members of the Joomul Department. Six of them, except for Shin Nari, were hostile. Shin Nari was calm. She was doing it because she had to. That was the feeling.

,I will explain the conditions for victory."

Kang Ji-young, the Head of Maruhan Academy, said.

,You win by either clearing the dungeon first or rendering the opponent unable to fight."

I stretched my shoulders as I listened to her words. The good thing about the Academy's artificial dungeon was that you wouldn't die even if you took fatal damage. So there was no need to hold back.

,First, Seong Yujin of the Spirit Department will enter, and the Joomul Department will enter 10 minutes later."

10 minutes.

The only advantage given to me.

,I don't really need it.'

I didn't have the slightest intention of winning by clearing the dungeon. I was going to defeat the seven members of the Joomul Department. That was my goal.

,Let's begin."

As soon as Kang Ji-young finished speaking, I entered the dungeon.

The scenery changed in an instant.

A dry sky and four hills greeted me. I crossed my arms and waited for the Joomul Department to enter.

Even after 10 minutes, the Joomul Department didn't appear.

,There are occasionally dungeons with strange time flows. But this is an artificial dungeon, so that shouldn't be the case....'

There was only one answer. Each starting point was different. I didn't know because the dungeon information wasn't disclosed in advance.

,Tch. That means I have to go find the Joomul Department bastards. This is annoying.'

I moved leisurely.

The Joomul Department's curses weren't suitable for fighting monsters. That's because the curses themselves were basically specialized for fighting people.

,Basically, dungeons require you to fight a large number of monsters. It's several times easier for the Joomul Department to fight me than to clear the dungeon.'

The Joomul Department bastards were the ones in a hurry, not me.

,I found you!! Seong Yujin!!"

A third-year student from the Joomul Department shouted. His voice was filled with hostility, but it only seemed ridiculous to me.

The third-year student had joomul (crafts) dangling all over his body. He had an old bamboo hat on his head, a black apple at his waist, and a broom with a black aura flowing from it in his hand. I aimed a rocket launcher at him and pulled the trigger. The rocket flew towards him.

A preemptive strike with a rocket without hesitation.

If it were a first-year student who hadn't gained much experience yet, they wouldn't be able to react and would be retired. But the opponent was a third-year student. He didn't just attend the Academy for show, and he created a barrier using magic. The rocket exploded, but it couldn't break the earth barrier.

,You brought a weapon like that into the dungeon? Are you kidding me?"

The third-year student growled. The Academy. No, heroes don't like modern weapons. They despise them. The reason was simple. The limitations of modern weapons and firearms were clear.

I ignored his words and loaded a new rocket into the launcher.

A smile naturally appeared on my face at the thought of feeling the hand taste of the RPG-7.

The rocket flew with smoke. The bastard didn't even think about dodging. He was trusting the barrier in front of him. That was his downfall.

Kwaaaang!

An explosive force three times stronger than a normal rocket occurred. The barrier was destroyed, and the bastard's body rolled across the ground.

,I asked my homeroom teacher, Yoon Hee-jung, for an enchantment.'

Originally, it wasn't allowed.

But it was an exception in this dungeon. It wasn't just the Joomul Department that could use items.

,Keueoeo, eoeok...."

I walked towards him step by step.

,Did you think that because the first attack was normal, the second attack would also be a normal rocket? You're so stupid that I thought it was a comedy."

The bastard's barrier suit was sparkling. It meant he was on the verge of being retired.

,Hmm?"

The bastard was bleeding. Originally, he shouldn't have been bleeding.

,...Did he deliberately adjust the barrier suit?"

Why?

The answer to that question came immediately.

,I got you...!!"

The bastard's old bamboo hat emitted an ominous aura.

I realized it as I looked at the bastard's smug face. It was a curse that was activated by the caster's pain and blood.

A sticky curse clung to my body. And then it disappeared.

Paseuseut!

The joomul (craft), the old bamboo hat, turned to dust and disappeared. It was the price for the curse failing.

,Wh, what?!"

I smiled and raised my wrist. A white rope was wrapped around my wrist.

,That item is...?!"

There were quite a few items that countered curses. One of the reasons why the Joomul Department was declining was because of that. Frankly speaking, even holy water alone could prevent low-level curses from taking effect.

,You guys were obviously going to bring dozens of joomul, did you think I wouldn't prepare anything?"

,Ev, even so, there must be a limit...!"

The bastard threw a black apple at me and swept the ground with a broom. Curses came crashing down. I calmly faced them. The curses couldn't even touch a hair on my head.

,No way.... What the hell is that item! Could it be... a holy object...?"

,You idiot. How would I have a holy object?"

I kicked the bastard's head. It was a waste to use a weapon. The bastard's body floated into the air and fell to the ground. His body disappeared. It was a retirement. The bastard would receive appropriate measures outside the dungeon.

,Six left.'

I checked the rope before moving.

『Rope of the Sacred Tree

Rank: S

Immune to curses.

Durability 43/50』

I widened my eyes.

Durability 7 was reduced at once.

,I only blocked three curses, but the durability disappeared in chunks!'

Was I seeing things wrong? I rubbed my eyes and checked the durability again. It was the same.

,...This is driving me crazy. The Joomul Department bastards brought dangerous items. If I had known this would happen, I wouldn't have acted so cocky and would have retired them right away.'

I regretted the reduced durability.

At the same time, a question arose.

,The Joomul Department had such dangerous joomul.... I underestimated the Joomul Department too much.'

I corrected my thought that Shin Nari was the only one to be careful of in the Joomul Department and moved to find the other bastards.

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

I opened both the Spirit Eye and the Heavenly Eye at the same time. My pupils turned golden. It wouldn't be difficult to find the Joomul Department bastards.
