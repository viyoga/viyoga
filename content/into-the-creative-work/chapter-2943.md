Wind emanates from the holes in Slatter’s Body.

At first glance, it seems like a physical ability rather than a Superpower, but it is the result of maximizing a Superpower.

Superpowers are not exclusive to humanity. Very occasionally, Superpower bugs appear in Gestag. The probability is much lower than for humans, making them extremely rare. So much so that the Queen Bug will Remodel the Body of a Superpower bug without question.

‘They say Superpowers are related to the Mind/Spirit. It’s almost impossible for Gestag, with their low intelligence and weak ego, to Awaken Superpowers.’

However, those who Awaken Superpowers and even undergo physical Remodeling surpass most other individuals.

「Confirming Slatter’s information.」

「Slatter’s first appearance: 22 years ago. Discovered on Planet Pallas.」

「Approximately 4,300 marines died during combat with Slatter.」

“He killed a lot, didn’t he?”

「Slatter’s combat record video is stored in the database. Would you like to view it?」

“No time for that.”

Peong!

The sound of wind bursting from the holes in Slatter’s back was heard, and simultaneously, it accelerated forward.

It was accelerating instantaneously by bursting a shockwave of wind.

I leaped onto the adjacent wall, dodging Slatter’s charge. Observing its abilities and Remodeled Body, I immediately predicted its combat style.

‘How many Ability Users and Monsters have I faced? I won’t be defeated by something like this.’

It’s common sense that a Monster with an unusual appearance possesses unusual abilities. This world is no different.

I fired my shotgun at Slatter.

Kwang!

Wind spewed from Slatter’s holes. The slug rounds were blocked by a temporarily created wind barrier.

If slug rounds are blocked so easily, it means most attacks would be utterly useless.

‘Superpowers can’t be used without a cost. They consume mental energy. If this turns into a war of attrition, that power will surely diminish.’

However, a war of attrition would put me at a disadvantage.

Killing Slatter isn’t the end. I have no idea how many more bugs there are. I need to conserve my stamina.

A short battle is not an option, but a necessity.

「Administering combat stimulant.」

Pusyuk.

A needle pierces my back. The drug flows in, spreading throughout my Body, causing my Flesh to become Aroused and my senses to sharpen.

“Shall we have a go, rookie?”

I charged at Slatter. Slatter simultaneously swung two bone Swords and two bone spears. Before it swung, I saw the wind shimmering around its Weapons.

I immediately summoned a shield, thrusting it forward as I approached.

Kakakang!

The blades of wind tore the shield apart. I discarded the shield. Slatter raised its Weapons once more. I summoned a new shield to block its path.

Kakakang!

Again, the shield was torn to shreds. But I was unharmed.

‘I have dozens of shields stacked in my Inventory. I can sacrifice as many as needed.’

Slatter changed its approach. With its massive Body, it lumbered towards me.

‘Mine deployment. Let’s see if it can block bombs that explode from the ground.’

I scattered mines in its path. The mines automatically stuck to the ground and activated their sensors.

Slatter didn’t stop. Instead, it swung its Sword at the mines as if annoyed. The blades of wind detonated the mines. Peong! The black smoke didn’t dissipate but swirled around Slatter’s Body. Slatter was dominating the surrounding wind.

‘Did it recognize the mines? Its intelligence is higher than other bugs.’

I held a Vibe Sword in each Hand. To penetrate the wind barrier, only the power of the Battle Exosuit and the Vibe Swords would suffice.

I walked forward. In my Head, Slatter’s attack patterns were unconsciously calculated. I’ve become so accustomed to combat that I automatically assess an opponent just by looking at them.

The result of most such assessments was the same.

‘I’ve won.’

The large, long-reaching Slatter had the initiative. As soon as I entered its range, it swung all Four of its arms simultaneously.

‘I predicted it. When attacks come from Four directions, it’s impossible to dodge no matter what you do.’

So, I feigned entering its range, then pulled back. A simple footwork trick.

“This is the Heavenly Demon’s moonwalk.”

I swung the Vibe Sword in my Right Hand. The vibrating blade touched the wind barrier enveloping its Body. I felt a force pushing the blade sideways.

Woooooong.

The Battle Exosuit’s engine output increased. My strength grew. I even added the power of the wing thrusters.

I broke through.

The flow of the wind barrier was severed. Wind streamed from the numerous holes in Slatter’s Body, attempting to reconstruct the wind barrier.

Naturally, I had no intention of missing this opportunity.

“Star Burst Stream!!”

I continuously swung the Swords in both Hands. Although my Position faltered in the middle, the AI’s timely assistance with the wing thrusters allowed me to successfully land 16 consecutive strikes.

Slatter convulsed, dismembered. It was rigor mortis.

“This is Aincrad style.”

I coolly turned my Body.

Of course, I had never been to Aincrad.

• • •

I continued deeper into the passage. Along the way, I eliminated hundreds of bugs and two named Monsters. Shotguns, bombs, poison gas, and flamethrowers were excellent Weapons. Especially the bugs poisoned by the gas, most of them died quickly, and even those that didn’t, suffered a decline in physical abilities.

‘They say the reason humanity doesn’t often use Gestag-specific poison gas is due to adaptation.’

Perhaps in the next battle, the poison gas might not work at all.

‘Who cares? You have to get through the present to have a future.’

I arrived at the Queen Bug’s location. The Queen Bug was a massive creature, over 10 meters tall. It sat alone in a wide cavern. Dozens of tentacle branches on its broad back connected to the main hive.

“It looks even more disgusting than I imagined.”

The Queen Bug.

The name might conjure images of an elegant, queen-like insect, but… Gestag have no gender. It’s called the Queen Bug because it’s the leader and producer of the Gestag swarm.

The Queen Bug stared at me intently. I sensed no will to fight. Despite its size, the Queen Bug had almost no combat power. The Queen Bug’s ability and primary function were genetic manipulation. It was the Queen Bug that evolved Gestag or created variants.

-Human. Have you come to kill me?

A voice echoed in my Mind/Spirit. At the same time, I felt my Mind/Spirit waver.

「A powerful psychic wave has been detected.」

「Administering combat stimulant.」

“Stop. I’m fine.”

「Canceling combat stimulant administration.」

The Queen Bug continued to emit psychic waves. My Mind/Spirit, which had initially wavered, seemed to have grown accustomed to it, feeling nothing.

“It’s useless, Queen Bug. My Mind/Spirit won’t be swayed by such thoughts.”

-You are a peculiar variant in many ways.

“If you don’t want to die, stop the forces attacking the outpost.”

-Hehehe.

The Queen Bug laughed. I saw its mouth move grotesquely. The psychic wave emanating from it indicated it was genuinely enjoying the moment.

I frowned and, as a warning, slashed the Queen Bug’s Leg. However, the Queen Bug didn’t stop laughing.

-Human. Did you think you had victory in your Hands? You are mistaken. Even if you kill me, the outcome is decided. I am weakened, and my lifespan is almost over. A new Queen will succeed me, and your genes will be absorbed by us.

Kugugugugugugu.

The walls and floor shook. This main hive was intact even after a nuclear attack. This wasn’t due to an external assault.

‘Is the Queen Bug directly manipulating the main hive?’

Should I give up on capturing it alive and just kill it?

While I hesitated for a moment, the walls and ceiling of the main hive cracked. Through the cracks, I could see the murky sky, where the aftermath of a nuclear explosion had not yet settled.

Something massive was flying in from an even higher altitude. A super-giant Monster the size of a battleship, accompanied by thousands of flying bugs.

There was only one reason for such a massive force to move as a single unit.

A new Queen Bug was finally descending upon this planet.

I made my decision immediately.

-Hehehe.

I charged at the Queen Bug, which was laughing, confident of victory. I sliced its neck and tore its Body apart. Even in its dying moments, it laughed as if mocking me.

What I pulled from its torn Flesh was a round organ next to its heart. It was about the size of a watermelon.

The Queen Bug’s genetic information storage organ.

‘Humanity doesn’t know, but I knew the world’s lore in reality. The Queen’s succession ceremony is completed by eating this genetic information storage organ. That’s how they gain the genetic information accumulated by the previous Queen Bug.’

If humanity has the essence of science, Gestag have the essence of Evolution. And this was truly the essence of Evolution.

I used a knife to cut into this organ, forcibly pried it open, and inserted a thermal pressure bomb. The organ contracted, making it barely noticeable from the outside.

And I waited, looking up at the sky.

The carapace of the super-giant bug opened, revealing a Queen Bug over 10 meters in size. Flying bugs gathered, forming a palanquin. The Queen Bug slowly descended. Its eyes never left me.

“I know this organ is more important to you than anything else.”

-Human. Even without that organ, we can live without any hindrance.

“That’s right. Genetic information can be re-recorded. But the hundreds of years of information accumulated on this planet, and the genetic information adapted and evolved on this planet, will be lost.”

-You are human, yet you know too much about us. What do you want?

“A temporary truce. I don’t ask for much. A week is enough. Oh, you know what a week is, right?”

-I know. I accept your deal. Hand over the organ.

I extended the organ in my Hand to the Queen Bug. One of the flying bugs approached and carefully took the organ.

‘As expected, it didn’t notice.’

I secretly smiled.

A deal with the Queen Bug? Of course, I didn’t trust it. The Queen Bug would probably pretend to accept the deal, get the organ, and then try to kill me. If I were the Queen Bug, I would have done the same.

‘This isn’t a war between humans. In a war of annihilation, honor means nothing.’

If I could capture that Queen Bug alive, perhaps. But with my current strength, I couldn’t face the thousands of flying bugs guarding that Queen Bug.

The Queen Bug opened its mouth wide and put the organ in. It was using the most precious Item in the safest way.

“Keke. Idiot. Goodbye.”

I must be the only human in History to have killed two Queen Bugs.

I laughed, watching the Queen Bug chew the organ.

Kwaaaaaaaang!

The Queen Bug’s Body exploded. Intense flames swept through its surroundings.

I immediately ran into the main hive’s passage.

‘Fighting outside is suicide. But inside this main hive… it will be fucking hard, but I can endure it.’

Only a limited number of things could enter the passage.

I spread out the turrets and bombs I had packed in my Inventory and held my ground.

I just needed to hold out for three days.

‘When the First Fleet arrives, they’ll wipe these bastards out.’

Surprisingly, no bugs immediately pursued me. The death of the Queen Bug had caused chaos in the command structure.

‘Still, it’s taking too long… Could it be that there wasn’t a single commanding individual other than the Queen Bug in that swarm?’

Perhaps they hadn’t expected the Queen Bug to die in such a way. Honestly, from their perspective, it was a rather absurd death.

‘I’ll have some breathing room until a General Bug appears nearby to Dominate them.’

I had no intention of breaking through. Even if they were dumber, their numbers were too overwhelming.

I built a small stronghold in the main hive passage. If a few streamed in, I shot them dead.

How would Gestag react if the Queen Bug died?

There were largely two reactions.

A General Bug would Dominate the forces and continue the war, or a General Bug would rally the forces and retreat.

Usually, it was the latter.

Gestag’s purpose is Evolution. They don’t evolve out of hatred like humans. For them, the war with humans is closer to a battle for instinctive territory.

A day passed.

They didn’t launch a full-scale attack on me.

Since it was unlikely all the General Bugs were dead, it seemed the General Bugs had decided to retreat.

Another day passed.

Radio communication, which had been jammed by the blocking bugs, reconnected.

「Communication request received from Commander Kalpas.」

I approved the request.

“Are you alive, hero?”

“Loyalty~”

I gave the popular double-Hand salute.

“Judging by your appearance, you seem to be alive.”

“Oh~ Immediate sarcasm?”

“The outpost is safe. They retreated off-planet with only their elite forces. Perhaps they detected the First Fleet’s movement. Did you kill the Queen Bug?”

“I didn’t have the luxury of capturing it alive.”

“There were two Queen Bugs, weren’t there? Did you kill both?”

“Yes. I played the hero a bit.”

“Hah. There will be no other hero like you. Can you return on your own?”

“I don’t know the situation outside. Can you help me?”

“I could send a unit by Coercion, but… we don’t have much leeway here either. If it’s not urgent, wait until tomorrow. The First Fleet arrives tomorrow.”

The quest was not yet complete.

Perhaps returning to the outpost would be even more dangerous.

“I’ll wait here. I’m looking forward to the fleet escort. Oh, are Marie and Emma safe?”

“They are safe. They’re running around frantically. I’ll contact you later.”

I drank a cola and waited.
