I directly moved to procure criminals.

The story with the 6th Legion was already over. I obtained the authority to judge the western traitors without trial, limited to the lands of treason. Of course, this was absolutely impossible under normal circumstances.

,That's how fucked up the Heist Empire is beyond imagination, making this possible.'

The Imperial Army is happy to reduce the number of traitors, and I'm happy to procure criminals. You could say our interests aligned. It was partly thanks to the 6th Legion Commander's favor towards me.

,It was quite frustrating to catch them while wearing a mask, but the results were well worth it.'

In just one week, I caught about 1,200 traitors. More than half of them were the families of the traitors. According to Imperial law, the families of traitors were also traitors, so there was no problem.

"Squad Leader-nim! All preparations are complete!"

A subordinate shouted from outside the barracks. I got up from the chair and took out a mask from the desk drawer. It's different from the mask I used to catch the traitors. A colorful, smiling mask. I made it myself, an artifact with simple magic that modulates the voice.

I put on the mask. It's a bit stuffy, but not uncomfortable.

I went outside.

The subordinate saluted when he saw me. I shook my head.

"From now on, I am not the Squad Leader. You are not a soldier either. Do not salute. If you call me Squad Leader, I'll kill you."

"Understood."

"Start the final inspection. We start in 30 minutes."

"Yes!"

The subordinate left. He was wearing a black coat instead of a military uniform. I glanced around.

This wasn't Lumen Prison. It was a wasteland a little further away from there. The place where I fought the necromancer. Unlike then, several buildings and devices were installed. Of course, it was basically spacious. Like a venue.

I walked around the wasteland, checking the condition. There were no problems.

Among the staff, there were guys wearing red clothes. They had bomb necklaces around their necks and wore crying face masks. They were prisoners sentenced to labor re-education. They were perfect for exploitation because they possessed specialized skills such as medicine, technology, and magic.

"Master! It's time!"

One of the subordinates wearing a black coat shouted. I nodded and headed to the stage.

Several state-of-the-art cameras were rotating around the stage.

I took a seat in the highest chair. A golden mask wearing a tuxedo approached me. He was the host of the show.

"How was the rehearsal?"

"Successful. All machines are working without malfunction. I also rotated the cameras. No problems. The condition of the prisoners... not bad."

I held a tablet in my hand.

What I planned was an internet broadcast called 'Rainbow Show'. It's about using prisoners who are going to be executed anyway as entertainment.

Of course, it's illegal. Even though this world is fucked up, it still maintains a certain line for ordinary people. Of course, ordinary people know how dangerous this world is, so they try not to get involved in that kind of thing.

,It would be a problem to film a show with ordinary people. But these guys are prisoners. It will ease the viewers' conscience.'

I could see viewers who had entered the site and were waiting for the broadcast. The number was 300,000. The chat window was going crazy.

-Are you really doing this crazy show?

-Start already!

-Open the door!!! Door!!

-Are you making a show out of people's lives? You'll be punished by heaven, you bastards.

-I just reported it to the police.

-Human rights are not meant to be ignored like this! Everyone! This site is crazy! Let's all speak out for human rights!

-Human rights my ass. I saw the promotional video, and all the guys here are criminals, right? There's even a serial killer.

The chat window was hot. It was thanks to spreading the video all over the site to promote it.

,It was a video of tearing a prisoner to death, so it must have been quite intense.'

It was a deliberately cruel video to attract aggro.

,What I want is not many viewers. It's rich viewers.'

From the second episode, only those with viewing rights will be able to watch the show. Viewing rights cost 3 million credits. It's a price that's not only burdensome but also makes ordinary people shake their heads.

,Even if only 300 rich viewers remain out of 300,000, it's a success.'

The chat window was a mess even as time passed. There were guys who were openly cursing, guys who were attracting aggro by saying they would report it, guys who were full of anticipation, and so on.

,Do they think the police will move right away if they report it.'

Even if a justice-filled detective were to move, this was the Heist Empire. The police of other countries cannot exercise public power. The military police of the Heist Empire? Their essence was soldiers. Soldiers who do what they're told from above. There was no way they would take the initiative to carry out the work.

1 minute until the broadcast time.

I gestured to the host.

"Start."

"Yes."

The host heads to the stage where dozens of cameras are filming. Seeing his slightly trembling body, he seems to be nervous.

The camera director sent a signal, and the show began. I checked the broadcast on the tablet PC. The camera director was from a broadcasting station, so the screen was pretty good.

"Hello! I am the host in charge of the Rainbow Show! My name is a secret! The person sitting in that high place is the Show Master! He is the one who planned and carried out this event!"

The camera turned to me. I didn't even acknowledge the camera. I just sat arrogantly and looked down on the show.

-Start already!

-Do you think I came here to see this kind of guy?

-Tick tock!

-I'll hold my breath until it starts. Hiss!

"Haha! It seems like your bodies are also heating up a lot!"

The chat window is also visible to the host in real time. As the person directly conducting the show, his mask was made more special than mine. He can see not only the chat window but also the staff's messages.

"Before we start, I'll briefly explain the Rainbow Show! The participants in the show are all criminals! There are no petty criminals like pickpockets. They are all people who have killed someone or committed a crime equivalent to that! Please put your conscience aside for a moment and enjoy the show! Ah, you don't have a conscience? You're the perfect viewer for the Rainbow Show!"

The host was rambling. He's stalling for time. The staff is busy moving to ensure smooth progress out of sight of the camera.

"The Rainbow Show is held once every 100 days. Maybe it will take more than that. Please understand that this is unavoidable due to the nature of the show. This first episode is free, but don't forget that you have to pay 3 million credits to watch the show from the second episode! While I'm talking, the first show is ready!"

The camera shines behind the host.

A man was being dragged in by the staff.

"Blue corner!! Crime Harman, a young executive of the Resistance Echoes! He is a mass murderer who committed a terrorist attack in a large city square, killing hundreds of people! Look at those fierce eyes! You can feel his will to kill anyone who gets in his way!"

"Who are the people following behind? Of course, they are criminals. They are Crime's family. The family of a criminal is a criminal."

-Wow, I'm seeing a guy I saw on the news here.

-Crime, you son of a bitch! Bring my brother back!!

-?? I heard he was caught and executed, but he's still alive?

-What are you doing? No matter how much of a terrorist he is, this kind of thing cannot be tolerated. He must be judged by the fair law!

-Damn it! Release Crime! He is a hero to the citizens!

-ㅋㅋㅋ I think there are some terrorists here?

However, they did not hesitate to show their murderous intent towards each other. Because not only their own lives but also the lives of their families were at stake.

"Now, before we start the first show, we will give you time to bet! Bet on who will win! Ah, you don't have to bet! But it's more fun to bet, right?"

Betting adds more immersion. And it fills my wallet.

I checked the betting situation. The betting amount started to rise at an incredible speed. It didn't take long for more than 100 million credits to be bet.

,The commission alone will be substantial.'
