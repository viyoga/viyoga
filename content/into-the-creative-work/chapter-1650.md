"Oh, ooh, oooo...!"

Admiration! More admiration!

I was constantly admiring the effects of the Book of the Dead. An SSS-rank item. Seeing it again and again, I am deeply moved. The hardships I've endured to get this Book of the Dead flash through my mind.

"It was worth the suffering!"

I'm not sure about being able to connect to Hell. I'll have to use it myself later.

"Revives the dead only 3 times. This must refer to complete resurrection."

3 resurrections.

I didn't think I could use it directly on myself. Because there's a condition that you must have the corpse of the person to be revived. Except in special cases, corpses cannot move.

As soon as I saw the resurrection, I thought of reality. It would be amazing if I could use this ability in reality as well. No, even if it's not reality, it will be a great help in other game worlds.

'...That's if I can use it.'

If you take it to reality or another world, the effects are reduced. Above all, these kinds of things from Atlantis often receive help from the system.

As I was about to go out of the building, Chrisema came in. He saw the Book of the Dead in my hand and swallowed hard.

"Congratulations. You have completed the Book of the Dead. I can feel tremendous power from the Book of the Dead."

"Hehe. The ability is also tremendous. Shall I show you the effects?"

I proudly showed him the effects of the Book of the Dead. Chrisema was shocked as expected. His eyes widened as if they would tear.

"How can this be! To be able to revive the dead! That, that is an amazing item! If that effect becomes a rumor...!"

"They'll come running with their eyes burning."

The strong people who are currently ruling Atlantis are strong people who have been rolling around for at least 5 years. They all must have had the experience of losing someone precious. If the corpse of a precious person remains, and they find out about the effects of the Book of the Dead? They would try to get it by any means. The same goes for those who haven't lost a precious person. Just having the Book of the Dead is like insurance. It can be said to be 3 extra lives.

"Chrisema. Do you want the Book of the Dead?"

"Ah, no. How dare I...."

Chrisema quickly lowered his head. He lowers his gaze to my feet and acts humble. To be honest, at first, I didn't know if it was acting. This guy was so good at acting that he could deceive me. If it were modern times, he would have succeeded as an actor or politician.

"Don't lie. I can feel greed in your eyes right now."

"......"

I struck the floor with Moses' staff. Mana spreads in all directions. I can feel it. The warriors of the Yeolbaram tribe surrounding this building from the outside. At least 500 warriors are giving up on this place.

"And listen. Did you think I wouldn't know your intentions until the end? You tried to indirectly kill me by using the second and third requests. You probably thought I would die in the second request, the trial."

Chrisema raised his head.

The humility is nowhere to be found. The eyes looking at me were extremely sharp.

"That's right. When you passed the trial, I was so surprised that I was shocked. That trial is the trial of sin. A trial that can never be passed if evil outweighs good on the scales of good and evil. Meaningless Death-nim would directly intervene... How on earth did you pass the trial?"

"I received help from the Contract God Seat. I shattered my own heart."

"Indeed. The Contract God Seat gave such help... You seem to be quite loved by that Contract God Seat."

"Love? That bastard is not that kind of guy."

"That doesn't matter. Let's get to the point. Give me the Book of the Dead. Then I will let you go without harming you. After that, go find Hatep, the ruler of the Golden Desert, or not, as you please. We are not interested in such petty battles."

"It's a matter of the hegemony of the western desert. But you're not interested?"

"We will leave the desert with the Book of the Dead. We are sick of this barren land. We will trade the Book of the Dead with the Ustia Empire. We can get at least a count's title."

"A marquis is also possible. Well, a duke is absolutely impossible, though."

"Give me the Book of the Dead."

"Do you think I'll give it to you?"

"You have no choice. If you don't give it... we'll just take it."

"Do you think you can take it?"

Chrisema struck the floor with his staff.

An obelisk rises up. My name was written on the surface of the obelisk.

"Have you forgotten this obelisk? You swore not to harm us. If you break the oath, you will be dragged to Hell. You cannot harm us."

"Hehe. Do you think I'm afraid of Hell or something? I've already experienced Hell before. In the first place, do you think I'll be dragged to Hell or something? As long as I'm wearing this golden mask, no curse will work on me."

I pulled up mana. The ominous mana of black magic spreads in all directions. Just that alone overwhelms the desert warriors and makes them step back.

"Don't back down! He can't kill us! Don't forget that you are warriors of the Yeolbaram tribe!"

Chrisema shouted. The old man is much better than the desert warriors.

"I didn't like you from the beginning. I'll kill you with the power of the Book of the Dead, especially you."

Of course, the intention was mostly to test the power of the Book of the Dead.

Activate the effect of the Book of the Dead. It's about connecting Hell and me.

I felt something cold connecting to me.

Deep, deep darkness.

I can feel the huge being at the end.

"Being of Hell! Give me strength! I promise to kill the guys in front of me and offer them to you!"

Drip.

"It seems the connection with Hell has been cut off."

"...Do you know something?"

"I felt that presence for a moment. The one I serve."

"......Meaningless Death."

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

"Keok! L, let go of this...!"

"Take a good look. It's the sword energy that will stab your belly."

"You will regret it, you will regret it...!"

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Hoicheon Essence Immersion (回天浸精).
