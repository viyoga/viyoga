A crimson island falls from the sky.

It wasn't plummeting like a meteor, burning itself up on the way down.

Slowly. But at a speed that was by no means slow, it was descending toward Seoul. Maruhan Academy was in Gangwon Province, so I could observe it relatively objectively.

'I don't know the exact size, but even at a glance it's larger than the area of Seoul. That thing is falling precisely over Seoul.'

The immense magical power radiating from the island. I could be certain. That was the work of demons.

'The magic power moving that island can't belong to individual demons. At minimum thousands. Perhaps tens of thousands of demons are up there. It's suspicious that there are that many demons, but… I have to assume they've gathered everything to their limit.'

An invasion of that scale.

It wasn't a plan cooked up over a few days of deliberation. At the very least, it had to be an all-out war prepared over several months.

'What happens if that lands inside Seoul? Does it end with ten million Seoul citizens dying? Or is there something after that? If the island itself is special, there must be something to it.'

From the Seoul side, something was fired toward the falling crimson island. It was glowing, so calling it a missile didn't quite fit. It was a kind of mana bombardment. I could also see several heroes capable of flight charging toward the island.

But they were blocked by the barrier protecting the island. The attacks couldn't even put a small scratch on the barrier, and the heroes were crushed against it and plummeted downward along with it.

I pulled a spatial teleportation scroll from my inventory.

'Whatever the demons' plan and purpose, what I need to do is simple. Interfere with their plan and kill the demons.'

Riiip.

I tore the spatial teleportation scroll and appeared on the rooftop of a building in Seoul.

'Machina.'

I summoned the machine spirit Machina. Machina, looking like a small girl in a white dress, spun around me before belatedly spotting the massive crimson island falling from the sky.

"Hieeeek?! What is that?!"

"It's an invasion by the demon legion. That day has finally come."

"Why today of all days! I was going to eat samgyeopsal today!"

Machina glared at the island indignantly.

Korea's heroes and military mobilized. They began attacking the falling island. Fighter jets scrambled, and heroes cast magic.

No attack could pierce the island's barrier. Conversely, long tentacles and beams shot out from the island, attacking the heroes and fighter jets. One fighter jet failed to dodge a beam and exploded midair. There was no time for the pilot to eject.

"Let's abandon Seoul! No matter how I think about it, there's no way we can do anything about that in time!"

Machina stated the easiest option without hesitation.

"If Seoul falls, the whole world will nuke the shit out of them! A few hundred nukes and what are demons gonna do! They'll die!"

"They might not die. You know as well as I do that the demons' authorities are bizarre. For all we know, that barrier itself might not be penetrable by nuclear weapons. It wouldn't be strange if that barrier had something like immunity to physical attacks attached to it."

More than anything, Seoul had several of my women in it. If I used a spatial teleportation scroll, I could grab them and run, but… I hadn't gone through all this hellish preparation just to run away now.

"Let's give it a shot."

I pulled the World Destroyer from my inventory.

"Huh? You're going to attack with the World Destroyer? Even if its power rivals a nuclear explosion, that island is bigger than Seoul! Even a nuclear explosion can't erase that island. What about the barrier you mentioned?!"

She was right.

Even if a nuclear bomb is lethal, a single nuke can't completely erase a major city like Seoul. Let alone a massive island? It was obviously impossible even with the World Destroyer.

"Destroying it is impossible, so I'll push it toward the sea."

The demons were descending slowly, whether to minimize their losses or conserve their strength. Even so, humanity had no means to stop it.

"What about the Storm Breaker? Aren't you going to wear the armor? You're going to fire the World Destroyer without covering your face?"

At this point in time, the World Destroyer was famous as Crimson Light's weapon. Pull the trigger and it fires a bullet with tactical nuclear-level power—there was no way that could be ignored.

"It doesn't matter if I reveal my identity now."

Playing dark hero as Crimson Light was closer to a hobby for bedding women. Crimson Light's popularity was no joke, so I could easily get most women into bed.

"Villain, hero, nation. Do you really think anyone can do anything to me now that they've figured out my identity?"

Behind me stood Seong Hari, unofficially recognized as an SS-rank hero. And my own combat power was already at S-rank level. Add the World Destroyer on top of that, and any sane nation knew that opposing me was an absurdly costly endeavor.

Thunder Heaven Style: Flying Thunder God.

I wreathed my entire body in lightning and shot up toward the sky.

I reached an altitude roughly level with the falling crimson island. The top of the crimson island was desolate, and demons were swarming there in droves. Demons of all different forms stared at me without hiding their killing intent.

"A human without fear."

"Is that the human Lord Mekin said to be wary of?"

"No, you idiot. Lord Mekin said to beware the woman with the spear. That one is a demon hunter. The one who inherited the power of that bastard who dared to hunt us without knowing his place."

"Whatever that one's identity is, Lord Mekin's orders are simple. Once the island lands, go out and kill and devour every last human without exception. It won't be long now."

The demons looking at me drooled openly.

I aimed the World Destroyer at the island. The World Destroyer was already equipped with a mana cube.

The currently set mode was explosive. In this situation, explosive mode was meaningless.

'Continuous mode.'

Click.

I heard the internal machinery turning.

'The goal is to push back the falling island with continuous mode, but… no matter how I think about it, the island is too big. The output won't be enough.'

『Using Spirit Eye.』

My pupils turned golden. Just activating the Spirit Eye raised my spirit affinity.

'Come, Arest.'

I summoned the highest-grade wind spirit, Arest.

Mana drained from within me in massive quantities, and wind blew.

The somewhat artificial wind swept past me and gathered in the air, taking the form of an orca. The orca flicked its tail. All the surrounding wind converged on the orca.

[Demon legion. Those disgusting hordes have finally invaded Earth.]

"I need to push that island to the West Sea. Help me."

[Very well.]

There was no need to explain in words, really. Since we had a contract, conveying the situation through thought alone was sufficient.

Arest swam through the sky. Every time his tail moved, wind gathered.

Soon the wind orca rammed its head into the massive demon island.

Kuuuuung!

Even Arest's charge couldn't pierce the barrier. But the entire crimson island shook along with the barrier. Arest kept his head pressed against the barrier, tail moving. Massive amounts of wind pushed the crimson island little by little.

I watched him and pulled the trigger.

Blue energy shot toward the crimson island. Its destructive power was lower compared to explosive or piercing modes, so it couldn't pierce the barrier. But continuous mode meant it kept firing energy.

With Arest's wind and the World Destroyer's energy pushing continuously, the crimson island began to be pushed back.

The demons inside laughed.

"Ooooh! He's pushing back the demon island! Impressive!"

"Kikiki. You think the city will be safe if this falls into the sea? The result doesn't change. We'll tear you all apart!"

『Demon Hunter (SSS) is displeased.』

『Demon Hunter (SSS) desires the death of all demons!』

I know.

Do you think I'd let those bastards live?

I'm going to kill all the demons anyway, Mekin included. Ah, except Baal. Baal isn't someone to kill—he's someone to save.

[Your strength is waning.]

Even though I'd switched the World Destroyer to continuous mode, it didn't last very long. I succeeded in pushing half the demon island toward the West Sea, but the remaining half was the problem. At this rate, Incheon gets wrecked. Seoul would definitely get caught in the aftermath too.

'The Netherworld mana cube… I shouldn't use that. The aura of death would scatter and kill people, turning them into undead. That's burning down the whole house to catch a bedbug.'

I made a quick judgment.

I put the World Destroyer into my inventory.

"Arest! Merge!"

[Understood.]

Arest, who had been pushing the demon island, turned and flew toward me.

I spread both arms and received Arest.

Spirit Descent.

I invoked Arest's descent into me.

My body became translucent. Blood and flesh, bones all turned to wind and swirled inside me.

It was far more stable than when I'd invoked other spirits. If there were a compatibility rating with spirits, I'd say it exceeded 100. Honestly, it was much more comfortable than Machina.

I wasn't even that close with Arest, and it was like this.

'Well, I have a guess why.'

『God of Storms (SSS) is responding.』

Fierce winds began to rage around me. Clouds turned dark and heavy, and the calm sea grew turbulent.

I clenched my fist, staring at the crimson island.

Wind gathered. The force of the converging wind was so savage it distorted even the surrounding space.

'This alone won't be enough.'

『Using Authority of Strength.』

The Authority of Strength is an authority obtained by synthesizing Authority of Brute Force (A-), Curse of Discipline (A), Authority of Truth (S+), Authority of Transformation (A+), Authority of Plague (A+), Authority of Extreme Cold (S+), Interference (A+), Invisibility (A+), Manipulation (A+), Reconstruction (S+), and Authority of Wrath (A).

The effect is simple.

It amplifies my strength.

There are no conditions for activation. It doesn't consume mana either. Instead, it took about 5 minutes before I could use it again.

Perhaps due to the amplification from the Authority of Strength, my right fist had turned pitch black like a black hole. Of course, it was only in appearance—it wasn't an actual black hole.

I flew in front of the crimson island and swung my right fist.

KWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWA!

The island's barrier shattered, and my fist connected with the island.

The demon island flew toward the West Sea and crashed rapidly into the ocean. With that massive mass hitting the sea at high speed, a colossal tsunami naturally erupted.

'That tsunami is unexpected, but… lucky me. I'll use that tsunami.'

I summoned the highest-grade water spirit, Tera.

A swirling sphere of water appeared from the sea side.

『God of Storms (SSS) is responding.』

Under the God of Storms' influence, Tera's power grew even stronger.

The direction of the tsunami, which had been heading toward the city, was redirected toward the demon island.

"The barrier broke?! This is insane!"

"W-wait? Why is that wave coming this way?!"

I didn't expect a tsunami to kill all the demons. That was impossible from the start.

'A few hundred low-rank demons will die. That's enough.'

Through the Demon Hunter (SSS) effect, I can grow stronger. Getting a few hundred authorities from pathetic demons is a bonus. No matter how pathetic, synthesizing hundreds of authorities into one would produce something useful.

"That won't do."

A heavy voice echoed from deep within the demon island.

The panicking demons all froze at once and swallowed their breath. A being that struck fear even into the cruelest demons.

A massive crimson demon with seven great horns. Blood-red eyes, and behind his back, eerie red wings that looked as if the skin had been peeled off. The only thing on his body that wasn't red was the goat legs covered in black fur.

Fifth Legion Commander.

Demon King of Destruction.

"Mekin…!"

I glared at him with hatred in my eyes.

"Kuhuhu. Did you think I wouldn't know your abilities? Did you really believe it was coincidence that you couldn't face demons all this time?"

Mekin raised the giant sword gripped in his right hand. A massive blade in a crude form, as if roughly carved from a giant boulder.

Crunch!

Suddenly, the ornate necklace hanging around Mekin's neck shattered and crumbled to dust.

One of the abilities of the Authority of Destruction that Mekin possessed.

By sacrificing something, he could amplify his power, or restore his stamina or heal his wounds.

Dark crimson magical energy blazed ominously along Mekin's greatsword.

Mekin swung his sword. A massive dark-crimson slash split the tsunami.

The tsunami lost its force and fell to the ground. My power and Tera's power that had been controlling the tsunami vanished. The power had been destroyed.

"Demon Hunter. I'll deal with you personally. As for the rest of the rabble, shouldn't rabble play with rabble? Heheh."
