I returned to the banquet hall with the Queen and Royal Head maid Florence. All eyes were on us.

Most seemed curious about what kind of conversation I had with the Queen, but a few who wielded significant influence in Neo London seemed to have guessed the content of our talk, exuding a relaxed atmosphere.

,So they've also finished their conversation with the Queen and will remain neutral in the succession battle.'

However, the Queen is someone who will eventually step down from the throne. I can't be sure they'll maintain neutrality until the end.

,The expressions of the Princess and Princes are stiff. As the parties involved, they probably understand the Queen's intentions better than anyone else.'

The Queen was old but still vigorous.

"I had a dream yesterday where three suns rose in the skies above Neo London. Two more suns rising because the sun couldn't set! Isn't that an auspicious dream?"

The Queen joked.

"If there are three suns, it might get too hot and Neo London might overheat."

Florence chimed in from the side. They seemed more like friends than a lord-servant relationship.

"Florence. Overheat? That's not something a lady should say."

"Why do you only think about that when you're at an age where you shouldn't even have sexual desires...."

"Don't you think about that? Or do you not?"

"I do, actually. Neo London is the Queen's country, so if it had a gender, shouldn't it be considered female?"

"Don't turn the city into a woman. I'm sometimes afraid of what's inside your head."

The nobles around them managed their expressions. It seemed familiar somehow. The Queen and the maid's banter was nothing new.

Still, the Queen was the center of this banquet.

"Oh, Count Tolline. It's been a while. Three years? I thought you were dead."

"Haha. I had some business troubles for a while. But I've appeared before Your Majesty like this. Your Majesty is as radiant as ever."

"Lady May. You look beautiful today as well. What kind of cosmetics do you use? Will you tell me the secret?"

"Oh, Your Majesty the Queen, you're too kind. Of course, I'll tell you."

The Queen walked around the banquet hall, talking to people. She exchanged pleasantries in a joking manner. She didn't say a single word about political matters. Everyone here was friendly to the Queen. At least, that's how it seemed on the surface.

,You can never know what's inside.'

It was then. Florence subtly stepped away and approached me. She took out a wine and a wine glass from her apron pocket.

"This is a wine that's not easily obtained. I'll give you, Count-nim, the honor of having a glass."

Florence's right hand swept over the wine bottle. The cork sealing the wine had already moved onto her palm.

,Transfer? A space-related ability? I didn't sense any magic.'

Florence also appears in the original game. But detailed information is not known at all. I don't know what abilities she has.

She poured the wine with an expressionless face. As expected of a Prime-level, I suppose. There was a scent of expertise in her movements.

"...Now that I see it, the label is strange. 137. A wine with only numbers written on it. I've never heard of it."

"It means it's the 137th wine. There's no other meaning."

"Could it be handmade?"

"Yes. It's a wine made directly by me, a master sommelier and Master of Wine. It's a wine that's hard to get even with money. Because I don't sell it."

Florence handed me the wine she had poured in moderation. The unique scent of the wine wafted even without bringing it close to my nose. I could tell without even wetting my tongue. This was definitely delicious.

"Did you know? The higher the grade, the more specially the body changes. There's a saying that a 7th-grade warrior's fingernail is harder than steel. There's also a saying that the bodily fluids of the saints of Divine France are holy water themselves. And I'm an 8th grade. I've added something extra to this wine that men will like."

"......Could it be. Did you put your bodily fluids in here?"

"Vaginal fluids? That's sexual harassment."

What is this woman? She was talking nonsense with an expressionless face.

I looked at her with bewildered eyes, then looked at the wine. The scent was good. There were no other peculiarities.

"Rest assured. What I added was a little bit of mandrake root juice. Yes. The one that's good for a man's... you know."

I glared. Mandrake was a well-known elixir even in this world. She put in the root juice? It was a tonic in itself. It was also true that it was good for a man's stamina.

"Isn't mandrake root famous for being very bitter? Is it okay to put it in wine?"

"I used alchemy to improve the bitterness to suit the wine, so it's okay. Please, try it."

I suspiciously brought the wine glass to my lips. It was hard to resist since it was said to be good for stamina. It was a man's instinct.

I took a sip and my eyes widened. Whether it was the effect of the mandrake or not, my body felt pleasantly warm and full of energy. Above all, the taste was outstanding.

"This is... amazing."

"I'm glad you liked it."

I enjoyed the wine for a while before asking her.

"Palmer-ssi."

"Florence is fine."

"...Florence-ssi. What is the purpose of you approaching me? What are you curious about regarding me?"

"You're so cold. Maybe I'm flirting because I admire you, Count-nim. I even treated you to a precious wine."

"I appreciate the wine, but I've heard stories about Florence-ssi. I heard that Florence-ssi doesn't directly step forward even if there's a royal event."

"All the servants of the royal family are competent, so there's no need for me to step forward directly. Still, I'm the one who sets the direction."

"That must be convenient."

"Yes. It's a really comfortable position. I only have to worry about Her Majesty the Queen. Well, sometimes Her Majesty the Queen makes unreasonable requests, which can be troublesome."

"......Is it because of Yuria?"

Florence remained expressionless. It seemed difficult to guess her inner thoughts from her expression.

"Yes. I was curious about what kind of person the owner of the next Prime-level maid would be. Of course, she hasn't become a Prime yet, but.... It seemed likely that she would become my junior."

"Junior, huh. Yuria won't be working in the royal palace, though."

"The final examiner for the Prime certification is Her Majesty the Queen. Her final test is to assist Her Majesty the Queen for 100 days. In other words, she will be my successor for 100 days. Even if that's not the case, she's still my junior. I'm also from the Maid Academy."

"Is it okay to leak the contents of the exam like this?"

"If she's truly a Prime-level talent, she would have guessed the final exam long ago. Also, knowing the contents of the exam in advance doesn't change anything. The final exam is a test to evaluate all of her abilities."

"In the end, Yuria will pass."

"Your trust is amazing. Ah, people who have met her in person say that she and I are similar. As her master, what do you think?"

The reason why other people feel that Yuria and she are similar is probably because of the unique feeling of genius.

Without realizing it, I scanned Florence up and down. She was beautiful. But to say she was as beautiful as Yuria, well.... She had jet-black hair, and Yuria had bluish-silver hair.

"Ah. Your heights are similar."

She was quite tall for a woman.

"Didn't you just look at my chest?"

"Yuria's are bigger."

"...To think you'd sexually harass me so openly. I did something too, so I'll let it slide this time. ...But are they really that big? I'm quite confident in my chest too."

Florence's chest was also large. I guessed it was around an E cup. Judging from the fact that her maid uniform subtly accentuated her chest, she seemed to have some pride in them.

"Yuria's are bigger."

"...Hmm. My junior is a formidable opponent."

Florence crossed her arms and supported her chest. Even so, Yuria's, who was just standing still, were bigger.

When I stood there blankly, she sighed and unfolded her arms. Unlike her consistently expressionless face, she seemed to have a bit of a playful side.

"It was a pleasure talking to you today, Count-nim. It's almost auction time, so I'll be going. I hope to see you again if I have the chance."

Florence returned to the Queen.

,Did she really come to talk to me because she was curious about me, Yuria's master?'

She was an unpredictable woman in many ways.

Maids and butlers entered the banquet hall. Moving in perfect order, they created an auction site in the center of the banquet hall.

Party attendees put up items for auction, and other attendees purchase the items. The purchased items are kept, but the proceeds from the sale are donated in the seller's name.

The important thing is not the donation. It's this place itself. Those who attended this party were all CEOs or executives of large corporations, or great nobles. Even nobles with weak power are still nobles.

,It's an opportunity to promote goods in front of them.'

And there are also reporters watching this place. I can't see a single reporter right now, but... it's obvious that all sorts of articles will come out within an hour after the banquet ends.

,It's an opportunity that entrepreneurs can't miss.'

Standing at the center of the auction was a butler wearing a rhombus-shaped badge with a cross engraved on it. A Superior-level butler, two levels higher than Triple, became the auctioneer and proceeded with the event.

"The first item is a necklace that Her Majesty the Queen used in her youth! It's an artifact with 5th-grade physical protection and health management magic! There's a limit of only being activated for 6 hours a day! Let's start from 1 million credits!"

It was a pretty good artifact. More than that, it was more valuable because the Queen used it in her youth.

,The current Queen's popularity is in the top five throughout history. Queen otaku can't stand it.'

And more than half of the nobles here are Queen otaku.

"5 million credits!"

"6 million!"

"8 million!!"

The nobles shouted one after another. Frankly, it was a sight that lacked dignity, but... the atmosphere of the auction heated up in an instant. The host, the Queen, was watching with a smile. Florence was expressionless by her side.

"60 million."

I shouted. The surroundings became quiet. It was a 5th-grade artifact, but with a 6-hour limit per day, 60 million credits was excessively expensive.

But this was also a way to prove loyalty to the Queen.

"70 million!!"

Someone made a higher bid. It was a great noble. I glanced at him for a moment and said.

"100 million."

When spending, spend big.

Some nobles were intimidated, and some nobles chuckled.

,Either way, 100 million will be donated in the Queen's name. You have to show off your money when you have to. Besides, if you don't swear in a place like this, you'll get sworn at instead.'

In particular, LK Corp. had been making crazy money recently, so it was easy to get sworn at. To avoid that, I had to spend money.

"The first item has been won by Count Eugene Meyer!"

The Queen was the first to applaud. Others followed suit and applauded.

The auction continued. The nobles put out their treasured items, and the entrepreneurs put out their company's new products and limited editions.

I spent money moderately and won items that I didn't need right away.

,The important thing is to spend money.'

I didn't bid on the Princess and Princes' items.

I, who had been flaunting my wealth by spending money like water, closed my mouth. Those gathered here were basically talented people. Of course, they couldn't help but notice the intention.

I have no interest in the succession battle. I'm neutral.

A signal that you're an idiot if you don't know.

The Queen smiled contentedly, and the expressions of the Princes and Princesses hardened.

"Next is an item from His Excellency Count Eugene Meyer! It's a metal called Adarium! It's even an Adamantium alloy! This Adarium has the ability to block all kinds of energy!"

"...Blocks all energy? Mana, heat, cold, electricity, everything?"

"Not an artifact, but a metal?"

"So LK Corp. didn't just have superconductors...."

"If this is true... the world will go crazy again."

The banquet hall began to become noisy.
