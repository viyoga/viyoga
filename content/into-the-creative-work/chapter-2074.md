,I was originally supposed to be knighted by Lord Dominic, but he died in battle before I could receive it. I had no choice but to miss out."

I overheard while touring the city that Lord Dominic died in a rebellion three months ago.

If I thought deeply and sent someone to investigate, my lie could be exposed immediately. But what's important now isn't this lie. With all the experience I've accumulated, how could I not anticipate Count Morck's thoughts?

,...You had no choice but to miss out, huh? Then you're not a knight?"

,Even without a letter of appointment, I hold chivalry in my heart, so I am a knight."

,...Hmm. I see."

A sophistry that wouldn't normally work.

But he knew my strength was extraordinary. He'd regret kicking me out.

,What I've learned wandering the Empire is that people are obsessed with material things. Many ignore me as a knight simply because I don't have a letter of appointment. Even though a true knight is one who embodies chivalry."

,Well, what can you do...? There are few knights as outstanding as you in this world. I've heard there are many cases of bandits impersonating knights."

,Yes. Those bastards should all be torn to shreds. Count-nim. Could you write me a letter of knighthood?"

Nobles have the right to knight people. Barons can do it once a year, viscounts three times a year, counts five times a year, marquises eight times, and dukes have no limit. It was stipulated by imperial law. Some nobles even made a business out of this right.

,Ahem. You're asking for a bit much. Knighthood isn't a right to be handed out willy-nilly."

The minimum conditions for knighthood are that the knight's martial prowess must be superhuman and they must have performed meritorious deeds.

,Of course, I'm not just making demands. That would go against my chivalry. I heard you're waging a territory war against Veroprin of the Solite Republic. I will participate and help you."

,The feeling of gaining Sir is like gaining a thousand troops and horses! But let me correct you, Veroprin is a neutral city. It hasn't officially become territory of the Solite Republic yet."

Morck drew a firm line. It must be a political issue.

,A question arises for a moment. Is the Archmage involved in this war?"

,No. The Solite Republic is the one mediating. And this isn't a war between nations, but a territory war. But we can't let our guard down because there's a possibility the Republic is pulling strings behind the scenes."

,Understood. I will do my best to ensure the Morck County wins this war."

,I understand. Is there anything you need?"

,Nothing much. My body is tired from a long wandering life, so I'd like two beauties and a servant to handle chores. Oh, and a clean, sunny room, please. I can't sleep well in unclean places.... I'd like the highest quality cooking. My tongue can't stand to swallow bad food. I don't need a personal mansion. Managing it would only be a hassle. I'll stay in the castle and protect you, Your Excellency. Oh, and I trust you'll take good care of my salary."

,......."

Count Morck glanced at the knights around him. His knights desperately pretended not to see. No one stepped forward. They realized I was stronger in the battle of wills just now.

,...Rest comfortably."

• • •

The interior of the room I was guided to by the servant was secondary; I liked that it was clean. The servant seemed bright, like he'd received basic education.

,Why aren't there two beauties?"

,I've sent someone to bring them. They should arrive before sunset."

,Bringing them from outside? Sounds like you're bringing prostitutes."

,You absolutely won't be disappointed."

The servant said nervously.

I nodded, though I was a little dissatisfied. There's no way there would be beauties scattered around the castle. I decided to be understanding to some extent.

,You can leave now."

,The armor...."

,I'll take care of it myself."

,Yes. Please call me anytime if you need anything."

The servant bowed and left the room. I rested in the room for a while before going outside.

I felt the gaze of surveillance sticking to me. I didn't care. Count Morck is doing this because he doesn't know about me. Count Morck must think I'm very suspicious.

,Sir Daykert."

A knight I happened to run into called out to me. One of the knights who was around Count Morck.

,...What is your name?"

,Rakgus. Call me Rakgus. I don't have a surname because I'm from a slave background."

,You were from a slave background? That's amazing."

The Empire values strength, so even if you were born a slave, you could become a knight or noble if you were strong. That's why it's also called the Empire of Opportunity. However, such things are very rare. The barriers of status were very high.

Rakgus smiled contentedly at the small compliment. My gaze went to the scar on his cheek.

,Are you curious about this scar on my right cheek? It was seven years ago. I was targeted by the Black Rain, a vicious group of assassins. This is a wound I got fighting a Black Rain assassin bastard. His sword was even coated with poison. If I had been even a little weaker then, it wouldn't have been him who died, but me. Do you know how I killed him? I split him in half from head to crotch."

Rakgus rattled off the words quickly. He was talkative for a knight.

,It must not be easy to deal with assassins, you're amazing."

,They're annoying bastards, though. But they're just cowardly assassins in the end. They're no match for us knights."

,That's right. But the war is just around the corner, isn't it? Is the war preparation going well?"

Veroprin was burning with fighting spirit throughout the city. On the other hand, it was quiet and gloomy here. It was difficult to know Count Morck's exact strength.

,Of course. Our Dread Knight Order consists of 52 knights. Adding Sir Daykert, that makes 53. And 10,000 soldiers have been conscripted and are receiving basic training. Adding 3,000 slaves, that's a total of 13,000. The reason there are no knights in this castle right now is because they've left the castle to train the soldiers."

Compared to the medieval era on Earth, that's an enormous number of soldiers for a single territory. No, in the first place, the population of this territory is only about 200,000, but 13,000? That's insane. It's exactly right to say they're betting their lives on it.

,...No. If you only count the population, Veroprin, which is similar, is also gathering about 10,000 people."

It was something I heard directly while helping Park Suho buy food. That they would conscript 10,000 people.

,Is it normal in this world to conscript so many people?"

I don't know even if I rack my brain.

I decided to assume that's how it is since it's a world where there are gods, magic is rampant, and monsters roam around.

,What is the soldiers' armament status?"

,Most of them are conscripts, so it's so-so. His Excellency the Count has decided to provide weapons. They'll have to acquire armor separately, though.... Just by providing weapons, the soldiers will be grateful to His Excellency the Count."

,Is His Excellency wealthy enough to provide weapons for over 10,000 soldiers?"

,...Um. I'll be honest. His Excellency borrowed money from all over the place. If we just win this territory war, we can pay it back. Doesn't increasing the population by 50,000 at once mean an increase in taxes too?"

I'm reminded once again that this is a territory war with 50,000 people on the line.

,Is there a place to accommodate those 50,000 people? What about food?"

Even if they receive additional war supplies, the sudden increase in population is a headache in itself.

,They'll live on their own. That's how it usually is. And His Excellency said he'd make money by selling about 20,000 as slaves."

,......Indeed. Aren't there regular troops? I'd like to see their training status and armament status."

,Haha. Sir Daykert's enthusiasm is amazing. Alright. I'll gladly show you, so follow me."

The soldiers were gathered at the training ground, conducting training. Still, as regular troops, they focused on training in formations and attacking while wearing armor.

,How is it? Isn't it amazing?"

,They're well-trained regular soldiers. I'm impressed."

I gave a bitter smile. Armor full of scratches, old swords and spears stained with dirt, and shields. It wasn't trustworthy.

Training? It was obvious they were doing it half-heartedly. They're better than the conscripts, but they're not good enough to be called elite. They don't seem to have much combat experience either.

,Park Suho said he's even hiring mercenaries. If the training status is so-so, then what's important is the armament status and high-level combat personnel...."

Park Suho is ahead in everything. Dwarf blacksmiths, elf archers, mages above a certain level, and even priests. Park Suho's city was full of high-level personnel.

,......Are there no mages in the territory?"

,Mages? There were about three. Those honor-less spellcasters who betrayed us and stuck to Veroprin! I swear on my name that I will definitely kill those spellcasters!"

,Betrayed?"

,That's right. The mayor of Veroprin came to the territory with the purpose of friendship... that bastard killed the young master and ran away with the slaves and mages."

It seems Park Suho not only killed the successor and freed the slaves, but also took away high-level personnel.

,His name was Suho, wasn't it? That mayor with a really strange name is a vicious bastard who doesn't even know the meaning of honor. The Republic is protecting such a bastard, so it's gone completely to the dogs. I guarantee the Republic won't last even 10 years."

Rakgus moved his mouth non-stop, cursing Park Suho, Veroprin, and the Solite Republic.

I listened to his words with one ear and let them out with the other, thinking seriously.

,We can't win this by fighting head-on."

If it were a war between ordinary humans without superhuman abilities, it would be doable. This side has an additional 3,000 slave soldiers.

,It's a fantasy where common sense doesn't apply. I can't even understand fighting with 10,000 people in a territory war...."

Before discussing common sense, a special measure was needed.

I put on my dark cloak in the dark dawn and used a space movement scroll to move to Veroprin.

And I poisoned the well that the soldiers were using.
