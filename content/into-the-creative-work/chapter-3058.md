Currently, I have 6,310 Heavenly Jade.

This is how much I've scraped together, clawing for every last one. Even including the 200 Heavenly Jade I received from the Heavenly River Star System when I reached the 5th stage of Harmony, this is all I've got.

'It's getting harder and harder to obtain Heavenly Jade.'

Heavenly Jade is typically a bead formed from condensed pure spiritual energy of nature. In some ways, obtaining a single Heavenly Jade is harder than finding a spiritual elixir in the mountains. However, unlike elixirs, it doesn't directly aid in cultivation.

'It's used for crafting artifacts, setting up barriers, and maintaining them. To manufacture it artificially, you'd need to master a technique for handling pure natural energy, and at minimum reach the Three Purities realm. Even crafting a single Heavenly Jade takes a long time.'

It was impossible for me to craft Heavenly Jade directly. Neither the Thunder Heaven Style nor the Heavenly Demon Divine Art could produce Heavenly Jade.

'Damn it. I never thought Heavenly Jade would become this scarce.'

To advance to VIP Stage 8, I needed 8,000 Heavenly Jade. Needing 1,700 more was a considerable wall.

'Even the Imperial Family says they're short on Heavenly Jade... I suppose I have no choice but to wait.'

I took out the Radiant Ascension Blade. Inside it was the Heavenly Demon Holy Sword. I wanted to finish strengthening the Heavenly Demon Holy Sword soon.

'There was also Mi-ryeong's request to strengthen the Supreme Peace Arcane Tome.'

After some deliberation, I decided to use a few Heavenly Jade.

I activated the Heavenly Divination Star and the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil. I placed the Radiant Ascension Blade on the floor and used the Timeflow Spirit Guidance. I'd seen Mi-ryeong use it several times, and had even received direct instruction on its essentials, so I could use the Timeflow Spirit Guidance.

Each Heavenly Jade accelerates time by 10 years.

After consuming a total of 3 Heavenly Jade to accelerate 30 years, the strengthening of the Heavenly Demon Holy Sword was complete. I placed it in my inventory and appraised it.

[Heavenly Demon Holy Sword

A sword crafted by a master artisan of immense power, imitating the power of a star.

‣ Mass Manipulation

The sword's mass can be manipulated up to a maximum of ten million geun. Consumes mana.

‣ Size Manipulation

The sword's size can be manipulated according to the user's capability. Consumes mana.]

The mass that could originally be manipulated up to a hundred thousand geun had increased by a whopping 100 times.

'Ten million geun is... about 6,000 tons.'

With this strengthening, it could now manipulate size as well.

'No matter how strong I am, swinging a 6,000-ton sword directly would be difficult. But firing it from the inventory has no such constraint. In other words, I can use it like a 6,000-ton arrow.'

6,000 tons could itself be called a mass weapon.

'It got strengthened amazingly. I've got a good feeling about this.'

Strengthening the Radiant Ascension Blade had a gacha-like feel to it, since you never knew how it would turn out.

'Should I use some Heavenly Jade to strengthen a few more things?'

I know that in the long term, it's right to save as many Heavenly Jade as possible, even one. But when have I ever acted purely on reason? I decided to throw various things into the Radiant Ascension Blade and use the Timeflow Spirit Guidance to strengthen them.

'Let's use exactly 10 Heavenly Jade. ...But what happens if I strengthen Heavenly Jade with the Radiant Ascension Blade?'

To resolve the curiosity that suddenly arose, I put Heavenly Jade into the Radiant Ascension Blade.

I used the Timeflow Spirit Guidance and waited 1 hour. That accelerated 10 years. Even so, the strengthening was still in progress in the Radiant Ascension Blade.

'10 years isn't enough, huh.'

This got my competitive spirit going. I used additional Heavenly Jade. In the end, I consumed 12 Heavenly Jade. 13 hours. Only after strengthening for a total of 130 years did it finish.

'Damn. I was going to use exactly 10...'

It couldn't be helped.

I felt anticipation. The strengthening time of the Radiant Ascension Blade and the result don't have a strong correlation. Just because the strengthening takes a long time doesn't necessarily mean the result will be good.

'But the longer the strengthening takes, the higher my expectations rise.'

I took out the strengthened Heavenly Jade, examined it with the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, and was shocked.

'Heavenly Gang Jade! A jade that can strengthen items!'

It was a treasure with strengthening abilities similar to the Radiant Ascension Blade. Unlike the Radiant Ascension Blade, it was single-use. On the other hand, unlike the Radiant Ascension Blade, it had no strengthening time. Use it, and it strengthens immediately.

'Should a treasure like this even exist?! I'm using it right now!'

The target was, of course, the Radiant Ascension Blade. How amazing would a strengthened Radiant Ascension Blade be?

I pushed the glowing Heavenly Gang Jade into the Radiant Ascension Blade. The Heavenly Gang Jade bounced off and rolled on the floor.

"...It won't work."

I'd had some intuition it would be this way, so I wasn't greatly disappointed. The Radiant Ascension Blade was simply a thing of an entirely different caliber.

"What about the Flaming Lotus Dart or Stormbreaker?"

They failed.

It wasn't because of an overwhelming difference in caliber like with the Radiant Ascension Blade.

'How should I put it. Compatibility? No, it feels like a kind of channel mismatch. Stormbreaker was reforged in this world, but... is it the essence that's the problem?'

Thanks to the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, I could quickly tell what the problem was.

'Only items containing this world's spiritual energy can be strengthened.'

Unlike the Radiant Ascension Blade, items from other worlds couldn't be strengthened.

After pondering for a moment, I took out the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar and pushed the Heavenly Gang Jade into it. The Heavenly Gang Jade didn't trigger a rejection response.

KWAJIJIJIK!

The strengthened Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar spewed ferocious lightning. It was so intense that I couldn't handle it for even a moment and dropped the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar from my hand. It didn't fall, but flashed in midair.

I stared blankly at the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar emitting blue-white electric currents, as if entranced. The thunder energy within me surged.

The Thunder Heaven Art was being drawn to the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar, and the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar was being drawn to the Thunder Heaven Art.

As a bonus, the Thunder Heaven Art saw the world inside the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar. A world where lightning simply struck down.

'A spirit altar without even a soul contains a mental realm? Is this possible?'

I stared blankly at the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar, then suddenly curled the corners of my mouth upward.

'As the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar was strengthened by the Heavenly Gang Jade, its essence itself partially changed. There were similar cases in the original work.'

Even if it had already been strengthened by the Radiant Ascension Blade, if its essence changes, it's treated as an entirely different item. In other words, it can be strengthened by the Radiant Ascension Blade again.

I grabbed the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar and put it into the Radiant Ascension Blade.

PAJIJIJIJIK!

The Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar resisted along the way, but I ignored it.

Having successfully placed the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar into the Radiant Ascension Blade, I checked my right hand. My body is one that feels nothing even when struck by falling lightning. Yet my right hand was charred black. I even felt a searing pain. It was different from electrocution. I'm immune to electrocution in the first place.

'When the strengthening of the Radiant Ascension Blade finishes... I can't wait to see what happens to the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.'

I used the Timeflow Spirit Guidance.

230 Heavenly Jade were consumed. 230 hours. The accelerated time was a whopping 2,300 years.

'The bleed is bigger than expected.'

Without hiding my anticipation, I took the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar out of the Radiant Ascension Blade.

KWAAAAAANG!

The shockwave that erupted the moment I took out the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar sent me flying backward, rolling on the floor several times. The Radiant Ascension Blade I'd been holding flew far away and fell to the ground.

The Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar floated in midair, spewing blue lightning. The lightning didn't extend beyond a certain distance from the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.

BANG!

A maid opened the door and entered. She must have heard the explosion from inside the room.

"Heavenly Demon Lord?!"

"Don't come! Get out right now! Approach is forbidden! Tell the others as well!"

"Yes, Heavenly Demon Lord!"

The maid followed the order well, as she'd been trained to.

I slowly raised myself and glared at the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.

I could see the mental realm it contained. It wasn't the mental realm of lightning striking down that I'd seen before. All kinds of lightning had gathered and were writhing. Even more, each bolt of that lightning was extraordinary.

"Heavenly Thunder, is it."

It was on a different level from lightning occurring in nature. Heavenly Thunder contained the power of heaven itself, a heavenly tribulation to punish those who would defy the heavens.

'Absorbing that... is madness at my current level. But I can't just leave it either. I need to put it in the inventory, but...'

Reverse summoning doesn't work.

It was likely because its essence had changed from the original Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar. So I had to grab it directly and put it in the inventory.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Heaven-Shattering Cataclysm.

Becoming the Transcendent Heavenly Demon state with hair turned pure white, I charged at the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

The Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step was meaningless. The Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar's power was outstanding, and though it contained a mental realm, it wasn't a living being.

Spatial movement was impossible. This space was already dominated by the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.

As I charged, a bolt of lightning lashed out like a whip. From top to bottom. Instead of dodging, I extended my right hand.

'That's lightning. If it's me, I can control lightning in reverse! If it's me!'

KWAJIK!

It was impossible.

My right arm flew off. My soul was wounded. No, something far more fundamental... my very existence as 'me' was wounded.

'Even with Heaven-Shattering Cataclysm active, this is the result?'

The next bolt of lightning flew in.

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 11]

I used Flash. I couldn't dodge completely. My right leg was torn off. Even though I'd protected myself with body-protecting qi, it was meaningless. Fortunately, the distance was close. Gritting my teeth, I advanced and grabbed the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar with my left hand.

『Warning! The probability of Ascension success is below 0.1%!』

I ignored the message that suddenly appeared before my eyes and put the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar into the inventory.

PAJIJIJIK!

The lightning the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar had been emitting burst my left hand, traveled up my arm, and burrowed into my heart. Heavenly Thunder doesn't just destroy the body—it bursts through my mental realm as well. The only thing intact was my mind.

[Death Resistance activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

I felt my body burning and my mental realm forcibly collapsing.

'To think I'd be helpless like this even with Heaven-Shattering Cataclysm active.'

Recalling the notification window from the Heavenly River Star System that had appeared earlier, I checked it belatedly.

The reason I'd been putting off consuming the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar was because it was the key to Ascension.

The Thunder Heaven Art, which contained a fragment of Ascension, had been continuously reacting to the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar.

[Using Full Recovery.]

Returning to my original state, I scratched my head.

The Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar stored in the inventory was a bomb. The moment anything approaches within 2 meters, it indiscriminately attacks with Heavenly Thunder regardless of who the target is.

'If I use the inventory's firing function, it might be usable as a weapon. The problem is that it's uncontrollable. In the worst case, reverse summoning might not work.'

There was a high chance reverse summoning would work. I had, after all, put the Ten Thousand Thunder God Altar into the inventory.

'Since it's an item of a different order, I can't be certain reverse summoning will work. The risk of testing it is too great... What's certain is that I can't handle it at my current level, so at the very least, the best course is not to touch it until I reach the Myriad Phenomena realm.'

I checked the wrecked room and once again grasped the Radiant Ascension Blade and Heavenly Jade in my hands.

'I should do what needs doing. If I can obtain more Heavenly Gang Jade, it's a huge gain.'

I strengthened 5 Heavenly Jade with the Radiant Ascension Blade. The Heavenly Jade's spiritual energy grew stronger, but it didn't become Heavenly Gang Jade.

'Ha. Even putting in identical Heavenly Jade gives different results. This is a troublesome item too.'

I used over 300 Heavenly Jade.

Shaking my head, I put the Supreme Peace Arcane Tome meant for Mi-ryeong into the Radiant Ascension Blade.

[Ending the game.]
