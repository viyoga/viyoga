I used the return stone to return to the Valentier Sanctuary.

As always, the Saintess was in the sanctuary. However, the atmosphere of the sanctuary was different from usual. She, who always maintained her composure, was moving busily, preparing something. There were unfamiliar objects around the sacred pond. Things like sculptures and jewels, all imbued with divine power. In other words, holy relics.

I approached her and said,

,Amelia. Things have gone wrong. I killed Colossus, but the Great Demon Bleat appeared, leading tens of thousands of demons. All the paladins except me are dead. This place is also dangerous. Bleat is aiming for the resurrection of the Great Demons. In other words, he will lead the demonic forces to advance in order to kill you and me."

The Saintess was calm.

,I know."

,You know...?"

,The Goddess has given me a revelation. The preparation for the purification ritual is almost complete. Please enter the sacred pond."

,What are those things around the sacred pond?"

,There is no time, so I decided to use holy relics for this purification ritual."

I quickly took off my clothes and walked into the sacred pond.

As I did, I became worried.

Unlike the original game, the purification ritual does not happen instantly. And right now, I was suffering from the souls of the Great Demon, as well as thousands of demon souls. If I proceeded with the purification ritual normally, it could take at least a week.

,How many hours will the purification ritual take?"

,I brought holy relics to reduce the time as much as possible. I will try to finish the purification ritual within a day."

The Saintess said with a determined voice.

I laid down in the sacred pond. I felt my body becoming comfortable. The momentum of the aura of corruption that had been eating away at my body weakened.

Through the clear water, I could see the Saintess offering prayers to the Goddess Herianne. The immense divine power she possessed touched the holy water.

I forcibly closed my eyes. There was nothing I could do by moving impatiently here. The more urgent things are, the more carefully I have to do them. The first step is the purification ritual.

,Fortunately, Valentier is far away from where the Great Demon Colossus was. Even if those demon bastards advance without rest for a day, it will take them several days to arrive.'

There was still time to spare.

•••

The purification ritual ended in a day.

I rose from the sacred pond. I saw the shattered holy relics around the sacred pond and Amelia collapsed.

I held Amelia, who had fallen asleep exhausted, in my arms. On her back was drawn a lizard picture, signifying the soul of the Great Demon Colossus.

,I have to get out of here. Bleat will relentlessly target me and Amelia. Especially Amelia.'

Unlike me, Amelia had no combat power. It would be difficult to withstand if the Great Demon Bleat deliberately targeted her. But I can't always stick with her. The best thing to do is to hide from Bleat's eyes, as the paladin of Zigoras said.

,The place to escape to... it's better to be as similar to the sanctuary as possible.'

If it were another paladin, it would have been very difficult. But I had the information and knowledge of the original game. I know a few special places that Bleat doesn't know.

I gritted my teeth.

The demon bastards will invade Valentier. The citizens of Valentier will be massacred by the demons. I don't like the fact that I have to run away knowing that. But I couldn't help it. There was too much at stake to make a dangerous choice just because I didn't like it.

,Demons can't annihilate humans. Because humans are their food.'

Human souls and bodies. All were the demons' prey. Demons also have brains. There is little benefit for demons to exterminate humans. Bleat's top priority would be to kill me and the Saintess and liberate the souls of the Great Demons. Secondarily, it would be to kill the momentum of the humans.

,It won't be as you wish, Bleat.'

After escaping from Valentier, I ran through the forest path with Amelia in my arms. The speed was much faster than riding a horse. Because my physical abilities had surpassed those of a human.

While running through the forest path, I saw a corrupted tree.

I couldn't just pass by the corrupted beast. I slightly changed direction and kicked the body of the corrupted beast, and stomped on the head of the fallen bastard to burst it.

,I realized I was being attacked just before I was hit. The effect of the cloak is excellent.'

Kiiiiiiing.

Holy Sight is activated, realizing the presence of corrupted trees nearby.

I gritted my teeth. I wanted to find them all and kill them, but what's important now is to take Amelia and escape to a safe place. I shouldn't mistake the priority of things.

After running for two days without rest, I arrived at my destination.

Mountain.

It was also an active volcano where volcanic activity was in progress.

There are no demons here. Even if they were demons, it would be difficult to withstand the hot heat emitted by the active volcano. Moreover, there were no humans nearby, who could be said to be the demons' playthings and food. Above all, this was the territory of an existence that even demons could not ignore.

,There is also a place underground that can replace the sanctuary.'

A long time ago.

Until the volcano became active, this place was one of the sacred sites of the Herianne Order.

I slowly walked around the volcano and found a cave. As soon as I found the cave, I went inside. In the deepest part of the cave, I dug the ground with the Execution Sword. No, it felt more like cutting through the rock. Because the ground here is made of solid rock.

After cutting and breaking the ground with the sword, a hole appeared. Without delay, I went down to the underground with the Saintess in my arms.

I landed on the floor about 10m away.

Originally, the temperature should be hot. Because this place is inside an active volcano. But the temperature inside was surprisingly more comfortable than outside.

,Kureureureureu...."

The howling of a beast echoes through the space. I firmly embraced the Saintess with my left hand, and prepared for battle with the Execution Sword in my right hand.

If I carelessly put the Saintess down on the floor, she might die in the worst case. It was much better to fight while holding her, even if it was a little uncomfortable.

The corrupted divine beast, Tegias.

,Whether it's a divine beast or whatever, if it's corrupted, it has to die.'

Tegias, feeling the murderous intent, kicked off the ground and jumped. Its massive body falls towards me.

,Sprint.'

,Light Shackles.'

Light appears and wraps around Tegias' body. The bound Tegias twitched in place. The Light Shackles don't last long. That might be the case for ordinary demons. Tegias is a monstrous bastard who chews up and eats even decent demons.

Immediately after, I strengthened my abilities by bursting Holy Aura, strengthened the Execution Sword with the Divine Sword, and stabbed the Execution Sword into the top of the bastard's head.
