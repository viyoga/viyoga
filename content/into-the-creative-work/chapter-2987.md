I entered the Blue House with Baek Ji-eun. The first thing I felt was a sense of incongruity. A feeling of my senses dulling for a moment.

‘Is it a Barrier?’

It wasn’t particularly surprising.

If the place where the Leader of the nation resided was shoddy, I would have been disappointed instead.

“It’s stifling, isn’t it? But bear with it. It’s for national security.”

“I haven’t said anything yet.”

Baek Ji-eun treated me like a bomb that could explode at any moment.

Before entering the Blue House building, I was frisked by men in black military uniforms. They wore military uniforms, but their Weapons were not guns, but cold Weapons like Swords or spears. Their faces were even covered with Masks.

‘Are they a special forces unit composed of Hunters? All of them are A-rank or higher.’

It was natural, as a special forces unit composed of Laypersons couldn’t handle even one A-rank Hunter.

‘Is this kind of frisking meaningful?’

I have an Inventory. I also have Ability Users related to subspace, and abilities that exert 100% efficiency even without Weapons. Especially, they couldn’t possibly not know that me pulling out Weapons from thin air was even recorded on video.

The frisking ended with a cursory glance over me and Baek Ji-eun.

‘Seeing them do it half-heartedly, it seems it was a dog-shit act ordered from above.’

They also knew that the frisking was a meaningless dog-shit act. If they had Grope my Body or Baek Ji-eun’s Body? My Fist would have immediately flown out and struck their Heads.

“Please do not use Mana or abilities inside. Things will get complicated. We will guide you to the reception Room where the President is waiting.”

“Has the Blue House ever been terrorized?”

“Surprisingly, quite often. Most of them are subdued at the entrance before we even have to step in, though.”

“It’s never mentioned in the news, though?”

“It’s reported as rioting citizens, not terrorism. The incidents are suppressed for reasons like national security and Awakened prejudice. Because if the image of the Awakened deteriorates due to a few Crazy Bastards, it’s bad in many ways. Ah, of course, they are properly punished. This is also confidential, but I’m telling you because you’re Hunter Seong Yujin. The nation has no intention of antagonizing Hunter Seong Yujin.”

I could feel them giving me a Hint.

‘As I revealed S-rank Hunter-level power, the treatment definitely changed.’

Isn’t her academic background too low? To quibble about her academic background, she had done too much so far.

‘Or rather, perhaps due to the power of history alteration, people didn’t care about the President’s academic background. As if they were under Hypnosis.’

Just as I had enjoyed Super Peak Realm popularity in high school but became ordinary in college. It was because an unknown power had intervened in the history alteration.

“Yes. It’s a pleasure.”

I did not refuse her handshake.

It was a bit awkward, though. As the masculine name Kang In-seop proved, she was originally a man.

But historically, it wasn’t particularly strange. There were quite a few female historical figures with masculine names. King Arthur is a prime example.

“Coke.”

“…Coffee is fine for me, Your Excellency.”

‘Unlike the luxurious-looking chair, it’s not very comfortable.’

Sitting in the chair, I looked at the ceiling, not the President.

I felt a presence above the ceiling. I don’t know what they’re doing up in the ceiling, they’re not even ninjas.

She probably became President for a purpose I didn’t know, and that purpose had already been achieved. As she seemed to have no lingering attachment to power.
