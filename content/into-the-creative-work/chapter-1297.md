I went outside and immediately activated the Celestial Eye.

I discovered a dark energy flowing from all over the barracks. It was different from mana. The energies were rising into the sky and gathering in one place.

"Hehehe."

A three-headed demon was there, clutching its stomach and laughing.

A pair of red horns sprouted from its forehead, a long, arrow-shaped black tail, and black skin made it unmistakably a demon. Its overall appearance was similar to a goblin.

'I can't believe it's doing this so openly.'

The identity of the dark energy the incubus was absorbing suddenly came to mind. Corrupted mana. Although the concentration was weaker than the corrupted mana I knew, it was definitely corrupted mana.

'Judging by the fact that no one else seems to notice it... does it also have the ability to hide itself?'

I pondered.

The incubus was being careless. It thought its presence hadn't been detected. How could I launch a perfect surprise attack on the incubus?

'If everyone is busy... if only I take a fighting stance, it will notice right away.'

After much deliberation, there was only one way.

To drop lightning on it.

I focused my mind. My mana reacted and rose into the sky. Fortunately, the incubus didn't sense anything strange.

Just before dropping the lightning, the incubus, which had been sneering at the soldiers, tilted its head, looking at the sky as if it had sensed something. Fortunately, it didn't recognize the attack, perhaps because it wasn't magic.

'Taste some lightning!'

A streak of blue lightning fell from the pitch-black night sky onto the incubus.

The incubus, struck by the lightning, fell downward. Only then did the thunder belatedly roar.

'Should I go finish it off?'

I was walking with the Hwaryeon Bido in hand when I stopped. The incubus, which had fallen to the ground, rose into the sky again. And it looked directly at me with red eyes. It seemed to have sensed my killing intent and realized I was the culprit.

'Is it going to run?'

The incubus looked weak on the surface. But contrary to my expectations, the incubus didn't run. It bared its sharp teeth and took a fighting stance.

I gripped the Hwaryeon Bido, enveloped in an aura blade, and charged at the incubus. The incubus reached out with both hands towards me. Black smoke billowed from the incubus's hands.

'Acceleration, Instant.'

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 6]

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 5]

I used the skills to avoid the black smoke. Several soldiers nearby were hit by the black smoke. The soldiers collapsed to the ground like puppets with their strings cut, falling asleep.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaak!"

And they screamed and writhed. I didn't know what principle this attack was based on.

'Just don't get hit.'

I jumped towards the incubus floating in the air and swung my sword. The incubus rose higher, dodging my attack.

Pajijik.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Empty Road (虛道).

I used lightning to create a foothold in the air. I stepped on the foothold and jumped. The incubus's face twisted in shock.

The sword touched the incubus's body. The sword aimed for the incubus's torso, but only managed to cut its shoulder.

The aura blade could easily cut through steel like tofu, but the demon's skin was tougher than steel.

'It's okay. I can just attack again.'

Pajijik.

I stepped on the foothold created with lightning and charged at the incubus again.

"Kkieeeeeeeek!"

The incubus screamed. The high-pitched voice made me involuntarily frown. I couldn't tell if it was a scream or a sonic attack. Judging by its pained face, it was probably closer to a scream.

"Shut up. Just die quietly! This is the price for disturbing my pleasure!"

It was the moment I was about to swing my sword. The incubus's body swelled up and then exploded. Black smoke clung to my body.

'...I'm screwed.'

It wasn't carelessness. I was simply caught off guard by the incubus's self-destruction.

My whole body was enveloped in black smoke, and I fell to the ground. Except for the fact that my vision was limited by the black smoke, I felt nothing. It didn't hurt, and I could move my limbs properly.

Kwang!

I fell to the ground. The impact came from my back. I managed to perform a rough landing, so the damage wasn't significant.

'What? The black smoke isn't falling off.'

The black smoke... something fundamentally different from corrupted mana, wriggled as if it were alive. When I didn't react, it seemed as if it was panicking like a living creature.

I focused my mind on the Celestial Eye and stared intently at the black smoke. I saw the incubus's presence. Or rather, it would be more accurate to say I felt it.

'This black smoke was its essence.'

The incubus is a demon related to dreams. So, this black smoke must also be something related to dreams.

'Lightning.'

I created lightning. The blue lightning that erupted from my body spread in all directions, electrocuting the black smoke. The black smoke surged greatly, then gathered in one place and took the form of the incubus. The incubus, missing one arm, staggered and fell to the ground.

I looked at the incubus and pondered for a moment.

Should I kill the incubus here? Or should I keep it alive and extract information?

'Demon bastards are so tough that it takes days for them to open their mouths even with torture. This one looks weak, so it probably doesn't know much information.'

I don't need trivial information. Even without such information, there's no problem in pushing back the enemies.

I raised the Hwaryeon Bido and struck the incubus's neck. The incubus's head fell to the ground along with black blood.

"Waaaaaaaah!"

Cheers erupted from the surroundings.

Surprised by the unexpected cheers, I looked around. I had been so focused on the incubus that I hadn't noticed the soldiers gathered around me.

"The Commander-nim has beheaded the demon!!"

"The Commander-nim has killed the demon and established justice!!!"

"Eugene Prukerus!! Eugene Prukerus!!!"

The soldiers' cheers were quite good.

I smiled and grabbed the demon's head, lifting it high. The cheers grew louder. I felt good hearing my name spread far and wide.

"You there. Soldier."

"Yes, Commander-nim!"

"Hang the demon's head on a pole so everyone can see it! These goddamn demons can't stop us! We are the army of the Goddess Serion who will save the continent from the demons!"

"The Commander-nim's words are correct! We are the army of the Goddess Serion!"

I patted the soldier's shoulder lightly with a satisfied expression and headed towards the carriage. I had a heroic tale to tell Yuria.

Dawn broke.

I woke up around noon in Yuria's arms. I had intended to wake up in the morning... but strangely, I didn't want to wake up in the morning. I was a man who did as he pleased.

I came out of the carriage.

A strange tension was in the air. The army was already prepared for battle.

Floi and Scarlet approached me.

I looked at Scarlet and asked.

"How many soldiers were affected by the incubus yesterday?"

"322 have died, and 15 are still in a coma. The mages say they will wake up by this evening at the latest."

"Not much damage."

The troops I brought numbered over 120,000. Including the slave soldiers captured as prisoners, 300 deaths were negligible.

"The siege preparations are all complete, right?"

"They are complete. You only need to give the order, my lord."

"What about Sharnel and the mages?"

"They have been deployed to the rear. The Plune Knights are escorting the mages."

The Plune Knights were a knight order belonging to the Prukerus County. As I became the Count of Prukerus, the Plune Knights naturally became mine as well.

I summoned Stormbreaker and put on the armor. Floi brought the horse, and Scarlet attached a red cloak to my shoulders.

"Floi. It's a mare, of course, right?"

"Of course, my lord."

I only mounted the horse after confirming its pussy. I don't ride stallions.

I pointed the tip of my sword at the enemy's castle walls and shouted.

"Charge!!!"

"Uwaaaaaaaaah!"

The slave soldiers with swords and shields charged towards the castle walls. The archers on the walls fired arrows all at once. The blue sky was dyed black by the arrows.

Our mages used wind magic. A large number of arrows, which had been falling to the ground in curves, collided with the strong wind and shattered. The arrows rained down on the ground.

The slave soldiers hooked their legs on the walls and climbed up. Of course, it didn't always go as planned. The enemies poured hot water or shot arrows, fiercely resisting. And at some point, the mages on the walls began to use magic against the slave soldiers.

The ground shook, and pillars of fire soared.

"The mages have finally stepped up. It's our turn too."

I said. Scarlet, who was next to me, conveyed the order to our mages through a magic tool.

A moment later, Sharnel's Hellfire and hundreds of fireballs crossed over our heads and bombarded the castle walls. The mages on the walls hurriedly created water barriers with magic or responded with defensive magic. It would have been different if they had prepared from the beginning. The mages, who had been exhausted dealing with the climbing slave soldiers, couldn't stop the magical bombardment.

Kwakwakwakwang!

With a huge explosion, the castle gate and walls collapsed. Not all the walls collapsed, but that alone determined the victory or defeat of the war.

"Floi! Let's go!"

"Yes, my lord! Everyone, follow my lord!!"

I grabbed the reins and ran. The Golden Rose Knights followed behind me, and elite soldiers followed behind them.

'Casiod! Your head is mine!'

I urged the horse and crossed the collapsed castle gate. The inside was already in the midst of battle. Everywhere was burning, and screams never ceased.

I ran towards the center of the city. The lord is usually in the center of the city, in the largest building. If not, he's in the back.

"Count Prukerus!!"

"You can't pass here!!"

Two knights stood in my way.

Floi didn't even need to step in. I cut down the two knights, armor and all, with my sword imbued with an aura blade and moved forward.

In front of the inner castle's main gate. Dozens of knights, including Casiod, were visible.

"Casiod! I've come!!"
