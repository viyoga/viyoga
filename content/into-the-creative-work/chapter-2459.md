I entered the S-class dungeon.

Originally, this dungeon was a closed-type S-class dungeon. Normally, once a raid team had entered, no one else could go in, but with the dungeon break, anyone could enter.

,The dungeon name isn't showing up. It's definitely a mess in there.'

Tiger Raptors near the entrance charged at us. I didn't even need to step in. The A-class Hunters who had entered first were already dealing with the Tiger Raptors, slaughtering them. Even though they were A-class monsters, the Hunters here were the elite of the elite. This was nothing to them.

The most outstanding Hunter was Kwon Ilchan.

"Euaaaaaaaagh!"

He roared and swung his fist at a Tiger Raptor. With a single punch, the Tiger Raptor's body exploded. The shockwave shattered the trees around it.

"Seong Yujin-ssi."

Baek Ji-eun called me. Her voice was stiff.

"Oh, Ji-eun-ah. I didn't say hello earlier because there were too many people."

"Listen carefully. My career is on the line with this operation. Don't act recklessly. Got it?"

Baek Ji-eun said seriously.

I nodded awkwardly with a smile.

"Okay."

"I'll trust you."

Baek Ji-eun stepped forward. She began to direct the Hunters. Or rather, it was more like coordinating them. Since they were all from different affiliations, they needed someone to take the lead. As a member of the Hunter Association, she was perfect for the job.

,Rescue, huh? No matter how I think about it, there's no hope.'

Just because the number of monsters coming out of the dungeon break was small didn't mean the raid team was alive. That was just wishful thinking. It was more likely that the boss monster inside the dungeon was preparing something.

,It's the raid team from Bluebird, one of the top 10 guilds. They must each have a Thread of Origin.'

Thread of Origin.

An item that allows for emergency escape from a dungeon, worth billions. A-class Hunters usually carry one as a backup. It's like having an extra life.

,The raid failed. But they're not escaping? That means they're all dead and unable to escape.'

It wouldn't be strange to rename the rescue team to a corpse recovery team.

,Well, it's all about image making. If they just sit still because it's obvious they're going to die, public opinion will turn sour.'

Baek Ji-eun and the Hunters were talking. From what I heard, they were deciding where to go.

"Usually, raid teams leave traces as they move. Sometimes, Hunters get separated during skirmishes. But now, it's hard to find traces due to the dungeon break. At this rate, the dungeon will completely collapse within three hours."

Baek Ji-eun said. The Hunters didn't argue. Even the experienced ones knew they had only three hours at most. Time was running out faster than they thought. Wandering around aimlessly was the worst thing they could do.

"I'll find the direction. I have a sensing skill."

All eyes turned to me.

"Wasn't your ability to control lightning?"

Choi Seojeong asked.

"Lightning is electricity. Electricity has many uses. I can use it like radar."

"That could save us time. Please."

I stepped into the air and rose into the sky.

Aside from radar, I could get a lot of information by looking around from the sky. I could also get a rough idea of the location.

,It's burning everywhere. Nothing special.'

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Wave.

Electromagnetic waves mixed with mana and lightning spread out from me.

With my increased mana stat, I could now perceive about 1,000 meters. Information from the surroundings entered my mind.

I already knew there was no raid team within 1km. What I needed to find were traces.

,The dungeon entrance doesn't change even with a dungeon break. The raid team's starting point must have been here too.'

I frowned. 99% of the information was useless. From the worms under the soil to each and every leaf, information flooded in. If I hadn't been mentally stable, my head might have exploded.

,Found it.'

I dropped to the ground.

"I found traces. It's to the right. I'll lead the way with my sensing skill."

"...Please do."

Choi Seojeong said. The other Hunters agreed. Kwon Ilchan was silent.

I led them forward. The electromagnetic waves caught them. I said nonchalantly.

"Four Tiger Raptors attacking from behind."

"I'll take care of it."

Kwon Ilchan moved alone. Kwaang! Kwang! Two explosions rang out, and the four Tiger Raptors were corpses rolling on the ground. No one collected the magic stones. Magic stones weren't the priority right now.

I found a discarded snack bag. It was clearly a modern item. The raid team had passed through here.

"We've got a rough direction. Then from here... let's start running."

Starting with me, the Hunters began to run. Of course, I was in the lead.

"Two Curse Beasts on the left."

"I'll handle them."

Baek Ji-eun said. She waved her hand, and strands of light appeared, striking the monsters.

As soon as I sensed a monster, I called it out, and the Hunters nearby or who had the capacity quickly dealt with it. We couldn't ignore them and just run. Being surrounded would be the worst.

,This is crazy as a raid strategy.'

The standard raid method is to prioritize safety and gradually conquer the dungeon.

Fighting recklessly like this would cause damage to our side as well.

"Kueugh! My shoulder!"

One of the Hunters was seriously injured during the battle. His arm was still attached, but the wound was so deep that his shoulder bone was visible.

"It's my turn. Don't worry. I can heal you. There will be no aftereffects. On your body, that is."

Jeong Jihak, one of the guild masters of Seventier, stepped forward. He placed his hand on the wound and used his ability.

The Hunter opened his mouth wide and screamed.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaaaaaaaagh!!"

An ability that inflicts immense pain in exchange for restoring even severed limbs. The screams of the Hunter being treated were desperate. The effect was certain. His shoulder was healing at a rapid pace.

"It's okay. The pain is only temporary. It's better than dying."

"Kkeueueueueueuk!!"

The Hunter foamed at the mouth. Jeong Jihak remained calm.

"Even if it hurts enough to faint, you can't faint. That's a characteristic of my ability. Now, it's almost fully healed. I'll heal you without leaving a single scar."

"Kkieeeeeeeeeeeek!"

The Hunter screamed like he was no longer human. As Jeong Jihak had promised, he was healed without a single scar. His face was covered in sweat.

"T-Thank you, Jeong Jihak-ssi. Your healing ability is indeed as outstanding as your reputation."

"You can get hurt as much as you want as long as I'm here. I can heal any external wound."

"...I won't get hurt again."

Kang Seoksu, who was next to me, smiled contentedly as he looked at Jeong Jihak.

"Jeong Jihak's healing ability is the real deal. He's a talent we worked really hard to bring to Seventier."

"...I have potions, so I don't need to be healed."

"You'll change your mind if your life is in danger."

I had never been so grateful for having complete recovery.

We found the traces left by the raid team and discovered them within an hour. The raid team was in front of a stone hill.

Half of the raid team was dead, and half was alive. The survivors were sitting on a stone floor with a red magic circle drawn on it, sweating profusely.

"Hanung-ah!"

Kwon Ilchan ran to a man. His son, Kwon Hanung, the leader of Bluebird's 1st raid team, was alive.

,He's not dead?'

I was surprised.

,He's not dead, so why is he here?'

If it were me, I would have escaped the dungeon immediately.

"Father. We need the help of an S-class Hunter. Did you come with one?"

"No, we came alone. Our purpose is rescue. Let's retreat first."

"...We can't. We're currently sealing the boss monster. The moment we leave here... the boss monster will awaken and attack. Since a dungeon break has occurred, if we escape, this thing will appear in Korea."

"What kind of boss monster is it?"

"It's a Lava Golem. And it's a Lava Golem that's 50 meters tall. It spews toxic gas and lava from its body. Just its appearance in Korea will cause immense damage. The Lava Golem must be dealt with here."

I checked the corpses. They had died without bleeding. Now that I looked closer, it seemed they had died from poison.

,Most of them are B-class, with some A-class mixed in. A toxicity that A-class Hunters can't withstand. A normal person would only last a few minutes.'

If a Lava Golem appeared in the middle of Seoul, it would be a guaranteed disaster.

"A 50-meter Lava Golem... I've never heard of one that big. Is that why the raid failed?"

"...The dungeon's objective was to find and destroy the Lava Golem's core. We failed the raid, and the Lava Golem awakened."

"I asked what caused the failure."

"......Arrogance. It was just a Lava Golem. A mere 5-meter A-class monster."

"You deliberately didn't destroy the core. To kill the dungeon's real boss and receive more rewards."

"Yes. My companions and I were blinded by the promise of immense rewards if we killed the dungeon's real boss."

"...Did Seongmin create the sealing formation?"

"Seongmin drew the sealing formation before he died. Seongmin's wish was that the Lava Golem should never be allowed to go to Korea."

"...The raid failed. No need to hesitate."

"Yes. I'm sorry."

Everyone held their breath. Various calculations ran through their minds.

"The Lava Golem must be dealt with here."

"We have to hunt the Lava Golem here."

Kwon Ilchan and Choi Seojeong said at the same time.

I mentally agreed. A 50-meter giant spreading lava and toxic substances? Even if we ignore the lava, the toxic substances could contaminate hundreds of meters if the wind blew the right way. It would be like a biochemical terror attack in Seoul.

"Father. The Lava Golem is too tough. Our raid team's firepower couldn't even break one of its arms."

"I'm here. If we give up and retreat here... Bluebird will never be able to rise again."

"And the trust in the Ewha Guild will also drop. Well, the same goes for the evaluation of the other Hunters here."

"...Representing the Hunter Association, we must prevent the Lava Golem from appearing in Korea at all costs."

Choi Seojeong and Baek Ji-eun spoke in succession. If we suggested running away now, we'd be sons of bitches.

"The Hunters gathered here are the elite. We can do it."

Kang Seoksu said with a glint in his eyes.

We received information from the raid team, devised a plan, and began the attack.

The Bluebird raid team members who had been maintaining the seal moved away from the sealing formation.

The earth trembled. Boom boom boom! Red lava erupted from various parts of the earth and fell back down. The jungle began to burn everywhere.

We avoided the lava while focusing on the front. What we thought was a hill was the Lava Golem's head. It had been lying face down against the cliff.

Below the cliff was filled with red lava and bright yellow toxic substances. They were also flowing from its body. The bright yellow toxic substances, which had been suppressed by the seal, slowly rose up.

Thanks to that, it was difficult to even approach the 50-meter Lava Golem.

,Shibal. Golems are not my strong suit.'

Clicking my tongue, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and held it in my hand.
