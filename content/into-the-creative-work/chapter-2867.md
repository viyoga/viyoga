My gaze went to the dark clouds above, in the sky above the Giant Basila.

The thunder-filled dark clouds flashed with lightning, roaring like a beast. But unlike its appearance, there wasn't enough time to generate the output needed to kill the Giant Basila in one go.

,It just started resonating, and the dark clouds are on the small side.'

I wasn't disappointed.

Rather, it was what I wanted.

,The final attack to kill the Giant Basila will be Stormbreaker.'

That's how I can feed experience to Stormbreaker.

I reach out towards the dark clouds. Kwarururururung! The lightning in the dark clouds roared even more fiercely.

,With this level of output, Lightning God is impossible.'

Not that I was planning on using Lightning God, though.

I lower my palm. I've set the direction.

Lightning Heaven Style Ultimate Art, Lightning Heaven's Decree.

A massive amount of lightning falls from the sky towards the Giant Basila all at once. Blue streaks connect the ground and the dark clouds.

The blue lightning filled the dungeon world before the sound. Kwarururururururung! Immediately after, the sound of thunder is heard. If it were an ordinary person, not a Hunter, their eyes and ears might have gone blind and deaf.

Not all of the hundreds, thousands of lightning bolts fell on a single Giant Basila. More than 30% fell in the wrong places. That's because I don't control every single one of those lightning bolts. All I did was set the direction for the lightning to fall.

,But that alone turned the forest into scorched earth.'

Kung!

The Giant Basila, which the two Troids had taken as a host, sat down on the ground and fell into a groggy state. The afterglow of the lightning remained on its scorched body, writhing.

The Basilas they were controlling? They were swept away by the lightning and ran away. Some of them quickly fled to places where the flames couldn't reach, as if their control had been released. Whether it's an A-class Hunter or a monster, their bodies can't withstand fire, and they need to breathe.

I grabbed the shaft of Stormbreaker, kicked off the air, leaped into the sky, and appeared in front of the slumped Giant Basila.

The two twins who had merged with the Giant Basila's shoulder were also not in a normal state. One of them opens his mouth.

,Why are you doing this to us? We haven't done anything yet."

His voice was filled with injustice.

I laughed at him.

,You've already eaten humans, and you say you haven't done anything? Did your conscience explode?"

,Isn't it natural for a lion to eat a rabbit?"

,It's also natural for humanity to kill dangerous monsters."

,......."

He closed his mouth, as if he had nothing to say.

Conversely, the other guy opened his mouth.

,Are you going to capture us alive?"

,It sounds like you're begging me to capture you alive."

,...We are life forms from a different universe than this place. From your point of view, aren't we intelligent aliens? Aren't you curious about us?"

,You're invaders, not travelers."

,......We have abilities and technologies that you humans would covet. Especially in genetic technology, the current human race can never catch up to us."

He went on and on about why they should be spared.

If it were Yura-ssi, not me, talking to him, she might have been tempted. It would be a great achievement in itself.

I have no intention of letting them live. I had to kill them to receive the quest reward. Even if it wasn't a quest, I wouldn't be attracted to their abilities. It's a technology and ability that I can have if the [Eternal Eden] world develops.

,Are your last words over?"

,...Spare us. We surrender."

,I refuse."

Pajijijijik!

Lightning gathers on the blade.

Lightning Heaven Style Electric Spear Form, Lightning Cut.

I swing the spear. A huge slash cut off the Giant Basila's neck and the Troids' heads.

Blue light could be seen in the severed neck of the Giant Basila. It was the dungeon core. If I break that, it becomes a high-purity magic stone, and the dungeon collapses.

[Quest completed.]

[Quest rewards are being given.]

[Inventory level has increased to 7.]

[Inventory level has increased to 8.]

,Oh, yes.'

The inventory level has increased.

Suddenly, I wondered if it would have reached level 9 if I had raised the inventory level in advance.

,I didn't have the points. Let's not dwell on the past.'

A new ability has been added to the inventory.

,Automatic reloading is convenient. It's not just for bullets, is it? Should I test it out?'

Yura-ssi was behind me, but it didn't really matter. She's seen me take things out of thin air several times. She probably thinks it's some kind of artifact.

I took a pistol out of the inventory, aimed it at the dungeon core in the Giant Basila's corpse, and pulled the trigger.

Tang tang tang tang tang tang tang tang tang tang tang!

The magazine was emptied in an instant.

And I felt a slight amount of mana draining away, and the magazine in the inventory and the empty magazine in the pistol were automatically replaced.

,There's a slight gap when replacing it. It's a short time, less than a second, so there's no problem.'

I also wore a potion belt and a dagger belt from the inventory. As expected, they were treated as consumables and automatically reloaded as soon as I used them. All of this is only possible if there are consumables in the inventory.

,Next is ejection. The inventory now has direct attack capabilities.'

I had predicted that this ability would appear someday, as the magic that is the basis of the inventory skill was also possible. I didn't know it would come out when I reached level 8, though.

I tried using ejection. A small amount of mana was consumed.

A space split open next to me, and a sword appeared. It was one of the iron swords in the inventory. A mass-produced longsword made by a dwarf slave in the [Baekhwan] world. It's treated as a masterpiece because it was made by a dwarf. Of course, it's not something that catches my eye.

,The mana consumed to open the ejection port is small. Hmm. I can feel that it's accurately aiming at where I'm aiming. I can shoot it to the side or behind.'

I can feel that I can use additional mana. But this time, I didn't use it and ejected it.

The sword is fired and flies in a straight line. The target is the Giant Basila's corpse. The sword bounced off without being able to pierce the corpse. I changed the target and shot it at the ground. The sword is stuck in the ground, standing upright.

,The speed is a bit slow. Is the power stronger than a gun? If I use more mana to increase the output....'

Kwang!

A small crater formed around the sword stuck in the ground. It's not bad power. The problem is that the ejected sword is also under stress. The durability of the ejected weapon is also important.

,An ordinary sword won't be able to withstand the maximum output and will break.'

The inventory has an automatic restoration function, but broken items are not restored.

,The ejection port can only be opened near me... With my current self, I can open up to about 70 of them. It's similar to a meteor sword, but faster.'

Meteor Sword feels like it's created in the air and dropped. The inventory is shooting prepared items. It doesn't have to be a sword. Rocks are also possible. I tried it once.

Kung!

A rock was ejected and fell to the ground. The power wasn't bad. The efficiency was bad.

,The mana consumed also varies depending on the size of the ejected item. A sword is more efficient.'

I had a good idea.

,Meteor Sword is a skill that is implemented with mana and dropped like a meteor. What if I store the created Meteor Sword in the inventory without dropping it?'

Meteor Sword naturally collapses and disappears over time, even if it is not used. That's because its essence is mana. But time stops in the inventory. In other words, if you put up with a little inconvenience, you can store hundreds, thousands of Meteor Swords in your inventory.

,Meteor Sword explodes even with a slight impact. If you give it an attribute, you can inflict attribute damage. In other words, it's no different from a cannonball in the form of a sword.'

I immediately tested it.

I created ten Meteor Swords with the familiar lightning attribute and put them in the inventory. As expected, they did not collapse.

Ten spaces split open around me and eject Meteor Swords towards the jungle.

The Meteor Swords that flew away exploded as soon as they collided with the trees. The shockwave and lightning burst and swept around.

,Wow. That's awesome, huh?"

All I have to do is eject the prepared Meteor Swords. It's much more mana-efficient than dropping lightning. It goes without saying that it's great for one-sided farming, and even in equal battles, this level of power is hard to ignore. You can annoy your opponent.

,Gate of Inventory. I like it."

However, it felt a bit boring to just shoot Meteor Swords.

,It would be nice to have a good quality weapon for throwing. I suddenly have a desire to collect weapons?'

I wanted to collect special weapons from other worlds.

It was something that I, who had the game life app, could do enough. It was a bit annoying to start right away, though.

,I've been watching you since a while ago because you seemed to be concentrating... Isn't that inefficient? The power feels ambiguous, but... it seems like you'll need a lot of money."

Yura-ssi said from behind. I wasn't surprised because I knew she was there.

,I have a lot of money. And this surprisingly doesn't cost much. Above all, it's cool."

Yura-ssi looked at me as if she was dumbfounded, then turned her head away.

,The Giant Basila's corpse... the inside has definitely been transformed. Sell it to the World Hunter Association. They'll give you more than a typical A-class boss monster."

,Okay. I don't care as long as they pay the right price."

,Are you going back right away? The dungeon core. Should I break it?"

,No. Let's hunt a little."

I got a new skill, so I should use it.

I hunted nearby monsters with the inventory ejection ability. I had to hit an A-class monster with 2-3 Meteor Swords.

,The problem with Meteor Sword is that when ejected at maximum output, the strength is weak, so it explodes as soon as it's shot.'

It has to be in the ranks of a masterpiece to withstand the maximum output of ejection to some extent.

After testing until my mana was exhausted, I put the dungeon core in the inventory. Even if I didn't break it, the connection with the dungeon was severed, so it became an ordinary magic stone, and the dungeon gradually began to collapse. Yura-ssi and I went outside the dungeon.
