'Is it human?'

The figure draped in black cloth wasn't spread wide—it was elongated. The upper portion was round. In size and build, it resembled a human. There was no reason for a Xenorage to wear such cumbersome cloth.

'Can't be certain. There are plenty of monsters that pretend to be human.'

It could be a Xenorage that mimicked human form.

Even if it were human, there was no way it was an ordinary one, not in this place. And with other worlds existing, there was no guarantee it was even from Earth.

It seemed to be looking somewhere other than at me.

Instead of speaking to it, I emitted electromagnetic waves. I intended to uncover its identity.

Thunder Heaven Style — Electromagnetic Wave.

The electromagnetic waves spread out from me, reflected back, and returned carrying information. But there was no information about it. Whatever method it used, the waves had been blocked.

'What did it block them with? Does it have a special ability? Or does that cloth itself possess some kind of power?'

It turned to face me. At the center of the black cloth was a golden mask. A golden mask shaped like a human face. Though eye holes were cut into it, no eyes were visible.

I immediately narrowed my eyes and stared at it.

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

Thunder Heaven Style — Thunder Art — Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.

I activated the skill Heavenly Eye and the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil stored in Storm Breaker simultaneously.

The Heavenly Eye, capable of seeing through matter.

The Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, capable of discerning a portion of one's inner power just by looking.

The moment I looked at it with both eyes—

Piiiiiiing!

A recoil struck both eyes. Capillaries must have burst, because blood began streaming from them.

“…….”

What pissed me off more than the pain radiating through my eyes was that even after going through all this shit, I still couldn't identify the bastard.

'The Thunder Eye Golden Pupil may be far inferior to the original, but to not yield even a single scrap of information...'

My pride stung, just a little.

Thunder Heaven Style — Thunder Flash.

I swung Hwaryeon Bido toward it. A blade aura wreathed in lightning flew forward.

A hand rose from beneath the black cloth. The exposed hand was similar in form to a human's, but the skin was pallid, and the purple nails were long.

A dark blue energy appeared mid-flight and blocked the blade aura.

'What the hell is that.'

I was genuinely surprised. Not because it had blocked the blade aura, but because that dark blue energy wasn't mana—it was something else entirely.

Was that magic? No, it couldn't be called magic.

'Even magic used on Earth relies on mana. It's impossible to use magic with energy that isn't mana.'

The very existence of a special energy that wasn't mana was astonishing in itself.

"What the hell are you?"

In the end, I couldn't hold back and asked.

“…….”

No answer came.

I couldn't tell whether it didn't understand Korean, or whether it understood and was pretending not to.

'Fine.'

I gave up on thinking deeply about it.

Either way, its presence here confirmed it was a suspicious bastard.

'I'll capture it and find out. If it gets annoying along the way, I'll just hand it over to the World Hunter Association.'

Thunder Heaven Style — Gale Swift Thunder.

Lightning wrapped around my entire body, elevating my physical abilities by a full stage as I charged at it.

"Kiiiiiiieeeeeek!"

Dozens of Xenorages radiated killing intent and blocked my path. Even after watching me mercilessly cut down their kin, they didn't back off.

'The ones I saw on the surface seemed to flee when they realized they couldn't win.'

Come to think of it, there was nowhere to run here.

Instead of dealing with the Xenorages one by one, I ignored them and sprinted toward it.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 11]

In an instant, I closed the distance between us.

The tip of Hwaryeon Bido thrust toward its golden mask. It spread its palm. I felt the movement of that unknowable dark blue energy.

My vision flipped.

Dozens of Xenorages were swarming around me.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 10]

'Forced spatial displacement?'

I swung Storm Breaker.

Thunder Heaven Style — Lightning Spear Form — Wave Source.

Spears of lightning swept in all directions. Dozens of Xenorages were crushed and torn apart in the sweep.

Pain flared in my left shoulder. It was because I'd channeled enough force to strain even an S-rank body in order to wipe out A-rank threats in a single sweep.

I check the distance between me and the Golden Mask.

200m.

Not as far as I thought.

Seems his forced spatial teleportation ability has its limits too.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 9]

Just like before, I close the gap in an instant.

Once again, that unknowable power of his surges.

[Using Leap.]

I teleported before he could teleport me. I move behind him and thrust Hwaryeon Bido forward. Just before the blade could land, his body vanished. He reappeared roughly 30 meters away.

'Spatial teleportation is a real pain in the ass to deal with.'

The Golden Mask extends a finger toward me. That same dark blue energy gathers and fires out like a laser.

I thrust Stormbreaker forward. I gather lightning at the spearhead and block the laser. The beam hitting the tip shreds apart and scatters in all directions. The Xenorage caught in the splash are instantly sliced apart and burned to ash.

The laser fades moments later. I seize the opening, catch my breath, and break into a sprint.

'The most effective counter against spatial teleportation... is dominating the space around me.'

[Domain Expansion]

Starlight bursts from Hwaryeon Bido and seizes control of the surrounding space. What's surprising is that his dark blue energy resists the domain. Meaning his power operates on a similar tier.

'What the hell is this guy.'

I shove the questions aside and charge at the Golden Mask.

Dark blue energy gathers behind the Golden Mask's back. Space begins to ripple. Despite the domain's interference, he's trying to use spatial teleportation.

'I'll subdue him before he can.'

Thunder Heaven Style — Thunder Light.

The fastest sword strike traces a red arc as it extends toward him.

He extends his right hand. The blade slows. More precisely, invisible forces block its path. The blade that was like a beam of light slows to a turtle's crawl.

I grit my teeth. I focus the domain into the blade. The domain-wreathed blade regains its speed. But he's already retreated and finished preparing his teleportation.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 8]

Shhk!

I use Flash Step and finally slice through his right hand. Blood bursts out. Unlike the Xenorage's blue blood, this was red like a human's.

Immediately after, the Golden Mask vanished.

Thunder Heaven Style — Electromagnetic Wave.

I spread the electromagnetic wave to its maximum range. I can't sense his presence.

'He fled.'

I toss his severed right hand into my inventory and scan my surroundings.

Hundreds of Xenorage have surrounded me.

"Guess I'll finish this while the domain holds."

Crackle.

Red lightning sparks off Hwaryeon Bido.

I swing the blade at the enemies. The domain-enhanced slash cuts down dozens at once.

• • •

I drive Hwaryeon Bido into the dungeon core guarded by a massive Xenorage over 20 meters tall.

KWAAK!

The dungeon core shatters and the corrupted space rapidly reverts to normal.

I don't look back and run toward the ghost ship.

Of course, as a Hunter, I collected every Xenorage corpse. I'll sell all of them to the World Hunter Association. I came all this way, might as well take what I can.

The ghost ship begins its return toward Earth. I look at the blue planet and massage my shoulder.

"Haven't exerted myself like that in a while. Fucking exhausted."

The ghost ship returned to the surface in 30 minutes.

James, the World Hunter Association Chairman, was waiting for me.

"Welcome back to Earth. How was your space trip?"

"I just popped outside the atmosphere for a bit. You call that a space trip? Oh, and there was some strange bastard out there."

"A monster other than the Xenorage?"

"Not a monster. He was draped in black cloth with a golden mask covering his face. Not sure if he was human, but he looked like one. He used some strange power that wasn't mana."

"...A power that isn't mana. That's a variable."

James's expression hardened. But his eyes stayed calm.

"Do you know who he is?"

"I have a few guesses. Can't say for certain."

"This is his hand. Study it or don't, do whatever you want."

I handed him the Golden Mask's right hand. His eyes widened as he received it.

"Ohh. Thank you. I will produce results without fail. For humanity."

"Do whatever you want with that. What about my payment for this job?"

"Of course, I'll pay as promised. I'll also buy the Xenorage corpses you brought at a good price. First, come with me to the Association headquarters. I'll take you directly to the warehouse."

James and I moved instantly to the World Hunter Association headquarters in New York with the help of a spatial teleportation ability user.

"This warehouse is special—only those who've been granted permission may enter. And those who've been granted that permission number no more than a handful, myself included. As promised, I'll give you one of the items inside, Eugene."

"Any item I want?"

"Yes. Every item in this warehouse is precious. However, you may only choose one."

"So the truly valuable or dangerous items aren't in here, I take it."

"Hahaha."

James offered no confirmation, only a smile.

I looked over the hundreds of treasures on display and curled the corners of my lips upward.
