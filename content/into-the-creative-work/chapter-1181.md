I turned my head to the side.

A hulking man in a doctor's gown, easily 2m tall, was looking at me with indifferent eyes.

,Who are you."

,Kaminoyama Kazuto. I'm the director of this hospital."

,Hospital director? This is a hospital?"

I looked around. There was blood and gore, but it didn't feel like a hospital at all. To me, this was a factory.

,The main house and the hospital are connected. It's designed this way for efficiency. The hospital makes it easy to get materials, and the main house manages the precious finished product, the Blood Pearls."

The hospital wasn't far from the mansion. I'd come through an underground passage to get here, so it wasn't strange that this was a hospital. Well, whether it was a hospital or not didn't really matter to me.

I gripped the hilt of my katana tightly.

Kaminoyama Kazuto, still with that indifferent look, pulled a weapon from his gown pocket. A black pistol appeared.

Colt M1911.

The barrel of the pistol, still loved after 100 years, was pointed at me. I glared at the bastard, tears of blood streaming down my face.

This was disadvantageous. No, it wasn't just disadvantageous. There were more than 10m between him and me. With my current physical abilities, I couldn't close that 10m gap in an instant. The moment I ran, a bullet would fly and put a hole in me.

,I'll have to use Complete Recovery.'

I was already prepared to die once.

,I have a proposition for you."

,...A proposition?"

,I heard you killed dozens of villagers? I'm interested in your abilities. I'll pay you a salary you won't be able to complain about. Work for me."

,Sorry, but I have no intention of being stuck in this village."

,Don't worry about that. Once this situation is taken care of, I'm planning to move the workshop. The Miko-nim will have to allow it, though... But thanks to someone, the value of this village has dropped, so she'll probably allow it."

,Still no. There's no way in hell I'm going to work under your ass."

,That's a shame."

He pulled the trigger. I, who had been watching his finger, immediately moved behind a nearby machine.

,To think you could dodge a bullet... Impressive."

,Kaminoyama Kazuto. Are you the supervisor of this village... no, this business?"

,Business, huh... I like that word. But I'm not the supervisor. If I had to say, I'm the second-in-command."

,So the shrine's Miko-nim is the supervisor. What's your relationship with the Miko?"

,She's my sister. Well, that monster bitch probably doesn't think of me as her brother, though..."

Kaminoyama Kazuto answered easily. I wanted him to come closer to kill me, but he didn't close the distance. Instead, he walked to the side, taking up a position where he could shoot me.

As soon as I saw him, I moved to the side of the machine. Ting! The bullet that hit the machine ricocheted off.

,A monster? She's quite pretty for a monster."

,I look at the inside of a person, not the outside. A person's exterior is just flesh, after all. The Miko, that woman, likes to play with people."

,Plays with them, huh..."

I nodded.

If the villagers intended to kill the Circle members, they could have killed them on the first day they entered the village.

People, weapons, terrain. Everything was in the village's favor. But the villagers didn't attack on the first day. Instead, they created a sense of unease. They created strangeness, unease, and fear. Like a well-crafted play. And the Miko was the writer and director of that play.

But to do that, one condition was necessary. The Miko had to completely control this village.

,How? How did the Miko take control of this village?"

,Faith and drugs. She fooled the villagers with that nonsense about the mountain god. She used drugs appropriately, and disguised the Blood Pearls as gifts from the gods. Surprisingly, the villagers believed it without much suspicion."

,Because the Blood Pearls are that good. If they see the effects, they have no choice but to believe."

Just by eating them, your physical abilities increase. If you eat them when you have a cold, the cold disappears in an instant.

In this world, Blood Pearls were an unrealistic item. And she even used drugs? It was basically game over.

,That woman would have ruled the village even without the Blood Pearls."

,You're overestimating your sister. Did you know? Before coming here, I killed the old man in the mansion."

,My father's dead, huh. So? Do you want me to feel anger towards you?"

,Your dad was murdered and you're not angry? You're a real son of a bitch."

,A useless human being just died. I actually feel a sense of kinship with you."

,You didn't get along with your dad."

Even as we continued the conversation, the battle continued. He carefully pulled the trigger, and I used the machine as cover to close the distance between us.

,It doesn't matter to me about my father. That woman, the Miko, distorted her face and screamed because of you. She was angry that her script was ruined. Seeing that, I felt like a decade-old indigestion had been relieved. It was very satisfying."

Bang.

A bullet flew. It barely grazed my cheek.

,3m. At this distance... I can kill him without using Complete Recovery.'

I was about to make up my mind and rush at him when.

Kwaang!

The door burst open as if it were breaking, and someone came in.

,He's here."

Kaminoyama Kazuto retreated, creating distance. He had been waiting for someone to break in all along. He put the gun in his gown pocket.

I climbed on top of the machine and looked at the intruder.

It was a huge man wearing black clothes, but his body proportions were strange. His lower body was normal, but his upper body was excessively large. His forearms were like tree trunks. His head was covered with a black cloth. Every time he exhaled, the black cloth covering his head shook.

,...Joseonjin. The Miko-nim told me to kill you."

The guy strode towards me.

,...Is that thing human?"

,He's human. I just modified him a bit using Blood Pearls. ...The problem is that he doesn't listen to me at all."

,Get out of the way, Hospital Director."

,Shibata. The machines here are expensive. Try not to break them."

,......"

The guy called Shibata rushed at me.

I jumped off the machine and dodged to the side, and Shibata collided with the machine. The machine broke. Human blood that was inside poured onto the floor.

,Still so ignorant."

Kaminoyama Kazuto clicked his tongue. He leaned against the wall, watching the situation with a relaxed expression. He took out a cigarette and put it in his mouth, showing no intention of intervening.

,Joseonjin...! I'll kill you...!"

Shibata's body, which had collided with the machine, wasn't in good shape. The shape of his right shoulder and forearm was distorted, and he was injured. The muscles of the guy twitched. The wound disappeared, and the distorted muscles returned to their original shape. It was an absurd recovery speed.

The guy lifted the machine with his large hands. He casually threw the machine, which probably weighed at least 500kg, at me.

I rolled on the floor to avoid the attack.

,You created a monster like this?!"

,The Miko said this. It's a miracle of the mountain god. And in my opinion, it's a coincidence close to a miracle."

Kaminoyama Kazuto replied.

,Miko-nim! I'll kill that bastard! Yes! According to the will of the mountain god!"

,That bastard. Anyone can see that his brain is fucked up."

,It's the result of the Miko's dedicated brainwashing. I saw it from the side... she brainwashed him very thoroughly. If I had been brainwashed like Shibata, I would have committed suicide without hesitation. Well, I understand. She wouldn't want to lose a monster like Shibata."

I couldn't respond to Kaminoyama Kazuto. I didn't have the luxury to do so. I was too busy dodging Shibata's attacks.

,I have to give up on the idea of fighting a human. This guy is... a monster.'

If I were to rank him, he'd be an E-class monster.

With my physical abilities in reality, I could blow his head off with one punch, but I can't even use Acceleration right now.

Shibata's attacks are simple and crude. His actions are so obvious that it's not that difficult to avoid them. But.

,At this rate, I'm going to die.'

I was out of breath. I could see the limits of my stamina. I dodged Shibata's attacks and swung my sword to counterattack. But it was ineffective. I didn't have enough strength to deliver a fatal blow.

,...To fight a monster, I have to become a monster too.'

I took out a Blood Pearl from my pocket.

Seven.

I took out three more. I threw 10 Blood Pearls into my mouth. Eujeogeujeok. I chewed and swallowed the Blood Pearls.

,He's crazy..."

Kaminoyama Kazuto watched me with fascinated eyes.

The red vision became even redder, and my whole body became hot. I gripped the hilt of my sword even harder to control the overflowing power. Ujiggeun. The skin on my hand tore, my bones broke, and blood splattered. But the strength of my grip didn't loosen; it became even stronger.

It's not that there's no pain. It's just that the pain is so faint that it's not even at the level of a pinch. This stimulation feels rather pleasant.

,Keu, keukeuk."

I could see Shibata's movements clearly. It was proof that my dynamic visual acuity had increased due to the effects of the Blood Pearls.

,I can do this.'

I collided with him head-on. I cut off half of his right arm, but I was hit by his left fist and flew backwards, crashing into the wall.

,...!!"

It's not working.

I chewed and swallowed 10 Blood Pearls, but my physical abilities were still inferior to his. I didn't die instantly thanks to the effects of the Blood Pearls, though. I think I broke about six or seven ribs.

,Heok..."

I turned my head to the side at the sound of a gasp. A flustered Kaminoyama Kazuto took out a pistol from his pocket and shot me in the side. The bullet lodged in my side. Since there was no pain, I didn't feel any sense of danger. I swung my sword and cut off Kaminoyama Kazuto's head.

I grabbed the falling head and threw it at Shibata. Shibata caught the head with his left hand as if swatting away a fly. The head flew towards the machine and shattered like a watermelon.

Shibata ran over.

I opened the door and ran away. I gave up on fighting him head-on. Even if I ate more Blood Pearls, I wouldn't be able to beat that bastard.

,Stop right there!!"

,Like hell I will."

As I went up the stairs, I came to the first floor of the hospital. The lights were on, and I could see a few nurses. There were no young nurses; they were all middle-aged.

,Kyaaaaaaak!"

The nurses screamed as soon as they saw me. I understand. I ate 10 Blood Pearls, so I probably don't look human right now.

I passed the nurses and went towards the lobby. The men in suits in the lobby jumped up and pulled out guns from their pockets, aiming at me. Their reaction was quite fast, but the gun barrels couldn't keep up with my movements. I cut down all the bastards with pistols and killed them.

,I got a pistol. Even that monster bastard will be equal in front of a gun.'

I shot the guy chasing me. I aimed for the head, but none of the bullets hit the head. Instead, a few bullets hit his body.

Guns weren't equal.

The bullets that hit his body were pushed out and fell to the floor. He didn't stop even for a moment until that moment.

,Come here and die quietly!"

,Will I die quietly."

I ran out of the hospital without looking back.
