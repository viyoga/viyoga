Marquis Lian Vergil has no interest in the throne.

To be precise, he tried to have no interest. After all, his succession rank was ninth. He was far removed from the throne.

Of course, the throne of present-day Neo London isn't inherited purely by order of succession. The days when the first in line unconditionally took the throne are long gone. The throne of present-day Neo London is passed down through abdication, at the king's discretion.

Marquis Vergil knew that in the current situation, no amount of effort on his part could secure him the succession. He wasn't close with Victoria VI, nor did he have a backing faction. So he gave up, like a fox eyeing grapes it could never reach.

Then a curious rumor reached his ears. His secretary and butler quietly opened his mouth.

"My lord. Strange stories are coming out of Buckingham Palace."

He had planted people in Buckingham Palace. A difficult task, but not an impossible one. In the old days, perhaps not. But the current queen, Victoria VI, had grown old. Her power, once like steel, had begun to rust. Even the servants working in the palace had to think about their futures, didn't they?

"What kind of story?"

"Are you familiar with Yuria Grace?"

"How could I not be? She's the maid challenging for Prime-rank, isn't she? The talent in present-day Neo London that's a sure bet to win the lottery. There's even a rumor spreading that her master is that mage. That mage must be raking in an enormous amount of money at this very moment, no? Thinking about it again, I'm so envious my stomach aches."

"There's a rumor circulating that she is a kingmaker. The key to the king."

"...A maid, of all things, a kingmaker?"

"They say the successor who earns her loyalty will ascend to the throne. It's hard to dismiss it as mere nonsense..."

"...The contents of yesterday's video conference come to mind. Her Majesty is someone who imbues even a single word with intent. So it wasn't a random remark but an implication..."

"It's not confirmed information. Verification work is needed."

The credibility was high.

After all, the reason Victoria VI was able to ascend to the throne was thanks to a Prime-rank maid named Florence. It wouldn't be strange for the queen to hold a high opinion of the next Prime-rank maid.

After a brief moment of deliberation, Marquis Vergil spoke.

"Tomorrow, I go to Buckingham Palace. The reason for the visit is Her Majesty the Queen... no, if I say that, she'll refuse. It's an audience with Yuria Grace. If the audience is granted, then there's a greater than 70% chance the rumor is true."

"Isn't that too hasty a move? If you fail to win her over... you'll have only informed others that the information is certain."

"It doesn't matter. Without this opportunity, I'll have no chance at the throne at all."

"What will you offer Yuria Grace?"

"What I have on hand isn't much. You said Yuria Grace is rank 7? She can obtain most things through her own power. What I can offer is royalty. And the position of future queen consort. Beyond that, if I dangle future power, wouldn't that be enough to persuade her?"

"Understood. I'll contact Buckingham Palace first."

The butler returned in less than five minutes.

"My lord. The audience request has been granted. However, as Yuria Grace is currently outside the palace, the visit has been set for 4 PM."

"That notoriously difficult Buckingham Palace granted a visit in under five minutes? It's confirmed then. Her Majesty intends to hand the throne to whoever obtains Yuria Grace. An old legend comes to mind. She's like a female Excalibur."

Marquis Vergil curled the corners of his lips upward. Tomorrow, he intended to stake everything on obtaining Yuria Grace. If he succeeded, he could have the throne. He would become the sun of this Neo London.

That was when it happened.

Darkness descended without warning. Perhaps because it was so sudden, he couldn't see anything around him. Not even a faint glimmer of light could be felt.

"...Did the lights go out?"

"It seems so. Judging by the darkness elsewhere as well, there must be a minor issue in the power room."

"Tch. Have the manager in charge fired."

"Yes. First, I'll go fetch candles to light."

As Marquis Vergil waited quietly, he belatedly realized he could no longer sense the butler's presence. No footsteps, of course, and not even the sound of a door opening or closing.

Perfect silence.

All that could be heard in this place was Marquis Vergil's own breathing and heartbeat.

"...Butler? Haven't you left yet? If you're there, speak."

No answer came.

Marquis Vergil recalled where the butler had been standing and reached out his hand. Then he realized. He felt nothing in his hand. He couldn't tell whether he had reached out or not.

'This is...!'

Startled, he tried to raise his body, but his body wouldn't rise. His voice wouldn't leave his mouth. Then suddenly, a sharp pain surged from his heart.

Instinct recognized death. Extreme terror struck his mind. Fortunately, the time wasn't long. His consciousness gradually faded and then vanished.

The light returned.

The butler died standing, and Vergil died seated.

Yuria quietly scanned the room. She checked for any evidence she might have missed. She had left nothing behind. Not even a single strand of hair. She had complete control of this space. Even if a psychometrist came, they would not find the truth.

The silver-haired maid vanished without a trace.

"Your Majesty."

Victoria VI, Queen of Neo London, looked at Florence, who had come to see her, with an expressionless face.

"Florence. I don't know how long it's been since you came to see me at this hour. And most of those times, you brought me terrible news. This time, I do hope it's at least less terrible."

"Marquis Lian Vergil died at his residence along with his butler. The cause of death was internal organ damage, with no external wounds. It is presumed to be assassination."

The queen's fingertips trembled faintly.

Marquis Lian Vergil.

They hadn't been particularly close, but he was the queen's grandson.

"Vergil contacted Buckingham Palace not long ago saying he would visit. And now he's been assassinated?"

"Your Majesty. This is information confirmed through the London Guard. Marquis Vergil died approximately thirty minutes ago. There were no traces of drugs or curses. According to the London Guard's assessment, they died without even being aware of their own deaths. The London Guard described it as a chillingly perfect assassination."

"...Hah. For something like this to happen... Poor Lian... The assassin, has the assassin been identified?"

"No traces were found at the scene. Furthermore, the psychometry ability user could read nothing. Marquis Vergil's butler was a rank 6 warrior. The fact that he was assassinated means... the assassin is estimated to be at least rank 7 or above."

"...Rank 7."

"Your Majesty. What matters in a case like this is the motive. The motive can be found by examining Marquis Vergil's activities. Here is the document recording Marquis Vergil's activities."

The queen quickly reviewed the documents.

"...There's nothing particularly unusual in his activities."

"Marquis Vergil requested an audience with Miss Yuria. In other words, Marquis Vergil was the quickest to discern Your Majesty's intentions. That is impossible with Marquis Vergil's abilities. Someone informed Marquis Vergil of the answer."

"Is that someone the culprit?"

"Most likely. And this is both a message and a warning. You know what I'm saying."

The queen shut her eyes tightly, then opened them. Her trembling had stopped. Those two eyes shone coldly.

"Yuria. Where is she now?"

"Unknown. The surveillance I had assigned to her lost track of her three minutes after she left the palace."

"If she is the culprit, why on earth did she kill Marquis Vergil? If she had complaints, couldn't she have told me, or you?"

"Your Majesty. Miss Yuria already expressed herself with firm resolve. It was Your Majesty who ignored it. Since words didn't get through, she raised her blade."

"...Can we punish her? Using this incident as a pretext to make her swear loyalty to the royal family..."

"Your Majesty. Have you still not let go? Miss Yuria is like me. Just as I devote my loyalty to Your Majesty, Miss Yuria devotes her loyalty to Count Meyer. Do you think I would betray Your Majesty?"

The queen swallowed her breath. She was silent for several minutes.

"I let my greed blind me too much. Looking at her, I thought of you so much. You, who helped me sit on the throne, and once I was seated, helped set the royal family right. If it weren't for you, I would have died that night, let alone taken the throne..."

"Your Majesty. It's not too late even now. This matter must be buried."

"...My grandson Lian is dead."

"Another prince or princess could have died. It is no coincidence that she chose Marquis Vergil."

"She knew even that my relationship with Lian was distant, and acted on it. She feels like a far more terrifying woman than you."

"Yes. I am afraid of her as well. To speak honestly, if she turned to the side of our enemies... I cannot guarantee that I could protect Your Majesty."

"That much? She is rank 7, and you are rank 8, are you not?"

"She and I differ in method. Above all, she is currently close to rank 8. Within a few years at most, she will rise to rank 8."

"...Rank 8 at that age. An absurd talent. If we cannot have her, then rather..."

"Your Majesty."

"I understand. I was merely speaking idly. I will give up on her. At the very least, I must not become her enemy."

"Yes. You have thought well. I will handle the aftermath of Marquis Vergil's matter. It's late. Please retire for the night."

"...Florence. I think I've grown far too old. In the old days, I wouldn't have been like this."

"Your Majesty. Everyone grows old."

"Do you not age?"

"Because I am rank 8."

"Yes. Yes. How enviable. Florence. Next year, I must abdicate. This crown is beginning to feel far too heavy."

"I will follow Your Majesty to the very end."

"I'm glad you're here. But... yes. In the end, rather than caring for this old woman, I hope you live your own life. Haven't you been unable to marry because of me?"

"I am content with my life. Stop with the pointless talk and go to sleep."

"My last wish is to see you meet a fine man and build a happy family."

"I said go to sleep."
