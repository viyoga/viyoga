The situation in the desert was turning unfavorable for the alliance.

7 districts joined the alliance, 4 declared neutrality, and the remaining 12 declared non-alignment.

,The desert alliance has failed.'

At least 10 districts needed to form an alliance to exert significant influence.

,Did I join the alliance for nothing?'

It might have been more profitable to attack the Desert Forest district without joining the alliance. Pillaging the dancers would also be a viable option.

,Those ambiguously neutral bastards are the most annoying.'

I could sense their intention to play both sides. They would try to get a piece of the pie if the alliance became advantageous, and they would turn to the non-aligned side if the alliance became unfavorable.

,I'm already annoyed, so the ruler of the Desert Forest, who can be said to be the main body of the desert alliance, must be even more pissed.'

I looked out the window. It was pouring rain. It's been a week since the oasis disappeared. I don't know about other districts, but there's no significant damage in the district I rule. Since I bring rain once a week, the citizens are praising me.

"Cheonma-nim."

Enas approached.

"What is it."

"We have received word from the Desert Forest. They have found traces of the oasis's owner."

"You're a bit late. Where is it?"

"District 5,144, the Golden Desert."

I curled up the corners of my lips. Things are getting interesting.

District 5,144, the Golden Desert, is one of the non-aligned districts. The bigger problem is that it's the largest district in the western Golden Desert area and the wealthiest district. That's because, as its name suggests, the Golden Desert has gold mines. In addition, it has one super-large oasis and five large oases.

,This is going to be a headache for the Desert Forest.'

To subjugate the owner of the oasis, they have to go to the Golden Desert. It was unlikely that the non-aligned Golden Desert would cooperate with the Desert Forest.

"Enas. How do you think the Desert Forest will react?"

"If I were the ruler of the Desert Forest... I would spread rumors."

"Rumors?"

"The rumor that the Golden Desert is joining hands with the owner of the oasis to take control of all the oases in the desert. They will use the rumors to gain the cooperation of the Golden Desert."

As Enas said, rumors began to circulate in the desert.

The rumor was that the owner of the oasis was in the Golden Desert. The eyes of the desert gathered on the Golden Desert.

I thought the Golden Desert would deny it or rather criticize the Desert Forest. But, breaking my expectations, the Golden Desert announced that they would cooperate with the Desert Forest.

"They haven't joined the alliance, but they will cooperate.... Wouldn't it be easier to join the alliance from the start if they're going to do this?"

"It seems the ruler of the Golden Desert is mindful of the citizens."

Human sacrifice.

It's nothing to rulers like me, but it must be really fucked up from the perspective of those being sacrificed.

• • •

Kalas, the ruler of the Desert Forest, summoned the alliance to subjugate the 'Owner of the Oasis'. Even if it was an alliance, the rulers didn't need to move directly. What Kalas wanted was combat personnel. But I directly led my troops to the Golden Desert.

Since the ruler of the Golden Desert limited the number of people, the number of people I could take was limited to 30. Including the alliance, the total force was 210.

I was able to meet Kalas, the ruler of the Desert Forest, in the outer region of the Golden Desert. He also brought his troops directly, like me.

Kalas was a desert elf with bronze skin. He's a man.

"Nice to meet you, Cheonma. I am Kalas, the ruler of the Desert Forest in district 5,140."

Unlike his appearance, which looked like he was in his early 20s, his voice was similar to that of a middle-aged man.

"I am the Cheonma, the master of the Cheonma Shingyo."

"I have heard of your fame. I was relieved that you joined the alliance."

Behind Kalas were people covered in black cloth. I could feel a restrained energy. They are all close to elite soldiers. The warriors sent from other alliances were below standard. They didn't seem to be of much help.

"Kalas. I would like you to share information about the owner of the oasis."

"Of course, I intend to do so. The owner of the oasis is a god named Set. Have you heard of him?"

"I know he's an Egyptian god."

"Set is the god of the land. He is a god who can create sandstorms and control oases. He is also the god of war."

"He's quite a high-ranking god. Why is a guy like that acting so petty?"

"This is just a shaman's speculation... Human sacrifice is to accumulate power. In other words, it means that Set is weakened right now. Now is the perfect opportunity to kill the god Set."

"Where is he?"

"I don't know the exact location. The ruler of the Golden Desert will help us, so don't worry."

"Do you think the ruler of the Golden Desert will cooperate until the end?"

"I heard that the ruler of the Golden Desert offered 500 women to the owner of the oasis. As the oasis grew and there were more, the sacrifices increased. From the ruler of the Golden Desert's point of view, he must desperately want us to subjugate the owner of the oasis."

"But he doesn't join the alliance? My back is itching."

"Don't trust the ruler of the Golden Desert too much. We are not his hunting dogs. Be careful not to get boiled alive."

We head to the center of the Golden Desert with the warriors.

It wasn't long before I realized the specialness of the Golden Desert. The temperature is about 2-3 degrees lower than the Scorpion Desert I rule. Cacti and wild camel herds are also frequently seen. The amazing thing is that you can see an oasis every 2-3km.

"Oases are frequently seen."

Although they are small oases, they will be of great help to those crossing the desert.

"Isn't this desert called the Golden Desert for no reason? I heard that this desert is full of small oases."

"You have a forest in your desert, and here we have an abundance of oases.... Shit. Only my district is barren."

"I heard that it rains frequently in the Scorpion Desert these days."

"It's artificial rain. I'm making it rain because there are no oases."

"That's even more amazing. Isn't it overcoming nature with human power? The people of the Scorpion Desert will praise you."

"They already praise me. Thanks to that, city management has become easier."

We passed by the oasis. We weren't free enough to spend time at the oasis. The desert alliance, which did not accept the oasis owner's proposal, is in a situation where the oasis is dry. We must subjugate the owner of the oasis as soon as possible and get the oasis back.

"We have arrived."

Kalas said.

In front of us was a half-destroyed temple.

Hwiiiiiing.

A faint sandstorm blew around the temple.

"The owner of the oasis... Set, will be in that temple."

"Judging by the looks of it, it seems someone has broken the seal. Isn't the ruler of the Golden Desert the cause?"

"He said no, but... I think he's the cause. But we can't hold him responsible."

There was only one reason why they couldn't hold him responsible. The wealthiest Golden Desert in the western desert area has a formidable power that is difficult to deal with.

"Move aside."

"...What are you planning to do?"

"Look at that temple. Doesn't it look like there are traps lurking everywhere? It's clear that we'll suffer from the moment we enter that temple."

"Hmm. Do you have a lot of experience in this area? You talk like a veteran adventurer."

"I've fallen into hell and killed the Demon King. No ordinary adventurer can even present their business card in front of me."

"So, what are you going to do?"

"I'm going to do this."

I stood in front of the temple and focused on my breathing. My heart is beating. The Cheonma energy generated from my dantian spreads throughout my body. I can feel the power of Cheonma Shingong.

I raised my right fist. A jet-black aura began to condense in my fist.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Fist.

I strike the temple with my fist. A shockwave arises from the fist that hit the air, and the condensed Cheonma energy rushes into the temple.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The temple collapses. It was a natural occurrence. The already half-destroyed temple could not withstand my fist.

「The Oasis Owner's temple is destroyed.」

「You have destroyed a sacred temple. The curse of God falls upon you.」

A jet-black aura fell on me from the sky. Kalas, who was watching from the side, widens his eyes and is shocked.

"A curse! God's curse has fallen! Cheonma! Are you okay?!"

"Shut up. This kind of curse is nothing."

I said calmly, but the curse was quite powerful.

「The curse of the desert falls upon you.」

「The monsters of the desert are hostile to you.」

「You cannot consume the water of the oasis.」

「You will suffer from thirst.」

「All stats are reduced by 20% while in the desert.」

The effects of the curse appeared immediately. As 20% of my stats decreased, my body suddenly became heavy. I immediately wanted to sit down on the floor and rest.

,Heavenly Heart.'

[Heavenly Heart is activated. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The curse disappears. My body became light again.

"The temple is destroyed, but it seems the owner of the oasis we need to subjugate is still intact. What are you going to do now?"

"The temple is more fuck...."

I stopped talking. Kalas was the same. It was because I felt a huge presence from the destroyed temple.

The destroyed temple shakes, and a figure with the head of a beast and a split tail walks out of the temple. Standing tall on the sand, he looked at me and Kalas.

「The Owner of the Oasis (False) is glaring at you.」

"Kueueueueuk!"

Kalas groaned and stepped back. I was momentarily embarrassed. I didn't understand why he suddenly stepped back.

Kalas, who was staggering, eventually headed behind me. Set stares at me intently.

"Heok. As, as expected, the god of war has a powerful force.... Just receiving his gaze gives me this kind of pressure.... Cheonma. Are you okay?"

"I can handle it."

"You are truly amazing."

In fact, I didn't feel any pressure or anything. It seemed that Set was playing some trick, but it didn't work because I was using Heavenly Heart.

「The Owner of the Oasis (False) despises you.」

"You who do not believe, you are not worthy of receiving the blessings of this land. I will soak the sand with your blood."

Set struck the ground with the staff in his hand.

The sand soared and became soldiers with spears and shields.
