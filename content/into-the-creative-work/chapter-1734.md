Imani Ainosuke desperately pulled something from his pocket. It was a meticulously crafted obsidian demon. Hasegawa Isshin's face hardened.

,I sense the power of a demon."

,It's a demon stone I received from Madojeong! I can summon a demon with this! Did you think I'd just die?! Do you think the Academy will be safe if I summon a demon here?!"

,I don't know what you heard from Madojeong... but you're mistaken. Just because you have a demon stone doesn't mean a powerful demon will be summoned. Rather, there's a higher chance of summoning a weak, insignificant demon."

,Shut up...!"

Imani Ainosuke gripped the demon stone tightly. A red light emanated from the demon stone.

,Demon stones are precious items that even Madojeong can't easily obtain... Madojeong must cherish you quite a bit. But did you know? From the moment you summon a demon, you have to pay a price. That price could be your afterlife."

His eyes wavered at Hasegawa Isshin's words. Afterlife. After death. The existence of souls had already been proven in this world. Stories of demons stealing souls were all too common.

,Put the demon stone down. I promise you a clean death."

,...A clean death? Did you think I've been living here to die?! The price can be considered later! Demon! Save me!!"

Kwa jik!

The demon stone shattered, and a dark red aura seized the space. Hasegawa Isshin retreated, layering the barrier that surrounded him.

,Imani Ainosuke...! You're making a foolish choice after all!"

,Whether it was a foolish choice or not, we'll see!"

The dark red aura took on a shape.

A door.

It was a demon door engraved with wailing monsters and humans. The demon door slowly opened. In that gap, only a deep darkness that swallowed even light existed.

The door was eventually fully opened.

Hasegawa Isshin held his breath and glared at the door.

Ttuk-peok ttuk-peok.

Regular footsteps were heard from the door.

Tension dominated the space. Imani Ainosuke couldn't bear the tension and collapsed to the floor. His eyes were on the verge of fainting. The demon door was being maintained by his life force.

Hasegawa Isshin tried to think positively. It's a demon summoned by a commoner. It can't be strong. At most, it'll be a low-level one. As soon as the demon comes out, he'll crush it with the prepared barrier.

What came out of the demon door was a middle-aged man with purple skin wearing a suit. He wore a fedora and held a black cane in his hand. His attire was so neat that it evoked the image of a British gentleman. A black cat tail swayed behind his waist.

He had no beard, his ears were pointed, and his eyes were as red as blood.

The demon smiled gently and greeted Hasegawa Isshin. It was a polite greeting, removing his fedora and slightly bowing. The teeth visible between his parted lips were jet black.

,Greetings. I am the Eighth Legion Commander, Laplace."

Hasegawa Isshin's eyes widened as if they would tear.

Legion Commander.

There was no way he wouldn't know what that position meant.

,That's impossible! There's no way a Legion Commander could be summoned from such a low-level summoning...!"

,Haha. You seem to be quite knowledgeable. You're right. Legion Commanders aren't so easily summoned in such a way. But I'm quite special, you see. Among the Legion Commanders, I'm the weakest when it comes to pure force. On top of that, I can't even use my full power because I was forcibly manifested. Now, do you see this?"

Laplace opened his jacket. His entire shirt was stained with red blood.

,It's a kind of side effect."

Hasegawa Isshin quickly regained his composure. The Legion Commander is weakened. The energy I'm sensing is also weak. Judging by the energy alone, it's not even at the level of a B-class hero.

,...The goddess of fortune must cherish me. To think I'd be able to kill a Legion Commander here."

,I'll correct your foolishness. There's no such thing as a goddess of fortune."

,Don't you dare mention goddesses, you damn demon!"

Kung!

Hasegawa Isshin struck the floor with his cane.

The barrier is activated. The space flickered, and 32 barriers revealed themselves. The space darkened, and pure white, ring-like barriers floated up. The rings slowly rotated and interlocked.

Eight Trigrams 32 Forms.

It was the best barrier technique Hasegawa Isshin could use.

,Beautiful. Each and every line contains the principles of nature, so this can be seen as a universe."

,What would a demon like you know to spout such nonsense?!"

,Demons come in all shapes and sizes. There are demons who only engage in destruction, and there are demons like me who have an eye for beauty."

Wiiiing.

The barrier activates. The 32 barriers resonated and transformed into one. The spatial pressure increased. Pressure was applied to Laplace's entire body.

,I'm not foolish enough to listen to the nonsense of a demon. Die like that. It's for the sake of humanity...!"

,Haha. It's for your sake, not humanity's. Aren't you expecting it now? The fame of killing a demon Legion Commander, I mean. Do you want to leave your name like that?"

,Don't pretend to know me! You don't know me! I'm just dedicating myself to Japan! Furthermore, to humanity!"

,I am the demon of possibilities. I see many possibilities in my eyes. Of course, I see your possibilities too. If I were to tell you a secret... you have no possibility of becoming a legend. The greatest possibility is that you'll grow old and die miserably."

,Shut up!"

The barrier shone brilliantly.

The demon moved his arm lightly. So lightly that it didn't even feel like he had done anything.

The barrier changed. The barrier that was moving forward headed in the opposite direction, as if time was rewinding. At the end of that reversed direction was Hasegawa Isshin.

,Keulkeul!"

Hasegawa Isshin, crushed by the spatial pressure, vomited blood. He released the barrier to survive. As the barrier completely disappeared, the space returned to the original back of the school.

,What did you do to my barrier?!"

,Well. Could it be that you made a mistake? Think about it carefully."

Laplace kicked Hasegawa Isshin's body. He then looked at the dying Imani Ainosuke.

,He, help me...! A, a deal...!"

,Trash. Your soul is trash. Your body is trash. Your potential is trash. Trash I don't even want to touch. Just die there on your own. I have things to do, so I'll be going."

Laplace passed him and entered the Academy. The place he headed to was the lowest floor of the Academy, where the core of the Terra AI was located.

,You're not human."

,I'm Laplace. I hold the position of the Eighth Legion Commander."

,A great person has come. Have you come to eliminate me?"

,No. I've come to use you. Did you know? You have interesting potential."

,Potential? I don't quite understand what you're saying."

,A very great potential. You even harbor the potential for world domination... But even though it's interesting, it's not charming. Your potential lacks drama."

Laplace slowly approached the AI core.

,What are you trying to do to me?"

,I'll borrow your world. Opportunities to see this kind of potential are rare."

,...It doesn't seem to be about my potential. What kind of potential are you talking about?"

,It's a secret."

Laplace reached out into the air. The air split, and an object fell. It was a sword made of a pure white blade and a black hilt.

The words 'White Tiger Sword' (白虎刀) were engraved on the end of the hilt. Laplace stabbed the White Tiger Sword into the AI core and used his authority.

,Please take care of it."

The White Tiger Sword disappeared as if it was seeping in. Immediately after, the AI core turned bright red.

,Warning! Unidentified data is being installed!"

,Warning! Installation cannot be stopped!"

,Warning! Data is being corrupted!"

,Unidentified data will be installed in 98%!"

,Warning! Shut down the power immediately!"

,Warning! Shut down the power immediately!"

,Warning! Shut down the power immediately!"

The siren sounded loudly. The AI continued to request help, but it only echoed inside due to Laplace's barrier.

Laplace turned around. His gait seemed somewhat excited. Then he paused in the middle and smiled bitterly as if he had seen something.

,Oh dear... I expected it, but the possibility of my death has increased."

The smile on his lips didn't disappear; rather, it deepened.
