Seira was incredibly fast climbing the rocky mountain. When a Yet Slug appeared before her eyes, she immediately unleashed Sword energy without a moment's pause, killing the Yet. She didn't bother collecting the Yet's corpse and moved on.

If it were any ordinary knight other than me, following Seira would have been impossible.

Seira climbed the rocky mountain in a straight line, then suddenly changed direction. At first, I thought it was to deal with the Yet Slugs wandering around. But after a while, I realized Seira didn't know the way.

,I guess the task of killing the Yet Slug Queen was originally handled directly by Woo Geom-gong-nim.'

It was only her first time, so it was natural for her to be lost.

,Judging by that look, she doesn't even have a clue where the Yet Slug Queen is.'

Of course, I don't know where the Yet Slug Queen is either. And this was actually a good thing for me. If she found and killed the Yet Slug Queen in an instant, there would be no point in me following her all this way.

From a distance, Yet Mountain looked about the size of Jirisan Mountain. It's impossible to search the entire Yet Mountain in just a day or two.

She left the city with great enthusiasm, but when she actually tried to solve the problem, it wasn't easy.

I quietly followed behind her and approached the Yet Slug's corpse. The sight of a large, yellow slug oozing slime as it lay dead was quite disgusting.

The reason I approached the Yet Slug was because of Yet powder. I was thinking of making Yet powder once. I had heard the method roughly before.

,The reason Yet powder is more expensive than drugs is because of two things.'

One is that Yet Slugs are only found on this Yet Mountain, and the other is salt. Salt isn't as expensive as it was in the Middle Ages, but it's still much more expensive compared to modern times.

,I can bring salt from reality, so I can use it as much as I want.'

First, I have to wait for the Yet Slug's corpse to dry. When the corpse dries, it shrinks to about 50cm.

,There's no time to dry it.'

Seira was currently taking a short break. Since I had to follow her, the amount of free time I had was only about 15 minutes.

,Lightning.'

Blue lightning crackled in my palm. A stream of electricity moved towards the Yet Slug's corpse. I was planning to dry it using electricity, but it didn't work as well as I had imagined. The Yet Slug's corpse began to bubble.

,......Is this not it? Would it have been better to dry it with a hair dryer?'

I stopped the lightning and stood there blankly for a moment. I was a little taken aback by the unexpected reaction.

,Oh well. Whatever.'

I poured a bag of salt I took out of my inventory onto the bubbling Yet Slug corpse. As soon as the corpse and salt touched, a strange smell stung my nose. But the smell disappeared immediately, and the bubbling corpse began to shrink.

As time passed, white salt particles remained where the Yet Slug had been. It was a bit coarse, but it was Yet powder. Even if I scraped it all together, it wouldn't amount to more than 20g.

,......What, did I succeed?'

It seemed like pouring a lot of salt helped. Or maybe the electricity had some unknown effect.

,They say you melt and burn the Yet Slug with salt repeatedly to create fine Yet powder, right?'

I couldn't be sure if I had succeeded. I felt uneasy.

I hesitated for a moment and then created lightning in my palm again. A stream of electricity began to burn the coarse Yet powder on the ground.

The Yet powder disappeared as if it were evaporating into the air.

,This isn't it... um?'

Suddenly, my senses became more sensitive. It was a similar feeling to when I ingested Yet powder. The difference was that my senses were now sharper. The sound of the wind became louder, and the wind touching my skin felt like small pebbles.

I cast my gaze into the distance. Even without using mana, my eyesight seemed to have increased more than twofold.

,Did the vaporized Yet powder enter through my respiratory system?'

I was startled when I touched my chin without thinking. The texture felt unfamiliar. It was because my senses had become too sensitive.

I smiled awkwardly. Because my senses had become too sensitive, my body didn't feel like my own. Even breathing felt awkward, and I could feel myself getting mentally tired quickly.

,Wait a minute. If I use this well....'

I looked at Seira, who was far away, and racked my brain.

•••

,Sir. How about coming forward now?'

I came out from behind the rock, revealed myself, and approached her. I wasn't flustered. I knew that Seira had already noticed my presence.

,I'm glad you're safe, Seira-gongnyeo-nim.'

,You disobeyed my orders without a second thought.'

,I am not a knight of Arpiss.'

,That's true. Can I punch you?'

Seira clenched her fist. A bluish energy formed in her fist. If I got hit by that, something would definitely break.

,I'll pass. And it seems like Seira-gongnyeo-nim is having a hard time considering how confidently you left.'

Seira's face turned slightly red. She must be embarrassed that she left the city confidently, thinking she would solve the problem, but is now wandering around.

,I thought I would find the Yet Slug Queen quickly. Since it's a special monster different from ordinary Yet Slugs, its presence should be different.'

,Were you trying to find the Yet Slug Queen using your sense of ki?'

Seira nodded.

,I thought it would be possible. I was confident in my sense of ki.'

,You mean you relied on that alone?'

,According to my father, the Yet Slug Queen is in a place where a lot of Yet Slugs are gathered. I thought I just needed to find a group of Yet Slugs.'

,And you failed.'

,I have no excuse. I found a few groups of Yet Slugs, but the Yet Slug Queen wasn't there. Right now, there are an abnormally large number of Yet Slugs on this Yet Mountain.'

,Wouldn't the Yet Slug Queen be near the top of the mountain? Usually, special monsters are located near the top of the mountain, aren't they?'

,You're right. So I'm thinking of going to the top of Yet Mountain. It'll take quite a bit of time, though....'

Yet Mountain was rugged and high. There were no hiking trails, of course. And it wasn't just a rocky mountain with rocks everywhere. There was vegetation that served as food for the Yet Slugs in various parts of the mountain.

,I'll follow you.'

,You won't listen to my orders, will you? You're Valdert's knight, after all.'

Seira's words were barbed. She seemed upset that I had easily disobeyed her orders.

I chuckled and said to her.

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim. Do you have any food?'

,.......'

Seira closed her mouth. She didn't bring any food because she thought the job would be easy. All she brought when she left the city was her greatsword, the Zweihander.

,I brought a week's worth of emergency rations, just in case.'

I proudly showed Seira the backpack I was carrying on my back. I have an inventory, but I don't plan to tell her. The inventory is too convenient. Sometimes, you need to suffer a little.

,I think it's fortunate that you came along.'

Seira said with a serious face. It was already past noon. She hadn't even had water since dawn.

,Let's have a late lunch here.'

,Alright.'

I handed her the emergency rations.

,Is this food?'

,It's the emergency rations from the world I used to live in. It's a slightly improved version of the combat rations used by the military. It doesn't taste good, but it's high in calories.'

Seira ate the emergency rations following my lead and widened her eyes.

,Isn't it delicious enough?'

,......You think this is delicious?'

,It's delicious enough. It's a taste I've never experienced before, and it's amazing. What's the name of this dish?'

,It's just fried rice. It still tastes bad to me even if I eat it again.'

,You didn't say anything was delicious even when you were at the mansion.'

,Did I? The Arpiss Duchy's cuisine was decent enough.'

,Decent enough, huh? Your taste is much more refined than mine.'

I couldn't argue with Seira's words. It was true.

Lately, most foods taste bland to me. They taste good when I'm hungry, but otherwise, they're all the same.

There's only one reason I can think of.

[Baekhwan]'s world's Yuria's cooking. And the cooking of the kitchen maids who were taught by Yuria.

I'm 100% sure that if the Michelin Guide came to my mansion, they would give it 3 stars.

,You saved me.'

,I'm glad I could help.'

,I'm not just saying that. I was considering returning because I didn't have any food. Thanks to you bringing food with you, I was able to save face.'

,The knights in the city must resent me.'

,That wouldn't have happened. They would have packed food and left for Yet Mountain right away.'

After finishing our meal, we walked side by side towards the top of the mountain. The Yet Slug groups were no problem for us. Without me having to step in, she would unleash Sword energy, and the slugs would die instantly.

I looked at the Yet Slug corpses with regretful eyes. I wanted to get Yet powder, but I was conscious of Seira's presence. Yet Slugs are the property of the Arpiss family.

We arrived at the top of the mountain around the time the sun began to set. It felt like we could touch the clouds in the sky if we reached out our hands.

,I don't know.'

,Pardon?'

,I mean the location of the Yet Slug Queen. I thought she would be near the top, but I don't see any Yet Slug Queen, or even ordinary Yet Slugs.'

,Don't you know any characteristics of the Yet Slug Queen?'

,I don't know anything other than that there are numerous Yet Slugs around the Yet Slug Queen.'

,Hmm....'

I looked down at the rocky mountain below. When I concentrated mana in my eyesight and looked down, I could see a few groups of Yet Slugs. But the Yet Slug Queen was nowhere to be found.

,Yet Slugs, like slugs, like shady places.'

,That's right.'

,Isn't there a high possibility that the Yet Slug Queen is holed up in a damp place like a cave?'

,There's some truth to what you say. But I don't know the geography of Yet Mountain.'

,Neither do I.'

,.......'

,.......'

We sensed it. A path of hardship had opened wide.

It became evening. We found a place between rocks where we could avoid the wind. Unfortunately, we didn't have a tent or blanket, so we had to rely on our coats to sleep. It was fortunate that our bodies weren't ordinary.

Seira had a rather dissatisfied expression.

,With our skills, shouldn't we be able to move around at night without any problems? Is there really a need to sleep?'

,Both I and gongnyeo-nim are human. The dark night makes people tired quickly and accumulates fatigue. It's also difficult to find the Yet Slug Queen in the dark. It's better to rest well and move actively during the day.'

,Sir. The city will only be safe if we kill the Yet Slug Queen as soon as possible.'

,There are knights and soldiers in the city. Don't you trust them, Seira-gongnyeo-nim?'

,......You're right, I have nothing to say.'

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim. I am an expert in this field. Please trust me and follow my lead.'

,...Alright. Make sure to wake me up in 4 hours. I won't forgive you if you give me unnecessary consideration just because I'm a gongnyeo.'

,Don't worry, I'll wake you up like clockwork. I like sleeping.'

I like sleeping with women even more, though. I swallowed those words.

Seira looked at the night sky and closed her eyes.

I waited until she was completely asleep. Of course, she wouldn't be completely defenseless. Touching her body was out of the question, and she would open her eyes wide even if I just got close.

,An hour has passed. She must be sleeping deeply now. I've been waiting for this moment.'

I took a bottle out of my inventory.

A transparent bottle.

But inside it contained a substance that vaporized Yet powder.

Next, I took a gas mask out of my inventory and put it on my face.

「Purification Gas Mask

Purifies the air.

Rank: C」

I gauged the direction of the wind and opened the mouth of the glass bottle. The colorless and odorless Yet smoke rode the wind towards Seira. In less than a minute, her body twitched. I took another bottle out of my inventory and opened its mouth.

,She'll be very sensitive when she wakes up. Heh heh.'

After using all five glass bottles, I moved away from her. I moved to hunt Yet Slugs nearby. I needed to replenish the Yet smoke.
