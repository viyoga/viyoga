There's an element that's indispensable in RPG games.

Dungeons.

The original game, "Dark Moon," naturally has dungeons too. However, in this game, dungeons are one-time use only. Once you clear it, you can't enter again. And some dungeons have entry conditions.

,The most common entry condition is that you can only enter when the Dark Moon is up.'

In this world, dungeons are a kind of singularity space. Usually, it's a place where space is distorted and strange powers arise due to the Dark Moon. Broadly speaking, special ancient ruins could also be considered dungeons. For example, the Floating Island on the Han River.

,In the original game, you can get to levels 1-6 just by raising your level. But to break through level 7 or higher, you need the basic level plus special quests or items.'

Level 7 is relatively easy. You can become level 7 just by clearing a dungeon that matches your class.

,After level 7, leveling up becomes disgustingly difficult, and the items you have to collect and the quests you have to clear also increase. ...No. There's no reason for me to worry about level 8 right now.'

What's worrying is that unlike the game, this is reality.

,The elements of the game aren't 100% implemented in reality. I've already confirmed that.'

For example, in the original game, there are boxes hidden all over Neo London where you can get items or money. But if you look for them directly, there are no boxes. Of course. If there were boxes, would people just leave them alone?

,I hope this isn't a wild goose chase.'

I put on the No.6 Hallucination Dream and changed my face with Absolute Polymorph. It was a face that roughly contained personal desires from my past life memories.

,Eugene Meyer has become too famous. Even country bumpkins recognize my face. If I move openly, there's a high chance I'll get caught up in unnecessary things.'

I also wore a robe and pulled down the hood. A getup that screams "wizard." It's a bit old-fashioned by today's standards, but some wizards dress like this, so it's not that unusual.

,People hardly talk to wizards openly. This is perfect.'

People subtly stepped aside when I passed by. They don't get close to wizards. It's basic common sense in this world.

The place I arrived at was a small village on the border of Neo London. But for a small village, there were a lot of people. And most of them were armed mercenaries.

Their destinations were divided into two places. One was a pub, the other was the bus terminal.

My steps headed towards the bus terminal.

The bus terminal building was made of pillars and tarps. The tarps were torn in places, as if they weren't doing even the minimum maintenance. Under the tarps were bus drivers wearing reinforced armor. They were leisurely enjoying tea time, sipping coffee and smoking cigarettes.

The mercenaries couldn't easily approach the bus drivers. They were careful with their words and actions. A driver is a combat weapon with at least level 4 or higher strength. And they're wearing reinforced armor? It's no different from a tank with mobility. They're not opponents that mercenaries can mess with carelessly.

The mercenaries waited with bated breath, so as not to offend the drivers.

Ttiring! Ttiring!

A cheap alarm clock placed on the drivers' table rings. One of the drivers who was sitting got up from his seat with a rotten expression.

"Haa."

"Hehehe. Is it your turn? Suffer, you bastard."

"Shut up. You're on night duty."

"But I'm resting like this now."

The driver gave his colleague the middle finger and headed to one of the buses parked next to the tarp. And he looked around at the mercenaries at the entrance.

"This bus goes to the Raven Zone in the combat zone. The bus fare is 500,000 credits."

One of the mercenaries timidly protested.

"500,000 credits...! It wasn't 400,000 credits until a week ago?! Suddenly, it's gone up by 100,000 credits in just one day!"

"The activities of hostile forces have increased this week. If the fare is too expensive, then don't go. Our Raven Knights don't force anyone. It's your choice to walk to the combat zone if you think the bus fare is too expensive. And it's the result of your choice if you die on the road."

"......."

The driver was nonchalant, whether the mercenaries glared or not. The relationship between the parties was clear. The mercenaries had no choice but to open their wallets. Seeing that there were no mercenaries short on money, it seems that 500,000 credits was within the expected range.

My turn came. It was the last one. I handed the driver five 100,000 credit bills.

The driver took the bills and said.

"Take off your hood. There are so many crazy bastards that I need to see your face."

I quietly took off my hood.

The driver's eyes widened when he saw my face.

"Well, you're handsome. I see why you were wearing a hood. Women must make a fuss every time you go somewhere. And you don't seem like some run-of-the-mill wizard either. What's your name?"

"Cha Eun-yu."

"Damn it. Even your exotic name is cool."

The driver, who seemed resentful, gritted his teeth and lowered his gaze a little more to look at my crotch. The gaze that seemed somewhat desperate soon changed to despair.

"...Get on. Life is so unfair."

I put my hood back on and got on the bus with a crow painted on it. I looked around. There were quite a few empty seats.

,The bus condition is... not too bad. At least it doesn't smell like Urine,Piss.'

I headed to the back of the bus. The seat I had marked was already taken by another mercenary. The front seat of the very back seat. The end seat was surprisingly uncomfortable, so I chose the seat in front of it. It was a seat where I could see the inside of the bus clearly.

"What is it, wizard? Can't you see I sat here first? Get lost."

"That's my seat. Move unless you want to die."

"What?"

Hwareureuk.

Flames blazed on the palm of my hand. The mercenary swallowed briefly. I cast magic without incantation. He realized that alone meant I was no ordinary person.

Pajijijijik!

Blue sparks flew around the flames. A sight of handling two elemental magics at the same time. The mercenary's spirit was crushed.

"I'll give you 5 seconds."

"...You fucking bastard!"

The mercenary jumped up. He walked towards the front of the bus, away from me. He was putting on a show, but his body was trembling. The mercenary spoke to the driver standing in front.

"Driver-yang! Take a look at what that crazy bastard is doing! He's causing a ruckus on the bus! Is this okay?!"

The driver had a lukewarm face. He picked at his ear with his pinky finger.

"My ear is itchy. Sounds like some weakling is barking...."

"Driver-yang!!"

"Shut up. I'm not your mom. Complain to your mom. Ah, your mom is in hell? You can go to hell after you die and complain to your mom then. Should I send you to your mom myself?"

The driver exuded killing intent. The mercenary stammered and backed away. The mercenaries nearby laughed and ridiculed him.

I looked at the mercenaries sitting in the back seats.

"Why aren't you guys leaving? Do you want me to burn you?"

"You're going to take up all these seats by yourself? Are you kidding me?"

"I get annoyed when I have guys like you behind me, making me nervous. Are your parents in hell too?"

"...Fuck. Fine. I get it. This is fucked up, seriously. You goddamn wizard bastard...."

The mercenaries vacated their seats, cursing. I seriously considered burning these uncivilized mercenary bastards who were insulting this noble, but I stopped.

,If I really cause an accident here, I'll get kicked out by the driver.'

I even paid 500,000 credits to ride the bus. It's tiring to walk to the combat zone, leaving the means of transportation behind. The reason why the bus fare is ridiculously expensive is because it's relatively safe, as well as fast.

"We're departing. The bus won't stop for 5 hours. If you make a fuss because you need to take a Urine,Piss or shit, I'll blow your fucking mouth off. Anyone who pisses or shits on the bus will be thrown out."

The driver stepped on the accelerator of the bus. The speed instantly reached 200km per hour. The bus sped across the prairie.

The bus was fast. The problem was that it was only fast. The inside of the bus shook like a ride, as it wasn't a proper road. Some mercenaries didn't look good even though it hadn't been 3 minutes yet. But they'll endure it. They're not ordinary people either.

I looked out the window and thought about the combat zone.

Combat Zone.

The center of the continent.

A place overflowing with all kinds of resources such as monsters, plants, minerals, and dungeons. A place where wars constantly break out to seize those resources.

In order to prevent it from turning into a major war, and to prevent the monopoly of a specific country, international treaties were created in the combat zone.

According to international treaties, countries cannot interfere in the combat zone. Of course, that means they can't do it openly. They were interfering behind the scenes.

Forces sponsored by countries and corporations occupy an area of the combat zone and mine resources.

,The reason why conflicts in the combat zone never end is because resources are infinite. To be exact, resources are regenerated.'

The center of the continent is heavily influenced by the Dark Moon. Even if you mine resources, they are restored when the Dark Moon rises.

Resources that pour out infinitely if you just have time. It's a region that any country can covet, so conflicts never cease.

,The combat zone was restricted by national treaties to prevent a world war that could arise from conflicts in the combat zone.'

A country that openly crosses the line in this combat zone becomes a global enemy and is destroyed.

At first, international treaties were not well observed, but now, 150 years later, they were being observed reasonably well.

At least countries don't openly intervene in the combat zone.

About an hour after the bus started running. A man on a motorcycle on the right side races towards the bus.

Behind the biker, whose face is covered with a mask, more than 30 water buffaloes are rushing fiercely.

Red-eyed water buffaloes with thorns sticking out all over their bodies. They are monster Thorn Water Buffaloes.

"What the fuck! What is that crazy bastard?!"

"He's running this way!"

While the mercenaries were panicking and gathering their belongings, the driver who was driving the bus chewed on the cigarette in his mouth and distorted his face.

"Don't panic, you newbie mercenary bastards. It's always happening in the combat zone. They're trying to kill us before we arrive to reduce the competition! What are you doing! You retards! Are the guns just for show?!"

"Are you going to support the cost of bullets?!"

"If you go to hell saving bullets, will your mom welcome you? Huh? You idiot bastard?"

"......."

The mercenaries cursed and fired their guns out the window.

A barrier automatically unfolds around the man on the motorcycle, blocking the bullets. Some mercenaries tried to break through the barrier using magic bullets, but it was in vain.

"It's a barrier of at least level 5 or higher!"

"Does anyone have magic bullets for large monsters?"

"Do you know how much that costs? Even if they have it, they'll pretend they don't."

When the atmosphere among the mercenaries turned bad, I also opened the window. The wind coming through the window was stinging.

[Fire Ball]

A fireball created in the air flies towards the biker. The biker couldn't ignore the fireball like the pouring bullets. He quickly turned the handle to attempt evasion. It was the moment when the fireball barely grazed the biker's shoulder.

,Explode.'

Pueoooong!

The fireball exploded. The impact broke the barrier, and the biker fell off the motorcycle and was trampled to death by the running Thorn Water Buffaloes.

"Nice! That bastard is dead!"

"Serves him right! You son of a bitch!"

"But that herd of water buffaloes isn't stopping!"

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

Fireballs fly towards the herd of water buffaloes in succession. The fireballs exploded as soon as they touched the herd of water buffaloes. Pieces of the water buffaloes' bodies, whose entire bodies were shattered by the impact and heat, flew everywhere.

The fireballs pouring in succession were no different from missiles.

Three fireballs wiped out half of the herd of water buffaloes, but the herd didn't stop. I continued to chant.

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

[Fire Ball]

Around the time when the mana load started to strain slightly due to the continuous magic casting.

The herd of Thorn Water Buffaloes that had been chasing the bus relentlessly was annihilated.

"......."

Instead of cheering, the mercenaries were watching me carefully. The mercenary who had a brief argument with me was trembling all over.

In the chilling atmosphere despite using fire magic, the driver's hearty laughter spread.

"Hahahahaha! There was a real one among the losers!"
