,Seong Yujin!!"

Just as Ju Seohyun was about to rush after Seong Yujin in desperation, four vampires appeared and blocked her path.

,Butcher...!"

The vampires blocking her way hesitated when they actually faced Ju Seohyun. The Butcher Ju Seohyun's notoriety was well-known among vampires. There wasn't a vampire who didn't know the story of how over ten vampires had attacked to kill Ju Seohyun but failed.

,Don't back down! That bitch is alone! If we can't kill her, Yana-nim will kill us! Surely you know how cruel Yana-nim is? It'd be better to die by that bitch's hand!"

,Damn it! This isn't why I became a vampire!"

,It's too late to shout about it now! Charge all at once!"

The four vampires charged simultaneously.

Ju Seohyun gritted her teeth and rushed toward them. She was pushing herself harder than usual. Normally, she would have regulated her breathing and countered the enemy's attacks. That was the most efficient way to deal with vampires who fought using their physical abilities. But right now, she was impatient.

,Seong Yujin isn't the Seong Yujin from Atlantis right now!'

Even though she felt impatient, the combat skills etched into her body didn't disappear. Her feet perfectly executed the footwork as she approached the vampires, and her hand drew her sword from her waist. The sword, sliding out of its scabbard, swept past the neck of the vampire in front faster than the wind.

Another vampire's hand aimed for Ju Seohyun's right forearm. The moment it was caught by the vampire's hand, her forearm would be crushed.

,Instant acceleration.'

She used the ability she had since birth. An ability she developed by observing Seong Yujin's fleeting moments. Her time sped up, and relatively, the world's time slowed down.

Ju Seohyun had to make quick decisions. Unlike Seong Yujin, she could only maintain this accelerated world for less than three seconds. In that short time, she sought the optimal sword path.

The sword's blade shone. The sword light drew a circle around Ju Seohyun, spinning. The vampires' arms that touched the circle were cut off, and sword wounds were etched into their bodies. Vampire blood splattered, staining Ju Seohyun's suit.

She had dealt with all the vampires and was about to go out the window when she heard a sound like something dragging along the ground, and something moved from the floor.

Ju Seohyun swung her sword. An arm that had been crawling on the floor shot upwards. It was a vampire's arm. Ju Seohyun clicked her tongue. It was a mistake. She was too hasty and hadn't confirmed the vampire's death. She moved to kill the vampire.

The vampire who had been lying on the floor turned black. The best surprise attack had failed. He desperately opened his mouth.

,B-Butcher! Aren't you curious about your partner's well-being?!"

Ju Seohyun didn't waver. Her eyes became even colder. Her mind, which had been clouded by impatience, began to turn coldly again.

,If it's Yana, she'll know. If it's that woman, she wouldn't kill Seong Yujin right away. If she had intended to kill Seong Yujin from the start, there would have been no need to capture him."

,Y-Yana-nim's target isn't your partner, it's you!"

,I know that without you telling me. We have a bad history."

,Spare me! I'll guide you to where Yana-nim is!"

,Don't lie. You don't know where Yana is, do you?"

Ju Seohyun wore a confident expression. The vampire instinctively knew that he couldn't persuade her.

,...How do you know so well?"

,I told you. We have a bad history."

Ju Seohyun's sword cut off the vampire's neck.

Ju Seohyun looked out the window. She couldn't sense any trace of the guy who had kidnapped Seong Yujin. She looked at the floor. There weren't even any footprints. Unfortunately, she didn't have the knack for chasing after vanished vampires.

She waited quietly in that spot. She didn't rashly contact the company to request support. Knowing Yana's personality, she would kill Seong Yujin the moment additional support arrived from the company.

,If Yana's target is me, she'll contact me. This situation is similar to the incident in Russia.'

Bbiririririririri!

A cell phone rang from one of the vampire corpses. Ju Seohyun rummaged through the vampire corpse's clothes and picked up the phone. It was a flip phone, a model from before smartphones. It was definitely a burner phone.

,....."

Ju Seohyun opened the phone and answered the call. A woman's laughter could be heard from the speaker.

-Long time no see, Butcher.

,Where are you?"

-Don't be noisy and listen to me.

,....."

Ju Seohyun closed her mouth.

Yana.

A noble-class vampire from Russia. Her notoriety was great within the company. When she was in Russia, she kidnapped Jaeger's child, took them hostage, and killed Jaeger and the children. It was best not to provoke her rashly.

-I wonder what kind of expression you're making right now? Is it like my face after you cut off my arm? It's a shame I can't see your face right now.

In the past, Ju Seohyun had failed to kill Yana and let her escape.

,....."

-Butcher. You were the most annoying bitch I've ever seen. You were too perfect. Beautiful, strong, and without family or friends, you were flawless. But, oh my god. You got a partner? And a partner you chose yourself? I knew it as soon as I saw the pictures of you and your partner taken from afar. You two are lovers, aren't you?

,You're wrong. He and I aren't like that."

-Then what kind of relationship is it?

,...Enemies. He's my enemy. An enemy I have to kill myself, with my own hands."

So she couldn't let him die at Yana's hands. Seong Yujin had to die by her hand.

-Huh? Ahahahaha! You think I'd fall for that kind of lie? You fucking bitch! If you want to deceive me, hide your trembling voice first! You're terrible at acting too. You can't do anything but swing a sword, can you? Just like the Butcher you are.

,....."

Yana cursed at Ju Seohyun for a long time.

Ju Seohyun silently listened to Yana's curses. She strongly felt that Yana was buying time while cursing at her.

Whenever Ju Seohyun tried to move, Yana said as if she were a ghost.

-Don't move from there.

About five minutes passed like that.

Yana's cursing finally ended.

-He's here, your man. So this is the type you like? He's less impressive than I thought. What's so good about this guy?

,I told you. He's my enemy."

-Ah, yes, yes. So that's how you want to play it? Then so be it. Here, it's your man's voice.

-Kkeuaaak!

Seong Yujin's scream echoed. Ju Seohyun reflexively raised her hand to the sword at her waist.

-Sorry. His fingers broke. Human bodies are so weak.

,....."

-They say love and hate are only a paper-thin apart. Surely you're not spouting nonsense about enemies because of that saying, are you? Hmm. Should I avenge your enemy for you?

,Don't do anything unnecessary. He has to die by my hand."

-Tone down your voice. Your murderous intent is coming through the phone. Ah, is that murderous intent directed at me? Or is it directed at your enemy?

,....."

-Aren't you going to answer? Well, whatever. Let's meet in person. Don't contact the company and come to the place written in the text message. If you do anything unnecessary or are late for the appointment, your man will die.

Ttuk.

The call was disconnected. And less than 10 seconds later, a text message arrived. It contained the time and the name of a hotel.

,A luxury hotel?'

It wasn't a suitable place for vampires to carry out their work. Ju Seohyun put her doubts aside and grabbed the steering wheel. There wasn't enough time to think slowly.

When Ju Seohyun's car arrived at the hotel, a message arrived with a new time and place.

Whether it was to check if there were any trackers, they were running Ju Seohyun around in circles. And Ju Seohyun had no choice but to follow.

,Shibal. I let my guard down. I never thought I'd be kidnapped. If I had been carrying a sword instead of a gun, this wouldn't have happened.'

Regret was always too late. I quietly raised my head and looked around.

It was an old warehouse. One side was full of trash, and the walls were rusty. The smell of the sea wafted subtly through the night breeze.

,I saw it when I was being captured earlier. I don't know exactly where, but it was a deserted warehouse. I don't know which sea it is, but... the guy who captured me was damn fast.'

The vampire who kidnapped me collapsed on the floor and fell asleep as soon as he entered the warehouse. Judging by the fact that he was sweating profusely, it seemed like it had been quite difficult for him.

My arms and upper body were bound by iron chains. Four fingers on my left hand, excluding the thumb, were broken. I could move my legs, but moving them would attract the attention of the vampires.

Knowing her true form, I wasn't turned on. Not only her, but the true forms of all vampires were basically ugly.

,She's probably planning to kill Ju Seohyun by taking me hostage.'

Over 60 vampires.

,Shibal. I'm the captured princess? I absolutely can't stand that kind of cancer-inducing development.'

Besides, that bitch broke my fingers. If a beautiful woman broke my fingers, I might forgive her once, but a monster bitch dared to break my fingers?

,I'll kill her.'

Jeolgeureok.

Kung!
