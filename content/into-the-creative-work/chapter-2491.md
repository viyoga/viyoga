Yujin-myeong began to tell his story.

Yujin-myeong survived the first Devil's Game and received a reward. After that, his life changed.

At a place called Devil Capital, 100 million won was deposited into his account under the guise of winning an event. Clean money that didn't even need to be taxed.

With that money, he first paid for hospital bills. His younger sister, who had an incurable disease, would be able to receive treatment without worrying about money for the time being.

He invested the remaining money in himself.

Debts? It wasn't enough to repay the money he borrowed from relatives, friends, banks, and so on. He judged that it would be better to invest in himself instead. To be exact, he decided to bet on the Devil's Game.

,I don't know if it's really a game hosted by the devil... but it's definitely a surreal thing. Maybe I can cure Jia's disease.,

He needed information. Information to play the next Devil's Game.

,Devil's Game. This might be the first time, but... I have to try what I can.,

If you win the Devil's Game, your life changes. In other words, you can find people whose lives have changed.

First, he searched for Devil's Game on the internet. Only comics, games, novels, and movies came up. There was no meaningful information.

After that, he looked for people who had succeeded in a short period of time or in an unnatural way. He spent some money in this process. It was a waste, but the most important thing right now was time.

He found eight people. Among them, three were from debtor backgrounds.

And all three of them had miraculously built up their businesses from being debtors and succeeded. And with his truth discernment ability, he knew that they were game participants.

"Two of them were walking around with personal bodyguards. I couldn't even approach them."

"The other one... a wasted guy?"

"He was the president of a construction company that people in the know knew about. But he wasn't doing any work and was fishing in the Han River. Even when a fish bit the bait, he didn't move. I carefully approached him and asked him about the Devil's Game."

The man told him how cruel the Devil's Game was. Monsters, people, friends, colleagues, lovers... a game where you have to kill anyone. He survived the Devil's Game, but he was on the verge of collapse.

"I heard information about the Devil's Game from him, and he committed suicide by jumping into the Han River yesterday."

Yujin-myeong quietly looked at the expressions of the people. They swallowed their sighs or frowned. Tension and anxiety were evident. Except for one person.

"So, the information?"

Seong Yujin didn't even flinch. He didn't seem to care who died.

"The first two games in the Devil's Game have predetermined events. Surviving a murderer and a room escape game with a murderer among the members. If you win the room escape game, you will receive game points. First, let's check the game point menu."

The moment he thought of the game point menu, a semi-transparent notification window appeared in the air.

「Game Point Menu

10 million won - 1 GP

Game tool and ability draw - 30 GP

Game tool and ability enhancement - 50 GP

Game participation invitation - 50 GP

Game escape ticket - 100 GP

Grand Game participation ticket (remaining seats: 71) - 100 GP」

「Available Game Points: 10」

"The Devil's Game has a season, and the Grand Game is the last game of the season. After the Grand Game ends, the Devil's Game will not happen for a while. The winners of the Grand Game can fulfill reasonable wishes and permanently acquire the abilities and tools obtained from the Devil's Game."

"What is a reasonable wish?"

"It means that the devil grants reasonable wishes. Absurd wishes like world domination are impossible. I guess it's not a reasonable wish from the devil's point of view. I heard that you can even revive the dead."

Revive. Everyone's eyes widened. In particular, Shin Do-hyun flinched greatly.

"That guy participated in the Grand Game last season and won?"

"He neither won nor lost in the Grand Game. He escaped with a game escape ticket."

He explained what kinds of events there were in the Devil's Game. Simple games and complex games. The common point of these games is that someone's blood must flow. Whether it's the participants or something the devil prepared.

"What the Devil's Game can change is your own life. It cannot change the lives of close friends or family. I want to cure my younger sister's incurable disease. To do that, I need a wish ticket. But the Devil's Game is not an easy game that you can win alone until the end. I need reliable colleagues. I hope you will become my colleagues."

Yujin-myeong looked at Seong Yujin.

The most needed person here was Seong Yujin. That skill of slicing people in 3 seconds. If he becomes a colleague, he will be very reliable when physical strength is needed.

,It's easy to know what Seong Yujin wants. From the beginning, he keeps looking at Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young whenever he has a chance. He pretends not to, but if you look closely, you can tell.,

He already seems to regard Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young as his women. He is especially interested in Shin Do-hyun.

,Seong Yujin is a beast. He can't be tamed. If I treat him carelessly, he will bite me to death.,

In short, a crazy bastard.

He is the type of person he would never have dealt with normally. But in the devil's ability, his combat ability is essential. He even has the fraudulent ability of advice.

If he becomes a colleague, there will be no one more reliable than Seong Yujin. Bae Hyun-soo, Shin Do-hyun, and Jo Na-young are all aware of this fact, so they are subtly watching Seong Yujin.

The problem is that Seong Yujin has never looked at him favorably until now.

,I'm not using Seong Yujin. I have to make Seong Yujin use me.,

Yujin-myeong's goal is to cure his younger sister with a wish ticket.

Nothing else mattered. Whether it's money, women, or alcohol. He can just give it to Seong Yujin.

Of course, he acknowledges the beauty of Shin Do-hyun and Jo Na-young.

,A woman with a strong spirit is a bit... It reminds me of that woman who abandoned her family for her own ambition....,

Yujin-myeong's belief is that it is better to live alone for the rest of his life than to be swayed by a woman.

"Ah, there is also a rule that the game organizer didn't tell you. If you kill a game participant in reality, you get the GP that participant has. So, it's best to hide the fact that you are a game participant as much as possible."

"I wasn't going to tell anyone even if you didn't say that. Even if I did tell them, I don't think they would believe me."

Jo Na-young said.

Even if that weren't the case, would a devil with god-like power not be able to control information?

"...Well, then, can I be sure that everyone here has agreed to be a team, a colleague? Ah, is there anyone who doesn't want to participate in the Grand Game? If so, I would like you to tell me now."

"......"

Fortunately, no one, including Seong Yujin, protested. Yujin-myeong breathed a sigh of relief inwardly. It seems that the first button was somehow buttoned up well.

"Now, I would like to confirm each other's goals. I think it's necessary to know as colleagues. Hyun-soo-ya. You go first."

"Uh, I'm going to be the best professional gamer in the world. I was originally bronze, but my skills improved with the game reward, so I became silver. My wish is to get the best gaming talent in the world."

Yujin-myeong was speechless inside, but he didn't show it.

"What is Jo Na-young-ssi's goal?"

"Me? It's similar to Hyun-soo-rang. To become the best idol in the world. After winning the last game, my skin got better and my dancing skills improved. Thanks to that, I was able to debut. If it wasn't for the devil's power... I wouldn't have been able to debut and would have been kicked out. But I can't be satisfied with just this."

Jo Na-young's eyes shone brightly with ambition.

Yujin-myeong obtained new information. The Devil's Game can affect not only talent but also appearance.

,A woman who can risk her life for her own ambition... I should be careful because she might betray me.,

Yujin-myeong's gaze turned to Shin Do-hyun. Shin Do-hyun frowned for a moment and then opened his mouth.

"...To revive the collapsing business. And... to save my dead mother. If what you said is true, I can revive my mother with a wish ticket."

Yujin-myeong saw Seong Yujin, who had been indifferent, nodding his head greatly at Shin Do-hyun's words. He needs to be careful when dealing with Shin Do-hyun.

"What is Yujin-ssi's goal?"

"To kill a loan shark and... a man who used to be a friend."

Seong Yujin said in a solemn tone for no reason. It was something worth saying. He is confidently declaring murder.

,A man who used to be a friend? I'll skip over personal issues that could be sensitive...,

"Are you suffering from loan sharks? I will help Yujin-ssi."

"Me? I did need help. That bastard is not an ordinary loan shark. He's a guy named Paeng Cheon-soo...."

Seong Yujin complained about how much of a son of a bitch Paeng Cheon-soo was. He even seemed excited. Maybe he needed someone to complain to.

"Wait. It's very strange that Paeng Cheon-soo is focusing on loan sharking even though he makes a lot of money from other things besides loan sharking."

"I think so too. But that guy is a natural-born psycho, so ordinary people can't understand him."

"...As far as I know, most of the participants in the Devil's Game are unhappy people. In modern society where capitalism is rampant, if you were to pick unhappy people... you could say they are debtors. This is just my speculation, but Paeng Cheon-soo may know about the Devil's Game and be trying to use it for his own purposes."

"Is that so?"

Seong Yujin seemed calm.

Rather, Yujin-myeong's face was serious. If Paeng Cheon-soo is professionally training Devil's Game participants... his goal is obviously the wish ticket.

Paeng Cheon-soo has no choice but to become the biggest and worst enemy.

"Regarding Paeng Cheon-soo... I will investigate him. Shin Do-hyun-ssi. Do you happen to be doing business in Seoul? If it's okay, I would like you to work with Yujin-ssi."

"Me?"

Shin Do-hyun had an unpleasant expression. But there's no choice. In Yujin-myeong's view, Shin Do-hyun is the only one who can control Seong Yujin in this place right now. Even now, Seong Yujin is looking at Shin Do-hyun with expectant eyes.

"Shin Do-hyun-ssi is the only one who can help Yujin-ssi. Even now, Yujin-ssi is being chased by Paeng Cheon-soo. If something happens to Yujin-ssi, it will be difficult for all of us."

Jo Na-young is an idol, so it's obviously impossible. Bae Hyun-soo? What can you expect from a high school student? Yujin-myeong himself was not confident that he could completely control Seong Yujin.

"...Ha. I said business, but it's actually a factory. A meal kit manufacturing factory. I don't need freeloaders, so you'll have to work at the factory as much as you eat. The location is near Suwon. Will you still come?"

"Did I mention that I used to work in a factory until recently? I'll help you as much as you want."

"Hmm."

Seong Yujin actively appealed to himself. Shin Do-hyun sighed and nodded. Yujin-myeong felt that Seong Yujin's eyes looking at him had softened a little. It didn't seem difficult to buy Seong Yujin's favor.

"We've exchanged phone numbers, so let's contact each other when we get back to reality. There's not much time left, so let's escape now."

They got up from their seats and went outside.
