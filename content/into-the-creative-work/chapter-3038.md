The moment I stepped inside the secret realm, what struck me was the coolness.

Thanks to the spiritual energy so abundant it couldn't even be compared to the outside, I felt a crispness and liberation like stepping out of a cramped room into wide-open space.

'The spiritual energy here is several times richer than the spirit vein of Nakyue Mountain. If one trained here, even a talentless mortal would easily break through realms.'

I spread out my qi waves to survey the surroundings.

'The bastards who entered first aren't even registering in my qi sense, let alone hiding to ambush me. I came in through a door, but there's no door behind me. Was there a spatial shift the moment I entered?'

The sky of the secret realm was the same as the sky outside, and the ground of the secret realm was no different from the ground outside. Before my eyes stood five mountains so massive I couldn't believe this was a secret realm. From each of the five mountains, a different energy emanated.

'The energies of the Five Elements. Each mountain is imbued with the energy of a different element.'

The leaves on the Fire mountain were red like autumn foliage. Heat like a shimmering haze rose toward the sky.

The Water mountain was a snow-capped peak. Just looking at it made my eyes sting from the yin energy radiating off it.

The Wood mountain was densely packed with trees. When I focused my qi sense, I could feel all manner of creatures frolicking within. It seemed I could easily procure food from the Wood mountain.

The Earth mountain was composed of soil and rock. Just looking at it made my throat go dry.

The trees on the Metal mountain were made of metal. Every plant was metallic, so even the slightest movement there would probably get you sliced.

"Wow. The Five Elements energies are incredible. If you went to the place matching your attribute and trained, the efficiency would be no joke."

Miryeong marveled.

She was right. There could be no better place for training than this. Especially for cultivators at the Five Qi realm who had just realized their attributes and begun wielding them, this would be a treasure beyond compare.

"If one could train here safely, that is. Even with my qi waves, I can't survey this entire world. Especially when I try to peer inside the mountains, I'm blocked every time."

"It's common for dangerous monsters to emerge from secret realms. Hmm. Should we first figure out the exit? There's probably an exit somewhere. I'd hope there's more than one, but if there's only one... whoever claims it first has the advantage. You could even collect tolls from others."

"And then the bastards who don't want to pay tolls will join hands and come at us. No need to tire ourselves out already. First, we look around. We've confirmed five mountains, so we should confirm the three lakes as well."

There were no objections.

I led the group and began to move.

Food and shelter? Food was no issue at all. I had the inventory. I could pull out something to eat at any time.

'The others won't have food problems either. Anyone who entered this secret realm would have a spatial pouch, and they'd have brought at least enough soldier pills to last decades. If worse comes to worst, they can just hunt animals and plants from the Wood mountain.'

As for shelter, we could just pick a suitable spot and set up a barrier.

"First, I'll thoroughly confirm the geography of this world."

I summoned clouds at my feet using the power of the Cloud-Riding Command. After confirming everyone had climbed onto the clouds, I moved them. The cloud carrying our group flew rapidly into the sky.

The cloud, which had been ascending without obstruction, suddenly stopped.

I reached my hand toward the sky. A transparent ceiling touched my palm. It had a texture similar to glass. Glass wall, my ass. Even if I attacked this ceiling with everything I had right now, I wouldn't be able to break through. This was also the wall of space dividing the secret realm from the world.

"I see. It's blocked so we can't go further. Even though it looks like a vast world, a secret realm is still a secret realm."

Above, I could see a shining sun and drifting clouds. A fake that looked endlessly real.

『Using the light of the Heavenly Star.』

『Heavenly Leader Star (天魁星)』

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Art (雷術) Thunder Eye Golden Pupil (雷眼金睛).

My pupils turned golden, and blue lightning danced in my irises.

I swept the world with my Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, then gazed steadily at the lake. It wasn't just me—the others were looking at the lake too.

"Wasn't this secret realm called Three Lakes Five Mountains? There's only one lake. And it's smaller than I expected."

Miryeong muttered, sounding disappointed.

"If the five mountains represent the Five Elements, then the three lakes must represent the Three Powers. That one is the Lake of Man (人)."

"Ho. As expected, those eyes are cheat-like. You can tell just by looking. But the Three Powers are Heaven, Earth, and Man. Where are the lakes of Heaven and Earth?"

"The Lake of Earth (地) is underground beneath that lake. Conversely, the Lake of Heaven is above that lake's sky. The problem is, the Lake of Heaven is above the invisible ceiling."

"It's up there? I can't see anything but clouds."

"I can see it."

"With those eyes of yours, husband, couldn't you tell what abilities or effects the lake has just by looking at it?"

"My eyes aren't omnipotent. If the target is of a higher rank than me, all I can discern is its name."

"...This lake is higher rank than you, husband?"

What could roughly—if not completely—be converted to rank was cultivation realm.

The human realm, the Human Stage (人境).

Entry Rite (入式) ‣ Emerging Will (出志) ‣ Five Qi (五氣) ‣ Three Peaks (三頂) ‣ Harmony (造化) ‣ Myriad Phenomena (萬象) ‣ Ascension (登仙).

Myriad Phenomena and Ascension were essentially the same realm. The realm of those who did not choose Ascension was called the Myriad Phenomena Stage.

I was at the early stage of Harmony. Someone like me couldn't see the lake's detailed abilities—I could barely see its name.

"If you measure that lake's rank by cultivation realm, it would be at least Myriad Phenomena. It might even belong to the Immortal Stage (仙境)."

My intuition was telling me.

That lake's rank was definitely the Immortal Stage.

"Husband. Are you perhaps planning to enter that lake directly?"

"Not now. First, we explore the secret realm and focus on training. If we're lucky, we can obtain spirit medicines or artifacts."

"Hmm. Then would it be better to split up and act separately?"

Every person has a different innate attribute. In Miryeong's case, it was Fire. For the father-daughter pair Yeon Seorang and Yeon Yeha, it was Metal.

'Thunder qi isn't an energy that belongs to the Five Elements. But if I force it into the framework of the Five Elements... it's closest to Wood. Honestly, I don't really know why, though.'

Even if I trained at the Wood mountain now, it wouldn't be very effective. No, the same went for the other elemental mountains. Thunder qi was heterogenous, so a place where pure lightning struck would be better for training than the energies of the Five Elements.

"Change of plans. You all focus on training. I'll handle roaming around and searching for treasures."

"Cult Leader. It's dangerous. If you were to encounter the Martial Alliance Leader...!"

The Right Protector tried to dissuade me. I shook my head with a resolute expression.

"The one in danger isn't me—it's him. And since the secret realm is this vast, the Martial Alliance won't be able to focus on us right away either. They'll also concentrate on finding the secret realm's treasures rather than killing us. You all focus on training for the next five years. That's an order."

"...We shall obey."

The eight members of the Heavenly Demon Cult bowed their heads. I looked at Miryeong.

"Miryeong. What will you do?"

"Didn't you just give me an order too?"

"You're not affiliated with the Heavenly Demon Cult. And you're at the Harmony realm. It'd be a waste for you to hole up in one place and only train."

"I want to do nothing but train. The fire qi of that Fire mountain is a fated opportunity you could never encounter elsewhere."

Miryeong licked her lips and gazed at the Fire mountain. It was the eye of a predator looking at prey.

"Do as you like. But set up barriers around the others' dwellings."

"That much is easy."

It was decided that each would train intensively for five years at the mountain matching their attribute. Danmok Soha and Yu Yeoryeo had Wood and Water attributes respectively, but since they couldn't bear to be apart, they decided to alternate between the Water mountain and the Wood mountain together.

"Dismissed. If you feel something is dangerous, use the talisman Miryeong gave you. It's a talisman that sends a signal to me."

"The Cult Leader's grace is as vast as the sea."

"I'll check on your progress every half year. Train hard. Especially you, Yeon Yeha. I know you're standing before a wall. If it's you, you'll be able to break through that wall."

"Yes. I will live up to the Cult Leader's expectations."

After sharing a brief heated look with Yeon Yeha, we dispersed. Miryeong followed them to set up barriers around their dwellings.

I rode the clouds, flying back and forth between the five mountains. The martial artists who had entered the secret realm busied themselves hiding whenever they sensed my presence.

'I'm openly revealing my presence, yet the Martial Alliance side is quiet. Does that mean they have no intention of clashing with me right now?'

That was fine by me too. Finding treasures was far more profitable than fighting those bastards.

Especially since I had the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil. I could find treasures far more easily than them.

'An unusual energy I can sense... found it.'

A deep pit-like valley in the Wood mountain. From its center, a heterogenous energy emanated. I immediately rode the clouds there.

The source of the unusual energy was a wooden statue. A 3-meter-tall general statue wearing armor and gripping a spear.

The Thunder Eye Golden Pupil revealed the statue's name.

"Wood Escape General (木遁將軍). True to its name. Its ability varies in combat power depending on the target. A training artifact."

The Wood Escape General opened both eyes. Green light poured out in streams.

A voice flowed from the mouthless Wood Escape General. It was a thick, deep bass like something speaking from a cave.

"After hardship comes sweetness. Cultivator. You must defeat me to obtain the sweet fruit."

The Wood Escape General raised its spear and took a stance. I also summoned Stormbreaker from my inventory and took my stance.

Without either waiting for the other, we lunged at each other simultaneously. Spear shaft clashed against spear shaft in midair, generating a shockwave. I channeled power into my whole body, enduring the shockwave as we exchanged blows.

'A mere artifact, yet it keeps up with the strength and speed of someone at the Harmony realm like me. ...No. Is its ability to replicate my exact physical specs?'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Spear Lightning Form (電槍式) Wave Source (波源).

Crackle.

The spear, accelerated by thunder qi, swept in a circle around me.

From the Wood Escape General's spear, a heavy wood energy gushed out. The Wood Escape General drew energy from the Wood mountain and used it as its own.

KWANG KWANG KWANG KWANG KWAANG!

A chain explosion from the collision of energies. I endured the explosions with my protective qi and thought coldly.

'That bastard is drawing nearly infinite amounts of energy from the Wood mountain. The longer this drags on, the more disadvantageous it becomes for me.'

Then I just needed to not drag it out.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art (天魔神功) Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step (天魔君臨步).

THUD!

A full-powered Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

Even the Wood Escape General, an inanimate object without a self, was affected—its stance broke apart.

Black Thunder (黑雷).

I wrapped black lightning around my spear and entire body and charged at it.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Spear Lightning Form (電槍式) Strong Quake (强震).

A technique that concentrates all power to the front, focused solely on breakthrough. It had the drawback of being unable to change direction or stop once used, but its penetrating power was the highest in the Thunder Heaven Style.

CRACK.

The spear tip pierced through the Wood Escape General's chest.

"Magnificent martial arts. I see the qualities of a Spear Immortal in you."

The Wood Escape General shattered into pieces and vanished.
