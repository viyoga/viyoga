Even though they were corpses, they were beautiful women, so the feeling wasn't entirely unpleasant... but the water ghosts' skin was cold, and I could feel something like scales. Because the Vajra club of Sakra Devanam Indra is much higher in rank as a divine treasure than Habaek's mirror. I struck down once more with the Vajra club, and the mirror shattered into pieces. I was able to go straight out of the mirror.

Thunder Heaven Style Thunder Light.
