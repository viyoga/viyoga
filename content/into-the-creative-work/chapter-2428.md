As the bewilderment faded, my mind started working, trying to figure out the reason I'd become a Trueblood.

,The Illusion Dream isn't the first artifact I've used. It's an artifact that the Great Wizard Merlin used a long time ago. That means, the genetic information of a Trueblood Vampire might have been left since Merlin used it.'

When I used the Illusion Dream, I simply decided to turn into a vampire. And Absolute Polymorph turned me into a Trueblood Vampire.

Unless there's another clue, that's the most plausible reason for now.

,Ha, magic is really... amazing and absurd at the same time. How the hell can I become a Trueblood Vampire?'

,...Arcane magic.'

,Okay, okay. I won't ask. But if you're a Trueblood, you should be careful.'

,You mean the sun?'

,The sun, yeah, but... Trueblood Vampires are very rare, you know. There will be many who covet you. Bio companies will also want you. If you're going to be a vampire, just be a regular vampire. Oh, and watch out for Vampire Hunters.'

,I'm fake anyway.'

Vampires and Vampire Hunters.

The relationship is famous. They used to kill each other in the old days... but now Vampire Hunters don't attack vampires recklessly unless they're criminals.

In the first place, most vampires in this era have become vampires by injecting factors. It's not as hard to get as an elf factor. Except for the fact that they have to consume blood, they are no different from humans. Getting blood is easy too. Artificial blood is cheap.

,I think ordinary humans become Class 2 just by injecting the vampire factor.'

In the original work, classes are divided according to level... but it's a bit different in this reality.

Class 1 refers to those who can feel and use mana.

Class 2 refers to those who can use mana to fight.

Most of those who work as mercenaries are usually Class 3.

,A Trueblood Vampire can turn an ordinary person into a Class 4 High Vampire with just a drop of blood.'

Class 4 is called veterans, excellent soldiers or warriors, living combat weapons, tanks in human form, and so on.

,High Vampires are Class 4 just by their physical abilities. If they gain a little experience, they can become Class 5 or higher.'

From Class 5, they can dominate small-scale battles. Still, up to Class 6, they are treated as humans. From Class 7, they are superhuman. Something beyond humans.

Can I, a Trueblood Vampire, mass-produce High Vampires using blood?

The answer is no.

The moment a part of the body that falls off in the transformed state interacts with something else, that body disappears. It doesn't just revert the transformation, it disappears. But the damage to the main body doesn't disappear.

,I've already tested it.'

Rose created a profile and showed it to me.

A profile made entirely of lies.

,...From the back alleys of Divine France?'

,Your name is French, isn't it? Isn't that what you wanted? Oh, you know Divine France hates vampires and werewolves, right? Be careful.'

,And let's take some pictures. I need them to forge your ID properly. I also need fingerprints and blood... but let's skip that, you're not trying to become an aristocrat, are you? Mercenaries have a lot of shady guys, so you only need minimal trust.'

,You'll vouch for that, right? I'll only take your requests.'

,If you know that, be careful. I'll reveal your identity if necessary. Okay, taking a picture. Smile.'

,......'

Of course, I didn't smile. Rose didn't seem to be serious either, and took a picture roughly. It was quite close.

She smelled good. A sweet smell. Instinctively, I looked for the part where the smell was strongest. It was Rose's nape. A pure white nape visible between the black hair.

Gulp.

I swallowed without realizing it.

,...Ha. Have you become a real vampire or something? You've been staring at my nape since a while ago.'

,I can't help it. It's a vampire's instinct.'

,That sounds very dangerous?'

,I can control it. Even if I can't control it... I can just undo the magic. Like this.'

I turned back into a human.

My appetite disappeared. I can't smell the sweet scent that Rose had either. Instead, there was a subtle rose scent.

However, since I released Absolute Polymorph, it will take some time before I can use it again.

,That's a relief. It seems like you won't be like those guys who can't overcome their vampire instincts and cause accidents. I'll go now. I'll contact you when a suitable request comes in. You'll have to take low-paying requests for a while. You don't have a reputation.'

,I know. I'm the experienced new employee. I'll have to put up with it until my reputation spreads appropriately.'

,Yeah, yeah. See you next time.'

• • •

The reputation of Eugene, the Vampire Warlock and Body Collector of Neo London, began to spread by word of mouth in the mercenary world.

The nickname that sounds like a serial killer is due to a recent incident.

I stole one of the hearts of a client who looked down on me because I was a warlock. I didn't kill him to complete the request.

,The client had three hearts through Life Magic. I just took one of the most expensive magic hearts.'

Originally, it was a type of magic called Magical Biology, but now it is called Life Magic and has become famous for selling artificial organs with magic. Well, it's still a minor magic.

Anyway, the rumors about this incident were exaggerated and distorted, and I got the nickname Body Collector.

Rose let the rumors spread. I remember what she said when I complained a little.

,She said it was a good thing. Thanks to that, your reputation is spreading faster.'

Won't clients be reluctant when they hear the rumors? She scoffed at my worries.

,Eugene. No, Eugene. The higher the level of the request, the higher the level of the client. Clients who are swayed by such rumors? They're just small fries. Clients who entrust you with really dangerous jobs will have figured out all the facts about the rumors.'

,...Well, I did take a real heart.'

,...That's a bit of a problem, but you didn't kill him. The incident started because of that client's mouth. It's up to the client to be careful.'

After that day, I got more requests. Originally, most of the jobs that used mercenaries were illegal, but the requests that specified me were particularly malicious. I handled the requests without discrimination. The success rate of the requests was 100%.

And Rose, whom I met today, was much more serious than usual.

,Congratulations, Body Collector Eugene. A request from a big shot in just three months.'

,A big shot?'

,Alpha Tier. You know?'

I flinched for a moment.

Alpha Tier Pharmaceutical Company. A name I can't forget even if I want to. The culprit who experimented on me. My emotions fluctuated for a moment, but I was confident in my poker face, so I wasn't caught.

,The client is the global pharmaceutical company Alpha Tier?'

,No. There's no way. Alpha Tier is on a different level. What would they use a vampire warlock mercenary who's just making a name for himself in Neo London for? Do you think the job of a corporate agent is there for nothing?'

She was right.

Would Alpha Tier be crazy enough to give me a request? I was momentarily disturbed by the name Alpha Tier and couldn't recall the obvious fact.

,...Is that big shot hostile to Alpha Tier?'

,That's right. Those who are openly hostile. Do you know about Liberals?'

,I know. It's a global newspaper company headquartered in Neo London. In a bad way. A place where anti-corporate activists gather.'

They are the guys who have been annoying me lately. Their anti-corporate sentiment is so extreme that they curse at any company. My company, LK, was no exception. Some of these reporters even tried to break into my mansion.

,When I caught them and asked, their words were ridiculous. They said they were trying to find evidence that LK had created a superconductor through human experimentation. Was the superconductor an invention made through human experimentation?'

At first, I thought they were spies sent by other companies disguised as reporters, but they were just crazy. The other reporters at the Liberals newspaper were all similar.

,That's right. And it's a place that receives enthusiastic support from people. Anti-corporate sentiment has been spreading recently.'

,I know. Most companies have a lot of ethical problems.'

,It's surprising to hear the president of a rising company say that.'

,Spare me the flattery. What's the request?'

,Escorting a reporter to a village outside Neo London and helping the client with their work.'

,...What is that reporter trying to do? It doesn't seem like a good thing if they're hiring a warlock.'

,I heard they got a source of information about a human experimentation village run by Alpha Tier. Liberals wants to confirm if that information is real.'

,It doesn't seem necessary to specify me.'

,They needed a magician who is proficient in blood magic. Specifically, a magician who can use the Class 5 magic, Blood Chaser.'

Blood Chaser is a Class 5 blood magic. Since there are no high-level blood mages in Neo London, the request came to me.

,It seems they don't know exactly where that human experimentation village is.'

,Maybe not. But the reward is pretty good. The location of Ilario Gomez's arcane magic workshop, where he was active 30 years ago. It's exactly the kind of reward you wanted.'

Ilario Gomez. A Class 7 warlock who is already dead at this point.

,...That's for sure. The request reward is so good that it's suspicious. What's the proof that the information is real?'

,The editor-in-chief is Ilario's grandson. He's just an ordinary person who doesn't even know the basics of black magic, but he said he's been to Ilario's workshop a few times when he was young.'

Ilario was famous as a necromancer. I heard he even used demons. I was interested in his arcane magic.

Besides, if the request is successful, I can screw over those Alpha Tier bastards.

I felt more motivated than ever and asked Rose.

,When should I start the request?'
