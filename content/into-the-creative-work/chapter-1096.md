I wake up at 6 AM and do morning roll call.

We run drills to the drill instructor's commands, shower, and then eat breakfast. The breakfast is plentiful for a slave's diet. It's because I'm both a slave and a soldier.

After breakfast, we receive mental training. The excellent points of the Freiselle army, the benefits of working in the Freiselle army, and the hope that one day we can escape slavery and become high-ranking officers are instilled in us.

,It's a ploy to handle successfully born battle mages more easily.'

Next is magic training. We learn basic magic from battle mages working in the field.

Number 211 hated this time.

Although I was forcibly initiated into magic by injecting mana essence into my body, I had no talent for magic.

I learned a few basic spells, but didn't succeed in casting a single one.

,Today, we will take a test. Those who fail to manifest magic will face the consequences. As you all know, it's far too late for you to learn magic. So, do your best.'

Usually, magic is learned by the age of 10 at the latest. Children with magical talent show signs from the age of 5 to 7, and the younger they are, the better they accept mana and the easier it is to form an astral body.

,Number 31. You're first. Come forward and use magic.'

,Yes, Instructor Vinus-nim.'

A woman with purple hair walks forward. White skin and red eyes. From her appearance alone, she was no ordinary woman. Her beauty is astonishingly outstanding. Her gait exudes the dignity of a noble.

The instructor's eyes, looking at Number 31, were complex yet sparkling with anticipation.

I could feel refined mana from Number 31. So far, there were no problems. It's only natural for me, a 1st-class mage, to feel the mana of another 1st-class mage.

,...It's not just mana I'm feeling. I can see a part of the spell that the mana holds. No, I can feel it.'

It was a sensation hard to describe. I could see what wasn't visible, and I could feel what wasn't felt.

I focused on Number 31's spell. I analyze it. The spell of basic magic comes to mind. I don't know more than 30% of the spell that Number 31 is showing. But it wasn't difficult to guess.

,This magic is... Gale.'

Number 31 opened her mouth. The spell was completed. To realize the spell in reality, she utters the final incantation.

,Gale.'

A strong wind blew.

Some of the children, including me, staggered from the wind. I barely managed to right myself just before falling. There were over 50 people here in total, and about 10 of them fell to the floor. They were the children who were relatively close to Number 31.

,Excellent, Number 31. You may do the training you desire.'

As always, Number 31 was given special privileges.

,Thank you, Instructor-nim.'

Number 31 calmly accepted and began reading a magic book in the corner.

,Next, Number 36.'

Instructor Vinus called the next child.

A man, very nervous, stepped forward to the instructor. The freckled man immediately constructs a magic spell at the instructor's gesture.

,A failure. Compared to Number 31, the movement of mana is very unstable.'

There was no way magic could be realized with such an unstable spell.

,Fi, Fire!'

Psh.

A flame flared up once and then died out in a second with a deflating sound. Number 36's face turned pale.

,Terrible. Follow the soldier outside to receive your punishment.'

Instructor Vinus's face was expressionless.

,...Yes, Instructor-nim.'

Number 36 walked out powerlessly.

The punishment Instructor Vinus is talking about is the injection of mana essence.

,It's called mana essence, but in reality, it's a liquid mixed with concentrated mana and drugs.'

Mana essence increases mana affinity. At first glance, it might seem good, but there are side effects. The aging of the internal organs progresses rapidly, and lifespan is reduced. In addition, the moment mana essence is injected into the body, tremendous pain overwhelms you. If you can't endure the pain, your astral body collapses, and you either die or become disabled.

The slaves here, including me, are periodically injected with mana essence.

It might seem good because mana affinity increases immediately, but it's not good in the long run. Receiving mana essence frequently, like the failed Number 36, only hastens the limit.

,Next, Number 37.'

Instructor Vinus continued the test.

,Number 211. Come out.'

My turn came in 20 minutes. I swallowed hard and walked forward. I had never succeeded in manifesting magic before.

,Don't dawdle and start immediately.'

Instructor Vinus spoke in a tone filled with annoyance. The eyes looking at me are emotionless. There wasn't even 1% of expectation.

I focused my concentration. The astral body reacts to my will, and mana races through the five mana roads. The mana roads construct a spell in the mana. The spells gather and merge into one. I confirmed once more through the astral body that the constructed spell was perfect.

All that remains is the realization of magic.

I recited the incantation with tension.

,Light.'

A pure white sphere of light appeared. The light spread in all directions, illuminating the surroundings.

Instructor Vinus's eyes widened for a moment and then returned to normal.

,...A clean magic manifestation. Your skills have improved so much compared to last time. I heard you experienced mana backflow yesterday... Did you gain some kind of enlightenment?'

,I'm not sure, but I feel like something has become easier.'

I answered vaguely. Honestly, I didn't even think I would succeed so easily.

,Did you unconsciously gain enlightenment? It happens sometimes. Number 211. Can you use other magic?'

The Gale magic that Number 31 used suddenly came to mind. I had only seen the Gale magic spell just now, but it didn't feel difficult, perhaps because it was the same 1st-class magic. I think I could do it if I tried.

,...There's no need to attract attention here. If I fail, there's no end to the embarrassment.'

Number 31 receives special treatment. There was no point in causing friction with her.

,...I think I can use Fire magic.'

,Try it.'

Light and Fire are called the basics among 1st-class magic. Perhaps that's why more than 80% of the spells are similar. As long as I don't make a big mistake, manifestation won't be difficult.

,Fire.'

I formed the spell and recited the incantation.

Hwareureureuk.

An orange flame blazed where the sphere of light had been.

The flame burning in the air without any flammable material was truly magic.

,That's enough. Turn it off.'

Instructor Vinus's command was given.

The flame flickered out at my will.

The instructor smiles with satisfaction.

,Perfect control too. Number 211. You should learn other magic. I personally recommend Ice. Have you learned Ice by any chance?'

,No. I haven't learned it.'

,Learn Ice and compare it with Fire. If you find out which one is more comfortable, you can find out your attribute. You seem like you'll easily rise to 2nd class... It wouldn't be bad to prepare for a specialized attribute from now on.'

,Thank you.'

I saluted the instructor.

I was a bit dazed. Instructor Vinus was a notoriously stingy instructor with praise. Right now, the only children who had received praise from him were Number 31 and me.

,Next, Number 212!'

Number 212 succeeded in manifesting magic, but only heard from Instructor Vinus to improve.

•••

After lunch, we receive military training. Drill, martial arts, marksmanship, tactics, and so on.

The Battle Mage Project, as the name suggests, is a project to create combat mages to be used in the military. It was only natural to learn military drill and combat skills.

Today was marksmanship.

Number 211 was confident in drill and tactics, but not in martial arts and marksmanship. I wasn't terrible, but I wasn't even average.

The marksmanship instructor was Lexi. A pink-haired woman.

The instructors, male and female, wore officer uniforms. The black-mixed design was a cool uniform.

Instructor Lexi greeted us with a hearty laugh. Whether she did it on purpose or not, she loosened the top of her uniform, revealing her ample breasts. I struggled to keep my eyes from wandering to her white cleavage.

,Alright, you brats. It's fun shooting time. You know the importance of a gun without me having to tell you, right? When your mana runs out, the only thing you can trust is a gun. You don't have a gun? Then you have no choice but to pray to the heavens.'

Instructor Lexi said with a smirk. She's saying it as a joke, but none of us understood the joke.

Instructor Lexi shook her head and said.

,Numbers 31 to 40. Stand on the firing line and get into position. The rest of you, repeatedly practice disassembling and assembling your guns!'

I spread a cloth on the floor and placed the rifle on it. It's an assault rifle of the FS66 model. I heard it's a weapon used by the elite of the Freiselle Kingdom army, but I don't know if it's good or bad.

I disassembled the rifle, recalling Number 211's memories. I knew the method, but I kept getting stuck because I didn't have the knack for it.

,You shouldn't just give it strength, you should pull it as if you're pulling it.'

After repeating the disassembly and assembly about ten times, I got the hang of it. The disassembly, which took about 50 seconds at first, has now been reduced to about 30 seconds. Assembly took about 10 seconds longer than disassembly.

,Next. Numbers 211 to 220. Stand on the firing line.'

I entered the first firing line. I took a stance and checked the target. The target is 50M away. When a bullet hits, the target is pushed back. The multi-talented Number 31 held a record, having hit a target at a maximum distance of 720M. Without a scope, of course.

Number 211's best record was 310M.

,Commence firing!'

Tang, tang, tang.

Gunshots rang out from the other firing lines.

,...Something doesn't feel right. Can I change my stance a bit?'

I looked at Instructor Lexi. She's serious about shooting. She could get angry if I changed my stance without permission.

,Let's change it. If she says something, I'll pretend it was a mistake. Surely she won't inject mana essence just because I made one mistake, right?'

I kept my shoulders and waist more comfortable. My body leans slightly to the left. It felt like the balance of this firing line was slightly tilted.

,Comfortable.'

I pulled the trigger.

Taang!

The bullet pierced the center of the target accurately.

The target moves 50M back.

I slightly adjusted the muzzle and pulled the trigger. This time too, I hit the center. I was a little surprised.

,Number 211 couldn't shoot this well... Is it Seong Yujin's skill, not Number 211's? ...Did I have this much talent for shooting?'

Hesitantly, I pulled the trigger again. I hit the 200M target as well. This time too, it was a bullseye.

,Hmm. I'm getting a feel for it? The wind is getting a little stronger.'

I calculate the wind. It felt more difficult than constructing a magic spell. I wasn't sure if my calculations were accurate.

,Let's just wing the rest.'

Taang.

The bullet pierced the center of the target.

Around the time I pierced the 500M target, the gazes of the children, including Lexi's, turned to me. I paused for a moment, but focused on the target again.

Hit.

Hit.

Another hit.

Before I knew it, I was aiming at the target at 720M, which was Number 31's best record.

,The target is barely visible.'

I was losing confidence here too. It wasn't easy to calculate because I couldn't see the target well. So, I left it to my senses and pulled the trigger.

Taang.

The target moves further back. It meant I hit it. My heart started pounding. I desperately calmed my excited feelings. I'm shooting now. Excitement wasn't good.

My best record ended at 850M.

I couldn't hit it because I couldn't see the target. I would have hit it if I could have seen the target. That's the feeling I get.

I put down the gun with a heart about to burst. When I turned around, Lexi was looking at me with a strange expression.
