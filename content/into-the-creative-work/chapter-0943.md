,Jiyeong-a!

I grabbed Kang Ji-young's arm as she walked toward the road and pulled her into my arms. The sports car sped past in an instant. This was also according to my plan. I could fully feel her voluptuous body in my arms.

"...What the hell are you doing?"

Kang Ji-young asked sternly.

I quickly pulled away from her.

"It's dangerous. What if you got hit by a car?"

Kang Ji-young watched the sports car disappear from sight and sighed.

"The dangerous thing isn't me, it's the sports car."

"...Oh. I guess so."

A beat late, I remembered that Kang Ji-young is an S-class hero. Plus, Kang Ji-young's durability stat is S+. At that level, she'd be fine even if a dump truck crashed into her, not just a sports car.

As I scratched my head, Kang Ji-young relaxed her expression and chuckled.

"I don't particularly intend to scold you. I was just a little surprised by the sudden situation. What you did was right."

"Thanks for saying that."

"Where are we going after the cafe?"

"Do you know about escape room cafes?"

"I've heard of them. A game where the goal is to solve the puzzles in the room and escape."

"You know it well. But you don't seem too excited?"

"Before I became the Chairman-nim, my main job was escaping dungeons. I don't think escaping a room is as thrilling as that."

"Escaping a room is different from escaping a dungeon. This is just for fun."

"......Okay."

Kang Ji-young nodded. She still didn't look like she was expecting anything from the escape room cafe.

I chose the escape room cafe for three reasons.

One, I knew in advance that Kang Ji-young doesn't really enjoy dramas or movies. Second, Kang Ji-young's physical abilities are so high that active games would be boring for her.

And third. Escape room cafes are easy to manipulate to my liking. I had already met with the owner of the existing escape room cafe a week ago and made a plan. It cost me some money, but it was pocket change for me, so I didn't care.

"...Love Connect?"

Kang Ji-young narrowed her brows as she read the sign in front of the escape room cafe.

I said nonchalantly.

"Huh? It was originally Apocalypse Black Corporation Escape or something, but it seems like it changed recently. Well, escape room cafes are all the same, so it probably won't be much different. Let's go in."

"...It says here that it's for couples only, though."

"We're on a date right now. We're a couple."

"...Hmm."

She frowned as if something was amiss, but soon she entered the escape room cafe with me.

"Welcome."

The cafe owner greeted us. She was a woman in her 30s, and she nodded subtly when she made eye contact with me.

"You're here to use Love Connect, right? Couple discount applies. And could you wait a moment? I need to tidy up the set. It'll only take about 10 minutes."

I calmly waited while drinking the orange juice that the cafe owner gave us. The cafe owner would be slightly changing the escape room set right now.

The cafe owner handed us a walkie-talkie and said.

"You can use the walkie-talkie for giving up or hints. You can use two hints. The escape time limit is one hour. It starts when you go in!"

Kang Ji-young and I officially started the escape room game.

The first room was a bright room that seemed to be themed after a princess room. A fancy bed and wardrobe. And bright pink wallpaper. However, there was an incongruous steel door on one side of the wall. It was a door lock. You have to enter the password to open the door.

"So it's like this...."

"Jiyeong-a. We have to find clues."

I looked around near the bed, and Kang Ji-young rummaged through the desk. I found a book, and Kang Ji-young stared intently at the laptop and camera on the desk.

"Jiyeong-a. Did you find anything?"

"...I'm concerned about the laptop on the desk. And there was an alphabet chart in the desk drawer."

The alphabets written on the alphabet chart were colored in various colors. There were over 200 alphabets and 6 colors.

"What's that book?"

"Ah, this? I found it under the desk."

It was a book with nearly 100 pages. The content was drawings. A person is posing. The person's appearance is distinguished by color.

"......I see."

Kang Ji-young seemed to have realized something.

"What's wrong?"

"The numbers and alphabet chart everywhere. And the book with people's poses drawn. It's obvious when you look at the laptop and camera."

Kang Ji-young pointed to the ceiling. The number 61 was written on the ceiling. Then she checked the alphabet. 61. The alphabets in the 6th and 1st positions were blue.

"The alphabets are A and I. If you substitute them with numbers, it's 1 and 9, so it's either page 19 or page 10 of the book. Check it out."

I checked page 19 first. It was a pose of a person spreading both arms. The person's color is red.

"It must be page 10."

I opened page 10. A drawing of a blue person with one hand raised.

"Adding the alphabet numbers was the answer."

"So you're saying we have to take a picture in this pose?"

"Probably."

I went to the camera and struck a pose exactly as it was in the book. I raised one hand. Click. Kang Ji-young pressed the enter key to take a picture. But nothing happened.

"...Isn't it?"

"......It's better to gather all the other clues first."

We gathered clues. There were many numbers written on the left leg of the bed, the inside of the wardrobe, under the laptop, etc.

"There are two poses of the same color.... I see why it's for couples only. It seems like you have to take the pose together as a couple, not alone. We can use the timer function on the camera for the picture...."

Kang Ji-young and I stood in front of the camera and struck a pose. It was a somewhat embarrassing pose, but Kang Ji-young, who was immersed in escaping the room, was much more concerned about the remaining time.

,There aren't any poses that cross the line, either.

I smirked inwardly and struck a pose. Our bodies touched, and skinship naturally occurred. When we struck the exact pose, a message flew to the laptop. It was a message with only numbers written on it. We then struck three poses in a row and received all the passwords to move on to the next stage of the room.

Kang Ji-young started looking for clues as soon as she entered the room. I was glad that she seemed to be enjoying it.

•••

We finished escaping the room in 50 minutes.

We received drinks as a success reward. And as a bonus, we received pictures of Kang Ji-young and I focusing on escaping the room. Among them was a picture of me hugging Kang Ji-young from behind.

"Jiyeong-a. It was fun, right?"

"It was fun in its own way."

My plan was to have a good time at the karaoke next, then have dinner and take a walk in a nearby park before parting ways. But a sudden emergency text message disrupted my plan.

Uuuuuung.

The content said that a monster had appeared nearby, so evacuate. Unlike reality, in this world, monsters suddenly appear in the city.

People who received the evacuation message quickly ran into nearby buildings or started running away to safer places.

I looked at Kang Ji-young. Kang Ji-young didn't seem to have any intention of running away.

"Are you going to fight the monster?"

"I have no reason to run away from the monster. If there's a monster nearby, it's better for me to step up and quickly get rid of it. It won't take long, so you stay here."

"No. I'm going too. Even if I look like this, I'm a student at Maruhan Academy."

"I'm the Chairman-nim of that academy, but... well, this will be a good experience for you too. Keep up."

She confidently ran off. She seemed to have already figured out where the monster was. I followed her. She was so fast that I had to run at full speed to barely keep up with her.

The guy who appeared in the city was a B-class monster called 'Sapphire Negative'.

This guy, who has a huge body over 3M, is a monster that walks on two legs like a human, and has blue jewels like sapphires embedded all over its body. But in reality, it's not a jewel. It was just a part of the body that looked like a sapphire.

It attacks the mind, so it's difficult for even a B-class hero to deal with, but Kang Ji-young strode up without any defense and swung her fist.

Kwaang!

That was the end of it.

The monster's head disappeared and the situation was over. As expected, her power as an S-class hero was amazing.

"It's not over yet."

"What?"

"There are a few more a little further away. Maybe a dungeon exploded near the city, and as a result, monsters appeared all over the city."

She, who had grasped the situation just by the appearance of the monster, ran again. It didn't even take 10 minutes to find and kill all the monsters in the city.

After that, she dealt with employees dispatched from the Hero Association and discussed the cleanup. The cleanup alone took over an hour.

I spent time in a suitable place until the cleanup was over. There's no point in being caught that I, an academy student, and she, the academy Chairman-nim, were together.

"I'm sorry. The cleanup took longer than I thought. You didn't have to wait and could have gone back...."

"No. It's okay. It's a date we've been looking forward to, so we can't end it like this. It's dinner time, so let's go eat. I've reserved a good place."

Kang Ji-young and I went to a fancy restaurant. It was a place where classical music flowed and was run by a Chef who often showed his face on TV.

While eating flower shrimp as an appetizer, I asked Kang Ji-young seriously.

"Jiyeong-a. To be honest, there's something I've been curious about for a while."

"Something you're curious about? I'll answer as long as it's not something difficult."

"Why did you become the academy Chairman-nim?"

"Is that what you were curious about? I was nervous because you said it with a serious face, but it's not a serious question after all."

"No. I'm asking quite seriously. There might be a special story I don't know about."

Chairman-nim of the Maruhan Hero Academy of Korea.

It's true that it's a huge title to hear.

The academy is where elite hero aspirants go. You can work as a C-class hero at a minimum just by graduating from the academy. It's also possible to get a job at a famous clan.

But even though it's such a great academy, it's not enough reason for Kang Ji-young, an S-class hero, to take on the position of academy Chairman-nim.

She is an S-class hero. She can enjoy wealth and fame even if she doesn't take on the position of academy Chairman-nim. If she wants money, it's better to join a clan, and if she wants fame and honor, she can join the Hero Association.

If she is sincere, she will be able to easily seize the position of Chairman-nim of the Korean Association.

"A special story.... It's not that special of a story."

She chuckled and took a sip of wine.

"I received a request from the 2nd academy Chairman-nim. I had a debt to him, so I decided to accept the request."

"That's all? You didn't have to grant that request."

"Surely that's not all. The academy is in a more precarious state than you think right now. Big and small problems are happening inside and outside. The current Chairman-nim of the Korean Hero Association doesn't like the academy very much. To be exact, the reason is that the budget allocated to the academy is too large."

"Is he crazy? The academy is the future of the Hero Association."

Kang Ji-young just shrugged.

"The problem is inside. I'll tell you since you probably know roughly. The Vice Chairman-nim. He's aiming for the Chairman-nim's position. The reason why the 2nd Chairman-nim asked me to take the Chairman-nim position is also because of the Vice Chairman-nim."

"Why the Vice Chairman-nim? He doesn't seem to have any big problems."

"He looks fine on the outside. But the Vice Chairman-nim is a person who can wield power for his own benefit. The students of the academy are immature. They're young. They waver even if you just blow a little wind. It's a good environment for brainwashing. And you don't know, but there's quite a lot to gain. Especially from clans...."

Kang Ji-young closed her mouth. It was because the waiter brought the main course. I gave a bitter smile. Looking at the situation, it seemed difficult to continue the conversation on this topic.

She and I picked up our forks and knives and cut the steak.

"In short, that's it. The reason I became the academy Chairman-nim is because I received a request from the 2nd and because I'm suited to teaching students."

"For someone who likes teaching, you only run the academy."

"I only have one body. I want to teach the students if I could, but I also have to run the academy. Still, I take the time to teach the 3rd graders from time to time."

"I hope I become a 3rd grader soon. That way I'll receive Ji-young's education."

"Well. I don't know if my teaching will be helpful to you."
