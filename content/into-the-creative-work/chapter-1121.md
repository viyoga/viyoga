,My mana returned to normal after a few seconds. You don't have much Hunter experience, but you use unique skills?"

I pulled the Hwaryeon Bido from its sheath at my waist and charged toward the woman. As promised to Ha Seunghee-ssi, I don't intend to kill her. But to secure a definite victory, I'll have to cut off her limbs.

'She doesn't feel as overwhelming as Jin Se-yeong-ssi. Against an opponent like this... I can win.'

First, I'm aiming for the woman's right arm.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

In the dark space, a red light flashed, aiming for the woman's arm. The woman quickly bent her upper body. Her body seeped into the floor and disappeared.

,These youngsters are scary these days. They have no respect for their seniors. Back in my day, we would bow politely to our seniors..."

Voices are coming from all directions. I spread my senses again, but I couldn't find the woman's exact location.

'Should I use Mountain Lightning again? Even if I've mastered Yeongcheon Ryu, using Extreme Techniques consecutively puts a strain on my body, so I should use something else.'

[Opening the Eye of Heaven (Heaven's Eye).]

My senses become more acute, and the mana in the air is visible to the naked eye. I rolled my eyes around. I saw the woman seeping into the wall, moving without a trace. She's moving like a flowing liquid.

'She'll react if I approach her directly. That woman doesn't know I've located her.'

I swung my sword in the air as if I was annoyed. After repeating it a few times, a sneer appeared on the woman's face.

,I'm not there, kid."

,If you're going to act like a senior, show yourself proudly first."

,I don't want to. To be honest, I'm not confident I can win in a head-on fight with you. I'll cook you in my own way."

,You ugly bitch."

,...Why is that? I've received worse insults than that, but strangely, that simple insult makes me irritated."

,It's because you're ugly. You ugly bitch."

Shwiik.

A dagger flies from the side. I swung my sword and knocked the dagger away. The dagger spun around and soared upwards. The dagger seeped into the ceiling and disappeared.

,...Oh, come on. I fell for a cheap provocation and made a pointless attack."

,That dagger. It's not real. Did you temporarily create it using your ability? Like the arm that was choking me."

,You noticed? You're faster than I thought... You have a good sense."

The voice came from the right. The real her was behind me. It was a pathetic trick.

'I have to play along for the right timing.'

I swung my sword. Sword energy containing lightning flew to the right, leaving scars on the wall.

,Good reaction. But that's not it."

The voice came from the ceiling. I stabbed the ceiling with my sword. Nothing happened.

,You're being annoying. You can't hide forever. Aren't you going to show yourself soon?"

,You're bluffing. Are you getting anxious because you can't see or feel me?"

,......"

,Okay, here's an offer. You have Ha Seunghee-ssi's research data, right? Hand it over. Of course, I'm not going to take it for free. I'll give you a price. 3 billion won. Isn't that enough for your trouble?"

,Who are you trying to fool with that petty cash?"

,Is that so? I was going to take the easy way... What a shame."

The woman was already close to me. She reaches out her hand to me. Her hand splits into dozens, trying to grab my body.

'Perfect timing.'

I gripped the hilt of my sword tightly and swung it sideways.

Pajijijik.

A stream of electricity flowed along the blade.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Extreme Technique (極技) Sealing Lightning (封雷).

The woman's arms that touched the blade disappear like burst water balloons. This is because of the effect of sealing mana that is applied the moment it touches the electricity. If Mountain Lightning is a wide-area skill, Sealing Lightning is an anti-personnel skill. The ability to seal mana is superior in Sealing Lightning than in Mountain Lightning.

The surprised woman tries to step back, but the Sealing Lightning extending from the blade pierces her shoulder.

,Kyaaaaaa!"

Her presence is completely revealed, and she becomes defenseless.

Seogeok.

The blade of the Hwaryeon Bido cuts off the woman's right arm. Instead of screaming and complaining of pain, the woman stepped back.

'A fleeting moment.'

Using instantaneous acceleration, I grabbed the fleeing woman's hair in one go. The woman's face contorted.

,Contact Ha Jun-soo. Tell him the job is over because of your defeat. Or should I contact him directly?"

,Over? What are you talking about? Do you think I'm the only one under the president?"

The woman said confidently, sweating profusely.

Kwaaaang!

The right wall was destroyed, and two men in suits appeared. One is a bear-like giant, and the other is as skinny as a skeleton, but exudes a sharper aura than anything else.

The two men muttered, looking at me. The bear-like man was wearing black gloves, and the skinny man pulled a sword over 2m long from its sheath.

,Wasn't he a B-rank brat?"

,Seeing him in person, he seems to be at least A-rank. The rumor that he's the most promising prospect in Korea wasn't a lie."

,When I was that age, I barely made it to C-rank... I envy his talent."

,Enough with the chatter... Let's get to work."

I threw the woman in my hand behind me. At the same time, the two men rushed at me.

'Acceleration, a fleeting moment.'

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 5]

[Using a Fleeting Moment (Instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

'The opponents are two A-rank Hunters. I have to defeat them with all my strength from the start.'

I approached the skinny man with the sword.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

I unleash the fastest sword strike. The blade, turned into a ray of light, aimed for the skinny man's leg. The skinny man's eyes flashed, and his body moved to the side at a rapid speed, dodging my sword strike.

I immediately realized what the skinny man had done.

'Instant acceleration! He was an acceleration ability user.'

[Using a Fleeting Moment (Instant). Remaining stacks: 3]

I regained my stance and used a Fleeting Moment. There are two opponents. I'll be at a disadvantage if I give up the initiative. In the worst case, I might not even be able to counterattack properly and get hit. It's better to attack even if it's reckless.

Suddenly, the bear-like man appeared in front of me with his fist raised.

'Teleportation?!'

He has an ability that's more troublesome than the skinny man.

[Using a Fleeting Moment (Instant). Remaining stacks: 2]

I used a Fleeting Moment to avoid his fist. In my head, I was already thinking about a counterattack. But the bear-like man's fist suddenly appeared in front of me. It was impossible to avoid.

,...He already knows how to deal with instant acceleration. Damn it.'

I was punched in the face and flew backwards, crashing into the wall. I spat out a broken tooth and immediately got up. I barely dodged the blade flying towards my arm and unleashed lightning.

The skinny man stepped back, avoiding the lightning. The ugly woman who lost her right arm to me had already disappeared.

,He's got a good sense."

,I can feel the experience in his movements. He seems to have a lot of practical experience."

The two of them were talking while slowly moving to secure the best position.

I changed my plan. Originally, I was going to fight them both at the same time, but...

'That bear-like man's teleportation ability is too annoying.'

The moment I faced the teleportation ability user, my chances of winning plummeted. If I actively used means such as Complete Recovery, Stormbreaker, and Heavenly Heart, I might be able to win somehow, but I have no intention of showing my cards here.

'Let's run.'

I shouldn't misunderstand the victory conditions.

The goal isn't to defeat all the enemies. I'm a security guard at the lab. The top priority is to protect the USB I have in my possession, Ha Seunghee-ssi's research data.

Pajijijijijik.

I indiscriminately scatter lightning. Blue lightning ran along the ground and walls. The two men were horrified and distanced themselves from me.

,Considering she got hit, it wouldn't be ordinary electricity."

,There's no point in being stupid enough to get hit on purpose."

I use the technique that suits me best among Yeongcheon Ryu's Extreme Techniques.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Extreme Technique (極技) Exploding Quake Lightning (爆震雷).

The lightning scattered in all directions explodes simultaneously.

I obscured the enemies' vision with the exploded debris of the lab and ran outside. My clothes were torn, my skin was burned, and broken concrete debris was stuck to my head. I couldn't be safe from the effects of the explosion either.

'A lab just flew away. Well, Ha Seunghee-ssi will take care of this.'

I came out behind the lab building and gripped the Hwaryeon Bido tightly in my hand instead of running away immediately.

'If he's a teleportation ability user...'

Pat.

As expected, the bear-like man appeared in front of me. And I scattered electricity around as if I had been waiting.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Extreme Technique (極技) Scattered Lightning (散雷).

I raised the hilt of my sword. The bear-like man tried to use his ability to avoid my attack, but soon a look of bewilderment appeared on his face.

,Mana...!"

His mana wasn't moving as he wanted. Before he could gather his wits, I struck him in the face with the end of the hilt. His nose was crushed, and teeth and blood splattered on the floor. His knees buckled, making it the perfect position to deliver an uppercut.

I actually delivered an uppercut.

His body floated up and fell to the floor.

,I'm the type to return what I receive."

I blew my nose hard to remove the foreign object blocking one of my nostrils. Blood instead of snot dripped down. I also spat out the blood pooled in my mouth. Seeing the mixed broken teeth made me angry for no reason. I kicked his abdomen.

,Keoeok!"

He screamed. At that moment, his body disappeared and appeared above my head. His large fist aims for my face.

'I knew it.'

The Eye of Heaven (天眼) revealed the spot where he would appear through teleportation. The strange twisting of the mana in the air was so conspicuous that I couldn't miss it.

'Lightning Fist.'

Pajijijik.

I threw a counter punch before his fist could reach me. Electrocuted, he immediately lost consciousness and fainted.

'Thanks to the Eye of Heaven, I dealt with him much easier than I thought. What's left is...'

The skinny man with the sword runs towards me from the lab destroyed by the explosion.

'It's worth a try in a 1-on-1 fight. I can use a Fleeting Moment two more times... And it's not often that I get the chance to fight an A-rank Hunter without worrying about my life.'

But I deliberately suppressed the rising fighting spirit.

I couldn't be sure that this was all the power Ha Jun-soo had prepared. In my estimation, there would be more. With Ha Jun-soo's ability, he could hire dozens of A-class Hunters and still have plenty left over.

'It doesn't matter if I lose... But if the data is taken, Ha Seunghee-ssi will be in trouble.'

I can't ignore Ha Seunghee-ssi's request because of my personal desires. I turned my back on the man rushing towards me with a fierce aura and started running towards the nearby village.

Yeongcheon Ryu (影天流) Lightning Sound Step (雷音歩).

The fastest footwork among Yeongcheon Ryu's footwork techniques. The flaw was that a small thunder sound rang loudly every time my foot touched the ground.

,He's running away!"

,Catch him!!"

,Don't let him get to the village!!"

Men in black suits popped out from everywhere. They are ability users of at least B-rank or higher.

'They were hiding well. Did they use magic or something?'

There are more than 20 of them. I gave the middle finger to those chasing me and ran with all my might.

When I arrived near the village, the presence of the pursuers chasing me disappeared.

This is just an internal competition within the Sejin Group. It was natural that they didn't want to cause a stir because they didn't want to give internal information to outsiders. The fact that they told me not to kill the opponent was also because it was a somewhat extreme sibling rivalry.

'Anyway, I protected the data.'

Ha Seunghee-ssi won the competition between siblings.
