I entered the reception room.

Two men were waiting for us.

One of them was a man I already knew. Third Prince Gidin Eustia. He was leaning back in his chair, waving his hand at me.

The other man was sitting with his legs crossed. Blonde hair that reached his waist. Sharp eyes and a composed demeanor. It was the Second Prince, Herix Eustia.

,Nice to meet you, Your Highness Herix. It's an honor to see you again, Your Highness Gidin."

,So, you're Lord Seong Yujin, who won the hunting competition? You're more ordinary than I thought."

Herix said bluntly. His calm, red eyes scanned my body up and down. I felt unpleasant. My temper wanted to punch him in the face right then and there.

,Brother. Judging people by their appearance is not a good habit. He conquered and dominated the 2,350th District, which the Imperial family failed to conquer."

Gidin said. At first glance, it seemed like he was defending me, but Gidin's eyes were looking at me as if observing a strange creature.

,Your Highnesses. Are you ignoring me? I'm a little disappointed."

Elena smiled and stepped forward. Herix and Gidin sighed almost simultaneously. Then, they stood up and greeted Elena. They exchanged formal greetings.

,Duke Valderdt... You haven't changed since I saw you two years ago."

,It seems that Your Highness Gidin has become a bit more mature. It's a blessing for the Empire."

Elena replied leisurely. She then naturally sat down on a chair.

Herix looked a little uncomfortable.

,I didn't expect you to come with Lord Seong Yujin. It seems you cherish Lord Seong Yujin."

,Yes. He's a cherished knight. As you can see, he's quite capable."

,He not only won the hunting competition but also conquered the 2,350th District, so he seems to be capable indeed."

I stood next to Elena. It was a burdensome place for me to sit down.

,Lord Yujin."

Gidin called me. He handed me a luxurious box.

,Thank you, Your Highness Gidin."

,No need to thank me. I'm just handing over the prize for winning the hunting competition. Open the box and check it yourself."

Everyone's eyes turned to me. It was not appropriate to just take the box. I opened the box in front of them.

There was a red cloak. It was a cloak that felt luxurious even at a glance. I took the cloak out of the box. The fabric was very soft.

,Hecate Cape. It's an SS-rank cloak."

Gidin said. He looked at the cloak in my hand with regretful eyes. If it weren't for the anomaly called me, he would have been the owner of this cloak.

,Hecate Cape

All stats increase by 1.

Acquire magic resistance of A-rank or lower.

Adapt to polar environments.

B-rank natural healing effect.

S-rank stealth available.

Rank: SS"

I knew it, but it has great abilities as a winning prize.

,Lord Yujin. Do you know the name Hecate?"

,I know her as the goddess of magic."

Hecate is a goddess in Greek mythology. She is not one of the 12 gods of Olympus, but I know that she is a goddess that even the master of the sky cannot treat carelessly.

,You know her well. All magicians worship her. Did you know? The Imperial family offered wealth equivalent to the 10-year budget of a large city to the goddess Hecate to obtain that cloak."

,It's an incredibly expensive item. I don't know if I dare to receive something like this..."

Since it was already in my hands, I didn't want to give it to anyone else.

,You are the winner, so you are qualified to have it. Use it carefully."

Gidin said to me, soothing his disappointment.

,Speaking of Hecate."

Herix opened his mouth. He looked at the red cloak in my hand once and then turned his gaze to Elena.

,Wasn't the divine seat that the Duke contracted with Hecate?"

,Yes. That's right. Only a very small number of people, including Your Highnesses, know that fact in this world. Please keep it a secret."

The divine seat that Elena contracted with is the Remaining Moon in the Darkness. Her true name is Hecate.

,Sorry. I have a habit of blurting out whatever comes to mind sometimes... I'm trying to fix it, but it's not working."

Herix said nonchalantly. At the same time, he was watching my reaction.

,You don't seem surprised. Did you know who Duke Valderdt's contracted divine seat was?"

,Yes. I knew."

I answered calmly. In fact, I hadn't known for long either. All I knew was that Elena's skills from Hecate were related to illusion.

,That's unexpected. Duke Valderdt seems to cherish him very much."

,Didn't I say from the beginning that he's a cherished knight?"

,You did."

A cold energy flowed between Herix and Elena. They seemed to be wary of each other. Gidin glanced back and forth but said nothing.

After a while, a maid brought tea and refreshments. However, no one reached out for the tea and refreshments.

,Duke Valderdt. No one will think that you came to us for no reason, just for one knight. Isn't it time to talk about the purpose of your visit?"

Herix said with his arms crossed. Elena looked at him and then turned her gaze to Gidin.

,Your Highness Gidin. I heard that your life was targeted at the hunting competition this time."

,...That's right. I would have been in danger if it weren't for the help of the exile Kang Myungjin. Ah, now that I think about it, Lord Seong Yujin is in the same Legion as Kang Myungjin, right? This is a truly mysterious coincidence."

,Do you really think so?"

,What do you mean by that, Duke Valderdt? What do you think I did knowing that Lord Seong Yujin and Kang Myungjin are in the same Legion..."

,No. The coincidence I'm talking about is not that. The terrorism at the hunting competition, the assassination attempt on Your Highness Gidin. Do you really think it was dangerous?"

,...I almost lost my life to the attackers. If it weren't for Kang Myungjin, I might not be here."

,Your Highness Gidin. I discovered a suspicious person yesterday, and I interrogated them to obtain information."

,......"

Gidin's face distorted. In addition, Herix's atmosphere turned cold.

,Your Highness. I am Elena Valderdt. I am the Illusionist Duke that people talk about. If I personally interrogate a target, there is nothing I cannot find out. Because the target's mind is in my palm. I can forcibly stir up and pull out even what the target doesn't know or has forgotten. I have that ability."

,...Are you saying. You're going to interrogate me?"

,If necessary, I am considering requesting an interrogation of Your Highness from His Majesty the Emperor."

,......"

Silence.

After a heavy silence, Gidin sighed and shook his head.

,As expected, you are scary. You wouldn't even bat an eye no matter what I said. So, just in case, I'll ask. Illusionist Duke, did you cast an illusion on me?"

,I didn't. But... how do you distinguish that Your Highness is under an illusion? Do you have a way to distinguish whether this is a dream, a simple delusion, or a reality that is nothing? "

Gidin and Herix acted. Gidin fiddled with his necklace, and Herix stroked his bracelet. I noticed that those items were meant to counter Elena's illusions.

,...It's definitely reality now. To have to doubt whether reality is reality... It's really scary. I'm willing to be honest with Duke Valderdt. Duke Valderdt came to see me and my brother after all the calculations. But..."

Gidin glanced at me.

,Your knight is not qualified to talk with us. No matter how much you cherish him."

Herix said it outright.

,Yujin. Go outside for a while. I'd appreciate it if you could wait at the entrance of the Imperial Palace. I don't know how long it will take..."

,I'll wait at the entrance of the Imperial Palace."

I bowed to them and went outside. As I came out into the hallway, the heavy air felt much lighter.

,Let's go quietly. If I wander around the Imperial Palace and get caught up in unnecessary commotion, I'll be the only one who gets tired."

I can roughly guess what they're going to talk about anyway. Elena is planning to use this opportunity to bring in the Second and Third Princes to remove the Crown Prince from his position.

,...Is it possible?"

I recalled the original story.

In the original story, the Crown Prince protects his position until the end and ascends to the throne as Emperor. However, he does not enjoy power as Emperor for long. After all, the protagonist of the original story is Kang Myungjin, and the Emperor is one of the villains.

I stood with my back against the wall at the entrance of the Imperial Palace. I quietly waited for Elena.

At that time. I felt some kind of pressure.

,You've arrived! Duke Ronmasid-kakha!"

The Imperial knights bowed deeply towards someone approaching. The Imperial knights were as tense as privates seeing a general. Their attitude was more extreme than when they faced Elena.

I looked at Duke Ronmasid, whom they were talking about.

A huge physique reaching 2.2m. Short crimson hair and scars on his face. His body is full of bulging muscles.

Like Elena, he is one of the Empire Ogong. Iron-blooded Duke. Holaind Ronmasid.

Also known as the guardian of the Eustia Imperial family, he has the greatest loyalty among the Empire Ogong and always resides in the Imperial Palace.

His brown eyes turned to me.

,I don't recognize your face. You have quite a bit of power... Who are you?"

,Nice to meet you, Duke Ronmasid-kakha. I am Seong Yujin, a knight of Duke Valderdt."

,Are you the one who won the hunting competition? Congratulations on your victory. It seems you have already received the winning prize. Why are you waiting at the entrance of the Imperial Palace?"

,I am waiting for my lord."

,Valderdt came? Did she go to His Majesty the Emperor?"

,She is talking with the Princes-jeoha."

,Hmm. Is that so."

Ronmasid didn't seem interested in Elena or the Princes. He maintains political neutrality. What he protects is only the Imperial family, and what he follows is only the Emperor. Even the Crown Prince cannot give him orders.

,This is good."

It was lucky to encounter him here. It would be difficult to find him separately later.

,Duke Ronmasid-kakha."

He, who was entering the Imperial Palace, stopped at my call. He turned around and looked at me.

,Do you have something to say to me?"

,Will you listen?"

,I can listen. However, don't think that I will move according to your words."

,It's a simple question. Do you happen to know about a family called Felsman?"

His brown eyes shook.

,...I don't know."

A firm voice rang out. I decided to abandon my plan to provoke him. He was reacting more sensitively than I expected. This could put me in danger instead.

,I understand. It seems I have only wasted the Duke-kakha's time. I'm sorry."

,Where did you hear about the Felsman family?"

,I just happened to find out."

,......"

Ronmasid turned around and walked back into the Imperial Palace.

And 30 minutes later, Elena walked outside. She looked quite happy. It seemed that things had gone her way.

,You look happy. Did you get what you wanted?"

,Not perfect, but I've gathered the cards well enough."

The Ten-Year Festival is coming to an end.

The Ten-Year Festival ends with tonight's ball and tomorrow's closing.

Clatter, clatter.

The Valderdt family's splendid four-horse carriage ran. The destination is the Eustia Imperial Palace, where many nobles gather.

Inside the carriage were me, Elena, and Kang Myungjin.

Elena is one of the Empire Ogong, so she naturally attends, and I participate as her partner. In Kang Myungjin's case, he participates as the master of the Legion sponsored by Elena.

Kang Myungjin, dressed up, looked quite handsome. Of course, I pride myself on being no less than him.

,......"

We didn't chat.

It was necessary to organize our thoughts with our respective plans.

Kang Myungjin will seek the Legion's leap forward with this opportunity.

Elena is determined to bring down the Crown Prince.

And I was thinking about how to seduce Elena. Elena is so perfect that there was no room to squeeze into her heart. Basic trust has been built, but anything more than that was difficult.

,We've arrived."

Elena said. We got out of the carriage.

The glittering Imperial Palace welcomed us.
