,Seong Yujin-haksaeng."

Kwon Sang-hoon lowered his voice and called me. The place was the auditorium, so his voice echoed.

The gazes of the people still remaining in the auditorium turned to Kwon Sang-hoon. Kwon Sang-hoon's voice just now was threatening for calling a student. In other words, it meant that Kwon Sang-hoon was angry enough to not consider the time and place.

Seong Hari-ssi, who was nearby, frowned. I raised my hand to stop her from getting up from her seat. Fortunately, Seong Hari-ssi followed my lead.

,Yes. Dean Kwon Sang-hoon-hakjangnim. Please speak."

,As far as I know, the student council president election is done by electronic voting using smartphones, not by raising hands. I didn't hear that the voting method would be changed either. Did you change the voting method as you pleased?"

,I made a suggestion, and the students agreed. Raising hands is overwhelmingly easier than electronic voting."

,...Without consulting the faculty? Seong Yujin-haksaeng. You are just a student. The academy's operation is the job of the faculty. You are now encroaching on the faculty's work. I hope this doesn't happen again."

,So, Dean-hakjangnim proceeded with the reforms without consulting the students?"

,Heo. It seems Seong Yujin-haksaeng doesn't like the academy's reforms. Reforms were necessary. The world is changing at a rapid pace. The academy also needs to change."

,It sounds like you're saying that Dean-hakjangnim is the owner of the academy. The academy is not a cram school."

,The academy was established with the support of the state and the association... I can't say that I am the owner of the academy. But I am the operator of the academy. The authority to operate the academy is mine."

,That's wrong. The owner of the academy is neither the state nor the association."

,...Then, who is the owner of the academy?"

,The students. The students are the owners of the academy."

,Heo...."

Kwon Sang-hoon sighed as if he was dumbfounded. He subtly turned his head to check the surrounding reactions. Outsiders were watching with great interest, and the faculty looked embarrassed. They probably didn't expect me to be so bold.

,Rather than being bold, it's presumptuous. From Kwon Sang-hoon's perspective, I'm just a student."

A student who is good at skills and has good backing, so he can't be treated carelessly though.

For reference, Seong Hari-ssi is looking at me with sparkling eyes.

『Seong Hari-ssi's psychology: Our Yujin-gun, who isn't pushed back by the Dean-hakjangnim's momentum, is so cool....』

,I've heard your opinion well. The saying that the owner of the academy is the student. It's not wrong. But doesn't a student have a student's job, and a Dean-hakjangnim has a Dean-hakjangnim's job? We just need to focus on each other's work. Then nothing will happen."

,You're not making any sense. Dean-hakjangnim. Let me be more clear. Our students do not want reform. Please return the academy's curriculum to the previous curriculum."

Kwon Sang-hoon frowned and didn't hide his displeasure.

,......What qualifications do you have to make such a request? If you're a student, concentrate on your studies."

,As the student council president, I am making this request on behalf of the academy's students."

Kwon Sang-hoon inhaled and then closed his mouth. He held back from shouting. He then sighed and said.

,I've heard the student's opinion well. I will consider it."

Consider it? That's just lip service. Kwon Sang-hoon won't even consider it. Because if he stops the reform here, it's as good as doing nothing.

Kwon Sang-hoon got up from his seat and left the auditorium with his secretary. The faculty members looked around and followed the Dean-hakjangnim. Most of the faculty had no choice but to follow the Dean-hakjangnim. The Dean-hakjangnim had the authority to cut off their livelihood.

The outsiders also left, and I and Seong Hari-ssi were left in the auditorium.

,Yujin-ah. You don't like Dean Kwon Sang-hoon-hakjangnim, do you? Should Mom use her power a little?"

,It's okay. This is a matter between the Dean-hakjangnim and the students. We have to solve it ourselves. More than that, what about Go Young-jin?"

,Ah. That arrogant junior who dropped out?"

,His dad is the clan leader of the Buster Clan. Isn't he doing something?"

,I definitely contacted the Dean-hakjangnim and told him to issue a direct disciplinary action. Hmm. I don't know the Buster Clan leader well either... Just trust this mom. I won't let him do anything weird to our Yujin-gun."

,Thanks, Mom."

We looked at each other with deep eyes. The distance is getting closer and closer. Even if we are mother and son, there is a line that cannot be crossed, but... there was no such thing between me and Seong Hari-ssi. We had already crossed the line a long time ago.

[Please.... Stop it. Just because there's no one watching doesn't mean you can do whatever you want! This is the academy...!]

Kang Ji-young, who is only visible to my eyes, shouted.

She was now used to it and was quiet even when I formed this kind of atmosphere with other women, but she couldn't stand Seong Hari-ssi and Choi Jeong-hwa-ssi. Because they are her friends.

,Yujin-ah...."

,Mom...."

Our lips touched and rubbed together. Seong Hari-ssi's tongue peeked out from between her lips. Catching him, I actively mixed my tongue with Seong Hari-ssi's.

[Keuek... Please...!]

Kang Ji-young, who shares my senses, trembled. But I didn't stop. In the empty auditorium, we got hotter.

Three days had passed.

The rumor that I and Kwon Sang-hoon had clashed spread quickly inside the academy. The faculty members kept their mouths shut, but not the outsiders. Now, even outside the academy, there were rumors that I and Kwon Sang-hoon were clashing.

,Kwon Sang-hoon. Are you ignoring my words after all."

[Kwon Sang-hoon can't listen to your words and stop the reform. From the moment he stops the reform, the Dean-hakjangnim's authority will plummet. A Dean-hakjangnim without authority is a living scarecrow. No, he can only be seen as a machine that approves documents.]

It was as Kang Ji-young said. Kwon Sang-hoon had come too far to give up on the reform now. Kwon Sang-hoon is riding on the back of a running tiger.

,Still, he could have at least negotiated with me. Not even having the minimum negotiation or conversation means... he's ignoring me."

Kwon Sang-hoon decided to establish his own authority instead of negotiating.

Then what about my authority?

,I'll have to establish it myself."

As the student council president, I gathered the first and second year students. Some of the influential ones instead of all the students.

,As the student council president, representing the academy's students, I declare martial law in the academy."

,Martial law! President-hoejangnim, what should we do?!"

,Occupy the academy's main building. Kick out the faculty and outsiders and gather supplies to hold out. I'll provide the supplies, so don't worry. I'll turn the classrooms into PC rooms and movie theaters this time."

,That... seems like things will get too big."

The students' eyes wavered uneasily.

I held up and shook Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood in front of them.

,I will give appropriate rewards to those who build merit."

The students' attitude changed completely. Their morale soared in an instant, and their eyes burned.

Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood. Elixirs that cannot be obtained with money. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that you can change your life if you have these!

,I heard that Kwon Sang-hoon recently erected his own statue at the entrance of the academy? Is there anyone brave enough to break the statue?"

,Me! I'll break it! Just tell me to!"

Most of the students raised their hands.

,We need a slogan to express our opinions. Even if we disappear, we will not reform. That should be enough. Is there anyone who can decorate the academy so that people can see our will well?"

,Please leave it to our art club! We will decorate the academy with the skills we have honed in the club!"

,There was even an art club? Well, I'm trusting you."

,President-hoejangnim! Should we summon the third graders too?"

,Leave the third graders alone. They'll be busy preparing for graduation. You just need to ask for understanding from a few third graders. All first and second graders must attend."

,It's a kind of protest. How long will the protest last?"

,Of course, until Kwon Sang-hoon cancels the reform."

,Isn't it possible that Kwon Sang-hoon will make us repeat a grade?"

,It's not just one person repeating a grade, but everyone repeating a grade. If one person is expelled for a reason that doesn't even make sense... the entire grade will drop out. You have to use a super strong move for a strong move. Scholarship? Don't worry. I'll support you. I have a lot of money."

The students nodded at my words. I had a reliable background called Seong Hari-ssi behind me. For reference, the association is also on my side.

,No matter how I think about it, I can't picture myself losing."

Of course, there were variables.

The academy reform is not only beneficial to Kwon Sang-hoon. Those who joined hands with Kwon Sang-hoon. They won't stay still.

,The preparation is over."

I poured out items from my inventory. I had done something similar in the [God's Atlantis] world, so all the items, including the lacquer, were in my inventory. The difference from then is that I am the student representative.

,Let's start."

With me at the lead, the students invaded the academy. Guards and heroes guarding the academy? There was nothing they could do against the students.

Occupying the main building was also easy. The faculty members who tried to resist quietly left the academy's main building as soon as they saw me.

,We will not stop the occupation until Dean Kwon Sang-hoon withdraws the reform and restores the academy. Please tell Dean Kwon Sang-hoon clearly."

And after coming inside, I poured out this and that. I remodeled the classrooms, etc. I roughly created facilities for leisure such as PC rooms, movie theaters, restaurants, bedrooms, and board game rooms. Remodeling was easy because I had the machine spirit Machina. I also piled up snacks like mountains.

,The main building will be occupied by students taking turns. Since there are about 700 of us, we can take turns with 200 people a day. Ah, tonight's dinner is a catering buffet."

,Uh... we're happy, but. Shouldn't we be doing something if it's a protest?"

,Protest? That's hard, so let's do a uniform protest. Just put the uniforms in front of the building."

,How should I say this. It feels like we're on vacation."

If you have money and ability, you can do it comfortably. That's no exception even if it's a protest. And I had a lot of money and ability.
