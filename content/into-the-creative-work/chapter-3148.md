"Nanomachines?! The nanomachines I know don't look like that dangerous shit!"

Wolfgang grumbled as he pushed himself up. I, being the kind wizard that I am, decided to enlighten him.

"Stop advertising your lack of education, mercenary. The technology of True China that existed in ancient times surpasses the modern era. You didn't even know that?"

"Fuck off! How the hell would I know that?! You can live just fine without knowing that shit!"

"Well, it's causing you trouble right now, isn't it."

"Whatever. If it's nanomachines, they'd be weak to lightning, right? Count. Blast 'em with that spicy shit from before and wipe out the nanomachines or whatever the hell they are!"

"You really are an idiot. Does that look like ordinary nanomachines to you?"

"...High-performance ancient nanomachines?"

"It's necromancy. Soul fragments, finely divided, were imbued into the nanomachines. Those nanomachines are machines and undead at the same time. And since they're part of a lich, he can use his unique skill to regenerate them. Destroying the nanomachines won't be the end of it."

"Even so, there's gotta be a limit to regeneration, right? Let's just keep smashing until he can't recover anymore."

"He's a dungeon boss. This dungeon is essentially his workshop. We can't even grasp how many hidden jiangshi he has stashed away. He'll resurrect infinitely in this place."

"Damn it. So he's immortal."

Wolfgang, true to his dumb warrior nature, doesn't seem to know what a lich is.

'It's not immortality through transcendence—it's a half-baked immortality achieved through cheap tricks. I just need to buy time until Nora finds Mr. Chwe's life vessel. Fortunately, Mr. Chwe doesn't even suspect Nora's existence.'

The restoration by nanomachines is nearly complete. The time I can afford to relax is running short.

"Mercenary."

"Hold on. Count. Aren't you a mercenary too?"

"I do mercenary work as a hobby. Shut up and listen to me. You and I can't be in his field of vision at the same time. If we both get petrified by the basilisk and become immobilized, we'll be finished by the undead he commands. And three minutes. Buy me three minutes."

"You want me to hold that thing off for three minutes? Count. I may not look it, but my body isn't exactly in peak condition right now."

"I need to recover mana and remove the burden on my mana rod. This is strategy for victory. If you refuse... this dungeon becomes our grave."

"You motherfucker! Exactly three minutes!"

Wolfgang dashed to the side, putting distance between us. To prevent both of us from being caught in Mr. Chwe's field of vision simultaneously. I began drinking potions from my inventory, starting with mana recovery potions. The mana rod that had overheated from the battle's aftermath began to settle.

'If only I could properly utilize Phase Shift, I wouldn't need to do this annoying shit.'

It can't be helped.

Since I sold Phase Shift to Marit in exchange for unique skills and magic, I can't use it for the time being.

Mr. Chwe also fully recovers and rises to his feet. Nanomachines slowly seeped out from over his body. Now he'll actively deploy the nanomachines.

"To think the two of you were companions. This one was careless."

Mr. Chwe gripped a magic nunchaku in his right hand and began swinging it with sharp whooshes.

"You half-dead old bastard! This time I'll fucking kill you!!"

"Tough-bodied barbarian. With nanomachines deployed, this one's physical abilities increase by 150%! I'll turn you into minced meat! Abyoooo!"

Mr. Chwe's magic nunchaku and Wolfgang's sword collided, sending sparks flying in all directions.

"Hao! Abyo! Hao!!"

"Fucking! Shit! Motherfucker!!"

Mr. Chwe's bizarre battle cries and Wolfgang's vulgar ones intermingled. Every time they crossed, shockwaves erupted and the ground cracked. A true warrior-versus-warrior battle.

'At a glance, that's what it looks like. But look a little deeper... it's not simply trading blows.'

Curses.

In close combat, Mr. Chwe isn't just moving his limbs—he's weaving in curses. Debuff-type curses that erode the opponent's mental fortitude and physical abilities.

'The curse flying at Wolfgang bounces back at Mr. Chwe instead. Reflection ability. It's obviously not magic. No artifact either. Above all, unconditionally reflecting the curses poured out by a rank-7 lich is beyond common sense. The only thing capable of displaying such an ability beyond common sense...'

Is a unique skill.

"Hao!! Abyoooo! Haoooooo!"

Mr. Chwe let out flamboyant battle cries.

When throwing a punch—Hao.

When swinging the nunchaku—Abyo.

'He's planting them.'

Subconsciously conditioning repetitive learning.

'You get used to the pattern. Once your ears grow accustomed before your eyes, your hands move before your head. Then if he switches the battle cry for a feint? You can't help but fall for it. Damn. Old memories are surfacing. I suffered quite a bit because of this fucking feint.'

On Earth, I was an ordinary person. The Wolfgang fighting Mr. Chwe right now is a rank-7 warrior. A superhuman warrior wouldn't fall for such a simple feint.

"Haoooo!"

Mr. Chwe threw the nunchaku with a battle-cry feint. Wolfgang, who had been preparing to counter the fist, was caught off guard and his reaction was delayed.

'That dumbass. Is he really just a body with no brain?'

The chain portion of the nunchaku wrapped around Wolfgang's sword. The magic nunchaku can increase its weight, so the sword gets dragged downward.

Wolfgang's guard vanished as his breath was stolen. Mr. Chwe's nanomachine fists came crashing in like a band of thieves.

"Haohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohaohao."

"Kraaaaaaah!"

A killing-intent-filled fist rush that made even me, just watching, feel dizzy.

"Haoooooo!"

With a powerful finishing punch, Wolfgang's body was sent flying like a basketball, spraying blood. Yet he caught his balance and stood on the ground without falling.

'...The body of a rank-7 warrior is impressive. To survive after getting hit like that.'

Wolfgang spat out the blood pooling in his mouth and tossed away his shattered sunglasses. His revealed eyes, contrary to his image, were round and doe-like. That characteristic delinquent vibe of his died instantly. There was a reason he wore sunglasses even at night.

"Count! Not yet?!"

"Only one minute and twenty seconds have passed."

"Dammit~!"

I wasn't the only one impressed by Wolfgang's durability.

"Hao hao. To still be standing after taking that. A remarkable martial artist. If I had my way, I'd break that fighting spirit with pure martial arts alone... but the mage from Great Korea is bothering me."

Mr. Chwe's dark mana surged explosively and gathered in the air. The moment I saw the spell formula, I felt it. It was summoning magic. Different from the summoning I use. Not calling a being from elsewhere, but a necromantic summoning that calls forth a soul and constructs a body from dark mana.

"Come forth! My lovely loyal servant! Chingching!"

A massive black cat jiangshi, reaching 3 meters, appeared.

"Nyangnyang!"

"Hao hao! How adorable! Hao hao!"

One of Mr. Chwe's character settings is that he's an animal lover. He kills humans like insects but won't easily kill animals.

Mr. Chwe and the jiangshi cat Chingching ganged up on him.

"Haohaohaohaohaohao!"

"Nyangnyangnyangnyangnyang!"

Wolfgang got beaten to a pulp. I watched that spectacle while drinking a sweet-tasting mana potion and adjusting my Memorize.

"Kraaaaaah! Not yet, Count!!"

"One minute left. Hold out just one more minute."

"Kraaaaaah! I'll hold!!"

Wolfgang gritted his teeth and endured. But as a result, his face became a steamed bun. To only end up with a steamed bun face after all that beating. Incredible durability.

"Nyaaaaaang!"

The black cat Chingching lunged to finish Wolfgang off.

[Thunder Blade]

[Attribute Enchant: Divine]

Chingching was split in half and killed by the golden lightning slash.

"Chingching!!!"

Mr. Chwe let out a scream, but Chingching turned to ash and vanished. Its soul wasn't annihilated, but it would take time to resummon.

"Count...! I believed in you!"

"Don't be gross."

Mr. Chwe glared at me with a furious expression. I also calmly regarded Mr. Chwe.

'Hitting him with Thunder Break won't kill Mr. Chwe. The best I can do right now is buy time. Wolfgang showed me the way. That close combat is better than slinging magic.'

I raised my right hand, the one not holding the Adamantine Trust Light Saber.

Bzzt bzzt!

Blue lightning gathered in my palm and transformed into the shape of a long needle. I took the lightning needle and, without hesitation, drove it into my own temple.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Art (雷術) Marionette Needle (傀儡針).

Blue current spread from my head down to my toes.

Stimulating body and mind to forcibly draw out their latent potential. As a backlash, an immense load began to bear down on my brain.

The current flowing through my entire body was a kind of thread. The one holding the end of that thread is me.

Kiriik. Kirik.

Every time I tried to move a limb, a sound like hinges screaming rang out.

I manipulated my body not through its nerves, but through my mind—my astral body.

'Moving my body indirectly, like controlling a character in a fighting game. ...I expected it, but it moves better than I thought.'

I have almost no talent for the physical. Ordinary exercise is fine, but when I try to move beyond that, my body's senses go slightly out of sync. It's like my body can't keep up with the speed of my mind.

'But with this. If I can manipulate my body through mind alone, the story changes.'

The tip of the Adamantine Trust Light Saber in my left hand pointed at Mr. Chwe.

"I sense tremendous power. Golden magic... I see. Your brain will burn out with time. Hmph!"

Mr. Chwe's eyes flew wide open. His pupils transformed into those of a snake.

He's trying to bind my body with the basilisk's petrification.

Unique Skill (固有技) Absolute Star (絕對星).

The curse directed at me couldn't reach, and the surrounding mana density rose, glittering like starlight.

When the basilisk's petrification failed, a flustered Mr. Chwe bent his own fingers and used the nail curse.

A curse that drives nails of pain directly into the soul.

But even that curse didn't work. To be precise, my soul wasn't connected to him.

"The me of now exists alone and whole. I am not entangled with, nor bound by, anything."

"What?!"

Kiriririk.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

My lightning-wreathed body arrived before him in an instant and drove a plasma blade into his chest.

Mr. Chwe's face contorted as he raised his nunchaku.

Slow.

Far too slow.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A blue light flashed, and Mr. Chwe's head fell.
