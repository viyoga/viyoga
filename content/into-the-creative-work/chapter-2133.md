,To revive the dead...? What kind of bizarre thing are you saying?"

Son Taehyung couldn't hold back and opened his mouth. The eyes of the cleaners gathered on him at the serious question. They were surprised that Son Taehyung, who always had dead eyes and worked silently, spoke first.

,Oh, so Son-ssi can talk too. Well, it's just a rumor. Does it make sense that dead people come back to life?"

Most of the cleaners nodded. Suddenly, the D virus spread to the world, causing a zombie apocalypse, and those who awakened superpowers appeared, but it was common sense that the dead could not come back to life.

Zombies? Who can say they've come back to life when looking at those things? In the first place, zombies weren't corpses but living creatures. At least their hearts are beating.

,I saw it last time when they were rescuing people trapped in their homes, right? At that time, a woman was attacked by a zombie and her neck was torn off, but I saw with my own eyes that Representative Seong healed her perfectly."

,Isn't that healing, not reviving the dead?"

,I'm telling you, her neck was half torn off? That injury is instant death. An instantly dead human got up again and moved perfectly, so it's reviving! Besides, that woman wasn't even infected!"

,Eh. You're talking nonsense. In the first place, the injury wasn't enough to kill her. And the infection... she must have been lucky."

,Luck my ass. Even awakened ones are done for if they get infected because there's no vaccine. Representative Seong can definitely revive the dead!"

,Alright, be quiet! If the manager hears us having this conversation... we'll be imprisoned. You know how shitty the detention center is!"

,The detention center... right. We have to be careful what we say if we don't want to be dragged there. The prisoners in the detention center eat animal feed, not rice."

,Even though our job is shitty, it's much better than the prisoners in the detention center, right? At least we don't fill our bellies with feed."

The cleaners' conversation topic shifted to the detention center. They comforted themselves by chewing on the prisoners locked in the detention room, saying they were better off.

Son Taehyung couldn't hear their conversation. His head was complicated. Nothing was certain. Reviving the dead was just a rumor, but.

But what if that rumor is true? It's a world where zombies are born and superpowered people appear. It wouldn't be surprising to have a superpower that revives the dead.

,...Even if Representative Seong has such an ability, there's no way he'd use it for me.'

Seong Yujin is the one who created the order of the current Seongak Elementary School. The dictator Seong Yujin who threw democracy into the mud. The Constitution of the Republic of Korea was meaningless here. Seong Yujin's will was the law.

,Representative Seong is a man of clear rewards and punishments. If I prove my ability and make achievements.... Maybe I can receive Representative Seong's benefits.'

The images of his beloved wife and lovely daughter flickered before Son Taehyung's eyes.

The dead coming back to life is absurd. It's an act against the truth. The Father in heaven would not allow it either.

But he wanted to see his family even if he fell into hell.

,...In the first place. If God exists, he shouldn't have made the world like this. Does a place called hell even exist?'

The former pastor abandoned his last remaining faith.

The way for him, a cleaner, to make achievements. Focus on cleaning work? No way. No matter how cleanly he cleaned, he couldn't escape his status.

The two methods Son Taehyung came up with were:

One is snitching. Finding and snitching on dissidents. Of course, since snitching itself isn't good, his social reputation could drop.

The other is to volunteer for the periodic zombie extermination. It's dangerous to fight zombies at the risk of his life, but it's perfect for making achievements. If he's lucky, he can enter the security team with just one extermination.

Son Taehyung decided to do both.

He snitched on the same cleaner. When cleaning up trash, sometimes intact items come out. Originally, these items were supposed to be given to the manager. But the cleaners took the intact trash while watching the manager's eyes. Son Taehyung snitched on that cleaner to the manager. He received money as compensation.

,Y-You dirty bastard! You were a pastor and you did this!! You sold out your colleague for that petty money?! You colleague-selling bastard! Do you think you can live well after doing this?!"

The cleaner poured out his hatred as he was dragged to the detention center. Originally, it's right to hide the snitch, but the rumor spread about who the snitch was because it was such a closed place. Son Taehyung didn't care.

From that day on, Son Taehyung became a construction worker instead of a cleaner. He mainly digs the ground and builds houses. The treatment is much better than a cleaner. However, because he became a worker through snitching, his colleagues didn't approach him. Son Taehyung didn't care about this either.

The announcement he had been waiting for was posted on the community bulletin board. It was an announcement recruiting an extermination team for safety and expansion. Son Taehyung didn't hesitate and volunteered for the extermination team.

These days, information about my abilities was circulating within Seongak Elementary School. Everyone knows about the lightning, and the healing ability is becoming known.

,Did I use the healing ability too openly last time?'

Rumors were subtly circulating that I could even revive the dead. Of course, no one easily believes it. Logically, reviving the dead doesn't make sense.

,I have to hide that at least. I'll have to be more careful when using my abilities in the future.'

It might be okay to announce the healing ability at this time. Announce it only to the extent that I can heal wounds, no more.

,But I can't use my healing ability on just anyone. I should only give benefits to those who will become my slaves.'

Discrimination was essential.

That way, the class society I want will become more solid.

I spread out a map on the desk. A map drawn with Seongak Elementary School as the center.

,Seongak Elementary School is slowly becoming saturated.'

I accepted people nearby as much as possible, thinking of increasing the size first. Of course, after going through a primary screening process.

,The stockpiled food is okay. The problem is that there's not enough space for people to stay.'

I made them build container boxes in the playground and even build houses with planks. But that's also reaching its limit now. Too many people have gathered.

,I have to expand Seongak Elementary School.'

I didn't intend to break the existing walls. I'm thinking of building new walls based on Seongak Elementary School to create an external village.

,Slaves should live with slaves.'

I want to eat and dominate the entire Seoul, but... unfortunately, I don't have the capacity to do that right now. The government is alive. At least it's maintaining the national system.

,I might have to fight the Korean government later... so I should focus on training troops and making weapons now.'

The day of the extermination.

I turned around at the school gate entrance. About 120 people were standing in an orderly manner. Half were security team members, and the other half were those who volunteered for this extermination team. Surprisingly, quite a few women volunteered too.

Only the security team had guns among them. The rest were armed with cold weapons such as spears, bats, and swords. Well, I'm the same with a sword.

,The population of Seongak Elementary School is increasing day by day."

If you look at the internet, there are rumors that Seongak Elementary School is safe and has a lot of supplies. Seeing that, there were those who sought Seongak Elementary School as a family. More than 70% die on the way.

,But the space of Seongak Elementary School is limited. The time has come to expand the land. But we can't just expand it recklessly. Because there's a shortage of supplies such as building materials. We will extract supplies from furniture stores, marts, and hardware stores 2km away."

They're abandoned anyway. No one will say anything even if we take them on a large scale. Even if there's an owner, we can kill the owner and take them.

,Those who make achievements will be given reasonable rewards, and those who disrupt the order will be punished. Let's go."

I started moving, leading the troops. I felt pretty good seeing over 100 guys groveling at my words. A drone slowly flew in the sky, following behind.

The drone's role was reconnaissance. The method was that if Na Chaeyoung discovered something through the camera attached to the drone, she would tell me.

-Five zombies ahead.

Na Chaeyoung's indifferent voice was heard through the earphones. Old memories come to mind. Na Chaeyoung's voice was stiff and nervous when she first did it.

,Five zombies are nothing.'

I gestured to the security team nearby. The security team, who had gained some experience, quickly dealt with the five zombies with silenced pistols.

The supporters from the general public put the dead zombies on a cart. Zombies and living things can be put into the decomposer to obtain resources.

Zombies kept popping out as we moved forward. The soldiers dealt with the zombies without me having to step in directly.

The cases where I step in directly are when there are too many zombies or when mutants pop out.

Mutants are dangerous. They're difficult for even awakened ones to deal with. Really powerful mutants are difficult for even me to deal with now.

,Fortunately, there don't seem to be that many powerful mutants in Seoul.'

I arrived at a large mart. I sent a drone first to scout the inside.

-It's been looted. There are toys left that aren't very helpful for survival... but these won't be very valuable as resources either.

,Are there no materials like iron, wood, or paint?"

-No. It seems like a group of survivors looted everything.

A group of survivors.

As the world has become like this, people have started to gather. It was natural for families to gather, and it was common for apartment complexes to gather.

,Are there zombies inside?"

-There are zombie corpses. They're rotting. Looking at the wounds, it seems like they were beaten to death with something like a golf club. ...It's quite severe. It would be difficult with the strength of an ordinary human.

,It must be an awakened one."

Awakened ones were appearing as time passed. There were a few awakened ones in Seongak Elementary School too. They were 1-star (★) awakened ones who weren't very helpful, though.

,At least there won't be any technologists or bionics. Because they can use the decomposer. They would have taken everything, leaving no corpses behind.'

I took my eyes off the mart and said to the soldiers.

,There's no time to rest here. Keep moving."
