As I was running through the tunnel, Na Chae-young's urgent voice came through.

-"I've confirmed that a Roachist suddenly appeared outside and is running towards the tunnel! We've blocked the one where we're positioned... but there's a high possibility they'll enter through another tunnel!"

Roachists.

Those bug bastards basically move in packs of at least 10 or more.

'According to the data China sent us, Roachists have the ability to absorb the genetic material of Variants and make it their own.'

What the bug bastards are after isn't Magmanium, but the genetic material of the Magma Eater. A bug covered in the Magma Eater's carapace? That means ordinary bullets won't work. Just imagining it was horrifying.

'It doesn't seem like they evolve immediately just by having the genetic material, though...'

Can't let those bug bastards have their way.

-Currently, the Magma Eater is engaged in battle with Thunderbolt Knights Squad 1, Veritas, and military forces belonging to Jilin Province.

Jena reported.

Not that I needed to be told, the sounds of gunfire are reaching me here.

A wide space opened up. Judging from the barricades scattered here and there, it seemed to be a place where the Chinese army had been temporarily stationed.

The floor was covered with Chinese soldiers' corpses and blood, and a wounded Magma Eater was fighting the knights while Son Tae-hyung was battling a multi-legged tank controlled by artificial intelligence head-on. Son Tae-hyung's left arm was mangled, and two of the multi-legged tank's front legs had been cut off. A fierce back-and-forth had taken place in just a short time.

'The situation is a mess.'

Should I eliminate the weakest-looking remnants of the Chinese army first? Or should I deal with the most troublesome enemy, the multi-legged tank, together with Son Tae-hyung?

'...No. I'll kill the Magma Eater before it runs away again.'

I ran towards the Magma Eater.

Lightning Sky Style, Gale Lightning Flash.

My body and mind accelerate.

The Magma Eater reacted to my speed. As expected of a powerful Variant. It tried to avoid me instead of fighting.

'Accelerate one more time.'

Pajijijik!

Acceleration upon acceleration. As a side effect, it feels like my nerves are burning. No, my body actually couldn't handle it. If I had mana, it might be different. A body without mana was more fragile than I expected.

'Endure with recovery.'

Kwajik!

I jump and stab my sword into the Magma Eater's head. An attack with the weight of the power armor combined with the super-vibrating blade enhanced with lightning pierced its carapace.

'...There's no sensation of piercing the brain. Is the brain further inside?'

I couldn't kill it in one go.

But it doesn't matter. The moment the sword was stuck in its head, the match was decided.

Wiiiiiiiiiiing!

The generator built into the power armor spins at maximum output.

Pajijijijijijijijijijijijijijijijijijik!

Blue lightning bursts out, illuminating the dark tunnel and flowing into the Magma Eater's body. It fried not only its brain but also its internal organs to a crisp.

The Magma Eater thrashed as if making a final struggle. Its sharp legs scratched the surface of my power armor. But the force wasn't properly applied, and it soon convulsed and slumped.

I put one of the Magma Eater's teeth into my waist socket. It's a kind of insurance. Even if I lose the Magma Eater's corpse, I can recover it with this one tooth fragment.

I threw the Magma Eater's smoking corpse to the knights.

"You guys guard the Magma Eater's corpse. If necessary, self-destruct with the corpse. The corpse must not fall into the hands of the enemy."

"Shouldn't we deal with the blood the Magma Eater shed?"

"I heard that the Magma Eater's blood immediately cools and hardens when it meets the air. No genetic material comes out of it, so you just need to guard the corpse well."

-Reinforcements have arrived near the entrance. Marking the retreat route.

Tteuk.

Jena's words were cut off.

Immediately after, Son Tae-hyung was blown away with a roar. His body rolled to my feet. Son Tae-hyung's head was gone. It was smashed to pieces.

Looking at the multi-legged tank that had been fighting Son Tae-hyung, it was revealing a hidden cannon barrel. Smoke was flowing from the end of the barrel.

"It's a special weapon prepared for the Great Magma Eater, but I never thought it would be used on a human."

"Amazing. To think a machine could beat a Knight Commander-level guy."

Son Tae-hyung is one of the top five strongest in Korea. It was truly unexpected for such Son Tae-hyung to be defeated.

'The power armor is difficult to move with just the strength of the human body, so it receives assistance from Jena. Just now, the connection with Jena was cut off. Did they use the cannon to kill Son Tae-hyung in the gap where Jena's connection was severed?'

The knights, not caring about the death of their commander Son Tae-hyung, began to carry the Magma Eater's corpse outside. They manually operate the power armor. It's tough without Jena's assistance, but it's not impossible. The knights ran, trampling the remaining Chinese soldiers to death.

Kiing, kiing!

The sensors of the nearly half-destroyed multi-legged tank turn towards the knights. I blocked its path.

"Did you cut off external communication?"

"Yes. You guys can't properly control the power armor without the help of artificial intelligence. Artificial intelligence Jena seems competent, so I can't block it for long, but I can last for 5 minutes. And 5 minutes is more than enough time to kill you."

"Haha. You're talking like you've already won just because you've neutralized one artificial intelligence."

"What can you guys do without the help of artificial intelligence?"

"I've never relied on artificial intelligence. In the end, the only one you can trust is yourself."

Chijijijijik!

I seize the current generated from the generator and send that current through the power armor to control it. The current was my nerves, and the power armor was my body. In some ways, it's more than receiving Jena's assistance.

"If you promise to let me observe a part of the Magma Eater's body, I will help you retreat. Roachists are approaching at a rapid pace. This is a mutually beneficial deal."

"A deal? Don't try to match yourself with me, you machine."

I charged towards it.

The multi-legged tank raises its legs and points its cannon at me.

Pajijijik!

Blue lightning grabs the tank's body and cannon, pulling them down.

"...Magnetism?!"

The panicked artificial intelligence fired the cannon without properly aiming. The shell flew towards my lower body and stopped. It was also blocked by magnetism and caught in the air. Like a small bug caught in a spider web.

"...That's impossible. Even if you control electromagnetic force, this output is not an output that a human can handle."

"It's not easy, I'll admit."

Although it's hidden by the helmet, blood is trickling down my nose. The generator's power provides the output, but controlling the current was solely my responsibility. It feels like my brain is being overworked to the point of being cooked.

'My consciousness is getting a little blurry. Is it actually cooking? It's okay. I'll use recovery.'

Using abilities in succession consumes a large amount of stamina. If I hadn't been constantly training, I wouldn't have been able to endure it and would have collapsed.

Peok!

I stabbed my sword into the body of the multi-legged tank.

"I'll analyze and use the technology in this tin can well. Thanks to you, I saved on technology development costs."

"...Do you think a human can utilize this technology 100%? Or are you trusting artificial intelligence Jena? Do you think she, a strong AI, will follow your orders forever?"

"That's a transparent attempt to drive a wedge between us. To put it bluntly... I don't care whether Jena betrays me or not. The moment she betrays me, I'll crush Jena's main body. Ah, was it Veritas? Make sure to hide your main body well. The moment your main body is discovered... I'll go and kill you myself."

"......"

I send the current through. All the internal circuit boards of the multi-legged tank burn and disappear.

I take some parts of the multi-legged tank and go outside.

Jena was still unresponsive.

'To think that communication would be so easily seized by him. It seems like countermeasures are needed.'

Na Chae-young will suffer a bit.

On the way out, I met Veritas again. He was controlling a robot dog instead of a multi-legged tank. The robot dog was surrounded by a group of Roachists and struggling.

"We meet again."

"You tiresome bastard."

"The Roachists will target both you and me at the same time. This time, we'll have to fight back-to-back."

I approached the robot dog. And I stabbed my sword into its body, splitting it in half. Even artificial intelligence becomes careless the moment it has an ego.

"...What foolishness are you doing?"

"Know your place. You and the bugs are just enemies I have to kill."

Kwajik!

I stomped on the robot dog's head and crushed it.

And I look around. At a glance, there are more than 30 bugs shining their eyes and dripping saliva.

"Disgusting as hell."

Generator at maximum output.

Wiiiiiiiiiiiiing.

Dozens of streams of lightning spewed out from the power armor. I controlled each and every one of those streams of lightning and rotated them.

Lightning Sky Style, Myriad Lightning Resonance.

I compress and amplify my lightning and the generator's electricity in a myriad lightning method, causing them to resonate with each other.

The bugs couldn't dare to rush at me and maintained their encirclement.

I discharge the lightning amplified to its limit.

Kwareureureureureung!

Lightning Sky Style, Thunderous Thousand Blades.

Thousands of streams of lightning as sharp as knives swept around. The bugs couldn't even scream and were ground up as if they had been put in a mixer.

Soon the lightning subsided, but it remained on the floor, sparking blue lightning.

The floor, walls, and ceiling were full of traces as if they had been cut by thousands of blades.

I took a moment to catch my breath and went outside.

On the way, I found a pile of bug corpses. Dozens of bugs were dead, forming a wall.

The knights were lying next to it. Only one was barely alive. Some of the power armor was torn off, and blood was pooling through the gaps. I thought about saving him here, but I stopped. I had to prepare for the worst-case scenario. I needed to conserve stamina.

"Your Excellency.... I carried out the order. I protected the Magma Eater's corpse. The tunnel could collapse, so I didn't self-destruct."

"...You've done well. I'll take the Magma Eater's corpse. The reinforcements will recover your corpse. See you again."

Even as I say this, I don't really know the other knight's name.

I wasn't very interested in male knights. If Jena had been functioning properly, she would have told me the knight's name.

"...Yes. Seong-nim...."

The knight died.

I picked up the Magma Eater's corpse and moved. Even with the power armor's strength, it was difficult to lift alone, so I dragged it outside.

On the way, I encountered the reinforcement knights.

I left the cleanup to the knights and returned to the transport helicopter.

I obtained Magmanium.

Even if it's difficult to use right away, Korea will be ahead of other countries.
