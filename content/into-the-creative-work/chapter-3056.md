I was at the heart of the Mountain of Wood.

The place that could be called the source of the vital force emanating from this secret realm, the spiritual energy of the Mountain of Wood.

Those who had previously occupied this place and focused on their training were the Murim Alliance, but the Murim Alliance had all left the secret realm by my command. Even martial artists from other backgrounds—the Unorthodox Faction, wandering blades—all read the mood and departed the secret realm.

Currently, this secret realm was occupied by me, Mi-ryeong, and the martial artists belonging to the Heavenly Demon Cult.

'With the death of Namhwa Noseon, the administrator of the secret realm, the spiritual energy within is withering and the realm itself is slowly collapsing. I'll need to leave within a few years.'

I sat cross-legged in a place overflowing with the energy of life. Breathing quietly, I assimilated myself with nature. My body, which had been seated on the ground, floated gently into the air.

Body, mind, and soul resonated with the heavenly and earthly spiritual energy. I could feel the life around me. From the tiny sprout budding in a corner to the life of a single small worm crawling through the soil.

'This feels different from when I surveyed my surroundings with electromagnetic waves.'

The preparations were complete.

I took out the Thunderstorm Black Horn from my inventory. And I took out the Manroe Shin Dan—the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill—that I had been saving.

A blue gemstone, a pill like a jewel containing lightning writhing within it like a storm.

The Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill enhanced through the Radiant Ascension Path was not inferior to the Thunderstorm Black Horn when it came to pure lightning energy alone.

'No, in terms of ferocity, the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill is rougher. In purity of lightning energy, the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill wins by a landslide. It seems its essence changed slightly when I enhanced it with the Radiant Ascension Path.'

That certainly didn't mean it was a bad thing.

I had roughly anticipated what would happen if I consumed both spiritual medicines simultaneously.

'Even though they're both lightning-attribute spiritual objects, the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill is too rough. And the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill... I felt it before, but even now, it seems the time isn't right.'

Just reaching the realm of Samjeong allows one to sense things ordinary people cannot. You could call it a sixth sense, or an intuition about the future. Of course, you don't feel this sensation at just any time.

'According to Wi-yu, it's a sensation felt when something is deeply connected to one's fate.'

I placed the Ten Thousand Thunder God Pill back into my inventory.

I raised the Thunderstorm Black Horn. I channeled power into it as bright yellow currents flickered across its surface. Just because something is a spiritual medicine doesn't mean it must be consumed orally.

The spiritual energy of the Thunderstorm Black Horn connected with me.

KWAARRRRRNG!

The Thunderstorm Black Horn erupted with yellow lightning and thunderclaps, resisting. It was like a beast not just on guard, but openly hostile.

'As expected of a spiritual object belonging to an immortal—it's extraordinary.'

Yellow lightning flew toward me. Lightning intense enough to reduce even an absolute master of the Samjeong realm to ash in an instant, and to leave even an avatar of the Johwa realm with horrific internal injuries.

'Thunderbolt.'

However, the blue lightning that erupted from my fingertips lunged at the yellow lightning and subdued it in an instant. The snarling yellow lightning scattered and the thunderclaps vanished. I crooked my finger toward the now-docile Thunderstorm Black Horn.

The Thunderstorm Black Horn melted in midair and became a single golden bolt of lightning, flashing brilliantly.

'Come.'

The golden lightning embedded itself precisely in my chest. I closed my eyes and breathed. The golden lightning began to be absorbed into me, little by little.

It was a heavy energy, condensed spiritual force.

'Even for me, absorbing this in a short time won't be possible.'

I fell into a state of selflessness.

Time passed.

The golden lightning embedded in my chest was absorbed, little by little, bit by bit.

The flow of spiritual energy that had coursed through the secret realm collapsed, and the realm descended into chaos.

Snow fell on the Mountain of Wood, which should have been sunny all 365 days of the year; scorching heat blazed down; fierce winds swept through; earthquakes struck. Yet the vegetation of the Mountain of Wood did not lose its verdant freshness. Because I, at the heart of the Mountain of Wood, controlled the vital force.

I did not control the vital force absolutely and absorb all of it.

Absorbing the spiritual energy of the Thunderstorm Black Horn alone was sufficient. I absorbed a portion of the vital force, but most of it had to circulate.

Vital force that does not move and stagnates is not true vital force. The collapsing spiritual energy tried to scatter the vital force, but I became the center of the spiritual energy and manipulated the circulation of the vital force.

Bzzt, bzzzzt!

From the trees and grasses, the small animals and insects of the Mountain of Wood, pale green lightning sparked. The lightning interacted with other flora and fauna throughout the mountain, circulating.

The wounds on trees from stones flung by the fierce winds healed; flowers that had withered and died from the scorching heat became fresh as if reversing time. Animals bred, flowers bloomed on the earth, and fruit ripened on the trees.

I absorbed all of the Thunderstorm Black Horn's spiritual energy and emerged from the state of selflessness, opening my eyes. Instinctively, I knew three years had passed.

I had ascended to the fifth stage of the Johwa realm. In merely three years of training, I had risen two whole stages.

"Wow. This is totally click-and-done training!"

Mi-ryeong was before my eyes. Behind her stood my subordinates belonging to the Heavenly Demon Cult. I looked over my subordinates once. Overall, they had risen two to three stages.

"Everyone, well done."

Yeon Seo-rang, the Right Protector and Sword Demon, bowed his head deeply.

"...I have no face to show. Despite receiving full support from the Cult Leader, I could not break through the wall of the Johwa realm."

Yeon Seo-rang's realm was the tenth stage of Samjeong. He had been at the ninth stage before entering the secret realm, and had risen one stage, but could not break through the wall of Johwa.

"It would be a lie to say I didn't have expectations. However, I know that failing to cross the wall is simply unavoidable. Those who reach the Johwa realm are a minuscule few even among absolute masters. Even if I offered advice, if you cannot realize it yourself, it becomes empty words—or in the worst case, a delusion. Just keep pushing forward."

"Yes. Cult Leader. Thank you."

Seven years since entering the secret realm.

One could call it a short time, but the results of the training surpassed spending decades outside.

'The truce with the Murim Alliance must have ended by now.'

War?

I wasn't worried. Unless Zhuge Yun had gone mad, there was no way the Murim Alliance would attack the Heavenly Demon Cult after their leader died. They were already the ones who had surrendered and submitted to me.

We went outside.

• • •

Zhuge Yun, the military strategist of the Murim Alliance, let out a sigh the moment he stepped out of the secret realm. Unlike the depressive mood that made him feel suicidal impulses, the sky was clear as if it held no worries at all.

As the martial artists of the Murim Alliance emerged from the secret realm, the warrior monks of Shaolin who had been guarding the entrance appeared, along with their abbot, the Fist Buddha Master Wu-gyeong.

Zhuge Yun momentarily devised a plan to install Master Wu-gyeong as the new Murim Alliance Leader. To throw away the Murim Alliance's honor and strike the Heavenly Demon Cult first while the truce was in effect...

Zhuge Yun stopped his thoughts and smiled bitterly.

The Fist Buddha Master Wu-gyeong had recently ascended to the Johwa realm. His stage was lower than the Heavenly Demon's, and he was weaker than the dead Murim Alliance Leader Chwiseon-gae. Even if the Hundred-Step Divine Fist that Kwon-bul had mastered was strong against demonic arts, his opponent was the Heavenly Demon who had killed the Murim Alliance Leader. There was no way he could win.

Moreover, he had already knelt at the Heavenly Demon's feet, bowing his head and begging for mercy. He was already a defeated man.

"Hyeonseong. What brings you out so early? Where is the Alliance Leader?"

"...The Alliance Leader has passed away."

“…….”

Kwon-bul gripped his prayer beads tightly. Among the masters who had entered the secret realm, the strongest—the Murim Alliance Leader—was dead? There was only one picture that formed.

Within the secret realm, the Heavenly Demon and the Murim Alliance Leader had fought a decisive battle, and the Murim Alliance Leader had lost.

"...Amitabha."

"The Murim Alliance has lost. Fortunately, the Heavenly Demon will not annihilate the Orthodox Faction. He desires dominion. Naturally, there are conditions. One of them is the sealing of Shaolin's gates."

"Shaolin does not submit to demons."

"I know. However, there is no chance of victory. Abbot, could you fight the Heavenly Demon and win?"

“…….”

Kwon-bul squeezed his eyes shut. Even as a lie, it was hard to see any chance of victory. The Heavenly Demon had already killed the Murim Alliance Leader, who could be called the Orthodox Faction's greatest master.

"...If we joined forces with the Wudang's Taiji Heavenly Lord, there would be a chance."

The former Murim Alliance Leader, the Taiji Heavenly Lord Gong Baek-sung.

"You know as well as I do. Wudang will not step forward. Moreover, it was the Murim Alliance that drove him out. We are in no position to ask him for favors now."

"...What about requesting aid from His Imperial Majesty..."

"The principle of non-interference between the government and the martial world. An unwritten rule that has held until now. We ask His Imperial Majesty for help simply because we are desperate? What meaning would that have? Above all, His Imperial Majesty favors the Heavenly Demon. That Heavenly Demon also did not ask His Imperial Majesty for help in martial world affairs."

“…….”

"Abbot. We have lost. Our era has become the Heavenly Demon's era. The Heavenly Demon's era will end someday. We, the defeated, have no choice but to comply and wait for that day. Fighting with death resolved is a noble and magnificent thing, but I wish for my children to live on, even if they feel humiliation. Abbot. Think of Shaolin's future."

"...I will consider it."

A few days later, Shaolin declared the sealing of its gates indefinitely.

The Murim Alliance officially announced its defeat in the Great War of Orthodox versus Demonic.

Naturally, not all martial artists of the Orthodox Faction accepted this. However, reality was not so easy, so even if they could not accept it, they endured. Yet some martial artists could not suppress their righteous spirit.

"Master. This unworthy disciple has committed the crime of betraying his sect. Please punish me with expulsion."

"Is staining the clothes I cherished a crime of betrayal? I can forgive you. Would I, your master, not know your heart? Can you not lay down your righteous spirit? This martial world has become the demon's, but that will not last forever."

"I clenched my fist to spread righteousness, and raised my sword to destroy evil. Darkness has fallen over the martial world, so I will become the light that practices righteousness and chivalry, and drive out the darkness."

"Do you know that path is a road of thorns?"

"I learned martial arts to cut through a road of thorns."

"...I expel you."

"Thank you, Master."

Martial artists possessing righteousness and chivalry gathered and formed a single force, and it was called the Righteous Heaven Society.

It was a force less than half of half compared to the Murim Alliance. It had no fixed base. Most of its martial artists were wanderers, and it was a cell-based organization.

The prestigious Orthodox Faction sects accepted the situation, but the small and mid-sized sects did not.

"Because we're the Orthodox Faction, we have to pay half our taxes to the Heavenly Demon Cult? And spiritual medicines on top of that?"

"They're trying to squeeze blood from a flea."

"We'll convert to the Unorthodox Faction. The Heavenly Demon Cult will be too busy managing the great sects to spare attention for small sects like ours."

In truth, the Heavenly Demon Cult had no capacity to manage the small and mid-sized sects that had converted to the Unorthodox Faction. For the time being, at least.

And three years passed.

Having heard in advance the news that the Heavenly Demon would emerge, hundreds of thousands of martial artists from the Orthodox, Unorthodox, and Demonic factions gathered before the secret realm to greet the Supreme of the Martial World.

The slowly collapsing gate of the secret realm opened, and the Heavenly Demon appeared.

Hundreds of thousands of martial artists raised their fists in salute in unison. All of them became one and shouted.

"We greet the Supreme of the Martial World!"
