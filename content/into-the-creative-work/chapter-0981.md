,"I am General Baeun, serving the Western Ox Divine General. People call me White Snow Spear. I am currently in charge of managing the Myriad Demon Tower, and I am responsible for the first floor trial of the Myriad Demon Tower."

The Western Ox Divine General is one of the Four Divine Generals of the Jin Empire. As the name suggests, the Western Ox Divine General is the general in charge of the west. For reference, the Four Divine Generals are among the top ten most powerful people in the empire.

,"Without further ado... the trial of the first floor is 1 gold nyang. Pay me 1 gold nyang before the sun sets today. Then I will send you up to the second floor."

1 gold nyang is by no means a small amount. The average commoner's monthly income is about 5 silver nyang. To put it simply, 1 gold nyang is the equivalent of a commoner's two-year earnings.

And most of the martial artists who visit the Myriad Demon Tower are ronin. There is a widespread perception that ronin are basically poor. And that's actually true. Ronin tend to spend their money as soon as they earn it, rather than saving it. They don't know when they're going to die, so they spend their money without regret until they die. That was the ronin's mindset.

Therefore, it was only natural that complaints erupted from all directions.

,"We came to break through the trials of the Myriad Demon Tower with our skills! Not to spend money!"

,"As far as I know, the last Myriad Demon Tower's first floor trial was a martial arts competition! So why is it like this this year? This Myriad Demon Tower is my last chance!"

It became as noisy as a marketplace. Baeun looked around once and stomped his foot. *Kwang!* The loud sound was a threat that spread in all directions. The ronin who felt the threat shut their mouths.

,"Money represents value. The most reasonable standard of value is money. A martial artist who can't earn 1 gold nyang is not even worth a third-rate martial artist. It doesn't matter how. Borrow money, steal money, sell your belongings, make 1 gold nyang. Prove your minimum value."

,"...You said it doesn't matter how. Do you really mean that?"

,"However, do not harm civilians. If you harm the citizens of Myriad Demon City, I will execute you. I will cut off your heads and display them in the square of Myriad Demon City."

,"......"

Baeun's words were brutal. And he had the power to do so.

,"I am Oh Jimu, the young Head of the family of the Three Cutting Sect. I will give you 55 gold nyang here. Can I go up to the second floor of the Myriad Demon Tower?"

,"...Pass. Go in."

,"May I ask what the trial of the second floor of the Myriad Demon Tower is?"

,"You will find out when you go up to the second floor. And you will receive a reward for passing the first floor trial."

,"A reward... I guess it's a reward worth less than 1 gold nyang? I don't have much expectations."

,"You talk too much. Do you want to upset me that much?"

Oh Jimu immediately lowered his tail. He knows that there is no benefit in fighting with the manager of the Myriad Demon Tower.

,"Why should I do that? It's already money I paid. Get out of here."

I watched the series of events and turned around. All the members of the Black Iron Squad were rummaging through their pockets.

Then you can get more elixirs.
