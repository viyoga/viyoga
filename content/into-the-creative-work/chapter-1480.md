I arrived at the witch's house, located deep inside the forest.

The witch's house was ordinary. Just a common house made of wood.

Standing in front of the witch's house, I turned around. Hans was sweating profusely, breathing heavily. His complexion was ashen. He looked like he was about to have a heart attack and die, so anxious. It was the result of constantly hearing the ghosts' screams on the way here.

"I'll face the witch head-on. You aim for her from behind."

"...Understood. I'll watch for an opportunity and stab that dagger into the witch's back, for sure."

Knowing that his condition wasn't fit for a frontal fight, Hans agreed to my instructions and hid himself in the forest.

'He's a mercenary from a hunter background, as they say; his ability to hide is excellent.'

At this rate, there's no way Hans will be detected by the witch.

I strode towards the witch's house. Clenching my left fist tightly, I smashed the wooden door. The door flew backward, smashing the table. The things on the table fell and shattered. The witch's house instantly became a mess.

"Who the hell?!"

A witch with red hair burst out from inside the room. Dressed in black, the witch's appearance was beautiful.

Flawless, snow-white skin and distinct features. Any man would say she was beautiful.

And what about her figure? The black dress, clinging tightly to her body, revealed her captivating lines. A shapely waist and voluptuous hips. Truly, a body that men would prefer.

However, I won't fall for her.

Kiiiiiiing.

I activate Holy Aura. Demonic power surged around her.

The witch, who had been wearing a venomous expression, burst into laughter when she saw me.

"You! The new Paladin of Valentiere! Hahaha! I never thought the rumors about you coming to kill me would be true!"

"Witch Helen. In the name of the Goddess Herianne, I will execute you."

"A greenhorn who's only been a Paladin for a month? I've lived three times your life. I made a contract with a demon and obtained eternal youth. If I kill you and offer your corpse to the Great Demon... Ah. Just imagining it is thrilling. I'm getting wet down there. How about it? Wanna go to bed together?"

"You hideous bitch! Go roll around with monsters in hell!"

"Hahaha! You know better than anyone that there's no hell! We just wander this world after we die! Or get eaten by demons!"

"My soul will be taken by the Goddess Herianne. Die, witch!"

[Using Acceleration. Duration: 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 6]

Grasping the sword hilt with both hands, I used Dash and Acceleration to rush at the witch. Thump, thump, thump! Three heavy footsteps echoed in succession. Arriving in front of the witch, I swung my sword imbued with golden divinity.

Kwaang!

An explosion rang out.

My body flew backward, past the cabin, and rolled on the ground.

'Air Burst! Magic, huh. It would have been dangerous if I didn't have armor.'

I jumped up and swung my sword. Golden sword energy sliced through the cabin and flew towards the witch.

The witch covered her mouth with the back of her hand and laughed. Just before the sword energy reached her body, another Air Burst occurred, scattering the sword energy. The scattered fragments of sword energy made a mess of the house.

"Hahaha. My house is a mess? But it's okay. I was planning to move my base somewhere else anyway. A Paladin's corpse as a housewarming gift... How wonderful."

The witch reached out her hand. A broom lying on the floor flew into the witch's hand. As the witch swung the broom, the broken house and fallen objects floated into the sky. It was the witch's magic.

All the objects floating in the air pointed at me.

"Don't die so easily. It's been a while since I've used this much magic... It wouldn't be fun if it ended too quickly. Hahaha."

With the witch's frivolous laughter, the objects flew towards me.

Trusting in my armor, I moved forward. The objects flew and shattered against the armor. I felt an invisible force trying to push me back. It felt like walking into a storm.

'Holy Aura!'

Sacred energy spread in all directions, centered on my body. The magical force that had been constricting me was neutralized, making my movements easier. I started running towards the witch.

The witch's laughter faltered. The corners of her mouth drooped. The witch rode her broom and ascended.

"Young Paladin. I have some babies prepared for times like these."

The ground shook, and monsters popped out. These monsters had human bodies, but they weren't in human form.

"How are my babies? Cute, right? I made them one by one whenever I had time. At first, it was just to kill time... But it became fun as I did it. Now it's a hobby."

What caught my eye was a particularly small monster. A monster with the heads of a young boy and girl. Its four arms and four legs were twisted, and its two torsos were seared together face-to-face. Its intestines were wrapped around its torso like a belt.

"...A disgusting hobby, befitting a woman who offered her family to demons."

"Hahaha! You like it? You haven't been able to take your eyes off it since earlier. Were they kids you knew, by any chance?"

They're kids I don't know.

But I know they're Hans' children.

Hans is still hiding. I was inwardly impressed by Hans' patience. Hans is enduring his anger, watching for an opportunity to stab the witch in the back with his dagger.

'I need to create that opportunity for him.'

The monsters rushed at me. I ran towards them, swinging my greatsword. One monster was crushed by the sword, spraying blood everywhere. I didn't stop and continued to swing my sword.

Kwaang!

A wind fell from above, pressing down on my body. It was the witch's magic. I barely managed to endure by planting my sword in the ground just before kneeling. The monsters didn't miss the opportunity and clung to my body.

"Hahahahaha! Just die like that! I'll take good care of your corpse!"

The monsters that were Hans' children clung to my shoulders. They opened their mouths wide, trying to gnaw at my helmet. The other monsters also pounded on my armor.

I could feel the armor slowly breaking and tearing. This is dangerous.

'Holy Aura!'

Using divine power, I increased the output of Holy Aura. The monsters hesitated and backed away. I grabbed the head of the monster that was Hans' child with my grip and crushed it, then slashed the nearby monsters to death with my greatsword.

Greatsword, fist, foot. Using everything, I killed the monsters around me one by one.

The witch stared at me with a look of dismay.

"You cleaned up those kids so quickly? There really was a monster in front of me. Do you know what you look like right now? A demon covered in blood from head to toe, that's what you are."

"Shut up, witch. You're next."

"Heung. What are you going to do to me? You don't even have wings."

Being able to fly is an absolute advantage. Especially for a witch who specializes in ranged attacks. But the witch isn't the only one who can attack from a distance.

I picked up any object on the ground and threw it at the witch.

It didn't reach the witch's body. Magic protected the witch.

'But there are limits to the witch's power.'

As proof, the smile on the witch's face has completely disappeared. The witch pointed at me with her finger. An invisible bullet of air was fired at me. Relying on my combat senses, I dodged the air bullet. Even if I got hit by accident, the armor could withstand it once or twice.

Pushing off the ground, I ran towards the witch. Reaching a distance where I could barely reach with a double jump, the witch was horrified and went higher. My sword barely missed the witch's ankle.

I landed on the ground with a thud and groaned softly. The weight of the armor worked against me.

'I've confirmed that the magic protecting the witch has disappeared.'

The witch raised her hand. Wind swirled around her hand. The wind was so fierce that it was visible. If I were hit by that now, I would surely die instantly.

'I have a skill prepared for this moment. Holy Light.'

A sacred light fell from the sky towards the witch.

The witch's body, struck by the sacred light, burst into flames. Since the sacred light was weak, it couldn't finish the witch off in one go.

"Kyaaaaaaaak!"

The witch screamed in pain and fell to the ground. While falling, the witch used magic to create as much distance as possible from me. It was a wise choice and a desperate struggle, but I wasn't the only one here.

"Die, witch!! This is revenge for Bill and Dora, and Julia!!"

Hans stabbed the witch with the dagger I gave him. Stab, stab, stab! The dagger moved without stopping. The witch's screams gradually diminished.

I approached Hans.

"Keuheuheuk! Bill... Dora... Julia...!"

"Step aside, Hans."

"Paladin-nim! I! I killed the witch! I finished my revenge!"

"The witch is not human. Don't be careless and step aside. We need to deal with her properly. I'll finish her with my sword... Hans!"

The witch's arm grabbed Hans' head and pulled him in. Hans' head was buried in the witch's chest. No, it was being absorbed.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

Using Instant, I leaped forward and cut off the witch's head with my holy sword. The witch's soul screamed and clung to me. My body staggered. I gritted my teeth and endured. The witch's soul was more powerful than any demon or corrupted being I had killed so far.

"Paladin-nim! My body! My body is strange!"

Hans' body was trembling. The surface of his skin was necrotizing, and his blood vessels were wriggling like earthworms. Hans' face was filled with terror. I pointed my greatsword at Hans.

"It's the witch's curse. You are being corrupted now. Probably for about 3 minutes at most."

"...I've heard that if you become corrupted, you won't recognize your family. Is that true?"

"You'll recognize them. It's not that your memories disappear. It's just that you'll regard humans as less than livestock. Corruption affects not only your body but also your mind."

"......I can feel the change. The hatred for the witch is disappearing. I can feel the love for my family disappearing. Paladin-isiyeo, please kill me. I want to die as a human... as a father."

"There's still time. Do you have any last words?"

"No. My family and friends are not in this world."

"Goddess-isiyeo. Have mercy on this poor soul."

I beheaded Hans. Hans' head rolled across the floor. The corruption from the curse was still in progress. I stomped on Hans' head and crushed it.

'It's over.'

It was the moment I was about to turn around. The witch's corpse rose with grotesque movements like a puppet.

Jjeoeok!

The witch's body split in half. The entrails, butchered by the dagger, were pieced together and transformed into the shape of a mouth.

I immediately realized what was happening. It was the work of the demon who had a contract with the witch.

"Paladin of Valentiere! That one is waiti...."

I swung the greatsword, overlaid with holy sword energy, and slashed the witch's corpse.

"Shut up. You and your master will die by my hand. Quietly await the day of judgment."

"Hehehe. Arrogant...."

Peok!

I kicked the talking corpse. The corpse collided with a tree and exploded.
