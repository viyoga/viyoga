Keul-kkeok.

Konparode swallowed hard. His whole body was tense. He didn't even get this nervous when meeting Baron Vylon, the master of this city.

The reason he's so tense is because of the woman following behind him.

,My neck is getting chilly. This woman is dangerous.'

Konparode has been a slave merchant for over 50 years. His father and grandfather were also slave merchants, and he started helping his father with this work from a young age and has continued to this day.

In the process, he developed one ability: the ability to observe people. He's confident that no one can match his ability to understand and see through people.

That ability is telling him that this woman is dangerous. The moment he screws up here, he, as well as most of the people here, will die at her hands. He had a feeling.

,It was better when her master... Eugene Prukerus-ssi was around.'

Even though he thought she was dangerous, he didn't feel like he had his head in a beast's mouth like he does now.

,The woman Eugene Prukerus-ssi brings around... She's probably his exclusive maid. Her name was Yuria, wasn't it...?'

Eugene Prukerus-ssi wasn't some mediocre noble, so he memorized that information. This place, Vylon, is quite close to the Prukerus estate, after all.

"It seems like we've been walking for quite a while... How far are you going?"

Yuria asked Konparode.

Two burly male slaves are guarding him, glaring at Yuria.

"We're almost there. This is where the female slaves are. Just so you know... the slaves here aren't just mine."

"I know that without you telling me, Konparode-ong."

This place is where Konparode is the chief manager, but other slave merchants also operate here.

The shares of this slave market are 20% for Baron Vylon, 40% for Konparode, and 40% for the remaining slave merchants.

Konparode opened a door.

Kkiririk. Cheolkeong.

The place was a series of rooms, reminiscent of a marketplace. There were also dozens of employees managing the slaves.

"This is the Vylon Slave Market. There are over 80 slave merchants doing business here, and over 1,000 slaves are sold here."

The mansion-like place they were in earlier was a place where special and unique slaves for VIPs were gathered.

"It's a place where ordinary slaves are kept, but if you look carefully, you might be able to find a special slave here."

"That's enough explanation. The slave I want is a young and pretty female slave. I don't mind if she's young. I won't discriminate based on background, but I'd prefer a virgin slave if possible."

"Hmm. That's quite a broad condition. You'll be wandering around here for a long time... Is that alright with you?"

"I don't mind."

"What's your budget?"

"10 billion Ner."

"......That's quite a lot. But the slaves here are quite expensive. I hope you understand that."

Yuria nodded.

Since it's an illegal slave market, it can't be helped that it's more expensive than a regular slave. And since they're qualitatively superior, she can understand it well enough.

"Konparode-ong! What brings you here?"

A male slave merchant approached Konparode and greeted him.

"It's been a while, Sutron. An important guest has come, so I'm personally escorting her. May I take a look at your slaves?"

The slave merchant glanced at Yuria. She's trying to hide her appearance as much as possible by wearing a cloak, but her exposed face is very beautiful.

However, the slave merchant immediately turned his gaze away. There are things you naturally realize when you work in a place like this. One of them is that unnecessary curiosity only hastens your demise.

"Of course. What kind of slave are you looking for?"

"A female slave. I'd like her to be young and pretty."

"I have a few decent slaves. Take a look."

The slave merchant pointed to a cage. Inside the cage, 12 women with handcuffs and shackles were sitting. Unlike the slaves in the mansion, they were wearing ragged clothes.

"I want to see their faces and bodies properly."

At Yuria's order, the slave merchant immediately ordered the women in the cage.

"Did you hear the guest? Take off your clothes and show your bodies! If you don't listen properly, I'll lock you up in solitary confinement."

As the slave merchant threatened them, the female slaves began to take off their clothes.

Yuria frowned slightly. The hygiene wasn't as good as she thought. Fortunately, it's not serious.

The women are all different ages, but they all seem to have gone through their coming-of-age ceremonies.

"Are they all virgins?"

"They're virgins except for 4 of them."

"......."

Yuria narrowed her eyes. You can roughly guess their personalities by looking at their faces and bodies. Their eyes and expressions say a lot.

"I'll take that slave."

The most important criterion for judgment is appearance. If her appearance is below average, she has no intention of buying her as a slave.

The slave merchant said the female slave's name.

"Riesel, is it."

Konparode, who was watching from the side, looked at Yuria as if he was surprised.

The slave Yuria chose was a girl with orange hair. Her body was so skinny that you couldn't call her beautiful. Her eyes were also blurry, and she looked somewhat lacking.

"That's unexpected. Wouldn't the woman next to her be healthier and better?"

She has a more outstanding beauty than the slave called Riesel. The flaw is that she's older, but it's only a 3-4 year difference.

"I've decided on that slave."

"Hmm. Are you looking at the future rather than the present?"

Yuria nodded.

As Konparode said, she thought about Riesel's future. If she's fed and managed properly, she'll have a more outstanding beauty than the woman next to her.

And above all, her physique was quite outstanding. Yuria, a strong person who reached the highest level of Aura Expert, could see the talent that Riesel possessed.

Even if he has excellent human observation skills, it's a field that Konparode, who is just a slave merchant, can't recognize.

"It's 120 million Ner. Haha. Riesel is a virgin, so the price is a bit higher. You understand, right?"

Yuria paid the price right away.

"Haha. Thank you! Don't you need any other slaves?"

"There are no decent slaves."

If she wanted to, she could haggle the price, but there wasn't enough time for that. She had to buy at least 10 slaves.

"Konparode-ong. Let's go to the next one right away."

Yuria took the slave certificate in her hand and walked around the market, leading the slave with handcuffs and shackles.

Most of the slave merchants treated Yuria politely. However, some of the slave merchants looked at Yuria with disrespectful eyes.

"Konparode-ong. This woman... She seems to be worth quite a bit... Can't we make her a slave?"

"Are you crazy? This person is a precious guest. You can't treat her carelessly."

"Ah. She's a precious guest. I'm sorry. Keu keu."

The rude slave merchant immediately stepped back at Konparode's shout.

Yuria frowned. She was being followed. Armed men were following her from a short distance away. They were quite skilled slave hunters. The moment she left the slave market, the slave hunters would attack right away.

"Why did you suddenly stop?"

"The vermin doesn't know its place. I don't want my master to go through unnecessary trouble, so I'll have to take care of it here."

"Yes?"

Konparode asked back, not understanding. But Yuria, who was in front of him, had already disappeared. Konparode didn't see her move at all.

Konparode panicked and looked around. Then he found them. Three slave hunters were dead with their heads cut off.

"......."

Konparode had a pale face. It wasn't because of the corpses. It's only been 10 seconds since Yuria moved. In other words, three skilled slave hunters died in just 10 seconds.

Konparode ordered his slave to stay in place and moved quickly. It was obvious where Yuria had gone.

And as he thought, Yuria was killing the rude slave merchant.

"P, please save me! Please! I gave you all the money!"

"Who would believe that a mere 300 million Ner is all you have."

"That's all I have! The rest of my assets are all slaves......"

The slave merchant's head fell to the floor as he was talking.

Konparode saw it. The black aura wrapped around her right hand was taking the shape of a blade.

,To be able to create such a skill with aura... She's at least a high-level Expert.'

He was expecting it, but seeing it in person made his spine tingle. If things went wrong, that woman would kill him too.

"Konparode-ong. The management of the slave merchants under your command is a mess."

"I, I'm sorry. I'll manage them properly from now on."

"Let's go right away since we don't have much time."

Konparode wiped the cold sweat from his forehead as he looked at her walking away.

Then he asked into the air.

"Can you kill that woman?"

A small voice was heard in his ear.

"It's impossible. That woman has noticed my existence and even my location."

"......Vylon's best assassin, and you were caught?"

He secretly called this assassin when he came here. It wasn't to harm Yuria. Konparode called him to protect himself.

"To be honest, I missed the movement when that woman killed the three slave hunters earlier. Currently, Vylon's best assassin isn't me, it's that woman."

"Aren't you at the level of a high-level Aura Expert?"

"That woman is beyond that."

"Beyond that means the highest level......"

"If I have to kill that woman, I'll just give up the request. Contract or whatever, I'll live first... Oh shit."

"Why, why is that?"

"That woman is sending me a signal. Maybe... it means I have to be in her sight. If I run away, I'll definitely die. Konparode. Where did you get that monster-like woman?"

"...I didn't bring her. She came to me."

Konparode swallowed hard. She stopped and was looking at him. It probably meant he should come quickly and guide her.
