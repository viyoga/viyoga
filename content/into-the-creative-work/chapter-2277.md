Perhaps it's thanks to the 「Military Strategy」 technology I obtained after destroying the Roman civilization. A hierarchy has formed among the tribe members. In other words, a class system has emerged.

It's broadly divided into three types.

Women. Men. Goblins.

Women were the ruling class. They train in martial arts from a young age and become soldiers when they come of age. Their main tasks are hunting and training. Of course, there are women who don't do that. They work as artisans. Others are engaged in farming.

Men were the subjugated class. Castrated from birth, they are only allowed to labor. Dirty and difficult tasks are handled by men. Among them, men labeled as useless live as impregnation machines. Goblins were livestock. The lifespan of a goblin is 30 years, but they grow quickly, reaching adulthood in three years. Adult goblins have human uteruses implanted and repeat pregnancy and childbirth until they die, producing humans.

In a way, goblins were the most important resource in the Cho-America civilization.

There are also goblin breeding farms. These were facilities for producing goblins. As many goblins are needed, they naturally have to exist.

At the goblin breeding farms, the "seeds" were managed. The seeds were chosen to ensure that goblins most conducive to pregnancy and childbirth were born. It was a form of eugenics. Thanks to this, the average lifespan of goblins seems to be increasing slightly.
