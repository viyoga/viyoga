The atmosphere was completely ruined to continue training. Wiyu and I checked that the sky was back to normal and returned home.

Milyeong was waiting at the entrance. As soon as she saw me, she made a fuss.

,Darling-nim! The sky was weird! What kind of trouble did you cause?!"

,I was trying to seize a sudden enlightenment."

,Ah, so that was enlightenment! If enlightenment comes twice, the world will be destroyed...?! Are you okay?"

,I'm fine. I'm perfectly fine."

Wiyu ignored us, went inside, and sat on the sofa. There were leftover snacks on the table.

I approached her. Milyeong followed behind me.

,Master-nim. What does it mean that the sky does not allow going against the heavens?"

I can't quite recall even when thinking about the original work. This world has changed a lot as various worlds have become mixed together.

,You know that there is Cheon Gi in this world. The sun rises during the day, the moon rises at night, and the starlight twinkles in its place, a natural order. Things that should be that way. Cheon Gi is the natural order, and going against the heavens means defying that natural order."

,Why is my enlightenment defying the natural order?"

Her tone was sharp. She felt wronged, and her voice was filled with emotion. Calling someone else's enlightenment defying the heavens. What's the standard for that? Isn't this just being unfairly critical?

,How can humans understand the will of the heavens?"

,Defying the heavens? That was a real thing?!"

Milyeong is surprised. I looked at her.

,You know about defying the heavens too?"

,When I was learning magic under my mother... I heard about it. I was told to be careful about defying the heavens. And I heard that I should move forward within the natural order. At that time, I didn't know anything about defying the heavens or the natural order, so I just brushed it off... So defying the heavens was a real thing."

,Is Cheon Gi like fate?"

,No."

,It's not."

The two shook their heads at the same time.

,Darling-nim. Unfortunately, I don't know much about Cheon Gi. Reading and interpreting Cheon Gi requires special talent and understanding, separate from magical abilities. But I know that Cheon Gi is not fate."

My gaze turned to Wiyu. Wiyu could read Cheon Gi, though only slightly. It wasn't a very helpful level, though.

,You know the concept of fate. You're asking if everything is predetermined."

,Yes. Isn't defying the heavens because I tried to go against that fate?"

,No. There is no such thing as fate. Life begins by chance, and death also ends by chance. But connections exist. Just as people are drawn to each other, there are connections where something attracts something else. In the first place, the idea that the heavens determine human fate... don't you think humans are being too arrogant?"

,Humans are arrogant, not the heavens?"

,Think from the heavens' perspective. Why would they have to grant fate to each and every human? If they had to grant fate, what would be the purpose?"

,...Certainly. If I were the heavens, granting fate to humans would be... a bothersome and uninteresting thing to do. But when people die, don't they go to the underworld? Yamra, the Great King's register has the names of people who are going to die written on it.... Doesn't that mean people's fates are predetermined?"

,You've killed people, so you should know. Have you ever seen a grim reaper?"

,No."

I've seen a messenger in the Manmu Tower. But these guys aren't the grim reapers Wiyu is talking about. Grim reapers take the dead. These guys write the names of the living in the register, kill them, and take them.

Since then, even though I've killed people, I've never seen a messenger. The grim reapers I know and the messengers here are similar but different beings.

,Even without grim reapers, this world is already flowing according to the natural order. To put it in a way that's easy for you to understand... the natural order is a kind of system. Don't ask any more than that. I don't know much either."

The conversation paused for a moment. They had their own things to think about, and I had mine.

,Rather, I want to ask you in reverse. What enlightenment did you gain that the heavens designated you as someone who defies the heavens?"

,It's hard to explain in words... I focused on my breathing and tried to become lightning. Then enlightenment came... and Master-nim killed me."

,It was for your own good. Do it like when you gained enlightenment. If it seems dangerous, I'll knock you out to stop you."

,Well, I guess."

Sssseuuuueeeup, hoo.

Sssseuuuueeeup, hoo.

Sssseuuuueeeup, hoo.

I focused on my breathing. Sparks flew every time I exhaled. But the sensation didn't intensify like before. I focused on my breathing for 10 minutes, but nothing happened. I furrowed my brow.

,Why is it doing this?"

,It was an enlightenment that came by chance. I think it's fortunate."

,Fortunate? My enlightenment disappeared!"

,You said you were trying to become lightning? At that time, you were turning into a monster, not lightning. A monster with multiple legs... a beast-like monster that's hard to describe."

,A monster..."

,The enlightenment you gained is ultimately half-baked. You were trying to become lightning? You must have only been thinking about the Lightning Sky Style. But the martial arts you're learning now aren't just the Lightning Sky Style, are they?"

,Cheonma Shingong... Are you saying that even if I had gained enlightenment, it wouldn't have turned out the way I wanted?"

,Disciple. Let go of your attachment to enlightenment. What's important is... the Lightning Sky Style and the Cheonma Shingong. You know better which one is bigger."

Of course, it's Cheonma Shingong, not Lightning Sky Style. I didn't know before, but now it's grown even bigger as I've absorbed several Cheonma Shingongs.

Lightning Sky Style and Cheonma Shingong. The Lightning Sky Style is more familiar to me. Unlike Cheonma Shingong, the Lightning Sky Style is supported as a feature of the Yuhee Life app. The Lightning Sky Style was bound to be more comfortable.

Reading Cheon Gi is not something anyone can do. It requires special talent and understanding, separate from magical abilities.

,Martial artists do not learn more than two martial arts. To be precise, it's the mind technique that's like that. The mind is originally one, so there can't be more than two minds. But there are exceptions, and you are one of those exceptions. You seem to be maintaining a good balance while learning two martial arts, but... it seems like you're reaching your limit. The Devilish Qi has become too big. If you leave it any longer, it will have a negative impact in some way."

Come to think of it, even the protagonists of martial arts novels mostly have one central mind technique, even if they've learned several martial arts.

,What should I do? I think I heard about a way to learn two mind techniques.... Should I learn that?"

,It's useless. The Wudang Sect's famous Yin-Yang Mind Technique allows you to think about two things at the same time, but the body is ultimately only one. Ah, there's also the Mind Division Technique. I heard that a master of the Demonic Sect learned it... but their ego split and they self-destructed."

,...Is there no way?"

,How did you strengthen the Cheonma Shingong?"

,By absorbing other Cheonma Shingongs... Ah! I can absorb other Lightning Sky Styles!"

Wiyu sighed and shook her head.

,According to what you said, it's different from the Cheonma Shingong that came from another world. The Lightning Sky Style is unique."

Wiyu doesn't know about the Yuhee Life app, but she knows that I can travel to other worlds. Perhaps because the Immortal Realm and the Underworld exist, she accepted it without much doubt.

,...Then how do I strengthen the Lightning Sky Style?"

,There is no perfect martial art in this world. The Lightning Sky Style is the same. Is there any answer other than training and research?"

,Both seem like they'll take a lot of time."

,Referencing other martial arts is also a method. I also know thousands of sword techniques. Take what's useful, discard what's useless, and develop it."

,Indeed. You're saying to find martial arts famous for lightning energy and refer to them. But lightning attribute martial arts aren't that common... Is there an easy way to get them? Like martial arts you know...."

,I don't know. There are many people in the Immortal Cultivation Sect who learn unusual martial arts, so look for them."

Immortal Cultivation Sect.

That's what they call sects that train for ascension rather than wealth and glory.

Most Immortal Cultivation Sects operate discreetly, unlike the Martial Arts Alliance or the Cheonma Church. They avoid getting involved in the secular world. And many of them are full of elitist consciousness.

,Those guys are basically holed up in places untouched by people... It's hard to find them without a plan.'

Something flashed through my mind.

Muyeong Shintu's notes.

Since the scale has become larger than in the original work, the treasures in the notes will also be extraordinary. There will probably be lightning attribute martial arts too.

I've set my goal this time. I'm going to get lightning attribute martial arts no matter what.

,Milyeong-ah. Are you free? Would you like to go on a journey through the Jianghu for the first time in a while?"

,Darling-nim. I don't think I can this time."

,Why?"

,The barrier was damaged by what just happened, so I have to fix it. And... after seeing the angry sky, I feel like I'm getting some kind of clue. I can't keep falling behind Darling-nim... I need to train too."

,Don't do anything dangerous."

I decided to do this alone. The Cheonma Church also had some leeway, so it was okay.

,The absolute master is going out.'

Even the famous works of Van Gogh just look like scribbles to my eyes. Well, I feel like I understand a little bit recently, though.

In the forest.

A master shines when there are jjotbaps.
