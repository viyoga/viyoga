The woman, the only flower in the bunch, woke up first. Her face was a mess from crying. Her beauty was slightly above average.

The woman scanned her surroundings with bleary eyes and immediately hardened her expression.

,"...Who are you guys?"

She asked in clumsy Korean. It wasn't so much that she recognized I was Korean, but rather that this place was Korea.

"I'm the one asking the questions."

I kindly spoke to her in Chinese. In a fleeting moment, the woman's eyes wavered. It was less than a second. I wouldn't have noticed if I hadn't been focusing on her eyes.

"What's your purpose for coming here?"

"......"

"Not answering?"

"......"

I grabbed the woman's shoulder, which was bound by shadow chains.

Pajijik. Lightning coursed through the woman's body. The woman gritted her teeth. The pain of being electrocuted must have been unbearable, but she had a decent amount of mental fortitude. Or maybe she was trained to endure torture.

,'It would be easier to just ask Elena to mess with her mind.'

But the desire to test the lie detector was stronger.

,'I have plenty of time. I can just torture her until she talks.'

I was thinking about how to use the Authority of Truth when a large amount of mana drained away, and a notification window popped up.

『You have discovered the target's name with the Authority of Truth.』

『The target's name is Peng Liao.』

『You can use Karma: Good (3) to see Peng Liao's true life.』

Use Karma: Good? And three of them?

The memory of struggling to raise Karma: Good flashed through my mind.

,'Using Karma: Good is unexpected, but... considering the future, I need to know exactly what kind of ability it is.'

It was better to try it out now.

,'But is Peng Liao her name?'

Finding out the name didn't require Karma. Instead, it consumed a large amount of mana. About 30% of my mana disappeared. Mana can be replenished over time, but losing 30% at once is a burden. It's fatal in combat situations.

,'So I need to know about the ability in advance. I'll use Karma.'

『Using Karma: Good (3).』

Peng Liao's life flooded into my mind.

It wasn't directly experiencing Peng Liao's life. It was more like watching her life on TV. I skimmed through 35 years of her life in less than a second. It wasn't even close to being a vicarious experience. It was just watching Peng Liao's life without any meaning.

"A bitch who almost got killed by her own father. A bitch who stabbed her teacher in the back. A bitch who put a bullet in her friend's head."

Peng Liao is horrified. She moved her legs while restrained, as if trying to get away from me.

"W-What did you do? How... how do you know me?!"

"Peng Liao. Answer my question. Has your Guirak Valley started targeting Korea in earnest?"

I asked even though I knew the truth. Because I needed confirmation.

"......"

There was no answer. I raised my hand. Blue sparks flew between my fingers like a stun gun.

Peng Liao gritted her teeth and averted her gaze several times before finally opening her mouth.

"I don't know what plans the Valley has for Korea. I just follow orders from above."

『The target's statement is true.』

A large amount of mana drained away. 20% of the total. The mana consumed was less than when I found out her name, but it was by no means negligible.

,'I can't overuse it.'

I could overuse it right now. Because Elena and Yuria are by my side. I can push myself because I have those trustworthy women. Conversely, it was difficult to push myself when they weren't around.

"Tell a simple lie. For example, say I'm a man?"

Peng Liao looked at me with an incomprehensible expression.

"...I'm a man...?"

『The target's statement is false.』

Even though it was an obvious lie, 20% of my mana drained away. In an instant, 70% of my mana disappeared, leaving only 30%. Losing more than half of my mana in a short amount of time was painful, even for me.

I endured the pain. Because I couldn't stumble around pathetically.

I confirmed the ability of the Authority of Truth. Among them, discerning the truth is an ability that I can use enough when I have mana potions and am feeling generous. That alone was enough.

,'If I use Karma: Good, I can know the target's entire life. This will also be a useful ability depending on the situation.'

When the time comes to use it, I shouldn't hesitate.

After that, I asked Peng Liao questions. I asked questions I already knew and received answers I already knew. I killed Peng Liao cleanly. There was nothing more to find out. On the other hand, it wasn't the same for the two men. There was no reason to waste Karma: Good on such trivial matters.

I'll leave it to Elena.

They were already mentally weakened because of this dungeon, and in less than three minutes, they spilled all the information they knew and committed suicide.

"The existence called Guimo is the problem. If she really has precognitive abilities... she will continue to interfere with you in the future."

"Although it's limited, she has successfully foreseen the future. She may interfere with Master-nim even more in the future. I think we should deal with her right away."

Elena and Yuria were wary of Guimo.

Guimo, one of the Four Great Heads of Guirak Valley. In terms of power alone, she's close to being the second-in-command of Guirak Valley. Her precognitive ability is that outstanding and useful.

,'But Guimo's ability in the original story wasn't this great. And she's supposed to be assassinated even if I don't do anything, right?'

I don't even know who the assassin is. Because it didn't appear in the original story. People speculate that it's one of the other Four Great Heads.

,'The future may have changed because of me.'

I couldn't tell what the cause was. I've caused so many incidents. When you think about it, the incident in Japan shouldn't have blown up like that either.

After careful consideration, I asked Elena.

"Can you bring Guimo here with your ability?"

"I could if I die once."

"Go ahead and die once."

"Ha."

Elena looked at me with a bitter smile. She didn't even refuse. As if she wanted something in return.

There's no such thing as a free lunch. Just as I was about to say that I would grant her request, Yuria interjected.

"Master-nim. There's no need to ask such a materialistic woman like Elena. I will kidnap Guimo."

"I don't want you using Limit Break."

"I'm confident even without using Limit Break. If you give me ample preparation time... and just three days, I will kidnap Guimo."

"If I help you, I can shorten the time. It might not be a bad idea to throw a bomb into Guirak Valley and blow it up. They'll be stuck on repairs for at least two months."

Elena hurriedly interjected.

"Wait a minute. When did I say I wouldn't do it? I was just thinking about it for a moment. Don't go around making me out to be a materialistic woman!"

"Isn't Elena-ssi a materialistic woman? You always consider the benefits in everything you do.... I don't think I've misjudged you."

"You've misjudged me. I only consider the benefits when I need to. This time, it's Eugene's request, so of course I have to grant it. Guimo, was it? I'll bring her here in ten minutes."

Elena confidently walked out of the dungeon.

I briefly looked around. This wasn't a typical dungeon. The fact that the dungeon didn't disappear even after the owner died was proof of that.

,'A space created to seal an ancient demon. The space has been distorted by prolonged exposure to the demon's magic. Even in the original story, it says that this is the perfect space to practice black magic or summon demons.'

I was planning to summon a legion commander-level demon using the corrupted space inside here. But before that, I need to throw out bait to send a signal to the legion commander I want.

I came outside. Elena was gone. Tera, the highest-ranking water spirit, approached and returned the mana cube.

"You're here? I don't have to do that anymore?"

"Yeah. I'll call you again if I need you later, so go back now."

"Yay!"

Tera was reverse-summoned. The old temple sank back under the lake and disappeared. Yuria and I waited for Elena, chatting idly about what to have for lunch tomorrow.

Elena appeared before us in 15 minutes. She was holding a hunched-over old woman in her right hand. Elena threw Guimo to the ground as if she was a nuisance.

"This is Guimo. I was a little nervous because they said she had precognitive abilities... but she's surprisingly nothing special. It was almost boring."

Guimo knelt on the ground and raised her upper body to look at me.

"......So. The center of fate was here."

"You can foresee the future, but you couldn't foresee your own future?"

"From the moment you foresee the future, there is no perfect future...."

"Try to foresee the future once. If it's an interesting edge, I might spare you."

Guimo blankly stared at me. Then she raised her head and looked at the night sky. Guimo lowered her head again and chuckled. An eerie energy spread out. Ghosts gathered around her.

I swung Hwaryeon Bido. A flash of red lightning killed the ghosts that were crawling out.

"Did you think you could do anything to us with that kind of thing? Your own martial prowess is truly pathetic."

"Kikik. Nothing. I can't see anything. It's dark. Darker than the night sky. Since I can't see the future, is this truly a blessing? Or is it a curse? Killing me won't change much. My existence is insignificant."

"Killing you or not doesn't change anything? If I kill you, at least you won't bother me. Let's extract information first before that."

I winked at Elena. I wanted her to manipulate Guimo's mind and extract information like she did earlier. Elena shrugged her shoulders with a tired expression.

"I tried using a light illusion before coming here, but it didn't work at all."

"What?"

Elena raised her hand. She pointed to her head with her index finger and spun it around.

"She's already gone mad. The cause is possession. Countless ghosts have been gnawing at and manipulating her body and mind. Her precognitive ability is close to being a byproduct of that. That woman is a medium. She's completely insane now and doesn't even know her own name."

"Kikik. What is there to fear when the universe protects us? Good deeds. Good deeds will be the key to everything. Oh, God. Have mercy on us!"

Guimo laughed like a madwoman. I frowned and used the Authority of Truth. I'll probably have to deal with Guirak Valley in the future. So, information about Guimo, who can be said to be the top executive of Guirak Valley, will be a great help.

『Using Karma: Good (3) to confirm the target's true life.』

Guimo's life was revealed.

A child who saw ghosts was kidnapped by a bad Taoist and became a medium. After that, she lived a life of being used. By her colleagues, her friends, her lovers, and Guirak Valley.

Even though she gained great power as Guimo, there was nothing she could do. Because her mind wasn't sound at the time. She was possessed by a powerful ghost and used like a doll.

,'She lived a shitty life. But Guimo is Guimo. I obtained the secrets and information of Guirak Valley.'

Seogeok!

I killed Guimo.

"Let's put Guimo's body in America."

"America, huh? Is it to sow discord?"

"China and America don't get along in the first place. It might work. If it doesn't, oh well."

Kkiaaaaaaaak!

A ghostly wail echoed.
