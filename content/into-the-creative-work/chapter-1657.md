Jiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiing.

The sky, turned pitch black, splits in half, and the mandala of hell spreads, covering the entire sky.

The sky shone brilliantly. More dazzling than any jewel, each and every pattern signified hell and demons. The mandala of hell seemed to stretch on infinitely, as if it covered the entire sky.

-Euaaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!

-What the hell are you doing!

-It's horrifying! Horrifying!

-Just obliterate us instead!

The 700,190 souls I offered to the King of Macheon are sucked into the sky. These souls will be reduced to mere toys of the devil in that hell, suffering before being absorbed and annihilated.

,I can feel what each and every pattern of the mandala signifies.'

I know it, even though I don't possess the knowledge.

This knowledge isn't mine. It originates from the King of Macheon, who is descending upon my body.

Right now, the King of Macheon was descending upon my body. From the tips of my toes to the ends of my hair. The feeling of having someone seize control of everything that is me was, to put it mildly, not good.

The only fortunate thing was that I knew this sensation wouldn't last long.

,At most, a few minutes. I can endure that much.'

Apart from the unpleasantness, it was rare for a god to directly descend upon a body, so I focused my mind. There might be something to gain.

The tip of my finger twitched.

I wasn't the one moving it.

My hand gradually rose, and as it did, the mandala of hell covering the sky reacted. When my hand was completely raised, the mandala of hell flew towards me. What had covered the sky was compressed into a small form and enveloped my body.

I felt the King of Macheon fully settle within me.

"Hmm? You haven't lost consciousness? Impressive."

I said. Or rather, the King of Macheon was speaking with my mouth and voice.

"Watch closely. It will be a good experience for you too."

With every breath the King of Macheon took, demonic energy filled the air. It was endless, making it feel distant.

「Meaningless death stares at you.」

"Tathagata-jñāna-ketu-dhāraṇīśvara-rāja-nim, is that human worth moving your precious feet for? That human is nothing more than a loach stirring up chaos in the world. It would be right to dispose of him here."

Osiris's voice echoed lowly. This world was upholding his words. This was Osiris's hell.

The King of Macheon lowered his gaze and looked at Osiris, standing on the gray desert. I felt overwhelmed for a moment. The dignity of a god seen through the eyes of a god was beyond imagination. If I were an ordinary human, I would have gone insane just from seeing a fraction of that essence.

"That's what makes this human interesting."

"Are you setting aside the safety of the world to pursue shallow pleasures? Disappointing, Tathagata-jñāna-ketu-dhāraṇīśvara-rāja-nim."

"What's disappointing is me. If you're going to call yourself a god of hell, act more like one. The safety of the dead? The order of the underworld? All nonsense! What kind of god of hell works for the dead?"

"How far are you going to disappoint me, Tathagata-jñāna-ketu-dhāraṇīśvara-rāja-nim! The cycle of life and death is the foundation that constitutes this world! If you are truly a god of hell, you must respect and protect this natural order!"

"Wrong. A god should not respect life and death, but dominate them."

Osiris frowned. The world shook in response.

The King of Macheon laughed as he looked at the hell that was hostile to him. As if he was going crazy with how much he was enjoying this situation. In fact, he was thoroughly enjoying it right now. Since this body was mine, I could feel his emotions directly.

"For ten million years, the world has merged and expanded. Countless gods have appeared. Among them, there have been many who called themselves the rulers of hell. Osiris, once the ruler of the earth. Prove to me that you are qualified to rule hell."

"You're crossing the line. Very well. I shall punish the famous originator of demons with my own hands."

Osiris's power spread like waves in all directions. Gray sandstorms scattered, and hundreds of thousands of mummies screamed and reached out their arms towards me.

The rotten arms wrapped in old bandages stretched out endlessly, trying to grab my clothes.

"You want to play with toys? I don't mind joining in."

The King of Macheon chuckled lowly and waved his hand. The mandala wrapped around his body shone, and otherworldly demons poured out. The demons with hideous appearances rushed at the mummies on the ground and began to fight.

It was too vulgar to be called a battle of gods.

Leaving aside the King of Macheon, who was enjoying himself, Osiris was just standing still, staring intently at the King of Macheon for some reason.

"You seem to have a question."

The King of Macheon said.

"Two gods have descended upon Atlantis. This is not normally allowed."

"Ah, I played around with the system a bit when descending. They won't be able to interfere for a while. Well, I can't do anything about the spectators, though."

The King of Macheon raised his head.

I could feel countless gazes. Countless.

"The price for that will be considerable. Are you saying you're paying that price just for that human? I cannot understand you."

"This human is interesting. Perhaps a human who will never appear again. And the calculation of karma is already complete. Given the system's tendencies so far, it will end with a reprimand."

"I thought you were acting impulsively... So this was also part of your plan? I truly cannot understand. Isn't this like setting yourself on fire for a moment's pleasure? Is becoming a moth your hobby?"

"Haha. You're saying something similar to Yama. The gods of the underworld are all alike. You boring thing, pleasure is the ultimate of desire."

"I cannot understand. Nor do I want to understand."

Wind blew from Osiris's feet.

It wasn't an ordinary wind. I could tell with the King of Macheon's gaze and senses. I could feel another divine presence in that wind. Two divine presences were mixed and became one. I easily inferred the identity of the other divine presence.

,It's Set's.'

Osiris had absorbed Set. Even if Set was just a vestige, a god is still a god.

The wind blew.

That wind, embodying death, swept through the demons and mummies. It had no effect on the mummies, who were already dead, but it was different for the demons. As soon as they touched the wind, they died and fell onto the sandy ground. The demons' corpses quietly seeped into the gray desert.

The King of Macheon didn't care about the demons at all. To him, the demons were just tiresome toys. Nothing more, nothing less.

The wind of death also reached the King of Macheon. But it couldn't penetrate the mandala that the King of Macheon wore.

The King of Macheon's gaze slowly swept across the gray desert. The bodies of the mummies touched by that gaze burst into flames. Black flames burned the mummies, leaving no ashes behind. The dark flames spread rapidly. It didn't take long for the black flames to cover the entire desert.

However, there was a place where the black flames couldn't reach. It was around Osiris. As for his surroundings in the burning desert, it was quiet. No, empty would be the right word.

The King of Macheon smirked.

"Empty death. That is your essence. Because there is nothing, it is light. Is that why you can covet life as death? Interesting."

"I wonder how long you'll find it interesting."

Osiris said, spitting out the words.

Immediately after, the gray sand around him soared with the wind. The sand swept around, extinguishing the black flames and shaping something.

A giant with the head of a beast.

It's Set.

Osiris resurrected the dead Set.

Set, who had become Osiris's incarnation, reached out to me. No, to the King of Macheon. What was absurd was that the King of Macheon was just watching.

I was the one who was getting anxious.

,King of Macheon! What are you doing! Move!'

The King of Macheon blinked.

"Right. You were here. It's been so long since I've stepped up like this that I briefly forgot about your existence."

,That bastard...!'

"I was wondering how to deal with him, but matching your style might not be bad."

The King of Macheon bent his finger joints and raised his nails. The light of the mandala swirled at the tips of his nails.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Bijou (Demonic Flying Claw).

He swung his nails at Set. Not only did he tear apart Set's massive body, but he also left long scars on the desert.

,...Is that Cheonma Shingong?'

The scale was completely different.

It was hard to believe, but that Cheonma Bijou just now was pure Cheonma Shingong without any divine power. I could tell because the King of Macheon was using my body.

The torn Set returned to its original form. Because that body was made of sand. Set raised his fist and swung it. The sky and the ground vibrated from the aftermath.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Gunrimbo (Demonic Monarch's Descent Step).

Suddenly, Set's fist appeared to be moving slowly.

,Did I unknowingly use a fleeting moment?'

No. The feeling was different from using a fleeting moment.

The King of Macheon's knowledge flowed into me. The answer was surprisingly simple. The Cheonma Gunrimbo that the King of Macheon used controlled not only space but also time. In other words, it wasn't that I had become faster, but that the world had become slower.

,This is insane.'

The King of Macheon's Cheonma Shingong didn't end there. He stretched out his index finger and spun it clockwise.

Cheonma Shingong, Hoecheon Mapung (Returning Heaven Demonic Wind).

The world rotated.

The sky and the desert tilted, and sand flowed down. Set somehow maintained his balance and continued his attack.

But his fist never reached me. Because the power that rotated the world gathered on Set and swept him away.

"...Oops. Did I play too much?"

The King of Macheon said.

It didn't take long to understand the meaning of those words.

Osiris commanded death.

The world accepted the command and began to collapse. The problem was that the King of Macheon was being taken as a companion on the path to death.

My body fell.

At the end of the fall, the death of the world awaited. No matter how powerful the King of Macheon was, he wouldn't be safe in the face of that death. That's what I thought.

Splash!

I fell into the sea of death along with the world.
