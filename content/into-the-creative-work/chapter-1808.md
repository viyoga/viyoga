,Four is already a good price! It's a White Night Herb with spiritual energy, but originally, the White Night Herb is a Medicinal herb used for detoxification and the sick! I can't give you any more than that!"

The merchant shouted as if he was screaming. The desperate cry drew attention to this side. I could feel the ronin affiliated with the Yuun Commerce Guild stealthily gathering here.

Most of them were just Martial artist who had reached the Emerging Earth Realm, but there were many of them. I calmed down my energy.

,I'll do the deal later. I should look around the market first."

,There won't be a merchant who will trade that White Night Herb for more than four celestial jades!"

,Shut up. I'll look around and come back, so wait."

I hid my greed and looked around the stalls. If I saw anything that caught my eye, I asked the merchant.

,What are these gloves?"

,They're gloves called Poison Life. They're a magical artifact that turns the wearer's qi into poison qi."

,A magical artifact that changes the attribute of qi. That's a valuable item. How much is it?"

,Sixty celestial jades. It's cheap. You can't find anything like this at this price anywhere else!"

I quietly calculated.

,The price of a decent elixir is 15 celestial jades... 60 jades is the price of 4 elixirs. It's really cheap.'

Wouldn't it be a loss if I didn't buy this? What if someone else buys it before I do?

The Poison Life magical artifact is too cheap. But why hasn't anyone bought it? Judging by the crowd of people around, there must have been dozens of customers who came before me.

,Don't be fooled by the price.'

,There are some minor side effects... but you can use detoxification medicine. It will be a great help when training in poison arts."

That's right. It seemed quite useful as a hidden card. You have to do anything when you're in danger. Besides, I had Complete Recovery. But this glove wasn't such a great item that I would risk death for it.

,I don't need it."

The Meteor Sword, which has now become my skill. I didn't see any magical artifacts at the level of the original magical artifact, Meteor Sword Heaven.
