The Emperor of the Eustia Empire sat in his office, reviewing a report.

It was a report concerning the Imperial Academy. The Emperor had ordered a secret agent to investigate whether there was external involvement in this incident, as there were several suspicious points.

In conclusion, there was external involvement. It was discovered that a certain entity had been sponsoring and encouraging the Hanahoe with lacquer and food.

Unfortunately, they were unable to identify the entity. It is presumed that the person quietly slipped away on the first day of the protest. Tracing the source of the lacquer also failed, as it was not lacquer that had been smuggled out of the Kingdom of Entertainment. In the first place, items from that place cannot be easily smuggled to other districts.

,And Starway disappeared that day. If the students aren't the culprits, then that person must be. Was their goal the Archmage Haren's bracelet, which the Imperial Academy was storing?'

Starway's abilities were nothing special. Their rank was only B.

They could learn Archmage Haren's magic arrow? The magic arrow itself wasn't a powerful spell. It was a powerful spell because Archmage Haren used it.

,Even though the thief who stole Starway is annoying... I don't have the time to chase after a thief. And this is the Imperial Academy's business.'

What's most troublesome right now is the volcanic island. Ash from the volcanic island is scattering. In three years. No, now it's roughly two years until the Apocalypse Dragon awakens if they don't completely conquer the volcanic island area.

,Then the Empire, this world, will be destroyed.'

Compared to this, the Imperial Academy incident is just a trivial happening.

,The stupidity of the students being swayed by a single rumor is quite disappointing. They're supposed to be children of nobles and educated talents, yet they're in this state.'

The Emperor cut off funding to the Imperial Academy. As the Empire's only academy collapsed, other smaller academies sprang up like mushrooms. The Emperor didn't bother to restrict them.

,Nurturing talent is crucial for the Empire's future. But what's urgent now isn't nurturing talent. They need to avoid the imminent destruction right in front of them.'

This world has become Atlantis, a playground for the gods. There is no peace. Problems will arise for the sake of the gods' amusement.

The appearance of the volcanic island is just something that was bound to happen.

,It's regrettable that only a very few, including I, are aware of this serious problem and preparing for it.'

Especially the Duke of Illusion.

Even though the volcanic island is spreading curses, the Duke of Illusion is only focused on his wedding.

,If the Duke of Illusion actively cooperated, things would be several times easier...'

The relationship between the Emperor and the Duke of Illusion has already grown distant. The Duke of Illusion, who used to be loyal to the Empire, is no longer there. He might pretend to obey imperial orders, but... it's questionable whether he will properly carry out the orders.

Even if the Emperor doesn't like the Duke of Illusion, he can't suppress him with authority.

,...They'll have to do it step by step. First, they'll improve their relationship with the Duke of Illusion on this occasion.'

The Emperor knew very well that there are no eternal enemies or eternal friends.

They don't need to be friends with the Duke of Illusion. The Emperor knows that it's impossible right now. It's enough to just have an alliance where they can receive help in times of emergency.

,If only the Crown Prince and the princes didn't do stupid things... tsk.'

The Emperor, who had been lost in thought for a moment, took out a stone tablet from his pocket.

The greatest treasure of the Eustia Imperial family.

It was a tablet of prophecy that even the Crown Prince and the imperial family didn't know existed.

「Tablet of Prophecy

Predicts future events related to the tablet's owner once a month.

Rank: SSS」

It was now given the rank of SSS due to the influence of the Atlantis system, but this tablet was a treasure that had been with the Eustia Empire since its beginning.

,The Tablet of Prophecy also predicted that this world would become the setting for Atlantis. But I couldn't stop that prophecy.'

It couldn't be helped.

The original Earth on which the Eustia Empire existed died and was destroyed. Before being destroyed together, they accepted the system and the gods' proposal and became the stage for the 8th Atlantis.

This was a reprieve, an opportunity.

If they conquer all the districts of Atlantis, the Eustia Empire, this world, can survive even after Atlantis ends.

Conversely, if they fail to conquer it.

If the system determines that they cannot conquer it, the 8th Atlantis will end there, and the world will be destroyed.

What rests on the Emperor's shoulders is not just the fate of the Empire.

The Emperor looked at the tablet. The existing prophecy written on the tablet was (Crown Prince Delsar Eustia will die without inheriting the throne). The Crown Prince is the Emperor's child and is related to the Emperor, so he appeared on the Tablet of Prophecy.

The prophecies on the tablet don't always come true. Prophecies can be changed. In fact, the Emperor has changed several prophecies. Although there are quite a few prophecies that he couldn't change.

Changing this prophecy is also easy. He will die without inheriting the throne? Then, before the Crown Prince dies, he can just pass on the throne to him tomorrow. Then the prophecy itself will be destroyed.

Of course, the Emperor had no intention of doing that. The Crown Prince was lacking in many ways to become the Emperor. He couldn't pass on the throne during such an important time as now.

,Now, show me a new prophecy.'

The Emperor carefully stroked the surface of the tablet.

The tablet glows.

Letters are assembled in the light, and a new prophecy is about to appear. The Emperor opened his eyes wide and read the prophecy. (The Emperor will realize the truth and scream in despair.)

Jijik. Chijijijik.

Black, spark-like noise flickered on the surface of the tablet. The completed prophecy letters change into other letters.

,This has never happened before. The prophecy has started to change? Did something intervene? Could it be the system...'

The Emperor, who had been panicking, was soon shocked.

(The Emperor will commit suicide.)

"......"

He almost dropped the tablet he was holding. The Emperor, frozen, read the words on the tablet over and over again, gritting his teeth. Veins popped out on the back of his hands holding the tablet.

"I, commit suicide in the end? I, of all people...?!"

The Emperor hated suicide.

To him, suicide was an escape. It was an act of not taking responsibility.

The Emperor imagined his end. Passing the throne to the Crown Prince and having a peaceful death, dying suddenly due to deteriorating health, being beheaded by a traitor. He had imagined all sorts of deaths, but suicide was not among them.

"...I will not commit suicide. Tablet of Prophecy, this prophecy will never come true."
