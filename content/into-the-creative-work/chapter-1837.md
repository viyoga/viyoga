Dong Sucheon, who had come outside the main building, trembled. Humiliation, anger, and hatred surged within him. And on the other hand, fear began to bloom.

"Gaju-nim."

"How did the plan go? Did you succeed?"

"That bastard told me to bring him my son and brother's heads within an hour."

"...He found out about the plan. Do you know which part he discovered?"

"I may have acted a bit hastily, but you know how stupid that bastard is. The plan actually worked. I felt the agitation. But after looking into some strange object, the atmosphere completely changed."

"It seems to be a special magical artifact. What about that side? Didn't you say that fox-like woman, Milyeong, was more troublesome?"

"From the first day, I naturally and gradually leaked information to the maids who were attached to that woman, but...."

It wasn't difficult to guess what had happened. That woman was probably dead.

"......"

"Gaju-nim. As you can see from their actions so far, they have no intention of ruling the Dong family for long. They will soon get everything they want and leave the Dong family. And they will annihilate everyone in the family to prevent any future troubles."

"...Just the order that bastard gave me proves it. You're right."

"Please make a decision."

"I have no choice but to make a decision. I have no intention of killing my brothers and sons. How far along is the preparation?"

"It's not perfect. But the sects and assassins of Haeosi have agreed to cooperate."

"What about the commoners?"

"According to the shaman Tangjeong, 700,000 can be mobilized in half a day."

"700,000? That's more than three times what I expected. It's more than enough."

"The problem is after the shamanism is lifted. Tangjeong said that the memories of being under the shamanism won't disappear. The quick-witted ones will realize they were under a spell, and word could get to the government. If that happens...."

The Dong family is finished. The Gaju-nim also knew it. It's the same even if the number of mobilized commoners is reduced to less than half.

"I'm prepared. If I die whether I stay still or struggle... I'll kill those bastards and die."

"I feel the same way as Gaju-nim. I will order Tangjeong to start the work."

Dong Suo quietly disappeared.

Left alone, Dong Sucheon glared at the main building where Seong Yujin was, gritting his teeth.

The bastards who suddenly appeared in the family were a disaster. An irresistible disaster. He even blamed the sky meaninglessly. Why give me such a disaster? But now he only had one thought.

'I will definitely kill you...!'

The Jianghu is a world where the law of the jungle prevails. But sometimes, the weak devour the strong in the Jianghu.

Seong Yujin and Milyeong, who had dominated the Dong family with force, lived as they pleased.

"Bring me beauties."

Seong Yujin said. There was no one here who dared to defy his will. Seong Yujin ruthlessly used his power if he felt that anyone had disobeyed his words even slightly. There were no warnings or anything. The effect was excellent. They trembled at his ruthlessness and granted most of what Seong Yujin wanted.

He summoned not only beautiful courtesans but also famous beauties from the city. It didn't matter if they had husbands or not. He just wanted every beauty he could get.

If a beauty was the mistress of a sect? He coveted her even if it meant destroying the sect.

Seong Yujin didn't think about the consequences at all. After all, he would never come to this city again, and thanks to Milyeong's magic, no one in this city would remember his and Milyeong's faces.

"I heard there's a big-breasted beauty in a village near the coast? Bring her!"

Of course, his demands weren't limited to beauties.

"Bring me Heavenly Jade. If you don't bring me Heavenly Jade, I will kill you one by one."

Heavenly Jade (天玉). A large amount of Heavenly Jade was required to increase the VIP level of the Cheongangseong System. Raising the VIP level would enhance martial arts abilities and improve talents, so he needed to acquire Heavenly Jade.

However, Heavenly Jade was not easy to obtain. It was worth 100 gold coins, so even the families in large cities only had 20 to 30 pieces.

'Before leaving, I should visit other sects and steal all their Heavenly Jade. Every hidden vault they have.'

Speaking of vaults, one thing he had forgotten came to mind.

'Muyeong Shintu's vault. There was a bounty of 20,000 gold coins on Muyeong Shintu's head alone... the vault must be filled with treasures, right?'

He had to get all those treasures. But right now, he didn't have the time to find Muyeong Shintu's vault. He was just focusing on enjoying his short vacation.

"Bring me the next woman!"

Unlike Seong Yujin, who was looking for beauties, Milyeong was the embodiment of greed. She had been suppressed in Nakwolsan all this time. Living in Nakwolsan was okay. She had to be mindful of Wiyu's eyes, but she also learned from Wiyu. But she was still tired of living in the mountains. She even missed her boring hometown. The only reason she could endure without complaint was thanks to the modern things that Seong Yujin brought. Food, comics, novels, movies, dramas, etc. She could endure because there were things to enjoy.

Of course, she didn't just play around. She felt a sense of crisis due to Seong Yujin's terrifying growth rate, so she had greatly increased her training time these days. That's why she was under a lot of stress.

"I don't like the clothes I'm wearing. Bring me new clothes."

If there was a maid who talked back, she would be burned alive, so the maids didn't dare to disobey her words and brought her clothes. Milyeong received the fancy and high-quality clothes and changed dozens of times a day, standing in front of the mirror.

"Ah, you are beautiful."

"That person will also be amazed by Milyeong-nim's beauty."

"You are the most beautiful woman I have ever seen in my life, Milyeong-nim."

The maids desperately flattered her. It was unavoidable that they knew Milyeong's name. Seong Yujin openly called her name. Milyeong didn't care much. She was going to kill them all before leaving anyway. That's why she hired ugly women as maids. Seong Yujin didn't care if ugly women died or not.

"My husband wouldn't care about these clothes, would he?"

She knew Seong Yujin's taste. He liked clothes that showed off the body line and had some exposure, rather than elegant and fancy clothes. Of course, the appearance had to be outstanding before that.

"We, we are sorry."

"We were presumptuous!"

"P, please save us!"

The maids immediately knelt down and bowed their heads.

"Now, forget about the clothes and bring me jewelry and accessories."

Milyeong, sitting with her legs crossed on a fancy chair, checked the nails on her right hand. Her fingers were well-suited to the word "slender and jade-like hands." Milyeong's nails were clean and beautiful thanks to daily care.

'I want to go to a nail shop.'

She glanced at the maids trembling as they watched her.

'I can't entrust my nails to non-professionals.'

Milyeong knew the reason. As she came into contact with modern culture, her aesthetic standards were adjusted to modern standards.

'But these jewels can help with magic training.'

Energy was hidden in the sparkling jewels. It was a very weak level compared to Heavenly Jade, but if the value of jewels was used well, they could be used as materials for excellent magic or shamanism. And when she was short on money, she could dispose of them appropriately.

"Bring me Medicinal herbs from the nearby pharmacy. Any Medicinal herb is fine, just bring them all. Ah, if there are any byproducts of monsters, be sure to bring them."

Unlike humans, monsters are special from their bodies. Therefore, they are also used as materials for magic or shamanism.

Milyeong said with a smile. The two maids who were pointed out froze like stones, not knowing what to do. Soon, the smile disappeared from Milyeong's face.

"...Are my words not understandable?"

"A, no!"

Milyeong. My name is Sang Gisu, but I am also called Haeyang (海陽). Today, I have come to present Heavenly Jade to the beautiful Milyeong.
