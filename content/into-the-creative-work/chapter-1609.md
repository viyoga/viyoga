Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The ground shook slightly with an explosion that was hard to ignore.

I quickly turned my gaze to the source of the sound. In front of the castle gate. It was where the golems were fighting. The battle between the golems had reached its conclusion.

The victor was the enemy's golem. The golem that Pesers had proudly controlled himself was smashed and collapsed. The only positive thing was that its wreckage was blocking the entrance to the castle gate, and the magic alliance's golem wasn't in perfect condition either.

,The magic alliance's golem has half of its body destroyed.'

The magic alliance's golem, which had won the battle, was slumped over like a doll with its strings cut. It wasn't moving even though it was right in front of the castle gate.

,It doesn't have the leeway to attack the castle gate.'

,The easiest way among them is... to target me.'

I could feel a massive flow of mana from the sky. I raised my head. A red magic circle was being drawn in the sky as if someone was doodling.

Flames erupted in the center of the magic circle, which was completed in an instant. A large meteor was slowly being summoned from there.

,He's decided to kill me even if it means taking damage.'

The size of the meteor was small. However, it was more than large enough to devastate the area where I was.

Both the enemy and ally forces were buzzing. It seemed like they had already succeeded in changing the atmosphere of the battlefield.

,There's no way to deal with that. It's best to avoid it.'

Avoiding it wasn't difficult. I could use a space-time warp scroll. I had given space-time warp scrolls to the Golden Rose Knights as well, just in case, so they would avoid a dog's death by meteor.

,Heh heh. Their trump card has become useless.'

I can't miss this opportunity.

,What the hell is that bastard doing there?'
