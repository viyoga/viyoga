A few days passed, and Seong Jigone-nim appeared before me. Salwang was beside him.

She was a bit different from the Salwang I remembered. She was definitely a venomous old hag, but the old hag over there now had mismatched makeup on and was giggling.

Seeing her clinging to Seong Jigone-nim, it seemed she had definitely been captured.

,Yujin-ah. Ha Mae has something to say."

Seong Jigone-nim spoke calmly as always. Ha Mae? Leaving Salwang's name aside, wasn't 'Mae' (妹) a term for a younger woman? If he had to use a term, 'Jeo' (姐) would be more appropriate.

I suppressed the curiosity that momentarily tried to burst out. There was no need to provoke them and listen to their story.

I turned my gaze to look at Salwang. Salwang was grinning and looking at me.

,I am grateful to you. If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have met Gaga, and I wouldn't have realized my destiny. Thanks to you becoming my Ojakgyo Bridge, I found true love."

What the hell is she talking about? This old hag.

I barely held back the words that were about to burst out. Even if she was an annoying old hag, she was Seong Jigone-nim's woman. That alone was enough reason to respect her.

,...It's fortunate that you found your destiny, even late in life. Well, congratulations."

,Hehe. I'll tell you who the client is, as a special favor. The client is Sanwang (Mountain King). Sanwang is after you. That bastard told me to kill you before he arrives, or at least cripple you."

,Sanwang had a reputation for being magnanimous... but he's a dirty bastard."

,He's a sly and treacherous bastard with a dark heart. But his skills are the real deal. He's coming, so you better be careful."

,The previous Sanwang died by my hand. Just because he's the current Sanwang doesn't change anything. More important is the Namgung Clan. What's their relationship?"

,Aren't you guessing already? I have no relationship with the Namgung Clan. I joined hands with Namgung Tak, the already dead Cheongun Head. I happened to find Namgung Tak on the street before coming to this city. Namgung Tak has disliked you from the start. I often heard him badmouthing you with his colleagues. After looking into it, I found out that Namgung Tak was in love with your wife. I thought I could use this, so I secretly met with Namgung Tak and agreed to join forces only for this matter."

Namgung Tak was acting alone, without any connection to the Namgung Clan. After hearing the situation, I nodded roughly.

It was what I had expected. If the Namgung Clan was going to kill me, there was no reason to send Namgung Tak to ambush me. It would be more certain to quietly take me to the Namgung Clan and kill me there. Cowardly? The Righteous Sects would rather rejoice that they killed the future Demonic Master.

,How did you get in, avoiding the Martial Alliance's Heavenly Net?"

,The Martial Alliance thoroughly investigates those leaving the Heavenly Net, but they roughly inspect those entering. It was easy to enter by disguising myself with Cheongun Daeju. Their arrogance regarding the Heavenly Net is considerable."

,They must be confident, so they're still doing things like the Heavenly Net. And there's also the purpose of showing off."

It was then. Seong Jigone-nim looked out the window and said.

,Yujin-ah. Does it look dangerous? Should I help you?"

Outside the window, in the sky, a net-like barrier glowing faintly in gold could be seen. The Martial Alliance's Heavenly Net had narrowed to the point where it was visible to the naked eye.

,Wouldn't things get more complicated if you, an Imperial General, stepped in?"

,It's okay to cause a few accidents. I have enough connections. My teacher alone was the former Crown Prince's teacher, Guwol Seonja. And the opponent is just the Martial Alliance. It'll just end with me getting scolded, right?"

The Martial Alliance, which could be said to be one pillar of the martial world, was just the Martial Alliance. From the perspective of the Imperial Army, the Martial Alliance was nothing special.

,It's okay. I'll take care of this myself."

,Well, if that's the case. You'll take care of it yourself because it's you. Oh, right. Did you know that a national millennium celebration banquet is being held in two years? The Emperor himself is hosting the banquet at the Imperial Palace. Do you want to go together this time?"

,Ah. That?"

I took out the Emperor's invitation from my inventory. Seong Jigone-nim's eyes widened when he saw the Imperial Seal. He couldn't hide his admiration.

,As expected, you had a plan, Yujin-ah."

,Of course. Keukeu."

Seong Jigone-nim and Salwang soon disappeared with the space-time scroll I gave them. I didn't like that Salwang found out about the space-time scroll, but Seong Jigone-nim would manage Salwang on his own.

,Among the old women who became Seong Jigone-nim's women, there wasn't a single one who betrayed him.'

There won't be in the future either. From the old women's perspective, they'll never be able to find a man like Seong Jigone-nim.

The city was sealed off.

The Martial Alliance's Heavenly Net had enveloped the city. I felt a pressure as if my shoulders were being pressed down. Even my inner energy was being suppressed by the Heavenly Net. Moreover, a gaze as if watching me was constantly felt from the sky.

I sent out the courtesans I had been playing with. Soon, the Martial Alliance's experts would come crashing in. No matter how strong I was, I didn't have the confidence to protect all of them while fighting.

As I was left alone, taking out my tablet and watching porn, I felt the presence of dozens of people approaching me.

,Two absolute experts of the Samjeong level. About twenty Ogi level? And at least five hundred others who have reached the stage of emergence.'

It wasn't strange that there were quite a few Ogi Kyung experts, who were at the level of the heads of small and medium-sized sects. The Martial Alliance was made up of the Eight Great Families and One Alliance. Prestigious Righteous Sects with a high reputation even among the Righteous Sects. They had raised Ogi-level experts with the power they had accumulated over thousands of years of continuing their sects.

,They must have received a lot of support. Moreover, the headquarters of prestigious Righteous Sects are located on fertile land where spiritual energy flows abundantly.'

Prestigious Righteous Sects were places that carefully selected talent when accepting disciples.

I put the tablet into my inventory.

Kwakwakwakwang!

I felt through my senses that hundreds of people were firing palm strikes at the building I was in. I raised my energy barrier to block the collapsing roof. Dust and dirt rose thickly along with the debris of the building.

The wind blew. An artificial wind mixed with energy. The wind created through sorcery blew away the dust and debris of the building. I endured with my protective aura. I couldn't be blown away by such a wind in such an embarrassing way.

I looked at the two absolute experts of the Samjeong Kyung standing in front of me.

,Haha. Is this the Martial Alliance's vaunted Heavenly Net? What a waste of effort. I never had any intention of running away from the start."

One was a Taoist holding a sword. Seeing the plum blossoms drawn on his Taoist robe, he seemed to be from the ruined Hwasan Sect. His gaze fixed on me was full of murderous intent.

,You demonic bastards only spout fancy words. The plum blossoms of Hwasan have not yet withered. Today, the sword of Hwasan will drink the blood of the enemy and bloom again...!"

The other was a Taoist with long white hair and a long white beard, with the appearance of a divine immortal. He was holding a sword, but he had the mysterious atmosphere unique to sorcerers.

,Calm down, Mae Hwa Parang (Plum Blossom Wave). You are too excited right now. Regain your composure. I'm worried that you might fall into a demonic state due to your desire for revenge."

,You Taoist. What's your name?"

,I am Yulgu from the Gongdong Sect."

,The Martial Alliance's Elder Taeheo Jin-in (Great Void True Man)."

I had known the names and inner strength of the Martial Alliance's Elders in advance. They were so famous that even the innkeepers on the street knew them all.

,To complete the trial, I have to kill one of these bastards.'

The energy felt from Mae Hwa Parang and Taeheo Jin-in was similar. I didn't know the details of their combat power, but in terms of their realm, they were on the same level. Samjeong Middle Stage (Three Peaks Middle Stage). A little higher than me.

,Mae Hwa Parang, who is pissed off, seems easier to deal with than the composed Taeheo Jin-in.'

Mae Hwa Parang Makso's eyes were bloodshot with revenge. Even if the Righteous Sect's inner cultivation was pure, problems would arise if he was that angry.

I aimed Hwaryeon Bido at Mae Hwa Parang and shouted.

,Mae Hwa Parang! I challenge you to a one-on-one duel! A life-and-death match! Let's fight until one of us dies!! Surely you're not too scared to refuse?!"

...From now on, I will dominate So Cheonma Seong Yujin. Warriors of the Jinryong Unit, execute the Hundred Evil Extermination Formation. Sorcerers, release the true power of the Heavenly Net.
