It was when I was sitting at my desk in the office, downloading and playing mobile games of this world.

The door burst open, and a woman appeared. No, she was too young to be called a woman. Even if I looked at her kindly, she looked about 8 years old. Moreover, her hairstyle was brown twin tails. The clothes she was wearing were a suit, but it didn't suit her because of her young appearance.

However, the magic power emanating from her could not be ignored. At least, if you only consider the total amount of magic power, it is similar to or greater than mine.

,"Are you the one who crippled my younger brother?!"

Yoo Minha.

Commander of the 3rd Battalion. She's the older sister of Yoo Sseokchang, the deputy commander. Through the original work, I know that her strength has nothing to do with her appearance. She is stronger than Yoo Sseokchang to the point where I doubt if they have the same genes.

,"Doesn't the 3rd Battalion have anything else to do? They keep coming to pick fights and acting like a bitch."

,"It seems like the rumors are true that the new 8th Battalion Commander is a total psycho. Hey, come outside. Let's have a go at it in the training ground."

,"Weren't you here to avenge your brother?"

,"Ah. I have to do it as a side job. It's not like I killed Sseokchang, so I'll only half-kill you!"

It was a statement worthy of the most belligerent woman in the Order of Knights.

She wasn't my type, so I wasn't very interested in her. She's not weak either, so fighting her now would be tiring in many ways.

,"It's almost lunchtime. Let's eat before we fight. 2 PM. Wait for me at the 3rd Battalion training ground. I'll come find you."

,"Oh. I like your confident attitude. I'll test for myself whether you're a wizard worthy of being a commander. See you in three hours."

She ran outside.

She was as dumb as in the original work.

• • •

2 PM.

Yoo Minha waited for Seong Yujin, the 8th Battalion Commander, with her arms crossed at the 3rd Magic Division Headquarters training ground. The training ground was full of wizards who had come to watch the sparring match. They came to see Seong Yujin's skills, which were not yet known.

Yoo Minha was pleased with them. It's more fun to fight when there's an audience.

,'Sseokchang was defeated... but he didn't die. It's just that his arms and legs were torn off and he suffered burns all over his body. The 6th Battalion healed him, so it's okay.'

It would have been different if he had died. He didn't die. She had experienced having her arms and legs torn off several times. The burns all over his body were a bit much, though.

,'Sseokchang has been acting up too much lately, so I was going to educate him, but this is good. Of course, I have to avenge him as his older sister.'

Regardless of his personality, Yoo Sseokchang's skills were certain. His position as deputy commander was not simply because he was her younger brother.

He said that he was ambushed while off guard, but it was his skill that allowed him to subdue him in an instant.

,'Fire, wind, water. A sorcerer who handles three elements. It's amazing that he handles three elements. When I saw him earlier, his magic power was similar to mine.... I'm even more excited about his combat skills.'

The names of wizards differ slightly depending on their magic.

Sorcerer, Warlock, Wizard.

Sorcerers are wizards who handle the purest elemental magic.

Warlocks are wizards who handle familiars.

Wizards are wizards who handle other magic.

Sorcerers and Warlocks were rare in themselves. Most wizards are Wizards.

Sorcerers are as strong as they are rare. Elemental magic is no different from handling nature.

,'Seong Yujin will probably try to overwhelm me with the firepower of elemental magic. Even if the Director-General recognizes his talent, he hasn't bloomed yet. He must lack combat experience. If he had combat experience... there's no way a sorcerer of that caliber wouldn't have made a name for himself by now. Let's engage in close combat as soon as the sparring starts. The space in the training ground is limited, so he can't run away.'

She continued her combat simulation in her head and suddenly realized that more than 5 minutes had passed.

,"Why isn't that bastard coming? Is he scared? Someone bring the 8th Battalion Commander here!!"

At her shout, several members of the 3rd Battalion left the training ground. And in less than 3 minutes, they brought news.

,"Commander! The 8th Battalion Commander was not at headquarters! He left headquarters an hour ago!"

It was a no-show.

She immediately frowned.

,"Where did that bastard go?"

,"We only know that he went out on a mission."

,"I'm asking where he went."

,"We don't know. The 8th Battalion Commander has independent operation rights, so... we can't know the details. We have to ask the Director-General, but...."

The Director-General of the National Order of Knights is not someone that a mere member can meet just because they want to. He is a minister-level public official and is often called to the President. The Director-General's office must be empty now.

,"Damn it!"

Yoo Minha was frustrated, but there was nothing she could do.

• • •

Tae-eul Prison, hidden in the mountains of Gangwon-do.

This prison, protected by a special magic barrier, only houses magic criminals. Most of the prisoners have their grimoires destroyed and cannot use magic, but they have magic power, so they have bodies beyond humans and can become the owners of grimoires without owners.

For the past 30 years, Tae-eul Prison has been hidden and not revealed. But that's until now.

A group of people appeared near Tae-eul Prison, breaking through the barrier. Among them, a man and a woman stood out.

A man with a muscular body and a buzz cut. Baek Joo-hyuk frowned and complained.

,"To think that we have to advance the prison raid plan because of that brat. No matter how much he's been appointed as the next executive, is he worth modifying the plan?"

A beautiful woman with orange hair that reached her waist, Im Hyeon-ah, crossed her arms. Her already voluptuous breasts swelled even more.

,"That's funny. Are you jealous because he's a candidate for executive like you?"

,"No, Hyeon-ah-nim. I don't know why a guy who's already been defeated and captured is important. Frankly, aren't I stronger than him?"

,"Ji Hyeon-soo has more potential. The magic to transform into a demon has more possibilities than you thought. And the organization seems very curious about the skills of the new 8th Battalion Commander. So much so that they want to ask the person who fought him."

,"Haa. I'm worried for some reason.... I guess we can't postpone the operation until next time, right?"

He shook his head, looking at the 20 magicians, members of the organization, standing behind him.

,"You know it well. Kill the guards and release the prisoners. You brought the grimoires, right? Don't forget to scout some of the prisoners into the organization. I'll take care of Ji Hyeon-soo."

Petals appeared from somewhere and stuck to Im Hyeon-ah's body. In less than a second, the petals fell off and disappeared. Her appearance had changed. A woman in her 30s with dull black hair and dark circles under her eyes. Her clothes also changed, and she was wearing a black coat.

,"Kiya. As expected of Hyeon-ah-nim. Your transformation magic is perfect. Even the atmosphere has changed completely, so I would have been fooled if I hadn't seen it myself."

,"My magic reproduces some of the target's memories or experiences. Well, only when I'm transformed."

,"Is that woman the warden of Tae-eul Prison?"

,"That's right. Han So-wol. She has magic that binds the target. You need this woman's permission to enter and exit Tae-eul Prison smoothly. I'll take care of that, so you don't have to worry about it. Follow me."

She and the members of the organization confidently entered the Tae-eul Prison building.

They felt something strange right after entering the prison.

The prison was quiet. Too quiet. It was strange.

,"......."

,"Hyeon-ah-nim. I smell blood. A lot of blood. I don't see any guards either. Something's wrong."

Im Hyeon-ah rummaged through Han So-wol's memories and headed to a panel on one wall. She could control the doors of the prison cells from here.

Clang!

All the doors of the prison cells opened. Instead of the prisoners who should have been happy and running out, blood that had not yet cooled flowed out and filled the hallway.

The members of the organization were flustered.

,"Hyeon-ah-nim?! The prisoners are all dead!"

,"They were all attacked by something sharp!"

,"Is this a trap?!"

,"Hyeon-ah-nim! What should we do?!"

It's a trap.

Im Hyeon-ah's expression hardened. But why? Why did they kill all the prisoners? If it was a trap by the National Order of Knights, there would have been no need to kill the prisoners.

,'Questions later. We have to run now.'

She immediately shouted to the members of the organization.

,"Retreat!"

,"Not so fast. I've been waiting for you here, why are you leaving already?"

A man appeared at the entrance of the building. Im Hyeon-ah and the other members of the organization immediately recognized his identity. They had received and studied his documents this morning.

,"The 8th Battalion Commander, Seong Yujin...!"

,"I became the commander yesterday, and it's already known. There was a traitor inside after all. Here, this is a gift. Isn't this what you came to get?"

Seong Yujin threw something in front of them. It was Ji Hyeon-soo, whose limbs were cut off and whose whole body was burned. His eyes were gone, and he was oozing pus from his whole body, only moving his mouth.

,"Ki, kill me. Please.... I told you everything I know... please kill me...."

It was Ji Hyeon-soo, their target. While everyone was shocked by his miserable appearance, Im Hyeon-ah made a decision faster than anyone else.

The ability to judge danger, which is separate from magic. It was a kind of intuition and instinct that made her who she is today.

,'Transformation target! Malena!'

Her body changed. Into a slender Italian beauty with red hair. Malena's ability is to pass through matter like a ghost. It was magic that only she could do. She immediately passed through the wall and ran outside.

Fortunately, Seong Yujin did not follow her.

,'How am I supposed to deal with that monster! The best thing to do is to run away and report to the organization! I can't handle it!'

She shouted inwardly as if making excuses to herself and ran through the forest.

Seong Yujin did not move even after seeing Im Hyeon-ah disappear. It's because he knows she won't go far anyway.

He held out one finger to the 21 magicians who were nervous looking at him.

,"One person. I'll spare only one person. So kill each other from now on."

Baek Joo-hyuk shouted.

,"Don't listen to him! He didn't say he'd let that one person live properly! He'll definitely torture you like Ji Hyeon-soo over there! The only way for us to survive is to kill that bastard!"

,"The muscle monster is smart. But what can you guys do?"

Seong Yujin wiggled his finger.

The wind moved.

The magicians, sensing something strange, tried to respond, but it was too late. Their necks were cut by the invisible wind and fell to the floor.

The only one alive was Baek Joo-hyuk, who sensed the magic power and protected his neck with both arms. There were cuts on both of his arms from the blade.

,"You sensed my magic power and strengthened your arms with magic power to block the Wind Cutter. You're pretty good."

,"You...!"

Baek Joo-hyuk gritted his teeth and charged at Seong Yujin. At the same time, he uses the acceleration magic he has used thousands of times. His movements became more than two steps faster.

He closes the distance and throws a punch at Seong Yujin's face. But Seong Yujin easily dodges and grabs Baek Joo-hyuk by the collar and throws him away.

,"Everyone and their dog has acceleration abilities. I'll play with you for about 3 minutes to improve my skills."
