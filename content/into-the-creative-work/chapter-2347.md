Some quick-witted bandits turned tail and ran the moment their boss bit the dust. But none of them managed to escape successfully. Before they could even get out of the bandit camp, I was already slicing and dicing them with Hwaryeon Bido, sending out waves of ganggi.

Then, a few bandits changed their tactics. They tried to hide in the crates while I was momentarily distracted.

It was a pointless effort. Even without emitting electromagnetic waves, I could sense everything around me as if I were using a map hack.

,There's no way these petty bandits can trick my senses.'

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷光).

I deliberately used Thunder Flash to kill the bandits. It was like using a butcher knife to kill a bug, but I was frustrated, too. Since the conditions were slightly different from my training, I wondered if I might gain something.

,Doesn't mean shit....'

Killing them by swinging my sword randomly and killing them with Thunder Flash. The only difference between the two was the effort involved. I didn't feel anything else.

I killed all the bandits I could see. That didn't mean I killed all the humans. I didn't kill the people tied up in the shabby huts. Anyone could see they weren't bandits.

Shiver, shiver.

The people trembled and cowered as they looked at me. Even though I had saved them, they were frozen with fear and couldn't even say thank you. What rude bastards. I almost killed them, but I held back. An absolute master needed fame. If I killed them all, who would spread my reputation?

,Rest assured. I, the Absolute Divine Hero, have taken care of the bandits who captured you. You are safe now."

,Th, thank you...."

,I am the Absolute Divine Hero. Don't forget it. If I hadn't saved you... you would probably have become food for monsters."

Human trafficking wasn't easy in cities or towns. The Emperor had forbidden it. In other words, human trafficking was easy outside the cities and towns.

Especially since many types of monsters grew by eating humans, there was a steady demand.

,No matter how great His Majesty the Emperor is, he can't control everything since his territory is so vast.'

There was a reason why he entrusted the management of cities and towns to the martial arts sects. It was all because of a lack of administrative power.

I turned around and left the bandit camp. The people were tied up with ropes, but... they could probably handle that themselves.

,What was it some guy said... teach a man to fish rather than give him a fish, right?'

I was their savior, so the reputation of the Absolute Divine Hero would spread far and wide. I left them behind and departed from the bandit camp.

Even after walking for a long time, no cities or towns appeared.

,Running is vulgar... but I can't just waste time, so should I walk while training?'

The training method was Sky Walking. But I didn't necessarily have to walk in the sky. The important thing was to train in delicately handling qi.

1cm.

I walked with my shoes 1cm above the ground. At first, I thought it was a trivial exercise, but it became quite difficult as time passed.

,If I'm careless, I'll step on the ground, and if I use too much energy, the air will spread or I'll float more than 3cm.'

Breathing was also important. Sky Walking consumed a lot of internal energy. I had to constantly circulate my energy through breathing to replenish it.

After about an hour, a drop of sweat appeared. The training I started on impulse was quite effective.

Then, I got annoyed halfway through and switched to auto-mode.

• • •

I repeated training and auto-mode. As a result, even though a month had passed in [Bright Light Ascension], the time I experienced was less than half of that.

Arriving in the city, I set everything else aside and looked for the Lower Five Gates (下五門) first.

Along with the Beggars' Sect, it was one of the most renowned sects for its intelligence gathering in the martial world.

It was a ragtag sect formed by the lower classes, but its intelligence gathering ability was comparable to that of the Beggars' Sect. I entered the Lower Five Gates branch and made a deal with them.

Information about the sects in this area that had mastered lightning-attribute martial arts.

I checked the paper with the information and glanced at the branch leader. The branch leader, who had scraped together the information in half a day after the deal, was a one-armed man with only his left arm. I had cut off his right arm because I didn't like the way he looked me up and down when I first saw him.

,Hey."

,Yes! Absolute Divine Hero Daehyeop-nim! Please speak! I will gladly obey!"

,Is the information accurate? If you're messing with me, I'll kill you. Your family, your friends, your relatives. All of them."

,I have investigated to the best of my ability!"

The branch leader said, sweating profusely. I had paid for the information. I tossed him some silver taels from my pocket as I grabbed them.

I glanced through the information. The nearest sect here was the Lightning Arrow Gate (霹矢門), a cultivation sect. They practice the Lightning Splitter Divine Bow Skill (裂雷神弓功), which fires arrows like lightning.

Other sects were also listed. Compared to the Lightning Arrow Gate, they were nothing, sects that wouldn't even be considered third-rate. The martial arts they practiced were also listed, but aside from being lightning-attribute, they were really nothing special.

Just as I was about to be disappointed with the information, the last sect listed caught my eye. The sect located the furthest away from here.

Thunder Sound Temple (雷音寺).

I had heard that name before. Wasn't it one of the Outer Realm martial arts sects that often appeared in martial arts novels?

,But this is Xianxia, so there's no such thing as Outer Realm martial arts.'

Only the location of the Thunder Sound Temple was listed. There was less information compared to the other sects. In particular, there was no information about the Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts, which was what I was most curious about.

,Why is there no information about the Thunder Sound Temple?"

The Thunder Sound Temple is a very old cultivation sect, even among cultivation sects, but it is famous for rarely participating in external affairs. It is known for handling thunder qi (雷氣), but there is no other information.

It probably wasn't a lie. Why would the Lower Five Gates try to trick me about the Thunder Sound Temple?

,The information is satisfactory."

,Th, then now...."

,I have a personal question. Is the Head of the Lower Five Gates a woman?"

,Yes?"

,I asked if she's a stunning beauty from a courtesan background."

Lower Five Gates.

It was a famous power along with the Nine Great Sects. The Lower Five Gates almost always appeared in martial arts stories. And the Head of the Lower Five Gates was often a woman. In several of the novels mixed into [Bright Light Ascension], the Head of the Lower Five Gates was a woman.

,I don't know where you heard that information... but the Head of the Lower Five Gates is a man."

,Did you see him yourself?"

,Yes. Before becoming a branch leader, I met the Head of the family in person and had a private audience."

I lost interest. I was thinking of visiting if the Head of the Lower Five Gates was a woman.

• • •

The Lightning Arrow Gate (霹矢門) was situated on the mountainside, but the terrain was so rough that ordinary people couldn't climb it.

There was no one at the entrance with the signboard hanging. Instead, a barrier was set up. It was ridiculously crude compared to Milyeong's barrier.

,A barrier that looks like it would tear with just a flick of the wrist.'

I hesitated for a moment before entering.

My purpose was the Lightning Splitter Divine Bow Skill (裂雷神弓功), the martial art of the Lightning Arrow Gate. I had no other purpose.

Would it be faster to kill all the people of the Lightning Arrow Gate and find the secret manual of the Lightning Splitter Divine Bow Skill? Or would it be faster to just ask for it?

,It's too much of a hassle to find it myself, so I should go with the latter. They should give it to me if I ask. What can they do?'

I moved forward.

Pajijijik.

The barrier created using lightning qi rejected me. As I tried to force my way in, electricity crackled and surged into me. It didn't even tickle. Ignoring the barrier's resistance, I stepped inside.

Jjeoeoeoeong!

The barrier, unable to withstand my presence, exploded. The Lightning Arrow Gate must have noticed my presence inside as well. I pushed open the main gate and went inside. As if they had been waiting, people popped out from all directions.

Dressed in white martial robes, they were all holding bows and aiming at me.

There were about 200 of them. They were all trash, not even worth checking their cultivation levels.

,Lower your bows."

I spoke with force. They gasped and trembled. When I reached for the sword hilt at my waist, they quickly lowered their bows.

,These bastards... they know I'm much stronger than them, but they're still testing me. Should I kill them all?'

A middle-aged man stepped forward from among them, holding a jade bow in his hand. His cultivation was at the Five Qi level. The three others around him had also just barely reached the Five Qi stage.

Perhaps because they were a cultivation sect, their power was quite strong for a sect stuck in the mountains.

,Senior-nim, what brings you to our sect?"

,I'm curious about the Lightning Splitter Divine Bow Skill. Bring the secret manual and offer it to me."

,...The Lightning Splitter Divine Skill (裂雷神功) is everything to our Lightning Arrow Gate. No matter how much Senior wishes for it, we cannot give it to you. Please, with your great generosity, consider us juniors.

,Consideration? I'm talking to you without killing you. What else would you call this if not consideration?"

,Senior-nim has reached the Three Divine Realm, but you are alone. You have not revealed your name either. We will not ask about Senior-nim in detail, and we will refrain from mentioning Senior-nim in the future. So please leave this sect."

The Lightning Arrow Gate Head's eyes gleamed sharply. Those around him also ignited their fighting spirit.

They looked down on me because I was alone.

And it seemed they realized that I hadn't been in the Three Divine Realm for long.

,Four at Five Qi and 162 at Foundation Building. The rest are at Qi Gathering.'

It seemed they had decided they could handle me.

,You arrogant bastards. So you insist on drinking the poison... Regret your foolishness."

I drew Hwaryeon Bido. The Lightning Arrow Gate Head aimed his bow at me, drew the string, and shot an arrow. The arrow, filled with lightning qi, flashed as it flew towards me.

Thunder Sky Style (Lei Tian Flow) Electromagnetic Barrier

The arrow pierced through the electromagnetic barrier. I quickly swung Hwaryeon Bido to deflect the arrow.

,It's impressive that it pierced the electromagnetic barrier. And did the electromagnetic barrier's resistance weaken because it's the same attribute?'

,Shoot!"

The Lightning Arrow Gate Head shouted. All the members of the Lightning Arrow Gate drew their bowstrings.

Flash. Flash. The arrows they shot shone with lightning and surged towards me.

They were different from the arrow shot by the Lightning Arrow Gate Head. I couldn't feel any intent at all. It was just an attack that shot out lightning qi contained in arrows.

I spread my intent. I seized all the lightning qi that entered my domain and slammed it down. The arrows changed direction, crashed into the ground, and shattered.

This is crazy... The opponent is a master of thunder techniques (雷功)! Don't use lightning qi recklessly!

,Head of the family! Then how should we fight him?!"

Disciples below the Foundation Building, use the Qi Transfer Technique to transfer your lightning qi to me! Only the Lightning Splitter Arrow (裂雷矢) containing all of our power can kill him! Elders, protect me!

Centered around the Lightning Arrow Gate Head, the sect members held hands with each other. They transferred their lightning qi to the Head of the family. It was a kind of technique. A technique only possible because it was lightning qi.

The three elders at the Five Qi realm stood in front of me, protecting the Head of the family. I didn't attack and kill them.

,Is the Lightning Splitter Arrow the best you can do? I'm curious how great it is that you're making such a fuss. Give it a try."

With anticipation, I watched the Lightning Arrow Gate Head with sparkling eyes. If I liked it, a new technique would be added to the Thunder Sky Style today.
