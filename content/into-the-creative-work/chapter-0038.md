South Korea is pathologically strict about firearms.

Even if it's low, at night, the vampire's physical abilities become so high that humans can't handle it, so they hunt safely with sniping.

However, vampires also know that fact. Vampires don't go to places that are good for being sniped.

"I've seen this in a game... what was it. M24?"

When I think about it, I especially liked assassin characters.
