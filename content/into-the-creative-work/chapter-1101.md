When I opened my eyes, I was in a hospital bed.

I wasn't surprised or flustered because I expected it. The Freyssel Kingdom army must have picked me up while I was passed out.

I sit up and check my condition. Except for a bit of stuffiness, it wasn't bad. There was an IV needle in my right hand, and the bracelet I received from Instructor Lexie was gone; someone must have taken it.

The Astral, which rose to Level 2, is in perfect condition without any problems, and the mana road, half of which had become unusable, has recovered to some extent.

,This isn't just an IV drip; it's a recovery potion.'

If it's a Level 1 recovery potion being used instead of an IV drip, that's pretty good.

After roughly grasping the situation, I dredged up my memories. I recalled my battle experience with Neros. Such experiences were rare. It will definitely be helpful in the future.

,I awakened a new ability, an instant, and I also learned that I can use magic of a higher level than my own by using the environment.'

The biggest gain was the instant.

The instant is an ability that drastically increases thinking speed. It's an ability that doesn't originally exist in the game Dark Moon.

,The system window popped up when I used the instant. Is it like a perk given when I came to this world?'

Whatever the source of the instant, it was a power that helped me.

,The mana road becomes unusable when I use the instant, but it's easy to calculate in advance with the accelerated thinking speed. Fortunately, unlike the mana road, the instant's time is maintained even if I open the Astral.'

The biggest problem for magicians is casting speed. Casting magic during battle requires a high degree of concentration. This is also why there are so few battle mages who directly engage in combat. Usually, magicians play a role in providing fire support from the rear.

,In the game, magic is activated just by pressing a hotkey, but reality is different.'

In that sense, the instant, which accelerates thinking, is almost like a cheat key for magicians. It reduces casting speed by more than 90 percent.

,I also have the ability to generate electricity. Even if it's impossible to use the Level 5 lightning magic, Thunder Bolt, like when I fought Neros, I can still easily use a few Level 3 lightning magic spells now.'

This world is reality, not a game. Levels are not an absolute standard.

,The problem is mana. Can I use Level 3 lightning magic more than three times in my current state?'

I shook my head slightly. I'm skeptical. Unless a powerful firepower is needed, it is more efficient to use Level 2 magic.

,To do that, I need Level 2 magic knowledge.'

Step.

I felt a presence from beyond the door. I shook off my thoughts and looked at the hospital room door. The door opened and a familiar face entered. It's Instructor Venus, the magic officer. His rank is only Staff Sergeant, but if you look closely at the military, your position is not determined by rank alone. Instructor Venus was the most senior of the instructors.

"I heard you woke up and came to see you. How's your body?"

I tried not to look at the CCTV installed in the corner of the hospital room, saluted him, and answered.

"I'm fine except for a slight stuffiness."

"The doctor said it would take three days. Rest well in the hospital room for three days."

Instructor Venus plopped down on the chair next to the bed. He looked at me with sharply flashing eyes.

"Let's talk about the mission. You and the armored vehicle driver are the only survivors, so we need your testimony."

I kept my face as poker-faced as possible while racking my brain. The armored vehicle driver must have been about 2km away from the mission area, so it was natural that he was alive. The important thing is how he reported to the unit.

,Did he observe me using Thunder Bolt? Thunder Bolt is as flashy as it is powerful, so it's easy to recognize. I want to hide my talent as much as possible....'

Too much talent can arouse the envy of others.

In particular, I have awakened two abilities. Magic talent and natural awakening ability. This alone was enough reason to become another guinea pig for experimentation.

"...I remember Instructor Velha recording the mission site with a camera."

"The camera was smashed. The radio's communication record was cut off from the middle. Neros. That bastard happened to awaken as a Lightning Dragon, so all the surrounding electronic devices went haywire. We found you three hours after the communication was cut off."

That's what I expected. The mana of lightning that Neros scattered everywhere would have had a kind of EMP effect.

What's bothering me is the armored vehicle driver.

"What happened to the armored vehicle driver...? Is he safe?"

Instructor Venus didn't answer right away. He stared at me with sunken eyes before opening his mouth.

"The driver is in custody."

"Yes?"

"He was caught trying to desert."

Instructor Venus clicked his tongue loudly as if he didn't like it.

All the clues were gathered. I reasoned out the situation.

,They found me three hours after the communication was cut off.... The armored vehicle driver probably ran away when the radio was cut off... before the Thunder Bolt fell. There's a high probability that he didn't see the Thunder Bolt while running away.... Even if he did see it, he didn't confirm it.'

The armored vehicle driver did not recover me. He doesn't know the details.

,Even if my testimony and the driver's testimony are different... they'll believe me. The driver is a criminal who tried to desert.'

The future of the driver who tried to desert was obvious.

Execution.

If not, he would live as a slave for the rest of his life. He could become an experimental subject somewhere, like me.

"I will tell you everything that happened to me at the mission site."

I recalled the traces I saw just before falling asleep. It comes to mind as vividly as looking at a photograph. Having become Number 211, I could easily recall things I had seen once without forgetting them.

"The mission was going smoothly until Neros appeared. ...I sniped at Neros when he appeared, and he reflected the bullet. Since the sniper rifle was broken, I went to join them right away."

"The communication record remains until Instructor Velha stepped up. What about after that?"

I told the truth. Instructor Venus already had a grasp of the situation to some extent. The military is full of experts related to combat. They would have figured out more than 70% of what happened just from the traces of the battle.

The testimony I have to give is not about telling lies, but about subtracting from the truth.

I eliminate my achievements and praise Instructor Velha's achievements. The traces of Thunder Bolt can be glossed over as Neros's work. Fortunately, he awakened as a Lightning Dragon.

"...I was just lucky to survive."

"This, you mean."

Instructor Venus took out a bracelet from his pocket. It's an artifact I received from Instructor Lexie. He returned the artifact to me.

"Instructor Lexie gave it to you, I hear. It's quite a thing. Take good care of it."

"...Thank you for returning it."

"When did you become Level 2?"

"I felt my Astral expanding just before I passed out."

"It happens sometimes. Soldiers who grow during battle. You not only safely overcame the mana backflow, but also benefited from it... and this time you survived and became Level 2."

"......"

"People like you are usually said to have strong bad luck. Well, that luck rarely lasts until the end. Anyway, congratulations on surviving and reaching Level 2."

"......Thank you."

"But don't forget that your position hasn't changed. Do you understand, Private Number 211?"

"Private.... Do I have a rank now?"

"Everyone who succeeded in the mission got a rank."

"...What happened to those who failed?"

"They died."

He said it as if he was used to it and left the hospital room.

•••

As Instructor Venus said, my position did not change even though I became a Private. I was still a slave and an experimental subject.

Before being discharged from the hospital room, a terrible time came.

It was time for the mana essence to be injected into my body. Even if I didn't do anything wrong, as long as I was an experimental subject in the 'Battle Mage Project', I would periodically receive mana essence injections.

I lay on the operating table with my limbs restrained. I was completely naked, without a single thread on, and electrodes were attached to various parts of my body, including my chest, shoulders, and neck, making a ttadaktadak sound.

Step. Step.

A man wearing a surgical cap and mask approached me with emotionless eyes. His hand held a syringe filled with blue liquid. Before injecting the mana essence, he put a gag in my mouth. The memory of Number 211 writhing in pain comes to mind. I gritted my teeth as if to break the gag.

The syringe touches my neck. The unfamiliar and cold sensation of the metal needle sends chills down my spine. The man pushed the piston. Cold, heavy mana essence flows through my veins.

The mana essence flowed through my veins and spread throughout my body. The Astral and mana road react.

"......!!"

Pain occurred in the process.

It was a pain as if my whole body was being torn apart from the inside. The pain lasted until the mana essence was completely absorbed into the body.

The 12 mana roads are activated by the mana essence. I activated the mana road as much as possible in the pain. The pain got worse, but it was a struggle to gain even a little more benefit.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!'

I screamed in my heart. If my limbs weren't restrained, I would have squirmed like a trampled bug on the floor.

The time of pain was long. Even though I didn't use the instant, it felt like time was flowing slowly. It was so damn cursed that time was relative.

"The Astral is more stable than last time. Did the efficiency increase as you became Level 2? We'll inject an additional 20%."

The man brought a new syringe and stuck it in my neck.

,Ssiibaaaaaaaal!!!!'

The pain that was calming down was like pouring gasoline on a fire. I strained my eyes that were about to turn over. If I passed out here, I could die in the worst case. I have to hold on to my mind as much as possible.

,Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!!!'

The badge on the man's collar came into view. A badge in the shape of a red tear. The game knowledge of 'Dark Moon' comes to mind. That badge is a badge that means Alpha Tear Pharmaceutical Company.

,I'll kill them!! I'm going to kill all the bastards involved in this!!!'

I raised anger and hatred over the burning pain. The three were mixed into smoke and engraved in my mind as a desire for revenge.

•••

Receiving the mana essence was not the end.

For the sake of the experiment results, they took my blood and examined my body with machines I had never heard of on Earth. The Alpha Tear bastards looked at the results and nodded to each other, liking it. I had to desperately suppress the rising murderous intent towards them.

I took my eyes off them. Just looking at them keeps making me angry.

I observed my body through the Astral.

The effect of the injected mana essence was there. The mana increased and the quality of the mana road improved a little more. The increase in mana is good news. I was already worried about the lack of mana.

,It pisses me off even more when I think about exchanging it for my lifespan.'

There are no major side effects yet, but I couldn't guarantee how long that would last. This body has almost no physical talent compared to its magical talent.

,I hope it's okay at least until I escape from here....'
