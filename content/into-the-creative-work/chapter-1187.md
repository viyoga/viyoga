The day had dawned.

No, looking out the window, it wasn't just dawn; it was around noon. I grabbed my throbbing head and sat up. Honestly, I wanted to lie back down and sleep for another five hours, but... the situation wasn't that relaxed. I had to deal with the Yakuza coming up to the village.

I got up. Nakagami Risa-senpai, Hagawa Ryoko-yang, and the shrine maiden, who should have been by my side, were nowhere to be seen. I looked at the folded clothes right next to me. Black clothes. Similar to the Japanese traditional clothes Shibata was wearing. I don't know the name.

,Did they prepare this for me to wear? Well, the clothes I was wearing were covered in blood.'

I put on the clothes. The Blood Pill and weapons were next to the clothes, so I took them. The Yakuza hadn't arrived in the village yet, it seemed. That's why I was able to sleep until now.

,Even so, there are still a few hours left until the cooldown for Full Recovery.'

I opened the door and went outside.

The shrine, which was eerie in the dark, felt somewhat sacred in the daytime.

I walked inside the shrine and met the shrine maiden.

,You're awake.'

The shrine maiden was there. She wasn't alone. Ten people in black clothes were standing quietly around her.

The sight of her grimacing and groaning under me was nowhere to be found. She was neatly dressed in her shrine maiden clothes, her long black hair was tidy, and her face was calmer than ever.

I deliberately stared into the shrine maiden's eyes. Usually, she would feel embarrassed, but she was completely unfazed.

,How are you feeling?'

,I'm a little tired.'

I stroked my side. The area where I got hit by Wada Kyoshiro and bruised. It wasn't enough to interfere with the battle, but the pain was quite annoying.

,Take this medicine. It will make you feel a little better.'

I received the medicine she handed me. It was a clear liquid of about 10ml.

,What is this?'

,It's a secret medicine of the Kaminoyama family. It's effective for external injuries and will relieve some of the fatigue.'

,Is it a drug?'

,It's different from the Blood Pill. It's a medicine made purely by blending and refining Medicinal herbs. Compared to the Blood Pill, the effect is less, but there are no side effects.'

The shrine maiden and I decided to be on the same boat. There was no reason for her to betray and kill me now. It was just as dangerous for her. I poured the liquid into my mouth. It tasted very bitter. So bitter that my face contorted.

,It doesn't taste very good.'

,They say good medicine tastes bitter. How's your body?'

,Definitely... I think I'm less tired than before. Does it usually work this fast?'

,It's the Kaminoyama family's secret medicine, after all.'

,The Kaminoyama family is amazing. They also made the Blood Pill.'

,No. I made the Blood Pill. I made the prototype of the Blood Pill about 10 years ago.'

,Wasn't the ingredient a drug? You managed to get it.'

,Even back then, the Kaminomiya family was trading with the Yakuza. It wasn't difficult to get drugs. Getting a human corpse was a little difficult, though.'

I nodded roughly.

It's logically absurd, but this is a world in a creative work. Almost anything is possible. The shrine maiden is probably a genius of pharmaceuticals or something.

,What about the Yakuza?'

,They haven't arrived yet. I contacted them, but they just kept repeating that they would arrive soon.'

,They're targeting you, aren't they? Right?'

,Yes. They seem to have roughly grasped the situation in the village.'

,What about Risa-senpai and Ryoko-yang?'

,They're having a meal at the restaurant.'

I looked around again.

,Is everyone here all the people you have?'

,There are about five more, though not here.'

,How many Yakuza do you think will come?'

,Probably more than 100. They know we're not easy to deal with.'

,The difference in power is too great. Can't we use the villagers?'

,They've already been taken care of.'

,Taken care of...? You killed them?'

,Yes. They knew too much to leave them alone.'

,The villagers would have been loyal to you, right?'

,It's a different story if the opponent is the Yakuza. I don't trust the loyalty of toys.'

,The brainwashing isn't perfect, is it.'

,I didn't brainwash them. Even without that, I can play with them enough.'

She was truly an amazing woman.

I nodded roughly. 100 Yakuza. I'm not afraid. I have the Blood Pill and weapons. I was confident that I could handle about 100 Yakuza. The problem is that the Yakuza will have guns.

If they shoot a gun from the front, I can roughly dodge it. I can infer the direction of the bullet from the finger pulling the trigger and the muzzle. But it requires concentration, and even I have no answer to a gun shooting from a blind spot.

Chaeng!

I took out a Japanese sword from my waist. Despite the sudden unsheathing, the surroundings were quiet. Not to mention the shrine maiden, the men in black clothes were also standing still. They were well-trained dogs.

,Are you going to cut me down?'

,Not that. I was checking the Japanese sword. Look, the blade is a bit chipped. Don't you have a decent weapon?'

,We have a few handguns.'

,What about Japanese swords?'

,...We do have some, but does it have to be a Japanese sword?'

,I'm not good with guns. A Japanese sword is more comfortable.'

The shrine maiden turned her head to the side and gave an order to the people standing still.

,Bring all the Japanese swords in the village.'

,Yes, Shrine Maiden-nim.'

The people in black clothes answered and moved all at once.

,Ah, do you have bulletproof vests too?'

,...Bring the bulletproof vests too.'

,Yes, Shrine Maiden-nim.'

They brought 11 Japanese swords and 3 bulletproof vests in 20 minutes. I took the best bulletproof vest and carefully examined the Japanese swords with cautious eyes.

,This one is the best.'

,Is there a difference from the other Japanese swords?'

,You can feel the different aura just by looking at it. This is at the level of a craftsman's work.'

,......I can't tell even if I look.'

I grabbed the hilt and swung it a few times in the air. I was satisfied.

,What happened to Wada Kyoshiro?'

,...You mean that man Hagawa-yang mentioned. We haven't found him.'

It's probably one of two things. He either went down the mountain or is hiding in the village or the mountain.

I think he's hiding in the mountain. Wada Kyoshiro would also know that there are external forces below the mountain.

,Ah, right. What about the women in the woodworking shop?'

,The woodworking shop... you mean the place where they rape women. There were only corpses in the woodworking shop.'

,Did those women run away...? But they seemed to rape women regularly?'

,It's for stress management for the villagers. Being trapped in the village can create various desires and act as a variable. They used the village women as a means to control their desires.'

,That's crazy. They can endure that much desire.'

,They had already experienced murder, and it was difficult to suppress their desires due to drugs. It was easier to manage them by letting them release their desires appropriately.'

,But there would still be problems, right?'

,If it seemed like it wouldn't work out, we just took care of them.'

,I see.'

I nodded roughly.

•••

I looked down at the village from the shrine with the shrine maiden. The shrine was in a high place, so it was perfect for looking down at the village. It was also easy to monitor the inside of the village with binoculars.

Cars were coming in a line through the village entrance. The types of cars were different. There were luxury sedans and cheap compact cars. The most common type of car was a van. The cars stopped appropriately near the village entrance.

The car doors opened, and men with sturdy physiques poured out.

They all had dirty looks on their faces and had weapons such as Japanese swords, crowbars, and hammers in their hands. There were also men who didn't carry weapons, but their pockets were bulging. They exuded a fierce aura and confidently walked towards the shrine.

,About 120 people.'

,It's full of impact when you see it in person. If Shibata were here...'

,Stop looking for a dead guy. I killed Shibata. That means I'm stronger than him.'

,...Can you fight and win against them?'

,I have to try. It would be great if we had something like a bomb.'

,We don't have bombs.'

,I know.'

The Yakuza stopped in front of the shrine.

The man with sunglasses at the very front of them held up a walkie-talkie.

Jijijijik.

The walkie-talkie in the shrine maiden's hand reacted.

,Yo, Kaminoyama-yang. I heard there was a commotion in the village, but it's quieter than I thought?'

,Kizaki-ssi came himself. Aren't you busy?'

,I'm busy. But what can I do? The boss ordered it himself. And it's related to our Kaminoyama-yang. I have to step in. You know I like you a lot, right?'

My lips twitched. How dare he target my woman...

The shrine maiden had no change in expression.

,You came too late.'

,Kaminoyama-yang is smart, so you know how the situation is going, right?'

,You want the Blood Pill manufacturing method.'

,The boss wants that. You know how great and cruel our boss is. Let's go nicely, nicely. You don't want to become a cheap whore and live a life worse than a dog, do you?'

,Do you think I don't know anything? If I hand over the manufacturing method, you'll kill me to shut my mouth. If not, you'll imprison me somewhere forever.'

,I told you, I like Kaminoyama-yang. Become my lover. Then you can get freedom. It won't be the same level of freedom as now, but you won't have to go through rough things.'

,I'm sorry. I don't have any feelings for Kizaki-ssi.'

Kizaki took off his sunglasses. I narrowed my eyes and looked at the guy's face. His skin was tanned, and there were wrinkles around his eyes. He looked to be in his early to mid-40s.

,...Well, you're kicking away the opportunity I'm giving you. I should cancel the word 'smart'. It's your choice. It's no use regretting it later.'

,......I'm a little surprised that you came so openly. Don't you think I'll commit suicide?'

,The boss said this about you. She's a woman who will tenaciously live and look for opportunities even if she rolls in a pile of shit. He added that if you were born a man, he would have made you his successor.'

,...That's an overstatement.'

,Anyway, just wait there. I'm going up now.'

Kizaki cut off the walkie-talkie. Then he gestured to his subordinates. The subordinates began to climb the stairs.

,What are you going to do now?'

The shrine maiden asked me. Not only her, but also Nakagami Risa-senpai and Hagawa Ryoko-yang's gazes were directed at me. The guys in black clothes were quietly standing guard. It's not like the Yakuza in front of us are all there are.

,These stairs, did you deliberately make them so steep and high?'

,Yes. It's easier to monitor the village, and hundreds of stairs are hard to climb with a comfortable mind. Above all, it has authority because it's close to the sky.'

,I don't know about authority, but it's definitely a shitty staircase. The slope is steep, and there are a lot of stairs.'

,You're thinking of fighting on the stairs.'

,If we fight on flat ground, the chances of winning won't be even 20%. Ah, I'm the only one fighting on the stairs.'
