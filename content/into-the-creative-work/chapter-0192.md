,I can see the cabin below. I really did arrive in 10 minutes.'

I hid myself in a protruding part of the cliff. The height was about 20M, a height I could descend in 10 seconds.

,Now all that's left is to wait. That's the hardest part.'

In that old cabin down there lives an old man.

He's called a beggar old man in the city and is despised. But the old man isn't as weak as he's despised.

,The old man is at the Chulji (Achieving Ambition) stage, level 7.'

The beggar old man's name is Ha Seok.

He's a criminal who committed crimes and was even wanted, and 10 years ago, he accidentally discovered the cabin below the cliff and has been living in seclusion ever since. Since he's a character from the original work, I know him at least roughly.

,He occasionally comes to the city to beg or steal food. And he also picks up things that others have thrown away.'

The Bright Light Ascension Chart was originally a painting hanging in the cabin.

The person who lived there before Ha Seok was a hunter who died of illness a long time ago.

,Ha Seok is a shaman. Since his level is higher than mine, it's best not to fight.'

The protagonist of the original work is captured by Ha Seok. Ha Seok was careless because of the protagonist's weakness, and taking advantage of that carelessness, the protagonist kills Ha Seok and, while taking things that could be worth money from the cabin, accidentally discovers the secret of the Bright Light Ascension Chart.

,I'm the protagonist. There's no chance of the same thing happening as in the original. There's no need to fight with Ha Seok.'

I went to the brothels and subtly collected information about Ha Seok, and I monitored Ha Seok for several days with a drone camera to find out his lifestyle pattern.

Ha Seok spends most of his time inside the cabin. I don't know what he does inside the cabin. Going out to the city is random. He might go out in a week, or he might go out in 3 months.

,It's not impossible to aim for the gap when Ha Seok goes out to the city.'

Because I can't be monitoring Ha Seok forever.

,However, Ha Seok goes out of the cabin once every dawn to urinate.'

Perhaps because of the smell of excrement, he moves to a place a bit far from the cabin.

Taking advantage of the gap when Ha Seok urinates, I'll break into the cabin, steal the Bright Light Ascension Chart, and quietly run away into the mountains. That's the plan I've made.

,A guy living in seclusion can't chase me deeply. Even if he gets angry, he'll have no choice but to live with it.'

I watched the cabin, waiting for him to come out quickly.

About 2 hours passed like that.

Ha Seok came out of the cabin.

He's wearing black rags. His hair has severe hair loss, with only a few strands of gray hair.

Just by looking at him, his hygiene doesn't seem very good. I feel like I'll get sick if I get close.

Ha Seok moved further and further away from the old cabin.

,Now!'

Tat. Tat. Tat!

Quickly descending to the ground, I went inside the cabin.

The inside of the cabin was dark and full of a musty smell. There were animal bones and pieces of wood of unknown use everywhere.

I ignored everything else and approached the Bright Light Ascension Chart hanging on one side of the wall.

The size is roughly similar to a 27-inch monitor.

It's a painting of the sky and mountains, with clouds in the sky. Light comes down from between the clouds, illuminating the ground.

And in the center of the painting is a white dove flying in the sky.

,Okay. I found it!'

I took the Bright Light Ascension Chart off the wall. The name '光明昇天圖' (Bright Light Ascension Chart) is written in small letters on the back of the chart.

I tried hard to hold back the excitement filling my chest and turned around to run away from the cabin.

,Was that painting your purpose? Why? Wasn't it just an ordinary painting?'

Ha Seok was standing in front of the door.

,...You're already here?!'

,Keu keu. I knew from the start that you were hiding on the cliff and watching me. Did you think I'd let my guard down just because it's a cliff?'

I looked around. The cabin has no windows, only one entrance.

,Since it's come to this... I have no choice but to fight!'

I took out the Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory.

Hoh. What is that thing emitting light? Seeing you take things out of that little thing, it’s definitely at least a high-grade Dharma tool! Hand it over! I have to have it!

Ha Seok stretches out both hands towards me. A reddish energy is forming in his hands.

,You're old, but you're as greedy as a toad! Die!'

I pretended to slash at him with my sword and kicked the wall right next to him. If there are no windows or exits, I just have to make a new one.

,You bastard! How dare you break my house?!'

Ha Seok swung his palm towards me. A palm strike containing red energy flew towards me.

,Challna (Fleeting Moment)!'

,Keu keu keu. Your movements are quite nimble... but it's a futile effort....'

I heard Ha Seok laughing at me from behind, but I ignored it. Even if I'm annoyed by him, it's hard to defeat him head-on with my current skills.

,There's no need to take the risk. I can just get stronger and kill him.'

If I return to the Shooting Star Sword Sect, Ha Seok can't do anything. The Shooting Star Sword Sect is full of skilled people who can defeat Ha Seok head-on.

,A stupid old man who doesn't even recognize a treasure when it's right next to him. Keu keu keu. I'll use the Bright Light Ascension Chart very well.'

,Hoh.... You say the Bright Light Ascension Chart is a treasure?'

,What abilities does it have that you call it a treasure? I'm curious, so tell me.'

,Fuck.'

[Using Challna (Fleeting Moment). Remaining stacks: 3]

Just now, Ha Seok appeared in front of me, not behind me. Unless Ha Seok can teleport, that's impossible.

Yes. I've deployed the Invariable Formation (不易阵), one of the formations. You won't be able to escape from the formation for the next two hours.

That's bullshit.

Ha Seok is a criminal. If he had any integrity, he wouldn't have become a criminal and be living in seclusion here in the first place.

,Eat this!'

Kwaang!

Ha Seok's voice came from behind.

[Using Challna (Fleeting Moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

Yeongcheon Ryu (Shadow Heaven Style) Yuseon (Stream).

Magic based on evil. In other words, it's a curse.
