Hades accepted Zeus's words to rule the underworld without a fuss. He knew it was his destiny.

The underworld was important.

Every creature except the gods eventually meets the end called death, and they soon descend to the underworld to settle their accumulated karma. The underworld could be said to be one of the axes of the world.

Due to the nature of the underworld, it was not easy to ascend, and there was always a shortage of gods, so he was always busy. Except for those points, Hades had no complaints about the underworld.

Above all, he liked that the gods and humans on the surface were always watching Zeus, but in the underworld, there was no need to do so. In this underworld, Hades was the king.

However, there was something that Hades found hard to bear.

It was loneliness.

The loneliness he felt from time to time was enough to draw a sigh from Hades. Every time that happened, he didn't like the solemn silence of the underworld. He felt a strong desire to have someone by his side.

That day was also a day when he felt lonely. Not only that, but he also felt bored.

,So much time has passed since I became the king of the underworld. Must I continue to exist in this world where no light enters?'

Reality hits him hard.

The wise Hades took a break at times like this. Forcing himself to work would only make him feel disgusted with the work.

When Hades took a break, he looked over the surface. The unfamiliar surface was more interesting than the familiar underworld.

A goddess caught Hades's eye as he surveyed the surface. A goddess laughing with Demeter, who could be called his sister. It was a goddess he had never seen before, but it was not difficult to guess her identity. Just because he was in the underworld didn't mean he didn't hear news from the surface.

,Is that Persephone, the daughter of Demeter and Zeus!'

He also knew that Zeus had violated Persephone on the night she was born. It was already a famous story among the gods. It was not right. But no one could punish Zeus. Because Zeus is the king of the gods.

,...I've come to understand Zeus's feelings. I never thought it would happen... Look at that beautiful goddess. She's as fresh and vibrant as spring, who could dislike her? Demeter also cherishes her daughter.'

He felt greed for the first time in his life.

If Persephone were by his side, wouldn't this gloomy underworld become a little brighter?

Hades imagined Persephone sitting next to him. It suited her very well.

,This is not just for me, but also for the underworld.'

Having made up his mind, Hades watched Persephone.

He wanted to go up and propose formally.

,Would anyone in their right mind come down to this underworld? She would definitely refuse. Above all, Demeter and Zeus, who cherish Persephone, would not allow it.'

It was also difficult to bring her secretly because he didn't have an invisible helmet. But opportunity always comes. Hades continued to look for an opportunity.

An opportunity arose.

As the harvest season came, Demeter became busy. Zeus was so obsessed with women that he couldn't pay attention to Persephone. Hades went up to the surface, kidnapped Persephone, and returned to the underworld.

He prepared a throne for her and proposed, handing her flowers that only bloom in the underworld.

,Oh, Persephone. Goddess of spring. Become the mistress of the underworld and bring spring to this desolate underworld.'

Persephone, who had been kidnapped, did not have a good expression, to put it mildly.

,Hades-nim. Please send me back. The surface is where I belong. Also, my mother, Demeter-nim, and my father and husband, Zeus-nim, will not allow this marriage. It's not too late yet. Please send me back to the surface before they get angry.'

,I cannot do that. You have already entered the underworld, so you must become my wife according to the laws of the underworld.'

,Laws?'

,I am the king of the underworld. You must become my wife. I have just established that law.'

,...Do you know that I am the daughter and wife of Zeus-nim? I love Zeus-nim.'

,You're lying. Well, I understand. Zeus's power is no joke, so you must be afraid. Even if you are not pure, it doesn't matter. I love you. And this love is real. It's different from Zeus's lust.'

Persephone shook her head. To her, Zeus and Hades were the same.

,If Zeus-nim allows it, I will marry you.'

Zeus would never allow it. Zeus never gave his woman to another.

Contrary to Persephone's thoughts, Hades thought it was a simple matter. The source of his confidence was in this underworld.

,Even Zeus cannot interfere with the underworld at will. Because it is not the realm of Olympus. If I go on strike and the order of the underworld is not properly maintained... the surface will have to bear the brunt of the chaos.'

Of course, the underworld would not be unscathed either. But not as much as the surface.

Hades did not contact Olympus immediately. He waited for the right time, taking his hands off the work of the underworld.

,The more chaos the surface experiences, the more advantageous this side will be.'

So he waited for chaos to erupt on the surface, for the dead to overflow and cause problems.

About three days passed.

A message came from Olympus. Hades calmly sat on his throne and received their message. The figures of the Olympian gods appeared in the air.

,.....'

The atmosphere of the twelve Olympian gods was different from what Hades had expected. A big event had occurred, so it should have been chaotic. But it was quiet. The atmosphere of Olympus seemed frozen.

The gods of Olympus were watching Zeus.

Hermes, who had been leading the atmosphere, was quietly silent, and Aphrodite, who had been wearing a seductive smile, stood expressionlessly. Even Hera, who usually didn't care about Zeus's eyes, was silent and watching Zeus. Even Demeter, who should have been the most excited about Persephone's kidnapping, was quiet.

Hades felt uneasy.

,Hades. I'll give you one last chance. Bring Persephone safely back to the surface right now.'

Zeus's voice was filled with murderous intent. A murderous intent that even Hades, who was accustomed to death, flinched at.

,Calm down. Even if Zeus is angry, there's nothing he can do. This is the underworld.'

Hades spoke in a calm tone. He had to be as rational as possible.

,Zeus-isiyeo. I want to marry Persephone. She will enjoy many things as my partner.'

,Are you throwing away the last chance like this?'

,You know that the laws of the underworld and the surface are different. No matter who you are, you cannot influence the underworld. Even if you want war, I will not back down. Also, until you acknowledge my marriage with Persephone, the order of the underworld will also stop. As time goes by, the chaos on the surface will grow. To fix that chaos, you will have to give me the answer I want.'

,Hades. Do you think that's right?'

Hera asked. Hades did not avoid her eyes and answered.

,I have decided to bet everything. Please understand.'

Zeus rose from his seat. Anger flowed from his eyes.

,I have heard your declaration of war. From now on, it's war.'

With Zeus's declaration, the connection was cut off.

Immediately after.

Kuuuuuung.

The ground shakes.

,Is it an earthquake?'

It was something that happened occasionally, so he didn't think much of it.

Kuuuuuung! Kuuuuuuuuuuuuuung!

,It's not an earthquake.'

The direction of the impact is the ceiling. Something from the surface was hitting the underworld.

If the impact is being transmitted to the underworld to this extent...

,The Olympian gods are attacking the underworld. They are reluctant to come down to the underworld directly, so they are protesting in this way.'

Hades chuckled.

,Those who don't even have the courage to come down to the underworld directly. I just need to hold out in the underworld. Then they will give in on their own.'

He was uneasy when he committed the act, but when he thought about it, it was a good thing. Because he could show the greatness of the underworld to the Olympian gods.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The explosion grew louder.

The entire underworld shakes violently. Dirt and debris fell from the ceiling.

,The place where I am is one of the deepest parts of the underworld, even if it's not Tartarus. If the vibration is felt all the way here... what on earth are they hitting the underworld with?'

Kwaaaang! Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The impact is felt all the time.

It was just when he was about to feel annoyed. The lower gods of the underworld urgently sought Hades.

,Hades-nim! It's a disaster! A hole is being drilled in the underworld!'

,Thousands, tens of thousands of souls have already been annihilated! If we continue like this, we will have to worry about the very existence of the underworld!'

,Picking a fight with the Olympian gods was too reckless.'

Hades raised his hand to calm them down. They closed their mouths, but their eyes trembled anxiously.

,A hole has been drilled between the surface and the underworld? What on earth drilled the hole?'

,It's lightning! Zeus's lightning is digging into the underworld and ravaging souls!'

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

Immediately after, lightning fell through the ceiling. The blue lightning discharged and spread in all directions, electrocuting Hades and the other gods.

,Aaaaaaack!'

The gods screamed in pain and rolled on the floor. Hades gritted his teeth and endured the pain.

Kururureung.

The sound of thunder is heard. Hades reached out his hand towards the ceiling and used his power. He blocked the lightning that was falling again. This was only once or twice. Hades sensed that he would not be able to hold out for long.

,This is crazy. Was Zeus's power this great!'

,Hades-nim! The underworld cannot withstand it! We must surrender now!'

,Just hold on for a few days. If we just hold on for a few days, the surface will not be able to withstand it...'

Hades's power that was blocking the ceiling was torn apart, and lightning fell to the floor. The lightning does not disappear and takes the shape of a person. The lightning soon became Zeus. Zeus grabbed Hades by the collar, who was in shock.

,Are you going to continue?'
