Traits: Dullard, Lecher, Purity, Agility. Infinite.」

Just because you're dumb doesn't mean you can't exercise.

In the end, the most efficient thing is to train without trying to be clever.

Me, holding the highest power in Korea, training my body like this might seem foolish. But this is a kind of routine for me. Since a long time ago, I haven't skipped training myself unless there's something really special. No exceptions, whether it's reality or a game world.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

,The power of lightning has gotten stronger. Before, all that happened was that the steel scarecrow moved blandly.'
