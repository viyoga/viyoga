Heist Empire.

The world's only empire.

There were three reasons why the Heist Empire was called an empire: vast land, a population as large as the land, and military power superior to other nations.

But as they say, no nation lasts forever. Now, the Heist Empire was faltering.

The cause was the Kaiser's old age and the absence of an heir. Originally, there was a crown prince, but he died trying to surpass the realm of the 7th level superhuman. After that, the position of heir was vacant, but for some reason, the Kaiser did not declare an heir.

Another reason was the problem of the resistance and rebels seething inside the empire. In particular, there were many rebels in the western outskirts of the empire, because there were many minority tribes in the west who were dissatisfied with the empire. Nowadays, if someone is from a minority tribe, people suspect them of being a rebel first.

The west of the empire, a small village.

The village, which had been full of life until a few years ago, was now filled with a gloomy atmosphere. Jackson, a young man from the village, buttoned up his coat and carefully walked down the village street.

The village was now ruled by a bandit gang. The public officials were in on it too. Taxes had skyrocketed ridiculously, and if you ran into those bastards, you'd get extorted for money under the guise of a toll. If you had to go outside, you had to act as carefully as possible.

,When the hell are the imperial troops coming?'

Jackson gritted his teeth. It had already been three months since the bandit gang, who claimed to be the resistance, had taken over. The internet and phone lines were cut. He couldn't contact the outside world. It was hard to go outside because the bandits were guarding the village.

,I hope Uncle Nicol, who escaped from the village about a month ago, arrived safely in the city...?'

The chances were slim.

Because the imperial troops hadn't come even after a month. He could have been attacked by a monster on the way, or he could have run into another bandit gang. In the worst case, he could have been dragged away by a gang that committed massacres and enslaved him.

,How did the west become like this?'

He muttered in despair. Of course, he knew the reason.

Rebellion.

The 9th Corps, which had ruled the west, rebelled and failed. The problem was that it failed in a half-assed way. The empire didn't have the resources to clean up the west. There were signs of a monster wave in the east. There were signs of a small-scale war on the southern front lines. The north was suffering from abnormal weather. The Kaiser's children were engaged in a fierce power struggle for the position of crown prince. Now, all sorts of problems were erupting in the empire as if they had been waiting for this moment.

Jackson's feet stopped as he headed to the grocery store. He turned his head and was startled to find a woman sprawled in the alley.

,Ro, Rona?!'

It was Rona, his childhood friend. Jackson grabbed Rona's body. It was cold. There was no warmth. Her clothes were torn, and a whitish liquid and blood flowed from her lower body. There was a dent in the back of her head. She had been raped and murdered.

,These, these bandit bastards!'

Jackson was filled with anger. His childhood friend, whom he had talked to just yesterday, was now a cold corpse in an alley. It was when he was trembling, unable to control his anger.

,Who's this? It's Jackson.'

A man appeared from inside the alley.

It was a man with a sword and gun on his hip. A member of the bandit gang that currently ruled this village. The moment Jackson saw him, he felt his anger disappear like dust and fear take its place.

,No, Norman...'

,Ah, perfect timing. Give me some money to buy alcohol. It just so happens that the person who sells alcohol is the boss's younger brother, so I can't just take it... Tsk. You clean up that woman. She's your friend, isn't she? She resisted a bit, but I didn't expect her to die like that... Tsk.'

The bandit approached and held out his hand. Jackson reflexively rummaged through his pockets, then stopped. A small knife that he had brought just in case caught in his hand.

Could he kill him if he ambushed him now? It was a moment of hesitation, feeling murderous intent.

Peok!

Norman's foot kicked Jackson in the stomach. The wallet and knife in his pocket fell out and rolled on the floor.

,A knife? This bastard is trying to kill me?'

Norman, his face twisted hideously, drew his sword from his hip. A sword that was incomparably longer and sharper than Jackson's knife.

,A slave dares to rebel?'

,Yo, you guys are the rebels!!'

,This bastard! The boss said it last time! We're not rebels, we're saviors! Saviors who resist this goddamn empire and save the citizens who are ruled by the empire!'

,Shut up with your bullshit! You're just rebels! Cowardly rebels who are holed up here without even the will to fight the imperial army!'

,Are you dying to die? Alright, I'll kill you as you wish!'

Norman raised his sword high and swung it. But the sword stopped in mid-air, as if caught by an invisible force.

,You said it well. As you said, these guys are just cowardly rebels.'

A man's voice was heard from the entrance of the village. Jackson stared at the sword tip stopped in the air, then turned his head back.

There was an imperial soldier wearing a white mask. His face was covered, and there was nothing on his shoulder where the affiliation mark should have been, but he was an imperial soldier wearing an imperial army cap and uniform. There were more than 20 imperial soldiers in total, and all of them were standing at attention.

Tteok, tteok.

In front of them, the man who appeared to be their leader waved his hand wearing black gloves. Norman, who had been caught by telekinesis, flew to the side and crashed to the ground. The soldiers moved and handcuffed Norman's wrists.

,Imperial army?! Do you think we'll just stand by and watch?! This is our territory! Kaiser's dogs are messing with us?!'

,Quiet. Make him shut up.'

He ordered. The soldiers all took out batons from their waists. And they mercilessly beat Norman.

Peok, peok, peok, peok, peok, peok! They just beat him without saying a word. Norman closed his mouth and curled up on the floor as much as possible.

Norman, who had been subdued like that, was dragged by one of the soldiers and loaded onto a truck at the entrance of the village. Jackson saw Norman, covered in blood, being dragged away like a pig being dragged to the slaughterhouse. Of course, he didn't feel sorry for him at all.

Jackson, who had been blankly staring, suddenly came to his senses and stood up to thank the soldier who had saved him.

,Th, thank you.'

,Forget the thanks. Where's their hideout?'

,It's the biggest building in the center over there. The bandits occupied the village hall, and the bandit boss lives there. But... what's your affiliation...?'

,It's classified.'

,Ah, okay...'

The man walked forward. The soldiers followed him. Cheok, cheok, cheok. The soldiers' momentum, marching in step without a sound, was sharp yet restrained. Jackson swallowed hard and followed them as if possessed.

The villagers, who usually stayed cooped up at home, also came out. They also followed the imperial soldiers like Jackson. Their eyes were sparkling with anticipation.

The man walking towards the center stopped abruptly. Naturally, the soldiers' feet also stopped. The man gestured towards the two-story building. Six soldiers entered the building. In less than a minute, three men who had been in the house were dragged out.

,We're not bandits!'

,He's lying! Those guys are bandits!'

,You're the bandit!'

The leader of the soldiers looked at Jackson. Jackson immediately realized what that gaze meant and pointed to two men pretending to be villagers.

,Those two are bandits! They're traitors who have insulted His Majesty even on normal days!'

,I see. How dare you lie in front of me. Since you're so impudent, pluck out their tongues and eyes.'

The soldiers moved immediately. As if they had done this more than once or twice, they plucked out the bandits' tongues and eyes. It was a mechanically precise and merciless handiwork. Jackson felt fear and thrill at the same time towards the soldiers.

The screams of the bandits who had tormented them were quite sweet.

The soldiers continued to move forward.

The bandit gang was gathered in front of the central building, staging a sit-in. They made barricades with cars and aimed rifles at the soldiers.

The bandit leader with a brown beard shouted at the soldiers.

,Stop! This is your only warning! If you come any closer, we'll shoot!'

The man ignored him and walked forward.

,Shoot!!'

The bandits pulled the trigger. Jackson believed in the soldier. A soldier who used superpowers. There was no way an imperial army officer would be hit by mere bullets. In fact, the bullets didn't work at all.

,A barrier?'

A large barrier surrounded the entire group of soldiers, deflecting the bullets. The bullets didn't work at all.

,He wasn't a psychic, he was a mage!'

Psychics were rare, but battle mages were even rarer.

,Stop! We have hostages here! Can't you see the hostages?!'

The bandit leader brought a woman forward and pointed a gun at her head. A naked woman. Her body was covered in signs of rape.

,You're being annoying.'

The mage muttered. Immediately after, all the bandits stopped. They were caught by telekinesis. The hostages, including the woman, were pushed back, and the captured bandits floated into the air. The mage flicked his hand downwards.

Kung! The bandits crashed to the ground.

,Shibal...!'

Some fainted, and some wriggled and got up. The bandit leader was the latter.

,Judging by the way you're doing things, you're at least a 5th level mage! Why is a guy like you in this backwater?'

,I came to catch traitors.'

,Why is a guy like you doing that!! Wearing a weird mask! Even hiding your affiliation? Are you really an imperial soldier?!'

,What if I said no? Do you think the imperial army would protect traitors?'

,We're not traitors! We're saviors!!'

,Ha.'

The mage just sneered.

The bandit leader drew strength from his body. His skin turned red and his muscles swelled.

,A secret technique from the minority tribes. A technique that temporarily inflates the body. A trash technique that drains your stamina after using it.'

,Trash? It's a technique that will kill you!'

The bandit leader ran towards the mage. The mage used telekinesis. The bandit leader's legs stopped for a moment. But he charged again, emitting a fierce aura.

Kwang!

The bandit leader's fist pounded on the barrier. Cracks began to appear on the barrier. The mage didn't even blink an eye and used magic.

Kwakwakwang!

A streak of blue lightning struck the bandit leader.

[Lightning Strike]

,This little lightning...!'

The bandit leader endured it. Ignoring the smoke and pain rising from his body, he took a step towards the mage.

Kwang!

A second lightning strike fell without warning. The bandit leader's eyes were about to roll back, but barely regained their original position.

Kwang!

The third lightning strike.

The bandit leader couldn't withstand it and collapsed. The mage turned his body. The soldiers grabbed the remaining bandits whose momentum had been broken. The soldiers were merciless. When the bandits resisted, they took out batons and beat them mercilessly.

,Ugh...'

The bandit leader wriggled his body.

The mage approached the bandit leader and stomped on his head, ordering the soldiers.

,There are public officials who have joined hands with the traitors. Catch them all without exception. The traitors' families as well.'

After ordering the soldiers, the mage stood still and looked at the sky. Then, he kicked the bandit leader, who was wriggling, with his hard military boots.

That's how all the traitors were caught in the center of the village.

,How many are there in total?'

,There are 74 traitors, and a total of 167 including their families.'

,Lucky. I got a good haul. Load them all onto the truck.'

,The thing is... there's not enough space in the truck. We'll need to call for an additional truck.'

,That won't do. It'll take too long. Cram them in somehow. If there's really no room, hang them on the truck. Or cut off their limbs and put them in.'

,How about executing some of them here?'

,Traitors have their uses.'

The soldiers loaded the traitors and their families onto the truck and left. Only then did the villagers cheer. The villagers, including Jackson, were more afraid of the soldiers than the bandit leader, so they couldn't even offer their thanks.
