The effect of Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal isn't just about seeing through magic or the opponent's inner power. The insight itself is maximized to an absurd level.

Even if I couldn't read their minds, I could roughly tell what emotions they were feeling.

,Katsuragi and the other Tengu bastards are all scared shitless. It's not an act.'

Do they think I'm stronger than they expected? That's part of it. But that's not all.

I stared intently at Daitengu Katsuragi. Katsuragi flinched and avoided my eyes.

,It's because of Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal.'

Among the abilities of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven, Sun Wukong, the Fiery Eyes Golden Gaze is famous. What if Katsuragi knows about that Fiery Eyes Golden Gaze?

,Ssanghong admitted I was a descendant of Sun Wukong as soon as I used Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal. He had no choice but to admit it. And for these bastards, Sun Wukong's name is... a natural enemy. Something more than that.'

Katsuragi was afraid. Of me. No, of the name Sun Wukong.

,Things are working out. But I can't let my guard down.'

I know this world well.

A world where you get backstabbed if you let your guard down, even with close friends. There are very few people you can trust.

Even if Katsuragi is afraid of me right now, I don't know how he'll act later.

I moved my empty left hand. Yokai energy rose, becoming magic.

Wind-attribute magic. I roughly grasped the principle by watching the Tengu use it. The abilities of Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal and Heavenly Star of Genius created a synergy.

,It doesn't matter if my attribute isn't wind.'

Overwhelming talent ignored the limits of innate attributes.

A small wind blew from my left hand.

The wind grew as it moved and spread throughout the city. Then it gathered back into my left hand. Along with all the poison that had spread through the city.

,This is ridiculous... that magic is the basis of the wind magic we use! We shouldn't be able to use it without knowing the secret!!'

,...There's a legend that Sun Wukong can copy any magic he sees for the first time. Was that legend true!!'

,Hey, hey. Is that real...!'

The Tengu trembled in shock. They were already scared, but they sweated even more while surrounded by the wind.

I focused on the poison gathered in my hand.

,Poison compressed by the wind. It's incredibly toxic. An ordinary container would melt the moment it touched the poison.'

Fortunately, there was a suitable container next to me.

Ssanghong's corpse, the original owner of this poison. Using wind blades, I chopped it up, lifted it with yokai energy, and shaped it into a container. I put the poison into that meat container and sealed it with magic. As a result, I held a poison bomb the size of a grenade in my hand.

It was a bomb with the poison that destroyed a city compressed into it. In another sense, it was a nuclear bomb.

,Even the absolute masters of the Three Jeonggyeong would be helpless against this poison, making it more dangerous than a nuclear bomb.'

A disposable weapon of mass destruction was created in my hand. And the Tengu saw it with their own eyes.

,I keep my promises. Since you accepted the deal, Ssanghong's inner core is yours.'

I threw Ssanghong's inner core to Katsuragi.

,Hwaaaaaaa!'

Katsuragi screamed and used wind magic. Ssanghong's inner core was caught in the wind and stopped.

Ssanghong's inner core was itself extremely poisonous. Anyone unprepared who touched it would be poisoned and die. Even a great yokai would have a hard time without protection.

I put the poison bomb in my inventory and said to the Tengu.

,We need to have a sincere conversation as comrades. Unfortunately, I don't have a place to stay... Let's talk at your place.'

Katsuragi quickly came to his senses and answered.

,W-wait a minute! Can't we just talk here?'

,I'm hungry. If I get extremely hungry, I lose my mind and indiscriminately devour any yokai nearby.'

,There's no food around here... The poison has killed all the creatures and even the plants. B-but my subordinates can get food!'

,I can't eat unless it's in a safe place. Don't tell me... you don't recognize me as a comrade?'

I unleashed my yokai energy and grabbed my iron staff.

,N-no! We will invite you, Seong Yujin, the descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven!'

,You should have done that sooner. I'm too lazy to fly myself, so prepare a mount.'

,A mount?'

,Don't you have mounted warriors flying around you?'

I pointed to the horned Oni Tengu. Their muscles were bulging, and they even had wings. I had no choice but to recognize them as A-class mounted warriors.

The Oni Tengu's eyes flickered with malice for a moment, but they quickly returned to normal when I glared at them.

I headed to Geumhan Island with the Flan as my mount.

• • •

Katsuragi felt like he was going crazy.

His plan was to punish the foolish monkey yokai who had messed with Geumhan Island and save Geumhan Island's face.

But that monkey yokai is a descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven, Sun Wukong. Is it a lie? Look at those fiercely glowing blue eyes. The moment you meet those eyes, you feel like everything about you is being dissected. It's a bit different from the descriptions in the legends, but it was definitely Fiery Eyes Golden Gaze. It seems to have lightning in it instead of fire, though.

,If I attack with the other Daitengu as soon as we arrive at Geumhan Island... No. That bastard has Ssanghong's poison. That terrible poison that even a great yokai can't withstand! If that explodes on Geumhan Island...'

Geumhan Island would be destroyed just like Ssanghong's city was in an instant.

Katsuragi swallowed hard and looked to the side.

Ten Oni Tengu were carrying a makeshift mount, and Seong Yujin was sitting on the large chair on top of it with a languid expression. His drooping face was that of a stupid monkey. But those blue eyes that emitted a cold light made his legs tremble just by looking at them.

Katsuragi suddenly remembered a rumor he had heard about ten years ago and cautiously asked Seong Yujin.

,Seong Yujin-gong.'

,What.'

,About ten years ago, I heard a rumor about a human general with eyes similar to yours. They called him the Lightning General. His name was also similar, Seong Yujin. Are you perhaps related?'

Seong Yujin picked his nose with his finger and answered as if he was annoyed.

,Seong Yujin and I are brothers from the same school. That's why I learned the Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal technique too.'

,...A yokai and a human were from the same school?'

,Actually, Seong Yujin is also a descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven, Sun Wukong. The blood is a bit diluted, though.'

,Ah, a descendant born between the Great Sage Equaling Heaven and Sanzang.'

Seong Yujin paused for a moment and then nodded.

,Well, yeah.'

Katsuragi was definitely convinced. It wasn't surprising that Sun Wukong had human descendants. Just looking at Sun Wukong's actions, he was a famous human-lover. It wasn't uncommon for yokai to be human-lovers either.

Katsuragi watched Seong Yujin carefully. Since he didn't seem to be in a bad mood, he very cautiously opened his mouth.

,Seong-gong. That Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal technique... Could I perhaps learn it too?'

He asked, swallowing his embarrassment. Even a degraded version of the legendary Fiery Eyes Golden Gaze was a very desirable technique.

,You? Cerebral Eye Golden Crystal? Ukkikkikkikki! This is a bloodline magic that only those who inherit the blood of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven can use!'

,If it's bloodline magic, then there's nothing I can do.'

Katsuragi understood, but he felt the urge to hit Seong Yujin's head as he laughed, "Ukkigi." That monkey yokai laughed so annoyingly.

After a while, they could see Geumhan Island. A small island on the sea. In the center was a castle built in the Japanese style.

The sea around the island was unusually rough. Strangely, the waves didn't enter the island.

,Is that a barrier? If you try to enter from a direction other than the designated one, you'll get swept away by the waves.'

,You saw it right away, Seong-gong. As expected, those eyes are amazing.'

,The sound of the waves is annoying. I'll take care of it.'

,What?'

Before Katsuragi could stop him, Seong Yujin threw his iron staff. The iron staff, filled with immense yokai energy, forcibly broke through the barrier and shattered the mermaid statue that was the core of the barrier.

The rough waves calmed down instantly.

,This is crazy. I worked so hard for years to create this barrier...!'

He was about to argue with Seong Yujin, but he held back. He felt like that iron staff would break his skull if he argued.

,He didn't break the barrier just because the sound of the waves was annoying. ...He broke the barrier in preparation for a possible battle. The barrier would have an effect in some way.'

The opponent is a descendant of that Sun Wukong. He must never forget that fact.

,I'll guide you to the reception room first. I'll have my subordinates serve you food. We can talk after that.'

,I scanned the island roughly and saw there are Onna Tengu. Quite a few beauties. Have them attend to me.'

The Onna Tengu had an appearance that was no different from humans except for the crow wings on their backs. They were said to seduce humans, so there were many beautiful women.

,Onna Tengu? Sun Wukong wasn't that interested in women...'

,I'm different from Sun Wukong. I'm a descendant of Sun Wukong.'

,I understand.'

It didn't really matter whether the Onna Tengu were violated by Seong Yujin or not. They wouldn't die, would they? More than anything, it would be even more beneficial if the Onna Tengu became pregnant.

,If Sun Wukong's blood is mixed in...! The Tengu clan will become stronger. Isn't this a blessing in disguise!'

Katsuragi pushed Seong Yujin into the guest room and called the other Daitengu for an emergency meeting.

The other two Daitengu were very uncomfortable.

,Katsuragi! A monkey yokai! And you even brought a great yokai while breaking the barrier? Have you gone mad?!'

Daitengu Kamakura, with a blue face, white hair, and wings, snapped.

,I heard you took my Onna Tengu without permission and ordered them to attend to a monkey yokai? Do you want to fight to the death with me?'

Daitengu Asuka, a beautiful woman with white skin, black wings, black lips, and black nails, spoke in a gentle voice filled with anger.

,Calm down. I can explain everything.'

Katsuragi really explained.

Kamakura and Asuka were stunned after hearing the explanation.

,That monkey yokai is a descendant of Sun Wukong?'

,...You're going to help him attack Baekakseon. Do you really think that's possible?'

,You're misunderstanding something. We don't have a choice. We either help Seong Yujin attack Baekakseon, or we attack Seong Yujin.'

,Wouldn't it be better to attack Seong Yujin?!'

,Seong Yujin is alone. On the other hand, we have three great yokai. If we mobilize our subordinates, we can kill one monkey great yokai.'

Katsuragi shook his head.

,Seong Yujin has Ssanghong's poison. Even without that, Seong Yujin is a descendant of that Sun Wukong. From what I've seen, there's no way to win. It's best to join hands with Seong Yujin.'

,.......'

,.......'

There was disbelief on Kamakura and Asuka's faces. Katsuragi knew that his explanation hadn't resonated with them.

,They won't accept it until they experience it themselves. Idiots.'

Katsuragi ridiculed them. But he couldn't just stand by. His future and the future of Geumhan Island were at stake.

Just as Katsuragi was about to open his mouth again to persuade the Daitengu.

Kwa kwa kwang!

The wall broke, and Seong Yujin appeared. His eyes, flashing with lightning, scanned Daitengu Asuka. He licked his lips with a lewd smile.

,There was the most beautiful woman in Geumhan Island. Ukkikki.'

,.......'

The Daitengu trembled slightly. They all thought of one story at the same time.

The legend of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven, Sun Wukong.

The yokai who opposed Sun Wukong were all beaten to death.

Perhaps they would end up like that today too.
