I dashed out of the building and shivered in the cold.

,Ugh, shit. It's hard to even move.'

I immediately went back inside the building.

If it were my real body, this level of cold wouldn't be anything.

Right now, my physical abilities are similar to or below E-rank based on real-world Hunter standards. Plus, I can't even use mana.

-Yujin. You didn't even grab your weapon. Are you out of your mind?

Na Chaeyeong's voice, coming through the building's speakers, was full of disbelief.

'Right. I can't put it in my inventory. I can put it in, but I can't take it out in this world. I've been using the inventory so much that I forgot.'

I have to carry things like swords and equipment around all the time. I sighed at the inconvenience and turned around to go get my gear.

The cutlass I use in this world, the cold-weather gear I absolutely have to wear because of the weather, and a gun and grenades just in case.

-Choi Hyejin and Irina are on standby too. Should I deploy them?

They were the strongest forces at the current Seongak Elementary School. Except for me, of course.

I can't send them to fight Variants. If the weather was okay, maybe. Sending them to fight Variants in this shitty weather wasn't a good idea. And I needed them to protect Na Chaeyeong in case something happened.

"Leave them be. The Holy Guard needs to gain experience too. And the combat AI as well. But when can I use the combat AI?"

-You can use it right away. It'll probably be much better than elite soldiers. The downside is that it's not good at dealing with variables... And there aren't many combat machines for the combat AI to control. You know that. The resources we can collect are limited. Resources are steadily accumulating, but not enough to produce combat machines.

I can't help it for now.

Seongak Elementary School didn't control all of Seoul.

'It's a matter of time. The seeds of the plan to swallow all of South Korea, beyond Seoul, have already been sown.'

I grabbed my gear and went outside.

The cold-weather gear didn't completely block the chill. But I think I can be active for a few hours. It was hard to even move earlier.

I was wearing a combat helmet on my head, which felt a bit awkward.

-Can you hear me?

"I can hear you fine. But do I really have to wear this?"

-That helmet will also block the cold. Why? Can't you see well?

"I can see fine. The HUD design is pretty good too. But it's a bit stuffy."

UHD popped up on the display. A small map, weather information, heart rate, etc., were being transmitted in real time. Even if I didn't care about the other stuff, the game mini-map was useful.

-The stuffiness can't be helped. Get used to it. I'll put up the navigation, so go as you see it.

The mini-map reacts. I'm not controlling it; Na Chaeyeong is controlling it in real time.

-The blue dot on the map is you. Allies are green, enemies are red. The red triangles are Variants or boss-level enemies. You roughly understand the situation by looking at the map, right?

The Variants are outside the elementary school. They're not moving yet. I don't know why. Thanks to that, I could afford to relax.

"The Holy Guard is fighting well. Show me the video. I should see how they're fighting."

A video popped up on the display.

Holy Guard members, armed with combat helmets, cold-weather gear, assault rifles, swords, and grenades, were overwhelming the Variant Mu Hounds. They calmly fired their assault rifles while keeping their distance, and when the distance closed, two members stepped forward to block and cut them down.

"Processing complete. No injuries."

"Five Mu Hounds in the 2 o'clock direction. They're running this way. Squad Leader-nim. Can I use grenades?"

"It's an empty lot. Use them."

There was no fear in the members' voices. I nodded in satisfaction.

"Good. They're useful."

-We've invested quite a bit in them. It's their first time in a real battle with Mu Hounds, but there are no problems. The real problem is the Variants. We don't know what abilities they have yet. Be careful.

Na Chaeyeong is overly cautious about the Variants. Of course, I understand her. Even well-trained military units have a hard time dealing with Variants.

Running across the snow-covered white field, I arrived where the Variant was. There was a red-skinned dog over 2m in size. It didn't have any fur, so it was amazing how it was enduring this cold.

"Keureureureureu...."

The Variant drooled and growled. White steam flowed from its mouth, revealing sharp teeth.

"Something's strange."

-What's strange? ...You're not being careless, are you?

"Its eyes are strange."

-...You can recognize the Variant's eyes?

Na Chaeyeong asked back. Her reaction was natural. It would look like a normal monster, eyes and all.

I can tell because I've faced all sorts of monsters from different worlds.

"Its eyes are gone bad. Variants and Mutants aren't usually normal, but this feels like it's gone bad in a different way."

-...You mean the Variant isn't in its right mind right now?

"That's how I feel."

-Maybe it's the work of a Player. Some Espers' psychic powers include controlling the minds of Variants.

"Someone deliberately sent a Variant here to attack?"

-It's just a guess. But the possibility is high.

There were more than three suspicious bastards.

First, the looter gang. These guys are criminals. It wouldn't be strange if they did anything. I had cleaned up all the looters nearby, but they keep reappearing like cockroaches.

The Ilwoo Group, with whom I have a bad history. They might have been involved again.

The American CIA. I didn't have a very good relationship with them.

It wouldn't be strange if another enemy suddenly appeared.

'...Anyway. This bastard has been sizing me up since earlier. Why isn't it attacking?'

I kept preparing to counterattack if it attacked. Aiming for a one-hit kill is several times easier than pointlessly grappling.

'Could it be that this dog bastard knows it'll die if it comes in? It's quite clever for a dog bastard.'

If it doesn't come, I'll have to go.

I walk through the snow. Then my leg gets caught in the accumulated snow and sinks in. My balance is temporarily lost. Taking advantage of that gap, the Mu Hound Variant charged.

'Now that I see it, it was playing tricks. Is this its trick? Or....'

The Mu Hound swings its front paw with sharp claws. I quickly regain my balance and block the claws with my cutlass, and with my left hand, I pull out the pistol from my thigh.

'Even if my shooting skills are terrible, I can't miss at this distance.'

Tang, tang, tang, tang, tang!

Special armor-piercing bullets made for Variants pierced its body. It doesn't even flinch and shoves its snout forward. It's clearly strange. No matter how much of a Variant it is, it's still a living thing, so it has to feel pain. But it doesn't even show any signs of feeling pain.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Yeolroe (裂雷).

Lightning extending from the blade of the cutlass split into dozens of strands and pierced the monster's torso. I stabbed the silver-shining blade into the neck of the monster, which seemed paralyzed and stopped moving. Just in case, I immediately cut off its head and killed it.

"It's over. It wasn't a big deal."

-I saw it. We've finished cleaning up here too. Two injured. They weren't hurt fighting, they slipped because of the snow. The injuries are minor.

"So, in short, there's no damage? Good. Not bad. I'm returning."

-Bring the Variant's corpse. The Variant's corpse is a valuable resource.

"Ah."

I paused, about to put it in my inventory. Not being able to use the inventory was really annoying.

Jo Jeonghong, who had been sitting in a chair, opened his eyes. A drop of sweat ran down his forehead. Marcus, a white man next to him, swept back his orange hair and asked.

"How did the operation go? Was it successful?"

Jo Jeonghong's eyes fluttered. He touched his neck with his right hand. He didn't feel any pain.

"It failed. He's not ordinary. Seong Yujin personally stepped in and killed me... no, the Variant."

"The saying that he's the strongest Awakened in Korea isn't just a rumor. What about his forces?"

"There was a force armed to the level of a special forces unit. All of them were elite. They didn't hesitate to face the Mu Hounds."

"We can't break through with just Mutants. This has become troublesome."

Marcus stroked his chin.

He and Jo Jeonghong are members of an American faction called 'Signals'. A faction created by powerful people and entrepreneurs to prepare for the apocalypse.

They had two goals.

Securing Seong Yujin, a healing ability user. Healing ability users are very rare among ability users, so they had to succeed.

The other goal was to ruin the talks between South and North Korea. Many in their faction, 'Signals', didn't like the talks themselves. To be specific, those who would benefit from a Korean War breaking out.

The higher-ups believe that Seong Yujin could be the beginning of the immortality project. In many ways, Seong Yujin is very much needed.

"Still, shouldn't we report?"

"I guess so."

Marcus picked up the communicator. He uses a dedicated communicator to prevent possible eavesdropping.

Afterward, Marcus sighed deeply.

"What's wrong?"

"The summit between South and North Korea is proceeding as planned. They told us to ruin the summit. And to bring Seong Yujin at all costs. Bringing him alive is the best, but... if that's difficult, bring his corpse."

"...Even if we have to kill him? Did they really say that?"

"If Bionix develops the technology, they can create clones. Of course, it's impossible right now... so they're planning to store Seong Yujin's corpse and then create a clone when the technology is complete."

"Is an Awakened's clone an Awakened?"

"No. Most of them are ordinary people without abilities. But very rarely, a clone appears that awakens the same abilities as the original. I think they're aiming for that."

Marcus smiled bitterly.

"It seems like the world has already ended."

"What are you going to do?"

"Is there a choice? I have to do what the higher-ups tell me to do. Fortunately, they said they'll provide support... I have to make sure I don't fail. You're in a worse situation than me. You're a Player, but you're acting like a hunting dog."

"This is right. Espers are the most brilliant class in the early stages of the apocalypse."
