,"Delicious."

Young-hari said while eating toast.

The virtual reality Terra Simulator was surprisingly crude, yet surprisingly well-made.

,'There's a satiety system.'

If I didn't eat like in real life, I would get hungry. If I kept starving, my life force would decrease and I could die, so I had to eat food from time to time.

Objectively speaking, the quality of the taste implemented in virtual reality wasn't bad. But subjectively, the story changes.

,'The taste feels kind of indirect.'

It felt like eating food with a thin condom over my tongue.

Sung-hari and Young-hari were eating the toast deliciously, so it seemed like I was the only one who felt that way.

Crunch, crunch.

I chewed and swallowed the toast. Even if it wasn't tasty, I had to eat it to avoid starving to death.

,'Everything's good, but mealtime is a chore.'

After finishing a simple breakfast, we re-entered the Forest of Bewilderment.

The Forest of Bewilderment wasn't a simple place. Monsters aside, the environment itself was hostile. That's why we had quite a hard time yesterday. But not today.

It was hard to see beyond 10m, but now I could see beyond 100m. There was no longer any worry of getting lost.

「Arinna's Flower exerts its power.」

「The bewilderment of the Forest of Bewilderment dissipates.」

There were no status ailments from the Forest of Bewilderment either.

,'Very good.'

I felt refreshed, unlike when I came here yesterday.

,"Here..."

Sung-hari's reaction was a bit strange. She climbed up a tree and looked around the entire forest. Then, after coming down, she tried to hide her embarrassment and said to me.

,"Yujin-ah. Let's go that way."

Sung-hari pointed to the right. I was taken aback by the sudden suggestion.

,"Suddenly? Did you see something?"

,"It's not accurate yet. You'll know if you go. I hope it's not...."

Sung-hari trailed off and glared at Young-hari. Young-hari narrowed her brows as if she didn't like that.

We moved, following Sung-hari. Sung-hari was very serious, not knowing what she was worried about. The place we arrived at was a place with a large rock. Such a big rock in the forest? It was somewhat out of place.

,"Hey. Move that rock."

Sung-hari called Young-hari.

,"How unpleasant.... I thought if the ajumma-nim told me to, I'd say 'Yes!' and do it?"

,"I'm asking you because you're the strongest here. Stop nagging and do it quickly. You're not going to say it's difficult, are you?"

,"Ajumma-nim can do that much, right?"

,"...I don't have that much strength right now."

Young-hari openly clicked her tongue. She glared at Sung-hari with dissatisfied eyes and placed both hands on the huge rock.

The rock is pushed away. What was underneath was a cave. Looking closely at the rock, there was some strange pattern written on the bottom. It seems that it wasn't originally meant to be pushed away with force.

,"Wow. Mom. How did you know there was a cave here?"

,"......"

Sung-hari glared at the inside of the cave as if she wanted to kill someone.

,"Mom?"

,"Ah. Sorry. I was lost in thought for a moment."

Worry appeared on Sung-hari's face. Normally, I might have just brushed it off. But the situation is different now. This virtual reality feels like it's flowing around Sung-hari. She might have something important.

,"Mom. What is it? You know something, don't you?"

,"...What should I say? This forest, it's a forest I know. I've been here before a long time ago. I couldn't see far yesterday, so I didn't know, but now I know for sure."

,"Where is this?"

,"It's one of the mountains in southern China. I forgot the name of the mountain, but I'm sure."

,"This isn't a mountain, it's a forest?"

,"So at first, I didn't recognize it right away either. But now it feels like they've unfolded the mountain into a forest. The proof is this."

Sung-hari pointed to the cave. She knew, so she found this hidden cave.

,"A long time ago. Um. Before Yujin, you were born, I worked with the Chinese side."

,"Is it related to Guirak Valley by any chance?"

,"You could say it's similar. But how do you know that, Yujin?"

,"A Jiangshi sorcerer came out yesterday. Then it's probably related to Guirak Valley."

If it's Guirak Valley, which can be said to be China's biggest villain group, it's understandable that Sung-hari is stepping up. Moreover, it seems like those bastards are testing with spirits from behind.

,"To be more precise, it's the Corpse Spirit Sect. It's a place that was absorbed into Guirak Valley and disappeared now... It wasn't when I was active. My job at that time was to wipe out the Corpse Spirit Sect. One of their hideouts is here."

Young-hari interjected.

,"Wait. If it's the Corpse Spirit Sect, that's where Wang Zi-xin was. We crushed those bastards without leaving a single one behind? There was no place like this back then."

,"I killed Wang Zi-xin myself too. But I didn't bother touching the Corpse Spirit Sect. I didn't have the justification to do so. But you...."

,"Ah. That's right. Ajumma-nim betrayed the Spirit King, right? I can't understand it. Why did you give up on revenge?"

,"Too many people were involved. Some of them were involved without even knowing it."

,"That's ridiculous. Whether they knew it or not... they have to pay the price for what they did."

The atmosphere becomes tense.

This topic was sensitive. It's because their beliefs clash. Sung-hari, who remained a hero from beginning to end, and Young-hari, who became a villain because I intervened. That incident that separates them was a topic to be avoided.

,"Calm down. We didn't come here to fight each other. Mom, so what's under this cave?"

,"I don't remember the name... but there will be a cadre of the Corpse Spirit Sect. He's a crazy bastard who put all sorts of drugs and necromancy on his body and became a Living Jiangshi."

,"So this is the boss room. Is there a weakness to the Living Jiangshi?"

,"It was hard to deal with because of the aura of death or something. The best thing to do is to drag him outside... but the Living Jiangshi wouldn't come out no matter what I did. At least his back was weak. Should I say the drugs didn't soak in as much?"

Sung-hari said it was hard to deal with in her prime. That alone makes the Living Jiangshi an unexpected monster-level threat. But now we had Young-hari.

,"Let's go down."

We go down into the cave. It smells like corpses.

,'They've implemented this well.'

As we went deeper, a large space appeared. In the center, there was a small pond, and the Living Jiangshi was inside. The Living Jiangshi stared at us from inside the pond and slowly rose. The Living Jiangshi stared intently at Sung-hari and opened his mouth.

,"Sung, Ha, Ri."

The rough voice repeated Sung-hari's name intermittently.

The two Sung-haris frowned at the same time.

,"What's with calling someone else's name so carelessly?"

,"For a dead bastard to call my name as he pleases... how unpleasant."

,"Our, Ri, Ui, Obstruction, Hae, Ja. Kill, In, Da!"

The Living Jiangshi ran. At the same time, Young-hari rushed at the Living Jiangshi first and swung her spear. The Living Jiangshi's unbelievably hard skin wasn't cut by the spear blade. Instead, he flew back like he had been hit by a club and crashed into the wall.

,"Oppa! Use that!"

,"Holy Power! Holy Blessing!"

I used buff skills on both Young-hari and Sung-hari. They drive the Living Jiangshi into a corner. Young-hari with strength, Sung-hari with technique. Apart from not getting along, the two's teamwork was close to perfect.

,'But to withstand the Shuang-hari's teamwork for more than 5 minutes?'

Living Jiangshi.

He was stronger than I thought. The aura of death around him was giving the Living Jiangshi strength. I aimed for the Living Jiangshi's weak point, his back, but I couldn't inflict fatal damage.

,'Did he get an additional buff because it's virtual reality?'

Kwaaaang!

With a loud noise, Sung-hari flew away. She rolled on the floor and shook off the impact.

,"Heal!"

I restore Sung-hari's life force. Sung-hari, who was breathing roughly, smiled.

,"Thank you."

Then she rushes towards the Living Jiangshi again. Somehow, she seems to be enjoying facing a strong enemy. The same goes for Young-hari.

,'But I can't just stand still like this.'

Even if it's virtual reality, I get tired. On the other hand, the Living Jiangshi doesn't get tired. As time passes, the chances of winning decrease.

,'The Living Jiangshi has a brain. A clumsy lure won't work.'

I couldn't rashly interfere in Sung-hari's battle. The level was too high. If I interfered, I would only hold them back. There was a limit to supporting them from behind with buff skills.

,'If I could use an instant, I would jump in and deal a blow.'

Something flashed through my mind. I can't use Instant, and I can't use Full Recovery either. But I can use hacking.

,'Eliminating monsters using hacking was treated as an error and immediately restored. But what if I use it in a different way?'

It was worth experimenting.

I quietly watched for an opportunity.

"Death (死), Life (生), Pass (關), Head (頭)!"

The Living Jiangshi shouts. His power explodes and the Sung-haris fly back. The aura of death fluctuates. I, who was standing apart, even took damage from the aftermath.

The Living Jiangshi jumps from the front. It seems he decided to kill the weakest me first.

,'It's the perfect timing.'

[Hacking successful.]

[Hacking Terra Simulator for 1.1 seconds.]

I draw my sword and swing it at the Living Jiangshi.

What I changed with hacking was the sword's attack power. I changed the sword's attack power to the maximum number I could change it to.

「Hunter's Sword

A sword used by a hunter.

Attack Power +99999

Attack Speed +5%

Status Ailment Bleeding Occurrence Rate +3%」

The tip of the sword touched the Living Jiangshi's chest. That alone was enough.

Virtual reality.

The world of games.

Unlike reality, there was an absolute law, and no one could avoid that law.

The Living Jiangshi died because the tip of the sword touched his body.

As soon as the hacking duration ended, a notification window popped up.

「An unknown error has occurred.」

「Restoring the error.」

「Hunter's Sword

A sword used by a hunter.

Attack Power +30

Attack Speed +5%

Status Ailment Bleeding Occurrence Rate +3%」

The Hunter's Sword returned to its original state. Fortunately, the dead Living Jiangshi was not regenerated.

,'This hacking was 1.1 seconds. Wasn't it 1.3 seconds when I used it last time?'

What does this mean?

,'...Did the Terra Simulator grow? No. In this case, it would be more accurate to say it was upgraded.'

Anyway, it was a good thing that a way to use hacking was created. The problem was the hacking's cooldown. The cooldown follows real-time, so I could only use it once every 90 hours of virtual reality time.

,'First, let's check the items dropped from the Living Jiangshi.'
