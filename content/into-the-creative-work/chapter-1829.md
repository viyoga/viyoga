,Heok...!,

The First Cheonma, Cheon Gal-son, fell to the floor as soon as he entered the new space. He inhaled deeply, mouth wide open. All the pressure that had been weighing him down vanished. It felt like he'd been dragged up from the deepest depths. Ironically, he was in the deepest part of this mental world.

The First Cheonma looked around. And was shocked again. Because there was nothing in this space. Literally nothing. Only a pure white space existed.

The deepest part of the mental world held a person's weaknesses. Mental scars, you could say. At the same time, it was the location of the mind's core, a pure spirit without any pretense. The First Cheonma intended to destroy Seong Yujin-ssi's mind core.

,I knew it coming here. This bastard isn't satisfied with the Cheonma position.,

He would constantly try to become stronger, reaching higher and wider. He had the ability to do so. He'd probably throw away the Cheonma Shin-gyo without a second thought for his own sake. Saying something like, ,Cheonma Shin-gyo my ass.,

The Cheonma Shin-gyo was the First Cheonma's greatest achievement. It was the history he left behind in the world.

,I left the Cheonma Shin-gyo because I couldn't achieve ascension to immortality. Leaving behind my soul in this way is to confirm the existence of the Cheonma Shin-gyo.,

The world said he gave up ascension to devote himself to the Cheonma Shin-gyo, but the truth was different. He lacked the enlightenment and lifespan to ascend. Passing on the Cheonma Shin-gyo in this way was also to confirm the Cheonma Shin-gyo even after death and exert influence.

,It all started when I found that nameless Demonic martial art in the tomb back then....,

He named that nameless Demonic martial art Cheonma Shingong. Even now, he didn't know the origin of that Demonic martial art. All he knew for sure was that Cheonma Shingong was an unparalleled martial art, different in class from other Demonic martial arts.

The First Cheonma, whose stamina had recovered somewhat, stopped his deepening thoughts and stood up.

,I don't need a Cheonma who doesn't respect me. I'll crush that mind core and kill him.,

Then, suddenly, this thought occurred to him. What if he crushed the mind core and implemented his own mind core in its place? Wouldn't he be able to take over this talented body? Without having to wait for the day of resurrection.

,I've never tried it before... but there might be a possibility. Cheonma Shingong has techniques related to the soul. The reason why the souls of the successive Cheonmas weren't dragged to the underworld and could be preserved in coffins was also thanks to Cheonma Shingong.... It's worth a try.,

He blindly believed in Cheonma Shingong. Cheonma Shingong was him, and he was Cheonma Shingong.

He walked forward. He didn't know the direction. How could he know the direction in a place where there was nothing? He just walked forward. Walking backward or sideways was something his pride wouldn't allow.

,......!,

A night sky appeared in the empty space.

Stars were shining. Shining very brightly.

,...The energy of the stars.,

It was only natural, but the energy of the stars also affected the mental world. However, it was a bit unexpected for it to affect it in this way. Because he was born under the Jiamseong (Earthly Darkness Star), he could indirectly feel the energy of the stars.

,Cheongangseong (Celestial Gang Star)! This bastard was born under the Cheongangseong! Such an amazing guy...!,

Cheongangseong, said to be the best of the stars. With the energy of that Cheongangseong, he would definitely have tremendous potential.

However, he couldn't know what the abilities of Cheongangseong were. As far as he knew, there hadn't been a single person born with the energy of Cheongangseong until now.

,If I can only get this body...!,

He would be able to succeed in ascension to immortality, which he couldn't achieve in his lifetime. No, maybe he could achieve something even greater.

The First Cheonma, who was trying to hide his joyful heart, stopped walking forward.

There was a dragon.

It was a black dragon. A black dragon wrapped in chains was coiling and crouching. In the center was a faintly shining bead. It was a Yeouiju (Dragon Ball).

,No, that's the mind core. The dragon is just there to defend the mind core.,

The black dragon. Its figure was magnificent. Its oppressive force was greater than the emperor he had just faced. It was truly a Demonic Dragon.

Fortunately, the Demonic Dragon was asleep.

He suppressed his presence as much as possible and approached the Demonic Dragon. The Demonic Dragon showed no signs of waking up. The First Cheonma didn't let his guard down and maintained his tension as he passed the Demonic Dragon and headed for the mind core.

The mind core floating in the air was a sphere. It was about the size of a head. He focused on the shape of the mind core. A perfect white sphere.

,The mind core originally takes the form of the most meaningful object, but....,

Does this sphere have a special meaning? He felt something was strange, but there was no time to think. He didn't know when the Demonic Dragon would wake up.

The First Cheonma reached out his hand towards the mind core. He felt a smooth texture.

He used Cheonma Shingong. He planned to burst the mind core using Demonic energy and immediately construct his own existence as a new mind core.

It was the moment when the black Demonic energy was about to penetrate the pure white mind core. The world expanded. The world was constantly expanding.

The First Cheonma saw it. In front of it, he was less than a speck of dust.

,......!!,

The First Cheonma was startled and stepped back. Cold sweat poured down his face like a waterfall. He slowly looked around and swallowed hard.

,What was that just now? I feel like I saw something... What the hell did I see?,

The mind core was still floating in the air. The pure white sphere showed off its perfect form.

He reached out his hand again to destroy the mind core. His hand trembled. His instincts were refusing to touch it. His hand couldn't move forward.

,What is this....,

His mind became complicated with incomprehensible events.

-I can feel your agony.

A voice was heard from the Demonic Dragon, who he thought was asleep. The startled First Cheonma turned his head to the side. The Demonic Dragon's red eyes captured the First Cheonma.

,...Were you awake?,

-I've been watching what you're doing, but... you're as expected.

Pagak!

When the Demonic Dragon moved, the chains wrapped around its massive body disappeared.

The First Cheonma, sensing something was wrong, drew his sword from his waist. The Demonic Dragon opened its mouth wide towards him.

He saw the inside of the Demonic Dragon's mouth and his body froze. He realized the Demonic Dragon's true identity.

The dragon's body was merely a shell.

This was Ma (Demon).

It was just pure Ma (Demon).

Kwajik!

Half of his body was chewed by the Demonic Dragon.

,Kuaaaaaaak!,

The First Cheonma screamed. It wasn't just his body being chewed. His existence was being torn apart. He couldn't help but scream. The Demonic Dragon enjoyed his screams and simply soared upwards.

,You bastard!,

He felt the Demonic Dragon's malice. Even though it could have swallowed him in one bite, it deliberately chewed half of him.

-Struggle a little more. Stimulate me more!

The First Cheonma swung the sword he held in one hand. It was useless. The blade couldn't penetrate the dragon's scales. The First Cheonma gritted his teeth. A last resort that he should never use flashed through his mind.

,I have to live! I can't die!,

He desperately erased that method from his mind and activated Cheonma Shingong. And it was taken away by the Demonic Dragon. The Demonic energy was sucked into the Demonic Dragon.

His Cheonma Shingong had succumbed to the Ma (Demon).

,Kuaaaaaaaaaaaak!,

The First Cheonma, without Cheonma Shingong, couldn't do anything.

-It was a unique delicacy.

The Demonic Dragon, having completely chewed up the First Cheonma, continued to rise upwards.

Higher up. To escape from this tiresome place.

Chwareureureuk!

Chains that had risen from the ground wrapped around the Demonic Dragon's massive body. The Demonic Dragon resigned itself to the inescapable restraint.

-...That's what I thought.

Its body was forcibly pulled down to the ground. The chains came out from the mind core. The Demonic Dragon, captured by the mind core, coiled up as it had done before.

-Fucking Cheongangseong. Absolute spirit to chew on.

The Ma (Demon) couldn't do anything. It was just being used.

-Seong Yujin-ssi, you son of a bitch.

Should I just die once and teleport away?

Even if it was just the remnants of those souls, I could use them. I consumed the souls of the Cheonmas to protect my body and attempted to control the massive energy.
