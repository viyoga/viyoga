Ting.

Paeng Cheonsu frowned at Jo Sukyeong's corpse lying in the elevator that had come up.

,So there's no negotiation, huh.'

He looked at the radio in his hand. He could try to negotiate with this. The problem is that Park Taesan would hear all the negotiation details.

,No. The radio is useless. He has no intention of negotiating... If I try to talk, I'll only be mocked. Then my reputation will be ruined. My leadership is already being questioned... I can't allow that.'

Paeng Cheonsu grabbed his disheveled hair with his right hand. Things were going terribly wrong. Now he didn't even know what the problem was.

,Holding out won't work. My subordinates might betray me. There's only one option. To attack.'

Paeng Cheonsu lowered his hand. His eyes gleamed fiercely. The atmosphere grew heavy.

,It's war. Half go down the elevator, half down the stairs. We need to arrive on the first floor simultaneously, so we'll set a time for the elevator to descend. Daemyeong-ah.'

,Yes, Chairman-nim. Tell me.'

,You have the ability to create shields, so take the elevator. Your shield should be able to withstand bullets.'

Paeng Cheonsu knew that guns weren't the problem. Bombs, poison gas, and other weapons of mass destruction were likely installed in front of the elevator on the first floor.

,They could have broken the elevator, but they didn't. Obviously, they're trying to use it as a trap.'

He didn't bother saying that. It would only demoralize his subordinates.

Paeng Cheonsu then divided the elevator team. Seven people for the elevator. The remaining ten would take the stairs. Just thinking about going down to the first floor by stairs made him sigh already.

,We've rested too much. We'll start now. We'll kill those bastards and get our wish.'

Paeng Cheonsu stood up from his seat.

,Wait. Wait a minute.'

Park Taesan, who had been quietly sitting, raised an objection.

,Park Taesan. Go down the stairs. You go first. You have a good sense, so you'll find any traps. If you whine, I'll kill you myself.'

Park Taesan was intimidated by Paeng Cheonsu's murderous glare.

,Midnight. Let's go down at midnight. We have to roll the dice. This time, it really has to hit the jackpot. Midnight's not far off. We have nothing to lose, right?'

,Yeah. It's not far off. And we can roll the dice on the stairs too. Go down. This is my last warning.'

,.......'

Park Taesan swallowed hard and started down the stairs.

Around midnight, Park Taesan, who was sweating profusely, took out the dice from his pocket. He glared at the dice. Paeng Cheonsu, who was watching from behind, clicked his tongue. Paeng Cheonsu's shirt was also soaked with sweat.

,What are you doing? If you're going to throw it, throw it already.'

,Wait. My gut feeling... Yeah. Now's the time.'

Degururu.

The dice that rolled down the stairs stopped. The number that came up was 6. Park Taesan smiled brightly. Yesterday and the day before, 5 and 4 had come up.

,6 is incomparable to 4 and 5. That's it. Things will go well now.'

That's when it happened.

A supply box appeared in the air.

Paeng Cheonsu let out a hollow laugh. Now he didn't even get angry at Park Taesan because he was so pathetic.

,Well. Thanks to you, we'll get some water. I was getting thirsty.'

,Sh, Shit. This can't be happening....'

,Park Taesan. You don't get to drink the water. And give me your gun, you bastard.'

,H, Here it is.'

Park Taesan handed the pistol to Paeng Cheonsu. He had no choice because Paeng Cheonsu's gun was pointed at the back of his head. If he didn't hand over the gun here, he would die. He knew that much without his intuition telling him.

They took a short break and went downstairs.

The end was now in sight. Paeng Cheonsu checked the time on his wristwatch. There was still time before the time he had promised the elevator team.

,Stop. We'll take a little break here. We'll consume all the food and water we brought right here.'

,Yes. Chairman-nim.'

The appointed time was drawing near.

Paeng Cheonsu kicked Park Taesan's back as he sat on the stairs.

,Park Taesan. Go down.'

,N, No way.'

Park Taesan trembled. He couldn't even swallow his saliva and trembled.

He felt a sense of foreboding. It was the first time he had felt such a sense of foreboding since gaining his intuitive abilities. If he went down, he would die. He would definitely die. And he would die even if he didn't go down.

,Go down.'

Paeng Cheonsu's eyes turned cold.

Park Taesan went down. But he stopped on the third floor. He couldn't go down any further. His intuition was fluctuating.

He had to go down now. No. He shouldn't go down now.

Annoyingly, both were ominous feelings.

,Park Taesan. Do you really want to die by my hand?'

,N, No. There's something down there! My intuition is telling me!'

It was a lie. Park Taesan didn't know what was down there. He just said something random because he didn't want to go. The lie worked.

,A trap, huh. It's not surprising at this point. Go down carefully, watching out for traps.'

,Shit....'

He would die if he didn't go. Park Taesan went down as slowly as possible.

Paeng Cheonsu checked his wristwatch. The appointed time was approaching. But he didn't urge Park Taesan. He had originally intended to use the elevator team as bait anyway. Fight together? That wouldn't be bad, but his own safety was the top priority.

And that judgment was correct.

Kwaaaaaaang!

The dynamite installed in front of the elevator and on the stairs exploded. A shockwave occurred, but Paeng Cheonsu's group on the stairs wasn't caught in it. There was less dynamite installed on the stairs.

,Now's the time! There won't be any more bombs! Run out now and kill those bastards!!'

Paeng Cheonsu shouted. His subordinates rushed down. Paeng Cheonsu went down, pushing Park Taesan ahead of him.

Ting.

The elevator that came down opened. Bullets poured out before the elevator doors were even fully open.

We were already taking cover behind the pillars. Looking at the reflections in the broken glass that had been laid out on the floor in advance, there were seven men in the elevator. One was holding a shield, and the man behind him was holding a pistol.

Before they could get out of the elevator, Shin Dohyeon pressed the detonator.

Kwaaaaaaang!

The dynamite installed by Yu Jinmyeong exploded. The one installed on the stairs must have exploded too. Although the quantity was insufficient to blow away the stairs themselves.

,Are they all dead?'

&lt;Paeng Cheonsu and Park Taesan, along with 10 others, are alive. They're rushing out of the stairs to attack you.&gt;

I glanced at the entrance to the stairs. I couldn't see anything because of the smoke.

,But I can hear running footsteps.'

That was enough. I aimed my gun at the direction of the footsteps and pulled the trigger.

Tik. Tik. Tiktik.

It didn't move even after pulling the trigger several times.

,Hyeon Jichan used his ability.'

I had already figured out the ability of Hyeon Jichan, Paeng Cheonsu's right-hand man. Once a day, he could neutralize firearms within 300m for 10 minutes.

I gestured to Yu Jinmyeong. Seong Yujin used a slashing cutter knife. The slash cut through the smoke and even cut through people's bodies.

,Aaaaaaaagh!'

,You motherfuckers!!'

,Uwaaaaaaaah!!'

I could hear the enemies falling. I couldn't tell exactly how many had fallen because of the smoke. The smoke was dissipating quickly thanks to the intact ventilation shaft.

Paching!

Yu Jinmyeong's cutter knife broke and disappeared.

I grabbed a knife and an axe and ran towards the enemies. There were three people standing in the dissipating smoke. Paeng Cheonsu, Park Taesan, and Hyeon Jichan. In Hyeon Jichan's case, seeing him in person rather than in a photo gave off a different kind of force.

,S, Seong Yujin! You son of a bitch!'

Park Taesan threw a stone. I knocked it away with the axe. What was important now wasn't Park Taesan and Paeng Cheonsu.

,Hyeon Jichan. This bastard is no joke. They said he was a legendary gangster. They weren't kidding.'

He was the most dangerous bastard I had ever faced.

,Chairman-nim. I'll take care of this.'

,Jichan-ah! I'm counting on you!'

,I, I'll get you later!'

Paeng Cheonsu and Park Taesan ran away in different directions. I ignored them and focused on Hyeon Jichan. I wasn't alone either. The team members would take care of those two.

,Hyeon Jichan. Why is a guy like you working as Paeng Cheonsu's lackey?'

,Hyung-nim. ...No, Chairman-nim has done a lot for me. And the world isn't just about strength.'

Hyeon Jichan, wearing knuckles, took out a pill from his pocket and put it in his mouth. It was a drug that temporarily increased his strength. He took a stance. It was similar to the basic stance of boxing, but slightly different.

,Hyeon Jichan's knuckles are a game item that increases the wearer's physical abilities. He's already taken a drug, so he must be a monster.'

,Dohyeon-ah! Knife!'

Seong Yujin, who was chasing Paeng Cheonsu with Yu Jinmyeong, took out a Japanese sword from subspace and threw it to me. Unfortunately, her strength was insufficient, and the Japanese sword fell to the ground. I ran towards the Japanese sword.

,I won't let you do that.'

Hyeon Jichan ran towards me with all his might. I immediately turned my body. I had no intention of receiving the Japanese sword from the moment it fell in the middle.

Pajijik.

The lightning axe was enough.

Super Focus was activated, and Hyeon Jichan's movements slowed down. No, they seemed to slow down.

,Is that a jab.'

The plan to take the hit and cut off his neck was discarded as soon as it came to mind. That wasn't an ordinary jab. It was a left jab enhanced by the power of the game item and the drug. Getting hit head-on would definitely be fatal.

I swung the lightning axe. The knuckles and the axe blade seemed like they would collide, but they grazed past each other. Just as I had intended.

,You're electrocuted.'

If I cut off the neck of the electrocuted Hyeon Jichan, it would be over.

About 1 second. That was the time Hyeon Jichan was paralyzed. Before I could swing the axe, he broke free from the paralysis and threw a punch with his right hand.

Super Focus was activated. I tilted my head and dodged the punch. Then came the left-hand punch. How fast it was. Even with Super Focus, it wasn't easy to counterattack. In the end, I created some distance.

,Ah, shit. If I had a knife, I could have just sliced him. I should have prepared it in advance.'

I glanced sideways at where the Japanese sword had fallen. It was about 6m away. The problem was that Hyeon Jichan was closer to the Japanese sword than I was.

,You can see my punch?'

,I dodged it because I could see it.'

,You're the first guy to see my punch accurately and dodge it.'

,If you were so confident in your punch, you should have been an athlete instead of a gangster.'

,I accidentally killed someone during a match.'

,Oh, yeah. Did you buy enough time? But wouldn't it be disadvantageous for you if you buy time? The drug is starting to wear off.'

,10 seconds is enough.'

,I think so too.'

Buying time until his drug wore off was also an option, but it wasn't my style. Hyeon Jichan and I charged at each other with all our strength.

The axe was swung, and punches rained down. Before the axe collided with the knuckles of the punch, I let go of the handle. The axe that was hit by the punch flew away. At the same time, Hyeon Jichan was paralyzed by the electric shock.

1 second.

In that short moment. Instead of attacking, I passed him and picked up the Japanese sword that had fallen on the floor.

Seureureung.

The blade of the Japanese sword that came out of the scabbard shone coldly.

,I just have a sword, but... I don't feel like I'm going to lose. I'm sure I was a Sword Master in my past life.'

Hyeon Jichan frowned.

,The guys who relied on just a sword all ended up as mincemeat by my punches. You're no different.'

,You talk too much. I won't use Super Focus especially for you. I think I can win without it.'

Hyeon Jichan charged at me instead of answering. His footwork became more cautious than before. When I swung the sword, his fist came out to meet it. He intended to knock the sword away with his knuckles. Considering Hyeon Jichan's ridiculous strength, the sword would fly far away.

I used a little technique.

I slightly twisted the blade to deflect most of the force on the fist, and then used the force that I couldn't deflect to swing the sword downwards.

His right thigh was cut. Hyeon Jichan gritted his teeth and swung his fist with a collapsed stance. With his stance collapsed, there was no way he could put proper strength and speed into his fist. I cut off both arms and the remaining leg and stabbed the sword into his solar plexus.
