I became an angel and transcended the limits of the flesh.

It was an indescribable joy. If I had to compare it, it felt like tasting true freedom.

But when I focused my senses, I could feel the sensations of my body. My current state was a contradictory one, where I had a body and yet didn't.

Beliar stopped moving and looked at me, then glanced around. It was as if he was looking for someone.

,"Ha, Han-nam?"

Is he going on about Han-nam again? What an absurd bastard. I spoke to Orsia before fully engaging in the battle.

,"Orsia-ssi. Please protect the Invisible Blade from the aftermath of the battle."

Orsia had suffered internal injuries, but she should be able to handle protecting it from the battle's aftereffects.

Orsia looked at me with confused eyes.

,"...Were you perhaps an angel from the start?"

Couldn't she recognize the 10th-grade magic, Absolute Polymorph? I understand. It's such a high-level magic, and it's not common sense that someone can use such magic easily.

,"No. I'll explain later."

Pajijijik.

I spread blue wings made of lightning bolts. My body floated into the air. I instinctively knew how to use these wings. They weren't so much organs for flying, but more like a construct of power.

Using the power, I rushed towards Beliar.

,"You, nasty bastard...!"

Beliar was horrified and swung his fist at me. I also countered with a punch. I'm an angel now, after all. I judged that with the power of an angel, I could easily block Beliar's abnormal fist.

Kwadeudeuk!

It was a misjudgment. My fist shattered. Beliar's fist was also injured, but it was nothing compared to my completely broken fist.

From my miserably broken fist, a faint light flowed out like blood. It was divine mana, the power of an angel.

The moment I saw it, I realized. The senses that had been heightened by becoming an angel, a higher being, calmed down in an instant.

,'Even if I become an angel, my essence doesn't change. I am not a warrior, but a mage.'

,"Hehehe."

Beliar chuckled and opened his mouth wide. He was full of the intention to devour me.

Pajijijik!

The wings shone and burst with energy. I quickly shook him off and retreated, creating distance.

,"Disgusting bastard...."

A sense of disgust towards Beliar surged. An emotion close to instinct. Perhaps it was because I am an angel now.

I glanced at my broken hand. Divine mana was still flowing out.

Divine mana.

Mana processed through faith. Divine mana is mana that can only be obtained by having faith or being born with a special lineage. In this case... it's close to both. It goes without saying that an angel is special, and I trust myself.

,"Heal."

I used 3rd-grade divine magic. My broken arm returned to normal. It seems like an amazing magic, but... in the case of Heal, it doesn't work on subjects with severe injuries, and it has the disadvantage of consuming the target's stamina.

,'I didn't even create a proper formula. Yet I used divine magic. ...Divine magic used without a formula. Can this be called magic?'

Is this how angels are originally? Or am I special? Either way, this power is a great help to me.

,"You, feel bad."

Beliar put strength in his knees and jumped up at me, who was flying in the sky. I flapped my wings and dodged the attack to the side. Immediately after, it was the moment I was about to gather divine mana in my hands and manifest it as magic for a counterattack.

,'...Mana is insufficient.'

It couldn't be helped. Even before polymorphing into an angel, my mana was scraping the bottom. Becoming an angel is rapidly restoring mana, but... it was still insufficient.

,'Beliar eats creatures and recovers strength and stamina. Is it impossible for me as an angel?'

The creatures I can devour are... Orsia and the Invisible Blade. No matter how urgent it is, there's no way I would devour them.

,'Does the target to be devoured necessarily have to be a living thing?'

What caught my eye was the lightning wings that were constantly extending from my back. I raised my head again. I can see the power lines in the broken wall. It's the power of the Floating Island that I used when I used [Thunder Break].

,'It seems like I can use it as energy.'

I dominate the flowing electricity.

I pull the current from the power lines and absorb it into my wings. Then, I suddenly realized one of the abilities I had.

Energy Absorption.

The ability to absorb the life force of a target to restore one's own life force and mana.

,'It can be said to be a similar ability to Beliar eating humans.'

Beliar did not give up the attack. He bounced off the floor, walls, and ceiling like a bouncy ball and rushed at me.

I prioritized absorbing the current and dodged his attacks. Unavoidable attacks were blocked by deploying a barrier.

,"Holy Barrier."

The barrier deployed with divine mana blocked Beliar's attack.

,"Bbibik! Electricity thief! Stop stealing immediately!"

,"Shut up. I am a special Han-nam. I have the right to use the electricity of the Floating Island."

,"Bbibik! You are not Han-nam but an angel! Angel Eugenius! Stop stealing electricity!"

Ignoring the words of the artificial intelligence, I continued to absorb electricity. A generator must be constantly running somewhere on the Floating Island, so there's no chance that the Floating Island will sink into the sea because there's no electricity.

,"Keueueueu...."

Beliar gnashed his teeth as he failed to inflict meaningful damage on me. Then, suddenly, his gaze turned away from me and towards Orsia. Beliar smacked his lips.

,'He's targeting them? No way.'

I stopped absorbing power and pointed at Beliar.

,"Holy Thunder."

The golden lightning that stretched out from the tip of my finger struck Beliar. In terms of pure power, it's not even as good as Thunder Bolt. But this lightning was lightning made of divine mana.

,"Kuaaaaa!"

Beliar screamed in pain and rolled around on the floor.

I flew towards him at a rapid pace. Covering my whole body with divine mana, I swung my fist at him. It's a body strengthened with divine mana. It wouldn't break as easily as before.

,"Holy Thunder! Holy Punch! Holy Kick!"

I poured holy attacks on the demon. The attacks were effective. The powerful Beliar acted like he was allergic to divine mana.

Occasionally, Beliar's magic bounced off and attacked me, but the frequency was low.

,'Just as divine mana is the opposite of demons, magic is a dangerous power for angels.'

Still, I was at an advantage. Beliar is not intelligent enough to use his power effectively.

Peok! Peo-eok! Peok!

I relieved my stress by unilaterally beating him.

,'Shall we finish it off soon?'

Pajik, Pajijijik.

Golden lightning gathered in my grasp and took the form of a spear. A spear of lightning made with a lot of divine mana. This holy lightning spear should be enough to kill Beliar.

I was about to grab the completed spear and stab it into his heart. Kwang! Magic exploded from his body and pushed me away. I balanced myself in the air with my wings and swung the lightning spear, but he jumped upwards and escaped out of the ceiling.

,'The bastard who was rushing in head-on ran away?'

Did he suddenly gain reason as death approached? Leaving the question behind, I soared upwards to chase him. It would be safer for Orsia and the Invisible Blade to stay here.

Coming out above, I checked the Floating Island, which was in a mess.

Collapsed buildings, roads full of debris, and scorched gray parks.

A scene of a destroyed city was here.

,'Where did he go?'

Pajijik.

The lightning wings flapped once. Electromagnetic waves spread in all directions, scanning the Floating Island.

I sensed Beliar. And it wasn't just him that was detected.

On the outskirts of the Floating Island, the presence of thousands of people rushing towards this way was detected.

,'The company's forces that the black magician mentioned.'

Beliar hadn't run away. He moved to eat humans, recover stamina, and gain strength.

,"It won't be as you wish."

I flew towards where Beliar was.

• • •

The total number of troops from the companies dispatched to the Floating Island was 1,783. It's not soldiers dispatched from one company. They formed a temporary alliance to obtain the ancient ruins of Great Korea. Their goal is to annihilate the enemies and obtain the rights to the Floating Island.

Five airships with identification numbers erased landed on the outskirts of the Floating Island.

,"They poured in a round of missiles. At this rate, will there be anything left?"

,"It's a floating island. According to the report, it's called the Floating Island. Just obtaining this island is a profit."

,"The advance team was annihilated. The enemy is estimated to be a 7th-grade space-type magician. His companions are a 6th-grade magician and a 6th-grade mercenary warrior. They must be exhausted from the continued battle, but don't let your guard down."

,"There's a rumor that the 7th-grade space-type magician belongs to the High-tel School?"

,"So what? They're going to die here anyway. Besides, this place is too far from Neo London. Neo London won't be able to conduct a proper investigation. Advance. We just need to do what we have to do."

What stood in their way was a demon with horns. He rushed in without warning, grabbed a soldier, and chewed him up.

,"Demon appearance!!"

,"Maintain formation! Even a demon can't withstand a large army! Furthermore, the demon is currently injured!"

,"Airship! Prepare for bombing! It is estimated to be at least an 8th-grade demon! We must kill it even if we have to blow up part of the island!"

,"What is the type and name of the demon? Can we at least know which great demon's subordinate it is?!"

,"I don't know! There is no information! We have no choice but to respond physically!"

They poured special weapons and other heavy equipment on the demon. There were soldiers around the demon who couldn't escape, but the situation was too urgent to care about them one by one.

,"Damn it. It's not working. What about the airship?!"

,"Airship, converting to bombardment form! It takes 70 seconds!"

,"70 seconds... it's bearable. Deploy a force field! Overlay a barrier and build a steel wall!"

Kwareureureureung! Kwang kwang kwang!

Lightning struck from the sky, sweeping away the troops. More than 30 soldiers turned to ashes without being able to do anything. Kwakwakwang! The lightning continued to strike. They identified the being that appeared with the lightning.

The thing wearing a coat was wearing a hood, but the hood was empty. The most impressive thing was that the wings made of lightning on its back were flashing. Also, a white halo rotated above the hood, emitting a faint light.

,"A faceless angel...?"
