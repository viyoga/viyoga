This dungeon isn't just filled with mages.

A dungeon is a dungeon. Mages are a type of named monster, and there are also monsters called mobs.

,Shasseueueueu,.

A low, crawling breath.

Kukuru and I stopped moving and turned our gaze towards the sound. Cracking through the bushes, a Lizardman appeared, with the appearance of a mix between a lizard and a human.

In the distant past, they were treated as monsters, but now, like elves, they are classified as one of the demihuman races. However, their numbers have drastically decreased in modern times, so they have been designated as a protected species. A kind of natural monument.

,It's a pretext to extract Lizardman factors stably, not to protect them by talking about human rights....,

Lizardmen have superior bodies and mana affinity compared to humans. However, their average intelligence is lower than that of humans, so they are more suited to shamanism than magic. If a human accepts the Lizardman factor, the disadvantage of intelligence disappears.

,If I catch them and sell them on the black market, I can make a fortune that would change the life of an ordinary person.,

,Shasseue.... Human... kill...!,

The Lizardman's hostility and murderous intent are directed at me. Kukuru's existence is being ignored like a backdrop. This is because both of them are bound to this dungeon.

The Lizardman runs. A larger build than a human, a thick tail, and a spear made of iron tightly gripped in his hand.

[Wind Cutter]

A level 2 wind magic is activated. Invisible blades of wind rush towards the Lizardman.

The Lizardman raised both arms. He felt the invisible magic. The blades of wind reached the Lizardman's wrists. Scratches appeared, but that was it. It couldn't cut him. Not a single drop of blood came out. The Lizardman's durability is as expected.

,Even if he strengthened his body using mana, it's a bit shocking that there's no damage at all.,

Wind Cutter is a level 2 magic, but the caster is me, a level 6 mage. Even the same magic can vary greatly in power depending on the level of the mage.

,The physical durability must be at least level 4.,

Everything else is crude. Movement, speed, etc. With this, I can attack continuously.

[Wind Cutter]

Another blade of wind sent flying. But the result was different from before. It cut off the Lizardman's wrists and beheaded him.

The Lizardman's corpse fell to the floor with a thud. Kukuru's eyes sparkled as he looked at the Lizardman's corpse.

,He added other magic to the Wind Cutter. It seems like he slightly modified the magic formula... If it were another element like fire or electricity, it would have been immediately visible. What did he add to the Wind Cutter?,

,Telekinesis.,

,...That trash magic? Why bother...?,

,The biggest advantage of Wind Cutter is that it's invisible. Even if you feel the mana, you can't help but be slower than seeing and reacting. If you mix fire and electricity, the power will increase, but it will become too flashy and you'll be able to see and avoid it. And just because it's telekinesis doesn't mean it's weak.,

,Indeed. I guess even dog poop has its uses. But I don't want to use telekinesis magic. It's more beneficial to use other magic than that. Telekinesis consumes too much mental power compared to its effect. Honestly, I still find it hard to understand. You're a mage, so you must know how important mental management is for a mage.,

Telekinesis is a magic that is activated with a ratio of mental power 9 to mana 1. Even if you increase your proficiency in telekinesis, the mental power consumption remains the same. After using telekinesis once, your mind becomes fatigued, so mages don't use it often, to the point of hating it.

I use telekinesis often because I don't have any mental power consumption at all. It's convenient in many ways. But even if I say this as it is, Kukuru will think it's a bluff.

,It's a matter of taste.,

,Geez, what a taste.... Even a mage like you has flaws.,

I barely resisted the urge to smack Kukuru on the head.

Kukuru approached the headless Lizardman corpse.

,Undead Rise.,

He chanted. His dark mana seeped into the Lizardman's corpse. The severed head and wrists reattach. The corpse propped itself up with its hands on the ground. It gripped the spear as it had done in life.

,You know what? The Lizardmen here weren't eaten by Transcendents. They are actually alive and are residents living in this dungeon.,

,I know that the Transcendent Belk is from a Lizardman, not a human. Living here means... they weren't devoured, but made into subordinates?,

,That's right. Mages who have been eaten by Transcendents are obligated to protect Lizardmen. Of course, I'm an exception because I control myself.,

,Is this a paradise for Lizardmen created by Transcendents?,

,Paradise? Keu keu. Absolutely not. What paradise when demihumans with intelligence similar to humans are gathered in one place. I've seen them kill each other in war so many times. And the strongest guy takes power. These guys are just frogs in a well, fish in a tank.,

He sarcastically ridiculed the Lizardmen.

After that, I moved following him.

Several Lizardmen appeared. As soon as the Lizardmen saw me, they attacked me unconditionally. Since they were trying to kill me, I counterattacked and killed them too. No need for conversation.

,The Lizardmen's equipment is getting better and better. At first, there were guys without any armor, but now they're wearing light armor.,

It meant we were going in the right direction. Kukuru turned every Lizardman corpse into an undead. There were over 11 Lizardman undead following him.

And then I found a mage.

,It's cold. It's too cold.... It's cold, so I need to start a fire....,

As if it were natural, the insane mage cast Fire Tornado as soon as he saw me. Flames rotated and soared into the sky. That tornado sucked everything in, rotated, and slowly approached me.

If I hadn't grabbed my body with telekinesis, I would have been sucked in and burned to death.

Fire Tornado is a level 5 magic, but the magic that mage unleashed was at level 7.

,The magic and the mage are connected. He's manipulating it to maintain the magic. Then the answer is to target the mage.,

[Chain Lightning]

A lightning bolt extending from the tip of my finger flew towards a tree. The lightning bolt burned the tree and bounced off to another tree. It did that several times. The final destination of the lightning bolt was the fire mage.

The lightning bolt had weakened as it bounced off continuously, so it couldn't kill the fire mage in one go.

,But the Fire Tornado is cut off.,

In the forest where ashes were scattering, I cast magic on the fire mage who was unable to move due to being electrocuted.

[Telekinesis]

I grabbed his head with telekinesis and twisted it.

It usually doesn't work. Especially mages have high magic resistance, so telekinesis doesn't work at all. Not to mention if they are at the same level 6 as me.

,But he's been electrocuted and he's not in his right mind. He's casting magic, but he's close to an empty shell without substance.,

The mage's clothes and corpse disappeared and a golden light appeared. The light flew quickly and seeped into my head.

,What magic did I get this time?,

,...Fire Mushroom.,

I used the magic directly.

A 30cm red mushroom was created in the air. The red mushroom bounced and moved forward.

,Hehe. What a cute mushroom.,

The red mushroom exploded at a suitable point.

Kwaaaaaaang!

10m in all directions are swept up in the explosion and devastated. Kukuru, who was giggling, stopped laughing.

,A moving bomb, huh. It could be quite useful depending on the situation. Judging by the burning ground, even if you withstand the impact, the fire will stick to you. Unlike its cute appearance, its killing power is disgustingly high. Especially, it's not compatible with the undead. What level of magic is it?,

,Level 3. It's a fire-type esoteric magic.,

,Not Arcane, but esoteric magic. Certainly, it's worthy of being called esoteric at this level.,

Just because it wasn't Arcane didn't mean esoteric magic was weak. Why else would they secretly define magic?

,I like esoteric magic that I can understand the principles of and control 100% more than Arcane magic that I can't understand the principles of.,

After that, I killed 3 mages with Kukuru's help. Unfortunately, all I got were ordinary magic spells. The number of undead following Kukuru exceeded 100. I was a little nervous because I didn't trust Kukuru.

,I've been saving as much mana as possible while coming here. If worst comes to worst, there's also Leap... Even if he suddenly betrays me, I won't be caught.,

,I've killed a total of 5 named mages, so now only the boss is left.,

The named mages that appear in this dungeon are random. But the dungeon boss doesn't change.

,Now, we perform the final ritual at the altar. The altar is in the center of the Lizardman city. Lizardman shamans and soldiers will interfere. I'll block them, so you finish the ritual in the center of the altar. With that ritual, you will become level 7, and I will be freed from the dungeon.,

Each other's goals are achieved. If Kukuru's goal is really liberation, that is.

,Do you need a rest?,

I sucked down an expensive mana potion from my inventory.

,I'm good. I have plenty of energy left. Let's go right away.,

I realized the anomaly after getting closer to the city.

The city was empty. Of course, there were a few Lizardmen standing guard. However, there were far fewer of them than expected.

Kukuru was lost in thought for a moment, then burst into hearty laughter.

,Hahahahahahaha! This is lucky! You said there would be guys following you into this dungeon! It's those guys! The Lizardmen have all gone to kill those guys because they came with considerable power!,

,Then we need to hurry. Whether the Lizardmen win or the guys who chased me win, they will definitely interfere with me.,

,That's right. Let's move when time is on our side.,

It wasn't a bloodless entry. The remaining Lizardmen were killed by magic.

I took a look at the stone houses and roads before using magic.

[Fire Mushroom]

The red mushroom that popped out of the air bounced and settled in one of the streets. And then it became transparent.

,Hmm? Did it also have a transparency function?,

,Instead, it can't move. It explodes when someone approaches. It's a kind of landmine. I can't maintain it for long... The maximum limit is 1 hour for now.,

,1 hour should be enough.,

I planted mushrooms all over the streets.

Suddenly, I remembered my past life. A game I used to enjoy. I was a jungler who planted mushrooms in the jungle of that canyon.

First, I would go to the enemy jungle to steal camps and plant mushrooms, and the thrill when the enemy jungler stepped on the mushroom.... I still remember it vividly.

,...That's in the past.,

Anyway, I was able to reach the pyramid-like altar in the center of the city without the dungeon boss's interference.
