Entering the operating room, I tensed up and looked around.

It was definitely not a typical operating room. The equipment commonly associated with operating rooms was absent. There wasn't even a scalpel.

,There's no need to be nervous. It's called an operating room, but... it's not like we're going to be tearing flesh with a scalpel or rummaging through your insides."

,How do you restore a mana road? I'm not asking you to teach me the secret art. I just want to know the general method of restoration."

,A mage's mana road isn't like a physical organ or nerve. While black mages have a special organ called the Dark Hole, ordinary mages don't. It's more like an expansion of the mind... creating a mana road, which can be considered a virtual nerve, centered around the astral. You know this better than I do."

,Some people claim that mana roads are mental nerves."

,That's not exactly wrong. The mana road itself is closely related to the mind."

Opening the astral and utilizing the mana road are both acts of will. It wouldn't be strange to call it a mental nerve.

,Does restoring a broken mana road... mean you can also create mana roads?"

,It's different. Your mana road is broken, but it hasn't disappeared, has it? What I'm doing is restoration. It's not creating something from nothing, but rather creating a path using the traces of the original mana road as a blueprint. To put it in terms of buildings, the foundation is already laid."

,I roughly understand. Restoring a mana road... does that mean you're messing with my mind?"

,It's not messing with the mind itself. It's more like working on the branches extending from the mind. Why are you getting scared? You can quit if you don't want to."

It was unsettling, to be honest.

In the original game, it was over in a flash, but... this isn't a game.

But the mana road must be restored to become even stronger than it is now.

,I'll do it."

,Lie down on the operating table."

I lay down on the operating table. I stare at the lights on the ceiling.

A familiar shitty feeling creeps up. I remember when I was injected with mana essence. Without realizing it, I clenched my fist tightly.

Lana Gomez manipulates the machines next to the operating table. The machines quietly start up. She attaches two electrodes to my forehead and temples.

,What are you doing?"

,It's a machine that stabilizes the mind. In simple terms, it's anesthesia. If your will affects the mana road during mana restoration, it will be difficult to restore. Take a nap. Then all 16 of your mana roads will be restored."

,......."

I closed my eyes. But it didn't feel like I was being anesthetized.

,Your mind is excessively tense. I know your mental barrier is excellent, so relax. Open your mental barrier and accept the waves. And sleep. You must be tired since you came straight after work."

I relaxed my body.

I decided to relax. I closed my eyes tightly and prepared to accept whatever mental wave she was talking about.

Then I felt something trying to enter my head through the electrodes. I knew this was the mental wave she was talking about.

,I can feel it now. Is the mental wave weak? Can I be anesthetized with this?"

I wondered.

I thought it would be more reliable to be anesthetized with drugs.

,It's troublesome. Usually, they pass out in 10 seconds.... Don't think complicated thoughts. Slowly. Focus on your breathing. Accept the mental waves."

Lana Gomez's flat, monotonous voice began to sound comfortable. I started to feel sleepy. This was probably normal. As she said, I focused on my breathing and fell asleep.

My consciousness sinks into the water. It's still water with a good temperature. It's as languid as being in a swimming pool. But just because I'm in the water doesn't mean I'm completely cut off from the outside. I can faintly hear voices from outside.

,You're here. You're late. Quickly...."

,Mom. This is... real...."

,...Believe. ...Help...."

,The mage is... Kabbalah...."

,Start...."

Words heard intermittently.

Among them, there was a word that grated on my nerves.

Kabbalah.

Kabbalah Industries.

That damn company that treats bio-experiments like meals. Kabbalah Industries is one of the companies I least want to get involved with in this world.

,...Why is the name Kabbalah coming up?"

I felt my sunken consciousness rising to the surface. I didn't open my eyes. My consciousness became clearer, and I remembered that I was currently restoring my mana road. If I fully awaken here, the restoration might fail.

Instead, I listened to the sounds coming from outside. Their voices were clearer than before.

,Ah, shit. 16 mana roads are broken? It's such a pain in the ass!"

,Mila. Focus on assisting."

,It doesn't really matter. Just by looking at him, it seems like his mana road was damaged due to overload... This is a common customer for Mom, isn't it?"

,He's not a common customer. I've been running this shop for decades, and I've never seen a mage like this. His mana affinity potential is at least level 9, and his mana sensitivity is also above level 8."

,What, that's just sweet talk, right? There's no way there's a monster with that kind of talent."

,Isn't he right in front of you? I've already contacted Kabbalah. We'll sell him to Kabbalah and go to the Freeseel Kingdom."

,You know I've sold my entire career to follow Mom. You have to keep your promise."

,It's not me who has to keep the promise, it's Kabbalah. And Kabbalah will keep their promise. 10 billion credits and a noble title in the Freeseel Kingdom will be nothing to them."

,This guy is so pitiful. How did he end up with a woman like Mom...? Is this the tragedy of a magical genius? Mom killed a future grand mage."

,Stop talking nonsense and focus on your work. We need to restore all the mana roads to get the right price."

,Okay, okay. Stop nagging. The Blue Distortion gas injected into mana road number 8 has stabilized."

,We just need to calibrate mana road number 8. You can do that much, right?"

,I may have quit this job for a while, but I've been assisting Mom for over a dozen years. This is a piece of cake."

,How's his mental state?"

,Why? Are you scared he'll wake up?"

,His mental strength is not normal compared to other mages. It took him over 2 minutes to fall asleep after being hit by the mental wave."

,Hee. So his mental talent is as good as his magical talent? His mind is stabilized. Why are you so worried when the mental wave is set to maximum output?"

,We need to ensure safety just in case. I'm starting to restore mana road number 10."

I barely managed to hold back my rising anger.

I understood what this mother and daughter were trying to do from the context of their conversation.

They were trying to sell me to Kabbalah Industries.

,Shit. This is shitty."

I trusted Lana Gomez.

I trusted her, who had been working here for decades.

,No, actually, I trusted Lana Gomez, the NPC in the game. She had never betrayed the player even once. I didn't even know she was connected to those Kabbalah bastards."

I was getting various benefits by using my game knowledge, so I mistakenly thought I knew everything. It was arrogance.

I shouldn't have done that.

The information I know is only a small part of this world.

,I want to kill these bitches right now, but...."

These women were restoring my mana road to sell me for a good price.

,...I'll wait until the mana road is fully restored."

Fortunately, these women had no idea that I was half-conscious.

,I can feel the waves, but I can shake them off whenever I want. This is the maximum output of the mental wave? Mental waves are nothing special."

I quietly waited for the restoration of the mana road to finish. It was quite a test of patience.

Only the news that the broken mana roads were being restored one by one made me happy.

,It ended faster than I thought?"

,I restored the mana road easily because of his high mana affinity and mana sensitivity. I never thought that there wouldn't be a single rebound."

,I was surprised too. Usually, a rebound occurs about 5 times when restoring 1 mana road...."

,Mila. You stay here and watch him. Someone from Kabbalah is supposed to come in 3 hours...."

,Is it an artifact containing Blaze magic? It's a compressed firing method. It would have been quite threatening if it had been activated."

But they couldn't penetrate my barrier. It was a barrier that I had carefully created using 50 mana roads. It's not a barrier that can be penetrated by mere low-level magic bullets.

[Electric Field]

,Just beg me to save you, you bitch."
