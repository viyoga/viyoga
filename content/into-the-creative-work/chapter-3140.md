—Yuria. You're contacting me directly? I thought you said you'd be working at Buckingham Palace for a while.

Invisible Blade's voice came through the phone light and casual. Yuria could tell she was in a good mood. Even for a ninja, Invisible Blade was honest about her feelings, and Yuria had figured out that on good days, her voice pitched just a little higher.

"Unlike at the Maid Academy, I have relatively free time at Buckingham Palace. This time, I'd like to entrust a request to you, Shizuka."

—Perfect timing. I was just bored anyway. Since it's your request, I'll do it for free. I owe you a lot. It's not like you had nothing to do with me reaching Grade 7. And even if that weren't the case, I'm a maid of the Meyer estate now. If you give an order, I follow it.

"I'm aware you run contract work on the side. Precisely because it's that kind of job, I should make sure you're properly compensated."

—If that's what makes you comfortable, fine. I'll charge you cheap. Schedule's clear. Ever since I hit Grade 7, the direct requests have dropped off surprisingly. Guess my price went up?

She wasn't an ordinary mercenary. She was a ninja—a professional assassin. Hiring a Grade 7 assassin was burdensome for clients in every way. If possible, not getting tangled up with one was for the best.

Yuria kept walking even during the call. Every time her footstep landed on a shadow, the scenery around her shifted in sweeping flashes. Despite people being right there in the open nearby, not a single one noticed her presence.

"Are you familiar with Viscount Morland?"

—I know him. He's the president of a cosmetics company. The one the Queen favors—Layered! You want him dead?

"If Viscount Morland's corruption is confirmed, he needs to die. Put in a request with Rose to verify information on Viscount Morland, and if corruption is found, eliminate him."

—What company in this city doesn't commit corruption? Dig into any of them and it's a hundred percent. So that viscount fell out of the royal family's favor, huh? Did he insult the crown or something?

Invisible Blade seemed to have grasped the situation from just a few clues.

"Who knows. I'm not privy to the details. What's certain is that he's fallen out of the Sun's favor."

She couldn't have cared less.

She had a complete grasp of the personal information of every important figure in Neo London. Viscount Morland wasn't on that list. He was at a level where even if he vanished from the city this instant, there'd be no ripple effect. No influence on the LK Group or the Meyer Count household either.

—Got it. I'll move now. Can I use the budget however I want? Rose is pretty damn fussy when it comes to intel.

"Yes. Use the budget as you see fit."

—I'll have it handled by tonight. So what are you planning to do while I clean up? Judging by the call, doesn't sound like you're heading back to the estate.

She couldn't return to the Meyer Count household. If she went there, the royal family's eyes would follow a hundred percent.

"I intend to handle LK Group matters. I've heard there are those recently sticking their hands into the LK Group."

—Bugs swarming treasure is only natural. But how did you find out, Yuria? Getting intel from the royal family isn't easy.

"That's a secret."

—Well. If it's you, Yuria, you must've found a way. Can I ask you one thing?

"Yes. Go ahead."

—Why do you gather intel on Alphatier so intensively but never make a single move against them? If you're going to stab, you could stab clean.

"Since it's you, Shizuka, I suppose you can know. You're aware the Master holds a grudge against Alphatier, aren't you?"

—How could I not? I don't know the details, but his expression hardens the moment Alphatier's name comes up. But he doesn't do anything directly to them. And you haven't lifted a finger either.

"The pleasure of revenge belongs to the Master. I would never dare steal the Master's pleasure."

—…I don't get it. Isn't revenge something you'd want to achieve even if you had to mobilize everything you have? I figured you, of all people, would exact his revenge by any means necessary.

"By common sense, yes. Fufu. Someday, Shizuka, the day will come when you understand the Master too. I'll end the call here."

Yuria handled the matters related to the LK Group. Most were affairs difficult to conduct through official channels.

LK-99.

Superconductors.

There were far too many coveting the treasure that could overturn the world's balance of power. Even with Neo London's full support, one couldn't afford to be careless. And the businesses the LK Group operated weren't limited to superconductors.

'Attempts to buy off LK Group employees with money are so common they're not even novel anymore.'

Most employees refused. The LK Group was notorious for certain retaliation, so employees didn't betray for money alone. Even if offered ten billion, it was useless if you ended up dead.

Some employees believed they could betray the company and survive. Among them, not a single one was still alive.

'Terror attacks on the LK Group within the last three months: six total. No real damage, but the mere fact that attacks occurred is bad press.'

Of course, retaliation was thorough.

Killing the terrorists went without saying, and their families were found and eliminated as well. Retaliation was carried out against the organizations behind the terrorists too. Of course, the cases where Yuria personally stepped in and pulled strings were almost nonexistent. She wielded the LK Group's money and power. Thanks to that, few even knew of Yuria's existence.

Yuria stared at her terminal with an expressionless face.

&lt;37 hackers who breached the group's internal server…….&gt;

Yuria's hand, which had been reading reports sent to her terminal and issuing orders, stopped dead.

Queen Victoria VI's unofficial video conference.

Instinctively, she knew it was related to her. If the Queen had summoned the succession candidates, it was highly likely related to the next royal succession. However, if it was conducted via video conference—which carried high hacking risk—rather than meeting in person, its importance would be lower.

She skimmed the summarized information in the report once, then checked the footage directly.

Victoria VI exchanged greetings with the succession candidates, asked after their well-being, and checked their achievements. The royals, while watching the Queen's mood, confidently presented their accomplishments.

—A king must have trustworthy limbs, you see. A kingmaker. The talent to support the throne is never far away. For me, that was Florence. Without Florence, I would never have ascended to the throne. You lot should find your own kingmakers.

She said it in passing, near the end of the meeting.

From Yuria's perspective, there was no need to say those words. She focused on a few words.

Throne. Kingmaker. Florence.

“…….”

It sounded like a declaration: the one who receives Yuria's loyalty will ascend to the throne.

Yuria wasn't overthinking it. Recently, the royal family had been blatantly trying to recruit her. Victoria VI's gaze toward Yuria in particular was growing more intense by the day.

'I conveyed my intentions to Her Majesty the Queen, but… I didn't expect her to come out like this. She's overestimating me.'

The clever royals had probably caught on to some degree. And yet they'd remain uncertain. The Queen would reveal her intentions little by little—but unmistakably.

The situation after that would clearly turn into a complete shitshow.

'At this rate, things will get troublesome regardless of my opinion.'

Even if she obtained Prime grade, the likelihood of the royal family letting her go was high. Before that, the probability of causing trouble for the Master due to royals with their eyes on the throne was high.

'Reporting directly to Her Majesty the Queen won't help either.'

She had already stated her intentions plainly several times. Victoria VI, despite her gentle appearance, possessed a heart tougher than iron sinew and cold as ice. Persuasion through words wouldn't work.

'If words won't work, I'll have to communicate my intentions through action.'

Yuria thought of one of the royals.

Marquis Rian Vergil.

Ninth in the line of succession, he was a royal notorious for being thin-skinned and greedy. He had a decent faction of his own. However, he lacked the influence and power to cause a stir in Neo London.

Even if Marquis Vergil died, Neo London would spend today the same as yesterday.

'I'll deliberately leak information to Marquis Vergil and goad him.'

Assassinate him when he makes his move.

This would send a clear message to the Queen.

That her Master was only one.
