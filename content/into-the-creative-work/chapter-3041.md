"It's been a while for both of you. Call me whatever's comfortable."

Standing before the Shandong Fist Empress, Hwangbo Gahye, and the Black Wind Sword Hero, Baek Muhan, I openly scrutinized them both. An incredibly rude attitude, yet neither of them furrowed a brow, enduring it silently. They understood that showing displeasure toward someone far above their cultivation realm was tantamount to suicide.

'Both are at the early Three Purities stage—their realms are similar.'

If the two fought, Baek Muhan would win. When cultivation realms are comparable, combat experience, the quality of one's techniques, and elemental compatibility all play significant roles.

'The Heavenly Demon Divine Art that Baek Muhan has mastered doesn't suffer from elemental disadvantage unless it's against the sublime martial arts of Shaolin. The same applies to my own Heavenly Demon Divine Art.'

I spoke with a feigned tone of admiration.

"Both of you have grown incomparably stronger than when I last saw you."

"Compared to you, Heavenly Demon, it amounts to nothing."

Hwangbo Gahye said calmly.

"That's true enough."

Brazen, but it was the truth. Looking back through the history of the martial world, there is no strong practitioner who reached the Harmony Realm in as short a time as I did. Even the Emperor himself likely couldn't match my pace. Accurate records are lacking, making comparison difficult, but it was probably so.

"Why are you two together?"

"Our Hwangbo Clan received help from Hero Baek. Since then, we've been working together for mutual benefit."

"Help?"

"There was friction with the martial artists of the Martial Alliance."

"I see."

One might ask whether the Martial Alliance and the Hwangbo Clan aren't both on the orthodox side.

However, in this martial world, being on the same orthodox side didn't mean they were allies. Especially between the Martial Alliance and the great clans—relations tended to be poor. If this weren't the Secret Realm, they would have avoided friction with each other.

'But this is the Secret Realm. No matter what happens here, it wouldn't be strange. They wouldn't openly antagonize each other outside the Secret Realm either. The Martial Alliance must have judged that they could handle the Hwangbo Clan.'

The Hwangbo Clan ranked among the lower tier of the martial world's famous family powers.

"It doesn't seem you entered the Secret Realm with the Martial Alliance's permission, did you?"

"Correct. We received the imperial family's permission through the help of a high official with whom we have connections."

"Baek Muhan. And you?"

"Similar to the Hwangbo Clan Head. I obtained a permit issued by the imperial family through the help of an official I know."

"The imperial family scattered Secret Realm permits?"

"You didn't know? His Imperial Majesty took pity on wanderers like us and granted us the opportunity to enter the Secret Realm. It's quite a famous story in the streets, actually."

"I don't listen unless it's truly important information."

Not all intelligence gathered by the Heavenly Demon Cult reached my ears. If it did, I'd spend my entire life working with no time to fuck.

'It's not particularly high-priority information, anyway.'

And even if I knew, there was nothing I could do about it. Neither the Heavenly Demon Cult nor anyone else could stop what the imperial family had decreed.

I spoke to Baek Muhan with a serious expression.

"Baek Muhan. I want to speak with you alone. It's important."

"Yes. Understood. Let's go to my tent. It's a humble place for hosting someone of your stature, but…"

"Doesn't matter."

I entered his tent. The one-person tent was narrower than I'd expected. Not wanting to stay long, I erected a qi barrier around us and got to the point.

"Baek Muhan. I know that you are the Heavenly Demon who was reincarnated. And that you were reincarnated from another world."

Baek Muhan's face went rigid. His eyes—described in the original work as being skilled at concealing emotion—shook like a madman's.

He reflexively returned the hand that had been reaching for his waist back to its place, then composed his emotions.

"…You're certain, then. How did you come to know?"

"My eyes are a bit special. Just by looking at a target's origins, I can discern a fair amount."

"Those eyes are rather like the Monkey King's Fiery Golden Eyes. An absurd divine power."

"You cut right to the core. Incidentally, I can also see the seal binding your body and soul. Even with my eyes, I can't fully grasp its origins. Could it be a seal placed by an immortal directly?"

It wasn't a lie. I could see the outline of the seal wrapping around Baek Muhan's soul to some degree. Perhaps due to the gap in our levels, I couldn't examine it in detail.

"Truly remarkable. Yes, it is a seal."

"Whose seal is it?"

"I apologize. Because of the seal, I cannot speak of it. Originally, I couldn't even mention anything related to the seal at all, but… perhaps because you noticed first, I can say this much. This seal is connected to my past life. What is it that you want from me?"

"The Heavenly Demon Divine Art of another world."

“…….”

Baek Muhan's expression hardened.

No matter how much of a life-debt benefactor someone might be, handing over one's martial arts—especially divine techniques—was an entirely separate matter. In the martial world, more than half of practitioners would sooner choose death than pass their martial arts to another.

"Someone who has already reached the Harmony Realm wouldn't covet my Heavenly Demon Divine Art for power…. Is it curiosity?"

"Correct. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art of another world—aren't you curious? Of course, I'm not entirely shameless. I'll teach you my Heavenly Demon Divine Art as well. Aren't you curious about this world's Heavenly Demon Divine Art?"

Baek Muhan's Adam's apple bobs. A strange fervor blooms in his eyes.

'He hasn't fully reformed yet. Will he join the Heavenly Demon Cult like in the original?'

The latter half of the original work, 'The Fallen Heavenly Demon.' Baek Muhan enters the Heavenly Demon Cult—a den of evil—to accumulate good deeds. He transforms the Heavenly Demon Cult and ascends to the position of Heavenly Demon once more. By then, he has already reformed and let go of his obsession with power.

'But that's a matter of the original's future. It doesn't seem likely he'll join the Heavenly Demon Cult while I'm reigning over it.'

I had no particular interest in Baek Muhan.

I may not have known before, but now I don't care whether Baek Muhan lives or dies. Even if he dies, there's no harm that comes to me, no loss I'd have to bear. If Baek Muhan refused my proposal, I intended to subdue him, extract the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, and kill him.

"…You are my benefactor. If it were up to my heart alone, I would gladly share the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, but… I too am curious about this world's Heavenly Demon Divine Art. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art is originally the most demonic of demonic arts—those without the talent are devoured by it, but… you have already mastered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, so you should be fine."

"You've thought it through well. I'm also curious whether you'll be able to master my Heavenly Demon Divine Art."

Baek Muhan and I exchanged our Heavenly Demon Divine Arts. Not just the Heavenly Demon Divine Art that the King of Demon Heaven gave me. I taught him the entirety of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art I had accumulated until now.

Baek Muhan's Heavenly Demon Divine Art leaned toward external techniques. And it contained many forms designed to kill people efficiently. In some ways, it resembled the martial arts of an assassin.

'Not the Heavenly Demon Reign Step, but the Heavenly Demon Phantom Step. I knew of it through the original work, but hearing the oral formulas directly and confirming it… interesting.'

At first glance, the Heavenly Demon Phantom Step appears to be a movement technique specialized in stealth. It could be said to be a movement art on an entirely different track from the Heavenly Demon Reign Step, which dominates everything in a single step.

But its essence lies not in movement, but in assimilation with nature.

"This is absurd…. Is this truly a martial art?"

"Hm? What's the problem?"

"Martial arts are originally a means for humans to strengthen themselves. Therefore, one can only grow stronger step by step. However, your Heavenly Demon Divine Art… does not grow stronger step by step. It builds a foundation through external means, ignores the process, and summons the result. It looks less like a martial art and more like some strange sorcery. Martial arts should be of humans, for humans… but this seems like something that isn't human, forced into the mold of humanity."

"But you can still master it, can't you?"

"I can master it. Though I would suffer from vicious inner demons. From my perspective, it's astonishing that you remain unscathed after mastering this Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Are you not afraid of inner demons?"

I hadn't felt even the slightest trace of an inner demon. Of course, I knew it was thanks to the Absolute Mind.

'Is it dangerous enough to shock even Baek Muhan?'

My master, Wiyu, had said nothing in particular about the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

'…Is it because I don't use the Heavenly Demon Divine Art much in front of Wiyu? Perhaps it's because the Heavenly Demon Divine Art was altered after being mixed with various other things.'

I didn't think deeply about it. It wasn't a great danger to me, anyway.

"I use the Heavenly Demon Divine Art well. I always have, and I always will. More importantly, what is the level of completion of my Heavenly Demon Divine Art?"

"It's not a divine art I dare to evaluate, but… if you were to achieve great mastery… there would be none in this world who could oppose you."

"Can you master my Heavenly Demon Divine Art?"

"It's impossible."

Baek Muhan smiled bitterly.

Crack.

Black sparks burst from his head. Baek Muhan grimaced as if enduring pain.

"Just recalling the Heavenly Demon Divine Art you taught me invites inner demons. This is only manageable because the accursed seal tormenting me resists the inner demons…. If the seal weren't there, my mind might already be half-mad."

Crack, crackle.

The black sparks flickering from Baek Muhan's head grow gradually more intense.

Baek Muhan gritted his teeth and endured, then suddenly snapped his eyes wide open.

"Ah."

"What is it?"

"The seal says it will erase my memory—the oral formulas of your Heavenly Demon Divine Art."

I channeled my inner power into my Brain-Eye Golden Pupils and focused my mind. Then the notification window that Baek Muhan was seeing appeared faintly before my eyes as well.

『Dangerous energy detected in the practitioner!』

『Corrupted memories are eroding the practitioner's mind.』

『Yama's Seal. Isn't it troublesome? Don't you want to be rid of it? Leave it to me. I can remove it.』

『Removing corrupted memories.』

Crack-crackle!

Black lightning erupts.

Baek Muhan's eyes widen as if about to split apart.

"Ah, aaaaah…!"

Baek Muhan was looking at something other than me, who stood right in front of him.

"Baek Muhan. What are you looking at?"

"Hell…! Hell!! Kuaaaaak!"

Baek Muhan screamed and collapsed. I simply watched him as he lost consciousness. I didn't catch him as he fell, nor did I lift him up or support him. I didn't want to touch another man.

Five minutes later, Baek Muhan regained consciousness and stood up.

"Are you alright?"

"I'm fine. The oral formulas of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art you taught me… I've forgotten them all."

"Isn't that a loss for you? Your face looks relieved, though."

"Your Heavenly Demon Divine Art was not a demonic art I could handle. And… I've realized something through this experience. Perhaps I've been clinging to too many useless things all along. Sir. May I train?"

"Go ahead. I'll clear the space for you. Consolidate your enlightenment."

"Thank you, sir."

I left Baek Muhan's tent.

'His reaction earlier was similar to those who'd been struck by the Heavenly Demon Reign Step. Does that mean my Heavenly Demon Divine Art itself is a demon?'

That wasn't bad.
