,Kill him! Shoot the poison arrows and throw the chain nets!"

A bandit shouted as if he was spewing venom. The frozen bandits finally began to respond.

Poison-tipped arrows?

They don't even tickle. They're far too weak to pierce a knight's full plate armor. Besides, my armor is a masterpiece among masterpieces made by dwarves. Arrows without mana can't penetrate it.

Of course, the bastards knew that too, so they occasionally shot poison arrows imbued with mana. Since I caught them all with my senses, I just knocked those arrows away with my sword.

I made sure to dodge the thrown chain nets. If I got tangled up with a chain net, I'd face the worst-case scenario.

I kept moving forward.

,Heeeuuuk!"

A bandit who met my gaze gasped. His sword trembled. I could sense mana from him, but he didn't seem particularly strong. He knew how to use mana but couldn't manifest Sword energy.

,D-Die!! You monster bastard!!"

The bandit swung his sword.

I didn't even feel it was worth dodging.

Kkang!

The bandit's sword bounced off. The bandit, having lost his sword, stared at me with a blank look. I must have seemed like a tank to him.

Syeokeok.

I swung my sword and beheaded the bandit. I snatched the falling head with one hand and threw it at a bandit who was trying to throw a chain net at me. A precise headshot burst the bandit's head. He was wearing a crude helmet, but it was too weak to stop my strength-infused projectile.

Perhaps because the scene was so gruesome, the surrounding bandits hesitated. Some glanced around, looking for a chance to escape.

,You morons! Surround him! Surround him and throw the nets all at once!"

A bandit shouted as if he was bursting with frustration. The bandits then seemed to realize and surrounded me. But it was already too late. I didn't come alone.

,Help Sir Dakerte!"

,You honorless bastards! My spear shall judge you!"

,I am Rakguseu! Remember my name as you die!"

The knights, having avoided the traps, charged towards the bandits on horseback. The knights' charge was special. They used mana to reinforce not only their weapons but also their horses and armor, as if covering them with a barrier.

The bandits couldn't stop the knights' charge. If they blocked the way, their entire bodies exploded. Attack from the side? The horses were too fast for that. Besides, the knights ignored most attacks.

However, the knights' charge didn't last long. The bandits retreated behind barricades to stop the horses. The knights didn't care. They dismounted and charged towards the bandits.

,Die!"

,You bastards aren't worth living!"

I was at the forefront of the knights. If a barricade blocked the way, I pushed through with force and killed the bandits. Most of the bandits could use mana, but they couldn't stop us.

,Damn it. Burn all the food and retreat!"

The man at the very back of the bandits shouted. He looked like the boss at a glance.

,Even if I miss the other bastards, I can't miss you, the apparent leader!"

Some bandits were trying to set fire to the food.

,Set fire to the food? These bastards aren't ordinary bandits! But no way! I'll show you my secret technique! Water Sword!"

Every time Rakguseu swung his sword, water was created and soaked the ground. Is this a secret technique or magic? I swallowed a hollow laugh and ran towards the bandit boss.

The bandit boss took out a rolled-up parchment from his chest. I could sense a strange power.

,A magic scroll?'

The moment the boss unfurled the scroll, a thick fog spread. The magic fog began to obscure not only vision but also sound and presence.

,These despicable bastards!"

,Everything you do is filthy!"

,If you're an Imperial, fight honorably!"

The knights burst out in frustration.

[Opening the Mind's Eye.]

The Mind's Eye pierced through the magic fog. I could see the boss's back. I ran through the fog.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 6]

My body became faster. I quickly caught up to the boss. I was going to cut off both legs first and then knock him unconscious by hitting the back of his head. The boss didn't pass out. His head was harder than I thought.

Surprisingly, the boss retaliated against me. He aimed for the gap in my neck below the helmet with a small knife hidden under his wrist. I quickly ducked my head forward. His knife touched the helmet and slipped off.

,He reacted to my speed just now. He's skilled.'

But it doesn't matter.

His legs were already cut off while he was running with his back exposed. His last counterattack didn't work either. If he had fought without running away, he would have annoyed me more.

,Shit."

The boss cursed and tried to stab himself in the neck with the knife. I swung my foot and shattered the wrist holding the knife. Before the boss could attempt another suicide, I punched him in the head and knocked him unconscious. Fortunately, he passed out this time.

The fog disappeared after 5 minutes. The knights approached me.

,Amazing, Sir Dakerte. To think you'd chase down and subdue the boss in that fog. I'm once again impressed by your skills."

The knights, including Cotton, praised me with admiration.

,Haha. I was lucky. Of course, they say luck is also a skill."

I modestly brushed it off.

,Sir Rakguseu also did well. When those ruthless bastards tried to set fire to the food and run away, he protected the food with his secret technique. If it weren't for Sir Rakguseu, some of the food would have been burned."

,I was just doing what I had to do."

The knights were in a cheerful mood. This battle was a great victory for the knights. The enemies had underestimated the knights too much. I had also underestimated the Imperial knights until now.

,The minimum combat strength of the knights is at least B-rank in Hunter terms. Can't underestimate them.'

On the other hand, more than half of the bandits were dead.

The squires, who had scattered to clean up and investigate the surroundings, ran over to report.

,Sir Cotton! Half of the war supplies are missing! It seems they've already smuggled half of them away!"

,What?! Damn it! I knew those ordinary bandits were going to be a pain in the ass! First, you run to His Excellency the Count and report, then return with soldiers to clean up this place. The other squires will guard this place!"

Rakguseu watched the situation unfold and said to me.

,Sir Dakerte. It seems we need to wake up the boss you captured and interrogate him. Leave it to me. I learned a few interrogation techniques from a torture technician in the past."

,Sir Rakguseu is multi-talented. I'll leave this bastard to you."

Rakguseu forcibly woke up the unconscious boss using mana. His mana manipulation was quite unique. You could say it was similar to Pressure point techniques. The other knights also stared at Rakguseu with curiosity. But they didn't ask for details. People in this world were sensitive about techniques.

,Kkeuaaaaaak!

Rakguseu cut off the boss's remaining arms and then tortured him.

,If you want to die peacefully, answer the questions truthfully! If you don't answer or lie, you'll taste suffering for the rest of your life!"

,I-I'll talk...."

He didn't even lie about letting him live. That's what made it more effective. The bandit boss, realizing it was all over, explained the situation.

,We are the Thorn Wing Mercenary Corps. We robbed the merchant group on behalf of a witch. We were only told to stop a few knights.... I'm the vice-captain of the mercenary corps and didn't receive the request directly."

,Who is the mercenary captain?"

,Derek Kalluan. A one-eyed man. He took half of the goods obtained from robbing the merchant group and left for the witch. I don't know any more details than that... He had his subordinates carry the goods and leave, so they wouldn't have been able to erase their tracks."

Elmeyer ordered his squire to find the tracks.

,Sir Elmeyer! I found the tracks! They lead towards the forest!"

The knights clicked their tongues.

,The forest, huh. Tracking will be a pain in the ass. We can't even ride our horses...."

,We must chase them down right now. If we give up here or let them get away, our honor will be tarnished. We won't be able to face our lord."

,More than that, I'm more worried about the witch. What kind of magic will that witch use... It would be a disaster if she cursed us."

Witch.

A term used in the Pelheim Empire to refer to rare, naturally born magic users. Since only women are born as natural magic users, they are called witches. But outside the Empire, they are simply called mages.

It's said that witches are strangely born more often in the Pelheim Empire.

,Even if we get cursed, we can just get it removed later, right? What's urgent now is catching Derek Kalluan. After that, we catch the witch who commissioned him."

I looked back at the knights and said.

,Let's leave half of the squires here and move out. We need to move diligently to catch those bastards."

,Sir Dakerte is right. Let's move out to catch those bastards."

We followed the tracks and walked into the forest.

But since we weren't on horseback, our speed wasn't great. Still, the tracks hadn't disappeared, so we had no choice but to keep going.

After some time, the sun began to set, and the tracks suddenly disappeared. The knights were flustered.

,The tracks are gone. What should we do?"

,We must remain calm in times like these."

,Sir Elmeyer. Calm or not, I think we're screwed...."

,There must be a reason why those bastards suddenly erased their tracks."

,Isn't it because they realized we were tracking them?"

,That could be the case. But in my opinion, they were focused on escaping, not erasing their tracks. There's a distance between us, after all. It would be difficult for us to chase them if they left the territory. It would cause disputes with other lords."

,...So, the reason they erased their tracks is to hide nearby?"

,That's my opinion for now. So, we need to search this area."

Following Elmeyer's suggestion, we searched the area. The day was ending, and what we found was a small village.

Cotton called the knights together.

,It's hard to find them without tracks. Since the day is ending, those bastards are probably hiding somewhere and resting. So, how about we rest in that village and look again when the sun rises tomorrow?"

I immediately agreed.

,Blindly searching isn't a good idea. How about we go to the village and get help from the residents? Have the residents search for them. It's for His Excellency the Count, so the villagers will naturally cooperate."

,Ooh, good idea, Sir Dakerte!"

,But what if the villagers don't cooperate?"

,Not cooperating with us is the same as rebelling against His Excellency the Count. We can kill a few as an example."

,Excellent wisdom, Sir Dakerte. But we should count people as 'myeong (people)' not 'mari (animals)'."

,Ah, my mistake. I'll be careful, Sir Elmeyer."

,Haha. You don't have to be that careful."

We laughed cheerfully and entered the village.

The village chief rushed out to greet us. Cotton stepped forward and asserted the rights of a knight to the village chief.

,We will be staying in this village tonight. Prepare good lodging, delicious food, cool liquor, and pretty women!"

I liked the Empire too much.
