,Hobgoblins have mutated! Spread out and use Ice Barrier magic!!"

I shouted to the squad members, who were startled as they jumped to their feet.

Fortunately, the squad members weren't completely out of it. As soon as they heard my command, they immediately started moving their mana and preparing their magic.

The fastest was, of course, Number 31. She seemed to have been preparing her magic even before I finished speaking, and she was the first to use the Tier 2 magic, Ice Arrow.

The ice arrow accurately aimed for the hobgoblin's head and struck.

Jjeo-eong!

The rainwater and cold air flowing on the hobgoblin's head met and instantly froze. It was the same magic that killed the gargoyle in an instant, but the hobgoblin shook its head and brushed off the ice. Even though it had the ,Magic Resistance' trait due to the influence of the Dark Moon, it wasn't completely ineffective. Its face was wounded and covered in blood.

,Kiaaaaaaaaah!"

The hobgoblin let out a roar of anger. The target of that anger was, of course, Number 31. Number 31 had a calm face. She wasn't surprised, nor did she back down. Did it not like that? The hobgoblin grabbed a piece of lumber lying around and threw it at Number 31.

,Barrier."

A protective barrier appeared, enveloping Number 31's body. The barrier that blocked the lumber only shook a little.

,Ice!!"

The squad members, who had surrounded the hobgoblin, simultaneously used the Tier 1 magic, Ice. To be precise, the Tier 1 magic, Ice, has the effect of creating ice using cold air. It's less effective in dry desert areas, but its effect is doubled in situations like this, where it's raining.

Jjeo-jeok, jjeo-jeong.

The hobgoblin's entire body froze. The hobgoblin's movements stopped. But the hobgoblin wasn't dead. The hobgoblin's eyes were rolling around inside the ice.

,Damn magic resistance. I can't miss this chance. I have to kill it.'

I calculated what the most effective magic would be in this situation.

Tier 2 magic, Air Strike.

Among Tier 2 magic, it's one of the most powerful in terms of physical force. Although this is also magic, so it will be affected by ,Magic Resistance', there's no other choice.

,The power of fire magic is weakened due to the rainy weather, and it will only melt the hobgoblin's frozen body. Earth-based magic is for similar reasons.'

Now that I've made a decision, there's no reason to hesitate. Hesitating even for a second during combat can be dangerous.

I drew up 12 mana rods and created a spell.

,Air Strike."

,Air Strike."

Number 31 and I chanted at the same time.

The air distorted. Originally, Air Strike is wind. It can't be seen with the naked eye, but the torrential rain that was constantly falling from the sky revealed the appearance of Air Strike.

The distorted space collided with the frozen hobgoblin as it was.

Kwaaaang!

The ice shattered, and the hobgoblin's red blood fell on the wet ground.

,Kiaaaaaaaak!"

The hobgoblin roared and moved its legs.

,Maintain a certain distance and use Ice magic! Go the same way as before! If we repeat it 2-3 times, we'll be able to kill it!"

I kicked a small pebble rolling on the ground and hit the hobgoblin's head. It wasn't meant to be an aggressive attack. From the hobgoblin's point of view, it was only ticklish. But what if you get hit in the head with a pebble when you're already furious? Your mood will plummet and you'll get even more annoyed.

The hobgoblin's attention turned to me. It made a ferocious expression and ran straight towards me. It targeted me, the furthest away, not Number 31, who was the closest. Just as I intended.

,Ice Arrow."

The activated magic flew and hit the hobgoblin's left leg, freezing it. The hobgoblin's body tilted.

,I bought some time. Now, the squad members can freeze its body with Ice magic, and Number 31 and I can use Air Strike.'

The hobgoblin's mediocre intelligence was helpful. In game terms, it was easy to manage aggro.

,......?!"

I suddenly felt a chill. Every single cell in my body was surprised, and I felt an unpleasant sensation that made my hair stand on end.

,The mana in the atmosphere has changed. The mana, which was already fierce due to the influence of the Dark Moon, has become even more intense.'

Even though I knew the hobgoblin was limping towards me with its frozen leg, I couldn't help but turn my gaze away.

It was Number 31.

She was drawing magic in the center of the intense mana, her red eyes gleaming.

,Tier 2 is definitely not it. Tier 3...? No. This is similar to when Instructor Venus uses magic....'

Number 31's robe fluttered. Her purple hair fluttered upwards. There was no wind blowing right now. The wind around her right now was the movement of mana.

,Number 31! Stop!!"

I shouted, pulling in all my strength.

It was no use. Number 31's red eyes were fixed on the hobgoblin. She didn't even give me a glance.

,What's going on? Why is Number 31 going berserk?'

Number 31's magic manifested. A smile appeared on Number 31's lips. I felt a sense of dread and quickly distanced myself from the hobgoblin.

The wind blew.

It gathered towards the hobgoblin, leaving traces as if carving claw marks into the ground.

The hobgoblin swung its fists and feet at the formless wind. Needless to say, it couldn't stop the wind with just that.

The wind gradually increased over time and began to rotate around the hobgoblin.

Tier 4 magic. Tornado.

,It's not an ordinary tornado. Even though its size is small, it has given physicality to each and every gust of wind. In other words, it's a tornado that applies Wind Cutter to each and every gust of wind.'

The hobgoblin's body was being sliced by the wind. The tornado began to be painted red. It was the hobgoblin's blood.

The tornado lasted for about 30 seconds before disappearing.

The hobgoblin had turned into a mixture of blood, flesh, and bone fragments.

,Ahahaha, ahahahahaha."

I heard Number 31's laughter. I felt cold sweat on my back as I looked at Number 31. She was laughing as she looked at the black moon, and she was clearly different from her usual self.

,......."

The squad members, including me, didn't relax our guard. We felt a strong sense of unease from Number 31 and prepared to engage in combat at any moment.

Chi-jik. Chik.

A sound came from the radio. I quickly picked up the radio.

,Number 211. Do you hear me?"

,...Instructor Venus-nim. I hear you."

,I'm watching the situation. I'm on my way, so retreat with your squad members! Hurry!"

I could sense urgency in Instructor Venus's tone, who always spoke calmly. This meant that this situation was unexpected even for him.

,Move away from Number 31! Quickly!"

The squad members acted quickly. They immediately distanced themselves from Number 31.

,Ahahahahaha."

Number 31 was still laughing.

Mana gathered around her, fluctuating wildly. The mud on the ground stirred, and the rain falling from the sky avoided only her.

While I was tense enough to break out in a cold sweat, I calmly speculated about Number 31's identity in one corner of my mind.

Several things came to mind. After comparing various possibilities, the most likely speculation was one.

,Witch factor. She seemed to be in a good mood when the Dark Moon rose, and her magical abilities increased even though she's not a black magician.'

If Number 31 had a witch factor, everything would be explained.

Considering the instructors' reactions, she probably had a witch factor from birth.

,I don't know exactly what her background is, but it's 100% certain that she has a special lineage.'

Mana gathered in front of her.

I focused my mind and deciphered the spell.

Tier 3 magic, Air Boom.

Tier 4 magic, Levitation.

Number 31's body floated into the sky. It was the effect of the Tier 4 magic, Levitation.

The problem was the Tier 3 magic, Air Boom. As a result of calculating the spell and mana, it was said to be Tier 3 magic, but its power was so strong that it reached the level of Tier 5 magic.

,Is she planning to sweep away this entire area with Air Boom?'

If I were alone, I could somehow endure it. I could activate multiple barriers and use the artifact I received from Instructor Lexi. But the other squad members were different. They couldn't cast magic as quickly as I could.

I glanced at the sky.

Even if Thunder Bolt was impossible, I could probably use the Tier 4 magic, Lightning Strike, by using the dark clouds.

,No, I won't use Lightning Strike.'

Instructor Venus was coming from behind. I can't show off my power and talent here. I steeled my heart. Even if the squad members die, I'll hide my skills for the future.

Just as I was about to create the spell for the barrier, Instructor Venus appeared. As soon as he revealed himself, he let out a shout and used magic.

,Retreat!!"

An invisible wall of mana that stretched out from him enveloped Number 31. Air Boom occurred, but it was blocked by the wall of mana. All the squad members were safe.

I knew it was a kind of barrier magic.

,Even though the spell is sloppy because he activated it in a hurry, he barely managed to block Number 31's Air Boom. Is that the level of a Tier 5 magician?'

Instructor Venus ran towards Number 31. Magic sparkled beneath his feet.

,The magic that serves as a foothold is... an application of Barrier?'

In terms of spells, it's simpler than Tier 1 magic. But the casting speed is incredibly fast. Considering that you could fall and suffer fatal injuries if you slip even a little due to the nature of the foothold floating in the air, you also have to time it well.

It seemed impossible to apply the magic itself like Instructor Venus, even if it was easy to follow.

I retreated with the squad members and watched the battle between Number 31 and Instructor Venus.

,Instructor Venus is trying to subdue Number 31. Number 31 isn't being pushed back much either.'

Instructor Venus used magic efficiently. He uses appropriate magic at the right time. For example, if Number 31 uses the Tier 2 magic, Ice Arrow, he blocks it with the Tier 1 magic, Fire.

,He's not just blocking and neutralizing it, but neutralizing the cold air of Ice Arrow with Fire.'

You can see that he's using the differences in attribute compatibility to the extreme.

,It's helpful just to watch.'

The battle continued. Instructor Venus's hand blade grazed Number 31's neck. Number 31, startled, soared into the sky. Number 31's smile disappeared completely against the backdrop of the dark clouds. Number 31's mana soared into the sky.

Kureureureung.

The dark clouds flickered.

,Lightning Spike...!'

Lightning fell towards Instructor Venus.

,Dome Ground."

I heard Instructor Venus's faint voice. Immediately after, dirt soared into the sky around him and formed a dome. The temporary earth structure easily blocked the Lightning Spike.

The dome, having fulfilled its role, did not collapse as it was, but soared upwards and flew towards Number 31.

Number 31's Levitation faltered as she was hit in the forehead with a pile of dirt. Her body went down and stopped midway. Barely maintaining her magic, Number 31 glared at Instructor Venus as if to kill him and stirred up her mana.

I felt mana from Number 31 that was incomparable to anything before.

,Stop! Stop it and come to your senses, Number 31!"

Instructor Venus shouted.

There was no way Number 31, who was in a berserk state, would listen to that. Instructor Venus activated Dome Ground once again.

Kwang.

Kwakwakwang. Kwang.

Explosions rang out.

The Tier 3 magic, Air Boom, occurred indiscriminately.

In less than 10 seconds, Air Boom occurred more than 10 times, devastating the ground. Instructor Venus's Dome Ground was also shattered. Instructor Venus tried to protect his body by wrapping a barrier around it, but even that wasn't easy. His body was flying around here and there due to the aftermath of Air Boom. However, his barrier was not broken and continued to be maintained.

,No...!"

Instructor Venus shouted.

This was because Number 31, who had actually used the magic, was unable to protect her own body and was caught up in the aftermath of Air Boom, flying around in the air. In addition, Number 31 had fainted in the middle, so Levitation was released and she fell downwards. Unfortunately, there was a waterfall below.

,Number 211!!! Save Number 31!!"

Instructor Venus shouted to me, who was closest to Number 31.

I gritted my teeth and ran towards Number 31.

If I don't do anything here, Instructor Venus will put the blame on me and kill me. It's obvious without even looking.

,I have to save Number 31 to survive!'
