In the underworld, there were five rivers. One of these five rivers was the famous River Styx.

I stared at the river before me.

It was so large that it looked more like an ocean than a river.

,This must be the River Styx.'

It didn't particularly look like a river with negative power. Gods swore oaths on the River Styx at every chance they got, so it was easy to mistake it for a terrifying river, but surprisingly, the River Styx itself was in decent shape.

,It's definitely a mysterious river. Even now, standing before it, I can feel its mystical power.'

It wasn't as ominous as I thought.

,Should I flip it over?'

Tsujujujujujujut.

I summon power.

Lightning flowing from my body rides the air of the underworld and scatters. The dark underworld brightened in an instant.

I gather lightning in my fist, forming it into the shape of a spear. When I felt enough power had gathered, I threw it towards the River Styx. The lightning shook the entire River Styx. The surface of the river shook violently like waves, and the water soared to the ceiling.

"......"

I frowned. This was just a river. No matter how many times I struck it with lightning, it wouldn't mean much. Perhaps because it was a river in the underworld, it didn't seem like any living creatures inhabited it.

,Should I use more power to stir it up?'

Just as I was about to use divine power, the River Styx split open, and a goddess appeared.

"Stop! Please stop! If you continue, the River Styx and the other rivers might mix! That would become another chaos. You don't want that, do you!"

It was a goddess I remembered.

Styx.

The owner of this river.

"Styx. It's been a while. Is this the first time since that war?"

"Yes. It's been a long time, Zeus-nim. Please stop. Your anger has already been etched into the underworld. Even after thousands, tens of thousands of years, that scar will not disappear."

"Hmm."

"...If you mix all five rivers... the order of the underworld will be disrupted, and it will have to be rewritten. There will be chaos for a very long time. You don't want that, do you?"

"I came all the way here, so it's a bit much to just go back. If you want to appease my anger... appease my anger."

I stared at Styx with blatant eyes. Styx was already a married woman with children. Although her husband was stuck in Tartarus.

"You really are something...."

Styx shuddered but didn't run away. It seemed she had decided to sacrifice herself for the sake of the underworld.

I grinned.

• • •

After spending a pleasant time with Styx, I flew around the underworld, looking at various places. As a tourist destination, the underworld was terrible. There was nothing worth seeing. The five rivers were interesting, but after a while, I got used to them.

,It's harder to fly than usual. Is it because it's the underworld?'

I discovered something unusual. An island floating in a corner of the River Styx. In the center of that island, filled with poppies, an old man was sleeping as if dead.

I looked down at the island from the air. The white-haired old man was a god I had never seen before. Of course, just because I'm Zeus doesn't mean I know every god.

,Since there's a god in a place like this, I'm curious.'

The sleeping old god seemed to sense my presence and slowly lifted his left eyelid. The eyelid didn't rise more than halfway. His pupils were unfocused. It seemed he hadn't fully woken up yet.

"The king of the gods... I see. I heard a new god-king has appeared. His name was Zeus, wasn't it...?"

"I am Zeus. You're out of touch with the news from the surface, being in a place like this. It's been several years since I became the king of the gods... ah, never mind."

The god before me was dozing off. It meant he wasn't even listening to me. The desire to talk with him disappeared. Still, I needed to know his identity.

"Be careful."

"Out of the blue like that? Ah, are you one of the prophet gods too?"

"Dreams reveal much...."

Blood flows from the old man's half-open eyes. I was slightly surprised by the inexplicable situation. He didn't seem to care.

"The trials are already twisted. The stars have finished their preparations, and chaos is crawling.... Destruction is already prepared... you must prepare for the end."

"The end? You're not talking about the Gigantomachy, are you."

"Not the Gigantomachy or anything like it."

"You seem to know that this world is a trial."

"All of this is also a process."

"Who are you?"

Paaaaaaat!

He vomited blood. His unfocused pupils began to spin wildly. Blood continued to flow out. Even in this state, he didn't seem to want to wake up.

"Hyp... nos...."

Hypnos, the god of sleep.

It wasn't surprising since it was the name I had expected.

Cough.

Hypnos vomited blood. His crazed pupils began to reflect something else.

A passage.

A dark passage was connected to his pupils. Something was approaching at high speed from the end of the passage. Moving its eight legs actively, it was a spider. A spider with a human head.

,A human-faced spider? No, it's not some low-level being like that.'

A spider leg popped out of Hypnos's pupil. The length of that leg alone was over 10 meters.

,The legs are too long to come out through the pupil.'

I didn't know the details, but it seemed like space itself was being forced open. It was right to assume that something was wrong with Hypnos's dream.

"Zeus, die, you...."

Hypnos said in a fragmented voice. Even in the midst of that, four more spider legs were popping out, struggling. The spider's face and torso emerged. The spider's torso was one thing, but its face was distorted as if screaming. I felt an instinctive revulsion.

"Disgusting."

Pajijijijijijijijik!

I threw Astrape at the spider bastard. The spider swung its legs and knocked Astrape away.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaaaak!"

Hypnos screamed. The spider leg that popped out of his pupil pierced Hypnos's chest. Even so, Hypnos was still asleep.

I approached the spider. I grabbed the spider's legs, which were over 10 meters long, with both hands and snapped them off. The remaining spider legs faltered but soon rushed at me. I grabbed those legs as well and rubbed them together.

The broken legs regenerated quickly.

,I need to finish this before it fully regenerates.'

Pajijijijik.

Manroe (卍 Thunder).

The lightning condensed in my fist rotated, increasing its power.

I slammed Manroe into the spider's human head.

Kieeeeeeeeeeeeeeeek!

The spider twisted its body with a shriek. It was trying to escape back into Hypnos's pupil, into his dream. I grabbed the spider's torso with one hand and gathered lightning in the other, swinging it.

,Astrape.'

Once wasn't enough.

I swung the lightning again and again.

I wonder if I had slammed Astrape into its head about 13 times. The spider's body slumped and stopped moving.

,It's finally dead.'

I killed the disgusting bug. I felt proud of that fact.

"Kkeueueu...."

Hypnos groaned.

"There's a traitor.... There's a traitor...!"

"A traitor? Who is it?"

"......"

Hypnos didn't answer. He was dead.

"You should have told me who the traitor was before dying! No, before that, is he really dead?"

I was flustered. The gods of Greece are basically immortal. They're beings who shouldn't die. For such a being to die so easily, the word 'immortal' felt like a joke.

Even if complete immortality doesn't exist, wasn't this too sudden?

,I don't know what's going on. Is he really dead?'

I waited for a few minutes, but Hypnos didn't move. I burned Hypnos's and the spider's corpses with lightning. Hypnos still didn't regenerate.

,This is ominous. Very ominous.'

• • •

Several years have passed since Hades's abduction of Persephone. The surface and the underworld have stabilized again.

Everything has returned to normal. There was no chaos caused by death.

But I felt an ominous aura lingering around Olympus.

The goddesses were the same as usual.

The problem was the male gods.

An ominous atmosphere was flowing, centered around Poseidon, Ares, and Apollo. Everyone else would feel it was just their imagination. But who am I? Having experienced tyranny several times, my senses have developed in their own way.

,These bastards. They're dreaming of rebellion.'

I can tell at a glance.

They want to pull me down, just like I usurped the throne from Cronus.

,There's probably a high chance Hades is involved too.'

Subduing them wouldn't be difficult.

I could fight and win against all the male gods combined in my current state.

,But I don't have any evidence. They haven't even committed the rebellion yet. No matter how much it's me, I can't throw the male gods into Tartarus for a rebellion that hasn't happened.'

Using prophecy as a reason was also impossible.

Fate was so twisted that even the Moirai, the three goddesses of fate, gave up. In this situation, prophecy has no meaning.

,I have to wait until those bastards start a rebellion. The only good thing is that the goddesses are on my side.'

Perhaps because I had been good to the goddesses, they didn't seem to have any particular complaints. And it didn't seem like all the male gods had joined hands with them. Hephaestus and Hermes were prime examples. They weren't particularly interested in things like power.

,I need to prepare.'

I could suppress a rebellion with my power alone. What's worrisome is what will come after the rebellion.

,I need more power. To do that, I need to receive more worship from humans.'

There was a limit to just scaring and killing humans.

I pondered and came up with a method.

"Hephaestus!!"

"Yes, Father. Do you need another piece of jewelry to gift to a goddess?"

"Humans have greatly advanced by obtaining fire. However, it is too slow for them to advance beyond that. Therefore, I intend to bestow lightning upon humans."

"...Father. Humans cannot dare to handle lightning."

"I know. But if I have your help, the story will change. Hephaestus, create a generator. And create machines and weapons that use electricity."

"Isn't it too early for humans now? I'm worried that humans will try to stand above the gods without knowing their place."

"Those who don't know their place will be punished by heaven."
