Ttaak.

The sound of the slate echoed. More than ten cameras prepared for the battle scene began to roll.

Na Ayeon is walking along a park path in broad daylight, looking at her smartphone.

Oh Junhyuk and I moved according to the pre-arranged route and walked towards Na Ayeon. We were wearing black suits like gangsters. Our roles are Organization Members A and B of the Black Guild, who are after the protagonist's sister.

Na Ayeon flinched when she saw us. Oh Junhyuk is wearing steel gauntlets, and I am holding a long knife in my hand. It was natural for ordinary people to be surprised to see us.

Her body shrinks and her eyes tremble with anxiety.

'Wow. If it weren't for the cameras, I'd think it was real.'

I admired inwardly. As expected of an actress with over 10 years of experience. If there were no cameras, I would have mistaken it for real.

Na Ayeon looked around. There was no one to ask for help. Na Ayeon turned around and started running away.

"Jjaba!"

Pfft!

At Oh Junhyuk's words, laughter reflexively burst from my mouth. No, I couldn't help it. It would have been okay if his pronunciation was just a little off, but 'jjaba' was so bad that it destroyed the serious atmosphere.

"Cut! Again! Let's do it again."

The director said.

"I'm sorry! I'm sorry!"

Oh Junhyuk bowed his head repeatedly with a flushed face. The staff gave bitter smiles. They also know that we are not professional actors.

"Hey. Hey. We can do it again."

I patted Oh Junhyuk's shoulder.

Oh Junhyuk has two lines. One is the 'jjaba' from just now, and the other is a fairly long sentence. On the other hand, I had no lines at all. I'm just playing the role of fighting Son Ando-ssi.

I was a little worried about Oh Junhyuk.

'That bastard. He said he was going to be a star Hunter, but he's worse than I thought.'

It was obvious that he was too nervous. I had a bad feeling that we might have to film the same scene more than ten times today.

And that premonition came true.

"...Let's do it again."

"NG!"

"......This isn't going to work."

The director sighed.

Oh Junhyuk, who had made seven NGs, was almost on the verge of tears.

The director pondered something for a while and then spoke to us.

"Oh Junhyuk-ssi and Seong Yujin-ssi. Let's switch roles. Seong Yujin-ssi. You know Organization Member A's lines, right?"

In the end, Oh Junhyuk was cut from the role. I looked at Oh Junhyuk's expression. He seemed more pleased than disappointed.

"Yes. I know them."

It's just two lines anyway. There was no way I wouldn't know them, since I had practiced with Oh Junhyuk several times.

"Then let's try it once. Do you need practice time?"

"No. I'm okay."

As I said that, I glanced at the man next to the director. He is said to be a man dispatched from the Hunter Association in preparation for an emergency.

'That emergency must mean the battle scene between the Hunters becoming excessively heated.'

That man's job will be to stop us if the battle scene gets too rough.

"Okay. Let's start then. Ready, action!"

The director's signal dropped.

Na Ayeon started acting again as before. I felt her professionalism in not getting annoyed even after doing the same acting several times.

I was a little nervous. That's because I wasn't very confident in my acting either.

Na Ayeon turns around and runs away.

"Catch her!"

I shouted. And Oh Junhyuk and I started running to catch Na Ayeon. We are E-class Hunters. It's easy to catch up with Na Ayeon even if she runs at full speed as an ordinary person.

We quickly caught up with Na Ayeon and started grabbing her body. I grabbed her left side, and Oh Junhyuk grabbed the other side.

"Aaaah! Let go of me! I said let go!"

Na Ayeon started struggling realistically, but she couldn't shake us off.

At that moment. Son Ando-ssi ran towards us at an incredible speed from far away and blew us backwards. We rolled on the floor several times before getting up. My and Oh Junhyuk's faces were contorted as much as possible. It was acting.

"Noona! Are you okay?!"

"Ha, Hauna!"

Son Ando-ssi checked on Na Ayeon. After confirming that there were no injuries on Na Ayeon's body, he turned around and glared at us. The tip of the sword he held in his hand was pointed at us.

"Don't tell me. Did that woman Kim Seona send you?!"

"The young lady doesn't know. It's the boss's order. It's good that we met you here. You'll have to die here. Has-been."

I said. I thought it wouldn't be strange if there was an NG here, but even I thought my voice was steady and not trembling.

And it seems like there's no problem, judging by the fact that the director isn't stopping.

"Noona. Step back. I have to take care of these bastards here."

"Ha, Hauna. Wouldn't it be better to run away and report it to the police?!"

"They'll definitely follow us even if we run away. You step away and report it to the police. Hurry!"

Na Ayeon started to step back.

Oh Junhyuk and I started running towards Son Ando-ssi with our weapons. Son Ando-ssi previously told us to go all out. We decided to follow his words. Son Ando-ssi, who is an A-class Hunter, would be able to subdue us easily even if there were ten of us.

"Aaaaaaaah!"

Oh Junhyuk shouts. His arms and shoulders turned to steel. Oh Junhyuk charged towards Son Ando-ssi with his entire weight on him.

Hwareureuk!

A bright red flame rose from Son Ando-ssi's sword. It was using Son Ando-ssi's ability, flame manipulation.

Son Ando-ssi's sword and Oh Junhyuk's fist collide.

Kwang!

A flame explosion occurred. It only looks incredibly dangerous. We weren't very surprised because we already knew it would happen. And we were given special protective gear before filming. We won't be injured by most attacks.

The flame explosion subsided, and Oh Junhyuk's body flew backwards. I left Oh Junhyuk behind and ran towards Son Ando-ssi.

Kaang! Kang!

His flame-filled sword and my Hwaryeon Bido collide. Son Ando-ssi deliberately shows me openings and encourages me to attack.

This is because it's filming. If we only search for openings and probe each other, the viewers will be bored.

I thought while exchanging attacks with him.

'There's rarely a chance to fight an A-class Hunter at full power in such a safe situation. He told me to go all out, right? I'll give you all the power you want.'

Pajijik!

Lightning crackles on my knife. It's imitating Astrape, a skill from the 'God's Atlantis' world. Even if its power isn't complete, the lightning itself can electrocute the opponent, so it can't be denied that it's powerful.

Son Ando-ssi didn't look very surprised. I had already told him that I was a secondary Awakener and had awakened lightning. Secondary Awakening refers to a Hunter who has awakened an ability awakening again to gain a new ability. In other words, it can be said that they are Hunters with two abilities. Secondary Awakening Hunters are very rare, but they are not completely non-existent.

'Electrocution doesn't work at all.'

When the lightning tried to move from my knife to his sword, it disappeared immediately. I knew that the lightning couldn't invade because of the mana that Son Ando-ssi was handling.

'Then how about this.'

Yeongcheon Ryu Lightning Snake.

I created distance from Son Ando-ssi and stabbed my knife at him.

My knife turned into a lightning streak and stabbed in, wriggling like a snake. Son Ando-ssi held his sword to the side, created a flame, and easily blocked the Lightning Snake.

Yeongcheon Ryu Lightning Flash.

I swung an extremely fast sword using the power of lightning. But Son Ando-ssi blocked it with his sword too easily.

I gritted my teeth. My full power didn't work at all against an A-class.

Yeongcheon Ryu Lightning Sound Step

It is a footwork that abandons the secrecy of Yeongcheon Ryu and pursues only speed. Every time I step on the Lightning Sound Step, a small thunder sound is heard.

Kaang! Kang! Kang!

It's no match at all. Son Ando-ssi is just matching my attacks for the sake of the drama.

'I know I can't win anyway. But let's do everything we can.'

I created distance from Son Ando-ssi. I glanced at Oh Junhyuk, and he was sitting far away, opening his mouth and blankly watching my and Son Ando-ssi's battle. He didn't seem to have any intention of fighting.

'I have Cheonma Shingong besides Yeongcheon Ryu.'

The problem is that I can't properly unleash that Cheonma Shingong. Even if I imitate the shape in a plausible way, the proper power will not come out.

'Let's just try using it once. Maybe it will work.'

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Demonic Monarch's Gait).

It is one of the techniques of Cheonma Shingong that I am trying to use.

Cheonma Gunrimbo is a footwork that can be said to be the ultimate among them.

A footwork named Gunrim (Monarch's Advent) because it dominates the space around oneself.

Of course, I can't use it now. I'm just going to imitate this footwork in my own way. Perhaps I won't even be able to unleash 5% of the original Cheonma Gunrimbo's power.

"Sseup, ha. Sseup... ha!"

Full Concentration Demonic Breathing.

Form 69.

Cheonma Step!

My legs stepped on flashy footwork and approached Son Ando-ssi. Just as I was about to swing my lightning-filled knife, a flame explosion occurred.

"Keueoeoeok!"

My body flew several meters in the air and fell to the floor.

Son Ando-ssi grabbed his sword with both hands. A red flame formed on the sword like a tornado. I sensed the end and stretched out my left hand forward while sitting.

Son Ando-ssi swung his sword. A dragon made of flames flies towards me.

'I can't just lose. It's my last resort!'

A myriad of lightning (卍雷) appears in front of my left hand. I gathered all the mana I had and unleashed it on the myriad of lightning.

Kwareureureung!

Lightning is fired from the myriad of lightning. The lightning tears apart the dragon of flames and disappears.

"It's over."

Son Ando-ssi, who had already approached, pointed his sword at me. I felt the hot heat from the sword with the blazing flame.

"...Damn it."

I said a line that wasn't in the script and dropped the Hwaryeon Bido I was holding in my hand.

"Cut! Okay! Perfect!"

The director's cut sign dropped, and the staff all started clapping.

"Wow! That was really amazing."

"Isn't this more of a hit than using any CG?"

"Fire and lightning... it's really awesome."

It must be a rare scene. There are quite a few flame ability users, but Hunters with lightning abilities are so few that it's hard to find them.

"You were amazing? You didn't look like an E-class Hunter at all."

Son Ando-ssi held out his hand to me. I gave a bitter smile and took his hand and got up.

"Son Ando-ssi is very strong. I don't think I can win."

"I'm an A-class even though I look like this. Yujin-ssi, you seem to be able to use mana too, so you'll get stronger quickly. I want to scout you to our guild."

"No. I have circumstances, so scouting is a bit..."

"I know. It's related to Yeongcheon Ryu, right?"

"...Oh, you know Yeongcheon Ryu?"

"I've worked with Jin Woosung-ssi a few times."

It's a bit unexpected, but it's not surprising. Son Ando-ssi is older than he looks and is an A-class Hunter like Jin Woosung-ssi. It's possible enough.

"The footwork at the end wasn't Yeongcheon Ryu... Did you develop it independently?"

"Yes. That's right."

I nodded shamelessly. There is no King of Demonic Heavens here anyway.

"It's not finished yet, though..."

"I see. I think it will be a powerful footwork if it is properly completed. What is the name of the footwork? I want to know in advance because it will become famous later."

"Cheonma Step."

"Yes?"

"Cheonma Step."

"Ha, haha. You're a funny person. Yes. Cheonma Step. I remembered it."

Son Ando-ssi smiled refreshingly.

I smiled awkwardly.

'I can't call this kind of footwork Cheonma Gunrimbo.'

I couldn't even bring out 5% of the power of the true Cheonma Gunrimbo.

It is an insult to Cheonma Shingong to call this kind of footwork Cheonma Gunrimbo. That's why I decided to call it Cheonma Step until I complete Cheonma Gunrimbo. Although I may never be able to complete Cheonma Gunrimbo...

After that, I filmed another scene.

It wasn't a big deal, it was a scene where I was caught by the police and Hunter Association members. There wasn't a single line. Oh Junhyuk and I just had to bow our heads and be dragged away.

The filming we were supposed to do was over. We received 300,000 won each on the spot. No matter how I think about it, 300,000 won seems too little, but... well. I think it was a good experience.

"Seong Yujin-ssi. Can I have a word with you for a moment?"

It was the director. His name is Noh Hansoo. He is a man in his late 30s with a gentle impression.

"Yes. What's the matter?"
