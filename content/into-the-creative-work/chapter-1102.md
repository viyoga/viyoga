About 50 people died during this first mission. Quite a few people died, but the military had no reaction. They didn't even hold a simple funeral. They just calmly did what they had to do.

The kids just accepted it. Some were saddened by the death of close friends, but most accepted the situation without a word.

The dormitory changed. I originally lived in Dorm 11, but as the number of people decreased, it was reorganized.

Dorm Chief 205, who was in charge of Dorm 11, died on the first mission. A skinny guy with a good personality. I had talked to him a few times, so he occasionally popped into my head. Well, it was also because my human relationships were narrow.

When I arrived at Dorm 1, I recognized a few familiar faces.

Number 212, who was next to me, Number 202, a burly guy who wasn't exactly friendly, and the famous Number 31.

,Including me, there are 12 people in total. It's not a random assignment.... Judging by the fact that there are girls who often hang out with Number 31, I guess they considered grades and human relationships.'

Four guys. Eight girls. The proportion of girls was high. I couldn't shake the feeling that they were being considerate of Number 31.

My seat was fixed. Number 212 on the right, Number 202 on the left. Number 31's seat was on the opposite bunk.

,I heard you became a cripple. Looking at you, you're safe and sound.'

Number 202 sneered.

Hearing his sneer after a long time felt strangely welcome. I chuckled and waved at him. Number 202 scowled and turned his head away.

,I'm glad you're safe. I heard you got promoted to Level 2?'

Number 212 greeted me with a smile. I can't judge whether her true feelings match her expression. Of course, it's not necessarily bad if the outside and inside are different. In my opinion, it's stranger if the outside and inside match.

,I got lucky. But who's the Dorm Chief? Have you decided yet?'

Everyone around looked at Number 31. I felt like I asked something too obvious. Dorm Chief. It's a position similar to class president on Earth. It's insignificant, but it's a position with some power and privileges.

,Number 211.'

Number 31 called me. Everyone, including me, focused their attention on Number 31. Strangely enough, she had the power to attract people's attention with just her voice.

,I'd like you to be the Dorm Chief.'

To be honest, I wanted to refuse. There was little benefit to me being the Dorm Chief. The minor perks are literally minor. If I calculate it, it's just a waste of time. But it was difficult to refuse Number 31's words.

,Okay. I'll do it.'

I decided that it was better to just accept a little loss than to risk any possible disadvantages from refusing.

Number 31 nodded and started reading a book. The cover showed a Level 3 magic book.

After that, Instructor Venus called me.

,Number 211. Congratulations on becoming the Dorm Chief.'

,...Thank you.'

I held back a wry smile. I knew that the military was monitoring even the dormitory, but realizing it like this made me realize my slave status anew.

,You're the Squad Leader of Squad 1.'

,Squad Leader?'

,This time, we decided to operate in squads when organizing the dormitory. You, as the Dorm Chief, are the Squad Leader.'

I should have refused Number 31's words. I regretted it belatedly.

Instructor Venus gave me a shoulder mark signifying Squad Leader.

,I have one request. Treat Number 31 with sincerity. She's fundamentally different from you. Do you understand?'

,I will keep that in mind.'

,You're smart, so I believe you'll do well. Oh, right, have a drink before you go. I'll buy it to celebrate you becoming Squad Leader.'

Venus put a can of cola on the table.

I knew it from game knowledge. That there are drinks like cola and cider in this world too. But knowing it and actually seeing it felt different. I felt nostalgic for Earth and grabbed the can of cola. It was lukewarm. Just as I was about to be disappointed, Venus said.

,Cola is best served cold. And wizards can put that truth into practice. If you're Level 2, you should be able to control it.'

,...I'll try.'

The thought that it might be a trap crossed my mind. But the temptation of cola was great. I decided to compromise to some extent.

,Ice.'

Cold air swirled from my hand. Half of the cola froze. I pretended to have failed to control the cold on purpose.

,Not bad for the first time.'

,......'

Was this also a kind of test?

I lifted the cola and put it in my mouth. It was electrifying. It felt like every single cell in my body was waking up. Maybe the cola in this world is much tastier than the one on Earth.

Enjoying a moment of pleasure, I lowered the can of cola. I regretted the frozen half, but it couldn't be helped.

,A small reward. I might unexpectedly like it.'

Instructor Venus nodded at me.

,Go ahead and leave. Don't forget what I said.'

,Yes. I'll be on my way.'

As Squad Leader, I finished the evening roll call and lay down on the thin mattress.

I felt, goddamn it, comfortable.

,...I can't sleep because of the caffeine in the cola I drank earlier.'

And I thought of Earth.

I felt unpleasant. Because my memories of Earth were vague. I couldn't remember the faces of my friends or family very well.

I tossed and turned a little before falling asleep.

,...!!'

I woke up with my eyes wide open. The fluorescent lights on the ceiling were on. The digital clock on the wall showed that the current time was 4:07 AM.

I had a dream.

It was a dream with beautiful maids. In the dream, I mingled with the maids. As soon as I opened my eyes, the memory of the dream disappeared, but I know it was such a lewd dream that a priest would be horrified. I also seemed to have a special affection for one of the many maids.

,...Shibal.'

My crotch was wet.

It's obvious what the state is without having to check.

,I can't believe I had a wet dream.'

I looked around and headed to the bathroom.

Instructor Lexie said, patting me on the shoulder. She still had her uniform top unbuttoned, revealing some of her ample breasts. What happened this morning came to mind, and I became conscious of her for no reason.

,...Thank you. I survived thanks to the artifact Instructor Lexie-nim gave me.'

I said with sincerity. If it weren't for the Level 4 barrier of the artifact she gave me, I would have died at Nero's hands in all likelihood.

,You survived because you did well. More than that, I want to hear your impressions. How was it?'

,...What kind of impressions are you talking about?'

,Impressions of experiencing your first mission as a sniper.'

,Can I speak honestly?'

,Speak honestly.'

,I felt the limitations of a sniper.'

Nero easily recognized my sniping and reflected the bullet. If my reaction had been a little slower then, I would have been a goner.

Lexie nodded calmly at my words.

,I knew it. I felt the same way. Now, today I'll show you one level above.'

Lexie handed me a rifle and took out bullets. They weren't ordinary bullets. Red, blue, yellow, green, etc., bullets of various colors. There were formulas written on the surface, and the material was a metal other than copper.

,...Magic bullets.'

,That's right. Bullets enchanted with magic. If ordinary bullets don't work, you can use special bullets.'

,I know that magic bullets are expensive.'

,It's cheaper than your life. And this is the military. They support you to some extent. Well, I prefer the black market ones though.'

She chuckled.

,Can I take a closer look?'

,It's okay.'

I picked up a magic bullet with my thumb and forefinger and examined it closely. The depth of the formula engraved on the surface of the bullet was constant. It seems to have been engraved using a machine, not by a person.

,The formula itself is simple. Did they mix explosion with the fire system? Is it a mechanism that explodes the moment it hits?'

I narrowed my eyes. As a result of simulating the formula in my head, the power was insufficient with this formula alone.

,It's natural that there's a limit to the formula that can be engraved on a small bullet.... In this state, it's almost impossible to use. Did they engrave it on the inside of the cartridge too?'

After examining the magic bullet a few times, I realized that it didn't just contain magic formulas.

,The core of the magic bullet is alchemy. The magic formula is just a supporting level.'

I immediately abandoned the idea of making magic bullets myself. I had no opportunity or time to learn alchemy.

,In my case, it would be faster to cast magic on the bullets.'

Of course, bullets made of special materials were essential for that.

,Try using it once. You'll like it when you shoot it.'

At Lexie's words, I decided to shoot it once. I loaded the magic bullet, aimed at a target 500M away, and fired. The magic bullet flew through the air, leaving a red trail, and hit the target.

A small explosion occurred. The size is small, but the power is enough to blow off a person's head. Soon, crimson flames stuck to the target.

,A bursting bullet. It's quite expensive, but the performance is as you can see. It's effective when breaking barriers.'

She then handed me other bullets. Frost bullets that freeze and slow down the enemy, electric bullets that electrocute the enemy or disable machines, poison bullets that poison the target they hit, armor-piercing magic bullets that maximize penetration, etc. There were more types of magic bullets than I thought.

,It has the advantage of being able to choose according to the situation and target. The fact that it's easy to use is also an advantage.'

But I saw three disadvantages. One is, of course, the considerable price. And the fact that magic bullets are consumables and that their power is not as great as I thought.

,From my point of view, it's better to learn attack magic.'

I felt my interest in sniping wane. Thinking about my relationship with Instructor Lexie, I hid that fact and received shooting training. I'm going to do as much as I can since I've received something from Instructor Lexie.

Lexie probably intends to let me shoot magic bullets to show me the advantages of a sniper, but in reality, contrary to her plan, my desire for magic burned even more.

,You're doing well. You'll be the best sniper. Later, I'll teach you how to enchant magic on bullets.'

,...Yes. Thank you.'

,It's the second mission. Tonight, we'll wipe out a goblin horde that has taken up residence near the base without knowing their place.'

Morning roll call time. Instructor Venus said to us.

We hardened our faces.

Goblins are the weakest of the monsters. Even ordinary people can easily kill them with just a gun. Number 211 in my memory also fought and won against goblins under the pretext of training. Goblins are clearly easy opponents.

If it weren't for tonight.

,Tonight is the night the Dark Moon rises.'

,Dark Moon' is the main title of the game and one of the important settings of the game.

The Dark Moon is a black moon. A black moon that rises in the sky every 100 days.

Every time the Dark Moon rises, monsters become violent and stronger. Ghosts that are usually nowhere to be seen wander the city, and those with monster-type factors undergo mutations with a low probability. The mana in the atmosphere also becomes fierce. Also, monsters from the Abyss could appear through the black moon.

People in this world stay home and refrain from going outside as much as possible on the day the Dark Moon rises. It was natural because they didn't want to die, and that was common sense in this world.

But some people go out even on the Dark Moon.

Because there are many things you can get as the risk increases. Even in the game, the Dark Moon becomes a basic condition for doing special things. For example, a special dungeon opens, or you get a chance to get a special item.

,It's a good day to train.'

Instructor Venus said as if he was enjoying it. It was a statement that no one here, including me, could sympathize with.
