,Meteor Sword.'

A giant sword is created in the high sky.

The sudden creation of the giant sword caused the surrounding air to tremble. The Dragon God must have felt it too. But the Dragon God didn't take his eyes off me.

-It has changed. Now I can't even be sure of the near future.

"Shut up, lizard. I am enough to be the master of this world. Die."

The Meteor Sword falls, emitting a blue light. A blue trajectory is drawn like a meteor.

The Dragon God did not move even while watching the fall of the Meteor Sword. He is just floating in the same spot as when I first saw him.

That was confidence and arrogance. And the Dragon God deserved to show arrogance.

Air mixed with magic gathers. The air began to glow golden due to the magic. The air, which became a giant shield, blocked the falling giant Meteor Sword.

"Explode."

The Meteor Sword exploded and blue lightning burst out. The shield breaks and lightning flows into the Dragon God's body. It is definitely not ordinary lightning. Lightning imbued with my will and magic. Even the Dragon God had no choice but to stagger in the air.

But the Dragon God does not fall. The magic around him gathered into the Dragon God's body. The wounds the Dragon God suffered regenerate in an instant.

I clicked my tongue as I watched the Dragon God's appearance.

"That's ridiculous. The magic of this world moves on its own and gives power without compensation."

The Dragon God was no different from having infinite magic. Of course, there were limits to the output. Even if there is a well that never runs dry, the amount of water that can be scooped up at once is limited.

I turned my eyes and looked at Levre. Magic is being transferred to me from him. The land around Levre was rapidly becoming desolate and barren. It was the effect of forcibly sucking the energy of this planet.

,The energy Levre sends is not infinite. The amount sent to me at one time is only a drop in the bucket compared to the magic the Dragon God receives from the world. The longer I drag this out, the more disadvantageous I am.'

Of course, I had no intention of dragging out the battle for long.

The rocks embedded in the ground soared. More than hundreds of rocks rushed towards me all at once.

I waved my hand at the rocks while flying through the sky with telekinesis.

Pajijijijijik!

Thunderbolt Stream (雷天流) Lightning Split (裂雷)

One generated lightning splits and divides into several strands, touching hundreds of rocks.

Thunderbolt Stream (Raiden-ryu) Ultimate Skill (Gokugi) Explosion Thunder (Bakushinrai).

The lightning that touched the rock explodes. The rocks were shattered and the debris fell to the ground.

The movement of magic changes rapidly. It is compressed to one point and pours down on me.

,Gravity, huh. It's a method of crushing the space I'm in, not me.'

It's enough to evade before the Dragon God's magic turns into gravity.

[Using Lightning Leap.]

I teleported through the lightning remaining in the air.

I took out the Vajra Divine Lightning from the air and threw it at the Dragon God. The Vajra Divine Lightning became golden lightning and struck the Dragon God's forehead. Its power was too weak to penetrate the Dragon God's thick magic shield.

-It's an object containing mysterious power.

The Vajra Divine Lightning bounces upwards. Just as expected.

Great Vajra Guardian Art (大金剛護法術) Boji Daejangbu (寶志大丈夫).

Lightning bursts out from the center of the Vajra Divine Lightning, taking the form of a giant woman. A female Vajra warrior made of semi-transparent blue lightning. She is holding a giant sword similar to Hwaryeon Bido. As much as I poured in as much magic as possible, its size is about 70% of the Dragon God's.

Boji Daejangbu wields her sword in the sky in the style of Thunderbolt Stream. The Dragon God did not just stand still and take it. He counterattacked with his head and forelimbs.

Kwaaaaaang! Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

A monster fight between a blue giant woman and a golden giant dragon. Every time they clash, shockwaves occur, clouds are shattered, and the ground is split.

,Not good. The difference in magic is clear.'

The Dragon God's magic is being restored as soon as it is used. On the other hand, Boji Daejangbu did not have infinite magic. At this rate, it will be difficult to last even a minute.

"Bug. Can't you give me more energy?"

-This is the best I can do.

"Tch."

I clicked my tongue. It was difficult to maintain Boji Daejangbu with the magic Levre was sending me. I want to join that battle directly, but my magic is already running out.

,If this were the Tyrant World, I would have used the life force I had prepared in advance with black magic, but... there's no such thing here.'

But there is still a way.

There are mana potions in the inventory.

,Even if I drink 100 of the highest-grade mana potions, it's not enough.'

Even if one bottle is 300 milliliters, 100 bottles is 30 liters. It's not an amount that a human can drink all at once.

,Fortunately, there's something with good efficiency. Dragon Heart Blood.'

I opened the inventory and recklessly drank the Dragon Heart Blood. From Dragon King class to Ancient Dragon class to Sacred Dragon class. I drank them all without discrimination. Even when my stomach was full, I forced myself to drink. Originally, elixirs should be taken carefully, but I don't have time now.

,Since I have complete recovery, it doesn't matter if I die. I just need to be able to use my strength right now.'

My body now is a tyrant. With the talent of a tyrant, I should be able to suppress the side effects for a while and digest the elixir.

Feeling the power filling me up to the point of bursting, I went back to the battlefield.

"Bug. When I send a signal, lift me and the Dragon God up."

I jumped into the battle. The Dragon God's gaze turned to me. He remained calm even while fighting Boji Daejangbu.

-You have consumed a large amount of Dragon Heart Blood. But it is meaningless. I am the progenitor of all dragons, so the power of dragons cannot do anything to me.

"It's mine from the moment I have it."

I pointed at the Dragon God with my right index and middle fingers. Three Meteor Swords created in the sky fall with blue trajectories.

-It's useless.

Magic bursts out from the Dragon God. Immediately after, the space is distorted. When I frowned, Hytera's magic intervened and suppressed the distorted space.

"...It's not easy. I won't be able to do it continuously."

Hytera, who regained her senses, said with difficulty.

"Good job, Hytera."

Thanks to her, three Meteor Swords were embedded in the Dragon God's body.

"Explode."

Three Meteor Swords exploded at the same time. Even the Dragon God had no choice but to have his body destroyed since he was hit directly.

A fatal wound so severe that the giant intestines fell to the ground and formed a small mountain. Boji Daejangbu took advantage of the gap and slashed the Dragon God's body with her sword. But the Dragon God's body naturally gathers magic and regenerates.

"As expected. You can't regenerate magic while regenerating your body. The reason why you can't even defend against or counterattack Boji Daejangbu's attacks is because of that."

-Even if you realize that, nothing will change.

"No. A lot will change."

Boji Daejangbu grabs the Dragon God, lifts him up, and takes a throwing stance.

"Bug. Now is the time."

-I'm not a bug. I'm Levre.

At the same time as Boji Daejangbu throws the Dragon God, Levre pushed the Dragon God with telekinesis. But this alone was not enough.

"Vajra Divine Lightning."

Boji Daejangbu disappears and returns to the Vajra pestle. Instead, several giant lightning chains stretched out from the Vajra Divine Lightning and wrapped around the Dragon God.

Thunderbolt Stream Lightning Art Lightning Chain Curse Binding.

I grabbed the Vajra Divine Lightning with one hand.

"Go."

I throw it towards the sky.

The Vajra Divine Lightning soared vertically and flew into space with the Dragon God, who was regenerating his body.

It's not just lifting it with pure strength. It feels like creating a virtual orbital elevator using electromagnetic force and pulling it up. It's a principle similar to a coilgun rather than a railgun.

Thunderbolt Stream (Raikiri) Flying Thunder God (Hiraishin).

I turned my body into lightning and soared into space, chasing after the Dragon God.

I met him in space. The Lightning Chain Curse Binding was released, but the Dragon God's regeneration was much slower than when he was on the ground.

,As expected. There is no magic in space. It's no different from losing the magic received from the world.'

But the Dragon God was still the Dragon God. He drastically increased the regeneration speed of his body by making the size of his body smaller. His body regenerated in an instant. But the size was less than 10m.

-I admit it. Your idea wasn't bad. But I can just go back.

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido.

Domain Release.

The domain swept around.

I smiled slyly and said, imbuing my voice with magic. So that the Dragon God can hear my words even in space.

"Do you think I will allow you to return through space travel? You cannot return to Earth by any means other than physical methods until you defeat me. And with your current body, you will burn up due to atmospheric friction before your body falls to the ground."

-.......

The Dragon God was silent.

I held the Vajra Divine Lightning in my left hand and Hwaryeon Bido in my right hand. My body is slowly reaching its limit. The side effects of Dragon Heart Blood have begun to appear. So now I really had to make a decision.

-I have lost. The magic I can use now is less than half of what I usually have... Even if I kill you, if I use up all the remaining magic... there is no way to return to the ground. This body cannot withstand frictional heat.

"...I see. You are closer to a soul than a body, and what can be called a real body is that Earth. No. Should I call that Earth your Dragon Heart?"

-That's right. In that respect, even if I die, my Dragon Heart will continue to function. Even if the balance collapses, you can rest assured that the world will not be destroyed immediately.

"I have no intention of letting you off just because you admit defeat."

-That's what I should be saying. I have no intention of begging you for my life.

Pajijijijik.

Red lightning wraps around the blade of Hwaryeon Bido.

Thunderbolt Stream (雷天流) Thunder Gale Blade (雷罡刃).

The Dragon God and I rushed at each other.

The Dragon Heart was split in half and died. The Dragon God's body turned to dust and floated in space.

Victorious, I used a space travel scroll to return to the ground.
