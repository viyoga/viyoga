60km.

Surprisingly, it wasn't that far. Even more so if flying through the sky.

I rode the drone and flew through the sky, arriving at the Byeogam Highlands where the Blazing Dragon Impulse was.

It was a high altitude area filled with black rocks. As I came to this area, I felt a scorching heat. It's not because of the weather. It's the influence of one dragon.

,There are so many rocks that it's hard to see even from the sky. Where is it?

[Opening the Celestial Eye].

I started searching with the Celestial Eye instead of electromagnetic waves.

Electromagnetic waves are good for carefully searching the surrounding environment, but there is a limit to the range, and the Celestial Eye has no range limit as long as the gaze reaches.

,In the current situation, the Celestial Eye is better than electromagnetic waves. Furthermore, I can even see the flow of mana... no, the flow of energy called magic in this world with my eyes.

I tried to see through the rocks to find the hidden Blazing Dragon. But when I actually used the Celestial Eye, there was no need to do that.

The magic flowing in the space was slightly different from what I knew, and when I looked at the center of it, there was the Blazing Dragon.

The Blazing Dragon, who had been lying down behind a huge rock and sleeping until just now, slowly got up as if it felt my gaze.

It was a 20m huge black lizard. The skin was bumpy like a rock. Even when lying down, the height alone exceeds 10m. If it stood on two feet, it would easily exceed 50m. It has thick black wings and its head looks similar to a chicken's head.

"Keureureureu...."

Orange flames flow from the growling guy's mouth.

,Is that a High-Dragon class? It's stronger than I thought.

For reference, in the original work, it is judged that 10 A-class Dragon Hunters are needed to take on a High-Dragon class dragon without any preparation. If you use the dragon's weaknesses or expensive equipment, you can hunt with only 3-5 people.

,In reality, it seems like even an S-class Hunter can't guarantee 100% victory.

A High-Dragon class is a dragon that can easily destroy a city.

The Blazing Dragon spreads its large wings. It's thinking of flying.

"Since we met, I should say hello."

Tungsten bullets fall from the inventory. The bullets are fixed in front of me by electromagnetic force. I laid down an electromagnetic rail and strengthened the bullets by adding magic as if applying a coating liquid.

I struck the prepared tungsten bullet with my fist.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Cannon (電磁砲).

The railgun was fired, drawing a trajectory in the air with blue plasma, and hit the Blazing Dragon's head.

Kwaaaaaang!

The Blazing Dragon's head is bent to the side. The guy's horn is broken and part of his beak-like snout is broken. But his neck was not broken. The dragon's eyes, filled with murderous intent, turn to me.

The guy opened his snout and spat out a fireball. Even though the distance between me and him is about 900m, the fireball flew like a missile.

I reverse-summoned the drone I was riding and ran through the air.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I avoid the fireball and concentrate.

Kwareureureung!

A thick streak of lightning fell from the clear sky, accurately striking the Blazing Dragon's torso as it flew up. The Blazing Dragon got up without even staggering.

,Is it because of that damn thick skin? Lightning doesn't work.

Two effective ways to attack the Blazing Dragon come to mind. One is to strike directly beneath the skin, inside the body. The Inner Heavy Hand Technique (內家重手法) is effective for this. The other method is to use overwhelming physical force or an attack with high penetration.

,The railgun works. However, it seems like I need to hit it 2-3 times in a row.

There was a problem.

I also need some time to concentrate on the railgun. It's not a technique that can be fired randomly.

,And is that guy aware of that too...? He's attacking non-stop.

He continuously spits out fireballs from his mouth, and when he needs to catch his breath, he swings his forelimbs and tail. Slashes from his claws and shockwaves from his tail are flying towards me.

,Since I can see it, it's not difficult to avoid.

Aerial combat isn't a big problem for me. Since I can move through the air, it's the same as fighting on the ground. Moreover, by using the Flying Thunder God (飛雷神), I was able to perform movements that are impossible for a human body.

,This bastard. He's openly narrowing the distance. Is he confident in close combat?

It's not something I can't understand. Even though he's called the Blazing Dragon, his body is damn big.

I was just as confident, so I didn't increase the distance.

I summon the Hwaryeon Bido and grip it tightly and swing it.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷閃).

The blue spirit energy wrapped in red lightning struck the Blazing Dragon's wings. The Blazing Dragon took the hit without avoiding it. Blood burst from the wings, but considering the Blazing Dragon's size, it was only a minor wound.

As he gets closer, the surrounding heat is rising even more. The humidity disappears and it becomes dry.

,This is a bit dangerous.

I summon the Jeunryeong, an indigo dodecagonal metal badge. A large amount of mana is drained in an instant, and dark clouds appear in the sky.

Kureureureung.

A torrential rain pours down with the sound of thunder. The temperature drops and the humidity rises. The Blazing Dragon rolled its eyes as if embarrassed, and soon wrapped its whole body in flames. As soon as the rainwater touches the Blazing Dragon, it evaporates and a thick mist rises.

Of course, the fog couldn't cover my Celestial Eye.

,The flames are burning using magic as fuel. Even a protective aura won't last long against those flames, will it?

Close combat is canceled.

Pajeujeujeujeut. I changed about half of my body into electricity.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

The body is pulled back. Temporarily changing the entire body into lightning allows for faster movement, but... I need a body to attack while moving.

I hold a tungsten bullet in my hand and aim. The Blazing Dragon was startled and wildly fired slashes and shockwaves. It's preventing me from using the railgun.

,Judging from the way he's freaking out, he quickly developed railgun trauma.

It's enough to exert physical force in another way.

[Using Meteor Sword.]

A giant iron sword, 5m wide and 100m long, rushes towards the Blazing Dragon from the sky. The Blazing Dragon, who was freaking out at the railgun, was slow to react to the Meteor Sword.

The Blazing Dragon is hit by the Meteor Sword and crashes to the ground. A huge shockwave rises and sweeps the ground. The huge rocks cause dust to rise.

Hwareureureureuk!

The Meteor Sword begins to burn in the Blazing Dragon's flames.

Instead of exploding the Meteor Sword, I created a new giant Meteor Sword and dropped it on the Blazing Dragon. A Meteor Sword falling vertically like a guillotine.

"Kaaaaaaaak!"

"Are two not enough? Then how about three?"

[Using Meteor Sword.]

Three giant Meteor Swords fell on the Blazing Dragon.

It's called a Meteor Sword, but considering its size, it wouldn't be strange if it were an apartment. The Blazing Dragon, who couldn't avoid it and was hit 5 times in a row, died on the spot.

"Hunting complete."

It was a relief because my mana was slowly becoming precarious. I didn't need to drink a mana potion.

I approached the corpse of the dead Blazing Dragon. He was dead with his head cut off and his torso split open. A large amount of blood and guts were pouring out. It wasn't a very pleasant sight.

,The body is damn big, but the heart is only the size of a human fist.

I doubt this can act as a heart, but the Dragon Heart is different from other animal hearts. It is the source of power and a magic organ. The reason why large dragons can live without necessarily consuming nutrients is because of this Dragon Heart.

To extract this Dragon Heart, I had to search through the dragon's intestines. Fortunately, I had the Celestial Eye, so I could figure out the location of the Dragon Heart.

,The problem is that even so, I have to go inside that corpse to extract the Dragon Heart. Damn it.

When a dragon is alive, the Dragon Heart is harder than anything else. This is because the dragon unconsciously uses magic to protect the Dragon Heart first and foremost.

But when a dragon dies and becomes a corpse, the Dragon Heart is not protected by magic. For reference, the durability of the Dragon Heart is so weak that it can be crushed even by human hands.

,In other words, you have to be careful when extracting the Dragon Heart.

Fortunately, even if the body is in terrible condition, the Dragon Heart is mostly intact because it is protected by the outer heart chamber, also known as the Dragon Heart pouch.

,The outer heart chamber is bigger than my body.

This huge outer heart chamber acts as the animal's heart. The Dragon Heart is a magic organ inside the heart.

Anyway, I took the heart out, cut it with a knife, and took out a fist-sized Dragon Heart. I took out the Dragon Heart blood separately, put the Dragon Heart in the inventory, and appraised it.

[Blazing Dragon's Heart

This is the heart of the Blazing Dragon.

If a person with compatibility consumes it, they will acquire fire manipulation with a high probability.

If consumed in reality, there is a low probability of acquiring Fire (Lv.1 Trait). Compatibility is required.

Using the original can increase the probability of acquiring skills and traits.]

,Not a very low probability, but a low probability of obtaining a trait.

The Fire Trait is probably a trait similar to the Lightning Trait.

The problem was that there was no compatibility. I'm not drawn to the Dragon Heart. Since there is no compatibility, the possibility was completely 0%.

,I didn't really need it.

Anyway, I'm satisfied because I got the Dragon Heart.

I put the rest of the Blazing Dragon's corpse into the inventory. It's not as valuable as the Dragon Heart, but it sells for a high price. Claws, skin, bones, etc. are also used for Hunter equipment, and the insides of High-Dragon class dragons are also used in alchemy, so there was nothing to throw away. Therefore, if you catch one High-Dragon class dragon, you can earn at least 100 billion.

,I caught this alone. It's all mine.

I smiled and put it in the inventory. The torso, which was next to the head, did not fit into the inventory.

[Inventory space is insufficient.]

"Huh?"

My current inventory capacity is 1,000,000㎥. It's literally an enormous capacity, but it's blocked?

"It's not enough to not be able to put in one dragon... Ah. It's not like there are only dragons in the inventory."

Not only were there miscellaneous items, but also large items such as cars and bikes in the inventory. All kinds of resources were also in my inventory. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that an entire logistics warehouse is in my inventory.

,I should increase the size of my inventory this time.

[Would you like to increase the horizontal space of the inventory by consuming 10 points?]

After all, I'm going to use the inventory for the rest of my life, right? I decided to increase it generously.

[Current inventory internal space

Width: 1,000m.

Length: 1,000m.

Height: 1,000m.]

I used 27,000 points.

To be honest, I didn't really get a sense of how much space it was.

,Can I put a city in it? ...Is that difficult? It's only 1km wide, long, and high. I could fit a village and still have room to spare.

I put the dragon's corpse in the inventory and returned to the metropolis Gigantria.
