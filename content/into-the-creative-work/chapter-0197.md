,'The only way is to poison the well.'

To safely steal the medicinal herbs and spiritual medicines of the Herb Garden, the only way is to kill all the martial artists of the Herb Garden before entering.

,'There's a well in the Herb Garden. They use the groundwater from that well as drinking water.'

In this world, the higher your cultivation level, the stronger your body becomes, but it doesn't mean you develop immunity to poison.

,'Unless they've specifically trained to build immunity to poison.... The martial artists of the Shooting Star Sword Sect aren't like that. Still, they have a certain level of cultivation, so ordinary poisons won't work.'

The martial artists of the Herb Garden have skills that are at least above the mid-level of Chulji (出志). Even if they are poisoned by a fairly strong poison, they can overcome it by expelling the poison from their bodies.

Poison at the level of a Mamushi snake, commonly found in the mountains, won't do.

,'It's okay. I know a specialist in poisons.'

Having made up my mind, I started planning the schedule.

,'Let's do it tomorrow right away. After all, the Herb Garden spends every day in the same routine, so there's little to consider.'

And there's not much time left for the special mission. I need to steal the spiritual medicine, enhance it, eat it, and digest it.

•••

To poison the well in the Herb Garden, I need to get inside the Herb Garden. Throwing poison into the well from outside the Herb Garden is too far and impossible.

,'But I have a drone.'

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the drone for 35 minutes.]

I was confident in controlling the drone, but I used hacking. Using the hacking skill allows me to move things according to my will, so I can definitely release the poison. It's better to succeed reliably in one go than to accidentally screw up while controlling it with my own two hands.

The drone quietly flew towards the well inside the Herb Garden. Poison was hanging from the bottom of the drone.

,'Yuria-yang's meticulously crafted ultimate poison. Last Twilight.'

This poison, named Last Twilight, allows you to be perfectly fine for about 6 hours even if you're poisoned. It hides in your body for 6 hours after being poisoned, then starts to move in earnest, destroying your internal organs and brain.

,'It's such an incredibly dangerous poison that the cost of the ingredients alone was over 1 billion won in Korean currency.'

The drone went inside the well and dropped the poison. This completes the preparation. All that's left is to wait 6 hours.

,'It'll be around midnight in 6 hours.'

I retreated for now and started eating dinner with Seong Jigone-gun. While doing so, I walked around with Seong Jigone-gun to make sure I made an impression on other people. Since something will happen tonight, it's good to create an alibi.

,"The landlady of the desolate inn has big boobs. She's in her 40s, but she has beauty that even you, Yujin-gun, would be satisfied with. The number of men who have proposed to that woman is over 5...."

I was listening to Seong Jigone-gun's words. Seong Jigone-gun brought up stories about women every time he was alone with me. 70% of the stories were lewd jokes, and 30% were stories about beautiful middle-aged women. He's consistent with his tastes.

,"In short, you're saying let's go to Hunan City today?"

,"Yeah. I haven't been to the city lately because I've been learning the Shooting Star Sword Technique."

,"We have servants, though."

,"The servants are okay, but I'm getting a little tired of them. The city has a large population and various women."

Seong Jigone-gun doesn't know anything about the incident I'm about to commit at the Herb Garden.

That's because I didn't want to include Seong Jigone-gun and share my share. And this is something I can do well enough on my own.

,"Hey. Hey. We haven't been members of the Shooting Star Unit for very long, but we're going to play in Hunan City. What will other people think? Show some restraint."

,"Then when are you going to go to Hunan City?"

,"Around the week after next...."

I quickly shut my mouth. Because someone was approaching us.

,"Haha. New recruits. Training late into the evening again today?"

It was a member of the Shooting Star Unit. With short hair and scars on his neck and ears, he pretended to know us.

His name is Baek Doheo. He's our senior... no, he's a man who can be called our superior.

,"We greet Baek Doheo-nim."

,"We greet Baek Doheo-nim."

Seong Jigone-gun and I hurriedly performed the fist salute.

,"Greetings are enough. Are you guys going to the training hall again?"

,"Yes. We want to get stronger even a little faster."

I answered.

In reality, I'm just following Seong Jigone-gun's movements.

To maintain the routine of training with Seong Jigone-gun, I have to do it even if I don't want to. This way, if I use auto-progress, I can see myself training with Seong Jigone-gun.

,"That's commendable. Good. I'll hang out with you today."

,"Thank you!"

,"Thank you!"

There was no particular reason to refuse since our superior was helping us. Also, from my point of view, it's beneficial because I can create a solid alibi.

Midnight.

I made my body transparent with the Illusitor and headed to the Herb Garden without anyone knowing.

I dispelled the invisibility near a tree near the Herb Garden and launched the drone. I couldn't just go in recklessly. There might be guys who didn't eat anything in the evening and weren't poisoned.

Also, this world has mysterious objects called magical artifacts. It wouldn't be strange if the martial artists of the Herb Garden had magical artifacts that detoxify poison.

,'It's hard to figure it out completely with the drone. Then....'

I used the drone to drop a noisy toy in the center of the Herb Garden.

If the martial artists of the Herb Garden were awake, they would come to check the noisy toy. However, even after 15 minutes, no martial artist came to check the toy.

,'It's a pretty noisy toy, but no one's coming out? They must all be dead. Kkkkk.'

I entered the Herb Garden with a leisurely gait.

There's plenty of time. Until the servants of the Herb Garden come in the morning, no one will notice the abnormality of the Herb Garden.

I ran to the herb field first. There was an herb field that wasn't too wide. The medicinal herbs being grown here are all ingredients for spiritual pills, and if they're recklessly mass-produced, the probability of the quality dropping is very high.

Also, to artificially grow medicinal herbs that become spiritual medicines, it must be a place with spiritual veins where the spiritual energy of the earth flows.

,'Hehe. There must be about 100 of them.'

I took out a hoe and a basket from my inventory and started digging up the medicinal herbs in earnest. Every time I moved busily, medicinal herbs piled up in the basket.

,'This one seems to be less grown...? Oh well, I don't know. Let's dig it up for now, even if I have to dispose of it later.'

Even if they're less grown, I have the Bright Light Ascension. If I use the Bright Light Ascension, I'll be able to transform even less grown medicinal herbs into fully grown medicinal herbs.

,'If I sell all these medicinal herbs, I'll be able to live without worrying about money for the rest of my life.'

Of course, I had no intention of selling them at all. It's not like I'm short on money.

After digging up all the medicinal herbs, I entered the building inside the Herb Garden. It was a place that smelled of medicine.

,"......."

I found a man lying on the floor. It was a corpse with blood on its mouth. He died from the poison I released into the well.

I didn't feel any sympathy.

I rummaged through the man's chest. It was because I thought that since he was a martial artist of the Herb Garden, he might have at least one spiritual medicine.

,'Tsk. He's broke.'

I passed him and started searching the Herb Garden thoroughly.

I couldn't find any spiritual medicine easily. The only place I hadn't searched was the room of Seong Dochun, the Head of the Herb Garden.

,'Is Seong Dochun managing the spiritual medicine separately?'

It was understandable. Spiritual medicine is no different from treasure. From Seong Dochun's point of view, he can't easily trust the martial artists of the Herb Garden either.

I entered the room of Seong Dochun, the Head of the Herb Garden, and looked around.

The first thing I saw was Seong Dochun lying on the floor. Seong Dochun was holding a medicine box in his hand and died. It seemed he died trying to detoxify.

I searched Seong Dochun's body first. I thought Seong Dochun might be carrying an excellent spiritual medicine in his arms.

,'Why is this bastard lying down with a marble?'

If you look closely at his left hand, he was holding a marble in his hand. I left it alone because the black marble didn't seem like it would be worth any money. Except for the marble, Seong Dochun didn't have anything.

,'Is it in the medicine box?'

I opened the medicine box. There were medicines, but they weren't spiritual medicines. They were probably medicines with detoxification effects.

,'Let's take them for now. I can find out what kind of medicine it is later.'

I started rummaging through Seong Dochun's room. When I opened the luxurious medicine boxes in the room, I was able to find the spiritual medicine I had been wanting so much.

I found the Spirit Binding Pill, Fragrant Vermilion Pill, and Summer Accomplishment Pill.

There were 10 Fragrant Vermilion Pills and Summer Accomplishment Pills each, and 3 Spirit Binding Pills. Among these, the Spirit Binding Pill was the best spiritual medicine.

,'I want to eat it right now, but... I should eat it after enhancing it with the Bright Light Ascension.'

That's the most efficient way.

After taking all the spiritual medicines, I was about to run away but stopped when I saw the study.

,'Are these books related to alchemy? If I learn alchemy, I can make spiritual medicines myself. Let's take this too.'

After putting everything that looked expensive into my inventory, I ran away from the Herb Garden.

A satisfied smile spread across my face as I returned to my room.

,'I feel best on days like this.'

I put the Summer Accomplishment Pill into the Bright Light Ascension first. If I put in the Spirit Binding Pill, it would obviously take a long time, so I'm thinking of aiming for the sixth stage of Ipsik (立息) by using a spiritual medicine that can be enhanced relatively quickly.

,'If I eat the enhanced Spirit Binding Pill in my current state... I can aim for the seventh... no, even the eighth stage of Ipsik.'

I'm thinking of raising my cultivation level through spiritual medicine slowly.

It hasn't been long since the tragedy happened at the Herb Garden, but my cultivation level suddenly increases? Unless people are complete morons, they'll definitely suspect me.

,'Since there are many spiritual medicines in this world, I'll take care of myself in this world to some extent first... and I'll take a few spiritual medicines to reality. I'll take care of Yuria-yang's too while I'm at it.'

I was happily looking at the Bright Light Ascension when something suddenly came to mind.

There's an event in the original work that I really like. That event will happen soon.

,'...Damn it. The exact date wasn't specified.... Still, I know the location roughly, so I have no choice but to install surveillance cameras and check it every day.'

The next day.

As expected, the Shooting Star Sword Sect was turned upside down.

The martial artists of the Herb Garden, the most important place in the sect, were all poisoned and killed, and even the spiritual medicines and spiritual pill techniques were stolen. It would be strange if there wasn't a commotion.

Seong Godan, the Head of the family, declared a state of emergency. The sect's security became severely strict, and there were restrictions on entering and leaving the sect.

Going out of the sect and going in, both required the permission of the Head of the family.

In the unprecedented situation, the people of the Shooting Star Sword Sect carried out their daily tasks with tense faces. They couldn't just give up on their work altogether.

The servants working in the sect trembled as they worked. They moved carefully and cautiously. They paid attention to every step they took to avoid getting caught.

Seong Jigone-gun and I couldn't train. We had to stand guard in one of the areas within the sect.

,'It's still better. The other bastards are searching, rummaging through the mountains and even Hunan City.'

Seong Jigone-gun, standing next to me, looked at me with serious eyes.

,"This incident at the Herb Garden.... You didn't do it, did you?"

My face immediately frowned.

,"Are you crazy? Even if I'm a mess, I'm not that much of a mess."

I was surprised that Seong Jigone-gun had such a good sense.

,"Really? I was just saying."

,"Realistically, how would I get the poison that kills the Head of the Herb Garden?"

,"You got sleeping powder last time."

,"Is sleeping powder the same as the poison that killed the Head of the Herb Garden?"

I guess not. There's no way you could easily get such an extreme poison that could kill the Head of the Herb Garden, who's at the Five Energies (五氣) realm. But when will this situation end?

,"I don't know, but it'll last at least three months."

,"......You're saying I can't go to Hunan City for three months?"

,"That's the minimum. And Hunan City isn't important right now. You, don't touch the servants."

,"The servants... wouldn't it be okay if it's just occasionally?"

,"That occasionally could be the day of your memorial service, so please be patient."

,"Hoo. Who the hell is the culprit? I can't get anything done because of that damn bastard."

I answered calmly to Seong Jigone-gun, who was venting his frustration.

,"You're telling me."

I heard unbelievable news.

,'Seong Dochun, the Head of the Herb Garden, is alive...!'

I wanted to believe it was a lie, but I saw Seong Dochun moving with the elders with my own two eyes.

It wasn't difficult to find out why Seong Dochun was alive. The Shooting Star Sword Sect was buzzing with the fact that Seong Dochun was alive.

,'...He had the Blood Poison Pearl? Was the thing he was holding in his hand back then the Blood Poison Pearl?!'

The Blood Poison Pearl absorbed the poison.
