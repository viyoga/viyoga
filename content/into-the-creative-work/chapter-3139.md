Upon entering Buckingham Palace, Yuria met Florence Palmer, the Royal Head Maid.

A black-haired beauty in a maid's uniform.

An immaculate impression, not a single thread out of place.

On her chest she wore a badge bearing the sun emblem, signifying Prime.

Unlike Yuria, who smiled faintly while concealing her true intentions and feigning kindness, this woman always maintained a blank expression even before others.

"I've been waiting for you, Miss Yuria."

"It's an honor that you waited for me, Miss Florence. Should I call you Head Maid while I'm here?"

"Call me whatever is comfortable for you. Even though this is the royal palace, we're not so inflexible. You may call me Flora. Though the other maids usually call me Head Maid."

Was that her attempt at a joke? With that expressionless face, it was hard to tell.

"I see. I'll call you Head Maid as well."

"A shame. As my successor, I'd hoped you would treat me more casually."

"I'll only be working at the palace for 100 days. A short period. I don't wish to involve myself deeply with the royal household during that time. Both you and I would find ourselves in an awkward position."

"You still have no intention of working for the royal household? There are many benefits, you know."

"None. There is only one person to whom I pledge my wholehearted loyalty."

"Her Majesty the Queen will not give up. She will try to redirect your loyalty. In exchange, Her Majesty will grant you anything you desire. You could even receive a noble title."

"I find it difficult to understand why you would go to such lengths to recruit me. Doesn't the current royal household have you, Head Maid, to support it?"

"I am loyal only to Her Majesty the Queen. Her Majesty is concerned about the next generation."

"...When Her Majesty steps down, will you step down as well, Head Maid?"

"Yes. It is a promise made long ago. Your troublesome predecessor will likely not remain for long and will disappear, so your era will arrive swiftly. Therefore, do not rush to conclusions and take your time considering the future."

"You flatter me too much. What if someone overhears?"

"Surely you've already determined that there are no ears nearby."

"You hold me in high regard."

"If one cannot manage at least that much, one cannot become Prime-rank. That would be a demerit."

Yuria nodded calmly. She had grasped the moment she entered that there were no listening devices or watchers here.

"For 100 days, you will stay in the annex used by the servants. Each room is single-occupancy at the level of a luxury hotel, with a dedicated maid managing the facilities, so you won't experience much inconvenience."

Yuria looked around the room she would be using. The interior was luxurious enough to be called a noble's bedchamber.

"There are no electronic devices."

"To bring appliances into Buckingham Palace, one must receive the administrator's approval. Most are not approved for aesthetic reasons. We use artifacts where possible. Is there any artifact you need?"

"No. It just reminded me of the Maid Academy."

"It doesn't suit the times. I understand."

Throughout the annex, she encountered maids working for the royal household. They bowed to Florence, then examined Yuria with curious eyes. Yuria also quietly observed them. As expected of royal maids, perhaps.

Maids working in the royal household are at minimum Superior-rank. Talents that nobles would covet if one prepared seriously.

'And I don't see any maids below Grade 4.'

In effect, the servants could be considered the Queen's private soldiers.

"Miss Yuria, for the next three days you will be managing the palace facilities. You'll start with simple tasks like cleaning, cooking, and laundry."

"That's unexpected."

"A Head Maid must know how to handle every task. Afterward, you'll start by checking business partners and be assigned more confidential work."

"Understood."

Through Florence's introduction, Yuria met Diana, the Head of Facility Management. After the introduction, Florence disappeared to attend to her own duties. Yuria tried to track her presence through her senses, but the barrier installed throughout Buckingham Palace interfered. Using magic or abilities carelessly within the palace would result in immediate detection. Even for Yuria, openly using shadows was difficult.

"Miss Yuria. I've heard your name. They say you're the one who'll become the next Head Maid?"

Diana was a woman in her mid-thirties with brown hair. She was a beauty who exuded the air of an elegant noblewoman. Not just her—the servants working in the royal household were all exceptionally beautiful.

"The rumors are exaggerated. I'm merely a maid taking the Prime-rank examination."

"Ah. I see. Not yet, then."

Diana smiled. She interpreted Yuria's words as modesty. To Diana, a Prime-rank maid was synonymous with the Royal Head Maid.

Yuria did not actively explain her circumstances to Diana. She would be leaving Buckingham Palace in 100 days anyway.

"Follow me. I'll show you how the palace is cleaned. Though you won't be personally holding a broom to clean, Miss Yuria."

Diana led Yuria through the palace and annex, explaining as they went. Cleaning was the pretext; the real purpose was to familiarize her with the palace's internal facilities.

Of course, she didn't show everything. Certain spaces were deliberately avoided.

Yuria followed her movements while drawing a map in her mind. The blank spaces in that map were precisely Buckingham Palace's hidden areas. They could be secret passages connected to the outside, or places where illegal activities were conducted.

'They can't reveal everything inside the palace to an outsider like me.'

Yuria merely committed it to memory. She had no interest in those places.

Yuria checked the cleanliness of the buildings. They were spotless, maintained daily. Just from the cleaning standards, one could tell the royal servants were ability users.

"The principle is to clean by hand, not with machines."

"...With just brooms and rags? That sounds exhausting."

"In exchange, we use eco-friendly cleaning products. They're expensive, so even a light sweep removes stains easily. If you're running low on cleaning tools or detergent, just tell me or the butlers. The butlers handle inventory and vendor management."

"Are artifacts not allowed either?"

"As long as you don't get caught, it's fine. There aren't enough hands to truly clean this vast palace by hand alone."

Diana said with a smile. Even these exceptional individuals were still human. They didn't foolishly cling to principles. When there were no eyes watching, they cleaned with artifacts or magic.

'They're extremely sensitive to outside scrutiny. The servants must be under a lot of stress.'

When servant stress runs high, incident rates increase. Yuria controlled servant stress by not assigning unnecessary work and providing welfare.

'The royal servants aren't ordinary people, so there's no need to worry to that extent.'

It wasn't something Yuria needed to concern herself with in the first place. It was just her occupational habit surfacing briefly.

Yuria silently listened to Diana's explanations.

• • •

Over the past week, Florence had assigned Yuria a different task each day.

Starting from cleaning the palace to being dispatched to verify business partners. So far, Yuria's handling of tasks had been flawless. Every servant working in the royal household gave Yuria positive evaluations.

In Florence's eyes, Yuria would undoubtedly handle the remaining tasks perfectly as well.

Exceptional. Beyond reproach. Once she accumulated more experience, she would surely surpass Florence herself.

That was precisely why it was troubling.

'How can I bring Miss Yuria into the royal household.'

Yuria was a talent the royal household needed. If she became Florence's successor, the royal household would remain unshaken for the next 100 years or more.

The problem was that Yuria had no interest in the royal household whatsoever.

Her work was flawless, but passive. She had no intention of doing anything beyond what she was told. Even when probed subtly, she never revealed any loyalty toward the royal household.

'If I had my way, I'd drag her into the royal household by force.... But that's not possible.'

The work she did was important.

It could influence not just the royal household but all of Neo London. If Yuria, brought in by force, harbored resentment? Florence would wager that not only the royal household but Neo London itself could be endangered.

'Trust matters more than ability. Her loyalty is directed solely at Count Meyer.'

Count Meyer, as chairman of the LK Group, had money. This wasn't a problem that could be solved with money.

A noble title was the same. Yuria showed no interest in honor.

'If I forcibly separate the two, Miss Yuria would harbor hatred toward the royal household. If her loyalty is that firm... there's nothing I can do.'

Still, Florence would continue persuading her for the remaining 93 days. If Yuria refused to the very end, there would be no choice but to give up. One couldn't force such an important position—one that influenced the royal household and Neo London—onto someone who didn't want it.

Should she try attaching a royal to her? Just as Florence herself had come to devote her loyalty to the current Queen, perhaps Yuria could be made to devote herself to one of the royals? She considered it, but the possibility was slim. Yuria already had someone to whom she pledged her loyalty.

'......I hold Miss Yuria's loyalty in high regard. It's merely regrettable that the direction of that loyalty isn't toward the royal household.'

The current Queen and Florence's master, Victoria VI, would not give up on Yuria easily.

Florence and the Queen's positions differed. Florence pledged loyalty to the Queen, but the Queen could not help but think of the royal household.

'Her Majesty appears gentle, but she has the temperament of a gambler. However, that gambler's instinct sometimes leads her to cross the line. Most cases can be resolved, but... this time, it seems it won't be.'

If the Queen seemed about to cross the line, Florence would step in to stop her herself. It had always been that way.

• • •

On the tenth day of Yuria working at the palace.

She received an external assignment from Florence.

To investigate for three days a noble who operated a corporation in which the royal household held shares. If corruption was discovered, it would be dealt with.

The investigation method was free. She could even request assistance from the royal household.

'If I receive help from the royal household, my score will be docked.'

She confirmed the assignment and left the palace. She felt the gaze of a watcher following her.

Yuria stepped into the shadows of an alleyway. The watchers hurriedly moved into the alley, but Yuria was already gone.

Emerging from a different alley some distance away, Yuria took out her phone from within the shadows and contacted someone.

"Miss Shizuka. Are you available to talk right now?"

Haga Shizuka.

A female ninja otherwise known as the Invisible Blade.
