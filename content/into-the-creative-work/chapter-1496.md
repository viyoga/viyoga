Kwaang!

Wearing full body armor, my heavy body pierced through the ceiling and landed on the floor.

I looked around.

It was a slightly different place than before. The floor was damp, and a thin membrane was stretched across the walls. Inside the membrane, a creature that looked like a combination of a baby and a larva was wriggling. In one corner, a human corpse was sprawled out. It was their food.

,I've successfully arrived at the hatchery.'

That meant the heart of the Colossus was beneath this floor I was standing on.

It was something to be happy about. But I felt uneasy.

,Why aren't there any demons managing the demon hatchery? Even if the paladins above are drawing the demons' attention... this is too empty.'

Even I can't ignore how powerful these guys are now. But there was no one in the hatchery right now.

,Something seems to have gone wrong....'

The problem was that I couldn't tell where things went wrong, and even if I knew, I couldn't stop it.

I held the Execution Sword and swung it at the wall. The wall cracked open. Along with orange liquid, demon seeds poured onto the floor. Demons with the faces of babies and the bodies of larvae. I crushed them all to death and stuck the Execution Sword into the floor.

,Divine Release!'

Kwaang!

The floor collapsed and my body fell. Having landed successfully this time as well, I quietly stared ahead.

Kukung! Kukung! Kukung!

A huge purple heart was beating.

It was the heart of the Colossus. Every time the giant heart pulsed, a huge wave of magic power was felt.

I took a step towards the heart.

The wave of magic power pushed me back. I tensed my body and took one step at a time towards the heart.

,Die, Colossus.'

I thrust the Execution Sword into the heart.

Because it was such a sharp sword, it felt like sticking a knife into tofu.

Black blood poured out of the giant heart. It was blood condensed with the magic power of the Great Demon Colossus. It could be said to be a curse in itself. I wrapped my body in divine power. The Colossus's blood dared not invade my body.

I slashed the Execution Sword downwards. A large amount of blood drained from the heart. The heart shrank slightly.

Kiiiiiiiiing.

I examined the inside of the heart with the Holy Eye.

There was a heart within the heart. That heart was small enough to fit in one hand.

If the large outer heart was for maintaining the Colossus's giant body, the small heart within the heart was where the Colossus's soul was located.

To kill him, I naturally had to cut this small heart.

,And I can enhance my master skill with this small heart.'

I tore the small heart out with my hand and cut it with the Execution Sword.

Kuweo-eoeoeoeoeoeoeong!

The Colossus screamed. And immediately after, the floor began to shake as if there was an earthquake. Even explosions could be heard. I realized that the Colossus's giant body had collapsed.

The Colossus's soul clung to my body. The energy of corruption swept from my toes to the top of my head. But I endured the energy of corruption without batting an eye. At this point, this level of corruption energy couldn't do anything to me.

,The Colossus is dead. Now all that's left is to escape.'

I looked at the ceiling. I had to go up.

,Wings of Dawn.'

Golden wings appeared behind my back. I instinctively used the wings. My body soared towards the ceiling.

I returned to the five-way intersection through the broken ceiling hole. I retracted the wings of light and ran through the passage. The Wings of Dawn were all good, but the consumption of divine power was too great. It was better not to use them unless necessary.

,There it is.'

I found the exit. I came out of the exit without hesitation.

There was no time to enjoy the fresh air.

,Kkueaaaaaaak!'

With a desperate scream, the paladin's body was torn to pieces in mid-air. Limbs fell and rolled on the floor, and blood and guts flowed from the torso. The paladin's head rose into the sky like a rocket, then was chewed on by a demon resembling an eagle.

,What the....'

Demons were overflowing. Even if this was Colossus, there were too many.

Tens of thousands of demons were endlessly pouring out from under Colossus, and winged demons were swarming in the sky, covering the blue sky.

I gritted my teeth. Seeing the demons, irritation and anger surged. I swung the Execution Sword towards the sky. The Divine Sword manifested, and golden Sword energy swept away six or seven demons in an instant.

,Valentier's Paladin!!'

Just as I was about to swing the Execution Sword again, someone called out to me.

It was a paladin from Zhigoulas, his whole body soaked in demon blood. He was missing his left arm, and three paladins around him were fighting against the demons. The corpses of two paladins were lying around him.

,Zhigoulas's Paladin! What happened?!'

,A Great Demon! A Great Demon has appeared, leading the demon forces!'

,A Great Demon...?!'

I regained my composure. Then I felt a presence more intense than any demon. My gaze turned to the sky.

It dared to stand against the sun, as a demon. The sun peeked through as if it were a halo.

The demon had the body of a woman.

She had bat wings, and her hair was as black as the abyss. Her voluptuous breasts and genitals were barely covered. Her knees and calves were those of a goat, and six horns sprouted around her head.

The Great Demon Bleat.

A demon with a face very similar to the Goddess Herianne was smiling in the sky.

,Why is that demon here?!'

Bleat gave me a look and smiled with her eyes. She was confident of her victory.

,It's blasphemy.'

Spreading evil with an appearance similar to the Goddess Herianne.

Bleat's existence itself was blasphemy.

I gripped the hilt of the Execution Sword tightly.

,I'll kill her.'

A demon was in front of me. What I had to do as a paladin was clear.

,Divine Sword.'

Golden divinity gathered on the Execution Sword, forming a sword body. I swung the sword towards Bleat. The brilliantly shining golden Sword energy tore through the air and flew. But the Sword energy never reached the Great Demon. Countless demons flying in the sky flew in and became shields, blocking the Sword energy.

More than 30 demons fell to the ground. But Bleat didn't even twitch an eyebrow. Rather, she laughed loudly as if she was very pleased with the current situation.

,Kalkalkalkal! You think you can kill me with just that? Wake up to reality. The reason you humans are still alive is not because you're good. It's not because of Herianne, that damn bitch. You're just livestock. Our food. A source of nutrition. The only reason you've been alive until now is because we've kept you alive.'

The Great Demon Bleat's voice was exactly like the Goddess Herianne's voice. Because of that ridiculous bullshit, anger surged.

,That damn bitch.... I'll kill you right now.'

I'll wipe them out with the biggest hit I can do.

,Sacrifice!'

I give up my life.

It's okay.

Because I have one resurrection and full recovery.

I tasted the sensation of the cells in my whole body exploding. Immediately after, a miracle occurred due to Sacrifice.

Holy energy began to gather in the sky. The clouds receded and golden light took their place.

The Great Demon Bleat's expression hardened. Bleat glared at the sky as if to kill it and muttered.

,That damn bitch. Playing tricks?'

The light of the heavens fell.

,Kkiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiik!'

The demons screamed all at once. The demons flying in the sky fell to the ground after being hit by the light, and the demons on the ground burst into flames. Thousands of demons were erased at once. Only a large crater was left in the place where they had been.

And the Great Demon Bleat was fine.

She was still flying in the sky with her arms crossed. She looked at the sky as if she felt bad, then lowered her head and looked at me.

I was resurrected. I took a stance and aimed the Execution Sword at Bleat.

,I don't know where a human like you came from... but the game is over. You've gone too far for livestock.'

Ttaak.

Bleat snapped her fingers. At her command, demons began to rush in like a tidal wave.

Even after eliminating thousands of demons, tens of thousands of demons still remained.

I was about to use Sacrifice again, but hesitated.

,...No. Even if I use the Light of the Heavens again to kill thousands... I won't be able to withstand it.'

Thousands of demon souls clung to me, causing the energy of corruption. The energy of corruption was enveloping my body. It was hard for me to endure it in my current state.

,Holy Water...!'

I summoned Holy Water and sprinkled it on my body. The energy of corruption decreased, but that was it. It wasn't a fundamental solution.

,Now, demons. Go. Go and die.'

Bleat was trying to corrupt me by abandoning the demons. What an inefficient yet evil scheme.

,Valentier's Paladin! We have no chance of winning right now! We have to run away!'

Zhigoulas's Paladin touched my shoulder. I didn't want to admit it, but he was right.

,...Do you think we can escape? Demons are everywhere.'

,We'll open the way. You focus on running away.'

,...It only sounds like you're saying you'll sacrifice yourselves.'

,If you die, Valentier's Saintess will also die. It means the Great Demon's soul will be released. Even if we all die, you must survive. That's the only way you can kill those damn Great Demons!'

,Zhigoulas's Paladin is right!'

,Even if we all die, you must live!'

,Valentier's Paladin! Survive! And kill that bitch and exterminate the demons!'

The paladins shouted.

Reaching their limits, they threw themselves towards the relatively weak forces to open the way.

,Valentier's Paladin! Take out your wings! I'll take the lead!'

Zhigoulas's Paladin manifested the Wings of Dawn.

,...Escaping is the best option here.'

I also took out the Wings of Dawn. The wings of light fluttered and flew in one direction.

,Chase him.'

Bleat ordered the demons. The demons chased after me like crazy, but the remaining three paladins blocked their path.

Bleat didn't move.

,No, she can't move. She's pretending to be fine, but she was damaged when the Light of the Heavens fell. It'll take time to recover.'

While flying, winged demons attacked. They were demons that the three paladins had let through. Before I could step in, Zhigoulas's Paladin said.

,I know you're at your limit. I'll take care of the demons. If you become corrupted... everything will be over.'

A 30-minute chase.

The exhausted me and Zhigoulas's Paladin landed in a quiet forest. Zhigoulas's Paladin grabbed my shoulder with one hand.

,Valentier's Paladin. Listen carefully. Bleat will start a full-scale invasion. The Order will fight back as much as possible, but... all they can do is hold out. Take Valentier's Saintess and take refuge in a safe place first. And... deal with Bleat in the best possible condition. When Bleat dies, the demon forces will naturally scatter. That's just how those guys are.'

,You're talking like you're going to die. First, take out the Return Stone and return to the Sanctuary. We'll figure out a plan after that.'

There were no demons around, so I should be able to return to the Sanctuary right away.

,...No. I can't go back. I've already exceeded my limit.'

Kiiiiing.

I used the Holy Eye.

Zhigoulas's Paladin had already begun to be corrupted. The energy of corruption was mutating his insides. Since he had already started to be corrupted, the Return Stone wouldn't react.

I took the Execution Sword and stabbed the paladin in the chest.

The paladin coughed up blood and laughed.

,You're quick. As expected of Valentier's Paladin, who has no blood or tears.'

,...Any last words?'

,No. I don't have a family. No, I have one, but... she'll die soon. I just feel sorry for her. Valentier's Paladin! Swear to kill the Great Demon Bleat and destroy all evil!'

,I swear on my name and my faith. I will destroy all evil.'

,...I believe you. You'll be able to do it.'

Zhigoulas's Paladin's head dropped.

Immediately after, the paladin's fallen head was lifted up.

,Kkueaaaaaaah!'

Reddened face, elongated fangs. It wasn't the paladin's face.

Seogeok.

I beheaded the corrupted one.
