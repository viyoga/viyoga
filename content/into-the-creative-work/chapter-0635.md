Lilsne meticulously examined the dress.

Free.

She could get a luxury dress worth a small house for free. Even if she felt burdened, she, being crazy about money, wouldn't miss this opportunity. Although the price of such dresses plummets when sold secondhand.

,"How about this dress? Don't you think it would suit Agassi's green eyes?"

The dress Madame recommended to Lilsne was a pale yellow color. It was a simple dress that revealed the curves of the body. It would look great on Lilsne, who had long legs and arms and a slim, toned figure.

"It's a nice dress. But it's not my style."

Lilsne refused with a blank face. Her gaze was directed towards another dress.

It was a black dress. It gave off the opposite vibe of the innocent dress the Madame had recommended. The chest and shoulder areas were open, and the side of the dress was torn, so her thighs would be visible when she wore it. However, large jewels were embedded in the skirt and chest.

I understood immediately. The reason Lilsne was interested in that dress was because of the jewels.

"Madame. What is the price of this dress?"

When Lilsne asked, the Madame said with a smile still on her face.

,"It's called 'Autumn Starry Night' and it's my masterpiece. The price is 52 million Penny. It's a bit expensive, right? But I can't help it. The jewel on the chest is called 'Luna Night' and it's very expensive."

"Luna Night...."

Lilsne muttered softly and looked at the jewel. It was a shimmering sky-blue jewel, and when you looked into it deeply, small lights inside the jewel sparkled like stars in the night sky. Even I thought it was a mysterious and beautiful jewel.

Lilsne quietly looked at me, gauging my reaction. She seemed to like the dress, but she seemed burdened by the expensive price. 55 million Penny was an amount that even a commoner couldn't easily save even if they worked for 20 years.

Probably, if she took out only the 'Luna Night' jewel attached to that dress and sold it separately, it would fetch 30 million Penny.

"...I think another dress would be better."

I grabbed her as she tried to pass by.

"If you like that one, go with that one."

"No. Another dress is better."

Lilsne unnaturally avoided looking at the black dress. It must be because she thought she would have lingering feelings if she looked at it.

"Lilsne. You picked out a tuxedo for me. So shouldn't I pick out your dress this time? I recommend this dress. You're basically beautiful, so this dress will look good on you too."

"The gentleman is right. Agassi has fair skin and an excellent figure, so this dress will look great on you."

Even the Madame was helping me out. From her point of view, she would want to sell this expensive dress.

"Bu, but...."

"You're not worried about my financial situation, are you? I can buy this entire building."

The Madame looked at me, surprised. It wasn't a bluff. I had that much Penny. I couldn't convert it into AP, the currency of the system store, so there weren't many places to use it anyway.

Lilsne stared blankly at me for a moment. A reddish blush appeared on her white face. She seemed overwhelmed by my wealth.

"Th, then I'll accept Eugene-ssi's opinion and choose this dress."

"You've made a good decision! It's a choice you won't regret. You will be the main characters of this decennial!"

The Madame clapped her hands with joy and continued.

"We need to adjust the dress and tuxedo to your sizes for a moment."

"Will it take long?"

I asked.

"Hehe. What do you think of my skills...? It will take 30 minutes. Should I send it through someone? Or will you take it yourself?"

"There's no need to bother using someone. Right, Lilsne?"

"Yes. It's no problem to carry it. Please take good care of us, Madame."

"Shall we start with Agassi first? There's a dressing room over there, so try changing into it once."

•••

Lilsne, who came out of the store, hugged the box preciously. She glared around with a fierce look in her eyes. She seemed to be going crazy with anxiety because of the enormous treasure. Well, it was a treasure from her point of view. Because what was in the box was a dress worth a whopping 55 million Penny.

"Lilsne. I'm sorry, but there's a place I need to stop by."

"......Can't we just go back right away?"

"We need to buy shoes and accessories to match the dress. Imagine it. Expensive clothes with shabby shoes. Would that match? I'll buy you shoes too."

"I just keep receiving like this...."

"I want to buy them for you."

I looked at Lilsne with serious eyes. Lilsne's face turned red all the way to her long ears. As expected, showing off my wealth was the answer for her.

"A, alright."

"Before that, let's leave the clothes somewhere since they're cumbersome."

Lilsne was startled.

"What? This dress is worth so much, how can we entrust it to someone!"

"It's not worth much, so we can just buy it again. And I'm thinking of entrusting it to a reliable place for a few hours. And Lilsne. Now that it's come to this, let's eat dinner here too. Is that okay?"

"Until dinner...."

Lilsne seemed reluctant. But when I kept looking at her with pleading eyes, she reluctantly nodded. If it were her usual self, she would have rejected my suggestion outright, but now she had received a big gift and had no choice but to be mindful of me. In terms of money, it was like receiving a house as a gift, so how could she not be mindful?

,'If it were a normal woman, she would have spread her legs from the moment I gifted her a house. Hehe.'

Lilsne is a money-grubber who likes money, but she knows how to keep the line. She keeps contracts related to money like iron, and she doesn't sell her body for money.

,'That's Lilsne's charm.'

Lilsne and I headed to a fairly famous luxury hotel in this area. It would be nice to come to sleep, but our purpose is to leave our belongings.

I said to the hotel owner.

"Please take care of this box until tonight. I'll come to pick it up right after dinner."

"I'm sorry. Customer. We are not a place to keep things."

"I'll give you 30,000 Penny as a tip."

"All of our hotel's security guards are former outcasts who used to be something. If you just entrust us with the item, we will keep it safe."

Money was great.

We left our luggage at the luxury hotel and went around shoe stores and accessory stores. Even after that, we had some time left, so we took a walk around the city and went around to various places.

For dinner, we ate whole roasted pig, which is said to be popular among the wealthy these days. It wasn't as good as the steak I had for lunch, but it was quite delicious.

"I just keep receiving like this, so I keep feeling sorry to Eugene-ssi."

"I want to do it for you, so don't feel burdened and eat comfortably. I'm happy to be here with you."

"Still...."

It was then.

Through the restaurant window, I saw the security forces rushing by.

"The security forces are fully armed."

"Someone must have started a fight in the city. The city's security forces will take care of it, so we don't need to worry about it. Let's continue our meal."

"Yes. Still, it's impossible for the two of us to eat a whole roasted pig...."

"You don't have to force yourself to eat it. Just eat the delicious parts. It'll be thrown away anyway."

"To throw away such food...."

"Originally, this restaurant is for luxury. Sometimes nobles come here too."

"Luxury, is it.... I'm getting it for free, so I don't know if I can call it luxury...."

"Don't think too deeply. Enjoy this moment."

"...Yes. But I feel a bit bad to be enjoying it alone. Wouldn't Seohee-ssi and Seohyun-ssi get angry if they found out?"

You don't have to worry about that. I promised to come to this city with them on different days.

I smiled at her.

"I told you to enjoy this moment."

"...Yes."

After finishing dinner, we returned to the luxury hotel where we had left our clothes. However, what greeted us was not a decent luxury hotel, but a building on the verge of ruin with shattered windows, collapsed walls, and even bloodstains on the floor.

The security forces and people were crowded around the building.

"The reason the security forces moved so urgently, could it be...!"

Lilsne's face turned pale.

I passed by the security forces and entered the building. Lilsne followed behind me with staggering steps.

I found the hotel owner sitting among the wreckage of the furniture. His face was covered in blue bruises as if he had been severely beaten, and his neat clothes were covered in blood. He opened his beaten lips. A few of his teeth were missing.

"I'm sorry.... I couldn't protect the item you entrusted to me...."

"Who was the opponent?"

"It was the Alborg Bandit Gang.... It was not enough to stop them with our hotel's security guards. Fortunately, the security guards are still alive, but.... I'm sorry. I have nothing to say."

"No. It's unlikely that the bandits suddenly attacked the hotel.... It must also be because we moved carelessly. I will compensate for the damage the hotel has suffered."

The hotel owner shed tears.

"Huu.... Thank you. Thank you."

Badeut. Badeut. Badeut.

I heard the sound of grinding teeth from behind. The hotel owner and I were surprised and looked at Lilsne.

"The Alborg Bandit Gang.... I see. How dare a bandit gang steal our belongings. They must have a lot of nerve."

"Li, Lilsne. Calm down."

"Calm down? I can't. How can I calm down when we've lost so much money."

"It's okay. I'll get you another dress."

"Eugene-ssi. I can't let this go. I happen to see traces around here. I'll show you my skills as a ranger. I will definitely get the dress back by today!"

Lilsne's eyes shine. She frantically looked around, searching for traces of the Alborg Bandit Gang. It seemed that I and the hotel owner were no longer in her eyes.

I turned my head slightly.

I met eyes with the hotel owner.

I raised my thumb towards him. And he also raised his thumb.

What is there to hide? This whole situation was all according to my plan.

I met the hotel owner a few days ago and made a deal. The hotel owner moved according to the plan and succeeded.

,'I can't just go back to the A-Plan Legion castle with Lilsne like this. You have to create opportunities.'

Lilsne, who is a money-grubber, moved as expected. The bandits stole the items and ran away, leaving traces. And Lilsne happened to be a ranger with excellent skills. There was no way she, a money-grubber, would easily give up an expensive dress.

,'The hotel owner seems happy.'

It was natural since he would get a huge amount of money. He will now dispose of the luxury hotel and return to another large city. He will start a new business with the money I gave him as capital.

"Eugene-ssi! I found a trace! We need to track those guys down right now!"

Lilsne called me in 5 minutes. The way she found the traces was simple. She asked the security forces and citizens. There was nothing as certain as the statements of witnesses.

"What is the information about the Alborg Bandit Gang?"

Lilsne looked at the security forces. A soldier stepped forward and said to me.

"They are a bandit gang that has been gaining fame near the city these days. The bandits who attacked this hotel are 8, but the total number of the Alborg Bandit Gang is over 30. The security forces have attempted to subdue them, but have failed every time. They are not ordinary guys. Be careful if you track them down. We would like to help you, but we cannot move because it violates the rules."

"Thank you for letting me know. Is there a bounty on those guys?"

"There is 62 million Penny on the head of the boss, Alborg Madekas."

Lilsne's face hardened at the amount. This was because the bounty was quite high for a bandit. A bounty exceeding 50 million Penny was not an opponent to be taken lightly.

"But I can't give up. Eugene-ssi. We need to track those guys down quickly before they completely hide."

It was a sound to move right away.

The soldier raised his hand and pointed to the right.

"They ran away to the south. It is certain that they have left the city, but.... We don't know where their hideout is."

"I'm a ranger. I'll track down the bandits' traces."

Before starting the full-scale pursuit, Lilsne bought a suitable bow and 30 arrows from the weapon store and began to track the Alborg Bandit Gang.
