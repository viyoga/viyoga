No matter how many times I tried, my potential never bloomed.

Even Seong Hari's fuss didn't change the outcome. Then, suddenly, Seong Hari's attitude changed.

,"Well, what can you do? Yujin-ie has no choice but to stay here."

,"No, Mom. You know how strong I am to some extent. I'm a spirit user in the first place."

Spirit users are more influenced by the power of the spirit they contract with than their own skills.

,"Seong Hari. Don't overprotect your son. You also know that Seong Yujin's individual skills are already beyond the student level. The reason why potential blooming doesn't work must be because his growth has ended."

,"Aren't there any items for me?"

,"Of course, there are."

She held out a bracelet with a rainbow-colored jewel embedded in it. It was a design that suited women better than men.

『Bracelet of Arrest

Rank: S

Spirit affinity increases.

Arrest can be summoned once a week.』

,"This is."

,"Hehe. As expected, you recognized it right away. It's a bracelet imbued with the power of a spirit. It has the ability to increase spirit affinity."

,"You're giving me something precious."

Items that increase spirit affinity are extremely rare. It was definitely an effect that would help a spirit user.

,"This item is called the Bracelet of Arrest. It allows you to summon Arrest, a top-tier wind spirit."

Seong Hari chimed in from the side.

,"Wait a minute. Being able to summon a spirit is different from controlling it. You're not just able to summon the spirit, are you?"

,"...Being able to summon a top-tier wind spirit is already an amazing item. What happens after that depends on the spirit user's skills."

,"You know that wind spirits are fickle, right?"

,"Isn't your son the best spirit user with Spirit Eyes? What are you so worried about?"

,"It would be strange if I wasn't worried! Yujin-ah! You know how dangerous top-tier spirits are without me having to tell you, right? Don't summon it if you can help it. No, only summon it when I'm around! I'll help you contract with the spirit!"

,"Just because Mom is here doesn't mean I'll succeed in contracting. I think the chances of failure are higher."

Spirits fear Seong Hari. That's the reaction of low-level spirits. Top-tier spirits, on the other hand, don't fear Seong Hari but rather antagonize her. Seong Hari being around would more likely hinder the contract.

Seong Hari knew that too, so she couldn't argue back.

I put on the Bracelet of Arrest. I'm not going to summon Arrest and try to contract with it right away.

,'There's a high chance of failing the contract. And I don't necessarily need to contract with it. I just need to be able to use its power.'

Tenrai Nagisa probably gave me this because of Susanoo's power. She must have vaguely thought there was some connection.

,"I want you guys to disrupt the Tsukuyomi ritual. Komagome Tokinosuke, an executive of Madojeong, is conducting the ritual."

,"Dean-nim. Isn't the Amaterasu side more dangerous? In terms of power, Amaterasu is more dangerous than Tsukuyomi."

Mieko's words made Tenrai Nagisa nod.

,"That's true in terms of power alone. But we've already sent someone to the Amaterasu side."

,"...I understand. I'll trust you on that side, Dean-nim."

,"To continue, the Tsukuyomi ritual is being held in Kyoto. To be exact, it's being held in one of the abandoned shrines. I'll use my ability to send you to Kyoto. Stop the ritual there. Don't forget that our victory condition isn't to defeat the enemy, but to buy time."

While everyone nodded firmly, I asked Tenrai Nagisa a question.

,"Where is the Amaterasu ritual being held?"

,"You're curious about that?"

,"It would be strange if I wasn't curious. I heard it was originally Mount Fuji."

,"The reason it was Mount Fuji is because of its strong fire energy. The place where the Amaterasu ritual is being held now is Sakurajima Island. The Sakurajima volcano recently erupted. It's not as good as Mount Fuji, but it's a place with strong fire energy. Has your curiosity been satisfied?"

,"Yes. Thank you."

,"Then let's begin."

Tenrai Nagisa used her magic. A portal connected to Kyoto appeared in front of her. She smiled at us, sweating profusely.

,"I believe in you all."

,"We will definitely stop the ritual."

The students entered the portal. Just before I entered the portal last, Seong Hari grabbed my shoulder.

,"Yujin-ah. Be careful. Don't overdo it. If it seems really dangerous, call Mom. Mom will come right away."

,"If Mom comes, a bomb will explode here, right?"

,"......You, Yujin-ie, are the most important thing to me."

It was a somewhat dangerous thing to say. And it was also a reassuring thing to say.

,"I'll take care of it, so don't worry."

I turned my back on Seong Hari and entered the portal.

The scenery changes in an instant. We were in the mountains of Kyoto.

Mieko, who had been closing her eyes and concentrating, opened her eyes and said.

,"I can feel Tsukuyomi's power over there."

,"...Shall we go? It's dangerous, but we'll manage somehow."

Hyodo Yuuki smiled brightly, uttering an optimistic line.

,"Sorry, but go without me. I suddenly have urgent business to attend to."

,"Yujin? Urgent business?"

I used the power of the storm.

Wind blew around me. A gentle wind wrapped around my body and moved upwards. My body floated up.

,"I'm going to the Amaterasu side."

,"What? Wait!"

Hyodo Yuuki was startled and tried to grab my body, but it was too late. I avoided his hand and flew into the sky.

,"No matter how I think about it, Madojeong's real target is the Amaterasu side. You guys will be enough for the Tsukuyomi side."

,"Stop!! Seong Yujin!!"

Ignoring his words, I soared into the sky. I checked the location of Sakurajima Island on my smartphone, and it was at the southernmost tip of Japan.

,'If I fly at full speed, I'll arrive within 15 minutes.'

I entrusted my body to the wind.

• • •

Brrrrrrrrr.

My smartphone rang. The caller was, of course, Seong Hari. I deliberately didn't answer it. Isn't it easier to ask for forgiveness than permission? It's not too late to contact her after killing Sanada Kenshi, the head of Madojeong.

,'I've arrived. That's one hell of a volcano to look at.'

Black smoke was billowing from the crater of the Sakurajima volcano. And beneath it, I could feel a huge energy. An energy too immense to be human.

,'Susanoo. It's similar to when I met that bastard. Is the ritual in its final stages? So fast?'

Not far from the crater, those bastards were conducting the ritual.

They built a small shrine, and in front of it, an old man was kneeling and chanting something in unison. The old man was Sanada Kenshi, the head of Madojeong. Around him, the Majin of Madojeong were standing guard.

,"Makina. Moka."

I summoned two spirits.

[It's the day of the final battle!]

Makina clenched her small fist and swung it in the air.

,"Kkuuuk!"

Thunder Owl Moka spread its wings wide and waited for my command.

I pointed my finger at the ground. The bastards on the ground also noticed me. Even the bastards who had been hiding in the air revealed themselves.

Even from this far away, I could smell the stench. The stench of the Majin.

『Demon Hunter (S) has detected the presence of Majin.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against Majin temporarily increases.』

,"Kill them all."

Moka flew high into the sky and entered the clouds. The white clouds instantly turned into dark clouds and dropped lightning towards the ground.

[Lend me Stormbreaker!]

I summoned Stormbreaker for Makina. Makina, possessed by Stormbreaker, transforms into the form of a dragon, not a human.

[Cyber Dragon, sortie!]

The Cyber Dragon spewed flames towards the ground.

I held Hwaryeon Bido in my hand and wrapped red lightning around my body. The tip of the blade was pointed at Sanada Kenshi, the head of Madojeong.

Lightning Flash of the Thunder Sky Style

I became a streak of lightning and fell to the ground.

Sanada Kenshi didn't even react in his kneeling position. Instead, a man with roughly dyed blue hair blocked my path. He casually swung the stake in his hand and knocked me away. I flew backwards, barely regained my balance, and landed on the ground.

,'...He knocked me away so easily?'

I even took the elixir I bought with money two days ago, so I've become stronger. No ordinary A-rank could knock me away so easily.

,'Is he an S-rank?'

There aren't only S-rank heroes in this world. Even though they are fewer in number than heroes, S-rank villains also exist. I was about to get up again when I belatedly discovered the corpses around me.

Gruesomely dismembered corpses. They hadn't been dead for more than 30 minutes. I guessed that they were the ones Tenrai Nagisa had sent.

Majin began to gather around me.

,"To think he'd come alone."

,"That's the face in the data. The son of Gwancheon. Since he can't move directly, he sent his son? What confidence does he have?"

,"I have a bad feeling about this.... Let's kill him right away."

I didn't even glance at the Majin surrounding me. The Majin here are no match for me. Even though there are some A-ranks mixed in, there's no problem as long as Demon Hunter (S) is here.

The one I had to be most wary of was the roughly dyed blue-haired man, presumed to be an S-rank. He was wearing a black leather outfit and walked towards me, flailing his long limbs. The way he walked was very bizarre.

The bastard greeted me with a cracking voice.

,"Hello. I'm Beta."

,"Beta?"

His status window doesn't appear.

,"That's not his real name."

,"Beta can't be his real name. Are you retarded?"

,"You're the retarded one."

,"Don't you know me? I think I'm quite famous in Japan...."

Kwaaaaang!

Moka dropped lightning towards the bastard. Beta raised the stake in his hand upwards. The lightning bounced off to the side and attacked a Majin.

,"Ah, sorry."

Beta apologized with a sly smile, but the Majin was already dead.

The atmosphere among the Majin sank. Instead of directing their killing intent at Beta, the Majin focused their killing intent on me. It seems they think I'm an easy target.

,'I don't know what that bastard's ability is. Is the stake a special item? Or is it a special ability to handle the stake?'

I couldn't figure it out even if I racked my brain. There's a lack of information. Makina is dealing with the other Majin on one side, and Moka is constantly dropping lightning.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido tightly in my hand. Hwaryeon Bido's power hasn't been fully restored.

,'Ah, whatever. Let's just use Seon-gung and kill him.'

Domain.

Starlight burst from Hwaryeon Bido.
