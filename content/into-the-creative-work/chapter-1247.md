The 82nd Anniversary celebration was held in a newly developed city area a bit away from the capital. They must have been aiming to promote the new city by broadcasting the 82nd Anniversary celebration on TV.

Well, the place where the celebration is being held is on the outskirts of the new city, though.

My unit, 667, had to stay in the dorm except for a brief attendance during the morning rehearsal. Even if those who know already know, Unit 667 was an unofficial unit. The Battle Mage Project that Unit 667 is working on cannot be revealed to the public, of course.

We sat on chairs under the control of the instructor and blankly watched the anniversary celebration broadcast.

Several countries were scheduled to attend this anniversary celebration. Starting from small city-states with populations of less than a million, to the diplomatic corps of the Hapeil Republic, which is currently in a truce, the Heist Empire, Divine France, Neo London, etc., which possess the strongest power in the current world, are attending. The diplomatic corps that attended was splendid.

What I paid attention to was the Heist Empire.

This country, whose name is literally "robber," is notorious for its gangster diplomacy. All other countries are criticizing the Heist Empire. Recently, it has been quiet due to internal problems. The motif of the Heist Empire is modern and contemporary Germany.

,If it's according to the original game's content, the Kaiser is repeating purges and slowly declining now.'

The Heist Empire's diplomatic corps was visible on TV. They are wearing snazzy black military uniforms.

,50% of the reason why original newbie players choose the Heist Empire is because of that military uniform.'

The camera mainly focused on a blonde woman.

,Bridget. I didn't know that woman would attend the anniversary celebration.'

A woman with brilliant blonde hair and blood-red eyes. She had a black coat slung over her shoulders and was looking down at her surroundings with arrogant eyes.

And the camera eventually turns to the sky. An airship with the red cross of the Knights Templar drawn on its surface flew in and landed on the ground.

The doors of the airship opened, and the diplomatic corps of Divine France appeared. Everyone is dressed in all black and wearing a red cross badge.

At the center was a woman with long green hair. Her hairstyle covered her left eye.

One of the Divine Saints of Divine France, the Guardian Saint Marta. She is wearing a black suit. She is wearing a tight-fitting jacket and pants. The upper part of her chest and pelvis are slightly visible. She exudes an atmosphere more like a mafia boss than a saint. A red cross badge is attached to her black jacket.

She was smoking a cigarette in her mouth as if it were natural, even while the camera was filming.

,Not only her appearance, but her personality is also far from that of a saint.'

The screen moves back to the sky as she gets into a luxury car.

"Hahahahahahahahahahaha!"

With a refreshing laugh, London's Greatest Swordsman, Pucksibal, appeared. The mechanical horse he was riding was running through the sky. He was wearing full plate armor that matched the horse, and a greatsword was equipped on his back. His brown bobbed hair flutters violently in the wind. Looking at his face alone, he was a fairly handsome middle-aged man.

He is a member of the Neo Round Table, the highest authority in Neo London, and London's Greatest Swordsman, Pucksibal.

Watching the broadcast, I felt a strange feeling. In my memory, they were just NPCs. NPCs are becoming people and moving. They are breathing in the same sky. I realized anew that I was in the world of the game.

Afterwards, the King of the Freecell Kingdom appeared and began his speech. He is a middle-aged man in his 50s with almost no impact compared to Bridget, Marta, and Pucksibal.

"Now the full-fledged anniversary celebration will begin."

Instructor Venus, who was sitting nearby, said.

The anniversary celebration was splendid.

Thousands of soldiers marched through the city pulling tanks, and dozens of tanks fired simultaneously, showcasing their firepower. And then there was a spectacular performance by a globally famous idol girl group.

They boast about newly developed weapons and award medals to nobles who have made achievements. I can bet my right wrist that those medals are made with money and connections.

"Number 31, Number 211. Get ready. It's almost your turn."

Number 31 and I got up from our seats. First, I checked my outfit and put on my mask. I grabbed my pistol, knife, and elemental sword.

After finishing our preparations, Number 31 and I followed Instructor Venus to the training hall. It was close to the stadium.

Number 31 and I were guided by a soldier and walked to the center of the training hall, looking at each other.

"We are installing broadcasting equipment. Please wait a moment as it won't take long."

Spectators gradually gather. The King and nobles are not visible. They are trying to watch it through TV without seeing it directly.

From my point of view, it was rather fortunate. It's too much pressure to have high-ranking people nearby.

I made eye contact with Number 31. She looked more serious than ever. Number 31 will take this sparring seriously. I had to match her appropriately and lose.

,...But I can be serious until the early to mid-stages, right? No matter what, I have no intention of showing magic above level 4.'

I simulate in my head how Number 31 will come out.

,Number 31's specialty is wind attribute magic and martial arts centered on mobility. Her combat style is very aggressive. Kill before being killed. That's the feeling.'

Officially, Number 31 is the strongest in the unit, excluding the instructors. She had a talent for combat.

"Broadcasting preparation is complete. Magic special forces members, please prepare for battle... Announcer!"

"Yes! Shall we start?"

A beautiful announcer ran from behind. A short, tight skirt that showed her thighs and her bouncing, voluminous breasts. She was an announcer who was enjoying great popularity these days.

She was a little different from the announcer I knew in my common sense, but anyway, she was an announcer who made my eyes happy when I looked at her.

"Start!"

The camera director said. A red light came on in the camera he was carrying on his shoulder.

"Hello, viewers! This place is a little away from the venue...."

After the announcer's brief explanation, the camera films Number 31 and I.

"There will be a combat demonstration by two members of the Magic Special Forces!"

Immediately after the announcer's words, Instructor Venus's voice was heard from the mask.

-Combat start.

Number 31 was the first to make a move. She raised her right arm high. A strong wind, visible to the naked eye, gathers above her right palm. And in an instant, flames rise around her and mix with the wind.

Double casting. It's also magic with good compatibility between wind and fire attributes.

,It's within the expected range.'

The spread of fire through the wind. She is trying to dominate the space from the start.

I couldn't just stand still. I responded in a similar way to Number 31. I open the Astral and calculate the formula.

[Air Ball]

I use the level 3 wind magic that Number 31 used just before on my palm. The wind gathers. Of course, this alone is far from enough.

[Torrent]

Transparent water is mixed into the air ball and compressed.

As Number 31's attack flew in, I also threw the prepared air ball. The two air balls collided in the air and exploded.

Along with a strong wind, flames and water spread in all directions and fight for territory. As a result, pure white steam spread thickly like a dawn mist. It's not a normal phenomenon.

,Is she holding the steam in place with magic?'

The broadcast will be seen well. The cameras in this world are more high-performance than I thought, so steam is not a big problem.

An attack flies towards me. It is a spear made of wind. Thanks to the steam, the shape of the wind is clearly visible.

[Barrier]

I block the attack with a barrier and pull out my pistol, pulling the trigger three times in the direction the attack came from. Three bullets pierced through the steam and stretched out. I don't have the feeling of hitting anything. I didn't expect much, so I'm not disappointed either.

[Detection]

I used detection magic, spreading mana in all directions.

I gasped. I detected a total of five reactions: two on the right, one in front, and two in the rear. She used level 2 magic, Decoy, to confuse me.

,Are you going to keep fighting in this steam?'

It was as soon as I finished speaking.

Kwang!

An air boom occurred next to me with a loud explosion. The barrier protecting my body shatters. I stepped back and used the barrier again. The aftermath of the air boom caused some of the steam to disappear, and Number 31's purple hair was briefly visible before disappearing.

Kwaang!

This time, an air boom occurred from behind. The barrier broke again. I activated the barrier again as if it were natural. Maintaining the barrier was the basics of a battle mage.

,I've been hit enough. Shall I counterattack now?'

As the steam slowly disappeared, the approximate location of Number 31 was visible. There is a high possibility that it is a decoy, but there is no problem. Just attack everything that is suspected.

I spread my left hand wide. Pajijijik. Electricity flickers and bursts out from the center of my palm.

[Electric Whip]

The blue electric whip is swung and strikes Number 31's figure. The figure disappeared without a trace. It's a decoy. I rotated my body and swung the electric whip right next to me.

This time there was a reaction. The whip collided with Number 31's barrier.

,Explode.'

I explode the Electric Whip. This is not magic, but using my ability. Well, from the perspective of the people watching, it will look like magic.

The Electric Whip exploded, and lightning spread in all directions. The steam disappears. I saw Number 31, wearing a mask and holding a knife, running towards me. Her body is layered with barriers.

,Are you thinking of engaging in close combat.'

Not bad. It is difficult to maintain a barrier in close combat. There is no need to engage in unnecessary power struggles.

Other ordinary magicians would curse me for being crazy, but Number 31 and I are Battle Mages. Preparations for close combat have been made, of course.

Kiiiing.

A blade of wind is created from Number 31's knife.

I put the pistol I was holding in my hand and took out my knife.

[Water Blade]

A long blade of water is created on the short knife. The water blade was moving at high speed, increasing its cutting power.

Originally, I don't engage in close combat. I have no talent for close combat because I am clumsy. I am the worst at close combat among my peers.

,Usually, in this case, I would actively use magic to create distance...'

I have to lose this battle.

Therefore, I will engage in close combat and lose appropriately.

,If I use magic a little desperately, it won't look like I lost on purpose.'

Fortunately, I was confident in my acting.

Number 31 approached. The blade of wind and the blade of water collide.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

Number 31 and I simultaneously stopped fighting at the sudden, huge explosion. The place where the explosion was heard was where the VIPs, including the King, were gathered. In other words, it also meant that something absurd had happened now.

"It's a terrorist attack! What crazy bastards committed terrorism!! It's a scoop! What are you doing, announcer! Get ready to move right now!"

The camera director shouted. It was a voice filled with crazy joy.
