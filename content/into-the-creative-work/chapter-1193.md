I found the bastard.

He was sitting on a rock, cloaked. His black hair, reaching his shoulders, was curly, and his eyes were sunken. He saw us and lowered the wooden flute he had to his lips.

,B-Rank Hunter Seong Yujin, and Ha Seunghee of Sejin Group. Am I correct?"

He spoke somewhat awkward Korean in a sharp voice. He might be making it awkward on purpose.

,Why are you confirming something you already knew?"

,I once killed an innocent Hunter who wasn't my target. I believed he was the target, but he wasn't. All Korean faces look similar."

,I am Seong Yujin. And you?"

,You don't need to know."

,This son of a bitch!"

I swung Hwaryeon Bido at him. Sword energy tore through the air. He took a backstep and lightly dodged the sword energy.

Seeing his movements, I clicked my tongue. He's at least a B-Rank.

,You're impatient."

,Is that what a killer should be saying?"

,Calm down. I have no intention of killing you."

,Is that a fart or what? We almost died because of you."

,Did you die, though? The Fog Snake attack was my greeting. Knowing that you, a B-Rank Hunter, were there, I knew a few hundred Fog Snakes wouldn't be able to do anything to you."

,That was a shitty greeting. What country are you from?"

,......."

The bastard shut his mouth. He seemed determined to hide any information about himself.

,If that bastard really didn't intend to kill us and manipulated the Fog Snakes to attack us... his purpose must have been to weaken me, even a little bit."

Of course, I don't believe his words. I remain wary of him.

,...What country is he from? Not Southeast Asia... More like China or Japan... Or maybe he's even Korean. He's just making his Korean sound awkward on purpose."

I stopped thinking. Suspicion leads to more suspicion. Continuing these thoughts won't help. The answer is right in front of me anyway. I just need to catch him and interrogate him.

His gaze turned to Ha Seunghee.

,I only want one thing. The drug that grows plants instantly. If you hand over that drug, I won't touch you. We can leave the dungeon without any problems."

,No problems my ass."

The moment I hand over HB-1 here, Ha Seunghee's plans and dreams will be shattered. I didn't want that. Ha Seunghee risked her body and made a deal with me for her ambition. That was one of the reasons she was so attractive. If I give in to that bastard here, her value and charm will be diminished.

,Flash."

I kicked off the ground with all my might and leaped forward. The bastard's eyes followed me a beat late. He hadn't perfectly grasped my movements.

The bastard gripped the wooden flute tightly with both hands. The red blade of Hwaryeon Bido fell on his right shoulder.

Kwang!

A shockwave erupted. My body flew backward. I spun in the air and landed on the ground.

,...Wind?"

I saw it right before the shockwave. The presence of wind surrounding the bastard's body. The wind was protecting him.

The bastard brought the wooden flute to his lips.

Pajijijik.

Electricity sparked from my body.

Immediately after, a bolt of lightning fell from the sky towards the bastard. He didn't even glance at the lightning. He didn't even try to dodge it. The armor of wind protecting his body blocked even the lightning.

,Is it wind manipulation like Jin Se-yeong-ssi's?"

The bastard blew the wooden flute.

It wasn't making the unpleasant sound it made when he was controlling the Fog Snakes. It was a performance. The wind that passed through the flute became music, and the music dug into my head through my ears.

,Aaaaaaack!"

Ha Seunghee screamed and grabbed her ears. Losing her balance, she staggered and collapsed to the ground, tightly closing her eyes.

,You son of a bitch! What did you do!"

I need to save Ha Seunghee first.

I gathered lightning in my grip.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Extreme Technique (極技) Scattered Thunder (散雷).

I scattered the lightning into the air. The lightning wriggled and spread in all directions. The mana fluctuating in the space was driven out by Sanroe and disappeared. Ha Seunghee, who had been covering her ears, lowered her hands. Her expression also looked more comfortable than before.

,Did you temporarily drive out the mana around here? Impressive."

The bastard, who spoke bluntly, played the flute again.

The sound was different from before. The wind surrounding the bastard's body was emitted as if it was rapidly expanding. The wind was moving in sync with the sound of the performance.

I glanced at Ha Seunghee, who was still collapsed. Protecting her from the invisible wind, which moved freely in straight or curved lines, was a distant prospect.

,Stormbreaker."

Geochang was summoned in front of me. I grabbed Geochang with one hand and threw it at Ha Seunghee. Ha Seunghee was startled. She reached out her hand as if to block Geochang. Just before Geochang pierced her hand, it disintegrated into pieces and clung to her body, becoming armor.

I acknowledge Stormbreaker's defensive power. This should alleviate my worries about Ha Seunghee.

,...Yujin-sunbae-nim. You should have told me it was this kind of thing beforehand. I thought you were trying to kill me."

,There was no time."

The wind blew.

The skin on the hand holding the sword was cut by the wind, and blood droplets splattered out. I hurriedly moved mana to strengthen my body.

,Sunbae-nim. This is armor, but surprisingly, it's not uncomfortable to wear. It doesn't feel like armor. Would you sell it to me?"

,No. I only have one of those."

The bastard's performance became more intense. In response, the wind also became stronger.

,Cheonan."

[Cheonan (Heavenly Eye) is opened.]

I could see the mana mixed in with the wind. It was the cause of the wind. The wind and mana were spreading in sync with the flute sound.

I raised my sword. Uuung. Hwaryeon Bido trembled. Blue sword energy swirled around the red blade.

Even if the wind is formless, I can cut it if I can see it.

I swung the sword down.

The wind in front of me disappeared. A path opened. Without hesitation, I jumped into the path.

I saw the bastard's flustered face. His shaking eyes reflected me. The bastard's fingers frantically played the flute.

A wind barrier was created. What I need to cut is not the wind, but the flow of mana.

,It's here."

I swung my sword. The wind barrier easily disappeared.

Bbieeeeeeeeeeeek!

A sharp sound rang out. The ground beneath my feet surged upward. Thanks to Cheonan, I noticed it in advance. I narrowly avoided the surging ground and ran towards the bastard.

,Flash."

The surroundings slowed down.

I identified the starting point of the wind armor surrounding the bastard's body. The bastard's wind armor was starting from his chest.

,His ability isn't wind, it's sound. He's controlling the wind, the ground, the monsters, and people with sound. There must be conditions."

I realized that the source of the wind armor was the sound of his heartbeat. That constant and subtle sound was maintaining the wind armor.

,This bastard underestimated me too much. I may be a B-Rank, but my skills aren't B-Rank."

I lowered my sword towards the bastard's shoulder.

The wind armor tried to push the blade away. A shockwave erupted. I knew it, but I couldn't be defeated. I forcibly endured and gave strength, and the sword pierced through the wind armor and cleanly cut off the bastard's right shoulder blade.

,Keueuk...."

The bastard swallowed the scream that was trying to escape.

,...Hmm?"

He wasn't swallowing the scream.

He was gathering the scream. The bastard's Adam's apple twitched several times, and his chest swelled. The bastard then opened his mouth wide and screamed.

,Keuaaaaaaaaaaaaaaack!!"

The sound soon became physical force. I flew backward with the surrounding trees and rolled on the ground.

It's okay. This is just a slight bruise. I got up.

A vine popped out from beside my foot and grabbed my ankle.

,...Monster?"

I lifted my foot and tore off the vine.

Kwakwakakang!

A red flower bud popped out of the ground. A giant flower, reaching 3m in height, bloomed. The flower was full of sharp teeth, and 20 vine tentacles swept around like whips from below the flower.

Allax.

It's a plant-type monster.

It's a boss-level monster in this dungeon.

Bbieeek! Bbiriririt, Bbiririk!

The bastard continued to play the flute. In response, Allax approached me. Allax was also being controlled by the bastard.

Tch. I clicked my tongue. While Allax was approaching, the bastard was getting further and further away. He was planning to hold me back with Allax and escape.

,It's difficult to kill a boss monster in one go.... But if I let that bastard get away, the information about HB-1 will be revealed to the world."

Which is more urgent?

There's no need to hesitate. I ignored Allax and ran towards the bastard. Allax's vines aimed for my back.

Pajik.

It was when I was about to burn Allax's vines with lightning. Ha Seunghee appeared behind me and cut off the vines with her ability, invisible claws.

,Catch that man. I'll take care of this monster."

,It's a boss monster. Are you sure you'll be okay?"

,This armor is really good. I feel overflowing with power while wearing it."

Stormbreaker's performance varies depending on the owner's skill.

Ha Seunghee was wearing Stormbreaker, but the owner was still me. In other words, Stormbreaker was being maintained by my mana, and its performance was only slightly lower than when I wore it myself.

And with that much, she should be able to handle Allax easily.

,I'm counting on you."

I entrusted Allax to her and chased after the escaping bastard.

,Acceleration."

Even with acceleration, it wasn't easy to catch the bastard. He was almost flying using the wind. On top of that, he was changing the traces on the ground to mislead me. I might have been deceived if it weren't for Cheonan.

,He won't be able to run far with his arm cut off."

The distance was gradually closing. The bastard's back was visible to the naked eye.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Noeseom (雷閃).

I fired sword energy imbued with lightning. The sword energy cut through the branches blocking the way and flew, hitting the bastard's back. The bastard coughed up blood and collapsed.

,It looks like we can finally have a conversation."

I approached the bastard. I kicked the bastard's side, who was lying face down, and turned him over to face up.

,Cough, cough!"

The bastard coughed up blood. The bastard's face was turning black. I frowned. I had cut off the bastard's right arm and slashed his back with sword energy, but from a Hunter's perspective, these injuries weren't serious fatal wounds. The bastard had skills above a B-Rank Hunter, after all.

,Did he take poison?"

,I can't... give you information."

Even if I had a potion, I couldn't heal the bastard. Potions don't eliminate poison. An antidote? He wouldn't carry an easy poison for suicide that a universal antidote would work on. Besides, this bastard's heart was already destroyed. He destroyed his heart using his ability. Both poison and heart destruction. What a meticulous bastard.

,Why go to such lengths? You can't use the money you've saved when you die. Or is there a reason you have to go this far?"

,You won't get any information."

,I saw your face. Your corpse will tell me a lot. Do you know about DNA testing?"

,My information is nowhere. I exist, yet I do not exist."

The bastard died.
