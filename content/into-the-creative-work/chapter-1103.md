The unit I belong to is officially a nameless unit.

The Battle Mage Project is a project that proceeds in secret. Human rights are practically nonexistent in this world, but civilization has still developed, and minimal human rights beyond the medieval era exist. No matter how much of a slave someone is, using humans for human experimentation is illegal.

,The ones who actually follow the law in this world are few and far between.'

The biggest reason for hiding this project is to avoid being targeted by politicians. Ambitious politicians want to gain achievements and climb to the top, even if it means sacrificing the military.

This unit may not have an official name, but it does have an unofficial one: Unit 667. I don't know what significance 667 has, but it's a unit that exists solely for the Battle Mage Project.

I, a private in Unit 667, a slave, and a test subject, was looking at a map.

A map that Instructor Vinus gave to each squad.

It depicted a portion of the terrain near the unit. The unit was vaguely represented with a circle and some writing, as if they didn't want us to properly understand the facilities.

I dredged up my memories and drew a virtual blueprint of the unit in my head.

There's a shooting range to the south and a medical room to the west. Project-related materials are stored in the west, so the western perimeter is the most heavily guarded. In the center of the unit are the barracks where we stay, the mana training ground, and the cafeteria.

There are training grounds for martial arts, swordsmanship, spear techniques, etc., to the east. The morning roll call and morning run take place to the east.

The north is a place I don't remember, but it's presumed to be the quarters where unit personnel stay.

I memorized the terrain near the unit. Someday, this information will definitely be helpful.

,This map only contains information related to the mission. That means the other squads have similar maps to the one we received, but with different information. I'd like to see those maps, but... I'd draw too much attention to myself from the instructors.'

CCTVs are installed throughout the unit, and soldiers on patrol move back and forth periodically. I mustn't forget that there are eyes watching everywhere. I had to move carefully and discreetly to avoid raising suspicion as much as possible.

"Number 211. When are we going to have the mission briefing?"

Number 212 approached me and asked. She leaned her head slightly to look at the map in my hand.

This time, the instructors aren't intervening. Everything from planning the operation to preparing the equipment had to be done by the squad.

"I'm going to decide in a meeting after lunch. If you have any opinions about the operation, let me know then."

The mission start time is after dinner, when the sun starts to set. There's no training today, and there's quite a bit of time left.

"Okay. By the way, do you know what's for lunch today?"

"Hmm. It feels like seafood will be coming out soon."

Number 212's face scrunched up.

"I just hope they don't serve fried fish. That stuff is really awful."

I agreed with that sentiment. And fried fish was served for lunch.

•••

After lunch, we gathered in the barracks and started the meeting.

I taped the map to the wall of the barracks and said,

"Our mission is to occupy this area and maintain it until dawn tomorrow. Tomorrow at dawn, the instructors will personally go around to assess the mission status and assign scores. I don't know the exact criteria. However, if we receive a failing grade... you know what that means, right?"

Some of the kids trembled. Fear clouded their faces.

Mana essence injection.

It was something no one here liked.

I glanced at Number 31. She was definitely going to receive a mana essence injection as well, but there wasn't any change in her expression.

And Number 31's opinion is the most important here. No matter how good a plan we come up with, the moment Number 31 says she doesn't like it, the plan is scrapped.

Fortunately, Number 31 quietly watched the meeting. She doesn't seem to be planning any trolling.

"Number 211. I heard you were excellent at sniping in the last mission. Will you be sniping this time too?"

"No. As you can see from the terrain, the area we need to occupy is near the mountaintop. There aren't any suitable sniping points nearby. We'll all move together without splitting up."

Mountains aren't suitable for snipers. The dense trees obstruct sniping. Unless you're in a high-altitude location, that is. This mission required us to climb the mountain.

,Besides, today is the day the Dark Moon rises. Moving alone is even more dangerous.'

The meeting about the operation lasted about an hour. Everyone had received the same operational tactics training, so the opinions were quite similar.

"What about equipment?"

Number 212 asked. Everyone voiced their opinions. This is where the opinions diverged. One side argued that it was better to bring as much equipment as possible, including bombs, while the other side argued for bringing only the minimum necessary equipment.

I raised my hand for the latter.

"The key to this mission is the mountain climb. It's better to keep the equipment light. And bombs are powerful, but they're too loud. There's no need to attract the attention of other monsters. We mustn't forget that tonight is the Dark Moon."

And, of course, there was a backlash.

"I'm not saying we should fill our packs with bombs. I'm saying we should take only what we need. In the worst-case scenario, we might have to wipe out the monsters with bombs to survive."

Number 88.

One of the only four men in Squad 1, including me, and a bomb enthusiast. Usually quiet, he spoke with determination in his eyes, showing no signs of backing down.

I heard that he survived the last mission by wiping out the enemies with bombs.

,This is going to be a headache.'

I try to think of words to persuade Number 88. Looking at those stubborn eyes, I'll have to persuade him for at least 30 minutes. It's tough being a squad leader without any authority. It would be different if I had a higher rank, but everyone here is a private.

"I agree with Number 211's opinion. Let's do as Number 211 says."

Number 31, who had been quietly watching the meeting, said.

"O-okay."

Number 88 relaxed his eyes. He glanced at Number 31 and lowered his gaze. My feelings were complicated. I was grateful that she helped me, but I wasn't much different from Number 88. If Number 31 started trolling, I wouldn't be able to stop her.

After the meeting, everyone had their own personal time.

I wrote down a list of necessary equipment on paper and met with Instructor Vinus. Instructor Vinus quickly scanned the equipment list and nodded.

"You've put it together efficiently. However, grenades and other explosives are not allowed. Flashbangs are also not allowed."

I had requested grenades for emergencies, but it was rejected. Instructor Vinus explained that loud attacks weren't good during the Dark Moon. I didn't think that was the real reason, but I nodded vaguely.

"...Understood. I'll be going now."

"Wait. I have something to tell you."

Instructor Vinus took out a cola and handed it to me. It was lukewarm cola.

"......May I use ice magic?"

"It's fine. I hope your skills have improved since last time."

"I've been practicing."

I opened the cola can and used ice magic.

Last time, I froze half of it, but this time, I froze 20% of it. I gulped down the cola. The intense carbonation hit my throat. With this feeling, I could drink cola every day.

,To be in a situation where I can't even drink a cola the way I want... it really pisses me off.'

I put down the cola can. Instructor Vinus had been quietly watching me until then, twisting his lips.

"Your Squad 1 will be under my charge."

"...I see. So the instructors are coming along."

It was a matter of course. What would they trust the slaves to do? It was natural for the slaves to have a supervisor. Even if the slave dies, the supervisor won't step in.

"I'll get straight to the point. If something goes wrong, protect Number 31 at the cost of your life. I don't ask for much. Just hold out for 30 seconds until I arrive. You have the level 4 barrier artifact you received from Sergeant Lexi, so it shouldn't be difficult."

Blatant favoritism towards Number 31. I didn't feel much about it anymore.

"As ordered, I will protect Number 31 at all costs. Even if it costs me my life."

"That's the answer I like to hear. Number 211, I think you're competent."

Instructor Vinus handed me two six-packs of cola. Twelve cans. It was meant for me to share with the squad members.

"This is a reward for the efficient operation and equipment support. Share it with your squad members."

"...Thank you."

Whether this is really a reward is another matter, but I accepted it since he was giving it to me.

,Is he giving the cola to Number 31?'

The prediction was spot on.

As soon as Number 31 took the cola, she used ice magic to chill it and drank it. It seemed Number 31 liked cola.

•••

After dinner, we went outside the unit.

A total of 156 people.

That was the number of privates in Unit 667. When I first entered the unit, the number was originally close to 300, but it had decreased by nearly half. They died from not being able to withstand the mana essence injections, died on missions, and died from insubordination.

,Probably more than 30 people will die in this mission too.'

I had no intention of being among those 30 people and dying.

,In this regard, being in Squad 1 is fortunate. Number 31, the strongest among us, is included. Just her being there increases the survival rate more than other squads.'

We looked up at the sky and waited. The blue sky gradually darkened, and a round, white moon rose.

Originally, today was the cycle for a half-moon to rise. But today is the Dark Moon. Regardless of the moon's cycle, a full moon rises.

The moon took its place in the center of the night sky. The pure white surface of the moon turned black. It was a dark night sky, but strangely enough, the Dark Moon was clearly visible.

"The Dark Moon has risen."

Instructor Vinus said with a smirk.

I swallowed.

The mana in the atmosphere, which had been calm until just now, raged wildly.

The Astral opened, and the mana road was stimulated. I felt a strange desire rising within me.

,So this is the Dark Moon....'

Looking around, it seemed I wasn't the only one affected. Number 31's reaction was particularly intense. A faint smile appeared on her always expressionless face, and her cheeks flushed slightly.

Was it the influence of the Dark Moon? I felt curiosity towards Number 31. I lowered my gaze to the ground and suppressed the curiosity.

It's not good to be deeply involved with her. That thought remains the same even now.

"Begin the mission. I won't wish you good luck."

Instructor Vinus said.

Everyone retreated like a receding tide.

I led Squad 1 and stopped at the entrance of the mountain. It was to put on our equipment.

The most important equipment in this operation was the night vision goggles and the silencer. The key to the operation was to move stealthily, discover the goblin village first, and quietly eliminate them.

I put on the night vision goggles and started the mountain climb. I carefully stepped on the ground covered with fallen leaves and advanced.

I didn't just focus on sight. I strained my nerves to utilize hearing and touch. Feeling the surrounding mana would be the best, but the mana in the atmosphere had become fierce due to the Dark Moon, so I had no choice but to focus on physical senses.

I, who was walking ahead, raised my hand. The squad's movement stopped.
