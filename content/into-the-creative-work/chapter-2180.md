Orshia trembled as if in agony, then revealed a cold glint in her eyes and rose from her seat.

She glared at me as if she would kill me, and removed her metal-rimmed glasses with one hand. Orshia's blue eyes instantly turned silver-gray. Magic Eye.

Immediately after, more than 10 spaces opened in the sky. Inside the spaces were beings with powerful forces. I realized they were Phantasmal Beasts and raised both hands in the air. Unlike her neat impression, she was raising very hideous things in subspace.

,I lost. Let's stop here."

,...Where did all that momentum from before go?"

,The mana rod is overheating. If I use magic even once in this state, the mana rod will be damaged. I don't want to suffer such a loss here. Hasn't my skill and talent already been proven?"

,Haa."

Orshia sighed and put her glasses back on. Her silver-gray eyes returned to blue. The subspaces that had appeared in the sky also disappeared.

,She's not wearing glasses because her eyesight is bad, but to suppress her Magic Eye.'

That meant that a 7th-circle mage couldn't perfectly control her Magic Eye. Just how powerful is that Magic Eye?

Curiosity raised its head, but I barely held it back. It didn't seem like the other person was in a good mood to ask questions.

Orshia habitually tidied her slightly disheveled hair and looked straight at me. The light of anger from before had disappeared. Orshia approached me and said.

,You seem to have talent in all attributes. From my personal point of view, you probably have talent in space magic as well. Would you like to join the Heitel Faction?"

,My specialty is lightning magic, though."

,There's also a way to register with both the Leishen Faction and the Heitel Faction at the same time. However, you won't be able to rise to important positions within the faction, and there will be restrictions on learning arcane magic."

It was such a good offer that I almost shouted YES unconsciously. It's not like I have a great attachment to the Leishen Faction. Can't learn lightning arcane magic? Can't rise to high positions within the faction? I didn't care.

For me, who has knowledge of the original game, there are plenty of ways to obtain arcane magic. High positions within the faction? It's more comfortable without them. It also means fewer troublesome obligations.

,...That's a tempting offer, but will the Leishen Faction allow it?"

,Even if I don't know about others, Ambrac Leishen will allow it. He'll be feeling uneasy because of your appearance."

Because my appearance could shake the position of successor to the Leishen Faction. As she said, Ambrac would allow it. Rather, he would actively want me to accept the offer.

,Is the Heitel Faction wanting me because of my mana affinity?"

,Yes."

,......"

,You seem to be hesitating. If you accept the offer, I will help you three times. Of course, unreasonable requests such as asking me to teach you arcane magic are out of the question, but I can teach you the basics of space magic theory."

It was an amazing reward.

,They're evaluating me higher than I thought. Well, I did get hit by a magic barrage 63 times in a row.'

Plus 16 multiple casting.

If I were them, I would try to recruit a magical talent like me no matter what.

,...It's such a good condition that it would be idiotic to refuse. I accept. But... please keep this a secret for the time being. I have something to talk about with Ambrac Leishen."

If I use this well, I might be able to squeeze something out of Ambrac Leishen as well. I should at least try.

,Indeed. Ambrac Leishen can be shaken with just a little nudge. He tends to be impatient."

Orshia realized my intention and nodded. It was a natural reaction. Mages are a species that doesn't miss even the smallest of gains.

She lowered her head for a moment and fell into thought.

,Huh?'

It seemed like Orshia's nostrils flared just now. The look in her eyes behind her glasses was as cold as ever. There's no way I can ask her if her nostrils flared here....

,Did I see it wrong.'

• • •

,Welcome to the Magic Association."

I shook hands with Vice Chairman Ansing. They had recognized me. I exchanged greetings with mages from other factions as well. Each of them subtly tried to lure me to their faction. I gave them an awkward smile and avoided giving a definite answer.

Ansing and the other Magic Association mages were so busy that they left right after finishing their greetings. I was handed a card engraved with an eight-pointed star, signifying that I was a member of the Magic Association. You can think of it as another ID card.

After that, I met Ambrac Leishen. Unlike when we first met, his eyes were wary as he looked at me.

,I received a recommendation from Orshia Heitel-nim. She asked me if I had any intention of belonging to the Heitel Faction as well."

Ambrac's face brightened. The wariness towards me also decreased.

,The Heitel Faction is no exaggeration to say that it is the best faction in Neo London. It boasts not only tradition but also the forefront in space magic. Of course, the Leishen Faction is also a faction that is not far behind."

He praises the Heitel Faction, raising it up.

,But I am a lightning mage. I am also interested in the Leishen Faction's arcane magic."

,Does that mean you're...?"

,I know what Ambrac-ssi is worried about. You're worried that my presence will shake your position as successor, aren't you? Ambrac-ssi. Would you be willing to teach me lightning-based arcane magic separately? In return, I will never covet the position of successor to the Leishen Faction. As proof, I will belong to both the Heitel Faction and the Leishen Faction."

,......"

Ambrac was silent and pondered.

It's difficult to simply dismiss a talent like me. Other mages have already confirmed my talent. Especially since I'm a lightning mage. From the Leishen Faction's point of view, not Ambrac's personal situation, I'm a talent they shouldn't miss.

But if he accepts me, his position will be threatened.

I spoke to him as if driving a wedge into his worries:

,I'm not asking you to teach me all the arcane magic. Just teach me the arcane magic that I'm allowed to know."

,...Can you swear on your mana that you will not covet the position of successor to the Leishen Faction?"

I wouldn't take it even if you gave it to me.

Swallowing my true feelings, I answered with mana.

,I swear. I don't mind drawing up a separate magic contract."

Ambrac's expression relaxed.

,A magic contract is not necessary. A mage of Eugene-ssi's caliber would never go back on his word. I want to maintain a good relationship with Eugene-ssi. I'll also teach you some decent arcane magic."

,Ah, I'd also like to tour the Leishen Power Plant. As a lightning mage, I'm very curious."

,Haha. Let's move to tour it right now. I'll guide you myself. It's the best power plant in Neo London. No, it's the best in this world, so you'll be amazed when you see it."

As he boasted, I was quite surprised after touring the power plant. I realized once again that the Leishen Power Plant was not a power plant that could be replicated even if you poured money into it.

That afternoon, I stamped the membership contracts for the Leishen Faction and the Heitel Faction. The rank was general faction member. I had to pay tuition every month, but I could receive the faction's protection and look at general-level research materials within the faction. Of course, I could also learn lightning magic.

The tuition was quite expensive, but it wasn't burdensome for a mage. Mages are highly skilled workers, so they earn good money. If you can't afford to pay tuition, working within the faction is also an option.

Ambrac smiled broadly when he saw me send the contract to the Heitel Faction.

,Welcome to the Leishen Faction! Eugene-ssi, you will make a mark in history as a lightning mage! I, Ambrac Leishen, guarantee your talent and skill!"

,Uh, well. Thank you."

His tension shot up as his wariness towards me disappeared.

,Eugene-ssi seems to be very interested in arcane magic. What kind of arcane magic do you want?"

,Since I'm also active as a mercenary, I'd like magic that's helpful in combat."

I didn't even hope for industrial arcane magic. That's the most important of the arcane magic. If I used it carelessly, I could end up fighting with a corporation.

,Offensive magic, huh. Ah, this would be good. It's an arcane magic called Plasma Battery."

He took out a small metal bead from his pocket.

,This metal bead itself is just an ordinary lump of metal, but if you activate the magic, it becomes like this...."

When he placed the metal bead on his palm, it emitted light and increased in size. Eventually, it became the size of a watermelon, emitting a blue light and brightly illuminating the surroundings.

,To be exact, it's not an attack magic."

,Yes. But you can store the electricity that lightning mages need most in this battery. And it can also be used for attack. You can throw it at your opponent to discharge it. The amount of electricity that can be stored depends on the mage's ability, but Eugene-ssi should be able to store a considerable amount of electricity."

,It's a magic that can be used in various ways. Is it an arcane magic that uses a sealing formula?"

,That's right. It's a byproduct that occurred when the Leishen Faction was heavily focused on battery research. The new battery... failed, though. This becomes very unstable if it's separated from the user by a certain distance."

,What if it's close to the user?"

,It's absolutely safe. It will never explode without the user's will."

,I'll learn it."

[Plasma Battery] was a 5th-circle magic. He taught me the formula and told me about the image and precautions to take when using the magic.

,I'll teach you other arcane magic next time. It'll be difficult enough for you to learn Plasma Battery right now. It took me a month for the magic to succeed."

,Thank you. I'll see you again next time."

I went home and perfectly used [Plasma Battery].

It took exactly 9 hours.
