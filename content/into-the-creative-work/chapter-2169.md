Pajijijik.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

They're filming this right now. It's not live, but it's going to be released to the public, so I'm refraining from showing anything too brutal. Whether I kill them or let them live after turning off the camera, I have to at least pretend to try to subdue them for now.

It's a sight that would terrify ordinary people. Because for ordinary people, guns mean more than just weapons. A gun that doesn't work? That's another word for monster.

But the man in front of me hesitated for a moment, but wasn't terrified.

,The bullets don't work. We need to re-evaluate the target's abilities. Use the bombs.'

Three people who had been hiding jumped out and threw bombs at me. At the same time, they scattered and ran away. Some through the living room window, some through the terrace connected to the room. Their coordinated movements showed that they were experts among experts.

Click.

The sound of the switch being pressed was especially loud.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Jilpungsinroe (疾風迅雷).

I enhanced my body with lightning and ran towards those bastards. In an instant, I leaped out the window like those bastards who had escaped at a speed that transcended human limits.

Kwaaaaaang!

I staggered without realizing it. Jilpungsinroe is too much of a strain on my current body.

,Recover.'

I recover my body, which was injured by the excessive movement, and look around. Four terrorists are scattering and running away. Even for me, it's impossible to catch all four of them.

,There must be a leader among them... They're covering their faces, so there's no way to tell. I'll have to catch the bastard who sprayed me with the assault rifle earlier.'

I ran at full speed towards the fleeing bastard and tried to smash the back of his head. The bastard twisted his body to the side and avoided my attack.

,He's going to fight me in close combat? His arrogance is sky-high.'

My combat videos are already all over the internet. If he had watched even one of those videos, he would know that he has absolutely no chance in a one-on-one close combat fight, right?

,He's underestimating me. Or rather, he must have a hidden card.'

Gulp.

I heard something being swallowed from behind the cloth covering his face. At the same time, my question was resolved.

,It was a drug.'

Kung!

,But he's just an ordinary human.'

A superhuman body obtained in an instant. Even if he's used to combat, can he perfectly control it? Of course not. Besides, I'm an expert at dealing with things that are more than human.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Byeokgye (碧溪).
