「Area 6,999, we have captured the church in the torrential rain.」

「This is a special hidden area. You will acquire a special item instead of area dominance.」

「You have been granted the title of 'Calm Hunter'.」

「You have acquired 1 bonus stat point.」

「You have acquired 300,000 AP.」

「You have acquired the Rainstorm Flute.」

「The Messenger of Prophecy (False) delivers a prophecy to you.」

「Ill-fated relationships will hinder you.」

The capture is over.

Lee Minjeong and I were transported to the forest outside the church. I inadvertently looked up at the sky. It was bright blue. It felt like a lie that just moments ago, dark clouds had darkened the sky, and thunder and lightning had raged. Looking around, the trees weren't wet from the rain, and the ground was dry.

In my hand, I held a simple black flute. The "Rainstorm Flute."

"Yujin-ssi!"

Seohee Yu called out to me as she approached. The rest of the group was with her. No one had been left behind.

'There must be other survivors inside the church besides us.... Are they somewhere else?'

They weren't dangerous guys, so I didn't think I needed to worry about them.

"Are you hurt anywhere?"

"No. I'm fine. That nasty feeling disappeared as soon as I got out of that area."

"Really? Then let's move. We'll reach the island soon if we go a little further. Even if it's tough, it's better to rest on the island."

「Name: Seong Yujin

Class: Lightning Disaster

Title: Fate Destroyer

Divine Seat: Lord of the Sky

Affiliation: AL 401 Earth

Strength: 88 Agility: 76 Stamina: 82 Mana: 96 Luck: 38

Unique Trait: Deception (SS)

Trait: Lightning (S)

Skills: Astrape (B), Manroe (B), Lightning Speed (D)

(Applying Status Window)」

I immediately used the bonus stat point. There's a saying that if you save it, it becomes poop, and I already knew where to use the bonus stat. Luck. When I get bonus stat points, I raise this stat, which is hard to raise by normal means.

Next, I checked the item I got as a reward for capturing this special hidden area.

「Rainstorm Flute

One-time use.

Can summon a rainstorm.

The rainstorm lasts for three hours.

Rank: S」

It was an item that changed the climate.

It was hard for me to evaluate it. These kinds of items are only as good as how you use them. In an area where it doesn't rain, it would be worth more than an S rank, but in an area where it does rain, it would be worth less than a C rank.

Anyway, I kept the Rainstorm Flute safe. You never know when or where you might need to use it.

'It's a shame that special hidden areas can't be dominated.'

As I led the group towards the island, I thought about the one remaining reward.

The prophecy I received.

「Ill-fated relationships will hinder you.」

It was an absurdly broad statement.

Ill-fated relationships? There are plenty of people in this world with whom I have ill-fated relationships. And it's only natural that ill-fated relationships would hinder me. Anyone who has an ill-fated relationship with me would hate me.

'Does it mean that an ill-fated relationship will appear at an important moment and hinder me? Or does it mean that I shouldn't form any ill-fated relationships from now on?'

I wouldn't know unless they told me more specifically. It was just annoying to be told something so vague. Even if I wanted to ignore it, it's not easy to control your mind.

「Area 2,255. Welcome to the Eustia Island!」

We arrived at the island.

Even though the ten-year system hasn't been officially declared yet, the island was bustling with a festive atmosphere. The number of tourists was enormous. People everywhere you looked.

The group constantly exclaimed in admiration as they looked at the island. Like country bumpkins who had come to the big city. That's exactly what they looked like.

"Don't get swept away by the crowd and follow me closely."

I said that and led the way. The first thing we needed to do was find accommodations to stay in during the festival. Kang Myungjin said it would be fine to find a decent place, but I wouldn't allow a cheap place.

'It's not like I don't have money, so there's no need to stay in a cheap place.'

And in the current situation, it's hard to find a cheap place anyway. The people who have already come to the island have taken up most of the accommodations. It's hard to find a carriage in the nearby city, so why would accommodations be any different?

'In the worst case, we might not be able to find accommodations and have to sleep outside the island.'

I definitely wanted to avoid sleeping outside.

The place I found was a hotel on the inner part of the island. As with any city, the closer you get to the downtown area, the more expensive it becomes. A tall, five-story building, with walls over 5M high and sturdy guards protecting the entrance.

It was a hotel that deserved to be called luxurious.

"I'm sorry, sir. All of our hotel rooms are currently booked, so we don't have any rooms available."

The hotel manager bowed politely. Judging by his attitude and grace, he must have worked in a noble family.

"I'll pay three times the price. Is that not possible?"

"I'm sorry. Our hotel values trust with our guests above all else."

I stepped back. It was the reaction I expected. There would be too much to lose to cancel existing reservations just for money. More than anything, people who use hotels of this level are likely to be important figures. At the very least, they're probably from a mid-sized Legion.

'If I get into a fight with a mid-sized Legion, it's us who will be at a disadvantage.'

The A-Plan Legion wasn't a mid-sized Legion yet.

'Hoo. When you're weak, there's nothing you can do as you please. It was good when I was the Emperor....'

The game world where my words were absolute law. I missed that absolute power.

"Do you know of any other luxury hotels? I don't care how much it costs."

"There are a few hotels on the island that are better than ours.... But they're also fully booked, so you won't be able to find a room there either. If you had come a little earlier, maybe. At this time, it will be difficult to find a vacant room in any hotel."

The manager was right. But I couldn't give up. I and the group went around to all the luxury hotels. But there were no vacancies.

The day began to wane. The island was bathed in the light of the sunset, but none of us could simply enjoy the beautiful scenery.

"Sub Master. Wouldn't it be okay to find a reasonably good place to stay?"

Jiyoungbin asked.

"The situation is the same with reasonably good places. And look over there."

I pointed to one side with my finger. People could be seen trudging along the main street and out of the city gates. They were giving up on finding accommodations and were going to pitch tents and sleep outside the city. The problem was that there were thousands of them.

"They couldn't find accommodations, so how could we easily find one?"

"......Aren't we going to have to go outside and sleep there too? It looks like we'll have to find a place to sleep outside too...."

"Youngbin-ssi is right. Let's go out and find a place quickly. If we're late, we won't be able to get a good spot."

Lilsne tapped her feet a few times. She seemed anxious seeing so many people leaving the island.

"...Haa. I hate sleeping outside...."

Yu Seohee muttered. Like her, no one wanted to sleep outside, even if they didn't say it out loud. Especially sleeping outside with strangers. Just thinking about it made me feel bad, and there would be many problems.

"We're not sleeping outside. Coming all the way here to sleep outside... are you crazy? Follow me. I'll show you the Sub Master's abilities."

"Yujin-ssi. Are you thinking of buying a house? If it's a short-term rental... I think it's possible."

"That's not much different from the hotels. Since it's this time of year, aside from getting ripped off, Legions of mid-sized scale or so have plenty of money."

I led them and moved.

Eustia Island is a planned city. A city that was planned systematically from the city's design stage. Commercial areas, residential areas, military areas, etc. are divided. Among the residential areas, there is an area exclusively for nobles.

'It takes more than ordinary money and status to buy a mansion in this area, but.'

I strode towards the noble district.

I was stopped at the entrance to the noble district. Surprisingly, knights, not soldiers, were acting as gatekeepers.

"Please stop for a moment. This is not an ordinary area. Are you perhaps a noble?"

"I'm not a noble."

The knight's eyes narrowed. It was because my tone was arrogant even though I wasn't a noble. It was a tone of looking down from above.

"...Are you from a famous Legion? Even if you're from a famous Legion, you can't enter this place unless you're a noble. Unless you have an appointment with a noble. If not, please step back."

The knight put his hand on the hilt of his sword. It meant that he would use force if I didn't step back. The group tensed up, except for me. The pressure felt from the knight was not ordinary. A knight working on the island. He was different from the riffraff I had met so far.

"I am this kind of person."

I handed the knight a piece of paper. The knight looked at the paper with narrowed eyes and was startled.

"An Inspector Appointment?! You were the Inspector of Valdert?! I didn't recognize you, Inspector-nim. I'm sorry!"

The knight hurriedly bowed his head.

It would be one thing if I were an ordinary inspector. I was an inspector recognized by the Valdert Dukedom. An inspector personally appointed by the Illusion Duke, one of the five pillars of the Empire!

'I'm glad I had the appointment letter.'

I have unofficially relinquished my position as inspector, but this appointment letter is real. I'll explain the situation to Elena later and she'll forgive me. I've worked so hard for her, she can at least do this much for me.

"Can I go inside now? And I'd like you to show me where the Valdert mansion is."

"That... I'm sorry, but we need to verify your identity."

"What? You're saying that even after seeing this Inspector Appointment?"

"It's an order that came directly from above.... And just sending you in with only an appointment letter... there are so many crazy people in the world, there's a possibility of impersonation. I'm really sorry!"

"......Tsk. I can't help it. How will you verify my identity?"

"We will send someone to the Valdert mansion to contact them. If the Valdert family's butler vouches for you, the identity verification will be complete."

It was efficient. But it was difficult for me. Elena isn't on the island right now. She is scheduled to appear during the festival.

My plan to freeload at the Valdert villa might be ruined.

"Wait a moment."

"Yes?"

"I can vouch for that matter."

I took out the conch shell I had received from Elena in the past. Of course, it wasn't an ordinary conch shell.

「Voice Buried in the Wind

A pair of conch shells.

No matter how far away you are from the target, you can have real-time conversations as long as you are under the same sky.

Rank: A」

"Ah, is that a communication tool? You have a convenient item."

I nodded at the knight's question and sent a signal to the conch shell. After waiting for about 30 seconds, I was connected to the other party.

"What is it? I'm busy. If it's a trivial story, tell me later."

I heard Elena's curt voice.

"Your Excellency, the esteemed Valdert Duke! This is Seong Yujin, your Excellency's loyal inspector!"

I shouted loudly.

"......What is it?"

I explained the situation. Elena's sigh was transmitted through the conch shell.

"...You knight there. Listen, this man is an inspector I appointed, and a servant of the Valdert family. There was a slight misunderstanding and I couldn't inform the villa's butler. I will vouch for his identity, so let him in."

The knight was flustered. His eyes shook like a ship facing rough waves. Cold sweat dripped down his forehead.

"But, but Your Excellency...."

The knight couldn't continue. He couldn't dare to say that he couldn't confirm whether you were really Elena Valdert with just your voice.

"You're doing a good job. I don't dislike diligent knights like you. Wait there for a moment. I'll send a message to the villa's butler."

"Yes, yes. Thank you. Valdert Duke-nim!"

The knight bowed deeply towards the conch shell. The connection to the conch shell had already been cut. It was a funny sight.

And a little later, an old butler appeared from inside the noble district. He bowed politely to us.

"I am the butler, Segio. I heard about you from the Lady and came to escort you."

"Please guide us."

I and the group followed the butler into the noble district. As I did, I patted the knight on the shoulder.
