"I knew it, but... you're strong after all."

"I just got lucky this time. I should say it's thanks to the Spirit Eye."

"Even without the Spirit Eye, I know there's a difference in skill."

Shinbo Reika said firmly.

The wind blows. It was her contracted spirit, Eiris, approaching. It had blocked Moka's lightning with its spirit body, and it seemed perfectly fine. The spirit was also affected by the Aurora Simulator.

Moka flew to my side.

"I realized that the difference in the power of spirits is on a different level. The difference between intermediate and advanced was bigger than I thought."

"If Eiris had blocked the lightning in a different way, the battle would have been different."

"That was the best I could do at the time. Eiris's power wasn't enough to completely block that lightning. Hey, how did you grow your spirit? I know the Thunder Owl is a low-level lightning spirit by birth."

Shinbo Reika's eyes sparkled.

As a member of the Yggdrasil Knights, to which the elves belong, wouldn't she know the method better than I do? I put aside such questions for now and answered.

"Ah, that's..."

Tonight, I have to leave for Caso Academy in Fukuoka with Seong Hari.

"So, how about wearing a school uniform today?"

"No. I only have one school uniform. And you should sleep, right? We have to go to school in 3-4 hours."

"Mom, you're going to school as a student for the first time in a long time, right? How do you feel?"

The accommodation wasn't bad. However, I couldn't fall asleep because I slept in the car. Seong Hari seemed to be the same, her eyes were sparkling.

3-4 hours.

Isn't it an ambiguous time to force yourself to sleep now?

I headed to the entrance of Caso Academy at the appointed time.

Seong Hari walked next to me, feeling awkward. It was partly because she was uncomfortable with the school uniform she was wearing, but also because the school uniform Tenrai Nagisa gave her was too short. At least the new school uniform Tenrai Nagisa gave her covered her belly button.

'If you just look at the outfit, she's a female delinquent.'

Caso Academy was in the mountains. Perhaps because it was still an unofficial academy, the building was deliberately built in a place where people's eyes couldn't reach it.

"Mom. Walk confidently. If you look around, it just looks weirder. And there's no one around."

"C-Confidently? Isn't this confident enough?"

"Why don't you straighten your back first?"

Seong Hari took a few deep breaths, then straightened her back and started walking confidently. Her face was stiff, but that suited her even better. It was like she was a delinquent who had something unpleasant happen to her.

"H-How is it?"

"It's okay. Even though the skirt is short, you can't see your butt or panties, so walk confidently like that."

"Hoo. I'm trusting you, Yujin-ah."

It felt more like looking at the private property of a rich family than the entrance to an academy. Of course, the name of the academy wasn't written at the entrance either.

"Welcome."

He was an old man in a suit. He was standing with a cane, but his back was straight like bamboo. The cane just looked like a decoration. His hair and beard were snow-white, and he wore round glasses.

"I am Hasegawa Isshin, the principal of Caso Academy. Welcome, Seong Hari-nim, Seong Yujin-gun. I have heard the details from my senior."

The senior he was talking about must be Tenrai Nagisa. It felt awkward for an old man with snow-white hair and beard to call Tenrai Nagisa a senior. The Tenrai Nagisa in my head had the appearance of a child.

"I didn't expect the principal to come out to greet us himself. Is that okay?"

Seong Hari asked. Her face was slightly red, perhaps because of her embarrassing outfit.

"I am the only one who knows your identities at Caso Academy. I came out to greet you to explain Caso Academy in detail. As a bonus, I had nothing to do."

"This is the disguise identity I prepared. You are my relatives and entered the academy as parachuters. That's the setting."

"Won't that damage the principal's reputation?"
