The carriage rumbles on.

Inside the carriage, I stared out the window at the passing scenery. A landscape full of trees swayed. More accurately, the carriage was swaying. In this world, properly paved roads were only found inside cities; there were no cases of roads being paved outside the city. The carriage shook, but the vibrations I felt were comfortable. It was the power of magic.

This carriage was heading to the main family estate.

Of course, I wasn't the only one heading to the main estate.

Yuria was in the carriage with me. Yuria, sitting next to me, had her eyes closed and her head resting on my shoulder. She was exhausted, perhaps because we had just intertwined our bodies.

,Even though Yuria became a Grand Master, I still win at the bedroom game.'

Physically, I was fine. However, I was mentally exhausted by the continuous pleasure. No, it might be more accurate to say I was relaxed. I touched Yuria's waist as I continued to look outside.

This carriage wasn't alone. Female knights were escorting the carriage on horseback. It was Floi, an Aura Master and the commander of the knights. Neither monsters nor bandits were a match for her.

In addition, Kyle was in the carriage behind us. Excluding Yuria, there were three Aura Masters. It was a force capable of collapsing and occupying a decent-sized city in a single day.

The carriage, which had been running smoothly, stopped around evening. The knights and maids who had come along skillfully prepared camp.

,Master-nim. It's the enemy. They seem to be assassins. Thirty-five of them. They are approaching slowly from about 2km away. Judging by how careful they are, they are all top-notch assassins. If you order, I will take care of them.'

To notice assassins approaching from 2km away. Is this what it means to be a Grand Master? I admired her as I looked around. Neither I, nor Floi, nor Kyle had noticed the presence of the assassins.

Who sent the assassins?

,Obviously, it's Jent. Even in the original story, he didn't hesitate to use any means necessary when cornered.'

He must have read the letter I sent to Ention. Whether he intercepted it along the way or secretly looked at the contents. Otherwise, he wouldn't have acted so hastily.

,Jent probably thought it would be over if I arrived at the main estate.'

The distance to the main estate was now short. We could arrive at the main estate in about two hours, but we decided to arrive tomorrow morning. Arriving tomorrow morning was Yuria's idea. The plan was to start with a bang from the morning and end the succession issue in one go.

,It's impossible to hire 35 top-notch assassins overnight. He must have been preparing in advance. Maybe it was a move he was preparing against Kyle, not me.'

Aura Masters are called beings who have transcended humanity, but they are still human after all. Looking through history books, there were frequent cases of them being assassinated and killed. Especially when they had just become Aura Masters.

,Is Jent planning to kill me and Kyle at the same time? Two Aura Masters?'

Yuria wasn't known yet, and Floi hadn't officially returned. If Jent had known that Floi was going to be my escort, he wouldn't have sent assassins, no matter how desperate he was. Floi was already famous for being experienced.

,He must be very desperate, Jent. I'm disappointed that your last-ditch effort is only this much.'

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and held it in my hand.

,Are you planning to take action yourself?'

,I haven't had a proper real battle since becoming an Aura Master. I should feel the power of an Aura Master this time.'

,Understood.'

I asked Yuria and headed in the direction of the assassins. I told the knights, including Floi, to rest comfortably.

,Thirty-five top-notch assassins. Since I know about them in advance, the assassins' greatest advantage, surprise, won't work.'

Most of them would probably be Aura Expert Intermediate or higher.

,I can do this? I'll show them why an Aura Master is an Aura Master.'

From some point, Yuria's figure disappeared. She had concealed her appearance using shadow magic. It was certain that she was nearby. I concentrated and spread my senses. I couldn't feel Yuria's presence at all. When I used the Heavenly Eye, I could see Yuria's Outline faintly. She was about 5M away from me.

I crossed my arms and waited for the assassins to arrive. About 15 minutes later, the assassins appeared.

I could see 12 of them. The other 23 were hiding from me. They were all wearing masks and holding weapons, as befitting assassins. As expected of assassins. They moved without a sound, like ghosts.

,Were you waiting for us?'

One of the assassins said. His voice was low and thick. Judging by his age, he was at least in his 40s. He was probably the leader of the assassins.

,I won't beat around the bush. Did Jent send you?'

,We do not speak about our clients.'

It was definitely Jent.

Jent was the only one who would do something stupid when cornered. There was also a political rival named Princess Aileen, but she became more composed when cornered.

,Who are you guys?'

,Are you asking an assassin for their identity?'

,I don't want a proper answer. I'm asking what organization you belong to.'

In this world, there were several assassin groups, also known as assassin guilds. They would not reveal their identities, but they would always reveal the name of the assassin group. Assassination was, after all, a way to make a living. Fame was essential to gain higher value. Even if it was infamy.

,Haha. You're so calm. You have the spirit befitting someone who reached the realm of Aura Master at a young age. Good. I'll tell you. We are the Dark Emperor (暗帝)'

,...The Dark Emperor?'

,Haha. You're surprised, as expected. Of course. Even little kids know our name.'

Dark Emperor (暗帝)

They were legendary assassins who annihilated the Helbrit Duchy, causing a stir in the world. The Helbrit Duchy's tragedy was so legendary that its infamy was high. Even now, the Dark Emperor occasionally operated, and it was said that anyone targeted by the Dark Emperor could never survive.

The identity of that legendary assassin, the Dark Emperor, was Yuria.

,You're saying you guys are the real Dark Emperor?'

,That's right.'

,Wasn't the Dark Emperor a single person?'

I heard a sound of air escaping from him. He was mocking me.

,Many people misunderstand. But think about it logically. Is it possible for an assassin to annihilate the Helbrit Duchy alone? Of course, it's impossible alone. But if it's not alone, the story is different.'

,So you're saying you annihilated the Helbrit Duchy?'

,That's right. Our assassin group is the Dark Emperor. Consider it an honor to die by our hands.'

He was confidently lying.

The idea that the Dark Emperor was not actually a single person but a word referring to an assassin group was quite plausible. If I didn't know the identity of the Dark Emperor, I might have believed it.

,That's scary.'

I raised Hwaryeon Bido. Mana moved from my core. Mana gathered on the blade. The blue sword energy that had been burning fiercely condensed into Sword energy.

Sword energy.

Aura Blade.

Even though I had already used it in the [God's Atlantis] world and the [Ascension to Enlightenment] world, it felt new when I used it in the [Baekhwan] world. In addition, the actual sensation was slightly different in each world.

,...Do you think you can survive against us? Give up. Then I'll give you some time to write your will.'

,Why is the Dark Emperor so long-winded? Are you scared because you think you're going to lose to me?'

,Ha.... You're arrogant because you became an Aura Master at a young age. I'll end that life of yours. ...Before that, let me ask you one last thing. How did you know we were coming? Only we and the client know about this. Did the client leak information?'

,I predicted you would come. Tomorrow is a very important day. If your client is the guy I think he is... he would target me before I arrive at the main estate.'

,...Prediction, huh. Impressive.'

He admired quietly.

And immediately after, five assassins jumped out of the darkness and rushed towards me.

,Acceleration, Instant.'

I moved mana in a slowing world. Even if I didn't consciously do it, the Spirit Heaven Art reacted to my will.

Pajijijik.

Lightning gathered in my lower body. I used that lightning to jump upwards.

My vision changed. The surroundings opened up. I could see the bewildered assassins below and the tops of the trees. I had soared about 20M into the sky. I was slightly taken aback. I had jumped about four times higher than I had expected.

,It wasn't this much when I trained after becoming an Aura Master....'

Maybe it was because it was a real battle, but my condition was good today.

I inhaled while falling. The cold night air entered my body deeply. As if stimulated by it, the lightning inside my body wriggled.

Pajijijijijik.

Blue electricity wriggled throughout my body. I gripped Hwaryeon Bido tightly. The lightning headed towards Hwaryeon Bido and became red lightning. I held the sword in reverse grip. The tip of the sword pointed towards the ground.

Spirit Heaven Style (Ying Tian Style) Lightning Strike (Lightning).

I fell to the ground, wrapped in red lightning all over my body.

Kwaaaang!

As I fell to the ground, red electricity swept through the assassins. The assassins who were hit by the electricity fell to the ground without even resisting.

,That lightning is dangerous! Get away! Get away and fight from a distance!'

The assassins quickly created distance. Twenty assassins had revealed themselves. About 10 were still hiding in the darkness.

,How many did I miss?'

It was difficult to grasp all the opponents simultaneously in this urgent battle situation.

,I just need to reduce them one by one.'

Kwareureureureung!

Wrapped in red lightning, I started running towards the nearest assassin. My body felt unusually light. It was as if I had become real lightning and the mass of my body had disappeared.

I swung my sword towards the neck of the assassin in front. The assassin's head flew off. The assassin's eyes widened belatedly as he realized he was dead. At the same time, hidden weapons flew from all directions. Daggers, blades smaller than fingernails, poisoned throwing stars.

I reacted instinctively.

I emitted lightning in all directions. The hidden weapons that touched the lightning fell vertically to the ground and were buried. I had used magnetism.

I ran across the ground, revealing my killing intent towards the enemies. I swung my sword, which was lighter than a feather. Every time I swung my sword, an assassin died. Red lightning roared and followed behind the blade.

,Slow...! Too slow!'

I hadn't even used Instant, but the world seemed slow. Well, it was a little faster than when I used Instant. When I used Instant, it seemed much slower.

Still, it was much better than Instant. Instant returned to normal as soon as I moved even a little. But now I was moving in a slowed world. Even if I moved, the world still moved slowly.

Kwareureureung!

It was when I had cut down seven assassins. I didn't know how many seconds had passed in real time, but it hadn't even been 10 seconds in my perception when something strange happened to my body. My body was hot. My legs and arms, my internal organs, were screaming. My head was throbbing too.

But I didn't want to miss this moment, even if I died. I ignored my body's screams and moved.

Kwareureureureung!

I broke through the poisonous smoke and chased after the fleeing assassin. The slash scratched the ground and tore through the trees. The scorched marks were traces of lightning.

I had cut down 26 assassins.

My limit had come.

It was a real limit. The slowed world regained its original speed. But somehow, the world was dyed gray.

,...My body is a mess. It's hard to distinguish colors. My internal organs seem to be already dead.... Is my heart even beating?'

It didn't seem to be beating.

I looked at the assassin in front of me. The guy who seemed to be the leader of the assassins was looking at me like I was a monster.

,This isn't the first time we've faced an Aura Master. What is this... what is this...!'

It was Spirit Heaven Style and lightning.

I had only completely abandoned the Dark Shadow in Spirit Heaven Style. That alone produced this much power.

,...Honestly, I don't know the details either. Wiyu. You were right.'

My body lost strength. Yuria appeared next to me.

,You've done well. I'll take care of the rest. Rest, Master-nim.'
