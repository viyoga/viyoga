,That's logical. But this isn't a situation where we can afford to act logically. Giving in to Madojeong's threats and doing nothing will only lead to the worst possible outcome."

No matter what, first-year academy students can't compare to A-class heroes.

They're lacking in every aspect, including combat experience and stats. Making these students face Madojeong? It's no different from suicide.

,Surely you don't think I'd recklessly throw the students in without considering that? There are ways to temporarily boost the students' potential. The potential of the students gathered here is above A-class. You can't deny that either."

,...You know that potential isn't everything for an A-class hero. Even if you boost their potential with your abilities and items, it won't be enough. Madojeong isn't a group that a few A-class heroes can take down."

,First, we need to correct a misconception. You're overestimating Madojeong too much. It's often said that Madojeong is in collusion with the Japanese government and controls the Japanese underworld. That's an undeniable fact. But that doesn't mean they're the strongest armed group in Japan. If they were, they wouldn't be operating in secret. Until now, the Japanese government and other entities have put pressure on us and interfered with matters related to Madojeong. But not anymore. Madojeong has crossed the line. They've crossed it way too far."

,Even so, sending the kids is insane."

,I have no intention of assigning the students the task of dealing with Madojeong. They just need to disrupt the ritual. They just need to buy time so that other heroes can move and all the bombs installed in the academy can be disarmed."

,I don't know. I don't really see the need to do that. Can't we just clean up after the ritual is over?"

,How arrogant. But that's just like you. The power of a god is beyond your and my imagination. Were you perhaps thinking of Susanoo's power? Susanoo's power isn't power received through a ritual. Why do you think those Madojeong bastards crossed the line? It's because they're confident that they can clean everything up after successfully completing the ritual. Therefore, we must stop their ritual at all costs."

,I think we should move as the headmaster says. We may not be able to defeat Madojeong with our strength alone. But we can at least disrupt them."

Mieko then said in a determined voice.

,I don't know about anything else, but we have to stop the ritual."

Shinbo Reika also didn't stay still.

,We're still students, but we're not children. If the headmaster can temporarily awaken our potential, I think it's definitely possible."

I didn't stay still either.

,Mom. Don't underestimate us too much."

Seong Hari's eyes wavered as if she was flustered.

Honestly, Seong Hari is 100 times right. But the potential of these three is real. If you only consider potential, they're all above S-class. Even if they lack combat experience, their potential alone is enough to be helpful. The protagonist's party wouldn't be the protagonist's party for no reason.

,It doesn't matter if Seong Hari opposes me until the end."

I've already decided to do it. As the headmaster said, Japan could really be finished. And they won't be satisfied with just Japan. I'll take care of it before things get more extreme. Above all, I've been wronged by Madojeong, so I have to pay them back.

I know that Amaterasu's Jewel is on Mount Fuji. But I didn't know the exact location. I searched near Mount Fuji with Makina last time, but I couldn't find it. There were no traces of Madojeong either.

,I might be able to find them now that they're performing the ritual."

The thought of killing Sanada Kenshi, the leader of Madojeong, already makes my hands itch.

All the students looked at Seong Hari with determined eyes. Seong Hari relaxed her furrowed expression.

,...I have a condition. You said you'd temporarily awaken their potential, right? If that power is below my standards, I won't allow it."

『Name: Hyodo Yuki
