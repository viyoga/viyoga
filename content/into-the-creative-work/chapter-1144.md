"Ryu Hana. Here, you wanted it, right? Take it."

I held out the wrapped Vermilion Bird Sword to her.

"I can't accept it."

"Why? It's the sword you wanted."

"...It's too expensive to receive as a gift so casually. And I don't understand you. No matter how I think about it, this sword isn't worth 30 billion. Even 10 billion is too much. I stayed quiet because I didn't want to meddle in others' affairs... but you've been ripped off. Go back now and get a refund."

30 billion.

She was talking about an amount that's absurd for ordinary people as if it were 3 million won. This showed her sense of money.

'Well, if she borrows the power of the North Diamond Clan, 30 billion is nothing.'

However, Ryu Hana doesn't try to borrow the power of the North Diamond Clan. Not until she graduates from the academy. She tries to live on her own strength.

"Do you think this is an ordinary sword?"

"No. Since it's C-rank, it's not ordinary. It would be at the level of a famous sword. Still, it's not worth 30 billion."

"You really don't know the value of this sword at all. You have no eye for swords."

"......."

Wrinkles formed between Ryu Hana's brows. She, who is more interested in swords than anyone and takes them seriously, reacted as if she'd heard her parents cursed at my weak provocation.

"...Again. Show me the Vermilion Bird Sword again."

"Well, that's not hard."

I unwrapped the Vermilion Bird Sword. Ryu Hana examined the sword with concentration.

Ryu Hana had enough eye for swords. In the first place, she was the one who discovered this sword. Wasn't she staring at it intently through the shop window?

"You can hold it."

"...Didn't the weapon shop owner say you can't touch it because of the heat?"

"You'll be fine."

"......"

Ryu Hana hesitated before gripping the hilt. Ryu Hana's eyes widened. Her mouth opened, and her body trembled. She stared blankly into the air. I waited quietly for her.

'As long as Ryu Hana has the trait Sword Maiden (SS), no sword can reject her.'

It didn't matter if it was a holy sword or a demonic sword.

"......Seong Yujin. You knew from the beginning. This sword... is worth more than 30 billion."

"Can you reveal the true form of the Vermilion Bird Sword?"

"Yes. But...."

"What are you hesitating for? Start right now."

"...This sword is yours. And you'll get involved?"

"I'm giving it to you as a gift. And anyway, I can't even touch it. Instead, can I watch the process of you being recognized by the sword?"

Ryu Hana slowly nodded. Then she lifted the sword in her hand. As she raised her mana, the Vermilion Bird Sword reacted as if it had been waiting. Hwarrrrk. Flames engulfed the blade and burned. The burning flames momentarily took the shape of a vermilion bird before returning to their original blaze.

The space in front of Ryu Hana distorted. The distorted space sucked Ryu Hana in. I hurriedly placed my hand on her shoulder. Ryu Hana and I were sucked into the space.

『You have entered the special dungeon, ???.』

I twitched my eyebrows at the rising notification window.

'It's different.'

It's different from what I know.

If ??? is attached to the special dungeon name, there's only one reason. The original dungeon has mutated.

'Did it mutate because I entered with her? This is annoying.'

•••

Ryu Hana and I stood on top of a Seoul building, looking down below.

We saw a night view of Seoul that was slightly different from what we knew. The buildings that should be towering were low, and some roads were closed. There were few people walking around. Seoul, which should be full of vitality, somehow gave off an eerie atmosphere.

"This is... the past?"

"Yes. The past implemented by the dungeon. Probably around 20 years ago? It could be 19 years ago or 21 years ago."

I said ambiguously, recalling the original story.

"Ryu Hana. You received the trial of the Vermilion Bird, right? What is it?"

"...Find and defeat the enemy."

"Finding it won't be hard. The enemy will be looking for you too."

"Why do you know so well?"

"I've experienced something similar once."

It wasn't referring to the original game. In the first playthrough, I stole the Vermilion Bird Sword, Azure Dragon Spear, Black Tortoise Armor, and White Tiger Sword, forcibly awakened them, and used them. Well, I didn't use them for long. They were particularly picky with me, so I used them as sacrifices for Ruin (S).

I also found out in the first playthrough that you can come to this space with someone else.

"It'll be annoying but not difficult."

"...Where are you going?"

"I have something to check for a moment. Ah, right. Just in case, the dungeon isn't reality. It's not even composed based on reality. It's one of the parallel worlds implemented. You know about parallel theory, right?"

"......A world of possibilities that might exist."

"Simply put, someone who lost the presidential election could become president, and someone who died could be alive in this world. Whatever it is, it has nothing to do with us, so don't waver."

After advising Ryu Hana, I climbed down the rooftop wall to the ground. Landing lightly on the ground, I walked to the road.

Screeeech!

A running car braked urgently upon seeing me. I approached the stopped car.

I smashed the driver's window with my fist and forcibly opened the door.

"Aaaaah! What the hell are you!!"

A salaryman with blood smeared all over his face screamed. I grabbed him, dragged him out, and threw him onto the road.

"This car is mine from now on."

I closed the driver's door. The man trembled in fear, rolling only his eyes. Ignoring him, I stepped on the accelerator. I sped off, ignoring traffic lights. The road became chaotic because of me, and traffic accidents occurred, but who cares. This isn't reality; it's a dungeon anyway.

Beep beep beep.

"Kekeke. Damn cop bastards."

It had been a while since a chase in the city, so I started getting excited.

I sharply turned the steering wheel to show the police a fancy drift, but ended up crashing the car into a telephone pole.

"Haha. My mistake."

It's fine. There are plenty of cars. I stopped a driving car, hijacked it, and continued the chase by stepping on the accelerator again.

The police sirens were loud.

"Get down immediately if you don't want to die!!"

Up ahead. A man blocking the center of the road shouted. A hero. This world was one with heroes.

"No one can stop me."

Boom boom boom boom!

Lightning struck the hero blocking the way. The hero couldn't even dodge and got hit directly. He didn't die but staggered. I floored the accelerator and hit the hero with the car. The hero flew far away and rolled on the ground.

"Haha! You stupid bastard!"

I summoned a bomb and threw it behind me. Seeing the pursuing police cars flying in the sky made me laugh naturally.

"Today is Seoul Fire Sea Day!"

I got out of the car. It was an alley full of houses, and radio sounds came through an open window.

-Emergency news! Namsan Tower has been hit by a bomb terror attack! Namsan Tower is collapsing! Police are panicking! Another update! The police headquarters has been terrorized! They say bombs fell from the sky!

'This really relieves stress.'

I smirked and walked through the alley.

Dodging heroes and police in a heated chase was easy. I just needed to use a space movement scroll.

'Hm?'

The place I found was Seong Hari's house. Precisely, the house where Seong Hari and I lived together until I entered the academy dormitory.

'It wasn't this rundown.'

I felt signs of people in the house.

I opened the door and went in. The door was locked. I broke the doorknob with force and entered.

"......?"

"W-Who are you?"

A middle-aged man and woman watching TV looked at me. I stared intently at their faces. No memory. Unrelated to Seong Hari. I looked around. It was an unfamiliar house.

-...Wanted for terrorism! The exact identity is not yet known, but if you see this face, report to the police immediately!

My face appeared on TV.

The middle-aged couple alternated between the TV and my face, then their complexions turned pale in an instant.

I turned around and went out the front door.

"Kyaaaaaaaah!"

"P-Police! Police!!!"

Ignoring their flustered voices, I scratched my head.

'Seong Hari said she bought that house after I was born.... Is this an era before I was born?'

Since it's one of the parallel worlds, it might be a world where I don't exist at all.

'Ah, right. If I check the year, I can roughly calculate what Seong Hari is doing now.'

I turned back and entered the house again. I intimidated the middle-aged couple to find out the time period.

'Ha. To think it's not even the time when Seong Hari attends the academy.'

The place to go was decided.

•••

Jinryeong Seongga (True Spirit Family).

I stopped, looking at the Jinryeong Seongga ahead. Jinryeong Seongga is one of Korea's long-established prestigious families. If I raid it with my current skills, I'll be the one subdued instead.

This dungeon is a fake implementing a parallel world, but my life isn't fake.

I activated Spirit Eye and Heavenly Eye simultaneously.

I pierced through the interior of Jinryeong Seongga. Barrier? It doesn't matter since I'm not entering directly. It's not a tremendous barrier that can block Spirit Eye and Heavenly Eye.

'Still a lot of spirits. Seong Hangu and Seong Myeongsaeng are... sitting side by side watching TV.'

Seong Hari wasn't visible. She seemed not at home.

I sat on a nearby bench.

The reason I'm looking for Seong Hari is to dig into the secret of my birth. Even if the dungeon's background is a parallel world, with luck, things related to Seong Hari might not have changed.

However, the moment I learned the year, I realized it was meaningless.

'Too far in the past. Not to mention before I was born, even before Seong Hari entered the academy.'

Still, the reason I came to Jinryeong Seongga was simply because I wanted to see Seong Hari once. In the end, I didn't meet that Seong Hari either.

'She might be somewhere else.... Or, this world is one where Seong Hari doesn't exist. Ryu Hana should be doing well, right?'

According to the original, Ryu Hana would be fighting herself. And she would win without difficulty. The difficulty was quite easy in the original too.

I suddenly looked around. Low-level spirits were gathering around me.

'Lots of spirits near Jinryeong Seongga.'

They were drawn by my high spirit affinity.

I thought about chasing them away, but since they weren't trying to harm me, I decided to leave them be.

I thought of Moka and Makina. This dungeon is special, so I couldn't summon Moka and Makina.

'Probably time outside the dungeon is stopped. More precisely, it flows very slowly as if stopped.'

This dungeon was special enough to have a different axis of time and space.

'Should I rest a bit and rampage once more? Or finding a beauty and fucking her wouldn't be bad.'

While pondering,

I felt someone's presence.

I looked up toward the direction of the presence. Under the streetlight, a woman was walking this way. Black school uniform different from the academy, black bob hair barely reaching her shoulders. On her back, something over 2m long was slung diagonally.

"Wow. A ton of kids are gathered. Are you a spirit master too, oppa?"

Short stature. Breasts at a modest level. Baby fat still remaining on her face.

I knew the moment I saw her.

She is Seong Hari from her middle school days.
