Upon appearing on the Chinese continent, I first concealed Susanoo's power.

'The bastards here are belligerent. If I keep flaunting my strength, they'll come looking for me first.'

My goal isn't to fight the continent's strongmen. There's no need to waste my strength pointlessly.

'I am curious whether killing the bastards here would grow the Storm God trait, but...'

My circumstances aren't good enough to satisfy my curiosity. I mustn't forget what needs to be done.

'My destination is the Heavenly Realm.'

Or what is also called the Immortal Realm.

Just as Japan had Takamagahara, the continent surely has a Heavenly Realm as well.

'Climbing blindly into the sky won't reveal the Heavenly Realm. There must be a place that connects to it.'

This continent is vast beyond measure.

Naturally, there can't be only one path connected to the Heavenly Realm.

'Two paths come to mind immediately. Mount Tai and Kunlun.'

Mount Tai is where emperors perform the Fengshan ritual to announce their ascension to the Heavenly and Earthly Deities.

Kunlun is where numerous immortals are said to dwell. They say the Queen Mother of the West resides there as well. It's also said that none may enter Kunlun without permission. Thus, Kunlun had to be regarded as half the Heavenly Realm itself.

'Even for a vast continent, flying on the wind shouldn't take long. Mount Tai first, since it's closer.'

I flew while suppressing Susanoo's power as much as possible.

Upon arriving at Mount Tai, I confirmed the path leading to the Heavenly Realm. Heavenly Soldiers were guarding the way up. Even if they were Heavenly Soldiers, they weren't particularly difficult opponents for me now.

'Killing them and ascending to the Heavenly Realm is easy. But if I do that, my only option becomes fighting the Heavenly Realm.'

I was the one at a disadvantage.

I released the Susanoo power I had been suppressing. The Heavenly Soldiers all turned their heads to look at me at once.

"A god of the Eastern Sea, I see. For what business have you come all this way?"

One of the Heavenly Soldiers spoke. Polite in speech, yet making no effort to hide his wariness.

"I've come to meet the Jade Emperor."

"...Even if you are a god of the Eastern Sea, that one is not someone who can be met simply because one wishes to."

"It's an important matter. Japan's future hangs in the balance. I am Takehaya Susanoo-no-Mikoto, and the chief deity of Takamagahara is my sister."

The moment I revealed my identity, the Heavenly Soldiers' pupils wavered.

"Please wait a moment. I will inform the Heavenly Realm of the facts and return with permission."

"I trust it won't take long."

The Heavenly Soldier used transformation arts. He became an elegant crane and ascended the heavenly path, flying toward the Heavenly Realm.

Since I was bored while waiting, I pulled a manga from my inventory and read.

"...God of the Eastern Sea. What is that book? Is it a picture collection?"

"Ah. You don't know? This is called a manga. Want to see?"

"It's a book I've never seen before, so I'm curious. May I take a look?"

"Look as much as you like."

I gladly tossed the manga to the Heavenly Soldier. Mangas could be obtained anytime.

I tossed him a type that didn't require reading the speech bubbles to enjoy.

"Hek, th-this is...! Such embarrassing illustrations...!"

The Heavenly Soldiers were startled, yet couldn't take their eyes off the erotic manga. It's only natural—it's a man's instinct.

"How about it. This is our Japan's culture!"

"I-isn't it too open-minded?"

"It's a cultural difference. Understand it. Or are you disrespecting our culture?"

"N-no, I'm not."

"There must be some whose tastes don't match. Here's a gravure photo collection for those guys."

"This can't be! Such realistic illustrations!!"

It was when I was spreading Japan's proud culture. The Heavenly Soldier who had ascended to the Heavenly Realm came back down. He wasn't alone. He had brought dozens of immortals and soldiers with him.

'These bastards. A stab in the back right away? And they call themselves immortals.... No, is it because they're immortals?'

Having read xianxia novels and knowing the true nature of immortals, I prepared to tear a spatial teleportation scroll and surveyed my surroundings.

'Hm. No hostility. Was I overreacting?'

The immortals that stood out were the three in front.

Erlang Shen, clad in splendid armor and gripping a three-pointed double-edged lance.

Nezha, flying on Wind-Fire Wheels beneath his feet and holding a fire-tipped spear.

Sword Immortal Lü Dongbin, armed with a sword on his back.

"God from the Eastern Sea. His Majesty has granted you an audience."

"Those eyes—they're similar to that monkey bastard, aren't they? Though it's lightning instead of fire inside, the essence is nearly identical. ...No? Compared to those eyes, his seem weaker, maybe?"

"Susanoo. I've heard your name. They say you're Japan's foremost war god. I'd like to witness your sword, but... it seems there's no time for that."

I quietly watched them and assessed. It was an instinctive act.

'If I fight three against one, I lose without question. Even one-on-one, I can't guarantee victory. No, the odds are high that I lose.'

That said, I wasn't particularly discouraged. The three before me were among the most renowned divine generals even within the Heavenly Realm.

"Follow me."

"Understood."

I silently followed them.

I was able to see the Jade Emperor wearing his crown. A great number of immortals had gathered around him, silently staring at me. It was clearly a ploy to crush my spirit first. Of course, I'm not the kind to cower at mere pressure like this.

Instead, I made my Mind's Eye gleam and examined them one by one. Most of the immortals who met my eyes flinched and trembled. Not a few gods broke into cold sweats and showed PTSD reactions. The immortals whispered quietly.

"Those eyes.... Could he be someone else in disguise?"

"Impossible. We've already finished verifying. He is definitely the war god of the Eastern Sea."

"Ugh.... My back still aches from where that bastard hit me."

"They say that one caused havoc in the Eastern Sea's Heavenly Realm and was chased out. I don't know for certain, but his temperament is likely similar. Everyone, be careful."

Just because they served under the Jade Emperor didn't mean they were all unconditionally great beings. Most failed to catch my interest. However. Among them, a woman in white robes caught my gaze.

Our eyes met. The woman smiled gently.

I was startled. It felt as though everything inside me was being laid bare to her. This woman seemed more formidable than the Jade Emperor himself.

'Perhaps because of the gap in rank, I can't see her name even with the Mind's Eye. Well, that's true of most immortals here, though.'

I couldn't see the names of the Jade Emperor, Erlang Shen, Nezha, or Lü Dongbin either. Even without being able to see their names, they're so famous that I know them anyway.

'That woman's identity is probably....'

Guan Yin Bodhisattva.

I don't know why she's here, but.

I decided to wrap up the battle of wills and knelt before the Jade Emperor. I was the one at a disadvantage, so kneeling was something I could do.

"Your Majesty! The surface of present-day Japan is being thrown into chaos by a most heinous being called Yamata-no-Orochi! I sought to subdue Yamata-no-Orochi for Japan's peace and order, but my own strength was insufficient! I have come to the Heavenly Realm to request aid!"

"I see. Yamata-no-Orochi.... Even I have heard tidings of the eight-headed serpent from afar. However... Japan is not my domain. Does Japan not have Takamagahara? Would it not be proper to seek aid from your sister?"

"I have been exiled from Takamagahara, so I cannot meet my sister now."

"For what reason were you exiled?"

"I got kicked out for causing some trouble. Well, that's not the important part. Please help me. This is a matter for justice. As for compensation for your aid... my sister will handle it."

"...Huu. This is a matter unrelated to us, so it's difficult to help. Return to Japan. I will speak to Takamagahara of the situation. Would Takamagahara ignore matters of the surface?"

"You cannot say it's entirely unrelated to the Heavenly Realm!"

"What do you mean?"

"Yamata-no-Orochi has joined hands with the Nine-Tailed Fox of the White Face and Golden Fur. That fox's true identity is Daji. Because the Heavenly Realm failed to subdue Daji in time and let her escape, our Japan has fallen into dire straits."

"So Daji is running rampant in Japan. Letting Daji escape is... certainly our responsibility, I can say. However, with only that..."

That was when Guan Yin Bodhisattva interjected.

"Your Majesty. Present-day Takamagahara has no capacity to look after the surface. It must be the aftermath of Japan's sun disappearing not long ago. Takamagahara is currently sealed, and Susanoo's bloodline is more than sufficient to represent Takamagahara. There should be no problem in giving him aid."

"If the Bodhisattva says so.... However, if we dispatch heavenly troops, there will surely be friction with Takamagahara in the future. Moreover, if heavenly troops disappear, won't the yokai of the surface run wild once more?"

"I have a good idea. Let us ask Sun Wukong. Currently, he has been stripped of his post and holds the status of Tripitaka's disciple, so if he aids Susanoo, it will be a matter unrelated to the Heavenly Realm."

"Hmm. I understand the Bodhisattva's words. But... will he agree to help so readily?"

"Sun Wukong is not the monkey he once was. I will introduce Susanoo to him personally."

"If the Bodhisattva takes charge of the matter, I am reassured. I leave it to you."

The matter was settled for now.

I received tea at Guan Yin Bodhisattva's residence.

"I have sent word to Sun Wukong, so he will arrive soon. Are you perhaps acquainted with Sun Wukong?"

"I know of him. He's an incredibly famous figure, after all."

"Including whether he ultimately reaches Tianzhu, I suppose?"

"...How much do you know?"

"I know that this world is fake. But to you, it must be real. If I were to make an excuse.... That was the best we could do."

An excuse for what.

I felt she was speaking not of this world, but of the world outside the dungeon. The world the gods abandoned.

What should I say.

Should I tell her I have no interest in such things. While I was frowning and deliberating, she spoke.

"It seems he has arrived."

Sun Wukong opened the door with surprising courtesy. He shows proper respect to Guan Yin Bodhisattva.

"Bodhisattva. I heard the story. Is it this one?"

A monkey wearing a golden fillet on his head looked at me. His two eyes blazed red as if flames were flickering, and the pupils at their center were golden.

Sun Wukong peered into my eyes, then chuckled.

"You implemented my eyes with a spell? Interesting. What's more interesting is... that a human is playing at being a god. I was going to refuse because I'm busy, but... fine. I'll help you. Sung Yoojin."

"You recognized me immediately. As expected of the Great Sage Equal to Heaven. How far did you see?"

The Fiery Golden Eyes can discern the name and history of a target. In other words, it means he can even know when I was born.

"That you're a human from several thousand years in the future. Beyond that, even I don't know the details. There's only one reason I'm helping. What happens to me in the future? Do I successfully reach Tianzhu?"

I glanced at Guan Yin Bodhisattva. She merely smiled benevolently without a word.

She knows.

That no matter what conversation I have with Sun Wukong here, no matter what information I convey, it's all meaningless.

"Victorious Fighting Buddha. I'm reassured that you'll help."

"Hehehe. Victorious Fighting Buddha, is it. I see. Hehe."

Before I departed with Sun Wukong, Guan Yin Bodhisattva spoke one last time.

"You are doing well. Everything will work out."

It was a pleasant blessing to hear.
