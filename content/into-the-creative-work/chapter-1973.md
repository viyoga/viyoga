The village chief didn't have much information. It was less fruitful than I thought, and I started to get pissed off.

,I don't know where Andvari's vault is, but I know who does!

,What kind of guy is he to get into an important vault related to Fafnir's power?

,I heard he went to clean up because the gold was piled up indiscriminately. I heard that Fafnir shoved gold into the vault haphazardly, blocking the entrance.

Come to think of it, even if Fafnir collected gold here, there wouldn't be much place to use it.

,According to legend, his obsession with gold was due to a curse, wasn't it.

Anyway, I'm going to gobble up that gold. It's probably at least dozens of tons.

,Is that village far from here?

,You can get there if you walk steadily for half a day.

,How many villages like this are in this area?

,There are four in total. We're on the outermost side.

,You all received the system's transaction guarantee authority like your village?

,Yes, yes! That's right. The system said it wouldn't interfere with how we use that authority!

I had a hunch.

This was a kind of runaround. A quest runaround in the way of listening to the village's requests and conditions and getting information.

,System. That bastard only learned the bad stuff.

I clicked my tongue. Coaxing the villagers was originally part of the strategy.

,It's impossible to conquer this in three days. Fafnir will come looking for it in three days. Do I get gold from the village or borrow it to offer to Fafnir to buy time?

Four villages in this area.

They probably all have different information about Fafnir.

,Those villages are probably mining gold too.

,Yes. We regularly offer gold to Fafnir. Fafnir doesn't ask for anything else if we just offer gold... I guess we've gotten used to it. Actually, we're not that afraid of Fafnir.

,Is there anything unique about your village that differentiates it from the other villages?

,We, we're....

The village chief was sweating. It wasn't sweat from the pain of his severed arms and legs. He was hiding something from me.

,You know you've decided to pledge absolute loyalty, right? You better talk well.

As my gaze sharpened, the village chief hurriedly opened his mouth.

,Our village has an alchemy elixir that turns gold into other metals. It's an elixir made by our ancestor Arta... so we call it Arta's Elixir. We were able to mine more gold than other villages with that elixir.

,Does it permanently turn gold into other metals? That's a big loss.

,It turns back into the original gold over time. We change the gold through this elixir every time Fafnir comes. So Fafnir doesn't know we're hoarding gold.

,I have to see it myself. Hand it over.

The village chief made a tearful face and had a young man from the village bring Arta's Elixir. It was a liquid that looked like mercury.

「Arta's Elixir

Temporarily turns gold into random metals.

The mutation lasts for 40 minutes.

Rank: S」

,The metal changes randomly? That's exactly my style. How much gold can you change with one bottle?

,The elixir turns into a gas and spreads everywhere when it touches gold. All the gold that touches that gas turns into other metals.

,You mean I should sprinkle it in Fafnir's gold vault to weaken Fafnir.

I took Arta's Elixir.

,Is there anything else in that western village? Something special like you have. Think carefully. You wouldn't want to be the only ones getting robbed, would you?

I stared at the village chief and asked. The village chief, who didn't seem to have a good personality, said without much thought.

,Those guys have a treasure called Valkyrie's Wings. It's a treasure that allows you to fly in the sky for a while. They swaggered around, saying they were descendants of the Valkyries, and the proof was Valkyrie's Wings.

,If they're descendants of the Valkyries, they must be pretty. I'm looking forward to it.

,No. It's the same here and there. It's clear that the descendants of the Valkyries are bullshit.

,What about the other villages?

,I don't know much because there's little exchange.

,I knew it.

I didn't think I could get all the information at once anyway. It's probably the rule to go to the next village and get information about the village after that.

,Village chief. Don't be lazy just because you don't have arms and legs, and work diligently. If there isn't enough gold to satisfy me when I come back... I'll tear you apart and kill you.

,Hiiiik! I, I'll collect the gold!

I didn't like the village chief, but there was no need to kill him. The village chief was now my slave, and a slave was soon property.

I called the Omicos Mercenary Corps together in one place and shared some of the information I had extracted from the village chief.

,There are three more villages like this. Each village probably has something special. I'm going to treat the other villages the same way I treat this village. I won't force you. Those who feel uncomfortable killing villagers or taking their property, stay here or leave the area.

,I'll secretly kill anyone who says they're leaving.

No one among the mercenaries said they would leave. Their eyes were shining with a strange desire. Some were blatantly fondling the gold in their hands. It was worth giving out the gold. Greed blinds people. The old saying is never wrong.

,Well, that's how mercenaries are.

The only one who's frowning at my words is Omicos, the leader of the mercenary corps.

,Omicos is a very rare person among mercenaries. That's for sure. He's inflexible? He was inflexible by mercenary standards.

But even that Omicos had something he was greedy for.

Fafnir's blood.

Immortality.

With that greed, he remained silent even after seeing his comrades' greed. In a way, Omicos was the most greedy.

,No matter how I think about it, these bastards seem like they're going to betray me in the end.

I hid my true feelings and smiled.

,There will be at least dozens of tons of gold in Fafnir's gold vault. If you follow me to the end... I'll give you 20% of the gold piled up in the gold vault after everything is over.

,I'll do my best to serve Yujin-nim!

,There's not much time. The village chief said it's half a day away... but it won't take us even half of that. Let's go! For the gold!
