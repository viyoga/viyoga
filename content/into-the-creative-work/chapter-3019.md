I traveled to China with a teleportation Awakened from the Association.

"Then I wish you good fortune, Hunter Sung Yujin."

He dropped me off at a base in Sichuan, China, and vanished immediately.

'A teleportation ability. Convenient, I'll give it that.'

Unlike spatial movement scrolls, teleportation doesn't require memorizing coordinates each time you shift through space. The tradeoff was that you could only teleport to places you'd already visited.

'Teleportation Awakened start at minimum B-rank without any questions asked.'

They don't even fight monsters directly despite being Hunters. Teleporters handle urgent deliveries, just like what I'd experienced. Most belong to the World Hunter Association, and even if they're S-rank, you can't just order them around without justification.

'Honestly, I'm not that envious. A convenient ability comes with equally convenient restrictions.'

Within cities, barriers restrict teleportation to designated locations like Association headquarters. They also have to implant location trackers in their bodies.

'That's the aftermath of a teleportation Awakened terrorizing major cities like New York in the past.'

People envy teleporters and fear them in equal measure. If one suddenly appeared and threw a bomb, there'd be no stopping it. They could commit the perfect crime with terrifying ease.

'I know the cheat-like nature of long-distance spatial movement better than anyone.'

Regardless, thanks to that ability, I'd arrived comfortably enough.

I looked around.

The city was in a state of ruin. Buildings collapsed everywhere, grass and trees growing through the cracks in concrete. On the surface they looked like ordinary plants, but they'd been enhanced by Phyron's influence. Nothing like man-eating vegetation, so there was no real danger.

Before me stood the Chinese Hunter Association's Sichuan Branch building. Other structures had been swallowed by vegetation, but this one alone remained pristine. The sense of active maintenance was unmistakable.

Before I could step inside, Chinese Hunters poured out and bowed their heads in greeting. What came from his mouth was awkward Korean.

"Hunter Sung Yujin! Welcome to China! We've been waiting for your arrival! But... did you come alone?"

Originally, Yura was supposed to accompany me. Yura felt like a secretary or aide assigned by the World Hunter Association, comfortable enough. Though in reality, there was probably an element of surveillance involved.

But with Yura's rapid growth recently, she needed recalibration. For an ordinary Hunter, maybe not. But she was using a portion of the yokai's power sealed within her body, which required delicate tuning.

'I told her to hold off and stopped her from following.'

That would be more helpful for Yura. And even without her, there's no major problem. I'm Sung Yujin, the language genius who can handle Chinese too.

"You can speak in Chinese."

"Your pronunciation is better than mine. Now I understand why you came alone. I am Gwan Hacheon, Branch Manager of the Chinese Hunter Association's Sichuan Branch."

A man in his late thirties, he wore a crisp suit, but his hair was beyond disheveled—practically matted. Deep dark circles sat beneath both eyes. I was somewhat surprised that an A-rank Hunter could look so worn down by fatigue.

The other Chinese Hunters nearby looked tired too, though not to Gwan Hacheon's degree.

"I don't sense any S-rank Hunter presence. Don't tell me I'm the only one who arrived? Wasn't this a mission with other S-rank Hunters?"

"I just received word that those individuals encountered a situation. It seems they'll arrive a bit late."

"What kind of situation?"

"That's... I don't know the details either. They didn't tell me."

"Who are the S-rank Hunters I'll be working with?"

Even though we'd be on the same mission, I hadn't received prior information. That's how highly S-rank Hunter information was guarded.

"I apologize. It's classified, so I cannot say."

Currently, there are eleven active S-rank Hunters operating in China.

Compared to Korea or Japan, that number was excessive—and half of them, six, had become S-rank within the past three years.

It wasn't that China had faked it to show off. The World Hunter Association doesn't operate so loosely.

'It means China's situation is that dire. And as a rule, difficult times produce exceptional individuals.'

Peaceful Korea and Japan each have only two active S-rank Hunters.

Korea has me and Han Ahyoung.

Japan has the Wind Shrine Maiden Amatsuka Koyori and the Ninja King. Koyori resides at Fuka Shrine, while the Ninja King's whereabouts have been unknown since ancient times.

'Korea and Japan have fewer active S-rank Hunters than the previous generation.'

You can't become S-rank through talent alone. Experience matters just as much. In today's comparatively peaceful era, that experience is inevitably scarce.

'Korea especially was more peaceful than Japan.'

Korea had no active S-rank Hunters for several years before Han Ahyoung achieved the rank.

In East Asia, only Korea lacking an S-rank Hunter? From Korea's perspective, it was a national crisis. So the Korean government and Korean Hunter Association gave Han Ahyoung every possible resource.

Thunder Heaven Style—Electromagnetic Waves.

I used electromagnetic waves to scan my surroundings. Gwan Hacheon flinched in surprise.

"Oh. You sensed the dissonance? Your mana perception is excellent. Most A-rank Hunters wouldn't even notice."

"My ability is super-sensory perception. I can detect even the finest changes through my senses. It's developed particularly toward detection."

Super-sensory perception is an ability both common and uncommon, much like acceleration abilities.

Unlike acceleration, it's a permanently active ability, and focusing sharpens the senses even further.

"I read online that super-sensory ability users feel sexual pleasure more intensely than others. Is that true?"

Gwan Hacheon's expression turned impure for a split second before he immediately corrected it.

"...I couldn't lie to you, Hunter Sung Yujin. It's true."

Gwan Hacheon let out a small sigh.

Super-sensory perception was famous enough that the internet was full of rumors about it. My question was just one of many.

"There are people hiding in this ruined city. Not residents, obviously... Can I kill them?"

"They're self-proclaimed journalist Hunters. Despite holding Hunter licenses, they have no intention of hunting monsters—they mainly film restricted areas off-limits to civilians and upload the footage online for view-count revenue. Most have awakened stealth-type abilities below D-rank. Occasionally they tail us and secretly film."

"So they're leeches. Has the mission been leaked?"

"No. They were here six months ago too. I should have dealt with them beforehand... This is my oversight. I'll have it handled within thirty minutes."

"Forget it. They've actually saved me the trouble."

"Pardon?"

"Ah. You weren't about to ask me to protect them, were you?"

"This is a restricted zone off-limits to civilians."

The implication was clear—killing them would pose no problem.

"No monsters around. Clean things up properly and they could live. Why is it empty of people?"

"Sichuan is under Phyron's control. It may look peaceful now, but... when people gather in numbers, they emerge."

"Why does China leave the Phyrons alone? Couldn't you mobilize S-rank Hunters and wipe them out in one sweep?"

"Our problem isn't just the Phyrons. The simultaneous dungeon breaks that erupted across China that day—the aftermath of the Great Cataclysm—still hasn't been contained. And for an all-out war, the abilities the Phyrons possess are the issue."

A Phyron's combat capability rivals an A-rank Hunter. The problem is their special abilities.

Phyrons use invisible wavelengths to control lower-tier monsters, interfere with spatial movement, and disrupt communications. And recently, it was discovered that Phyrons can trigger dungeon breaks.

'They can't cause dungeon breaks indiscriminately. If they could, they'd have completely taken over China by now. There must be conditions.'

The problem was that there wasn't even a sliver of a clue as to what those conditions were.

"We—China—do not want another Great Cataclysm."

"No country wants that. If China falls now, Korea and Japan fall too. America and Europe would suffer tremendous damage as well. But this is Phyron territory, yet there's no communication disruption or spatial interference."

"We're located at the outermost edge of Phyron territory. Do you see that dense forest over there?"

"It's so thick it looks dark even in broad daylight."

"The Phyrons are in there. At first, we thought Phyrons were monsters that operated individually. That was a misconception. They possess a sense of kinship that ordinary monsters lack. They're social animals, like humans."

"Their true form is plant-based, though. So the Phyrons in that forest are mimicking ordinary trees."

I approached the forest and used electromagnetic waves.

The electromagnetic waves couldn't penetrate the forest's interior. The Phyron's communication disruption ability was clearly active.

"Eliminating the special entity is the objective of this mission. What intel do you have on it?"

"Here it is."

Gwan Hacheon held out a tablet.

A video was playing. In a dark forest, a pure white tree glowed brilliantly.

-"Oh, oooh... It's divine! A divine tree...!"

The man filming muttered as he walked toward the white tree. I paused the video.

"Who's the one who filmed this? The quality's too good for a camera phone. What I understand even less is what possessed him to go in there."

"He was an A-rank Hunter journalist with a stealth ability."

"Hunter journalist? Ah, so that's what you call the leeches hiding around here."

"Yes. The Chinese Hunter Association bought this footage for one million dollars. He retired from hunting immediately after."

"He came back alive? Capable bastard."

I resumed the video.

He approached the white tree and reached out his hand. His hand touched the surface that looked smooth, as if it weren't wood at all. His hand sank into the tree. Slowly, like sinking into a swamp. Red blood ran down the pure white trunk. It was eating the man's arm, little by little.

His fingers, the back of his hand, his wrist, his forearm—all consumed. When only his elbow remained, the man snapped to his senses.

-"Hngh?! Kyaaaaaaah! My arm!!!"

White branches moved like tentacles, trying to grab the man. But their movement was somehow sluggish. The man, having created distance, fled while still filming the white tree to the very end.

"He filmed well even in that situation. Should I call it professional dedication?"

"I'd say it's an impressive obsession with money."

"Now I understand why the Chinese government and World Hunter Association dispatched S-rank Hunters. Even immature, it dazed an A-rank Hunter for about twenty seconds. If it fully matures, even S-rank Hunters might struggle against it."

An A-rank Hunter, reduced to this.

Hunters below D-rank and ordinary civilians wouldn't even be able to resist.

'Phyrons use wavelengths to disrupt communications and spatial movement. That special entity must be using wavelengths to seize the minds of living creatures.'

Against me, with my absolute mind, it was nothing. As long as it only had that one ability.

"Yes. It's exactly as you've assessed, Hunter Sung Yujin. If this mission fails, the government is... considering nuclear options."

"The justification is sufficient."

If Korea, America, Japan, Russia and others heard about this thing, they'd immediately call for a nuclear strike.

Even I could feel how dangerous this creature was.

'One wrong move with this thing and reality could go to hell.'

A typical Phyron emits wavelengths spanning at least ten kilometers. In other words, if the special entity matures, it could effortlessly obliterate an entire city.

'But a white tree. The mural I saw in the deserted island dungeon had a red tree. Is it related to the red tree? If that thing matures, it won't turn into a red tree, will it?'

I was lost in thought when it happened.

I felt mana surging through my ki-sense. The precursor phenomenon of teleportation.

Immediately after, three men and women appeared from empty air. Excluding the teleporter, two of them were faces I recognized.
