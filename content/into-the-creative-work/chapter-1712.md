[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

Using Instant, I dodged Kwak Soo-hyuk's attack to the side, summoned Moonlight Sonata 7, grabbed it, and swung it at the bastard.

Kkang!

The sword failed to completely cut through Kwak Soo-hyuk's armor. The blade did sink in a bit, but it didn't reach his skin.

Kwak Soo-hyuk's armor wriggled, and hundreds of tiny spikes sprouted on the surface, shooting towards me.

The spikes hammered against the armor. Most of them bounced off, but some pierced the armor.

,"Tough stuff. Where the hell did you get that? Is it another one of the witch's trinkets?"

As I spoke, Kwak Soo-hyuk swung his greatsword. I hurriedly retreated, dodging the attack.

,'Damn it. Normally, the spikes wouldn't even be able to scratch Stormbreaker.'

That wasn't all. I couldn't use most of Stormbreaker's functions. Like combining with other weapons, or creating clones.

,'If I could just use the clone ability... Here it comes!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk threw his greatsword at me. The greatsword changed shape in mid-air. A chain net. If I got caught in that net, it was game over.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 4]

,'He's been using his head a bit. The way he utilizes his abilities has improved a lot since the last time I saw him.'

Kwak Soo-hyuk had realized that weapons weren't everything. Yoon Seo-jin must have taught him.

Using Instant, I dodged the net and approached Kwak Soo-hyuk.

,"I knew you'd come right in! You arrogant bastard!"

Kwak Soo-hyuk shouted, raising his greatsword.

Yeongcheon Style (影天流) - Blue Stream (碧溪).

I shook my body, stepping with a special footwork. Kwak Soo-hyuk faltered. He was confused, unable to grasp the distance between us.

,"Where did you learn those cheap tricks...!"

,"These cheap tricks are going to kill you."

,"There!"

He seemed to have figured out my location by hearing my voice. But it didn't matter. My goal was to get right up close. At the same time as reaching out my hand, I summoned a bomb from my inventory.

I could feel Kwak Soo-hyuk's surprise all the way over here. If I had summoned the bomb from afar and thrown it, he would have either dodged it or used his ability to detonate it from a distance, nine times out of ten.

,'At this distance, he can't dodge or react in time.'

Of course, I would also be within the blast radius, so I would take damage too.

,'I trust Stormbreaker. It'll withstand some of it, if not all.'

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 3]

Before the bomb exploded, I threw myself away using Instant. This should allow me to avoid a direct hit.

Kwaaaaaang!

A sonic boom echoed before the bomb exploded. A shockwave that struck from the side blew the bomb in the opposite direction. I rolled across the floor, while Kwak Soo-hyuk was caught in the shockwave and explosion, flying upwards before crashing down.

I raised my head. The source of the shockwave was Yoon Seo-jin. Yoon Seo-jin and Joo Seo-hyun were fighting. Joo Seo-hyun was moving as if dancing, trying to close the distance, while Yoon Seo-jin was using her abilities while keeping her distance.

Every time Yoon Seo-jin swung her hand, it became a massive slash, instantly cutting through trees and stone floors.

,'Super-vibrating blade wind, huh. You'd die instantly if that touched you.'

Jeobeok.

Kwak Soo-hyuk got up. Some of the armor covering his body was shattered, and blood was flowing down. The slightly exposed face showed no sign of pain. Instead, intense anger and hatred were etched there.

,"Seong Yujin...! If it weren't for you...! If it weren't for you, Joo Seo-hyun would be with me...!"

,"Even if it wasn't me, Joo Seo-hyun wouldn't have anything to do with a guy like you. You know that too."

,"Shut up!"

Kwak Soo-hyuk swung his left arm. A large amount of blood scattered into the air from the wound on his arm.

I tensed up. Kwak Soo-hyuk's ability was to turn his blood into iron. Its strength was far greater than ordinary iron, and he could change its shape at will. Most annoying of all, he could control the transformed weapons, albeit slightly.

The scattered blood droplets transformed into swords. The swords, densely filling the air, stopped as if time had stopped. The swords were simply waiting for their master's command.

,"I'm going to turn you into mincemeat! Seong Yujin!!"

A massive number of swords fell simultaneously. I couldn't dodge this even with Instant. Because there was nowhere to dodge. I couldn't even be sure if Stormbreaker would hold up.

,'The only thing I can trust in times like these is Complete Recovery.'

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

I used Instant to move forward a little faster. Thanks to that, I avoided dozens of swords. But hundreds of other swords pierced my body.

Tutututung!

The glancing blows were all deflected. But the impact wasn't completely gone. And the swords that pierced properly tore through the armor and ripped my flesh.

I raised my shoulders and lowered my head, somehow preventing the swords from piercing my head.

When the rain of swords ended, I was covered in blood. The armor was tattered, and dozens of swords were stuck in my back like hedgehog quills, not falling out.

,"Hahaha! I won! Farewell! Joo Seo-hyun will be with me!"

,"That's not going to happen."

Pook.

I shoved Moonlight Sonata 7 through the gap in the broken armor. The blade started from his shoulder and pierced his heart.

,"Keoeok! How, how are you still alive?!"

,"I'm kind of used to this sort of thing."

Moonlight Sonata 7's great magic seeped into Kwak Soo-hyuk. Kwak Soo-hyuk's face twisted in pain, and his legs gave way, collapsing downwards.

,"Sh, shit. Just one hit and... this can't be happening.... I didn't become a vampire to meet such an end... Keueueueueu...."

The armor covering Kwak Soo-hyuk's body turned into blood and fell away.

,"You're the one who's going to become mincemeat."

Before he completely died, I swung my sword in rapid succession. Cutting off his arms, slicing his thighs, stabbing his entrails. After that, I swung the sword indiscriminately. At first, he screamed, but at some point, he fell silent. He was completely dead.

The mincemeat was bathed in the sunset and began to burn.

I lost all strength in my body and collapsed to the floor.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

[Using Complete Recovery.]

I used Complete Recovery. All the swords that were stuck in my body were pushed out and fell away.

I dispelled Stormbreaker. It was tattered, so I would have to focus on repairing it again for a while.

Kwaaaaaang!

An explosion was heard not far away.

There was no smoke or flames, which are characteristic of explosions. It was definitely the shockwave that Yoon Seo-jin was using with his ability.

,'Joo Seo-hyun and Yoon Seo-jin are still fighting. I need to go help Joo Seo-hyun.'

I got up. I was quite anxious. I had a feeling that Joo Seo-hyun might die like this. In this world, she was just an ordinary human. Her physical limitations would soon catch up.

,'A Vampire Lord is a tough opponent for Joo Seo-hyun too.'

Joo Seo-hyun's chances of winning in a head-on fight would be very low.

I ran towards the sound of the explosion.

The situation was even worse. Joo Seo-hyun was sitting on the floor. Leaning on her sword for support, she glared at Yoon Seo-jin. Parts of her clothes were soaked with blood.

,"Butcher. I'm most looking forward to seeing what abilities you'll have when you become a vampire."

Yoon Seo-jin took out a small syringe from his coat pocket. The syringe was filled with a bright red liquid.

,"It contains my blood. Fresh blood drawn yesterday. This much should be more than enough to turn you into a vampire."

,"I'd rather...."

,"Rather die? Do you think I'm stupid enough to just stand by and watch?"

I concealed my presence as much as possible. Yoon Seo-jin was focused on Joo Seo-hyun. I could use Instant at least twice more. I also had Moonlight Sonata 7 in my hand, which was more powerful than a silver sword against a vampire.

,'If I seize the opportunity and attack... I can kill him!'

I quietly approached Joo Seo-hyun. In order to inject the syringe into Joo Seo-hyun, Yoon Seo-jin would have no choice but to approach Joo Seo-hyun himself.

I moved for a moment of opportunity.

Joo Seo-hyun noticed me. But she didn't say anything and pretended not to know. She didn't even look at me. As expected, Joo Seo-hyun was calm even in this dangerous moment.

,"Yoon Seo-jin!!!!"

With a shout filled with anger, Choi Sang-woo suddenly popped out. Yoon Seo-jin, who was approaching Joo Seo-hyun, stopped and turned his head sharply. His eyes turned from Choi Sang-woo to me.

,'Shit....'

Choi Sang-woo appeared. And to make matters worse, right next to me. Thanks to that, I was discovered.

,"The butcher's partner and the wolf pup, huh."

Choi Sang-woo, seemingly unaware that I was there, jumped towards Choi Sang-woo, completely excited. A leaping power of over 10m at once was the strength of a werewolf.

,"I'm going to kill you, Yoon Seo-jin!!"

,"That filthy animal smell.... I've smelled it before. So. Is he that wolf's son? Did he come for revenge?"

Choi Sang-woo reached out his hand towards Yoon Seo-jin's face.

,'That idiot!'

Choi Sang-woo was too excited. He hadn't even transformed into a werewolf like in the original story.

,'To transform into a werewolf, you need to be injected with werewolf serum once... Choi Sang-woo didn't get the werewolf serum at this time!'

Choi Sang-woo was a quarter, not even half a werewolf. He was closer to a human than a werewolf, so there was no way he could handle Yoon Seo-jin. Choi Sang-woo met Yoon Seo-jin too early in an immature state.

Yoon Seo-jin grabbed Choi Sang-woo's hand. Choi Sang-woo raised his leg. He tried to kick Yoon Seo-jin in the head, but Yoon Seo-jin used his ability faster. The power of vibration burst Choi Sang-woo's body as it was. Pieces of blood, flesh, and entrails fell to the floor all around.

,"The wolf smell is still disgusting."

It was a terrible and futile end, unbelievable as the protagonist's demise.

Yoon Seo-jin reached out his hand to me.

,"The fact that you're here means Kwak Soo-hyuk is dead, right. What a waste."

I could see the air trembling. A shockwave was coming. I raised my sword. I was planning to dodge the attack using Instant and then approach. But the plan was distorted.

Joo Seo-hyun, who was sitting down, aimed for Yoon Seo-jin's back. It was too hasty. Yoon Seo-jin's gaze was directed at me, but all his senses were directed at Joo Seo-hyun. Because the most dangerous person to him was Joo Seo-hyun.

Teop!

He grabbed Joo Seo-hyun's sword. Joo Seo-hyun's sword vibrated and broke too easily. The vibration then traveled up her right arm. Her right arm shattered.

At this rate, Joo Seo-hyun would die.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 0]

I used Instant twice in a row, instantly closing the distance with the bastard. Yoon Seo-jin turned away from Joo Seo-hyun and reached out his hand to me, grabbing my shoulder. My sword pierced the bastard's side.

Peong!

My shoulder exploded. No, not just my shoulder. The ribs in my chest shattered, and my heart was destroyed.

,"Hmm...."

Yoon Seo-jin groaned softly. He checked his injuries and immediately retreated, creating distance.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

I'm screwed.
