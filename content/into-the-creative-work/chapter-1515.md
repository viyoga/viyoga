I stared blankly at Milyeong. Milyeong had a serious expression on her face. Honestly, I didn't understand her.

,I know. Typically, there's the Divine Realm, the Underworld, the Phantasmal Beast Realm, and the Interdimensional Realm, right?

And ascending is an incredibly difficult thing to do.

,Each world has its own laws. These laws interlock to maintain the world. But Underworld magic is wandering in the middle? This could be seen as the Underworld trying to influence the Interdimensional Realm. Starting with this, in the worst-case scenario, the balance could be shaken, and the laws could change.

,Chaos will come to the Interdimensional Realm. A very great chaos.

,Is Muk Ji-ryeon spreading the Reverse Demon Divine Art in the Interdimensional Realm the will of the Underworld?

,...Hmm. So, you're saying we need to stop the Reverse Demon Divine Art from spreading?

,No.

,Then what should we do?

,Hmm. That's true. There's no way the Underworld's plan would be so sloppy... and the Divine Realm won't just stand by either. It's highly likely that it will end as a kind of happening.

,We just need to do what we have to do. Let's wrap things up and go. What are you going to do with that, the Reverse Demon Divine Art?

The Gwangmyeong Seungcheon-do (Bright Light Ascension Chart) that can enhance anything. It's an amazing thing after all.

Milyeong was happy. She put the Reverse Demon Divine Art into her own space created with space magic.
