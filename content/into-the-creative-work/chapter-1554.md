,Don't bother running away. We already control this area. There's nowhere for you to escape."

,Control it? I don't feel anything around here, though."

It wasn't a lie; I really didn't feel anything.

,That's your level. This surrounding space is practically in the palm of our hands. You seem to have decent sensory perception since you noticed us, but that's about it. You lack the insight to recognize magic. The media was hyping you up as a promising star, but in reality, you're just a mediocre B-grade."

Magic.

I frowned.

The magic of reality was different from the magic in the game world. I know that the magic in this world is also activated with mana, an energy. However, I don't know the exact principles. I have memories of being educated about it in university, but I didn't understand any of the principles of magic.

And the magic of reality has slightly different principles in each region. For example, there's Onmyōdō in neighboring Japan.

,Even though he said it's magic, there's a possibility it's not magic. It's foolish to believe the enemy's words without question."

I decided to think about the minor details later and focused on the situation at hand. As I stirred up my will to fight, they took their positions as if they had been waiting for it.

They're unsettling guys. Even though they're confident in their victory, they don't let their guard down at all.

,Did No Ji-su send you?"

,That woman isn't our master. We're just the execution team. The decision was made from above, so we're just following orders. Well, we're ready, so... just die."

In an instant, the space shook. The ground began to die, turning black, and black smoke enveloped me.

I looked at the guy furthest back among them. This phenomenon started with him. The flow of mana was telling me. The guy furthest back was wearing a hood and a mask. I focused my gaze a little more. He wasn't East Asian; he was Black.

,What kind of magic is this?"

,Do you know voodoo? It's magic from the African side."

Dung. Dung. Dung. Dung. Dung.

The sound of drums was heard from within the black smoke.

,You'll hear a slow rhythm. Sounds good, right? Comfortable? Just entrust your mind and body to the rhythm. You'll be able to feel the ultimate comfort."

,Ah, yeah.... It's comfortable. Too comfortable...."

I staggered to the sound of the drums. I noticed their tension easing as I staggered as if I were about to collapse at any moment. Carelessness. I don't miss that opening.

[Using an instant (moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Whirlwind Flash (Swift Wind, Rapid Thunder).

My body, wrapped in lightning, shot forward. The guys who had let their guard down were slow to react. Thanks to that, I reached the Black guy at the very back.

,I heard the drums well. You black bastard."

,You son of a bitch. That's racist... Keuaaaaack!"

My sword pierces his abdomen. I slowly raised the sword upward. This guy is as good as dead already. It's no fun if I kill him too quickly.

The sound of drums was no longer heard. The black smoke that had surrounded me also disappeared. However, the ground that had turned black did not come back to life. Before something wriggling from within the ground could rise to the surface, Hwaryeon Bido tore through his heart.

,Just die, you nigger."

The Black guy died. He didn't last as long as I thought. Isn't it basic to last at least 30 seconds even if your heart is destroyed?

,Eddie died...!"

,Goddamn it! Kill him!"

,This is revenge for Eddie!"

The enemies rush in. I leisurely drew my sword.

They couldn't even react to the combo of Whirlwind Flash and instant. It meant they were just a bunch of nobodies compared to how much they were posturing. I didn't think for a moment that I would lose to these guys.

[Using an instant (moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

I beheaded the enemy who was closest. The guy next to him was startled and used his ability. Three discs were created in the air. Sharp discs spinning around.

[Using an instant (moment). Remaining stacks: 4]

,No matter how I look at it, they look like shields. Is it an ability to control and summon shields?"

Rotating the circular shields seems to be a strategy to simultaneously attack and defend.

,If it were me, I would have focused on defense with one big shield."

I thrust Hwaryeon Bido into the slight gap between the three spinning discs.

Kakakakak!

The shield rotated and made contact with the sword spine of Hwaryeon Bido. A tremendous amount of sparks flew. It rotated at high speed like a cutting machine, but it couldn't cut Hwaryeon Bido.

,Do you think Hwaryeon Bido looks like a pushover? Even if it's cracked, it can block most attacks."

The tip of Hwaryeon Bido pierced his head. As he fell, the rotating shields disappeared as well.

,Captain! I'll hold him down! In the meantime...."

[Using an instant (moment). Remaining stacks: 3]

One guy suddenly appeared behind me and tried to restrain my body with both hands. That attempt was successful. In fact, I couldn't break free from him even though I used an instant.

,It's not a space teleportation ability... is it an ability to hide one's presence?"

I gave up on dodging. Instead, I held Hwaryeon Bido in reverse grip and stabbed my abdomen as if committing seppuku. Of course, I manipulated it so that my internal organs wouldn't be damaged. I've become skilled at this kind of thing from killing a lot and being killed a lot.

,Keueoeok?! He, he stabbed his own body? Th, this crazy bastard...!"

,I just have a hole in my abs, but you got stabbed in the heart. It's a total win-win trade. You agree? Oh, yeah, I agree~

I confirmed that he was dead and pulled out the sword. I said it calmly, but it didn't mean it didn't hurt. What's fortunate is that the pain feels less severe thanks to the endorphins secreted after engaging in combat.

,Well, I don't know if they're actually being secreted, though."

Two left.

The captain and the short guy.

The captain grits his teeth and rushes in. The knuckles he was wearing on his hand instantly grew larger, turning into a menacing weapon that could kill a person in an instant. There were even thorns growing on those large knuckles.

I pretended to rush at the captain, then changed direction.

[Using an instant (moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Flying Tiger (飛虎).

A momentary charge skill using a leap. I rushed at the short guy with the momentum of a tiger. He tried to take something out of the bag at his waist, but my sword beheaded him. It was as rough as if a tiger's claws had scratched him.

Tuk.

The dying guy tapped my shoulder. A green mark spread across my shoulder, becoming a pattern.

,Is it something like magic? He did something before he died."

I feel the captain's presence approaching. His eyes, distorted with anger, were full of hatred.

,Goddamn it! You killed my subordinates?! I'll kill you and honor their souls!"

,You're too slow. At that speed... huh?"

I was using Whirlwind Flash. However, my body didn't move as fast as I thought it would. I couldn't avoid the guy rushing at me. I was so flustered that I forgot to use an instant for a moment. His fist hit my cheek.

My body flew backward and fell to the ground.

The damage was minimal. I used mana to intensively defend my face right before getting hit. At most, I just have a nosebleed.

,Ah, shit."

However, my concentration was broken at the moment I was hit, and Whirlwind Flash disappeared. I raised my upper body. The speed is sluggish. I could guess the cause.

,That midget bastard put something on my shoulder... that's the cause."

Kung!

The captain ran up and mounted my upper body. He took the mount position. He first snatched Hwaryeon Bido from my hand and threw it away.

,You're finished. Please don't die too quickly."

,...A guy getting on top of me. It feels really bad."

,I'll see how long you can keep that composure!"

Thunderbolt.

Pajijijijijijijijijijijik!

Thunderbolts burst from my body. The thunderbolts electrocuted him and soared into the sky. It was lightning rising from the ground to the sky.

,Keueueueueueue! This mere current...! Do you think I can't withstand it?!"

,You would have withstood it if it was an ordinary current."

The current thunderbolt was Noecheonryu's thunderbolt. A thunderbolt containing the energy of Noecheongyeol and my will. Soon, his pupils rolled back. He fainted.

I pushed him to the side and raised my body.

,Cough!"

It was the aftermath of internal injuries.

Was it because of the guys? No. It was because I used Whirlwind Flash excessively. In the [Gwangmyeongseungcheon-do] world, I could use it several times without any problems, but it wasn't the same for me in reality.

,My gi-hyeol is damaged. It's not a problem with my body, but a problem with my technique. It'll get better if I raise my Noecheonryu trait level further. Or I could just focus on training Whirlwind Flash."

No matter how hard I tried to train, I wasn't confident that I could perfectly handle Whirlwind Flash.

,And it's a matter of efficiency. It's faster to earn points and raise the Noecheonryu level."

There's no problem. I can just use Complete Recovery.

,Before using Complete Recovery, should I try using another technique?"

,How do I use Noecheonryu (雷天流) Simjeukgeom (心卽劍)...?

It's a technique where you infuse your will into the sword and swing it quickly.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

Because I have Bunguen Chakgol. I'm not confident that I can do as well as I did in the [Gwangmyeongseungcheon-do] world... but I should at least try.
