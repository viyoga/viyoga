No matter what, you might think I'm causing too much of a ruckus, but this prison in the boonies was special.

Being so far out in the boonies, it was a place where the Empire's reach didn't extend.

A place where criminals who were difficult to deal with for various reasons were shoved in.

One of the places where soldiers who displeased their superiors or caused problems were sent.

Lumen Prison was such a place.

A small kingdom.

A small kingdom maintained by its own laws. The prison warden was the king here.

The problem was that the king's power was unstable. It was only natural that the underlings would frequently stage rebellions. My actions just now were a kind of preemptive strike.

A warning that the new prison warden was a crazy bastard, so they better watch their step.

,I don't know if that warning will work well.'

I didn't really care if the higher-ups intervened or if they started a rebellion. I had no intention of seriously doing the prison warden's job anyway. I just needed to achieve my goal. The deal with Leon Schneider? We didn't even write up a contract. He's a dead man anyway. I'm not taking it seriously.

Jeobeok jeobeok.

I was led to the warden's office by one of the guards. Along the way, I looked around.

The prison was gloomy. The floors and ceilings were all covered in gray concrete. Mold was growing in the corners. It gave off a distinctly eerie feeling.

,Don't they clean properly?'

,W-well, we're short on manpower... W-we still do it regularly. And the building is quite old, so it only looks good on the outside.'

The guard's voice trembled. He had every reason to be nervous after I messed up the deputy warden like that.

,Only on the outside?'

,The entire prison is protected by defensive magic. Even though it looks like old concrete walls, its value is enormous. Only about 30 prisoners have ever escaped from our Lumen Prison!'

,So, 30 people have escaped.'

,W-well, I've heard that other prisons have far more...'

,.......'

I didn't know about other prisons. I changed the subject. I wasn't interested in the traditions or history of this prison.

,What's the current status of the prison?'

,We have a total of 150 guards and 1,574 inmates. Of these, 300 are female inmates.'

I already knew there were female inmates. Of course, they were managed in separate areas from the male inmates.

,There's more than a 10-to-1 difference between guards and inmates.'

,Yes. The situation is dire. But somehow, we're managing to keep it running.'

The guard sighed. Soon, the warden's office came into view. He opened the door for me and greeted me with a salute. I nodded once and entered the office.

,...It's cold.'

The warden's office was, to put it nicely, clean. With only a desk, a chair, and a bookshelf, it couldn't help but be clean.

,Anyone would think this is a prison cell.'

I rummaged through the bookshelf and drawers. There were documents containing information about the inmates.

,In this day and age, how can there not be a single computer in the office... Does that make sense?'

Looking closer, there were traces of a computer. Someone had removed it. Why? To keep information from the warden? Or was it a tacit message not to do anything?

I immediately left the warden's office. The guard, who was about to turn around, widened his eyes.

,There must be a place where the prison's administration is handled.'

,You mean the administration department?'

,Yes. Take me there.'

The guard swallowed hard and led me to the administration department. I opened the door and went inside. Guards in casual attire were sitting at their desks. There were nine people, three of whom were women. Not many of them were actually working.

The man sitting in the highest seat slowly rose from his chair. His large belly jiggled.

,Oh my. Are you the new warden? I've heard the stories. You were a famous combat alchemist from a noble family... What brings you here?'

,Are you the head of the administration department?'

,Yes. I am the head of the administration department.'

The middle-aged man in his 40s swaggered. The pig-like man had a sly smile on his face. He didn't seem to know what had happened to the deputy warden.

,You've gotten lax.'

,Excuse me?'

,Your military discipline has gotten lax. Head of the administration department. Where did you sell your rank and name?'

,Ah, aah. I'm Lieutenant Stefan Weissmuller. As you can see, I'm the head of the administration department.'

His expression twisted. He was deliberately hiding his expression. He was completely ignoring me. I looked around. The guards were watching Stefan's reaction, not mine. From a distance, it would look like Stefan was the warden.

,You're openly ignoring me like this... I can't stand it.'

I strode towards Stefan. Stefan seemed flustered and hesitated.

,W-warden? I may have made a mistake, but take it easy. There are laws here that are unique to this place.'

,Are they above imperial law? The military police would love to hear that. Should I go report it?'

,You know that's not what I meant. I know what kind of person you are, Warden, but this is Lumen Prison.'

,You know me? No, you don't.'

I raised my fist and punched him in the cheek.

Peok!

But Stefan didn't fall. On the contrary, his head didn't even shake. He frowned as if he was displeased and lowered his voice.

,What do you think you're doing?'

,I was educating an impudent lieutenant... Your body is tougher than it looks.'

Stefan smirked.

,It's a meager talent I possess. By the way, assaulting a subordinate is illegal. Oh dear, I can already picture the military police raiding Lumen Prison.'

,The lieutenant shows no sign of remorse.'

,I haven't done anything wrong. Why should I be remorseful?'

,The lieutenant lacks respect for his superior. Even just talking back like that is an insult to a superior and an act of insubordination.'

,What kind of bullshit is this...? Hah, I'll report this to the higher-ups, so be warned. Now that the greetings are over, please go back, Warden. Your place isn't here, it's in the warden's office.'

Tang!

I pulled out my pistol and shot Stefan in the right eye.

,Aaaaaaaagh!'

Stefan grabbed his right eye and collapsed.

,It seems that meager talent of yours doesn't protect your eyes.'

His eyes were also enhanced. Since they were enhanced, he didn't die, and it ended with his eyeball bursting. Stefan, who had been writhing in pain, regained his senses and jumped to his feet.

,You son of a bitch!!'

Tang!

,Aaaaaagh!'

I shot him in the remaining left eye as well. The guy who had lost both eyes collapsed on the floor and screamed.

[Strength]

I enhanced my strength with magic and kicked Stefan in the head. Ppeong! A refreshing sound rang out as Stefan's head hit the concrete floor. I kicked his head a few more times. Stefan's body went limp. He had fainted.

I looked around. The guards were startled. Their backs stiffened.

,You must be keeping the budget ledgers. Bring them to me right now.'

They looked at each other. Stefan, who could be called their leader, had been taken down, so they didn't know what to do.

,Are you going to disobey me like Stefan?'

,N-no! I'll prepare them right away! W-we should send the lieutenant to the infirmary...'

,Lieutenant Stefan is a soldier of the great Heist Empire. He won't die from just this.'

,.......'

,Just bring me the ledgers.'

,...Yes.'

After a minute, the budget ledgers were in my hands. Even though I look like this, I run a business. I knew how to read ledgers. Of course, Yuria-ssi taught me.

I widened my eyes as I looked at the ledgers. The budget allocated to the prison was more than I expected.

,It's quite large, so I guess it's natural. And...'

Something was a little strange. A lot of money was being spent on facility maintenance. But the prison facilities I saw on the way here were just dilapidated.

,Were they siphoning off the budget in this way?'

Well, that probably wasn't the only thing. They must have been siphoning off the budget in subtle ways that I didn't notice.

,The lieutenant has been tampering with the budget. Not a surprising thing. I'll give you a week. Bring me the siphoned-off budget in cash. I'll use those funds for good causes for the prison.'

I could get at least 300 million credits.

I was short on money these days because I was investing in my business, so this was perfect.

,Prison warden. This is a sweet gig.'

I looked at the guards. They all looked pale and terrified.

,W-warden-nim. I'm sorry to say this, but we've used all the budget. The remaining budget has already been allocated.'

,Ah, is that so? Should I report it to the military police? Or do you want to become eye cripples like the lieutenant?'

,.......'

,Was a week too long? Bring me the siphoned-off budget and put it on my desk within four days.'

,Four days...! That's impossible!'

,If you can't do it, the military police will come barging in. The inmates would love it if you were locked up in prison.'

,.......'

,It's exactly four days. Break your savings, take out a loan... If you don't want to see the military police barging in, bring me the siphoned-off budget.'

I cut off Stefan's arms and legs in front of them and threw him into the infirmary. Stefan. I couldn't kill him carelessly. He must have siphoned off a huge amount of the budget so far.

I sat down in the warden's office chair. I closed my eyes and pondered. I had caused a big accident as soon as I came to the prison. But I wasn't nervous. Right now, I was Leon Schneider, not Eugene Meyer. My real identity would not be revealed.

,I have the World Door. If it seems dangerous, I can just run away.'

As long as there was a door, I could use the World Door. As long as I had this, escaping was a piece of cake.

Knock knock knock.

I opened my eyes at the sound of knocking.

,Warden-nim. This is Captain Kevin.'

,Come in.'

Kevin came in. He was soaked to the bone from the rain.

,I've brought all the corpses and disposed of them with magic. The soldiers' corpses will be handed over to their families, and the attackers' corpses will be cremated.'

,They were the ones who attacked me. Did you identify them?'

,...I didn't have time to do that. I think they're from a gang or the mafia.'

,Find out exactly who they are and bring me the information. You can go now.'

But Kevin didn't leave right away.

,...I heard you turned the administration department upside down.'

,They were messing with the budget. I held back from executing them all.'

,......Why did you cut off Lieutenant Stefan's arms and legs?'

,I cut them off because there was a risk of resistance. But are you interrogating me now? With your rank of captain?'
