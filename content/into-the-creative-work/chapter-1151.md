『You are qualified to challenge the trial. Shall we begin the Trial of the Azure Dragon?』

,I accept the challenge.'

The streams of water gathered in one spot in the air, creating a door leading to a dungeon.

I looked at Ryu Hana. With determined eyes, Ryu Hana nodded. She grabbed my shoulder. As if waiting, the dungeon sucked Ryu Hana and I in.

『You have entered the special dungeon, ???.』

,Seoul is... a mess. Part of the city is collapsed. Aren't they restoring it?'

The atmosphere of the city, which should be the most vibrant on the Korean peninsula, had sunk coldly.

I asked someone nearby what year it was.

"Ryu Hana. You've already noticed, but this dungeon is also set in the past. Is there anywhere you want to go?"

Ryu Hana's head turned to one side. It was towards Seocho-dong.

"...No. There's nowhere I want to go."

"Then, how about we go to the Academy? The old Academy. Aren't you curious?"

My goal was Sung Hari. At this time, Sung Hari would have been at the Academy.

"I'm curious. What was the old Academy like? Changing this place won't change reality, but... we might learn some forgotten facts. Well, there's a good chance it's completely different in a parallel world."

"Okay. Let's go to the Academy."

"...Is it okay to do that?"

"This world has nothing to do with reality. People and things are all fake. Don't forget that."

"How are you going to start it?"

"She's a machine spirit. A rather special spirit."

Burururureung.
