[Crisis Detection activated. Remaining stacks: 7]

The world stopped.

No, my thought process sped up.

,Crisis Detection? I didn't know I had this ability!'

Looking at the notification window, my inventory comes to mind. When I used the inventory, a notification window appeared and explained it to me.

Perhaps the momentary and the inventory are essentially the same ability.

,I thought I had several different supernatural powers... Maybe the root of the supernatural power is one thing.'

I put aside the complicated thoughts.

The important thing now is the cause of the activated crisis detection.

,It must have been automatically activated by detecting danger as the word says. But I don't see any danger towards me.'

There's no way the crisis detection would have been activated by accident.

,Then one thing. There is an invisible danger.'

Since I can't see or feel it, it's impossible to avoid. So, the only way is to block it.

After calculating the formula, I put a load on the mana rod. Mana is instantly processed into a formula and creates magic.

[Blood Barrier]

A barrier made of blood surrounded me.

Kwaang!

An invisible shockwave struck the Blood Barrier. The Blood Barrier didn't last long and was torn apart as if crumpled. In the meantime, I ran away without looking back.

,It's not wind... Is it a psychic attack? Looking at the power, I would have suffered a fatal wound if I hadn't blocked it.'

Fortunately, there was no sign of the flesh tree monster chasing me. The guy with the tongue in his armpit that I saw when I entered the cave was crushed to death by a large rock. Aisa must have taken care of it.

Aisa was waiting for me at the entrance of the cave.

,You're late. Is it because you're a wizard? Aren't vampires basically more physically capable than humans?'

,The sun is still up. And I think you are more physically capable than I am.'

,The basics of a druid start with training the body to some extent. Wizards memorize math workbooks, right?'

,...Well. I'm a vampire, so I don't know.'

I wasn't a normal wizard. Since I was from the military, I trained my body more than the average person, but... I had no talent for physical abilities. All that improved was my stamina and reflexes. I just wasn't good at using my body in other ways.

,Anyway, it doesn't look like that monster will come out of the cave. What are we going to do now? Are we going back?'

,Aisa. Are you scared, by any chance? Of Eugene, the Body Collector?'

,My mission is to escort you. It's better to go back as soon as possible.'

I was thinking of taking Aisa back and then returning here. That monster of flesh and the uncivilized things that worship him are unpleasant and disgusting, but they piqued my interest. The fact that it's related to Alphatier is another reason. I want to screw over Alphatier somehow.

,Too bad. I have no intention of going back at all. I need to know what the truth is here.'

,I knew you'd say that. First, let's get out of here and go somewhere safe. ...I don't know if there is a safe place, though.'

,I know how to use a small barrier.'

• • •

We set up a barrier in a suitable place and took a break.

Looking at the sky, the sun was starting to set. The characteristics of a vampire are revealed. As night fell, my body began to feel energized. It was like being trapped in a stuffy place and then coming outside.

,Do you know what that flesh tree is?'

,Well. Isn't it a monster caused by genetic manipulation?'

,That wasn't an ordinary monster. You must have seen it too. My magic was interfered with and suppressed. That's not the kind of monster you can create simply through genetic manipulation. In the first place, genetic manipulation has already developed as much as it can. I don't think Alphatier would bother researching genetic manipulation now.'

,...I have one guess.'

,What is it?'

,A god from another world. There's a chance it's a god from another world. Ah, you know that other worlds exist, right?'

,I know that the world we live in is not everything, and that there are countless worlds.'

,In order for a god from another world to exist in our world, faith is needed. Faith becomes the cornerstone of existence.'

,It's too early to decide that he's a god from another world. We still don't have enough information.'

,That's right. The easiest way to get information is to ask someone who knows. Hmm. Actually, I want to do some reporting when it gets light... But that won't happen, will it?'

At that moment, a voice suddenly echoed from the sky.

,There's no need for that, Aisa.'

Aisa and I were startled and looked up at the sky where the voice came from.

There, a man wearing white cloth clothes was standing in the air. The man with red tattoos instead of hair looked down at us with sunken eyes.

,Rahul-sunbae-nim...! Why are you here, sunbae-nim?!'

,That's a foolish question, Aisa. You are the ones who came here, not me. Your barrier skills have improved compared to before... But they are still immature.'

Rahul raised his hand. The wind blew. The trees around him shook. The wind gathered in his hand.

,No, sunbae-nim. Wait a minute! Is this what you're going to do after seeing me for the first time in a while?!'

,I have cut off all ties. To transcend, you must cut off everything that binds you and be free.'

,Wow. You've become such a son of a bitch after seeing you for the first time in a while! If Master saw you, he wouldn't leave you alone, would he?'

,I told you. I have cut off all ties. Even if that person appears before me, I will not waver.'

,So why are you here?'

,Is there a need to explain the reason to those who will die soon? Now, die.'

Rahul drops a compressed sphere of wind to the ground.

A bomb, like a kind of missile, was falling. I stepped forward and took out a Dark Hole from my inventory.

There is no Dark Hole without a real black magician. Instead, I use black magic with the Dark Hole of another black magician. I could use the Dark Hole like a kind of external hard drive. Therefore, the Dark Hole is consumed the more I use it.

It doesn't matter. It's not very difficult to get Dark Holes with money.

Black mana flows out from the Rank 4 Dark Hole, which looks like a small black bead. I mixed the black mana with blood, which is the medium for blood magic. Blood magic and black magic were particularly compatible.

[Black Parade]

Black blood churned and appeared on the ground. The pooled black blood moved like waves, swallowing and absorbing the compressed wind that had fallen to the ground. Immediately after, it used all the absorbed power to recklessly soar and attack Rahul.

Rahul flew higher to avoid the black blood and moved both hands. The hand movements were slow, but there was precision.

,Is it a beast transformation? I can't recognize it because it's not magic.'

Dudeudeudeudeu.

The ground shakes.

Rahul, who finished the beast transformation, raised his hand. A huge hand-shaped rock rose from the ground and slammed down on the floor.

Kwaaaaaang!

It's not just a shockwave containing physical force. The mana and formula that made up the Black Parade shook and shattered.

The giant rock hand slams the ground again.

[Blood Barrier]

I blocked the impact with a blood barrier and took care of Aisa first. I grabbed her by the nape of her neck and stepped back.

Rocks that sprang from the ground flew at me.

[Blood Surfing]

Blood rose from under my feet and moved me quickly.

,First, I need to throw Aisa in a safe place and then have a showdown. I don't like that guy for some reason, like those crazy bastards, so I'm going to kill him no matter what.'

,What?'

,...If I kill your precious sunbae-nim, it will be safer.'

,How do you know?'

,Well, Rahul-sunbae-nim is good at clone magic. Why would the main body do something dangerous when he can use clones?'

Passing the risk on to the clone. That's the most ideal way to use a clone master.

,Even if you win now, it's not a win. Rather, you're just giving information to sunbae-nim. Running away is the best option here. I happen to have a way to avoid sunbae-nim's eyes. It's a bit of a waste, but I have to use it when I have to.'

Even if you win, it's not a win. Those words completely cut off my motivation.

But retreating like this didn't suit my nature.

Even if I fight him, I'll only give him information? What a joke. I'm not Eugene Meyer now, I'm Eugene. The information he has is only a part of what I have.

I threw Aisa back.

,Kyaaak?!'
