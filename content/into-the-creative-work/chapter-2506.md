「Grand Game Entry Ticket (Seats Remaining: 40) - 100 GP」

The remaining seats for the Grand Game started filling up quickly. It was clear that us buying the remaining seats had provoked the Devil's Game participants.

「Grand Game Entry Ticket (Seats Remaining: 41) - 100 GP」

,Huh? The number of seats increased?'

It didn't seem likely that the Devil's Game organizer would add more seats.

,Did an existing participant die?'

According to Yu Jin-myeong's words, the Devil's Game wasn't just happening in Korea, and the number of participants was at least in the thousands, if not tens of thousands.

,First of all, those bastards who bought the Grand Game entry tickets are no joke. Collecting 100 GP is hard enough. I shouldn't let my guard down.'

Before worrying about the Grand Game, I had to kill Paeng Cheon-su first. And that U Jeol-pa or whatever he's called.

After that, I met Ji Byeong-su, as the boss of the 'Seonghae' organization, together with Yu Jin-myeong.

As soon as Ji Byeong-su saw me, he immediately knelt on the floor and bowed his head.

"I greet the boss of Seonghae! Please accept me into the organization! I'll do anything...!"

I exchanged glances with Yu Jin-myeong. Yu Jin-myeong quietly nodded. We had finished discussing what to do with Ji Byeong-su.

"Ji Byeong-su. I'm very disappointed. I even gave you a gun, but you couldn't kill Paeng Cheon-su."

"The gun didn't work on Paeng Cheon-su! And their subordinates were monsters! With just one punch, my subordinates fell like autumn leaves! This is clearly strange!"

The gun didn't work? I almost shouted at him to stop talking nonsense, but I held back. Because a part of my mind thought it was possible.

,If they're close to Paeng Cheon-su, they must be Devil's Game participants.'

And I was bothered that the gun didn't work on Paeng Cheon-su.

,Paeng Cheon-su's ability is mind-reading. The game tool he has is a game-exclusive recovery potion. The fact that the gun didn't work must be his subordinate's ability.'

Should I use a tip?

I hesitated for a moment. Because using a tip on Paeng Cheon-su's subordinates felt like a waste. In the current situation, each tip is precious.

,Why didn't firearms work on Paeng Cheon-su?'

&lt;It is Paeng Cheon-su's subordinate, Hyeon Ji-chan's ability. The ability is firearm prohibition. Once a day, firearms cannot be used within 300m for 10 minutes.&gt;

I had heard that name, Hyeon Ji-chan, from Yu Jin-myeong.

Paeng Cheon-su's closest aide, and a legendary gangster who once crushed three organizations with just his fists.

"What's this about Paeng Cheon-su's subordinate being a monster?"

"They ate some strange medicine and became as strong as monsters! Maybe it's an ability or tool they got from the Devil's Game...."

Well, I think so too. What I was curious about was something else.

"How strong are they?"

"With one punch, a person flew nearly 5m. It was like a car's power."

"Was the speed like a car too?"

"Ah, no. The speed was normal."

If the speed was superhumanly fast too, I wouldn't have been able to run away from that spot.

"What about durability? There's no way you fought without any tools... Did knives or hammers not work?"

"The attacks did work. However... even when stabbed with a knife, they acted as if they didn't feel any pain at all."

Resistance to pain and monstrous strength?

I imagine the enemy in my head. Fighting 1-on-1 under the assumption that I can't use a gun. The opponent is a monster who can send an adult man flying more than 5m with one punch. In my hand is a long knife.

,I think I can win?'

In short, wouldn't it be possible to just slice them up before they attack? After running a simulation in my head, it was definitely possible.

"Ji Byeong-su. Do you know about Gang U-jeol?"

"Are you talking about that hand cripple?"

Gang U-jeol was called a hand cripple because he only had three fingers on his left hand. Of course, the only person who could openly call him a hand cripple to his face was Paeng Cheon-su.

"I heard Paeng Cheon-su is using that bastard."

"That bastard is hard for even Paeng Cheon-su to control. Since I'm gone, he's planning to use him as an acting leader in a hurry and then discard him. That hand cripple is a real loose cannon, even if he's bad at everything else. He even kidnapped a detective's family once. We almost got fucked because of him. He's a guy who lives like there's no tomorrow."

I killed two of Gang U-jeol's subordinates this afternoon and dumped them in the sea. He's probably starting to realize that his subordinates are dead.

,If he's the type to kidnap a detective's family... he might target Do-hyeon. I have to kill him today.'

I won't tolerate this, even if Yu Jin-myeong tries to stop me.

"Do you know where that bastard is now?"

"That bastard isn't even afraid of the police. He's probably drinking and selling drugs at the club he runs."

"Take me there."

"A-are you accepting me into the organization?"

"I'll accept you if you guide me properly."

I was originally going to pretend to accept Ji Byeong-su into the organization. The information Ji Byeong-su has is helpful enough to us. However, attacking Gang U-jeol now was my own decision.

Yu Jin-myeong didn't argue and headed to the car.

"I prepared a weapon for the boss, just in case."

"Did you predict this situation too?"

"That's not it."

Yu Jin-myeong opened the car trunk. There was a luxurious, long box inside. When I opened the box, there was a Japanese sword.

"How did you get this?"

"I got it through a broker. It was easier than getting a gun. Do you not like it?"

I pulled the Japanese sword from its matte black scabbard. The flawless blade emitted an eerie aura.

It's not as good as the red straight sword I saw last time, but it's a pretty decent item.

I swung the sword a few times in the air and then nodded.

"I like it. You got a good one."

From now on, I'm going to go to the club where Gang U-jeol is and kill Gang U-jeol and all his subordinates. I'm going to massacre at least 10 people. Gang U-jeol is a loose cannon? I'm going to be even more of a loose cannon than him. That's how it will be a warning to Paeng Cheon-su.

I looked at the blade and calmed my trembling heart. Even if it's just one or two people, killing more than ten people makes me a little nervous.

We made a simple plan. What's more difficult than killing Gang U-jeol is the escape plan.

Ji Byeong-su twisted his lips after hearing the escape plan.

"The Korean police? Those bastards are so incompetent. Even if you're wanted, you can just play a round of the Devil's Game and come out. The wanted status will all disappear."

"The police don't know about the Devil's Game?"

"They don't. Paeng Cheon-su says that even if the police find out about the Devil's Game, they immediately forget about it. There are also hardly any cases of the police participating in the Devil's Game. But are we really doing this with just the three of us? Gang U-jeol... he's not an easy guy."

"Don't want to do it?"

I fiddled with the pistol in my pocket. Ji Byeong-su quickly shook his head.

"No. I will be with the boss. My life is now the boss's."

I didn't believe Ji Byeong-su's lip service loyalty at all. I'll kill him as soon as he becomes useless.

On the way, I bought a suitable mask. I did put on makeup, but there was a risk of being recognized.

Incheon's famous club. We headed to the entrance.

There was a bouncer with a large build at the front entrance. His eyes were fierce and he was wearing a suit. He was dressed like a security guard, but the atmosphere was that of a gangster.

"Cosplay? Not just anyone can enter here. First, take off your mask."

I placed my hand on the sword hilt hanging on my waist in front of him. The bastard was nonchalant, as if he didn't think it was a real sword at all. His eyes were filled with annoyance.

"Breath of Thunder. Thunderclap and Flash."

I draw the sword as I please.

The blade flashed and grazed the bouncer's neck. The bouncer's head separated from his body and fell to the floor. His body also collapsed belatedly.

"This is crazy.... A person's head can be cut off so easily."

Ignoring the admiring Ji Byeong-su, I entered the club.

Loud music sounds. No, it would be more accurate to say it's beating.

Young men and women are mixed together on the stage, busy dancing. As I was looking around with curious eyes, a man in a suit approached us. Wearing a suit here? He was most likely a gangster belonging to U Jeol-pa.

"You guys! What's with those outfits?! How did you get in here?!"

His shouting was quite threatening.

Before I could step forward, Yu Jin-myeong moved. He held a pistol, aimed it at the man's head, and pulled the trigger.

Taang!

The gunshot pierced through the loud music. The body of the man with a bullet in his head collapsed to the floor. The eyes of everyone in the club turned to us.

The music was still exciting, but people were no longer excited.

"Kyaaaaaa!"

With a piercing scream, people ran out through the entrance and emergency exits. We didn't bother catching them.

"Where is Gang U-jeol?"

"This way!"

Ji Byeong-su pointed to where the VIP rooms were located. Men in suits ran out from that side. Each of them had tools in their hands.

"What crazy bastard!"

"Where do you think you are?!"

"Where did you come from?!"

They shouted at the top of their lungs and charged at me. I ran towards them.

Super Focus is activated. Time slows down and their faces and bodies come into view. The direction of the swinging tools, the movement of the leg muscles with force, and the direction of the pupils.

I see everything.

At the same time, I knew how I had to move.

First, I slit the throat of the guy in front, and then I cut the torso of the guy next to him. A baseball bat narrowly brushed past the side of my head. Of course, everything is as calculated.

I take another breath and swing the blade from bottom to top. One more guy dies and blood is splattered.

There are 5 more left.

,Forward. If I lose this momentum, I'll die.'

Super Focus is actually a hindrance in the current situation. The flow is interrupted. Also, even if I use Super Focus, I can't breathe anyway.

I entrusted my body to the loud music echoing in the club. And I swung my sword as if dancing, slicing up the remaining five.

Only then did I exhale and shake off the blood on the sword. My heated body slowly cools down.

The hallway connected to the VIP room was a mess of blood and corpses.

,Did I do this? It's thrilling.'

I belatedly examined the blade. The condition of the blade wasn't very good. Still, I could probably cut down a few more people.

"This is crazy.... How many people with just one sword...."

"Ji Byeong-su. Guide me."

"Yes, yes. Boss."

Ji Byeong-su went ahead. He headed to the management office, not the VIP room. It's a steel door. You wouldn't be able to break through it even if you shot it.

"Boss. Step back a little. They're probably preparing a welcome inside."

Ji Byeong-su turned the doorknob with a cautious expression. Fortunately, it wasn't locked. Ji Byeong-su pushed the door open and quickly stuck to the wall for cover.

Tangtangtangtangtangtangtang!

Gunshots keep going off.

If I go into that room now, I'll definitely get shot at the entrance and die.

,What should I do? Should I bring a table and use it as a shield?'

Yu Jin-myeong picked up a corpse and threw it into the management office.

Tangtangtangtangtangtang!

More gunshots rang out and bullets hit the corpse.

Yu Jin-myeong continues to pick up corpses. It seems like he's trying to use up the bullets in the same way. Even if that's not the case, using a moderately thick corpse as a shield didn't seem like a bad idea. It seems like the bullets are small caliber and can't penetrate the corpse.

,Since I seem to have time, should I roll the dice?'

I took out the dice from my pocket. Seeing that the warning from the tip isn't appearing, it seems like the worst-case scenario won't happen.

Degu-ruru.

The number on the dice is 5.

"Ugh! Cockroach!"

Tang!

"You fucker! What are you using the gun for?!"

"Ack! Cockroach! Ack! It's flying this way! Someone get rid of it!"

The inside of the management office quickly becomes chaotic. I took the gun and headed to the entrance of the management office. I didn't pick up the dice. I experimented and it reappears next to me at midnight.

Super Focus is activated. I checked the inside.

Gang U-jeol and three men who appeared to be his subordinates. Two naked women were passed out, drugged. I also saw a cockroach. Not lying, a cockroach bigger than my palm was flying through the air.

The guys who were panicking aimed their pistols at me. But I, who was prepared, was faster.

I put bullets in the foreheads of the three men, one bullet in Gang U-jeol's right hand holding the pistol, and one bullet in the flying giant cockroach.

Tangtangtangtangtang!

The situation is resolved in an instant. I approached Gang U-jeol and aimed my sword instead of the pistol.

"Are you a killer? Where did you come from? Who ordered you?"

Gang U-jeol growled.

"You bastard. You still haven't come to your senses. You're about to die."

"How much did you agree to get paid? I'll give you three times that amount. Join my side. A skilled killer like you is welcome! Someday, I'll even hand over my business to you!"

"You still have some pride, so you won't just beg for your life? I would have spared you if you had begged for your life."

"...Spare me."

"Did you believe that, you idiot."

I swung my sword and cut off his head.

I felt the sword get slightly caught in the middle. The blade seems to be damaged after all.

I turned around and said to Yu Jin-myeong and Ji Byeong-su.

"Let's get out of here before the cops come."

There was no flashy road chase with the police. We abandoned the car in a suitable place and ran away. It was successful.
