Gwangroe, who had gone out to subjugate the Lightning Tiger Dragon, returned to Gigantria.

People gathered at the entrance of the association headquarters. Not to welcome Gwangroe, but to see the corpse of the Ancient Dragon-class dragon, the Lightning Tiger Dragon.

It was already known that Gwangroe had spatial storage abilities and kept the corpses of the dragons he hunted there. However, Gwangroe mostly shows off the dragon corpses once at the association headquarters. To be exact, he's showing off his power and achievements. Even though Gwangroe is now an S-class Hunter.

Still, the people gathered in front of the association headquarters were anticipating it.

,Gwangroe and the Lightning Tiger Dragon. Did Gwangroe's lightning abilities work on the Lightning Tiger Dragon too?

,Even if it worked, it wouldn't have been that effective, right? If you compare the abilities of humans and dragons, dragons are ultimately superior. After all, the source of the power that Hunters have is dragons.

Hunters exist because dragons exist. That was the common sense of this world.

A large carriage pulled by six horses enters the main street. It wouldn't be strange to call it a moving house. Did the heads of state that existed during the Great War ride around in carriages like that? People admired the carriage incessantly. The merchants and workers around the carriage didn't really catch their eye.

Mir was among the people. Mir looked around the carriage, not at the carriage itself. Hiteira wasn't there. Eventually, Mir's gaze turned to the carriage.

,Gwangroe and Hiteira are probably riding in the same carriage. No matter how strong Gwangroe is, he can't ignore a mage of Hiteira's level. That's for sure. Nothing else happened.

The carriage went out and came back in a week. He deliberately ignored the fact that Hiteira and Gwangroe had been together for that week. The Hiteira that Mir knows isn't very interested in men.

Mir narrowed his eyes and looked at the carriage window. The inside is pitch black. It doesn't feel like the curtains are drawn. It's just pitch black.

,Did they put something like a black cloth on the window?

Mir, who was ignorant of magic, couldn't know that it was a magical effect at all.

The carriage stops. The merchants and workers push the surrounding people back and create space. It felt like a king was making an appearance.

,Move aside! Gwangroe-nim will show you the corpse of the Lightning Tiger Dragon for you all!

,Don't come close! I'm warning you in advance, only look at the corpse of the Lightning Tiger Dragon with your eyes! If you touch the corpse of the Lightning Tiger Dragon, I'll report you to the association! Gwangroe-nim won't stand still either!

,Don't forget that the owner of the Lightning Tiger Dragon's corpse is Gwangroe-nim!!

The merchants were screaming.

The spectators nodded and understood. A dragon's corpse is like a treasure. And that's even an Ancient Dragon-class dragon? Just showing it is more than enough.

When the space was secured, the carriage door opened. Strangely, I couldn't see inside the door. The spectators didn't pay much attention. Because it was Gwangroe who came out of the carriage. Gwangroe looked around and then gestured to the empty space.

The void splits and the corpse of the Lightning Tiger Dragon falls out with a thud. It had already been nicely prepared, the internal organs discarded, and only the skin, flesh, and bones remained. People cheered at just that.

,Oooooooooh! Is that the Lightning Tiger Dragon! It definitely looks more like a tiger than a dragon!

,Look at that terrifying head. Just looking at it makes my legs tremble.

,The Lightning Tiger Dragon's heart? Aren't they going to show the dragon heart?

,Do you think they'd show a treasure worth at least 50 billion Skel in a place like this? And that Gwangroe-nim? Forget about it.

Mir swallowed hard as he looked at the corpse of the Lightning Tiger Dragon. The force itself was different from the White Strong Dragon he had hunted a while ago. It was only natural. The White Strong Dragon was weak even among Adult Dragons, and the Lightning Tiger Dragon was an Ancient Dragon-class that could destroy a city.

In the original manga, Mir feels a sense of rivalry when he sees the Lightning Tiger Dragon. But now, unlike the original, Mir felt fear. Since coming to Gigantria, his growth has slowed, and everything he does hasn't been working out. The Eye Wolf Clan is close to being unknown, so there's no sign of new members joining, and the relationship between his colleagues, Hiteira and Jina, feels like it's getting more awkward as time goes on.

,Hiteira. Hiteira is...?!

Hiteira came out of the carriage a little late. A typical mage wearing a robe and holding a staff. The blonde, blue-eyed beauty mage got out of the carriage as expressionless as ever. Even when men's eyes are on her, her pupils don't waver an inch.

Mir was relieved by her usual appearance.

,Hoo. I guess nothing happened after all. Even if I worked with Gwangroe-nim, I wouldn't leave the clan. Because I have a promise with Hiteira and me. So I have to get stronger. Even for that promise.

Mir smiled and waved at Hiteira as he approached her. Hiteira made eye contact with Mir and nodded. Nothing had changed.

-I heard a report that Gwangroe is frequently contacting the Wolf Eye's clan members.

He deliberately ignored Baek Jeongsu's words that flashed through his head. He can just ask Hiteira and Jina later. Then he can prove that Baek Jeongsu's words were wrong. Mir ignored the anxiety that had filled a corner of his heart.

,Hiteira! Welcome back! How was the Lightning Tiger Dragon subjugation?

,It went smoother than I thought. Has there been any trouble in the clan?

,Ah. Of course not. Nothing happened. So little that it's depressing. I hunted a few dragons while you were gone. I also learned swordsmanship from the Chairman-nim.

,I'm glad you're doing well. Is Jina... in the clan house?

,She went to train. I heard you were coming today... it seems like training is urgent. Don't be angry.

,I've never been angry. I'll meet Jina at the clan house later, so it's right for Jina to focus on training. Mir doesn't need to come out to meet me.

,It's only natural for the clan leader to welcome the clan members.

,Yes. Let's go back to the clan house. I have something to give to Mir.

,To me? What is it?

Hiteira looked around. There were too many people. And there are those who are listening to this side without knowing it.

,...I'll tell you at the clan house.

Mir and Hiteira returned to the clan house.

,How is it? Our house. You've been out for a week, so you missed home, right?

,...Yes. I missed it.

Hiteira told a white lie. The current Mir is still immature both physically and mentally. There's no need to make him feel uneasy.

While in the Lightning Tiger Dragon subjugation team, she never thought of the clan house even once. She didn't have time for that. She had to be held and teased in Seong Yujin's arms. When she came back, she mostly only had anal sex. She didn't even think of Mir more than 10 times.

When they entered the lobby on the first floor, Jina was there. She was sitting on a chair with her legs crossed. There's no Babar tribe traditional makeup. Instead, she wore seductive makeup.

Hiteira tilted her head. Was it just her imagination? The atmosphere she felt from her also felt very different from before.

,Hiteira. You're back?

,Yes. I'm back. Jina. Weren't you going to train?

,It's the day you come back. I finished training early today.

Hiteira nodded. It didn't matter if Jina was here. Jina already knew her plan.

,Mir.

Hiteira, who called Mir, waved her hand in the air. The space split and the Lightning Tiger Dragon's heart fell into her hand with a thud.

,Hiteira? Th, that's....

Mir widened his eyes. His pupils were fixed on the heart and didn't move. That heart was telling him. Hurry up and chew me up.

,It's the heart of the Lightning Tiger Dragon. I know that Mir has compatibility with this dragon heart. Eat the heart of the Lightning Tiger Dragon.

Mir resisted the urge to rush at the heart. If he ate that, he would definitely become stronger. He would be able to jump to an A-class Dragon Hunter. But.

,...Why do you have that? Gwangroe-nim, who is obsessed with dragon hearts and dragon heart blood, has no reason to give you the Lightning Tiger Dragon's heart for no reason!

Even if it was another Hunter and not Gwangroe, it would be the same. If it's an Ancient Dragon-class dragon heart, it wouldn't be strange for internal strife to occur even among the same clan members over the treasure. Giving that to an outsider? Not only Hunters but also ordinary people wouldn't understand.

,I was more active than Gwangroe-nim in the battle with the Lightning Tiger Dragon. The criteria for Hunters to share dragon by-products is usually based on contribution. I received the dragon heart according to my contribution to the battle.

Contribution to the battle. He could understand that to some extent. Hiteira is a mage who is different from ordinary mages. She would be able to do things that an S-class Hunter couldn't do.

Mir glanced at the Lightning Tiger Dragon's heart. His instincts were constantly drawn to it due to compatibility. If he took that, he could become incredibly strong.

,...That's yours. An Ancient Dragon-class dragon heart would be a great help to you too.

,Mir has compatibility with this dragon heart. It's more efficient for Mir to eat it. More than anything, Mir, you haven't forgotten the promise you made with me, have you?

,Of course I haven't forgotten! Damn it, I understand! I'll eat it! But I'll definitely repay this debt! Definitely!

,That's enough. Eat it in a safe place.

,...I'll eat this and go to the association for a bit. I think I'll need some advice. The Chairman-nim will definitely give me the advice I need.

Mir ate the heart of the Lightning Tiger Dragon.

Pajijijik.

Bright yellow electricity sparked from Mir's body.

Mir became stronger in an instant. Magic power, physical ability. All of that rises one step. He smiled as he felt the electricity flowing through his body.

,I'm going to the association.

,Yes. Come back safely.

,Yeah. See you later.

Mir left the clan house with a happy expression.
