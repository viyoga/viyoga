Having earned a large sum of coins, I headed to the 5th floor's public area with Yuria and Elena.

The 5th floor's public area is the Tower's official shopping district.

Rat-kin merchants employed by the Tower sell various goods. They only accept coins as currency.

It's one of the busiest public areas in the Tower, as Hunters from all dimensions gather here.

Originally, Yuria and Elena would have attracted attention. Their beauty was exceptionally outstanding. However, thanks to Elena's illusion magic, they didn't draw any gazes.

,It's perception interference. Unless we speak to them first, they'll perceive us as part of the background. Fortunately, there doesn't seem to be anyone here strong enough to ignore my illusion magic."

"There are quite a few A-rank Hunters around, though? Why don't they notice?"

"It's not illusion magic that directly interferes with the mind. It's closer to subtly nudging the subconscious. Unless they have resistance to illusion magic, that is. They haven't even prepared a mental barrier, let alone prepared for illusion magic."

It meant that the Hunters in this world were particularly vulnerable to mental attacks. Come to think of it, abilities related to the mind didn't appear much in the original 'Martial Arts Tower' either.

"Thanks to you, we can shop comfortably. Tell me if you both need anything. I'll buy it for you."

"I'm alright, Master-nim."

"Hmm. The quality of the items here isn't very good."

Most of them are normal or magic-grade items. Our standards were too high to be satisfied with magic-grade items.

We entered 'Lufil's General Store,' a shop that also appeared in the original story.

"Jjiik.... Welcome. Jjijjik."

A squeaking rat-kin greeted us. It was a 130cm tall male rat-kin with a rat's head and tail. He was characterized by his gray fur and a mechanical right eye.

"You're new customers. Jjik. I'm Lufil. I buy everything I can buy, and I sell everything I can sell. Jjijijijik."

The rat-kin Lufil smiled slyly. I decided to take the initiative first.

"War Merchant Lufil. Am I right?"

"Jjiik?! How did you know?!!"

The rat-kin Lufil jumped in surprise and subtly moved towards the counter. There was something like an emergency call button under the counter.

"The Tower told me."

The merchants in the 5th floor's public area are all contracted with the Tower. However, not all merchants trade the same items. Each merchant handles different items.

"Jjiik.... It's hard to believe, but since you've come to find me, I can't not believe it. What do you want?"

"Give me a catalog. Not the general goods, but the real catalog that a war merchant handles."

"Jjik.... Since it's the customer's request, I have no choice."

Lufil handed over the catalog with a reluctant expression. It's amazing how his expressions are as diverse as a human's, even though his head is that of a rat.

I glanced at Elena as if I was examining the catalog. Elena shook her head slightly.

'As expected, mental domination isn't possible. It must be because he's a merchant contracted with the Tower.'

Unfortunately, it was impossible to dominate Lufil's mind and exploit him for everything.

"Do you also buy gold or jewels?"

"I buy gold and jewels recognized by the Tower. Jjik."

"Too bad."

I looked through the catalog with them. It was full of expensive items, with a minimum unit of 100,000 coins.

Lufil hid his identity even from the original protagonist, only revealing it directly after the protagonist reached around the 20th floor. It was because he had become somewhat close to the protagonist.

I found the item I wanted in the catalog.

『Attack Detector

Grade: Unique

Detects when attacked by a special item and identifies the attacker.

Price: 50,000 Coins per day』

"I'll buy the attack detector."

"This is buying a kind of right. 50,000 coins will be automatically paid every day, and you can give up the right. If you sign this document, the purchase is complete."

It's called an attack detector, but it has no form. If attacked by a special item, the system detects it and informs you.

In terms of efficiency, it's not good. It's not like I'm attacked every day.

'I've drawn a bit too much aggro.'

The important thing isn't detecting the attack in advance. It's being able to know who's attacking me.

'I'll be sure to retaliate.'

"I, I'll buy them! But I'm a little troubled by items with, with aftereffects! So I can't just buy them at a high price!"

Even if they were from the same world, so what? My identity will be revealed? Everyone who already knows my identity already knows.

As soon as Seong Yujin left, Lufil closed the store door.

Inside his general store, there were plenty of food, daily necessities, and luxury goods. Things that are easy to obtain outside the Tower, but difficult to obtain inside the Tower.

Lufil took out a space bundle and put the items in it. Not all of the items fit into the space bundle, which was a privilege of the Tower merchants. It's okay. He could go and sell more after coming back.

'I can finally do something worthy of a merchant. Jjik!'

Lufil didn't want to become a war merchant. It was because that was the only merchant position left, so he reluctantly contracted with the Tower. It was dangerous to his life if it became known that he was a war merchant, but the Tower merchant's space bundle and the right to move around the public areas were worth it.

'When that human found out that I was a war merchant, I thought my heart was going to drop! But what do you know! That person was my light! Jjijijijik!'

Lufil, having packed his things, headed to the 82nd floor.

The higher the floor's public area, the more gullible they are. It can't be helped, because the more coins they earn, the stronger they are, the more they reside on the higher floors.
