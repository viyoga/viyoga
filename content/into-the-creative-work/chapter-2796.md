There were forces that were displeased with the winning streak of Thunderclap General Seong Yujin-ssi. Quite a few, actually. Envy always follows those who are doing well, after all.

Normally, they would have sent assassins, poisoned him, or used political power to keep Seong Yujin-ssi in check. But right now, Seong Yujin-ssi's backing was terrifying. The prestigious Namgung Clan, one of the top martial arts families, and the Cheonma Shingyo, the heart of the Demonic Path. Those two alone were burdensome, but he even had the Emperor as his backer.

If they messed with Seong Yujin-ssi, not only themselves but their entire family would be annihilated. So, usually, they couldn't touch Seong Yujin-ssi. But there were those who couldn't leave Seong Yujin-ssi alone, even knowing the danger.

The Murim Alliance was one of them.

It was a coalition of the Eight Great Sects, the representatives of the orthodox Murim world, excluding the destroyed Hwasan Sect and the Beggars' Sect, which was destroyed by the Emperor. They saw Seong Yujin-ssi as an extremely dangerous figure.

However, opinions were divided within the Murim Alliance.

The current Murim Alliance Leader, Supreme Celestial Lord Gong Baekseung, said to the Murim Alliance elders.

,I know your concerns well. You're saying we need to do something about Little Cheonma Seong Yujin-ssi before he gets any bigger? Seong Yujin-ssi is already out of our hands. You've all heard the news that he has reached the realm of Three Stars and 9th Level, haven't you?"

,The problem is Little Cheonma's talent! If he continues like this, Little Cheonma Seong Yujin-ssi will become the Cheonma! That Seong Yujin-ssi is now corrupted by demonic energy, killing people, monsters, and spirit creatures alike every single day! If such a Seong Yujin-ssi becomes the Cheonma? The Jianghu will face a bloodbath like never before!"

Heo Seong, the White Radiance Adept from the Cheongseong Sect, said passionately. Most of the Murim Alliance nodded in agreement.

In their eyes, Seong Yujin-ssi seemed to be driven mad by the side effects of the Cheonma Shingong. The most common side effect of demonic martial arts was slaughter. The way he was senselessly killing made it impossible to deny that he was driven mad by the side effects of the Demonic Art.

If a guy like this becomes the Cheonma, the master of the Cheonma Shingyo? The future was as clear as day.

,I have met him in person. He was a bit of a smartass... but he didn't seem like he would be harmful unless provoked first. He didn't seem interested in the Murim world. He was more sincere about his own training. Besides, he became a general with the Emperor's favor. There's no reason for him to show his teeth to the Murim Alliance. The recent killings he's been doing aren't a big deal if you look closely."

Orthodox martial artists also killed spirit creatures to obtain their inner cores and byproducts.

Killing monsters? That was obviously a given.

In the case of killing humans, most of them were death row inmates. If he had truly gone insane due to the side effects of the Demonic Art, he would have rampaged without distinguishing between people's identities.

,He has risen to a position where he is not an exaggeration to say that he is second only to one and above all others as the Thunderclap General. I don't think he would have much interest in the Murim world. You are too immersed in the Murim world. The Jianghu may seem vast, but it is ultimately only a part of the world."

,...."

An uncomfortable silence descended.

They also knew that both the Murim Alliance and the Cheonma Shingyo were ultimately only a part of the world.

The center of the current world was the Emperor.

If the Emperor so desired, the Murim world would disappear.

,...Wouldn't it be possible to somehow change the Emperor's favor towards Seong Yujin-ssi?"

,Do you know why the Emperor favors Seong Yujin-ssi? No one knows except for the Emperor and Seong Yujin-ssi himself. What method will you use to change the Emperor's mind? You know without me having to say it, but even a small mistake in regards to the Emperor can become irreversible."

,...."

,The best we can do is maintain the status quo."

Kung!

The conference room door opened, and an outsider entered. It was a neatly dressed, white-haired old man holding a bamboo staff. He confidently walked in, fluttering his beard, which he had grown like a Taoist immortal.

Gong Baekseung was startled. On the other hand, the elders were calm, as if they had known in advance.

,Chwiseongae...! You, you were safe!"

Chwiseongae Wang Jiyuk. He was the former leader of the Beggars' Sect.

In fact, the current Beggars' Sect had been destroyed, but there was a force that had not been destroyed.

,Supreme Celestial Lord. You are wrong. Maintaining the status quo is not the best option, but the worst. The Cheonma Shingyo destroyed the Hwasan Sect, and the Beggars' Sect disappeared due to Seong Yujin-ssi's advice."

The Beggars' Sect is a sect of beggars.

But Seong Yujin-ssi wanted the beggars to die, and the Emperor accepted this, and the beggars disappeared from the Empire.

The Emperor did not cause a beggar massacre. The Emperor was not incompetent. He caught the beggars and made them not beggars. This meant that he provided them with food, clothing, and shelter and made them work. What about young children who had difficulty working? He solved the problem by creating numerous orphanages.

The Emperor, who lacked nothing in terms of power, authority, and wealth, was almost omnipotent within the Empire.

The beggars of the Beggars' Sect did not die. But their identity was completely torn away. Now, only a few hundred people, including the leader Chwiseongae, were carrying on the legacy of the Beggars' Sect. Even they could not act as beggars.

,I feel a little bad saying this, but you look better than before. Even your clothes seem better than mine."

,It can't be helped. If I go around looking like a beggar, the soldiers of the Imperial Palace come and yell at me. If I resist to the end, I will die. I worked for more than ten years, creating a sect in a quiet city to preserve the martial arts of the Beggars' Sect! It was a humiliating time as the leader of the Beggars' Sect...!"

,Hmm."

Was it really humiliating?

His face was gleaming with oil, though. He seemed to be eating and living too well. What was even more surprising was that he had reached the 3rd level even after achieving the realm of Harmonious Unity.

I suspected that he had given up his beggar life and gained great enlightenment.

,As a friend, I advise you. Seong Yujin-ssi must be punished for the sake of the Murim world. If you don't... the Eight Great Sects will be destroyed, just like the Beggars' Sect was."

Gong Baekseung and Wang Jiyuk were comrades and friends who had traveled the Jianghu together. At that time, they could trust each other's backs. Now, after so many years....

Gong Baekseung sighed.

Gong Baekseung had not only power but also a sense of politics as the Murim Alliance Leader.

The sudden appearance of Wang Jiyuk of the Harmonious Unity realm, and the Murim Alliance elders who remained silent about his intrusion. The answer came from just looking at these things.

,...I don't think you'll listen even if I repeat myself several times. I will step down from the position of Murim Alliance Leader. And it seems that the Wudang Sect cannot be with the Murim Alliance, so I will withdraw."

,Alliance Leader, no, Supreme Celestial Lord! Is this the opinion of the entire Wudang Sect? No matter how much it is you, representing the entire Wudang Sect is...."

The elders tried to stop him. The Wudang Sect was the best among the Eight Great Sects in terms of size, along with Shaolin. "North: Supreme Shaolin, South: Revered Wudang" was a phrase that even ordinary people casually uttered.

,I will persuade the sect leader. In the end, the Wudang Sect's withdrawal from the Murim Alliance is a foregone conclusion."

Gong Baekseung stepped down from the Alliance Leader's seat. He walked and left the Murim Alliance. His footsteps as he walked out of the shining gate seemed somehow light.

Wang Jiyuk sat in the Alliance Leader's seat as if it were natural. The elders did not object because it was already an agreed-upon matter.

Wang Jiyuk sat blankly in the Alliance Leader's seat and then opened his mouth.

,Before coming to the Murim Alliance, I met the Cheonma."

• • •

I returned to the Cheonma Shingyo.

Since the Cheonma Shingyo was so large, there were many death row inmates that I could kill right away. Thanks to that, I was able to easily gain experience for Stormbreaker.

,It's good to occupy a high position in a famous force. You can pass on the annoying tasks to the underlings and do auto-hunting."

Today, too, the underlings bring death row inmates to Little Cheonma Palace.

Since there were so many people in this world, there were almost two or three death row inmates every day. The death row inmates were usually bandits or robbers. I spent my time killing the death row inmates they brought.

But today, a slightly special guest came to visit.

It was a man equipped with a large sword on his waist. His realm was Three Stars 8th Level.

It was the Left Protector. Blood-Spreading Blade Gu Juk. He was a former elder belonging to the Council of Elders.

,Little Cheonma. I have come to deliver the Cheonma-nim's order."

The Left and Right Protectors of the Cheonma Shingyo were those who only followed and carried out the Cheonma's orders. In terms of position, I, the Cheonma's successor, was slightly higher, but the Left and Right Protectors did not listen to my orders.

,For the Left Protector to move personally. What kind of order did you bring?"

I took him to the reception room and served him tea. It was green tea that you could tell was a high-quality product just by smelling the fragrance. But the Left Protector didn't even give the tea a glance.

I felt something strange here. Even if he was busy, he didn't have time for a cup of tea? To me, the rightful successor of the Cheonma Shingyo?

,I will now deliver the Cheonma-nim's order. Little Cheonma Seong Yujin-ssi, listen. Form an alliance with the Blood Hegemony City and destroy the Thousand Intent Alliance."

,...."

It seemed that the Cheonma had decided to eliminate the Thousand Intent Alliance.

The Thousand Intent Alliance was a force formed by the union of over a thousand small and medium-sized sects. Simply looking at its size, it was larger than the Cheonma Shingyo. Its relationship with the Cheonma Shingyo was also not good.

,The Thousand Intent Alliance and the Cheonma Shingyo wouldn't be strange even if a war broke out right now. However, is there enough merit for the Cheonma Shingyo to attack first? The Cheonma Shingyo is currently wary of the Murim Alliance and doesn't have much leeway, right?"

The key was the Blood Hegemony City.

The Blood Hegemony City was a force established by the descendants of the Blood Demon Church, which was once on par with the Cheonma Shingyo. Of course, its power was weaker than when it was the Blood Demon Church, but it was still a Murim force that the Cheonma Shingyo could not ignore. If they attacked the Thousand Intent Alliance together with the Blood Hegemony City... it would be quite doable.

,Left Protector. Has there been prior discussion with the Blood Hegemony City?"

The Left Protector shook his head.

,No. It is Little Cheonma's responsibility to persuade the Blood Hegemony City. There has been no separate discussion with the Blood Hegemony City."

,You're entrusting me with a difficult task. It's as if you're trying to test me."

,A test.... It's not much different. The Cheonma-nim hinted that he will decide whether to retire depending on the success or failure of this matter. Furthermore, the destruction of the Thousand Intent Alliance will in itself become Little Cheonma's achievement, so even the Council of Elders will have no choice but to acknowledge it."

I thought of the current state of the current Cheonma. He was unable to appear in official events due to qi deviation.

,If you're going to die, just die peacefully and pass on the Cheonma position.... You're being annoying."

I muttered words that I couldn't say out loud in my heart.

It was obvious, but I couldn't refuse the Cheonma's order. The moment I refused the order, my position would be shaken.

I clasped my hands in salute to the Left Protector and said.

,I will carry out the Cheonma's order. But, to what extent can I mobilize the power of the Divine Church for this matter?"

,Little Cheonma can use the name of the Cheonma."

,That means I can do most things. Can I borrow the power of the Council of Elders?"

,The Council of Elders will also lend its power to Little Cheonma. But... I can't guarantee to what extent they will lend it."

,They'll probably only lend the minimum amount of power. My relationship with the Council of Elders isn't very good, after all."

I was not on good terms with the Council of Elders. From the Council of Elders' point of view, a rolling stone from somewhere suddenly occupied the position of Little Cheonma.

There were several opportunities to improve my relationship with the Council of Elders. The Council of Elders also reached out first. I was the one who rejected that hand. I would become the Cheonma even without the help of the Council of Elders. There was no need to leave unnecessary debts.

,When I become the Cheonma, I'm going to purge all of the Council of Elders except for the Sword Demon for the sake of power."

That thought was still firm.

,Left Protector. I want to have a private audience with the Cheonma-nim."

,Impossible."

The Left Protector said firmly. It seemed that the Cheonma's condition was very bad.

,I understand."
