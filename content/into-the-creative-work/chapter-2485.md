I left the chaotic classroom and went out into the hallway.

A clean hallway without a single piece of trash. It was similar to the high school I attended a few years ago, yet different. I glanced back and saw the 3-2 sign on the door.

,I'm tired.'

I was physically tired, but even more mentally exhausted. It felt like I had escaped from doing meaningless repetitive tasks for hours.

Should I go up? Or should I go down?

,The top is probably the roof. There are limited places to run or hide. Let's go to the first floor.'

I walked down the hallway, looking out the windows. There was an annex in addition to the main building. If I'm looking for a place to hide, the first floor is better. There are more places to escape if things go wrong.

,I think the first floor has more places to hide.'

Right now, the only things on the 3rd floor are classrooms and hallways. There are only 2 places to hide in the classroom. And even those are easy to check.

,Out of the 3 hours I have to endure, not even 10 minutes have passed yet.'

I sighed and carefully went down to the first floor. There happened to be a nurse's office, so I went inside.

Deureureuk.

I could see beds and cabinets. Something felt strange. It felt like I could smell something. Like a cosmetic smell.

,Is a woman hiding?'

First, I rummaged through the desk and drawers. Maybe there's something useful.

I found bandages.

I also found medicine. The medicine bottles were written in English, so I couldn't understand them. I don't know English. I can read it okay... but this was too long to even bother reading. Even if I did read it, I wouldn't know what it meant.

,Let's take the bandages for now... and there's Madecassol and Albothyl too. Should I take them?'

I paused while taking a few. The empty space in the drawer was particularly unnatural. As if someone had just taken something that was there.

,I smelled cosmetics, and... is someone hiding here?'

There are limited places for a person to hide. Inside the cabinet and under the bed. I quietly gripped the kitchen knife in both hands. If she's under the bed, she'll only be able to see my legs.

,I know you're hiding. Come out. I'm not a murderer. I don't intend to fight either."

If this is really a game, then the only goal is survival. There's no rule that survivors have to fight each other. In a way, survivors are on the same team.

,The problem is that team killing is possible. There's always some troll bastard wherever you go.'

I tightened my grip on the kitchen knife in both hands. The person hiding might be a murderer like Jang Hwichun.

,It's like a horror game. A jump scare where they hide and then suddenly attack.'

It's quiet. She doesn't come out. Was I mistaken? I spoke again.

,Did you happen to receive a suspicious black letter and move here? I also received that invitation. And if I were a murderer, would I be talking so politely?"

I thought of Jang Hwichun, whom I had just killed. He didn't say a word, so something was strange about him.

,...Stay there."

A woman's voice echoed from under the bed. It sounded quite nice.

My body tensed up. Come to think of it, how long has it been since I talked to a woman? Other than the convenience store clerk and the factory ajumma, is this the first time since high school?

With a rustling sound, a woman crawled out from under the hospital bed. Long, flowing hair, a suit jacket and skirt. Her legs had runs in her stockings and high heels. Underneath her suit jacket, her blouse was so tight it looked like it would burst.

,How can she hide under the bed with those boobs?'

I tried to look up. Her tightly closed red lips and fierce eyes glared at me. In her beautiful hands, she held scissors. Silver scissors sharp enough to kill someone.

,...You're the one who looks like a murderer?"

,No, this... there's a reason for this."

I panicked and lowered the kitchen knives in my hands. I realized belatedly that my clothes were covered in blood. I looked like a murderer to anyone who saw me.

I explained what had just happened before she misunderstood any further.

,...Jang Hwichun was there? That famous murderer Jang Hwichun from 10 years ago? You killed Jang Hwichun?"

,Yes. The body is on the 3rd floor. If you're really suspicious, go and check it out. I'll be here."

,......"

She frowned. It didn't feel like she was angry. It seemed like she was thinking about something.

,Okay. I'll believe you. But regardless, don't come near me."

,Alright, Nuna."

,Who's your Nuna?"

,Was it Dongsaeng? How old are you?"

Even though she was a pretty beauty, she didn't feel young. Anyone could tell that the clothes she was wearing were for a company employee. And it felt like she had a lot of life experience.

,...Twenty-five."

,You're right, Nuna. I'm Seong Yujin. Since we've met like this, can I drop the formalities?"

,You're quite brazen, aren't you?"

I was a little surprised myself. I couldn't even talk to the girls in my class very well before. But now, words are just flowing out.

,So, what's your name? It's beneficial to cooperate with each other here."

,Shin Dohyeon. Don't do anything stupid. If you do anything even slightly suspicious, I won't let it slide."

What are you going to do if I don't let it slide? I barely managed to hold back from sneering without realizing it.

,Shin Dohyeon. That's kind of a masculine name."

,You're one to talk with your feminine name."

I nodded. The name Yujin was quite common. I've met two people with the same name so far, and they were both women.

We faced each other warily. We decided to cooperate, but we couldn't trust each other at all.

Dadadadadada!

The sound of hurried footsteps running down the hallway.

,Euaaaaaaak!"

A man was screaming and running away. I could hear footsteps chasing after him, but unlike the fleeing man, they were calm and quiet.

Shin Dohyeon crawled under the bed. Honestly, I found it a bit cute. In my opinion, hiding under the bed was pointless. It would be better to hide near the window. That way, you can escape through the window if necessary.

I crouched next to the nurse's office entrance. My plan was to stab the guy's leg with the kitchen knife when he came in, then get up and stab his neck.

My plan wasn't executed. The guys passed by the nurse's office in an instant. I cautiously opened the door and checked outside.

A man in black clothes was chasing after the man. The man in black threw a kitchen knife at the man's back. Fortunately, the man dodged the kitchen knife. The chase continued.

,That back looks like Jang Hwichun? No, that can't be. I killed Jang Hwichun.'

Either the dead Jang Hwichun came back to life, or there were multiple Jang Hwichuns... Either way, it couldn't be real.

,In the first place, me being here right now is a bit different from reality.'

Shin Dohyeon crawled out from under the bed. I couldn't see her panties between her thighs covered in stockings. No light was coming in, so it was pitch black. Maybe she was wearing pitch-black panties. Well, she was wearing pantyhose, though.

,Dohyeon-ah. What are you going to do?"

,...You. You're quite rude, aren't you?"

,So, what are you going to do? I think we should move. If we keep staying here, we'll eventually get caught."

It wasn't good to keep hiding. The view from here was limited.

,...Where are we going to go?"

,The annex on the hill. It's on top of a hill, so the view is good and the path is good too. I think it's much better than being in this main building."

,Alright. Let's do as you say. You move first."

I opened the door and went out of the nurse's office. Shin Dohyeon's gaze on my back was cold. It didn't feel good to be showing my back to someone else. I wondered if she would stab me in the back with the scissors in her hand if something went wrong.

,But she's a beauty. Let's be patient.'

,Why are you going that way? That's the direction those guys went in earlier."

,If we go the other way, we'll have to go too far. And we have to go to this hallway so we don't get noticed."

,If you're walking so openly, they'll see you through the hallway window, won't they?"

,We can see them too. And the hallway faces the playground. If we see something, we can deal with it first."

,......It feels strangely familiar? Is this not your first time?"

,It's my first time. There's no way I'd have many experiences like this."

I was honestly surprised that I was saying that. I didn't know I could do this much. And even as I'm talking, I'm rolling my eyes and being wary of my surroundings.

I found a corpse in front of the door leading out of the hallway. It was a brutally butchered corpse. Blood and organs were dug up.

,Ugh..."

Shin Dohyeon covered her mouth with her hand and staggered. Judging by the trembling around her eyes, she seemed to have been greatly shocked.

Of course, I was also mentally shocked. Honestly, I felt nauseous. I didn't want to know what the inside of a human body looked like in this way.

,I feel like I'm going to throw up... but I can't throw up in front of a woman and embarrass myself.'

You have to put on a show at times like this.

,That guy went up to the 2nd floor using the stairs next to him. Let's go to the annex in the meantime."

I could see bloody footprints going up to the 2nd floor. Unless he suddenly comes down, we won't run into him.

,You're calm. What's your job? Are you perhaps a soldier...?"

,I'm a draft dodger and I work at a factory."

,...A meat processing plant?"

,A dish factory."

My job was to pack and carry the finished dishes.

I ran into someone else on the way to the annex. It was a man who looked like a college student and a male student who looked like a high school student. It was easy to recognize the high school student because he was wearing a school uniform.

As Shin Dohyeon and I were wary of the two, the man who looked like a college student urgently pointed to the annex.

,Are you two heading to the annex too? We don't have time to be doing this here, so let's go and talk. If we dawdle here, the murderer Jang Hwichun might notice and follow us."

The college student was right. We arrived at the annex. The first-floor cafeteria was large. I tried to lock the entrance door, but it wouldn't lock because the lock seemed to be broken.

,The door here doesn't lock either."

,It doesn't either?"

I asked back.

,Yes. We were on the 2nd floor of the classroom, but we went to the roof first and then came down. The roof door was open and didn't lock either. I think all the doors here don't lock."

I looked at the man again with a fresh perspective. His impression was ordinary without any particular characteristics. But he was very calm. It was a stark contrast to the high school student next to him, who was trembling and avoiding my eyes.

I don't know about the high school student, but this guy seemed quite useful. Of course, not as good as Shin Dohyeon. Because Shin Dohyeon is pretty.

,I'm Seong Yujin. You are?"

,Yujinmyeong."

I held out my hand as a sign of goodwill. It was a hand with a little blood on it. Yujinmyeong paused for a moment and then took my hand.
