Surrounded by ten A-rank Hunters, I quickly scanned the environment.

It was a spacious interior. Training equipment was visible here and there. The facilities and tools were all state-of-the-art. It seemed I had been forcibly summoned to a Hunter training ground.

'Am I in a crisis situation right now?'

A question I posed to myself. The answer came back immediately.

'No.'

It would be a different story if I had been ambushed as soon as I was summoned.

Toda Hiroshi hadn't taken any action against me. Yet, he was smiling confidently. He was clearly being careless.

'Well, from that bastard's perspective, I'm an F-rank Hunter. It doesn't make sense to bring ten A-rank Hunters here.'

Still, bringing ten A-rank Hunters meant he was thorough in his own way.

"You're quiet. Scared? I understand. Any one of us here could kill you with a single finger."

Some of the A-rank Hunters chuckled. I remembered the faces of those bastards who laughed at me. Those bastards would writhe in agony as they died.

"How did you summon me?"

"...You're asking me questions in this situation? You're definitely not ordinary. Haven't you grasped the situation yet?"

He intentionally exuded killing intent. Judging by the killing intent, he seemed to have killed quite a few people. It was laughable to me, though.

He took out a piece of paper from his pocket. He tossed the crumpled paper in front of me with a thud.

I didn't pick up the paper that fell at my feet, but looked down at it.

『Slave Contract

Rank: Rare

This is a slave contract. The effects of the contract will be in effect until the contract is terminated.

1 billion coins are required to terminate the contract.』

It was an item from the Tower. I was a little surprised because it didn't appear in the original story.

The content of the contract was a single line.

「- becomes a slave of Mineki Hosei.」

Who was Mineki Hosei again?

I remembered. He was the current Prime Minister of Japan. A villain who appeared in the original story.

"Write your name in the blank space there. Write it with blood or spit, it doesn't matter. If you don't write your name, you'll die. There's a saying in Korea, isn't there? It's better to live rolling in dog poop than to die. Kihehe."

The intention was clear.

He was going to make me a slave and exploit me until I died. You can terminate it if you have 1 billion coins? Even an S-rank Hunter can't easily get 1 billion coins. Even if I become an S-rank Hunter, as long as I'm a slave, the coins will be taken away from me.

'If I become a slave, can't I kill the master? Well, even if that's not the case, it's just a rare-grade contract... I can terminate it in other ways.'

Of course, I had no intention of writing my name on this contract.

Instead of my hand, I stomped on the contract with my foot. The crumpled contract was soon torn.

The faces of the A-rank Hunters who had been laughing at me stiffened. They all became serious and exuded killing intent.

"It seems you don't want to live. I won't kill you right away. I need to know how you became the number one ranked Hunter. I'll start by cutting off your arms and legs."

"You guys must have been prepared when you started this. Don't even think about dying peacefully."

"Kihehe. You crazy bastard. What can you do?"

『Summoning Wiyu using 1,000 coins.』

『Summoning Yuria Grace using 1,000 coins.』

『Summoning Elena Valderte using 1,000 coins.』

"The world isn't always peaceful."

"How dare you, you ignorant insects...."

Thud.
