[Acquired Pet Cage for reaching level 95.]

I immediately took out the Pet Cage from my inventory.

The Pet Cage itself looked ordinary. A small, blue plastic box. The inside was visible through the bars on the entrance and sides. It was a suitable size to carry in one hand.

,The important thing is its ability. It's a reward for reaching level 95, so it can't be useless.'

I checked in the Random Draw Shop.

[Pet Cage

You can put pets in the cage.

Humanoids, including humans, are not allowed.

You can put the pet in the inventory while it is in the cage. The cage is deactivated and slowly recovers its stamina.

You can put pets in regardless of their size.

Stamina is slowly consumed when the pet is active in another world. When stamina is completely exhausted, it is reverse summoned to the Pet Cage. If the pet suffers critical damage in another world, it is reverse summoned to the Pet Cage.

Only pets that have submitted to their master can be put in. The pet is bound upon first use.

Stamina: 100/100

Price: 5,000,000 points

Note: Depending on the pet's disposition, it may find the cage uncomfortable. The pet's stats may change in another world.]

The first thing I checked was the price.

A whopping 5 million. There weren't many things more expensive than this in the Random Draw Shop. But after reading the details, the price made sense.

,This means I can take animals to other play worlds.'

It's not like I can just take them, there are conditions attached. It was impossible to put humans or humanoids in the Pet Cage.

Humanoids.

Races that are similar to humans but different. For example, elves.

,Can't I put women in and carry them around? Is it possible with monsters?'

I wouldn't know unless I experimented.

,Dinosaurs or dragons aren't human, so I should be able to put them in, right?'

Come out, Tyrannosaurus!

I thought about fighting using a Tyrannosaurus. It was just big, but it seemed like a pushover. It might get beaten to death by an ogre.

On the other hand, a dragon?

,Can I put Priscilla from the Baekhwan world in the Pet Cage?'

If I took her with me, it would be a trump card. I don't know if it's possible yet because I haven't tested it.

,I can't just test it recklessly. It says the pet is bound upon first use.'

Priscilla was a dragon, so she had a high pride. Even now, in a close relationship, she often refused my requests. Moreover, she often disappeared without a word when she had something she wanted to do. In short, she was difficult to control.

,It seems better to find a pet that is helpful to me rather than a strong one.'

Let's put it on hold for now.

[Available points: 9,705]

[Would you like to use 8,000 points to increase the level of Inventory Lv.4?]

I raised the level of the inventory. The inventory gained new abilities each time it leveled up, so I was quite excited.

This time, the inventory didn't gain an ability, but one restriction disappeared.

,The weight limit is gone. More precisely, the restriction that I have to be able to lift it is gone.'

This means that even items with a weight I can't lift can be put in the inventory.

,I can even put an aircraft carrier in and carry it around?'

A smile spread across my face. The inventory became a broken skill.

[Would you like to use 20,000 points to increase the level of Inventory Lv.5?]

[Insufficient points.]

It required a whopping 20,000 points to level up to the next level though.

,Isn't this much better than the Pet Cage?'

I ran out of the house. If I got the unlimited inventory skill, what should I do?

,Of course, it's fucking theft. Everything in this world is mine.'

Since I have a hacking skill, I can manipulate cameras.

The first place I arrived at was the parking lot.

The officetel I live in is quite a high-end officetel. Because the standard of living is high, the parking lot was full of foreign cars.

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the surveillance camera for 8 hours.]

I disabled the camera and approached the red Ferrari.

"This car is mine now."

I put my hand on the body of the Ferrari and used the inventory.

"...Uh. It doesn't work?"

[This is an item with ownership.]

[Would you like to use 5 points to steal ownership?]

,Steal ownership? Ownership applies to cars that don't have special powers? What the hell.'

I hesitated for a moment.

5 points. It's enough points to do a random draw 5 times. Is it right to use this on a Ferrari? If I use 5 points for a random draw, I might get an item worth millions of points?

,Let's try it once for now. I need to experiment to see what happens.'

[Stole ownership by consuming 5 points.]

Nothing much changed.

,Inventory.'

Instead, the Ferrari went into the inventory. I took the Ferrari out again. I tried to open the car door, but... it didn't open because I didn't have the car key.

,Even if I steal ownership, it doesn't mean I get the car key that I didn't have. Then why does ownership exist?'

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the car for 3 hours.]

The car key didn't really matter because I had hacking.

I didn't drive the Ferrari. The owner of this Ferrari on paper isn't me. I could have caused annoying problems if I drove this Ferrari.

I parked the Ferrari in the inventory and walked outside. I needed to experiment with the inventory a little more.

I walked around the street and put various things in.

Stones and plants went in without ownership. I tried to put in a roadside tree, but it was impossible.

[Not an independent object. Cannot be put in.]

The roadside tree is firmly rooted in the ground, so it seems it cannot be recognized as an independent object.

For reference, you can't put living things in the inventory, but you can put plants in. It seems they don't consider plants as living things.

,Can I put plant-type monsters in? I don't know about that.'

It feels like the standard for living things is whether or not a soul exists. No matter what, I can't put the entire Earth in the inventory.

,No. If the inventory is really big, can I put it in?'

I put aside the meaningless speculation and reached out to the large flower pot next to me.

[This is an item with ownership.]

[Would you like to use 2 points to steal ownership?]

,Even a roadside flower pot has ownership? Ah, is the district office the owner?'

I didn't have any points to use on a flower pot, so I didn't steal ownership.

,Now that I see it, it seems like the inventory has been nerfed....'

I looked around and, after confirming that no one was around, I broke the flower pot. Then I was able to put it in the inventory regardless of ownership.

After that, I was able to put coins found on the street into the inventory without any conditions.

After doing various experiments, I soon thought of bound items.

,Among the items used by Hunters and magic tools, there are bound items. Items that don't properly activate their abilities if they're not the owner.'

Bound magic is clearly magic that exists in reality.

I headed to the Hunter pawnshop. It's a store used by C-class and lower Hunters when they need quick cash. B-class and higher can get loans from first-tier banks without any conditions.

When I entered the Hunter pawnshop, there were Hunter items lined up. Things for sale. And things that weren't for sale yet.

"Welcome. You... you seem to have come to buy something."

The 50-year-old pawnshop owner said, looking at me. I felt mana from him. Judging by mana alone, he's roughly a B-class Hunter.

"How did you know?"

"The guys who want to leave things and borrow money have a different atmosphere. Besides, aren't you an A-class Hunter? What's an A-class Hunter lacking that they would borrow money from a pawnshop."

"You recognize me."

"You're not even trying to hide it. Well, those who have eyes but can't see wouldn't know.... What are you looking for?"

"I was curious about the pawnshop, so I came in to take a look. I'll take a look around."

"Go ahead."

I looked around and grabbed a dagger. It was a dagger with a gleaming green blade.

"How much is this?"

"That's the Poisonous Dagger. C-class and lower monsters are poisoned the moment they're stabbed by that dagger. The poisoning speed is a bit slow though... on average, they all die in 10 minutes."

"So, the price is?"

"800 million. It's originally worth 1.1 billion."

"Isn't it bound by magic?"

"That's why I'm giving you a 300 million discount. Don't buy it if you don't like it."

You can break the bound magic that a person cast, but not the item's own bound magic. You need a highly skilled magician, so you'll need some money.

"I'll buy it."

"Thank you. I don't know why someone with your skills needs such a weapon though."

"It's a hobby."

"That's a gruesome hobby. Take care, Seong Yujin Hunter."

"...You know me?"

"How could I not know Korea's supernova following Han Ah-young, a new S-class Hunter candidate."

"I've become famous. Make a lot of money."

I returned home and stabbed myself in the palm with the Poisonous Dagger. A little blood came out, but there was no poison.

,Because of the bound magic, you can't use the ability if you're not the owner. Steal ownership.'

[This is an item with ownership.]

[Would you like to use 20 points to steal ownership?]

20 points. It's a waste, but it's for the experiment.

[Stole ownership by consuming 20 points.]

I felt something coming from the Poisonous Dagger connecting to me. It wasn't harmful.

,Maybe this is bound magic.'

Green poison flows out of the blade that was fine until just now. Pok. I stabbed my palm with the tip of the knife like before.

Poison penetrates.

But only for a moment. When I raised the energy of the Lightning Sky Style, the poison was suppressed in an instant.

,It doesn't seem like a very strong poison... is it because of poison resistance?'

Anyway, I confirmed something important.

If you steal a bound item, the binding is passed on to you.

,Good?'

Of course, it wasn't that this Poisonous Dagger was good. It was a piece of trash to me, even if it wasn't to others. I decided to just throw the Poisonous Dagger into the inventory. I probably won't use it again.

[Starting the play.]

I looked for Makina. Makina was kneeling on top of the robot vacuum cleaner and staring at the TV with a blank expression.

The TV was broadcasting a children's animation. I don't watch it because it's boring, but Makina was watching it intently. It was proof that Makina's mental age wasn't much different from children. If you count by age, it must be at least several decades.

,Is it because of her appearance? Or is it because she's a spirit and doesn't grow.'

I took the Pet Cage out of the inventory and said to Makina.

"Makina. You're my spirit. Come into this cage."

"What? No. I won't. Get away."

She said without even looking this way. I was so annoyed that I almost gave her a knuckle sandwich, but I exercised patience. If I hit Makina, Seong Hari might tell me off.

"You're my spirit. You have to do what I say."

"I'm busy. I have to watch Pink Boong."

"What the fuck is Pink Boong, you weeb."

"Uh. Bad word. I'm going to tell Ajumma everything."

"You're my pet. If you admit it, I'll give you one Spirit Orb."

"Spirit Orb?!"

Makina turned her head and looked at me. Even the most magical animation couldn't beat the Spirit Orb.

"You're my pet, right?"

I shook the Spirit Orb. Saliva flows from Makina's △-shaped mouth.

"Y-yes, that's right. I'm your pet!"

It was the moment Makina admitted it. Light poured out from the Pet Cage and pulled Makina in. Makina, who was instantly pulled into the Pet Cage, was shrunk to fit the size of the Pet Cage. Makina grabbed the bars with her small hands and shook them.

"W-what is this! Let me out! It's stuffy! Let me out~~~!!"

"Shut up. Quiet and eat this."

I threw the Spirit Orb into the cage. Makina, who was grabbing and shaking the bars, grabbed the Spirit Orb and sucked on it.

,This stupid bitch is my spirit... I sigh. I would have thrown her away if her abilities weren't useful. I hope my daughter doesn't grow up like her.'

I put the Pet Cage in the inventory.
