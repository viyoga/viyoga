"Feast your eyes and be amazed, you fools. This is the Heart of Flame, forged from the souls of 20,000 humans and 66 fire spirits!"

Hartunggeu laughed, his face flushed with glee.

Puk.

A blade, seemingly out of nowhere, pierced Hartunggeu's neck.

Hartunggeu staggered back, his massive body wobbling.

I was taken aback. Instinctively, I used Chronos. The world slowed, giving me time to think.

'A bit early for the plan. The idea was to off Hartunggeu when he was riding high on the crowd's cheers.'

The sword that flew and pierced Hartunggeu's neck wasn't Yuria's attack. There's no way Yuria would attack so simply by throwing a sword. Yuria mainly uses daggers, and would have ambushed more secretly and surely.

I glanced at the ceiling. The place where Yuria was hiding, enveloping her body in shadow and perfectly erasing her presence.

'The sword came from behind me.'

I moved.

Chronos was released.

The people around me didn't scream but stared at where the sword had come from. There were no panicking simpletons screaming at witnessing a murder here. Those kinds of clumsy bastards wouldn't have received Hartunggeu's invitation in the first place.

'Who is it?'

There was a man in a sword-throwing stance. A man wearing a flamboyant red mask.

None of the people around him stepped up for Hartunggeu. Instead, they shut their mouths and watched the man with interest.

They didn't care whether Hartunggeu lived or died.

"How amusing."

Hartunggeu's voice came out. Even with a sword through his neck, he spoke calmly without falling to the ground. I felt an ominous energy from Hartunggeu. Those who had been nonchalant until now flinched and distanced themselves from Hartunggeu.

"Tough bastard. Still posturing even after being hit by a holy sword. I know you're a demon. And I know that body is your true form."

"A holy sword? This shoddy blade?"

Hartunggeu grabbed the sword stuck in his neck with his bare hand. A pure white light emanated from the sword, resisting Hartunggeu. But Hartunggeu ignored the light attacking his body and pulled out the sword. The sword vanished in an instant.

Instead of blood, a greenish sulfur flame flickered from Hartunggeu's neck. His skin turned black like a volcanic region, and horns sprouted on his head.

"Revealing your disgusting form, are we? You've had your fun in the human realm; it's time to pay for your sins."

The red mask didn't panic and conjured swords in both hands. They looked exactly like the sword that had been stuck in Hartunggeu's neck.

I used Chronos again to accelerate my thoughts.

'That red mask... I know who it is.'

Zen Height.

An S-class Hunter who operates secretly without revealing his identity to the world. No, he probably isn't S-class yet at this point in time.

He's a Hunter who specializes in killing demons. His nickname is Devil Hunter.

His trait is 'Holy Sword Incarnate (S+)', which allows him to temporarily create holy swords and imbue his body with the abilities of a holy sword.

As his nickname Devil Hunter suggests, it was an ability specialized for killing demons.

'But why the hell is he here? There wasn't any information in the original story about him causing a ruckus at Hartunggeu's party?'

The answer came easily.

This world is moving on a completely different route than the one in the original story.

'Well, the original story didn't have Seong Hari's son in the first place. The original story was twisted from the moment I was born.'

That butterfly effect could be happening now.

I turned off my concerns. Thinking coldly, this situation was beneficial to me. If I used Zen's power, I could deal with Hartunggeu without Yuria having to use Limit Break. Since I thought this way, Yuria would definitely think so too.

Limit Break is a last resort that can only be used for one minute. She would use Limit Break as late as possible, only when it was unavoidable or in a sure situation where she could kill Hartunggeu.

'Everyone's attention is on Hartunggeu and Zen. Good. My job is to steal the Heart of Flame.'

The Heart of Flame was suspended in mid-air, still burning on its own even after falling from Hartunggeu's hand.

It was the moment I got nearly 5M closer to Hartunggeu.

Yuria moved.

Black shadows soared from the ceiling and the ground, attacking Hartunggeu.

"What?"

Hartunggeu, who had been focused on Zen, was helplessly attacked by the shadows. And the shadow hand grabbed the Heart of Flame and threw it towards me.

I snatched the flying Heart of Flame with my right hand. I felt heat in my hand. I wondered if my hand was burning, but the hand I checked with my own eyes was fine.

'Damn it.'

The Spirit Eyes were forcibly activated. My eyes stung.

'It's a rejection. The original plan was for me to absorb the Heart of Flame. Seeing that the Spirit Eyes have been activated... is the problem that 66 fire spirits were used as ingredients?'

According to the original story, there were three ways to use the Heart of Flame: absorb it, use it as a material for weapons or armor, or sell it to someone.

'Chronos.'

I used Chronos for the third time. I put the Heart of Flame in my inventory and ran away with all my might.

"Huh. So, you were thieves. How many years has it been since I've been had like this? 40 years?"

Hartunggeu's voice rang out from behind. Glancing back, Hartunggeu was unharmed despite being attacked. Yuria revealed herself, hiding her identity by enveloping her body in shadows. She held a dagger in her hand. A jet-black aura rippled from the blade.

"There have been many thieves who stole my things. And I cut off all of their hands and feet and escorted them to the warmest place in hell. Heu... now that I think about it, I'm happy to be able to escort thieves again after a long time."

Green flames soared from Hartunggeu's body. His fat body transformed into a giant over 3M tall.

I confirmed that this entire cruise ship was Hartunggeu's domain. There was no escaping this.

And I felt that he was watching me.

'To think he'd throw away his pride and unleash his abilities to the fullest. Hartunggeu values the Heart of Flame more than I thought.'

I took out a space-time scroll from my pocket and tore it. There was no reaction.

'Shibal! Is it because of Hartunggeu's authority?!'

Cold sweat formed on my forehead.

"Hand over the Heart of Flame. Then I'll show mercy and escort you to a slightly less warm place."

Hartunggeu tried to stride towards me, but Yuria and Zen attacked him simultaneously. Surprisingly, their teamwork was good.

'Yuria must have given Zen instructions through telepathy.'

I stopped running.

There was no way to shake off his gaze even if I went outside. Above all, this cruise ship was full of his subordinates. Seeing him going all out because of the Heart of Flame, he would immediately order his subordinates if I ran away now.

"Damn demon bastard... the title of Duke isn't just for show. To think he'd unleash this much power in the human realm."

"You are worthy of being acknowledged just for not being burned by my hellfire. Look, those guys are screaming nicely because they can't withstand my hellfire."

The ones burning were the spectators who had been here until just now, the guests invited by Hartunggeu. Some had escaped, but most of the others were burning in green hellfire.

'The reason I'm not dying must be because of the Heart of Flame.'

If I just waited for Yuria to aim for Hartunggeu's weakness and use Limit Break...

'Wait a minute.'

Yuria and Zen were gradually being pushed back.

Zen's holy sword was barely cutting through Hartunggeu's black skin, and Yuria's shadow couldn't even approach Hartunggeu's burning body. The only thing that allowed Yuria to damage him was the Black Thunder she occasionally used. However, even that couldn't inflict a fatal wound.

'This bastard is getting stronger and stronger?!'

Thump. Thump.

Hartunggeu's slaves who were around him collapsed. The strong ones were collapsing one by one.

I immediately realized what was happening.

'Sacrifice. He's using the contracted slaves as sacrifices.'

Unlike the original story, Hartunggeu abandoned his arrogance. Missing the moment when he was off guard at the very beginning was quite big. If Yuria had used Limit Break at that time...

'No. I don't know with the sacrifices.'

I didn't know his obsession with the Heart of Flame would be this great.

"Is the Heart of Flame that important?"

"200 years. The Heart of Flame is what I created after 200 years of trial and error. With that, I can release some of the restrictions and enjoy life in the human realm more freely."

"You're already playing freely, yet you're disgustingly greedy."

"There is no end to the desire for freedom. Isn't that the same for you humans? Hmm. Is this the limit of the output that can be offered as a sacrifice?"

Hartunggeu swung his fist. Zen, who was hit, soared into the night sky along with the ceiling. Yuria was swept away by the aftermath and rolled on the floor. Blood flowed from Yuria's face.

I clicked my tongue briefly.

-Master-nim. I will use Limit Break. The authority of hellfire is not compatible with my shadows. It will be difficult to deal with him in one minute, but I think I can send Master-nim away. I will send a signal, so prepare the scroll.

"It's okay."

I stood proudly.

"Now that I think about it. I have to kill that Mekin bastard too, so it doesn't make sense to run away from a bastard like this."

Hartunggeu, who was approaching me, stopped. He showed interest in me.

"Mekin? Are you talking about the fifth legion commander? The ambition of a young one is so great that it's appalling. Thief. What is your name?"

I looked around.

I and Yuria were the only ones alive here. It would be right to say that he was keeping us alive.

"Seong Yujin. A man under the protection of the law."

"Keu keu. You're a funny one. I'll remember the name."

I took out the Heart of Flame from my inventory. My eyes forcibly opened the Spirit Eyes. The Heart of Flame was still hot. The miraculous event of the rejection disappearing suddenly didn't happen.

"To think you'd hand over the Heart of Flame to me so easily. Disappointing."

"Who said I'd give it to you? I'm going to use this."

The corners of Hartunggeu's mouth stretched out. His sharp teeth were burning with green fire.

"You? I'll wait. Try it if you can."

Hartunggeu's arrogance returned. From his point of view, the current situation was already settled. In his eyes, I probably looked like a wriggling earthworm.

"I might kill you in an instant, so I'll tell you in advance."

"I like listening to the last words of the deceased. Speak."

"Your Heart of Flame was awesome."

I used Genius Time.

The Heart of Flame was no longer hot. The rejection had disappeared. Still, absorption was impossible. There was no way I could absorb all of the Heart of Flame during the time Genius Time was maintained.

I first resonated the Heart of Flame using mana and used the Spirit Contract skill.

'I'll give the Heart of Flame as the price of the contract. Anyone who wants the Heart of Flame, come out now.'

Since I used the Heart of Flame, a fire spirit would pop out.

This was a cruise ship floating on the sea, but there was no problem. There were volcanoes under the sea too.

Even if it was tens of kilometers away, a top-tier fire spirit would be able to sense it.

'Connected.'

-I will contract.

Immediately after hearing the rock-like voice, a giant turtle made of red lava appeared in front of me.

"You damn bastard! Do you think I'll hand over the Heart of Flame to a spirit!"

Hartunggeu, who was startled by the appearance of the top-tier spirit, hurriedly shouted and rushed towards me. But it was too late. The contract between the Lava Turtle and me had already been completed even before it appeared.

The Lava Turtle laughed at Hartunggeu and swallowed the Heart of Flame.

'Come.'

Only 8 seconds had passed. Genius Time was being maintained.

'Spirit Possession!'

The Lava Turtle entered my body.

I felt a huge presence.

Lava Turtle.

This bastard wasn't a good guy. If he had even a little conscience, he wouldn't have entered my body without the slightest hesitation. Logically, I would almost certainly not be able to handle it and die.

'If I die, he's planning to eat the Heart of Flame and disappear. A very likeable spirit. I'll be sure to remember this.'

I felt the huge presence and power inside me.

Nothing more, nothing less.

My body was burning red. But it wasn't hot. Currently, I had become a spirit due to Spirit Possession. At first glance, it looked like the flames were burning my body, but in fact, they were protecting my body. This flame was my power.
