A wizard from the Raytheon School moved a pile of scrap metal called a Voltgazer with magic, glancing at me.

375.1 billion volts. That was the voltage of the Thunderbolt the wizard told me about. He said it was a new record among 6th-circle wizards... Well. It wasn't very comforting. It meant that if I went to 7th-circle, I'd get an even higher voltage.

,Whether it's a new record or whatever, it's unpleasant.'

Being tested by someone. This situation itself was something I agreed to when I received the invitation from the Magic Association, but it was still unpleasant. But I can't just do it carelessly. Considering my position and influence within the Magic Association, I have to prove myself.

,It doesn't matter if I show my full power.'

Because I'll continue to grow in the future. I'm not satisfied with this level right now.

Of course, I have no intention of showing off Numbers Artifacts or anything. They're still things I can't handle on my own. If I carelessly show them off, thugs will target me.

While I was lost in thought, the Raytheon School wizard informed me of a change in the next test. They were skipping the minor tests such as mana capacity, mana control, and magic knowledge, and moving on to magic combat.

,Thunderbolt must have been quite impressive. It's a good thing since I can save time.'

Do they know? That Thunderbolt was an impromptu magic using the mysteries of myriad thunderbolts.

They won't know. Even if I told them, they wouldn't believe me, thinking it was just boasting.

The Raytheon School wizard activated the barrier in the training ground and left. I looked around the barrier. The main body of the barrier seems to be underground. As befitting the name Raytheon, it's a very sturdy barrier.

,I don't think I can break through it even if I keep hitting it with Thunderbolts.'

I can feel lightning attribute from the barrier. Lightning elemental barrier. Unless you have overwhelming power, breaking through this barrier with lightning element is close to impossible. The most efficient way is to analyze the barrier and reverse engineer it, but that's something even professional barrier masters struggle with, and it takes time.

I was trying to find a way to break through the barrier in my head when a woman walked into the training ground.

She was a blonde woman with glasses. She wore a neat white two-piece suit with a cardigan-like black robe over her top. The blue circular mark engraved on the shoulder of the robe was a symbol representing the Hitel School.

Her blonde hair, which seemed to sparkle in the sunlight, was parted 8 to 2. Her hair came down to her waist. She tucked one side of her hair behind her ear and wore silver semi-rimless glasses. Her face was as beautiful as a doll, but she exuded an emotionless, even picky and ruthless atmosphere.

If you lower your gaze slightly, her chest is plump and swollen. Is it because of her picky impression? For some reason, that chest looks vulgar. Her waist was slender, and her hips, wrapped in an H-line skirt that covered her knees, were also large.

,No, this is not the time for this.'

I'm about to fight. I know I shouldn't be like this, but... it wasn't easy to overcome a man's instincts.

With a small sigh, I suppressed my lust and looked at my opponent once more.

I noticed that the tips of her ears were very slightly pointed.

,If she had been injected with elf factors, her ears would have been long... It seems like she has elves in her ancestry.'

She opened her mouth.

,I am Orsia Hitel, your opponent. Please do your best."

The voice coming from between her glossy pink lips was as cold as her impression, but it was clear enough to stick in my ears. Just listening to her voice, you'd think she was a professional announcer.

,I'm Eugene Meyer. I never thought I'd be fighting a wizard from the famous Hitel School. And against the successor of the Hitel School at that."

Hitel School.

It's one of the oldest schools in Neo London. They mainly study space magic. It's such a famous school that there's a saying that if you want to learn space magic, go to the Hitel School in Neo London.

It would be a lie to say I'm not interested in space magic. Just learning short-range space teleportation magic can give you an overwhelming advantage in battle. But I'm still wary of the Hitel School. Because it's a very rigid place that has maintained its tradition and reputation for a long time.

,Are you ready?"

Orsia asked without batting an eye.

,Yes."

,Then let's begin."

With those business-like words, Orsia opened her Astral.

An invisible force takes over her surroundings. I couldn't help but flinch at her sharp-as-a-knife wizard's spirit.

7th-circle.

A wizard who has reached the realm of superhuman.

I swallowed hard. There was a difference between 6th-circle and 7th-circle that couldn't be easily bridged. If I used an artifact, I'd have a chance of winning, but... the possibility of winning with pure magic was infinitely low.

,It doesn't matter if I lose. A 7th-circle wizard. It's an opportunity to fight a space magic user at that.'

An opportunity you can't buy for a thousand gold. This experience will definitely be a stepping stone for growth.

[Electric Field]

Currents spread in all directions. This isn't an attack, but an environmental setup for battle. Elemental wizards are inevitably affected by the environment due to their nature.

Orsia didn't stay still. She made a hand seal and manifested magic. The Electric Field disappears, crushed by spatial pressure. The lightning element that filled the surroundings is pushed out, and pure natural mana fills its place.

The problem is that those mana are under Orsia's control.

,She's a 7th-circle wizard. I'll give up mana dominance.'

I had to give up what I had to give up.

I walked forward.

Close combat using Lightning Sky Style was impossible. Even if I had the information of Lightning Sky Style in my head, unfolding it with my body is a different story. I didn't have the physical talent, so I cleanly gave up on combat using Lightning Sky Style.

[Lightning Spear]

A spear of lightning flies towards Orsia.

[Wormhole]

Orsia chanted. A pitch-black space appeared in front of her, swallowed the Lightning Spear, and then spat it out again.

,5th-circle space magic, Wormhole. Projectile reflection magic.'

I controlled the flying Lightning Spear and slammed it into the ground. It was possible because it was a lightning magic I used. If it was an attack of a different attribute, it would have been impossible to control.

,I should assume that ordinary projectile attacks won't work. Even Thunderbolt will be reflected.'

Area-of-effect skills like Electric Field will be crushed by spatial pressure, and projectile attacks will be reflected. Then what about a very powerful area-of-effect skill?

,She won't give me time to chant.'

Keudeudeuk.

My body, which was walking forward, stopped. The distance between her and me was 5m. An invisible wall of spatial pressure was pushing me away.

,I can't break through it with a wizard's body.'

[Lightning Strike]

Lightning falls from the sky. Orsia's Wormhole also accepts the lightning and reflects it. I didn't avoid the lightning that was flying straight at me like a train. It was also because I couldn't avoid it because the distance was too close.

I took the lightning with my body. Electrocution? A lightning wizard getting electrocuted is ridiculous. I controlled the lightning using the mysteries of Lightning Sky Style. Even if sparks fly from my whole body, it doesn't harm me.

[Gale Lightning]

Orsia draws her hand in the air. A blade of space is created and flies towards me. Of course, I dodged to the side... and almost fell over. It was because my physical abilities suddenly increased due to Gale Lightning, and I couldn't adapt.

,First, I need to shake off the spatial pressure using gravity. Then I'll freeze her with Frost.'

[Reverse Gravity]

Anti-gravity pushes away the spatial pressure. Orsia's legs also floated slightly into the air, but she used magic and came back down.

[Frost]

Cold air rushes towards her. The barrier surrounding her body freezes. I ran towards her. I reached out my hand towards the frozen barrier and chanted magic.

[Lightning Grab]

Pajijijijik! Just before my hand, sparkling with lightning, touched the frozen barrier, my body stopped. I lowered my gaze. Shackles of space were holding my legs.

,Your fast casting speed and magic linkage are excellent. You have some of the best talent among the battle mages I've seen."

,...You seem quite disappointed, though?"

,Because I have some elven blood mixed in me, I can feel things that others can't. For example, the target's mana affinity. I felt a strong mana scent from you, but... that's all."

,......."

So. Does she mean she wants to get hit by me right now? Looking at that contemptuous look, I think my guess is correct.

,Even if I can't win, I have to punch her at least once.'

I activate Phase Astral, the Astral Vision that the transcendent me, who exists in the distant future and whose existence I'm not even sure of, gave me.

The Astral expands and the mana road is fully activated. I release the shackles of space holding my ankles with mana resistance.

Wince.

Orsia's eyes widened as if she was surprised. But she quickly regained her composure and gathered the surrounding mana.

[Phase Overlap]

I overlap the original Astral with the Phase Astral. My mind is forcibly heightened. A strange sensation as if two of me have merged into one. The mana road doubles and my body begins to feel the strain. I can't last long in this state.

,I'm going for a short-term battle. Let's see how long she can last.'

I cast magic until my mind can handle it.

A magic circle is drawn in the air.

At first, it was drawn in front of me, but then a magic circle was drawn on Orsia's right side as well. The two magic circles became four, and then eight. In the end, 16 magic circles appeared all over the training ground, aiming their artillery at her.

16-fold incantation consisting of attack magic below 5th-circle.

,A barrage of magic pouring in from all directions. Reflect it all if you can.'

I didn't activate all 16 magics at once. That would be easier to defend against. I started the magic bombardment with a slight delay.

The used magic immediately casts another magic, maintaining 16 multiple incantations.

The mana road is burning and my head feels like it's about to explode. But I can endure it. The transcendent's Astral Vision made the ridiculous magic bombardment possible.

,Ugh!"

Orsia used three Wormholes to reflect the magic pouring at her back at me. But I spun around, dodging the reflected magic and chanting. Surprisingly, the reflected magic had low accuracy. On the other hand, my magic accurately targets the places I'm aiming for.

Magic of all attributes such as water, fire, wind, ice, rock, and lightning targets her gaps. Orsia was sweating profusely, focusing on the Wormholes and barrier. She endures the magic attacks that she couldn't reflect with the Wormholes with the barrier.

,Damn it. I'm going to pass out before the barrier breaks.'

The good thing is that Orsia also seems out of it.

Even the magic bombardment that seemed like it would continue endlessly had an end. The 62nd. The last Lightning Spear fired hit Orsia's barrier. The barrier fluctuated and then disappeared. As soon as the magic bombardment ended, I used the last magic on her, who was off guard.

,Psychic Punch.'

A sense of relief that the magic bombardment was over, an invisible attack. She felt the psychokinesis at the last moment, but she couldn't react in time and allowed the attack. The psychic punch hits her lower abdomen. The area beyond that is where her uterus would be.

,Kehek...!"

With a somewhat mismatched scream, her knees fell to the floor.
