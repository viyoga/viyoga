Elena, who had been sitting leaning against a rock, jumped up from her spot.

A one-eyed giant walked out from the black pillar. It was Balor. Balor had descended upon Atlantis.

It's not a false god. If he was going to descend as just a false god, he wouldn't have used such a troublesome method, and Samuel, the boss of Helten, wouldn't have even been interested in this.

Balor didn't seem to be in a normal state. The system's sanctions were being applied to him.

,I understand that Balor has descended as his true self. But... why, exactly?

From a god's perspective, there was no need to risk the system's sanctions and the crisis of divine annihilation.

Atlantis is just a playground for the gods. There's no need to deliberately throw away their absolute position.

,Elena. Do you know the difference between a god and a human?

Samuel asked. His voice, focused on Balor, was strangely excited.

,The differences are excessively numerous.

,Power.

,Are you talking about authority?

,That too, and you could say it's the absolute power that a god possesses. Other than that, they're not much different from humans. It's just that their power and individuality are excessively strong.

,...That power is absolute, so they are gods.

Elena seemed to know what Samuel was trying to say.

,The proof that gods aren't perfect is this world. They couldn't stand the boredom and created this giant playground called Atlantis.

,And we're the gods' toys moving around in that playground. ...It's disgusting.

,I agree, Elena. Do you think all the numerous gods enjoy the playground equally?

,Those gods? No way.

,That's right. Some gods don't pay any attention, some want to ruin the game on purpose, and some bet all their assets.

,...Is Balor the third type?

,No. The fourth type. He went all in, lost everything, and even bet his own life on the game board. Gambling addiction is a word that applies to gods too.

This Atlantis is the 8th one. There were 7 previous Atlantises, and they've already ended. Balor seems to have taken a big loss in the last Atlantis.

,I understand that Balor's goal is to sweep up the stakes placed on Atlantis. But isn't the boss going to step in? It's a chance to kill a god.

,It's a rare opportunity, so it's a shame to end it like this. I'll observe until it gets dangerous. The important thing isn't Balor, but the gods who are looking down from up there. And there's still something that bothers me.

,...Something that bothers you?

,There's a high possibility that another god is involved. It's too big of a job for Balor to handle alone. The problem is, I don't know who that god is. It's obvious they won't show themselves... I wish I could get even a clue.

,Hmm. I'm going to step in directly.

,Do as you please. I've already confirmed most of what I needed to, so I won't stop you.

Elena turned her head away from Samuel and White, who were quietly sitting and watching the situation. Judging by their expressions, it seemed they really weren't going to step in.

Observe until it gets dangerous? Elena couldn't know where that standard was.

•••

I swallowed hard, looking up at the giant.

The one-eyed god. Balor of the Evil Eye. A god has descended into this world.

The situation is the worst. Just as I was about to make that judgment, Balor's huge body faltered. I didn't miss that moment.

,The system's sanctions are working properly. And he's just descended, so he hasn't fully adapted to Atlantis yet.

There might be a much better chance than I thought.

I gripped the sword hilt with both hands and swung it towards Balor, who was more than 30 meters away. A blue slash containing Red thunder struck Balor's body.

Psh. The giant's skin split and blood splattered. From my perspective, it was enough blood for two or three people, but from the giant's perspective, it was just a slight bleeding.

Balor, who had been staggering, straightened up, putting strength into his legs. He lifted his heavy eyelids and looked at me with his red eyes.

Flint.

My body trembled on its own.

,He doesn't die. Is it because he's already dead....

Balor's voice echoed in the space. It was a voice similar to the wave of death that the black pillar was emitting.

,...Is Qing Guoli dead?

,Qing Guoli?

Balor stroked his wound with his own hand. The wound healed in an instant. It wasn't a natural recovery.

,Ah, are you talking about the human who summoned me? If it's that human, isn't he right in front of you? He will live forever as a part of my body.

,What happened to Qing Guoli's soul?

,You seem to have a bad head. I clearly said it. He will live forever as a part of my body.

,......

Qing Guoli was eaten. You could see it that way. Qing Guoli was probably backstabbed by Balor. I don't know what kind of deal Qing Guoli made with Balor, but... Qing Guoli trusted Balor too much. It's not like all gods value contracts, you know.

,I feel a gaze.

Balor raised his chin towards the sky.

,Gods, are you watching? I, Balor, am here. I will build my foundation anew here. I will scrape together the source power, and I will make this world my territory and take a new leap forward.

「The owner of the sky laughs.」

「The fallen star cackles.」

「The shining spear is silent.」

,Don't forget! I remember the past humiliation! I will definitely repay the humiliation!

Balor let out a roar.

Beyond the eardrum-bursting noise, there was a power that instinctively made the listener flinch.

Balor's gaze is fixed on me. Despite being attacked by me, he has no emotion towards me.

,The beginning will be you, human.

I summoned Stormbreaker. I held it in one hand in the form of a great spear and took a throwing stance. I even gave the spear Astrape. The spear crackled with lightning.

I threw the spear, aiming for Balor's eye. The spear flew like lightning, but stopped like a lie in front of the giant's eye. It felt like time had stopped.

,The guys who fought me always aimed for my eye. At first, it was annoying, but I decided to understand. For them, my gaze must be fear itself.

Balor flicked his index finger at the spear that was stopped in mid-air. The spear flew in the wrong direction and stuck into the ground. I groaned.

,...I used quite a bit of power for that attack, but it's blocked so futilely?

Mana surged from Balor.

Magic.

Magic of a much higher dimension than the magic used by mages.

,I already knew that my gaze doesn't work on the dead. My eyes are dangerous, but their limitations were clear. So I pondered. How can I overcome the limitations and kill the dead? It was a stupid pondering. If I can't kill with my gaze alone... I just have to kill with physical force.

Balor's pupils blazed. A red beam shot out from his eyes, aiming for me.

Surprised, I hurriedly dodged, taking advantage of the moment. Balor's beam melted the sandy ground. The sand turned red and boiled.

,The destructive power is out of the ordinary. If that beam touches my body even once, it's over.

I used the power of Tutankhamun's golden mask. As Qing Guoli disappeared, the nearby undead returned to their wild state. I dominated those undead and made them charge at Balor.

,Things that smell like corpses. I used to look for ways to dominate you. But what can mere corpses do? Disappear without dirtying my territory.

The undead, who don't know death, didn't feel fear even against a god. The red beam swept through the undead. The undead's bodies burst into flames. They evaporated without even leaving ashes. But the undead army still had many left.

Balor swung his arm as if chasing away annoying bugs. The Death Knight flew more than 100 meters and crashed into the ground. The Death Knight, whose armor was shattered, couldn't get up again.

,The Death Knight is one-shot.

I clicked my tongue and used the Illusiter to make my body transparent and secretly approached Balor. Balor's arrogance was a great help.

I arrived under the giant. Seeing him up close made me feel much more pressured than seeing him from afar.

,He didn't notice.

I met the conditions for a surprise attack.

Balor was showing off his power, smashing the undead army. Balor's movements gradually became smoother. He was adapting to that body.

,Timing is important to see the maximum effect of a surprise attack.

I quietly waited for the optimal timing to come. That moment soon came. When he had smashed half of the undead army with overwhelming power, Balor's tension eased a little, and he closed his eyelids as if to give his eyes, which had become hot from shooting the beams, a rest.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

The ultimate quick sword that I can use is activated. A red sword light left a trail in the air and cut off Balor's right ankle.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!

Balor screamed and fell forward. Blood poured out from his severed right ankle.

,How dare a human! Not knowing your place!!

Balor's mana surged. I quickly retreated. Flames soared from the ground. The flames spread in all directions. My skin turned red even though the flames didn't directly touch my body.

The ground touched by the fire was being eroded into black ground.

,...No. Strictly speaking, it's just returning to its original state.

I looked around and checked my forces. Less than half of the undead army remained. Even now, they were engulfed in the burning flames and their numbers were decreasing. The wild undead were running towards Balor. Balor's huge life force must have stimulated the undead.

,I have to deal with Balor when there are undead. The odds aren't high anyway, and if there are no undead, it's over.

Balor, who lost his right ankle, got up. His right foot was still cut off. He was supporting his body with only one left foot. He opened his eyes, which had been closed. His eyes were bloodshot, as if he would shed tears of blood at any moment.

,I never thought I would receive such humiliation from a mere human.... I admit it. You are not an ordinary human, but a hero. I feel the fate of a hero in you. But that fate will crumble here.

I summoned the spear and threw it, aiming for his eye. It didn't work. The spear was blocked by an invisible wall and bounced off.

,...It's not magic. An enormous amount of mana surged through his body and bounced the spear off.

It could be called a kind of protective aura. However, it's not a protective aura that Balor intended. It's just a phenomenon that occurred in the process of using mana.

The enormous mana gathered in the air and took the shape of eyes. Twelve red eyes, identical to Balor's, appeared in the air. Together with Balor's, a total of 13 Evil Eyes (死眼) fired red beams toward the black earth.

The earth burned. The undead melted. The 13 beams devastated the surrounding area.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

I stepped on Cheonma Gunrimbo amidst the indiscriminate bombing and dodged the beams. But even that soon reached its limit. The problem was that the land where the beams had passed once was red-hot, in a state similar to lava. There was no place to set foot.

,Damn it...!

It's impossible to get close and fight. The area around Balor is already boiling. Unless I have resistance to heat, my body will melt the moment I go there. The only way is to attack from a distance, but the spear throw with all my power doesn't work.

,Is there no way? Modern weapons are only meaningful when dealing with noobs. They're meaningless when dealing with Balor. Another way....

Thirteen beams aimed at me and flew from all directions. It was already too late to deal with them with Cheonma Gunrimbo. The full power of Cheonma Gunrimbo is only exerted when the surrounding space is controlled. But the space touched by those beams easily eliminates my control. With beams in all directions, Cheonma Gunrimbo is blocked.

,I was prepared to die once... but I thought I would take at least one of his arms.

It was then. A blue butterfly that didn't fit the situation fluttered towards me.
