It took ten minutes of flight to reach Geoje Island at the southern tip of the Korean Peninsula. I could have moved faster, but I didn't bother. Enjoying the scenery while flying had its own charm.

I leaped down from the midslope of Garasan. The police and hunters who had Garasan under lockdown watched me with wary expressions. I ignored them and looked around.

'I landed where a lot of people are gathered. Don't see Jin Seyeong, though.'

The surroundings stirred with murmurs. Their wariness noticeably faded.

Police and hunters alike had recognized my face.

South Korea's youngest S-rank hunter, Seong Yujin.

If they didn't recognize me, they'd have to be spies. No—even spies would recognize me.

While they were stealing glances at each other, agonizing over how to treat me, a middle-aged man came shoving through their ranks, huffing and puffing.

A short buzz cut, an angular face. A man with a menacing impression and skin tanned brown.

He wore a white shirt and suit trousers. Through the fabric of his shirt, a purple cloud tattoo covering his entire upper body was faintly visible.

Anyone could tell he looked like a gangster—he was Gu Seonggwang, master of the Jaun Guild.

"Oh my, Hunter Seong Yujin! What brings you all the way out here?!"

We were acquainted. But we'd never had a serious conversation. He'd been among the group when I'd put the top-ten guilds in line not long ago. His eyes trembled uneasily.

"I heard a meteor fell and came out of curiosity. Am I in the way?"

"In the way?! Not at all! You were curious about the meteor! Of course you would be. Yes. Allow me to escort you, Hunter Seong Yujin!"

His attitude was far too servile for someone called a guild master. One might wonder if an S-rank should have to grovel like this before a twenty-four-year-old kid.

'If I hadn't put them in line recently, he probably wouldn't have.'

Gu Seonggwang had witnessed Hwang Chiseung of the Goryeo Guild—hailed as South Korea's undisputed greatest guild—get beaten like a dog on a summer slaughter day. He knew that if things went sideways, the Jaun Guild and everything else could go down with him at my hands. Would he dare act out?

"F-First, let's go inside. The meteor that fell this time isn't an ordinary one. I'll brief you separately inside."

"Is that so."

I followed Gu Seonggwang into a barracks. It was empty and tidy inside, but it reeked of cigarette smoke. I immediately furrowed my brow, sat down on the prepared chair, and propped my feet up on the table.

Gu Seonggwang, watching my reaction, couldn't even sit in the chair across from me and stood at attention. Cold sweat beaded thickly on his forehead.

"Hey. Gu Seonggwang."

"Yes. Yes. Hunter Seong Yujin."

"What's with the cigarette smell? You trying to piss me off the moment I walk in?"

"I-I'm sorry! I'll ventilate it right away!"

"Forget it. You know I'm from the Yeongcheon Sword Hall, right?"

"O-Of course. Woosung bragged so much about how a blockage cleared in his martial vein. You have no idea how envious I was hearing it."

Woosung.

Who's that again.

After racking my brain for a moment, I realized he meant Jin Woosung. Jin Seyeong's father and the headmaster of the Yeongcheon Sword Hall.

"Ah. The headmaster. You close with him?"

"We went to the same high school! Even before I founded the Jaun Guild, it was just me and Woosung, the two of us."

"Enough with the sentimental crap. You know Seyeong's noona, right?"

"Seyeong? She's like a niece to me."

"She's my Yeongcheon-style teacher. Take good care of her. If word reaches my ears that Seyeong's noona is being treated unfairly..."

I let a hint of killing intent seep out as a warning.

The difference in magnitude contained within that killing intent. Gu Seonggwang felt it, and his face turned ashen blue.

"Th-That will never happen!"

"Good. Let's keep things smooth. So I never have to convene the top-ten guild masters again."

Having said my piece, I rose from my seat.

"Y-You're leaving right away? The b-briefing..."

"You want me to sit in this reeking cigarette hole and listen to your briefing?"

"N-No, that's not it!"

"Does a meteor even need a briefing? I'll just go see it. Ah, Seyeong's noona is here, right?"

"Seyeong is monitoring the meteor together with Woosung. Ah, it's not just us—there are also hunters dispatched by the Association."

It was only natural, given the World Hunter Association would be paying attention to this matter.

Following him toward where the meteor had fallen, I ran into an unexpected figure.

"Park Suho?"

"Yujin hyung?"

I'd run into Park Suho, who was manning one position in the encirclement.

"Why are you here?"

"Me? I came to clear a dungeon nearby and got asked by the Association to help out."

"A guy who lives in Seoul came all the way here to clear a dungeon?"

"You must not know since you haven't been going into dungeons lately, hyung, but there's no dungeon in Seoul worth calling a honey pot. Open-type dungeons can be entered anytime, but what you can get from them is limited. Clear dungeons in the provinces and you get extra pay on top. Commuting's a bit of a pain, but you definitely earn more money."

Despite having skills on par with an A-rank hunter, Park Suho seemed strapped for cash. Probably investing every penny he earned into his own territory in the tattoo world. I understood. That was how Park Suho got stronger.

"But weren't you a guild master? What about An Sihyeon—you're moving around alone?"

"Sihyeon doesn't like going outside Seoul."

"Other guild members? Still haven't recruited any?"

"I interviewed a few candidates, but none of them really clicked with me. Running a guild is tough in its own way, you know."

Park Suho, who'd been grumbling, shifted his gaze to my side. There sat Gu Seonggwang, the master of Jaun Guild, quietly watching the room.

"Ah. You're the Jaun Guild master, aren't you? I'm Park Suho, master of the newly founded Lumen Ark."

"...Pleasure to meet you, Master Park Suho. I'm Gu Seonggwang. It seems you're quite close with Hunter Seong Yujin."

"Close? You could say that."

The two shook hands.

It was a little funny, seeing how Park Suho was subtly dropping my name and looking so proud of it.

Moving deeper inside, where the meteor was visible, I spotted Jin Seyeong and Jin Useong.

Jin Seyeong, seeing her after so long, was unchanged. Her waist-length hair was tied back in one bundle, and she wore a tracksuit that was easy to move in. The tracksuit fit her snugly, showing off the firm lines of her body. Her hips were lifted and round, and her C-cup breasts looked taut and full.

Beside her stood Jin Useong, a middle-aged man with an eyepatch over his left eye. He was dressed in a tracksuit as well.

Both of them widened their eyes when they saw me. Jin Seyeong especially looked startled, then waved at me. Just as she opened her mouth to call my name—

Kuuung.

A mana-laden pulse rippled out from the meteor.

I turned my head toward the meteor at the center of the crater. Half-buried in the ground, it was quite large—over three meters.

'Something that big fell, and the crater isn't even ten meters across. I don't know the physics, but shouldn't a bigger meteor mean a bigger impact?'

They say meteors burn up and vanish in the atmosphere, so if this one actually hit the ground, how big must it have been originally?

'...No, wait. That thing wasn't an ordinary meteor to begin with. What kind of meteor emits mana waves?'

And not just any mana waves—strong enough to put A-rank hunters on edge.

I examined the meteor again. Aside from its surface being a dark purple, it looked like any ordinary meteor.

Kuuung.

A stronger mana pulse than before burst out. As if it were alive.

"Suho. Was that thing always emitting waves like that?"

"It's been doing it periodically since I got here... but the waves seem to have gotten stronger since you arrived, hyung. Is it reacting to you?"

My reunion with Jin Seyeong would have to wait. I walked toward the meteor that was bothering me first.

Kuuuung!

A stronger pulse than before burst out. Just as Park Suho said, it was reacting to me.

'Is it wary of me?'

I grew curious about what was inside that meteor.

The distance between me and the meteor closed.

Kwajik. Kwajijik. Kwajijik!

The meteor cracked and splintered all over. From the fissures, a gas—smoke or vapor, I couldn't tell—leaked out. My poison resistance didn't react, so it wasn't poison.

Sshk!

From the cracked openings, creatures that looked like withered, shriveled medicinal herbs burst out. The moment they emerged, they began swelling in size.

'Looks like they're absorbing moisture and growing larger.'

Like a compressed mattress expanding rapidly the moment you unseal it.

Things that had been smaller than my forearm had grown to two meters before I knew it.

They had two legs and two arms like humans, and their chitinous skin looked smooth as metal yet gleamed with luster. They had tails, and the tails were sharp as blades.

On their pitch-black heads, three yellow eyes glinted. The one in front of me thrust its neck forward, pushing its head right into my face. The three pupils stared at me as if observing.

'So this is the alien lifeform the Association mentioned—the Xenorage, another invasive species.'

As I furrowed my brow, the lower part of its head split wide open, revealing sharp teeth, a tongue covered in barbs, and a throat from which a shriek rang out along with a stench.

"Kieeeeeeeeeek!"

"Kieeeeeeeeeek!"

"Kieeeeeeeek!"

When one let out a sound that was either a scream or a roar, the others around it cried out in resonance.

Seven in total.

They were alien creatures that had crawled out of the meteor. The power and presence they gave off rivaled A-rank monsters.

"Loud and reeking."

I clenched my fist.

Bzzzt! Blue lightning sparked from my knuckles.

The Xenorage rolled its three eyes and reacted.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 11]

My fist, accelerated by Flash Step, slammed into the head of the Xenorage right in front of me. The Xenorage burst like a watermelon. Its brain—far smaller than a human's—blue blood, and black bones all shattered at once, scattering across the ground.

The headless body crumpled limply to the floor.

"Kyaaaaaak!"

Another Xenorage charged at me with a roar of fury.

It lowered its stance and aimed for my legs. In its hand gleamed a black blade. A blade made of bone. Now that I looked closer, these things' bodies were similar yet different from one another.

Thunder Heaven Style—Gale Thunder.

Lightning erupted from my entire body as I accelerated.

I kicked the head of the one aiming for my legs, bursting its skull and killing it.

Presences felt from my back and my right.

'Discharge.'

Lightning branched out in every direction from where I stood, electrocuting two of them and cooking them until they went limp. The remaining three pretended to lunge at me before abruptly pivoting and sprinting toward the sea. Membranous wings fluttered from their backs.

"Shit!"

"We have to stop them!"

The A-rank Hunters who'd been watching my fight in a daze reacted too late. They hadn't expected the bastards to bolt without even glancing back.

I raised my hand. Lightning gathered and split into three elongated spears.

There was no need to throw them.

The three spears of lightning, responding to my will, tore through the air and struck the three fleeing Xenorages dead-on. The electrocuted alien creatures dropped instantly, dead on impact.

The whole thing was over in ten seconds.
