Pajijijik!

I wrapped my entire Body in black lightning and kicked off into the air, charging towards Mahir. Kwang! The mere aftershock caused a shockwave to erupt in the air.

I appeared before Mahir in the blink of an eye. I stood on the surface of the sea without falling in, taking a stance to swing my Fist.

The sea I stood upon undulated. Black seawater surged from the sea, forming tentacles that rushed towards me.

‘Release.’

I released black lightning along with my protective aura.

The black lightning, spreading in all directions, collided with the black seawater tentacles, causing an explosion.

Cheonma Shingong Dragon Fist.

It was different from the long-range attack I launched earlier.

This time, my Fist definitely reached Mahir’s solar plexus. The dragon-shaped Demonic Energy, released from my Fist, pierced through Mahir’s torso, then sped across the sea before vanishing.

However, Mahir’s expression didn’t change in the slightest. From his gaping torso, black seawater flowed instead of blood.

The black seawater touched my Hand.

I felt its weight.

It was power. It strongly constricted my Hand. It was sharp as a Sword and tried to seep in like poison.

I retreated and shook off the black seawater clinging to my Hand. Part of the skin on my Hand transformed. It almost became that of a fish.

Cheonma Shingong Adamant Magic Dragon.

I protected my Body with the power of the strongest Magic Dragon. My transforming right Hand returned to a human Hand.

“A power that turns humans into fish, huh. Quite a disgusting power.”

“You’re kicking away the chance to become a resident of the sea yourself. All things began in the sea, so the true Mother of all things is not the land, but the sea. Do not reject the Mother’s embrace. If you accept it, it will be more comfortable than anything else.”

“If it were a pretty Mother, maybe. But I don’t need a fishy-smelling Mother.”

Cheonma Shingong Severing Demonic Dragon.

A black blade plunged into Mahir’s Head. Mahir staggered for the first time.

The Severing Demonic Dragon’s blade had damaged his Soul. But that alone couldn’t kill Mahir. Mahir’s Body regenerated rapidly.

Hwareureuk.

I threw the black flames burning in my Hand at Mahir.

Cheonma Shingong Black Flame Magic Dragon.

Mahir’s Body burst into flames. Steam constantly rose, but Mahir was unharmed, not even letting out a Scream. Massive black seawater flowed from his Body, engulfing and extinguishing the black flames.

Cheonma Shingong Sakpungmaryong Gwangpungnyeong.

Dozens of black winds swept over Mahir. Mahir, whose entire Body had been shredded, stood as if nothing had happened once the black winds vanished.

Cheonma Shingong Poison Cloud Magic Dragon Cheonbyeonmandok.

The poison mist seeped into the sea. The sea showed no reaction whatsoever. A mere handful of poison compared to the sea could never poison it.

“Ha.”

A hollow laugh escaped me.

I had a feeling, but no attack worked.

‘At least the Severing Demonic Dragon had some effect… but he recovers immediately after a while. It feels like fighting a punching bag or a scarecrow. …No. Even punching bags and scarecrows eventually break when hit.’

It feels like I’m fighting the sea itself.

My Head spun rapidly. Among the answers that emerged, a few were:

‘The System would never allow a mere human such power. If it did, there must be a way to overcome it.’

There’s a high probability of a gimmick I don’t know about.

‘Perhaps enduring is the answer, not defeating him. Or his very source is located elsewhere.’

Chwaaaaaak!

Black seawater tentacles surged from various parts of the sea. Hundreds of tentacles were aimed solely at me.

Cheonma Shingong Cheonma Gunrimbo Wave Form.

The energy waves of Cheonma Gunrimbo spread, bursting the tentacles. However, the tentacles, whose essence was merely seawater, regenerated again.

Fighting like this would only exhaust me in the end.

‘…I currently have two spare lives. One is complete recovery, the other is the Black Ankh. And I also have the Water’s Blessing, which allows me to breathe underwater.’

Having made my decision, I immediately put it into action.

Pungdeong!

I plunged into the sea.

Thanks to the effect of the ‘Water’s Blessing’ skill, I feel comfortable when I’m underwater. I can also manipulate the flow of water to some extent.

But not now.

I was clearly breathing, yet I felt suffocated. I hadn’t even gone deep into the sea, yet I felt intense water pressure. Even using my Heavenly Eye, it was difficult to see through the sea.

I used the Water’s Blessing. I tried to manipulate the sea’s current to make my Body float upwards. But it was useless. My Body slowly sank deeper into the sea. It was dark below, and I couldn’t see anything.

“You’ve plunged into the embrace of the sea.”

Even underwater, Mahir’s voice was clearly audible.

“Behold the great City at the bottom of the deep sea. The City shall be your cradle, for the sea, the stars, and chaos are your Nanny.”

Deep sea.

Less than a few tens of seconds after descending, I was in the deep sea. And I was still sinking.

The darkness that obscured my eyes lifted. It was still dark, but I could distinguish objects.

As Mahir said, there was a City in the deep sea. It was filled with bizarrely twisted structures. Merfolk appeared throughout the City, swimming up to the surface.

「Entered ???? Sector.」

「Abnormal access.」

「Cannot receive Sector entry reward.」

A towering stone pillar was visible. No, I couldn’t tell if it was stone. It merely resembled stone on the surface.

At the top of the stone pillar was a familiar human.

Mahir.

The Sage of the Desert sat atop the stone pillar, his eyes closed.

His aura was completely different from the one on the sea surface. I instinctively knew this was his true Body.

I knew that was his true Body. If I killed that, Mahir would surely die.

After arriving in this deep-sea City, I was able to properly use my power, so I operated Cheonma Shingong.

Cheonma Shingong Pajeonhwang.

As I shattered and burned my own Soul, immense power erupted within me. I swept back my now white Hair once and took a step forward in the sea.

Cheonma Shingong Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Immense pressure bore down on this City.

The entire Bodies of the deep-sea dwellers burst apart. However, the City itself was unharmed. It effortlessly withstood pressure that would normally cause any City to collapse. I realized that this City itself was protected by something.

‘Is it something even I, using Pajeonhwang now, cannot deal with?’

It’s a Sector that didn’t even appear in the Original Work. I couldn’t tell exactly what it was.

‘Kill him and get out of here. The rest can wait.’

I had long since given up on exploring this City, this unknown Sector.

“You saw this City, yet you didn’t dedicate yourself? Did you reject the truth? I cannot understand. Are you afraid of madness? Normal People call it madness, but they only call it madness because they don’t understand it. Behold the City. And witness the truth.”

“I’m tired of listening to your bullshit. Now die.”

A fierce ocean current engulfed me. It contained an incomprehensible power, so I couldn’t resist and was swept away.

Mahir, having bought a little time, rose from atop the stone pillar. Instantly, his Body transformed.

All the hair on his Body fell out, and his eyes grew larger. Fish scales grew on his skin. Even his Body grew larger.

In an instant, he became a deep-sea dweller over 10 meters tall. It wasn’t just his size that increased. The nearby ocean currents were moving around him. The stone pillar glowed faintly with a violet light, and the entire City pulsed and writhed.

The City was pulsating around the monster he had become.

‘If I drag this out, I’ll be in danger.’

I had no intention of wasting time. Why else would I have used Pajeonhwang?

Cheonma Shingong Severing Demonic Dragon Dosangeomsu.

Tens of thousands of black blades appeared on his Body. The black blades vanished before I could even blink. His Body was already Slashed to pieces. Fragments of his Flesh became bubbles and clung to me.

Cheonma Shingong Sakpungmaryong.

I tried to create a wind to burst the bubbles and push them away. The bubbles completely ignored me and approached. I was instantly surrounded by bubbles. The bubbles held me captive. I couldn’t escape.

I glared at the bubbles. Something was reflected on the surface of the bubbles.

‘Something I’ve never seen before. …Mahir? Is it Mahir’s memory?’

It was a third-person perspective, too, to be a memory.

Each bubble reflected a part of Mahir’s life. Honestly, I wasn’t particularly interested. An enemy. And a man’s life at that, what did I care? It was just that the bubbles wouldn’t let me go, so I had no choice but to watch.

Mahir was a man born in Israel. As a doctor’s son, he followed an elite path. Then he was exiled and arrived in Atlantis.

‘…He received help from the Black Pharaoh, huh. What did the Black Pharaoh see in this bastard?’

I couldn’t tell. However, if the Black Pharaoh’s true identity was what I thought it was, it was highly likely to be a mere whim.

With the knowledge he gained from the Black Pharaoh, Mahir became a Grand Spellcaster known as the Sage of the Desert. The only price was occasionally suffering from nightmares.

He met the Emperor and joined Hands with Shahram. Surprisingly, his Relationship with Shahram wasn’t deep. Mahir wanted to escape this tiresome desert.

Jibril, Gabriel, had been influencing the desert’s Spellcasters and Assassins for quite some time. However, Mahir, possessing the Black Pharaoh’s knowledge, did not blindly follow Gabriel like the others.

‘Having failed repeatedly to kill me, Mahir, cornered, sought out the Black Pharaoh again.’

As Mahir wished, the Black Pharaoh imparted wicked knowledge. It was knowledge that allowed him to meet the Crouching Sea.

Mahir confronted the Crouching Sea in his dream. Not the fake of this world, but the true monster of the sea.

In his dream, he went mad. Through these bubbles, I could see what he saw in his madness. Most of it was monsters and a distorted world.

‘To go mad from seeing such trivial things. How boring.’

The truth Mahir spoke of? Honestly, I couldn’t understand it at all.

The bubbles sank into the City.

Mahir became a Submissive of the Crouching Sea in exchange for power. Now, even in death, he would pay the price.

‘Did you want to show me this kind of shit? To drive me mad?’

Unfortunately, as long as I possess my absolute Mind, I will not go mad.

「Death Resistance activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.」

The effect of Pajeonhwang ended, and I met my death.

Just as I was about to use complete recovery, I felt my existence being pulled into the depths of the deep sea.

It was right before my eyes.

A colossal deep-sea dweller, crouching.

Its colossal eyes, larger than my Body, stared intently at me.

「Unauthorized intervention detected!」

「The System protects you!」

「Close your eyes!」

I did not close my eyes.

I faced the ancient entity.

Did it see something in me? It closed its eyes.

「Attempting emergency escape!」

The space abruptly shifted.

I was floating on the sea like a Corpse.

‘Since I’m dead, I suppose I am a Corpse.’

「Using complete recovery.」
