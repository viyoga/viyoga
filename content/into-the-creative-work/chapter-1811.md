The gazes around me turn towards me. People looked at me with tense faces, their eyes filled with hope for a miracle. Baekran was already freeing the restrained martial artists. Thanks to that, it felt like the spotlight was only on me.

I dusted off my bloodless sword once and said to them.

,We fell into their trap."

,Does Daein-nim know exactly what's happening here now?"

Someone asked me.

,I don't know exactly. One thing is certain: these bastards are trying to do something with us."

,Yoo-Woon Commerce... those damn bastards. We shouldn't have looked for such a suspicious commerce from the start...!"

,It's too late to regret it. You have two choices. Either stay sharp and fight those Yoo-Woon Commerce bastards, or do nothing and just wait for death. Fortunately, we're not alone."

The martial artists' eyes darted around. They looked at each other, then soon picked up their weapons.

,Daein-nim is right. We're already in the tiger's mouth, so we need to stay sharp."

,We can't just die like this."

,If Daein-nims are with us... Yoo-Woon Commerce or whatever, they won't be able to harm us."

They ignited their will to fight. I grinned and said to them.

,There are things we need to do first. Catch those merchants and kill them."

The martial artists' eyes gleamed as they looked for opportunities to run away or hide, chasing after the merchants and swinging their weapons. The merchants who had learned martial arts resisted, but they couldn't handle ten hands with one. They weren't masters like me.

Taking advantage of the moment when attention was diverted from me, I squatted in front of a corpse and rummaged through its pockets. Then I clicked my tongue.

,A master only had seven Cheonok? What a beggar."

There was no magical artifact either. When one reaches the realm of Ogi, it's basic to carry at least one magical artifact. That was a bit strange.

,Either he was incredibly confident in his skills, or... he couldn't afford a magical artifact."

As I got up, Baekran approached me.

,You. Are those swords you showed earlier your hidden card?"

,That's right. It's the power of the magical artifact I have."

,Is it okay to show your trump card so easily?"

,Do you know the saying 'akkiddong'?"

,...I don't know. That's the first time I've heard that word. Is it perhaps the name of a spell?"

,It means 'if you hoard it, it becomes shit.' You have to use it when you need to, no matter what it is."

,Hmm. It's a somewhat cheap word, but it's not wrong. What are you going to do now?"

,I need to kill the bastards like before and join the martial artists. It's more advantageous for us if our numbers grow."

,It doesn't seem that easy. The enemies seem to have noticed the change."

The martial artists made a commotion while killing the merchants. It would be stranger if the enemies nearby didn't notice.

,So we need to increase our numbers before those bastards gather."

I stepped forward in front of the martial artists. The corpses of the dead merchants caught my eye. The corpses had already been looted.

,There's no time, so I'll have to collect them later."

I shouted to the martial artists.

,Let's go!"

Chamgwi Sword Technique (斬鬼刀法) Gwisal Theater (鬼殺劇).

Demonic energy exploded from me. My muscles expanded and I was overflowing with power. The blood gushing from my chest also stopped due to the muscles.

Either I die, or the bastard dies. It's all or nothing.

Jiiiiiiiiing!

It's a Goheoldan (膏血丹). Is this your first time seeing it?

Goheoldan. I knew what this was. If you eat Goheoldan, you can recover from external and internal injuries in an instant. In simple terms, it's a fantasy potion. Of course, there are limits to treatment.

I used a method of exploding demonic energy and using powerful force in an instant. The advantage of this combat method is that it's fast and powerful, but the disadvantage is that the giheol is slightly damaged. In other words, it becomes more dangerous as the battle gets longer.
