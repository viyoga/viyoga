Alcheri's light aircraft were already right next to us. They even had weapons attached. If they attacked or pretended to crash, we would have no choice but to die.

"We won't die. If they were going to blow up this plane, they would have done it already. They're after Amy's map. They won't blow up the plane, but if we get captured, the end is obvious. Mac!"

"Yeah. What can I do for you?"

Mac moved busily and spoke. He threw parachute bags to me and Amy. While putting on the parachute bag, I looked at Amy. Her face was full of tension.

"Ladies and gentlemen. Please understand that the driving will be a bit rough from now on!"

It wasn't just a bit rough. The plane rotated 360 degrees or even tilted to the side. No amusement park ride could compare to this.

"Keuk...."

"Now! Jump out right now!"

Bruno went out first, followed by Amy. Next was me, and finally Mac jumped out. Since we were being chased, we shouldn't deploy the parachute until we reached a precarious point. I should follow Bruno's lead on when to deploy the parachute.

Kwaaaang!

A loud noise rang out.

It was the sound of the private jet crashing into a cliff and exploding spectacularly. I could see Alcheri's light aircraft panicking. Well, it was clear that they would soon be tracking us.

We quickly joined up after descending into the jungle via parachute.

"If we stay here, the Alcheri bastards will chase us and kill us. We don't have the luxury of sending a rescue request and waiting for rescue. We have to move as quickly as possible."

"Here it is. Bruno-ssi."

"We can't assume that there's only one map. Maybe the contents of the map have been leaked and the Alcheri bastards know about it. It would be better to go around a bit. Let's move."

'There's automatic progress, but I have to work on Amy myself to get laid.'

"Grrrrr."

Bang!
