A red mass of light streaks across the sky.

Mana is felt. It was definitely an attack magic from the Magic Federation. That red energy mass falls in an arc towards the mansion.

The mass of light collides with the transparent barrier wall protecting Tevra.

,,,It seems there's a slight problem with the barrier. Normally, it wouldn't shake from an attack of that magnitude."

Tevra's barrier was one that she, the Grand Archmage, had installed with a great deal of time and money. It was sturdy enough to withstand even a few dragon breaths.

"If it's been bombarded for days, it's no wonder the barrier is shaking. It was a good thing we came to Tevra. If we had gone to the Alkairon Mountains or the Forest of Evil, Tevra would have been in danger."

Seven masses of light fall on the territory and explode. Fortunately, the barrier held, deflecting all the attacks.

I head outside the mansion, to the outer city area where the citizens live.

"There are no citizens."

"They'll be in the shelters built for emergencies."

"I guess it's a good thing there's nothing to get in the way."

Soldiers carrying supplies were visible. Maids with rifles slung over their shoulders glared at the soldiers from behind, monitoring them. In this situation, ordinary soldiers couldn't be trusted, so it was only natural.

In the distance, a tall and sturdy fortress wall is visible. After building the mansion, I had the dwarves, who were just loafing around, build the fortress wall because I hated seeing them idle. I poured money into it, determined to do it right.

'It's the sturdiest fortress wall on the continent.'

It was made with money, time, and the blood and sweat of artisans. It would be stranger if it wasn't sturdy.

The Golden Rose Knights and maids stood on the fortress wall. The knights seemed ready to jump out of the fortress wall at any moment, and the maids quietly gripped their machine guns, watching out for enemies. At their center stood Nephia, the Head maid, and Ellain, the wife of Ention Pruker and Kyle's biological mother.

They look at me. A murmuring sound is heard. Fortunately, the atmosphere is better than before. I climbed the fortress wall, receiving their expectant gazes.

Nephia's face was a mess. Dark circles were deeply set under her eyes, her hair was greasy, and her maid uniform was stained here and there.

She is the Head maid, following in Yuria's footsteps. There's no need to mention how outstanding her sincerity and abilities are. For her to neglect her self-care? It was inevitable.

"Master-nim. First, the report..."

"W, well, I haven't been able to wash for 5 days. Haa. I'm sorry. I know I shouldn't be like this as a Head maid..."

"D, don't cry. I'm not trying to scold you. Nephia-ssi has done well. I know that Tevra was able to be safe because of all the hard work Nephia-ssi has done. Now, let's go back to the mansion and get some rest. Master-nim has arrived, so you can rest assured now."

Yuria soothed Nephia and headed towards the mansion. Similar hair color and similar outfits. They looked like sisters.

I walked towards the railing of the fortress wall.

Countless knights, soldiers, and mages were surrounding the fortress wall.

'Roughly 50,000? I don't know where they gathered such a large army, but they're really determined.'

I take a closer look.

There were fewer knights compared to the size of the army. Knights are the flower of the battlefield. An army with few knights is not to be feared.

'That's the case in normal situations. This army has an abnormally large number of mages. If knights are the flower of the battlefield, then mages are the grim reapers of the battlefield.'

Just thinking about those enormous mages casting wide-area magic all at once makes even me feel a chill down my spine.

'What's the level of armament of the soldiers?'

I roughly scan them.

As is the case in this world, professional soldiers make up less than 30%. Most are slave soldiers or farmers.

'The level of armament is below average. They're the kind of guys who'll be swept away by machine guns.'

What needs to be noted are the guys mixed in among the soldiers. Their equipment is noticeably better than the other soldiers.

'Mercenaries. There are more mercenaries than knights. The Magic Tower has united... they're spending money like crazy. If they win this war, they can make up for it, huh?'

It's different from the era when Magic Towers were rampant. Now that the Magic Towers have united, the market will become a monopoly of the Magic Federation once I disappear. And with a dragon standing firmly behind them, they have nothing to fear.

'The level of the mercenaries... they'll be better than the average soldiers. I'll roughly consider them lower-level Experts... the real problem is the mages behind the soldiers.'

Kwaaaang!

Ellain seems to have realized the convenience of the mansion.
