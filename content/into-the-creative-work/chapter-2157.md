Lee Yebin liked her current life.

Even though her life was threatened by zombies and monsters, she could somehow survive. Parasites? They were annoying, but she didn't think they were extremely dangerous. They didn't have the kind of overwhelming power that monsters did. If she had reliable guards nearby, the parasites couldn't do anything to her.

And she had an ability. The ability to manipulate people. It didn't work well on women, and all it did was make her likable to some men... but most men couldn't take their eyes off her. They fell in love with her and were willing to give her their liver and gall bladder.

,My ability to make men love me. It didn't work on Seong Yujin-ssi because he's an Awakened... but other men love me.'

Lee Yebin didn't know that her ability was a pheromone ability. She just vaguely recognized it as an ability to make men love her.

Because of this ability, she was able to live a rich life even in a world that was half-destroyed. Men brought her luxury goods and food. Satisfying her sexual desires was no problem either. Turning young, handsome men into horny dogs was a piece of cake.

For her, Saetbyeol Apartment was her kingdom. Kang Sang-gi-ssi and Woo Tae-hyun-ssi, the resident representatives, were a bit annoying, but... in the end, they were also in the palm of her hand.

,My original plan was to make Seong Yujin-ssi my slave....'

If you looked at the internet or the news, there were constant reports about Seong Yujin-ssi. A psychic and hero who kills monsters. The people didn't know the truth about Seong Yujin-ssi and praised him.

,If my ability had worked on Seong Yujin-ssi, Seongak Elementary School would have been mine too.'

She had used her ability on Seong Yujin-ssi several times.

It seemed to work on the first day they met. But as time went on, it didn't work as well. She had just used her ability on Seong Yujin-ssi, but he hadn't reacted at all.

,It's not too late yet. Let's hold out in Saetbyeol Apartment. The government will take care of the monsters. After that, we'll get new residents. If I step in, the number of residents will increase as much as I want.'

In the worst case, she could just leave for another place.

Lee Yebin was confident that she could live well anywhere else.

Lee Yebin, who had come down to the first floor, looked back at Kang Sang-gi-ssi and Woo Tae-hyun-ssi. She used her ability. Unfortunately, these two weren't the type to fall completely for her. Still, they felt some affection for her. The fact that their stiff faces relaxed was proof of that.

,It's a shame to part ways like this. Shall we have dinner together for the first time in a while?'

Woo Tae-hyun-ssi openly showed his ulterior motives. He was aggressive, perhaps because he was young. She could read the sticky lust in his eyes. Lee Yebin swallowed a sneer. She knew that Woo Tae-hyun-ssi was hitting on Irina. Woo Tae-hyun-ssi's obsession was so strong that her ability didn't work well because of Irina. But now he was showing this reaction?

,Am I a second choice? Is he crazy?'

Woo Tae-hyun-ssi was disqualified. He didn't deserve to be her man. And there were plenty of men like Woo Tae-hyun-ssi.

,Let's have dinner later. You know the atmosphere isn't good right now.'

,That's true. Building 101 is having a hard time. Irina's absence is especially noticeable. There are already people saying, 'What if a monster comes into the apartment?'"

There had been a few times in the past when monsters had tried to enter the apartment. Each time, Irina had shot arrows to keep the monsters in check. It was also thanks to Irina that they were able to safely dispose of the zombies that had flooded the apartment and the surrounding area in the early stages of the crisis. Irina was a hero of Saetbyeol Apartment.

,We were close enough to call each other sisters... but she went and attached herself to Seong Yujin-ssi without saying a word to me. She acted all high and mighty on her own... she was a light woman after all. As soon as the opportunity came, she switched sides. She probably spread her legs for Seong Yujin-ssi too, huh? You bitch.'

It was a good thing. It was also an opportunity to dominate men who were so infatuated with Irina's beauty that her ability didn't work well, like Woo Tae-hyun-ssi.

,Irina's matter can't be helped. You guys in Building 101 tried to kill Irina.'

,We didn't try to kill her. If you guys had just voted properly...! Irina would have been safe and grateful to me!"

,Don't raise your voice! Where do you think you are, raising your voice?! Don't pass your mistakes on to us!"

,My mistake? You're the ones who didn't keep your promise!!"

The atmosphere between Woo Tae-hyun-ssi and Kang Sang-gi-ssi became fierce. Building 101 and Building 103. They still didn't get along. If Lee Yebin wasn't there, they wouldn't even be facing each other like this.

,Calm down, both of you. That's not the problem right now. Irina's matter is regrettable, but it's already in the past. We need to think about the future. Right?'

,...Yebin-ssi is right. We need to think about the future. So, about that... shouldn't we just kill Seong Yujin-ssi? We can do it if we join forces. And then we can get Irina back. I'll persuade Irina.'

Woo Tae-hyun-ssi still hadn't given up his obsession with Irina. The way he was grinding his teeth made it seem like it had gotten even worse. Lee Yebin felt disgusted by Woo Tae-hyun-ssi, but she didn't show it and continued to speak.

,Now is not the time for that. What we need to do right now is to overcome the crisis that has struck Saetbyeol Apartment. The most urgent thing is the parasites.'

,...I guess so. Those bastards kill people every day.'

,It seems like there aren't many parasites in Building 101....'

,We need to stop the parasites. Those guys think like people, so they'll know that Seong Yujin-ssi has given up. They'll be more active in the future. So, let's each find and kill the parasites through voting.'

,That's not a bad plan. There were a few guys on our side who were suspected of being parasites.'

,There's a person in our Building 101 who is suspected of being a parasite. I'm in favor of the vote.'

Half of those they suspected were those who were dissatisfied with them. They would frame them as parasites and execute them. Then their power would become stronger.

There was one problem left. The food problem.

Lee Yebin looked straight at Kang Sang-gi-ssi and opened her mouth. She was using her ability to the fullest. She could feel Kang Sang-gi-ssi's gaze burning.

,I know that Building 103 has a lot of food. Please share some of the food you've stored. We should help each other as neighbors in the same apartment.'

,Ha, really. I'd like to do that, but... we don't have that much food stored.'

,You still have to share. The other buildings are in worse shape than Building 103. We should help each other as residents of the same apartment.'

,I'm telling you, we don't have any food. And even though the amount of food has decreased, it's not like you don't have any food at all. It's not like you're not getting food support. Food is not a serious problem.'

,Where's that dog barking? I heard that the food in Building 103 is enough to feed the entire apartment for two weeks.'

,Dog? Is this bastard crazy? We don't have any food. Do you think I don't know that you guys in Building 101 pretend to be nice but do all sorts of dirty things behind our backs? Do you know that most of the guys who hit on the women in our building are from Building 101? Huh?!"

Lee Yebin looked at Kang Sang-gi-ssi's stubborn face and sensed the future. Building 103 would not provide food to Building 101, even if it meant fighting with Building 101.

,I need to get support from Building 102 at least. I'll have to talk to them separately later.'

To be honest, the future of Building 101 was bleak. Woo Tae-hyun-ssi was the real power in Building 101, but Irina had her own popularity and served as a focal point. Her archery skills were real. But what about Woo Tae-hyun-ssi? He was smart, but it was difficult for him to become the focal point of the entire Building 101.

,The number of people in Building 102 has decreased because of the parasites. I need to accept people from Building 101 to fill it up.'

Having a lot of people is power in itself. Hadn't the neighboring country of China proven that fact?

,But that causes a lot of problems too. I heard that China is having problems right now because the population is too large.'

In particular, the population was concentrated in the cities, so I heard that they were having a lot of trouble during this crisis. India, which lacked infrastructure, was already close to being in a groggy state.

Well, it wasn't something Lee Yebin needed to worry about.

She sent Woo Tae-hyun-ssi away and had a secret meeting with Kang Sang-gi-ssi. She tried to seduce Kang Sang-gi-ssi by using her ability to the fullest to get food, but... she failed. The ability was successful. It was just that Building 103 didn't have any food.

,Wh-what do you mean there's no food?! What are you talking about?!"

,A parasite bastard pissed and shat in the food storage. They had opened all the canned goods and everything, so they rotted.'

,Why are you telling me this now?!"

,If I said we didn't have any food, you guys would ignore us. Especially Building 101. I can't let those bastards ignore us.'

,Ha, haha....'

,So, about that... why don't we merge? You, the representative of Building 102, and me, the representative of Building 103, get married. A marriage alliance. Isn't that a very long tradition? If we merge, Building 101 is nothing.'

Kang Sang-gi-ssi subtly reached out to Lee Yebin. Lee Yebin coldly slapped his hand away.

,Where are you touching? You're an old man with nothing!!"

,O-old man? You bitch! You're the one who seduced me first!"

,Ha, I didn't know you were an old man with nothing. You don't have any food? Good. Building 103 can starve to death on its own. If Building 103 is gone, we can survive with the reduced amount of food. Go write your will and kill yourself!"

,You fucking bitch!"

Kang Sang-gi-ssi exploded with anger and stood up. But it was already too late. Lee Yebin had already run out.

This was Building 102. Even if she caused a commotion, the enemies would gather here first. Kang Sang-gi-ssi gritted his teeth and returned to Building 103.
