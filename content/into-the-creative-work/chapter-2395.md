&lt;You have entered the Carnage Bloodbath of the Fittest.&gt;

As soon as I entered the closed dungeon, the notification window popped up.

Rank A dungeon, Carnage Bloodbath of the Fittest.

I wasn't surprised because I had received information from Ciel in advance. And I didn't come alone. Ciel and Han Ah-young were next to me.

,Closed dungeons sometimes have special missions or special rules set.,

Most Hunters avoid such dungeons. It's not a dungeon that can be conquered simply by being strong. Even Rank S Hunters don't take on these dungeons often.

,There are guilds that specialize in conquering these complex dungeons. I heard they receive subsidies from the association and make a lot of money because ordinary Hunters avoid them.,

Dungeons with special rules and missions usually have generous rewards. It means there's a high chance of hitting the jackpot.

,The Carnage Bloodbath of the Fittest is a place with special rules but a simple strategy.,

Bloodbath.

As you can guess from the name, it is a place where you fight monsters one-on-one.

If you break the one-on-one rule, the difficulty of the dungeon increases due to violation of the rules.

,Rank S Hunter Lee Yeon-sook deliberately broke the rules in this dungeon to keep raising the dungeon difficulty. I wonder if the reason was out of curiosity. She's such a crazy woman. And the result was... the difficulty rose to Rank S dungeon after breaking the rule 5 times.,

The strategy for the Carnage Bloodbath of the Fittest is simple. Win 10 times in a one-on-one fight. If you have companions, you don't have to win 10 times alone.

,Since there's Rank S Hunter, Han Ah-young, the Eternal Frost, here, there's no way we'll fail.,

I would say that safety is guaranteed as long as I am with Han Ah-young.

"It reminds me of the old days."

Han Ah-young said, looking around. This dungeon looks like a colosseum. The wide arena is made of compacted earth, and the spectator seats were chairs roughly carved out of rock.

"The old days? Did you come to this dungeon before, Nuna-ssi?"

"About 3 times? But that's not what I'm talking about. It was before I became a Rank S Hunter candidate. I had to show my strength to the World Hunter Association like you. And if I was recognized for my potential, I would officially become a Rank S candidate."

Official and unofficial. The difference is quite significant. If possible, it wouldn't be bad to be officially recognized as a Rank S candidate this time.

&lt;All personnel except Bloodbath participants, please move to the spectator seats.&gt;

"Yujin-ah, fighting!"

"I will intervene if I deem it dangerous."

Han Ah-young and Ciel moved to the spectator seats.

&lt;The first match will begin in 30 seconds.&gt;

30 seconds.

Preparation time was given. Preparation was completed in 1 second. All I did was summon Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory. I waited for the enemy to appear, placing the blade of Hwaryeon Bido on my shoulder.

,I heard the enemies in the Bloodbath are random. I want to get this over with and go back... How much strength should I show? Hmm. Do I need to hide my strength?,

Showing everything is a bit much. Isn't there such a saying in the Jianghu? Hide 30% of your power.

,First, I'll fight with skills and ability without using items.,

I can't use Hwaryeon Bido's domain. It hasn't been many days since I used it in the [Modern Magic War] world. Honestly, if I use the domain, can't I become Rank S right away? Even if it's just weapon power, it wouldn't be bad.

,But I can't use it.,

Kung.

A bipedal cow over 3m tall appeared in the air. It was wearing armor and even holding a spear. It is a Minotaur, a Rank A monster. It uses mana with excellent physical abilities. Its intelligence is estimated to be higher than that of a dolphin.

,This is my first time with a Minotaur. I heard that there are individual differences, so there are also Rank S Minotaurs, right?,

The monster in front of me was definitely not a Rank S monster.

"Kuweoreo-eo-eo-eo-eo!"

The Minotaur screamed as soon as it saw me.

The mana-filled scream was louder than an airplane. If it were an ordinary person, their eardrums would have burst.

"Fall."

Kwang!

A streak of lightning falls on the Minotaur. The Minotaur momentarily loses its balance and staggers, but quickly regains its balance.

,Is all that happened was its fur got scorched? It doesn't seem to work at all, but the efficiency is not good.,

The opponent is a Rank A monster. It would be stranger if it died from one lightning strike.

,I have to kill 9 more after killing that guy. I have to distribute my stamina well.,

I grip the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido tightly and take a stance.

Just because it's a Rank A monster doesn't mean it's on par with a Rank A Hunter. Hunters must be able to hunt monsters of the same rank. The story would be different if the opponent was a boss-level monster, but they would naturally win against ordinary monsters of the same rank.

The Minotaur rushed at me, breathing hot air.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

I lightly dodge the Minotaur's spear falling towards my head. The spear hit the ground, creating a small crater.

I almost burst out laughing. Its strength is great, but my speed is greater. Above all, the Minotaur's posture when wielding the spear was excessively clumsy. It just swings it around, relying only on its strength without any skill.

The Minotaur turns its body to the side, chasing after me.

Thunderclap Flash (雷天流) Flash of Lightning (雷光).

Seogeok!

A red trajectory cut off the Minotaur's head.

&lt;The second match will begin in 30 seconds.&gt;

• • •

Ciel's eyes sparkled as she watched Seong Yujin and the Minotaur's battle.

"Amazing. It's said that Minotaurs are easy to deal with among Rank A monsters... but to hunt a Minotaur in less than 10 seconds."

"I was surprised too. I knew Yujin-gun's growth rate was absurd... but I didn't know it would be this much already."

Han Ah-young was also impressed.

"His fighting style is unexpected. I thought he would fight using electricity since he has the ability to manipulate electricity... but he learned martial arts. Is it a unique Korean martial art?"

"Well. I don't know much about that. But it's a clean fighting style."

Han Ah-young stared intently at Seong Yujin. Seong Yujin looked even cooler today. Regardless, Ciel looked at Seong Yujin as if analyzing him.

"It's an efficient fighting style. That's good. However... from my point of view, I would like to see him handle his abilities. His potential is higher in that area."

"Still, can't you see enough potential just by looking at his current appearance? It's been 2 years since Yujin-gun first awakened as a Hunter. To be exact, it's almost been 3 years... It doesn't originally make sense to be at that level in just 3 years. You know that too, right?"

Becoming a Rank A Hunter in 3 years is an amazing feat worldwide. It's a talent that wouldn't be strange to call a genius among geniuses. The World Hunter Association took notice of Seong Yujin's tremendous growth.

"Amazing. But as you know, there is a huge gap between Rank A and Rank S. The reality is that many promising talents called geniuses fail to overcome that wall and become frustrated."

"Yujin-gun will overcome that wall and stand next to me. And it probably won't take long? By then, will the whole world try to cling to Yujin-gun? A lot of women will probably stick to him too? That's a bit... not good."

The 3rd battle ended quickly. Seong Yujin easily sliced up the monsters.

A tricky monster appeared from the 4th battle.

Great Wraith.

A ghost-type Rank A monster made up of dozens of wraiths. The characteristic of the Great Wraith is that physical attacks do not work.

Han Ah-young was not worried about Seong Yujin. Because Seong Yujin doesn't just have martial arts.

Pajijik.

Static electricity occurs. Han Ah-young and Ciel grabbed their floating hair and protected their bodies with mana. Immediately after, red lightning soared from the center of the arena. The Great Wraith disappears, leaving behind a magic stone.

"Ciel-ssi. I think we can make a decision now? What do you think?"

"...That's right. Just considering the combat power he has shown so far, he has exceeded the level of an ordinary Rank A Hunter. If he continues to grow steadily like this... he will be more than enough to become Rank S. I will report what I saw to the World Hunter Association."

"Wow. It's the moment a new Rank S candidate is born in Korea. People will be happy. Will Harin-yang... be jealous? Or...."

Han Ah-young closed her mouth with a strange smile.

Seong Yujin continued to fight. Originally, Han Ah-young was planning to intervene at an appropriate time. Even for a Rank A Hunter, fighting Rank A monsters one-on-one in a row is physically burdensome.

But Seong Yujin hunts Rank A monsters calmly without showing any signs of fatigue.

"...He knows all the Rank A monsters. That's unexpected. According to you, I didn't think he was a model student."

"According to Harin-yang, he went to university well until he awakened. He probably learned it then. Most of what you learn in the Hunter department is about monsters, right?"

"Hmm. He fights too well for that. He's not a Hunter with less than 5 years of experience, but a veteran Hunter who has been rolling around for decades."

"That's true. Why does he fight so well? Is it because Yujin-gun is a genius?"

Han Ah-young tilted her head. Ciel gave a bitter smile. From Ciel's point of view, Han Ah-young was also a genius comparable to Seong Yujin. She became a Rank S Hunter in her 20s, so what else would she be if not a genius?

"Ciel-ssi. This is another personal matter... I heard a rumor that I encountered someone from another world in a dungeon in the United States. Is it true?"

"Where did you hear such a rumor?"

"I've built up connections by traveling around the world. Is it a false rumor?"

"...It's probably true. But don't ask me. I don't know the details either. It's not under my jurisdiction."

"The World Hunter Association must be in trouble too."

"Hmm. Well. The atmosphere of the association was the same as usual? It seemed like they didn't care much. As if they knew in advance."

"Sometimes the World Hunter Association feels like the mastermind of this world. What the hell is that place?"

"A place for the order of the world? If you're really curious, you can become a member of the World Hunter Association. You will definitely get a key position if it's you."

"No thanks. I'm satisfied with the current situation."

By now, the 10th battle had begun. Seong Yujin looked tired from the continuous battles. His breathing was rough and sweat was dripping from his forehead. Han Ah-young clenched her fists tightly and raised her concentration. She was planning to intervene immediately if it looked dangerous.

The 10th battle opponent is the Rank A monster, Berry Kane. An insect-type monster over 5m in size. It is famous for rolling its body like a pill bug and rampaging around, devastating everything. And how hard is that carapace? Most attacks don't even work.

Seong Yujin took out metal from the air. A fist-sized metal is fixed in the air by electromagnetic force. Then he hits the metal with his fist.

Railgun.

Overwhelming physical force pierced through Berry Kane.

Han Ah-young opened her mouth slightly.

"Was that a railgun just now? To be able to use a railgun with bare hands... his ability to apply his abilities is outstanding. As expected of Yujin-gun. Amazing."

"...Ah-young-ah. Could you block that attack?"

"I can block it if I know the attack is coming. Two ice walls would be enough."

If Han Ah-young put her mind to it, she could create ice several times harder than steel. Does that not make sense? Rank S are originally such beings. Ability users who deny common sense itself.

The battle is over.

Seong Yujin gulped down mineral water taken out of his inventory. Han Ah-young cheered for him.

Then, a group of lights gathered in front of Seong Yujin and turned into one object. It was the dungeon conquest reward.
