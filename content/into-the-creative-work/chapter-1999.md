The World Tree burned.

The northern humans fell into a panic as the world's destruction loomed.

Just because the World Tree collapsed didn't mean the human world actually crumbled. Maybe it was always like this, but this World Tree was a kind of passage. A passage that allowed you to go to other worlds.

As the World Tree burned, that passage was blocked and disappeared. The beings of other worlds would not be able to interfere with the human world recklessly.

,I guess they wouldn't have the power to interfere anyway.'

After killing Odin, the chief god of Norse mythology, obtaining his power, and drinking from the Well of Wisdom to gain wisdom, I could see the other worlds connected to the World Tree. Not only that, but I also killed all the gods hiding with lightning.

,Doesn't seem like I killed them all.'

Some skilled gods were able to evade my eyes. Just because I have eyes looking down from the sky doesn't mean I can grasp everything. The same goes for lightning. It's different if I attack directly, but attacking from a distance like this inevitably reduces its power.

,I couldn't kill Hel. Jormungandr didn't even flinch from the lightning.'

Jormungandr, yawning openly while being struck by lightning, was annoying. But I wasn't free enough to go and kill him directly.

,I'll ignore him for now. I can kill Jormungandr anytime I remember him later.'

What I need to do right now is to take care of the humans who have fallen into chaos. Of course, it's not because I feel sorry for the humans.

,If I receive the faith of humans, I might become a little stronger.'

I was already strong enough to destroy a mythology on my own, but there were too many enemies targeting me. I had to become even stronger.

Taking care of the humans was easy.

I just had to drop lightning and kill those who didn't offer me faith. Then, they would offer me faith even if they just wanted to live.

After roughly organizing the north, I headed to Olympus.

I quickly returned home riding on the clouds, but Olympus, which should have welcomed me, was half-destroyed.

,...You. You're too late. Where have you been?"

Hera snapped in a cold voice. She was still beautiful and haughty, but she was full of exhaustion.

"It took me some time to wipe out the north. But why is Olympus like this? Even if I'm not here, you and the other gods should be here."

Hera and the other goddesses were there. Their power was somewhat weakened.

"The Titans, including Cronus, have returned."

As soon as I heard Hera's words, I realized what had happened.

"Hades-gun. Hades opened the gates of Tartarus and released those bastards. ...Hades shouldn't have the authority to open the gates of Tartarus..."

I muttered and looked around again. It was fortunate that the gods of Olympus were immortal. If they didn't have immortality like the northern gods, more than half of them would have died.

"Everyone endured well. Where did Cronus and the Titans go? I can't see them even with the eyes of the sky."

Hera sighed.

"Hades and the Titans took Gaia and turned the land upside down. Since the land is no longer the land, it will be difficult for you to survey the ground even with your powers."

"......Gaia?"

What are they trying to do by taking Gaia now? Is Cronus trying to rape Gaia? Aside from the fact that it's absolutely unacceptable, there was no reason for Gaia to cooperate with Cronus. I had to assume there was another purpose.

,There are several Mother Earths, but there is no Mother Earth as outstanding as Gaia. Gaia can be said to be the root itself. If I only have Gaia, I can create a new mythology again.... Ah, is that the purpose? Cronus is planning to sweep everything away and start anew.'

Cronus kidnapped Gaia in anticipation of the aftermath. So, I don't have to worry about Gaia's safety. Because he will need Gaia's cooperation later.

"Hera. You did well. It must have been a pretty tough fight."

"...I was lucky. If the Gigantes hadn't suddenly caused internal strife, we would have been dragged into Tartarus."

"Gigantes? Ah, I see. Those bastards must have been trapped in Tartarus too. The Titans led by Cronus and the Gigas led by Typhon joined forces. It's worse than the Gigantomachy... I'm surprised that you're still intact to this extent. The reason the Gigantes betrayed must be because of Gaia. Did the Titans win?"

"Cronus, who obtained Gaia, retreated without any regrets. The Gigantes hurriedly chased after him.... But are you really the Zeus I know?"

Hera looked at me with suspicious eyes.

"Are you suspicious because I'm too smart? Heh, I drank the Well of Wisdom while conquering the north. I am now Zeus, who has obtained endless wisdom!"

I smiled slyly.

The gods, including Hera, looked at me with suspicious eyes despite my explanation.

I understand.

The age of peace was shattered as easily as a glass cup, so they must be bewildered in many ways.

"Don't worry, now that I'm here. With my current power, I can easily subdue Poseidon or Hades. Cronus? Typhon? They are nothing. Even if they cover the eyes of the sky, they won't be able to collapse the sky itself!"

I said confidently. At times like this, I, the king of the gods, had to show a strong image.

"Zeus-nim's words are correct."

"You conquered the north? You conquered those rude bastards alone. As expected of Zeus-nim."

"I can feel Zeus-nim's power. As long as Zeus-nim is here, Olympus will be eternal."

It worked. The gloomy gods of Olympus began to regain their energy. It was only natural. Because I, the supreme being of Olympus, had arrived.

,Should I give a speech? There's nothing like a speech to boost morale.'

Then, I belatedly realized that the number of Olympian gods was insufficient.

"There are no gods. Helios in particular is not visible. Where did he go?"

The answer came from above.

"All the gods who are not here have sided with Poseidon and Apollo! Hades is on their side, but it feels like a separate force has allied with them!"

Hermes said as he flew in.

"Zeus-nim! You're back! Of course, I believed it! The war situation is still being pushed back, but surprisingly, the humans are holding out well! I'll tell you the war situation!"

"Hermes. You've been through a lot. Come closer."

"Yes! Zeus-nim!"

Hermes approached me without any suspicion. I grabbed Hermes' neck and clutched it.

"Keok?! Z-Zeus-nim?!"

All the other gods were flustered. They were watching me and Hermes. Hera stepped forward as a representative.

"Zeus! You're mistaken. Hermes is not a traitor! Even when Cronus and Typhon attacked, we were able to prepare in advance thanks to Hermes' information. His contribution is not small!"

Hera actively defended Hermes.

,For the cold Hera to say this much.... He must have worked diligently while I was away.'

But that's that. This is this.

"Everyone listen. Hermes is a traitor."

"Zeus-nim...?!"

Hermes' eyes shook. He whimpered with a look full of injustice. Some gods looked back and forth between me and Hermes with pitiful eyes.

"Hypnos said before he died that there was a traitor. I thought he was talking about Hades and Poseidon. They actually betrayed and rebelled against me."

Originally, I didn't pay any attention to Hypnos' words at all. Hades' and Poseidon's betrayal and rebellion? They were full of complaints, so it wasn't surprising.

"After drinking all the water from the Well of Wisdom, I started to think differently. I had some time left, so I slowly thought about my past actions."

"Zeus-nim! I am not a traitor! I am Zeus-nim's... sh-shuttle? I am a shuttle god! I only acted according to Zeus-nim's orders!"

"You pretended to be. But you subtly manipulated me."

"How can I manipulate Zeus-nim, the god of gods?!"

"The manipulation I'm talking about isn't domination, hypnosis, or brainwashing caused by power and magic. You induced my choices with clever words. As if I were choosing on my own."

"I-I never did that! Doesn't Zeus-nim make the choices?!"

With my Absolute Spirit, I am not susceptible to hypnosis or brainwashing. However, that doesn't mean I have a perfect spirit. In the first place, the Absolute Spirit is on the side of defending the spirit. It doesn't have a big impact on the way I think.

For example, if you see red often, you become familiar with red, and when you have to make a choice, you unknowingly choose what you are familiar with.

I didn't realize it when I didn't have wisdom. That Hermes subtly controlled me with visual information and words to make me make choices.

"You must have induced me to make the choice to go to the north. Really... It's terrible that a guy like you was by my side."

"It's a misunderstanding! The gods of the north were spouting nonsense to Zeus-nim! I heard there is a god of deception in the north! That god must have done something to Zeus-nim! Everyone, don't just watch and stop Zeus-nim!"

The gods look at each other. The reason they don't come forward easily is because the goddesses who are close to me are looking at Hermes with suspicious eyes.

I threw Hermes down as if slamming him into the floor.

"Keueok!"

Hermes tried to fly up and escape using the reaction force.

,No way.'

Kwack!

I stomp on Hermes' head and press down. Hermes' head dug into the ground. I raised my eyebrows. Surprisingly, Hermes' head did not explode.

,Even if I didn't use my full power, my current strength should be enough to easily tear Hermes apart.'

I gained another conviction.

Pajijijik.

Lightning arose and began to bind Hermes' arms and legs like shackles.

Kwack! Kwack! Kwack!

I stomped on Hermes even more. As the power increased, the entire Olympus began to shake. Nevertheless, Hermes' head did not shatter. Then, I created a lightning spear and stabbed it into Hermes' thighs and upper arms.

"Heu. Heuheuheuheuh."

Hermes laughs.

The gods were horrified. It wasn't because of the gloomy laughter. It was because the energy felt from Hermes was very foreign. I stomped on Hermes' head a few more times.

"I'll admit that you noticed. As expected of the Well of Wisdom. It lives up to its name. I didn't expect to be caught so easily."

A dark and viscous power emanates from Hermes. I clicked my tongue and stepped back slightly. I needed preparation time to deal with this.

Hermes' head changes. Tentacles protrude and wrap around his head as if spinning. Small tentacles also protruded from various parts of his body, repeating the process of curling and shrinking. Just looking at it causes instinctive disgust.

I glared fiercely at him, holding Astrape in my right hand.

"Nyarlathotep...!"
