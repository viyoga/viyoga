Aura surged from my sword and theirs.

I don't know the odds. I don't have an objective grasp of how strong I am right now. But one thing is certain: I don't feel like I'm going to lose at all.

,Acceleration.'

I use the skill. I and the Royal Knights left the ground at the same time. I charged straight ahead, and the Royal Knights spread out to the sides, trying to surround me. They don't miss the numerical advantage. The Royal Knights' actions were cold and efficient.

Kwaang!

,Kueueueueuk!'

,Kuaaaaaaak!'

My sword touched his body. The sword, imbued with blue aura, lightly cut through his armor. Red blood gushed from his neck and chest.

I stepped on his falling body and jumped up. Dozens of swords simultaneously stabbed into the place where I had been just now. The Royal Knights had surrounded me.

,Lightning.'

Pajijijijik.

My body fell to the ground with a blue lightning. The Royal Knights, electrocuted by the lightning, stiffened. I didn't miss the gap and swung my sword wildly in all directions.

Half of the Royal Knights were cut down. They spew red blood and fall to the floor. In the face of their comrades' deaths, they don't panic at all and swing their swords to kill me. Even I wouldn't be able to deal with them one by one right now.

But the Royal Knights' swords never touched my skin. The aura was imbued in the sword, but the durability of Stormbreaker far exceeded their imagination. To penetrate my armor, you need at least an aura blade and swordsmanship skills to match it.

,What the hell is that armor....'

,The attack itself doesn't work...!'

,Block him! Block the traitor!'

The Royal Knights' morale drops at the hardness of the armor. I didn't miss the gap and killed the Royal Knights one by one.

I walked through the sea of blood and headed to the Great Hall. There was no time to hesitate. Time was not on my side.

,If time passes, the Knights and soldiers will rush in. No matter how much I have Stormbreaker, I can't face them all alone.'

I punched open the Great Hall's big and fancy door.

Kwaang!

The door opened as if it would break, and the gazes of the many people enjoying the banquet focused on me. It became chaotic in an instant.

Cheolkeok.

According to my will, the helmet automatically opened and was stored in the armor. A new emperor will be enthroned today. I needed to show them clearly who the emperor was.

,Eugene Bellacros?! Why is that bastard here...!'

,C-calm down and look behind you. All the Royal Knights who were guarding the Great Hall are dead!'

,Uweeeeek!'

,Treason! It's treason!!'

The face of the man sitting on the throne was distorted. Long black hair reaching to his shoulders and a beard coming down to his chest.

He had the charisma of an emperor. That's what I would have thought if I didn't know the original work. The current emperor is a pathetic and greedy bastard. Originally, if he were an emperor, the vassals should be looking at the emperor's face, but that bastard was the opposite.

,Eugene! You vulgar bastard! Can't you step back right now!'

The Emperor's roar echoed loudly. But that didn't stop my steps.

Kwang! Kwang Kwang!

The Emperor slammed the throne with his fist, expressing his frustration and anger. It was effective. Before I even reached half of the red carpet, a middle-aged man stood in my way. A red aura blade is created from his sword.

He is Count Yulhanes Lairoth, the Captain of the Royal Knights. An aura master... no, he is one of the greatest who has reached the realm of Sword Master in this world.

,Your Highness Eugene. You've gone too far. Drop your sword and surrender.'

Your Highness.

Most people called me Eugene-nim. It also meant that they didn't recognize me as royalty, and there were no disadvantages to them calling me that. Until now.

,Captain of the Royal Knights. You're useful. Step aside. I will use you heavily later.'

,My loyalty is only directed to His Majesty the Emperor.'

,I see. Die honorably.'

Cheolkeok.

As I took a step with the will to fight, the helmet was automatically put on.

But I can't ignore it. That red aura blade is more threatening than any famous sword.

,An aura blade, huh. ......It's worth a try.'

,That's a great aura blade. You've been hiding your strength.'

,Well, that's all you'll see.'

,Your Highness Eugene. That armor is no ordinary treasure. Where did you get it?'

[Activating Genius Time for 10 seconds.]

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 3]

[Using Moment. Remaining stacks: 1]
