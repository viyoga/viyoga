Seong Hari and I disembarked the ship as if nothing had happened. However, Seong Hari's face was slightly flushed. The Chinese people nearby were too engrossed in their own affairs to notice her face.

Seong Hari seemed relieved. She probably thought people didn't know we had sex on the ship.

In reality, the rumors had spread everywhere.

We spent more than half the day having sex. And Seong Hari, focused on the sex, screamed her lungs out. Since the soundproofing couldn't be perfect, it was only natural that others would hear. They were just pretending not to know.

"Hari-ya. Do you feel anything?"

"...It's strange. It feels like I've come home."

"The Jinryeong Seong family?"

"Yeah. Our house uses barriers to create an environment similar to the Spirit Realm for the spirits."

'I've been to the Spirit Realm before.'

Rather than entering, I had only dipped my toes in.

'What did it feel like back then?'

It was quite a while ago, so I couldn't remember well.

"Anyway, seeing Makina materialize so easily on her own volition, it's certain that she's been influenced by the Spirit Realm."

"Yeah. I think so too."

We entered the temporary base headquarters. The building was not great. It was old. No, it was tacky.

We entered the conference room inside the building. The conference room was large, and more than 20 people were seated. They quietly stared at us. Some looked at us with wary eyes, others with dissatisfied gazes. No one offered a greeting. Seong Hari and I sat in the empty seats.

"Everyone who needs to be here has gathered. Let's begin the briefing."

A man walked to the center of the conference room and spoke.

"First, please look at the third page of the documents you were given. It's a map of Hainan. Do you see the red area? That's the Spirit Realm zone. The Spirit Realm zone is expanding. We estimate it will take about 80 days for the entire island to turn into the Spirit Realm. We must resolve this situation before it becomes irreversible."

I picked up the documents on the desk.

Palangpalangpalang.

I flipped through the documents roughly. There was a map of Hainan Island, information about the spirits occupying Hainan Island, and the attack methods to be aware of. In Chinese.

I glanced sideways at Seong Hari. She was frowning at the documents, a crease forming between her eyebrows. Her documents were also in Chinese.

It goes without saying, but she couldn't understand Chinese. There was no translator either.

'Are they telling me to translate on my own?'

I can do that much.

I can, but I felt disgusted. If only the documents were in Korean, I would have let it slide.

Seeing Seong Hari struggling to understand the documents made me even more annoyed.

They need Seong Hari's strength, yet they treat her like this... It twists my insides.

I sat crookedly in my chair and put my feet on the desk.

"......"

The briefing stopped. The gazes directed at me were not friendly. Especially the man who was giving the briefing, his face turned bright red.

"Pfft."

He looked like a nicely roasted pig, so I couldn't help but laugh.

"Ah, sorry. I was thinking of a roasted pig. Continue."

"...Are Koreans so ignorant of etiquette?"

I raised my middle finger.

Wageuk.

The documents in the man's hand crumpled. At the same time, several people around him gathered mana. The air trembled. Seong Hari, realizing the situation, sighed and prepared for battle.

I also moved my mana.

Pajik.

Electricity sparked from my fingertips.

To be honest, we were at a disadvantage. There were over 20 enemies, all skilled individuals above A-class heroes. If we fought fairly, we would lose. At my current level, I could barely handle 2-3 of them.

'But I have weapons and consumables.'

If I used them properly, I could kill about half of those bastards. If it seemed impossible, I could just run away.

"Enough."

A voice like scraping metal echoed from the corner.

Everyone's gaze naturally turned to the corner. It was a man with a pale complexion. An old man wearing a black Hanfu. Bald, he stared straight ahead and continued speaking.

"Stop the pointless power struggle and continue with the operation briefing."

"Y-yes, Wang Daein-nim."

The man who resembled a roasted pig continued the briefing, sweating profusely. Those who had been hostile towards me also focused on the documents, watching the old man's every move.

'What kind of old geezer is he?'

I stared intently at the old man in the corner. The old man didn't turn his head, even though he felt my gaze.

'Looking closely, his skin is pale to the point of being gray. He feels like a corpse.'

I explained the operation to Seong Hari. Loudly and clearly in Korean. The Chinese frowned and glared at me, but they couldn't do anything, watching the old man's every move.

"The operation will begin in two hours. This concludes the briefing."

"......"

Everyone watched the old man's every move. When the old man got up from his seat and went outside, only then did the others get up from their seats.

They glared at us once and left the conference room.

"This matter should be sufficient with Yang Daein-nim joining us. Why did they bring Koreans on this mission? I don't know what the Association is thinking."

"The Association must have judged that we alone wouldn't be enough. I don't like it."

"Would the Association listen to us?"
