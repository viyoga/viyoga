The place we arrived at by car was a factory on the outskirts of Seoul.

It was a closed-down food factory. A quick search online revealed that a fire had broken out a few months ago, and the factory went bankrupt because of it.

Joo Seo-hyun and I parked the car out of sight and approached the factory under the cover of darkness in the alleyway.

,"Is there a vampire inside?"

,"Wait a moment."

She leaned against the wall and fiddled with her smartphone. A quick glance showed that she was receiving work orders through the company's exclusive app.

,"There's no one inside the factory right now. A vampire will appear at midnight to trade with people."

,"Trade? Don't tell me it's a drug deal?"

,"Vampire blood. There are quite a few people who want to become vampires."

,"Is eternal life that tempting? Enough to endure a life of sucking human blood forever?"

,"It's not just eternal life. If you become a vampire, incurable diseases or terminal cancer can all be cured. People who are psychologically cornered might think it's better to become a vampire."

,"I understand there are various reasons. How much does vampire blood cost?"

,"It depends on the grade. Slave blood doesn't even cost 3 million won, Common grade is at least 10 million won or more. Noble grade is at least in the hundreds of millions of won."

,"What about Lord grade?"

,"You can't put a price on it. It's something you can't get even if you want to."

Joo Seo-hyun was basically taciturn, but she answered my questions diligently. I thought about continuing to talk to Joo Seo-hyun, but I stopped. I might make a mistake without realizing it if I blabbered carelessly. And Joo Seo-hyun and I are meeting for the first time today. A proper distance is needed.

As midnight approached, a car entered the factory. Three men got out of the car.

,"Department Head Joo Seo-hyun-nim. They've appeared."

,"They're human."

,"Yes? How do you know?"

,"They're looking around. It's proof that they've come here for the first time. And there's no way a vampire would move so openly. Keep an eye on the dark side where the light doesn't reach."

About five minutes passed. As Joo Seo-hyun said, something moving in the darkness was visible. It was so secretive that I would have missed it if I hadn't been focused.

,"It's a vampire."

Joo Seo-hyun said as if warning me.

,"My specialty is sniping... Unfortunately, I don't think I can snipe here."

There were too many obstacles to snipe. If those bastards noticed the sniping and went inside the factory, there would be nothing I could do.

I took out a silenced pistol from my chest and fiddled with it. The magazine was, of course, filled with silver bullets. She looked at my waist.

,"...You're not going to use a sword?"

,"Of course, I'm confident with a sword, but I'm better at shooting."

,"You can use a sword. I'll allow it."

,"It's a bit much for me to face a vampire... I'll definitely assist Department Head Joo Seo-hyun-nim."

,"......"

Joo Seo-hyun forcibly took her lingering gaze away. I sighed inwardly.

,'Joo Seo-hyun. Do you think I don't know your intentions? You want to see my swordsmanship.'

Joo Seo-hyun is a genius with the sword. A genius who can watch my swordsmanship a few times and imitate it.

There was no way she wouldn't remember my swordsmanship after a year had passed.

,'I know very well how great her eye for swordsmanship is. If I fight a vampire with a sword and unconsciously use Yeongcheon Ryu, it's over. So, the best thing is not to hold a sword.'

So, I try not to use any kind of sword as much as possible when I'm with Joo Seo-hyun.

Joo Seo-hyun waited for the right timing and said.

,"Follow me."

Joo Seo-hyun moved. She wasn't heading straight for the factory. She moved while hiding in the darkness like a vampire. She perfectly erased her presence and approached the factory.

However, she was caught as soon as she entered the factory. There was a vampire who was on high alert.

,"Who is it!"

The vampire shouted.

,"Shoot."

Joo Seo-hyun said briefly and ran forward. Her hand was on the sword hilt, ready to draw it at any moment.

Tang! Tang! Tang!

I shot the vampires with my pistol. The vampires dodged by throwing themselves as soon as they saw the gun.

,"They're from the company!"

,"Shit. Isn't that woman the butcher?!"

,"Run away... Keuaaaak!"

One vampire was sliced by Joo Seo-hyun's sword. Its upper and lower body were separated in half. To cut a vampire, whose physical durability is stronger than a human, so easily. As expected of Joo Seo-hyun.

Tang!

A silver bullet pierced the vampire's head. The vampire died as its brain melted from the silver bullet. It was a stupid bastard who was so surprised by Joo Seo-hyun's speed that it took its eyes off the gun.

,'Are you ignoring my shooting skills?'

,"Damn it!"

The remaining vampire ran out of the factory. It looked like a Common grade vampire. Before I could even aim my gun, Joo Seo-hyun took out a small dagger from her pocket and threw it. The dagger accurately pierced the vampire's nape.

,'She's damn good at throwing daggers too.'

I suddenly remembered what Joo Seo-hyun's specialty was. Igieogum. Considering that she can move swords freely, I think throwing is nothing.

The battle ended quickly. It probably didn't even take 10 seconds. It felt too anticlimactic for fighting vampires who have transcended humans.

,'Those bastards were scared shitless when they saw Joo Seo-hyun before they even fought properly.'

Their will to fight was broken as soon as they saw Joo Seo-hyun. Should I call it the power of reputation?

But the situation wasn't over yet.

The three people who traded with the vampire were lying on the floor and not getting up. Joo Seo-hyun clicked her tongue as she looked at the syringes scattered on the floor. A few drops of red liquid remained in the syringes.

,"Vampire blood. To think they would inject vampire blood as soon as they received it."

,"What do we do in this case?"

,"Capture. The principle is to capture them if we can. There should be special handcuffs in my car. Go and get them."

,"Yes."

The words, "You go and get them," almost came out, but I realized my current position and immediately ran to the car.

When I brought the special handcuffs, there were only two people left out of the three. One bastard was dead with a hole in his chest. His heart was pierced.

,"I killed him because he was trying to transform into a vampire and go wild."

,"What about suppression?"

,"I can't do it with my strength because I don't have special handcuffs."

I nodded.

Swordsmanship skills and suppression skills were different. I could just cut off their limbs to suppress them, but unlike me, Joo Seo-hyun tries not to do such things as much as possible. She kills even enemies cleanly. That was Joo Seo-hyun.

I put special handcuffs on the two people who hadn't transformed yet.

,"Put them on their feet too and don't take your eyes off them. Vampires can mutate into unique individuals with a very low probability."

,"Yes. I learned it in training camp. I heard that there was even a crazy bastard among the unique individuals who sprayed chemicals?"

,"I heard that they appeared in Europe a few years ago. I don't know the details either."

Fortunately, none of the remaining two transformed into unique individuals. However, they transformed into Common grade instead of Slave.

It's easy to distinguish between Slave and Common. If their skin rots and they look alien to anyone, they're Slave, and if they look similar to humans, they're Common.

,"Department Head Joo Seo-hyun-nim. Are Common vampires usually created this easily? I don't think Noble's blood is that abundant...."

,"I heard that these cases are increasing recently."

We waited there for about 15 minutes.

Support department employees appeared with a truck. They systematically cleaned up the situation. They put the dead vampire's body in a van, and the living vampires were wrapped tightly in chains and put into a special vehicle.

The head of the support department smiled and walked towards Joo Seo-hyun and me.

,"Department Head Joo! You've worked hard again today!"

,"Section Chief Kim-nim, you're working hard at this late hour too."

,"Isn't it just you guys who are fighting vampires? Is the guy next to you her partner?"

,"Yes. He's my partner."

,"I'm Seong Yujin-nim, who joined the company this afternoon."

,"Haha. I heard rumors that you're crazy, but you're surprisingly normal. Leave the cleanup to us and you guys can go."

,"Thank you."

Joo Seo-hyun walked past him towards the car as if she was used to it.

I had been subtly acting like I was trembling since earlier. I'm a rookie who officially hunted vampires for the first time. It was natural to be nervous.

Joo Seo-hyun naturally took the driver's seat.

She glanced at me, who was sitting in the passenger seat, and opened her mouth.

,"I heard the story from the training camp."

,"Yes?"

,"I heard that you didn't tremble at all when the vampires attacked."

,"...Who told you?"

,"The head instructor."

,"...You're close to the head instructor-nim."

,"We're not close. We just have a passing acquaintance because we used to work together."

I hesitated whether to continue acting like I was trembling subtly, but I stopped.

,"I'm just insensitive in this area. Actually, I'm a psychopath."

,"I knew it."

,"......Yes?"

Wasn't she taking my joke too seriously? I was a little embarrassed, but she continued to drive the car.

The car stopped in front of the company dormitory. Company employees could receive dormitory support, and I chose the dormitory. Joo Seo-hyun had a separate house.

,"Come to work in the afternoon tomorrow. There probably won't be any work."

,"Yes. I understand."

I scratched my head as I watched the car drive away.

,'Did she find out? Or didn't she? If she found out, she wouldn't have left me alone, so she probably didn't find out, right?'

Joo Seo-hyun's mind, who was holding the steering wheel, was complicated.

Because of Seong Yujin.

There was something strange about Seong Yujin's actions today. I don't know what it is.

,'Maybe....'

Maybe Seong Yujin has the memories of Atlantis, I thought.

,'If it's Seong Yujin, he might be pretending to have lost his memory and making fun of me....'

The problem was that there was no definite evidence. If Seong Yujin denies it to the end, there's nothing I can say. I need definite evidence so that Seong Yujin can't deny it.

Then, Seong Yujin's parents' house address suddenly came to mind. The words that there is a special sword and a small key in Seong Yujin's parents' house warehouse.

,'Should I go and check it out now?'

Forcibly break in and open the warehouse to check the sword and key. Maybe that key is a chastity belt.

,'If that's the chastity belt key? Unlock the chastity belt and....'

Unlock the chastity belt and kill Seong Yujin? Does it really end with that? Can I be satisfied?

,'......As I thought before, it's meaningless to kill Seong Yujin without memories. If Seong Yujin really lost his memory, I have to make him regain his memory.'

If Seong Yujin really lost his memory, I'll make him regain his memory and then take revenge.

If he's pretending to have lost his memory... I'll have to get evidence so that he can't do that ridiculous acting anymore.
