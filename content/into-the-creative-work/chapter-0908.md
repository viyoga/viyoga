,Hey kid, having some blasphemous thoughts?"

Hel gave me a chilling smile as she looked at me.

The cold from the floor traveled up my legs, along my spine, and chilled my head. I hurriedly shook my head in denial.

,Ah, no. How could I dare do such a thing!"

,The reactions of humans who see me fall into two main categories. They either fear me, or they despise me. You're the latter."

,Absolutely not!"

,It's fine. Don't bother making excuses. I don't care either way."

,......."

Since she told me not to make excuses, I didn't.

Hel's eyebrows twitched. Soon, her laughter echoed throughout the palace.

,You're an interesting human. You really don't fear me."

,No. I am afraid. Because I'd lose if we fought."

,You don't seem to have any intention of fighting? What brings you here to me?"

,I want to leave the Hell Zone."

,You wish to use the gate of Niflheim. Fortunately, you are qualified."

I knew what those qualifications were.

One, to be a living being, not one of the dead.

The other, to be a noble of Hell.

As a noble of Hell, I could rightfully make this request to her. This is why I'm talking to Hel in front of Elena.

,Since you seem to know everything, I won't beat around the bush. What will you offer in return?"

,I will pay with AP."

Atlantis Points.

Something that could be considered influence and power for a God.

,I have no need for it. I already have enough AP."

,......We only came thinking of AP as payment. It's disconcerting that you say you don't need it. What kind of compensation do you want?"

,Originally, I do not govern the entirety of Niflheim."

,Yes. I am aware that you rule Helheim, which is part of Niflheim."

,But this time, for Atlantis, I've been put in charge of all of Niflheim. Niflheim is vast. So vast that it's giving me a headache."

,......."

,So, I would like you to do the work of a manager."

,We want to get out of the Hell Zone."

,Once you finish the manager's work, I will send you out immediately."

,......What kind of work is it?"

I didn't like it, but I had no choice but to do what Hel wanted. Hel was in the position of power right now.

,Niflheim is so vast that there are places my power doesn't reach. Clever undead and dead frost giants avoid me there. Go there, clean up the undead, and pluck out their fingernails and toenails, and bring them to me. Ten thousand. The giant's fingernails and toenails must be at least three thousand."

It was a breathtaking quest.

This is Niflheim. The longer I stay here, the faster time flows outside.

,Ten thousand... that seems like too many. Please reduce it a little."

,No. If you don't like it, then give up. I don't care if you stay here for ten thousand years. Ah, did you know that the cold of Niflheim freezes even time?"

,...I know."

,Good to know. Now, go to work. Be careful not to be too late."

We were dismissed.

Before Elena and I could even move, we were outside Hel's palace. Hel had used her authority to send us out.

,I feel like a slave. Wouldn't it be worth fighting that goddess instead?"

Elena said as she got up. She wasn't serious. It was just a lamenting remark.

,Even if you have complete recovery, you can't win. At least we have to fight somewhere else."

,Do you even know where that goddess's power doesn't reach?"

,It's obvious. You saw it too when we came here."

,I saw it too...? Ah, indeed. Is it there."

We looked around as we came to Hel's palace. And we found something peculiar. In some places, it was snowing heavily, and in others, it was cold but not snowing.

,It doesn't snow in the goddess's territory."

,That's right. And we weren't attacked even when we made eye contact with the undead on our way here, right? Hel completely dominates the places where it doesn't snow."

,Now that the goal is set, we need to act. I don't have time, so I'll teleport."

,Is it possible?"

,I'm not confident in magic other than illusion, but my illusions are mostly omnipotent."

Elena snapped her fingers.

Ten blue butterflies appeared. The butterflies fluttered around Elena and me. The space around the butterflies warped.

The space changed in the blink of an eye. Strong winds and heavy snow blew in. The ground was covered with accumulated snow.

,Are you okay? I'll give you the Hecate Cape if you want."

,It's okay. I already gave it to you. And there's something else."

I summoned a red cloak from my inventory.

,Ermita's Cloak A cloak made by turning the properties of ironwood into fiber. It has excellent durability and does not get dirty easily. It has high cold resistance. Rank: A"

It was a cloak I had made in the past by asking the Iron Root Dwarves.

When I put on the cloak, the cold that felt like it was cutting my skin disappeared. I stopped shivering.

,Now all that's left is to find and eliminate the undead. Ten thousand fingernails and toenails. Hmm. Eugene, how many days do you think it will take?"

,Well. If we're lucky, we might finish within a month. Humans have 20 fingernails and toenails, right?"

We can get 2,000 fingernails and toenails from 100 undead. If there are 500 undead, collecting 10,000 is easy.

,The annoying part will be collecting 3,000 fingernails and toenails from the dead frost giants."

,Undead."

Elena pointed straight ahead. There was no need to delay. I summoned Stormbreaker in the form of a longsword, grabbed it, and charged at the undead.

The undead was a walking corpse. The skin was peeled off, and some parts of the body were bone. Yet it moved. It raised its fingernails at me and charged.

,Lightning."

Pajijijik.

I created lightning and Sword energy on the blade and cut through the undead's torso.

,Kieeeeeeeeeeeeeee!"

The undead screamed a horrible scream. It felt like stabbing the brain with an awl.

,The undead curses you."

,Your high mental strength shakes off the undead's curse."

,Kieeeeeee! Kieeeeek! Kieeee!"

,You're too noisy for a low-level undead. Just die already."

I cut off its head. But the undead's screams didn't stop. After smashing its skull and cutting its body into pieces, the undead became quiet.

,Eugene. You need to recover the fingernails and toenails."

,I know. I don't want to touch the corpse with my hands, but... I can't help it. ...This is fucked up."

,What's wrong?"

,This bastard only has 3 fingernails and toenails."

,Since the corpse is a mess, it doesn't have any intact fingernails and toenails. I guess this will take longer than I thought."

I clicked my tongue and recovered the fingernails and toenails.

We wandered around looking for undead. In the process, we also encountered dead frost giants. The size of the frost giants averaged over 10m. As big as they were, they were incomparably stronger than ordinary undead. It took at least 3 minutes to fight a dead frost giant 1-on-1. Of course, if Elena helped, we could defeat them faster, but Elena had to focus on finding the undead.

,This won't work."

,Have you given up? Keep moving instead of grumbling."

,It can't be like this. It takes too long. It's not efficient."

,I agree with that, but this is the only way, right? This place called Niflheim. It's not normal, so my illusions don't work well either."

,50 fingernails a day? Does that make sense? If this goes on, at least 3 years will have passed when we get out."

,......I still can't think of a way. We have no choice but to move diligently."

,Wait a minute."

In times like these, it's best to look through what I have. There might be an item with an effect that helps in this situation.

,Lure Fishing Hook... This doesn't work on the undead, so it's meaningless.... I found it! If it's this...!"

,Tutankhamun's Golden Mask When wearing the golden mask, no curse can affect you. You become a dead person. Mana increases by 50. You can control the undead according to your mana level. However, mummies can be controlled regardless. Destroys the wearer's mind. If the mind is intact, you can become the complete owner. But if the mind is destroyed, you will be reduced to a mummy. Rank: SSS"

I held up the golden mask.

The undead are undead. They are moving corpses, no different from zombies or ghouls. Perhaps because of the influence of Niflheim, these zombies are a bit too strong.

,You took out another strange thing."

,Now the undead are nothing!"

I put the majestic golden mask of Tutankhamun on my head. My mana increased by 50, and my whole body was overflowing with power.

At the same time, my ki spread in all directions.

Undead buried in the snow, undead walking aimlessly, frost giant undead quietly sharpening their weapons.

I could feel the presence of the undead. I knew instinctively. I can dominate these undead.

,It's not enough. I need to expand my presence a little more.... Ah, that's right!"

[Open the Third Eye.]

I activated the Third Eye.

The range of my ki sense more than doubled. Even the snowstorm scattered by the strong wind couldn't block my gaze.

,Undead! Come here!"

I gave orders to the undead I had identified. The ordinary undead accepted my domination without complaint. The dead frost giants resisted, but eventually came under my control.

A total of 34. Among them, only 4 were frost giants.

,Hoo. This will increase efficiency."

,Keukeu. You stupid undead bastards. Hurry up and pluck out your fingernails and toenails and offer them to me!"

Uwoooo....

The undead could not refuse my orders. They plucked out their fingernails and toenails and offered them to me politely.

,There are 337 in total. Among them, the frost giant's fingernails and toenails are not even 50. Eugene, now get rid of the undead."

,No. It's too wasteful to get rid of these useful guys like this."

,Be honest. You're enjoying this right now, aren't you?"

,There's that too."

Cheok!

I pointed straight ahead with my finger. The undead walked forward.

,You slowpokes, move faster! You there, the one without front teeth! You're too slow. What are you doing, undead? Eat that undead without front teeth!"

,Kieeeeeeee!"

,Uwooooooooo...."

The undead rushed at the undead I pointed at. They tore at the undead's arms, neck, and shoulders. Cannibalism! It wasn't scary, but it looked like a cheap B-grade movie.

,As expected. The undead got stronger when they ate another undead. This is fun. Keukeu."

It reminded me of a raising game. A simple but incredibly addictive raising game.

,I see a group of undead wandering around over there. Kill them. Offer the fingernails and toenails to me, and eat the bodies of those undead."

The undead I controlled and the wandering undead group fought together. Elena and I stepped back and just watched. The undead's battle was quite a sight to see.

The ones who won were the undead I controlled. There were more of them from the beginning.

,The Black Pharaoh is watching you."

Perhaps because I was wearing Tutankhamun's golden mask, a certain God was watching me. I ignored it since he didn't do anything else. There were many Gods watching me besides the Black Pharaoh.

Elena narrowed her eyes and looked at me as if she couldn't understand.

,No matter how I think about it, it's a meaningless act. I was thinking of collecting fingernails and toenails quickly... um!"

Elena, who was complaining, suddenly widened her eyes.

,I know your intention. It's to prepare for when we meet strong frost giant undead. Since there are few frost giant undead, stronger ones will come out as time goes by."

The undead get stronger when they eat another undead.

That wasn't just true for the undead I controlled.

,...Uh. You're right. There are few frost giant undead, so there will be many strong ones. We need to increase our power to deal with them. There's a limit to what you and I can do alone."

,You had it all figured out. Not bad."

Elena smiled and stroked my arm, looking at me proudly. She seemed to have realized how great I am. I wrapped my arm around Elena's waist.

,Elena. Let's make a warm carriage with your illusion. Let the undead pull the carriage... and we can do... eat rice cakes."

,Not bad. Okay. But do you have to wear that mask? It makes me uncomfortable to look at it."

,I have to keep wearing this to control the undead. The moment I take this off, the undead will try to kill us."

,I guess there's no choice."
