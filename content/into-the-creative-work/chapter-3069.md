"Kiiiiieeeeeek!"

The harpies shriek. It was a roar that resembled a human scream. They circled the sky, screeching sounds that made listeners cower.

'Harpies. A troublesome opponent for E-rank hunters.'

A harpy's physical abilities are below an orc's. But they fly. Their attacks consist of diving down to swing sharp talons, or snatching prey and carrying them into the sky before dropping them.

'Even with a gun, they're not easy to deal with since they stay airborne.'

That's the story for ordinary hunters.

Elena, Yuria, and I gazed at the harpies indifferently.

"Their intelligence doesn't seem particularly high. We could disturb their minds and make them perceive their own kind as enemies."

"That would take too long. Targeting the Harpy Queen would be more efficient."

Wiping out all the harpies at once with an area-of-effect skill was difficult. The power of the summoned Yuria and Elena was limited to less than 5% of their original strength. The sharpness of their techniques was world-class, but the total quantity of power was insufficient.

"No need to drag this out. Let's just kill the Harpy Queen and be done with it."

I said. Finding the Harpy Queen wasn't difficult.

The Harpy Queen was larger than the other harpies, with red tips on her wings. Unlike the others noisily flying about, she perched at the center of the sky, the highest point, looking down. I could feel the arrogance and confidence of a leader.

"Yes, Master. I'll handle it. However, she's too high up. Flying there with magic would take time. Elena."

"Got it. I'll help. Just need me to send you up there?"

"Yes."

A single blue butterfly fluttered around Elena before carefully settling on Yuria's shoulder.

Yuria vanished.

The maid appeared above the Harpy Queen and plummeted toward the ground. Her blue-white hair and long maid skirt billowed in the wind.

"Kiiieek?!"

The Harpy Queen, sensing Yuria's spatial teleportation, was startled and frantically flapped her wings.

Yuria spread her hand. From the small shadow in her palm emerged a shadow chain—Glaive's wedge—piercing through the Harpy Queen's body.

『Challenge complete!』

『Floor 14 record: 33 seconds! An unbelievable record!』

『Achieving Global Ranking #1!』

『Registering ranking. Please decide on a name to register.』

The moment the Harpy Queen died, the challenge ended. The ordinary harpies in the sky all vanished at once.

Yuria was still falling from the sky, but she used magic herself to control her descent speed. She landed softly like a cat and tidied her disheveled hair and clothing.

'Well now. Too easy.'

I didn't even need to lift a finger. Just by entering with them, everything gets finished. I just have to stand still.

Of course, this is all my power. Because I'm the summoner who called them forth.

『1st. Too Easy (Korea)

2nd. Chris Nelson (USA)

3rd. Wang Suan (China)

4th. Michelle Turner (UK)

5th. Marco Loggia (Italy)

…….』

『As a reward for Ranking #1, you may choose one of the following effects.』

『1. Your affiliated nation's Tower coin acquisition rate increases by 5%.』

『2. All stats increase by 1%.』

『You have selected: Affiliated nation's Tower coin acquisition rate increases by 5%.』

Since Lee Yeonhee had asked, I chose the 5% coin acquisition rate increase.

'The US, having lost first place, must be seething, but because of Lee Yeonhee's plan, they can't openly criticize Korea.'

The Ranking #1 reward doesn't end here.

『You acquire additional rewards for the highest record.』

『You acquire 1,400,000 coins.』

『You acquire the Harpy's Wing Shoes.』

『You acquire the key to the Harpy Cliff Valley's secret hideout.』

『Harpy Wing Shoes

Grade: Unique

Once every 24 hours, you may fly freely in the sky for 10 minutes.』

The Harpy Wing Shoes were a reward that appeared in the original work. The catch with these shoes is that if the effect ends while you're airborne, you fall. You have to calculate your descent to land before the 10 minutes are up.

'The key is something I've never seen before. Was the original protagonist's record in the 1-minute range? Maybe you get extra rewards for clearing it faster.'

I could guess its effect. The effects of key-type items in this world are all similar.

『Key to the Harpy Cliff Valley's Secret Hideout.

Grade: Unique

Using the key allows entry into the Floor 14 dungeon zone.

You may enter the hidden location: the Secret Hideout.』

'A dungeon zone entry ticket. This can be sold to others, too.'

Even if I sell it, I'd prioritize selling to my own country.

Dungeons are far more difficult than floor challenges. Instead, coins earned from killing monsters in dungeons are doubled, and clearing a dungeon yields items of at least Unique grade—including special equipment—along with massive amounts of coins.

'Classic high risk, high return. I heard only A-rank hunters and above are deployed to ensure dungeon clears.'

The key point is that you can obtain items of Unique grade or higher.

In other words, I might be able to obtain a top-grade summoning stone from this dungeon.

'I need a top-grade summoning stone to guarantee summoning Miryeong.'

The probability of getting a top-grade summoning stone from a dungeon is less than 1%. If top-grade summoning stones were easy to obtain, Lee Yeonhee would have brought one to me herself.

'But you never know.'

Even if it's high risk, high return, it's still just a 14-floor dungeon.

"We're going into the dungeon right now—that okay? Anyone need to use the bathroom?"

"It hasn't even been 3 minutes since we started the floor challenge. I'm fine."

"Master. I am ready at any time."

"Okay. Let's go."

I gripped the key in my hand.

『Using the Key to the Harpy Cliff Valley's Secret Hideout.』

『Entering Floor 14 dungeon zone.』

『Entering with summons.』

『Slay the Hundred-Winged Harpy Queen.』

We were transported to a parched cliff valley. Front, back, sides—cliffs on all four sides. Hundreds, thousands of harpies perched on the cliff faces.

The harpies, each doing their own thing, froze mid-motion and turned their heads toward us. Women's heads atop bird bodies all swiveled toward us in unison. Immediately after, hostility and killing intent toward humans poured out.

"Too many of them. Dealing with all of them would take time and effort. Better to hide for now."

Snap.

Elena flicked her finger. Her illusion magic wrapped around us.

The harpies that had been directing hostility at us looked confused, turning their heads this way and that.

"I've concealed us with illusion magic. It's a simple illusion, barely more than a visual trick, but it seems sufficient for these creatures."

"Nice. Still, those harpies are coins too—shame to just leave them."

I pulled a poison gas grenade from my inventory and threw it far.

Bang!

Poison gas billowed up in thick clouds. Normally, it's hard to get much effect outdoors, but in this place—cliffs on all sides except the sky—it should be somewhat effective.

Several harpies near the gas grenade staggered and collapsed.

『You acquire 31 coins.』

『You acquire 30 coins.』

『You acquire 33 coins.』

'Easy money. Now the only question is where the hidden location is.'

This is where I'm better than Yuria or Elena.

Thunder Heaven Style—Electromagnetic Wave.

Right now my electromagnetic waves only reach 30 meters. It might honestly be faster to search with the naked eye. However, the advantage of electromagnetic waves is that they can reliably find things that are cleverly hidden, beyond the reach of sight.

"Not here. Let's go elsewhere."

"Yes, Master. Just in case, I'll draw a map."

After wandering for 20 minutes, I found the hidden location. It was a rock cave. A small entrance that required bending at the waist to enter. Going inside revealed a dust-covered hideout.

There were living furnishings—a desk, chair, bed—and miscellaneous items piled in one corner.

On the desk was a journal. It roughly described how adventurers who chased the Hundred-Winged Harpy ended up trapped in this cliff valley. The adventurers, their food running out, resolved to fight the harpies and die.

"Ho. Eugene. I found something interesting here. Hidden in the corner of the bed, tucked away where it's hard to see."

What Elena pulled out was a gold ring set with a rectangular blue gemstone. By my standards, the design was a bit tacky. Still, Elena wouldn't have called it interesting for no reason.

I took the ring and checked it.

『Coin Plunder Ring

Grade: Unique

Plunders 50% of the target's coins and stores coins without limit.

The target to plunder must be within 10 meters.

A target whose coins have been plundered can be plundered again after 10 days.

Currently stored coins: 3,000,000

Plunder uses remaining: 10』

'This is... insanely good? S-rank hunters basically carry hundreds of thousands to tens of millions on them. And I can plunder it just by being within 10 meters? This is a profit among profits.'

For now, the fact that I could plunder other people's coins was just too fun.

'Worth coming all the way here. Now I just need to kill the dungeon boss and head back.'

I'd already pinpointed the location of the dungeon boss—the Hundred-Winged Harpy Queen—on the way here.

"Elena, Yuria. Handle the boss."

"As summons, we should earn our keep."

"Yes. We'll carry it out."

Not even a minute after they disappeared, notification windows popped up.

『You have slain the Hundred-Winged Harpy Queen.』

『Dungeon cleared.』

『You acquire 3,000,000 coins.』

『You acquire a Nation Coin Acquisition Rate 30% Plunder Ticket.』

『Nation Coin Acquisition Rate 30% Plunder Ticket

Grade: Unique

Plunders 30% of the target nation's coin acquisition rate for one week.』

'Heh heh. Now this is interesting.'

The Tower's intent was obvious. It wanted us to fight other nations. The Tower wants a world war to break out.

I exited the Tower. For about 30 minutes of investment, I'd gained a lot.
