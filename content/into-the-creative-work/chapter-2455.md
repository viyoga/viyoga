Recently, the whole world has been buzzing because of one Awakened being. If you turned on the TV or went online, you could easily find stories about this Awakened one.

-An ordinary person Awakened in America at the late age of 40! What's the reason?!

Awakening usually happens in the late teens when physical growth is complete. At the latest, around the 20s. I myself Awakened in my early 20s, didn't I?

Even if you don't Awaken, you can find out if you have the potential through a potential test.

In other words, Awakened beings are all identified before they turn 20, and they can choose their path. Whether to become a Hunter, a monster hunter, or to live as an ordinary person. These days, they choose the former. It's dangerous, but it's safer than in the old days, and the income level is higher than that of ordinary people. There's also the fact that they can be admired by ordinary people.

-How did Mason Awaken despite having no potential?

Awakening despite having no potential was so unusual that even I was surprised. Even I, who Awakened later than others, had potential. Thanks to that, I was able to enter the Hunter Department of Gukcheon University without even taking the college entrance exam.

,Could there have been an error in the potential test? I think I heard that errors sometimes occur.'

Even if there is an error in the potential test, it doesn't matter. If you have potential, you'll eventually Awaken around your 20s.

-Mason was an illegal porter. He illegally forged his identity and entered dungeons even though he wasn't a Hunter!

It goes without saying that ordinary people can't enter dungeons. Even Hunters die if they're careless, so you can't put ordinary people with no abilities in dungeons. It's only natural that it's controlled. That's why association employees control access at the dungeon entrance. To prevent ordinary people from entering, just in case.

For that reason, all porters are Hunters. The lowest-ranked, F-rank Hunters with no background do porter work. As they gain experience, they move away from porter work and start hunting monsters. That's the standard route.

It's not for nothing that there are Hunter training centers and Hunter academies. It's all to learn combat skills as quickly as possible and become a hunter instead of a porter.

-Mason said, "I started working as a porter by forging my identity three years ago because it became difficult to make a living. That's when I started entering dungeons. I went in 2-3 times a week, and at some point, I Awakened. I believe that ordinary people can Awaken if they consistently enter dungeons. It's unfair that ordinary people can't enter dungeons because they're dangerous. The Hunter Association is taking away ordinary people's chances of Awakening."

It's not an impossible story.

There's more that's unknown about dungeons than what's known. Most dungeons have a higher mana concentration than Earth. There's a chance that mana Awakened Mason.

,The association is fucked.'

Most ordinary people envy Hunters. In other words, it also means they're jealous.

They just got lucky and Awakened, but they earn money more easily than others, use mysterious and convenient abilities, and live longer without suffering from minor illnesses. It would be strange not to be jealous.

Until now, they've suppressed it simply because there was no way. Hunters fight risking their lives, so they couldn't easily mess with them.

,If ordinary people can become Awakened, the story changes. They won't hate Hunters, but they'll hate the Hunter Association, which monopolizes dungeons under the pretext of managing them. Of course, ordinary people will demand that they be allowed to enter dungeons too.'

The Hunter Association will refuse for safety reasons. Ordinary people can die even in F-rank dungeons. Hunters can protect ordinary people? Why would a Hunter do that? Does that make more money than hunting monsters? Who's responsible if an ordinary person dies?

,Even I'm thinking this way, so how much more would other Hunters think? It's going to be noisy for a while.'

Having had various experiences, I could clearly see how things would turn out in the future.

Brrr.

My smartphone vibrated. The caller was Kang Seok-soo.

The master and representative of the Seventier Guild.

Of course, I was also a master of the Seventier Guild. Seventier has a total of 7 guild masters, including me and Kang Seok-soo. The representative of Seventier is chosen every two years through a vote and meeting among the masters.

"It's me."

-You still answer the phone strangely.

"Whatever. What's the matter?"

I'm just a name on the list in the Seventier Guild.

I'm not very interested in guild activities. Guild support? I have a life of leisure, so why bother. If I wanted my own proper power, I would have created and run my own guild.

The reason I put my name on Seventier is to block other guilds from bothering me.

-You know that Seventier has become known to the public. Taking advantage of the momentum, we're recruiting guild members this time.

"Is it a problem because no one wants to join Seventier? Well, who would join a no-name guild."

-No, it's the opposite. Too many people have gathered. Even those who belonged to the existing top 10 guilds have subtly conveyed their desire to join Seventier to me.

"Really? I don't know what they're trusting to join Seventier. Was your popularity that good?"

-What are you talking about? It's because of you. S-rank candidate Seong Yujin. If you become an S-rank Hunter like Han Ah-young, Seventier's leap will be comparable to the top 10 guilds. Those who are fed up with the existing top 10 guilds are pinning their hopes on the new change that is Seventier.

It's only natural that Seventier's stock price is going crazy just because there's an S-rank candidate. Even though Seventier isn't a listed company.

"Can't you just accept them all?"

-I wish I could, but if I do, it won't be manageable. And the top 10 guilds won't stand still. I don't want to provoke the top 10 guilds right now.

"Hmm. So? What's the real matter?"

-This time, we've decided to film a program introducing Seventier on cable TV. Of course, I'd like you to come out. They say strike while the iron is hot, so we need to strike properly.

Do you think I'd do something like that?

I stopped myself from snapping back.

"...Do celebrities come too? When I saw the guild introduction broadcast last time, celebrities came and were like, 'Wow, amazing!'"

-I heard that celebrities are needed for the fun and progress of the broadcast.

"Hmm. It's not bad to appear on TV. It's not like it's my first time appearing on TV."

It would be good for Seventier's growth too. If I cooperate, Seventier will grow faster.

-Good. I'll send you the schedule in a message later. Contact me so we can adjust it to your side.

"Okay. By the way, Kang Seok-soo. Has your mind changed since you recruited me into Seventier?"

-Of course not. The order formed by the existing top 10 guilds. My goal is to break that order.

At first, I wasn't very interested in Kang Seok-soo's goal. But things have changed with various events.

"I've also started to dislike the top 10 guilds. Especially the Suwol Guild. If I get the chance, I'm going to destroy the Suwol Guild."

"Ah-young noona is on my side."

-In that case, there's nothing to worry about. If the Suwol Guild collapses, the Hunter world will change greatly. But not right now. Seventier is still lacking in power.

=Breaking the order of the top 10 guilds is the same as antagonizing the top 10 guilds. I've already made up my mind.

Certainly, when I received the offer from Kang Seok-soo, I heard about that purpose. I was so full of thoughts of using Seventier that I didn't think it was that important.

Unlike me, who does things half-heartedly, the other guild masters must have agreed with Kang Seok-soo, so they became Seventier guild masters.

Yoo Jungmi.

Kang Seok-soo's childhood friend and girlfriend. I had sex with Yoo Jungmi.

-She said she developed feelings for someone else.
