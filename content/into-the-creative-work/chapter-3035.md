After meeting the women and briefly stopping by my hometown to see my parents, I returned home and started the game.

[You have selected Radiant Ascension Path.]

[Starting the game.]

In the world of Radiant Ascension Path, I had finally become the master of the Heavenly Demon Cult.

Heavenly Demon became my name, and within the Heavenly Demon Cult, I gained absolute power that none could challenge.

So, now that I'd become the Heavenly Demon, should I go to war with the Martial Alliance? I mulled it over briefly, then immediately shook my head.

'Too much hassle. More than anything, the gains wouldn't be that significant.'

The world is vast.

The territory the Martial Alliance controls is a mere handful. Sects belonging to the Martial Alliance ruling cities? Don't make me laugh. The one ruling this world is the Emperor. The prestigious orthodox sects under the Martial Alliance are nothing more than regional administrators operating under the Emperor's grace.

'In Joseon terms, they're basically magistrates.'

Whether it's the Heavenly Demon Cult or the Martial Alliance, if the Emperor decided to move, he could wipe them out anytime. That's precisely why the Emperor doesn't pay much attention to the martial world.

'Even if I went to war with the Martial Alliance and won, there's almost nothing to gain. At best, I'd get a hollow title like Strongest in the Martial World.'

As long as the Emperor existed, I could never become the strongest under heaven.

'Still, within the Heavenly Demon Cult, I am king.'

Leaning back in an ornate chair, I crooked a finger. A maidservant who had been waiting silently in a corner of the hall approached without a sound and bowed her head.

"Yes, Cult Leader."

"Recite today's schedule."

"Yes. This morning, the bestowal of grace is scheduled, and this afternoon, the Martial Alliance's delegation will arrive at the main cult."

Bestowal of grace.

As the Heavenly Demon, I bestow grace upon female martial artists. Put simply, it's sex. Having mastered the Thunder Heaven Style and the Heavenly Demon Arts, I overflowed with immense yang energy, and through dual cultivation techniques, I could transfer that vast yang energy to women.

For female martial artists, my semen was itself a tremendous elixir.

'By normal common sense, this is insane. No matter how much yang energy you have, if you keep getting sucked dry, you'll hit rock bottom. Especially with the Plunder Yang to Supplement Yin or Plunder Yin to Supplement Yang techniques—if you transfer massive energy to a partner, you'll damage your own meridians.'

You ask if dual cultivation techniques make it okay? Dual cultivation only has meaning when two energies are constantly harmonized and circulated. When one side simply absorbs, it's not much different from the Absorption Grand Technique.

'Well, it is safer and more efficient than the Absorption Grand Technique.'

For me, since I have Complete Recovery, there's practically no downside. Even if I get sucked dry until my yang energy hits bottom, Complete Recovery restores me to full in an instant.

'I feel good from the sex, and the Heavenly Demon Cult gets stronger. Hehe.'

Have I been doing this for ten years already? I had auto-progression running, so it's probably about that long.

Currently, the Heavenly Demon Cult has a considerable number of female martial artists. And many of them were beautiful women. That's because I primarily bestowed grace upon beautiful women.

'In a few more decades, the Heavenly Demon Cult might become a female-dominated group. Hehe.'

And I control those women.

'If I become stronger than the Emperor someday far in the future... this world will be mine to rule.'

But before that, first.

I looked at the maidservant.

Her beauty was exceptional. Naturally—I had selected her for her looks rather than her skill. That said, she wasn't completely without ability. You couldn't hold the position of Heavenly Demon's maidservant without skill.

"Today, I'll bestow grace upon you specially."

"Thank you, Cult Leader!"

The maidservant smiled brightly and undid her clothing ties. She approached me, displaying her pale white naked body.

• • •

When afternoon came, I received the Martial Alliance's delegation.

I greeted the Martial Alliance's delegation alongside the Heavenly Demon Cult's elders. The delegation's representative, a man called Bear Beggar from the Beggar's Sect, stepped forward without hesitation and cupped his fists in salute.

"Bear Beggar of the Martial Alliance greets the Heavenly Demon."

"Yeah. True to your name, you're built like a bear."

Nearly 2 meters tall with burly muscles, even his martial uniform. He looked like a powerful martial artist, not a beggar.

Ever since I'd asked the Emperor back then, there were no beggars in the Empire. If beggars appeared, government troops would arrest them and force them to work so they'd stop being beggars.

The Beggar's Sect, the beggars' sect, was no exception. Because of this, the current Beggar's Sect had lost its identity as beggars and was closer to an ordinary martial sect.

'For all that, he's tied a straw rope knot at his waist, claiming Beggar's Sect origins.'

Seven knots.

It meant elder-level even within the Beggar's Sect.

"Your business?"

"How cold. Does the Heavenly Demon Cult not even offer a guest a cup of tea?"

"You're alive because you're a guest. If you weren't a guest, you'd already be dead with just your head hanging in the marketplace."

Bear Beggar and the Martial Alliance delegation's martial artists glared with bulging eyes and raised their killing intent. I could feel their determination not to be taken lightly.

"Enough."

Thud.

While seated, I lightly rolled my right foot.

Heavenly Demon Arts—Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

The wave of energy crushed the delegation's momentum.

"Kuh!"

Even Bear Beggar, an absolute master at the Seventh Stage of the Three Realms, was no exception. He gritted his teeth and endured the pressure of the Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

'I only intended to suppress their momentum... and he's struggling that much? A so-called Seventh Stage of the Three Realms?'

Even if cultivation level isn't everything when it comes to strength, he seemed excessively weak. By my standards, I'd say he's at the early stage of the Three Realms, not even the middle stage.

'Beggar's Sect origins. Wasn't the current Martial Alliance Leader from the Beggar's Sect? It's coming together. They must have focused on raising his cultivation level with elixirs alone.'

I looked down at Bear Beggar, who was sweating cold sweat and catching his breath, and smirked.

"Beggar bastards never know their place. Considering your lowly origins, I'll forgive your rudeness just this once. There won't be a second time."

"Grit... Thank you, Heavenly Demon."

"I can hear that gnashing from here."

"It's an unfortunate habit of mine... Grind. Whenever I speak, without realizing... Grit, I grind my teeth."

"What a pathetic habit. I understand."

When I chuckled, some of the elders beside me also snickered and sneered at the Martial Alliance delegation. The delegation's faces flushed crimson. They looked like they might burst, but they clamped their mouths shut and endured.

'If they'd mouthed off, I would've killed them on the spot.'

Killing them without justification would damage the Heavenly Demon Cult's prestige. I didn't possess absolute power like the Emperor, so I needed to maintain a certain external image.

"Your business? I'm asking for the second time."

"The Martial Alliance wishes for a truce with the Heavenly Demon Cult. A fifty-year truce. What do you say?"

Fifty years was the average lifespan of an ordinary person. Even for martial artists whose lifespans were extended through qi cultivation, it was no small span of time.

"What's the reason for wanting a truce?"

"Recently, battles between the Martial Alliance and the Heavenly Demon Cult have been few and far between. Yet because we're technically at war, there are many losses in various ways. The main alliance has judged that the current Heavenly Demon Cult has no will for expansion."

"We could attack the Martial Alliance right now if we wanted."

"The Martial Alliance is not to be taken lightly, Heavenly Demon."

"You're the ones hoping for a truce."

"I understand the Heavenly Demon Cult has yet to recover from the losses of ten years ago. For all its reputation, even the elder seats haven't all been filled."

It was true that the Heavenly Demon Cult currently had some vacant elder positions. It was the aftermath of ten years ago. But I couldn't just promote any random person to elder rank, so I'd left them empty.

Objectively speaking, it was undeniable that the Heavenly Demon Cult's power was weaker than ten years ago.

"It seems our cult has been looked down upon quite thoroughly. Cult Leader, if you give the order, I will personally cut down that insolent wretch and announce to the world that our cult remains strong."

Sword Demon Yeon Sirang, the Right Guardian, stroked his sword hilt and radiated sharp killing intent. At the killing intent of the man who could be called second strongest in the Heavenly Demon Cult after me, the Martial Alliance delegation's faces went pale. Even so, Bear Beggar, being the representative, tried to stand his ground.

"Enough. If I kill the delegation here, what does that make me?"

"...My thoughts were short-sighted, Cult Leader."

"Indeed. Let's do well."

Yeon Sirang withdrew his killing intent. The inflated atmosphere loosened, if only slightly.

"What's the real reason the Martial Alliance wants a truce?"

"As I said, we merely judged that both sides need time."

"Bullshit. Who'd believe that? For the past ten years, our cult and the Martial Alliance haven't had any notable battles. To the point where rumors that the war was over were circulating in the streets. And now you come asking for a formal truce?"

"We've merely come to convey the main alliance's wishes."

I put my mind to work.

With my level of experience, I could make a decent guess.

"The reason you want a truce... you don't have the capacity to guard against the Heavenly Demon Cult? No—are you about to lose that capacity?"

“…….”

"Hehe. I'm getting a feel for it. Is a secret realm about to open near the Martial Alliance?"

"A secret realm? This is the first I've heard of it."

Secret realm.

You could call it a kind of dungeon. Compared to dungeons in reality, they're far more dangerous, but the rewards you can gain are proportionally immense. Simply put, high risk, high return.

In this world too, secret realms open fairly often. Most are handled by the Imperial Army, who monopolize the rewards. And there are almost none impressive enough to catch my attention. Thanks to the Heavenly River Star system, I can obtain elixirs just by sitting still.

But for the Martial Alliance to propose a fifty-year truce? A tremendous secret realm must have appeared, or is certain to appear.

"Are you pretending not to know? Or do you really not know? Well, fine. Go back and tell that beggar bastard. The truce period will be ten years. The condition is that the Heavenly Demon Cult also gets to enter the secret realm."

"That's absurd! Do you think the main alliance would accept such a proposal?!"

"If it's not a truce, then there's no problem with us attacking you, is there? Even if a secret realm opened in your front yard, you wouldn't be able to commit your forces because of us. Hehe. The moment you send people into the secret realm, we'll raid your empty house."

"...I will convey your proposal to the Alliance Leader."

"Good. I'm looking forward to what's in that secret realm. Hehe."
