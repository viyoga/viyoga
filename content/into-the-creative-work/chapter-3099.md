Park Soo-ho and I headed to New York, where the World Hunter Association headquarters was located, while Han Ah-young returned to Korea. At first, Han Ah-young wanted to come along too, but both Korea and the US refused.

Korea was uneasy about all its active S-rank hunters going to America, and the US was wary of two foreign S-rank hunters entering its borders.

We traveled to New York aboard a warship.

"I'm glad you came with me, hyung. Just thinking about going to the World Hunter Association headquarters alone.... Whew. I'm nervous for no reason. They say the World Hunter Association is an organization even the US can't ignore.... You think we'll be fine?"

There was no shortage of rumors about the World Hunter Association. Whispers circulated freely that it was some kind of extralegal body, a dark magic power that ruled the world. Other rumors claimed it was just an organization with a fancy name and nothing more.

The truth was that the World Hunter Association rarely operated publicly, which was what spawned all the wild speculation.

"It's surprisingly nothing special. It's a place that even walks on eggshells around S-rank hunters."

"But I'm not an S-rank hunter."

"At your level, you're an S-rank candidate."

Park Soo-ho's ability was so peculiar that even I had a hard time gauging the full extent of his power. Still, it was certain he was at least above your average A-rank hunter.

"Hyung. What have you been doing on your smartphone this whole time? Doesn't look like you're gaming."

"Just surfing the web. Do you read webtoons?"

"Maybe one or two when I've got time to kill?"

[Hacking duration remaining: 27 minutes 5 seconds.]

In reality, I was hacking the smartphones of Juan and his secretary, who were aboard the same warship.

'Took a look—the secretary's real identity is FBI. Seems he works with the FBI on various things. Handling jobs for them in exchange for money, tax breaks, and other benefits.'

He was connected to the mafia, gangs, and more. Not just them, but also some fairly well-known Las Vegas businesses.

'Well, he's definitely raking in a fuckton of money, but he's spending a fuckton too.'

His contacts were packed with famous American celebrities.

'Even drugs?'

As an S-rank hunter, drugs wouldn't have much effect on him. Hunters had higher drug resistance than ordinary people.

'Nectar. So he was one of the Great Medicine's main customers. Is this bastard's money sitting in my account?'

First, I looked into Juan's family.

If he had parents or children, I planned to cut their heads off and throw a surprise party.

'Parents divorced when he was young. Ties with his father severed, and his mother was captured and killed by a criminal guild ten years ago.'

Before becoming an S-rank hunter, Juan ran with criminal organizations. He lived like absolute trash until luck smiled on him and he became an S-rank. He had some relatives, but no contact with them. Cutting off their heads as a gift wouldn't get any reaction.

'There are women, but... they're all sugar babies. Not a single one he's kept in touch with for more than half a year.'

On the surface, it looked like a run-of-the-mill fucked-up life, but Juan had money and power. The US government even smoothed things over for him.

'A piece of trash this bad—I can kill him out in the open.'

The US wouldn't want anyone digging into Juan's affairs. If the dirty business tied to him got out, America's standing would plummet. The president probably wouldn't escape responsibility either.

'What's annoying is the FBI.'

Juan's secretary had written up a report on me and sent it to the FBI. Skimming through it, the assessment was that I had a temper as nasty as Juan's and would be just as hard to control. My lightning ability was judged even more dangerous than Juan's.

Eliminating me would be ideal, but it would require at least two S-rank hunters and would provoke pushback from the World Hunter Association—so the recommendation was to hold off and maintain friendly relations.

'You believe you can eliminate me if you decide to? Really?'

I decided to kill Juan's secretary and the FBI director too.

'Kill them all.'

America becomes my enemy? Not a big problem. I was confident.

'For the time being, I should stay at the World Hunter Association headquarters with Park Soo-ho. He'll be my alibi. The World Hunter Association seems to suspect I have a means of spatial movement.... But there's no evidence.'

Even without using Information Erasure, hacking and spatial movement scrolls alone made the perfect crime easy enough.

'Heh heh. This should be fun for a change. The FBI director has a family too—I'll have to kill them as well.'

The FBI had done some investigation into my family. It was obvious they intended to take my family hostage or kill them if things went sideways.

'If Mom and Dad die because of this... I'll take thorough revenge, then bring them back.'

Park Soo-ho and I rested at a luxury hotel in New York before heading to the World Hunter Association headquarters.

"Whoa. This building is no joke."

Beside the towering UN headquarters building stood another one even larger. The World Hunter Association headquarters. It was a massive structure that would have been a landmark if it were in any ordinary city instead of New York.

"There's something more important than the building. Can you feel it?"

"Yes. Powerful magic and barriers.... Somehow it feels more cutthroat than a military base."

We followed our guide into the first-floor lobby.

In the first-floor lobby, a middle-aged man with salt-and-pepper hair and a beard was waiting. Dressed immaculately in a suit like a gentleman, he smiled softly as he looked at Park Soo-ho and me.

"Welcome to New York, Hunter Sung Yoo-jin, Hunter Park Soo-ho. I am James Craft, serving as the Association President."

Park Soo-ho stammered, flustered that the Association President himself had come out to meet us.

"I-I'm Park Soo-ho. Y-you speak Korean incredibly well?"

"No. I'm not good at Korean. It's only thanks to a translation artifact that it sounds like your native tongue."

Looking at the Association President, I felt a strange sensation.

'When the world was destroyed because of the Virgin Ghost, we met and talked.'

I was the only one who remembered that.

"Now, both of you, please come inside. I've prepared a welcome tea."

We followed him.

The space shifted in an instant. Park Soo-ho and I were in a reception room. I plopped down carelessly on a prepared chair. Park Soo-ho, startled by the sudden spatial shift, took his seat.

"Magic is cast throughout the headquarters building, allowing spatial movement like this. Most people visiting the headquarters for the first time are quite surprised... but you're not surprised at all, Yoo-jin."

It wasn't that I wasn't surprised. When I came here before the regression, the place had been in complete chaos.

"It's just spatial movement."

"Haha. That's right. Just spatial movement. Do either of you have any questions for me? I'll answer as sincerely as I can."

I drank the black tea he'd prepared. Questions? I didn't really have any. Whatever he was truly hiding, he obviously wouldn't tell us.

Park Soo-ho was nervously choosing his words. I tossed a question at James offhandedly.

"Hypothetically, if S-rank hunters from different countries fight each other, how does the World Hunter Association respond?"

"Our World Hunter Association does not intervene in personal disputes between hunters, but in the case of S-rank hunters, we attempt mediation to the fullest extent possible."

"And if the S-rank hunters refuse mediation?"

"We determine fault and impose penalties. If the dispute still doesn't stop.... There's nothing we can do. We focus on damage control."

"That's all? Surprising."

"The World Hunter Association does not take sides with individuals or specific nations. We will maintain neutrality to the greatest extent possible. From the start, an S-rank hunter is like a king. We respect the power and authority of S-rank hunters."

"Even if an S-rank hunter commits murder?"

"Unless it directly opposes the Association, non-intervention is our principle."

It was the answer I'd expected. What the World Hunter Association pursued wasn't justice.

Unlike me, Park Soo-ho couldn't accept James's words right away. His eyes went wide with shock.

"How can that.... Is the World Hunter Association really allowed to do that?!"

"Soo-ho. The World Hunter Association is not the police. The purpose of our existence is world order. To prevent this world's destruction and protect it. We exist for that, and for that, we will do anything."

James's tone and demeanor were calm, but his aura was akin to madness.

Park Soo-ho faltered. It seemed he was meeting someone like James for the first time.

"...World destruction? You can't just say something like that out of nowhere...."

"Soo-ho. Even if it sounds like chasing clouds, the world stands on the brink of destruction. This world, Earth, is not the only world of civilization. Other dimensions exist. You know this well enough without me telling you, Soo-ho."

"......You know about Selbriar?"

"So the dimension you know is called Selbriar. There are many other dimensions, so I'd have to check the records, but it has likely affected this world, Earth. Soo-ho. We desperately need your help—your ability to travel to other dimensions."

A tedious conversation began between James and Park Soo-ho. I picked up my smartphone and read webtoons.

Their conversation ended two hours later. In conclusion, Park Soo-ho agreed to cooperate with the World Hunter Association. In exchange, he would receive various benefits and support.

"Yoo-jin hyung. I've got some things to look into, so I've decided to stay at the headquarters for a bit. About three days. You can head back to Korea first, hyung."

"No. Since we're here, I'll stay too. It's New York, after all."

Staying at the World Hunter Association headquarters was the better option.

'Let's start with the FBI first.'

I'd already mapped out all their locations, so it wouldn't take long.

Juan Perez resided on the top floor of the tallest building in Las Vegas. Inside a room with dazzling, ornate interior, he gazed down through the window and picked up the nectar in his hand. Just as he was about to swallow it, something caught his mana sense.

'I gave orders that no one is to come within five floors of the top without my permission. Roy may be insolent, but he follows my orders like clockwork. An assassin?'

Juan let out a hollow laugh.

An assassin.

It was the first time since he'd officially become an S-rank hunter.

'Back in the day, all sorts of trash came at me. Now that I think about it, I almost miss it. Let's see what kind of bold bastard this is.'

He'd been bored, so this was welcome.

He wouldn't just kill them. He'd find out who was backing them, then kill everyone behind them too.

Juan set down the nectar and waited for the intruder, both feet propped on the table in an arrogant pose.

CRASH!

The door flew off its hinges. The intruder had kicked it in. The moment Juan confirmed the intruder's identity, his face twisted.

"You goddamn little punk.... Do you even know what you've just done?"

"Of course I know. Here, this is a gift. Picked it up on the way."

Sung Yoo-jin hurled the corpse he was holding at Juan. The body flew through the air, then suddenly lost momentum and tumbled onto the table.

Automatic Weakening.

Everything that entered within a 5-meter radius of Juan was weakened. Bullets, missiles, bombs—no material was exempt.

Juan checked the corpse.

Roy.

His secretary and an FBI agent, dead with limbs torn apart and eyeballs burst.

"This one is an FBI agent. You just killed a US FBI agent. You've made an enemy of America."

"Yeah. I killed the bastard who touched me. And these ones too, as a bonus."

Sung Yoo-jin flicked his finger. The air split open and dozens of corpses dropped out. From obvious agents to women and boys who looked like civilians. Juan's one eye widened as he took in their bodies.

"...The FBI director? What the fuck. Are you on drugs or something?"

"You're the one on drugs. That thing on the table is nectar."

"You lunatic. You've messed with America. Do you know how many S-rank hunters operate in the US? Officially twenty-two, but counting unofficial ones, it's over thirty. Even a quarter of that is more than enough firepower to kill you and have plenty left over."

"Thirty S-rank hunters. Doesn't really feel like I'd lose."

"Tch. I should've never gotten tangled up with a retard like you from the start."

"No need to stall for time. I've blocked all communications. No matter how many times you hit the emergency beacon, no backup is coming for a while."

Juan irritably tossed the emergency pager in his hand. It flew at Sung Yoo-jin like a bullet, but before it could even get close, blue sparks flashed in midair and it exploded.

"Ha. Fuck. Guess I can't wrap this up half-assed. I can already picture the government throwing a fit about a foreign S-rank hunter being killed."

Juan rose from his seat, pulled gloves from his pocket, and put them on. The gloves glowed faintly, enhancing his body.

Enhance himself while weakening the opponent with his ability, then beat them down. It was Juan's simple yet surefire combat method.

Thunder Heaven Style—Thunder Soul Arhat.

BZZZZT!

Blue lightning crackled from Sung Yoo-jin's body. His entire form blazed with lightning. He looked like a half-human, half-lightning hybrid.

Juan was unbothered. No matter what form his opponent took, they couldn't escape his weakening.

Juan raised both hands and took a basic boxing stance.

Sung Yoo-jin approached. He took a single step, yet was suddenly right in front of Juan. Juan wasn't flustered. He already knew his opponent's ability was acceleration.

'Once you're in my range, you have no choice but to weaken.'

Left straight.

The fist cleanly struck Sung Yoo-jin's jaw. Yet Sung Yoo-jin didn't even budge.

"...What?"

He looked utterly indifferent, as if he hadn't taken any damage at all.

He wasn't weakened?

As Juan panicked and tried to create distance, Sung Yoo-jin's fist slammed into his face.

KWAARRGH!

Juan flew backward and was embedded into the wall.
