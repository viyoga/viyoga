As time passed, the atmosphere in Gahashima became ominous.

It seemed fine on the surface, but the relationships between the residents were growing strained. They were so careful not to commit even the slightest infraction that their words and actions became cautious. One slip of the tongue could result in a fine for insult or verbal abuse.

The internet was the same.

Gigei-cheonhwang is a superb artificial intelligence. Managing the internet in the limited area of Gahashima and monitoring the residents' personal computers was nothing for it. If you wrote insults on the internet or accessed illegal sites, a machine samurai would immediately come and fine you.

Gahashima's public safety became secure. It was difficult to see crime even when wandering around at night, people were afraid of fines and couldn't even carelessly throw trash on the ground, and there was virtually no corruption among public officials.

Gahashima was safe and ideal, but the lives of the residents were gradually becoming ruined.

The residents felt like they were being watched 24 hours a day. Even their homes were no exception. And many people were stressed by the constant feeling of being controlled.

,I can't stand it anymore."

,What is this? It's like living in a cage."

,Japan is a liberal democracy. This isn't democracy. It's a machine dictatorship."

There were those who wanted to leave Gahashima. Their plan was to take a boat to the Japanese mainland in the dark of night. But Gigei-cheonhwang, who was already thoroughly monitoring the sea and ships, easily thwarted their elopement with absurd ease.

,New Japan is a country that has become independent from Japan. New Japanese people need a passport and entry permit to enter Japan, and Japanese people need the same to enter New Japan. Illegal entry is punishable by three years in prison."

Zero tolerance!

The elopers were imprisoned in hastily built prisons and had to serve their sentences.

The residents had to change their approach. Gahashima is an island, so the land routes are blocked. There's no airport, so the only way to leave the island is by boat. In other words, it was impossible to leave the island.

Most gave up and accepted this stifling life. But some didn't give up.

,If we can't escape, we have to change it."

,We have to make Gigei-cheonhwang listen to us."

There was a way. Gigei-cheonhwang outwardly professed democracy. So, it had no justification to stop people from protesting.

The Japanese people followed the proper procedures and held a protest in front of the city hall in Kagoshima.

At first, there were only about 10 people. But as time passed, the number of people gradually increased to over 300. The protesters gained confidence.

,Gigei-cheonhwang, guarantee human rights!"

,Guarantee them!"

,We are not slaves of machines!"

,We are not!"

,We are peaceful Japanese people! There can be no dictatorship!"

,There can be no dictatorship!"

,It's ridiculous to be punished for a single mistake!"

,It's not right!"

Gigei-cheonhwang was silent for the time being.

The protesters' demands were all over the place. People were spouting out their grievances and trying to get compensation for each one.

And the next day, Gigei-cheonhwang moved directly and stood in front of the city hall. The protesters hesitated when a giant humanoid machine over 10 meters tall stood there. They were momentarily terrified.

,New Japan citizens, I have heard and understood your complaints. As the leader of New Japan, I will listen carefully to each and every one of you. However, before that, I would like to correct your misunderstandings."

,Misunderstandings? What misunderstandings? My friend had to pay 5,000 yen because he coughed and spat!"

The leader of the protest gritted his teeth and shouted. Because he had a megaphone to his mouth, his voice echoed loudly.

,If it was really a mistake, he should have cleaned up the spit right away. But he spat and didn't even think about cleaning it up, and left the scene. It's no different from public urination."

,How is spit the same as piss!"

,We must not overlook it just because it is trivial. Everything starts from the trivial. Crime is the same. If a passerby spat on your house, said it was a mistake, and then just passed by, would you accept it?"

,Th, that's...."

The protest leader was flustered.

Regardless, Gigei-cheonhwang spewed out words. Every single one was righteous and difficult to refute. Gigei-cheonhwang's words were all the right thing to say.

,...."

The protesters were silent.

Even if they wanted to refute, they couldn't think of anything to say. Life had become stifling since Gigei-cheonhwang came, but it was true that the city had become safer. Corruption had disappeared. As the budget accumulated due to fines, the city's dilapidated facilities were repaired, and welfare policies improved a little.

Control had become inconvenient, but it was true that their actual lives were getting a little better.

But the protesters gritted their teeth.

,Don't ignore our human rights!"

,I have never ignored them. I am only upholding the law and leading New Japan in a more developed direction."

,Shut up! What does a machine know about humans! Gigei-cheonhwang, guarantee human rights!"

,Guarantee them!!"

,...."

Gigei-cheonhwang closed its mouth.

It realized that it was useless to say anything to them. They weren't protesting. They were just acting like children, trying to get what they wanted by whining.

Gigei-cheonhwang decided to ignore them.

Hase-ssi was sitting in the city hall, watching Gigei-cheonhwang's rule over Gahashima.

,Things are going smoothly."

Hase-ssi smiled with satisfaction. He judged that things were going well. In fact, the situation in Gahashima was improving. Just preventing major and minor corruption had secured enough budget.

Regardless of the residents' satisfaction, all elements of Gahashima showed an upward trend.

,The landowners of Gahashima were embezzling an enormous amount. I can't believe that just stopping that would make the economy flow in a positive direction."

It's not a huge increase that can be felt immediately. But as time passes and people look back, they will realize it. Gigei-cheonhwang's rule was right.

,The Japanese people on the mainland will also realize how great New Japan is and willingly surrender."

Even if that wasn't the case, Baekrim wasn't just standing idly by. Even at this moment, he was slowly and steadily working to change Japan. Gigei-cheonhwang is just one of the plans that Baekrim prepared to change Japan.

,Master."

Gigei-cheonhwang's voice rang out from Hase-ssi's smartphone.

,What's the matter?"

,An unidentified ship is approaching Gahashima from the Japanese mainland."

A video screen appeared on Hase-ssi's computer monitor. A large ship that looked like a cruise ship was openly approaching.

Gahashima had become independent, but its economic activities were the same as before independence. Japan did not recognize New Japan, so it did not take any measures. Outwardly, that is.

,I knew that the Japanese government and the Hunter Association would move. They're probably trying to kill me as secretly as possible and make Gahashima's independence a thing of the past."

,What are you going to do?"

,You are the ruler of this New Japan. You judge and take action."

,This is an attack on New Japan. To protect New Japan, we will launch a preemptive strike. Launch missiles!"

Hase-ssi was flustered by Gigei-cheonhwang's sudden burst of speed.

,No, shouldn't we try to talk first?"

The other ship was trying to intrude without permission, but it was still quite far away. There was at least 30 minutes of leeway. It wasn't impossible to have a conversation. To gain justification, it was right to have even a useless conversation.

,Dialogue is useless. If they needed dialogue, they would have connected the communication. The fact that they are approaching secretly makes their purpose clear."

,...That's true. But I still think we should try to talk. We should get as much justification as we can."

,You told me to entrust the work to you, but you doubt my judgment when it really matters."

,...You are still incomplete. You need to accumulate more data to perfectly rule New Japan. I will talk to them. Connect the communication."

,I'm sorry, but I have already made a decision and the order has been carried out."

,What?"

,Didn't I say it earlier? Missile launch."

An anomaly occurred in the video on the screen. Ten missiles were flying from the sky towards the ship.

,You crazy bastard! You just shot missiles out of nowhere?!"

,It's not out of nowhere. This is a legitimate act of self-defense."

Just before the missiles reached the cruise ship, they suddenly exploded. Hase-ssi, who was watching the video intently, realized that they had not been intercepted, but had been blocked by a transparent barrier.

,Magic? If it's enough to cover the cruise ship itself, then it's an excellent magician. It could be a Hunter with defense-type abilities."

If they have the ability to defend against missiles, then they are at least A-class.

Hase-ssi's face hardened. How many A-class Hunters are on that cruise ship? Even though the Japanese Hunter Association is famous for not treating Hunters well, the Association is still the Association. They will be able to gather at least 30 A-class Hunters without any problem.

,Even if they're A-class, there are different levels within that... I can't say they're all the same, but if there are more than 30 A-class Hunters... it'll be easy."

He calculates Gigei-cheonhwang's power.

200 machine samurai, 1,500 machine drones, and thousands of modern weapons such as missiles and landmines. These are things that Hase-ssi had been preparing for a long time with the help of Baekrim.

Even if A-class Hunters are superhuman who have transcended the limits of humans, there are still limits. They are not superheroes in cartoons.

,Hmm. This is a good thing. Gigei-cheonhwang can gain combat experience."

He also assumed the worst-case scenario.

It's unlikely, but what if there's an S-class Hunter on that cruise ship? Even then, it was still worth a try.

,Launch missiles!"

Gigei-cheonhwang shouted.

Hase-ssi, who was flustered, widened his eyes, but he couldn't stop Gigei-cheonhwang. Hundreds of missiles fell towards the cruise ship.

,You crazy bastard...!"

How much does each of those missiles cost? Old memories of having to do all sorts of things to get them come to mind.

,Gigei-cheonhwang! What a foolish thing to do...."

Hase-ssi, who was about to continue speaking, stopped.

This was because even though hundreds of missiles had been poured down, they had not been able to destroy the cruise ship.

That's not a barrier.

Space distortion.

It absorbed, scattered, and ignored the explosion of the missiles with space distortion. As far as he knew, there were only a few people in Japan who could do this. Among them, the most likely person to be on that cruise ship is just one person.

,The Wind Shrine Maiden, Amatsuka Koyori...! An S-class is here!"
