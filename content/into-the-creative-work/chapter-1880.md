The disgusting smell greeted me as soon as I entered the spider-infested building. You could call it a musty smell. A smell that seemed to stick thickly to my bronchial tubes. Fortunately, it didn't seem to be poisonous gas, but if you smelled this for a long time, you'd get a headache.

"Do as you were trained. Maintain formation."

Gu Tae-hee commanded her subordinates emotionlessly. The subordinates accepted the order and paid attention to the formation. The guys in plate armor standing close together looked as solid as a wall.

On the other hand, I sighed when I saw the security team. There was no formation or anything. They just looked like gun-toting morons. At least Park Hae-gil-ssi, from PS Security, and his subordinates were well-positioned. Holding assault rifles, they leaned their upper bodies slightly and took a defensive posture. A special forces background wasn't just for show.

"Who's going to be in the lead?"

Gu Tae-hee asked me. Looking at her eyes, she wanted me to be in the lead. I didn't like the idea of gun barrels pointing at the back of my head, but there was no choice. It was more rational for me to be in front.

"I'll lead. Security team, follow right behind me. Taewang, you guys are in the rear."

Pulling Taewang to the back was to check the security team's combat strength. They were given generous equipment support. I even allowed them some privileges. If they wanted to keep those privileges, they had to earn their keep.

"Yujin-ssi, isn't there a specific operation plan?"

Park Hae-gil-ssi asked me. He was injured in his right hand and holding a gun in his left, but funnily enough, Park Hae-gil-ssi was the most reliable guy in the security team.

"What kind of operation do you need to kill bugs? Shoot when you see them. The spiders' weakness is... well, how can they live if their heads are full of holes? These guys aren't zombies."

"Understood. We'll prioritize the head. Did everyone hear that?"

The security team members nodded, tense. Guys wearing stab-proof vests and bulletproof helmets were scared. I strode forward. The first-floor lobby was a mess with strange slime and spiderwebs, but I didn't see any monster spiders.

I pressed the button in front of the elevator.

"...Are you going to take the elevator now?"

Park Hae-gil-ssi asked, slightly dumbfounded.

"Taking the stairs makes my legs hurt and it's tiring. The elevator is more comfortable. No, shit. But why is it out of order?!"

"Well, of course, it's out of order. The building is in such a mess..."

"The elevator might still work. It's not good to take anything for granted."

Deureureureuk.

Suddenly, the elevator doors opened. What appeared was a spider. From the moment the elevator started to open, my hand was drawing my sword. It wasn't conscious. It was a reflexive action ingrained in my body.

The cutlass gleamed silver. The monster spider pushed its sharp, straight legs, like spears, through the partially opened doors, aiming at me.

But the cutlass cut them off. Instead of red blood, a grayish-white slime gushed from those legs.

The spider, with its legs cut off, staggered and counterattacked with its other legs. I leisurely cut those legs off too. It's not like I'm fighting monsters for the first or second time, could I not even do this much?

"What are you doing, you morons? Pull the trigger."

Tudatadatadatada!

The assault rifles of the security team members, including Park Hae-gil-ssi, spat fire. The monster, met with dozens of bullets to the head, died instantly. Kung! The monster spider's body fell down the elevator shaft.

"See? It's nothing. Just aim for the head."

"...To say it's nothing, the spider is excessively large. I already hated spiders... I hate them even more now."

Park Hae-gil-ssi said with a sigh. I examined the reactions of the other security team members. Most were very tense, but none were sitting down in fear or panicking. It might be because they had become somewhat accustomed to the zombie situation.

"Yujin-ssi, we need to form teams. There were too many bullets wasted just now. There are 30 people in the security team, so 3 people each... no, for safety, it's better to form 6 teams of 5 people each."

"Do that."

Park Hae-gil-ssi quickly grouped them into teams on the spot and divided the directions they should mainly guard.

"Shoot as soon as you see a spider. As you saw earlier, unlike zombies, their head is their weak point. If you mess up their head, they die instantly."

"Yes, team leader."

It seemed like the basic system was in place. I headed towards the stairs, and the security team followed behind.

Stairs going down to the basement and stairs going up.

'It's certain there'll be some in the basement too... Seeing that the spider bastard was ambushing in the elevator earlier, it has the intelligence to set traps.'

There were monsters in reality that set traps. It's not that they have the intelligence to set traps. They're just doing annoying things according to their instincts. Why, there are antlions, aren't there? Guys whose habits are a kind of trap. Annoying guys to deal with even when you know about it.

'These bastards don't do it out of habit. At least the queen spider knows how to use her head. If we go down to the basement, I don't know what she'll do after blocking the entrance.'

Having made a decision, I headed upwards.

"Are you going to kill the queen spider right away?"

Gu Tae-hee asked. I turned around and looked at her. In one hand, she held a mace, and in the other, a pistol, her only right eye shining brightly.

"It's easier to kill the head first."

"If I were the queen, I would order my subordinates to ambush and wait for you to come up."

"...Do you have a better idea?"

"We can go up while clearing them out one by one. That way, we eliminate the reinforcements the queen can use in an emergency. We can also secure a retreat route."

"What about the guys in the basement? You're not saying we should go down to the basement first, are you?"

"I brought some things. Claymores. I'm going to install them on the basement stairs. The spider bastards will taste hell when they come up. It's not enough firepower to collapse the building, so we can ignore the aftermath."

"You're not saying you're going to clear out the guys in the basement with just a few landmines, are you? That would be a bit disappointing."

"I also have poison gas. It's a special gas that settles low, so it won't rise. We can roughly block the entrance with rolling furniture."

"How did you get your hands on that stuff?"

"......"

Gu Tae-hee didn't answer. Did a technologist other than Na Chae-young make it? No, I dismissed that possibility. Claymores and poison gas are things that can be easily made with modern technology.

'It must be the military. It seems like Korea in this world has more military power than Korea in other worlds. No, the fact that Korea has nuclear missiles says it all.'

"Will it take long?"

"It'll take 10 minutes."

"Do it quickly."

Gu Tae-hee and her subordinates started working. Their swift movements were not boring to watch.

"...Now that I see it, they're all from the military."

"South Korea has conscription. It's not uncommon for gangsters to be from the military."

"It's not the skill level of conscripts. At least special forces. And those who have experienced real combat. Where did you gather such people... Taewang is amazing the more I see it."

Gu Tae-hee's skills are a given, but it seemed like the high-ranking official behind Gu Tae-hee had put in a lot of effort.

'Is she like a political gangster?'

10 minutes later, the work was done.

Peong!

A poison gas grenade exploded inside the basement. We looked at the basement blocked with furniture from above the stairs.

"Poison gas. It really won't come up, right?"

"It's an item that's already been verified. They say the toxic components disappear after about 4 hours, leaving only residue."

Gigigigigigigigigigik!

A strange sound came from the basement. It wasn't just one or two. I could easily guess that it was the sound of the monster spiders convulsing.

Gu Tae-hee clicked her tongue briefly.

"There are more than I thought. Were they ambushing in the basement...?"

I gestured to the security team.

"What are you doing? There are a lot of them. They might come up, so aim your guns. If they come up, don't ask questions, just shoot."

Kwangkwangkwang!

The installed Claymores exploded in succession. And the monster spiders that the Claymores failed to kill violently pounded on the blocked basement entrance. The security team members flinched. It looked like they wanted to unload bullets at the entrance right now.

"Don't waste bullets. Shoot when you see their heads."

The entrance blocked with furniture collapsed, and the head of a scarred monster spider was revealed. The team members unloaded bullets as is. The spider died instantly. After that, a few more spiders came up, but they only died with bullets in their heads.

Soon, no more spiders came up, and it became quiet. We blocked the entrance with other furniture and decided to go up.

On the 2nd floor, the security team moved cautiously. On the other hand, I strode forward. A spider attached to the ceiling came down. Before the security team could react, I swung my sword first, cutting the spider in half and killing it. A foul smell came from the dead monster spider.

"Seeing this, these bastards are only hiding in dark places. Be careful in dark places."

I nodded towards the security teams.

"What are you doing? Go search and kill the spider bastards. You didn't think I'd just keep being in front, did you? You're the security team. You have enough equipment, so you have to earn your keep. I'll fire anyone who can't earn their keep from the security team. Move."

"J-Just us?"

"Did you not understand what I said? Or are you pretending not to understand?"

"No, we'll take care of it."

Park Hae-gil-ssi answered. He skillfully gave the security team the instructions they needed to follow. The orders were short, concise, and intuitive.

A moment later, gunshots rang out, tang tang tang.

I waited in the hallway for them to finish clearing out.

"Aaaaaaah!"

One of the security team members screamed. It seemed like he was attacked by a spider. I didn't move. Another team that was nearby went to support. The guy who screamed was bleeding from his thigh, having been attacked by a spider.

'Wearing armor and carrying guns, and 5 of them are moving together, and he gets attacked by a spider? That guy is out.'

Gu Tae-hee approached me.

"Are you just going to watch?"

"Monster spiders are nothing. They're more annoying than zombies, but they're just bugs. They need to gain real combat experience at times like this. I need to conserve my strength as much as possible. You hired me because of the queen spider, right?"

"...That's right. The queen spider is the most annoying. Keep in mind that there's almost no information about the queen spider."

The continuous gunshots soon stopped.

"Let's go up to the next floor."
