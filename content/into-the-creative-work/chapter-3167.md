The martial arts tournament finals.

Kang Myungjin and I faced each other in the arena.

Kang Myungjin's attire and weapon were different from usual. He typically wore light armor or a coat that was easy to move in, but now he was in garishly colored clothes. It looked like he'd thrown on mismatched pieces from different sets. Every single one of those equipment pieces was gear that boosted lightning resistance.

The spear in his hand was a charging spear—one with the ability to absorb electricity and convert it into energy.

'I knew from seeing the future, but seeing it in person just makes me laugh.'

His appearance was a ridiculous clown show, but Kang Myungjin's expression was dead serious.

"Kang Myungjin. You said you were preparing for the tournament—turns out you only prepared to deal with me?"

"Obviously. I determined you'd be the biggest wall in this tournament. And as predicted, here we are."

"You're going this far just to beat me?"

"I determined that this is how far I need to go to win."

That's Kang Myungjin for you.

He's fully aware that my stats are higher. The fact that he's willing to endure this humiliation for victory was impressive.

'That's the kind of bastard he is. Someone who'll eat the loss if it serves the goal.'

Still, the advantage was mine.

Even if I couldn't use the Heavenly Demon's power, even if lightning didn't work well on him, my stats were higher, and my combat experience was incomparable to Kang Myungjin's.

'The absurd part is that even with my advantage, there's a future where I lose.'

There's a reason the original work got tagged as a "growth-type munchkin."

"There's a saying in the martial world—hide thirty percent of your skill. Are you going to go all out here?"

"I entered the martial arts tournament intending to go all out. Just being here will make A-Plan Legion the most famous in Atlantis for a while. Even if I reveal my full power here, it doesn't matter. We'll grow in the end anyway."

His words were slick, but having checked countless futures, I knew Kang Myungjin would hide his trump card until the very end. Hiding the Azure Dragon, Biya, for instance.

'A Dragon Knight who doesn't use his dragon, and he calls that going all out.'

Honestly, I couldn't say anything about that. I wasn't using the Heavenly Demon's power either.

"If I win, the Legion Master position becomes mine?"

Kang Myungjin let out a rare smirk.

"If you think you can handle it."

"I'm joking. Running A-Plan Legion on top of everything else would be way too much work."

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido in my hand.

Neither of us intended for this fight to drag on. The longer it went, the more information got exposed.

I activated the power of foresight. The power I had left wasn't much. I had no intention of saving it.

「Beginning the match start countdown.」

「3.」

「2.」

「1.」

「Begin.」

Kang Myungjin thrust his spear forward and charged.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Lightning Flash (雷閃).

A slash wreathed in lightning flew out. The lightning was absorbed into the spear, and the blade aura shattered against the spearhead. Simple enough. His spearhead was harder than my blade aura.

I dodged his spear to the side and instead of swinging my sword, I threw a punch.

“……!”

Kang Myungjin's eyebrow twitched—he'd predicted a sword swing.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Wave Suppression (霹波鎭).

Kang Myungjin raised his spear shaft to block my fist.

Kwaaaang.

Kang Myungjin's body was pushed back. Thanks to that, the turn didn't pass—I seized the initiative.

Bzzt!

Red lightning bloomed from Hwaryeon Bido's blade.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Lightning Radiance (雷光).

A red trajectory like a beam of light traced through the air, but unlike usual, it lacked power. Part of the lightning had been absorbed by Kang Myungjin's spear. The blade was blocked by his spear.

"I don't know what trick you used, but right now you're seeing the future. Am I wrong?"

"Correct. I drank a potion that temporarily lets me see the future."

"…Did such a potion even exist? No, in this world, it wouldn't be strange if it did. The easiest way to deal with an enemy who sees the future is to make it so they can't avoid it even knowing what's coming."

Mana poured from Kang Myungjin's entire body. Energy surged from the spear that had absorbed the electricity as well.

"That method only works on those who can see the near future."

"……How far into the future did you see?"

"The future where I win."

I raised my left hand. Lightning flickered between my thumb and index finger.

I flicked my hand. The small spark became a bolt and flew at Kang Myungjin.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Ultimate Technique (極技) — Sealing Thunder (封雷).

Kang Myungjin tried to absorb the lightning with his spear, but couldn't absorb all of it. If that spear could absorb all lightning at once, he wouldn't have bothered finding and wearing equipment that boosted lightning resistance.

Part of the Sealing Thunder reached Kang Myungjin's body.

If it were ordinary lightning, it would have vanished meaninglessly against his high lightning resistance.

But Sealing Thunder was no ordinary lightning. Lightning imbued with my will partially disrupted the flow of mana through Kang Myungjin's body.

'If it had been a full-powered Sealing Thunder without losing potency, it would have temporarily sealed his mana.'

For now, creating even a single moment's opening was the best I could do.

I tightened my grip on the sword hilt.

'With my intuition maximized through the power of foresight.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind Blade (心卽劍).

There is no failure.

I had already seen the future where it succeeds.

The sword swung faster than lightning.

Chwaaaaak!

It cut through Kang Myungjin's body. From shoulder to flank. Blood spurted from the diagonal wound. His mana and equipment had prevented a fatal blow.

Kang Myungjin's pupils dropped downward, then widened in shock. Only after being cut and seeing blood did he register my attack. He immediately retreated backward. Putting distance between us, he used mana to staunch the bleeding.

"That strike just now…!"

"Kang Myungjin. You're not the only one who can prepare."

This time, I charged at Kang Myungjin.

Kang Myungjin gritted his teeth and swung his spear. He was trying to block my approach by exploiting the spear's longer reach compared to a sword.

A textbook response. At the same time, it was the best move Kang Myungjin could make right now. He needed time to catch his breath.

'There's nothing special about Kang Myungjin's spearmanship. It's faithful to the fundamentals. That's what makes it strong.'

Of course, there was subtlety woven into his spearmanship as well. A spear genius had no reason to cling only to the basics.

'But compared to Sunghari's, it's clumsy. I've watched Sunghari's spearmanship from the sidelines for years and learned it directly.'

When facing a spear-wielder, the moment you get scared, it's over.

The instant the spearhead grazed past my body by a hair's breadth, I moved forward. I'd stepped inside the spear's range. When the distance gets too close, a spear-wielder has difficulty responding.

Kang Myungjin stepped back. He shortened his grip on the spear shaft to narrow the range.

The shortened spear range. He swung the spear again to keep me in check.

I didn't retreat. I gripped my sword in reverse and pushed forward.

Kaaaaang!

Spear and sword collided. The spear tried to push me back, and I poured strength into my sword to hold.

I spread my free left hand.

Bzzzzzzzzzzzt!

Lightning crackled in all directions and compressed in front of my palm.

"You know this is Chidori, right?"

"…I liked ninja manga too."

In that brief moment, Kang Myungjin tried to protect his body with protective qi. I drove my left hand into his abdomen without hesitation.

The lightning couldn't pierce through Kang Myungjin's protective qi and lightning resistance, and discharged.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Ultimate Technique (極技) — Absolute Zero (絶對零度).

Where the lightning had passed, cold air settled. Kang Myungjin's body froze in an instant.

"Hnngh…!"

Kang Myungjin swallowed his breath and snapped his eyes wide open.

His eyes transformed into bright yellow dragon eyes, and along with draconic energy, a shockwave burst from his body, shattering the ice.

Dragon Roar. A skill that instills instinctive fear in all living creatures nearby.

I, possessing Absolute Mind, felt not even a trace of fear.

"Grrrr…."

A sound resembling a howl rang from Kang Myungjin's throat. He used Dragon Soul, one of his skills. Parts of his body were transforming into a dragon's. His nails lengthened, horns sprouted from his head, and scales rose on his skin.

Kang Myungjin's physical abilities as a dragonkin were probably on par with mine.

"Kang Myungjin. Just as you were wary of me and procured lightning resistance equipment, the one I was most wary of in this tournament was you."

I unsummoned Hwaryeon Bido and gripped the sword I'd prepared.

「Nothung

A sword that cuts dragon bone and flesh.

Rank: S」

Kang Myungjin wasn't a full dragon. No need to go as far as Balmung or Gram.

I swung Nothung.

Draconic energy blocked Nothung, but Nothung scoffed at the draconic energy and cut through Kang Myungjin's body.

Flesh, bone, organs. It cut clean through. Instead of a pain-filled scream, Kang Myungjin let out a bitter smile.

"……Now I understand why Biya fears you. To think a dragon's natural enemy was right beside me."

Kang Myungjin's body disappeared.

「Congratulations on winning the martial arts tournament!」

「You acquire 1,000,000 AP as the winner's reward.」

「You acquire the title, 'Peerless Under Heaven'!」

「All stats increase by 3.」

「As a reward for winning the martial arts tournament, you acquire dominion over District 7,414, the Valkyrie's Arena.」

「As the ruler of the Valkyrie's Arena, you acquire 150,000 AP every 30 days.」

「In the Valkyrie's Arena, death is not death.」

「Only those permitted by the ruler may enter the Valkyrie's Arena.」

'The Valkyrie's Arena. Good for using as a training facility, and as the district name suggests, I can hold arena matches. Just selling admission tickets would be profitable.'

「Numerous divine thrones congratulate your victory!」

「The Master of the Firmament is satisfied with your performance.」

「Please give your victory speech.」

I looked at the empty air and felt something was off.

'These bastards aren't sponsoring me? What's going on? Can't they at least sponsor 1,000 AP?'

What was strange was that even the Master of the Firmament, my contracted divine throne, and the King of Demon Heaven were silent.

'Neither of them is a god weak enough to be a lower-tier deity. But they're completely tight-lipped?'

It was ambiguous to press the issue.

'…Still, they'll give the promised reward soon enough. I still have some foresight power left—should I use it?'

I hadn't seen the future after winning, so I didn't know it.

I looked into the future to check whether the Master of the Firmament would welch on the deal. Fortunately, there was no welching.

I still had foresight power left after that, so I decided to use all of it.

The furthest future I could see.

The end of this Atlantis continent.

I saw the most probable final future of Atlantis as of now.

"Oh, fuck."

I don't know the exact timing of the future I saw. What's certain is that it's not that far off.

Understanding how Odin must have felt in the myths, I shouted toward the world that was watching me.

"Ragnarok is coming!!"
