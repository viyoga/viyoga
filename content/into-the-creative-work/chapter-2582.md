The soldiers rushed towards the still-living monster troops, swinging their weapons. The monster troops, having lost their will to fight, offered little resistance. Few monsters fought to the end. Most tried to escape.

I focused on the fleeing remnants of the monster army, dropping lightning bolts. With the Thunder Eye Vajra, not a single one escaped.

Pretending to be dead? Even without my orders, the soldiers, as if accustomed to it, searched the corpses of the monster troops to confirm their deaths. And they loot the corpses. No, it's collecting spoils of war. The armor and weapons of the monster troops are helpful to us as well. If we're lucky, we can also obtain low-grade magic items or low-grade elixirs. To me now, they just look like junk items.

The soldiers gathered the spoils in one place. From the grunts to the officers. Everyone was watching me. They didn't dare to steal the spoils. Meanwhile, I was surveying the surroundings from atop the collapsed castle wall.

,Thunder General-nim. I'd like to introduce myself. I am On Gaejeong, the training officer. While the General-nim was away, I managed Jimang Fortress on your behalf."

A middle-aged man with a stern face and demeanor, as if saying, "Anyone can tell I'm a soldier," approached and spoke. He and the officers around him performed a fist-and-palm salute towards me.

,I am Thunder General Seong Yujin. You've worked hard during my absence."

,General-nim. How would you like us to handle the spoils? The soldiers desire the spoils."

,Heavenly Jade, excellent magic items, high-grade elixirs, unique objects, and pretty women are mine."

,We will handle the spoils according to the General-nim's command."

As long as I have the Thunder Eye Vajra, the soldiers can't deceive me with the spoils. I've already identified all the spoils here. Thirty-one Heavenly Jades. There was nothing else that caught my eye. If there are any bastards who steal my things, I will kill them without exception.

Officer On continued his report.

,Thanks to the General-nim's timely arrival, the damage to the soldiers is minimal, unlike the collapsed Jimang Fortress. This victory is all thanks to Thunder General-nim. However, we cannot be complacent. This Jimang Fortress is one of the front lines where we are always at war with monsters. We never know when the monster army will come down again. For that time, we must rebuild the walls and barriers of Jimang Fortress. General-nim. Please request support from the Imperial Palace and mobilize the soldiers and people of the Northern Army to rebuild Jimang Fortress."

,I am here. The monster army has been annihilated once, so they cannot recklessly invade. They will need time too."

I spoke with conviction. How many rebel armies have I faced? I can roughly predict how the enemies will come out.

There is only one case where the enemies will invade again in the near future. When the leader of the enemies directly leads the army. But even that is not an easy task, and I rather welcome it. Because it makes things easier.

,The reconstruction of Jimang Fortress will be postponed. All soldiers, except for some who will organize and guard the spoils, will advance to the enemies' cities and villages. Since we were attacked, we must repay them. Retaliation is natural."

,General-nim! Please reconsider the operation! If we leave, Jimang Fortress will be completely empty! If a separate monster unit attacks this place... the powerless people could be massacred!"

I reached out towards Officer On. The power of telekinesis using the magnetism and internal energy of thunder and lightning was activated, pulling Officer On's body towards me. My hand grabbed Officer On's neck. I glared at him once.

,Officer On. I have already made my decision. The people? I already know that the people here are criminals or descendants of criminals. It doesn't matter if they die. The population will be replenished anyway. Officer On. I warn you, if you object to my words one more time with such useless matters... I will immediately cut off your head. Do you understand?"

,...I understand, General-nim. I apologize for overstepping my bounds."

I released Officer On's neck. He's still got some sense. Otherwise, I would have killed him right away.

,However, General-nim. There is a practical problem. The monster cities and villages of the Mirrored Pond are protected by magic. Unless you are an excellent sorcerer, you cannot even find the location of the monster cities. It is impossible to see with the naked eye, and there is a high possibility of falling into traps or being cursed by sorcerers if you enter recklessly. This is not my speculation but a confirmed fact. There are also records of it."

,That's fine. Whether it's a trap or whatever, I can see it all. Now, gather the soldiers."

,Yes. General-nim."

The officers moved to gather the soldiers. I looked around at the soldiers with the Thunder Eye Vajra. The number of soldiers came into view at a glance. A total of 2,370 soldiers. This was the number of healthy soldiers, excluding those who were assigned to guard the spoils of Jimang Fortress and the wounded soldiers.

It could be said that it was fewer than I expected, but all of them were soldiers of at least the Initial Foundation stage, fully equipped with armor. The elite of the elite.

,Is this all there is?"

,These are all the soldiers we can mobilize right now. If the General-nim issues an order to the entire Northern Army... we can mobilize at least 300,000 soldiers."

300,000. Considering the size of the Empire, it was very small. But the soldiers of the Northern Army are at least at the Initial Foundation stage. An army composed only of the elite of the elite.

,It would take ages just to gather them. We'll advance as is. But before that...."

I pointed to one of the lined-up soldiers. The soldier tensed up and approached me.

,There's a thief in my army."

,N, no! I didn't intend to steal it!"

The surprised soldier quickly took out one Heavenly Jade from his chest. I swung the Hundred Battle Spear, beheading the soldier, and recovered the Heavenly Jade.

,Keep just one thing in mind. Obey my orders. Anyone who ignores my orders will be executed, regardless of their rank."

After warning the soldiers once, I used the Cloud Riding Command to put the soldiers on the clouds.

I didn't like putting these guys on my clouds... but I couldn't help it. If I ordered the soldiers to run using light-body techniques, it would be too late. Besides, the soldiers would be exhausted from running.

,The monster bastards don't know yet that the monster army has been annihilated. Now is the perfect time for a surprise attack. And I need to kill as many monster bastards as possible before the effects of the Violet Gold Pill wear off. It would be even better if I could kill the Hexa Demon King."

The clouds carrying the soldiers raced through the sky.

Mirrored Pond (Yaochi Mirror).

The area of the monsters that the Empire, which dominates the continent, has not yet completely conquered. Most of the monsters on the continent live in this Mirrored Pond.

,If the Emperor himself steps in, this Mirrored Pond can be conquered easily. But the Emperor didn't do that."

Therefore, the Emperor deliberately left the monsters alone. Is it because monsters also occur naturally and cannot be completely exterminated? Of course, that might be one of the reasons. But in my opinion, it seems to be to maintain and train the Empire's army.

,From the perspective of controlling the country, it's quite convenient to have an external enemy."

There are other continents in this world that are not ruled by the Jin Empire. But they are damn far away. Without the Mirrored Pond, the Empire would be isolated without enemies. It wasn't good in many ways.

,The Emperor's real enemies are the Underworld and the Immortal Realm. To that end, the cultivation of the army is essential. The monsters of the Mirrored Pond are the human enemies for him."

The Mirrored Pond is vast, and monsters have formed various factions. The Hexa Demon King I'm aiming for is just one of the monster factions. Even if I kill the Hexa Demon King, the Mirrored Pond will not be purified.

I saw a monster passing by. I threw a Vajra Divine Lightning, killing every monster I saw. It would be troublesome if they went to the cities or villages and spread the news of our advance.

,The Thunder Eye Vajra is a great help at times like this."

I arrived at a village. It was a village where 375 monsters lived. It was hidden and protected by magic, but it couldn't deceive the Thunder Eye Vajra.

,The center of the magic is the pillar erected in the center of the village."

Magical characters were engraved on the surface of the pillar, which emitted an ominous aura. The Thunder Eye Vajra instantly interpreted the unfamiliar magical characters.

&lt;In the name of Habaek, I will twist the energy of heaven and earth to protect this village.&gt;

I know Habaek. He is a divine immortal who governs the Yellow Sea in the west of the Mirrored Pond. Of course, since all divine immortals are in the Immortal Realm, Habaek is not in the Yellow Sea. That's just magic that slightly borrows the power of the Yellow Sea.

I raise my left hand. The Vajra Divine Lightning spun around in front of my left hand.

Pajik, pajijik!

Golden lightning flashed around the Vajra Divine Lightning. As I flicked my left hand, the Vajra Divine Lightning became a golden lightning bolt and fell. The magic protecting the monster village could not stop the Vajra Divine Lightning, which had anti-devil power. The Vajra Divine Lightning shattered the magic pillar in an instant.

The magic that was protecting the monster village disappears.

,I'll give you an hour. Kill them all and loot everything."

,Yes! General-nim!"

The soldiers on the clouds jumped down to the village with beaming smiles.

Monsters are basically stronger than humans. Some monsters knew how to use innate magic. But the Northern Army is the elite of the elite. Ordinary monsters are no match at all. A massacre took place in the village.

The soldiers of the Northern Army, who were always tormented by monsters, had no mercy. From young monsters to old monsters. If they were monsters, they killed them.

I squatted on the cloud, looking down at the village with popcorn, watching the massacre. The soldiers also diligently collected the byproducts of the monsters, which could be used as ingredients for magic items or elixirs.

,General-nim! There's a beauty here!"

The soldiers, who had made a fortune from looting, approached with smiles.

There were scales on her face, and her breasts were not even A-cup size. Her hips weren't great either. Her features were passable, but... she was not at all at a level to be called a beauty.

,Isn't she pretty enough for a monster at this level?"

,Beauty my ass."

,If the General-nim doesn't like her... can we take this one? We'll take care of her ourselves!"

,You're horny for something like that? I don't understand... well, it's not like you're gay, so I'll respect your tastes. Do whatever the fuck you want."
