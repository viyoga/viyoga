,Tyrant for You' was a romance fantasy novel.

The heroine of this novel is a transmigrator. She was a person from Earth, but she transmigrated into the daughter of a count in the novel. For political reasons, she became the wife of a tyrant, an empress.

As empress, she used her knowledge of the original story to take care of the tyrant. However, in the end, she was assassinated in place of the tyrant and died. Enraged at the assassin and regretting not protecting the heroine, the tyrant regressed, and the story of ,Tyrant for You' truly begins.

In short, it was a romance fantasy where a tyrant who regressed in regret clings to the heroine.

,Searching like this makes me remember. I remember seeing it as a webtoon before.'

It was originally a novel, but I didn't remember reading the novel.

,...Now that it's come to this. I should watch the webtoon again and read the novel too.'

In the case of ,Tyrant for You', the webtoon was more popular than the original novel. This is because the webtoon is more immersive.

,I need to focus more on the novel. There's more information I can get from the novel than the webtoon.'

Settings and information that are mentioned in passing in a single sentence were more important to me than anything.

After gathering enough information, I checked the quest again.

My right to the throne is 22nd. I have 11 younger siblings below me, but as an illegitimate child, my right to the throne was the lowest.

I don't feel anger.

I am Seong Yujin. I won't be consumed by the settings of the game world.

Of course, that wasn't the reason why Miranda treated me carelessly. Most of the servants in the Imperial Palace, including her, treated me carelessly. It's because I don't have any power.
