-Eugene Meyer.

A mechanical voice called my name.

There was no way I couldn't know what this mechanical voice was. Droid. The enemy of Neo London, the machines that betrayed humanity.

,"Droid. I knew you guys were behind the George Mercenary Corps. Those born as slaves of humans are now enslaving humans."

Memories of my past life faintly come to mind.

The technology of my past life hadn't advanced enough to create androids. It was just the point where artificial intelligence technology was starting to take hold.

People believed that artificial intelligence would do the dirty work, and humans would do the noble work. But the reality was the opposite.

Machines were more expensive than humans.

-Eugene Meyer. You have no reason to hate us. Don't join in Neo London's hatred.

"I've fought you guys a few times. Even if that weren't the case, I'm a citizen of Neo London and a magician. That's reason enough to hate you."

-We know you hate Alphatière.

My brow furrowed.

Unpleasant memories come to mind along with anger. I habitually circulated mana in my mana road. Feeling the mana road that hasn't cooled down yet, I cool down my anger. There was nothing to gain from giving in to anger now.

,'As my fame spreads, it's only natural that my past is dug up. Maybe people who know will know. Alphatière is probably watching me too. They don't want their dirty past to be known, and they're just keeping quiet because I'm in Neo London.'

I glared at the radio.

"So?"

-Our Droids are willing to join hands with you and attack Alphatière. We exist everywhere in this world.

"And in return, you want the superconductor recipe?"

-That's right. We can also provide you with eternal wealth and power.

"I refuse. I can seize wealth and power with my own abilities. I'll take care of anything related to Alphatière myself."

I have no intention of handing over my revenge to someone else.

-Sell us LK-99, the superconductor. We'll give you information about Alphatière. If you continue to trade and hand over more superconductors... we'll hand over some of our machine technology to you.

"......"

I quietly mulled over the offer. The surroundings were quiet. Yuria-ssi, Deneb, and Invisible Blade were all silent.

This offer is dangerous. Droids are the enemy of Neo London, so accepting this offer is like betraying Neo London. But if everyone here keeps their mouths shut, no one will know.

'I can trust Yuria. If Yuria were planning to betray me, she would have had plenty of opportunities. Deneb? She's my slave, so there's no need to worry about that. Invisible Blade is a bit uneasy, but... it's true that he's tight-lipped like a ninja.'

Psh.

I let out a laugh. I was amused by my own serious thinking. The answer was already decided anyway.

"I refuse."

The most untrustworthy entity was the Droid, the trading partner.

Androids who betrayed humanity.

These are the guys who wouldn't be strange to betray me the moment they find out the superconductor recipe or receive a certain amount of superconductors.

If this deal itself becomes known, everything I've achieved in Neo London will disappear completely. In other words, agreeing to the deal is like handing over my leash to the Droids.

"I'm not such a moron that I'm blinded by a little profit."

-You don't trust us? We're not like you humans.

"You're not human, so you don't betray? Or do you want to say that the reason you betrayed was because of humans? Whatever. I'm not changing my decision."

-You can't even take the opportunity when it's given to you. Are humans just humans?

Click.

The radio connection was cut off.

I stared at the radio for a moment. I wanted to throw it away, but it would be better to give this radio to the Council. It's up to the Council to research this radio. It's better not to get involved with the Droids as much as possible.

"Things worked out much better than expected. Now...."

Let's go back.

That was the moment I was about to speak.

Kugugugugugugugugung.

The ground rumbles.

Something is approaching at a rapid speed from below the wasteland.

We all jumped towards the sky.

[Levitation]

I cast levitation magic on myself, Yuria-ssi, and Invisible Blade, excluding Deneb, who was flying in the sky on a broom. We floated high into the sky.

[Phase Shift]

I exchange the burdened astral with a clean phase astral.

I took out a mana potion from my inventory, drank it, and looked at the ground.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwa!

A giant mechanical worm popped out of the ground. At the mouth, a giant grinder was spinning, finely crushing dirt and rocks.

"What is that? A worm-shaped grinder? It came out of the ground, so is it a excavator? Whatever it is, it'll crush a small building in an instant, huh?"

Invisible Blade swung the sword he was holding in reverse, with a strangely admiring look. A water-mixed slash flew and collided with the surface of the mechanical worm. Not even a scratch.

"...An unbelievably hard metal, and even magic resistance?"

Invisible Blade's face becomes serious.

"If water doesn't work, how about fire?"

Deneb, riding on a broom, raised her finger up. A blue fireball rotates and burns above her head. It's Hellfire, a 7th-circle magic. It seems she modified the formula to amplify the power more than a normal Hellfire.

As she lowered her finger, a Hellfire with a diameter of 3m fell on the mechanical worm.

Kwaaaaaaang!

Hellfire explodes inside the mechanical worm's mouth. The blue flame burned without going out, but it was sucked into the grinder and ground away.

"...It ground up Hellfire? What is that? Magic?"

Deneb opens her mouth wide as if she's dumbfounded.

"It's amazing to actually see the Droid's technology. I can feel a misalignment of space inside the grinder. It seems they used the concept of space isolation using gravity."

Yuria-ssi was analyzing the mechanical worm with a cool head. Just in case, she tried to move the shadows inside the grinder to break it, but instead, the shadows were torn away.

"Did they create a special metal using alchemy and give the grinder a concept by casting consciousness magic? The Droid reaching out to magic too... it's not that surprising."

The Droids in this world are just machines and androids in name only, but if you change your perspective a little, you could say they're a heterogeneous race.

Kugugugugugugugugung!

The mechanical worm jumps up. The worm didn't have wings, but it did have thrusters. We quickly dodged to the side.

,'The instantaneous speed is quite fast because of the thrusters. I'll get caught off guard if I'm not careful.'

Invisible Blade, Deneb, and Yuria-ssi's attacks were all blocked. It was an absurd defense. Invisible Blade has been swinging his sword wildly since earlier, constantly firing water-mixed slashes. The slashes that cut through rocks without any difficulty were worse than eggs in front of the mechanical worm's armor plate.

,'It's annoying and tiring to slowly find and attack the weak points. Let's end this quickly.'

The mechanical worm jumps up again.

[Violet Lightning]

I throw the violet lightning I'm holding in my hand at it. The Violet Lightning hits and flows along its armor plate. Then, the Violet Lightning spreads throughout its entire body and is slammed to the ground by magnetic force.

"It's huge, but it's still just metal and a machine."

Pajijijik.

The mechanical worm falls to the ground and wriggles like a stepped-on earthworm. The thrusters spew fire like crazy, but the metal body bound by the Violet Lightning can't escape.

I leisurely chant magic in front of the bound enemy.

The more effort you put into the formula, the more elaborate and powerful the magic becomes. I slowly increased the size of the formula as much as possible.

Pajijik, pajijik...!

Blue lightning gathers in the dark sky, brightly illuminating the ground. The lightning slowly rotates in one direction and begins to amplify. I slowly amplify its power to the limit that the formula can withstand.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwa!

The mechanical worm's resistance intensified as it sensed danger. But it still couldn't escape from the Violet Lightning. The Droid slammed its head into the ground.

Since it can't jump up and interfere with me, it seems it decided to run away downwards, crushing the ground and digging a pit. Even though it's hard to move while bound by the Violet Lightning, it could fall downwards.

I declared to it.

[Thunder Break]

A pillar of lightning falls towards the pit, scattering an overwhelming amount of light that seems to blind the eyes.

The light disappeared, and the mechanical worm that was digging the pit stopped moving. I used the Violet Lightning to pull the mechanical worm back to the ground.

The mechanical worm's metal armor plate looked fine except for some melting. At least, it looked that way on the outside.

,'I can feel it. This armor plate blocked 99% of the Thunder Break. It's not strange since it's a guy who put everything into defense.'

But the remaining 1%.

The 1% of electricity that flowed into the mechanical worm damaged the inside of the mechanical worm. Originally, the inside of a machine is the most sensitive and important part.

A magician who handles metal and lightning, I was the natural enemy of machines.

I smiled as I looked at the loot as big as a small building.

Having dealt with the Droid's mechanical worm, I will be praised by Neo London.

And with my one word, the George Mercenary Corps will become traitors to humanity who colluded with the Droids.

We landed near the fallen mechanical worm.

Invisible Blade suddenly swings his sword at the scrap metal that used to be the mechanical worm.

Kang!

"Why won't it cut?!"

A metal plate that a 6th-circle ninja's attack doesn't work on at all. You could make a lot of money just by tearing that off and selling it.

"The Droids won't approach Master-nim for the time being. They must have achieved their purpose."

"Purpose?"

"It's information gathering. The Droids must have also realized that they don't have good compatibility with Master-nim."

"...I see. These guys are basically connected as one."

They must have been collecting information in real time.

"Yes. When we return to the mansion... we should hold a banquet."

"......How far?"

"Neo London hasn't defeated a Droid of this size in recent decades. The Council and the royal family will be paying attention to Master-nim. We must send an invitation to the Queen as well."

I don't think the Queen will accept the invitation.

"It's annoying to become a big shot like this. Can I leave the banquet to you, Yuria-ssi?"

"Of course, Master-nim."
