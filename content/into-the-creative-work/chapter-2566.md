,You crazy bastard! Wearing the imperial decree on your body! Are you out of your mind! Your actions are an insult to His Majesty the Emperor!"

The Martial Alliance Lord shouted as he ran away.

,I couldn't properly hold the imperial decree during the sparring match, so I just wore it!"

,Why are you even taking out the imperial decree during a sparring match?!"

,Shut up! The benevolent Emperor will forgive me! Of course, only because I'm friends with His Majesty!"

In reality, I've never even met the Emperor, but even the Emperor wouldn't be able to ignore Wiyu.

I continued to chase after the fleeing Martial Alliance Lord. I couldn't recklessly use the Bireoshin or Cheonma Gunrimbo. The imperial decree could be damaged in the process of using my inner energy. The imperial decree was equal to all. Even if I was the one holding it, I couldn't damage it. So, I had to move carefully.

From the Martial Alliance Lord's perspective, running away was the only option. He couldn't hit me. But he couldn't just focus on blocking my attacks either. With my level, even blocking my attacks wasn't really blocking them. And he probably didn't have the confidence to block perfectly because he was worried about the imperial decree.

Kill me and fabricate the incident as if nothing happened?

Impossible. There were quite a few Martial Alliance martial artists here right now. Plus, the fact that I was here had already spread as a rumor throughout the surrounding area and the Jianghu. The situation had already blown up too big to kill me and make it seem like nothing happened. Absolutely impossible.

In the end, the Martial Alliance Lord had no choice but to run away, unable to do anything else.

This childish game of tag continued for several hours.

,To be the Martial Alliance Lord and only run away... Aren't you ashamed?"

,You're one to talk, using the imperial decree as a shield!"

,Come and fight. And then lose."

,I will never lose."

The difference in realm came into play here. The Martial Alliance Lord was older than me, but he was at a higher realm. Moreover, he was a martial artist who had trained for hundreds of years. In terms of pure physical ability alone, the Martial Alliance Lord was higher than me. That was why I couldn't catch the Martial Alliance Lord.

,...So that's how it's going to be."

I took out the Hwaryeon Bido. And I approached the Martial Alliance martial artists and swung it. The Martial Alliance martial artists were horrified and retreated, stepping back.

,You! Little Demonic One! What are you doing?!"

The surprised Martial Alliance Lord screamed.

,Since you won't fight me, I'll have to deal with these other scrubs. If even these guys run away... I'll find the city's residents and attack them next."

,...You're definitely a spawn of the devil. You're incredibly wicked...!"

The Martial Alliance Lord shut his mouth.

If he were just a normal expert, he would have ignored me. But he was the Martial Alliance Lord. In the end, he sighed and approached.

,...I've lost. The sparring match is your victory. So, let's stop now. As promised, the Martial Alliance will not harm you."

,The sparring match isn't over."

I reverse-summoned the Hwaryeon Bido and approached the Martial Alliance Lord, swinging my fist.

A fist without inner energy. The Martial Alliance Lord also blocked my fist with a palm without inner energy.

It was the same no matter how many times I swung my fist. He blocked it so easily that it felt futile.

,Your fist lacks profundity. It seems you haven't trained your fist much?"

,Saying that just pisses me off."

I used just enough inner energy so that the imperial decree wouldn't tear. Pajijik. Blue lightning, unmixed with demonic energy, flashed from my fist.

Since the Martial Alliance Lord didn't seem to have any intention of dodging anyway, I swung my fist with all the power I could muster.

The Martial Alliance Lord moved his palm exquisitely.

Hwagyeong (Transformation Strength). As a master of Taijiquan, he would naturally predict that my attack would flow away.

I tried to use that force to my advantage, but... before I knew it, my fist was caught in his palm and wouldn't move. The power and lightning in my fist passed through his body and disappeared somewhere.

I definitely hit the person, but somehow, I felt a strange sense of unease, as if I had hit empty air.

,Will you continue?"

,...That's enough. Let's stop here. I won the sparring match, so keep your promise."

I turned around. I carefully organized the imperial decree I had been wearing and put it in my inventory. Since the Hwaryong Gate had fallen, I headed to the pleasure house.
