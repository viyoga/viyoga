Kwak Soo-hyuk's family home was in a neighborhood famous as one of the wealthiest in Seoul. A place where renowned Korean businessmen gathered to live.

It was a bit old-fashioned, but it was probably the old Chairman's taste, so there was nothing strange about it.

,I should go in and grab Kwak Soo-hyuk's family and interrogate them.'

No matter how much family they are, they won't keep their mouths shut when a knife is coming at them.

,I'm going to find Kwak Soo-hyuk's current location and contact information. And I have to film a video of his family dying and send it to him. That'll piss him off.'

I hope Kwak Soo-hyuk gets pissed off.

I ignored the security guard guarding the entrance to the house and climbed over the wall. I landed neatly in the clean garden. The sound of insects tickled my ears.

,It's a little too quiet. Did they all run away as a group?'

I know very well that that's not possible. The opponent is the owner family of the Kangryong Group. The richest people in Korea. There's no way they'd run away in the middle of the night because they're afraid of me.

,The lights are on, so there must be someone there.'

I look up at the sky for a moment.

The sky, which had been dark when I left the motel, was quickly brightening.

,As expected of a chaebol family. They seem to be moving diligently from early in the morning.'

To me, who goes to bed late and wakes up late, those who go to bed early and wake up early seemed extraordinary. And those who go to bed late and wake up early are crazy.

I head towards the building.

Ba-srak.

I step on the grass and approach carefully.

There will almost certainly be a housekeeper in a house like that.

,If there are too many people, it'll be noisy and chaotic, so let's kill the housekeeper first. After that, I'll kill everyone else in the order I don't like them. I'll kill the Chairman, who has the most information, last.'

The plan was perfect. It would be annoying if the guards rushed over, so I moved to what I assumed was the back door of the kitchen.

I carefully pulled the doorknob. As expected, the door was not locked. There was no sign of anyone on the other side of the door, so I opened it right away. It was a kitchen. A kitchen with three refrigerators.

,The size and design are like a hotel kitchen.'

I pulled out Moonlight Sonata 7 and tiptoed out of the kitchen into the living room. If someone jumps out, I'll immediately swing my sword and slice them up.

,You, you bastard!!'

An angry old man's voice echoed loudly.

I stopped walking. For a moment, I thought I had been discovered. But it wasn't. The old voice was venting his anger at someone other than me.

,Are you really crazy?! There's a limit to how much you can tarnish the family name! It's not too late! Come here! I'll smooth things over if it's about company matters!'

,Grandfather... We have to kill that bastard.'

It was Kwak Soo-hyuk's voice.

My quick mind roughly guessed how the situation was unfolding. The Chairman and Kwak Soo-hyuk were fighting.

,Kwak Soo-hyuk. This bastard was here? Perfect.'

I'll annihilate the Kangryong Group's owner family. Then I won't have to worry too much about the aftermath. The Kangryong Group, without its owner, will have to face tremendous chaos.

,But something's strange. Kwak Soo-hyuk. Why is this bastard so calm?'

On the other hand, the Chairman's voice is urgent. Kwak Soo-hyuk caused the accident. But Kwak Soo-hyuk is calmer? Something's wrong. It seems like the Chairman and Kwak Soo-hyuk's positions are reversed.

,A chance will come someday! Until then, stay abroad! It's also a skill to plan for the future overseas!'

,Grandfather. I can't wait any longer! I have to kill that bastard right now! If I spend time meaninglessly overseas... During that time, Joo Seo-hyun will be with that bastard...!'

,Are you really doing this for just one woman?!'

,Just a woman? There's no woman like Joo Seo-hyun in this world! Only Joo Seo-hyun deserves to stand by my side!'

,You're crazy! I spoiled you too much!'

,Grandfather. Give me the Kangryong Group. Then I won't touch your family.'

,Get a grip! You're being used by that bastard! If that bastard gets what he wants, you'll be abandoned! Please, don't abandon your humanity!'

,Don't abandon your humanity... I always grew up hearing those words. But I've never been impressed by those words even once.'

,What... No way....'

,How long do I have to watch this farce?'

A man's voice, not the Chairman's or Kwak Soo-hyuk's, echoed. The languid voice instantly dominates the space.

,...I was going to end it now. Grandfather. If you don't give it to me, I'll take it by force. I can't understand you, Grandfather. You can get so much more if you abandon your humanity, so why are you clinging to something useless...'

,That's the stupidity of humans.'

,I will never...!'

Kwang!

I kicked the door open. The eyes of the people in the living room turned to me.

There were surprisingly many people. The family members were gathered in the corner, sitting and trembling, and the Chairman and Kwak Soo-hyuk were confronting each other in the center of the living room. The sight of the Chairman sitting on the floor and Kwak Soo-hyuk standing upright was somehow strange.

A white-haired young man was standing next to Kwak Soo-hyuk. The suit that fit his body perfectly was neat, and his skin was pale to the point of being white. The atmosphere was cold, and his eyes were as red as blood.

And I knew him.

,Yoon Seo-jin.'

The enemy of Choi Sang-woo, the protagonist of the original work, and the final boss of the original work.

,.......'

Yoon Seo-jin looked at me and was silent. Judging by his expression, it seemed like he already knew me.

,You are....'

The Chairman also knew me. His eyes sparkled with hope.

,This bastard! How did you know and follow me here?!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk immediately burst into anger. It seems like he has anger management issues.

,I can kill the mosquito boss and a dog who doesn't know his place in one place.'

I tense my body. It feels like every single cell in my body is awakening in anticipation of the battle.

,I'm lucky.'

The plan has changed. I won't kill the Chairman. That bastard tried to commit patricide. Killing the Chairman and his family would be helping Kwak Soo-hyuk.

,Instead, I'll kill Kwak Soo-hyuk and Yoon Seo-jin.'

The priority is Kwak Soo-hyuk.

It was the moment I put strength in my legs and bent my knees to close the distance with him. Kwak Soo-hyuk flicked his finger. A sword appeared in the air. The sword aimed for my head and flew accurately.

I had already put strength in my lower body, so it was too late to dodge. There was only one option.

,Cut it down.'

Kkaang!

The sword I cut down flew to the side and hit the wall. The sword broke and turned into a drop of blood before falling.

,You're not the lucky one, I am! Seong Yujin! You're going to die by my hand!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk drew his hand across. Five swords appeared in front of him. The swords rushed at me in different directions and at different speeds.

,This bastard became a Noble-class Vampire. Did he make a deal with the Vampire Lord?'

Since the Vampire Lord is next to him, that's for sure.

,What's surprising is Kwak Soo-hyuk's power.'

It's only been a few hours since Kwak Soo-hyuk became a Noble-class Vampire. But he's using his abilities at such a threatening level?

This could only be seen as talent. I understand why Yoon Seo-jin, who is famous for moving secretly, is sticking by Kwak Soo-hyuk's side. He probably recognized Kwak Soo-hyuk's talent.

Kkaang! Kkang!

I dodge the swords, and cut down the swords I can't dodge.

It might have been a little difficult if Kwak Soo-hyuk had fully bloomed that talent. But right now, Kwak Soo-hyuk was a rookie.

,Aaaaaaack!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk screams.

The number of swords flying at me increased to 10. The swords rushing in different directions are even flashy. If I let my guard down even a little, I can be deceived by the swords. The moment I'm deceived, those swords will tear my body to shreds.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 6]

I use Ephemeral Moment to break through in a straight line. I passed through five swords in an instant.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 5]

The remaining five swords didn't fly at the same time. The intention is to block the variables by giving different speeds separately. But my experience and skill are not shallow enough to eliminate variables with just this much.

I step on the Yeongcheonryu footwork.

I float my body and move forward, using the flying swords as stepping stones.

What I need to do well here is to match the timing.

,It would be strange if I couldn't do it when I have Ephemeral Moment.'

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 4]

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 3]

Using Ephemeral Moment, I stepped on the flying swords with perfect timing and approached Kwak Soo-hyuk right in front of his nose.

,What the hell is this...!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk showed his teeth. The fangs, which are the symbol of a vampire, sparkled as they caught the light. He raises his hand. In that hand, he was already holding an antique revolver. The muzzle of the revolver was pointed directly at my head.

Kwak Soo-hyuk smiles. He must be thinking that I can't dodge it at this distance.

I used Ephemeral Moment as he pulled the trigger.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 2]

I can see the bullet spewing out of the revolver. Ironically, it was a silver bullet that was especially effective against vampires.

I quickly turned my head and dodged the bullet that was aiming for my forehead. I swung my sword, enjoying Kwak Soo-hyuk's expression contorted with shock and fear.

,That's enough.'

Kwang!

Yoon Seo-jin, who had been watching quietly from the side, held out his palm, and at the same time, a shockwave occurred, pushing my body back. It feels like being hit with a hammer, but it's not enough to lose consciousness or have a body part explode.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 1]

I put strength in my waist and tumbled in the air. Both feet touch the ceiling. I fell like lightning on Yoon Seo-jin. Yoon Seo-jin moved calmly. My sword barely grazed his shoulder. Yoon Seo-jin, who had been expressionless, frowned.

,As expected of Joo Seo-hyun's partner... His combat power is amazing. I wonder if he's even human.'

,Die, you bastard!'

Kwak Soo-hyuk, who was next to Yoon Seo-jin admiring him, rushed in angrily.

,He's rushing in here? That's a complete thank you.'

I twist my arm and swing my sword. I'm aiming for Kwak Soo-hyuk's neck. Kwak Soo-hyuk smirks. He took out a blade hidden in his sleeve and knocked down my sword.

,As expected, that bastard is stronger.'

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 0]

I summon a new sword from the inventory. All I have to do is stab his heart before he notices.

,Stop!'

Yoon Seo-jin grabbed Kwak Soo-hyuk's shoulder and pulled him back. Jjaeng-geu-rang! He smashed the living room window and went out into the yard.
