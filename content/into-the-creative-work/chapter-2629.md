I arrived at the battlefield, in the Raven Knights' zone.

It was a fortress. A fortress with a large, sturdy barrier. Weapons, including machine guns and missile launchers, were prepared on the barrier. It seemed the only way to enter the fortress was through the gate in the center of the barrier.

Fly in? I don't know how many planes it would take to get into that fortress full of anti-air weapons.

The bus stopped after entering the fortress. Knights and soldiers popped out from somewhere and surrounded the bus.

,"We've arrived. Get off. If you do anything even slightly suspicious, you're dead."

The bus driver's face was serious and sharp. The mercenaries swallowed hard and got off the bus. The interrogation began. Mercenaries with problematic IDs were subdued and dragged away somewhere.

,'They do the inspection after arriving that they could have done before getting on the bus? Malicious.'

Even if they managed to escape after being dragged away, it would be hard to return, even if they were lucky enough to escape in the battlefield zone.

I leisurely stayed on the bus until the end, then slowly got off. Two knights approached me. One was the bus driver.

,"I saw your skills earlier on the bus. At least a Rank 5 or higher Battle Mage. This screening might not be to your liking. But it's just procedure, so please understand. Anyone entering the Raven Knights' fortress must undergo screening."

The bus driver, who had been like a thug, spoke politely. I almost doubted if he was the same guy who had disregarded the mercenaries.

,"Like immigration. I understand."

I handed the knights the ID I had prepared in advance. Did they think I wouldn't prepare something like this?

,"An ID issued by the Neo London Magic Association. The name is Cha Eun-yu. Rank 6 magician. A baronet title... You have a title? My, you're an aristocrat. Excuse me."

,"Aren't you a knight too? If you're a knight, you're a quasi-aristocrat, so there's no need to be so humble."

,"Haha. Less than 10% of the Raven Knights are formally appointed knights. In fact, unlike the name, we're closer to a private military company."

,"Neo London hired you?"

,"It's a well-known fact, but it's kept quiet. In the battlefield, it doesn't matter which country or company is behind you. What matters is surviving without defeat and occupying the area."

The knight confirmed that the ID wasn't forged and returned it to me. Even if the identity was fake, the ID was real, issued by the Magic Association.

,"May I ask what your purpose is for coming to the battlefield?"

,"I heard there's a dungeon nearby. I came because I was interested."

,"A dungeon nearby... There are so many that it's hard to get a sense of it. Be careful with dungeons. Even veterans die if they're careless."

,"I'll take care of it myself."

,"Would you consider staying with the Raven Knights after you finish your work? Magician power is important. I guarantee a solid reward. You'll make a decent amount even if you only work for a few months."

Battlefield. A place where war happens as often as eating. It's an extremely dangerous place, but people are gathering. There's only one reason. Because it pays. However, mercenaries don't head to the battlefield solely for the money.

,"Moreover, in the battlefield, you can easily obtain elixirs and such due to the influence of the Dark Moon. If you find them yourself, they're yours, and if you achieve merits, the Raven Knights will also bestow elixirs."

Working here can turn you into a veteran in just a few months. Because the Raven Knights provide combat know-how and elixirs. Mercenaries with no money or skills gain the opportunity to grow in exchange for risking their lives.

,"Sorry, but I'll decline the offer. I have enough money, and I came to the battlefield for my own purposes."

,"Is that so? That's a shame."

The screening was over. I looked around the fortress in a suitable place. It was like a small city.

As the sun set, the sky gradually darkened, but the buildings within the fortress emitted light instead. It was the entertainment district. Scantily clad whores promoted their shops, and men who had spent a hard day were drawn in as if possessed.

I headed to the most luxurious-looking building.

,"Oh my. A rare handsome man here. How about a round? I'll do it for free, especially for you."

,"What are you worth? Oppa. I'll do it for you. I'm 20 years old."

Even if I wore a hood, my face would be visible if you looked closely. The whores who saw my face gathered around me in an instant. I looked around at the other whores and shook my head. Cheap whores promoting on the street couldn't satisfy my eyes.

,"Not interested."

[Telekinesis]

I pushed away the scantily clad whores and went on my way.

The hotel I arrived at was a building with a luxury that wouldn't be out of place even in Neo London's C District. Of course, the building size was incomparably smaller, though.

I opened the door and entered, and a neat-looking old butler greeted me. He was wearing a butler's uniform and a badge with three rhombuses forming a triangle, signifying Triple rank.

,"Welcome. It seems you're a first-time visitor."

,"...Butler? Does a butler run a hotel? And a Triple at that?"

,"Haha. There's no rule that a butler can only be a butler. And broadly speaking, managing a hotel is also managing a house, so isn't it a butler's job?"

,"...Are you the manager of this hotel?"

,"No. My master is the commander of the Raven Knights. This hotel is my master's business. Managing my master's business is also a butler's job."

,"I want to rent a room. How much is it per night?"

,"900,000 credits."

,"...900,000? Per night?"

,"Yes. The price includes dinner and breakfast. There are no discounts."

,"Are there even any guests in this fortress who use a hotel at that price?"

,"It's a place to treat special guests who come to the fortress. Mercenaries also come on very special days."

,"Birthday?"

,"Birthdays, weddings, successful dungeon explorations, successful survival... Any special day can be named, can't it?"

Mercenaries working in the battlefield would receive a lot of allowance, so they could afford to use a hotel like this occasionally. Convinced, I paid the price and checked in.

,"I will prepare dinner. It will take about 30 minutes."

,"Okay. Ah, can I also get a whore? Not the cheap ones on the street."

,"Of course, we have them. Here's the list. I don't think you necessarily need a whore, sir...."

,"It's hard to find pretty women among the female mercenaries."

I pointed out the woman on the list who was somewhat to my liking and headed to the hotel room. It was quite luxurious. It was worth the expensive price.

The prepared dinner was satisfactory.

,"It's better than most restaurants in Neo London."

,"I'm glad you're satisfied."

I spent the night holding the whore who came into the room.

The next day. After finishing a satisfactory breakfast, I went outside the hotel. Unlike last night, I walked along the deserted street towards the fortress entrance.

At the entrance, knights and soldiers gathered to check personnel, receive supplies, and go outside.

The Raven Knights' zone isn't just this fortress. The surrounding area centered on the fortress is the Raven Knights' zone. Patrols to protect the zone and explorations to gain profits are carried out every day.

I approached the knight who was guarding the fortress entrance and writing in the entry-exit log.

,"I want to leave the fortress."

,"Ah. You're the magician? The entertainment district was buzzing about a handsome magician coming. What is your purpose for leaving?"

,"Dungeon exploration."

,"It will take at least a few days. Take this pass."

The pass was a card the size of a business card. A black raven was drawn on a white background. I felt the magic contained in the card.

,"Recognition magic that lasts for about a week. Did they scatter the spell arrangement haphazardly? It won't be easy to copy."

It won't be easy, but it's not impossible. Even though a fairly delicate lock is in place, I could decipher it in 30 minutes.

,"You recognized it right away. Yes. The magic on that card lasts for a week. So you must return within a week."

,"What if I can't return?"

,"You will be processed as deceased. Even if you return after a week, you will not be able to enter the fortress. We will think of you as something that has taken over your body. Please leave the battlefield on your own. Otherwise, a rather troublesome screening will proceed."

,"I understand."

I went outside the fortress.

I took out a compass from my pocket.

,'If it's the same as the original game, the dungeon is hidden. Only magicians of Rank 6 or higher can detect it.'

I walked northwest for now. When I opened the Astral, I became sensitive to mana. However, the mana detection range was lower than I thought. Perhaps due to the aftermath of the war, the mana concentration in the battlefield fluctuated.

,'It will be difficult to use magic sensitive to mana concentration.'

[Electric Detection]

I moved forward, using search magic at regular intervals. Had an hour passed? An anomaly occurred.

,'The search magic isn't detecting the 300m point behind me. The magic is blocked by something.'

It detects other places, but not the area behind me? Of course, that means there's something behind me.

,'Am I being followed? They're experts. It's hard to grasp with just pure presence. Since they said my story spread within the fortress... They must know my purpose is the dungeon and are chasing me.'

They probably didn't expect that I would move while detecting all directions with search magic.

,'Should I fight here? Or should I ignore them and enter the dungeon?'

In the original game, only players who met certain conditions could enter dungeons like this. It was originally a single open-world game.

,'Even if they're chasing me, if only I can enter like in the original game... The followers will be left chasing chickens.'

I think it's worth testing. Whether the characteristics of the dungeon in the game are maintained in reality.

Even if they follow me into the dungeon... I didn't think I would necessarily lose. Considering the characteristics within the dungeon, I would be more advantageous.

I walked forward.

And I arrived at my destination.

A meadow full of grass. There was nothing unusual on the surface.

,'I can feel it through the Astral. An enormous presence.'

The distortion of space that can be felt even though it's not visible. The fragmented magic circles scattered there.

I looked at that place, gathered the scattered fragments of the magic circles, and combined them. It didn't take long. Even though it was a magic puzzle, it was an easy answer that even a Rank 3 magician could understand.

,'The important thing isn't the answer to the puzzle. It's the sense of feeling the puzzle itself.'

A completed purple magic circle appears in the air.

The magic circle shone intensely and then exploded. But no physical impact occurred. Instead, a somewhat heterogeneous mana wave swept around. The grass fluttered.

A purple portal appeared where the magic circle had been. It's a dungeon.

Ssuuk.

Dozens of arms made of magic appeared from within the portal, trying to grab my body.

,"Stop."

[Telekinesis]

The countless magic hands stopped just before touching my body. I twisted the telekinesis. The purple arms caught in the telekinesis were twisted and torn off. The arms were converted into mana and disappeared.

,"I'll walk in on my own without being dragged."

I walked step by step and crossed the portal.
