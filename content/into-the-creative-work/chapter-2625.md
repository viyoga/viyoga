,Whose horoscope is it?"

,We're curious too. The client thoroughly hid their identity and commissioned us. I guess they didn't trust us much."

,You took a job without even knowing who the client is? You, a Triple-rank?"

Even a Triple-rank can be hired by noble families or large corporations. The average annual salary is 100 million credits, which, converted to Korean money from my past life, is about 3 billion won.

Executives at large corporations in this world earn much more than that, but even considering that, it's a considerable high salary.

And there's always a reason for receiving a high salary. Shea before me was once a famous mercenary.

,Well, I'm almost 40, you know? But unlike other maids, all I know how to do is shoot. My skills decline as I get older, and it's hard to save money because of the messes I made when I was younger... It's all quite depressing. That's when the client appeared like a savior. Or rather, the client's agent did."

,A maid or butler who betrays once has their license revoked. Was it that much money to gamble your future on?"

,Yes. The down payment alone was enough to pay off my debts. And the remaining success fee was enough for a comfortable retirement fund."

,The employment contract I drew up with you was top-tier in the industry, and the benefits weren't bad, were they?"

,Ah, I was satisfied with that. But the client's compensation was more appealing."

Something invisible flew towards me.

A type of power that interferes with the mind. An attack carried out silently from the outside, taking advantage of the conversation.

,This is an attack aimed at disturbing the mind and reducing concentration."

If concentration drops, the power of magic would drastically decrease, or the casting speed would inevitably slow down.

,How long have you been preparing?"

,Two months. We wouldn't have executed it this hastily under normal circumstances."

,...Because of the cherry blossom tree?"

,Yes. It exerts influence within the mansion and uses electricity. Things wouldn't go well with an electric-type magician around. Originally, we were planning to figure out when you would leave the mansion and then make our move."

,So you took advantage of the fact that the cherry blossom tree isn't doing its job and moved quickly."

,That's right. That's why I'm stalling for time by answering you like this."

Mercenaries from outside the mansion are installing machines and scattering talismans and cursed objects everywhere.

The mana in the atmosphere fluctuates and becomes contaminated.

,Are they deliberately spreading curses to prevent the use of mana?"

All of those things wouldn't be cheap.

,You guys are so bloodthirsty. Did you come to kill me instead of capturing me? If you kill me, you won't get the LK-99 recipe, will you?"

Most of the attacks I've been suffering lately aren't aimed at killing me directly, but at kidnapping me. The kidnappers' goal is LK-99, the recipe for the superconductor, so it was obvious.

,The client wanted your corpse. Well, I don't know exactly, but maybe they're planning to use black magic? Or use a psychometry-type ability user."

,Ha. You think I wouldn't have prepared for that much?"

,Who knows? We're just doing what the client wants."

Perhaps the premise itself was wrong. The goal isn't LK-99, but my death itself.

Ttuk-ttuk.

A huge man walks in, exuding his presence.

,Why are you leisurely chatting? Let's finish this before the cops show up. Well, I've bribed the cop bastards, so it'll be quiet for a while."

The bald man had red tattoos all over his body. Magic enhancement surgery. He had permanent magic circles engraved on his body to strengthen his physique. Apart from that, the aura emanating from him was that of a 6th rank, like me.

,One 6th rank. Four 5th ranks, seven 4th ranks. Twelve 3rd ranks."

They overinvested to kill one 6th rank magician. They must have wanted to kill me that badly.

,...Yujin-ssi. I'll stall for time. It seems better to cause as much commotion as possible here than to fight. Wouldn't that bring the police or something?"

,Don't step forward."

,Huh?"

,You're a guest. I can't ask a guest to do something dangerous."

I raised my right hand. Pajijik. A current that seemed to wrap around my palm and the back of my hand flickered once.

,It's not like I was just talking. The formula is already complete."

Kkattak.

I lowered my palm.

[Thunder Break]

A pillar-like bolt of lightning, as thick as a pillar, brightens the sky and falls from the gloomy sky of Neo London. The target is the 6th rank warrior with tattoos all over his body.

However, Thunder Break flew and struck a nearby 5-story building instead of the target.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwang!!

With a roar, the building is half-destroyed and collapses.

At first, I was flustered. There were hardly any cases where I couldn't hit something with magic or a gun. Especially Thunder Break, which takes time to prepare, is a great magic. It's a magic that I cast with special care. It's not a magic to be wasted so absurdly.

,It's not my mistake. ...Is it an external factor?"

The 6th rank warrior in front of me bursts into laughter.

,Hahaha! Did you think I'd come to face an electric-type magician without any preparation? I knew this would happen, so I prepared a special lightning rod!"

,...You used an entire building as a lightning rod. That's a luxurious lightning rod that can't be used twice."

,Once is enough. You can't continuously fire 7th rank great magic either!"

The guy extends his fist towards the barrier. In an instant, the size of his fist grows to nearly 1m. It grew without any warning. It was an ability, not magic or sorcery.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 10]

,Barrier, 5 layers."

As soon as it touches the fist, the barrier shatters, and the remaining barriers are destroyed by the shockwave. It's not just an attack from physical impact. The wave generated from the fist shakes and destroys the magic structure.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 9]

I use Ephemeral Moment to squeeze out and use a formula.

[Reverse Gravity]

[Air Strike]

I nullify the gravity on me and Aisa and use impact magic. Aisa and I flew in different directions. I used telekinesis to land on the garden floor a little further away.

Some of the maids, butlers, and mercenaries who were surrounding us aimed their guns at Aisa, but immediately turned them towards me.

,Leave the woman alone! What's important now is killing Count Yujin Meyer!"

,Time is not on our side!!"

,If it's a big shot like the Count, there's a high possibility that the Magic Association or the London Knights will move! Concentrate your firepower and kill the Count before those guys move!!"

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 8]

,Each one is a magic bullet specialized for great magic. A barrier is a bad move in this situation."

After quickly assessing the situation, I use a different magic instead of a barrier.

[Ice Wall]

I raise six thick ice pillars to protect my body. They are ice pillars that are more than 50cm thick. Because they are pillars without impurities, they are much harder than ordinary ice. Moreover, they are strengthened by mana. They cannot be broken by ordinary physical attacks.

The bullets didn't even get embedded in the ice pillars; they bounced off. But the damage is gradually accumulating.

,Get out of the way!"

The 6th rank warrior. He runs towards me. He closes the distance in an instant by stepping on some kind of footwork and throws a fist at the pillar.

The ice pillar, which even withstood missiles, shattered like broken glass. It collapses absurdly easily.

[Hallucination]

I didn't hide. If I hide, Aisa will be in danger. Aisa is also a 5th rank druid, but her combat abilities are poor.

,I'll praise you for not running away far. But what are you going to do after this? You've been putting a strain on your mana road by using magic in quick succession, haven't you? How many magicians like you have I killed? Do you think I wouldn't know?"

Unfortunately, that was right. Starting with the 7th rank magic Thunder Break, the mana road is hot from the aftermath of the magic used in quick succession in a short time. If I use magic like this, the mana road will most likely be damaged.

,7th rank. It sounds good. Because if you reach that level, your life itself changes. But if anyone could reach it, would they bother calling it superhuman? Rather than being obsessed with 7th rank like you and dying, it's a hundred times better to give up early and enjoy the present!"

[Frost Horn]
