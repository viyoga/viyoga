I returned to Neo London without using the World Door.

The reason I deliberately avoided the World Door was to conceal the fact that I possess a means of long-distance spatial travel.

'If I move as the chairman of LK Group, like it or not, eyes will follow. There will certainly be those who investigate my movements.'

I didn't want to give them even the slightest hint that could be linked to the World Door.

'The World Door, which has no penalty other than needing a door and designated coordinates, is itself a trump card. A trump card is only effective when others don't know about it.'

Even if it was somewhat troublesome, it was worth enduring.

By the time I arrived in Neo London that way, the Dark Moon had set and a new sun had risen.

The city was fine. Blood was splattered on the roads, and some streetlamps were dented, but it was fine. For a night when the Dark Moon had risen, it was rather tame.

I rode a carriage through the streets of Neo London and arrived at the estate.

My eyes nearly split open when I saw the estate.

The estate's walls and part of the annex had been half-destroyed.

I nearly burst into tears at the sight.

"You've arrived, Master."

A maid who had been cleaning up near the collapsed wall greeted me. Beside her, large black bags stood in a row. Through the gaps of the open bags, building debris and pieces of human corpses were visible.

"Yes. You've worked hard again today. But what happened here?"

"There was a terrorist attack at dawn."

Terrorist attacks were a relatively common occurrence. It wasn't because they hated me, of course—their goal was to capture me alive, find my weaknesses, or uncover the secrets of LK-99. In short, they were after the superconductor.

"None of the previous attacks managed to bring down the wall. Was there a Grade 7 among the terrorists? Kirsi should have been here."

A Grade 7 wouldn't take on such dangerous work even for a lot of money. Their earnings were already at the level of the world's top 0.1%. There was no reason to risk antagonizing LK Group just to earn a quick lump sum.

And most terror attacks were handled at the line of Kirsi, the spirit beast cherry tree planted in the estate's garden.

"The enemies used special equipment to neutralize Kirsi's barrier and sprayed herbicide on Kirsi. According to Deneb, the herbicide contained ingredients that are a natural counter to spirit beasts. Normally it wouldn't have been enough to affect Kirsi, but Kirsi had been weakened, and the Dark Moon caused a slight mutation in the herbicide."

Kirsi hadn't fully recovered from the damage sustained in the last battle. And then the variable of the Dark Moon was added on top of that.

"Any casualties?"

"Four with minor injuries, that's all. Just bruises—no hindrance to work. Thanks to Invisible Blade and Deneb stepping in, we were able to subdue the terrorists quickly."

"Did you capture any of the terrorists alive?"

"Yes. They're locked in the underground prison. However, it's unlikely they know who's behind this."

"Figured. They must be people with nothing left to lose. Anyone in their right mind wouldn't think of committing terrorism in the heart of Neo London."

Most terrorists are hopeless trash whose lives have gone to complete shit. The enormous sums they received would have gone into their family's or friends' accounts. Or they were forced into terrorism by some organization holding them captive.

"Find out the identities of the terrorists by any means necessary."

I will definitely retaliate. If I stay still, others will think I'm a pushover and come for me too.

I'll find the families and friends of the dead and kill them. Same for whatever organization they belonged to.

"Yes, Master."

"...You've been through a lot. I'll add a bonus this month."

"Thank you, Master!"

The maid beamed. Bonuses make even elite maids smile.

I briefly looked around the estate's interior, then entered the workshop. There were no signs of intrusion in the workshop.

The LK-Dispenser was in one corner of the workshop.

A device that looked like a water purifier that output LK special material. It was a legacy of Great Korea that wouldn't operate for anyone but me.

The object that could be called the core of LK Group.

It was a bottomless money pit that endlessly poured out cash.

'At any rate, the workshop is safe. As long as LK Group monopolizes the superconductor, attacks will continue. Kirsi needs to prepare for evolution too. I can't keep leaving the estate's barrier solely to Kirsi forever.'

Fortunately, I had obtained barrier magic this time.

Eight Gates Boundary Formation.

It was certain to be more effective than the barrier Kirsi used instinctively.

• • •

In a spacious area set aside for training in one corner of the workshop, I pulled out the grimoire Necromantic Command Heart that hung at my waist.

It was to check Chwe Sik-yi's combat power. If I was going to use him as a familiar, I needed to know the exact capabilities of my familiar.

Didn't I already know from fighting him?

'There's a saying. An enemy that becomes an ally grows weaker. In the original game, the dungeon boss Mister Chwe couldn't be made into a familiar due to the game system. I need to confirm what his current specs are.'

I converted mana into dark mana.

As the black mana seeped into the grimoire, the book floated up into the air on its own.

Originally, using dark mana required a special organ called a Dark Hole, but after becoming a demon last time and grasping the essence of dark mana, I could freely convert mana into dark mana. Same with divine power.

Necromantic Command Heart unfolds.

"Come forth, Chwe Sik-yi."

I left the Life Vessel in the grimoire and summoned only Chwe Sik-yi's body.

Dark mana gathered before the grimoire and became a black coffin. The black coffin didn't fall to the floor but slowly opened its lid.

Inside the black coffin, Chwe Sik-yi—a Chinese man with a stern face and a braided queue—had his arms crossed in an X over his chest. He was glaring with both eyes wide open. His beard twitched, and then his mouth opened.

"Ppang-jeu ppang-jeu. Just how much humiliation do you intend to inflict upon this body..."

"Shut up and come out. Or should I destroy you as you wish? To be honest, this isn't entirely a bad opportunity for you. You were originally already dead. I'm not talking about becoming a lich."

Just because Chwe Sik-yi was bound to the grimoire didn't mean he would unconditionally obey my words like he was hypnotized. To use Chwe Sik-yi as a familiar, I needed to persuade him.

"...Words I cannot deny. This one died. But the world did not let this one go. I was revived as a sentinel in the cracks of the world. I was like a mirage that would vanish along with the crack when it closed."

"This is outside the dungeon. To be precise, inside my workshop. You should know that normally, it's impossible for a dungeon's existence to come outside."

It was possible because Chwe Sik-yi was a lich, not an ordinary creature, combined with the fact that the Necromantic Command Heart grimoire specialized in handling souls. Throughout history, it was the first time a being from inside a dungeon had come outside.

In other words, it was no different from Chwe Sik-yi being given another chance. There was no way Chwe Sik-yi himself, the party in question, didn't know that.

He soon cupped his fist toward me in salute.

"...Hao hao. Dage! This one shall follow Dage until I achieve true immortality!"

"Good. Let's work well together from now on."

I then decided to test Chwe Sik-yi's abilities through sparring.

The result: he had gotten weaker. Less than half of what he was when we fought inside the dungeon.

"You're weak. Where did the pride of True China go?"

"Gal! Necromancy is a school that requires more preparation than other types of magic! Without a single corpse, of course I'd be weaker. Have you forgotten what my innate ability is?"

"Your curses were pathetic too."

"Is this not your workshop?!"

He had a point.

Using curses on me inside my own workshop meant the power of the curses was inevitably weakened. Conversely, my magic's power was further amplified.

On top of that, I was an elemental mage who could instantly cast magic without any preparation and had strong burst firepower.

In every way, the advantage was mine.

"Next is close combat."

Fighting Chwe Sik-yi had sparked my interest in close combat. If I used magic well, couldn't I become a close-combat specialist?

I picked up a sword, and Chwe Sik-yi pulled a magic nunchaku from his sleeve.

The result: I was almost toyed with.

"Hao hao. Your talent for martial arts is terrifyingly nonexistent."

"...I did use martial arts."

"You called it Thunder Heaven Style? The greatness of that martial art can be seen even from a fragment. But what can a pig do with a dragon pearl in its mouth? All you do is swing! You haven't grasped even a hint of its profound principles! You're just flailing around relying on your body enhanced by magic! Even a Grade 3 warrior wouldn't lose to you!"

“…….”

I felt it too. I couldn't properly execute even one basic form of Thunder Heaven Style. Unless I used the Discordant Needle, the martial arts of Thunder Heaven Style were nothing but a pie in the sky.

'I have magic anyway.'

I cleanly gave up on martial arts.

"Chwe Sik-yi. I'll give you basic freedom. Build your power and collect corpses on your own. However, whatever I order you to do, execute it without complaint."

"...Kreuk. It's humiliating, but as long as my Life Vessel is in your possession, I have no choice."

"Don't do anything insane like massacring a village to gather corpses. When I summon you, don't refuse—respond."

Chwe Sik-yi nodded with a sullen expression.

"Understood. First, I should go to my homeland and assess the situation. With my connections, I should be able to receive various support."

"Homeland? Ah, True China doesn't exist."

"What?! You mean to say that True China lost to Great Korea?! Don't speak nonsense!"

"True China, Great Korea, Unbelievable Japan. They all fell."

"...You wouldn't be foolish enough to tell a lie that would be exposed immediately. What is the reason the three great powers were destroyed?"

"They fought each other and mutually destroyed themselves. In this era, it's nothing but an ancient tale. Ordinary people don't even know that the three nations ever existed."

"That's... impossible..."

Thud.

Chwe Sik-yi collapsed to the floor.

I watched him without emotion. Whether Chwe Sik-yi's world crumbled or not was none of my concern.

"Now get out of my workshop. Go outside and work. I don't need a slave without an auto-hunting function, so don't even think about slacking off."

I drove Chwe Sik-yi out of the estate.

Occasionally, when I had questions about necromancy, I would summon Chwe Sik-yi and ask him.

And some time later, a new gang called Hao Dragon was born in the outskirts of Neo London. There were rumors that Hao Dragon took subcontracting work from some corporation, handling all sorts of dirty jobs.
