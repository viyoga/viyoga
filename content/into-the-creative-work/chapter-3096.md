Back in reality, I checked my points first thing.

'With just 600 more points it'd be 100,000. What a waste.'

I decided to level up my Lightning attribute for the first time in a while. This was exactly why I'd been saving points.

[Spend 80,000 points to raise Lightning Lv.15?]

'Do it.'

[Lightning Lv.16

You can manipulate lightning at will. Mana and vitality are consumed when manipulating lightning.]

Bzzt.

Blue sparks burst around my body in an instant. The lightning energy had momentarily amplified and slipped slightly beyond my control.

I immediately focused my mind and reined in the amplified lightning energy. It was contained in a flash.

'That was just a little hiccup from the output spiking momentarily. Nothing dangerous.'

If there'd been gasoline nearby it would've been a disaster, but I was standing in the middle of my living room.

'As the Lightning level went up, my lightning transformation got stronger. Or should I say it evolved?'

Body lightning transformation became possible when Lightning reached level 14. But I couldn't control it perfectly. The maximum time I could maintain it was barely 10 seconds. Even with high mana, 10 seconds was the limit.

'This time I can maintain lightning transformation for over 10 seconds. Instead, it continuously drains mana. Let's give it a try.'

Bzzzzzzzt!

Sparks flew from my entire body as it transformed into lightning in an instant. But that lightning didn't damage the house. Every bolt flowing from my body was under my control.

I clenched a fist glowing deep blue.

Kwaaaang.

Even in lightning form, it carried physical force.

'Conversely, my body now doesn't take ordinary physical force. Mana-infused attacks would work, but... my body's durability has gone through the roof.'

Being in lightning form didn't mean I'd become pure lightning. It was more accurate to say my body had been transformed as close to lightning as possible. Thanks to that, both the properties of flesh and the properties of lightning coexisted.

'From a combat standpoint, this is better. If I were simply lightning, my weaknesses would be obvious. But this is similar to Lightning Soul Arhat.'

Thunder Heaven Style — Lightning Soul Arhat.

Originally a technique from the Thunder Sound Temple in the 'Bright Ascension Path' world, reinterpreted in the Thunder Heaven Style.

Bzzt.

Electricity sparked from the appliances and outlets around me. The electricity was naturally absorbed into me, becoming energy.

'This wasn't intentional.'

As if it were meant to be. The electricity around me was absorbed into me, becoming power.

'Even though it's energy, it's not mana, so having electricity around doesn't mean I can maintain lightning transformation indefinitely. Instead, the lightning power I can wield grows stronger.'

I released lightning transformation and Lightning Soul Arhat.

Mana had been consumed, but there were no side effects.

'I can maintain it for at least 5 minutes.'

That's more than enough time. I'd use Lightning Soul Arhat in combat, and most fights end within 5 minutes anyway.

'Can I transform just part of my body into lightning?'

Bzzt!

My right arm glows with a deep blue lightning radiance.

'Partial lightning transformation is possible too. The output is lower than expected though. Still, it absorbs ambient electricity.'

I gripped the Flaming Lightning Dagger in my left hand and swung it toward my right arm.

Bzzzzzzzt!

The red lightning of the Flaming Lightning Dagger and the blue lightning of my right arm tangled, repelled, and canceled each other out. But my will was firm, so a cut was carved into my right arm. Instead of blood, condensed lightning flowed out like liquid. The lightning dripped to the floor, writhed, then vanished as if evaporating. A bit of power drained away.

I shook my right arm in the air.

The blue lightning scattered into the air like melting, and my right arm returned to normal. There was no wound.

'When I release Lightning Soul Arhat, the damage I took also disappears and doesn't remain on my body. Instead, mana consumption increases.'

In game terms, it's like consuming MP instead of HP.

'This is insane. If I have enough mana, I'm basically immortal.'

One of the effects of Heaven's Heart is that mana becomes infinite for 1 minute.

'In other words, combined with Heaven's Heart, I become immortal for 1 minute. On top of that, Heaven's Heart's effect includes status ailment immunity. This is a 1-minute invincibility.'

My mood lifted.

At this rate, with my real body, even in the 'Bright Ascension Path' world I could hold my own...

'Maybe not that far. Even if I don't die for 1 minute, once that minute passes I'm done for.'

A 1-minute ultimate shield. But I didn't have an ultimate spear.

I was killing time appropriately, about to enter the 'Eternal Eden' world.

That is, if Yura hadn't come to my house.

"You free?"

"For now. Shall we go to the bedroom?"

"Sorry, but that's not why I came. The World Hunter Association wants to assign you a job."

"The World Hunter Association? I haven't heard anything about Korea blowing up."

"Korea is just a part of the world. If you don't want to, you can refuse. I'm not forcing you. No need to get upset."

"I'm not upset. You're just trying to give me a job, that's all."

The World Hunter Association understood the power of S-rank hunters very well.

They don't do stupid things like trying to recklessly boss around people with power comparable to natural disasters—individuals who could single-handedly stand against most nations.

"Right. It's nothing serious either. Even if you refuse, there are other S-rank hunters."

I let out a bitter smile.

You're not the only S-rank hunter. It was also a subtle remark meant to keep me in check.

"Anyway, the commission fee is 100 million dollars. …You're not interested in money, are you?"

"If I put my mind to it, I could earn 100 million dollars in a single day. Still, people don't move on money alone. What kind of problem blew up?"

"Do you know about the Bermuda Triangle?"

"Roughly. It's a mysterious place in the ocean, right? Ships passing through go missing, or something like that. I've heard rumors that an SS-rank dungeon exists in the Bermuda Triangle. Isn't it kind of like an urban legend?"

"Urban legend… Seems some information leaked out. There's an Old Gate in the Bermuda Triangle. The seal periodically breaks, so it has to be re-sealed. This time, the seal is breaking faster than usual. Originally it was supposed to be five years from now."

"Wait. What's an Old Gate? Is it a dungeon?"

"It's not a dungeon. It's a spatial anomaly that's existed for hundreds, thousands of years. From what I hear, it's connected to a world different from Earth. You can think of it as a primitive dungeon, roughly. Except it's connected to another world rather than being a dungeon."

Not a dungeon, but something like a dimensional gate connected to another dimension.

"You want me to destroy that Old Gate?"

"Currently, there's no way to destroy an Old Gate. While the World Hunter Association seals the Old Gate, you just need to eliminate the monsters coming through it. It's called Nephilim, I think. The information is here."

Yura shows me a tablet. Inside the tablet were photos and information about the Nephilim.

The Nephilim was a 300-meter-tall winged giant. It was naked but had no genitalia, and like a human, it had hair and a face.

"You want me to kill this monster?"

"You just need to hold it back. I hear it returns just before the Old Gate is sealed."

"This document says Old Gate-16. By any chance, are there more than sixteen Old Gates in this world?"

"The only materials I received are these, so I don't know the details. But probably… your guess is right."

I let out a hollow laugh.

I knew this reality was a world that could be destroyed at any moment, but I never expected something like this to exist.

"I'll take this job."

"Why? …I thought you'd refuse."

"If another S-rank hunter screws up, the world goes to shit. Besides, Ahyoung-noona down there is scheduled to participate too."

"Don't misunderstand. I didn't assign the job to Hunter Han Ahyoung to drag you in."

"I know. This is the ocean, after all. You probably asked her because her abilities are well-suited for it."

The World Hunter Association isn't stupid, and there's no way they'd force work on Han Ahyoung. Nor could they.

"The other hunters participating this time are… undecided?"

"It's not confirmed yet, but there's a high chance one of America's S-rank hunters will participate."

"As long as they don't grab my ankle, I don't care who it is. The schedule is in three days? That's pretty tight."

"Seems the World Hunter Association also only recently noticed the collapse of Old Gate-16's seal."

"You said the seal was originally supposed to break in five years? Why is it breaking five years early? There has to be a cause."

"No separate information has come down regarding that. Whether headquarters is hiding it, or headquarters itself hasn't figured it out. I can't know. Want me to ask in your name? They might tell you something even if they won't tell me, if you say you're curious."

"Ask them."

Yura fiddled with her smartphone. The World Hunter Association's reply was fast.

"…They say they're also in the process of figuring out the cause. There's no accurate information."

The quick reply was more disappointing than expected.

'I rode an American warship in the Martial Arts Tower world not long ago too.'

"Noona. You okay?"

Unless it's an emergency or they request it from the World Hunter Association, even S-rank hunters don't use teleportation often.

No matter how S-rank a hunter is, they feel a bit of motion sickness from unfamiliar spatial movement. Well, even if I hadn't been there, Han Ahyoung wouldn't have fallen. An S-rank hunter's motion sickness would've disappeared in less than a second.

'The important thing is that I grabbed Han Ahyoung's shoulder.'

Han Ahyoung and I gazed at each other with tender eyes. Her cheeks flushed slightly and she smiled shyly.

It's been like this ever since I gave her the ring last time. Han Ahyoung was always kind and affectionate to begin with, but after receiving the ring from me, she's never once gotten angry at me. She even goes along with somewhat perverted play without refusing. You could say she's become incredibly easy.

The distance between us just got closer, yet suddenly it feels like a pink aura is forming around us.

'Should I kiss her? Han Ahyoung looks like she's expecting it too.'
