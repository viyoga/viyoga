Creeeeak.

The door, which appeared ponderous, swung open.

Beyond the door, nothing but darkness was visible. The first thing to emerge from that darkness was a hand clutching a book.

There was writing on the book's surface, but its contents could not be deciphered.

The fingernails were pitch-black and the fingers long. Then, along with old-fashioned sleeves, a body clad in a noble's garments appeared. Above that neck were four heads. The heads of a man, a woman, an old man, and a child.

「The Forbidden Book's Debauchee (False) descends.」

His presence devoured the audience chamber. Not only the Empire's Five Dukes but even the Emperor lost their presence before him.

The weak staggered back, their bodies trembling. They felt instinctive terror before a demon that had crawled up from hell. His demonic energy was a darkness that would not vanish even with light present.

"Greetings. I am Dantalion, Duke of Hell. Seventy-first among the seventy-two demons. Ah, I trust none of you are fools who take that number as a rank. We demons are all equal."

Dantalion.

The demon uttered his true name.

Most of those who heard that true name let out groans. The faint-hearted collapsed as strength drained from their legs.

The demon looked at the Emperor standing before him.

The Emperor gripped the armrest of his throne. The Millennium Duke standing behind him fingered the sword at his waist. The moment the demon took even a single step forward, he was poised to draw.

Elena observed the demon with narrowly opened eyes. She seemed to have no intention of stepping forward for now.

The first to break the uncomfortable silence was the angel, who had not spoken a single word until now.

"An unclean being has risen to the surface. There is no one here who welcomes uninvited guests. Would you kindly return to where you originally belong?"

Dantalion turned his head to the side. It was not as smooth as a human's. It was slow, like a crude wooden puppet creaking.

"Oh. A single repulsive pigeon has been pecking at the surface's scraps. Rest assured. The lords of hell, myself included, have no business with you."

"...Are you saying you represent the demons of hell?"

"Indeed. So you stay quiet. Atlantis is not our battlefield."

“…….”

Gabriel shut her mouth. Since she wore a veil, her expression could not be seen.

Dantalion turns his head again.

With exaggerated gestures like an actor on a stage, he greeted the Emperor.

"You who sit at the highest place of humanity, rightful ruler of the Empire, contractor of the beast. I come representing the lords of hell to make a deal with you. One might call it a kind of contract."

"...A deal, you say. I did not know a demon could be so courteous. As far as I know, demons are monsters who delight in toying with humans. Have you come to seduce and deceive me with that wicked mouth of yours?"

"Haha. That is an undeniable statement. But not in this world. We cannot act freely in Atlantis. Escaping from hell is no easy feat either."

"You stand before me as an uninvited guest, do you not."

"It is possible precisely because I am an envoy representing hell."

"An envoy. Then I shall ask. What does hell want?"

"Peace. And coexistence."

“…….”

The Emperor's mouth fell open. He slowly closed his eyes and opened them. And that reaction was not the Emperor's alone.

"Demons are evil beings. They cannot be good, so the peace they utter is an insult, and the coexistence they desire is domination. Your Majesty. Do not lend an ear to the demon's words."

Gabriel said. Those who heard her voice became wary of Dantalion, as if coming to their senses.

"Are you trying to say demons desire only unconditional destruction? Even after thousands, tens of thousands of years pass, you lot from the heavenly realm are hopelessly narrow-minded. Emperor. In Atlantis, gods and monsters already coexist. What would change by adding demons to that? Angels are good. Demons are evil. It is merely a prejudice that began in an old era. If you are truly the lamp of humanity, think only of humanity's future."

"...As far as I know, the seventy-first demon knows the past and the future, reads people's hearts, and can even toy with those hearts. Are you reading my heart even now?"

"I am not the main body. I am a manifestation. I cannot fully wield the authorities the main body possesses. Of course, I can use them to some degree, but... Are you not protecting yourself with that crown?"

"Demon. If we accept hell's proposal, what does humanity lose, and what does it gain?"

"Humanity will lose a bit of order. But there will be no Ragnarok. Coexistence does not mean all the demons of hell will move to the mortal world. Only selected demons will walk the surface, even from hell. Hell's armies will not trample, and hell's fire will not burn humans."

"And if I do not accept that proposal?"

The demon laughed low, then answered.

"Ragnarok will become inevitable."

It was a threat wrapped in pleasant-sounding words.

The Emperor did not waver. His gaze left the demon and turned toward me.

"Prophet of Ragnarok. Will your prophecy come to pass?"

I answered.

"It will not come to pass."

The Emperor's gaze turned to the angel.

"Gabriel. I have heard that demons do not break contracts. Can demons be trusted?"

"Demons find loopholes in contracts and exploit them. Not everything in this world revolves around contracts alone, so demons are beings that cannot be trusted. Furthermore, even if that one represents the demons of hell, he does not represent all beings of hell."

The Emperor's gaze turned to Elena.

"Duke of Fantasy, I wish to hear your view."

"The Empire does not grovel. Even if the opponent is a demon of hell, the Empire will overcome and prevail."

The Emperor called the names of others besides these and posed questions.

"Marquis, are you afraid?"

"The Empire's nobles do not fear demons."

"You are an exile. Will you fight the demon?"

"There is no reason not to fight."

"Master of the Aeflan Legion. You played a great role in the Volcano Island assault. How do you see this matter?"

"Your Majesty. The absence of Ragnarok does not mean Atlantis will not perish. For Atlantis's future, we must conquer hell as well."

The Emperor looked at the demon again. Dantalion was relaxed even though he knew the mood was turning.

"Emperor. Make your decision for humanity."

"You ask only for my opinion."

"The one who sits at the highest place of humanity represents humanity. Above all else, your opinion matters most."

"As humanity's representative, I have no choice but to follow humanity's will. Humanity does not desire coexistence with demons, nor a false peace. As Emperor, I command. Millennium Duke. Kill the enemy of humanity."

"Yes."

The Millennium Duke, Kalon Brantis, who answered briefly, moved.

The conclusion came in the blink of an eye.

The Millennium Duke's sword pierced the demon's heart. Truly, one closed their eyes and opened them, and it was so.

Dantalion did not let out even a single groan. He did not give a single glance to the Millennium Duke before him, and looked at the Emperor.

"Emperor. I respect your choice. As a final gesture, I will take one of your questions. You may ask the question in your heart."

"...You speak as though you have peered into my mind."

"Even without peering, guessing human thoughts is not difficult. Ask what you are most curious about. Are you curious about the Empire's future?"

"I do not trust prophecies. A prophecy revealed will ultimately crumble."

"To my eyes, you seem to believe in prophecy more than anyone. If you have no question, so be it. I will pass that right to another."

The Millennium Duke, who had driven his sword into the demon's heart, furrowed his brow. He tried to swing the sword again, but it seemed difficult. Some other power was preventing the demon's death.

The demon's gaze turned toward me.

"Prophet of Ragnarok. Is there something you are curious about?"

"I just thought it in my head. Try answering."

"Regrettably, I cannot read your thoughts. Ask directly."

"What is the exact cause of Ragnarok?"

If I could find out the exact cause of Ragnarok, future matters would become easier. Even if it was too late to remove that cause, knowing the cause and result precisely would allow me to infer the process.

"The board has grown large. Too large, in fact. No small number of gods desire Atlantis's destruction."

Bang!

Dantalion's limbs burst. It was not the Millennium Duke's power. Nor could I sense the intervention of any other power. As if it were only natural, the demon's limbs burst.

'Is this the system's doing? That statement just now must have crossed a line by the system's standards.'

"Hehehe."

The demon laughed for several seconds.

He laughed until the moment his body collapsed, became dust, and vanished.

Everyone gathered in the audience chamber shut their mouths. Rather than rejoicing in the demon's death, they felt discomfort at the laughter it left behind.

Creeeeeeeak.

「The gates of hell close.」
