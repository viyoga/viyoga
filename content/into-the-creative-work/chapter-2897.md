The Great Witch Beatrice slowly raised a hand. The long, sharp fingernail of her index finger pointed at me.

,Opportunity? Aren't you the one who's desperate? If I don't help you, you'll die. I'm the only one in this world who can remove the curse of the Fallen in such a short time. Isn't that why you collected the witch's tokens and came to find me?"

What she said was true.

In this world. To be exact, among mortals, there is no one as skilled in curses as she is. She reached 9th Circle through curses.

The Great Witch studied the curse that was the witch's quirk. After researching and researching, she came to one conclusion: this curse will never disappear unless the caster of the curse dies.

,Don't tell me you think I'm terrified of death and came to you unconditionally to beg for my life? I've already made a backup plan. It doesn't matter even if you don't accept the deal."

Of course, I had a backup plan. It's just a bit too dangerous and troublesome.

,Hmph. It just looks like a bluff to me. I can see the curse of the Fallen coiling in your heart getting stronger and stronger. You don't have much time left. Have you left a will?"

,Looks like the negotiation has broken down. The witch will be tormented by her quirk forever now. Great Witch. I hope you don't regret today's events in the distant future."

,...You're an arrogant wizard. And hasty too. I haven't made a decision yet. Removing the curse of the Fallen means making an enemy of that Fallen. It's no small matter. I need time to think, so wait a moment. Yes. In the meantime, I need to hear what the Saintess next to you wants."

I nodded. But I didn't back down and was about to open my mouth. I was planning to set the stage as I had told Marta.

But Marta opened her mouth before I could.

,Great Witch. I've come to avenge my family."

Marta went straight to the point. I was about to jump in but stopped. Marta isn't a moron who ruins things with her hasty personality.

,The fact that she's stepping up now means her intuition is kicking in... or she might have received a revelation. Maybe she received a prophecy before.'

The Great Witch puffed on her pipe. Smoke billowed out. Marta couldn't stand it either and took out a cigarette and put it in her mouth. Is there some kind of strange sense of camaraderie among smokers? The Great Witch didn't look displeased, but instead looked at Marta with interest.

,A grudge. And a grudge related to family."

The Great Witch slowly scanned the witches surrounding the center of the plaza.

,Who killed the Saintess's family?"

,..."

It was quiet. The witches were too busy watching each other's reactions.

,Ha, it seems even our witches don't know. Saintess. May I ask what happened?"

,It happened before I was chosen by Lord Gabriel, before I became a Saintess. My parents and younger sister were eaten alive by a witch right in front of my eyes."

,Eaten alive, you say..."

The Great Witch tapped her pipe in the air and looked at one of the witches. The other witches also paid attention to that witch.

It was a fat witch about 2m tall. Her large hooked nose and the corners of her mouth were torn up to her ears. Her teeth were sharp like a shark's.

Her yellow eyes shone with murderous intent through her orange shaggy hair. She wore an ill-fitting yellow dress and adorned her body with flashy jewels. Her fashion sense was so messed up that even the saying "pearls before swine" didn't fit.

It was a disgusting appearance. Unlike Deneb, she was ostracized among the witches in a different way, and there wasn't a single witch around her. They didn't even try to make eye contact with her.

The problem is that even though she looks like that, she's an 8th Circle witch and not an easy opponent at all.

,I dunnoo that weell?"

The cannibal witch Orga dragged out her words. Her trying to act cute, which didn't suit her at all, made me frown involuntarily. Her unpleasant, beast-like voice also played a part.

If I, who have no particular grudge against her, am like this, what about Marta, who is her enemy? Surprisingly, she was maintaining her composure.

,It happened 17 years ago. On Christmas Day, you suddenly fell into our fireplace instead of Santa."

,Did I?"

Pa-deuk.

I could hear Marta grinding her teeth.

,Do you think I'm lying?"

,Nooope. I dunnoo. How can I remember every meeeal?"

Pat.

A coat appeared on Marta's body. Absolute Polymorph was released, and her hair returned from purple to its original dark green. I grabbed the cape coat flying towards me with telekinesis and put it on my shoulders.

Holy mana flowed from Marta's body.

,Hieeek...!"

,She was a real Saintess! I thought the Great Witch was being sarcastic because she looked like a Saintess!"

,The Guardian Saintess has appeared on Walpurgis Night...!"

The witches were horrified. Some were preparing for battle.

Ting.

When the Great Witch flicked her pipe in the air, the chaotic atmosphere was immediately cleared up.

,As the Great Witch, I have a duty to protect the witches. However, that doesn't mean I'm responsible for the actions of individual witches. Saintess. You participated in Walpurgis Night alone, even though you could have mobilized other saints and holy knights. Isn't that proof that you came to resolve a personal grudge? Also, since you participated as a guest, I, as the host, must grant your request."

The Great Witch pointed at Orga with her pipe.

,It's what you did. You resolve your own grudge. This is a witch trial that I recognize."

Witch trial.

It's a way to resolve disputes between witches. Simply put, the one who wins is right. It's a duel trial changed into a witch-style witch trial.

,Okayyy."

The witch trial began.

The cannibal witch Orga stepped forward. When her heavy legs stepped on the ground, the ground rumbled with a thud.

Kung! Kung! Kung!

Orga slowly headed towards the center of the plaza.

,She's layering body enhancement magic on herself every time she walks. She's cunning, unlike her stupid appearance and way of speaking.'

But I had told Marta about the witch trial in advance before coming.

Hwaaaaaak!

Two pairs of pure white wings of light spread from Marta's back, who had activated her stigmata. Holding a holy hammer in her hand, she kicked off the ground and instantly arrived in front of Orga and swung her hammer.

Kwaaaaaang!

Light burst out with a loud explosion.

A holy light that shone more brightly than the stars in the night sky.

The fragments of light that should have faded away immediately did not disappear and hovered in the air as fragments. The fragments of light scattered in the slight breeze that blew from somewhere.

,Hiiiiiek!"

The witches instinctively feared the fragments of light and cast their own defensive magic.

I also layered barriers to defend myself and distanced myself. My eyes watched the battle between Marta and Orga.

Bang! Kwaaaang!

Light continued to burst out and loud noises rang out.

,It's hard to see because of the light, but... I can tell they're colliding with each other. ...I can't see their movements well. One thing is certain, each attack is filled with murderous intent. They're exchanging blows with the power to destroy a small hill in an instant like jabs.'

The reason why the plaza isn't collapsing and the aftermath isn't harming the witches is because this is the Great Witch's space.

It was the moment when Marta and Orga separated from each other and the battle briefly subsided.

The fragments of light scattered around stuck to Marta's body and formed a white and holy armor.

It's her Unique Skill, Le Graal blanc (White Holy Grail). I knew it through my knowledge of the original game.

In response to Marta, Orga also used her Unique Skill.

Orga's skin turned white, her stomach split open, and hundreds of organs of all kinds popped out, scattering curses.

Unique Skill, Cannibal Holocaust.

They couldn't move as if time had stopped after deploying their Unique Skills. But the battle continued.

Light and curses collided and disappeared. But that was only for a moment. The hundreds of organs, the source of the curse, burned and disappeared, and the light became dominant. Marta took a step towards Orga. Marta was ahead in terms of compatibility.

,D-don't come near meeee!"

Orga shouted desperately. Of course, Marta didn't stop.

As Marta got closer and closer, Orga swung her fist. In response, Marta swung her holy hammer. Orga's arm burst. Blood, flesh, and bones evaporated without a trace upon touching the light.

,G-Great Witch-nim! Help meeeee!"

Orga cried out pathetically, shedding tears.

The Great Witch ignored her, sipping the smoke from her pipe with a languid look.

,...The Great Witch has no pity for Orga. To abandon an 8th Circle witch like this. Did she know about Orga's atrocities?'

The cannibal witch Orga. The targets of her cannibalism weren't just humans. Orga also ate other witches. If things didn't go well, I was going to spread the word about that fact and make her a public enemy of the witches. But the Great Witch seems to know much more than I thought.

Marta, who had seized the victory, raised her holy hammer high into the sky. When she struck Orga with her holy hammer, a pillar of holy light fell and burned Orga's body and soul to death.

Then the light faded and disappeared. The full-body armor that had been covering Marta's body also disappeared.

Marta put a cigarette in her trembling hand and stared blankly at the sky. She had killed her family's enemy and completed her revenge, so her feelings were solely her own, and it was difficult for me to even guess what she was feeling right now.

,The witch trial is over."

The Great Witch declared calmly. No witch raised any objections. On the contrary, more than half of them were smiling.

The Great Witch looked at me.

,There is a condition. You must prove in this place that you can kill Mephistopheles."

,I don't like it. Why should I do what you want? If you lack the information to judge me... isn't that a problem with your judgment?"

The Great Witch swallowed a hollow laugh. It was the witches nearby who were furious.

,What outrageous things is that wizard saying to the Great Witch-nim?!"

,The Great Witch-nim has been insulted! Let's kill him right now!!"

,Insulting the Great Witch-nim is no different from insulting us witches...!! We must hang that wizard!!"

The witches were revealing their murderous intent.

,Quiet."

They cowered at the Great Witch's one word.

,Human wizard. If you prove that you can destroy Mephistopheles, that son of a bitch demon... you will become an ally of the witches."

I smiled.

The witch forces are not weak. Although they are not as powerful as a strong nation like Neo London, they have the power to destroy a small kingdom. Becoming an ally with the witch forces? It's beneficial in many ways.

,What method do I need to use to prove it?"

,Remi. Come out."

The Great Witch called out to one of the witches.

,M-me?"

The one who answered was a witch who looked clueless. A witch with brown hair and freckles. The power I felt was 5th Circle.

,Yes. Come forward."

,Y-yes..."

The witch carefully walked to the center of the plaza, watching the Great Witch's reaction, and faced me. She avoided my gaze, turned her head, and shrugged her shoulders.

,Why did she call out the dimwitted Remi?"

,Isn't it because of her quirk? Remi's quirk is collecting wizards' hair."

,Is that so?"

The witches murmured.

,Remi? I remember seeing her in the game. An extra NPC with no interactions. I know her because her modeling is decent for an extra.'

There was no information to speak of.

How could I know her information when she's an NPC with no interactions? She's just a background character that enriches the game's setting.

The Great Witch didn't care and waved her pipe in the air.

I felt the space where Remi and I were distorted and isolated. When I quickly raised my head, Remi also raised her head.

,Wait a minute. The timing of this reaction... This witch sensed the change in space? A witch who is only 5th Circle?'

I raised my guard against the witch in front of me.

The power I feel is definitely 5th Circle. As much as she's called the dimwitted Remi, her skills must be terrible. But she noticed the anomaly in space at a similar time as me?

,Was she hiding her power?'

I tensed my body.

The witch was flustered and her voice trembled.

,G-Great Witch-nim...?"

,You're disgusting, Mephistopheles. Did you think I didn't know that that was your incarnation?"

The witch's expression disappeared in an instant. Soon, the witch snapped her head and looked at the Great Witch.

,How did you know? No, since when did you know?"

The Great Witch did not answer the demon's question.

,Wizard. You said you would kill Mephistopheles. Kill that incarnation and prove it to me. That you can kill Mephistopheles."

I answered with magic.
