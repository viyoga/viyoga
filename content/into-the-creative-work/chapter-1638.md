Heading to the nearest oasis was the right call.

I discovered a tribe settled near the oasis. The oasis was small, but the tribe's size wasn't. It looked like at least 300 people.

,This might be a decent size here.'

In the Scorpion Desert area I controlled, 300 people was a large tribe.

I approached the tribe. The tribe's warriors watched me, then drew their swords and blocked my path.

,Halt. This is the territory of our Sandclaw Tribe. Strangers are not allowed without permission.'

,The oasis also belongs to our tribe. If you want water from the oasis, pay up. 10,000 Penda per liter.'

10,000 Penda.

That was 100,000 won in Korean currency. Fucking expensive. Even for the desert, that price was ridiculous. They were adding a desert traveler premium. These bastards were deliberately trying to rip me off.

,You're an unpleasant bastard in many ways.'

The desert warriors looked at me with a mixture of contempt and disgust.

Right now, I was wearing Tutankhamun's golden mask on my head, shirtless, and only wearing pants. Since I was already doing this, I chose a proper pharaoh fashion.

,How dare they be so disrespectful.'

,Disrespectful? The weather's already making me annoyed... Some weirdo just showed up. If you want water, take off that mask. We won't sell you water until you remove that bizarre helmet.'

The apparent leader spoke threateningly. I could feel the killing intent every time he spoke. He's definitely got experience drowning people.

,Boss-nim. That looks like solid gold.'

,Ha. Don't talk nonsense. What idiot would make something like that out of gold?'

,It's real gold. I used to work in a gold mine until half a year ago, so I know. That luster can only be gold.'

,Hmm.'

The desert warriors' eyes changed. I could feel their greed for gold.

The leader looked around. He confirmed that I was alone. The leader grinned and raised his hand, and his subordinates surrounded me.

,Hand over the helmet to us. Then we'll spare your life. Of course, we won't give you any water from the oasis, so take off the helmet and fuck off.'

,I watched to see what you'd do... You're just a bunch of morons who can't even recognize the difference in levels.'

,Haha. You're rejecting the mercy we're offering? Admiring your spirit, we'll bury your body in a sunny spot!'

The leader drew his sword and swung it at me. The series of movements was flawless. I could tell how experienced a warrior he was.

But.

Kkaang!

The leader's sword was blocked by a transparent barrier. It was the barrier I used.

,I've decided how to kill you. You guys will kill each other.'

,What?'

,Death Puppets.'

Dark magic manifested.

Threads made of black energy seeped into the warriors' bodies.

Ppigeudeok ppigeudeok.

The warriors' limbs moved like broken machines. And they wielded their weapons, swinging them at their comrades.

,Euaaaaaaak!'

,M-My arm is moving on its own!'

,Leader-nim!! Help me!'

,Goddamn it! This is sorcery! If I just keep my wits about me, I can escape... Keueok!'

Blood splattered everywhere.

I stood still and watched the puppets' performance.

It was the first time I used dark magic. Actually, I don't even know how I used dark magic. I just manifested and used the dark magic formula that came to mind. All I really did was decide to use dark magic.

,For a warrior of this level to not even resist dark magic... It's not bad, I guess.'

The dead warriors collapsed on the floor.

I could see the warriors' souls. The souls were trying to escape their dead bodies, but my energy held them back from leaving.

,I will not grant you rest. Arise.'

,Geueooooooo....'

The dead warriors rose as undead. Their souls were trapped, unable to leave their dead bodies. Since their souls were trapped in their corpses, they would be much stronger than ordinary undead.

I pointed my finger at the tribe.

,Go and kill all of your tribesmen, leaving no one alive!'

The undead grabbed their swords and began running towards the tribesmen. They looked no different from when they were alive.

,Kuaaaaaaaak!'

,What the hell is going on?!'

,Is the desert leader crazy?!'

,It's a sorcerer! A sorcerer is using evil magic. Find the sorcerer and kill him!'

The quick-witted ones found me and charged. It was a useless effort. I'm not stupid or weak enough to be taken down by someone who just charges recklessly.

,Death Grab!'

The Death Grasp erupted from the ground. A large hand with long, black fingernails grabbed the guy charging at me and crushed him. Blood and guts fell on the sand.

,You guys won't die peacefully either. Arise.'

The guy who was crushed to death rose as a ghoul. The ghoul crawled on all fours and began eating his own guts that had fallen on the ground. I left the ghoul behind and headed towards the oasis.

,Oasis... I don't feel thirsty, so seeing the oasis doesn't affect me. But I can't just pass it by because I have a bad personality.'

I put my hand into the clear water of the small oasis.

I couldn't feel the water temperature. That's because I'm dead right now.

,Curse of Decay.'

The water in the oasis rapidly rotted.

,Poison.'

I added poison as well.

The rotten water in the oasis turned purple. Just then, I saw a small lizard passing by. I snatched the lizard as if catching it and threw it into the oasis.

Chiiiiik!

The lizard's flesh and guts all melted away, leaving only bones. The lizard, now just bones, became undead and swam inside the oasis with its short arms.

,Alright, then....'

I moved towards where the tribesmen were.

,The beauties will live, and the rest will die!'

• • •

I raided 5 tribes.

I walked through the desert leading an undead army of 1,000. The undead didn't tire. They could walk for hours in the scorching sun without a single complaint. However, the smell of rotting corpses was quite annoying.

,1,000 is not enough. I need to gather at least 20,000. I should gather about 5,000 and then storm the city.'

I didn't walk myself but rode in a palanquin. I had the undead carry a huge wooden plank. The wooden plank was a whopping 30 pyeong in size.

Sitting comfortably in a chair on the palanquin, I watched the beauties dancing on the wooden plank. 20 beauties were shaking their bodies and acting coy to survive. It was a satisfying sight.

,Huh?'

I saw tents in the distance. People were gathered there.

,Is it a desert tribe.'

It looked like about 100 people.

,There are a lot of camels. Lots of luggage too. Was it not a tribe but a caravan crossing the desert? It's a huge scale for a caravan.'

They spotted me too. I could faintly hear their shouts. They were moving in a flurry, not looking like they wanted to fight, but trying to escape.

,With an undead army of over 1,000... Is it natural for them to run away.'

I spoke to the undead.

,Increase the speed.'

The undead followed my orders absolutely. The undead began to run. The palanquin shook up and down.

,Kyaaaaaak!'

The beauties riding in the palanquin lay flat on the palanquin and gripped the handles tightly to avoid falling off.

The caravan was desperately trying to escape. The caravan was much faster than I expected. At this rate, I might miss the caravan.

,You can't escape without my permission! Death Field!'

Dark mana spread out from me in all directions. The golden desert sand turned black. Black sand rose from the ground, blocking the caravan's escape route.

,You can't escape! Prepare for battle!'

The warriors guarding the caravan drew their swords.

,Stop.'

The undead, about to charge at the caravan, stopped abruptly.

,These stupid undead can't distinguish beauties. If I recklessly order a charge, they might even kill the beauties.'

I should secure the beauties first and then order the undead to charge.

The palanquin continued to move forward. When it came to the front of the undead army, I got off the palanquin.

,Are you the master of this undead army?'

A man with kind-looking gray hair spoke to me first.

I tilted my head for a moment. It was a voice I had heard somewhere before. I scanned the man up and down. He was a merchant wearing fancy clothes.

,Who are you?'

,I am Harmo, the owner of the Licoss Merchant Guild. Do you know the Licoss Merchant Guild?'

,How could I not know the Licoss Merchant Guild, the best merchant guild west of Atlantis.'

The Licoss Merchant Guild was also the most important and influential guild in the western desert region.

,Then the story will be quick. Let's make a deal. I will give you the goods you want, so let us go.'

,You want to negotiate? Well, not bad. First offer. Sparkle.'

Harmo's eyes widened as if they would tear.

Sparkle.

That was the codename Harmo used in Helten.

,That voice... I thought it sounded familiar, could it be Heavenly Demon?'

,Haha. It's nice to see you like this. Heavenly Demon. What are you doing here?'

I looked around. There were many eyes watching.

,Let's talk separately.'

,I understand. I'll have the tent set up. Ah, everyone here is trustworthy, so don't worry.'

,I don't mind if my identity is revealed.'

,Because you're openly active. I envy that.'

The tent was quickly completed. Harmo and I sat facing each other inside the tent.

,Heavenly Demon. It's nice to see you like this. I never dreamed that Tutankhamun would be you.'

,How do you know about Tutankhamun?'

,Didn't you know? The story of Tutankhamun has already spread throughout the Golden Desert. Hatep, the ruler of the Golden Desert, swore to the citizens that he would kill Tutankhamun and prove his greatness.'

,Hatep. He's the guy I need to kill. That bastard has been flapping his gums unnecessarily.'

,He wants to show off his achievements. Heavenly Demon. Could you explain how you ended up like this from beginning to end?'

I explained roughly. From the owner of the oasis to Tutankhamun's golden mask and the special event.

A smile appeared on Harmo's lips after hearing the whole story.

,It's an interesting situation. Heavenly Demon. Let's make a deal. I will support you with equipment to arm the undead and equipment used by necromancers.'

,The price?'

,I want a monopoly on trade in the Golden Desert.'

,Good.'
