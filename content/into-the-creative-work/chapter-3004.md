According to Iste's words, there were two living Legion Commanders: Mekin and Baal.

The 5th Legion Commander and the 8th Legion Commander, respectively. Baal in particular was practically a fresh recruit, having become a Legion Commander less than a year ago.

'Why are these two the ones left alive?'

Just what kind of event could have killed three Legion Commanders?

An assault from another dimension? The odds were low. Physically impossible. And even if it were possible, three Legion Commanders dying at once? That was out of the question. Demon Legion Commanders aren't weak.

'The idea that the Demon God the demons worship stepped in and disposed of the Legion Commanders doesn't hold up either. If the Demon God were going to act directly, it would have already done so. The current Demon God only accepts offerings of creatures and planets from dimensions the demons have invaded, and grants immortality in return—that's the extent of it.'

The name "Demon God" exists only because it's the god the demons follow. What its actual name is, no one knows, and whether it holds any attachment to the demons is equally unknowable.

'To the Demon God, demons and humans are like ants that can be killed with a single finger. It wouldn't hold that much attachment to demons. There's no way it purged the Demon Legion Commanders for failing to invade Earth. The Demon God has no reason to fixate on Earth.'

Punish failed demons for their failure? Would a being of that caliber really hold such expectations for creatures that amount to ants?

'Whether the ants succeed or fail at their task, who cares. They're just ants in the end. If those ants offer something to me, I might take a slight interest and grant them a small candy as a reward... but that's all. Even if those ants die, it'll just be a shrug.'

So a purge by the Demon God was also out of the question. Therefore, the most likely possibility in my estimation was—

'Civil war.'

Demons killing each other in infighting.

You ask if they get along poorly because they're all demons? There's a reason they're called demons. There's no way those bastards would develop any sense of camaraderie just because they share the same species.

'What's strange is that Mekin and Baal survived.'

Baal should be the weakest among the Demon Legion Commanders. He became a demon most recently, so it stands to reason he'd be weaker than the other Legion Commanders who've lived for at least hundreds, if not thousands, of years.

'Mekin ranks high among the Demon Legion Commanders when it comes to raw power alone. But he's not at the top. The strongest among the Demon Legion Commanders is the 1st Legion Commander.'

And that 1st Legion Commander was dead.

'...An incomprehensible outcome. But I can't deny reality just because of that. If a civil war really did happen, and those two survived... then those two could be stronger than the three who died.'

Was this a good thing, or a bad thing?

It was still hard to gauge.

"Ninth Apostle, the preparations are complete."

One of the Gu-Mok Church's followers approached me and spoke. I nodded and headed to the prepared ritual hall.

Hundreds of followers had gathered in the ritual hall. Among them were the Apostles, including myself and Cassandra. Unlike ordinary followers, they were the ones born with the Eyes.

At the center of the ritual hall, the Saint Iste knelt with both hands clasped together. Eyes symbolizing the Gu-Mok Church were painted on the floor, ceiling, and walls.

A bizarre ritual befitting a cult. That phrase fit the scene perfectly.

"You have come, Ninth Apostle. We shall lend the power of the ritual to bolster the Saint, and the Saint shall become your eyes. You shall be the head and guide the eyes. Your place is behind the Saint."

"Understood."

I stood behind Iste. She wore garments similar to a nun's habit. Befitting a Saint, she exuded a refined yet holy atmosphere.

'Her ass and tits are holy in a whole different sense.'

While I was entertaining those blasphemous thoughts, the ritual began.

The Apostles and followers, excluding myself, knelt and offered prayers to the god with nine eyes. They muttered in some alien tongue, so even I didn't know the details of what they were saying.

Though I was called the Ninth Apostle, I actually knew very little about the Gu-Mok Church's doctrines. I had no particular interest in them either.

"Eugene-nim. I can see countless possibilities of the future. Shall we examine them one by one?"

Iste asked, gazing into empty space.

"No. First, I want to know about the past. Did a civil war occur in the Demon Realm?"

"The Demon God's influence is protecting the Demon Realm, but... it isn't very strong. It's thin. The demons' faith toward the Demon God is practically nonexistent. If their faith toward the Demon God had been strong, if their power were as it once was, peering into the past would have been extremely difficult."

"Demons having deep faith is a funny thought in itself. What do you see?"

"War. Demons are killing and devouring each other."

"...Devouring? Not as a metaphor, but literally?"

"Yes. The Demon Realm is like a den of beasts. Among demons, there is no being that respects or loves its own kind."

"The Legion Commanders too?"

"Yes. The 5th Legion Commander devoured the 1st Legion Commander. The 8th Legion Commander preyed upon the 6th Legion Commander using her authority."

The 5th Legion Commander was Mekin, and the 8th Legion Commander was Baal.

Baal devouring a fellow demon made sense. Baal's authority was predation itself. Just as Seong Ha-ri gained immense power by devouring spirits, she too gained power by devouring demons.

'...Perhaps Baal might be even stronger than Seong Ha-ri.'

Mekin devoured the 1st Legion Commander? He wasn't the type to do something like that without reason.

'Do demons get stronger by eating other demons? It's possible. Demons may be evil, but they're not stupid. They wouldn't resort to cannibalizing their own kind without reason. Even if they can't absorb authority like Baal's predation, what if they can absorb a portion of the power?'

That was reason enough to devour one's own kind.

"Can you see the demons' present?"

The followers' prayers grew louder. Then, suddenly, several followers vomited blood and collapsed. Iste's upper body, which had been holding firm, wobbled.

"...Eugene-nim. Seeing the Demon Realm's present appears to be difficult."

"The demon bastards aren't fools either, so they must be guarding against our tricks. Let's move on to the future."

More precisely, we would be viewing the possibilities of the future.

Since it wasn't a 100% certain future, the burden was less than viewing the past or present. The goal was to confirm as many future possibilities as possible and compile the information.

"Kuhlk!"

"Bleeegh!"

"Keheok—huk!"

As time passed, followers who had been praying vomited blood and lost consciousness. This was because they were sharing the burden Iste bore. Even if they didn't die, they would need to focus on recovery for the time being.

"Iste. When will the demons invade?"

"Among the possibilities, there was a future where they invade Earth today. However, that number was small. The most common possibility is four months from now. At the latest, the demons will invade by the end of this year."

"So four months from now is the most likely. Where on Earth do they appear?"

"Mostly Seoul, the capital of Korea. The next highest possibilities are the United States, Europe, and Russia, in that order."

"That's not much different from my expectations. What about the outcome?"

"Aaaagh!"

Iste screamed. A dark, writhing energy began emanating from her body.

Thud! Thud! Thud!

Dozens of followers nearby collapsed without even managing to scream.

『Demon Hunter (SSS) has detected demonic energy.』

『Demon Hunter (SSS) is suppressing the demonic energy.』

The dark energy emanating from Iste's body weakened.

'So the demons weren't pulling any tricks. She was affected by viewing the demons' future. Thanks to Demon Hunter (SSS), I can roughly understand what happened.'

I waited calmly. There was no way Iste, the Saint of the Gu-Mok Church, would die from something like this.

Iste composed herself. Breaking out in a cold sweat, she spoke.

"In 90% of the futures I saw, humanity is defeated. The aftermath of the defeat is largely the same. All of humanity on Earth is slaughtered by the demons, and humanity's souls are offered to the Demon God. Eugene-nim... you become the 8th Legion Commander's plaything...."

Me, becoming Baal's plaything? I'm a little curious what kind of life that would be.

'That aside. The fact that 90% of future possibilities end in defeat is a bit shocking. I should assume that Mekin and Baal's power is greater than I expected.'

Conversely, 10% of the possibilities end in victory.

"How does my future self fight?"

"In various ways. Sometimes fighting with the power of spirits, sometimes becoming lightning itself to fight, and sometimes fighting with demonic power as well."

"What about my leisure life?"

"Pardon? I'm not sure. Let me examine the future possibilities in more detail."

"No. Forget it. No matter how many times you look, it probably won't show up."

I could roughly guess how it worked.

Unless I revealed it myself, the existence of the leisure life couldn't be discovered through viewing the future. If even transcendent beings couldn't confirm it, there was no way Iste could.

'The future possibilities Iste saw must not have the variable of the leisure life applied. In other words, the victory or defeat doesn't mean much.'

I decided to stop caring about victory and defeat and focus on gathering information.

The strategies the demons use, information on particularly active demons, the bastards who betray humanity and side with the demons, and so on. There was plenty of information to be gleaned through the possibilities.

"Hahk, huk.... Eugene-nim. I'm reaching my limit."

Iste panted. Her clothes were soaked with sweat and clung tightly to her body. Fewer than fifty followers remained nearby still praying properly. The Apostles, including Cassandra, had each vomited handfuls of blood.

"This is the last one. In the future where humanity—where I—won, is there a possibility that Baal is on our side?"

"It's few, but yes, there is...!"

"How? Did I persuade Baal? Or did I force Baal into submission?"

"Through karma... Aaaagh!"

Iste let out a tearing scream and toppled backward. Her eyes were closed as she lay unconscious, and blood flowed from her mouth and nose. I hurried over to check her condition. Her internal injuries were severe, but she wasn't dead. It wasn't at a level that a potion could fix instantly.

'A few days of proper recovery and she'll be fine.'

I let out a small sigh.

I had gained a lot of information.

Among it all, the thing that concerned me most was what pertained to Baal.

'Karma, huh. For the future I desire, what I need most is karma. I'm not sure if the karma I currently possess is enough.'
