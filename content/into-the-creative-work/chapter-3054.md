The Poison Cloud Demon Dragon is an extremely powerful technique against living creatures. The problem is that, being a heterodox poison art even within the Heavenly Demon Divine Art, it consumes a massive amount of internal energy with each use, and afterward, residual poison lingers in the dantian and energy meridians, making it impossible to immediately use another technique.

Wang Ji-yuk knew this and charged at me. Jade-green energy surged from both his palms.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 7]

My thoughts accelerate and the world slows. Yet even in this slowed world, Wang Ji-yuk was remarkably fast.

'Against someone of Wang Ji-yuk's caliber, perfectly evading with instant acceleration is impossible. I've already used Flash Step against him several times. He's probably accounting for me dodging with instant acceleration as well.'

Two options surfaced in my mind.

The first is to deliberately take the hit. With my durability, I should be able to endure one blow. Even if I'm injured, it won't be fatal.

The second is to risk evasion. I could fail to dodge, and in that case, I would definitely suffer a fatal wound.

'A damnable binary choice.'

I made my decision.

I summoned Stormbreaker from my inventory to block. The reason I chose it over the more familiar Hwaryeon Flying Blade is that Stormbreaker's wide spear form makes it easier to defend with.

Flash Step releases, and Wang Ji-yuk, now upon me, strikes the spear shaft with both palms.

KWAAAAANG!

I blocked it.

The impact was weaker than expected—but that surprise lasted only a moment before my brain and internal organs were rattled.

“……!!”

I vomited a massive surge of blood from my mouth as I was sent flying backward.

'Damn it. That was an attack imbued with the mysteries of the Internal Heavy Hand technique.'

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 6]

Even as I flew back, I didn't take my eyes off Wang Ji-yuk. I could see blood flowing from his nose. To conceal the number of Internal Heavy Hand strikes in his arsenal, he'd risked self-injury. Compared to the internal damage I'd taken, though, it was a drop in the bucket.

'That last attack ruptured my organs and severed my energy meridians. It's a critical wound, but not immediately fatal.'

I am not an ordinary martial artist—I am the incarnation of the Harmony Realm. What would be a fatal wound for a normal martial artist is not so for the incarnation of the Harmony Realm. Even if my dantian were destroyed or my arm severed, I could recover within days.

'He knows that too.'

So Wang Ji-yuk, without letting his guard down, stepped the Drunken Eight Immortals Stance and pursued me as I flew backward.

'Falling Star Swords.'

While flying back, I opened my inventory. Hundreds of Falling Star Swords flew at Wang Ji-yuk. He swung his palms, and a jade-green water dragon surged upward, shattering dozens of incoming swords in one sweep.

Thousands of Falling Star Swords were consumed in mere seconds.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 5]

'Even if it looks like a meaningless attack, I bought a few seconds. And I think I've figured out his mind-image trait.'

Mind-image trait.

Upon reaching the Harmony stage, one naturally awakens a portion of their mind-image. The power that naturally melds with or is realized through it.

In my case, the mind-image trait is Black Lightning, a fusion of thunder energy and Heavenly Demon energy.

'Wang Ji-yuk's mind-image trait is Suppression. The energy that penetrated my body doesn't dissipate—it lingers and inhibits regeneration.'

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 4]

THUD!

My flying body collided with an invisible wall and collapsed. I'd flown all the way to the edge of the lake. I briefly considered entering the water to force an underwater battle, then dismissed it.

'Every time he swings his palm techniques, the water reacts. It means he's naturally attuned to water energy. Relying solely on the Blessing of Water to fight underwater... would actually put me at a disadvantage. Especially since I mustn't forget that Namhwa Line is backing him up with Taiping Sorcery.'

I stood atop the water's surface.

Bzzt.

I circulated thunder energy to manipulate my ravaged body. I abandoned the internal organs unnecessary for immediate combat. Instead, I reinforced my energy meridians, heart, and nervous system.

"I've heard the Heavenly Demon Divine Art contains a final secret technique that burns one's own soul for a mutual destruction gambit."

"You mean Heaven-Shattering? You've done your research."

"You have a way to survive even after using that technique. If you're going to use it, now would be the best time."

"I see. So you're wary of Heaven-Shattering."

Too obvious.

He probably has a countermeasure for Heaven-Shattering. And in all likelihood, it's Namhwa Line.

A plan surfaced.

"But you're not worth Heaven-Shattering."

"You bastard..."

After sneering at Wang Ji-yuk once, I summoned the Hwaryeon Flying Blade and gripped it in my hand.

Domain Expansion.

The domain bursting from the Hwaryeon Flying Blade seized the surrounding space.

"Using cheap sorcery, are you!"

Wang Ji-yuk swallowed a breath and poured strength into his entire body. The energy wave erupting from him resisted the domain.

The domain could not suppress him.

'I knew it wouldn't work. The incarnation of the Harmony Realm firmly possesses their own laws.'

I wrapped the spread domain around my own body.

Bzzt-bzzt.

Thunder Heaven Style ultimate technique: Thunder Light Crimson Wheel.

A crimson halo flashed and rotated behind my back.

It didn't end there. I used the Heavenly Heart I'd been saving.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Duration: 1 minute.]

Heavenly Heart's basic effect is amplification of physical abilities.

My body grows strong enough to match Wang Ji-yuk, who stands at the upper tier of the Harmony Realm. Sensing this, Wang Ji-yuk's eyes turned fierce.

'While using Heavenly Heart enhanced by Dragon Heart, my mana becomes infinite. In other words, right now my internal energy is limitless.'

Wang Ji-yuk's face contorted into sheer shock.

To Wang Ji-yuk, it must feel as though I suddenly grew stronger and my internal energy recovered. He knows of Heaven-Shattering's existence, but he's never seen it in person, so...

'He'll mistakenly think I used Heaven-Shattering.'

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

I stepped the Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step with all my might.

The lake's surface churned violently, and Wang Ji-yuk's eyes briefly went hazy before snapping back.

Having mastered a direct-combat lineage divine art and being at a higher stage than me, the Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step didn't take full effect.

'Is Namhwa Line inside him protecting his mind? Even so, he seems surprised that it partially worked.'

Wang Ji-yuk was certain.

"Namhwa Line! The Heavenly Demon has used Heaven-Shattering! Now is the time! If we overcome this crisis, we can kill the Heavenly Demon!"

Namhwa Line, who had been hiding inside Wang Ji-yuk's body watching the situation, intervened.

Namhwa Line's Taiping Sorcery, as observed through Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, is a type of spell replication. It can use a target's unique spells after seeing them even once, but the power is degraded compared to the original.

-Demon-Subduing True Scripture.

Light that suppresses demons burst from somewhere. I felt a thick wave of revulsion.

-Demon-Quelling Divine Buddha.

A golden Buddha statue appeared behind Wang Ji-yuk. I felt pressure from the statue's gaze.

-Advent of the Demon-Facing One.

-White light generated from above pierced down into Wang Ji-yuk's crown. A temporary enhancement spell. Wang Ji-yuk's power surged. Even the unpleasant demon-quelling aura grew stronger.

"Heavenly Demon! I will kill you and restore peace to the martial world! And the Beggar's Sect will revive and endure forever!"

"I get the gist. Namhwa Line, you're weaker than I thought? I expected someone called a Zenith-rank to be absurdly strong, but... that wasn't the case."

"Nonsense!"

Wang Ji-yuk, his entire body glowing, charged.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Heaven-Shattering.

My soul shattered and ignited.

Heavenly Demon energy incomparable to anything before erupted, seizing the space in an instant. My hair turned pure white and stood on end.

Thunder Light Crimson Wheel.

Heavenly Heart.

Heaven-Shattering.

The three overlapped, and I felt overwhelming power.

Wang Ji-yuk, who had been charging, faltered for a moment, then gritted his teeth and moved both palms. He became a dragon. His hands were the dragon's fangs.

What a ridiculous sight. Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms. A palm technique for subduing dragons, and in the end he becomes one.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Dragon Fist—North Wind Demon Dragon.

I thrust my fist.

A dragon of black wind shot from my fist, tearing apart Wang Ji-yuk and the lake beyond before vanishing.

Wang Ji-yuk, his entire body shredded with only his head remaining, serenely accepted death—but glared at me as if he'd kill me still.

"I was clearly at a higher stage than you...! Not only that, I even had Namhwa Line's help!"

"The loser talks too much. I have no interest in you. Come out, Namhwa Line."

"Namhwa Line said this. The Heavenly Fiend Star is too dangerous! Every immortal in the Celestial Realm is watching you, and they will kill you! Heavenly Demon! I'll be waiting for you in the Netherworld!"

Wang Ji-yuk died, leaving behind a curse.

I watched Wang Ji-yuk's head. Inside his body—specifically, within his mind-image—I could see Namhwa Line, who had been hiding there, using a spell to secretly slip out. If I'd left him alone, he would have died along with Wang Ji-yuk inside his mind-image.

'He used a presence-concealing spell. But I can see right through it.'

Taigong Wang's words came to mind. He'd said that with these eyes, I could use it without difficulty. So this is what he meant.

I caught the fleeing Namhwa Line. In my grip, the old man's neck was seized and he thrashed.

"Kuhk, kuk! You're not even an Ascended One...!"

"Weak. You can't even break free?"

"You fool...! Don't you know that immortals can't properly use their power in the lower realms! If this were the Celestial Realm, you'd already be dead!"

My eyes saw through his lie.

"A lie. Being an immortal doesn't automatically make you strong."

Or maybe Namhwa Line was just particularly pathetic.

The tip of the Hwaryeon Flying Blade aimed at Namhwa Line's heart. Even facing death, Namhwa Line showed no fear.

"Do you know the greatest difference between you and immortals? Immortals have achieved eternal life."

"You're not immortal and indestructible, though."

"Heh heh. The Netherworld is governed by the laws of the Celestial Realm. Immortals do not die even when killed. I will remember this. You will pay the price."

"I see. Now I understand why the Emperor and Taigong Wang want my power. You're saying I'm the only one who can kill you immortals."

『Using the light of the Heavenly Gang Star.』

『Heavenly Slaughter Star』

Death Execution, one of the Heavenly Slaughter Star's two abilities.

The absolute power of death that kills even the target's reincarnation cycle coils around the tip of the Hwaryeon Flying Blade.

Namhwa Line's composed expression changed. It became stained with shock and terror.

"Th-that power, don't tell me! Heavenly Slaughter Star! The Heavenly Slaughter Star should be dead! No, the Heavenly Slaughter Star maintaining sanity?! No, before that, is it even possible to be born under two stars?! You—what in the world are you?!"

"That's a pathetic last word."

"S-stop! A deal—let's make a deal! Don't you want treasure?! An immortal's treasure! You could live in luxury... GRAAAAAAGH!"

The blade pierced Namhwa Line's heart. Namhwa Line died, screaming a horrible shriek.

I confirmed Namhwa Line's complete death.
