As soon as Milyeong woke up from the bed, she moved busily. First, she kicked us out of the room and changed her clothes. Wiyu, Sama-ryeong, and I chatted in the living room.

Sama-ryeong was very conscious of Wiyu. It was inevitable since Wiyu was strong.

As we talked, the conversation naturally turned to Milyeong.

,You said her name is Milyeong. A Hoin clan member... I was surprised because they are a mixed-blood clan not easily seen in the Empire."

,Come to think of it, I haven't seen a race like the Hoin clan in the Empire. Do you call them a mixed-blood clan? I thought you'd call them half-demons."

Objectively speaking, Milyeong was very beautiful. But to others, her fox ears and tail could be off-putting. To me, it's like she's cosplaying, so it's hot. But to the people of this world, there was a good chance she would look like a monster.

,His Majesty the Emperor has decided to call them the mixed-blood clan. We can't call them half-demons since they don't use demonic energy."

Mixed-blood clan. It wasn't exactly wrong.

The ancestors of the Hoin clan are spiritual beings. The famous Gumiho, no less. It is said that the spiritual being, who was a fox, married a human, left descendants, ascended, and went up to the Immortal Realm. In a way, the Hoin clan were descendants of gods.

The Hoin clan, who inherited the characteristics of spiritual beings, had special abilities. One is the ability to charm people, and the other is the ability to absorb the vital energy of others. Both are said to be abilities that they rarely use.

,It's closer to an ability they can't use. After all, the Hoin clan live together in Guyugok."

Using their abilities among themselves? That would be no different from a declaration of war.

Another reason why the Hoin clan live together in Guyugok is because Guyugok is rich in vital energy. Vital energy is also the life force of nature. In many ways, Guyugok is the perfect place for the Hoin clan.

Even so, it doesn't mean that the Hoin clan are a race that just stays cooped up in a corner. They know how to use their abilities. Absorbing the vital energy of humans alone helps with training.

Besides, this is the world of Bright Light Ascension. For the sake of training, for fun, for greed, etc., there are people everywhere who can do anything for all sorts of reasons.

,When I saw her earlier, Milyeong's energy was weakened. She seems to need some rest and recuperation."

Wiyu said.

I nodded too. Unlike her healthy appearance, I could feel that Milyeong's energy had weakened.

,It's natural since she failed to ascend to a higher realm and was in a coma for several months. I think I'll have to give her my vital energy for the next few months."

According to Milyeong, my vital energy is the best. It's said to be excellent in terms of quality and quantity. It was like that even before my stamina stat was low, but what about now? I was a walking tonic for Milyeong.

,Recently, she hasn't coveted my vital energy at all. Was she feeling sorry for absorbing my vital energy?"

I didn't care if my vital energy was sucked out until I died. I had complete recovery, after all. No, could she even absorb my vital energy until I died?

It was then.

Uuung. The air vibrated. The barrier that Milyeong had set up on Nakwol Mountain reacted. In other words, uninvited guests had entered Nakwol Mountain. They weren't just passing through Nakwol Mountain. If they were, the barrier itself wouldn't react.

Wiyu, the owner of Nakwol Mountain, remained calm. The intrusion of uninvited guests happens several times a year. Each time, she either drives them away or kills them.

,The barrier has detected intruders."

,It's nothing. There's no reason for Master to step in. Disciple."

I got up from my seat.

,Yes. Yes. I'll take care of it and come back."

Wiyu would only step in herself when I or Milyeong weren't around. When I'm around, I usually take care of it.

,This is Nakwol Mountain. Do not kill recklessly."

,Yes."

I revised my plan to kill them all and come back immediately. I didn't want to cause conflict with Wiyu over something like this.

I went outside and spread electromagnetic waves.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Waves.

The electromagnetic waves quickly covered the entire Nakwol Mountain. It searches for signs of humans, excluding creatures in the ground and animals playing around.

There were five humans. All of them had learned martial arts and were climbing up Nakwol Mountain at a rapid pace.

I hesitated as I was about to descend with the Lightning Flash.

,Now that it's come to this, should I use the Cloud Riding Token?"

I summoned the Cloud Riding Token from my inventory.

A dodecagonal azure metal badge. As I infused it with energy, it glowed. At the same time, clouds gathered and formed in front of me. I climbed onto the cloud and descended.

The five martial artists running up Nakwol Mountain stopped. Two masters of Fifth Stage and three of Initial Stage. They clasped their fists towards me.

We greet the elder of the Jianghu. We recently became Muyas (無野) due to an incident in our sect.

Muya. It means they have no affiliation. I raised my hand to stop them. I wasn't interested in their circumstances.

,I'm not interested in your circumstances. I'll show you special mercy. Leave this place. Nakwol Mountain already has an owner."

Most of the guys who come to Nakwol Mountain come to settle down. Nakwol Mountain has a large spiritual vein, making it a great place to train.

Usually, when I tell them to get lost, they do. I'm an absolute master who has reached the Third Level of Essence, Qi, and Spirit. These guys' strength can't beat me.

But these guys didn't back down.

,I heard from the village below that the owner of Nakwol Mountain is a woman called the Nakwol Divine Woman. I heard that she hasn't come down from the mountain for decades, so she must be dead."

I provide all the necessities. There was no need for Wiyu to go down to the village below. It seemed that Wiyu had become a living legend in the village below.

,So?"

,We were destroyed by Gang Suchang (降水槍). It is said that a gentleman's revenge is never too late, even after 10 years. We need a spiritual edge for that revenge. I understand that you, Senior, have been staying at Nakwol Mountain. But it seems you are alone. If you had disciples or subordinates, they would have already come forward.

,Get to the point."

,We are going to establish a new sect here. We will give 50% of all profits from dominating the villages below to Senior. If you wish, we will provide it in the form of spiritual pills or elixirs that help with training. So please allow us to use this mountain."

It was a tempting offer to others. They would make a profit even if they did nothing. But not to me. I have the Heavenly Gang Star System and the Demonic Cult.

,You guys are lucky. It's rare for me to show mercy more than once. Offer your right arm and get lost. Then I'll spare your lives."

,...We'll offer 70%."

,You kicked away the mercy I gave you twice. If you want to die so badly, it's only right that I kill you."

I used the Cloud Riding Token. Dark clouds were created in the sky in an instant, blocking the sunlight.

,Die with the thunder."

Kwakwakwakwakwang!

Thick lightning flashed and struck the bastards. It wasn't ordinary lightning. It was lightning filled with my power. The efficiency was even better thanks to the clouds, of course. In terms of power, it would be more than enough to turn the bastards into ash in an instant.

But the bastards survived.

The three Initial Stage guys disappeared as ash as expected, but the two Fifth Stage guys were still alive and well.

I frowned and glared at the bastards.

,You bastards aren't human."

,We are human."

,Don't give me that bullshit. I felt you using demonic energy when you protected yourselves from the lightning."

It's not surprising that monsters disguise themselves as humans. This continent ruled by the Emperor is a tough place for monsters to live. What's puzzling is why these bastards are trying to settle in Nakwol Mountain while disguising themselves.

,You should know that it's not you who showed mercy, but us."

,Heh.... I was going to let it go... Your arrogance has finally brought death upon you."

The appearance of humans melted away, and monsters appeared that looked like they had the body of a horse and the fur of a tiger. Both were monsters of the Third Level of Essence, Qi, and Spirit.

We are the Nokchuk (鹿蜀) clan.

,That Nakwol bitch killed our parents and grandparents in the past."

The bastards' voices were beautiful enough to charm people. At the same time, they contained a sinister power.

,Judging by the fact that my body is fine, it seems to be a voice that affects the mind."

Of course, I didn't feel anything.

When I remained silent, the bastards seemed to think it was effective and continued to speak. Apart from their beautiful voices, the stench of blood was strong from their mouths. Their snouts reeked of blood from how many creatures they had devoured.

,We were planning to approach that bitch disguised as humans and ambush her."

,You ruined it all. We know Wiyu is alive."

,You have shown us mercy twice, so we will also show you mercy twice."

,Offer your right arm and get lost."

Mercy my ass. These bastards don't want to fight me and waste their strength before fighting Wiyu.

The two of them were monsters of the Initial Stage of Third Level of Essence, Qi, and Spirit, similar to my level. If they attacked together like this, it was natural that I would lose, but... I'm not an ordinary Initial Stage of Third Level of Essence, Qi, and Spirit.

Lightning Sky Style Lightning Flash.

Pajijik. Sparks flew from my body. I used lightning energy to soar into the sky. The bastards flew after me.

The bastards were reckless. As soon as they heard that I was Wiyu's disciple, their eyes turned to rage and madness.

,Their hatred for Wiyu is immense. Besides, these bastards. Their movements are clumsy compared to their energy."

Why is this happening? The answer came immediately. The stench of blood from the bastards' snouts was the clue.

,You've been focused on devouring creatures and raising your level. That's why your skills are declining. You stupid bastards."

I entered the dark clouds that filled the sky. The bastards, covered in demonic energy, didn't care and followed me into the dark clouds.
