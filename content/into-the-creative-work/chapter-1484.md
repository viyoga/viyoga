I was descending the spire.

Salote Castle was starting to get hectic.

They must have discovered the remains of the undead and the corpses of the corrupted. They hadn't figured out my existence yet. It just made them more vigilant.

I descended the stairs faster. I just needed to get the Holy Spear. Then this game would be as good as over.

,Wait a minute! Slowly, please go a little slower!"

Princess Polaris, who was following me down the stairs, said, gasping for breath. I sighed and stopped. I knew it couldn't be helped due to the difference in our physical abilities, but I couldn't help but feel frustrated.

After waiting a bit, she came down the stairs, sweating profusely. Something pale emerged behind her. It was a ghost. Without a moment's hesitation, I approached Princess Polaris and swung my sword.

,Kkyaaaaaaak!"

The ghost screamed and vanished. Thankfully, the ghost's scream only echoed inside the staircase, so it didn't escape outside.

.......

Princess Polaris's face was deathly pale.

,What's wrong? It can't be your first time seeing a ghost... Don't tell me you were scared by a scream?"

,...I thought you were going to kill me, Paladin-nim."

,You're not a corrupted one. There's no reason to kill you. Or... are you a heretic who's been messing around with demons?"

,Absolutely not! Take that back! That's an insult to me!"

,I take it back. Stop yapping and follow me. I'll slow down from now on. If you don't want to die, stay behind me."

I left the spire. I could see the corrupted and the undead moving around busily. Most of them were heading towards the entrance of Salote Castle. The corrupted were running around and shouting.

,Damn it...! The Holy Knights are swarming the entrance! You corpse bastards! Move it, move it! Why are you guys so slow when you never get tired!"

,Weapons! Prepare the weapons!"

,It's the Demon-nims' order! Defend the entrance to the death! Hold the line!"

The Holy Knights and Inquisitors outside the castle noticed the anomaly and were drawing attention. Thanks to that, I was able to move comfortably. I took Polaris to the castle's basement.

A corrupted one was taking out weapons piled up in the underground warehouse. They must be planning to arm the undead.

I hid my presence and approached the corrupted one from behind. I covered the corrupted one's mouth with my left hand and stabbed them with my sword at the same time. I cleanly dealt with one of the corrupted.

I checked on Polaris's condition. If her mental state was shaken by something like this, it would be troublesome in the future. She was calm. She looked down at the dead corrupted one with a cold gaze. Her faith was stronger than I expected. I proceeded into the basement.

The deeper I went into the basement, the more corrupted and undead I saw. I advanced while killing them. I didn't assassinate them secretly like the first corrupted one. I was already deep underground. There was no way their screams would escape to the surface.

,I'll kill them all. Each and every one of these bastards is experience points.'

I even found and killed the ones hiding in the corners.

.......

Princess Polaris's eyes, as she looked at me, were disrespectful.

,Do you have something to say?"

,...Weren't we in a hurry? I don't understand why you're wasting time unnecessarily."

,No matter how urgent it is, demons and the corrupted must be killed. They don't deserve to live in this world. Or what is it? Are you saying we should show them mercy? Are you a heretic?"

I pointed my sword at her neck. She stepped back in surprise.

,No, no! There's no way I'm a heretic! Paladin-nim is right! Demons and the corrupted are bugs that eat away at this world! They all deserve to die! Long live Heryanne Goddess-nim! Long live the Order! Long live the Paladin!"

,My apologies. I doubted your faith for a moment."

I lowered my sword. She then let out a sigh of relief.

,I'm glad you understand. ...But were you really going to kill me?"

,I kill heretics."

,...Didn't you want to undo the Holy Spear's seal?"

,I know how to undo the seal."

Princess Polaris trembled. She wrapped her arms around her trembling body and looked at me with fearful eyes.

,...If I die, my father won't let it go."

,Duke Austell doesn't even know you're here, let alone that you're dead. And... if the daughter is a heretic, there's a high chance the father is a heretic too. If you're a heretic, I'll go to your family and interrogate them later."

.......

Princess Polaris pursed her lips.

It was a wide space. All sorts of unholy energies were gathered there. In the center of the space, a spear was stuck upside down in the ground. The spear looked like a gray statue.

,W-what is that?"

Princess Polaris pointed to the area around the Holy Spear, Lottis. Bizarre shapes and human corpses were spread out as if forming a magic circle.

,Can't you tell by looking? It's the demons' trickery."

,I know that. But what purpose could they possibly have with this...."

,It's obvious. They're trying to destroy the Holy Spear. The existence of the Holy Spear in this world is annoying enough for them."

,Do you think the Holy Spear can be destroyed just by being broken? The Holy Spear can never be destroyed."

,There's no such thing as absolute in this world."

I stepped onto the magic circle.

There was an immediate backlash. The unholy energy that was pouring into the Holy Spear rushed towards me, trying to consume me.

,Holy Aura!'

A divine aura burst forth from me. The golden aura offset the dark red unholy energy. I endured the pressure pouring over my entire body and approached the Holy Spear.

I grabbed the Holy Spear. The Holy Spear, which had turned to stone, regained its original pure white appearance. But since it was still sealed, it was just an ordinary spear for now. I infused Holy Aura into the tip of the spear and swung it. The holy energy surged like a storm, sweeping away all the unholy energy.

,The Holy Aura was amplified for a moment. Even when sealed, the Holy Spear is still the Holy Spear, huh.'

I approached Princess Polaris and held out the Holy Spear.

,Undo the seal."

,Okay."

She took out a dagger from her bosom. It was something she had picked up on the way here. She gripped the blade of the dagger tightly with her left hand. Blood started to flow from her palm. She sprinkled her blood on the blade of the Holy Spear.

The Holy Spear began to shine brilliantly. The seal was undone.

,It eats blood to undo the seal.... It's more like a Demonic Spear than a Holy Spear."

,...It's a measure to prevent anyone other than our family from using the Holy Spear carelessly. Not only that, but the Holy Spear tends to be picky about who it chooses... Seeing the Holy Spear being so docile, it seems like it recognizes Paladin-nim."

,Even if it's the Holy Spear, it's still an object created with the power of the Goddess-nim. I am a Paladin who has received the Goddess-nim's revelation. Follow me. We're going up."

,What are we going to do now?"

,Open the entrance to Salote Castle. The Holy Knights and Inquisitors will come rushing in. You wait outside the castle with the Holy Knights until the battle is over."

,I will do so. As you know... the Holy Spear's power isn't infinite."

The Holy Spear, Lottis.

It's a concept of accumulating and releasing power.

Right now, Lottis has been accumulating power for a very long time. Roughly 200 years or more in terms of time. The Austell family seems to have used it little by little in the meantime, but looking at the enormous power accumulated now, it's negligible.

,There's no time to hesitate. Let's go."

I went up with her.

As soon as I arrived on the surface, the enemies' gazes turned to me. It was because of the Holy Spear. It was impossible not to notice it, emitting a brilliant light and divine power, exuding its presence.

,That, that armor is...! A Paladin! It's the Paladin of Valentier!"

,The Paladin is holding the Holy Spear!"

,Where are the Demon-nims?! Where are the Demon-nims?! Ask the Demon-nims for help!"

,Undead! Attack the Paladin quickly! Hurry!"

I raised my Holy Aura. The Holy Aura reacted with the Holy Spear. Feeling the holy power gathered on the spearhead, I swung the Holy Spear at the enemies.

A massive energy swept through the enemies. Dozens of undead and dozens of corrupted were swept away in an instant.

,Very good."

I killed all the enemies in sight and headed towards the entrance. I operated the mechanism to open the castle gate.

,The gate is opening!"

,The Paladin-nim has opened the gate!"

,Charge! Annihilate the evil ones!!"

The Holy Knights and Inquisitors shouted and rushed into the castle. They quickly pounced on the undead and the corrupted.

The undead fought to the end, and the corrupted began to flee in panic. The Inquisitors relentlessly chased after the corrupted and killed them.

I called two Holy Knights nearby.

,This is Princess Polaris, who was captured by the demons. Get out of the castle and protect the Princess."

,We obey the Paladin-nim's command!"

With this, Princess Polaris's safety was secured. It was as good as owing a debt to the Austell family. I gripped the Holy Spear tightly and headed towards the center of the castle. I ordered the Holy Knights to deal with the enemies scattered throughout the castle.

The corridor leading to the Grand Hall was filled with blood. It was proof that the Great Demon resurrection ritual was proceeding successfully. I was relieved.

,I thought I came too early... but thankfully.'

My goal was to kill the Great Demon. In order to kill the Great Demon, the Great Demon had to be resurrected first.

,The ritual must succeed.'

Everything was going according to my plan.

I arrived at the entrance to the Grand Hall. I could feel a foul and evil energy emanating from the door. I put strength into my hand and opened the large door.

It was a damp place. In the center, an altar was erected, and it was surrounded by human corpses. More than ten demons were gathered around the altar. The demons glanced at me and then focused on the ritual. One of the demons climbed onto the altar himself.

,Suton-isiyeo! I offer this being to you! Aaaaaaaaargh!"

The demon pulled out his own heart and placed it on the altar. The demon's heart and the demon's body burst into flames. The demons around the altar bowed their heads and began to mutter something.

,Stop this bullshit right now! You bug bastards!"

I shouted, spreading my Holy Aura. The demons flinched, but they didn't stop the ritual.

,Oh dear. We have an uninvited guest. As you can see, those people are very busy proceeding with the ritual right now. Paladin of Valentier, I will be your opponent."

A man wearing a black robe approached me. He was holding a staff decorated with a skull.

,Babaroyaks! A demon who betrayed humanity! How dare you show your face in front of me!"

,Betrayal? I have never been on humanity's side. It's troublesome if you think that all humans believe in Heryanne Goddess-nim. Of course, I'm not on the same side either."

,You sold your soul to a demon and you're still so brazen. Don't you know shame?"

,I didn't sell my soul. I evolved."

,Evolved? How laughable. That's just corruption. And the end of corruption is only death. I will kill you and then kill all the demons behind you."

,I see that words don't work. Paladin, I will kill you to celebrate Suton-nim's resurrection."

Ttaak.

Babaroyaks tapped the floor with his staff.

In an instant, the space where I and Babaroyaks were shook.

The scenery changed.

Red sky, black land.

Babaroyaks smiled at me.

,Welcome to the Plains of Death."

The ground rumbled and thousands of undead began to rise.
