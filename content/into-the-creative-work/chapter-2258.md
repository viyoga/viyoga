I took the Lerytree and met Yuria. She was with the maids, including Melissa.

I took out the Lerytree and handed it to Yuria. Yuria received the blue peach with a slightly surprised expression.

,Lerytree. To think it actually exists. The rumors were true."

Next to her, who was purely surprised, Melissa widened her eyes.

,So it really exists... It's not like they painted a peach, right? How the hell did you get the legendary fruit?"

There was nothing to hide. I briefly explained the background of how I obtained the Lerytree.

Yuria accepted my words without any fuss. In contrast, Melissa was shocked and opened her mouth.

,You said you haven't been in the mansion lately... You dominated the Eastern Continent? Is that even allowed? Why don't you just settle down there?"

,I have no intention of playing king."

If I wanted to be a king, I would have taken the throne of the Rapelli Kingdom as well.

Even if I became the king of Kairak, there wouldn't be much I'd have to do. Most of the underlings take care of it themselves. There are clear disadvantages to that, but I wouldn't have to deal with annoying tasks.

After destroying and absorbing the Thorn Kingdom, my plan was to step back appropriately and exploit them.

The development of Kairak? No way. I can exploit them more easily if Kairak doesn't develop.

Melissa shook her head after hearing my plan.

,You're trying to suck a continent dry. How pathetic."

,Master-nim will achieve the purpose you desire. Because you are Master-nim."

Yuria cheered me on.

,As expected, Yuria understands me right away.'

A maid brought a tray and a fruit knife. My head became complicated when I saw Melissa directly holding the knife and the Lerytree.

,Can I just feed it to her like this? There's no guarantee it's a Lerytree, right? What if that old man played a trick? What if there's poison inside?'

There was a way to check if that Lerytree was real or fake...

,Wait. Stop. I haven't checked it yet."

,Are you worried about poison? Poison doesn't work on Yuria, though. She's such a toxic woman that poison becomes meaningless."

,Melissa. Your words are harsh. I'm not toxic. It's just that I've reached the realm of immunity to all poisons."

,And that's a realm you reached by handling all sorts of poisons. Master-nim. Get rid of your useless worries. There's no one in this world more knowledgeable about poison than Yuria."

I felt relieved hearing those words. As I quietly nodded, Melissa peeled the blue peach. The blue peach was blue even on the inside. The smell was that of a normal peach.

,Is it the influence of the color? It doesn't look appetizing.'

The peach, cut for easy eating, was placed in front of Yuria. Yuria took the peach with a fork and put it in her mouth. I heard a crisp chewing sound.

,Yuria. How does it taste? Is it good?"

,It's a legendary peach. There's no way it wouldn't taste good."

I wanted to take a bite myself, but to see the effects of the Lerytree, the pregnant woman had to eat all the fruit.

,The taste is... that of a normal peach. There's no poison or anything. I'm not sure if it's even an elixir."

Yuria has eaten all sorts of elixirs until now. She, who could be called an elixir expert, doesn't know if it's an elixir?

,Was I scammed?'

It was when a sense of unease was creeping up.

Yuria ate all the peach, and an anomaly occurred. Her entire body began to glow faintly.

,Yuria?!"

,Are you okay?!"

Melissa and I approached Yuria. Yuria checked her body and said calmly.

,I'm fine. This is more like evidence of a contract than an elixir."

The faint light gathered towards the center of Yuria and diminished. As if being absorbed into her pregnant belly.

,Evidence of a contract? What exactly do you mean?"

,I received the blessing of a spirit. It bestows a blessing upon the pregnant woman who eats the Lerytree and her child. It's a very old spirit contract."

,If it's a contract, there must be a price. I feel like I've been scammed."

,The price was paid long ago. There is no price for me to pay. It will be a good gift for this child."

Yuria stroked her pregnant belly.

,Can you tell what abilities the child will gain when they're born?"

,I'm sorry. I don't know that far."

It was obvious since they hadn't even been born yet.

,The due date is less than a week away. You know that, right?"

Melissa asked. It was a scolding to stay put since the due date was near.

,Of course I know. My schedule is completely empty on that day. Even if the Eastern Continent is destroyed, I'll be by Yuria's side, so don't worry."

,...If it's that serious, shouldn't you be in the Eastern Continent?"

,Yuria and my daughter are more important than the Eastern Continent."

,Ah, Master-nim...!"

Yuria had a moved expression. Looking at her smile, I recalled a concern I had been putting off.

,What should I name the daughter who will be born? She's a child I acknowledge, so I can't just give her a random name.'

I had no choice but to keep worrying about this issue.

,I must see the end of this war. Gather all the warriors. I will personally lead the army to the front lines."

I declared as the War King of Kairak.

There were several problems. Gathering soldiers alone required time.

,Your Highness. The harvest season is less than a month away. We need farmers to do the farming. Women alone can ruin the farming."

,It doesn't matter if the farming is ruined. I'll take responsibility. Endure as much as possible."

,Your Highness. We are short on military rations. The front lines are requesting military rations from all over."

,I will send combat rations. As long as I am here, there will be no shortage of military rations."

,Your Highness. We have caught deserters. The number of deserters is increasing. We need a solution."

,Apply the system of guilt by association and execute them by unit. Those who fear battle deserve to die."

I led the army and arrived at one of the front lines. The Great Warrior there welcomed me. He was a bald warrior with a rough face. He was an Aura Master. In Kairak, only Aura Masters are called Great Warriors.

,I greet the new War King. I am Great Warrior Chairun."

I wondered if he would resist, but he lowered his head on his own. It didn't seem like he would start a rebellion right away.

,I heard you're short on military rations? I brought military rations myself. Feed the warriors well."

,Thank you. We can die fighting instead of starving to death. The warriors will be pleased."

,Instead of thinking about dying honorably, you should be thinking about fighting and winning. What's the situation?"

,The resistance of the Thorn bastards is great. We have attacked that damn fortress three times, but all have been blocked, and we are continuing the confrontation."

I looked at the fortress far away. Located in the center of a rocky valley. The fortress, situated in an exquisite location, looked sturdy enough to block anything.

,It seems like we can bypass it?"

,To target the capital of the Thorn Kingdom, we must capture that fortress. The detour is long, and there are many monsters. Supply will be several times more difficult."

I looked around.

The warriors, who were able to fill their bellies for the first time in a while, had smiles on their faces. But I, who had experienced war several times, couldn't be fooled. The warriors' morale was at rock bottom. A gloomy atmosphere was ingrained and wouldn't disappear.

,The warriors' morale is low."

,It's because of repeated defeats. Just once. If we win just once, morale will rise like crazy."

,Well. It doesn't seem like defeat is the only reason. Chairun. Tell me honestly. What are the warriors dissatisfied with?"

,The previous War King restricted destruction and looting. He ordered not to harm children, women, and the elderly as much as possible."

,Why?"

,He said they would also become Kairak."

,He was trying to take Thorn as intact as possible. The previous War King is dead, so a new policy is needed. I allow unlimited looting. However, the beauties are mine. If you discover a beauty, capture her gently and offer her to me. If there is anyone who ignores my orders and desires a beauty... I will kill them by unit."

Strictly speaking, it wasn't unlimited looting, but I couldn't let the beauties be raped. Because I have to take them.

Great Warrior Chairun trembled slightly as he looked at my fierce eyes.

,It will be as the War King wishes. When will the offensive begin?"

,I want to say in 1 hour, but unfortunately, I have something important to do. Maintain the state of confrontation for the time being."

I went into my personal military tent and tore a space movement scroll.

Yuria's due date was today. War wasn't that important.

Entering the mansion, it was generally bustling. The maids were clearly nervous, even though they weren't the ones giving birth. The fact that my child is being born isn't a special event in itself. What's special is that she's being born from Yuria. In the future, that child will be my successor.

I met Yuria. She was quite nervous as she prepared for childbirth.

I waited outside the delivery room. There was nothing I could do even if I was inside the delivery room. Rather, my presence would make others feel burdened. More than 10 maids, who could be called childbirth experts, were gathered, so I just had to trust them and wait. I could rest assured because Melissa was by Yuria's side.

I sat in front of a chair and passed the time.

It was a strange feeling. Until now, I hadn't felt much even when my children were born, but I was quite nervous that Yuria was giving birth.

,That must mean Yuria is that special to me.'

It was when I was starting to get impatient.

,Uwaaaaaaaah!"

I heard a loud baby's cry and the cheers of the maids.

I couldn't stand it.

I jumped up from the chair, opened the door, and went inside.

The midwife was holding a bloody infant. Yuria was lying on the bed with an exhausted look. She had a smile on her face. The mother seemed healthy.

,Master-nim. A young lady has been born."

The midwife carefully handed me the bloody infant. I received the child with both hands.

Once I received her, there wasn't much to say. I blankly looked at my daughter and said.

,I am your father. I will do whatever you want."

The child cried. Her cry was strong, so she seemed healthy.

,Your name is Yuna Prucus."
