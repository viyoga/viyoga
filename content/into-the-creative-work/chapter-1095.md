,What are you doing? Focus. Mana backflow is important to clean up. If you don't handle it cleanly, there will be aftereffects on the mana road."

A man, presumably a doctor, said irritably.

I put my confused mind behind me. There's nothing wrong with what he said. It's a setting that came out in the game too.

,Mana backflow must be treated in time so you don't suffer later. The urgent thing is not my memory, but my body right now.'

I remember the mana breathing technique I learned a few years ago.

,That breathing technique won't work. You'll lose more than 70% of the mana in your body.'

This was also a small stroke of luck. It was too good to give up 70%. This kind of luck may never come again.

Then, my mind flashed. Knowledge about mana breathing comes to mind as scattered pieces are put together on their own.

,If I slightly modify the breathing technique, I think I can lower the loss rate to 30%....'

The mana breathing technique, Raytal style, is complete in itself. Even if it is only a 2nd grade mana breathing technique, an irreversible situation will occur if you rashly touch it. Ironically, I could have died as soon as I entered the game.

,...But it's hard to ignore these things that come to mind. You're giving me the answer, but you're telling me to ignore it?'

The more I pondered, the more confident I became in my knowledge. I decided to accept this knowledge.

I opened the Astral, called the mind of a wizard. The mana road in my body becomes clear. Mana flows through the mana road like a train speeding along the rails. A total of 3 mana roads are connected to the Astral. It is the average for a 1st class wizard.

,Not enough. There's too much mana for 3 mana roads to handle.'

I pondered and tried to expand the Astral. I was going to stop immediately if something went wrong, but strangely enough, I instinctively felt how far my Astral's limit was.

,It's impossible to expand the Astral enough to move up to 2nd grade right now.'

My Astral was currently empty. In game terms, it means I have no experience. Whether it's a magic formula or combat experience, I needed something to fill the Astral. Of course, my body is not ready either.

Two mana roads extended from the Astral. All 5 mana roads followed my will. The mana that was full in the ship began to circulate as it was organized. I watched the circulation route. I was going to take immediate action if a problem occurred because I had slightly changed it from the original circulation route. But there was no problem. The 5 mana roads were stable.

I opened my eyes.

The doctor looked at me with emotionless eyes.

,The mana is stable. You seem to be fine, so go back."

,Yes. Thank you."

I gave a greeting that contained not a bit of sincerity. I instinctively know that it's not good to provoke the doctor's displeasure.

The doctor waved his hand as if he was annoyed. I got out of bed, put on my shoes, and went out of the infirmary.

Outside the door was a hallway.

Gray concrete walls and floors stretched out. Memories come to mind. I headed to the right. I organized my memories as I walked.

,I am Seong Yujin. An F-class Hunter of South Korea. I am 21 years old. I don't have a girlfriend. I don't have any abilities as a Hunter. I'm a special case where only my physical abilities are awakened without any abilities.'

I frowned as I recalled my memories. I remember the names of the university and high school, but I don't remember anything that happened there. I don't know my parents' faces either. My memory is like a net with holes all over it.

On the other hand, the memories of Room 211 and the Dark Moon game are clear.

,I was born to slave parents. Naturally, I became a slave. After my parents died, the owner sold me to the Prisel Kingdom. I grew up without any shortages until I was 10 years old. The kingdom provided food, clothing, and shelter. The problem is that the purpose is because of the Battle Mage Project.'

Battle Mage Project.

It is the Prisel Kingdom's plan to secretly gather children with 1st class mana affinity and train them as Battle Mages.

It's called training, but it's actually closer to an experiment. To become a Battle Mage, you need a talent of 3rd class or higher in mana affinity.

The reality of the project is an experiment to forcibly raise 1st class mana affinity to 3rd class mana affinity.

,And this experiment has already succeeded. The problem is that the success rate is about 5%.'

The Prisel Kingdom is satisfied with the results of the project and continues to carry it out. If 5 out of 100 children with 1st class mana affinity talent become Battle Mages, it is beneficial enough.

,211. It's the name given to me, but... do you think I'll accept a name like that? My name is Yujin.'

The organization of memories was almost finished. There were no great memories in the life of 211.

,My ego is Seong Yujin. The memories of 211 feel like I'm reading something.'

I saw a glass window in the hallway. Seong Yujin's face is reflected in the glass beyond the iron bars. Black hair, black eyes. Except for being a little young, it was definitely me, Seong Yujin.

I turned away from the window and headed to the barracks in my memory.

Barracks No. 11.

When I opened the heavy-looking door, I found a place similar to the old Korean military barracks. It wasn't strange. The place where the Battle Mage Project was being carried out was the Prisel Kingdom Army.

There were 17 children of my age in the barracks. A total of 18 including me. It was a mixed gender. 8 are girls and 10 are boys. In this world, human rights are a mess, but gender is surprisingly equal.

,Everyone's here.'

Two people died two months ago because they couldn't beat the goblin in a 1-on-1 battle.

The children are doing their own things. Some are giggling with close friends, some are sleeping, and some are reading books.

,211. I heard you fainted from mana backflow while practicing mana breathing? Are you okay?"

A skinny boy smiled and spoke. No. 205. He is the head of the 11th barracks and the squad leader. He had a high sense of responsibility as a squad leader. In my memory, he was a good guy, although he was a bit nosy.

,Oh, I'm fine."

,If you weren't okay, you would have been disposed of."

I heard a sarcastic voice.

A sturdy boy sitting leaning against the locker said. No. 202. His left eye is cross-eyed. Unlike No. 205, his personality was not good.

,Well, if it were you, you would have been disposed of due to mana backflow. You're not good at handling mana."

,...Are you trying to fight me now?"

,Try it."

I laughed at No. 202. He can't do anything anyway. If he causes an accident here, only annoying torture awaits him.

Knowing that, No. 205 didn't stop us either.

,Tsk. I'll see you later."

No. 202 clicked his tongue and then lay down on the floor and closed his eyes.

I shrugged my shoulders once and went to my seat. I like it because it's next to the wall.

,What happened?"

A woman sitting next to me spoke. She was a woman with short brown hair. No. 212. She was an ordinary woman. I reflexively scanned her body and then fixed my gaze on her eyes.

,What?"

,Mana backflow. No. 177 died from internal organs bursting due to mana backflow."

I remember. It was a guy who died about 3 weeks ago from mana backflow because he couldn't withstand the mana essence injected into his body.

,No. 177 and I are different. I only had mana backflow while practicing mana breathing."

,The fact that mana backflowed is the same."

,The situation is different. As you know, I'm sluggish. It hasn't been long since I became a 1st class wizard. There wasn't much mana that backflowed. You could say I was lucky."

I was really lucky.

The mana that filled my body through mana backflow was at a level that I could handle, and the doctor did nothing. If a quack doctor touched me carelessly, the situation could have gotten worse.

,Is it really all luck? Is there something... like a special secret technique?"

No. 212's eyes sparkle. Was this her real purpose? I let out a hollow laugh.

,There's no way a slave would have something like that."

,I heard No. 31 has a secret magic?"

,...That's because he's special. There's a rumor that he's from a noble family."

No. 31.

There is a rumor that he is the illegitimate child of a noble. The managers of the Battle Project also showed difficulty when dealing with No. 31. He is receiving special treatment in many ways.

,Were you really lucky? I'm glad you're safe. Congratulations."

No. 212 said with a smile. Her mouth is smiling, but the corners of her eyes are fluttering. Her voice is also quite fake.

,I wouldn't have acted like that.'

I decided to ignore her and focused on my complicated mind. I needed to organize information about the game Dark Moon.

After 10 minutes, a heavy male voice came from the speaker on the ceiling.

,We will start the evening roll call in 5 minutes."

I woke up from my thoughts and smiled bitterly. I was a slave, a soldier, and a test subject. As 3 similar words decorated me, I felt sad for no reason.

After the familiar evening roll call was over, I lay down on the bed. I closed my eyes, but I couldn't fall asleep. As the surroundings became quiet, I realized the reality I was in now.

,......What should I do from now on? If I open my eyes, will I go back to reality?'

That's not likely.

I have confirmed several times that this place is not a dream.

I don't know why I came into the game, but I have to live in this world. Even though this world is fucked up, it's not to the point where I can't live.

,I don't know about anything else, but the information about Dark Moon comes to mind clearly. I know that there are no players in my memory.'

Dark Moon allows players to choose their starting point. The fact that I'm not here means that the player itself doesn't exist, or it could be somewhere else. I think it's the former. Because there's a possibility that I'm the player.

,Dark Moon players could freely develop by taking stats and skill trees. Then maybe....'

I muttered in my heart.

,Status window.'

Nothing happened.

,......Status window."

Maybe I have to say it out loud. I muttered in a very small voice.

Nothing happened.

Swallowing the sigh that was about to burst out, I eliminated unnecessary thoughts and thought about what to do in the future.

,I don't have any intention of staying in the military. I'll escape when I get the chance later. I want freedom. I don't want to live as a slave for the rest of my life.'

I touched my right forearm.

A strange sentence made up of 7 triangles is drawn.

It is the Prisel Kingdom's high-level slave seal.

This seal can be used to easily kill slaves and track down escaped slaves.

,The fact that my life is in the hands of others.... It's terrible.'

To get out of here, I have to get rid of this seal first.

Several methods come to mind in my head. In less than 3 minutes, 5 methods came to mind.

,Was my memory this good?'

Even at this moment, a 6th method comes to mind. Judging the probability of success is later. First, I come up with a method, eliminate those with a low probability of success, and make a plan.

I fell asleep after midnight.
