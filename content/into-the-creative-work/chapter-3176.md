"Master. To wage war, we must prepare supplies. Food is especially essential. An army without food cannot fight."

The beauty with half her face hidden behind golden hair, Human Commander Alexandra, spoke.

I knew well enough the importance of supplies. And there were plenty of ways to solve it.

'I can just bring supplies from another world. The problem is whether the System will acknowledge that solution.'

If it's about enough food for a hundred people, the System would probably let it slide. But what if that food is for ten thousand? What if it's an amount that could wreck Atlantis's economy?

'The System will obviously ask about the source.'

I can't explain Pleasure Life to the System. Nor do I want to.

'I've sold food brought from reality, or used it myself. The System didn't particularly stop me. But that's probably because the scale was small. Dominating a single district is far more profitable than selling a few dozen modern items. But once the scale grows... the story changes.'

Once it reaches a scale that shakes the economy of the Atlantis continent, the System won't sit idle. The very reason for the System's existence is to maintain balance.

'The System is one thing, but the Divine Thrones won't stay quiet either. They'll kick up all sorts of fuss about special treatment. The sight of Divine Thrones flooding the System with complaints is already forming in my head.'

The number of Divine Thrones who'd take my side is only a handful. In Atlantis, Divine Thrones are fundamentally competitors.

"Prada. How much is one meal?"

"It depends on the food. From a minimum of 1 AP gruel to a full course of top-grade cuisine exceeding 100 AP. Combat rations suitable for soldiers are 3 AP."

"For a hundred thousand, that's 300,000 AP per meal. 900,000 AP a day. Nine million AP over ten days. What a headache."

"There is also the option of reducing it to two meals a day."

"With two meals a day, the soldiers' combat strength will drop. If morale falls too low, some will desert or commit mutiny. On top of that, demons basically eat more than humans."

"Most demons are larger than humans, after all."

An army is a money-devouring hippo. Setting everything else aside and calculating just food alone comes to this much. Factor in other necessities and war equipment, and...

'Even by rough calculation, 40 million AP is nowhere near enough.'

Of course, an army of a hundred thousand is still a distant future matter, but as I conquer Hell's districts, the size of my forces will undoubtedly swell fast.

"I'll need to secure food through support."

Prada wiped the commercial smile off her face.

"Customer. We are not a charity organization. We do not provide support to anyone for free."

"I'm not talking about you."

The Surface.

I'll use the power of the Heavenly Demon Cult. The time has come to spend the money I'd gathered during the Volcano Island incident. Of course, I have no intention of shouldering that money alone.

The Emperor.

Under the pretext of conquering Hell's districts, I'll squeeze the Emperor and the Empire's nobles. The nobles won't refuse, since by supporting some food they get to claim a share of the credit for conquering Hell's districts.

"Prada. Go back today. I have no intention of buying anything else."

"Understood. I'll give you this as a gift."

Prada held out a marble.

「Hell Merchant Emergency Summoning Marble.

Breaking the marble summons the nearest Hell Merchant.

Rank: B」

"You're giving these for free?"

"Rather than free, it's a kind of investment. You are a VIP, after all, Eugene."

"Right. I'll call when I need you."

"I'll take my leave now."

Prada bowed politely and left the mansion.

Seven mercenaries remained in the room. Beautiful mercenaries, each flaunting a different charm.

"Is there any among you who wishes to become my woman?"

“…….”

I felt a bit slighted. Honestly, with my looks, shouldn't they fall for me at first sight?

'They're not slaves, so I can't just force myself on them either. Tch.'

My gaze turned to Alexandra, who was staring holes into the Hell district map. She had a golden bob with a side part, hiding the left side of her face with hair. From what I saw earlier, there was no scar on the left side of her face either.

"Alexandra. Are you from Atlantis by any chance?"

"...No. I'm from PKM 1177 Earth."

This world had countless Earths. It wasn't a parallel world concept. Each Earth had different environments, races, civilization levels, and so on.

"How did you end up a mercenary? Or can't you say?"

"...I was the princess and general of a kingdom at war. The kingdom was on the verge of defeat for lack of supplies. Defeat meant death, so I made a deal with the Demon of Greed in exchange for supplies. The kingdom won the war, but we couldn't repay the debt and fell to Hell, where my existence was mortgaged to the Demon of Greed."

Hearing Alexandra's story, the picture became clear.

"You're all from different Earths, aren't you? Each of you made a contract with that bastard for different reasons."

"...That's right."

"And you're mercenaries rather than slaves because you have skills?"

This time it was Helvis who answered, not Alexandra.

"Correct. You're surprisingly sharp, Employer. No—Master."

One might wonder if a single demon could pull that off, but Mammon is no ordinary demon. He's a Demon King-class fiend representing the desire of 'Greed.'

"Helvis. How long do you work as a mercenary?"

"Until the debt is fully repaid. Until then, even dying isn't permitted."

"Earlier I heard that to free a slave, you'd need to pay a thousand times the subscription fee in a lump sum. Does the same apply to you?"

"Correct. It's practically impossible. Expensive mercenaries like us don't get hired often. At best, if we're lucky, we get dragged into Father's army and earn merit. Well, realistically that won't happen. Father hates spending money, so he won't go to war unless absolutely necessary."

"A thousand times 300,000 AP is... 300 million AP. Helvis. If I pay 300 million AP... would you be willing to become my woman?"

Helvis's lips curled up dangerously.

"Of course, Master. If you repay my debt on my behalf... I'll be your woman until the day you die, Master."

"Do the rest of you share the same opinion?"

"...If you repay the debt."

"It's far better than spending hundreds or thousands more years in that place."

They seemed to share Helvis's opinion.

Mammon's Hell seemed to be a terrible place. Well, is there any Hell that isn't terrible?

I calculated quietly.

The monthly subscription fee for seven mercenaries is 1.09 million. A thousand times that is 1.09 billion. 1.1 billion AP.

'I receive 43 million AP per month... so roughly two years will do it. The variable here is that my income can increase further as I conquer Hell's districts.'

The calculation checked out.

"I'll free you from Mammon. In exchange, become my women."

I could get beauties of this caliber for a mere 1.1 billion AP? Absolute steal.

Helvis pushed her glasses up with a finger and spoke.

"Master. That offer is nothing short of salvation to us. However, I doubt whether you have the ability to pay 1.09 billion AP. If you prove your capability... we'll write the contract. We'll gladly become your women."

The other mercenaries nodded.

"Kukuku. In three days, 40 million AP will come in. If I show you that, will you believe me?"

"I'd like to believe Master's words."
