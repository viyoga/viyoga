Meteor swords pour towards the enemies through the cracks in the split space. Just because there are 30 launch ports doesn't mean only 30 are fired. A meteor sword is fired from each launch port every 0.5 seconds without stopping. If I could use mana, I could increase the number of launch ports, speed, and power.

Leaving a trail of blue mana light in the air, they cause an electric explosion the moment they hit their target. An electric shock and a shockwave explode simultaneously.

Enough power to instantly kill an ordinary person.

But the A-rank Hunters in front of me are elites who have completed all preparations. They minimize damage by covering their entire bodies with mana. They also wore expensive equipment all over their bodies as Suwol Guild members. And the speed of the meteor swords being fired isn't at a level that an A-rank Hunter can't avoid if they concentrate.

,Their resistance to electricity is excellent. Is it obvious since they came to kill me?'

The fact that I'm an ability user who handles electricity is already famous.

,But lightning isn't the only attribute that can be given to meteor swords.'

I mix and pour out meteor swords with fire, water, ice, and wind attributes.

Pung, pung, pung, pung, pung, pung, pung, pung!

Thanks to that, the area around the beach was devastated, and sand dust was carried away by the wind.

"Kuaaaaaack!"

"Damn it! How long are you going to keep pouring this out?!"

"There are too many! I'm sure your mana was sealed!!"

Those who approached me screamed as the damage accumulated one by one.

Unfortunately for them, I didn't even use 10% of the meteor swords I had. I watched their struggles with my arms crossed and a smile.

,My stamina is draining quite a bit. Even though I'm standing still, it feels like I'm running at full speed.'

Still, I could last at least 15 minutes. The problem is that the enemies can barely last 3 minutes, let alone 15 minutes. They can't even approach because of the pouring meteor swords.

If they block or parry the flying meteor swords, an explosion occurs immediately, causing damage.

So, they avoid it? Unless they avoid it from a distance, the meteor swords collide with the ground and explode. Explosion. The damage accumulates little by little.

It's even hard to avoid. This bombardment that I lead is so accurate due to the influence of the shooting trait.

Pung, pung, pung, pung, pung, pung, pung!

Covering the body with mana requires concentration. If they lose focus even a little, the mana covering their bodies disappears. Above all, using mana to cover the body consumes too much mana. The efficiency is not good.

Their desperate long-range attacks? I just twist the direction of one or two launch ports and intercept them with meteor swords. Even if I don't hit the meteor swords perfectly, I can explode them at my will.

"Aaaaaaaack!"

In the end, the A-rank Hunters collapse.

"How boring. Can't you even come close?"

"Damn it! Everyone gather here! I'll open the way! I'll create an opening, so approach him and kill him!"

The most senior guy among the group shouted. I know his face, but I don't know his name. Since he's going to die anyway, I didn't even want to know.

A-rank Hunters gather around him. But only half of them were left, so there were only 5. I was a little surprised that Jang Gojun was among them. But that's it. I openly laughed at them.

"Gather? You didn't think that the firepower would be concentrated? Or is it that? United we stand, divided we fall? In front of a bomb, everyone dies when they're united."

The meteor swords concentrated on them fly away. I deliberately fired 30 at a time in syncopation.

"Haaaah!"

Daggers floated around the guy who shouted.

,It's not an ability, but an artifact.'

One dagger flies and collides with a meteor sword. The meteor sword explodes from the impact. The meteor swords flying behind are caught in the explosion and explode in succession. The meteor swords that were fired in succession became poison instead.

The meteor sword chain explosion reaches near me. I quickly stopped firing for a moment. The chain explosion didn't reach near me because of that.

I fired the meteor swords again, but the guys were already approaching me. The guy throws the dagger again to intercept the meteor sword. The trajectory of the meteor sword was twisted, but the dagger also twisted its trajectory and eventually collided with the meteor sword.

Pung!

An explosion occurs in the air.

The meteor swords explode in a chain reaction again because of that.

"Those swords explode even with weak attacks! If you intercept just one properly, you can neutralize dozens! Your attacks won't work anymore!!"

"Do you think you can block my attacks forever with only 3 daggers left?"

"3 is enough to approach you...!"

I fire the meteor swords again.

This time, the guy laughed at me. He might think I don't have the ability to learn. Or he might think I can only do this.

The guy threw the dagger this time too. The falling sword and dagger collide.

Kkang!

The sword doesn't explode and bounces off, spinning and flying towards me. I didn't even twitch an eyebrow. The sword disappeared after being reverse summoned before it touched my body.

"It doesn't explode...?!"

"I can fire more than just one type."

Stormbreaker, Hwaryeon Bido, and Vajra Trust, were fired at the guy from the launch port.

The Vajra Trust, which had been charged in advance, pierced the guy's chest first, shining with golden light. Following that, the spear-shaped Stormbreaker and Hwaryeon Bido were stuck in the guy's body. The dying guy falls to the ground.

"Seong Yujin!"

"Die!!"

They wield their weapons, approaching right in front of me, each holding a spear and a sword.

Of course, I knew.

Hubalseonji (Arriving Late but Ahead).

They say it's the ultimate principle in martial arts.

There was a simple way to achieve that.

Just be stronger than the opponent.

I was faster, stronger, and technically superior to those guys. My physical abilities exceeded the average of S-rank. The guys in front of me used mana, but it's a little lacking to reach the body of an S-rank.

I dodged the sword and grabbed the spear shaft. I used force to roll the spear and take it away, then stabbed the spear into the head of the guy holding the sword. Uuung!

"Uaaaaaaaah!"

"Disappointing for an A-rank."

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Byeokgye (碧溪).

Jang Gojun changes the sticks he was holding in both hands into swords. Shape transformation. The ability to change the shape of objects.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Jilpungsinroe (疾風迅雷).

"Aaaaaaaaack!"
