,Wait a minute! Stop for a second!

As I headed towards the school's main gate, Yang Su-bin grabbed my arm. I looked at her with a puzzled expression.

,Why? Weren't you in a hurry?

,I am. I'm in a hurry, but we can't just go like this, just the two of us. The outside is no different from hell right now. What about the others?

,Other people? It's just us. You and I together make up 40% of the total force. 40% is being used to save your boyfriend.

I had to make it sound like a big deal at times like this. That way, she'd be even more grateful.

Contrary to my expectations, Yang Su-bin didn't seem grateful at all; her face turned pale.

,The two of us together make up 40% of the total force?

,No, to be exact, it's 90%. You're about 1%, and I'm 89%.

The remaining 10% is Na Chae-yeong. After getting the war AI, she's also preparing for battle in her own way. The reason I can briefly leave the elementary school like this is because I trust Na Chae-yeong. Na Chae-yeong should be able to hold out for a few hours without me.

,...There aren't that many people in this vast facility?

,I couldn't trust anyone. It would have been a hassle if I had brought in people from the beginning and they turned into zombies. We didn't have much time either. So I invested in the facility instead of people.

Either way, people will gather. It's safe here, and there are plenty of resources. It's only natural for people to seek safe places.

Then I realized that Yang Su-bin and Park Hae-gil-ssi had been misunderstanding all along. Seongak Elementary School. It's too big a facility to occupy and use with just two people. They must have thought there would be at least dozens of people.

In reality, there were so few people that even facility management wasn't being done properly.

,So, are you not going?

,......

Yang Su-bin lowered her head. She even offered her body to me to save Park Hae-gil-ssi. There's no way she could choose not to go now.

,Just follow me. You seem to think this is just a simple zombie virus outbreak... but in reality, it's a bit more complicated.

The main gate and back gate had thick steel hammered into them to create the gates. There was a camera installed on the upper part of the steel gate, and there was a small reinforced glass window next to the gate to see the outside situation.

,We can't do it this way.

,What now?

,Hae-gil-ssi told you about a route where he can move relatively safely, right?

,Now you want to go the way that bastard told you? If we go that way, we'll be going around in circles. It's too slow.

,I followed your opinion because I thought you would have a plan. Hae-gil-ssi told me to do that too. But now you're trying to go on the main road. That's crazy.

,I... Ugh, never mind. I'll show you, so just follow me.

I stood in front of the steel gate. There was no way to open the steel gate from the inside. It was an electronic lock, so you needed the controller's permission to open the steel gate. The controller right now is Na Chae-yeong.

,Dr. Na. Are you watching? Open the door.

-Be back before 3 PM.

Na Chae-yeong's voice came from the small wireless device I had plugged into my left ear.

,I'll be back before lunch. I can't miss the lunch special from the Michelin three-star chef.

-If you see anyone on the road who has nowhere to go, you can bring them back. We need manpower.

Because the building was so big, there were many things to do. Cleaning, for example. And organizing logistics, and so on. I was also thinking of farming, so I had prepared soil, fertilizer, and seeds. It's unsettling to rely solely on the food we've stockpiled. And on one side, I had also created a livestock pen to raise chickens. And I had no intention of managing the livestock at all.

,Okay.

Click!

The steel gate opens.

Asphalt road. And cars scattered haphazardly. Among them were zombies slowly wandering around.

,Heup...!

Yang Su-bin gasped from behind. She looked at the wandering zombies with fear in her eyes.

,There are fewer than I thought.

-Originally, this neighborhood has a small population for Seoul.

Zombies don't look much different from humans on the outside. However, if they vomit blood when they die due to the D virus, have blood on their bodies, and their eyes are messed up, they are 100% zombies.

Humans wouldn't shamble around like that in the first place.

,They're like stray dogs looking for food.

-Cars are valuable resources. Whether it's iron or oil, there's almost nothing to throw away. Don't break them unnecessarily.

,I know that much.

Sreureung.

I drew the cutlass sword from its scabbard. The cutlass sword was already glowing silver because it had been charged with electricity.

I grabbed the sword and ran towards the nearest zombie. Tatatat! I deliberately made noise, and the zombies nearby all turned their heads towards me and charged. Zombies are generally slightly stronger than ordinary adult men.

,But they can't be my match.

Their actions were simple. They were obviously just trying to approach. They didn't care at all what kind of weapon I had. Zombies were like wild boars.

I swung the cutlass sword. A slash that didn't use lightning, but simply used the sharpness of the sword and my own strength.

Seogeok!

I beheaded the zombie in the front.

Seogeok! Seogeok!

I continued to behead the two zombies that were rushing in one after another. Blood spurted from the severed necks. Nevertheless, the zombies didn't stop.

,Why aren't they dying?

I took a step back and climbed onto a car. The headless zombies missed me and flailed their arms around haphazardly, starting to tangle with each other.

-I told you before. Aim for the heart to kill zombies for sure!

,Ah, you did.

Zombies have beating hearts. But their brains are brain-dead. They're half alive and half dead. Of course, there's a 0% chance of turning a zombie back into its original human form.

Zombies have become different creatures from humans due to the D virus.

The zombies with their heads cut off thrashed around for about 3 minutes before collapsing. They were dead.

-There's a zombie in a nearby car. Take care of it while you're at it.

,It's not a difficult task.

I walked towards the car making thumping noises. The zombie sitting in the driver's seat was banging on the window and steering wheel. On closer inspection, the seatbelt was restraining him. Even though he had become stronger than an adult man, he wasn't strong enough to break a car with his bare hands.

I thrust the silver-glowing cutlass sword towards the car. Pook! It easily pierced the car's armor and stabbed the zombie's heart. The zombie's body slumped. He died instantly.

In addition, if I saw any zombies wandering around, I went and sliced them without asking any questions.

-If possible, stab them in the chest to kill them in one go. It's easier to clean up that way.

,It's okay. Other guys will do the cleaning.

Cutting felt better than stabbing.

After I sliced all the zombies I could see around me, I looked at Yang Su-bin. Yang Su-bin was looking at me with awe in her eyes.

,...I misjudged you. What do you do?

,I used to be a college student. Well, I haven't been to college in a month.

I took the lead and walked confidently, and Yang Su-bin followed behind me. She had been very nervous, but after seeing my strength, she seemed to have relaxed somewhat.

While walking down the road, a zombie suddenly popped out of an alleyway.

,Kyaaaa!

Yang Su-bin screamed reflexively. The zombies nearby flocked to this side.

First, I slashed and killed the zombie that popped out and looked around. Because it was a commercial street, more than 30 zombies popped out at once. More than half of them were cases of being bitten by zombies and becoming zombies themselves.

,I, I'm sorry. I didn't mean to...

Yang Su-bin said with a look as if she was about to pass out. I was going to punch her, but I stopped. That was a genuine mistake.

It's not difficult to deal with 30 zombies if I struggle a bit. The problem was Yang Su-bin. It was difficult to protect her with just my strength while dealing with 30 zombies.

,I have a long way to go. I can't waste my strength here. I didn't expect to use this so soon.

I took out something that looked like a grenade from my pocket and pressed the button. Kwakakakakakakang! A loud noise rang out. Even more zombies ran towards this side. Zombies react to sound first and most sensitively. So, you can use sound to draw aggro.

I threw the sound grenade that Na Chae-yeong made far away. The zombies all ran towards the sound grenade.

,I brought 8. Now I have 7 left.

I took one of them out of my bag and put it in my pocket. I'll have to use it again in case of an emergency.

,Okay. Let's go.

,...I'm sorry.

,Well, you can do that once. Don't do it twice. You're not that stupid, are you?

,I won't scream stupidly again.

In fact, even when zombies suddenly popped out, she didn't scream. I just nonchalantly sliced the zombies that popped out.

There were people cautiously walking around the streets like us. They were desperate people. People who didn't have anything stored at home. They were heading to nearby convenience stores or supermarkets. Of course, the store owners weren't stupid either. They closed the shutters and tried to block them in any way they could. There were convenience stores that distributed food to people, but they were rare.

,Uh, excuse me.

A man blocked my path. He looked like a college student.

,What.

,Plea, please share some food with me. I don't have any food at home. I think I'm going to starve to death like this.

,It hasn't even been 12 hours since the virus broke out. Starve to death my ass. If you want to get food, go to a convenience store or supermarket.

,All the nearby convenience stores and supermarkets are closed, so I can't go! Please, just a little! Please share some food with me!

,This is your last warning. Get lost.

I placed my hand on the hilt of my sword. The man took a step back and watched me warily.

,That's not a real sword, is it? Like a wooden sword...

Seogeok!

The college student's head fell to the floor. As the smell of blood spread, the zombies turned their bodies towards this side.

I grabbed Yang Su-bin's stiff hand and ran slowly to minimize the noise. The gathered zombies began to devour the corpse.

,You didn't have to kill him.

,There was no reason not to kill him.

,......

Yang Su-bin opened and closed her mouth repeatedly. Her fear towards me seemed to have grown stronger.
