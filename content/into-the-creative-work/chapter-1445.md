I returned home after receiving a call from Yuria-ssi.

The three uninvited guests were tied up, their limbs bound, and their mouths gagged, imprisoned in the basement. I reflexively scanned the basement. The basement was the space I usually used for magic training and research. Apart from a few magical tools, there was nothing special.

I had told Yuria-ssi that she could use the basement as she pleased, so there was nothing to hold her accountable for. Above all, my magical research data was all in my head.

"...How did you subdue them? I can't even imagine them moving."

"I severed their spinal cords. Since the nerves are cut, they can't move their arms and legs. But they can still move their necks. Of course, they can also speak."

"What's with tying their arms and legs?"

"Just in case. They might have some secret arts that I don't know about, so I tied them up."

I approached them. The wounds on their backs were covered with old, rag-like cloths. It felt like they had only received rough first aid, not caring at all about diseases.

'Impressive skills.'

Looking at them, I couldn't see any other wounds. This meant that she had subdued them with overwhelming skill.

'As expected of someone from a knight training academy....'

It wasn't that surprising that she had subdued them. She is a maid. From the first time I saw her, I had guessed that she possessed a certain level of strength.

I grabbed one of the guys and sat him on a chair. His eyes were shaking, and cold sweat was flowing from his forehead. I deliberately chose the one who seemed the most mentally weak.

[Silence]

I used magic before removing the gag from his mouth. I blocked the sound in the basement. It would be troublesome if the screams he made leaked outside.

I removed the gag. The guy didn't scream. He bit his trembling lips tightly.

"...Kill me."

"Why should I? Dying would be the greatest mercy for you right now."

"I can't feel anything in my body. That means no matter what torture you inflict, I won't feel any pain. It would be easier to just kill me. Or are you going to take care of my piss and Urine for the rest of my life? Hehe. That's not bad either. A life where such a pretty maid takes care of my piss and Urine... Keok!"

Tzack!

I slapped him.

I was furious when I heard him talk about Yuria-ssi. Yuria-ssi was my woman, someone I had to take responsibility for. I couldn't let some insignificant bastard defile her.

I grabbed the guy's head and moved my mana. I was going to scan his body once with mana.

Pajik.

A blue flame flickered. I felt a sense of doubt. I didn't use electric magic. The mana just naturally turned into a weak current.

'...It seems like this would be more convenient for scanning a human body.'

I chose the easier option.

A subtle current flowed through the guy's body, transmitting that information to me.

'He's not a mana awakener. But compared to my body, his physique is extremely well-trained. Can you reach this level with just ordinary training without mana...?'

The answer to my question came immediately.

Drugs.

I had heard about drugs that enhance muscles. They're quite expensive, but not so expensive that you can't get your hands on them at all.

'As Yuria-ssi said, his nerves are severed. Cleanly severed. Amazing skill. But this... I feel like I can do something about this?'

The way a person moves is ultimately through electrical signals sent from the brain. If I could control those electrical signals....

Pajik.

A current flows. The guy suddenly stretched out his left foot. He stretched it out, putting strength into the tip of his toes.

"W-What did you do?!"

"Is this the right foot?"

I controlled the current differently. This time, the guy moved his right arm to the side. Ujikkun! His elbow broke, completely turning behind the chair.

"M-My arm...!"

The arm didn't stop there and even rotated 360 degrees. Of course, the arm was completely wrecked as a result.

I conducted various other experiments as well. Forcibly moving other arms and legs, or stopping the biological activity of internal organs.

In the middle of it, I got excited. Mana taking the form of electricity.

The body reacting to it.

Magical inspiration flashed and passed by.

What if the current could manipulate even the brain? If it were possible to manipulate the other person's memories or mind?

'Magic that can manipulate humans according to my will. It's not impossible to create such magic.'

I moved the subtle currents into the guy's brain with anticipation.

The result was a failure.

The moment the subtle current touched the guy's brain, he rolled his eyes and died. A person's brain was much more complex and sensitive than I had thought.

I felt my excitement fade away in an instant. It was because I realized that no matter how many times I tried this method, it wouldn't work.

"I accidentally killed him. But I've roughly got the hang of it."

I dragged the corpse off the chair and sat another guy down. The guy swallowed hard and watched me carefully. His gaze towards me was full of fear. He had seen me conducting human experiments on a person right in front of him.

I placed my hand on the guy's shoulder. Pajik. A subtle current flowed through the guy's body.

"Kkeuaaaaaaak!"

The guy screamed. Perhaps it felt like his entire cells were being tortured.

"If you want to be comfortable, you'll have to tell me everything, starting with your name."

The guy couldn't last even a minute.

"I'll talk...! I'll talk, so stop it...!"

I took my hand off the guy's shoulder. The guy let out a sigh of relief as the pain disappeared. At the same time, he quietly looked at his comrade. That comrade slowly nodded. I could see a light of resignation in his eyes. It seemed like he had chosen to speak and die rather than continue to be tortured.

"We belong to the Fraxion Mercenary Corps."

I didn't think they were mafia or a gang. Their atmosphere was a bit different from them.

"Did you receive a request?"

"...I thought it would be a decent way to earn some pocket money."

"I'm not interested in your thoughts. Who is the client and what is the request?"

"The client is Oren Habers. The boss of the Habers Gang. The request is to kidnap you. He said he wants the chicken recipe."

"Why did he hire you guys? He could have moved himself for something like this, right?"

"The Habers Gang is currently under close watch by the council and is lying low. If he moved directly, the council might step in. He hired us to avoid the worst-case scenario."

"I've never heard of that gang before... Are they a big enough force for the council to care about?"

"I heard they killed a council official."

I understood immediately.

Any force becomes ruthless when you mess with them. Especially messing with public officials is no different from turning the country itself into an enemy. It's rather strange that the Habers Gang, who killed a public official, hasn't been suppressed yet.

I got the information I needed. Still, I asked about other things just in case. There wasn't much useful information. Just that the Habers Gang belongs to Jupiter, a gang alliance in Neo London. Of course, I've never heard of the Jupiter gang alliance either.

"We are the Fraxion Mercenary Corps. If you release us, we will end things here. If you kill us, the Fraxion Mercenary Corps won't stand still."

"That's funny. Do you think I don't know the situation in this industry? No matter how I kill you, the Fraxion Mercenary Corps won't move."

I didn't attack the Fraxion Mercenary Corps. The Fraxion Mercenary Corps has nothing to do with this. As the guys said earlier, they targeted me for pocket money, and I killed them in self-defense.

Just because a mercenary fails a request doesn't mean that the mercenary corps they belong to will directly retaliate. Because if they did, there would be no end to it. Another mercenary might take over the request to restore honor... but before that, the Fraxion Mercenary Corps will hold the Habers Gang accountable.

"You know this industry well? Are you in the same business?"

Instead of answering, I turned my gaze away. It was time to cross-check if the information I heard from the guy was correct.

•••

I extracted the information from the captured guys and killed them cleanly. I incinerated the corpses with magic. Even though I resented the guys, there was no need to keep them captured and torture them. That was a waste of manpower and time.

I left the house immediately. I planned to finish the troublesome business before dawn. The destination was District V, which was notorious for having many gangs. The Habers Gang was also located in this district.

I entered District V. I walked along, adjusting my coat for no reason.

I didn't deliberately hide my face.

This was revenge. The Habers Gang touched me first, and I was just retaliating for it. It was a natural occurrence in the underworld.

'I need to show my strength here so that things won't be troublesome in the future.'

The Habers Gang wouldn't be the only ones trying to interfere with and take away my business. I had to show them. That I'm not someone to be trifled with.

'Even if I take out a small gang, it won't make me so famous that I can't live my daily life. Even if I do become famous, I just won't go to work at the chicken restaurant directly.'

If I raise the hourly wage, I can easily find manpower. I walked along, looking at the walls and alleys. Gangs mark their territory by drawing graffiti on the walls, like dogs marking their territory with Piss. If you look closely at the markings on the walls, you can find the gang's hideout.

'The real gangs aren't in places like this. Those guys' organizations are similar to corporations.'

Most of the guys here were small fries. That was why I was openly walking through this street.

'Found it.'

An alleyway with the Habers Gang's mark drawn on it.

I walked down the alleyway, thinking. The Habers Gang belongs to the Jupiter gang alliance. The reason they haven't been suppressed yet even after killing a Neo London public official is because of that.

'It's obvious. They must be connected to high-ranking officials. If I get rid of the Habers Gang... they'll probably try to kill me.'

What I have to do is obvious.

I show the power I have. And I negotiate with Jupiter. I just need to make them realize that turning me into an enemy is more of a loss than insisting on revenge.

'An alliance. A gang of that size can't act emotionally. The Habers guys touched me first, so I have the justification.'

My advancing feet stopped.

Three thugs leaning against the wall, chewing gum and watching me. Their weapons were brutal. Assault rifles, pistols, grenade belts. Threatening tattoos like skulls and monsters were engraved on their rough skin.

"What, you?"

"Do you know whose territory you've entered?"

"If you're a guest, say the password. I'll give you 5 seconds."

I ignored them and walked forward. In my head, I was contemplating how to kill them.

The guys reacted sensitively. They immediately pointed their guns at me and pulled the trigger. Bullets poured out. But none of the bullets touched my body.

Tingtingtingtingtingting!

It was a barrier deployed by a 5th-circle magician. Ordinary bullets can never penetrate it.

"What the fuck!"

A grenade with the safety pin removed flew towards me.
