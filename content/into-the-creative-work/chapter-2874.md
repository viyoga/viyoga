I arrived at Baron Peeble's mansion in the same D District. Since we lived in the same district, I could move this spontaneously.

The three-story mansion was modest for a noble's house. More like a wealthy citizen's level. Compared to the mansion I stay in, it's not even half of half of half the size.

,Still, being in D District means ordinary citizens don't even look at it. It's a more expensive area than Gangnam was in my past life.'

I checked the information about Baron Peeble in the carriage.

He was a doctor and professor working at the medical school. The baronial title was granted to his grandfather, a great scholar, by the royal family.

,He's not particularly wealthy for a noble... but his connections are excellent due to his family and influence as a medical school professor.'

He's a neutral party who distances himself from noble politics. Thanks to his profession as a doctor, he's quite respected in noble society. In other words, he's not a bad person to befriend.

As the carriage arrived at the entrance of the mansion, a maid came out. The brown-haired maid quickly checked the carriage's emblem and bowed her head.

,Welcome to the Peeble family's residence, Earl Mayer-kakha."

,I apologize for coming without prior notice."

As I spoke, I quietly spread my aura. Using magic recklessly was rude. I didn't come to fight, so there was no reason to commit such rudeness.

I sensed two additional presences inside the mansion. Probably Baron Peeble and his daughter. It seemed there were no other servants besides the maid in front of me.

,Not at all. I'm sure you came after reading the letter our master sent. I apologize for the lack of hospitality due to the lack of preparation."

,I didn't come to receive hospitality, so it's fine. It seems the situation is urgent, so I'd like to meet Baron Peeble right away."

,I will guide you."

Guided by the maid, I entered the mansion. She knocked on the bedroom door and said.

,Master. Earl Mayer-kakha has arrived. He urgently came after reading the letter you sent earlier."

,Oh! Earl Mayer?!"

With a hurried movement, the door opened from the inside. There was a stench. It was similar to the smell of a decaying corpse.

A slightly haggard middle-aged man appeared. His orange hair was disheveled, and there were dark circles under his eyes.

His daughter, Ellie Peeble, lay on the bed as if dead. She was so emaciated that her skin seemed to cling to her bones.

,After reading the letter, it seemed urgent, so I came without contacting you. Can you explain the situation?"

Baron Peeble hesitated for a moment before gritting his teeth.

,I know it's against etiquette to say this to a guest who came to help... but can you keep a secret?"

Seeing him act like this, it seemed to be something related to honor.

,Of course. I swear on my name."

I didn't care about Baron Peeble's honor. What I was interested in was the curse. A curse that a renowned medical professor couldn't handle.

,Ellie.... This foolish daughter of mine associated with a Void Worshiper."

Void Worshiper.

Those who believe that the cause of the Dark Moon lies in the Void, and that they can go to the Void through the Dark Moon. They say they can achieve everything with the power of the Void.

In simple terms, they were people who worshiped the Dark Moon.

Naturally, the public's perception of Void Worshipers is fucked. In some areas, they kill Void Worshipers without question. Even in the relatively free Neo London, the perception isn't good.

,It's strange to be cursed just by associating with them. Did the Void Worshiper cast the curse directly?"

,Last month, on the day the Dark Moon rose, Ellie secretly performed a ritual with a Void Worshiper without my knowledge. The Void Worshiper died during the ritual, and Ellie is slowly rotting away due to the curse. I'm doing everything I can to stop the curse from progressing... but even that is reaching its limit. Please, save my deficient daughter!"

Dark Moon. Ritual. Curse.

All words that piqued my interest. Rituals and curses, broadly speaking, belong to the category of magic, and the Dark Moon could be said to be the center of this world.

,Didn't you hire a curse caster or a dispeller?"

,I hired a 6th-grade curse caster and a 5th-grade dispeller with the family's wealth! But those bastards ran away saying it was impossible with their power! Please help me! If you can solve Ellie's curse, I will give you half of the family's wealth!"

,It's alright. I didn't come here for money."

I wasn't interested in such petty money.

,The first thing we need to focus on is the ritual. Can you tell me what kind of ritual it was?"

,There's a book that the Void Worshiper brought. The curse caster and dispeller said it was nonsense. I wanted to burn it, but I kept it because it might be a clue to the curse."

He looked at the maid. The maid quickly brought the book and handed it to me. It was a book with a black leather cover. I felt an ominous energy.

,Is it human skin? It's not surprising. Humans are easy to obtain on the black market. The byproducts are even cheaper.'

I opened the book.

Ignoring the useless content praising the Dark Moon, I only looked at the ritual-related content.

,Nonsense."

A ritual that seemed to be scribbled by a non-expert with shallow knowledge.

The purpose of the ritual was a sacrificial rite to offer sacrifices to a great being and obtain what they wanted.

,The purpose sounds good. But everything from the direction of the ritual to the ritual circle is all nonsense.'

Originally, nothing would happen with such a nonsensical ritual.

Unless the day the ritual was performed was the Dark Moon.

On the day the Dark Moon rises, it wouldn't be strange for anything to happen.

,What did Ellie-yeongae want when she performed the ritual? What did she offer as a sacrifice in the ritual?"

,The sacrifice was a stray cat that the Void Worshiper caught. What Ellie wanted... was appearance."

I glanced at Ellie lying on the bed. She was emaciated due to the curse, so of course she was ugly.

,Considering that, she must have had an ordinary face.'

Currently, appearance is naturally considered in Neo London's noble society. And the standards are quite high compared to ordinary people.

Ordinary appearance? That's ugly in noble society.

,If she were a male noble, maybe. In female noble society, appearance is as important as family.'

Plastic surgery?

If the rumors spread, she can't live as a noble. No, she can't get married. There's no reason for a male noble to marry a woman who has had plastic surgery.

,There's nothing I can learn from this book. Can I examine the young lady directly?"

,O-of course!"

I approached the bed.

The stench was more intense. Looking at the bed, it was a state-of-the-art medical device. Numerous tubes were inserted into her arms, legs, and torso. They were using drugs to recover her rotting body from the curse.

,Potions are mixed in with the drugs. But enduring is the best they can do. If they can't get rid of the curse, she'll eventually die.'

I focused on the curse that was rotting the young lady's body.

I even used detection magic.

I was also knowledgeable in black magic, so I could roughly grasp the curse.

,It's not a curse cast by a human being. It's a curse cast by a being of the Void. That nonsensical ritual succeeded due to the influence of the Dark Moon.'

Being of the Void.

I know the identities of those beings that Void Worshipers worship as great beings.

,Fallen ones who failed to transcend. This nonsensical ritual accidentally succeeded with the power of the Dark Moon... and reached a Fallen one.'

But would the Fallen one be satisfied with the ritual? Some ignorant bastards accidentally succeeded and offered a stray cat as a sacrifice. If I were a Fallen one, I would be angry instead.

,So they bestowed a curse.'

This is my speculation so far.

,First, I'll weaken the power of the curse, then collect it, take it away, and study it.'

I opened the mind of a magician, Astral, and raised my right hand. White light shone from my right hand. Mana turned into divine mana in my hand. Baron Peeble, who was next to me, was startled.

,Divine mana! Wasn't Earl Mayer an electric magician?!"

,There's no law that says an electric magician can't use divine mana. Ah, this is a secret."

[Cure]

A 4th-grade divine magic that purifies and heals impure things, including curses.

The effect was excellent. The power of the curse that was eating away at the young lady's body rapidly weakened, and vitality began to return to her body.

,Oh, oooooh!"

Baron Peeble admired with joy. But a drop of sweat ran down my forehead.

,It's not easy.'

Contrary to appearances, the curse had weakened but not disappeared. I hadn't eliminated the source of the curse.

,It was eating away not only at the young lady's body but also at her soul.'

More divine mana is needed.

I emitted a large amount of divine mana. I wasn't a priest, so the efficiency was zero. I may believe in the existence of angels, but I have no faith.

Kung!

A shock that shook my whole body. I grabbed the edge of the bed and held on so I wouldn't fall.

The source of the shock was in front of me. A gray mass that appeared on the young lady's chest. It's the source of the curse.

-How absurd. A magician from the lower realm dares to interfere with me? Fine. You die cursed instead of this woman.

The gray source of the curse flew towards me. I tried to respond with divine mana, but the curse easily pierced through the divine mana and burrowed into my body.

Along with the loss of strength in my body, a pain like my fingers were being torn apart assailed me.

I reacted instinctively. I circulated divine mana through all the mana roads and sealed the heart containing the source of the curse with divine magic. I used sealing magic by improvising and modifying it into divine magic.

,Earl-kakha! Are you alright?!"

,The young lady will be fine."

,No. I'm worried about your well-being! You're bleeding right now!"

,It's nothing. The young lady's curse has been transferred to me, and I've sealed it with divine magic. The young lady will be healthy if she rests well. That's your specialty. I'll be going now."

,The curse has been transferred! Is, is there anything I can do to help?!"

,I'll take care of my own business."

I confidently walked out of the mansion.

A Fallen one's curse?

I didn't think seriously about it. I could handle this divine mana and even use divine magic. And my talent related to magic is real. I thought I could get rid of this curse within a week.

And fifteen days passed.

I couldn't solve the curse.

Due to the influence of the curse, my energy was disappearing. My appetite is disappearing.

,I'm fucked.'

I was slowly dying.
