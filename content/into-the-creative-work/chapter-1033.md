Returning to the mansion, I noticed the maids' eyes sparkling with an almost burdensome intensity. They were especially alternating glances between Yuria and me, smiling contentedly.

The reason was obvious. They knew what had happened in the mental world. Elena, who had already left this world, must have blabbed to the maids.

,Welcome back, Master,-

The maids grabbed their skirts and bowed their heads in greeting. The sight of them lined up on both sides from the mansion entrance and bowing in unison was spectacular. Especially since each of them was a stunning beauty, it felt even more impressive.

I felt a surge of pride. I could see why the chairmen-nim of large corporations enjoyed receiving greetings from their employees.

,Yeah. Anything happen while I was gone?-

,The army that went north is returning victorious. And... Elena-nim has departed,-

Nephia, the head maid's deputy, spoke on behalf of the others.

The army's victory was something I already knew, so it wasn't surprising.

,With this, the victory of the Rapelli Kingdom is assured. Once the war is completely over, the Rapelli Kingdom will grow large enough to call itself an empire,-

The Rapelli Kingdom would soon suffer growing pains. The princess and the prince would fight over the heir position. Unlike the original story, the timing was a bit delayed, but it would eventually happen. Only one person could sit on the throne.

,Master-nim! Elena-nim left a gift for you!-

Nephia said with a slightly excited tone.

,A gift?-

,Yes! I'll guide you! You'll be surprised when you see it too, Master-nim,-

Curious about what Elena had left behind, I obediently followed Nephia. Nephia led me to the first-floor hall. A large frame was hanging on the wall in the center of the hall.

It was a photo of me in a tuxedo and Yuria in a wedding dress. A scene from the wedding in the mental world was captured in a large photo. Of course, we didn't take any photos, but nothing was impossible with Elena's abilities.

,It's such a beautiful photo! I heard you had a wedding in the mental world? Congratulations on your marriage,-

,Congratulations on your marriage!-

The maids shouted, led by Nephia. I scratched my head, feeling somewhat embarrassed, and Yuria's cheeks flushed slightly. Seeing her gaze linger on the photo, she seemed to like it quite a bit.

,Sorry, but we're going to have another wedding. About two more times?-

One would be a wedding held independently within the mansion.

The other would be outside the mansion. A wedding to which not only Count Prukus but also all sorts of nobles would be invited.

The reason for having two separate weddings was also my intention not to mistreat the maids in the mansion. In the first place, I didn't care about the guys outside the mansion... but there was also such a thing as social status.

,In conclusion, I'll be having a wedding with Yuria three times,-

Yuria didn't dislike it. Rather, she seemed slightly pleased.

I informed Nephia of this plan. The second wedding was scheduled to take place two weeks later. It was a bit of a tight schedule, but it was possible with the maids of my mansion. Since Yuria would be in command of the maids, I thought the preparations could be completed within a week if we set our minds to it.

,I understand, Master-nim! I'll prepare a perfect and beautiful wedding!-

Nephia was burning with enthusiasm even though it wasn't her job. I realized that Nephia tended to like formalities.

,Ah, right, Elena-nim's gift isn't just this,-

,There was more left behind?-

,Yes. It's so special that we created a separate space for it in the basement,-

A gift that required a separate space in the basement. My expectations began to rise.

Guided by Nephia, I went to the basement and burst out laughing.

The basement space. It was a wine cellar. A place where a constant temperature and humidity were maintained by magic. About 10,000 bottles of wine were stored here. I picked up one of the stored wine bottles. The inside of the corked wine bottle sloshed.

,I was really surprised when Elena-nim gestured and wines suddenly appeared out of thin air. Immediately after, Elena-nim disappeared without a trace...,-

I checked the label on the surface of the bottle. It was a top-class wine even within the [God's Atlantis] world.

,And there's a wine that Elena-nim specifically told us to handle with care before she left,-

It was in the center of the storage.

A total of 100 bottles.

I recognized it immediately.

Dionysus' Wine.

A special wine that increased mana the moment you drank it. And a wine with a taste and aroma close to perfection.

I immediately understood what had happened.

,Elena used Limit Break before leaving. And she must have burned all of her lifespan,-

She used Limit Break to use the Butterfly Dream Technique with penalty-free power, burned all of her lifespan, used Complete Recovery again, and then used the Butterfly Dream Technique again to make the wine.

Otherwise, this enormous amount of wine wouldn't make sense. Especially since there were as many as 100 bottles of Dionysus' Wine.

,I'll have to thank Elena later,-

Dionysus' Wine was to be used for more constructive purposes. If I gave it to hardworking maids or talented maids with this and that reason, they would be happy.

,Let's use these wines at the wedding in two weeks,-

,Yes, Master-nim. I will,-

Saving top-class wine just makes it go to waste. You have to use it when you need to.

I took a bottle of Dionysus' Wine and went outside. It wouldn't hurt if I drank a few bottles.

•••

The maids were busy preparing for the wedding. I wasn't just playing around during that time.

There was something I had to take care of.

It was Kyle and his group who had been captured.

Over the past week, Yuria had succeeded in restoring Kyle to his original state after torturing Bitsol, the demon of desire.

Geulbet Baron only stimulated strong desires, but the demon Bitsol was able to stimulate small desires or conversely, make swelling desires smaller. Bitsol returned Kyle to his original state by calming Kyle's desires.

Kyle, who had been sitting blankly in the center of the prison, raised his head as soon as I entered.

,...Eugene. I'm so sorry. To be manipulated by a demon and act so disgracefully.... I'm a failure as a brother,-

Kyle sat on the prison floor and muttered in a depressed tone. Of course, I didn't feel any sympathy.

,Are you really reflecting on what you did?-

,...I think it wouldn't be strange if you killed me. I'll calmly accept whatever punishment you give me,-

,Even if I kill you, my brother?-

,It doesn't matter. I tried to kill you.... If you want, I'll take my own life,-

Kyle lowered his head deeply.

It wasn't acting. Before coming here, I used Bitsol to stimulate Kyle's emotions of regret and sadness. But I didn't stimulate him too much.

,If you stimulate desires and emotions too much, they become extreme. Like Yuria in the mental world. If I stimulate Kyle too much, he might commit suicide,-

However, if I used Bitsol's power well, I could take Kyle into my grasp and use him. Just like Geulbet Baron used Kyle's desire for Yuria to manipulate him.

Of course, it was quite difficult. Kyle was not an ordinary person. However, his weakened mental strength while imprisoned and the undeniable justification I had made it possible to control Kyle.

,That won't do. Atonement is too easy,-

,...Eugene. You want me to live and suffer,-

,Of course. Dying is too easy,-

,What do you want me to do?-

Kyle said weakly.

,First, resign from the position of successor. And support me,-

,...Okay. The tide has already turned in your favor anyway. However... Jent-hyung won't easily accept it,-

I knew that. With Jent's personality, he would never acknowledge me until the end. I expected Jent to use even despicable means until the very end.

,I have no intention of being taken in,-

I have Yuria, who is a Grand Master. Even if Yuria wasn't there, I wouldn't think that I would be taken in by someone like Jent.

,And second. You have to become a knight, brother. Pledge your allegiance to me, not the family,-

It was common for a noble who lost in the successor fight to become a knight. However, a large territory like Prukus was a bit different. Some of the territory was taken away and given. Just like I received the port city of Tebrah.

,Pledge allegiance to you...?-

,Why? Don't you like the idea of serving your younger brother?-

,That's not it. Eugene, you're my younger brother, but you're much more amazing than me. I have no complaints about pledging allegiance and serving you. Others will acknowledge it too. However... I have a dream,-

,Yuria?-

,Ah, no!-

Kyle shook his head in embarrassment.

,I've given up on my feelings for Yuria. Yuria already... loves you firmly. I don't have the right to enter between you,-

,You realized that only now?-

,......Haa. I don't know why I did that either,-

Kyle sighed deeply.

I stroked my chin and thought. If Kyle had a dream, it would be that.

,The Hwasan Sect?-

,That's right. The Hwasan Sect. I told you before. You said you would help me, but... our relationship has become like this.... But I still haven't given up on that dream,-

,I'll help you. But pledge your allegiance to me,-

That was even better.

It was better to have the Hwasan Sect in my grasp.

Since the Hwasan Sect was a martial arts group, it could be used like a knight order.

,If you help me... the Hwasan Sect will be safe. Okay. I'll pledge my allegiance to you,-

Finally, Kyle was in my hands. I tried hard to lower the corners of my mouth, which were about to rise.

I had Yuria, but Yuria only had one body, and she had to be by my side most of the time. The more cards I could use besides Yuria, the better.

,Kyle Prukus. As proof of your allegiance, bow your head and kneel,-

I commanded solemnly.

Kyle followed my words without complaint. He knelt and slammed his forehead on the ground.

,Kyle Prukus. I will become the sword of my lord from now on. My loyalty will never change,-

Kyle's complexion brightened. His sense of guilt towards me had faded slightly.

,Kyle-hyung. But this isn't enough. You tried to kill me and take Yuria away. But it's a bit much to end it with just a pledge of allegiance. You were manipulated by Geulbet Baron? If you had dealt with it properly, you wouldn't have been manipulated,-

,...Eugene. You're right. I harbored emotions that I shouldn't have. To not be able to control even one desire properly.... I'm disqualified as a martial artist and a Taoist of the Hwasan Sect,-

Kyle reproached himself.

,Hyung. If you've done wrong, you have to be punished. Cut off your left arm,-

,...My left arm...,-

,I'll spare your right arm since you need to hold a sword,-

Kyle's eyes shook. But he soon bowed his head to me.

,I'm sorry, Eugene. I was a bad brother. And thank you for forgiving me with just one left arm,-

I pushed a sword into the prison.

Kyle grabbed the sword and cut off his left arm as it was.

,Kueueueuk!-

Thud.

His left arm fell with blood. Kyle, who had stopped the bleeding with pressure points, was sweating profusely and held out the sword outside the prison. His arm was blocked by the bars, so it was impossible. I opened the bars and retrieved the arm. Kyle was docile.

,Kyle-hyung. I can't release you from here right away,-

,I understand. And I'm okay. I need to sort out my complicated head here,-

,By the way, I've decided to have a wedding with Yuria next week,-

,...Another wedding? Ah, that's right. That time was in the mental world,-

I could see Kyle desperately trying to control his expression. He still had feelings for Yuria after all. But Yuria would never look at Kyle.

,We're going to have two more weddings in total. This wedding will be held simply within the mansion, and the second wedding will be held officially and lavishly. I'm sorry, hyung, but please stay in prison while the first wedding is taking place. This wedding is just an event for the servants in the mansion,-

,...Okay. I understand. Congratulations on your wedding, Eugene,-

I turned around and left, and Kyle closed his eyes and focused on circulating his qi.
