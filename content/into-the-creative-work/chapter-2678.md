After annihilating an entire city, I shook off the blood stench clinging to my body and headed towards the Valderth mansion.

Naturally, Valderth's territory was in an uproar. Soldiers patrolled the territory, controlling the people, and knights were awake even at this late hour.

It was only natural, considering a nearby city had vanished from the map in a matter of hours.

I moved using Deception (SSS). I blatantly passed by a knight. The Valderth knight didn't notice a thing.

,I'm a little disappointed. The knights of 17 years later would have sensed something at least.'

What I felt while dealing with the city earlier was that the Empire's overall martial prowess was lower compared to 17 years later.

Well, it was to be expected.

,Right now, this era is before the system fully intervened. There's no God's Seat either.'

This world wasn't the Atlantis I knew yet.

Ttuk.

My advancing steps halted. My senses caught something. The space in front of me rippled, and a person appeared.

It was a man with dark blue hair, draped in a black coat, looking exhausted. He looked irritable, perhaps because he seemed tired, but even considering that, he had a rather gentle impression. His right hand rested on the ground with an ornate staff. The size was a bit different, but the design was similar to the staff Elena-ssi often used.

,How dare you crawl in here.'

Ricardo Valderth.

A great mage known as the Three Extremes Duke (三極公) for mastering three types of magic. He is the current head of the Valderth family and Elena's father.

,Nice to meet you, father-in-law. I'm Seong Yujin.'

,What?'

Intense killing intent was directed at me from Ricardo-nim. The tips of my fingers tingled. How long has it been since I've faced this level of killing intent head-on?

I subtly circulated the Cheonma Shingong, and the tingling disappeared.

,You're no ordinary guy. Was that bullshit you spouted to provoke me? Cheonma. It won't go your way.'

Cheonma.

I called myself that while destroying the city. I was a bit high-strung back then. Some survivors escaped the city, and my infamy spread quickly.

,It's neither a joke nor a provocation. I plan to become the Valderth family's son-in-law.'

,Not on my watch.'

Mana gathered around Ricardo-nim. The rapidly gathering mana quickly transformed into magic, dominating the surroundings. I blankly watched it. Since I didn't feel any will to fight at all, Ricardo-nim only frowned and didn't attack first.

,That's up to Elena-ssi, the head of the family.'

,Elena-ssi is the heir, but not yet the head of the family. I am the head of Valderth.'

,Elena-ssi is the head of the family. This is just a world inside a mirror created based on Elena-ssi's memories.'

I didn't hide it. There was no reason to hide it.

,Are you trying to buy time by spouting nonsense?'

,Atlantis. Most strange things can be explained with one word: Atlantis. Ah, but this isn't Atlantis yet, so you probably don't know?'

Ricardo-nim's eyes wavered greatly. Then he used magic. Not towards me, but towards the sky. Blue mana flashed and covered the night sky like a spiderweb before disappearing.

,...It's real. This star and the stars in that sky. None of them have life. Me, this world is fake...?'

Ricardo-nim's body swayed greatly. But he didn't fall. He regained his balance.

,...Okay. I'll believe that you're going to marry Elena-ssi and become the Valderth family's son-in-law. But why did you massacre an entire city? Why can a murderer like you be Elena-ssi's lover? No matter how I think about it, I don't think my Elena-ssi would take a murderer as a partner.'

,Massacre? I just did some training. The people living in the city? They're not people. They're worse than NPCs... Ah. I guess you don't know what NPCs are. Just think of them as moving paper. Calling me a murderer for tearing some paper is a bit of an exaggeration, father-in-law.'

,...So in your eyes, I also look like moving paper.'

,Yes. But I'm still treating you respectfully because you're my father-in-law.'

,...To think such a guy is Valderth's son-in-law. Why on earth did Elena-ssi...?'

,You keep mentioning Elena-ssi, but I know more about Elena-ssi than you do, father-in-law.'

,I can't stand that. I'm Elena-ssi's father.'

,I'll tell you about your future as a special service, father-in-law. In five years, you'll be poisoned by your wife, and your wife will be killed by Elena-ssi's hand.'

Ricardo-nim stared intently at me. His subtly trembling pupils and fingertips showed how shocked he was.

,...Don't lie... Erica and Elena-ssi would never do that.'

,Why would I lie to moving paper?'

If it were real. If this world were something like a parallel world, I wouldn't have said such things. Because it's Elena-ssi's business, I would have respected Elena-ssi. But this world was neither a parallel world nor anything else.

,If you really can't believe it, ask Elena-ssi.'

Ricardo-nim followed my gaze and looked behind him. There, a young Elena-ssi was standing. She must have felt the huge flow of mana and came looking.

I could tell that Elena-ssi was flustered through her trembling eyes. Like father, like daughter. Their flustered expressions were identical.

,Elena-ssi...! Is what this guy saying true?'

Elena-ssi hesitated for a moment before admitting it with a sigh.

,...Yes. Father. It's true.'

,......'

An uncomfortable silence descended.

Elena-ssi and I made eye contact. Her eyes looking at me contained a hint of resentment. I shrugged.

,Elena-ssi. I think we need to talk. But before that... is this guy really the man who will become your husband?'

,Yes. Father. There is no man more suitable for me than Yujin.'

Elena-ssi said confidently. Ricardo-nim looked at Elena-ssi with a complicated expression.

,Elena-ssi, father-in-law. I'll wait inside the mansion.'

I didn't care what kind of conversation Elena-ssi and Ricardo-nim had. It would be meaningless to me, if not to Elena-ssi.

• • •

I waited in the reception room, reading a novel.

About an hour later, there was a knock, and the door opened, and Ricardo-nim entered. I was a little surprised that it wasn't Elena-ssi.

,I heard from Elena-ssi. That you are Elena-ssi's... and the Valderth family's benefactor. As the head of the family. As a father, I express my gratitude to you.'

Ricardo-nim bowed to me. It was a noble's dignity.

,Please raise your head. Honestly, even if you bow, father-in-law, I won't feel anything.'

,Is it because I'm fake?'

,Yes. Even if you know the future, the truth, father-in-law, nothing will change. And... personally, I can't understand you. Why do you tolerate your wife's affair? If I were you, father-in-law, I would have broken my wife's legs first.'

,...I love Erica. I trust her as much as I love her. And... I also feel sorry for Erica.'

Forcibly brought her. Did he threaten her with the family's power or something? I erased the romance story that was being drawn in my head.

,Do you love your wife more than Elena-ssi, father-in-law?'

Ricardo-nim paused before answering.

,...I love both of them.'

He loves a woman who commits adultery as much as his own child? The answer was as good as out.

,What did you do when Elena-ssi was having a hard time? From what I heard, you were often away from the family... Is work more important than family?'

,There's not much time left until Atlantis. At most, two years. I had to carry out the orders given by His Majesty the Emperor and lay the foundation for the family in preparation for after Atlantis. I firmly believed that doing what was best for the family was also doing what was best for Erica and Elena-ssi.'

,Ah, is that so.'

I was the one who asked the questions, but now that I've heard the answers, I feel like I'm losing interest. I probably won't be able to understand the man in front of me for the rest of my life.

,...What do you love about Elena-ssi?'

,Hmm. Her beauty? Elena-ssi is beautiful, after all.'

,...Don't you look at her personality?'

,Of course, I do. Elena-ssi has a beautiful personality too.'

,...I don't know why Elena-ssi loves someone like you.'

,I don't know why Elena-ssi respects you, either, father-in-law.'

,......'

,......'

It's a good thing Ricardo-nim died early. If he hadn't died and continued to live, I would have clashed with him over everything. And because he's Elena-ssi's father, I wouldn't have been able to kill him carelessly.

Ttok, ttok, ttok.

With a knock, Elena-ssi entered. Elena-ssi surveyed the atmosphere and sighed.

,I expected it, but... the atmosphere is terrible.'

,Ahem.'

Ricardo-nim cleared his throat as if to shake off the awkwardness.

,......'

I just remained silent.

Elena-ssi approached me. Leaving the perfectly good seat next to me, she sat on my lap as if it were natural. I wrapped my arm around Elena-ssi's waist.

,E, Elena-ssi? There's a chair next to you.'

,Maybe it's because I'm small... but this is more comfortable. Yujin. Let's get to the point. Did you get any information?'

,I found out that the person who introduced the tutor to your mother was Baron Dumple. When I went to find that bastard and grilled him, the name Duke Suui came up.'

,Baron Dumple and Duke Suui, huh. Baron Dumple... as far as I remember, his entire family was attacked by bandits while traveling and annihilated before this world became Atlantis. Now that I see it, it wasn't the work of bandits, but the work of Duke Suui. He finished cleaning up before I even inherited the family... It's no wonder I couldn't find him.'

Elena-ssi let out a cold laugh. In contrast, Ricardo-nim's expression was stiff.

,Duke Suui? Why? He's the same Empire Ogong, but there aren't many points of contact.'

,Father. You don't have to have a grudge to stab someone in the back. It could be to keep our family in check... or they may have judged you to be dangerous. You are currently the most active of the current Empire Ogong, and you directly cleaned up the cult that was eating away at the Empire, didn't you? Duke Suui may have a grudge against you.'

,...That was His Majesty the Emperor's order..., no, wait. Is there a connection between the cult and Duke Suui?'

,Yes. It's not certain, but there are rumors. Ah, there probably aren't any such rumors in this era.'

,...Elena-ssi. Yujin. What are you planning to do from now on?'

,Originally, I was going to quietly gather information. But... this idiot ruined it all.'

Elena-ssi turned her body. The ends of her eyebrows were slightly raised. Elena-ssi grabbed my cheeks with both hands and shook them.

,Since it's come to this, I'm going to go wild in this idiot's way. Yes. I'm going to storm Duke Suui's territory and hear the truth directly from Duke Suui.'

Elena-ssi took her hands off my cheeks. My cheeks are a little numb. I resisted the urge to take revenge on her.

,...Currently, His Majesty the Emperor has summoned the Empire Ogong. An entire city that was fine was annihilated in a matter of hours, so the entire Empire is on alert.'

I chuckled.

,That's good. I'll just have to take care of Duke Suui and kill the Emperor too. I didn't like the Emperor very much either.'

Elena-ssi got off the chair, looked straight at Ricardo-nim, and said.

,Father. Please help me. As the head of the Valderth family, I need to know the truth.'

Ricardo-nim tightly closed his eyes. He clenched his fists so tightly that the blood drained from them as he agonized, then opened his eyes again.

,No one can covet the Valderth family. I'll help you, Elena-ssi.'
