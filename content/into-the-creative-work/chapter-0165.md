The arrow I shot flies towards Kang Seok-su.

The distance is about 500M. I wouldn't have been able to do this in the past, but now I have the Shooting Trait. Because of that, I've become quite confident in hitting things I throw these days.

I even imbued the arrow with mana, and Kang Seok-su, being a magician, isn't the type to move around a lot. It's not difficult to hit him.

,Just get hit properly just once.'

However, contrary to my wishes, Kang Seok-su raised his hand and created a magic barrier. The arrow I shot bounces off the barrier.

,Seeing how fast he reacted, he must have been paying attention to me from the start.'

In a way, it's only natural. After all, I'm the one who made this situation a mess. Kang Seok-su can't help but be wary of what I might do next.

I continued to shoot arrows. All seven were blocked by the magic barrier.

,It's not like Kang Seok-su's mana is infinite. He's using the barrier while also paying attention to the other guys, so he'll get even more tired. I'm at an advantage.'

Since I ran out of arrows, I discarded the bow without hesitation. And I chased after the Hunters trying to escape from this place and eliminated them.

,I'm watching with my eyes wide open, where do you think you're going to escape to?'

I look around.

The prairie, which was full of chaos, had become deserted in no time.

Now there are only 3 Hunters left. Me, Kang Seok-su, and Han Ha-rin-ssi. Everyone else has been eliminated. It was a big deal that Kang Seok-su prioritized attacking the allied Hunters.

,What should I do with those troublesome monsters?'

The Basalt Golem is fighting Kang Seok-su. The black body of the Basalt Golem is cracked all over. It's on the verge of breaking.

And the Little Kraken, who just eliminated a Hunter, finds me and comes closer. Only 4 tentacles are left, and red blood is flowing from the wound on its head. The overwhelming force it had when I first saw it is now gone.

,I think I can handle that much on my own.'

There's no way to control the Kraken, so it's better to get rid of it neatly before fighting Kang Seok-su. If I leave it alone, the Kraken might interfere at a crucial moment.

,Paeng... paeng... what was it. There was a four-character idiom to use in times like this, but I can't remember it. Ah, damn it. When this happens suddenly, it's hard to concentrate on the battle because I'm worried.'

I desperately racked my brain and remembered. *Tosa-gupaeng*. A hunting dog that has become useless is boiled and eaten.

,Squid. Let's taste you.'

I grabbed my sword and ran towards the Kraken.

•••

Dungeon Survival Reforged Studio.

,The Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team! Takes the C-rank boss monsters, the Little Kraken and the Basalt Golem, to the decisive battle...! How can they think of something like this....'

Jo Seok-hoo clicked his tongue.

And a little later, the Little Kraken and the Basalt Golem began to rampage in the decisive battle.

Jo Seok-hoo opened his mouth slightly. It was more of a mess than he thought.

,T, this! It's a cauldron of chaos! The battle on the prairie! The battle continues to break out! The alliance no longer makes sense! Even Hunter Kang Seok-su, a strong candidate for the win, is showing a flustered face!'

Jo Seok-hoo shouted excitedly. The situation was a mess, but from a viewer's point of view, it couldn't be more stimulating and fun.

Dungeon Survival Reforged will end soon. He had a strong feeling about it.

Then he suddenly looks at the director. What does the director think of this situation? Isn't he watching with a stiff face because of the messed up situation?

However, contrary to Jo Seok-hoo's expectations, the director was looking at the screen with a satisfied smile.

Dungeon Survival Reforged is not a real survival game. It's just a broadcast program.

A smooth survival? That doesn't attract much popularity. Why did they gather 150 Hunters? It's all for the battle.

This messed up situation is much more surprising and stimulating than Kang Seok-su's team winning smoothly.

The director sensed it. This scene will be remembered as the best scene of Dungeon Survival Reforged.

,......Hoo. Was it really necessary to do it that way? It's too extreme.'

Hong Soo-won sighed as if frustrated. Baek Ji-eun opened her mouth right away.

No one here didn't know about the conflict between Hong Soo-won and Baek Ji-eun.

,The Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team was ostracized from the alliance. To be honest, I thought the Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team was over from the moment the alliance was formed. But the Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team changed the situation with a brilliant idea. Who could have thought of bringing in the Little Kraken and the Basalt Golem in that situation.'

,I admit it's brilliant, but strictly speaking, that's a monster shift. It doesn't look good. And it's too dangerous. There are two C-rank boss monsters in one place. It wouldn't be strange if an accident happened.'

Monster shift.

It refers to the malicious act of passing on monsters to other Hunters. It's a clear crime. If caught, it doesn't just end with a fine.

,This is Dungeon Survival. The Hunters here are competitors. It's right to see it as a strategy, not a crime. And there are Hunter Association employees there. They are at least C-rank Hunters, and there are many B-rank Hunters. There are also a few A-rank Hunters. Even so, do you think it's dangerous, Hong Soo-won-ssi?'

Hong Soo-won chose his words for a moment.

His Bluebird Guild is one of the top 10 guilds in Korea, but he can't help but be aware of the Association. In fact, the Association is the one that dominates the Hunter world. It's something to be avoided to be completely at odds with the Association.

,...I meant that an accident could happen. Of course, the possibility of an accident is low because the Association is there.'

Baek Ji-eun smiled.

,Association employees are working busily to eliminate even that small possibility of an accident. I, Baek Ji-eun... no, I will say it in the name of the Korean Hunter Association that Dungeon Survival Reforged is safe.'

Hong Soo-won closed his mouth.

If it was Baek Ji-eun's personal name, he would have nitpicked here and there, but the name of the Hunter Association was at stake. Even if he didn't like it, he couldn't be at odds with the Hunter Association over such a small matter.

The situation in the screen began to flow quickly.

,The alliance has collapsed! The allied Hunters are attacking each other, prioritizing their own survival!'

,Hmm. That's the right decision. The one who survives in that situation is likely to win. The best thing to do is to go to the rest area and wait for the situation to calm down. But... the situation is so urgent that there are few who can think that far.'

,The Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team has planned the strategy well. Hunter Seong Yujin is prioritizing chasing and killing the escaping Hunters, and Hunter Han Ha-rin is guarding the entrance to the rest area. The Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team knows. Now is the last chance.'

,Ah! The last allied Hunter is eliminated!'

,This... Hunter Kang Seok-su prepared too well. Originally, it should have been devastated by the Little Kraken and the Basalt Golem, but Kang Seok-su spent over 30 hours staying up all night to prepare the magic circle, which made the monsters seriously injured. There's a saying that the prepared magician is the strongest... Seeing Hunter Kang Seok-su makes me think that saying is true.'

,Hunter Kang Seok-su's Ice Strike magic shatters the Basalt Golem's body. And Hunter Seong Yujin finishes it off by stabbing the Little Kraken's eye with a sword. Now there are 3 people left alive. The Kuk Cheon Dae Hunter Team is numerically advantageous, but... as you all know, Hunter Kang Seok-su has a ,Resurrection' treasure.'

,Using Resurrection doesn't restore the lost stamina or consumed mana. It just replaces the Energy Protect with a new one. Hunter Kang Seok-su will definitely not forget and fight.'

,That's right. Ah! But Hunter Han Ha-rin is staggering!'

•••

Puk!

I stabbed the Little Kraken's golden eyes with my sword. The Little Kraken, who was wriggling and struggling, soon loses strength and droops down. It's dead.

,The Basalt Golem is also dead. Now all that's left is Kang Seok-su. I can deal with Kang Seok-su by attacking him together with Han Ha-rin-ssi.'

I looked towards Han Ha-rin-ssi to signal her.

My face hardened. Han Ha-rin-ssi was staggering. I could tell even from a distance that she wasn't in good condition.

,Han Ha-rin-ssi is too tired.'

She used gravity manipulation in the volcanic zone to fly the Basalt all the way here. She also played the role of guarding the entrance to the rest area. Unlike me, Han Ha-rin-ssi used her abilities continuously.

She's already at her limit.

,......I can't give up after coming this far. I'll fight Kang Seok-su even if I'm alone.'

I drew my sword and ran towards Kang Seok-su. The good thing is that most of the magic installed around him was used on the Kraken and the Basalt.

,If I fight 1v1, there's a chance. Acceleration.'

[Acceleration is used. It lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 3]

My body gets faster. I think it's more beneficial to use *Chalna* than Acceleration, but I have no choice but to use Acceleration to arrive quickly without giving Kang Seok-su time.

,So you're the last one. I never expected you to bring the Little Kraken and the Basalt Golem here.'

,It was a bit of a struggle to bring them.'

I looked to his right as I ran. Mana flickers and ice in the form of a sword appears.

,No matter how hard you try, I'm the one who will win in the end.'

The ice sword is fired.

I swung my sword and easily knocked away the ice sword. If I can't knock it away when it's flying straight at me from the front, I might as well quit being a Hunter.

,There's a limit to how much you can ignore me. With just this....'

I couldn't finish my sentence. Because the ice sword that I had clearly knocked away was flying towards me again.

,It's a magic called Isferth. There are only two cases where the ice sword stops. Either the ice breaks, or you fall.'

,Then I'll just smash it.'

This time, I used an excessive amount of mana to knock away the ice sword.

Kaang!

The ice sword didn't break. It was much harder than I thought.

,The strength of the ice sword is better than steel. It's impossible for you to break the ice sword with your strength. Just accept defeat.'

,Thinking about it, I don't think I need to break it.'

Kang Seok-su was right in front of me.

I swung my sword as if dancing. I knock away the ice sword flying at me and attack Kang Seok-su at the same time.

Kang Seok-su frowned and avoided my sword. It's not a coincidence.

,Rather than seeing my sword and dodging, it seems like he's predicting and dodging. Did he use the ability Sensory Amplification?'

His movements are clumsy, but he's dodging all of my attacks. It doesn't make sense unless it's Sensory Amplification.

,.......'

Kang Seok-su quietly flicked his left hand. I didn't miss that small signal and quickly stepped back. A wall of fire soared in the place where I was.

,You have good instincts.'

,You have good senses.'

Kaang!

I knock away the flying ice sword. It's not threatening, but it's annoying.

,Let's end this quickly, even if I have to push myself a little.'

At that time. I saw Han Ha-rin-ssi, who was barely hanging on at the end of my vision, gesturing something to me. I understood her sign without moving my eyes as much as possible.

Kaang!

I knock away the ice sword, kick off the ground, and approach Kang Seok-su closely. It was the moment Kang Seok-su stepped back. Han Ha-rin-ssi used a gravity field. My body and Kang Seok-su's body become heavy.

,I'm always sparring with Han Ha-rin-ssi. I'm used to the gravity field.'

Moreover, I knew in advance through Han Ha-rin-ssi's sign.

,I'll end it here. And if he revives, I'll use *Chalna* 3 times in a row....'

My eyes widened as I was about to swing my sword. Kang Seok-su is smiling triumphantly. I sensed that something had gone wrong.

,...No way. Did he read Han Ha-rin-ssi's sign without seeing it with Sensory Amplification?!'

Kang Seok-su reaches out his left hand towards me. A red magic circle is drawn on his palm.

,*Chalna*!'

The accelerated world gets faster. There was no time to save *Chalna*. My sword heads for Kang Seok-su's neck.

Kang Seok-su leaned back and dodged my sword. He dodged it even though I used *Chalna*.

,I knew his physical abilities were better than mine. But to dodge it like this... Did he read that short moment with Sensory Amplification!'

I gritted my teeth and activated *Chalna* again. I can't come this far and back down without doing anything.

I forcibly changed the trajectory of the sword. From horizontal to vertical. The recoil will be handled by the Energy Protect.

The sword cut Kang Seok-su's body from top to bottom.

,Keueup?!'

Kang Seok-su knelt down. His Energy Protect turned red.
