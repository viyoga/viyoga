I rose from the shadows. It felt like I had been asleep for a long time.

My body felt lighter than before I sank into the shadows. I took a step. I could feel surging power. The center of the power was my heart.

Thud.

Something fell from my body. It was dead skin. Seeing that, I realized I had undergone a complete transformation.

,"Are you feeling unwell anywhere?"

Yuria, who had been quietly waiting, asked.

,"I feel great. Like I was just born. The fact that my heart is pounding... must be because of you."

I sent her a subtle glance. A smile formed on Yuria's lips.

,"I adjusted your body with the heart as the focus. Just by breathing, mana will naturally flow in and out of your body, Master-nim."

,"So, I'll get stronger just by breathing?"

,"It's not to that extent. The recovery speed of your body and your stamina will increase."

Yuria said firmly.

That alone is more than enough.

I'm still unsure about my power, but I think it's at least at the upper or highest level of an Aura Master.

Guided by Yuria, I came out of the administrator's area.

According to her, I had been asleep for about fifteen days. I was surprised because I had been asleep longer than I thought.

I came to the castle and was shocked when I looked around. The labyrinth was collapsing little by little. Yuria's shadow chains were holding onto the collapsing labyrinth, maintaining it.

,"We need to leave this labyrinth quickly."

,"We can still hold out comfortably for about two more days."

,"There's no need for you to suffer. Let's prepare immediately and get out of the labyrinth. I'm worried that we've stayed in the labyrinth for too long."

I returned to the castle. The maids had already finished preparing to leave. They had anticipation rather than regret.

In front of me, 23 adventurers were kneeling.

They were the adventurers who survived in the labyrinth. Rabit had cleared the labyrinth's floors and brought them down here. At the very front of them was Tatari, the Adventurer's Guild Master. Tatari looked up at me confidently with strength in his eyes.

,"Count-kakka. I will admit to all the crimes I have committed."

,"You're too confident for a criminal admitting his crimes. What are you relying on to act like that?"

,"I will give you everything in the labyrinth city of Rabit."

,"Tatari. You are just the master of the Adventurer's Guild. The labyrinth city is not yours. You are merely the city's representative."

,"Use me. I have the connections and abilities I have built up until now. I will serve Count-kakka for the rest of my life. I will make everything in this city belong to Count-kakka. The city's adventurers will become Count-kakka's property. The adventurers will become Count-kakka's soldiers, and the citizens will sing of Count-kakka's glory. I will make it so."

,"Ooh. That's not appealing at all."

,"...The Adventurer's Guild isn't just in Rabit. Adventurers are spread throughout the entire continent. Use me to gain the power of the adventurers."

I swung my leg.

Bbak!

Tatari, hit in the temple, fell to the side. I stepped on Tatari's head and sneered.

,"You adventurers are mistaken if you think you're great. Adventurer. It's a fancy name. But what is it really? Aren't you just mercenaries chasing after money?"

,"We are! We are different from mercenaries chasing money! We maintain the monster population! We adventurers are the ones protecting this continent!"

He said with a vein popping out of his neck while being trampled on. It was just funny. If adventurers were such great beings, adventurers would be treated better than knights.

,"I'm tired of hearing your nonsense. You couldn't let go of everything until the moment you die."

,"...I'll let go. I'll lick your shoes if you want! Please spare me! Count-kakka! I! I am useful!"

,"It's too late. Too late. You should have come out like that from the first time we met."

I took my foot off Tatari and looked around at the maids. I checked the number of people. I was relieved that there were no casualties.

,"Master-nim. How about a firing squad? We can hang him up and riddle him with bullets. Turn him into a rag."

,"Hanging is appropriate."

,"Beheading for a traitor."

,"Burning at the stake."

,"I don't even want to see his face, so let's just bury him."

The maids each gave their opinions. It seems they had a lot built up against the adventurers. They must have been stressed from managing the adventurers in Doan while I was away.

,"That's enough. It's a waste of time."

At my words, the maids fell silent. In Melissa's case, she glared as if she couldn't believe it.

,"...You're sparing him? Wait. Who are you? Are you a doppelganger! You bastard! Where is the real Master-nim?!"

,"Melissa."

Yuria quietly called Melissa.

,"Madam. This is not a joke. Is that really Master-nim? Did Madam perhaps modify Master-nim's brain?"

,"Melissa. Master-nim's intention is that there is no need to spend time and effort to kill these bugs. The labyrinth is about to collapse."

,"Ah. So, there's no need to kill them, and if we leave them alone, they'll die with this labyrinth."

The adventurers' faces changed completely. They felt the vague death coming much closer. They approached me on their knees, bound by ropes.

,"Count-kakka! Count-kakka!"

,"Please save us!"

,"We were wrong! We were wrong!"

They were crying and snotting. I didn't feel any sympathy at all. I kicked away the ones rushing at me and went outside the castle. Rabit, in the form of Grisha, was standing outside the castle. Rabit's gaze was directed at Yuria.

,"Administrator-nim. I have created a door to go outside. You can go out through there."

There was a door I hadn't seen before on one side of the castle wall. I checked the door and nodded.

,"Rabit. Do you have any regrets?"

,"No, I don't."

Rabit answered immediately.

Yuria slightly bowed to Rabit.

,"Thank you, Rabit."

,"Administrator Yuria-nim is stronger than Grisha-nim. Everything will work out well. I hope that the future is full of light. Because there must be light for shadows to exist."

,"I already have light."

,"...Then that's a relief."

I opened the door and went outside the labyrinth. When I turned around, I could see the collapsing labyrinth through the space. Rabit's figure was not visible.

The screams of the adventurers left there faintly reached me.

Kwajik, Kwajijik!

The entrance to the labyrinth cracked and then collapsed. With this, the labyrinth had completely disappeared.

I looked around. The adventurers who had been waiting at the entrance to the labyrinth were flustered and didn't know what to do.

,'The number of adventurers is small. Did the guys related to the guild master run away? They're quick to notice.'

Even if that wasn't the case, it was natural to leave the city since the labyrinth was blocked. The foundation of this city is the labyrinth. If they can't use the labyrinth, there's no reason to stay.

,"Count-kakka."

I was looking at the sky, contemplating the fate of this city. Someone called me. It was the voice of an old man. It was a soft and stable tone, so it wasn't unpleasant to listen to. I could tell just from his voice. He's someone who has dealt with people of high status a lot.

There was a middle-aged man who looked to be in his 50s. His face was fox-like, and the clothes he was wearing looked ordinary but were neat. He was definitely not an ordinary man.

,"...Your face looks familiar."

,"I am Trevire Blurk. I have come to repay my debt to Count-kakka."

The man's atmosphere was unusual. I couldn't just tell him to get lost, saying it was some kind of trick.

,"I'm sorry. I don't know who you are."

,"I understand. It's been quite a while. The Pfrek Bandit Group and Artel Village. Have you perhaps forgotten?"

,"As if I could forget that. I know who you are now."

I glanced at Yuria. Yuria was still. There was no change in her expression.

Artel Village.

It was the village where Yuria and I first met. The Pfrek Bandit Group attacked that place and killed Yuria's mother. Of course, I finished avenging the Pfrek Bandit Group.

Trevire Blurk.

He was the branch manager of the Information Guild at the time. Back then, things got a bit twisted when an Information Guild member betrayed us.

Trevire had promised to give me special mid-grade information for free as compensation for that apology.

To be honest, I had completely forgotten about it. Even without the Information Guild, I had the information from the original work, and when I needed small pieces of information, I paid for it. I already had enough money.

,"I remember. You had a somewhat unlucky face, but it's changed quite a bit now."

,"Haha. I hear that often. I used to be a bit more energetic in the past. But that doesn't mean my enthusiasm for this matter has disappeared. My enthusiasm is burning even more than before."

,"That's a relief. You said you came to repay your debt? I don't have high expectations, but I'll listen. What kind of information did you bring?"

I really didn't have any expectations.

Even without the Information Guild's information, there was no problem. The information about the Demon King that I really wanted was something that the Information Guild wouldn't have.

Trevire glanced around.

,"May I speak here?"

I moved my mana. An invisible wall of mana surrounded him, me, and the maids.

,"...Amazing. To create a mana wall so easily...."

,"The maids are my hands and feet. You can trust them, so speak."

,"The city of Pters has been captured by the Magic Union. The Pruker retainers have taken refuge in the territory of Tebrah. More than 2,000 soldiers have died, but the Golden Rose Knights and Lady Ellain have moved to Tebrah and are holding out."

,"...What is the Magic Union?"

,"It is a union of magic towers. It is a force that has risen to drive out the evil Count Pruker, for the peace of the continent, and for the Kingdom of Rapelli."

,"Princess Aileen said she would join hands with the magic towers... so things have turned out like this."

,"Princess Aileen is just one of the justifications. The magic towers were already preparing to antagonize Count-kakka."

,"That's probably true. Seeing how quickly they're showing results. What's the situation in Tebrah?"

,"It is surrounded, but it is holding out well. Tebrah's defense capabilities are surprisingly good. Perhaps because it has a port, supplies are being delivered smoothly."

In terms of territory strength alone, Tebrah is stronger than Pters, where the Pruker Count's mansion is located.

,'They enslaved dwarves to make various things, and Tebrah is the starting point for the maids. They also have modern weapons... and there's a naval base at the port. No matter how much magic there is, it can't be captured in a short period of time. The missiles there alone can wipe out most territories.'

Still, I'm annoyed that they were attacked. Perhaps Tebrah is also in a dangerous state. There is a limit to the speed of information transfer in this world.

I want to use a space-time scroll to return to Tebrah right now.

,"Pters' forces are by no means weak. My brother, Kyle Pruker, who is an Aura Master, should still be there. What happened to him?"

,"Kyle Pruker is......"
