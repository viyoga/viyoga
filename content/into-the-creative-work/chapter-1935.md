Ttuk-ttuk.

I walked down the hallway.

Bergman, my target, was sure to be on the top floor. Normally, I would have taken the elevator or stairs up immediately. It would be a disaster if Bergman were to escape.

,Bergman can't escape.'

This was Bergman's only residence. Bergman, already branded a traitor, leaving the corporation? There was no way the Imperial Army watching this place would allow it.

Bergman's only way to survive was to somehow kill me, publicly denounce the Imperial Army, and buy time.

[Detection]

I used detection magic. I could feel the enemies' locations in real-time, as well as the building's internal structure. However, there were places that felt like they were covered in fog. Places that had been specially treated to block detection magic on purpose.

Too obvious to be a trap.

,This is a corporate building. Most of it is filled with corporate technology.'

Most corporations have several places where magic is blocked. They've prepared to prevent their technology from being stolen by magic.

I turned my steps towards that place. Since I was raiding the corporation anyway, I might as well take some technology.

,Since it's a corporation that's been built up to this size, the technology must be worth a lot. It might even be technology I need for my business.'

A large iron door blocked my path. It wasn't ordinary iron. It was a special wall made with special technology and materials to greatly increase magic resistance.

[Telekinesis]

I tried to forcibly open the iron door using telekinesis. But as soon as the telekinesis touched the iron door, it scattered.

-You stupid bastard. Do you know how much money we poured into that room to stop thieving mages like you? You seem to be eyeing our corporation's technology, but give up and come up here. Your grave isn't here, it's upstairs.

Bergman's voice echoed from the speaker on the ceiling. He was checking my location with cameras installed everywhere.

I thought about smashing the cameras, but I stopped. I came for an all-out war anyway. I had no intention of moving while hiding. It was better to act confidently and mess with his head.

[Air Strike]

Kwaaaaaang!

The wind created by magic struck the iron door. The aftermath became a powerful wind, causing my hair to flutter.

,Just because it's a wall and door with high magic resistance doesn't mean there are no gaps. I can exert physical force indirectly.'

[Air Strike]

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

The seemingly sturdy iron door couldn't withstand the impact and began to dent. Even though Air Strike was only a 2nd-grade magic, I, who was using it, was a 5th-grade mage. It was only natural that its power was much greater.

[Air Strike]

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

The dented iron door flew backward and fell to the floor. I, who had entered, curled up the corners of my lips.

,The air itself is different.'

The white floor and walls were for cleanliness and hygiene. The temperature was cool thanks to the air conditioner that was blasting. A little further ahead, I saw artificial organ incubators. However, the organs were slightly different in appearance from what I knew.

,Modified organs.'

Artificial organ technology. The technology to create human organs was common in this world. You didn't have to kidnap ordinary people and steal their organs; you could create organs with just science and technology.

,Artificial organs themselves don't make much money.'

That's why there are modified artificial organs. Organs with special powers made by imitating the organs of monsters. For example, a dark mage's Dark Hole. Just by transplanting it into your body, you can gain special powers.

[Wind Cutter]

Blades of wind shatter the incubators standing in a row. The incubators burst, and the culture medium and artificial organs spill onto the floor.

-That crazy bastard dares!!!

It must seem to Bergman like money is being poured onto the floor. Because special artificial organs are expensive.

,I'm going to smash everything and take anything that looks like data. I can sell it to other companies for a high price. Come down if you want to stop me.'

He won't be able to come down.

Bergman must have his own plans, and he's most worried about a diversion.

First, I'll take any data chips I see. My pockets became heavy.

That's when it happened.

I felt a presence stealthily approaching my senses. A very faint presence.

,An assassin.'

There was no sound, and their body was transparent, as if they had used magic.

,But even so, their existence doesn't disappear, so they can't hide from someone with keen senses. Just like how I caught them now.'

I pretended not to notice on purpose. The moment I turned my body and tried to go outside, the assassin swung their sword.

The barrier split as easily as tofu.

,A Barrier Eater.'

A special weapon focused solely on eliminating a mage's barrier.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 11]

I dodged the attack by stepping back. The eyes of the assassin wearing the special suit shook. It was understandable. Even I thought I had dodged it too cleanly.

,It would be weirder if I couldn't dodge it with Instant.'

[Absolute Polymorph]

I transformed my right hand into a hand cannon and aimed it at the bastard. Just before I pulled the trigger, the assassin jumped upwards. They stuck their feet to the ceiling and moved dazzlingly, approaching me.

[Air Strike]

Kwaaaaaang!

The assassin, hit by the shockwave, flew backward and rolled on the floor. Sparks flew as if the suit was damaged.

,How stupid. Didn't they know they could be hit by the shockwave too?

,I'm fine. Did you really think I would rely solely on one barrier for my body?

The assassin didn't seem surprised, but threw their sword at me. The sword stopped in front of my eyes. Not a barrier. Telekinesis. I surrounded my whole body with telekinesis to replace the barrier. That was why I was unharmed by the shockwave of Air Strike.

It requires more concentration than using a barrier, but it's a great help when the barrier is useless like now.

,...I heard you were an alchemist, but now that I see it, you're just a battle mage.'

The assassin got up as if he was feeling dejected. There was no reason to just watch.

I shot the assassin with the hand cannon. The assassin's upper body flew away. I moved on, leaving the corpse behind. If there was anything that looked expensive, I smashed it no matter what. Bergman, who was watching me through the cameras, must have a terrible case of indigestion.

• • •

I advanced openly.

Traps and enemies greeted me, but they couldn't stop me.

That was the scary thing about battle mages. Mages who specialize in combat. The power of magic itself was a powerful force that was difficult for ordinary people to understand. It wasn't for nothing that high-level battle mages were treated as tactical weapons.

,Attack!!

As soon as I got to the 7th floor of the building, a dozen enemies attacked me at once. I had foreseen the enemies' presence with detection magic, so I used the magic I had been preparing.

[Explosion]

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaang!

An explosion occurred, sweeping away the enemies.

I used explosion magic, regardless of whether the building was destroyed or not. The effect was excellent. All the bastards who had rushed at me at once were caught up in it. Most of them died instantly. Even the lucky ones who survived were not unscathed.

I shot the surviving enemies in the head with a pistol. I had to make sure they were dead. I couldn't risk letting them live and then getting up and targeting me from behind.

,Especially since I'm alone here. I need to reduce the variables.'

Killing, smashing, and taking, I arrived at the top floor.

What greeted me on the top floor was Bergman and his family. One woman and three men. Bergman, the eldest, and his siblings.

Bergman, the eldest, held a sword in the center, the second held a gun, the third was a battle mage, and the fourth, the only woman, clenched her fists tightly.

,So, you finally made it all the way up here.'

Bergman, the eldest, said. His voice was full of annoyance. He glared at me with eyes full of murderous intent.

,That's why I said, hyung-nim, we should have gone down and dealt with him ourselves earlier. You can't trust mercenaries.'

The second said, pulling back the bolt of his rifle.

,As a fellow battle mage, I respect you. I could never become a battle mage as skilled as you, even if I died and came back to life... But in the end, you're the one who's going to die. You must have consumed a lot of stamina and mana coming all the way here. It's easy to hunt a tired beast.'

The third spoke at length. But he was right. I was somewhat exhausted from coming all the way here.

,Oppa-deul. Let's kill that bastard instead of talking nonsense. I'll go first.'

The fourth, the only woman, clenched her fists and rushed at me. Scales sprouted on the woman's skin.

,Dragon scales? A dragonkin with dragon factors?

The fourth's fist pounded on the barrier. The barrier swayed, but it held. Her physical abilities were lacking for a dragonkin.

One hypothesis came to mind.

,Did she have a dragon organ transplanted?

,I replaced a human pancreas with a dragon's pancreas. It's possible to do things like this!

Her fingernails grew long. Her fingernails tore through the barrier and aimed for my neck. But they were blocked by the second barrier and bounced off.

,Even if it's not a dragon factor, it's strong enough to easily neutralize one barrier. If I don't go all out, I might be the one who gets hurt.'

I took out the Golden Gift from my pocket. I put the potion for escape, which I had obtained from the ancient ruins, into my mouth without hesitation.

The potion was as sweet as honey. And my body began to overflow with power. A potion that increases all stats by 50% for 3 minutes.

Feeling my senses sharpen, I used magic.

[Chain Lightning]

The lightning bolt that started from my fingertips spread to the Bergman brothers, starting with the nearest fourth.

The third used a barrier, but Chain Lightning easily pierced through the barrier and electrocuted them all at once.

,They're not dying and are holding on, so they must have some skill.'

I reached out and grabbed the head of the nearest fourth. She swung her fist at me, counterattacking. It was only blocked by the barrier.

,The barrier is stronger than before?! What did this bastard eat?!

,I ate something good. It's a bit boring that your last words are just that.'

[Explosion]

Kwaaaaaang!

An explosion occurred. Black smoke rose, but it was immediately carried away by the wind that came in through the open window.

, Ugh, ugh...

,You endured this?

Slightly surprised, I used magic again.

[Explosion]

Kwaaaaaang!

[Explosion]

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaang!

The woman's body shattered and scattered in all directions.
