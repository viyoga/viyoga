I was summoned to an endlessly sprawling plain where clouds drifted lazily overhead.

I wasn't the only one summoned. Across this plain, participants of the Martial Arts Tournament preliminaries stretched out in every direction. Their numbers were overwhelming. At a glance, it looked like there could be hundreds of thousands of them.

「You must run the marathon.」

「Time remaining until preliminary ends: 1:59:57」

「↑」

The quick-witted ones immediately started running in the direction the arrows pointed.

Among them, the cunning and capable ones took to the skies using magic and abilities.

Spears materialized from the blue sky and flew toward those soaring through the air. Sharp spearheads pierced through their bodies. Their screams spread across the field. Their bodies, bleeding and plummeting to the ground, vanished the moment their breath stopped.

"Aaaagh!"

"Damn it! What the hell is this!"

"I'm dying! Aaaagh!"

Even if you dodged one spear, the next one came flying. If you kept dodging, the spears grew faster or their numbers multiplied.

"Don't fly! Just don't fly and you're fine!"

"Damn it, System! You should've warned us beforehand that flying was prohibited!"

Most descended to the ground and began running on two legs. Some, watching for openings, took to the sky. They dodged falling spears, and when the spears grew faster and more numerous, they descended to the ground, then took flight again, repeating the cycle.

They were flying while gauging the System's reactions. Flying for brief bursts seemed less efficient than simply running.

'Still, the fast ones have already covered a kilometer in the air.'

Even so, those who would reach the finish line by flying would be few enough to count on one hand. The closer to the finish line, the more vicious the System's interference became.

"If I can't fly... then I'll just ride a horse! Hyah!"

"Help me, Sylphy!"

Some summoned horses or spirits and galloped forward. Of course, the System didn't permit that either. Spears rained down on them as well. Blood and screams erupted everywhere.

「Many Divine Thrones are laughing as they watch the tournament.」

"Ohhhhh! Divine Thrones! Please watch over this Crossen!"

"Kill that bastard? Understood! Divine Throne!"

"...You want me to dance here? Seriously?"

Among those participating in the Martial Arts Tournament, almost none were purely aiming for victory.

The majority entered knowing that the Divine Thrones' eyes would be fixed on the tournament. If they were lucky, they could forge a contract with a decent Divine Throne. If not that, they'd been pushed into it by a Divine Throne.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool proposes a mission to you.」

「Mission Condition 1. Do not move for 5 minutes.」

「Mission Success Reward: 30,000 AP」

「Mission Failure Penalty: None」

「Will you accept the mission?」

Prank-Obsessed Fool.

That true name belonged to Loki, the God of Deception. Naturally, this mission was packed with the intent to screw me over. The moment I accepted it, the others nearby would attack me.

'Most of the Norse gods don't like me.'

There were multiple reasons, but the biggest was probably that I was the contractor of the Lord of the Heavens. It was common knowledge in this world that the Norse faction of Divine Thrones and the Olympus faction despised each other.

「The Lord of the Heavens glares at Prank-Obsessed Fool.」

「Prank-Obsessed Fool speaks with a smile.」

「Smile. It's a festival today, isn't it?」

「Light-Footed One shakes his head and warily watches Prank-Obsessed Fool.」

「Prank-Obsessed Fool waves his hand toward Light-Footed One.」

The tension was already extraordinary.

Normally, such blatant hostility almost never occurred. In this world, Divine Thrones weren't limited to just the Norse and Olympus factions. If they openly fought and bloodied each other, only other Divine Throne factions would benefit from the fisherman's gain.

But the Martial Arts Tournament was an exception. During the tournament, even if you died, you didn't truly die. It was the perfect stage for showing off their avatars and flexing their pride.

"Only 30,000 AP? Do the Norse gods not have any AP? If it were an Olympus god, the minimum would've been 100,000 AP."

「The Invisible Sword lets out a scoff at your insolent attitude.」

「Rainbow Foam smiles at your wisdom.」

The Invisible Sword was Frey, and Rainbow Foam was Aphrodite.

Rainbow Foam called me wise, but from a human's standpoint, provoking a god was not a wise move.

'In my case, I'm already doomed to never have good relations with the Norse gods anyway. While I'm at it, building trust with the Olympus gods is the better play.'

「The King of the Demon Realm chews on popcorn.」

The King of the Demon Realm was merely spectating. That bastard would surely watch from a step back, even if it involved matters related to his own faction.

"Prank-Obsessed Fool. Out of respect for your request, I'll specially refrain from moving for the next 5 minutes. Do whatever you want."

「The majority of Norse faction Divine Thrones are enraged at you.」

That was when it happened.

The gazes of those around me converged on me.

"Is it true? If I kill that man, you'll give me 100,000 AP?"

"New traits and skills...!"

"I will definitely kill him. I'll prove myself, so have the contract ready!"

Dozens of people holding weapons surrounded me. They warily eyed each other while staring at me with gazes full of killing intent and greed.

The Norse Divine Thrones had issued missions to the people around me to kill me. Of course, not everyone nearby had received the mission.

"I don't know. I don't want to carry out an absurd mission."

"My apologies. The Divine Throne I'm contracted with doesn't wish to get involved in this matter."

"I don't want to make an enemy of the Phantom Duke's husband for such a meager reward. You say there won't be retaliation since it happened during the tournament? Haha. How can you guarantee that?"

The fame of Sung Yujin, affiliated with A-Plan Legion rather than Cheonma, hadn't spread as much as I'd thought.

In the Legion's case, fame inevitably concentrated on the representative, Kang Myeongjin. The position of being the Phantom Duke's husband was the same. Elena, to begin with, tended to find public attention bothersome.

The ones surrounding me didn't attack immediately and instead watched each other. They looked like hyenas trying to safely snatch the spoils.

'How stupid. They should've just rushed me all at once.'

That would've been the method with at least some chance of killing me. Of course, I had no intention whatsoever of letting them have their way.

"Damn it. What the hell is this frustrating bullshit. Cowardly bastards! Watch how this body handles things!"

One man leaped out with a sword in hand. He was a mercenary with no Legion affiliation. Blue aura coalesced along his blade.

"Hey, friend. This is just me following a Divine Throne's mission. No hard feelings. We don't even really die here anyway."

"You took the words right out of my mouth. No hard feelings."

Lightning Discharge.

Blue lightning bolts shot out in all directions from my body.

More than half were electrocuted and died upon contact. They were eliminated and vanished. But the ones who remained were the real deal. Those with the fortitude to withstand the lightning.

"Stop gawking and attack!!"

I summoned the Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory. The moment I gripped the hilt, red lightning flashed.

Thunder Heaven Style — Lightning Flash.

I cut down the mercenary charging at me head-on, as well as the bodies of those surrounding me. They died with expressions of shock.

I didn't manage to kill them all, but the survivors flinched and backed away from me. No matter how much the tournament prevented true death, none of them wanted to die a pointless death like this.

"What the hell... I didn't even see the sword move."

"He's a monster... He's not someone we can handle."

They abandoned their missions and started running to pass the preliminaries.

'In the end, they're just mediocrities who get jerked around by Divine Thrones. The truly strong don't get pushed around even when they're mindful of the Divine Thrones.'

Four minutes left. I stood still and waited for time to pass.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool clicks his tongue.」

「The Lord of the Heavens is pleased.」

I watched the people running. Many members of A-Plan Legion, including Kang Myeongjin and Ju Seohyeon, had entered the Martial Arts Tournament. It wasn't surprising since I'd already known.

'The ones advancing to the main round will probably be Kang Myeongjin and Ju Seohyeon.'

Those who had been watching me from a slight distance also ran toward the finish line. They were the contractors of the Norse Divine Thrones. Beyond them, I spotted other familiar faces.

The remaining ones were troublemakers with no real interest in the tournament. They started fighting from the very beginning, following the missions their Divine Thrones had given them.

The sound of weapons clashing. The sound of magic exploding. The sound of arrows burrowing into flesh.

Some attacked me without knowing their place. A single arrow flew toward my head. An arrow imbued with silver energy.

Thunder Heaven Style — Electromagnetic Barrier.

The arrow failed to penetrate the electromagnetic barrier and fell to the ground.

I looked at the archer who had fired the arrow. The distance was over 600 meters, but when I focused mana into my eyes to enhance my vision, I could see as clearly as if they were 10 meters away.

My eyes met the archer's. The startled archer gave an awkward smile. I beckoned him with my finger.

Kwaaaaaang!

Lightning struck from the clear sky and split the archer's body in half. The lightning that lit up the grassland in pale blue silenced the surroundings for a moment.

They glanced at me once, then pretended not to see and resumed fighting among themselves.

The five minutes I'd mentioned had passed.

There was no one around me. The ones who were going to die had died, and the ones who lived had run off. All that remained on this grassland were traces of battle.

「The Lord of the Heavens expects victory.」

"I have no intention of being eliminated in the preliminaries, let alone the main round."

I stirred the mana within me.

The lightning energy originating from my dantian spread throughout my body, amplifying my physical abilities. My body grew lighter.

I took a short breath and ran toward the direction of the arrows.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

With one step, a fine electric current grabbed my body and pushed me from behind. By the second step, I'd already broken past 30 meters. Since I wasn't flying through the air, the System didn't restrict me.

The grassland that seemed like it would stretch on forever had an end. At that end was a maze. A maze blocked by massive walls. A maze teeming with all manner of traps.

I caught my breath briefly in front of the maze. Flying Thunder God was fast, but it drained a lot of stamina.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool looks forward to the spectacle about to unfold.」

I knew the God of Mischief had set up pranks in this maze through his contractor. Especially since I'd started five minutes late, his contractor would've had ample time to rig it.

'He's probably plastered it with every trap imaginable.'

Looking at the maze walls, I could see wedge-shaped markings. But those were fake markings.

'I can't destroy walls protected by the System. If I try to run over the walls or fly, the System will naturally attack. Enduring those attacks is difficult even for me. I haven't even covered half the distance yet, so I need to manage my stamina.'

After catching my breath, I stepped into the maze with electricity coiling around my body.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

The shortest route to the maze's exit was already drawn in my mind. Following the fine electric currents accordingly, I ran without any loss of speed. Flying Thunder God, running along the current's path, had no problem with sharp 90-degree turns.

I could see strange symbols carved into the walls and floor. Traps prepared by Prank-Obsessed Fool's contractor.

「Using Deception.」

I deceived the traps with my unique trait, Deception (SSS).

The traps failed to recognize me. The traps didn't activate.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool stares at you intently.」

Come to think of it, I remembered killing Loki, Thor, and Odin when I became Zeus and underwent the trial. I'd killed Loki and my Deception rank had risen.

'Since it happened inside the Olympus trial, the real Loki himself probably doesn't know. Same for Thor and Odin. No, Odin probably knows.'

I used a very tiny fraction of the power of foresight. I didn't see the future.

The remaining power of foresight I possessed didn't simply show the future — it elevated my intuition as well.

Anyway, since I was using Deception (SSS), aside from simple physical traps, nothing reacted to me. My physical abilities were exceptional even among the Outcasts, so physical traps were equally meaningless.

As I went deeper into the maze, I saw participants wandering lost.

"Damn it. Where the hell am I supposed to go?!"

"Why isn't the left-hand rule working either?!"

Ignoring their despair, I ran forward. I spotted Ju Seohyeon. I could see her running ahead.

Since I was using Deception (SSS), Ju Seohyeon also failed to notice my presence.

I grinned and slapped Ju Seohyeon's ass as I passed her.

Smaaack!

The startled Ju Seohyeon reflexively drew her sword and swung. Unfortunately for her, I was already past her.

"Sung Yujin!!!"

I blew a finger heart to Ju Seohyeon as she shouted my name.

Thirty seconds later.

Having started running the latest, I was the first among the participants to escape the maze.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool shouts.」

「This is cheating!!」

It was a bit of a cheat play, admittedly.

After all, I'd seen the future and grasped every trap and path in the maze.

Even Kang Myeongjin doesn't know the maze's detailed routes or the types of traps in detail. Kang Myeongjin isn't a regressor or a returner. Right now, Kang Myeongjin is probably fighting vicious bandits.

"For a regressor like me, it's easy."

「Prank-Obsessed Fool's eyes go wide.」

Did he fall for it?

He seemed to have fallen for it. His head was probably spinning.

'Oops. Without meaning to, I made a joke that didn't exist in the future.'

I decided to confirm the future using the power of foresight after the preliminaries ended. The highest-tier gods can detect when I use the power of foresight to see the future, so I needed to refrain as much as possible during the early stages of the tournament.

Incidentally, those watching my screen in Atlantis probably couldn't hear what I just said. With Deception (SSS), not only my voice but even the shape of my lips would be read as something else.

「Prank-Obsessed Fool asks if you're a real regressor.」

I ignored that question. I had neither the loyalty nor the reason to answer.
