I followed Kang Jiwoo down the underground passage.

The only thing illuminating the dark passage was the small lantern in Kang Jiwoo's hand. The lantern's light was faint, not brightly illuminating the surroundings.

I examined the walls of the passage. The stone walls were smooth, and like the training center building in Busan, they were densely covered with bizarre, meaningless patterns.

,I feel bad since entering this passage.'

There were several reasons.

Perhaps because there was a hot spring nearby, the air was lukewarm and humid. It felt like being inside someone's mouth.

And the second reason was that I had no idea what was at the end of this passage.

"Yujin-ssi. You seem tense."

"Wouldn't anyone be tense going to an unknown place? It feels like we've been walking for quite a while, but there's no sign of arriving. How much further do we have to go?"

"Well, this is my first time walking this passage too... We should arrive soon, probably."

"Your first time?"

"Yes. The Japanese branch made this passage, and I'm in charge of Korea. I've been to Japan a few times, but this is the first time I've come for work like this."

"Please tell me what's at the end of this passage."

Unable to bear the frustration, I asked directly. Kang Jiwoo smiled slyly.

"See it with your own eyes, Yujin-ssi. Gwangshin-nim will want that too."

I got nothing. I sighed and walked on, step by step.

Then I noticed something strange. It was too quiet. There were 20 people here. Kang Jiwoo hadn't explicitly forbidden chatting. He even answered my questions kindly when I asked.

,...What? They're all spaced out.'

The followers, including Park Suho, were in a daze. Their eyes were unfocused, and drool was dripping from their open mouths. They seemed to have lost their reason.

"Park Suho."

I grabbed Park Suho's shoulder.

"Uh, uh... Yes, hyung. What's wrong?"

Park Suho snapped out of his dazed expression. But his eyes were still unfocused.

"...No. It's nothing."

It seemed like nothing I said would get through to him.

"It's nothing, got it."

Park Suho looked straight ahead and walked on, returning to his dazed expression.

,It's probably the same as brainwashing. The patterns on the passage walls are affecting Park Suho and the others. I'm fine because I'm completely conscious... Is Kang Jiwoo unaffected because he's the guide?'

My neck prickled. Instinct and intuition were telling me it was dangerous.

,Let's prepare in advance.'

I looked around. Kang Jiwoo at the front was busy moving forward, and the followers around him were all following Kang Jiwoo with blank faces. Even if I flipped them off right in front of their faces, the followers wouldn't react. They wouldn't look this way unless I physically touched them.

I took out my smartphone. Then I realized that the Wi-Fi wasn't working. Well, that's okay. The problem wasn't the Wi-Fi, but that the signal indicator was also at 0. In other words, there was no phone connection in this underground passage.

,What have they done to the passage?'

I was in a situation where I couldn't ask for outside help.

I used the Game World to change the items in my inventory to be suitable for combat. And just in case, I wrapped a spatial movement scroll around my wrist. If it seemed dangerous, I could use the spatial movement scroll to escape.

,Alright. Preparation complete. Let's go.'

I was fully prepared.

Even if the opponent was an A-class Hunter, I was confident that I could win by using item buffs. It wouldn't work against the top A-class guys, though... But I could probably run away.

,This is reality. If I die, it's over, so let's prioritize survival.'

If I died in the Game World, only the Game World would end, but not reality. I couldn't die yet when I hadn't even fully enjoyed the Game Life app.

Whoooooooosh.

"......!!"

I opened my mouth and gasped. It felt like the space had changed abruptly. I knew this sensation well.

,Dungeon! Did we just enter a dungeon?!'

I looked at Kang Jiwoo. Kang Jiwoo was walking as if nothing had happened.

"It seems like we're almost there now."

At the end of the passage was a door. A huge door. A black door with all sorts of ornate carvings, like the gate to hell.

Kang Jiwoo grabbed the door and pushed. Creeeeeak. The door slowly opened. Contrary to its heavy appearance, the door seemed light.

"Everyone. This is your first time coming to the Gwangwon Church's temple, right? Actually, it's my first time too. The temple is a place where only those who are permitted can enter. Gwangshin-nim will gain divine authority here and descend to save this world."

I felt like I was entering a dungeon again.

The world that opened up through the door was a completely different world.

A pure white, magnificent building built under a high, blue sky. Clear water flowing around it. Clean marble floors filling the area around the temple.

,Is that the Gwangwon Church's temple....'

I felt like I had entered a fantasy world. No, even in a fantasy world, buildings like that aren't common. I know well because I have experience going to fantasy worlds.

Followers of the Gwangwon Church were walking around the temple. There were not only Asians but also Westerners. I looked around and saw that there were three more black doors like the one we came through.

,I felt like I was entering a dungeon twice on the way here. Could it be... a dungeon within a dungeon?'

Even though I look like this, I'm a university Hunter major, so I pride myself on having a lot of knowledge about the Hunter world. Among that knowledge was also about dungeons that open within dungeons. The cause is unknown, but it occurs with very rare probability.

I checked my smartphone. The signal still wasn't working. And looking at the bright blue sky, unlike the night sky in Japan, I had to admit it. This is a dungeon. I don't know if it's a dungeon within a dungeon, but it's definitely a dungeon.

That's when it happened.

A group of people appeared from the main gate of the temple.

It was a middle-aged man with short blond hair and a white robe. Looking at the ornate clothing embroidered with gold thread, I realized that his status was extraordinary.

"Oh! Noble guest! We have been waiting for you to come to the temple!"

I was flustered. It was one thing that a foreigner suddenly spoke fluent Korean, but it was because he welcomed me far too much.

I slightly turned my gaze to the side and looked at Kang Jiwoo. Kang Jiwoo was smiling.

"That is Giorgio Micheli, the leader of the Gwangwon Church. It seems he came out to greet you personally to welcome you, Yujin-ssi."

Giorgio, the leader of the Gwangwon Church, ran up to me and hugged me. I felt terrible when a guy hugged me. I wanted to stab him right there and then, but... there were many people around. Especially the guys around the leader had the skills of an A-class Hunter.

"...It's uncomfortable, Leader-nim."

I relaxed my strength and pushed him away. Giorgio easily stepped back.

"Oh dear.... I made a mistake. I was too happy, that's all.... Please forgive me, noble guest."

"It's okay since I'm not hurt anywhere. I didn't know you would welcome me like this, Leader-nim."

"It's only natural since you are a noble guest. But there is confusion in the eyes of the noble guest."

I looked around.

Everyone in the Gwangwon Church was friendly to me. And I was curious about the identity of this temple. I didn't know the details, but it was certain that it was extraordinary.

"I'll ask you frankly. Where the hell is this place? Where on Earth is it?"

"Haha. It seems the noble guest is curious about the temple. This temple is nowhere on Earth."

"......Is it a dungeon?"

"It's similar, but slightly different. It would take more time to explain in detail... so I'll postpone it for later. However, to put it simply, it can be said that Gwangshin-nim's authority has permeated this space."

The leader easily answered my questions. He didn't seem displeased at all.

"There's something else I don't understand. Why am I a noble guest?"

"Because Gwangshin-nim chose you as the noble guest."

"I'm asking why that Gwangshin-nim chose me. Until recently, I was someone who had nothing to do with the Gwangwon Church."

If it hadn't been for Park Suho, I wouldn't have been involved. And even if they said I became a noble guest because of a divine revelation, it was hard to understand. Why did Gwangshin give such a revelation? Why did Gwangshin choose me? More fundamental questions arose.

Kang Jiwoo would probably say that the will of God is too deep to fathom, but the leader in front of me would be different. Unlike Kang Jiwoo, who was the lowest-ranking member of the Gwangwon Church, he would be the highest-ranking leader.

"Haha. Now that the noble guest has come this far, I must explain it."

Since he said it so easily, suspicion began to grow.

"First of all, the noble guest must have encountered Gwangshin-nim before."

"I met Gwangshin-nim? When are you talking about?"

"Wouldn't the noble guest know that? Try to remember carefully."

I focused on my memories and soon recalled.

An existence worthy of being called a god.

However, that was not a god of this world, but a god of Park Suho's tattoo world.

Brahmasen.

That was definitely the name.

An evil god.

A god of madness who spreads madness.

,So Gwangshin wasn't talking about a god of light, but a real crazy god.'

I knew the identity of Gwangshin.

Brahmasen. I didn't know how he did it, but he was being worshiped on Earth. And at the same time, I had a question. Wasn't Brahmasen hostile to me? Why was he treating me like a noble guest now? To him, I should be an enemy he wants to kill immediately.

"Gwangshin-nim wants the noble guest."

"He wants me?"

"Yes. I don't know the reason, but Gwangshin-nim wants the noble guest."

"......."

When I met Brahmasen last time, he also tried to entice me. I clearly refused him flatly at that time.

"Noble guest. Do you perhaps know the hero of Selbretina? He is the worst enemy and the most heretical of heretics. We have obtained that heretic... the best sacrifice."

The leader's gaze turned to Park Suho. Park Suho still had a blank face. Like the other ordinary followers, he knelt and bowed his head to the leader. The leader smiled at Park Suho. It was a sneer.

"To think that the real hero of Selbretina would approach us without any preparation. I didn't expect it, and Gwangshin-nim didn't expect it either. Gwangshin-nim was very pleased with the good fortune that came to us."

I had a vague idea that Park Suho was a hero. Park Suho, not me, was the one deeply involved with the tattoo world.

"......Park Suho is a sacrifice? What do you mean?"

"The hero of Selbretina is special. He is a kind of apostle who has received the power of Selbretina. Gwangshin-nim will steal the power of Selbretina through the sacrificed hero and gain divine authority. That's how he will descend to Earth. Hahaha. Originally, it would have taken more than half a year, but with the hero in our hands, the success rate has increased, and the timing has been moved up!"

"If Park Suho is a sacrifice, what is my role? Am I also a sacrifice?"

"Make the noble guest a sacrifice... We would never do such an impious thing. The noble guest's role is to face the descended Gwangshin-nim. In my opinion, Gwangshin-nim probably intends to make the noble guest an apostle."

The leader looked at me with eyes dripping with envy and respect. I wasn't happy at all.

"...When will Gwangshin-nim descend?"

"Tomorrow. We will hold the divine authority ritual tomorrow! I wish I could hold the ritual right now... but rituals require preparation."

I think I'm fucked.

Not just me, but the entire Earth.

I pondered for a moment and then nodded. I gave a bright smile that I hadn't shown until now.

"I'm very much looking forward to tomorrow. I have finished preparing to meet Gwangshin-nim."

Since I already seemed fucked, I decided to choose the best option. To get on Brahmasen's line. Then, even if the world gets fucked, at least I'll be safe. What's important to me is to survive and enjoy my Game Life.

From now on, I am a follower of the Gwangshin Church.
