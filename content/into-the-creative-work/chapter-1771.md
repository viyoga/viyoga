In front of a hotel in Busan, an eight-lane road was stained with blood and brain matter. The headless corpses were scattered on the road like trash.

I hadn't anticipated that 95% of the 100,000 citizens would die with their heads exploding, but I remained calm.

It just means I've delivered an even stronger warning to those watching this spectacle. It's different from using a nuclear bomb. A nuclear bomb that wipes everything out feels unrealistic. But this horrific sight before their eyes will be etched into people's minds and never disappear.

Normally, this would ignite anger and hatred towards me... but due to the nature of this epidemic, the moment they antagonize me, their heads explode and they die.

If they don't want their heads to explode, they have no choice but to submit.

,I'll say it again. The epidemic has begun to spread worldwide. You will now die the moment you antagonize me. Don't resent me, don't be angry, don't hate me. Just accept it. That is your way to live. If you need someone to be angry at, blame the bastards who forced me to use the epidemic."

I turned my body sharply.

Secretary Kim was, of course, unharmed, but some of the soldiers guarding me had died with their heads exploded.

,About 10% of the soldiers died.'

It was fewer than I expected. They may fear me, but they didn't antagonize me.

I entered the hotel with Secretary Kim. Even while riding the elevator up to the Berry Sweet Room, I saw corpses. The corpses of hotel staff and guests.

,More people died than I thought. There's no one to clean up the bodies.'

Now that I've given a warning, the number of people dying should decrease.

,To think you created such a terrible epidemic... You're amazing, as expected of Chairman-nim. However, I'm concerned that more people will die from the epidemic than expected."

,Those who will be angry at me? No. Not many will die."

,Yes?"

,Fear overcomes anger and hatred."

Humans become powerless in the face of overwhelming fear. The 100,000 people who died in front of the hotel earlier. None of those who were in front of the hotel survived. The further back you went, the more survivors there were. They felt fear towards me after seeing heads exploding in front.

,Secretary Kim. The mayor of Busan died from the epidemic."

,Yes. He pretended to cooperate with us, but he was secretly antagonizing us."

,Busan will become the new capital of Korea. It will be the city that will become the base of the Daechun Group."

,I will appoint a capable and easy-to-use person as the new mayor of Busan. ...Chairman-nim. I'm sorry to say this, but Korea is finished. It would be better to move our base overseas. I recommend California in the United States."

,Koreans should live in Korea. It's easier to manage Korea. America. That land is too damn big."

,I understand."

I entered the hotel room and took out a space-time scroll.

,Chairman-nim. Where are you going?"

,I need to spread the epidemic all over the world."

It's obvious how those bastards will react afterward. Since the origin of the epidemic is Korea, they will thoroughly isolate Korea and close the borders.

,This epidemic spreads through water as well as air... but there's no guarantee that it can infect the entire world within 30 days.'

I have to travel around the world myself and spread the epidemic.

,America, Europe, Russia, Japan, China.... I can't leave out Australia and Africa either.'

Jjieeik!

I tore the space-time scroll.

I appeared in an alley in California, USA. The men who were leaning against the wall smoking marijuana were startled by my sudden appearance and pulled out guns from their waistbands and aimed them at me.

,Oh shit. Where did this fucking yellow come from all of a sudden."

,To not even recognize who I am.... You have nothing in your head. Just die."

I run towards them. They were already aiming their guns at me, so they pulled the trigger without hesitation.

There's no need to use an instant. I check the direction of the gun muzzle and move my body. The bullets graze past my body.

Since the distance has closed, I swung my fist.

Kwang!

With an impact sound like a bomb exploding, the man's body explodes. The guy next to him tries to move his gun and aim it at my head.

,Slow.'

Before the guy can pull the trigger, my elbow touches the guy's body first. Kwang! That was the end of him. But there are still two more left. I dealt with each of them with a fist and a kick. And I confidently walked out of the alley.

I can see people running away. It was natural since they heard the gunshots.

,California, which Secretary Kim recommended... it's not very good after all.'

California is not the California of 100 years ago. In the past, the American economy was ruined by the Daechun Group, and it began to decline.

Still, as the saying goes, even if a rich man goes bankrupt, three generations will still eat, America was still America. Even if the economy collapsed, it showed more potential than most countries. The problem was in areas other than the economy. Especially the public safety was fucked up. California is a bit better, but some other states have become lawless zones where the law doesn't work.

[Spreading the epidemic. Remaining count: 8]

,I successfully spread the epidemic in LA. Should I go to Europe next.'
