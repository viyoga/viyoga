I killed the cannibals as soon as I saw them. Their corpses were dried up and chopped into pieces. I didn't want to kill them easily, so I deliberately cut off their bodies little by little to inflict pain. When they died, I sucked up their blood with Blood Orb and stored it.

The fist-sized Blood Orb was faintly glowing crimson.

I looked at the last remaining cannibal. Unlike the other naked bastards with crude weapons, he decorated his body with jade. He was different from the other cannibals. It was obvious at a glance that this bastard was leading the cannibals.

I'd find out if he was a great warrior or the chief himself if I asked.

I flicked my finger.

Blood cleave turned into a blood cleave from the Blood Orb floating next to me and sliced off the bastard's lower body. The bastard's lower body, covered in disgusting mold, was cut off. The blood was red.

,Kkiaaaaaaak!'

,Let's ask. Why are you guys eating people? You can live without cannibalism. The Tiat tribe has lived that way.'

,Kkieeek! Kkiaaaak!'

The bastard went crazy. Mold flew every time he moved. I don't know what kind of mold it was, but it was clear that he was deliberately trying to spread it. Even if I wanted to get hit, I couldn't let it happen. I blew away the mold with wind magic and flicked my finger.

Seogeok!

The blood blade cut off both of his arms.

,Don't ignore me pretending to be a beast. I know you can speak the common language.'

,Keu, keueeek... Keukeukeu....'

The bastard, who had been wriggling his body, suddenly started laughing. Madness could be seen in his eyes as he looked up at me.

,The reason we eat humans? Because humans are delicious. And the more humans we eat, the stronger we become. The idiots of the Tiat tribe are weak because they don't eat humans.'

,Does becoming stronger mean evolution? In my eyes, it looks like you're getting weaker and degenerating, not stronger.'

,Kkieeek...!'

The bastard ignored me and struggled. He crawled like a bug and tried to bite my ankle.

,Do you want to abandon your intelligence and become a bug even lower than a beast? Fine. Then die like a bug.'

I fixed him in the air with a blood blade and telekinesis, then dismantled his body piece by piece. I controlled his blood to keep him alive. In less than 5 minutes, only his head and spine remained. The bastard, barely alive by blood magic, looked at me with half-crazed eyes and moved his mouth. I inferred its meaning from the shape of his mouth.

,Akar. Are you Akar-isiyeo...?!'

,Akar my ass.'

Peong!

The bastard's blood, which had been barely holding onto life, moved and destroyed his head. After sucking the blood into the Blood Orb from among the shattered pieces of his head, I turned my body.

I can see the burning tribe.

The survivors of the Tiat tribe were gathering towards me. There were few warriors, mostly women and children.

They were looking at Rag, the chief, not me. As if they were hoping he would do something.

,What are these trash looking at? Tiat is ruined. You guys live as you want. Well, you'll all die soon anyway. The women are better off, though. If you're lucky, you might get caught by another tribe and live as a slave giving birth to children.'

,...Honey. What should we do now?'

One of the chief's wives asked. The chief clicked his tongue as if he was annoyed.

,I told you. Do whatever you want. If you don't want to live, just kill yourselves.'

,Honey! How can you do this?! You're the chief of our tribe! Lead us as usual! You, who came down from the sky, the special you, can do it!'

,This bitch.... How dare you raise your voice at me? You're just a test subject!!'

The chief strode towards his wife, swung his fist, and kicked her.

Aisa-ssi was startled and took out a camera from her chest. I'm amazed at her direct mentality that she would hold up a camera instead of stopping him at this moment.

,Aisa-ssi doesn't have a good impression of the Tiat tribe either.'

She hadn't stayed long enough to have a good impression of the entire tribe.

The chief's wife curled up and took the violence. The other tribesmen nearby only looked at the chief's face and couldn't dare to stop him.

,Ga, Garga...! Help me, please...!'

The chief's wife asked me for help. She was the woman who had taken my dead child and disappeared earlier today. I stood there with my arms crossed.

The chief finally killed his own wife.

,Hoo, you fucking bitch.... How dare you step up and make a fuss. You should have just stayed quiet....'

The chief's eyes flashed as he scanned the surroundings. The tribesmen froze like mice in front of a cat and couldn't do anything.

,It's about time they came....'

The chief raised his head and looked at the dark night sky. He stared intently at the north, then smiled coolly.

,They're here!'

Two transport helicopters were flying this way. There was no sound.

,They've silenced their presence with magic. There's at least one magician.'

The Blood Orb rippled. Then a spear of blood was fired at the helicopter. A circular barrier flashed and blocked the spear of blood. The spear of blood, having lost its power, fell to the ground with a thud.

,Hehehehe! Garga! No, Eugene! You're finished now! The headquarters' direct recovery unit has arrived!'

I was dumbfounded.

The spear of blood I just threw was an attack to say hello. It's like a jab in boxing. If you get knocked down by it, you're a cripple, and even if you don't, it doesn't really matter. The amount of blood currently stored in the Blood Orb is enough to fire hundreds of spears of blood and still have plenty left over.

,Let's see how great these bastards who are coming are.'

It's a good thing the spear of blood was blocked. I can't recover the blood if they die far away.

,I need to gather more blood to kill Rahul.'

He's been here for several years. He must have a lot of preparations. I need to prepare as well to fight against him.

The helicopter stopped in the air. The doors opened and soldiers armed with guns and battle suits fell to the ground.

Kung! Kuung! Kung!

A heavy sound like iron pillars falling spread. Immediately after, the soldiers fired their guns. Not at me, but at the surviving Tiat tribesmen.

,Aaaaaaaak!'

The short screams ended, and the smell of gunpowder and blood took their place.

Then the soldiers protected the chief and guarded me.

,Why did you kill them?'

The answer came from the chief. He must have thought it was safe, because he had a deep smile on his face.

,I disposed of them. Instead of recovering the living and rebuilding the tribe, it's easier to start over from the beginning.'

,I see. Aren't you guys going to attack?'

,That's right! Kill that vampire bastard! I almost died because of him! I was even tortured! He's an enemy of our company!'

The soldiers cleanly ignored the chief's words.

The one who seemed to be the leader among the soldiers stepped forward.

,I have a message from Director Oht. We'll make a deal. The condition is to keep silent about what you've seen here. The price is 10 billion credits. Or something equivalent. What do you say?'

Instead of answering, I looked at Aisa-ssi. Aisa-ssi raised her middle finger at the soldier.

,I wouldn't do it even if you gave me 1 trillion credits. People's right to know the truth is more important than money!'

,So that's what she says.'

,...You've made a foolish choice.'

Some of the soldiers activated mana wave devices. Then a barrage of bullets poured down.

Blood spurted out of the Blood Orb and became a barrier, defending me and Aisa-ssi. Machine guns armed on the transport helicopter spewed fire, supporting them. Even missiles flew and struck the blood barrier.

,Don't stop attacking! His blood is gradually decreasing! Don't give him a chance to counterattack!'

The soldiers' leader shouted.

In reality, the stored blood is being consumed as much as the blood barrier is taking damage.

,Perhaps... is this a crisis? Should we dig into the ground and run away?'

,No. I was just gauging the situation.'

,...Gauging?'

,Look. They're gradually gathering, aren't they? They're planning to break through the blood barrier by concentrating their firepower. Even the helicopters are gathering.'

,They're going to focus their fire on one part of the barrier. ...That looks dangerous.'

,...Not yet. A little more.'

,Yes?'

,When I grabbed that bastard's neck earlier, I injected my blood into him. It was blood I planted thinking I would track him if he ran away... I'm using it like this.'

The soldiers began to gather as if protecting the chief.

,Is it now.'

[Blood Burst]

The chief's face, which had been full of vigor, hardened. Immediately after, his face contorted in pain. Blood vessels stood out and writhed all over his body, including his face. As the chief suffered, some of the soldiers who sensed something strange turned to look at him.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The chief's body exploded. A powerful shockwave swept through the surroundings. The helicopter, which was flying low for the attack, was also swept away, lost its balance, and fell.

I removed the barrier and walked towards the fallen soldiers. Of course, it was to absorb their blood.

The leader and a few soldiers survived. The latest battle suit had temporarily saved their lives.

,Heoeok... Heok...! I can't die like this...!'

,Even if I die, I'll land one blow!'

,Fuck.... I don't want to die....'

With their bodies already in tatters, they squeezed the broken battle suit to its limit and raised their bodies. They slowly aimed their guns at me.

,There's a lot of the chief's blood on them. I can use it one more time.'

,...Fuck?'

[Blood Burst]

Kwaaaang!

The explosive power was less than before, but it was enough to finish them off. Their corpses scattered. I only absorbed the blood from among them.

When the situation was somewhat settled, I turned around and looked at Aisa-ssi.

,Aisa-ssi. I'm going to wipe out everything here. Regardless of Alphatiers or tribes, I'm going to wipe them all out. You get out of here. It doesn't matter if the escort mission is considered a failure.'

,No. I'll be with you until the end.'

,Is it because of the coverage?'

,...Um. That's one of the reasons. I promise I won't hold Eugene-ssi back.'

,I might not be able to protect you.'

,Hey. I'm not just a weak woman, you know? I'm a 5th grade druid. Maybe there will be a situation where I can help Eugene-ssi.'

,Okay. I'll ask for your help when the time comes.'
