I entered the room.

The space changed in an instant.

A Goddess appeared in the center of the desert, dyed in the light of the setting sun, holding a staff in one hand and an ankh in the other.

The Goddess, with brown skin and black hair, had intense eyes.

Unlike Aphrodite, the Goddess of Beauty, she was neither flamboyant nor overflowing with Sexiness. Instead, she was refined. Her Body had perfect proportions, and she was pure yet dignified. On her Head was a crown.

「The Mother of Healing looks at you.」

I inwardly admired her beauty and knelt.

What was before my eyes was not a mere prestige but a true Goddess. I had no intention of being hostile towards her. Rather, it would be good to maintain a good relationship.

Though my relationship with her husband, Osiris, was fucked up.

“King of the Desert. Pharaoh. One who accumulates all sorts of evil deeds, one watched by the Black Pharaoh, one who walks the Demonic path. For what purpose have you come to this place?”

Isis’s voice was benevolent.

Isis probably already knew my purpose. Since Isis is also the Goddess of Wisdom.

“I have come to obtain Isis’s cloth. As a bonus, I wish to bring Isis-nim’s Temple under the Demonic Cult’s wing. I swear I will not harm the followers. Ah, the matter with meaningless death was not my true intention.”

“His matter is fine. Since he was the one who started it first… Also, I have no authority to judge the Pharaoh. Pharaoh-isiyeo, who sits on the throne by his own will, not God’s will, I know that you desire my cloth for your wife. However, my cloth is one of the Treasures that humans cannot exchange even for gold. I cannot give it out by my will alone. The System also would not wish for it.”

“I had no intention of obtaining Isis-nim’s cloth for free from the beginning. What do you desire? If it is something I can do, I will. Would it be enough if I offer thousands of rebels to Isis-nim?”

“…I do not accept human sacrifices. Pharaoh-isiyeo. I will bestow a trial upon you. If you solve this, you will obtain all that you desire.”

“I will willingly accept the trial.”

“Pharaoh-isiyeo. Do you know that there is a river that pierces through this desert?”

“Such a river existed?”

As far as I knew, there was no giant river piercing through the desert zone. That’s why water was even more precious in the desert zone.

“It existed before this world became Atlantis. It was called the Nute River. It is a river that flows in from the outer sea, not inland.”

“Can something flowing in from the sea be called a river? No, before that, can’t you not drink seawater?”

“Very long ago, a great sage of the desert cast Magic on the Nute River, changing seawater into fresh water.”

Very long ago, indeed.

It seems to be a matter from before Atlantis began. Since it was a world where Magic existed even before Atlantis, it wasn’t particularly surprising.

‘Since Magic exists even in reality. Ah, but the scale of casting Magic on a river is absurd.’

No, that’s not right.

Sometimes, absurd beings can appear.

Instead of applying my common sense, I decided to roughly accept it. It seems this world’s common sense is like that.

“I understand. Restoring that Magic to its original state is the trial.”

“No. The river’s Magic is still operating. However, because the Hippocampus is blocking seawater from entering at the river’s entrance, the river is simply not forming. Pharaoh-isiyeo, subdue the Hippocampus for this desert.”

「Hippocampus Subjugation

Subdue the Hippocampus blocking the Nute River.

The trial cannot be abandoned midway.

Trial Success Reward – Isis’s Cloth, Domination Right of the 5,173rd Magic Desert Sector, Isis Temple.

Upon Trial Failure – All stats decrease by 10.

Divine Seat Bestowing Trial – Mother of Healing」

「You can refuse the trial.」

「Will you accept the trial?」

I quietly scanned the notification window.

All stats decrease by 10 if the trial fails? This is a rather big penalty.

‘But the reward isn’t just Isis’s cloth.’

How strong is this Hippocampus?

What even is a Hippocampus? I had never heard of it.

‘Shall I return to reality for a moment and search for it?’

Search results. The Hippocampus was Poseidon’s horse. The horse that pulls Poseidon’s chariot. A horse-fish that runs under the sea.

‘A Loser, then.’

This trial is a freebie.

“I will finish the trial as quickly as possible.”

「Trial accepted.」

“After the trial is finished, return to the Temple. I will have the reward prepared.”

Isis disappeared.

The desert landscape, upon which the setting sun had descended, also faded like a dream.

Before me was a statue imitating Isis’s form.

Nadia, the Priestess of Isis Temple, entered.

“You have received Goddess-nim’s trial.”

“Yes. It’s a simple task of just killing one horse. I’ll be back soon, so wait.”

I took out a spatial movement scroll from my Inventory and tore it.

「This is a space where spatial movement is forbidden.」

“The Temple is a sacred place.”

“…I had forgotten that spatial movement is impossible within a proper Temple.”

I wasted just one precious spatial movement scroll. I clicked my tongue briefly and tore a spatial movement scroll outside the Temple.

「This is a space where spatial movement is forbidden.」

“Why isn’t it working?”

“Our village has a Barrier installed to block spatial movement, to prevent intruders.”

“Priestess! Why didn’t you tell me in advance?”

“Before I could even speak, Pharaoh-kkeso used the spatial movement scroll.”

I did act hastily.

I tried to run out of the village.

“Wait. Do you know the location of the trial?”

“Can’t I just find the Nute River? It’s a simple task.”

“The Nute River does not exist in the current desert. Even if the desert dwellers know of the Nute River’s existence, they do not know its exact location. If you search for a Map where the Nute River is recorded, it exists, but since this world became Atlantis and the terrain also changed, the Map cannot be trusted. We have a recorded Map, so I will lend it to you.”

“Thank you, Priestess. You are quite smart. But is it okay for you to help me like this?”

“Receiving appropriate help is also part of the trial-taker’s capability.”

“It seems you hope for me to succeed in the trial.”

“You will succeed even without my help.”

“Is it a prophecy?”

“No. It is a prediction.”

A moment later, a Priestess brought a Map. The Map’s material was papyrus.

The desert zone was recorded. I nodded my Head.

“Even looking at it, I don’t quite understand.”

“…Yes? It is recorded in detail by sector. If you look here, the Nute River is also marked. It is on the opposite side of the Scorpion Desert where Pharaoh-kkeso is staying. This line is the Nute River. Although it is a line drawn by identifying only its traces since there is no river currently, I am confident that its accuracy is over 90%.”

“Since you are a smart Mage, you would know well. But it’s a bit complicated to find my way just by looking at one Map. It would be good if you guided me.”

“……I am the Priestess of Isis Temple, but.”

“It won’t take long. The trial will end within today. I also have spatial movement scrolls.”

“…I understand.”

Nadia joined the party.

Thanks to her, finding the way was no problem.

It took 3 hours to reach the destination. The biggest drawback of spatial movement scrolls is that coordinates must be saved. Ultimately, I had to spatially move to a place close to the destination and then ride a vehicle for 3 hours.

“Is this it?”

Before me, a canyon and the sea were visible. What appeared to be a canyon was the trace of a river. Normally, seawater should flow in, but an unseen wall was blocking the canyon entrance.

In the sea before the unseen wall, a Hippocampus exceeding 30m in size was peacefully sleeping.

A monster with the upper body of a horse and the lower body and tail of an elongated fish.

“Why has no one dealt with such a monster, gripping the desert’s Collar/Leash so tightly?”

“We could not deal with it. The Hippocampus is not an ordinary Monster. In the sea, it exerts power like a lower-ranked God.”

“If one of the Empire Ogong were to step forward… Ah. There’s no reason they would, is there?”

From the Empire’s perspective, whether the desert’s river dried up or not was of no interest. No, since it meant the desert’s power would weaken, it was something to be welcomed.

“Pharaoh-isiyeo. What will you do?”

Humans become weak if they enter the sea. There’s no way they can properly exert power when they can’t even breathe properly in the sea.

I do have the blessing of water, but….

‘If I enter the sea, my Body gets wet. Honestly, I cannot deny that I would be weaker than fighting on land.’

I took out the Pussy Magic Tower’s seal.

“What is that?”

“It is the Pussy Magic Tower’s seal.”

“…You mean that eyesore recently built in the desert.”

“Did you just call it an eyesore?”

“It was a slip of the tongue.”

I designated the coordinates for the spatial bombardment.

「The Pussy Magic Tower uses spatial bombardment.」

「The Pussy Magic Tower’s remaining energy is currently 99%.」

「After using spatial bombardment, the Magic Tower enters an overload state. The overload time is determined by the amount of energy used. During the overload state, the Magic Tower’s functions are limited.」

「Spatial bombardment consumes a minimum of 30% energy. How many additional percent of energy will you consume?」

“90%.”

「Approved.」

Jii-ii-ii-ii-iing.

A red line fell from the sky to the ground. The line entered the sea and precisely aimed at the Hippocampus’s Head. The Hippocampus was still sleeping. That red line was for checking coordinates, so it had no physical power at all.

「15 seconds remain until spatial bombardment.」

…….

「1 second remains until spatial bombardment.」

Immense Mana surged.

The sleeping Hippocampus opened its eyes and raised its Body. It lifted its Head upwards and looked at the sky. However, it was already too late.

High up in the sky, a Warp Gate had already opened.

From the black space, a giant blue energy blast fired by the Pussy Magic Tower fell to the ground. The orbital bombardment had begun.

KWA-AAAA-AAANG!

A shockwave burst from the single fallen shot. A small mushroom cloud tried to rise above the water surface, but the orbital bombardment continued consecutively.

Although the Hippocampus, being in the sea, had easily endured one shot, it had no choice but to suffer from the continuously falling energy blasts.

A total of 25 shots of bombardment ended in less than a minute.

Below the sea. The shredded, torn, and crushed Corpse of the Hippocampus scattered everywhere in the sea.

The unseen wall disappeared, and seawater flowed into the canyon. The Nute River’s Magic automatically activated, and the seawater turned into fresh water. Large and small waves arose in the aftermath of the shockwave. It was not tsunami level.

「Trial completed. Obtain the reward at Isis Temple.」

“Kekeke. How is it, Nadia? This is my power.”

Nadia, who had been astonished with her mouth slightly agape, answered in a trembling voice.

“…Truly, it is an absurd power.”
