"I will pass the baronet title to the son who has made the greatest contribution in this war."

Numerous nobles were momentarily speechless, a statement so explosive it left them dazed.

"......Is he out of his mind? A baronet title?"

Marquis Petras's remark was somewhat rude, but it represented the feelings of most nobles, including me.

Ention's statement meant that he would drive his three heir candidates, his own children, onto the battlefield. In general common sense, an heir is someone to be protected. Someone who must lead the family if something happens to oneself.

Even if a noble himself goes to the battlefield when a war breaks out, he does not send his heir. The heir is the future of the family.

"I am in my right mind. Jent, Kyle, Eugene. Step forward."

The brothers, scattered throughout the banquet hall, stepped forward. I maintained a poker face as much as possible while observing the complexions of my other brothers.

Jent momentarily contorted his face before feigning his usual relaxed expression. I didn't miss the fleeting look of betrayal on Jent's face.

'I thought he knew Ention's intentions since he was paying attention to mercenaries... Looks like he had no idea.'

Kyle, on the other hand, had a consistently stiff expression. It seems Kyle also didn't like Ention's statement.

Ention looked at the three of us once and said.

"The Prucus family was built in war. Therefore, whoever bears the name of Prucus must not fear war. Nor must they be incompetent. Prove your worth in war."

Ention's words continued.

"If you want to withdraw from the war, say it here now. There will be no disadvantages except that you cannot become the heir to the Prucus family."

But no one had any intention of withdrawing.

"I will prove myself on the battlefield as my father wishes."

Jent said. He had been living with Ention on the front lines. He was not likely to be greatly afraid of war.

"Father. What is the deadline? Is it until the end of the war?"

Kyle asked about the end.

"Basically, yes. But if the war drags on, it will be three years. If three years have passed since the start of the war, I will directly assess your contributions and declare the heir."

The other brothers spoke up, and I couldn't stay still either.

"What kind of support can we receive from the family?"

"There is no support. Preparing for war is also your capability. You can join the military and rise from the bottom. You can participate with the help of someone else. The only condition to become the heir is to make contributions in the war."

This makes things complicated.

Each brother had the power to lead soldiers.

As the eldest son, Jent can draft soldiers with his solid foundation. The noble forces that follow him will be a great help.

The second son, Kyle, is a top-tier Expert. Soldiers will naturally follow him.

'On the other hand, I have money. It's not a bad condition.'

None of us backed down. Ention nodded in satisfaction at the sight.

"Tsk. Those Prucus bastards are all displeasing."

Marquis Petras muttered with annoyance.

"Second. If war breaks out, we will not insist on defense only. We will invade the Valt Kingdom and seize territory."

Valt Kingdom.

The kingdom bordering the eastern border of the Rapelli Kingdom.

"Baron Prucus! What is the meaning of saying that so proudly here?! Do you think there are no ears of the Valt Kingdom here?!"

The Duke raised his voice and asked. Ention answered without batting an eye.

"The Valt Kingdom was an enemy country. In the last great war, tens of thousands of soldiers lost their lives in the war with the Valt Kingdom. The same goes for the previous wars. We have already built an irreversible history with the Valt Kingdom. The Valt Kingdom is a country that must be destroyed for our future. Does Your Excellency the Duke wish for friendship with the Valt Kingdom?"

"......"

The Duke closed his mouth. Invading was also unpleasant, but defending the Valt Kingdom was an act of treason.

"Everyone. War is approaching. Do not forget this fact and enjoy today's banquet. I will withdraw now as my presence will be a hindrance."

Ention threw a storm into the banquet hall and quietly left.

Nobles moved busily. They either followed Ention out of the banquet hall or approached our brothers instead.

'Things will get busy from now on. Minerals, armor, weapons, food... everything will rise in price overall. Other countries will also prepare for war in earnest.'

The war of resources has already begun. And the winner of this war will be me. I have prepared in advance, and I can also bring things from another world.

'Keu keu. Things have flowed a bit differently from my expectations, but it's good anyway. I can make a lot of money.'

As evidence, nobles are already approaching me with the best smiles they can muster. I am clearly a young noble in their eyes, but they are watching my every move.

"Baron Prucus-nim. I am Baron Renmidle, serving Count Repulge of the South. If you don't mind, could we talk for a moment in private? It's not a story that will harm the Baron-nim. It's a story for all of us, and for the Rapelli Kingdom."

"Empty words are pouring out of your mouth again. Didn't I tell you last time that there was no sincerity in your words? Baron Prucus. Talk to me. I have a story that will prick up your ears."

"As expected, Baron Prucus-nim is very popular. I have come to make a proposal to the Baron-nim that will be beneficial and profitable. I promise. You will never regret it."

Numerous nobles flocked to me.

Most of them were coming because of the money I had and the Korea Trading Company.

"Now, calm down. I'm not running away anywhere. There's plenty of time, so let's talk slowly."

Yuria is my wild card. I plan to hide her as long as I can. I have no intention of giving Princess Eileen information now.
