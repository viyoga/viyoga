Thump.

The chunk of meat that used to be a witch pulsed.

Blood, flesh, and guts nearby gathered towards the meat chunk, clumping together.

,Even after dying, the magic hasn't disappeared. But this feeling, familiar yet unpleasant... Brahmasen. It's similar to that bastard.'

Did this world say it had fought and won against Brahmasen in the past? Maybe that influence remains as the Forbidden Arts.

,Even if not, it seems like that bastard keeps meddling.'

Not just in the Tattoo World, but also on Earth where I live. Of course, he's probably reaching out to other dimensions too.

Thump. Thump.

The meat chunk's pulse grows stronger. Each beat sends out invisible waves, absorbing blood and flesh.

In a short time, its size more than doubled.

,Meteor Sword.'

Dozens of swords fall from the sky. Pak pak pak! The swords slam into the ground, piercing and tearing apart the meat chunk. The meat chunk was being shredded by dozens of swords.

But the meat chunk didn't give up. It continued to regenerate with blood-red magic.

,Simply piercing and tearing has its limits. Then...'

Meteor Sword Attribute Enchantment.

Pajijik.

Lightning crackled on the falling swords. Lightning flowed from the swords stuck in the meat chunk, electrocuting and burning it. The burning meat emitted a foul odor. The effect was excellent. The meat chunk's regeneration weakened, and it died completely along with the unpleasant magic.

I turned my head. Derek, covered in blood, was running away. The way he staggered, it seemed he didn't have much strength left. Of course, he wasn't fast either.

One Meteor Sword appeared above my head. A single Meteor Sword, lying diagonally, floated without falling.

The moment I pointed my finger at him, the Meteor Sword shot out as if it had been waiting.

The Meteor Sword pierced Derek's right thigh.

,Aaaaaaaagh!'

Derek screamed. Because it consumed 2-3 times more mana than a normal Meteor Sword, the Meteor Sword stuck in his thigh didn't disappear immediately.

I walked towards Derek. Derek, collapsed on the ground, looked back at me, becoming anxious. He desperately pulled out the Meteor Sword stuck in his thigh and limped away on one leg. His desperate struggle was quite entertaining to watch.

I flicked my hand. A Meteor Sword appeared in the air and shot out. The Meteor Sword pierced his left thigh. Derek, who had fallen, took out a dagger from his pocket instead of pulling out the Meteor Sword.

,Suicide? No way.'

This time, four Meteor Swords. They pierced Derek's shoulders and wrists. He couldn't do anything anymore. At best, he could bite his tongue. He was stronger than the average person, though not comparable to a knight. He wouldn't die just from biting his tongue. I leisurely walked towards him.

,Just, please just kill me! Please!'

,There's something I'm curious about.'

,Ask away! I'll tell you everything!'

,I heard the witch was your client. Is that right? No matter how I think about it, why would a witch like that steal war supplies? I'd think she'd rather massacre a village and research the Forbidden Arts or something.'

,Galena did hire us! And behind Galena is Baron Gledenberch! In fact, Baron Gledenberch planned this!'

,From thieves to nobles. Quite a sight.'

Baron Gledenberch. I've heard the name. I couldn't help but have, since Baron Gledenberch's territory is right next to Count Mork's territory. And one of the nobles who lent money to Count Mork is Baron Gledenberch.

,Whoa, originally, we would have safely crossed into Baron Gledenberch's territory, and the job would have been over. But the knights chased us, so we asked the witch for help...'

,But we won in the end.'

,That's right! We have all the stolen supplies too! Please, just kill me! I beg you! Knight-nim! I'll give you the gold coins I have!'

,Dare you try to buy a knight's honor with money?!'

,Hic! I, I'm sorry! I misspoke!'

,Count Mork-kakka will decide your fate. Well, you'll probably be tortured and then executed.'

,Kheuheuheuk...'

Robbing a noble's property, threatening a village to try to kill a knight, offering territory residents to a witch. It's crazy to expect to die peacefully after doing these things. If I were a noble, I would use taxes to torture him.

,Sir Daikert! The rest are taken care of! No casualties on our side! It's all thanks to Sir Daikert handling the witch with overwhelming power!'

Sir Philius, the hero of the Empire. He's basic common sense in the Empire, so I know roughly about him too. Philius is praised as the Lord of Swords, and his secret technique is said to be creating and wielding over a thousand swords.

,I'm sorry. I can't talk about the secret technique.'

,If we leave him like this, he'll die from excessive bleeding. Does anyone have a potion?'

,I have a potion. It's something I obtained with difficulty, but... Count Mork-kakka isn't a stingy person, so he'll give me a potion later.'

Rakgus stepped forward and used the potion to stop Derek's bleeding. With this, the matter was settled for now.

,You all rest. I will go to the village and order the squires to bring the soldiers.'

After that, the cleanup was the responsibility of the squires and soldiers. The knights, including me, cheerfully took the mercenaries and returned to Count Mork's castle.

The mercenaries would be tortured and executed, and the village women would live in other villages or cities.

Count Mork, having learned the whole situation, was naturally furious. He immediately sent a messenger to Baron Gledenberch. The messenger returned a week later. In his hand, he held a magic tool instead of a letter. Count Mork used the magic tool.

The air rippled, and Baron Gledenberch's figure appeared. The magic tool seemed to be a semi-transparent hologram communication device.

The Empire wasn't ignorant of magic. With its deep history, it had accumulated considerable magical knowledge. In the first place, the starting point of the Magic Tower was the Empire. But now that it has fallen, there are very few magicians in the Empire. Even the remaining magicians are gradually leaving the Empire.

,It's been a while, Count Mork.'

,...Are you saying you want to have a territory war?'

,That's right. As you may know, when nobles of the same empire wage territory wars, no mediator is needed. Well, it's called a territory war, but in reality, it's closer to an all-out war. Of course, I won't kill your people. They will soon become my people.'

Count Mork's face twisted with anger.

,Even if you betray us like this, do you think the surrounding nobles will stand by? We will condemn you for disrupting the peace and impose appropriate punishment!'

,You...! Aren't you ashamed as a noble of the Empire!!'
