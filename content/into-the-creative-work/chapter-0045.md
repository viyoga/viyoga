"Superpower...! A True Ancestor, huh!"

It's not that surprising. There are quite a few True Ancestor vampires.

Because when a vampire sucks the blood of five other vampires, they awaken as a True Ancestor. This is called a wild True Ancestor, but since it's ultimately an awakening through cannibalism, they are hated and targeted even by other vampires. This is because if a regular vampire sucks the blood of a True Ancestor, they immediately awaken as a True Ancestor.

"Yeah. But are you really human? How can you deflect all my attacks? That's not human reflexes."

The whip-knife slumped on the floor shrank back down.

Extending and retracting. It seems he can do it freely.

He swings the knife at me again. The blade of the knife extends like a whip. He's handling two knives simultaneously with serious skill.

Kang! Kaang! Kang!

Deflecting the knife-whip by positioning my sword this way and that isn't difficult. The problem is that it's hard to get close. If I try to approach, the knife curves around, encircling me like a noose.

'Damn it. Annoying. Should I just take a gamble? ...Nah. Let's go easy and fast.'

I retreated for now. It wouldn't have been easy if I wasn't in Acceleration state.

"...Running away?"

"Who's running away."

I took out my smartphone from my pocket. I ended the game to return to reality and took out the item I had put in my inventory again. There was a slight gap in this process, but fortunately, he was also taking the time to regroup, so it was all good.

"......A revolver? You took it out of your smartphone? What kind of trickery is that?"

"No trickery. It's as you see it."

I aimed the revolver at him. It's a vampire hunter's weapon. The revolver's bullets are silver bullets, the vampire's weakness. No matter how great their physical abilities and superpowers, being weak to silver doesn't change, even for a True Ancestor.

Tang tang tang tang tang tang!

I fired all six bullets from the revolver.

Just one bullet. If I hit him properly with just one bullet, he'll be like a mosquito hit head-on with bug spray.

He moved his body and dodged all the bullets. Then, he frowned as he looked at the bullets that had fallen to the floor.

"Silver bullets... is it."

"......This bastard, he dodges damn well."

"If you shoot from the front, there's nothing I can't dodge. And you're just bad at it. Even if I didn't dodge, you'd only hit me with about two. How can you be so bad at aiming from this distance?"

"It's my first time shooting a gun...."

"Even if it's your first time, that's a disastrous level. You have no talent for shooting compared to your high-level swordsmanship."

"Ah. Yes. That's right."

I answered casually and threw a grenade with the pin pulled at him.

"It's a holy grenade!"

It's a specially made fragmentation grenade filled with small silver beads inside. It's extremely deadly to vampires. I quickly went up the back stairs and got out of the blast radius.

Kwaang!

The grenade exploded. I went back down to the basement. No matter who he is, this place is narrow. There's nowhere to properly avoid it, so he has no choice but to get hit by the grenade.

'It's not just a regular grenade, it's an anti-vampire grenade. Even if he's a True Ancestor, he'll at least be seriously injured.'

But, contrary to my expectations, he was fine.

He made the blade of his knife as large as a wall to block the explosion of the grenade.

"Not just the length, but he can freely adjust the width too...!"

"Yeah."

He took a stance. His momentum was different from before. The passive guy had turned active.

"I was planning to gather information while fighting you... but you're more dangerous than I thought. I'll stop holding back and kill you now."

He runs towards me. He's only slightly slower than me using Acceleration. The remaining duration of Acceleration is 4 minutes. That's enough.

I use Byeokgye (碧溪) on him.

Kang!

The knife deflected my sword. It was about half effective. However, his combat sense and reflexes were more outstanding than I thought.

"What is it? A superpower... no, swordsmanship. To shake my sense of distance... Did such swordsmanship even exist? Interesting."

He laughed. He's enjoying the battle itself.

And so was I. It wasn't to the point of laughing out loud, but it was a bit fun.

'The guys upstairs were too boring.'

He's neither weaker nor stronger than me. Similar is the right word. His physical abilities are slightly better, but I have Acceleration and Yeongcheonryu.

'If he was so strong that I couldn't even touch him, I would have run away immediately... but this much is worth trying.'

He doesn't engage in close combat and wields the knife like a whip while maintaining a certain distance. It's proof that he's afraid of my swordsmanship, Yeongcheonryu.

'...That ability. It's really annoying.'

It suddenly extends or suddenly retracts. I can't let my guard down. The moment I lose sight of his knife, the lengthened blade will surely wrap around my body.

'Should I use that? I didn't think I'd ever use something like this.'

Yeongcheonryu Practical Technique: Swallow Dance (燕子舞)

My movements changed. Before, I was so focused on approaching him at maximum speed that my body was a bit hunched over, but now my back and waist are straightened out proudly.

I move as if I'm dancing. There's no sharp momentum like before, but sharpness is hidden within the softness.

Kang! Kaang! Kaka-kang!

I deflect his knife and slowly approach.

"...Hmm?"

He seemed flustered and swung his knife again. The speed of the knife increased, but I dodged or deflected even that completely.

Swallow Dance is a technique focused on evasion and defense. It uses footwork and body movement to the fullest. Because of that, it looks like I'm dancing.

If Jin Se-yeong-ssi were here, she might be able to use Swallow Dance for attacks as well, but I don't have that level of skill yet.

I had already approached right in front of him.

"This is ridiculous...."

My sword cuts through his body. He staggered but still swung his knife at me. I bent my waist to avoid the knife, which had become as big as a club, and swung my sword again.

The sword cut his leg and shoulder before piercing his heart.

"Keueok!"

He collapsed to the floor. Even so, he tried to swing his arm, so I stepped on his wrist with my sneaker.

"I'm just asking in case. Do you know the safe password?"

"49685817361."

"......Oh. I didn't think you'd actually tell me. That's a lie, right?"

"The password is real. Fingerprint recognition and iris recognition are additionally required. It's impossible for you to move the safe alone, and even if you try to destroy the safe here, it will take time. By then, reinforcements will have arrived and turned you into a beehive."

"Kekeke."

Seeing him seriously spouting such words even as he was dying, I couldn't help but laugh.

"What's so funny?"

"It's like the heavens are helping me."

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the safe for 2 minutes.]

Click.

The door of the massive safe opened.

"What?!"

"Fingerprint recognition or password, it didn't matter. I was just asking once."

I swung my sword. His neck was cut off. He seemed to have been planning to counterattack by extending the length of a blade hidden in his pants pocket. I had anticipated it early on, seeing him subtly moving.

I kicked his corpse away and looked at the safe.

It was about half the size of my self-boarding room in reality.

It's impossible to put the entire safe into my inventory. The condition for the inventory is limited to items I can carry. No matter how big it is, if I can't lift it, it won't go into the inventory. I confirmed it before. On the other hand, even if it's big, if I can lift it, it goes into the inventory.

I was quite amazed when I put a huge styrofoam box over 5M into my inventory before.

"Ooh-mi...."

I entered the safe and let out an exclamation.

The inside of the safe wasn't just filled with cash. There were works of art like paintings and ceramics, as well as jewels and gold bars. The stacked bills were won and dollars. Two types.

"Wow, how many 1kg gold bars are there? Over 200."

I don't know the exact price, but I remember seeing on TV before that a 1kg gold bar was worth 60 million won or something.

"I should sell the gold bars in reality. I should start escaping my self-boarding room soon."

While packing the items, I thought about where to use them.

First of all, the place I'm moving to is a luxury officetel. I've gotten used to Hera's house, so I keep being drawn to luxury officetels.

I'll keep the jewels for now, and I'll exchange the dollars for won. I had no intention of going to America.

'The artwork... I should go find Kang Wook-seong.'

Since this is ultimately stolen goods, it's difficult to handle it openly. It's safer and more convenient to entrust it to a professional broker, even if I lose some money.

After taking all the items from the safe, I got into the Benz parked in front of the village hall. The car keys were near the front door, so there was no need to search the corpse.

"Making money is this easy! Keuhahahahaha!"

When I left the village, I stretched my hand out of the window and gave the middle finger to the CCTV camera. I hoped that Kim Chun-seok would be very angry when he saw this.

'Wow. So this is why people talk about foreign cars, foreign cars.'

Having driven a bit, I knew that it was superior to domestic cars in every way.

'Kya. This is fun.'

The mountain road was winding, but I maintained a speed of 70km/h, barely seeing the road ahead with the headlights. Other people might not be able to do it, but my reflexes made dangerous mountain road driving possible.

With just a slight mistake, the car would overturn or crash into a tree. Maybe it would even roll down below.

'That's what makes it thrilling.'

I was as focused as when I was fighting the vampire True Ancestor bastard earlier.

Kkieeeuk kung!

Something jumped out while I was turning a curve and hit the car. It couldn't be helped. A dark night. And it suddenly jumped out while I was turning a curve on a mountain road, so I couldn't even react.

"You crazy!"

Surprised, I immediately stopped the car and got out.

"Shibal. A roe deer bastard!"

A roe deer with its legs and neck wrecked was dead with its tongue sticking out. I grimaced.

I heard that Gangwon-do roe deer are especially crazy.

'This isn't just crazy. Shibal.'

The front of the car was badly dented. I clicked my tongue strongly and went back to the driver's seat. I was going to slow down my driving speed because of the roe deer.

The car went for about 30 seconds before suddenly stalling.

"Huh? What's wrong?"

I turned the car key to try to start it again. But the car remained silent. Something must have gone wrong because of the roe deer earlier.

After trying a few more times, I finally got out of the car. Hacking was also on cooldown, so I couldn't use it.

'It's too late to go back up... I have no choice but to walk down.'

Fortunately, the mountain road isn't that long, so if I go down a little, I'll find the car I came in.

"Damn mosquito bastards. I want to catch them all and burn them to death. And those roe deer, I have to catch them all and tear them apart. ...Gae-shibal."

I got so pissed off along the way that I bought a bicycle in reality and brought it to ride down.

I was originally going to buy a motorcycle, but I didn't have a license.

While pedaling down the bicycle, I found a roe deer.

I took out a pistol from my inventory and pulled the trigger at the roe deer.

Tang tang tang tang!

The gun muzzle spat fire and the shell casings fell to the floor. I fired 17 bullets at once, but didn't hit a single one.

"......Tsk."

It's partly because the roe deer was dodging this way and that, but it's also because my shooting skills were terrible. It's as the vampire bastard said earlier. I had no talent for shooting.

"Ugh. What am I doing. This."

I had a strong moment of clarity and was just going to ignore the roe deer and leave. Today is a good day. I can be generous and let the roe deer go.

But this damn roe deer bastard didn't run away and smiled at me.

It's not my paranoia. It really smiled. It really smirked and laughed at me.

"Shib-sae-kki!"

In the end, I took out the K-2 assault rifle, steeped in Korea's long tradition.

It's night now, and this is a forest full of trees. And roe deer are surprisingly fast. I was only able to catch it after using up 5 magazines.

I sighed deeply at my truly hopeless shooting skills and took out my smartphone to put the gun into my inventory.

'It requires 3 times more than Yeongcheonryu.'

The Yeongcheonryu trait is limited to the martial art Yeongcheonryu. But Shooting includes not only firearms but the entire act of firing or shooting something. The range of application for Shooting is greater than that of Yeongcheonryu.

'I need to quickly gather points and unlock the trait.'

I felt good. I made money, caught a roe deer, and I can unlock the Shooting trait as soon as I gather enough points.

I couldn't help but hum a tune. The mosquito bastards were annoying, but I could be generous.

Until I met another damn roe deer bastard.
