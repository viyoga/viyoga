,Perfect Body', ,Wings of Dawn', ,Sacrifice'.

Perfect Body, a skill that can maximize the abilities of the body.

Wings of Dawn, which maximizes mobility.

Sacrifice, which temporarily creates a miracle by sacrificing oneself.

Each had its pros and cons.

How strong can I become through Sacrifice?
