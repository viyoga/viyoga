,I will take your death away."

Osiris declared.

,What a load of crap. Undead Legion!"

I shouted.

The undead, swept up in the sandstorm and scattered here and there, rise to their feet. Their number is less than 1,000 compared to before. Most of those that still retain their form were high-level undead.

,Kill that bastard!"

The undead rise and head towards Osiris.

To be honest, I don't think these undead can kill Osiris. What I want is not victory, but information. I hope to get even a little information about Osiris.

,Your enemy is not me."

Osiris said.

The undead, who were marching towards Osiris, stopped dead in their tracks. They turned around and marched towards me.

I was taken aback and hesitated.

,S-Stop!"

I command the undead.

It didn't work. The undead didn't stop. They slowly walk towards me, showing hostility.

-I will kill you.

-The true master of death wants your life.

-Now the world is finally turning right.

-Die! I will constantly torment you in hell and take revenge!

-Enemy of my father!

It was noisy all around.

The 700,000 souls I was holding onto were breaking free from my control and running wild.

Thanks to the souls cursing me, I was able to regain my composure.

,It's clear that my control has weakened. In a way, it's only natural. The opponent is Osiris. He's the god of the underworld, after all.'

Even with my unique trait, No Life King (SS), I would have lost control over the undead.

,Normally, when control disappears, the souls I've captured should scatter, but they're gathered here and cursing me. I guess I should see it as Osiris' influence.'

I quickly took off Tutankhamun's golden mask. Aside from being stuffy, I don't know what Osiris will do to me while I'm wearing the golden mask, since I'm being treated as a dead person.

The stuffiness disappeared when I took off the golden mask. My body felt much lighter. I took the opportunity to take off the accessories hanging all over my body. They were all related to necromancy, so I didn't need them now.

,Hoo. It's much better to take it off."

An undead runs towards me. It was a Death Knight clad in armor all over its body. The Death Knight was not in a normal state. It was missing its left arm, and its helmet had flown off somewhere, exposing its rotten skull. He swings a sword with the aura of death flowing towards me.

,Maybe it's because he's not normal, but he's slower than usual, so I can see his attacks clearly. Do you think I'll be hit by such an attack?'

I twisted my upper body to avoid the Death Knight's attack. I gathered the force generated by the rotation of my upper body into my right arm and swung my fist.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Dragon Fist.

Kwaaaang!

The Death Knight's upper body armor is shattered, and its body flies backwards and crashes into the sand. The Death Knight, who was twitching and trying to get up, soon fell silent completely.

-Ughhhhh.

-Why is he so strong even though he took off the golden mask?!

-God of death! Please kill that bastard!

-That's a creepy aura.

-The god of death will drag you to hell!

,Shut up, it's noisy."

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Wave.

As I stepped forward, demonic energy spread in all directions. The souls that were cursing me immediately shut their mouths.

,Kkeueoaaaaaaa!"

The undead swarmed in. I walked forward and knocked down all the undead that attacked me. The only thing that was a threat to me now was the high-level undead, the Death Knight, but there were few Death Knights, and even they were not in good condition.

,Osiris or whatever, I'll just defeat him.'

The undead here can't stop me.

,Master!"

Gula was running from far away.

I was momentarily startled. If Gula turned against me, it would be very troublesome. Fortunately, Gula was not hostile to me.

,Gula! Did you defeat the golden warrior?!"

,Dodge!"

I saw a shadow covering the ground. I took a step back and dodged. The golden warrior fell like a meteor where I had been standing. The impact caused the sand on the ground to surge upwards.

A spear flew through the sand. My reaction was slightly delayed due to the appearance of the golden warrior. Gula jumped in front of me and deflected the spear with her sword.

,Master! I will stop his actions! Master, strike him! If we work together, we can defeat him quickly!"

Gula rushes at the golden warrior. The golden warrior reaches out his hand. Sand gathers in his hand and turns into a spear. Spear and sword collide.

Gula's explosive swordsmanship is blocked by the golden warrior's elegant spear. The reason they can't come to a conclusion is simply because their skills are similar.

Gula forces her way forward. The spear struck her side. The armor cracked. Gula threw her sword at the golden warrior. Aiming for the golden warrior to lean back and dodge the sword, she closes the distance and grabs the golden warrior's arm, blocking his movement.

,Master! Now's your chance!"

,Do I really need to go that far? If I attack now, you won't be safe!"

,There's no time!! Hurry!!"

Gula's voice was more urgent than ever.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent.

I stepped on the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent and arrived in front of the golden warrior, transcending space. I swung my fist without hesitation. Hesitating here would be disrespectful to Gula.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Dragon Fist.

Bang!

My fist strikes the golden warrior's chest. The shockwave reached Gula. Gula gritted her teeth.

,It's not enough to defeat the golden warrior.'

Cheonma Shingong Returning Heaven Immersing Essence.

Demonic energy rotates from inside the golden warrior. Sand bursts out from the golden warrior's body. The sand poured out like blood. Nevertheless, the golden warrior does not collapse. He reaches out his hand towards me.

Thwack!

Gula's fist shattered the golden warrior's head.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Explosion.

Demonic energy exploded. Only then did the golden warrior's body collapse.

,Gula!"

I caught Gula's falling body.

,Master. Listen carefully. This place is turning into hell. I have to get out of here. I cannot refuse the expulsion order of the owner of this hell. Even the Living Corpse-nim cannot help. His hell is the opposite of this place. So as soon as possible... before this desert is completely hellified...!"

I couldn't hear Gula's words to the end. Cold air flowed out of Gula's body and she disappeared.

,...Was she expelled?'

I straightened my body.

The sky was getting darker and darker. The golden sands were gradually losing their color and turning gray.

「Hellification is in progress.」

「The wind of death blows. It steals your life force.」

I couldn't avoid or block the wind. If I keep standing still like this, I'll just die.

「The Master of the Sky protests to the system.」

「Meaningless death has clearly crossed the line now.」

「The system responds.」

「It has not crossed the line. Causality is allowed. It is barely within the permissible range.」

Allowed?

Even though you're so blatantly interfering?

I thought for a moment and asked the system.

,Was Hatep's contracted god Osiris? Hatep is dead anyway, so you can tell me."

「Yes.」

The situation is becoming clear. Hatep must have made some kind of contract with Osiris. From Hatep's point of view, it wouldn't have been a bad choice to have insurance. Osiris is the god of death, so it would be a contract related to death... Osiris used that contract to descend.

,The Owner of the Oasis (Fake) might have mediated. Hatep said he offered hundreds of thousands of people to the Owner of the Oasis...'

I cleared all the thoughts that came to my mind. What's important now is not the cause.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent.

I recklessly stepped on the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent to close the distance with Osiris. But spatial movement did not occur.

,...I can't control space.'

The power of the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent comes from controlling space. Since I can't control space, the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent has become useless.

I didn't give up. Even if I can't teleport with the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent, I can run. It was the moment I took a step.

An invisible, enormous force grabbed my body and lifted me up.

,Keoeok?!"

Pressure crushes my body. If I let my guard down even a little, my whole body would explode.

I grit my teeth and endure the pressure, looking around. The sky is already pitch black, and the desert has turned gray.

Osiris, with his green skin, stands still.

The dead rise from the gray sand. Mummies with rotten bodies wrapped in old bandages. The dead are constantly appearing. There are 100,000 of them just in sight now. It will multiply several times more as time passes.

,It's completely different from when I fought Balor...! This world itself is my enemy!'

At this rate, I will die without being able to do anything.

Even if I have Complete Recovery and the Black Ankh, it's meaningless. I'll just die again if I come back to life. I need to find another way. The items I have in my head come to mind and disappear. What do I need in this situation?

「The King of Great Heavens sponsors 1,000 AP.

"The Book of the Dead."」

He told me the answer in a secretive voice, as if telling me the secrets of the world, and in a lewd voice that would tempt even the Buddha.

,...I see."

Most of the abilities of the Book of the Dead are related to the undead. But the abilities I need to use now are not those kinds of things.

There is only one ability I need here.

Connection with hell.

,There's Hel and Osiris. Surely that bastard will be there too.'

My senses reach out. The senses that were reaching out very far eventually connect with one.

The Book of the Dead drips black Kiwoon.

I felt familiarity. This aura was demonic energy.

,I want to make a deal, King of Great Heavens."
