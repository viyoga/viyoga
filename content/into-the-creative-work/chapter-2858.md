Jeong Minho, who was an ordinary young man in his twenties in South Korea, realized that his life had changed as the world changed.

July 31, 2020.

The day all the countries of the Earth joined forces to smash the 128th meteorite falling to Earth with nuclear missiles.

The day the D virus spread and the zombie apocalypse broke out.

As a sergeant nearing discharge, he received notification that his discharge date was being postponed.

It was absurd. He understood the situation well enough that he couldn't argue with his superiors. Still, he was worried about his family. Fortunately, his father awakened as an Awakened One, relieving some of his worries.

Sergeant Jeong Minho, nearing discharge, was thrown into actual combat. His job was to spray zombies with bullets. It wasn't difficult. He wasn't alone, and the special forces took charge of the dangerous operations.

The operation became dangerous as Variants, also called Mutants, appeared. Some of his comrades died. But he survived. He even earned some merit.

,Sergeant Jeong Minho. Your discharge date has been confirmed. Before congratulating you... have you considered staying in the military? If you stay in the military now, you will be promoted to staff sergeant immediately.,

Join the military for life? Are you crazy?

He couldn't say that, because the situation was bad. At that time, Seoul was such a dangerous area that he couldn't even walk around freely, and prices were skyrocketing, making it difficult to even get ramen. If he stayed in the military, at least he wouldn't starve.

And Jeong Minho was fairly well-suited to the military. He had even caught a few weak Variants.

,Alright, Company Commander-nim. The salary will definitely go up, right?,

,Of course. At least you won't starve to death. And work hard. We're in a state of war right now. If you accumulate enough merit, you can be promoted endlessly.,

The company commander's words were true.

Promotions came quickly. He became a second lieutenant after luckily killing a Variant and surviving several near-death experiences. He got various scars on his body, but it was a minor matter.

Jeong Minho thought he would only be dealing with zombies or Variants for the rest of his life. But when he came to his senses, he was a member of the coup forces.

,How did this happen?,

He ended up like that because he did what he was told. He became an officer as a second lieutenant, but what he did didn't really change.

Fortunately, he was lucky.

The coup succeeded.

His military life, which he thought would fall into the abyss due to the coup, actually ascended. He became a captain and led a company. His name gradually spread in the military. At the same time, dangerous missions began to be assigned.

,They say you shouldn't stand out too much in the military. That's exactly how it is.,

Still, it was manageable.

His company consistently suffered casualties, but he continued to survive and complete his missions. Fortunately, they didn't give him completely impossible missions.

As he frequently participated in Variant extermination operations, he also met the Knights often.

At first, he was awkward with the knights. They just did what they had to do. But as they encountered each other often and became familiar, they started talking.

,Captain Jeong Minho. That was good support. Your command was brilliant.,

,Thank you, Knight-nim.,

,May His grace be with you.,

The Thunderbolt Knights were gentle. On the surface, that is. If you looked closely, you could see that they were all fanatics of the Seongjin Church.

It was a bit unsettling, but only at first. Every time they appeared, the unfavorable war situation was reversed, and the extermination ended successfully. The knights carried out their operations even if they died in the most dangerous places. He expressed his gratitude as he watched the knights die.

,The knights always maintain a certain number. Three died yesterday, but five came today. Are there more knights than I thought? Those elite troops?,

He couldn't see their faces because they were always wearing helmets.

The knights didn't run away from the battlefield. If a situation arose where they had to run away, they chose to self-destruct. Thanks to that, they often won.

He became interested in the Seongjin Church, which he hadn't been interested in despite numerous proselytizing attempts. Was it just his imagination? From then on, his physical abilities improved, and he became less tired.

In the cold winter, there was a Variant extermination operation in Gangwon Province. Weasel Variants were the targets. Ten Thunderbolt Knights and the company participated together.

It was their trap. Among the weasels, there was one with intelligence comparable to humans.

The knight stepped forward. One of the knights was in danger. Jeong Minho stepped forward without realizing it. He took the weasel's attack instead of the knight and lost his right arm. He didn't die, thanks to the knight injecting him with a drug.

,Captain Jeong Minho. Why did you do that?,

,...I have received a lot from you. And when I thought about it calmly, it's better for you to survive than me.,

People's values were not the same. That was one of the things he realized while leading the company as a captain.

,Sacrificing oneself for others is not something that can be done with ordinary determination. Your dedication is worthy of respect. You are noble.,

The knight disappeared after finishing his praise.

He was taken to a military hospital and expected to be discharged as is. But there was no discharge. Instead, the National Intelligence Service came to his hospital.

In the center of the men in black suits was a black-haired woman with a coat draped over her shoulders. She spoke with an inscrutable smile on her face, even in the hospital where patients were groaning.

,I am Lee Yeonhee, the head of the National Intelligence Service. Captain Jeong Minho-nim. It is an honor to meet a hero of the nation.,

The nation.

It had been a long time since he had heard someone directly say a word he only heard during ideological education.

,I have never committed any military corruption.,

,I know. I didn't come because of that. I came to make you an offer. Would you like to join the Holy Knights? The Knights have chosen you.,

Holy Knights.

No matter how many times he heard it, the name didn't suit the military.

,...Thank you for the offer, but I lost my right arm. It's hard for me to even shoot a gun properly with this body.,

,That's a minor problem. The factors that the Knights evaluated in you are your talent and will. I can't explain the benefits the Knights receive because they are classified, but I can tell you that they are enormous.,

,...If I join the Knights, will He heal my arm?,

Seong Yujin's healing abilities were already famous. The problem was that not everyone who was injured could receive his healing abilities. If you were lucky, you could be healed when he occasionally came to volunteer for the military.

,Of course.,

,...I will join. I'd feel a bit wronged to be discharged like this.,

,You won't regret it.,

He followed Lee Yeonhee to the Knights' training center. It was a place that only a few people knew about.

There, he was able to meet other knight trainees who had been selected similarly to him. There were less than 20 of them. All of them were exceptionally talented.

He met Him and had his right arm healed. The sight of his severed right arm growing back in an instant was a miracle in itself.

,So you're a knight trainee. I look forward to the day you become a knight.,

,Yes. Your Excellency. No, Seong-nim.,

Combat training. Mental training. Food. Sleep. A little free time.

The rigorous curriculum continued.

It was bearable. At least it was much better than rolling around with Variants on the battlefield. The food was delicious, and the salary was high.

He also learned about the benefits of becoming a knight.

Immortality.

You don't die.

You don't age.

You can share those benefits with your family and friends.

,My father would be very happy to know about this benefit.,

He thought of his slightly immature father, mother, and siblings.

If his family members died, he could ask Him to revive them. That was the price of devotion to Him.

,The gods above don't grant wishes even if you pray, but He uses His power for us.,

He witnessed a miracle.

A comrade who died in a training exercise that was like actual combat was born again.

He experienced a miracle.

He died in training and was reborn. He was resurrected using the heart he had offered a few months ago as a medium.

He felt the memories and experiences from before death intact. Jeong Minho realized the existence of the soul as he looked at his dead body. If the soul didn't reside in this body from that body, it wouldn't make sense for his memories to continue when he was resurrected with the heart he had offered a few months ago.

,God exists. He is our savior.,

After a long training,

He became a knight. He became a knight of the Thunderbolt Knights and performed his first mission in a large-scale Roach extermination operation.

Jeong Minho killed 230 Roaches that day.

Missions continued afterward.

At first, it was Variant extermination, but gradually, he had to deal with humans more often.

Foolish rebels who could not accept His rule.

And heretics who ridiculed faith in Him.

One day, while performing his mission with a heart devoted to Him.

,Knight Jeong Minho. You are being discharged from the Thunderbolt Knights.,

,Yes? Did I do something wrong?,

,Your tendencies and fighting style do not suit the Thunderbolt Knights. Your fighting style is closer to the military.,

,...I understand. I can't shake off my habits from my military days. Am I going to the Second Knights?,

,No, that place doesn't suit your tendencies either. You will become a member of the Sixth Knights.,

,The Sixth Knights? It seems like it's being newly established. If someone like me is going to gather... it's probably a knight order with a military-like atmosphere rather than the existing knights. Who is the commander of the knight order?,

,You have to decide the name of the knight order. Do you have a name in mind?,

,...!!,

He became the commander of the 6th Knights, Millitrigger. He named it by combining the words Military and Trigger.

His personal color was khaki. It was a color that Koreans commonly called military green.

The symbolic emblem was a pistol with its muzzle pointing upward.

It was the most military-like knight order among the knight orders.

-Knight Commander Jeong Minho. This is Millitrigger's first mission. Execute the rebel Jeong Jugyeong and his entire family.

Jeong Minho paused at the voice of the combat AI, Jena.

,...Jeong Jugyeong? Could it be.,

-Yes. He is your father. He is plotting a rebellion in Hamgyeong Province in collusion with a general from the Northern Army.

,...I know that the three generations of rebels are annihilated. Why was I excluded?,

-You are a knight. He trusts you.

Is this His test?

Or is it a loyalty test?

Jeong Minho, who had been contemplating briefly, spoke.

,I want to meet Him.,

-Approved.

• • •

Kung.

A khaki-colored heavy power armor rose from inside an assault helicopter.

Jeong Minho and the 10 Millitrigger knights checked their weapons.

It was a fortress that had been completely remodeled from a Northern Army military base. The rebels were trying to grow their power by barricading themselves there. Their mission was to go inside and execute the rebels and their families.

,Check equipment.,

Cheolkeok. Cheolkeok. Cheolkeok.

The knights checked their guns, swords, and grenades. The most important thing was the rifle.

,Equipment check complete. All clear.,

They were silent.

Jeong Minho didn't give a speech or anything.

This mission was just one of countless missions.

Then, the assault helicopter headed toward the fortress area.

Tutatatatatatatatang.

Machine guns fired from the ground. But they were blocked by the armor of the assault helicopter. The special assault helicopter could withstand even small missiles.

Cheolkeok!

The helicopter door opened, and the knights jumped out without hesitation. They landed from a moderate height, trusting in their power armor.

As if they had been waiting, bullets and shells flew from all directions.

,You goddamn knights! Die!,

Jeong Minho and the knights responded with rifles and moved quickly.

Kwaaaang!

A knight was directly hit by a shell fired by a tank and flew away with the explosion. The power armor withstood the shell, but it couldn't absorb all the impact. One knight died. Jeong Minho and the other knights didn't care.

,Team 1, follow me to execute the rebel leader. Team 2, take care of the tank and execute all the rebels without exception.,

Jeong Minho, who gave the order, ran inside the building with Team 1, consisting of four people. Originally, there were five people in a team, but one had died from the tank's attack earlier.

They shot and killed the enemies they saw with rifles and smashed the iron doors with grenades.

Rebels died every time a gunshot rang out. The rebels mainly responded with bombs, but the effect was not great. The trained knights killed the enemies first before the bombs exploded.

Jeong Minho met the rebel leader in the best mansion inside the fortress. It was his father.

,You dictator's bastards...!,

Jeong Jugyeong used his ability. Hwareureuk. Flames rose and surrounded Jeong Minho and the knights. Jeong Minho took out a fire extinguishing grenade from his waist and threw it on the floor. The special fire extinguishing liquid burst and extinguished the flames at once.

He had prepared it because he knew Jeong Jugyeong's ability.

,Fire, the fire isn't rising...?!,

Tang.

Jeong Minho's rifle bullet hit Jeong Jugyeong's thigh. Because the caliber was so large, his leg was shattered.

Jeong Minho approached Jeong Jugyeong, and his helmet was removed, revealing his head.

,Minho?!,

,Father.,

,You, why are you...?! Didn't you say you were in the military?!,

,My existence is classified. Father. Why did you commit treason?,

,Seong Yujin! That bastard is a dictator! He will definitely plunge Korea into hell someday!,

,...So you joined hands with a rebel?,

,I will kill him and save Korea! I will become the true leader of this Korea! Son, help me! If you help me, we can drive out the dictator and save Korea!,

It's no use talking.

He can understand that he's rambling in the face of death.

And Jeong Minho knew why his father had started the rebellion. He must have thought he was special because he became an Awakened One and gained power. But Korea cannot favor people just because they are Awakened Ones. Unless they are very outstanding Awakened Ones, they are just ordinary citizens.

Jeong Jugyeong was an ordinary person who was intoxicated with his own abilities and harbored ambitions that were beyond his capabilities.

Jeong Minho's gun was aimed at Jeong Jugyeong's head.

,...Minho?,

,Father. Originally, the leaders of the rebellion do not die easily. They are not granted the mercy of death. I asked Him for mercy, and He allowed your death. The condition is that I bear your sins and atone for them.,

,Minho! I'm your father! I'm your father! Are you going to ignore the natural order and commit patricide?!,

,He is above the heavens.,

Tang.

Jeong Minho's head exploded. Jeong Minho, who quietly watched the scattering fragments, headed to the next room.

His mother and two younger siblings were there. They were hugging each other and trembling.

,Min, Minho.,

,Hyung...!,

,O, Oppa....,

Jeong Minho pointed his gun at his family.

Jeong Minho didn't cry.

There was His mercy.

,Mother. This is not a permanent farewell. I will atone for the sins of my mother and siblings. On the day all sins disappear, our family will meet again.,

Jeong Minho pulled the trigger.

Tangtangtang.

Jeong Minho quietly collected their bodies.

Five minutes later, the rebels and their families were all executed. The mission was over.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

It was when they were preparing to withdraw. Suddenly, a huge explosion rang out, and the ground shook.

,Jena. What was that just now? Did a bomb explode nearby?,

-Mount Baekdu has erupted.
