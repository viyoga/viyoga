,You dropped your weapon, Paladin!!"

The giant raven, Parason, flapping its wings in the air, descended upon the Paladin as soon as it spotted an opportunity. Its sharp beak, capable of chewing through steel, aimed for the back of the Paladin's neck.

The Paladin spun around. He grabbed Parason's nape with his left hand and seized its wing with his right.

,Even without a sword, I can kill the likes of you."

The Paladin exerted force in his hands.

,Kkaaaaaaak!"

Parason let out a painful scream. Blood burst from its wing. The Paladin, covered in blood, continued to apply pressure. Finally, Parason's wing was completely torn off and fell to the ground.

The Paladin grabbed the other wing and tore it off just like before.

Parason, with both wings torn off, trembled. It almost looked pitiful. Of course, the Paladin, without an ounce of sympathy, grabbed Parason's neck and legs, tearing it apart to its death.

Baron Heilgaide stared at the Paladin with a stiff expression.

The brute strength to tear a demon apart alive and the divine power to evaporate his magic.

It was unexpected.

Valentier's Paladin had already reached a state of perfection.

Cold sweat trickled down.

,It hasn't even been three months since that bastard received the revelation. But he's gotten this strong so quickly? No way...!"

There must be some trickery he didn't know about.

,Even if there's trickery, can he really get this strong?"

Dangerous.

The Paladin before him was more dangerous than any Paladin he had ever seen. This Paladin would become even stronger than he was now, strong enough to threaten the Great Demon. The Great Demon was the heart and future of the demons. He had to kill the Paladin before him today.

,Do you know? Demon blood contains magic power. Therefore, it is more special than any other blood."

,Your blood will be spilled too."

The Paladin approached. The Baron, feigning composure, desperately moved his magic power to cast a spell. Blood scattered in all directions gathered. At the same time, the blood bubbled like lava.

The Paladin didn't stop. He gripped the greatsword stuck in the ground again and stepped through the hot blood, approaching Baron Heilgaide.

The Baron laughed. A laugh that welled up from the bottom of his heart.

,You've walked into your own grave. Blame your ignorance and arrogance."

The blood bubbling around him surged and flew towards the Paladin. The blood enveloped the Paladin in a large sphere shape.

The Baron was certain of his victory.

No human or demon had ever survived this magic. Especially this time, it was magic cast with demon blood. The Paladin would be compressed by the blood and die without even leaving a proper corpse.

But once again, the Paladin defied his expectations.

The blade of the sword popped out from the compressing sphere of blood. Golden energy flowed along the blade. The Baron desperately squeezed out his magic power.

It was useless.

When the Paladin swung his sword, the sphere of blood split apart with ridiculous ease. The sphere of blood lost its power and poured onto the ground. The Paladin, showering in demon blood, reached the Baron.

,No way...!"

,What's impossible is you bastards. Your existence cannot be tolerated."

The Paladin raised his greatsword. The Baron moved faster. He swung his long claws at the Paladin. But before his claws could even touch the Paladin's armor, the Baron's lower body was already severed. Blood and guts spilled onto the floor.

The Paladin grabbed the head of the collapsing Baron and lifted it up. Their eyes met. But the Baron couldn't see the Paladin's eyes. The solid helmet completely concealed the Paladin's eyes. The Baron tried to remain dignified until the end, but he couldn't. Fear consumed the Baron's face.

,You will lose as much as the blood you have drunk."

,What...?"

The Paladin moved his sword. He cut off both of Baron Heilgaide's arms as if he were doing a job.

Immediately after, a cross appeared in front of the Paladin. He placed the Baron on the cross, which emitted a sacred aura, and placed a stake against his chest. The Paladin drove the stake in with his fist instead of a hammer.

,Aaaaaaaak! You, you bastard! Just kill me!"

At this point, the Baron predicted what the Paladin was about to do.

Vampire blood, if properly purified, could be a good medicinal ingredient. This terrifying Paladin before him was planning to seal him on the cross and constantly draw blood from him! He was going to turn him into livestock, like a milk cow.

Feeling immense fear and humiliation, Baron Heilgaide shouted at the Paladin, who was about to carry the cross.

,Kill me! Kill me! Uwaaaaaaaak! I am your enemy! Will you spare your enemy! Kill me quickly!"

,Shut up."

The Paladin's rough hand tore off the Baron's jaw. Only then did the Baron fall silent. For the first time in his life, the Baron resented his superior demon body. If he had been just a little less superior, he would have died before experiencing such humiliation.

The Paladin carried the cross and went outside. The holy knights and inquisitors bowed as if they had been waiting.

,Congratulations on your victory."

,It was only natural."

,...You have also achieved your purpose. I don't really like it, but I will follow your will for the future of the Order."

The holy knight said. The holy knights felt uneasy and uncomfortable about not killing Baron Heilgaide.

On the other hand, the inquisitors were different. They admired the new Paladin and showed him infinite reverence.

,You are amazing, Paladin-isiyeo! Leave the management of the vampire to us! We will make him spill all the information!"

,That's what I was planning from the start. Take care of the demon's corpse and clean up the surroundings. We're going back to the cathedral."

•••

After returning to the cathedral, I entrusted Baron Heilgaide to the inquisitors and headed to the sanctuary. I immediately entered the sacred pond and received a purification ritual from the Saintess.

The purification ritual, which would normally end quickly, felt particularly long today.

This was because there were three demon souls clinging to me. Moreover, they were of a higher caliber than the demons I had faced so far. If I were to face even stronger demons in the future, the purification ritual would take even longer.

After the purification ritual, I used the energy of blessing to learn a new skill.

[Name: Eugene

Origin: Holy Knight

Level: 62

Strength: 42 Agility: 20 Stamina: 20 Divine Power: 25

Skills: Holy Sight (Lv. Master), Holy Aura (Lv.7), Holy Sword (Lv.7), Sprint (Lv.3), Holy Light (Lv.3) Divine Enhancement (Lv. Master)]

The newly acquired skill, Divine Enhancement, was a passive skill. It enhances the effects of divine power and other skills.

I felt satisfied and rose from the sacred pond. I wiped my body with the prepared towel and put on my clothes. Afterwards, I sat on a chair and rested quietly.

,As expected, resting in the sanctuary is much better, both mentally and physically, than resting anywhere else."

The sanctuary was overflowing with more sacred energy than anywhere else. Just being here made me feel stable.

,Paladin-isiyeo."

The Saintess approached. She naturally sat next to me. Unlike when we first met, she treated me quite comfortably. The Saintess handed me something.

,What's this?"

,It's Prube's wine."

,...Wine manufactured by the Papal State. It wouldn't have been easy to obtain."

,I asked the Papal State directly."

Even if the Saintess's position was lower than expected, it couldn't be completely ignored. The Papal State must have been taken aback when the Saintess suddenly requested alcohol.

,Are you giving it to me?"

,Yes. Please accept it. You have given me so much food and items, Paladin-kke. Please accept it."

Seeing the wine reminded me of Elena.

The finest wine from another world. She would definitely drool and rush over to get it. But I had no intention of taking this wine before me.

,Have you ever drunk alcohol?"

,No. I heard that men like alcohol, so I prepared it."

,Is that so?"

I took out a wine glass from my inventory. I was now openly using my inventory. If anyone asked what power it was, I would vaguely gloss over it. No one questioned me deeply. Because I am the Paladin chosen by the Goddess.

I placed two wine glasses on the table. One in front of me, one in front of her.

,It's a clean glass. It's pretty."

,It's a wine glass. It's suitable for drinking wine."

,...Are you offering me wine? I can't drink alcohol."

,That's true when you're a nun. You're not a nun, you're a Saintess. There shouldn't be any restrictions on abstinence, right?"

,That's true, but..."

,Drinking alcohol doesn't make you corrupt. And they say you shouldn't drink alcohol alone."

,......"

I opened the wine cork and poured the wine into the glasses. A sweet scent that stimulated the sense of smell spread throughout the sanctuary. Amelia hesitated, then grabbed the wine glass.

She took off her veil with her other hand. I stared at her intently. It had been quite some time since I met her, but it was the first time I had seen her without her veil. That meant she thought of me as close to her.

,She's pretty."

Blue eyes and bright blonde hair. Pink, plump lips. A face as perfect as a sculpture.

I picked up the wine glass. She stared blankly, then belatedly seemed to realize something and picked up her glass.

Cheers!

The glasses clinked. I enjoyed the aroma and then sipped a bit of wine before swallowing. The aroma and taste were both excellent. The aftertaste was also clean. It was worthy of being the best wine in this world.

Amelia followed me and drank the wine. Soon, her eyes widened.

,...It's delicious. So this is what it tastes like. But it feels like... my body is getting hot."

,That's probably the alcohol circulating in your body. It's not a bad thing."

Afterwards, we drank the wine quietly. Amelia surprisingly drank the wine well. I wondered if she had developed a taste for wine.

,Paladin-isiyeo. We have obtained information that an evil ritual is taking place at Salrot Castle."

Salrot Castle.

As soon as I heard that name, I quietly swallowed my breath.

It felt like what was coming had finally arrived.

,An evil ritual? Do you know what kind of ritual it is?"

,The ritual is being conducted by a demon named Barbaroyax. He is a subordinate of the Great Demon Suton. He was originally human, but he made a contract with Suton and became a necromancer. He is conducting a ritual to resurrect Suton."

,Suton... the Great Demon that the Paladins gathered to kill."

,Yes. The previous Valentier's Paladin died in that holy war. Originally, the resurrection of a Great Demon would take decades... Barbaroyax is shortening that period through the ritual. We must stop the ritual. If Suton is resurrected, he will target the Paladin. Paladin-keseo still needs more time to grow."

,I understand. I will infiltrate Salrot Castle alone."

,...Yes?"

,All other troops should stand by nearby. Barbaroyax is a cunning bastard. He must have prepared traps. If we just blindly knock on the castle, only sacrifices will increase. I will infiltrate the castle first, eliminate the traps, and open the gates."

,...Isn't that too dangerous?"

,It is dangerous. But the rewards are certain as much as it is dangerous."

I know the strategy for Salrot Castle. I can get a large amount of experience and guarantee a certain victory. I couldn't miss this opportunity.

The Saintess knew that my will was firm and said as if she couldn't help it.

,...I will follow the Paladin's will."
