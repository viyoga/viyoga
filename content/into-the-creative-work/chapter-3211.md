『Karma: Using 15 Good.』

『Karma: Using 21 Good.』

『Karma: Using 11 Good.』

Notification windows had been popping up in the corner of my vision for a while now. I hadn't consciously used Karma at all, yet it was being consumed automatically.

'Surely the System hasn't suddenly glitched out in the middle of this critical situation.'

The very idea of an error occurring in the System—the law of this world—was impossible.

'Maybe Karma is being used according to my actions, perhaps even unconsciously, in accordance with what I desire.'

Spirits poured out from the Spirit Gate and cooperated with me, and thousands of heroes arrived right on time. On top of that, Justin had brought two additional S-rank American heroes.

This much firepower showed up less than five minutes after the demons invaded, as if it had been waiting for this moment?

'In the original work too, high Good Karma gave you good feelings about probabilistic events. Like critical hits landing more often. You could say that consuming Karma brings great fortune to follow you.'

Heroes and demons began fighting. A few fearless heroes targeted Mekin directly. Mekin furrowed his brow.

"Insects who don't know their place."

Mekin raised his right foot and stomped the ground. A shockwave mixed with dark crimson demonic energy radiated outward from him. The bodies of heroes, demons, and spirits who failed to dodge the shockwave burst apart without even feeling the pressure. Everyone trembled at the ruthlessness of killing indiscriminately, ally and enemy alike.

Justin pulled his hand from his pocket. Light gathered in his hand and took the form of a sword.

"That demon is the Demon King of Destruction, the 5th Corps Commander. I'll handle him. Heroes, please take care of the other demons."

Justin, his entire body wrapped in golden light, charged at Mekin. So fast that even I could barely track his speed with full concentration.

Beside Mekin's head, space split open and a golden demon statue fell out. Before the statue could even drop past Mekin's shoulder, it shattered into pieces.

Mekin swung his fist at Justin, who was charging straight at him.

KWAAAAAAANG!

A dark crimson flash covered the golden light and blazed. Justin, struck, came flying at me like a bullet.

I extended my hand and used the power of wind.

I caught Justin in midair, minimizing the damage as much as possible. His body stopped before it could collide with mine.

It seemed he'd been hit in the face—his handsome features were a mess. His nose bridge was broken, blood streamed from his mouth, and his left eye couldn't even open properly.

"...What fucking brute force. To think you could deal this much damage to me when I've turned half my body into light."

"It's the Authority of Destruction he possesses. He gains power by sacrificing what's his. The more precious the offering, the better the effect. What's annoying is that this power is fixed damage, in game terms. It's a force that ignores defense. No matter how excellent your armor, no matter if you use a barrier that could withstand a nuclear bomb, blocking it is impossible."

"...You're telling me not to fight him head-on?"

"Dodging is best. If that's difficult, canceling his attack with your own is second best."

Mekin swung his greatsword.

A massive dark crimson slash came flying. I dodged with wind, Justin with the power of light.

"...It would be best if you and I fought together. One person draws his attention while the other stabs at his openings."

"No. We've never once coordinated our movements. It would be a minus, not a plus. Why do you think that bastard is leisurely watching us talk? He's hoping we'll cooperate. I'll handle him."

"...Understood. You predicted the demon legion's invasion, so I'll trust you. I'll make sure the other demons can't interfere with you. Win."

Justin, wrapped in light, charged toward the other demons.

"You don't need to tell me. I'm going to win."

Bzzt, crackle.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder Spear (卍雷槍).

I hurled the compressed lightning generated from my palm at Mekin. Mekin lightly jumped to dodge. His wings moved. He didn't fall—he soared upward.

"You sent that shining one away. You intend to face me alone?"

"To beat your opponent, the standard approach is to not do what they want."

"Kuhuhu. Well said."

"Mekin. Have you prepared enough offerings?"

"I've scraped together plenty, so don't you worry."

Mekin charged first. One of the offerings he pulled from his subspace shattered.

Immediately after, his body appeared before me. He'd used the immense power gained from the Authority of Destruction for movement.

Just like the Flash Step I use.

[Using Flash Step (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

I dodged Mekin's greatsword aimed at my neck and summoned the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade (火蓮飛刀), driving it into his sagging gut.

Blood spurted.

Mekin swung his bare fist as if nothing had happened.

[Using Leap.]

Appearing above Mekin's head, I brought my fist down on his skull with all my strength.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Wave Strike (霹波鎭).

Mekin's head burst. I was taken aback seeing the scattered brains and skull fragments.

'He died this easily?'

Below his burst head, in the spot where his molars should have been, a gem that looked like an artifact was embedded instead of teeth. Suddenly the gem shattered.

'He used it as an offering?!'

He'd hidden them so that his offerings couldn't be identified by appearance alone.

Thump.

Mekin's hand grabbed my leg. His shattered head had regenerated in an instant.

"Kuhuhu. Finally caught the fly."

[Leap cannot be used.]

His demonic energy interfered with spatial movement. Mekin thrust his greatsword into my torso without hesitation.

Puuuuushk.

No blood came out. My body right now was a spirit made of wind. But that didn't mean there was no damage. His Authority of Destruction was definitely applied. It was just that Arest took the damage I received in my stead.

[Kyaaaaaaah!]

While Arest endured, I pulled out the World Destroyer equipped with the Netherworld Mana Cube.

Piercing mode.

The barrel aimed at Mekin. Naturally, I adjusted the angle so the damage wouldn't extend downward.

Mekin protected his body with the demonic energy gained from using three offerings.

"Even you can't dodge this from here."

Click.

The moment I pulled the trigger, green light shot from the barrel toward the sky. The overwhelming output of the attack blew away his upper body in one shot. Mekin's body, left with only the lower half, fell limply downward.

[Krrgh... This is my limit too...]

Arest, in the spirit-summoned state, was reverse-summoned and I returned to my original body.

『Using Thunder Eye Golden Pupil (雷眼金睛).』

Mekin had hidden offerings in both his shins. The offerings embedded in both shins cracked and his body regenerated.

'What he's most wary of is instant death. In preparation for that, he's embedded offerings throughout his body. Like my Complete Recovery's death resistance, does he retain some consciousness even when his head is blown off? Not the body—the mind. Does he have some power related to the soul? In a world where necromancy exists, it wouldn't be strange.'

There's no law saying demons can't learn necromancy.

'If it's not that, then maybe he programmed his Authority to activate upon near-death.'

Mekin's rank wasn't low, so it was hard to fully grasp with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.

Mekin stood atop the Demon Island as if nothing had happened.

"That was dangerous. If my legs had been blown off too... I might have died. Kuhuhu."

"Liar. Even if your legs had been blown off, those things you embedded in your shins wouldn't have been. Even if your legs were destroyed, you'd have revived through the offerings. From the looks of it, those are gems that absolutely cannot be destroyed. Did you pre-store your Authority in them?"

"You recognized the nature of my treasures immediately. You've got interesting eyes. Not that it matters. I was planning to slowly learn what cards you hold... but it seems you have quite a few. From here on, I'll go all out."

Space tore open above Mekin's head and the entrance to his subspace gaped wide. Dazzling treasures poured down.

Crshk!

The treasures shattered, becoming power that seeped into Mekin. Dark crimson demonic energy blazed like fire across his entire body.

"World Destroyer, was it? Unless it's a weapon of that caliber, you can't kill me. And you can't use that weapon for a while."

Mekin swung his greatsword like a madman, unleashing a barrage of slashes.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Dodging sideways as much as possible, I extended my right hand toward the sky.

'Heavenly Cloud Sword (天叢雲劍).'

The dark clouds in the sky condensed in an instant, becoming a single sword that fell into my hand like lightning.

'Flaming Lotus Flying Blade (火蓮飛刀).'

I gripped the red hilt with my left hand.

'Domain Expansion.'

I grabbed the domain expanding to seize the space with my will and wrapped it around my body.

"So this is the power that killed Jarkan."

Mekin pulled out dozens more offerings and shattered them, loading the power of destruction into his body. His mere existence shattered the Demon Island, made the sea surge, and shook the heavens. If Mekin threw a punch now, an entire city would vanish.

"I've decided. You, above all others, will die by my hand."

[Activating Heaven's Heart (天心). Lasts for 1 minute.]

My physical abilities easily surpassed their limits, and my mana became infinite.

I poured infinite mana into the Heavenly Cloud Sword and Flaming Lotus Flying Blade, squeezing to the limit regardless of internal injuries.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Calamity (雷劫).

Blue lightning gathered on the Heavenly Cloud Sword, red lightning on the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade.

But this alone wouldn't be enough. I already had the means to fill that gap.

『Using the Authority of Strength (SSS).』

Mekin twisted his waist and swung his greatsword with everything he had.

Our weapons crossed.

Even though force and force collided and canceled each other out to some degree, a massive shockwave erupted and swept the surroundings. Mekin and I, our skin bursting and tearing until we were drenched in blood, didn't stop our contest of strength. Each other's muscles ruptured, and the bones of our arms tore through flesh and jutted out.

Dark crimson demonic energy and red and blue lightning devoured each other.

I used mana and lightning to control my arms so they wouldn't lose strength.

'That bastard's shoulder is wrecked too! He's holding on with demonic energy! Whoever backs down here loses!'

Offerings fell from Mekin's subspace. Mekin didn't convert all the offerings into power—he used a portion of them to heal his body.

I looked at him and curled my lips into a smirk.

"You talked all that big game about pride, but looks like you don't want to die after all?"

"I merely made the efficient choice."

"Thanks to that, an opening came to me!"

I swung the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade. Mekin twisted his head aside as if he'd predicted it. The red blade of the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade passed his head and sank halfway into his subspace. Bzzt, crackle! Red lightning and my domain instantly seized the subspace.

A domain is fundamentally a power that governs laws. If you stab it in directly like this, even Mekin can't use his subspace.

"...You were aiming for the subspace from the beginning?!"

"The biggest weakness of the Authority of Destruction. Even if you have offerings, you can only use them if they're nearby. You can't remotely sacrifice the ones in your subspace, can you?"

"...Even if I can't pull out more offerings, what I've already used is more than enough to kill you."

"Same goes for me."

I stepped back. Putting about 3 meters of distance between me and Mekin, I raised the Heavenly Cloud Sword high.

The Heavenly Cloud Sword is itself a cloud. A sword condensed from the vast dark clouds that could overturn a city and still have more to spare.

From the moment the Heavenly Cloud Sword was summoned, the lightning within its dark clouds resonated and generated power without stopping for even an instant.

The blade of the Heavenly Cloud Sword, compressed with tens of millions of lightning bolts, shone blue. Sensing something ominous, Mekin sacrificed even the greatsword in his hand as an offering to amplify his demonic energy. Dark crimson demonic energy compressed into his fist.

I swung my sword at him.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Ultimate Technique (極技) — Thunder God's Command (雷神命).

A compressed lightning in the form of a sword aura annihilated Mekin's dark crimson demonic energy and split the Demon Island and the sea in half.

Mekin's entire body except his head was obliterated, and the seawater that directly touched the sword aura boiled and churned, generating massive amounts of steam.

Mekin's face, left with only a head, contorted like a vengeful demon.

"This damn... This kind of ending... Did the Demon God truly abandon me, abandon us."

I walked toward Mekin's head, carrying the Heavenly Cloud Sword crackling with lightning.

"Mekin. Did you think you'd be safe after backstabbing me?"

"...Backstab? What nonsense are you spouting. Weren't you a demon hunter who hated me because I'm a demon?"

"I never let anyone who backstabs me live. It took longer than I expected, but... I always repay what's done to me."

"...To think I'd die to a psychopath like this..."

I drove the Heavenly Cloud Sword into Mekin's head. Lightning exploded, burning Mekin's head entirely to ash.

I trembled unknowingly at the sweetness of revenge achieved by my own power. Of course, it didn't feel as good as sex.

"Mekin, cut down. He was more of a pushover than I thought."
