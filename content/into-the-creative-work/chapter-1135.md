Space shatters like a mirror. Emerging from the cracks in the shattered space is Kang Ji-young. Behind her, the flustered Academy teachers and students could be glimpsed.

Heundeul.

I couldn't just stand by and be attacked. As I was gathering my mana to respond, Kang Ji-young said to me.

,I'll take care of it, so stay put.

Kang Ji-young took a stance. The blood that had been forming into arms shattered and splattered against the cave walls. The skull charms that had been spewing blood were destroyed.

Before I knew it, she was in front of the 1st-year student, swinging her fist with a distorted face.

,Kihiiiiiit!

The 1st-year student screamed.

,...I'm sorry.

Kang Ji-young apologized softly and swung her fist again. The ghost endured this time as well. The immense curse was the ghost's driving force. But Kang Ji-young's attack was just beginning. Each time she swung her fist, the space trembled.

Pasak! Pat! Pasasak!

,Kiheeeeeeeek!

The ghost desperately spewed out curses. The curses pierced Kang Ji-young's body, but Kang Ji-young's fist didn't stop.

,Seong Yujin-ssi. The duel is your victory against the spirit. Go outside and wait. Don't act rashly.

The outside was chaos itself.

The 50-year-old Vice-Principal Kwon Sang-hoon shouted, veins bulging in his neck. The teachers he pointed out rushed off.

Non-combat teachers were escorting the students, while combat teachers were searching for Kim Je-o and running outside.

,If Kim Je-o resists, you can kill him! Just don't let him get away!

,Kim Je-o from the original game wasn't like this....

The change didn't matter much. It was inevitable that things would change as an anomaly like me entered this world.

What I was curious about was the reason for the change. Not knowing made me feel uneasy.

He didn't doubt the Charm Department's victory. The precious charms he had taken out of the old warehouse and the charms he had received from them. With those, Seong Yujin wouldn't last 30 minutes before collapsing.

That's what he thought.

The situation didn't unfold as Kim Je-o had predicted.

Seong Yujin had brought an item that blocked curses. It easily blocked intermediate and advanced curses. It was clearly not an ordinary item.

,Seong Hari! Seong Hari, that woman intervened for her son!

Kim Je-o bit his lip tightly. He wanted to shout that this wasn't fair, but he knew that the other teachers and students would think differently.

,It's okay. Unless it's a sacred object, there's bound to be a limit to how long he can withstand the curse.

Seong Yujin wouldn't last long.

That's how it had to be.

But the situation continued differently from Kim Je-o's wishes.

,...Ki Ha-wook started the curse.

Ki Ha-wook.

A 1st-year student who entered the Charm Department this year.

Although a 1st-year student, his talent was second only to Shin Nari. Kim Je-o had handed Ki Ha-wook dozens of dangerous offerings. He had calculated that Ki Ha-wook could last at least an hour.

Ki Ha-wook's curse was perfect.

Using Seong Yujin's hair, which he had prepared in advance, he specified the target of the curse and invoked the curse with the power of the charms.

But Seong Yujin withstood that curse as well.

,Just how amazing of an item did he bring...!

Once Ki Ha-wook's curse started, it wouldn't stop. It couldn't stop.

Curses have severe risks. If it fails once, the curse returns to the caster. Charms are used to minimize the risk. But even that has its limits.

Although there was a barrier suit that blocked fatal damage, the risk of the curse was different in nature, so it didn't work properly.

If too many risks accumulated, Ki Ha-wook would die.

Kim Je-o's fingertips trembled.

Kim Je-o breathed a sigh of relief at Shin Nari's appearance.

The battle scene between Shin Nari and Seong Yujin was being broadcast on a large screen. Shin Nari was pouring on the attacks, but Seong Yujin had consistently maintained control of the battle.

,...Amazing. His swordsmanship is already at the level of a master. But do you know what that swordsmanship is?

,With that power, she'll be able to undo the Demon Subduing Bandage.

Shin Nari's seal was released.

The reason why Shin Nari, a 2nd-year student, didn't go on field training was simple. Because she couldn't fully control that power.

Immense curses spread in all directions. Shin Nari's body was a charm itself. The curse was her power.

Ki Ha-wook.
