As time passed, my expression worsened.

Because I wasn't getting much. I did manage to get some stuff from the hardware store... but it was far less than I had originally planned.

My face hardened.

,I planned this with a big heart... but the things I'm actually getting are too few.'

I underestimated Seoul too much. No, I underestimated Seoul's population too much. Even with the situation like this, people are thriftily gathering supplies and trying to survive somehow.

"Zombie horde spotted on the road ahead! Estimated number: 70!"

I focused on the front. A horde of zombies was running towards us. I drew my pure white sword, the Cutlass Sword, from my waist.

Pajijijik.

As the white blade absorbed electricity, it sparkled in silver. That was why this sword was called the Cutlass Sword.

Lightning Heaven Style, Lightning Flash.

Swinging it towards the empty air, sword energy wrapped in lightning flew out, cutting through the approaching zombie horde. But I couldn't wipe them all out at once. The survivors fearlessly charged at us. The security team with guns stepped forward, pulled the triggers, and finished off the rest.

Pareureureu.

My hand trembled.

,Damn it. I only unleashed one sword energy with all my power, and my hand is shaking.'

The technique that I could spam countless times in the Guangmyeong Ascendant World was not the same in this world. I had to be careful about this.

The last destination of the subjugation force was a factory. The factory's name was Baepyo. It was a factory specializing in making wooden interior materials. The wood materials were important, but the machines were the main goal. With the power of the Technologist, Na Chaeyoung-ssi, it was possible to modify the machines.

A drone flew ahead into the factory.

Tang, tang, tang!

With the sound of gunfire, the reconnaissance drone that was flying well crashed.

-Armed humans are occupying the factory.

"They have guns? Are they government affiliated?"

-Not the military. The clothes they're wearing were casual.

"But they have guns...."

Could there be a Technologist like Na Chaeyoung-ssi among them? The possibility is low. Why would a Player join hands with those kinds of guys? They'd rather join hands with the national government or a corporation.

"They probably got them through the black market. Or from the military."

Since the world is a mess, anything is possible.

-What are you going to do? Are you going to back down?

"Are you sure there are machines in the factory?"

-For sure. Otherwise, there's no reason to occupy the factory, right? There's a rumor going around that the government is buying up intact factory machines these days... they're probably occupying it because of that.

"If there are machines, we have to get them."

We couldn't use the combat drones. Right now, the combat drones are being used to protect Seongak Elementary School. And if we were going to use combat drones, we wouldn't have brought out this many troops.

"Should we go head-on?"

Should I take the lead? Or should I push in the troops? Taking the lead is a great way to get shot. Getting shot hurts. Is there any need for me to endure that kind of pain? It's better to lose troops. We can always draft more troops. There are slaves and humans all over the place.

"We don't need a strategy. Our equipment is better anyway. Security team, prepare to charge. Ram them with the trucks."

"Wait a minute! Representative Seong-nim! I have a good idea!"

Someone suddenly popped out. It was a middle-aged man holding a spear in his hand. Looking at his condition, he wasn't part of the security team either.

The security team members around him were shocked.

"This crazy bastard!"

"How dare you speak so carelessly to someone?!"

"Do you want to die?!!"

A security team member grabbed his body.

I stared at him indifferently, then smiled as I looked into the man's eyes. A half-crazed look. He was no different from a madman. The security team around him was also tense.

"Good. Tell me. If it's useless, I'll make you pay the price."

"I will infiltrate the factory by going around it. Just give me a rifle."

"Why?"

"...I can cause confusion among them and make it easier to take the factory."

"That's only if you succeed. Can you do that alone? What, are you from a special forces unit or something?"

"I was discharged from the Special Forces."

"If you're from the Special Forces, you could have gone to the security team."

"......I deliberately hid my background. After being discharged, I worked as a pastor."

"A pastor? Do you believe in God?"

"I don't."

"Keukeu. You're an interesting guy. Good. Give this bastard a rifle and magazines. But if you fail, you're dead."

"I'm prepared to die. So, if I succeed..."

"If you succeed? What do you want?"

"...Please use me importantly."

Is it a desire for power?

No, guys with that kind of look in their eyes usually want something of their own, not power. And if he wanted power, he would have made allies. Looking around, he was alone.

In these cases, it's one of two things. He's either the real deal, or just a crazy bastard.

"What's your name?"

"Son Taehyung-ssi."

"Is that so? Go ahead."

Son Taehyung-ssi moved. He was agile for his age. Looking at his quick actions, it seems he really was from the Special Forces.

,If he blatantly lied, I would have killed him immediately.'

To be honest, I was still doubtful, but I could always judge later.

"Ah, aah!"

A voice amplified by a megaphone echoed from the factory. I turned the troops' heads towards the factory. Since there was a wall between the entrance and us, we didn't have to worry about bullets flying over.

"This is Ilwoo Group's factory! Do not approach! If you approach, we will fire in self-defense!"

"Ilwoo Group? What kind of guys are they?"

"...It's a large corporation."

A security team member next to me said cautiously. Everyone else seemed to know the name of Ilwoo Group.

,So it's a famous company in this world.'

"I repeat! This is Ilwoo Group's factory! Do not come closer and turn back! We may fire in self-defense! We do not want to fight!"

The voice amplified by the megaphone echoed loudly. I frowned.

"Who the hell are they to tell me what to do?"

Unlike me, who was annoyed, the morale of the troops was dropping.

"Representative-nim. Wouldn't it be dangerous to mess with Ilwoo Group? Rumor has it that they're from a gangster background and grew with funds from Japan.... That's why even the government can't touch them easily."

"What bullshit are you talking about? That's just a rumor."

"......That's true, but seeing as they're armed with guns, isn't there a high possibility?"

"So. You're saying we should run away?"

I stared intently at the security team member. The guy with the title of Team Leader 2 swallowed hard and sweated profusely.

"Listen carefully. Whether those bastards are Ilwoo Group or Ilwoo Group's grandpa, as long as they're hostile to me, they're enemies to be killed. Nothing more, nothing less. Got it? We're going to kill those bastards and loot their supplies. Anyone have any complaints?"

I looked around. Everyone kept their mouths shut and watched my reaction. I tried to solve it democratically, but my opinion was unanimously passed.

,Should I check the entrance before going in?'

They had built barricades with trucks, container boxes, and drums, and were aiming their guns from behind them.

,If the barricade is like that, it'll be difficult to break through head-on, right? Judging by their appearance, it doesn't seem like there are any Awakened ones....'

If I charged in now, the soldiers would all be ground to dust. I would feel bad about losing the skilled security team, even if I didn't care about the others. It takes time to build up experience.

"Doctor Na, is a drone strike possible?"

-It's possible. But it'll consume a lot of resources. Is that factory worth that much?

"If it wasn't, would those guys be guarding it?"

-...Alright. I'll send a combat drone now.

Kwaaaang!

Suddenly, an explosion occurred at the rear of the factory. The enemies panicked and were thrown into confusion.

,Son Taehyung-ssi. Is that what that guy did after getting in? He must have used the enemies' bombs. With that kind of equipment, it wouldn't be strange for them to have bombs there.'

The guys guarding the entrance are retreating. It's becoming manageable.

"We don't need the combat drone. We're charging with the trucks in front. Let's go."

I held up a square shield and ran forward.

Tatatatatatatang!

Bullets rained down. There were fewer than I expected, but it was not enough to block everything with just one shield. Some of the bullets grazed my body or hit my arms and legs.

,It fucking hurts.'

I used Recovery. My arms and legs wouldn't move, as if the tendons had been severed, so I had no choice. The bullets that had been lodged in my arms and legs popped out. The wounds were healed without a trace. The troops behind me seemed surprised, but it didn't matter if this much was revealed.

Buaaaaang! Kwang!

The truck suddenly accelerated and narrowly passed by my side, crashing into the factory entrance.

,What the fuck, did they suddenly go crazy? We need to match our pace... no, the driver got shot and died.'

The rain of bullets stopped for a moment. The sudden accident worked as a boon. I immediately threw away the shield, drew my sword, and ran forward.

I stepped on the 3m-high barricade and jumped over it in one go. The guy on top of it was shocked and aimed his gun at me.

Seogeok!

My sword was faster, slicing the guy's neck. At the same time as he fell, I used his corpse as a shield and approached the guy next to him.

Tatatatatatatang!

Gunshots rang out from our side as well. The enemies were flustered. Some shot at me to kill me, while others held back the approaching enemies. The enemies' forces were scattered. A melee was breaking out.

Seogeok!

I cut down and kill the enemy. A melee is what I want.

Pajijik.

Stimulating my muscles with lightning, I relentlessly charged at the enemies.

Lightning Heaven Style, Electromagnetic Wave.

I spread out electromagnetic waves to find the hidden guys. In the Guangmyeong Ascendant World, it was enough to cover the entire mountain, but now it was only 15m based on my standards. Still, that was enough to find the hidden guys.

"Found you."

The guy cowering in the corner fired his pistol. I turned my body to the side to dodge the bullet, but the guy was lucky, and one of the bullets hit my right eye.

Recovery.

The bullet that pierced my brain popped out, and my brain and eyeball were healed without a trace.

Cheolkeok, cheolkeok.

The guy trembled and pulled the trigger, but no bullets came out.

"M-monster bastard."

"Hmm. Thankfully, no one saw it."

Recovering from an injury and being instantly killed and then coming back to life are on different levels.

The gunshots gradually subsided. The battle was over. Looking around roughly, there were casualties on our side as well. Three security team members died, and eight civilians died. There were some who suffered minor injuries. I was going to ignore them.

-Heal them. You need to gain at least some public sentiment.

Na Chaeyoung-ssi's voice came from the earpiece. If someone else had said it, I would have ignored them... but since it was Na Chaeyoung-ssi, there was no reason not to listen.

I healed those who suffered minor injuries.

"T-thank you! Representative-nim!"

"I'll work harder in the future!"

"M-my finger has been healed!"

The little finger that had been blown away by a bullet had been restored. The people around him were shocked. The way they looked at me became even more respectful.

,Is this the taste of a healer.'

To be honest, I didn't intend to heal the finger. I failed to control my ability. It was difficult to heal small wounds as much as I wanted.

"The scar disappeared...."

"My body feels lighter!"

"Thank you! Thank you, Representative-nim!"

I received their praise. Especially those who had recovered from minor injuries praised me with all their hearts. It wasn't a bad feeling.

"I'm a little tired from using my power. You guys take care of the rest. Pile up the corpses.... Security team, enter the factory. Kill all those who resist. Take the rest as slaves."

There were no barricades inside the factory. That meant there was little resistance. Under these conditions, the security team with equipment had the advantage.

"Representative-nim! We've confirmed that dozens of people are dead inside! They died from grenade explosions!"

The security team members reported to me while bringing someone. He collapsed in front of me as if kneeling.

It was Son Taehyung-ssi, who had been shot twice in the thigh and three times in the abdomen. He was still alive, whether he had avoided the vital organs. He looked like he was about to die at any moment, but his eyes shone brightly.

"Representative-nim.... I succeeded."

"Yeah. You did pretty well."

I grabbed Son Taehyung-ssi's head. I used Recovery. The bleeding stopped, and the bullets came out and rolled on the floor. The holes were filled. The large and small, old scars on his body disappeared. Son Taehyung-ssi, whose complexion had improved, shed tears and trembled. He prostrated himself at my feet and began to sob.

"Thank you, thank you... God-isiyeo.... Thank you...!"

"You said you didn't believe in God."

"...Yes. I did. But God... seems to exist."

"Is he crazy? Anyway, do well. You seem more useful than the other guys. Obey my orders absolutely."

"Yes. I will."

Anyone can say that with words. I didn't trust Son Taehyung-ssi.
