The succession ceremony and the engagement ceremony proceeded right then and there.

There was opposition from a few vassals, but it wasn't enough to make Ention-nim retract his words. The Prukerus Marquisate was structured such that power was concentrated in the head of the family. Therefore, within the Prukerus territory, the head of the family was essentially the king.

I sat in the seat that Ention-nim had occupied, and Yuria-yang sat next to me. We weren't officially married yet, but it also meant that I already treated Yuria-yang as my wife.

,Looking around from this seat... it feels quite new.'

The head of the family's chair was at the highest point in the Great Hall. Naturally, I looked down on everyone, and everyone looked up at me.

,Kyle in the original story got to this position pretty easily.'

I wasn't in the original story. Kyle easily dealt with Jent-gun to some extent. Maybe if I hadn't had Kyle, I would have become the head of the family a few years earlier.

I glared at some of the vassals. Those who opposed my becoming the head of the family. And those who had clashed with me a few times. Most of them quickly lowered their eyes to the floor. But a few, as if they had some conviction, did not avoid my gaze and resisted to the end.

,Those bastards are definitely getting the death penalty. I need to find fault and kill them. If there's no fault to find, I'll create one and kill them anyway. If all else fails, I'll assassinate them.'

They're useless to have around.

And an example is needed. An example to show that I'm not easy to mess with.

"I cannot accept this!!"

Jent-gun shouted. Everyone's attention is focused on one place. It wasn't far from me. Jent-gun was causing a commotion towards Ention-nim.

"Father! The war is not over yet! I can achieve more merit than Eugene!!"

Jent-gun didn't care about the gathering attention. I'll give him credit for that thick skin.

"Jent-gun. Don't be stubborn. The difference in ability between you and Eugene is already clear. Only Kyle could stand against Eugene."

"If I had the time and money, things would have been different!!"

"Time flows equally for everyone. ...No, actually. You, as the eldest son, have had more time. As for money, I didn't support anyone. Eugene earned money without anyone's help and fought the war with his own money."

"...Keuk. Father! I won't ask for much! Please keep your initial promise! Postpone the succession ceremony until the end of the war!"

"No matter what you say, the succession ceremony is already over. I have handed over the family seal to Eugene. Jent-gun, serve the new Prukerus head of the family. Eugene is your brother."

"I cannot accept this!!"

Jent-gun's stubbornness was on full display. Ention-nim let out a deep sigh.

"Jent-gun. I thought you had grown through the war. But now I see you're standing still. Jent-gun. Don't turn a blind eye to reality. The difference in ability between you and Eugene is clear, and no one can deny it. When you captured one castle, Eugene captured three. When you struggled in the south, Eugene pacified the north. On top of that, Eugene even gained Kyle's support. You couldn't even get the support of your younger brothers."

"......!!"

Jent-gun contorted his face. He gritted his teeth and clenched his fists tightly. He clenched his fists so hard that his nails dug into his palms, and blood dripped down.

"Father. I am the eldest son of the Prukerus family. The position of head of the family was originally mine...!"

"Only those who are capable can become the master of the Prukerus family."

"...That ability. I will prove it...!"

Jent-gun ignored Ention-nim and strode towards me. He took off the glove he was wearing and threw it at me.

I snatched the glove before it touched my face. Throwing a glove at someone was an act of challenging them to a duel.

"Eugene Prukerus! I challenge you to a duel! If I win, hand over the position of head of the Prukerus family to me! It's a position that doesn't suit you!"

"Alright. I accept your duel challenge."

I got up from my seat.

At the same time, I felt something strange in my head.

No matter how cornered he was, he wouldn't have forgotten that I was an Aura Master.

There are two possibilities in this case. Either he was just blurting out whatever came to mind based on his emotions, or there was something else. Judging from the fact that there was no change in his expression even though I accepted the duel, it seems like it's probably the latter.

"You're finally causing trouble."

I heard Ention-nim's voice mixed with lamentation. Maybe it's because he stepped down from the position of head of the family, but he feels much older. I pretended not to hear and approached Jent-gun.

People immediately made room for the rare spectacle. A clearing was created in the center of the Great Hall. I and Jent-gun ordered servants to bring swords.

Ention-nim is in charge of presiding over the duel.

"This is a war. And you are brothers who share blood. Do not take each other's lives."

Ention-nim said, putting strength into his words. I could feel his strong will that he wouldn't stand still if they killed each other.

"I had no intention of killing my younger brother either."

Jent-gun said. It was a lie that came out as naturally as breathing. If he had no intention of killing me, he wouldn't have sent assassins.

"Me neither."

Killing is too easy. I will take everything from Jent-gun.

,I won't kill him. But since he stood up to me, he has to be punished. I'll have to cut off one of his arms.'

Jent-gun and Kyle. The two brothers will both be crippled in one arm, so it will be quite a sight. People will admire them as brothers who only have one arm each, just like real brothers.

I, holding the sword, first thought of suppressing Jent-gun's spirit and raised an Aura Blade. Exclamations burst out from the surroundings at the clear blue Aura Blade. Especially the knights' gazes were hot.

"Hyung-nim. It's not too late yet. Give up."

I said words that I didn't mean. It's a matter of honor. It was a word I said because I knew Jent-gun would definitely not accept it.

"There's no such thing as giving up. I will win this duel... and find my place."

Jent-gun raised his aura. The blazing aura began to gather on the sword.

Aura Blade.

"How can this be! Is Jent-nim also an Aura Master? All three Prukerus brothers are Aura Masters!"

"Are you blind? Look closely. Jent-gun's Aura Blade is unstable. He's only temporarily gathering aura to create an Aura Blade. If it clashes with a real Aura Blade, it won't last long."

"I thought it would be a one-sided duel... it's turning out to be quite interesting."

Jent-gun was a top-tier Aura Expert.

I remember it wasn't this level originally.

,It seems like he didn't just stand still through this war.'

But Jent-gun couldn't cross the wall. In my case, I had a lucky chance, but Jent-gun didn't.

Pajijik.

Blue lightning flashed on the blue Aura Blade. Ignoring the surprised onlookers, I rushed towards Jent-gun.

Kaaang!

A loud noise echoed everywhere as sword and sword collided.

I was relaxed, but Jent-gun's expression was distorted. He must have felt the difference in level with just one sword strike.

I lifted the sword up and swung it down with all my might. Lightning followed the trajectory of the sword.

Kwarurureureung!

"Keueueueueuk!"

Jent-gun somehow managed to block my sword strike, but he had no choice but to step back as he was electrocuted by the following lightning. Jent-gun's shoulders dropped, and both legs trembled.

,He can't handle the lightning at all.'

It's because he forcibly created the Aura Blade. Originally, you should manage mana leisurely to block the current flowing through your body and prepare for electrocution.

,Is it because he hasn't faced someone like me much? He's more disappointing than I thought. Did he not have any hidden cards?'

Jent-gun counterattacks. He runs while lowering his body as much as possible. Jent-gun, who approached my right, aimed for my left leg. The purpose is to take away my mobility. But my eyes had already perfectly captured him. I had never missed him.

One step back, one step to the side.

Jent-gun's sword narrowly grazed my thigh. It's time to counterattack. Using the left foot in front as a pivot, I turned and kicked with my right foot. Peoeok! A roundhouse kick exploded on Jent-gun's face.

Jent-gun, who had fallen backward, jumped up. His nose was broken, and his mouth was burst, with blood flowing down. His eyes were still blazing. Jent-gun's fighting spirit had not disappeared.

After that, I exchanged about 10 more blows with Jent-gun. It wasn't very meaningful. I was just playing around moderately because it would be a shame if it ended too easily. I also provided a spectacle for the people around me and showed off my skills while mocking Jent-gun.

Kkaaang! Kkang! Kwang!

Jent-gun's forcibly created Aura Blade is already on the verge of breaking.

,Shall I end it? Kyle was useful, so I cut off his left arm... but not you. I'll cut off your right arm!'

I knocked away Jent-gun's sword. Jent-gun's arm opened outward. It was the perfect opportunity to cut off his right arm. I took a step forward and was about to swing my sword. I saw the corners of Jent-gun's mouth twisting up.

,A moment.'

A faint light settled on Jent-gun's shoes, and the brooch on his chest shone.

,He brought two artifacts. He was trusting in this. Is the effect of the shoes automatic movement? He's automatically moving back. The effect of the brooch is....'

Jent-gun's muscles swelled, and his unstable Aura Blade seemed to regain stability in an instant. With the effect of physical enhancement, he forcibly twisted the trajectory of the sword and aimed for my shoulder.

,He's quite prepared.'

But it was meaningless.

Because my sword cut off his right arm before Jent-gun's sword reached my shoulder.

Seogeoeok!

Jent-gun couldn't react properly to the accelerated strike in that moment. His right arm, holding the sword, was cut off and rolled on the floor.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaak!"

Red blood dirtied the floor along with the scream.

"The duel is over! Eugene is the winner! Use the prepared potion! Mage! Healer! Quickly heal Jent-gun's wound!"

Ention-nim shouted to the surroundings. The healer quickly picked up Jent-gun's arm and tried to bring it to his shoulder. I grabbed the healer's shoulder and blocked him.

"Father. I am the winner of the duel. I will exercise the rights of the winner. Do not reattach Jent-gun's arm."

"...Jent-gun is your brother."

"I know. But Jent-gun was a challenger. He challenged the authority of the head of the family. I will forgive that sin with one right arm."

Ention-nim hesitated and said.

"...I will follow the will of the head of the family."

I quietly watched Jent-gun's back as he was carried out.

After that, I held a banquet. The banquet, which lasted until late at night, ended in just one day due to the war.

I prepared for a purge but didn't carry it out immediately. I was a bit conscious of the war situation, and there were still quite a few guys who were on the fence.

However, Jent-gun's arms and legs had to be cut off. It's not a literal expression, but a figurative one. I executed 11 vassals who supported Jent-gun, using the assassination squad that Jent-gun sent to me as an excuse.

Jent-gun's vassals trembled at the merciless execution. Some defected to other kingdoms altogether, and some betrayed Jent-gun by offering their wealth to me.

I sent assassins to kill all the fleeing guys. I spared the guys who offered me bribes for now. This was according to Yuria-yang's advice that it's easier to manage the other guys if I pretend to be merciful. Well, I'll take care of them roughly later.

Ention-nim, who stepped down from the position of head of the family, did not retire and returned to the military. It seems he has decided to live on the battlefield until the end. It was good for me. It meant that the cards I could use, including Kyle, had increased.

,If he keeps trying to act like an old fart, I'll have to kill him.'

I am the master of Prukerus.

Sitting in the position of head of the family, I smiled contentedly and returned to reality.
