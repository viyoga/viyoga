The thirteen Demonic Gods entered my mind, and I was sucked in with them. It was only natural. You can't just welcome guests without a host, can you?

Even as my consciousness was being sucked into my mind, my body was circulating the Cheonma Shingong.

『Assisting the user's energy circulation.』

If I stopped circulating energy now, the energy of the elixir and the aftereffects of the yin energy would surely tear my body to shreds, so the Cheongangseong System assisted. It was like my body was forced into a state of selflessness.

Suuuuuuuuk.

With the feeling of my consciousness stretching out, I stood on a sports field.

Taking a quick look around, I could see Gukcheon University over there.

'Was this my original mind? It didn't look like this last time I came.'

Right. I've had a similar experience before. It must have been when I absorbed the Cheonma Shingong of the [Other World Demonic God].

'That wasn't my mind world back then.'

Black sky and white earth. That was all there was to that mind. It was too bland to be someone's mind. In a way, this place was my mind. A mind etched with the memories of my life.

"What a fascinating place."

I whipped my head around. A young man with long black hair was standing there. He looked like a nobleman, holding a fan in one hand. A sword was hanging at his waist.

It was someone I'd never seen before.

Could there be someone I've never seen in this mind?

'It'd be weird if there was some unknown bastard in my mind.'

So, his identity was obvious. One of the thirteen Demonic Gods in the coffin. It certainly wasn't the First Demonic God. The First Demonic God was more distinct in form and presence than the other Demonic Gods. An old man with long white hair and a long white beard. No, he didn't have a single wrinkle on his face to call him an old man.

"Who are you?"

I asked warily. There wouldn't be a single one of the thirteen past Demonic Gods weaker than me. I had to be on my guard if I didn't want to get eaten.

"You have no manners. I should say you're just like a Demonic God, or that you're a mere pup who doesn't know the terror of a tiger. I am Cheon Hyeon-sak. Show some respect and greet me again."

Cheon Hyeon-sak said, waving his fan. His gigolo-like behavior pissed me off.

"Cheon Hyeon-sak, huh? Which generation Demonic God are you?"

"...You don't recognize me even after hearing my name?"

"You're just a dead bastard anyway. Is there any need to know? All I know is the First Demonic God. The First Demonic God is the best of all the Demonic Gods."

The First Demonic God is said to have given up ascension to immortality for the sake of future generations and chose to die and enter the coffin. The strength of the First Demonic God is comparable to the current Emperor. That's the assessment of the Demonic God Church. Whether he's actually stronger than the Emperor is unknown. There was no Emperor when the First Demonic God was active.

"I cannot deny that the First Demonic God is the best among us... but your tone is extremely offensive."

"Are you going to fight?"

Even if he was stronger than me, this was my mind. There's a saying that even a mutt gets half a meal in its own home. I felt like I could somehow win.

"I refuse. Although you are not of the Cheon clan and have no manners, I must say that your talent is the greatest of all time. Those born with the energy of the stars are always special. You have the potential to obtain the most important power for a Demonic God."

"You overestimate the energy of the stars."

"In my time, there were many who were born with the energy of the stars. Seven were active, and the world was divided into seven. Unfortunately, the Demonic God Church was not among them. I was not born with the energy of the stars, so I had to live as a weakling. But you are different. You, born with the energy of the stars, are qualified to become the strongest in the world."

Cheon Hyeon-sak was being cheesy. I had no interest in his story, so I let it go in one ear and out the other.

The important thing was to obtain the Cheonma Shingong.

'If I kill this bastard, can I get the Cheonma Shingong?'

It didn't matter if I failed. Besides this bastard, there were still twelve Demonic Gods somewhere in my mind.

The moment I thought that I needed the Hwaryeon Bido, the Hwaryeon Bido was in my hand. Probably because this was my mind, it was possible. I pointed the Hwaryeon Bido, in perfect condition without any cracks, at Cheon Hyeon-sak's neck.

"Hand over the Cheonma Shingong."

"Unfortunately, I cannot. The First Demonic God decides who receives the Cheonma Shingong. I cannot pass on the Cheonma Shingong until the First Demonic God makes a decision."

"In other words, you're useless."

"Don't be so hasty. Aren't you curious about the mind? I can answer what you don't know."

I lowered the Hwaryeon Bido. It was true that I didn't know about the mind.

"What is the mind?"

"It is the heart, experience, and desire."

Cheon Hyeon-sak raised his fan and pointed to the university building. A structure built in a style not seen in the [Gwangmyeong Ascending to Heaven] world.

"This building is excessively detailed. If this building is not your desire, then this building must actually exist in reality. Has the world changed while I was dead?"

He looked around the world. High-rise buildings beyond the university building were lined up around him. Cars were moving, and people were walking around.

"These are strange clothes. Even riding in strange carriages.... No matter how I think about it, this is beyond common sense. But there is order. I see. Is this another world? Interesting and intriguing."

"Tell me more about the mind. If possible, I'd like you to tell me what I can do in this mind."

"The mind is also your spirit. If you set your mind to it, you can completely manipulate the mind. Just like you created the sword just now."

"If I set my mind to it, I can get rid of you in my mind too."

"Does everything in this world happen just because you set your mind to it? The same goes for the mind. The mind is not composed of just you. There's a saying: Samjeong (三頂) is the realm of recognizing the mind, Johwa (造化) is the realm of unfolding the mind, and Mansang (萬象) is the realm of projecting the mind. If you truly want to harness the power of the mind... reach the realm of Johwa."

"...I have recognized the mind. Does that mean I have reached the Samjeong realm?"

"You know the answer to that question better than I do. You, who have reached the end of stubbornness, will see the wall of the realm."

"......"

I couldn't argue with that.

In fact, even if I just observed a little, I could see a huge wall blocking my path. I could see this wall even now, in my mind.

"People often say that enlightenment is needed to overcome the wall. What is that enlightenment? Is it an inspiration that comes in an instant? No one can explain it for sure. The enlightenment to overcome the wall is different for everyone."

"Shut up. Don't talk like you're my teacher. You talk about the mind."

He shrugged his shoulders once and said.

"It's your mind. Find out for yourself. Let me give you one piece of advice... don't break. The moment your mind breaks, your mind will collapse."

"Don't worry, I won't break. Where are the other Demonic Gods?"

"They'll be somewhere in this mind. They're all selfish... so there will be those who wreak havoc in this mind to break your spirit."

"Is the First Demonic God wreaking havoc?"

"No, he probably wants to see what's in your deepest place. Just like he did with Cheon Yu-un."

"What's in the deepest part of Cheon Yu-un's mind?"

"Only he has confirmed it. According to him, there is a snake that will swallow the sky, and he was satisfied."

"What's at the bottom of my mind?"

"You know that better than I do."

"I'm asking because I don't know. Are you a little slow?"

"How dare you... If you don't know, go and find out for yourself."

Right.

This was my mind. It wouldn't be bad to move and check it out. Then I'll kill Cheon Hyeon-sak.

It was then.

Something with a massive presence fell from the sky. It was a huge man enveloped in black, demonic energy.

"Hehehehehe!"

The man, who had created a crater in the playground, swung a giant sword over 2m long and walked towards me. He was using the Levitation skill. His feet did not touch the ground.

"You arrogant bastard! So, you were here!"

"The 4th Demonic God, Cheon Bae-bok."

Cheon Hyeon-sak said.

The 4th Demonic God Cheon Bae-bok had a red face and a beard. That's why he reminded me of Jangbi from the Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

"Step back, 11th! I will not tolerate interference."

Cheon Hyeon-sak stepped back as if it were natural. He went under a suitable tree and fanned himself.

Cheon Bae-bok strode towards me.

"Young one! I admit that your talent is outstanding. But you are not qualified! I can never accept a Demonic God who is not of the Cheon bloodline! I will break your spirit before he decides to pass on the power. Then he will make the right decision! 11th! I'll say it again! Don't interfere!"

"Don't worry, I have no intention of interfering. If you break at this level, it's the same as not being qualified as a Demonic God. I must have misjudged you."

I was dumbfounded. I didn't like either of these bastards. I decided to kill Cheon Bae-bok in front of me first, and then kill Cheon Hyeon-sak.

It was the moment I raised the Hwaryeon Bido.

『Absorbed the essence of Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功)』

『More essence is needed to master the complete Cheonma Shingong.』

『Absorb the essence.』

The Cheongangseong System's notification window popped up. At the same time, information about the Cheonma Shingong came in. Not the Cheonma Shingong I had learned, but information about a new Cheonma Shingong. However, the information about this Cheonma Shingong was empty in places.

'The essence of Cheonma Shingong.'

I knew intuitively what he was talking about.

The Demonic God in front of me.

What else could these bastards be but the essence of Cheonma Shingong?

I had the answer to what to do. I smiled and swung my sword at Cheon Bae-bok. A huge slash flew towards Cheon Bae-bok.

Cheon Bae-bok was startled and hurriedly stepped aside.

'It's as I thought. This is my mind. The attack is buffed.'

I didn't feel like I could lose in this world.

I used the Cheonma Shingong and began to fight in earnest.
