The thieves who escaped Maruhan Academy with Namgung Hwayeon-ssi's Guiryeonghwan Sword were ecstatic. Even now, speeding north in their getaway car, their excitement only grew.

Maseongju, behind the wheel, struggled to control his foot from constantly slamming on the accelerator.

32.6 billion won! That massive sum of money would soon be in his hands! Maseongju planned to keep 2.6 billion for himself, and use the remaining 30 billion to multiply his wealth at the casino.

He'd always lost money gambling because he didn't have enough to begin with. In his mind, gambling was like capitalism - the rich get richer. In other words, he was finally about to start winning.

,I'm going to dominate the casino with 30 billion! ...Wait a minute. Can I even dominate a casino with 30 billion? There are so many rich bastards there, it might not be enough....'

An easy and quick way to make money popped into his head. If the two lovebirds next to and behind him died, wouldn't his share get bigger? 13 billion won danced before his eyes.

,Fuck! We did it! No pursuit from the academy! 32.6 billion! That's enough to start a new life!"

,It's all thanks to my ability! We couldn't have escaped without my teleport! The academy's probably struggling to find our trail. Teleportation leaves no trace, so it's impossible to find us right away. Keuheuhe."

,What nonsense. It's thanks to me for breaking through the academy's barrier."

,You idiots. I was the one who caught the cat and swapped it with the Guiryeonghwan Sword while you were all floundering. You were able to escape from that spot because of me. This operation wouldn't have succeeded without me."

,That's enough, Hakjun-gun. We all worked hard. We're about to have 32.6 billion in our hands, no need to ruin the mood. Let's just think about the good things!"

Gamdonggi chuckled as he spoke. Right. 32.6 billion. That money was about to be in their possession. How reassuring.

Maseongju listened to their conversation, his eyes gleaming. He was exhausted. He'd also sustained internal injuries. His body had been strong since he was young, so he didn't feel it was a big problem. Just a little tired, though.

Gamdonggi seemed to have worse external injuries than him. He was smiling as if nothing was wrong, but his pupils were trying to roll upwards.

Simeunji-ssi was bleeding from her nose and mouth. The barrier she had set up in the magic and old church had been shaken, causing her internal injuries. Even if Simeunji-ssi had been fine, he could have taken her out with a surprise attack.

The most troublesome one was Hakjun-gun. He looked tired, but he could probably use his ability a few more times. Besides, he had the Guiryeonghwan Sword. If he ran off with it, there would be no solution.

,He can't run off alone. I'm the only one here who knows the meeting place with Guirak Valley.'

After driving north for about 30 minutes, the car stopped in a dark, empty lot. Another car was waiting in the lot.

,Get out. We're switching to that car. I'll drive this time too."

,Does it really have to be so annoying?"

Simeunji-ssi asked, wiping the steadily flowing nosebleed with a handkerchief.

,We need to give the guys chasing us even a little bit of confusion. Hakjun-gun's teleport will have to be used one more time when we cross the border."

,Even for me, I can't teleport all three of you from here directly to China."

They groaned and got out of the car. Once the excitement faded, the pain came.

As everyone got out of the car, Maseongju struck the defenseless Gamdonggi in the back of the head. Peok! Gamdonggi's occipital bone caved in and he collapsed. A small knife fell from his sleeve to the ground as he died instantly.

It was Gamdonggi's prized possession. A throwing knife. If you get the enemy's blood on the blade, it automatically flies and sticks into the enemy. The blade already had blood on it.

,Maseongju! What the hell are you doing?!"

Simeunji-ssi shouted in shock. Maseongju calmly picked up the small knife that had fallen on the ground.

,This is the knife Gamdonggi had. You know what kind of item it is, right? There's blood on the blade. Whose blood do you think it is?"

Simeunji-ssi used the handkerchief she had to wipe the blood flowing from her mouth and nose. Hakjun-gun was just tired, he wasn't bleeding. Maseongju was covered in blood.

,If I hadn't killed this bastard first... I would have died. Well, think of it positively. Since Gamdonggi died, our share has increased."

Cheolkeok.

Hakjun-gun's gun was pointed at Simeunji-ssi's head. Simeunji-ssi's expression hardened. She glared at Hakjun-gun as if she would kill him.

,...Why?"

,A clean split between two is better than an ambiguous split between three."

,Did you plan this with that bastard Maseongju from the start?"

,That's not it. Simeunji-ssi, you've been subtly looking down on me too much. Did you think I was such an idiot that I wouldn't notice that? Farewell, Simeunji-ssi. My first love, whom I loved when I didn't know anything... I was ashamed that a slut like you was my first love."

Tang!

Blood spurted from Simeunji-ssi's head and she collapsed to the ground. Maseongju and Hakjun-gun were wary of each other as they got into the car.

After they disappeared, a water droplet appeared from Simeunji-ssi's head. The water droplet spat out the bullet that was about to pierce Simeunji-ssi's head. The crushed bullet rolled on the dirt floor. Then, leaving the unconscious Simeunji-ssi behind, the water droplet disappeared. A white owl landed around Simeunji-ssi from the sky.

Inside the speeding car, Maseongju and Hakjun-gun eyed each other. They didn't trust each other.

,Exactly where in China are we going?"

Hakjun-gun asked him.

,Do you think I don't know you're planning to kill me after finding out the destination? Even if you kill me and search my smartphone, you won't be able to find out the destination."

,I'll keep the Guiryeonghwan Sword. Don't even think about killing me and taking it. I'm constantly watching you. Don't forget that my nickname is the Master of Escape."

,Okay. Okay. We'll split the 100 million dollars between the two of us. 32.6 billion won has become 65.2 billion won. 65 billion won is more than enough."

,I'm worried about something else, though."

,Something else?"

,Guirak Valley. Do you think those Chinese bastards will give us the money willingly?"

,They'll give it. This is also a matter of Guirak Valley's credibility."

,A villain organization talking about trust? That's ridiculous."

,Because they're a villain organization, they have to show trust. That's the only way they won't be isolated. Don't worry about the money. Guirak Valley are people who control even the Chinese party cadres. 100 million dollars? It's not a lot of money to them."

,Hmm. I've heard that Korean chaebols can't compare to Chinese chaebols. As expected, population is king."

They changed cars several times and arrived near the border.

,Where should we go? Do we just need to cross the border?"

In response to Hakjun-gun's question, Maseongju pointed in one direction.

,40km in that direction. The meeting place is near there. With your current ability, you should be able to move there in an instant."

,Haha. Of course. I have that much ability."

Tang!

A bullet was lodged in Maseongju's abdomen. Maseongju widened his eyes and slumped to the ground.

,You idiot...! You were blinded by greed! Did you think you could get the money from Guirak Valley by yourself?!"

,You underestimate me too much. Do you think I can't handle a simple deal like that?"

,You don't know the exact location of the meeting place!"

,The needle on your wristwatch must be pointing to the meeting place. Did you think I wouldn't notice you glancing at your wristwatch instead of the easy-to-see digital clock in the car?"

,Guirak Valley isn't an easy place! Unlike me, you don't have any connections!"

,Instead, I have the connections. The other three are all dead, and I'm the only one left. Hehe. 100 million dollars.... 130 billion won.... I'm going to go to Japan, the home of VTubers, with that money. And I'm going to marry a VTuber. Keuheuhe. 130 billion won is worth it."

Tangtangtangtangtang!

,Thanks for driving."

Hakjun-gun, who had finished off Maseongju, took off his wristwatch and put it on. It felt luxurious, so he liked it quite a bit. There were several needles besides the ones that told the time. One of them, a needle that only pointed in a specific direction, was pointing to Guirak Valley's meeting place.

,Did he say 40km? I'm going right away.'

Pat.

His body disappeared. He didn't realize the small stain on the end of his sleeve at all.

Hakjun-gun arrived at the meeting place. It was a village. A village made up of traditional Chinese houses. Only the moonlight illuminated the village, which had no streetlights. Perhaps that's why it felt quite eerie.

-Stop.

Hakjun-gun, who was about to enter the village, stopped after hearing a low-pitched male voice echoing from all directions. It was Chinese, but it sounded like Korean. It seemed the wristwatch also had translation capabilities.

Hakjun-gun swallowed nervously. At least 300 people or more were watching him. There were no ordinary people among them.

-Where is Maseongju, and why did you show up?

,Maseongju and the other colleagues died from their wounds opening up. But I brought the Guiryeonghwan Sword that you wanted."

Hakjun-gun took out the Guiryeonghwan Sword and raised it.

They murmured.

,It's really the Guiryeonghwan Sword. I can feel the ghostly energy in the sword."

,If we extract that ghostly energy and make it into a Jiangshi... It'll be fun just imagining it."

Put away your greed. This is the sword that Chamsalgwi (斬殺鬼) wants.

A smile spread across Hakjun-gun's face. Judging from their reactions, it seemed like things would go well.

,Bring the 100 million dollars!"

,Why should we?"

,...What? You promised!"

,We never made any promises with you. We only announced that we would give 100 million dollars if someone brought the Guiryeonghwan Sword."

,You fucking bastards! You're changing your tune now?! Give me the 100 million dollars!"

,Judging by the looks of it, it seems like he doesn't have any backing. That means we can kill him here without any repercussions. Ah, don't worry. We'll use your 100 million dollars well. Hehe. We'll make money with this and even raise our standing within Guirak Valley."

,You fuck! Do you think I'll just be taken down so futilely?!"

Hakjun-gun used the teleport that made him the Master of Escape. But his body remained still. The teleport wasn't used.

,I, insane! What is it?!"

-This place is our territory. Look around you.

It was full of fog. No, what he thought was fog were actually wandering ghosts. Hakjun-gun felt a chill and gritted his teeth. After hesitating, he knelt down on the ground with a thud.

,I, I'll give you the Guiryeonghwan Sword. Just spare me...!"

,You have the brains to assess the situation. Your ability is special... so we'll offer you and the sword to Chamsalgwi-nim. He will decide your fate."

,I, I've actually admired Guirak Valley since a long time ago. I also love China. The origins of all Korean culture are Chinese! Kimchi is pao cai! Ni shi palerma! I love Tiananmen!"

,It's pao cai. Hand over the Guiryeonghwan Sword."

Hakjun-gun held out the sword to him. He reached out his hand to grab the sword. Suddenly, the sword disappeared. Both of their pupils widened at the same time.

,I, it wasn't me!"

,You bastard! Can't you take out the Guiryeonghwan Sword right now! Take it out before I kill you here!!"

,I didn't do it! I can't teleport objects through space!"

Hakjun-gun desperately shook his hands. The man glared at Hakjun-gun, then was startled when he saw Hakjun-gun's sleeve.

,Why is your sleeve wet? ...When I concentrate, I can feel the energy of a spirit. Were you a spiritist?!"

,A spirit? What nonsense are you talking about?!"

The moisture that had been soaking his sleeve came out as a single water droplet. The water droplet seeped into his skin. It was when Hakjun-gun was startled and stood up. He felt all the blood in his body rushing to his head.

,Keueueuk.... The blood, the blood is strange! Save me...!"

Before the man could do anything, Hakjun-gun's neck exploded with a bang. Blood spurted from Hakjun-gun's neck, and his head soared into the sky. Like a water rocket.

Hakjun-gun's head, which had been flying for a while, soon lost its momentum and fell to the ground.

The man from Guirak Valley didn't even glance at the corpse and stared at the empty air. A water droplet floating in the air rotated and gradually increased in size.

,...It's a water spirit. It's definitely not a low-level or mid-level one."

,Is it high-level?"

,There's a high probability of that."

,Why did the water spirit kill that bastard?"

,That's something that water spirit would know. Or maybe it's the bastard who controls that water spirit. Spiritist, reveal yourself! I know you're watching! We can even recognize the gaze of ghosts! Do you think we wouldn't recognize your gaze!"

The water spirit had already taken the form of a round ball. Inside it, water was constantly swirling.

-As expected of Guirak Valley.

A voice echoed from the spirit. The voice of a young man. It was the spiritist who controlled that spirit.

-I'm a bit busy, so I don't have time to talk to you bastards. Just die.

The spirit's body exploded and became a giant tsunami. The tsunami swept everything away and began to surge on the ground.

Everyone, whether human or ghost, fell into the water and floundered.

The ghosts couldn't dare to escape out of the water, and even if people grabbed onto trees or rocks, they couldn't hold on. The trees and rocks were also swept away and destroyed.

What had been surging soon swirled and ground everything away.

At some point, the water disappeared.

All that remained there were damp, wet remains.
