Flack Trading Company.

The best trading company in the great city of Gigantria. Which meant, it could be said to be the best trading company in the world.

The Chairman of the Flack Trading Company was Oswald. Originally, he was just a merchant, but he ousted the Flack Trading Company Chairman and beat out his competitors to take the Chairman's seat.

It was an open secret that Seong Yujin, the Lightning Berserker, had helped him. No, Oswald himself subtly fueled the rumors. He established his authority by letting it be known that the Lightning Berserker was behind him. It was possible because he had the Lightning Berserker's permission. Oswald was a merchant who knew his place.

Such Oswald received a mission from the Lightning Berserker. An instruction to spread a video throughout Gigantria. It wasn't a difficult task. If it hadn't been an instruction directly from the Lightning Berserker himself, Oswald would have had a subordinate do it and wouldn't have given it a second thought.

,This is Yujin-nim's order. I will personally check the work from start to finish.'

First, Oswald checked the video.

It was a video of Seong Yujin hunting the Thunderclap Dragon. Oswald frowned.

,...Yujin-nim seems to be getting pushed back by the Thunderclap Dragon. I've seen Yujin-nim rampage in the city before. With the power Yujin-nim had then, he should be overwhelming the Thunderclap Dragon. But this video is....'

A video of the Lightning Berserker becoming a meat shield and a blonde beauty mage severing the Thunderclap Dragon's breath in one go with great magic. Anyone could see that the main character was the mage.

Oswald, who had been lost in thought while watching the video, snapped his fingers.

,Yujin-nim has many enemies! Most of the enemies have only heard rumors of Yujin-nim's power. The purpose of this video is to mislead the enemies, including the Emperor of Thieves, into misjudging him! I understand your intentions, even if it's late!'

Oswald, who had come to a conclusion alone in his office, started working.

The video taken with the magic tool would be re-shot and shown to many people in the theater.

,I should save it on a separate DVD and sell it to other cities.'

Most of those with DVD players were rich, and those rich people would have great interest and a desire to collect this video. Because it was a battle video of the S-class Hunter Lightning Berserker and the high dragon-class Dragon Thunderclap Dragon.

Oswald stroked his neck and smiled. His neck felt chilly. He had sided with the Lightning Berserker. He had bet everything on the Lightning Berserker. Thanks to that, he had snatched the Chairman's seat and gained wealth and power.

If the Lightning Berserker lost and died, Oswald would also lose everything and die.

But if the Lightning Berserker won, Oswald would gain more wealth and power than he had now.

• • •

The Emperor of Thieves, Baek Jeongsu, watched the Thunderclap Dragon hunting video. Contrary to expectations, the Lightning Berserker did not play an active role in the battle. The one who took the lead was the blonde, blue-eyed beautiful mage. Already famous as a skilled mage, she earned the nickname Cheonam (天巖) because of this incident. She was called Cheonam because she had dropped a huge rock from the sky.

,Mir's clanmate. I heard that she gave Mir the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon without any compensation. So she didn't offer her body to the Lightning Berserker and get it. There are only a few mages of that level among the A-class Hunters. The Lightning Berserker probably handed over the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon considering the future relationship. I would have done the same.'

If he had his way, he would go find Cheonam right away and try to recruit her into the association. Since the association was also involved in magic tool production and distribution, they needed a mage with excellent skills, even if it wasn't necessarily in combat.

,Recruiting her will be difficult. Aside from the relationship with Mir.... She's a mage that the Lightning Berserker is watching closely enough to hand over the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon. Approaching her carelessly would be like provoking the Lightning Berserker. There's no need to provoke the Lightning Berserker right now. And the fact that the Lightning Berserker couldn't recruit her means... she's a mage who can't be recruited even with the heart of a high dragon.'

He decided to keep an eye on Cheonam. Anyway, she seemed to have a strong relationship with Mir. For now, it was enough that she didn't antagonize the association.

Baek Jeongsu focused on something else. The Lightning Berserker's battle.

,He's being pushed back by the Thunderclap Dragon much more than I thought. Is it because the Lightning Berserker's ability, lightning, doesn't work on the Thunderclap Dragon?'

Baek Jeongsu saw the Lightning Berserker's martial prowess in single-handedly annihilating the Extreme Sword Squad. He also received reports that the Lightning Berserker had destroyed a city.

,...The Lightning Berserker, who doesn't use lightning, has outstanding martial arts left.'

Even if other people didn't, Baek Jeongsu could see the Lightning Berserker's skill.

,Ordinary Hunters use specially made large weapons and large shields. I also use a large sword with a wide blade. Basically, to fight a dragon that is larger than a human, the equipment must also be large. The Lightning Berserker is fighting the dragon head-on in a 1-on-1 fight with an ordinary straight sword.'

It was absurd.

No matter how much of an S-class Hunter he was, he couldn't compete with a high dragon-class dragon with just his physical abilities. Strengthening the body using magic power? High dragon-class dragons also know how to use magic power. Therefore, humans can never catch up with high dragon-class dragons with just their physical abilities.

,The Lightning Berserker deflected the Thunderclap Dragon's forefoot with martial arts. That's why the Extreme Sword Squad was annihilated. The difference in skill is too great.'

Baek Jeongsu's brow furrowed. He simulated fighting the Lightning Berserker 1-on-1 in his head. Of course, the Lightning Berserker in his head was summoning lightning.

,...Under the condition that I eat an electric immunity potion made using the dragon heart blood of a high dragon-class thunder dragon and fight, my win rate is about 75%. How absurd. Even though the Lightning Berserker's lightning doesn't work on me, my win rate is only 75%.'

Was there no way to beat the Lightning Berserker 100%?

Mobilize the vice-chairmen? Unfortunately, the vice-chairmen were not in collusion. The Lightning Berserker had also used his influence on the vice-chairmen, but it was also a problem that existed even before the Lightning Berserker appeared.

,If the vice-chairmen had just followed my orders, the Lightning Berserker wouldn't be running wild like this.... The world is never easy.'

Baek Jeongsu thought of the dragon king-class dragon. But that was only a last resort.

,...Hmm.'

Baek Jeongsu's worries deepened.

And some time later, Baek Jeongsu met Mir. Mir, who had eaten the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon, had definitely become stronger. In just one day, he was wrapping yellow lightning around his body and launching tricky attacks.

,Mir-gun. You seem to be in a good mood today. It doesn't seem to be just because you've gotten stronger. Did anything else happen yesterday?'

According to Baek Jeongsu's plan, Mir should be full of anger towards the Lightning Berserker right now.

,Ah, nothing special happened. I was just suspecting my colleagues. It was a bit of a big deal last night... but we resolved it amicably over breakfast today.'

,...Is it related to the Lightning Berserker?'

,Yes. Hytera and Jina said they had met the Lightning Berserker separately. But that was it. Nothing special really happened. I trust Hytera and Jina.'

You can resolve the doubts in your heart right away just by talking a little? Baek Jeongsu didn't believe it.

,...He used a virginity detector magic tool. Looking at Mir's expression, both of them were virgins. The Lightning Berserker didn't violate those two.... I feel like I've been hit in the back of the head.'

Baek Jeongsu avoided the topic of the Lightning Berserker. Any more than this would be too blatant. Even the immature Mir could realize his intentions.

,It seems like nothing has been going well lately.'

His mouth tasted bitter.

• • •

Unlike the Emperor of Thieves, who gave a relatively proper evaluation after watching the video, the public's evaluation was quite harsh.

,Look at this? The Lightning Berserker just kept getting beat up by the Thunderclap Dragon and became covered in blood. Could it be that the Lightning Berserker's power is exaggerated?'

,Thinking about it, it was strange. I heard that the Lightning Berserker messed up other cities? But there's not much of that in Gigantria.'

,Cheonam did everything. Cheonam took the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon, and I heard that Cheonam's colleague, a Hunter named Mir, ate the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon.'

,At this point... I think I can hunt a high dragon-class dragon too? Anyone want to hunt a high dragon with me?'

,The high dragons that were nearby were hunted by the Lightning Berserker. Shit. I was a little scared because they kept saying high dragon-class, high dragon-class, but after watching this video, high dragon-class is nothing special.'

,Was it true that most of the Lightning Berserker's rumors were created because someone else helped the Lightning Berserker....'

Mir nodded as he watched the Hunters chattering at the Hunter Association headquarters.

,Lightning Berserker. After watching the video, you seem to be worse than the rumors. You would have failed the hunt if it weren't for Hytera. It was natural for Hytera to take the heart of the Thunderclap Dragon. It would have been better if I had seen this video at the association yesterday.'

If possible, before using the virginity detector. It was a meaningless thought. The past does not come back.

,Should we raid him this time? I don't know about anything else, but the Lightning Berserker has a lot of things. What was it, I heard that the Lightning Berserker is the owner of the popular novels and comics these days? In addition to that, he buys dragon hearts and dragon heart blood with the money he earns from seasonings and spices. If we do well, we might be able to change our lives, right?'

,Raid the Lightning Berserker who killed the Murderous Devil? Are you as crazy as the Lightning Berserker? But I also feel like it's possible for some reason.'

Those who were afraid of the Lightning Berserker and couldn't even say the Lightning Berserker's name properly until yesterday were mocking the Lightning Berserker today. Mir let out a hollow laugh at their attitude and focused on watching the video that was being played repeatedly on the wall of the cafeteria on the first floor of the headquarters.

Even to Mir, the Lightning Berserker was being pushed back without dealing any significant damage to the Thunderclap Dragon. But if you look a little closer, you can find something surprising.

,Even though he's covered in blood, there's actually very little fatal damage. Fighting a high dragon-class dragon head-on in a 1-on-1 fight... is that even possible?'

Mir swallowed hard. Mir, who was being mentored by the Emperor of Thieves, was growing rapidly. In his opinion, the Lightning Berserker was not weak at all.

,Still, the Chairman-nim should be able to do this much. And I can follow along this much if I have time.'

The fear of the Lightning Berserker that everyone felt was gradually receding in real time.
