Inside an abandoned church in the Valentier region.

Four demons gathered there for a meeting.

Originally, demons rarely gathered. Demons were proud and self-willed. They rarely banded together. If demons ever did unite, it was for one of two reasons: either they had succumbed to a demon with powerful strength, or an enemy too strong to face alone had appeared.

This meeting was clearly the latter.

The four demons gathered in the church each positioned themselves in their own way.

The first to speak was the crow demon sitting on the broken cross. A crow with gleaming green eyes, he was Parason, who loved to display human corpses.

,The paladin of Valentier, chosen by the Goddess this time, is no ordinary one. Even compared to paladins from other regions, he's killing us off at an abnormally fast rate. It's like a predator hunting its prey."

Taking Parason's words was Goat, a demon covered in fur. Goat adorned his body with human eyeballs, tongues, and entrails. The human corpses were decaying, and flies swarmed around them.

"That human is so damn annoying. My friend was killed by that paladin a week ago. Let's go and kill him."

Parason shook his crow head.

"Just us barging into the Valentier Cathedral? That's no different from suicide. The Goddess's power is strong in the cathedral, and it's swarming with the Goddess's servants. It's impossible to conquer the Valentier Cathedral with just our strength. But I agree that we need to kill the Valentier paladin as soon as possible."

Paladins grow stronger over time. The Valentier paladin's growth rate is so fast that he could be considered the best ever. Moreover, he seems to have a grudge against demons, exterminating them every single day without fail. At this rate, the Valentier paladin might become the worst paladin in history.

"I agree. The paladin must be killed. If we don't kill him, we'll die."

Boned, a demon with a solid build and three pig heads, said. The three voices merged into one sounded like a beast howling.

Goat turned his gaze to the fourth demon in the room.

"Baron. What do you think? The fact that you're here means you agree that we need to kill that paladin quickly, right?"

Sitting with his legs crossed on an old chair, he had a very human-like appearance, unlike the other demons. However, his skin was as pale as a corpse compared to humans. Baron Heilgaide. As a human nobleman, he ruled over a small territory and was a vampire who devoured humans. He called himself a Progenitor.

Baron Heilgaide opened his mouth leisurely.

"That paladin is a madman."

But his voice was not leisurely. It couldn't be leisurely.

"Yesterday, one of my territory's villages was annihilated by him."

"...Is that important now? That paladin can distinguish us. It's not strange that they were annihilated."

Goat said nonchalantly. He assumed that the village referred to the Fallen.

To demons, the Fallen were useful slaves. Most of the Fallen were overwhelmingly weaker than the demons. Occasionally, a Fallen stronger than a demon would appear, but it was rare.

"That village was not a village of the Fallen."

"...He annihilated a village of ordinary humans? A paladin?"

Boned asked, as if he couldn't believe it.

"The paladin's sanctum isn't omnipotent. He can distinguish demons and the Fallen... but he can't distinguish our collaborators."

Collaborators.

Humans were funny creatures. Even if they weren't Fallen, they would readily join hands with demons if it benefited them. Of course, in most cases, they would become Fallen within 10 years, but the demons had no reason to care about that. They simply used them conveniently.

There was a Great Demon in another region who created human collaborators on a large scale. Baron Heilgaide found the method interesting, but that wasn't the important thing now.

"My human collaborator was living in that village as a merchant."

Boned opened his mouth, as if he had guessed what had happened.

"The village was collectively executed because there was a collaborator."

"To be more precise, they were executed because a collaborator was hiding there. The paladin didn't know the details of the collaborator's information. There was no evidence either. Just conjecture. He annihilated an entire village based on suspicion alone. And he suppressed the priests' and holy knights' opposition with his authority and started a propaganda campaign. I was so impressed when I heard the report about how excellent that propaganda was."

"...I understand that the paladin is a merciless bastard. So, Baron, what are you trying to say?"

"The paladin's nature and actions are close to ours. Perhaps we can corrupt the paladin. Incite the paladin's desires. Twist the desire to kill demons and tempt him to crave power... it's definitely possible."

"No. The paladin must be killed."

"I agree with Goat."

"Baron. There's no guarantee that your plan will succeed. We must kill the paladin before he gets stronger."

The other demons all protested. Baron Heilgaide, however, did not lose his composure and leisure.

"I will lead the plan myself. The paladin will fall. Even if you search history, there aren't many such cases. That's why it's even more interesting. Don't you guys want to see a corrupted paladin?"

"......"

Everyone was silent. The meaning was obvious.

Basically, demons were beings faithful to their desires. With no set lifespan, they could live for hundreds or even thousands of years before they died. They always craved thrilling stimulation.

"If the Baron is going to take the lead and command... it might not be bad to watch for a while."

"I can't wait long."

"I wonder what a corrupted paladin tastes like."

"It's decided then. But just in case, let's call other demons. So that we can kill the paladin immediately if we judge that the plan has failed."

It was then.

The front entrance of the abandoned church burst open, and something flew in and landed among the demons.

It was a human, or rather, a demon, with only his upper body remaining. Baron Heilgaide stared at the corpse and said calmly.

"That's my son."

His son, who had torn his mother's womb 15 years ago to be born, was brutally murdered.

Heavy footsteps echoed. The demons looked at the source of the footsteps and were horrified. The greatsword he gripped, the heavy full-body armor, the unpleasant aura emanating from his entire body. There was no way they wouldn't know who he was.

"Paladin...!"

"How did you know we were here!"

"Heh heh, you've come to die."

"Did you find out about this place through my son..."

Click clack. (Click clack.)

The paladin walked.

The demons tensed up. The paladin they faced directly was more intense than they had expected.

"You disgusting demons. Were you all gathered here jerking each other off?"

"Heh, heh heh. How foolish! You've come to die!"

Goat, the demon covered in fur, laughed and leaped towards the paladin. As he floated into the air, his fur became stiff like steel. He looked like a hedgehog with its quills raised.

"You're the foolish one."

The church windows, barely holding their shape, shattered. Specially treated crossbow bolts rained down from all directions. The crossbow bolts fell not only on the demons but also on the paladin. However, the crossbow bolts could not penetrate the paladin's armor.

"Kaaaaaaak!"

Goat, with arrows embedded all over his body, fell to the ground and writhed. The sacred poison applied to the arrowheads gnawed at his body. One arrow wouldn't have been a problem, but there were at least 30 arrows embedded in his body.

The paladin gripped his sword in reverse with both hands and slammed it down on Goat.

Bang!

Goat's head was severed.

"This is a chance to kill four demons at once. You wouldn't have come alone. Holy knights and inquisitors have surrounded this church. You have no chance to escape."

"Paladiiin...!!"

Goat, with only his head remaining, called out to the paladin with hatred. Immediately after, his fur bristled and aimed for the paladin's head.

The paladin tilted his head as if he had anticipated it, avoiding the final attack. The paladin finished him off by cutting Goat's head and body, then confidently walked forward. The paladin's entire body glowed with Holy Aura, and the paladin's sword was enveloped in divinity.

"Parason, Boned. Are you alright?"

"I have a few arrows stuck in my body... but I'm fine except for feeling unpleasant."

"...Krrr. There are quite a few human bastards outside the church."

"That paladin has made an arrogant choice. He dares to face us alone. Let's ignore the guys outside the church for now and kill the paladin in front of us first. Boned, face the paladin head-on. Parason, aim for the paladin's back. I'll provide cover with magic."

Boned spread both hands wide. The center of his palms wriggled, and sharp bones protruded. He gripped the bones like swords and charged towards the paladin. At the same time, Parason spread his wings and circled in the air, aiming for the paladin's back.

Baron Heilgaide used magic. Blood appeared with his gesture. The blood flowed from the floor and approached the paladin.

The paladin's movements became faster. He approached Boned's chest at a speed that even the demons' eyes could not properly follow and stabbed his sword into it. Boned blew out a powerful gust of wind with his three pig heads and swung his twin swords.

Bang! (Kkaang!) Bang! (Kkang!) Bang! (Kkang!)

Boned's bone swords failed to cut through the paladin's solid armor completely. They only managed to hit parts of the armor.

"To get such armor! You cowardly bastard! Parason! Baron! What are you doing! Kill him quickly."

Parason surged from behind, and the Baron's magic swirled around.

"What a load of crap."

Crackle! (Pajijijijijik!)

Golden lightning erupted from the paladin's entire body. Parason was startled and stepped back, and the Baron's magic evaporated. Most of the golden lightning flowed into Boned's body. Boned's body, electrocuted, lost its strength.

The paladin did not miss the opportunity. He swung his sword and dismembered Boned's body. Boned's corpse fell to the floor.

Wriggle.

A fourth head, smaller than a palm, popped out from Boned's dismembered corpse. It moved its rat-like legs attached to its head and ran away. The paladin was not flustered. He calmly threw his greatsword.

The greatsword accurately pierced the back of the fleeing demon's head.
