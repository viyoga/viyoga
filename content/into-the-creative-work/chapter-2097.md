I took the unconscious Milyeong back home. I couldn't just leave her in that desolate cave with nothing.

I laid the naked Milyeong on the bed in her room. It felt like the temperature in the room rose by 5 degrees just from that.

,It feels like midsummer has arrived. Shouldn't we turn on the air conditioner?

"It's better to leave it as it is. If the surrounding temperature drops, the amount of fire energy flowing out will increase. Then she'll dry up and die faster than expected."

I looked at Milyeong. With her eyes tightly closed, she looked like she was sleeping peacefully. If I grabbed her and shook her, it felt like she would wake up and smile brightly right away. But in reality, even with Wiyu putting clothes on her, she didn't react at all.

"...How did it come to this?"

I clicked my tongue. Those who fail trying to transcend their realm. I had heard stories, but I never imagined Milyeong would be one of them.

"Half of it is your fault. She must have been stimulated by seeing you getting stronger at such an absurd rate. This girl always said she wanted to be helpful to you."

"...She's already plenty helpful without pushing herself like this, though."

"It seems the person in question doesn't feel that way."

We went outside the room to discuss things. The answer we came to was one: find an expert and find a way to deal with it. I was planning to go find the regressor Baekran. Wiyu might have a better plan than I do, so I asked her.

"Master, do you know any sorcerers we can ask for help?"

"It's been a long time since I cut ties with the secular world. All the sorcerers I know have left this world. There are some who maintain faint ties, like the Namgung Clan... but I don't think they'll be helpful in this situation."

"If we ask the Namgung Clan for help..."

"The Namgung Clan is a martial arts family. They probably have sorcerers working for them. But do you think they'll be helpful to a sorcerer who failed trying to reach the realm of harmony?"

"I don't think so."

No matter how I thought about it, it seemed reasonable to go find Baekran. Other renowned sorcerers? I couldn't trust those bastards easily. If there was absolutely no other way, I'd have to go find them as a last resort.

I ended my short conversation with Wiyu and left Nakwolsan Mountain. Wiyu decided to stay at Nakwolsan Mountain to take care of Milyeong. If it was her, she could handle any unforeseen circumstances.

• • •

Regressor Cheonma Baekran. As a regressor, she is currently working as a First-Class Royal Guard, belonging to the Imperial Palace, not the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

She wasn't someone I could just meet because I wanted to. Of course, I had a way to contact her. She and I were collaborators who had joined hands.

'To meet and talk as quickly as possible... I have to move myself.'

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Biraeshin (Flying Thunder God).

I became a bolt of lightning from Nakwolsan Mountain and dashed through the sky. The fastest means of transportation I could use was a space-time warp scroll, but unfortunately, I didn't have a space-time warp scroll with the destination's coordinates marked.

'Biraeshin is much faster than words, so running with Biraeshin is the best option in this situation.'

The destination was a city near the imperial capital. The land was so vast that I had to travel for several days.

It goes without saying that it wasn't easy to run using Biraeshin without rest. Just because it's a lightness skill doesn't mean stamina isn't consumed. In the case of Biraeshin, it feels like flying on the outside, as the name suggests, but you're actually running hard. I was getting tired as time passed.

Originally, I would rest in the middle to replenish my stamina, but... I ran until my heart was about to explode. When my body was exhausted and my legs wouldn't move, I used Complete Recovery. The cooldown of Complete Recovery came back faster than I got tired, so I could run almost infinitely. Food? I ate while running.

Fourth day.

Today, as always, I was running through the sky and crossing a mountain when a thunderous voice echoed.

"Stop!!"

The cry, clearly directed at me, was filled with undisguised hostility.

I tried to ignore it and pass by. I wasn't the type to stop just because someone told me to. But they didn't let me go.

'Is this a formation?'

I couldn't go forward. It was blocked. Before I knew it, I was trapped inside a formation set up on a large mountain.

I had little knowledge in this area. I couldn't tell exactly what kind of formation it was. One thing was certain: it was a troublesome formation that even I, who had reached the Samjeong realm, couldn't ignore.

I descended from the sky. It was like lightning was falling. Sparks were crackling around my body. I spoke without concealing my lightning energy.

"Come out."

They revealed themselves. Perhaps due to the influence of the formation, it was as if they suddenly appeared from within the thick fog.

They were dressed in ragged clothes made of leather patches and held weapons. They were bandits.

"This is absurd. You bandit bastards dare to block my path?"

The one who appeared to be the boss stepped forward. His face was so full of scars that it was difficult to make out his features. He laughed heartily, as if he should.

Hahaha! I don't know what kind of renowned expert you are, but this Yigirangsan (二狼山) is the territory of our Paereungsanchae (霸陵山蔡). Anyone who wants to pass through the mountain must pay a toll, no exceptions.

I narrowed my eyes and sized up the bandit boss. Judging from the energy I felt, his realm was Ogijungdan (Five Energies Middle Stage). It was excessively high for the realm of a mere bandit boss.

"Are you affiliated with Nokrim?"

Nokrim is a group of bandits trained in martial arts, and it serves under the Sanwang (山王) of the Sawangryeon (四王聯), a coalition of evil sects.

They pretend to be something by saying Nokrim and whatnot, but in reality, they are just a group of criminals made up of criminals. The reason why the bandits learned martial arts is because they were originally from martial arts sects. You can think of it as a gathering of criminals who were excommunicated for committing crimes or who were being chased.

Haha. What else would we be if not Nokrim? If you want to pass through this mountain, you have to pay a toll. The only exception is the government army... but obviously, you’re not a government soldier, daehyup (great hero). The toll varies for each person. For you, it’s 10 cheonok (heavenly jade).

Hand over cheonok to these bastards? Cheonok was both currency and a resource for getting stronger for me. I couldn't give even one cheonok to the bandit bastards.

"Are you so crazy that you want to die? Do you think the likes of you can stop me?"

"Why are you so edgy? What, did your family die recently or something? Think calmly. Do you think we would have blocked daehyup's path without anything? The formation set up on this mountain doesn't just block daehyup's path. It also strengthens our power."

The energy of the bandit boss and his subordinates began to become increasingly rough. They were temporarily getting stronger by using the power of the formation. It wouldn't be a power without a price. I didn't know what the price was, and I didn't care.

"Even if daehyup is an absolute master who has reached the Samjeong realm, you're not invincible. If you understand, pay the toll. I'm telling you nicely."

"You bastards should be kneeling and begging right now..."

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功)

Dark magic energy flowed out of my body. The magic energy did not rise upward but sank gloomily and spread in all directions. The bandit boss's expression twisted. The scars engraved on his face wriggled like worms.

"Magic energy?! Damn it. I thought he was an unknown expert, but was he from the Demonic Sect?!

The bandit leader swung his sword. Red gangqi (罡氣) sliced through the magical energy. But the magical energy didn't vanish completely.

"Ugh, boss..."

"Don't be scared by that phenomenon. We just have to kill that bastard! Attack!"

Before the bandits could rush at me all at once, I lifted my right foot and stomped on the ground.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Overlord Step (天魔君臨步) Wave Form (波形).

The magic energy wave, lying low on the ground, spread in all directions centered on me. The bandits who touched the wave trembled as if they had malaria. Among them, there were those who couldn't even breathe properly and collapsed on the floor.

The bandit boss's complexion also changed.

"This is bullshit.... To have my insides turned upside down with just one Jingak (眞覺)... This is Cheonmagunrimbo?! Could it be that he's the one who rose to Socheonma 30 years ago...?!"

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and held it in my hand. Pitch-black magic energy surged onto the red blade, transforming into the shape of a blade.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Sword (天魔劍).

"Damn it! Why is Socheonma here?!"

The bandit boss, shouting as if in despair, threw his sword at me. I lightly deflected the sword with Hwaryeon Bido. His sword spun around in the air and returned to the boss's grasp. It was a magic weapon, not an ordinary sword.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Sword (天魔劍) Hwicheon (Turning Back the Heavens).

I swung Hwaryeon Bido. Ganggi swept around in an instant. The air vibrated as if resonating with it. The upper and lower bodies of the bandits who were nearby were separated and fell.

But this was not enough. There were more living bastards than dead ones. Not to mention the boss, who was wearing a protective ganggi and rushing at me.

I moved my will. The magic energy lying low around me slowly moved clockwise.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Hwicheonmaryong (Reversing Heaven Demon Dragon).

The magic energy rotated, cutting off the world in an instant. Severance was synonymous with cutting.

Once, twice, three times, as the rotation was repeated, everything around me was torn apart. The severed bodies of the bandits, including the bandit boss, spun around in the air and fell to the ground.

I let out a long breath. My insides shook slightly, perhaps because I had overexerted myself momentarily. It was a level of internal injury that was embarrassing to even mention.

'The formation is still being maintained.'

There was no way the bandit bastards could have set up this formation themselves, so someone must have set up the formation for them. Probably a skilled sorcerer.

'I don't know how to deactivate a formation with sorcery. But that doesn't mean there's no way at all.'

Formations have a subject. If you destroy or change that, the formation will disappear. It would take a long time for me to go out and find it myself. The core of the formation is basically hidden. There's no way I, an amateur, can find it right away.

'In the case of this kind of formation, it uses the power of the mountain. In other words, you just have to smash the mountain.'

I swung Hwaryeon Bido once again. The black rotation became a small storm and swept through the mountain.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Hwicheonmaryong (Reversing Heaven Demon Dragon).

Trees were cut down, the ground was overturned, and rocks fell far away.

As expected, the formation failed to function as the mountain was damaged. I became a bolt of lightning and dashed through the sky.
