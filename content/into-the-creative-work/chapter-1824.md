I followed the trickling water, just as Baekran had said.

The thought of getting my hands on the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, started to excite me.

,Come to think of it, the story of [I Woke Up and Became the Cheonma] has changed quite a bit. Well, what can you do.'

The original story of the possessed Cheonma was set in a Murim world. But the setting of this world is Xianxia. That alone was a difference.

,It's like he got a Xianxia buff or something, Cheon Yu-un's personality seems to have become even more sinister.'

And the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.

The Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, would be stronger than in the original.

Before I knew it, the trickling water stopped. I hadn't walked for long. About 40 steps, give or take. I stared intently at the ground.

The flowing water was going into a small hole in the ground.

[Opening the Celestial Eye].

I used clairvoyance to look beneath the ground. As expected, there was a space. The water continued to flow underground.

,This is an artificially constructed passage.'

An artificial underground facility. This is the real Cheonma Tomb. I've come to the right place.

,There's a cleverly hidden door. It's fine since I can open the door with force... but there's too much water.'

It wasn't just any water. Water filled with Yin energy was constantly flowing through the underground passage. If I let my guard down for even a moment, my whole body could freeze to death.

,This much danger is nothing to obtain the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.'

I put my finger into the hollowed-out floor. I pushed in my Qi to strengthen my finger strength and lifted the door, which was over 30cm thick. The underground was pitch black. I could continue to see through it using the Celestial Eye, but it would constantly consume my energy.

I took out a flashlight from my inventory and went down below. I stepped on the floor, being careful not to touch the flowing water.

Jjeoeong! Pajijijik! Jjeoeong!

The electromagnetic field and the Yin energy collided. Because the lightning energy was frozen, I had no choice but to increase the output of the electromagnetic field. Then it became bearable to some extent.

,The energy consumption has increased several times over. If I hesitate, I'll freeze to death.'

Wearing more clothes wouldn't help. This Yin energy could penetrate clothing easily. Even the Black Tiger Robe I'm wearing can't block this terrifying Yin energy.

I walked along the passage, following the flowing water.

Kwajik!

The flashlight shattered. The Yin energy had invaded even the flashlight. It couldn't be helped since the flashlight didn't have a body as tough as mine.

[Opening the Celestial Eye].

It was clear that taking out a new flashlight would only lead to it shattering again, so I decided to just use the Celestial Eye. I slightly increased my walking speed.

A stone chamber appeared. It was a fairly large stone chamber. In the center of the stone chamber was a round floor, and flowing water was rotating around it.

,Is the water flowing out somewhere inside and then flowing back this way? Anyway, there's an awful lot of water. I think I need to go to the center... but the Yin energy is too severe. Even I'm not confident I can last more than an hour.'

For now, I decided not to go to the center and looked around. Coffins were standing against the walls. The coffins were dark and grimy, and old talismans were haphazardly attached to them.

There were a total of 13 coffins. Each coffin had a number written on it. However, instead of 'one (一),' it was written as 'first (初).'

The meaning was obvious. The body of the first Cheonma, Demonic martial art, must be enshrined in those coffins.

,The number of Cheonmas throughout history can't be just 13. The current Cheonma is the 21st generation. There should be 20 coffins.'

Looking at the numbers, they weren't continuous, and several were missing here and there.

,So there are seven Cheonma corpses that haven't been recovered. Should I say that's more than I thought...'

I arrived at my destination. But no matter how much I looked around, I couldn't see any books that looked like martial arts manuals.

,Looking at those coffins, they're sealed with talismans. The probability of the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, being inside the coffins is low.'

In the first place, if they were simply going to put the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, inside the coffins, would there be a need to come to a place like this to obtain it?

A round floor in the center that's just right for sitting. Coffins attached to the walls, surrounding it. If you have any sense, you couldn't not know what to do.

I walked towards the center of the flowing water.

Pajijik, pajik, pajijijijik!

As expected, the Yin energy was incredibly strong compared to other places. Just walking to the center was causing the electromagnetic field to shake.

I gritted my teeth.

,Even I, who am at the peak of the Fifth Stage, am having a hard time enduring it... Cheon Yu-un came in when he was weaker than me and got his hands on the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art. How?'

The difference between me and Cheon Yu-un.

Cheon Yu-un is the 21st Cheonma, Demonic martial art, a direct descendant of the current Cheonma. A golden spoon among golden spoons.

,Cheon Yu-un was a golden spoon in the original story too, so he received support with all sorts of elixirs.'

The electromagnetic field couldn't withstand the Yin energy and disappeared. Intense Yin energy penetrated into my body. Even using my lightning energy to the fullest to resist the Yin energy wasn't enough. My body trembled from the cold I felt. As soon as I arrived at the center, I sat cross-legged and chanted the Lightning Heavenly Art.

,This is a temporary measure. Cheon Yu-un probably ate an elixir containing Yang energy. Then I can also take an elixir containing Yang energy. It's not like I don't have any elixirs.'

That's the only way I can be safe from this damn Yin energy.

I took out the elixirs I had collected from my spatial storage.

Most of them were of poor quality, but I had over 200 elixirs. Among them was the Myriad Lightning Divine Pill (萬雷神丹), but I took it off the list. It was too valuable to use right now.

,...Should I take a gamble here.'

To swallow all the elixirs. Instead of resisting this Yin energy, if I use it in reverse, maybe I can reach the realm of the Three Perfections.

,If I fail, I'll die from Qi Deviation... but I have Complete Recovery.'

I first used the Water Noble Crystal (水貴晶) that I had obtained most recently. This elixir, infused with the power of water, would at least partially protect my body from the overwhelming spiritual energy.

Woojuk woojuk.

I repeatedly stuffed elixirs into my mouth, chewed, and swallowed. Since there were over 200 elixirs, this was also a task. Somehow, after eating all the elixirs, my energy became explosively strong. To the point where I couldn't even feel that terrible Yin energy.

,My body was already saturated even before eating the elixirs. This is a temporary phenomenon. The Yin energy is balancing it out, but if I make even a small mistake, my body won't be able to withstand the energy and will burst like a balloon.'

I calmed my breathing and used the Lightning Heavenly Art....

,No, wait a minute. I didn't come here to train in the Lightning Heavenly Style.'

I came to obtain the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art. Even after doing all this, those coffins aren't reacting. There's only one way to stimulate them.

,Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.'

I pushed the lightning energy flowing through my body deep into my dantian and brought out the dormant Demonic energy. The Demonic energy was wilder than the lightning energy. It wasn't as fast as lightning energy, but it was stronger.

Black energy slowly leaked out from my body, which was sitting cross-legged. The Demonic energy pushed away the Yin energy and reached the coffins.

Deolkeodeong deolkeong!

The coffins standing against the walls shook all at once. As if responding to the Demonic energy, Demonic energy also flowed out from the coffins. It was similar to the Demonic energy I had, but also different.

-I can feel high-quality Demonic energy....

-The guy who will become the 22nd hasn't even been here yet, and the 23rd is already here?

-Has something happened to the Divine Cult?!

-Could the Emperor be targeting the Cheonma, Demonic martial art, Divine Cult?!

-Be quiet. Show the dignity of a Cheonma, Demonic martial art.

The Demonic energy that came out of the coffins each took the form of a person. A total of 13 past Cheonmas appeared in the air and looked down at me. Among them, the one who caught my eye was the man in the center. A man with neatly styled white hair and a white beard. He was clearly old, but his face had no wrinkles.

I knew it instinctively.

He was the first Cheonma, Demonic martial art.

-First One-isiyeo, this guy is strange.

-This child isn't from the Cheon (天) clan....

-There's no law that the Cheonma, Demonic martial art, has to be from the Cheon clan. If they have the talent, what does the clan matter! Are the seeds of kings and generals separate!

-Shut up! The blood of the Cheon family is the most precious! This guy doesn't deserve to learn the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.

-Be quiet. What we need to see is whether this person has the talent for the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.

-Doesn't he have enough talent? This guy is already walking the path of the Demonic. I don't know what kind of Demonic art he has learned, but he has learned a Demonic art comparable to the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.

-Comparable to the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art? Are you crazy?

-I'm in favor. This guy deserves to be the Cheonma, Demonic martial art. Not only is he walking the path of the Demonic, but I can even feel the energy of a star. I don't know what kind of star it is... but it's the energy of a star that's definitely not weak. Perhaps like the First One....

-.......

As soon as the word "First One" came out, the Cheonmas who had been talking loudly fell silent. They all respected the First One. The beginning of the Cheonma, Demonic martial art, Divine Cult and the greatest Cheonma, Demonic martial art.

-First One-isiyeo. This person might have talent, but he lacks the qualifications. For one thing, it's only been a short time since Cheon Yu-un received the Cheonma Jade (天魔玉). This guy doesn't have the Cheonma Jade. Could he be a grave robber who illegally entered our tomb?

-First One-nim, if you only look at talent, he's the best ever. Look at how he's perfectly controlling that fierce Demonic energy. He has the mental strength to overcome the Demonic nature of the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, and even the energy of a star. He has talent comparable to the First One-nim. He might even be able to surpass that arrogant Emperor.

-We need to look at talent, not bloodline. The Cheonma, Demonic martial art, must be the best, and this guy might be able to rule the whole world.

-No. We must not pass on the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, to someone whose clan we don't even know!

Just as their debate was about to become heated again, the First Cheonma, Demonic martial art, raised his left hand. Everyone closed their mouths as if they had been waiting for it.

-I will ask you.

The past Demonic Venerable asked me a question.

-What is it that you desire?

I, who was circulating the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, found it difficult to open my mouth right away. If I forced myself to open my mouth and answer now, I would most likely suffer internal injuries. I might even die.

But I had a feeling that if I kept my mouth shut here, these guys wouldn't pass on the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, to me.

I forced myself to open my mouth.

"Cheonma, Demonic martial art."

-Hooh?

"Only, I alone, am the Cheonma, Demonic martial art!"

-Such impudence!

-Are you daring to deny us now?!

-Arrogant, so arrogant!

-As expected, this guy doesn't have the qualifications....

-Hahahahahahahahahahahahaha!

While everyone else frowned, only the First Cheonma, Demonic martial art, burst into laughter. He laughed as if he was very happy, and then reached out his hand towards me.

-I must see your inner self. If your ambition is a lie, you will pay the price.

I did not refuse him.

The 13 Cheonmas entered into me.
