We cautiously exited the mansion.

,Hiiiik...,'

Nevata Noriko, a plain woman with short, glasses, covered her mouth with both hands. It was because the front of the mansion was littered with corpses.

We ignored the corpses and headed towards the shrine, keeping an eye on our surroundings. We didn't encounter a single villager on the way to the stairs leading to the shrine.

I looked up at the stairs from the bottom.

It was dizzying.

It was dark, and I couldn't see the top of the stairs. Maybe it was an optical illusion, but it felt like the stairs continued infinitely.

,Climbing these stairs is no easy task. It'll take at least five minutes. If they start shooting from up there... the thought alone is terrifying.'

Mori Masahiro's words made all the circle members shudder. But there was no other way. To climb to the shrine by a different path than the stairs? Climbing the mountain without a paved road would be difficult. What could be done in five minutes could take over an hour.

We slowly climbed the stairs. If we went too fast, we'd get tired quickly. Fortunately, there were no attacks while climbing the stairs.

,Shibata. That monster must be at the shrine, right?'

To be honest, I wasn't very confident.

I couldn't use Complete Recovery right now.

Blood Pills? I had them, but eating too many could lead to death as a side effect. I had no intention of dying together with Shibata. If I died, the quest would end in failure. The quest had to succeed.

The end of the stairs came into view. I, who was leading the way, stopped. Fortunately, there were no enemies lying in ambush on the stairs.

,Let's come up with a plan.'

,A plan?'

,It's nothing grand. There will definitely be enemies when we go up there. They might have guns, so confronting them head-on isn't a good idea. So...'

I looked at both sides of the stairs. There was sloping ground and trees. It was quite steep, but if we crawled up... we could reach the shrine without using the stairs. The destination was already close.

,I see. You're saying we should move through a side path.'

,Yes. We'll split up. Mori-senpai, Fudo-senpai, Nevata-senpai, and I will go around the side, and Wada-kun will go up the stairs as is.'

,...Nakagami-ssi and Hagawa-ssi will go around the side of the stairs? The personnel arrangement seems a bit strange?'

,No. This is right. They'll be most wary of Mori-senpai and me, and if there are too few people, they'll find it suspicious. If there aren't any ambushes on the stairs... I want Nakagami-senpai and Hagawa-ssi to move as a separate unit and capture the shrine maiden without joining us.'

,Wait a minute, Seong! Aren't Nakagami-senpai and Hagawa-ssi in too much danger?!'

Wada Kyoshiro flared up. He glanced at Hagawa Ryoko, looking worried about her. But Hagawa Ryoko was listening to me without any objections.

,Not bad.'

Nakagami Risa said. Holding a hand axe, she curled her lips while looking at Hagawa Ryoko.

,It's like a mission that ninjas get. Right? Ryoko?'

,...Nakagami-senpai. I'm not a ninja. And please don't call me by my first name.'

,We're close enough to do that, aren't we? You can call me by my first name too.'

,.......'

Wada Kyoshiro stared back and forth between Nakagami Risa and Hagawa Ryoko with wide eyes. He seemed curious about when they had become so close. I couldn't tell him that it was because the three of us had been naked together.

,Anyway, you don't have any complaints about this plan, do you?'

Nakagami Risa asked Hagawa Ryoko. Hagawa Ryoko nodded, fiddling with the dagger hidden in her bosom.

,Whether it's reasonable or not... I'm worried about all of us gathering and breaking through head-on.'

,Since that's been decided, we'll move according to Yujin's words.'

They walked into the mountains next to the stairs.

They would do well. At least, they had no hesitation in killing people. In Nakagami Risa's case, her skills were a bit unreliable, but with Hagawa Ryoko from a ninja family, it would be fine.

,...Seong. You seem to have gotten close to Ryoko.'

Wada Kyoshiro said to me.

,Me, close to Hagawa-ssi? What are you mistaken about?'

,It's not a mistake. Ryoko... she kept watching Seong. I can tell, even if no one else can.'

Does he have a good sense?

Or does he know Hagawa Ryoko's habits or mannerisms as a childhood friend?

Either way, I don't have time to talk to Wada Kyoshiro here right now.

,Let's talk later. Now is not the time to talk about that.'

,...Okay.'

We arrived at the entrance of the shrine.

Was it called a torii? A large red gate greeted us. We passed through the torii and walked along the stone path to the shrine.

The shrine was quiet. There was no one around. Only the sound of insects echoed from all directions. The overall atmosphere was eerie.

I frowned as I looked at the shrine buildings. There were several buildings.

,Where do you think the shrine maiden is?'

I asked Mori Masahiro. He had been to the shrine during festivals.

,...I don't know either. The shrine is so big that there are many places for the shrine maiden to hide. I think she might be in the inner quarters.'

,Not in the main hall?'

,When I looked around yesterday, the main hall was a building for rituals. It's not a place where people stay.'

,...Well, we'll find out where she is if we wander around slowly.'

,I'm worried that there aren't many people around. They might be lying in ambush.'

,.......'

I had a rough idea.

This village was already finished. The shrine maiden, who controlled the village and used it as a drug factory, would have a lot to clean up.

,And she even sent people to kill me. Those bastards died by my hand instead.'

The shrine maiden must be very busy right now.

We searched the shrine here and there. There was nothing special.

It was when we were leaving the main hall without any results.

Kwaang!

With a rough sound, the door of the detached building opened. A huge man appeared through the small door.

A disproportionately large upper body compared to his lower body, black clothes covering his face.

It was Shibata.

,W-what is that monster!'

Wada Kyoshiro was shocked. He unconsciously tensed up and pointed his baseball bat at Shibata.

,...It seems like that monster Seong was talking about. I was half-doubtful when I heard about it... but seeing it in person makes my legs tremble even more.'

Mori Masahiro, holding a spear, glared at Shibata.

,We have to fight that monster?'

,W-what should we do?!'

Fudo Jun and Nevata Noriko panicked and trembled in fear.

Shibata, the object of fear, didn't pay attention to them and only looked at me. I couldn't see his exact gaze because his face was covered with cloth, but I instinctively knew that he was watching me.

,You! Why are you still alive!'

Shibata's voice was trembling. I grinned. He was hiding it with an angry voice, but he was afraid of me. I looked at Shibata's right arm. His right arm, which I had cut off, was attached. If you looked closely, you could see that his arm was sewn together with thick wire.

That alone couldn't have attached his arm. His right arm was drooping. It was clear that he couldn't move it.

,Where is the shrine maiden?'

,...The shrine maiden told me to kill you. I'll kill you.'

Shibata started running.

His speed was slower than when I first saw him. But it wasn't a speed to be ignored.

,Scatter! Scatter and kill him!'

Holding Japanese swords in both hands, I said while running backwards. Shibata was blatantly aiming for me.

I had anticipated the battle with Shibata and had already told the circle members.

I drew Shibata's attention, and Mori Masahiro and Wada Kyoshiro attacked Shibata from behind.

,Annoying!'

Kung, kung kung!

Shibata flailed his left arm.

He picked up a statue decorated in the shrine and threw it. Ridiculously, the first victim to be hit by the statue was Nevata Noriko, who was hiding in the farthest place.

,Kyaaaaaaak!'

Peo-eok.

She couldn't avoid the flying statue and her head was smashed, killing her.

,You bastard...!'

Fudo Jun, who was next to Nevata Noriko, threw five knives at Shibata. Two missed, but three hit. Two were stuck in his back, and one was stuck in his head.

The stuck knives pushed themselves out and fell to the floor. Thanks to that, the cloth covering Shibata's head fell off. Shibata's exposed face was disgusting. His red eyes looked like they were about to pop out, his jaw was bent in an S shape, and the center of his forehead was protruding like a horn.

His face was as inhuman as his physique.

,Y-you...!!'

Shibata turned his body and shouted at Fudo Jun. What was absurd was that he was moving without any problems even though a knife was stuck in his head.

,Is he really a monster? ...No, that can't be. This bastard's eyes are gone. He's high on drugs. He's not crying blood... but he must have eaten a Blood Pill.'

Shibata started running towards Fudo Jun. Fudo Jun was surprised and tried to run away, but tripped over a stone and fell.

,I'll smash that head of yours!!'

Shibata grabbed Fudo Jun's head with his left hand. With a thud! Blood and brain fluid flowed from his left hand.

While Shibata was killing Fudo Jun, I secretly approached and cut his right thigh. I couldn't cut it completely. His thigh muscles were as tough as his upper body.

,But I succeeded in making him limp.'

Shibata looked back.

,My leg!!'

,Where's the shrine maiden.'

,Die!!'

I hurriedly retreated and avoided his attack. Shibata started aiming for me again. Was it because I took away his mobility? He's not as threatening as before.

,Mori-senpai! Wada-kun! Just maintain the distance! We can hunt him easily if we just maintain the distance!'

,Hunting?! That monster is still a human being!'

,I like what Seong is saying. Is someone who grabs a human head and smashes it a human being?!'

Mori Masahiro and Wada Kyoshiro actively engaged in battle. They were furious at the deaths of two circle members.

,Uwaaaaaaaah!!'

Shibata screamed and went berserk. We continued the battle, maintaining a certain distance from him, who had lost his mobility.

Kung!

And finally, Shibata fell.

I ran to the fallen bastard and shoved my sword into his eye socket. The bastard trembled and died. The tedious fight was over.

Thud.

Mori Masahiro sat down. His left arm, which had been hit by Shibata's attack during the battle, was horribly broken. I approached him and checked his arm.

,Mori-senpai... To be honest, this arm is hopeless.'

,......I'm grateful to be alive. If he hadn't weakened... I would have lost my life instead of my arm.'

,Can you stand up?'

,My legs are fine, so I can walk. But I want to rest a little.'

,Why don't you rest forever while you're at it?'

,What...? Keo-eok?!'

I stabbed Mori Masahiro's chest with my sword. I felt the sensation of piercing his heart in my hand.

,I didn't like you acting like a senior. Die.'

He was a bastard I brought to deal with Shibata. Now that Shibata is dead, I don't need Mori Masahiro. And Wada Kyoshiro will be killed here for the same reason. If Wada Kyoshiro dies, Hagawa Ryoko will be mine.

,Seong!!!'

Wada Kyoshiro shouted and ran towards me, swinging his dented baseball bat.

I dodged the baseball bat and swung my sword. The sword cut Wada Kyoshiro's torso. But it was shallow. I didn't feel like I was cutting through his intestines.

Peo-eok!

The dodged baseball bat hit my side. The trajectory had changed in the middle.

,...Not bad?'

I raised my sword to finish him off. Wada Kyoshiro threw his bat at me and ran away. As I was about to follow him, my aching side, which had been hit, got in the way. I touched my side and clicked my tongue. I lifted my clothes and checked my side, and it was bruised blue.

Wada Kyoshiro ran into the mountains in an instant. It was too late to chase him.
