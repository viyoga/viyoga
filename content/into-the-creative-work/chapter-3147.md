"Abyoooooo!!"

Mr. Chwe swings his nunchaku at me. The nunchaku engraved with a golden dragon gleams, and a shockwave of compressed air erupts.

Pwaaaang!

My body blurs and vanishes from the shockwave. I was standing a short distance away from where I'd been.

One of the thirteen purple rose petals in my right eye peeled off and disappeared. The rose petals were the petals of the Demon Eye, Avalonia.

"Illusion? An illusion powerful enough to completely fool this sovereign, even for a moment! Is that the power of your Demon Eye?! A treasure ill-suited to the likes of you, bingz-bingz!"

Whiff, whiff!

Mr. Chwe twirls his nunchaku back into position and glares at me.

A madman who's a lich yet uses nunchaku as his primary weapon.

The problem is that those nunchaku aren't an ordinary weapon. According to the original setting, there's a record that the Absolute of the Steppe, Jing. Jing. Jinggis Khan, used these magic nunchaku. Whether that record is authentic or not, one thing's certain—it's a troublesome weapon.

'Thanks to the Demon Eye, I can cast illusions instantaneously without any spell formula... but I can't overuse it. It takes time for the Demon Eye to recover the energy it expends. Recovery during combat is impossible. And the Demon Eye's true ability, the Last Illusion that turns reality into fantasy, can only be used when at least ten petals remain.'

Using Last Illusion to turn Mr. Chwe into an illusion was, of course, impossible. The Last Illusion cannot erase an existence.

Mr. Chwe creeps toward me. I immediately pulled back, maintaining a distance of over five meters.

As a lich, he exudes the curse of impurity. A curse that siphons life from any living creature within five meters. A brief approach is fine, but staying near him for long visibly weakens you.

"Ho-oh. Unlike the youngsters these days, you seem to know about liches. You're bingz, but at least you've got the basics down!"

Mr. Chwe, who'd been twirling the magic nunchaku this way and that as if loosening up his hands, suddenly swung it mid-conversation. The magic nunchaku stretched out long and came down at my crown. I used telekinesis to twist my body sideways.

"For a lich, you fight more petty than I expected."

"What of it. In a life-or-death duel, where is there room for pettiness or cowardice? All that remains is victory."

"A dead man lecturing me on life-or-death duels. Laughable."

"Heh heh. Precisely why I must lecture all the more. This sovereign has already crossed the boundary between life and death!"

Mr. Chwe hurled the magic nunchaku. It spun like a boomerang, flying toward me on a curved trajectory. That one can't be dodged. Even if I evade, it follows and hits.

I ejected the Vajra Bell from my inventory. Even with the steep rental fee, it's a sure weapon that can match the magic nunchaku. The golden vajra and the nunchaku collided in midair. The weapons, each falling toward the ground, suddenly reversed direction and flew back to their respective owners.

The magic nunchaku landed in Mr. Chwe's hand, and the vajra floated up beside me.

"It smells of lightning and incense. That vajra. Have you received the consecration of Indra himself?"

"I don't know the full history of this thing either. What's certain is that it's exceptionally effective against the impure."

I gripped the vajra in my left hand.

[Light Saber]

Bzzt!

Lightning gathered on the Vajra Bell, and a blue, glowing plasma blade materialized. Around the blade, the golden currents unique to the Vajra Bell flickered.

I'd modified a rank-6 lightning spell in my own way and used it.

"Ho-oh. You intend to point your blade at this sovereign?"

"Of course not. It's just in case."

Close combat? If we fought, I'd lose 100%. Mr. Chwe was a kung fu master, while I had no talent for martial arts.

I couldn't fight Mr. Chwe by wrapping a barrier around my whole body. One curse from him and the barrier would melt away like ice cream under a scorching sun.

Mr. Chwe raised his left hand.

Black mana writhed from his body and converged on his left index finger.

Ttudeuk!

His left index finger bent back at a gruesome angle.

Immediately after, an image of a spike being driven into my chest came with an immense pain surging through my solar plexus.

“……!!”

The agony made it hard to even breathe. I nearly lost focus and collapsed to the floor.

'It wasn't my solar plexus that was actually struck. This is nothing but phantom pain caused by a curse.'

It wasn't the kind of curse that directly interferes with the mind. My mind was intact right now. It was fake pain inflicted solely on the body.

What was more baffling was that it was a curse I didn't know. A technique that didn't exist in the original game. I realized anew that this world was reality, not a game.

"Ha-oh, ha-oh. What a formidable spirit."

Mr. Chwe drew up the black mana again. Kkeodeudeuk! This time, his middle finger bent in a zigzag.

The curse's spike drove into my head.

"...Kkh!!"

An agony that made me think passing out would be preferable.

Yet even amid the agony, my mind didn't waver. I couldn't just keep taking it. I wove a spell formula through the pain.

'...Getting hit a second time makes it clear. This isn't phantom pain originating from the body. It's pain hammered directly into the soul. No matter how you slice it, that bastard's essence is a lich.'

A lich.

A mage who resurrected even himself from death through necromancy.

Amid the skull-splitting agony, I gritted my teeth and completed the spell.

A dark crimson magic circle formed in an instant.

[Death Ray]

A dark crimson beam shot toward Mr. Chwe. Too fast to dodge with the braid-helicopter. The moment it touched him, his body would be pulverized.

"Haoooooo!"

Mr. Chwe swung his right leg with a kiai. In a single kick, the Death Ray curved sideways—an absurd sight. Of course, the price was that Mr. Chwe's right leg was completely obliterated, but that was better than his torso being destroyed.

Dududududududu!

The braid-helicopter spun without issue, so he was still flying majestically.

"Excellent! Half-blood! State your name!"

"...Eugene Meyer."

"Eugene of the Ma clan! A splendid name! Do you happen to like mountains?"

"You crazy bastard. Who are you calling a descendant of Ma Su?"

"You know of Ma Su! Indeed, the Romance of the Three Kingdoms is a treasure of the world...! This sovereign acknowledges it! You are undoubtedly a descendant of Ma Su!"

"My surname isn't Ma, it's Meyer. A noble of Neo London."

"Ha-oh? You're a foreign devil? Are you joking? The stench of paocai reeks from your soul!"

"It's not paocai, it's kimchi! ...Forget it. I'm done talking."

I stalled for time until the agony subsided. As I prepared my next move, black mana surged from Mr. Chwe's entire left arm.

"I pay respects to your spirit! Let's see if you can endure this one as well."

Black mana coiled around his left arm.

Kkadeudeuk! Kkeudeudeuk! Kkeudeuk! Tteudeuk!

From fingers to shoulder. His entire left arm twisted, contorted, and bent. Watching his left arm wrench so violently that bones tearing through flesh and jutting out wouldn't have been surprising, I swallowed dryly.

In that instant, an idea flashed through my mind.

I immediately opened my inventory. A grimoire I'd obtained some time ago popped out. On the cover was written: Grimoire of the Necromantic Heart.

'Here it comes...!'

Over ten curse spikes drove into my entire body. Pain surged. The pain was less than expected.

"You halved the soul-targeting curse with a necromancy grimoire."

Mr. Chwe drew his right hand back and swung it. The magic nunchaku in his hand stretched long.

Amid the curse's pain, I held the Light Saber sideways and blocked the falling nunchaku.

Kwaaaang!

The nunchaku didn't fall—it stuck fast to the Light Saber and pressed down.

"Ha-oh, ha-oh! You endure well! But how about this! One-Ton Hammer!"

Mass was added to the nunchaku.

Unable to hold out, I was crushed down to the ground.

[Levitation]

[Telekinesis]

[Wind Surfing]

I used three spells in succession to disperse the impact as much as possible and landed on the ground.

I checked the damage. Aside from my throbbing left arm, there was no major harm.

Thump. Thump. Thump.

Jiangshi gathered around me. They formed an encirclement centered on me. I looked straight ahead instead of at the jiangshi. Mr. Chwe slowly descended to the ground. He glanced at the jiangshi gathered around. Their bodies melted and became black smoke-like energy that was absorbed into Mr. Chwe's body.

Mr. Chwe's twisted left arm returned to normal, and his obliterated right leg regenerated.

His unique skill: Corpse Devour.

"A shitty ability that summons trash mobs and uses them as healing potions... Still Joat-tier no matter how many times I see it."

"Heh heh. Feel free to despair. An enemy's despair is the finest praise this sovereign could ever receive."

Dozens of jiangshi lunged from all sides, claws extended.

[Electric Field]

[Attribute Grant: Holy]

Golden lightning bolts shot out in all directions from me. With a sizzling sound, dozens of jiangshi were electrocuted. Normally, electrocution has little effect on undead, but with the opposing holy attribute granted, the electrocution effect was amplified.

"Holy power?! How does a mage wield such pure holy power...?!"

The astonished Mr. Chwe was electrocuted as well.

I swung the Light Saber.

[Lightning Blade]

[Attribute Grant: Holy]

A sword aura carrying golden lightning spread out in a circular shape. It sliced through the waists of dozens of jiangshi, including Mr. Chwe.

The jiangshi turned to ash, and Mr. Chwe, his upper and lower body separated, was about to collapse when his braid began to spin and his upper body lifted into the air.

Mr. Chwe's eyes changed. The irises turned yellow, and the pupils became elongated.

A snake's eyes.

Rank-7 curse: Basilisk's Petrification.

In exchange for being unable to move himself, it renders anyone caught in his gaze unable to move as well. Not even invisible mana threads could be used. The one saving grace was that the heart still beat.

An ability that seemed far too simple for a rank-7 curse. The reason this curse is rank-7 is purely because its output is immense. Even a rank-7 of the same level could be bound for at least a minute. If it's the lich Mr. Chwe, he could bind someone for over three minutes.

'Even for me, escaping this curse without using my unique skill would take some time. He's already started recovering.'

Mr. Chwe's lower body sprang up and began attaching itself to his upper body. It wouldn't even take three minutes. He'd recover within thirty seconds.

"Ah. What a nasty curse."

"Ho-oh. You can move your mouth? What tremendous talent. But no matter how brilliant a genius, on the battlefield they fall in vain... I'll grant you a last word. Speak."

"You mercenary bastard. How long are you going to just watch?"

"Hm?!"

Mr. Chwe's eyes wavered. But having used the Basilisk's Petrification, he couldn't move either. The braid-helicopter? That's closer to external equipment than part of his body.

"You son of a biiiitch!! This is for my men!!"

From the sky, a mercenary in leather armor fell, sword extended. Seogeo-eok! Mr. Chwe's body was split in half.

The mercenary with slicked-back platinum blond hair and sunglasses grinned at me.

"There you are, Count. You owe me now."

"You idiot. Look behind you."

"What?!"

Mr. Chwe's bisected body rapidly fused back together and swung his nunchaku.

Kaaaang!

Wolfgang barely blocked it with his sword, but his stance was sloppy. His body flew and slammed into the ground.

"Fuck, even for an undead, that's just ridiculous!"

From Mr. Chwe's veins, something like black powder poured out instead of blood.

"Nanomachines, brat!! True China's nanomachines are the world's finest!!!"

Zzzzzzzzzz.

The nanomachines covering Mr. Chwe's body swarmed like tiny insects.

Phase 2 begins.

'What a shitty boss.'
