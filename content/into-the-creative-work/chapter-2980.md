I, who had completed the trial, returned to the Isis Temple. I had moved near the Isis Temple with a spatial movement scroll.

Even if this trial was easy for me, it would have been a difficult trial for others who were not me. A colossal Monster being in the sea, not on land, was not easy from the start.

‘Luck also followed. He was in a sleeping state. Since it was a Horse-Fish (Hippocampus), as soon as it realized something was wrong, it could have instantly escaped outside the influence of the orbital bombardment.’

Because it was in a sleeping state, by the time it realized, it was already too late.

I held the spoils obtained from catching the Hippocampus in my hand. It was a blue orb, and its temperature was uniquely cool.

「Hippocampus Orb.

Can change seawater into freshwater.

Can breathe underwater.

Can summon and command young Hippocampi.

Rank: SS」

‘I tried summoning a young Hippocampus. A normal horse-sized Monster that can only operate underwater. It can also fight. It would be somewhat helpful underwater.’

But there weren’t that many occasions to go underwater.

‘Summoning cannot be used in other worlds. Because the Atlantis System is involved.’

The ability to breathe underwater? I had the Blessing of Water.

Change seawater into freshwater? My Inventory alone is overflowing with clean mineral water.

‘Well, I should put it in my Inventory for now.’

I might find a use for it someday. Or give it as a gift to someone. I might also put it in my Inventory and forget about it.
