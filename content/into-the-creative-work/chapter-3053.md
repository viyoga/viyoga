The Lake of Heaven.

I looked down at my feet. A transparent, colossal lake floating in the sky lay beneath me.

It was dizzying, as though I might plummet at any moment, yet a sense of mystery washed over me—an unknowable power was holding the lake aloft.

'No. Is the lake floating in the sky due to its own power?'

Like the Lake of Benevolence and the Lake of Earth, the Lake of Heaven offered nothing knowable beyond its name. The lake itself was of a higher order than I was.

I stepped across the surface of the Lake of Heaven and headed toward its center.

There, the Martial Alliance Leader—Wang Jiyuk, the Green Precursor—sat cross-legged atop the water, circulating his qi. The energies of heaven and earth flowed quietly around him, and a handful of water became a small dragon that coiled around him.

I checked the aura emanating from Wang Jiyuk and furrowed my brow.

'Upper Harmony stage. He rose from Middle Harmony to Upper Harmony in just a few years?'

By any reasonable measure, that rate was absurd.

Even I, who possessed the power of the Heavenly River Star, could not have grown at such a pace.

'Was the lake truly such a remarkable method of training? Enough to elevate one's realm so drastically in mere years?'

My shock was brief.

There was no backing down now. A gap in realms? I knew well that realms were not invincible.

Crackle.

Black lightning ignited at my fingertips.

Thunder qi and Heavenly Demon qi intermingled, and I seized the most destructive force of all—black thunder—in my hand and hurled it. The black lightning ruptured the air as it barreled toward Wang Jiyuk.

The small water dragon coiling around Wang Jiyuk's body met the black lightning head-on.

KWAAAAAANG!

Steam billowed thickly with the explosion. Of course, mere steam could not blind my eyes.

"To ambush an opponent in the midst of qi circulation without hesitation. A Demonic Cultist indeed—not a shred of honor in your actions."

Wang Jiyuk opened his eyes. He rose slowly to his feet.

"Ambush? Can attacking openly from the front like this be called an ambush? You noticed the moment I appeared here, yet you pretend otherwise. You're putting on quite the pathetic show."

Heavenly Demon Divine Art—Heavenly Demon Lord's Step.

I stomped down on the lake's surface with all my might.

A massive ripple erupted across the lake, and the entire expanse undulated once.

It was a step meant to seize control of this space, but unfortunately, the initiative here was firmly in Wang Jiyuk's grip.

"Heavenly Demon. Compared to when I last saw you, your power and realm have not changed much. Did you fail to gain enlightenment through this lake?"

"I find you curious. How does one gain enlightenment from mere illusions?"

"I lived countless lives. From the life of a lowly insect to the life of a great immortal. Even if they were not real, I experienced the cycle of reincarnation indirectly. It was an experience no amount of gold could buy. A mere Demonic Head like you could never understand."

"You were already a piece of shit, and now your tone and attitude have made you even more of a piece of shit. I know you're not alone here. Where is Namhwa Noseon?"

"I encountered him at the end of reincarnation. How do you know of him?"

Normally, I would have simply ignored him. Freely spilling information that benefits an enemy is the work of amateurs.

But sometimes, deliberately revealing information and observing the reaction can yield profit. Besides, by my judgment, Wang Jiyuk was a dead man walking.

"I met Taegongmang's clone at the Lake of Earth. He kindly informed me of just how fucked the Immortal Realm is."

A voice of shock burst from Wang Jiyuk.

-Taegongmang! The traitor of the Immortal Realm! That arch-criminal was hiding in my secret realm?!

The voice was far too gruff to belong to Wang Jiyuk.

I narrowed my eyes and looked at Wang Jiyuk, then let out a hollow laugh. There is a great difference between seeing with knowledge and seeing without. I could vaguely perceive the existence within Wang Jiyuk.

"I wondered where you were hiding. Turns out you were tucked inside a man's mind."

"I am not hiding. He graciously aids me from within my mindscape. Namhwa Noseon is truly a compassionate and wondrous being."

Wang Jiyuk seemed bewitched by the immortal. I decided to grant charity to this foolish beggar—the truth.

"Wang Jiyuk. Did you know? The purpose of this secret realm is also to cultivate Ascendants and devour them. The moment you ascend to the Immortal Realm, you become a slave to the immortals, worked for ages, and eventually butchered like livestock. Kgh—haaack!"

A sudden, immense pressure crushed down on me, and I vomited blood.

The Heavenly Prohibition.

It was the price for recklessly divulging information about the Immortal Realm. Outside, it seemed this level was tolerable, but perhaps because this was a secret realm, the prohibition was stronger.

"You dare try to bewitch me with such worthless nonsense! I have already experienced the Immortal Realm! Becoming an immortal is achieving eternal life. Ascension is the goal of all beings!"

They say the Lake of Heaven shows hallucinations of becoming a god.

Wang Jiyuk had likely experienced the hallucination of becoming an immortal and had false information implanted along with it.

'It seems he received Namhwa Noseon's help in raising his realm. It's only natural he cannot believe the truth even when his enemy speaks it.'

Even from Wang Jiyuk's perspective, if an enemy suddenly spouted the truth, I wouldn't believe it either. Naturally, he would take it as a deception tactic.

Wang Jiyuk's complexion suddenly flushed red and blue by turns.

'Did he suddenly go mad?'

That did not seem to be the case.

He was probably conversing with Namhwa Noseon in his mindscape. Specifically, talking shit about me.

"Heavenly Demon! Speaking with you was a mistake! Your tongue tricks no longer work on me!"

"Tongue tricks? For fuck's sake. I have no tongue tricks for you."

Wang Jiyuk moved both palms through the air in sweeping motions. It looked ridiculous, like he was dancing, but with each movement of his hands, his internal energy grew stronger. The surrounding water rippled and transformed into the shape of dragons.

Then Wang Jiyuk thrust both palms straight toward me.

Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms. The demon-quelling energy mingles with the water, becoming a dragon that flies toward me.

Palm technique meets palm technique.

Crackle.

I thrust my palm forward, black lightning coiling around it.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Heavenly Demon Divine Palm.

A black palm silhouette rises and collides with the dragon.

I had my confidence. Among martial arts of the same tier, the Heavenly Demon Divine Art could be called the pinnacle when it came to sheer destructive force alone.

Yet the Heavenly Demon Divine Palm failed to push back the dragon—it was pierced through.

'Damn demon-quelling energy. The elemental disadvantage is one thing, but the gap in cultivation realm isn't something to dismiss either.'

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Heavenly Demon Phantom Step.

Srrrk.

Leaving afterimages, I moved stealthily yet swiftly to evade the dragon. The Heavenly Demon Phantom Step was closer to a divine movement art than a footwork technique.

Wang Ji-yuk keeps sweeping his palms through the air this way and that. Each time, his energy becomes a dragon and surges toward me.

There's nothing here but the lake's water, so that dragon shape may seem like nothing at all—but the power of that dragon shape is something even I cannot afford to ignore.

'Even one hit means internal injuries, guaranteed. The force is too strong to deflect with the Golden Steel Demon Dragon, and the demon-quelling energy is grating besides.'

Using Heaven-Shattering Emperor recklessly would leave me hung up on Namhwa Noseon. Heaven-Shattering Emperor had to be used only when I was certain.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 10]

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 9]

When even the Heavenly Demon Phantom Step couldn't manage evasion, I used Instant. With the battle continuing without pause through several exchanges, Wang Ji-yuk's attacks were starting to become familiar.

"Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms—Mad Dragon Palm!"

Wang Ji-yuk shouted as he unleashed the technique at full power. Boong-jjeu boong-jjeu. It was such a ridiculous-looking move that the onomatopoeia played automatically in my head. Yet its power was murderous. Six water dragons flew in, each moving differently.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 8]

By the time I used Instant, it was already too late. There was no opening to dodge.

[Using Leap.]

[This space cannot be leaped through.]

I couldn't use Leap, one of my trump cards. This space itself was special.

'If I can't avoid it, I'll have to take it head-on.'

I extended both arms and grasped at the air. Space caught on the tips of my ten fingers. I clenched it hard. Originally a technique that required dominating space to use. Fortunately, I could dominate about a meter of space around me.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Returning Heaven Demon Dragon. Heaven and Earth Return to Heaven.

The moment I swung both arms, space twisted. The six water dragons surging toward me had their heads torn off, sending water spray scattering in all directions.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. North Wind Demon Dragon. Mad Wind Command.

Dozens of sharp winds swept in all directions toward Wang Ji-yuk. Some of the lake's water mixed with the wind, making each one a powerful blade in its own right.

Wang Ji-yuk staggered across the water's surface. It was a footwork and divine movement art of utter chaos. It even looked like a drunk old man making a fool of himself.

'Is that the famous Drunken Eight Immortals Step of the Beggar's Sect?'

Once I realized it, I could see the order within the chaos. They were the Beggar's Sect, a sect of beggars, but their martial arts were absolute techniques.

Wang Ji-yuk circled around me with the Drunken Eight Immortals Step, swinging his palms wildly and firing palm winds. I hastily raised protective qi to block the palm winds.

Despite the ridiculous-looking movements, their power was incomparably strong. My protective qi began collapsing rapidly.

I drew in a breath and exhaled.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Poison Cloud Demon Dragon. Thousand Transformations Ten Thousand Poisons.

The breath I exhaled became a black-green poison mist spreading in all directions.

Wang Ji-yuk quickly retreated backward to avoid the poison.

"No wonder they call you a demon head—using such cowardly tricks! You despicable bastard!"

"Kukuku. Underhandedness is the virtue of the demon path."

I swung my palm toward Wang Ji-yuk as he widened the distance. A fierce wind rose, and the poison chased after him. Wang Ji-yuk held his breath as if prepared to risk poisoning. Then suddenly, the poison vanished without a trace.

My face twisted. I could see that Namhwa Noseon had used Taepyeong sorcery to erase the poison mist.

"Namhwa Noseon...!"

"My thanks, Namhwa Noseon!"

Wang Ji-yuk kicked up water spray and closed the distance to me in an instant, swinging his palm.
