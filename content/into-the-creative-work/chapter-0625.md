I came out of the Tomb of Swords with Seira.

She changed from her training clothes back into her original outfit. Clothes that clung to her body for easy movement, yet felt elegant. It was an outfit that looked similar to the formal wear that knights often wore.

As we moved together, we naturally started talking. Once the conversation started, talking with her flowed smoothly.

,Arpis-gongnyeo-nim, used a greatsword?"

And it wasn't just any greatsword. It was a Zweihänder bigger than her own height.

,The disadvantage of the Tomb of Swords is that the practice sword forms are limited to longswords."

,It must have been very inconvenient during the duel."

,I am also familiar with swords. The length of the sword is just a slight difference."

From other people's perspectives, that slight difference is a difference that can never be ignored.

Unlike other ordinary noble young ladies, I had to talk to Seira about topics such as training, martial arts, and knights.

,Since when did ,gongnyeo-nim, take up the sword?"

,Hmm.... I don't remember."

Seira raised her hand. Her hands were rough, unlike her beautiful appearance. They were calloused, and there were old scars.

I'm a little disappointed. I would have preferred her hands to be smooth if possible.

And at the same time, I had a question. With the power of Seira's family, it would be easy to manage her hands using magic or items.

,I can't pinpoint exactly when I started holding a sword. I just remember holding a sword since I was very young. What about you, ,gyeong,?"

,I haven't been holding a sword for long."

Based on real-world standards, it hasn't even been four years since I picked up a sword. I held a wooden sword in high school and picked up a real sword for the first time when I entered university. If it weren't for my trait, Yeongcheon Style, I might still not have mastered the basics of swordsmanship.

,Ah, you were an exile. I understand."

Seira lowered her hand.

I couldn't help but ask her.

,Gongnyeo-nim,. May I ask why you don't erase the scars on your hands?"

To make the excuse that calluses formed, creating the optimal hand shape for gripping a sword... her level was too high for that. At Seira's level, the presence or absence of calluses doesn't matter.

,It's proof of the training I've done so far. Every time I look at my hands, I am reminded of the training I've done, and I can regain my mindset. And every time I look at my hands, I realize what path I am walking."

,...That's amazing. Arpis-gongnyeo-nim."

,It's just stubbornness."

While talking with Seira, I intentionally brought up Elena's name. Seira felt a certain sense of inferiority towards Elena, so it was perfect for stimulating her.

,I have received a lot of help from Elena in many ways. The Legion I belong to was also able to grow rapidly with Elena's support."

,I can also support the Legion. More than that, the honorific is a bit annoying."

,Honorific? If you tell me which part is bothering you, I will correct it."

,You call that witch by her name, but you call me by my family name."

,I can't treat Arpis-gongnyeo-nim, carelessly."

,Is it okay to treat Duke Valderth carelessly? Call me by my name too."

,...If that is ,gongnyeo-nim's, wish, I understand. May I call you Seira-gongnyeo-nim,?"

,Yes."

The issue of honorifics was something I secretly hoped for. If the honorifics become closer, the relationship will also become closer.

It was then. Seira suddenly stopped abruptly. Her head goes up. I also stopped and followed her gaze.

A pigeon was flying vigorously towards the Arpis territory in the sky.

,Seira-gongnyeo-nim,. Are there wild pigeons around here?"

,That's not a pigeon, it's magic. As you know, this area is a place where space teleportation scrolls or communication magic don't work. As a result, methods of physically sending messages have developed. That magical messenger pigeon is a prime example."

To use a space teleportation scroll, I had to go to a place a little further away from here.

,Could something serious have happened?"

,I don't know. It could be a simple report, or an incident could have occurred."

,Shouldn't we hurry back?"

,It's okay. My father is in the city. And even if you and I go quickly, there's nothing we can do."

That was true.

This is Duke Arpis' territory. It's not an incident that I, an outsider, should interfere in every little thing.

We arrived at Arpis City while maintaining the same pace.

I entered the inner castle with Seira.

Knights, soldiers, servants, and citizens greeted Seira warmly. She was popular with people. This is probably because she is a beautiful woman with an attractive appearance and her personality isn't picky.

We were guided by the knights and soldiers and entered the audience chamber.

Finally, I met the Foolish Sword Duke, Noite Arpis.

He was sitting with his legs crossed on a fancy chair.

Even though he was sitting, I could tell that his body was large. If he stood up, he would probably be well over 2M tall. He also has a solid body trained with muscles.

He was a man with ash-gray hair and a thick beard, just like Seira. The wrinkles around his eyes are soft. Rather than a duke, he gives off the feeling of a free-spirited mercenary.

I knelt on one knee in front of Noite. All kinds of people's gazes are directed at me, including Noite, vassals, knights, and servants.

,I am Seong Yujin, a knight of Valderth."

,Welcome. Knight of Valderth. I have already heard the news that you passed through the Tomb of Swords. And... you managed to bring my daughter back. Is it only been three months? You look the same as ever, Seira."

Noite smiled and didn't hide his delight. On the other hand, Seira was still expressionless.

,Father, you are the same as ever."

,Daughter. Training is good, but also look around the world. I don't want my daughter to become a frog in a well."

,What's important for a swordsman is constant training. You were the one who said that, Father, have you already forgotten?"

,Hoo. You don't want to lose a single word."

Noite smiled bitterly and shook his head, then turned his gaze back to me.

,Seong Yujin-gyeong,. You must have something to tell me."

,Here it is."

I took out a letter from my chest. A knight checked the letter and then handed it to Noite politely.

Noite quietly unfolded the letter and read the contents. There was no change in his expression. His eyes were as serious as when I first saw him.

,Do you know the contents of this letter?"

,I don't."

Even if I don't know, I can roughly guess. It's probably a story related to the Empire's Ten-Year Plan, and Elena is asking him for cooperation.

,I'm told to send the reply to the letter through you. You seem to have the trust of Duke Valderth."

,I consider it an honor as a knight."

,The contents of the letter are not an easy topic that can be decided right now. It seems like it will take some time to make a decision, so can you wait?"

,Receiving the reply is also my mission. I will wait."

,Listen."

He said. All the vassals, knights, and servants focus their attention on Noite.

,Seong Yujin-gyeong, played along with my prank and passed through the Tomb of Swords, and even brought Seira back. Treat him with the utmost hospitality as a distinguished guest. You may step back now."

It was an order to leave.

I bowed to Noite and stepped back from the audience chamber.

After leaving the audience chamber, I wiped the sweat flowing on my forehead with the back of my hand.

From the middle on, Noite's momentum pressured me. I don't know if he did it on purpose, but I definitely felt that he was no ordinary person.

,Empire Ogong. That reputation can't be false."

If I fight, I will lose 100%.

Noite looked at Seira.

He didn't expect Seong Yujin to bring Seira. He sent him to the Tomb of Swords to stimulate Seira, but he didn't expect him to bring Seira back at all.

,What happened in the Tomb of Swords?"

,Nothing happened."

,You came back with him, and you expect me to believe that?"

,......."

Seira pursed her lips tightly.

Noite didn't try to force her to open her mouth. As a father, he knew very well that it would only backfire.

,Okay. I won't ask deeply. You probably had a duel with him anyway."

Noite's eyes didn't miss the fact that Seira flinched for a moment.

,Seira. I don't know what you think of that knight. He's not as great a knight as you think. I don't know why you're showing interest in that knight."

,I've talked to him on the way here, so I know that much."

,Then what exactly did you see in him?"

,......."

Seira closed her eyes for a moment. The duel she had with Seong Yujin comes to mind.

It wasn't a duel to the death, but a duel closer to a sparring match. But he risked his life in that duel. If he made a mistake, he could have died from internal injuries. The recovery speed is faster in the Tomb of Swords, but that doesn't mean you can't die inside.

,He is not a formal knight."

,...Hoo. That's a bit of an unexpected statement. And?"

,If he becomes a knight of our family, it will be a great help."

,I never thought you would say such a thing. Did you perhaps fall for him?"

,......."

Noite, who received his daughter's cold gaze, cleared his throat.

,Ahem. I don't know what you saw in him, but his actions are by no means worthy of being called a knight."

Noite called a servant and ordered him to bring information about Seong Yujin. He handed the data to Seira.

,Recently, he killed Baron Estor. He brutally killed the baron's soldiers and knights and left."

,Baron Estor hired an assassin to kill him. It's a bit unconventional, but he deserved to die."

,A bit unconventional? If it weren't for the Valderth Dukedom, he would already be on the run."

,If Father were in his situation, would you have spared Baron Estor?"

,Why should I spare a guy who tried to kill me? Seira. I am different from him."

,Yes. You are different. That's why I like him more."

,As you can see from the data, there are some missing parts here and there. Duke Valderth probably manipulated it. To conclude, he is Valderth's hunting dog."

Hunting dog.

It refers to those who do dirty work that cannot be officially announced.

,And tell me honestly. More than half of the reason you want to make him a knight of Arpis is because of Duke Valderth, right?"

,It's too much of a waste for him to be under that witch."

,If that's what you want, I won't stop you. However, I have no intention of helping you with this matter. Experience for yourself how difficult it is to get people."

,I have no intention of receiving help."

,Still, let me give you some advice. There's a rumor that he likes women. If you use your beauty, wouldn't he fall for you right away?"

,......"

Seira glared at Noite and left the room without a word.
