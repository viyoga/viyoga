Strength 150. Estimated to be the strength of an S-Class Hunter.

Here, strength refers to the pure physical power without using mana.

In fact, the exact strength of an S-Class Hunter is unknown. Even among S-Class Hunters, there were significant differences depending on their awakened abilities and training.

For example, Han Ah-young-ssi, an S-Class Hunter, would likely have lower strength than an A-Class Hunter. Han Ah-young's combat style is long-range attacks using her abilities, not physical strength.

,Even if you don't train, you can temporarily enhance your body by using mana.'

Enhancing the body with mana is a basic skill. No proper Hunter doesn't know how to do it. However, it's not used often because it's not very efficient.

Right now, I can easily lift about 1 ton without using mana. If I push myself a bit, I could probably manage 1.5 tons.

If I use mana here? I don't even know how much weight I could lift.

,It depends on how much mana you use. And it feels like it goes in as a multiplicative operation rather than an additive one in a game, so the efficiency is better.'

I clenched my fist and punched the air.

Pung.

A light sonic boom echoes. It's a very light punch, but it's powerful enough to kill an ordinary person if they were hit.

,If I match stamina and agility to 150 as well, I'll become a human weapon.'

There was a slight problem.

It's that controlling my strength has become a bit difficult as my power has increased. When I tried to sit on a chair and grabbed the armrest, the armrest crumpled and broke.

I guess it's a side effect of raising 10 stats at once instead of raising them one by one gradually.

,I can figure out the strength control if I struggle for a few hours.'

It was something I had experienced a few times, and it wasn't particularly difficult.

,Should I go to a dungeon or something.'

• • •

I entered an open A-Class dungeon.

Inside the dungeon, I could easily see A-Class Hunters wandering around.

&gt;

,Those are just A-Class Hunters in name only.'

Even though it's an A-Class dungeon, the profitability is low here, so it's not a popular dungeon for A-Class Hunters. There are more B-Class Hunters than A-Class. The A-Class Hunters here? Most of them are just scrubs.

They became A-Class Hunters, but they're far below average and scraping the bottom.

A-Class Hunters above average don't come to places like this unless they're here for fun like me. It's a waste of energy and time.

I felt the presence of a monster in my senses. After moving a bit, I found the monster.

Iron Lizard.

A lizard big enough to chew up a person. Its skin is harder than steel. Sword energy doesn't work well on this guy. It's fast and strong, but its attacks are simple. For a proper A-Class Hunter, it's nothing more or less than a punching bag. Of course, I don't mean a literal punching bag. It just means it's easy to handle.

,Blunt weapons are effective when hunting this guy.'

I'm only going to fight with my fists. I won't use weapons, armor, or abilities.

"Krrr...."

The Iron Lizard growled and charged. It charges without hesitation. It's got one hell of a nasty temper.

I dodged slightly to the side and threw a punch. The punch landed squarely on the Iron Lizard's side.

Peok!

The Iron Lizard, sent flying, fell to the ground and flailed its legs. I don't know if it's because of the pain or because its bones are broken, but it's out of it.

I approached and finished it off by slamming my fist down on the Iron Lizard's head.

,Fuck. This is it.'

Even though it's called a punching bag by A-Class Hunters, it's still an A-Class monster. There are no A-Class Hunters who kill it with their bare hands like me.

,My fist hurts a bit, but that's always the case.'

I put the Iron Lizard's corpse in my inventory. Originally, I would have to cut open the body and take out the magic stone, but since I have an inventory, I can do it all at once later. The other byproducts don't make much money. The skin of the dead Iron Lizard becomes less durable.

Excited, I clenched my fists and wandered around the dungeon, hunting Iron Lizards. Hunters spoke to me along the way.

"Seong Yujin-ssi! Aren't you Seong Yujin-ssi?! Did you come alone?!"

I'm the Hunter Association's official S-Class Hunter candidate. My recognition is already beyond that of a decent celebrity. No, the whole world probably knows my name. The influence of an S-Class Hunter isn't limited to just one country.

"Yes. I came in to warm up."

"I saw you catching Iron Lizards with your bare hands without using mana. As expected of Seong Yujin-ssi. You're amazing. Your ability is electricity manipulation, not physical enhancement...."

"You idiot. Hunter Seong Yujin is also an acceleration ability user. The acceleration ability itself is a type of physical enhancement."

"Ah. Is that so. Anyway, it's an honor to meet you here. Haha."

Requests for handshakes flooded in.

I endured my annoyance and responded to their requests.

This is reality. It'll be a pain if bad rumors spread.

,I'm not an S-Class Hunter yet.'

After that, the hunting didn't go smoothly. People flocked to me, probably because word got out. I had no choice but to give up hunting and go back. As I was going to the parking lot, a reporter asked me for an interview. It was a short interview, so I just responded roughly.

,Damn. It seems like it'll be difficult to go to open dungeons without hiding my face in the future.'

It'll be difficult to leave the house carelessly.

,Well, it doesn't matter. I have the Amusement Life app. If I want to go outside, I can just go to another world.'

[You have selected Savior of the Academy.]

[Starting amusement.]

Having entered the amusement world, I looked up at the sky. White snow was falling.

,One of the Academy's events. The pure white hell.'

The snow will continue to fall until the cause is resolved. How to solve the cause? Of course, I know. But it's a bit tough to solve it alone.

,The climax of the pure white hell is on December 24th. Christmas Eve. On that day, the 3rd Legion invades. In addition, all sorts of villains, demons, and monsters start rampaging in December. The cause is obviously due to the influence of the Legion's invasion.'

The masters of the original game... of course, don't skip this event. Instead, they deal with troublesome enemies until December 24th, bringing them down to a manageable level.

,There's too much to gain to ignore this event.'

Above all, it's a golden opportunity to kill one of the Legion Commanders. It's too good to pass up this event just because it's dangerous.

,I can handle it well enough now.'

Besides, Seong Hari was at the Academy now, which wasn't in the original story. Although she's weakened compared to her prime, it's reassuring just to have her at the Academy.

,Before making a plan... should I use Future Information?'

[Future Information

Acquire future information randomly.

Future information only provides information that will definitely be helpful to you.

Price: 200,000 points

※Caution

You determine the true value of future information.]

[Would you like to use Future Information?]

,Use it.'

It's something I've already decided. My will is firm.

Darkness covered me. I couldn't resist that darkness. That's because this isn't real darkness.

Instead of snow, ashes were falling from the sky. The ground was burning. The place I was in was the Academy, but it was full of corpses. From active Heroes to students wearing Academy uniforms.

Kwakwakwakwang!

A booming sound echoed from the sky. When I glanced up, the Demon Legion and the Spirit Legion were fighting. Leading the Spirit Legion was Thunder Owl Mocha. Other top-tier spirits I knew were fighting the Legion.

Covered in blood, I walked forward. The Academy had become a battlefield. There was no way the buildings would be intact. After passing over various buildings, I arrived at a place where the white-haired Seong Hari was dead.

Not the real Seong Hari, but the being that was Seong Hari in the dungeon. I once called her Young Hari, referring to her as a young Seong Hari.

And now, the 8th Legion Commander, Baal.

Baal was dead. A broken spear was embedded in his abdomen. Seong Hari's spear. Baal fought Seong Hari and was defeated and killed. But where is the victor, Seong Hari?

I stared at her corpse for a while before coming to my senses.

Even though I'm covered in blood, I don't feel any pain. Even if I try to move my arms, they don't move. The only senses I'm allowed are sight and hearing.

,...This isn't reality. It must be the aftermath of using Future Information. I didn't expect it to show me such an illusion.'

The emotions that had been boiling up as I looked at the dead Baal subsided. Instead, I tried my best to get information.

,It's a war. The Spirit Legion is probably the one I gathered. Leaving aside how I maintain those spirits, there are too many enemies. This means that at least 3 Legions had to invade. Seong Hari... killed Baal. Then where is she?'

She could be fighting elsewhere, or she could have died after killing Baal.

,Status window.'

It doesn't pop up.

I realized even more that this isn't reality.

,The clothes I'm wearing now are... a school uniform. This means that this future will happen within at least 3 years. It's an event that didn't exist in the original game. I'm very curious how it got to this point.'

Kung.

A red giant fell from the sky.

A demon with a red body, red wings, goat legs, and 7 large horns. The Fifth Legion Commander, Mekin, also known as the Demon King of Destruction.

He threw something at me. It was Kang Ji-young-ssi. Her lower body below the abdomen was gone. The blood and intestines flowing out were fresh, as if she had just died.

"......."

"Haha. I just picked her up on the way because it would be boring to come empty-handed. Oh my, you're so touched that you can't even speak."

"Mekin...!!"

"Seong Yujin. You've been meddling too much."

Mekin approached me. The ground rumbled with each step.

"Did you think we'd just keep getting beaten? Well, this time, the sacrifices were great to force it to proceed. But looking at your dick, I think it was well worth it. I'll give you a special seat to watch the destruction of this world. After that, you'll be embalmed in the center of hell as an example. It's just for you, who dared to kill a Legion Commander, so you can consider it an honor."

I looked at Mekin and then looked up at the sky. After looking around the world that was turning into a mess, I lowered my head again.

"Ah, fuck. I didn't want to use this until the end... but it's too fucked up, so I can't help it. You guys just suffer forever."

And the darkness disappeared.
