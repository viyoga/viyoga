The gangsters held small, red beads in their hands. If they were real beads, they wouldn't have put them in their mouths, so it was definitely a drug.

The appearance of the gangsters who took the drug didn't change. However, a smile flickered across their faces, which had been fearful of the bullets pouring from the turrets. It was combat excitement. They returned fire despite the bullets raining down.

Tang, tang, tang, tang!

It hit the turret accurately. The accuracy was considerable. This also seemed to be a doping effect.

,The turret's counterattack is slow. The accuracy is also low. This is a manually operated type.'

It was already a crude turret with only one gun attached. After taking a few bullets, it couldn't withstand it and exploded with a 'peong'.

It didn't take long for the three turrets to be destroyed. On the other hand, the gangsters suffered no casualties except for the initial three who died while fleeing. All 17 were unharmed. As soon as the turrets were neutralized, they jumped to their feet.

"Hahaha! Did you think you could do anything to us with such crude turrets? Na-baksa! Life is a choice, and you've made the worst one! Don't expect to be treated as guests by us!"

Jin Gang-gu and the other 17 gangsters approached, laughing. Some of them were pointlessly shooting their guns at the wall.

"...How long do I have to keep watching? Weren't you going to prove it?"

Na Chae-young's voice was heard through the speaker. She was trying to hide it as much as possible, but her voice was trembling. She seemed to be shitting her pants.

I fiddled with the hilt of my sword. So-taedo. I didn't like that the blade was curved. My preference was for a straight sword.

,In my current state, it's only once. And that would only last a few seconds. I have to kill them all within that time.'

The gangster bastards spread out as they walked. They would shoot me from the side. If I moved next to the wall, another bastard would shoot me.

About 5 seconds. If I dragged it out for more than 10 seconds, I would lose.

,This is doable.'

I jumped out from behind the pillar and ran towards them. Thanks to the bastards approaching, I reduced the time to close the distance.

"You stupid bastard. I'll show you the greatness of guns! Kill him!"

Tang, tang, tang, tang, tang, tang!

A barrage of bullets poured down as if they had been waiting for it. But none of them touched my body. They either missed or fell to the ground.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

"What the fuck?!"

"Why are the bullets missing?!"

"Die! Just fucking die!"

I slashed the necks of two nearby guys. I couldn't cut them completely. I only cut the carotid arteries. But that alone was fatal. Blood spurted out.

The electromagnetic barrier was still maintained. There was no time to exhale. I moved forward again. A gun was pointed at my forehead, but I trusted the electromagnetic barrier. The bullet grazed my head.

I cut the carotid artery of another neck again.

"Ssibal!!"

The bastard cursed. It seemed he couldn't feel the pain due to the drug's influence.

,Annoying.'

Even though I had killed two people in an instant, no one was backing down. The gangster bastards were spreading out. I kicked the body of the guy whose neck was half-cut. The bastard's body was pushed into the gangsters. They couldn't shoot their comrade, no matter what, so the gangsters' barrage of bullets stopped.

,Now's the time.'

I cut, cut, and cut again. I aimed for the neck as much as possible. The bastard who tried to protect his neck, I stabbed his eyes with the sword. I pushed the cut guys into the gangsters. Because that was the only way to create even a slight opening.

The electromagnetic barrier disappeared.

And some bastards started throwing themselves on the floor. I finished them off with the pistols I had taken from those bastards. Even if I'm not good at shooting, could I really miss an enemy 1m away?

Five left.

,I'm getting tired.'

My legs were getting heavy. The five of them simultaneously pointed their guns at me and pulled the trigger. I grabbed a corpse lying on the floor and used it to shield myself. Bullets hit the corpse. Occasionally, there were penetrating bullets. Fortunately, I wasn't injured, but my neck felt cold.

Cheolkeok, cheolkeok!

"Damn it, the bullets are gone!"

"Me too!"

"You morons! You're supposed to take turns shooting!"

I chuckled. It was fortunate that they were morons. I ran forward and stabbed the gangster's neck with the dulled sword. I shot the other bastards with the stolen pistol.

Cheolkeok, cheolkeok.

The pistol's bullets were also gone. But it was okay. Because out of the 17 enemies, only one bastard was standing unharmed.

When I threw away the pistol, Jin Gang-gu also threw away his pistol.

"You're throwing away your gun? Are you stupid?"

"I'm throwing it away because there are no bullets, you moron."

"...You didn't even bring extra bullets?"

"If I had known there was a bastard like you, I would have brought them in bundles. And you don't have any bullets either. Don't worry, I'll kill you with my fists."

"Can't you see the sword?"

"That blood-soaked sword? After slicing more than 10 people, there's no way the blade is intact. And listen, man. I've beaten more than 10 swordsmen like you to death. Especially a tired swordsman. You're no different from a punching bag."

"......"

My hands and feet trembled. My heart had been pounding like it was about to explode, and it was hard to control my breathing. Was it fear? No. It was simply the limit of my body.

Jin Gang-gu's fist flew towards me.

I can't dodge this.

I can see all the scars on his fist, but I can't avoid it. Regardless of what I see, my body can't keep up.

Soon, his fist reached my face.

Pajijijijik!

I unleashed lightning. The output was weak. But it was more than enough to surpass an electric shocker.

Jin Gang-gu and I fell at the same time. I fell because I was punched, and Jin Gang-gu fainted from the electric shock.

,The legend of 17 to 1 is being written today....'

My nose hurts. It felt like my nose bone was broken. What was flowing out must be blood. He said he was confident in his fists, and it was definitely a bit spicy.

,Fucking hell. That bastard is definitely going to be my punching bag.'

Ttuk, ttuk.

Na Chae-young walked out of the building. In her hand, wearing a white gown, was a pistol.

"Chae-young-ah."

"Just call me Na-baksa."

"There will be a few survivors. Kill them. Ah, don't kill Jin Gang-gu. I need to interrogate him."

"......"

Her body was trembling. The reason was obvious. Even though she made and sold guns, she had never directly killed anyone.

"You're not scared, are you? You'll have to kill a few people later anyway. Well, if you really don't want to get your hands dirty, just wait a little. I promised to be your sword, so I'll take care of it."

"...It's okay. I can do this much."

Three gunshots rang out.

30 seconds later. I slowly raised my upper body. I could move my body, even though it ached here and there. It was probably thanks to the recovery effect. The nose bone that seemed to be broken was already fine. Wounds heal faster than stamina.

,Or did I consume additional stamina while recovering?'

Na Chae-young was sighing as she looked at the scattered corpses.

"Na-baksa. What are you going to do?"

"......About what?"

"The deal. I proved myself. One hour a day. I can use the sharpest sword?"

"There's a condition."

"Tell me."

"...I don't necessarily have to swing the sword once a day. So, I'd like to be able to accumulate the time when I don't use it."

"Accumulation, huh. Not bad. Well, since the deal is done, let's shake hands."

I held out my blood-soaked hand to Na Chae-young. She paused for a moment, then took my hand.

,Is he really a dunce? He's not even a regular person, and he defeated 17 gangsters armed with guns all by himself.'

The sword fight that started from the moment he approached. The trajectory of the sword, swung without hesitation, was even beautiful.

,I knew he wasn't normal. But can he really kill people without hesitation like that...?'

What kind of man is he?

I checked the things placed on the workbench. Some cash, the pistols the gangsters had, and the red drug the gangsters were taking.

There were nearly 30 pills of the drug. They were carrying so many, so it didn't seem like an expensive drug. I picked up a pill and asked Na Chae-young.

"Na-baksa, do you know what this drug is?"

Gulp.

She quickly swallowed a pill.

"It's probably Bister I... You idiot! Why are you swallowing a drug you don't even know what it is?!"

Na Chae-young glared at me and then sighed.

I ate it because I was curious. Seeing the gangsters eating it without hesitation, I knew the side effects weren't that significant. Besides, my ability was recovery. Unless it was a deadly poison, I wouldn't die.

Dugun, dugun.

I could feel my heart beating rapidly. My senses were slightly clearer, and my physical abilities... I wasn't sure. It seemed like they had increased. The doping effect was less than I expected. Disappointing.

"It's not that great."

"Bister I is a basic drug that Bionics can make in the early stages. The ingredient is the blood of a quadrupedal beast. The effect is a slight increase in physical abilities and not being afraid of combat. The pain will also disappear to some extent. The drug's duration is 10 minutes, and if you only take it once every 10 hours, there are no side effects."

"A slight increase in physical abilities? It's hard to feel."

"Bister I is mainly used to remove pain and fear. It's mainly given to soldiers with weak mental fortitude."

"The fact that the gangsters have this drug means."

"...It means Bionics is attached to the gangsters. Even if that's not the case, it's definitely related to Bionics."
