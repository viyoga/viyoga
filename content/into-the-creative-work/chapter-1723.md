[Using Complete Recovery.]

Getting up from the floor, I nervously kicked Kanex's corpse.

I found a magic stone inside the bastard's corpse. This magic stone was proof that he was a monster.

Did he claim to be a god?

,God my ass. Just a monster.

I was about to shake off Kanex's blood and guts from my body and go outside. A black, round jewel on the floor catches my eye. It wasn't there until just now, but appeared as soon as Kanex died?

,Must be a dungeon clear reward.

Since the appraiser is at home, I couldn't immediately know what this was. I took the jewel along with the magic stone and came out of the temple. In the distance, I could see the gate open.

Before leaving the dungeon, I moved my steps to Do Hae-young's house. Kanex's actions might have been a trick to shake me up. I'm going to check on Do Hae-young with that hope.

Result. Do Hae-young was dead.

I sighed and turned my steps. This dungeon will soon become her grave. A grave that can never be found.

Before coming out of the dungeon, I used Illusiter to make my body transparent.

In front of the gate, association employees were panicking.

,It's natural since an open type became a closed type. Originally, they would have reported it to the higher-ups immediately, but if the association investigates properly, their corruption will be exposed, so they can't do anything and are waiting for Berta to solve it.

But Berta was annihilated. The cleared closed-type dungeon will soon disappear.

,Seeing them flustered, they didn't know that the Ihwa Guild was behind Berta. Were they simply bribed by Berta?

An urge to throw a bomb as a gift soared. But I endure. This isn't inside the dungeon, but outside. If I wipe out association employees with a bomb, things will get big.

No matter how much I make my body transparent with Illusiter and hide my identity with a mask, there are plenty of Hunters with extraordinary abilities in the world, so it's good to be careful.

I leisurely slipped out of there.

• • •

I met the dark merchant Park Jung-gu. As soon as Park Jung-gu saw me, he frowned and shouted in anger. This is because I explained the whole situation over the phone while returning here.

,You crazy bastard! You could have just worked in another dungeon! Is 50 billion won a joke?!

,Shut up. I didn't lose out.

I put three fruits containing Vanilla's mana seeds and HB-1 on the table.

,I only used one drop of HB-1.

It was a profit, not a loss. Park Jung-gu's expression softens in an instant. He hesitates before taking the HB-1.

,Yeah. You made a profit. Let's do it one more time. How about it? This time, if we do it in a safer dungeon....

,I'm not doing it.

,What?

,I said I'm not doing it. Too bothersome.

Park Jung-gu's attitude changed completely. He spoke in a cautious tone as if trying to appease me.

,Hey, hey. I know you had a hard time this time. But it's 50 billion won. You've experienced that this plan isn't just a pipe dream, right? A whopping 50 billion! If we split it 50/50, your share is 25 billion!

,If I say I'm not doing it, I'm not doing it. Find someone else.

,Damn it! You're being incredibly firm!

I put a laptop on the table. Park Jung-gu, who was annoyedly drumming on the armrest of the chair, looks at the laptop with shining eyes.

,A military laptop. This is quite an expensive model.... Is this what you got from the dungeon?

,I also took some interesting videos.

It was a video of offering humans to Berta and Kanex and obtaining something called an enhancer. Park Jung-gu chuckled after watching the video.

,Human sacrifice? I never thought I'd see that with my own two eyes....

,The Ihwa Guild hired Berta. There's evidence of that in the laptop data.

,Hoo. And? What do you want me to do?

,Sell the data along with the enhancer to a large guild that opposes the Ihwa Guild.

,Hey, hey. This isn't something you can easily sell just because you want to. What if the Ihwa Guild finds out? My life will be over.

,What the hell is a dark merchant whining about? You should know that this is data that can bring profit.

,Can you compare selling drugs to the risk of being involved with a top 10 guild? If you're going to commission me, pay the commission fee.

,This is a sales agency. Is a commission fee necessary? Take your commission as you see fit.

,Just like you don't do things you don't want to do, I don't do things I don't want to do either.

I clicked my tongue.

It wasn't that I didn't fully understand his attitude.

,I'll give up my share of the mana seed fruits. You can take all the money you get from selling the laptop and the enhancer. Instead, the condition is that the Ihwa Guild must take a significant hit.

Park Jung-gu grins. As if he had been waiting for these words, he takes the laptop and the enhancer.

,Then that changes things. But do you have a grudge against the Ihwa Guild?

,Not really.

,Then why are you trying so hard to screw over the Ihwa Guild?

,I want to see how much the Ihwa Guild shakes.

My goal is the Suwol Guild, which is ranked 2nd among the top 10 guilds. Han Ha-rin-yang died because of those bastards. Even thinking about it again, I can't forgive them. Attacking the Ihwa Guild was also a kind of experiment. An experiment to see how much the top 10 guilds shake when a major scandal breaks out.

,The Ihwa Guild at the bottom of the top 10 guilds and the Suwol Guild, which is ranked 2nd in Korea, have different statures, though.

If the Ihwa Guild shakes, the Suwol Guild will also shake to some extent.

,Looking at you, you also have a bad personality. I'll definitely take care of this matter. There are plenty of large guilds that want to bring down the top 10 guilds... Among them, there are a few large guilds that don't have a good relationship with the Ihwa Guild.

,Are there many large guilds that don't have a good relationship with the top 10 guilds?

,There are many large guilds that can't see the light of day because of the top 10 guilds. And there probably isn't a large guild in Korea right now that hasn't been wronged by the top 10 guilds? Just because the top 10 guilds are famous doesn't mean they're nice.

,There must be large guilds that oppose the Suwol Guild as well. Maybe one of the top 10 guilds opposes the Suwol Guild.

There's a big risk in asking Park Jung-gu directly. I mustn't forget that Park Jung-gu is ultimately a dark merchant who moves according to profit.

I get up from my seat.

,I'm going.

,Oh, okay. Don't come here for the time being until I contact you. I won't be here even if you come.

I checked the news every day for a week without fail.

However, there wasn't a single broadcasting station that covered the Ihwa Guild. The same goes for internet articles. It's quiet.

,Did Park Jung-gu fail? Or didn't he do it?

Human sacrifice.

Moreover, there's a video of human sacrifice to a monster that's been completely recorded. It's a video that can't just be ignored.

,Should I use hacking to spread it on the internet even now? Even if there are claims of manipulation or whatever, it will still damage the Ihwa Guild.

A video with an unknown source lacks credibility.

Even if I try to verify the video, Kanex is dead, and the dungeon has disappeared. If the Ihwa Guild launches a public opinion campaign, I'll have no choice but to be defeated.

,Should I trust Park Jung-gu and wait?

I received a call from Park Jung-gu around evening. Of course, it was a burner phone.

-Yeah, it's me.

,It's already been a week. Even if I look at the news or internet articles, there's nothing related to Berta.

-The deal is done. I sold the data to a large guild that's grinding its teeth at the Ihwa Guild. The large guild immediately took action. They tried to stab the Ihwa Guild in the back by reporting it to the association. The problem was the association. The association buried the matter. Since the large guild couldn't turn the association into an enemy, they received something from the association and backed off.

,The association? Were the association and the Ihwa Guild that close?

-That's not it. According to the informant, the association separately imposed sanctions on the Ihwa Guild. They judged that this incident would damage not only the Ihwa Guild but all of the top 10 guilds, and furthermore, the status of Hunters, so they buried it. The enhancer is also with the association. Under the pretext of investigation.

,I didn't know that the Korea Hunter Association was this corrupt.

-This isn't even corruption. The association just moved to protect itself. Honestly, if this had spread, the entire Hunter world would have been turned upside down. You know why the association is obsessed with the Hunter image, right?

,Because Hunters have power.

From the perspective of ordinary people, Hunters were walking bombs.

Just as a safety pin on a grenade fears the grenade itself, people fear Hunters because they can harm them. Because there are occasionally defective grenades.

,What if I spread that data on the internet?

-You crazy bastard. Are you trying to kill me?!

,It's a hypothetical. If I spread it on the internet... would the Ihwa Guild have shaken?

-It would have ended with some criticism. Because the association would move immediately. And you would have been caught by the association too. Anyway, screwing over the Ihwa Guild failed. I'm not going to return the commission or the commission fee, so give up.

It wasn't a complete loss.

At least I knew that I couldn't do anything to the top 10 guilds in this way. And didn't you say that the Ihwa Guild also received sanctions from the association?

,Park Jung-gu. If it were you, how would you screw over the top 10 guilds?

-Are you crazy? Why would I screw over the top 10 guilds?

,It's a hypothetical. A hypothetical.

-Hoo. It's impossible to screw over all of the top 10 guilds. If all of the top 10 guilds collapse? That means Korea is collapsing. The government and the association wouldn't stand still.

,What if you only screw over one of the top 10 guilds?

-That... um. Well, you'd have to make them a public enemy. This bastard... now that I see it, do you have a grudge against the top 10 guilds? Don't even think about dragging me into that. Well, if there's enough money, I might help.

I don't completely trust Park Jung-gu. There's a possibility that this bastard will hit me in the back of the head at an important moment.

-Anyway, don't contact me for the time being. Because I have to go into hiding for a while. Oh, you should be careful too. There's a rumor that the Ihwa Guild is looking for you. For reference, the guys who were guarding the dungeon gate were all disciplined and fired.

Click.

The call was disconnected.
