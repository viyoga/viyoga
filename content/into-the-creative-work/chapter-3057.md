When I emerged from the secret realm, hundreds of thousands of martial artists were gathered.

On one side, the familiar martial artists of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. On the opposite side, those belonging to the Orthodox Faction. In between stood the martial artists of the Unorthodox Faction. It seemed the Unorthodox artists were serving as a buffer between the Orthodox and Demonic sides.

The one who orchestrated this was not the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, but the Martial Alliance. More precisely, it was Zhuge Yun who led it. As for timing it, they had sent people into the secret realm to roughly estimate when my training would conclude. Mi-ryeong had informed them.

The reason Mi-ryeong cooperated was because she knew I enjoyed this sort of thing.

'Heh heh. Just picturing the hundreds of thousands of martial artists who busted their asses for this ceremony puts me in a good mood. This is what power feels like.'

A ceremony is, by nature, a spectacle with no productivity whatsoever. What does it display? Power.

In Henan, the home base of Shaolin Temple, tens of thousands of Heavenly Demon Divine Cult members had gathered. That alone signified the Martial Alliance's defeat, and even the Martial Alliance's own martial artists were now before me, cupping their fists in salute.

'Zhuge Yun. Not a bad piece of work.'

Iaised my right foot and stepped forward into a true stance.

Thoom.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art — Heavenly Demon Lord's Descent Step.

The qi wave of the Heavenly Demon Lord's Descent Step spread in all directions from me as the center. I had moderated the force, of course. This wasn't meant to kill the martial artists here, but to instill a sense of overwhelming pressure.

"Guhk…."

"This is……."

“…….”

The martial artists silently swallowed their groans. Their shoulders sagged. They felt a pressure clamping down on their windpipes.

The only ones relatively free from this pressure were the absolute masters at the Three Peak Realm and above.

"You've all worked hard to come this far. Today is a special day — the day the Orthodox-Demonic War officially ends. As the host of today's proceedings, I shall throw you a feast. Therefore, by the name of the Heavenly Demon, I command you: while you remain here, forget the grudges and debts of the martial world, and fill your bellies with wine and meat."

China has always valued face, after all. If I didn't display generosity here, I'd be looked down upon.

I flicked my finger toward the sky.

Snap!

Wine and meat of the highest quality from another world rained down from the sky onto pre-prepared locations.

The martial artists gaped at the miraculous sight, eyes wide with admiration.

'This is modern, selectively bred meat and alcohol that can't be obtained in this era no matter how much money you have. Even without spiritual energy infused, the taste alone is enough to inspire awe.'

Wine and meat piled up like mountains. I had emptied out most of the alcohol and meat in my inventory. Even for me, instantly releasing enough to feed hundreds of thousands was quite a drain.

'I haven't even brought out the truly top-grade stuff, so I can just refill from the real world or the Baekhwan World. Honestly, money isn't the issue — restocking is the annoying part.'

Just thinking about the annoying future made my brow furrow, but I couldn't show it in this setting.

I raised my cup as the supreme ruler of the martial world and spoke.

"Drink and enjoy, all of you. I permit it."

• • •

I returned to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult.

Becoming the supreme ruler of the martial world didn't mean I could abandon the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. I had no intention of directly governing it from the start, anyway.

The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult I returned to was visibly wealthy at a glance. Buildings I'd never seen before had sprung up in the city, and the cult's martial artists all wore expensive clothing. The taxes the Orthodox Faction paid to the Emperor — half of that now flowed to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult as well. It was only natural.

'More than half the Empire is managed by martial sects on behalf of the government. And among them, the Orthodox Faction holds the largest share.'

Squeezing the common folk dry to pay the additional taxes? That was obviously impossible. Not because of the Orthodox Faction's principles.

'The Emperor is watching with both eyes wide open. The moment rumors spread that commoners are suffering under tax burdens, the Embroidered Guard will move in and begin investigations and inspections. Get caught, and you're just fucked.'

Wouldn't paying an additional half in taxes be burdensome? Of course it would. But not to an unmanageable degree.

'A sect holds considerable power as long as it has tax collection rights in its region. Want to do business in that area? You bribe the sect. Being Orthodox doesn't change that.'

I looked at the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult's warehouses and smirked.

The original five warehouses were packed full, and even the five additional warehouses built on top of that were completely filled.

"Right Protector."

"Yes, Cult Leader."

Sword Demon Yeon Seo-rang answered respectfully.

"Looking at this, we have too much wealth. Offer three-tenths of it to the Imperial household. If we just sit on this, problems will definitely arise. The fact that no issues came up while I was gone is frankly a relief."

"In fact, several high officials did submit memorials to His Majesty regarding this matter."

"How did the Emperor respond?"

"He rejected the memorials. Rumors are circulating that His Majesty still favors you, Cult Leader."

"The Emperor could have easily suppressed those rumors if he wanted to. The fact that he didn't means… he's trying to keep me indebted to him. After all, the Emperor is the one who wants something."

"…You mean His Majesty wants something from you?"

Yeon Seo-rang asked back, not understanding.

The Emperor was the strongest under heaven, the greatest of all time. A superhuman who had ruled this Empire for hundreds of years. If the Emperor wanted something, he could have anything. That such an Emperor would want something was itself incomprehensible.

The Emperor desired the power of the Heavenly Slaughter. I possessed the power of the Heavenly Slaughter. The Emperor was oiling the blade called me.

"Something like that."

"Understood. Shall the remaining five-tenths be stockpiled as is?"

"Money is meant to be spent. The remaining five-tenths, I'll use for myself. Any complaints?"

"Of course not. The Heavenly Demon Divine Cult itself belongs to you, Cult Leader. And with just the two-tenths you've graciously permitted us, we far exceed the cult's average budget."

As expected, the Right Protector knew her place well.

"The remaining five-tenths will be used to procure Heavenly Jade. Pay above market price if you have to."

"Heavenly Jade is currently more valuable than it has ever been. There are even rumors that the Imperial household is focusing on acquiring as much as they can."

The Imperial household acquiring Heavenly Jade was probably because of me too.

"I know. Procure as much as possible anyway. And send discreet word to the Orthodox Faction as well. Tell them that offering Heavenly Jade will earn them a significant tax reduction."

"Ah. And among the orthodox sects, specially treat any that send beauties."

"Understood."

"Keh keh."

Now the underlings would be the ones toiling.

Massive sums of money flowed in on a regular basis, and with that money he moved the underlings. He simply reaped power and rewards as if running an idle game on autopilot.

"Cult Leader. What would you like done about the Uicheon Society?"

"Ah. Those bastards?"

The Uicheon Society.

An organization of martial artists who had been expelled from their sects. Mostly young warriors who had banded together with the goal of overthrowing the Heavenly Demon Cult.

Though called an organization, it was a cell-based structure, and intelligence suggested there were no absolute masters of the Three Realms or above among them.

'A kind of independence movement, or resistance, I suppose.'

It was familiar to him.

Every time he had ruled as a dictator, these types always cropped up.

"They're vermin. Kill them on sight. That said, no need to pay them much mind. What they're capable of is limited."

"Understood. Shall I apply sanctions to the sects that expelled them?"

"Insolent as they are, the moment they were expelled, they became strangers. Leave them be. And in my experience, trying to control every last one of them tends to backfire more often than not. Better to give them some breathing room. In time, they'll get used to it anyway."

• • •

The Emperor gazed down at the go board. The world—the Jin Empire—was laid out in this Qin Unification Go Board. Here and there across the board, lights glowed. Secret realms had appeared within the territory of the Jin Empire.

The Emperor moved a black stone, and the imperial army would move to conquer a secret realm.

'The entrance to the Henan secret realm has collapsed.'

The Emperor surveyed the world with both eyes, replaying recent events in his mind.

Normally, when a secret realm vanished, it vanished quietly. There was only one case where its entrance collapsed: the realm's administrator had died, and the secret realm itself had crumbled.

"Cheonsal killed the administrator."

And he had killed them thoroughly.

If he hadn't, the Immortal Realm would have intervened. Those who heeded the Immortal Realm's voice would not have sat idle.

"In all likelihood, he encountered one. And yet he killed the administrator regardless… That settles it."

The corner of the Emperor's mouth curled up quietly, then immediately fell. It was proven that Cheonsalseong could completely kill an immortal.

But his power was still too weak to contend with the Immortal Realm. He needed to ascend to the realm of Mansang. That was the minimum requirement.

"Your Majesty."

A eunuch called to him. The Emperor answered without even glancing at the eunuch prostrated below.

"What is it?"

"High officials, led by the Left Inspector, have submitted a memorial."

"Its contents?"

"It states that Thunder General Seong Yujin, who has been seizing martial sect assets, must be punished."

"A matter I have already dismissed. Did the Left Inspector find my words amusing? I hereby order the Left Inspector to one year of house arrest."

"I shall relay the imperial decree."

The eunuch withdrew.

The Emperor, who had been staring at the go board, raised his head. Within the imperial crown, his pupils glowed gold.

After gazing into empty space for a long while, the Emperor finally let out a small sigh.

"Still far from enough."
