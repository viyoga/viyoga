After that, I ran out of the lab with Na Chaeyoung.

,"I did something."

Too lazy to explain. And I was incredibly tired.

Keulkeul.

,"Just wait a bit. It's much better than last time."

,"......"

Jeong Hyeonsu, the head of the Korean War Weapons Research Institute, was trembling under the control room desk with his eyes closed. He was the only one who survived in the control room. The rest of the researchers had their brains eaten and died by the Brain Devourer.

He was lucky. As soon as the Brain Devourer came in, he pushed the researchers aside, went under the desk, held his breath, and hid. He closed his eyes and prayed to God, whom he didn't usually believe in, to save him. His prayer worked. The Brain Devourer didn't see Jeong Hyeonsu and went upstairs.

The surviving Jeong Hyeonsu didn't move from under the desk. The moment he went out from under the desk, the Brain Devourer would grab his body with its short tentacles, open his skull, and eat his brain. Trembling with fear, he waited for someone to rescue him.

How many hours had passed?

Bbeep!

The control room door opened.

Someone had opened the door, not the broken iron wall. Even though there was no need to come in through the door.

Ttokakttokak.

Several sounds of hard shoes were heard. A sound that the footless Brain Devourer could never make.

Only then did Jeong Hyeonsu crawl out from under the desk. With the most pitiful expression he could muster.

,"Heueo... C-can I have some water...?"

They were men in black suits. At the center was a black-haired beauty. Around her, the men in suits were moving around inside the control room. They weren't so much cleaning up the situation as they were looking for something.

Suits.

In the 21st century, it was the clothing worn by most members of society. But their atmosphere was different from ordinary people. They exuded an atmosphere that made you tense just by looking at them. Gangsters? No. A more dangerous atmosphere than that.

,"A-are you from the military?"

,"No. We're from the National Intelligence Service."

The black-haired beauty in a suit said. Her voice was as lovely as her innocent face. But Jeong Hyeonsu felt fear.

National Intelligence Service. Commonly known as Angibu.

The notoriety that those caught by the Angibu could never get out was widespread even among ordinary citizens. The people protested to abolish the Angibu several times, but the protests disappeared without a trace, and the Angibu has been maintained to this day.

The Angibu had been moving quietly for the past 20 years. They completely erased their presence in the public eye and only operated in the shadows. But Jeong Hyeonsu, being a powerful figure, knew. The Angibu hadn't changed.

And Jeong Hyeonsu had also heard rumors about the woman in front of him. From her skills to her background, she lacked nothing. The blood of the Korean Empire's royal family flowed in her veins.

,"Angibu Deputy Director Lee Yeonhee...! Why, why are you here?!"

,"The Ministry of National Defense has given up on this research institute. They judged that it had no value since the Brain Devourer was dead. We were put in charge of the cleanup. Since Awakened Ones and monsters are involved, I, as the Deputy Director, came out."

Jeong Hyeonsu felt his head turn white. If he made a mistake, he might be dragged to the Angibu. And there, he might be subjected to all sorts of harsh treatment, including electric torture and water torture.

,"T-there was an intruder! A Player, and an Esper class at that! He stirred up the research institute and released the Brain Devourer! His goal was the combat AI! He lured the Brain Devourer to the control room and then ran away! I was hiding here! That's all there is to this! Ah, is the Brain Devourer's corpse still there? I was the one who led the research on the Brain Devourer. If you let me study the corpse, I'll show you meaningful results! The Brain Devourer's corpse will definitely be helpful to the nation!!"

,"Have you said everything?"

Lee Yeonhee asked with a smile. That smile, which seemed completely out of place with the surrounding situation, was alien. Jeong Hyeonsu's shoulders trembled. He knelt on the floor and lowered his head.

,"P-please save me..."

,"I have unfortunate news for you, Director. The Brain Devourer's corpse is gone. We did find its remains in the B1 power room... neatly cut in half and burned black. It's probably the Brain Devourer's heart."

,"...Cut in half?"

,"Oh dear. You didn't know because you were hiding under the desk. The research institute is cut in half. From the ground to the fifth basement floor. Very neatly cut in half. So neatly that the building doesn't even show signs of collapsing."

,"......"

,"Now, tell me in detail what happened at this research institute from the beginning. There's not enough information to grasp the situation from the action cams of the soldiers we found on the ground."

,"I'll tell you! I'll tell you everything!"

Jeong Hyeonsu told Lee Yeonhee everything he knew. Lee Yeonhee listened to his words and nodded quietly. About 20 minutes passed. One of the suit-wearers approached Lee Yeonhee and handed her a tablet.

,"Deputy Director. These are the camera videos. We've temporarily pieced together the videos chronologically based on the intruder."

Lee Yeonhee received the tablet.

,"The video is unnatural. Was it hacked?"

,"Yes. It's presumed he was nearby. He manipulated the cameras, but there's no manipulation from a certain point on."

,"By the time the control room realized it, he had already entered the lab with the combat AI on the 4th floor. Hmm. Did anything come up that could identify him?"

,"We can't confirm it with the camera. As you can see, the intruder covered everything from head to toe."

,"What about DNA?"

,"It's impossible to isolate the intruder's DNA alone. There are too many corpses everywhere."

Lee Yeonhee focused on the tablet.

The intruder acted normally even with the Brain Devourer in front of him. In front of that Brain Devourer that emitted mental waves that drove people crazy.

,"He uses a sword."

,"I've already ordered an investigation, but it won't mean anything. It's a sword that's fine even after killing several people. In other words, it's presumed to be a weapon made by a Technologist."

,"There's a possibility that the collaborator is a Technologist. Hmm. I can't find him right away. Certainly, looking at the video, there's a high probability that he's an Esper."

An Esper and a Technologist joined hands? It wasn't impossible.

The intruder lured the Brain Devourer to the control room and went up to the ground. What happened next was incomprehensible. The intruder didn't run away from the research institute but chose to fight the Brain Devourer.

,'Why?'

Lee Yeonhee asked herself in her heart, seeking an answer.

,'Did he suddenly want to fight the Brain Devourer? Because of the Brain Devourer's mental waves? Did he realize the danger of the Brain Devourer?'

The intruder fought the Brain Devourer. He rushed in bravely and was in a life-threatening situation. Then he dragged the Brain Devourer into the power room. The video ended just as he was about to do something using the electricity from the generator.

,"The camera broke down due to the electricity."

,"Yes. After that, there's a video of the intruder leaving the research institute. He's probably out of Paju by now."

,"The characteristics are electric ability. And a sword. Hmmm. He used his power openly. It doesn't seem like he was trying to hide it thoroughly. I have a feeling I'll be meeting him soon."

,"...It seems you like the intruder."

,"Yes. The Brain Devourer was too dangerous, so I was going to kill it... but he killed the Brain Devourer for me instead. I like him. How can I bring him to the Angibu?"

,"I'll pretend I didn't hear that."

,"How boring. Ah, there's someone else who heard it."

Lee Yeonhee's gaze turned to Jeong Hyeonsu. Jeong Hyeonsu said with a pale complexion.

,"I, I'll pretend I didn't hear it either!"

,"......"

,"Please save me! I'm helpful to the country! I really am! I know a lot too! Don't you need the research institute's data? I'll give you everything!"

,"We already have that. We knew that Players would target the research institute, so do you think we wouldn't have taken any measures? Combat AI data, Brain Devourer research and experiment data, next-generation weapon research data... we have all of that."

,"Y-you planted someone in the research institute...?"

,"Yes."

The suit-wearer next to Lee Yeonhee sighed. Lee Yeonhee was startled by that and opened her mouth.

,"Ah! This is also classified... I said it without thinking. Well, it can't be helped. There's no choice but to clean up. Director Jeong Hyeonsu. It's for the sake of the country. It's for the sake of the country. Please understand."

,"S-save..."

,"Take him away and see if he missed anything. Don't forget to cross-validate the data."

Following Lee Yeonhee's orders, two men in black suits approached, subdued Jeong Hyeonsu, and took him away. The number of survivors at the research institute will be zero.

,"Deputy Director. What should we do about the intruder? If you order it, I'll try to track him down somehow. If we check all the surrounding cameras, black boxes, etc., we might be able to catch his tail."

,"No, there's no time for that. That day is coming soon. I have a lot to do. I heard the gibbon escaped from the research institute?"

,"It may have already left this place."

,"They're gathered 600m east. A total of 8. Cooperate with the military unit and take care of them. Divide the corpses in half with the military."

,"Is it okay to give it to the military? The military is a risk factor."

,"We and the military aren't enemies, we're fellow public officials. We need to maintain a good relationship."

,"Understood."
