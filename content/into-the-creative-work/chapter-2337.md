I arrived near the Cult village Iste told me about.

There was no barrier. A large-scale barrier that could surround a village isn't easy to install unless you're an expert, and even if you do install one, there's a high chance it'll get caught by the Hero Association. Even if it's private land, you need government permission to install a barrier.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Waves.

Before entering the village, I checked the situation inside with electromagnetic waves.

,135 people. Considering the size of the village, it looks like only about 50 people would live here, but the population is more than double.'

More than half of them are staying at the village hall. It's the biggest building in the village, but it's not big enough for 70 people to stay and live in. There was another space underground. That's where the religious people were living and training.

,These bastards are the real deal, even among Cults. I can't even call them a Cult.'

Usually, Cults rip off their followers for money. But the Heavenly Salvation Society wasn't like that. They force their followers to pray, not give money. Because they sincerely worship the Angel God.

In a way, they were as dangerous as the Gumokgyo.

,The reason they've been quiet until now must be because their power is weak.'

If they had the power of Gumokgyo, these bastards would surely be running wild too.

"Moka."

The Thunder Owl Moka appeared next to me.

"Kkuuuk."

Moka was still an Advanced-level spirit. I've been steadily feeding her Vitality Orbs to grow her, so she's close to being a Supreme-level spirit, but she hasn't broken through the wall yet.

I asked Moka, shining my golden eyes.

"You feel it too, right?"

"Kkuk!"

She's a lightning spirit. There's no way she couldn't use electromagnetic waves like a radar like I do.

"Keep watch here and wait. If anyone tries to escape out of the village, kill them."

I was planning to annihilate all 135 of them.

Being similar to Gumokgyo meant they also offered sacrifices to their god. Since they're sacrificing people, my Karma won't drop even if I kill them.

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and entered the village with the blade resting on my shoulder.

It was that moment. The quiet village suddenly became bustling. It was like stirring up a beehive that was just sitting still.

,They noticed I infiltrated the village. It doesn't seem like a barrier... Did they prepare something?'

The villagers moved all at once. Not towards me, the intruder, but towards the village hall.

It seems like they're going to gather in one place to greet me. That's even better. It's easier to kill them if they're gathered in one place.

,Electromagnetic waves are blocked.'

I couldn't tell what they were doing at the village hall. They either activated a barrier or were interfering with detection in some other way.

,They say strike first to gain the upper hand. Even if that wasn't the case, it's better to strike first than to get hit first.'

I focused my mind and activated my skill.

Above the village hall, a giant sword appeared in the night sky. The tip of the sword, over 50m long, was pointing directly at the village hall.

Pajijik.

The meteor sword wrapped in lightning fell to the ground with a heavy thud.

Golden wings flashed on the roof of the village hall. It was easy to see because it was the night sky. The golden wings, which felt even sacred, wrapped around the roof and protected it.

The giant meteor sword failed to penetrate the wings and bounced off to the side. I commanded the meteor sword in my mind.

,Explode.'

Kwaaaaaang!

The meteor sword exploded, scattering fragments and lightning everywhere. But the golden wings defended against all of it.

,It seems like things are going to be a little more difficult than I thought.'

Still, I didn't have a feeling that I would lose. There's no way a guy hiding in a place like this would be stronger than an S-class hero.

The door of the village hall opened and people poured out. Most of them were ordinary people.

What caught my eye was the man in the center of them. His presence was extraordinary compared to the others. The clothes he was wearing were also as fancy as a pope's, a white priestly robe. His face was so fierce that the words soldier or villain came to mind before clergyman. He was holding a thick book in his left hand.

His appearance was as I heard from Iste. He is Ham Seong-gu, the leader of the Heavenly Salvation Society.

"With what grudge did you come to the Heavenly Salvation Society and cause such a ruckus?"

"I need the sacred object you have. Hand it over."

"Is that the will of Gumokgyo?"

Ham Seong-gu said, looking straight into my eyes. Indeed. He's running a religion, so he seems like a smart guy.

,Shall I see what kind of abilities he has?'

『Name: Ham Seong-gu

Strength: C Stamina: B- Agility: D+ Durability: C+ Mana: C

Trait: Heavenly Gospel (SS).

Skill: Ritual (S)

Trust: 0』

『Heavenly Gospel (SS)

Trait Rank: SS

Receives the call of God.

Worships God.』

His stats aren't anything special. He's below a C-rank hero. The ranks of his trait and skill are high, but they don't seem to be directly helpful in combat.

,No. Is the trait a little different? It's SS rank. The power of that trait might be what blocked the meteor sword.'

There was no reason to let my guard down.

Lightning Sky Style Myriad Lightning.

I pointed the blade of Hwaryeon Bido at him. Red lightning gathered at the tip of the blade and rotated in the shape of a swastika. Then, red lightning was fired at him.

Golden wings appeared on Ham Seong-gu's forearm and blocked the red lightning.

"Our Heavenly Salvation Society has yielded much to Gumokgyo. All for the sake of peace. Why are you trying to break the tacit peace now?"

"What tacit peace? You were just suppressed because you were weaker than Gumokgyo. I only have one thing to say. Hand over the sacred object. Then I'll spare your lives."

I stared intently at the book Ham Seong-gu was holding in his left hand. That book, which looked like a Bible, was the sacred object. I could see an alien power, different from mana, flowing out of that book. Ham Seong-gu was accepting that power without any problem.

"The Angel God whispered to me. You are lying right now."

"You're not completely without divine power."

Even if Ham Seong-gu handed over the sacred object to me, I had no intention of letting these bastards live.

I swung my sword at him. Red lightning curved like a whip and rushed towards him.

Lightning Sky Style Ultimate Technique Exploding Quake Lightning.

Red thunder caused an explosion. It was an explosion with the power to turn ordinary humans to dust, but Ham Seong-gu blocked it with his golden wings again.

Thud. Thud. Thud.

Three followers around Ham Seong-gu fell one after another. My eyes, which could see spirits, saw their souls being sucked into Ham Seong-gu's book.

"Are you gaining power by sacrificing your followers?"

"It's not a sacrifice, it's salvation. They all knelt in the face of miserable reality and despaired, so they turned to the Angel God. The Angel God will embrace their souls with wings of light."

I was going to point out his bullshit, but I stopped. There was nothing to be gained by arguing with him. A fanatic is a fanatic for a reason. These bastards are already out of their minds. Their eyes aren't normal.

Ham Seong-gu held the book out in front of him. The book fluttered and opened on its own, even though there was no wind.

I read the words written on the cover of the book.

"Heavenly Ritual Book?"

"Hoo. You can read the Ritual Book? As expected of the ninth apostle of Gumokgyo. You have outstanding talent."

"...How did you know I was the ninth apostle?"

There's no way the information could have leaked. The fanatics of Gumokgyo are crazy bastards. They won't reveal my identity even if they're tortured to death.

"God told me. He also knows that you have the Scales. He didn't tell me what those Scales are, though...."

Scales.

A word that's only mentioned about 2-3 times when you look it up in the original work. According to a user's interpretation on the internet, the Scales refer to the player. To be exact, it's the power to see the status window, which can be said to be the player's ability, and the power to confirm good and evil Karma.

"Your god seems to be a pretty high-ranking god."

"Oh.... The Angel God gave me a revelation. He told me to capture you alive and offer you as a sacrifice."

"That's not going to happen, even if I die and come back to life."

"Hahaha! Everything is according to God's will!"

Ham Seong-gu's body glowed like a light bulb. The surroundings became as bright as day. It was fortunate that this was a small village on the outskirts. Otherwise, the heroes would have been dispatched a long time ago.

The followers of the Heavenly Salvation Society, who had been standing still, suddenly opened their mouths all at once.

"Ah. Aaaah. Aaaaaaaa~!"

It was a hymn.

The more the sacred hymn spread, the stronger the light became. Thud. Thud. Ham Seong-gu sacrificed his followers and received power from the Angel God. More than 20 golden wings sprouted from all over his body.

"Great Angel God! Please look upon us! Have mercy on us and grant us salvation! Lead us to the high heavens!!"

Thud, thud, thud!

All the followers who were singing the hymn died. The shining Ham Seong-gu flapped more than 30 golden wings and flew into the sky. The light that came from him became feathers and scattered in all directions.

"I will ask you. Will you abandon the god with nine eyes and turn to the Angel God?"

"Is the Angel God stronger than the god of Gumokgyo?"

"The Angel God is also one of the Outer Gods. Strength is meaningless to the Outer Gods. What's important is that the Angel God looks upon us. The god with nine eyes can see many things, but humans are not in his field of vision. The Angel God says. That god is just watching from the outside."

"You're just saying in a long way that he's weaker than the god with nine eyes."

"...Blasphemy. How blasphemous!"

Feathers of light rushed towards me from all directions.

Lightning Sky Style Flying Thunder God.

I soared into the sky. Using the Storm God (A) trait, I wrapped my body in wind. I could fly in the sky without wings. I avoided all the feathers.

,Terra.'

I summoned Terra, the Supreme-level water spirit. Swirling water appeared next to me. The water whispered to me.

-Did you call? I want to eat Spirit Orbs. What should I do?

,Make it rain.'

Clouds were created in the sky. The essence of clouds is water vapor. The Supreme-level water spirit could create clouds as much as she wanted if she used her power. The clouds soon became dark clouds and began to pour down.

Pajijik.

The lightning generated in my left hand soared upwards and entered the dark clouds.

Lightning Sky Style Thunder.

Kwarreung! Lightning flashed in the dark clouds. Thunder rumbled from all directions.

"Rain and lightning.... It's certainly a threatening combination. But nature alone cannot stop the Angel God's wings."

Ham Seong-gu spread all his wings wide. Golden light stretched out in all directions and pushed the dark clouds away in an instant. The dark night sky became as bright as day. Ham Seong-gu was the sun.

"Worship the light."

A pillar of light fell from the sky and pressed down on my body, pushing me to the ground. I couldn't avoid it and was slammed into the ground.

CheomBeong!

Terra became a water droplet cushion and caught my body. If it wasn't for Terra, I would have been seriously injured at the very least.

-Are you okay? I don't like that human. Should I kill him?

"You don't need to step in. Moka, finish him off."

Moka, who had gathered enough lightning from the scattered dark clouds, burst out with a thunderous roar. Moka, who had become lightning itself, collided with Ham Seong-gu. Ham Seong-gu's wings burned in the lightning and fell to the ground.

I got up and walked to where Ham Seong-gu had fallen. Surprisingly, Ham Seong-gu wasn't dead. His wings were broken, but they hadn't disappeared and remained.

"You're still alive after being hit by a body blow with over ten thousand compressed lightning bolts? Your power is indeed great. But I wouldn't have taken that hit if I were you."

Ham Seong-gu was clumsy in combat compared to the power he possessed. I understand since he probably can't use the power he gets from sacrificing his followers often.

"The, the Angel God will save me...!"

"It's not salvation, it's being eaten. There's no such thing as heaven in this world."

Seogeok.

I cut off Ham Seong-gu's head. Ham Seong-gu's soul was sucked into the Heavenly Ritual Book as if it were natural.
