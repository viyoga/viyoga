"Nice. It's way better than my real body."

Seong Yujin smiled, looking at the ghosts surrounding him.

Before Seong Yujin regained control of the body, he had some anxiety. It was about the possibility of not being able to use the ghost energy as he wished.

But ghost energy was just energy. Rather, since the ghost energy moved and reacted according to his will, in a way, it could be said to be more comfortable than mana.

Although it was a bit inconvenient not having the characteristics of the Lightning Sky Style, he had the knowledge and senses of the Lightning Sky Style, so he could use the ghost energy to use the Lightning Sky Style.

"Kkieeeeek!"

A bird-like ghost screamed and rushed at Seong Yujin. Seong Yujin was relaxed. His energy spread, and his senses expanded, allowing him to feel the presence of all the ghosts here. Even though he could feel them, there was no reason to fear the ghosts.

"Watch closely. This is what a proper sword strike is."

He showed off to the Rakshasa demon Dojin, who was watching from inside him, and moved his arm.

Lightning Sky Style Lightning Flash.

The moment the blue lightning flashed, the ghost was already split in half and dead.

-...No way. A human can handle ghost energy this easily?! Could it be, you... Were you a ghost, not a human?!

"Heh heh. You dumb bastard. Do I look like a ghost to you? You're stuck to my body, you should know without me telling you. I'm not a ghost, I'm a human."

Even as he spoke, Seong Yujin steadily swung his sword. With minimal movement, he used the perfectly controlled ghost energy to massacre the ghosts.

It was different from Dojin, who fought crudely using brute force. Seong Yujin's sword was clean. So clean that it felt like art.

-...Impressive. I acknowledge your swordsmanship. I can't help but acknowledge it after seeing this spectacle.

Had he cut down 30 ghosts with the Ghost Subduing Sword? The ghosts, who had been rushing in without hesitation, hesitated. They looked at each other. They even retreated when Seong Yujin approached.

"You're scared even though you're ghosts. Just rush me, don't be annoying. You're all going to die by my hand anyway."

"Uwaaaaa! Don't get cocky!!"

A bald Kappa ghost rushed in. Among the average ghosts, he had particularly strong ghost energy.

Seong Yujin narrowed his eyes and concentrated. Then, he stepped forward and slashed past the Kappa.

Shadow Sky Style Azure Stream.

The Kappa fell, his eyes wide, seemingly not even knowing how he had been cut.

"It's been a while, but it's going well."

-What was that movement just now...? It was like a ghost's movement.

"Like a ghost, huh...? It's funny that you, a ghost, are saying that."

Seong Yujin deliberately grabbed the Kappa, who was only critically injured and not killed, by the neck and lifted him up.

"Dojin. How can I absorb ghost energy...?"

-Is a mere human trying to imitate my Ghost Eating?

"I'm doing it because it seems like you're using ghost energy without thinking, so it's going to be insufficient."

-Give it up. You are human. Fight like a human.

"Do I have to eat it directly? I'm a bit of a gourmet, so I don't like that. The Ghost Eating you're talking about is close to an ability, so..."

Seong Yujin recalled the memories of the Guangmyeong Ascension Art.

Among the martial arts he had learned, there were also martial arts that absorbed the opponent's energy. The Demonic martial art Dark Annihilation was one, and so was the Star Sucking Art.

'I can't use the Demonic martial art. To be exact, I could use it, but... there's a problem with the aftermath if I use it recklessly. I don't have Absolute Spirit right now.'

All that was left was the Star Sucking Art. Seong Yujin moved the ghost energy according to the incantation of the Star Sucking Art.

He succeeded.

The Kappa's ghost energy was absorbed. The Kappa, who had his ghost energy taken away, became shriveled like a mummy.

Seong Yujin was surprised. It was because he didn't expect to succeed on his first try.

'...There's no way I'm this talented.... Ah, is that it? Was the original body in this world talented? He said it wasn't an ordinary family, so... there's a high possibility it's due to bloodline.'

Good things were good. I smiled.

-You little bug...! What do you think you're doing, crawling in here!

"Tsk. Why are you suddenly shouting and causing a fuss? Do you want to die?"

-Don't you even know that the Kappa's vengeful spirit has crawled in? The Ghost Eating you performed isn't perfect. If you had used Ghost Eating perfectly, the ghost's vengeful spirit wouldn't have been able to invade your mind.

"Ah, really? You're surprisingly useful. You take care of all the ghosts that enter my mind."

-This bastard... He's using me like a subordinate. ...Well, fine. Go wild as you please. Your swordsmanship, your fighting style. I'll watch it all.

"You little shit. You're openly trying to exploit me."

Seong Yujin chuckled and repeatedly grabbed and absorbed the ghost energy of lesser ghosts. Meanwhile, Nurarihyon, the master of the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons, looked down at Seong Yujin and observed him.

"A human has taken over the body.... But what is that? Why does he seem stronger than when the Rakshasa demon took over the body...? Moreover, he handles ghost energy better than most ghosts, it's absurd."

"Boss. Are you going to keep watching? The lesser ghosts are dying."

Daitengu said.

Nurarihyon, who was interested in the way Seong Yujin used ghost energy, looked around. The ghosts wanted to kill Seong Yujin immediately. Nurarihyon wanted to observe a little more, but he decided to accept the ghosts' opinions. Nurarihyon nodded in agreement.

The first to step forward was Daitengu.

He flew like a gale and tried to smash Seong Yujin's head with his foot in an instant.

Lightning Sky Style Gale Lightning.

In an instant, Seong Yujin's body disappeared. It was the moment Daitengu, flustered, stopped his body. Daitengu felt his vision spinning. In the spinning world, he could see his own body with its head gone.

'...I, of all people, had my head cut off?! And I realized it so late...!'

Seong Yujin absorbed Daitengu's ghost energy. Daitengu felt his existence being sucked into Seong Yujin.

'I won't give up like this! I'll become a curse and gnaw at your mind!'

Daitengu's vengeful spirit entered Seong Yujin's mind. And what he met was Dojin, holding a sword.

"Nice to meet you, long-nose. Welcome to my territory. I was getting tired of playing with this baldy anyway."

"Kkiaaaaaaak!"

The Kappa, with a sword stuck in his heart, screamed horribly and disappeared.

"Rakshasa demon...!"

"I have never lost to anyone in a 1v1 fight. Moreover, this is my territory. As long as I'm here, my power will only get stronger. It'll be different from before."

"I will kill you and destroy that human's mind!"

"You dumb bastard. If that were possible, would I be here like this right now?"

While Dojin and Daitengu clashed in the mental world, Seong Yujin was swinging the Ghost Subduing Sword with an excited heart.

His body was light.

His hands and feet moved as he wished.

The Ghost Subduing Sword was as amazing as the Hwaryeon Bido.

Moreover, ghost energy, which could be called energy, was abundant. If he felt he was lacking ghost energy, he could just catch a ghost and absorb it.

With this overwhelming power, he massacred all the ghosts that rushed at him as if he were playing a musou game.

"This is fucking fun! All the stress I've been accumulating is gone!"

"Don't get cocky, human!"

Inugami rushed in. Wielding sharp claws, he tried to tear Seong Yujin's body apart.

Lightning Sky Style Lightning Adamantine Blade.

The lightning blade flashed, and Inugami's claws broke. The lightning blade didn't stop there and cut through Inugami's body.

"No way...! My claws, which are harder than iron...!"

"Ah, this is sword energy. It can even cut through dragon scales."

Kwaaaang!

From above, a giant bone hand pressed down on Seong Yujin. Seong Yujin moved to the side just before the bone hand touched his body. It was possible to evade because he had grasped the presence of Gashadokuro in advance through his energy sense. Immediately after, he stepped on Gashadokuro's arm and soared upwards. He plunged the Ghost Subduing Sword into the center of Gashadokuro's skull.

"Heh heh... How foolish.... You can't cut my head with that mere sword...."

"It's harder than that dog's claws. Then what about the inside?"

Pajijijijik.

Lightning entered the skull through the Ghost Subduing Sword. Seong Yujin felt the empty skull filling with lightning.

"It's useless.... I am not a... weak creature like you...!"

Seong Yujin pulled the sword out of Gashadokuro's head and fell straight to the ground.

"That's enough. Just die already."

"What?"

Lightning Sky Style Ultimate Technique Exploding Quake Lightning.

The lightning that had filled Gashadokuro's skull exploded all at once. Gashadokuro's skull shattered into pieces and scattered in all directions like shrapnel.

Seong Yujin, who had landed on the ground, licked his lips and looked up at the sky. Hundreds of ghosts floating in the dark sky flinched all at once.

"Daitengu-nim and Inugami-nim were defeated, and now even Gashadokuro-nim...!"

"The prophecy was right! That human will be the calamity of us ghosts!"

"The Rakshasa demon Dojin is the worst traitor!"

"We must kill him here! For the sake of the ghosts!"

"A mere human!"

The ghosts didn't hide their fear towards Seong Yujin and rushed at Seong Yujin as if screaming.

Lightning Sky Style Myriad Lightning.

Streaks of lightning stretched out from Seong Yujin. The streaks of lightning rotated like a spinning top and roamed in all directions. Hundreds of ghosts swept away by the lightning were electrocuted and annihilated. A few ghosts survived, but they were cut down by the sword energy Seong Yujin unleashed and died.

"Heh heh. Nice."

Ghost energy overflowed in all directions. Seong Yujin slowly absorbed the ghost energy using the Star Sucking Art and looked up at Nurarihyon. The lightning around Seong Yujin didn't disappear and remained in the space.

"Now you're the only one left. To think you wouldn't move until your subordinates were annihilated. Was I that scary?"

"Don't be mistaken, I was just learning from you. The way you use ghost energy... It's very efficient. Thanks to you, I learned something good. I can just create the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons again."

Nurarihyon raised his index finger and pointed at me. A streak of blue lightning shot out and was fired at me. Surprised, I quickly dodged to the side.

"This bastard.... Did he imitate the Lightning Sky Style just by seeing it?"

"Is this what you call the Lightning Sky Style? I don't like it. I'll kill you and give it another name."

"There's no next time for you. You're going to die here. The preparations are already complete."

"The preparations are complete...? Hmm?"

Nurarihyon looked up at the sky. The ghost energy that had been pooling in the space instantly transformed into lightning energy and reacted in the sky. Kwarurururururung! An ear-splitting thunderous sound rang out.

"...Is the lightning resonating with each other?"

"Lightning's Cry. No, unlike Lightning's Cry, I'm not controlling the lightning, so... I should call it something different. I've decided. This is the Lightning Sky Style's new ultimate technique."

Kwajijijijijijik!

With a roar that seemed to distort space, a giant lightning bolt appeared in the center of the sky.

Nurarihyon sensed that it was too late to escape. He swallowed a sigh and muttered.

"Unbelievable.... How can a mere human possess such power...."

Lightning Sky Style Ultimate Technique Lightning Sky Decree.

A giant lightning bolt fell from the sky.

It was a waterfall, and it was a tsunami.

The moment it struck the ground, it swallowed and swept away everything.

Seong Yujin opened his closed eyes. He had protected his body as much as possible with aura barrier, so he was able to withstand the Lightning Sky Decree without much damage.

There was nothing in front of him.

The place where the mountain had been was now a desolate plain. The ground was black. It was because the ground had been scorched by the lightning. Literally everything had been swept away by the lightning tsunami.

Seong Yujin walked forward.

Surprisingly, Nurarihyon was alive. Only his head remained. Even then, half of his head had melted away.

Seong Yujin gripped the Ghost Subduing Sword in reverse and raised it upwards to finish him off.

"...Heavens. Why did you send such a human to earth...."

"No matter how much you lament, the heavens won't do anything. Just die already."

Thwack.

The sword pierced Nurarihyon.
