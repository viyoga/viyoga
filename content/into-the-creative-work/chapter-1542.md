Dokgo Clan.

The Dokgo Clan, once renowned as the greatest family under Heaven, was now in a state of decline.

A family with an ambiguous power, larger than a small sect but smaller than a prestigious one.

The Dokgo Clan always yearned to climb higher.

Dokgo Cheongha, the young master of the Dokgo Clan, possessed a talent for martial arts from a young age. She was a genius. With that talent, she overwhelmed her male siblings and seized the position of young master.

She would one day become the head of the Dokgo Clan. However, the current state of the Dokgo Clan did not please her. Their power was too small. Even if she tried to increase their power, she had to be mindful of the Martial Alliance. The Martial Alliance had been constantly keeping the Dokgo Clan in check. This was because one of the Dokgo Clan's blood relatives had caused a bloody massacre in the past.

,If things continue like this, even if I become the head of the family, the Dokgo Clan will not become the best martial arts family.'

As she fretted, an opportunity arose.

A war broke out between the Martial Alliance and the Demonic Cult. The Martial Alliance did not want the Dokgo Clan's help. They believed that the Martial Alliance's power alone was sufficient, even without the Dokgo Clan. However, the Beggars' Sect, which belonged to the Nine Great Sects at the heart of the Martial Alliance, had a different opinion.

The Beggars' Sect secretly approached the Dokgo Clan.

An old beggar with seven knots, signifying a Beggars' Sect elder, came to visit.

Soongae (Ssa Oong-gae). A beggar from the Beggars' Sect, famous for wandering the Jianghu. The Dokgo Clan welcomed him. Although he was a beggar, he was an elder of the Beggars' Sect. He was a master who could not be ignored.

The senior members of the Dokgo Clan welcomed him.

,Soongae-nim! I have heard much about your reputation. Welcome to the Dokgo Clan.'

,I didn't come to the Dokgo Clan to be welcomed. I have a proposal for the Dokgo Clan.'

Dokgo Han, the current head of the Dokgo Clan, asked cautiously.

,Is this the Martial Alliance's intention?'

,The Martial Alliance is very busy right now. The Demonic Cult's Annihilation Unit annihilated the Mount Hua Sect and is scattering and fleeing. The Martial Alliance is frantically chasing after them. In the process, our Beggars' Sect, which is in charge of the Martial Alliance's information, is running around with sweat on our heels.'

,Of course, we are aware of the Beggars' Sect's hard work. Not only us, but all the righteous sects will respect the Beggars' Sect.'

,Enough with the sweet talk.'

Soongae cut him off. Dokgo Han was displeased but did not show it.

,Our Beggars' Sect needs the Dokgo Clan's help.'

,...It's difficult for you to suddenly say that you need help.'

,The Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok of the Annihilation Unit will pass through this place in a week and head to the Demonic Sect. This is one of the pieces of information our Beggars' Sect has obtained.'

Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok!

Dokgo Han had heard that name several times. Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok had become famous for his bad blood with the Beggars' Sect. There was a rumor spreading that he had massacred all the beggars in a city to find the Beggars' Sect's spy.

It wasn't just a simple massacre. It was said that he would behead beggars and stick their heads on spears, and that he had even boiled beggars alive in a scalding pot.

Because of this, the Beggars' Sect gnashed their teeth at the name Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok.

,I will kill Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok. I want the Dokgo Clan to help me with that.'

,...Didn't you say that the Martial Alliance is chasing the Annihilation Unit members? What is the Martial Alliance doing?'

,Yeom Guseok is a young punk who hasn't been in the Annihilation Unit for long. The Martial Alliance is aiming for bigger fish than Yeom Guseok. That's why the other elders of the Beggars' Sect can't step in. The Beggars' Sect is in charge of the Martial Alliance's information, so we can't focus on just one person, Yeom Guseok.'

,I understand the situation. However, this is not something that concerns our family. The Dokgo Clan does not belong to the Martial Alliance, and we have no personal grudge against Lightning Blade.'

,I know. But please help us. If you help us, the Beggars' Sect will support the Dokgo Clan.'

,Support means...'

,I will help the Dokgo Clan enter the Martial Alliance. Isn't it time for the Dokgo Clan to break free from the shackles of the past and run through the vast fields of the Jianghu?'

,Father! No, Clan Head-nim! This is an opportunity! An opportunity for our family to shine again!'

The young master Dokgo Cheongha, who had been listening silently, stepped forward. The eyes of the refined beauty shone with desire. The retainers around her also spoke to Dokgo Han in the same manner.

,Clan Head-nim! Accept it!'

,This is an opportunity that may never come again if we miss it!'

,The time has come to show the world that the Dokgo Clan is still strong!'

,If we can only belong to the Martial Alliance, the Dokgo Clan will soar!'

Dokgo Han was silent. Soongae's proposal was a good one that could not be refused. It was such a good proposal that it was suspicious. But even that suspicion disappeared. This was because he understood the Beggars' Sect's motive for wanting to kill Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok.

,The Beggars' Sect must want to kill Yeom Guseok at this opportunity. If Yeom Guseok returns to the Demonic Cult, the Beggars' Sect will have no chance to kill him.'

It was right to accept Soongae's proposal. Help him get rid of Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok. Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok's known martial prowess was in the early stages of Ogwi. If the Dokgo Clan sincerely stepped up, they could easily handle him.

,It's easy. It's an easy task. But... why am I so uneasy?'

He wanted to reject the proposal, but he had no good reason. Reject such a good opportunity just because he had a bad feeling? The retainers would not be convinced.

Above all....

Dokgo Han looked at his daughter. Dokgo Cheongha's eyes were full of desire. Dokgo Han knew his daughter's ambition. He hoped that the family would not hold her back.

,...The Dokgo Clan will help Soongae-nim.'

,Thank you, Clan Head Dokgo. You will never regret that choice. I promise in the name of the Beggars' Sect.'

The atmosphere relaxed. Soongae smiled, and the retainers also smiled. Dokgo Cheongha was also faintly smiling. None of them thought that the plan would fail. Dokgo Han felt the same way.

Dokgo Han brought out expensive tea and offered it to Soongae.

,I want to know the information about Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok.'

,I will tell you everything I know.'

,How did you track Yeom Guseok? As far as I know, the Annihilation Unit members are all scattered and hiding.'

,They are hiding and attacking the promising disciples of the Martial Alliance. Lightning Blade killed Gwanggeom, the top disciple of the Wudang Sect. He reported it to the Demonic Sect through a carrier pigeon, and our Beggars' Sect was able to eavesdrop on the report and confirm Lightning Blade's location. He was calling himself the best swordsman in Joseon as he traveled around.'

,He was openly moving around with a fake identity?'

,That's right. He's a bold bastard. Do you know what's even more absurd? He's returning to the Demonic Sect while traveling around the cities like a tourist. He's not only harassing the courtesans in the brothels, but he's also deliberately picking fights with martial artists he doesn't like and crippling them.'

Dokgo Han frowned. He could feel the evil just from hearing about Yeom Guseok's actions.

,He's a guy who shouldn't be allowed to live for the sake of the future of the righteous faction.'

,You see right through him. He will become a great demon in the near future. We must kill him before that happens.'

,Do you have a plan?'

,There's not much of a plan. Judging from his actions, he will definitely stop by this city. He will spend a day at the best brothel and leave. We can surround the brothel and ambush him to kill him. Since it's killing a demon, no one will criticize us. They will praise us instead.'

,I will prepare. The Dokgo Search Unit of the Dokgo Clan will surround him. In addition, I, the young master, and the elders will all step up to face Lightning Blade Yeom Guseok.'

,Thank you! That's more than enough power! Hahaha! Just thinking about that evil bastard dying makes me laugh!'

Their atmosphere was very friendly.
