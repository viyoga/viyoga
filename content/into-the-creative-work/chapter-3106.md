Japan's top power brokers, including Prime Minister Yamahata Matsuda, received Oneko's report. They held an emergency meeting, Oneko included.

For a country supposedly so analog, the meeting was conducted via digital video conference.

In Japan's ongoing S-Clone Project, an awakener with a potential rating of 5 stars had appeared. By protocol, any clone possessing mental resistance was to be terminated without exception.

There was one reason. Brainwashing—the ability wielded by Oneko and others like her—simply didn't take. An S-Clone immune to brainwashing was a nightmare to control. At first they'd seem obedient, then they'd rebel at the worst possible moment.

Control them through torture? Whether it was the mental resistance or something else, fear induced by pain proved utterly meaningless.

Slap a bomb collar on them? The detonator was held by a handler. But no handler could monitor a clone twenty-four hours a day. The watcher was only human—gaps were inevitable.

They read through the incidents committed by S-Clones, all attached to the report.

S017—raped a senior official's daughter, then committed suicide alongside her.

S054—killed a military base commander and self-detonated using a missile.

S090—attempted to steal a nuclear weapon Japan was secretly developing. Failed.

S111—harbored resentment over torture, massacred a Lower House member's entire family, then killed himself.

S137—stole explosives from a military base and launched a suicide bombing in central Tokyo.

These were the acts of S-Clones who either couldn't be brainwashed or had broken free of it. Japan's most powerful found their level of defiance incomprehensible.

Was it a matter of treatment?

They had treated clones like ordinary Japanese citizens before. In their minds, that alone was an enormous privilege. A mere clone, treated as a human being.

S391—raped eleven young women. Killed three responding police officers. Special forces deployed, clone eliminated.

After that, they understood with absolute clarity. The nature of S-Clones was rotten from the very start.

Some argued it might be an effect of the mental resistance trait, but other S-Clones without mental resistance also caused problems when brainwashing failed.

In the end, they concluded that brainwashing was essential for controlling S-Clones. The protocol became: any individual that couldn't be brainwashed was to be shot on the spot.

"Potential rating of 5 stars. Abilities: mental resistance, electrokinesis, regeneration. All innate, and no dullard trait either. This may be the closest thing to the original—perhaps even an identical clone."

Prime Minister Yamahata Matsuda said.

"This is remarkable. He can use regeneration like the original?"

One of the power brokers asked Oneko.

"It hasn't been confirmed yet, but without the dullard trait, his growth should be rapid. We possess collected data on the original. If we provide support based on that, S1599 will develop quickly."

Oneko answered without emotion.

"At last, it seems the time has come to bid farewell to these wretched wrinkles."

"I was so envious when I heard Korean politicians grow younger by the day…."

"My son was born unable to see. It's true that he can be healed without an awakening serum, yes?"

The atmosphere of the meeting was warmer than it had ever been.

Even Oneko found it unfamiliar. These meetings were normally conducted with solemn gravity. They determined Japan's future. In this room, thousands, tens of thousands of lives crumbled like sandcastles.

Of course, Oneko understood the mood.

Youth.

They were on the verge of grasping what those in power desired above all else.

"By precedent, clones with mental resistance that cannot be brainwashed are to be disposed of. However, S1599's case is exceptional. Is there anyone here who wishes to dispose of S1599?"

"According to the report, having mental resistance doesn't mean brainwashing is entirely ineffective. Can't Oneko simply apply it one-on-one every day?"

"Even if not, why not assign over a hundred personnel for surveillance and management?"

"S1599 is special. If you're worried about his temperament, just provide women, alcohol, drugs. Special treatment is warranted for a special asset."

"Special treatment for a Joseon bastard seems a bit much, doesn't it?"

"Its original may be Korean, but it was born in Japan, making it Japanese."

The power brokers bickered gleefully, not even pretending to maintain their dignity. When asked who would manage S1599, every single one of them volunteered.

Ultimately, S1599's management fell to Oneko. Because she had decided to brainwash S1599 personally, every day.

"The future is promising."

Prime Minister Yamahata Matsuda said with a smile. Every power broker except Oneko wore a smile.

Behind her mask, Oneko's expression was blank.

The entity designated S1599 was given the name Q. The 9 in 1599—pronounced "kyu." Hence the name Q.

Calling it a name was almost insultingly lazy.

But after spending a few days in Japan, Q understood this was a privilege.

*Japan doesn't see clones as people. Just weapons or tools that eat food instead of oil.*

Q lived more comfortably than an ordinary Japanese citizen, more comfortably than a Japanese military officer, more comfortably than most of Japan's powerful.

In an era where food was precious, he could eat delicious meals to his heart's content.

There was censorship, but he could consume games, manga, movies—as much as he wanted.

Above all, he could embrace women whenever he desired. Celebrity-tier beauties, no less. Though those women were nothing more than whores.

*I want to fuck Oneko.*

He had never seen Oneko's face. But instinct told him. Beneath that mask, Oneko's bare face was surely beautiful.

Q met Oneko for about a minute each day. She came to brainwash him.

At first, her brainwashing left his mind foggy all day, and he did whatever he was told. But recently, he'd built up a resistance—after two or three hours, the brainwashing wore off.

Q was reviewing data on his original, Sung Yujin, through a monitor installed in his room.

*They want me to look at Sung Yujin's data and grow as fast as he did.*

Most of the data related to Sung Yujin was true, but some had been subtly manipulated. Particularly to emphasize his cruelty.

*They're scheming to make me hostile toward the original. Did they think I wouldn't notice? Sometimes I think these bastards see me as having the intelligence of a monkey.*

Q kept reading Sung Yujin's data. Sung Yujin was not alone. There were many women around him.

*…Na Chaeyoung. Player, class: Technologist. Big tits and very pretty. My type. The original is surrounded by beauties like this.*

They were on a completely different level from the whores he embraced. Even among celebrities, women of that caliber were hard to find. Q felt envy and jealousy toward the original.

*The Japanese are worried I'll break free of their control and side with the original. Pointless worry. If I were the original, I'd want to kill a clone that looked exactly like me—if only out of sheer disgust.*

Q felt an instinctive revulsion toward Sung Yujin. Not unbearable, but if possible, he wanted to kill him as soon as he could.

*Could I kill the original and take his place? Being king of Korea sounds better than being livestock for Japan.*

Pretending to be Sung Yujin was impossible. Q had none of Sung Yujin's memories. He didn't know what colors he liked, what foods he enjoyed.

*I heard he's a gourmet. I don't really get fine dining. Even poorly rated food tastes good to me. Same genes, but different palate?*

Realizing the differences from the original lifted his mood.

After reading data on the original came training.

The original was a master swordsman. Absurdly so. Even the Japanese, who prided themselves on their swordsmanship, acknowledged the original in that domain. One blade, no abilities, cutting down mutants—they had no choice but to acknowledge it.

Swordsmanship training.

The result: he had no talent. The sword master dispatched to teach him shook his head.

"A mediocrity, the common sort. With relentless dedication, you could reach my level someday…. But it will require immense effort and time."

Q clenched his fist. He hated to admit it, but he had to. He could never surpass the original with a sword.

Swordsmanship training was abandoned. Q's handlers and researchers accepted it indifferently. They'd never expected anything. No—they'd never cared.

Next was ability training.

Wielding electrokinesis.

Bzzt-crackle!

Blue current sparked from Q's hand. But he couldn't control it at will.

"There's intel that Sung Yujin manipulates electrical currents to accelerate his nerves. Try it."

"Nerves? I just need to run current through my spine?"

Bzzt-crackle!

Q collapsed to the floor. His nerves and brain burned. He'd failed to control the current. Fortunately, his regeneration activated automatically and saved him.

The researchers were stunned.

"His nerves were completely burned, yet they regenerated without a trace!"

"Not just the nerves—his brain melted and regenerated too! This is the equivalent of dying and coming back to life!"

Q lay on the floor, gasping for breath. The researchers had zero interest in his swordsmanship or his electrokinesis.

*…Goddammit. My head is splitting. Can barely move a finger… If I close my eyes now, I'll pass out.*

Q simply closed his eyes.

Q opened his eyes three days later. Shortly after, Oneko appeared in a suit. Still wearing the mask.

"You came to brainwash me?"

"…It's pointless, but orders from above."

Oneko's hand seized Q's head.

"You smell good. If you became my woman, I could be loyal forever. How about it?"

"A clone dares run his mouth. Shut up."

Q was brainwashed.

His mind went blank. Normally he couldn't even feel this, but his mental resistance let him perceive it.

He began regeneration training under Oneko's and the researchers' orders. Regeneration training was torture. Inflict wounds on the body, then heal them.

"Injuries or illnesses experienced firsthand can be regenerated faster and more efficiently. This is a known fact from other S-Clones."

Two hours later. The brainwashing worn off, Q refused the torture.

"Don't! You bastards!"

"Very well. It's about time we moved from training to live deployment."

"Live deployment?"

"You'll be treating patients, as many as possible."

He didn't like it, but there was no choice. The comforts Q enjoyed weren't free.

"Where's Oneko?"

"She is extremely busy. She has almost no time to invest in a single individual like you."

"Having to be around a bastard like you. Feels like shit. Bring me a woman."

"As you wish."

"If possible, not a whore. A pretty virgin would be ideal."

"If you desire better treatment, restore youth. If you can reverse aging… your treatment will be on an entirely different level."

"Still just a gilded cage."

Q understood why S-Clones who escaped brainwashing went berserk.

A controlled life was infuriating in itself. So in the end, they went out in a blaze.

He could endure because at least minimal freedom and women were guaranteed.

Three months passed.

Q became able to reverse a subject's aging by one year using his regeneration. The cost: he couldn't use his ability for a while afterward. Stamina was an issue, but mental energy was the real problem.

*Fucking hell. After reversing aging with regeneration, my body doesn't want to move…. My time disappears. Just when I try to heal up and get some sex in, the next old bastard shows up and throws a fit…. Pissing me off.*

He was irritated.

Even when he voiced complaints, the old men blinded by youth didn't even pretend to listen. They fought among themselves for a single year of youth.

*In the end, I'm just a convenient clone to use.*

He hadn't seen Oneko recently, and most of the women were whores—compared to the original's women, they were low quality.

The original was living large in the neighboring country, even creating a department filled with his women—National Service Bureau or whatever—while he was stuck here. Why did he have to hold the hands of old men?

*Fucking bullshit.*

He knew he shouldn't compare himself to the original, but he did anyway. If he were the original… he wouldn't be in this situation.

*Everything's fucking bullshit.*

There was less than a month until the Japan-Korea War.
