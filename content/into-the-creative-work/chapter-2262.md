I saw a magnificent castle ahead. It was bigger than any castle I had seen in this world.

The capital of the Thorn Kingdom.

It had the imposing presence of a castle with over 500 years of history.

And that castle was surrounded by 100,000 Kaihrak warriors. It was the result of summoning all the forces from other battlefields to this place.

According to the information, the King of the Thorn Kingdom couldn't abandon the royal castle and was holed up inside. If they captured the capital, this war would be a victory for Kaihrak. There was enough reason to wage an all-out war.

"Unexpected."

War Chief Sopong received the muttered words.

"What is it, Your Highness?"

"Shouldn't they have surrendered by now? It wouldn't be strange if the populace revolted?"

The smart ones know that the Thorn Kingdom has lost the war. It wouldn't be strange if they started a rebellion, captured the king, handed him over to me, and begged for mercy. But looking at it from here, there's no sign of rebellion at all.

"The Thorn Kingdom has a deep history. That history is the pride of the Thorn Kingdom. Even the people have that pride. Until the previous War King declared war on Kaihrak, those bastards called us uncivilized barbarians. That useless pride is what's making them refuse to surrender."

"What a bunch of morons."

I cursed with my mouth, but I was happy inside.

100,000 warriors were too many. I needed to reduce their numbers, and I could do it here.

'50% need to die to make the subsequent rule easier. No, actually, I don't really have any intention of ruling.'

Anyway, I had to do some pheasant plucking after the hunt.

"It's time to end this tiresome war. Charge!"

Ppuuuuuuuuu!

The horn blared, and the warriors charged, shouting.

Arrows and magic naturally flew from the castle. I crossed my arms and watched from behind. Even if there were magicians or superhumans, the patterns of war in this world were mostly similar.

Arrows didn't mean much, and magic could be seen as siege weapons. High-level warriors would unleash Sword energy before the magic flew, cutting the magic down. This was quite effective.

'Still, magicians have an unavoidable advantage on the battlefield. There's an Archmage stepping up too. That Archmage I saw last time.'

Great warriors who were Aura Masters stepped forward to check the Archmage. Similarly, an Aura Master called a general protected the Archmage around him.

The warriors were having trouble attacking the walls, probably because they were enchanted.

"Your Highness. Aren't you going to step up yourself?"

Sopong asked. I frowned and answered.

"I am the king. Just watching is enough. Or do you want me to go there to that sweaty place and swing an axe?"

"You seem displeased. I had no intention of insulting Your Highness. It's just that there's some bullshit going around among the lower ranks that the War King is a coward."

"I haven't been directly involved in the war recently. Investigate the bastards who spouted that nonsense. I'll kill them as soon as the war is over."

"...Understood."

"Sopong. You don't think that way too, do you?"

"Of course not. Kaihrak is able to push Thorn this far thanks to Your Highness's supplies. Only ignorant fools would doubt Your Highness. The war chiefs, including me, know of Your Highness's achievements."

"Good to know."

1 hour.

I thought the entrance to the castle would be breached in about an hour, but my expectations were wrong.

"...What is that?"

"Yes?"

"Can't you see it? Enemies are jumping down from the castle walls and fighting the warriors. They're fighting with only weapons and no decent armor, but how are they overwhelming the skilled warriors?"

Sopong focused mana in his eyes and looked at the battlefield.

"...That's strange. Those guys' movements are clumsy as hell. They're definitely ignorant people who have never wielded weapons. But they're dodging and countering the warriors' attacks. They don't seem to be using Aura."

I needed to see more closely.

[Opening the Celestial Eye.]

I looked at the enemies as if observing them. I could see their facial features and even the patterns on their clothes clearly.

"Their ears have become pointed, they have three eyes, and their skin has become rough like trees."

"...Those aren't humans, are they?"

"Did such monsters live in the Thorn Kingdom?"

"There are no demi-humans in the Eastern Continent. There was a demi-human oppression in the past, and all the demi-humans migrated to the Western Continent."

"Unless the Thorn Kingdom has gone crazy, they wouldn't have brought monsters into the capital... So they're human."

They turned humans into monsters. No, it was a bit lacking to call them monsters. Their physical abilities were excellent, but that was it. The warriors were getting used to the enemies and regaining the upper hand.

'What kind of power did they use to transform them? It's not magic, anyway. I've never heard of such magic.'

What I suspected was the divine power, the power of demons. Because that in itself was a power beyond common sense. Even if something like this happened, I could accept it.

'Did demons really intervene? I can't tell at this stage.'

Besides that, there are ancient artifacts. When strange things happen in this world, pointing to demons or ancient artifacts usually hits the mark.

2 hours passed.

The castle gates didn't open. 30,000 out of 100,000 troops had died. The enemies were now coming out of the castle to fight.

"Your Highness. This is strange no matter how I think about it! It's certain that the enemies are casting evil spells! Let's retreat first and then reorganize!"

"Shut up, Sopong. I can't allow a retreat. We'll end the war here today."

Sopong quietly turned his head. His face didn't look convinced, but there was nothing he could do. He looked up at the sky and suddenly shouted.

"Your Highness! It's the enemy's magic attack!"

I knew. I felt it before he shouted. Lightning manifested by magic fell from the clear blue sky.

The thick lightning struck the ground and caused an explosion. Having prepared in advance, I protected my body with an Aura Barrier. The Aura Barrier surrounding my body flickered uneasily like a candle in the wind.

'If it wasn't for the familiar lightning, the Aura Barrier would have been pierced. Sopong is... dead.'

It wasn't just Sopong. The escort troops around me couldn't withstand a single lightning strike, and their bodies were torn apart.

I glared at the empty air. Because I had opened the Celestial Eye, I could see the artificial flow of mana.

Pat.

An Archmage appeared in the empty air.

"Oh my. I struck you directly, but you don't have a single scratch. As expected of the War King. Amazing."

"It's that magician from back then. You look very different? I almost didn't recognize you."

The court magician of the Thorn Kingdom and Archmage Biiffu had rejuvenated to his 20s. He had three eyes on his head, and his right hand holding the magic staff had three more fingers, making it eight. Tentacle-like things wriggled under his robe.

"Did you sell your soul to a demon?"

"No. I decided to serve Ompas-nim."

"Ompas? What kind of demon is that?"

"Ompas-nim is not a demon but an ancient spirit. He is the one who created the Lerritri you gave to them."

I was horrified as soon as I heard that.

"Shit. My wife and daughter aren't going to turn into something weird like you, are they?!"

"Haha. Rest assured. That won't happen. This is the price of power and evolution. In the case of Lerritri's blessing, the price was paid long ago, so you just have to enjoy it."

"If that appearance is human evolution, I don't particularly want to do it."

"Evolution is something you can't control in the first place. Your Highness. End the war here. If you pay a reasonable compensation... Thorn is willing to end the war here."

"What if I don't want to?"

"Kaihrak will be destroyed."

"Try it if you can. Isn't that why you came to negotiate, because you can't?"

"We prioritized negotiation because continuing the war would only result in losses. Since the negotiation has fallen through... we'll have to see the end now."

Biiffu gestured. A living wind swirled around him. The wind soon became a storm and swept around. Warriors were dragged into the sky and ravaged by the wind. Some were torn apart, some were crushed, and some fell.

"Hahaha! Even using this much magic, my mana doesn't decrease! This is the power of Ompas-nim! Isn't it amazing?!"

The wind naturally rushed at me as well. I resisted the wind with an Aura Barrier.

"To wrap your body in an Aura Blade. That's an amazing way to use it!"

The Aura Barrier was being worn away little by little by the wind. I can't hold out for long.

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido to my right hand. I could see the flow of the wind. To be precise, the flow of mana that created the wind.

'It's as messy as a child's rough brushstrokes.'

I can see it clearly. The center that creates the wind in this mess. Pajijik. Lightning appeared in various places in that center. Dozens of lightning bolts rotated and bloomed in the shape of flower buds.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning Bloom (卍雷開花).

The lightning petals scattered in all directions, devouring the wind and dominating the space. Electricity flowed throughout the surrounding space, whether it was the ground or the sky.

"The wind is...?!"

I kicked off the ground and leaped towards him.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Lightning grabbed my body and pulled me.

Surprised by the faster-than-expected speed, Biiffu used the magic contained in his magic staff. Blink. A short-range teleportation magic.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Falling Lightning (落雷).

I stepped on the empty air and fell to the ground like lightning. Biiffu appeared where I was falling.

Red lightning cut through his waist and fell to the ground. I straightened my bent knees and looked at the fallen Biiffu.

"...How did you know in advance where I would move with Blink? Did you read the future or something?"

"I can see the flow of mana. Seeing means I can feel it. I read the small anomalies in the mana."

"To have such a cheating ability...."

Puk.

I ended him by embedding Hwaryeon Bido in Biiffu's head.

But I couldn't be happy just because I killed this one guy. Because I felt an enormous presence suppressing the surroundings from that royal castle.

'Hmm. I don't think I'll lose.'
