"Go-jun-ah! Let's back off from here! It's different from yesterday! The boss monster has changed!"

"...Please retreat immediately! The infiltration-type dungeon raid ends here! The situation has changed! The boss monster that was originally here was a King Slime of B-rank! Not something like that! Please back off now!"

But it was already too late.

From the lake-like swimming pool, its body soared upwards.

It was a dragon made of water. It didn't seem to have any intention of letting us go.

'......Water Dragon?'

I called it that without thinking, but there was no monster called a Water Dragon. In other words, it's the first monster to appear here. It may look like a dragon, but it probably isn't actually a dragon.

"N-no way...!"

One of the Hunters, a woman, was shocked. I don't know her name, but I know she has a special tool for identifying the monster's condition and information. The Hunters, including me, perked up our ears and focused on what she said.

Of course, the Water Dragon didn't wait for us in the meantime, but Han Ah-young created a huge ice shield to block its charge. Frosty water splashed everywhere.

"That's a fusion! A fusion of a Ghost, a Water Man, and a King Slime! And at the center of it... what is this?"

"It's probably the dungeon core. Originally, the dungeon core was inside the King Slime. I can't believe things would get this twisted in just one day..."

Jang Go-jun took out his equipment for the first time.

Two long sticks. They have a simple appearance, like two iron lumps stretched out lengthwise. There are Hunters who use staffs, but those are so bad they can't even be called weapons.

But soon, the shape of the sticks began to change.

Turning into a pair of swords with a glossy sheen and sharp edges.

'That's Jang Go-jun's ability, shape transformation.'

It's the ability to temporarily transform the shape of objects. However, the durability of the object does not change. Even if you transform mud into a hammer, it's still just a lump of mud.

"Hunters! Listen up! Now that it's come to this, we'll take care of it here! Association members, please stand by in case of an emergency! Fortunately, this is a place with water! Ah-young is at an advantage in terms of compatibility! Ah-young-ah! Can't you freeze it completely?!"

"I've already tried, but it's impossible to freeze it completely. At best, I can freeze the surface to reduce its mobility. It has excellent mana resistance!"

The Water Dragon jumped into the air. Its target is us, who haven't yet chosen our battle positions.

"Ha-rin-ah!!"

Han Ah-young shouted. Han Ha-rin bit her lip tightly and used gravity manipulation. Changing the direction of gravity to the side instead of down. Turning the direction of the Water Dragon, which probably weighs hundreds of tons.

It was a success. The Water Dragon flew sideways and crashed into the ground. Jang Go-jun wielded his twin swords, infused with Sword energy, over the Water Dragon's body.

Blood trickled down from Han Ha-rin's nose.

"Sunbae-nim! Are you okay?!"

I grabbed her faltering arm and supported her.

"...I'm okay. I used too much of my ability at once. I can use my ability again after a short rest. You do what you have to do."

"Yes. Sunbae-nim. I'll tear that bastard's throat out, so please stay away."

"......Be careful."

"Yes."

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido tightly and ran towards the Water Dragon that had fallen to the ground. It had barely come out of the lake swimming pool. I can't miss this opportunity.

'I can't kill it in one hit, but... I can wound it.'

Hwareureureuk! Sword energy blazed.

But I didn't get the chance to swing my sword.

Han Ah-young created a huge ice chunk in the sky and dropped it on the Water Dragon's head. Its head shattered, and water splashed everywhere.

'...She created this huge ice chunk in less than 10 seconds? You have to be this good to become an S-class candidate.'

This is actually the first time I've seen Han Ah-young's skills, and I could sincerely understand why she's said to be a future S-class Hunter.

But the situation wasn't over.

The Water Dragon's body split into hundreds of mini-sized Water Dragons, each 3M in size.

"The dungeon core wasn't in its head! One of those things must have the dungeon core! We have to smash the dungeon core!"

Han Ah-young shouted. The Hunters rushed in and slaughtered the mini Water Dragons that were splashing and running away into the lake swimming pool.

"It's too late! The dungeon core went into the swimming pool!"

The female Hunter said. The Hunters clicked their tongues and distanced themselves from the swimming pool. The swimming pool is the enemy's territory. The moment you accidentally fall into the swimming pool, death will be right in front of you.

The Water Dragon soared upwards from the swimming pool again. The shattered head had returned to normal, and the body size was the same as before.

There are two ways to defeat it. One is to aim for the dungeon core and smash it, and the other is to eliminate all the water in this space.

'The latter is impossible.'

It's still raining in the sky, and water is pooling everywhere in the water park. What a frustrating boss monster.

Wiiiiiiiiing! Wiiiiiiiiing!

The alarm sounded, and the Hunters' faces turned pale. They've heard that sound a few times coming here. A tsunami will hit soon.

'Shit!'

I ran to Han Ha-rin. The situation was the worst. The height of the wave crashing in from afar is over 10M. Han Ah-young is busy blocking the Water Dragon's attacks, so running away was the best option.

'Send Han Ha-rin to a safe place with a space 이동 spell scroll and Han Ah-young...'

Han Ha-rin indifferently looked around at the waves crashing in from all directions. Her atmosphere was completely different from the terrified Hunters. Han Ha-rin had no intention of running away. She's trying to fight back.

"Ha-rin Sunbae-nim!"

"I'll block it."

Han Ha-rin floated from the ground into the sky. Her black hair fluttered intensely.

Immediately after, an earthquake occurred.

To be more precise, the aftermath of Han Ha-rin using her ability is an earthquake.

'...Gravity is fluctuating.'

Some things are light, some are heavy, some are to the right, some are to the sky. As gravity began to run wild, the 10M-high tsunami couldn't get close and shattered.

An invisible storm of gravity is smashing the tsunami.

In the end, the tsunami didn't reach us, and Han Ha-rin, who had exerted her abilities beyond her limits, fell down. I caught her.

'Han Ha-rin has gone this far... I can't disgracefully run away.'

I took her to the female Hunter. The only Hunter who knows the location of the dungeon core.

"Excuse me. Can you help me?"

"Ah, yes. You mean taking care of Ha-rin-ssi? I'll protect Ha-rin-ssi. She protected us, so I can't just leave her alone. My ability is specialized in protection, so I'm confident."

"I'd like to ask you to do that, but... you know where the dungeon core is, right?"

"...Just a moment. I'll try to find it. I need concentration, so please protect me."

"Yes. I'll definitely protect you."

She put on thick-rimmed glasses from her pocket and looked at the Water Dragon. The lenses of the glasses shone blue.

About 50 seconds later. She shouted as if for the Hunters to hear.

"The dungeon core is near its tail! The tail is inside the swimming pool!"

The Hunters' faces contorted. The Water Dragon's body is transparent, so it was difficult to tell where its tail was. The probability of hitting the dungeon core from a distance is very low. To smash the dungeon core, you have to go into the lake swimming pool, into its territory.

"Thank you. I'll leave Ha-rin Sunbae-nim to you."

"Wait a minute! Are you planning to go in alone?! It's dangerous! There will be a chance for it to come out of the swimming pool like before. Aim for that time!"

"I don't think Ah-young Nuna or Jang Go-jun-ssi can hold out until then. Unless you're going to abandon them and run away, we have to fight now."

The Hunters become mournful. Most of them are C-class Hunters, so they must be feeling powerless.

"Staying still will only lead to death. Let's move. I'll go into the swimming pool with Yoo-jin-ssi. Are there anyone who will go in with us?"

"......I can't stand being beaten up. I'll join you."

"Honestly, I just want to pester from afar... but the situation is like this."

"I'll provide cover from afar. How can I, an archer, go in? Leave the cover to me."

The Hunters began to do their jobs. I was impressed by their teamwork. They are indeed Hunters from the Suwol Guild.

I ran across the water of the swimming pool with Gong Ji-young and ran towards the Water Dragon. Its scary points are its strength due to its huge size and the fact that it constantly regenerates as long as there is water, even if it is injured.

'Han Ah-young and Jang Go-jun are doing a good job of attracting its attention. If I can avoid its sudden movements...!'

As I got closer, I could see the shape of the tail in the swimming pool. I could see its shape because it wasn't stopped in the water but was constantly moving.

And the blue crystal inside the tail! It was definitely the dungeon core.

"That's the dungeon core!"

"Ji-young-ssi!"

"Yes! I'll blow it away!"

Her fist plunged into the water where its tail was.

Kwaaaaaaaang!

The shockwave spread throughout the swimming pool, and water gushed out. But the tail was only slightly crushed in shape, and the dungeon core was safe.

"I underestimated the water... its durability is beyond imagination... At this rate... Yoo-jin-ssi?!"

I created Sword energy and stabbed it towards the dungeon core. But the dungeon core moved from the tail towards the body, as if mocking me.

'Damn it. It could move? Don't tell me the dungeon core has a will of its own?'

No. It doesn't seem like it. It must have moved instinctively.

"Yoo-jin-ah! Dodge!"

An arm stretched out from the Water Dragon's body and struck me and Gong Ji-young.

Gong Ji-young jumped and easily dodged it, but I, who was focused on the dungeon core, was a little late to react.

'Challna!'

I jumped upwards, riding on its slow arm. Thanks to the unexpected foothold, I had another chance to aim for the dungeon core.

Pook!

Hwaryeon Bido dug inside. It barely grazed the dungeon core.

'Red thunder!!'

Red lightning spread throughout its body.

'It hit!'

It hit, but only a part of the dungeon core was smashed. The dungeon core went up further. I clicked my tongue and jumped upwards, stepping on its body.

Arms shot up from its body on both sides, aiming for me.

"Thank you. It was hard to go down... you pulled the dungeon core up."

Jang Go-jun, who had transformed the twin swords infused with Sword energy into a greatsword, cut off the arms and swung it horizontally towards the dungeon core.

"Damn it! I missed...!"

The Water Dragon's body was cut, but the dungeon core remained intact. Just before its body reattached, I dug inside.

'Thanks to the Blessing of Water, I can breathe even in here. I thought it was a useless skill at first, but... it was a completely awesome skill.'

Of course, it wasn't peaceful inside. I could feel a pressure that seemed to crush my body. Just because I can breathe doesn't mean I can last long here.

'Are you planning to crush me with water pressure...? Do you think I'll just stand still!'

I kicked the water and soared upwards.

The red blade aimed for the dungeon core. The water hand pulled my body, but my sword had already pierced the dungeon core.

Pajik!

The dungeon core disappeared and turned into a magic stone bigger than my head. The Water Dragon's form turned into ordinary water and fell down.

I also fall down. The height seems to be about 20M, but the bottom is a swimming pool, and I am a Hunter. If I strengthen my body with mana, diving from this height is no problem.

As I fell down, I looked at the water park. The dark clouds in the sky disappeared, and the transformed structures regained their original appearance.

'As expected, the last hit is the most thrilling!'

Pungdeong!
