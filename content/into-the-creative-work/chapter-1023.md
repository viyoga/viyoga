Kyle cast his gaze far away. Beyond the flowing river, the enemies were visible.

The Cobalt Kingdom's army.

Their numbers hovered around 50,000. It was a very small number to represent the entire Cobalt Kingdom's army. But Kyle couldn't afford to dismiss it with a sneer, because he hadn't been able to defeat this army. Each and every soldier of the Cobalt Kingdom's army was elite.

From the lowest private to the highest knight. He didn't know what tricks they used, but they were all strong.

Kyle focused his eyes. As an Aura Master, he could see the leader of the Cobalt Kingdom. King Doolivad, with his red hair fluttering. He was a formidable opponent, not inferior to Kyle himself.

Kyle had faced him several times, but he could never figure out what he was thinking. Eventually, Doolivad gave some orders to his army. One of the knights blew a trumpet. The soldiers of the Cobalt Kingdom turned around.

Before leaving, Doolivad glared at Kyle with burning eyes. No, he glared past Kyle before retreating.

,Congratulations, Kyle-nim! The war is finally over!

A somewhat frivolous voice, unsuited to the battlefield, rang out. Kyle turned his gaze away from the retreating Cobalt Kingdom's army.

There stood a flamboyant man with soft blonde hair and blue eyes. A deep smile was etched on his lips. He was Olrond Geulbet Baron, who had begun serving Kyle a few months ago. The moment Kyle saw him, he felt his mind stabilize.

,The war here is over, but the continental war is still ongoing.

,I know. But isn't it true that one war has ended? Kyle-nim. Enjoy this victory now. Kyle-nim is the clear victor.

,Victory... It's a victory, but it feels unsettling.

Kyle sighed. For a victory, all he had gained was fame. The Cobalt Kingdom's army had merely retreated before them.

,Where's Terius?

,He's resting at his quarters. He kept wanting to go out on the battlefield... Oh dear. The battlefield is gone. I should offer him some words of sympathy later.

Geulbet Baron said playfully. Kyle didn't pay much attention. Geulbet Baron's tone was usually like that.

Terius.

Terius, Kyle's soldier, had gained fame through the battlefield.

Berserker Terius.

It wasn't a good title. It was a reputation he gained from rampaging madly on the battlefield.

,I should meet him later.

Kyle had many capable subordinates. Compared to those capable subordinates, Terius was somewhat lacking. His martial prowess was outstanding, but he would go berserk at the sight of blood, making him difficult to control. He was the most troublesome of his subordinates.

But strangely, Terius kept bothering him. It was probably because they shared a common enemy.

,Kyle-nim. I was wondering, but you've looked unwell since earlier. Is something troubling you?

,...It's a family matter.

,Haha. Why are you like this? I've been assisting Kyle-nim with all my strength until now. Are you trying to push me away now? I'm truly disappointed.

Kyle was flustered. Was it a matter of human compatibility? For some reason, he found it much harder to deal with Geulbet than with others.

,No... It's not like that. I just thought that if I return like this... Father would choose Eugene as his successor.

Kyle had been on a winning streak. He had won repeatedly in battles. People were already calling him a hero. However, Kyle wasn't the only one who had become a hero. Eugene had been victorious in the north, and a few days ago, he had crushed the army of the Bitrasse Kingdom in the west and saved an elf village. Kyle's achievements were overshadowed by Eugene's.

In addition, Eugene was subtly pulling strings behind the scenes. Although the princess was opposing Eugene, Eugene's influence, with his inexhaustible wealth, was growing day by day.

,Kyle-nim. You're talking weakly.

,...It's not weak talk. That's the reality. Eugene is more capable than me.

Olrond erased the smile from his face. Kyle flinched without realizing it. Olrond, when serious, possessed a bizarre spirit that momentarily overwhelmed him.

,Are you giving up the Fruckus County for such a reason?

,...It's not about giving up, that's just the reality.

,Kyle-nim. Eugene Fruckus Baron is outstanding. Yes. He is an unparalleled hero. But think about it. Does his personality suit a hero? Will people be happy if he inherits the county?

,...That's....

Kyle recalled the rumors he had heard about Eugene recently. Eugene had executed thousands of slaves. The publicly known reason was a slave rebellion. There were more than a few suspicious points.

,Kyle-nim. Think of Yuria Grace. She, who is closest to Eugene Fruckus, might be being abused.

,T-That can't be... Eugene and Yuria's relationship is close to that of lovers.

Kyle trembled. Just thinking about Yuria made his heart race and his face flush. His mind was filled with Yuria. How long had it been since he last saw her? He wanted to meet her.

,Remember the slave massacre that happened recently. Eugene Fruckus's true personality is violent. He's definitely treating Yuria like a slave. In the worst case, he might be raping her every day.

There was a possibility.

Kyle imagined Yuria being forcibly violated by Eugene and clenched his teeth. Anger and hatred surged.

,...I have to save Yuria. But if I don't become the successor, there's no way....

,Kyle-nim. The easiest way to become the successor is to eliminate the competition. It's also the last resort at the same time.

,.......

Kill Eugene. That's what he was saying. Kyle found himself drawn to that method. But he couldn't just barge in and kill him.

,I have good news for Kyle-nim. Eugene Fruckus is currently in the Hebersite Mountains.

The Hebersite Mountains were located in the northern part of the Rapelli Kingdom. They were relatively small and so barren that neither monsters nor people lived there.

,...Eugene? Why there?

,I don't know. One thing is certain: Yuria Grace is with Eugene Fruckus. They might be plotting something sinister.

Kyle swallowed.

Several words floated in his mind.

Opportunity, Yuria, Eugene, Hebersite Mountains, Fruckus County. And so on.

He felt dizzy. Amidst the dizziness, Geulbet's voice reached his ears. With a pleasant voice, he spoke the words he wanted to hear. Among them, there was a word that shook Kyle's heart.

,Yuria. You must save Yuria. Kyle-nim.

,...That's right. I have to save Yuria.

,Doesn't Kyle-nim love Yuria more than anyone else? You must save Yuria from being abused by Eugene.

,I love Yuria. Yuria....

,Fortunately, the war is over. Even if it's just a short break, you have some free time. I will accompany Kyle-nim. I will guide Kyle-nim to the Hebersite Mountains. Oh, and take Terius with you. He will definitely be helpful. Surely you're not still hesitating?

,...No. I've decided not to hesitate. I will kill Eugene and save Yuria. And I will... make Yuria happy.

,That's exactly it, Kyle-nim! Yuria will surely be waiting for Kyle-nim too.

Kyle narrowed his eyes and stared blankly into the air. Yuria flickered in his vision.

I headed to the Hebersite Mountains with Yuria and Elena. This barren mountain range in the northern part of the Rapelli Kingdom held the power to lead Yuria to the realm of Grand Master.

Of course, it wasn't guaranteed that Yuria would become a Grand Master. There was a good chance of failure. But I believed in Yuria. Yuria would reach the realm of Grand Master.

However, what I didn't anticipate was the state of the Hebersite Mountains. They looked fine from the outside, but the sight that greeted us after climbing the mountains directly left me speechless.

Demon Stone Gate.

A large, diamond-shaped black stone was scattering demonic energy from the center of the mountain range. And the Demon Stone Gate was also called a Demon Gate.

In other words, the Demon Society bastards had already occupied the Hebersite Mountains.

,This didn't happen in the original story... What caused it to change like this?

There were too many guesses to make.

,Demon Stone Gate.... I saw it once at Goldway Academy a long time ago. Priscilla, the dragon, took care of it back then... But Priscilla isn't here now.

There was no way to call Priscilla. I had to handle it myself. And as far as I knew, ordinary power wouldn't be enough to get rid of that thing.

,Would it be possible with Yuria and Elena?

I stared intently at the Demon Stone Gate using my Mind's Eye. Demons and magical beasts could emerge from the Demon World through the Demon Stone Gate. But I couldn't see any demons or magical beasts.

,There's a high chance they're hiding somewhere. But since I can't see them, they must be hiding quite deep.

I pondered what to do.

First of all, it was better to get rid of the Demon Stone Gate. The purpose of the Demon Stone Gate was twofold: to summon magical beasts and demons from the Demon World and to transform the environment of this world into that of the Demon World.

And the ultimate goal of the Demon Society, which installed this Demon Stone Gate, was to summon the Demon King to this world.

,Elena. Can you erase that without a trace using illusion magic?

Elena quietly watched the Demon Stone Gate. Then she shook her head.

,It's impossible unless I use Limit Break. I can feel a level of power from that stone lump that's similar to the Plague Demon I saw last time.

,Yuria. What about you?

,If you command me to, I will destroy it right now. However, if we destroy it with force, the demonic energy of the Demon Stone Gate may explode and sweep the surroundings.

,......Let's destroy it later.

There was one thing I was worried about in destroying the Demon Stone Gate right now. It was the Dragon Lord's heart, which was somewhere in this mountain range. Yuria had to obtain the heart. That was the only way she could reach the realm of Grand Master.

,It would be the worst if the Demon Stone Gate exploded and affected the Dragon Lord's heart. The Demon Stone Gate isn't immediately dangerous anyway, so we can deal with it later.

Having come to a conclusion, I pretended not to notice the Demon Stone Gate. It wasn't urgent. Even if the Demon Stone Gate existed for a little longer, it wouldn't change much.

,I hope the Dragon Lord's heart hasn't been affected.

I looked around the barren mountain range. The trees in the mountain range were withered, and the ground was full of black rocks. It wasn't famous for its barrenness for no reason. I had to find the remains of the Dragon Lord somewhere in this mountain range.

,You don't know the exact location?

Elena grumbled discontentedly. I scratched my head and nodded.

,That's how it is.

,Haa. This is a pain in the ass. Then tell me at least a clue. I'll use magic to find it for you.

,That's... There's no clue.

This was something I couldn't help either.

Kyle, the protagonist of the original story, had also discovered the Dragon Lord's remains by chance. Moreover, the information about its location was vaguely described, making it impossible to pinpoint a specific location. All I knew was that it was somewhere in the Hebersite Mountains.

,...Hoo. Let's move for now.

As Elena said, we had to move for now. We wouldn't gain anything by just standing still.

We wandered around here and there in the mountain range. Apart from the Demon Stone Gate, there was nothing special.

Kugugugugugung.

Our footsteps, which had been trudging along the mountain range, came to a halt. The ground was shaking. It wasn't just shaking. There was something underground.

I lowered my gaze to my feet. Activating my Mind's Eye, I looked through the ground. A huge, earthworm-like magical beast was rising towards us.

,Yuria, Elena! It's coming from underground!!
