,Oh, Cheonma-isiyeo! Little Cheonma-gun has been attacked!

A martial artist from the Macheokdae, the Cheonma's personal guard, knelt before Cheonma Cheon Seongjin.

The Cheonma, sitting on the jade throne, looked down at him with vacant eyes. Even now, the inner demon was gnawing at him. If not for the Cheonma Sacred Seat, a special magical artifact, he would have long been consumed by the inner demon, rampaging and dying.

"......"

Little Cheonma. That name made the Cheonma's fading will shine faintly. But that was all. The Cheonma remained seated on the Cheonma Sacred Seat. The inner demon did not disappear, constantly gnawing at his mind.

The Cheonma was dying alive. The inner demon was a disease, a curse. The source of this inner demon was the Emperor. The moment he saw the Emperor, he realized it as if facing a long-buried truth.

That even if he staked his entire life, even if he sacrificed everything in the Demonic Sect, he could never reach the Emperor.

That despair became an inner demon, gnawing at the Cheonma.

,...Eighth Elder Cheong Susaekma Kwon Man-ok has died at Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok's hands. If you don't act now, Little Cheonma-gun won't be able to hold out and will die.

"......"

There were dozens of powerful figures here. The Macheokdae, the Cheonma's personal guard, were elite Person skilled in martial arts even within the Demonic Sect. And the Right Protector and Left Protector, who could be called the Cheonma's left and right arms, were stronger than the elders.

Their characteristic was that they did not move without the Cheonma's permission. Only the Cheonma's command could move them. Even if it meant the death of the Cheonma's son, Little Cheonma-gun.

,...The Eighth Elder died?

The Cheonma said in a cracked voice. His eyes were still vacant, but a small light flickered within.

,Yes. We have confirmed his death.

,Did Little Cheonma-gun also die?

,He is not dead yet. But Little Cheonma-gun as he is now cannot stop Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok. Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok is aiming for Little Cheonma-gun's position. Behind him is First-Class Royal Guard Baek Ran. It may be a scheme by the Imperial family.

,The Imperial family....

The Cheonma's face twisted. He reacted more violently to the word "Imperial family" than to the fact that Little Cheonma-gun was in danger.

,I must see.

,We will escort you immediately....

The Macheokdae martial artist suddenly shut his mouth. It was because an alien and powerful energy was felt from the Little Cheonma Palace. Immediately after, Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok soared through the roof of the Little Cheonma Palace.

Before he knew it, the Cheonma had moved. His Cheonma Sacred Seat moved to the window. As he was in the highest place within the Demonic Sect, he could see outside through the window.

,A Shura, huh.

The Cheonma's emptiness disappeared. It was such a shocking event that he had to suppress the inner demon by force, even if it was just for the moment. Why would an entity from the Netherworld suddenly appear? There was only one reason. Little Cheonma Cheon Yu-un had meddled with the Netherworld.

What if this reached the Emperor's ears? The Demonic Sect would be finished immediately. To avoid annihilation, the Demonic Sect had to kill Cheon Yu-un. Convincing the Emperor was also a task to be done. First-Class Royal Guard Baek Ran, was it? He had to coordinate with him.

Little Cheonma-gun and the Demonic Sect. If he had to abandon one, it would naturally be Little Cheonma-gun. The Cheonma's eyes sank coldly.

,Please send me and the Left Protector. We will capture the Shura and Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok immediately.

The Right Protector said.

,Wait. The two are fighting fiercely. It's not too late to subdue them after this battle ends. It seems that the Ghost Shura will win.

The Cheonma's gaze began to sink again. Ghost Shura. An inhuman existence. But it was nothing compared to the Emperor. He was weaker than him, so of course he couldn't compare to the Emperor. He couldn't understand why the Emperor, who possessed such tremendous power, was wary of beings from other worlds.

As his thoughts turned to the Emperor, the inner demon raised its head again. The Cheonma's eyes began to die again.

The change occurred then. Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok's energy, which had been dying, exploded.

The Cheonma, as a user of the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, knew what that was.

,He used the Pateonhwang. That crazy bastard.

A technique that destroys and burns one's own soul. It was a technique that he didn't know why it existed in the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art.

,...What is this power? It instantly surpassed the Ghost Shura's power. No matter how much he used the Pateonhwang, it's not logically possible for such a gap to occur.

But that logically impossible thing was reality.

He overwhelmed the Ghost Shura. He was playing with him.

,There's no need to intervene anymore.

The Ghost Shura would die. Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok, who used the Pateonhwang, would also die. They would fight each other to the death and self-destruct. It was a trivial ending.

That's when it happened. The hair on his entire body stood on end. The Cheonma, the Protectors, the Macheokdae, all trembled without realizing it. It was fear based on instinct. The Cheonma's eyes widened. He had heard of it.

,Forcibly drawing out the fear of death, this is the power of the Cheonsalseong!

What was the Cheonsalseong?

It could be said to be a power corresponding to the Emperor's Jamiseong. The Cheonma didn't even know exactly what kind of power the Emperor's Jamiseong had. The only thing that was certain was that the opposite of Jamiseong was Cheonsalseong!

,Cheonsalseong... Cheonsalseong was right nearby!

A talent who could oppose the Emperor! But it was already too late. The Cheonsalseong had used Pateonhwang to destroy his own soul.

The battle was over.

The Cheonsalseong killed the Ghost Shura and even Little Cheonma Cheon Yu-un died. He sent all the energy the Cheonsalseong possessed into the sky. The thick demonic energy of the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art spread in the sky. Some of the Macheokdae martial artists trembled in fear without realizing it.

It was the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, but it was slightly different from the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art. He felt a power that subjugated all demons. If the Macheokdae were like this, it wouldn't be strange for the demons within the Demonic Sect to kneel and tremble.

The Cheonma's eyes, which had sparkled when he realized it was Cheonsalseong, turned vacant again.

That's when it happened.

Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok raised his body.

That energy was definitely felt. He had used Pateonhwang, but he didn't die. The energy of the Cheonsalseong was gone, but he was definitely alive.

The Cheonma jumped up from the Cheonma Sacred Seat. A strange desire bloomed instead of the inner demon.

The enemy who killed his son? He had also mastered the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, so it didn't matter. His son died because he was weak. A new successor had simply seized Little Cheonma-gun's position with his own strength.

,If it's him...! If it's him, he can oppose the Emperor! Killing the Emperor, a true Cheonma will become my successor!

The Demonic Sect must be eternal, and the Cheonma must be the best in the world.

,Listen. In the name of the Cheonma, I appoint Seomjeondo Yeom Guseok as Little Cheonma-gun. No objections allowed.

,We shall obey the Cheonma's command!

,Treat him and serve him as Little Cheonma-gun. He is my legitimate successor. I... I need to deal with this damn inner demon first. I must enter seclusion.

The martial artists of the Macheokdae, including the Right Protector and Left Protector, were moved.

,Finally, the Cheonma is ready to overcome the inner demon! The Cheonma who has overcome the inner demon will become even stronger!

• • •

I walked back to the Demonic Sect. As soon as I entered the Demonic Sect, the atmosphere changed. It felt subdued. I could feel ordinary people looking at me with tension.

,Is it because of my clothes?

My clothes were a mess due to the battle. The Black Tiger Robe was in tatters, but my body had no wounds. Complete recovery doesn't repair items.

Tadadadadat!

Armed martial artists rushed over. They were demons belonging to the Demonic Sect. Their level was low, but there were a few masters of the Fifth Stage of Mastery mixed in. They were equivalent to squad leaders. Judging by their appearance, their affiliated units were also different.

,...Did they come to arrest me for killing Little Cheonma-gun?

It was as Baek Ran had said. Since I killed Little Cheonma-gun, I would be arrested first. From now on, what I had to do was show off the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art so that they couldn't treat me carelessly. Since Little Cheonma-gun was dead, I would be the only successor.

,I just need to hold out until the elders and the heads of the Demonic clans come. The heads of the Demonic clans will support me.

There were many eyes watching here.

It was perfect. I would show the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art here. Then the fact that I had mastered the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art would spread throughout the Demonic Sect.

Just as I was about to use the Cheonma Shingong, Demonic martial art, the martial artists who had rushed over took a fist salute towards me.

,We greet Little Cheonma-nim!

,We greet Little Cheonma-nim!!!!

Their thunderous voices echoed throughout the city.

"......"

It hadn't even been 30 minutes since I killed Little Cheonma-gun. Even in the Demonic Sect, which followed the law of the jungle, could they treat me as Little Cheonma-gun so quickly?

,Impossible. These guys wouldn't arbitrarily recognize me as Little Cheonma-gun unless they were crazy.

Even if the elders had given them orders. And there were no elders in the current Demonic Sect who could give orders. The one who gave these guys orders was someone sitting in a higher position. A position that everyone would acknowledge without any objections.

,Did the Cheonma recognize me as Little Cheonma-gun?

Did Baek Ran expect this? No, if she had expected it, she would have said something. She was sparing in her words about the current Cheonma. It wasn't to hide information, but rather because she didn't know much about him.

,Well, good. It's not bad since things have become easier.

I smiled.

I spoke to the martial artists of the Demonic Sect who had given me a fist salute. Since I killed Cheon Yu-un, there was no reason to pretend to be Yeom Guseok.

,I am Little Cheonma Seong Yujin. The previous Little Cheonma-gun died by my hand. I proved my worth through strength.

Cheonma Shingong, demonic martial art (天魔神功), Cheonma Force (天魔 Force).

I scattered the Cheonma's energy, constricting people. It was especially effective against monsters and those who had mastered Demonic martial art. The martial artists who had given me a fist salute lowered their heads and dripped with sweat.

,Step aside. I must pay my respects to the Cheonma.

Like the Red Sea parting, people stepped aside. I walked slowly along that path. I continued to emit the Cheonma Force as I walked, so that they would remember who the new Little Cheonma-gun was.

Finally, when I arrived at the center of the Demonic Sect, in front of the Cheonma Palace where the Cheonma resided.

,Let's just say hello and come out. Since he recognized me as Little Cheonma-gun, he won't harm me.

As soon as the gatekeepers of the Cheonma Palace saw me, they bowed politely.

-Little Cheonma-nim. Currently, the Cheonma has entered seclusion and cannot meet with anyone.

I heard a telepathic message.

Seclusion. There were rumors about that. But it was the Cheonma who recognized me as Little Cheonma-gun. Suddenly saying that he had entered seclusion could only be seen as trying to avoid me.

,I have come to be recognized as the new Little Cheonma-gun.

-The Cheonma has recognized Little Cheonma-nim as Little Cheonma-gun and has formally appointed you. The Little Cheonma Palace is Little Cheonma-nim's.

,...Is that so?

I turned around. There was no need to force a meeting if he didn't want to meet me. I had gotten what I needed.
