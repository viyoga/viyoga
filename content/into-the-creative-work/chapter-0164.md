I found out the alliance's operation through hacking. It seems they're planning to attack at 1 PM, three hours from now.

They also know we ran into each other at the rest area and suspect we've formed an alliance.

,...I know it, so there's no need.."

,Just give up if it seems dangerous."

Han Ha-rin-yang glared at me, probably because my words rubbed her the wrong way.

,That won't happen. You just worry about yourself."

,Let's start. You need to be on time."

,...Worry about yourself. I can fly, so I'm fine, but you can't, right? That's seriously dangerous."

I smirked. Despite everything, Han Ha-rin-yang is worried about me. In the old days, she wouldn't have worried about me at all, let alone listen to me.

,It's okay. I have a plan."

We split up. She went north, and I went south. Four association employees each, who know our plan, are chasing after us.

,To use B-grade Hunter manpower in a place like this... How much are the association guys expecting from Dungeon Survival Reforged?'

Still, I'm sure it will be a hot topic if this is broadcast. There hasn't been a broadcast program like this since the first Dungeon Survival in Korea failed miserably.

,Shall we go then?'

I looked at the tablet's map and moved without hesitation. Han Ha-rin-yang will also look at the tablet she snatched from some Hunter bastard and move to the designated location.

The place I arrived at was W-7 area. It was a lake with a huge waterfall.

,Surface walking.'

Using the skill, I walked on the lake towards the waterfall. I could feel the temperature dropping as I got closer to the waterfall.

Beyond the waterfall, there was a cave half-submerged in water.

It's the treasure I got before. The real-time map shows the location of not only humans but also monsters.

Our survival points were running low, so we couldn't beat the Aqua Stone team even if we actively hunted monsters. Instead, we attacked Hunters to reduce their numbers.

,The real-time map shows not only normal monsters but also boss-level monsters.'

There's a boss monster in the cave beyond this waterfall.

I threw a stone I was holding in my right hand into the cave.

Peo-eok!

Instead of a splash, I heard the sound of something being hit.

Ku-geu-geu-geu.

The cave shook, and the waves of the lake became rough like waves. I kept my balance so I wouldn't fall and stared into the cave.

White squid tentacles stretched out. I didn't hesitate and turned around to run away.

,If I make a mistake, I'm fucked.'

Soon, it revealed itself.

A giant squid over 10M in size.

It's a C-grade boss monster, Little Kraken.

Krakens vary in size from individual to individual, and their grade and name are determined by their size. The S-grade boss monster, King Kraken, is a monster over 50M.

The Kraken hesitated as it tried to follow me.

,Was one stone not enough to aggro it?'

Pajijik.

I threw lightning at the Kraken. The Kraken, hit by the lightning, paused for a moment and then started glaring at me. It had eerie yellow eyes.

More than 12 thick tentacles wriggled and started swimming towards me across the lake.

,It's seriously pissed! Just as planned!'

I ran across the lake without looking back.

,Acceleration!'

[Acceleration is used. It lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 4]

The swimming Kraken was so fast that I had no choice but to use acceleration. After crossing the lake and reaching the shore, I turned around and looked at the Kraken. I was worried that the aggro would be released and it would return to the cave.

That didn't happen.

It's splashing and following me. I can feel its will to kill me.

,Haha. This way."

I picked up a nearby branch and threw it at the Kraken.

There was no way it could hurt it. It was intended to anger the Kraken. And that intention worked well.

The Kraken came up to the shore. I started running towards the forest.

,I need to adjust the distance appropriately here.'

The Kraken can move on land, but its speed is slow. It's clear that the Kraken will return to the lake as soon as it loses me.

I occasionally threw stones or branches at the Kraken to provoke it.

,Here it is. Squid."

The Kraken spewed acidic ink at me. I quickly stepped back. It didn't even take 3 seconds for the tree hit by the acidic ink to melt and disappear.

,Whoa. If I get hit head-on, even my bones will melt.'

The Kraken was terrifying. Its crawling speed was slow, but the power it possessed was monstrous enough to remind me of Superman. The Kraken doesn't avoid trees or stones that block its path. It charges straight ahead, smashing them and chasing after me.

,The thrill is amazing!'

Kung! Kung! Kung!

Just as it's difficult to distinguish between several dogs of the same breed, monsters also have a hard time distinguishing between humans wearing the same clothes.

I subtly check Han Ha-rin-yang's location. She's guarding the entrance to the rest area cabin as planned.

Kang Seok-su chose this place not only because of the distance and the wide view of the grassland, but also because of the rest area.

If things go wrong, they can escape to the rest area, which is a no-combat zone. He must be thinking that way.

If not only Kang Seok-su but also other Hunters enter the rest area, we could lose.

Our current survival points are -4825.

If any Hunter enters the rest area and doesn't come out until the end of Dungeon Survival, we will most likely lose.

Some are trying to go to the rest area, but the magic traps and the Basalt Golem are getting in the way. Han Ha-rin-yang is also guarding the entrance with her eyes wide open, so there seems to be no need to worry.

,My job is to kill the ones who are running away first. If I miss them and they hole up in the rest area in another area, things will go wrong.'

The priority is the ones who are running away, and the ones who are attacking from a distance.

,You had to do such a cowardly thing... Did you really have to go that far?!"

So they have no choice but to act to prevent casualties.

It's a kind of insurance.

Some quick-witted Hunters besides me will know that.

Kang Seok-su will eventually survive. That's how the situation is going. So I need to save the strength to face Kang Seok-su.

I saw it right.

This place is a mess right now. The alliance has already broken, and the Hunters are fighting among themselves. They've finally realized that the one who survives here will win.
