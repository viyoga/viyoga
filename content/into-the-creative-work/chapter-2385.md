Uncontrolled Tactical Seong Yujin. This was the public's assessment of me.

The battle ended without a winner or loser. When 3rd Unit Commander Yu Min-ha intervened during the battle, the Remegeton man shook off his position without regret and disappeared. The video ended there as well.

,This isn't a video uploaded by the Anti-Magic Unit, but by Remegeton.'

Would the Anti-Magic Unit be crazy enough to upload a video exposing their power to the internet? There's no possibility that a third party filmed it. They're not idiots, they would know if there was a camera filming them.

,Do they want to break Korea's morale by revealing that the Anti-Magic Unit isn't a big deal?'

Is there a need for that? Korea is a mess no matter who looks at it, right? Frankly, even during the broadcasting station incident, the damage suffered by the Anti-Magic Unit was almost non-existent compared to Remegeton's involvement. Even considering that the Anti-Magic Unit has potions.

,I still don't know what Remegeton's real purpose is. It's definitely not revenge for Sage, who died at my hands.'

I shook my head and checked the battle video again.

4th Unit Commander Park Son's magic is flower magic that doesn't match his muscular appearance. To be exact, it's magic that summons magical flowers.

The magical effect differs depending on the type of flower summoned. For example, sunflowers explode like grenades, and lilacs emit poisonous smoke. It's one magic, but at the same time, it's like 100 different magics.

,On the other hand, is the opponent's magic to temporarily create weapons?'

Sword, spear, shield, gun, bomb.

From cold weapons to modern firearms. He was handling them very skillfully. And he created hundreds of swords in the air and dropped them on his opponent. It's a bit much to call it psychokinesis. It feels more like shooting, so it's closer to ejection.

,......Wait. Ejection? Is it really magic that creates weapons?'

Feeling something strange, I focused more and looked at the video.

I looked closely at the weapons that Remegeton created, not the Remegeton magician.

,If you look closely, the weapons have signs of use. There are scratches and wounds. This shield even has a slightly broken bottom.'

It's not magic that creates weapons.

It's magic that brings weapons.

,In other words, there's a high possibility it's subspace magic. It's similar to the subspace I've been looking for.'

I felt my heart beating.

That magician used a large number of weapons. The capacity is much larger than my inventory.

,Weapons appear like summons, and used weapons are automatically unsummoned and disappear. Just like my item summoning skill. This is exactly the ability I want.'

Even now, due to the capacity pressure of the inventory, I have the inconvenience of having to go back and forth to other worlds unless it's a pre-designated item. I coveted the subspace that magician had.

,The source of magic in this world is the grimoire. If I kill that magician and steal the grimoire, I might be able to use that magic too. The probability is... less than 1%, but.'

Just because you have a grimoire and magic power doesn't mean you can use magic unconditionally.

,Even if it's less than 1%, there's still a possibility, so isn't it worth doing?'

I contacted Im Hyeon-a with a light heart.

"There's a guy I want to find."
