After passing the cliff, I discovered a tribe. And not a small one. Roughly estimating, it seemed to be a large tribe of over 3,000 people. It wouldn't be strange to consider it a small city.

,Why is such a large tribe in this outer region?'

In this kind of desert, large tribes exist in two scenarios. One is forming a city around a large oasis, or the tribesmen scatter and leave for other cities.

Most of the time, it's the latter. Even if there's an oasis, everything is poor here because it's a desert. Because it's a desert, farming is impossible.

,But they've settled here anyway... that means they've solved the food problem. Is there a lot of game?'

The infrastructure seems poor compared to its size. It seems they hardly interact with the outside world.

,A tribe that refuses interaction... I've heard of them, but this is the first time I've seen one in person.'

I ordered the undead army to advance.

3,000 people? That's not enough to stop my army. Even if the opponent had 10,000, I wouldn't lose.

,A surrender, huh.'

I was indifferent.

Just because the opponent surrenders doesn't mean I have to accept it.

Right now, I need corpses, and the map indicates that the Book of the Dead (3) is here. I can kill these bastards and leisurely find the Book of the Dead (3).
