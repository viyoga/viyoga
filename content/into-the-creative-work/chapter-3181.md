"So. If I want to become the Demon King, I need to find these things called Regalia? With zero clues? And not just one, but five?"

I glared at the system notification window. The system notification window offered no reply. It wouldn't even tell me what I could do once I collected the Regalia and became king. Unhelpful bastard.

*If you become a Hell Duke, this notification window must be what pops up. Are the other Hell Dukes also searching for Regalia? Statistically speaking, there's a high probability several of them already have some.*

This was information that hadn't been publicly disclosed. The Hell Dukes had kept their mouths shut, hoarding it for themselves. I would've done the same. If this information got out, it would only work against them.

Just because you needed to collect five Regalia didn't mean there were only five in existence.

There could be ten. Or twenty. There was no rule saying the King of Hell had to be unique.

*The fact that a King of Hell has never been born yet suggests the number of Regalia is probably fewer than twenty.*

Alexandra and Helbis approached and spoke.

"Master. The enemy forces have surrendered. None are resisting to the end. When the lord changes, they obey the new lord. The armies of Hell are convenient in that regard."

"Thanks to the war ending quickly, our losses are lighter than expected. We have roughly three hundred casualties, but… since we've gained enemy forces, you could say it's a net gain. The problem is that food consumption will increase significantly. Hell Ogres and Black Orcs consume far more rations than humans."

Helbis brought up money first. For an ordinary Hell Lord, this would've been a headache. An army doesn't sustain itself on manpower alone.

"We won our first war, so we'll be able to receive more support. Don't skimp on rations. No need to downsize the army either. Survey the newly acquired territories. There are no invasion plans for the next few days, so let the soldiers rest in shifts. I'm going to meet a gentlemanly demon."

• • •

I met the gentlemanly demon, Mammon. Not the real one, of course—just a proxy.

"Please, come in."

"Don't command me."

I walked slowly.

"Please, take your time."

"…Don't command me."

I closed the distance with quick strides. The demon looked at me with an expression of disbelief and set a glass of liquor on the table before me.

"A service for our VIP. Please, drink."

"Trying to get me piss-drunk and squeeze money out of me?"

"…I may be a miser, but I don't pull tricks on my customers."

"It was a joke."

I drank.

No notification window appeared saying my stats had increased. Must be ordinary alcohol. It tasted good, at least.

「The gentlemanly demon awaits your words.」

I paused while reading the notification.

The system notification window sometimes announces the presence of a proxy. When it does, the character (僞) is appended after the modifier. And the notification that had just appeared had no (僞) attached.

There were two possibilities. One: the message was sent by the true entity on the Divine Throne. Or two: the being before me wasn't a proxy but the genuine article.

I looked at the demon before me with a sour expression.

"…Why did you suddenly become the real one?"

"It's trendy these days, so I jumped on the bandwagon. I'm quite sensitive to trends, you see. Trends turn into money."

"Then you must know the trending dance these days is the Zero Two."

"The Zero Two has been obsolete for ages."

"Bullshit. The Zero Two is still going strong."

As if this demon bastard would understand human trends.

"On some Earth somewhere, the Zero Two is probably trending. Anyway, I heard the news that you've become a Hell Duke. Congratulations. No being has ever risen to Hell Duke in such a short time as you, customer. You've achieved something remarkable."

"It's been less than thirty minutes since I became a Hell Duke. How did you find out so fast? Did Helbis tell you?"

"Helbis may be my daughter, but she's not the type to give away information for free. Especially not now that she's working under you, customer. Helbis understands the importance of trust. You needn't worry about betrayal from her. The source of my information is a secret. It's information I don't sell."

"…Then let me change the question. Why did you come as your true body? What do you gain from it?"

"As I just answered. Trends turn into money."

He clearly had no intention of giving a straight answer.

"They say you need to collect the Regalia to become the King of Hell."

"That is the task of a Hell Duke. All current Hell Dukes have halted their conquest activities. They're searching for the Regalia of Hell."

"Wasn't it to invade the surface world?"

"That too, but the top priority of the Hell Dukes is the Regalia."

"…So the Hell Dukes aren't the cause of Ragnarok."

The demon's eyes widened as if genuinely startled.

"You struck the heart of the matter."

“…….”

To react like this over something so trivial… This bastard. Just how stupid did he think I was? I imagined what it would be like to fight Mammon. Just because he's the true body rather than a proxy doesn't mean he's free from the system's interference. If anything, being the true body means he's even more constrained by the system.

*Even so, winning wouldn't be easy. The name Mammon can't be just for show.*

I couldn't guarantee victory. Fighting itself would cost more than it gained.

*Let's let this one slide.*

I glared at Mammon and asked.

"What can you do once you become the King of Hell?"

"I don't know. Across all previous iterations of Atlantis, a King of Hell has never once been born. If you become the King of Hell, customer, you'll be the first in Atlantis's history."

"…To clear Atlantis, don't you have to conquer all the Hell zones too? And yet there's never been a King of Hell?"

"Even if you rule dozens of Hell zones, you cannot become the King of Hell without collecting the Regalia."

Conquering Hell zones and becoming the King of Hell were separate matters. In hindsight, that was obvious.

"Do you know where the Regalia are?"

"I only know clues about a few Regalia that the Hell Dukes haven't found yet. If you give me 5 million AP, I'll tell you."

"You're still doing business even now?"

"For your reference, this is information I haven't sold even to Hell Dukes. More precisely, it's information the proxies didn't know. This is your chance to get ahead of the curve, customer."

「Paying 5,000,000 AP.」

"Spit it out. I hope it's not disappointing."

"The Hell Raiment (地獄御衣) is in the possession of the Proxy of Distracted Delirium. Loki's location… I'll tell you if you pay an additional 3 million AP. The Hell Seal (地獄玉璽) is at the eastern edge of the Hell zones. The Hell Orb (地獄寶珠) is in the highest sky of Hell."

"Only three? After taking 5 million AP? Even if I find all three, I can't become the King of Hell."

"Haha. Some of the rest are held by Hell Dukes. If you pay 3 million AP, I'll tell you which Hell Duke holds which Regalia, and what abilities each Regalia possesses."

"You've got a real gift for business, I'll give you that. The Hell Dukes… I'll hold off on that."

If a Hell Duke possessed a Regalia, there was a chance rumors had spread. Something like possessing a special item similar to a Regalia. Using my subordinates to gather information without paying 3 million AP would be the cheaper route.

*If it comes down to it, I can just buy the information from this bastard.*

There was no rush.

"The value of information can rise with time."

"Or it could drop. I need to know Loki's location."

「Paying 3,000,000 AP.」

I'd spent 8 million AP just to get some information. Even receiving 40 million AP a month, it stung.

Mammon produced a map depicting the entirety of the Hell zones. He pointed to a zone at the eastern edge.

"Zone 9,141, Mahābalukma. The Proxy of Distracted Delirium is trapped in this hidden zone of Hell. He is no ordinary being—if you go too late, you won't be able to find him."

"If it's a hidden zone, there must be entry conditions."

"Who knows. I'm afraid I don't know that much."

"…I paid 3 million AP."

"I told you the exact location of the Proxy of Distracted Delirium, did I not? Well, Mahābalukma is the lowest among the Hells I've sold information on, so… it's probably somewhere in the Frozen Hell."

Tap. Tap.

Now that I thought about it, his finger had been pointing at the Frozen Hell zone. I'd assumed he was just pointing for show.

*…He was giving me the approximate location from the start. Dumb bastard. He could've just said it.*

I had the information, so I rose from my seat.

Detailed information about the remaining Regalia? If I asked, he'd demand another million-plus AP.

*It's not a price I can't pay, but he's so blatantly overcharging that I don't want to ask. I'll just use my subordinates to find out. There's a limit to how much you should gouge someone.*

It was a legitimate transaction, yet I felt swindled. Maybe that's what dealing with a demon does to you. I tried to leave without even a farewell.

"Please come again. I'll be waiting, customer."

"Don't command me. I'll do as I please."

"Yes. Do as you please."

• • •

I didn't return to the Hell zone base and headed straight east.

Among my subordinates was a flying demon beast, so I rode it. After several hours, I arrived at a Hell where everything was frozen solid.

「Zone 9,139, Frozen Hell.」

「The more lecherous you are, the more you freeze.」

「Your lecherousness has exceeded all bounds. One of your testicles has frozen.」

「A harsh chill attempts to freeze your body.」

A nasty cold crept up through my lower half. I reached into my crotch to check. One of my balls had turned into a ball of ice.

"You fucking baaaastaaaard!"

「The King of the Demon Realm is giggling.」

「The Master of the Firmament clutches his crotch and closes his eyes.」

The one silver lining was that, thanks to the effect of the Hero's Testicle, it still seemed to function.
