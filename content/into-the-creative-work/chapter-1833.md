My fist tingled.

It didn't hurt, though. More like a pleasant thrill. Reaching the Samjeong realm, my attacks were far more potent than I'd imagined.

,The Dragon Strike was originally meant to focus power into a single blow... but this kind of force?'

Not only had the amount of my inner energy increased, but its quality had also improved. Especially the speed at which I could unleash it, and the total amount I could use at once. My physical strength had naturally increased, and my stamina recovery rate probably had too.

,So this is why people strive to reach higher realms.'

I grinned, taking in the scene I'd just created.

A hole over 2 meters in diameter stretched in a straight line from the front gate to the main building. Of course, the main building also had a gaping hole in it. The building itself wasn't so shoddily built that it would collapse from that alone, but some people had been caught in the blast.

The three gatekeepers who dared to block my path, and two guys inside the main building. A total of five people had crossed the River Styx from a casual strike.

,I told you to bow your heads.'

I said in a cool voice. Milyeong, who was holding my left arm, waved her hand gracefully at the gatekeepers.

,Now, now, my 서방님 (seobangnim) has shown you mercy, hasn't he? Hurry up and bow your heads to the ground. Or do you want to die?'

Thump.

The gatekeepers all slammed their heads into the ground. Thankfully, they weren't completely clueless.

Milyeong and I walked through the gaping gate. I could feel eyes on us from all directions. But no one actually appeared before me.

We're two Samjeong realm masters. No matter how many Ogijeong realm experts there are, even ten of them can't take on two absolute masters at once. They must be testing the waters, afraid of dying if they step up now.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

Demonic energy spread in all directions, dominating the space. With a single step, I had subdued the Dong family. I could feel the exact location of those hiding.

,Keueok!'

,Keuk...!'

Those who were somewhat trained clutched their chests and collapsed to the ground. They couldn't withstand the pressure of the Cheonma Gunrimbo. These guys were the lucky ones. Those who hadn't learned martial arts simply fainted. Mostly servants who handled odd jobs.

,I'll count to ten. Come out before me and kneel.'

,One~'

As soon as I finished speaking, Milyeong started counting.

,Two~'

Her voice was playful. At least, that's how it sounded to me. How did the ones hiding feel? Probably like it was mockery aimed at them. And they didn't have the right to be offended. Because they were powerless.

,Three~'

An unexpected event occurred. One of the Ogijeong realm experts who had been hiding suddenly tried to escape. He seemed confident in his gyeonggong, as he was quite fast.

I frowned. He was still within my domain. I could use Cheonma Gunrimbo to transcend space and instantly sever his head. Just as I was about to step forward, Milyeong gripped my arm tightly.

Looking at her, I saw she had made a circle with her thumb and forefinger on her left hand. The space inside the circle distorted, and I could see the back of the fleeing martial artist's head. Milyeong giggled softly.

,I caught him?'

Blue flames erupted on the fleeing martial artist's body. The blue flames spread instantly throughout his entire body.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaaaaak!'

A horrifying scream echoed between Milyeong's fingers. The scream lasted for about 15 seconds before abruptly stopping. He was dead. Milyeong shook her hand. The faint smell of burning mixed with the blowing wind and disappeared.

,Four~'

There was a change. Some of those hiding couldn't withstand the pressure and came out. They walked over, sweating profusely, knelt, and prostrated themselves.

,Five~'

There were 9 Ogijeong martial artists remaining. Those bastards were stubbornly refusing to come out.

,I guess I can kill one of them. Meteor Sword.'

A sword appeared in the sky above the detached building next to the main building. The sword itself had a crude shape, but it was covered in demonic energy. I gestured with my chin, and the Meteor Sword fell toward the detached building.

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

With a deafening roar, the detached building collapsed instantly. The debris flew all the way to where I was. Milyeong lightly flicked her fingers, creating a barrier that blocked the debris.

At the spot where the Meteor Sword had fallen, there was a pile of collapsed rubble and a martial artist with his abdomen pierced by the Meteor Sword, dead.

,Six~'

Milyeong's beautiful voice rang out. I looked around. One wasn't enough, after all. Who else should I kill?

,Seven~ Eight~ Nine~'

Milyeong counted the numbers in succession.

The Ogijeong martial artists who had been hiding sprang out. Using gyeonggong to rush over, they immediately bowed prostrate before us.

,You could have come this quickly, so you came out before I counted the final ten? How naughty~'

Milyeong pointed her index finger at the Ogijeong martial artist who had arrived last and muttered an incantation.

Kwok.

Space distorted, twisting the martial artist's body. The martial artist, who had been kneeling and bowing his head, couldn't even put up a proper resistance and died with his body twisted. Blood and entrails spilled onto the floor.

Tremble, tremble, tremble.

None of those kneeling and bowing their heads weren't trembling. It was understandable. Milyeong had killed an Ogijeong realm expert horribly with just a single finger right in front of them. Even I, who knew it was magic, didn't understand what was going on. If I thought about fighting a sorcerer like Milyeong... my head started to ache.

,Who's the head of the Dong family?'

,I, I am, Elder-nim.'

A middle-aged man carefully raised his body. He was one of the seven surviving Ogijeong experts. Looking more closely, the clothes he was wearing were more luxurious than the others.

,Since we've come to visit the Dong family in person, the Dong family should hold a welcoming party. I'll give you an hour. Hold a banquet. But don't call any riffraff.'

,Ah, and I want to receive clothes and jewels as gifts. Got it?'

,Th, that's...'

The family head panicked and rolled his eyes. He was probably calculating what to do in his head. There probably wasn't a good solution. Two absolute masters of the Samjeong realm. They weren't beings that a backwater city like this could handle.

I broke the family head's kneecap. I could feel his shin bone fracturing. I didn't care. It wasn't my leg.

The family head forcibly suppressed a scream. He must have built up some experience as the family head, as he had some sense. If he had screamed and dirtied my ears, an absolute master, I would have cut off his right arm.

,The women over there have outstanding beauty. They are highly qualified to receive my grace. Wash them, dress them in fine clothes, and send them to me. You'll have to prepare delicious liquor and food with sincerity. Ah, and don't forget to send skilled courtesans.'

,Ah, I've put up a barrier around this area, so you won't be able to go outside without our permission. Don't even dream of doing anything strange. Hehe.'

Milyeong and I went up to the top floor of the main building. It was furnished with quite luxurious furniture, as if it were the family head's room. Of course, Milyeong and I weren't interested in furniture, so we threw it out the window. The wall was smashed in the process, but the cool sea breeze blowing in wasn't bad.

Oh, sir. There's some good spirit medicine here.

,What is it?'

,Snow ginseng. At this level, it's probably close to 500 years old? You know how good snow ginseng is for the body, right?'

Snow ginseng was among the highest-ranking spirit medicines. Its medicinal effects were outstanding even among spirit medicines of similar age. Also, if it was over 500 years old, it could even extend one's lifespan.

,You eat it.'

,...I thought you'd take it right away. Why are you giving it to me for once?'

,If it's snow ginseng over 500 years old, it extends your lifespan, right? You're older than me, so you should eat it.'

,Ah, really! Do you want to get hit?!'

Milyeong clenched her fist and ran at me. Surprisingly, her stance was decent. Just because she was a sorcerer didn't mean she couldn't learn martial arts... she seemed to have learned basic martial arts. But that was only for a sorcerer.

I pushed away Milyeong's fist with my palm and bent down to hug Milyeong's waist. Naturally, I buried my face in her chest. The soft, warm sensation felt through her clothes was felt through my face. The scent that stirred a man's heart was a bonus.

Burying my face in this chest was 100 times better than acting like a master.

,Aang. Isn't doing it here a bit much?'

Unlike her words, she was pressing down on the back of my head.

Just before I became intoxicated with her, Milyeong pushed my head away slightly.

,That's true.'

I answered, hiding my disappointment. Because I knew that Milyeong would be panting in front of me when night came. She was my woman.

,Then shall we decorate a little?'

Milyeong used magic. She blocked the broken wall with a wall made of magic. The space in the room expanded greatly.

I was curious and asked about the principle. She explained that she twisted the space axis by overlapping barriers and so on, but I didn't understand anything.

High-quality flooring was laid on the floor, and lights were installed on the ceiling, flashing brightly. She took out a large table in the center of the room. I sat down in what seemed to be the seat of honor. Milyeong sat to my right and leaned against me.

Soon after, servants began to carry food and liquor.

,Ah~ say ah.'

,Ah~'

Milyeong put meat in my mouth. Chewing on the texture, it was roasted suckling pig.

,How is it?'

Milyeong was expecting something. She probably wasn't expecting me to say it was delicious because she fed it to me... Knowing my picky taste, she might have wanted me to overturn the table.

,It's delicious?'

,...Really?'

There was no reason to lie. It was really delicious. Since it was the family that ruled this city, the head chef in charge of the kitchen was probably the best in the city.

Milyeong ate a piece of meat and nodded.

,Well, not bad skills. Then how about the liquor.'

, It's Yeoa Hong.'

Yeoa Hong was a high-end liquor. I drank a glass poured for me by Milyeong. It wasn't bad.

,But why aren't the women coming up? Did they run away?'

,That's not it. No one has left the barrier without permission. It's not a barrier that can be easily broken through.'

Soon after, the women came up.
