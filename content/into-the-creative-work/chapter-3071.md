When an attack authority targeting a nation rather than an individual is used, a system notification pops up across the entire world.

『Pakistan has used an Entry Ban on South Korea.』

『Pakistan has used a Physical Weakening Curse on South Korea.』

『Pakistan has used Silence on South Korea.』

It wasn't the first time the world had seen a national attack notification. In the early days when the Tower first appeared and the world was plunged into extreme chaos, war had broken out in the Middle East. Back then, attack authorities flew back and forth every single day.

Most of the world's population didn't take the news of war between obscure Middle Eastern nations all that seriously, even if it made them uneasy.

But the weight carried by the name "South Korea" couldn't even be compared to back then.

South Korea was no weak nation. It was now firmly recognized as a developed country, and recently it had been raising its stock even further by partnering with the United States in Tower distribution. Above all, South Korea's military power ranked fifth in the world.

"Has Pakistan lost its goddamn mind?"

"Why the hell would Pakistan attack South Korea without even a declaration of war? On what grounds?"

The world watched with bated breath, nerves stretched taut. If South Korea and Pakistan entered full-scale war, the flames could spread across East Asia.

The nations of East Asia. China, Japan, Russia, North Korea.

With the exception of North Korea, all of them were great powers. Their military strength ranked at the very top globally. Even North Korea, that infamous dictatorship everyone loved to mock, ranked high when measured purely by military might. There was a reason people said that if war broke out in East Asia, World War III might follow.

"Uwaaaaaaah! What are you doing! America! Step up for peace!!"

"UN! Stop South Korea and sanction Pakistan!"

"You idiot! The UN can't do shit when reality actually hits! They did nothing during the Middle East war either!"

Unlike the few who were running around in a panic, there were those who analyzed the situation with cold detachment.

"The national attack authorities Pakistan used are a bit strange. For something meant for warfare, it's at a level where it barely has any element of surprise."

"Pakistan is a nuclear state and ranks above North Korea in military power. However, there's no news of the Pakistani military mobilizing."

"...These aren't attack authorities meant for attacking South Korea. This is an assassination."

"Pakistan is a well-known pro-China nation. China had 20% of its coin acquisition rate stolen by the Sex King. In fact, within China, they regard the Sex King as an irreconcilable enemy."

"The likelihood of war breaking out is low."

Pakistan's President Adran issued an official statement with unusual speed.

"This use of attack authorities against South Korea was not Pakistan's will. It was carried out by certain terrorists holding Pakistani nationality who deliberately targeted South Korea. Pakistan has no intention whatsoever of attacking South Korea. We will provide all information related to this incident to the South Korean side. Pakistan stands for peace."

The possibility of war between Pakistan and South Korea diminished.

"A head of state has the authority to identify who used an attack authority when their own citizen uses one. The culprit will be revealed immediately."

"Weren't attack authorities supposed to be managed by national governments? How could an individual possess attack authorities?"

"They must have deceived the government and smuggled them out."

"Smuggled out enough national attack authorities that even an A-rank Hunter would be lucky to see one in their entire lifetime—and used three in a row? Does that even make sense? And those three seem connected, don't they?"

"Someone may have provided them from behind the scenes."

It wasn't hard to guess who that someone was.

No matter what, the most important thing was South Korea's response. It was no exaggeration to say that South Korea currently stood at the center of the world. Depending on how South Korea reacted, the entire world would shake.

• • •

Lee Yeonhee had been staying at the Hunter Management Bureau. She hadn't gone home for three days. She hadn't slept either. Thanks to the Seon-ui-ju that Seong Yujin had given her, her body remained fresh even after pulling all-nighters for days. Just carrying it on her person restored her body. It was the finest treasure.

Lee Yeonhee hadn't panicked even when Pakistan used attack authorities to strike South Korea.

When she heard belatedly about the assassination attempt on Seong Yujin. When she learned the identity of the assassins—China's hunting dogs, called the Black Wolves—she wasn't surprised either.

Of course not. She had predicted China would make a move.

"You look like you have a lot on your mind."

Lee Yeonhee raised her head. At some point, a woman with blue bobbed hair had appeared by the window.

"Lady Elena. What brings you here? If you had contacted me, I would have come to you."

"It's a sort of stroll. And Yujin has made his move."

"His Excellency said so directly? China? Or Pakistan?"

"China."

"...It would be troublesome if the Chinese President died suddenly. And before that, someone at the level of a head of state would have an enormous number of defensive measures—it won't be easy."

"He said he has no intention of killing. But let me give you one piece of advice. Don't touch China. South Korea cannot yet handle China. America? Those bastards will ultimately make decisions benefiting their own interests when the critical moment comes."

"...Yes. I know that too. That's precisely why I have so many concerns. However, if South Korea just lets this slide, the world—China—will look down on South Korea."

"Didn't I tell you Yujin has stepped in? Since it's unofficial, China won't be able to openly protest about it. They used Pakistani nationality precisely to avoid the worst-case scenario. If they started it first, they must have been prepared to take a hit."

"...Understood. China is uninvolved in this matter, then. I'll handle things quietly with Pakistan."

"Demanding coins is a third-rate move."

"Yes. I'm aware. Coins are the people's lives, after all. Moreover, coins are difficult to trade secretly. It would be better to receive dollars instead. Pakistan is also famous for its copper—receiving copper wouldn't be bad either."

"And the South Korean President?"

"Still the same. There's no sign of Lady Elena's brainwashing breaking."

"Don't trust brainwashing too much. I'll be keeping an eye on it as well, but brainwashing can unravel in an instant for the most absurd reasons."

"From my perspective, I don't think that will happen, but I'll keep your advice in mind."

This was the reason the President did nothing even as Lee Yeonhee openly made political moves.

• • •

The reward from ranking first in this 15th-floor challenge was a National Affiliation Change Authority. He had obtained one from the 11th-floor challenge reward, and now he'd obtained another from the 15th-floor challenge.

『National Affiliation Change Authority

Grade: Unique

Changes national affiliation.』

Why the hell was this thing Unique?

One could certainly say that.

However, in this world, changing national affiliation was no easy task. To change national affiliation, one needed permission from both the nation being left and the nation being joined. That permission was the authority of the head of state.

In a way, it was a natural measure. If nationality could be changed easily, Hunters coming from outside could cause havoc from within. Like the Chinese assassins with Pakistani affiliation in this very incident.

'If I use this National Affiliation Change Authority, I can change my national affiliation without any procedures. It means I don't need permission from a head of state.'

It was perfect for operating as a spy, or for raising hell.

『Using the National Affiliation Change Authority.』

『National affiliation changes from South Korea to China.』

『The 30% coin acquisition rate increase effect changes from South Korea to China.』

"Yī èr sān sì."

I became Chinese.

Immediately, I tore a Spatial Teleportation Scroll and moved to Beijing, China's capital. Beijing was in the dark hours of early dawn. Due to the time difference, Beijing's time was one hour behind South Korea's.

I took out the Ghost Cloak from my inventory and put it on, then moved to Zhongnanhai. Zhongnanhai had a defensive barrier set up that prevented foreigners from entering. That was the reason I'd used the National Affiliation Change Authority.

The security was fierce. Armed military forces guarded the perimeter of Zhongnanhai, and Hunters of at least B-rank or higher protected the interior with ironclad vigilance.

The security detail staying with the President was an A-rank Hunter, no less. With the Ghost Cloak's effect, I could conceal my presence, but...

'There's a barrier. A different kind of barrier from those made with magic or shamanism. A barrier created with a Hunter's ability?'

What kind of barrier was it?

My current physical abilities were too weak to pull out Stormbreaker and instantly analyze the barrier with Thunder Eye Calm. For now, I observed the barrier quietly.

One of the security agents, who had been utterly motionless, moved swiftly. He'd detected a single small rat entering the barrier's interior like a ghost. I used the blowing wind to toss a leaf. The security agent detected the leaf just as ghost-like and picked it up and disposed of it.

'So it detects all objects entering the barrier? But it doesn't react to leaves falling from inside the barrier.'

[Using Lightning Step.]

I moved to a place where electricity flowed through the building's internal wiring. The security agents didn't move.

I found President Wang Jieze's office. The door was closed, so I couldn't enter inside.

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

I peered through the wall into the office interior. The office was thick with cigarette smoke, and Wang Jieze was shouting into a phone receiver clutched in his hand.

"Damn it! I said find out why that bastard suddenly became Chinese!!"

A 30% increase in coin acquisition rate. Wang Jieze wasn't thanking his sudden fortune—he was suspicious and throwing a fit.

I held back the urge to slit Wang Jieze's throat. If I killed him now, China would descend into chaos, and that influence would spread beyond South Korea to the entire world.

'The chaos of this world isn't what I want. But he tried to kill me—I can't just let that slide. Instead, I'll tear out some compensation.'

There was a wall between us, but the distance between me and Wang Jieze was within 10 meters. The condition was met.

'Coin Plunder!'

I plundered 50% of the coins Wang Jieze possessed.

『Coin Plunder Ring

Grade: Unique

Plunders 50% of the target's coins and stores coins without limit.

The target to plunder coins from must be within 10 meters.

A target whose coins have been plundered can be plundered again after 10 days.

Currently stored coins: 1,100,000,000

Remaining uses: 9』

“……!!”

1.1 billion coins were stored.

It was an amount of coins that far exceeded my expectations.

'An individual was holding 2.2 billion? Enough coins for 50 million South Koreans to survive for 22 days—held by one person?'

China's population was 1.4 billion. Thinking about it that way, 2.2 billion was enough coins for China to survive only a single day.

'Even if you only squeeze one dollar from each of China's 1.4 billion people, that's 1.4 billion—but South Korea only amounts to 50 million dollars.'

I felt the absurd difference in scale, the continental magnitude, in my bones.

I took out a Spatial Teleportation Scroll from my inventory and saved the coordinates.

'Heh heh. Just in case, I should save the coordinates. I might come back later to squeeze out more coins, and when the time is right, I could assassinate Wang Jieze.'

Thanks for the meal.

I returned to South Korea.
