Disguised, Baekran and I blended into the crowd, wandering through the market. First, we needed to grasp the market's layout.

The market was much larger than it appeared, and the crowd grew thicker as time passed. Most people were martial artists, with some sorcerers mixed in.

,About 2,000 people, I guess.,

It seemed too many, but when I thought about the scale of this world, I figured it was normal. Humans were abundant in this world.

,Looking at those merchant bastards makes my hands itch.,

If there weren't so many people around, I would have killed the merchants and looted all the goods and Heavenly Jade from their stalls. But there were too many people to get away with it.

,Did you notice?,

Baekran, who was next to me, asked.

,Notice what?,

,The market space here is subtly divided. Look. That bend in the road. Doesn't it feel narrower than the others? And that shop over there is slightly pushed forward. It means there's an invisible, subtly hidden space in the center here.,

Baekran pointed here and there, explaining. Honestly, I didn't understand a word she was saying.

,What the hell is she?,

The road is narrower than other places? Who walks around paying attention to the width of the road?

The shop is pushed forward? The shops here were similar yet different. How would I know if it was pushed forward or set back?

,Can't act like I didn't understand, how embarrassing.,

Fortunately, I had the ability I needed at this moment.

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

Things that were invisible began to appear.

In the end, Baekran was right. There was a hidden space in the center of the market. In that space, there was a building. It was a building that looked like it had been hastily made by modifying a carriage. Strange pictures and shapes were drawn on the outer walls, and 24 magical artifacts were embedded in the ceiling. A sign was hanging in front of the building's door.

,Baekran. You're right. The main branch of the Yuun Trading Company is in the hidden space.,

,You sound so sure. Can you see the hidden space?,

I hesitated and shook my head.

,I can't see it. It would be nice to have such a convenient ability.,

Baekran didn't reveal her hand. So, I didn't need to reveal mine either.

,It feels like she's subtly digging for information. The way to attract these women is to not reveal all your cards.,

If I could attract her interest in a good way, it would turn into affection. At that point, half of the conquest would be over.

,So. What are we going to do now?,

Charge in and kill them.

That's probably not the answer she wants. There's still too little information. I focused my eyes. I used Clairvoyance, one of the Heavenly Eye's abilities. There was nothing inside the building. It was empty. There wasn't even a common chair or desk.

,Is this not their base...?,

Even if we could get into that isolated space, we wouldn't be able to get anything even if we went.

I looked around. There were still many people, and the merchants of the Yuun Trading Company were still operating while hiding their true colors. But time continued to pass. It was darker than before. The sun was setting.

,...First of all, there's too little information. We need to catch a suitable bastard and extract information.,

,Ah, that's pointless.,

,Why?,

,I've already tried it. There's a seal on their heads. When I tried to forcibly extract information about the Yuun Trading Company, their minds went blank.,

,......,

Should I just go wild right now? I said, feeling a strong urge.

,We secretly kill the merchants one by one. And we also take what they have. Maybe there's something among their possessions that could be information.,

It was no different from saying I wanted to loot. Baekran didn't object to my words at all and nodded.

,Okay.,

She's too compliant. Should I have just said let's go wild?

• • •

,Hehehe.,

The elixir merchant checked a heavy pouch inside the stall, where customers couldn't see, and smiled sinisterly.

,With this much profit, even after giving it to the Yuun Trading Company, my share will be...,

I approached the bastard who was muttering in joy. Because I had completely suppressed my presence, the bastard didn't notice I was right behind him. I quietly drew my sword.

He flinched.

The merchant seemed to sense something and turned around. Too late. My sword had already delivered a death sentence to him.

Swish!

The merchant's head rolled on the floor. I acquired the pouch the merchant was holding.

It contained 24 Heavenly Jades. A smile spread across my face at the considerable income.

Adding these, I now have a total of 663 Heavenly Jades. I only need 37 more to raise my VIP level.

,Would it be too embarrassing to ask Baekran to lend me 37?,

I turned around at the rustling sound. Baekran was rummaging through the shop, looking for elixirs. She gave me her usual smile when our eyes met.

,Since you have the Heavenly Jade, I'll take the elixirs. They're all low-grade, so I'm at a loss.,

,Those elixirs wouldn't mean anything to you.,

,Do you know how to extract the maximum efficiency from low-quality elixirs? By making them into spiritual pills.,

,You know how to make pills?,

,I used to study it. It's such a brutal world that I couldn't leave precious elixirs in someone else's hands. Well, it wasn't that difficult for me. I only had to study for a few years.,

I made a strange expression. Didn't she say she was younger than Yeom Guseok? But she mastered those skills and even learned pill making? While mastering sorcery and disguise?

,Is she a genius on the level of Yuria, the best in the world?,

Thump!

Suddenly, pressure crushed my entire body. It felt like I had been thrown from land into the deep sea. Baekran and I looked at each other.

,It seems like their harvest has begun.,

She, who had put all the Medicinal herbs into the spatial storage, said calmly.

,In this case, we don't have to go wild openly. Let's quietly observe the situation first.,

We quietly went outside. It seemed that we weren't the only ones who felt the strangeness, as the people walking around the market were also bewildered.

,Suddenly, my body feels heavy. Is there something wrong with the barrier?,

,This feels bad. What are those Yuun Trading Company bastards doing with the barrier management?,

,Hey! Look at the moon in the sky! It's a blood-red moon! Doesn't it feel ominous?,

,How can the moon be red? It's probably the effect of the barrier.,

,Hey, merchant! Where is the head of the Yuun Trading Company? I want to have a serious conversation with him!,

,This is ominous. I need to get out of here!,

The market was in an uproar due to the sudden change.

Baekran looked at the red moon in the night sky and said quietly.

,Be careful.,

Before I could ask what she meant, someone appeared and stomped their foot. Kwang! The shockwave that started from his foot swept around. The murmuring people closed their mouths and looked at the suddenly appearing man.

,Quiet!,

He was a martial artist with a stern and serious face.

,We will now begin the selection. Stay still!,

The man was a master who had reached the realm of Ogi, so most people kept their mouths shut and watched his every move. However, some people stepped forward and argued. A man in fancy clothes was a prime example. He hadn't even reached the realm of Chulji, but he was confident despite his weakness.

,I am a member of the Yoon family, who manages Bae Young-ssi! How dare you threaten me?! Let me meet the head of the Yuun Trading Company! I must thoroughly investigate this matter!,

He looked at the member of the Yoon family with emotionless eyes.

,A husk.,

,What?,

,Even fancy clothes can't hide your inferiority and incompetence.,

The man waved his hand. It seemed like a light wave, but there was a mystery embedded in that hand movement. It was a considerable hand technique.

Pak!

The head of the man in fancy clothes exploded. The merchants who were at the stalls came out, dragged the corpse to the side, and cleaned it up.

The man still looked at the others with emotionless eyes.

,Next.,

The people trembled with fear.

The man selected people one by one. If they didn't meet the standard, he smashed their heads, and if they met the standard, he restrained them with Pressure points and magical artifacts. Even if the martial artists resisted, he didn't kill them recklessly. The merchants nearby followed and caught those who ran away. I watched the approaching bastard and asked Baekran.

,There seems to be a standard for selection. Do you know what it is?,

,I think it's martial talent. Look over there. He's sparing those who show promise even if they attack him. And he's also sparing those who have reached the realm of Chulji stage 5 or higher.,

Screams could be heard from a short distance away. Probably similar things were happening in other areas.

,How's your condition?,

,Not the best. I can still feel the pressure.,

,The moment that bastard comes in front of us, we attack immediately. If we cooperate, we can kill that Ogi master in an instant.,

,Hmm. Are you going to reveal yourself after disguising yourself so well? Getting caught on purpose is also one way.,

,That's too dangerous. And we have this opportunity because we're disguised.,

The Hong family, who were in cahoots with that bastard, knew our faces. If we hadn't disguised ourselves, they would have tried to kill or capture Baekran and me as a top priority.

,It's much more beneficial if we can reduce the number of Ogi realm masters by one.,

The man who had selected 30 people approached us. The man twitched his eyebrows as he looked at me.

,You're ambiguous. I'll have to see your skills.,

He raised his hand without warning. I clicked my tongue inwardly. I wanted him to completely let his guard down, but that was out of the question.

I grabbed the hilt of my sword and took a stance to draw it. His eyes widened. As expected of an Ogi. He seemed to realize something was wrong. Before the bastard could react, I drew my sword. At the same time as the sword came out of its sheath, it was engulfed in demonic energy.

Ghost Cutting Sword Technique (斬鬼刀法) Evil Slash (惡斬).

The bastard hurriedly gathered his energy to protect his hand and grabbed my sword. The bastard's white hand aura flickered like a candle.

,So you're the Lightning Flash Sword Yeom Guseok that Hong Gang-hu mentioned. I didn't expect you to be hiding like a rat.,

,If you want to pretend to be calm, hide the blood coming out of your mouth. I know you injured yourself internally by forcibly gathering your energy just now.,

,Hmph. Your sword is caught in my hand. A swordsman who can't swing his sword. It's as good as over.,

I smiled and said.

,There's a sword going in next to you.,

The bastard just stared at me. He seemed to think what I said was a lie. That was the bastard's miscalculation. Baekran's sword stabbed into the bastard's neck. The killing sword, which had completely suppressed its presence, was exquisite and fatal.

The bastard checked the sword in the corner of his vision and forcibly twisted his body. In that instant, he created a protective aura to protect his neck from being cut.

,He's pushing himself again.,

He had no choice. He would have died if he hadn't pushed himself. The price for pushing himself was a severe internal injury, and the bastard's protective aura quickly faded. I didn't miss that moment.

,Meteor Sword.,

There was no need to drop the Meteor Sword from high in the sky. 10 Meteor Swords were instantly created around the bastard and stabbed into his defenseless body.

Thwack!

,Keuk!,

The bastard, with swords embedded all over his body, looked at me as if in vain and fell to the floor. The Meteor Swords disappeared into dust as if they had finished their job.
