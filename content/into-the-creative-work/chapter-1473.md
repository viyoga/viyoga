Long ago, evil ran rampant.

Evil seduces, deceives, and devours humans.

It's hard.

Paladin was a game famous for its hellish difficulty.

The map is full of traps, and even a slight mistake can lead to death by mobs. The viciousness of the boss monsters goes without saying.

However, the high difficulty was a popular element of the game. Other elements besides difficulty, such as the story, graphics, and music, were all praised as perfect.

,What's important to me right now isn't Paladin's gameplay. It's the Incarnation, the quest reward.'

[Incarnation

Summons and recreates yourself from the desired ending world.

The duration and ability reproduction of the Incarnation depend on the total stats.

Price: 10,000 points

※Caution

All applied penalties are also reproduced.]

,So, I can reproduce my power from the world where I saw the ending?'

For example, it means I can summon the emperor of the 'Tyrant for You' world and use that power.

,Even if the ability reproduction varies depending on the total stats... If I have the emperor's power, I might be able to kill all those goddamn bastards and break through.'

There's also an ending called 'Kill Gay King'. In that world, I became a truly omnipotent god. Of course, I don't think I can fully reproduce myself from that world with my total stats.

,What I should pay attention to is the price of the Incarnation. 10,000 points. It's not a burdensome price for me right now. Once I get it, I can buy it again from the random draw shop... It would be good to get it now.'

I didn't know when I could get it again if not now.

Random draw?

There's a higher chance it won't come out even if I invest 100,000 points.

,I'm not in a position to be picky about this and that right now... Let's do it.'

,Origin. It's the same as the beginning of the game.'

In Paladin, stats vary depending on the origin. The content of the game doesn't change much, but the choices and NPC reactions are also affected by the origin.

,You can say it's like choosing a difficulty level. The origins that receive stat bonuses are Monk, Free Knight, Paladin....'

Monk has the best stats. In particular, Monks start with a weapon called 'Monk's Iron Mace' at the beginning. It's a decent weapon that can be used until the mid-game.

,But it's not just advantages.'

Monks have quite a few restrictions on their choices. They cannot refuse the orders of the Order, and they cannot attack NPCs recklessly.

,If those restrictions are applied as they are in the Play World... Choosing a Monk could be the worst choice.'

However, I had no intention of choosing an origin with weak stats. I didn't choose this quest to enjoy the Play World. My goal right now is to clear the quest as quickly and efficiently as possible.

,Excluding the Monk... I have to choose between Free Knight, Paladin, Noble, and Errand Boy.'

Among these, the most moderate and recommended origin for beginners is the Free Knight. The stats are decent, there are few restrictions to follow, and they are not ignored by NPCs. Next are Noble and Paladin. The stats are decent and they are revered by NPCs, but like Monks, there are restrictions to follow. Errand Boys have few restrictions, but are ignored by NPCs.

,I'll go with Paladin, who has the best stats after Monk. Paladins don't have as many strict things to follow as Monks... and they can refuse the Order's unfair requests.'

I was in the darkness.

My consciousness is awake, but my body is submerged in darkness and does not move.

1 hour. 2 hours. 3 hours....

Time keeps flowing.

The empty darkness seemed to be eating away at my mind.

Do I have to stay submerged in the darkness like this? Until when? Wouldn't it be better to just die?

It was around the time I started to feel fear in the darkness.

Light fell from the sky. The warm golden light shines directly on me. The darkness disappears and my body moves. I reached out as if to grab the light. Of course, I couldn't grab the light.

-Yujin-gun. My faithful servant.

A sacred voice was heard. I immediately knelt down on the spot.

"Heri-anne-nim, Goddess!"

-The end times are approaching. You will illuminate this world as a ray of light.

Light permeated my body.

I bowed my head and simply accepted the light.

The great being disappeared.

Three paths appeared before me. I stood blankly in front of the three paths.

,...The beginning of the game.'

I was briefly confused as I accepted the memories and experiences of the Paladin. I gathered my senses and looked around the three paths.

Three paths. I have to choose one of these and move forward. At the end of each path, there was a different skill.

,You can get other skills in the later part, but... In the early part, a lot changes depending on what choice you make.'

If you walk the left path, you can get the skill 'Perfect Body'. As you can tell from the name, it is a skill related to physical abilities. With Perfect Body, the difficulty of the early part becomes easier. But it doesn't mean much in the mid-to-late game because the boss monsters are so strong.

The middle path allows you to obtain 'Wings of Dawn'. Most people choose these wings. This is because mobility becomes incredibly good.

The right path allows you to obtain 'Holy Eye'. It doesn't help in combat. But if you have this, you can distinguish between demons disguised as humans and the corrupted. Unlike the other two skills, it does not grow.

I chose the right path.

,Being able to distinguish demons with the Holy Eye means... There are more enemies I can kill.'

There was a semi-transparent notification window in front of me.

It was the interface of the original game.

[Name: Yujin

Origin: Paladin

Level: 1

Strength: 7 Agility: 5 Stamina: 5 Divinity: 2

Skills: Holy Aura (Lv.1)]

The game system was being applied.

Experience is needed to raise levels and stats. And the only way to get experience is to kill enemies.

,Find those bastards with the Holy Eye, kill them, and grow.'

At the end of the road, a blue light was sparkling in the air. I reached out to the light without hesitation.

[Holy Eye (Lv. Master) dwells within you.]

My eyes burned hotly.

"Keueueuk!"

I covered my eyes with my left hand and writhed. The pain was enormous. To the point where I regretted my choice for a moment. The pain slowly subsided.

-Go to the Valentiere Cathedral. That child will lead you....

The voice of Heri-anne-nim, Goddess, rings out.

And I opened my eyes.

I jumped up from the old bed. And slowly look around.

The room is small and old. There was a faint but foul smell. My sword and armor were placed next to the bed. I stared blankly at the ceiling for a moment, recalling the memories of the Paladin and the memories of the original game.

I became a Paladin two years ago.

I was sold as a slave and taken in by an Inquisitor at the age of 5. There was no special reason. I was recognized for my talent. I trained for 20 years and became a Paladin two years ago. I am currently staying in a rural village called Golti on a mission.

The mission I received was to find and kill the demon near the village.

,I was destined to fight demons and die as a Paladin, but...'

My destiny has now changed.

I became a Paladin after receiving a revelation from Heri-anne-nim, Goddess. A divine message must have been sent to the Vatican as well.

I got out of bed.

What I had to do was clear.

Kill demons and the corrupted and become stronger. After becoming as strong as possible, kill the archdemons. Fortunately, I knew all the locations of the archdemons and the powers they possessed.

I got out of bed and reached for the armor, but frowned.

,What is this trash-like armor?'

Some of the armor is dented and even smells. It feels like I'll get sick the moment I put it on. As I looked at the armor, I immediately remembered.

,It's the armor that the senior Paladin used to wear....'

The senior Paladin was ambushed and killed by a ghoul. I inherited his armor and sword. In this world, just wearing full plate armor puts you in the very wealthy class, but....

,There's no need for me to wear this kind of trash.'

I recall the penalties of this world. Stats decreased and the Absolute Spirit changed. That was all. I can use weapons and armor from other worlds.

,Stormbreaker.'

Geochang appeared before my eyes.

I send my will to Stormbreaker. Stormbreaker disassembles into pieces and sticks to my body, becoming armor.

I looked at my hand wearing the gauntlet and clenched my fist.

The power I feel is weak. The durability of the armor doesn't seem as strong as I thought.

,I can't help it. Stormbreaker's performance is affected by the wearer's strength.'

I was much weaker now than I was in reality.

,Hwaryeon Bido.'

It was the moment I held Hwaryeon Bido in my hand.

Pajik!

Red lightning arose.

The strength in my hand was released. Hwaryeon Bido fell to the floor with a thud.

,...Is it rejecting me? Why?'

I don't know even if I rack my brain. There are several possible reasons. First, because I am a Paladin. Second, the repulsion with my divinity. Third, the Holy Spirit.

,If you don't like it, fine.'

I reverse-summon Hwaryeon Bido and pick up the Paladin's sword. There are many other excellent swords in my inventory. Among them, there is also a sword with the power of exorcism.

,The Paladin's sword isn't weak either. It's a sword specially forged to kill demons.'

A small mirror was placed on the side table next to me. It was a mirror of poor quality, but it reflected me properly.

There I was, wearing silver full body armor. The sword in my hand was a greatsword so large that it could easily split a person in half. Since I wasn't wearing a helmet, my face was clearly visible. I looked in the mirror and used the Holy Eye.

Kieeeing.

Divinity gathers in my eyes.

The two black pupils turn blue, and a cross pattern with outstretched wings appears in the center of the pupils.

I closed my eyes and opened them. The pupils returned to their original black color.

,It's just like the original game.'

I took the Paladin's sword and a small bag and went out of the room.
