Park Hae-gil, who became an insect after getting the evolution serum and ran away from Seongak Elementary School.

He was currently in China. He quietly breathed in a dark cave where no sunlight entered.

His appearance had changed from before. He had four arms and four legs each, insect wings on his back, and hard antennae on his head. His torso was protected by a carapace that looked like he was wearing thick armor, and his mouth resembled an ant's jaw.

The only parts that could be said to be similar to humans were his nose and eyes.

It was the result of evolution.

At first, it was disgusting, but now he didn't feel anything.

Basseurak.

Insects came around him and rubbed their heads against his legs, showing affection. It was still an immature creature. In a few days, it would become an adult and run out of this cave.

The insect's antennae trembled. Park Hae-gil's antennae also trembled. The sub-adult insect's antennae emitted a special wavelength. Park Hae-gil's antennae accepted it. The insect was expressing its will that it was hungry.

Park Hae-gil threw the insect some meat that was nearby. Was it a human? It didn't matter.

He evolved by receiving various help from Chinese bionics in exchange for cooperating with China. In the process, his humanity decreased.

,The hormones coming from the body are different, so the emotions felt by humans are different.'

The bionics said that the more they studied living things, humans, the more they realized the greatness of hormones. It was content that Park Hae-gil didn't care about now.

Park Hae-gil went deeper into the cave, into the hatchery.

Slowly wriggling eggs were attached to the ground and walls everywhere.

Park Hae-gil went further inside. There was a giant insect that looked like it was made by clumping together 5m-sized lumps of flesh. It was the queen. It was called the queen, but its role was just an egg-laying machine. It was an individual evolved through the genetic material of termite queen ants.

When the queen spewed out eggs containing genetic information, Park Hae-gil sprinkled his sperm on the eggs. The fertilized eggs would hatch in a few days.

The queen eats the food brought by small insects to replenish her nutrition.

Park Hae-gil went out of the cave after finishing his daily routine.

An owl-sized insect flew and sat on his shoulder. They exchanged information through their antennae.

A scene was drawn in his head like a video.

,North Korea is starting to move in earnest. I guess we can know how much we can handle with our current strength.'

He couldn't back out because he had a deal with China.

,North Korea's military power is 34th, regardless of its economic power. It's natural for us to lose. But I think they're doing well. If we evolve a little more and increase in number... the day will come when we don't have to hide in caves like this.'

Cooperation with China?

He had no intention of cooperating sincerely. When the time is right, he will attack China and hide. China's current situation is very unstable, so it won't be long. The top priority is to secure China's bionics. He had all the plans for that.

,Korea calls us Roachists. It's better than the Chinese bastards who just call us bugs.'

Roachist.

It wasn't a bad name.

The past week.

Jeong Yena had a very busy schedule.

On the first day, she had an examination and moved to a place called the Seongjin Church, where she met a saintess named Kim Jihee.

,You're the new believer! Nice to meet you! I've heard the situation, so there's no need to talk about it. What's more important is to follow the rules of the Seongjin Church. I'll explain it to you well, so don't worry."

Kim Jihee speaks in a soft tone. At the same time, her voice is clearly etched in her ears. Jeong Yena, who was a trainee, immediately realized that the voice was the product of effort.

,...Um, what am I supposed to do here? I'm an atheist and I don't know anything...."

,Atheist? Really? You don't believe even after experiencing a miracle?"

,...That person is human. He's the president."

,We are living in his era. We are at the starting point of mythology. He will live forever and can save us from death. If he's not a god... then who the hell is a god?"

,......"

Jeong Yena couldn't refute it. She was the person who experienced the miracle.

,The first thing you need to do is believe in him. And believe in me, his saintess."

,Believe... what exactly do I have to do?"

,You don't have to do anything specific. You already believe in him. I can feel it. Now, shall we pray with me?"

Jeong Yena followed Kim Jihee and prayed. She just thanked Seong Yujin-nim. It was all thanks to his power that she was able to return from a mutant to a human.

,Is there anything you want from him? You said you were an idol trainee. Do you want to become an idol again? Or eternal youth? He can make it all come true."

,...Can I see my parents again?"

,Have your parents passed away?"

,I don't know. They're in the Netherlands... I can't reach them now."

,Even if Yena-ssi's parents have passed away, he can save them if they have only a part of their body. However, there is no free lunch in this world. You have to believe in him and dedicate yourself. Then he will reward your dedication."

,How can I dedicate myself?"

,You can do whatever he wants. First, you need to get training. He has chosen you as the fifth knight order."

,Knight?"

,Yes. It's a glorious position where you can dedicate yourself in his closest place. Yena-ssi, thank me."

,Huh? Suddenly?"

,I strongly opposed Jeong Yena-ssi's 5th Knight Order being named K-Pop Knights and stopped it. Especially Lee Yeonhee. That woman was actively in favor of it, so it was a headache."

Kim Jihee didn't like Lee Yeonhee. Lee Yeonhee was the de facto number two in Korea that everyone knew. For Kim Jihee, who was aiming for the number two position, she was a competitor to be wary of.

,K-Pop Knights is a bit...."

,That's right. Originally, the name of the knight order is decided by the knight order leader. Yena-ssi can decide."

,Um... Pink Angel? How about that?"

,Angel is another religion. No, if we interpret it in our own way, it might be okay, but... isn't pink a bit...?"

,I like pink."

,...A knight order isn't like a girl group."

Kim Jihee explained about the knight order. Jeong Yena's complexion turned pale when she learned about the Holy Knight Order.

,A combat unit fighting on the front lines. I'm not a soldier?!"

,But you have the right abilities. According to him, the 5th Knight Order is a unit for support rather than combat. It's not a unit where combat ability is important."

,Don't I have the right to refuse?"

,Are you going to refuse? Shall I tell you about the privileges of a knight? They don't age and they don't die. Above all, you can receive miracles from him. A miracle that can revive dead family or friends."

Jeong Yena closed her eyes tightly. The faces of her parents flicker before her eyes. Then the figure of Seong Yujin, who revived her, appears and disappears.

Coming to the church, she learned how the world had become. America has fallen and China and other countries are also falling. No, the whole world was falling. Her dream of becoming an idol? Even if she debuts in this dying world... she can't picture a good future.

,I'll do it."

,You've thought it through well."

After that, a week of hell training began. She also had to receive combat training. There was no choice. Since knights go to the battlefield, they had to secure at least minimal force.

Combat training in the morning, ability training using abilities in the afternoon, and mental training with Kim Jihee in the evening.

,I've decided to name the knight order Siren."

,Siren Knight Order. That's good. Does it have a meaning?"

,My ability is similar to the Siren in Greek mythology."

,Now that I hear it, I don't like that it's the name of a being from another mythology. But... it's your knight order, so I'll respect it."

That's how time passed and the day came to go into actual combat.

It was a battle with the Roachists who formed a large group in Hamgyeong Province, North Korea.

The battlefield was a rice field. The Korean army had already surrounded thousands of Roachists. They used hormones to lure the Roachists to one place and strategically moved to drive them out.

More than 4,000 giant bugs were swarming and wriggling.

Jeong Yena's body trembled.

Bugs. And they were human-sized bugs. It was scary to see them in person.

-Knight Order Leader Jeong Yena. There's no need to tremble. The Thunderbolt Knight Order in charge of your escort will protect you. Even if you die, he will save you from death, so don't worry.

Combat AI Jena said through the wireless earphones in her ear. She feels relieved that she's not alone.

Jeong Yena looked around.

Knights armed from head to toe and elite units bringing armored power were confronting the bugs. Dozens of armed helicopters were floating in the sky. The power seemed overwhelmingly advantageous to the Korean side.

It was actually advantageous. It was a force of less than 2,000 people, but all the equipment here was the latest.

This was a war where victory was promised. Someone who made the plan had already decided the victory.

-Do you see that drone camera over there? He is watching you. And the battle video will be edited and spread all over the world. You're going to be a world star. Congratulations in advance.

It was a joke to ease Jeong Yena's mood. Jeong Yena doubted whether Jena was really an AI every time. She wondered if AI was a lie and if she was actually an ordinary operator.

,...I didn't want to become a star like this."

-Your heart rate is relatively stable. It's still beating fast, though. Are you ready?

Jeong Yena took a deep breath in and out.

,I'm ready."

Behind her, music started playing from the speakers on the truck.

A cheerful sound that didn't suit the battlefield where death was rampant.

Jeong Yena started singing to the beat.

,Look at me, Baby~!"

It was K-Pop.

There was no choice. It was the genre of song that she, who was an idol trainee, was most confident in.
