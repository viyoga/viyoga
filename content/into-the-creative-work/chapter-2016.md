Wolta Tribe.

Strictly speaking, they are closer to a different species than monsters.

These guys resemble condors in appearance. They have no lower body, and in front of their wings are two arms with seven fingers. Half of their body, the right half, is made of machinery.

They possess intelligence similar to humans, so they can deploy tactical strategies.

The basic combat method of the Wolta Tribe is to use their wings. They fly freely in the sky and glide at a speed that, while slower than bullets, is by no means negligible, attacking with their wings. What you need to be careful of is that they have hands and intelligence. They can use human weapons. They also had their own unique weapons.

Kkiiiiiiik!

The Wolta Tribe's unique cry echoes in the sky. Jung Suyeon and I raised our heads. It hasn't even been 3 minutes since entering the encroachment phenomenon, and we've already been discovered by one of them. Fortunately, there are no others nearby.

,How are you going to fight?"

Jung Suyeon asked me. There was a strange expectation in her voice. I could feel her eagerness to see my skills.

,Lightning is perfect for those kinds of guys."

Half of its body is machine? From its perspective, lightning is its natural enemy. Just getting hit by lightning would cause an EMP effect.

I glared at it and dropped lightning.

The lightning passed by its side and struck the gray ground.

,......."

I had momentarily forgotten. I didn't have a shooting skill right now. I thought if I dropped it roughly around there, I'd hit it, but I guess I was mistaken.

,No, even if I didn't hit it, it fell really close, right next to it!'

The guy who almost got hit by lightning raised its alertness.

,It's not easy to hit with long-range sniping anyway."

I made an excuse without panicking.

,That's true. Sniper rifles at least have a scope, but... it's not an ability."

She acknowledged it. That was a relief.

I focus my mind. I pull out the information about the Wolta Tribe that's dormant in my head. An efficient way to deal with that guy... doesn't exist.

,Damn it. There's only basic information about the Wolta Tribe. There's no information that's actually helpful.'

I had never faced such a dangerous enemy like the Wolta Tribe before.

I clicked my tongue.

All I can rely on is my experience as a Hunter. The easiest way to deal with an enemy flying in the sky is with an area-of-effect attack. An attack on a plane that can't be avoided even with a report. Most of the guys flying in the sky have weak durability, so if you just hit them, you can kill them somehow.

,If only I had a shooting trait, I'd be spamming lightning.'

Without shooting, there's a high chance I won't hit them even if I spam lightning.

,I'll aim for a counter when that guy comes down to attack.'

The Wolta Tribe in front of me doesn't have weapons in either hand. It will glide and attack with its right steel wing as a weapon.

It glides. With a little paint mixed in. It's trying to attack using its beak instead of its wings, which is its traditional attack method.

,That's not it either. It's clenching its hand. I can't see it in my field of vision. I should assume it's hiding something in its palm.'

Even a small blade less than 10cm would be enough to kill a human.

I jumped into the air, timing it with when it fell.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Heodo (虚道).

Stepping on the air, I raise Hwaryeon Bido and slash upwards. The surprised guy forcibly twisted its body in mid-air. In the midst of that, it tried to counterattack me using its steel wing.

My feet went higher. My body spun around as if stepping on the wall and ceiling, and I cut off the Wolta Tribe's head. The Wolta Tribe's head fell with a thud. The body, having lost its head, flew a little further and crashed into the ground. Half of it was a living thing, so the ground became covered in blood.

,How did you do that?"

Jung Suyeon asked me with sparkling eyes. They were sparkling so much that it was burdensome.

,Its attack methods are its wings, beak, and hands. If you just focus on these three, it's not difficult to predict its movements."

,What I'm talking about isn't the counterattack timing. Of course, you can tell that just by looking. You stepped on the air. Not some forced double jump by bouncing your body."

,Ah, that."

I paused, about to speak.

It was annoying to explain Noecheonryu one by one. She would definitely ask me to teach her Noecheonryu. It wasn't difficult to teach her itself.

The problem is that even if she's a genius, it's impossible for her to master Noecheonryu and achieve greatness in a short period of time. She would need several more years. That would be a problem for me too. It would be like I'm really playing the role of a master.

,You can use Force and lightning appropriately, mixing them together. It's like creating a barrier under your feet with Force."

I showed her Heodo directly. Jung Suyeon stared intently at my feet and then nodded.

,Indeed. It feels like you're standing on something invisible. Did you make lightning into an invisible wall or something? They say that the application skills of outstanding ability users go beyond common sense... That's what they meant."

Pajijik.

Jung Suyeon tried to walk in the air as if following Heodo, operating Force and lightning at the same time. Of course, she failed. It's not a simple technique that you can follow just by seeing it once.

,...But just now, when her foot was about to fall, didn't she pause in mid-air? Like there was something in the air.'

She tried a few more times and then shook her head.

,It's hard to practice right now. But I've got a feel for it."

The method she's using is different from what I do. She's giving a characteristic to lightning. The characteristic of being solid to lightning.

,Did she realize it instinctively? Or is it a characteristic of this world?'

Even so, she's giving characteristics to lightning too easily.

Another hypothesis comes to mind. My lightning's level as a trait was 12. That became a Guardian ability in this world.

,What Jung Suyeon stole wasn't simply the ability to handle lightning... but the ability of lightning (Lv.12).'

Then I could understand. If it's lightning (lv.12), she can easily give it a characteristic that's familiar to lightning. The characteristic of being solid would be very familiar to her, who has been training her body every day without fail.

We walked a little. Three Wolta Tribe appeared at the same time.

,Are you going to use that foothold again this time? Fighting the Wolta Tribe in the air... That sounds fun."

,Fun my ass. Even if you can walk in the air, the opponent is a winged guy. It's much better to fight on familiar ground. And there's no need to bother dealing with troublesome enemies."

A Hunter has the right to choose their prey. They don't hunt indiscriminately just because prey appears.

,I'm going to go easy this time. Those guys have a fatal weakness."

The three Wolta Tribe are flying in from different directions. I created lightning on my right palm. Pajijik. The lightning gathered and formed a sphere.

I gave this sphere a characteristic. The EMP characteristic.

When I clenched my fist, the sphere of lightning exploded and pulses spread in all directions. The Wolta Tribe, whose bodies were half machine, fell to the ground as they were and writhed.

,EMP...! You can use it like that too. I see how ignorant I was to simply drop lightning or throw lightning."

Jung Suyeon was constantly impressed. The way she looked at me seemed to have changed slightly.

I finished off two of the fallen Wolta Tribe, and Jung Suyeon finished off one. The Wolta Tribe were said to be seven, so there are three left.

Once you kill one, you can assume you've avoided last place. There will be other Guardians in this encroachment phenomenon besides us.

,There's a hill over there that's perfect for hiding... Would she do it if I asked her to have sex behind there? She wouldn't, would she?'

Jung Suyeon is more serious than anyone else in battle. She won't do it.

,Can't you adjust that EMP you just used as you want? Like, only disabling the machines you want to."

,It's possible if you fine-tune the EMP and shoot it out. But wouldn't it be more powerful and efficient to just detonate it all at once?"

,My smartphone broke because of your EMP just now."

,Ah."

That was unexpected. My smartphone doesn't break even if it's hit directly by an EMP thanks to the Amusement Life app.

,...I'll buy you a new one."

Jung Suyeon accepted it as if it were natural.

While walking together, we discovered traces of people. Footprints. Since the Wolta Tribe have no lower body, it must be human.

Jung Suyeon suddenly grabbed my hand.

The presence of me and her fades.

,This is?"

,It's one of my abilities. Faint presence. It works 100% on ordinary people, but be careful because Guardians will notice if you get too close."

,You're wary of Guardians. Ah, is it to kill Guardians and steal their abilities?"

,I don't kill Guardians indiscriminately just because they're Guardians."

,......Really?"

I don't think she killed indiscriminately in the original work either. But didn't she kill me?

,I only kill guys who get in my way, or guys with bounties on their heads."

,Did I get in your way?"

,You had a bounty on your head."

,What crazy bastard?"

Aren't they afraid of the Cheonil Group, even in the underworld? They'll track down the client, and even the guy who took the request, to the ends of the earth.

,Me. I put a bounty on your head as soon as I saw you. About 2,000 won. I bought a drink with the bounty on the way back after killing you."

,...Coke? Cider?"

,Pine Tree Flavor."

,You crazy bitch. Do you eat mint chocolate too?"

,It's in the fridge. Want to share some?"

,Pineapple pizza?"

,I order it sometimes."

Is she really crazy? Those are things that I, a gourmet, can't even touch. I'm reminded again that I'm involved with a crazy bitch. But I can't give this woman to another guy.

,Why did you put a bounty on my head?"

,You're a reckless bastard. I thought you might be related to the Yeonggwang Society. And I wanted to steal your abilities while I was at it."

An irresistible prey was lying defenseless, intoxicated with drugs. The reason she killed me is understandable.

,What's the Yeonggwang Society? Why are you chasing those guys?"

,The scum of society. Guys who are trying to play with Korea as they please. They stole my father's research results and killed him... My enemies."

A chilling murderous intent flashed in her eyes. But she quickly suppressed it. Calm as usual. She hides her emotions.

,I'll tell you more about the Yeonggwang Society later. I'll need your help too."

,It's your revenge, but you need my help?"

,I'm going to use everything I can. You're no exception. Even my body."

If I stay like this, I feel like I'll keep getting tired. So I need to find and eliminate the Yeonggwang Society as soon as possible. Then Jung Suyeon's venom will subside a bit.

I stopped walking. I felt the presence of other people through my senses. Jung Suyeon, who was holding my hand, didn't realize it and kept walking forward until she stopped.

,Why?"

,There are people nearby."

She and I moved forward more cautiously. Three men were gathered. Around them were two Wolta Tribe corpses and five human corpses. It seems they had a battle and finished it.

,...Jojakgwi."

Jung Suyeon muttered, seeing the man with the Taegeuk tattoo on his face.

,Jojakgwi? Is he famous?"

,A 200 million won bounty. He has the ability to manipulate threads. The guy next to him is... Mental Destroyer. 300 million won bounty."

Mental Destroyer was a middle-aged man who was only 150cm tall. He was frowning and drinking beer.

,Mental Destroyer?"

,Just like his nickname, he's a guy with the ability to destroy the target's mind."

,Ah... I see."
