I left the room and headed to the kitchen.

The kitchen was outside. It was a place shared by men and women.

I didn't have an umbrella or raincoat, so I braved the wind and rain to get to the kitchen. I looked around the spacious kitchen. Opening the refrigerator, it was full of canned beer. Since I was there, I cracked open a can of beer and chugged it down while looking for the storage shed.

The storage building was outside. I went through the wind and rain again to get to the shed. Fortunately, the shed door wasn't locked.

There were cleaning tools, ropes, farming tools, wooden planks, and pretty much everything else you could think of. I estimated the size of the window, grabbed a toolbox and some wooden planks, and came out of the shed.

I felt a presence.

I quickly turned my head and looked into the forest. *Swaaaaa*. The trees and grass swayed in the wind and rain. I couldn't see anyone.

,Was it just my imagination?'

The eerie atmosphere must have made me more sensitive. I went past the kitchen and headed to the lodging. Before going inside, I looked at the women's lodging. The lights were on. They must have known the wind was unusual and were preparing for various things.

Kuuung!

I heard the sound of something heavy falling over. I thought a tree had fallen, but it turned out the van parked next to the lodging had fallen on its side.

I pretended not to see it and went inside the lodging. Wada Kyoshiro was still blocking the window with a blanket.

,You're back! I heard a loud noise earlier. Is everything okay outside?"

,The car fell over."

,The car we came in?"

,Yeah. I didn't think I could lift it up by myself, so I left it."

,Good thinking. It's not good to move around carelessly in this weather. Especially when it's dark with no streetlights."

I added wooden planks behind the window and fixed them with nails.

,The landlord's going to say something."

,No. The landlord will be grateful. If we left it, the whole room would be a mess."

Wada Kyoshiro checked the wooden planks and nodded. He threw the wet blanket outside and took out a new blanket from the closet.

,Good work, Seong-sang."

,You too."

I fell asleep again.

I was going to wake up before 4 AM, but when I woke up, it was morning. The first night's *yobai* was gone with the wind.

•••

Even though it was morning, the weather hadn't improved at all.

Mori Masahiro dragged me and the other male students to right the van. We moved the van next to the storage shed, which could at least block some of the wind and rain. The problem was that the engine was busted, so we had to push the van to move it.

After finishing the work, we gathered in the large living room on the first floor of the lodging. It was to decide what to do next.

,Mori-senpai! The car is broken. We have to go back tomorrow, but we can't! What are we going to do now?!"

A man with a thin build compared to his height said. Fudo Jun. Wearing glasses, his cheeks were sunken. His hair was also long, and his bangs seemed to poke his eyes.

,Fudo. Calm down."

Mori Masahiro said. Even in this situation, he was calm.

,Calm down? Senpai. We're isolated in this village right now. It's no different from being stranded!"

,Stranded.... That's a bit harsh. We're in a village, Fudo. Watch your tone. Other juniors might get agitated."

,Harsh? I'm just expressing a reasonable concern. We can't even leave tomorrow as planned."

,We can fix the car. If not, we can borrow a car from other residents. There are a few trucks. If we can just get to a bigger city nearby, it won't be hard to get back."

,That's a relief. Can we go back tomorrow as planned?"

Fudo Jun held out his smartphone. The people who checked the smartphone all sighed.

A nationwide evacuation order had been issued in Japan. It instructed people to move to the nearest shelter and live there. Fudo Jun touched his smartphone and showed a news article.

,Earthquake?!"

Wada Kyoshiro exclaimed in shock.

An 8.0 magnitude earthquake briefly occurred in Kanto and Tohoku. The confirmed number of casualties was over 1,000, and it was a disaster with the typhoon on top of that. Fear flashed across people's faces.

Kanto.

In our words, it was the Kanto region.

The Great Kanto Earthquake. It's a word that triggers trauma for Japanese people.

,Mori-senpai. The situation is more serious than you think. Can we really get to the city tomorrow?"

,Calm down, Fudo. I know you're sensitive because of the competition in three weeks. You probably want to practice for the competition. But getting anxious won't get you anything. Let's go around the village together and see if we can leave or not."

Fudo glared at Mori Masahiro with a still dissatisfied expression before reluctantly nodding.

,...Yes. I understand."

We ate a simple breakfast and the group moved. Tanaka Hiroshi and the other three delinquents gathered in a room on the second floor and snickered, while me and Wada Kyoshiro were in charge of washing the dishes. Mori Masahiro and Fudo Jun put on raincoats purchased from the convenience store and went around the village.

Kuuuung!

A loud noise rang out. Wada Kyoshiro and I went outside. A small tree had fallen in a nearby field.

,I can't believe a tree fell over so easily. The summer festival tonight... it'll be tough, right?"

Wada Kyoshiro shook his head, looking at the fallen tree.

,Holding a festival when there's a typhoon is crazy. If you think about it logically, they'll postpone it until the weather calms down."

It's not just the wind, but the rain keeps falling.

,Looking around the fields... most of them are shallowly flooded. I guess this year's farming is over.'

The roads won't be flooded. This is a mountain with a high altitude. The water will keep flowing downwards.

I'm more worried about landslides than flooding.

As I was about to go inside the lodging, a middle-aged man in a raincoat was approaching from afar. It was a face I'd never seen before.

,Hello. My name is Honda Taichi, and I work at the Ookaruma Shrine."

He was a middle-aged man with an average build and tanned skin. He had a square jaw and a calm impression.

,I'm Wada Kyoshiro, a freshman at Niao University."

,I'm Seong Yujin."

Honda Taichi stared at my face for a few seconds.

,Is he a right-winger?'

There were many middle-aged Japanese men who didn't think highly of Korea.

I shut my mouth and stayed quiet next to Wada Kyoshiro.

,Let's go inside first."

,Yes. Thank you."

We sat facing each other in the living room. Wada Kyoshiro naturally brought out green tea. Honda Taichi took a sip of the green tea and said.

,The festival has been postponed."

It wasn't good news.

The Japan Festival Study Circle needed achievements to avoid being disbanded. It was too late to go to another village's festival now.

,...How many days has it been postponed?"

,The exact date hasn't been decided yet. We plan to start the festival after the typhoon passes and the village is cleaned up."

,You're not canceling the festival?"

,It's just late. That will never happen."

,Thank you for letting us know, Honda-sang. Is there a shelter in Ookaruma Village?"

,There is no shelter in the village."

,There's a shrine, isn't there?"

,The shrine is not a shelter."

Honda Taichi said sternly. Wada Kyoshiro's face stiffened for a moment. Honda Taichi's stern expression was quite intimidating.

,Ahem. The shrine is a sacred place. And Ookaruma Village is not greatly affected by typhoons. Unlike other places, it is geographically safe. There's no need to evacuate."

,...That's a relief then. Ookaruma Village doesn't have any flood damage, right?"

,That's not true. The fields are flooded, causing various losses. But it's not at a serious level."

,Hmm. I see. Are the roads okay? We might have to leave the village tomorrow."

Honda Taichi made a troubled expression and said.

,It's impossible to leave the village right now."

,Huh?"

,The road below the village has collapsed. It's hard for cars to get out now."

,R, really?"

,Why would I lie? And even if the roads were fine, it would be hard to get to the city. I heard from a friend in the city that the city is currently being flooded. Japan is currently in a disaster situation."

,......."

Wada Kyoshiro dropped his head. He must have felt miserable being isolated in the village. On top of that, it's not easy to expect outside help because of the disaster that hit the entire Japan.

,Don't be too discouraged. The village has stockpiled food in case of an emergency, so we can last for a month. Just spend time until things outside are sorted out."

,...It's a relief that we don't have to worry about food."

The atmosphere was still gloomy.

Honda Taichi finished all the green tea and opened his mouth.

,Everyone. Have you seen Jiro-sang by any chance?"

,Jiro-sang?"

,He's an old man who works in the potato field. He usually wakes up early in the morning and works until late, but today, even though it's noon, no one in the village has seen him."

,Is it the potato field next to the convenience store?"

,Yes. You know him."

,I interviewed him last night. After that... I don't know."

I calmly drank the green tea. I already realized that the person Honda Taichi was talking about was the old man I killed yesterday.

,Since I have acting traits, he won't feel anything strange just by looking at my face.'

Honda Taichi looked back and forth between me and Wada Kyoshiro, then got up from his seat.

,I understand. I'll be going now. If you find out any information about Jiro-sang, please come to the building next to the shrine. Even if you have any inconveniences or need anything, don't hesitate to come. I'll help you as much as I can."

Honda Taichi left.

,Hoo."

Wada Kyoshiro just sighed deeply.

An hour later, Mori Masahiro and Fudo Jun returned. Their expressions weren't good. They must have realized that the road below the village had collapsed.

,I've seen the road situation. It's impossible to pass by car. It's too dangerous to go down the mountain on foot in this weather. I heard from the villagers that there are many wild boars and sometimes bears."

Mori Masahiro explained. Behind him, Fudo Jun went up to the second floor with staggering steps.

,Mori-senpai. Fudo-senpai is...."

,Wada. Leave Fudo alone. The competition has been canceled because of the earthquake in Kanto and Tohoku this time."

,Ah."

,Wada. Are Tanaka and the others inside?"

,Yes. They're on the second floor."

,I see. Seong. I'm sorry, but can you call the women? We can't leave the village right now, so we have to explain it."

,Yes. I'll call them. But Mori-senpai. I heard that the old man I interviewed last night is missing. Do you have any idea about it?"

,Missing... you said?"

Mori Masahiro's eyes widened. He seemed quite surprised.

,The questions I asked the old man yesterday.... Isn't it related to the disappearance?"

I asked, feigning ignorance. There was something in this village.

,...I'll explain when everyone is gathered. Since it's come to this... we should call Fudo too."
