There were a total of 5 boats. Each could hold up to 6 people, but we split into groups of 3. Of course, I wasn't planning on rowing all the way to Tsushima.

The boats naturally had motors. The average speed was 30 knots. If everything went according to plan, we'd arrive at our destination, Tsushima Island, before sunrise.

I boarded a boat with Choi Hye-jin and Irina. I confirmed that the other Holy Guard members had also boarded their boats. We had, of course, brought our combat gear.

Before departing, I looked back at the Holy Guard. Their faces were hidden by their combat helmets. But though they were tense, they didn't show any signs of fear. They were hardened by experiencing death several times. Their faith couldn't be broken even by death. They were the elite of the elite.

,You guys obviously know this, but the sea isn't safe. Marine animals also mutate. It's actually more dangerous because it's the sea. That's why trade using sea routes is blocked right now."

Land, sea, and air. The most dangerous of these is the sea. Next is land. The sky was the safest of the three. Of course, there were mutated creatures that could fly, but none were fast enough to keep up with an airplane. At least, not yet.

The sea is dangerous, but it's okay if you don't go too far from land. The dangerous mutated bastards avoid being near land.

The motor started, and the boat moved forward. The direction indicator on the combat helmet's HUD worked well, so there was no need to worry about getting lost.

,This helmet is so stuffy, can't I take it off?"

Choi Hye-jin asked, tapping her combat helmet.

,Keep it on. We're fucked if we get caught. And it has a radar effect, too. You need to wear the combat helmet to immediately detect approaching mutated creatures."

Unfortunately, this boat wasn't built very well due to the rush. If we were unlucky, it could be smashed by a mutated creature's attack.

,Seongha-nim."

Son Tae-hyung, the captain of the Holy Guard, called me. He originally called me Seongnim, but I never got used to it no matter how many times I heard it, so I ordered him to call me Seongha-nim.

,Speak."

,The radar has detected two mutated creatures. They're approximately 3m in size. We estimate we'll encounter them in about 22 seconds. May we eliminate them?"

,Eliminate them."

,Jonmyeong."

Son Tae-hyung immediately issued orders to his subordinates.

,Park Hyuk-woon, Ki Min-saeng. You two, use the harpoons equipped on the boat to eliminate the approaching mutated creatures."

,Jonmyeong."

,Jonmyeong."

I don't know why they say "jonmyeong." I just noticed they were doing it. There was nothing wrong with it, so I just went with it. I even liked it a little.

The mutated creatures naturally charged from below the boat, from the depths of the sea. But in preparation for this, harpoons were equipped on the underside of the boat. A Holy Guard member manipulated the controls and fired the harpoons.

,Direct hit."

,Direct hit here too."

It was at that moment. The sea near the boat glowed, illuminating the surroundings. It was the effect of the harpoons. The moment they hit, they discharged electricity, ensuring the opponent's death. The fish nearby also died en masse, but the effect was certain.

,Check the battery."

,Battery check. 30% of the power consumed."

,40 spare batteries. No problem."

The boat continued heading south.

The mutated creatures kept attacking as if they never got tired. It happened 4-5 times an hour. It was fortunate that there weren't any overwhelmingly powerful mutated creatures.

But it wasn't all perfect victories. Even though the Holy Guard were the elite of the elite, they had physical limitations. Moreover, this wasn't land, but the sea.

Cheolssuk!

A stingray jumped out of the water. It wasn't just any stingray. It was a mutated creature with three poisonous barbs and wings as hard and sharp as blades. The stingray pounced on one of the Holy Guard members. The Holy Guard member extended his left arm.

Kwaduk!

The Holy Guard member's left arm was bitten by the stingray. The skintight combat suit, which had anti-cut and bulletproof functions, was torn apart so easily it was absurd, and his left arm was destroyed.

The Holy Guard member didn't even groan, and with his right hand, he pulled out a pistol, pressed the muzzle against the stingray, and pulled the trigger.

Tang, tang, tang, tang!

The dead mutated stingray splashed into the sea. The Holy Guard member who killed the stingray staggered and collapsed onto the boat. Another Holy Guard member next to him checked on him and reported impassively.

,Holy Guard member Park Hyuk-woon has died. Apparent heart failure due to poison."

It seemed the mutated stingray had poison not only in its tail but also in its teeth.

Son Tae-hyung bowed his head to me.

,I'm sorry, Seongha-nim. The inadequacy of the Holy Guard member has caused you disappointment."

,I am disappointed that he was taken down by a mere stingray... but I like that he didn't even scream before he died."

His combat power wasn't bad either.

I went over to the boat with the deceased and revived the Holy Guard member. And as a bonus, I fixed his broken combat suit too.

The Holy Guard are immortal soldiers that I can use. I promised them immortality. That promise is the source of their faith. If I left the corpse here to die, their faith would waver.

The Holy Guard member who had died and been resurrected knelt before me and bowed his head. His hands were clasped together as if in prayer.

,Thank you for the grace you have bestowed upon me, Seongha-nim."

,Yes. Do well."

We arrived near Tsushima Island. We turned off the motors and quietly rested. At the same time, we reconfirmed the operation plan. Na Chae-young, who is at Seongak Elementary School, is leading the briefing. Her voice can be heard through the combat helmets.

-Tsushima Island's defense system is focused on the air. Landing via the sea won't be difficult. The problem is what comes next. At least 300 armed soldiers and artificial mutated creatures will try to kill the intruders no matter what.

We silently waited for her next words.

-As soon as you land on Tsushima Island, activate the communication jammer. It'll cause confusion for the enemies for about an hour. As a bonus, they won't be able to contact the mainland. Well, even if they could contact them, they're too far away to arrive quickly.

,After that, the Holy Guard will deliberately cause a ruckus to draw attention, and we'll infiltrate deep into the secret laboratory, take Yoo Seo-jung and her daughter, and get out. Then we'll take the boat and enter the port of Busan. Right?"

-That's right. Even if Tsushima Island becomes a mess, Japan won't be able to protest openly. Because it was a laboratory where human experimentation was taking place. Rather, they'll try to hide it at all costs, right?

,Shouldn't we expose what happened here later?"

-The Ilwoo Group will take a hit. But it won't be a real hit. They can just abandon the name Ilwoo and do the same thing under a different name. Even if they apologize, it'll only be for then. After that, they'll conduct human experiments even more carefully.

Japan can't abandon the Ilwoo Group. Right now, the Japanese government and the Ilwoo Group are practically one and the same. Japan sees the Ilwoo Group as its future. The Ilwoo Group family has Korean names, but Japanese blood flows in their veins. I learned this while torturing Jin Chung-hyun.

-And it's not good to have an all-out war with Japan. Unlike the Signals incident, we can't expose it anonymously. Got it? Try to leave as few traces as possible. And take good care of your bodies.

,Take good care of my body? Even if it's me, I wouldn't have sex right here."

-That's not what I mean. I mean don't leave behind any body fragments or blood... any genetic material. The enemy could use your genes to create a clone. Of course, it would take at least several years of research to create a clone... it's best not to give them any chance from the start.

,Hmm. Well, I'll try."

Honestly, I don't think a human cloned from me would be the same as me. Could they replicate my ability, the Play Life, by cloning me? If it were that easy, Yog-Sothoth wouldn't have killed himself.

,Even if they clone me, that bastard is different from me. He's just another guy with the same genes as me.'

Still, the idea of cloning me was unpleasant.

After that, I rested while listening to Na Chae-young's nagging.

,Rest is over. Start the operation."

I said, activating the communication jammer.

The 12 Holy Guard members moved in teams of 3. The Holy Guard began to wreak havoc near the main gate of the secret laboratory.

Choi Hye-jin, Irina, and I quietly headed towards the residential area. We entered the kitchen of a closed ramen shop. When we moved the refrigerator to the side, we saw stairs leading down to the basement. It was a secret passage connected to the secret laboratory.

,I'll go first."

As planned, Irina went down the passage first. Her stealth ability is specialized for infiltration.

I've been intensively training in how to conceal my presence, so if I'm determined to hide it, even I have a hard time finding her. Of course, that's only when I'm not using electromagnetic waves.

,I was told not to use electromagnetic waves as much as possible inside the secret laboratory.'

According to Na Chae-young, there might be a machine that can detect electromagnetic waves. Honestly, I doubt the defenses are that strong... but it doesn't hurt to be careful.

,Even if I don't use electromagnetic waves, I know the way.'

The path is displayed on the combat screen display.

The destination is the laboratory on the lowest level of this secret laboratory, on the 5th basement floor.

,Yes. It's quiet. Come down."

Following Irina's words, Choi Hye-jin and I went down the passage. We talked through the combat helmet communicators as we headed towards our destination.

,Two people ahead."

Irina said in a whisper. Since we were wearing combat helmets, our voices wouldn't leak out.

As she said, two soldiers were standing there with guns. They were wearing combat uniforms and helmets. They were holding assault rifles in their hands. The two men frowned and talked into their radios.

,What's going on? Suddenly, the communication isn't working?"

,Mine too. Is there an error with the equipment? Well, we can deal with it later. Let's just wait until the relief personnel arrive. I don't want to get my salary cut for leaving without permission."

Without me even needing to step in, Irina solved the problem with a small crossbow attached to her left arm. Two small arrows were silently fired, piercing the enemies' necks.

,It shouldn't originally have that much power to penetrate... is it being enhanced by her ability?'

Irina's ability is Archery. An ability that enhances her skill in handling bows. It's no exception even for a small crossbow.

,Oh, not bad, Blondie?"

Choi Hye-jin, who was watching from the side, exclaimed in admiration.

,P, please call me Irina."

,What? What's wrong with calling Blondie Blondie?"

,Ah, that... yes."

Irina was watching Choi Hye-jin's reaction. Choi Hye-jin, regardless, swung her metal bat in the air. She seemed to be itching for a fight, with her combat-crazed tendencies.

,No time to stop. Let's keep going."

I urged them on and headed forward.

Thanks to Irina, we were able to easily get down to the 3rd basement floor.

Aeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeee!

When we were about halfway through the 3rd floor, a siren went off.

We weren't discovered. We were learning about the situation on the surface, which we couldn't know due to the communication jammer. A booming voice echoed from somewhere.

,Everyone listen up! Since the radios are down, I'm announcing this verbally! Intruders have appeared on the surface! They're attacking the secret laboratory! The enemies are in squad-sized units! Judging by their equipment, they're presumed to be special forces from some faction! Except for the guards in charge of your areas, everyone go up to the surface and respond!"

Irina, who was walking ahead, turned to look at me. I couldn't see her face because of the combat helmet, but I imagined she was making a troubled expression.

,Y, Yu-jin-nim. What should we do?"

,Just be careful and keep going. They don't know we exist yet."

As soon as I finished speaking, the booming voice echoed again.

,We've discovered corpses! The enemies have already infiltrated this far! The control room is checking the intruders through CCTV! The enemies are presumed to be Awakened, so don't leave your areas and stay alert! The indoor defense system will be activated soon!"

It seems they found the corpses I carelessly left behind while coming here.

Stealthy infiltration is out the window.

,Run. It's going to be a blitzkrieg now."

I took the lead and ran.
