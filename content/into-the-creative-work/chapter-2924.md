Explosions echoed from here and there, shaking the ship. The Forte ship continued its voyage even while under attack from the Gestalgs.

Even if a spaceship's armor plating is sturdy and thick, you can't underestimate the Gestalgs' attack power. Eventually, the ship's armor will be breached, and those bastards will infiltrate the ship.

,No, they probably already have.'

Fortunately, the ship has several safety devices, so all the air inside won't escape. As long as the sub-engine is running, the artificial gravity device will also be maintained.

I ran down the hallway and arrived at the medical bay area. A place where medical rooms are lined up.

When I arrived, blood and corpses were already strewn across the hallway. The red blood of humans and the blue blood of Gestalgs.

The human corpses were full of cuts and stab wounds.

The Soldier bugs, about the size of humans, were full of bullet holes.

Tang tang tang tang tang tang tang!

Gunshots rang out incessantly inside the ship. Even if they're medical officers, they're still soldiers. Of course, they could fight.

,But all they can do is hold out. Even a regular space marine has less than a 30% chance of winning in close combat against a basic combat bug.'

The most basic tactic for dealing with Gestalgs is to project firepower and kill them before they get close.

,In the narrow confines of the ship, that basic tactic is almost impossible.'

I nudged the corpse of a dead medical officer. The corpse and all its equipment went into my inventory.

It's not, of course, to return even a soldier's corpse to Earth. It's to rummage through the corpse's equipment later. Even if there's little to gain, it's a profit if I can get even one bullet.

,Even though it's a separate inventory from the real-world inventory, its size is the same. 1 km wide, long, and high. You can put in hundreds of thousands of corpses.'

I've never actually put in hundreds of thousands of corpses, though.

,...Using the search function, I can automatically know what items a corpse has when I put the whole corpse in.'

It was also possible to exclude the equipment of the corpse and take out only the corpse separately. However, it's impossible to take out only the blood or organs of the corpse separately. The inventory treats the corpse as a single item.

,If I need organs, I have to take out the corpse and then extract the organs. The same goes for other items. When I put in a loaded gun, it's impossible to take out only the parts that make up the gun or the loaded bullets.'

I could take out the clothes I was wearing or the items I had in my pockets separately. However, I can't take out items inside a locked box.

,The inventory is convenient, but it has its complexities.'

I ignored the bug corpses.

Bug corpses are almost worthless. Gestalgs have toxic substances, so only their own kind can eat them.

,I don't have toxin resistance right now. Even if I did, I wouldn't eat them.'

I went inside the medical room.

A Soldier bug that had been quietly ambushing next to the door appeared and attacked. The assault rifle I was holding was already aimed at its head. My finger pulled the trigger faster than I thought. The muzzle flashed. Three bullets were lodged in the Soldier bug's head.

Kung!

The Soldier bug fell to the side.

The Soldier bug was 2.5m tall and looked like a praying mantis. It had 4 legs and 4 arms. The 4 arms were knives in themselves. They were so sharp that they could cut through power suits made of special alloys.

When they enter combat, they don't feel pain. Even if dozens of bullets are lodged in their torso, they can last for more than an hour if their heart or brain is intact.

I stepped over the convulsing corpse and looked around inside the medical room. The dark medical room was only flashing with a single red emergency light.

Inside were the corpses of humans and bugs.

First, I nudged the human corpses and put them in my inventory.

The reason I don't just throw away the corpses? Using the inventory consumes a very small amount of mental power. It was a fact I never even noticed when I was sane.

,There's so much to farm. I need to save what I can for now.'

After putting in 2 corpses, it was time for the tools and medicines in the medical room.

,I did take the things that Marie stole yesterday, but the more medicine, the better.'

I mainly took what I could see. There wasn't time to take everything meticulously.

I went into the next medical room. This time, there was no ambush. Instead, a few bugs were wriggling around. First, I put the soldier's corpse in my inventory.

,Kekeke. Convenient. I'm glad I wasn't near the medical bay area after all. This is what you call a windfall.'

That was the reason I was in the hallway away from the medical bay area.

I went into the next medical room. Each medical room has slightly different types of medicine that can be obtained, so I have to check them all.

,Is this an operating room? The state-of-the-art operating table... it's integrated with the operating room, so I can't take it.'

I took all the other individual tools.

I go into the next medical room.

Medical Room 1.

Tang tang tang!

I heard gunshots before I even went inside.

,You bugs! Did you think I'd die so easily?!'

The faggy Major Bates, who had grabbed my shoulder, was shouting and engaging two fallen Soldier bugs. He was holding a pistol, not a rifle. He seemed to have quite a few injuries, but he was surprisingly holding out.

He must have received special enhancement surgery because he's an officer.

Bates found me at the entrance and shouted.

,Private Seong! Don't just stand there, help me!'

,The bugs are too close to you, Major-nim, so I can't shoot!'

I moved my rifle around, stammering.

,Come closer and help me! Aren't you good with a knife, enough to beat the instructor?!'

Tang tang tang!

Bates shot the bastards with his pistol even as he spoke. Soldier bugs may be stupid, but they understand guns. They turn their heads to avoid the muzzle. They also move their bodies, so it was difficult to aim accurately at their hearts.

,That was a kind of sparring match. The basic rule of engagement with Gestalgs is to avoid close combat as much as possible.'

,Is this the time for that?! Then shoot, at least! Aren't you a space marine?! You can't possibly miss at this distance?!'

,You're the one who told me to shoot?'

I tried to hide my laughter and pulled the trigger.

I hit Bates and the 2 bugs at the same time. Bates was wearing a power suit, but space marine power suits are for dealing with Gestalgs, so they were vulnerable to guns. Space marine rifles were also much stronger than regular rifles.

Bates, with 4 bullets lodged in his abdomen, collapsed to the floor along with the bugs.

,Ah. I'm sorry. I'm not very confident in my shooting. It was friendly fire.'

,Goddamn it.... Fratricide in this situation.... How are you going to handle the aftermath? This situation will be recorded through the camera and stored in the database. Did you forget that there's a personal cam in the power suit too? Private Seong. Even if you survive, you'll be court-martialed and die.'

,I was trying to be nice, but you don't understand the situation? The camera is out of order, and I can just smash the personal cam later.'

,...The combat footage from the personal cam is automatically transmitted to the command center.'

,That's when the signal is connected. Did you think I, a space marine, wouldn't know that?'

I approached him. The muzzle was aimed at Bates' helmet.

,Private Seong. I'll apologize. So please, spare me. Please give me some first aid with the emergency kit over there. I'll definitely repay this favor.'

,Just die, you fag.'

Tang tang tang!

I finished off Bates and tore off the insignia attached to his power suit. His corpse was put in my inventory like the other soldiers' corpses.

After taking everything I needed, I went out of the medical room. I stood in front of the iron door at the end of the medical room.

I put Major Bates' insignia on the door.

Bbiik!

The door opened, revealing a well-organized warehouse.

I went inside and started taking all the stored drugs.

At the very back of the warehouse, there were boxes stacked full of stimulant drugs.

,This is it. This is why I ran to the medical bay area first.'

A drug that temporarily turns humans into superhumans.

The side effect is addiction at the level of narcotics?

,I'm about to die right now, who cares about that bullshit.'

I took all the stimulant drugs and went out of the drug warehouse.

,Kieeeeeeeee...'

Before I knew it, several Soldier bugs were wandering around the medical room hallway, and they all turned around when they found me. They had crawled out of the broken hallway ceiling.

I pressed my waist with one hand. Then, a line of system windows appeared on the screen display.

[Administering stimulant drug.]

Pshwiiik.

A needle installed in the power suit pierced my lower back. I could feel the cold drug flowing into the needle.

It was the stimulant drug I received from Marie Wong.

The drug spread throughout my body in the blink of an eye. The effects of the drug spread instantly.

My heart beat rapidly, and my blood flow accelerated. My whole body became hot, and my senses expanded. My existence became clear. I felt like I could do anything now.

,I feel so High right now!'

I summoned an additional rifle from my inventory.

I held an assault rifle in each hand. I aimed the assault rifles in each hand at the front and pulled the trigger.

Flashes occurred from the muzzles. It was easy to see because the hallway was dark. Of course, I couldn't see the dozens of bullets flying.

,Kieeeeeeeeek!'

The Soldier bugs charged. The ones closest to me became honeycombs and fell dead. But the Soldier bugs behind them charged without stopping at all. They also became honeycombs and died.

Blue blood splattered every time a bullet hit their bodies. The impact was no joke.

More than hundreds of empty cartridges piled up at my feet, and more than 6 Soldier bugs died without even getting close.

,Ah.'

I belatedly realized that I had wasted bullets.

Originally, space marine rifles have a basic 3-round burst. That's because it's cost-effective.

Full auto? Space marine rifles could fire 20 rounds per second. But the magazine only holds 50 rounds. Considering it's a .50 caliber bullet, 50 rounds is a lot.

There's a limit to the number of magazines a space marine can carry. When the distance to the bugs is close like now, it becomes vulnerable when changing magazines.

,Not for me. One of the abilities of the inventory is automatic reloading.'

As long as there are bullets in the inventory, I can keep firing without having to change magazines. Heat generation is another matter.

,Bullets aren't infinite. I can't waste them here.'

I was too excited by the effects of the drug.

Even now, I just want to spray bullets, but I held back for the future.

Tang tang tang!

I switched to 3-round bursts and pulled the trigger.

Of course, the stopping power decreased, and the distance to the Soldier bugs became closer.

I reverse-summoned the rifles in both hands, summoned a knife, and grabbed it. It's called a knife, but since it's for space marines, it was the size of a short sword.

I charged into the arms of the bug in front of me, quickly cut its throat, and threw the knife at the head of the one behind it. As soon as I confirmed that the knife was lodged in its head, I reverse-summoned it and summoned it back into my hand.

I moved forward and dealt with the bugs with my knife.

,Kieeeek!'

One Soldier bug ran up the wall and jumped to the ceiling. It aimed for the timing when I couldn't use my hands because I was cutting the throat of another Soldier bug. It's a smart one for a bug.

,Ejection.'

A space split next to my head, and a knife was fired. The jumping Soldier bug couldn't avoid it and fell to the floor with the knife lodged in its head and died. I recovered the knife with reverse-summoning.

I lowered my raised hand. That last one was the last one. There were no additional reinforcements either. I looked back before running. Almost 30 bug corpses were scattered in the hallway.

,I killed them in a trance, and I killed 30 of them.'

There was no particular physical exertion. My breathing was a little ragged, but even that quickly recovered.

,In my memory, the stimulant drug didn't have this much effect. Did my mind affect the drug's effects? Or has my way of using my body changed?'

I suddenly came to my senses.

There was no time to contemplate trivial problems. Next is the armory. I need to replenish my guns and bullets.

,The closest armory first.'
