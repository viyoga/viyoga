The Cursed Object Department.

Those bastards dared to try and dismantle my Sex Appeal Department. There are other small departments, but the Cursed Object Department is taking the lead. My steps, filled with righteous fury, headed towards the Cursed Object Department building.

The Cursed Object Department was located on the 1st basement floor of the academy's annex. True to its name, it was in a damp and gloomy place. On the ground floor, there was some incomprehensible department called Magic Sound or something.

,Yujin-ah. Calm down. You're not planning to start a fight in the Cursed Object Department, are you? I absolutely cannot allow that."

Kales-ssi, who had been following me, grabbed my shoulder in front of the stairs leading down to the basement.

,Teacher. I'm calm. I won't do anything that would get me expelled, so don't worry."

Kales-ssi calmly looked into my eyes and nodded.

,...Don't go too far. The Cursed Object Department may be small in size, but its history is comparable to the Sword Dance Department. The teacher who took the representative position when the Cursed Object Department was first established is a very famous person."

,I see. But I've never been a fan of history."

I went down the stairs. About halfway down, the temperature suddenly dropped, and it became chilly.

,A barrier. Not to keep people out... but to protect people outside from the things inside."

A steel door appeared at the end of the stairs. I didn't bother knocking. I opened the door and went inside. The Cursed Object Department should be grateful that I didn't kick the door in.

,......"

As soon as I entered, eight pairs of eyes were fixed on me. Seven were students, and one was a teacher.

I quietly looked around. Even though the fluorescent lights were on, it didn't feel bright. Bizarre objects were hanging on the walls, and dried-up corpses of people or monsters were placed on the bookshelves.

,It's even gloomier than I thought. It's definitely the Cursed Object Department."

,...Suddenly barging in and spouting nonsense. What business does the Spirit Department have here?"

A man sitting on the sofa spoke as if annoyed.

His black, seaweed-like hair flowed down to his waist, and his upper body looked lean but solid. His face was handsome, but the dark circles under his eyes gave him a bad impression.

It was Kim Jeo, the representative teacher of the Cursed Object Department.

I confidently walked over and sat opposite him.

Kim Jeo glared at me as if to kill me, then turned his gaze to Kales-ssi. A sharp voice flowed from his mouth.

,...Teacher Kales-ssi. What is this rude behavior? Did you find my remarks at the meeting so amusing that you came all the way here? And to put a student forward like this.... I thought you were an extremely sensible person, but... I seem to have misjudged you."

,Yujin-gun said he had something to say to you. I just followed him in case something happened."

,...In case something happened? Do you think I would hit this student?"

His voice became more agitated. Kales-ssi didn't waver in the slightest. Instead, she narrowed her eyes and smiled. She wasn't the type of woman to be intimidated by someone coming on strong.

,That's not what I meant. This is the Cursed Object Department. It's full of cursed objects. There's a possibility that Yujin-gun, the only student in the Spirit Department, could be cursed by a cursed object."

,So you're saying you don't trust me even though I'm here."

,I heard there was an accident in the Cursed Object Department last year."

,......"

Kim Jeo closed his mouth. He glared at Kales-ssi once, then shifted his gaze back to me.

,Tell me why you came to the Cursed Object Department."

,Don't you even offer a cup of coffee here?"

,Eating and drinking are prohibited in the Cursed Object Department. Not even water is allowed. If you want to drink coffee, go outside."

He wasn't just saying that. It was because of the cursed objects.

Cursed objects refer to items that are cursed. They are different from cursed tools, which are tools used for curses.

However, there are ability users in this world who use cursed objects. The Cursed Object Department is where those people gather. Since this place is full of cursed objects, it's natural to be careful about food intake.

,I heard you want our Spirit Department to be dismantled."

,You're the only student in the Spirit Department. It was created to give you special treatment. That's something not only I, but everyone else, doesn't approve of."

I turned my head. Blue sleeves. I spoke to a first-year male student.

,Hey, don't you approve either?"

,Uh, uh...? I, I...."

The male student panicked and stepped back. Cold sweat formed on his forehead.

In the academy, rank was equivalent to class and power. I was ranked 5th upon admission. Most guys couldn't even dare to defy me.

,I'm asking you."

,Th, that's...."

The male student's eyes wavered. He desperately sent signals to Kim Jeo.

Kim Jeo frowned.

,Stop the intimidation."

,Intimidation? I was just asking a question."

,...What you want is for the Spirit Department to continue to exist."

,Yes. It's not that I'm receiving special treatment because I'm the only one in the Spirit Department, but that the academy is simply taking care of even a small number of people. Anyway, the Spirit Department will disappear when I graduate. Is there any need to forcibly eliminate it now?"

,The situation won't change just because you persuade me. There are over 20 teachers who want the Spirit Department to be dismantled. Do you think you can persuade all of them?"

,Well, it seems like I only need to persuade you."

,I will never give up. I recommend you look into other departments instead of wasting your time."

,If the Spirit Department gives up its budget, will the dismantling be called off?"

,What's the point of a department without a budget? In my opinion, the Spirit Department is a department that doesn't help the academy students at all. That building is just taking up land. The Spirit Department should be eliminated."

,Actually, giving up the budget was just something I said. Teacher Kales-ssi and I have no intention of giving up the Spirit Department's budget. And of course, dismantling the Spirit Department is out of the question."

,......"

There was no compromise. Kim Jeo's eyes were saying that.

,Let's make a bet on the dismantling of the Spirit Department. If you win, I'll cleanly accept the dismantling of the Spirit Department."

,That's ridiculous. The dismantling of the Spirit Department will be decided at the next meeting. Since that's the general opinion, the Chairman will have no choice but to accept it."

,So, if we win the bet, I'd like you to help us maintain the Spirit Department."

,I said there's no reason to make a bet."

,Really none?"

I looked around.

Three first-year students. One second-year student. Three third-year students. A total of seven students. Among them, the first-year students' faces hardened when they met my eyes.

,Do you know who my mom is?"

,...It would be stranger if I didn't. Why, are you going to go to your mother and whine about keeping the Spirit Department alive?"

,Yes. That's what I'll do. I'll ask her to get rid of the Cursed Object Department."

,Ha, that's useless. Do you think the academy would be pressured by outsiders?"

,My mom is the president of the parents' association. She's not an outsider. And... with my mom's power... she could handle someone like you."

I deliberately cut myself off and twisted the corners of my mouth. Kim Jeo didn't object even though he guessed what I was going to say. He kept his mouth shut. What flashed in his eyes was fear.

,Are you curious about how persistent I can be? Let me tell you in advance. I'm confident I can be persistent all the way to hell."

,......"

Kim Jeo's jaw twitched. He was clenching his teeth.

,Can we really end this cleanly with a bet?"

,I'll ask the Chairman and Vice-Chairman to notarize it."

,What are the terms of the bet?"

,A match between the Spirit Department and the Cursed Object Department. Isn't it simple? You can decide the details of the match. I recommend a head-on confrontation. It'll be over quickly, won't it?"

,The Spirit Department has two people, you and Teacher Kales-ssi. Do you really think you can beat us?"

,Even if the Cursed Object Department gangs up on me, they can't beat me. Because that's a fact."

Badeudeuk.

The sound of Kim Jeo grinding his teeth was clear.

,...Fine. I'll report the bet to the Chairman and Vice-Chairman and get their approval. It'll be a good opportunity to show the academy the incompetence of the Spirit Department...."

,Then I'll take that as a yes and be on my way."

I got up from my seat.

,But since this is the Cursed Object Department, there are quite a few interesting things."

,...Don't touch anything and get out. These aren't things you can handle."

,I'll just take a look. Just a look."

There were many strange things, such as a spoon with blood droplets, a portrait with moving eyes, a psychic photo with a ghost, a notebook with a red name, and a dried-up leg.

But what caught my interest more than anything else here was a female student in the corner.

,The ace of the Cursed Object Department. The decisive reason why Kim Jeo accepted the bet."

She was short. She was one year older than me, but she looked about five years younger.

Her pale white hair reached her waist, and she had dull red eyes. Underneath her academy uniform, she had white bandages wrapped around her entire body. Her neck and part of her face were also wrapped in bandages.

Shin Nari. Class 3, 2nd grade. Ranked 65th.

Her grades were 65th, but her skills were among the top of the 2nd graders.

,......??"

Shin Nari tilted her head when I stared at her.

I activated my Spirit Eyes. My eyes glowed gold. At the same time, I could see the entity behind Shin Nari's body.

It was a female ghost. To be more precise, it was a higher-ranking evil spirit than a ghost.

Long black hair, red skin, hollow eyes, and black fingernails over 10cm long. The evil spirit was hugging Shin Nari from behind and looking at me.

,It looks even worse than it did in the game."

Shin Nari checked the direction of my gaze and opened her mouth.

,You can see it."

,My eyes are a bit special, so I can see things well. Senior, can I help you?"

,...Of the people who said they would help me, only one person has actually helped me properly."

The person Shin Nari was talking about was Kim Jeo. I shrugged. Her reaction was as expected. She wouldn't trust me and follow me just because I said a few words. It was more certain to show Shin Nari through direct action.

I deactivated my Spirit Eyes and left the Cursed Object Department with Kales-ssi.

,Yujin-ah. Were you planning to make a bet with the Cursed Object Department from the beginning?"

,Yes."

If it were somewhere else, I could have ended it nicely by contacting Jin Ryungsung or Sung Hari. But this was the academy. The names of Jin Ryungsung and Sung Hari didn't work well.

,If I really step up, I can easily handle someone like Kim Jeo... but it's better to go this way if I want to get Shin Nari."

Shin Nari wasn't my type.

,But I can't give a beauty like Shin Nari to someone else."

I was also planning to take care of other things in the Cursed Object Department.

,...Just because the Cursed Object Department is small doesn't mean it's easy to deal with. Some people fear curses more than magic. Do you have a way to deal with curses?"

,I'll tell you this especially, Teacher Kales-ssi. No curse can bind me."

I said confidently, but Kales-ssi just rubbed my shoulder as if worried.
