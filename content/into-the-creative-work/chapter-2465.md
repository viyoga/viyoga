Peong!

The water cannon that hit my outstretched fist explodes, creating a shockwave. The water droplets scattering in all directions were like shrapnel from a grenade.

,That's quite powerful for water magic.'

But I was wearing a protective aura of Cheonma energy. With the power of a grenade, it can never penetrate my protective aura.

Peong! Peongpeongpeong!

I deflect the water cannons and confidently walk forward.

A huge magic circle appeared in front of the magic tower. A large amount of water is shot like a laser from the center of the magic circle.

,I can't just deflect that.'

I focus my mind on my right fist and unleash it.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Dragon Fist.

The black Cheonma energy fired from my fist extends in a straight line, cutting through the water stream and reaching the wall of the magic tower. A transparent barrier that rose from the wall blocked the attack.

,I knew there would be a defense.'

Through my Cheonan, I saw complex magic formulas surrounding the inside and outside of the magic tower, but I didn't have enough magical knowledge to know what kind of magic it was. If I were in the [Dark Moon] world, I might have understood the formula the moment I saw it and used it in reverse.

,It doesn't matter even if I don't know. The Aquari Magic Tower can't stop me now.'

I straightened my shoulders and confidently headed towards the magic tower.

,Surrender now and swear allegiance to me. Then I promise you good treatment."

-To come all the way here and tell us to become your subordinates? Did you think our school was that easy to mess with? I'll make you regret it!

,You refuse even when I give you a chance. You wizards are so stupid."

I got closer to the entrance of the magic tower.

A mass of water soared from a puddle near the magic tower. It was a soldier made of water. From the body to the armor, to the weapons like spears and swords they held, everything was made of water.

Because they were made of water, they didn't look strong.

However, the fact that there were dozens of them was annoying.

The water soldiers rushed at me all at once.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step.

With a single step, dozens of water soldiers collapsed and seeped into the puddle. Their essence is ultimately magic. As the magic was disrupted by the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step, they returned to their original water form.

-What the hell?! There's no way our magic is so weak that it can be broken so easily...!

,This is the step that dominates space, the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step."

Giving off the pressure of a living creature is an additional function of the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step.

The key to the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step is to dominate space. Like now, it can shock the essence of formations or magic and break them down.

,Even so, it has no effect on the magic tower.'

It was a relief.

If the magic tower had collapsed with just one use of the Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step, I would have been very disappointed.

-Don't act so cocky...! Water becomes stronger when it becomes one!

The huge puddle of water writhed and soared high into the sky. It seemed like it was trying to fall on me and crush me to death.

,If it's only this much, there's nothing to see."

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demonic Descent Step.

The magic circle that controlled the water shook and collapsed. There was no momentum like before, it just poured down like a fountain.

A tightly closed iron door. Unusually, there was no handle.

-...Ha, okay. I'll admit you're amazing. But do you think you can take on the magic tower alone?

,You guys.... Do you want to work at a water park instead of a magic tower? Even kids would like this level of water splashing."
