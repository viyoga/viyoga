As much as I wanted to torture the Blood Demon right then and there, I had things to do. I had to return to the Cheonma Shin-gyo and end the civil war. No, not a civil war, a purge. Because I was the one who won.

,Even if you're at the Harmony Realm of Water, you're still at the Harmony Realm. Just because you don't have arms and legs doesn't mean you can't pull any tricks. Especially since you're a wicked sorcerer."

Carrying this guy around was a pain in the ass. I planned to toss him to some nearby subordinates and order them to keep an eye on him.

Fortunately, Cheonma Shingong had an excellent acupressure point technique.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Bongcheon (Sealed Heaven).

Cheonma energy dug into the Blood Demon's body, sealing his meridians and suppressing his inner core. The Blood Demon might be at the Harmony Realm, but so was I. This would neutralize him.

,Kkeueueueueu...."

The Blood Demon trembled and pissed himself. His eyes were half-gone. The forced sealing of his acupressure points had caused him immense pain, deprivation, and fear. To the Blood Demon, a sorcerer of the Harmony Realm, it must have felt like going from being human to suddenly being a bug.

,What a filthy bastard."

I reacted immediately, so no dirty Urine got on me.

Not wanting to touch the Blood Demon directly, I used telekinesis to lift him and flew to the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

,S-Small Cheonma-nim! Let's make a deal, a deal! I know all sorts of sorcery and martial arts passed down from the Blood Cult! I even know how to maintain eternal youth! Aren't you tempted by the Blood Cult's secret treasures?!"

I sneered at the Blood Demon. My Mind's Eye of Gold discerned the truth. The claim about hidden Blood Cult treasures was a lie.

,You think I'd believe that crap? If you had such great treasures, you would've used them already."

,It's a treasure that's hard to use in battle! Its purpose is for cultivation!"

,Your voice is too raspy; I don't even want to hear it."

I flicked my index finger. Sword energy moved freely, slicing out the Blood Demon's vocal cords. Blood flowed from the Blood Demon's throat. The bleeding stopped quickly without any special measures. Even though he was suppressed, he still had the body of a Transformation Realm expert. Ordinary wounds would heal on their own.

Arriving at the Cheonma Shin-gyo, I moved towards the seemingly deserted Baehyeol Fortress.

It must be awkward for them. They followed me, but there was the issue of being an external force, making it difficult to jump into the Cheonma Shin-gyo's civil war without hesitation.

Small Blood Emperor Ryu Mil-won and Blood Flower Ryu Yang-mi approached me.

,Brother-in-law. Congratulations on your victory. The Cheonma Shin-gyo people have started fighting each other... As Baehyeol Fortress, it's hard for us to intervene, so we've been maintaining neutrality. If you allow it, we'll jump in right away."

,I don't understand why they're fighting when victory has already tilted. But husband... is that the Blood Demon?"

,I understand. Baehyeol Fortress is an ally, but you couldn't intervene without my permission. Good job. This is an internal matter for the Cheonma Shin-gyo. If you get involved, things will only get more complicated. The elders are resisting to the end because they know they'll eventually die by my hand. Yang-mi, take care of the Blood Demon."

I dropped the Blood Demon at Ryu Yang-mi's feet. The Blood Demon gaped and writhed. Ryu Yang-mi's gaze as she looked down at him was chilling.

,It's a Pressure point restriction. An excellent Pressure point technique. Husband, may I torture the Blood Demon?"

,Don't kill him. Even death is too good for this bastard."

,Don't worry. I'll torture his mind, not his body."

Ryu Yang-mi's inner energy moved, transforming into a spell. Red blood energy flowed from her body. The Blood Demon desperately gaped, trying to say something. Of course, Ryu Yang-mi didn't stop.

I flew to where the civil war was taking place.

,Stop! I am the new Cheonma! There's no need for us to fight each other!"

The battlefield fell silent for a moment. The Shin-gyo people's gazes turned to me.

,Fight!"

It was the Great Elder, who was fighting Sword Demon, who shouted. The well-built old man continued to shout, veins popping in his neck.

,Do you think Small Cheonma will spare us?! Small Cheonma is exhausted! Look at those wounds! He's trying to stop the battle because he has no energy left!"

The momentarily halted battle began again.

I smiled inwardly with pleasure.

I would have been in trouble if they had actually stopped fighting. I was planning to purge them all this time.

,Great Elder, you're making a foolish choice. And the Cheonma's protectors are trying to kill me instead of protecting me? I am the rightful Cheonma. You should be protecting me, the supreme being whose orders you must obey."

The Right Protector and Left Protector swallowed hard, watching me carefully. They looked conflicted as they watched me. The Great Elder shouted, putting his inner energy into his voice.

,Small Cheonma!!"

,Not Small Cheonma. Cheonma."

,...New Cheonma! If we acknowledge you, will you spare us?!"

,You are traitors. If you had succeeded, it might have been different. But since you failed, isn't your fate sealed? However, today is a good day. I will show special mercy and spare some of you, except for the ringleaders."

,What a crazy bastard...!! Aren't you the traitor!!"

,What nonsense are you spouting? I am the Cheonma, and aren't you the traitors who are rebelling against me, the Cheonma?"

I chuckled and sneered at the Great Elder. The Great Elder distorted his face and vented his anger.

,Look! The new Cheonma has no intention of sparing us! We must kill him to survive!!"

,Hmm. If I die, who will become the leader of the Cheonma Shin-gyo? Great Elder, are you trying to become the new Cheonma? You, who haven't even mastered the Cheonma Shingong?"

The Cheonma Shingong remains with the Cheon family (天家)! We will choose the rightful successor of the Cheonma! The Cheonma Shingong will not be defeated.

,Huh. Select the Cheonma? What qualifications do you have? It's so absurd it's not even funny."

The Cheonma Shin-gyo began with the Four Demonic Venerable Families! What would you, an outsider, know?

The Great Elder, gathering his inner energy, threw a punch at me. A massive fist aura of crimson energy hurtled toward me. True to his name, Soul-Chasing Fist Demon (追魂拳魔), it was a swift and powerful fist aura.

,It would be difficult to block it in my current exhausted state. So..."

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Pacheonhwang (破天荒).

Breaking one's own soul, burning one's karma, and gaining immense power.

Overwhelming power erupted from my body.

Cheonma energy wrapped around my entire body, and black lightning naturally manifested. My hair shone white and soared upwards.

The Great Elder's fist aura disappeared as soon as it touched the Cheonma energy flowing out of me, before it could even reach my body. The Great Elder widened his eyes in shock.

I looked down at the Cheonma Shin-gyo and raised my right foot.

,I will personally engrave what a Cheonma is into your bodies and souls."

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

An intangible energy wave pressed down on the entire Cheonma Shin-gyo.

There were two reactions.

Those who prostrated themselves on the ground and submitted to me. They were sincerely moved by me and avoided disaster.

And those who dared to resist me. They vomited blood and were forced to kneel. They looked at me with blank expressions. I could feel their fear, their anguish.

,Cheonma Rules Over All Demons, Countless Demons Bow. I have already shown mercy.

I slowly raised my right foot and stepped into the air.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

Kuuuung!

An intense energy wave spread out.

The bodies of all the demons who did not submit swelled up and burst. Their blood and entrails scattered uselessly on the ground.

The Left Protector, Right Protector, and elders were no exception.

,...Is this what a Cheonma is...!"

Even the Great Elder, who had held out until the end, eventually burst and died.

I slowly looked around the Cheonma Shin-gyo. There was no one who didn't bow their head to me.

I raised my head and looked at the sky. I didn't feel the Emperor's gaze. The Emperor had little interest in the Cheonma Shin-gyo or the Murim.

,If I fought the Emperor in my current state... I would lose. Pacheonhwang temporarily provides immense power, but it doesn't forcibly raise my realm. It's hard to close the gap in realm with mere power, especially at higher realms."

I have no intention of fighting the Emperor. The position of being second to none was more comfortable than I had imagined. It was like... pleasure without responsibility. Of course, not sex.

,I've achieved one of my long-term goals. But I don't intend to stop here."

The one truth I realized while experiencing countless game worlds.

,Only the strong can enjoy everything. I must become stronger."

I reached out my hand into the air.

The Cheonma Throne and Cheonma Sword, which had fallen nearby, flew to me.

I sat on the Cheonma Throne and held the Cheonma Sword in my hand.

,But I still have to enjoy what I can."

The Blood Demon died after being tortured for over a week. He didn't just die. He was offered to a being of hell by Ryu Yang-mi's sorcery.

The dead Blood Demon, in his soul state, was sucked into the underworld, mocking the Cheonma and the Blood Goddess.

,Foolish bastards. Do they think the beings of hell easily accept offerings? And they even want me to suffer eternally in hell as the price for the offering?"

The offering and the wish overlapped. In reality, there was no point in making the offering. It couldn't be fulfilled unless the being of hell showed great generosity.

,How picky are the beings of hell? There's no way it'll be fulfilled without other offerings. But shouldn't a grim reaper appear when someone dies? If possible, I hope the grim reaper takes me to King Chogang."

I had made quite a few offerings to King Chogang. Although it didn't end well, King Chogang had received something, so he would make a good judgment.

,I heard that even sinners in hell can cultivate. If I meet King Chogang and things go well, I might be able to work as a warden instead of a sinner."

The Blood Demon fell into hell at the end of a long passage.

Something was wrong.

Instead of grim reapers, the surroundings were full of devils. The devils all had ferocious faces and powerful strength. The devils' eyes were fixed on the Blood Demon.

,Why is a human here?"

,He's mastered a demonic art. Looks delicious."

,Ooh! Let's offer him to that person! He's a precious one, so that person will be pleased too!"

The devils suddenly started to get excited. The Blood Demon rolled his eyes, assessing the atmosphere.

That's when it happened.

The excited devils suddenly knelt on the floor of hell and bowed their heads in one direction.

,Ah! That person is coming!!"

,Our heaven...!"

Appearing from the endless distance was a black being. A being covered in thick black demonic energy.

The Blood Demon trembled. He instinctively realized that his level was different. The Blood Demon bowed his head, feeling grateful. He instinctively realized that he could still maintain his sanity because he was hiding his true form.

,Ah, welcome to hell, A Ssei!"
