,Eum. Sex."

It was so delicious that my legs almost gave out and I nearly collapsed.

I habitually put my tongue to the Holy Grail, but not a single drop remained. 100 tons of gold had turned into less than 100ml and disappeared into my stomach.

,There's no time to keep indulging in the afterglow.'

I shook off my disappointment and took out Arta's Elixir from my inventory. I dropped the elixir onto the gold in the warehouse. The elixir turned into gas the moment it touched the gold. The gas filled the warehouse and turned the gold into random metals.

Iron, lead, aluminum, zinc, tin, and so on. It turned into metals other than gold. The 200 tons of gold that had filled the warehouse disappeared in an instant.

「The effect of Arta's Elixir lasts for 30 minutes.」

I watched Fafnir from outside the warehouse.

The effect was immediate. Mercenaries used to be cursed just by getting near Fafnir, but not anymore. Fafnir's momentum weakened by more than 30%.

,Not even half, but 30%.'

Roughly, it's 10% per 100 tons.

,The cost-effectiveness is the worst.'

South Korea's gold reserves are about 100 tons, right? Even though I had three times that amount, I only got this much stronger. Andvari's warehouse wasn't that tempting.

,Even weakened, Fafnir is still Fafnir.'

More than 20 mercenaries had already been taken down. The weakened Fafnir is still stronger than the mercenaries.

,Die more.'

I watched the battle with my arms crossed. It's more helpful for the mercenaries to die here than to leave alive. The Reaper's contract has already been broken, so if there are any survivors, I'm going to kill them myself.

,I'll create an opening!"

Omikos charged towards the front of Fafnir. He was as dignified as Siegfried in mythology.

-Do you think you're Siegfried?!

Fafnir retorted. He seemed to have the same thought. Omikos was amazing. He was holding out against Fafnir, who was dozens of times bigger than a human, almost evenly.

Mercenaries rush in through his opening. They shoot arrows, approach and swing swords, and attack with magic.

,The attacks are working!"

,He's much weaker than before!"

,He's bleeding! We can kill him! We can kill him!"

Fafnir opened his mouth wide. At that, Omikos shouted urgently.

,It's breath! Evade it!"

The mercenaries avoided the snout in perfect order. Most of them clung to Fafnir's body. Because of the nature of breathing out of the mouth, the closer you are, the safer you become.

A sneer appeared in Fafnir's eyes. The curse spewed out from Fafnir's body. The breath was a trick.

Hwaaaaaaak!

The curse, which spread like black poisonous smoke, swept through the mercenaries. Most of the mercenaries who touched the curse collapsed on the floor. Only those who could barely move were those who had some resistance to the curse.

-You! How dare you cast magic at me? I'll tear your limbs apart and kill you slowly!

Fafnir smiled wickedly and began to play cruelly with one of the collapsed mercenaries. Ironically, the sight was as pure as a child catching and dissecting a bug.

Omikos shouted.

,Why should I?"

,We were supposed to fight together! That was the contract!"

,You mercenaries broke the contract. You ignored the contract first, but I should keep the contract? Are you out of your mind?"

I sneered at Omikos to my heart's content.

,I'm sorry about that! My colleagues were not in their right minds as if they were possessed by something! I'll pay the price for my mistakes later! Shouldn't we survive first! We need to join forces to defeat Fafnir."

It was the right thing to say. It's better to join forces to kill Fafnir. No one could deny it. Wasn't that why I hired those mercenaries to gang up on Fafnir?

But the situation had changed. The wisdom I gained from the Golden Holy Grail was telling me the answer.

,Well. It seems like you have to pay the price now."

,......?!"
