After roughly looking around the city of monsters, I realized it wasn't much different from a human city.

,The technical level of the buildings and the aesthetic sense are a bit lacking.'

Still, it wasn't at a very crude level.

It felt like they valued efficiency, and it was relatively clean, as if they did some cleaning. At least the main roads running through the city were.

,The monsters use other monsters instead of horses or donkeys.'

Most of them tamed monsters without intelligence. Even if they had intelligence, monsters without power were used as slaves.

,There are ant monsters, rabbit monsters, iron plate monsters, and even mosquito monsters, but no monkey monsters.'

I know the reason. Even now, the eyes of the monsters looking at me, a monkey monster, aren't kind. If I hadn't subtly leaked my demonic energy and revealed my skills, some of them would have deliberately picked a fight.

,But they lower their eyes when my gaze reaches them.'

I put the iron staff on my shoulder and continued walking along the main street. My destination was the largest building in the center of the main street. I planned to meet the owner of this city there and ask about the descendants of Chaos. A great monster would know something.

As I headed towards the city center, the smell of blood hit me. It was a bit different from the smell of monster blood.

,Come on, come on, it's not every day you see this! Fresh human meat here!'

,Selling the miraculous innards of a Five Viscera Realm human sorcerer!'

,We also have young human children! Boys and girls! Both are virgins! Good for magic ingredients, or you can breed them, raise them, and eat them!'

,Buying Five Viscera Realm humans with fire attribute!'

Monsters were shouting here and there. Human trafficking was in full swing. Many monsters watched the merchandise in front of the stores.

,Human meat is best from women. It's so tender, you know?'

,Taste aside, Martial artists and sorcerers have spiritual energy, don't they? Perfect for nourishing the body.'

,I'm thinking of raising humans myself this time... Humans are good, but they grow so slowly.'

,You can use magic to make them grow faster. However, the quality is lower than natural ones. Unless you're just using them for meat, get natural ones.'

,This human has no wounds and is born with wood attribute energy. Shopkeeper-yang. Where did you get this top-quality item?'

,I brought them in through the sea. Hehe. I didn't hunt them myself... I trade with humans professionally.'

I didn't expect to see humans here.

But when I thought about it, it wasn't surprising. Just because they're monsters doesn't mean they're stuck in the Monster Mirror.

This continent is disgustingly vast, so even an empire can't perfectly control its borders and seas. It's not strange to have smugglers.

I looked at the humans who had become monster slaves. They were naked, with their hands and feet bound by chains. Their faces were all filled with despair. I looked at the women. There weren't any pretty ones.

I just looked around roughly and went inside.

There was a butcher shop. It sold chopped corpses of monsters and humans alike.

,The security is surprisingly tight. Why? I don't see any soldiers maintaining security separately.'

I killed a cow-headed monster at the city entrance. But there were no sanctions against me. The reaction of the monsters nearby was surprisingly quiet.

,There's a rule in this city that I don't know about. Maybe it's not just this city, but a rule of the Monster Mirror.'

Should I try killing a few monsters as a test? Then I'll know how the security of this city is maintained, whether I like it or not.

,No. It's not too late to do it after I'm done with my business.'

I confidently entered the entrance of the building in the city center. The door was wide open, as if inviting anyone to come in. There was no gatekeeper. Instead, the monsters around me looked at me. They didn't stop me, they just watched.

I crossed the gate. The air stuck to me thickly. I knew there was poison mixed in the air. An invisible barrier was preventing the poison from escaping.

[Absorbed poison. Resisting poison.]

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

,This isn't ordinary poison. It's a deadly poison that works even on absolute masters.'

It was actually a good environment for me, who had a poison resistance trait. Over time, my resistance to poison increased, and only my vitality and mana were restored.

I slowly walked into the large building.

Inside, a huge red snake was coiled up. The two-headed snake monster shook its purple tongue and said. Its golden eyes with vertical pupils shone even in the darkness.

,You're the monkey monster who caused a commotion at the city entrance. What brings you here?'

,This body is Son Yujin, descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven. I have something to ask you.'

,Hehehehe. I can see it now that you're closer. Monkey monster. You're hiding your power, aren't you?'

,You put demonic energy in your voice. Don't try to seduce me with your wicked voice.'

,Hehehe. So, what do you want to ask?'

The large snake moved quietly. From the ceiling, from the walls, from the floor. It moved little by little, surrounding me as if to envelop me.

I tapped the iron staff on my shoulder.

,I'm looking for the descendants of Chaos.'

,Ah. Those descendants of Chaos? They're deep in the north. They've hidden their identities to protect the treasure and built a force called the White Peak Ship. But everyone who knows, knows. The monsters at the center of the White Peak Ship are the descendants of Chaos.'

,Can I see them if I keep going north from here? About how far is it?'

,Hmmmm. About 300,000 li? If you keep going north, you'll see a pure white mountain range. It looks like a ship, so you'll recognize it right away.'

I summoned my smartphone and calculated it.

,Is that a magical artifact? I don't feel any spiritual energy at all.'

I ignored the snake's useless comment.

Approximately 117,900 km

As far as I know, the distance from Korea to the United States is about 10,000 km.

,Is that the distance of going back and forth between Korea and the United States six times? What the fuck. That's damn far.'

But in terms of the scale of this world, 300,000 li was short.

,Is there anything else you're curious about?'

,How is the security of this city maintained?'

,Hoo. Hoo. You're asking such common sense questions, so you must be a monster from outside the Monster Mirror! Well, a monkey monster born in this land running around openly doesn't make sense. Especially a great monster-level monkey monster like you suddenly appearing!'

,Yes. I came from outside the Monster Mirror. I had expectations because it was called the Monster Mirror. But it's not that great.'

,Hehehehe. Still, in the Monster Mirror, you don't have to worry about human armies.'

,So, how is the security of this city maintained?'

,The monsters of the Monster Mirror belong to forces, whether big or small. If you kill a monster smaller than you recklessly, that force will be angry and become hostile.'

,Then wouldn't there be conflicts between forces?'

,Before a conflict arises, they determine who's at fault. It's common for the one who causes the problem to be expelled from the force.'

Monsters form forces and protect themselves. And those forces are bound to be intertwined here and there.

,Have you found out everything you were curious about?'

,Yes. I appreciate you trying to stall for time, but this body's constitution is a bit special, so poison doesn't work.'

,Hehehehe! Morons only know one thing and not two. I hid real poison in the poison! You won't be able to use your demonic energy! I'll take the price for your questions!'

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

I've long since adapted to that poison hidden in the poison.

,Dumbass. I told you it doesn't work.'

The bastard rushes at me. I created clouds under my feet and rose high. I broke through the ceiling and came outside.

I raised my left hand to chest height and made a mudra that encapsulated the incantation by raising only my index and middle fingers, and activated the magic.

Thunder Sky Style Thunder Art Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.

Pajijik!

My pupils turned gold, and my irises turned blue, emitting lightning.

By looking at the red snake monster, I could find out information.

The red snake monster's name was Ssanghong. Its realm was Three Flowers Completion Stage 3rd level. It had two heads, but only one soul. It had lived for 330 years and 173 days.

,The magic to control clouds and those eyes...!! Could it be that you truly are a descendant of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven?!!'

,I told you that from the beginning.'

Ssanghong's two heads licked their purple tongues and laughed.

,Hehehehe! I'll eat you! I'll eat you and gain the power of the Great Sage Equaling Heaven!!'

Ssanghong revealed its blatant greed and soared upwards. It was just a brief flying magic. An inefficient magic not worth imitating.

,Grow.'

The iron staff in my right hand grew to over 10m. The problem with this simple magic was that the weight didn't change.

But there was a way. Because I can use two kinds of star power at the same time.

I swung the iron staff and used the light of the Heavenly Dipper Star at the right timing.

,Using the light of the Heavenly Dipper Star.

,Dark Star of the Earth』

The Dark Star of the Earth's ability is the power to manipulate mass. The weight of the iron staff increased hundreds of times. A weight that was even difficult for me to swing with my strength.

The iron staff crushed Ssanghong's left head as it was, and dropped Ssanghong's large body to the floor. I threw the iron staff at that body. The mass itself became a weapon, crushing Ssanghong.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!'

Ssanghong screamed with its one remaining head. At the same time as drawing attention, it secretly manipulated the barrier surrounding the entire city with magic.

,Futile effort.'

I could see everything through the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil. I waved my left hand in the air. Demonic energy flowed out and destroyed the core of the barrier. With just that, the barrier was lifted.

I summoned the iron staff again and held it in my right hand.

Ssanghong, who had fallen to the floor, trembled.

,Poison and barriers. Are you afraid now that your tricks have been blocked?'

,Plea, please spare me. I'll give you everything in this city in return!!'

,I don't need it.'

A blade of wind flew from a short distance away. Tengu who had hidden their appearance with wind appeared and attacked me. It was the Great Tengu Katsuragi that the Crow Tengu had mentioned.

Knowing this already, I manipulated the clouds to fall to the ground and infused lightning energy into the iron staff.

Pajijijik!

Blue lightning energy gathered at the end of the iron staff, forming a spearhead.

Thunder Sky Style Spear Art Lightning Cut.

The spearhead, maximized with lightning energy, cut off Ssanghong's head. An unusually black blood spurted out and soaked my body.

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

As Ssanghong died, the barrier that had been trapping the poison also disappeared. The invisible poison combined with the poison coming from Ssanghong's corpse and spread throughout the city.

,Aaaaaaaak!'

Monsters and humans alike screamed and died. The Five Viscera Realm monsters managed to hold out for a while... but in the end, they had no choice but to be poisoned and die. The lucky ones were the monsters on the outskirts of the city. Among them, the ones with good instincts ran away before touching the poison.

,Even if it can't fight, a great monster is a great monster. It killed an entire city with poison in an instant.'

I looked up at the sky. The Tengu were gathered in one place, blocking the poison from reaching them with wind magic.

How should I kill those things? As I pondered, I remembered the White Peak Ship that Ssanghong had mentioned.

,It's a force created with determination by the descendants of Chaos. Even if I don't know, it's not something I can handle alone.'

The descendants of Chaos are monsters who have survived for a long time.

,Katsuragi. Let's make a deal. I'll give you Ssanghong's inner core. In return, you guys help me.'

,Help you? With what?'

,I'm going to attack the White Peak Ship.'

,The White Peak Ship? You're a crazy bastard!'

,If you don't want to make a deal, I can't help it. Die here.'
