Having passed through Dokgo City, I returned to the Cheonma Shin-gyo (Demonic Cult).

The Cheonma Shin-gyo was both busy and idle due to the war with the Murim Alliance. A war but idle? It was an unfitting combination of words, but it was the truth.

,This world is so vast that even if a war breaks out, it's not like the enemies will come charging in the next day.

The war between the Murim Alliance and the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

Saying it like this makes it sound like a big deal, but this war depends on your point of view. In the end, both the Cheonma Shin-gyo and the Murim Alliance are within the Jin Empire. If you look at it in a fantasy style, it's closer to a territorial war.

,In the end, if the Emperor intervenes, the war will end in an instant.

With a single word from the Emperor, both the Murim Alliance and the Cheonma Shin-gyo would have no choice but to grovel.

,Is the Emperor's goal to reduce the power of the Murim forces? Assuming the Emperor doesn't intervene, if this war continues as it is....

The war won't end immediately and will continue for at least 10 years. Maybe even 200 years or more.

,This world is too large in scale.

Both spatially and temporally.

Anyway, it means that we're not doing anything immediately. The Murim Alliance and the Cheonma Shin-gyo are too far apart, so a full-scale war is difficult. And both sides are avoiding a full-scale war. If the scale of the war becomes too large, the Emperor might intervene.

"You're back."

As I entered the room, Cheon Yu-un said to me in a light tone. He's the protagonist of [I Woke Up and Became a Cheonma]. Roughly speaking, a possessed Cheonma. As the son of the current Cheonma, he held considerable power.

,Well, it's not like he's the only one with the Cheonma's blood.

Cheon Yu-un is the only direct descendant. Moreover, Cheon Yu-un is a talented individual who's showing promise. You could say he's the closest to becoming the next Cheonma.

I sat down in front of Cheon Yu-un. Cheon Yu-un's hand moved, preparing tea. A fragrant tea scent spread. It must be expensive tea.

,Did he put poison in it?

Cheon Yu-un was a sly bastard. He's the type who doesn't care about the means as long as he achieves his goals. Usually, web novel protagonists don't use poison, but this guy will use it if necessary.

Cheon Yu-un drank the tea. I also drank the tea. There was no poison in it. It was only natural since I'm Cheon Yu-un's subordinate right now.

"Yeom Gu-seok. You've become stronger since I last saw you. I heard you turned the Dokgo Clan upside down?"

The Dokgo Clan entered seclusion. This also meant that they would be withdrawing from this war.

"The Dokgo Clan wasn't as strong as I'd heard. They're nothing compared to the Five Great Righteous Clans."

"They're lacking compared to the Five Great Clans. But still, it's the Dokgo Clan. A family that was once called the best in the world. To force such a family into seclusion alone... When did you get so strong?"

Cheon Yu-un's gaze sharpened.

My intuition warns me. I'm drinking warm tea, but the chilling sensation doesn't disappear.

,Is he testing me? Does he suspect me? Why?

I don't know. He's so skilled at hiding his true feelings that I have no idea what he suspects.

"The effects of the Black Heaven Devouring Art were great. I was able to grow stronger quickly by sucking up the internal energy of the Murim Alliance bastards."

"That's the greatest advantage of the Black Heaven Devouring Art. But don't forget that the Black Heaven Devouring Art has clear limitations. The moment you misjudge those limitations even slightly, your body will explode and you'll die."

"I know. Because it's internal energy that wasn't originally mine. I'm working hard to make that internal energy my own."

Cheon Yu-un nodded. His sharp gaze shifted from me to the teacup. He quietly took a sip of tea.

I thought about the Yeom Gu-seok that I'm currently playing. He's hot-blooded and has a quick temper. Cheon Yu-un knew that fact well.

"Why did you call me? I don't think you called me just to chat."

"There's something I want you to do."

"Something you want me to do?"

"Something annoying happened recently. Someone broke into my room."

"An assassin?"

"I don't know. What's certain is that the traces were almost non-existent. I wouldn't have even known someone broke in if I hadn't set up some kind of device."

The original story came to mind. Cheon Yu-un sets up devices on his room's window and door. Well, it's embarrassing to call them devices. It's just enough to know if the door has been opened. Like deliberately putting dust on the window, or marking the doorframe.

"It could be a thief."

"No, nothing was stolen. It's highly likely they had another purpose."

"...And?"

"I want you to track down and catch him."

"Impossible. How am I supposed to track down a criminal who's already disappeared?"

"There's a clue. As I said before, I set up a device."

Cheon Yu-un grinned. He stood up from his seat and brought something from the corner of the room.

It was a small steel cage about the size of a palm. Inside the steel cage was a red rat. Like a prisoner in a jail, it was grabbing the bars and looking in one direction.

"Blood Rat?"

The red monster rat that eats blood was famous in many ways.

"No. This is a Manri Seojong (Ten Thousand Li Rat Tracker). It has the characteristic of chasing the Ten Thousand Li Tracking Incense that it's been imprinted with. The Ten Thousand Li Tracking Incense was left on the criminal's clothes by the device. Take this guy and track down the criminal."

Ten Thousand Li Tracking Incense. It's a scent, but it's a special scent that humans can't smell. I heard that you need a special method to smell this scent.

,I didn't know there was such a thing as a Manri Seojong.

I held back a sigh that was about to come out of my mouth. It was very annoying, but since Cheon Yu-un called me separately and ordered me directly, I couldn't refuse.

"Do I really have to do it? It's not like there aren't guys who specialize in tracking for nothing."

"Given the current situation, I don't want to make things bigger."

You do it. I had no choice but to nod at the eyes filled with firm determination.

"He's not inside the Cheonma Shin-gyo, right?"

"If he was, I would have already caught him. You'll know by looking at where the Manri Seojong's snout is pointing, but he headed west."

I looked at the Manri Seojong trapped in the cage. The small, red rat was only looking in one direction. That must be west.

"Is there anything special in the west?"

"Nothing comes to mind."

Cheon Yu-un put down his teacup. The bottom of the teacup was visible.

"Then I'll leave it to you."

I wasn't so clueless that I couldn't understand the dismissal order. I got up from my seat and went outside the building.

The gray sky was especially dry. The blowing wind was as cold as a blade.

"Winter is coming."

I deliberately mumbled, pretending to look cool. I had already achieved Hanseobulchim (Unaffected by Cold and Heat) long ago, so I didn't care whether it was winter or summer.

,Cheon Yu-un is the biggest obstacle to me becoming the Cheonma. I have to kill him to become the Cheonma.

It's not like I'll immediately become the Cheonma if Cheon Yu-un dies, but I'll have a chance if Cheon Yu-un is gone.

,It would be better if I could use him until the end... but I feel like he suspects me.

I didn't have any immediate ideas. I trudged towards the dormitory.

『Attendance Event! Rewards are given for every week of attendance! Currently, 7 days of attendance have been completed.』

『You have achieved one week of attendance. Rewards are given randomly.』

『You have been given Honbaek Jeongsu (Soul Spirit Pure Water).』

『Honbaek Jeongsu (Soul Spirit Pure Water)

This is water imbued with a portion of the soul and spirit. It clears the mind.』

As soon as I opened my eyes in the morning, I received a spirit medicine from the Cheongangseong System.

It was the attendance reward I received every week.

,The one I received a while ago came out again.

Honbaek Jeongsu was a decent spirit medicine. If you drink it, your internal energy increases slightly, and it partially purifies evil energy. It was especially effective for those who had learned Demonic or Evil Arts.

I rummaged around and packed the Honbaek Jeongsu. I was currently abstaining from spirit medicines. Because the effects weren't very good even if I took them.

,Mediocre spirit medicines aren't very helpful.

I got out of bed, changed my clothes, and sat cross-legged on the floor. Since coming to this world, I've been doing Qi Circulation every morning.

,I have nothing to do.

There's nothing to do in the morning without a woman. And after doing Qi Circulation, my body feels refreshed. There was no particular training effect.

I'm currently at the 10th level of Five Energies. To move on to the next realm, Three Flowers Gathering, I need something special. Enlightenment is secondary. In this Xianxia world, what's important is energy and power.

After finishing Qi Circulation, I left the room and moved to the personal training room.

,The Yeom Gu-seok in the original story was hot-blooded but trained diligently. He's the type who believes that diligent training will become his strength.

To play Yeom Gu-seok, I had to follow his actions. Otherwise, I'd be suspected by Cheon Yu-un.

Chamgwidobeop (Demon Execution Sword Art) Yeokgwichu (Reverse Ghost Pursuit).

I raised the sword from below and slashed upwards. The wind blew with the sound of tearing through the air, tickling my hair.

"Hmm?"

Something's missing. It definitely didn't feel like this when I trained yesterday. I performed Reverse Ghost Pursuit once again.

,Something's tickling me. It feels like I'm about to sneeze but can't.

Demonic energy leaks out. It wasn't Cheonma Shingong. But perhaps because it was influenced by Cheonma Shingong, the demonic energy was thick.

I swing the sword. The deficiency is not filled.

,Should I use the Time of Genius? ...No. Let's just try it once this time.

I swung the sword. I didn't keep swinging it in the same way. Because I wouldn't be able to find what's missing if I did that.

After swinging the sword for over 2 hours, there was a change.

Pajik.

Lightning energy arose. It happened naturally without me intending it. The lightning energy mixed with the demonic energy. The color of the lightning, which was bright blue, darkened. At the same time, it became heavier. This lightning continued to descend.

"Interesting."

Demonic energy and lightning energy mix even though I didn't intend it. I couldn't tell if it was good or bad. What was certain was that I had gained a new technique.

,My energy is running low, so should I finish up?

Pajijijik.

Navy blue lightning flowed along the blade. I held the hilt in reverse. The tip of the sword faced the ground.

,Thousand-Kilogram Drop.

I focused my energy on my center of gravity, instantly increasing my weight. I transferred all that weight to the sword in my hand and knelt down.

The blade pierced deep into the hard ground.

Pajijijijijijik!

Navy blue lightning moved into the ground. I got up from my seat and pulled the sword out of the ground. The navy blue lightning was still lingering on the ground, moving. If you looked closely, the soil was cracking every time the navy blue lightning moved.

,It doesn't spread out and gnaws away... It's like it's eroding.

I pondered as I watched this. This new lightning definitely needs a name.

,It was born from a mixture of demonic energy, so Demonic Lightning? No. The sound is not good. It's like poison, so Poison Lightning? This is even worse.

After much deliberation, I came to a conclusion.

,Evil Lightning. I learned it while training the Evil Ghost Sword Art, and it looks vicious at first glance, so it's Evil Lightning.

A new lightning technique was born. I had a good feeling from the morning today.
