Kwaaaaaaaang!

With the explosive sound, my body was forcibly launched into the air.

Thanks to Milyeong's brief warning before the explosion on the ground, I reacted unconsciously and surrounded my body with mana to minimize the damage. It wasn't my first time falling into a trap, so I wasn't too flustered. Still, it couldn't help but make me feel like shit.

"Aww, goddamn it."

I picked myself up. Even with mana surrounding my body, I couldn't completely escape the aftermath of the impact. My head was buzzing, and my vision was blurry. Looking around, the ground was overturned, and trees and rocks were shattered and scattered around. If it had been an ordinary person instead of a Hunter, they would have died instantly.

'Where's Milyeong?'

She was lying face down, covered in dirt.

"Milyeong-ah. Are you okay?"

Milyeong's unfocused eyes wavered before locking onto me.

"Yes. Y-yes. Somehow. I managed to protect my body with magic before the explosion. But I couldn't do anything about the ringing in my head."

"We've entered the enemy's base, so this kind of trap is to be expected. I wasn't careful enough."

There was another reason why I wasn't careful. Lee Yeon-suk. With an S-Class Hunter leading the way, I had a naive thought that nothing could possibly happen.

I belatedly looked for Lee Yeon-suk. As an S-Class Hunter, she should be in better shape than us. Especially since she always had dozens of barriers around her body. There was no way she'd be taken down by an explosion of this level.

But my expectations were off. She was lying on the ground. She looked fine on the outside, but her eyes were bloodshot, and blood was flowing from her mouth and nose.

"...An S-Class Hunter taken down by an explosion like this? Was her retirement because her skills deteriorated?"

"Shut up. Goddamn it...."

Lee Yeon-suk glared at me. She tried to lift herself up with trembling arms but lost strength and fell back down. This was an S-Class Hunter? It was pathetic. Lee Yeon-suk's gaze turned to Milyeong.

"You must know what's going on.... Can you help?"

"I have to help. Things will go wrong if I don't. Unfortunately, we can't run away either. A barrier was erected at the same time as the curse explosion. We're trapped on this mountain. Of course, Seo Bang-nim would have a way out, though."

I knew she was talking about a space-time scroll. But was it right to abandon Lee Yeon-suk and run away like this? There are people who know that we're moving together with Lee Yeon-suk. If Lee Yeon-suk suddenly dies here, there's a high possibility that the responsibility will fall on us.

And more than anything, now that we've come to his base, there's a high chance that we've become Rezo's target. That means Rezo could target us later. It's best to help Lee Yeon-suk and see this through to the end if possible.

"What exactly is going on? There's no way an S-Class would be taken down by an explosion like that."

"This is a curse explosion. The physical explosion is just a bonus; the real deal is in inflicting the curse."

"It doesn't seem like you or I have been cursed."

"That's because it's a curse targeting a specific person. A curse is hatred. It starts from hateful emotions like 'I hope you're unhappy,' 'I hope you die,' and so on. A world-class curse caster hated the enemy who killed his son for ten years and created this curse. That curse is destroying Lee Yeon-suk-ssi's barrier and affecting her body. Ah, the barrier is still being eaten away by the curse. If we leave it like this, she won't last even ten minutes."

"A curse prepared by an S-Class curse caster for ten years. It sounds terrifying just hearing about it."

Unlike my words, I wasn't afraid. A curse prepared for ten years? So what. Even if it was prepared for 100 years, it wouldn't be able to kill me. I have Complete Recovery and Heavenly Heart. For a curse caster, I'm probably the worst possible match they could face.

"Lee Yeon-suk-ssi is lucky that I'm here. I can't completely remove the curse on the spot... but I can neutralize it to some extent. The problem is that it takes time."

I knew what she meant.

"You mean I have to deal with Rezo."

"Only Seo Bang-nim can do it. Or should we abandon this woman and run away? It wouldn't be bad to hand this woman over to Rezo and guarantee our safety. Judging from the curse, it seems Rezo hates this woman a lot."

Milyeong smiled faintly. The aura of a villainess was flowing out of her. I lowered my gaze to check Lee Yeon-suk's expression. As expected, it was contorted. She knew that the decision-making power was in my hands, and she opened her mouth with difficulty.

"I always repay my debts. Even though I've officially retired, you can see that my influence isn't small. I'll grant you any favor, even if it's somewhat illegal. Help me."

Seeing her speak in a commanding tone even in this situation made me wonder if I should just hand her over to Rezo. Once Rezo finishes his revenge, wouldn't he leave Korea and return to Europe?

'...Let's think about the practical benefits. Lee Yeon-suk's influence is real. I saw Kim Ji-hak, the director of the Magecraft Foundation, groveling in front of Lee Yeon-suk.'

An S-Class, and someone who works unofficially under the government.

If I incur a debt now, I can use it later when I need it.

"Alright. I'll help you."

"A wise choice. Don't think about defeating Rezo, just hold out and buy time. I'll go as soon as my body recovers."

"Separate from the debt, if this is successfully completed, please promote me to A-Class. I'm too lazy for the promotion test."

"I will. That's something that can be done by putting a little pressure on the association."

That was something only an S-Class could do.

I briefly focused my mind and emitted electromagnetic waves in all directions.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Waves.

Invisible electromagnetic waves swept through the mountain. Information about the surrounding area entered my mind, and the terrain was mapped out.

I found him. Rezo was standing in front of a small cabin in the middle of the mountain. He didn't even try to hide. He must have been aware of the situation, but he wasn't coming this way.... There must be something important in his residence, or he's preparing something else.

I should go and draw aggro before Rezo launches another attack.

"Seo Bang-nim. Take this with you."

What she handed me was a metal badge about half the size of my palm. A badge in the shape of a fox's head.

"What's this? Did you make it yourself?"

"It's a charm. It will protect Seo Bang-nim from the curse to some extent."

"Thank you."

I attached the badge to my outer garment and headed towards where Rezo was. On the way, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and held it in my hand.

I was a little nervous about facing an S-Class.

Of course, I had experience fighting an S-Class in America. The opponent was Chad Lovell. Nickname: Bloody Drake. A guy with transformation abilities.

'That guy was hardly a true S-Class. He became an S-Class thanks to the president's backing.'

He wasn't even a pseudo S-Class. If he had been a real S-Class, I wouldn't have been able to win because his stats were much lower than they are now.

'But even then, I barely won with item buffs.'

I arrived.

Rezo's appearance was completely different from what I knew. I knew Rezo as an African American, but... the man in front of me was a bearded man in his mid-40s. The clothes he wore were also a modified hanbok, looking like a naturalized person.

"Are you Rezo?"

"Yes. I am Rezo."

"The information I have is different. Weren't you originally black? Can you even get race plastic surgery?"

"Do you believe that living beings have souls?"

"That's already been proven. Souls exist. But is that important? Or what, do you want to say you transferred your soul to another body?"

"When the body dies, the soul disappears. Where the soul disappears to has not been revealed. But there is a brief moment before it disappears. I used this body to possess a nearby corpse with my soul. The corpse was in good condition, so I was able to come back to life. I was very lucky."

"That shouldn't be possible. Just because there's a body doesn't mean the soul will settle. If you could transfer bodies like that, all the mages would have obtained immortal bodies."

"This body is a medium. When I looked into it, he was the son of a shaman. His bloodline was special from the start. Furthermore, I am a master of voodoo. Coincidence overlapped, and a miracle occurred. It was already fate. It was bad luck for him, who came to Europe on vacation and died from a curse."

"Thanks for blabbing about what I was curious about. Now, die."

Pajijik.

Red lightning crackled on the blade of Hwaryeon Bido. I threw the lightning-covered Hwaryeon Bido at Rezo.

Rezo didn't react. It wasn't that he couldn't react, but that he didn't need to. The thrown Hwaryeon Bido stopped in front of him, lost its power, and fell to the ground, sticking into it.

'Telekinesis? Or some kind of sorcery?'

[Opening the Heavenly Eye.]

Things that were invisible became visible. The mana surrounding Rezo and the mana extending in all directions. I had entered his domain. And some of it was directed at me. So subtly that it wouldn't be caught by my senses.

"You moved your gaze around and finally found me. You weren't caught in the illusion?"

"Did you secretly cast a curse or something?"

"I did. The purpose was to shake your mind and scatter your senses with a curse. That's my fighting style. But it didn't work. That charm alone shouldn't have been able to completely block my curse."

"It's probably because your curse is insignificant."

"Maybe so. Lee Yeon-suk, who was affected by that insignificant curse, must be even more insignificant. I don't want to waste my strength unnecessarily, so I'll make a suggestion. Leave Lee Yeon-suk alone and leave this place with your companion. I have no great interest in Korea. Once I achieve my goal, I will leave Korea and go to Europe."

"The guy who committed the subway terror today has no interest? Yeah, right."

"I was originally planning to come forward in a different way. I didn't have any particular grudges against Korea. But a variable occurred a few days ago, and I changed my plans and decided to go ahead."

"What kind of plan is blowing up a subway? You didn't just do it for fun, did you?"

If that were the case, I would have understood immediately. If I had a bomb in my hand, I would want to use it at least once. Of course, that's in fictional works. There's no reason to do such a thing in reality.

"Terrorism is a means of communication. You can communicate with words? That's bullshit. Even if you raise your voice, you'll be ignored if you don't have power. To not be ignored, you have to show that you're not to be trifled with. The reason why civilized people are more rude than barbarians is because their heads don't get split open. If we don't do this, they won't even try to understand us."

"Hmm. I still don't understand. Terrorism is basically done for fun, right?"

"......Is he crazy?"

"I'm normal, and you're the crazy one. Human bomber Rezo!"

"......Indeed. Is that why mental curses don't work because he's already crazy? I know crazy people because I've dealt with them a few times. They're the ones you can't reason with no matter what you do. How unfortunate."

Kwareureureung.

A blue lightning bolt struck Rezo's head from the pitch-black night sky.
