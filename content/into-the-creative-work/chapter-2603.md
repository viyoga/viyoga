Lee Yeonhee came into my office with reports and data.

"It's about Jecheon City in North Chungcheong Province."

"Jecheon City? Ah, that city that got eaten by plant mutants, right? I remember seeing it on the news a while back."

The D virus infects both plants and animals indiscriminately. Some remain unaffected, while others get infected. Humans turn into zombies when infected, but most other plants and animals mutate.

"Yes. Last September, plant mutants appeared in Jecheon City and covered the entire city. The mutated plant was the Korean pasque flower, so the plant mutant in Jecheon City was named the Jecheon pasque flower."

"That's a pretty straightforward name. I heard it eats humans, like a proper mutant plant should."

"To be exact, it sprays juice to dissolve humans and then sucks them up with its roots."

"So? Is there a problem?"

"This photo was taken yesterday from the sky above Jecheon City."

I looked at the photo Lee Yeonhee handed me. Since it was full spring, the Korean pasque flowers covering Jecheon City were in full bloom. The purple petals were quite a sight. However, the size of the flowers wasn't uniform and varied wildly. Most were larger than a person, over 2m tall, and some were as big as buildings. There were so many flowers that it was hard to tell if it was even a city anymore.

"It looks kind of creepy like this."

"And this photo was taken last September."

I looked at the photo and my eyes widened. In the September photo, the flowers were small, and the city's structure was still visible. Most importantly, the area covered by the Jecheon pasque flowers was about half the size of the current photo.

"The Jecheon pasque flower's territory has expanded."

"Yes. The Jecheon pasque flower's territory is still expanding in real-time. The speed is increasing as time goes on. Researchers say it's sucking up nutrients from the soil as much as it can. Plus, it's indiscriminately eating the plants and animals around Jecheon City. If we leave it like this, the ecosystem of Chungcheong Province could be ruined beyond repair."

"...This winter was unusually shitty with the abnormal weather, but these things survived just fine."

"Korean pasque flowers are perennial plants to begin with, and they've mutated to become even stronger. Still, they were dormant in the winter, so Jecheon City was pushed down the priority list for city restoration."

"So, now that the weather's getting warmer, the situation has become seriously fucked. But it's still just a plant, right? Can't we just burn it all down?"

"Yes. The government seems to have thought so too. They've already dispatched military forces once."

Lee Yeonhee held out a tablet.

A video was playing. It was a video of soldiers armed with flamethrowers spewing flames at a group of Jecheon pasque flowers over 2m tall. But it didn't burn as well as expected.

The Jecheon pasque flowers endured the flames and sprayed juice. The soldiers melted along with their uniforms. Roots shot up from below the Jecheon pasque flowers, sucking up the melted remains as if absorbing them. In the end, all that was left were the soldiers' flamethrowers, helmets, and dog tags.

Then, the Jecheon pasque flower slowly moved, pushed the flamethrower and remains aside with its stem, and planted a seed in the ground.

,I guess the juice can't melt metal.'

I replayed the video once more. And I came to one conclusion.

"That's dangerous. It can move, even if it's slow, and it even uses its stems. We need to take care of this before it spreads. How about we just wipe out Jecheon City itself with bombers?"

"It's the easiest and most reliable method, even if it's expensive. But is that really okay?"

"Why?"

"As you can see, the Jecheon pasque flowers aren't interested in metal or building materials. There are tens of thousands of cars left in Jecheon City, as well as appliances like computers inside the buildings."

"...Resources, huh? It's true that it's a waste to just blow it all away."

In this fucked-up world, resources are extremely important. Especially electrical appliances? Even used ones are a godsend.

"Seeing as you came here in person, is this an issue that requires me to step in directly?"

"To be exact, I was hoping you could lend us Yoo Jin-ssi's Sacred Guard. According to the researchers' findings, the Jecheon pasque flower shows some response to fire and herbicides. For now, the most efficient method seems to be mowing. We can neutralize the Jecheon pasque flower by cutting off the lower part of its stem. Plus, we can obtain the juice from the Jecheon pasque flower, and the flower itself can be used as a resource."

The decomposer is a basic skill shared by Technologists and Bionics. The decomposer made it possible to break down the corpses of zombies or mutants into resources.

"There are too many Jecheon pasque flowers. They cover more than just Jecheon City. It's good that we can get resources, but I can't afford to spend too much time on it. I have a mountain of things to do."

The number of Sacred Guards was small. It would take at least three months to clear out all the Jecheon pasque flowers.

"Researchers say that the Jecheon pasque flower isn't a colony but a single entity. If you look at this data, the roots of the Jecheon pasque flower are divided into two types. One is the fine roots that absorb nutrients, and the other is the core root that connects to other individuals."

"......The roots are connected? So, the Korean pasque flower that acts as the heart is just one? It's kind of like a hive mind."

"It's closer to fingers and toes than a hive mind, they say."

"Have you figured out the location of the root cause?"

"They all look the same on the outside... so we haven't figured it out yet. The researchers say it's likely to be in the safest place."

"That's obvious."

Usually, those in power don't fight directly and enjoy the honey in a safe place. Isn't that what power is for?

I looked through the data and pondered.

Using bombers would be an easy way to deal with it. The biggest downside is that Jecheon City itself would be reduced to ashes by the bombing. Bombs aren't free.

Dealing with it manually would take time and be dangerous. However, we could obtain the resources left in Jecheon City.

,Well, the answer is already decided. I can't give up the resources.'

As long as I'm here, I can supply manpower. Of course, resources can also be restored through recovery, but it's not as efficient as people.

,And it doesn't make sense for me to work like a slave. I'd rather die than do that.'

The conclusion was reached.

"I and the Sacred Guard will move directly."

"Directly... you mean?"

"Yeah. Sometimes you need some exercise. And I'm a professional in this kind of thing."

As a Hunter, I was confident in dealing with monsters.

• • •

I headed to Jecheon City with Choi Hyejin, Irina, Lee Yeonhee, and 30 Sacred Guard members. It was quick by helicopter. When we arrived at our destination, the military forces had already set up tents and established a base.

"Reconnaissance should come first."

"...Should I do it?"

Irina asked timidly. Her stealth ability was a skill specialized for reconnaissance and searching. In fact, she had received search training.

I shook my head.

"No. General reconnaissance won't work against our enemy, the Jecheon pasque flower. The Jecheon pasque flower doesn't have eyes or ears. Instead, it senses vibrations through its fine roots and attacks enemies."

Stealth only weakens your presence, it doesn't make your body disappear. In other words, it's impossible to secretly enter Jecheon City. Once you enter Jecheon City, you have no choice but to fight the Jecheon pasque flower.

"Then how are you going to do reconnaissance?"

Choi Hyejin asked curtly. She seemed on edge, probably because I forced her to come when she wanted to rest. Of course, I didn't care in the slightest.

Lee Yeonhee snapped her fingers. Space opened, and a plastic box fell onto the table with a thud. Inside the box was a drone made for reconnaissance.

"Anyone know how to operate a drone?"

Lee Yeonhee, Irina, and all the Sacred Guard members raised their hands. I was surprised by Irina, even if no one else.

"You know how to do it too?"

"W-well, a little as a hobby."

"Try it."

I handed her the controller. Irina seemed burdened when she actually held the controller.

"Wouldn't it be better if someone else did it? It's an important task...."

"It's okay if we wreck one drone. And the AI will assist with the operation. There shouldn't be any problems."

"Yes. I'll give it a try."

She operated the drone. The screens from the four cameras equipped on the drone appeared on the tablet. The drone flew towards Jecheon City.

The scenery inside Jecheon City was shown on the screen. It was full of Korean pasque flowers. The Jecheon pasque flowers covered everything, including the asphalt roads, streetlights, and concrete building walls. It was an even more overwhelming sight than what we saw from the sky.

,The drone must have a microphone... but it's as quiet as a mouse except for the sound of the drone's wings.'

Not even a common pigeon or a small bug was visible. In a way, it was a city landscape befitting the apocalypse.

"Wait. The manhole is open. It could be underground too. The drone has a light, right? Try going inside."

"Yes."

The drone went down the manhole. The movement was steady. Her drone piloting skills were excellent.

Thwack!

A root appeared and hit the drone. The drone plummeted underground because its wing was attacked. The camera was still alive... but countless roots appeared and crushed the drone.

"There are a lot of roots underground. It was suffocating even just looking at the screen. Could the source of the Jecheon pasque flower be underground?"

"I don't think so."

I said.

"Yes?"

"Plant-type monsters don't usually set up shop underground. It's hard to photosynthesize. They're usually in places with plenty of sunlight. Well, unless they tore down the underground ceiling, the story would be different."

"The reason there are so many roots underground is...."

"Roots usually grow underground. Looking at the things growing in the city, the roots have no choice but to spread to the sewers. We need more information. Deploy more drones. Ignore the underground and just fly over the ground to collect information."

Six reconnaissance drones flew up at the same time. Every time the drones moved, the AI created a map on the computer. It was a map that allowed you to check 360 degrees, like a street view.

,It's supposed to be a combat AI, but it's good at this too. It's definitely convenient.'

After about three hours, the map was completed. Even so, it was impossible to check the inside of the buildings.

,I wasn't planning on going inside the buildings anyway.'

I looked at the map and marked three places in the center of Jecheon City in red.

"The source is probably in one of these three places."

"Three places? I don't understand. Why those three places? Looking at it, they're the same as any other place."

Choi Hyejin stared at the map and asked.

"There were guys who looked stronger than the other Jecheon pasque flowers stationed near those three places."

"...They all look like the same plants to me?"

"Look closely. The thickness of the stems, the gloss of the leaves, and the size are different. Plus, they're densely arranged."

"Now that I hear it, it seems like that's the case...."

"There's no point in dragging it out. Alright, let's move right away."

I got up from my seat and put on my combat helmet.
