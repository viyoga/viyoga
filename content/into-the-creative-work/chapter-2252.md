Kweitan felt suffocated by the oppressive aura.

It wasn't his first time, of course. He'd felt similar sensations surprisingly often.

The first time was when he received his title from the late King. Since then, he'd felt varying degrees of pressure whenever he met famous figures. Most recently, it was when he met the Grand General.

,Even when I met the Grand General, there was this much blood in the air. But the Grand General had just returned from war... Wait, blood? It's not just my imagination, there really is the smell of blood.'

Did he kill someone and come straight to the audience chamber?

Fortunately, that wasn't the case.

One of the maids following Duke Pruckers threw a monster corpse she had been carrying on her back onto the floor of the audience chamber.

It was a fresh corpse, as if it had just died.

,It's a troll. We happened to run into it before coming to the Royal Palace, so we hunted it down. Trolls, with their strong vitality, are a tonic in themselves. I offer it to Your Majesty."

It was rude.

Even if a troll's corpse is valuable, was it really necessary to bring it into the audience chamber? He could have just handed it over to a servant of the Royal Palace.

None of the nobles pointed out the rudeness. The Queen's expression stiffened. But was she really a queen? She wasn't as intimidated as the nobles.

,I gratefully accept the Duke's gift. Was your journey to the Royal Palace uncomfortable or tiring in any way?"

,Who do you think I am? What could possibly happen just coming to the Royal Palace? The trip was comfortable. But by the way, there's an unfamiliar guest here."

Duke Pruckers twisted his lips and approached. Kweitan and the rest of the delegation tried to hide their trembling legs, straightened their backs, and expressed their dignity. They were representatives of the Poten Kingdom. To be intimidated would be disloyal.

,It is an honor to meet Your Excellency the Duke, the hero of the Great War and savior of the people."

Kweitan explained the situation in the East Continent and the purpose of the delegation's visit to the Rapelli Kingdom to the Duke.

,Ah, I see. But this is the Imperial Court, and you're standing way too straight. Her Majesty the Queen is right in front of you. Kneel down first. Don't you dare try to be equal to me, you foreign envoys."

The Duke unleashed his aura.

The powerful aura of an Aura Master, a realm beyond human, seized the surroundings. Kweitan, standing right in front of the Duke, almost lost strength in his legs and collapsed in an unsightly manner.

,I am not a noble of Rapelli. Nor am I a subject of Her Majesty the Queen. I am here representing the Thorn Kingdom... Please, Duke, show some respect."

,Coming to request troops and yet your knees are so stiff. Well, I understand, you must be old and your knees aren't what they used to be. I'll give you a hand."

Peok.

The Duke kicked Kweitan's knee. Kweitan felt his kneecap shatter and he collapsed to the floor. An enormous pain washed over him. It happened so suddenly that he missed the timing to scream.

,Master told you to kneel."

,So clueless."

,You retards."

The Duke's maids kicked the knees of the delegation members one by one. All of them were skilled Aura Experts or higher. The delegation members screamed and were forced to kneel.

The Duke looked around and chuckled with satisfaction.

,Now it looks like we're ready to talk."

Kweitan forcibly swallowed his pain and glared at the Duke.

,We are here representing the Thorn Kingdom! Attacking us is no different from attacking the Thorn Kingdom! Do you intend to start a war with us?!"

,I just bent your stiff knees, what's with the fuss? War? I wouldn't mind it."

The Duke reeked of blood. It was so strong that Kweitan turned his head away. The Duke was a madman. There was no reasoning with him. So he shouted to Queen Eileen, who could understand him.

,Your Majesty! Please remove this man from the palace! I do not wish to speak with a madman! Surely Your Majesty does not desire war?!"

,...The Duke's words don't seem wrong. You have been quite rude. Are you not now threatening us with war? We desire peace, but we do not fear war."

Starting with Queen Eileen, the nobles watched the Duke's every move and spoke.

,Her Majesty is right. They were rude."

,War? Very well. Let us show them the power of the Rapelli Kingdom."

,We have His Excellency Duke Pruckers. There is no defeat."

Kweitan realized belatedly.

That the true master of the Rapelli Kingdom was not them, but Duke Pruckers.

,...Your Excellency Duke. We have been rude. I apologize, so please forgive us."

,I forgive you."

Contrary to his words, the Duke held a red sword in his hand.

,...Didn't you say you forgave us."

,I forgave your rudeness, but didn't you declare war on us? I will cut off your head and deliver it to the King of the Thorn Kingdom."

The maids moved. They grabbed the delegation members by the hair, and with their other hand, they held a sword and shoved it into their necks. Blood splattered everywhere. The maids, made up of beautiful women, did not erase the sneer on their faces even as the delegation members died.

,Be grateful that you're not being tortured to death."

Kweitan couldn't answer the Duke's words. Because his head had rolled to the ground.

I can get help from an excellent magician.
