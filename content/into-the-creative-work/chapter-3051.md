-Know your own wretchedness.

I was a farmer.

The sun rose and lit up the whole world, and my eyes naturally opened.

I rose from the worn-out bed, picked up my farming tools, and stepped outside. The house I'd inherited from my parents grated on my nerves for no good reason. It was old and shabby. The wind that had slipped through the cracks in the walls the night before made me start worrying about the future. The weather would turn cold soon enough.

I did the same field work as always. The hand gripping the tool was missing the ring finger on the right side. The price of a quarrel with a martial artist at a tavern I'd visited for the first time in ages two years back.

No nickname, no proper job, and that bastard who was younger than me strutted around acting tough because he'd learned a bit of martial arts at a nearby military academy.

I thought I could win. I worked the fields every day, and even within the village, my raw strength was unmatched. It was a delusion. My fist never even touched him. I paid with a finger and most of my savings just to keep living.

My parents passed half a year ago. A plague that swept through the village took half the villagers' lives. Every elder in the village died. More than half the young people died too.

I got married. My wife was a woman as ill-fated as I was. Poor and ugly. Bad-tempered too. If it were up to me, I wouldn't have married her. But it was out of my hands. In this village, if you don't marry by my age, you get looked down on and ostracized. And I couldn't defy the village chief's orders.

Martial artists had died in droves near the village. Several sects had fought over interests. By the chief's command, I had to clear away twenty corpses by myself.

A female martial artist with a blade lodged in her neck caught my eye. She was the prettiest woman I'd ever seen in my life. Big breasts too. At least she was definitely prettier than my wife. Knowing I shouldn't, I still stripped the clothes off the corpse.

The work finished much later than expected. Thankful there was no one around.

Drought came. This year's harvest was ruined. Not just me—the entire village starved.

A suspicious merchant came. I sold one of my two children. I ate beef while shedding tears. Beef I hadn't tasted in three years was so delicious it brought tears to my eyes. My wife and the remaining child ate while crying too. I swore I'd never eat beef again for the rest of my life.

When I returned home, my wife and child were dead. The child I'd sold had come back and was tearing into their corpses. The blood-soaked child grinned, saying it was so delicious. Eyes red as blood. It was clear a demon had possessed him.

Before I could even turn my body to flee, the child lunged. My limbs broke, and I was devoured by my own child.

"You fucking son of a bitch…."

I clenched my fist, feeling the phantom pain.

This is an illusion.

This isn't my life.

My sense of time was completely shattered, but I hadn't experienced the farmer's entire decades-long life.

"What am I supposed to realize from a shitty illusion like this?"

Unpleasant and boring.

-You were simply lucky.

I was a wanderer.

When I was young, a martial arts manual and a spirit pill I found on a dead martial artist's body in a back alley determined my life.

I locked myself at home and trained. Thanks to the spirit pill, I reached the mid-stage of Entry in three years.

I wanted to climb higher. The problem was no money meant no elixirs. Training without elixirs was inefficient no matter how I thought about it.

After much deliberation, there were two options. Join a decent sect, or become a wanderer.

I looked into it, and the only sect that would take someone with unclear origins was a Black Path sect. No way I could train properly in a place that just gobbled up peasants. I decided to become a wanderer.

The hand I looked down at was full of wrinkles. One arm gone, and my cultivation had stalled at the upper stage of Entry. Too old to focus on training, yet I had nothing.

If I couldn't raise my cultivation, I wouldn't last ten more years.

It was all or nothing. I killed a thirty-year friend and stole his wealth to obtain an elixir.

I attempted to break through.

I failed.

I suffered internal injuries. I needed at least three years of recovery.

I had neither the energy nor the wealth for that. As rumors spread that I'd killed my thirty-year friend and robbed him of his elixir, my reputation was destroyed. I couldn't find work.

I drove a dagger into my neck and ended it.

"What's the point of this shit?"

-The principles of the world are contained within human life.

"Bullshit."

My mind begins to go hazy once more.

Memories of the past surface. A conversation I'd had with Elena in the 'God's Atlantis' world.

'Training through illusion arts? I don't know. I doubt you'd get the results you're hoping for. Illusion arts are fundamentally like dreams—highly volatile. My illusions are a bit different, but… ultimately, the limits of illusion arts are clear.'

'Distort your sense of time with illusion arts to focus on training? Possible, but meaningless. After all, the distortion of time is itself just an illusion. You invested decades of time training inside a hallucination? You merely deluded yourself into thinking so. Most illusionists are like that. Am I different? What a ridiculous question. I can deceive even the world itself.'

'You want to learn illusion arts? …You're not planning to hypnotize women and jump them, are you?'

'Simply put, it's impossible.'

'It's not a matter of talent. The wall of talent doesn't really mean much to you anyway, does it. You can gain illusion talent in another world and acquire illusion-related skills or traits…. The reason you can't use illusion arts is because of your Absolute Mind. Illusion arts aren't the one-sided interference you think they are. The foundation of illusion arts is resonating with the target's mind first.'

'You want me to spar with you with my Absolute Mind released? No. I'd be at an overwhelming disadvantage. Huh? You didn't know? Even without Absolute Mind, your mind has already surpassed the ordinary level. Hasn't your mind been under Absolute Mind's influence for decades, hundreds of years? You possess experiences fundamentally different from ordinary people from the start. And now you want to call it an ordinary mind? Does that make any sense?'

'A way to escape illusion arts even without Absolute Mind. It's simple. First, recognize the hallucination. Now, deny the hallucination.'

'Escape illusions through physical pain? That might work for mediocrities. Skilled illusionists will prevent you from feeling physical pain. Once they've dominated your senses, manipulating pain is child's play.'

'Deny the hallucination. Deny it ceaselessly. Once you submit even a single time to illusion arts, you'll never escape.'

I repeated the words of Elena, the undisputed Queen of Illusions, and denied the hallucination.

Even as my mind went hazy and I floundered in the hallucination, there were moments when clarity returned. Each time, I denied the hallucination.

-Humans are always ruled by desire.

The life of a corrupt official.

"Can't find a shred of fun in this. A trashy drama would be more entertaining."

-Everything can crumble in an instant.

A life born as crown prince, becoming king, ending with a subject's betrayal.

"Drink some booze, embrace a few women, kill a few peasants, and that makes me a tyrant? Small scale. If you're going to embrace women, embrace all of them."

-Yin and yang. Humans possess duality.

The life of a hypocrite who joined the destroyed Huashan Sect and became the Martial Alliance Leader.

"Hey. There's no detailed formula for the Purple Haze Divine Art. An illusion is just an illusion after all."

At some point, my mind stopped going hazy.

Originally, once I fell into a hallucination I could do nothing, but as time passed, I regained clarity even within the hallucinations. The moment I recognized the hallucination, that life unfolded according to my will. Like a lucid dream. And each time, cracks formed in the hallucination.

Even embracing women inside the hallucination brought no pleasure. It was ultimately just an illusion—it couldn't compare to reality.

-Humans cannot live alone.

"Idiot. Who doesn't know that? I can't live without women either. But that doesn't mean I need to understand every single woman to the depths of their souls."

Kkadeuk.

The crack in the hallucination grew larger. Inside the crack was a fishing hook. The fishing line stretched up toward the sky above.

I pondered for a moment. Since the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil wasn't activating here, I couldn't tell whether that fishing hook was a trap or not. But I made my judgment soon enough.

'Trap or whatever. It's better than wasting time in a meaningless hallucination.'

I reached out and grabbed the fishing hook.

The fishing hook yanked upward.

Splash!

I emerged from the lake.

"A big catch after a long while."

Instead of sky, there was a bumpy stone ceiling. I was underground. I turned my gaze and saw a man holding a fishing rod. A middle-aged man with graying hair tied back, wearing a shabby coat. He was stroking his rough stubble, watching me.

I looked at the man with the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.

I could learn his name.

Jiang Ziya.

At the same time, I learned that he was not the main body but a thought-form, a clone.

Jiang Ziya is commonly known in Korea as Kang Tae-gong. And in this world, he is an immortal.

If he had been the main body, the gap in cultivation would have prevented even the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil from revealing his true name.

"You have the same eerie eyes as that monkey."

"…Taegongwang. Why are you here?"

He smiled faintly.

"The heavens are sick, the heavens are swinging, and I was waiting for the blade that will kill the heavens."
