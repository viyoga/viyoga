I handed the butler the packaged chicken.

The butler bowed politely and left the store.

,It's inconvenient that there's no delivery.

Neo London was picky about food delivery. It seemed something had happened in the past because of deliveries, and they didn't allow food delivery unless it was a certified company. It was almost impossible for a small store like YuYu Chicken to get permission for food delivery.

"Jackpot."

A customer who had been watching the situation said. He neatly tore off a piece of chicken, ate it, and got up from his seat.

"The world of nobles is small. If a restaurant gets a reputation for being delicious in that world, it's as good as a success in Neo London. Congratulations, boss. I guess I won't be able to eat this chicken leisurely anymore because it'll be too crowded."

"...Thank you. I plan to expand the business, so don't worry about that."

"Really? That's good to hear. Boss, there are a lot of twisted bastards in Neo London. They're jealous of those who are doing well and try to rip off the powerless. If you can't handle it, one way is to join hands with a large corporation. Other than that... hire people. Anyway, boss, I believe you'll do well. You don't seem like an ordinary person. I'll come again later."

"Please come again."

He left the store.

I crossed my arms and stared at the fish and chips shop across the street before letting out a sigh.

I wanted to barge in right now, but the aftermath was the problem. I was a citizen of Neo London and the owner of a chicken shop. Not an outlaw.

,Should I sell fish and chips at my store? I have a fryer, so if I just prepare the ingredients.... No. This is a chicken shop. There's no need to be ambiguous.

There was no definite proof that the fish and chips owner had spread the rumors. I decided to watch for now.

The butler who had packed the chicken, Hyde, came again the next day.

"My master-nim really liked the chicken. He said it goes well with wine. Please pack one fried chicken and one seasoned chicken again today."

And the next day, a maid from another family came.

"Is it possible to pack seven fried chickens and seven seasoned chickens each?"

The black-haired maid was polite.

"Yes, it is. However, we're a bit backed up with orders right now.... You'll have to wait a bit."

"I'll wait, so please cook it deliciously."

Ttalang!

The door opened and another maid appeared. As soon as she came in, her eyes widened.

"Garnet! How long has it been since you graduated from the Academy? Did you come to buy chicken for your master-nim too?"

"Yes. Sera. It's been a while. Has it been two years?"

"I'm working at the Hennis baronetcy. What about you?"

"I'm working at the Gotvel countship."

"Ooh, the countship...! Should I say, as expected. You had good grades in the Academy too. Oh, right. Let me order quickly. Please give me five fried chickens and five seasoned chickens each!"

They started chatting while waiting for the chicken.

I worked in the kitchen, pricking up my ears to listen to their conversation. I thought there might be information related to nobles that I couldn't easily access.

But they were more thorough than I thought. They didn't say anything about the noble families they worked for. They just asked about each other's well-being and reminisced about the old days.

I could feel a thorough sense of professionalism even in their seemingly light words and actions. They were pros.

"Excuse me, miss. What is this?"

"Ah, that? It's a coupon. If you collect 10 coupons and bring them, you can exchange them for one chicken. You can use it now."

"Is this a system for regulars? That's interesting."

The maids left without using the coupons. They didn't say they would come again. They didn't have a choice. If the master liked it, they would come again on their own.

"Wow. A maid. They're so cool. Don't you think?"

"Yeah. I wanted to be a maid once... but it was too hard, so I gave up."

The part-timers stared at the departing maids with admiring eyes.

It wasn't strange. In Neo London, butlers and maids were recognized as jobs only the elite of the elite could have.

The next day. As the next day passed, the number of maids and butlers visiting the store increased. Fried chicken and seasoned chicken had become known in high society.

The citizens of Neo London envied and admired the nobles. They tried to imitate what the nobles did and taste the dishes the nobles ate. Thanks to that, YuYu Chicken's sales graph continued to rise. It was literally piercing the sky.

Rumors that they were using bad oil? Those rumors disappeared without a trace.

"I heard you're looking for franchisees... Can I get a detailed explanation?"

"Of course. However, I'm busy right now, so I'd like to talk about it separately after the store closes. Please read this first."

"What's this?"

"It's a booklet about YuYu Chicken. The details of the franchise agreement are also written in an easy-to-understand way. Please read it and return it."

It was a booklet close to a business plan. I didn't make it. I was busy enough running the chicken shop, so when would I have time for that? This was something Yuria made because she was bored at home.

,She said she's free these days because all the housework is done in the morning.... Well, that makes sense. Our house is small and we don't have any guests.

Yuria said she would help with the chicken shop, but I stopped her. I didn't want to use such a great maid like her at a mere chicken shop. After I refused, Yuria seemed to be studying separately at home.

I glanced at her studying the other day, but I don't know what she's studying. Her subjects of study were so vast. Law, programming, tax accounting, magic, urban history, etc. The types were so vast that I wondered if she could even study properly.

,I should start looking for someone to take over the kitchen and start my magic research.

I couldn't keep running the kitchen myself.

,I need to hire at least two people to handle it. Would three be appropriate?

Three people just for the kitchen. It seemed like too many, but... YuYu Chicken was doing really well right now. It felt like three people wouldn't be enough.

,Even if I hire people, the taste of YuYu Chicken won't change.

All the kitchen has to do is fry the chicken for a set amount of time. The frying time is also set with an alarm. There's nothing difficult.

John Clark, who runs the fish and chips shop across from YuYu Chicken, gritted his teeth and glared at YuYu Chicken.

"Damn it! Why is that chicken shop doing so well?!"

He even spread rumors that YuYu Chicken was using old and dirty oil, but it didn't have much effect. YuYu Chicken's excellent response? That was part of it, but YuYu Chicken's taste was so good that it ignored the rumors. As even nobles started looking for it, the rumors John Clark spread disappeared without a trace.

"Damn...!"

You might ask if it's good to have a successful store nearby. Not at all. People only look for successful stores and don't even glance at the stores nearby. Rather, they even steal customers from nearby stores. Immediately, the number of customers visiting his store dropped by more than 30% compared to usual. People who came to eat fish and chips saw the people lined up in front of YuYu Chicken and started lining up themselves.

,I'm so jealous I could die!

He couldn't just sit still like this.

He called a younger brother he knew.

"What's up, hyung-nim."

"See that chicken shop? Go get some takeout from their menu."

"Ah. That's the famous chicken shop these days."

"You know about it?"

"The social world is buzzing these days about the appearance of a new chicken that's breaking the prejudice against chicken. It started in the noble world and is spreading to the upper class."

"It's that famous?"

"Aren't those butlers and maids lined up over there? Not all of them are servants of noble families, and most of them are probably servants of the upper class."

"It's just a flash in the pan. It'll die down over time."

"Hmm. Maybe."

"......"

John changed his plan. Originally, he was going to spread false information on the internet that there was a foreign substance in the chicken. But he had an even greater desire.

What if he sold YuYu Chicken's chicken himself? His store already had a fryer. If he just knew the ingredients and recipe, frying the chicken would be a piece of cake.

"Order the chicken and find out the recipe. I'll give you plenty of money."

"Well, I don't know if it'll work, but I'll try."

His younger brother returned with the chicken after two hours. The line was too long.

John, who tasted the Korean fried chicken and seasoned chicken, clenched his fist. It was delicious. He never dreamed that chicken could be such a delicious food.

,It can't even be compared to the fish and chips I made....

Once he tasted Korean chicken, he couldn't even touch the fish and chips. Seasoned chicken, in particular, was revolutionary.

"The recipe?"

"I asked the employee while waiting. I wanted to see the kitchen, but it was blocked off so I couldn't see it. I don't know the exact recipe, but they said they fry the chicken twice."

"With chicken like this, it's worth hiding the recipe.... But they fried it twice? Shouldn't it be more greasy if you fry it twice?"

"Well. I don't know much about cooking...."

"Tell your boss. There's a business that's worth money."

"...Certainly. This chicken would definitely make money. It's already spread to high society.... But are you okay, John-nim? Haven't you washed your hands?"

"This is really the last time."

"Haha. I'll tell the boss. I should pack some Korean chicken to take with me when I go."

10 a.m.

I frowned as soon as I went to work at YuYu Chicken. The store looked the same as yesterday, but if you looked closely, it had changed.

,The counter chair is slightly pushed back, and the kitchen door, which was slightly open, is tightly closed.

It was proof that someone had entered my store.

Not a thief.

There couldn't be a thief. Because the citizens of Neo London don't pay in cash.

,They must have come to steal the recipe. YuYu Chicken is raising sales like crazy.

In fact, famous companies approached me, saying they would buy the Korean chicken recipe for a huge amount of money. I refused. It was the same as selling a business item. Of course, they would find out the chicken recipe in a few days, but... it was important that YuYu Chicken was the original Korean chicken.

,It's not the work of a large corporation. There's no need to use such a dangerous method. It's much better to gather experts and research the chicken.
