Before entering the Forest of Pairon in Sichuan, the option of burning the forest down was impossible.

Whether you set fire to the forest or fire a missile into it, the wavelengths emitted by the Pairons block it immediately. The Chinese government mentioned considering nuclear weapons, but whether a nuke could actually blow away the Forest of Pairon is uncertain. They've never used one before, after all.

'A famous Chinese guild that had cleared S-Rank dungeons tried to conquer the Forest of Pairon and failed in less than two days.'

Simply put, the Forest of Pairon was beyond an ordinary S-Rank dungeon.

'The World Hunter Association and the Chinese government aren't idiots—they wouldn't have dispatched three S-Rank Hunters for no reason. They judged that at minimum, two S-Rank Hunters were necessary.'

For reference, there are four Forests of Pairon in China total. Sichuan, Shanxi, Yunnan, Jiangxi. This time, we were only conquering the one in Sichuan.

"Oh my, Mr. Zhang Haran. You haven't changed a bit since I last saw you. He's not an escort secretary you hired—he's a Korean S-Rank Hunter. Can't you show some dignity as a Chinese S-Rank Hunter?"

"For an A-Rank Hunter, your mouth is still as loose as ever. How nice for you, coming from an S-Rank Hunter family."

It seemed like lightning was crackling between the two women.

Apparently the two already knew each other. The relationship didn't look particularly friendly, though. Ryu Gicheon, standing nearby, let out a sigh and shook his head. He spoke to me in a low voice.

"I'll apologize on behalf of the Mansu Empress. This won't be much comfort, but… her temper is quite famous. She's not being hysterical because she's discriminating against you. That's just how she is."

"Yes. Well, it's fine."

Zhang Haran was going to get fucked by me eventually anyway.

Zhang Haran's temper is famous overseas too. Search her name online and what pops up are videos of her throwing fits. Chinese public opinion is, unsurprisingly, not favorable. The reason she can still operate without issue is because she's an S-Rank Hunter and because she's never truly crossed the line.

'She's the type of woman who's all hysterics—a nightmare customer who specializes in throwing her weight around. But she's not a robber or a mass murderer.'

Surprisingly, Zhang Haran has quite a few devoted fans.

The reason? At mid-thirties, she's on the younger side among S-Rank Hunters, and before becoming a Hunter she was an actress—her face is pretty and her tits are big.

'Looking at her, her ass is big too. Separate from the viciousness, there's something decadent about her atmosphere.'

As a woman, she could be called considerably attractive.

Anyway, we entered the Forest of Pairon.

The interior of the forest was dark as night despite it being broad daylight. The massive trees grew so densely together that they completely blocked out the sunlight.

Even for S-Rank Hunters, the difference between having light and not having it is quite significant.

Ryu Cheongseol and I held flashlights to illuminate the way ahead. Normally I wouldn't be doing this kind of grunt work, but… if we told Zhang Haran to hold a flashlight with her temper, she'd throw a fucking fit for sure. Ryu Gicheon is Ryu Cheongseol's father, so he needs to be able to see well.

In the end, the only people who could hold flashlights were me and Ryu Cheongseol.

"Ha, seriously. What a disgusting place."

"This is an unpleasant place."

Zhang Haran and Ryu Gicheon's expressions hardened the moment they entered the Forest of Pairon.

I hadn't said anything, but my impressions were similar.

'My senses are being restricted by the wavelengths the Pairons emit. It was information I knew in advance, but… experiencing it directly is beyond what I expected.'

The detection abilities of S-Rank Hunters were restricted.

Even when using mana to focus my senses, the limit was roughly 10 meters.

"Father. I can't tell which direction we're going. What if we get lost and stranded?"

Ryu Cheongseol asked, looking around in all directions.

"It's fine. If it comes to it, we can go up into the sky and escape. I've prepared an artifact for that purpose too…. Anyway. This forest is far more magnificent than it looked before we entered. The trees alone are at least 10 meters tall…."

Ryu Gicheon was marveling even in this situation.

"Chatter chatter, so noisy. I want to finish this quickly and get back to Shanghai, you know? Ryu Cheongseol. You take the lead and move. The most useless one here is you, isn't it? At least guide the way."

This time, it was Ryu Gicheon's brow that furrowed rather than Ryu Cheongseol's. If Ryu Gicheon, who doted on his daughter, started bickering with Zhang Haran… my first mission could blow up from infighting among team members.

'Honestly, I'd planned to finish this alone and make the other S-Rank Hunters into folding screens… but Ryu Cheongseol is here.'

My eyes met Ryu Cheongseol's. Lately, we kept making eye contact with each other. It meant we were both conscious of each other.

'Can't be helped.'

I stepped forward.

"I'll take the lead. But what about the ones following behind us?"

Hunter reporters who had seen us enter the Forest of Pairon had tagged along. Even with our senses restricted, they were following so openly that we couldn't not notice.

"Doesn't matter."

Zhang Haran said that while tidying her slightly disheveled side hair. Having been an actress, she seemed to enjoy the attention.

"Those types are never just one or two. Even if you warn them, it only lasts that moment. If they have the courage to set foot in a dangerous place, they should fight monsters instead… tch. They wouldn't have come in without any preparation either, so leave them be."

I took the lead and moved.

I thought it might be better to deal with the Pairon's main body trees that were disguised as we moved, but in this Forest of Pairon, even ordinary trees require considerable force to fell. It's because the trees influenced by the Pairons have become disgustingly hard.

'Even S-Rank Hunters don't have infinite stamina and mana. If I waste strength unnecessarily, I won't have it when I need it.'

"Krrrrrrr."

Our gazes turned toward where the beast's growl came from.

There stood a massive creature over 5 meters tall. An A-Rank monster—an Ogre. It has no special abilities, but its raw strength and durability are immense.

"I'll handle it."

Ryu Cheongseol stepped forward, gripping a massive sword—a zanmadao—in her hand. Ryu Gicheon watched with arms crossed, and Zhang Haran examined her own fingernails.

A red aura settled on the blade of Ryu Cheongseol's zanmadao.

"That looks like an Ogre the Pairons have tampered with. Will she be alright?"

"An ordinary A-Rank Hunter would struggle. But Cheongseol is not an ordinary A-Rank Hunter. Watch."

Ryu Cheongseol moved. By my standards, and by A-Rank Hunter standards, it wasn't fast. Her movements were simple too. Movements you wouldn't make against a human opponent—but her opponent was an Ogre. The Ogre stupidly charges straight in from the front.

'Even A-Rank Hunters don't fight Ogres head-on, normally.'

Fighting head-on, nine times out of ten the A-Rank Hunter loses. That's how absurd the Ogre's physicality is.

"KRAAAAAAAH!"

The Ogre let out an eardrum-shattering roar and swung its fist at Ryu Cheongseol.

In that instant, Ryu Cheongseol set her stance. She drove strength into her lower body, fixing her body in place with enough force to gouge the ground. With a single breath, she circulated her internal energy throughout her body, amplifying her full-body strength, then swung the zanmadao.

The Ogre died, split in half in the exact pose of its extended fist.

"Haha. Did you see that? That's my daughter. An Ogre is no match for her."

"I didn't know before… but the Overlord Blade Technique's core is instantaneous physical enhancement."

"Oh. You grasped it immediately. You have a good eye. Even among S-Ranks, few possess that level of insight."

"Yes. I have a decent eye. But that method of mana operation must place considerable strain on the body. Is there a safety mechanism I'm unaware of?"

"It's a problem solved by training the body."

Simple and crude, but not wrong.

Ryu Cheongseol even collected the Ogre's magic stone and approached us. She was wearing a black martial arts uniform similar to Ryu Gicheon's, and not a single drop of blood had splattered on her clothes. It meant the battle was relaxed enough that she could pay attention to even a single drop of blood.

"What should we do with this magic stone? Settle it later?"

"Keep that pocket change for yourself."

Zhang Haran was still vicious.

"Young Lady Ryu Cheongseol caught the Ogre alone. I, who did nothing, can't claim a share of it."

There are no S-Rank Hunters obsessed with money.

An S-Rank Hunter is someone who single-handedly handles A-Rank dungeons that large guilds tackle with tension. Money could be earned endlessly if one simply set their mind to it.

Ryu Cheongseol placed the magic stone, roughly 13 centimeters in diameter, into a subspace artifact. An A-Rank magic stone averages around 3 million won.

For reference, an S-Rank magic stone starts at 100 million. The problem was that even killing S-Rank monsters rarely produced S-Rank magic stones. Conversely, with extraordinary luck, one could kill an A-Rank monster and obtain an S-Rank magic stone.

We kept moving.

Monsters between C-Rank and A-Rank appeared steadily. Whenever miscellaneous monsters showed up, Ryu Cheongseol handled them all. The S-Rank Hunters, including myself, conserved our strength.

The atmosphere was not good.

An hour had passed before we knew it.

Results? Nothing beyond killing a few monsters. This was the Forest of Pairon, yet no Pairons were in sight.

'Starting to get irritated, aren't we.'

Zhang Haran's brow was furrowed, and Ryu Gicheon and Ryu Cheongseol—who had been chatting intermittently until thirty minutes ago—had also fallen silent.

Why were they doing this? It was obvious. They were scared. They knew that fighting head-on meant losing.

'It's not like I'm wandering lost because I have no sense of direction. If that were the case, Zhang Haran wouldn't be able to endure it with that temper of hers.'

Zhang Haran and Ryu Gicheon also couldn't be certain which way was correct.

'It must be the Pairons' trick. This forest is their territory. It's common knowledge that they influence plants. They're making us wander the forest.'

And hoping we'd get exhausted and leave… probably not. Even they must know that's impossible.

They want us to tire, even a little. If not that, they're stalling for time as if preparing something.

When I stopped walking, Ryu Cheongseol tilted her head and asked.

"Why did you stop? Did you find something?"

"No. I just got curious about something."

Crackle.

The mana inside me churned violently, and blue electricity sparked around me. Ryu Cheongseol flinched and stepped back. Ryu Gicheon and Zhang Haran watched with interest.

'The output is weaker than I thought. So they're interfering not just with radio waves but with mana too.'

The answer was simple. Just raise the output higher.

Bzzzzzzzzzt!

The blue lightning that had been wildly thrashing in all directions compressed in an instant above my palm, rotating slowly.

Thunder Heaven Style—Ten Thousand Thunders.

Lightning amplified by the power of rotation is processed into the form of a sphere.

Thunder Heaven Style—Lightning Sphere.

The sphere, glowing like a blood-red lamp, flew forward slowly. The lightning sphere flew, avoiding the trees blocking the way, and stopped at a certain point. The sphere was connected to me, and I could still control it.

Thunder Heaven Style—Ultimate Technique—Explosive Quake Thunder.

KWAAAAAAAAANG!

The lightning sphere exploded, and a massive shockwave and lightning swept the surroundings. The shattered trees burned before they could even hit the ground, and the residual lightning that discharged lingered on the ground, flickering.

KWA-RRRRRRNG!

Dozens of lightning bolts fell from the sky to the ground, burning the forest. The invisible wavelengths of the Pairons suppressed the fire from spreading, but they couldn't block the lightning.

The lightning scattered in all directions was condensed into one place. The mystery of Ten Thousand Thunders was applied to it, and it rotated slowly.

Thunder Heaven Style—Thunder Sound Thousand Blades.

The lightning discharged, and thousands of strands of lightning imbued with the property of sharpness flew in all directions, rampaging. It was lightning and simultaneously a blade. The moment you touched the lightning, you were cut and burned. With thousands of strands, it ground up part of the forest like a blender.

When the lightning lost its power and fell silent, a blackened clearing the size of a sports field spread out before me. Embers remained among the broken tree debris, sending up smoke. At its center stood a tattered black tree. The Pairon's main body.

Thunder Heaven Style—Flying Thunder God.

I closed hundreds of meters in an instant and kicked the black tree's trunk.

KWADIK!

The black tree couldn't hold and toppled. The aura emanating from the black tree disappeared. One Pairon was dead.

'I did it out of irritation, but… the efficiency is poor.'

The size of a sports field? The Forest of Pairon is a thousand times that. At this rate, even after 100 days there'd be no results.

—"Negotiation!"

A voice echoed in my ears.

It wasn't an attack on my mind. This was a Pairon sending a telepathic message through wavelengths.

—"We are willing to negotiate with you!"
