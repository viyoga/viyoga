Kang Ji-young-nim, the head of Maruhan Academy, believed in me and did everything she could. I heard she even requested large-scale support from the Hero Association. But it was rejected.

China, in particular, is seen as a dangerous dictatorship by other countries. So, it's no exaggeration to say that the South Korean Ministry of National Defense and the Hero Association are focusing all their attention on China.

We just need to hold out until the South Korean army and South Korean heroes arrive. It will be 1-2 hours at the latest.

,But I can't miss this golden opportunity to kill the 3rd Legion Commander, who has been weakened by a reckless invasion.'

I looked at the sky, which was pouring down white snow, and waited for the coming day of reckoning.
