It became night.

Yuria and I dressed warmly and prepared to go out. Our destination was, of course, Mount Hughes.

Going to Mount Hughes at night is dangerous and not a good idea. Of course, I knew that, but I had no choice. To act without being seen, I had to move at night.

I came to Mount Hughes for two reasons.

,Travel? Why would I come to a place like this for travel? It's a boring place with nothing to see but a snowy mountain.,

If a real trip was the goal, I would have just relaxed somewhere nice near the Prucus Barony, without having to go all the way to Mount Hughes.

I fastened my warm winter clothes that I brought from reality, made sure to take my dagger, and then looked at Yuria.

Yuria was covered from head to toe in a coat and cloak, instead of her usual maid uniform. She was even wearing a black mask, so there was nothing exposed except her blue eyes. Weapons such as a sword, gun, dagger, and axe were equipped at her waist.

"Are you ready?"

"Yes. I have put all the necessities in the shadow."

I nodded. Even if she didn't bring necessities, I had my smartphone, so there was no problem.

"We have to be back by lunchtime tomorrow at the latest. Let's go."

"Yes, Master-nim."

We hid our presence and went out of the mansion. Then, we went around a bit and headed towards Mount Hughes. We didn't go straight there to avoid leaving traces. If the knights found our traces tomorrow, things could get annoying.

At the entrance of Mount Hughes, I took something out of my inventory.

"Master-nim. What is that?"

"This? A snowmobile. It takes too much time and effort to just run. It wasn't as expensive as I thought."

It's a two-person snowmobile. It has skis in the front and tracks in the back. I got this from the 'Vampire Detective' world. It doesn't have any special powers, so its performance doesn't degrade even when used in another world.

I put on a helmet and grabbed the steering wheel. Yuria sat behind me and wrapped her arms around my waist.

We started to climb Mount Hughes on the snowmobile. It was definitely faster and more comfortable than walking or running.

,I need to find the rock that looks like an ogre's face. In the original story, it was said to be around the middle of Mount Hughes.,

But soon, a problem arose.

It wasn't the snowmobile or our fault. The snowmobile was working fine.

"Master-nim. Three ice trolls are chasing us from the right."

"Ice trolls?"

I turned my head to the right.

A large ice troll with blue skin was chasing us at a fast speed. I narrowed my brows.

Ice troll. The name sounds fancy, but its physical abilities are about the same as a regular troll. The only difference from a regular troll is that it has adapted to cold environments. It's a monster that exists in reality too. The author probably just used the real-life monster in the original story.

,Trolls are D-rank monsters. They are not a big threat to us.,

If I used mana now, I could easily deal with three trolls. Not to mention Yuria.

"They keep following us. Annoying... Kill them."

"Yes."

Yuria held up her pistol and aimed at the trolls. Just before she pulled the trigger, a magic circle was drawn in front of the muzzle. She pulled the trigger when the magic circle was completely drawn.

Tang, tang, tang!

The fired bullets passed through the magic circle. It's a power amplification magic circle. A regular pistol can't harm a troll, so the power must be amplified with magic.

The bullets accurately pierced the trolls' weak spots, their eye sockets, and penetrated their brains. The three trolls fell on the snowfield.

"As expected of Yuria."

"It's nothing... Master-nim! In front!"

Yuria said urgently. I discovered what was in front of us a little later than Yuria. I couldn't help it. I can't see through the darkness as well as Yuria.

"Damn it. What is that again."

There was something whitish in front of us. It was not moving in the air and was looking at us. It didn't seem that dangerous. The fact that it wasn't moving meant we could avoid it by going around it.

"It's a wraith. I made a fuss."

"It's a guy who hasn't properly taken shape. We don't need to turn back."

Wraiths are monsters with significant individual differences. Proper ones are difficult for even veteran Hunters to deal with. But the opponent is a wraith like a whitish smoke that hasn't taken shape. Except for the fact that ordinary physical attacks don't work, there's nothing special about it.

"I'll take care of it."

Yuria held a dagger in her hand.

A blue aura formed on the blade of the dagger.

Buuuuuuung.

I didn't slow down the snowmobile. The moment the snowmobile passed under the wraith, Yuria swung her dagger.

Kkiaaaaaaaak!

The wraith screamed and disappeared.

The simplest and most efficient way to kill a wraith is to attack it using mana. Strengthening the body using mana like me doesn't work.

I can't kill a wraith like Yuria because I haven't awakened my aura yet.

,Instead, I can kill wraiths with lightning.,

The lightning attribute I use consumes mana and vitality. Since the source of lightning is mana, I can successfully damage wraiths by using lightning.

"Are there any more guys chasing us now?"

"......"

Rarely, Yuria didn't answer my question. That's unusual. It was proof that her mind was elsewhere.

Yuria was looking at the upper left diagonal direction. My gaze turned that way.

A figure was visible on the pure white hill.

"...It's a yeti. I don't think I can handle it."

"Is it looking at us?"

"Yes. It definitely saw us."

The yeti waved its hand. No, it seems to be pretending to throw something. It was too dark for me to see well.

"Ice spears are coming! To the right!"

As Yuria said, I turned the snowmobile to the right.

Puk, puk, puk. Three large icicle-like ice spears were embedded in the place where we had been.

I felt a tension that made my lips feel parched.

Yeti, that is, the snow man, is a high-level B-rank monster even in reality. It has the ability to control ice. Most of them act alone, but sometimes they act in groups. When they are in groups, even A-rank Hunters avoid them.

,The yeti in this world even has the ability to control wind.,

Therefore, it is safe to assume that the yeti is an A-rank monster. It's a monster worth fighting only if you are at the upper level of Aura Expert.

"U-turn to the left!"

I drove the snowmobile according to Yuria's words. Puk, puk, puk, puk, puk! Ice spears were embedded in the ground.

,Ugh, damn... It looks terrifying just looking at it.,

I want to send the yeti to hell, but fighting it is not easy. Moreover, the yeti is far away, barely visible to me. Even if I fight, there is not much chance of winning.

Awoooooooooooooo.

I heard the sound of a wolf howling. I reflexively looked up at the sky. Today is a full moon. It's a perfect day to be crazy.

,If it's a wolf in Mount Hughes... it's a dire wolf.,

It's a C-rank monster.

I looked in the direction where the sound was coming from. Twenty dire wolves were running towards us. Dire wolves are weaker than trolls individually, but they always act in groups and are agile.

Sseueueueueueueu.

I can see wraiths popping out here and there. They have clearer bodies than the whitish ones from before.

"Seven ice trolls and a group of ice boars are coming from the left."

"Even wild boars smelled us. It feels like we've become a popular restaurant."

I spoke as if I was calm, but my insides were burning. It's a desperate crisis. If we stop here, we will become food for the monsters.

,Shibal. I didn't think it would be this bad.,

It didn't end here. Of course, monsters came out from the front as well. Thanks to Yuria quickly dealing with the ones in front, I didn't have to worry too much, but the monsters chasing from behind were the problem.

There are already over 80 monsters.

,More will gather as time passes. It would have been better if it was daytime... Should I have decided to move at night?,

Fortunately, the yeti started throwing ice spears at the monsters instead of us. From the yeti's point of view, we and the monsters probably look like the same food, so it started catching the ones that were easier to catch.

,It's a relief that it's far away. The yeti probably doesn't know we're human.,

If it knew we were human, it would have targeted us. Monsters instinctively target humans first. You can tell just by looking at the monsters following us.

,Ogre face rock... Ogre face rock...! Where is it!,

Kkaaaaaaaak!

I heard the sound of a monster in the air.

Yuria, who was sitting behind me, reacted quickly. She threw a dagger towards the sky. Something fell to the ground from the sky. It's a monster bat over 1M long.

,More monsters are increasing here.,

Buuuuuuung!

The snowmobile engine was loud. If necessary, I'm thinking of using hacking to exceed the snowmobile's speed limit. I learned from 'Vampire Detective' that hacking can temporarily transcend performance.

"...I found it!"

There was an ogre face rock.

I glanced back. They are still following us.

"I need to shake them off...."

"I think we can shake them off for a while using darkness."

"Please do it right now!"

"Yes."

Darkness poured out from Yuria's shadow. This is not magic.

Yeongcheon Ryu Dark Shift.

It emits darkness and immerses the surroundings in darkness. Only the caster can see through the darkness. Or, if you have the skill to handle mana above the caster or have a special ability, you can see through the darkness.

I can't use Dark Shift because I haven't mastered Dark Shadow, but I know it in terms of knowledge.

My vision went dark. I estimated the distance in my head and put the running snowmobile into my inventory as it was.

Yuria caught my body as I was about to fall to the ground. Yuria picked me up and started running forward.

"Yuria! There's a space on the front lower side of the ogre face rock, so jump in there!"

"I understand."

Puk!

I felt our bodies plunge into the snow and go down. The darkness cleared and my vision returned along the way.

,My vision is still dark. But I can see if I use mana.,

We were able to stand on relatively hard snow. I looked at the ceiling. There was a hole about 5M high.

"......Won't the monsters chase us here?"

Yuria said worriedly.

I shook my head firmly.

"That won't happen. This place is protected by a barrier. Even if the dire wolves have an amazing sense of smell, they won't be able to chase us."

"A barrier... Is this place a dungeon?"

"You can say it's a similar place."

We brushed the snow off our bodies. It's okay to go back. There's a safe secret passage. If we use that, we can quickly go down the mountain. The problem is that it's one-way.

"It's probably this way. Let's go."

We started walking inside, stepping on the pure white snow.

As we went inside, the snow gradually disappeared and the temperature became warmer.

"It's a cave. I can feel the heat."

"One of the secrets of Mount Hughes is that the inside is warm, unlike the outside."

We walked through the cave. I felt like I was going to live after being in a cold place and entering a warm place.

"......Could it be that this cave is warm because of the dragon vein?"

"Oh. You noticed right away. You're right. To be exact, it's a place where dragon veins gather. You can call it a spirit vein. Do you remember the legend of Mount Hughes?"

"Are you talking about the story that an Ancient Silver Dragon died on the mountain and the snow on Mount Hughes doesn't melt?"

"That legend is true. To be exact, this was the Silver Dragon's nest. The reason why Mount Hughes became a perpetual snow mountain is because that Silver Dragon made it that way with all its last strength."

I don't know the reason because it doesn't appear in the original story.

"Is the Dragon Lair Master-nim's purpose?"

"No."

The legend of Mount Hughes is a story from a very long time ago.

Dragon Lair? It would have already been discovered by adventurers a long time ago. You can't call it a Dragon Lair now.

"The dwarves living here are my purpose."
