The Murim Alliance delegation that had withdrawn returned half a year later.

Because communication arts were banned within the Heavenly Demon Cult, they had no choice but to contact the Alliance Leader from outside Cult territory, or return to the Murim Alliance directly.

The delegation had gone back to the Murim Alliance first. Since they used a teleportation artifact, it likely hadn't taken long.

'Even so, the fact that half a year passed means opinions were divided enough within the Alliance that they couldn't reach a conclusion easily.'

There were quite a few spies planted in the Murim Alliance, but top-tier secrets and intelligence related to the Alliance Leader couldn't be easily obtained even by spies.

"The Alliance has decided to accept the Heavenly Demon Cult's proposal. However, there are conditions."

At the word "conditions," the Cult elders around me made no effort to hide their displeasure. The delegation leader, Ung Gae, maintained a calm expression, as if he'd expected this.

"Let's hear them first. What are the conditions?"

"The number of personnel the Heavenly Demon Cult may dispatch to the secret realm is limited to ten."

The ones who immediately pushed back were the elders.

"Only ten?"

"They know the scale of our Cult, and they'd limit us to a mere ten?"

"Cult Leader! This practically means the Murim Alliance has no intention of accepting!"

I raised my hand.

The elders clamped their mouths shut at once. I felt satisfaction. Everyone was watching my reaction.

'This is power.'

The elders seemed deeply displeased with the Alliance's conditions, but I didn't particularly care.

"Fine. I accept the Murim Alliance's conditions."

The tense shoulders of the Murim Alliance delegation slowly dropped.

"Cult Leader. Even so, ten people is dangerous. The Murim Alliance could have laid traps in the secret realm."

I knew well what the elders were worried about.

A secret realm was like a dungeon in reality—difficult to interfere with from outside. Whatever happened inside, it was impossible to verify from the outside.

"We are martial artists representing the righteous faction. We do not resort to the petty tricks of the unorthodox or the Demonic Cult."

"...You've got a loose mouth, running it so freely. Have you forgotten where you are?"

"The Heavenly Demon accepted the proposal, and we are under a truce. Surely you don't intend to fight not even a day after establishing the truce? Do you not respect the Heavenly Demon?"

"You bastards...!"

Ung Gae came out far more brazenly than before. Even when the elders radiated killing intent, he calmly pushed back.

Since I had chosen the truce, the elders couldn't fight the Murim Alliance. Fighting them now would mean openly defying my authority.

"Enough. Don't push it. Don't think I agreed to the truce because I fear you. I'm only enduring this for the sake of the secret realm."

“…….”

When I stepped in, Ung Gae shut his mouth. He quietly gauged my reaction. He knew as well—that in the Heavenly Demon Cult, the Cult Leader's will was paramount.

"Tell me about the secret realm. Surely you don't expect me to investigate on my own? If so, I'll personally pay the Murim Alliance a visit to find out."

"No. From the moment the truce was established, we intended to share information about the secret realm."

"At least you have basic decency. But how did you learn of the secret realm's existence? Judging by the lack of rumors, it seems it hasn't even opened yet."

"The Alliance's diviners and the imperial court's diviners determined through divination that a secret realm will open in Henan three years from now."

"The imperial court's diviners? I see. So His Imperial Majesty knows of the secret realm as well."

"Of course. The Alliance has been formally granted administrative authority over the secret realm by His Imperial Majesty."

How?

The Emperor, whatever else, does not easily hand over administrative authority over a secret realm to another faction.

'Bribes wouldn't work on the Emperor either. Does the Emperor have some scheme?'

No answer would come from thinking too deeply about it.

I questioned Ung Gae.

"The name of the secret realm? Divination should have revealed at least that much."

"Its name is Samhoosan—Three Lakes, Five Mountains."

A name inherently reveals much.

"Three lakes and five mountains. Not much to go on. Do you have any other information about the secret realm?"

"The Alliance also has no information about the interior of the secret realm. I can swear this on the Murim Alliance's name."

"If you have no information about the interior, how do you know it's a secret realm impressive enough to warrant a truce?"

"Before we even began divination, there were auspicious signs. A qilin descended and roamed Henan, five-colored clouds appeared in the sky, and all the minor ailments of Henan's people were cured."

"Truly auspicious signs."

"Furthermore, His Imperial Majesty declared it the highest-tier secret realm to appear in centuries."

"Say that first, you idiot."

If the Emperor had said so, I would have believed it without question. Unless these fools had lost their minds, they wouldn't dare commit fraud in the Emperor's name.

'Henan, though. It's appearing in a troublesome place.'

Henan was practically the Murim Alliance's home turf.

It wasn't where the Alliance headquarters was located, but Henan was home to Shaolin Temple, counted among the highest tier of the great righteous sects.

"Northern Shaolin, Southern Wudang"—there's a reason that saying exists.

I threw question after question at him.

The information I gained was nearly nothing. Ung Gae himself didn't know much about the secret realm.

One thing was certain: the Murim Alliance was serious enough about this secret realm to propose the truce first. It wouldn't be strange to say they'd staked their fate on it.

'A highest-tier secret realm acknowledged by the Emperor. Worth gambling the Murim Alliance's fate on. The Alliance will likely send multiple elder-level or above into the realm. The Alliance Leader himself might even enter directly.'

I pulled up one corner of my mouth.

I intended to enter this secret realm myself. That was why I'd accepted the ten-person limit without objection.

"We're done here. Get lost. Surely you didn't think our Cult would throw a banquet for the likes of you?"

"A banquet? I have no interest. Do not forget the ten-year truce."

The Murim Alliance delegation departed.

"Cult Leader. You're not... intending to enter the secret realm yourself, are you?"

The Right Guardian, Sword Demon Yeon Seorang, asked cautiously.

"Just like Yeon Seorang. Quick to catch on."

"Absolutely not! There's no telling what tricks the Murim Alliance has planted in that secret realm! Please send me and the elders instead!"

"That's exactly why I'm going. Even if the Murim Alliance has set traps, my power can smash through them in reverse. Ah, you and a few elders will accompany me, so prepare well in the meantime. Dismissed."

The Right Guardian and the elders disappeared, leaving only me in the great hall. I sat in the chair and sank into thought for a moment.

'The limit of ten. The number is too small. To gain anything from the secret realm, we have no choice but to send a small elite force. The Alliance Leader—that beggar bastard—might be trying to lure me into moving personally into the secret realm.'

I stared into empty space.

A bait that didn't exist appeared before me. The bait was a treasure of unknowable identity. I let out a soft laugh.

'If you're preparing something, you'd better prepare thoroughly, beggar bastard.'

I traveled to Nakyueolsan, where Wiyu and Miryeong were.

In the living room, someone was following a video lecture on the TV, doing pilates in leggings that clung tight to the body. The outfit laid her figure bare, and it was pleasant to look at.

"Miryeong. Do you even need to do pilates?"

Miryeong's level was that of Harmony Realm. Even if she hadn't trained her body specifically as a martial artist, the influence of her high realm alone gave her a body without an ounce of excess fat. A realm where the mind influences the body, not the body the mind.

"This is my hobby. I miss modern civilization so much... I suppose. You're going to summon me to that infinite-something world too, right? Right? You summoned Yuria and Elena! It hurts my feelings that you're leaving only me out!"

"I said I'd summon you."

"You haven't, that's why I'm saying it. It's already been ten years."

"I haven't entered that world in ten years."

"You didn't go in without me, did you?"

"You dare doubt your husband, you wench?"

I pinched both of Miryeong's cheeks with my fingers and pulled.

"Aaahng! Okay, I get it! I was just bored! I'm telling you, this hurts!"

I played with her for a bit, then let go.

"Where's Master?"

"At this hour, she's training. She'll be back soon."

Wiyu was diligent. Even her so-called training was nothing more than gripping a sword and swinging it. Compared to her realm, it was training that didn't even count as basic conditioning. She knew that herself, and still trained steadily.

'Thanks to Wiyu's influence, I've been keeping up with basic training too.'

It was something Wiyu had ordered, and it helped maintain my senses, so I kept up with basic training consistently.

"And Hongye?"

"Same as ever. She went back to her hometown ten years ago and there's been no word. She'll come when she comes. She has a spatial teleportation scroll, after all."

"Isn't that dangerous?"

"No news is good news, as they say. I even did a divination once—no major problems. And she's not the kind of woman to get taken down wherever she goes. If anything, she'd be the one flipping things upside down."

Yang Hongye was also a powerhouse of the Harmony Realm. Worrying about someone like her was as pointless as it gets.

"And it's only been ten years. Ah, has it been more than ten?"

Watching Miryeong speak so casually, I realized anew.

'Her sense of time is different from mine.'

Part of it was because she was a martial artist, but she was a Ho-in tribe—not an ordinary human. Even compared to humans of the same realm, her lifespan was more than double.

"Miryeong. In three years, a highest-tier secret realm will open. Come with me then."

"Oh! A secret realm that you'd call highest-tier, husband? I'm intrigued. Sure. Let's go together."

"If I had my way, I'd bring Master too."

"Come on. That won't work. Wiyu hasn't left Nakyueolsan even once the entire time I've been here. There must be circumstances we don't know about."

"She might be able to leave for a few days, but... once you enter a secret realm, there's no telling when you'll come out."

In the worst case, one might be trapped inside for decades. That's how dangerous a secret realm was.

While I was blankly watching Miryeong's pilates, the door opened and Wiyu returned in her training clothes.

"Master."

"You've come? Good timing. My body was feeling stiff anyway. Follow me."

"Huh? Right after you get back?"

The only thought in my head was that I was screwed. Sparring with Wiyu basically didn't end until I was on the verge of death.

"Husband. Fighting!"

I smacked Miryeong on the head for no reason—she'd earned my ire—and followed Wiyu to the training grounds.
