He couldn’t just rush at the red dragon and ram his Dragon Slaying Sword into it.

There were too many eyes watching for that.

‘It had to be Heavenly Demon Seong Yujin who killed the red dragon, not Lightning Severing Master Seong Yujin.’

Looking at the red dragon’s power, it didn’t seem weak enough to be immediately subjugated by other Legions. It would last at least several tens of minutes.

‘Even though it’s appeared right before their eyes, they’re quiet. Are they all watching each other for a Hint? Indeed, they wouldn’t want to play the role of weakening the red dragon instead of killing it.’

They would only move when they were confident of success.

‘That’s no fun. Should I let them know that if they just stand by, they’ll lose everything?’

He raised his right Hand.

Blue lightning flashed.

Kwa-reureureureung!

Unlike usual, it roared with an intense light and thunder. The blue lightning stretched in all directions, momentarily attempting to resonate with the pitch-black volcanic clouds. However, his will did not permit it. He compressed the lightning directly into his Hand and clenched it.

Astrape (EX).

Although the rank of Lightning (SSS) was one step lower than Astrape, and as a result, Astrape could not fully utilize its EX-rank power, it was still a powerful attack.

‘Its Power might be slightly lower compared to a meteor. But it won’t be able to shatter or neutralize it with a laser Breath like a meteor.’

Because lightning was too fast for that.

He threw Astrape at the red dragon floating in the sky.

The trajectory of the lightning, which had been tearing through the air and flying in a straight line, suddenly twisted. It dropped slightly, hitting the volcanic crater instead of the red dragon.

Kwa-aaaaa-aaaaa-aaang!

The lightning exploded. A blinding flash illuminated the surroundings, and a part of the crater was completely shattered. Perhaps it had affected the volcano, as lava shot up into the sky.

His mistake, of course, it was not.

‘As if I’d make such an absurd mistake.’

Even the slight twist in Astrape’s trajectory was extremely unnatural.

‘Someone interfered with my Astrape.’

It wasn’t the target, the red dragon. It didn’t have the ability to do so right now.

‘There’s only one bastard capable of such a thing.’

One who wielded the same power of lightning as him, possessed the same Astrape skill as him, and contracted with the same Divine Seat as him.

“They seem to have been startled by that attack. They’ve started to move.”

Kang Myungjin said, not hiding his tension. As he said, the Legions, startled by his attack, began to move busily. They seemed to have felt a sense of crisis that if they just kept watching, they might not be able to do anything.

They attacked the red dragon while keeping each other in check. The red dragon, which had been preparing a Breath attack towards him, eventually gave up and began fighting the nearest Legions.

Mount Hua Island instantly became a chaotic mess.

“They’re coming.”

Kang Myungjin’s warning, whose eyes were sharp.

Even without it, Seong Yujin and Joo Seohyun could feel it.

A spear flew. Kang Myungjin swung his own spear, the Sealed Bryunac (SS), deflecting the incoming spear. Just before the deflected spear spun and plunged into the ground, a black-haired boy with blue skin appeared and caught it.

It was the Master of the Tetrix Legion, Boy Peerless Rain Brick.

He wasn’t alone. To his left and right were Golden Lightning Alterio and Demonic Fighting Overlord Lee Hsin.

‘The other two executives aren’t visible. Even if I don’t know about the others, Meld Norris is also an excellent sniper. I can’t let my guard down.’

There was a skill for times like this.

Lightning Heaven Flow (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

The electromagnetic waves spread out from him, then canceled each other out. There was someone emitting electromagnetic waves right nearby.

“A radar using radio waves. Isn’t that basic?”

Golden Lightning Alterio. He smiled faintly.

“Tetrix Legion Master. The enemy you should be aiming that spear at isn’t us, is it? Are you intending to interfere with the Mount Hua Island conquest?”

Kang Myungjin asked, his gaze fixed on Rain Brick.

“We will conquer Mount Hua Island. We will, not you. So, won’t you withdraw from this conquest? As I said before, a lot is at stake this time. I promise to give you ample compensation.”

“Do we have to stop just because you tell us to? You are not our Legion’s Master.”

“That’s why I’m asking you like this, aren’t I? If words don’t work… there might be only one way. We’re not Beasts. Let’s be civilized. Let’s end this nicely with words?”

“If there’s a place where everything can be resolved with just words… that would be a Utopia. It’s impossible in Atlantis, just like anywhere else.”

“Can’t be helped then. I’ll tell you in advance. I don’t particularly hold any ill will towards the A-Plan Legion.”

“I know. I can roughly Guess the circumstances behind your actions. But will you be alright? If you expend your strength fighting us, you’ll struggle when you try to catch the red dragon.”

“It’s fine. I have a wealthy patron-nim, so I’ve prepared various things.”

“Is that so.”

Kang Myungjin and Rain Brick clashed. With that as a start, Demonic Fighting Overlord Lee Hsin and Joo Seohyun also began their battle.

All that remained were Seong Yujin and Alterio.

They had already begun their fight. They tried to keep each other within their detection range using invisible radio waves.

‘It’s difficult. The electromagnetic waves are constantly being canceled out.’

Alterio was by no means weak.

Seong Yujin recalled Kang Myungjin’s words, which he had heard before coming here. Kang Myungjin could see a target’s Status Window and had told him Alterio’s.

「Name: Alterio Kephalas

Class: Lightning Master

Title: Golden Lightning

Divine Seat: Lord of the Sky

Affiliation: DO 12 District

Strength: 135 Agility: 137 Stamina: 110 Mana: 168 Luck: 30

Unique Trait: Golden Blessing (SS)

Traits: Lightning (SS), Ten Thunders (S)

Skills: Astrape (SS), Lightning Familiar (S), Strong Lightning (A), Lightning Flash (S)」

This was Alterio’s Status Window at the current moment.

All his stats except Mana were lower than Seong Yujin’s. Seong Yujin’s total stats were higher. However, he couldn’t be ignored. Even if Seong Yujin was superior in terms of stats, not all battles were decided by stats alone.

‘Alterio’s Unique Trait, Golden Blessing (SS). Even though its rank is lower than my Deception, it’s a Unique Trait incomparably better than Deception. There are almost no Unique Traits that rival it.’

Even Kang Myungjin’s Dragon Eye couldn’t compare to Golden Blessing.

‘Traits and skill ranks below the Golden Blessing rank increase by one step. It also slightly adjusts stats.’

The ranks of Lightning and Astrape had to be considered SSS.

He was a formidable opponent who could never be underestimated.

“Unbelievable.”

Alterio said. Holding his spear, he couldn’t hide his astonishment.

“What’s unbelievable?”

“I remember when you first came to Atlantis, when we met at the Starting Temple. You, whom I could have killed with a single Finger… have become so strong in such a short time that I can’t guarantee victory or defeat? Logically, do you think this makes sense?”

“Why couldn’t it? And that’s your logic, not mine.”

In this world or any other, there were countless beings beyond logic.

“So, logic doesn’t apply to a genius. That’s not wrong.”

Alterio convinced himself arbitrarily.

Pa-ji-ji-jik.

Seong Yujin gathered lightning in his Hand. Blue lightning rotated in one direction, compressing.

Ten Thousand Thunders (Manrae) was not a skill directly received from the Lord of the Sky, but a class skill. Therefore, Alterio could not use it. Instead, Alterio had a class skill called Lightning Familiar.

“You use lightning in quite an interesting way. By the way, are you alright?”

“Alright? What about?”

“Didn’t you use Astrape just now? It was quite a powerful output. You must have used a considerable amount of Mana. If you fight me now, you’ll be at a great disadvantage. Are you still going to fight me?”

“Are you always so nosy? Let me tell you one thing. I don’t fight battles I’m going to lose.”

“I see.”

Alterio gripped his spear tightly. Golden lightning erupted from the ornate spear decorated with gold.

He had imbued the power of Astrape into his spear.

Seong Yujin shot the prepared Ten Thousand Thunders (Manrae) at him.

Alterio moved sideways, dodging the attack. He knew the exact direction the lightning was being shot and moved only as much as necessary. His movements were, of course, accelerated.

‘Acceleration using the power of lightning. And even reading the movements and direction of lightning. He’s not an easy opponent. He even forcibly twisted the direction of the Astrape I threw earlier… I should assume that long-range lightning attacks won’t work unless he’s careless.’

Seong Yujin took out his Hwaryeon Bido from his Inventory. The blue lightning flowing from his Body turned red as it traveled along the Hwaryeon Bido, strengthening the lightning with the knife’s power.

「The Lord of the Sky is perplexed.」

「The Lord of the Sky asks if it can’t be settled with words.」

“It cannot.”

“Further conversation is meaningless.”

Alterio and Seong Yujin spoke, yet never took their eyes off each other.

Seong Yujin didn’t fail to understand the Lord of the Sky’s feelings. A Divine Seat invested in a contractor, and the more successful the contractor became, the more the investment was returned twofold. That was the Divine Seat contract in Atlantis.

Two contractors fighting and dying? For a God, it was nothing but a loss.

Seong Yujin took a step.

He performed the footwork of Lightning Heaven Flow, and Alterio performed the footwork of an unknown martial art.

"Uses Lightning Flash (Electrifying Speed)."

As the distance closed, they both used Lightning Flash almost simultaneously, accelerating. Alterio’s speed was faster. Seong Yujin’s was A-rank, while Alterio’s Lightning Flash had the power of SS-rank.

Lightning Heaven Flow (Raitenryu) Flying Thunder God (Hiraishin)

[Uses Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

‘I need to use Flying Thunder God and Acceleration just to match his speed? Was SS-rank Lightning Flash such an outstanding skill? Or is it the influence of his Equipment?’

Kka-aaang!

Sword and spear clashed.

Pa-ji-ji-ji-ji-ji-ji-jik!

Red lightning and yellow lightning intertwined, repelling and canceling each other out. But Seong Yujin could feel it. The red lightning, enhanced by Hwaryeon Bido, was slowly but surely seizing the yellow lightning, pressing it.

Lightning Flash is deactivated.

Lightning Flash dissipated.

His Body returned to that of a human. Alterio’s Lightning Flash, however, remained active. It wasn’t a matter of concentration. It was simply a performance difference due to the skill’s rank difference.

Kka-aang!

From the clashing Weapons, his lightning burrowed into Seong Yujin’s Flesh.

“How’s my lightning? Is it electrifying?”

“It’s certainly a bit electrifying. But I don’t need it, so I’ll give it back.”

Seong Yujin kicked Alterio’s spear shaft, creating distance. In the process, more of Alterio’s lightning entered his Body through his foot. He didn’t care. He rotated his Body in mid-air, gathering all the lightning within him to the tip of his Sword, and swung it.

A large quantity of red lightning shot out towards Alterio.

The attack was too fast to dodge, so he thrust his spear forward to block it.

Kwa-aaaaa-aaaaa-aa!

Alterio, engulfed in red lightning, fell to the ground. Even with Lightning Flash active, he was electrocuted. Of course, the electrocution didn’t last long. Just as Seong Yujin’s Lightning rank was SSS-class, so was his.

“Did you enhance my lightning by channeling it through that Sword and then unleash it?”

“It’s called Lightning Reversal (Noebane). Don’t you know it?”

“Reflecting lightning is a concept I’ve never considered. I’ve rarely even faced an enemy who wields lightning, to begin with.”

Kki-ru-ru-ru-ru-ru-ru-ru-ruk!

Seong Yujin raised his Head at the sudden roar. An eagle wreathed in yellow lightning was plummeting from the sky.

Just for a moment, his attention was diverted.

Pit.

His ankle was cut. He lowered his gaze to see a giant rat, also wreathed in yellow lightning, holding a blood-stained dagger in one Hand, cackling. He lifted his cut left foot and stomped on the rat’s Head.

Peong!

The rat’s Body burst. Yellow lightning discharged, temporarily electrocuting his Body.

That brief electrocution, less than a second.

Taking advantage of that opening, the eagle instantly accelerated, snatched his Shoulder, and flew upwards.
