With the help of the succubus Lena, I found the gambling den where the Witch of Wagers was around lunchtime.

According to Lena, the Witch of Wagers was quite famous among the demonkin.

The gambling den Lena led me to was in a sparsely populated village. Despite it being broad daylight, the gambling den's doors were open. Inside, people were gathered, gambling. Most were playing poker.

I headed inside with Marta-nim on my right and Lena-ssi on my left. I didn't intentionally put them on either side; it just naturally happened that I ended up in the middle.

As I entered the place, thick with the smell of alcohol and stale cigarette smoke, all eyes turned to me.

"A priest?"

"What priest comes to a gambling den with two priestesses in broad daylight? He must be cosplaying as a priest."

"That's still pretty crazy, isn't it? Wait a minute. That person is...!"

The gamblers, who had been chattering away, were startled as soon as they saw Marta-nim, their faces turning pale. They immediately dropped to their knees.

"M-Marta-nim. What brings you to such a humble place...."

"W-We run a legitimate gambling den. We pay our taxes to the church properly!"

Marta-nim sighed and spoke to the gamblers who were watching her every move.

"I didn't come here to see you. Go home. Right now."

"Y-Yes!"

The gamblers gathered their belongings and hurried out of the gambling den. It didn't take long for them to disappear from sight.

The only one left in the gambling den was a black-haired girl with twintails, wearing a black qipao and sitting at the poker table. She had been silently watching the cards in her hand until the gamblers left, but now she turned her gaze to us.

"Thanks. The hand I had was the worst of the worst. Anyway... a mage, a succubus, and a saintess. What an unexpected combination. What brings you here?"

She had a figure that could rival even the succubus Lena-ssi's voluptuousness. Her lightly made-up face exuded a decadent and seductive aura.

But she had twintails.

When a grown woman has twintails, you can't help but think, 'Is she crazy?' The problem was, the woman in front of me really was crazy.

"Deneb, Witch of Wagers. I've come to make a bet."

"Ahaha. As the Witch of Wagers, I can't refuse a bet. You want a token? You've already got one. It's the token of Olen, the Witch of Mia. Olen isn't a bad witch."

I wasn't surprised, since I knew that witches could recognize each other's tokens.

Even as I looked at Deneb, who was laughing so carelessly, I didn't let my guard down. She was a witch who had lived for over 100 years.

"Yes. I want a witch's token. What do you want?"

"Your right hand."

I was silent. Instead, holy mana began to flow from Marta-nim beside me.

"Would that be too witch-like to say? I don't really need a person's right hand. It wouldn't taste very good anyway. It's common sense that pork is tastier. 100 million credits. That's pocket change for President Eugene Meyer, right?"

100 million credits is equivalent to about 3 billion won in South Korean currency from my past life. In Neo-London, it's the annual salary of an elite employee at a major corporation.

Of course, it wasn't a lot of money for me now.

"Is that all the conditions?"

"The bet is three rounds. One round wouldn't be enough for you, would it? Oh, you can change the stakes for each round. But you can choose the type of bet. How about poker?"

"Let's keep it simple. Coin toss."

"Ha, ahahahahaha! You know me well?!"

How could I not?

I was a famous NPC among the original game players. Systematically, I couldn't beat her in any gambling game other than coin toss.

'She's a woman who's been gambling for over 100 years. She's mastered all sorts of gambling techniques. It's impossible to win in any gambling game where skill can be involved.'

Therefore, the gambling game with the highest chance of winning against the Witch of Wagers is a pure game of chance.

"Just asking, but would you be willing to give me the witch's token for 1 billion credits?"

"Ah. I'd hate to do that, even if I died. If I handed over the token like that, the Great Witch would slap me."

I didn't really expect her to agree.

I took out a money box from my inventory. The witch casually checked the money box containing exactly 100 million credits in cash.

"You came prepared from the start?"

"Cash comes in handy."

Unlike my past life, currency is universal in this world. Cash works everywhere. And it can be a good lubricant when doing business.

A large amount of mana moved within Deneb's body. It wasn't forming a spell. This wasn't magic. Her mana was creating something, forming the shape of a person. But it wasn't a person. A goat in a tuxedo. A grotesque figure with a human body and a goat's head.

"Unique skill. The Fair Devil. This devil dealer can detect any kind of external interference. Cheating? Of course, it's impossible. Feel free to try."

"...A unique skill that materializes a creature. I've seen something rare."

"Didn't you come to me knowing all this?"

"Knowing and seeing it in person are different."

Everyone knows what a tiger is. But seeing a tiger at the zoo is different from facing a tiger without a cage.

'I know more than you think. The Fair Devil? There's no such devil.'

It's true that the devil dealer has the ability to detect external interference.

'The real ability that Deneb is deliberately hiding is... manipulating the probability of certain situations. The actual probability of the coin toss isn't 50/50, but 70/30.'

Of course, it's 70 for my side.

'If I lose the first round, the probability drops by 10%, and if I lose the second round, the probability drops by another 10%, and only in the third round does it become 50/50.'

Conversely, the more I win, the probability increases by 10%. Should I say it decreases for me?

'She's a gambling-crazed woman, but she likes winning much more than losing, and she considers cheating to be part of gambling. The standard strategy is to take a potion that increases the probability... but I didn't have time to get one.'

30%.

It was doable.

Whatever happens, my goal is the witch's token. I only need to win one out of three rounds. If I lose all three rounds? Then it's best to look for another witch. She's not the type to give in to threats.

"You can take out the coin and toss it yourself. The dealer will detect any tricks. Feel free to try it out."

"...I'll use a 1-credit coin. An ordinary 1-credit coin with no magic or tricks."

"The dealer isn't reacting, so it must be true."

"Can I toss it now?"

"It doesn't matter. Oh, the dealer will immediately detect any force adjustments. There are occasionally gamblers who manipulate the front and back of the coin by adjusting the force."

"I envy that. I don't have that kind of physical talent."

"I'll bet on heads. The side with the numbers, not the letters, is heads. Some people don't even know that."

"I see. I'll toss it."

Ting.

I flicked the coin with my thumb.

Binggeulbinggeulbinggeulbinggeul.

The coin fell. It landed on tails, the side with the letters.

The Witch of Wagers nonchalantly crossed her arms and licked her lips with her tongue.

"...I lost. Fine. I'll give you the token. A bet is a bet."

Deneb's hand, which was extended to me, was trembling slightly. Was it from anger? No. It was excitement and pleasure. Deneb sought victory in bets, but she was a true crazy bitch who felt dopamine even from defeat.

'If you eat and gamble for over 100 years, it's no wonder your brain gets messed up.'

Anyway, I received the token from her.

'Now, if I get just one more token, I can officially participate in Walpurgis Night.'

"Didn't you forget? The bet is a total of three rounds. Two more rounds left. What are you betting this time?"

I've achieved what I needed to achieve. Even if I bet casually, the bet will be made.

'In the original game, the stakes you could bet were limited. 500 million credits was the limit. If you bet an absurdly expensive item, Deneb would refuse. Saying there was nothing with equivalent value.'

Frankly, it was a system that didn't fit with the setting that Deneb loved gambling.

'There's a rumor that these system limitations were put in place because there were players who would whine to the developers if they bet everything and lost it.'

This isn't a game inside a monitor. There are no system limitations set by the developers.

"I'll bet 1 billion credits."

"...Ha. You must have money to burn? Are you sure? The last round could have been beginner's luck. You're really going to bet 1 billion credits on a single coin toss? Even for you, that's not a small amount of money. I know that the company's money and personal money are different."

"You don't need to worry about me. Instead, you should bet an arcane magic. If you're a 7th-circle witch, you should know one or two arcane magics."

"That's a loss for me? Arcane magic isn't easy to get even if you have money."

"Nonsense. You can get a decent arcane magic for 1 billion credits."

Even if a great magician is really short on money, they'll sell arcane magic.

Although it's true that you can't get really useful arcane magic for 1 billion credits.

"Hmm. Fine. I'll bet a 6th-circle arcane magic. Mana Break. It's a curse-type arcane magic that destroys the target's mana, temporarily preventing them from using it."

"Is it an arcane?"

"No. It's just a regular arcane magic."

I know. I just asked because it would seem suspicious if I didn't.

'In the original game, this is what you bet when you win the first and second rounds and enter the final third round. Betting higher stakes has definitely changed things from the original.'

"This time, it's tails. What are you doing? Toss the coin."

Deneb said, her shoulders trembling slightly. Her eyes were more serious than before. Her breathing was also faster. She was trying her best to hide it, but she couldn't fool my senses.

'What an incurable gambling addict.'

Ting.

I flicked the coin.

Unlike before, the coin rolled across the floor before stopping.

It's heads.

Silence fell.

After checking the coin, I looked up at Deneb.

Deneb's expression, as she covered her face with both hands, was distorted. Absurdly, anger, joy, and despair coexisted. Frankly speaking, she was making a very perverted expression.

'She's a beauty... but I don't even want to get close to her.'

Deneb swallowed hard several times and said.

"Ah, there's still one round left. You never know until the end in gambling. Gambling is where you can make up for your losses with one big win."

"Really? Then I'll bet 1 billion and an arcane magic. Oh, not the arcane magic you bet. It's a Death Ray, an offensive arcane magic. It's a 6th-circle magic, but it has the power of a 7th-circle arcane magic. Depending on the situation, it's even higher than a 7th-circle."

"...If it's an arcane, it's much more precious than Mana Breaker. You're betting that?"

"Getting cold feet?"

Her eyes widened at that one word.

"Cold feet? Me? I've been gambling for over 100 years, but I've never had cold feet. In the end, I was always the one laughing. Can you really handle it?"

"I can handle it. So, what are you going to bet?"

"...I'll bet this qipao. It may look like this, but it's a 7th-grade relic. A relic from the ancient country of True China."

"Where did you get something so precious?"

"It's my mother's keepsake."

"......"

Crazy bitch.
