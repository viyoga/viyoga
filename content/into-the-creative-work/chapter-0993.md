Hwangbo Gahye and Oh Jimu quickly parted ways. Oh Jimu moved to find orbs, and Hwangbo Gahye entered an underground space and set up a barrier. Oh Jimu's subordinates, Yeonju and Jogung, hid at the entrance and stood guard.

Hwangbo Gahye took out the space storage item she had taken from Seong Yujin from her pocket.

A hard, rectangular object. The surface was smooth, and the weight was very light. She had seen top-tier magical treasures (beopgi) like space storage items a few times. More than 80% of them were in the form of small boxes, and the remaining 20% were in the form of pouches.

,Box-shaped ones could be used by opening the box and taking out the items... How do I use this one?'

When she pressed what seemed like a button, light appeared on the surface. Hwangbo Gahye wasn't flustered. She had secretly watched Seong Yujin operate his smartphone a few times. The screen showed nine dots. A pattern lock screen appeared.

,He moved his fingers like this....'

It was fascinating to see lines drawn on the glowing screen. She recalled how Seong Yujin did it and somehow managed to unlock it.

The problem was what came next.

It was full of unfamiliar letters and pictures. When she touched various things with her finger, the screen reacted, but she had no idea how to take out the items.

After fiddling with the smartphone for over an hour, Hwangbo Gahye sighed and carefully placed the smartphone on the ground.

,I can't do this here. I'll have to entrust it to a magical treasure (beopgi) expert later....'

Hwangbo Gahye sat cross-legged in the center of the underground space. She took out the Solar Bloom from her pocket. The blazing Solar Bloom was mysterious and beautiful.

Currently, Hwangbo Gahye's realm was the 10th stage of Exceeding Aspirations (Chulji). She was on the verge of reaching the Five Qi Realm (Ogi). If she could properly harness the effects of this Solar Bloom, she would more than reach the Five Qi Realm (Ogi).

"Huuu...."

She took a breath as if to shake off her nervousness and shoved the Solar Bloom into her mouth. Her mouth was hot. The heat quickly spread to the center of her body. Hwangbo Gahye focused on circulating her energy. A hot wind continued to blow around her body, and her clothes became soaked with sweat.

She concentrated and concentrated to form her inner core.
