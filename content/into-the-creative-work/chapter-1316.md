,I am Rufus, the Vice Chairman of Pantheon."

Melissa drew her sword from her waist.

,The Vice Chairman of Pantheon... You're a bigger deal than I thought. Do you usually take action yourself?"

Rufus sighed.

,I don't like getting involved directly either. Thanks to someone, Pantheon is short on manpower, so I had no choice but to step in."

,Looks like there aren't many Demon Contractors."

,Demons are picky too. Not just anyone can become a Demon Contractor."

,Where is the Chairman of Pantheon? Is he in the Royal Castle?"

,Yes. He is in the Royal Castle. I was just communicating with him... He said there was an intruder. The Chairman is a cruel person, so I feel sorry for the intruders. They won't die easily. Ah, is the intruder perhaps an acquaintance of the Duke-nim? That's too bad."

,Haha, hahahahaha."

Melissa laughed out loud. Rufus, whose mood soured, glared at Melissa.

,Why are you laughing?"

,It's because you said it so confidently. You seem to think the Chairman of Pantheon is a big deal... But the intruder is too far outside the norm... I can only imagine that Chairman dying. Though, I don't even know what the Chairman of Pantheon looks like."

,That's absurd. Laughing for such a reason..."

,You can say that because you don't know her. If someone knew her... they would pity the Chairman of Pantheon."

,Enough. I was going to humor the conversation and maybe get some decent information... but I'll postpone that for later. If I kill you, King Doolibad will definitely throw a fit, so I'll take you alive."

Rufus snapped his fingers.

The demon behind him reacted. It inflated its upper body and let out a roar towards the sky. Rufus's subordinates reacted to the wolf-like howling. Their heads and torsos transformed into beasts. However, parts of their bodies were twisted, like having arms on their heads or human faces on their butts.

,The Beast Demon, Korkoz."

,So you know Korkoz. Yes. He has the ability to turn people into beasts. There are conditions that must be met, but I don't intend to tell you that."

,The condition of eating the fingers of the target you want to turn into a beast?"

,...You even know that? You seem to have studied demons a lot."

,I didn't study demons. I just heard about it. I also know the beast's weakness. It's weak to silver."

,That's right. Then do you also know this? Each beast is as skilled as a knight. In other words, you have to face 70 knight-level beasts alone."

,That's terrible."

,Surrender. That's the only way to make it less terrible."

,Don't you think things are going too well for you?"

,......What do you mean?"

,Isn't it strange that I, the leader of the rebels, am here alone like this?"

,Are you trying to say this is your trap? I've already confirmed that there's no one around here. Soldiers and knights. They all charged towards the Royal Castle. There are no troops to help you."

,I currently have two identities. One is Duke Argent, the leader of the rebels. The other is... the leader of the AM Unit under Eugene Phroukas."

,AM Unit? What's that?"

,It's something like this."

No sooner had Melissa finished speaking than the space around her rippled, and several people appeared.

Rufus knew that they had appeared through space teleportation. It wasn't magic. It was something else.

,Probably an artifact.'

Rufus burst out laughing at the sight of the women who appeared. There were about 30 women, all wearing maid outfits.

,Knights? Not even soldiers, but maids. What can you do with maids?"

,They're not just maids. They're combat maids. They may look as beautiful as greenhouse flowers, but they've been through thick and thin."

Click.

The maids of the AM Unit aimed assault rifles at the beasts. Rufus, who knew nothing about firearms, didn't think much of it even after seeing their relaxed posture and atmosphere. His beasts, more than twice their number, would tear them apart.

,That's enough. Dealing with you is making my head feel weird. I'll kill all the maids and take only you alive."

Rufus waved his hand and ordered the beasts. The beasts charged towards them.

,Fire."

Melissa said briefly.

The maids simultaneously pulled the triggers. The muzzles of the assault rifles spat fire, showering the charging beasts with a rain of silver bullets.

,Kkeueoeoeok!"

,Kkaegaegaegaeng!"

,Kkiiiiiik..."

The beasts couldn't avoid the rain of silver bullets and screamed as they fell. If they were ordinary bullets, they would have ignored them and kept running, but they were all made of silver. Moreover, the silver bullets were engraved with special magic that amplified their effectiveness.

Rufus blankly watched the dying beasts. The 70 beasts couldn't even get close to the maids.

,...What kind of weapon is that?"

,It's a gun. A weapon that can easily kill living things. If you don't know about knights, you'll get screwed. Even if you do, it's not easy to deal with if there's no cover."

,...Everything that was fired was silver. Did you know my identity too?"

,No. I didn't know your identity. I just always have silver bullets prepared."

,Is that so. In that case, I have no choice but to step in myself. I don't like becoming a beast, but... I have no choice."

The demon behind Rufus seeped into Rufus's body. Rufus's body began to change. His snout protruded forward, and his body grew larger. Thick, black fur grew long on his skin.

Thwack!

A silver bullet flew from behind Rufus and pierced his back. Rufus's body staggered. Then, silver bullets flew in succession and pierced Rufus's forearms and thighs.

,Keuheok...!"

Rufus collapsed to the ground. Perhaps because he was a demon, no blood came out of his body.

,We don't have the patience to wait for your transformation."

,Wh-where...!"

,I've deployed snipers on the mountain behind us. In fact, from the moment you appeared, we could have blown your head off at any time. The reason we didn't kill you is... you probably know without me saying it. I hope you have a lot of information."

Melissa said to the maids around her.

,Everyone, good work."

,It's nothing, Leader. This was the easiest mission we've ever had."

,That's right. The marquis assassination mission we did last time was harder. There were so many damn traps."

,Wasn't it a waste of a space teleportation scroll?"

,I'm going to get cake later... Anyone want to join me?"

,I only eat chocolate cake."

,Shouldn't we take a bath first before going back to being human?"

The surroundings suddenly became noisy. Melissa chuckled without realizing it. Even though she knew this was a battlefield, being with them eased her tension.

,There might be more attacks. Just chat in the barracks. You can use the space teleportation scrolls as much as you want. It's all the Master-nim's property anyway. Snipers, I'm sorry, but keep holding your positions."

,Yes."

,Does anyone have any black tea?"

,I brought Choco Pies."

The maids entered the barracks. The surroundings became quiet again. Melissa gazed at the battlefield. Just then, the castle gate was destroyed. The soldiers shouted and entered the castle.

Three men and women appeared before Yuria, who was waiting in front of the Blue Dragon.

The woman with loose light brown hair was familiar to Yuria.

Vessel.

She was a professor of magic at Goldway Academy. The contractor of Revs, the Demon of Dreams. She had her left arm cut off by Yuria in the past. Currently, her left arm was perfectly attached.

She glared at Yuria with narrowed eyes.

,Oh my. Fancy meeting you here?"

,...Yes. It's been a while. I'll be sure to end your life today."

Vessel shuddered. She forced a relaxed smile to hide her nervous body.

,You're different from when you were so excited back then? Have you grown a bit... Hiiik?!"

Thorns sprouted from Vessel's shadow. Surprised, Vessel screamed and jumped up. If her reaction had been even a little slower, her body would have been pierced and killed by the shadow thorns.

Yuria was slightly impressed.

,I can't believe you dodged that. You've grown too, haven't you."

,You damn bitch!"

Vessel gritted her teeth. The space around her distorted. Yuria quietly held a dagger in her hand.

,Vessel. Step back. If you die, the seal on the Blue Dragon will be broken. Then it will be irreversible."

A man stepped forward. His golden hair reached down to his waist. Vessel quietly stepped back at the man's words. Vessel was secretly delighted because she didn't want to face Yuria.

,Who are you?"

Yuria looked at the man. She already knew his identity but didn't show it.

,I am Wayne Lysisthea, the Chairman of Pantheon. We know about you. Yuria Grace. After hearing that Vessel was defeated by you, I collected information about you. The results were surprising. To think that Eugene Phroukas's maid was actually the legendary assassin, Dark Empress, who single-handedly annihilated her own Helbrit marquisate. Do you know how surprised I was when I heard that information?"

,I'm not interested. The boy next to you... he's not human, he's a demon."

Next to Wayne stood a boy who looked about 10 years old with an expressionless face.

,That's right. He is Rorff, the Demon of Lightning."

,...Did you reincarnate a demon as a human?"

,You recognized it right away? Impressive."

,It's ambiguous to say he successfully reincarnated. He lost his demon body, so does his reincarnation have any meaning?"

,Still, he retained the demon's spirit and authority. He succeeded in reincarnating, so he will continue to reincarnate and live forever. He has become immortal."

,That's ridiculous."

Unlike those words, Yuria didn't laugh. She wasn't very interested in the demon's reincarnation. She was just checking if the information from the original story was correct.

Rorff distorted his lips at Yuria's attitude.

,You're not afraid of me at all. Lysisthea. May I kill this impudent human?"

Wayne answered leisurely.

,I don't mind."

,Kuhaha! I'll kill you, you impudent human!"

Pajijijijik!

Rorff's body turned into golden lightning. The demon, transformed into lightning, rushed towards Yuria. It was truly the speed of lightning.

Yuria lightly waved her left hand as if swatting a fly.

Yeongcheon Style Ultimate Technique Shadow Lightning.

The black lightning shot from Yuria's left hand collided with the golden lightning. The black lightning was neutralized, but the golden lightning was slammed into the ground. The golden lightning returned to its child form.

,Ugh... This, this can't be happening... I, defeated by the lightning of a mere human...!"

Rorff, who had turned back into golden lightning, rushed towards Yuria. He was faster than before. Yuria wrapped black lightning around her right foot and kicked the golden lightning like a soccer ball.

The kicked golden lightning repeatedly slammed into the ceiling, walls, and floor before embedding itself deep into the ceiling. A beat later, Rorff, who had returned to his original body, fell powerlessly to the ground. He was already dead.

,Rorff!!!"

Wayne shouted and used his power towards Rorff. Rorff, who had stopped breathing, opened his eyes wide and raised his body.

,Uwaaaaaaaak!"

Rorff let out a roar. In response, golden lightning seized the surrounding space.

Yeongcheon Style Ultimate Technique Shadow Lightning.

A streak of black lightning ignored the golden lightning and pierced Rorff's chest. Rorff, who died instantly, collapsed.

,Rorff!!!!!"
