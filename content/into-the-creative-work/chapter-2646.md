I sat in the workshop, which I had emptied for the move, and stared at the glass bottle on the desk.

A glass bottle filled with red liquid. Alchemic blood. It was something Yuria gave me.

,Alpatier coveted this thing, huh.'

Judging by the fact that they tried to get it by sending a mediocre corporate agent, it might not be a very important item.

,If they really wanted to get their hands on it, they would have sent the real deal, not some half-assed guys.'

From Alpatier's perspective, alchemic blood is something that would be nice to have, but they wouldn't particularly care if they didn't have it.

,Blood that allows you to use alchemy even if you don't know alchemy.'

Of course, the alchemy you can do is limited. All you can do is turn the blood itself into metal, or cause the metal to deform. Since it uses mana, ordinary people without mana cannot use it.

,Even if its value has risen due to the influence of Adarium, it's not powerful enough for a giant corporation like Alpatier to covet. Alchemy is objectively weak in combat. It's inefficient to use alchemic blood for self-defense. If you need alchemy, you can just hire an alchemist. ...What Alpatier coveted was probably the method of embedding alchemic formulas in the blood.'

I figured out how to do it. It wasn't that difficult. I analyzed it with magic and the answer came right out.

,They just engraved a cell-level formula into the blood of a 5th grade or higher alchemist.'

I don't know how they engraved it. Alpatier is probably coveting that technology, or maybe they're coveting the blood itself.

I looked at the alchemic blood and pondered two options.

One is to request an analysis of the alchemic blood from a specialized research institute. However, there is a good chance that Alpatier will get involved. That will make them fully aware of my existence.

,Even though LK Corporation is growing at a frightening rate, it will take a lot of time to become competitive with giant corporations like Alpatier.'

The second is to use blood magic.

Of course, this is easier for me. The problem is that the accuracy is lower than if a research institute analyzed it. Specialized research institutes have accumulated data over the years, but I had nothing. You put the blood in a machine and click a button, and all sorts of blood information comes out.

,On the other hand, blood magic isn't that convenient. The information you can find out is limited. Besides, this blood has already been processed once. Blood magic may not perform to its full potential. The worst case scenario is that the alchemic blood itself could be damaged by the aftermath of the blood magic. Even if the blood magic is successful, the results may not be as good as expected.'

After much deliberation, I chose blood magic.

It was a difference in risk. It's better to fail alone than for Alpatier to know that I'm hostile to them.

Dudungshil.

The glass bottle floats in the air. The lid of the glass bottle falls off due to telekinesis, and the alchemic blood pops out. A magic circle is drawn in the air, and the alchemic blood is fixed in the center and does not fall.

[Blood Scan]

The 5th grade blood magic was successfully activated. The blood's information pops into my head.

,A man in his 30s. Brown hair. Blue eyes. Congenital asthma patient. ...That's all I can find out.'

I stored the alchemic blood for now.

I might be able to track it with Blood Chaser magic, but I didn't have time for that right now.

The thing I needed to focus on now was moving the mansion.

,I can't be 100% sure that the original owner of this blood is related to Alpatier. Maybe he's already dead. Whatever it is, it's not urgent enough to do right now.'

The thing I needed to focus on now was moving.

• • •

The day before the move.

I headed to the mansion in District D with Yuria.

It was more than three times larger than the mansion in District G where I was staying. It was literally a mansion. In terms of size alone, it was one of the best in District D.

Plus, although it was in District D, it was close to the border of District A, so the location was good. Not only was security guaranteed, but there was also a small ley line under the mansion. If I set up a workshop, it would be of considerable help for magical research and training.

The mansion building itself was completely demolished and rebuilt five years ago, so it was quite nice.

,Even if I'm doing well now, I wouldn't be able to get a mansion like this normally. I still lack money and connections compared to the great nobles of Neo-London.'

In other words, there must inevitably be a flaw in this mansion.

For the record, this mansion was cursed by a demon. Not like some superstition, but for real. A demon on par with a Transcender cursed the mansion.

The former owner of this mansion was royalty, but as a demon worshiper, he succeeded in summoning a demon through human sacrifice, but he tried to play a little trick in the contract and was cursed by the demon. The demon worshiper died and the Council recovered the mansion and put it up for auction.

And I was the one who bid on and received this mansion. The demon's curse remained in the mansion, so I got it for an incredibly low price.

Today, I came with Yuria to resolve the demon's curse.

"You don't have to come with me. Why don't you go back to the mansion and rest since you finally got a vacation?"

"It's already too late, Master-nim. I won't be a hindrance to Master-nim in my current state. And since this is the mansion that I will be managing in the future, I feel a sense of responsibility."

To be honest, I felt reassured having Yuria with me, so I didn't force her to go back. Two 7th graders. We'll be able to resolve the mansion's curse even more reliably.

We walked to the entrance of the mansion. Unlike other places, the mansion was gray. The entire mansion site had turned gray due to the influence of the curse.

Before entering through the main gate, I looked at the ground. There were several footprints. It seemed that someone had crossed the gate and entered inside.

"When I came the day before yesterday, there weren't any footprints like this."

"An intruder, huh? District D has good security, so there are no homeless people. They clearly broke in with intent."

"I'm surprised they entered through the main gate so openly. Does that mean they have no intention of hiding? Now I'm curious about what the intruder's intentions are."

I took out the key from my inventory and inserted it into the gate. Chak! Even though I didn't turn the key, a strange force acted and turned the key. And the door opens on its own.

"It doesn't seem to be the type of curse that rejects people."

"The Council's report said that it wasn't a dangerous and malicious curse that required the mansion itself to be destroyed immediately."

"What kind of curse is it specifically?"

"A curse that screws with you."

"Hmm. When you put it that way, it sounds like a really trivial curse."

"Three knights, seven Triple-class servants, and eleven Double-class or lower servants died. Don't let your guard down."

"Yes, Master-nim."

[Electric Detection]

I started by using search magic. But the search magic didn't extend out as if it was blocked by something.

,...There's no problem with the flow of mana. Is it interfering with the phenomenon itself rather than the mana?'

I walked through the garden, wary of my surroundings.

-Ppuppuppu! The train is leaving!

A voice mixed with laughter was heard, and the air distorted and a train appeared. The train was aiming directly at me and Yuria and was running at full speed.

Before I could even use Chronos, Yuria grabbed my hand and pulled me to the side. The train quickly passed where we were and disappeared into the distorted space.

"Was that an presumptuous intervention?"

"No. Thanks to you, I saved my strength."

I realized that Yuria's reaction speed was much faster than mine. It's not that I'm slow, it's that Yuria is excessively fast.

Anyway, we continued towards the mansion. The curse continued. The ground suddenly turned into lava, or thousands of man-eating rats fell from the sky.

"So this is the level of screwing around in Neo-London. I'm surprised."

"...That report itself was screwing with us. Maybe the curse drove the report writer crazy."

Even a 7th grader will die if they let their guard down.

And we passed through the garden and reached the front of the mansion. A man was hanging from a tree in front of the mansion. The man's face was forcibly peeled off and painted white. The clothes he was wearing were bulletproof light armor that mercenaries mainly wear. His right arm looked like it had been eaten by a monster.

"Judging by the size and shape of the shoes, this is the intruder."

"...The thing in the torn backpack is a bomb. That bomb would blow up this mansion. ...Did he enter the mansion with the intention of blowing himself up? He could be a victim of the curse or a family member."

That's when it happened.

The hanging corpse's body twitches and swells.

[Barrier]

The barrier that I used as soon as I sensed something covered me and Yuria.

The corpse swelled up to its fullest and then soared into the sky. And it exploded. Bang! Colorful fireworks are embroidered in the sky.

"Kekakakaka! Pretty, isn't it?! Isn't it pretty?! You humans like fireworks, don't you! Say it's pretty!!"

The mansion door was already open. In front of it, a doll with a long nose was laughing loudly.

I narrowed my eyes and looked at the doll.

That's not a demon. But it was also a demon. A medium of curse containing the thoughts of a demon.

"I can't believe you showed up on your own. You saved me some time."

I raised my right hand. Pure white light emanates from the center of my hand.

"Ew. You handle that terrible thing so well."

"Holy mana. A powerful force that is effective against you demons. Let me ask you before I kill you. What's your name?"

"It's Baal!"

"Baal, the #1 ranked? You're spouting nonsense that even a passing dog wouldn't believe. Just die."

[Divine Buster]

Pure white light explodes.

There was no particular physical force. Divine Buster is a holy magic that only damages the enemy. It is very fatal to undead or demon-type enemies.

But the doll was fine, with only its surface scorched.

"Kekakakaka! Weak, weak! I won't die with just this much."

"......"

I silently prepared another magic. If holy magic doesn't work, I'm just going to smash it with physical force. The mansion building will be partially damaged... but it can't be helped. Getting rid of the trash in front of me is the top priority.

Pajijijik....

Blue lightning flashes and disappears.

Space is distorted. The curse that was surrounding the mansion began to reorganize around me and Yuria.

It was a colosseum. We were facing each other on the arena.

The doll sitting in the audience seats laughed fiercely and said.

"I hate couples! It pisses me off when I see them! Now, did you catch on? Hurry up and kill each other!!"
