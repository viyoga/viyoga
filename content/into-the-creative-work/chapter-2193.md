I stared blankly at the entrance that had opened for a moment.

An unexpected variable had occurred. A huge variable that would require me to change more than half of the plans I had laid out.

,It's not an unpleasant variable. It's rather a welcome one.'

The ancient ruins of Great Korea are Korean-friendly. It's difficult to meet this condition, but once you're recognized as Korean, things become easier for many.

,It's a lucky situation.'

Even the entrance right in front of me opened automatically, didn't it? Perhaps the traps lurking inside will also be deactivated.

,It's so good that I wonder if it's okay. I can afford to be a little more relaxed.'

I hesitated before entering the Sebit Floating Island.

,Even if I'm considered Korean... the women beside me aren't Korean. Is it okay to go in together?'

,Bbibik. It is natural for a Hannam to have several foreign wives through international marriage.'

,...Is that so.'

To be honest, such cases are rare even in games, so I don't know much about it.

Thinking that good is good, I head inside the Sebit Floating Island with my party. A long corridor stretched out. Entrance 11 had fewer traps than other entrances and was relatively safe, but the path was long.

,If I had known this, I should have started from Entrance 1.'

Entrance 1, the most dangerous but also the fastest for my plan.

I shake off the lingering regrets and start planning again from now on. Just because I'm recognized as Korean doesn't mean it's unconditionally safe. This ancient ruin is no different from a dungeon after so much time has passed. I mustn't be careless.

,How fascinating.'

Orsia said. Her voice echoed in the corridor.

,What is it?'

,Yujin-ssi, I mean. You seem to know a lot about the ancient ruins of Great Korea... I didn't know you were a legitimate descendant of Great Korea. If your K-DNA is over 99%, aren't you Great Korean itself? Were you perhaps a frozen human of Great Korea?'

,That's not it. I'm an orphan... so I don't know much about my roots either.'

I told the truth, but Orsia didn't seem to believe me.

To be honest, I was curious about my identity too. But it was the kind of problem that no one could answer clearly.

,He wouldn't be a frozen human. The AI said there's no information in the database.'

Invisible Blade said.

,...True. If he were a frozen human of Great Korea, there would be information in the database. Then it's a coincidence... it's still fascinating.'

,It might not be a coincidence, but fate.'

,Fate. I don't believe in fatalism.'

,Yeah. Me neither.'

,...I've been thinking this for a while, but Invisible Blade-ssi needs to be more polite.'

,Well. I've lived just fine without that so far. Isn't it annoying to nitpick everything? Especially since you're strong.'

,Leaving aside strength, people should have culture. A person without culture is just a beast and a barbarian.'

,Are you saying I'm a beast? If you peel back a layer of people, they're all pretty much the same. Even you, with all your culture, must have aspects you want to hide.'

Listening to their conversation, I had a feeling I couldn't just stand by.

,Stop.'

If I leave them alone, they'll start to fall apart from the inside.

,This is an ancient ruin. Orsia-ssi. Please restrain yourself. Invisible Blade, you too. Especially you, you should have something to do, right?'

,...I'm sorry. It wasn't the time to have such a conversation.'

,Ah, the traps, you mean? I found a few... but I ignored them because they weren't working at all. Even if they do work, I can handle them, so don't worry.'

The moment to see her skills with my own eyes had come.

,Stop.'

At Invisible Blade's words, who was walking ahead, the party stopped. Invisible Blade made a simple hand sign. A water shuriken appeared between her fingers. As she swung her hand, the water shuriken flew with fierce momentum and pierced the floor.

Kwaaaang!

The floor exploded.

,It's a landmine-type bomb. They're advanced enough to install AI at the entrance, but landmines are quite old-fashioned. Usually, when there are such mismatched weapons... it means a battle occurred inside. Maybe a rebellion happened here.'

I was inwardly impressed by Invisible Blade's insight. The internal rebellion of Sebit Floating Island had actually happened. If you wander around these ancient ruins, you can see several records related to it.

,The path has split into two. Up and down. Which way should we go?'

To get the LK-99 superconductor, we need to go down. The superconductor research and production facility is located in the central core area.

,Let's go up.'

Having come this far, it would be foolish to just get the LK-99 and leave. Since we're here, we should get everything we can.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion resounded from quite a distance. Pirates. No, it's obvious that the corporations' dogs have rammed into Sebit Floating Island.

Wieeeeeeeeng!

A siren sounded throughout Sebit Floating Island.

-Sebit Floating Island is under attack. We have identified enemies trying to invade Sebit Floating Island. Switching to emergency security system. Citizens, please move to the shelters.

Click. Click.

Doors opened in various parts of the corridor, and bipedal robots walked out in an orderly manner. Orsia and Invisible Blade unconsciously tensed up, but the robots with the Korean words "Special Police Robot" written on their bodies quietly passed us by.

,Sebit Floating Island's power is considerable, but... since the corporations have decided to step up properly, Sebit Floating Island will eventually collapse.'

We went up the stairs. The camera installed on the ceiling shone. I stood in front of a thick steel door.

,Bbibik. You have a special resident card. Welcome. This is the combat robot research lab of Sebit Floating Island. Currently, the combat robot research lab is inoperative due to the death of all researchers, including the director.'

,I want to go inside. Can you open the door?'

,Bbibik. Permission granted.'

Originally, even Koreans couldn't enter this place. It seemed to be thanks to the special resident card. Although I don't actually have something like a resident registration card.

I went inside. The lab was a mess. Metal parts littered the floor.

,Bbibik. Do you have any desired materials? All research data has been discarded.'

,Yeah. I'll take care of it myself.'

I picked up a spherical component lying in the corner. The only intact component here.

I threw it to Invisible Blade.

,Huh? Are you giving this to me?'

,It's a core component of the octuple compression barrier technology. Take it.'

,I'm a ninja, though?'

,A ninja from a corporation. Acrotex is said to have a lot of people from the East.'

,Hmm. I'll gratefully accept it. It's a technology they'd probably like. If applied to a cyber suit, it would create a pretty useful item.'

Next, we moved to the bio lab. I gave all the remaining research samples there to Orsia.

,Those are probably heterologous stabilizer samples. You can research them yourself, or you can sell them to a company specializing in bio research.'

,...A heterologous stabilizer... is that the drug I'm thinking of?'

,Yes. That's probably it. It's a drug that reduces the side effects of those with mixed heterologous blood. Elves may not have significant side effects... but other races tend to have major and minor side effects. It will be more stable than any existing heterologous stabilizer. Don't you need it?'

,...No. It's a necessary item. If the Hitel School announces this stabilizer... yes. We can gain a great advantage. However, that would make it my achievement. Are you okay with that?'

,I don't mind. Instead, I'll take the rights to the LK-99 superconductor.'

I'm not just giving them things for no reason. The real deal is the superconductor. I have to give them things like this so they won't covet the superconductor.

We also took other small things. Great Korea's combat rations, and strange-branded drinks like Great Cider. Even if they're not of great value, we should take what we can.

We went up to the highest building on Sebit Floating Island and could look around. Various parts of Sebit Floating Island were on fire.

Combat personnel dispatched from corporations and combat robots from Sebit Floating Island were fighting. The corporate employees used high-powered weapons without hesitation. Missiles were planted everywhere, causing powerful explosions.

They didn't stop even when employees dispatched from other corporations were caught up in it. No, it seemed the goal was to get them caught up. In the end, corporations also recognize each other as competitors.

This was already a battlefield.

I clicked my tongue as I watched some of the buildings I had been eyeing collapse. I regretted the decent artifacts that would have been inside.

,These bastards are putting finding artifacts on the back burner and trying to eliminate competitors first. Even so, this is strange. Corporations would want intact artifact technology as much as possible... Did one of the corporations betray the others? Then this mess makes sense.'

As I was watching, something caught my senses.

Not fast, but something quiet and secretive was targeting me.

,...A curse?'

Pajik.

I swung the electricity I generated from my fingertips into the air. The electricity soared and burned the curse that was flying in secretly.

,A black magic curse. If I hadn't been steadily researching black magic... I might not have noticed.'

I don't know what kind of curse it is.

I looked around to find the culprit who cast the curse on me, but the surroundings were too chaotic to identify them.

,There seems to be a black rat on the battlefield. It won't be easy to track.'

Orsia said. There was no way she, a 7th-grade mage, wouldn't have noticed the curse that I, a 6th-grade mage, had noticed.

,I don't like being attacked... but even if we participate in this battlefield right now, there's nothing we can gain. We're going down to the core area. It doesn't seem like we can afford to be too relaxed.'

,That's true. It seems like at least six or more corporations are involved in this. They're rampaging with a vengeance. They must be desperate.'

We went down. The lower we went, the quieter it became. The core of Sebit Floating Island is in the center. In terms of total area, it's less than 1% of the space.

We went down, opened a splendid door, and arrived at a plaza-like place. The first guests who arrived pointed their guns and swords at us. Among them were familiar faces.

,Magicians-nim. We've met before, haven't we?'

,To meet you here... it seems luck is on our side. You guys must know the information about this place?'

It was Takeshi, a middle-aged man with a sharp impression, and Uhee, a square-faced woman wearing an eye patch. Behind them, employees belonging to the corporation were lined up.
