I looked at the cherry tree in the center of the garden.

365 days a year. Regardless of the season, the cherry tree, always in full bloom with pink cherry blossoms, was the pride of this mansion.

The cherry tree also had its own will, so it also protected the mansion from intruders. In the case of too strong of an intruder, it couldn't repel them and could only alert the servants. But that alone was enough to do its job.

Its appearance was quite beautiful, so it was rumored to be pretty among the nobles.

I reached out to the cherry tree and tried to connect with it. Usually, I could feel the cherry tree's will when I did this.

But this time, I couldn't feel the cherry tree's will.

,It's not dead. I can still feel the movement of mana and a subtle electric current in the tree. Is it distracted by something else?

I used magic to get the cherry tree's attention.

[Electric Spark]

An electric sphere created in my grasp.

The cherry tree devoured high-quality electricity. It especially liked the electricity I made. If it were the original cherry tree, it would have absorbed my electricity right away, but the current cherry tree didn't react.

My brow furrowed.

,What's the problem?

I thought about it carefully. Phantasmal cherry tree. I was taken aback by this happening after there had been no problems until now.

,Information about phantasmal cherry trees is... almost nonexistent.

When an unknown problem arises, what is the fastest and most reliable way to solve it?

The answer is simple.

,Call an expert.

There are two occupations related to phantasmal cherry trees.

,Summoners and Druids.

Both are rare professions. Summoning is a branch of magic, but because it requires special talent and magic, there are hardly any magicians who enter the path of summoning. Druidism is quite different from magic from the very beginning.

,It's hard to see a druid in a megacity like Neo London.

Fortunately, I knew one druid. A druid with whom I had a close relationship, close enough to share bodies.

Aisa Gomez. I invited her, a reporter for the Liberals, to the mansion.

• • •

Aisa Gomez came the next day.

A woman with green hair and bronze skin. With a coke bottle figure, she showed off her elastic skin in a tank top and hot pants. I could feel health itself from her.

,Wow. I'm getting invited to the mansion of the CEO of LK Corporation, who's been making waves these days.

,Back in the day, the Liberals reporters used to try all sorts of tricks to get over the mansion's fence. Thinking about that time makes me feel stressed. And LK is a privately held company.

,When will LK Corporation go public? If it does, everyone will come looking for it with bundles of money, you know?

,There are no plans to go public anytime soon.

Even though I'm the CEO of LK Corporation, I can't make all the decisions alone. I also have to talk to the Magic Association and the Neo Round Table Council, who hold shares in LK Corporation.

,It'll probably continue to be privately held. What's important to them isn't the mass supply of superconductors, but the monopoly. There's no need to grow the size of LK Corporation unnecessarily.

It's better for me to be privately held too. If I go public, I can raise investment quickly, but various regulations will be attached.

,Unless other countries or companies develop superconductors, there's no need to rush.

I took Aisa to the cherry tree.

Aisa's eyes lit up as she looked at the cherry tree.

,Wow. It really is a phantasmal cherry tree. It must have been quite a struggle to bring it from the Phantasmal Realm. How did you manage to bring it here?

,It's spoils of war from killing a summoner.

,...Ah, um. You're lucky. But I think you've raised it well. At least it looks very healthy on the outside. I can feel its spirit too.

,Spirit?

,Phantasmal creatures usually have both the characteristics of ordinary creatures and spirits. Spirit is the power and source of the spirit. For phantasmal creatures, it's their power and life force.

,I don't know much about that spirit thing.

,You need special training to feel the spirit. Yujin-ssi would probably figure it out and feel the spirit without having to train. Ah, just kidding.

I focused my mind.

Spirit. A power different from mana. I wouldn't have known if I didn't know. Now that I knew, I couldn't just ignore it.

After focusing my mind a few times, I roughly understood.

,I definitely feel something different.

I asked Aisa to confirm if what I felt was spirit.

,It feels like slightly cool water. Is that spirit?

Aisa's eyes widened as if they would tear. She opened her mouth and tried to say something, but only a breath was heard instead of a voice.

,Is it that surprising?

,Is it surprising? I did corpse play for a month to feel the spirit. It wasn't just lying still, I actually stopped breathing. It was a state of suspended animation thanks to the power of magic... I only managed to feel the spirit after experiencing astral projection five times? And you did it in less than 10 seconds. Ah. Seriously. I knew the world was fucked up, but I didn't know it was this bad....

She, who had been rattling off words like a machine gun, shook her head with a sigh.

,Spirit. I didn't just realize it. It's somewhat familiar because it's similar to when I was training in black magic.

I didn't say it out loud and only muttered to myself. It was clear that saying this would only sound like arrogance. This kind of situation had happened several times before.

Aisa, who had been grumbling for a while, soon composed herself and reached out to the cherry tree. Her palm gently stroked the trunk of the cherry tree.

,It's growing pains. This cherry tree has reached the limit of its growth. It's trying to break through the limit and grow. Right now, it's enduring the growing pains caused by that.

,......In short. It's getting stronger.

,Wouldn't it be more accurate to say it's evolving into a higher being? Since it's a phantasmal creature.

,That's all good, but suddenly evolving? Is that the case for all phantasmal creatures?

,Of course not. It's when the conditions for growth are met. Moreover, this isn't even a Phantasmal Realm environment, so its growth should be slow. Has the cherry tree done anything recently? Like giving it a special elixir.

,I've been steadily supplying it with electricity. It liked the electricity I supplied with magic. It's connected to the electrical system in the mansion, so it can suck up electricity even when I'm not here. The gardener has been steadily taking care of it, and except for occasionally asking for cola and pouring it on....

Something suddenly came to mind.

A letter I received from Yuria-nim a while ago. Yuria-nim was trapped in a certain space during the Dark Moon, and what was by her side at that time was a cherry tree. To be exact, it was a clone imbued in a brooch made of cherry tree branches, or so she said.

,...The clone probably shared its experiences with the main body as it returned to its original form. Could that have had an effect?

Dark Moon.

When you think about it, the cherry tree is always outside in the garden whenever the Dark Moon rises. The energy of the Dark Moon must have affected the cherry tree.

,Is there a Dark Moon in the Phantasmal Realm too?

,I haven't heard of such a thing. Ah. The cherry tree might have been affected by the Dark Moon.

,It looked fine the day after the Dark Moon.

,The Dark Moon mostly has a negative effect on humans... but it surprisingly has a positive effect on things that aren't human. This is the first time this has happened, so I don't really know either.

,What should I do to help the cherry tree regain consciousness?

,It'll regain consciousness if it overcomes the growing pains and evolves.

,......Is it possible that it won't overcome them?

,Yes. It won't die unless it's the worst-case scenario. I'm sorry I couldn't be of more help.

,No. The information you gave me was helpful enough. At least I know it's not a serious problem.

Still, I felt a little bad just standing by and doing nothing. I am the owner of the cherry tree. If I could at least give it something like a nutritional supplement....

,Wait. You said phantasmal creatures also have the characteristics of spirits? Does that mean it's a spirit?

,Yes. That's right. You can say it's a spirit, even if it's a bit ambiguous. This cherry tree eats and uses electricity, doesn't it? Well, spiritists would never admit it, though.

I took out a small blue gem from my inventory.

,...I can feel the pure power of nature. As a druid, I might be a little tempted. What is that?

,Spirit Orb.

I found it while rummaging through the items inside the inventory.

[Purchase the Spirit Orb for 200 Inventory Points.]

200 Inventory Points.

In terms of gold, 2kg was consumed.

Perhaps because the Spirit Orb was a consumable item, it couldn't be rented and had to be purchased.

,I could naturally understand how to use the items in the inventory, except for a few that seemed to be locked. The Spirit Orb is one of them.

A stone that spirits like. It can also be applied as a kind of elixir for spirits.

I threw the Spirit Orb at the cherry tree.

The unconscious cherry tree quietly absorbed the Spirit Orb. Pajijik! The cherry blossom petals shone, and a slight electric current sparked.

That was the end of the reaction.

,It's effective. Its spirit has become a little stronger, though it's only a little. It might be okay to give it as a nutritional supplement.

A nutritional supplement worth a whopping 2kg of gold.

,I can feel the cherry tree rejoicing. You called it a Spirit Orb? That's a really good treasure.

,Rejoicing, huh. I don't really know.

,I'm a druid, after all.

It was the words of an expert. Who would I trust if I didn't trust an expert? It was the moment I was about to nod at her.

A rapid movement of mana felt not far away. A sudden increase in the number of presences. A surge of murderous intent from somewhere. The magician's senses grasped all of that in an instant.

,Astral Open.

The magician's mind is awakened, and the senses are heightened even further.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 11]

Putting everything else aside, I use an Instant. The code of conduct that says, "If you feel something strange, use an Instant first," was engraved in my body.

,Barrier.

As soon as the accelerated thinking ended, a translucent barrier spread out, enveloping me, Aisa, and the cherry tree.

Kkaang!

A bullet flying from behind collides with the barrier. Instead of panicking, Aisa immediately took a fighting stance, and I turned around.

The bullet that collided with the barrier did not lose its power and rotated, trying to penetrate the barrier. The barrier actually cracked. It wasn't an ordinary bullet, but a magic bullet. On top of that, the gunslinger had used a technique.

,Barrier Triple Layer.

Three additional barriers are created. Immediately after, 11 bullets flew and hit the barrier. Two barriers were torn apart in an instant, and the third was on the verge of breaking.

[Magnetic]

A 3rd-grade magic that manipulates magnetism is activated. The bullets that were trying to penetrate the barrier were drawn to the ground by magnetism.

,Ugh. Can't we go a little easier?

The one who revealed herself with a grumbling voice was the maid I hired. Her name was Shea, I think. A triple-class maid who is hard to hire.

In addition to her, butlers and maids were approaching from all over the garden, forming a siege. Also, my senses caught those approaching from outside the mansion.

,Who's behind this?
