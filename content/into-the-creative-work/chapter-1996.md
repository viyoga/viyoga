,What's the matter?"

I asked calmly. Things had been going well for me lately. As my majesty spread throughout the world, humans offered me beautiful women on their own. I was in a good mood, so I could tolerate most rudeness.

,The war! It's not a war anymore!"

,What the hell are you talking about now?"

Even as civilizations developed, wars occurred periodically among humans. Since wars hadn't disappeared even in modern times, I figured it was just something that happened. Humans would wage war even in space if they advanced into the space age.

,They kill humans with a single finger and fight in the sky with fighter planes instead of chariots! A single bomb can wipe out an entire village...! This isn't war!"

,They kill and are killed for each other's benefit. If that's not war, then what is?"

,No! War is! Wielding swords! Clashing! Creating heroes in blood! There are no heroes in this war!"

,There are heroes. You just don't know them. And isn't the war you desire too barbaric?"

Isn't it better to run around with a gun than with a sword?

Of course, from the perspective of gods or superhumans, running around with a sword is better. Guns have such clear limitations.

,This isn't war! Humans have become too arrogant! They dare to claim ownership of the land, sea, and sky as if they were theirs! Take away their electricity! Make them realize who the master of this world is!"

,Impossible. Even if I take away their electricity, they're already too advanced. Humans will find another way."

,Cause a great flood! Wipe out the humans and recreate them! Just like Father did in the past! It's the second time, how hard could it be!"

,That was possible because Gaia cooperated. The current Gaia would refuse. Creating tens or hundreds of thousands of humans is burdensome even for Gaia."

,Kkeueueueueu...."

Ares clenched his fist tightly.

Why was Ares being so passionate? It was because humans didn't worship Ares that much anymore. Ares was the god of war itself, unrelated to victory or defeat. What human would want a god of war who doesn't guarantee victory?

,If you really want to wipe out the humans that badly, persuade more than half of the twelve Olympian gods and then come back."

,...I understand."

Ares showed a rebellious look in his eyes before disappearing from my sight.

,I don't like this. Should I throw him into Tartarus? What would Hera say?'

The possibility of Ares persuading more than half of the twelve gods was low.

Humans had developed civilization, but their primal greed hadn't changed. They pursued power, relied on family and love, and feared death.

No matter how much civilization developed, humans were still humans.

That day was like any other.

,I've already slept with all the goddesses of Olympus... I should check on the humans on Earth.'

I wondered if there was a woman who would conceive my child.

I was slowly looking over the Earth when it happened.

,Zeus-nim! Zeus-nim! It's a disaster!!"

Hermes, the god of the shuttle, flew over. The guy, who never gets tired of flying through the sky thanks to the effects of magic and shoes, was panting heavily.

Hermes was a guy who had rolled and rolled as my shuttle, so he didn't get flustered by ordinary things. He was Hermes, who only wore a detached expression even when I had affairs with married goddesses.

,Hmm. Is the thing that was going to happen finally happening?'

I was calm. It was probably what I had expected. It was a bit late, but what was going to happen would eventually happen.

,Did Poseidon, Hades, and Ares start a rebellion or something?"

Hermes was startled.

,How did you know? Did someone make a prophecy or something? Prophecies have become useless since the fall of fate...."

,It's not a prophecy, but the result of cold reasoning."

Poseidon, Hades, and Ares were full of complaints about me. Because they had suffered a lot. Especially Poseidon, it wouldn't be strange if he tried to assassinate me.

Because I had slept with all the women related to him, from the woman Poseidon had set his sights on to his wife and daughter.

,Hades is trying to invade by organizing an army of the dead. Artemis, Demeter, and Persephone are keeping him in check."

,Those three goddesses are having a hard time. But can they stop the army of the dead with just that? It can't be just Hades."

,Humans have decided to join forces. Isn't that why we developed humans in preparation for times like these?"

That wasn't it. I only developed civilization with the thought of sucking up their faith.

,Everything is going according to plan."

,Ares has joined hands with barbarians!"

,Barbarians? Are there any barbarians left in this world?"

,They are humans from another world."

He wasn't talking about humans from a real different world. The other world Hermes was talking about was a place from another mythology. A place ruled by other gods.

,Which side is it?"

,It's the north. A thunder god named Thor has dared to declare that he will kill Zeus-nim and steal his lightning."

,How presumptuous. Is it urgent?"

,No matter how great a god is, they can't enter our world carelessly. Conversely, it's hard for us to invade them. Barbarians are just barbarians. They can't defeat our humans who have achieved a high level of civilization."

,Ares is a fool, so there won't be any problems. As long as he doesn't step forward himself...."

,He's not stepping forward directly. He seems to have wanted the barbarians' war, so he's watching from behind and enjoying it. Even if he does step forward, Hephaestus is watching him, so there won't be any problems."

Hephaestus and Ares were brothers, but their relationship was very bad. They would kill each other if they could, there would be no holding back.

,It seems like Apollo has also sided with Poseidon. The sun is gradually descending to the ground... If we keep leaving it like this, all the humans will soon burn to death, or the crops will wither."

,Apollo has joined the rebellion?"

I was surprised. I really didn't expect Apollo's rebellion. Apollo seemed to follow me very well on the surface.

,Isn't it because of Artemis and Daphne?"

,...That could be it."

Artemis was strangely obedient to me. It might have been displeasing to Apollo, who cherished his younger sister.

In Daphne's case, she was a nymph who Apollo fell in love with due to Eros's arrow. To the point where he even stalked Daphne. I rolled around with Daphne in front of Apollo's eyes. To be honest, I didn't even know if that nymph was Daphne. I just slept with a pretty nymph, and it just happened to be Daphne.

,Of course, I would have slept with her even if I had known.'

At that time, the enraged Apollo confronted me, and I suppressed Apollo with force. After locking him up in Tartarus for a few years, he became docile.

,I thought he had forgotten about that incident, but I guess not.'

I looked at Hermes. He hadn't mentioned the most important guy yet.

,Poseidon has joined hands with monsters in the deep sea!"

,...What?"

,That monster or god, I don't know which, that's crouching in the deep sea. Poseidon has joined hands with it and is invading the land with sea monsters! Its followers call themselves Deep Ones."

,...How's the war situation?"

,All of the humans' ships have been sunk. No matter how strong the ships made of steel are, it's hard to use their power in the sea, which is Poseidon's territory. The sea route is as good as blocked. But since there's a sky route, it's not a big chaos. The humans are dealing well with the monsters coming up to land. Guns and bombs are effective against monsters too."

,So our side has the advantage."

,We can't let our guard down. They're trying to win over the humans. Their goal is to build power with the humans' faith."

,That's obvious. Is the humans' faith wavering?"

Hermes smirked.

,Don't you already know?"

That's right.

The faith in me hadn't wavered.

Humans feared me more than the army of the dead and the sea's forces. To be exact, they feared the disappearance of civilization.

,What are you going to do?"

Hermes asked.

Suddenly, what Hypnos had said came to mind.

There is a traitor.

Was he talking about the rebels? Or was he talking about another god? When it comes to betrayal, the most suspicious and fatal one was Hermes. As a shuttle god, he was taking on all sorts of troublesome tasks. If Hermes played tricks, it would become quite annoying.

,Why didn't you join the rebellion?"

,You should always bet on the winning side."

It was a valid point, so there was nothing to refute.

I got up from the throne.

,Are you already stepping forward yourself?"

,The situation is serious. You know that too."

,Still, I didn't expect you to step forward right away. Where are you going first?"

,I should go for the easy one."

,Aha. Ares, I presume."

,And give a warning to the north as a bonus."

,...Um. It might be better not to provoke the north too much."

,I'll take care of it, so don't worry."

Hermes's eyes were shaking uneasily as he looked at me. I almost hit him, but I stopped. If Hermes joined the rebellion, things would become even more troublesome.
