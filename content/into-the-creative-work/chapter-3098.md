The attack landed clean.

Yet the Nephilim didn't die—it was still alive.

Blood streaming from its head, its entire body wreathed in frost, it refused to die and kept on living.

"Strange. I know its body is absurdly huge, but it took a direct hit in a weakened state and still won't die? By all rights, that should've been a fatal wound."

The Ten-Thousand Thunder Spear I'd just hurled was an attack I'd put considerable focus into. Even an S-rank boss monster would be critically wounded if it took a proper hit.

'And yet all it did was bleed. As if the spear's power had been cut in half.'

I quietly glanced at Juan standing behind me, then immediately looked away.

'That bastard didn't weaken my attack with his ability. If he'd pulled something like that right in front of me, I'd have noticed instantly. No—it makes more sense to look at the Nephilim's own ability or traits.'

The Nephilim's exact abilities and specs weren't even in the data provided by the World Hunter Association. The only estimate placed it at the very top tier among SS-rank boss monsters.

'That's why they chose to seal the Old Gate rather than recklessly subjugate it. Because subjugating it would require S-rank hunters to risk their lives.'

Sealing was safer than deliberately courting danger.

Drrrrrrrk!

The frost coating the Nephilim's body shattered. Its massive wings began to move slowly.

"Damn it. My ability's gradually losing its grip! Don't tell me it's a weakening effect like mine...? No. That's not it. It's just simply getting stronger over time! You lot! Are you just going to stand there watching?! Do something!"

Juan shouted. Annoying bastard. I'd love to tear him apart before the Nephilim, but there were too many eyes watching.

Han Ah-young raised her hand toward the empty air. As she focused, cold energy gathered in the void. The cold became ice, and the ice instantly swelled into a massive iceberg.

The iceberg, overwhelming in mass alone, plummeted toward the Nephilim.

The Nephilim moved. Its ankles were trapped in the frozen sea, unable to flee, but it extended both arms and caught the falling iceberg.

Han Ah-young's expression hardened.

"...It caught that? What absurd strength."

Han Ah-young swallowed her breath and clenched her fist.

Kkjejeok!

The iceberg's surface cracked like glass, then exploded outright. Sharp, massive shards of ice embedded themselves in the Nephilim's body along with the cold. The cold energy was injected into its flesh, beginning to freeze the Nephilim. The giant's body was once again blanketed in frost.

"Well now. For such a hot-looking face, she's brutally cold."

I furrowed my brow.

"Juan Perez. You're useless, so stop flapping your gums and keep your mouth shut."

"Hah. I'm useless? If I hadn't weakened that monster, do you think we could've caught it this easily? The useless one here is you. Didn't you see that monster moving just fine after taking your attack earlier? Your attacks are all flash and no substance."

"Whether they really lack substance—you can verify that with your own body."

"Yujin!!"

At Han Ah-young's urgent voice, my gaze shifted from Juan to the Nephilim. Half-frozen, it forced its wings to flap and lunged at me.

It wasn't a speed I couldn't dodge. As I tried to sidestep, I confirmed the Nephilim's movement suddenly accelerating. Juan's weakening power had vanished.

[Using Flash(刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

Even with Flash active, dodging the Nephilim's body slam was impossible. Its speed was one thing, but its sheer size was too great.

Instead of dodging, I sent a bolt of lightning to the warship.

[Using Lightning Step.]

Appearing atop the warship, I looked up at the sky.

Kwaaaang!

The shockwave from the Nephilim colliding with the barrier shook the warship.

"Juan Perez! Why did you release your ability?!"

Han Ah-young glared at Juan and shouted.

Juan scratched his head and spoke without a trace of apology.

"It was a mistake. Anyone can make a mistake. S-rank hunters are human too."

“…….”

Han Ah-young's eyes began to grow colder. Juan shrugged once and extended his hand toward the Nephilim floating in the sky.

"I won't make a mistake this time, so don't worry."

The Nephilim weakened. Its massive body, which had been about to attack the barrier, lost balance and wavered.

Han Ah-young shook her hand as if flicking something off. A massive hand formed of cold energy seized the Nephilim. The cold became enormous ice shackles, dragging the Nephilim down into the frozen sea.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido and summoned lightning.

Pajijijijijijik!

The erupting lightning even caught Juan nearby. He raised his hand to block it, but some of the lightning he couldn't fully stop scorched his skin.

"Agh! That stings! You bastard! You want to go?!"

I openly sneered at him.

"A mistake. Like someone said—anyone can make a mistake."

"...You little shit...."

I flipped him off with my left hand, and with Hwaryeon Bido gripped in my right, I charged at the falling Nephilim.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Gale Swift Thunder (疾風迅雷).

Lightning originating from my spine spread through my entire body, reinforcing and accelerating my flesh.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Hundreds, thousands of micro-currents imbued with my will guided my body.

I landed on the falling Nephilim's shoulder. It still kept its mouth shut, quietly watching me.

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 6]

In the slowing time, I concentrated my will, body, and mana into Hwaryeon Bido.

From the red blade of Hwaryeon Bido, a blue aura blade and red lightning surged upward. I compressed the raw, unrefined energies into the blade with sheer will.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Blade (雷罡刃).

The red lightning blade fell upon the Nephilim's neck.

A massive amount of blood burst forth.

Unfortunately, even Thunder Blade couldn't cut all the way through. What I managed to sever was half the neck. For an ordinary lifeform, damage to the cervical spine alone would be a fatal wound—death wouldn't be surprising. But the Nephilim moved as if nothing had happened.

The Nephilim's massive hand seized my body. I reflexively raised a protective aura, but it shattered in less than a second. The result of pure physical strength with not a shred of supernatural power behind it.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Soul Arhat (雷魂羅漢).

My body became lightning.

Simple physical force without any special power couldn't harm me. The Nephilim's hand gripped my body, but it couldn't touch my essence.

I moved past the Nephilim's hand. I ran along its arm and reached its shoulder.

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 1]

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Blade (雷罡刃).

I swung the red lightning blade as before. This time, I cleanly sliced through the remaining half of its neck.

The moment the Nephilim died, Han Ah-young's cold energy instantly froze its head and body. With death, the power dwelling in its flesh vanished, leaving only a lump of meat that couldn't resist her power.

Before the Nephilim's corpse could crash into the frozen sea, I kicked off its shoulder and landed quietly on the warship.

Han Ah-young immediately ran to me.

"Yujin! Are you okay?! You're not hurt anywhere?!"

Han Ah-young checked my body all over. My clothes and body were sticky with blood, but it wasn't mine—it was the Nephilim's. She let out a sigh of relief.

The masked sealing unit's mage approached us.

"Impressive. To think you'd not only hold off the Nephilim but subjugate it. Thanks to you, our work will be much easier."

Juan walked over and cut into the conversation.

"It's all thanks to me. If I hadn't weakened that monster, things would've gotten far messier."

"What Hunter Juan says is also true. Thanks to everyone's efforts, the world is a safer place."

I ignored Juan and looked at the Old Gate. Even with the Nephilim dead, the Old Gate hadn't disappeared.

"Why isn't that thing disappearing?"

"The Old Gate isn't a dungeon. Unlike dungeons, it doesn't vanish even if you kill the boss monster. With current technology, we can't eliminate the Old Gate itself. Sealing it is the best we can do."

"Will new monsters come out later? Or will another Nephilim appear?"

"We can't know. There are cases where new monsters emerge, but monsters like the Nephilim are rare. Variables could arise later, so... please keep watch until everything is finished."

Perhaps because the Nephilim was dead, the sealing unit's mages began sealing the Old Gate with a more relaxed atmosphere.

Kugugung!

Suddenly, the Old Gate shook.

"A variable! Something is trying to come out of the Old Gate!"

The mage shouted.

Han Ah-young, Juan, and I tensed and stared at the Old Gate.

A person burst out of the Old Gate. He flailed in the air for a moment, then as if he'd used magic, began floating and slowly descended.

Immediately after, Old Gate-16 trembled and vanished without a trace.

"That's impossible...! The Old Gate was destroyed?!"

Whether the mage was shocked or not, I was staring at the falling man with a dumbfounded expression.

"Y-Yujin hyung? Why are you here?"

"That's my line. Park Su-ho. Why are you coming out of there?"

"Well... I was exploring ancient ruins and fighting a giant monster.... It died. Did you kill it, hyung?"

"You fought that monster alone? Were you always that strong?"

"Huh? That giant was big, but it wasn't that strong? If you're an S-rank hunter, hyung, you'd kill it in an instant."

“…….”

“…….”

Something about the conversation didn't line up.

There was a disconnect between the information we each knew.

Park Su-ho looked around for a moment, then his face went pale. Park Su-ho had been hiding his ability. I was the only one who knew about it. But as of today, his ability would become known to the World Hunter Association and America. There were too many eyes to hide it now.

"H-Hyung. If you could just help me a little...."

I shrugged.

"You won't die or end up as a lab rat."
