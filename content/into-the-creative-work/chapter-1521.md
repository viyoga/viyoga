The place the Rakshasa Woman was heading was the place Milyeong said she shouldn't go. The place Milyeong said felt like a dangerous thing was sealed.

Seeing the Rakshasa Woman moving forward without hesitation, it was clear that her destination was there.

"This attack. Does Cheon Yu-un know about it?"

I asked the Rakshasa Woman who was walking ahead.

"No. This is my solo act. Knowing his personality, there's no way he'd allow something like this."

"Is it okay to pull something like this? Cheon Yu-un's influence must still remain in Mookji-ryeon, right?"

"It's fine as long as I succeed. If I succeed, that bastard won't be able to say anything to me, right?"

Did we move too openly? A martial artist from the Jongnam Sect who was passing by saw us and rushed over. A blonde beauty and a masked man. Anyone could tell we looked suspicious.

"Who are you?! Stop right there...."

The Rakshasa Woman swung her twin swords. Two streams of sword energy shot out like rays of light towards the Jongnam Sect martial artist. One sliced the martial artist's neck, and the other sliced through his chest.

The Jongnam Sect martial artist's face contorted as it fell to the ground. It was proof that he couldn't react properly.

"Impressive. What kind of swordsmanship is that?"

"It's not particularly impressive swordsmanship."

She answered as if she was annoyed.

She was different from ordinary martial artists.

If you asked ordinary martial artists about their swordsmanship, they would explain their swordsmanship with great pride. Martial artists liked nothing more than having their martial arts praised.

We arrived at a dangerous place.

The Rakshasa Woman extended her twin swords forward in parallel.

Uuuuuung.

The two twin swords resonated. It didn't feel like internal energy was moving.

'A magical artifact, huh? Someone like the Head of Mookji-ryeon wouldn't have ordinary weapons. But what is she doing?'

I decided to watch for now.

Kiiiiiiing.

A silver wind rose from the two resonating swords. It was a small wind, but it wasn't ordinary. The fact that the wind was silver was unusual from the start.

The silver wind moved slowly.

Geulgeok. I swallowed dryly and tensed up without realizing it. I couldn't help it. The space where the peaceful and mysterious-looking silver wind passed was distorted like crumpled tissue paper.

The silver wind shattered the barrier. It was a truly crude yet elegant sight.

"What is that? Can you break a barrier with such a crude method?"

"It's nothing special. It's just a trick to shatter barriers. It's too slow to use for attacking."

The silver wind was indeed too slow. Slow enough to easily avoid without using even a fraction of a second. But its power was dangerous. The result was right before my eyes. The barrier was completely shattered, turned into rags, and disappeared. The building that the barrier was hiding was also revealed.

The building itself was ordinary.

"...The energy I feel from that building is not ordinary. It's a very foreign energy."

"Doesn't it feel good?"

"If I had to choose between good and bad... it's a bad feeling. I don't want to get close if I can help it."

"Hmm. Is that so? So you are a Demonic Cultist after all."

The Rakshasa Woman walked towards the front of the building. And she swung her twin swords without hesitation. Each time the swords were swung, sword winds rose and cut through the building. The building collapsed, and what was inside was revealed.

There was a white giant. Only its upper body remained, and it was 5m tall. The white giant was bound by chains and couldn't move. Looking closely, there were traces of cuts all over its flesh.

"What is this? It doesn't feel like a monster at all."

"It's a Celestial Soldier."

"A Celestial Soldier?"

"It's an artificial soldier from the Celestial Realm. It's a living being, but it has no ego. It's a living doll and a weapon."

"Why is it here?"

"It's a gift from the Celestial Realm. They threw a snack to a well-behaved mutt."

"A snack?"

"Look closely. Do you see the cuts all over its flesh? The Celestial Soldier's flesh itself is no different from a spiritual medicine. If humans eat it little by little, their bodies will become stronger, and their internal energy will gather. Their lifespan will also increase little by little, right? How good of a spiritual medicine would it be since it even regenerates little by little?"

In fact, I knew about the Celestial Soldier. It was a biological weapon that appeared in the later part of the original Bright Light Ascension story. Of course, it didn't appear often.

"It won't have much effect even if you eat it."

"I don't particularly intend to eat it. Did you come to steal this?"

"That's right. It's too good of an item for the Jongnam Sect to have. Since it's alive, it won't go into a spatial storage. You take care of this. I was going to see your skills, but it seems like it won't work out. I'm...."

I felt a huge energy. My and the Rakshasa Woman's heads snapped to the side. An old man flew down from the sky, his robe fluttering, and landed on the ground. The sword standing upright between his fluttering sleeves shone brightly.

"Because this old man will be your opponent."

An unusual aura emanated from the Rakshasa Woman.

"Who are you to cause this commotion?"

The old man spoke calmly. But his eyes were so fierce that they were almost ominous.

"Hyeil Jin-in. I didn't expect the former Head of the Jongnam Sect to step out. You must be quite surprised?"

The Rakshasa Woman said mockingly.

"You thieving bitch, do you know what you've done? You tried to steal Jongnam's treasure. Your crime deserves death."

Hyeil Jin-in swung his sword. From right to left. If someone who didn't know anything saw it, it would look like a light horizontal slash. But I felt a force as huge as Mount Tai from Hyeil Jin-in's sword.

'I have to avoid this.'

Before my legs could move, the Rakshasa Woman reacted first. She crossed her twin swords and blocked Hyeil Jin-in's Mount Tai-like sword strike.

"Get out of here with that!"

"Got it!"

I grabbed the Celestial Soldier's arm and ran away from the place. The Celestial Soldier's upper body was dragged along.

"Do you think I'll let you get away!"

Hyeil Jin-in shouted and stepped forward. At the same time, the Rakshasa Woman also stepped forward and followed Hyeil Jin-in. Hyeil Jin-in couldn't ignore the Rakshasa Woman's fierce twin swordsmanship.

Chaeng! Chaeng!

Their swords crossed. As a result, the surrounding air shook and spread in all directions.

'They're still just testing each other.'

These were absolute masters who had reached the realm of the Three Peaks. If they were serious, it wouldn't end with the surrounding area being devastated. It was best to get out of here before getting caught up in it.

'The Rakshasa Woman seems confident, so she should be fine. She seems to have hidden skills as well.'

I hesitated for a moment before unleashing my lightness skill.

Lightning Sky Style was too noticeable. I mainly used Lightning Sky Style while working as Joseon's Greatest Swordsman.

'If I use Lightning Sky Style, the Jongnam Sect can deduce my identity.'

So I had to use a different martial art. Since I was wearing a mask, there was a perfect martial art for it. I had decided to ride the same boat as the Rakshasa Woman, so there wouldn't be any big problems.

"......"

I quietly inhaled.

Kuoong.

The sound of my heart pounded in my head. At the same time, the dormant demonic energy began to stir and activate. The demonic energy raced through my meridians. I felt the power of my body becoming stronger.

I channeled the boiling demonic energy into my right foot and stepped on the ground.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Dominating space, folding space.

I felt my body stretching and shrinking like a rubber band before disappearing.

A warp that transcended space. A footwork technique that could be called Shukuchi. But it wasn't perfect.

'With my current level, I can only move about 10m at most.'

It consumed a tremendous amount of concentration and demonic energy. Unless it was urgent, it was better to just run.

"You bastard!! That ominous energy...! It's Cheonma Shingong! Is the Demonic Cult daring to attack the Jongnam Sect!!"

Hyeil Jin-in shouted from behind.

'He recognized Cheonma Shingong right away?'

I was briefly puzzled before realizing that all Cheonma Shingong techniques were similar.

'If he knew Cheonma Shingong properly, he would have distinguished it right away.'

He was Hyeil Jin-in of the Jongnam Sect. It was natural that he didn't know much since he wasn't a Demonic Cultist. It seemed like I had accidentally given Hyeil Jin-in a huge misunderstanding.

'Oh well, whatever.'

Since I couldn't have a conversation to clear up the misunderstanding, I focused on escaping.

"Martial artists of the Jongnam Sect! Kill him! We must never lose the Celestial Soldier! It will be the foundation of the Jongnam Sect for a thousand years!"

Hyeil Jin-in's voice echoed throughout the entire Jongnam Sect. And the voices of the Jongnam Sect martial artists could be heard from everywhere.

"Head of the family! Leave it to me! I, Byeok-o, will eliminate the intruder!"

A man jumped out.

He happened to be a martial artist near me. He shouted as if to show off and swung his sword.

"Take this, you evil villain! This is Jongnam's Hajeol Sword!"

"Playing around."

Cheonma Shingong, Dragon Fist.

I punched towards the incoming sword. The moment the sword and fist met, the Cheonma energy compressed in my fist burst out in a straight line. The sword couldn't withstand it and shattered. The Dragon Fist didn't stop. It pierced the martial artist's heart and continued to extend backward.

I didn't even check the body of the falling martial artist and sprinted forward.

Martial artists from the Jongnam Sect rushed to kill me. First-generation disciples, second-generation disciples, they all came running. They attacked while forming a formation, but it was just laughable in my eyes.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Sword.

The rushing martial artists fell like autumn leaves. But the enemies didn't back down. They focused on their formation and rushed at me all at once. The flow of air changed in their formation. I felt my range of movement narrowing.

'They know how to fight a master. All the free space has been suppressed.'

It slowly tightened like a net. If I got completely caught in this net, I would be like a trapped fish.

'I have to tear the net before that happens.'

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Gunrimbo Wave.

I stepped on the ground. The energy of Cheonma Gunrimbo became a wave and spread in all directions. The enemies who touched the wave lost their balance. The enemies vomited blood or had nosebleeds. It was internal damage caused by energy.

Cheonma Shingong, Cheonma Sword Rotation.

I rotated the demonic energy and put it into my sword. I spun around and swung my sword. A whirlwind rose and swept away the enemies. The enemies disappeared, becoming a handful of blood as if they had been ground in a mixer.

As I was about to move forward, someone else jumped out. It was a middle-aged man holding a sword. He had neatly combed back half-white hair and had no wrinkles on his face. It was in contrast to his long beard that came down to his chest.

"Half-white hair and a long beard. And a young face. Is he Oh Young-geom Song Jik, the Head of the Jongnam Sect?"

"Who are you?"

"I am Cheonma."

"That's ridiculous. I heard that the current Cheonma doesn't move from the Demonic Cult. If you are truly Cheonma, take off your mask and reveal your face."

"Whatever you say, I am Cheonma."

Cheonma Shingong, Black Flame Demonic Dragon.

Hwareureuk!

Black flames formed on the blade.
