I looked at the mana cube mounted on the back of the World Destroyer. The mana cube that had once glowed softly blue was now a gray cube, drained of light. I couldn't feel any mana from it either. But I could sense faint traces of mana moving around the cube's core.

'Is this the aftermath of pushing the mana cube to its absolute limit? I won't be able to use it for a while.'

What was astonishing was that despite displaying destructive power exceeding that of a tactical nuke, the World Destroyer showed no heat buildup.

'In other words, if I could secure multiple power sources like the mana cube, I could fire off rounds with the yield of nuclear bombs endlessly.'

It was a remarkable piece of hardware. The problem being that power sources like the mana cube didn't exist in this world.

I looked down at the ground below.

The shockwave from the explosion had been partially dampened thanks to the barrier those bastards had set up. The heroes who'd been monitoring the Villain Alliance had sustained almost no damage. They'd been keeping their distance from the start, after all.

The civilians who'd come to gawk without a shred of self-preservation, and the broadcast station helicopter, had been in some danger, but with so many heroes gathered, there were no casualties.

Ignoring their horrified reactions, I gazed at the factory district. The mushroom cloud still lingered, and smoke obscured my vision. But it couldn't block my Cheonan and Noean Geumjeong.

'99.9% were reduced to ash without even leaving bones behind. Which means 0.1% survived.'

The ambush had been perfect.

I'd fired the World Destroyer the moment I appeared—there was no way it could have been anything less than perfect.

'Jamal. That bastard fled right before I fired the World Destroyer.'

In the center of the ruined factory district, a torn-open space revealed a dungeon gate. One of the characteristics of dungeon gates was that they couldn't be destroyed by physical force alone.

'Did he have an ability to open and close dungeon gates? Or was it an artifact's power? It's not strange—there are items that can create dungeons if the conditions are met.'

How had Jamal noticed the World Destroyer and dodged it?

'Did his crisis detection—one of his suspected abilities—activate?'

There was no other explanation.

Of course, I knew that ability wasn't perfect. If it had truly been exceptional, he wouldn't have done something as stupid as turning me into an enemy.

'Even if he sensed the crisis and ran, I would have chased him to the ends of the earth and killed him. A big-name villain like Jamal is too valuable to let go.'

I descended to the ground.

If he'd gone into the dungeon, I'd just follow him in and kill him. Simple.

Heat still lingered in the factory district. The charred black earth glowed red like embers. The Stormbreaker armor protected me from the heat.

Without touching the ground, I entered the dungeon gate.

『You have entered the Special Dungeon: Primeval Jungle.』

『Collect 30 different primeval essences and place them in the lake.』

Unlike the outside, which had been night, it was broad daylight with the sun beating down.

It was a massive jungle where ordinary trees exceeded 20 meters in height.

Special dungeons gave good clear rewards. However, since there was no information about this dungeon at all, even I didn't know what the reward would be.

'Now that I'm actually trying to clear it, it's a pain in the ass. More than anything, if this takes too long, I don't know how the US government and Hero Association will react.'

Things were different before and after stepping into the restroom, so to speak. There was a chance the Hero Association, having confirmed the Villain Alliance's annihilation, would focus their attention on hunting me.

I flew up into the sky. I looked down at the ground. The primeval jungle was far larger than LA.

Doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo-doo.

I turned my head at the sound of something beating against the air. A massive dragonfly over 5 meters long was flapping its transparent wings, hurtling straight toward me.

-Eeek! A huge dragonfly! Gross!!

Machina recoiled in horror.

I summoned a tungsten round from my inventory. I punched the falling tungsten round.

Bzzzzzt!

Thunder Heaven Style—Railgun.

The tungsten round, accelerated to Mach 8, traced blue plasma as it pierced through the primeval dragonfly's head and body. From the corpse of the dead dragonfly, a fist-sized orb of light slowly fell.

'Is that an essence?'

Thanks to Noean Geumjeong, identifying it wasn't difficult. The essence of an essence was life force. As it was, it was too weak for a human to absorb.

'Different primeval essences means I need to hunt different monster species. I get the gist. The reward for this dungeon is probably, with high probability, a stat-enhancement elixir.'

It might not be, but dungeon rewards were usually related to the dungeon itself in most cases.

It wasn't a reward I particularly coveted.

I pulled out the World Destroyer. I mounted the Netherworld mana cube, glowing faintly green, onto the World Destroyer.

'Tracking Jamal is a hassle, so I'll just wipe out the entire dungeon.'

I was also curious what kind of power the Netherworld mana cube would display.

"Jeokgwang! Wait a moment! Negotiation! Let's negotiate!"

An urgent voice called out to me. About 300 meters away, Jamal stood atop a tree. He was drenched in cold sweat, staring at me.

"Jamal. Now you want to talk? Where did all that bravado go—calling me a cowardly, frightened assassin?"

"I didn't know you had that goddamn nuclear weapon. If I'd known your power from the start... I wouldn't have done something so stupid."

"Kukuku. It hasn't even been ten minutes since you mouthed off with that arrogant bullshit, but regret is always too late no matter how fast you feel it. Are you the only villain in this dungeon?"

"I'm the only one. When I sensed the crisis, there was no time to grab the others. Coming into this dungeon alone was the best I could do."

"What are your terms? Don't tell me you were just flapping your gums about negotiating."

"...The Villain Alliance is made up of high-grade villains gathered from across the United States. It's no exaggeration to say we rule America's night, its underworld."

"Yeah. Yeah. The same bastards who got wiped out without lasting a single second."

"...Not all of America's villains are gone. Low-level street criminals remain scattered everywhere. They'll gobble up the organizations whose bosses and executives have vanished. Then they'll be crushed by heroes when they can't handle it. Jeokgwang. Korea has a saying for this, doesn't it? Tosa-gupaeng. Once the prey is caught, the hunting dog is boiled and eaten. Why do you think the Hero Association hasn't actively tried to capture you? You were just being used by the Hero Association."

"Oh. That's quite the compelling argument. So?"

"Jeokgwang! Join me! Your strength and abilities! My mind and organizational management expertise! If we work together, we can dominate America's underworld!"

"You're a smooth talker. I have one question left."

"...What is it?"

"I didn't sense a single trace of a demon contractor or a demon in the Villain Alliance. Aside from you, that is. Out of thirty thousand people, why are you the only demon contractor?"

Noean Geumjeong detected the demonic energy hidden within his body.

『Demon Hunter (SSS) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

Even without Noean Geumjeong, the trait Demon Hunter (SSS) reacted.

The existence of demon contractors—demon-possessed humans—wasn't strange in itself. What was strange was that among the Villain Alliance, Jamal was the only one.

'In Korea, China, and Japan, demon contractors run rampant. But in America, which has even more villains, there are none? That doesn't make sense.'

"I thought I'd concealed my demonic energy perfectly... So the title Demon Hunter was real. I'll answer your question. Recently, the demons unilaterally broke their contracts with existing demon contractors and vanished. Absurdly, the backlash killed the vast majority of the contractors."

The demons broke their contracts and disappeared.

I'd assumed the demons were stockpiling power for some wasteful purpose. There was no other reason to break contracts.

A reason to stockpile power? Only one possibility came to mind.

'Just as I'm preparing, those demon bastards are preparing too. For the final battle.'

I deployed the Netherworld mana cube to its limit. Not blue light, but green light traced faintly along the World Destroyer's white surface.

"Jamal, this is truly the last question. If you were me, would you take your hand?"

"...No. If you'd pursued the path of a villain, you would have become one long ago. So negotiations have broken down after all. Nagdael!!"

Jamal shouted and drew up the demonic energy he'd been concealing.

The demon sealed within Jamal's body emerged. A demon in the form of a female statue with crow's wings. The demon was tightly bound by black chains connected to Jamal.

'He used his own body as a medium to seal the demon so he could use it. Befitting the boss of a massive criminal organization—his resourcefulness is impressive.'

『Demon Hunter (SSS) sees through the demon.』

『Nagdael. Demon Count. 2nd Legion.』

『Woodrest's authority is Wrath. Causes intense rage toward a specific target.』

Aaaaaaaaahhhhhhh!

The statue-like demon opened its mouth and screamed. The primeval insects nearby flapped their wings and surged upward in unison. The primeval insects, consumed by rage, charged at me to kill.

I couldn't pull the World Destroyer's trigger just yet. Its charge wasn't complete, and the distance was too close. At this range, I'd be caught in the blast too.

I shot upward into the sky with the jetpack.

"I don't know why you're raging at yourself... but the boss monster of this dungeon is swimming in the sky."

"I know."

I'd already grasped it with Noean Geumjeong without needing to be told.

Above the clouds, there existed a massive primeval moth with a wavelength invisible to the human eye. It too was affected by the demon's authority, feeling rage toward me as it descended. Far faster than the other insects.

-Aaaah! Something like that was up there?!

'Machina. Say hello. It's your friend Tinkerbell.'

-Shut uuuup!!!

Mid-surge into the sky, I stepped once on empty air.

Heaven Demon God Art—Heaven Demon Sovereign Step.

In this world, the Heaven Demon God Art wasn't my primary skill, and perhaps because the demon lore was different here, I couldn't subjugate demons with it. Still, using it as a surprise attack like now could shake them for a moment.

The demon's authority wavered.

The primeval insects chasing me fell into confused disarray, hesitating.

"What are you doing, Megdael!!"

Aaaaaaaaahhhhhhh!

At Jamal's urging, the demon screamed again and used its authority.

But it was too late.

By then, the charge was complete, and I'd used Lightning Leap to reach the maximum height the dungeon permitted.

"Farewell, Jamal. Thanks to you, I raked in plenty of karma."

I pulled the trigger.

A green circular round fell toward the ground. In the process, it passed by the giant moth, whose body rotted and plummeted to the earth.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

A thunderous boom rang out.

But no physical explosion actually occurred.

Instead, a massive green wave swept across the surroundings. Living things touched by the wave—plants and animals alike—rotted and died, then soon twitched and rose again.

Dead, yet not dead.

Undead.

Jamal and the demon were no exception.

『Karma: Good increases by 15.』

『Karma: Good increases by 28.』

『Successfully hunted a demon. The effects of Demon Hunter (SSS) are enhanced.』

『Hunted a demon. Partially restores health and mana.』

『Demon Hunter (SSS) absorbs a portion of the slain demon's authority.』

『Authority of Wrath

Rank: A

Temporarily causes the target to feel intense rage toward a specific target.』

-Aaaah! Those corpse insects are flying toward us! Weren't they supposed to listen to you?!

"Why would they listen to me? I'm not a necromancer."

In the 'God's Atlantis' world, the Tutankhamun series items required the Scales of Famine as a price, but there was the Book of the Dead. But this wasn't Atlantis, so the odds of the Book of the Dead activating properly were low.

'There's no time to test that.'

-The dungeon is collapsing fast!! At this rate, we won't even make it out and we'll die!

"Stop being so dramatic. We just need to leave before that."

I knew the dungeon gate's location, and it was close.

I reached the dungeon gate in 15 seconds. Since I had time to spare, I took a look around the dungeon. Even the plants had become undead—branches and leaf stems moved like tentacles, and small bug undead charged at me too. Though the small bugs couldn't even pierce my armor.

-What are you doing!! Hurry up and get out!!

I smirked at Machina's shouting and stepped through the dungeon gate.

Just before exiting, in the collapsing black space of the sky, I saw a scale perfectly tilted to one side.
