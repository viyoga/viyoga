Colman took on the tanking, and I aimed for openings to stab with my sword. Of course, the Baron didn't just take it like a punching bag. He fought back too, launching bone spikes and swinging his claws. But the most threatening thing was his grip. Once caught, you'd be crushed like my leg was.

In that sense, Kang Seok-su's magic was a great help. Kang Seok-su didn't try to directly harm the Baron, but instead created openings with his magic when Colman and I were in danger.

The battle lasted for more than 20 minutes. Colman and I were so exhausted we could barely speak.

,You insects! Dare to defy me! Brahma-sen-isiyeo! I will offer these bastards to you soon! Watch over me!"

The Baron charged again.

I got lucky.

The Baron, who had comfortably reigned over the city just because he was born a noble, didn't know how to fight properly.

,Goddess-isiyeo!"

Colman cried out to the Goddess and swung his holy mace. The mace crushed the Baron's thigh. The Baron collapsed to the floor.

,Now's my chance!'

I quickly ran up and plunged my sword into the back of the Baron's neck, unleashing lightning. Red lightning crackled from Stormbreaker, fused with Hwaryeon Bido.

The red lightning was effective. The Baron's body trembled like an aspen leaf.

,Kkeu, ah, aaaaaaah!"

Just because he became a monster doesn't mean he's immune to pain. No, maybe it's because he has reason that he feels pain.

I turned my eyes to the side.

The light of the Rainbow Fruit was fading like a candle in the wind. When that light completely died out, I would be able to kill the Baron.

,He's getting up!"

At Colman's words, I pulled out my sword and backed away. The Baron rose, letting out an irritated roar. It was deafening.

,I'll knock him down again."

Colman charged towards the Baron.

A mistake happened. Colman's leg faltered. It was because of Colman's depleted stamina. And the mistake was fatal. Colman was grabbed by the shoulder and leg by the Baron.

,Aaaaaaah, aaaaaaah!"

The Baron twisted and squeezed Colman's body like wringing out a rag. Colman's armor shattered, and red blood splattered on the floor.

,You filthy heretic!!!"

Thwack! Thwack! Thwack!

Colman swung his mace, hitting the Baron's head. The power of the holy mace was gradually weakening.

,Yujin! Kill this bastard! Hurry!"

,You've done well, Colman-nim!"

Confirming that all the light from the Rainbow Fruit had disappeared, I ran behind the Baron and stabbed him with my sword. Hard bone blocked the sword's path. I gritted my teeth and used all my mana to thrust the sword in. The sword reached his heart.

,Keoeok! Brahma-sen-isiyeo...!"

The Baron's body slumped forward. Colman was crushed under his heavy body. I pulled out my sword. Colman was already dead, so taking it out now was meaningless.

,This is the end of this dungeon."

My revenge on the Baroness was also over.

I approached Kang Seok-su. But there was no joy on Kang Seok-su's face. Rather, it was distorted. The face of a loser.

,Kang Seok-su. What's wrong?"

,The Rainbow Fruit is shining."

His words were true. The Rainbow Fruit, which had lost its light, was shining again. At the same time, the Baron's body twitched. The wounds I inflicted, the pierced heart, were regenerating.

,Damn it. What is this?!"

,The Rainbow Fruit. That's probably the source of that monster's power. You can think of it as an engine. It must be operating by consuming fuel like an engine."

,...Fuel?"

,It must be the people in this city. Otherwise, there would be no need to keep them locked up."

In short, everyone in this city was a sacrifice. The rampaging monsters kill people and offer them as sacrifices to the evil god.

,Since the strongest Inquisitor among us is dead, there's no hope. I'm washing my hands of this."

Kang Seok-su took out the Thread of Origin and poured mana into it. The thread burned.

,Seong Yujin. You should give up too. There's no answer. We failed the raid. It'll be a bit of a blemish on our careers... but it's a new monster and a new type of dungeon, so we can make excuses. Seong Yujin?"

,...Go ahead. I'm going to kill that bastard and then leave."

,Why go so far? This is a dungeon. Even if we fail, a dungeon break won't happen. We can just try raiding the dungeon again later."

,I'm the type who doesn't let anyone who messes with my woman get away with it."

,...Woman?"

Kang Seok-su gave me a look of incomprehension. Soon, he disappeared.

,Kkeureureureu...."

The Baron got up. I summoned all the bombs I could summon and threw them at him, then ran away.

Bang! Kwakwakwakwang! Bang!

I transformed Stormbreaker into a shield, partially blocking the explosion. My back was a little hot, but it would take some time for the Baron to recover again.

,If Kang Seok-su is right... wouldn't the light of the Rainbow Fruit weaken if I killed all the people in the city?'

I'd kill them all before they could be offered to the evil god Brahma-sen or whatever.

,If I used a nuclear bomb....'

I could wipe them out all at once.

,But the sacrifices are already somewhat gathered. Can I handle that bastard after using a nuclear bomb?'

Colman was dead, and Kang Seok-su, who would have covered me, was gone. It was impossible for me to handle the Baron alone. Cheonsim is on cooldown, and even if I surprise him with complete recovery, what if he regenerates again with the power of the Rainbow Fruit?

,There's a way....'

A few things come to mind. One of them is Absolute Authority. If I use that one-time thing, I can get rid of the Baron. But it's too precious to use on the likes of the Baron.

,Summoning Elena is also a way. With Elena's power, I could wipe him out.'

The summoned Elena has Limit Break. If I use her original power for 1 minute with Limit Break, the Baron wouldn't be able to handle it.

,Summon Elena... no. There's Random Summon, right? If I hit the jackpot with Random Summon, there's no need to summon Elena.'

Even in this situation, I was itching for Random Summon. As urgent as the situation is, I want to test my luck.

,If I summon Elena and use her for only 1 minute and then she's reverse summoned, I won't be able to have sex in reality.'

Even if that wasn't the case, if I summoned her inside the dungeon, she might not be able to go outside with me when I succeed in raiding the dungeon. Because she didn't enter the dungeon in a normal way.

,It's meaningless!! You will die according to His will!"

The Baron's angry voice echoed from behind. I stopped running and frowned.

,Let's go with Random Summon.'

I made up my mind. I couldn't use Random Summon because of the cooldown, but I had a way to reset the cooldown.

[Skill Cooldown Reset Ticket

Resets the cooldown of a skill.

Only 1 stack will be recharged for consumed stacks.]

[Using Skill Cooldown Reset Ticket.]

[The cooldown of the Random Summon skill has been reset.]

,I have 1,150 points. That's enough to do a Random Summon.'

[Starting Random Summon, consuming 500 points.]

[Rebre from the Warmage world has been summoned.]

[The Warmage Amusement World is deactivated.]

[The summoning duration is 15 days.]

,Warmage? That world is a sci-fi fantasy game world.'

A bug appeared in front of me. A light pink caterpillar the size of a human head. It floats in the air. Its 20 legs wiggle simultaneously. The single fist-sized golden eye in its head stares intently at me.

Looking at Rebre's appearance, I remembered its identity.

-Did you, call me?

A murky voice is heard in my head. It's not telepathy. This is telepathy.

,A fucking dangerous guy has been summoned!'

Rebre is a villain called the Parasite Lord in the [Warmage] world. Its origin is a mutant born on a polluted planet. It can use telekinesis, telepathy, and teleportation, and divides its body to parasitize living things. It parasitizes not only humans and monsters, but also robots and androids.

Rebre is a natural disaster. Because it travels around planets and continues to reproduce, that is, parasitize. The planet where Rebre appears is doomed in itself.

,Are you going to parasitize me?"

-If you allow, I want to, parasitize.

,...If I allow? Since when have you been asking for permission to parasitize?"

-You are, different from, ordinary humans, I, know, your abilities. I want to, form a friendly relationship.

,Well, my abilities are pretty awesome."

I remember when Milyeong was first summoned to me. Milyeong also knew something about me.

-I, want to, reproduce, in more worlds, more planets.

,Reproduction, huh. I understand. I like sex too."

-I, can't have sex. No genitals.

,I know. You dickless bug bastard."

-.......

Kiiiiiiiiiiit!

A sound of madness was heard. Four monsters appeared around me. The monsters charged fiercely at me.

,Hey, do something about those bastards!"

-Can I, parasitize?

,Yes! You can parasitize everything here except me!"

-Good.

Small, thumb-sized bugs appeared from Rebre's back. It was Rebre's ability, division. The four small bugs flew towards the monsters. The monsters didn't react to the bugs. They didn't seem to feel the danger itself.

The parasitic bugs burrowed into the host's body. Even bone spikes with the strength to block Sword energy couldn't stop the parasitic bugs.

-Parasitism, good heart.

The parasitism ended in an instant. The monsters that were rushing at me stopped abruptly. Small tentacles popped out of the monsters' mouths, eyes, and ears, wriggling.

,Hey. You have to kill a really strong guy. So reproduce as quickly as possible."

-Okay. Many, hosts, nearby.

The parasitized monsters moved. Rebre and I chased after the parasitic monsters. The parasitic monsters reproduced like zombies. They reproduced by biting humans or monsters with their mouths. It didn't take long to reproduce. There were individual differences, but reproduction was completed in 30 seconds at most.

,The parasitism speed of humans is slower than that of monsters. Is it because humans are smarter than monsters?'

The parasitized monsters and humans scattered in all directions, continuing to parasitize.

-Reproduction, very good.

,You bastard! What are you doing to my sacrifices! Brahma-sen is angry!"

Thump, thump thump.

The Baron's voice echoed from afar. The thumping was the sound of him approaching this way. His figure was visible. His face was distorted with anger.

-What, is that?

,The guy who killed my woman."

-Weak. But, I am, weaker.

Rebre immediately judged the difference in strength. I don't know what Rebre's original stats are, but now those stats were adjusted to me. But Rebre's strength is not the strength of the body. The parasitic ability, like a plague, is Rebre's real strength.

,Kill him."

-Revenge, should be, done, with your own hands, shouldn't it?

,I'm the one who summoned you."

-Okay.

Rebre used telepathy. A horde of zombies swarmed from all directions. The parasitized monsters and humans rushed at the Baron.

,No matter how many of you there are, you can't beat me!"

The Baron was not easy to deal with. He crushed monsters with his hands and threw humans. But the parasitized humans moved even when their heads were broken or their arms were torn off. Because the parasitic bug somewhere in their body is the main body.

I watched the situation with a smile.

The Baron was dominant at first. He trampled monsters and humans with his tremendous power. But as time passed, the Baron's overwhelming divine power could no longer be seen.

If the Baron defeated 3 enemies, more than 10 enemies would appear and cling to him. Those 10 would soon become 30, and 30 would become 100.

It didn't take long for thousands of them to cling to the Baron.

,Aaaaaaaah! Brahma-sen-isiyeo!! Power! Give me power!!! Aaaaaaah!!"

The Baron was eaten alive by the sacrifices he had offered to the evil god. The regenerative power that the Rainbow Fruit gave him became poison. Because he was eaten alive for almost an hour.

-City, reproduction, complete.

,Aren't you going to parasitize that?"

-For, your, revenge, I deliberately, didn't parasitize.

,You can if you want to?"

-A little, difficult, but, possible.

,Okay. Don't do it. I have to kill that bastard. And thanks for your hard work."

I walked to the garden of the castle where the Rainbow Fruit was. Rebre quietly followed behind me.

Feeling complicated as I looked at the Baroness's corpse, I obtained the Rainbow Fruit, which had lost its light.

The Rainbow Fruit disappeared from my hand.
