I stood at the head of the group.

I looked around at my team members. No one seemed dissatisfied. Rather, they looked at me with eyes full of trust, almost to a burdensome degree.

,They really trust me completely in this kind of thing, huh.'

Well, there's no one better than me in the field of combat.

Even the smart Yu Jin-myung listens to my opinions and trusts me completely when it comes to combat.

To be honest, it would be a lie to say I don't feel pressured. I didn't professionally learn combat, and I didn't serve in the military either. I just fight instinctively, without thinking.

,But I've never failed.'

I didn't think I would fail today either.

I checked the map with Bae Hyun-soo's guidance. I roughly grasped the enemies' positions.

,It's just as dark for us as it is for those bastards.'

Those bastards had turned off the lights too. Probably to hide their positions. Instead, or so it seemed, they had created cover based on the terrain.

,Looking at the map, I guess I should assume it's roughly like a trench.'

They must have guns. With Peng Chun-soo's power, he could get a few guns. He probably armed his most important subordinates here with guns. There's no way they would have created this kind of terrain otherwise.

,I should assume they have night vision goggles.'

Night vision goggles are easy to get. You can find them with an internet search. Judging by the fact that they didn't spot us approaching from the sea, the performance probably isn't very good, though.

,Or maybe there aren't many people.'

Peng Chun-soo took his subordinates off the island to fight Ji Byung-soo. The subordinates remaining on this island are likely those he can trust rather than those who are strong. After all, the piggy bank is a more important resource than money during the Devil's Game.

,Even though the island is small, it's not completely open. There are enough rocks to provide cover.'

I decided to go around. I seemed to have a rough idea of where and how to move.

I moved with a dagger in my right hand. I would use the gun only when absolutely necessary.

I moved stealthily. Bae Hyun-soo and Jo Na-young seemed particularly nervous. I left them alone since they weren't getting in the way.

I moved forward. I saw an enemy. As expected, he was wearing night vision goggles. However, his attitude showed that his guard was completely down. He was yawning widely and staring blankly ahead. His military discipline was completely gone.

,I guess that's natural since they're just gangsters, not soldiers.'

I quietly approached and, in the same motion as covering his mouth with one hand, I shoved the dagger into his neck.

I killed one person in an instant.

Have I already become accustomed to killing? I don't feel any particular emotion. Rather, I felt more annoyed.

,...Annoyed. Have I really become a murderer?'

No. A murderer wouldn't feel annoyed when killing someone. Killing people is fun for them, so that's why they're called murderers.

I carefully lowered the corpse to the ground. I habitually checked on my team members. No one was surprised to see the corpse. Shin Do-hyun stored the pistol the bastard had in her subspace. I took the night vision goggles and put them on.

I continued to move.

,Ah, shit. How long do I have to be stuck on this island? I'm doing guard duty again after being discharged from the army."

,Hey, hey. Still, it's better than going outside. The boss and Ji Byung-soo are at war right now."

,Fuck. I guess it's better than going out to fight. The boss will win the war, right."

,You should know your place. How can that Ji Byung-soo bastard compare to the boss? Wanna bet? I'll bet 1 million won that Ji Byung-soo kicks the bucket."

,I'll bet 2 million won that Ji Byung-soo kicks the bucket."

,That's not a fair bet."

They were standing in front of a 1-meter-tall stone wall, chuckling. They weren't even using night vision goggles.

,I heard the boss has the ability to read people's minds. Is that true?"

,It's true. I saw the boss interrogate one of the piggy banks last time. That idiot. He died after lying to the boss."

,Didn't a few bastards die last time too? Why are those idiots lying to the boss?"

,Because they're idiots. Most of them are gambling addicts or drug addicts. Don't do gambling or drugs. Your life will be fucked for real."

Two people.

This was a bit annoying. No matter how good I am, I can't kill two people at the same time with just one dagger. I wish one of them would go somewhere... but they didn't seem to be showing any signs of doing so.

,I'll have to kill both of them at the same time. Yu Jin-myung... no, Shin Do-hyun would be better.'

Yu Jin-myung is smart, quick-witted, and competent, but I don't know about her combat abilities. On the other hand, Shin Do-hyun exercises and practices shooting with me every day. I've been teaching her this and that according to my instincts, and she's learning well. She had talent.

,Talent for sex too. Of course, I'm better.'

I put aside my distracting thoughts and signaled to Shin Do-hyun with a hand gesture. It wasn't a pre-arranged signal, but that's just how Shin Do-hyun and I are. She immediately took out her dagger, her hand trembling slightly.

We quietly approached the bastards. And at the same time, we covered their mouths with one hand and shoved the daggers into their necks. It was perfect. We lowered the two people's corpses to the ground.

Shin Do-hyun was trembling. I remembered that it was her first time killing someone with a knife.

,Do-hyun trembling... hot.'

I'd probably get hit if I said that. Knowing Shin Do-hyun well, I decided to quietly shut my mouth. Instead, I raised my hand as if asking for a high-five. It was a hand covered in blood, but... that was the same for Shin Do-hyun.

,......"

Shin Do-hyun quietly put her hand on mine. Her trembling stopped. Instead, her breathing became a little rougher. She seemed excited.

,I guess I'll get ambushed when we get back.'

Of course, I have no intention of refusing. Sex is the perfect act to forget bad memories.

I took the lead and moved again. How many more people did I kill? I was getting closer to my destination.

,Damn it. These bastards aren't answering the radio?"

,...Did something happen?"

,What should we do? Why don't you go check it out."

,Go alone? Are you crazy? The boss told us not to move alone."

Four people were trembling with anxiety. They had already noticed our presence. Judging by the way they were looking around and aiming their guns, it was impossible to assassinate them quietly.

I threw away the dagger and held automatic pistols in each hand.

,Dual pistols are cool.'

As soon as I made up my mind, I took action.

I suddenly stood up from behind the cover.

,Huh?!"

One of the four saw me and was surprised, trying to pull the trigger. Unfortunately, I was faster.

Tang, tang, tang, tang!

Exactly four shots. All four shots hit the bastards' heads.

「You have acquired 27 game points.」

,As expected of Yu-jin-ssi. Your skills are amazing."

Yu Jin-myung looked at me as if he was newly impressed. The other team members were just as surprised. I ended my reaction with a shrug.

,Now that the gunshots have rung out, will the remaining bastards on the island come?'

I stared at Bae Hyun-soo's map. This map only shows a 100m radius. In other words, I would know if bastards outside of 100m were approaching.

,Two on the right. Three on the left, huh.'

Should I have a shootout here? No. Is there really a need to do that? It's not like there are a lot of them.

I decided to have my team members take cover and move alone.

,In the end, the one who shoots and hits first wins with a gun. I have the talent of being a perfect shot and super concentration.'

Besides, I know the bastards' locations thanks to the map. I don't know how to read a normal map, but this map is like a game mini-map, so it's easy to understand.

Tang, tang, tang!

Three shots as soon as I saw the enemy. Even though I was shooting while running, I got consecutive headshots. I turned around, admiring my own insane shooting talent.

I easily dealt with the remaining two. I looked at the map, but I didn't see any other enemies. Or maybe they were afraid of us and hiding in the corner of the island. Anyway, I got additional game points, so some of these bastards must have been game participants.

Anyway, now that things were settled, I went inside the building and met the piggy banks.

They were imprisoned in shackles and handcuffs inside the prison. There were signs that they had been periodically abused. Most of them had lost their minds.

,Judging by this state, even if they survive the Devil's Game, the situation won't automatically change much.'

The Devil's power doesn't reach the participants. No, it doesn't reach anyone who knows about the Devil's Game, even if they're not participants. That was also the reason why Peng Chun-soo had been alive all this time.

,Shit. I could have ended up like this if I had made a mistake. Thank God. Really.'

I almost got completely fucked.

Now it was time to crack open Peng Chun-soo's piggy banks. I raised my pistol.

,P-Please save me."

,Please save us... I don't want to die here...!"

,If you save me, I'll do anything!"

Everyone except me hesitated. Even though they had come to crack open the piggy banks, they felt a sense of revulsion when they actually tried to do it. Even Yu Jin-myung seemed hesitant.

Tang!

I cracked open one of the piggy banks inside the prison.

「You have acquired 13 game points.」

Everyone's eyes turned to me.

,Don't forget why we came here."

,...Yes. Yu-jin-ssi is right."

Yu Jin-myung raised his pistol and killed the bastard in the prison. Starting with him, Shin Do-hyun, Bae Hyun-soo, and Jo Na-young raised their pistols.

First, they decided to kill one person each, and then crack open the piggy banks according to the game points they had.

I had quite a few points, so I took a step back.

Tang, tang, tang, tang, tang.

The gunshots continued to ring out.

We acquired a huge amount of game points and returned to the ship.

It was quiet on the way back, even though there were no enemies. We had just massacred defenseless humans.

To be honest, I didn't think it was that big of a deal, but I had a sense of propriety, so I quietly shut my mouth.

,Why do they feel guilty when they had already decided to kill them?'

I grumbled inwardly.

I got on the ship. Yu Jin-myung drove the ship. Bae Hyun-soo chattered beside Yu Jin-myung. Jo Na-young sat on the deck, staring blankly at the sea.

I went inside the ship with Shin Do-hyun. Shin Do-hyun sat next to me. She quietly leaned her head on my shoulder. I grabbed her waist and quietly stroked it.

After being like that for a while, Shin Do-hyun and I made eye contact. Shin Do-hyun stood up and sat on my lap. She kissed my lips. Soon, we fell into ecstasy.

I saw Jo Na-young secretly peeking at us from the entrance.
