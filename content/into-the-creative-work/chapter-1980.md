Somewhere far removed from all worlds.

There, gods including Zeus gathered.

Not only the twelve Olympians, but even Hades, the master of the underworld.

Zeus was filled with emotion as he looked at the assembled gods.

The world had been turned upside down several times in the meantime. The relationships of the gods, who used to meet and talk often, had gradually thinned out over the eternally long and boring time. It had been a long time since they had gathered like this. So long that he couldn't even remember how much time had passed.

They didn't ask after each other's well-being.

The recent lives of the gods were all pretty much the same. The only things they could intervene in were specific human worlds and Atlantis. They were tired of the human world, so most of them were focused on Atlantis. And the reason they had gathered today was also related to Atlantis.

Zeus stroked his beard and looked at the empty air.

There was Seong Yujin-ssi, his contractor. Seong Yujin-ssi heading towards Mount Olympus in Atlantis.

,He's come this far.'

He remembered when he first made a contract with that human.

He was drawn to something when he saw that human. So he took a loss and made a contract. That choice was right. Now, watching Seong Yujin-ssi's actions was the most fun. Because Seong Yujin-ssi was different from other humans.

,It's been a while since we've gathered like this. And to think we'd gather like this because of one human. How long has it been? Is it the first time since the Trojan War?"

Zeus spoke first. Of course, they had gathered a few times since the Trojan War. It's just that the topic wasn't about humans. This was the first time they had gathered on the subject of pure humans.

,Useless words.... Let's get to the point quickly."

Hera, Zeus's ex-wife, spoke sharply. Zeus was startled. Zeus found it difficult to deal with his divorced ex-wife.

,It's too early for that human to enter."

Zeus's son, Ares, said. Ares didn't like Seong Yujin-ssi very much. Because of Aphrodite.

,Do we have a reason to intervene? He entered Olympus with his own power. It's not something we should gather and argue about. I'm here to make sure you guys don't interfere."

Athena said. She was relatively friendly to Seong Yujin-ssi.

,That crazy human might degrade the authority of Olympus! He must be punished!"

Apollo shouted, glaring at Seong Yujin-ssi.

,He is the hero of heroes. A hero who even killed a god. He will surpass Heracles someday."

Artemis said as if prophesying. Honey dripped from her eyes as she looked at Seong Yujin-ssi. Apollo was even more furious at that.

,Haha. He's an interesting guy. It'll be fun to watch."

Poseidon had no thoughts.

,There are many living who die by him, and many who will die by him. The order of the underworld is in danger.... So taking his life here might be one way. I'll take the penalty."

Hades said. The dark circles under his eyes were strong. He was a god, but he was exhausted. You could tell how much work he was doing.

,Why are you butting in when you're not one of the twelve gods?"

Demeter nagged Hades. She had a similar vibe to Hera. Hades shut his mouth in front of his mother-in-law. From the moment Hades married Persephone, Demeter hated Hades. She almost hated him.

,It's about Atlantis anyway. Let him do what he wants."

Aphrodite said. She watched the mortal with interested eyes.

The gods each gave their opinions.

Some were friendly to him, some were hostile, and some remained neutral.

Zeus raised his hand at the appropriate time. The gods shut their mouths and paid attention to Zeus. Although his authority had declined somewhat, Zeus was Zeus.

,I'm going to give him a trial."

Zeus grinned.

The gods were shocked when they heard the trial he had in mind.

,Why are you going so far? Is that human your hidden son or something?"

Hera asked.

,That's definitely not it."

Zeus, who made it clear before Hera made a strange misunderstanding, continued to explain the reason.

,The only reason I'm giving him this trial is because it seems fun."

The other gods nodded.

Fun.

Yes. The gods want fun.

,Your consent is needed for this trial. What do you say? Will you agree?"

,I have a condition. If that human fails the trial, you also have to step down from this Atlantis."

Hera said boldly. Her eyes glaring at Seong Yujin-ssi were very sharp.

,Oh! Very good! Very interesting!"

Apollo liked it. He desperately wanted Seong Yujin-ssi to fail the trial. He couldn't intervene because he was watching Zeus's mood. What if Zeus stepped down at this time? He would be able to mess with Seong Yujin-ssi a bit.

,This trial is not easy. That human could die. It's good for the world if that human dies. I agree."

Zeus looked at Artemis. Surprisingly, she didn't refuse. When Zeus asked her why, Artemis said as if it were natural.

,I don't think he will succumb to such a trial."

,He? Oh, my sister! That human is just a human!"

,Shut up. I don't want to hear that from you. You're just a gay who likes young men."

,N, no. Sister...!"

Anyway, it was decided to proceed with the trial.

The gods who hated him and the gods who liked him were interested. Even from the gods' point of view, opportunities to enjoy such sights were rare.

Now all that's left is negotiation with the system. No problem. It's not a bad proposal for the system either. In reality, Seong Yujin-ssi's opinion, who is the person in charge of the trial, was more important than the system's opinion. If Seong Yujin-ssi accepts the trial, all the system does is adjust the balance.

,System. We have decided to give him a trial."

「There must be a reward at the end of the trial.」

,Of course."

Zeus nodded readily. A hero who overcomes a trial must be rewarded. It's something that all the gods acknowledge.

,And there needs to be a price for failing the trial."

Hera said. She glared at Zeus as if she didn't like something. Zeus tried to ignore her gaze as calmly as possible. His crotch ached for no reason.

,If he fails the trial, he will have to get rid of that hideous constellation."

Hideous constellation. Everyone knew which constellation she was talking about. The goddesses all nodded. Artemis was no exception. Even so, that constellation was a bit much.

「I will convey it to him.」

• • •

I was in a large common area. There were twelve gods sitting high above me. Twelve majestic beings led by Zeus. They were frozen as they looked down at me.

,What are they doing?'

If you call someone, shouldn't you say something?

But instead of opening their mouths, they were stiff and unmoving like dolls. Judging by the feeling, it seemed like they had really become dolls.

,Even if they're not real gods, but majestic beings, can this happen?'

I couldn't see the faces of the twelve gods properly. Because the gods didn't allow it. I thought about using the Heavenly Eye to force myself to see, but I stopped. There weren't just one or two gods, but twelve. If I acted recklessly, I could turn all twelve gods into enemies.

How many more minutes passed?

Zeus opened his mouth.

,Human. I know what purpose you came to Olympus for. We have decided to give you a trial to see if you are qualified."

Trial.

My ears perked up.

In this world, a trial was a great opportunity. It was a quest with a huge reward.

Of course, the difficulty is enormous enough to be called a trial. It's on a completely different level from the missions given by the God Seats.

,One of the ways to surpass SSS-rank.'

I felt excited.

,May I ask what kind of trial it is?"

,I can't tell you. That is also your trial."

「Olympus Trial

The gods of Olympus have decided to give you a trial.

When you overcome the trial.

-Acquire Astrape (EX).

-You can use Hephaestus's forge.

-Acquire control of one of the Olympus areas.

When you succumb to the trial.

-Face an unavoidable death.

-The constellation disappears.」

I read the contents of the trial. What caught my eye was the reward. Astrape (EX). It's definitely talking about the skill Astrape. I can't believe Astrape will transcend its limits.

It was very exciting and worrying at the same time. Because I didn't know the contents of the trial at all.

,It's not an unfair trial, is it?'

In the worst case, they could give me a trial not for the purpose of the trial, but for the purpose of killing me.

,That possibility... is small. The system won't be negligent. Looking at the rewards, it seems like Zeus's intentions are also involved.'

「Will you accept the trial?」

,Will you accept the trial?"

The voices of the system and the fake Zeus overlapped. I nodded confidently.

,I will accept it."

,Good, human. I will watch to see if you are qualified."

Bang!

Lightning flashed before my eyes.

There was no pain, but I felt my vision gradually darkening.

I lost consciousness for a moment and regained consciousness. As I got up, I noticed the change.

Something is wrong.

It feels like my body is not my own. From the breathing to the visible field of vision, to the detailed sensations of the body, everything has changed. It's not bad. Rather, the senses have improved.

I looked down at my hand.

It's certain.

This is not my hand. It's a little bigger than my hand.

,Could it be. Did I possess someone else's body?'

I looked around. I can see a forest and a flowing river. I ran towards the river to check my appearance.

I checked my face and was shocked. It was much younger than I knew, but this face was definitely his face.

,Zeus?!'

I became Zeus.
