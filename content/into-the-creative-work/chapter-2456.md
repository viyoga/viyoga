The first floor of the Seventier Guild headquarters was bustling with visitors from outside.

The broadcasting station crew, with their cameras and broadcasting equipment, were cautiously observing their surroundings.

,It seems Seventier's situation is worse than I thought...',

,I wouldn't mind the commoners, but I didn't expect Hunters from other top 10 guilds to show up. Did our schedule leak?',

,The PD-nim... actually seems to like it. Don't tell me he's going to push through with the filming like this?',

The commoners held up their protest signs even more passionately. The signs demanded that the guild hire commoners. Despite being allowed inside the headquarters thanks to Seventier's consideration, they showed no gratitude towards Seventier.

,If Seventier helps me, I can awaken too.',

,Even if it's a bit of a nuisance, I can repay them after I awaken.',

,A famous guild can help us. And the broadcasting station is here too. I should do well if they ask for an interview.',

The Hunters from the top 10 guilds were quiet. They were A-rank Hunters. They didn't come to the Seventier Guild with good intentions. Their goal was to embarrass the Seventier Guild during the broadcast.

If the Seventier Guild kicked them out? They would obediently retreat then. However, after that, rumors would spread that the Seventier Guild was cowardly. In the Hunter world, where strength is everything, being labeled a coward would lead to various disadvantages. At the very least, Seventier's growth would be stunted.

,I heard Seong Yujin is appearing on the broadcast today.',

,I'll challenge Seong Yujin to a duel.',

They weren't just here because of the guild's orders. Even top 10 guilds couldn't recklessly use their A-rank Hunters. They were here not because of the guild's coercion.

,Seong Yujin has low experience. Even if the Association recognized him, he only recently became an A-rank Hunter. It hasn't been long since he became an S-rank candidate.',

,I don't deny Seong Yujin's growth and potential are great. But an S-rank candidate who just became A-rank? I heard he's friends with Han Ah-young. He probably became an S-rank candidate with Han Ah-young's help.',

,If I defeat Seong Yujin... I might become an S-rank candidate too. Even if not, just the fact that I beat an S-rank candidate will solidify my career. There will be criticism from people, but I can just say the guild ordered me to do it.',

Everyone had already finished their calculations.

Soon, Kang Seok-su, the representative of Seventier, came out. Kang Seok-su looked around the crowded first floor and barely suppressed a sigh. The first place he headed was towards the broadcasting station. The broadcasting station, with whom he had made an appointment in advance, wasn't an uninvited guest.

Kang Seok-su approached Yoon Dae-shik PD, who had a shaggy beard.

,Yoon PD-nim.',

,Ah, Representative Kang. I'm sorry about this. It seems information leaked from our side. What should we do about today's broadcast...? If Representative Kang wants to cancel, we will.',

Contrary to his words, Yoon Dae-shik PD's eyes were shining. As a former C-rank Hunter, he welcomed the current situation. It would be more popular than a normal guild promotional video, and the viewership ratings would be good too.

,The information leak... is unavoidable. Even if we tried to hide it, it would have leaked somehow. And we can't postpone the broadcast schedule because of something like this. I'll talk to the Hunters from the other guilds. However, we might have to change the filming schedule.',

,Haha. Don't worry about that. We can use the entire day today!',

A deep smile spread across Yoon Dae-shik PD's face.

Kang Seok-su walked towards the Hunters. They were slightly frowning, seemingly displeased that he didn't come to them first.

,What brings you all here, and where are you from?',

,Don't you know us?',

,Haha. Even if the Korean Hunter world is small, I don't know everyone in the industry.',

,...Park Seo-un from the Sambak Guild.',

,Kim Ji-oh from Bluebird.',

,Kang Dong-woo from Ihwa.',

Sambak, Bluebird, Ihwa.

The 7th, 9th, and 10th ranked guilds. Of course, the rankings are unofficial. But strangely, people accept them as official.

,Guilds with weak presence even among the top 10 guilds.',

They were also guilds that were very nervous about Seventier's emergence. They could lose their status as a top 10 guild if they made a mistake.

,The difference between being in the top 10 guilds and not is quite significant.',

The Hunters stated their demands directly, as if their time was precious.

,We want to have an exchange with the Seventier Guild. In the form of a duel. Since this is the guild headquarters, you must have a training ground. What do you say?',

,...So suddenly?',

,Well, if you don't want to, there's nothing we can do.',

If he turned them away here, rumors would spread that Seventier was a coward who avoided duels. Behind Kang Seok-su, Yoon Dae-shik PD was looking at them with sparkling eyes.

,Hmm. Alright. Do you have a Hunter in mind as your opponent?',

,...Oh. I heard the representative of Seventier is magnanimous... It's as I heard. I want to duel with Hunter Seong Yujin.',

,I also came to see Hunter Seong Yujin.',

,Me too.',

The Hunters frowned and glared at each other.

No matter what, three people couldn't fight Seong Yujin one at a time.

,I came and waited before you two. I think I have the opportunity.',

,What's the big deal about coming first when you didn't even make an appointment with Representative Kang in advance? The Sambak Guild is higher in rank, so I, as a member of the Sambak Guild, should go first.',

,Rank? That's unofficial. That rank, isn't it determined by commoners who don't even know the Hunter world? Our Ihwa Guild is not just 10th.',

Kang Seok-su chuckled as he listened to their conversation. Even the top 10 guilds, when you look closely, consider each other rivals.

,The relationship between the top 10 guilds is not solid. If one of the top 10 guilds suffers a fatal blow, they will not hesitate to tear it apart.',

As Kang Seok-su, who wanted the collapse of the top 10 guilds, it was a really fortunate thing.

,Everyone. Calm down. Since everyone wants a duel with Seong Yujin... Shouldn't the choice be given to the person in question? Seong Yujin. What do you think?',

Seong Yujin walked out from behind.

Seong Yujin, who was decked out in luxury brands from head to toe, including a luxury suit, luxury watch, and luxury shoes, looked around at the crowd.

Hunters and commoners alike were momentarily overwhelmed. It wasn't just because he was wearing luxury clothes. They felt something indescribable about Seong Yujin.

The Hunters, in particular, reacted even more strongly.

,This... it feels like I'm looking at our guild master.',

,So there's a reason why he's an S-rank candidate.',

Seong Yujin glanced at the Hunters and smirked. A sneer that everyone could recognize.

,You can all go in order. It seems the result is already determined.',

The Hunters, who had been overwhelmed for a moment, were dumbfounded. What did they just hear?

,...Hunter Seong Yujin. Can you handle that statement?',

,Handle? That's what you challengers have to do, not me. Don't you even know that?',

,You're acting arrogant as if you're an S-rank Hunter. No, even S-rank Hunters wouldn't be as arrogant as you.',

,Did I seem arrogant? Well, there's a difference in level, so it can't be helped.',

The Hunters' foreheads were bulging with veins.

,I heard Seong Yujin has a good personality... but he's actually very arrogant. I guess you can't trust rumors.',

,That cocky bastard...',

,I was going to go easy on him, but I guess I can't.',

The Hunters' eyes blazed fiercely.

• • •

,There were people from the broadcasting station too. Did you really have to come out like that?',

Kang Seok-su started his nagging. The guy who wears a coat even in the middle of summer was surprisingly talkative.

,I knew the cameras weren't rolling.',

,Still, rumors will spread. That Seong Yujin is an asshole.',

,I don't care. I don't plan on becoming a friendly Hunter.',

,...You're confident about the duel, right?',

,Looking at them, they're all mediocre. I can win even if all three of them attack me at the same time.',

The reason why I didn't provoke them more was because I didn't want to show my full strength. There was no need to hide my strength, but the very fact that someone knew my strength was unpleasant.

,If you lose, you're screwed. Not only Seventier's reputation, but... your reputation will plummet to the ground.',

,I won't lose.',

Among the Hunter ranks, the rank with the biggest difference in individual skill is A-rank. Up to A-rank, you can become one if you meet certain skills and achievements, but it's not easy to move on to S-rank.

A Hunter who couldn't become S-rank because they lacked a bit of strength is A-rank, and a Hunter who barely became A-rank with the help of their guild or colleagues is also A-rank.

,Those three are below average even among A-ranks.',

I could tell just by looking at them. They were probably in ambiguous positions even within their guilds.

,If I win against all three of them in a row, my reputation will rise.',

This is reality, so I won't kill them. I don't plan on going that far.

A little later, the surroundings became noisy. The celebrities who were the broadcast MCs had arrived. I straightened my clothes.

I frowned when I saw the celebrity entering through the main gate.

,It's a guy.',

,He's a popular actor these days. It was quite difficult to scout him. It would have been difficult to scout him if it wasn't for my connections.',

,I just want to quit everything and go home.',

Kang Seok-su grabbed my shoulder.

,Don't go. If you're going to go, clean up the mess first.',

,I was just saying. Take your hand off. I don't like guys touching my body.',

,I don't like it either.',

Kang Seok-su quickly took his hand off. I headed to the waiting room with half of my motivation gone. I was planning a fun one-night stand with a beautiful celebrity... but it was ruined from the start.

,You have to do an interview later. The questions and answers are prepared in advance. Memorize them.',

,Alright. Alright.',

• • •

The Seventier Guild's training ground.

I looked around from the center. Six cameras were filming me from different directions. The broadcasting station PD had a very gleeful expression.

In this era, duel videos between Hunters are common, but the story changes if my name comes out there. The title of S-rank candidate is enough to gather the expectations of the entire nation.

,Where are you looking? Focus on me.',

The first duel opponent is Park Seo-un from the Sambak Guild. His eyes are fierce, probably because my provocation earlier worked.

Park Seo-un held a training sword without a blade, and I also held a training sword in the same way.

,What's with that clumsy stance?',

,......Is that how it looks?',

,...Is there something about that stance?',

,......Don't you even know that?',

Park Seo-un gritted his teeth. Then, the sound of the duel starting rang out from the speaker.

Bbiik!

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 10]

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

The fastest strike after using double acceleration.

Park Seo-un's sword moves smoothly, blocking my attack.

,Block? This? No, he didn't even see my attack properly.',

You can tell by looking at his shaking eyes, as if he was in shock. He didn't block it knowing my attack. He only realized my attack after blocking it.

,It's inexplicable. Is it an ability?',

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 9]

I narrow the distance a little more and swing my sword where Park Seo-un's gaze doesn't reach.

Kkaang!

But it was blocked.

,He blocked an unknown attack. Judging by his appearance, he didn't realize it with his senses either. Then... it's an ability.',

Judging by the situation, it's an ability where his body moves on its own. In other words, it's like an auto-battle ability.

,If he can block my attacks... it's a pretty good ability. But how will he block attacks that can't be blocked?',

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

Lightning gathers and rotates in his left hand. He notices and tries to pull his body back, but I follow him and attack, gathering lightning and then detonating it.

Discharge.

Blue lightning spread in all directions. I was fine because I was resistant to lightning, but Park Seo-un wasn't.

Thud.

Park Seo-un, who was electrocuted, collapsed. He fainted.

Perhaps because the duel ended faster than expected. The surroundings were silent.
