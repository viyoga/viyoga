,What the fuck?"

Panicked by the corpse's heart stopping, I hastily stripped its clothes off, intending to check the heart with my own two eyes.

,What are you doing right now? Are you really trying to defile a corpse?"

Na Chae-yeong said, as if she couldn't believe it.

I tried to explain the situation to her but staggered again. My legs wouldn't hold me. Luckily, I had some strength left in my arms, so I grabbed the edge of the desk and held on.

,N-no, it's not like that. I used Recovery on this corpse, and its heart started beating! It's real!"

,...You're saying you revived a corpse?"

Na Chae-yeong approached with a serious face. As expected of Na Chae-yeong. She didn't doubt my words and believed them. She carefully reached out her hand towards the corpse. The place her hand was heading was the wrist. It seemed she was planning to check the pulse first.

Just as I was about to say that the heart wasn't beating again, my throat tightened. I was too exhausted to speak.

Na Chae-yeong, while holding the wrist, was looking at the air in surprise. Her eyes were looking at the air, not the corpse. As if she was seeing something invisible.

,...It really came back to life. No, wait a minute. The wound disappeared. It's dead. It briefly came back to life and then died."

Na Chae-yeong stared at the corpse with complicated eyes.

Players can see the name, ability, and traits of an Awakened when they come into contact with them. In other words, the corpse was an Awakened. I caught my breath and stood up. The hand holding the desk was still trembling.

,Take off her clothes and check her chest."

,...She died from being pierced through the heart. The rotten smell is gone, and blood is coming out from around her solar plexus."

Na Chae-yeong removed the corpse's top. A pierced wound was visible between the blood-stained, plain bra. The wound wasn't as big as I thought. Compared to the hole in the clothes, the wound was smaller.

,The wound has healed. Her heart was beating, just like you said. But she died immediately. You can tell even if you look closely. Her heart wasn't completely healed. The bite wound on her calf has also healed more than halfway. It healed not only her chest and heart but her whole body."

Na Chae-yeong said analytically.

A person couldn't come back to life just by making their heart beat. A person is a very complex machine. Even if the engine, the heart, is beating, it won't work if the other parts are messed up.

,Maybe your Recovery can work not only on living things but also on objects."

,...You mean I can revive a skeleton?"

,It might be possible."

,Hoo. As expected of me. I've awakened an amazing ability."

,This isn't something to say lightly in a joking manner. The fact that you can revive dead people must be kept a secret. What do you think will happen if other people find out about this? They'll all be after you. Whether it's America, China... any country in the world will try to get their hands on you. Because you can be the best insurance. If they can wield that power... you know without me having to say it, right?"

,Use me? Those bastards?"

When I smiled with my lips pursed, Na Chae-yeong quietly nodded.

,I admit you're amazing. You definitely have a mind of your own, so you won't be mentally dominated and become a puppet... but they'll try to get their hands on you no matter what. If not, they'll try to kill you no matter what. Even if you don't like it, it's best to hide the fact that you can revive people. You don't want things to get annoying, do you?"

,Hmm. That's true. I should at least hide this power. But I'm going to revive her."

,...Who knows that you brought this corpse?"

,No one. I sneaked it in secretly because I thought it would cause unnecessary rumors."

,That's good news at least."

With trembling legs, I approached a nearby chair and sat down. My body was still tired, but it was much more comfortable than standing.

,By the way, this is so exhausting."

,You revived a dead person. Not just someone who died recently, but someone who died a few days ago. Although the decomposition wasn't too bad... it must be thanks to the spiderwebs. It seems spiderwebs have a preservative effect too. You should be grateful for Absolute Mentality. If you didn't have Absolute Mentality... you might have died?"

There are two things needed when an Awakened uses their ability: stamina and mental strength. It's been a long time since my stamina was drained this much. As she said, I might have died if I didn't have Absolute Mentality.

,Let's leave the corpse here."

,...What?"

,The air conditioning is great here. And there's a refrigerator."

,That's my sparkling water refrigerator. It's not a place to put corpses."

Na Chae-yeong liked sparkling water. To the point where she would fill the lab refrigerator with it.

,Let's just leave it here. I really have nowhere else to put it."

Currently, this lab was Na Chae-yeong's personal room. She sleeps with me at night, but she spends most of her time in the lab during the day.

,Haa. Okay. I'll bring a black cloth to cover it. But you know we can't just leave it like this, right? No matter how much we keep the air conditioning on, the corpse will rot."

,I'll use Recovery every day. It won't rot and get maggots. By the way, did you see it earlier? That person's name and ability."

,Yeah. You picked up something amazing."

「Individual Name: Choi Hye-jin

Potential: ★★★★★

Awakened Ability: Golden Body.

Traits: Instinct, Purity. Warrior.」

That was the information about the corpse that Na Chae-yeong told me.

,Her name is Choi Hye-jin. Her potential is 5 stars, just like me. I picked up something amazing."

,...Potential is just potential. No matter how great the potential is, they're not strong from the start. You're the unusual one."

,What about Golden Body? Is it the ability to turn the body into gold?"

,No, it can be said to be the highest-level ability in the body enhancement category. It's like a passive ability like your Absolute Mentality. Even if she just breathes, her body will gradually get stronger over time. It'll get stronger faster if she trains. The biggest advantage is adaptability."

,Adaptability?"

,It means she won't have any problems even if she undergoes body modification, evolution, or mutation. In Choi Hye-jin's case, the efficiency will be halved because of her Purity trait."

Purity. It was the same trait as me.

The Warrior trait increases combat ability.

Instinct was rare. It raises the limits of physical abilities. It's also said to sometimes cause special events. However, it's not all good. It can also cause problems because of Instinct.

,Typically, it's anger management. They can't control their rising anger and cause accidents."

,I see. What's the overall evaluation?"

,In a game-like sense, it's perfect."

,In a realistic sense?"

,It depends on what Choi Hye-jin's personality is like."

Anyway, we made a space in the corner of the lab and restrained Choi Hye-jin. We restrained Choi Hye-jin with shackles and handcuffs. Na Chae-yeong didn't stop there and even made an iron cage. Just in case, she even installed two turrets in front of it. So we could kill her immediately if necessary.

,She's not just a human, she's an Awakened. It'll be troublesome if she goes on a rampage."

,Hmm. Well, Choi Hye-jin would understand. But Choi Hye-jin was bitten by a zombie. Won't she become a zombie if we revive her?"

,Hmm. Well, I think your Recovery ability will return her to the state before she was bitten by the zombie, so it should be fine... but it hasn't happened yet, so I can't be sure."

,Can my ability revert zombies too?"

,Wouldn't it be possible? Your ability doesn't revert time like turning it back. You're not losing your memories because you're constantly using Recovery. Besides, you're also building resistance to Vister I. It means it doesn't revert things that aren't harmful to the body."

,Thinking about it calmly, something's strange."

,The world itself is strange, what are you talking about."

Na Chae-yeong said with a self-deprecating smile. She's a player who entered the game. It might not be strange even if strange things happen from her perspective. And I'm a guy who entered a work of fiction.

The next day.

I appeared on the morning news.

A scene of me fighting the Queen Spider was filmed and spread on the internet. And it was vivid without any mosaics.

,Tae-wang took it, didn't he? The video is edited."

Na Chae-yeong frowned and glared at the TV.

,I don't think so. Gu Tae-hee isn't the type of woman to do that. The editing is focused on me too. And the security team isn't visible."

,...It's like a video trying to make you a hero."

The sight of me constantly shooting blue lightning and fighting the Spider Queen was fun to watch. It was even more exciting because it wasn't a movie.

,That's a damn well-made mad movie."

I was impressed. I don't know who edited the video, but they were definitely a professional. Maybe even a current worker. I'd even want to hire them.

,...Aren't you angry? They used your face without permission?"

,It's not a video dissing me."

I went on the internet and checked people's reactions. Korean netizens naturally liked it. An Awakened with powerful strength had appeared. They were comparing me to some guy called Daejeon Thor and praising me to the skies.

Seoul Zeus.

That was my nickname. It seems I got this nickname because of Daejeon Thor. If Daejeon Thor was the Thunder Emperor, I was the Thunder God.

,I like that I'm higher up than Daejeon Thor, at least. That's a correct assessment.'

The world was a mess right now. The population was rapidly decreasing because of the damn zombies and mutant monsters. In this desperate situation, people couldn't help but be happy that a hero had appeared.

,Dr. Na. I've come up with an amazing plan."

,...It sounds like bullshit, but I'll listen."

,I'll become the superhero Eugene the Zeus and save the people. I'll gather public sentiment. And with that foundation, I'll enter politics. I'll join a decent party and run for president. I'll be elected president thanks to the popularity of the superhero. After that, I'll change South Korea to my liking... and become an eternal, immortal super president."

,I've heard your nonsense. I think the country will be ruined if you become president, so I'll vote for another candidate."

,Where else is there a leader more outstanding than me in this chaotic world? You don't seem to know, but I have experience ruling the world. Even a skilled shaman said that I'm destined to become the Emperor of South Korea."

,Ah, yes. I'm sure you are."

Na Chae-yeong answered as if she was annoyed and drank her sparkling water.
