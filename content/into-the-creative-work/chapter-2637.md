Entering the banquet hall, I scanned the surroundings.

Men in tuxedos and women in dazzling dresses. The orchestra played, and the food arranged on the tables was expensive and extravagant. At first glance, it seemed like an ordinary party. But if you knew the faces of those attending, no one would dare call it ordinary.

Council members of the Round Table who decided Neo London's policies, CEOs and executives of major corporations whose names you'd recognize just by hearing them, knights and mercenaries renowned solely for their martial prowess-individuals with immense fame in Neo London were gathered in one place.

,It's not everyone, but... these are faces you can't ignore.'

I headed toward a suitable table. It was a table where mages from the Magic Association, whom I had met several times, were gathered.

In particular, one of them was a woman with whom I had a deep relationship.

A woman with blonde hair reaching her waist and wearing glasses. With slightly pointed ears, she looked at me with shining eyes.

Orsia Heitel, the successor of the Heitel School and a 7th Circle mage. Although she wasn't a combat mage like me, I had learned the basics of spatial magic from her.

,Yujin-ssi. It's been a while. I heard the news. I sincerely congratulate you on reaching the 7th Circle."

,Orsia. It's been a while. Are you here representing the Magic Association?"

,Yes. The Chairman is currently not in Neo London, and the Vice Chairmen passed the work onto me."

The smile on her lips was somehow chilly. Being stuck with the grunt work, she couldn't possibly be in a good mood.

,You've had a rough time."

,Yujin-ssi. I know you're busy, but please stop by the Magic Association later. You need to fill out paperwork to receive the items and benefits given to 7th Circle mages affiliated with the Magic Association."

,Are you saying I have to talk about my Unique Ability too?"

,Of course not. Unique Abilities are the essence of the 7th Circle itself. That will never happen. I swear on my name."

,Hmm. I'll stop by when I have time later. That dress suits you well today. How about a dance?"

I scanned her body and extended my hand. She was wearing a light green dress with little exposure. Only her wrists were visible at most. Her chest was completely covered, but I knew she had quite a large chest.

,...I'll decline the dance. I'm uncomfortable dancing in front of people."

,I understand. Forcing it wouldn't be gentlemanly."

We looked at each other with meaningful glances. It had been a while since we met, but... not that long. We meet regularly and mingle bodies. We're not dating, just sex partners.

I also spoke with other mages affiliated with the Magic Association around her. Most of them were 6th Circle mages, so they were very interested in me.

,Did you gain enlightenment while raiding dungeons? Or is there a special secret to reaching the 7th Circle?"

,The dungeon was related to mages. I obtained some information and headed to the dungeon, concealing my identity."

,There are dungeons that help mages. I've never been interested in dungeons until now... I should look for one this time."

I couldn't talk with the mages for long. There were quite a few people approaching me, giving me the eye.

,Haha. I see the CEO of LK Corporation here. I have some questions about superconductors...."

,Congratulations on reaching the 7th Circle this time. I heard you're unmarried, would you be willing to meet my daughter? My daughter is a mage, even if she's only 3rd Circle."

,I have a good investment opportunity, are you perhaps interested? I made a 130% profit through this investment. It's a reliable investment that's even been featured in the news."

People swarmed me like hyenas hunting for prey. I didn't change my expression and dealt with them appropriately. There's nothing wrong with talking about business at a party. In the first place, there's nothing else to talk about besides business.

,Even seemingly insignificant stories can become enormous information once they get out.'

Each piece of information exchanged here is worth its weight in gold. That's why even nobles with weak power force themselves to attend famous banquets, knowing they'll be despised.

,Duchess Elsa Bennevis is entering!"

,Duke William Snowden is entering!"

,Duchess Laura Hebellin is entering!"

Princesses and princes with ducal titles entered.

I quietly observed the royals.

Elsa Bennevis. A woman with dazzling blonde hair and emerald eyes. She was a glamorous woman. She had a voluptuous chest and a great figure. She was popular with both men and women. It was already well-known that she ran several businesses.

William Snowden. A handsome man with blonde hair. He enjoyed wearing white uniforms, and he attended the party in one this time as well. He wore a confident smile. Like Elsa, he was a man of great ability. He was said to have the support of the navy. He was second in the line of succession.

Laura Hebellin. The third in line to the throne. With blonde hair and red eyes, she was shorter and had a smaller chest compared to Elsa. However, Laura's body proportions and health were superior. She was a princess with a knight's license. There were rumors that the London Knights supported her.

The royals who entered one after another scattered in different directions.

Neo London's businesspeople mainly gathered around Elsa. Old nobles and naval officials gathered around William. There were many famous knights and emerging nobles around Laura. They had already formed their own factions.

,And they're glancing at me.'

They were eager to bring me into their factions.

Whether it was martial prowess or money, my influence after reaching the 7th Circle was incomparable to before.

,They're too busy watching each other to approach me first. If one of the three tries to contact me, the other two will keep them in check.'

Frankly, it wasn't a pretty sight to see them openly flirting with me.

,They'll probably ask to see me separately through their servants later.'

After that, they would whisper sweet words and try to bring me into their faction.

,The closest to the throne right now is Princess Elsa Bennevis, the first in line. But the Queen hasn't declared her as the successor. That means there's still a chance for the other royals. And there isn't an overwhelming gap between Princess Elsa and the other royals.'

The game could be tilted by me joining one side. I could become a kingmaker.

,First, Prince William is excluded. Most of Neo London's monarchs have been queens. Therefore, Neo London's citizens are bound to have more affection and support for a princess. Princess Elsa and Princess Laura. It's one of the two....'

Princess Laura's power is weaker compared to Princess Elsa. Therefore, if I help Princess Laura become queen, I could receive enormous rewards. Princess Elsa is a businesswoman who runs businesses, so she might hinder me. Princess Laura would be easier to control than Princess Elsa.

I was slowly leaning towards Princess Laura when.

A woman in a maid uniform approached me. Unlike other maids, she was wearing a much more luxurious and dress-like maid uniform. She was a black-haired woman who looked to be in her late twenties, but....

,The moment she approaches me, my mana trembles and I become tense. And it's hard to gauge her depth. She's at least 7th Circle or higher. Maybe even 8th Circle.'

Age doesn't really matter for a woman with this much power.

I looked at the sun-patterned badge decorated on her chest. It was a symbol signifying Prime.

In this era, there's only one person who can wear this badge.

,Royal Head maid Florence Palmer.'

She wasn't just at least 7th Circle, she was a definite 8th Circle transcendent. Late twenties? Unlike her appearance, she was much older than that. Unfortunately, detailed information wasn't known, so I didn't know much. What was certain was that she was loyal not to the royal family, but to the Queen personally. Therefore, only the Queen could give her orders.

,Earl Meyer-nim. Her Majesty the Queen summons you. I will personally guide you to Her Majesty."

,Since Her Majesty the Queen has summoned me, I must go."

I followed behind Florence. As she moved, the servants didn't dare block her path and stepped aside. I could feel even high-ranking nobles and royals watching her carefully.

In the empty hallway, I followed behind her and asked.

,Head maid Florence, I heard you only follow the orders of Her Majesty the Queen. Do you serve an individual? Or do you serve the throne?"

,I only serve Her Majesty Victoria VI."

,...I see."

I followed her into the Queen's room. Florence didn't leave the room and stood next to the Queen.

,I greet Her Majesty the Queen."

I knelt before the Queen and carefully observed her. The Queen's impression was like that of a kind grandmother. However, an aura of authority that couldn't be treated lightly emanated from her.

,Rise, Earl."

,...Yes, Your Majesty."

,I don't have much time, so I'll get straight to the point. Do not interfere in the succession to the throne. If you interfere before this succession, the game will flow uncontrollably. That is not what I desire. I do not ask much of you. Maintain neutrality in this succession war."

,Does Her Majesty the Queen wish for the princesses and princes to fight each other to the death?"

,That is correct. This position should not be easily passed on. I want those children to take this position themselves. I have demanded neutrality from everyone who could become an asymmetrical force, including you."

This wasn't a request made only to me. I couldn't refuse. No, I could refuse. If I was prepared to be on bad terms with the current Queen.

,The future is important, but the present must exist. And the justification that I couldn't intervene due to Victoria VI's orders will also work on the future queen.'

The calculation was complete.

,I will do as Her Majesty the Queen wishes."

,Thank you, Earl. I am always grateful for the Earl's hard work for Neo London. Florence, that."

,Yes, Your Majesty."

Florence, who answered briefly, put her hand into the front pocket of her apron. What she took out was a thick book.

,...Is it a subspace-type artifact?'

,I heard that you recently reached the 7th Circle. This is a magic tome that I bestow upon you."

I accepted the magic tome that the Queen personally handed over.

,Thank you, Your Majesty!"

I checked the cover of the magic tome. The excessively clean cover made it seem like it was a copy rather than the original.

,It is the magic tome of German, who was a court mage 130 years ago. It is said to contain 8th Circle lightning magic and some 7th Circle magic. I am not a mage, so I do not know much, but you, as a mage, will know its value well."

,...It is a priceless treasure. I can only be grateful for Your Majesty's grace."

I spoke with sincerity.

This was something I needed more than anything else right now.

,Now, I must go to the banquet hall. Earl Meyer, can you escort me?"

,Of course, Your Majesty."

I gladly took her hand.
