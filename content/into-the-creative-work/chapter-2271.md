Elena-ssi disappeared, leaving me alone with White.

"......."

"......."

The atmosphere was awkward. Generally, that's what I feel. White was standing still like a tree, and I quietly observed White. Since he was wearing a mask and covering his entire body with a black robe, there was nothing I could figure out through observation.

"Hey."

"......."

Even when I spoke, there was no answer coming back.

Just in case, I send out some killing intent. White immediately counters with killing intent, looking this way. Fortunately, he didn't rush at me right away. When I withdrew my killing intent, White's killing intent also disappeared.

After waiting a few more minutes, Samuel-ssi came. He wasn't alone. He was with three seahorses with reins on them. The seahorses were over 2m in size and floated above the ground as if swimming.

"I'm a little late. I had some trouble putting on the reins in the middle."

"What are those seahorses? Are they monsters?"

"If you call them monsters, you could say they are. But this time, they're mounts. Here, grab the reins and get on."

I got on the seahorse. The seahorse was docile. It's not that it's naturally like that, it's the effect of the reins.

「Reins of Obedience

Equip it on a mount to make it obey.

Rank: C」

"What's the identity of this seahorse? If we need a horse, there's no need for it to be a seahorse."

"The area we're trying to attack has several entry conditions. One of them is entering with a creature related to dragons. These seahorses are distant descendants of sea dragons. You could say they have a tiny bit of sea dragon blood mixed in."

"A creature related to dragons? The entry conditions are annoying from the start. Of course, space-time scrolls won't work there, right?"

"That's right. Not only can't you use space-time scrolls, but there's a high possibility that even time will be distorted. That's why it's quite a dangerous place. You don't have to go if you don't want to. I have no intention of forcing you."

I pondered. Time distortion was quite annoying. There was a high probability that time within the area would be faster. One second outside would be distorted to one minute or one hour inside the area. The opposite case is almost non-existent.

'A day outside could be a year inside the area.'

If the attack is successful, the time distortion of the area will naturally disappear.

'That means the difficulty is high. The risk is great. The greater the risk, the greater the return.'

Atlantis is a place where the rewards are certain. The more dangerous and difficult it is, the sweeter the rewards will be.

"I'll do it. You'll guarantee the rewards, right?"

"Of course. There's only one thing I want. You can take the rest of the rewards. Even the rights to rule the area."

"I need to know what that one thing is."

"I'll explain it on the way. Let's start first. We'll have to run for 3 hours."

The seahorses carrying us on their backs went up into the air instead of the ground and started running in the air. It felt like a flying bike. The speed seemed to be about 120km per hour.

"Start talking. What area are you trying to attack?"

"Area 7,500, City of Brilliant Civilization. That's the area. Have you ever heard of it? You're a regressor, so do you know?"

"......I don't know. Just because I'm a regressor doesn't mean I know everything. Atlantis is too vast for one person to know everything."

It was an area that didn't appear in the original work. It wasn't even in the settings. There was no information at all.

"That's true. Area 7,500 is also an area that people haven't reached yet. There's only one thing I want. The heart of Tiamat, the hidden boss. That's enough for me."

"Tiamat. Was she an evil god in Mesopotamian mythology?"

"Yes. The important point here is that Tiamat is an enemy of the gods."

"Tiamat must have failed."

"I told you, the process is more important than the result. What's important here is that Tiamat fought against the gods."

"...Just because it's the power of a god who was an enemy of the gods doesn't mean you can kill a god."

"I know that too. But Tiamat's power and narrative will be a great help to us."

"Let's move on to a more constructive conversation. What are the attack conditions for that area? What are the conditions for the hidden boss to appear?"

"Sorry. I don't have detailed information about the attack. We'll have to investigate and find out how to summon the hidden boss separately."

"...You don't know? You?"

"Just because it's me doesn't mean I know everything."

Samuel-ssi gave a bitter smile.

It wouldn't be a lie. I'm his collaborator. He needs my help, so there's no reason to lie.

'...He asked for help from me and Elena-ssi because he didn't know. If he knew, he would have tried to attack it alone.'

After running for 3 hours, we arrived in front of a cave. A cool breeze blows from inside the cave. A coolness that makes my body, which is unshakable even in a severe cold wind, tremble.

"Have you ever been inside this area?"

"No. That's why I'm a little nervous. Do you want to go in first? Or should I go in first?"

"Of course, I'll go in first."

Atlantis had a first-time bonus. If you achieve something for the first time or reach somewhere, you get a reward. Last time when I went to the back of the moon for the first time, all my stats increased by 1. All stats 1 is by no means a small stat.

Samuel-ssi shrugged and stepped aside. I confidently entered the cave. An invisible hand grabbed my body and pulled me in.

「Entry conditions have been met.」

「You have entered Area 7,500, City of Brilliant Civilization.」

「You are entering with a descendant of the Sea Dragon. You are given 3 bonus civilization points.」

「The attack condition for the Brilliant Civilization is world domination.」

「Cultivate civilization and conquer the world.」

「The first civilization points given are 10.」

「The minimum number of participating players, 5, has been met.」

「Summoning players. Starting the attack. There are currently 7 participating players.」

「You will lose your qualification to attack if you lose. Surrender is also allowed.」

「Time distortion is applied. 1 year in Area 7,500 is 1 minute in Atlantis time.」

When I came to my senses, I was sitting in a cave. Animal skins, bones, branches, and fruits were scattered around. A large animal fur was spread on the floor.

'...No. Wait. My clothes?'

I was only wearing a rag that didn't even feel like it had any civilization's writing on it. Even though I had a decent body with well-defined muscles, my lower half felt very empty. Of course, since I wasn't wearing the rag, but wrapping it around me. Every time I moved, my lower half dangled.

"Um. Shit. What is it? I feel like my body has suddenly become heavier. Status window."

「Name: Seong Yujin

Class: Cheonma (Heavenly Demon)

Divine Seat: King of the Demonic Sky

Affiliation: AL 401 Earth.

Strength: 10 Agility: 10 Stamina: 10 Mana: 10 Luck: 10

(Applying status window)」

All stats have fallen equally to 10.

The good thing is that it was not affected by skills and traits. I was thinking about what happened and went out of the cave. There were primitive tribe members who hadn't even washed properly. They didn't move at all as if time had stopped. I could see the soil bouncing upwards from under their feet. The soil didn't fall and looked like it was embalmed.

'Time has really stopped.'

I try to touch them with my hand. An invisible barrier blocks my hand.

'What do you want me to do?'

「Confirmed the participation of all players. There are 7 players. The total number of participants is 15.」

「You are the leaders of civilization. Achieve a brilliant civilization.」

「In 10 minutes, time will flow again. Before that, use civilization points to determine the direction of civilization. You will receive one civilization point per turn. You will receive 1 interest for every 10 civilization points. You can earn civilization points for special discoveries or achievements.」

'I get it. This is a civilization-building game, right? Fun.'

A smile spreads across my face.

When I was in middle school, I was so addicted to civilization-building games that I wasted a week of winter vacation. It couldn't be helped. When I came to my senses, a week had passed. After that, I got tired of it and quit.

'Let's see. What can I do with civilization points....'

I could do quite a lot with civilization points. Acquire technology, summon great people, or bestow gifts on humans.

'There are also things I can do only for the first time.'

Right, setting racial characteristics.

You could only set one racial characteristic for the first time.

「Innate Physique (10p): Overall physical abilities increase.

Excellent Brain (8p): Average intelligence increases.

Twisted Fate (10p): Special individuals are born with a low probability

The best traits I saw were Innate Physique and Excellent Brain.

'If my prediction is correct, physical abilities are important in the early stages, and technology becomes more important as we move into the later stages. In other words, Excellent Brain is a trait for the later stages.'

After thinking about it, I chose Twisted Fate. There was a reason. I have the Great Luck (EX) skill. It's much better if there's randomness.

'Even though my stats have dropped, my traits and skills haven't been nerfed.'

It also meant that I could use skills and traits.

What can I do with the remaining 3 civilization points? As I looked around, I chose one.

「Name your civilization.」

'A name. That's right. A name is important....'

After thinking about it, I chose a name.

'Wishing my civilization to become the strongest in the world.'
