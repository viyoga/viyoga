I rode along the main road with Cotton. We occasionally encountered merchants, travelers, and mercenaries. Of course, they all bowed their heads and showed respect. A knight was a quasi-noble rank below a noble, so it was only natural.

Riding a horse wasn't all that boring. Monsters occasionally popped out and attacked. Things like wild goblins or kobolds. But they were slaughtered by me and Cotton's swords in less than three minutes.

,These disgusting monsters never disappear no matter how many times you kill them. Good work, Sir Daycart."

,It's nothing to praise. This is the fifth attack today alone. There seem to be a lot of monsters nearby, why don't you subjugate them?"

,If there are trolls or orcs, Count Mork-kakka will lead the troops to subjugate them. Goblins and kobolds can be easily dealt with by adult men if they have weapons, so we tend to leave them alone. In the first place, the people of the territory hardly ever leave their villages or cities. These guys don't come down to where people live either, so there are rarely any subjugation parties formed."

Unlike Earth, this world treated monsters like wild beasts. It wasn't strange. For them, it was natural for monsters to exist.

,Do you know what kind of person Baron Triway is, the noble to whom I must deliver Count Mork-kakka's letter?"

I asked Cotton. I didn't know much about the tangled relationships of the nobles here, and I was bored riding a horse.

,The father of His Excellency's third wife. In other words, His Excellency's father-in-law."

,Indeed. If he's family, he'll help."

Count Mork, who is on the verge of a territorial war, has no choice but to rely on an alliance to survive. In other words, with the power of an alliance, he can avoid a two-front war.

Because it was close and the road was well-paved, we were able to arrive at Baron Triway's territory in two days. Compared to the County, it was definitely smaller in scale, but it had everything it needed. It was definitely a developing city.

We met Baron Triway at his mansion. He was a thin man with intense eyes. His children and servants were busy watching the Baron's every move. He seemed like an authoritarian figure.

,I heard you brought a letter from Count Mork? Triway welcomes you. I have prepared a meal for you. There may be many shortcomings, but please rest well for now."

Baron Triway accepted the letter. Then, he immediately broke the seal on the letter and read its contents.

,A war with Viscount Gledenbergh... Has he finally revealed his dark intentions? War is unpleasant, but it must be done when the time comes. Summon the army!"

Baron Triway issued a summons. I asked him calmly.

,Did you know that a war would break out? You didn't show a moment's hesitation from reading the letter to summoning the army."

,I love peace. But the times do not desire peace. So shouldn't we prepare in advance? Please tell Count Mork. I will protect the rear while the territorial war with Veroprin is in progress."

I can see Count Mork's plan.

Win the territorial war and receive 50,000 territory residents and war supplies from Veroprin to attack Viscount Glenbergh. If he arms 50,000 territory residents and pushes them onto the battlefield, victory is almost certain.

In the end, the important thing is victory. The victor will take everything.

We spent the night in Baron Triway's territory and set off again in the morning.

Next, we met Baron Allsreg. The mustachioed baron looked at us with cold eyes.

,I understand Count Mork's intentions well. But Count Mork's inner thoughts are sinister, so I cannot be with him. On the other hand, Viscount Gledenbergh has a great cause. I support Viscount Gledenbergh. So please leave my territory right now."

A flat rejection.

We were kicked out without even getting a glass of water. Cotton's expression wasn't good. It was like an enemy had increased.

,Sir Daycart. We must hurry. We need to check the movements of the other nobles!"

We rode like that for three days and met four more nobles. The results were fifty-fifty.

,This war is going to be bigger than I thought.'

Count Mork, Viscount Gledenbergh, and these guys were all preparing for war. There was no true peace in this fallen empire.

The horse walked. Even a horse specialized in running couldn't help but get tired. Horses are living things too.

But today, Cotton next to me looked dark.

,Sir Cotton. You don't look good. Is it because of the approaching war? No, that's an insult to Sir Cotton. I'm sorry."

,...Sir Daycart. Do you sincerely have no intention of serving Count Mork?"

,As I said before, I am looking for a lord of destiny. Count Mork-kakka is an excellent noble, but I don't feel that it's destiny. As promised, I will participate in this territorial war and lead Count Mork to victory. And after receiving the letter of appointment, I will leave."

,...Sir Daycart. I have a request. Give up the letter of appointment and leave now. You are an excellent knight even without a letter of appointment. You have the secrets of a knight and are armed with sturdy armor and a sword. Even without a letter of appointment, you are a knight who knows honor."

,If I don't have a letter of appointment, I'll be ignored. Didn't Sir Cotton do that?"

,Please forget about that time. It was my mistake. You are young. You will have another chance to get a letter of appointment. So please give it up this time."

Cotton, as a knight, would know very well that a knight without a letter of appointment is not officially a knight.

But still, give up the letter of appointment and leave?

There was only one reason I could guess.

,...Did Count Mork tell you to kill me?"

,That is so."

,I'm disappointed in Count Mork. I thought he was a noble who knew honor..."

,Viscount Gledenbergh's declaration of war was something His Excellency did not expect. His Excellency is currently suspicious that Viscount Gledenbergh has been up to something in his territory. The most suspicious thing is..."

,It's me. I suddenly appeared before the war broke out... Count Mork didn't trust me."

Above all, if he had someone investigate me, he would have realized that my tracks don't exist at all. A knight named Daycart does not exist in this world.

Count Mork decided to have Cotton eliminate the seeds of anxiety.

,Why are you telling me all of this? You could have killed me quietly. Is it because of honor?"

Sneaking up on me with a sword while I sleep, or putting poison in my water, etc. There were many ways to kill me. Honestly, I've been careless lately. Cotton is the guy who even brought me a concubine.

,Didn't Sir Daycart save my life? In that village, when I collapsed from sleeping pills, I would have died if Sir Daycart hadn't been there. So please leave now."

Count Mork stabbed me in the back. I want to kill Count Mork right now. But if Count Mork dies now, the territorial war will fail. Veroprin will win and Park Suho will grow. Maybe. Just maybe, Park Suho might reach S-class before me. That must be stopped.

,I have already promised Count Mork. I will fight for him until the territorial war and receive the letter of appointment. Leaving now is abandoning my honor."

,Honor can be rebuilt. You will be welcomed wherever you go."

,It's not like honor that's been thrown away once will come back."

,...Are you really going to do this?"

,Sir Cotton. You can report that you couldn't stop me. Count Mork will also care about his reputation, so he won't kill me openly."

,Do you know why I'm talking here now? There are soldiers and knights ambushing ahead disguised as bandits. If you don't turn back here now, you will be killed by bandits. It's a great disgrace."

,Well. I don't think I'll lose."

,Arrogance. No, you have great confidence. I understand since you're Sir Daycart, not just any knight."

Cotton got off his horse and drew his sword. I still looked at him while riding my horse.

,Sir Daycart! Draw your sword!"

,Are you going to fight here? Didn't you say there's an ambush ahead?"

,I am only acting for my honor."

,...I see. Sir Cotton. You are a true knight."

I turn the horse's head. When I kick the horse's belly, the horse cries out and starts running at full speed.

,Sir Daycart?!"

Cotton, who was flustered behind me, called me in a loud voice. I ignored him and sprinted.

It was the moment I entered the excessively quiet forest path. A spear appeared, piercing through the trunk of a tree next to me. The spear was aimed directly at me.

I draw my sword and deflect the spear. Bandits who were hiding in various places reveal themselves. To be exact, knights and soldiers pretending to be bandits.

,A total of 33 people. There are 3 knights and the rest are soldiers.'

If you include Cotton, there are 4 knights. 30 soldiers are clumsy. Their role is close to being meat shields. They will try to sacrifice them to create an opening when necessary.

I could feel their will to definitely take me down. I jumped up and got off the horse. The horse is just cumbersome here.

A spear flies in a straight line. It penetrates and advances even if there is a tree in front. A spear that never stops. I raise my sword and deflect the spear. Only then does the spear lose its power and fall from the sky to the ground.

[The Celestial Eye is opened.]

I could see the direction where the knight who threw the spear was. I grabbed the falling spear and threw it in the same direction it came from. The knight couldn't avoid it and was killed with his heart pierced.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Wave.

Electromagnetic waves spread out from me. I can directly feel the location of the guys hiding without having to see them with my own eyes. Surprisingly, it's more comfortable than seeing it directly with my eyes.

,There's a knight hiding under the ground. Is this also a secret technique of the knight? What the hell is the secret technique of the knight that makes even this possible? What's certain is that the secret technique is not martial arts.'

Meteor Sword.

A sword wrapped in blue lightning falls from the sky to the ground.

Kwaaaang!

The meteor sword stuck in the ground explodes with lightning. The ground is turned upside down and a crater is created. It was only natural that the knight hiding underground was shattered into pieces.

,Go and kill him! I will make the one who kills him my follower!"

,Waaaaaaa!"

The soldiers who were hiding charged all at once.
