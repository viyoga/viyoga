,Were you planning to steal the secret manuals and elixirs and run away like a damn thief? They say chivalry and righteousness are alive in the Hwasan Sect... All bullshit."

Gi Seong-oh flared up. He glared at me, his one eye wide as if to kill me. His imposing manner was quite fierce, as if he hadn't just risen to the position of Hwasan Sect Leader.

,Typical of a Demonic Sect bastard, you know nothing. Even if the Hwasan Sect is destroyed, if a small seed remains, the Hwasan Sect will sprout again and flourish."

,So you're that seed? Too bad for you. Looks like that seed is about to get trampled."

I drew my sword. Gi Seong-oh also pointed his sword at me. A purplish aura seeped out from his body. It was the renowned Violet Mist Divine Art of the Hwasan Sect.

I smelled plum blossoms. The piercing plum blossom scent was so strong it was unpleasant.

,It's not too late now. Back off. I don't have time to deal with the likes of you."

,Even if you try to act all high and mighty, I know you're not in perfect condition. You must have consumed stamina and inner energy in the previous battles. Don't you think this is the perfect moment to kill the Hwasan Sect Leader?"

,Foolish bastard. I give you a chance, and you kick it away! Even if I'm exhausted now, do you think I can't handle a little devil like you!"

,Little devil?"

,Jeomjeon Island Yeom Gu-seok! I know about you. You haven't been in the Annihilation Squad for long. You have decent skills... but at best, you're just a low-ranking member of the Annihilation Squad. Other than being cruel, there's nothing special about you."

,You know nothing about me."

I wore a sneer. Gi Seong-oh's face twisted.

,Fine. If you insist on being crushed by my sword... I'll kill you now!"

Gi Seong-oh took a step forward and swung his sword. The sword approached, passing through the fastest path. Just from that one move, I could tell that Gi Seong-oh was no pushover.

I raised my sword and blocked Gi Seong-oh's sword. The sharp sound of steel clashing against steel rang out.

,You've got the basics down."

Kaka kaka kak!

Gi Seong-oh's sword moved, skimming across my sword. Sparks flew from the friction.

The movement of the sparks was strange. They should have scattered in all directions, but they began to gather as if drawn by something invisible.

[The Celestial Eye is opening.]

I could see what was invisible.

It was a transparent flower bud. The flower bud, made of inner energy, bloomed and was dyed purple at the same time. Plum blossoms began to bloom.

I thought of Kyle from the [Baekhwan] world. Kyle was also a Plum Blossom Sword Master who had trained in the Violet Mist Divine Art. I had researched Kyle, so I could confidently say I knew a bit about the Plum Blossom Sword Technique and the Violet Mist Divine Art.

,This is completely different from Kyle's Plum Blossom Sword Technique and Violet Mist Divine Art."

The pressure alone was different. If I let my guard down even a little, I would be overwhelmed. Moreover, the plum blossoms blooming in the air boasted a strength on the level of sword energy. If I touched them carelessly, I wouldn't even be able to gather my bones.

,The plum blossoms are moving."

They moved splendidly. Even knowing they were flowers of death, they were so dazzling I was almost bewitched.

Gi Seong-oh swung his sword. It was a swordsmanship that resembled plum blossom petals scattering. Every time his sword moved, a purplish aura gathered and plum blossoms bloomed. This was not good. The space was being dominated.

Ghost Cutting Sword Art (斬鬼刀法) Evil Ghost Decimation (惡鬼十殺).

A cross-shaped sword energy flew out. I didn't aim directly at Gi Seong-oh. I aimed at the purple plum blossoms blooming in the air.

The torn plum blossoms became sword energy and rushed towards me. I hastily raised my protective energy. The plum blossoms that collided with the protective energy disappeared like dust.

Even in the midst of that, Gi Seong-oh was swinging his sword, drawing plum blossoms. The more plum blossoms bloomed, the stronger his aura became.

,Twelve plum blossoms have been drawn. You and I are inside the plum blossoms. Isn't it beautiful?"

This was not good. The space was being dominated. I hadn't made any particular mistakes, but the plum blossoms continued to increase, exceeding 20. I noticed the secret a little late. His left sleeve was fluttering.

,He was drawing plum blossoms with his fingers inside his sleeve. It's a cheap trick, but effective. I can't hide his years of experience."

For masters at the level of me and Gi Seong-oh, the finger itself was a kind of sword. There are five fingers. In other words, it's the same as having five swords in one hand.

,A total of 24 plum blossoms are about to bloom. The Twenty-Four Plum Blossom Sword Technique is famous even in the Jianghu. They say that once 24 plum blossoms bloom, no one can stop it?"

I felt the urge to use Lightning Sky Style and Cheonma Shingong to break through head-on.

But there were Annihilation Squad members a little distance away. If I used Cheonma Shingong, things would get fucked up. Even if I used Lightning Sky Style, I would be watched. Because Jeomjeon Island Yeom Gu-seok only knows the Ghost Cutting Sword Art.

Ghost Cutting Sword Art (斬鬼刀法) Evil Ghost Decimation (惡鬼十殺).

I swung my sword towards the wall. I jumped out through the collapsing wall. If Gi Seong-oh's plum blossoms dominated the space, then I just had to go to another space.

Gi Seong-oh followed me outside. But I didn't feel the same hostile aura as before.

,Are you running away? Fine. I'm busy too, so let's promise to meet next time. You're lucky, Jeomjeon Island."

,Don't talk shit, Gi Seong-oh. If you run away from here, I'll call the Annihilation Squad members nearby. If you want to escape without being chased by the Annihilation Squad, you'll have to kill me and go. There's not much time left either. The Annihilation Squad will be storming in here soon. What are you going to do?"

,You little bastard, you must be crazy to want to die!"

Gi Seong-oh rushed at me urgently. It seemed like he was starting to sweat bullets at my words. He swung his sword. The afterimages of the ultimate Illusion Sword were each and every one like the real thing.

,Even if he confuses my eyes, the sword he swings is still just one."

The trajectory in which he could swing his sword was fixed. Plum blossoms were drawn around him. I retreated. It was best not to get close to the plum blossoms.

Kaang! Kang! Kang!

I blocked Gi Seong-oh's sword and continued to be pushed back.

And at some point, I was outside the Hwasan Sect. I checked the corpses of the Annihilation Squad members scattered around me.

,These are your comrades. They rushed at me without knowing their place, just like you."

,The cause of death is strange. You didn't kill them. Was there a trap here?"

,Do you see that cave? A drop of Azure Stone Oil comes out every 50 years. It's one of our Hwasan Sect's treasures. These bastards were blinded by greed and fell into the trap. Even though I have the Azure Stone Oil in my possession. Hahaha!"

,They abandoned their mission and moved separately to get the Azure Stone Oil? There were quite a few bastards like me."

It wasn't strange. The Annihilation Squad basically had strong pride. On top of that, they were demons. That meant they were crazy bastards who wouldn't listen to their superiors' orders.

,It's cut off from the outside. The barrier installed in the Hwasan Sect should have been neutralized... Is it a different kind of barrier?"

The only fortunate thing was that there were no traps. The traps had been used up on the Annihilation Squad members who came earlier.

,It's cut off from the outside. Good?"

It wasn't completely cut off. If I used something like Cheonma Shingong, even the barrier wouldn't be able to withstand it. The Annihilation Squad was already sensitive to demonic energy.

,Lightning Sky Style should be okay."

The moment I saw Gi Seong-oh's Plum Blossom Sword Technique, there was something I wanted to try.

Plum Blossom Sword Technique. It was familiar to me. I had studied Kyle's Plum Blossom Sword Technique with Yuria, so there was no way it wouldn't be familiar. I knew the essence of the Plum Blossom Sword Technique. It was different from the Plum Blossom Sword Technique of this world, but similar. That was a fact I confirmed while watching the bastard's sword swing just now.

,If I were from another world, I wouldn't have even tried it, but I have talent in this world."

It wasn't just talent. It was talent assisted by the Cheon Gangseong System. The reason why I in this world reached the realm of the Five Energies much faster than others was thanks to the Cheon Gangseong System. I in this world was a genius.

[Activating the time of genius for 10 seconds.]

Pajijik.

From my dantian, inner energy flowed through my meridians and into my whole body.

I stared straight ahead. Plum blossoms were blooming one by one around Gi Seong-oh. That was a martial art that mixed the essence of the Illusion Sword.

The Plum Blossom Sword Techniques that I had researched with Yuria came to mind. ...In fact, most of the research was done by Yuria, and I just added a spoon. Actually, I had never understood the Plum Blossom Sword Technique at all until now. But now that I was activating the time of genius, now that my talent was being assisted by the Cheon Gangseong System, things were different.

I swung my sword.

A flower was drawn in the air. The flower bud, which had not yet bloomed, was shining blue. A blue current flowed through the petals.

,This crazy bastard! How dare you imitate the Hwasan Sect's Plum Blossom Sword Technique!!!"

Gi Seong-oh spat out violent anger. He looked at me like I was his mortal enemy. It seemed like he didn't like the fact that I, a demon, was using the elegant Plum Blossom Sword Technique.

24 plum blossoms bloomed. Blood vessels burst in Gi Seong-oh's only eye, and bloody tears flowed, and blood trickled from his nostrils. It was proof that he was overdoing it.

On the other hand, the flowers I drew were seven.

Lightning Sky Style (Raiden-ryu) Myriad Lightning Bloom (Manji Raika).

The lightning flower buds rotated clockwise and began to bloom. The blooming lightning flowers scattered currents in all directions and dominated the space. Gi Seong-oh's plum blossoms fluttered as if struggling. But they couldn't penetrate the seven lightning flowers that were focused on defense.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Mind Sword (The mind is the sword).

I plunged into Gi Seong-oh's chest and swung my sword. A sword swung with the mind. Gi Seong-oh surprisingly reacted. He held his sword sideways and blocked the Mind Sword. But he didn't block it completely. A sword wound was carved on his shoulder, and blood burst out.

,This bastard... he was hiding his skills!"

I stabbed my sword into Gi Seong-oh's faltering chest. I felt the sensation of piercing his heart. I pulled out my sword. A fountain of blood erupted from his chest. Gi Seong-oh's knees fell to the ground.

,The Hwasan Sect... will bloom... again...!"

,That's a boring last will. You should have begged me to save you."

,......Save me."

,No."

Seogeok.

I cut off Gi Seong-oh's head. I searched the fallen bastard's chest and found a space pocket. I opened the space pocket and checked the contents. It was full of good stuff, including the Violet Mist Divine Art and Azure Stone Oil. It was a satisfying farming session.

,Now I just have to avoid getting caught."

I left that place and headed back to my original position.
