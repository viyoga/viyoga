The second time I came to the Spirit Realm. But unlike before, I had some spare time.

The Spirit Realm, on the surface, looked similar to Earth. Earth in a natural state, without human civilization. You could say it's a kind of primitive Earth.

However, strictly speaking, that nature isn't matter either. If you feel it closely, you can sense the power of nature, similar to the nature of spirits.

Forces in their natural state, such as earth, wind, water, fire, light, and darkness, exist and are intertwined. To my eyes, it looks as if the territories are divided.

And far away.

A giant tree is visible. But it's not a tree. It's a giant that looks like a tree. The being that is the beginning and source of all spirits.

,[What is that....]

Makina murmured, seemingly overwhelmed by the presence of the Spirit King.

"It's the god and king of the spirits, you know. Don't you feel a sense of kinship as a fellow spirit?"

,[Kinship? It feels like I'm being crushed to death. When you think about it, I'm an artificial spirit. Do I really have to go to the Spirit King? I don't want to go. I want to go home.]

Spirits are said to feel comfortable in the Spirit Realm, but Makina seems to be an exception. I understand. Makina is an artificial spirit created in a dungeon. Moreover, isn't she a mechanical spirit, far removed from nature?

,'Makina's power seems to have weakened too.'

Spirits become stronger when they are in an environment that suits their attributes. Therefore, Makina, a mechanical spirit, becomes stronger in a civilized, modern world with many machines. Unfortunately, there were no machines in the Spirit Realm.

,'The Spirit King must have noticed that I came to the Spirit Realm. But there's no reaction.'

Is he not interested?

Or is he telling me to do as I please?

,'It's a relief that he's not hostile.'

First, I approach the Spirit King. If the distance gets closer, even the Spirit King won't ignore me. If he ignores me until the end, I was planning to cause a ruckus.

"Kkuuk?!"

Moka, who was flying, tilts her body. An invisible force is pulling her to the ground.

,'Is this gravity? The gravity suddenly got stronger.'

,[What is it!! I want to go home!!]

,'This isn't the time for that, so shut up. Otherwise, I'll leave you here.'

,[Heok! I can't let that happen!! Okay. I'll be quiet! Don't abandon me!!]

,'Shut up, I said.'

,[.......]

I grabbed Moka's back as she was being pulled to the ground and racked my brain. Something strange happened in the Spirit Realm? It's most likely the work of a spirit. But I don't particularly feel hostility.

,'Some of the earth spirits control gravity.'

This isn't an attack, but a call. The reason why the speed of falling to the ground isn't too fast is also because of that.

I looked down at the ground with Spirit Eyes (S+). There was a rock. A rock over 5m was standing alone on the prairie.

My expression hardened. The presence felt from that rock is above that of a highest-grade spirit.

,'Considering that this is the Spirit Realm... is it the highest-grade earth spirit?'

Can I win if I fight? It was hard to gauge because this is the Spirit Realm. No. I didn't come here to fight.

"Moka. Let's go down carefully."

"Kkuuk."

Moka completely changed direction and descended to the ground. Then the pulling gravity disappeared.

The time to descend to the ground was quite long. The distance that Moka would have reached in 10 seconds originally, she couldn't reach even after 30 seconds had passed.

,'...The space is wider than it looks. Is it increasing in real time?'

The concept of space is a mess. Moka's emotions of being flustered were also conveyed to me.

Anyway, I arrived on the ground.

I cautiously tried to get off on the ground, but stopped and sat quietly on Moka. The texture of the ground was strange. It wasn't hard, but squishy. The influence of an unknown space is also affecting the ground.

"Did you call us?"

The rock didn't speak. Instead, it spoke to me with its will.

-Human. Human with the eyes of a spirit. Human that the King is paying attention to.

"Yeah. I'm human. What purpose did you call me for? It's not to fight, is it."

-I heard from the Wind Whale. You have a very delicious stone. I'm curious about the taste. Give it to me.

"Delicious stone? Are you talking about Spirit Stones? If it's the Wind Whale... you must be talking about Arrest."

-That's right. Arrest. That was his name.

Arrest. The highest-grade wind spirit I contracted with.

Considering that wind spirits basically like to wander around, it's not that special. Wind spirits have light mouths as much as their light bodies.

"Spirit Stones are very precious. Are you just asking for that precious thing now? Without any compensation?"

-Hmm. My mistake. What do you want?

I inwardly smirked. This moron is trying to make a deal when he could just threaten me with force. It was a good thing for me.

"Contract with me. Then I'll give you a Spirit Stone."

I take out a Spirit Stone from my inventory.

Kukung.

The rock shook.

-Is that a Spirit Stone. I'm definitely drawn to it instinctively. I don't know about the taste... but I want it.

"If you contract with me, I'll give you three. I'll give you as many as three."

-No. I don't contract with humans. Humans are too impatient. In what they want, and in how they live.

"I won't call you often. You just have to respond to my summons occasionally and help me. As compensation, I'll provide Spirit Stones each time."

-I won't.

The earth spirit was extremely firm. You can tell by listening to its voice. It would refuse even if I gave it 10 Spirit Stones.

That being said, it wasn't good to forcibly make a contract with a spirit. You could rather incur its wrath. Well, it's impossible to forcibly contract with a highest-grade spirit.

"Damn it. Then at least tell me some information. What is the Spirit King doing right now?"

-The King-kkeseo is in that place.

"You're frustrating. What's the reason why the Spirit King is standing still?"

-The King-kkeseo is great, so he can maintain the center just by standing still. Also, just by existing, the power of nature spreads out like roots to all kinds of worlds. That's why spirits are born even in worlds other than the Spirit Realm. That spirit that's with you seems a bit strange though.

"You're talking about Makina. What do you think of Makina? Are you hostile? Do you want to kill her?"

I threw the Spirit Stone. Once you taste a Spirit Stone, you'll want to eat more. I aimed for that point.

"Here, take it."

The thrown Spirit Stone went into the rock's body.

-Ooh.... Is this what they mean by delicious. It's definitely delicious. I want to eat it again.

"If you contract with me, I'll give you three."

-I won't.

You little shit. You're disgustingly firm.

"Then spit out some other helpful information. Something that the Spirit King likes, or something that will help us."

"......."

This bastard, he was pretending to be stupid, but he knows exactly why I came to the Spirit Realm.

"How do I persuade him to lift his curse?"

-I don't know. That's entirely up to you.

-The Thunder Owl can surpass its limits if it has the right qualifications. There is a spring of lightning far away. Immerse yourself in it and accept the essence of lightning. Then you will be able to raise your qualifications.

The Thunder Owl, Moka, is currently a high-grade spirit. The rock told Moka how to rise to the highest grade.

I didn't want to get on the wrong side of a highest-grade spirit for just one Spirit Stone, so I obediently threw the Spirit Stone.

-Hmm. Hmm. Good. Very delicious. I feel fulfilled.

"......."

You unlucky bastard. I cursed inwardly and sent Moka the will to fly. Moka's body rises up.

-I'll tell you one last thing. There are many spirits aiming for Spirit Stones. Some of them are mischievous, so be careful.
