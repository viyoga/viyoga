An Si-hyun took a breath as she saw the six masked figures in front of the dungeon gate. Their red eyes turned towards An Si-hyun.

The association employee who should have been guarding the entrance to the B-grade dungeon gate was bleeding and dead. Judging from the ever-increasing pool of blood, he hadn't been dead for long. They had ambushed and killed the association employee after confirming she was coming.

,An Si-hyun. You ignored his revelation, his solemn command."

One of them, the one who seemed to be the leader, spoke in a solemn voice. There was hostility, but no murderous intent.

,Solemn command my ass. I am not a slave to evil gods like you."

An Si-hyun held a knife in each hand. She tensed her body in anticipation of the battle. Black smoke appeared around her body. The moment they touched this smoke, they would see hallucinations. If her ability activated properly, the battle would be manageable.

,Slaves? We are not his slaves."

,Why? Did I strike a nerve?"

,We are beings less than slaves. We cannot even become his livestock. Compared to him, we are microorganisms, the dust of the universe."

,...I knew they were fanatics, but they're even crazier than I thought."

Whether An Si-hyun muttered or not, he continued.

,Therefore, we must receive his baptism. Only by receiving baptism will our existence have meaning."

,I understand that you guys are crazy. Shall we fight now?"

She had no intention of just enduring while hoping for his or Park Suho's help. The best thing to do was to kill them. Surely, she could do it.

,He does not wish for your death. You still do not know his power. From now on, I will show you his power, a part of it. Witness it clearly. And worship him."

The man stretched his hand to the side. Next to him was a space shimmering in blue, a dungeon gate. Normally, his hand should pass through the dungeon gate as it was. But the man's hand did not pass through. He grabbed the shimmering space and twisted it.

Kkigigigigigigik!

The blue dungeon gate turned into a bloody, ominous color. At the same time, the space around the gate was breaking apart.

An Si-hyun's eyes widened as if they would tear. She sensed it. This dungeon was undergoing a mutation like back then. On top of that, they were forcibly causing a stable dungeon to go berserk and break down. It was a dungeon break.

,No way.... To artificially manipulate a dungeon gate...!"

Dungeon gates are not subject to physical or magical interference. Even the power of an S-grade Hunter doesn't work. There are only two cases where a dungeon gate disappears: dungeon conquest and dungeon break. In the case of a dungeon break, it naturally disappears after some time.

That was common sense. And just now, common sense about dungeons was shattered.

,There is no need to be surprised. This is only a small part of his power."

A lizardman with blood vessels protruding from the broken dungeon gate appeared. A bipedal lizardman similar in size to a human. It was a monster An Si-hyun was seeing for the first time. Judging from the power she felt, it was definitely an A-grade monster.

Blood vessel lizardmen continued to appear from the broken gate. From 5 to 10... It didn't take long to reach 20. Without stopping there, monsters continued to appear.

A sudden A-grade dungeon break.

It was fortunate that it was a sparsely populated area. If this place had been a city, at least thousands of casualties would have occurred.

An Si-hyun's hands trembled.

,I can't win this."

Six A-grade Hunters and dozens of A-grade monsters. They were not fighting each other. The monsters and humans recognized each other as allies.

It was not a force that An Si-hyun could handle alone.

,The best thing to do is to find a gap and run away. With my ability... I can escape...!"

An Si-hyun hadn't just been playing around. She had been training, learning how to use and utilize her ability from Father Geris.

,I can do it. Create an opening, drive the car, and run away with all my might."

She wasn't the type of person with a good enough personality to fight nobly and die. She could endure the humiliation for now. She could get stronger and pay them back later.

An Si-hyun focused her mind and gave strength to her whole body.

,Heup...!"

Black smoke spread in all directions like a burst smoke grenade. A technique that causes temporary mental confusion to all living things that touch the black smoke. Of course, it worked on monsters as well.

However, they stood firm even in the black smoke. Originally, when mental confusion occurs, they would stagger or attack allies. But these guys were fine.

,Kkeureureureu. Kkeureureureureu."

Some of the blood vessel lizardmen even blatantly mocked An Si-hyun.

,An Si-hyun. I know your ability. That is why he values you so highly. But since we have already received his blessing, mental attacks do not work."

An Si-hyun was sweating coldly. Now, all she could trust were the knives in her hands.

,...Blessing? It's not that, it's that you're already crazy so mental attacks don't work!"

,You still don't understand. How foolish."

He grabbed the dungeon gate and twisted it once more.

The gate that was causing the dungeon break changed. It took on the form of a heterogeneous gate that sucked everything in like a black hole and swirled.

,An Si-hyun. Rejoice. He will directly awaken your ignorance."

The blood vessel lizardmen took a step in unison. To grab An Si-hyun and throw her into that unknown gate.

Kureureureung.

A thunderous sound like a beast growling was heard.

Everyone there reflexively looked up at the sky and widened their eyes.

Dark clouds had gathered in the night sky, which had been cloudless until just now. It was not raining. Instead, blue lightning flashed in the dark clouds.

An Si-hyun, as well as the masked men and the blood vessel lizardmen, were on guard.

,Suddenly thunderclouds appeared? This is not a normal situation. ...Could this also be his influence? Oh.... Great God-isiyeo...!"

Lightning struck.

A blue lightning bolt brightly illuminated the surroundings and struck one of the blood vessel lizardmen. The blood vessel lizardman collapsed to the ground, emitting smoke from his entire body, and died.

,......!!"

Everyone was shocked. An A-grade monster dying from a single lightning strike? It was impossible. That was not ordinary lightning from nature.

The masked men, who had been looking at the sky and seeking God, hesitated and watched the thunderclouds. A low rumble of thunder was still heard in the thunderclouds.

Uuuuuuung.

A motorcycle came speeding from the end of the road. An Si-hyun was surprised by the motorcycle coming from behind her, but the motorcycle ignored An Si-hyun and went forward, stopping between An Si-hyun and her enemies. A man wearing a helmet got off the motorcycle.

An Si-hyun stared intently at the man.

,There's no way he would have suddenly appeared here. Could it be him...?! Looking at his height and build, he seems similar to him...."

She couldn't be sure based on height and build alone. She needed other definite evidence.

The relaxed atmosphere he exuded. It was that of a clear strongman. In addition, just his appearance was causing the mana in the atmosphere to tremble.

,Who the hell are you...?!"

The enemy in front of the gate asked warily.

The man took off his helmet. The face that was revealed was surprisingly ordinary. He was wearing gloves on his hands, so she couldn't check.

,Just a Hunter passing by."

One of the masked men, the one working at the association, gasped and was shocked.

,It's Seong Yujin! S-grade candidate Seong Yujin! Why is he here?!"

,There's someone who knows me. I just said it. I was just passing by. As a Hunter, I saw a dungeon break happening, so I couldn't just pass by."

Seong Yujin summoned Hwaryeon Bido and slung it over his shoulder. Conversely, the motorcycle was reverse-summoned and disappeared. Seong Yujin leisurely headed towards the enemies.

The masked man leader regained his composure. He felt relieved now that he knew at least roughly who the enemy was.

,Even if he is an S-grade Hunter candidate, he is not an S-grade Hunter now. Attack! Let that guy know his place!"

The blood vessel lizardmen gave strength to their whole bodies and kicked off the ground.

Seong Yujin flicked his hand as if he was annoyed.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwang!!

Dozens of lightning bolts fell on the blood vessel lizardmen. The blood vessel lizardmen who were hit by one or more lightning bolts died on the spot.

,......!!"

The masked men widened their eyes in disbelief and froze.

Dozens of A-grade monsters being swept away at once? The only beings capable of such a thing were S-grade Hunters.

A walking natural disaster.

Guardian of humanity.

The pinnacle of Hunters.

,Such a being, an S-grade Hunter who has not yet been officially recognized, suddenly appeared? How can this be so absurd...! God-isiyeo...! Is this also your trial?!"

Whether the fanatics were seeking God or not, Seong Yujin swung his knife horizontally. Sword energy wrapped in red lightning flew and aimed for the masked men's knees. Three of the masked men jumped in place and avoided the sword energy, but the remaining half could not avoid it and had their knees cut off.

,I'm curious about the power that forcibly causes a dungeon break, so I won't kill you."

Seong Yujin swung his knife again. The same sword energy as before flew in the same trajectory. The three masked men were very nervous, so they were able to avoid it this time as well.

But thanks to being distracted by the sword energy, they could not avoid the chains of blue electricity that were being fired from all directions.

Thunder Heaven Style Lightning Art Lightning Shackles.

All of the masked men, including the ones lying on the ground, were caught in chains of lightning. When they tried to move forcibly, the chains of lightning flashed and electrocuted them.

Seong Yujin leisurely approached the masked men.

,Nothing special. But first, should I cut off your useless limbs for sure?"

A monster was approaching.

The baptism candidates struggled to break free from the chains of lightning, but the more they did, the more electricity flowed into their bodies, causing only pain. The pain of electrocution was difficult to bear even with the power of faith.

,Kkeueueueueu... God-isiyeo...!!"

One of the masked men squeezed out his strength to the limit and exploded. A strong wave occurred and offset the chains of lightning. In return, he collapsed and died, bleeding.

Seong Yujin used Instantaneous Step and Swift Lightning to rush forward and subdue the masked men in the blink of an eye. Except for one person, the leader who was right in front of the gate. As soon as the chains of lightning disappeared, the leader threw himself into the heterogeneous gate.

,He should have just given up. He's being annoying."

Seong Yujin stared blankly at the gate. Should he go inside? It was clear that it was not an ordinary dungeon. If he went in, could he come back?

,Evil God Brahmasen. Is it a trap that guy made? No, it doesn't feel like that. It feels like they arbitrarily modified a dungeon. Something prepared to make An Si-hyun his follower. The essence is a dungeon. If it's a dungeon, I can get out."

It was certain that the evil god Brahmasen was a guy with tremendous power. But this was Earth. If Brahmasen could exert his power, Earth would have already been destroyed. Even if he used a dungeon, that restriction would not disappear.

,Shall I check it out? If things go wrong, I can just grind a bit with the Leisure Life App."

Seong Yujin reached out his hand to the swirling dungeon like a black hole.

,Wait a minute! Going into a dungeon you don't even know the identity of is suicide!"

An Si-hyun shouted. Seong Yujin briefly turned to look at An Si-hyun and chuckled.

,I'm okay. Just watch over the ones lying here."

Three of the masked men were swept away by the wave and died, and two were unconscious and not moving. Judging from the blood flowing from their mouths, noses, and ears, even if they regained consciousness, they would not be able to use their strength right away.

Seong Yujin entered the gate without even hearing An Si-hyun's answer.
