,I guess the rumor that the Son of Thunder entered this time is true. If he's Seong Hari-nim's son, I wonder how amazing he'll be.... I'm already looking forward to it. Jong-hoon-ssi, do you know anything about the Son of Thunder?

Rumors that Seong Hari had a son had been circulating for a long time. However, the Hero Association did not disclose information related to Seong Hari, and the clans were afraid of getting involved with Seong Hari for no reason, so they kept a low profile. As a result, Yang Eun-ah also did not know about Seong Hari's son.

,I don't know. I've only heard rumors. As a fellow scout, let me give you some advice. Seong Hari is a bomb. If you touch her carelessly, you won't be able to turn back.

,That's Seong Hari's charm. A hero with such unique power that even a giant clan can't easily touch her.... As a woman..., no, as a hero, I can't help but admire Seong Hari.

Yang Eun-ah's eyes sparkled.

Seong Hari had many fans, knowingly or unknowingly. They were drawn to her overwhelming power. Yang Eun-ah was one of her fans who was drawn to Seong Hari's power. Yang Eun-ah felt like she wanted to fly to Seong Hari right now and ask for her autograph.

,Hoo. She doesn't know how scary that woman is.

,Scary? She's just beautiful.

Seong Hari was young for her age. She had beauty that would make you believe she was in her early 20s. Time, which should be equal to everyone, seemed to favor only her.

But it wasn't strange. The higher the hero rank, the stronger the power, the slower the aging. Depending on the characteristics or skills, there are cases where aging does not progress at all.

,Jong-hoon-ssi, you've worked with Seong Hari before, right?

,It was when I hadn't been active as a hero for long. It's an old story.

,I'm jealous that you worked with Seong Hari.

,There's nothing to be jealous about. That's all I worked for. The difference in skill was so great that it was hard to keep up. And I still remember it. The sight of her destroying the Gaonnuri Clan, which was one of the giant clans at the time.

,Ah. I know about that too. The association is trying to hide it, but everyone who knows knows about it.

,Don't go around telling people. The association won't stand still.

,I know the difference between what I should say and what I shouldn't say. More than that, Seong Hari's son. Do you know who it is? If he's Seong Hari's son, I think he'll be in the top 30 at least.

Yang Eun-ah picked up the list and scanned it. It was the ranking of the 37th entrance class of Maruhan Academy. The names from Ryu Hana, who was ranked 1st, to the last place were all written down.

,Among the students in the top 30, the identities of the parents of about 20 people are clearly known. Since he's a son, excluding the female students... 7 people..

,Just because Seong Hari is a monster doesn't mean her son is a monster. Prejudice is not good.

,Well, my gut tells me it's right. Ah, could Kim Cheon-woo, who is the runner-up, be Seong Hari's son?

,Kim Cheon-woo is an orphan.

,Actually, Kim Cheon-woo's parents were Seong Hari.

,That's the least funny joke I've heard this year.

Yang Eun-ah gave a bitter smile. She also knew that Kim Cheon-woo was not Seong Hari's son.

,Let's be serious. The most likely is Seong Yujin.

,Because his last name is Seong?

,Yes. Seong Hari's family is the Jinryeong Seong family. It's not strange to give her son her last name.

,Well, we'll find out if we investigate.

Yang Eun-ah watched Seong Hari. Seong Hari was sitting in the parents' chair, quietly watching the TV screen. She didn't care at all even though the surroundings were noisy. Those who knew about Seong Hari hurriedly gathered around her. The reporters were also watching Seong Hari in the same way. The reason why they couldn't dare to approach her was probably because of the sense of intimidation she gave off.

,Jong-hoon-ssi, aren't you going down?

,...I have to go. Even if I don't want to meet her, it's my job.

Bi Jong-hoon went down with powerless steps. Yang Eun-ah followed Bi Jong-hoon.

•••

Seong Hari, who was sitting in the parents' seat, stared blankly at the screen.

Old memories come to mind. It's the memory of when she entered the academy. At that time, the entrance ceremony was held on the playground, not in this auditorium. The principal who went on stage was not Kang Ji-young either.

,That old man was long-winded about history and stuff, but Ji-young is super short.

The representative of the new students came up on stage. She was a beauty with light purple hair. Soon, the clear voice of the representative of the new students was heard through the speaker.

,She's the successor of the North Diamond Clan. Hmm.

Seong Hari crossed her arms. She thought of her son, Seong Yujin. Seong Yujin was ranked 5th. It was also a great result, but the regret that Seong Yujin would have been the valedictorian if he had tried a little harder did not disappear.

,If Yujin had only studied, he would have been the valedictorian.

Seong Hari believed in Seong Yujin.

,Our Yujin is smart, but he doesn't study. If he had only studied, it would have been easy to get a perfect score on the written test.

The entrance ceremony ended with Kang Ji-young's last request. The students return to their classes according to the teacher's instructions.

,Check the dormitory where Yujin will be staying... take a commemorative photo and then go back.

It was the moment Seong Hari was about to get up. Someone approached her. She was a woman in an elegant and flashy outfit that would make Cheongdam-dong's daughters-in-law go away.

,Seong Hari. It's been a while. I never thought I'd meet you here. It's a long life. Don't you think?

She was a beauty. Luxurious black bobbed hair, red lips, calm eyes. A body that goes in when it goes in and comes out when it comes out. The black mink coat suited her very well.

Seong Hari immediately recognized who she was.

,Choi Jeong-hwa. Why are you here?

The head of the Golden Tree Clan and the daughter of the chairman of the Geumhwa Group. A second-generation chaebol. A-class hero. She was Seong Hari's classmate at Maruhan Academy, along with Kang Ji-young. But they weren't close enough to be called friends. It's been a few years since they've met like this.

,Oh my. There's only one reason why I'm here. It's the same reason as you. My daughter entered the academy. By the way, my daughter's entrance ranking is 4th, what's your son's ranking?

The classmate she met after a long time was so childish that she couldn't help but laugh. She must be asking even though she knows who her son is and what his ranking is. It was a childish act. But for some reason, Seong Hari felt like her insides were boiling.

,...5th.

,Ah, is that so. He's right below my daughter. Your son has quite outstanding skills? Hehehe.

Choi Jeong-hwa covers her mouth with her hand and laughs. Seong Hari clenched her fist without realizing it. She wanted to hit her. But she soon released her fist. She's past the age of grabbing hair and fighting like in the old days.

,You know that entrance ranking doesn't mean much, so why are you saying that?

The new students of Maruhan Academy are unpolished gems. The academy's curriculum will hone the talents of the new students and make them shine brilliantly. Even if the entrance ranking is the last place, they can graduate as the valedictorian. Such a thing has actually happened.

,Humph. It's not like that happens easily. If there are no exceptions, the entrance ranking will be the graduation ranking. You don't know because you didn't graduate from the academy.

Choi Jeong-hwa turned her body back.

,There are too many eyes here to talk to you. See you later. Seong Hari. If we have a chance to see each other later.

,Wait a minute.

Seong Hari grabbed Choi Jeong-hwa's shoulder and stopped her.

,What is it? Are you not going to take this hand off?

,Don't react so sharply. I don't want to fight with you. I have a proposal for you.

,...A proposal? I'll listen. Tell me.

,I'm going to create a parents' association for Maruhan Academy this time. Let's do it together.

,.......

Choi Jeong-hwa tried to laugh as if she was dumbfounded, but soon she became serious and pondered. The accident at the American academy that she had seen a while ago came to her mind. The horrific incident in which an academy teacher killed 12 students and ran away.

Academies, by their nature, cannot be interfered with from the outside. But if a parents' association is created, the story changes.

,A parents' association. That's a good idea. But... do you think a parents' association can be created?

,There are parents' associations in universities too. It's not a strange thing. And the academy is also a school after all. There's no reason why a parents' association can't be created. Especially if it's you and me.

Seong Hari, an S-class hero and once called the strongest hero.

Choi Jeong-hwa, the head of the Golden Tree Clan and the daughter of the chairman of the Geumhwa Group.

It's hard to ignore even one person, but two people have gathered. The association, as well as the academy, will not be able to easily ignore the demands of these two.

,How much has the work progressed? You're not just butting your head against the ground, are you?

,I'm going to meet Ji-young now. I have to settle things there.

,Ha, you're butting your head against the ground. I don't like it, but it's for Da-yeon....

They headed to the door side by side. But the people who were watching around them rushed in as if it was an opportunity and blocked their way.

,I'm Oh Gu-seong, a reporter from Shinju Ilbo! Seong Hari-ssi, can I have an interview? It'll only take a moment! Who is your son who entered this time?!

,I'm Kim Ye-rin, a reporter from the hero magazine, The Savior! Seong Hari-ssi. You've shown yourself after a long time, what have you been doing? What is your relationship with Choi Jeong-hwa-ssi of the Golden Tree Clan?

,Seong Hari-nim. Hello. I'm Park Gi-soo, a scout from the Wolf Run Clan. I'd like to talk to you about your son. Our clan can promise the best support!

,I'm Hwang Hee-cheol from the Sky Whale Clan! Please accept my business card here! I'd like to have a calm conversation later!

Seong Hari didn't even pretend to listen and ignored them all and moved forward. The people who were suppressed by Seong Hari's momentum couldn't dare to block her way.

,You're very popular.

Choi Jeong-hwa sneered. The reporters also clung to her. Of course, she ignored all the reporters.

Seong Hari's steps stopped. It was because a person she knew appeared.

,...Seong Hari-ssi. Do you remember me?

,Bi Jong-hoon. Aren't you that coward from back then?

,Haha.... I'm honored that you remember me. I'm working as a scout for the Black Snow Clan. Seong Hari-ssi hasn't changed since then or now.

Bi Jong-hoon scratched his cheek and said.

,My son will decide which clan my son will join. I have no intention of interfering. And it's only the entrance ceremony now. It's too early to decide on a clan.

,Yes. Yes. I know that too. But from the clan's point of view, it can't be helped. In this industry, if you're late, you won't get anything. I won't say much. Won't you at least accept this business card?

,...If it's just one business card.

Seong Hari accepted the black business card he handed over and put it in her pocket. At that time, a young woman with a ponytail next to Bi Jong-hoon popped out.

,Hello! Seong Hari-nim! I'm a really big fan! I'm Yang Eun-ah, a scout from the Glass Lantern Clan! Ah, this is my business card. Please accept it!

Before Seong Hari could open her mouth, Yang Eun-ah quickly put her business card on Seong Hari's hand. Seong Hari, who had been contemplating for a while, accepted the business card. She liked Yang Eun-ah's friendly first impression, and the Glass Lantern Clan is one of the leading clans in Korea. Seong Yujin might be interested.

Seong Hari went outside and walked down the hallway. A cool March breeze blows through the open window. The reporters clung to her from behind, but they were immediately subdued by the academy staff.

,It's good to be so popular.

Choi Jeong-hwa said.

,Unlike you, I'm just annoyed.

,Hmm. Your son, you said he's ranked 5th? He's qualified to join our clan.

Seong Hari's eyes became sharp. She glared at Choi Jeong-hwa and said.

,Don't flirt with my son.

,Fli, flirt? Are you going to talk to me like that?! Don't you know that your body isn't the same as it used to be!

,...Are you crazy? Are you really trying to start something?! Don't you know that my body isn't the same as it used to be!

,Try it. Even if I'm weaker than before, I can still beat the Golden Tree Clan.

Seong Hari raised her right hand. Choi Jeong-hwa's complexion turned slightly pale when she saw that slender hand. Old memories come to mind. Her hair was grabbed by that right hand.

,...I, I don't waste time. My 1 second is more expensive than your 1 second. I'm enduring it because I don't want to lose.

,Hoot.

Seong Hari lowered her right hand. Choi Jeong-hwa, who was relieved without realizing it, gritted her teeth.
