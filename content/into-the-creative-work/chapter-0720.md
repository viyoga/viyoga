"Cheating will absolutely not be tolerated. The moment cheating is detected, you will be immediately disqualified. The use of mana is also strictly prohibited. The moment you move even a little mana, it will be considered cheating. No other means are allowed either. For your information, a barrier is installed in this place. The barrier is sensitive to any kind of cheating. If you're confident you can cheat without getting caught, go ahead and try."

The male supervisor of the written exam said with a stiff face. There were more than five supervisors in the written exam room. With a barrier installed, it was impossible to summon Mocha or Machina to cheat.

"Begin."

I checked the exam paper.

I nodded, looking at the black ink densely written on the exam paper.

The hand holding the pen moved, filling in the answers on the OMR card.

I started guessing from the first question.

'The ratio of practical to written is 8:2. Even if I screw up the written exam, I can pass if I do well on the practical.'

I glanced at the last page of the exam paper and saw that there were a total of 40 questions. Roughly 2.5 points per question. Among them, 30 were multiple choice. If I think positively, there's no way I'll get 0 points.

'I still don't understand even if I read the question again.'

The monsters in this world were a little different from the monsters in reality. The original game's settings? I didn't have time to memorize even the minor settings about the monsters.

'Wait. I've been studying steadily, haven't I? ...Not me, but the auto-progress avatar.'

If I use auto-progress, I might be able to get a good score on the written exam.

I had submitted my smartphone before the exam, so it wasn't in my possession. But it didn't matter. Even without a smartphone, I could end the game whenever I wanted. The Game Life app was manifested through the smartphone, but it was clearly my ability.

'Game End.'

I used auto-progress in reality. But, contrary to my expectations, the avatar was stupid. After quickly looking at the exam paper, it started rolling the pen on the desk to guess the answers. It acted as if it had never studied.

'This bastard. Did you forget everything you studied with auto-progress?'

I clicked my tongue and went back into the game world. I guessed without even looking at the questions. I roughly wrote the answers for the short answer questions. After finishing the written exam in 5 minutes, I slammed my head on the desk and slept.

•••

Hong Ji-wook, an A-class Hero who helps the Korean Hero Association with the Academy exam proctoring every year, maintained a poker face and looked around inside the written exam room.

You can recognize a promising tree from its sprouts. Some examinees who seemed likely to stand out at the Academy caught his eye.

'The most outstanding is definitely Ryu Hana-gun. As the successor of the North Diamond Clan, the written exam must be a joke for her.'

The rumor that the successor of the North Diamond Clan was a genius was an open secret among Heroes. But he couldn't be sure that she would be the top student.

'There's talk that this class is a golden generation, with many noticeable geniuses gathering from all over the country. No matter how good Ryu Hana is, it's hard to guarantee she'll be the top student.'

Ryu Hana was calmly solving the exam paper with a expressionless face. The pen in her hand was moving more than it was still. It meant that she was deriving the correct answer as soon as she read the question.

'It's not even an operator written exam, so Ryu Hana should get a perfect score.'

Hong Ji-wook's gaze turned to the back of Ryu Hana. There was only one examinee who was moving their pen faster than Ryu Hana.

'He's marking the OMR card without even looking at the questions.'

Hong Ji-wook was dumbfounded. It was a style he was seeing for the first time in his five years as a proctor. Most examinees do their best to solve the questions in order to get even one more question right, even if they are not confident in the written exam. Because admission to Maruhan Academy is a turning point in life.

In addition, Maruhan Academy offers various benefits depending on the ranking.

The examinee who had finished marking all the answers immediately fell asleep on the desk.

'What is he doing? Did he give up on entering the Academy?'

It was a type he was seeing for the first time in five years. Feeling curious, Hong Ji-wook got up from his seat and slowly walked around the inside of the classroom, looking at the examinees' answer sheets.

All the questions were related to Hero activities. As a veteran, he could naturally see the answers.

'This guy has studied a bit. The short answer questions are also correct. Does he have a Hero in his family? And this guy.... He's working hard, but his grades aren't good.'

He also checked Ryu Hana's answer sheet.

'Perfect. There's nothing to criticize even in the short answer questions. Even her handwriting is excellent.'

He then approached the examinee who was sleeping on the desk. When he saw the OMR card sticking out next to his wrist, he almost burst out laughing.

'He guessed all the multiple-choice questions, and more than 60% of them are correct. Could it be that he didn't guess, but solved the problems?'

To get this score by guessing. Just how lucky is he?

He then looked at the short answer questions.

(Question 22.

You are isolated in situation A. A group of C monsters is approaching. The operator has instructed you to stop the C monster group. What will you do?

Answer: Order difference! The operator is so bad. What were you doing until it got to this point? Even my neighborhood dog would give better orders than you!)

Hong Ji-wook almost chuckled at the answer.

'He's crazy. But I understand. Operators sometimes give incomprehensible orders.'

(Question 34.

An A-class monster, Acid Blow, has appeared in front of you. You are a C-class Hero. You need to buy time for 10 minutes until support arrives. What will you do?

Answer: Call Mom.)

If you aim for the weaknesses, even a C-class Hero can easily buy time for 10 minutes against Acid Blow.

'Call Mom, huh. Was he a mama's boy, unlike what I saw? He seems a bit lacking, but he's an interesting guy. Let's see, his name is... Seong Yujin?'

Hong Ji-wook paused. It was a name he had heard recently. The name of the son of Seong Hari, the Thunder of Gwancheon.

He widened his eyes.

'I heard rumors that Seong Hari's son was entering the Academy. But could it be this guy? ......Is it just someone with the same name?'

There was no way that the son of Seong Hari, Korea's S-class Hero and a legend, could be a guy like this. Hong Ji-wook wanted to believe that.

•••

The results of the written exam came out in 10 minutes. They were posted on the whiteboard with rankings. The examinees flocked to the front.

I got 33 points. Thanks to the karma, I luckily got more than 60% of the multiple-choice questions I guessed right. But the reason why my score didn't exceed 40 points was because there were many points allocated to the short answer questions.

My ranking was 61st out of 70 people. There were a whopping 9 people below me.

'They're worse than me who guessed. Are they retards?'

There were 4 people in first place. All of them got perfect scores. Of course, Ryu Hana's name was among the 4.

-You have worked hard on the written exam. After a 1-hour lunch break, the practical exam will begin at 1 PM. Please gather at the East Auditorium by 1 PM. I will repeat. 1-hour lunch break....

"Hey. You're Ryu Hana of North Diamond, right? I'm Seok Dong-min of Taecheonbo. It's a coincidence, so why don't we have lunch together? There are other famous clan members too."

A man was hitting on Ryu Hana. He was decent looking. He must be quite confident in his face and skills, but his approach was wrong. He shouldn't approach Ryu Hana directly when he doesn't even know her.

"Famous clan? I'm sorry, but I'm hearing the name Taecheonbo for the first time today. And I have a lunch appointment, so goodbye."

The man froze. Ryu Hana left without looking back.

•••

There are three things that the supervisors look at in the practical exam.

Talent, teamwork, and mentality.

Therefore, examinees must take two practical exams.

One is individual combat. The other is team combat.

I was confident in either one. Because in the end, it's all about hunting monsters quickly.

The test monster was a gnoll. Stronger than a goblin, weaker than an orc. And their appearance is almost identical to humans, except for the dog head. At first glance, they look more like people than monsters.

'The Academy intentionally chose gnolls as the test monster to check the examinees' mentality. Because Heroes have to deal not only with monsters but also with criminals called villains.'

The door to the waiting room opened, and the examinees who had taken the test before me entered.

Including Ryu Hana, there were a total of 5 people.

Ryu Hana still walked expressionlessly to her seat and fiddled with her smartphone. When I glanced at it, she was watching a monster lecture video. The other 4 were not in good condition. 2 had stiff faces, and the other 2 were trembling slightly.

-Team 2, please enter.

It's my turn.

•••

Hong Ji-wook, who was in the control room, was surprised and stood up from his seat when he saw the person who opened the door and entered.

"D, Director Kang Ji-young-nim! What brings you here?!"

"I had some time, so I came to check. Don't worry, I'm not going to blame you."

Kang Ji-young sat in an empty chair and looked at the monitors. There were more than 20 monitors, each showing 5 examinees from different angles. And numbers were displayed at the bottom of the monitors. It was a stress index based on the association's data.

"Did you really just come?"

"That's an unpleasant thing to say. Do you think I'm going to commit fraud or something?"

"Ah, no. That's not it. Didn't the successor of North Diamond, who is rumored to be a genius, come here? I thought you came to see her."

"Ryu Hana, you mean? She's a talented child. She might become an S-class Hunter in the distant future."

Hong Ji-wook remembered Kang Ji-young's words.

Kang Ji-young, who is called the Pioneer of Prophecy, has an excellent eye for seeing and judging talent. The Heroes that Kang Ji-young judged would do well really did well, and the Heroes she said were dangerous didn't last long and either quit being Heroes or died.

People who know about Kang Ji-young also call her a physiognomist.

"...It seems you didn't come to see Ryu Hana. Did you perhaps come to see Seong Hari-nim's son? I thought it was just someone with the same name, but seeing Director Kang Ji-young-nim come in person... It seems he really is Seong Hari-nim's son."

"...Yes. I came to see how Seong Yujin is doing and make a judgment."

"You came to make a judgment.... Have you perhaps met him separately?"

"I've already met him several times. But every time, I didn't know. In my opinion, that Seong Yujin has no talent. His spirit eye is excellent, but everything else is lacking. Jinryeong Seongga is placing high expectations on Seong Yujin, but in my opinion, Seong Yujin will have a hard time being more than a first-class spirit master."

"A first-class spirit master is great enough...."

"That's not all. That child's very existence feels like it's beyond common sense."

"...I don't know. Ah, the field supervisors have started the exam."

The examinees fought the gnolls. The average combat time was 3 minutes. The examinees gave it their all. Because if they lost here, they would be immediately disqualified.

"This class of examinees is excellent."

"Yes. Ryu Hana is especially overwhelming. She killed the gnoll in 27 seconds."

"What's the average stress index?"

"The average is 30. It's at the level of an active Hero. However, the stress index briefly soared to 200 when she killed the gnoll."

"Seeing the stress index drop immediately, it seems she has trained her composure. As expected of that woman's daughter."

"It's not just her composure, but her skills are also amazing. With that level of skill, she's sure to be the top student."

"It's too early to judge that yet."

The examinees returned to the waiting room, and the next examinees entered the exam hall.

Seong Yujin was there.

Kang Ji-young, resting her chin on her hand with her elbow on the chair, stared intently at the monitor showing Seong Yujin.

The battle began.

Lightning struck the gnoll. The monitor flashed brightly and then returned to normal.

Seong Yujin's battle ended in 1 second. 1.394 seconds to be exact.

"Crazy! 1 second! Isn't that the best ever? No, before that, there's no way a gnoll could die from a single lightning strike. Something's wrong...."

"No. There's no cheating. It just wasn't ordinary lightning. More than that, I'm more concerned about the stress index."

"The stress index is... 0?! Doesn't that mean he didn't feel anything even while killing the gnoll?!"

"Yes. He killed the gnoll as naturally as breathing, like an ant he carelessly stepped on while walking."
