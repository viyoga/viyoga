Dojin looked up at the sky. He could see hundreds of ghosts.

It's not like the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons only has exactly one hundred ghosts. The term "Night Parade of One Hundred Demons" is more symbolic. The actual number of ghosts could be hundreds or thousands.

,Estimating roughly, there are over 300.

He wasn't afraid. Rather, he was excited.

The concentrated ghost energy, sealed in a jar for 60 years, writhes. With this excellent body and the True Ghost Sword, he could kill hundreds of ghosts alone.

Dojin burned with fighting spirit. However, he didn't charge recklessly like a wild boar. The sky was wide open. It would be the perfect situation to be surrounded and attacked by enemies. Even if he was excited, he wouldn't do anything stupid. And anyway, those bastards would come down to the ground to kill him.

,Rakshasa Demon.

Someone called him.

The ghosts of the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons retreated, watching someone's expression. An old man wearing a kimono stepped forward. The monster, with a head so disproportionately large that he couldn't be considered human, possessed an enormous amount of ghost energy, contrary to his appearance.

Dojin immediately recognized that the old man was Nurarihyon, the leader of the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons.

,I heard rumors of a Rakshasa Demon rampaging on this peninsula decades ago. It seems you are the protagonist of those rumors.

,That's right. I am the Rakshasa Demon, Dojin.

Their conversation was neither Japanese nor Korean. A language that exists in this world, but cannot be used by humans. It was the language of ghosts. Seong Yujin, who was possessed by a ghost, could understand their conversation.

,I've heard rumors about you for hundreds of years. Why have you come to the peninsula when you're based in the archipelago?

,We have come to kill the human you are possessing.

,Is it a grudge?

,No. We have no relationship with that human. This is actually the first time we've seen him. We simply judged that the human is dangerous.

,Kuhahaha. So, is that it? Nip it in the bud? To be afraid of just one human... the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons has really gone downhill.

,...We have a ghost who can predict the future on our side. He said that if that human is left alone, thousands, tens of thousands of ghosts could be harmed. And that human has already exorcised dozens of ghosts in the past three days. He has accomplished something that even a first-rate exorcist would find impossible. That human must die here.

Dojin let out an exclamation at Nurarihyon's attitude, which was full of hostility and vigilance.

,I knew he wasn't an ordinary human, but I never imagined he would be this much.

,Rakshasa Demon, come out of that human's body. We and you are the same ghosts. There's no need to point swords at each other. I'll forgive you for cutting down the Oni. You didn't know the circumstances, and the Oni was too hasty.

,I refuse. This body is mine now. You said there's a prophet among you? Predict again. As long as I occupy this body... the things you're talking about will not happen.

Nurarihyon crossed his arms. He looked back at the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons.

,Kudan.

Among the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons, a ghost with the body of a cow and the face of a human stepped forward. It was a monster with the ability to predict the future.

,Has the future changed?

Kudan slowly looked around and slowly replied.

,It has not changed.... That human will become a disaster for ghosts....

Dojin burst into a hollow laugh.

,That's absurd. You call that a prediction? That's just nonsense.

,Rakshasa Demon, Kudan's predictions have a success rate of over 90%.

,So, 10% is bullshit.

,The 90% possibility cannot be ignored. Rakshasa Demon, come out of that human's body. We have no intention of confronting you, who are the same ghost as us.

Dojin raised the True Ghost Sword and pointed it at Nurarihyon.

,How many times do I have to say it? This body is mine now.

,...You're not making any sense. Abandon that body. You can always get another human body, can't you?

,Hahaha. You don't know anything. Finding a body with such good compatibility is impossible even after decades or hundreds of years.

,I can't understand. You're supposed to be a ghost, so why are you so obsessed with a human body?

,The sensation of being alive and breathing is like a drug. Once you taste it, you can never quit.

Nurarihyon frowned.

He raised his right hand. The ghosts belonging to the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons emitted ghost energy and stirred up their fighting spirit. Dojin grinned. His body, facing the battle, trembled with excitement.

,I have kept my duty as a ghost to you. If you like that human body so much... die with that human.

Three ghosts shot out like arrows from the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons.

A Daitengu with a long nose and black wings.

An Inugami with the face of a dog.

A Gashadokuro, a giant skeleton.

The strongest among the Night Parade of One Hundred Demons rushed at Dojin as if they had been waiting for this moment.

,Rakshasa Demon Dojin! I've been wondering how strong you are since I heard your name!

,I'll devour you and become stronger.

,Hehehe, hehehe.... This one is my prey...!

Dojin scoffed at them and swung the True Ghost Sword mightily.

,You petty ghosts.... If it were the old days, you wouldn't even be able to see my shadow. Die!

A giant sword energy, formed by compressed ghost energy, flew towards the three monsters. However, the monsters easily avoided Dojin's sword energy. The Daitengu flew upwards, the Inugami ran along the ground, and the Gashadokuro jumped lightly, despite its massive body.

Nurarihyon smiled and said to the flustered Dojin.

,Rakshasa Demon. The peninsula and the archipelago are different. Unlike the peninsula, where exorcists are strong, the archipelago is the land of ghosts. There were too many ghosts. That's why territorial disputes between ghosts were always happening. You are on a different level from the ghosts of the peninsula, who are steeped in peace!

The moment Nurarihyon finished speaking, the Daitengu, who had flown on the wind, took Dojin from behind. His fist struck Dojin's back. Dojin allowed the attack but counterattacked. The True Ghost Sword cut off the Daitengu's right arm.

,Hehehe....

From above, the Gashadokuro's giant bone hand pressed down on Dojin. Dojin moved his legs violently without a moment to catch his breath, avoiding the Gashadokuro's hand. He climbed up the arm in reverse and stabbed the Gashadokuro's skull forehead with his sword.

,I am the Rakshasa Demon Dojin! Starting with this fucking big skeleton, I'll cut down and kill all of you!

Dojin shouted boldly and tried to cut off the Gashadokuro's head. However, the sword stuck in its forehead barely moved. The Gashadokuro's durability was beyond imagination.

-What are you doing, you idiot? It's coming from below.

At Seong Yujin's voice, he urgently lowered his gaze. The Inugami was jumping up, opening its long snout. Dojin used ghost energy. Ghost energy enveloped his body. Immediately after, his body reappeared on the ground. It was Dojin's secret technique, Chukji (earth shrinking).

Dojin kicked off the ground and aimed for the defenseless Daitengu's back. The Daitengu took out a feather fan from his chest and waved it. The wind struck Dojin's body. Small wounds appeared on Dojin's body from the blades of wind. His skin tore, and drops of blood fell to the ground.

,Keeeuk....

Dojin, who had lost a considerable amount of blood, staggered.

,Rakshasa Demon, you are strong. But you are too ignorant. Is it the difference of decades? Your movements are not refined. I don't know about 1-on-1, but 1-on-many doesn't seem to suit you.

Nurarihyon's voice touched Dojin's temper.

,Shut up! After I cut down these bastards, I'll cut off your head too!

,Hoo. Foolish thing.... Your downfall is that you are alone. If you abandon that body now and bow your head to me... I will give you the opportunity to join my Night Parade of One Hundred Demons.

,Your Night Parade of One Hundred Demons ends here. I will end it!

Nurarihyon shook his head. When he raised his left hand, countless ghosts who had been watching the situation from the sky fell towards Dojin all at once.

,Kehehe! Kill the Rakshasa Demon!

,If we kill the Rakshasa Demon, we can become executives!

,Weak Rakshasa Demon!

Dojin distorted his face and swung the True Ghost Sword.

,You fucking petty ghosts...!

The weak ghosts were swept away by the True Ghost Sword without even being able to counterattack. Nevertheless, the ghosts did not retreat and clung to Dojin. Dojin opened his mouth wide and swallowed the rushing petty ghosts.

Nurarihyon stroked his chin as he watched Dojin's battle.

,Oho. A Rakshasa who eats people eats ghosts to recover ghost energy and stamina.... Amazing. He is truly a fighting god. But he cannot stand against my Night Parade of One Hundred Demons.

Dojin continued to swing his sword at the ghosts. The petty ghosts could not avoid his sword and died. But the problem was the real ones mixed in with the petty ghosts. The strong ones that Dojin couldn't ignore attacked Dojin, mixed in with the petty ghost crowd.

,Keueueueuk!

A Kappa's headbutt struck Dojin's abdomen. Dojin, who flew straight back, hit a rock and fell to the ground.

-What are you doing? You were so confident, but you're getting completely fucked. If that's the case, hand over your body to me. I'll show you exactly what swordsmanship is.

,Shut up.... What can you do, you human... This body is mine.

Dojin forced himself to get up. The body that had undergone three rebirths had already recovered most of its injuries, and he could steal the lacking ghost energy by chewing and eating petty ghosts. He could still fight much more.

Pudeudeudeuk.

Rotten tree roots sprouted from under his feet and wrapped around Dojin's body. Dojin was flustered. It was impossible to retreat using Chukji. To use Chukji, he had to move his feet first.

,Kehehehe! Now's the time! Kill him!

A nearby tree shouted, bursting into vulgar laughter. It was a ghost tree that had been hiding its appearance as a tree.

,This is a damn mess....

Dozens of petty ghosts rushed towards Dojin at the same time.

-Do you want power?

The human in his head started spouting nonsense. Annoyed, Dojin tried to shout. He tried to burst out ghost energy and get rid of the petty ghosts clinging to him at once. But his body didn't move as he wanted.

,I haven't suffered that much damage yet! There's no reason why my body shouldn't move....

-You're a fucking idiot. You really can't fight. I can't take it anymore. Give me my body back.

The initiative of the body was being transferred to Seong Yujin.

,That bastard.... Could it be that he had the ability to take the initiative from the beginning, but he was handing it over to me?! Why?!

Seong Yujin did not answer his question, even though he was listening to Dojin's thoughts.

,Don't do it, Seong Yujin! You can't handle ghost energy! Even if you take the initiative of the body in this state, you'll just be killed by those petty ghosts! Do you want a dog's death?!

-You're the idiot. It's the opposite. I'm stepping in because it looks like you're going to die.

Dojin resisted to the end, but the initiative of the body eventually passed to Seong Yujin.

Pajik. Pajijik.

Ghost energy turned into brain energy, and blue sparks flew.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Barrier Maximum Output.

A circular electromagnetic barrier made of lightning temporarily grew larger and pushed away the clinging petty ghosts all at once. Most of the pushed-away petty ghosts were electrocuted and died. Seong Yujin personally swung his sword at the surviving petty ghosts.

The petty ghosts were cut down without being able to do anything by the slashes filled with experience that could not be compared to Dojin's.

,Good. It's much better than my real body.

Seong Yujin grinned as he looked at the ghosts surrounding him.
