That was the number of soldiers I killed by the time I reached the lab entrance. Two of them were instructors. I had no choice but to use magic against the instructors. If I hadn't been quick, I would have struggled quite a bit.

More than half of the soldiers I killed were faces I knew. But even killing them directly didn't stir any emotion in me. I was a little surprised by my own ruthlessness.

At the lab entrance, I ran into another familiar face.

A sturdy man with a lazy left eye. Room 202, who used the same dormitory as me.

His face was twisted in anger. I could see anger and betrayal.

,Room 211! I never dreamed you'd do this! Why the hell did you betray us?!"

Room 202 shouted and took a fighting stance.

I'm sorry for betraying you guys. But I don't regret it. Step aside, Room 202. I'm going to kill Instructor Vinus and eliminate the Battle Mage Project. That'll be better for your futures, too."

"Shit! What the hell are you complaining about?! Weren't you supposed to be assigned to the Capital Defense Force?! Why is a guy with a guaranteed elite future trying to crawl into hell?!"

"That future is hell to me. Step aside, Room 202. I don't want to kill you."

Room 202 let out a hollow laugh.

"You're still keeping up that attitude even now. Do you think I'm a joke? Do you think you can kill me anytime you want?"

I felt mana surging from Room 202.

"You're too excited. It's not too late yet, Room 202. Step aside."

"I'm calm. What you said about it not being too late also applies to you. Go back and take care of the invaders. You'll be punished, but you won't die."

"...I can't get through to you."

I walked forward. Unfortunately, I didn't have time to argue with Room 202.

"That's what I should be saying. You must think you're so great. But when it comes to close combat, I'm better."

Room 202, clenching his knuckles tightly, kicked off the ground. He knew that I was the worst at close combat among my peers. My close combat skills were famous among my peers, so it was nothing new.

In contrast to me, Room 202 specialized in close combat. He was an infighter who enhanced his physical abilities with auxiliary magic that strengthened his body and fought by imbuing his knuckles with attributes using enchantment magic.

Room 202's fist flew towards me.

[Barrier]

I reached out my right hand towards his fist. His fist was blocked by my hand. His knuckles blazed with a fiery red light, but they didn't do any damage to me.

"...You minimized the barrier on your right hand to block my fist...?!"

"I increased the strength instead of widening the range of the barrier. It's not difficult."

"......You saw me throwing my fist here?"

"Just as you know me well, I also know you. You always start with a straight punch when you make a preemptive strike."

"Damn it!"

Room 202's right leg moved. But before that, the attribute sword moved from my chest. The attribute sword, moved silently by psychokinesis, pierced his knee accurately.

"Keueuk?!"

Room 202 gritted his teeth and stepped back.

I won't allow it.

[Gravity]

Gravity crushed Room 202's body. Room 202's mana surged violently. He pulled out the attribute sword stuck in his knee with his hand and used enhancement magic to the point of straining his body. His muscles swelled, and blood vessels appeared in his eyes. Flames swirled around his left knuckle, and wind swirled around his right knuckle.

"Don't act so smug, Room 211! I have a couple of trump cards too!"

"Trump cards? Ah, are you talking about the attribute sword? You're mistaken about something. That's not a trump card or anything. It was just a normal counterattack."

"You son of a bitch. I've always hated your smug attitude!"

He extended his fist. I covered my body with psychokinesis and dodged all of his punches. It's not difficult to dodge as long as I can see it.

"How is this...! Don't tell me, you've been hiding your strength until now?!"

"Room 212. We're mages. No matter how much you train your fists, you can't match a real warrior. And...."

"What?"

"You came too close to me with an injured leg."

[Frost]

"Aaaaaaack!"

Room 202 screamed and collapsed. This was because his injured knee had frozen instantly. Since the cold air of the 4th circle magic, Frost, had quickly permeated through his bleeding wound, it could be said that his right leg was finished.

I flicked my finger. The attribute sword connected to my psychokinesis flew and pierced Room 202's back. Room 202 spat out blood.

"Room 202. I told you I was sorry last time. Do you remember?"

"...I remember. It was a meaningless apology. Were you predicting the current situation when you said that?"

"No. No matter how you look at it, there's no way I could have predicted the future months from now. Back then. I had sex with your beloved Number 99. Did you know? Number 99 has a mole next to her anus. ...No. There's no way you would know."

"You son of a bitch...."

Room 202 trembled. He pushed his Astral to the limit and created a magic formula. It was an outstanding concentration.

I waved my finger. The attribute sword stuck in his back rotated once.

"Aaaaaaack!"

Room 202's last magic casting was canceled. Blood gushed from his mouth.

"Actually, I wasn't sorry at all back then. I thought Number 99 was too good for you. When I said I was sorry to you, it was meant as a mockery. It would be good if you realized the meaning, and it wouldn't have mattered if you didn't notice."

I looked at Room 202. He was already dying to the point where he couldn't even answer. His life had at most about 30 seconds left.

Old memories came to mind. It was a memory related to Room 202. He was an annoying guy to the point where I sometimes wanted to kill him, but I didn't hate him to the point of being disgusted.

"I was serious when I said I didn't want to kill you. ...It's sad. I felt this way when I killed Number 88 too. I'll be sad for about 3 more days."

"Keureureureureu...."

Room 202 glared at me as if he would kill me, shedding tears of blood. I watched him die until the end to eliminate any possibility.

"Ah, lastly, Number 99, who you had a crush on, was a good woman. Her voluptuous breasts were the best, not to mention her hot Pussy. Number 99 liked to hug my head... is she dead."

I pulled out the attribute sword stuck in his back and headed to the lab.

[Material Conversion]

I changed the metal door blocking the entrance to wood. Fortunately, it was a door with not very high mana resistance. I kicked and broke the wooden door and went inside. The broken wooden door turned back into metal.

I went a little further inside the lab. Before entering the office, I ran into Instructor Vinus sitting in the lounge.

"Room 211. Why did you start a rebellion?"

"Do you have to ask when you know?"

I checked my condition. I had used up more than half of my mana getting here. This was quite significant.

"No matter how much I think about it, I don't understand. You were an excellent guy. You were skilled, and you had a good head on your shoulders. And you were loyal. ...That's what I thought. You were a guy who was going to rise to the top. I was going to make you that way. Why the hell did you betray me?"

"What betrayal? I knew from the start that you never trusted me. Rise to the top? I'll rise. But even so, it won't change the fact that I'll be groveling like a dog under you. I refuse to clean up after you anymore."

"You stupid bastard."

Vinus took out a marble from his pocket. It was a magic tool that controlled the slave seal. The marble glowed in response to his mana, but nothing happened.

"......You released the slave seal?"

"It was quite a struggle. I had to solve the encrypted formula, interpret the formulas of the magic circle one by one, reverse-release them, and even introduce a new formula. I was so pissed off that I wanted to quit halfway through... but I finally solved it."

"You solved it? Don't make me laugh! This is a magic circle created by an 8th circle Archmage!"

"I spent two years going crazy trying to solve this. I'd be even more upset if I couldn't solve it after investing that much time into one magic circle."

Vinus's face turned bright red. Vinus wasn't just feeling anger. I read the inferiority complex that flashed across his face.

Jijijijik.

Noise came from the walkie-talkie on Vinus's shoulder.

-Instructor Vinus-nim! The control room and armory have been seized! We've lost contact with the outside! We're engaging the enemies in the 3rd conference room! We need support! Instructor Vinus-nim!

Vinus didn't answer the radio. He glared at me and slowly opened his mouth.

"Room 211. I'll overlook what's happened so far. Surrender. And kill the enemy."

I took out an ampule from my chest and stabbed it into my thigh.

Kwack!

"Fuck off. My enemy is you."

The ampule's drug entered my body and permeated through me.

[Overload]

My Astral surged, and my senses expanded. The amount of information my brain processed instantly increased more than threefold. Also, my mana seethed violently. 50 mana rods were activated in an instant and began to overload.

[Electric Field]

I stomped my right foot. Starting with me, electricity spread in all directions. It didn't take long for the lounge to be filled with blue electricity.

The stage was set, so next I would use the stage.

As if sensing something, Vinus layered barriers to cover his body. At the same time, he created ice spears and threw them at me to restrain me. I twisted my body to avoid the ice spears and continued the formula.

The overloaded mana rods screamed and caused pain. I gritted my teeth. I was prepared for this much.

[Thunder Bolt]

The electricity that had been filling the lounge gathered on the ceiling and was compressed. Then, the moment I felt as if everything had suddenly stopped, a huge lightning bolt fell from the ceiling.

Kwaaaaaang!

Direct hit!

But Vinus was fine. His savagely distorted face could be seen through the melted barrier.

"The preparation time was too long. I clearly taught you before that great magic that you prepare openly isn't threatening."

"Of course I remember. How could I forget whose teachings they are."

Pajijik.

Blue sparks flew from the cooled ceiling.

Vinus's eyes widened as he sensed the premonitory signs.

And the second Thunder Bolt, which had been prepared above the ceiling, smashed through the ceiling and fell on Vinus.

"Heueueueueup!"

Vinus reacted. In that short moment, he raised his left hand and reduced the barrier to the size of his palm to block the Thunder Bolt.

It was a shockingly fast reaction, but it wasn't perfect. His left arm was charred black. No matter how you looked at it, it seemed impossible to revive his left arm.

'It's a shame I couldn't kill him instantly..., but I finished off his left arm. I'm more advantageous.'

Kiiiiiiing.

I prepared magic while listening to the mana rods wailing.
