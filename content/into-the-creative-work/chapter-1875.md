I shook the blood off my sword and approached Yang Su-bin. She was trembling, her face filled with terror. It was only natural for her to be scared, considering a muscle monster had suddenly fallen from the sky and tried to attack her. Unlike me, who was used to dealing with monsters, she was just an ordinary doctor.

"Are you okay?"

"Ah, yes. I'm okay. Was that monster also a variant?"

"Yeah."

"...Are there many variants? I've seen two today alone."

"It's not usually this common. It's just the first day the D virus has spread. You could say you've just been unlucky to encounter two variants."

"...Do variants evolve by consuming living things, like zombies?"

I nodded.

"The D virus is the cause of evolution, after all."

"Then variants will get stronger over time, I suppose."

"That's right. And the same goes for humans. Evolution isn't exclusive to zombies."

If an Awakened is bitten by a zombie, there's a high chance they'll immediately become a variant zombie. That muscle monster was probably an Awakened with body enhancement abilities.

"...Thank you for saving me. If it weren't for you, I would have been eaten alive."

"I can't just let you die when you've agreed to cooperate with me so wholeheartedly."

She looked at me with a renewed gaze. I struck a pose, acting all cool. About five minutes later, eight guards came out of the building. Two of them were being carried on stretchers.

Park Hae-gil approached me and bowed.

"Thank you for saving Su-bin-ssi!"

"Yeah, sure."

Receiving Park Hae-gil's polite and sincere greeting didn't move me at all.

Yang Su-bin started checking on the patients, starting with those who were unconscious. She examined their heads closely and said there were no major problems. She wanted to do a thorough examination... but I don't know. Seongak Elementary School didn't have the latest medical equipment. She gave painkillers and splinted the legs of the guy with the double leg fracture. It didn't seem like he needed surgery.

I took the lead and started moving. Park Hae-gil came up beside me.

"The movements and strength you showed when fighting that monster... are you perhaps a psychic?"

"Would you believe me if I said I was a psychic?"

"With zombies and monsters around, how can I not believe in psychics?"

"That's right. I'm a psychic. Usually called an Awakened."

"How can one become an Awakened?"

"You either got it or you don't?"

I don't know how one becomes an Awakened either. The cause is also largely unknown. The D virus? It's one of the causes, but nothing has been accurately identified. Awakened are rare. Even now that the D virus has started to actively spread, Awakened are not easily found. And early Awakened, who awakened before the zombie outbreak, are even rarer.

"...Did you perhaps awaken today?"

"No. I awakened about a month ago. Do you want to have powers that badly?"

"I want to have power. Because it seems like an era where power is most important has come."

"If you really want to become an Awakened that badly, try eating a zombie or something. Maybe you'll evolve and awaken, who knows."

Humans are different from zombies. No matter how much a human eats zombies, the probability of evolving is low. In the first place, evolution isn't something you can do as you please.

The exception is Players. Among them, Lords and Bionics.

Lords can forcibly awaken those who are loyal to them. The price is that loyalty.

Bionics could evolve humans through genetic manipulation, enhancement, and so on. The downside was that it took an incredibly long time to evolve them in the desired direction.

Of course, I had no intention of telling Park Hae-gil this fact. We weren't that close, and it was annoying.

I looked at Park Hae-gil. He was seriously considering whether to eat a zombie.

'Is he the type to be obsessed with getting stronger?'

Park Hae-gil's first impression was that he was burly and kind. But people's true nature only becomes visible after peeling back a layer, as they say. I couldn't tell whether Park Hae-gil was truly kind or just a hypocrite.

"Since you've decided to work for us, I'll tell you in advance. There may be times when you have to kill people. Kill them then. Don't hesitate."

"...Did I look pathetic to you? I know that you have to kill when you have to. I've killed people on the battlefield before."

"I don't know how much that experience will help, but don't do anything stupid."

Perhaps because we killed zombies on the way here, there were fewer zombies on the way back. Thanks to that, the return trip was easy.

'I hope it stays easy until the end.'

That thought had barely finished when it happened.

"Save me!!"

Someone shouted from a dark alley. Since it was a man's voice, I looked at the alley with a rather indifferent expression. There was a man being torn apart by three insects the size of medium-sized dogs. The man reached out to us.

"Wh-what is that?!"

Park Hae-gil was startled. He immediately pulled out the baton hanging from his waist belt.

"Bugs, huh."

There was no rule that bugs couldn't evolve. However, it was too fast to say that the bugs had evolved after the D virus spread. They must have been evolving even before the D virus spread.

"Can you really be saying that so leisurely? We have to save that person!"

"Save me! Please save me!! Aaaagh!"

I crossed my arms. I didn't feel any need to save him at all. The man's limbs were already stained red with blood, and even if we saved him, we'd have to give him first aid.

I looked at Park Hae-gil's colleagues. The frozen colleagues were hesitant. They were afraid of the bugs. Even though they were guards with special forces backgrounds, they only had batons in their hands instead of guns. They probably didn't want to risk their lives with just that.

"Ignore it."

I said. The insects didn't try to come out of the dark alley. Even after seeing us, they focused on the man they had caught. The man's strength was gradually fading. The bugs were trying to drag the man into the manhole.

'Did they nest in the sewer? It can't be that big of a sewer. Maybe they dug underground and made another hole.'

It was very unpleasant to think that bugs like that were in the Seoul underground that I would soon control. I wanted to wipe them all out, but....

'I don't know exactly how big the bugs' scale is. I could end up fucked if I recklessly act alone.'

I couldn't handle everything alone.

It was the moment I turned my head. Park Hae-gil charged at the bugs. He struck the bug's head with his baton. It hit the center of the bug's head perfectly. The bug's head shattered, and translucent green slime splattered, dirtying the floor.

Kieeeeek!

The bugs rushed at Park Hae-gil. Park Hae-gil swung his baton around, but he allowed an opening to a bug crawling from below. The bug bit Park Hae-gil's right hand. Blood burst out, and Park Hae-gil bled.

"Hae-gil-ah!"

"President-nim!"

"Damn it!!"

The guards rushed in. They also held batons and repeatedly struck the bugs.

I watched the scene with a cold gaze.

"You didn't even pretend to listen to me."

"I-I'm sorry. Hae-gil can't just ignore injustice...."

"Looks like it."

Park Hae-gil. Should I just kill him now? No. Yang Su-bin's gaze is right behind me. If I'm going to kill him, I have to kill him secretly. Preferably by a natural accident.

"I want to treat you well, but I don't think I can. You understand, right?"

"Y-you're not going to kill Hae-gil or anything, are you?"

"I won't kill him. But that's it. Still, I'll let it slide once because of you. I'll use him as a guard dog, and if he becomes useless, I'll kick him out. I'm making a big concession. You're not going to complain, are you?"

"I'll talk to Hae-gil later. He won't disappoint you again."

I wasn't disappointed. Because I didn't expect anything from the start.

The guards, who belatedly rushed in, killed all the bugs and saved Park Hae-gil and the man who had eventually lost consciousness. Park Hae-gil's right hand was wrecked, and the man's limbs were a mess. Especially his legs.

Yang Su-bin deliberately pretended not to see the man and approached Park Hae-gil first to examine his wound. I liked that action more.

"I'm fine. It hurts a bit, but it's not serious. Take care of this person first."

The guards laid the unconscious man flat on the ground. Yang Su-bin's face soon hardened after seeing the wound.

"...There's too much bleeding. It looks like he's in shock. He needs a blood transfusion right now, but...."

"......"

A blood transfusion here? You can't just do a blood transfusion just because you have people. You need medical equipment. Above all, no one knew the man's blood type.

Yang Su-bin started to stop the bleeding as best she could. Her hands and clothes became covered in blood. Her face didn't look good.

"Even if we do surgery here, his lower body is... ah."

She sighed and stopped the bleeding. The man was dead. In the silence, a sigh was heard. It was Park Hae-gil. He held out his right hand to Yang Su-bin.

"Su-bin-ah. Can you treat me?"

"...I can do first aid. I have bandages and medicine. I'll give you painkillers too. But I'll have to look at the wound again when we get back."

While Park Hae-gil was receiving first aid, I looked at the guards. The guards who rushed to save Park Hae-gil. Would everyone be like Park Hae-gil?

'At least those three aren't.'

Three people were looking at Park Hae-gil with dissatisfaction. I don't know about the one who was unconscious, but the other two were looking at Park Hae-gil with concern. The one with the double leg fracture was blankly looking at the corpse.

Soon, the first aid was finished, and Yang Su-bin got up. She came to my side.

"Um... can you get infected if you're bitten by those monster bugs?"

"Unless those monsters are zombies, you won't get infected since they're not zombies."

"Haa. That's a relief."

"You might get other diseases, though."

"Pardon?"

"They're monster bugs. How do I know if there are other viruses?"

"...I-is there no way to deal with it?"

"I wouldn't know. I'm not a doctor."

I took them back. Was it because there were many people? There were quite a few people in the houses asking to be saved. I saved them. They weren't injured like the man who was attacked by the bugs, and they were able-bodied. Besides, they didn't have much combat power, so they didn't seem like they would cause trouble - just ordinary people.

'Na Chae-young did tell me to bring people back. Labor is needed for a comfortable life.'
