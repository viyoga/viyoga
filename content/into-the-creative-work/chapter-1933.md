Tinee stomped down the dormitory hallway, heading back to her room.

Her expression wasn't good. She'd just gotten a warning from the instructor about her grades.

-Tinee-yang. This incident has put your grades on thin ice. One more mistake and you're expelled, so watch your behavior and focus only on your studies.

Spilling wine on Yuria during the test today wasn't a mistake, it was deliberate. An impulsive act without much thought.

She was just so damn jealous of how good the noble dress looked on her.

,We're both commoners, both orphans, so why the difference?

She heard that Yuria-ssi was an orphan like her. She just got lucky and entered the Knight Training Center and became a maid.

She had to struggle every day as a lower-class citizen, while someone else not only got into the Knight Training Center by sheer luck but also kicked that blessing aside to enter the Maid Academy, receiving sponsorship from some noble and showing off her talents.

,Why is she the only one doing well?

Tinee bit her lip.

On the verge of expulsion.

It wasn't set in stone yet, but honestly, she was struggling to keep up with the classes.

,I thought maids just needed to be good at fighting....

Most of the maids and butlers she'd seen were guarding their employers. She thought she could easily get a maid license if she was good at fighting. That was her mistake. In reality, they were learning all sorts of odd jobs like economics, etiquette, and cooking.

Tinee could easily foresee her future. She would eventually be expelled. She'd managed to hold on until now, but this was her limit. The instructors and other maid students didn't look at her kindly, so she couldn't even get help.

,If I'm going to be expelled anyway....

She had to repay the humiliation she'd suffered. That was how a street urchin lived.

Even if she couldn't kill Yuria, she wanted to leave a scar on that pretty face. Or cripple one of her limbs. In some way, she wanted to darken that brilliantly shining future. Malice born of jealousy writhed within her.

,If we fight, I'll win. So, if I can just get her to fight....

• • •

Combat was one of the things maid students learned.

Stepping forward to protect their employer in case of emergency. That was also a maid's job.

Since maids were often hired as bodyguards, combat practice was quite important.

Combat practice at the Maid Academy focused on duels. This was because maid students had different combat styles. The instructors only gave advice on real combat, without interfering in the actual training direction.

Yuria picked up a sword.

It was a safe choice. Neo London had reverence for knights, so they preferred swords. Knowing swordsmanship earned you a certain amount of respect.

,Yuria-yang. Would you be my opponent?

Tinee approached and spoke politely. It was a different attitude and tone than usual. On the other hand, her eyes were blazing as she looked at Yuria. She was clearly applying for a duel with bad intentions.

Juliet, who was next to Yuria, clicked her tongue and glared at Tinee.

,What kind of scheme are you plotting now?

,Ha, I wasn't talking to you. Why don't you piss off, you scum?

,...Scum? Are you running your mouth right now?

Before Juliet could shout, Yuria stepped forward. It was obvious what Tinee was up to. Looking at those eyes, she was planning something.

,Juliet-yang. I'm fine. Tinee-yang, what kind of training do you want?

,A duel like in real combat. Surely the student with the best grades ever wouldn't back down, would she?

Tinee's tone became arrogant again. She was sure that Yuria had no intention of running away.

,A real combat duel, huh. Then I'll have to tell the instructor to use the central training ground.

,You're really going to do it with me? Do you know where I'm from?

,You said yourself that you're from the slums of Neo London.

Tinee proudly talked about her origins. People were afraid of those from the slums. Because the lower class had poor security.

,Fighting is an everyday occurrence there. There are corpses lying in the alleys. I've killed people a few times too.

,I don't think that's something you should be saying so proudly.

Looking around, the students who were watching them were frowning.

Tinee didn't care. She had already given up on the Maid Academy.

,I'm just stating the facts.

,You want me to be intimidated and shrink back.

Tinee, feeling stung, clicked her tongue and turned around.

,Just follow me. You're not going to back out now, are you?

Yuria followed her to the central training ground.

The instructor acted as the duel referee, and Yuria and Tinee stood facing each other.

,Even though it's a real combat duel, you're not actually fighting. It's just a duel like real combat, so don't forget that and respect each other.

The instructor said.

If the duel became too intense or dangerous, he would intervene immediately. But even if the instructor intervened, accidents could still happen.

Tinee was aiming for just one moment.

,Duel start.

With the instructor's words, Tinee, holding a sword in each hand, suddenly rushed at Yuria and swung her swords. She swung the two swords in different directions to confuse Yuria. However, Tinee's attack was blocked ridiculously easily.

Kkaang.

Tinee, whose attack was blocked by Yuria's sword, bit her lip and stepped back. That surprise attack was a technique she learned from a retired assassin. Swift movements, two swords moving differently. A surprise attack. An attack with those three advantages was blocked so easily it was absurd.

Tinee then performed a sword dance she'd learned over the shoulder. She continued her attack following the rhythm created by her heart. The two swords relentlessly pushed Yuria.

On the surface, Yuria was struggling to block Tinee's attacks. Her retreating feet and shaking sword, even her stiff expression. It felt like an irreversible disaster would happen if she made even the slightest mistake.

But in reality, she was relaxed. She could see all of Tinee's movements. She could slip through the gaps and cut her throat right now.

,There are too many eyes watching.

She had to hold back even if she wanted to kill her. Killing Tinee here would bring more losses than gains. The moment she got the title of murderer, her reputation would plummet.

They exchanged blows for a while.

Tinee's increasingly impatient feet slightly twisted. A very small mistake. Yuria didn't miss that gap and counterattacked.

Kkaaaaaang!

The two swords were knocked away, and Tinee stumbled back and fell on her butt.

,Stop! The duel ends here! The winner is Yuria-yang!

The instructor intervened and ended the duel. He checked that neither of them was injured and nodded.

,.......

Tinee blankly followed the instructor's instructions. She was in a state of great mental shock.

,I lost? Me? If I lose even in a fight, what's better about me than that woman?

She couldn't accept it. She didn't want to accept it. Returning to her dormitory, she gritted her teeth.

• • •

The door opened quietly.

Yuria, who was lying in bed, slowly opened her eyes and met the eyes of Tinee, who was sneaking closer. Tinee was startled and swung her sword.

Yuria ducked her head and lightly dodged, getting out of bed.

,I expected it, but I didn't think you'd actually try an ambush. Did you hate me that much?

,If it weren't for you, things wouldn't have gone this wrong!

Yuria sighed.

There were often people who did incomprehensible things like this. In her experience, they all acted for trivial reasons.

,You could have just accepted things as they were. Was that so difficult?

Tinee's eyes wavered. Feelings of regret surged. She wondered why she was doing this. But it was too late to regret.

Tinee gritted her teeth and swung her sword. Yuria dodged the attack as if staggering, burrowed into Tinee's arms, and threw a punch. It landed squarely on her abdomen. She then twisted the wrist holding the sword to take the weapon and finish her off, but stopped.

,Something's strange.

Her gaze and breathing were all unstable. No matter how excited she was, it didn't make sense for her to be this unstable.

,Brainwashing, huh.

She couldn't tell if it was due to magic or repeated learning. Yuria lacked knowledge about this world right now. Besides, Yuria couldn't even use magic.

Yuria gave up on killing her and punched Tinee in the face. Tinee flew back and collided with the door. The door broke, and Tinee fell unconscious.

The students who were sleeping in the dormitory rooms woke up and came outside at the loud noise.

,What, what?! An attack?!

,It's Tinee! Was Tinee trying to kill Yuria?!

The situation quickly became chaotic. Yuria, feigning a tired expression, racked her brain. Thinking about it again, Tinee was definitely brainwashed. In other words, someone was using Tinee to target her.

It was incomprehensible. She hadn't particularly earned anyone's resentment.

,...This is troublesome.

Yuria sighed inwardly. It was one thing for her to be targeted, but what if the aftermath reached Eugene? Yuria's eyes turned cold for a moment.

The instructors entered the dormitory and began to clean up.

Tinee was expelled and arrested by the London Guard at the same time and dragged away. She would receive a 100% prison sentence. Since it happened at the Maid Academy, she would receive at least 10 years in prison.

• • •

Maria, the head of the Maid Academy, and the instructors gathered in the conference room.

,Traces of brainwashing were found on Tinee-yang. It's not magic, but it's judged to be an ability.

One of the instructors said in a serious tone.

Since it was an unfortunate incident that occurred at the Maid Academy, they put all their efforts into the investigation and discovered something strange.

Maria calmly asked the instructors.

,Have you grasped Tinee-yang's actions before entering the Maid Academy?
