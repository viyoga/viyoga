"Squeak-squeak! Mr. Eugene! I've received payment from the Transcendent Zibro!"

I took the pendant that Rupil handed me.

『Music of Weakening

Grade: Unique

A pendant containing the Music of Weakening.

All enemies who hear this music are weakened.』

'It's the item from the original story. It's a shame it's single-use.'

On the surface it looks like an ordinary pendant, but press the button on the back and the music spreads. Its range is a whopping 10km. Every target I recognize as an enemy is weakened. This isn't ordinary music—it's a kind of magic.

'In this world, enhancement is better when dealing with weaklings, and weakening is more efficient when facing strong enemies.'

This is for war.

Of course, against modern nations on Earth it's practically useless. In modern warfare where missiles fly from hundreds, thousands of kilometers away, 10km is nothing.

'But when using an army that relies on close-quarters combat, it's a different story.'

Depending on how it's used, it boasts the power of a strategic weapon.

'To think I can get something far better than a missile worth hundreds of billions for just tens of millions of won.'

I couldn't help but marvel at the overwhelming cost-effectiveness.

No wonder the original protagonist kissed up to the Transcendents.

"Satisfactory."

"Squeak-squeak. Thank you."

"How was the reaction to the chicken?"

"The reaction was incredible! They even contacted me separately, begging me to sell chicken only to them! Squeak!"

"It's not ordinary franchise chicken—it's chicken Yuria made herself. Heh heh."

It's top-grade chicken that perfectly satisfies even my palate. Most of the Transcendents here come from medieval fantasy civilizations. In that era, even the food nobles ate was likely less tasty than modern food.

'The Middle Ages were an era where proper salt, pepper, and sugar were hard to come by.'

I know well because I've lived in a medieval fantasy world. An ordinary medieval fantasy era is not easy for a modern person.

"Squeak-squeak! I've clearly realized that volume sales are not the answer when dealing with Transcendents! Squeak!"

"Right. I know the answer. You just do as I say and collect your commission. That alone will make you rich in no time."

"I'll keep that in mind. Squeak!"

"Which Transcendent had the best reaction to the chicken?"

"Hent, the Sage of Iron! Squeak!"

"A big shot."

Hent, the Sage of Iron. Among the Transcendents, he's famous for being wealthy.

A combat alchemist whose skill is certain even if his temper is foul. Alchemy specialized in handling metals rather than restrictions. In the original story, he's lured in with potato chips. That he'd react to chicken was unexpected.

'It'd be strange not to react after eating that chicken. Anyway, if Hent showed interest, I should tell Lee Yeonhee to start collecting silver.'

Hent can make 1kg of true silver from 1,000kg of silver. That's why he's wealthy.

"Rupil. Approach Hent carefully. Give him some gifts and get close to him."

"Squeak-squeak?! I-if I do that, the other Transcendents will be furious!"

"Hent is among the stronger Transcendents, isn't he. And true silver isn't something even Transcendents can easily obtain. The other Transcendents will have no choice but to be mindful of Hent. If you get on Hent's good side, he'll protect you to some degree. If I say I'm interested in true silver, the other Transcendents will understand to some extent. Don't forget that even if you're a rat, you're a super rat."

Without Rupil, the Transcendents would have difficulty obtaining modern goods.

And if they make an enemy of me, they'll never be able to obtain modern goods again.

"Squeak-squeak! Understood!"

"Good. Let's do well."

I challenged the 17th floor.

『Defeat the imps.』

It was a dark, abandoned city.

Moonlight hung in the sky, but it was so faint I could barely make out the shapes around me.

Kihihihi.

Mocking laughter echoes from somewhere in the city. It's the voice of imps hiding in the darkness.

"Let's go by the book."

Imps hate light. Just being in front of light weakens them by more than half. When there's light, they have a habit of moving to places without it.

I took a grenade launcher from my inventory and fired a flare into the sky.

The flare emits a bright light as it slowly falls to the ground. A single flare was enough to illuminate the abandoned city.

Is there such a thing as magic? That's magic, right there. Since I don't know the first thing about how flares work, they felt like magic to me.

"Firing just one feels unsatisfying."

I fired three more flares. Now it was as bright as daylight.

"Kiiieeeek!"

An imp with a goblin's build scrambles in panic, darting into a building where darkness remains.

"Master. Shall I handle it?"

"I'll do it."

I took out an anti-tank missile, the Hyeongung, and fired. The domestic missile flies and embeds itself in the abandoned building the imp entered.

Even as they spoke, the Ministers of State glanced at the person seated at the head of the table. Originally the Deputy Prime Minister, but now Ushida Sotaro, who had inherited the position of head of state after former Prime Minister Mineki Hosei. He calmly drank his green tea.

*Damn it. If times were normal, I wouldn't have to watch Ushida's every move like this.*

Ushida had been Deputy Prime Minister, but he was an ambiguous figure in many ways. That ambiguity had led him to the Deputy Prime Ministership, but the political world's judgment was that he wasn't Prime Minister material.

However, the situation had changed.

The former Prime Minister died, and he, the Deputy Prime Minister, became head of state.

Normally he wouldn't last long. But the situation had changed. With the Tower's appearance, every nation is in a state of emergency. Elections? Hold elections in this situation? Are you insane? Even the US couldn't insist on holding presidential elections as long as a national crisis exists.

The current era was one where the head of state's power was stronger than at any other time.
