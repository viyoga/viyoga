The First Cheonma, along with the six other Cheonmas, entered Seong Yujin's deepest mindscape from the very beginning. This was possible because they had all reached at least the Jowagyeong (Creation Realm) in their lifetimes and were familiar with mindscapes.

,The other seven aren't visible.

A man with long hair swept back and a snake-like face spoke. It was the Second Cheonma, Yu Heogun. Although he wasn't a Cheon clan member, the First Cheonma had directly appointed him to the position of Cheonma before his death.

,Weren't those bastards just embarrassing riffraff even to call them Cheonmas? It's better this way since they'd only be a hindrance even if they were here.

A man with short hair and a body full of scars said. He was the Third Cheonma, Cheon Goshin.

Kwaarrrrrreureureung!

Thunder rumbled in the clear blue sky. It was impossible. Thunder required dark clouds. But no one there found it strange.

This was a mindscape. Nothing that happened here was strange. Thunder in a clear sky? That itself could be something special about the owner of this mindscape.

,For a mere whelp, he has a vast mindscape. The sensation of air and moisture, the blowing wind, and the solid ground... The mindscape's reproduction is considerable. And even the energy of the stars... Perfect qualities and talent. First-isiyeo, this guy is more than qualified to become a Cheonma.

The Eighteenth Cheonma, Cheon Unra, said. She was a short-haired woman with burn scars that melted half of her face. Her eyes were so sharp that she resembled a hungry beast rather than a woman. She was a Cheonma who had been active during the current Emperor's prime.

Kwakwakwakwakwang!

A huge lightning bolt fell from the sky. A lightning bolt that seemed capable of instantly obliterating a small hill.

The First Cheonma swung his fist imbued with demonic energy, batting away the lightning. The lightning flew far away and crashed into a mountain. Part of the mountain disappeared.

The Seventh Cheonma, Cheon Palbi, in the form of a boy, watched the scene with a smile.

,How intriguing. Since the mindscape reflects the state of its owner's mind, it's not strange that it attacks us intruders, but it's interesting that such powerful lightning is attacking us. Most guys wouldn't have been able to withstand that attack and would have been annihilated.

At the very end of them was a man with a faint presence. The assassin-turned-Cheonma with his face and body covered in black bandages, the Thirteenth Cheonma, Jeoksal. The taciturn man spoke quietly.

,I wonder what's at the very bottom.

The First Cheonma, Cheon Galson, quietly looked around. His eyes, which had once almost reached the realm of ascension, could see where to go.

,Follow me. I will check what is at the end of this and make a decision.

He stroked his long white beard and walked. He never moved hastily.

Kwakwang! Kwakwakwakwakwang!

Lightning struck incessantly. The Cheonmas responded lightly. Deflecting, absorbing, blocking, or dodging. To them, who had once been called the best in the world or close to it, the relentless barrage of massive lightning strikes was nothing.

,To be so hostile towards us... How presumptuous. I guess that ambition to become the sole Cheonma wasn't a lie.

The Seventh Cheonma said with a smile. There was no soul in his laughter. A faint annoyance flickered in his eyes.

The Third Cheonma, Cheon Goshin, was interested in something else. Something a little further away. Modern cities and Western medieval mansions and castles caught his eye.

,There are many unusual types of buildings. Buildings I never saw even when I was alive....

He wanted to go in and look around if he could. But the First Cheonma simply moved forward as if he had no interest at all.

The Eighteenth Cheonma, Cheon Unra, noticed something strange.

,We've walked quite a bit, but there isn't a single corpse to be seen.

At that, several Cheonmas narrowed their eyes.

A mindscape was a mind. A mind was memories and experiences. In any mindscape that hasn't been organized, there are corpses. The memory of the first time one committed murder remains until death.

And for those who have experience killing ruthless people and for the demonic practitioners close to death, corpses were inseparable.

,That guy is just a whelp who hasn't even reached the Samjeong stage... So, there are two conclusions.

The First Cheonma continued indifferently.

,Either he has no interest in those he killed at all, or he has somehow organized his mindscape.

The former was more likely.

Organizing a mindscape was only possible for those who were at least at the upper level of Samjeong.

,.......

The Cheonmas all fell silent, each thinking about Seong Yujin. Some felt disgust and displeasure, some felt affirmation, and others felt interest.

The First Cheonma, who was walking ahead, stopped. The space had suddenly changed.

,Hmm.

The First Cheonma frowned slightly. At the same time, he and the other Cheonmas used Cheonma Shingong. They wrapped their bodies in the demonic energy of Cheonma Shingong. Protecting their bodies with a shallow protective aura.

They had no choice because this space was filled with lightning energy. Electricity flowed like water not only in the sky but also on the ground. Even the air contained subtle lightning energy that tried to penetrate their bodies.

To them, lightning energy was poison. Unless there was someone who knew how to handle lightning energy. Lightning energy was a very rare attribute compared to other attributes. Even if someone was born with lightning energy, they might not be able to handle it since they weren't the owner of this mindscape.

The Seventh Cheonma looked up at the sky filled with thousands of lightning bolts.

,This isn't a simple phenomenon. There's something above that sky. I feel a powerful presence.

The Second Cheonma glanced at the sky and then lowered his gaze again.

,I don't feel any hostility. Is there any need to check? Let's ignore it. This isn't the bottom of the mindscape.

Everyone looked at the First Cheonma. In the end, it was the First Cheonma who made the decision.

The First Cheonma was intrigued. He could tell just from the presence. The being above that sky was a being with very powerful strength. At the same time, an alien being.

,It's not human, is it a god?

The First Cheonma had encountered a god before.

Gods were basically strong, but they weren't beings that couldn't be defeated. Just because someone was a god didn't mean they were unconditionally strong. The biggest characteristic of gods was their alienness. A feeling that they were fundamentally different from humans. Rather, it would be more accurate to say that they were closer to living nature.

,The beings in the mindscape are indirect representations of beings that the owner of the mindscape felt deeply about. Since this mindscape is so vast and clear, it must be almost perfectly realized.

In other words, the owner of the mindscape had met a god. And probably a god related to the sky and lightning.

,The sky and lightning, huh? A god representing something unusual. Definitely not low in status. I want to check it out.

The First Cheonma raised his hand. Seeing that signal, the assassin-turned-Thirteenth Cheonma, Jeoksal, stepped forward.

,Yes, First-nim.

He was the most skilled in lightness techniques except for the First Cheonma. He was also stealthy.

,Confirm the being above the sky.

,Yes.

The Thirteenth Cheonma's answer was short and concise. His knees bent slightly and then straightened. His body soared upwards. To them, flying or running was nothing.

-How dare you try to capture a god in your eyes without permission.

A deep, resonant voice filled with subtle anger echoed in the sky. The movement of the lightning filling the sky became rougher.

The Thirteenth Cheonma protected his body even more thoroughly while dodging the incoming lightning. He ran through the air with all his might to confirm the being above the clouds. This lightning, it wasn't ordinary lightning. Continuing to run while being hit by this was difficult even for him.

,Achieve the objective as quickly as possible.

He pierced through the clouds.

The being was sitting on a throne above the clouds.

Its appearance was not visible. It had a human form, but it was not human. It was lightning.

-To ascend to the sky without permission... There is a limit to impiety. Offer your life as the price.

It grabbed lightning in its hand and threw it.

The Thirteenth Cheonma focused all his mind.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Demonic Emperor's Gait) Hwe (Return).

The Thirteenth Cheonma's Cheonma Gunrimbo was different from the other Cheonmas. He had imbued his mindscape into the Cheonma Gunrimbo. His Cheonma Gunrimbo erased his existence for a moment and then returned. In that moment when his existence was erased, he was not affected by anything.

But there was something he hadn't considered.

This world was a world within someone's mindscape. An existence that disappears and returns? Such petty tricks don't work.

The lightning pierced through his body. Even though he had strengthened his entire body using demonic energy, he couldn't block the lightning that it had thrown directly. His consciousness gradually faded, and he felt as if his existence was being sucked somewhere.

-The price is insufficient.

The being's gaze turned to the ground. It threw lightning at one of the five Cheonmas looking up at the sky.

The Eighteenth Cheonma, Cheon Unra, was the target. She moved her hands. Unlike the other Cheonmas who used swords, she had mastered hand techniques. She even used the martial arts of the Wudang Sect to create her own unique techniques.

,I'll handle it.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art) Taegeuk Main (Taiji Demonic Attraction).

Her hands drew a large Taiji symbol. The direction of the lightning that was aiming for her head changed. The lightning was sucked into the center of the Taiji. The Taiji should have disappeared along with the lightning, but the lightning pierced through even the Taiji and destroyed her heart.

With a face distorted in shock as her last expression, her existence disappeared.

,.......

The Cheonmas were silent. Then their eyes turned fierce.

,We're ruining the Cheonma's reputation.

,It's certain that he's no ordinary guy.

,How about we kill him before we leave?

The First Cheonma shook his head.

,He's a being in the mindscape. You all know it's meaningless.

,...First-nim. What will he think if we retreat like this?

,We can ask him about that sin later. I need to check what's at this bottom right now.

The First Cheonma strode forward without hesitation. The Second, Third, and Seventh Cheonmas exchanged glances and followed him. To be honest, they weren't confident that they could fight and win against the being above the clouds without the First Cheonma's help.
