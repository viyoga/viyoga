As if possessed, I hold out my palm to Yang Hong-ye, who approaches me, to stop her. Fortunately, I haven't completely lost my mind, as she stops in front of me. But her gaze is still fixed on the Vajra Divine Lightning.

Her golden eyes seem to sparkle.

Dragon Eyes.

A natural ability inherited from dragon's blood. She'll see through almost anything.

,"If it's the Vajra pestle, it must be from the Buddhist side. If there's a Law Artifact full of lightning energy there... Sakra Devanam Indra. To think that a Law Artifact symbolizing the Emperor of Heaven's lightning would be in the Middle Realm.... But the power it holds is weak. It's too disappointing to be called a Law Artifact. Well, it's only natural since it's held in human hands."

By the time she finished speaking, she had calmed down. It felt like her interest in the Law Artifact had waned slightly.

This is an unexpected variable.

"Come under me. In return, I'll let you study the Vajra Divine Lightning whenever you have time. This opportunity won't come twice in your life."

"That's not a very appealing offer. Unless you give me the Vajra Divine Lightning altogether."

"Are you crazy? Do you think you're worth the Vajra Divine Lightning?"

"I'm the best artisan in the human realm. You know that, that's why you came looking for me like this. Studying Law Artifacts? Sounds good. But just because I study Law Artifacts doesn't mean I can make them. The Emperor's offer to let me handle all the materials in this world is more appealing."

"......The Emperor made you an offer?"

That didn't happen in the original story. But I wasn't too surprised. Well, the Emperor didn't exist in the original Gwangmyeong Seungcheon Painting.

"Yeah. I met him in person about 50 years ago. He was a man I couldn't dare to judge. I wouldn't hesitate to call him the greatest of all time. Compared to him, you're... I don't know the details. But well, you don't feel too bad compared to the other guys."

"Other guys?"

"Guys who came to recruit me like you. Most of them were rotten to the core. Especially the ones who tried to take me away by force using their martial prowess; there were quite a few of them."

The fact that Yang Hong-ye is still here means that all those guys failed.

'She has dozens of Law Treasures and thousands of Law Instruments, and she knows how to handle them all. Plus, she has the special trait of dragon blood. Assuming we're at the same level, it'd be hard to beat her in a one-on-one fight.'

If I fought her with all my power? I think I could somehow win if I used the Divine Demon Unleashed.

'The problem is Yang Hong-ye's personality. There's no point in taking her by force; she obviously won't work.'

The plan to entice her with the Vajra Divine Lightning failed.

'Should I try to entice her with the Gwangmyeong Seungcheon Painting?'

No. I can't predict how Yang Hong-ye will react to seeing the Gwangmyeong Seungcheon Painting. Even the original protagonist never showed the Gwangmyeong Seungcheon Painting to anyone of his own volition. And every guy who happened to find out about the Gwangmyeong Seungcheon Painting tried to steal it.

'...I'll entice her with something else. Modern machines? Law Instruments are more mysterious and perform better than machines, so she probably won't be interested. I wish I could just stick a hypnosis sticker on her.'

Considering her level, a hypnosis sticker wouldn't even work.

"What do you want?"

She smirks.

"You're the one who came looking for me because you wanted something. You're the one who has to make an offer. If you have nothing, get lost."

"If I pique your interest, will you come under me?"

"I can work under you. If you can pique my interest in a way that even the Emperor couldn't."

"......"

It's no exaggeration to say that the Emperor is the master of this world. He's an existence who can give you everything in this world.

'Wait. Everything in this world?'

Then what about something that's not from this world?

I took out the Hwaryeon Bido and the Stormbreaker from my inventory. Yang Hong-ye's eyes spark with interest as she looks at the red knife and the massive spear.

"That's an amazing knife. It may not be a Law Artifact, but I can tell that it's a knife forged by a special being with powers and methods I don't know."

"Hwaryeon Bido. It's my favorite knife. Don't you want to study it?"

"A knife forged by some transcendent being with special powers. Even if I study that knife, I won't gain much. But that spear is a bit interesting. From the material to the unknown spells engraved on it. No, is it not a spell? Anyway, it doesn't seem like it was made by a transcendent being. What's the name of that spear?"

"Stormbreaker. In your terms, it'd be something like Storm Destroyer. Actually, I was planning to recruit you and ask you to enhance the Stormbreaker."

That's a lie. I had no idea she'd be interested in the Stormbreaker. But it wouldn't be bad to enhance the Stormbreaker on this occasion.

"No way. You've piqued my interest, but it's not enough. And offer a suitable price. I don't do free labor."

"...You can enhance the Stormbreaker?"

"Who do you think I am? The technology and spells that went into it are very complex and amazing, but they haven't transcended the laws. Unless you're talking about creating something completely new based on it. Enhancing it is no problem."

She was full of confidence.

'I've succeeded in piquing her interest, so the problem is the price. She won't be interested in elixirs, and the Law Instruments I got from the Thunder Sound Temple won't even catch her eye.'

As I pondered, I took out a Star Fragment from my inventory. The Star Fragment that the Cheongangseong System gave me.

『Star Fragment

It contains the energy of a star.』

I kept holding onto it because I didn't know what to use it for. I had a total of 6 of them now.

The Star Fragment on my palm sparkled like a star.

"A star?! If you had such a treasure, you should have taken it out sooner!"

"Do you know about Star Fragments?"

"This is the first time I've seen one in person. According to the records passed down in my family, you can make a very special Law Treasure with a stone containing the energy of a star. If you give me this star, I'll accept your offer. I can work under you for about 30 years, right? Ah, I don't mean I'll work like a slave. I'll get fairly compensated for my labor."

Star Fragments must be very expensive crafting materials.

"I have more Star Fragments. I'd like you to enhance the Stormbreaker with this Star Fragment."

"You have things that even the Emperor doesn't have. Okay. What exactly are the abilities of this Stormbreaker, and how do you want me to enhance it?"

I used the Stormbreaker directly to show her its abilities.

"Of course, it should have the existing abilities... I want you to enhance its defensive abilities."

It's already excellent as a weapon. But I can't deny that its offensive capabilities are inferior compared to the Hwaryeon Bido or the Vajra Divine Lightning. On the other hand, I didn't have any proper armor. I'll take this opportunity to enhance the Stormbreaker's defensive abilities.

"I have a few ideas. The problem is... there are too few Star Fragments. You have more Star Fragments, right?"

"Too few? Star Fragments are priceless items. Perhaps...."

"I don't steal materials. I'll let it slide just this once. Because that's a very serious insult to me."

"......How many do you need?"

"Considering the size and abilities of the Stormbreaker, I need at least three."

I took out three more. I can invest three Star Fragments in the Stormbreaker.

"And as you know... these aren't the only materials I need."

"I'm not going to get materials like Star Fragments."

"Fortunately, it's not that bad. It'll be a bit of a hassle, but you should be able to get them."

200 Heavenly Jades. A piece of the shell of the Great Yokai Taesan Shimbyeol (太山沈鼈), Moonlight Water from Dongting Lake, scales of the Lava Fish (龍巖漁), and a skilled mage. Of course, it has to be a mage you can trust.

I can understand the need for Heavenly Jades. A Heavenly Jade is a concentrated mass of pure spiritual energy (靈氣), so it’s used in many ways, like maintaining barriers or making Law Instruments.

"Do you really need 200 Heavenly Jades? Really?"

"Think about the power of the Stormbreaker. That's a treasure beyond a Law Treasure. It'll be relatively easy to get the materials except for the shell of the Great Mountain Turtle. You can get them with money. The important thing is the mage. You need the help of a skilled mage to safely interpret the spells of the Stormbreaker."

"I have a mage I can trust. He's at the Samjeong Realm."

"The most troublesome problem has been solved. I'll be studying the Stormbreaker, so bring the mage. And of course, bring the materials I mentioned."

"Does it have to be done here? I have a place where I stay. Let's go there. We can go there in one go with spatial movement."

"Do you think artisans stay here for no reason? This place has spiritual veins flowing through it, so it's a good place to make Law Instruments."

"Does your place have spiritual veins too?"

"Really? I need to pack my furnace and tools, so wait a moment."

It was really just a moment.

Her building became as small as a toy and fit right into her spatial storage. The building itself was a Law Instrument.

'The barrier's gone and it's exposed. If I leave it like this, people will flock here.'

I handed Yang Hong-ye a spatial movement scroll and tore it.

Jjieeeek.

I moved to Nakwol Mountain with Yang Hong-ye. It didn't take long to run into Milyeong. Milyeong stared at Yang Hong-ye intently before smiling brightly.

"Oh my. My husband has brought a guest. I'll bring some tea."

It was a little different from when she greeted Yuria and Danmok Soha. She was more friendly, I guess. No matter how much of a fox Milyeong is, she can't treat a woman of similar level carelessly.

"A fox clan, huh? How unique."

Unlike her words, Yang Hong-ye's reaction was indifferent. I pondered how I could get Yang Hong-ye to fall for me. Even when I recalled the information from the original story, I couldn't think of a good way.

I explained the situation to Milyeong, who brought the tea.

"If that's the case, of course I'll cooperate! Just leave it to me!"

Since Milyeong is cooperating, the Stormbreaker research will proceed without major setbacks. Even if I get stuck along the way, I can go to Yuria and ask for answers. Things are going smoothly.

• • •

I was focused on finding the other materials.

It was hard to get 200 Heavenly Jades at once. I sold all the elixirs, Law Instruments, and treasures I received as my share after looting the Thunder Sound Temple, and it still wasn't enough, so I even sold the elixirs I personally had.

Killing martial artists and taking them? There weren't many martial artists carrying Heavenly Jades. The chumps who are easy to kill don't even carry things like Heavenly Jades.

But it's also hard to wipe out small and medium-sized sects and steal their Heavenly Jades. If I did that too much, I'd be marked as a public enemy, and the Imperial Palace would step in directly.

So I endured for now. Even if you're an absolute master, you can't face the world alone.

'I thought about selling the Thunder Sound Temple's martial arts... but the other forces in the Murim or the Imperial Palace are wary of martial arts above a certain level being released to the public.'

All that remains is the shell fragment of the Great Yokai Taesan Shimbyeol (太山沈鼈) and the Moonlight Water of Dongting Lake.

First, I headed to Dongting Lake to get the Moonlight Water, which is relatively easy to obtain.

Moonlight Water is a mysterious water that can be obtained from the lake of Dongting Lake on the night of the full moon. The owner of Dongting Lake is, of course, the Emperor, and the Emperor didn't place any particular restrictions on obtaining Moonlight Water.

Anyone can get this elixir. That was the problem.

There was a long line in front of Dongting Lake. Roughly, there were martial artists lining up, probably around 6,000 of them.

'If there are 6,000 people... if I wait in line honestly, I'll have to wait 180,000 days until it's my turn. Am I crazy to wait that long?'

Judging by the looks of it, the people in line seem to be taking turns waiting for a certain period of time.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

I spread my senses to check the surroundings. Most of the guys in line are at the Foundation Establishment stage. There aren't even 100 martial artists who have reached the Origin Qi Realm. In other words, there's no one stronger than me.

I confidently walked past the line and went forward. Most of the martial artists in line were suppressed by my aura and couldn't say a word.

As I got closer to the front of the line, the level of the martial artists increased proportionally. Among them, there was a martial artist who dared to block my path.

"Senior. If you've come to get Moonlight Water, please line up. The juniors here have been patiently waiting in line for a long time. Please understand the hardships of the juniors."

He takes a fist salute, but his eyes shine sharply. His attitude is so confident that I suspect he's an absolute master like me, so I looked at him again. He was a scrub at the mid-stage of the Origin Qi.

"I understand. But I'm in a bit of a hurry. You, as a junior, should also understand my situation as a senior. That's the right way of the Jianghu. I'll take tonight's Moonlight Water."

The other martial artists in the Origin Qi Realm all looked at me at once. Their gazes became a silent pressure and overwhelmed me.

"Senior. A long time ago, there was never a day when blood didn't dry in front of Dongting Lake. If the order that's being maintained now collapses, blood may flow again in the peaceful Dongting Lake."

"Are you daring to lecture me?"

"Senior! This isn't a lecture, but...!"

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷光).

Seogeok!

I cut the scrub in front of me in the waist. I shook off the blood on the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido and looked around.

"From now on, I'll kill anyone who blocks me. No exceptions."

I kicked the corpse of the martial artist blocking my path and strode forward. No one stopped me.
