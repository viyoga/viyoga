The corpse of the dead Gain disappeared without leaving a single drop of blood. However, the ground that was dug up and the traces of battle remained here.

,I'm satisfied since I've completed the revenge I couldn't before.'

The saying, 'Revenge is futile,' has been proven to be bullshit again.

,It's a shame about the points consumed while killing that bastard.'

Destiny 1,000 points, 24-hour summoning cost 65 points. It was a waste even though I used Destiny as an experiment.

,I've confirmed the power of the Meteor Sword and know what the time acceleration feels like, so shall I go back.'

My steps to get out of the dungeon were light.

[Vampire Detective] ended and one game slot was empty. A new game was about to begin.

,Which game world should I enter? It's fun just thinking about it.'

Thanks to the creations I've been steadily watching, there were many options. Too many. So many that choosing one takes forever.

It was when I was walking happily through the field. I belatedly discovered more than 30 Spirit Orcs gathered in front of me.

I was too excited and my attention was lacking. You can say I was careless.

The name of this dungeon is Spirit Orc Legion. It means that the Spirit Orcs act in groups like an army.

,It's true that I was complacent, but honestly, I don't care if I'm complacent.'

Even if the Spirit Orcs are gathered, I don't think I'll lose.

,I have a chance to relieve stress before leaving the dungeon, so it's actually a good thing.'

Usually, Hunters run away without looking back when they see a group of Orcs. One of the characteristics of Spirit Orcs is that their synergy effect is stronger when they are together. But in the face of a different level of power, that doesn't matter at all.

,Meteor Sword.'

I don't increase the size of the Meteor Sword. The same goes for durability. What I focused on was maximizing the number of Meteor Swords.

Mana is draining in large quantities. At the same time, 72 Meteor Swords appeared in the sky. If I push myself, I could probably make 100, but I had to leave some leeway in case something happened in the future.

,72 is more than enough to overkill.'

The Spirit Orc group prepares to charge. They are drawing out the power of the spirits. Fire, water, ice, rock, etc. I'm suffocated by the dense power of nature. But there's no need to be afraid. Before that power reaches me, my power will reach them first.

I release the Meteor Swords that I was holding with my will. Like firing a gun, 72 Meteor Swords filled with brain energy fall to the ground all at once.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwang!

Along with the shockwave, a storm of lightning covers the Spirit Orc group. There was nothing the Spirit Orc group could do. Most of them died instantly when the Meteor Swords fell, and those who were lucky enough to survive lost their lives to the raging lightning.

The place where the Spirit Orc group was located was devastated. Meteor Swords are stuck in the hollowed-out ground like tombstones, and blue lightning remains on the ground and slowly disappears.

I smelled burning meat. The fragmented pieces of meat were being crisply grilled by the electricity. The fallen Meteor Swords didn't last long and disappeared.

,Even this magic stone is broken.'

I clicked my tongue briefly. The Spirit Orc became minced meat, so there was no way the magic stone would be intact.

A C-grade magic stone is worth an average of 500,000 won. Spirit Orcs are top-tier even among C-grades, so you can get about 700,000 won. If there are 30 of them, that's 21 million won. If you deduct taxes and exchange fees from here, you'll get 60-70% in your hands. If there are party members, it's divided according to their share.

,Most people calculate Hunter income before taxes. In reality, the money Hunters earn is less than you think. Besides, you have to invest in equipment regularly....'

There's a saying that Hunters don't make as much money as you think. It's not completely nonsense. You might think that the income you get compared to risking your life fighting monsters is small. But they still earn much more than ordinary office workers.

,Hunters' income increases exponentially as their rank increases.'

Of course, I don't have much attachment to money. I have the game life app, so I can earn money whenever I want. Even if that's not the case, the money I'm getting through Ha Seunghee-ssi is enormous.

,Achievements are more important to me. Magic stones are also proof that I caught a monster. All the achievements of 30 monsters are gone. Even if I take the magic stone fragments, they won't give me a proper settlement.'

I walked to the devastated land and looked around.

,It's been bothering me since earlier... the amount of electricity is more than I thought.'

Electricity usually scatters. It's rare for it to remain on the ground like this. Unless there's a special action.

,Is it because of mana? Hmm. The output of the lightning is stronger than expected....'

I was staring intently at the electricity when I remembered something.

,Resonance. Did the brain energy in the Meteor Swords resonate with each other?'

I don't know for sure, but it seemed like I saw a new possibility for the Meteor Sword.

,Controlling resonance is difficult even for me now.'

I summoned a Meteor Sword. I grab it with my right hand and swing it in the air. The crude form of the Meteor Sword was neither good nor bad as a sword. But the Meteor Sword I swung once disappeared like dust.

,I can't maintain the Meteor Sword for long. This is probably a problem that will be solved when the level of the Meteor Sword rises.'

Anyway, I've found out everything I need to know.

I was about to move again when I tensed up at the sudden feeling of a presence.

,It's not a monster. It's a human. Quite close. About 20m? To be able to narrow the distance this far without being caught by my senses. It's not a C-grade.'

I looked at one side of the field. I can't see anything with my eyes. But my senses are telling me that someone is there.

The space flickers for a moment.

,No, that's an illusion. It's not the space flickering, it's the wind.'

The person who was hiding his body with the wind revealed himself. It was someone I knew.

,Yujin-hyung!'

It was Park Suho. It was the first time I'd seen him in a while, so Park Suho was quite happy.

,I didn't expect to meet hyung here. Doesn't hyung usually go to B-grade dungeons?'

,I was tired today, so I came to a C-grade dungeon. Also to test my strength.'

,I know this is a dungeon that even B-grade Hunters avoid... Yujin-hyung is amazing as expected.'

,Enough with the flattery. Why are you here? If you're going to make money, other dungeons are more efficient.'

,There's something I want to get.'

I stared intently at Park Suho. Park Suho was stronger than when I saw him before. A more solid body, blatant confidence, refined spirit. It was certain. Park Suho had surpassed the strength of a C-grade. B-grade. A complete B-grade at that. If you only consider physical ability, he's close to an A-grade.

,If you consider Park Suho's tattoo ability, there's no one among the B-grades who can match him.'

It's not for me to say, but Park Suho's growth was excessively fast.

,Your body was completely invisible, did you awaken a new ability? Or is it magic?'

,Ah. It's thanks to this guy.'

Park Suho pointed to the side. The wind gathered and took on a shape. It looked like a seahorse. It was strangely familiar.

,...A spirit?'

,Yes. It's Maya, the spirit of the wind. I made a contract with her a while ago. She's still young, so her power is weak. The reason I was able to hide my appearance is also Maya's power.'

,Amazing. I heard that spirits are really rare.'

There were spiritists who had contracts with spirits in reality as well. But they were very rare. Even in Korea, which has a large number of Hunters compared to the population, there are less than 100 spiritists.

,As hyung can guess, Maya is not a spirit from this world.'

,The tattoo world?'

,Yes. I met and made a contract in the tattoo world. But I can bring her to reality.'

,.......'

Is it because she's a spirit? Or is Park Suho special?

,Did you come to this dungeon because of the spirit?'

,Yes. I heard that Spirit Orc fangs are good for spirits. Maya is still young, so I have to take care of her steadily like this so she can grow quickly.'

,Did she grow?'

Park Suho sighed and shook his head.

,No. The growth is much slower than I thought. It's not that there's no effect at all, but I wouldn't say it has the effect I expected.'

,I don't know much about spirits either, so I can't say anything.'

,It's okay. Spiritists are rare even in the tattoo world. Ah, did Yujin-hyung do this?'

,I was just experimenting with some new techniques. The results are as you can see. You could say it's a kind of ultimate move.'

,That's amazing. But the magic stones are broken too... that's a bit of a waste.'

Park Suho looked at the ruined land and licked his lips. You could say he's a money-crazed bastard.

,It's been a while since I've met Park Suho... shall I build up some goodwill?'

Park Suho is a capable guy, so I'll put him in debt first.

,I happen to have something good for spirits. Shall I give it to you?'

,You have something like that?'

,I also got it by chance in the dungeon.'

What I summoned from the inventory as if taking it out of my pocket was a small blue jewel, a Spirit Jade.

[Spirit Jade

A jewel imbued with the power of pure nature.

Spirits will love it.

Price: 20 points.

※Caution

Only effective for spirits.]

As soon as Park Suho's wind spirit saw the Spirit Jade on my palm, it flew towards me as if hypnotized.

,Maya?!'

Park Suho grabbed the spirit in a panic. The spirit didn't shake off Park Suho's hand, but its gaze was still rooted in my hand.

,It's called a Spirit Jade, and it's said to contain the power of pure nature.'

,Judging from Maya's reaction, it seems like an incredibly good item. I can't just take it.'

,It's okay. I have a few more.'

I flicked the Spirit Jade with my finger and threw it to the spirit. The spirit, which resembled a seahorse, opened its mouth wide, unlike its appearance, and swallowed the Spirit Jade in one gulp.

The spirit's power exploded from the spirit's body, which was slightly larger than my palm. Wind blew and rotated around the spirit. The swirling wind soon seeped into the spirit's body and disappeared.

There were also changes in the spirit's body. Its size became a little larger, and I felt a more powerful force than before.

,It's so weak that it grows noticeably with just one Spirit Jade.'

Park Suho had a dumbfounded expression. He didn't expect the effect to be this good. But that was only for a moment, and soon Park Suho began to get excited.
