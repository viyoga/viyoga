Kyle, the Head of the small Hwasan Sect, had a monotonous daily life.

5 AM. Kyle wakes up before sunrise. He makes his bed and goes outside. He sits cross-legged under a tree and trains his mind and body.

At 7 AM, after breakfast, he teaches the newbies who have recently joined the Hwasan Sect until dinner.

It was an excessively monotonous daily life.

However, Kyle was satisfied. No, he had to be satisfied. The founding of the Hwasan Sect was his dream. Although he couldn't become Count Prucus himself, he was able to establish the Hwasan Sect with his brother's help. His dream had come true.

Since his dream had come true, it was definitely a satisfying life. However, Kyle felt a sense of emptiness in one corner of his heart. That emptiness did not easily disappear even after several days.

Whenever Kyle felt empty, he would stop training and go for a walk. If he tried to train with a scattered mind, he could fall into Juha ipma. He had already experienced falling into Juha ipma once, so he couldn't help but be cautious of it.

Kyle's walking route was around the Hwasan Sect and the outskirts of the city. Refugees had settled on the outskirts of the city. They consisted of women, children, and the elderly. It was hard to see young men. A young man he happened to see was missing a leg. Just like he was missing his left arm.

,Their lives will become more and more desolate as time goes on. The war needs to end as soon as possible....'

He wanted to support the refugees with food, but all Kyle had was the Hwasan Sect. He felt bad asking Eugene, Count Prucus and his younger brother, for help with this. Kyle felt guilty towards Eugene.

Kyle's meeting with Silly-ssi was accidental.

Silly-ssi was running down the street, seemingly in a hurry, when she tripped over a rock and fell to the ground, and Kyle took her hand and helped her up.

,Th, thank you.'

Silly-ssi blushed as if she was embarrassed by her fall and expressed her gratitude. Kyle was momentarily surprised when he saw Silly-ssi's face head-on. It was because memories of his past life suddenly came to him.

,Nanyoung Sajae....'

She looked very similar to Nanyoung Sajae of the Hwasan Sect, whom he could no longer see. Not only her appearance but also her aura.

,Do you have something urgent?'

,I was on my way to deliver potato soup to Grandma Elga. Grandma Elga really hates being late.'

,You still need to be careful. Are you hurt anywhere?'

,I'm okay. I've been falling a lot since I was young, so I'm used to it. You could say I have a knack for falling without getting hurt...? And the potato soup is safe too! Thank you for your help!'

After checking the potato soup in the container, Silly-ssi immediately prepared to run again. Kyle unknowingly blocked her path.

,Uh... I'm Kyle from the Hwasan Sect.'

,Kyle... Gasp! You're Kyle Prucus-ssi, right? I, I dare not recognize a nobleman...!'

Kyle hurriedly stopped her as she tried to kneel on the ground.

,Please don't do that. I didn't reveal my name to make you kneel. I just wanted you to know my name. What's your name?'

,Ah.... I'm Silly-ssi Zefnik.'

Kyle scratched his head. He was not used to the current situation. However, what was certain was that he didn't want to end his relationship with Silly-ssi like this.

The container in Silly-ssi's hand caught his eye.

,That potato soup smells delicious. Did you perhaps make it yourself?'

,Yes, I did make it....'

Silly-ssi's eyes trembled anxiously. She hoped that Kyle wouldn't ask for the potato soup.

,Would you perhaps be willing to help with the kitchen work at the Hwasan Sect? We have a lot of people, so we're a bit short-handed in the kitchen. I'll make sure you're well compensated. How about 2 million Nerus a month?'

Silly-ssi's expression brightened momentarily. 2 million Nerus. It was definitely not a small amount of money for commoners. If she had a fixed income of 2 million Nerus a month... her life would be much better.

However, her face hardened in an instant as she recalled what her mother, Martina, used to say.

,It's a kind offer, but.... I have to work in the fields during the day. I can't leave my mother to work alone.'

Kyle felt her wariness towards him. Normally, he would have backed off here.

,Then, could you come in the afternoon and just help with dinner?'

The field work ended in the morning. The field itself wasn't that big, and there were plenty of people who wanted to work. If she could work in the afternoon, which was a less busy time... Silly-ssi hesitated before making a decision.

,I'll work. So, I can go to the Hwasan Sect tomorrow afternoon and help with the kitchen work, right?'

,Yes. I'll be waiting.'

,Th... Can I go now?'

,Of course.'

Kyle stepped aside. Silly-ssi ran off somewhere as eagerly as she had before. Seeing her swaying body, Kyle felt the emptiness disappear.

More than ten days had passed since then.

Kyle headed towards the entrance of the Hwasan Sect. He could see Silly-ssi running from afar. Perhaps it was because she had been working in the fields. Surprisingly, she was in good shape and always ran as if she was in a hurry. The same was true when she came to work and when she left.

,Haha. Kyle-nim. It seems you like her.'

A knight who trained under him spoke to Kyle. Kyle chuckled and shook his head.

,It's not like that. I'm just worried that you guys might bother Silly-ssi, so I'm keeping an eye on her.'

,She's beautiful, so she's popular among us. The day before yesterday, a new recruit confessed to her.'

,What?'

,Haha. Kyle-nim. Cheer up. The new recruit was immediately rejected. According to the new recruit, it seemed like she already had someone in her heart, so there was no chance at all.'

,Someone she likes...?'

,Who else could it be? Who else is there besides Kyle-nim? Kyle-nim has a good personality, a good family, and good abilities. If I were a woman, I would have fallen for Kyle-nim too.'

,...Stop talking nonsense and go train.'

,Understood. I'm really looking forward to dinner tonight too. Ah, Kyle-nim. I heard that the 3rd Corps, which went up to the northern front, will be returning in a week. They say they're holding a homecoming festival to coincide with that time.'

,I heard about that too.'

,Enjoy the festival with her. Opportunities like this don't come often. I also met my current wife at a festival.'

The knight smiled and headed to the training ground.

,A festival....'

Kyle momentarily thought of Yuria-ssi. He shook his head violently. She was a woman who would soon become his brother's wife.

,Kyle!'

Silly-ssi called him. Over the past few weeks, Kyle and Silly-ssi had become quite close. When they were alone, they didn't care about each other's status and enjoyed talking to each other.

,You're here?'

,Yeah. Sorry. Am I a little late?'

,You're a little late. Did something happen?'

,Well... I forgot about the time while playing with Elsie.'

,Elsie is Silly-ssi's younger sister, right? If it's hard to take care of your younger sister, you can bring her with you.'

,She won't come. Elsie really likes playing at home. I'm going to the kitchen now.'

,Silly-ssi.'

Kyle hurriedly stopped her as she headed to the kitchen.

,Yeah?'

,I heard that they're holding a festival next week to coincide with the return of the 3rd Corps. Would you like to go to the festival with me?'

,A festival.... I wonder if I'll have time then....'

Kyle was flustered. Unlike her usual lively self, her reaction was unexpectedly lukewarm. Kyle thought for a moment and said.

,You said you wanted to see the Prucus Count's mansion last time, right? I'll give you a tour during this festival.'

Silly-ssi, who was startled, hurriedly waved her hands.

,N, no! I was just wondering what it would be like to see the mansion up close!'

,It's okay. It would be difficult to formally invite you... but giving you a tour is possible. I'm also a member of the Prucus family.'

,B, but.'

,There's no problem. We're just going into the mansion and coming out.'

,...Is it really okay?'

,I guarantee it.'

Kyle said with a confident face.

Silly-ssi hesitated before accepting his offer.

She was very curious about the mansion where the Prucus Count usually lived.

,Thank you, Kyle.'

,It's nothing.'

,I have to prepare dinner, so I'll go to the kitchen now.'

I entered the Zefnik house in the morning and played with Elsie. Perhaps because I played with her too excitedly, Elsie fell asleep as soon as she sat down in a chair. I took Elsie to her room so she could take a comfortable nap.

,Silly-ssi went to work at the Hwasan Sect... and Elsie, the only troublemaker, just fell asleep.'

Elsie would wake up around evening.

I headed to Martina's bedroom.

Knock knock knock.

,Martina. May I come in?'

,Yes, of course. Count-nim.'

I opened the door.

Martina was sitting on the bed.

She was wearing comfortable clothes. Her long brown hair was tied in a low bun, revealing her slender, white neckline. She smiled when she made eye contact with me.

,How's your left leg?'

I asked as I sat down on the chair next to the bed.

,It doesn't hurt anymore. I feel like I can walk right now.'

,No. Follow the doctor's orders and rest a little more.'

,Understood.'

Martina replied calmly.

,.......'

I looked at the window next to the bed with a serious face. The scenery visible beyond the window was quiet. Perhaps because it was on the outskirts of the city, I couldn't see many people passing by.

,Th... Count-nim?'

Martina's face also hardened due to the unusual atmosphere.

,Do you have any worries? I don't have the ability to solve Count-nim's worries, but... I can listen to them. I'm tight-lipped.'

,Hoo.... Martina. It's not that. This is a problem for the Zefnik family.'

,Yes?'

I pretended to hesitate for a long time before speaking.

,Since it's a problem for the Zefnik family... I should tell you. Martina, brace yourself and listen. Alphonse Zefnik has been killed in action.'

,Ye, yes? Th, that's a lie, right?'

,...I only heard the information this morning. The battle was so fierce that they couldn't even recover his body.'

,Ah, ah... Alphonse....'

Martina's upper body staggered. I got up from my seat and grabbed her shoulder. Tears streamed down her eyes.

,Sniff.... Sniff....'

,I'm sorry to have to deliver this news to you, Martina. And... I'm sorry to you. There was nothing I could do.'

,Sniff.... It's not Count-nim's fault.... It's not Count-nim's fault at all. Alphonse... Sniff.... Sniff.'

I hugged Martina's shoulder. She, who always tried to be confident as the mother of two daughters, collapsed and hugged me. The tears she shed soaked my top. I stroked her back without saying anything, as if to comfort her.
