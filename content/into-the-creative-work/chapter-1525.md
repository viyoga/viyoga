I arrived at the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

I had been away for almost a year, but nothing had changed. They say even mountains and rivers change in ten years, but I guarantee this world won't change much even after ten years.

,Still, the atmosphere has changed.'

I can see worry on people's faces. This is because the war between the Cheonma Shin-gyo and the Martial Arts Alliance is imminent.

Maybe martial artists are different. I don't think there are any ordinary people who like war.

I, who returned to the Cheonma Shin-gyo, went to the Jeokmyeoldae headquarters.

I can see the Jeokmyeoldae members. The last time I saw them, they seemed somewhat relaxed, but this time it was different. I can feel the tension from them. Some were lightly swinging their weapons in the training hall.

,The youngest is here!'

,Did you finish your mission well?'

,You haven't even been in the Jeokmyeoldae for a few years, and now there's a war... the youngest's fate is also unfortunate. Well, that's how all Jeokmyeoldae members are.'

The Jeokmyeoldae members said a word to me. They weren't close, but they were people I had talked to a few times before. I dealt with them while hiding my inner thoughts full of annoyance.

,The mission failed. It was a mission to win over Head Jang, but Head Jang didn't even pretend to listen to me. In the end, I had no choice but to deal with Head Jang.'

I explained about the mission. The Jeokmyeoldae members nodded with a faint expression.

,A recruitment mission... it's not an easy mission.'

,The ignorant think of the Cheonma Shin-gyo as a den of evil, hell on earth.'

,Reminds me of the old days. I kidnapped the person I was supposed to recruit and brought them to the Shin-gyo. Thanks to that, I got an earful from Daeju-nim.'

The Jeokmyeoldae members each poured out various stories. I listened to their words appropriately and asked.

,I don't see Oh-bong-nim. Did he go out on a mission?'

Oh-bong, the 6th squad leader of the Jeokmyeoldae. He was the man who could be said to be my direct superior.

,Oh-bong-nim went out on a mission. I heard it was a mission related to the Martial Arts Alliance.'

,Come to think of it, didn't the youngest come from Jongnam Mountain? What exactly happened in the Jongnam sect?'

Everyone's eyes are on me. Of course, I had prepared an excuse. It was the moment I was about to open my mouth. A man broke through the crowd and came to me.

,6th Squad Member Yeom Gu-seok! Daeju-nim is calling for you! Go to Daeju-nim's room right now!'

The crowd that surrounded me parted, creating a path. I headed straight to Daeju-nim's room along that path.

Jeokmyeoldaeju (寂滅隊主) Jeok Ran-am Gwi (赤鸞唵鬼) Shin Ga-gyeom. The red-haired man was sitting in a chair, drinking tea. It was a somewhat incongruous sight considering his muscular, massive body and scarred face.

,Jeokmyeoldae 6th Squad Yeom Gu-seok! I have returned!'

,You've come? You've worked hard. How was the mission?'

,The mission target, Hwang So-dong, rejected the proposal, so I dealt with him. I killed his family and burned down the smithy.'

,I like that you handle things thoroughly. You know how the political situation in the Murim is going right now, right?'

,I heard that the war is right around the corner.'

,The war has begun. It wouldn't be strange if the Martial Arts Alliance bastards attacked today.'

,Do those bastards start wars without a declaration of war? Those who brag about being righteous factions have no justice.'

,The Martial Arts Alliance is claiming that we attacked the Jongnam sect first. Most people are swept away by that public opinion and believe that all the blame lies with the Shin-gyo. They are ignorant.'

,I am ready to kill the Martial Arts Alliance trash.'

I said in a strong voice. Jeokmyeoldaeju chuckled and nodded.

,Including you, no one in the Jeokmyeoldae is afraid of war. But before the war, there is a question. The Jongnam sect attack incident. The Martial Arts Alliance says that the Shin-gyo attacked the Jongnam sect, but in reality, the Shin-gyo did nothing. You were dispatched to a city near Jongnam Mountain at that time due to a mission. Can you tell me what happened?'

I shouldn't hesitate or show any signs of hiding anything here. In the worst case, they could sell me to the Martial Arts Alliance to stop the war. Of course, it's just one of the very slim possibilities, but being suspected itself wasn't good for me.

,At that time, I happened to be in Jongnam City. It was for the purpose of seeing how great Jongnam's Heavenly Emperor of Jongnam was.'

,Hooh. Did you see the Heavenly Emperor of Jongnam?'

,I couldn't. Before entering the Jongnam sect, monsters attacked.'

Jeokmyeoldaeju straightened his back. Even though he already knew from the report, it meant that he would listen to my words seriously. Or it could be for the purpose of comparing information.

I poured out the words that I had prepared in advance with the Rakshasa Woman.

,How many monsters were there? What was their level?'

,I don't know the exact number of monsters, but I don't think there were more than a hundred. Their level was quite high. There were even monsters ranging from Chulji to Ogi at the minimum.'

,Didn't you fight the monsters?'

,I couldn't reveal my identity, so I quietly hid and watched. A battle between the Jongnam sect martial artists and the monsters took place. At first, the monsters unilaterally massacred people, but as time passed, the Jongnam sect martial artists subjugated most of the monsters. I saw the surviving monsters run out of the city.'

,Was that all? Weren't there anything special about the monsters?'

,...It seemed like someone was supporting the monsters with magic. I don't know much about magic, so I don't know the details.'

,A sorcerer. Since a barrier would have been set up in Jongnam City as well, the monster would have had to solve the barrier problem to attack. The bastards who planned and executed this are not ordinary bastards. There is a rumor that a Shin-gyo person attacked the Jongnam sect. Did you see that Shin-gyo person?'

,I was hiding in Jongnam City, so I didn't see it directly, but I felt a strong Demonic energy.'

,You felt Demonic energy even in the city below Jongnam sect?'

Jeokmyeoldaeju widened his eyes as if surprised.

As the person who used Cheonma Shingong, I don't know how far the Cheonma energy spread. However, according to Rakshasa Woman, ordinary people felt the eerie presence of Demonic energy. That's why the Martial Arts Alliance is convinced that it was the work of the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

,I felt it. It was an enormous Demonic energy. It was a qualitatively different feeling from the Demonic energy I know. Perhaps Cheonma-nim... .'

,Stop!'

Jeokmyeoldaeju's energy exploded.

I was overwhelmed and quickly shut my mouth. This was not acting. I was actually overwhelmed by Jeokmyeoldaeju's power for a moment.

,...Considering Jeokmyeoldaeju's power, it's not a strange thing.'

It is said that Jeokmyeoldaeju Shin Ga-gyeom is a master who has reached at least the 5th level of Samjeong. That's quite an old saying. Nowadays, there are rumors that he is peeking into the next level of Samjeong, Johwa (造化).

Jeokmyeoldaeju sighed and calmed his energy.

,I'm sorry. I got a little upset for a moment. Cheonma-nim is always staying in the Shin-gyo. There's no way Cheonma-nim would have gone to Jongnam. There's no reason to go. And if Cheonma-nim had gone... Jongnam would have disappeared.'

,It may be a rude question, but... .'

,What is it?'

,Why isn't Cheonma-nim active?'

,...Cheonma-nim is preparing to reach the realm of Mansang. That's why you can't enter the Cheonma Palace recklessly. Don't question him. We just have to follow him. He is the supreme being of all things.'

Jeokmyeoldaeju says. There was not a single doubt in his voice praising Cheonma.

I was lost in thought for a moment.

In-gyeong (human realm).

The seven realms of humans.

Ipsik (Entering Form) ‣ Chulji (Revealing Will) ‣ Ogi (Five Energies) ‣ Samjeong (Three Peaks) ‣ Johwa (Creation) ‣ Mansang (All Phenomena) ‣ Deungseon (Ascend to Immortality).

Mansang is a realm beyond Johwa-gyeong. Mansang is virtually the highest realm that humans can reach. The moment you reach the realm of Deungseon, you are reborn as a Seonin, not a human, and ascend to the Seon-gye.

,If it's the same as the original, the current Cheonma is sick and groaning.'

Unlike the original, it was a Xianxia background, so it may not be a disease. The important thing is that the current Cheonma, Cheon Seong-jin, does not meet anyone except for the important figures of the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

,Jeokmyeoldaeju knows the state of Cheonma. Cheon Yu-un must also know the state of Cheonma.'

War is inevitable.

For the past few decades, the Murim has been too peaceful. It's time for a bloodbath. The Empire, the Cheonma Shin-gyo, and the Martial Arts Alliance. The three forces are expecting to gain benefits through war.

,Yeom Gu-seok. Do you understand?'

,Of course. I have never doubted Cheonma-nim. What should I do in the future? Has a mission been assigned to the Jeokmyeoldae?'

,Only a few have received missions. The rest will return. Cheonma-nim has ordered an internal cleanup before the war. His will is supreme, so our Jeokmyeoldae cannot avoid it.'

,Internal cleanup... is it a purge? Are we the targets of the purge?'

,You are not the target of the purge, but the target of appreciation. Do you have anything to feel guilty about?'

,No. My loyalty to the Shin-gyo has never wavered.'

,Then there will be no problem. Wait while the audit is in progress. Ah, there will be someone from another unit looking for you. You just have to tell them what you experienced in the Jongnam sect.'

,I understand. May I ask which unit is in charge of the audit?'

Jeokmyeoldaeju's face was annoyed.

There is only one unit that can do this kind of work: the Yeommadae (閻魔隊).

It was the unit that Cheon Yu-un and Je Gal Mo-sun belonged to.

•••

Cheon Yu-un and the Yeommadae came to the Jeokmyeoldae two days later.

The Jeokmyeoldae members glared at the Yeommadae, who were confidently walking into the entrance, as if they were going to kill them. The Jeokmyeoldae and the Yeommadae have traditionally had a bad relationship.

Usually, the Jeokmyeoldae unknowingly interferes with the Yeommadae's audit, but this time they couldn't do anything. This is because Cheonma ordered this audit. If you interfere with the Yeommadae now, it won't end with just dying. The best thing to do is to cooperate willingly.

The Yeommadae members began to search the Jeokmyeoldae's headquarters. The Jeokmyeoldae members watched the scene with annoyed eyes.

,I will start one-on-one interviews from now on.'

The audit began.

,Jeokmyeoldae 6th Squad Yeom Gu-seok.'

Cheon Yu-un called my name. I followed him into the room. We sat facing each other in a closed room with the windows tightly closed.

,It's been a while, Yeom Gu-seok.'

,...Yeah. It's been a while. Originally, I was going to meet you as soon as I arrived at the Shin-gyo, but... I couldn't.'

,I understand. Since the situation is what it is, it's better to avoid suspicion. You made a good choice. First, let's finish what we have to do. Heavenly Emperor of Jongnam. Tell me about the events of that day.'
