"Dark Lightning!!"

Black lightning shoots towards the Cyclops' eye. The giant eye, recognizing the black lightning, wavers. It seems to realize it's too late to dodge and tightly shuts its eye.

The black lightning strikes the eyelid and bounces off. The giant eye slightly opens. It didn't inflict any meaningful damage. The Cyclops' lips curl up. The bastard was mocking me.

"This son of a bitch...."

I wanted to show him what real lightning tasted like. But to do that, I'd have to change my status window, and I don't know what might happen if I change it while this special event is in progress.

In the worst case, the undead army could evaporate, and I could lose the souls I've captured. I may like gambling, but I'm not stupid enough to gamble when I have more to lose than gain.

"He's trying to use his laser again. Cannon Zombies, aim for his eye."

"Kuaaaa...."

The Cannon Zombies raise their cannon arms. They're aimed at the Cyclops' head.

Kwaaaang!

The attack begins with a roar that vibrates the space. The Cannon Zombies fire balls of fire, balls of ice, and balls of energy.

The accuracy was terrible. They aimed for the Cyclops' head, but didn't hit it once. Most of them hit the Cyclops' upper body. That alone was helpful. The Cyclops' massive body shook slightly from being hit by the cannons.

"Kuaaaaaaaaah!!"

The Cyclops screams and rages. The laser shot from his single eye burns the Cannon Zombies, and his giant fists and feet slam into the Cannon Zombies.

'His intelligence doesn't seem that high. That should make him easier to deal with.'

I waved my hand. The giant grasshoppers started running around like crazy. The surprised Cyclops falters and waves his arms to catch the giant grasshoppers. From the Cyclops' perspective, the giant grasshoppers would feel like pests biting at his flesh.

Kwajik!

Perhaps because the Cyclops is so huge, the giant grasshoppers are swept away and killed even by his casual flailing of arms and legs. But their deaths weren't meaningless. The giant horsehair worms that were parasitizing the giant grasshoppers burrowed into the Cyclops' body.

'They can't get in very deep.'

Even though they're giant horsehair worms, the Cyclops is just too big. And the Cyclops' flesh isn't ordinary. It's not as hard as steel, but it's harder than stone. That's why the giant grasshoppers can't easily tear through the giant's skin.

Energy gathers in the Cyclops' eye. I cast a curse on him. An invisible curse, a curse that drains his energy. Hit by two curses, the Cyclops couldn't fire his laser.

'At this rate, I'll win. I'm sure of it.'

If I can just keep him from firing his laser, the undead army will take care of the rest.

Even small damage, when accumulated, can become a major injury that threatens one's life.

You can't ignore an enemy just because they're small. There are humans who have died from being bitten by ants smaller than fingernails, and there are humans who have died from being stung by wasps. And these are undead. They're much more toxic than ants or wasps.

"Kuaaaaaaaaah!"

The Cyclops lets out a painful roar from the undead army's continued attacks.

That's when the change happened. A massive wave of power erupted from the Cyclops' body, shaking off the undead clinging to his skin. Complex red patterns appeared on his skin, emitting light. I felt an undeniable power from those patterns.

'I need to step up and be more active.'

I wanted to observe the power of the undead army, but I felt like the worst-case scenario could happen if I wasn't careful.

I used the black magic I had learned this time. It was a black magic called Wraith Shackles.

Chanting the spell, I slammed Moses' staff into the ground. Starting from that point, a jet-black shadow raced towards the Cyclops. The shadow, taking root beneath the Cyclops' feet, grew in size.

When the area beneath the giant's feet was completely darkened, ten giant tentacles resembling squid burst out. The tentacles grabbed and restrained the Cyclops' body.

'Is it because he's so big? This unexpectedly requires concentration.'

Thanks to my Black Magic Aptitude (SSS), I can learn and use black magic easily. But using and maintaining black magic doesn't suit my temperament. Unlike raising the undead, curses and offensive black magic required constant concentration.

'It feels like I'm studying.'

Squirm, squirm, squirm.

The tentacles grab the giant's body and pull him down. The giant knelt.

"Kuaaaaaaaaah!"

The giant thrashes. The tentacles break one by one. The giant's strength was beyond imagination.

"What are you doing! Don't just stand there and watch, attack him! Finish him off!!"

I shouted at the undead army. I was pissed off at the undead bastards' attitude of just watching while I, their master, was suffering. The undead army, having been yelled at, desperately charged at the Cyclops.

Kuuung!

The Cyclops' upper body fell to the ground. His wounds were a bloody mess from the accumulation of small attacks. His skin was torn, and his flesh was visible. The undead riding on his body continued their attacks without getting tired.

"Krurururu...."

The Cyclops made a beast-like sound. His single giant eye shed tears of blood and emitted light.

Sensing that the final attack was coming, I leaped forward.

The Cyclops' shining eye stared directly at me. The light gathered in the center of his eye, then became a laser with overwhelming heat and shot towards me.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Shinjang (Heavenly Demon Divine Palm)

I held out my palm. I concentrated the Cheonma energy into my palm. Using my palm, overflowing with Cheonma energy, as a shield, I moved forward.

The laser fired by the Cyclops collided with my palm. I put strength in my legs and approached one step at a time.

"Kuaaaaaaaaah!"

The bastard screamed. The laser output became stronger. I stopped advancing. I put strength in my feet and planted them on the ground. I focused on enduring.

"Kill him while I hold out!"

The undead army's attacks didn't stop. While I endured, they continued to attack the fallen giant.

Smoke rose from my palm. My hand was on fire. The Cheonma Shinjang (天魔神掌) was also nearing its limit.

And then the laser output decreased. The Cyclops' eye slowly began to close.

I saw a giant fly clinging to his eyelid. I squeezed out my mana and used black magic.

"Corpse Explosion!!"

The giant fly exploded. Blood splattered. The Cyclops couldn't even scream. His large eye closed, and his head limply drooped to the floor, never to move again.

「You have defeated the Eye of Light (False).」

「You have slain a giant.」

「You have been granted the title 'Giant Slayer'.」

「Strength permanently increased by 1.」

The Cyclops died.

Thump, thump, thump!

The undead attack the Cyclops on top of his corpse.

"You clueless bastards! Get lost!"

When I shouted, the undead retreated from the Cyclops' corpse.

Normally, when a dignitary dies, their corpse immediately disappears. Instead of the corpse, an item with special effects is given as a reward. But the Cyclops in front of me now, his corpse hasn't disappeared.

'Is it because I'm a necromancer right now? This corpse itself is a reward for me.'

I'll raise the Cyclops as an undead. The type didn't matter. Just the Cyclops' size alone would make him a useful undead.

"Arise."

I used black magic. The black magic permeated the giant's corpse.

The giant's corpse didn't even twitch. Thanks to my Black Magic Aptitude (SSS), I immediately realized what had failed.

'I can create even high-level undead. To make this bastard an undead in his complete state, I need the highest-level undead creation method.'

Of course, there are other ways to utilize the giant's corpse. Like using this corpse to create several high-level undead.

'That's a second-best option. The only way to properly utilize the giant's corpse is to make the giant himself an undead.'

There was no need to be impatient.

By killing the Cyclops, I satisfied the condition to obtain the Book of the Dead (3). If I can complete the Book of the Dead, I'll be able to make him a supreme-grade undead.

I separated about 200 undead.

"You guys, guard the giant's corpse."

While I'm away, another group could appear and take the giant's corpse. Or a monster could appear and eat the corpse.

"Guard it no matter what. I'd like to take the corpse with me, but... it would be too late."

"Do not worry. We will protect the giant's corpse even if we have to give our lives."

The Skeleton Priest replied.

With this, I can unlock the seal on the Book of the Dead (3). I grinned silently, splitting my mouth. But no one could see my face because of the golden mask.

"I will go to the altar."

"I will guide you."

"That's okay. You're too slow."

I dashed towards the altar where the Book of the Dead (3) was.

In an instant, I climbed onto the altar and reached out to the Book of the Dead (3). The three seals shone and disappeared along with the seal. My hand grabbed the Book of the Dead.

"It's time for fusion."
