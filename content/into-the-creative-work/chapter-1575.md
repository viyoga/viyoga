,Keukeu. You're holding on pretty well? Let's see if you can hold on even with your arm chopped off and your eyeballs burst? Wanna feel what it's like to have your brain physically churned?"

,You think I can't? I don't die even if I'm stabbed in the heart!"

,More importantly, where did you get that red gem? Let's share the good stuff. Is the power evolution permanent? Or is it temporary?"

,You son of a bitch! You're bluffing. I was going to play with you a bit before killing you, but... the Overlord Sword Clan will be here soon, so just die already!"

Blood gushed out of my thigh along with intense pain. At the same time, Go Sook suffered the same wound on his thigh as I did.

,Kkeuaaaak! You bastard...!"

Certainly, Go Sook's wound was shallower than mine. But that didn't mean it wasn't dangerous. Even a small wound could become dangerous if it continued. So, it was necessary to push him before his wound healed.

Pook!

I stabbed Hwaryeon Bido into my other thigh.

,Aaaaaaaak!"

I was the one who harmed myself, but it was Go Sook who screamed in pain. In reality, I would feel more pain. Even if the wounds were the same, I would suffer them bigger and deeper.

I think it's a difference in familiarity. I became accustomed to pain as I died. Perhaps the influence of Absolute Spirit was also a factor. But what about the other guy?

,He doesn't have Absolute Spirit, and he's never fought like this as a daily routine.'

Go Sook writhed in pain, grinding his teeth. He committed suicide to escape the pain and end this battle. Of course, I wasn't going to stand still. I cut off my own left arm with Hwaryeon Bido.

Seogeok.

,You crazy bastard!!"

,Tell me where you got the red gem you ate. Then I'll kill you cleanly."

,Shut up! This is my power! I'm the one who has the upper hand!"

Go Sook prepared to stab himself in the heart with his trembling hand.

There was hesitation.

,What are you doing? If you're going to do it, do it right."

,Kkeuaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!"

This was hard for me too. I gritted my teeth and swallowed my screams. I had no intention of collapsing to the floor and writhing like a bug like him.

,I understand! I lost! I lost, so stop! Stop it!!"

,Who is that Party cadre?"

,I don't know."

,You don't know? Are you kidding me right now? You bribe someone you don't know?"

I wasn't getting the information I wanted. The more I continued the conversation, the more annoyed I became.

,I don't have the shrine! The one I just ate was the last shrine I had! Why, are you pissed? Kill me if you can! If you can kill me, that is!"

,I've known for a while that you're at your limit. Just die already!"

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]
