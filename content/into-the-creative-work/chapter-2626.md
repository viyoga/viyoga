London Knights.

The undisputed top military force of Neo London.

If the London Guard is the police responsible for the security of Neo London, the London Knights are a special military unit that maintains the existence of Neo London.

The London Knights step in to resolve issues that the London Guard's military strength usually can't handle. However, the London Knights aren't under the London Guard. They cooperate with the London Guard but are an independent organization. Only the Neo Round Table Council could directly command the London Knights.

The London Knights are divided into four orders: Wales, Ireland, England, and Scotland.

,Once they move, they're known for wiping out all the dangerous elements in London. That's why they don't move easily. Most information about the London Knights is classified, so the information released to the press is limited.,

But the London Knights moved.

I can't say I don't understand. This is G District, a wealthy neighborhood. Lower nobles, quasi-nobles, and some knights mainly live here. It's a bit backward for a Count like me, but I like this quiet neighborhood.

,A 7th-circle magic like Thunder Break fell in this neighborhood... It's understandable why the London Knights would be dispatched.,

The London Knights' transport helicopter landed on the ground. The doors opened, and knights wearing the latest full plate armor, commonly called Powered Armor, disembarked. Of course, their faces were hidden by their helmets.

Only a few London Knights' personal information is known, mainly the commanders.

Landing lightly on the ground, they moved swiftly, subduing and restraining the maids and butlers who were still alive.

Then, one of them, a knight in particularly luxurious-looking powered armor, approached. A red dragon was painted on their left shoulder, indicating they belonged to the London Knights Wales Order.

The knight came to me and removed their helmet.

It was a woman with fiery red hair. Her eyes were red like her hair. She was a strikingly beautiful woman.

"It seems we're too late, Count Mayer-kakka."

"...I never thought I'd meet Armina Braver like this."

"You know me?"

"There's no one in Neo London who doesn't know you. Who wouldn't know the Knight of Blazing Flames, the future commander candidate of the Wales Order?"

"Is my fame as great as Count Mayer-kakka, the owner of room-temperature superconductors?"

Armina stared intently at me, as if appraising me, measuring my worth.

"Ahem."

Slightly burdened, I cleared my throat. Her gaze dropped.

"I apologize. I was rude, meeting such a celebrity."

"...I don't think that was the only reason for that look just now."

"I was also curious about Yuria's owner."

"Yuria? Ah, I heard Yuria was in the knight training academy before she met me."

"Yes. She was my classmate and friend. We haven't been in touch much lately... I guess it can't be helped since she's at the maid academy. I'm sure Count-kakka has heard a lot about me from Yuria."

Armina said it as if it were obvious. Her eyes sparkled when she said Yuria's name.

Unfortunately, I've never heard Yuria talk about Armina. Yuria didn't talk about her past much in the first place. I was the same, so we didn't ask each other about our pasts.

"Well, I've heard a bit."

"I suppose so. Yuria and I were roommates since we were young, so it's impossible for my name not to come up."

"Childhood friends?"

"We're more than that."

More than childhood friends... Lovers? No. Yuria didn't seem interested in Armina at all. When Armina's interview video happened to be on TV when they were together, she didn't even bat an eye.

It wasn't dislike, but the gaze of someone looking at a 100% stranger who had nothing to do with her.

"Honestly, I think Yuria should be a knight... but it's Yuria's choice, and I shouldn't say this to Count-kakka."

Didn't you just say it?

Armina glared at me for a moment before turning her gaze to the restrained maids and butlers.

"We'll take them for interrogation. If you wish to interrogate them yourself, contact the Neo Round Table Council."

"...Aren't you being too confident for arriving so late? I almost died here."

"We dispatched as quickly as possible. The council's approval was unusually fast."

She wasn't wrong. The battle time between me and the enemies was less than 5 minutes, including the conversation.

"If you're dissatisfied with our slowness, subscribe to London Knights Insurance. Only those who are insured can be dispatched without the Neo Round Table Council's approval."

"I know. Isn't that the most expensive personal insurance in Neo London?"

The insurance subscription fee alone is 1 billion credits, with a monthly maintenance fee of 100 million credits. And there's a dispatch fee every time they're called out.

"It's expensive, but their skills are guaranteed. If you sign up now, you'll receive a London Knights Longsword replica as a gift."

"That's quite an attractive benefit. Sadly, I don't need it."

I wasn't sad at all.

To subscribe to this reliable insurance, you have to have a terminal implanted in your body. With this terminal, you can call the London Knights if you lose consciousness or are in a dangerous situation. The problem is that the London Knights receive real-time information.

The London Knights claim they only receive biometric information and that the information is regularly discarded. Of course, the idiots who believe that without question don't survive in this city.

The maids and butlers are dragged into the transport helicopter. One of them, Shea, looked at me and shouted.

"Master-nim! Can't you give me one more chance?! I'll be truly loyal! Am I going to be executed like this?!"

"The employment contract ended when you betrayed me. Execution is 100% certain, so write a will. Oh, unless you have more money than me."

"Master-nim!! Please!!"

I gave her a carefully crafted middle finger. Is that undignified? Whatever.

Armina, who had been watching from the side, asked me.

"Qualified maids and butlers rarely betray... You must have terrible luck. Or your enemies are formidable."

"Finding someone you can trust in this city is as hard as plucking a star from the sky. It's enough to make you distrust humanity."

"You seem quite calm for someone who feels that way."

"Actually, I didn't trust the maids and butlers from the start. It's a relationship based solely on money. I know very well that you can't buy trust with money alone. If they get more money, they'll easily betray me, like now."

But if it wasn't just money.

If I had the outstanding authority that other nobles had.

Or if I were a 7th-circle mage. Would Shea and the other employees have dared to betray me? Of course not.

"Yuria wouldn't have betrayed me."

"I certainly think so too."

"...Let me be frank. Yuria is too good to be under you, Count-kakka. Can't you let her go? I'll return the sponsorship money you invested in Yuria tenfold."

Armina looked at me. Her red eyes seemed to be blazing.

"My relationship with Yuria isn't just about money. Yuria takes a portion of the profits from my restaurant business. Sponsorship money? It's only an amount that Yuria can refuse if she wants to."

"Then what kind of relationship do you have with Yuria?"

I paused for a moment. The moments from when I met Yuria until now flashed through my mind.

She came to me as if by fate.

And from some point on, she blatantly seduced me.

"...Explaining it wouldn't be cool."

"...Huh?"

Armina looked at me in disbelief. I avoided her gaze and looked at the transport helicopter.

Soon, she headed towards the transport helicopter for the last time. She said to me just before getting on the helicopter.

"I'm quite surprised by Count Mayer-kakka's skills. Looking around, I see several pieces of equipment designed to kill mages. Yet, you not only survived but also subdued the enemies. Many people will re-evaluate you, Count-kakka."

"I was lucky."

"You're good at joking too. That bald guy you killed is known as the Mage Stalker Plak, famous for hunting mages. He's not someone you can kill with luck alone."

"...Come to think of it, aren't you taking the body?"

"That body is your property, Count-kakka. We can dispose of it for you for free if you wish."

It's the body of a 6th-circle warrior. It's worth hundreds of millions, even billions of credits on its own. If you're lucky, you can get even more.

"I was just asking. I'll take care of the body. I would have been a little angry if you had tried to take it."

"We'll be on our way now. Oh, using attack magic within the city is illegal. Especially large-scale attack magic like Thunder Break, which is enough to dispatch us just for casting it, but we'll overlook it this time as self-defense. The Neo Round Table Council seems to think quite highly of you, Count-kakka."

It seems the Neo Round Table Council had some influence.

It was natural to give me preferential treatment. If I were to go down, the Neo Round Table Council would also suffer losses. Above all, I'm 100% the victim in this case.

The transport helicopter rose into the air and disappeared.

I looked around at the mess and sighed.

,Hiring staff will cost time as well as money. I can't just use anyone....

Above all, the building that collapsed from the first Thunder Break. The bill for that will definitely come to me. Even if the enemies used the building as a lightning rod, those enemies are all in prison, so I won't be able to receive compensation. If I were a building owner in this city, I'd sue me to extort money.

,I can't pay the full price of a building, so I should hire a lawyer... no, a law firm would be better. Next is....

Click!

Suddenly, the sound of a shutter.

I turned my head to the side. Aisa was holding a camera in the corner of the garden.

"That was great just now. The ruined land and the expression of the devastated landowner! The knight should come out pretty well!"

"...You didn't show your face when the London Knights came. You were there? Your stealth skills are admirable."

"It's a reporter's skill. The London Knights... I don't get along with them very well."

"Why?"

"I've written a lot of negative articles about the London Knights."

"...I hope you write this article well. Make me look like a pitiful victim as much as possible."

"Don't worry. I'm a reporter who pursues the truth."

If that were the case, the Liberals newspaper wouldn't be so infamous. Instead of arguing, I put on a pitiful expression.

"Please."

Aisa hesitated for a moment, then smiled.

"I'll think about it if you treat me to a nice dinner tonight."

"I'll give you the best treatment."

Aisa wrote the article on the spot. She wrote and uploaded an article in almost 10 minutes.

"You have to release these kinds of articles right away. There are a lot of trash reporters in Neo London, so if the first article that comes up is positive, mostly positive articles come up."

"Because they copy and paste?"

"You know the habits of trash reporters well."

I left the ruined mansion to the remaining staff and headed to C District for dinner. The destination was a famous hotel. And we parted the next morning.
