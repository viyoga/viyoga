The dead Blood Demon felt both anxiety and hope as he realized that this hell was different from the one he knew.

Originally, the envoy of the Underworld would take the dead and bring them to the judge of hell. Note that I'm not talking about the Ten Kings of the Underworld like King Yama. They were the kings of hell, beings of a much higher caliber than mere judges.

A being like King Yama or King Chogang is not someone a mere sinner can meet just because they want to.

,I don't know exactly, but the being in front of me is at least at the level of the Ten Kings of the Underworld! Only beings with that much power can ignore the rules of hell...! This could be an opportunity for me instead!,

If he receives the protection of such a being, he can cultivate stably even in hell.

,It's not like you can only become a god by ascending! There are also records of those who cultivated in the Underworld and became gods...!,

Although it would be more difficult than training in the Interworld or the Immortal Realm, the beings of the Underworld had the advantage of not dying, even if they were insignificant sinners. Even if the reason for that was to suffer in hell.

"Who are you?"

The Blood Demon mustered his courage and asked.

He knows that speaking to such a different being is dangerous in itself. Still, he spoke. He is grateful that the being's interest is directed at him.

"You will know soon enough."

"Are you perhaps the one who will judge my sins? In which hell and for how long must I suffer?"

"That too, you will know. Follow me."

Mara Cheon moves.

It was a speed that the Blood Demon could easily keep up with. The Blood Demon was slightly relieved by that. He doesn't know the purpose, but he was moved by the fact that a powerful being was considerate of him.

Thanks to this, the Blood Demon had the leisure to look around hell.

It was different from the hell he knew. Demons were seen everywhere. They plowed the land, harvested crops, and raised livestock. There were also demons building buildings. It was like a country in the Interworld, not hell. It was unusual that they were demons instead of humans, though.

,Hell is also a world. Why else would they call it the Underworld.,

He also saw demons cultivating. Seeing them made his heart race. He too would soon be cultivating here like them.

When the demons sensed Mara Cheon's presence, they stopped what they were doing and prostrated themselves on the ground in awe.

He imagined a happy future of becoming an immortal. Hundreds, thousands of years from now, he might become the one who rules over one of the districts of hell.

Then he discovered a huge palace. It was larger and more extravagant than the palace where the emperor of the Jin Empire resided, even larger than a metropolis.

"That palace is...."

"It is my palace. That is not our destination."

Mara Cheon flies over the palace.

The speed was not fast, so the Blood Demon could leisurely look around below the palace.

Surprisingly, the ones working in the palace were human women. And they were young women with such beauty that his eyes popped out. If women like that were in the Interworld... he would have earned a title even without knowing any martial arts. They were that beautiful.

,Are they celestial maidens? They are working in the palace.,

But he didn't get the feeling that they were celestial maidens. What should he say? The women in the palace were beautiful, but they had a venomous feeling.

Of course, the women also prostrate themselves and worship Mara Cheon.

,Hehe. I have gained the favor of that being... so those women will soon be in my arms. I wasn't that interested in anything other than cultivation... but if they are beauties of that level, it's a different story.,

To think that his life would suddenly turn out so well. If he had known it would be like this, he would have died sooner.

The destination was behind the palace.

There was a small hell there.

A hell created only for one person, embodying all the hells of the Underworld. Demons with hideous appearances bowed to Mara Cheon.

"The long-awaited Ahjjae has arrived. Begin."

As soon as Mara Cheon spoke, the demons moved. Sensing something was wrong, the Blood Demon couldn't escape and was caught by the demons and dragged into the small hell.

"What, what are you doing! Weren't you taking me to make me your disciple?!"

"Hehe. Why would I make a bug like you my disciple? That level of delusion is a disease. Hehehe."

Bringing the Blood Demon here slowly and creating hell behind the palace were all intentional. That's because it would make the Blood Demon feel deprived.

As soon as he entered hell, the demons butchered the Blood Demon.

"Kuaaaaaaak!"

Blades stabbing all over his body. The Blood Demon couldn't stand it and screamed. What was even more terrible was that he couldn't die. Even if his head is cut off, he can feel the pain in his body very vividly.

Then, after some time, his body recovers. The demons took turns tormenting the Blood Demon. He is thrown alive into boiling blood, crushed by huge boulders, and his whole body is set on fire.

"Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!"

The Blood Demon, writhing in pain, became accustomed to the pain and noticed the beings watching him.

Beautiful women laughing and watching him from a splendid palace as if watching an interesting play.

Demons gathering around and lining up to torment him.

Above the sky, a black being sitting on a splendid throne is looking at him.

The Blood Demon knew the name of that black being, the owner of this hell. He overheard the demons talking. But he couldn't dare to say that name out loud. He had to be careful even when thinking about it.

"I have not even received a judgment from the judge of hell! This is against the rules of hell!"

"The rules of hell? How dare you discuss the rules of hell in front of me. Hehe. But... you're not exactly wrong. Good. I will specially give you a judgment. Your sins are particularly serious... so you will suffer in hell for all eternity."

"N-No way! No matter how deep my sins are, all eternity?! I know a thing or two about the Underworld too! Please give me a proper judgment!"

"Haha! How insolent! It's the first time in my life that I've seen a bug shouting at me! It was a very refreshing experience!"

When Mara Cheon laughed, the demons and women also laughed loudly.

The Blood Demon despaired.

He closed his eyes tightly as he looked at the hungry ghosts eating him alive and muttered.

"Oh, Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva, who examines sinners! Please have mercy on this poor sinner and show compassion...!"

"Hehe. There is not a shred of mercy in this hell. Abandon all hope, Ahjjae."

"Uaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!"
