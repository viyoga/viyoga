The torn barrier of the Great Witch began to recover at a rapid pace. The Great Witch was concentrating. The surrounding witches watched me in silence with horrified eyes.

No one could have predicted that I would become an angel. Moreover, I, who was only Level 7, tore the Great Witch's barrier and dealt with Mephistopheles, who was Level 8, even if he was only an incarnation.

,Honestly, even I can't help but be surprised.'

A Level 7 killed a Level 8. That in itself isn't a miraculously surprising event. The difference of one level isn't absolute. Besides, not all Level 8s are the same, and I had a clear advantage in compatibility.

,Thanks to Barachiel's blessing, my output as an angel became stronger. The problem is that it put more strain on my body.'

I released Absolute Polymorph. The coat that had permeated me reappeared and wrapped around my shoulders.

A momentary dizziness washed over me. But I didn't show it and braced myself by putting strength in my lower body.

I quickly checked my condition.

My mana road is at its limit. If I use even one magic spell here, I won't be able to use several mana roads for a while.

,My stamina is also at rock bottom, and I'm hungry. I'm even thirsty.'

I took out a cola from my inventory and gulped it down.

,To face a transcendent-level demon's incarnation and win this well is almost a perfect victory.'

It was also possible because I used Absolute Polymorph to transform into an angel.

"Yujin! Are you okay? Are you hurt anywhere?"

Marta ran to my side and asked.

"I'm not. The Great Witch seems to be busy restoring the barrier right now... I'll meditate for a bit. Please."

Trusting Marta, I closed my eyes.

I observe myself.

My unique skill was related to my mental image. Just now, I became an angel and used a completely different unique skill, so I had to check my mental image.

The result of the check was that it was fine.

One star shining at the center of the universe. A universe moving around that star. I saw particularly large stars among those stars. I realized that one of those stars was another mental image. Unfortunately, I couldn't observe it in detail.

,What's certain is... this star influenced the Sword of Annihilation, the unique skill I had when I was an angel. Perhaps the ability I have and this mental image are related in some way.'

It goes without saying that I couldn't use other unique skills in human form. The Sword of Annihilation could only be used when I became an angel.

,There are other brightly shining stars. Can I use them as other unique skills if I meet certain conditions?'

Having confirmed that there were no problems with my mental image, I opened my eyes. I felt like more than 15 minutes had passed.

Looking up at the night sky, the Great Witch's barrier that had been torn was also restored.

"Magician, Yujin Meyer."

The Great Witch Beatrice called me.

"You have proven yourself by killing Mephistopheles' incarnation. Therefore, we witches acknowledge you as an ally. This is my decision as the Great Witch representing the witches. If there is any witch who opposes, come forward."

Of course, there was no one. For witches, the Great Witch was a much greater being than a country's representative or king.

"Great Witch-nim. We have no complaints."

The Great Witch smiled contentedly at the witches' response and rose from her seat.

"Witches. We will proceed with a ritual to undo the curse of the Corruptor that has taken root in the ally's heart. Everyone, prepare yourselves."

"Yes, Great Witch-nim."

The witches moved. Some ran, and some flew on broomsticks. As they cast magic everywhere, the space itself reacted. The plaza quickly transformed into a large-scale ritual site.

"Great Witch. Is there anything I need to do?"

"Don't interfere and stay with the Saintess."

I decided to just watch. I shook my head as I watched the witches prepare for the grand ritual.

,This isn't a ritual I can do. There's a restriction on the ritual itself that only witches can do it.'

Because there was a restriction, the effect of the ritual would be amplified even more.

The Great Witch seemed to have finished her preparations early, as she was smoking a long pipe.

"Great Witch. Did you know from the beginning that Mephistopheles' incarnation was hiding among the witches? Why didn't you deal with it until now? With your power, you could have easily crushed a Level 8 incarnation."

"That guy is like a cockroach, so even if you kill him, he crawls out from somewhere."

"...Were you watching him without killing him?"

"He only observes us and doesn't do anything else. He watches over us as if he were treating us like his own daughters. He's a very, very unpleasant guy...."

Unlike angels, demons are difficult to understand with common sense.

Moreover, Mephistopheles wasn't one of the 72 demons, but a demon transcender. He wasn't a demon bound by the agreement.

,Because there's no agreement, he's relatively free. But now that his Level 8 incarnation is dead, he won't be able to influence this world for a while.'

The important thing is that I and Mephistopheles are enemies. There's no way he'll leave me alone after I killed his incarnation. This is why the Great Witch recognized me as an ally. We have the same demon as our enemy.

"The ritual preparations are complete. You, go up to the altar. Saintess, you, go outside the plaza. The presence of a saint with stigmata in the ritual site itself is negative."

"Who are you calling negative?"

Marta grumbled but went outside the plaza.

I stepped onto the altar's stairs. The altar, hastily made with magic, was taller than any building here. When I got on the altar, I saw six piles of wood surrounding it. Around them stood witches of at least Level 4 or higher.

At first glance, it seemed disorganized, but I realized that each of their positions corresponded to constellations and magic circles.

At the focal point of the altar, the Great Witch slammed a very long and old wooden staff into the ground.

Hwareureureuk!

The piles of wood burst into flames, and the witches laughed and danced.

"Kalkalkalkalkalkalkal!"

It was very noisy as the witches laughed from all directions. At the same time, the mana density increased. I felt my body being uplifted and looked up at the night sky. The stars in the night sky were flowing as if they would pour down to the ground.

"Kalkalkalkalkalkalkalkalkalkalkal!"

The witches' laughter was a spell in itself.

The witches danced and spun around the plaza. The Ganggangsullae from my past life came to mind.

As they spun, the ground began to spin as well. The laughter spun, the wind spun, the smoke spun, and even the stars in the night sky spun.

Binggeulbinggeulbinggeulbinggeul.

Everything spun. The only exception was me. Only I stood perfectly still on the altar.

"Release the sealed curse."

The Great Witch's voice was precisely embedded in my ear amidst the witches' laughter. As instructed, I released the Corruptor's curse sealed in my heart.

The curse instantly covered my body. It was much stronger than when I first sealed the curse. Just as it was becoming difficult to breathe, the curse fell from my body and began to spin.

The more the curse spun, the weaker its power became. As if it were being ground away by sandpaper.

-Do the cursed dare to touch my curse....

The Corruptor. The voice of Calyx of Corruption is heard.

"Failure."

I said.

Corruptors. Those who failed to transcend and clung to the beings of the Void with a desperate heart.

Binggeulbinggeul.

The curse continued to be shaved off and became smaller.

-...Do you think this is the end? You ignorant magician! You tried to eliminate my, Calyx of Corruption's, curse! You have touched my honor!

"Failure."

-I am the great Corruptor Calyx. How can you, who have only just begun to walk the path of transcendence, do anything to me? I declare. I will crush you to death like an ant.

"Failure."

-Is that all you can say? What a pathetic last words. On the day the moon turns black, your fate will end.

"Failure."

-...You goddamn mortal...! I will definitely kill you!

"Failure."

-Inooooooom!!!

The spinning curse was completely ground away and disappeared.

The Corruptor's curse that had threatened my life was resolved.

I didn't act rashly and stayed still. The ending of a ritual was as important as its beginning.

Following the Great Witch's lead, the grand ritual slowly came to an end. Most of the witches slumped to the floor of the plaza.

"Haa, haa.... It's been a while since I've done a grand ritual, so it's a bit tough."

"Ah. My throat hurts."

"Even the Corruptor's curse can't withstand a grand ritual."

"I heard that the Great Witch-nim originally created the grand ritual to eliminate quirks. Is that true?"

"It's true. Quirks are the essence of us witches, so... if we force it, our lives and even our existence will be ground away, so it became meaningless."

"But what's a Corruptor? Does anyone know?"

"I don't know either."

"Quiet. It's not good for you to know. I'll tell you when you become a witch of at least Level 7 or higher."

The Great Witch sat down again as if she were lying down.

"Witches. The night is far from over, so enjoy the festival as you please."

"Yes, Great Witch-nim!"

The witches began to enjoy the festival in their own ways. Marta, who had been outside the plaza, approached. I looked around and found Deneb and flicked my finger.

Deneb, the witch of gambling and my slave, walked gracefully. The slit skirt of her cheongsam swayed.

"You solved the curse? Congratulations."

"Kneel."

"...What?"

"That's an order."

"Ugh...."

Deneb, as a slave, couldn't refuse. It wasn't an order related to her life.

I sat on Deneb's back as she knelt. Silence fell over the plaza in an instant. The witches who had been laughing and playing among themselves looked at us in shock, and even the Great Witch slightly opened her mouth.

"That's Deneb. That crazy bitch who cheated even the Great Witch-nim after challenging her to a bet because of her quirk."

"Even if you don't know anything else, she's a witch who's known for her skills. Why did she become a human chair?"

"It's obvious just by looking. She lost a bet and became a human slave!"

"...Is it true? She's not denying it even though she must have heard us."

"......A witch became a human slave? Isn't this the first time for a witch?"

"Deneb, the first witch to become a human slave!!"

Deneb's name burst out from everywhere. Budeulbudeul. The chair trembled. Looking at her ears, which were bowed, they were very red.

Marta, who was next to me, looked at me with disgusted eyes.

"Do you want to use a woman as a chair?"

"It's just a slave doing slave work, so there's no problem. Isn't that right, Deneb?"

"Tha... at's...."

The Great Witch sighed and shook her head.

"Haa, Deneb. I knew you'd cause trouble someday, but... this is too unexpected. Can't you restrain yourself?"

"...I've been restraining myself lately."

The Great Witch didn't seem to believe Deneb's words. I was also dubious.

"Magician. The Corruptor has a grudge against you. Have you prepared for this?"

"There's nothing to prepare. The only time he can move is at most this Dark Moon. If I survive, it's my victory. And... I'm not foolish enough to be cursed by him twice."

"Hmm. Tell us if you need the help of us witches. Ally."

"I will."
