Mana erupted from Yura's body like a detonation after she swallowed the Golden Sun Pill. This wasn't ordinary mana. It was scorching hot. Just standing nearby made the ambient temperature spike noticeably.

There was no mistaking it—this was a yang-essence elixir.

Yura's complexion deteriorated in an instant. She was sweating profusely, focusing entirely on her breathing.

I furrowed my brow watching her.

"Looks more like poison than medicine. Old man. Were you trying to feed me this?"

"How could I? The Golden Sun Pill is our family's secret treasure—its safety profile is unmatched. Didn't I tell you earlier? If an ordinary person takes it, they'll enjoy lifelong health and longevity. It's safe enough for a civilian to consume, yet dangerous for a Hunter? That makes no sense."

"Simply put, Yura is special."

The answer came immediately.

The yokai sealed as tattoos on Yura's body—they were rebelling against her right now.

"Do you need my help, by any chance?"

"I'll handle it myself."

I reached my hand toward Yura's back. Even through her clothes, I could feel her body heat had become scorching. An ordinary civilian would have suffered low-temperature burns.

As I focused my senses, I could feel the yang and yin energies clashing within Yura's body. Normally, the yang energy should dominate and consume the yin. Thanks to the Golden Sun Pill, the yang force was far stronger than the yin.

*'Due to the yokai's resistance, Yura can't control the yang energy.'*

From the yokai's perspective, they don't want Yura getting stronger. And now the yang energy is intensifying? For beings that are essentially yin energy incarnate, they have no choice but to resist for their own future.

Crackle.

I channeled a thread of lightning qi into Yura's body. Infused with my will, the lightning qi pierced through the massive yang energy and burrowed inside. It drew the yang energy along, circulating smoothly through Yura's body. It suppressed the yin energy within, forcibly blending them together, while burning away impurities with the power of the yang.

In that instant, the yin energy surged upward.

Its source was, needless to say, the yokai. Though they were sealed within tattoos, those tattoos were ultimately part of Yura's body. In the end, Yura had sealed the yokai inside herself.

*'Know your place. Seal or no seal, I can undo it right now and kill every last one of you.'*

Perhaps my will reached the yokai. The yin energy that had been surging up to interfere settled down like a calm lake.

The massive yang energy that had been bursting from Yura's body seeped back in, mixing with the yin. Her body temperature lowered and stabilized.

Instead of sweat, smoke rose from Yura's entire body. The acrid smell was proof that impurities had been burned away—waste products that had accumulated inside her.

"Remarkable. For true qi manipulation, experience matters more than talent... Your skill in true qi manipulation seems superior to mine. Where on earth did you learn it?"

Hwang Cheonseong asked, but I didn't bother answering and just chewed on it silently.

Rather than having learned it, it would be more accurate to say I taught myself.

The Thunder Heaven Art had been consistently practiced not only in reality but in other worlds as well. Just counting that time alone, it would be at least several hundred years. On top of that, I had massage skills. Using qi for massage requires knowing all sorts of things.

"Haaah."

Yura exhaled deeply. The black smoke that escaped her mouth dispersed and vanished. It was tar residue that had accumulated in her lungs from smoking. Even an A-Rank Hunter couldn't escape the toxicity of cigarettes. Cigarettes really are this harmful to the body.

*'Not for S-Ranks though. They can push out or extract the tobacco toxins with mana.'*

Or just click their tongue and use full recovery.

"...You startled me shoving it in my mouth without warning."

"I didn't know it would turn out like this either."

"Like I said, it's an elixir safe enough for ordinary people to consume."

This happened because of those yokai sealed inside Yura's body.

*'I was careless too, I suppose.'*

Still, it was a well-founded carelessness. I had an elixir in my inventory.

"Anyway, the result was good. How do you feel?"

"My mana capacity has increased dramatically, and my body itself feels like it's gotten a step stronger. Even my body, which usually felt cold... feels comfortable now. More than anything, breathing is much easier."

"How about quitting smoking while you're at it?"

"No. I can't live without cigarettes."

That firm declaration made me laugh.

"Take care. I was going to say that as an S-Rank Hunter, you don't need to mind the government... but you seem more like an S-Rank than any S-Rank Hunter I've ever seen. In the past, S-Rank Hunters were called living gods in Japan, and immortals in China. Don't forget that S-Rank Hunters are special by nature."

"I know that without you telling me."

"I'm saying it because there are those who don't understand that obvious truth even as S-Rank Hunters."

Han Ahyeong's face flashed through my mind.

Despite wielding the power of an S-Rank Hunter, she lived righteously. She was a completely different kind of human from me or Hwang Cheonseong.

"Lastly... What an S-Rank Hunter needs to watch out for isn't dungeons or monsters—it's other S-Rank Hunters. I learned that too late, and look at me now."

"It's not that you learned too late—it's that you were weak."

"Haha. I can't deny that."

I returned home and checked the internet.

Hwang Chiseung, the master of the Goryeo Guild, had really resigned.

He held a press conference and officially announced his resignation. Now there was no going back. If he returned, he'd only become a laughingstock.

"I feel my own inadequacy and hereby resign as master of the Goryeo Guild. At the same time, I retire as a Hunter. I will not set foot in the Hunter world in any capacity, and will return to my hometown to live out my remaining years."

Hwang Chiseung spoke with a complicated expression. Anyone could see these weren't words he wanted to say.

Fifty-something isn't young, but it's not old enough to retire either. Isn't the retirement age sixty?

"Master Hwang Chiseung, haven't you been running the Goryeo Guild well all this time? Why on earth are you suddenly resigning out of nowhere?"

A reporter asked. Of course, asking didn't mean he'd answer.

"It's all my fault."

The moment Hwang Chiseung spoke the truth, he wouldn't just be retiring—his life would be forfeit.

No matter how many times the reporters asked the same question, Hwang Chiseung only gave the same answer like a parrot.

In the end, it was the reporters who gave up first.

"Has the new guild master of the Goryeo Guild been decided? If so, who is it?"

"The candidate for the Goryeo Guild's sixth master is Hunter Son Gayeon. Barring any special circumstances, Hunter Son Gayeon will become the Goryeo Guild's sixth master before long."

"Wait. Hunter Son Gayeon? Who is that? I don't recall a Son Gayeon among the Goryeo Guild's executives!"

"Hunter Son Gayeon is not from within the guild. However, I can guarantee her skill on the honor of the Goryeo Guild's name. Detailed information will be released later."

Watching the press conference, Son Gayeon came to mind.

Acting head of the Northern Baek Son Clan.

F-cup breasts, suffers from hyperhidrosis so she sweats even when standing still. Her hobby is fishing.

*'The Northern Baek Son Clan is one of Korea's five great families. The Goryeo Guild itself was founded by the five great families working together. You could say she meets the minimum qualification to become Goryeo Guild master.'*

But it was only the minimum qualification.

Son Gayeon was in her late twenties.

For an ordinary guild, maybe. But she was far too young to become the master of the Goryeo Guild. The masters of the other top-ten guilds were at minimum middle-aged men in their forties. Moreover, Son Gayeon wasn't even affiliated with the Goryeo Guild.

*'Even if I were a Goryeo Guild executive, I wouldn't accept this. And yet she became the candidate?'*

There had to be another reason.

Someone with enough influence to pull strings in the Goryeo Guild.

*'It must be the retired S-Rank Hunters connected to the five great families, including Hwang Cheonseong.'*

And among them, the most influential appeared to be Hwang Cheonseong.

*'Son Gayeon becoming the Goryeo Guild master candidate... is it because of me?'*

Son Gayeon and I met occasionally, not often. We'd meet at a secluded fishing spot in the countryside, enjoy fishing, spend the night at a motel, and return to Seoul. For reference, in Korea, when a man and woman enter a motel together, there's only one meaning.

*'He put Son Gayeon in the Goryeo Guild master position to curry favor with me? Hwang Cheonseong. This old man's got some moves.'*

Actually, I had planned to meet Hwang Chiseung soon regarding this matter. But it seemed I'd need to change my plans. In my mind, the name Hwang Chiseung had already been erased.

Just the fact that the Goryeo Guild's master was my woman sent my favorability toward the Goryeo Guild skyrocketing.

*'I acknowledge it. The Goryeo Guild really is Korea's number one guild!'*

I should meet Son Gayeon soon. For her sake—someone who'd be suspected of being a parachute appointment—I, an S-Rank Hunter, needed to show off our connection.

Ding-dong.

The doorbell rang.

I checked the intercom—it was Hell Tiger Yi Yeonsuk. I'd heard she retired as an S-Rank Hunter and now worked as an instructor on the government side.

*'She must have come because of my meeting with Hwang Cheonseong.'*

I welcomed Yi Yeonsuk in. Her face was filled with annoyance.

"Would you like some water at least?"

"No need. It's a simple matter, so I'll finish at the doorway. Can you tell me what you discussed with Hwang Cheonseong?"

"There was some friction between me and the Goryeo Guild, you see."

"...So Hwang Chiseung's resignation was related to you. Well, as long as nothing happened, it's fine. Hwang Chiseung—be careful of that failure."

"That old geezer? He didn't seem like much."

"That may be so now, but he was once called Korea's strongest Hunter. And it's not widely known, but... he once attempted a coup and failed. He's a dangerous old man in many ways."

"For someone who came in person, is that all you wanted to say?"

"I came personally because it's you. There's no one of your caliber in the government. Ah, the government has no intention of surveilling or antagonizing you, so don't waste your thoughts on that."

"I know that. I have no intention of antagonizing the government either."

Yi Yeonsuk turned to leave.

"Wait. That coup you stopped, by any chance—"

"It was me. If I hadn't stopped Hwang Cheonseong back then, South Korea would have gone to hell. The other S-Rank Hunters had no interest in that sort of thing... so it was only me."

Yi Yeonsuk left.

I smirked to myself.

"That old man. He lived quite the interesting life back in his day."
