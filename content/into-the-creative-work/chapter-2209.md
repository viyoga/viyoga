Eugene used a Flash while taking out the prepared gift.

[Using Flash (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

He needed time to think.

He attended this banquet today at the invitation of Princess Elsa. When he was invited, joy preceded his worries. Princess Elsa, the first in line to the throne, the Crown Princess, was an undeniable key power figure in Neo London.

,I didn't expect to meet Yuria here, though.'

The Maid Academy is stricter than the military. Students within the academy can't just make phone calls as they please. At least they can exchange letters regularly, but the intervals are quite long. There were limits to checking on each other's well-being through letters.

,Is it the field training she mentioned last time? To think she'd be doing her field training under Princess Elsa. Is the royal family paying attention to Yuria after all.'

Eugene suppressed the urge to greet Yuria, whom he hadn't seen in a long time. Now wasn't the time.

,Princess Elsa mentioned the title of Count. Even though my promotion was a given, for her to reveal a fact not yet announced by the royal family here...'

It's devoid of political meaning... Except the other party was Princess Elsa. A person whose every action was bound to be politically linked.

Was it discussed with the royal family? Or did she announce the news of the promotion on her own accord?

,...I don't know. She can be considered a representative of the royal family, so she wouldn't antagonize them. Is it just a favor?'

In fact, the promotion itself was expected by everyone. Superconductors have that much impact.

Princess Elsa in front of him was smiling gently. He had no idea what she was thinking. He decided to move on for now.

Flash was deactivated. Eugene presented a luxurious and ornate metal box he took out from his pocket. Eugene slowly opened the box. Then, with a cheerful melody, a metal ballerina doll inside the box floated into the air and began to dance.

The onlookers were slightly disappointed.

,A levitating music box? A bit disappointing. Such toys are just expensive, aren't they.'

,...That one's probably more expensive. Don't you get it yet? There's no magic involved at all. It's a magnetic levitation music box made purely with science and technology.'

,Magnetic levitation with just technology and no magic? ...A superconductor, then. I heard superconductors cost over 10 million credits per kilogram...'

,The important thing isn't just the monetary value. The paying companies are busy researching superconductors. They don't have the luxury of making toys like music boxes with superconductors. In other words, that's the first superconductor music box ever made. Except for the ancient nations, that is. It has plenty of historical value.'

Eugene smiled inwardly, hearing the murmurs around him. As expected of nobles, they recognized the value.

The music box stopped. Princess Elsa opened her mouth slightly, looking surprised, and carefully accepted his gift.

,Thank you so much, Baron Meyer. I'll probably never forget today's gift. Did the Baron make this music box himself?'

,No, I'm not an engineer but a magician, so I'm clueless about technology. Acrotex Corporation made the music box.'

,Indeed, it's very precise, as expected of a music box with Acrotex's technology. It looks like a single sculpture to the naked eye... but there must be hundreds of mechanical parts inside. Baron Meyer, please enjoy the banquet. Today's banquet is to celebrate the development of Neo London... In a way, the Baron could be the main character.'

,It's absurd to call me the main character.'

,There's no need to be so modest.'

Eugene finished his greetings and distanced himself from Princess Elsa. Countless gazes followed him. Eugene wanted to talk to Yuria, but... the timing wasn't right. He had to look for another opportunity.

,Baron Meyer. No, since your promotion is promised, I should call you Count. Could I have a word with you? I'm personally interested in LK Corporation. I promise to invest.'

,I heard the Baron is still unmarried. Are you perhaps interested in marriage? My cousin is looking for a marriage partner...'

Eugene calmly talked with them.

• • •

10 PM. The banquet was still bustling. Yuria stood behind Princess Elsa, quietly watching the banquet. Her gaze was directed at Eugene.

At that moment, a maid approached with a stiff expression and reported something to Princess Elsa in a whisper. Princess Elsa's eyes seemed to flash coldly for a moment, but she soon covered her mouth with a fan and smiled.

,She gave the maid some instructions.'

Agnes, standing next to Princess Elsa, created an invisible wall to block the sound. Yuria couldn't even see their lip movements from where she was, so she couldn't know the content at all.

,It doesn't seem like an urgent matter.'

The maid soon left.

And about 3 minutes later, the butler at the entrance of the banquet hall began to shout loudly.

,His Highness Prince William Snowden is entering!!'

The doors of the banquet hall opened, and a group led by a blond man in uniform entered. Leading 3 butlers and 2 maids, he scanned the banquet hall with confident eyes.

Then, he slowly approached Princess Elsa, the host of the banquet.

,Elsa-nunim. Long time no see.'

,...Prince William. How dare you come to a place you weren't invited to. Since this is a public event, please show some respect.'

,Haha. Understood. Your Highness.'

Prince William Snowden. Princess Elsa's younger brother. The second in line to the throne.

As long as Elsa is there, he cannot ascend to the throne. Conversely, if Elsa is not there, he can inherit the throne.

The noisy banquet had become quiet. The guests watched the princess and prince with great interest.

,Prince, what brings you here?'

,I found a pretty flower on my way. I came to gift it to you, Your Highness.'

William's butler handed a bouquet of flowers to Agnes.

,How are the flowers?'

,Beautiful.'

,Since you seem to be holding a banquet... may I also attend?'

William asked with a smile. Princess Elsa couldn't refuse. William was a political rival, but he was her younger brother. A family member belonging to the same royal family. She couldn't treat him coldly in front of everyone.

,How can I mistreat a brother who came to gift flowers? William, enjoy the banquet.'

,As expected of Your Highness. You take care of this brother. By the way... is the maid behind you Yuria Grace?'

William's eyes, looking at Yuria, shone like a snake's. Prince William's desire for talent was no less than Elsa's. Since his position was lower than Princess Elsa's, he needed talent even more.

Just as Yuria was about to greet him, Elsa stepped forward.

,Prince William, do not oppress my servants.'

,Oppress? I'm just trying to say hello. I didn't expect to see the genius of the Maid Academy here.'

The genius of the Maid Academy. It was enough to attract attention. The guests murmured. William smiled kindly and said to Yuria.

,Miss Grace, could we talk separately later? It's definitely not a story that will harm you. I promise.'

,Your Highness, I am currently working under Her Highness Princess Elsa. I cannot act on my own without Her Highness's permission.'

William's gaze turned to Elsa.

,Your Highness, please allow it. It won't take long. 10 minutes will do.'

Elsa thought. Even if William dangled any sweet bait, Yuria didn't seem likely to fall for it.

,If it's 10 minutes... you can have a conversation in the room next door right now. Miss Yuria, what do you say?'

,...Yes. I will speak with Your Highness.'

,Prince William, it's exactly 10 minutes. I cannot allow any more than that.'

,Thank you, Your Highness.'

Yuria moved to the next room with William's party.

As soon as William entered the room, he sat on the sofa and crossed his legs. Next to him was a sharp-looking red-haired butler, and behind William, the servants stood quietly.

Yuria calmly endured the pressure they exuded.

,Yuria Grace-yang. I'll get straight to the point. After graduating from the Maid Academy, would you like to work under me? I promise you the best treatment in the industry.'

,I'm sorry, Your Highness. I want to focus on my studies right now.'

,Have you already decided to work under Her Highness the Princess? Or is it directly under the royal family?'

,I cannot say.'

She couldn't say that she already had a master. William's reputation had more suspicious aspects compared to Elsa. Mentioning Eugene's name could cause harm to Eugene. Above all, she felt a sense of unease from the butler and maids.

,I suppose my first impression wasn't very good. It's only natural since I'm an uninvited guest who came to the banquet without an invitation. But I couldn't help it. Her Highness the Princess and I aren't on the best of terms, you see.'

,.......'

,Don't you want to get stronger quickly? Isaac, the head butler next to me, surpassed the wall of superhuman by contracting with me. Those behind me also gained power by contracting with me.'

,You mean you raised your rank through artificial means other than training. Is that even possible?'

,It's possible. Don't you want to get stronger quickly? With Miss Grace's talent, you could rise to 7th grade within a year. I promise.'

,I tend to be cautious about contracts. Making a contract to gain incomprehensible power is too risky.'

,There's always a price to pay for gaining power. I'll give you power in exchange for working under me for 10 years. Isn't that tempting?'

It's good to have power. Most power makes things easier. But Yuria had no intention of betraying her master and gaining power.

,I'll decline.'

,You're firm. I understand. Our meeting was too short to trust each other. Isaac, show Miss Grace some of your power.'

,Yes, Master.'

The red-haired, golden-eyed butler, Isaac, stepped forward with a bitter smile.

Yuria quietly tensed. The banquet hall was right next door. The possibility of a battle was infinitesimally small, but it wasn't 0%.

,Please watch carefully. I'll show you some of the power I've gained.'
