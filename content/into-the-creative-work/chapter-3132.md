The guards of Lumen Prison stood in formation before the main gate, fully armed. They wore ballistic helmets and gripped rifles in their hands. Swords hung at their waists. Some of them were mounted on armored vehicles, seated behind mounted machine guns, awaiting my command.

Marit and I stood before the soldiers.

'Even with thorough armament, the moment a superhuman enters the battlefield, it's ultimately the superhumans who decide the outcome of combat. Unless firepower is concentrated, forces of Grade 2 or below can't hope to contend with enemies of Grade 5 or higher.'

If we could fully concentrate the firepower of hundreds of soldiers, the story would be different... but there isn't just one enemy.

Beyond the main gate's fence, I can see the dust clouds drawing closer.

Haukal.

A resistance organization centered on barbarian tribes operating within the Empire.

Calling them barbarians might conjure images of them charging on horseback, but in reality, they race down roads on armed motorcycles and armored vehicles.

However, unlike their modernized means of transport, their clothing is the highly revealing barbarian garb. Their weapons aren't guns either—they're axes, greatswords, or spears. They were exactly the barbarians you'd commonly see in games.

'In the original game, if you started as a barbarian tribe, you could choose between a barbarian warrior and a barbarian shaman.'

The barbarian shaman wasn't a particularly good class. The shamanism they used was specialized in strengthening allies.

'Barbarian warriors can start by choosing one of dozens of oaths. Since the initial stats are determined by the oath, the low-end potential was top-tier among all classes.'

I wasn't interested in barbarian warriors, so I didn't know the details. Still, I knew roughly what oaths were available.

'It's a kind of penalty. From simple oaths like smoking a cigarette once a day, to absurd ones like never dodging to the right. There was even one about not wearing armor.'

In the original game, breaking an oath killed your character. In this world that had become reality, breaking an oath didn't kill you. Instead, you lost your power and could never swear the oath again.

"Oho. A Hero-class is leading the pack. We've hooked a big one."

Marit said from beside me.

Hero was a rank the barbarians used among themselves.

Grade 2 and above were called Warriors. Grade 6 was Great Warrior. Grade 7 was Hero. Grade 8 was Great Hero.

"I can sense a Grade 7 presence. They're not even trying to hide it."

"The barbarian folk may be uneducated, but they're not stupid. Charging head-on without any strategy or tactics means they're already certain of victory."

Naturally, they had every reason to be.

In the information they possessed, Marit's existence wouldn't be there, and Leon Schneider was merely a Grade 5 alchemist. Cases of Grade 5 defeating Grade 6 happened often depending on the environment. But a Grade 5 defeating a Grade 7 was rare no matter how far back you searched through all of history.

"You there. I'll handle the Grade 7."

"No, I'll handle him."

"You're not planning to reveal your identity, are you? You intend to use only a portion of your true strength?"

"Now that we've openly clashed with the barbarian tribes, problems are bound to erupt afterward regardless. If that's the case, it's better to reveal that I'm Grade 7."

"Not a bad idea. It's true that the deterrent of a Grade 7's warfare alone can suppress combat. Very well. I'll leave the Hero-class to you. Ah, if possible, capture him alive. We need to find out their main base."

"I will. Against a Grade 7, I can use alchemy, so it's perfect."

"Hm? You're saying you'll fight using only alchemy? You currently can't even use your unique ability, can you? What about other magic?"

"Leon Schneider is an alchemist."

Even though I'd revealed my identity to Marit, I had no intention of discarding the identity of Leon Schneider.

If rumors spread that Eugene Meyer was actually the warden of the Heist Empire's prison!—the only thing I'd gain was loss. Probably both Neo London and the Heist Empire would pressure and suspect me.

"Well, I'll respect your choice. If it seems dangerous, I'll help."

Even in the brief moment we exchanged words, the enemies had drawn closer.

I raised my hand and commanded the soldiers.

"Fire."

Firepower pours out.

Bullets and shells concentrate toward the enemies. However, almost none of the attacks hit. The accuracy was strangely low. Bullets and shells veered off into random places.

'Shamanism—Wind's Blessing. A Grade 6 shamanism that distorts projectiles fired at oneself. The strange patterns drawn on the vehicles and motorcycles must be Wind's Blessing.'

Unlike magic circles, barbarian shaman patterns have no fixed formula. Each shaman's pattern is different, so it was impossible to determine the effect of the shamanism just by looking at the pattern.

'There's even a barrier spell layered on. The occasional attacks that do hit are blocked.'

Low-quality, ordinary physical force can't stop them. At this rate, the enemies look like they'll reach right in front of us.

"Marit. Can you cast one Grade 7 spell?"

"You mean the spell I sold you? I can cast it, but... it feels a bit wasteful."

Marit looks at me with suggestive eyes.

She doesn't seem willing to cast it for free. I understand. In exchange for selling me a Grade 7 spell, she'd get to embrace me ten times.

"Later, I'll sell you a Grade 10 spell for free."

"Well, in that case."

Marit grinned. At her gesture, a single contract appeared in the air.

It was a contract containing the Grade 7 lightning-element spell [Thunder Break].

Riiip!

The contract tears and the spell activates. With a sensation like every hair on my body standing on end, a massive lightning bolt struck down from the sky. The thunderbolt illuminated the world brilliantly.

KWAAAAAAAH!

Thunder Break smashed through the Grade 6 shamanism Wind's Blessing head-on, tore apart the barrier, and burst the enemies. A massive crater formed where they had been, and blackened corpses rose in wisps of smoke.

"Ahah, ahahahaha! Incredible! So this is the power of Thunder Break, said to be among the highest in attack power even among Grade 7 spells! More than half the enemies evaporated! This is fucking amazing!!"

She rejoiced enough to make me feel proud for no reason.

"Keep in mind that not every mage can use Thunder Break."

Elemental affinity is also a talent.

There are almost no mages like me who have such an abundance of magical talent that they can use every attribute of magic. No, there are none at all. A normal mage can use one or two attributes.

'It's surprising even to me. The power goes without saying since it's my spell. But Marit cast a Grade 7 spell with almost no cost. At most, the mana used to summon the contract was all. Even that mana was less than half of what's consumed when casting a Grade 1 spell.'

In theory, she could rapid-fire dozens, hundreds of Grade 7 spells.

'The problem is obtaining Grade 7 spells. As a penalty for selling Thunder Break, thirty mana rods became unusable for a hundred days. For me, since I have Phase Astral, it's not a big loss, but... for other Grade 7 mages, it's a loss comparable to internal injury.'

When mana rods decrease, spell casting speed, spell power, and overall magical efficiency plummet.

'In effect, there's no one but me who can procure Grade 7 spells for Marit.'

I was pondering whether I'd have to sell another Thunder Break when—

From far in the distance, a man's bellow echoed thunderously.

"MARIT VON EISENRAT!! YOU IMPERIAL GRAVE-ROBBING BITCH!! SO YOU WERE HERE!! TODAY I'LL KILL YOU AND GRIND YOUR HEART TO DRINK IT!!"

I glanced at Marit. She was twisting her lips.

"Grave-robbing bitch?"

"Hm? Ah, the barbarians call me that. The barbarian tribes have a culture of burying the dead with the objects they used in life. Most of it's precious metals, so digging up graves makes decent money."

"...I see."

"The owner of that voice is Rega of the Swift Legs. He fights like a monkey with blades attached to both feet. His oath is to never close both eyes at once. He alternates which eye he closes. Even when sleeping, he sleeps with only one eye shut."

"You know him well."

"I've fought him twice. Couldn't kill him. Though ultimately, I won on points."

"His unique ability?"

"It's a unique ability called Rekr. The effect is complete evasion. He perfectly evades any attack, once. When he uses the ability, his hair turns white as a characteristic. After evading once, it takes some time before he can use it again."

Rega shot up toward the sky. Blades over 50 centimeters long were attached to both his feet.

"Thanks to that, the battle should be easier. I'll handle him, so I'll leave the rest of the rabble to you. If possible, I'd prefer my subordinates not die."

"Understood. Don't worry about it. Ah, here he comes."

The bastard began falling from the sky like a meteor, the blades on his feet aimed at Marit.

I stepped forward and used magic. In my hand, I gripped a single plastic button.

[Steel Alchemy]

[Form Transmutation]

The button transforms into a steel spear.

[Mass Grant]

The spear becomes more than twice as heavy. I levitated the spear into the air with telekinesis.

[Element Grant]

Bzzzzzzzt!

Lightning surged from the spear imbued with the lightning element.

I extended my finger, pointing at the falling Rega. The lightning-charged spear shoots out.

Rega showed no intention of dodging. Without even twisting his body, he looked like he'd strike the spear away with the blades on his feet.

CLANG!

The spear is deflected. However, the lightning element imbued in the spear flowed into his body and electrocuted him. He didn't stop.

"Barbarian indeed—his body's disgustingly tough. Status effects of any reasonable level don't work, do they."

"That's the trait of these uneducated brutes. It's easier to aim for the head with high-penetration attacks."

I ignored Marit's advice.

"Open your eyes wide and watch. I'll show you a miracle."

I raised both hands to chest height. A magic circle is drawn between my facing palms. To classify precisely, it's an alchemy circle, but alchemy is ultimately magic too, so broadly speaking, it's a magic circle.

The air gathered in the magic circle transforms into matter. The matter soon takes the form of an object.

A hammer engraved with runic letters.

[Matter Projection]

Grade 7 Vision Alchemy. A spell I developed myself.

Convert air into matter, and project that matter into an object from my memory. The projected object could be maintained for dozens of seconds. The condition for objects I could project was that they be of a lower grade than my own level.

"You bastard! That hammer...!! How dare you!!"

"Seeing as you recognize the replica of Mjölnir, your eyes aren't completely rotten."

I grabbed the handle of the projected Mjölnir replica and hurled it at the aghast Rega.

Mjölnir flew automatically and struck Rega's head.

BOOM!

A lightning-attribute shockwave erupted. Rega's body lost balance and fell to the ground. Mjölnir didn't stop and followed Rega down. To automatically attack the still-living enemy.

[Matter Projection]

In the meantime, I projected and threw another Mjölnir replica.

Rega, having landed on the ground, swung both legs and struck Mjölnir away. His gaze was now directed at me, not Marit. Rega, veins bulging on his forehead, glared at me as if he'd kill me.

"You! Alchemist!! How dare you insult Mjölnir!!! I will never forgive you!!"

"You've been noisy for a while now. Can you do nothing but shout?"

[Matter Projection]

I projected and threw a third Mjölnir replica.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

His legs and Mjölnir collide, generating lightning shockwaves continuously. Every time Rega moves, droplets of blood fall little by little. Damage is steadily accumulating.

But that too was brief. The first Mjölnir couldn't even last 20 seconds before it shattered and disappeared.

'...It gets destroyed before the duration even ends. It's natural that its durability is lower than the original, but... it's weaker than expected.'

Following that, the second and third Mjölnir also shattered.

"UWAAAAAAAH!!"

Rega screamed and charged at me.

The barbarian warrior's skill, Roar of Fury. Temporary physical enhancement.

I extended my right arm toward him.

[Biological Transmutation]

I activate Grade 7 Vision Alchemy.

The form below my right wrist transformed into a metallic cannon barrel.

WHEEEEEEEENG!

Mana becomes visible at the end of the barrel, turning into blue photons. I fired the photon cannon at him.
