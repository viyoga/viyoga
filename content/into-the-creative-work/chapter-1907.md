What kind of power does this cherry tree have? How can I use it?

I felt curious as I looked at the faintly glowing cherry blossom petals.

Even now, seeing it with my own eyes, I don't know exactly what kind of power it has. The only thing certain is that this cherry tree is by no means ordinary.

Pajik, pajijik.

The faintly glowing cherry blossoms suddenly lose their light. The cherry blossom petals that bloomed from its gaunt body fell silently to the ground.

'...Did it die?'

It was difficult to grasp since it was a plant, not a living creature.

'If it dies from just this much, then that's all it is.'

I picked up a fallen cherry blossom petal. It was a petal with the pastel pink color unique to cherry blossoms.

'The petals themselves seem ordinary though.'

I looked around and injected mana, even using lightning, but the cherry blossom petals didn't react. It's just an ordinary cherry blossom petal.

'I don't know. There's too little information.'

The corners of my lips went up. Rather than being frustrated by the lack of information, I was excited. Even if I don't know the exact identity of these saplings, it was certain that they were trees from the Phantasmal World. It will be worth it to research and uncover the unknown with my own hands. I might even gain magical achievements in the process.

I took the cherry blossom petal and put it in my pocket.

• • •

The next day, I contacted Marik, the section chief of the real estate brokerage firm, and met him.

Seeing his strange expression, it seems he roughly grasped the situation. This time, it's his defeat. Marik tried to raise the price of the factory by pitting me and Gillian against each other, but neither of us were people who would solve it with money alone.

"I'll buy the factory for 400 million credits."

"Hmm. In the end, I couldn't even sell it at the original price."

Marik handed over the contract. It wasn't me, but Yuria who received the contract first. She quickly scanned the contract.

Marik seemed less motivated than when I first saw him. He leaned back in his chair and said.

"Gillian-nim was quite capable. Apart from his character, that is."

"...Well, he seemed like a capable smuggler."

I gained quite a bit from killing Gillian and raiding his workshop. Just the cash alone was several hundred million. I even thought that it might be more profitable to find and kill guys like Gillian than to run a business.

'If I recklessly kill and plunder, I'll be targeted instead. More than anything, there aren't many guys like Gillian.'

It meant that I didn't have to worry about money for the time being.

"Master-nim. There are no problems with the contract. It seems you can sign it as is."

"Haha. We, Hug Ground, are a leading company in this industry. We value our trust with customers more than anything else. We're not third-rate guys who mess with contracts."

That was bullshit.

There's a common saying that large corporations in Neo-London have no conscience for a reason. It meant that they would screw over even customers without hesitation if it benefited them. But there was no need to argue about it here. I nodded roughly.

"I see."

First, I also skimmed through the contract once. It wasn't that I didn't trust Yuria's words, but if I just listened to Yuria and signed it, it was clear that Marik would look down on me.

I signed the contract. I took a picture of the contract and we each took a copy.

Since I finished my business, I got up from my seat. Yuria opens the office door. It was the moment I turned around to leave.

"This glamorous city is like a den of beasts."

Marik said. I only turned my head to look at him. Marik was wearing a bitter smile.

"A place where the beast that wins today might become tomorrow's sustenance."

"Is that a warning?"

"I'm different from you."

"I understand. Please be careful on your way."

• • •

I got the factory. It's not over. It's just the beginning. I had to find magic engineering machines to put in the factory, and I had to find blueprints for the items to produce and sell.

'Should I start by imitating existing products?'

Launching innovative products and dominating the Neo-London market! That was a pipe dream. Innovative products? Considering Neo-London's long history, there are hardly any innovative products.

It's difficult even if I make innovative products. The big corporations wouldn't stand still. If they know I'm a powerless businessman, they'll try to take it away somehow.

'I'll imitate minor magic tools. Of course, I'll have to modify them so I don't get sued.'

I can think about the products slowly. I have plenty of time thanks to the things I got from raiding Gillian's workshop.

"Master-nim. I've prepared tea."

She brought a teapot and teacups. I immediately noticed that it wasn't the black tea I usually drink. I smelled a sweet cherry blossom scent instead of the deep scent of black tea.

Inside the transparent teapot, cherry blossom petals were being steeped. The brewed tea was a pretty pink color.

"I tried brewing tea with the cherry blossom petals you gave me, Master-nim. I'm very satisfied with the quality."

Yesterday, Yuria asked me for some cherry blossom petals, so I shared them. I didn't expect her to brew tea with them.

I looked at the teacup. The cherry blossom tea was perfect from the fragrance to the color. I could feel that it would be delicious even without tasting it.

"This has marketability."

"Yes. If it's called Phantasmal World cherry blossom tea, it can be sold at a high price. First, check the taste."

Warm tea entered my mouth. The fragrant cherry blossom scent made me feel like it was spring.

"I like it."

There was nothing more to say.

Ding-dong!

The doorbell rings. Me and Yuria exchanged glances.

"Did you have a guest scheduled to come today?"

"No, I don't. It's an uninvited guest."

Uninvited guests were not welcome. I wanted to tell them to just fuck off, but that would damage my reputation.

[Detection]

I used detection magic. It's to figure out how many guests are outside the door.

"......!"

I widened my eyes and straightened my posture. Detection didn't work. It was canceled in the middle. It means there's someone skilled enough to cancel my magic in an instant.

'They rang the doorbell, so they came as guests. I don't know what their purpose is, but they're not enemies.'

I sighed and said to Yuria.

"Bring them in. Prepare new cherry blossom tea too."

"Yes, Master-nim."

Soon, the guests came in. It was a middle-aged man accompanied by a butler and a maid. I got up from my seat and quickly scanned them. Maid and butler. Both of them are not ordinary. I can't figure out how skilled they are.

"Eugene Meyer. Is that right?"

Thanks to his arrogant tone, I realized he was a noble. The problem is that I don't know his name exactly. A proud noble can take even that as an insult.

Fortunately, the savior's voice was heard.

-He is Baron Arpon. He operates large hospitals in District D and District F.

"...I greet Baron Arpon-nim. What brings you to this humble place?"

"I heard you're a popular mercenary these days?"

"I'm running a restaurant."

"That's enough. Let's sit down and talk."

He plopped down first. He wasn't friendly to me. But he wasn't hostile either. I sat across from him. Seeing his prickly attitude, he didn't come to ask for a favor.

"Did you come to commission me? Please request the commission through an intermediary. That's the procedure."

"Commission? What would I use a magician like you for?"

"......."

I couldn't argue. He runs large hospitals in District D and District F, which can be said to be upper-class districts. He must have more money than he knows what to do with, and since he also has the title of Baron, it wouldn't be difficult to find a mercenary better than me.

Yuria brought out the newly brewed cherry blossom tea and poured it. She poured the tea into my teacup first as if it were natural. Baron Arpon's eyebrows twitched at that, but he soon relaxed his expression after smelling the cherry blossom scent that filled the air.

"Cherry blossom tea, huh? Even the fragrance is not ordinary."

Tea time can be said to be Neo-London's representative culture. There's nothing better than serving good tea to win someone over.

"It's not just any tea. It's tea brewed with cherry blossom petals from a cherry tree that grows in the Phantasmal World."

"I figured."

'...He figured?'

He says it as if he already knew. He took a sip of the cherry blossom tea and smiled contentedly.

"Even if you get precious tea leaves, if they fall into the hands of a bumpkin, they're no better than cheap coffee. But this tea perfectly brewed the tea leaves. It's been a long time since I've tasted such delicious tea. Lady, you're amazing."

"You flatter me."

Yuria, who answered briefly, retreated behind me.

"Gillian was my trading partner. Gillian was supposed to sell me a Phantasmal World tree sapling, and I was supposed to pay the price."

"...Gillian is no more."

"But you're here. I went to Gillian's workshop, and it was a mess. There were almost no traces left. If I didn't have information that you and Gillian had a conflict over a factory, I wouldn't have been able to come here."

"Gillian...."

"I know you killed him. Like that's a big deal. Gillian was a pain in the ass. I expected this to happen. I didn't expect him to die while trading with me, though."

I relaxed slightly. Being too nervous wasn't good because it made my thoughts rigid. Baron Arpon came to trade with me instead of the dead Gillian.

He doesn't want all three saplings. If it's one of them, there's no reason not to trade.

"Which sapling is it? Is it a cherry tree? Or a maple tree?"

"Neither. A lilac tree. I want that."

"I'll sell it."

"On the same terms as Gillian-guy?"

"I'll listen and decide. What did Gillian ask of the Baron-nim? I don't think he just asked for money."

There were things more important than money in Neo-London.

"He was going to give me the title of Vice Baron."

The qualification to enter noble society even though he was a commoner.

A title that can be called an honorary noble.

"...Even if it's a Vice Baron, a title is a title. Does the Baron-nim have the authority to grant a title?"
