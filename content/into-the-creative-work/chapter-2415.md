The cult leader's room that I temporarily gave to Kim Jihee-ssi had become a room with a Cult-like feel. On her desk were various religious books, like the Bible. It seems she's studying to be a cult leader. Though I don't know if that's the right direction.

The clothes Kim Jihee-ssi was wearing were also surprising. She was wearing white, flowing clothes. Similar to a priest's robe, but more extravagant. Anyway, it had a religious feel.

,Welcome, Your Holiness.'

Judging by her tone, she seemed to have become a religious person already.

,You've decorated the annex quite nicely in just one day. It must have been hard to find everything.'

,Yes. I received them from the people living in Seongak Elementary School. They gave them to me willingly.'

Superficially, there was freedom of religion. Christianity, Catholicism, Buddhism, shamanism, etc., were quite widespread. It seems she received religious items from them. No, it would be more accurate to say she took them. They can't refuse if she brings the Sacred Guard.

,To think she'd use her power to requisition things in just one day. In a way, she's an amazing woman.'

I should reconsider giving Kim Jihee-ssi the Sacred Guard.

,For now, I should keep them around as Kim Jihee-ssi's guard and for surveillance purposes. I could create another armed force and attach it to Kim Jihee-ssi. Of course, filled with fanatics who would jump into a pit of fire at my command.'

Anyway, that's not what's important right now.

,We need to mobilize the Seongjin Church. There are quite a few of them, so it's time for them to slowly stick their heads out into the open.'

Korea is a country with freedom of religion.

People have the perception that anything that isn't a major religion is a Cult, but there are definitely religions that aren't Cults.

,...The Seongjin Church is going out into the open as soon as I become the cult leader. Is this some kind of test for me?'

,The situation has changed. The First Deputy Director of the National Intelligence Service came yesterday afternoon and threw a fit.'

,Ah. I heard. Your Holiness killed the Satanic demon who called your younger sister by name.'

Kim Jihee-ssi didn't show much change in expression. She was better at hiding her emotions than First Deputy Director Shin Youngsoo, whom I met yesterday.

,What's important isn't that, but that the President is trying to dismantle Seongak Elementary School and target me and my women.'

,He doesn't know his place. Shall we declare a holy war?'

,Are you serious?'

,About half. Of course, Your Holiness' opinion is the most important.'

Kim Jihee-ssi's eyes sparkled. She felt like a child who had gotten a new toy. I know that feeling well. The feeling of wanting to use the power you've gained right away.

,Don't do anything unnecessary. What you need to do is incite the believers to protest with Son Taehyung-ssi. Remove all religious aspects and disguise them as civilian protesters.'

,Isn't Korea under martial law right now? Is it okay to protest?'

,The Seongjin Church's believers exist in all levels of society. It was a bit difficult with the military... but I've already won over some politicians. The same goes for journalists. Even if martial law has been declared, this is Korea. Are they really going to run us over with tanks? Peaceful protesters who aren't carrying weapons?'

,They can't do that, I suppose. But... what's the theme of the protest?'

,Anything that can attack the current government, President Jeong Woo-hyun, is fine. But the most representative thing... yeah. This would be good. Gun control. Mutants and Variants are all over the city, and they're regulating guns? That's insane. The people actually want guns. They need a way to protect themselves.'

,...That sounds fun. Yes. I will do as Your Holiness wishes.'

I don't think I can drag Jeong Woo-hyun down from the presidency with protesters. But I can attract attention. If attention is drawn, it will become public opinion, and that will become a spark.

,Propaganda and fabrication... I'll burn down his approval ratings using every means possible.'

And the moment his approval ratings drop and a suitable justification is created....

,A coup d'état.'

I don't intend to take the lead myself.

There are several guys who will take the lead. There are at least three high-ranking politicians who have devoted themselves to the Seongjin Church. I just need to entrust it to one of them.

,I'll become the shadow power broker!'

And at the right time, I'll ascend to the throne as the Emperor of Korea!

,It's a perfect scenario.'

To do that, I have to move diligently.

I couldn't succumb to something like food. If I protested again, he would definitely start talking about the people. And a few hours later, there would be an article about how Seong Yujin-ssi is hoarding food even though the people are having a hard time.

I went straight to Na Chaeyoung-ssi.

,Dr. Na, how much food do we have in reserve? How many months can we last?'

,...It seems the National Intelligence Service has started messing with the food supply. With the food we have in reserve, we can only last for a month.'

,...A month? I've been stockpiling food whenever I had the chance, and we can only last for a month?'

,There are over 5,000 people gathered here at Seongak Elementary School right now. You accepted a large number of people to grow the Seongjin Church.'

,...To expand the influence of the Seongjin Church, we need a lot of believers. We also have to provide separate education. People have no choice but to gather here.'

That's right. The capacity of Seongak Elementary School is already beyond its limit. Most of the people at Seongak Elementary School are living in cramped spaces, like a chicken coop. The reason why complaints don't come out is because I suppressed them with force. And because I guarantee a minimum amount of food.

,The good thing is that we have a lot of money. The believers are competing to donate. The problem is that even if we have a lot of money, there's nowhere to spend it.'

It's an era where zombies and Variants roam the streets. Convenience stores and supermarkets have obviously gone out of business. Delivery? Of course not. The government has almost monopolized the food supply.

Even if you have money, you can't get food. Even if you can get it, it's questionable whether you can buy as much as you want. I heard that the price of a single potato is at least 20,000 won.

,...What about smart farms or hydroponics? We prepared them in advance.'

,We postponed that. What was urgent during that time wasn't food, but defense and security. We're still improving the combat AI and weapons. Even if we focus on hydroponics or smart farms from now on, we won't be able to get enough food to satisfy Seongak Elementary School.'

I scratched my head roughly. Most of the reason why Seongak Elementary School is maintained is food. If food isn't supplied, a rebellion will happen 100%. Even if that's not the case, there will be a flood of guys trying to escape from Seongak Elementary School.

,We need to get food in other ways.'

,It won't be easy. The National Intelligence Service will interfere no matter what.'

,If not, I'll have to settle things within the next month.'

Late at night.

I secretly left Seongak Elementary School and headed to a wealthy neighborhood.

Perhaps because it was a neighborhood where rich people lived, it was quiet without any zombies or Variants. I walked into a mansion with the lights on and rang the doorbell.

Ding-dong. Click.

As if they had been waiting, the front door opened. I confidently entered the mansion.

As I entered, a middle-aged man sitting on the living room sofa jumped up in surprise.

,Who are you?! Honey! Who is this person?!'

His wife didn't even look at her husband and knelt down in front of me.

,Ah! You've come, Seong-nim! Please give our family blessings and mercy!!'

,Yeah. I've come. Bring me a Coke if you have one.'

,I, I'm sorry. We don't have Coke in our house, but we have coffee....'

,I understand. It must have been difficult to get Coke. Bring me water then.'

The middle-aged woman jumped up and ran to the kitchen.

The middle-aged man who had been sitting on the sofa was now holding a gun and pointing it at me. I calmly looked at him and asked.

,Are you Jin Soohak, the Chief of Staff of the Army?'

,...That appearance. I remember now. The Thunder God, Seong Yujin. Why did you come to my house? No, how did you get here? There should be guards near the house.'

,I'm good at moving quietly. I also received help from your wife.'

I also received information from Jin Soohak's wife that Chief of Staff Jin Soohak would be coming home today.

,Put the gun down and let's talk. As you can see, I don't have any weapons either.'

,...Do you think I don't know that you're an Awakened?'

,So I hope you'll understand my sincerity even more. If I was going to kill an ordinary person, I would have killed you already.'

,......'

In the end, Jin Soohak lowered his gun. His eyes were full of vigilance as he looked at me.

His wife, who had gone to the kitchen, appeared. In one hand, she held a cup of clean water, and in the other, she held an urn.

She politely handed me the water and prostrated herself on the floor.

I gulped down the water. It was quite cool.

,Please, please save my son! Seong-nim! Please!'

That damn Seong-nim.

I was about to let out a hollow laugh, but I stopped. Let them call me whatever they want.

,Seong Yujin! What did you do to my wife?!'

,Why? Do you think I brainwashed her or something?'

,Otherwise, there's no way my wife would act like this! What's your purpose?!'

,Don't shout. First of all....'

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Waves (Leitian Flow Electromagnetic Waves).

There were no bugs or small cameras.

,Good. Let's get straight to the point. I'll save your son who died 20 years ago. There's only one condition. Come under me. And for trust, I'll take one of your fingers. If you refuse, I'll kill you to keep the secret.'

,Are you crazy?'

He said as if he was beyond angry and dumbfounded. His wife was horrified and grabbed Jin Soohak's leg. She was desperate enough to shed tears.

,You!! This is a chance to save Minwoo! Quickly kneel before Seong-nim! Kneel! Minwoo! You have to meet Minwoo!!'

,Honey. Calm down! Saving someone! That's impossible!'

,I saw it! I saw it! I saw with my own two eyes that Seong-nim revived a dead baby!!'

,This woman is really.... Seong Yujin! What the hell did you do to my wife?!'

,Tsk. There's no progress in the conversation. I guess it's better to show you directly.'

I opened the urn, grabbed the remains inside, and used Recovery.

It was a bit difficult, but Recovery was activated properly. What was just remains began to regenerate as if time was being reversed. Bones and flesh regenerated, transforming into the appearance of a young boy about 6 years old.

,Ah, ah! Minwoo! Minwoo!!'

Jin Soohak's wife was moved and shed tears. Jin Soohak stared blankly at the body of the boy in my hand with his mouth open.

,...Really. Did you really save my son, Minwoo?'

,He's not alive yet. He has a heart, but it's not beating. If you want to save your son, accept my conditions.'

,This. Is this possible? Reviving a dead person...!'

,The world is already a mess. The zombie virus has spread, and people have turned into monsters called Variants. Players have appeared and claim that this world was originally a game, and Awakened people use incomprehensible superpowers. It's not that strange to have the ability to revive the dead, is it?'

,You! Say you'll do it quickly! Quickly!! I want to see Minwoo! Poor Minwoo! Quickly pledge allegiance to Seong-nim!!'

His wife urged him as if she was screaming.

,......'

Jin Soohak stared blankly at his son in my hand. His eyes were shaking violently.

Thud.

Jin Soohak's knees fell to the floor.

,Who are you.... Who are you?'

,I am the God of Life.'

,...I know that everything has a price. What is the price I have to pay?'

,A coup d'état. Jeong Woo-hyun is possessed by a Satanic demon. That bastard will ruin the Republic of Korea. So we have to bring him down before that happens.'

Jin Soohak's eyes twitched. He pondered for a long time before finally speaking.

,I will pledge allegiance to you. Please save my son.'

I used Recovery. His child's heart began to beat again.

,Ugh....'

The child opened his eyes, whimpering. The child was startled when he saw me. I handed the child to his mother.

The mother shed tears and hugged her son tightly.

,Minwoo! Minwoo!!'

,Ack! O, ow! Where, where's Mom? Mom!'

,Mom!!'

The child finally recognized his mother.

Jin Soohak, who witnessed this scene, had a choked expression and his eyes were red.

,You have a lot to do, so I'll give you youth later. Try to hide the child's existence for now.'

,Yes. Your Excellency. But... I can't carry out a coup d'état alone. I need people who share my intentions.'

,I plan to win over several people. I'd like you to make a list. What we can give them as a reward for being won over is youth. Is that not enough?'

Jin Soohak looked at his wife and child, who were hugging and crying. His wife, who had become younger. His child, who had been revived. He swallowed the overwhelming emotions with difficulty and said.

,It will be enough. To them, youth will be a second life.'
