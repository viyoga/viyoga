,"The back of the moon. A place swarming with terrible monsters. I wouldn't go there if I were you. It's a very dangerous place."

,"Okuto. Do you know about the back of the moon?"

,"Of course. That's where I came from, you know."

,"Is it overflowing with monsters like Tsukuyomi said?"

,"Tsukuyomi-nim exaggerated a bit, you know. There are a lot of monsters, but it's a place with its own order. Of course, if you're careless, you'll get eaten right away, you know."

,"The fairies don't even approach the back of the moon, you know. It's dangerous, as Tsukuyomi-nim said."

It seemed best to escape to the back of the moon if things got rough. Of course, I had no intention of losing to Hang-a-yang at all.

Unlike when I came up, Mt. Wol didn't have any fog at all. Thanks to that, I could see and enjoy the excellent scenery of Mt. Wol with my own eyes. I could also see creatures wandering around here and there.

,"This way, you know!"

Okuto went into the dense trees. The path was so dense that it was hard to see. Had we moved without rest for about 5 minutes? I and Okuto were already in a cave. The cave's unique humidity sticks to my skin.

「Entered Sector 7,900, Lake of the Moon.」

「This is a hidden sector.」

「Disconnected from the outside.」

「All stats decreased by 10%.」

「You must give up one of your five senses every 3 hours. Choose which sense to give up: taste, touch, sight, smell, hearing.」

「If you do not choose within 50 seconds, it will be chosen randomly.」

「This is an ungovernable sector.」

Notification windows popped up in a row.

,'Hidden sector. This is unexpected... but the conditions are a bit harsh.'

Being disconnected from the outside meant that I couldn't use space movement scrolls. Hang-a-yang probably wouldn't be able to peek at this place with her Moon Mirror either.

,'All stats decreasing by 10% is one thing, but....'

Having to give up one of my senses was too much.

「If you do not choose within 37 seconds, it will be chosen randomly.」

,"I give up taste."

There was no choice. In terms of the importance of the senses, taste was the lowest. Next, I'd give up smell.

,'Smell is the next problem. Touch, sight, hearing. These are senses that are absolutely necessary for combat. This isn't a place to stay for long.'

As I moved, Okuto also started to move. I asked Okuto.

,"Okuto. You didn't give up a sense."

,"Yes. I believe you."

Okuto's voice had no strength. It seemed like it was thinking that I would die in all likelihood.

,"No. It's my first time, you know. There's no reason to deliberately come into such a dangerous place, you know."

,"You know the way even though it's your first time?"

,"What's the criteria for judging the path?"

,"Hmm. It's hard to put into words... I just feel it, should I say?"

,"Yes, yes. It's similar, you know."

,"If you run away, you'll die."

,"Hiiiik...! The Imugi saw me, you know! Even if I run away, the Imugi will chase me and kill me! Cheonma-nim and I are in this together, you know!"

,"You're saying unpleasant things."

The calm surface of the lake began to ripple roughly. The huge presence in the lake had begun to move.

A black shadow and a red light flashed under the blue lake water. The red light was the Imugi's eyes.

-I was hungry, and the food came to me on its own. I'm lucky after all.

The Imugi soars above the lake. Water splashed everywhere in the aftermath. Soaked in water, I clicked my tongue and checked the Imugi's appearance. It was a huge snake that could swallow a person whole in one bite. Its red eyes glaring at me were full of murderous intent.

-Come here.

The Imugi's red eyes flashed.

I quietly watched the Imugi. I was gauging the Imugi's strength. It was my first time facing an Imugi, so I didn't know if it was stronger than me. I'd have to fight it directly to find out.

,'Telekinesis.'

I take a breath and awaken the Demonic Energy from my dantian. The Demonic Energy races through my meridians and spreads throughout my body. Black energy flows out of my body little by little, resisting the telekinesis.

The Imugi faltered.

Then it opens its mouth wide and spits poison at me.

Hot flames rise from my body. The surrounding water instantly rose. The Imugi, which was swimming towards me, was startled and retreated, circling around me.

I can't maintain the Black Flame, which consumes a lot of mana, for long.

I have [Blessing of Water], but it's not very helpful in this situation.
