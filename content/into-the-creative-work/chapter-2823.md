Seeing the summoned Yuria and Elena, I felt a sense of novelty. It felt like it had been a really long time since I'd seen them.

,I was in the World of Ascension to Enlightenment for quite a while, so it's true that it's been a long time.'

Unlike me, they had met me recently. There was a time gap between me and them. This was inevitable.

"Wiyu-nim, I've heard stories from my master. I am honored to meet my master's teacher. I am Yuria Grace, master's wife and maid."

Wiyu slowly nodded.

"I've heard stories about you too. Seeing you in person, you are indeed impressive. Sim-gi-che. There is nothing to criticize."

"I am Elena Valdert. A magician. I hear you are very strong? Frankly, I can't quite imagine it."

"I've heard about you too. A illusionist. You were born with a rare talent."

Their brief greetings ended.

Yuria looked around. She examined every corner of the house. Perhaps it was the instinct of the Head maid. She seemed to be looking for places that were not properly cleaned.

,Honestly, there are quite a few lacking spots.'

Wiyu and I were the type to only clean the areas that were easily visible. We didn't bother to dig up and clean places that weren't in sight.

"It seems like it needs some tidying up. If you don't have anything urgent, I will do it. It's been a while since I've cleaned myself."

In the 'Baekhwan' world, Yuria's status is that of a duchess. It doesn't make sense for her to clean herself. The maids below take care of cleaning the mansion. But there were no maids here. Except for her.

"Cleaning as soon as you arrive? Yuria. Isn't that a bit too extreme? It looks clean enough to me? Isn't that a bit of obsessive-compulsive disorder?"

"I just have slightly higher standards for cleanliness than others. If you look at the top of the TV in the living room and behind the dust, there is dust settled. Shouldn't we clean that? Cleanliness is a basic requirement for a maid."

Yuria looked at me. It was a look asking for my permission.

"Before that. Yuria and Elena. To what extent can you use your power right now?"

"Just a moment."

Yuria and Elena checked their own bodies.

Yuria's shadow flickered. The shadow grew and then shrank.

Elena flicked her fingers, which glowed with mana, in the air. Blue butterflies briefly appeared around her and then disappeared.

"I can use Aura without any problems. If I push myself, I can use Aura Blade for a short time. Large-scale magic is impossible."

"It's hard for me to say in as much detail as Yuria. I know it's less than half of my original power."

They had become much weaker than I thought.

Is it natural since they are level 1 now?

I decided to use points to raise their levels. Since the United States and other powerful countries could become my enemies, I needed to secure as much power as possible.

『Name: Yuria Grace

Level: 20

Summoner: Seong Yujin

Summoning Maintenance Cost: 24 hours 20,000 Coins』

『Name: Elena Valdert

Level: 20

Summoner: Seong Yujin

Summoning Maintenance Cost: 24 hours 20,000 Coins』

I used a total of 1.18 million coins.

"My body has become stronger. It feels like something that was blocked has been cleared. I think I can use Aura Blade without overdoing it."

"It seems like it's more than half of what it used to be. I don't know how this world is... but unless it's a really strong opponent, they won't be able to escape my illusion."

It's burdensome to use more coins.

I'm still on the lower floors of the tower, so I'm not making much money.

,Including Wiyu's summoning maintenance fee, a total of 70,000 coins goes out per day.'

To continue maintaining their summons, I need to earn more than 70,000 coins a day.

『Name: Seong Yujin

Age: 21

Affiliated Dimension: Earth

Affiliated Country: Korea

Level: 5

Coins: 1,484,107

Unique Ability: Character Summon (Lv.1)

*All abilities increase by 5% due to being ranked 1st.』

I can maintain their summons for 21 days right now.

Why not just summon them when I need them? I needed them all the time. Just having them by my side makes me feel good.

,The amount of points consumed increases as the level increases. Should I choose to increase the coin acquisition rate from the next floor challenge?'

Honestly, that seems like it would be more profitable.

,Right now, 1 coin is 30 dollars, so 70,000 coins is....'

I was too lazy to calculate, so I asked Yuria.

"It's about ₩2,898,000,000 in Korean currency."

The price of 70,000 coins is ₩2.9 billion. It's more expensive than I thought.

,I need to consider that the inflation of money in this world is a bit higher than in reality.'

This world looks stable on the surface. But if you look closely, you can see that it's falling apart.

,Was the price of a can of cola ₩7,000?'

Not only the economy but also the birth rate has collapsed. The global birth rate is 0.2, and it's continuing to decline.

Humanity is currently living on borrowed time. And having children doesn't increase their lifespan; it decreases it. The more people there are, the more coins need to be used in the tower.

,It's a surprisingly dystopian world.'

I was lost in thought for a moment and looked around.

Yuria was focused on cleaning. She was using magic generously for cleaning, so it seemed like the cleaning would be finished in 3 minutes.

Elena and Wiyu were sitting on the sofa, watching TV dramas and enjoying wine. Perhaps it was because Wiyu liked alcohol. Surprisingly, the two got along well.

,The house is a bit small.'

When it was just Wiyu and me, I thought it was okay. An apartment in the 20 pyeong range. It was adequate for two people to live in. But with four people, it was different. The house felt small.

,Should I sell coins and move to a new house? Or should I take out a superconductor?'

But even if I sell the superconductor, I don't think I'll make as much money as I think. Right now, this world is struggling to survive. Civilizational development is not the top priority.

,Should I just deal with 100,000 coins? I can earn about ₩4 billion. ...Will it be cut in half after taxes?'

I wasn't surprised because Hunters are taxed a lot in reality.

But even in this world, Hunters are taxed 50%?

This can't be helped. It's something the Hunters agreed to a long time ago.

,Taxes are essential for a country to be maintained. Not only in Korea but also worldwide, Hunters' taxes are over 50%.'

Honestly, I wasn't very interested because I wasn't short on money. I even found the current situation where I needed money to be fun.

,Let's think about the future. The number of summoned creatures will increase day by day. One apartment unit won't be able to handle it. It wouldn't be bad to buy an entire apartment.'

It didn't have to be in Seoul.

,Hmm. Building a new one will take time, right? First, I need to collect coins. It's hunting time.'

• • •

Seong Yujin entered the tower to earn coins, and only three women were left at home. It was Seong Yujin's consideration for them to rest comfortably at home on the first day.

"Your cooking skills are as excellent as Milyeong's said. It's hard to tell who is better between you and Milyeong."

It was Wiyu's evaluation after eating Yuria's cooking. Yuria was slightly surprised but soon understood. Milyeong also had a talent for cooking. She would have invested more time in cooking than Yuria, so it was natural for her skills to catch up.

Elena, who enjoyed wine with Wiyu, made wine for refreshment. Wiyu, who was next to him, admired him.

The magic of turning illusions into reality. You're creating a harmony that even immortals couldn't achieve.

"Hehe. Thank you for the high praise. It's quite a convenient ability."

Elena poured a glass of wine for Wiyu and Yuria and asked.

"What are you going to do in the future? According to Yujin, Earth's power is not that strong. If we use our power well, we can dominate it enough. I can even dominate the minds of the presidents of each country right now."

"Well. There is a variable called the tower. If the mind control is released, the situation could worsen. In the worst case, a nuclear war might break out."

"Nuclear war? You seem to be going too far, Yuria."

"The humans in this world are driven to the extreme. The lifespan of adults and children alike does not exceed one year."

"...Now that I hear it, it wouldn't be strange if a nuclear war broke out tomorrow. The feeling of knowing that your lifespan is not long.... I'm reminded of a fact I had forgotten for a while."

"Either way, it's not a problem we decided. The decision is made by the master. All we can do is advise."

"What if Yujin chooses the destruction of humanity?"

"Humanity deserves to be destroyed."

"...Sometimes you seem to worship Yujin like a god."

"That's not true. My master is greater than God."

Elena couldn't refute Yuria's words. Yujin's ability is truly greater than God.

Anyway, Elena knew Yuria's thoughts. Yuria doesn't care about this world. She doesn't care whether it's destroyed or not. The only thing that matters to Yuria is Seong Yujin.

"Wiyu. What do you think?"

"Right now, I'm just Yujin's summoned creature. But I don't think it's right to carry out the destruction of humanity. I'll stop Yujin at least once."

"Even if Yujin wants the destruction of humanity?"

"It's a pity, but I don't have any great cause. As a summoned creature, I will follow Yujin."

Wiyu sipped her wine. There was emptiness in her eyes.

"You're leaving the choice to Yujin. So you've both been ignoring what's outside."

Elena. We must not interfere with our master's play.

"I know. I'm going to play around and enjoy a vacation this time. But what if the guys outside cross the line first?"

"As a maid, I must exterminate the bugs that crawl into the house."

"If the other party doesn't respect us, we don't need to respect them either."

Yuria and Wiyu said firmly.

Elena smiled and agreed.

"I think the same way. Protecting the house is also the role of a wife."

Blue butterflies fluttered and disappeared.

"You don't have to do that. As my master's wife and maid, I have already tampered with their shadows."

"Isn't my illusion more reliable than shadows? I can completely dominate their minds."

"It's cleaner to erase them with shadows the moment they cross the line."

"You have to handle it perfectly, not cleanly."

"......"

"......"

The two quietly looked at each other. Just because they don't get along doesn't mean they can shout and fight without dignity.

Wiyu, who was watching, let out a hollow laugh and said.

"You guys. You seem to get along well, but you don't."
