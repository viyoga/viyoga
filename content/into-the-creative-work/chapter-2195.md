Most of my past life memories are focused on the original game, 'Dark Moon.' Recently, other memories have been popping up occasionally... but they're just minor things.

,A memorial service. It's been so long. How did I do it again?'

There was a memorial service event in the original game as well. But it was streamlined. Just click a button and the memorial service would proceed and end on its own. You could even skip it. The memorial service itself was also as concise as possible.

'Who in their right mind would spend more than 30 seconds on a memorial service in a game? The game would be doomed.'

The Dark Moon game developers knew that kind of thing well. So, I decided to streamline it roughly too.

I poured refined rice wine into a glass, circled the glass three times over the cyber hologram incense burner, and placed it on the memorial table.

The cyber ancient ancestor deity nods in satisfaction.

'Is this right? I guess it is.'

Next, I bow.

Bowing to someone, even kneeling, wasn't a pleasant act. But I had no choice. I had to endure it because there was something to gain.

,Hmm. You bow well. You can't fool the Korean blood in you.'

Am I doing it well? I just did it as I felt.

,But why aren't the ladies behind you bowing? Are they perhaps Christian? Surely they don't see our Korean customs as primitive?'

The deity's voice grew colder towards the end. Creating a hostile atmosphere with the deity after coming all this way wasn't a good idea.

,They are foreigners and don't know much about Korean memorial services yet.'

A scanning light came from the tablet and swept over Orphelia and Invisible Blade. The cyber ancestor nods.

,I see. A Japanese woman and an English woman. It's natural they wouldn't know about memorial services. They can learn about memorial services gradually in the future. What is your relationship with these ladies?'

Saying we have no relationship would be obviously wrong to anyone. They could be chased away or face hostility. Great Korea doesn't exactly welcome outsiders.

,They are women I've promised to marry. You could say they are my future wives.'

So, if they're future family, the story would be different.

Fortunately, they didn't object to my words.

,You've managed to take such pretty ladies as wives. Truly a *Han-nam*. Back in my day, international marriages were popular. Korean women didn't want to give birth....'

The deity, about to continue, seemed to notice the chilling atmosphere and cleared his throat before speaking differently.

,Anyway, they are women who will become your wives in the future. Why don't you try bowing once?'

,Hmm. You want me to do a dogeza?'

,Not a dogeza, a bow!'

,Well, it's not a difficult task.'

Invisible Blade bowed. It wasn't a Korean bow, but a dogeza close to the proper form. Befitting a ninja, there was a certain precision to her dogeza.

,Her chest is shaking so much that my gaze keeps going there.'

The deity. This old man's gaze is also directed towards her chest. For a moment, I was furious and considered flipping the table.

,...If this is part of the ritual, then it can't be helped.'

Orphelia, being perceptive, also cooperated. She performed a dogeza following Invisible Blade.

,I said, don't do a dogeza, do a bow!'

The deity got angry, but that was it. He soon cleared his throat and said.

,Ahem. I'll let it slide since you're foreigners. Make sure to bow properly next time. Ah, descendant. You've chosen your women well. After examining them, I've confirmed that the Japanese woman has the potential to be a virtuous wife, and the English woman has inherited mystery. Korea always welcomes talent.'

,I'm glad you approve.'

,Yes. Now, tell me. Why have you come all the way to Sebitseom?'

,I wish to be granted the rights to LK-99, the origin of Great Korea and the beginning of its brilliant history.'

,LK-99.... It is everything to this Sebitseom. It is also the starting point of war. Are you aware? We hid LK-99 for decades after its development. But there is no eternal secret.... China and Japan found out and coveted LK-99, starting a war.'

,What was the result of the war?'

,If you, a future person, don't know... then I don't know either. Sebitseom was the first place to be attacked. I willingly sealed this Sebitseom for Korea, along with this artificial intelligence that has copied my mind. Can you handle LK-99?'

Of course, I can't do it alone. But the Bado Association is a different story. Also, the current era is different from the old days. In this world where magic engineering is common, the value of LK-99 is bound to fade. Superconductor? It can be created by actively using magic. However, it just can't match the cost-effectiveness of LK-99.

,I can handle it.'

,Good. Korean technology should be in the hands of Koreans. I will grant you the rights to LK-99.'

The memorial table hologram disappears as if it were turned off. Then, the memorial table splits and a machine rises up. It was a familiar shape.

,...A water purifier?'

,Ahem. A water purifier, you say. This is our great Korean invention, the LK-Dispenser.'

,What is that?'

I asked in confusion. In the original work, it gave the rights to the superconductor and only handed over a special material called the essence of the superconductor. I was planning to receive this and study it separately. I'm quite confident in my alchemy skills.

,LK-99 is made of copper, lead, and a special substance called LK. In fact, the LK special substance is the real deal. This LK-Dispenser can create 5L of LK special substance every 24 hours.'

,...What does LK stand for?'

,It stands for Legend Korea.'

,.......'

He doesn't seem to be joking. I grabbed the water purifier. A sound came from the water purifier.

,BEEP. *Han-nam* Eugene. You have been certified as the manager of LK-99.'

,How much superconductor can be made with this special substance?'

,It depends on how you use it. If the purpose is simply to mass-produce superconductors, and the mass-production facilities are optimized, you can make more than 10 tons of superconductor per liter.'

,Are there any other uses for the LK special substance?'

,That.... I don't know. Strangely, only that information has been erased. Find out the other uses of the LK special substance yourself.'

It's not like I know everything about the original game. The game's world was vast, but as far as I remember, it didn't have an ending. It was an open-world game with continuous patches and updates. It wouldn't be strange if there were settings I didn't know about.

I've secured what I needed for now. I asked Orphelia to store the LK-Dispenser in her subspace. Then, I asked the deity for the detailed recipe of LK-99. The recipe for LK-99 was simpler than I thought. Just add lead, copper, and the LK special substance, bake it appropriately, and *jya-jan*, a room-temperature ambient-pressure superconductor is complete.

,I've obtained the LK-99 I was aiming for with this. I'll have to go back to Neo London to think about how to mass-produce and distribute it.'

Kwaaaang!

I was quietly resting after getting what I needed when suddenly the ground shook.

,Those bastards. The intruders are too rough. I don't think I can hold out for long. I'd like to give you the management rights of this Sebitseom, but... I don't think it's possible. Most of Sebitseom's authority has disappeared, leaving very little. But I can't hand it over to someone else either....'

,Are you self-destructing?'

,You know well. Sebitseom will meet its end in an hour. Get out of here before then. I... it's time for me to disappear. It was nice meeting you.'

Pung.

The tablet exploded.

We, who were resting, had no choice but to get up.

,Sebitseom's self-destruction was a predetermined course, but... it's self-destructing already? This situation is worse than I thought. The capabilities of the corporations are greater than I expected.'

I looked at Orphelia.

,Orphelia-ssi. Can you escape with Mass Teleport?'

,...Long-distance space movement is impossible. The power of the ruins is interfering. We need to get outside at least.'

,I see. It won't help to stay here, so let's get out.'

Kwaaaaang!

A shockwave exploded from the ceiling. My experience as a soldier is telling me. A bunker buster type missile has been dropped on the ceiling.

But the ceiling held. Sebitseom is an ancient ruin of Great Korea. It wouldn't collapse from a single bunker buster. But what if it's not just one?

Kwaaaaang! Kwaaaaang!

Shockwaves explode in succession.

,...Using bunker busters in succession? ...There's a possibility that not just corporations but a country is involved.'

More than that, I couldn't understand why they were coming out so urgently.

,It's as if they know we got our hands on the superconductor. Did they notice something?'

Kwaaaaang!

The ceiling collapsed and sunlight illuminated the interior. We tensed up in preparation for battle. Armed men slowly descend from the ceiling.

,Mage detected. Capture is the top priority. Return to engagement.'

The battle began.

• • •

Aman and Doctor Hong Baejeok were caught in a trap and separated from the group. Fortunately, they survived, but the fact that they fell deep underground was not good news.

,Fucking hell.... The corporations are shooting each other to death right now, and instead of escaping, I have to take care of this baggage....'

Aman was very unhappy with the situation. He wanted to abandon Doctor Hong and run away, but if he did, he could completely fall out with the corporation. The only consolation was that Doctor Hong wasn't completely useless.

,Look at that sign! There's a Supernatural Creature Research Institute! Supernatural creatures! It's definitely a place that researches monsters with abilities! It's definitely worth checking out!'

,...If I get those research materials, I can make a solid fortune, right? Not bad.'

Aman imagined himself handing over the research materials to the corporation and becoming an executive. He didn't want to live as a pirate forever. They say crisis and opportunity come together. He never thought that an opportunity to change his fate would come so readily.

,Doctor Hong, guide me to that research lab or whatever.'

,This way!'

Doctor Hong ran off excitedly. The ancient ruins of Great Korea were waiting for him.
