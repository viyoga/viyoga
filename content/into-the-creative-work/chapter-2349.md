I looked into Muyeong Shintu to find traces of Danmok Soha.

In this world, Muyeong Shintu (無影神偸) is a nickname given to the best thief of the era. Danmok Soha is more than worthy of being called Muyeong Shintu. She is also Muyeong Shintu's granddaughter.

So, I went to the Hao Mun. Because it's the fastest and easiest way to get information.

,It's an honor to meet you! Great Hero of Absolute Divine Chivalry! What brings you to the Hao Mun?!"

As soon as they saw me, the Hao Mun branch leader and his subordinates, whom I was meeting for the first time, bent over and greeted me. Information about me has spread throughout the Hao Mun. They probably don't know my real name, so they won't know my true identity.

Even if they know my identity, it doesn't matter. There's nothing the Hao Mun can do.

,Bring me information about Muyeong Shintu from the last 20 years."

,Um... Great Hero. We don't know Muyeong Shintu's identity either."

,When did I ask you to tell me their identity? I told you to bring me information. Information about where they've been active recently."

,I understand! Please wait a moment! Ah, I'll bring you some tea!"

,You wouldn't put poison in the tea, would you?!"

,Absolutely not!"

The Hao Mun branch leader immediately knelt on the floor and shouted.

,Just kidding."

,Haha. Yes..."

The branch leader was constantly sweating and maintained a very cautious attitude. I took a sip of the tea he brought and put it down. It tasted bad.

About 10 minutes later, low-quality papers were piled up in front of me.

,You're bringing information pretty quickly. Was the Hao Mun keeping an eye on Muyeong Shintu?"

,One of the best-selling pieces of information at the Hao Mun is information about Muyeong Shintu."

,Why?"

,Wanderers see Muyeong Shintu as an opportunity to turn their lives around. They believe that if they catch Muyeong Shintu... they can get their hands on rising martial arts and elixirs that can make them masters. For that reason, there are even chasing squads that specialize in tracking down Muyeong Shintu."

,Has Muyeong Shintu ever been caught by those kinds of guys?"

,Surprisingly, yes. A group of wanderers called Jeonggu Shibbong (正口十棒) captured Muyeong Shintu 15 years ago. They acquired advanced martial arts, elixirs, and magical artifacts and established a sect. This tale is well-known among wanderers, though it's somewhat forgotten these days. Besides that, there are quite a few stories of people capturing Muyeong Shintu and rising to fame.

,Was the Muyeong Shintu who was caught 15 years ago a woman?"

,He was a man with a big lump on his cheek."

,What about this generation's Muyeong Shintu?"

,As I said before, we don't know either. This generation's Muyeong Shintu is one of the most meticulous and secretive Muyeong Shintus in history. At best, we can only understand their actions after the fact. Oh, by the way, two years ago, the Hwaik Mun (火翼門), which governs this area, had its secret manuals and elixirs stolen by Muyeong Shintu.

I listened to his words and checked the last page of the data. It was the place where Muyeong Shintu was predicted to be.

Jianzhao City, Xinjiang

It was one of the major cities in Xinjiang, and it was my target. Because the Loeum Temple is in this big city.

,Jianzhao City, huh. What a coincidence. That's exactly where I'm going now."

,Um, we can't be sure that Muyeong Shintu is in Jianzhao City. We've only been tracking their movements based on the data so far..."

,There must be a good reason for that guess."

,Jianzhao City was originally where the Loeum Temple was located. When bandits and thieves were rampant, people flocked to the Loeum Temple in search of peace. Over time, it gradually developed into a large city. But now the Loeum Temple is closed."

,I've never heard of the Loeum Temple closing."

,They haven't officially declared closure. However, since the Loeum Temple hasn't engaged in any official activities for the past 10 years, people consider it closed."

,What happened at the Loeum Temple 10 years ago?"

,I'm sorry. We don't know that far. The Loeum Temple is such a secretive sect... it's not easy to find out internal information."

,Is the Loeum Temple so great that the Hao Mun can't influence it?"

,Yes? Of course, that's obvious... If you only consider the power of the Loeum Temple, it's greater than the famous sects belonging to the Nine Great Sects."

,...I might have to change my plan then."

I was going to go there, kill them all, and steal their martial arts. But if their power is greater than the famous sects, it's impossible. There must be at least one absolute master who has reached the Samjeong realm. Unless I have detailed information about the Loeum Temple, it's right to approach cautiously.

,I'll have to see the situation after I arrive in Jianzhao City.'

I threw some silver coins to the Hao Mun branch leader as payment for the information and was about to go outside.

Great Hero! Among the information we obtained, there is news that the Great Evil Eight Evils Association is targeting Jianzhao City.

,What's that?"

,It's a group of evil martial arts masters. There is information that they are targeting Jianzhao City, taking advantage of the fact that the Loeum Temple is not active. Please be careful..."

,Careful? Do I have to be mindful of those kinds of guys? Are you ignoring me right now?"

,I-It was a slip of the tongue!"

,I'm in a good mood right now, so I'll let it slide. Make sure you know your place."

I clicked my tongue and was about to leave, but I stopped.

The Great Evil Eight Evils Association. I feel like I've heard of it somewhere? I was lost in thought for a moment, but I roughly brushed it off. If I can't think of it right away, they probably aren't that important.

• • •

,Hoo..."

The Hao Mun branch leader wiped the sweat from his forehead and let out a sigh of relief.

The Samjeong master who calls himself the Absolute Divine Chivalry was quite famous around here. The existence that single-handedly destroyed a sect called Byeoksi Mun was bound to be famous.

People called him the Absolute Divine Chivalry in front of him, but behind his back, they called him the Blood Light Demon. This is because his blade was red, and he would cut down anyone he didn't like, whether they were good or evil. Fortunately, he doesn't indiscriminately massacre people.

If you bow down and cater to the Blood Light Demon as much as possible, you can survive. The best thing is not to be noticed by the Blood Light Demon.

He muttered as if trying to calm his frantically beating heart.

,When the Blood Light Demon came here... I thought today was my death anniversary. I could even smell incense that wasn't there. But I survived."

It felt like he had put his head in a tiger's mouth... He never wanted to experience that again.

,I need to report to the higher-ups quickly. That the Blood Light Demon is heading to Jianzhao City.'

The Blood Light Demon is moving around, destroying several sects. There are rumors that the government is also keeping an eye on the Blood Light Demon.

,Muyeong Shintu, the Great Evil Eight Evils Association, the Blood Light Demon... They're all gathering in Jianzhao City. I can smell blood. A very strong smell of blood...'

He shook his head. He didn't want to get involved in whatever was going to happen in that city. He just felt sorry for the branch leader of the Jianzhao City Hao Mun branch.

• • •

I headed towards the entrance of Jianzhao City at a leisurely pace. Befitting its name as a large city, the city's splendor could be seen from the entrance.

There were many people trying to enter Jianzhao City. Escorts, merchants, wanderers, and so on. As the city is large, people gather.

The city entrance was wide open. There were no soldiers controlling the road and conducting inspections. This is because Jianzhao City is under the jurisdiction of the Loeum Temple.

But as I got closer to the entrance, I felt a gaze. The problem was that I couldn't tell exactly where the gaze was coming from.

,That guy's pretty skilled. But if the distance gets closer, I can tell where the gaze is coming from.'

The gaze disappeared. It was clear that he was aware of me and turned his gaze away.

I was going to find that arrogant bastard and kill him...

Even for me, it was impossible to track a gaze that had already been averted.

I passed through the entrance and entered Jianzhao City.

,How should I find Muyeong Shintu? And how do I get the Loeum Temple's martial arts?'

It wasn't good to act recklessly without a plan. Muyeong Shintu could run away, and the Loeum Temple could lock its doors tightly and barricade itself.

Kwang!

Kwakwakwakwang!

Along with the explosion, one of the inns on the street was being destroyed in real time. Fragments were flying and dust was rising. The aftermath of the clash between strong energies was affecting the surroundings.

,Aaaaaaah! Step back!!"

,Masters are fighting!!"

,What are the monks of the Loeum Temple doing?!"

,Run away instead of complaining! It's only a loss for us if we die!"

People were shocked and stepped back. Some of them kept a little distance from the inn and watched with interest.

It's the battle between the Black Wind Swordsman (黑風劍俠) and Ilgwan Tuak (一貫鬪惡)! You have to see this! You'll regret it if you don't!

,They wouldn't use their skills on us commoners, would they? It's safe if we watch from a distance!"

I turned my steps and headed towards the inn where the sound of battle was coming from.

It was natural that I, as an absolute master, couldn't just pass by such a fight, and a name I knew had come up.

Black Wind Swordsman.

It was one of the nicknames of Baek Mu-han, the protagonist of the original [Fallen Demon King].

,I remember now. The Great Evil Eight Evils Association. One of the villains from the original [Fallen Demon King].'

I went inside the inn. Baek Mu-han and a martial artist wielding a spear were fighting each other, scattering blood and energy. The two people's realms were similar.

,Stop."

I gathered my energy to the fullest and released it in all directions. Startled, the two people separated from each other and looked at me.

,...Who are you? How dare you interfere in a life-or-death duel?!"

The guy wielding the spear, probably the one nicknamed Ilgwan Tuak (一貫鬪惡), glared at me with bloodshot eyes. From the look of it, having lost half of his sanity, he seemed to be deeply affected by demonic arts.

,You are...!!"

Baek Mu-han seemed to recognize me. Frankly, I didn't like the fact that both of them were holding their heads high even after seeing me.

,But I can't kill Baek Mu-han. I need to get Baek Mu-han's Cheonma Shingong later.'

I looked at Ilgwan Tuak.

,You rude bastard... If you see a senior in the Jianghu, you should bow your head first. How dare you arrogantly look at your senior with your eyes wide open? You're hopeless. Die."

Loecheonryu (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I closed the distance with the guy in an instant. The guy was flustered and widened his eyes.

Loecheonryu (雷天流) Loegwang (雷光).

I used Loegwang at the same time as drawing the Hwaryeon Bido. Red lightning flashed and cut through the guy's torso. The guy raised his spear to block my attack. However, the blood vessels in the spear-wielding guy's arms burst. He couldn't withstand my strength.

,Block? How arrogant. Try blocking one more time."

,That bamboo hat and dark clothes... and that red sword. I've heard of it. You're the Blood Light Demon who's been making a name for himself recently!"

,I'm not the Blood Light Demon, I'm the Absolute Divine Chivalry. A chivalrous person who kills evil like you."

As I pretended to swing my sword, the guy tried to block it by spinning his spear. The real attack is a kick. I kicked the guy's knee with my right foot. He was easily caught off guard because he was so wary of my sword.

,Kkeuaaaaak! My leg!!"

It was the moment I was about to finish off the guy who had lost his balance and fallen.

A throwing knife flew silently from outside the inn.

,Did he have a companion.'

I ignored it and stabbed the Hwaryeon Bido into the guy's head.

Peok!

The throwing knife was stuck in my left back. I kept ignoring it and swung my sword.

Peokpeokpeok!

A few more throwing knives were stuck in my back. I had protected my body with a protective aura, but it still pierced through. The throwing knives had considerable power, but they weren't enough to kill me.

After cutting Ilgwan Tuak into 30 pieces, I turned my body and looked outside the inn. My vision was shaking. It seemed like the throwing knives were coated with poison.

,Come out right now."

I said, pulling out the throwing knives stuck in my back one by one.
