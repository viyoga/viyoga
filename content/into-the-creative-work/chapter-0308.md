,The Tyrant Legion is a Legion residing in our district after paying a fair price. If you've done this for no reason, I won't forgive you. This is a matter of our Reeves Legion's reputation.

In other words, the Tyrant Legion was paying taxes to the Reeves Legion and residing in District 174.

And since the management of the house is also the responsibility of the landlord, they can't just back down.

If I were in the Heavenly Demon state, I would have just ignored it and run away, but I am not currently the Heavenly Demon.

,I am Seong Yujin, a member of the A-Plan Legion.

,A-Plan Legion...? Ah, that's the Legion that's been making a fuss lately. I am Ophelia, the 3rd Squad Leader of the Reeves Legion.

Ophelia.

I decide to remember that name. I don't care about the names of guys, but if it's a beautiful woman's name, the story is different.

,It'll be easier to understand if you see this rather than me explaining it.

I took a crumpled piece of paper out of my pocket and threw it to Ophelia. Ophelia cautiously accepted the paper and carefully unfolded it. Her eyes widened slightly as she checked the contents.

Emerald green eyes that remind me of emeralds. Attractive eyes. There was a slight reaction between my legs.

,This is....

The paper is, of course, a letter sent by the Tyrant Legion to the A-Plan Legion. A letter telling them to hand over the district if they don't want to die.

,This is a declaration of war sent by the Tyrant to our Legion. Yes. They literally declared war in a letter.

,...I see. This is not a problem for us to get involved in.

Since the Tyrant declared war first, there is no reason for the Reeves Legion to intervene. The Tyrant and Reeves are not exactly in an alliance, and even if they were, they would be reluctant to get involved.

,They could jump in with some kind of justification, but... thankfully, Reeves seems to be a relatively moderate Legion, judging by the fact that they're talking about reputation.

I haven't heard much about the Reeves Legion. The fact that it has no distinguishing features means that it's not a criminal Legion.

,Excuse me.

,No. I'm sorry for the commotion. Ophelia-ssi, are you always managing this place?

Ophelia nodded readily.

,It's my job to manage District 174.

Good. I got some information. Should I come back in my Heavenly Demon state and violate her?

,But what are you going to do with the corpse?

,Ah. I'm thinking of just burning it.

,Aren't you going to collect the spoils of war?

I was originally going to collect them, but when I actually tried to do it, it was incredibly annoying. And I have a schedule after this. It was better to save time.

,I'm a little busy... Ah, could you collect the spoils of war for me instead? I'll give you half.

,Half? You're giving a lot. Good.

,Then I'll leave it to you. Please bring the spoils of war to the castle in District 601.

,Okay.

I greeted them and passed by.

When they were out of sight, I took a piece of paper out of my inventory while walking between the zelkova trees. This was also a letter.

The content was more than three times longer than the letter sent by the Tyrant Legion, but the summary was similar. Hand over the district if you don't want to die.

,Those damn idiots.

Normal people would think it's amazing to see a Legion that has conquered a district that no one has been able to conquer.

But stupid people think differently.

You conquered District 601? That's because you couldn't even try to conquer it because of the entry requirements. It's a fluke. What? A Legion tried to conquer it a long time ago and was wiped out? That's because they were noobs.

That's how they think.

,They're looking down on the A-Plan Legion because it's a Legion made up of rookies, saying things like beginner's luck.

I took out another letter.

,The content is... pretty much the same. There are too many losers.

I had to go to two more Legions.

I thought it would be better to talk it out if possible. There's no big reason, I'm just tired.

But life doesn't always go the way I want it to.

A few days later, rumors spread that the A-Plan Legion was a merciless and ruthless Legion, but I didn't care.

One of the members of the Reeves Legion watched Seong Yujin's back as he left and asked Ophelia.

,Is it okay to just let him go like that? He's not ordinary. It would be better to put a tail on him....

Ophelia shook her head.

,He's a man who single-handedly annihilated the Tyrant. If we put a clumsy tail on him, things will only get more complicated. Leave him alone. There's no need to make him an enemy.

,I understand.

Her subordinates began to dispose of the corpses and collect the spoils of war. Ophelia watched her subordinates work and thought about Seong Yujin and the A-Plan Legion in her head.

A Legion that dominated a district in just two months.

It's not a big deal in the old days. There were many easy districts that hadn't been conquered back then. But there are no easy districts left now. Even if the entry requirements were the problem, figuring that out is also a skill and ability.

,A promising Legion has appeared after a long time. And the man named Seong Yujin used lightning.

Ophelia clearly saw and heard it. The huge lightning that soared from the ground to the sky and the thunder that was so loud it could burst eardrums.

Thinking of Seong Yujin, who controls lightning, she inadvertently thinks of that man.

,Anyway, it's certain that he's unusual. I need to emphasize it when I report to the Master.

I arrived at District 4, the AP Store.

District 4, as the name suggests, is a district with a store where you can use AP. The location is right next to Today's City in District 2.

You can buy special items, potions, and daily necessities with AP, and you can exchange pennies, the currency used in Atlantis. Conversely, it is impossible to exchange pennies for AP.

The exchange rate is 1:100.

1 AP is 100 pennies.

The value of a penny is about 1 million won for 100,000 pennies.

Of course, I didn't come here to exchange AP for pennies.

,There are a lot of people.

People are constantly coming and going from the store. No one blocks the store or causes trouble. This is because this district is directly managed by the system. Causing trouble here? You'll be subdued by the system in 1 second and receive a penalty.

The interesting thing about this store is that there is no need to wait in line, even though many people use it. The reason for this can be found by going directly into the store.

Ttangnang.

I opened the door of the store and went inside.

It was a completely different space.

It was a slightly dark room. There was a door leading outside on the wall, and there were chairs and a table in the center of the room.

,The store is the same for everyone who enters, but they go to a different space. In game terms, you could say it's a different channel.

I sat down in the chair in front of the table.

「Welcome to the AP Store.」

「The AP Store allows you to buy or sell items using AP. Only ranked items can be sold, and they can only be sold at 30% of the market price.」

「The AP Store can only be used for 2 hours a day.」

「What do you want?」

At first glance, the store is similar to an NPC store that always sells items in games, but in reality, the quantity of items is limited.

,My current AP is....

「AP Owned: 178,943」

It's a bit ambiguous from my perspective, but other rookie Exiles would be envious to the point of bursting.

I looked at the potions first.

「Top-Grade Recovery Potion - 11,000 AP」

「Remaining Quantity - 2,194,914」

,I thought so, but it's still expensive. It's better to get top-grade potions from the modern world.

「Lowest-Grade Recovery Potion - 200 AP」

「Remaining Quantity - 171,241,561」

These are the potions that rookies buy the most.

I don't use this kind of low-quality stuff, but rookies would be grateful.

,Let's skip the potions and look at the special items since I'm here.

「Permanent Mana Increase Potion - 100,000 AP」

「This item can only be purchased once.」

「Remaining Quantity - 0」

「There is currently no stock.」

,...Well, I expected this.

A potion that increases mana stat by 1 for a relatively cheap price of 100,000 AP.

It's a win-win because your stats increase even if you eat it yourself, and even if you don't eat it, you can sell it to someone else for 100 million pennies, which is more than 1 billion won, so everyone is aiming for it.

,The situation is the same for other stat-increasing potions.

I looked for other items.

,D-Rank or higher. Price under 100,000 AP. Skills, traits, equipment, consumables, and materials are all included.

Information about the items sold at the store appears in front of my eyes. I quickly scanned them with my eyes. I only have 2 hours, so I don't have time to seriously look and examine them.

,Oh. This is good.

What I found was a skill.

「Lightning Speed - 100,000 AP」

「This item can only be purchased once.」

「Remaining Quantity - 3」

「Lightning Speed

Temporarily transforms the body into lightning and moves. Becomes immune to physical attacks.

Type: Skill

Rank: D」

「This skill can only be used if you have a trait related to lightning or if your attribute is lightning.」

It was a skill for me.

,Physical immunity....

It can't be complete physical attack immunity. The rank of this skill is only D. Physical immunity will eventually only apply to attacks that don't contain mana. I don't know what will happen if the rank increases.

,I can slowly raise the rank. I'll buy it first!

「You have purchased Lightning Speed.」

「100,000 AP will be paid.」

「You have learned a new skill.」

,Lightning Speed!

I used the skill right away.

My body crackled and turned into lightning. I looked down at my body and shouted.

,I am God Yujin!

I shouted it once because it reminded me of a guy who ate a strange fruit, but it wasn't fun or anything.

Jijijik.

The body transformed into lightning only lasted for 3 seconds. My body returned to normal. There was no pain or anything. I didn't feel like anything had changed, except that my body felt lighter.

I tried to use Lightning Speed again, but it failed.

「This skill cannot be used consecutively.」

After that, I used the skill every 5 seconds, and I realized that it was a skill that could be used roughly every 30 seconds. And it consumes quite a bit of mana.

,My movement speed increases when I'm using this. I need to experiment more later, but it's a useful skill. To be able to get a skill like this for only 100,000 AP... I'm lucky!

I could easily guess why this good skill wasn't sold and was still in stock.

The skill condition is that you must have a trait related to lightning. Lightning is a very difficult attribute to obtain compared to other attributes.

,Then next is....

The real reason I came here.

「District 20 Teleport Scroll - 57,000 AP」

「Remaining Quantity - 13,209」

Originally, I was going to buy this and then buy a town teleport scroll with the remaining AP. I would have done that if I didn't have Lightning Speed. For reference, the AP price of a space teleport scroll is 54,611 AP.

,There's a lot of stock. Well, District 20 isn't a popular place. Because there are no monsters.

The reason I bought this is because of the suspicious request that Pesica gave me two weeks ago. A request to come to a specific place in two weeks. That specific place was District 20.

,It's a place that's a week's journey from Today's City in District 2 without resting.

I thought I had plenty of time because I had two weeks, but I also had to worry about my identity as Thunderclap Demonic Swordsman besides the Heavenly Demon. I couldn't miss the task of conquering District 601 of the A-Plan Legion because of personal reasons.

,Living two lives is so hard.

I grumbled inwardly, got up from the chair, and went outside the store.

Because I couldn't use the teleport scroll inside the store.

I put on a clown mask in a place where people couldn't see me and tore the District 20 teleport scroll.

「You have entered District 20, the Plains of Silence.」

A cold night breeze blew.

I raised my head and looked around.

A vast plain stretched out under the night sky with countless stars twinkling. The starlight was so bright that the plain was not dark and was very visible. There were large stones like dolmens here and there throughout the plain.

,...Where should I go?

The request only stated to come to District 20 by 10 PM, but it didn't state the exact location.
