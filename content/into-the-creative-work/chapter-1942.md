The being revealed herself.

The form of a beautiful woman with snow-white skin.

The black leather barely covering the vital parts of her voluptuous body was smaller than the palm of a hand. Blonde hair shining like the dazzling sun, and blood-red eyes. A seductive smile hung on her lips.

Behind her back were black bat wings, signifying a demon, and a long, slender black tail.

The tail was stained with blood. Because it was that tail that pierced and killed the imp.

I could feel cold sweat running down my back.

Somewhat embarrassingly, my legs were trembling. The very space I was standing in was being dominated by her. Frankly speaking, it felt like being placed on a monster's tongue. A helpless feeling of being swallowed and killed by the monster at any moment.

There was nothing I could do in front of her.

,Shibal. Astaroth is here...!'

Astaroth looked at me, scanned her surroundings, and then looked back at me. I was the only one who seemed to pique Astaroth's interest. The fact that she revealed herself directly was 100% because of me.

The 167 sacrifices merely bowed their heads, and the staff members fell to the floor, whimpering in pleasure. Their crotches became damp, as if they had come just from Astaroth's gaze.

On the other hand, I didn't feel any sexual desire for Astaroth. An unsurpassed beauty? It all means nothing. I know better than anyone that Astaroth's true form is a hideous monster. That appearance of Astaroth was her true form, yet not her true form. She created an incarnation and trapped her true form inside it.

If she didn't, most beings would go crazy the moment they saw her true form.

The 72 demons were more like Demon Gods than demons.

"How amusing. Very amusing."

The lives of all living things here depended on her whims. If she told them to die, they had to die. To her, we were like insects.

She's coming towards me.

Kwa jiji jiji jik!

The space screams. It seemed as if the world was desperately trying to stop her by any means. Astaroth ignored the world's resistance, crushing it with her power.

"I smell a nostalgic scent on you. A very old... scent from that time."

What is she talking about?

I gripped my trembling legs. It was difficult to open my mouth due to the pressure, but my mind was racing.

An old scent. Is that why she's interested in me?

Even in the midst of all this, I hoped the camera was working properly. Because that's how I can make money properly.

"Good. I'll take a look at your future. You're curious about your future too, aren't you?"

• • •

The Demon Realm. One of the 72 demons who rule what is otherwise known as Hell, Astaroth felt a presence calling her after a long time.

A very faint presence.

It was outrageous and unpleasant to be summoned with the offering of only 167 humans, but she also felt a strange feeling because it was a formal summoning.

But only up to that point.

There was no reason to step in directly. It wasn't worth it to step in using only 167 humans as an excuse. One of the demons who swore allegiance to her would take care of it on their own.

Then suddenly.

She smelled a scent that piqued her interest.

A very old past. The scent of the time when gods and deities frolicked.

Astaroth, her mind changed, moved directly. She dealt with the annoying imp in an instant, and forcibly suppressed the will of the world that was rejecting and pushing her away, and descended into the mortal realm.

There were many humans.

But there was no one as special as the magician who summoned her.

Astaroth smiled.

She liked special humans. Because they were fun to watch.

"Good. I'll take a look at your future. You're curious about your future too, aren't you?"

One of the powers Astaroth possessed. She had the power to see the past and future of a specific human. The future she sees is just one of countless futures, an unstable future. That's why seeing the future was more fun.

"I'm not particularly curious, though."

The magician said, sweating profusely. Astaroth chuckled. The magician didn't get turned on even after seeing her. Does he have the power to protect his mind? If not, then some special item? Either way, she became more interested in the magician.

"Your opinion doesn't matter. What matters is that I want to see your future."

Astaroth used her power.

The magician's futures appear in her eyes. As if proving that he's not ordinary, he had more than 10 times more futures than an average human. All of those futures were shining brightly. If she could, she would like to look into each and every one of them and savor them... but she wasn't that relaxed right now.

There's only one future she can see right now.

Astaroth looked into the future that was the most brilliant yet made her feel ominous.

That place was a space where stars twinkled. It wasn't Earth, it was space. However, what was puzzling was that there were many constellations she didn't know. She could see many familiar starlight, but their positions were all jumbled up. It felt as if someone had messed up the constellations.

When she turned her gaze slightly, she saw something floating in space.

It was a corpse.

All sorts of corpses were lined up. But no two corpses were alike. They were all beings that transcended rank. They were all beings similar to Astaroth.

Astaroth swallowed hard. Those were all true forms. The real bodies of beings who could easily manipulate an entire world if they wanted to.

Those people were now dead here like trash.

,What happened? Did a war break out between us? I can even see the corpses of the Void bastards.'

She was a little surprised, but that was it. This is just one of countless possible futures. A war broke out in the future over the fate of the world? She felt that it could happen. They had a bad relationship from the start anyway.

As she turned her gaze, she soon discovered something enormous.

A giant mass of flesh with 100,000 wings. That being, similar in size to the moon, was the true form of Metatron. In terms of the 72 demons, he was in a similar position to Baal, the first in the hierarchy. Baal himself could not guarantee victory even if he fought him, and that being was dead.

And the magician was standing on top of that corpse. The magician, who was completely covered in a robe, was staring intently at the universe.

The insignificant magician who summoned her in the present. The magician's face was different from what she knew. It wasn't surprising at all.

,He was hiding his appearance with magic, so that's probably his real face.'

The magician, who was quietly looking at the universe, turns his head. Exactly in the direction where Astaroth is.

"......!!"

Astaroth was startled, but quickly calmed down. This is not a confirmed future. It is a future that does not actually exist. It's just a coincidence that the magician moved his head and looked at her.

,What are those eyes?'

The magician's eyes were strange. It was as if the universe had been embedded in those small eyes, and countless starlight was shining within them. It was hard to say that it was just a flashy-looking eye, and its presence was extraordinary.

The magician opened his mouth.

"How impudent."

It seemed like he was talking to her.

,That can't be. What I'm seeing is an unconfirmed, unstable future. To use an analogy from human civilization, it's the same as a being on TV talking to you.'

It's absolutely impossible.

The magician raised his hand.

At that moment, Astaroth could understand the reason for the strange constellations. The moment the magician raised his hand, the stars moved and began to draw the shape of a magic circle. Those many stars were being used as elements of the magic circle.

,Ah, aah...!'

Astaroth realized what that being was.

A very long time ago, the goal of a forgotten being she was friendly with.

Using the origin of magic as a key, he realizes the truth of the universe and transcends everything, a true transcendence.

The forgotten being named that goal in the magic system he created.

One who has transcended everything.

Overmind.

,No way. That thing that only existed in theory... is it really possible?!'

In the brief moment she was lost in thought, the magic circle made of stars and starlight was completed. What came out of the magic circle was a streak of lightning in five colors.

Feeling the transcendental power contained in that lightning, Astaroth knew that this was an unstable future, but she closed her eyes tightly as if running away and released her power.

In front of her was a magician sweating profusely.

Astaroth smiled.

She saw something amazing. What would happen if she told this to other beings? It would all be the same. A mere mortal achieves transcendence of transcendence? Everyone would disbelieve and laugh.

"I've seen a very interesting future."

She gave a strange smile. Will the insignificant magician in front of her be able to reach that future? It would be fun just to watch. And if he does reach it... she has to suck up to him to survive. There's a stock that has the potential to skyrocket right in front of her, so is there any need to hesitate to invest?

It was when she was about to move her hand as if to seduce the magician. Astaroth's complexion hardened. She hurriedly raised her head and looked at the empty air.

Paji jiji jiji jik!

A streak of lightning in five colors tore through the air and struck Astaroth.

"N, no way...!"

The lightning arrived, piercing through space and time. It's not the same.

An unconfirmed future. An unstable future. A future that does not yet exist. 0 possibility. It appeared ignoring all of that.

"Aaaaaaack!"

Astaroth fell to the floor. She felt intense pain. She tried to use her power to shake off the lightning, but the lightning tore apart even her power and dug into her essence. Immortality is damaged. If she stays like this, she will die.

"S, save me!"

Astaroth instinctively reached out to the magician in front of her. The future magician and the magician in front of her are the same being. Then, wouldn't this magician be the only one who can save her?

The magician had a dazed expression on his face. The place where his gaze was directed was not Astaroth, but the five-colored lightning that was killing Astaroth.

Soon, the magician took Astaroth's hand. The five-colored lightning moved to the magician.
