Seong Jigone and I went outside the city together.

Bandit subjugation. Originally, Seong Jigone would have proceeded with this with his subordinates. The plan changed when I decided to help Seong Jigone. It's faster and more certain for Seong Jigone and I to take care of it ourselves than to lead troops unnecessarily.

,Those Paereung Mountain Fortress bandits don't know their place and are running wild... It seems bandits are rampant nationwide these days."

,Yeah, that's right. Other cities are also troubled by bandit problems."

,If the Emperor directly ordered it, the army would take the lead and wipe out all the bandits."

,The Emperor isn't very interested in human affairs."

,In human affairs?"

,Yeah. Remember what happened at the Tower of Myriad Tomes? I fought against a being from the Underworld. The Emperor is focused on matters related to the Underworld. The real elite troops are fighting monsters from the Underworld. He won't directly step in for mere bandit affairs."

We started getting closer to the mountain fortress. Seong Jigone and I intentionally suppressed our presence. We also lowered our voices.

,Was it called Jiyeom Mountain Fortress? There weren't any bandits around here originally. I heard they appeared about three months ago? They didn't have much presence, so I didn't even know they existed. By the time I realized it, they had become too powerful to easily deal with."

I couldn't understand Seong Jigone's words.

,They're just bandits."

,There was a clever bastard among the bandits. He offered some of the wealth he gained from banditry to the city's powerful figures. Thanks to that, I found out late. Politics isn't easy."

Seong Jigone smiled bitterly. I could sense his regret. I remembered that Seong Jigone wanted to become a wandering hero when he was young.

,Well, don't be too disappointed. If there's a clever bastard among the bandits, he must have kept a ledger. If we get our hands on the ledger, we can punish the guys who took the bribes."

Since he's smart enough to bribe the powerful, he must have kept a ledger. The ledger could become a dagger to stab the powerful figures.

,It's good if it exists. It doesn't really matter even if it doesn't. If I report it after the job is done, the higher-ups will take care of it. The Brocade-Clad Guard will probably be dispatched. More than that, Yujin-ah. Doesn't it remind you of the old days when I mention bandits?"

,It does. We fucked the bandit leader's mother and wife."

,I miss the old days sometimes. We could have fun without thinking back then."

We chuckled and approached the mountain fortress. Our conversation soon disappeared. Even when stepping on the fallen leaves on the ground, there was no sound.

A state where no footprints are left even when stepping on snow. Was it called 'treading on snow without leaving a trace'? Seong Jigone and I had already reached that state long ago.

When we arrived at the mountain fortress, we sensed something was wrong.

There was a smell of rotting corpses. And there was no sign of life in the mountain fortress. Seong Jigone and I looked at each other and rushed into the mountain fortress. I had a strong feeling that something had gone wrong.

We entered the mountain fortress. Corpses were scattered everywhere. The flesh was rotting black, and maggots and flies were devouring the corpses. Blood had soaked into the floor and wooden walls, staining them.

Seong Jigone quietly examined the corpses.

,Their abdomens have all been torn open. It could be the work of a human who has mastered demonic arts, but... it's rare for someone to do this so openly. It seems like the work of a monster."

,Did a monster eat their insides?"

,Hmm. There are traces of them being eaten one bite at a time."

,A one-bite bug?"

I shook my head. At the same time, I heightened my tension. The monster might still be hiding in the mountain fortress. Even if I don't sense any other presence, I can't be sure. It might be hiding by deceiving my senses with a special ability.

I entered the largest building in the center of the mountain fortress. It was no different. The humans inside the building were all dead with their abdomens torn open.

,There are no living bandits. What do you want to do?"

,We need to search in case the monster is nearby. We can't leave such an evil and powerful monster unattended."

Seong Jigone took out a small, shabby wooden box from his pocket. It was a space box. He opened the lid and took out something much larger than the box. It was a steel cross. I could feel a mysterious energy. It was definitely a magical artifact.

,It doesn't seem like a combat magical artifact. What kind of magical artifact is it?"

,It's a Demon Energy Device. It's a magical artifact that absorbs and tracks the faint demonic energy left at the scene. It's one of the magical artifacts supplied to the company commander level, and it's quite useful."

Is that possible?

As far as I could feel, there was no demonic energy left at the scene. How can it absorb and track it if there's no demonic energy?

As if mocking my negative thoughts, the end of the Demon Energy Device's cross turned red. It had absorbed demonic energy.

,There really was demonic energy?"

,It's a magical artifact that can discover and absorb even very faint demonic energy that's hard for people to sense."

,It's quite a useful magical artifact."

,Its effect is excellent, but it can't be used for long. You have to replace it with a new one after using it a few times. And it doesn't activate if the monster is too far away."

The Demon Energy Device pointed in one direction and led us. Seong Jigone and I started running.

The place we arrived at was a dilapidated house at the end of the mountain. It was a two-story house, but it wasn't easily noticeable because it was hidden by nearby trees.

Kkiiiiiiik.

Before we could even enter the house, the door opened and an old woman appeared. She was an old woman holding a cane in one hand. Her half-white hair was disheveled, and her eyes were shining gold. The Demon Energy Device was pointing at the old woman.

,What brings you two to this humble place?"

The old woman spoke politely. Judging from the demonic energy I felt, she was definitely a monster, but I didn't feel any hostility or disregard towards humans.

The power I felt from the monster was about Stage 3 of Demonic Energy. We would overwhelmingly win if we fought.

,...Beautiful."

Seong Jigone muttered in a small voice. I pretended not to hear him.

Ignoring Seong Jigone, who was charmed by the monster, I asked.

,Did you kill the bandits and eat their insides?"

,Ah, you mean those foolish bastards. Yes, I killed them and ate some of their insides."

,You're confident even after killing humans."

,They're humans, but they're bandits. Criminals. Judging from the fact that you're holding a Demon Energy Device, you seem to be from the Qin army... I swear I've never touched any humans other than bandits. Do you think I'm crazy enough to touch the innocent people of His Majesty the Emperor?"

Even the monster feared the Emperor. I could feel the Emperor's prestige even in a place like this, and I let out a hollow laugh.

,You're different from the monsters I know. If you were afraid of the army, you wouldn't stay here and would have left far away."

,Why should I abandon my home and run away when I've done nothing wrong?"

,Most monsters aren't interested in human society. Bandits and farmers are all the same to them. The measure by which monsters distinguish humans is strength."

,That is indeed true. But I am a monster, yet not a monster, and a human, yet not a human."

,...A half-monster?"

A hybrid of monster and human.

An existence rejected by both monsters and humans.

,Among monsters, there are those who feel a desire for humans when they see them. They are commonly called ghouls. Half of the blood flowing through my body is that of a ghoul. Ghouls become healthier and stronger when they eat humans. Conversely, they become weaker if they don't eat humans. I ate humans to survive. But I swear I only ate humans who deserved to die. Please forgive me."

I stared at her indifferently. I didn't care whether she ate humans or monsters. It would be much more beneficial to kill the half-monster in front of me and finish the job. The reason I'm not stepping forward right away is because of Seong Jigone.

This area belongs to Seong Jigone. As such, I respect him.

,If you spare me, I'll give you some information. It's information I accidentally learned while killing the bandits. It's information I don't need... but it might be necessary for you -nim."

A deal?

I turned my head to the side and looked at Seong Jigone. He was looking at the half-monster with sparkling eyes. Seong Jigone opened his mouth.

,Half-monster. What is your name?"

,...I am called Bakdal."

,Bakdal-ah, I have heard your opinion well. But even if they are bandits, you have no authority to punish them."

,...Are you going to kill me?"

,You are a half-monster. Half means human. You are also a subject of His Majesty the Emperor. You killed heinous bandits and upheld chivalry and justice... How could I punish you?"

,..."

Bakdal was silent. It was clear that she didn't understand Seong Jigone's words. I didn't either. Regardless, Seong Jigone continued to spout his words.

,Unfortunately, half of you is a monster. As the general of Hayang City, I have a duty to kill monsters and protect the people.... It's truly a regrettable thing."

,...I don't want to die."

,What living thing wants to die? But I can't just let it go. So I came up with a brilliant idea."

,Yes. I'm listening."

,Bakdal-ah, become my woman. Become my woman and prove your humanity!"

,..."

,..."

Not only me but even Bakdal opened her mouth. Why did the story end like that? I glanced at Seong Jigone. His face was endlessly serious.

,You intend to mock me."

Bakdal's eyes sharpened. Demonic energy flowed from her body. Anger could be felt on her distorted face.

,I'd rather fight and die than be mocked."

It was natural to think of it as mockery. Bakdal's appearance was that of an old woman in her 70s at the youngest. It was definitely not an appearance that a man would feel attracted to. Except for one person, Seong Jigone.

,Oh dear... It's a pity that my sincerity wasn't conveyed. There's no choice, Yujin-ah. Help me. I have to subdue Bakdal and take her by force, even if it's against her will. I'll definitely regret it for the rest of my life if I miss such a beauty."

Seong Jigone asked me for help with a desperate look.

,Uh, okay."

I decided to cooperate since I had received quite a bit from Seong Jigone.

Seong Jigone drew his sword from his waist and ran towards Bakdal.

,Let's see your jjampji (cunt)!!"

Is he really crazy?
