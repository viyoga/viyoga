In this world, a dragon's heart, yongsim, is a treasure that only special people can obtain.

However, even among these yongsim, there are troublesome ones.

That would be the heart of a Poison Dragon (dokryong).

A Poison Dragon's heart contains poison condensed along with magical power. The yongsim of a Lesser Dragon-level Poison Dragon can be managed somehow. But what about the heart of a Saint Dragon-level or higher Poison Dragon? Humans can barely even touch it. The moment they touch it, they get poisoned and die. The same goes for Dragon Hunters, unless they are hunters specialized in poison.

So, until now, most Poison Dragon hearts were usually discarded.

,One of the association's designated no-entry zones was a place to discard Poison Dragon hearts.'

In reality, it's like a Chernobyl soaked in radiation, you could say. Of course, the forbidden zone in this world was more toxic. Even dragons of the same poison lineage can't enter here.

I, with the [Poison Resistance] trait, am an exception. Of course, I'm not completely immune to poison, so I struggled a bit, but I succeeded in recovering the discarded Poison Dragon hearts. And with Hytera's help, I also succeeded in turning them into one poison.

"Hytera."

"Yes. Starting the isolation."

Hytera uses her ability towards the sea.

No matter how skilled she is, it's impossible to forcibly teleport Calodon, the Dragon King-level dragon, the Deep Sea Sky Dragon (Simhae Cheonryong), who is in the sea. A Dragon King-level dragon has extraordinary magic resistance. But it's a different story if she sets up a spatial barrier to isolate a part of the sea.

I confirmed through Cheonan that an invisible wall of space was being erected. The only way to escape that isolated space was to soar into the sky. That's why I left the upper part open.

Calodon also seemed to have noticed and began to be wary. But he doesn't move. He knows that coming out of the sea is quite disadvantageous to him.

I took out a bottle from my inventory. A jet-black poison was condensed inside a magic-infused glass bottle. Of course, that alone couldn't block all the poison.

[Absorbed poison. Resisting poison.]

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

[Absorbed poison. Resisting poison.]

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

My physical condition is repeatedly getting better and worse. Overall, it was on the bad side. The toxicity is stronger than the vitality recovery.

,I could raise my poison resistance level further, but why bother? It's not like I'm going to keep using this poison.'

I opened the stopper of the glass bottle and dropped it into the sea. It was a bit of a waste of a magic-infused glass bottle, but....

,The poison even affected the magic. I can't use that.'

I can only use that as a disposable item.

Because I have an inventory, I was able to quickly let go of my attachment. If I didn't have an inventory in the first place, it would have been difficult to bring that poison here.

Plop!

As soon as the poison was released, the isolated sea began to turn black at a rapid pace. Fish died instantly in the black sea, and the poison gradually went down.

,Even if I can't poison the entire sea, I can more than do it to this isolated sea.'

How long can Calodon endure in the sea poisoned with extreme poison?

Even if a dragon's body is the pinnacle of living things, it won't last 5 minutes.

"Kuaaaaaaaaack!"

Calodon let out a scream-like roar from under that sea. He soared upwards, revealing his body.

A blue, elongated body. Dragons that live in rivers or seas in this world resembled Eastern dragons. Calodon was the same. Horns that looked similar to those of a deer on his head, thick and long whiskers and eyebrows, and a blue body. Calodon's overall appearance was similar to the Azure Dragon, one of the Four Gods.

"Calodon, you've finally come up."

-These damn bastards dare to...! Pour poison into the sea that even I can't control? Are you trying to wipe out the sea creatures?!

"That's an exaggeration. No matter how poisonous it is, it can't poison the entire sea."

-Do you know how many lives will die until that poison is naturally purified by the power of the sea? The sea is home to more creatures than the land! You guys tried to destroy that sea!!

"That's why I isolated it. And it's because of you that the sea is in that state. Since you were holed up in the sea like a coward, I had no choice but to use this method."

-This crazy bastard...!! Terrarisk! Why the hell are you with that crazy bastard?!

"I'm not Terrarisk, I'm Hytera. And even if the sea creatures become extinct... um. I don't think I particularly care."

Although Hytera became human, some of the dragon's temperament and way of thinking remained. Since her essence is a dragon, it was only natural.

I smiled as I watched Calodon's reaction. Calodon was more obsessed with the sea than I expected. Then, of course, I should use that part.

"Hytera. Release the isolation."

"...The poison will spread to the entire sea, though."

"I don't care."

"Understood."

-Stop it!!!!

We didn't even pretend to listen to Calodon's will.

Hytera released the spatial isolation. The poison was about to spread to the entire sea but stopped.

Calodon used his power. His ability is water manipulation. Just as seawater is water, so was the poisoned seawater.

"Hehe. Calodon. You're greedy. Do you think you can protect the sea and beat us?"

I summon Hwaryeon Bido and grip the hilt.

,Domain Release.'

Starlight burst out from the sword god. I grab the domain that was scattering in all directions and trying to dominate the space with my will and wrap it around my body.

Kiiiiiiing.

A red halo forms behind my back with lightning.

Lightning Heaven Style Ultimate Technique: Lightning Red Wheel.

"Calodon. Let's have some fun."

I flew towards Calodon, stepping on the air. Intense energy soared from the blade of Hwaryeon Bido, forming a huge blade. Large enough to cut even a large dragon. Of course, since the power of the domain is imbued in the blade, Calodon cannot ignore my attack.

-You bastard...!!

Calodon's mouth opened. Magical power moved violently. The air above the sea vibrated from the aftermath. Calodon's magical power became breath, and the breath soon became water.

A huge stream of water spewed out at me.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Pajijijijijik!

I avoid the attack by turning more than half of my body into lightning. He turns his head and chases me, but he couldn't be fast enough to chase me, who was using the Flying Thunder God with all my might.

Eventually, Calodon stops breathing. I immediately closed the distance with Calodon. Calodon dodged. He moved using magic since he didn't have wings. He was trying to get into the sea. But I'm not the only one here.

"That won't do."

A spatial barrier appeared with Hytera's firm voice, preventing Calodon from entering the sea. No matter how strong Calodon was, he couldn't shatter the spatial barrier imbued with Hytera's power in an instant.

-You bastards!!

In the gap where Calodon hesitated, I approached him and swung my sword. Calodon's body was pulled sideways by an invisible attractive force.

Chwaaak!

I managed to cut Calodon's body, but I didn't inflict a fatal wound.

,He guided his body with magic. He moves his body in a way that's somewhat similar to the Flying Thunder God.'

Still, this side is overwhelmingly advantageous.

First, I gesture towards Calodon, who is trying to create distance. A blue thunderbolt falls from the cloudless, dry sky. Calodon couldn't avoid it. The thunderbolt was too fast, and Calodon's body was too big.

-Kkeueueueueueueuk!

The power is too weak to cut off his breath with lightning. I ran towards him again.

The same situation was repeated several times. Hytera blocks Calodon from entering the sea, and I approach and attack. Calodon twisted his whole body to avoid fatal injuries.

-If I could just get into the sea, you guys would be nothing!

"You talk a lot for someone who can't get into that sea."

-...I've got a good idea. If I can't get into the sea, I just have to bring the sea!!

A large amount of seawater soared into the sky as if reversing time.

"Yujin...!!"

Hytera hurriedly began to manipulate space, but she couldn't isolate the vast space at once.

Eventually, a small seawater was created in the air, and I faced Calodon inside it. Calodon twisted his lips and smiled. He was sure of his victory.

-You look like you're having a hard time breathing!

,I'm breathing just fine.'

I could breathe underwater with the [Blessing of Water] skill. I can also manipulate water, but... it was very crude compared to Calodon's ability.

-Just get crushed and die.

The water pressure gradually increased. I frowned and endured. It was difficult to move because the water was holding my whole body.

,And that bastard....'

[Absorbed poison. Resisting poison.]

[Absorbed poison. Vitality and mana are restored.]

,He's even using the poison I poured into the sea.'

I, who am in the sea, have become the enemy of the world called the sea.

,But this isn't the real sea, it's the sky. I don't think I'll lose.'

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts 1 minute.]

The seawater can't hold me anymore. Now, at best, it's just putting pressure on my body.

-No?! What did you just do?!

Kiiiiiiing.

The Lightning Red Wheel behind my back rotates violently. The pressure that was pressing down on my body also weakened.

I held Hwaryeon Bido with both hands and swung it at him. At the same time, I released the Lightning Red Wheel. The remaining domain instantly dominates the space in the seawater, suppressing Calodon's body.

-Is this also your power? Overwhelming my power in the sea?! No way...!!

Lightning Heaven Style (Lei Tian Style) Lightning Gale Blade (Lei Gang Blade).

The red lightning blade split Calodon's body. The sea that was floating in the sky lost its power and poured back into the sea.

I put Calodon's corpse in my inventory before it fell into the sea and looked at Hytera.

"It's over. Let's go back."

"Are you going to leave the poisoned sea like this?"

"The sea will purify itself."

"It will take at least several decades, and many species will become extinct in the process."

"New sea creatures will emerge."

"Um. That's true too."

We left the poisoned sea and went back.

People in this world won't be able to eat seafood for the next few decades, but it wasn't my business.
