Entering the 190th floor, I tensed up.

'It's not here.'

There were no special agents. No, they weren't visible. The special agent's mission was to guard the main system room. A highly trained special agent wouldn't abandon their mission and leave their post without permission.

'...Shit. Being invisible is more annoying than I thought.'

I thought about the Illusiter again.

The Illusiter only hides the appearance. It cannot hide mass, sound, or body temperature. If there is a thermal imaging camera, it will be caught without fail. Also, if the Illusiter moves roughly, the space around them seems to flicker.

'I prepared thermal imaging glasses in my inventory just in case.'

But I didn't use them. Because I was confident.

When I asked Jin Se-yeong-ssi in reality how to deal with an invisible enemy a while ago. She said that she could deal with it if she could feel it, even if she couldn't see it.

'And she deliberately wore an eye patch and sparred with me.'

The result was my defeat. I was definitely wearing an eye patch, but Jin Se-yeong-ssi was perfectly aware of my movements.

'Should I take out the thermal detection glasses now? ...No. I can do it.'

I glared and concentrated. I didn't bother closing my eyes.

'If they shoot, it will make a sound, so they will approach slowly and swing a knife to deal with me for sure.'

I picture the bastard in my head. Since there is an emergency staircase behind me, they will approach silently from the front.

'Mass doesn't disappear. It's unlikely to be a full-body cyborg, so they'll be breathing.'

I concentrated and concentrated.

And as a result. I felt something. It's on the right side of my vision, centered on me. It's getting closer and closer to me.

It's a presence.

The closer it gets to me, the clearer the form of its presence becomes.

'They're holding a knife in their right hand in reverse grip.'

I can feel it getting closer and closer.

I finally realized belatedly. This is the sense of touch that Jin Se-yeong-ssi was talking about.

'I thought the sense of touch used mana like a bat's ultrasound... Was it this kind of concept?'

It's hard to explain in words. You just have to feel it.

'The range I can feel is... about 1M? That's a little disappointing.'

I moved before they did.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes.]

Yeongcheonryu Practical Technique: Dawn.

At the same time as using the Acceleration skill, I used the fastest practical technique I could use.

My sword becomes a ray of light and cuts through their body.

They reacted, but my blade was faster than their movements. From the right shoulder to the side. I cut through their heart in their chest for sure. Even if their body is a machine, it is impossible to have the strength to block a plasma blade.

Their body collapsed with a thud. I could see their lower body, but not their upper body. This is because the Illusiter is still activated even after they become a corpse.

'...They're amazing. They didn't even scream, let alone leave a last word, even as they were dying.'

Only recognized special agents could use the Illusiter. The special agent in front of me was more than worthy of being called an elite.

'If they had fought me head-on, I don't know what would have happened.'

I squatted down on the floor. I groped for the invisible special agent's upper body and removed the Illusiter wrapped around their left arm. Then the special agent's corpse was revealed. They died with their eyes open and their mouth tightly shut.

'I got it. The Illusiter!'

The Illusiter looked like an ordinary black string. Letters were floating on the surface of the Illusiter.

&gt;Concealing. Remaining concealment time: 47 minutes 52 seconds.

When I grabbed both sides of the Illusiter and pulled, it stretched out like a rubber band.

'It is said that it can be extended up to 70cm when installed.'

When I let go of one hand, it slowly began to shrink. I wrapped the Illusiter around my left arm like the special agent did. My body instantly became transparent. When I grabbed the sword that I had briefly placed on the floor, the sword also became transparent.

'I can't even see my own body.'

It was something I already knew. And considering the effect of the Illusiter, I can handle it. The problem is that I have to calculate the activation time of the Illusiter well.

'Okay. I'll get two more Illusiters and go back to reality right away!'

I was about to head straight to the emergency staircase, but I stopped and looked at the main system room. If I go in there and kill all the technicians, won't the confusion last a little longer?

I swung my sword at the door of the main system room.

Kang!

The sword bounced off. The door was scratched, but I couldn't cut it. As expected of the city hall's main system room. It is protected by a door made of a material that cannot be cut even with a plasma blade.

'...I can't do it.'

The walls must be made of the same material. I shook off my regrets and ran towards the emergency staircase.

Flickering, flickering. I could see the space rippling as I moved roughly.

'This isn't good.'

When I slowed down my movement speed a little, the flickering decreased.

'...I wonder what it would be like to use Gieunbo.'

Then, surprisingly, most of the flickering disappeared. Even though I was moving at almost running speed.

'Illusiter. This has such good compatibility with Yeongcheonryu.'

Heehik, heehik.

I kept laughing. I tried to suppress my emotions and went down the stairs. I ran into the special police force on the way, but they didn't see me. I passed them. There was no need to waste my energy killing them.

151st floor. I put on thermal imaging glasses before entering the party hall. I thought it would be better to go comfortably after all.

150th floor.

I didn't go straight into the party hall, but secretly peeked inside through the crack in the door.

The party hall was a mess. Tables and food were on the floor. People were gathered in one place and using their Smart watches to light up the area.

'...There are 3 special agents. This won't work.'

Three special agents who revealed themselves are guarding Seoul Mayor Won Cheon-su-ssi.

'They probably have thermal detection eye implants. The moment I try to approach secretly, they will shoot me.'

I turned around. The party hall was not as chaotic as I thought. And since Son Jeong-hu-ssi wasn't here, it seemed like Son Jeong-hu-ssi had already achieved his goal.

'I'm giving up on 3 Illusiters.'

The conditions aren't right. I didn't think I could handle them alone.

'There's a jewelry store on the 36th floor. Should I stop by for a moment?'

I was tempted for a moment, but decided to just pass by. My goal is the Illusiter. I needed to get one more for backup.

I was going down the stairs on the 60th floor. I saw Yu Han-su-ssi lying face down on the floor, passed out.

'It looks like he fought with Son Jeong-hu-ssi as in the original webtoon.'

Yu Han-su-ssi and Son Jeong-hu-ssi will clash several times after this. It's a story that has nothing to do with me.

My goal was the 1st floor.

There is special agent Park Sin-han-ssi there.

I found out while hacking the main system. The special police force was a fake identity. Park Sin-han-ssi's real identity is a special agent.

When I arrived on the 1st floor. I frowned. All the special police forces were dead.

Just in case, I looked around and saw two people standing there nonchalantly in front of the main entrance. It's Hyeon-ji-yang and Yu Geun-tae-ssi. Park Sin-han-ssi's corpse was at their feet.

I frowned, quietly released the Illusiter, put it in my inventory along with the thermal imaging glasses. And pretending to be calm, I approached them.

"What are you doing there? It looks like you've secured an escape route, why aren't you running away?"

When I spoke naturally, Yu Geun-tae-ssi turned to me and smiled. And he held out the Illusiter in his hand as if to show it off.

"Taker! Look! This is an Illusiter! I thought this guy was just an ordinary special police force, but it turns out he was a special agent! I totally hit the jackpot today!"

"Ahjussi. The Illusiter is mine!"

"No. Hyeon-ji-yang! We worked together to catch him, right? Let's just sell this on the black market! It will sell for several trillion! You know there are restrictions on the Illusiter too!"

"...Hmm. Well. These days, every dog and cow has thermal imaging eyes. It might not be bad to just get money."

They were noisy.

When I looked down, Park Sin-han-ssi's corpse was dismembered.

"Is it a real Illusiter? Let me see it!"

I strode towards them.

"Okay. Okay. Just take a look. It's a precious item... Huh?"

I swung my sword. I cut off Hyeon-ji-yang's neck in an instant. Hyeon-ji-yang's head fell to the floor and her body collapsed.

"Ta, Taker?! You crazy bastard!"

Yu Geun-tae-ssi, who quickly grasped the situation, moved away from me and held out his hands. But I cut off his mechanical arms first, dug into his arms, and stabbed his heart.

"Keuheup! Be, betrayal!"

"My original goal was the Illusiter. Goodbye, Ahjussi."

I slashed the sword downwards. Blood and guts flowed out. I put the second Illusiter in my inventory.

Things were going smoothly as I had planned.

"Okay! Goal achieved! Hahahahaha!"

I felt great. I spread my arms and ran out of the entrance.

"Keuhahahahaha!"

I laughed loudly like a madman.

Now that I've become an invisible man, I can do all sorts of plays. I'm a little scared to use it in reality where there are many monster-like Hunters, but... the story changes in other games, including 'Vampire Prosecutor'.

"First of all, it's the women's bathhouse! I'll see the secrets of the women's bathhouse with my own eyes...."

Taang!

A bullet that flew from behind pierced my heart.

My body falls down. I was speechless at the excruciating pain I felt in my chest.

I'm dying. With that thought, anger surged.

Who is it? Who shot me?

I desperately turn my head back.

The culprit was Ulla. She sniped me with a rifle from the 30th floor window. The sniper with the elegant face moved away from the window with a ruthless expression.

'Shit! I'm so pissed! Complete recovery!'

My pierced heart and chest are instantly healed. The pain I felt in my chest disappeared like a lie.

Ulla's mistake was not aiming for my head. If she had destroyed my brain, I would have died instantly without being able to use Complete Recovery.

'That bitch. She must have aimed for my heart because she knew I was a normal person, not a cyborg. That way, I can die regretting my betrayal.'

Ulla was a cruel bitch even in the original story. Even though she can do headshots, she deliberately aims for the heart if the opponent is normal. She mumbles about a stay of execution with fancy words, but in my opinion, she's just a chuuni psycho.

'I'll kill you! You bitch! You're aiming for the back of my head?!'

I was a selfish bastard who applied double standards. It's okay for me to stab someone in the back, but it's not okay to be stabbed in the back at all.

I grabbed my sword and ran back into Seoul City Hall.

I was furious.

'Wait! Thinking about it, shit! Son Jeong-hu-ssi must have told Ulla to monitor me!'

It's certain when I think about him telling me to take Ulla-ssi to dinner.

And the reason I was sniped and killed by Ulla-ssi was not because I betrayed them.

Ulla-ssi was on the 30th floor. In other words, she wouldn't have seen my betrayal on the 1st floor.

Ulla-ssi killed me because she wanted to kill me.

Baddedeuk.

I gritted my teeth so hard that blood came out of my gums, and I went up, then changed direction and went down to the basement.

Basement 22nd floor.

I head to the 23rd basement floor, which does not exist in the blueprints, through a secret underground passage hidden in the bathroom there.

Originally, this place appears in the very last part of the original story.

The last boss in the original story is not Son Jeong-hu-ssi, but Seoul Mayor Won Cheon-su-ssi. He has the ambition to bring Korea completely under his control.

A huge door blocked me as I moved forward without hesitation. It is made of the same material as the door in the main system.

'This door is designed to be opened manually in case of hacking.'

I know how to open the door because I know the original story.

I press the button on the lower right side of the iron door. There was a clanging sound. Then, I press the hidden button about 3M away on the left.

After pressing the 4 hidden buttons in order like this, I pushed the door with all my might.

The door opened with a creaking sound.

I ran through the passage and arrived at my destination.

"It's so big!"

There was a white sphere over 10M in front of me. The sphere was emitting a red light.

'Eclaus Nuclear Fusion Reactor. The starting point of the Heart of Project Korea.'

Mayor Won Cheon-su-ssi has the ambition to control Korea's energy with the Eclaus nuclear fusion reactor. Energy is more important than anything else in this world, and the Eclaus nuclear fusion reactor is an absurd thing that can supply energy to the entire Korea and still have energy left over.

At this point in time, 'Project: Heart of Korea' is a secret that no one knows except Mayor Won Cheon-su-ssi.

'It comes out in the original story! It's very vulnerable to shock because it's not yet complete!'

What would happen if I gave it a shock here?

The answer also came out in the original story.

The Eclaus nuclear fusion reactor explodes. It doesn't just explode, it has such an enormous explosive power that it will turn half of Korea, including Seoul, into dust.

I walked up to the nuclear fusion reactor and raised my sword high.

"Let's all die together! You fucking bastards!!!"

I couldn't see anything right now.

This is the most effective way to kill Son Jeong-hu-ssi and Ulla-ssi at the same time. No, this was the only way I could think of because I was out of my mind.

"Game over!"

I shouted and swung my sword. With this, I will safely return to reality....

...Huh?

A light containing enormous heat covered me.
