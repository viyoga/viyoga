Kim Jaeji, the Vice President of Joseon Korea, was aware of his situation.

As time passed, the people of North Korea would be assimilated into South Korea. South Korea's technological prowess, cultural influence, and affluence were world-class; how could the North Korean people resist? Rather, they would actively seek assimilation.

Would those North Korean people, assimilated into South Korea, support him? Kim Jaeji shook his head.

,The time I have left, even if I'm generous, is a year. At worst, I'll disappear within half a year.'

Fortunately, the North Korean military was still in his hands.

If he didn't want to relinquish power, he had to make a quick decision. Even with a decision, it wouldn't be easy.

Looking back, he wondered if unification was a mistake, but when he looked in the mirror and saw the youth and health he had gained, his regrets vanished.

,All I have to do is get Seong Yujin. The hero of Joseon Korea? The world's greatest Awakened? He's just a human. I'll lock him up, coax him, or brainwash him to use his abilities for me forever. I will become the ruler of the world.'

Kim Jaeji, once the supreme leader of North Korea, couldn't be satisfied with the title of Vice President. He knew that the people of South Korea didn't look upon him favorably. As things stood, a terrible end was inevitable.

The only reason he proceeded with unification, knowing all this, was because he was confident he could become the supreme leader of Joseon Korea.

Kim Jaeji met with a four-term National Assembly member from Joseon Korea.

Park Hanmin.

The head of the Conservative Party.

Now, he was nothing more than a scarecrow. The young Seong Yujin, who had suddenly appeared, became President, and Seong Yujin kept the National Assembly members in check as if telling them to drop dead.

Even if the National Assembly tried to do something, the situation made it impossible. The zombies and mutants threatening Korea were a very good justification for dictatorship. If necessary, they could declare martial law without anyone being able to refute it. It was true that the Republic of Korea was in crisis.

Even Seong Yujin's approval rating as President was not decreasing but steadily increasing. He had taken all the credit for unification. His current approval rating was at least 80% or higher.

"What business do you have calling me?"

Park Hanmin asked in a blunt tone.

Kim Jaeji felt a surge of anger. He didn't like the sight of a mere National Assembly member holding his head high in front of him. At the same time, he felt pathetic about his own situation, being looked down upon by this bastard.

"The National Assembly is getting weaker day by day. How long are you going to live like that?"

"Wouldn't you know better, being the former leader of North Korea? How does it feel to be second-in-command? Well, congratulations on getting younger. Was that youth worth giving up power?"

Kim Jaeji chuckled.

"It was. My arms and legs, which used to ache even when I was still, are now perfectly fine. And this thing stands tall every morning, which is quite troublesome. Can you see this hair? It's thick and stiff. But the best thing is that there's no old man smell when I lift the blanket. I don't even remember what it feels like to be old anymore. How about you?"

"......"

Park Hanmin's expression stiffened.

Everyone who needed to know knew that Seong Yujin's ability could rejuvenate people. Seong Yujin didn't bestow rejuvenation on just anyone. He only bestowed it on his close aides. Park Hanmin, who could be considered a political opponent, had never received Seong Yujin's healing ability, not even when he had a cold.

"Help me. We can all enjoy power and youth. All of it."

Park Hanmin swallowed hard.

This was dangerous.

He knew that Seong Yujin had the strongest power of any president in history. Seong Yujin had even more power than the dictator president who was shot and killed in the past.

But he coveted power and youth.

Some of his fellow National Assembly members who had sided with Seong Yujin wore their National Assembly badges as young men in their early to mid-twenties. How envious he was when he saw them. He even felt the urge to grab Seong Yujin's pant leg and beg.

"What's the plan? Tell me that first."

"Are you going to help? Decide for sure here."

"...I'll help."

Kim Jaeji smiled, having heard the answer he wanted.

"I have a unit that follows only me. A special forces unit made up of only the elite of the elite. A unit that only I know about. I will have them kidnap Seong Yujin."

"Seong Yujin? Seong Yujin is said to be the strongest among the Awakened. Haven't you seen the video of him fighting the mutants?"

"That propaganda video? The editing was very clever. It was like watching a foreign movie. Have you ever seen Seong Yujin fight in person, Assemblyman Park?"

"......"

He hadn't. Park Hanmin hadn't even gone near a place where there were mutants.

Listening to Kim Jaeji, it seemed like the video had been cleverly edited. Even if Seong Yujin was strong, there were limits to what an individual could do.

"What will you do after you kidnap him? Will you threaten him? That's a coup. A coup without justification. The people won't accept it, and neither will the military. There are quite a few generals who follow him."

Seong Yujin had taken control of the military with his ability. There were no old men among the generals. The generals who weren't young were politically isolated and left the military.

"I will set up a double."

"A double? Are you kidding me right now? It'll be obvious even if you look closely. You're calling that a plan?"

"Listen. One of my close aides has the ability to transform. With just a strand of the target's hair, he can transform perfectly. Even the genetic information changes."

"...Don't tell me the ability changes too."

"Unfortunately, no. Only the target's information and genetic information change. We can't get the target's memories or abilities."

"Hmm. If he can transform perfectly, even just the appearance...."

"The parts that are lacking can be filled by me and the National Assembly. We'll take Seong Yujin's power little by little. Seong Yujin's ability? It's already ours. What can that bastard do when he's in our hands? He will follow our orders."

Park Hanmin slowly nodded, his eyes fixed on the power and youth shimmering before him.

All he had to do was succeed. And in Park Hanmin's eyes, Kim Jaeji's plan seemed quite plausible. It wasn't like they were overturning the entire Blue House; all they had to do was kidnap Seong Yujin.

"When is the execution date?"

"You bring me the information. I don't know the President's schedule."

"Aren't you the Vice President?"

"A Vice President is just a Vice President. It's just an empty title. The South Koreans are very wary of me."

It would be strange if someone who had been an enemy for decades was trusted in an instant. Park Hanmin nodded.

Kim Jaeji sneered inwardly as he looked at Park Hanmin. He planned to pin the blame on Park Hanmin if things went wrong. But if things went so wrong that they couldn't be salvaged....

,It's a coup. I have nuclear weapons. If I fire one nuclear weapon each at Gyeongsang Province and Jeolla Province, the South Korean bastards will have no choice but to surrender.'

What Kim Jaeji truly believed in, then and now, was nuclear weapons.

Irina, the commander of the 3rd Knights Order, Silence Moon, moved quietly alone.

Silence Moon, as a Knights Order specializing in search and infiltration, assassination of key figures, and covert operations, wore combat suits designed for covert action.

Irina quietly moved through a mountain in Hamgyeong Province. With her stealth abilities, she was very quiet. Even wild animals she happened to encounter didn't notice her presence, even when she was right in front of them.

Irina continued to move deeper into the mountain. Before long, she found her target.

Roachists.

Disgusting-looking insects that looked like a mix of cockroaches and grasshoppers. The human-sized ones clung to trees or crawled into the ground, avoiding the sunlight and resting. Next to the insects were human clothes soaked in blood.

There were about 30 Roachists.

At the level of the North Korean military, it would take a heavily armed infantry battalion to deal with them.

-Irina. Carry out the mission.

The voice of the combat AI, Zena, was heard inside the helmet. Irina, who had been spacing out for a moment, took out a tumbler from her waist with trembling hands. When she pressed the button on the special tumbler, the opening opened.

The insects all raised their heads and looked at Irina. Black eyes, long antennae moving from side to side, twitching sharp mouths.

,Eugh....'

Irina shuddered at the instinctive disgust that welled up inside her.

-It's coming. I will guide you. Run.

Thirty Roachists charged.

They were faster because they had more legs than humans. But Irina's combat suit was a model specialized for speed. She could run faster than the Roachists.

Irina continued to run, following the direction Zena guided her.

"Zena. I'm starting to get winded... How long do I have to run?"

-You will arrive at your destination soon. If it's too hard, I will administer Bister II.

"It's not to the point where I need to administer drugs."

After running for 30 minutes, she finally arrived at the North Korean military base. Now, all she had to do was throw the tumbler containing the hormone substance that would lure the Roachists into it.

Irina hesitated slightly before finishing the mission. She knew the tragedy that would unfold as a result.

-Irina. This is a top-secret operation that Master has given you.

"...I know."

Irina gripped the tumbler tightly and threw it towards the military base. Because the Awakened threw it with all their might, it flew very far at an incredible speed.

The Roachists were lured by the hormones and crossed the walls of the military base.

"Bugs!! It's a bug attack!"

Gunshots rang out along with the sound of sirens.

"Aaaaaaah!"

But what followed was screams.

Unlike the South Korean military, the North Korean military had not yet been provided with the latest equipment and drugs.

Fighting the Roachist horde with the North Korean military's outdated equipment was suicide. In less than 10 minutes, more than 200 North Korean soldiers were torn to pieces and killed.

-Operation complete. Good work. The next operation is waiting. I will guide you to the location. Do you need any psychiatric counseling?

"No. I'm not weak enough to break down over something like this. I'm already used to it."

It wasn't just Irina who was carrying out this mission. Her subordinates were also carrying out similar missions in various locations.

Today alone, at least 7,000 North Korean soldiers would die. They would die the next day, and the day after that.

-The next operation is to induce chaos using the Roachists and eliminate Major General Baek Hanji. I recommend sniping.

"A gun? Won't I get caught if I kill him with a gun?"

-It's okay. The traitors within the North Korean military are already prepared. They will soon start a civil war within the North Korean military.

"You prepared traitors? How?"

-They were promised.

"......"

She didn't ask what they were promised. She could guess.

After that, Irina focused on her mission. She didn't want to disappoint Seong Yujin.
