Stopping at a rest stop along the way, it took about 1 hour and 30 minutes to reach our destination.

At the Dungeon entrance, Heroes belonging to the Hero Association had set up tents and were guarding the entrance. Even though they were Heroes, most of them were closer to Staff, being E- to F-rank Heroes.

“You’ve come again today.”

They bowed their Heads to me in greeting. They looked at Clara next to me but said nothing. They were tight-lipped Staff.

“Here’s the permit.”

“Yes. Confirmed.”

When I showed them the permit I had obtained by asking Seong Hari, they readily opened the entrance.

Clara and I entered the Dungeon Gate.

The scenery changed in an instant.

A peaceful forest in broad daylight, not night. The sound of leaves rustling in the wind, the singing of birds somewhere, and the gurgling of a stream could be heard. And before us stood a building as massive as a mountain.

Before my eyes was a colossal stone building, like a mountain.

『You have entered the Special Dungeon, Hall of Malice.』

“We really came to an A-rank Dungeon.”

Clara exclaimed in admiration, yet she didn’t let go of the flute in her Hand and remained vigilant of her surroundings.

“There’s no need to be so nervous. Even if it’s an A-rank Dungeon, the combat difficulty isn’t that high. If it weren’t, would my Mother have easily given me the permit?”

Seong Hari was quite overprotective. Even knowing my abilities, she wouldn’t let me enter an A-rank Dungeon alone. The reason she easily gave the permit was because she knew this Dungeon wouldn’t pose a great danger to me.

“Still, Monsters appear, don’t they?”

“It’s a Dungeon, after all. There’s no time to hesitate. Let’s go in.”

I took the lead and headed towards the building before us. Clara followed behind me, tense. The building entrance was open. We went inside. Some streaks of light entered through the ceiling, but it wasn’t particularly bright.

Sullung-sullung.

In the darkness untouched by light, something stirred.

It was a pitch-black shadow Monster. An amorphous Monster resembling a spirit. Annoying noises emanated from their Bodies. The Association had named them B-rank Monsters, Shadow Noise. Their only red eyes, on their Bodies, were filled with hostility towards us.

“Monsters?! I’ve never seen these Monsters before!”

“Shadow Noise. They’re B-rank Monsters recently added as a new species. The information hasn’t spread much for Academy learning. Since they only appear in this Dungeon, their importance is low. Their characteristic is that physical and Magic damage are reduced by half.”

“Can you really be so nonchalant? There are too many of them! More than 30 have already appeared!”

“Mocha.”

“Kku-uk!”

Mocha, the supreme lightning spirit, appeared in the air. An eagle over 5m in size appeared and spread its wings.

Kwarurururururung!

Thunder roared, and vivid blue lightning bolts shot out from Mocha in all directions. Hundreds of lightning strikes instantly tore apart the surrounding Shadow Noise.

Damage reduction ability? Meaningless before overwhelming damage.

“Wow… this is. The power of a Spirit User.”

“Keukeu. It’s all because I’m so outstanding.”

Mocha flew forward, and as soon as Shadow Noise appeared, it annihilated them all with lightning. We walked leisurely, not shedding a single drop of sweat.

“It’s too flashy, my eyes hurt. There’s also a strange smell, and my ears seem to hurt.”

Clara, who had been extremely tense, burst out with complaints. The overwhelming display seemed to have relaxed her tension.

“Bear with that much. You’re going safely thanks to me.”

“It seems it’s thanks to the spirit, not you.”

“Spirit Power is the power of a Spirit User, I tell you.”

“The Shadow Noise… doesn’t even leave a Corpse.”

“That’s why this Dungeon isn’t popular. There’s nothing much to gain from it.”

And we reached a dead end.

More precisely, a place with a colossal stone gate.

『Offer music of praise.』

“So this is why the conquest was blocked. I instinctively know. It has to be a personally composed song.”

“Exactly. How many Heroes can compose songs? And bringing a Layperson into the Dungeon is a serious violation.”

So the Hero Association decided to simply manage this Dungeon haphazardly and neglect it. It was also a Dungeon that didn’t experience Dungeon Breaks, so it was quickly forgotten within the Association.

“Can you do it?”

“Praise…. I’ve composed songs with religious overtones before.”

“Christian?”

“No religion.”

Clara confidently brought the flute to her Lips.

Music of diverse and beautiful tones, unbelievable for a flute, resonated.

The melody was soft yet grand. It was music that brought churches to mind.

After about 2 minutes, the music ended. However, the door showed no reaction.

“…Could playing music not be the answer? Like writing sheet music on the door or something.”

“Clara. Don’t run away. You know. It just doesn’t meet the Dungeon’s criteria.”

“…I have other songs. I’ve been composing since I was little. I can do it as much as I want.”

Clara played another song. She seemed much more focused than before, and her sound contained Mana.

But, putting that aside, if you asked if it was better than the first song she played….

‘It’s not great. It feels like the completeness is lacking.’

No matter how excellent a composer she was, not all her songs could be good.

Clara probably played songs that were merely practice pieces out of pride.

The Dungeon did not react. Clara, in her frustration, played 7 more songs. The result did not change. The stone gate did not open.

“What’s the standard?! If you don’t like my song, tell me why!”

Clara shouted and kicked the stone gate with her foot. The stone gate didn’t budge.

“It’s no use. That door is protected by the Dungeon’s power. Even if Mocha gave its all, it couldn’t break it. And you’re not a physical Ability User. You’ll just sprain your ankle for nothing, so don’t bother with clumsy kicks.”

“Fuck…!”

“Oh. Clara cursing.”

“Shut up. I can curse too. Ssi-bal.”

“What are you going to do? Give up like this?”

“Give up? There’s no way I’d give up after coming all this way. The songs I just played, the newest one was composed 5 years ago. If I compose a song now, it will be different. It has to be different.”

“That’s impressive. But you can’t compose a song right now, can you? Should we leave the Dungeon for now? Do you want to go back to the Academy? Or hang out for a bit?”

“…Shouldn’t we go straight back to the Academy?”

“We have plenty of time. If there’s somewhere you want to stop by, we can do that and then go back. I get bored if I go back too quickly.”

“……”

Clara’s eyes wavered. She slowly pondered before opening her mouth.

“I want to go to a place related to religion. Since it’s late, even if we can’t go inside a church… just seeing a cathedral or church building would be enough.”

“Good. Let’s go.”

We left the Dungeon and got into the car.

It was a late-night drive and a date. However, Clara seemed not to think so, judging by her consistently serious expression.

We drove around to various religious buildings until we returned to the Academy. Along the way, we went into a 24-hour restaurant or had a meal in front of a convenience store. We barely made it back inside the Academy just before sunrise.

『Clara Feirede’s Affection: 35』

Her Affection increased by 1.

• • •

The next day.

Clara dozed off little by little during the regular lecture. It was natural, as she had been out with me until dawn. I openly slept during the lecture, but Clara couldn’t, and it was clear she was struggling quite a bit.

Clara began frantically searching for information related to religion.

“Clara.”

“Why?”

Her voice was irritable. I understood. Things weren’t going well for her.

“I have a Friend who is religious. In the world, she’s treated as a Cult, but… it is a real religion. Do you want to meet her?”

A small light sparkled in Clara’s dull, frozen-fish-like eyes. The original diligent Clara would have kept her distance just hearing the word Cult.

“I’ll meet her.”

I introduced Iste, the Saintess of the Gumokgyo religion and a transfer student in her second year, to Clara.

“Hello. My name is Iste Meyer.”

“I’m Clara Feirede.”

I left the spot. Since religious conversation was bound to be boring, I went and had a round of Sex and came back.

Iste left, and Clara, concentrating like mad, composed a song. The meeting with Iste seemed to have been a good stimulus.

That night, I moved to the Hall of Malice Dungeon with Clara.

『Offer music of praise.』

She performed in front of the stone gate. It was clearly a pleasant song to listen to… but the stone gate did not react.

Clara bit her Lips and hung her Head low.

“Shall we wander around a bit today too?”

“…No. I’m a bit tired today. Let’s go back to the Academy.”

“Clara. Are you free tomorrow? It’s a holiday, isn’t it?”

“I have to compose. I don’t have time to play.”

“You can’t compose without inspiration anyway. And you won’t be satisfied with a Forced song yourself.”

“……”

Clara couldn’t deny it. Instead, she sent me a resentful gaze.

“You’re too engrossed in religion. The theme presented by the stone gate is praise, not religion. I’ll take you to a place related to praise. It’ll be much better than being stuck in the Academy Dormitory. How about it?”

“Alright. I’ll make time. But where are we going?”

“There’s a good subculture event happening. But to properly enjoy it, you’ll have to cosplay. I’ll provide the cosplay Clothes.”

“A subculture event? Cosplay? Is it like Halloween? I’m a Celebrity, so if I go to a place like that without saying anything, there’ll only be trouble.”

“It’s fine, it’s fine. I have a way.”

I grinned at her.
