While Seong Yujin was taking care of personal matters, Yuria and Elena changed into Arashi Academy uniforms. It was the result of Seong Yujin's influence, who wanted to do a uniform play.

,Wearing these clothes makes me feel like a student. It's a little uncomfortable, but not bad."

Elena said, looking in the mirror. The uniform suited her, as she was a beautiful woman.

Yuria next to her tidied her hair and clothes. Yuria's hands wouldn't allow a single wrinkle on the uniform.

,Elena-ssi, have you never attended an academy? Elena-ssi's empire must be quite developed, so there should be educational institutions."

,Of course, academies exist. Talented commoners receive sponsorship from nobles and are admitted. I also actively sponsor academies and new students. It's an opportunity to bring in talent."

,It seems nobles don't attend academies."

,The children of lower nobles below the rank of Baron enter the academy. It's quite helpful in building connections."

,Elena-ssi wouldn't, then."

,I can just invite a renowned scholar to the family, so there's no need to enter the academy. If I throw a party, nobles flock like clouds for connections. If I sweet-talk them under the guise of business, I can use them appropriately. What about you? Have you ever attended an academy?"

,I am attending an academy. Though it's a story from another world."

,Hoo. Is it similar to the academy here?"

,It's completely different. There are more things to protect and learn."

,Is the academy enjoyable?"

Yuria paused. She thought of the maid academy. After contemplating for quite a while, she nodded.

,It was enjoyable in its own way."

,That's an unexpected answer."

,Of course, serving Master is more enjoyable."

,That's the answer I expected."

After checking their outfits one last time, they went outside the room. Originally, they were going to wait in the room until Seong Yujin returned, but at Elena's request, they decided to look around the cultural festival.

For Elena, it was no different from being on vacation. She wanted to put everything down and enjoy the culture and food of this world. The Valdert family, which always weighed heavily on her shoulders, didn't exist in this world, so she could act lightly.

,Where are you going?"

Seong Hari, who was leaning against the hallway wall, asked.

Elena smirked at her.

,Were you watching us?"

,You two look suspicious no matter how I look at it, so I investigated you separately. Do you know what the result was? There was nothing. As if you don't exist in this world. So, I'm watching you directly. To make sure you don't do anything strange. So I can subdue you immediately if you do anything strange."

,You're buying yourself tiring work. Didn't you see our relationship with Yujin directly?"

,Eugh."

Seong Hari's cheeks turned red. She remembered the sight of the two women in front of her and Seong Yujin naked and intertwined. It was truly an obscene sight in itself. A sight that made the viewer more embarrassed.

,That's that, and this is this. I've never acknowledged you as my daughters-in-law."

,Are you still going to play mommy with Yujin? You've got a thick face."

,...Mommy play? You, your tone has been a bit off since yesterday? There's a limit to my patience."

,Be grateful to Yujin. If it weren't for Yujin, you'd be sitting on the floor crying your eyes out."

A fierce smile appeared on Seong Hari's lips. At the same time, her energy rose. Elena wasn't flustered at all. She crossed her arms and raised her chin as if to say, 'Go ahead and try.'

Yuria stepped between them.

,Both of you, stop it. If you cause a ruckus here, it will be a nuisance to Master. Elena-ssi, we are strangers. Mother, we don't want to cause a commotion and disappoint Master. I won't tell you to reconcile. At least, please bear with it for now. We don't mind if Mother watches us."

Seong Hari sighed and dispersed her energy. Elena turned her head away as if she wasn't interested.

,Hey. Was it Yuria? That title of 'Mother' is a bit much."

,It's a sign of respect."

,I know. I know, but... Even so, being called 'Mother' is a bit much. It's awkward, and it makes me look too old."

,You're misunderstanding something. This title isn't respect for you. It's respect for Master."

,...What?"

,Pfft."

It was Elena who burst into laughter. She could somewhat infer Yuria's way of thinking. Yuria's center is Seong Yujin. The reason she calls Seong Hari 'Mother' but doesn't truly treat her as a mother-in-law is because Seong Yujin doesn't really think of Seong Hari as his mother.

Seong Yujin's mommy play. Yuria is just going along with it appropriately.

,If I meet Seong Yujin's real mother, I'll probably fawn over her with all my might. ...Hmm. There's no information about this. Seong Yujin's mother is also my future mother-in-law, so I need to make her an ally. Using illusion magic is the worst... Is a gift offensive the answer?'

Under Seong Hari's glaring watch, Elena and Yuria came out of the dormitory building.

As soon as they went out, men bothered them. Their appearance was too noticeable. From blatant questions about whether they had boyfriends to requests to take pictures together, nothing was not annoying.

Feeling unpleasant, Elena used illusion magic to chase the men away. The men would have traumas they didn't even know about, but that wasn't something Elena had to consider.

,This is annoying as it is. I'm going to use illusion magic. Is that okay?"

,Please do."

Elena's gaze turned to Seong Hari.

,Oh, me too?"

,Think about your appearance too. It's not like men only talked to us."

,...I'll ask too. You're not doing anything weird, are you?"

Elena waved her hand as if it wasn't even worth answering. The illusion magic was activated, and the gazes that had been focused on them disappeared. Now, other people would see them as ordinary women.

Elena grabbed Yuria's arm and walked briskly. The festive atmosphere of the cultural festival made her excited. Seong Hari followed behind them.

Their footsteps stopped in front of a street stall.

,What is this?"

,It's cotton candy. Have you never seen it before?"

,You don't know cotton candy?"

Yuria and Seong Hari were surprised for different reasons.

,Cotton candy? It looks like cotton. I should try it."

,...Why are you looking at me?"

,I don't have money from this world. I'd like you to lend me some."

,Unfortunately, I don't have yen either."

Elena's gaze turned to Seong Hari.

,Lend it to me."

,Out of the blue?!"

,Isn't it a small amount? Don't be petty."

,You're too confident for someone who's borrowing. ...I'm lending it to you because I pity you. To think you've never even eaten cotton candy."

,I don't know what you're thinking, but pitying me... It hurts my pride, but I'll forgive you since you lent me money."

Elena bought three cotton candies and distributed them to Yuria and Seong Hari.

,I'm okay."

,You already bought it. How arbitrary are you, being a noble?"

,It's a festival. You guys eat it too."

,Are you embarrassed to eat alone?"

,...It's been a long time since I've had cotton candy."

They opened their mouths wide and tore off pieces of cotton candy to eat. Elena made a subtle expression.

,It's sweet. I like that it melts as soon as I put it in my mouth, but... It's too sweet."

,It seems they're using cheap sugar. If it were me, I'd make cotton candy in a slightly different way..."

,I used to like it a lot... I wonder if my taste has changed."

The impressions they spat out were nothing but criticism. Elena threw the cotton candy in the trash, and Yuria melted the cotton candy with magic to dispose of it. Only Seong Hari stubbornly ate the cotton candy.

,By the way, Elena-ssi. How do you plan to repay the money? It's not going to be a crime, is it?"

,What do you take me for? I've already planned everything. First, I'll use the gold coins I have as seed money to invest in stocks."

,Selling the gold coins alone would be enough money."

,I want to try stocks. There's no such thing as stocks in my world."

Seong Hari, who was listening to the conversation, frowned and interjected.

,Stocks? Don't do it."

,Why?"

,If I tell you not to, don't do it. Half of your entire fortune could be blown away. I heard there's a basement under the floor... No, I really thought it was the floor..."

,......"

The next place they headed to was the art gallery.

A place where the works of Arashi Academy students were exhibited. Elena looked around the entrance of the art gallery once and smiled.

,Garbage that I wouldn't even take if you gave it to me is on display. Modern art? The level of civilization has increased, but art seems to have regressed. Yuria, what do you think?"

,I think everyone has their own standards. What's here might be art to someone."

,So, in your eyes, what's here is art?"

,No. It's garbage."

Seong Hari watched the two of them.

,Honestly, aren't these decent works?'

In her eyes, they looked like decent works. So much so that she thought it would be okay to buy a few and decorate her house with them.

The next place they visited after the gallery was the haunted house. All three of them felt nothing but boredom, rather than being surprised by the ghosts. They could feel the presence of all the people hiding, and the ghost makeup felt somewhat clumsy.

,Why don't we go see the monster circus? Arashi Academy is famous for taming monsters and doing circuses."

Rarely, Seong Hari offered an opinion. She had become friendly with the people she was monitoring by going around here and there.

,Monster circus? They're doing something interesting."

,It's certainly intriguing."

The monster circus was enough to surprise them. Taming and training monsters. The words are easy, but in reality, it's never easy.

After watching the monster circus for about an hour, they were satisfied. Afterwards, they had dinner at the cooking club's restaurant, which was famous for its delicious food.

,The omelet rice isn't bad."

,What? This is incredibly delicious. Especially the cooking club's omelet rice is very famous?"

,Seong Hari is right. I have no choice but to acknowledge this omelet rice."

,...If you say so, then I guess so."

After that, they went around here and there and then watched the magic fireworks before returning to the dormitory.
