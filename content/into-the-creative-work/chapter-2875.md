,Well, I have no intention of dying from just this kind of curse.'

Even while being eaten away by the curse, my mind was clear.

I used my Unique Skill in a workshop protected by a barrier.

Unique Skill: Absoluteness.

Blue mana gathers around my body, emitting light. A scene as if sparkling blue starlight is flowing down, centered on me.

While Absoluteness is activated, I become immune to debuffs. The Corrupted's curse that was eating away at me is no exception.

But that didn't mean my body felt better. While Absoluteness is activated, a burden continues to accumulate on my body and mana road. Therefore, even if I do nothing, I can't maintain this state for long.

,The reason I didn't think much of the curse was because of Absoluteness rather than Holy magic. Debuff immunity. Even if it's a curse that was eating away at my body, from the moment I activate Absoluteness, the curse on me disappears. That's normal. I actually cursed myself to confirm it.'

But the Corrupted's curse was different.

I deactivate Absoluteness. The mana that had gathered around me scatters, and the curse that had disappeared settles in my heart again. I sealed the curse with Holy magic. No, it would be more accurate to say I suppressed it. Because it's impossible to completely seal it with my magic right now.

,The curse that disappeared when I activated Absoluteness returns and settles in my heart as soon as I deactivate Absoluteness.'

Thanks to that, I got a sense of this curse to some extent. Originally, it would have taken quite a while to realize this. But I had knowledge of the original game.

,Curse of Causality.'

The target of the Corrupted's curse is me, but also not me.

The reason I can analyze it coolly even as death approaches from time to time.
