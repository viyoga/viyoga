Beatrice ended her short meeting with Seong Yujin and immediately returned to the World Hunter Association headquarters in New York.

Originally, it was rare for her to travel to other countries directly.

Even if you're an S-Class Hunter, you have to come to New York to meet her, a director of the World Hunter Association.

This case is special. Seong Yujin wasn't just any S-Class Hunter.

Does he know he's getting special treatment?

At the World Hunter Association headquarters, Beatrice met with Chairman James Kraft. The middle-aged man with half-gray hair and a beard smiled gently as always.

,You've come. What did you think of him when you saw him in person? You can see a person's essence and danger just by looking at them, can't you?

Beatrice also smiled and replied.

,It was worth going to Korea to meet him in person. I can't figure out his essence either. Just like you.

,Like me? That means there's something special. Or he's achieved something. What about the danger? Is he someone who will disrupt the order?

,He was arrogant. I felt confidence in himself in his voice. He's not afraid of the World Hunter Association. If the Association were to fight him... I'd opt out.

,Is it that serious? Don't worry about that. Unless he's an enemy of humanity who disrupts the order... there's no reason for the Association to be at odds with him. Is he perhaps a Returnee?

This world isn't the only one that exists. Other worlds exist too. The Association usually calls them other dimensions. There are beings from other dimensions hiding all over Earth. One of the Association's jobs is to find and exterminate them. If they can't be eliminated, they're sealed away. Believing that they can be dealt with later.

Occasionally, there are those who accidentally fall into other worlds and return to Earth. They are the Returnees. But it's very rare. Was the last Returnee decades ago?

,A Returnee? Hmm. I didn't get that feeling. But I can't be sure.

,I believe your words. Anyway, there's no need to become enemies.

,Yes. All he wants from us is to be promoted to S-Class.

,His promotion to S-Class will be done according to the regulations. Otherwise, there will be a lot of talk from the board of directors.

,Other S-Class Hunters might be dissatisfied.

,There are many among the S-Class Hunters who are like kings. What's his performance level right now?

,Over 80%.

,I haven't heard any news of him attacking dangerous A-Class or S-Class dungeons....

,Have you forgotten? He killed the vampire. In a way, he saved the world.

,Ah. That's right. I've been so out of it lately that I forgot. Thanks to his efforts, the world is safer... it's a grateful thing. He solved a difficult problem for the S-Class Hunters too. I'd like to promote him just for that....

,Others wouldn't understand. Even if you talk about the danger of vampires, they won't believe it unless they've experienced it themselves.

,Hoo. Being the Chairman is so tiring. You. Would you like to try being the Chairman once?

,I can't, even if I wanted to. I can't handle the Trinity. And wasn't there a prophecy that you're the last owner of the Trinity?

,...An ominous prophecy. But that prophecy will never come true. Ah, right. Another prophecy has fallen. Cultists in Northern Europe are trying to make a deal with a god from another world.

,A god from another world.... In reality, they're more like demons.

,Most of them are. But there are real, dangerous gods from other worlds. Unfortunately, they're targeting Earth.

,I know.

,Then why don't you help me out a bit? Go to Northern Europe.

,My back aches after going to Korea.

,...I'll attach a space-movement ability user this time. It's urgent.

,A healthy organization should have timely generational changes. I believe in the leading figures of the next generation.

Beatrice smiled and left. She meant that she wouldn't go to Northern Europe.

James sighed. He couldn't force her to go, knowing her age.

,Hoo. Why do prophecies of doom keep popping up when I'm in the Chairman's seat.... Really, even the gods are heartless.... ...No, is this not something I should be saying?

James, left alone, smiled bitterly.

To Park Joo-jung, Park Seon-hoo is a sore spot. Even more so because he couldn't tell Park Seon-hoo that he was his dad. As a father, and as an uncle, he hadn't done anything for him. Park Seon-hoo doesn't even know that Park Joo-jung is his real dad.

,Seon-hoo-ya, this father will get revenge for you if nothing else.

The method is simple.

Follow Seong Yujin into the dungeon and kill him.

Of course, Park Joo-jung wouldn't step forward directly. As a big shot, attention gathers every time he moves. Especially after the Park Seon-hoo incident, the World Hunter Association was suspicious of the Park Joo-jung family.

,The blood of monsters flows in our family, so he became a monster? These cheeky bastards...!

Surprisingly, that was the World Hunter Association's answer when he questioned them about this. He couldn't even argue, knowing the power they possessed.

He would repay this humiliation someday.

The plan had already begun.

The Suwol Guild had many capable Hunters who followed his orders well. Hunters who usually attacked dungeons like ordinary Hunters, but could become ruthless killers when his orders were given.

,Seon-hoo. How much power did that kid want to make such a choice....

The power to become a monster.

It was obvious. He had used a cursed power or something. If you've been in the Hunter industry for a long time, you can often hear stories of Hunters reaching for forbidden powers.

,I'm sorry I couldn't take care of you, this father.

He sniffled, looking at Park Seon-hoo's picture on his smartphone. He was getting old and going through menopause.

I entered an A-Class closed dungeon. It was a dungeon with a beach. The monsters were in that beach. In other words, to kill the monsters, I had to go into the sea and fight. That's why even large guilds avoided this dungeon.

,There aren't many Hunters who can fight underwater. Usually, a guild that specializes in attacking these dungeons takes it on. While also collecting the Association's commission.

Instead of attacking right away, I took out a sunbed in front of the beach and lay down on it. I also took a sip of a cool Jack and Coke.

The sunny weather, the slowly breaking waves, etc. The scenery of the beach was excellent. Except for the fact that A-Class monsters were swarming in that sea.

After enjoying the leisure for a while, I soon smiled.

I could feel it through the heightened senses I had been using since earlier. About 10 A-Class Hunters were moving quietly, surrounding me.

,The guest I've been waiting for has finally arrived.

If there's a Hunter you don't like, you kill them in the dungeon.

It was common sense in the Hunter industry.

What if that guy you don't like is attacking a dungeon alone? Isn't it the perfect situation to kill him? If I were Park Joo-jung, I would have killed him in the dungeon too.

,However, if it were me, I would have moved myself.

I got up from the sunbed and looked behind me. I glared at the few rocks and palm trees on the beach and opened my mouth.

,I know everything. Come out.

,So you're an S-Class candidate. Your senses are sharp.

10 A-Class Hunters sent by the Suwol Guild appeared. They weren't covering their faces, armed as they were. I could feel their determination to kill me here for sure. Some were veteran A-Class Hunters with known faces.

,The Suwol Guild? Why are fairly well-known Hunters trying to kill me?

,We just received orders. However, I heard that you killed Park Seon-hoo first.

I looked around at them and found a familiar face.

Jang Go-joon.

He was an A-Class Hunter from the Suwol Guild who once approached my woman, Han Ah-young. Now, they've become distant, unable to even talk to Han Ah-young. It was something I asked Han Ah-young to do.

,Jang Go-joon. I didn't expect you to be among these guys. Does Ah-young-nim know?

,...Ah-young doesn't know. There's no need for Ah-young to know about the dirty work that someone has to do.

,Ah-young? You're not in a position to call Ah-young-nim so familiarly.

,What's it to you?

,Haha. My relationship with Ah-young-nim? You know even if I don't tell you directly.

Pudeuk.

Jang Go-joon gritted his teeth.

It seems he hasn't given up on his feelings for Han Ah-young, even now that they've become distant.

I understand. Han Ah-young is a charming woman with everything: face, body, and ability. Especially since Jang Go-joon was a classmate who was relatively close to Han Ah-young.

,This has nothing to do with Ah-young. And I have no personal feelings for you.

,It looks like you do to anyone. Keu keu.

Some of the guys took out dark red beads from their pockets. As they injected mana into the beads, a dark red, smoky energy flew towards me.

I dodged to the side. The energy changed direction and rushed at my body. A lukewarm and sticky energy permeated my body.

,Is this the curse of mana sealing?

My energy channels were blocked. My mana wouldn't move. It was a stuffiness, like not being able to breathe properly.

My brain resisted the cursed energy. It seemed to have an effect, but there wasn't time to concentrate on driving out the cursed energy.

,I could use Heavenly Heart or Complete Recovery. But it doesn't seem like I'll lose even if I don't use them.

When I frowned, the guys who were nervous smiled.

,I can't feel any mana. The curse worked properly.

,Even an S-Class Hunter is worse than an A-Class if they can't use mana.

,This guy isn't even an S-Class Hunter. This mission ended easily.

,To rise to fame as an S-Class Hunter candidate and die like this. Wouldn't you be too wronged to close your eyes easily?

,Ah-young will be sad.

I was dumbfounded, looking at the guys who were acting like they had already won. Especially Jang Go-joon, who was trying to act serious but his lips were twitching. Does he think that if I die, Han Ah-young will go to him? It was so absurd that I couldn't help but laugh.

,I've heard your last words well.

I raised my hand into the air.

Dozens of spaces split in the air, and launch ports appeared. A total of 30 launch ports aimed at the enemies.

,What is it? You shouldn't be able to use your abilities since your mana is blocked...?!

The descriptions of my skills say that they consume mana and vitality. In terms of ratio, it's 9 mana and 1 vitality. If I can't use mana, I can use skills with vitality alone. However, the power won't be the same as when using mana.

So what is vitality? It wasn't life force. It's closer to stamina and vigor in that it makes you tired after using it. And I was confident in my stamina and vigor.

,There are abilities I can use even without mana.

,Is it an artifact!

Wrong.

I didn't bother explaining.

,Just die already.

Meteor swords prepared in the 30 launch ports poured out.
