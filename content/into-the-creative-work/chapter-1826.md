In the beginning, Cheon Bae Bok had the advantage in the battle between Cheon Bae Bok and Seong Yujin. Seong Yujin's attacks became threatening with the aid of his mind's eye, but there was an insurmountable gap between Cheon Bae Bok and Seong Yujin from the start.

,I thought it would be one of two things: either Cheon Bae Bok would acknowledge Seong Yujin, or Seong Yujin would be defeated by Cheon Bae Bok.'

The superiority of the mind's eye? That only happens in the very beginning. The mind's eye is, in essence, spirit. The power of the mind's eye can be said to be mental strength.

Seong Yujin lacked the experience to handle the power of the mind's eye with finesse. He was wielding the power of the mind's eye in a simple, brute-force manner, so that power should have been running out quickly. It should have.

,The power of the mind's eye is being wielded endlessly.'

Rather, Seong Yujin became accustomed to the power of the mind's eye. Cheon Bae Bok was quickly growing tired. Originally, there was a limit to what one could do in someone else's mind's eye, and they were already dead spirits.

,What is that Demonic martial art he's using?'

It was very similar to Cheonma Shingong, but it wasn't Cheonma Shingong. However, to simply call it a Demonic martial art created by imitating Cheonma Shingong would be an understatement, as its depth was comparable to Cheonma Shingong.

Cheon Bae Bok desperately inflicted a fatal wound on Seong Yujin. A long, fatal cut along his side, near the waist. ,Uwaaaaaaaagh!'

Cheon Bae Bok shouted. It was too desperate to be called a battle cry. Seong Yujin grinned, as if he had realized something.

Seong Yujin completely offered up his body. He attacked without caring whether his arm was cut off, his entrails were spilling out of his torso, or his head was smashed. Lightning and swords fell upon Cheon Bae Bok, and the dense Demonic energy flowing from Seong Yujin's body took the form of a dragon.

Seong Yujin was like an immortal. Even if he died, he wouldn't stay dead. No, Seong Yujin's spirit would not be broken.

,It should take mental strength to recover his body as well.... Is his mental strength infinite?'

He chuckled in disbelief.

,Damn it.... I, I'm losing to such a young punk!!'

Cheon Bae Bok was defeated. His massive body collapsed and disappeared. Cheon Hyeonsak's eyebrows twitched. He wasn't simply disappearing because he was defeated. His existence was being absorbed somewhere.

,Crazy. It's no different from absorbing the souls of the Four Great Demons. If he absorbs the souls of higher realms... there's a high possibility he'll go crazy from the reverse influence.'

However, Seong Yujin was fine. The mind's eye, which should have been influenced by Cheon Bae Bok, was just as calm.

Seong Yujin abruptly turned. His eyes, gleaming with murderous intent, turned towards Cheon Hyeonsak. Cheon Hyeonsak unconsciously folded his fan and grabbed the hilt of the sword at his waist.
