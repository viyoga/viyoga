[Electric Field]

I used Electric Field for support, not attack. It's not for attacking. It's laying the groundwork to use electric magic more easily and powerfully.

A bright blue current spreads around, riding along the ground. But it's blocked midway and disappears. The heat of the maple tree Gillian summoned suppressed the current and scattered it. That heat was rising in real time.

,It doesn't seem to think and attack directly like an animal-type summon... Just its existence is influencing things, so it's very annoying.'

The biggest problem is that I have no information about that maple tree at all. I didn't even know there were trees in the Summoning Realm in the first place. I wasn't interested in summoning from the start anyway.

"Hahaha! Fireball! Fireball! Fireball!!"

Gillian laughs and throws fireballs.

I moved my body here and there with psychokinesis, dodging the fireballs. Bang! Bang! Bang! The fireballs explode near me, and the shockwave pounds the barrier. Unless it hit me directly. This much shockwave can't break my barrier.

,This is magic, but also an ability.'

The fireball isn't just magic made up of formulas. That maple tree isn't just giving the summoner an aptitude for fire magic.'

The harmony of elemental abilities and magic. I know better than anyone how efficient that is. That's because I'm using electric magic with the ability of lightning.

"I can't figure out that maple tree at all."

"That's what ignorant bastards are like. Well, I understand. This guy is very rare even in the Summoning Realm."

I wondered if he might tell me more since he was so arrogant, but as expected, he didn't seem to want to give details.

"I know it's a tree related to fire, even if I don't know anything else. Then I'll have to go the orthodox route."

[Water]

Water is created in the air. It's just a 1st-class magic. But I, who am using the magic, was 5th-class. Even if it's 1st-class magic, the power varies greatly depending on the magician's aptitude, skill, and class.

Magic that would normally only create enough water to fill a bucket was now pouring down like a waterfall. It's effective. At least the surrounding heat has subsided a bit.

"Even if you're 5th-class, you're creating that much water non-stop? Did you have an aptitude for water too?"

"I was born with it. I can use magic of other attributes without using tricks like someone else."

"You sure talk a good game."

The maple tree sways behind him. The red maple leaves shone even more brilliantly. Each maple leaf felt like a flame.

Chi-iiiiik!

The water that had fallen and spread on the ground evaporates. Steam rises, partially obscuring the view. But I couldn't stop pouring out water. Since the target is a fire magician, I need to cool down the heat even a little to increase my chances of winning.

"Did I say I'd fry you crispy when I called earlier? Then I'll bake you to the core. Well-done is the way to go with meat, after all."

Flames soared from the ground and swirled. I could feel the heat even though I was far away. I raised my altitude with psychokinesis. The vortex of flames chases after me.

I poured the falling water into the vortex. Chi-iiiiiiik! Steam rises. The water pouring down like a waterfall didn't cool the vortex down at all. Rather, the heat all around was rising even more due to the steam.

,This isn't the answer.'

I release Water.

,The approach is wrong. I need to get to the root of the problem.'

Before that, I have to deal with the vortex of flames trying to burn me.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 11]

The world slows down.

Only my mind and Astral had normal speed. Looking at the vortex of flames, I recall the magic I can use and calculate the formulas.

The moment I moved mana, the ephemeral moment ended. 52 mana rods simultaneously process mana into formulas. It seems a bit overloaded, but this much is fine. I know that limit better than anyone.

[Fire Tornado]

A red magic circle is drawn under my feet, and a Fire Tornado is created from there. The palm-sized vortex eats up the surrounding heat and oxygen and quickly grows in size, colliding with Gillian's vortex of flames.

Gillian's expression crumpled, as expected.

"You arrogant bastard! Do you think you can win with that kind of thing? I know you're an electric magician!"

"Fire magic isn't my specialty, but I think I can beat a half-assed fire magician?"

"You cheeky bastard!"

The two Fire Tornadoes collide.

Flame and flame. Originally, they would merge without colliding. That's how it should naturally be. But these flames are not ordinary flames, but flames created by magic. Flames containing the magician's will.

Kwakwakwakwakkwa!

The two Fire Tornadoes collide without mixing. They gnaw at each other's flames, gradually decreasing in size. It looked like two spinning tops fighting.

The rotational force of Gillian's Fire Tornado decreases. Its size rapidly decreases and then disappears. On the other hand, my Fire Tornado survived. It absorbs the surrounding heat and gradually increases the size that had been reduced.

"Shit."

Gillian's face was as crumpled as it could be.

He lost to an electric magician in the same fire magic. Magicians are a prideful bunch to begin with. Gillian will never forget the humiliation he feels now until the moment he dies.

I stretched out my index finger and pointed at Gillian. The Fire Tornado approaches, increasing in size.

Gillian didn't react.

,He can't have given up. What's going on?'

The Fire Tornado, which was approaching to burn Gillian, suddenly decreased in size. Heat and flames are sucked somewhere. It was the maple tree behind Gillian. To be exact, the maple leaves were absorbing the fire and starting to burn.

"Yeah, shit. I was wrong to try to fight like an elemental magician."

Gillian gritted his teeth and glared at me. He spat out a voice full of murderous intent.

"The game is over. I'm going all out. I'll kill you in the summoner's way."

"Are you going to summon a new beast?"

"I already summoned it."

Rustle, rustle.

The maple leaves move. The maple leaves themselves aren't moving. There was something inside the maple leaves. Soon, they revealed themselves.

"Sparrows?"

They were sparrows with red feathers. There were hundreds of them, but they didn't look dangerous at all because of their small and cute appearance.

"Do you know what the first thing a summoner hears from their teacher is?"

"...I don't know. Don't summon recklessly?"

"Don't judge by appearance. All the creatures in the Summoning Realm have at least one strange characteristic, even if they have small and insignificant appearances. For example, these Flame Birds...."

The sparrows began to peck at the maple leaves. The bodies of the sparrows that pecked quickly began to swell. The sparrow, which seemed to be less than 10cm, suddenly grew to nearly 3m. The absurd thing wasn't the sparrow's appearance. It grew legs and arms like a human. Its body was that of a muscular man, while its head was that of an innocent sparrow, making it feel out of place.

"Their bodies temporarily grow when they eat fire."

Hundreds of sparrows became hundreds of warriors. I moved before they could flap the wings attached to their backs.

,The number of beasts nearby has been decreasing since earlier. The reason why the sacrifices are decreasing is obvious. He's consuming them to maintain the summon.'

[Psychokinesis]

I levitate all the stones lying on the ground into the air.

[Steel Alchemy]

I temporarily turn the stones into steel.

[Shape Transformation]

I alchemize the steel into sharp knives and fly them with psychokinesis. To kill the beasts scattered and hidden.

"You're quick to notice, as expected. But do you think it will be that easy?"

Gillian sneers. I glanced behind me and saw hundreds of muscular sparrow monsters chasing me. I descended downwards. It's better to stand on the ground. At least they won't come from below.

[Smoke]

White smoke rises, obscuring their vision.

Cheep?! Cheep cheep!

The sparrows chasing me are flustered. I took advantage of the gap and aimed for the hidden beasts.

Since they weren't ordinary beasts, I had to concentrate to some extent and use psychokinesis to kill them even after identifying their location.

Cheep!

The sparrow monsters surround me.

[Smoke]

Bang!

I set off a smokescreen again and escape.

"You stupid bird-brains! Are your wings just for show? Blow away the smoke!!"

Gillian shouted, veins popping out of his neck. The sparrows flap their wings and blow away the smoke. But I had already escaped from their encirclement.

Thwack!

The steel knife controlled by psychokinesis pierces the head of the last beast.

"It seems I've won."

I smiled and looked at Gillian.

"Won my ass. Those guys are just conveniently modified sacrifices. What this guy really wants is...."

Whirr.

Flames rise in Gillian's right eye. I was wary, thinking it was a magic I didn't know, but I soon realized that wasn't the case. Gillian's eye was melting stickily.

,Since there are no sacrifices to offer to the summon, he's offering his own body as a sacrifice.'

I hurriedly erased the smile on my face.

It was worth getting rid of the beasts. The fact that he started offering himself as a sacrifice meant that his resources were starting to run out.

The temperature gets hotter. Pillars of fire soar from all directions. Gillian is the one in a hurry. He wants to end the battle somehow by killing me before he dies as a sacrifice.

,Just running away doesn't suit my personality.'

From the moment I saw Gillian's bottom line, the direction of the battle began to be drawn. I will win. I was sure of it.

Pajiijijijik!

I unleashed lightning and flew into the air. Flashing currents gathered in a circle around me, becoming a barrier.

The sparrows surrounded me but couldn't recklessly jump in. It was obvious they would be electrocuted and die.

"You morons!"

Gillian's right eye had completely melted. Then his left arm bursts into flames. The maple tree sways, and burning maple leaves flutter in the wind. Most of the maple leaves were absorbed by the sparrow monsters. The sparrow monsters grew even bigger.

"You've had a mysterious experience recently."

Pajiijijijik.

I create lightning in my grasp. Then, I assemble lightning and magic by giving formulas to the lightning.

"What are you doing, bird-brains! Attack!!"
