After finishing my interview with Commander Kalpas, I walked around inside the outpost.

It's called an outpost, but its size is that of a small city, so I drove around the roads where military vehicles were maintained. The unit also operated buses, so soldiers without cars could get around.

,'There are public vehicles, so if you have a license, you can use them.'

There were quite a few public vehicles in the command building. They were all the same military vehicles, so I just picked one and got in.

,'Since I have time, let's take a look around the outpost.'

I'll be living here for a while. It's better to know the geography inside the outpost.

,'There's a commercial district too.'

Several stores were set up. The stores were run by marines and robots. Prices were cheap with military discounts.

,'Seeing quite a few soldiers walking around even in this situation, it seems they've adapted.'

Currently, the outpost is surrounded by the Gestag army. Nevertheless, the soldiers are enjoying what they can. The upper echelons seem to have judged that the Gestag will not attack the outpost.

,'Well, I think I would make that judgment too. If they were going to attack, they would have already attacked. It seems the base also has nuclear bombs and antimatter bombs.'

Apart from the situation outside the barrier, the outpost inside the barrier is safe. That's how it looked on the surface.

,'If it were really safe, the quest would have been completed when I arrived at the outpost. Because my survival would be guaranteed.'

The condition of the quest is survival.

The fact that the quest hasn't been completed means this outpost isn't safe. No, to be precise, I'm not safe. The quest condition isn't the survival of the outpost, but my survival.

,'The outpost is safe. That's how the original story I know goes. But by me getting involved, the original story might have changed. Either the outpost isn't safe, or there's someone among the humans targeting me.'

Commander Kalpas? What reason do I have to trust the human I just met? Even if Kalpas shows me favor, that doesn't mean I should trust Kalpas.

,'A lieutenant general's position isn't something you can get with just luck and skill. You have to do some politicking too. Or be so damn skilled that you're called a hero.'

In my opinion, Kalpas was a capable general, but not to the extent of being called a hero.

I looked around the outpost here and there. Unfortunately, most places were off-limits to me. I couldn't even approach the armory.

Still, I roughly memorized the area locations and returned to the dormitory.

The dormitory is for guests. Marie and Emma and I belong to the Forte ship, but with the ship crashing and exploding, it's as good as our affiliated unit disappearing. Currently, we are awaiting unit transfer until our affiliation is confirmed.

The guest dormitory wasn't a very large building, but the facilities were quite good. This is because most of the guests who come to the outpost are high-ranking officials.

Marie was seen exercising in the gym inside the building. She was lightly dressed in pants and a tank top.

"Marie. Wanna work out with me?"

"Shut up. The workout you're talking about isn't very helpful."

"Wanna go out on a date? I saw it on the way here, but the facilities are pretty good."

"It's almost dinner time."

Marie's voice was firm. She picked up the dumbbell again.

I realized for the first time that Marie liked to exercise.

,'She's not very good at combat though.'

Combat and exercise were different.

If I mess with Marie now, I'll probably just get a lot of nagging, so I decided to let it go. I'll be meeting her in bed tonight anyway.

Emma was sitting in the conference room. She was staring at a tablet, working. It was probably because of report writing.

I looked at her arms and clicked my tongue. It hadn't even been a few hours, but her arms had regenerated.

"What. You said you'd use prosthetic arms, but you regenerated your arms?"

"These are prosthetic arms."

The prosthetic arms I imagined were robot arms with a cold metal design. I approached Emma and touched her forearm.

"It's definitely cold and hard. But I'd believe it even if you said the surface was real skin."

"Who said you could touch me carelessly?"

"Hey. What's with us. But can you attach and detach these?"

"......"

Emma glared at me and nodded.

I don't know why Marie and Emma are so prickly today. I don't think it's that time of the month.

I was curious about the prosthetic arms, so I touched them here and there.

"Do these have sensation too?"

"They have some. This is a high-end product for officers in the military. I got it thanks to Commander Kalpas' consideration."

"Wow. The technology is amazing."

"Shut up. Don't bother me and leave."

Emma pushes me away with her left hand. Of course, I resisted. But my body couldn't resist and was pushed back. It was an unbelievable force.

,'Is it the power of the prosthetic arms.'

It was the power of prosthetic arms befitting the words military and high-end product.

,'With this level of performance, I can tolerate the hassle of maintenance.'

Instead of fighting head-on, I avoided the prosthetic arms and clung to Emma. No matter how good the performance of the prosthetic arms, it was meaningless if the user couldn't utilize them well. I took Emma's back and massaged her chest with both hands.

"Haa."

Emma sighed as if she was annoyed and focused on her work.

And exactly 15 minutes later.

"Haaeeuuung!"

Emma and I were intertwined on the conference room desk.
