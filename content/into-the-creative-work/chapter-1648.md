I looked down into the pit.

Just as Chrisema-ssi had said, the pit was large and deep. Even though the sun was still up, the inside of the pit was so dark that I couldn't see anything at all.

,It's just like an antlion's pit.'

Once you go inside, it seems hard to get out again.

,You must go in alone. Only then will the warriors recognize you."

She meant to leave the undead behind and go in alone.

,I was planning to go alone from the start. Just this much of a trial. I'm more than enough on my own."

I jumped into the pit.

At first, it felt like riding a sand slide. Because of the slope, I went down underground at a rapid speed. When I went down completely, complete darkness, where not a single ray of light could be seen, greeted me.

As an undead, I pierced through the darkness.

,It's convenient that I don't need to light a fire.'

The road was one-way. I liked that I didn't need to find a path.

I walk forward.

From the outside, I thought it was a large and deep pit, but the inside was quite narrow. You could say it was at a level where one adult man could barely walk.

Shwiik.

Something flies in. When I felt the sound and presence, it was already too late to react. Thwack. What was lodged in my chest was an arrow. I nonchalantly stared at the embedded arrow before pulling it out with my hand.

,Is this some kind of trap?'

Shwiik! Shwiishwiishwiik!

Arrows fly in.

Unlike before, I wasn't careless. I form an Aura Armor. The arrows that were flying in collided with the Aura Armor and fell in droves.

,The arrows were accurately aiming for the head and heart. The accuracy is too high to say it's a trap by a machine.'

I came to one conclusion. Chrisema-ssi's words that this trial was a tradition of the tribe could be a lie. A lie to kill me here.

,Black Wave.'

I used black magic.

A black wave spread out from me. I could feel the objects touching the wave.

It wasn't a human that shot arrows at me. A statue with a bow and quiver was shooting arrows at me.

Tat tat tat.

Another statue runs and approaches me. In that statue's hand was a sword.

,I was a little nervous because it was said to be a trial where 99% die, but now that I've experienced it, it's nothing special."

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

I thrust out my fist. The fist energy stretched out like a dragon and destroyed the statue in front.

I swung my fist every time I saw a statue.

I break the statues blocking my way and move forward.

The narrow road widened as I moved forward. At the same time, the number of statues targeting me also increased.

,Do you think I'd be defeated by statues like these?'

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Palm (Heavenly Demon Divine Palm).

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Flying Claw (Heavenly Demon Flying Claw)

I move both hands without rest. I destroy the statues with my fists, palms, and fingernails. I even felt a strange pleasure in the statues being shattered into pieces.

,Easy."

I destroy over 100 statues and move forward.

Then, at some point, I started to get confused about whether I was moving forward. That's because I couldn't see the exit at all.

,It must be my imagination. This is a one-way street. It doesn't make sense to get lost. If I keep going straight, the exit will appear.'

If necessary, I can take out a space movement scroll from my inventory.

From some point on, statues stopped appearing. Since there were no statues attacking, I could walk comfortably.

,Do you really think you are right?"

My steps stopped at the voice that came from the front. I was surprised and looked straight ahead.

A man was standing there. The man wore Tutankhamun's golden mask, his upper body was undressed, and he wore golden bracelets and a skull necklace. The man was me. The difference was that the man's skin was green.

,What are you doing, taking my form?"

,I asked first. Answer my question."

That thing with my appearance and my voice is definitely not me.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

The attack didn't work. As if it didn't exist, the Dragon Fist passed through the guy's body. I clicked my tongue and walked forward. The guy was still, but strangely, the distance didn't narrow.

,Do you truly think that you are right?"

,Of course. I am right."

,Why?"

,Because I'm strong. Is there any other reason?"

,......."

Suddenly, the space changed.

It was a desert.

Two men and women were falling into quicksand.

,Save me!"

,Please save me!"

They reached out to me and asked for help. I thought for a moment about what this was all about.

,Are you testing my morality or something?'

I look at the man and woman. Half of their bodies were already buried in quicksand. The man was not worth saving, and the woman's beauty was average.

I ignored the man and woman and moved forward.

,You had the power to save both of them. Why didn't you save them?"

,I want to ask the opposite. Why should I save them?"

The space changed again.

An old man was kneeling and reaching out to me with both hands.

,Bread! Wouldn't you share that bread with me, even just a little?"

Before I knew it, I was holding a piece of bread in my hand. I looked back and forth between the bread and the old man. Giving the bread wasn't difficult.

,Okay. I'll give it to you. The bread doesn't seem to taste very good anyway."

I threw the bread. It flew almost 50M. The old man asked me with a dumbfounded expression.

,Why did you throw the bread over there? If you didn't want to give me the bread, you could have just said you didn't want to...."

,What are you talking about? I gave you the bread. Go pick it up and eat it."

,......."

,Old man. If you're going to beg, beg more politely."

The old man tried to say something, but soon drooped his shoulders and walked towards where the bread had fallen. I erased the old man from my mind and moved forward.

There was a child under a tree. The boy, who looked about 6 years old, was crying his eyes out, not knowing what was so sad. I ignored him and passed by.

In addition to that, several situations greeted me. I ignored everything unless they spoke directly to me. I knew they were fake anyway. Even if they were real, I would have ignored them.

The guy appeared.

,I see. You don't see humans as humans."

,Are you done with all the bullshit? I want to finish this crap soon."

,The judgment is over. You will bring chaos. It's better to die in this place."

I can feel an unusual energy from the guy's body.

「The King of the Demonic Sky chuckles.」

「The King of the Demonic Sky sponsors 10,000AP.

"Destroy your own heart."」

I pondered for a moment while looking at the notification window.

I couldn't understand why the King of the Demonic Sky suddenly sent a sponsorship. The content of the sponsorship was also strange.

,It seems like direct advice from the divine seat... Is there a need to send this kind of content even while being sanctioned by the system?'

Or it could just be a prank.

,...Well, there's no particular reason why I can't listen."

With my right hand, I pierced my own chest and took out my heart.

A heart that doesn't beat.

I destroyed the black heart as it was. Fragments of the destroyed heart splattered everywhere.

,I'm an undead now anyway. There's no pain, and I don't die.'

Clang!

The space shattered.

I was at a dead end. In front of the dead end, there was a scale. It was a tilted scale. There was nothing on the light side, and there was a feather on the heavily tilted side.

,This must be the feather Chrisema-ssi was talking about.'

I held the feather in my hand. Unlike what it looked like, the feather was surprisingly heavy.

I turned around and went outside. It didn't even take 10 minutes to get out of the pit.

「Meaningless Death is watching you.」

• • •

Chrisema-ssi was startled as soon as I came back with the feather.

,What's with that reaction? Did you think I wouldn't come back?"

,Ah, no! I was just surprised that you came back with the feather in less than an hour!"

,It was easy. Is it really a trial that 990 out of 1,000 people fail?"

,I didn't lie. That trial is a trial that only pure warriors can pass."

,Pure...? Well, whatever. Give me the second seal."

,...Please hold out your right hand."

A new red pattern appeared on the back of my right hand. I obtained the second seal.

I pass by Chrisema-ssi and go outside the village.

,Tutankhamun-isiyeo! Where are you going? Are you perhaps giving up on the seals?"

,I got the second seal, would I give up? I'm going to kill the giant below the cliff, so be prepared to hand over the third seal."

,...It won't be easy. The giant is strong. Thorough preparation is needed."

,I'll take care of it."

I led the undead army down the cliff. Since it was such a large cliff, it took more than 12 hours just to go down. When I arrived at the bottom of the cliff, the sun was rising.

,That must be the giant Chrisema-ssi was talking about.'

The giant, who was lying sprawled on the ground, opened his eyes and raised his body as if he had sensed someone. He only had one eye.

「The Eye of Light (False) recognizes you.」

It wasn't a monster, it was a Dignity.

,A one-eyed giant. I think it came from Greek mythology... Was it called a Cyclops?'

The guy, who raised his knees and straightened his back, was a whopping 30m tall. He looked around at me and the undead army, then curled up the corners of his mouth and shouted.

,Kuaaaaaaaa!!"

It was very noisy.

,More than 50 undead have fallen off the cliff because of you. It's time to get revenge! Kill him!!"

,Keueooooooo!"

The undead army began to charge towards the giant.

I had no doubt about the overwhelming victory of the undead army. No matter how powerful the giant was, he was just one. On the other hand, there are thousands on this side. There's no way he can overcome this overwhelming difference in numbers.

That's when it happened.

The giant's eye flashed and a red laser was fired, sweeping away the charging undead in a straight line. 80 undead evaporated in an instant.

,This is crazy."

The guy's eye flashes again.

I couldn't just stand by and watch this time. I pointed my finger at the guy's eye and used black magic.

,Dark Lightning!!"
