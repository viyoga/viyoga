My quarterfinal opponent was a hulking brute named Albert, a two-meter wall of muscle.

A man with filthy blond hair and a lower face completely swallowed by his beard.

He wore leather armor, and the weapon in his hand was a one-handed axe with runic letters carved into its blade.

"I am Albert. I've contracted with the Hefty Flash. I know you're one of the Sky Sovereign's contractors."

Thanks to the martial arts tournament, there were few left in Atlantis who didn't know my name or which god-throne I was contracted to.

"Never heard of you."

"I operate mainly in the northern district. I'll grant that the Empire is the heart of Atlantis, but don't assume every strongman is clustered within the Empire."

"Never assumed that. Just look at the Heavenly Demon in the west—proof enough that power exists beyond the Empire's borders. So is that all you wanted to say?"

"My god desires an honorable victory. He watches this battle even now, and I expect you to give your all as well."

「The Sky Sovereign desires victory.」

「The Sky Sovereign desires absolute victory!」

The Sky Sovereign fired off messages in rapid succession.

Albert's contracted god-throne, the Hefty Flash, had a true name: Thor. The thunder god famous from Norse mythology.

*'Zeus must be pissy that someone else presides over lightning the way he does.'*

Even without the Sky Sovereign nagging me, I had no intention of losing this match.

「Beginning the match start countdown.」

「3.」

「2.」

「1.」

「Begin.」

Albert hurled his one-handed axe. The axe, wreathed in blue lightning, came flying straight for my head.

Through the power of foresight, I already knew his move. Dodging would've been easy, but I activated Flash.

[Activating Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

My thoughts accelerate and the world slows to a crawl.

Albert had thrown the axe and was already charging at me simultaneously.

*'That axe isn't Mjölnir. But it also is Mjölnir.'*

Albert's god-throne skill, Mjölnir. A skill that imbues a weapon with the properties of Mjölnir. In a sense, it was similar to Astrafe.

Stealing control of the incoming Mjölnir by manipulating its lightning was impossible. Albert was not that weak.

*'Of course, when it comes to sheer lightning manipulation, he can't hold a candle to me.'*

And Albert knows that too. Despite his brutish appearance, he's the intelligent type—sharp where it counts.

*'Albert has no intention of deciding this with lightning.'*

I moved forward.

The distance between us closed in an instant. Albert swung a lightning-wreathed fist at me.

I swung Hwaryeon Bido, summoned from my inventory.

Jjeoooong!

The blade and fist never made contact. Before they could touch, refined mana collided in midair and the shockwave burst outward in all directions. His blue lightning and the red lightning reinforced through Hwaryeon Bido tangled and canceled each other out.

"That red lightning. You reinforced it through your sword?"

"And you drew runes on your palm to amplify your lightning. Playing the warrior while having a fox's brain."

"Haha. Caught me!"

Albert poured in more mana, raising his output. Under his increased force, my body was pushed back slightly.

「The Sky Sovereign is disappointed in you!」

Unlike Albert, I simply hadn't invested force, which was why my body was pushed back. The Sky Sovereign knew that perfectly well and was still throwing a fit.

*'From the start, Albert raising his output wasn't meant to push me away—it was to pin me down.'*

Mjölnir has the property of returning when thrown. The Mjölnir Albert hurled was currently flying back toward my head.

*'Lightning Speed won't work. Albert's axe is reinforced with runes.'*

Albert opened his mouth to draw more of my attention. And I opened mine faster.

"You know the future—"

"You know the future—huh?!"

Albert's eyes went wide, genuinely startled. I ducked my head in one clean motion. The lightning-wreathed Mjölnir sailed right over my skull. Albert hastily raised a hand and caught it.

"Astrafe."

KWA-KWA-KWA-KWANG!

A spear of lightning came crashing down from the sky, aimed at Albert's head. Albert swung Mjölnir and batted the lightning spear away.

KKAANG!

Albert's body was knocked back two steps.

"Krrrgh! This power...!"

It was an EX-rank Astrafe. Even used carelessly without preparation, it carried threatening force.

The Astrafe that hit the ground exploded. Blue lightning discharged in every direction, washing over Albert nearby. But Albert was also a contractor of the thunder god. His lightning resistance was considerable, so he wasn't electrocuted.

*'Well, I knew that. Against Albert, Astrafe is inefficient. Hwaryeon Bido is better.'*

Even so, I'd keep using Astrafe as my main tool.

There was one reason. To keep the Sky Sovereign happy.

*'Better to look good now so things are easier later.'*

I kept using Astrafe.

Spears of lightning rained down from the sky in rapid succession.

KWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAKWAK-BOOM!

Bolts falling from the heavens. And not just any bolts—these had mass. Albert, mid-swing with his axe, wore an expression of pure desperation.

"You can see the future, and this is how you fight?! Like a brute?!"

"Because I can see the future, I fight efficiently. I know exactly where and how to drop Astrafe to make you struggle."

"How arrogant! The moment you tire, I'll tear out your throat with my teeth!"

"Let me enlighten you—my stamina and mana stats are far higher than you think."

The Astrafe that had been falling one at a time began dropping in groups of three. And each one came on a trajectory that was nearly impossible to deflect.

KWAKWAKWAKWAKWAK-BOOM!

Three became five. Five became seven, all crashing down at once. His breathing grew ragged, but he managed to parry every single one.

*If I do nothing, he'll just keep deflecting. The moment he focuses, his field of vision narrows, and every time he knocks away an Astrafe, his sight and hearing wear down.*

I used Deception.

An Astrafe slipped through, perfectly deceiving his eyes and ears, and punched clean through Albert's thigh.

"Krrgh?!"

Albert didn't give up—he swung his axe, trying to knock the Astrafe away. But it was impossible. Losing proper use of one leg wasn't just about mobility. It meant he couldn't put proper force into his lower body. His physical balance collapsed in an instant.

Thk.

One of the Astrafes buried itself in his torso. Then another in his shoulder, and another in his chest.

They were clearly fatal wounds, yet Albert didn't fall. A hollow laugh escaped his lips.

"...You dropped that many spears of lightning and didn't even break a sweat. You're not just high-stat. And your skill rank—that's nothing to scoff at either. Every time I knocked away one of those lightning spears, I felt the strain pile up in my body. Mind if I ask what rank it is?"

How many beings were watching this scene? Like hell I'd tell him.

"Higher than you think."

The conversation had no point, so I ended it.

PENG!

The spears of lightning lodged in Albert's body exploded all at once.

「You have won the round of 8 in the Martial Arts Tournament finals.」

「The Lord of the Heavens is satisfied!」

「The Lord of the Heavens mocks the Heavy Flash!」

「The Heavy Flash congratulates you on your victory.」

Seems like in terms of personality—no, divinity—the Heavy Flash had won out.

• • •

「Beginning the match start countdown.」

「3.」

「2.」

「1.」

「Begin.」

The semifinals—the round of 4—had begun.

My opponent was a dark elf man with a slight, wiry build. A classic ranger type, wielding a dagger and a bow.

His name was Brif. He trembled as he looked at me.

"I-I've got a bad feeling about this..."

"Aren't you going to attack?"

I stared at him with a flat expression. Brif's trembling was all an act. He lures his opponent into letting their guard down, then strikes when the opening appears. If he were truly weak, he wouldn't have made it to the semifinals. On top of that, the moment he entered the arena, he'd scattered invisible traps all over the place. Playing the weakling while doing everything he needed to do.

Brif watched my reaction and activated a skill. A twenty-sided die appeared above his hand.

"P-please, Goddess! Grant me strength...!"

Rrroll.

The die tumbled.

With 10 as the baseline, higher numbers would inflate his stats, and lower numbers would drop them.

The die stopped.

The number was 1.

"Aaaaaaah!"

Brif screamed. The pressure radiating off him plummeted by more than half.

*Physical stats cut by 50%, huh.*

Brif grabbed the die once more.

"Th-this time, please weaken my opponent's abilities!"

Rrroll.

1.

「The power of fortune aids you.」

「All stats except Fortune increase by 50%.」

「Maintained for 10 minutes.」

He got stronger.

With astonishing ease.

"Hieeeeeeeek!"

Brif shrieked in terror. His legs seemed to give out as he collapsed onto the floor. Even now, using the power of foresight, I could tell it was an act.

"Looks like the Goddess of Fortune who contracted with you has abandoned you."

"Th-the Goddess of Fortune is a fair and just being. And in this world, fortune has clear standards, so... C-could I perhaps ask what your Fortune stat is...?"

"Do I look like I'd tell you?"

"Th-Three digits?"

“…….”

I didn't answer. Brif's face went pale. Hard to tell on a face that black, but it showed.

Since Luck can't be raised except through special means, there shouldn't be anyone in Atlantis with higher Luck than me. The dark elf in front of me is fifty at best. Even with every skill and item buff stacked, he won't break seventy.

My current Luck stat, on the other hand, is 125. For reference, the higher a stat gets, the bigger the gap a single point makes. The difference between 99 and 100 is far larger than the difference between 98 and 99.

"F-Finally, I'll wager my life, Goddess! Please…!"

I did nothing. Of course I didn't—I'd already seen the future. If I do nothing, there are no variables.

Brif didn't have the kind of Luck that could change the future.

Brif threw the die of fate.

Rattle, rattle.

1.

"Kaaagh!"

Brif spewed a fountain of blood from his mouth. The price of failing a gamble staked on his life—his heart had been destroyed.

'Apart from the gods, no one can touch me through Luck.'

Thanks to Brif, I could be certain of that.

「You have won the semifinals of the Martial Arts Tournament.」

All that's left is the final match.

My opponent is Kang Myeongjin.
