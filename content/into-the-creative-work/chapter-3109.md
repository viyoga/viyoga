I sat in the room, staring at the monitor screen. It showed the Gangneung coastline and the sea.

Soon, the Japanese forces would be coming across the water.

Led by a variant that resembled a massive sperm whale, dozens of variants were approaching in a wide formation. At the center, I could see Japanese soldiers riding on something that might have been a raft or might have been a plank. The planks and the variants were connected.

'Just eyeballing it, there must be tens of thousands of them... To think they'd plan a troop transport operation using such a crude method.'

Not even ships—planks. When the sea surged, a soldier at the edge tumbled into the water. Yet the Japanese forces didn't pause for even a second.

'I can tell from how they treat their soldiers. Clone troops, then.'

Cheap clone soldiers, stamped out of a factory. They were probably brainwashed the moment they were cultured as clones.

Even if it wasn't Oneko, Japan seemed to have quite a few brainwashing ability users.

'There might be Out-of-Place Artifacts or locations in Japan that induce awakening through brainwashing ability users, just like in the original work.'

In the sky on the screen, Korean and Japanese fighter jets were flying about, engaging in aerial combat. Bombs were exploding here and there. The advantage was currently Japan's. Their air power included variants, after all.

I spoke into the communicator connected to Lee Yeonhee.

"Lee Yeonhee. I authorize the use of artillery shells and missiles. Reduce their numbers as much as possible before they reach Gangneung."

"Yes. Your Excellency."

Within minutes, shells poured down toward the enemy.

The whale-like variant at the very front of the enemy forces suddenly leaped upward, opened its mouth, and let out a monstrous scream. The shockwave pulverized the coastline and sent the sea heaving violently.

The pouring shells and missiles detonated midair.

The variant's howl came through the speakers, but the sound was automatically adjusted, so there was no damage to my ears.

"Lee Yeonhee. Are you unharmed?"

Lee Yeonhee was in the Gangneung area.

"...Yes. Your Excellency. I am unharmed. I blocked it with a spatial barrier before the shockwave reached me."

"They said it was Grade 4. So it's true."

Korea's variant classification system.

Grade 1, or Level 1, can be handled by a platoon-sized armed force.

Grade 2 requires a battalion-sized armed force.

Grade 3 requires a single knight order.

Grade 4 requires legion-scale power.

Grade 5 is a monster that can only be described as a natural disaster, one that puts the very existence of a nation on the line.

"Yes. It's more powerful than expected. The recent shockwave annihilated the coastal artillery batteries. We need more time for our forces to assemble in Gangneung. It seems difficult to engage them at the coast."

"Let me say this in advance. I will not be going to Gangneung. You'll have to handle it."

"A wise decision. The situation is somewhat unfavorable, but the national military's forces alone are sufficient to respond."

It wouldn't look good for me to move personally just because something big happened. There's such a thing as dignity. A head of state shouldn't be running around recklessly.

"Keep firing the artillery. Even if it doesn't deal meaningful damage, it'll tire that bastard out and slow their march."

Every single shell costs money, but now isn't the time to be stingy. I can afford to give up Gangneung, but nothing beyond that.

"Yes. Your Excellency."

"By the way, Japan's military power is impressive. To think they can utilize dozens of variants including a Grade 4. How is that even possible?"

"There are individual differences among variants, but they are monsters that evolve over time.... They must have artificially created variants and brainwashed them from when they were weak, artificially inducing their evolution. Seeing that Oneko is on top of that variant, it seems certain they've been brainwashed."

"...Oneko is there?"

"Yes. Standing proudly atop the whale variant. It seems she's secured herself using special equipment. Can you not see her?"

I glared at the screen. But there was nothing on top of the whale variant in the footage.

"I don't see anything."

"...Please wait a moment."

A brief silence passed, then Lee Yeonhee spoke.

"I ordered my subordinates to verify. Oneko was not captured on camera. I belatedly realized that some of the information recorded about Oneko had also been lost. It seems most information related to Oneko is being manipulated."

That's quite a remarkable ability.

But wasn't Oneko's ability brainwashing? Even if she's an esper, can someone really possess two completely different types of supernatural powers?

'If it's not a supernatural power... then the cat mask is an Out-of-Place Artifact.'

The possibility was considerable.

If it weren't an Out-of-Place Artifact, there'd be no reason to wear that suffocating mask every single day.

'Oneko's influence over Japan is enormous. Yet until now, there's been strangely little information related to Oneko. If the reason lies with an Out-of-Place Artifact, it makes sense.'

Oneko is in Gangneung.

I licked my lips. My intuition is telling me. Oneko is a considerable beauty. I want to rush to Gangneung myself and capture Oneko.

"...Capture Oneko alive."

"I'll try my utmost."

I asked the AI, Jenna.

"Jenna. Can you find Oneko through sensors or cameras connected to you?"

"...Confirmed. I cannot find the entity called Oneko. It is not detected. Analyzing footage. I have discovered traces of pixel distortion in the area where the entity named Oneko was located."

Information manipulation capable of deceiving an AI. Even if it's an incredible ability, the fact that it leaves traces means it's not perfect.

'At least I found out now. If I'd relied solely on machines, I might have suffered a major setback.'

The Japanese forces that arrived at the Gangneung coast began marching without any establishment of positions or the like. Behind them, Japanese reinforcements continued to pour in endlessly.

'Isn't this too reckless an offensive? ...No. These bastards know too. That if not now, they'll lose air superiority.'

The moment they lose air superiority, Japan's opportunity to invade the Korean Peninsula disappears. Even if they try to cross the sea route using variants like now, they'd only become prey for fighter jets.

'Oneko, the supreme leader, has stepped forward personally. This offensive is Japan's do-or-die gambit.'

Right now it's millions, but counting all the reinforcements following behind the Japanese forces, tens of millions of troops will arrive on the Korean Peninsula.

'They're advancing straight toward Seoul.'

A mass offensive that even China would struggle to demonstrate. At its forefront are Oneko and the Grade 4 variant.

The Korean and Japanese forces clash.

There is no dialogue.

Bullets and shells pour indiscriminately toward each other's forces.

Korea's robot weapons including the Land Spider charge forward, while Japan's variants roar and enter the battlefield.

Korea's armored forces unleash fire. But the mobility of the variant legion was something armored forces couldn't match.

The variants devour Japanese clone soldiers, recovering energy in real time and beginning to evolve. Japan's meat shields weren't the variants—they were the clone soldiers.

Korea's Holy Knight Orders also played no small role. The Siren Knight Order used their awakening abilities to buff allies and debuff enemies. The Atonement Knight Order, composed of criminals, fought and died on the most dangerous fronts under the orders of the AI Jenna.

And Korea's front line was being pushed back.

'That Grade 4 whale variant is the problem. A monster that walks on land without issue despite being a whale, because it has dozens of legs under its body. And this bastard doesn't even get tired?'

The whale variant became a wall on the battlefield with its massive body. Whenever firepower from shells and missiles concentrated on it, it would scream and block the bombardment with shockwaves.

Ordinary anti-tank missiles couldn't even pierce the surface of the Grade 4 whale variant. At best, thermobaric bombs of over 1 ton dealt meaningful damage to the whale variant. Even then, the whale variant recovered instantly with its monstrous regeneration.

Watching through the screen, it was an undying, immortal monster.

'Should I step in myself?'

No. An enemy of this level should be manageable without me.

"Lee Yeonhee. Can we target Oneko? The reason Oneko is attached to that variant is probably to brainwash it in real time. If we just remove Oneko, the battle should become easier."

"I confirmed that Oneko entered the whale's body during combat. It's impossible to identify and kill only Oneko during battle. Sniping won't work either."

"That's troublesome."

A monster single-handedly absorbing 80% of the Korean military's firepower. Extremely annoying.

"Can we send the Atonement Knight Order inside that whale variant's body?"

"Creating a wound and entering the body is impossible. The body regenerates before they can even get in. I'll order them to enter through the mouth."

The whale variant didn't open its mouth. But when the knights kept attacking its lips, it eventually opened its mouth to chew and eat the knights. The knights didn't dodge—they went in on purpose.

"Kyaaaaaaaaaaah!"

They were pulverized, power armor and all, by the hundreds of thousands of teeth inside the mouth.

Inside the grinder-like teeth that resembled rotary saw blades, there was something even more shocking.

"...Your Excellency."

Lee Yeonhee's voice trembled as she watched the final footage sent by the Atonement Knight Order.

"Yes. I saw it. I was inside that monster's throat."

My eyes sank deep.

Me, naked, dozens of me, were bound to the flesh walls inside that monster's throat.

"Those Japanese bastards. Looks like they made a shitload of my clones. I'd anticipated this to some degree, but... confirming it like this pisses me off."

"...That explains the monster's mysterious vitality."

"Those clones are probably using their regeneration ability to restore the whale variant's stamina and wounds. I can see dozens, but... there must be at least hundreds inside that monster."

Biological batteries. No—it's carrying a bellyful of biological healing potions. Having confirmed that the enemy doesn't tire, I immediately scrapped the strategy of dragging this into a war of attrition.

Two strategies surfaced in my mind.

I hesitated for a moment.

"......Lee Yeonhee."

"Yes. Your Excellency."

"I've thought of two strategies. One is to use nuclear weapons, which I'd hoped to avoid using as much as possible in this war. If we land 4 to 5 nuclear missiles, even that monster will eventually die."

This would also allow Japan to use nuclear weapons. The primary objective of the Silence Gate Knight Order that infiltrated Japan was to neutralize and seize nuclear weapons, but there was no information confirming the mission's success.

Even if we could intercept Japan's nuclear weapons, it wouldn't be 100%. Two or three would land on the Korean Peninsula. The probability of them hitting Seoul couldn't be ignored.

"I prefer the second one."

"I haven't even told you yet."

"I teleport into that monster's body and detonate a tactical nuclear bomb. Isn't that it?"

"...I don't particularly like stories where my woman sacrifices herself."

"Your Excellency. Strictly speaking, this isn't a sacrifice. You can revive me, so this is merely an operation. In the past, Korea's great figures threw away their lives like grass for their homeland. Compared to them, I'm not even truly losing my life. I will carry out the operation. Long live the Republic of Korea."

The communication with Lee Yeonhee cut off.

And about 20 seconds later.

KWAaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

The body of the Grade 4 whale variant that had been advancing toward Seoul exploded. Blood and viscera launched into the sky rained down onto the ground with wet splats.

There was no mushroom cloud that would normally accompany a nuclear explosion. Perhaps because it detonated inside the monster's body, the explosive force escaping outward was diminished.

Japan's variants suddenly went berserk, attacking indiscriminately without distinguishing friend from foe. Some of Japan's clone soldiers became dazed. They sat down on the ground or fired their guns at allies.

'The brainwashing was undone. With Oneko—the caster and core of the brainwashing—dying, the ability was released.'

The Japanese forces were thrown into chaos in an instant.

'If the Japanese military's forces are like this... there's a high probability that the Japanese mainland has also fallen into chaos.'

I gave Jenna an order.

"Jenna. Inform all soldiers and knights. Firepower deployment is prohibited until Oneko's corpse fragments are collected. Only rifle-caliber rounds are permitted."

It would be easier for the war to just sweep them away with firepower while we have the chance, but I need to obtain Oneko. Her ability alone is worth it.

"Understood. Master."

Jenna carried out my order without complaint.

I moved to Seongsimdang and revived Lee Yeonhee.
