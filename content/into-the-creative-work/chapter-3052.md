I spoke as I stepped out of the lake.

"What you're describing sounds like the Heavenly Slaughter Star... but I'm not the Heavenly Slaughter Star. Seems you've mistaken me for someone else."

"No, I saw correctly. You are not the Heavenly Slaughter Star, but could you not also become some other star entirely?"

I stared at Taegongmang without blinking.

From what I knew, even if those bearing the power of the Heavenly Slaughter Star or the Purple Micro Star had appeared several times in this world, there had never been anyone in history who possessed the power of the Heavenly Gang Star—except me.

"How did you know I'm the Heavenly Gang Star? Today should be the first time we've met."

"I can read a fraction of the Heavenly Mechanism. Does that answer your question?"

"You can learn things like that through the Heavenly Mechanism? How convenient. Can you also tell when I'm going to die?"

"The ignorant fool themselves into thinking that if one can read the Heavenly Mechanism, one can know anything. But a single being cannot comprehend the heavens. At best, one steals a glimpse of a fragment. Moreover, the current heaven is not heaven. Even if you luckily read a portion of the Heavenly Mechanism, certainty is difficult."

"Says the man who met me thanks to that very Heavenly Mechanism."

"I left my avatar in a mystic realm spanning ten thousand li. That you and I met today is both the inevitability of one who prepared, and coincidence."

"From the looks of it, you weren't lying in wait to kill me. What do you want from me? It was unnecessary help, but since I received your assistance and made it out of this lake, I'll hear you out for now."

"Haha. I'm grateful. What I want from you is simply conversation. A moment's time is enough. More is fine too. If you're curious about anything, ask. If it's something I can answer, I will."

A conversation with Taegongmang.

Objectively speaking, this was an absurd stroke of fortune. Taegongmang's status was that of a disciple of the Primordial Heavenly Venerable. Even the Jade Emperor could not disregard someone of such standing.

"Ah. Let me say this in advance. Do not speak of my master. Especially—do not let that one's name pass your lips, even by accident."

"Why. Does something happen if you say it? What, is it like some being whose name must not be spoken?"

"In the mortal realm, speaking the name alone would be fine, but this temporarily constructed world is too close to the Immortal Realm. The mere utterance of that name would make us known."

"And if that being takes notice, what happens?"

"Death."

"...Just me?"

"Both of us."

Wasn't Taegongmang the beloved disciple of the Primordial Heavenly Venerable? Yet if the Primordial Heavenly Venerable noticed, he would kill him? This was not a normal situation.

"What is the reason he would want to kill you and me?"

"To him, your existence is a variable. And a variable on the unfavorable side. Eliminating and excluding it is the best course. I am currently a traitor to the Immortal Realm. My main body is hidden where the Immortal Realm's eyes cannot reach."

"I knew it, but it seems the Immortal Realm is no Peach Blossom Spring."

"The Immortal Realm is no different from the mortal world. In my personal view, the Immortal Realm is a more vicious place than the mortal world."

I fell silent for a moment, choosing my words, then asked Taegongmang.

"What exactly is this mystic realm? Is it connected to the Immortal Realm?"

"Mystic realms are created by the Immortal Realm and sent down to the mortal world. The immortals of the Immortal Realm claim they are bestowing mercy upon the ignorant masses."

"Mystic realms aren't naturally occurring? That's shocking."

Truthfully, I wasn't particularly surprised.

And the Emperor probably knew this truth. Every mystic realm that appeared in the Empire was under the Emperor's management.

"For something like that, your face is rather calm. Naturally occurring mystic realms do exist as well. Though the probability is extremely slim."

"I know that just because they're called immortals doesn't mean they're benevolent. They wouldn't give anything away without a price. What is the purpose of creating mystic realms and scattering them across the mortal world?"

"It depends on your perspective. An investment to recruit new immortals. Feed to fatten livestock."

"The latter, then."

"The way you state it with certainty suggests you know something of the Immortal Realm's ecology."

"I know that when a mortal cultivator ascends to the Immortal Realm, they become a slave to the immortals. Even if that weren't the case, those in power would never want new powers to rise."

But there was one thing I didn't understand.

The deliberate enslavement of ascenders.

People are born in the Immortal Realm too. If slaves were needed, couldn't they simply enslave weaker immortals?

I asked Taegongmang the question that had surfaced.

"Natural-born immortals and ascenders are of different caliber. Ascenders lose their power immediately after ascension, but with the Immortal Realm's vast spiritual energy—incomparable to the mortal world—they rapidly recover their strength and reach the stage of Earth Immortal."

"So it's kicking away the ladder, in the end. But if that's the case, why bother creating mystic realms and scattering them in the mortal world?"

"Because ascenders are of high caliber. In and of themselves, they make excellent spiritual creatures."

"...Spiritual creatures? Immortals devour immortals?"

This time, I was genuinely shocked.

When Taegongmang mentioned livestock, it wasn't a mere metaphor—the immortals of the Immortal Realm truly treated mortals as livestock.

"Mortals devour spiritual creatures too, do they not? And it's not only devouring. Some immortals' immortal arts use fellow immortals as sacrificial offerings."

"This isn't the Immortal Realm—it's a demon realm."

I said it deliberately, with sarcasm.

"Is the mortal world any different?"

It was true, and I couldn't refute it.

"Are all immortals in the Immortal Realm like that?"

"How could they be? However, most immortals currently holding key positions do not regard the mortal world or ascenders with much importance."

"Currently? Was it different in the past?"

"Before the Great Purge, it was still a place worthy of being called the Immortal Realm. But after the Great Purge occurred, everything changed. The Supreme Emperor submitted, the Lord Lao vanished, the Mysterious Maiden shut herself away. The Queen Mother followed the tide, and—ah."

Kkudeudeuk.

A chilling sound, like something being seized and torn away, came from Taegongmang. I focused and examined him. An unidentifiable force was attacking him.

"What attacked you?"

"A prohibition. If one carelessly spills information about the Immortal Realm, heaven imposes a prohibition."

"Heaven can't see us here. And we're inside a mystic realm, no less."

"You know that heaven is the world itself, don't you? It's only ending at this level because I'm using my own measures. Even so, speaking of the great immortals' deeds is still difficult."

"...Enough. Don't mention it. I have no particular interest in the great immortals' deeds. Even if I knew them, there's nothing I could do. What do you want from me?"

"I don't want you to die suddenly. Judging by your temperament, it seems you'll someday kill everything—Immortal Realm and all."

"You have a good eye for people."

"Still, with your current self, it's impossible. Ah, and the mystic realm's administrator must be killed with the power of Heavenly Slaughter. It's for your sake."

"...Mystic realms have administrators?"

"One of the duties of low-ranking immortals is managing mystic realms. The larger the mystic realm, the higher the administrator's rank... In this mystic realm, an Earth Immortal—Namhwa Nosun—is managing it. Administrators fundamentally do not interfere inside the mystic realm. A mystic realm opened in the mortal world is but the mortal world, so interfering requires no small amount of power."

"How is killing him to my benefit?"

"Administrators can know a portion of what happens inside the mystic realm. When the mystic realm closes and he returns to the Immortal Realm, he will report to the higher-ups: that there is a human with the eyes of a monstrous monkey and the stars. For the Immortal Realm's eyes to be on you now... it's too early."

Certainly, drawing the Immortal Realm's attention was not good.

"He needs to die, then. Do you know where he is?"

"Namhwa Nosun, among immortals, especially enjoys meddling in the mortal world. If you go up to the Lake of Heaven, you'll naturally encounter him."

"Now that I think of it, this is the Lake of Earth. Are there no spiritual creatures?"

"The lakes have no spiritual creatures. The lakes themselves can be called training facilities meant to grant enlightenment."

"Enlightenment? Through mere hallucinations?"

"The Lake of Benevolence lets you experience a human's life. The Lake of Earth lets you experience an animal's life. The Lake of Heaven lets you experience a god's life. It's a facility meant to indirectly grant enlightenment on reincarnation... but you rejected the hallucination. How fitting for a slaughtering star that kills reincarnation."

"Damn it. No spiritual creatures? What a waste. There's someone who entered the lake before me. Where is he now?"

"He experienced the Lake of Earth and moved to the Lake of Heaven. Most likely, he's making a deal with Namhwa Nosun."

"...A deal?"

"Namhwa Nosun is greedy. Returning to the Immortal Realm and merely reporting your existence won't be enough merit in his eyes. If he kills you here, he can accumulate far more merit... Why wouldn't he? Since acting directly is burdensome, he'll use that man to try to kill you."

"Ha. Just what I wanted. But is it alright to kill Namhwa Nosun here? Won't the Immortal Realm notice?"

"Once is fine. The immortals of the Immortal Realm have a somewhat different sense of time. Even if Namhwa Nosun dies, they won't care for at least three hundred years. If you're lucky, they won't even realize it at all. Namhwa Nosun's position is merely that of a mystic realm administrator."

"Do you know how to get to the Lake of Heaven? Surely I don't have to jump into that lake and go through that bullshit again?"

"I will open the path to the Lake of Heaven for you."

Taegongmang swung his fishing rod toward the empty air. The hook scraped through the void. A crack formed in the empty air, then tore apart with a zzjeok. The path to the Lake of Heaven opened.

"Any more questions?"

"Are you able to answer?"

Taegongmang's body was collapsing. From the moment he spoke Namhwa Nosun's name, his right eye had vanished and his left arm had turned to dust. Even now, his entire lower body below the waist was disappearing as if being shaved away.

"I am here to answer."

"What is Namhwa Nosun's power?"

"The Art of Supreme Peace. With those eyes of yours, you should be able to handle him without difficulty."

His right arm collapsed.

"Was Dalki beautiful?"

"She was lovely. So much so that the mortal world trembled like a candle flame at that temptress's gesture. You, who are with her descendant, can guess to some extent, can you not?"

"You even know that. Miryeong is quite pretty, yes."

I walked into the crack. I did not exchange any farewell with Taegongmang.

Behind me, Taegongmang's singing voice could be heard.

"There were three paths. The first path became the world, the second path rolled the world. The third path discussed all things, and now it discusses the world."
