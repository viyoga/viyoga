Cheonma's Return.

I set foot in the 5,146th District, the Scorpion Desert.

The endlessly stretched golden desert greeted me.

I looked straight ahead. The metropolis, the heart of the 5,146th District, the Scorpion Desert, was shining brightly. Reltia had an oasis.

That oasis is Reltia's growth engine. In this arid desert, Reltia is a place where water can be obtained stably.

As I walked forward, I glanced back. Kasha, Amar, Princess Yonggil, Okjeong, and Ercia were following behind me.

Each had a different expression and atmosphere.

Kasha is wearing a cool smile. I can feel the murderous intent from the corners of her lips drawing an arc.

Amar's face was stiff. He's feeling guilty because he couldn't manage the desert while I was gone.

Princess Yonggil's face is full of annoyance. It's because I forcibly dragged her out of the pyramid.

Okjeong is nervous. She's smart. She's anticipating what's going to happen.

Ercia's eyes are sparkling. Ercia hardly ever leaves the pyramid. Even when she does, she only goes to safe places with Kasha. This is her first time in a metropolis.

,Kasha. Didn't you say that the Cheonma Shingyo was on the verge of falling into the hands of the rebels?"

,This is Reltia. It's not the Cheonma Shingyo."

,......."

She's right, so I had nothing to say. Cheonma Shingyo isn't the name of a city, but the name of an organization.

,Most of the rebels are in the outer regions of Reltia. They're gathering strength while avoiding our eyes. In fact, we had no idea what those bastards were doing until two months ago."

,I'm sorry, Cheonma-nim. It's because I'm lacking."

,Ah, oppa. It can't be helped. Even we can't monitor this damn wide desert."

,...There were signs before that. If I had paid more attention... I could have suppressed it early."

,I'm telling you again, it's not oppa's fault. Karok! That son of a bitch was so meticulous!"

I intervened between my siblings.

,Stop it and explain the situation in Reltia. Who knows it better?"

,I mainly tracked Karok while traveling outside of Reltia. Kasha knows the internal situation of Reltia well now."

Kasha glared at Reltia for a moment before opening her mouth.

,It's a mess. After you disappeared, Karok betrayed oppa and started a rebellion, all sorts of bastards started running wild as if they had been waiting for it. Especially the wealthy. Those bastards are aiming for the oasis and are playing all sorts of tricks."

,Tricks like?"

,They incited the citizens. Cheonma abandoned Reltia. Cheonma is dead. Cheonma will not return. Reltia needs a new leader. The one who is suitable for that leader is... the same rumors were circulating in Reltia. The rebels also used that incitement."

,It seems the incitement was successful. But Reltia looks fine for that."

,The Cheonma Shingyo's military power is still intact. They're somehow protecting the palace and the oasis. They're appeasing the citizens by providing water from the oasis, but... there was a limit to that. The wealthy who had joined hands split up. The discord between the tribes and the greed of the wealthy held back the rebels. It was lucky for us."

,What's the internal situation now?"

,It's precarious. It's a situation right before a bomb explodes. If rebels with military power attack, it will be captured immediately. Well, that's the story when you're not here."

I nodded.

I don't know what she's talking about, but what I have to do doesn't change. Kill all the guys I don't like, the guys who dare to resist me.

I narrowed my eyes when I saw the entrance to Reltia.

,Kasha. What are those?"

Four men were standing at the entrance with swords at their waists. Soldiers.

As far as I know, all of Reltia's soldiers belong to the Cheonma Shingyo. But those soldiers don't look like they belong to the Cheonma Shingyo. That's because the Cheonma Shingyo people basically wear black clothes.

Kasha frowned.

,They're vigilantes."

,Vigilantes? Where did the Magyo people go and those bastards are guarding the entrance?"

,Many Gyo people died in the last rebellion. So, the combat force is insufficient. There's also a unit assigned to the task of tracking Karok... There's a shortage of manpower to protect Reltia. The citizens formed a vigilante group for that reason."

,Looking at the situation in Reltia now, it's unlikely that the vigilante group was voluntarily created by the citizens. Who's behind it?"

,I'm guessing there are rebels and wealthy people."

,Guessing?"

,I haven't secured any evidence yet. It would be more accurate to say that there's no manpower to do so."

,Why didn't you just get rid of them?"

,They're voluntarily taking up swords to protect the city and fight against the rebels.... There's no justification for stopping the vigilante group."

,Indeed, justification."

,Control yourself. If you mess with the vigilante group, the citizens will rise up. There's no need to add fuel to the already uneasy public sentiment."

,Kasha. I am Cheonma."

,Uh, yeah. You're Cheonma."

,And Cheonma doesn't care about justification."

I approached the entrance. A vigilante soldier stepped forward. They were shocked to see me.

,Ch, Cheonma!"

Their hands reflexively went to the swords hanging on their waists, but they didn't grab them. They immediately split to the sides and cleared the way.

,Y, you've returned, Cheonma-nim! We welcome Cheonma-nim's return... Keoeok?!"

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Flying Claw (Heavenly Demon Flying Claw)

This Atlantis is not peaceful. The weak will either die or be exploited.
