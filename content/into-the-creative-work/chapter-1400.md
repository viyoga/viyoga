Dark night.

I walked behind Hae Seon-mu-ssi.

Originally, going outside the Dormitory at this late hour is against the school rules, but hot-blooded students sometimes lightly ignore the rules.

,It's like being where a necromancer is. The surroundings are disgustingly gloomy.'

It was on the outskirts of downtown.

As expected of Gangwon-do, the nature was lush. Streetlights were not easily visible. Hae Seon-mu-ssi was heading to an abandoned house on the hill.

"Is it there?"

"Uh, yeah. It's there. It's my first time going to find him too... I didn't know it would be this dark."

Hae Seon-mu-ssi turned on the light with his smartphone and walked forward.

,It's different from the original. I don't know the exact location in the original, but I don't think it was an abandoned house.'

I arrived at the abandoned house.

Kkiiiiiik.

The door of the abandoned house opens.

What came out of it was a man wearing black clothes. He covered his body, arms, legs, and even his hair with black clothes, and only stuck his body out. He was like a No-Face from some cartoon movie.

"Hae Seon-mu-ssi. You came with someone else. Is that person also a customer?"

The necromancer asked me in a monotone voice. His empty pupils were very unsettling.

"Do you have any good stuff?"

"I have as many talismans as you want, like necromantic charms. It's 30 million won."

"That's different from what I heard. This guy said he bought a necromantic charm for 20 million won."

"It's popular, so the price has gone up. That's how the market works. If you don't have money, get lost."

"Are you going to come out like this?"

"Are you going to report me to the Hero Association or something? I've already finished preparing to leave this place. The Hero Association can't catch me."

"Okay. My bad. Do you have anything else?"

"I have a few things. But I don't think you, who doesn't even have the money to buy a necromantic charm, will buy anything else. Get lost now."

I took out the bag I had brought as if I had no choice. I opened the bag in front of the necromancer. It was full of 50,000 won bills.

"I brought 200 million, so show me something? You can't sell it openly to others anyway."

"...200 million. In cash. You do have money. Come in."

The man went inside the house. I followed him without hesitation, and Hae Seon-mu-ssi followed, trembling with anxiety.

As soon as I crossed the door, I smelled a strange smell. In a way, it was a familiar smell.

,The smell of rotting corpses.'

I don't know if it's human or animal. What's certain is that a corpse is rotting somewhere in this old house.

,That necromancer isn't saying anything. Is he showing off that he's a dangerous person?'

It worked. Hae Seon-mu-ssi's face was now dark.

Kkiiiiiik.

The necromancer opened an old door.

"It's here."

At the same time, the necromancer's mana moved. It quickly takes over the surrounding space. A smile appeared on the necromancer's expressionless face. Something grayish was visible on his shoulder.

『You have been cursed (A). All stats are reduced by 20%.』

It was a nasty feeling of something invisible grabbing and pulling my body.

"Keueoeok! Eok! Eoeoeok! M-my body... eoeok!"

Hae Seon-mu-ssi knelt on the floor. He opened his mouth and focused on breathing. It seemed like it was hard to even breathe because of the curse.

"You have curse resistance? You look like the same age as that guy... impressive."

"What is this? Is this how you treat a guest?"

"Guest? You're not a guest. Because I didn't acknowledge you."

"Looks like you don't want the money."

"You stupid bastard.... If I kill you, the 200 million becomes mine. If I can get an additional 200 million... I can finally liquidate this tiresome job and live like a normal person...."

His goal was money.

It was a bit anticlimactic. It would have been more exciting if it was for reasons like human experimentation.

I handed him the money bag.

"I'll give you the money. Save me."

"......I can't let you guys live. If I let you go alive here, you'll run to the association and report me.... I hate being chased."

"That's too much. I'm asking you to save me."

The necromancer narrowed his eyes. He looked at me as if he couldn't understand.

"That's not the tone and voice of someone begging for their life. Are you daring to joke right now?"

"Did you just realize that? You fucking idiot."

I kicked off the floor and ran towards him. A fist wrapped in lightning strikes at his solar plexus.

Bang!

My fist was blocked by a barrier. I put more strength into my fist. The barrier breaks. He had already distanced himself and escaped my fist.

"To shatter my barrier so easily.... You're quite something."

"Now that I know how hard I need to hit, there won't be a next time."

I lifted my leg to approach him. It didn't go up. Something was holding my leg. When I looked down to check, several rotten hands that had risen from the floor were tightly grabbing my calf.

Pajik.

I created a little lightning. Blue electricity burns the rotten hands.

I turned my gaze back to him. The grayish thing on his shoulder was entering his chest.

I knew he had used necromancy. The rotten hands were just a ploy to buy time for the necromancy.

"Do you know what the biggest advantage of necromancy is?"

"I'm a spiritist, so I know a little. Is it being able to control ghosts?"

"Hehe. That's right. Being able to control ghosts is also an advantage of necromancy. But the true power of necromancy is... being able to become anything with the power of ghosts. You can become a swordsman who dominated an era, or a forgotten magician."

His body swelled up. It was muscle. His muscles grew at least three times larger. The suddenly enlarged muscles looked like a monster.

"Looks like you can become a muscle monster too. I'm not particularly jealous."

"Hehe. These muscles are Volpre's muscles. I put Volpre's soul into my body... and became Volpre."

"Who's Volpre?"

"Are you saying you don't know the genius Kwon-sa who made a name for himself in Australia 200 years ago?! You ignorant bastard!"

"Shibal. How the fuck am I supposed to know if it's Volpre or Mulpre? He doesn't even seem that famous...."

"You goddamn bastard! How dare you insult my ancestor?! I'll grab your soul, torture it, and then annihilate it!"

His tone suddenly became ignorant. He also sounded slurred. It seemed like a side effect of necromancy.

I looked at him with my spirit eyes and realized what the problem was.

,The soul that was necromanced into the body has taken more control of the body.'

In simple terms, the necromancer had not completely controlled the ghost.

The necromancer, who had become a muscle man, approached me. The muscles were so ridiculous that they were repulsive, but his gait was that of a martial artist.

,Ksana.'

[Using Ksana (刹那). Remaining stacks: 6]

I moved first before things got annoying. I approached him immediately. Before he could raise his fist, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido in the air, grabbed the hilt, and swung it down. The necromancer's left arm was cut off.

"Kuaaaaaak!"

Unlike the painful screams, his body reacted like a master of martial arts. He rotated his body, put strength into his leg, and threw a side kick.

,This is hard to avoid. I don't want to get hit because it'll hurt if I do. Ksana.'

[Using Ksana (刹那). Remaining stacks: 5]

I dodged the side kick with my momentarily sped-up body and swung my knife. Seogeok! I cut off his leg. The body of the guy who lost his balance falls.

[Using Ksana (刹那). 4 stacks remaining]

Before he completely fell, I swung Hwaryeon Bido again. His remaining arms and legs were cut off.

"Eueueueueu...."

The necromancer, who had lost all his limbs, looked at me with fear-filled eyes. The soul that had been necromanced into his body escaped and disappeared. The monstrous muscles also disappeared.

"Game over, right?"

"Kill me...."

"Answer my questions well. Then I'll kill you comfortably."

"......."

He looked up at me. He stared into my eyes for a few seconds, then nodded as if he had given up on something.

"I've seen guys like you a few times.... I also know that it's best to cooperate as much as possible. Ask me. I'll answer everything...."

"A cooperative attitude! I like that very much. I promise I'll kill you cleanly."

"......."

"First question. Why are you here?"

"......The area near the Academy is heavily guarded. No matter how daring a guy is, he has no choice but to hold his breath near the Academy. The moment you touch the Academy... the association and the country will actively step in."

"My guess is that you ran away from somewhere. Where are you from?"

"China."

"Guirak Valley?"

"Looks like you know Guirak Valley. Yes. I ran away from Guirak Valley."

"Why?"

"Guirak Valley has begun to change. That place is not the Guirak Valley it used to be.... I didn't want to die as a consumable for those bastards. No, even if I die, I won't really die. The Guirak Valley bastards... catch ghosts and use them as slaves."

"Hmm. It wasn't as big of a deal as I thought."

Puk!

I stabbed the knife into his heart and got up.

"Heok!"

Hae Seon-mu-ssi gasped. I quietly looked at him. Hae Seon-mu-ssi trembled and crawled on the floor, wondering what I was thinking.

"Hae Seon-mu-ssi."

"S-save me! I won't tell anyone what I saw here!"

"What are you talking about? Report it to the association. Don't say the details, just say you accidentally found this place. Don't even mention the deal."

"I, I know that much. Because I was the one who made the deal first...."

"Ah, tell your homeroom teacher first, not the association. Then you'll be able to get help."

Seeing Hae Seon-mu-ssi take out his smartphone, I looked around the room. There were quite a few bizarre objects, but I didn't even glance at the useless ones. There was only one thing I wanted.

,Found it.'

A shabby cloak hanging on the wall.

I carefully reached out towards the cloak.

『Ghost Cloak

Rank: S

Conceals appearance and presence.』

A whopping S-rank item!

It seemed like an excessively simple ability for an S-rank, but that also meant that the ability was outstanding.

,If I have this, I can raid several special dungeons as if I'm eating them raw.'

I was very satisfied.
