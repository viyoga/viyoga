,I have to stop him.'

Hase gritted his teeth, glaring at Seong Yujin, who was about to detonate himself by triggering the Phoenix's Heart on the ground.

He absolutely had to stop him. That completed Phoenix's Heart was a treasure among treasures. Literally a perpetual motion machine that could generate energy forever with just a few conditions. It wasn't just that the Phoenix's Heart was special. He had poured a full five years into creating a Heart in that state. And it was something he could never get his hands on again.

So he absolutely had to stop him. He had to stop him, but he couldn't.

,Those stupid yokai bastards...!'

Hase was suppressing the yokai. His and Baekrim's goal was the development of Japan. If he released these guys now? No matter how strong the barrier and ice wall set up by S-class Hunters Koyori and Han Ah-young were, they would be destroyed in an instant, and the yokai would spread throughout Japan as if returning to their hometowns. And they would cause problems that couldn't even be compared to monsters.

On the one hand, he also thought it was a good thing. If he could wipe out these uncontrollable yokai all at once.

,Still, the Phoenix's Heart is too precious.'

The yokai were making a fuss. Trying to escape, or rushing to kill Seong Yujin in reverse. But the yokai couldn't get close to Seong Yujin. The heat of the Phoenix's Heart and the relentlessly extending lightning. Most yokai died just by getting close.

-This won't do.

-It's dangerous if we stay like this. We'll subdue him.

Two yokai who had been quietly watching from behind as if assessing the situation. The great yokai, who were comparable to S-class Hunters, stepped forward, and the other yokai scattered in all directions. The great yokai instantly raised their yokai energy. Just that alone put immense pressure on the surrounding area.

Hase sneered at them.

,It's already too late."

In his eyes, the Phoenix's Heart was about to explode.

,A suicide attack. Experiencing death in that way isn't so bad either."

Hase was thinking that because he was an immortal who couldn't die. But what about that guy? He wasn't an immortal like him, but he was going to commit suicide.

,Is a Korean guy committing suicide for Japan? He was annoying and crazy, but he's a remarkable guy in many ways.'

Hase was inwardly impressed, and before the great yokai could do anything, the Phoenix's Heart exploded.

Hase endured by surrounding himself with yin-yang magic. He wanted to survive to the end to avoid the worst-case scenario where some of the yokai survived.

With a massive explosion, flames soared. The fiery red flames instantly burned the yokai who had endured the explosion, soaring high into the sky. Hase had resistance to fire, so he could withstand it without turning to ash immediately. He was the Onmyoji who created the current Phoenix's Heart.

,Even the great yokai couldn't withstand it. The distance was too close to endure even a little.'

The flames soared into the sky. Koyori's barrier shattered in an instant. Hase's body was also burning. The only fortunate thing was that the ice wall set up by Han Ah-young prevented the flames from spreading in all directions. The ice wall was melting, but the flames were also quickly dying down.

,It was lucky because of the compatibility. With this, Kahashima won't burn down.'

Soon after, Hase also burned and disappeared.

,Hmm."

Somewhere overseas. In a place known only to Baekrim's executives, Hase groaned. Rimju, who was next to him, asked Hase.

,How did it go? Did you fail?"

,I failed. That guy detonated the Phoenix's Heart and committed suicide. I saw with my own eyes that all the yokai, including the great yokai, were annihilated."

,It's a relief that there's less trouble."

,...They took the Machine Emperor's artificial intelligence core. I don't know how they'll use it."

,We're giving up on the Machine Emperor."

Hase felt regret. How much investment had he put into artificial intelligence? But Rimju said firmly.

,The Machine Emperor betrayed you. You should know the problems with machines from this incident. Japan must develop through the power of people after all. Your job has failed, so help me."

,...Rimju. With just the Machine Emperor's artificial intelligence core, Japan could quickly shed the dishonorable name of being an analog country."

,To make all Japanese people familiar with machines, we need to change the policies first. The reason Japan is clinging to analog now is because the older generation can't escape their old nostalgia. We need to push them out and innovate."

,Rimju's plan was to become the Prime Minister. Do you think that's realistically possible?"

,If I was going to give up because it was difficult, I wouldn't have started in the first place. What's your condition now? Isn't this the first time all four of your clones have been defeated at once?"

Hase sighed.

,Three were sealed. Thanks to the fourth being caught in the explosion and dying, I can create a clone again. Because the soul has returned."

Hase's ability was soul clones. It was the ability to divide his soul into five and create clones. They were called clones, but in reality, all five clones were like the main body. The clones all shared thoughts and acted accordingly. Thanks to this ability, Hase could work or train five times more efficiently than others.

Originally, when a clone died, its soul returned to the remaining ones. And he could spend time creating a clone again. This information was only known to Hase himself and Rimju.

,If they're sealed, can't you create clones again? Can't you just call the souls? Didn't you say that the essence of your clones is the soul, so they don't die even if their heads are cut off or their hearts are destroyed?"

,The one who performed the sealing is Amatsuka Koyori. The best Onmyoji in Japan and the miko of Fuka Shrine. The solidity of that seal goes without saying. I don't even know where those souls are now."

,If Amatsuka Koyori sealed them, they're probably at Fuka Shrine."

,Amatsuka Koyori has already had the Tenyo Jade, a treasure of Fuka Shrine, stolen from us. If I were Amatsuka Koyori, I would have hidden them in a secret place other than Fuka Shrine. That's what I would have done."

Rimju nodded in agreement. Besides, there are three sealed clones, aren't there? Rather than keeping them all in one place, she would have hidden them in different places.

In reality, they were buried in the deep sea. Seong Yujin was too lazy, so he threw them away somewhere in the Pacific Ocean. And he stopped caring. Seong Yujin doesn't even know where the three sealed clones are in the deep sea.

,In your clones' case, I'll focus on obtaining information."

,Then what should I do now?"

With the Machine Emperor project failing, Hase had nothing to do. He had been running forward for the Machine Emperor project for several years, but that goal disappeared in an instant.

,Hmm. First, be careful. It's better to not move rashly and focus on training here."

,The time for growing through training has passed."

,There's no end to training and research. You need to be diligent to develop further."

,......"

He couldn't argue because it was the right thing to say. Rimju was sometimes too conservative.

,And if you act externally now, Baekrim will be in trouble."

,...What's the connection between me acting and Baekrim being in trouble?"

,Child Porn Hase. That's the nickname the media calls you. The internet... is even worse. You're already the laughingstock of the world. Even other Baekrim colleagues are cursing you."

Hase's face turned red. He felt both shame and anger rising up.

,I don't watch child pornography! It's all a false accusation!"

Hase, who was always calm and collected, was now shouting as if he was about to lose his mind, vehemently denying it. His reaction was so intense that it was suspicious.

,The truth doesn't matter. They just want to slander us and you. ...But your reaction is too strong. You're not really watching child pornography, are you?"

,Absolutely not!!"

,Is that so. Anyway, you have nothing to do now. I'll call you when something comes up. The nickname 'Child Porn Onmyoji Hase' will disappear over time too. People's fickle nature is all the same."

Rimju left.

Hase, left alone in the secret hideout, frowned deeply and picked up his smartphone. He went on the internet. He was sure people were cursing him, but he couldn't help it. He had too little to do here. Maybe it was because the Machine Emperor plan had failed, but he didn't feel motivated to train or research.

,Those damn bastards.... I don't watch child pornography...!"

The internet was as he expected.

But there was nothing he could do. Report for defamation? The view of Baekrim was that of a terrorist organization. There was actually a bounty on them. He would be caught if he reported it.

Hase searched for Machine Emperor.

Contrary to Hase, the evaluation of the Machine Emperor was surprisingly good. Hase felt proud for no reason. People were recognizing his masterpiece, which he had created.

-Honestly, the Machine Emperor is super cool.

-I'm confirming that I made a Machine Emperor figure.

-I'm a resident of Kahashima, and it wasn't bad when the Machine Emperor ruled. Bullying completely disappeared from our school at that time. If you bullied even a little, you'd get caught.

-What's it like now?

-Of course, bullying has revived! The criminals who were caught are also being released. Kahashima is a mess now. I really miss the Machine Emperor.

,There are enlightened Japanese people who recognize the true value of the Machine Emperor after all."

However, of course, there weren't only praises for the Machine Emperor. His teeth were grinding when he saw the guys who were criticizing the Machine Emperor. The Machine Emperor was the best masterpiece he had created over several years. In a way, it was his child.

-It's just a killing machine anyway. How dare a machine try to stand above people? This is crossing the line.

-The Machine Emperor eventually betrayed Child Porn Onmyoji Hase, right? That means he knows what's right.

-Didn't he say something about Sex Supreme?

-Who's that Sex Supreme? Is it me by any chance?

-Breaking news!!! The Machine Emperor is currently broadcasting with Amatsuka Koyori!!!

,What?!"

Hase hurriedly clicked on the link and entered the broadcasting site.

There was a woman on the broadcast. A woman with black hair and an expressionless face. It was closer to 2D than 3D. Next to her was a roughly taken picture of Amatsuka Koyori.

The broadcast title was 'Machine Emperor Madoka's First Broadcast! Special Guest is Amatsuka Koyori!'

,A VTuber that's popular these days? How dare they impersonate the Machine Emperor?"

The moment he was about to get angry, the avatar opened its mouth.

,Many of you are doubting me. I am the Machine Emperor. Madoka is my new name."

,She is the Machine Emperor. I, Amatsuka Koyori, guarantee it."

Hase's eyes widened. What crazy person would impersonate Amatsuka Koyori in Japan? As soon as he heard Amatsuka Koyori's voice, his credibility soared. The reaction in the chat window was the same.

,Is it real?"
