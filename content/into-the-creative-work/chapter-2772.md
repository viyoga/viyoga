I looked down at the ground from the sky above China.

Rugged mountains and forests far from the city. It was night, and with the eerie fog, I couldn't see inside at all. Judging by the complete lack of even the smallest light, it was definitely not a place where people lived.

,That's how it looks on the outside. This is a barrier.'

A large-scale barrier covering an area the size of Jeju Island. It doesn't have a physical shield effect. Instead, it was a barrier specialized in deceiving the eyes of others.

,Even if I don't know, this would have cost more than the barrier installed in the White House. Because it's a barrier that even deceives my electromagnetic waves.'

[Opening the Eye of Heaven (Heaven's Eye).]

Even if it deceives electromagnetic waves, it can't deceive my eyes. The Eye of Heaven pierced through the barrier.

What had been hidden by the mountains and forests was a city. However, it was a city made up only of traditional Chinese buildings made of wood. It was a city where modern civilization was excluded to the point where I momentarily wondered if I had entered a medieval martial arts world.

,There are fewer people than the size of the city. About 300,000 people, I guess.'

I concentrated and looked, then exclaimed.

,Out of 300,000 people, only a few hundred are living humans. The rest are all dead corpses. Zombies. No, are they Jiangshi?'

The Jiangshi are lying in their beds as if asleep. Even if they are corpses, they need energy to move. They are lying down to save energy.

,The reason why these many Jiangshi don't rot and are maintained is... because of the land and the barrier? There are many other things installed besides that. With fields and livestock, they can be self-sufficient.'

And I can feel a special power in various parts of the city.

Corrupted spirits that have turned into Demonic Spirits (魔靈).

Guardian Ghost Spirits (修好鬼靈) are created by combining Demonic Spirits with ghosts.

They were crawling in the ground of the city, flying in the air, and wandering around.

,The reason why the Spirit King told me to get rid of Guirak Valley is because of those Demonic Spirits.'

Once corrupted and turned into a Demonic Spirit, it could never return to being a spirit.

,Demonic Spirits are not a threat.'

Most Demonic Spirits are born as low-level, and sometimes mid-level. There are no high-level Demonic Spirits. There is a possibility that they were made into Guardian Ghost Spirits, or that they could not be captured from the beginning. Most spirits of high level or higher are in the spirit realm, and even if they are in the human realm, they are powerful.

,There are 3 Guardian Ghost Spirits. They are opponents that even I can't easily deal with right now.'

Considering this and that, this city has a military power comparable to a small country. Even I couldn't figure out the exact level. Since I'm not a necromancer, I don't know how strong the Jiangshi are.

[...It's more powerful than I thought. Yujin-ssi. Doing it alone is too reckless. At least get support from the United States. Three S-class heroes. You won't die with that much.]

It was Kang Ji-young's advice.

It's definitely difficult to face all the forces here.

"Originally, you just need to kill the necromancer to end this kind of thing."

My gaze turned to the center of the city, a particularly large building. There was Wang Wei, the Head of Guirak Valley. A middle-aged man with a queue and wearing a black qipao.

"In other words, it's doable."

I summon Hwaryeon Bido and Jeunryeong from my inventory.

I used Jeunryeong to call dark clouds into the sky. Lightning flashed from the dark clouds that gathered at an unnatural speed. In addition, it is natural that heavy rain pours down.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido in both hands and created lightning. I wrapped my whole body in the red lightning that soared from the sword. I didn't stop there, and about half of my body turned into lightning. The tip of the sword is aimed at Wang Wei, who is sitting on a chair inside the building and touching his smartphone.

Thunderbolt of the Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流 落雷)

I became a red lightning bolt and fell to the ground.

Kwa kwa kwa kwang!!

In an instant, I smashed the roof and walls of the building and aimed for Wang Wei's heart. However, a translucent hand that popped out of Wang Wei's body grabbed the sword and threw it to the side.

I let go of Hwaryeon Bido, summoned the Vajra Thunderbolt, gripped it, and aimed for Wang Wei's head.

Teop.

Wang Wei blocks my fist with his left palm. Even though it's not a sword, I was confident in my punching. It's not a fist that can be blocked so helplessly. Wang Wei's martial arts skills are at least at the level of a master.

"Jeokgwang. I knew you would come someday. No, should I call you Seong Yujin-ssi?"

"Did you find out my identity?"

"There aren't many ability users in Korea who use lightning. Moreover, if you are a skilled person who can kill one of the Four Great Heads of the family, even more so. However, I only found out recently."

Something is trying to enter me through his palm.

Pajijijik!

Golden lightning sparks from the Vajra Thunderbolt and blocks what was trying to enter. Wang Wei took his palm off and stepped back.

"To block my curse so easily. It's no ordinary Vajra."

I could feel the Jiangshi, Demonic Spirits, and Guardian Ghost Spirits in the city gathering through my senses.

Wang Wei is stronger than expected. I frowned and checked Wang Wei's status window.

『Name: Wang Wei

Strength: S+ Stamina: SS Agility: S+ Durability: SS Mana: SSS-

Trait: King of Curses (SS), Man'gwiju (SSS)

Skill: Martial Arts (S+), Curse (SS) Ghostly Energy (SS), Ghostly Poison (B+), Ghostly Eye (B).

Trust: -5』

,What the hell. These ridiculous stats!'

It's covered in S's.

However, considering that he has been ruling China for decades, it's not unreasonable. He would be able to monopolize the high-quality elixirs that come out of China.

"Seong Yujin. I don't have much interest in the small country of Korea. My goal is to transcend death and become an existence like a Demon Corps Commander. I don't want to have a life-or-death battle with you. If you promise non-interference with me and China, I will give you 10% of China's resources."

"10% of China's resources... That would be enormous."

"You will be able to live a life that even a king would envy."

"Haha. What makes you think I would accept such an offer? Even if I could trust you, it's an unacceptable offer."

I have to kill Wang Wei to release the Spirit King's curse on Seong Hari.

"Is that so. That's a pity."

Wang Wei snapped his fingers with an expression that was not at all regretful.

Dozens of curses pour out of his body towards me. The Vajra Thunderbolt in my hand creates golden lightning and blocks the curses. The Vajra Thunderbolt's anti-magic power uses mana to block the curses.

,Even an excellent curse caster can't use dozens of curses at the same time just by snapping their fingers once. That's probably the power of the ghosts inside him.'

Wang Wei had thousands of ghosts in his body.

"It doesn't block specific curses, but it can block all curses below a certain level. That's a really good item."

I could feel greed towards the Vajra Thunderbolt. Wang Wei's hand would burn as soon as he grabbed the Vajra Thunderbolt, but I had no intention of giving him the Vajra Thunderbolt before that.

"It's not difficult to lend it to you."

I put lightning into the Vajra Thunderbolt and threw it at Wang Wei. Golden lightning is fired at him.

Wang Wei didn't even blink, let alone dodge. The translucent ghosts that popped out of his body took the Vajra Thunderbolt instead and perished.

The Vajra Thunderbolt, which had lost its power, fell to the floor with a thud.

"I didn't know that 17 ghosts would be consumed by that attack. It's a more dangerous power than I thought."

Wang Wei reaches out towards the Vajra Thunderbolt that fell on the ground. A long, pale ghost arm appears from his hand and picks up the Vajra Thunderbolt.

Pajijijijijik!

The ghost's arm disappeared due to the anti-magic power contained in the Vajra Thunderbolt.

"Indeed. It's extremely incompatible with ghosts. It's a weapon I can't use."

I reached out towards the Vajra Thunderbolt. When I created mana, the Vajra Thunderbolt that had fallen on the floor was automatically drawn and flew into my hand.

"How many ghosts do you have inside your body?"

"8,437. There are conditions for harboring ghosts. All of these ghosts will die in my place. If you truly want to kill me, you must kill all 8,437 ghosts. Are you confident?"

"Confidence? I have more than enough."

Kwa kwa kwa kwang!

The door was broken and a giant man reaching 2m appeared. A rotten-bodied man with amulets plastered all over his body. Behind him, you can see the Demonic Spirits, Guardian Ghost Spirits, and Jiangshi surrounding the building.

I could guess the identity of the giant.

"Ghost Lord."

An unpleasant energy flowed out of the corpse giant's body. It's the power of the devil, demonic energy.

『Demon Hunter (SS+) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

『Demon Hunter (SS+) sees through the demon.』

『Geurutal. 3rd Chief of Staff. Affiliated with the 1st Corps.』

"A demon who made a contract with the Head of Guirak Valley. Your ability was the reason why the Head of Guirak Valley could control so many Jiangshi."

The Ghost Lord opened his rotten mouth. A voice that seemed to rise from a deep hole.

"It's Geurutal. So we meet like this. Our nemesis."

"A demon without form."

"Did you see through it even though it has no form? As expected of a human watched by a god with nine eyes, you have good eyes. Those are eyes that are too much for a human. I will kill you and take your eyes."

It was something I had never heard of before.

"A god with nine eyes is watching me, but you're going to kill me and take my eyes? Aren't you afraid of that god?"

"The god with nine eyes is just observing. That god has never intervened first. It will continue to be so in the future."

In front is Wang Wei, the Head of Guirak Valley. Behind is the great demon manipulating the Ghost Lord. Surrounded by Demonic Spirits, Guardian Ghost Spirits, and 300,000 Jiangshi centered around me.

[It's a desperate situation. Run away first. If you use the space movement scroll you have, those guys won't be able to chase you.]

But it's a shame to just leave.

A bomb with compressed mana.

It is a bomb with pure physical power. It can cleanly erase a city.

Price: 100,000 points

※Caution

It's a nuclear bomb without worrying about radiation.

,Let's look at it long-term. It's a profit just to be able to wipe out the Demonic Spirits and 300,000 Jiangshi.'

"See you again."

A scroll that is torn in half. But I was still here.

"......What?"

The Demonic Spirits around Geurutal disappear. That was a sacrifice. Geurutal was using power by sucking up the Demonic Spirits.

"...It doesn't matter. Still, the mana bomb will explode. Let's all explode together."

I had complete recovery.

The mana bomb exploded.

While trapped inside a certain space. The explosive power did not escape outside the space and swirled inside.

As expected of a great demon belonging to the elite 1st Corps. He's no ordinary guy.
