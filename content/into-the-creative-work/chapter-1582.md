,"Princess-mama. I have a proposal. Give me your lady-in-waiting. I will place the crown upon Your Highness's head."

Princess Eileen didn't budge. A poker face so thorough it was almost cruel.

,"Count Prucus. I've toured your mansion. It's nothing but women. And beautiful ones at that. I've heard rumors of your fondness for women, but I never imagined you'd covet even my lady-in-waiting."

,"I'm glad you know now."

,"...Aridna is my dearest friend and most loyal subject. What is your reason for wanting Aridna?"

I glanced at Aridna. Even the lady-in-waiting's expression was perfectly managed.

,"No grand reason. Just to play with."

,"...In what way do you intend to play with her?"

,"The way a man plays with a woman is obvious, isn't it? Drag her to bed and touch her here and there. And when I get bored... I'll just toss her to my subordinates. As Your Highness knows, more than a few of my soldiers have developed a taste for wartime plunder. They're the kind who'll charge with their third leg raised as long as it's a woman."

All of it was bullshit.

There was no way I'd hand over a beauty to someone else. It was a lie to scare her. Judging by the chilling atmosphere, the lie seemed to have worked.

,"Princess-mama. What will you do? This opportunity won't come again."

,"......"

I grinned, watching Princess Eileen's choice.

Princess Eileen rose from her seat. She looked down at me with eyes that had grown even colder.

,"I seem to have misjudged Count Prucus. I'll forget what you've said today, so please forget what I've said as well."

,"Are you leaving just like that? With nothing to show for it? Princess-mama. Do you think Prince Aiden, who seized the throne, will leave you alone?"

,"Count, the owner of the throne has not yet been decided."

She left the reception room with her lady-in-waiting.

Kwaang!

Judging by the noisy slamming of the door, the provocation had worked perfectly.

I was surprised. To think she still had the strength to be angry with me.

,'Is it a bluff?'

Princess Eileen isn't foolish enough to bluff without a reason. There might be some power I don't know about.

I asked Yuria, who was nestled in my arms.

,"Yuria. What do you think?"

,"It just looks like a last-ditch effort. If you command it... I will bring Princess Eileen here."

,"It's fine. Princess Eileen, who will surrender when that last-ditch effort is over, will be more interesting."

At best, all Princess Eileen can do is gather my political enemies and form an alliance. There are quite a few nobles who dislike me.

,'As I eliminate those bastards one by one, she'll realize how stupid a choice she's made.'

I lower my head. Just then, Yuria raised her head. We naturally locked lips. My hands slowly began to undress her.

It wouldn't be strange for him to go back on his word as easily as flipping his hand. Why does a man like that have so much power... Hooo."

,'Aiden. If that bastard ascends the throne, he'll try to get rid of me by any means necessary. Whether it's sending me off to marry a foreign country or hiring someone to assassinate me... I don't have much time left.'

,"Aridna. Is it possible... to assassinate Count Prucus?"

,"It's impossible. Count Prucus is an Aura Master. Even if there's a lucky chance for assassination... Yuria Grace is by his side."

Eileen already knew that assassination was impossible. This was just a word that came out of frustration.

,"Yuria Grace. Is that fallen Helbrit princess that strong?"

Aridna trembled slightly as she thought of Yuria.

,"I couldn't dare to measure how strong she is. All I know for sure is that someone like me could be killed by her at any time."

,"That means she's a monster. I'll have to be careful."

,"...Princess-nim. What will you do from now on? You're a princess, so you must have a plan. Will you gather the nobles who oppose Count Prucus?"

Princess Eileen paused for a moment before opening her mouth.

,"That alone is not enough. There will be few nobles with the guts to stand against Count Prucus, and even if they all gather, they won't have enough power to oppose Count Prucus."

,"You mean we need a force other than the nobles. Are you considering the power of foreign forces?"

,"That's a bad move that sells out the future."

,"Then...."

,"The Magic Tower. As Count Prucus operates the Korea Trading Company and rakes in the continent's money, the Magic Tower's interests are gradually disappearing. If they have any sense at all... they'll know that this is dangerous. I'm thinking of promising them interests and co-opting them. But no matter how I think about it, there's not enough time."

Princess Eileen sighed.

The Magic Tower and wizards. At first glance, they seem like mysterious and noble words, but in reality, they were just merchants with power. To join hands with the Magic Tower, she would have to give up considerable interests. Even if things go well and she ascends the throne, she wouldn't feel good about it.

,"Should I just give up everything and run away?"

,"...I will respect Your Highness's wishes."

,"Pretend you didn't hear that. It's just nonsense that came out of frustration."

Princess Eileen steadied her weakening heart. She had already come too far to give up everything.

The labyrinth city of Lapit is a special city located in the northeast of the Rapelli Kingdom.

This labyrinth city had no owner. That meant there was no lord. The nobles shunned the labyrinth city. A labyrinth located in the center of the city? From the nobles' point of view, it was unsettling just to imagine it. Similarly, the Rapelli royal family also gave up ownership of the labyrinth city. In practice, the Adventurer's Guild operates the labyrinth city. Instead, the Rapelli royal family has priority over the by-products obtained from the labyrinth.

I arrived at this labyrinth city of Lapit with the AM unit maids.

The first thing that catches my eye is, of course, the labyrinth in the center of the city. A huge skull rock mountain cave. It's oozing with an ominous atmosphere just by looking at it.

,'A labyrinth city. It's not here, but I went to a labyrinth city.'

I had quite a hard time there.

And I have a feeling I'm going to have a hard time this time too.

,'The Labyrinth of Lapit has been explored up to the 72nd floor underground, and the floors below that are unknown. The labyrinth city episode also appears in the original work...'

It's not particularly helpful. That's because it wasn't an episode that dealt with the labyrinth in detail. One thing is certain: even Kyle in the original work didn't completely clear the labyrinth.

,'There are a lot of adventurers.'

More than half of the people walking around the streets are armed adventurers. They buzzed as they looked at me and the AM unit.

,'Well, is it natural? They'll hardly ever see a noble.'

Besides, the AM unit were maids, true to their name. It was only natural that they would attract attention, as eye-poppingly beautiful maids surrounded and guarded me.

If you listen closely, you can hear them whispering.

,"Is he a noble?"

,"Why is a noble in a labyrinth city?"

,"His guards are maids. He's a noble with strange tastes."

,"Don't underestimate maids. They're much stronger than the average Joe."

,"Does anyone know which family he's from?"

I clicked my tongue.

I expected it, but they don't know who I am even after seeing my face.

,'Ignorant adventurers. How dare they not recognize me, the hero of this country?'

I barely resisted the urge to wipe them all out. The reason I came here isn't to punish the ignorant adventurers.

We headed to the Adventurer's Guild, which manages this city. There were people gathered in front of the Adventurer's Guild. Judging by their neat clothes, they seemed to be Adventurer's Guild employees. As soon as they saw me, they bowed and greeted me.

,"Welcome, Count Prucus-kakka! All of us at the Adventurer's Guild welcome Count Prucus-kakka's visit!"

At the center of them was a huge woman. A woman with tanned skin, bulging muscles, and fierce eyes. Scars were visible all over her body, and she had an axe equipped at her waist. She wasn't my type of beauty.

,"Who are you?"

,"I am Tatari, the Adventurer's Guild Master of the labyrinth city of Lapit."

,"You came out so quickly even though I came here without notice. I like it."

,"Thank you, Count-kakka. I will escort you inside."

Unlike her burly body, her attitude was very polite. I followed her into the Adventurer's Guild.

We sat facing each other in Tatari's office. Tatari brought out a drink. It was black tea. She brought out quite expensive black tea, but my eyebrows furrowed on their own when I smelled it.

,"...Do you dislike black tea?"

,"It's because the black tea scent is like garbage. It seems like good tea leaves... how can you brew it so badly?"

,"Ha, hahaha.... I, I'm sorry. Actually, I don't usually enjoy black tea, so I don't know much about it...."

,"Yuria. Show her how to brew black tea."

,"Yes, Master-nim."

,"This is it."

Tatari admires.
