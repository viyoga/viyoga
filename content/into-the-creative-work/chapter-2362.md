[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

Jeonjeong spat out blood and swung his palm at Yu Yeoryeo. Palm-shaped lightning energy stretched out towards Yu Yeoryeo like a thunderbolt.

Yu Yeoryeo lightly moved her body to avoid it, but the aftermath alone sent her flying. Her complexion worsened, and she immediately ran away. A wise decision. Yu Yeoryeo is at the 8th level of Samjeong, while Jeonjeong is at the 8th level of Ogi.

No matter how difficult it is for Jeonjeong due to external wounds, internal injuries, and poisoning, there is no chance if they fight head-on. Especially since Yu Yeoryeo is an assassin. Direct confrontation is not her specialty.

,Damn bitch.... I've memorized your face, so I'll definitely chase you down and kill you."

Jeonjeong deployed a Geumgang Sinroe (Diamond Divine Lightning) barrier and collapsed onto the floor. It seems he's trying to deal with the poison by using circulating qi as a temporary measure.

[Death Resistance remaining time: 3 seconds]

,Damn it. The best time to ambush a Martial artist is when they are concentrating on circulating qi, the moment they are most vulnerable... but there's no time."

There's no choice. I can't die like this.

The only fortunate thing is that I'm inside the barrier. He must be so sure of my death that he's completely stopped paying attention to me.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 7]

I jumped up and grabbed the Hwaryeon Bido. Jeonjeong reacted. His eyes widened, and he emitted a stream of inner light. That damn Mind's Eye immediately sensed my revival.

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 6]

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Simjeukgeom (The Mind Is the Sword).

The sword was swung at the same time as the mind acted. Fleeting moment and Simjeukgeom. The fastest slash I can do right now.

But it was seen through. The name Mind's Eye wasn't given for nothing; Jeonjeong rolled to the side to avoid it, threw Geumgang Sinroe at me, and quickly created distance.

Seogeok.

Jeonjeong's left arm falls off. The distance was too close, so he couldn't completely avoid it. I deflected the Geumgang Sinroe flying towards me and mocked Jeonjeong.

,That was the most impressive Naryeotagon I've ever seen. It's too good to watch alone; I'd like to show it to the monks of Noeeumsa."

Jeonjeong staunched the bleeding with a face like a demon. From his perspective, it's fortunate that there are no Noeeumsa monks or Dae Sa Pal Ak Hwe (Great Deathly Eight Evils Society) remnants around. Or would it be the worst, since he can't get help otherwise?

,You bastard...! I confirmed your death with the Mind's Eye. What kind of sorcery did you use to come back to life? All your fatal wounds have been healed too. Did you have a time-reversing magic treasure or something? No, but your clothes are dirty. Tell me! How did you come back to life!"

I can feel greed from him. He seems to covet Complete Recovery quite a bit.

I just laughed at Jeonjeong without answering.

,Well, it's understandable. Complete Recovery itself is a broken skill. He's almost in critical condition right now, so he must want it even more."

His left arm was cut off, and his side was stabbed by the Hwaryeon Bido. He's poisoned by Yu Yeoryeo's deadly poison, and his gi circulation must not be normal either. If he weren't an absolute master of Samjeong, he would have died immediately.

,On the other hand, I'm not just fine, but in top condition. The only variable is the Dae Sa Pal Ak Hwe remnants... but those bastards are busy fighting the Noeeumsa elders and monks."

Even though the Hwaryeon Bido's authority was released and I can't use it anymore, I was at an advantage.

,Hehe. Geumgang Sinroe is coming into my possession."

Pajik. Pajijijijijik!

The Geumgang Sinroe that returned to Jeonjeong emitted yellow electricity as it received Jeonjeong's lightning energy. The electricity then spread out into a complex formula and gathered back into the Geumgang Sinroe, forming a yellow, translucent sphere.

Geumgang Sinroe can store three techniques and quickly use them when needed! Even if it's a technique created by a god, a divine art, there are no exceptions!

,Geumgang Sinroe had that kind of ability? You said divine art. Does that mean I can use it too if I get the Geumgang Sinroe? But looking at your state right now, I can't help but laugh?"

Jeonjeong's face aged rapidly. His large, solid muscles, which were comparable to a young man's, lost their strength and became soft. He was using his innate energy to survive somehow.

The translucent sphere gradually grew larger and took the form of a person. It's a giant with his upper body exposed. His face was ferocious, and the muscles all over his body seemed stronger than steel. Jeonjeong shouted proudly from the giant's head.

,Dae Geumgang Hobeop Sool (Great Diamond Dharma Protector Technique). This is Jeseokcheon's divine art that embodies the Geumgang Yeoksa (Diamond Warrior) centered around the Geumgang Sinroe! Behold, the magnificent figure of my Geumgang Yeoksa, Gwancheol Nahanjang (Penetrating Arhat General)!"

,Magnificent my ass. He doesn't have a left arm. I can tell just by looking that the Geumgang Yeoksa is strongly influenced by the caster. It's a Geumgang Yeoksa summoned by a dying man like you. He's no different from a corpse, just like you."

,Ha, if you want to think that way, then think that way!"

The Geumgang Yeoksa moves. Since it's over 70m in size, I expected it to move sluggishly. I was wrong. The Geumgang Yeoksa seemed more agile than most Martial artists.

What's even more surprising is that it's taking the stance of Byeokryeok Sinjang (Thunderbolt Palm) while pulling its right hand back.

,Could it be that the Geumgang Yeoksa can also use the caster's martial arts?!"

Kwareureureureung.

Golden lightning flashed on the palm of the translucent Geumgang Yeoksa, illuminating the surroundings.

I felt a chill down my spine. To unleash Byeokryeok Sinjang at that size? If hit directly, the body will disappear without a trace.

The only fortunate thing is that it's not as fast as Jeonjeong, probably because of its size.

Watching the palm stretch out towards me, I soared into the sky.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Biraesin (Flying Thunder God).

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 5]

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 3]

The Geumgang Yeoksa takes the stance to use Byeokryeok Sinjang again. If I stay still, I'll be hit.

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 2]

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 1]

[Using fleeting moment (instant). Remaining stacks: 0]

,Noeswae Jubak (Lightning Shackles)."

From the Geumgang Sinroe in the Geumgang Yeoksa's chest, where the heart would be in a human, lightning chains stretched out and bound me.

,He predicted my movements? No, did he lure me!"

Lightning didn't gather in the Geumgang Yeoksa's hand. He only took the stance of Byeokryeok Sinjang to deceive me. It would take at least 5 seconds to forcibly break free from Jeonjeong's Noeswae Jubak. And in 5 seconds, Jeonjeong's Byeokryeok Sinjang would be more than enough to burst my heart.

[Using Leap.]

The distance I can move is 10m. I can move through space without any conditions. Even if I'm bound by special restraints.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Noegwang (雷光).

I didn't expect Jeonjeong's move at all, but anyway, I won.

,...That wasn't martial arts, sorcery, or a magical artifact. What kind of power is it?"

,It's a skill."

,Why aren't you killing me right away? Ah, I see. You want to revel in your victory by watching me die? How foolish."

,Yuseonggeom (Meteor Sword)."

Kukukung!

,...I lost. Hehe. To think that my fierce life would meet such an empty end...."

,Geumgang Sinroe...! That's mine! It's my last request...! Bury the Geumgang Sinroe with me!!"

There was no longer any inner light coming from Jeonjeong's eyes.

,I'll tell you why I didn't kill you right away. It's to kill you with the Geumgang Sinroe that you cherish so much."

Utteok.

,It shouldn't have a self.... Does it have a master protection function or something? Well, it wouldn't be strange if an object of this level had a self."

,Oh..., Geumgang Sinroe.... You're the only one after all...."

Peok.

,It's just an object after all."
