A horrific press conference hall where a shooting occurred.

Shot in the shoulder, I staggered to my feet. Pretending not to be conscious of the cameras, acting as if I was in pain, I headed towards the reporters who were still alive.

The situation shouldn't end with the terrorism.

,I have to take everything I can get.'

I healed the reporters in critical condition with my recovery ability.

I'm engraving my abilities onto the world once again. Also, I'm letting the people know that I'm a true hero, that I'm clearly on the side of good.

After healing the reporters who weren't dead, I collapsed on the floor and pretended to be unconscious.

,I wonder how those Awakened Union bastards will react. Keu keu.'

• • •

The Awakened Union office building in Pohang. The Union Leader's office, located on the highest floor, was filled with silence.

There were two people in the office. The man standing was Gu Youngbeon-ssi, the Union Leader, and the man in his late 20s sitting down was Japanese, not Korean. His name was Origumo. A player.

Origumo was dispatched from Japan to take control of Korea, starting with the Awakened Union. Originally, it was a project to be carried out slowly and gradually, but when Jin Suhak caused a coup and seized the military government, the project was hastily executed. He judged that now, with Korea in chaos, was the perfect time.

But things got twisted.

The Awakened Union became a national treasonous organization in an instant due to Seong Yujin's press conference.

,...Gu Youngbeon-ssi. What the hell happened? Did you really send that crazy bastard and order him to commit terrorism?"

In reality, the Awakened Union had a mind control ability user.

But Gu Youngbeon-ssi shook his head. His expression wasn't good.

"There are limits to mind control abilities. I can't force such extreme actions. More than anything... do you think I'm crazy enough to order something like that? I haven't forgotten that Seong Yujin is a target to be captured alive. Even if I were trying to kill him, I wouldn't use such a method. I'm not an idiot."

Origumo didn't rebuke Gu Youngbeon-ssi. He knew too. That Gu Youngbeon-ssi wasn't the type to commit such absurd acts.

From the start, the method of terrorism was a bit strange. Was it really necessary to be so loud about it? It's almost like they were desperately trying to make it known that it was the Awakened Union's doing.

"It's highly likely it was a staged event."

"I think so too. But... there have already been casualties. And it was broadcast live."

The incident grew. It's too provocative. Moreover, the victim is Seong Yujin, called the hero among heroes of South Korea. He struggled to save the reporters even after being shot in the shoulder. That was broadcast live. The Korean people have little to no experience with terrorism as it is. Public opinion will be boiling over.

,The Korean government will add more fuel to the fire, not try to stop it.'

The security guards at the press conference belatedly rushed in after everything was over and turned off the cameras. This alone makes it ninety-nine percent likely that it was a staged event.

,The Awakened Union stepped in shit from the very beginning.'

And not just any shit. Shit filled with poison.

"Gu Youngbeon-ssi. Do you have any countermeasures?"

"First, we will hold a press conference to respond. We will claim that this situation is a staged event by the government."

"...Well, do what you can."

Origumo answered listlessly. He had already given up on expectations for the Awakened Union and public opinion.

• • •

The Awakened Union expressed its position on the recent Seong Yujin terrorist attack. Union Leader Gu Youngbeon-ssi stepped up himself. The situation was too bad to send another spokesperson.

"Our Awakened Union is not a terrorist group! Our Awakened Union has no connection whatsoever with that terrorist! This terrorism is a staged event by the government! Observe the terrorist's words and actions carefully! What terrorist would act like that! It is clearly a calculated action to frame the Awakened Union as the culprit!!"

"That terrorist committed suicide as if to show off! Four reporters were killed instantly, and even National Assembly member Seong Yujin is hospitalized! You're saying this is a staged event by the government? Say something that makes sense!!"

The reporters' reactions were sharp. This was because reporters were killed and injured in this terrorist attack. If Seong Yujin hadn't healed the reporters with his ability, five more would have died. The reporters were furious about this. Of course, the fact that the trend was leaning towards the government was also a reason. The Awakened Union was already a public enemy.

"More than half of the Awakened Union's sponsors are Japanese companies! Is the Awakened Union really an organization launched under the instructions of Japan?!"

"What is the real reason for fortifying Pohang? Experts say it's a fortress to fight against the military, not monsters! Are you really planning to revolt against the Korean government?!"

"Are you willing to attend a government-led hearing?!"

Aggressive questions from Korean reporters poured out on Gu Youngbeon-ssi. Gu Youngbeon-ssi was sweating. There would be no end to answering each and every question.

"I'll say it again. This terrorist attack has nothing to do with our Awakened Union. This is all a government conspiracy. Also, I refuse to attend a government-led hearing. In my eyes, it only looks like a trap to capture me. If you hold the hearing in Pohang, I will attend. Finally, I want to meet Seong Yujin-ssi directly and have an honest conversation."

"Ah. You have nothing to do with the terrorism, but you refuse the hearing summons anyway? But you want to meet Seong Yujin-ssi directly? Why? Did you send your subordinates to fail to kill him, so you're going to kill Seong Yujin-ssi yourself?"

Gu Youngbeon-ssi glared at the reporter with a stiff expression.

"......Watch your mouth."

"You're the one who needs to watch your mouth, you terrorist bastard! My friend Youngcheol died at that press conference! Bring my friend back to life, you son of a bitch!!!!"

As soon as the reporter cursed, a security guard rushed in, grabbed the reporter, and took him outside.

"Did you see that? There should always be security guards at a press conference. But there were no security guards at that press conference. Do you see that as mere coincidence? That terrorism is a staged event by the government. The government should be the one holding the hearing, not me."

"Don't talk nonsense! You dirty terrorist bastard!!"

One of the reporters threw a camera. But the camera stopped in mid-air before it touched Gu Youngbeon-ssi's body. Telekinesis. It was Gu Youngbeon-ssi's awakened ability. Gu Youngbeon-ssi turned his body away from the reporter being kicked out and left the press conference hall.

Awakened beings gathered around him as he returned to the Awakened Union building. They were famous awakened beings who had been recruited with promises of immense wealth and power. They all frowned and glared at Gu Youngbeon-ssi.

"Union Leader-nim. What is this? Was the Awakened Union this kind of organization?!"

"Do you know that our SNS is in chaos right now? We've fallen from heroes to criminal terrorists! How are you going to take responsibility for this?!"

"You son of a bitch! You ruined our lives!!"

Loud voices were exchanged.

Gu Youngbeon-ssi also felt like shouting. He didn't know things would progress so rapidly. The original plan was to firmly establish the existence of the Awakened Union by playing hard to get with the Jin Suhak government, extorting time, supplies, and equipment. But the plan was ruined by a single terrorist incident.

"You guys have already signed the contract. Don't whine and stay in your positions."

The awakened beings quieted down at Gu Youngbeon-ssi's cold voice. They weren't overwhelmed. The awakened beings were quietly furious. Murderous intent flashed in the eyes of the awakened beings.

Then a man stepped forward. He was a man with a neat impression wearing a suit.

Bear Han Duho. He was an awakened being who was active in Gyeonggi Province. He was the one who had struggled to create an Awakened Federation before the Awakened Union appeared. He was the one who had proposed the creation of the federation to Seong Yujin as well. Half of the awakened beings in the Awakened Union were those who followed Han Duho.

"Union Leader Gu Youngbeon-ssi. I believed in the Union Leader's words that he was for the awakened beings. But now I know that those words were lies."

"Han Duho. Ordinary people don't understand us. The Korean government ultimately represents ordinary people as well. Are you going to be played by ordinary people's tricks?"

"Well, at least I have no intention of being played by your tricks. I and the awakened beings will withdraw from the Union and leave. Try to catch us if you can."

"......"

Gu Youngbeon-ssi couldn't catch Han Duho. This was because more than half of the awakened beings were following Han Duho.

,Damn it. I should have brainwashed the awakened beings...!'

Brainwashing an awakened being requires several times more effort and time than brainwashing an ordinary person. Awakened beings inherently had some resistance to abilities.

Half of the awakened beings disappeared. The ones who remained were those who were deeply entangled with the Awakened Union. Even they would try to leave as soon as the opportunity arose.

Gu Youngbeon-ssi entered the office with his mouth tightly shut.

Origumo stared at Han Duho with fierce eyes.

"Gu Youngbeon-ssi. Go meet Seong Yujin."

"...Did Seong Yujin say he would meet me?"

"Yes. The Korean government crossed the line this time. They brought up Japan at the press conference. The home country severely criticized the Korean government and arranged a meeting with Seong Yujin. Well, it's only natural. The home country protested, so what could the Korean government do."

"Then what I have to do is..."

"I'll attach two mind control ability users. Brainwash Seong Yujin and bring him here. And then leave Korea."

"Are we disbanding the Awakened Union?"

"Is there any other answer besides disbanding?"

"......"

Origumo's eyes shone fiercely.

"Oh my. Your loyalty is wavering. Don't forget. All of your awakening is thanks to my power."

"Yes. I won't forget. I will definitely brainwash Seong Yujin and bring him to Pohang."

"This project failed... but if we can bring only Seong Yujin to the home country, we can make up for it."
