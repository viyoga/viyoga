,"You've missed a golden opportunity. I didn't expect Seong Hari-ssi, who's become a has-been compared to her old days, to react so quickly."

Son Jeong-eon's tone was gentle, but his eyes were sharp.

Go Youngjin instinctively tried to push up his glasses, then gritted his teeth as he remembered Seong Yujin's fist had shattered them earlier.

,"I'll handle Seong Yujin. Don't interfere."

,"Interfere? If it weren't for me, Go Youngjin-gun would be dead. I used my only artifact to save Go Youngjin-gun. Please cooperate this time."

The straw doll Son Jeong-eon was holding turned to ash and scattered.

Go Youngjin's thigh wound was perfectly healed by the nanomachines. The nanomachines were consumed in the process, but it wasn't enough to affect the power supply.

,"I'll give you my father and the Buster Clan. I'll kill Seong Hari-ssi. After that, I don't care if you attack Seong Yujin together or not. Are you perhaps afraid of Seong Hari-ssi? If you need to, use the power of a demon."

,"Haha."

Son Jeong-eon laughed. His eyes didn't laugh at all.

,"Haven't you heard from the demons? The Demon Hunter Seong Yujin gets stronger when demons or demonic beings are nearby, influenced by the power of demons, to be exact. And don't underestimate Seong Hari-ssi. She may be weaker than in her prime, but she was the strongest hero in the world."

,"So, are you not going to do it?"

,"...I'll take care of it quickly and help you. Just hold on."

Son Jeong-eon clicked his tongue and said.

He didn't like following the words of Go Youngjin, who was young and inexperienced. But he had no choice. Only Go Youngjin, the contractor of Germadon, could command the Buster Clan, who were being controlled by nanomachines in the background.

On the dueling stage, Seong Yujin provoked him with a flick of his hand. Go Youngjin, filled with murderous intent, rushed toward Seong Yujin.

,'Seong Yujin or Go Youngjin... Are they only interested in killing each other in this situation?'

He didn't say it aloud because there were many ears listening, but having failed to kill Seong Yujin, he just needed to stall for time here. The mechanical demons that had descended upon robots and androids manufactured in various parts of Seoul were gathering here.

,'The demon said something about shaking the world last time. That was just a feint to draw attention. The real purpose is to gather machines in Korea.'

Demons can descend to Earth under specific conditions and contracts. Unlike forcibly descending to Earth, they are less restricted. In the case of the 7th Legion, the mechanical demons descended to Earth using machines and began their invasion.

This was why some of the mechanical demons that descended to Earth were plundering machines. To summon even more mechanical demons to Earth.

Son Jeong-eon sensed the presence of heroes affiliated with the Association in various parts of the dueling arena's spectator seats. It was only natural, as the Hero Association was closely watching this duel.

,"I'll take care of Seong Hari-ssi. You guys deal with the other heroes."

The Buster Clan members scattered and attacked the heroes.

Seong Hari-ssi moved, and Son Jeong-eon also moved. Spear and sword clashed in the air. Surprisingly, Seong Hari-ssi didn't back down.

,"Oh, dear. I never thought I'd be fighting Gwancheon (Piercing Heaven) like this. You've covered your whole body in expensive artifacts. How long will those artifacts support you?"

,"Five minutes at most. It won't take me five minutes to kill you."

Even as they spoke, they were fighting. True to his nickname, Hundred Swords (Baekgeom), swords appeared in the air around Son Jeong-eon, shooting or swinging. Seong Hari-ssi countered with a spear imbued with lightning and spirit energy.

,"I can't see any openings. Impressive. Is this you in your prime?"

,"If I were in my prime, you'd already be dead."

• • •

,"Seong Yujin!!"

Go Youngjin shouted and flew towards me. He was using wind magic as propulsion to maneuver in the air.

,'His casting speed is insane. It doesn't make sense that his magic skills would increase so dramatically just because his physical abilities have improved. Even with the Perfect Balance (A) trait, it's unnatural. Is a chip in his head casting for him?'

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

I swung my sword at Go Youngjin, who was flying towards me. Blue lightning flashed, aiming for his neck. Go Youngjin bent his upper body, narrowly dodging it, and then lunged at me.

,'I've already figured out how to deal with Go Youngjin.'

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Art (雷術) Lightning Chain Restraint (雷鎖呪縛).

Chains of lightning bound Go Youngjin. He failed to avoid the Lightning Chain Restraint this time as well.

Just as I was about to stab the sword into Go Youngjin's chest, the nanomachines flowing out of Go Youngjin's body spread out and approached me.

Nanomachines. It was clear that they intended to inject nanomachines into my body.

I wrapped my body in Aura Armor. Instead of rushing in like moths to a flame, the nanomachines scattered and lingered around me.

Pajijijik!

I casually unleashed lightning towards the nanomachines. It didn't have much effect. They had resistance to electromagnetic pulses and were strong against electricity.

,'It's obvious they were made to counter me. I could probably smash them with aura... but that's too wasteful. And I don't have time for that.'

Should I summon Makina? Since nanomachines are ultimately machines, Makina should be able to handle them. I hesitated and decided against it. Makina was currently performing a task I had assigned her.

,'Anyway, as long as I maintain my Aura Armor, the nanomachines can't approach me.'

In the meantime, Go Youngjin broke free from the lightning chains and turned his hand into a cannon, aiming it at me. The cannon glowed. It was the same railgun as before. If I took a direct hit, my Aura Armor would definitely be pierced.

Jeujeujeujeujeujeujeujeut!

Energy was released from the nanomachines surrounding me, forming a barrier.

,'My body feels heavy, as if I'm in a swamp. Are they restricting my movement?'

I could just use Leap to teleport away, but... I decided to face him head-on. I didn't think I would lose.

It was disadvantageous that I moved late... but I had a way to turn the tables.

[Using a Fleeting Moment (Instant). Remaining stacks: 6]

I summoned Vajra Thunderbolt from my inventory and floated it in the air.

Pajijijijik!

An electromagnetic rail formed around the Vajra Thunderbolt. At the same time as I punched the Vajra Thunderbolt, Go Youngjin fired the railgun.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Electromagnetic Cannon (電磁砲).

A projectile that easily surpassed the speed of sound collided in the air. The outcome was decided in an instant.

The Vajra Thunderbolt pierced through the projectile Go Youngjin used and went on to penetrate Go Youngjin's heart.

Thud!

Go Youngjin, with a hole in his chest, collapsed to the ground on his knees.

,'As expected of Vajra Thunderbolt. It consumes more mana than tungsten bullets, but its power is stronger.'

I walked towards Go Youngjin. Go Youngjin was still alive. The nanomachines in his body were temporarily acting as a heart, keeping Go Youngjin alive.

,"Goddamn it...! Why am I losing? I should be stronger than you?!"

It was a voice filled with spite, abandoning any pretense of politeness. But the situation didn't change. I raised my sword and said.

,"The 7th Legion's mechanical demons must have modified your body. Where is Germadon?"

I had ordered Makina to locate the mechanical demons that had descended upon Seoul. The mechanical demons were rampaging like crazy, but strangely, their legion commander, Germadon, was nowhere to be seen.

,"Germadon? I don't mind telling you."

Go Youngjin reached out to me. Immediately after, an android crashed down through the ceiling. I immediately stabbed my sword into Go Youngjin's head.

The steel android that fell from the ceiling rushed towards Go Youngjin, not me. Go Youngjin's body was instantly broken down into molecular units and attached to the android's body.

,'The nanomachines in Go Youngjin's body disassembled Go Youngjin's body...!'

All the nanomachines around gathered to the android.

-I found Germadon's information. The android in front of you is highly likely to be Germadon!!

,"...!!!"

[Using a Fleeting Moment (Instant). Remaining stacks: 5]

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

I slashed the android in half. The android fell to the ground and disintegrated. It turned into tiny dust-like nanomachines and enveloped me.

,"Got you."

A gloomy mechanical sound was heard.

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) discerns the demon.』

『Germadon. 7th Legion Commander.』

『Germadon's authority is Reconstruction. He can reconstruct himself and his targets. 』

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) desires the death of the demon legion commander.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

The nanomachines that enveloped me were reconstructed into a special space like a prison.

『Germadon uses the authority of Reconstruction on you.』

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) resists the authority.』

I could withstand the authority being exerted on my body.

The problem was that the building I was trapped in was a kind of barrier and a power plant. The power source was me. My mana and lightning were being exploited by Germadon in real time.

-Yujin!! Summon me!!

Makina shouted. I ignored Makina's words for now and surveyed the situation with my Mind's Eye (Cheonan).

Germadon reconstructed his body around the power plant where I was trapped. He disassembled and absorbed the materials of the dueling arena building, the robots and androids flying in from all directions, and the countless cars scattered on the roads with the authority of Reconstruction, growing his body.

Another power plant appeared next to the power plant where I was trapped. The highest-ranking spirit of darkness was being exploited in that power plant.

,"Yujinaaaaa!!"

Seong Hari-ssi, who was fighting Son Jeong-eon and the Buster Clan, was startled and threw her spear with all her might towards the power plant where I was trapped.

Kwajik!

Seong Hari-ssi's spear was embedded in one side of the power plant. But the spear was reconstructed, repairing the power plant and being absorbed into Germadon's body.

Germadon continued to grow his body with the ability of Reconstruction. Germadon, who had disassembled and absorbed the entire dueling arena and reconstructed it, was already over 10 meters tall.

,"This world is good because there are many resources to utilize."

Germadon repeatedly disassembled, absorbed, and reconstructed the machines nearby, gradually increasing in size.

There was no one who could stop Germadon. The heroes were busy dealing with the mechanical demons rampaging in various parts of Seoul, and Seong Hari-ssi was too busy dealing with Son Jeong-eon and the Buster Clan to intervene.

Germadon had already surpassed 20 meters in size.

,'If Germadon absorbs all the resources of Seoul like this... he'll grow to over 1,000 meters? I kind of want to see that.'

But if that happened, Seoul would truly be doomed.

,"Demon Hunter. Summon the Machine Spirit."

If I summoned her here, Makina would be trapped in the power plant like me. There was no guarantee that Makina's abilities would work against Germadon, and Makina could even be absorbed by Germadon.

Germadon.

A super artificial intelligence created and transcended in another world, becoming a demon legion commander.

I felt his enormous presence and smiled.

The Germadon here was definitely the main body.

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) is certain!』

There was no possibility that it wasn't Germadon's main body.

,"Germadon. I've been waiting for this moment."

I used the hacking that I had been saving and cherishing for this moment.

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the super artificial intelligence Germadon for 5 seconds.]

,"You?!"

,"Self-destruct, Germadon."

An order that no machine could ever refuse.

Germadon used his authority, Reconstruction, on himself. Everything about him crumbled into dust.

『You have killed the Seventh Legion Commander! The Demon Hunter (SS+) rejoices!』

It was an anticlimactic end for a legion commander.
