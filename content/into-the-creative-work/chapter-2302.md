I arrived at the Valdert mansion. From the entrance, I could feel its grandeur.

You could say it's fitting for one of the five dukes of the Empire Ogong, the most successful power in Atlantis.

The knights guarding the entrance saluted me as soon as they saw me.

"Seong Yujin-nim. Have you arrived?"

"I came to see Elena. Is she here?"

"The Duke-kakka has temporarily left the mansion due to a summons from the Imperial family. If there's nothing special, he said he would return around evening... Please go inside the mansion and wait."

"I will."

"I will guide you."

The knight of the Valdert Dukedom was polite. As I entered the mansion, the servants stood in an orderly manner and bowed their heads.

"Welcome back, Yujin-nim. We will serve you without any inconvenience."

I was satisfied with the servants' extreme hospitality. It was indeed worthy of a Dukedom. For your information, there was also my own room in the mansion. A room that doesn't pale in comparison to Elena's bedroom at all.

"This is Arel. He is a talent who will become Elena's adjutant. For now, treat him with utmost care as a guest."

"We will treat him as a VIP."

The servants of the Dukedom listened to me well. They were all elites and knew their place well.

Arel, who was next to me, nodded as if he understood something.

"Indeed. I understand the relationship between the Duke and you-kakka."

"As expected of Arel. You noticed right away. Elena and I are special lovers."

"It seems to be more than that. Anyway, since this is where I will be working, I will look around the mansion. I also want to learn about the Empire and the Valdert family."

"Ask the butler. He will tell you."

I decided to take a shower first.

• • •

Elena confidently walked through the Imperial palace. The Imperial family and the Valdert Dukedom had an awkward relationship since the last incident. The only fortunate thing was that they didn't openly antagonize each other.

To be exact, they couldn't. The Imperial family also had to consider the public's opinion. Elena had once sacrificed herself for the people, and that image continued to this day, with the people viewing Valdert positively. No matter how much of an Imperial family they were, they couldn't completely ignore the people's opinions.

And since the Valdert Dukedom was the same, the Imperial family and Valdert remained distant. They ignored each other. The Imperial family sat solemnly at the center of the Empire as always, and Valdert was busy fixing the declining family.

So today, when the Emperor summoned the Empire Ogong, was a special day. The nobles who knew about this summons predicted that the Imperial family and the Valdert Dukedom would finally return to their former relationship. Because the former Valdert was a loyal family belonging to the Emperor's faction.

In reality, Elena, the head of Valdert, had no loyalty to the Imperial family at all.

If the odds were good enough, she could betray the Imperial family and start a rebellion without hesitation.

She was completely different from her old self. The Imperial family? The Empire? It didn't matter if they collapsed. All that mattered to her now was one man and her family.

Elena's steps towards the conference room were lighter than ever. It felt almost frivolous. A smile hung on her lips, and her sapphire earrings swayed and sparkled with each step she took.

The knights and servants working in the Imperial palace bowed and greeted her. Elena didn't even glance at them.

The great nobles, called vassals of the Imperial family, cautiously spoke to Elena. From the outside, Elena seemed to be in a good mood.

"Duke of Illusion-kakka, I would like to bring Valdert's specialty, wine, to the Imperial palace. I wonder if a conversation is possible afterwards...."

"I am Viscount Biret, an Imperial administrator. I heard that you-kakka can restore ancient ruins. If you could help us, the Imperial family would offer various benefits...."

"Your Excellency. It would be mutually beneficial to resume trade between the Valdert territory and the Imperial family.... Your Excellency! A conversation! Please spare some time! Your Excellency!"

But it was a misunderstanding.

Elena openly ignored all the vassals of the Imperial family. It was an enormous rudeness, but no great noble dared to say anything. Even if they were nobles working in the Imperial family.

A knight was standing in front of the conference room, but she ignored him and opened the door. Inside the spacious conference room, everyone was gathered except for two people. Elena's gaze turned to the Emperor, who was located at the head seat. The Emperor looked down at Elena with sunken eyes.

"Greetings, Your Majesty. I am glad to see that you are in good health."

The Emperor's eyebrows twitched. Elena's voice showed no respect or loyalty at all.

"...Duke of Valdert. Take a seat. I believe you know what situation the Empire is in without me having to say it."

"I don't think it's a particularly dangerous situation..., but it seems that His Majesty the Emperor has a different opinion."

Elena sat down at the large round table. Three dukes were already seated. The rarely seen Duke of the Millennium, Kalon Brantis, the guardian of the Imperial family, Duke of the Iron Blood, Holind Ronmasid, and the unmotivated Duke of the Foolish Sword, Noite Arpis.

'I still can't figure out what the Duke of the Millennium is thinking. The Duke of the Iron Blood's gaze is intense. It's like he's going to breathe fire. It must be because of the Sleeping Princess. He's already requested a private meeting several times, but I've rejected them all. He must be anxious inside. I can use him. The Duke of the Foolish Sword is still the same.'

The empty chair belonged to the Duke of the Water Robe, Eilemus Overfrost.

'I deliberately came late to test the Emperor's reaction.... I didn't expect that sly man to be later than me.'

The Duke of the Water Robe was the most secretive man after the Duke of the Millennium. According to the information, he is associated with a cult.... But in Elena's eyes, it was not yet certain. Even if he was involved, he was not Elena's enemy. Until now.

Tick-tock.

The pendulum of the clock hanging on the conference room wall swings. Everyone remained silent.

'How boring. I thought the Emperor would react to something.... Is he planning to keep his mouth shut about the last incident?'

Elena spread out her palm. A blue butterfly appears. Everyone's eyes turned to Elena. The butterfly turned into a wine glass filled with wine.

「Dionysus's Wine

Wine made by God.

Mana increases by 2 upon tasting the first sip.

Rank: A」

Dionysus's wine is originally B rank. This is a wine made with Illusion Transformation magic that Seong Yujin enhanced with the Path to Enlightenment. More than 5 years of lifespan disappeared, but she didn't care.

Sip.

Taking a sip felt like falling into heaven. Elena smiled without realizing it.

"Duke of Illusion. You are drinking precious wine. Does this conference seem like a joke to you?"

"Your Majesty. I cannot live without wine. If I don't drink wine once an hour, my hands tremble and my thoughts stop. Please understand that wine is like the water of life to me."

"Was the rumor that the Duke of Illusion is an alcoholic true...? I understand. But I am also thirsty. Duke of Illusion, can you give me a glass of wine too?"

"I am sorry, Your Majesty. As you know, my ability consumes lifespan. This wine contains more than 5 years of lifespan."

"I heard that you have overcome your lifespan. Is it true?"

"How can a person overcome the limits of lifespan? It's just a rumor."

"......."

The Emperor didn't seem to believe it. Elena shrugged and drank her wine. Everyone here probably knows something about her lifespan to some extent. Of course, they don't know the details.

Gulp.

The taste of the ecstatic wine made her feel good.

About 10 minutes passed. The conference room door opened and a man with green hair appeared. The man, who had an unpleasant expression as if he had been beaten by someone, soon erased his expression, apologized to the Emperor, and sat in the empty seat.

"You must have seen what appeared when the sky broke. The sight of it drove all the people mad and killed them, and time was revived by the system. Since the people do not remember their deaths, it would be more accurate to say that time has turned back."

The Duke of the Foolish Sword opened his mouth.

"Your Majesty. It is already over. It has left somewhere. The system has completely erased its influence. There will be nothing to worry about."

"The gods have begun to actively intervene. Do you see that as a coincidence? I have decided that the destruction of Atlantis has begun. Do not forget. This is not the first time for Atlantis, and it is our last chance. If we fail to successfully conquer Atlantis, we have no future."

The Duke of the Foolish Sword closed his mouth. Instead, the Duke of the Water Robe spoke.

"Your Majesty... Do you want us to take the lead in the conquest?"

"I know that you are fulfilling your duty to protect the Empire. But that alone is not enough anymore. We are actively using the exiles to minimize the damage to the Empire... but it is too slow. You must now actively participate in the conquest of the areas. I will not discuss the method, but I forbid any evil methods."

"......I understand."

It was a warning to the Duke of the Water Robe, who is associated with a cult.

Elena drank the last sip of wine.

'In the end, the reason for summoning the Empire Ogong was to urge us on?'

The Emperor specifically ordered the dukes to do their jobs, like a professor giving assignments to students.

"Duke of the Millennium, tend to the garden."

"Yes."

Elena narrowed her brows. The garden? He probably doesn't mean to tend to the Imperial garden.

"Duke of the Iron Blood, crack down on the inside of the Empire. Punish the nobles who neglect their duties."

"Yes. Your Majesty."

A bloodbath will occur within the Empire. Elena remembered this information well. Because it will be very valuable information.

"Duke of the Foolish Sword, deal with the other races and inspect the borders."

"I understand."

The Duke of the Foolish Sword will be busy.

"Duke of the Water Robe, investigate the Empire's environment and prepare for pollution."

"Your Majesty. What kind of pollution are you talking about?"

"Before the end of this year, a large amount of volcanic ash will cover the continent."

"...Yes. I will."

The Duke of the Water Robe replied with a questioning expression.

Elena's mind was complicated.

'How did he know? Does the Emperor have the ability to see the future? ...Did information leak from Helten?'

It was also strange that the Emperor was suddenly taking such an active role.

"Duke of Illusion, restore the Empire's Guardian Stone in the south. It won't be difficult with your abilities."

Elena stared straight at the Emperor and replied.

"Your Majesty. I must fix my declining family."

"...Are you saying that your family is more important than the Empire right now?"

"Yes. Your Majesty."

"Without the Empire, your family would not exist."

"That is not certain."

"......."

"......."

Elena calmly accepted the Emperor's gaze. It didn't matter if she was declared a traitor. If Valdert wanted to be a traitor, she would gladly become one. Elena was confident. Because she had a man who was much greater than the Empire.

After several tens of seconds of confrontation, the Emperor sighed.

"Haa. I give up. I must apologize to the Duke of Illusion first. I officially apologize on behalf of the Imperial family for what happened last time. If there is anything else you want from me, tell me. I will try my best to fulfill your request."

Elena raised the corners of her lips. He should have come out like this from the beginning.

"Your Majesty. The biggest concern of the Valdert family is the lack of an heir."

"...Hmm. Not having an heir is an unsettling thing. Do you want a spouse? How about the Crown Prince? If you have two children, all problems will be solved."

Elena's face began to rot.

"Your Majesty. I wouldn't take that dud even if you gave him to me."

"......Dud. That's harsh."

"You seem calm for someone who was just called that."

"I know about your relationship with the Crown Prince. Since you are enemies, good words will not come out. I understand. Another spouse...."

"I already have a partner who I am promised to marry."

In fact, they haven't promised yet, but it will happen.

"...Hmm. That man. So? Should I give him a title?"

"That's not it. Please help me with the ceremony. I want to have the best wedding ever. More than the Imperial family's wedding. I want all the people of the Empire and all the gods to bless my wedding."

The Emperor didn't understand what Elena was saying for a moment. Suddenly, the best wedding ever? He thought for a moment and nodded. If the relationship with the Duke of Illusion improves, there is no reason not to cooperate.

"Good. When is your wedding? It would be nice to have at least a year of leeway."

"......It has not been decided yet. I will let you know when it is decided."

Elena said in a determined voice.

Right now, she didn't care about the Empire's crisis or anything else.
