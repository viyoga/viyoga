Gracia Hertis is lazy.

The Medicine King Kersha and others who knew her often judged her that way. Gracia didn't particularly feel any aversion to that assessment. She thought so herself.

But looking a little more closely at her life, rather than lazy, she simply had no interest in the world itself.

Her original world had been destroyed.

A world ruled by vampires.

A world where days were short and nights were long.

The Tower appeared in that world. For vampires, who were strong by nature, the Tower's trials themselves were nothing.

The problem was the sun.

The moment they challenged a floor, the intense sunlight pouring down killed vampires. Only True Ancestors and high-ranking vampires could withstand even a little of the direct sunlight. To vampires, the Tower was a calamity of annihilation itself.

Fortunately, Gracia reached the Tower's 70th floor and became a Transcendent before her world was completely destroyed.

The few surviving vampires who couldn't become Transcendents left for dimensions other than the Tower. They feared the Tower, and they lacked the power to become Transcendents.

Some True Ancestors wanted to stay with her. For the propagation of vampires. True Ancestors are born only through biological reproduction, not through minions.

Unfortunately for them, Gracia had zero interest in such things. She was the kind of person who could shrug even if vampires went extinct tomorrow. On top of that, her nature disliked entangling with other beings.

So Gracia holed up in the Tower.

The problem was that she was bored.

Even in her original world outside the Tower, nothing had caught her interest, and inside the Tower it was even worse. The one way she alleviated her boredom was reading novels, but inside the Tower, getting novels was difficult to the point of impossible.

Even when she occasionally got her hands on a novel, they were all dull. And writing novels herself held neither interest nor fun.

She had Kersha, a friend she'd made in the Tower, but they didn't meet every day, and she didn't want to. Even a friend is ultimately another person. Other people were a hassle.

With nothing else to do, all that remained for Gracia was sleep.

While asleep, she didn't have to do anything. She didn't have to feel bored. Time passed well. Fortunately, vampires were a race that could sleep as long as they wanted.

When Gracia woke after 5 years and obtained the panacea, honestly, she wasn't that happy. Overcome the sun and conquer the Tower? She had no grand goal of conquering the Tower.

In the end, it was just a pastime. Tower climbing was one of the few things available in the Tower.

And even that was starting to get old. She'd conquered the 90th floor but was stuck at the 91st. She had no intention whatsoever of training to conquer the 91st floor. Training was neither fun nor anything but a hassle.

Just as Gracia was starting to think about going back to sleep, she was summoned by Seong Yujin.

Terrifying women surrounded her. She felt it the moment she saw them. These women were batshit crazy.

And so, one way or another, she was dragged off by the crazy fox woman. Of all people, it had to be the scariest one of them all. Why was she scary? She just was. Her looks, her aura, everything. She wanted to run, even if it meant killing herself. The only reason she didn't was that it seemed like it would cause a truly massive disaster.

"Wh-what is this..."

"Ahh. This is called a smartphone. The second heart of modern people. You've gained your second heart today."

"I-I don't need a second heart..."

When she touched the rectangular black metal slab, light miraculously appeared. There were many unfamiliar terms, but the operation itself wasn't difficult.

"Shut up. Look at this. It's called Shorts."

It was video.

Since the crazy woman told her to, Gracia watched.

And 30 minutes had passed.

"Huh?!"

Gracia wiped the drool running down her chin. Her brain had apparently been melting until just now.

"Fufu. Fun, right? The trial version ends here. Now hand it over."

"N-no! This is my heart!"

Gracia clutched the smartphone tightly in both hands and pressed it to her chest. Her large breasts were perfectly suited for hiding a smartphone.

"I'm kidding. You can do way more with a smartphone. Look here. You can even order food. It's hubby's account, but... hubby won't care. Gracia. Have you heard of chicken?"

"Wh-what's that?"

She tasted chicken for the first time in her life.

It was garlic chicken.

She felt the malice of that crazy fox bitch trying to feed her garlic by any means, but fortunately, she'd overcome her garlic allergy with the panacea.

"Th-there's a taste like this..."

The taste of chicken was undeniably excellent. But what drove her crazier than the chicken was cola. The sweetness and refreshment of cola was shocking enough to bring tears to her eyes.

On top of that, delivery was incredibly convenient. A few finger movements and food arrived at the door in 30 minutes. She didn't even need to face the delivery person.

"To understand Earth, you just need to understand the internet."

Gracia followed Miryeong and sprawled on the highest-quality bed. Eating snacks and drinking cola, she surfed the internet as Miryeong guided her. When she came to her senses, 5 hours had vanished.

"This time, you try ordering delivery."

"L-like this?"

This time she ordered something called pizza. It was just as delicious as the chicken.

Gracia demolished a whole pizza and, dragging her full body along, flopped right onto the bed. Then she skillfully manipulated her smartphone, her lips curling into a grin. Shorts, webtoons, web novels—she picked whatever she wanted.

"Are you a vampire or a pig?"

"V-vampires don't gain weight."

"Whatever. Get up. Let's play a game together."

"A g-game?"

Gracia did as Miryeong told her. Everything Miryeong had shown her so far had been fun without exception. Now she was actually looking forward to it.

"You're a tool. Just do what I tell you. ...Hey! Don't hit the minions!"

"Y-you hit them too. Gold comes out."

"You crazy bitch! Why are you suddenly charging in?!"

"Sh-she's the one who made me look bad first."

"No! Why are you going in?! Don't you know our team isn't all here yet?!"

"I know."

"This crazy bitch, seriously!"

"Aagh! Aagh!"
