I briefly entered the world of [Ascension to the Light]. It was to create a martial art exclusively for the Holy Knights.

However, even with talent boosts, martial arts weren't just popping up out of nowhere.

After pondering alone for several tens of minutes, I came to a conclusion.

,I don't know. Let's get help from the expert, Wiyu-ssi.'

And so, I went to Wiyu-ssi and explained the situation.

Wiyu-ssi, who had her arms crossed as if showing off her large breasts, listened to my words, her lips curving into an arc.

,A martial art that doesn't use any internal energy at all, huh? Interesting.'

,I'm definitely not looking for external martial arts.'

,I know.'

External martial arts were also martial arts. External martial arts don't completely ignore internal energy. It's about training the body with the natural energy floating in the atmosphere to have a body that transcends human limits. That's the external martial arts without internal energy cultivation methods that I know.

,These days, external martial arts are only called external martial arts, but they all have internal energy cultivation methods.'

There is ki, a mysterious energy that exists in the world of [Ascension to the Light]. It's as obvious as the sky above and the ground below. It doesn't make sense not to use it, even in external martial arts.

,In the first place, martial arts is a combination of external and internal energy.'

,So, what's the direction of the martial art you want?

I thought for a moment at Wiyu-ssi's question and said.

,Versatility. The martial art shouldn't be too difficult. But it shouldn't be too easy either.'

,If that's the case, there doesn't seem to be any particular worry. The reason why martial arts are difficult is mostly due to internal energy cultivation methods. It's common to train external martial arts according to internal energy cultivation methods. What you want is a martial art that eliminates everything else and only has stances.'

,Yes. Because that world has no ki.'

,This world adapts people to martial arts. What you want is the opposite; you need a martial art that suits people. I need detailed information about those who will learn the martial art. If possible, an intuitive information medium like a video would be good.'

,...I'll go get it.'

I went to the [Eternal Eden] world and copied the Holy Knights' videos. The Holy Knights' basic combat helmets had recording functions, and they were collecting combat data with drones, so it wasn't difficult. I just had to order the underlings to do it.

Wiyu-ssi, after checking the videos I brought, burst into a hollow laugh.

,Ha. They're crazy bastards who have lost their minds. They don't fear death at all. Well, I guess it's natural since they said they'd be resurrected even if they die.'

,Master-nim. I hope they still don't fear death.'

,At the same time, they need to be able to deal with monsters other than humans. Originally, the basis of martial arts is to protect oneself.'

,...Is it not possible?

Wiyu-ssi laughed.

,Why wouldn't it be possible? There were already several martial arts that were based on death. Don't limit martial arts.'

We put our heads together and began to create a martial art.

,It's a martial art that doesn't use internal energy. That means you can't strengthen your body with internal energy. It's impossible to show overwhelming power with martial arts alone.'

,I know. So let's go in the most efficient direction possible. Ah, I hope it's not limited to swords or spears, and that any weapon can be used. Bare hands are a given, too.'

,Weapons are ultimately held and swung by hand. The martial art will become somewhat difficult, but... it's not a big problem. But you can't include everything. So, we'll remove as much as possible that's unnecessary. We'll simplify the stances and compress the martial principles. There's no need to understand the martial principles. The principles are already melted into the form itself.'

The martial art was created much faster than I thought. It was mostly due to Wiyu-ssi's abilities. Her level was one thing, but her depth of knowledge about martial arts was unbelievably high.

,A martial art that the martial artists of this world wouldn't even look at has been created. Have you decided on a name for the martial art?

,Yes. This is a martial art with no turning back. A martial art that gambles with death. In actual combat, even a slight mistake will result in serious injury, and if you're afraid of death, its power and efficiency will decrease. The moment you learn this, you have no choice but to move forward, so I'll call it Geukjinmu (Extreme Advance Martial Arts).'

,A good name. Now that the martial art has been created, all that's left is to test its power. If it's a world with advanced technology, you can run simulations with AI. Bring it. I want to see how great AI is.'

,...Yes? There is AI, but not to that extent....'

As I was speaking, something flashed through my mind. There's no need for AI from the Eternal Eden world. There's also Madoka, an AI in reality. Unlike the Eternal Eden world, which can only take things out, it can also put them back.

,Madoka seems to have a bit lower performance.'

Although it wasn't AI, there was something with better performance and efficiency than Madoka.

I took out a pet cage that looked like a small blue box from my inventory. Inside the cage was Makina.

,Eung? What is it againaaaa?!'

Makina grabbed the bars and shook them. Wiyu-ssi looked at Makina with an interesting expression.

,Judging by the energy I feel, it's definitely not human. Its essence is closer to a mystical beast.'

Makina, who had been rampaging, became quiet as soon as she saw Wiyu-ssi. Judging by her tense expression, she seemed to have sensed Wiyu-ssi's power to some extent. Makina seems to be oblivious, but she's also quick-witted.

As soon as I released Makina from the pet cage, she flew over and clung to my shoulder.

Makina, who was properly scared of Wiyu-ssi, did as she was told even without the Spirit Orb.

,Makina. Make a simulation machine.'

,Ah, okay....'

Makina used her ability. A simulation machine popped out of thin air. Of course, it used my mana.

As Wiyu-ssi's eyes widened in admiration, Makina put her small mouth to my ear and whispered.

,You have to give me the Spirit Orb later. Got it?

As expected, she was quick-witted.

,...Okay.'

I decided to just give it to her because she would definitely sulk and be annoying if I didn't.

The core of the simulation machine that Makina made was Makina.

If Makina used her computational power with determination, she could show computational power that couldn't even be compared to a supercomputer. She usually doesn't use much computational power, so she seems like an idiot.

,Of course, it'll eat up mana... but I in this world can handle it and have mana to spare.'

Data is needed to run the simulation. There was no problem with the data. Right now, Wiyu-ssi and I are here. Our sparring will be enough data. And the information about the monsters to be simulated can be brought from other worlds such as the Savior of Reality and the Academy, Baekhwan, and Eternal Eden.

,There's a lot of data to bring.'

It wasn't a difficult task.

I shoved information into the simulation machine and started running the simulation. 100 times per second. Mana began to drain quickly. I focused on my breathing. I had to fill it as much as it was drained.

After several tens of seconds, when I was about to be exhausted, Makina showed me a summary of the simulation. Unlike me, who was busy replenishing my mana, Wiyu-ssi leisurely watched the summary and said.

,It's rougher than I expected. It needs to be refined. Ah, try running the simulation with other weapons.'

,Ugh, yes.'

Makina, who answered quietly, began to take even more of my mana.

We were able to complete Geukjinmu by running hundreds of millions of simulations day and night. In the process, there were a few times when I couldn't drink mana potions and had all of my innate energy sucked out, causing me to die.

,Now that the martial art is complete, it's time to master it.'

Wiyu-ssi looked at me and smiled. I tensed up and stood up. You can't take things into the Eternal Eden world. What's allowed is experience and knowledge. To apply Geukjinmu to the Holy Knights, I had to master it and teach it to them.

,...Let's take it easy. Please.'

,Don't be so dramatic. It's training that doesn't even use internal energy, isn't it?

,.......'

I can guarantee it. I'm probably going to have a hell of a time for the next few days.

After learning Geukjinmu and entering the Eternal Eden world, I summoned the Holy Knights.

A total of 72 people stood in a line in front of me. Their gazes were directed at me. It was a bit creepy because they were all men.

,From now on, the Holy Knights, no, you'll be the Knights from now on. I will pass on Geukjinmu, a martial art exclusively for the Knights, to you. Geukjinmu is a martial art that can only be learned by those who do not fear death. In other words, it's a martial art for you. If you seek death, you will live, and if you seek life, you will die. Do not fear death.'

I first taught them the forms of Geukjinmu.

,It'll take at least two months just to master the basics of Geukjinmu. It'll take even longer to become proficient.'

As long as it takes, the combat power of the Holy Knights will become even stronger when they fully master Geukjinmu.

It didn't take long for the Knights to learn the stances of Geukjinmu. They were the elite of the elite. Most of them were smart, too. Their physiques were even better.

,Now fight each other. Fight like it's a real battle.'

Because I'm here, no one will die permanently. The Knights knew that, so they unleashed murderous attacks on each other without hesitation.

The Knights were trained through death.

,Honestly, I think it's lacking, but... the only thing left is to experience real battles and get stronger.'

Korea is still teeming with zombies, variants, and mutations. Problems were erupting everywhere, so I couldn't keep the Holy Knights, who were highly skilled personnel, cooped up in the training grounds.

It was then. Cardinal and Commander of the Guard, Son Tae-hyung, stepped forward.

,Your Holiness. Is the Holy Guard really being reorganized into the Knights?

,It hasn't been done officially yet, but that's the plan. The day I seize power, the Knights will be revealed to the world. The Knights will be specialized, and the 1st Knights will be taken over by the Guard.'

,So I'll be the commander of the 1st Knights. I heard that the commander of the Knights has the authority to name the Knights.'

It seems they heard it from Choi Hye-jin and Irina. I was too lazy to name them, so I left it to them.

I was thinking of naming the Knights in pure Korean to appeal to the people's patriotism, but Choi Hye-jin told me to fuck off and named the Knights Eagles.

,If I become the commander of the Knights... I will name the Knights Thunderbolt!

I was going to name them Mir. Honestly, Thunderbolt seems better than Mir.

,Reason?

,Lightning is a symbol of Your Holiness! We will become the divine punishment that Your Holiness inflicts on the enemies!

Lightning was indeed famous as my symbol.

,There's no reason to refuse. Do as you wish.'

Mokpo, South Jeolla Province.

Currently, Mokpo was occupied by a group of fish mutants, and the military was carrying out a recovery operation. In other words, it was a battlefield.

The situation on the battlefield was not good. Despite receiving maritime and aerial support, fish mutants were constantly pouring out. This is because the variant located in the center of the city devours the corpses of humans, animals, and plants, turning ordinary fish into fish mutants with legs that walk around.

Aerial bombardment? This variant knew how to use its head. It used the rubble of buildings to create air-raid shelters that were safe from bombing. In case of emergency, it dug into the ground and hid.

Fish mutants were resistant to bullets. Also, the water guns they shot out of their mouths had power similar to pistols. Since the fish were gathered in large numbers, the soldiers armed with rifles were being pushed back instead of gaining an advantage.

Moreover, Mokpo was being pushed back in the priority of recovery areas. Support wasn't great either.

,Those goddamn fish bastards! The stench is vibrating all the way here!!'

,Shibal.... I should have been discharged a year ago.... Gae shibal....'

,Shut up and shoot! Don't let them get close to the trenches! Even though those fish bastards' scales are tough, they don't completely deflect bullets!'

The soldiers' expressions were dark. They had rifles, but they couldn't fire bullets as they pleased. It was because of supply problems. They even had to pick up the shell casings after the battle. Even though it wasn't a training situation.

,The fish bastards are pushing in!!'

The division commander swallowed his breath at the adjutant's words.

,They're pushing in while sacrificing dozens of them. Are they going to see this through to the end?

He couldn't lose all of his troops here. The division commander was fiddling with the retreat card in his head. If the situation got worse, he would just pull out, regardless of the operation's failure. It would hurt his career, but there was no choice. It was better than having his soldiers annihilated here.

,Division Commander-nim! We have a radio message from headquarters! The Knights are coming to support us!'

,The Knights? I heard about the mercenaries. What are they going to do if they come now? The front line is already being pushed back.'

,Headquarters wouldn't have sent mercenaries without thinking. According to rumors that are circulating secretly, the Knights are all made up of ability users, and they are experts in dealing with variants.'

,Ability users, huh.... Then the story is different. I'll have to watch and see.'

You never know. They might have tremendous abilities and completely change the course of the battlefield.

A little later, a military helicopter flew in from the sky.

The division commander's expression was crumpled nicely. It was because the mercenaries who were coming to support them were only in one helicopter. The division commander immediately picked up the radio.

,Did you say the Knights? Mercenaries! Do you hear me?!'

-I hear you. This is Son Tae-hyung, commander of the Thunderbolt Knights. We will now begin hunting variants. I'll leave the cleanup to you.

The military helicopter came down to the ground and stopped. The door opened, and knights armed with the latest combat helmets, combat suits, guns, shields, and swords jumped to the ground. Even though it was a height of 5m, there was no hesitation.

Landing lightly on the ground, they advanced towards the enemies with their shields in front.

They massacred the fish while skillfully wielding guns, shields, and swords. There was not a trace of mercy in their attacks. They didn't seem to feel the fear that humans should have, and they advanced while stomping on, beating to death, and tearing apart the fish mutants with their hands.

The division commander and the military were overwhelmed by the sight for a moment. It was an unrealistic sight, as if watching a movie.

,Combat suits.... Those are just excellent in bulletproof performance. To think that those equipment alone would show such a sight. Was the potential of ability users this great?

No.

Their strength is not simply because they have physical abilities superior to humans. They were thoroughly trained. Looking at their movements, you can feel the cooperation and solidarity unique to soldiers.

,Is it a combat unit that the military is secretly raising instead of mercenaries...? Looking at the equipment, the possibility is even greater.'

There is hope. The division commander shouted to his subordinates.

,Support the Knights! Don't let the Knights be surrounded, support those who are breaking through the path!!'

And 30 minutes later.

The Thunderbolt Knights killed the variant that had been giving the division a hard time and dragged its corpse to the division.

,We will stay here for the time being to recover Mokpo and clean up the variants and mutations in the surrounding area. Please send the corpse of the variant to Seoul.'

The leader of the Thunderbolt Knights seemed to be surprisingly easy to talk to. The division commander asked him, relieved in one corner of his heart.

,...That's naturally what we have to do. But you... how are you so strong? Did you receive any special training?

,Special training? I did receive it. But there is another fundamental reason why we are strong.'

,What is it?

,It's because Seongha-nim protects us.'
