I led the troops and moved towards Jecheon City.

From the entrance, the Jecheon Pasque Flowers blocked our way.

Giant flowers reaching 2m in height. The purple petals were impressive. If they were ordinary plants, even at 2m or more, I could ignore them. But the Jecheon Pasque Flowers before me were no ordinary flowers. If you get too close, they spit sap or swing their stems to attack. For a plant, the stem strength is stronger than an average human.

,Seeing it in person, it's even more vivid than watching it on video.'

The most powerful attack of the Jecheon Pasque Flower is the sap that instantly melts the body. However, it cannot melt metal. The combat helmet doesn't melt at all, and the combat suit was specially coated with metal on the surface just for this occasion. It meant that it didn't matter if I got hit by the sap.

,Well, I'm used to killing monsters.'

I move forward. The Jecheon Pasque Flower, which was photosynthesizing, moved. The flower turned towards me and started shooting sap.

,If it senses movement, does it just shoot sap first?'

The speed at which the sap was shot wasn't fast. It was at a level where I could see the movements of the Jecheon Pasque Flower and dodge.

I take a step back.

Chwaaaak.

The sap that fell on the ground splashed everywhere, and some of it got on my body. The combat suit was metal, so it didn't melt.

,It has the same properties as a liquid. If I hadn't been armed with metal, it would have been quite troublesome.'

As soon as the Jecheon Pasque Flower realized that the sap wasn't working, it swung its stem. The stem stretched out and swung like a whip.

I swung the super-vibrating blade I was holding in my hand. It cut through the stem without any resistance. As the distance got closer, the purple petals moved and tried to strike me. I, who had been watching it without letting my guard down, ducked my head to avoid the attack.

,That's a new pattern I didn't identify before. It's not a difficult attack.'

Seogeok.

I cut off the central stem. A transparent liquid flowed out from the cross-section of the stem. The stem seemed to fall as it was, but suddenly it swung, aiming for my legs. I lightly jumped to avoid it and then cut the bottom of the stem. Only then did the Jecheon Pasque Flower completely fall silent.

,Did you see that? If you get close, it hits you with its petals. Don't ignore the petals just because they're petals. If you touch the petals, you'll see that, unlike the soft surface, they're quite thick and hard. And to kill it for sure, cut the bottom of the stem."

,Yes, Seongha-nim."

I grabbed the fallen Jecheon Pasque Flower. If I left it alone like this, it would definitely be a hindrance, so I had to get rid of it in time.

,It's about 70kg. Heavier than I thought.'

I threw the cut Jecheon Pasque Flower behind me. The soldiers in charge of the cleanup rushed over and loaded the Jecheon Pasque Flower onto the truck. Some of the Jecheon Pasque Flower will be sent to the laboratory to become specimens, and most of them will go into the disassembler to become resources.

,Start the work."

The 30 members of the Holy Guard moved in perfect order. The real elite soldiers who had experienced death several times in training due to combat-like conditions were not afraid even if the opponent was a plant monster. They approached the Jecheon Pasque Flower and cut the stems with their super-vibrating blades. It was a mechanical movement without any disorder.

It didn't matter if a variable occurred. All the Holy Guards could use the neural accelerator 'Lightning Dance Mk.1'. If it seemed dangerous, they used the neural accelerator to deal with it.

Due to the overwhelming difference in skill, it was more like a job than a battle. Well, I had predicted it.

,If they were going to struggle with things like this, I wouldn't have created the Holy Guard.'

I turned my back on the Holy Guard, who were doing well, and looked at Choi Hye-jin and Irina.

Irina was doing well. She cut only the stems with arrows that had longer fletchings than usual. Since she was attacking from a distance, the Jecheon Pasque Flower couldn't even react and died. The disadvantage was that it wasn't easy to recover the arrows she shot. Still, she was fighting well in cooperation with the Holy Guard.

,Lee Yeon-hee has powerful abilities, but the cost is high. It's right to save her strength now for important moments.'

Choi Hye-jin was the problem. She was holding a sword instead of the metal bat she usually used, but she wasn't using it well.

,Ah, shit! Why doesn't this cut?!"

It's a super-vibrating blade, but she's not using the super-vibration. It's not difficult to use either. All you have to do is press the button on the hilt.

,She has a good face and body, but her brain is a brick.'

,Choi Hye-jin-nim. You need to press the button on the hilt to vibrate the blade and increase its cutting power."

One of the Holy Guard members, unable to watch any longer, advised her.

,I know that!"

Choi Hye-jin frowned and shouted. She was a brick and had a bad temper too. Still, she somehow used the super-vibrating blade.

,Oh. This is fun?"

She swings the sword awkwardly with an awkward posture. Nevertheless, she's clearing out the Jecheon Pasque Flowers faster than the Holy Guard members.

,Physical abilities that overwhelm the Holy Guard members and instinctive movements that put even beasts to shame. A 5-star potential isn't just for show.'

The work continued.

We went further and further inside. The change happened around that time. A giant Jecheon Pasque Flower, over 5m tall, attached to a building, spread its petals wide and started scattering pollen.

-An unknown substance has been detected in the air. Automatically switching to gas mask mode.

I heard the AI voice from the combat helmet. Thanks to that, I wasn't affected by the pollen.

,Is it some kind of poison? Collect a sample."

,Collection complete."

,Even though the pollen makes the view a bit blurry, it's not like I can't see at all. Continue."

We advanced for 2 hours without rest. Just as we were getting too used to the battle and our tension was easing, a change occurred.

A screen appeared on the combat helmet display, showing the camera view from a drone flying in the sky.

,The Jecheon Pasque Flowers in other directions of Jecheon City are moving behind us. Are they trying to surround us? Even though there are rear troops, they won't be able to stop them. If we stay like this, we'll be trapped in Jecheon City.'

The intention of the Jecheon Pasque Flowers is simple. To overwhelm with numbers.

I checked the map that appeared on the display. We hadn't even reached half of half of our destination. I looked around. The Holy Guard was exhausted.

,The Holy Guard are ordinary people trained to their limits. They're bound to have physical limitations.'

I came to a conclusion.

,We retreat."

This is the first attempt. We got what we needed.

,Lee Yeon-hee. Open the escape route."

I said to Lee Yeon-hee, who had been quietly following us until now.

,Yes. I will."

We retreat. Before we could get out of Jecheon City, the Jecheon Pasque Flowers gathered and blocked our way. Lee Yeon-hee drew a line in the air with her hand. The space was cut, and the stems of about 20 Jecheon Pasque Flowers were cut, falling to the ground.

,Space Severance. It looks terrifying, but it takes some time to cast. And the gesture of waving her hand. Is it to increase her concentration?'

Lee Yeon-hee's breathing became slightly rough.

,She only used it once, and it's already like this. The cost of her ability is high after all. She can only use it 2-3 times in a row?'

But it's a powerful ability nonetheless. Space Severance. No matter what equipment you bring, it's impossible to block that attack.

• • •

3 days have passed since I came to Jecheon City.

The progress of the restoration of Jecheon City is... not even 5%. Even if we somehow kill the Jecheon Pasque Flowers and go inside, we get surrounded after a little time. To avoid being surrounded, we had to retreat. And when we tried again, the Jecheon Pasque Flowers had reproduced again.

,At this rate, it will be sluggish even after 3 months. Should I just do precision strikes with bombers? I want to secure the source of the Jecheon Pasque Flowers....'

After much deliberation, I came to a conclusion.

,Call a helicopter. We'll make an aerial assault from the air to where the source is and target only the source."

,It may be dangerous to retreat using helicopters and drones. The pollen is especially a problem. According to the investigation, the pollen itself has toxic substances and sticks to the machines in large quantities, causing problems. In the past three days, 13 drones have crashed due to pollen."

Lee Yeon-hee suggested using bombers instead.

,Bionics expert Yoo Seo-jung and biologists demanded the source of the Jecheon Pasque Flowers. The Jecheon Pasque Flowers we've captured so far are all duds, so they're not very helpful for research. And if we can capture the source in a relatively intact state... she asserted that bioengineering could develop greatly."

,...Then only the Holy Guard should move. There's no need for Yu Jin-ssi to step up."

,No. I'll move myself. Until now, I've been stepping back to allow the Holy Guard to gain experience, but... this time, I'll finish it for sure."

I didn't allow any objections.

After that, we took a helicopter and moved to the center of Jecheon City. Did they notice the presence of the helicopter? The Jecheon Pasque Flowers started spewing pollen. In an instant, the entire Jecheon City became hazy.

,These bastards. Their reaction is faster than it was 3 days ago. It's not just us who are gaining experience. After all, it was right to finish this quickly.'

The helicopter increased its altitude to a height where the pollen couldn't reach. The Holy Guard rapidly descended towards the destination using fast ropes. It was the rooftop of a building.

,One of the three destinations. If it's not here, we have to take the helicopter to another place right away.'

The Holy Guard members who descended first suppressed the Jecheon Pasque Flowers by swinging their super-vibrating blades in all directions. A Jecheon Pasque Flower, over 10m tall, attached to the building wall, swung its huge stem as big as its size. Lee Yeon-hee, who was still in the helicopter, cut the giant Jecheon Pasque Flower with Space Severance.

I frowned as I looked around after landing on the rooftop. It was hard to figure out what the source was because they all looked similar.

,We mustn't forget that the source may not be a flower.'

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Wave.

30m of electric waves spread out from me. Information from the rooftop, the building below the rooftop comes in.

,There are roots inside the building too. But there's nothing particularly special. Is this a bust?'

Electromagnetic wave exploration is not a panacea. If the source is disguised as a normal individual, I could be fooled.

,I need to be able to distinguish between normal individuals and the source. It seems difficult to distinguish with electromagnetic waves... what other way is there....'

I looked at the rooftop floor. I could see roots that were half buried in the floor. Looking at it like this, it was like an electric wire.

,...Electric wire?"

I immediately squatted down and cut the root to check. The root was a root. It's not an electric wire.

,Originally, plants don't have nerve cells. But this one is different. Looking at its combat methods so far, it has absorbed experience and grown. In other words, it has a brain.'

And it was found that normal individuals receive orders through their roots and move.

I grabbed the root and concentrated. Then I could feel the subtle bioelectricity flowing through the root.

,Heh heh. This makes it easy.'

I send a subtle electric current into the root to trace the location.

I found it.

The source was very close.

The ordinary-sized Pasque Flower attached to the railing below the rooftop was the source. I quickly ran over and pulled the Jecheon Pasque Flower that was the source. It looked ordinary on the outside. That's why it didn't get caught by the electromagnetic waves.

,The source looks the most ordinary."

Perhaps because it was forcibly pulled out, it wriggled in my grasp and then stopped. It was dead.

It didn't matter. Now that I had the corpse, I could just revive it. I ordered the Holy Guard.

,The goal has been achieved. We retreat."
