Light and exciting music, unfitting for the battlefield, blares out.

It wasn't the Roachists who were flustered, but the allied soldiers. To them, the battlefield is a miserable place. Even a small mistake can lead to their own death, not the enemy's. A battlefield that's light and exciting? That's the mark of a psycho.

,"That woman. Was she a singer? No. Just listening to the song, it sounds like idol music...?"

,"I know all the female idols, malimnida. There's no idol like that person. She's not a singer either. She sings well, too... and she dances?"

,"Didn't they say she's the 5th Knight Commander? On the battlefield?"

,"...Wait. Doesn't it feel like our bodies are overflowing with strength?"

A soldier swung his arm in the air. Light yet weighty. No stiffness in the shoulders either. It felt like they had taken a weak physical enhancement drug.

There's no way their bodies would get stronger for no reason. If there's a cause, there's only one.

,"Is it that song? Is the song strengthening us?"

,"Getting stronger by listening to a song. This is like a game. Just listening to a song makes you stronger... amazing. Unlike drugs, there won't be any side effects, right?"

,"Who knows? One thing's for sure... that woman's value is by no means less than the other knight orders. At least in large-scale battles, that woman's ability is essential."

,"That woman? Call her that person. She's a Knight Commander."

Becoming physically stronger means the probability of winning and surviving on the battlefield increases.

The soldiers' morale rises.

On the other hand, the knights were quiet.

The knights' morale doesn't react to most things. They don't feel fear even in desperate situations, and they don't get excited even when things are good. The attitude of a knight facing battle is close to that of a machine. There's only one factor that can directly affect their morale. No, just one person.

,"The bugs' reactions are strange...!"

,"Attacking their own kind?"

Some of the Roachists who had been fine suddenly attacked their comrades next to them.

Internal strife before a large-scale battle? It was incomprehensible. As far as the soldiers knew, Roachists don't kill their own kind, even if they die themselves. Thoroughly controlled biological weapons. That's what the soldiers thought of Roachists.

It was obvious that the cause was the cheerful song echoing on the battlefield.

Of course, not all Roachists were in a state of mental breakdown, attacking their own kind. In terms of percentage, it wasn't even 3%.

,"The Roachist bastards are rushing in! They're blatantly targeting the Siren Knight Commander!"

,"You bug bastards! Stop them! Don't spare the bullets, unload on them!!"

,"The bugs' reactions are a bit slow?"

,"Aim for the vitals!!"

The full-scale war began.

Bullets rained down, and missiles exploded, peongpeong.

The Roachists didn't stop. They feel pain, but not as strongly as humans. Humans also secrete substances in the brain during combat situations, so they don't feel pain. The same goes for Roachists. Roachists rarely retreated once a combat situation occurred.

But recklessly charging in didn't change the combat situation.

,"The bugs' movements have changed!"

,"They're scattering! Spreading out in all directions to induce close combat!"

,"Don't panic. We already knew in advance that some of the bugs are unusually smart. Even if they try to induce close combat, the fact that we're surrounding them doesn't change."

The formation crumbles.

The officers clicked their tongues as they watched him.

,'They could just drive them in and finish them off with bombing... why bother fighting them?'

The soldiers' combat experience. But the soldiers here are all veterans.

Some of the officers realized it was for the Siren Knight Commander's practical experience and ability testing, but they kept their mouths shut.

In any case, victory was certain.

They didn't want to get on the wrong side of the higher-ups by saying unnecessary things. Especially now, when the higher-ups have a lot of power.

,"These bug bastards! They keep coming even with their arms and legs torn off!"

,"They know they're going to die and are trying to take as many enemies with them as possible! These vicious things! The formation is about to collapse! What are the helicopters doing?! If they're just going to watch, why did they even come! Move the drones at least! If you have weapons, use them without holding back!!"

The officer at the front shouted into the radio. The soldiers at the very front were still safe. But it was only a matter of time.

Despite the officer's desperation, the reply from the radio was concise.

,"The knights have moved."

Tang!

A heavy gunshot rang out. No, it exploded.

The officer reflexively turned around.

Knights in white armor were approaching. Not the existing form-fitting metal combat suits. Thick. Their entire bodies were covered in thick metal.

,'...Power armor? I never thought I'd see weapons from movies and games in reality...'

White power armor. A red cloak was draped over its shoulders. A large lightning bolt mark was emblazoned on the chest.

The 1st Knight Order, Thunderbolt.

They approached, pulling the triggers of massive rifles twice the size of K2 rifles. When their guns spat fire, the Roachists' heads disappeared. It was an absurd level of power.

,"Annihilate His enemy."

Knight Commander Sohn Tae-hyung's cold voice spread.

It was solemn.

The officer even felt like he was in a church, not a battlefield.

They shot from a distance, and when bugs approached, they drew their swords and swung them. Each movement felt controlled. They used just enough force to kill the bugs. The battle to kill life even felt elegant.

,"Kyahahahahahaha!"

What awakened the officer's mind was the laughter of a woman, unfitting for the battlefield. He turned his gaze to the source of the laughter, which even felt frivolous.

There, knights in black armor were massacring bugs. They didn't use their guns, even though they had them, and hung them on their hips. Instead, they held melee weapons in their hands. Bats, hammers, swords, chains, and so on. There was even a knight holding a chair.

,"Kill them all!"

The 2nd Knight Order, Eagles.

They had eagle emblems engraved on the chests of their power armor, and thrusters on their backs. Every time the thrusters spat fire, they jumped nearly 5m. And they swept across the battlefield, falling like eagles. The battlefield was their playground.

,'No, I know they're strong, but if they rampage like that, the Roachists' formation will collapse too quickly, making it difficult to respond! It'll become chaos...!'

Look at it now.

A Roachist, pushed by the rampaging Eagle Knights, rushes at a nearby soldier.

And that Roachist died with its head blown off.

,'Sniping?!'

The officer looked around. And he accidentally discovered it. An unnatural space where heat haze seemed to flicker.

,'Optical camouflage? I've heard of it. Rumors of a knight order composed of excellent snipers...'

The 3rd Knight Order, Silence Moon.

They meticulously dealt with the Roachists that the other knights missed. The officer roughly grasped their location, but... when he looked again, there was nothing there. He was grateful that the invisible assassins were allies, not enemies.

Those wearing power armor that looked like it was made by attaching crude metal appeared. An Iron Maiden emblem wrapped in chains was engraved on their chests.

-You are to retreat. We will take your place.

The same voice was heard from the knights' bodies. Even if the regular soldiers didn't know, the officer knew it was the voice of the combat AI, Jena.

The 4th Knight Order, Atonement.

They walked step by step into the most dangerous areas. They didn't care even when surrounded by small groups of Roachists.

The officer saw them as corpses heading to the battlefield to die.

Sinners heading to hell.

The atmosphere of the battlefield changed.

This wasn't war. It was slaughter. A show of knights massacring bugs.

Some soldiers stared blankly at the knights' battle with their guns in hand, and some soldiers cheered for the knights.

,'I knew the knights' combat power was strong, but... one person can handle dozens of bugs and still have strength to spare. That's tank level. No, are they infantry stronger than tanks?'

There's no need to mention how idiotic it is to loosen up on the battlefield.

But the officer also realized that he had unknowingly relaxed.

Reassuring.

He was relieved by the appearance of the knights.

He was grateful that those who mercilessly slaughtered the enemy were allies.

The war with the Roachists ended in less than 10 minutes.

The floor was full of Roachist corpses. The knights walked directly through the battlefield, as if they wouldn't leave a single living Roachist, and finished the confirmation kills.

Joseon Korea had won.
