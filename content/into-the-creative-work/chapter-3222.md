The points, past sixty thousand and creeping toward seventy, filled me with satisfaction. Before entering the Savior of the Academy, I'd held roughly ten thousand points—meaning I'd earned over fifty thousand since.

'Doesn't seem like I spend much time in the Savior of the Academy world. Maybe because I killed Mekin. Accumulated more than I expected.'

Looking at the pile of points, the urge to invest in my stats welled up.

I held back. By Earth's standards, my stats are already top-tier. Even pushing them to 200, I doubt I could beat Bramasen.

'I already decided to raise stats through quests and other methods instead of points.'

Going from 160 to 161 required 5,000 points. From 170, it was 9,000. Naturally, 180 onward would demand even more.

'Rather than spending endless time in the Pleasure World grinding for stats, raising them through quests is far more efficient.'

More than anything, my stats weren't urgent right now.

[Conditions met. Would you like to determine the ending of Savior of the Academy?]

Ending conditions.

I didn't need the Pleasure Life system to spell it out.

'Graduation from the Academy, obviously. And I'm definitely not triggering the ending.'

It's a world packed with my women—Seong Hari, Baal, and others I can't just leave behind. On top of that, I've built a foundation in the Savior of the Academy world.

If I ever need modern goods, I can supply them from the Savior of the Academy world anytime. Triggering the ending just for some reward was far too wasteful.

'Next is Seong Hari's affinity level. Finally hit Level 10.'

Affinity Level 10 allows summoning into reality or other Pleasure Worlds. And I could receive a trait or skill shared from her.

'It's random, so there's that thrill of dread. Like with Yuria, her talent itself could become a skill. Or it could be something useless to me, like Miryeong's Mana Absorption.'

Feeling my heart thud against my ribs, I checked the result.

'Divine Martial Way?'

I checked the shared skill.

[Divine Martial Way (Character Shared Skill: Seong Hari)

For 10 minutes, you gain Seong Hari's talent for martial arts (武).

While Divine Martial Way is active, all talents other than martial arts decline.

Can only be used once every 30 days.]

'It's a skill that lets me use Seong Hari's talent in a limited way, just like with Yuria…!'

There were quite a few differences from Yuria's shared skill, Genius's Time.

Genius's Time had a duration of 10 seconds. A cooldown of 10 days. No other conditions.

'Divine Martial Way is a whopping 10 minutes! But in exchange, all other talents drop while it's active. How far do they drop?'

I thought of Seong Hari. Outside of combat, she wasn't particularly good at much. Cooking being the prime example. That said, it wasn't like she was so incompetent that daily life was a struggle.

'She cleans just fine. Physical labor, she's better than most people. …Well, her physique itself is extraordinary, so that tracks.

She couldn't use magic, and she couldn't use spirit arts either.

'But when it comes to combat, she's the best in the world.'

I wanted to try Divine Martial Way right now.

'Can't exactly use it at home. The cooldown's too long.'

I restrained my itching hands. When I did use it, I'd decided on a dungeon rather than a training ground. It's a combat skill—better to verify it in real combat than practice.

'Should I raise the character summon level too? I might end up summoning everyone across different worlds.'

I decided Seong Hari's first summon would be to the [Martial Arts Tower] world. If I'm summoning her anyway, I should introduce her to my other women.

I'd spent 18,000 points, but 40,000 remained. Satisfying.

'I'll ask about the Summoner Shared Skill in person and decide later.'

The Summoner Shared Skill is one I share with the summoned. It's not random—I choose it. Better to pick a skill or trait Seong Hari needs.

'Divine Martial Way. Gaining Seong Hari's combat talent… what does that even feel like? I'm dying to know.'

It might be similar to Genius's Time. In terms of versatility, Genius's Time is superior. It encompasses talent in all fields.

'Is it like how I feel in combat with Genius's Time active? But Genius's Time is only 10 seconds—way too short. Even Yuria can't go from ordinary to Sword Master in 10 seconds.'

10 minutes.

600 seconds. Sixty times longer than 10 seconds.

'Maybe because I have no talent. I'm dying to know what Divine Martial Way feels like.'

Forty thousand points remained. I decided to save those for later. More than that, I wanted to try Divine Martial Way.

• • •

I entered an A-rank closed dungeon.

This dungeon was originally slated for conquest by the Secal Guild, third among Korea's top ten guilds, but I'd asked for it and received the transfer. Naturally, I paid no transfer fee. If I ask for it, they hand it over—what right would they have to demand compensation?

'Chose this one because there are no S-rank dungeons in Korea.'

The Secal Guild Master gladly transferred the dungeon to me. A wise choice. If he'd refused, I would've called an assembly on him immediately.

&lt;Entering Castle of Kabbalah.&gt;

The dungeon's name was Castle of Kabbalah. I didn't know what Kabbalah meant, but the castle looked like it'd been built in the Middle Ages.

An old fortress exuding an eerie atmosphere beneath an ashen sky.

Perhaps because it was so old, the castle gate itself had crumbled. From within, a knight in black armor strode out. Inside the armor was a rotting corpse. Within the corpse's chest would be a magic stone.

It was a Death Knight—an A-rank undead monster.

Average combat power was around lower-tier A-rank hunter level, with individual variation.

The Death Knight spotted me and raised the sword in its hand. Pitch-black sword aura writhed from the blade. Then it took a combat stance. Watching it, I curled my lips upward.

'Death Knight. From a hunter's perspective, these bastards are worse than shit. They fight like humans. They even use tactics.'

That's why dungeons with Death Knights are handled by large guilds.

As an S-rank hunter, I could drop lightning on the Death Knight before me. Even if it's undead and doesn't die from electrocution, I could burn its body to ash.

But I summoned Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory. My hand gripped the hilt with familiarity. Blue light flickered faintly within the Death Knight's helmet.

[Activating Divine Martial Way for 10 minutes.]

The first thing I felt was discomfort.

Discomfort in the breath I exhaled and inhaled.

I slightly altered my breathing method, following the body's instinct to seek comfort. The discomfort vanished. Just by changing my breathing, the efficiency of mana flowing through my body seemed to rise by about 5%.

'My breathing was corrected by Wiyu… but there was still room for correction. Even Wiyu isn't inside my body, so I suppose that's natural.'

Minute differences in breathing that even Wiyu hadn't caught.

It was different from when I used Genius's Time. With Genius's Time, I didn't bother noticing such trivial things. No—should I say I tried not to notice?

'Even in my current stance, I can feel bits of discomfort.'

Finger placement, head angle, direction of my toes, and so on. I changed everything uncomfortable. These discomforts shifted in real time as the Death Knight approached. And at some point, the discomfort disappeared.

My heart moved before my hand did.

'Now.'

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Mind is Sword (心卽劍).

The Death Knight charging at me was split precisely in half.

Feeling the ache in my right arm muscles, I lowered my gaze. My arm, which had been at my side, was now positioned at the center of my body. The blade tip pointed downward.

Only belatedly did I register that I had swung the sword.

'This is a technique I can't use with my current abilities in reality… how did I do that?'

If asked to explain, I couldn't. But if asked to do it again, I felt I could—as many times as needed.
