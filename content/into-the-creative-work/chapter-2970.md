The strength of the eagle that snatched my shoulder was extraordinary. Probably, most Monsters would be torn to death by this eagle without even resisting.

‘It appeared suddenly from thin air. Seeing the yellow lightning coiling around its body, it must be a subordinate Altherio summoned. Like the rat that attacked my ankle earlier… is it a Lightning Familiar skill?’

I wasn’t careless when the rat attacked my ankle.

My Electromagnetic Wave had dropped while I was dealing with Altherio. On top of that, the rat must have had the ability to conceal its presence. He wouldn’t use an ordinary rat as a Familiar, after all.

‘Even though I bled, it’s no problem.’

Body enhancement using Mana is the most basic of basics.

Even if the rat attacked my ankle, only a little blood came out. There was no problem moving.

The same went for the eagle. Its sharp talons couldn’t pierce my shoulder, even as they gripped it.

‘Even though I’m currently in Lightning Severer Status Window mode, the Demonic Cult Master’s abilities don’t disappear.’

Through deception, I had two Status Windows. But ultimately, there was only one Status Window. Stats and Titles were shared. Skills and Traits were the same. Even while using the Lightning Severer Status Window, I could use Cheonma Shingong. Naturally, I was also affected by the Demonic Body.

‘The reason for having two Status Windows is for the dual contract with the Divine Seats and to deceive those who peek at my Status Window. There’s no other particular meaning.’

Plop!

I deeply thrust the blade of Hwaryeon Bido into the eagle’s body as it soared. Golden lightning, instead of blood, flowed from the eagle’s body.

‘It pierced its heart, yet it still stubbornly flies up without dying.’

Click!

I twisted the hilt and cut the inside. Red thunder mixed and churned within the beautifully golden lightning.

“Kkii-aaaaaahk!”

The eagle let out a scream full of agony. Yet, it didn’t let go of me. And finally, it reached the volcanic cloud mixed with ash.

Rrrrrrroar!

Thunder erupts. A colossal golden lightning flashed like the veins of the black volcanic cloud. It discharged, scattered, and then reassembled to form a single lightning bolt.

‘It’s similar to Thunder God’s Roar, yet different. That’s Astrape.’

To think he would use Astrape in such a way. I was a bit surprised by that idea.

Bang!

The eagle bursts.

The golden lightning exploded, then enveloped my body and turned into ice. The ice, connected to the golden lightning, held me in mid-air. A sight that completely disregarded the simple law of physics that objects fall due to gravity.

“Human common sense and God’s common sense are different. Long ago, I realized this when I obtained the Ten Thunders. What binds you is Ice Thunder, one of the ten thunders.”

Altherio spoke from below. His calm voice held pride.

‘I wondered what kind of Trait Ten Thunders was. Is it an ability to add or change properties to lightning?’

If it was that, I could do it too.

Crackle!

Blue lightning erupted from my entire body, which was restrained by ice. The blue lightning then turned into flames, beginning to melt the ice.

“…Remarkable. Did you also have an ability like the Ten Thunders?”

I merely imbued the lightning with the property of fire. However, I didn’t utter it aloud. It was clear he wouldn’t understand even if I explained it a hundred times. Most exiles like Altherio believe they can only use their power with the System’s assistance. It’s an unconscious limitation.

‘He’s an enemy, not my ally, so there’s no reason to give him helpful advice.’

All the ice that had restrained me melted. I regained my freedom.

“Too late. Astrape is already complete.”

I feel it. The completed Astrape inside that cloud is targeting me.

“Altherio. I can use Astrape too. If you could deflect the trajectory of the Astrape I threw, I can do it too.”

“Indeed. If you can, try it.”

I focused my consciousness on his Astrape. It would be impossible to seize control of it, no matter what. I just needed to twist it so it wouldn’t fall on me.

‘…It’s impossible. My will cannot intervene.’

This isn’t ordinary lightning.

I had no intention of stubbornly clinging to something impossible. I immediately changed my approach. The tip of Hwaryeon Bido pointed to the sky.

“You seem to have realized. The lightning that forms Astrape is special. Descending Thunder. It’s lightning gifted to me by the Lord of the Sky. A slight trace of his power is contained within it. In other words, this Astrape is a divine thunderbolt.”

“I felt a familiar sensation. It contains Authority. What did you offer the Lord of the Sky in return?”

“Offer? Do you not understand the meaning of a gift?”

“……”

He received a skill that wields lightning imbued with Authority, as a gift, without any cost? I knew the Lord of the Sky favored him, but I didn’t expect it to be this much.

‘He’s getting it for free just because he’s handsome.’

If it were a fucking beautiful woman, I could understand. Even I would want to give her something. But that was a man.

Rumble rumble!

The completed golden lightning, his Astrape, falls upon me.

‘The only thing that can effectively counter the power of Authority is the same Authority.’

「Authority Unleashed」

I imbued the red Authority erupting from Hwaryeon Bido, along with lightning, into the blade of Hwaryeon Bido and swung it.

Lightning Heaven Flow, Thunder Tribulation, Annihilation.

The red slash cut through the golden thunderbolt and split the sky. Its trace remained in the space, shattering like a glass window.

“He… cut the lightning…!”

Like a Lightning Severer, I severed the lightning.

I didn’t even use all of my Authority. I might not be able to use Annihilation, but I could use Authority for about 20 more seconds.

“That power…! It’s similar to Descending Thunder. Did you also receive a skill as a gift from him?”

“This is my power. Power I obtained through my own hard work.”

Strictly speaking, it was the power of Hwaryeon Bido. But isn’t the power of the tool also the power of the owner?

Lightning Heaven Flow, Flying Thunder God.

I instantly descended before Altherio. Altherio immediately used Lightning Flash to transform into his Electrified Body.

“Keke. A futile effort.”

Altherio was within the range of my Authority. Electrified Body was meaningless before Authority. From the moment he couldn’t resist Authority, all advantages were mine.

Altherio seemed to instinctively realize this, his face hardening terrifyingly.

I swung my sword.

Altherio blocked it with a spear wrapped in yellow lightning.

Ping!

The sword bounced off.

‘I felt the repulsive force of Authority from the spear just now. The lightning wrapped around the spear. That’s Descending Thunder.’

Altherio’s firm demeanor wavered. He looked exhausted in an instant.

‘It seems that while he uses lightning imbued with Authority as a skill, its cost-effectiveness is utterly shit.’

Unlike him, who uses it directly, I use Authority through Hwaryeon Bido. The disadvantage is a long cooldown after one use, but the advantage is that, apart from the cooldown, I can use Authority as I please without any burden.

‘I’ve won.’

Sensing victory, I assumed my stance.

Just then, I felt a projectile flying from behind me. I instantly changed the direction of my attack.

Lightning Heaven Flow, Lightning Ray.

A red line drew a trajectory in the air, striking down the projectile. Because I was using Authority, I could detect and react. The bullet, cut in half, fell to the ground. It was a considerably large bullet.

‘Those who use guns in Atlantis are limited. Among them, the one who could snipe me… the Old Monster of a hundred accurate shots, Meld Norris. That old hag.’

Altherio tried to create distance, seizing the momentary opening Meld had created.

‘Oh no, you don’t.’

「Uses Moment. Remaining stacks: 10」

I grabbed the shoulder of the man trying to escape in his Electrified Body state. As long as Authority was maintained, Electrified Body was meaningless. I plunged the blade into Altherio’s abdomen. To prepare for another sniper shot, I grabbed Altherio and pushed him forward. As long as Altherio was alive, even the Old Monster of a hundred accurate shots wouldn’t find sniping easy.

“A large amount of Red thunder has flowed into your body. Even if your Authority is released, you won’t be able to escape using Lightning Flash. It’s a Fatal Wound, so you’ll die in a few minutes. I won’t kill you immediately. If you die, that old hag hiding and aiming for a sniper opportunity will annoyingly keep shooting. It would be more efficient to find her location in this state.”

“…Meld, huh. It’ll be impossible to find her with electromagnetic waves. Meld is the comrade I’ve trained with the most.”

“That’s very helpful information.”

It was foolish to believe an enemy’s words at face value.

Lightning Heaven Flow, Electromagnetic Wave.

Perhaps because Altherio was still alive, the Electromagnetic Wave was instantly nullified.

‘Forget sniping, would it be better to quickly kill Altherio and then find that Old Monster?’

It was the moment I harbored murderous intent towards Altherio.

「The Lord of the Sky proposes to you!」

「The Lord of the Sky sponsors 10,000 AP.

“Please spare him! You don’t have deep enmity, do you?”」

Can one say there’s no deep enmity when we were trying to kill each other?

‘Hmm. I can do it. This guy didn’t touch my women, after all.’

In Atlantis, killing each other for mutual benefit was a given. There was nothing new about it.

“Why should I? Even without enmity, it’s better to kill him. Because it eliminates a competitor who might trip me up in the future.”

「The Lord of the Sky sponsors 10,000 AP.

“I will gift you the Descending Thunder skill.”」

“I don’t need it.”

Descending Thunder is an inferior version compared to Hwaryeon Bido’s Authority. At best, it’s just imbuing lightning with a bit of Authority and wielding it. And its efficiency is trash.

「The Lord of the Sky sponsors 10,000 AP.

“I will tell you a way to transcend the limits of lightning.”」

Transcending the limits of lightning. In other words, a way to reach EX rank.

This is somewhat tempting. It’s information not even in the Original Work.

‘If lightning reaches EX rank, then Astrape, which is EX rank, can also exert its full power.’

It’s an opportunity to gain powerful strength.

“Alright. Tell me.”

「The Lord of the Sky sponsors 10,000 AP.

“Kill Typhon and accumulate karma. Thereby, gain the recognition of lightning.”」

Typhon was Zeus’s Arch-Nemesis.

In terms of power, he was on par with Zeus.

‘Kill such a Monster? But where is it? If the Lord of the Sky says so, then a fake Typhon must be somewhere in Atlantis…’

「A rule violation has been detected.」

「The System sanctions the Lord of the Sky.」

I quietly let out a hollow laugh. Couldn’t the System have intervened sooner? Or perhaps the Lord of the Sky truly didn’t know he would reveal the information.

Whatever the reason, it was to my advantage.

I pulled the blade from Altherio’s abdomen.

“Keueueueuk…!”

Betraying the Lord of the Sky and killing Altherio would result in more losses than gains. Turning the Lord of the Sky into an enemy was a huge risk in itself.

“You’re even saving your life because of a God’s love. Keke. Must be nice to be born handsome.”

After mocking Altherio once, I dropped him to the ground.

“I’ve withdrawn the Red thunder. Take care of your treatment and recovery yourself. Surely you’re not incapable of even that.”

Bang!

As soon as I put down Altherio, my hostage, a sniper shot flew.

Only one gunshot.

But 10 bullets flew from different directions.

That was the reason he was called the Old Monster of a hundred accurate shots.

「Uses Moment. Remaining stacks: 9」

I leaped upwards, dodging the bullets.

‘Damn it. Since bullets are flying from different directions, I don’t know exactly which direction she’s in. Even trying to follow the gunshots, they seemed to echo from all directions. As it’s come to this… should I just smash them all to pieces?’

I took out a Mana Potion from my Inventory and poured it into my mouth.
