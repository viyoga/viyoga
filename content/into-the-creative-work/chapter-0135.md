After giving Fesserus a good dressing down, I took a look around the mansion that was being built.

I may have cursed at the dwarves, but objectively speaking, construction was progressing at an incredibly fast pace.

,I want to see the completed mansion soon.'

I designed the mansion. To be exact, I commissioned an architect to create a blueprint of the mansion in the shape I wanted.

The dwarves looked at the unfamiliar modern blueprint, understood it in just a few hours, and immediately started the groundwork.

,I need to build a prison away from the mansion, and a separate building for guests too... There's a lot to do.'

I have enough money. Most of the construction materials were obtained through the Korea Trading Company. Any construction materials that were really hard to find, I got from the modern world and brought over.

,Hehe.... I'm going to fill this big mansion with women.'

I had no intention of letting any men other than myself into my mansion.

Knights or soldiers? I don't need them right now. The security of the city of Tebrah is pretty good. Except for the marine monsters that occasionally come up to the city from the sea, there are no dangerous elements.

,The guards can handle that. If the city of Tebrah develops, I can hire them then.'

I couldn't bring the Plune Knights of the Prukerus County. They belong to the Prukerus County and have the mission of protecting the mansion.

,If I start filling up my forces here, others will think I'm preparing for war.'

Jent will probably react sensitively, so it's better to build up the forces slowly later.

,It wouldn't be good if there were only women in the mansion... but I can make them dress like men. Hehe.'

I smiled, imagining this mansion being completed.

A group of three adventurers came to the Prukerus County. Two were men and one was a woman. Surprisingly, it was Hent, the leader of the Plune Knights, who greeted them.

,This way. This way. This is where our Plune Knights' residence is.'

Hent guided the adventurers.

,Hent. I can't believe you became the leader of the Knights.'

The man with a sword at his waist muttered in disbelief. He had a brown beard that reached his chest, and he kept looking up and down at Hent's body, who was wearing a handsome knight's uniform.

,That's rude, Nopl. Hent has enough skills to become a knight.'

The only woman in the group, Pnerin, said. Wearing a robe and holding a staff in her hand, she was a typical magician. The face revealed through the robe was pretty, but the wrinkles showed that she couldn't help but be middle-aged.

,I thought Hent would succeed. I just didn't know he would succeed to the point of becoming the commander of the Grand Duke's Knights.'

The other one was an elf man with long blonde hair. He had white skin and long ears. Over 150 years old this year, he looked really young except for a few wrinkles on his face. His name was Beder. He's a spirit archer.

,Haha. You guys haven't changed much.'

Hent laughed happily.

Mercenaries and adventurers. They weren't that different in what they did, and they used to get together and travel the continent, attacking dungeons together. That was 15 years ago.

,But is it okay for us to come into the Prukerus County like this?'

Pnerin looked around and asked carefully. Even though she knew there was no one around, she couldn't help but be conscious of the fact that it was the Grand Duke's mansion.

,It's okay. I've already gotten permission from the Countess for you guys to enter. Besides, the mansion is kind of relaxed these days.... It's laid back in many ways.'

,Laid back? Isn't the Grand Duke's mansion supposed to be busy?'

Hent shrugged at Nopl's question.

,The Prukerus County is a bit special. Currently, there is only the Countess in this mansion. The head of the family, Ention-nim, and the eldest son, Jent-gongja, are living in Utren Castle.'

,Huh? Didn't the Count of Prukerus have three sons?'

,The third son, Eugene-gongja, received the title of Baron about two months ago and became the Lord of Tebrah. And the second son, Kyle-gongja....'

Hent let out a hollow laugh. It was an absurd thing to think about now.

,This is a well-known secret that everyone knows but pretends not to know, but Kyle-gongja ran away from home a month ago.'

,Ran away from home?! Why did the gongja? What was he lacking?'

,I don't know. I wasn't that close to Kyle-gongja. I went on a trip to Mount Hughes last time, and I guess he was impressed or something.'

Beder asked in disbelief at Hent's nonchalant tone.

,Is that something a knight should say?'

,It doesn't matter since Kyle-gongja isn't here. More importantly, come inside. This is our Plune Knights' training ground and dormitory. You could say it's our own barracks.'

The three followed Hent inside.

At the entrance, a knight squire was wearing armor and armed with a spear and sword, unlike usual. The squire's face, which was standing still, was filled with an unhideable sense of tension.

,You've arrived! Commander!'

The squire shouted loudly.

Hent nodded.

,Yes. You've worked hard today too.'

After a brief greeting, Hent took his old friends into the building.

,......He was really on his toes... Did you deliberately make him do that because we were coming?'

It was Nopl's question, who had excellent observation skills.

Hent smiled as if he had heard a funny joke.

,That sharp bastard Nopl. He noticed right away.'

In fact, Hent had given a stern warning to the entire Plune Knights. If his old friends came and saw a lax Knights, his image wouldn't be good. Hent wanted to show them his dignity.

,Why would you say such an insulting thing? Our Plune Knights are always on their toes. You never know when something will happen and we'll have to sortie. You have to keep your body tense so you can swing your sword properly when the time comes.'

,Ah.... Sorry. I remembered the old you, and it didn't seem to fit with the current you.'

,I understand. Sometimes when I think of the old me, I'm surprised that I'm the commander of the Knights now. Come in. This is the office I use.'

Hent introduced his old friends to his office. The office was sparkling. He had called the squires yesterday to clean every corner. Currently, Hent's office was so clean that you couldn't find a single speck of dust.

,Oooooh.'

Hent's nose went up at his old friends' exclamations. The swords that were decorated on the wall were especially popular.

,Wait a moment. I'll serve you tea myself.'

,Serve tea? You?'

,I enjoy tea often now. I'll take out a special tea for you.'

Hent took out the magic kettle and teacups that he had prepared in advance. When he turned on the magic kettle, the water inside began to boil.

Pnerin tilted her head, watching Hent take something in his hand and put it in the teacups.

,Huh? What's that?'

,Ah. This is. Instant coffee. Anyone can easily make coffee with just hot water and a teacup.'

,Coffee?'

As a magician who enjoyed tea, she had never heard of a tea called coffee.

,I only see black powder and white powder... Is this tea?'

,This is a type of tea invented by Eugene-gongja. It's popular in the Prukerus territory these days. The Countess also enjoys this coffee often.'

Hent picked up the magic kettle and began to pour hot water into the teacups.

,...This smells pretty good.'

,Is this the popular tea...?

,Did you say coffee? The color isn't very good. It's the color of muddy water.'

The appearance of the coffee wasn't good. The dark brown looked like muddy water. But the scent was good enough to stimulate their curiosity.

,Try it once. You'll know when you drink it.'

Hent said confidently. Hent didn't like coffee very much at first either. He wasn't the type to enjoy tea from the start. But strangely, this instant coffee is strangely appealing whenever he works. He drinks it at least once a day these days.

Hureureup.

As Hent drank the coffee first, his old friends carefully picked up their teacups and began to drink the coffee.

Their eyes widened.

,This is....'

,It's delicious! It's more delicious than black tea?!'

,It's really delicious, unlike its muddy appearance. The scent is also excellent. Is there sugar in it?'

Pnerin looked at a box on one side of the desk. Did he say it was instant coffee? You can easily make it by tearing open that bag, putting it in a teacup, and pouring hot water. And since it's packaged in a bag, it seemed easy to carry around.

,Did you say it's instant coffee? Where did you get it? Can I buy it separately?'

,This... is not something that is sold yet. According to Eugene-gongja, it will be properly sold through the Korea Trading Company in a few months. I have a few boxes, so I'll give you one box later.'

,Really? Thank you, Hent! No, Sir Hent!'

,Just call me comfortably. More than that, try this too. It's a snack invented by Eugene-gongja, and the knights really like it.'

,It looks strange. A snack?'

,Ah. This is a Choco Pie.'
