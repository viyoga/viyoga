I entered the Leisun Power Plant after being guided by an employee at the entrance.

The size of the Leisun Power Plant was larger than expected. It was about the size of two university campuses combined.

Comparing it to a university isn't a coincidence. It's called a power plant, but it's actually the headquarters of the Leisun School, with an atmosphere similar to a university.

I stared at the massive building in the center of the power plant. Above the building, a blue sphere was constantly rotating in the air, generating electricity.

,That's the essence of the Leisun School and the heart of the Leisun Power Plant, the Great Coil.'

I had seen it while playing the original game. But seeing it in person was a completely different feeling. Especially for a magician like me who can sense the power of the elements, the Great Coil felt even more special.

,It's said to be a great legacy created with lost magical engineering technology.'

I wanted to disassemble it and examine every single small part. I wanted to know what kind of magic was infused in it to feel such elemental power.

,If I did that, I'd become an enemy of Neo London.'

Calming my excitement, I tried to be satisfied with just looking.

,The Great Coil rotates, generates electricity, amplifies it, and then goes back into the coil...'

"Ugh."

Without realizing it, I grabbed my head. Unfamiliar knowledge suddenly flooded my mind, causing a headache. The knowledge that surfaced wasn't magic. It was a concept closer to a martial art called Brain Thunder Style. Among them, what particularly caught my attention was a technique called Manroe (卍 Thunder).

The employee who was guiding me approached me, flustered.

"Baron Meyer-nim?! Are you feeling unwell?!"

"It's nothing. I was just shocked by the Great Coil."

"I understand. I also felt a tingling shock when I first saw the Great Coil. The Great Coil is the core and treasure of the Leisun School. I'd love to explain the Great Coil in detail, but... the people from the Magic Association are waiting."

"I can't keep them waiting. I'm fine, so please guide me."

I was guided to a conference room. The guide opened the door and bowed. I walked into the room calmly. Eleven men and women were sitting in the conference room chairs. I could sense mana from all of them. More than half were Rank 7, and the other half were Rank 6 like me. Judging by rank alone, there wasn't a magician inferior to me.

Their gazes were fixed on me. Most of them showed interest in me.

"The up-and-coming independent magician has arrived. Welcome, Baron Eugene Meyer. I am Ansing, the Vice-Chairman of the Magic Association."

A middle-aged man with intense eyes spoke. I had heard of his reputation. There are three Vice-Chairmen in the Magic Association, and Ansing is said to be the fiercest as a fire-type magician.

Could the rumors be wrong? Ansing's first impression wasn't fierce, but rather cold and calculating. The other ten magicians didn't seem to have any intention of introducing themselves. They were just watching.

"I am Baron Eugene Meyer. I run a business in Neo London."

"Rank 6 magician. Nickname: Sun Cold. Uses lightning and ice attribute magic. A rare battle mage."

The woman next to him stated information about me matter-of-factly. There was no deep information. Sun Cold was something I jokingly blurted out here and there... and it had become one of my nicknames.

"Baron Meyer. The Magic Association wants a magician with your skills and talent. Will you become a member of the Magic Association? The Magic Association welcomes non-faction magicians. Or you can establish your own faction. There are also many other benefits. Magicians are rare, so they often become targets for all sorts of things. Shouldn't we magicians stick together?"

Honestly, being affiliated with something didn't sit well with me, but... the Magic Association's umbrella is necessary for me. If I had intended to refuse outright, I wouldn't have accepted the invitation in the first place.

"I've also been interested in the Magic Association. I've been wondering how to become a member. I'm grateful that the Magic Association has offered me a proposal."

"Hmm. I'm glad the conversation is going smoothly."

Ansing smiled contentedly before adopting a serious expression.

"It may be rude to you, but our Magic Association wants to confirm your skills and talent. I would like to say that it is related to your treatment."

"It's not difficult for me to show you my skills."

"That's good. Are you interested in any factions? Continuing to be a non-faction magician is very inefficient. If you join a faction, you can access the research data that the faction has accumulated. It's tempting as a magician."

"I'm personally interested in the Leisun School, but I don't know what the Leisun School thinks."

A handsome young man with blue hair wearing a suit jumped up from his seat.

"Our Leisun School welcomes Eugene Meyer-ssi! Ah, I am Ambrak Leisun of the Leisun School!"

Ambrak Leisun. As you can tell from his last name, he is the official successor of the Leisun School. Only the head of the school and his top disciple can use the Leisun surname.

The reason Ambrak is so excited and shouting is because I'm a Rank 6 and a battle mage. Both are rare talents.

"Ambrak Leisun. Calm down for now. The reason we gathered here is to see the skills and talent of Neo London's rising star with our own eyes. We're all busy people. Since he agreed, let's move on to the next schedule."

• • •

The magicians of the Magic Association watched Eugene Meyer, who was standing in the training ground. They were sitting in the safe viewing seats outside the training ground.

"Eugene Meyer. His origins are unclear. There are no records of him before he started working in Neo London. He's likely a defector."

"Well, that's not important. 15% of this city are defectors."

"That's right. His background as a defector isn't particularly important. Who in this city doesn't have a story? What matters is his skill and talent. Well, he reached Rank 6 with his own power without belonging to any faction, so it wouldn't be an exaggeration to call him a genius."

"You've been too excited since earlier. I understand that a new talent is joining the Leisun School, but... I'll say it again. Calm down."

When Ansing spoke strongly, Ambrak nodded and closed his mouth.

"You don't have to shut your mouth completely. This is the Leisun School's training ground, so we need your explanation. What is that can in front of Eugene Meyer?"

"That's a volt meter. We call it a Volt Gazer. Our Leisun School used to determine superiority by the volts of lightning magic. Of course, it's impossible to judge a magician's skill and talent with just that. It's like a passing fad. Lightning magicians use it for fun."

"Lightning magicians have fun."

"...I heard fire mages play by burning down buildings."

"Haha. That's not playing, that's training. You're Rank 6, aren't you? What's your best record?"

"Me? 225 billion volts. It's the highest record among the Leisun School's Rank 6 magicians."

"Hoo."

They were having a relaxed conversation, but the atmosphere soon changed.

As excellent magicians, they noticed that the concentration of mana had changed. The mana soon turned into lightning elements and soared into the sky. As befitting the Leisun School's training ground specializing in lightning, the sky was wide open.

A blue magic circle was drawn in the sky. It was an incomplete magic circle, but the magicians here immediately recognized it. That's because it's one of the widely known common magics.

"Is that a Rank 5 magic, Thunder Bolt? Even if it's Rank 5, Thunder Bolt is a large-scale magic. To cast it so quickly and stably. There's no doubt about his talent."

"Wait a minute. The magic circle of Thunder Bolt is a little different. It seems to have been modified and transformed. If he modified the Thunder Bolt magic himself... his magic theory is also at a considerable level."

The magic circle was completed, and lightning gathered in its center. The gathered lightning was compressed and began to rotate.

Originally, Thunder Bolt compresses and then strikes down. But the lightning in front of them was gathered and rotating. It seemed to be sucking in lightning as it rotated. It was the shape of a swastika (卍) gathering towards the center.

The magicians stared blankly at the sky. That's not the Thunder Bolt they know based on existing common sense. It has evolved one step further. It doesn't change like that just by modifying a part of the magic circle. It's the result of additional processes and the magician's special will seeping into and twisting the magic circle.

Magicians usually call such special and unique magic this: Secret Magic.

"Manroe (卍 Thunder)."

Immediately after Eugene Meyer's calm incantation ended, a huge lightning bolt struck the Volt Gazer from the sky.

The lightning momentarily overwhelmed the sunlight. The magicians, without realizing it, deployed barrier magic, worried about the aftermath. It was a pointless act. Eugene had perfectly calculated the aftermath and cast the magic.

Even though the lightning flashed and disappeared in an instant, the area around the training ground was filled with lightning elements. As if to prove it was a magician's training ground, the ground of the training ground and the Volt Gazer were intact.

The magicians looked at Ambrak as if urging him to do something. Vice-Chairman Ansing was the same.

"Ambrak. What's the result? How many volts?"

Ambrak, who had been staring blankly at the training ground, suddenly came to his senses and took out a terminal from his pocket.

"I'll check now, so just...."

Ambrak suddenly closed his mouth. Conversely, his eyes widened as if they were about to tear. Soon, his pupils trembled.

"375.1 billion volts...."

He barely read the number on the terminal. 1 billion volts is the voltage of lightning. Even with simple calculations, it's 370 times the lightning.

"Hoo. A new record has appeared. Let's cancel the next test and move on to the final test, magic combat. It seems that Eugene Meyer's capabilities have already been confirmed. Does anyone object?"

A blonde woman with glasses stood up from her seat as if she had been waiting. She did not object to Ansing's words.

"I will fight him."
