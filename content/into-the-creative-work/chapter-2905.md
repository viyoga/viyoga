,The development of common magic has stopped since hundreds of years ago.

There's nothing special about the reason. In this world, magicians are generally closer to researchers than combatants. That's why combat magicians are treated as precious. And most of the common magic is related to combat.

,There's no reason to develop combat-oriented common magic if you don't fight.

Until now, I thought it was enough.
