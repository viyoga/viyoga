The Korea-Japan War was over.

I didn't return to Korea right away, staying in Tokyo for the time being.

There was a lot to do politically. I also needed to decide how Japan would be governed going forward.

'The Japanese won't just quietly accept everything just because the war is over. There will definitely be dissent. Japanese independence activists will crawl out of the woodwork and run wild.'

Even so, Japanese independence would be impossible.

For one, there was no force willing to help and support Japanese independence. With the international community in ruins, nations large and small were focused on survival.

Korea's current technological prowess was on a different level from 100 years ago. The Japanese knowledge level is higher than 100 years ago? Doesn't matter.

The superintelligent AI Zena could manage Japan's internet, and reality could be surveilled through cameras. Above all, I had the card called Oneko.

'Using Oneko's brainwashing ability, I'll brainwash the unruly Japanese and the independence activists.'

Of course, I knew that even Oneko couldn't brainwash every single human in Japan.

'I only need to brainwash the key figures. Time will handle the rest. For now, I'll ban the use of Japanese in official Japanese documents.'

What was astonishing was that even in this situation, Japan was still clinging to paper documents and seals instead of electronic documents.

'This all needs to be fixed too. Electronic documents are so much more convenient.'

An administrative gap might occur? For now, there's a translation app, so things will somehow keep running.

'In the end, Japan needs time to become Koreanized.'

Lee Yeonhee and Oneko are capable women, so I trust they'll handle the governance of Japan well on their own.

'Next... the declaration of empire, to be done as soon as I return to Seoul.'

As soon as I return to Seoul, I could make a national address saying that from now on, Korea is an empire! All power was in my hands, so it was only natural. But that didn't look very good.

'I'll hold a vote. At least on the surface, I'll make it seem like the people choose.'

Considering my approval rating, over 70% of the people would vote in favor. And that was a conservative estimate. The people understand now too. That this era needs a superhuman to lead the nation.

'Wait. 70% approval doesn't look good. It needs to be around 90% to look right.'

I also decided on the name of the empire.

The Holy Great Korean Empire.

Naturally, I'd thought of the English name too. Great Korea.

'President isn't bad either, but Emperor is better after all. When a president tries to do something, there are so many annoying procedures.'

Even if I could crush those annoying procedures with power, the process itself was tiresome.

And it doesn't give off the same sense of absoluteness as Emperor.

Emperor versus President. Just from the feel of the words, doesn't Emperor sound way cooler?

'Old memories are coming back. Even that famous shaman said I had the face of an emperor. Though I don't know what happened to that shaman after she ran off.'

Given the state of the world, she might be dead. Maybe she became a zombie on the first day.

"Your Excellency. We've captured the traitors."

Lee Yeonhee entered the office leading her knights. Between the knights, Chairman Jin Sugyeol of Ilwoo Group, his daughter Jin Seonghwa, and the rest of the Jin clan entered in handcuffs. Seeing there were over 15 of them, they'd brought the entire family.

"Isn't calling them traitors a bit much? These bastards were Japanese from the start."

"Ah. Since they were using Korean names, I momentarily confused myself. I'll correct that. I've brought the war criminals, Your Excellency."

I looked at Jin Sugyeol in handcuffs. The former chairman of Ilwoo Group sat collapsed on the floor with a corpse-like face, bowing his head. Following him, his relatives knelt down.

"Y-Your Excellency! I was wrong! P-please spare me! I'll do anything! Please have mercy!"

"Looking at you, a famous saying comes to mind. The Japanese are utterly treacherous — never once have they been known to keep faith. Even if I show mercy here, you'll end up stabbing me in the back."

"No! Your Excellency! Just once! Please trust me just once! I'll give you everything I have! I am Korean!"

"A Korean with exceptional dogeza skills. It's enough to inspire admiration."

"Your Excellency...! Please, have mercy...!"

I rose from my seat and walked toward him.

"Hey, Jin Sugyeol."

"Yes, yes. Your Excellency!"

"It was your Ilwoo Group that sold my genes to Japan, wasn't it?"

"N-no! It was my daughter who did it on her own—"

Before Jin Sugyeol could finish, his daughter Jin Seonghwa raised her head and shouted.

"That's not true! It was Father who ordered it! I just did what I was told!"

"What?! You bitch, you're trying to sell out your own father?! Weren't you the one who obtained His Excellency's genes?!"

"It was Father who ordered it! Don't try to dodge responsibility!"

Jin Sugyeol and Jin Seonghwa began glaring at each other and raising their voices. They must have judged that whoever lost this exchange would die. It was a laughable farce.

Bzzt-bzzt!

When lightning burst from my hand, the two immediately shut their mouths and bowed their heads. Watching them tremble, I extinguished the lightning.

"Not a single one of you has any intention of taking responsibility. I called you here just in case, but as expected. Normally I would have killed all of you and been done with it, but... you dared to sell my genes? Thanks to you, things have become troublesome."

The clones made using my genes.

Not all the S-Clones died. The S-Clones being cultivated in the secret laboratory were all disposed of, but the S-Clones whose brainwashing had been undone scattered and are hiding somewhere in Japan. The Knights Order and military are going around the archipelago dealing with them.

They can't escape Japan, so they'll eventually be dealt with, but it was also true that a not-insignificant amount of manpower was being invested.

"You will become living history of the Empire. Take them away and have them stuffed."

A life more terrible than death was confirmed.

The knights dragged the Jin clan out. The Jin clan's complexions improved slightly. They hadn't fully understood my words and were relieved that they wouldn't die immediately. Before long, those faces would contort into despair. I wasn't particularly curious to see that.

"Lee Yeonhee. What about Japan's researchers and bionics?"

It was a confirmation.

Japan's three bionics had already been sent to the Korean Peninsula. The other researchers were to be sent additionally this time. It would have been a waste to purge all the researchers, who were high-grade personnel.

"As you instructed, Oneko brainwashed them and transported them to the Korean Peninsula. They will work like slaves in the biotechnology research institute until they die."

"Couldn't the brainwashing come undone during their research?"

"They are researchers, but the work they'll be doing will mostly be simple tasks. Even if the brainwashing were undone, we've implanted three bombs in their bodies. We can detonate them at any time."

It was only natural to take useful talent along.

I briefly turned my gaze to the side and saw Lee Yeonhee grinning. The moment my gaze reached her, she put on a serene, faint smile as if she'd never done any such thing.

Ever since winning the Korea-Japan War, Lee Yeonhee's mood had been beyond good — it was at the point of ascension.

'Making Lee Yeonhee mine will have to wait until after the Empire is established.'

Lee Yeonhee wouldn't be able to refuse me anymore either.

"This way."

Oneko, wearing a suit and a white cat mask, took the lead and moved. Lee Yeonhee and I followed behind her without a word.

Oneko used casual speech when it was just us. I'd ordered it. The cat-like Oneko suits casual speech well.

This place was the Aokigahara Sea of Trees.

A place at the foot of Mount Fuji.

It was indeed a place that often appeared in ghost stories. It was also famous as a suicide spot.

We went deep into the sea of trees and found a massive moss-covered tree.

That tree was a kind of OOPArt.

The Strange Tree of Aokigahara.

If you bring an ordinary person to this tree, they go mad and convulse for 10 minutes. 70% die, 25% faint. The remaining 5% awaken as psychic ability users. Most of the awakened are brainwashing-type ability users.

To Players and Awakened, it was an ordinary tree with no effect.

"A 5% chance isn't small. But why didn't the Japanese government actively use this?"

"Brainwashing doesn't work well on brainwashing ability users."

"So they had difficulty managing brainwashing ability users."

Lee Yeonhee added to my words.

"Japan's power holders probably avoided brainwashing ability users."

Even if not a power holder, people instinctively avoid brainwashing-type ability users.

I looked up at the tree and pondered.

'Should I destroy it? Or not.'

Even if it kills 70 out of 100, it's an OOPArt that can turn ordinary people into Awakened. It's a rare OOPArt even worldwide. It might even be the only OOPArt that forcibly awakens ordinary people.

'The problem is that the ability of forcibly awakened individuals is fixed to brainwashing abilities.'

Managing brainwashing ability users is no easy task.

'I have Oneko. I have the best brainwashing ability user, so I don't need mediocre brainwashing ability users right now.'

Any run-of-the-mill brainwashing ability user can't even compare to Oneko, who is an Esper. To match Oneko, you'd need to be a brainwashing ability user with at least 5-star potential.

'It's a waste to destroy it. But if I leave it here... there's a small chance someone else might benefit from it.'

Glance. I looked at Lee Yeonhee. She was quiet. She's the type who wouldn't hesitate to voice opinions that go against morality if it benefits the nation. Her being quiet meant she felt the same ambiguity I did.

"We'll cut down this tree, take it to Seoul, and store it."

"If you cut it down, the effect will disappear."

"Doesn't matter. When I need it, I'll take it out and restore it."

My ability works on OOPArts too. Restoring Oneko's shattered mask was also my ability.

"It might not work outside of Aokigahara."

"Then that's a shame."

"Your Excellency. I will use the Awakened unit and robots to fell it, then transport it to Seoul."

"Good. We're returning to Korea. Lee Yeonhee. Are the voting preparations complete?"

Lee Yeonhee's body trembled. She spoke with a thick smile and hot breath.

"Of course, Your Excellency. Korea is ready to become great."
