Han Ayoung and I turned around.

A Hispanic man in his forties stood there in a light shirt. He'd decked himself out in gold chains and gold bracelets.

The moment I laid eyes on him, his name surfaced.

An American S-rank Hunter operating out of Las Vegas.

His nicknames were the Gold Master, and the King of Las Vegas. There was a saying that Las Vegas casinos only operated normally because of him. His ability weakened targets. In the casinos he managed, Awakened couldn't use their powers, and even Hunters' physical abilities dropped to the level of ordinary civilians.

I furrowed my brow hard. Setting aside the fact that he was an American S-rank Hunter, I didn't like him butting in out of nowhere—and more than anything, the way he'd been subtly eyeing Han Ayoung's body since a moment ago pissed me off.

"No sense of awareness. If you came, you should've quietly fucked off into a corner. Why are you ruining the mood? Want to die?"

"...Oh. You've got no manners, unlike how you talk to the press. They say Korea's the land of etiquette. Can't even show respect to your senior?"

"Is there any reason to respect a racist Indian like you?"

"Goddamn. These days you can't even call a black person black or an Asian an Asian. What a shitty world."

Juan Perez put a cigar to his lips. He flicked his fingers and the tip of the cigar was cut clean and lit. It was a simple trick using mana.

"Put out the cigarette. If you want to smoke that badly, lock yourself in a room and do it. Don't pollute the air near me."

"Wow. Even the president can't tell me to put out a cigarette. How long has it been since I heard that? Twenty years? That was something my mama used to say to me. Want me to call you mama?"

Kwaaaaaang!

From the blue sky, a vicious bolt of lightning struck down toward him. The lightning weakened gradually as it fell toward his head, and vanished just before making contact.

'So lightning alone isn't enough.'

Bzzt, crackle.

Lightning flickered at my fingertips.

"That's far enough, Lightning Heaven. We're past the level of passing this off as a joke. Push any further and it won't end as a joke."

Juan Perez spoke, his voice dropping low. As he did, he blew out a plume of cigar smoke as if taunting.

"America's finished, then. Today your country's S-rank Hunter is going to go missing at sea."

"Sometimes in life you run into someone you just don't click with. Today's that day. Well, I get it. You just became an S-rank Hunter, so nothing looks like it's above you. The whole world looks beneath you, and every other Hunter looks like a piece of shit. I went through it too—the I'm-the-best disease. This senior will cure you."

Juan Perez clenched his fist tight.

"Stop!"

Han Ayoung cut in. She grabbed my arm and blocked my path. It was Han Ayoung, of all people, so I lowered my killing intent.

"Yujin, stop. And you, sir, please stop as well. There's nothing to gain from us fighting each other. Let's just each do our own jobs. We won't have any reason to meet again after this anyway."

Since I was the first to completely withdraw my will to fight, Juan Perez shrugged his shoulders and cleared the air.

"Han Ayoung. I'll let it go for your sake. ...You've got a hot face and body that doesn't look Asian. Interested in Las Vegas, by any chance? I could give you a special guided tour."

"No. I'm not interested."

"That's a shame."

Juan Perez turned and went back inside the ship. I stared at the back of his head, my fist trembling.

'How dare he make a pass at my woman right in front of me.'

How should I kill this bastard.

Squeeze.

Han Ayoung tightened her grip on my arm and held it. The sensation of her huge breasts pressed through my arm. My anger melted away.

"Don't pay him any mind. He was deliberately provoking you. And his reputation was never good to begin with."

Juan Perez. Among S-rank Hunters, he was famous for living a debauched life. The scandals connected to him numbered over three digits.

'I did feel hidden gazes from all over. Was he testing my reaction? Even so, that bastard lusting after my woman isn't something I can forgive.'

I'll kill him when I get the chance.

"You went too far."

Juan's gaze turned to a man leaning against the wall. A black-haired man in a neat suit.

Roy.

On the surface, Juan's secretary—but his true identity was an FBI agent. He served Juan's convenience while also being his watcher.

"What's the problem. I scratched that brat's itch just the way you wanted."

"If Hunter Han Ayoung hadn't been there, one of you two might have died."

"Yeah. She was a good woman. Too good for that bastard. Can't we bring her to America?"

"Hunter Han Ayoung firmly rejected America's offer. Korea is an ally of the United States, so we can't strong-arm her either. What matters right now is Hunter Sung Yujin. Can you gauge the extent of his limits?"

"I don't know. He's a different breed from the S-ranks I've met."

Juan could weaken a target's abilities. Put another way, it meant he could respond more sensitively to a target's abilities. Reading a target's power and limits was his specialty.

"One thing's certain—purely in terms of raw power, he's in the upper tier even among S-rank Hunters. In America, there are only a handful of S-rank Hunters, myself included, who could face that brat head-on."

"I see."

"If you're thinking of coaxing him over to America, drop it. You saw it too. That bastard showed genuine killing intent. A guy like that entering America? First the American Hunter world gets flipped upside down, and the whole country could turn into a shitshow."

"That's not for me to decide. If, hypothetically, we were to eliminate Hunter Sung Yujin, how much support do you think would be needed?"

"There it is—showing your true colors. So the FBI is leaning toward killing him?"

"Don't make baseless assumptions. It's nothing more than personal curiosity."

"Personal curiosity, my ass. Heh heh."

Juan might not look it, but he was a man who'd done dirty work with the FBI several times. He could roughly guess what the FBI, what the American government, was feeling as they kept Sung Yujin under close watch.

'America, the land of the free? Bullshit. They just wrapped shackles made of money and handcuffs forged from power in a pretty package called freedom.'

An S-rank Hunter was a tactical weapon on par with a nuclear device.

If an S-rank Hunter suddenly appeared in the middle of New York and went on a rampage, the damage could potentially exceed that of a nuclear detonation. And Sung Yujin was a lightning-wielder. Just that alone made his lethality immense.

From the American government's standpoint, they had no choice but to be wary of an S-rank Hunter they couldn't control.

The World Hunter Association exists? Even if the World Hunter Association and America have close ties, it's not an organization that moves solely for America's benefit.

If the world were fine, the World Hunter Association would watch America collapse without lifting a finger. In reality, if America falls, the world falls with it, so that would never happen—but still.

The other reason America was wary of Sung Yujin was his recent pro-China leanings. Everyone knew America wasn't just wary of China but outright hostile. Sung Yujin's actions in aiding China couldn't help but be a burden.

"My ability works on him too. That said, I can't completely neutralize him. If you just attach one suitable S-rank to me... I can kill him for certain."

"Are you certain?"

"Why are you like this? We've done this several times already. I've never done an official S-rank, but... it's no different."

Juan blew out cigar smoke. At some point the room had filled with smoke. Roy nodded with an expressionless face.

The warship that had been moving continuously came to a stop. About thirty meters from the warship, there was a distorted space. Looking at it brought to mind wavering heat haze, a shattered mirror.

'A kind of dimensional rift.'

So the Old Gate was, in the end, just an old dimensional rift.

A vessel appeared on the warship's starboard side. It was a wooden ship with sails.

'A wooden sailing ship? In this day and age? Sailing the Atlantic in something like that.'

When I focused, I could sense mana from the wooden sailing ship. It wasn't simply imbued with mana. The mana was circulating in a way I couldn't understand.

'Magic, is it. That sailing ship itself could be called a kind of magical tool.'

It was fascinating, but I didn't particularly want one. Compared to the warship, the magical sailing ship was smaller, and it looked inconvenient in every way.

People poured out from inside the sailing ship. About twenty of them, and every one of them had their faces covered with bizarre masks and their bodies wrapped in dark-colored robes.

"And what are those?"

"The World Hunter Association's Sealing Unit. All of them are mages."

Han Ayoung said in a calm voice. She'd become S-rank several years before me, so she apparently knew about the Sealing Unit in advance.

One of the Sealing Unit mages floated up into the air and descended onto the warship.

"I'll get straight to the point. Old Gate-16's seal is currently being maintained by a razor's edge. We will release the existing seal and apply a new one. The moment the seal is released, Nephilim will emerge. Before we begin, we'll erect a simple barrier, but it won't withstand Nephilim's power for long. You all just need to hold off Nephilim while the sealing is in progress."

Juan, standing some distance from us, asked him.

"How long does the sealing take?"

"We're estimating thirty minutes. An hour at most."

"When does the sealing start?"

"Two hours after the moon rises, once the barrier construction is complete."

"You're insisting on doing it at night?"

"This sealing technique partially draws on the moon's energy, so it's more efficient on a night when the moon is up."

"Ha. Magic—I hear the word and still can't understand it."

Two hours passed, and Old Gate-16's seal was released.

Crack!

A fissure split the Old Gate, and a giant with white wings, three hundred meters tall, emerged.

I narrowed my eyes and examined Nephilim. Blood was streaming from Nephilim's body. In particular, parts of its wings were scorched, as if burned by something.

'Judging by the state of it, looks like it was fighting something until just now... What was it fighting?'

Nephilim paid us no attention and flapped its wings, soaring toward the sky.

Thoom!

It was blocked by the barrier the mages had erected and couldn't get out.

"Seeing it in person, it's absurdly big. Well, even so, it's nothing but a monster!"

Juan extended his right hand toward Nephilim. He used his ability. An invisible force weakened Nephilim. Momentarily losing its balance, Nephilim plummeted to the ground.

"Hngh...!"

Han Ayoung swallowed a breath and released her power. Cold air burst out from her, freezing the sea in an instant. Nephilim crashed onto the frozen sea.

Kwa-kwa-kwa-kwa-kwa-kwa-kwa-kwang!

The frozen sea shattered and massive chunks of ice flew in every direction. Nephilim's arms and wings writhed, but the frost around it clung to its body. Nephilim's movements slowed.

Standing in the sky, I raised my right hand. Lightning flickered and gathered, spinning, then transformed into the shape of a spear.

Thunder Heaven Style—Myriad Thunder Spear.

I hurled the spinning spear of lightning at Nephilim's head.

The Myriad Thunder Spear discharged the moment it struck.

A vicious blue lightning flash lit the dark night into broad daylight in an instant.
