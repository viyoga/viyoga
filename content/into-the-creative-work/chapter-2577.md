The celebration banquet for the millennium of the Jin Empire's founding has ended.

The Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King have decided to return to the Martial Arts Alliance and the Namgung Clan, respectively.

,"Now, instead of the Eight Great Sects and One Union, only the Eight Great Sects remain. The Beggars' Sect was in charge of the Martial Arts Alliance's intelligence... Hooo. I'm worried about the future."

The Martial Arts Alliance Lord lamented, making sure I heard. I looked at him with an indifferent expression.

,"Don't talk nonsense. The Emperor only ordered to kill the beggars. In short, if you're not a beggar, you won't die. If the Beggars' Sect's faith in being beggars was so strong, they would have been annihilated... but that's impossible."

,"That's right. The Beggars' Sect has been destroyed, but not really. I heard that most of the Beggars' Sect members have become servers. It's said to be a job where you can easily get information besides begging. Thanks to that, there are often conflicts with the Hao Clan... Well, we avoided the worst-case scenario."

,"Tell those Beggars' Sect bastards. If they hang around me one more time... I'll lead my soldiers and kill them all."

,"Isn't your relationship with the Beggars' Sect already the worst? I can't stop the Beggars' Sect from resenting you. But the Beggars' Sect won't mess with you. You are the Second Rank Lightning General, directly appointed by His Majesty the Emperor."

,"Yeah. Do well in the future."

Second Rank.

In an instant, I became a high-ranking official. In modern terms, it could be said to be a minister-level position. Well, the Empire is so vast that there are quite a few minister-level officials.

The Emperor values martial prowess more than literature, and generals are treated better, so it has a special meaning.

Seong Jigone, who was on the military's formal path, had the background of Guwol Seonja-nim, who was the former Crown Prince's teacher, and endless support from several wives, was still a Third Rank Junior General. I received the Emperor's favor and became a Second Rank official in one fell swoop.

The officials were silent. They couldn't dare to argue with the Emperor. They rather watched my every move. Even the Crown Prince.

,"Son-in-law. I'm proud of your success."

,"Yes. Supreme Clan Leader-nim. Please go back safely."

,"...You seem to have become arrogant. Have you forgotten our last sparring match?"

,"Are you ignoring a Second Rank high-ranking official right now?"

The Sword King frowned, then closed his mouth and turned his head as if he had remembered something. He must have thought of the Emperor.

That day, after having an audience with the Emperor, the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King became extremely cautious about matters related to the Emperor. They couldn't even bring up the topic easily.

I asked the departing Martial Arts Alliance Lord.

,"Didn't you say you would have a private meeting with the Emperor to grasp his intentions? I can help you specially. The Emperor wouldn't ignore my request either."

,"...No, thanks. Even if I have a private meeting with the Emperor, I'm not confident in figuring out his intentions. I'm rather afraid of making a mistake. I just hope our relationship doesn't end here."

,"Okay. Get back safely. Thanks to you, I learned the Gwon Gaksul well. Supreme Clan Leader-nim. I'll visit the Namgung Clan when I have time. Please give my regards to Rin and Seol."

I was just saying it. If I wanted to, I could return to the Namgung Clan at any time with a space-time warp scroll. Of course, I had no intention of giving space-time warp scrolls to the Sword King or the Martial Arts Alliance Lord. Honey is only valuable if I'm the only one sucking it.

The Sword King and the Martial Arts Alliance Lord left.

I remained in the Imperial Palace. The Imperial Palace is where all the spiritual veins in the continent gather and intersect. I couldn't miss the opportunity to train here.

There was a special training ground in the Imperial Palace that the generals used. Now that I'm here, shouldn't I use it?

I took out 10,093 Sky Gems from my inventory. A mountain of Sky Gems piled up in the center of the training ground.

Sky Gem (天玉). A bead that contains spiritual energy. It is also used as currency, with one Sky Gem being worth about 100 gold nyang. However, you can't just acquire them recklessly even if you have the money. 100 gold nyang is just an average price; the cost can increase depending on the circumstances.

,'If I could easily get them just because I had money, my inventory would be overflowing with Sky Gems.'

Since they are created by condensing pure spiritual energy, most of them are created naturally. Only a small number of cultivators can create them artificially. Moreover, they can't just create them recklessly.

,'The Emperor just bestowed 10,000 Sky Gems right away. His class is different.'

I check the Cheon Gangseong System.

My current VIP level is 4.

To raise the VIP level, 1,500 Sky Gems were needed.

『Achieved VIP 5.』

『To raise the VIP level, 2,500 Sky Gems are needed.』

『Achieved VIP 6.』

『To raise the VIP level, 5,000 Sky Gems are needed.』

『Achieved VIP 7.』

『To raise the VIP level, 8,000 Sky Gems are needed.』

9,000 Sky Gems disappeared in an instant. 1,093 Sky Gems are left. But I didn't regret it at all.

,"Wow."

Rather, an exclamation came out.

『Achieved VIP 7.』

『To raise the VIP level, 8,000 Sky Gems are needed.』

『VIP 7 achievement perks are given.』

『Martial arts power increases by 140%.』

『Growth rate increases by 120%.』

『The effect of elixirs increases by 75%.』

『Attendance rewards get better.』

『Stamina and internal energy recovery rate increases by 120%.』

『Acquire 5 Star Fragments.』

『You can use the space-time warp system twice a day.』

『The total number of points that can be designated by the space-time warp system is 7.』

『The light of Cheon Gangseong is strengthened. You can maintain two lights of Cheon Gangseong at the same time.』

'Acquire the Elixir of Immortality (Immortal Elixir).'

Acquire a Summoning Scroll.

'Acquire the Eight Trigrams Alchemy Furnace (八極 煉丹爐).'

I just used Sky Gems, but I got stronger. The martial arts power alone became 30% stronger. Enemies in the same realm were already no match for me, but now I feel like I can completely overwhelm them.

,'You can maintain up to 2 lights of Cheon Gangseong at the same time? That means I can use the power of Cheonsalseong and Jiamsong at once.'

The additional items I got are also amazing.

First, the Elixir of Immortality. I held a golden shining pill.

Elixir of Immortality

Lifespan increases by 100 years.

You become younger and don't age.

Vitality increases.』

,'Doesn't not aging mean that lifespan disappears?'

I thought about this world for a moment. In this world, there are limited ways to transcend lifespan. The most well-known is becoming a divine immortal. If you can't, you can't escape lifespan even if you reach the realm of all things.

,'It might be applied differently in other worlds.'

It's an unnecessary item for me right now.

,'If lifespan really increases... it would be the ultimate treasure for Elena-ssi.'

I put it in my inventory for now.

'Summoning Scroll'

Randomly summons a high-ranking spirit beast and forcibly makes a contract.

Consumes energy depending on the summoned spirit beast.』

,'Forced summoning and forced contract? This is another absurd item.'

Moreover, it had randomness.

,'I feel like I received a one-time gacha ticket.'

My hands were itching. I wanted to use this Summoning Scroll right away.

,'It's a loss to use it now. I'll put the Summoning Scroll in the Bright Light Ascension.'

I unfolded the Bright Light Ascension and put it inside. I stared blankly at the Bright Light Ascension for a moment. Items are strengthened here. It's a simple yet amazing ability. What is the identity of this thing?

I was pondering for a moment. The light in the Bright Light Ascension painting disappeared. It meant that the strengthening was over.

,'This is crazy. The strengthening ended in 30 seconds? Could it be that it was strengthened shittily?'

'Summoning Scroll'

Randomly summons a top-ranking spirit beast and forcibly makes a contract.

Consumes energy depending on the summoned spirit beast.』

,'It got so much better.'

Doesn't a top-ranking spirit beast mean that I'm guaranteed to summon an SSR-class spirit beast? I held back the urge to summon a spirit beast right away.

The last item, the Eight Trigrams Pill Furnace, was a 1m-sized brazier. The brazier was octagonal overall, with a Taegeuk pattern below and trigram patterns engraved on the angular brazier walls.

'Eight Trigrams Pill Furnace (Ba Ji Alchemy Furnace)'

Automatically creates spirit pills when materials are put in.

Can find out the ingredients of spirit pills.

Once a production is successful or a discovered pill-making method is recorded.』

,'It automatically makes them. That's awesome. I had a lot of low-grade elixirs, so I needed to make them into spirit pills... I don't have to go to a pill maker.'

I pondered for a moment and took out the Elixir of Immortality from my inventory. This is also a spirit pill. Then, wouldn't I be able to find out the pill-making method through the Eight Trigrams Pill Furnace?

The prediction was correct.

『Elixir of Immortality (Immortal Rejuvenation Pill) Recipe

1. Elixir Plant.

2. 3 drops of Azure Petroleum.

3. 2 Black Pearls.

4. Vermilion Bird's Feather or Hwaseo's Leather.

.......

23. 12 Sky Gems.』

,'Even if I know the ingredients, I can't get them. Where do I have to get the Elixir Plant?'

I shook my head and took the Elixir of Immortality out of the Eight Trigrams Pill Furnace. I was about to put it in my inventory when the Bright Light Ascension caught my eye. I put the Elixir of Immortality in the Bright Light Ascension.

,'Since there are many low-grade elixirs, should I experiment once?'

I poured as many elixirs as I could into the pill furnace. I put in 50 kinds, and they all went in because the capacity was larger than it looked.

,'Do I have to light the fire myself?'

I closed the entrance of the pill furnace. The elixir that was in the pill furnace fell to the ground below. Then, flames began to blaze inside the pill furnace. The heat was so hot. I unknowingly stepped back.

,'I can feel the heat, but the elixir that fell below the pill furnace isn't burning.'

『Spirit pill production in progress.... 14 hours 55 minutes 21 seconds until production is complete』

,'That means I just have to wait 15 hours. I heard that pill makers originally had to constantly pay attention to the spirit pills, so they couldn't leave the pill furnace... But this is just a click if you have the ingredients.'

I was blankly looking at the burning pill furnace when I remembered the remaining Sky Gems. There was another use for Sky Gems besides raising the VIP level.

,'The light of Cheon Gangseong. If you consume Sky Gems, you can randomly find out the power of other stars.'

I thought I could get more Sky Gems from the Emperor again, so I decided to use it once.

『You can use 200 Sky Gems to randomly find out the power of another star.』

,'200? It was originally 100. Why did it suddenly go up?'

Is it because the VIP level went up? Or is it because I know the power of more stars? It was difficult to be sure because both were possible. I pondered for a moment and decided to use it.

,'Let's do it just twice. That should be okay.'

『Using 200 Sky Gems.』

Cheongyoseong (Heavenly Skill Star)

I realized the ability of Cheongyoseong.

It's shapeshifting using a medium. If the condition of the medium is met, you can shapeshift into monsters or divine immortals.

,'I don't really have a good idea of how to use it right now.'

『Using 200 Sky Gems.』

Cheongoeseong (Heavenly Star of Leadership)

My eyes sparkled. Cheongoeseong was a pretty famous star. In terms of class, it's on par with Cheonsalseong and Jamiseong. Of course, I don't know much about stars because I just heard it somewhere.

,'The ability of Cheongoeseong is... expansion of spirituality and talent for magic?'

I was bewildered and curious at the same time. I had no talent for magic at all. I couldn't resist the idea of something I didn't have appearing. It didn't seem particularly dangerous, so I used the power of Cheongoeseong.
