A suite in a luxury hotel in Seoul.

Baek Sang-gi sat in a chair, sipping green tea and looking down at Seoul. He looked at the scenery of the world almost always. There was no particular reason. Looking at the scenery of the world was his habit.

"Are you experiencing any discomfort, -nim?"

Lee Mun-hyeon asked from behind. Lee Mun-hyeon was voluntarily attending to Baek Sang-gi.

Consideration or surveillance. Either way, it didn't matter to Baek Sang-gi.

"The food is excellent, and the bedding is warm. How could I be uncomfortable?"

"I'm glad you like it."

"Yes. I especially like this scenery. Seoul has developed dazzlingly in the short time I've been away. I still vividly remember Hanyang from 300 years ago."

Baek Sang-gi stroked his chin. It was a habit he had whenever he recalled the old days.

He felt both accustomed to and regretful about not having a beard.

Even though aging had stopped, things like hair and nails continued to grow. Until 30 years ago, he had grown a beard. He cut off his beard because he realized the changes in Korea. Now, it was rare to see anyone with a beard on this peninsula.

"Did you find Jeok-gwang?"

"......I'm sorry. I couldn't find him."

"I don't intend to blame you. Isn't he the type who can even turn invisible? It won't be easy to find him. Anyway, time is meaningless to me. I'll patiently wait until Jeok-gwang appears."

"I will do my best to find him."

Baek Sang-gi simply brought the teacup to his lips. Lee Mun-hyeon's eyes were burning with hatred and anger. It was clear he wouldn't listen even if he told him to take it slow.

Silence filled the space. Baek Sang-gi continued to sip his tea and look out the window.

It was Lee Mun-hyeon who felt awkward. He rolled his eyes for a moment before opening his mouth.

"Elder, I heard that a foreign vampire entered Busan this morning."

"Busan, huh. It seems you're not neglecting surveillance."

"Yes. While searching for Jeok-gwang, we are maintaining a minimal surveillance system."

"That's how it should be. ...Hmm? Did you say a foreign vampire?"

"Yes. His identity is being investigated. An investigator is currently quietly tailing him. As an expert, he won't be noticed."

Baek Sang-gi put down his teacup.

"......He's probably a Kantra executive. I see. Has it already been 300 years? It's that time."

"Pardon?"

"Have you heard of the Bead of Life?"

At that question, Lee Mun-hyeon nodded. He had heard about it from the former head of the Korean Black Cross Society, who could be considered his benefactor, when he was young.

"I heard it's the fruit of immortality. And I know that you, -eolshin, consumed the Bead of Life."

"Yes. I ate the Bead of Life and gained physical abilities comparable to a vampire and became ageless. How much do you know about the Bead of Life?"

"I know that if a human eats it, they gain immortality and superhuman physical abilities, if a vampire eats it, they return to being human, and if a True Ancestor eats it, their supernatural powers become stronger."

"You know almost everything. Then, do you know what happens if you eat multiple Beads of Life?"

"......I don't know."

Lee Mun-hyeon said honestly. The Bead of Life is a rare item to obtain even one of. But to eat it multiple times? He had never heard of it.

"It has no effect on humans from the second time onwards. This is definite information from me, an experienced person. Vampires will become superhumans since they would have become human. The problem is those True Vampire bastards. True Vampires get stronger every time they eat a Bead of Life."

"Yes? Is that really true? Does that mean they can become infinitely strong if they just have the Bead of Life...."

"Theoretically, yes. However, there probably aren't many Seeds of Life left in the world."

"What is the Seed of Life?"

"It's something a True Vampire who existed long ago created with supernatural powers. If you nurture the Seed of Life, it becomes the Flower of Life, which bears the Bead of Life."

Lee Mun-hyeon's eyes widened. It was the first time he had heard this fact in his life.

Baek Sang-gi got up from his seat.

"300 years ago, three True Vampires came and absorbed the essence of the Korean Peninsula with the Flower of Life to create the Bead of Life. At that time, a total of four Beads of Life were borne from one Flower of Life. No matter how I think about it, the Bead of Life is too dangerous for those True Vampire bastards. Mun-hyeon-ah. We must go to Mount Seorak."

"...Yes. I will prepare the car immediately."

I considered the possibility of Moon Ji-hyuk obtaining the Bead of Life.

'I'm going to step in myself, so I don't think that will happen... but considering the original story, it's not something I can completely ignore.'

There is only one Bead of Life. If Moon Ji-hyuk gets it first and eats it right away, things could go wrong.

'I need to completely control Moon Ji-hyuk. To do that.......'

I picked up my smartphone. There was something more precious to the current Moon Ji-hyuk than his own life.

Taker - Hello, Yang Jeong-min-ssi.

I quietly waited for a reply. Today is a weekday, so Yang Jeong-min would be working.

Yang Jeong-min - Who is this?

Taker - This is Taker. You could say I'm Detective Moon's colleague.

Yang Jeong-min - You're Ji-hyuk-ssi's informant.

Yang Jeong-min knew about me. I was a little surprised. Did Moon Ji-hyuk tell her? No, he's not that kind of guy. Considering Yang Jeong-min's personality, there's no way she would have investigated Moon Ji-hyuk.

Then the remaining possibility is....

'Moon Ji-hyuk, you bastard.... Can't you manage your phone properly?'

Yang Jeong-min - You contacted me.... What's going on?

Taker - Detective Moon is in danger.

Yang Jeong-min - Are you with Ji-hyuk-ssi right now?!

Taker - Detective Moon is still safe. So please calm down for now. Stay calm and read my message.

Yang Jeong-min - ...Okay.

Taker - Detective Moon is trying to become human this time.

I told Yang Jeong-min about the Bead of Life and the vampires, including Doris.

Yang Jeong-min - Ji-hyuk-ssi is trying to do something like that....

Taker - Detective Moon wants to return to being human to the point of obsession. I think Yang Jeong-min-ssi is probably the reason.

Yang Jeong-min - ...Should I stop Ji-hyuk-ssi?

Yang Jeong-min - I guess it's better to stop him.

"No. That won't do. No way."

Startled, I hurriedly moved my fingers to send her a message.

Taker - Even if Yang Jeong-min-ssi stops him, Detective Moon will eventually head to Mount Seorak. He's that kind of person, isn't he?

Yang Jeong-min - Yes. Ji-hyuk-ssi is that kind of person. Then what should I do?

Taker - Please help Detective Moon. I will also go to Mount Seorak to help Detective Moon. The only people Detective Moon can trust are you and me.

In the original drama, it's the colleagues from the same detective division who help Moon Ji-hyuk. However, the current detective division colleagues are not as close as in the original, and there is no trust.

Yang Jeong-min - Okay. I'll go right now.

Yang Jeong-min - It's better not to tell Ji-hyuk-ssi....

Fortunately, Yang Jeong-min wasn't a complete idiot. If Yang Jeong-min had contacted Moon Ji-hyuk, I would have been in trouble.

Taker - I'll tell you the location. Let's move together in Mount Seorak. It's safer that way.

Yang Jeong-min - Okay. Thank you for letting me know. I'm glad Ji-hyuk-ssi has a friend like you.

Taker - It's a bit of a stretch to call us friends... I guess I've become attached to Detective Moon without realizing it.

I chuckled.

'Friends my ass. Well, it's been fun playing with him.'

Below Mount Seorak.

A black sedan stopped. The car door opened, and four men got out in a rush.

"Mr. Ziegler. Are you really going to be okay on your own?"

Kang Wook-sung asked Doris politely.

"I'm fine. Mr. Kang. This task that the boss has entrusted to me is a very important mission."

"May I ask what that task is?"

Doris's gentle eyes turned sharp.

Kang Wook-sung's face hardened. He had miscalculated. He thought that Doris's gentlemanly personality would allow for this level of questioning.

"I, I'm sorry. I dared to overstep my bounds."

"No. It's okay. I reacted a bit sensitively too. I'm sorry."

"It's nothing."

"Mr. Kang. This mission must be kept secret. You all must forget what happened today. Your mission ends here. Return to your original mission."

"...I understand. But there was surveillance this afternoon. The Black Cross Society may interfere with your work."

"I know. But the Korean Black Cross Society can't do anything right now. I was lucky. I want to recruit that Jeok-gwang guy. Is there any information about him?"

"No. Jeok-gwang moves thoroughly, leaving no trace."

"That's a shame. Then I'll be on my way. I'll return alone."

"Yes. Be careful."

"You too, return safely."

Doris moved. He lightly kicked the ground and jumped nearly 2 meters, starting to climb the mountain. He exerted the physical abilities of a True Ancestor vampire to the fullest.

Doris quickly disappeared from Kang Wook-sung's sight.

'That's the skill of an executive.... They said he's top-tier among the True Ancestors, and they weren't wrong.'

Kang Wook-sung admired silently. He had talked to or met executives a few times. But this was the first time he had glimpsed a fraction of their abilities.

"Our work is done. Let's go back."

Kang Wook-sung said as he got back into the car.

'I should go this way.'

Doris, who was running up Mount Seorak, occasionally took out a map he had prepared from his pocket to check the direction.

Doris's mission was to plant the Flower of Life in a designated location to absorb the essence of the Korean Peninsula and bear the Bead of Life. The Flower of Life can bear up to four fruits, and with the essence of the Korean Peninsula at this time, it can bear all four.

'The Bead of Life. Ah.... I'm really looking forward to it.'

Doris was planning to consume all four Beads of Life. He had already received permission from the boss. The boss intended to enhance his teleportation ability.

'Eating the Bead of Life will also extend my lifespan. Ah! What a joyful day this is!'

The boss had told him that eating one Bead of Life would extend a True Ancestor's lifespan by 15 years. Eating all four would extend it by a whopping 60 years.

Originally, Doris was scheduled to enter Korea in December. That would have been the case if the Korean vampire Hunters hadn't suffered near-total annihilation. He would have slowly observed the Korean Black Cross Society and acted sluggishly.

The current Korea was a relatively safe place for vampires. Since there was no need to be wary of vampire Hunters, his actions became bolder.

'This is it.'

Doris was able to reach his destination after running for over an hour. It was an open space with no trees around, where the moon was clearly visible.

He opened his briefcase and took out the Flower of Life. On the outside, it looked like a small 20cm sunflower. Doris carefully planted the Flower of Life on the ground in the open space.

'...Hoo. I'm getting nervous.'

A faint light flowed from the properly planted Flower of Life. It was evidence that it was absorbing essence.

'Thank goodness. I planted it properly.'

He looked at the Flower of Life with loving eyes. The flower resembled a sunflower, but paradoxically, the subtle light surrounding the flower was similar to moonlight.

'Two hours from now! It will be the longest two hours of my life.'

If the Flower of Life properly absorbed the essence of the Korean Peninsula, it would bear a Bead of Life every 30 minutes. Once four Beads of Life were borne, the Flower of Life would wither. The same would happen even if he plucked the Flower of Life he had planted once before.

Therefore, he needed to watch over the Flower of Life, worrying that it might be blown away by the wind or that a beast might come and harm it.

'How beautiful.......'

Doris stared blankly at the Flower of Life. Even so, time seemed to be passing incredibly slowly.

30 minutes passed.

A small Bead of Life formed in the center of the Flower of Life.

The Bead of Life was emitting a subtle rainbow light. Its appearance was closer to a jewel than a fruit. Doris inadvertently thought of an opal.

'...What a mistake. The Bead of Life is several times more beautiful than an opal....'

Doris swallowed hard. He wanted to put that bead in his mouth right away. According to the boss, it would melt the moment it was put in the mouth and be absorbed into the body.

'I must be patient.'

If he detached the Bead of Life now, the Flower of Life would wither as it is. If he was patient for a little longer, he could obtain three more Beads of Life.

He wouldn't do the foolish thing of being blinded by the immediate gain and missing the opportunity to obtain a greater gain.

Buseureok!

He heard a sound. Doris, who had been blankly looking at the Flower of Life, was startled and jumped up, shouting.

"Who's there!"

Tang!

The answer that came back was a silver bullet. Startled, Doris inadvertently used teleportation to hide behind a nearby tree.

Doris leaned against the tree and looked in the direction the silver bullet had come from.

There was a man holding a revolver in his hand.

It was Moon Ji-hyuk.
