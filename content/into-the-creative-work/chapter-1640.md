The owner of the voice was a warrior wearing particularly eye-catching golden armor. He was at the center of the army. He seemed to be the great warrior leading the warriors.

,Enemy of the great ruler of the Golden Desert! You will be buried in the sands of this desert with your stinking corpse! Charge!!"

He gives the order to charge out of the blue.

Warriors riding camels rush towards me, kicking up sand dust. The warriors' faces were full of confidence. Their morale is high enough to pierce the sky.

It was natural. The enemies numbered 3,000, while we only had 700 mid-level undead. Even in terms of numbers, there was more than a fourfold difference. Moreover, if they were elite warriors, they could easily take on two or three mid-level undead on their own.

The enemies were superior both quantitatively and qualitatively.

,If they were living soldiers instead of undead, their eyes would have rolled back so far they'd have deserted the battlefield.'

But the undead, completely dominated by me, felt no fear of the enemies. They were just preparing for the upcoming battle according to my will.

They don't fear the enemy, they don't eat or shit, and they don't rebel. The undead were the best army.

,There's something they've overlooked.'

In an ordinary world war, it would be impossible to overcome the difference in numbers and quality. Especially in this desert where strategy doesn't work.

,This is Atlantis. A world where mythology runs wild.'

I raise my right arm.

,On the battlefield of Atlantis, the outcome can be reversed by the presence of one being. And I'm here.'

,The 'Book of the Dead' didn't just contain methods for creating undead. Undead creation was just the basics written in the Book of the Dead.

I quietly muttered a spell. A huge amount of mana drains away.

Tuduk, tududuk.

Black rain falls from the sky. It was a strange thing. The bright blue sky was clear without a single cloud.

Black Sonata.

The undead, hit by the black rain, let out a roar. A terrible scream echoes across the battlefield.

,Kyaaaaaaaak!"

Also, the stats of the undead hit by the black rain increased somewhat.

,Go, Skeleton Knights!"

I pointed straight ahead with my index finger.

Skeleton Knights riding skeletal horses run towards the desert warriors.

Compared to the 3,000 enemy soldiers, the 100 Skeleton Knights were pathetic, even pitiful. Of course, the Skeleton Knights don't know anything like that. They just charge towards the enemies with their weapons as I tell them to.

Camel warriors and Skeleton Knights clash.

The result is as expected. The Skeleton Knights are smashed. The 100 Skeleton Knights couldn't stop the desert warriors' charge for even a single second.

,Corpse Explosion."

Kwaaaaaaaang!

The shattered bodies of the Skeleton Knights explode.

,I never even expected the Skeleton Knights to win from the start.'

I just wanted the bodies of the Skeleton Knights to be as close to the enemies as possible.

,Corpse Explosion!"

Kwaaaaaaaang!

A massive explosion occurs. The desert warriors, along with their camels, are caught in the explosion and fall to the sandy ground.

I continued to use Corpse Explosion.

,Corpse Explosion! Corpse Explosion! Corpse Explosion!"

Kwaaaang! Kwaaaang! Kwaaaaaaaang!

The continuous explosions were 3-4 times more powerful than grenades.

Originally, Corpse Explosion is a black magic that consumes mana. The explosive power isn't this strong either. How much explosive power could a small corpse have, even if it explodes?

,Right now, my mana stat is 210.'

Even if I use magic like Corpse Explosion, my mana doesn't decrease at all. On top of that, the destructive power of black magic has become enormous due to the influence of my mana stat.

,Charge! Don't back down! If we just kill Tutankhamun, we'll win! If you kill him, the great ruler of the Golden Desert will give us huge rewards! Booze! Women! Gold! Don't you want it?! The one who kills him will get honor and wealth! You'll get everything!"

,Waaaaaaaaaaaaaa!"

Even though there were nearly 500 casualties, the morale of the desert warriors didn't decrease.

It was a characteristic of the desert warriors.

Ignorance is bliss. The desert warriors were disgustingly ignorant. On top of that, they have a unique culture that doesn't fear death, so they don't easily give in unless there's an overwhelmingly large difference in power that makes your jaw drop.

The desert warriors collided with the main force. The undead army falters and is pushed back. The undead, strengthened by Black Sonata, fight desperately without retreating, but they couldn't completely stop them.

,Tutankhamun! I've come! I am Seturt, the great warrior of Hatep-nim!"

,Keukeu. You've got a good voice. Here's a gift! Dark Lightning!"

Black lightning made of black magic is fired at Seturt. Seturt didn't get the position of great warrior for show, as he swung his sword and deflected the black lightning.

,Stop him."

The Skeleton Knights, whom I had made my bodyguards, rush towards Seturt. The Skeleton Knights were equipped with weapons and armor received from Hamor. They won't be able to beat Seturt, but they will buy me the time I need right now.

,There's a saying that there's no one as scary as a necromancer on a large-scale battlefield. I'll show you why.'

I chanted a spooky spell.

The dead desert warriors rise as undead. They're not very useful in this state. Because they're just low-level undead. I chant the spell again to turn them into mid-level undead.

,The scary thing about a necromancer is that they can constantly replenish the undead in a battlefield overflowing with corpses!'

I can see souls.

Souls trying to escape from the dead bodies, souls trapped in the bodies of the undead, are trying to escape, taking advantage of the chaotic battlefield.

,Where do you think you're going! You're all mine!"

I used black magic to catch the fleeing souls. Souls were needed to create undead in the form of spirits.

Kkiaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!

The captured souls scream. It's a terrible scream to the living, but it sounds like a sweet voice to the undead. And I, wearing Tutankhamun's golden mask, was now undead.

,Keukeukeuk."

I wave my hand. Following my gesture, the undead jumped and dived at the enemies.

,Corpse Explosion."

I exploded nearly 100 undead at once. The explosion range was narrow, but the explosive power was enormous. A sandstorm arose and covered everything in an instant. The black rain falling from the sky quickly calmed the sand dust.

The momentum of the desert warriors was broken. This was because nearly 1,000 desert warriors were caught in the explosion and died. The undead army was also caught up in it quite a bit, but I'm satisfied with breaking the enemies' morale.

,You evil bastard!"

Great Warrior Seturt jumped off his camel. He stepped on the heads of the undead and approached me. It was a light movement.

Seturt swings his sword at me. Instead of using black magic or dodging, I threw a punch.

Just because I'm wearing Tutankhamun's golden mask doesn't mean I can't use Cheonma Shingong.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

Seturt's body twisted as he was hit in the stomach with a direct hit from the Dragon Fist. His body flew backwards and rolled on the ground. He knelt on the sandy ground and shouted, spitting blood.

,Scorpion King-isiyeo! Give me strength, hasoseo!!"

A giant scorpion appeared from under Seturt's feet. Seturt took a stance on top of the giant scorpion. The giant scorpion rushes towards me with a fierce momentum.

,Block it."

Zombies ran out in droves to block the scorpion's path. But the scorpion easily shook off the zombies.

,Tsk. You're not helping."

I swung my palm towards Seturt.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Shinjang (Heavenly Demon Divine Palm)

The claws of the giant scorpion burst from the Cheonma Shinjang. The guy who lost his balance swung his tail. Seturt flew towards me, using the swung tail as a stepping stone.

,It'll be different from before!"

,Yeah. I'll kill you for sure this time."

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Dragon Hand (天魔龍手).

I grab Seturt's sword with my fingers. The fingers filled with Kanggi tried to penetrate the sword, but he also covered the sword with Kanggi.

,Don't underestimate me!"

Seturt's strength suddenly increases.

,Did he use a buff-type skill?'

His sword pierced my right hand. Half of my palm is cut. Black blood flows. I didn't feel any pain because I was in an undead state.

,The condition of Cheonma Shingong is not good either. Is it because my heart isn't beating? Is it because I don't have explosive vitality? Either way, the efficiency of Cheonma Shingong is not good.'

Seturt's blade cuts through my chest. I calmly grabbed Seturt's shoulder with my left hand.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Hoicheon Essence Immersion (回天浸精).

Cheonmagi penetrated into his body and rotated. Not only the muscles but also the internal organs were ground away in an instant.

,......!!"

Seturt couldn't even scream and fell with his eyes rolled back. I looked down and checked my body condition. My chest was split open and my heart was visible. Both the blood and the heart are black.

,Come here."

I cast a soul-devouring spell on a nearby soul. My body returned to normal. The cut palm reattached, and the split chest was perfectly rejoined.

I look at the dead Seturt's corpse.

The corpse and soul of a strong person who can use Kanggi. It will definitely be helpful.

,Great Warrior Seturt-nim has fallen in battle! Retreat!!"

,Retreat!!"

The desert warriors, who had been charging bravely, quickly turned their camels' heads. Even though there were less than 200 of my undead left, they started running away as fast as they could.

,Even though there are 1,000 of them, they're running away. They're smart guys.'

They made a judgment.

Even if 1,000 people attacked me at the same time, it wouldn't work. It was a wise decision. I could replenish my troops with the corpses around me.

,It'll be hard to chase after the guys who are running away.'

First, I decided to take control of the corpses of the camels and desert warriors around me.

,Rise!"

The corpses rise.

In the case of camels, perhaps because of their large size, two low-level undead rose.

I felt dizzy for a moment as my mana drained quickly, but my mana filled up in the blink of an eye.

,Is it about 3,000 including the undead that rose from the camel corpses...? It's a shame about the corpses that disappeared after being caught in the Corpse Explosion."

I chanted a spell. I turned the low-level undead into mid-level undead. Nearly 1,000 mid-level undead were newly formed into my army.

,This isn't the end. Next time, they'll bring an army to fight me. Even in preparation for that time, I need to get my hands on Book of the Dead 2 and 3 quickly.'

I ordered the undead around me.

,Move without dawdling, you useless corpses!"

Despite my orders, I didn't like the slow movements of the zombies and ghouls. They're okay as meat shields, but they're too slow.

,Wait. There's a black magic that combines corpses to create something. Was it Chimera? I need to modify these slow-ass undead bastards into powered-up zealots.'
