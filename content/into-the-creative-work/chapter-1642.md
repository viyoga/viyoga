,Use Fire Ball."

Skeleton mages use Fire Ball according to my command. Fireballs pour toward the oasis.

Pueong! Peong! Pueoooong!

The Fire Balls exploded, and the water in the oasis evaporated in an instant. I glared at the oasis, which was spewing out cloudy water vapor.

The amount of water had decreased, but it was a negligible amount for the entire oasis.

,It would be faster to dig a waterway and drain the water."

Fortunately, there were tireless workers around me right now.

,Work, you corpses! Move quickly and drain the water from the oasis!"

The undead legion began to move to drain the water from the oasis. I even modified some of the undead to be workers. I gave them a shovel in one hand and a pickaxe in the other.

Like that, 1 hour.

All the water in the small oasis was drained, and the bottom was revealed. At the bottom was an antique door engraved with a sphinx. It also felt somewhat sacred.

,The Book of the Dead 2 is inside there.'

First, I sent the undead inside first and slowly went inside.

「You have entered Ramesseum for the first time!」

「You have acquired 30,000 AP.」

「The Son of the Sun is watching you.」

I came inside. It was a large space that could accommodate 1,000 troops. The pillars were large and thick, and the height of the ceiling alone seemed to be 20m. It was a magnificent building.

,Ramesseum? I think I've heard of it somewhere.'

In times like these, I can just go to reality for a bit.

I went to reality and learned about Ramesseum on the internet.

It was the temple and tomb of Ramses II.

,The Son of the Sun must be Ramses II.'

It didn't seem like Ramses II was particularly hostile to me.

,Is he the type who isn't very interested in Atlantis?'

From my point of view, it was rather good. It would be annoying if he interfered.

I checked the map first. The Blessing of the Desert was activated here as well. It shows me the map of this damn big and wide temple.

,It looks like there are 5 underground floors. I have to go there.'

The surroundings were quiet.

Quiet with nothing. There were no living creatures, and not even a breeze was blowing.

Of course, I wasn't careless. Because it was a definite fact that there was something in places like this.

I was walking for a while.

An alert window popped up with a heavy voice.

「Oh Pharaoh! Overcome all 10 plagues and prove your majesty!」

「The first plague begins.」

It was right after the voice ended. Something fell from the ceiling with a cheolpudeok. It was something with moisture.

Gae gul gae gul.

It was a frog.

Countless frogs fall from the ceiling.

Cheolpeok!

A frog fell on top of my mask. I grabbed the frog with a nervous gesture. I made eye contact with the frog.

,Annoying."

I threw the frog on the floor. The frog's body burst. Intestines and blood splattered.

I commanded the undead.

,Kill all the frogs."

The undead faithfully followed my command. Ghouls ate the frogs, and skeleton knights swung their swords at the frogs. Hwiing! The frog ran around and avoided the skeleton's sword.

,You fucking retards. Can't you even cut down one frog?"

I clicked my tongue.

I liked the skeleton mages the most. These guys were massacring the frogs by shooting Fire Balls mindlessly, whether allies were harmed or not. It feels like there are more allies dying from being burned to death... but I guess it's just my imagination.

,It doesn't matter if they get hurt. I can just replenish the undead. Arise."

The dead frogs rise. Here, Undead Modification (SS) shined. It was to create a super-large undead frog by combining the corpses of 1,000 frogs. The super-large frog ate the frogs with its long tongue. The super-large frog grew in size each time it ate a frog.

I used the frog corpses to create 10 super-large frogs. The super-large frogs' mealtime continued. And at some point, the sound of frogs croaking was no longer heard.

「You have overcome the first plague.」

「The King of the Myriad Heavens says it was a trivial disaster.」

「The second and third plagues begin.」

I was a little nervous. Even if the first plague was so trivial that it was absurd, I couldn't let my guard down.

About 3 minutes passed.

A new alert window popped up.

「You have overcome the second and third plagues.」

,What the hell happened?"

「The King of the Myriad Heavens tells you that it was a plague related to disease.」

,...I see."

It was natural that nothing happened.

Me and my troops were undead. Already dead bodies. What meaning does disease have for a corpse? It has no meaning.

I continued to move forward. I found stairs leading to the basement. I went down to the basement without hesitation.

The basement was a large space with one-way traffic. The problem was that the space was filled with water. It was impossible to drain the water because all sides were blocked by hard stone. I can't do anything with the water with magic either. The black magic I know is limited.

,Is the water depth about 5M?"

Should I even make a boat?

But that's too much of a hassle.

,Just move forward. You guys are already dead, so you won't drown. Go."

The undead army advanced into the water.

I sat comfortably on a wooden plank that I used as a palanquin. Large, reinforced ghouls grabbed the rope connected to the wooden plank and pulled.

,Tsk. It's shaking too much. Pull it carefully."

I give them a jab. I don't think the reinforced ghoul would have understood. It just adjusted its speed in accordance with my will. I glance down at the water. Armed undead are advancing step by step.

I can see the super-large frogs swimming. Even though they became undead, they swam well like frogs.

,Should I entrust the rope to the frogs instead? ...No. Now that I think about it, the ride would be the worst if I saw those bastards swimming. Let's go like this even if it's slow.'

「The fourth plague begins.」

The water turns red.

I put my hand in the water and took it out. It had viscosity to be called water.

,Blood.'

I couldn't smell it now because I was undead.

,The water has turned into blood.'

That's not all. Dozens of crocodiles are swimming in the water that has turned into blood. They were so big that they seemed to be at least 8M tall.

,How funny. You dare to stop me with just crocodiles! You stupid corpses! Tear those crocodiles apart and kill them! Bring me crocodile skin!"

The undead soldiers show hostility towards the crocodiles. However, the movement of the undead in the water was much slower than usual. The super-large frogs were the only ones moving quickly and attacking the crocodiles. But it wasn't easy.

Once bitten by a crocodile, it was over. Was it called a death roll? As soon as the crocodile bit the undead, it immediately rotated its body and tore off the undead's torso. Even if the undead were immortal, there was no way to move.

,Hoo, fuck. Stupid corpse bastards... They're not helpful."

There was also the point that I didn't modify them considering fighting in the water.

,This is a desert, so why should I consider fighting in the water.'

Of course, I had no intention of considering it now either.

I jumped up on the wooden plank and used black magic.

,Dark Lightning! Dark Lightning! Dark Lightning!"

I shot out black lightning. It was effective because it was in the water. The crocodile that was hit was electrocuted and went to hell.

,Don't even think about dying comfortably after blocking my way! Arise!"

I also make the dead crocodiles undead. I preserved the corpses as much as possible. Unlike humans, a giant body of 8M will definitely be helpful.

,What are you doing! Now that you've become undead crocodiles, quickly bite and kill your own kind!"

At my urging, the undead crocodiles nervously wag their tails and swim towards their fellow crocodiles.

,These bastards..."

I don't like them for some reason. I should use the undead crocodiles as modification materials later.

I was still shooting out Dark Lightning in the meantime. The damage to the undead army is reduced if I keep checking them. It seems like about 50 of them are already in an irreparable state... but losing more will be a greater loss.

,It's okay to lose them while fighting. But losing them like this without meaning is not okay.'

I have to kill the ruler of the golden desert. Now is the time to cherish the undead legion.

,Dark Lightning! Dark Lightning! Dark Lightning!!!"

I killed all the crocodiles.

After that, the crocodile herd attacked about 2 more times, but thanks to my active participation, I was able to cross the blood-turned water without much damage. 100 new undead crocodiles were created. Considering the undead who were attacked by the crocodiles, it's plus or minus zero.

At the end of the blood-turned water, I could see the door leading to the next floor.

「You have overcome the fourth plague.」

,Crossing the water itself was harder than the crocodiles."

I moved to the 2nd basement floor.

「The fifth plague begins.」

I looked around. I thought something unusual would pop out. But the surroundings were quiet.

,Was it like the third and fourth plagues, where disease was rampant?'

I waited 3 more minutes.

Nothing happened.

,I don't know what the plague is. Move forward."

Kung kung!

The undead march in step. The ones leading the way were the crocodile undead. They were fun to watch because they were big.

And I belatedly realized what the fifth plague was.

It was lice.

Head lice. Bugs that parasitize hair.

Lice stick to my skin and try to suck blood. I stared intently at what the lice were doing. It pierced my skin as if it were not ordinary lice. The lice seemed to be sucking blood, but immediately fell off my body.

I was undead.

Blood does not flow in my body. All the blood in my body was stagnant and rotten. And that was the same for other undead besides me. No, it could be said that it was even worse.

,It's nothing much. Let's ignore it and go."

「You have overcome the fifth plague.」

「The sixth plague begins.」

Wiiiiiiiiing!

I heard the sound of a drone flying. I looked straight ahead and opened my mouth.

A swarm of flies the size of 10cm appeared.
