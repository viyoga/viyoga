After ending [Bright Light Ascension], I returned to reality and immediately used Original on Stormbreaker.

As a bonus, I used it on the Heavenly Demon Throne as well. The Heavenly Demon Holy Sword was being enhanced by Bright Light Ascension, so I couldn't take it out.

The Heavenly Demon Throne, befitting its flashy appearance as a chair, had an uncomfortable structure, but when you actually sit on it, it's extremely comfortable. That's because it has the ability to stabilize the mind and body.

,Just sitting on it restores vitality and mana. It's more precious than other recovery items in that it restores mana. The really good ability is mental stabilization. Even if you fall into Mana Deviation, you can avoid the worst just by sitting on this chair.'

As a bonus, it also had a curse-ignoring effect.

I turned on the Game Life app and checked my status window.

[Seong Yujin

Level: 96

Strength: 150 Stamina: 150 Agility: 150 Intelligence: 150 Virility: 160 Mana: 160]

[Available Points: 31,179]

Combined with the points I already had, I had quite a bit.

I felt a strong urge. Should I use 30,000 points on stats? Or skills or traits?

After agonizing, I decided to use it on Strength.

[Seong Yujin

Level: 96

Strength: 160 Stamina: 150 Agility: 150 Intelligence: 150 Virility: 160 Mana: 160]

[Available Points: 1,179]

I could feel the power in my clenched fist.

I immediately moved to a suitable dungeon to test my Strength.

Without using mana, I could lift an object weighing 4 tons with just the power of my pure body.

,When my Strength was 150, it was about 1 ton... If I enhance my body with mana, I think I can lift even 10 tons.'

Now I feel like I can tear a bear apart with my bare hands without using mana.

,Can I kill monsters with my bare hands too? Should I try it once?'

I was currently in a B-grade dungeon. There wasn't an A-grade dungeon nearby that I could enter right away.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Wave.

An invisible electromagnetic wave mixed with mana spread out from me. I adjusted the range to 1km. The electromagnetic wave collided with objects and returned to me, conveying that information. The principle was similar to radar. But because it wasn't pure radio waves but mixed with mana, its effect was even better.

A detailed map was drawn in my head. From the bugs crawling on the ground to the movement of the swaying trees, I grasped everything. It felt like high-performance video was being collected and played back in my head.

,Found it. 300m to the right.'

I ran straight there.

B-grade monster, Strahlenhirsch.

It was a monster with the appearance of a sika deer with four eyes. Its horns were covered in gruesome little thorns.

As soon as it saw me, it emitted light from its whole body and charged. That was its trait. As soon as it sees a person, it charges unconditionally. It was worse than a roe deer. For reference, even a B-grade Hunter could die instantly if they were hit by that charge head-on.

I lightly dodged to the side. Quite large trees couldn't avoid the Strahlenhirsch's charge and fell. The guy who charged 30m turned his body again. Took a breath and charged again. A speed that a normal B-grade Hunter would have to move quickly to avoid.

,An A-grade Hunter wouldn't be hit unless they were careless. An S-grade Hunter can crush it head-on.'

I had a purpose, so I dodged for now.

The guy passed me and didn't stop even after crashing into the tree like before.

I ran up, grabbed its back, waited for the light to disappear from its body, and swung my fist. Pure physical strength without loading mana. The fist went straight into the guy's side.

Pueoooook!

,Kweeeeeeeek!'

The guy flew away and rolled on the ground.

I aimed for the heart, but it didn't die.

,So B-grade monsters are still monsters. Right. It was this kind of guy.'

Ordinary monsters below A-grade weren't much different to me. If I attack using mana, they die in one hit, so it's the same. Especially since I'm fast. Most monsters below A-grade can't even react properly.

The guy died only after being hit by three more punches.

,I really killed a B-grade monster with my bare hands.'

I was satisfied with the strengthened power. The stronger the basic body, the stronger it will be when using mana.

,It'll be hard to kill anything A-grade or higher with my bare hands.'

Of course, that's when I'm not using mana.

I then took out Stormbreaker. I did apply Original, but Stormbreaker's performance is basically affected by my strength and class as the user. I need to know its performance at least roughly so that I can be comfortable.

As soon as I touched the Stormbreaker in its giant axe form, I got a feel for it.

,The hardness of Hwaryeon Bido, it doesn't even reach half of that. When I flowed mana through it, it felt a bit stuffy. Even the responsiveness has dropped.'

It's a shame to remember that the mana responsiveness in [Bright Light Ascension] was better than Hwaryeon Bido.

,Armor form.'

Stormbreaker finely disassembled and shattered. The pieces stuck to my body and became a sleek armor. Not Eastern armor, but Western full body armor. There was a visor, so there were no gaps. The eyes implemented a special material metal so that the outside could be seen. Even if that wasn't the case, I could use Clairvoyance to see outside with my X-ray vision.

I found another Strahlenhirsch. As soon as it saw me, it emitted light from its whole body and charged. I deliberately didn't dodge. I took a stance and prepared for the impact.

Kwaaaaaang!

My body was pushed back 5m.

,Kkuwek?!'

The panicked guy stepped back. The light it was emitting from its whole body also went out. I didn't miss that gap and swung my fist. Unlike before, I loaded it with mana.

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

With an explosion, the guy's body burst.

I raised a Protective Aura to block the blood and guts pouring onto my body.

,My stomach hurts. Stormbreaker didn't absorb all of the impact. At this rate, I'll probably get a bruise.'

It felt like a waste to use my ultimate move, Full Recovery, for just this much. If it was home, maybe. This was a dungeon.

I took out a top-grade recovery potion and drank it.

,Stormbreaker is... dented. Should I be glad it wasn't pierced? At this rate, it can withstand a few attacks from A-grade monsters.'

That's the conclusion I came to considering that it was an attack that could kill a B-grade Hunter if they were hit head-on.

,It's not enough. At this rate, it won't even be able to properly withstand an S-grade's attack.'

I checked the experience points Stormbreaker gained. The experience points gained from killing one B-grade monster was 150. More than I thought. But the more I kill the same monster, the less experience points I'll get.

,A-grade monsters will give more experience points. But it's a bit of a hassle.'

It's not the end when you enter the dungeon. You have to move around to find monsters. And when all the monsters are dead, it takes time for them to respawn.

The number of monsters a Hunter can hunt in a day is surprisingly not that many.

,Wandering around dungeons for experience points is inefficient.'

If it were someone other than me, they would have no choice but to wander around dungeons and hunt monsters. Because that's the best they can do.

,But not me.'

All I had to do was find a Game World where I could kill a large number of various monsters.

As it happened, I thought of a suitable creation.

I finished hunting and went home.

[You have selected To the Top of the Tower with Infinite Pocket Dimension.]

It was a web novel I had seen recently. It was called Infinite Tower for short. Martial arts actually appeared in the work.

A world where towers appear in various countries, and Hunters who have awakened their abilities climb the towers, kill monsters, and hunt to earn coins. These coins can be used to delay the collapse of the towers. If the towers collapse, the countries disappear.

The protagonist of this work awakens a special ability called Infinite Pocket Dimension. It wasn't just an ability to store things in a pocket dimension.

What I paid attention to was that you can hunt monsters infinitely by climbing this tower.

,Even if the hunting efficiency drops due to repeated hunting... well, I can just hunt in another Game World at that time. There are many tower climbing stories.'

Basically a modern background. Like in reality, monsters occasionally appear in the city, but the Hunters take care of them. The number is so small that it's a safer world than reality for the average person.
