I snatched the golem's joystick controller from Pesserus as if I were stealing it.

Back in the day, I used to frequent arcades and play fighting games. I had more than enough confidence to control a golem.

I grabbed the joystick and moved it briskly.

But reality was quite different from my expectations. The golem, which should have been moving nimbly, creaked and groaned as it moved its arms and legs. Its arm slashed uselessly through the air, and its raised leg pointed sideways instead of forward.

Buuuwoong.

The golem's massive right arm grazed the castle wall. If it had been just a little closer, it would have attacked the wall.

"Y-Your Excellency!"

"What the fuck! The controls are shit. How the hell did you make this control system?!"

"It was based on fighting games... but reality and games are different...."

Is that supposed to be an answer?

Annoyed, I threw the joystick away. A shocked Pesserus dove to catch it.

"I, I can control it! Please believe me! The Great Golem King will fight for you, -kakka!"

"You better control it properly."

If I used hacking, I could probably control the golem as I pleased.

'Considering the size of the golem, the time I could control it with hacking would be less than a minute.'

It would be better to leave the control to Pesserus. Pesserus wasn't trustworthy, but he was an ally for now.

"Just leave it to me!"

He jumped off the castle wall and landed on top of the golem's head. Just then, a barrage of offensive magic fired from the magic artillery in front. The golem's barrier activated and blocked all the magic.

I shouted to Pesserus.

"Pesserus! How many more of these attacks can you withstand?!"

"We can easily withstand about 30 more!"

30 times.

I calculated in my head. The magic artillery had a slight gap after each attack. It was the time to insert the magic stone, the fuel for the magic artillery.

Considering that, the golem could withstand it for about 10 minutes.

'I have to wreak havoc on the battlefield within 10 minutes.'

I looked down at the ground from the top of the golem.

"Waaaaaaaah!"

Enemy soldiers were screaming and rushing at us like crazy.

Contrary to the loud shouts, the soldiers' faces were filled with fear instead of fighting spirit. Since they were mages full of elitist ideas, it was obvious how the soldiers were treated.

Judging by the fact that they were still forcing the soldiers to charge without hesitation, it meant that they didn't care how few soldiers there were.

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and held it in my hand.

Pajijik.

Red thunder wrapped around my entire body.

I pointed the tip of the blade towards the ground. The destination was the center of the rushing soldiers.

Pajijijijijijik.

The red thunder gathered at the tip of the sword, reaching its peak. I kicked off the golem's head and fell to the ground.

Thunderclap Flash (雷天流) Falling Thunder (落雷)

Kwaaaang!

The moment I landed on the ground, a shockwave spread in all directions. The enemy soldiers closest to me were shattered, their fragments flying everywhere, and the enemies nearby were knocked down or blown away.

Thunderclap Flash (Leitianliu) Cold Thunder (Hanlei).

Red lightning and aura spread out from me, sweeping away the enemy soldiers.

"Hiiiik!"

"It's Count Procus!"

"R-Run away!"

Their morale was already at rock bottom. The soldiers, who had completely lost their will to fight, were desperately trying to escape from me. It was a stupid choice. There was no way they could escape smoothly with so many people crowded together.

'It's not difficult to massacre these guys. I want to kill them all if I could. But killing these guys alone won't win the war.'

The guys I need to target are those mages in the back.

Kuuuuuung!

A heavy sound hit my ears. I turned my head slightly to check behind me. The gate behind the golem was open.

"Charge! Follow your lord!"

The Golden Rose Knights came running with Floy's shout. The eyes of the horses they were riding were shining with madness. They were forcibly boosting the physical abilities of those expensive warhorses with drugs and magic. Even if they won the war, the warhorses would die from the side effects. Using warhorses like consumables. That's how important this war is.

"My lord!"

Floy ran straight towards me, trampling enemy soldiers. Next to her was a warhorse clad in armor. I jumped up and mounted the half-crazed warhorse. I pulled the reins with all my might to control the warhorse.

"Let's go. Follow me closely."

"I will follow you to the ends of hell!"

"Hell, my ass. I'll lead you to heaven!"

The warhorse galloped forward. The warhorse, armed with dwarven armor, was no different from a tank. It was impossible for ordinary soldiers to block it. They were just trampled or run over.

Tatatatatatatang!

Gunshots rang out from behind. I had given assault rifles to our allies in this war.

Until now, I had refrained from using firearms in large-scale wars as much as possible. It was partly because it was a weapon that didn't fit this era and wasn't fun, but also because it would be a hassle if the enemies imitated the firearms.

But this war is the last one.

There will be no war for a while after this war ends.

"My lord! There's a mage in the sky ahead! It looks like Archmage Dutan! Dutan is a master of earth magic!"

A particularly sharp-eyed knight reported.

One of the seven Archmages was chanting magic in the sky. His mana fell to the ground and became magic. A rock barrier shot up from the ground. He intended to block us with a wall.

"Turn to the right!"

I forcibly changed direction. The horse was strained. I didn't care and galloped across the battlefield.

'I can't just go.'

Thunderclap Flash (雷天流) Dual Resonance (二重共鳴) Swirling Thunder Spiral (卍雷螺旋)

Red thunder and blue thunder resonated and became a laser, shooting towards the Archmage. The Archmage leisurely deployed a barrier.

The Swirling Thunder Spiral pierced through the barrier and penetrated the Archmage's upper body.

'He should have dodged without blocking. If he couldn't do that, he should have deployed the barrier with all his might. Did he think the Swirling Thunder Spiral was ordinary lightning?'

A moment of misjudgment. The price was his life. One of the seven Archmages died.

There was no time to be happy. We were surrounded by enemies. If we were careless even for a moment, we would be the ones to die.

"Don't stop! If you stop, you die!"

Cavalry must run.

Running is the only way to survive.

"My lord! Another Archmage! He's in the back!"

"Yeah. I see him. An old hag."

"It's Archmage Seganeu! She's famous as an ice master!"

I lowered my gaze and saw a blue energy scattered on the ground like fog. It was cold. My body, which had been heated by the heat of the battlefield, instantly turned cold.

Jjeojeojeok, jjeojeojeok!

Along with the sound of freezing, dozens of large icicles appeared in the sky. The tips of the icicles were pointed at us.

"Icicles are falling! Prepare for impact!"

The icicles fell in a flurry. The knights, including me, gritted our teeth. Sharp icicles fell and battered our bodies.

We can endure it. The knights here are veterans who have experienced all kinds of hardships. The armor they wear is a masterpiece made with all their heart by the dwarves.

Kkaang! Kkang! Kkaang!

We broke through the rain of icicles. About three of them seemed to have dropped out in the process, but we couldn't go back. All we could do was hope they would hold out.

"My lord! It's a trap!!"

Floy shouted urgently. Mercenaries were gathered in front. They were holding clattering chain nets in their hands and laughing at us. There were more than 100 mercenaries, and the chain nets they held in their hands totaled dozens of square meters. The moment we got caught in the net, the horses would not be able to run.

"You dare hold up a chain net in front of me? You've got some nerve. Floy! I'll go ahead and take care of it!"

I kicked the horse and leaped forward.

"Count -naeri is crazy!"

"Does he think he's invincible just because he's an aura master? Aura master or whatever, he's nothing if we catch him!"

"Throw the net!"

The net came flying. I deliberately let the net hit me. The sound of the chain net scraping against the armor was very annoying.

"Pull! If we kill this guy, our lives will be full of flowers!"

"The only road open to you is the road to hell!"

Pajijijijijijik!

Red thunder flowed along the chain net. The mercenaries who were trying to pull the net were electrocuted. They couldn't even scream, their brains were fried and they fell to the ground.

The mercenaries who weren't holding the chain net were startled and stepped back.

"Escape? Don't even dream about it."

I grabbed the sword hilt with both hands, rotated my body, and swung the sword.

Thunderclap Flash (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷闪).

The blue slash wrapped in red lightning instantly cut through the mercenaries who surrounded me. Blood soaked the battlefield.

"My lord!"

Floy came. I ran, jumped, and mounted the running horse.

"Hang in there! We'll wreak havoc on the battlefield one more time and return!"

After that, Yuria, who rescued Princess Eileen, would join us. That's when it would be time to end the Magic Federation.

That's when it happened.

A variable occurred.

The magic artillery that had been focusing on attacking the golem began to use magic in a different way.

The ground shook. At first, I thought they were taking the damage and using large-scale earthquake magic.

"That's...."

The ground was rising.

It was a golem.

They had used magic to create a giant golem that was not inferior to the Jjapjinga. It wasn't just any golem. Its body, made of earth and rock, was burning with flames.

'Was something like that even possible with magic artillery?'

The Fire Golem walked towards the golem controlled by Pesserus. The sizes were similar. The Fire Golem was crude, and Pesserus's golem was elaborate. The two golems did not back down and collided with each other.

Kwaaaaang!

The impact sound could be heard all the way here.

Fortunately, Pesserus's golem did not budge. Their strength was almost equal.

'It can hold out. That's good.'

I wanted to smash the magic artillery first, but it seemed difficult because the knights, mages, and veteran mercenaries were on high alert, guarding the magic artillery.

'There's no need to attack recklessly. First, the flow of the battlefield....'

Jjeojeojeojeojeok!

An ice wall shot up from the ground. Surprised, I dodged to the side. The problem was that the ice wall blocked the path between me and the knights. Floy struck the ice wall with her sword, but the ice wall, which was more than 1m thick, could not be easily broken even with an aura blade. It was no ordinary ice wall.

"Floy! Change of plans! Take care of the mages at that end and return to the castle! I'm...."

I stared straight ahead. Two aura masters were running towards me to fight me.

"I'll take care of those guys and go back."
