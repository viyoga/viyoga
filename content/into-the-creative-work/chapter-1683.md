"It's coming!"

A sharp-eyed instructor said in a tense voice. Immediately after, the bushes in the back mountain rustled. It was too unnatural to be just the wind.

"What's coming is here. Remember, don't pull the trigger recklessly. Don't waste bullets. If we run out of bullets, we're the ones who'll die."

The head instructor said sternly. Recruits with rifles couldn't hide their tension and swallowed hard.

"I, I saw it! A vampire just passed through the trees!"

"What are you doing! If you saw it, shoot! If you don't kill it first, you're the one who'll die!"

"Y-yes, sir!"

Tang! Taang! Tangtangtang!

Gunshots rang out in the dark night. The instructors and recruits fired at the back mountain. Screams were occasionally heard between the gunshots. They were the screams of vampires hit by bullets.

I, lying prone with my sniper rifle aimed at the main gate, didn't look back despite the gunshots and screams.

"Recruit number 25."

The head instructor next to me called me.

"Yes, Head Instructor-nim."

"That unwavering demeanor, it's excellent! There are vampires coming from behind. Aren't you afraid?"

"I trust Head Instructor-nim."

More accurately, I trusted the original work. In the original, he wins against the vampires despite being ambushed. Unlike the original, I know in advance and am prepared, so there's no way I'll lose.

"Recruit number 25. You move me. If there were more talents like you, the number of cockroach-like vampire bastards would have decreased a lot. The company wants talents like you...."

Before I could finish listening to the head instructor, I pulled the trigger. Not to interrupt the boring head instructor, but because a vampire appeared at the front entrance, beyond the parade ground. The vampire that came into my sight had a hole in its forehead and died.

The head instructor immediately put on infrared binoculars and exclaimed.

"To successfully snipe at a distance of over 500m at night so easily.... Your sniping skills alone are better than the instructors."

"Thank you."

Even while saying that, I saw an enemy. I pull the trigger. The vampire's reaction wasn't fast enough. With a distance of 500m, the vampire couldn't see the muzzle and dodge. The silver bullet accurately hit the vampire's head. The problem was that the vampire was wearing a helmet.

It wasn't an ordinary helmet. It was too big to be an ordinary helmet, and it looked like a solid piece of steel. It was a helmet that would break a human's neck if they wore it. Only vampires, who are stronger than humans, could wear such a helmet.

'It's not just the helmet. They've completely covered their bodies with steel plates.'

The vampire runs like an armored knight from the Middle Ages. The speed is slow, but the defense is that much higher. It feels like an armored vehicle is slowly coming towards me. Vampires follow behind him.

"Those dumbasses actually used their heads a little."

The head instructor clicked his tongue.

"Aim for the other vampires. That would be better. You can handle the vampires following like ducklings, right?"

"I will kill all those blood-sucking mosquito bastards, including that steel lump."

"I acknowledge your spirit, but realistically, that's impossible."

"I can do it. I'll show you."

[Using Momentary Lapse (Instant). Remaining stacks: 6]

Even if I couldn't use mana, I could use skills.

Using Momentary Lapse slows down the world. I maximized my concentration. Momentary Lapse feels like it's stopped, but it hasn't. The world is flowing, albeit very slowly.

'Since I used Momentary Lapse, pulling the trigger is instantaneous. But I have to calculate the speed at which the bullet flies.'

I was aiming for the gap in the neck. A small gap between the helmet and the armor. A part that wasn't covered, perhaps for convenience.

'To cover the eyes without a gap, but not the neck, is absurd.'

It's not that I don't understand. That gap is under the helmet, and the gap is so small that it's difficult to hit even when shooting from a distance of 10m. But I'm different. Not only do I have the shooting skills, but I also have Momentary Lapse. If I push my concentration to the limit, I can do snipes that are impossible for others.

'Now.'

I pull the trigger. With the gunshot, the silver bullet draws a straight line and accurately hits the neck of the armored vampire. The advancing vampire's steps stop. Eventually, the vampire falls forward. The thud reaches my ears.

"...Huh. This is beyond amazing. Recruit 25. Are you perhaps a vampire? Or does werewolf blood flow in your veins?"

"If I were a vampire or a werewolf, could I do what I just did?"

"I asked a stupid question...."

I continued to pull the trigger.

Because the bastards behind the armored vampire scattered and rushed at once.

There's no need to panic. The distance to those bastards is more than 400m. Even if they cover 100m in 8 seconds, I have more than 30 seconds.

'30 seconds is enough.'

I pull the trigger and slam silver bullets into the vampires' heads. It doesn't take even 2 seconds to change the magazine.

[Using Momentary Lapse (Instant). Remaining stacks: 5]

I use Momentary Lapse. I identify the positions of the vampires in the slowed time and pull the trigger in succession. The results are all hits.

Those bastards were annihilated without getting close to 100m.

"Processing complete."

"Perfect! But don't let your guard down until the end! Among the vampire bastards...."

Kwakwakwang!

A vampire soared from the roof floor. It was a vampire that looked like a mole. Before I could turn my gun at him, the head instructor moved. As if he knew in advance, he pulled a sword from his waist and cut off the vampire's neck in one go.

"There are bastards who crawl on the ground like earthworms."

The head instructor shouted at the recruits who were surprised and turned to look at this side.

"Look behind you! They haven't given up yet!"

The recruits turned their heads again and aimed their guns at the back mountain. Me too. All the crazy vampires running straight ahead were dead, so I aimed my gun at the back mountain.

"They're coming!!"

Choi Sangwoo shouted. The werewolf's senses detected the approaching vampires.

"Kyaaaaaaak!"

More than 10 vampires in the mountains rush to the building at once. The recruits panic and pull the trigger, and the instructors grit their teeth. Choi Sangwoo was fighting one-on-one with the vampire who had crossed over to the roof at the very front.

'It's too close to hit.'

I didn't panic. Maintaining my composure, I systematically deal with the bastards I can kill for sure.

The battle ended in 3 minutes. The vampires, terrified by the overwhelming battle, turned around and began to run away. I missed about 4 of them. I couldn't snipe them because they ran into the mountains.

"Rejoice! We have won! I am proud of you!"

The head instructor shouted and declared victory. The instructors and recruits shouted. I also shouted with joy. The number of vampires I killed alone was 17. I killed about half of the vampires who attacked.

'This means going to the Seoul branch is confirmed... and I'll even get a reward. Maybe I'll get promoted as soon as I join the company....'

My happy circuit is burning brightly.

And after a while, support troops arrived from the company. After that, buses and trucks came.

"I know you're tired. But this location is already known to the vampires. It wouldn't be strange if we were attacked at any time. We have to leave as soon as possible. Pack your bags. We're going to the company dormitory in Gangwon-do. We'll finish the training there."

Following the head instructor's orders, we had to move our luggage without even sleeping.

• • •

The graduation ceremony started early in the morning.

This is because we have to go straight to the company after the graduation ceremony.

The recruits, including me, wore suits before the graduation ceremony. Not ordinary suits. Suits with the essence of human technology melted into them. They have a stab-proof effect and are more comfortable to move in than sportswear. Not to mention the durability.

"Company employees wear suits. It's only natural. You all look good in suits."

The head instructor on the platform continues his speech. He tries to give lessons by comparing them to his past. I endured the time, suppressing my yawns.

"Recruit number 25, Seong Yujin. Come forward!"

I got up from my seat and approached the head instructor. This scene was being recorded by an instructor.

The head instructor handed me the certificate of completion himself.

"Recruit number 25. I hope you live a long life. Live a long life and get rid of those damn mosquitoes."

"I will show the mosquitoes what hell is. I will engrave on them that this world belongs to humans."

"I like that very much. No, Seong Yujin Employee-ssi."

The head instructor personally hung the employee ID around my neck.

"As the top graduate, you will receive 20 million won in support funds. Also, since you contributed to this vampire attack, an additional 100 million won will be given as compensation. Use it where you need it."

I swallowed my disappointment. It would have been better if they had given me personnel points instead of money.

"Thank you."

The 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place graduates were all assigned to the BC Company Seoul branch. 1st place was me, and 2nd and 3rd place were Choi Sangwoo and Ji Hyunseong. I got into the car provided by the company with them. I took the driver's seat. Choi Sangwoo sat in the passenger seat.

Ji Hyunseong, who was sitting in the back seat, started the conversation first.

"I heard that new recruits who have just joined the company are paired with veterans. Who do you want to be paired with?"

"You tell me first. I don't know anyone in the Seoul branch."

I asked. Choi Sangwoo was quietly looking out the window.

"Me? Of course, I want to be with Acting Manager Ju Seohyun-nim! Her skills are great, but her appearance is also as beautiful as a goddess. You guys saw that video at the training center, right? Her swordsmanship was unrealistic. Ah, but realistically, it might be impossible. Because Acting Manager Ju Seohyun-nim doesn't want a partner."

I was a little concerned about that too. It's obvious that she moves without a partner because it's more comfortable to move alone. That's Ju Seohyun's personality.

"Choi Sangwoo. What about you?"

"I...."
