Ju Seo-hyun was sitting face to face with Special Division Chief Kang Myung-sook.

Ju Seo-hyun was wearing a restraint suit exclusively for vampires. Two Jaegers stood on either side of her, tense. She could tear the restraint suit apart if she wanted to. Because she wasn't just an ordinary vampire, she was Noble-class. No, she was stronger than Noble-class.

,Ju Seo-hyun. I'm disappointed."

Kang Myung-sook said.

,I didn't think you would ever become a vampire. Even if you did become a vampire, I thought you would end your life right then and there. I guess I misjudged you."

,......."

Ju Seo-hyun couldn't chase Yoon Seo-jin right after becoming a vampire. It was difficult for her, who had just become a vampire, to track down a specific vampire's blood scent. Plus, you can't properly exert your strength when you first become a vampire. She needed to get used to the power of a vampire.

,There's something I don't understand. Why didn't you run away? You should have had plenty of time to escape. Why did you surrender to the company? Don't tell me you still think you're a company employee even after becoming a vampire?"

Ju Seo-hyun slowly opened her mouth.

,I need the company's help."

,...It's not about money."

,I need to kill Yoon Seo-jin. I need information to track him down."

,I heard your partner was killed by Yoon Seo-jin. Is it revenge?"

,...Yes."

,......."

Silence descended. In the suffocating silence, no one dared to speak rashly.

Ju Seo-hyun thought Kang Myung-sook would accept. Because she hates vampires more than anyone else, and Kang Myung-sook, as she knows her, is someone who can even use that vampire.

,...Losing a sword like you isn't good for us either. But there's one major problem. I can't trust you. Understand?"

,I will die after killing Yoon Seo-jin."

,That's quite a resolution. But do you know? I've seen dozens of guys who said they could maintain their humanity even after becoming vampires. Do you know what happened? After a week, not a trace of humanity remained. How can I trust you?"

Vampire blood even messes with the human brain, changing the way they think. It happens as quickly as a few hours, or at most a week.

That's why the company couldn't use vampires to fight vampires.

,Give me at least a week."

,What should I trust?"

,I once heard a story about an experiment to control vampires."

,It failed miserably. Oh, and by the way, there's no way to turn you back into a human."

,I heard they put a bomb collar around the vampire's neck back then. I'll wear that collar."

,You're not an ordinary vampire. A strong human becomes a strong vampire. That law exists. You might be able to disarm the bomb collar with your ability. Oh, I haven't asked about your ability yet. What is it?"

,...I can control blood."

,That's truly an ability for a vampire."

Kang Myung-sook fell into thought again. She got up from her chair. She took the key and unlocked Ju Seo-hyun's restraints.

,There are conditions. One is to wear the bomb collar. I'll put pieces made of silver in that bomb."

,...What's the second?"

,I will manage you directly. Listen to my orders."

,I will not listen to any orders other than those to kill vampires."

,What are you going to do if you don't listen? Don't you need the company's help?"

,I'll just have to try on my own."

,Are you confident you can get out of here?"

,Yes."

,As expected of the best Jaeger. I won't give you any orders other than to hunt down vampires. I have no intention of giving you any personal orders either. Please, as my sword, kill your fellow vampires."

,Tracking Yoon Seo-jin is a condition."

,Of course. He's the most dangerous vampire in this world."

,Then first..."

Kang Myung-sook slightly raised the corner of her lips.

,I know what you want. The first thing humans feel when they become vampires is hunger."

Kang Myung-sook took something out of the drawer. It was a pack filled with human blood. Ju Seo-hyun glanced at the blood pack and then looked straight at Kang Myung-sook again.

,Give me back my sword. I can't give that sword to anyone."

,Are you talking about your beloved boyfriend's keepsake? Look at this devoted woman. I never knew you were this kind of woman. I'm learning a lot of new things about you today."

,...Seong Yujin is not my boyfriend."

,Then what is he?"

,He's my enemy. The enemy I should have killed..."

,Ha... No one would believe that kind of talk looking at your face right now."

,What's wrong with my face?"

,It's the face of someone who has lost a loved one. A face I used to wear a long time ago."

,......."

Ju Seo-hyun slaughtered vampires as Kang Myung-sook's dog.

She fought more than five times a day, and sometimes she couldn't even sleep, slicing up vampires every day. But there was nothing wrong with her body. Her physical abilities were much stronger than other Noble-class vampires. Also, the acceleration ability she originally had became even stronger.

,Butcher! You became a vampire, so why are you attacking your own kind! Do you think humans will reward you?! It's not too late! Come to us!"

Noble-class vampire Baek Ji-oh shouted. In the past, he had his subordinate vampires attack a company-affiliated training center.

Ju Seo-hyun didn't respond at all.

The puddles of blood on the floor moved according to Ju Seo-hyun's will. The blood transformed into the shape of swords and flew through the air.

Igieo Geom.

She was using that technique that Ju Seo-hyun often used in Atlantis through the power of a vampire.

The blood swords moved as if they had their own will, slaughtering the vampires around them. The vampires couldn't withstand it. The subtleties of swordsmanship were imbued in each and every blood sword.

Even with the physical abilities of a vampire, they could barely keep up with the movement of the swords, and even the subtleties of swordsmanship were imbued in them? Ordinary vampires couldn't even respond. They could only die.

The vampires die.

The vampires' blood flowed out, and the blood swords became more numerous.

Baek Ji-oh was dumbfounded and opened his mouth blankly.

,...Yoon Seo-jin was wrong. Queen of the vampires? That's not a queen, that's a god. A god of vampires... who kills vampires."

Ju Seo-hyun drew her sword from her waist. Sonata sparkled in the moonlight. Baek Ji-oh blinked and opened his eyes. Before him, Ju Seo-hyun had already appeared, holding her sword to his neck.

She had traversed a distance of 100m in less than a second. It was unbelievable.

,Tell me where Yoon Seo-jin is."

,...I've been caught. I obtained the information that you were looking for Yoon Seo-jin. I knew you would ask me that."

Baek Ji-oh pressed the detonator he had hidden in his hand. The bomb hidden beneath Ju Seo-hyun's feet exploded.

Kwaang!

But Ju Seo-hyun was unharmed. Blood surged beneath her feet. The blood had perfectly protected Ju Seo-hyun's body.

,...Did you know?"

,No. My ability just moved on its own."

,What kind of ability is that?"

,Where is Yoon Seo-jin?"

,Haha. I don't know, you bitch!"

Baek Ji-oh's wrist was severed and flew towards Ju Seo-hyun's head. It was Baek Ji-oh's ability. Ju Seo-hyun lightly swung her sword. Baek Ji-oh's body and the severed hand were simultaneously split in half.

There were no vampires alive around her.

She touched the ring-shaped necklace around her neck.

,Chief. It's over."

-As expected. I didn't think you would eliminate more than 80% of the vampires in Korea alone in just five days. You're amazing as expected.

Chief Kang Myung-sook's voice echoed from the bomb necklace.

,Baek Ji-oh didn't know where Yoon Seo-jin was either."

-We found a trace on this side. Yoon Seo-jin left Korea two days ago. He's in Romania now. In a country occupied by vampires.

,...Did you tell me on purpose late?"

-Of course not. This information is fresh, I just heard it. So, what are you going to do?

,I'm going to Romania."

Kill Yoon Seo-jin and get revenge. That was the only thought dominating Ju Seo-hyun's mind right now.

Ju Seo-hyun met Yoon Seo-jin.

Yoon Seo-jin was not alone. He appeared with 3,000 vampires. Two other vampire Lords were with him.

,Butcher. Put down your sword. Join us. If you join us... the world will be ours, the vampires'. We are ready to serve you as our queen."

,I finally met you, Yoon Seo-jin... Die!!"

Ju Seo-hyun rushed towards Yoon Seo-jin. She narrowed the distance, slicing through the vampires blocking her path. Blood drained from the bodies of the dead vampires and became swords. The blood swords killed other vampires.

Soon, the blood, which had become so plentiful that it formed a small reservoir, began to indiscriminately kill the remaining vampires.

Yoon Seo-jin and the two vampire Lords couldn't close their mouths at the overwhelming sight.

,Yoon Seo-jin! I didn't hear that this woman was this strong!"

,Damn it! Why is she killing her own kind?! Even a human-born vampire should have affection for their own kind! There must be! There must be...!"

Unlike the two who were making a fuss, Yoon Seo-jin smiled bitterly.

,She's on a different level. Well, she was a woman who could compete with a vampire Lord even when she was human... I should have killed her back then. This is the first time I've ever regretted something so deeply."

The heads of the two vampire Lords flew upwards. Yoon Seo-jin could barely see the afterimage of Ju Seo-hyun's sword.

Ju Seo-hyun glared at Yoon Seo-jin and swung her sword.

Yoon Seo-jin used his ability. But before the vibration reached Ju Seo-hyun, the blade reached the top of his head. The sword split Yoon Seo-jin's body in half as it went down, killing him. Yoon Seo-jin's corpse fell to the ground and was submerged in blood.

Ju Seo-hyun exhaled.

The smell of blood.

The sweet smell of blood made her head spin.

,Revenge is over....'

It was the moment she was about to feel satisfaction and emptiness. The suppressed instinct awakens.

Kkoreureureuk.

I'm hungry.

She keeps thinking about the human blood she hasn't eaten all this time.

-Good job, Ju Seo-hyun.

Kwaang!
