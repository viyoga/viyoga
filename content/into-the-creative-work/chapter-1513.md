Eheuheueueung!

A tiger's roar echoed through the valley. Startled birds flew high into the sky.

The steps of the ronin walking towards the valley stopped at once. The relaxed atmosphere tightened. The ronin, without exception, grabbed their weapons and entered a state of alert.

I stared at the source of the sound with Milyeong.

Too far. Too far for even my eyesight to identify the monster. Of course, I couldn't figure it out with my senses either.

,I heard it.

,Yes, Seobang-nim. The Black Tiger's roar is thunderous.

,Some are overwhelmed by the energy.

,Well, they're not the guys I called to get rid of the Black Tiger. These guys are the goons to deal with the Black Tiger's goons.

They probably know it themselves.

Facing the Black Tiger was my job and Gujigeom Jeong Cheok, the branch leader of the Ronin Association.

,Can you find the exact location with magic?

,Well, I can do it if I want to. The problem is that it will take some time to prepare. There's nothing to build on. And I don't think they'll wait.

The tense ronin were slowly approaching the valley.

I pondered. Should I step up here, or should I stay still? They know nothing about Milyeong. They just know her as a woman I brought with me.

,What are you going to do?

,I don't want to step up. I can use any magic for Seobang-nim, but I have no intention of using magic for strangers I don't even know. And even for the Jongnam Heavenly Genius... it's better to hide my identity right now.

Even if I use magic, I have to hide at least my realm. If it becomes known that there is a magician who has reached the Samjeong realm, it will be chaos. It was natural to attract the attention of the Jongnampai.

,Okay. Let's watch for now.

There will be casualties, but it was none of my business.

Eheuheueueueung!

The tiger's roar echoed again. It was closer than before. The sound came from the front.

,Are they planning to ambush from the front? If it's a monster that has reached the Five Energies realm, its intelligence will be as high as a human's.

A word flashed through my mind.

Trap. You must abandon the arrogance that only humans can set traps.

The subjugation team tensed up and entered the dense forest. Despite being daytime, the surroundings were dark. The densely packed leaves blocked the sunlight.

,It's coming.

,It's coming.

Milyeong and I said at the same time.

The bushes all around are shaking. It was too late when the ronin realized something was wrong. Black tigers that had been crouching down jumped out. They bit into the ronin's bodies, shining terrifying red eyes.

,Aaaaaaagh! It's an ambush!!

,Don't fight alone! Gather together! Gather together and respond!

,Shibal! Maryeom is dead! That shibal cat bastard!

The ronin's reactions are varied. Ronin who don't try to fight and back away, ronin who confidently swing their swords believing in their martial arts, and ronin who are furious at the death of their comrades and go crazy.

There was no order among the ronin. Jeong Cheok, who should have been leading the ronin, gave up commanding. Instead, he actively stepped up and faced the black tigers. It's a wise decision. It doesn't seem easy to control the ronin.

I feel a presence behind me. As soon as I sensed the presence, I drew my sword and swung it. I swung it without looking, and that judgment was correct. The black tiger that was attacking me was cut in half and died.

I feel a sinister demonic energy from the dead black tiger.

,It's a monster about the fifth stage of Ipsik.

,Just about the level of the ronin.

If the realms of humans and monsters are similar, who is more advantageous? It varies from time to time. Generally, monsters are more advantageous. A monster's body is superior to a human's in itself. But humans have martial arts and magic.

,Not that monsters can't learn martial arts and magic.

The stronger one wins. That's all there is to it.

I killed the black tigers that were rushing at me. After killing about three, the black tigers couldn't dare to attack me and started targeting other ronin. Having become more relaxed, I stood still and watched the situation. I didn't bother helping the ronin.

Eheuheueueueung!

The Black Tiger's roar echoes. The black tigers quickly retreated as if they had been waiting for a signal.

The situation was a mess.

More than 100 ronin died, and about 30 black tiger corpses are visible.

Milyeong and my gazes towards him were cold.

,Why should I help you?

,The reason you're dying is because you stood up to me.

Thud.

,What's urgent now is the subjugation of the Black Tiger. If we hesitate like this, we'll be defeated. Think about avenging the dead ronin first. Advance!

,Invincible Yujin Daehyeop. I have a favor to ask.
