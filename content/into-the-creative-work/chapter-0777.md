A pigeon soared into the sky.

And then, pierced by an arrow from afar, it scattered blood and fell to the ground. A vexed voice came from the pigeon's mouth.

"This goddamn... intruder...."

Gray energy flowed from the pigeon's body and vanished into the air.

Shin Jongwoo approached the pigeon and examined its corpse. More precisely, he was looking at the arrow that had pierced the pigeon.

"I don't sense any magic power from the pigeon... The arrow is ordinary. It has no distinguishing features."

"...Shin-sohyeop-nim. Could it be them?"

"No one but them would try to interfere so blatantly. Our location must have been exposed as the pigeon flew away. We need to move."

Shin Jongwoo and Namgung Rin acted calmly and swiftly. It seemed they had experienced this a few times.

'At this point, the Black Dragon Star must be chasing Shin Jongwoo. They're after Dunmokjeong's assassination and Shin Jongwoo's martial arts.'

If the original story hasn't changed much, the enemy's identity should be as I expect. But I pretended not to know and asked Shin Jongwoo.

"What the hell is going on? Who are these enemies who suddenly attacked us?"

Shin Jongwoo paused for a moment before opening his mouth.

"I was going to remain silent because I didn't want to involve you, but since we've come this far, I have no choice but to tell you. They are the Black Dragon Star. They are after me and Dunmokjeong-eoreushin. Rin-sojeo and Seong Yujin-ssi are just caught up in this. I'm sorry."

"Shin-sohyeop-nim. Don't apologize. You haven't done anything wrong."

"...Black Dragon Star. I've heard that name before. They're famous for being ruthless... How did you get involved with such bastards?"

"They're after the martial arts I possess. Because my martial arts are unique, the Black Dragon Star became interested. As for Dunmokjeong-eoreushin's circumstances... I can't talk about it."

I glanced at Namgung Rin. She didn't seem to know that Dunmokjeong was Muyeong Shintu.

"What are you going to do? Just keep running away?"

"The enemies won't be just one or two. I don't know the exact number, but the three of us won't be able to handle them. It's best to avoid them if we can."

"This isn't the way out of the forest, is it?"

"We need to save Eun Josa. He's different from other sorcerers. He must know something... something."

"Are you moving because you know where Eun Josa is?"

"...No. I'm moving based on a hunch."

It was absurd, but I decided to go along with it. This guy is the protagonist. In this kind of crisis, the protagonist's hunch is more reliable than any information. Well, he doesn't seem to have a clue about what kind of relationship I have with Namgung Rin.

Pudeudeudeuk, pudeuk!

A flock of birds flew from the sky. Pigeons, hawks, sparrows, and all sorts of birds moved erratically and made a fuss.

It must be Eun Josa's magic. That sorcerer is famous for controlling birds.

"It's that way!"

"The direction is right. But the atmosphere is ominous. It looks like he's fighting the Black Dragon Star."

"We have to go. We must get an answer from Eun Josa."

"Since we decided to help, let's help until the end."

"I agree with Seong-sohyeop-nim."

"...Thank you."

We changed direction and headed towards the place where many birds were flying.

As expected, a battle was taking place. But the situation was the opposite of what we expected. Eun Josa, wearing a feather cloak, was overwhelming more than 20 Black Dragon Star martial artists. More than half of the Black Dragon Star martial artists were already dead.

"Damn it. Just a sorcerer...!"

A martial artist in black armor gritted his teeth. Every time he swung his spear, a bird attacked suicidally, spraying blood and falling to the ground.

"Just a sorcerer? In my eyes, you're just martial artists."

Eun Josa, hiding his body with a feather cloak, was a man with a short beard and an X-shaped scar on his left cheek. His eyes were so deep that he didn't look human.

"I didn't hear that you were this strong! Just a sorcerer holed up in this small forest...! What the hell are you?!"

"Don't equate me with the sorcerers out in the world. The only reason I'm based in this forest and don't go out into the world is to improve my cultivation. Comparing them to me is an insult to me."

I was puzzled for a moment. The martial artist is fighting birds. Not spiritual creatures or anything, just ordinary birds. The martial artist in black armor seems to have similar skills to me. He's not someone who would be defeated just because there are many birds.

'...No. Are those birds... enhanced with magic?'

I was shocked when I realized it. There are over 5,000 birds there, roughly estimated. Enhancing each and every one of them and turning them into beings of the Ipsik (入式) realm? Eun Josa is a monster beyond my imagination.

"Kuaaaaaaak!"

"Aaaaaaak!"

The Black Dragon Star martial artists screamed. Their eyeballs were pecked out by birds, and their skin was chewed. The stench of blood spread faintly to this place, a little further away.

The Black Dragon Star martial artists already have no chance of winning.

"This is ridiculous...! Eun Josa is that strong... If Eun Josa was that strong, the main family wouldn't have left him alone."

Namgung Rin, shocked, muttered in disbelief. That made me snap out of it. As Namgung Rin said, if there was someone this strong around the family, they wouldn't leave him alone. They would either subjugate him, appease him, or pay tribute to him.

It's also strange that the Namgung family didn't even mention Eun Josa to Namgung Rin separately.

"It's a trick. He's using magic to pretend to be strong."

Shin Jongwoo said. Unlike the surprised me and Namgung Rin, he was consistently calm. He seemed to have realized from the beginning that Eun Josa was bluffing.

"Kueueueuk.... Eun Josa! Let's make a deal!"

The black-armored martial artist, who was left alone, shouted. His whole body was covered in blood. Most of it was the blood of the birds he had killed.

"You're asking for a deal because you're at a disadvantage? I'll listen. Spit it out."

"I am Baek Chongmu, the 3rd squad leader of the Black Dragon Star's Assassin Squad!"

Eun Josa's eyes became even colder.

"So? Am I supposed to know the name of someone like you?"

"If I die, do you think the Black Dragon Star will leave you alone? The Black Dragon Star shows no mercy to its enemies. If I die, other experts from the Black Dragon Star will appear and kill you to show the Black Dragon Star's majesty!"

It wasn't entirely impossible. The Black Dragon Star is famous for being ruthless even in this heartless world.

"If you thought I would fear the Black Dragon Star, you're greatly mistaken."

Eun Josa raised his hand. The thousands of birds that covered the sky flew far away. Instead, a large hawk over 5M flew from the other side of the sky.

'That's a spiritual creature.'

I'm sure of it. Ordinary hawks can't grow to that size. I don't feel any ominous energy, so it's not a monster.

'The grade is quite low, but since it's a spiritual creature, it must have a core.'

Greed slowly raised its head.

The large hawk rushed towards the black-armored martial artist, extending its sharp claws. He hurriedly raised his sword to block the hawk's claws.

"Eun, Eun Josa! I haven't finished talking yet!"

"I'm done talking. Say whatever you want."

"Please spare me! I won't touch this place again! I'll talk to the Black Dragon Star! I swear!"

"Your oath has no weight. It's too light, not even worth a chick's feather."

"I'll pay the price! This armor! This armor I'm wearing is a treasure made from the scales of an Imugi!"

"Is that all? If it's a treasure, it must have special effects?"

Unlike his prickly words, Eun Josa seemed interested. When he waved his hand, the large hawk flew into the sky.

"Wearing the Irin Black Armor increases stamina."

"Surely that's not the only thing?"

"Of course not! If I, wearing the Irin Black Armor, receive a fatal attack, the Irin Black Armor will take the damage instead of me."

"Hoo. That's amazing. Is that all?"

Unlike his words that it was amazing, Eun Josa's tone and atmosphere were flat. The armor-clad martial artist, feeling impatient, quickly continued.

"I can control snakes!"

"...Could it be that you can also control spiritual beasts?"

"N, no. I can only control ordinary snakes."

"I see. If you were that outstanding, there's no way someone like you would have that item."

"The Irin Black Armor is an amazing item just for taking fatal damage instead! Wearing the Irin Black Armor is like having another life!"

"I acknowledge that ability. Of course, there must be a limit... But you know what? Do I really need to make a deal with you? If I kill you anyway, I'll get that armor."

"T, the Irin Black Armor can only be worn if the ownership is properly transferred!"

"Hehe.... That's a lie that even a dog wouldn't believe...."

The large hawk swooped down on him again.

At that moment, Eun Josa's voice was heard in our ears. Eun Josa had sent a telepathic message without the Black Dragon Star martial artist knowing.

-You guys. Don't just watch, ambush him and kill him! If you can't kill him, I'll consider you enemies too!

It turned out that he was bluffing. If Eun Josa was overwhelmingly strong, he wouldn't have asked us for help. The reason he stopped controlling thousands of birds must be because his strength was running out.

Me, Namgung Rin, and Shin Jongwoo exchanged glances and rushed towards the Black Dragon Star martial artist. The Black Dragon Star martial artist was an enemy we had to kill anyway.

"Heok?! Y, you bastards! That's a cowardly ambush!"

"You're not the one to talk."

I, who was the first to get behind him, smirked and swung Hwaryeon Bido. The martial artist was horrified and rolled on the floor. It was a splendid flurry of knives.

"Eun Josa! Are you an idiot? The Irin Black Armor takes fatal damage instead, which means you can't get the Irin Black Armor if I die! The Irin Black Armor will break before I do!"

"It's okay. I've already cast a spell. The Irin Black Armor you're wearing now is just a simple armor."

"W, when!"

A feather was stuck in the side of his armor, near his waist. That must be what's suppressing the effects of the Irin Black Armor.

Namgung Rin's sword rushed in. The martial artist cut his left arm. Unfortunately, the sword didn't inflict a deep wound.

"I, I can't die here! Get out of my way! You brats!"

He swung his sword wildly. No, it looked like he was swinging it wildly. If you look closely, there's a pattern. It's one of the stances.

'The unseen presence of the blade... It's sword wind.'

Me and Namgung Rin dodged to the sides at the same time. The sword wind passed through the place where we were. The martial artist's face distorted.

"You...!"

"It's over."

Shin Jongwoo said. The magic power gathered in his hands was shot at the Black Dragon Star martial artist. He, who had just used a big attack, couldn't avoid Shin Jongwoo's attack. The black energy erased his head. The body without a head fell to the ground.

"You stepped in at the right time. Good job."

Eun Josa approached leisurely. He praised us and then immediately approached the martial artist's corpse. He was recovering the Irin Black Armor.

"This black armor is mine. You don't have any complaints, do you?"

"Take it."

"I will."

Eun Josa clumsily took off the Irin Black Armor from the martial artist's body and wore it under his feather cloak. The armor was covered in brain fluid and blood, but he didn't care at all. I thought he was a weirdo, but it seems he's really crazy.

"It's not bad."

Eun Josa nodded and then passed us again.

"It's not good to talk here, so follow me. I'll serve you tea."

We followed him. Shin Jongwoo asked him with a serious expression and tone.

"Eun Josa-nim. I want to know information about other worlds."

"Unlike your calm atmosphere, you're impatient. Just follow me quietly. Important stories should be told in a suitable place."

"......Yes. I'm sorry."

Shin Jongwoo apologized. Shin Jongwoo was the one who had a lot to lose. He desperately wants to go back to Earth now.

However, I didn't have much interest in Eun Josa, nor did I have a reason to impress him, so I asked what I was curious about.

"Are all the other intruders dead?"

"They're all dead. Except for you guys."

"...Are you going to kill us too?"

"I've acknowledged you as guests. If that weren't the case, would I be guiding you myself like this? Think about it."

"......."

I don't like this bastard.
