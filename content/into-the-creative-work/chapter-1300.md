,Uwaaaaaaaah!"

The slave soldiers screamed in terror.

One by one, slave soldiers gave up their charge and tried to flee the battlefield. Of course, there was no intention of allowing the slave soldiers to desert. The fleeing slave soldiers were hunted down by friendly soldiers.

The slave soldiers had only two choices: charge, or be killed by their own side.

Peong, peong, peong, peong, peong!

With over 10,000 slave soldiers dead, the magic mines that had been exploding continuously finally quieted down.

Despite the magic mines' effectiveness, the enemy soldiers wore a look of disgust as they watched the slave soldiers continue to charge. They looked like they were staring at a bunch of lunatics.

,My lord. Please allow a magic bombardment."

,Already?"

,Even so, being unilaterally attacked isn't really my style. We took one hit, so shouldn't they take one too?"

,That's a good point. Start the magic bombardment. But don't do it towards the rear. Marquis Deikerd shouldn't get hurt."

,Understood. I will direct the magic bombardment towards the enemy's vanguard. There's a high chance our own troops will get caught up in it, but that's a minor problem."

Scarlett gave orders to the officers. The officers moved busily.

The allied mage division prepared their magic in unison. I could feel the mana surging even from where I was standing.

,We have Archmage Sharnel."

Mana surged from the enemy camp as well. They were preparing for the magic bombardment. I, who was looking at the enemy, raised the corner of my lips.

,This won't do much damage."

,Yes?"

,There's an Archmage on the enemy side. He's at Sharnel's level."

,...Count Halgeris, I see. He's an Archmage from the Solfmed Kingdom. He was a noble who maintained neutrality... It seems he's gone under Marquis Deikerd at some point."

Count Halgeris's presence could be felt among the enemy mages. He was an old man with a white beard, holding a magic staff and casting a large-scale defensive spell.

Count Halgeris was an old man with an impressive white beard. He was easily comparable to Sharnel just by his appearance. In this case, Sharnel, who became an Archmage at a young age, was absurdly amazing. He was probably the youngest on the continent.

The allied magic bombardment began. Numerous attack spells streaked across the sky towards the enemy. The magnificent sight was like hundreds of missiles being launched.

However, the enemy didn't just stand still. The attack spells were intercepted mid-air or blocked by defensive magic, canceling each other out.

,There's no Hellfire. Where's Sharnel's magic?"

It was the moment I felt a sense of doubt. Dark clouds formed in the sky, and the surroundings instantly darkened.

,Judging by the fact that the dark clouds are only gathered above the enemy's heads... that's Sharnel's magic."

Red-hot fire fell from the dark clouds to the ground.

It wasn't a rain of fire.

It was a rain of lava. In a way, it could be said to be even worse than fire. It wouldn't have the heat to melt armor instantly because the temperature dropped rapidly as it fell to the ground... but unlike rain, it had a heavy mass.

Not all of the enemy soldiers were wearing steel armor. It was the worst kind of magic attack for soldiers wearing leather or wooden armor.

,That's spectacular. I never imagined Sharnel-ssi could use magic of this level."

,Didn't you tell him to use that?"

,Sharnel-ssi doesn't listen even if I order him to. He only cooperates to some extent if I say the lord told him to do it. He probably used that magic to end the war even a little bit faster."

,How will the enemies respond?"

I watched the enemy with great interest. I didn't think the tide of the war would change with just one great magic spell. They had an Archmage too, after all.

Jjeo-eo-eo-eong.

A loud sound rang out. It was a sound that seemed to freeze the air.

I frowned, touching my ears at the sudden noise. Then, I widened my eyes as I looked at the enemy. A giant ice shield was created in the air, blocking the rain of lava. As soon as the rain of lava touched the ice, it turned into rock and rolled down the ice shield to the ground. The place where it fell was, of course, where there were no enemy soldiers.

,Ooh. Halge-whatever was an ice-type mage."

,He blocked it more easily than expected. The enemy's casualties are... about 7,000. We should give meaning to the fact that he used large-scale magic."

Even if he was an Archmage, he couldn't just spam large-scale magic. We wouldn't have to worry about large-scale magic for the time being.

Scarlett called the officers and gave orders. She was giving orders to the officers next to me... To be honest, I couldn't understand even half of what she was saying. There were a lot of technical terms, and she mixed in code words in her instructions, probably conscious of eavesdropping. She was a thorough woman.

,My lord, please move with the Golden Rose Knights."

,I was planning to do that from the start. Where should I go?"

Scarlett pointed to a certain spot with her finger.

,To the right. Do you see that place?"

,I see it. It's swarming with them."

,I will force a path open. Charge in a wedge formation and capture Marquis Deikerd in the rear. If that's not possible, just stirring up the enemy will be enough."

,...You don't think it will be decided in one go."

,The scale of the war is too large for that."

I nodded and got on my horse. I summoned Stormbreaker and made it into armor. After fully arming myself, I prepared for a frenzied charge with the Golden Rose Knights.

I looked back at them. They were all female knights who had shared a bed with me. They had kissed my Butthole and sworn their loyalty.

,Floi. You've prepared everything, right?"

,Yes. I have distributed space-time scrolls to everyone. Unless they die instantly, they will be able to leave the battlefield using the space-time scrolls."

,You're wearing armor made by dwarves, so you won't die instantly. If you think you're screwed, tear up the space-time scrolls. Your lives are more important to me than hundreds of thousands of soldiers. Don't forget that fact."

,My lord. We will not die."

The female knights, including Floi, said confidently.

This was war. Even with space-time scrolls and dwarven armor, I couldn't expect a 100% survival rate.

But that didn't mean I could stop them. They had chosen the path of a knight. Stopping them because it was dangerous would be no different from me disregarding their dignity.

,Hiiiing!"

I fed the horse a magic potion and tore up a magic scroll.

The magic potion enhanced the horse's physical abilities, and the magic scroll eliminated the horse's fear.

Jijik.

Scarlett's voice could be heard from the radio in my right ear.

-My lord. Please sortie.

Cheolkeok.

I put on my helmet.

I turned my head and looked around. The Golden Rose Knights were all wearing helmets as well. They held the reins in one hand and a lance in the other.

,Let's go. I'll lead the way."

I started running towards the enemy. The flamboyant red cloak draped over my shoulders fluttered. This level of flamboyance was perfect. That way, they would be able to follow me well.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!"

Ujiggeun.

It seemed the horse had stepped on an ally. It wasn't just any horse, but a warhorse doped with potions and magic. An ordinary human would die instantly just from being stepped on.

Allies were trampled to death by the horse. It seemed about a hundred allied soldiers had died. I didn't care. Ordinary soldiers were only slightly more useful than slave soldiers. They weren't much different in that they were expendable.

-My lord. You're slightly skewed to the right.

I immediately corrected my direction at Scarlett's radio message.

The enemies came into view. They were mixed in with the allies, fighting. The smell of blood was strong, and the sound of swords clashing sounded as gloomy as a requiem. The soldiers' screams were so commonplace that they sounded like the chirping of insects.

,We're breaking through."

I held my lance and lowered my upper body. I moved my aura. A blue aura blade enveloped not only the lance but also my entire body and even the horse.

Kwaaaaaang!

I collided with the enemy. The enemy soldiers' bodies shattered like dropped glass bottles, flying in all directions. Blood splattered against my armor.

Starting with that, I and the Golden Rose Knights began to gallop across the land filled with people. There were so many enemies that it was difficult to find direction.

-My lord. To the right.

Scarlett's timely orders came whenever I got lost.

Leona Deikerd, the marquis commanding the enemy, came into view at the edge of my sight. We were getting closer.

-...My lord. Could you go to the left?

,To the left?"

-I apologize, but the allied 15th unit is collapsing rapidly.

,Do I have to go?"

-It is necessary for a perfect victory.

I turned around. The bloodied Golden Rose Knights were following behind me. If I concentrated my hearing, I could hear their rough breathing.

After a moment of hesitation, I decided to follow Scarlett's words. I grabbed the reins and changed the horse's direction. The Golden Rose Knights followed me without a word of complaint, even with the sudden change of direction.

,Block them!!!"

,Throw the knight nets!"

,Just because they're knights doesn't mean they're invincible!"

The enemies screamed and struggled. We laughed at them and charged forward.

Just because they're knights doesn't mean they're invincible. That was right. Even if there were superhumans gathered as knights, they would be helpless if hundreds or thousands of soldiers charged at them at once. But the story was different if an aura master was present.

The nets thrown by the enemies were torn to shreds by the aura I emitted. When I swung my lance at the enemy, at least five or more people died. As long as I didn't get tired, the enemies couldn't stop me.

-The Balmuth Knights are chasing after the lord. The response is much smoother than expected. Leona Deikerd seems to be a better commander than her reputation suggests.

I glanced up and looked behind me. Knights wearing black helmets were running towards us. They were the elite knights possessed by Leona Deikerd.

-The Balmuth Knight Commander is an aura master. The lord and the Golden Rose Knights are tired. I recommend retreating.

,No, I'm not tired enough to retreat. And we have two aura masters. Floi. Any problems?"

,No."

,Okay. Make a wide right turn and collide with the Balmuth Knights."

The warhorse ran.

Ordinary soldiers and slave soldiers were terrified by the appearance of the knights and scattered in all directions. Thanks to that, the surroundings were quite deserted. There was room to spare, but I didn't give the horse a break. Once I rested, the flow would be cut off.

Ttaenggeurang!

I and the knights threw away our lances and grabbed our swords. When the opponent was a knight, a familiar sword was much better than a lance.

The knights and the knights collided with each other.

Kwaaaaaang!

My vision was reversed with a huge impact. I couldn't withstand the impact and fell off my horse. The horse I was riding exploded and died. I focused and checked my surroundings. More than 80% of the allies and enemies had fallen off their horses.

The damage seemed to be greater for the enemy. We had reduced the damage due to the armor made by the dwarves and the warhorses fed with magic potions.

Kwaaaang!

I heard the sound of impact before my body hit the ground. Floi and the enemy knight commander were fighting.

,...The enemy knight commander is over there? Then who was the guy who collided with me...?"

As soon as I landed on the ground, I looked for the enemy. The enemy who had collided with me was running towards me with a greatsword. A red aura blade flashed from his greatsword.

,Another aura master...?!"

I hurriedly raised my sword and blocked his attack.

Kiiiiiiiiing!

A strong noise struck my ears as the aura blade and aura blade met.

,It's been a while, Eugene Prukerus! Do you know how much I've longed for this day!"

The opponent's face was not visible because it was covered by a helmet.

But I had heard this voice somewhere someday.

,Dullibard...!!"
