"......."

Silence settled in.

Everyone gathered here rolled their eyes, assessing the situation.

The most powerful force on the surface was the monsters of Jeokgol Aggwi (Accumulated Bone Evil Ghost) Ban-o. The great monster Ban-o was a Samjeonggyeong, and more than 10 of his monsters were Ogigyeong.

Next was Gwihon Heuksu (Ghost Soul Black Hand). An army of 500 Jiangshi. Even if most of the Jiangshi were at the Chulji level, the number of 500 could not be ignored. It was also important to note that they were Jiangshi, not humans. Jiangshi don't know fear. Moreover, in the case of Gwihon Heuksu, there was a high possibility that they had hidden Jiangshi. Like a final weapon.

We were the weakest of these forces. They had at least dozens, but we only had three. A Samjeonggyeong martial artist, a sorcerer, and an Ogi 10th stage martial artist. It felt a little too shabby to introduce.

'I can't look too weak. They might try to eliminate the weakest one first and then fight among themselves.'

It was necessary to look strong, even if it meant bluffing.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art).

I scattered demonic energy in all directions. Black demonic energy spread out from me. The Jiangshi touched by the demonic energy trembled. The enemies' eyes turned to me.

Gwihon Heuksu's eyes flashed.

"Kekeke. Demonic energy... and such dense demonic energy at that. A guy from the Demonic Sect!"

The monsters hesitated. They looked at me as if they were wary.

"Ominous demonic energy."

"I don't want to deal with that guy...."

"We should just kill him right now...."

Should I take a step with Cheonmagunrimbo? No. If I acted up any more here, I might end up provoking both forces at the same time. Fighting those two at the same time would be insane.

The tension in the space continued to rise. But hostility and murderous intent were not clear. Who should I target first? The people here couldn't make a decision.

"Do we need to fight?"

The great monster Ban-o opened his mouth. A mid-bass voice spread. I withdrew my demonic energy as if agreeing with his words. Spreading demonic energy to oppress all directions consumes a lot of internal energy.

"Keke. Are you suggesting we join hands? Do you think that's possible? Only three can reach the top of Domyungsan. I'm alone, so I can make allies, but... you can't."

"My intention is that the competitors are not just us. You must have noticed that the summit you see behind you is fake. The space of Domyungsan is not the end here. There are competitors who are not here. Fighting among ourselves will only do them good. Therefore, I demand a truce until we move to the next space."

Ban-o didn't want to fight. The most powerful force here was Ban-o's monster force. In other words, the other two forces. He was wary of us and Gwihon Heuksu temporarily joining forces to attack Ban-o. If there were three Samjeong masters, Ban-o wouldn't be able to help but worry about defeat.

"Kekeke. You're a talkative young one. What are you going to do?"

Gwihon Heuksu asked us.

Seong Jigone and Sama Ryung looked at me. It meant they were leaving the decision to me. I didn't think deeply. In fact, I had made the decision from the moment that great monster appeared. It was the moment I was about to open my mouth. A new presence appeared.

It didn't appear by moving through space like us. The door of one of the three houses opened, and an old man with a long white beard appeared. The old man, who looked like a Taoist immortal himself, slowly approached us.

"Hehe. There are many gathered this time as well. Judging by the ominous atmosphere, it means the world hasn't changed even after thousands of years."

"......."

Everyone was silent.

The mysterious energy felt from the old man. It was the energy of nature. The old man was metal, water, earth, wood, and fire.

"Are you a Taoist immortal?"

Gwihon Heuksu asked. Her vulgar laughter had disappeared somewhere.

"No. I am just the dregs of them. Hmm, yes. In other words, I'm close to a ghost."

"What is the reason that ghost appeared before us?"

"Because you are not trying to fight. Didn't you enter Domyungsan to get what you want? Fight. Bite each other's necks and destroy each other's hearts. Kill your competitors and get what you want. This place is a battlefield for you."

"......."

The battle did not happen as the old man said. It was always the case that people didn't want to fight when told to fight.

The others were lost in thought for a moment.

In my case, I wanted to kill the old man. Suddenly appearing and telling us to fight? I didn't like being ordered around. Even if that was not real and just a ghost, I could kill him. I had the power to kill.

"I see, I understand."

Sama Ryung spoke before I could move.

"Domyungsan is not a space of blessing or a space of miracles, but a huge ritual."

"That's right, sorcerer. You have a pretty good head on your shoulders. What is your name?"

"I am Sama Ryung."

"Were you from the Sama clan? Indeed. Even after thousands of years, the Sama clan is still brilliant. I am Hyejin Seonin (Wise Advance Immortal). Although I am only a ghost...."

"A ritual. What does that mean?"

Ban-o interrupted and asked. Hyejin, the Taoist immortal old man, glared at Ban-o with contempt.

"The answer is already out.... You're asking that? As expected, monsters are things you can't associate with."

"Kekeke."

Gwihon Heuksu laughed vulgarly again.

"This Domyungsan itself is a sorcery ritual for human sacrifice. The ritual is held every thousand years... it's just that the ley lines, the sky, and the position of the stars coincide every thousand years."

The old man did not refute. He didn't give any additional information either.

I frowned as much as I could.

"I don't understand even if I hear it."

"Daehyeop (Great Hero). There is no need to think hard. Domyungsan is a place where rituals are performed. In other words, it is an altar. Those who die in Domyungsan are sacrifices. And the battles that take place in Domyungsan are in themselves a ritual procedure. The more difficult and large the procedure, the sweeter the reward given at the end."

"The Sama family's words are indeed correct. If you want to climb to the top and receive what you want, kill each other. There is no reward without a price."

Ban-o waved his right hand. Bone spears created in the air flew at a rapid speed and pierced Hyejin Seonin's head. Hyejin Seonin disappeared as if his body was collapsing. His last voice is carried on the wind.

"You cannot move on to the next without death."

To go to the next, we had to kill each other. There was no peace here. Seong Jigone drew his sword, and Sama Ryung quietly prepared her magic.

I called out to Gwihon Heuksu in a loud voice!

"Gwihon Heuksu!! How about we postpone our fight and deal with those monster bastards first?!"

"Kekeke! I like the sound of that! It's only a temporary alliance until we kill those monster bastards!"

"These insignificant human bastards...! My patience has already run out! Kill those impious humans!"

Murderous intent overflowed from Ban-o's voice.

The monsters screamed and ran towards the Jiangshi corps. The Jiangshi responded and a battle broke out.

Some of the monsters targeted Gwihon Heuksu, who was controlling the Jiangshi. Seong Jigone ran to Gwihon Heuksu. To kill Gwihon Heuksu? No way. Rather, he was trying to help Gwihon Heuksu and gain favor.

The great monster Ban-o watched me, not Gwihon Heuksu. It seemed that he judged me to be the most dangerous.

"Kkeueeeeeeek!"

"These Jiangshi bastards...! They're using martial arts!"

"There's a Jiangshi with a magical artifact!"

The Jiangshi overwhelmed the monsters. Some Jiangshi used magical artifacts at the right time to gain an advantage in the battle.

Ban-o moved. Bones poured out of his space pocket. There were few human bones. Most of them were monsters with demonic energy.

"Gwihon Heuksu. Your Jiangshi can never overcome my bone soldiers."

Bone soldiers and corpses collided. I, who was a little further away, let out a hollow laugh. I didn't know if this was martial arts or fantasy.

I held the Hwaryeon Bido (Splendid Lotus Flying Knife) in my hand and clicked my tongue. I wanted to charge in, but Ban-o was wary of me. Moreover, there were monsters and bone soldiers between him and me. If I charged recklessly, I could be attacked instead.

"Daehyeop (Great Hero)."

"Sama Gaju (Sama Clan Head). Don't worry. It's a bit of a mess, but I will protect Sama Gaju (Sama Clan Head)."

"Daehyeop (Great Hero). I am not weak. I will open the way for you. Kill Ban-o. You must kill him now while cooperating with Gwihon Heuksu."

"...You say you'll open the way, so I'll give it a try."

Her slender hands formed an incisive seal. A spell was implemented, distorting the surrounding laws.

It wasn't starting from the beginning. Sama Ryung had been laying the groundwork for this spell for several minutes. As the spells were implemented, each one filled the surroundings and activated.

"Honwon Junggwang Su Gye Sik (Primal Chaos Heavy Light Ordination)."

Light burst out and surrounded the area in a circle. Some of the monsters and bone soldiers running towards us were pushed back without breaking through the light barrier. I knew this was a kind of barrier.

Sama Ryung raised her right hand expressionlessly. Only her index finger was stretched out. Light was gathered at the tip of her index finger. Her index finger pointed at Ban-o.

The light stretched forward. At some point, the light had become a huge sword and flowing water. When the sword pierced through the bone soldiers and made a path, the flowing water behind it widened the path.

Unfortunately, the sword did not do meaningful damage to Ban-o. Ban-o knocked the sword away with his fist. Instead, the flowing water remained the same. The path to Ban-o was opened.

"The path is open. Go."

I got on the flowing water. The water flowed at a rapid speed, moving my body. An escalator. No, it felt like riding a water slide. The distance to Ban-o quickly narrowed.

Pat.

Even though the bone knife didn't have Ganggi, I couldn't cut it. I knew that the bone knife was just bone in shape, but in reality, it had an energy comparable to Ganggi.

"I am in the middle stage of Samjeong. Do you really think you, who are only in the early stage of Samjeong, can beat me?"
