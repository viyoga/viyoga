Park Hae-gil was speeding down the road.

The area near Seongak Elementary School was safe. Seong Yujin and the security team periodically came out to clean up the zombies. Whether word got out or not, people gathered around Seongak Elementary School and set up temporary shelters. Even without going inside Seongak Elementary School, just being outside provided some degree of safety.

Park Hae-gil was planning to die. But he didn't want to hang himself or jump into the Han River. If he was going to die, he wanted to die meaningfully.

And right now, there were many meaningful deaths in this world. Since the D virus spread and zombies were roaming around.

,Even killing one zombie is meaningful. ...It would be meaningful, right?

He saw a zombie. Park Hae-gil smacked the back of the zombie's head with his insect hand.

Pak!

The zombie's head shattered like a watermelon. His evolution was progressing in an unwanted direction, but his body had become incomparably stronger than that of an ordinary person. For him, zombies were nothing now. Of course, he wasn't on par with mutants.

,Euaaaak! Monster!!

,It's a mutant!

,S, Save me!

He rescued people in danger, taking care of the zombies he saw until the sun went down. But no one thanked him outright. It was because not only his hands but even his head were turning into something like that of an insect.

Park Hae-gil despaired when he saw himself in the mirror. Even he thought he was a monster, not a person. An insect monster.

,They said that the more ability users use their abilities, the stronger they become. Was it the same for evolvers?

―――――.

He heard a sound from somewhere.

No, was it a sound? What was certain was that something was asking for help. Park Hae-gil, who had been despairing while looking in the mirror, listened for the direction of the sound.

In a dark alley, a mutant with three heads and large insect monsters were fighting. The insect monsters were at a disadvantage. Even though they surrounded the mutant, they were being unilaterally beaten.

―――――.

This was the sound. It was a sound made by insects. The insects were saying. Let's help them kill this monster.

Park Hae-gil was taken aback. When he thought about it, wasn't it because of those insect monster bastards that his evolution had turned out this way? So, he should feel hatred towards the insect monsters. But he didn't feel any hatred at all. Rather, he was pleased by their existence. It felt like meeting an old friend.

,...I don't know. When have I ever thought deeply and acted?

He moved as his instincts dictated. He ran at the mutant and attacked the back of its head. One of the mutant's heads, which had been off guard while fighting the insect monsters, flew off.

,It's a mutant with low intelligence. When dealing with a guy like this... Hit and run is the best.

The security team had dealt with mutants a few times under Seong Yujin's instructions. Seong Yujin was better than anyone else when it came to hunting mutants, even if he wasn't good at anything else. Seong Yujin was an expert at hunting monsters. On the other hand, his command skills were terrible.

,―――(There! Pretend to attack and pull back!)

He emitted a high-frequency sound and commanded the insect monster. The insect monster moved as if it understood.

He could command them.

The movements of the insect monsters, which had only moved according to their instincts, became orderly. The bugs' mutant hunt began.

5 minutes later.

The mutant was lying on the floor as a corpse, killed by the insect monsters. The insects cut up the corpse and tried to take it to the manhole.

,What have I done? Why did I help the insect monsters...?

It was when he was about to turn his body. The insect monsters all looked at Park Hae-gil. Park Hae-gil flinched and reflexively took a fighting stance. But the insect monsters didn't rush at him.

――――――.

The insect monsters told him to follow them. The insect monsters' will contained familiarity. Some of them even rubbed their bodies against Park Hae-gil's legs.

,...I see. Have I become their comrade?

They needed him. As if possessed, Park Hae-gil followed the insect monsters into the manhole.

Park Hae-gil disappeared.

He had left Seongak Elementary School after his mentality broke down from seeing me and Yang Su-bin having sex.

,I thought he'd hold out for a while, but he left the elementary school right away. This is a bit unexpected.

But I wasn't anxious or uneasy. I just figured it would happen. Park Hae-gil was a guy I didn't like from the start. I just couldn't get rid of him directly because I was mindful of Yang Su-bin. It was better that he disappeared on his own.

He holds a grudge against me? So be it. I don't think he's the type to hold grudges against others in the first place, though.

,He had many chances to stab me in the back. The fact that he didn't means he knows his place well? He's not that kind of guy. He just gave up. He's the type to die a dog's death somewhere.

What mattered was Yang Su-bin's reaction. It would be a headache if Yang Su-bin looked for Park Hae-gil and said she was going outside.

,...Is that Hae-gil's choice? It can't be helped. I don't know why, but I thought it would turn out like this. From that day, when I injected the evolution serum.

Yang Su-bin sighed and that was all she said. Her reaction was more calm than I expected.

,Was she giving up on him? Park Hae-gil's bug hands were a bit much as a human.

It meant that there was a genetic problem in the evolution process. Yang Su-bin, as a doctor, would know that fact, so she probably felt less and less of Park Hae-gil's charm as a man, or more fundamentally, as a human. Especially if she hated bugs.

,But to find him and kill him... That's a pain in the ass.

Park Hae-gil was a guy I didn't like. A guy I would kill without hesitation if I had the chance. Unlike David Kim, I didn't have a deep enough grudge to go through the trouble.

I just decided to ignore him.

I reached out and touched Yang Su-bin's thigh. Yang Su-bin flinched. She didn't reject my touch. The medical room heated up like yesterday.

,It's the first time I've met you in person. Let me formally introduce myself. I'm Lee Yeon-hee, the 4th Deputy Director of the National Intelligence Service.

A beauty in a black suit smiled and introduced herself. She was a beauty that made my eyes pop. Long black hair and snow-white skin. She was truly a beauty that fit the word innocent and lovely.

My liking for the NIS increased. Originally, I was just going to have a conversation and leave. I straightened my posture and said with a serious expression.

,I'm Seong Yujin from Seongak Elementary School.

,Yes. I know. Well, I don't know if you know, but we were the ones who spread Yujin-ssi's video on the internet.

,You guys?

,Yes. At the time, the internet and news were full of gloomy content. The only thing that gave people hope was news about the awakened. We thought that the video of Yujin-ssi fighting the monster spider would be a source of hope for people, so we spread it. It was a rash act. Of course, I don't intend to apologize empty-handed.

I actually liked it. Everyone was praising me. I don't hate people sucking up to me. Besides, it's helpful for my goals.

,Ahem. It was a bit unpleasant. What's the compensation? You wouldn't have brought something trivial, would you?

,Do you know about Antispheres?

What she took out of her pocket was a glass bottle containing gray pills.

,You know about it? Don't tell me that's an Antisphere? It wouldn't have been easy to make, would it?

Antisphere. A pill that protects the mind.

It was a drug that I, who had an absolute mind, didn't need, but it was essential for Na Chae-young and Choi Hye-jin. Taking this temporarily greatly increases mental resistance.

,I thought you hated mental abilities a lot.

Lee Yeon-hee was hitting the nail on the head. It meant that she perfectly understood the cause and effect of this incident. Unlike her lovely face, her atmosphere was sharp.

,She looks like she's in her 20s, but there's a reason why she rose to the position of Deputy Director.

First, I took the Antispheres. If I thought I was caught in a mental ability, I could give them this. It's also effective to take it in advance when dealing with mental ability users. But I wouldn't have to take it.

,I'll use this well.

,Yes. If you don't mind, may I ask what Yujin-ssi's goal is?

,First of all, it's survival. I have to survive no matter what.

,What about after that? From what I see, it seems like Yujin-ssi has already achieved that goal.

That's right.

This area has been completely cleared up. Just the absence of zombies greatly reduces the probability of dangerous mutants being born. Safety is guaranteed.

,To rule South Korea.

I couldn't say that. The other party was the NIS. In a way, they could be considered potential enemies.

,Actually, I don't have any specific goals yet.

The world hasn't completely collapsed yet. No, Korea hasn't completely collapsed. Normally, the TV and water should have been cut off.

But even though it's been several months since the D virus broke out, the water and electricity haven't been cut off, I can use the internet, and broadcasts are still on.

Of course, this is only the case in a few cities, including Seoul. Some Korean cities have been devastated. The number of deaths alone is at least hundreds of thousands. If you include the missing, it's millions. Maybe even over ten million.

,It needs to collapse more.

It will collapse more.

,I see. We need Yujin-ssi's strength.

,You want to use me like a dog?

,We would never think of doing that. We will pay a fair price. Currently, South Korea desperately needs the help of every powerful awakened person.

,Come to think of it. Korea is in this state, but what is the military doing right now? Are they playing around?

,...It's classified, but I don't want to hide it from Yujin-ssi, who is a hero of Korea. Currently, they are sealing off the cities that have been destroyed by mutants. They are doing their best to prevent zombies and mutants from crawling out. They are also recovering soldiers who have become zombies due to the D virus, and the front lines are preparing for North Korea's invasion.

,They're doing useless things. Do you think North Korea will invade at this time? They don't have anything.

,North Korea's dictatorship is on the verge of collapse. For them, invading South Korea is the ultimate means. We think there is a 70% chance that North Korea will invade.

,...The South Korean War, huh.

,Yes. It's a sad thing.

I distorted my face.

If a war breaks out, Korea will win. Because I'm here. Even if I'm not here, the result will be Korea's victory. Now that the gap between North and South Korea is so great, it's impossible for South Korea to lose.

But Seoul will burn. At the very least, they'll drop a few missiles on Seoul. Seoul, which can be said to be the heart of Korea.

,The Korea that I will rule will become weaker.

And North Korea is also a country that I have to rule. The South Korean War was not good for me in any way.

,Let's unify.
