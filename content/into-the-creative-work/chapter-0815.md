She took out a space-time scroll from her bosom. Seong Yujin-ssi told her to stay with him for only about a week, but she didn't think she could endure it. She wanted to go back right now.

But Nephia couldn't tear the space-time scroll. It was clear that Seong Yujin-ssi would be disappointed in her if she went back right away. She also had pride as the Head maid's proxy.

,Let's endure for a week.... Let's just endure for a week. It's not a difficult task. A week will pass quickly. I'm the Head maid's proxy. I have to carry out Master-nim's orders.'

Nephia looked out the door.

Just like the other maids who left the mansion, maybe an AM unit maid was watching her. No, there was definitely a high probability of that.

,Nephia. Let's cheer up. This is a mission as the Head maid's proxy.'

Nephia encouraged herself.

•••

Terius returned home with light steps just before sunset.

"Nephia! I'm back!"

Nephia, who was sitting blankly in a chair, got up.

"Welcome back, Terius."

"I even brought the meat you like."

Terius smiled and handed over the meat wrapped in paper that he was holding in his left hand. He didn't have money to buy meat, so he begged his fellow knight and borrowed money to buy meat. Of course, he didn't tell Nephia that. Terius also had pride.

Nephia's face brightened when she saw the meat.

"Meat! Thank you, Terius!"

"It's nothing. Shall we have dinner first then?"

"I can at least grill the meat. Leave it to me."

"I can do it."

"Terius, you must be tired from work, right? Sit in the chair."

Terius felt happy. These small conversations and peaceful daily life with Nephia were what he had been hoping for.

Nephia took out a pot from the house. A frying pan would be good for grilling meat, but the frying pan was nowhere to be seen. The hygiene of the pot wasn't very good either. There were insect corpses and some kind of unknown residue on it.

"Terius. When was the last time you used this pot?"

"Uh.... It's been there since before I got this house... I don't know either."

"......."

Nephia used the collected water generously to wash the pot. Since there was no detergent, she put strength into the cloth she was holding and scrubbed it hard. It became somewhat clean, but it couldn't be helped that it was still dirty.

,There's no cooking oil or anything....'

There was no food in this house at all. This was because Terius had never cooked for himself until now. He mostly bought food or used the restaurant in the Prucus family. When he was a soldier, there was food in the barracks.

Nephia, who was about to put the pot on the stove, belatedly remembered the gifts she had received from the maid before leaving the mansion.

,Salt and pepper! The condition of the meat isn't very good anyway, so it won't taste good if I grill it as it is.'

She sprinkled a lot of salt and pepper from the backpack onto the lump of meat.

"N-Nephia. Is it okay to sprinkle that much salt and pepper?!"

Terius was flustered. Salt and pepper were very expensive items. After the Korea Merchant Group appeared, commoners were able to purchase good quality salt and pepper at relatively low prices, but the perception that they were still precious remained, so it was common to use them sparingly.

"......It's okay. We have to eat deliciously when we eat."

She would be leaving this place in a week anyway. Nephia swallowed the rest of her words. It was hard to say because she could easily imagine Terius's reaction of despair.

Terius stared blankly at the salt and pepper being sprinkled generously.

Nephia put the meat in the pot and grilled it. It was a little burnt, but it was grilled fairly well. There were no plates, so they had to eat the steak as it was in the pot.

"It's delicious! It's so delicious!"

Terius ate the steak while constantly exclaiming. He was grateful just to be able to eat meat like this.

"I'm glad it was grilled well. It's delicious."

Nephia gave a bitter smile. Objectively, the steak was delicious. There was no way it couldn't be delicious since she had thrown in a ton of salt and pepper. However, compared to the steak she had eaten at Yujin-ssi's mansion, it was not nearly as good. The meat was tough and there was hardly any juice. The quality of the meat itself was the worst.

Did he deliberately buy this cheap meat? Or was he scammed? Either way, Terius, who couldn't even buy a decent piece of meat, seemed incompetent.

After the meal, they sat at the table and talked. Terius led the conversation. What life was like for the soldiers, what today's training was like.... They were stories that were beyond boring and annoying.

Before preparing for bed, Terius called Nephia with a serious face.

"...Nephia."

"Huh?"

"Our relationship... is it a married couple?"

"What?"

Nephia's eyes widened. What nonsense had she just heard?

Terius, flustered by her reaction, which was different from what he had expected, quickly continued.

"We're going to live together from now on, so it's right to be a married couple. Everyone else will think so too. We didn't have a formal wedding ceremony... but let's have a grand wedding when the situation gets better. I promise."

Terius reached out his left hand. Nephia frowned and pushed away his only hand.

"Terius. We're not a married couple. Please don't do this."

"Why, why are you doing this? You agreed to marry me."

"......Did I say that? Don't make things up."

"No. You definitely did. Don't you remember? We sat by the lake a long time ago and promised to get married when we grew up...!"

"I'm sorry, but even if you say that, I don't remember. And what time are you talking about? I think you're a little weird."

"......."

Terius lowered his head. He could see the iron ring on his left ring finger. At the end of his vision, Nephia's left ring finger had a diamond ring, not an iron ring. Terius bit his lip and jumped up from his seat.

"Nephia! I spent 1 billion to save you! How can you do this to me?!"

"I never asked you to save me! You came and took me from the mansion without permission! To this shabby place!"

"Nephia...!!"

Terius, who was about to shout, closed his mouth. This was because tears were welling up in Nephia's eyes. Terius's shoulders drooped powerlessly. He didn't bring Nephia here to fight with her, to do this.

"......Nephia. I was too emotional. I'm sorry. Let's... just sleep for today. Do you want to use the bed?"

"...It's okay. You use the bed. I'll sleep in the chair."

"...Okay."

Terius trudged over and lay down on the bed. His head was complicated.

,Nephia hasn't adapted to freedom yet. She'll realize that the free life she has now is much better than the life of a slave after a few days.'

A free life where there is no master to serve and she can choose as she pleases. Nephia will definitely understand.

•••

The next day. Nephia couldn't even eat breakfast. The lives of commoners were not three meals a day, but one meal a day. Eating only two meals a day was a good life.

In the end, they decided to eat only dinner together.

Nephia, who used to eat three meals and snacks at the mansion, grabbed her hungry stomach and headed to the well. Time didn't pass well just staying at home like a glutton.

Nephia was surprised when she arrived at the well. Women were lined up in front of the well. There were at least 50 people in front of Nephia. Everyone was holding a large bucket in their hands.

,In the mansion, water comes out when you turn on the tap....'

Nephia stood in line at the very back. The woman in front of her stared at Nephia intently. The clothes she was wearing were dirty. There were traces of tears and mending in various places on her clothes.

"Oh my. Hello? Are you that one-armed... no, the wife of the one-armed knight-nim? You're incredibly beautiful as the rumors say. I'm Talia. What's your name?"

"...I'm Nephia. I'm not, Terius and I are not a married couple."

"You're not a married couple? But why are you living together?"

"...That's... there's a reason."

"Is that so? I don't know what the reason is... but you look depressed, so it doesn't seem like a good reason. Well, there's no way you'd feel good living with a one-armed knight."

The woman's tone was quite rough. It was also rude to say that to Terius, who was a knight. Nephia wasn't even angry. What did she care if Terius was being cursed or ridiculed? Rather, she sympathized with the woman's words and complained about her situation.

"That's right. Do you know what's in the house? There's nothing. There's nothing to eat.... There's only one pot.... Haa."

"It seems like you're going through a lot. Cheer up. Good days will come. Oh, but is that rumor true?"

"Rumor?"

"I'm talking about the rumor that the one-armed knight has a debt."

"...Could you tell me more about it?"

"I've only heard it second-hand, but I heard he has a huge debt of 200 million Ner? If my husband had such a debt... oh my. I'd just run away. How could I live with such a man?"

"......."

Nephia listened to her words silently with a stiff face.

•••

Terius, who was returning home, stopped. This was because four mercenaries wearing weapons were approaching on the street with a fierce atmosphere.

"Are you Knight Terius?"

"...I'm Terius. Who are you? I haven't seen you in the military."

"We're not from the military. We came from the Korea Merchant Group."

"......From the Korea Merchant Group? Why?"

"You know why, don't you?"

There was only one reason for the Korea Merchant Group to send people.

Money.

"...It hasn't even been a week since I borrowed the money."

"We know. We're just observers. Observers. Don't even think about running away. And we'll come find you every time you forget about your debt... got it? Hurry up and think about paying off your debt."

"......."

Terius's head dropped. This was a warning. An annual interest rate of 50% on 200 million. Just thinking about it made his head throb.

,I have to pay it off quickly. Interest is accruing even at this moment. After a year, the interest alone will be 100 million.'

He wished a war would break out. That way, he would have a chance to build achievements and earn money. But right now, Kyle didn't seem to have any intention of starting a war right away.

Terius clenched his fist. One method came to mind. It was an unsightly method. But... it was better than the debt increasing further.

He braced himself and returned home.

Nephia had finished cleaning up somehow and was preparing dinner. Four steamed potatoes. That was all for dinner tonight. Nephia thought it was really fortunate that she had salt.

"Nephia."

"Huh? Why do you have such a serious expression?"

"Nephia. Please don't misunderstand and listen. I have a debt of 200 million. It's money I borrowed from the Korea Merchant Group... the interest rate is 50% per year."

"......."

Nephia didn't answer. It was quiet. Terius was watching her closely. Her expressionless face felt like pressure.

"There was no other way to collect the 1 billion Ner to buy your freedom other than taking out a loan."

"...So?"

"The debt of 200 million is too much pressure. Next year, the debt will increase to 300 million. I want to get rid of the debt as soon as possible. ...Can't we sell the dress and the dwarf dagger you have?"

"Are you crazy? The dress and dagger are my treasures! How can you even think of that?!"

"We're going to live together from now on! We have to think about the future! The debt will be 300 million next year! I, I'll succeed and pay it back! I'll get you a better and more splendid dress and a better sword! Or let's dispose of that diamond ring! If we sell it, we can get at least 100 million Ner! For now, let's get rid of the debt first. Huh?"

Spat! (Ttsaak!)

Nephia's hand swung. Terius couldn't avoid it and was hit on the cheek as it was. His head turned to the side and blood flowed down his nose. Terius, holding his tingling cheek, looked at her with disbelief.

"...Nephia?"

"Why do you keep thinking I'm going to live with you? The dress and dagger are clearly my things. Of course, this diamond ring is mine too. Don't even think about selling other people's property without permission."

Terius clenched his fist tightly. His fist was trembling.

"......Aren't you thinking about our future? Are those things really necessary right now?"

"Why do you keep saying 'we'? I can leave this house right now. Who am I putting up with because of.... Haa. Forget it."

Nephia held her luggage backpack in her hand. It was the luggage backpack that she hadn't unpacked yet because the closet was dirty. Terius was horrified. He thought that Nephia could really leave. He immediately knelt on the floor.

"I'm sorry, I'm sorry Nephia. I must have been crazy for a moment. I'll take care of the debt myself! Please forgive me just once. Please...!"

Nephia hesitated. Seong Yujin-ssi's words to live for only a week came to mind. In the end, she sighed and put down her luggage backpack.

"...I'm only letting you off this time."

"Thank you! Thank you for believing in me, Nephia!"
