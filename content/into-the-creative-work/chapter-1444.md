First, I checked the kitchen.

The refrigerator was as it was, and the fryer was as it was. The CCTV was broken.

,I guess there's nothing to gain from the kitchen. We've used up all the chicken, and there are only ready-made sauces. If they were the type to research sauces, they wouldn't have done this.'

They must have been trying to guess the recipe from the ingredients I use. But all I do in this kitchen is fry chicken. The chicken is brined and the sauce is made entirely at the factory.

,...The bastards must know about the factory. So, what they'll target next is...'

The factory.

I sighed and prepared to open the store. The T district where my factory is located is an industrial zone. The district itself had security. Unless they're complete morons, there's no way they'd attack in broad daylight. So, I had until night.

,I need to run the store first.'

I glanced outside the kitchen.

Customers were already lined up in front of the store, waiting to taste Korean chicken.

•••

8 PM.

I left work early and headed to the factory in the T district instead of going home.

The factory was dark, and no one was there. The employees leave around 5 PM.

,There's no need to run the factory at full capacity right now.'

If the number of franchise stores increases, the factory might have to run 24/7. When that happens, I'll have to hire more employees.

,I should look into other factories too.'

At that time, it probably won't be a chicken factory. Even if it's Yuyu Chicken, I don't plan to just keep selling chicken. There's a clear limit to chicken.

,I'm going to expand the field, focusing on chicken.'

I was thinking of selling other foods.

,As long as people exist, food can be sold forever. Because there's no one who doesn't eat food.'

I went into the office inside the empty factory and sat down in a chair to wait. I deliberately didn't turn on the lights.

An hour passed.

,Did I come too early? Come to think of it, it would be easier for them to move around midnight.'

Even after midnight passed and it became 1 AM, there were no intruders.

,They're not coming even though it's past midnight? They're very cautious bastards.'

2 AM.

I started to think they might not come.

,There was no definite proof that they were coming from the start. I just assumed it... This is anticlimactic. I have to go to the store during the day... Should I just go back and sleep now?'

It was the moment I stood up. The pocket watch-type watch I had in my pocket vibrated. I got a call from Yuria.

"Yuria. I'll be right back."

-Yes. Master-nim. I'll prepare everything so you can wash up as soon as you arrive. And... I've captured the uninvited guests.

•••

A house without Yoojin.

Yuria quietly continued her studies, waiting for Yoojin to return.

Her method of studying was reading and watching. This world was more technologically advanced than Yoojin's world. The same was true for the internet, so she could find out most things online.

She began to acquire knowledge of this world. Just skimming through it was enough. She wasn't quite capable of perfect recall... but she was the type who didn't forget most things after seeing them once. First, she would acquire knowledge, and then she would use it.

What she looked at carefully was magic. The magic of this world and the magic of her world had different systems. They were so different that she even felt a sense of novelty. It was a fresh stimulus for her, who had reached the level of Grand Archmage.

,Basic knowledge of magic can be viewed by paying... but there's no high-level knowledge, after all.'

She quickly shook off her feelings of disappointment. It would be even stranger if she could obtain high-level magic knowledge online. Magic knowledge itself was a treasure that couldn't be exchanged for anything.

,It's a shame that I can't use magic in this world.'

Inability to use magic. It was one of the penalties she had in this world.

At first, she didn't think much of it. But it didn't take long for her to change her mind.

,It was inconvenient.'

It was a matter of efficiency and convenience. Things that could be quickly handled with magic had to be handled by moving her body directly. It was an inconvenience, and an inefficiency, like losing an arm.

So, Yuria thought.

,If I can't use magic...'

She could use similar powers that weren't magic. Fortunately, there were quite a few such powers in this world. A prime example was psychic abilities.

Most psychic abilities are known to be awakened innately, and it's almost impossible to develop them later. But for some reason, she felt like it was possible.

,Master-nim could use lightning even without magic. And he would be able to use lightning even without the Thunder Sky Style martial art...'

She had been watching Yoojin from his side for a long time. In her view, the way Yoojin used lightning was different from magic and martial arts.

Power manifested by will.

It was close to the divine power used by the demons of the [Baekhwan] world. Of course, it wasn't exactly the same.

,Master-nim's complete recovery is on a different level, so it's impossible to imitate...'

Wouldn't it be possible to imitate Yoojin's lightning to some extent? This was a thought that was possible because she had entered the realm of transcendence.

"......."

She got up from her seat.

Yuria didn't just gain penalties by entering this world.

Instead of being able to use magic, she gained a sharp sense. Her already excellent senses surpassed their limits and created a new sense. It's usually called the sixth sense, but Yuria just called it intuition.

Because it was such an unfamiliar feeling, even she couldn't handle it well yet. That intuition was telling her. That enemies were approaching.

,How dare they come here...'

She controlled her soaring anger with her cool reason. Her genius mind instantly calculated the situation and guessed the enemies' purpose.

The enemies were probably after the chicken recipe. Yoojin expected the enemies to target the factory, but they targeted Yoojin's house. Did they think the recipe was at home? No. The enemies were trying to kidnap Yoojin and find out the recipe. That was more certain and faster. It was an idea they could have because they didn't know Yoojin was a 5th-grade wizard.

,I can't forgive them any more.'

She wouldn't kill them.

There were many things she needed to find out. And death was too comfortable of a treatment.

She checked the daggers equipped on her thigh and went outside. She didn't want to let them into the house where Yoojin and she stayed.

When she came out of the house, three men came. They were wearing masks and holding guns and knives. They were nervous when they saw Yuria. There was only one reason.

"A maid...."

"Damn it. I didn't know he hired a maid."

"He made a lot of money with chicken... I guess it's true."

In Neo London, the status of butlers and maids is enormous. Combat, office work. Butlers and maids were beings who were not inferior in anything.

"What should we do?"

"Attack? There's a chance since there are three of us, right? It's not an area where the upper class lives, and the skills of a maid hired in the S district probably aren't anything special."

"No, we retreat. Even if she was hired by a resident of the S district, a maid is a maid. Besides, I can't feel anything from that maid. I even doubt if she's a living human."

There was no presence even though she was right in front of them.

If she hadn't been standing right in front of the house, they wouldn't have even noticed her existence.

They slowly backed away from the silver-haired maid.

"That's absurd. You come here as you please at this late hour, and now you're going to leave as you please? Even if you're uninvited guests, you're still guests. As a maid, I'll attend to you."

"Miss, we have urgent business...!!"

The man threw a smoke bomb he took out of his waist on the floor. Smoke rose instantly, obscuring all directions.

"Scatter!"

The men all ran away in different directions.

Yuria closed her eyes. She didn't listen to the sound either. She ignored the subtle vibrations felt through her skin. Relying on the new sense she gained in this world, her intuition, she threw the daggers equipped on her thigh.

Thwack! Thud! Thwack!

"Argh!"

"Keugh!"

"Ugh...."

The daggers flew and pierced their bodies. They were accurately stuck in one part of the spine that she was aiming for. The nerves must have been damaged, so they wouldn't be able to move for a while. But Yuria frowned when she saw the dagger she threw to the right. There was a mistake. The dagger thrown to the right didn't stick in the desired area. Thanks to that, he didn't fall to the ground and counterattacked.

Bang, bang, bang!

Bullets flew from the smoke. They were aiming accurately for Yuria's head and heart. The opponent also had unusual skills.

,I can feel the direction of the bullets.'

She lightly moved one step to the side. She felt it even before the opponent pulled the trigger. Even if her senses had become more sensitive, was this possible? Even Yuria herself was dumbfounded.

,...It might be related to the power of time I understood while fighting the Chairman of Pantheon last time.'

She opened her eyes. She couldn't see anything because of the thick smoke. She walked forward.

Bang, bang, bang, bang, bang!

Bullets poured down. But the bullets didn't even graze Yuria's clothes. She moved one step ahead of the bullets with a leisurely pace.

Click, click.

The enemy threw the pistol with the empty magazine at Yuria. Of course, even that didn't touch Yuria's body. The smoke cleared. Yuria could see the opponent sweating profusely. He pulled out a knife from his waist.

"You avoided bullets in the smoke? You're an absurd monster. Even warriors above 4th grade can't avoid bullets that can't be seen... Why is someone with your skills in the S district...!"

"Because I'm a maid. I'm where Master-nim is."

"You have a very thorough work ethic!"

The man charged with a knife. It was a reasonably fast movement. But Yuria could see everything.

Yuria raised her dagger and blocked the attack just before the blade reached her neck. She narrowed her brows as if she didn't like something.

,My intuition isn't working. This is troublesome.'

Of course, that didn't mean she couldn't deal with the enemy in front of her. She kicked the enemy's calf with her booted foot. She went behind him, who had lost his balance for a moment, and stabbed a dagger into his spine. He couldn't move and fell to the ground.

"You've become disabled, but modern medicine is excellent, so there won't be any major problems if you get treatment. If you want, you can replace it with a mechanical spinal cord."

"......You crazy bitch. Did you aim for the spinal cord with the dagger and cut off the nerves in the meantime...? What the hell are you really?"

"It's late at night. Please be quiet so you don't bother the neighbors."

Yuria grabbed their arms, dragged them, and went into the basement.
