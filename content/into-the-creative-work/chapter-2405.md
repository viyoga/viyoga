Aeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeeng!

The siren wailed in the secret laboratory. It was the siren that had only sounded twice in the history of the Daemado Secret Laboratory.

Once to check the siren's function, once when a test subject escaped the lab, and now, for the third time, when enemies had invaded.

"......"

Yu Seo-jeong stared at the monitor screen. CCTV footage was being transmitted in real-time. Three intruders in combat suits were dashing down the lab hallway. Their destination was the No. 1 Control Laboratory on basement level 5, where she was.

'They really came.'

One man and two women. The white sword the man was carrying was famous for being used by Seong Yujin. Judging by the skill with which he was taking down elite soldiers in an instant, it was definitely him.

The light returned to her dead eyes. Finally, an opportunity had come to escape this wretched place with her daughter. She'd be doing similar things at Seongak Elementary School, but... at least it would be a much better environment than working like a slave here.

'Instead of this, I need to prepare to run away with Jihee. At this rate, they'll be here in five minutes.'

It was the moment she rose from her lab chair. The lab door opened, and a group entered. Five fully armed Awakened soldiers, Jin Seonghwa, the second daughter of the Ilwoo Group Chairman, who held the highest position in the secret laboratory, and her secretary. And in the hands of the soldiers was a woman. A woman in a white straitjacket, high on drugs. Yu Seo-jeong's daughter, Kim Jihee.

"...President Jin Seonghwa-ssi. What's the meaning of barging in here like this? And there. Don't treat Jihee carelessly."

Yu Seo-jeong asked, barely concealing her trembling voice. She mustn't show any signs of abnormality. She knew how ruthless they could be from experience. They could kill them on the spot.

"What do you mean, what's the meaning? Can't you tell by looking? Intruders. That crazy bastard who terrorized Busan has come all the way to Daemado."

Jin Seonghwa's voice was full of annoyance. She pulled out Yu Seo-jeong's chair, sat down, and crossed her legs.

"...So? The soldiers are guarding the lab, aren't they?"

"He didn't come alone. It's a mess outside too. Enemies are rampaging outside. Most of the soldiers have gone to subdue them. To think they'd invade knowing Daemado is Japanese territory... He's a real madman. How did I get mixed up with a guy like that?"

Jin Seonghwa clicked her tongue. Her eyes, watching the CCTV screen, were vicious.

"You didn't answer my question. Why did you bring Jihee to the lab?"

"You're smart, aren't you? Do I have to spell everything out for you? Ha. Forget it. It's only a loss for me to fight with you. This is the safest place in the underground lab. And you can control the lab from here, right?"

"Don't tell me... you mean to release all the test subjects in the lab?"

"That's right. You're smart after all. If you understand, then do it. Even if they're failures, they're still mutant-level monsters... they'll last for a few hours. Even if we can't contact the mainland due to the communication interference, the mainland will notice something's wrong in a few hours and dispatch troops. Oh, don't release the Variants. Those are precious."

"Those mutants can't distinguish between friend and foe. If you release them without a plan, all the soldiers and researchers in the lab will die."

"I don't care. It's a bit of a waste, but ordinary soldiers and researchers can be replaced. The real core that's important in this lab is you. Only you and I need to be safe. Oh, and your daughter too. That way, you'll listen to me. Hehehe."

"......"

Yu Seo-jeong sighed inwardly. How did things come to this?

She had simply played the game [Eternal Eden] with her daughter, and then they had fallen into the world of the game. The bewilderment at that time was enough to make her collapse on the street, but there was no time to leisurely do so.

Because if this was the world of the game, the world would be destroyed.

To survive with her daughter, she needed the power of a large corporation. So, she chose a conglomerate. Among them, she chose the Ilwoo Group. There was no big reason. The most famous conglomerate in Busan, where they had appeared, was the Ilwoo Group.

At first, she had been treated like an executive. They already knew about the existence of players and were preparing for the apocalypse.

Yu Seo-jeong and Kim Jihee were assigned to the Daemado Secret Laboratory. That's when their treatment changed. Their every move was monitored, and they were urged to show results. It was impossible to escape, so they had no choice but to comply with their demands.

She still couldn't forget the first human experiment she had conducted. It sometimes appeared in her dreams. Those were the days when her bed was soaked with sweat.

Kim Jihee, unable to endure the hellish life, attempted to escape. She gained the loyalty of soldiers and test subjects, Awakened them, and plotted an escape.

The soldiers wanted to become Awakened, and in return, they were loyal to Kim Jihee. The test subjects simply wanted to get out of this lab.

It seemed to be going well. Until one of the soldiers betrayed them. Kim Jihee executed the escape before the plan was ready. And the soldiers and test subjects were killed by special forces from the Self-Defense Forces who came from the mainland, Japan.

From that day on, Kim Jihee wore a straitjacket and was confined to a room. She was constantly drugged under the guise of safe management. Kim Jihee had never been in her right mind since that day. Yu Seo-jeong had to work, even for her daughter's sake.

"What are you doing? Don't tell me you're rebelling after coming this far?"

Jin Seonghwa reached out her hand. Her secretary carefully handed her a pistol. Jin Seonghwa aimed the pistol at Kim Jihee.

"...I'll do it. So, put that gun away."

"You do it first."

Yu Seo-jeong pressed the buttons on one side. The mutant labs on the 4th and 5th floors opened, and the mutants poured out.

"Is that enough?"

Jin Seonghwa lowered her pistol.

"You should have done that from the start. You're our family's slave. A slave that can be replaced at any time. Don't forget that fact."

"......"

She had never forgotten it, not even once.
