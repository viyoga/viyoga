,The current me... is a Cheonma beyond Cheonma. A Super Cheonma.,

Guishura Guijeo hesitated. Not because of the incomprehensible word, "Super Cheonma?"

What the human in front of him used was a kind of magic. A magic that temporarily grants power at the cost of destroying one's own soul.

There were two things Guishura couldn't understand.

The first was the hatred that led to soul destruction. He couldn't understand what he had done to warrant choosing the annihilation of his soul. The Asura clan was a race obsessed with battle, but they didn't engage in combat to the point of grinding their souls. They needed to be able to fight again next time. But the guy in front of him was different.

The second was the power. No matter how much one destroyed their soul, it was strange to possess such overwhelming power.

Even if you destroyed and burned your soul, it was still just a human soul in the end. It was absurd that a mere human soul had that much value.

,The value of a soul's rank is the value of karma. To exert that much power, one must accumulate an enormous amount of karma.,

Karma isn't simply accumulated by killing people, and a soul's rank doesn't rise just by killing people. It's the life one has lived, and the influence one has on others. That is karma, and that raises the soul's rank.

Humans had limits. Only an emperor could raise their soul's rank to this extent. But before him was a human comparable to an emperor.

How could he possess this level of rank without being an emperor? He couldn't understand the reason. The unknown was unsettling. Guishura was slightly afraid.

,He's coming...!

Guishura could see a short future through his third eye. Only about 1-2 seconds. More than 5 seconds when his condition was good.

Now, he saw a future three seconds ahead. His own future, beaten to a pulp by Seong Yujin.

He gritted his teeth and dodged the fist flying towards his head. The fist passed Guishura's temple. The gust of wind from the punch blew away the small hill behind Guishura.

,I saw it!

The guy's fist striking his abdomen.

Guishura focused on evasion. Judging by the guy's power, taking even one hit would be fatal. The power from soul destruction was temporary. It wouldn't last long. He just needed to hold out until his soul was completely burned. Fortunately, his third eye was specialized in dodging attacks. How could he not dodge when he knew exactly where it was coming from?

Peo-eok!

He couldn't dodge it. Seong Yujin's fist slammed into Guishura's abdomen. It was too fast to dodge even though he knew it was coming. The difference between the present and the future was too short. He needed at least 2 seconds into the future to completely avoid Seong Yujin's attacks.

,Damn it...!

Guishura's body flew into the sky. Not the ground, but the sky. That was actually good. He would escape like this. Until the guy completely exhausted his power.

Seong Yujin on the ground slowly watched Guishura rise above the clouds, tilting his head. It was like hitting a home run and standing still to watch the trajectory.

Just as Guishura was about to disappear into the clouds, Seong Yujin moved. The moment he took a step, his body vanished. Immediately after, he appeared behind Guishura and slammed Guishura's head as if swinging a hammer.

Kwaaaaaang!

The shockwave instantly scattered the surrounding clouds in all directions, and Guishura fell to the ground like a meteor.

Meteor Sword.

A giant sword over 300m long fell from the sky towards Guishura.

Guishura, who knew through his third eye, grabbed four swords in each of his hands and deflected the Meteor Sword. The Meteor Sword bounced off and crashed into a mountain.

Guishura's body trembled. His muscles were screaming.

-Guijeo!

Cheon Yu-un's voice, who should have lost consciousness, echoed in Guishura's head. It was proof that Guishura had weakened.

-That's the Pateonhwang of the Cheonma Shingong. At most, it's limited to 3 minutes!

,Shut up. It's noisy, so shut your mouth. Do you think it's easy to endure against that monster? And what do you mean by 3 minutes.,

-If you had killed that bastard in one go, this wouldn't have happened...!

Cheon Yu-un's words were correct. He had dragged out the fight to play with the guy, and this was the result.

,...Wait. Pateonhwang. Can you use that too?,

-I can't. Do you think that's a simple technique? I can't guarantee you can use it even if you reach the realm of Three Jeong. It's strange that that guy who used Pateonhwang is so powerful. But you might be able to use it. You can see some of my memories now.

,Are you telling me to sacrifice myself? Do you think I'd do such a crazy thing.,

-Isn't there no other way anyway? Besides, I heard that Myeong-in don't reincarnate when they die. Isn't death and soul annihilation the same for you?

,You idiot! They're not the same! When a Myeong-in dies, their soul doesn't reincarnate, but becomes one with the Underworld and finds true rest! Pateonhwang? Soul annihilation is just a dog's death!,

-I heard that some races, including the Asura, are born in the Underworld. Isn't what you're saying just another reincarnation and order? A reincarnation unique to the Underworld. ...I see. The Underworld is....

Seong Yujin appeared right in front of him. He swung his sword. A crude strike filled with nothing but power, without any technique. He somehow dodged it thanks to his future sight.

-I'm going to die. Even the battle-crazed Asura clan is helpless before overwhelming power.

Cheon Yu-un sneered. He was deliberately provoking him. His intention was clear. He wanted to make Guishura use Pateonhwang.

,Heh heh. It's an Asura's glory to die in battle. You'll fall to the Underworld when you die. Humans become ugly in hell. They want to avoid pain at all costs. I wonder how ugly you'll become.,

-.......

,Well, I haven't given up yet. That guy is drunk on power right now. He's just wielding that powerful force mindlessly. I'll defeat him with technique.,

He said leisurely in his mind, but the situation was not easy. Seong Yujin continued to attack. He beat him with fists and legs. He occasionally swung his sword. Even with future sight, it was impossible to completely block his speed and power.

Swish.

Two left arms were cut off, and blood poured out. Guishura's concentration increased further.

,He's deliberately not killing me. He's playing with me. Just like I did.,

The guy stopped. He looked at Guishura as if admiring a work he had created.

,That arrogance will be the death of you.,

Guigeomnyeong (Ghost Sword Spirit).

Guishura released the ghosts within him. The ghosts tainted the surroundings with ghostly energy and rushed back at him. Guishura entrusted his body to the ghosts. He became the sword of ghosts. The Guigeomnyeong was perfect. He couldn't avoid whatever the guy did. The future was set.

It was the moment Guishura, who had become a sword, was about to fly towards the guy. His instincts warned him of death.

,......!

Instincts warning of death? That was absurd. Who was he? An Asura, one of the top races in the Underworld! How could he experience instinctive death at the hands of a human!

Pat.

His third eye activated, showing him the future one second ahead. A future where he died.

The existence that instilled the fear of instinctive death in anyone flashed through Guishura's mind.

,...Cheonsalseong (Heavenly Killing Star).,

Seong Yujin swung his sword down. The Guigeomnyeong couldn't withstand that overwhelming power and was destroyed. An irresistible death covered Guishura. All 244 ghosts within him met their eternal death and were erased. They had truly vanished from reincarnation.

,Damn it....,

And Guishura also met his true death.

When Guishura died, his body returned to the original Cheon Yu-un.

Cheon Yu-un sat down on the ground, trembling, and looked up at Seong Yujin.

,Heavenly Killing Star...! Why is a guy with the power of a star doing this kind of thing? You could have risen to a higher place without becoming a Cheonma!,

,Because I am the Cheonma.,

Swish!

Cheon Yu-un's neck was cut off.

Cheon Yu-un's soul would not go to the Underworld. He had also met a complete death by the power of the Heavenly Killing Star.

Seong Yujin stood still. He felt someone's gaze from the Cheonma Shin-gyo side. He wanted to shout at them to lower their eyes.

,This is the limit.,

The Demonic energy from Pateonhwang was dissipating. His existence was disappearing. Would Complete Recovery work? He was a little nervous. If Complete Recovery didn't work, he would die. No, if his soul was completely annihilated, wouldn't he be unable to use the Game Life app?

,...It's a Game Life app that even turns back time. Surely it can restore a single annihilated soul.,

Seong Yujin sent all the remaining Demonic energy into the sky. It was useless anyway since it would disappear after a while. The Demonic energy became a black dragon and rose into the sky, temporarily darkening the surroundings before disappearing. Seong Yujin, who had used all his Demonic energy, collapsed to the ground.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

[Using Complete Recovery.]

His pure white hair turned black again. Seong Yujin slowly raised his body. A smile appeared on his lips. The annihilated soul had returned perfectly thanks to Complete Recovery. His condition was at its best.

,As expected, Complete Recovery. You never betray me.,

Seong Yujin's steps towards the Cheonma Shin-gyo were more confident than ever.

,Little Cheonma Seong Yujin, huh? Not a bad ring to it.,
