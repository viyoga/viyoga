,Help? Are you going to help me escape?"

Park Jung-gu only seemed to think about running away. I clicked my tongue briefly.

,Are you just going to keep running away?"

,The opponent is Kamaise. One of the top black guilds in Japan. A black guild with at least 10 Class A criminals. There's no choice but to run away."

,If you run away this time, you'll go into hiding again for a while, right?"

,I have to hide. They're openly targeting me, what else can I do? Money is money, but my life is more important. Let's grab what we can and run. I have a few escape routes, so just help me pack things in that magic bag."

,If you go into hiding, I'll be in trouble."

Getting a large quantity of home appliances, food, firearms, ammunition, etc. in broad daylight will be too noticeable. If they start tracking me through taxes or something, things will get annoying.

,Did you say Kamaise? We can just get rid of them here without having to go far."

,I know you're confident in combat... but the opponent is a black guild. Running away is the best option here."

Black guild. A criminal guild composed of Awakened criminals. If a Hunter commits a crime and becomes wanted, they have a high chance of joining a black guild. Conversely, there are quite a few Hunters who aim for a black guild from the start. Hunters have quite a few restrictions in society. They receive aggravated punishment even for committing the same crimes as ordinary people.

,It's already too late to run away."

,It's not too late yet. You said there are two Class A guys? I can blind those two with my artifact and run away."

Park Jung-gu confidently held up a pen and said. Since I didn't have the Eye of Absolute Truth, I couldn't know the name or effect of the artifact. Judging by the feeling, it didn't seem like it would be of much help.

,They're coming."

,Huh?"

Kwaaaang!

One wall collapsed and a werewolf appeared. Behind him was a woman holding a rifle. The woman, whose face was half melted by burns, was squinting with all her might as if she hated the whole world.

,This werewolf has a transformation-type ability."

The gray-haired werewolf grabbed the debris of the broken wall and looked back and forth between me and Park Jung-gu.

,We're from Kamaise. You know why we're here without me having to say it, right? If you killed our comrade, you have to pay the price. But the boss is quite generous. He told us to bring you guys in peacefully. So, let's go quietly, huh?"

,Your Japanese is quite fluent."

The werewolf looked at me. He let out a scoff as if he was dumbfounded.

,I'm Japanese, so of course I speak Japanese well. Are you an idiot?"

,How am I supposed to know if you're Japanese or not when you look like an animal?"

,...Seriously. This bastard. You've been getting on my nerves since earlier, you know? Should I tear off one of your arms?"

The werewolf swings his claws. There was no need to use Instantaneous Movement. I dodge slightly to the side. The werewolf's eyes widened slightly.

,Ha. This bastard. So you were acting up because you had something to believe in. Looks like we're not going back quietly."

The werewolf swings his claws again. Sword energy flickers on his claws. The speed was also faster than before. Of course, it still looked slow to me.

I dodge the attack with minimal movement and throw an uppercut. My fist shattered the werewolf's jaw with pure strength alone. Broken teeth and blood fell to the floor in a shower.

,The gap between Class A and Class S. Most Class A Hunters can't land a single effective hit on a Class S Hunter. This guy is roughly below average even among Class A Hunters."

The werewolf has a full-body transformation-type ability. Transformation-type abilities generally have a high low-end but a low high-end. Of course, there are exceptions... but this guy doesn't seem to be one of them.

Still, the werewolf had fighting spirit. He didn't fall and kept swinging his hands wildly. The trajectories of his claws cut through the air. I dodged everything with minimal movement and placed my right hand on the werewolf's abdomen.

,Zeunryeong."

At the same time as summoning Zeunryeong in the air, I use my ability to control the wind.

Kwaaaang!

A shockwave occurred and the werewolf flew backward, falling onto the street.

,I was going to blow up his upper body... but he's an animal, so his durability is good."

I put Zeunryeong, which was floating in the air with the power of the wind, into my pocket and went outside through the broken wall.

The werewolf's comrade, the woman, aimed the rifle at me and pulled the trigger without hesitation. Bullets poured out.

I created a wind barrier. It blocked the bullets.

,I could reflect the bullets back by using lightning... but I can't give Park Jung-gu information about my true identity."

Park Jung-gu, being a black market dealer, might be able to figure out my identity with just a little bit of information. It's never bad to be careful. And even if I don't use lightning, wind alone is enough.

When all the bullets were blocked by the wind barrier, the woman's face contorted even more. She kept pulling the trigger. Mana-coated bullets were fired. They flew straight ahead, then suddenly changed course and aimed for the back of my head.

It's useless. The wind barrier blocks everything in a 360-degree circle around me. The back is no exception.

The bullet that was aiming for the back of my head was also blocked. But unlike the other bullets, it didn't fall to the ground. The stopped bullet suddenly started spinning again and began to slowly pierce the wind barrier.

,I thought it was an ability to change the trajectory of the bullets as desired... but is it an ability to control the bullets themselves?"

I glanced at the woman. She wasn't very pretty, and she seemed to have a bad temper. I looked at the woman and flicked my fingers.

Ten invisible wind blades created in the air flew towards the woman. The woman felt the mana in the wind blades and immediately moved her body to avoid them. Sweat dripped down her face as she rolled on the ground to avoid the wind blades.

,This time, 30."

Thirty wind blades poured out almost simultaneously. Instead of dodging, the woman stared blankly at the sky and muttered.

,Seriously. It's so unfair."

With a last will filled with resignation rather than anger, the woman was sliced into pieces of meat.

,Himiko!!!"

The werewolf charged in, enraged. Claws condensed with mana tore through the wind barrier and approached me. I tilted my head to dodge and kicked the werewolf's knee.

,Euaaaaaaaa!!"

The werewolf swung his arms while sitting on the ground. I cut off both of the werewolf's arms with two wind blades.

,Damn it!! Why is a Class S Hunter here!!"

,You guys attacked first."

I infused mana into my fist and swung it.

Thud!

The werewolf's head exploded.

Park Jung-gu, who had been watching from behind, carefully approached me.

,This is... I knew you were strong, but I didn't know you were a Class S Hunter, called a walking natural disaster. No wonder you looked down on Kamaise."

Park Jung-gu looked at me cautiously.

,I haven't been officially recognized as Class S."

,I guess so. There's no way an officially recognized Class S Hunter-nim would be looking for me. I'm quite curious about your circumstances. Do you want to tell me?"

,No."

,Okay. I give up."

,Do you know where Kamaise's headquarters is?"

,Are you going to wipe them all out this time?"

,Wouldn't that be better for you?"

,I'd be indebted to you. I don't like being indebted."

,Their real target isn't you, it's me. More precisely, they seem to be after the recipe for Nectar."

,Nectar makes money. The ingredients that are visible on the outside are just normal water, and it's an amazing drug with no side effects."

,It can be addictive."

,That's just a mental problem, not a physical one. Like computer addiction or sugar addiction. If you produce and distribute this Nectar directly, it's easy to sit on a pile of money. In that sense, can't you increase the distribution volume a bit?"

,I can't. It's not something that can be made easily."

I said firmly.

The Great Medicine is something that comes out through gacha. It's something that has no effect on me, who has an Absolute Spirit. It only costs 1 point, but it's a waste to use points to buy it.

,So, where is Kamaise's headquarters?"

,I don't know either. Don't look at me like that. There's no way a black guild would operate openly. Moreover, Kamaise is one of the top black guilds in Japan. I need time to find out."

Park Jung-gu picked up his cell phone. It wasn't a smartphone, but a flip phone. It was obviously a burner phone.

,Who are you calling?"

,A Kamaise customer who I mainly deal with. He's a Kamaise executive. I'm going to try to coax him into revealing the headquarters. It would have been easier if you hadn't killed those guys and captured them alive."

,I thought you knew Kamaise's headquarters."

Park Jung-gu shrugged his shoulders and put the flip phone to his ear. He talks to the Kamaise executive. Surprisingly, he didn't raise his voice and the conversation was short.

,He said he's coming soon. He seems to know the details of this situation."

,He's coming even though he knows? Is he bringing all his subordinates? That would be convenient in its own way."

,Yeah. I'm counting on you, Class S. Honestly, if you're Class S, you can handle dozens of Class A guys, right?"

,Class A is Class A, but it depends. It might be a bit difficult if it's a Class A made up of decent guys."

,Hey. Don't talk weak. You're making me anxious."

Thirty minutes passed.

A black mid-sized car slid and stopped. A woman wearing a short kimono got out of the driver's seat.

Inside the sleeveless kimono, a chainmail armor could be seen. The skirt was short like a miniskirt. She wore leggings and high heels, and chainmail gauntlets on her wrists. She had a sword on her back, a pistol on her waist, and a dagger and shuriken on her thigh.

It was an outfit that seemed to be saying, ,I'm a female ninja." It was so blatant that it looked like cosplay.

Her hairstyle was a bob, and she was characterized by heavy eye makeup and red lipstick. Her breasts were full with a D cup. The line leading from her waist to her hips was artistic.

She also had tattoos. A black snakeskin was drawn winding from her left neck to her left wrist, and a skull tattoo broken in half was on her right thigh.

,She's a beauty that's a shame to kill."

Should I subdue her and kidnap her? Where should I take her?

While I was thinking, she strode towards us.

She didn't say anything even after seeing the corpses of the werewolf and the woman.

Park Jung-gu stepped forward and waved awkwardly.

,Yura-ssi. It's a shame that things turned out this way. Please keep in mind that Kamaise started it first."

Park Jung-gu winked at me. It meant to subdue her. I didn't move. It was because I didn't feel any killing intent or hostility from her.

She stopped at a reasonable distance and took something out of her chest inside her kimono and held it out.

It was an ID card with certification from the World Hunter Association. Park Jung-gu was shocked, and I looked at her with interest.

,Yura, a member of the 11th Special Forces of the World Hunter Association. I'm currently on an infiltration mission into the Kamaise Black Guild. I'll get straight to the point. I need your cooperation."

She nonchalantly took out a cigarette from her front and put it in her mouth.
