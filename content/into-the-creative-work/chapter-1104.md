Walking ahead, I raised my hand. The squad's movement halted.

I felt like I heard a strange sound.

I activated my mana rod and sent mana to my ears. My hearing is enhanced. It's not magic, just physical enhancement through simple mana. It's less effective than magic, but it has the advantage of being easy to use in a pinch.

Drip, drip.

It was the sound of water droplets. My face instantly crumpled at the realization.

"Damn it. It's raining."

"Raining? Really?"

"Ugh. What's with this all of a sudden."

"I never thought I'd be wearing a raincoat in a real combat situation."

"Haa."

Annoyed reactions burst out from here and there.

Among the training situations we've experienced, the most annoying is when something falls from the sky. The humidity makes our clothes damp, and if the uniform gets wet, it feels like a straitjacket. Our vision narrows, and the sounds become noisy. The raincoat is stuffy, and the ground is muddy. And that subtly rising, strange fishy smell.

"We should be grateful that it's not snowing or hailing, at least."

It's summer now, but on days when the Dark Moon rises, it wouldn't be strange for snow to fall in the middle of summer.

I put on my raincoat. The raincoat was thin, but as soon as I put it on, I felt like I couldn't breathe.

The feet that had stopped began to move again. Soon, heavy rain poured down.

"Be careful not to slip and move. Especially, take good care of your radios so they don't get wet. They shouldn't break easily, but just in case."

"Got it."

We walked silently and arrived at the mountainside. I assessed the number of personnel and decided to take a break. Originally, I was going to carry out the mission immediately without a break, but I changed my plan due to the heavy rain. A rainy mountain doubles the physical exertion. A break is necessary.

'Also, I'm the weakest in terms of stamina among the 1st squad.'

Leaning against a suitable tree, I opened the canteen I had brought and took a sip of water. I open Astral and expand my senses. I want to pinpoint the location of Instructor Venus, who might be chasing us.

'...I don't know. I don't even know which direction he's in, let alone his exact location. It's not just because of the rain. It's a more fundamental reason. Instructor Venus is much stronger than I am.'

"Number 211."

Someone called me. It was hard to distinguish the voice in the rain. I turned my gaze and it was Number 31. Before I could open my mouth, Number 31 raised his hand and pointed to the sky. Reflexively, my head turns upward.

About 30M away, a monster was flapping its wings in the sky. I narrowed my eyes and examined the monster's shape in more detail. Wings and appearance resembling a bat. And a body made of stone.

"...It's a Gargoyle, a Class 2 monster."

I said it out loud on purpose. The kids who were resting jumped up from their seats and picked up their rifles. Dealing with Gargoyles is not part of the mission. Avoiding them is the best option.

'...It found us and is getting closer. We can't avoid a fight.'

Information about Gargoyles in the 'Dark Moon' game comes to mind.

The characteristics of the Class 2 monster, Gargoyle, are that its skin is as hard as stone and that it flies in the sky.

'Bullets won't work. It must be even more so since it has become somewhat stronger due to the influence of the Dark Moon. The Gargoyle's attack methods are claws and teeth. Even if it flies in the sky, its only means of attack is close combat.'

The Gargoyle has no choice but to come down to attack us.

'There's no need to waste bullets. I'll aim for a counter when the Gargoyle comes down. Since it's raining, I can easily catch it by using ice magic.'

Blocking the Gargoyle's attack can be done with barrier magic. If I deploy a Class 2 barrier, it will easily block one attack from the Gargoyle.

I was about to open my mouth to explain a simple plan to the squad members to catch the Gargoyle. I felt the flow of mana nearby. My reaction was delayed because I was thinking about how to deal with the Gargoyle.

"Ice Arrow."

Along with Number 31's neat voice, the Class 2 magic, Ice Arrow, flew towards the Gargoyle. The ice stalactite, as thick as my forearm, accurately hits the Gargoyle's body.

Jjeo-eong!

The Ice Arrow caused an explosion. Instead of heat, cold air covered the Gargoyle's body. The heavy rain, pouring down without a break, instantly froze due to the cold. The Gargoyle, its body hardened by the ice, could not fly and fell to the ground.

The Gargoyle's frozen body shattered. Unlike its skin made of stone, the inside was full of blood and flesh. After confirming the Gargoyle's death, I sighed.

"...Number 31."

"Why?"

"Why did you suddenly fire an Ice Arrow?"

"I judged that Ice Arrow was appropriate. Because it's raining. Fire Arrow is too noticeable."

"No, that's not it. I'm asking why you attacked suddenly. You're not the only one here right now."

"...That's right. I'll talk before attacking next time."

"Please do."

I ended it by asking again.

It's a relief that the monster that appeared was a Gargoyle. What if a monster stronger than a Gargoyle had appeared, and Number 31 had attacked recklessly? Just thinking about it was terrible.

We are moving as a squad now, so I hope individuals refrain from impulsive actions.

We left the Gargoyle's corpse behind and headed towards the mountaintop.

Walking ahead, I think about Number 31's magic.

'...Number 31's mana movement was a little different than usual. The casting speed was also much faster. Moreover, the power of the Ice Arrow was almost at Class 3.'

It's obvious that the cause is the black moon floating in the sky.

'Ordinary humans are not positively affected by the Dark Moon. At best, they just feel uplifted.'

That's also a rare case. When the Dark Moon rises, it's common to feel depressed and have a vague sense of fear.

'Number 31 is not a black magician, so... the reason he is affected by the Dark Moon is... a factor. He definitely has a special factor.'

After making the judgment, I clicked my tongue inwardly.

I was trying not to get involved with Number 31, but in my head, I was speculating about Number 31.

'I have to admit it. As a magician, Number 31 is an interesting human.'

There were many things to learn from Number 31. The way she handles mana, the way she creates formulas, and so on.

'The Ice Arrow just now was also interesting. Originally, arrow types are penetrating, but she changed part of the formula to explode the cold air.'

It was similar to the Class 3 magic, Ice Ball.

The other squad members who had not reached Class 2 just thought it was Number 31's magic and moved on, but I didn't.

My feet stopped.

I cleared my mind and focused on the front.

50M away, there was a wooden fence. It's a flimsy wooden fence that looks like it was built haphazardly. And inside, there's a roughly made wooden house.

It's a Goblin Village. They have intelligence similar to that of a child, so they build things like wooden fences and houses and settle down. The goblins who have survived for a long time also use simple strategies. They smear the poison of poisonous plants on wooden spears and swing them around.

I raised my hand and signaled to the squad members.

Everyone picked up their guns and activated their magic.

"Silence."

Class 1 non-attribute magic. It eliminated the surrounding sounds. It also eliminates the sound of footsteps and breathing. Class 1 level cannot eliminate loud sounds like gunshots, but the story is different if you have a gun equipped with a silencer.

The surroundings became quiet in an instant due to Silence.

'This is driving me crazy. Even the sound of the surrounding rain has disappeared.'

I swallowed a hollow laugh. The squad members cannot control magic as delicately as I do.

'Isn't this like telling the enemies that we're here?'

If there is a place where the sound of rain suddenly disappears when the sound of rain is heard from all directions, attention will surely be focused on it.

'Considering that the opponent is a goblin, it might not matter.'

First, I ordered the squad members to stand by and went on reconnaissance myself. I eliminated the sounds of my body except for the sound of rain and circled around the wooden fence. I checked the size of the village and the flimsy points of the wooden fence.

'About 70 of them, roughly. Goblins are fast breeders, so let's assume there are 100. The Hobgoblin is probably the leader.'

Shwaaaaaaa.

I heard a huge sound of water. I raised my head and looked at the back of the Goblin Village. There was a waterfall flowing that didn't suit the mountaintop. The direction of the waterfall was the opposite of where the 667 unit was.

I looked around for a moment.

'According to the game, it must be the Blena Mountains. It's a perfect place to conduct experiments that shouldn't be known if people don't come and go.'

The scenery was so outstanding that I couldn't help but admire it, even though it was hard to see at night.

I compared the terrain in the game with the real world and created a map in my head. This might be helpful later.

I went to where the squad members were waiting and sent a hand signal. The squad members guarded the surroundings and followed behind me.

I infiltrated through the flimsy part of the wooden fence that I had seen in advance.

It was successful. There was no commotion in the Goblin Village. We walked through the silence and darkness and began to kill the goblins.

When the trigger was pulled, the bullet flew silently and pierced the goblin's body. Goblins, who are weak even among Class 1 monsters, could not block or deflect bullets.

Even though we killed over 70 of them, the Goblin Village was quiet. The situation was going smoothly.

'All that's left is that building in the center.'

It was crude like other goblin buildings, but it was large and decorated with animal bones and bird feathers.

'I'll go in first.'

I sent a hand signal and went inside.

A goblin with red skin, not green skin, was lying down and sleeping in the center of the building. Hobgoblins are larger and have red skin than ordinary goblins.

'As expected, it's a Hobgoblin, but... it's too big.'

It's 3M tall and has a bulging belly. Its arms and legs are also muscular. It barely fills the house. If it were any bigger, the house would have collapsed.

'It mutated due to the influence of the Dark Moon.'

I quietly assessed the situation. The Giant Hobgoblin is fast asleep. Rainwater dripped down from the crude ceiling. Thanks to this, the Hobgoblin's body is also wet.

'It's best for me to handle it here. Since there's no sound due to Silence, and I can't be seen because of the building. If I hide the mana fluctuations as much as possible, even Number 31's senses won't be able to tell what magic I used.'

I open Astral and use 12 mana rods and the ability to generate electricity.

Blue electricity sparked over my right hand. The electricity increased more and more over time. After a few seconds, my hand was no longer visible due to the electricity.

Class 3 magic, Lightning Grab.

I grabbed the Hobgoblin's shoulder. The electricity gathered in my grip flowed through the Hobgoblin's body.

The Hobgoblin's eyes flashed open.

"Kkeueeeeeeeek!"

The Hobgoblin screamed strangely and raised its upper body. The building's ceiling collapses without lasting even a second.

I reflexively used an instant.

'...Shit. This is bad. Normally, it should have been electrocuted to death, let alone move.'

The reason was found in the game knowledge.

Monsters mutated through the Dark Moon become so-called elite monsters and gain at least 1 and up to 5 or more characteristics. In the game, it kindly tells you the characteristics above the monster's head, but this is reality. There's no such thing.

'Since Hobgoblins are low-level monsters, I thought they would only have one characteristic, gigantification....'

The attack didn't work properly.

'There are two suspicious characteristics. Magic resistance and attribute resistance.'

Magic resistance has a 50% magic damage reduction effect, and attribute resistance has a 90% reduction effect on specific attribute damage.

'It's magic resistance...! If electricity didn't work due to attribute resistance, it would have calmly gotten up and counterattacked without suffering....'

Of all things, it's magic resistance.

'...Damn it. I remember what I said to Number 31 earlier.'

I was so ashamed of my arrogance that my face burned. If I had used magic at the same time as the other squad members from the beginning, the result would have been different.

The Hobgoblin's large fist is swung.

I instantly created a formula and activated magic.

The barrier blocked the bastard's fist. Jjaenggeurang! The Class 2 barrier is easily broken to the point of being absurd, and my body breaks through the wooden wall and flies backward. I rolled on the muddy floor. Fortunately, the barrier absorbed most of the impact, so there was little damage compared to the wild flight. My mouth is torn, and I have some bruises all over my body.

"The Hobgoblin has mutated! Scatter and use ice magic!!"

I jumped up and shouted at the bewildered squad members.
