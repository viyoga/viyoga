The amusement park was chaos incarnate.

The stamp missions weren't all easy and normal, of course.

A mission to drive a bumper car bigger than a truck and hit ten people.

A mission to ride a vicious horse and gallop thirty kilometers.

A mission to ride the Gyro Drop, fly into outer space, and land without dying.

A mission to ride a roller coaster, fall into the ocean, and make it back.

And so on—there were quite a lot of batshit insane missions. But since you don't actually die here even if you die, they were doable.

With missions like these, the amusement park naturally turned into a complete shitshow. Everywhere you looked, there were explosions and screaming.

"What a fucking mess."

Ju Seohyeon said, deflecting a streetlamp flying at her with her sword.

"That's exactly what the Divine Thrones want."

"...The Divine Thrones want this?"

"Not all Divine Thrones are righteous. The evil-aligned ones would love this. As for the good-aligned ones, well, since nobody actually dies for real, they just chalk it up to a midsummer night's dream and let it slide."

"...I don't get it."

"You don't need to."

I took Ju Seohyeon and quickly cleared the missions we could knock out fast.

A mission to find cleaning tools for a janitor.

A mission to hand five fish to a chef.

A mission to give ten balloons to a mascot employee.

A mission to climb to the top of the observation deck and come back down at the request of the observation deck manager.

A mission to hand coffee beans to a barista.

Ju Seohyeon and I collected six stamps in under thirty minutes. Just two more stamps and we'd pass the preliminaries.

"...Is this even allowed?"

"No reason it wouldn't be. Gathering intel in advance is a skill too."

The Divine Thrones knew that fact well.

For the Divine Thrones, obtaining information in advance through future sight or prophecy was neither cheating nor anything of the sort. If that had been a problem, the Divine Thrones themselves would have objected. After all, myths are full of stories about the future.

"Are you going to do the remaining two the same way?"

"I've already done every mission I could cheese. The remaining two are annoying, but I'll have to put in some actual effort."

"...We're not riding that goddamn Gyro Drop, are we?"

I let out a hollow laugh as I watched the Gyro Drop blast into space faster than a rocket.

"That one takes way too long. And we can't even use spatial teleportation scrolls here."

I took Ju Seohyeon and headed for the haunted house. At the entrance of the eerie haunted house, an employee wearing an armband spoke.

"This is a real haunted house. If you make it out alive, we'll stamp your mission success. If you get eaten inside, we take no responsibility. Haha."

The smiling employee's mouth stretched wide open, splitting ear to ear. If you looked closely, you could see the employee's feet hovering about three centimeters off the ground. Ghostly power was overriding the horizontal coordinate system.

"Scared?"

“…….”

Ju Seohyeon didn't even bother to reply.

Ghosts and spirits—she'd already faced and killed plenty of them. Why would she be afraid? If anything, the ghosts should be afraid of her.

The moment we stepped inside the haunted house, the space shifted as if we'd entered a dungeon.

The sky was dark, with a red full moon hanging overhead. It was an abandoned mountain village, but there were no insect sounds.

"...Seong Yujin."

Ju Seohyeon quietly murmured my name. Her right hand stroked the hilt of the sword at her waist.

"I know. Things that aren't even ghosts are lying in ambush here?"

I flicked my finger.

KWA-RANG!

A bolt of lightning struck the ground, illuminating the surroundings.

Behind trees, in the shadows of walls, on rooftops—the silhouettes of assassins clad in black were revealed.

"You knew?"

"I didn't. If I'd known these bastards were here, I wouldn't have walked in."

The last time I'd used the power of foresight to check the future was before the first preliminary match of the martial arts tournament began.

There were two variables in the first preliminary.

One was slapping Ju Seohyeon's ass. As a result, Ju Seohyeon chased after me in the second preliminary. Which would mean these bastards were targeting Ju Seohyeon, not me... That's impossible. For one, aside from Legion activities, Ju Seohyeon barely does anything outside. She's hardly got any reason to make enemies.

The other variable was what I'd said to the God of Mischief, Loki.

"Is this the work of the God of Mischief?"

I threw the question at the assassins. No answer came back. The assassins silently raised their weapons and closed the distance.

Thunder Heaven Style—Electromagnetic Wave.

I used electromagnetic waves to determine the exact number of assassins, then furrowed my brow.

'I can't get a proper read on the number of enemies. The information I'm sensing through the electromagnetic waves is all over the place.'

I lightly used the power of foresight. Instead of seeing the future, I boosted my intuition. In an instant, my intuition peaked and produced an answer before the given information and logic could even be reorganized.

I turned my head to the side. There was an assassin holding a knife, unusually tall and gaunt.

"You. You're the one who designed this trap."

"Impressive. To think you'd notice my existence so quickly."

He removed his mask. He was a brown-haired man with a long face and narrow, slit-like eyes. Going by appearance alone, he looked to be in his early thirties.

"God of Mischief. Are you the contractor of the God of Deception?"

"...I don't think you'd believe me even if I said no. You're certain regardless of my answer. May I ask how you knew?"

Intuition.

If my unconscious had figured out the answer, there had to be a reason it reached that conclusion. Starting from the answer, I trace back to the process.

"You and I have never met. Since you have no connection to me, you've got no motive to kill me. A third party hired you? That doesn't add up. Killing me here just means elimination from the preliminaries."

"Your elimination could be the goal."

"If elimination were the goal, why go about it this way? If it were me, I'd have applied pressure at Atlantis, not during the tournament preliminaries. If killing me or eliminating me isn't the goal... then the goal must be testing my abilities. Did you come to dig up what I'm hiding?"

"An interesting conclusion. What's your basis for claiming I'm the God of Mischief's contractor?"

"I have an information-related ability. I just used it, and the information came out strange. The God of Mischief is also the God of Deception. You must have received a skill that distorts information. On top of that, the God of Mischief has shown interest in me since the first preliminary."

Deception.

The man before me must have used deception. It might not be an innate trait like mine, but the effect would be similar.

Clap. Clap. Clap.

He clapped quietly.

"Correct. Not a single word wrong. My god has been showing interest in you. Now I understand why. Ah, my name is Wade Reagan. Things have turned out this way, but I bear you no ill will."

「The God of Mischief sincerely believes you are a Regressor.」

「The King of the Demon Realm is laughing.」

「Hehehe.」

The God of Mischief hadn't immediately believed I was a Regressor—he'd doubted it. But seeing this scene, he seemed to have become certain.

「You have deceived the God of Deception. A wondrous achievement.」

「Title acquired: 'He Who Deceived a God.'」

「Bonus ability stat points +3 acquired.」

「One of the conditions for rank transcendence of the innate trait Deception (SSS) has been met.」

I used the bonus stat points immediately. Naturally, I invested them into Luck, which had limited methods of increase.

"I bear you no ill will either."

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido into my hand and charged at Wade. Ju Seohyeon drew her sword in response. While I handled Wade, the most dangerous one, she would take care of the rest.

Thunder Heaven Style—Gale Thunder.

Wade was no pushover—he reacted to my speed and moved his knife.

Sword and knife crossed.

Both vanished like mirages.

Wade's knife aimed for my neck; my sword pierced through Wade's heart.

His narrow, slit-like eyes tore wide open. His revealed eyes were pure white—four-blank eyes.

"Illusion...? No, this is the same deception as mine...!"

"Guess your Divine Throne didn't tell you the details of my abilities. Then again, that would be a rule violation, so it makes sense."

Wade's knife, shorter than my sword, never reached me. He didn't try to force the knife into my neck.

"...This is my miscalculation. I never anticipated you'd possess the same ability. It can't be something you gained through a contract with that one. Is it an innate trait?"

"Yes. You don't seem to have much talent for combat. Against an opponent who's reached a certain level, a surprise attack using deception won't work unless they let their guard down considerably."

"It's true that I have less combat experience than most."

"Aren't you going to use magic?"

"You saw through that too. Impressive. There are too many eyes here to reveal my hand... I'll quietly accept elimination."

Wade's breath stopped. Before I could pull my sword from his heart, the corpse vanished. All that remained was the blood he'd spilled. I swung my sword through the air to shake off the blood.

I looked around.

There was no one strong enough to threaten Ju Seohyeon. They were skilled assassins, sure, but assassins nonetheless. And they weren't even fighting seriously.

'She'd probably handle it on her own if I left her alone... but it's better to help. Ju Seohyeon acts like she doesn't care but she gets sulky easily.'

Not that a sulky Ju Seohyeon could actually do anything about it.

I helped Ju Seohyeon kill all the assassins. We caught our breath briefly, then headed for the haunted house exit. Not a single ghost was in sight. The assassins must have taken care of them while lying in ambush.

'I didn't ask those bastards why they ambushed us in the haunted house. Well, it's one of two reasons. Either they picked the mission nearby that looked easiest, or they used prophecy too. Wade might not have been acting purely on the God of Mischief's orders.'
