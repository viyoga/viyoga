"It's a drug called Hwamyeongdan. I obtained it from a dungeon in my younger days. The effect, as you can see, is temporary physical enhancement. From the moment I got it forty years ago, I've carried it in my bosom without missing a single day."

Mok Taesun spoke calmly.

"A drug that temporarily raises physical abilities to S-rank or above? That's cheating. Got any more?"

"It's not such a convenient drug. After the effects wear off, the body suffers permanent damage. It can't be restored through modern medicine or magic. I'll likely spend most of my remaining days bedridden after today."

It wasn't particularly surprising. I knew well enough that there's no power without a price. However, for someone like me who has complete recovery, it was a meaningless cost.

"So, are there any more of these Hwamyeongdan?"

"Your greed is remarkable. There are no spare Hwamyeongdan. They've never even appeared on the market. The barrier restoration is just about complete."

"Barrier?"

I looked around.

The barrier I had shattered was partially restored. Some of the heroes who had surrounded me had focused on repairing the barrier.

"You think you've trapped me inside the barrier?"

"It's not a barrier meant to trap. It's a barrier to block the aftermath of battle and protect Seoul."

Mok Taesun stomps the ground. Creeeeak! The floor splits apart.

[Seong Yujin! Run for now! Mok Taesun's power right now exceeds his prime! If the drug's effect is temporary, there's no need to fight him while he's at his strongest!]

Kang Jiyoung's words were correct.

If I were only aiming for victory, there was no need to fight right now. A barrier like this couldn't block a spatial teleportation scroll. I could escape using a teleport scroll and come back an hour later.

Just because the opponent was somewhat dangerous didn't mean I could only run away. More than anything.

'Why would I run when I'm going to win.'

Here he comes.

Mok Taesun charged at me just like before.

[Using Challa (Instant). Remaining stacks: 10]

This time as well, I use Challa to dodge Mok Taesun's fist. Mok Taesun immediately attacks with his left knee, as if the punch was a feint. Of course I knew. Just because the opponent clenches his fist doesn't mean you only watch the fist—that's for amateurs.

[Using Challa (Instant). Remaining stacks: 9]

While dodging, I became certain.

'With Cheonma Shingong that lacks the Atlantis system correction, I can't match his speed itself.'

Once Challa's stacks ran out, I'd have no choice but to be unilaterally beaten.

[Using Challa (Instant). Remaining stacks: 8]

I think with extremely accelerated cognition.

'I need to use Noecheonryu.'

Right now, I'm in the Jeokgwang state. If I use Noecheonryu, my identity could be exposed. Some people already know that I use not only spirits but also Noecheonryu. Also, sharp-eyed bastards could suspect my identity from Noecheonryu alone.

'Even if efficiency drops, using Pacheonhwang is also an option. The problem is whether I can use Pacheonhwang in my current state.'

Pacheonhwang shatters and burns one's own soul. But right now, my body doesn't contain only my soul.

'The worst case imaginable is Kang Jiyoung's soul burning together and being annihilated. I have complete recovery, but... for Kang Jiyoung, there's no method. If her soul is damaged even slightly, I can't guarantee it can be restored.'

The answer was clear.

'I can't gamble Kang Jiyoung's soul just to beat some bastard like him.'

I use Noecheonryu.

Even if people suspect my identity, I can just ignore it. Without clear evidence, they won't be able to interrogate me when I have the backing of Seong Hari.

I clench my fist.

Cheonma energy and lightning energy intermingle as black lightning flashes. I thrust my fist toward Mok Taesun. With the speed enhanced by Challa, Mok Taesun couldn't dodge. No—judging by how his pupils fixate on my fist, he didn't dodge.

KWAAANG!

Hardened stone crumbles and scatters from his face.

"You were covering yourself with a stone shell over your skin?"

"It's a special stone called Saengseok. This isn't dirt that peels off so easily, but... you're stronger than expected, Jeokgwang."

Noecheonryu (Thunder Heaven Style) — Jilpung Shinroe (Gale and Thunder).

An electric current originating from my spine spreads through my entire body, stimulating it. Physical abilities including reaction speed have increased. Mok Taesun's movements, which were hard to react to without Challa, began to become visible.

Dodge the fist, throw a fist.

That simple exchange occurs more than five times per second.

BOOM! BOOM! BOOM!

Even though neither fist connects, shockwaves burst from the aftermath.

'I won't use the sword. That would be too obviously declaring I'm Seong Yujin.'

There's a saying. That the sword is merely an extension of the hand.

I extend my fingers straight and form a hand blade. Blue lightning and sword energy gather and condense above the metal gauntlet.

Noecheonryu (Thunder Heaven Style) — Noegwang (Thunder Light).

A swift blade executed with the hand. The power was far weaker than I had thought.

Mok Taesun took it with his body without even blinking. Fine cracks appeared on his upper body. I hadn't been able to pierce through the Saengseok or whatever it was.

The reason Mok Taesun chose to block with his body rather than dodge, even after seeing my stance, was to counterattack.

[Using Challa (Instant). Remaining stacks: 7]

'Too late.'

Challa wasn't omnipotent. By the time I used Challa, Mok Taesun's fist had already reached my abdomen. At this distance, dodging was impossible.

'My pain tolerance isn't weak. And with Stormbreaker on top of that, I can take it.'

I release Flash Step.

KWAAAAAANG!

Mok Tae-sun's fist connected with my abdomen. Small stones—life stones, I realized—spun from his knuckles like drill bits, gouging and tearing through Stormbreaker's plating in an instant. Then his bare fist sank into my gut.

Amid the horrific agony of that drill carving into my abdomen, I reflexively moved my lightning qi.

Thunder Heaven Style — Electromagnetic Barrier, maximum output.

Mok Tae-sun's fist, which had been boring into my stomach, was blasted away.

“……!”

I clenched my teeth and swallowed the scream clawing up my throat. Instead of a scream, blood filled my mouth.

I pull myself together. If I don't do anything here, I take Mok Tae-sun's next attack head-on.

Thunder Heaven Style — Thunder Art: Blood Lightning.

The blood spraying through the air along with chunks of my innards transformed into crimson lightning. The red bolts lunged at Mok Tae-sun in unison.

Life stones rose around Mok Tae-sun, unfurling a barrier. The blood lightning paid it no mind and burrowed through. The moment it touched the life stones, it detonated. The shockwave sent fragments of the stones flying in every direction, and through that gap, the blood lightning reached Mok Tae-sun's body. It exploded.

Rattle, rattle.

Chunks of life stone clinging to Mok Tae-sun's skin dropped away. Beneath them, skin revealed itself—scorched red, split open, bleeding.

"Remarkable power. If it hadn't been for the life stones, I might have died. What's more remarkable is that you're still standing. Your flesh is carved open and part of your innards are ground to paste, yet you're still on your feet. Protecting your blood vessels with mana and substituting your organ function with electricity? Just how monstrous is your mana manipulation?"

"Fuck. I wasn't even off guard and I almost bit it. Not bad, old man."

I hadn't expected Stormbreaker to be pierced this easily.

-I'm trying to repair the armor with my power but it's not working! These little stone fragments are sticking to it and getting in the way!

'Just release mana and shake them off.'

-That kind of fine control is hard, I'm telling you!

There was no time to retort to Machina's grumbling. Mok Tae-sun stomped the ground—thoom, thoom—and the earth split apart as boulders reaching a meter in size shot skyward from the fissures. The number of those boulders kept growing, and in an instant they exceeded several hundred. Thanks to that, an incredibly deep sinkhole opened up in the courtyard.

"So this is why he's called the Thousand Boulders of the Heavy Heaven. If those all drop at once, a chunk of Seoul is going to be completely obliterated. Is this why he had his underlings restore the barrier?"

"Originally I had no intention of going this far—the cleanup would be a nightmare. The Association, not to mention the government, would obviously be in an uproar... But just now, after trading blows with you, I realized. That I can't kill you unless I go this far."

Mok Tae-sun gestured, and the hundreds of boulders floating in the sky began falling toward me. They didn't drop all at once. A few fell first, and the rest followed in sequence.

The reason those boulders weren't falling simultaneously was simple. The courtyard was too small for that.

'They say the best defense is a good offense.'

I ran at Mok Tae-sun. Now he'd have to deal with those falling boulders too. If Mok Tae-sun dodges? I'll go into Du Seok-man's mansion.

Mok Tae-sun didn't dodge. We traded fists and feet in close quarters.

I read the falling boulders ahead of time with my qi sense and avoided direct hits.

A boulder crashed down beside me, slamming into the ground and generating a shockwave. Caught in the blast, I faltered—and Mok Tae-sun slipped into my guard. I glared at him and used Flash Step.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 6]

My fist, wreathed in black lightning, struck Mok Tae-sun's temple. The life stones protecting his head scattered from the impact, and his body lurched violently.

Then the shockwave reflected back and flew at me. It was the effect of Impact Reflection (S-), one of Mok Tae-sun's skills.

'Ah, fuck.'

The moment I realized it, it was too late.

The boulders falling around me collided with each other, generating shockwaves. From the center of it all, Mok Tae-sun used Impact Reflection to manipulate the shockwaves so that the damage only flowed toward me.

THOOM THOOM THOOM THOOM THOOM THOOM THOOM THOOM!

Shockwaves hammered my body from every direction. The reflected and re-reflected blasts felt like thousands of fists pounding into me.

'Fortunately, it doesn't feel as overwhelming as when he punched my abdomen earlier. The power dropped a bit as the impacts reflected. For now I can block it with my body-hardening qi, but...'

The damage kept piling up. And hundreds of boulders were still floating in the sky.

'At this rate, I won't last.'

[Activating Heaven's Heart. Duration: 1 minute.]

My physical abilities surged and my mana became infinite. But unlike the infinite mana, there was a limit to output.

I took a single breath and released black lightning with everything I had.

'Grant physical force to the nature of lightning.'

Hundreds of strands of black lightning imbued with physical force shot toward the sky. The hundreds of boulders floating in the air were pierced through and shattered. The black lightning didn't stop there—it shattered the broken boulders again, reducing them to mere pebbles.

With the falling boulders halted, the shockwaves vanished.

I ran at Mok Tae-sun, and he wrapped life stones around his body and took his stance.

[Using Leap.]

THWACK!

My fist appeared before Mok Tae-sun and struck his abdomen. The life stones blocked it.

'The same way he did it earlier.'

I twisted and spun the black lightning wrapped around my fist. It tore through the life stones and burrowed into his body. Mok Tae-sun's abdomen was gouged open and his innards were ripped apart.

"...So you could do short-range spatial movement too. Not factoring that in was my miscalculation... Jeok-gwang. My children and Du Seok-man's know nothing. Don't touch them. I'm asking you."

I'd investigated Mok Tae-sun's and Du Seok-man's children beforehand. Unlike their fathers, they'd walked an honest, straight path. Killing them would yield no karma, so they were outside my interest. I had no grudge against Mok Tae-sun or Du Seok-man either.

"My target is Du Seok-man."

"...I'm grateful."

Mok Tae-sun collapsed.

My intuition told me that Mok Tae-sun could have resisted at the end, but he gave up after hearing my answer.

『Karma: Good (善) increases by 5.』

Before entering Duseok's mansion, I looked around. The Cheongsong Clan heroes who had surrounded the area were watching me with expressions of shock. I couldn't sense any fighting spirit from them.

'Well, that's expected. The battle just now would be hard to comprehend by ordinary standards.'

In this world too, just like reality, the gap between S-rank and A-rank was immense. If there hadn't been a barrier during that fight, this entire neighborhood would have been obliterated and then some.

'The Cheongsong Clan itself is relatively intact, so killing them would only chip away at my karma.'

I entered the mansion.
