Before I could even collect myself from the horrifying sensation of my balls freezing solid, the demonic beast I'd been riding plummeted to the ground.

Only belatedly did I realize the mount had frozen to death. It couldn't withstand the cold of the Ice Hell zone.

'Pathetic bastard.'

I watched the fall and timed it. As the ground drew closer, the terrain of the Ice Hell came into view. Everything was blanketed in ice. Call it a glacial world and I'd believe it.

Just before hitting the ground, I activated a skill.

「Using Lightning Flash.」

Electrification.

My body converted into lightning, ignoring physical impact. I managed to land on the frozen ground relatively safely.

The demonic beast's corpse shattered on impact. It had been frozen solid, so it broke apart. Looking at the fractured cross-sections, it had been frozen rock-hard.

'So anything below a certain stat threshold freezes instantly. Even with my current stats I'm feeling the cold, so... a common demonic beast wouldn't stand a chance.'

I let out a sigh.

The breath I exhaled froze in an instant. I could feel even the moisture inside my mouth crystallizing, so I shut my jaw immediately.

Crackle, snap...!

Lightning sparked across my entire body. I intended to raise my body temperature using mana and lightning. And as a bonus, restore one frozen testicle to normal while I was at it.

'If I had my way I'd just spam Full Recovery, but... I don't have any intel on the Ice Hell, so burning it carelessly feels wasteful.'

It worked. Mana burned like firewood and my body temperature climbed. However, the already-frozen testicle didn't thaw. Not even when I concentrated mana into it.

'...Setting aside the cold, it's not like it's shattered, so I'll let it slide for now. No telling what'll happen if more time passes. If it persists even outside the Ice Hell zone, that'd be the worst-case scenario.'

Regardless of what happened, I had Full Recovery as my last resort, so I could stay calm.

'What a dogshit place. Conquest this, strategy that—once I achieve my objective, I'm out of here immediately.'

I pulled winter clothes from my inventory and layered them on. No real effect. No matter how many thick layers I piled on, the cold seeped through. If anything, the clothes were just cumbersome, so I stripped them off and moved.

Chat-chat-chat!

My teeth chattered on their own. The cold burrowing into my body kept me trembling. Even if I could prevent my body from freezing with mana, I couldn't stop feeling the cold itself.

'This is a cold I couldn't even feel back when I was a total weakling...'

I guarantee you—if I died right now, stats and all that shit wouldn't matter. I'd just be dead.

I focused my eyes and scanned the surroundings. Ice, corpses, and a hazy cold like fog filled everything. Visibility was shit, to put it mildly.

"Astrafe."

Lightning sparked above my right hand. Dozens of lightning strands converged into one, forming a spear of electricity.

I gripped Astrafe as-is and slammed it into the ground.

KWAAAAAANG!

The lightning exploded, forcibly pushing back the surrounding cold. My field of view opened up in an instant. But not even five seconds passed before the cold rushed back in.

I looked at the ground. The ice surface had cracked, but it hadn't caved. Absurdly solid for ice.

'I heard Mahabalchuma is at the bottom of the hell, so I was going to smash through the floor and descend... but with ice this hard, it'd take ages to get even 1 meter down. I need to find another way.'

After pondering for a moment, I summoned Storm Breaker.

I used a spell stored in Storm Breaker.

『Using Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.』

Lightning settled into both my eyes. Blue lightning radiance flowed through my irises, and my pupils turned gold.

'Even in this world, the Great Sage Equal to Heaven exists, and it could draw unnecessary attention, so I tried to avoid using this... but I've got no choice.'

「The Master of the Heavens tilts his head.」

「The King of Demon Heaven observes your eyes with keen interest.」

Fortunate, perhaps. Only two divine thrones reacted.

'In reality, about 8 minutes is the limit, but here I should be able to maintain it for around 20 minutes. Storm Breaker has accumulated a fair amount of experience too... should I invest in Thunder Eye Golden Pupil? Though investing elsewhere would probably be more efficient.'

Storm Breaker's enhancement wasn't urgent, so I decided to settle that later.

I slowly scanned my surroundings again.

The visible range was different from before. Like the resolution had been forcibly upgraded several tiers.

'Feels like switching from a 5-year-old monitor to the latest model.'

The thick cold saturating the area? Thunder Eye Golden Pupil couldn't be obstructed by it.

'Even though the cold looks stationary, it's flowing.'

There must be something where the cold originates. Having determined my destination, I moved my feet without hesitation.

I saw frozen corpses. Most were collapsed on the ground, but some stood upright. No children—the place was full of adult male and female corpses. All of them naked.

Through Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, I could see they were alive. Their bodies had frozen to death, but their souls lived on, suffering.

'Their souls are bound to this hell itself. Even if I destroy the bodies, they can't be freed.'

The Ice Hell.

A hell meant to punish sinners.

I quickened my pace, feeling the cold burrowing into my body.

I sensed a presence. Focusing my eyes, I could pierce through the fog and identify the sources. Three demons holding armor and spears. Jailers of hell clad in Eastern-style armor, gripping spears. They spotted me.

"A human? A living human?!"

"...How did a human find their way to the Ice Hell?"

"They say a human hell-noble appeared in the west—could this human be that hell-noble?"

The hell's jailers, though flustered, leveled their spears at me and took combat stances. Their postures weren't sloppy. Proof they were elite soldiers.

"Human. Lower your weapon. This is not a place outsiders may enter. Cooperate peacefully and no harm will come to you."

I didn't lower Storm Breaker in my hand.

"No harm? You expect me to trust the word of demons?"

"We are not common demons! We are civil servants working in this hell! We will take you to the Great King. His Majesty is merciful—if you entered this hell by mistake, he will send you safely back to the surface!"

"The Great King—you mean the master of this hell?"

"Correct. The Ice Frost King! His Majesty is fair and just—he will show you mercy."

Two options lay before me.

Ignore the jailers and head toward where the cold originates.

Or follow the jailers and meet the Ice Frost King.

'Perhaps the destinations are the same. If he's the master of this hell, he might know the way into Mahabalchuma.'

I reached my conclusion.

"Guide me. Quickly. I don't have time."

"Follow me."

The jailers took the lead. I followed them. As expected, perhaps. They headed toward where the cold originated.

After passing about three ice hills, a dark ice cavern appeared. Cold poured out from this cavern. Yet paradoxically, a warm light flowed from within.

"Human. Can you endure?"

"...How are you three so unaffected?"

I could feel the sensation in my fingertips and toes growing dull. And this was with mana protecting my body.

"The cold of the Ice Hell only affects sinners. We have no sins, so though we feel the chill, we take no damage from it. Seeing you move fine in the Ice Hell, you must not have accumulated many sins. You may be proud of that."

“…….”

We entered the ice cavern. The jailers walked in without a care.

I, on the other hand, had to take the full brunt of the intense cold head-on. The cold became blades, slashing at me. My skin split and blood flowed. The droplets froze before they could even fall.

'Astrafe.'

Crackle-crack-crack...!

I imbued Astrafe into my heart.

With every heartbeat, Astrafe's lightning traveled through my blood and spread across my entire body, fighting back against the cold.

Crack-crackle, crack-crackle!

I advanced one step at a time. My whole body felt like it would shatter, but it wasn't unbearable.

Like that, in a daze, drenched head to toe in blood, I finally reached the end.

The cold vanished.

My frozen body began to slowly thaw. The sensation in my hands and feet, which had been fading, gradually returned.

"Huuuu."

The sigh I exhaled didn't freeze.

The three jailers looked at me and their eyes went wide.

"What the hell! You're covered in blood!"

"Don't tell me you actually did have sins...?!"

「You have conquered Zone 9,139, the Ice Hell.」

「You have gained dominion over Zone 9,139, the Ice Hell.」

「You acquire 500,000 AP.」

「As ruler of the Ice Hell, you acquire 250,000 AP every 30 days.」

I'd succeeded in the conquest by the skin of my teeth. It was unexpected. I'd thought I needed to kill the Ice Frost King.

I wiped the blood off with a handkerchief from my inventory and looked around. The building's interior was quite ornate. And from the far end, a man in black clothing slowly approached me.

「The Ice Frost King (False) approaches you.」

"I expected the one to conquer this zone would be someone without sin. Never in my wildest dreams did I think someone with sins as deep as yours would conquer this place."

"I know this hell isn't the real hell. Are you going to fight me?"

"Even if it's not the real hell, the sinners suffering here are real. Well, since the zone has been conquered, that ends too. I, the jailers, and the sinners will all vanish as of today. The sinners haven't yet cleared their sins, so they'll fall into the true Ice Hell."

"Don't care."

When I looked at him, he met my eyes and swallowed audibly.

"You have eyes similar to his... Eyes far too powerful for a human to possess. Is it because this place is Atlantis?"

"Enough useless chatter. What I want to know is how to enter the Mahabalchuma zone. Do you know?"

The Ice Frost King pointed to one wall. Unlike the ornate palace-like interior, this part was made of ice. The wall was cracked, and beyond it was pitch black—nothing visible. Even Thunder Eye Golden Pupil couldn't see inside. That meant it was at least a higher tier than Thunder Eye Golden Pupil (Lv.3).

"Enter there and you can reach the hidden zone, Mahabalchuma. Compared to the true Mahabalchuma it's nothing, but with your current self, you won't last ten minutes in there. Survive one hour inside and you'll be able to conquer Mahabalchuma."

"You're telling me surprisingly easily."

"It's not something I can choose to withhold. Human who even now accumulates sin, your sins will be judged someday. Abandon the certainty that death is rest."

"People get judged because they're weak. Once I'm stronger than the judge, there'll be no judgment for me. I will become stronger than all of you."

"Human arrogance and foolishness. And truly demon-like."

「The King of Demon Heaven likes your words.」

The Ice Frost King and the jailers vanished.

I slipped my hand into my underwear to check my balls. The frozen testicle had returned to normal. Full Recovery was available, and I had over half my mana left. The wounds from the recovery potion healed quickly too.

And then I stood before the ice wall.

'I roughly understand now. To conquer zones like the Ice Hell or Mahabalchuma, you need a human without sin. Or at least one with few sins. In any case, it's clear this place is my absolute worst matchup.'

The crack in the wall. I extended my foot toward the darkness that even Thunder Eye Golden Pupil couldn't penetrate.

'I can only last ten minutes? Then I just need to achieve my objective within ten minutes.'

Whether Thunder Eye Golden Pupil will help in Mahabalchuma or not—I'll find out when I get there.
