Jonas Muller was coldly assessing the situation.

Himself, who had recently reached Rank 6, versus an opponent who recently reached Rank 7. There was an absurd difference between them. The only hopeful thing was that the opponent's combat reputation was low. He had never heard of any notable achievements from Yuria.

,She probably reached Rank 7 through luck or with support.'

Yuria is a maid aiming for Prime rank. She must be able to fight to some extent.

,If I fight her, I'll lose.'

Jonas had no reason to fight Yuria. He was planning to find an opportunity to escape this place and flee to the border region. There was no reason to fight a losing battle.

,A Rank 7 with no achievements. That means she's inexperienced due to a lack of combat experience. I'll run away as soon as I get the chance.'

Jonas saw a vision.

A shadow blade falling from the ceiling, aiming for his neck. It was so stealthy. The future him died without even reacting. It wasn't just because the shadow blade was silent. It was accurately piercing the blind spot that a person couldn't perceive, the gap in his consciousness.

,But if I can see it, I can avoid it.'

Jonas had an ability to see a short future limited to himself. An ability that wasn't very helpful except during combat because he could only see less than 10 seconds into the future.

,However, in a battle where lives are at stake, it's the best ability.'

He dodged the shadow attacks flying from all directions by stepping carefully. When he dodged 15 shadow attacks, Yuria's attacks stopped.

Jonas slightly twisted his lips upward.

,Is that all? I was nervous because you're Rank 7, but... you're not as impressive as I thought.'

Two options came to Jonas's mind. One was to rush at Yuria and land a blow, and the other was to escape from here.

But neither option was the right answer.

If he tried to rush at Yuria and attack, shadows would rush at him from all directions. An attack that was impossible to avoid even knowing the future.

Escape? The moment he did, he would be torn to pieces by a slash. What was absurd was that even though he saw the future, he couldn't figure out where the slash was coming from.

Cold sweat ran down Jonas's cheek.

,...Is she playing with me right now?'

,No. I'm just adjusting my senses for a moment. The way of using power in this world is a bit unfamiliar.'

Yuria flicked her finger. The shadows on the floor rippled. A power that manipulates shadows as an ability, not magic. The essence of shadows might be the same, but the way of handling power was different.

,...So you're seeing me as a punching bag. I understand the difference in power, but if you come at me like this, I can't help but get heated.'

,What will change if you get angry?

,Yeah. I'll show you how things will change.'

Jonas, gritting his teeth, moved forward. Shadows soared from the ceiling and the floor.

Chains.

Jet-black shadow chains rushed toward Jonas. Jonas, who knew the trajectory of the chains in advance through future prediction, tried to twist his body to avoid them. But the chains suddenly changed direction and grabbed Jonas's wrist.

The chains pulled, and Jonas's body collided with the ceiling. His body was sucked into the shadows of the ceiling.

Kwang!

Immediately after, he was face-planting on the ground. Forced space movement through shadows. He, a Rank 6, couldn't even react properly.

Jonas's nose bone was broken. He was agitated, unaware of the nosebleed flowing down.

,How did she do that? There was nothing like this in the future I saw...!

,The future is bound to be variable unless it has arrived. Is what you saw really the future?

,Bullshit...! I've been using this ability for 20 years. There has never been a case where the future I confirmed has changed! Did you cast an illusion on me?!'

Jonas tore off the shadow chains that grabbed his wrist with mana-enhanced strength and stood up.

,You're the type who only believes what you want to believe. It's a common type, so I'm not surprised.'

Jonas's future prediction activated again. Shadow chains rushing from the left and right. Jonas didn't avoid them this time. Instead, blood gushed from his body.

Alchemy was activated, and the alchemic blood became a giant blade, sweeping in all directions. Yuria jumped into the air to avoid the blade.

,She won't be able to move in the air. I'll shoot alchemic blood with wind pressure from here...!

Just as he was about to take a stance and swing his fist, his right ankle was already grabbed by a shadow chain.

,Future prediction didn't activate! Why...?!'

Alchemic blood gushed from his thigh and turned into a sword. The moment he tried to break the shadow chain grabbing his ankle. A new shadow chain appeared and wrapped around the sword. The sword made of alchemic blood stops. It can't be controlled.

As bewilderment dominated his entire body, the shadow chain pulled his body.

Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang!

He was dragged around by the shadow chain, colliding with everything. He was dragged to the ceiling and then slammed into the floor, and when he was about to hit the wall, he was beaten by the chain. The space changed so quickly that it was impossible to keep track. Future prediction hasn't been activated since earlier.

,Rather than being beaten to death like this... I'd rather...!

He gives up the alchemic blood.

He could earn a huge amount of money if he sold it, but what's important now is not money but his life.

Jonas spewed blood from his entire body. The alchemic blood that was in his body came out and became steel spikes, hitting all directions, and then the alchemy was released, causing a shockwave. One, the shockwave of at least thousands of shockwaves exploding simultaneously is by no means weak.

Eventually, the building collapses due to the shockwave.

,I'll escape at this opportunity. It's still broad daylight outside. There aren't as many shadows as when I'm inside the building. She's a woman who manipulates shadows, so she's bound to be weaker. I'll avoid the shadows as much as possible...'

Jonas, who was about to go outside the collapsed building, couldn't move after seeing the outside.

Escape?

It was impossible.

The outside of the building was nothing but darkness. There was no light. The sky and the ground were buried in darkness. Even if he concentrated mana in his eyes, he couldn't see through the darkness.

"......"

Jonas, who regained his senses the fastest, clashed the knuckles he was wearing on both hands. Sparks flew. But the surroundings didn't brighten up at all. The light was unable to illuminate the darkness.

Jonas glared at the maid standing behind him with an expressionless face and asked.

,Is this your Unique Skill?

,Yes. I needed to use my Unique Skill in a real battle and adjust it. You happened to be a suitable opponent.'

,So that's what you meant by adjusting your senses. You were really treating me like a punching bag. Is your Unique Skill a barrier type?

As far as Jonas knew, there were several types of Unique Skills. Roughly three types. Single-use, continuous, and barrier.

Single-use types are powerful but end after one use. Like a bullet.

Continuous types are those where the effect continues. Most are buff types that strengthen oneself.

Barrier types are types that create their own space by blocking connections with the outside. It's a rare type among Unique Skills.

,Barrier type? Ah. Is that how it looked? You can definitely feel that the space itself has been separated.'

,...Does that mean it's not a barrier type?

,Since the adjustment is over... I'll show you for the final check.'

Yuria flicked her finger.

The darkness that covered the world disappears. Jonas felt his mind fading.

&gt;

Tuk.

Jonas snapped back to his senses at the sound of Yuria touching the table. He was inside the collapsed building. The shabby building was intact, and Yuria was sitting in front of the table. Around her, Alphatiere Corporation agents were standing or sitting, dead.

,...Was it an illusion? Or did she rewind time or something?

Jonas checked his broken nose and the throbbing pain all over his body. He could feel the pain. At least it wasn't an illusion. But what he couldn't understand was that the alchemic blood he had abandoned was back in his body. As a result, the collapsed building had returned to its original state.

His eyes quickly scanned the building. Parts of the building were broken. The aftermath of the previous battle remained intact.

,...She didn't rewind time. She eliminated the cause itself...! This is ridiculous! Interfering with causality is not easy even for transcendents! It's not something a Rank 7 can do!

,What's important is not the difference in power. What's more important is recognizing and understanding the concept.'

,Do you think transcendents are called transcendents for nothing? They handle transcendent power because they have transcended not only power but also their minds! It's impossible unless your mind reaches the level of a transcendent...!!'

,You're trembling with fear. Let's end this now.'

Yuria used her Unique Skill with indifferent eyes.

Unique Skill: Dark Horizon.

All light is reversed into darkness up to the boundary that her perception reaches. There was no distinction between the inside and outside of the building. There was only darkness there now.

The flustered Jonas clashed his knuckles and created a light, but there was heat but no flame was visible. It felt like the concept of light itself had disappeared.

,Within this boundary, I can control all the laws as I please. The concept of light, the concept of time, and the concept of causality are no exception. However, the problem is that I can't reject the changed laws either.'

That's why Yuria couldn't see through the darkness either. She also couldn't see anything in this darkness. However, there was no problem with her actions because she was recognizing the darkness through shadows. And shadows refer to the darkness that light doesn't reach.

,I think those of the same Rank 7 as me can resist to some extent... but you're not Rank 7.'

,...The reason why my future prediction isn't visible is because you changed the laws?

,Yes. Instead, I also become unable to predict the future within this boundary.'

The laws are applied fairly. It's just that Yuria was originally unable to predict the future.

,Can you also manipulate space at will within the boundary?

,It's possible, but what I did to you was shadow manipulation, not the boundary.'

,...Haa. To think that such a monstrous woman came to kill me... I would have been more comfortable facing the Iron Maid of the Maid Academy.'

Jonas took a fighting stance. Alchemic blood flowed from his entire body and became armor, covering his body.

It was a meaningless act.

Yuria exists the laws within the boundary. She prohibits alchemy. It's possible because she deeply understands alchemy.

The alchemic blood became ordinary blood and flowed down to the floor.

Soon, the darkness became a blade and slashed Jonas.

,Kuaaaaaaaak!

With the last scream, Jonas's body collapsed.

Yuria released her Unique Skill. Yuria looked at his corpse and pondered for a moment.

,Dark Horizon is a Unique Skill that I intentionally obtained... but it consumes more stamina than I thought.'

A Unique Skill to counter the Unique Skills of other espers.

The effect itself was quite satisfactory.
