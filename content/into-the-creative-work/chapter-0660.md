*Deolkeok!* The window flew open, and black smoke cloaked in a dark robe entered the room. The demon's curse had arrived.

Diona and Katia's faces turned blue.

"Why the Mundudu curse...?!"

"Aga-ssi!"

The naked women grabbed each other, trembling. They knew very well that this was a demon's curse that could lead to death.

I stepped in front of them. They were my whores. I couldn't let them be cursed.

'This bastard is annoying because physical attacks and even decent sword energy don't work on him. Yuria said I could easily get rid of him by crushing him with magical mass... but I can't use magic.'

It'd be better to just take it. I have Complete Recovery, so I can cleanly eliminate the curse.

'Still, I can't just take it. This might not be the only one targeting me... No. Wait a minute. Is it even targeting me?'

*Heul-kkeut.*

I looked at Diona behind me. The sign of the demon contract had disappeared, and the curse had arrived.

Mundudu unilaterally terminated the contract. If Mundudu wanted to kill Diona, he wouldn't bother sending a curse; he would have revealed himself and tried to kill Diona directly when he terminated the contract.

There's a possibility that Mundudu's new contractor sent this curse.

'Someone who wants me or Diona dead... Only one person comes to mind. Jerdin. That bastard... backstabbing me?'

If he made a contract with a demon, he should have reported it to me. But Jerdin didn't even show his face, let alone report it. Acting unilaterally without my permission is an act of betrayal in itself.

*Pajijijik.*

Lightning flashed in my grasp. Blue electricity wriggled like a clump of earthworms.

"Keuheuheuh."

The curse let out an eerie laugh and approached me. I was about to throw the lightning at him but stopped. I knew very well that throwing it in this state wouldn't inflict any damage.

'Mass, huh...'

The magical mass that Yuria mentioned probably doesn't mean weight. There's no way magic itself has weight in the first place.

'But I'm getting a feeling.'

*Pajijijijik.*

The lightning that had been spreading in all directions in my hand began to shrink. It shrank and compressed, turning into a single bolt of lightning.

'The waterfall training I did in reality... Let's remember that time.'

I compressed the lightning, increased its density, and threw it at the curse. The curse didn't even think about dodging the lightning and rushed towards me.

The lightning tore through the curse's body. The black robe shattered, and smoke billowed. But it didn't disappear.

'If one hit isn't enough, I'll give it a combo.'

Lightning was grasped in both of my hands.

'Challna.'

I dodged the curse's charge to the side. The curse changed direction and flew towards me. I threw the lightning from both hands at the same time. The curse's body faltered. I didn't miss the opportunity and continuously threw lightning compressed with thunder without rest.

"Geuaaaaaaaa..."

The curse, hit by eight bolts of lightning, let out a low scream and disappeared.

I wiped the sweat flowing on my forehead with the back of my hand. Compressing lightning wasn't easy. It required considerable concentration.

'There's a bit of a limit with my current skills.'

I turned to look at Diona and Katia.

"Get dressed. The curse might come again."

"Baron-nim! W-what should we do? If the demon runs wild, the Hisfield Gate will suffer great damage!"

Diona approached me. It was an intention to ask for help.

I bit her large breast with my mouth.

"Haaak?!"

When I took my mouth off, my teeth marks were clearly left on her chest.

"Didn't you understand me? Get dressed. And guide me."

"Guide... where to...?"

"To Jerdin."

While they were getting dressed, I took out the walkie-talkie from the inventory and gave orders to Yuria and Floe. I told Yuria to eliminate the wandering curses and capture or kill the demons using authority.

I told Floe to prepare in an armed state. If Jerdin moves the soldiers and knights, the knightesses will step forward and engage in battle.

'If Jerdin was targeting Diona instead of me, there wouldn't be any need for soldiers or knights to move... The demon Mundudu might be with Jerdin.'

Yuria stood on the roof of Hisfield Castle.

Deep night past midnight. The surroundings were dark. There wasn't a single light in the city. There were a few lights in the castle, but that light didn't shine on Yuria.

The shadow at her feet wriggled. When she spread her palm, a glass bottle popped out of the shadow and landed on her palm.

What was in the glass bottle was black powder. It wasn't real powder. It only looked like powder.

She opened the glass bottle cap. Black powder, impure mana, leaked out of the glass bottle and disappeared on the wind.

Impure mana.

This mana, also called contaminated mana, is very much liked by demons. This is because impure mana is like the source of demons. Demons could recover their bodies with impure mana and become stronger.

Yuria sprinkled impure mana into the air to lure out the demons. Her intention was successful.

"Good! It smells good!"

From a window on the lower side of the castle, a demon flapped its wings and flew into the sky.

"......"

Yuria narrowed her eyes and identified the demon. According to her master's words, the demon's name was Mundudu, the Black Serpent Demon.

Mundudu was a monkey with a pair of bat wings. His two arms were particularly long and drooped down. Mundudu looked at Yuria with red eyes and grinned. Between his lips were sharp, awl-like teeth.

"For a human, you know quite a bit about demons."

Mundudu knew that impure mana was bait to lure him out. Even though he knew, he revealed himself directly. Impure mana was worth it, and he didn't think for a moment that he would be defeated by a mere human.

"I'll ask for confirmation. Are you Mundudu?"

"You know my name? You've got the basics."

Mundudu's red eyes glowed. Black robes, curses, appeared around him. A total of five. They fluttered their robes and spun around Yuria.

"That's right."

"......Hmm?"

Mundudu tilted his head to the side. He asked Yuria as if he couldn't understand.

"You're angry. Why are you angry? Did I kill a human related to you? Or do you hate demons?"

On the surface, Yuria hadn't changed at all. But Mundudu had a small talent for instinctively sensing the emotions of others. It was a trivial talent that wasn't authority or anything.

"Your curse threatened Master-nim."

Yuria held a dagger in her hand.

"Master-nim? Were you someone's servant?"

"Did you think you could live comfortably after targeting Master-nim? I promise you. You will hate the fact that you exist."

"Hehe. Humans are foolish. They are swayed by emotions and fail to grasp the difference in level. Consider it an honor to die surrounded by my curses."

The five curses rushed towards Yuria all at once.

Yuria didn't react. But her shadow was different. Her shadow stretched out in all directions and then soared upwards, blowing away the curses themselves without a trace.

"Uh... uh?"

A dumb voice came out of Mundudu's mouth.

"What did I just see?"

Just as he was about to use the curse again in a situation he didn't understand. Yuria was walking towards Mundudu, stepping on the air.

Mundudu couldn't understand again. It's not magic. And humans don't have wings. He couldn't understand at all how she could walk in the air without falling.

Yuria swung her dagger from a certain distance. From top to bottom. The dagger honestly cut through the air.

"Are you making fun of me right now? Presumpt... Heo-eok?!"

Mundudu's right arm was cut off and fell down. The hard demon's body, which couldn't be scratched by sword energy, was cut so easily.

"C-crazy! You cut space? That's impossible! A mere human without authority...!"

The curse that came out of Mundudu's body targeted Yuria's front. A shadow soared from the ground, swallowed the curse, and disappeared.

She took a step into the air and swung her dagger. This time, Mundudu's left arm and wings were cut off. Mundudu, a high-ranking demon, could float in the air using magic even without wings.

'This is wrong! It's wrong by a long shot!'

It was to the point where he doubted whether the opponent was human. Mundudu quickly made a decision. The opponent is an absurdly skilled person who uses the power to cut space. Fighting was crazy. Mundudu descended to the ground and ran away from Yuria.

"You can't go."

Shadow chains shot from a place where the moonlight didn't reach grabbed Mundudu's body. Mundudu, caught in the shadows, became desperate.

"Contract! Let's make a contract, human! Is there someone you want to kill? I can kill them for sure with my curse without you being suspected!"

"That mere curse. I can do it without borrowing your power."

"What?"

Mundudu felt his body getting heavier. Not only that. Suddenly, pain surged. Pain that couldn't be compared to when his arms and wings were cut off.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaaaak!"

The demon's scream was terrible. But the scream was swallowed by the shadows and didn't spread.

"Jerdiiin! Call me! Call me right now!!"

"It's useless. No one can hear your voice except me. And I don't allow summons by contract."

The shadows bound Mundudu even more.

"Master-nim will be pleased that the second livestock has come into my hands. That's a good thing. He will definitely praise me. And maybe..."

A brief smile appeared on Yuria's lips and disappeared.
