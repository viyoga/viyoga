Necromancer Barbaroax's intentions were obvious.

Buying time.

He was trying to buy time so that the demons could sacrifice themselves to speed up the Great Demon's resurrection ritual.

,I'll have to play along appropriately.'

Undead rise from the ground. Most of them are low-level undead, skeletons. They're nothing to me, but there are thousands of them.

,Undead give less experience than the Corrupted.'

Most undead are weaker than the Corrupted. But experience is originally about gathering dust to level up. I kicked a nearby skeleton. The skeleton's body shattered like pottery dropped on the floor.

,It's a waste to use the Holy Spear on these kinds of guys.'

I put the Holy Spear in my inventory and took out the greatsword, gripping it in my hand.

The skeletons, with nothing but bones, charge at me. As low in intelligence as they are, they had no fear of me. I spun around, swinging the greatsword like a top. The skeletons break like reeds, and bone fragments scatter into the air.

,Come, my servant. Defeat my enemy."

Barbaroax thumped the ground with his skull staff. His magic spreads. Some of the skeletons reacted. Hundreds of skeletons gathered in one place, compressed, and began to take the form of a human wearing armor.

I punched away a charging skeleton, watching it.

,...Not an ordinary undead.'

I pretended not to know, even though I knew what it was.

,The paladin of the previous Valentier was great. Character and skill. Nothing was lacking. But he was foolish. He dared to challenge the Great Demon Suton-nim without knowing his place. I received the paladin's bones from Suton-nim. The pile of bones in front of me, and in front of you, is the paladin of the previous Valentier."

,You don't hesitate to do disgusting things.'

,I sometimes feel disgusted by what I do. Mostly when I'm fiddling with corpses. But look at my completed army! Your disgust will soon be fear of me! You too will become part of this army!"

,You called it the Plains of Death? You will sleep here forever with your bones."

I unleash Holy Aura.

The undead that were approaching me from all directions were swept away at once.

Thump Thump Thump (Kung Kung Kung).

The Skull Knight, who was a paladin in life, approaches. Made up of hundreds of skeletons, it weighs quite a bit. Its defense also seems formidable. The Skull Knight moves, pushing away the skeletons in front of it like a tank.

As I raised my greatsword and took a fighting stance, the Skull Knight also raised a bone greatsword and took a fighting stance. The Skull Knight's body, seen up close, was 2.5 times larger than me.

The Skull Knight swung its sword first. It was a clean strike, unbelievable for an undead. However.

,A Flash.'

[Using Flash (Instant). Remaining stacks: 6]

I narrowly avoided the Skull Knight's attack and launched a counterattack. My golden sword struck the Skull Knight's neck. I intended to cut it off, but... the Skull Knight's body was tougher than expected.

It immediately counterattacks. It was a counterattack possible because it was an undead that doesn't feel pain. I kicked off the ground and retreated.

,The previous paladin... Judging by his movements, he certainly has excellent swordsmanship. Most ordinary holy knights wouldn't be able to handle him.'

The Skull Knight swings its bone sword. I tilted my greatsword to deflect the Skull Knight's strike. The Skull Knight's sword slipped, and sparks flew everywhere.

My combat experience is different from other holy knights. Swordsmanship? I doubt there's anyone in this world who can compare to me when it comes to pure swordsmanship.

At that moment, the Skull Knight's stance, which had been exchanging blows, changed strangely. It retreated and pulled its sword back.

,I've bought enough time.'

Pajijik.

Golden lightning crackled from the blade.

Thunderbolt Style (Leitian Style) Myriad Lightning (Manrai).

Golden Myriad Lightning rotates and gathers around the greatsword.

I didn't use Thunderbolt Style often in this world. The first reason was that I could deal with demons and the Corrupted well enough with pure swordsmanship alone. I also preferred overwhelming them with power rather than technique.

The second and third reasons were that I didn't have enough divine power to use Thunderbolt Style freely, and it was inefficient in many ways to unleash Thunderbolt Style with a greatsword.

,If I need a powerful strike now, that's a different story.'

The Skull Knight swings its sword. I also swung my sword, imbued with Myriad Lightning, to match it.

Rrrrumble rumble rumble rumble! (Kwareureureureureureung!)

The sound of thunder roared. Golden lightning spread in all directions. My sword sliced through the Skull Knight's torso. Electricity started to seep into the Skull Knight's bones. The Skull Knight's evil energy was consumed by the golden lightning and vanished. Boom! The Skull Knight fell to the ground.

I climbed onto the fallen Skull Knight. I gripped the greatsword in reverse and slammed it down to finish it off.

,Barbaroax! You're next!"

I roared.

It was a shout meant to bring more skeletons immediately.

But Barbaroax, perhaps feeling differently about my shout, began to gather the skeletons instead of summoning more. The skeletons that had been piled up everywhere collapsed. All those bones flew towards Barbaroax.

,Is he planning to engage in combat directly?'

The flying bones take the shape of a giant elephant. Barbaroax's voice was heard from inside the elephant.

,I'll admit it. You are strong enough that I have to show my full power. I will crush you myself."

A large elephant, 10m tall, rushes towards me. Its long trunk was as sharp as a sword.

Facing it head-on would be insane. I immediately ran to the side, avoiding the elephant's charge. The elephant changed direction. Because it was so large and heavy, its speed slowed when it changed direction.

,This is my chance. Sprint.'

I activate the Sprint skill. It was similar to Acceleration or Flash, but different. When I used Sprint, I felt like an invisible force was pushing my body. In addition, I can do a double jump.

I couldn't get on top of the elephant with just one double jump. I kicked off the elephant's body and used double jump three times in a row to get on top of the elephant.

,It's useless!"

Barbaroax's voice echoed from inside the elephant. Immediately after, the elephant's bones began to transform. Sharp thorns shot up, aiming for me. I ran across the elephant's body, avoiding the thorns. Some of the thorns touched my body. Fortunately, they weren't powerful enough to pierce my armor in one go.

I didn't just run away. I dodged the thorns and struck the elephant's body with my greatsword. The elephant struggled, running across the dark ground, but maintaining my balance was easy.

Bang! (Kkaang!) Bang! (Kkang!) Bang! (Kkang!)

I strike down, and strike down again.

At some point, cracks began to appear in the bones. The thorns became sharper and tightened around my body. Bone arms soared upwards, trying to strike me.

,Damn it! Just die already! Why aren't the curses working?!"

,Curse? Did you curse me?"

,Yes, yes. A curse of madness! A curse that makes you see hallucinations! A curse that causes headaches! A curse that rots your whole body! I used every curse I could on you! Why aren't the curses working?! Please, tell me!"

,Do you think I'll tell you?"

Among the skills I have, there was no skill that resisted curses without ignoring them altogether.

I had a guess.

Sacred Mind.

,I knew it was having a big impact on me... Did it also have the effect of completely ignoring curses?'

Whatever it was, it wasn't a bad situation.

Bang bang bang! (Bang Bang Bang!)

I continued to strike down with the greatsword as if swinging a hammer.

The fruits of my labor came to fruition.

The cracked bones shattered.

The inside of the elephant was empty, unlike its solid exterior. Instead, Barbaroax was located at its center.

Barbaroax looked at me in awe.

,Amazing. I never thought it would come to this."

,You're still pretending to be relaxed until the end.'

I looked down and gauged the distance. I can reach him with a double jump. I can kill him instantly using Flash. But the reason I didn't do it right away was to buy time.

,The Great Demon's resurrection ritual must succeed.'

,You have a curious expression. Are you curious about why I'm trying to resurrect Suton-nim ahead of schedule?"

,...Are you willing to answer?"

Barbaroax laughs as I respond. He also had the goal of buying time.

,Suton-nim is trying to put an end to this boring war."

,Are you saying he's going to attack the Order? No matter how great a demon he is, he can't do it alone. If that were possible, the Order would have disappeared from this world already."

,What if he's not alone? What if the Great Demons who were guarding their territories attack the Order all at once?"

,...Nonsense. Do you think I don't know the nature of demons? They would kill their own kind without hesitation for their own desires."

,Isn't that the same for humans? Nevertheless, humans unite when they need to. There's no reason why demons can't unite. The plan to attack the Order is already underway."

,...."

It's a lie.

The plan hasn't even started. The full-scale plan will begin after the Great Demon Suton is resurrected. And the plan will never be executed. Because Suton will die by my hand.

,...That plan. Tell me about it in detail."

,Hahahaha! You're curious after all, aren't you? But what a pity! The resurrection ritual has just finished! I don't need to play with you anymore!!"

Barbaroax shouts. The bone elephant collapses. The collapsed bones begin to transform into the shape of swords.

,Good. It means I don't have to listen to your disgusting words anymore."

[Using Flash (Instant). Remaining stacks: 5]

I leaped using Flash.

[Using Flash (Instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

I then use Sprint. A double leap was formed, and I closed the distance in an instant. The greatsword pierces Barbaroax's chest accurately.

,How can you have such speed...!"

,It's probably because you're too slow."

I raised my sword upwards. His head split in half.

He and my body fall to the ground at the same time.

I stood still, doing nothing.

Kung.

I felt a slight impact on my back. I got up. It was inside the Great Hall. In front of me was Barbaroax's corpse, sprawled out.
