[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 3]

"(Get lost.)"

Cracks appeared on Dullibard's face, which had been full of composure. He endured the pain and screamed.

"Keeeuuuuk! (Tornado!)"

"(Wind, hear me!) (Fire, hear me!) (Steel, hear me!) (Frost, hear me!) (Kill him!)"

Wind blew, flames soared, steel swords fell from the sky, and frost gripped my legs. Those phenomena were filled with murderous intent toward me.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

'I'm sure of it. He can see my accelerated movement using Instant. Is it because of the Dragon Eyes?'

"(Press down.)"

Gravity intensified, pressing my body down.

I endured and focused my concentration.

Lightning Sky Style (Raiden-ryu) Ultimate Skill (Gokugi) Scattered Lightning (Sanrai).

Thousands of red electric currents flashed, traveled through space in all directions, and then disappeared. Not a single bit of mana remained in the surrounding area.

The dragon's so-called Dragon Words were, in the end, a reaction of mana. With the surrounding mana temporarily sealed, he couldn't use Dragon Words.

"Transfer!"

Dullibard shouted urgently. But there was no reaction.

"...Damn it!"

"Aura Blade... It's not working?!"

A red tail struck my abdomen. I flew backward, somersaulted in the air, and landed on the ground.

"No, your attack worked. My neck hurts. Even though it's a dragon's scale, it seems it can't perfectly block the Aura Blade."

Dullibard, who had become a Dragonkin, a combination of human and dragon, grabbed his bleeding neck with his left hand.

"(Heal.)"

The Dragon Word activated, and his bleeding disappeared. The effect of Scattered Lightning was over.

Dullibard grabbed the throne and stood up. His body was gradually growing larger with each passing second. He was transforming into a complete dragon form, not just a Dragonkin.

Into a Red Dragon with red scales.

-Good. It's incomparably more comfortable than in human form.

His voice echoed through the space. He was speaking through mana, not his vocal cords.

-(Press down.)

-Now that I've become a dragon, why start from a hatchling? I had to sacrifice my entire clan to skip being a hatchling... but it was worth it. Haha. It feels good. Eugene Pruker. You know what? I have two hearts. That's how I evolved.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Empty Path (虛道).

Pajijik. I used lightning to create a foothold in the air. I stepped on the foothold and went upwards.
