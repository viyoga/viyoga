-Sophia's Restaurant

The program 「Sophia's Restaurant」 is a famous talk show program.

It's a simple program where a famous MC named Sophia invites guests to a restaurant and has a talk. The viewership rating is less than 10%, but entertainment industry enthusiasts consistently watch the program.

Elena ended up appearing on this program.

,Today, we have Ms. Tia from Pobeutz, who is heating up Area 6,700!"

Sophia greeted Elena with a bright face. Elena looked around. Sophia's Restaurant is a restaurant with a slightly dark atmosphere. It's a bit luxurious and has a cozy vibe.

,Hoo. The atmosphere isn't bad."

,Aren't you going to welcome me?"

A woman next to Elena complained. Sophia smiled brightly and waved her hand.

,Of course, Sophia's Restaurant welcomes you too, Yuria-ssi. But aren't you coming a bit too often?"

,You keep calling me, so I come, what else can I do."

Yeon Yuria.

She, the leader of the top idol girl group Pink Love, entered with Elena. She naturally sat down at the table, preempting a spot that was well-exposed to the camera.

,Smiling like a puppy, but acting like a fox."

I was impressed by Yeon Yuria's actions. Should I call her a trendy celebrity after all? Every single action felt experienced.

Elena didn't sit down right away. She walked around the restaurant, looking around. The main PD was flustered, but didn't say anything. It's because Elena's attitude, as if she's critiquing the restaurant, is very natural.

,Her gestures and movements have class. Should I call her a high-ranking noble...."

In reality, Elena herself didn't seem to be acting with a deliberate intention. What caught Elena's attention was the wine refrigerator.

,Ms. Tia. Are you interested in wine?"

,Wine is the vitality of life. There are quite a few good wines here. Can I take one out? Or is it not allowed because of the broadcast?"

,It's okay. It's the first time a guest has looked for wine... but our broadcast pursues naturalness. But you shouldn't get too drunk. It's still a broadcast. I'll get you a wine glass. Yuria-ssi, you'll drink too, right?"

,I don't know much about wine... but Ms. Tia is reacting like that, so I'm very curious. I'll try it once."

,There's white wine from Area 202. It has a refreshing taste, so most people who are trying wine for the first time like it."

Elena took the white wine and headed to the table. Sophia's movements became busy.

,Oh, just a moment. I wonder where the wine opener is...."

,It's okay. You don't need an opener."

Elena flicked her finger at the wine. A magic circle appeared and began to envelop the wine bottle. When she used magic, the filming set stirred.

I, who was watching the recording with the main PD, quietly clicked my tongue.

,I told her not to use magic if possible...."

It wasn't a bad thing. The element of being a magician would make Elena stand out more.

,If she had used magic in a better place than here, it would have garnered more attention."

Elena's face, as she opened the wine with magic, was more serious than ever.

She poured the white wine into the other woman's wine glass as well. And she takes a sip without hesitation.

,It's a decent wine."

Elena smiled, seemingly pleased.

,It's delicious!"

,Hoo.... To think there was wine like this. If I had known it was such a good wine, I would have tasted it sooner... PD-nim! Why didn't you tell me there was wine like this until now?!"

Sophia shouted at the main PD. The main PD was flustered and shook his head. The camera captured all of that.

,Yuria-ssi. What kind of food should I make for you? The kimchi fried rice you had last time?"

,Kimchi fried rice... I do like it... but today I want to eat a dish that Sophia-eonni recommends."

,Then how about ramen?"

,......Really?"

,Hehe. I'm just kidding. Actually, I prepared a dish because Ms. Tia was coming today."

,For Ms. Tia... I'm jealous."

,I made a dish for Yuria-ssi last time, didn't I? Let Ms. Tia have it today."

Tia, who was tilting her wine glass, was calm even after hearing that it was a dish for her. Her expression didn't seem expectant at all.

That's understandable, since she ate Yuria's cooking almost every meal while living in the dorm. An ordinary dish wouldn't satisfy her tongue.

,What kind of dish is it? I'll say it in advance... I absolutely hate things like ramen."

,Hehe. I can't feed ramen to Ms. Tia, who is from a noble family. The dish I prepared is coq au vin."

,Coq au vin. Chicken cooked in wine, huh."

,Oh my. You knew."

,It's one of my favorite foods. I eat it often, so most coq au vin dishes don't satisfy my tongue."

,Ah. I'm getting nervous for no reason."

Sophia started cooking, and the talk began in earnest.

I was worried that Yeon Yuria would steal the spotlight before the broadcast started. But that worry was unfounded. The center of the talk was Elena. Elena's presence filled the restaurant. She leisurely tilted her wine glass and dominated this restaurant, this program.

,I never dreamed that Ms. Tia was a magician. Since when have you been learning magic?"

,I learned it since I was very young. My father was a magician. Well, it's a common magician family."

,It's said that magicians easily become nobles... was that true?"

,It used to be like that. These days, it's impossible to become a noble just because you're a magician. You have to achieve accomplishments or build merits to prove your abilities to become a noble."

,Area 6,700 doesn't have nobles or anything like that. You know that, right?"

,Well, that's probably the case now."

,Yuria-ssi is from South Korea, right? I heard that country doesn't have nobles...."

,Ah, that's right. Korea doesn't have nobles or kings. It's similar to Area 6,700."

The dish was completed.

Yeon Yuria, who took a bite of the coq au vin, opened her eyes wide in surprise.

,It's delicious! It's my first time eating it, but it's incredibly delicious!"

Yeon Yuria exclaimed.

Sophia's cooking skills were impeccable. I heard that if she hadn't become a celebrity, she would have become a chef.

,Elena! Say it's delicious!"

I looked at Elena's face and prayed in my heart. Elena happened to be looking this way. Did she read my mind? I felt like her eyes were shining.

,It's so-so."

,......."

The atmosphere instantly turned cold at Elena's evaluation.

,You... didn't like it?"

Sophia is smiling, but her lips are trembling. She said earlier that she had prepared the coq au vin for three days and nights.

,I didn't say it was bad. It's just ordinary."

,Wait a minute! That means it's bad! This delicious food is bad?! Are you doing this because of your noble concept? Don't force it!"

Yeon Yuria jumped up from her seat and glared at Elena. Her momentum was quite fierce. But Elena snorted as if it were ridiculous.

,Forcing it? I'm just saying my honest impressions."

,You ate it, so you know! Sophia-eonni's cooking isn't something to be ignored! Who are you to ignore Sophia-eonni's cooking?!"

Yeon Yuria pointed her finger at Elena.

The reason why Yeon Yuria is overreacting like this... is because of Sophia. Sophia, who is one of the most influential people in Area 6,700, is trying to make her an ally.

I called the main PD.

,PD-nim."

,Ah, Sung PD-nim. It's okay. We can just edit it out. Let's just watch. If it seems dangerous, we'll step in and stop them. This much is okay. Sophia-ssi will step in soon."

,The situation is dangerous right now."

,Yuria-ssi seems fierce, but she won't use her hands. Don't worry."

,No, the one I said was dangerous isn't Tia, but Yuria-ssi."

,Yes?"

I warned the main PD.

I didn't intend to step in directly. It seems like the main PD wants Yeon Yuria and Elena to fight. I decided to watch for now.

Elena looked at the standing Yeon Yuria with narrowly opened eyes.

,I just said what I felt. I never ignored Sophia's cooking."

,You said eonni's cooking was so-so!"

,Yes. So-so at the level of a top-class chef. At the level of being in charge of the kitchen of a great noble family."

,Is that... a compliment?"

,Well, it depends on how the person receiving it takes it. But... how long are you going to keep pointing your finger?"

,......!!"

Elena's momentum fills the restaurant. Her mana is quietly, but heavily, pressing down on the space. Most of the ordinary people here are overwhelmed by Elena and stiffened.

Elena took a sip of white wine and released her momentum. Yeon Yuria slumped back into her chair, and Sophia barely held on, grabbing the table.

,I'm sorry. I'm a noble now, but I'm also an idol. I momentarily forgot that fact. Well, I guess you won't be satisfied with just an apology."

Elena flicks her finger. A magic circle is drawn in the air, and a plate of food pops out from there.

,This is coq au vin made by our family's chef and coq au vin made by Yuria. Try it once."

,...Ms. Tia, do you carry food in a subspace?"

,Subspace is convenient. It has the advantage of being able to eat food whenever you want if you put it in there. It's especially great when you crave late-night snacks."

Sophia, who had stiffened, relaxed her expression at Elena's banter.

,Subspace.... If you have that, you don't need a separate refrigerator. I'm jealous. Yuria-ssi is Ms. Tia's colleague, right? First, shall we try the coq au vin made by Yuria-ssi?"

,No, try the one made by the family's chef first."

,Yes. Let's do that."

A look of surprise spreads across Sophia's face after taking a bite.

,......The coq au vin I made is literally so-so. Can I know the name of the chef?"

,It's a secret."

,......It seems like they're so famous that it would be difficult if they were revealed."

,Well, that's right."

,Next is Yuria-ssi's... gasp...!"

Sophia, who ate Yuria's coq au vin, screamed without realizing it. She trembled and kept moving her hands.

,No way...! Cooking at this level...! This is already beyond the realm of humans! Did Yuria-ssi really make this?!"

,I have no reason to lie to you."

,It's amazing! Compared to Yuria-ssi's coq au vin... my cooking is trash."

,So, Sophia-eonni?!"

,Yuria-ssi. Yuria-ssi, you should try it too. I can't eat something this good by myself."

,R, really that delicious?"

,You'll regret it for the rest of your life if you don't eat it."

Yeon Yuria ate Yuria's coq au vin. Yeon Yuria opened her eyes wide and then frantically lunged at the coq au vin.

I sighed as I watched that scene.

,The atmosphere has eased. A real accident almost happened... good thing."

But it shouldn't end here.

,PD-nim. Please edit out the scene from earlier."

,Edit it out? That's a great scene. It was resolved without any problems."

,I said edit it out."

I said it with murderous intent. The main PD flinched and nodded.

,Ye, yes. I understand."

This incident can just be passed off as a happening. But as long as there's even a slight possibility of controversy, I can't just let it pass.

,Pobeutz doesn't have time. There's no reason to choose a dangerous path."
