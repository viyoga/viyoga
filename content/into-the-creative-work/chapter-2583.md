I arrived at the monster city.

It wasn't a ridiculously large scale for a city. It was about 10 times the size of a monster village. Like the village, it was protected by magic. Unlike the village, the subject of the magic was a bit different.

,The giant monster tree embedded in the center of the city is protecting the city.'

One-Horned Black Tree.

Its appearance was that of a giant black tree, but there was a human face formation on its trunk. A single horn protruded from its forehead.

That's not the only unique thing. This bastard's roots were connected to the spirit vein. It could draw and use the spiritual energy of the spirit vein. It gave up something in return.

,General? What's wrong?"

When I was silent, On Gyowi, who was next to me, asked.

,The One-Horned Black Tree is using magic in the center of the city. It has noticed that we have arrived and is preparing for battle. The plan to enter the city as it is and sweep it away with a surprise attack is scrapped. There is a greater possibility that we will be attacked by the clones of that bastard planted throughout the city."

I have to build up achievements. I couldn't use soldiers like water. If the soldiers are annihilated here, my reputation will drop, and my achievements will be reduced.

,Then are you giving up?"

,No. I'm changing the plan. You guys can feel the signs of magic protecting the city from this distance, right? You guys surround the city and wait until I kill the One-Horned Black Tree. The moment the One-Horned Black Tree dies and the magic is released, revealing the city, advance into the city and massacre the monsters."

,Understood. I will follow the General-nim's command."

I dropped the soldiers to the ground. The soldiers moved in an orderly fashion. They used their light footwork to quickly surround the city. It was possible because it was not as big as the imperial city.

I flew to the center of the city with the Vajra Thunderbolt, which had anti-magic power, in the lead. I successfully entered the city.

The One-Horned Black Tree was naturally not still. It moved thousands of branches and used magic. In an instant, several spells were created and flew towards me. From simply throwing fireballs to curse-type magic that weakens the body.

,Is it splitting the incantation and entrusting it to the branches? It's a kind of division of labor. Since it can use near-infinite spiritual energy by plugging its roots into the spirit vein... I can call it a non-stop magic factory.'

It looks incredibly strong when you look at it this way, but it's actually different. Even if it's a magic factory, its size was small. There is a limit to the output.

The magic flying towards me right now is blocked by my protective aura just before it touches my body. The power alone is by no means weak. The One-Horned Black Tree was at least at the Middle Stage of the Three Flowers Gathered at the Crown realm, so it couldn't be weak.

,In addition, the helmet I'm wearing, the Cloud Dragon Pearl, protects me from magic. It can't neutralize the magic itself, but it weakens the magic that harms me.'

I move forward through hundreds of spells. Of course, there were a few spells that looked really dangerous. But I can't fool my Lightning Eye. As soon as I grasped the spell, I intercepted it with the Vajra Thunderbolt and the Hundred Battle Spear.

As the distance got closer, the One-Horned Black Tree opened its mouth.

,...You're grasping all the spells I'm pouring out. How the hell... Is it that eye? What do you see in that eye?!"

,I can see everything about you. Including the Light Burst magic you're preparing."

,You came knowing that? You arrogant bastard! The power of the Light Burst is absolute at this distance. Die!!"

The horn of the One-Horned Black Tree shines. The light was divided into dozens of strands and then merged again, pouring towards me.

Lightning Heaven Style Myriad Lightning.

The rotating lightning that appeared in the air blocked the Light Burst. The essence of the Light Burst was, after all, a mass of energy. It was offset by the Myriad Lightning, which can also be said to be energy.

,My Light Burst magic is blocked by that kind of spiritual energy...?!"

,I've already grasped your magic. I can immediately see which parts are weak. I can see the strategy in real time."

Pajijijik!

The lightning emitted from my body pierced through the magic pouring out from the One-Horned Black Tree. It was possible because I had grasped the essence of the magic.

,...You really grasped all my magic. It's an absurd skill. That eye is like... the eye of the famous Great Sage Equaling Heaven! How did a human like you get that eye, that magic?!"

,Do you think you'd tell me?"

,It doesn't matter! I'll kill you directly, devour you, and find the clue to that eye...!"

The floor rumbles.

The roots of the One-Horned Black Tree that had been dug into the ground all soared upwards at once. Thousands of roots filled with energy rushed towards me.

Pajijik!

Black thunder flashed.

I stomp the ground with my raised right foot.

Kung!

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Heavenly Demesne Steps.

The energy wave spread in all directions, dominating the space. The roots rushing towards me stopped in their tracks.

A brief gap forcibly created in a desperate situation.

I spread my arms wide. I bent all ten fingers. Pajijijik! Black lightning sparked from the tips of my fingers. I grabbed the intangible space and rotated my body as it was.

Cheonma Shingong Revolving Demonic Dragon Heaven and Earth Revolution.

The space dominated by the Demonic Heavenly Demesne Steps twisted and rotated. The One-Horned Black Tree in the space floated into the sky and fell, and its roots were forcibly torn apart in the air.

,Kuaaaaaaack!"

Looking at the falling One-Horned Black Tree, I reached out to the sky.

,Meteor Sword.'

A giant meteor sword is created in the sky in an instant. I grabbed the meteor sword with my will and swung it at the One-Horned Black Tree. The blade of the meteor sword split the One-Horned Black Tree and the city and disappeared.

The corpse of the One-Horned Black Tree, split in half, twitched for a moment before turning into dust and disappearing.

,I just wanted to become a god through cultivation... Ah... How resentful..."

In the place where that bastard's corpse was, a black branch with enormous spiritual energy was left alone.

I picked up the branch and examined it.

,Instead of an inner core, this is what's left. It contains spiritual energy instead of demonic power... It seems to be the effect of planting roots in the spirit vein and sucking it up. It absorbed the spiritual energy of the spirit vein, but it didn't have the ability to use it.'

It's not an elixir. But it's an excellent magical artifact material. In addition, it contains enormous spiritual energy, so it seems it can be used in various places.

I put it in my inventory.

,There are a total of five disciples of the Hexagon Demon King. One-Horned, Two-Horned, and Four-Horned were killed by me, and Three-Horned was killed by my predecessor, General Mujin, so only Five-Horned is left. There is less information about Five-Horned than Hexagon. Where the hell is he?'

I want to deal with him before fighting the Hexagon Demon King if possible. I wanted to avoid a situation where that bastard would barge in during the battle and I would have to face both of them at the same time.

It was then. Suddenly, a presence appeared next to me. It was too late when I realized it. A dagger with killing intent in itself aims for my neck.

[Crisis Detection is activated. Remaining stacks: 7]

A moment is automatically activated. Thanks to that, I was able to avoid it. I quickly pulled my body back and checked the enemy.

It was a woman with five horns and ten eyes. I clicked my tongue as I looked at the woman's name.

I almost died. If I didn't have Crisis Detection, I would have really died once. I was sweating a little because there had never been such a dangerous enemy. Above all, I was activating Lightning Eye. But I didn't notice it until she swung her dagger.

,Five-Horned Ten-Eyed. Were you hiding nearby? How do you feel about watching your fellow disciple die? Did you like it?"

,He is a pitiful person. So at least... as a fellow sajeo-nim who serves the same teacher, I will avenge him."

,Even if her realm is higher than mine, she escaped from the sight of Lightning Eye in an instant?'

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Wave.

I couldn't detect it even after spreading electromagnetic waves.

Various hypotheses come to mind in my head. The first thing that came to mind was teleportation. The case where Five-Horned Ten-Eyed appears and ambushes me with teleportation from outside my recognition range. This is impossible. There's no way Lightning Eye wouldn't be able to grasp the signs of teleportation.

Hypnosis or illusion? As long as I have Absolute Spirit, I can't manipulate my mind. It's impossible to deceive my eyes since I have Lightning Eye.

,This woman's ability is not simple magic. It seems to be an ability that uses mental images... What the hell is it?'

Fortunately, I had one more chance.

[Crisis Detection is activated. Remaining stacks: 2]

[Activating Genius Time for 10 seconds.]
