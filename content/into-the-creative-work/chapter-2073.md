I listened to what the store owner had to say.

It seemed there were some additional events leading up to the territorial dispute with Count Mork, a great noble of the Empire, but as a result, the territorial war was to be held under the mediation of the Empire and the Republic.

What's at stake in this territorial war isn't territory. The distance between Count Mork's territory and Veroprin was too great to wager land. Even if he were to gain territory, governance would be impossible.

What Count Mork wants are population, food, and war supplies such as weapons and armor.

What Park Suho wants is population. If he wins, he gains a population of 50,000. Conversely, if he loses the territorial war, he loses a population of 50,000 and must hand over war supplies. Since Count Mork has the justification, Park Suho has more to lose.

,Park Suho's abilities grow stronger depending on the size of the city. The current population of Veroprin is 150,000... if a population of 50,000 flows in at once, Park Suho will become incredibly strong.'

Park Suho might become an S-class Hunter faster than me. Thinking about it made me feel a little threatened. Park Suho becoming stronger than me?

'I just imagined it for a second, and it already pisses me off.'

I didn't want to have to watch Park Suho's every move because he was stronger than me.

'That Park Suho, that bastard, has some shady tendencies. They say a person's true nature comes out when they gain power... if he becomes stronger than me, he might reveal his dirty true colors.'

And my stomach hurt too.

I'm toiling away in the game world, earning points to get stronger, while that bastard is stirring up those primitive guys and handing out kimchi and samgyeopsal to get stronger easily.

'What a freeloader.'

I'm not exactly jealous, but it twists my gut, so I decided to interfere.

,So, mercenary. Are you going to participate in the war? If you are, I'll introduce you to a trustworthy employer.'

,Does a trustworthy employer even exist?'

,We have our mayor. I heard he's hiring mercenaries on a large scale this time. If you're confident in your skills, you should apply too. You can make a solid fortune.'

,...It's hard to decide right here and now. War. Getting involved in someone else's city's war doesn't seem like it'll end well.'

,You're much more cautious than other mercenaries.'

,Is that a compliment?'

,Of course. But I've seen many people regret being too cautious.'

,Okay. Thanks for the advice.'

I chugged my beer and went outside the tavern. I decided to check out the atmosphere of Count Mork's territory and make a plan.

'I need information to figure out how to intervene.'

Count Mork's territory was a week away from Veroprin. That's based on walking, not by carriage. It's said to be straight southwest.

'There are no merchant groups or mercenaries heading towards Count Mork's territory. I guess that's obvious since there's a war.'

I have to go alone. If I take out my bike and ride, I could probably arrive within three days, but... Park Suho would 100% notice. To avoid being detected by Park Suho, I had to cosplay as a local.

'Not a mercenary. I get the feeling I'm being looked down on in subtle ways.'

I heard that mercenaries aren't treated well in the Empire. That's because there are knights who are considered the Empire's military power.

'I should do some knight cosplay.'

To conceal my identity, I had to wear equipment different from Storm Breaker or Hwaryeon Bido, and refrain from using my conspicuous lightning abilities.

'I have so much masterfully crafted armor and swords made by dwarves that they're just rotting away. Let's get a horse or something.'

You can't leave out horses when it comes to the Empire's knights.

Horses are expensive. Even more so in this world, where they replace cars as a means of transportation. But I wasn't particularly worried about money. I'm the man who secretly controls the Psychotic Elf Territory. I had more than enough money to buy a horse.

,Looking to buy a horse? The timing isn't good. We're not selling horses right now.'

,Are you ignoring me because I'm a mercenary?'

,That's not it, so calm down. You're a mercenary, so you understand. War is just around the corner. The mayor has rented all the horses here.'

,Rented? Not bought?'

,Isn't war a battle of money? We decided to rent out the horses for the sake of the mayor and this beautiful city.'

,Wouldn't it be a huge loss if the horses die in the war?'

,We can afford that much. I want this city to win.'

I scratched my head. He was just a citizen living in the city, yet he was overly loyal. Does Park Suho give him something special? Or is this city just that special?

After that, I wandered around the city. I didn't necessarily have to buy a horse from the stable. I could just give a merchant or mercenary who owns a horse extra money and buy it.

'These citizens are a bit strange. They're preparing for war on their own, without the mayor even telling them to?'

I grabbed someone at random and asked why they were preparing for war so diligently, and they replied that they liked this city.

'I can't understand it. It's even unpleasant.'

Anyway, I got a horse through a merchant. It was a decent horse, except that it was a bit old. I rode the horse towards Count Mork's territory. Of course, it was a mare.

Nothing special happened along the way, except for monsters popping out. The monsters were sliced and diced by my hand.

'It only takes three days to arrive on horseback. Should I change my clothes since I'm getting closer?'

I changed from comfortable clothes into dwarf-made armor. Armor that anyone could tell was a masterpiece and brand new. I thought about putting armor on the horse too, but I stopped. It was clear that this old horse would get tired quickly if it wore horse armor.

I headed towards Count Mork's city more confidently than anyone. The guards who were guarding the entrance blocked my path with a nervous look.

,P, Please stop for a moment.'

,Stop? Are you daring to speak to me like that?'

As soon as I growled slightly, the guard immediately lowered his head.

,I'm sorry! G, On the orders of the Lord, we must investigate the identity of everyone without exception! Please cooperate as the territorial war with Veroprin is approaching!'

It was a bad idea to push my way in with the authority of a knight. They would react sensitively since the war was just around the corner.

,I am Aaron Daikert, son of Krom Daikert. As a wandering knight, I am wandering the Empire to find the lord of my destiny.'

Of course, it was all a lie. But the guard didn't suspect anything at all. It's a primitive world without things like resident registration cards. My identity is guaranteed by the equipment and horse I have.

,A wandering knight! Did you come to help our territory?!'

,It's not like I can't help.'

,Thank you, Sir Daikert! This friend will guide you to the Lord's castle!'

,That's okay. The Lord's castle is so big, do you think I can't go alone? You guys just guard the entrance here.'

I confidently entered the territory on horseback.

I didn't head straight to the Lord's castle and looked around the city.

It didn't have the same vibrancy as Veroprin. Most of the people's expressions were dark. Slaves wearing rags continued their hard labor. None of them blocked my path. They didn't even try to make eye contact. If our eyes happened to meet, they immediately knelt on the ground.

'It's completely different from Veroprin. Well, this is more familiar to me.'

It was similar to the territories in the [Baekhwan] world. Commoners act like commoners. Slaves act like slaves.

Unlike the Republic, the Empire was dark. But it's not just dark. The Felheim Empire gives commoners and slaves the opportunity to succeed. Power. In other words, they just have to prove their strength. As long as they become a knight by any means, their fate will be changed.

I looked around the city. It's shabby compared to Veroprin. I can't deny that. That's because Veroprin is a city that Park Suho, the lord of Veroprin, developed with modern knowledge and goods. But I liked this city.

'It's only natural for primitive bastards to live primitively. I can understand Park Suho too. The city has to develop to become stronger. But these guys will climb all over you if you treat them well.'

Rebellion because they've gotten a little smarter. Rebellion because they're not satisfied with just living comfortably and want to live even better. Rebellion because they raised taxes a little.

'It's easier to manage pigs and dogs if you raise them like pigs and dogs.'

Ttagak ttagak.

As I headed towards the Lord's castle, I felt an impure gaze from the alleyway. A gaze that wasn't friendly.

'Is it because I'm an outsider? Or because I'm a knight?'

Either way, it didn't seem like there was any need to do anything right away.

I was able to meet Count Mork easily. Usually, nobles are depicted as having greedy impressions, but Count Mork was a muscular middle-aged man with a height of 2m, closer to a warrior than a noble. The only thing about his appearance that could be considered noble was his white beard.

,Welcome to my territory. I welcome knights. Sir Daikert, I heard you're a wandering knight wandering the Empire? I know how difficult the life of a wandering knight is. I'll get straight to the point. Won't you become my knight?'

,Count-nim. I am not wandering the Empire for a peaceful life. It is to serve the true lord of my destiny someday.'

,The lord of destiny? Can't I be your lord of destiny?'

,I'm sorry, but I don't feel destiny from you, Count-nim.'

Count Mork let out a hollow laugh. The four knights around him were the ones who flared up.

,You insolent thing! How dare you insult His Excellency when you're just a wanderer?!'

,Daikert. I've never heard of that name. You're too stiff for a knight without fame.'

,Your Excellency. If you allow me, I will show that wanderer the power of the Dread Knights!'

,Your Excellency! Please leave the duel to me!'

The knights were competing to show their hostility. Did those guys really want to kill me? No. Those bastards were competing for loyalty. Count Mork was subtly enjoying it while pretending not to.

It's fun to watch the lower guys compete for loyalty.

,Everyone be quiet. Sir Daikert. I have a question. Your armor and sword are masterpieces among masterpieces, but I've never heard of your name. Who knighted you?'

,Are you suspecting me now?'

,Isn't this the kind of era we live in? I just want to be sure.'

Even in an era with many nobles, it would only be a matter of time before my identity was denied if I recklessly mentioned a noble's name. In times like this, you have to be even more brazen.

,That's unpleasant.'

I set the mood.

I put strength into my whole body and released mana around me. Count Mork's face hardened, and the nearby knights put their hands on their sword hilts. They also raised their momentum as if to oppose me.

They would know by now that my skills were extraordinary. I gathered my mana and released the tension.

,Oh, I'm sorry. I have a quick temper, so I committed an act of rudeness.'
