Duke King Kukutel. The Duke King of the Kukutel Dukedom was an Aura Master who had once been an adventurer and a mercenary. He was famous enough to be called the Mercenary King at one time. A commoner by birth, he solved the Curto Kingdom’s Calamity and, in return, received the Curto King’s youngest daughter and territory, becoming a Duke King.

In truth, he was only a Duke King in name, no different from an actual Duke. Furthermore, the Kukutel Dukedom was located in the western part of the Curto Kingdom, making it quite barren, and its diplomatic relations were severed, blocked by the Curto Kingdom. It was a situation where, without the Curto Kingdom, it could wither and die within a few years.

Kukutel himself was well aware of that fact.

‘But I am King. Even those noble bastards who ignored me because I was a commoner now bow their heads to me.’

The Dukedom could only be maintained thanks to Kukutel’s own strength.

An Aura Master. The Curto Kingdom’s greatest power. That was the reason he could receive the Curto Kingdom’s favor.

‘If my son cannot become an Aura Master before I die… then this country will eventually disappear into history.’

Then it would become a mere Duchy of the Curto Kingdom.

‘A lack of ability cannot be helped.’

For now, Kukutel himself would enjoy everything there was to enjoy as a Duke King.

Kwaaaaaaaang!

A thunderous boom, like a bolt from the blue, rang out. The startled Duke King rushed out to where the boom was heard.

What he saw was a collapsed castle gate, and a single spear proudly stuck in its debris. A letter was impaled on the spear shaft. A faint blue Aura remained on the spear. Someone had struck Aura into the spear and thrown it at the castle gate.

“What crazy Aura Master dares…!”

The Duke King burst into rage. But if one looked closely, cold sweat was running down his face. An Aura Master capable of such a feat was at least intermediate level or higher. There were only a few such individuals, including Kukutel himself.

“Yo-Your Highness…!”

His subordinates, flustered, merely stared. The soldiers and knights guarding the castle gate had been caught in the attack and died.

“Don’t be flustered. Bring that spear to me.”

He commanded the knights. It would damage his prestige to move directly as a king. The knights nervously brought the spear that had been stuck in the debris. Fortunately, there was no trick to the spear.

He tore off the letter and unfolded it. His expression, upon checking the contents, hardened at an incredible speed.

“Summon the vassals! Right now!”

The Duke King headed to the audience chamber.

Tens of minutes later. The vassals gathered in the audience chamber. Kukutel, sitting on the throne, was consistently frowning.

“Your Highness. I heard the news that we received a declaration of war. Whose doing is this bastard’s, how dare they?”

“It’s something that cannot be forgiven.”

“Just give the order! The Knight Order will crush the Dukedom’s enemy!”

The vassals uttered flattering words. Kukutel, who usually liked flattery, felt no emotion even upon hearing it today. He irritably threw the letter.

“Check it yourself.”

The vassals read the letter, watching for a Hint.

「Duke King Kukutel. You have insulted me. Therefore, I, Eugene Prucus, declare war on the Kukutel Dukedom. I will attack three days later. Offering my last generosity, Duke King Kukutel, who has read this letter, surrender immediately.」

Such insolence!

No one shouted such insolence. Everyone closed their mouths and only subtly rolled their eyes.

Eugene Prucus. An existence that neither the Duke King nor his vassals could possibly be unaware of. Everyone present had heard the subtle rumor that he was the true Dominator of this continent. Indeed, Eugene Prucus was also the owner of the Korea Merchant Guild, which held the continent’s economy in its hands.

“This… doesn’t make sense. Why would Duke Prucus declare war on us? The justification doesn’t make sense either. No, in the first place, is this really a declaration of war written by Duke Prucus himself?”

The justification that he had been insulted. Duke King Kukutel was not some Crazy Bastard who couldn’t distinguish right from wrong, so there was no reason for him to insult Duke Prucus. Above all, the Prucus Duchy was very far from here. To come from the Duchy to this place, one had to cross three countries. By their common sense, war was impossible. Therefore, this declaration of war was an impersonation. It was a reasonable opinion.

“With high probability, it is a declaration of war written by Duke Prucus himself. There are only a few Aura Masters capable of collapsing a castle gate by throwing a spear. One of them is Duke Prucus himself, the continent’s foremost.”

The true continent’s foremost was Yuria, but Yuria’s existence was not officially known.

“……Your Highness. Did you truly insult Duke Prucus?”

“Bullshit! I did nothing regarding Duke Prucus! I haven’t even left the castle in recent years! You all know that well, don’t you?! Duke Prucus covets my territory!”

“…….”

Coveting this territory… isn’t that a bit off? The vassals knew well how barren the Dukedom’s land was. There were many deserts, and trees didn’t grow well. There were also many Monsters. Without the Curto Kingdom’s help, all the Dukedom’s citizens would starve to death. At least, having a good quality iron mine was an advantage.

“Your Highness. If this is truly Duke Prucus’s declaration of war… we must request support from the Curto Kingdom.”

“Realistically. Do you think the Curto Kingdom will help?”

The Great War was not forgotten, not even a little. The Duke King and his vassals clearly remembered the Great War.

The History of Duke Prucus leading an army and trampling kingdoms. The hero who collapsed the Cobalt Kingdom and killed a dragon. Recently, there was even a rumor that he had killed the Demon King and saved the world.

“If he is truly Duke Prucus… Your Highness. Surrendering is the best option.”

The vassals’ will to fight disappeared even before it began.

“Surrender? Absolutely not! I cannot hand over the Dukedom without even fighting!”

“…But if the opponent is truly Duke Prucus, there is no chance of winning.”

“Isn’t there a high possibility of impersonation?!”

“Your Highness was the one who said there’s a high possibility it’s him. By any chance, is there a way to contend with Duke Prucus and his military force?”

“There is.”

The Duke King said proudly. The vassals looked at him with shining eyes.

The Duke King raised his royal scepter. An incomparably splendid Staff, adorned with a large gem and embellished with smaller ones.

“It’s an ancient artifact I obtained when I was called the Mercenary King. I almost died four times in the ancient ruins. At that time, the Monster in the ruins…”

“An artifact! What is its ability?!”

The Duke King glared once at the vassal who had interrupted him, then spoke.

“It’s connected to a high-ranking Demon. I can ask the high-ranking Demon for a favor.”

“Your Highness. There is a rumor that Duke Prucus killed the Demon King.”

“It’s a false rumor. I know from mercenary work that most rumors are just exaggerated. What Duke Prucus killed must have been a Demon, not the Demon King. Watch closely! I will make a contract with a high-ranking Demon!”

Strike while the iron is hot.

Duke King Kukutel raised the royal scepter with a flourish and poured Mana into it.

As more Mana than expected was sucked in, his face turned pale. But there was no stopping now. Eventually, the gem embedded in the royal scepter became cloudy and connected to an otherworldly being.

-The ancient pact calls me. O human, what do you desire?

“Kill Duke Prucus for me!”

-Do you think I’d be bound by such an ancient pact?! Kwaaaaaaaak!

Kwajik!

The gem embedded in the royal scepter shattered and fell. It was natural that the connection with the Demon was severed.

The Duke King swallowed hard.

He was a monumental figure who had risen from commoner to Duke King. Every time there was a crisis, he broke through with his quick Hint and intuition. This time too, his Hint and intuition were displayed.

The Duke King declared to his vassals.

“I will surrender.”

I had intended to go alone for this war.

Kukutel was merely a recently established Dukedom. Moreover, its territory and military power were only slightly superior to an ordinary Duchy.

Merely summoning an army to conquer just the Kukutel Dukedom itself would be a loss.

‘Since I sent the declaration of war, they must be preparing. I’ll get some exercise after a long time.’

I knew that Duke King Kukutel was an Aura Master who had once been called the Mercenary King.

‘Mercenary King. Wasn’t that a Title everyone used?’

Moreover, the Title of Mercenary King doesn’t last long. If one is called the Mercenary King, it’s enough to become a Noble. So usually, they retire and become a Noble.

‘It’s time.’

I tore a spatial movement scroll.

I appeared in front of the capital’s castle gate of the Kukutel Dukedom.

‘Huh?’

I expected there to be an army, including knights and soldiers, in front of the castle gate, but there was no one. Moreover, even the castle gate was wide open.

Nobles were standing quietly there. In their center was the Duke King, wearing a small crown.

“Duke Prucus. We have no intention of fighting you. We will surrender.”

Duke King Kukutel said with a serious face. I belatedly discovered the white flag hanging on the castle.

“You’ll surrender without even fighting?”

“I do not know what insult I committed against you, but I sincerely apologize. The Dukedom is grateful for your generosity. As the victor, tell me what you desire.”

I truly hadn’t expected them to actually surrender.

I felt deflated.

‘Since it’s come to this, I’ll have to exploit these bastards.’

If I made them do unpaid labor like Slaves, they would rebel within a few months, so I would pay at least a minimum wage. Anyway, I had money to spare. Since I held the economy of this continent in my hands.

“Wasn’t your purpose to become the Dukedom’s new Master?”

“I don’t need such barren land.”

“A black liquid that burns well and smells… To desire such a useless liquid, except for the fact that it burns well. Duke Prucus is peculiar.”

The Duke King looked at me as if I were an eccentric.

In this world, oil had no value. Scientific technology had not developed enough to use it as energy.

‘…Even in reality, the value of oil is gradually falling because of Magic Stones, but for the next few decades, oil will still be the mainstream.’

I don’t immediately need oil myself. The reason I’m collecting oil is to prepare for an unknown future. Because I might enter a world that needs oil.

“The oil fields are all mine. Sign it.”

“I understand. Haha. Take all the oil fields or whatever they are.”

The Duke King signed with a smile.
