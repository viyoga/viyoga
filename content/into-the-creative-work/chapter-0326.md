"Are these the people?"

A man came in, looked at us, and spoke to the association employee.

Since it was Chinese, not Korean, the Hunters other than me and the association employee couldn't understand a word he said.

The reason I can understand Chinese is because I entered the 'Supreme Ruler of the Tai Chi' world. I naturally learned Chinese back then.

"Yes. These 10 are the Hunters who will support the evacuation of the residents of Uchuk Village."

"Thank you for your help. Please come closer to me and stay near me. We don't have time, so let's move quickly."

The association employee translated his words and told us. We gathered our equipment and approached the Chinese man.

'Is this the A-class space movement ability user from China?'

I examined the Chinese man.

His clothes were grimy, and there were dark circles under his eyes. No wonder, since China had issued a Level 5 monster disaster alert, he probably hadn't been able to sleep properly and had to work.

'A space movement ability user. Especially one who can move to other countries, is very rare.'

Looking at that face that looks like he's about to collapse, I'm not particularly envious.

"Hoo.... I will move us."

He spoke and raised his mana. And the moment I felt my body shaking, we were transported to Uchuk Village in China. About 20 people who appeared to be Chinese Hunters were standing around us.

"*Kolk kolk*! *Kolk*!"

It was the Chinese man who teleported us who coughed. With a pale face, he looked like he was in such bad condition that I wondered if he would collapse.

"...I'll be on my way. I still have a lot of work to do...."

He spoke as if lamenting and disappeared using his ability.

'Wow. They're really working him hard, huh.'

Space movement ability users have rare and great abilities, so they are usually treated much better than ordinary Hunters. However, there is a risk of abusing their abilities, so they cannot use their abilities freely.

When moving to a village or city of a certain size, they must obtain permission from the association or government. So, people sometimes compare space movement ability users to dogs on a leash.

"Okay!"

A man gathered our attention. He was a man in his 30s with short hair and glasses.

"I am Ji Woo-jin from the Festio Guild. Is there anyone here who can speak Chinese?"

Festio Guild.

It was the first time I had heard of that guild name. And if I don't know it, there's a 70% or higher chance it's an insignificant guild.

"I can speak it."

I raised my hand. Han Ha-rin-ssi next to me looked at me with suspicious eyes. But I was confident.

"There's someone else who can speak Chinese besides me. Nice to meet you."

Ji Woo-jin approached me, shook my hand, and spoke in Chinese. It seemed like he was testing whether I really spoke Chinese.

"Yes. Nice to meet you. I'm Seong Yujin."

As we were shaking hands, someone approached us.

"Hey. Korean Hunters. Now is not the time to be leisurely greeting each other. You haven't forgotten our mission, have you?"

It was a man in his mid-30s wearing a martial arts uniform. The man with a long black beard reaching down to his chest was equipped with a large Zanbato on his back.

"Of course not. There's a saying that you should take your time when you're in a hurry, and I thought there was still time. Who are you?"

"I'm Gongon. A disciple of the Overlord Sword Sect. More than that, shouldn't we reassure the residents who are waiting for us now?"

Gongon pointed to one side. People who appeared to be villagers were watching us.

"Ah.... Gongon. You're right. Residents of Uchuk Village! We are Hunters who came to support you from Korea!"

•••

After that, we gathered and made a plan, and decided to evacuate to Lanzhou City at noon tomorrow.

And currently, I was looking down from the roof of the tallest building in the village. To be exact, I was looking at a woman.

She was a Chinese Hunter.

With long black hair tied back and snow-white skin, she was a rare beauty even in Korea. Like Gongon, she wore a martial arts uniform and was equipped with a large Zanbato on her back. I saw Gongon treating her politely several times.

'Similar martial arts uniform, similar weapon, the answer comes out. They belong to the same guild. Was it called the Overlord Sword Sect? The woman's status is higher than Gongon's.'

China refers to themselves as sects rather than guilds. And unlike guilds in other countries, Chinese guilds are closer to the concept of sects in martial arts novels.

Was it because I was staring at her too intently?

She looked at me. I didn't turn away.

'Hoo. She looks even prettier when I see her like this. She seems to have similar skills to me, so raping her is out of the question.... This is reality too. I should try to seduce her. But how should I seduce her?'

She was frowning. She doesn't seem to be in a good mood.

She's running towards me. Her footwork as she steps on the roof and jumps up is as light as a bird taking flight.

'It's light body skill.'

It didn't take even 5 minutes for her to come to me. She must have been training in martial arts since she was very young.

"Why have you been watching me since earlier?"

"Watching? That sounds bad. I was just looking. A beauty like you is not common."

"Nonsense.... You've been watching me since this afternoon. Do you think I wouldn't know that?"

"I told you, I was looking because you're pretty."

"......"

Even though I said she was pretty, her cold atmosphere didn't ease. Her eyes were full of suspicion as she looked at me.

"I know you're from Korea. Reveal your identity."

"I'm Seong Yujin, an E-class Hunter."

"I think you know that's not the identity I'm talking about. ...Wait, E-class? An E-class is doing this mission?"

"The association recognizes my skills as C-class. I can even show you my temporary Hunter certificate. And I don't have a guild, so there's nothing much to reveal. Oh, except for the Yeongcheon style?"

"Yeongcheon style...?! You're from the Yeongcheon style?!"

I nodded, seeing her somewhat excited reaction. It seems the Yeongcheon style is famous even in China.

"I've revealed my identity. Now it's your turn to reveal your identity."

"I'm Ryu Ja-yeong from the Overlord Sword Sect. I never thought I'd meet you like this. Good. Seong Yujin of the Yeongcheon style. I challenge you to a duel."

Ryu Ja-yeong casually lifted the Zanbato she had equipped on her back with her right hand. I don't know how much she has trained in martial arts, but her posture is flawless.

"Wait. A duel?! Not a sparring match, but a duel?!"

Sparring and duels are different. I know that from Jin Se-yeong-ssi.

In the case of sparring, it can be seen as training with an opponent, while a duel is closer to a fight to test each other's martial arts.

Duels are brutal. If you're unlucky, one side may lose their life.

"That's right. I heard about the martial arts of the Yeongcheon style from my father. He said it was fast and fierce. So, I will confirm with my own two eyes whether it matches my father's words."

"Suddenly a duel, what is that...."

I was about to refuse, but I closed my mouth.

My head is spinning.

"Ryu Ja-yeong. Do you know what a duel means?"

Ryu Ja-yeong's neatly stretched eyebrows twitched.

"Do you think I would challenge you to a duel without even knowing the meaning of a duel?"

"If you know, then you also know that you're being rude right now. Right?"

"...I admit my rudeness. I'm sorry. But I cannot withdraw my challenge to a duel."

"I know. I'm also willing to accept the duel. If you accept a condition."

"Condition?"

"The loser of the duel will grant any request of the winner."

"...Any request? I cannot accept such a vague condition. State the detailed conditions."

I smiled. Unexpectedly, Ryu Ja-yeong was picky. I was going to fuck her after winning the duel.... I should probably abandon that plan.

"Training. When I want.... Help me with my training about 3 times."

"If it's such a request.... I understand. I will grant it."

I wore a sly smile that kept trying to pop out. I didn't say exactly what kind of training I wanted.

"Then it's your turn. When you win. What request will you make of me?"

"When I win the duel.... It's okay. It's my side that committed the rudeness by challenging you to a duel. I'm simply curious about the Yeongcheon style. Even if I win the duel, I have no intention of requesting anything from you."

"Oh, okay."

"Now, the duel...."

"Wait, wait. You're thinking of doing it here?"

I pointed to below with my finger. We were currently standing on the roof. If we fight here, the house will be destroyed.

"......"

Ryu Ja-yeong looked around once, then equipped the Zanbato on her back again and said.

"I saw a decent open space behind the village earlier. Let's have the duel there."

"Okay."

•••

*Kaaang*!

I blocked the Zanbato falling from above by holding the Hwaryeon Bido sideways. Sparks flew, and my legs trembled as I was pushed back.

There was also the reason that Ryu Ja-yeong's physical abilities were superior to mine, but the Overlord Sword Technique that Ryu Ja-yeong used was a combat style that pushed with force, just like its name. If I collided head-on, I would have no choice but to be pushed back.

'It's an incredible force. Moreover, the Overlord Sword Technique is not at all ignorant, unlike what it looks like. If it was a truly ignorant martial art, I wouldn't have been pushed back like this.'

I created distance and looked at the red Sword energy rippling on Ryu Ja-yeong's Zanbato. Some people say that the energy generated from a sword should be called Sword energy, but most people don't make such distinctions and call it Sword energy. It's more common to distinguish the energy generated from a spear or fist, though.

"Yeongcheon style.... It's disappointing compared to what I heard."

"That's too hasty of a judgment. I haven't even started yet."

*Pajijijik*.

Red thunder fiercely wriggled from the knife with the rippling blue Sword energy.

Ryu Ja-yeong widened her eyes as if surprised, then quickly composed herself and took a more cautious stance than before.

The attitude that had been all-out attack changed to defensive.

'So, she's going to explore the Yeongcheon style first. ...Good. Try to explore if you can.'

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 4]

Stepping on Thunder Sound Steps, I ran towards Ryu Ja-yeong and swung my knife. Ryu Ja-yeong raised her mana to the fullest and swung her Zanbato.

I was caught.

Yeongcheon Style (影天流) - Blue Stream (碧溪).

Ryu Ja-yeong's Zanbato attacks right next to my body, in a strange place. I passed her and thrust my fist into her slender side. After all, I couldn't cut her side with a knife.

But my fist was blocked by something transparent 3cm in front of her body.

"......!"

*Buuwoong*!

Ryu Ja-yeong swung her Zanbato greatly. Unable to maintain my balance due to the huge wind generated from the Zanbato, I flew backwards. I frowned as I did a somersault in the air and landed on the ground.

"Ryu Ja-yeong. What is that?"

"It's my ability. Phase Defense. It's an ability where a barrier automatically protects me."

"...Do I have to break that barrier to beat you?"

"No. If you are stronger than me, I will admit defeat without hesitation."

"That's a desirable attitude."

I rushed towards her again and used Blue Stream.

"That technique won't work anymore."

I wasn't flustered because I expected it. I pretended to swing my knife at her, and then stretched out my leg to trip her. But both were blocked. The knife was blocked by the Zanbato, and I tripped her leg, but she didn't budge.

Ryu Ja-yeong was far ahead in strength.

*Buuuwoong*! *Buwoong*! *Kwaaaang*!

Ryu Ja-yeong's attacks are not fast. But they are powerful. Even if I get grazed, I will suffer a great injury from the aftermath.

Even right now, the ground around her was a mess. The floor was dug up, dust was flying, and pieces of stone were broken.

'Moreover, she has tremendous stamina. At this rate, I'll be the one to get tired first.'

She was a tough woman.

I wonder if she's this tough in bed too.

'Let's go all out this time. If it doesn't work... I'll have to use Complete Recovery to win.'

I used all my mana to create Red thunder.

"...That red lightning is dangerous."

"Are you telling me not to use Red thunder?"

"Don't be ridiculous. Do you think I would say such a thing? Come at me."

*Pajijijik*.

Each other's weapons collided. Red thunder rode the Zanbato and flowed into Ryu Ja-yeong's hand. The barrier surrounding her body flickered, but some of the Red thunder entered her body.

Ryu Ja-yeong gritted her teeth and swung her Zanbato.

[Using Transience (刹那). Remaining stacks: 3]

I dodged to the side and hit her side with the handle of my knife. It was blocked by the barrier, but it doesn't matter. My purpose is to make the current flow.

*Buuuuwoong*!

[Using Transience (刹那). Remaining stacks: 2]

Dodging the swing to the left, I dug into her arms.

*Pajik*. *Pajik*.

Ryu Ja-yeong's reaction, who was electrocuted by Red thunder, was much slower than before. The knife was aimed at her neck.

"*Keueueueuk*.... I lost."
