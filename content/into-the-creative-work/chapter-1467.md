The Droid's mechanical combat armor, the enemy of humanity.

Judging by its durability that withstands even mana distortion, it was clear that electricity wouldn't work.

But I didn't think I had no chance of winning.

I only needed one shot. The moment the current penetrates the machine, that massive mechanical combat armor will pathetically give out.

The problem is that one shot isn't easy.

"Hahaha! I don't know how long it's been since I used this!"

Keolkeong keolkeong.

Prong's arms transform into machine guns. Each of the three gun barrels on both hands aimed at me and fired. 50-caliber bullets pounded the barrier. The barrier shook under the barrage of bullets. One barrier couldn't even last 10 seconds before shattering into pieces.

I wasn't just taking it, though.

[Lightning Spear]

As low as possible.

The lightning spear, created below my knees, cut through the wasteland and flew towards him.

'Climb up from below.'

The Lightning Spear, upon touching the mechanical armor, exploded and scattered the current. The current, as I intended, clung to the nearby mechanical armor and raced across its surface. Yeah. It only raced across the surface. The current couldn't penetrate the mechanical armor at all.

"It's useless. It's a Droid's special battle suit. Did you really think we wouldn't have EMP countermeasures?"

Tututututututu.

The pouring bullets stopped. He ran out of bullets. I had only one barrier left. It was time to reinforce the barrier first. A jet engine appeared from behind him, and he leaped with his large, massive body, landing in front of me in an instant.

"Do you know why Droids inspire fear in humans? Because they are machines. The weak human body will eventually be crushed by a lump of steel!"

His arm transformed from a gun into a mace. The large mace, which looked like it would crush my upper body in an instant, struck the barrier.

The barrier shatters like a glass window.

I was taken aback by the more powerful force than I expected. But I quickly regained my composure and focused on the battle. The guy who loses focus in battle dies immediately.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

Fighting back is no different from committing suicide. First, I create some distance.

[Levitation]

I went up into the sky.

"Are you thinking of running away? Well, that's all you can choose. But do you think I'll let you?"

"I have no intention of running away. Today, this place is your grave."

He used the propulsion of the jet engine on his back to jump into the sky where I was. That tremendous leaping power made him pass me and go even higher. It's his mistake.

'Judging by his appearance, he's not used to operating that battle suit.'

Still, I could see that he was getting used to it quickly. If I drag this out, I'll be at an even greater disadvantage.

He aimed for my head as he fell. I dodged his attack by flying sideways.

At that moment, five fighter jets began to fly into the sky from Neo London. They were chasing the Droid's fighter jet that dropped the battle suit. The Droid's fighter jet didn't run away and engaged in combat.

'Is he deliberately drawing Neo London's attention? So that Neo London doesn't pay attention to this side?'

A 1-on-5 aerial battle. Surprisingly, the Droid's fighter jet was the one with the upper hand.

'Artificial intelligence. The best pilot who can control a fighter jet accurately without making a single mistake, unlike humans. Seeing it like this, it feels natural that Neo London is getting beaten by the Droids.'

I wasn't particularly worried about Neo London.

Neo London's greatest strength wasn't fighter jets, missiles, or tanks.

Knights.

The moment those monsters who roam the battlefield with their bare bodies step up, the Droid's fighter jet won't last long and will be shot down.

"Where are you looking, you idiot?!"

"I was watching because it's my first time seeing a fighter jet dogfight."

"You crazy bastard!"

The leaping guy appeared right in front of me.

"Of course, all my other senses were focused on you."

[Gravity]

I amplify gravity. It's a first-class magic, but even the same magic can vary greatly in power depending on the wizard who uses it. 50 times the gravity presses down on Prong's mechanical combat armor. This is in the air. There's no ground to support him. He falls as if being dragged to the ground.

[Not a chance!]

The jet engine's output increases. The massive body endures 50 times gravity and soars through the air. Watching his flashy aerial maneuvers, I quickly used Instant (刹那).

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

I barely dodge his attack. The mace grazed my head by 2cm. Since I didn't have a barrier either, my heart sank.

'The jet engine is burning red. He must be pushing himself too hard. He's pushing himself this hard... is he worried about me running away?'

Prong lost his subordinates because of me. It must feel like he lost most of his assets. He's pretending to be calm, but he must be seething inside.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 3]

'If it weren't for Instant (刹那), I would have already died. That's why I'm confronting him head-on, relying on Instant (刹那).'

Barely dodging the mace again, I put my hand in my coat pocket. I grab a Blue Distortion petal. I hesitated slightly. If I use everything in my pocket now, I won't be able to research Blue Distortion.

'...Blue Distortion can be obtained as much as I want if I have money and time.'

Hundreds of blue petals scattered from my hand. The petals slowly floated around me by telekinesis.

"What are you doing? That distortion won't work on me."

"It's different from what I thought."

Mana field distortion wasn't completely ineffective. His armor was slightly distorted by the spatial distortion. In other words, it's a matter of output.

'Hundreds of Blue Distortion petals, if I pour all the mana I have left... I have a chance of winning.'

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

Dodging the mace, I deploy the formula.

[Mana Field]

Hundreds of Blue Distortions resonate. The space itself turned blue. Prong's movement in the space slowed down.

Prong tilted his body towards the ground and maximized the output of the jet engine. He made the judgment to get out of this space first. But his movements were very slow due to the influence of the mana field.

I gritted my teeth. Managing the mana field wasn't easy at all. The moment I lose focus even a little, I'll get caught up in the mana field's space.

'I'll finish it before that.'

Spatial distortion occurs. The distortion headed towards the right shoulder of the combat machine armor. The space is distorted. The Droid's combat machine armor was torn off like a cheap robot toy. Prong was visible inside the armor.

'It's over.'

It was the moment when the distortion headed towards his head. Suddenly, the combat armor disassembled and blocked my view. He didn't miss that moment and escaped from the mana field, quickly falling to the ground.

'The tables have turned. I'll kill him before he runs away.'

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 1]

[Lightning Spear]

A lightning spear is created. This alone is not enough. He's a cyborg who withstood Thunder Break. There's no way I can finish him off with just one Lightning Spear.

The Blue Distortion petals scattered in the air began to gather by telekinesis. Hundreds of petals clung to the Lightning Spear.

Lightning Spear. Blue Distortion. Ultra-miniaturized Mana Field. I threw the triple-layered weapon towards the falling guy.

The spear accurately pierced his chest and caused an explosion.

Pajijijijijijijik!

Spatial distortion occurs along with a massive current.

I swallowed hard as I watched the scene. It was a strike I used without thinking deeply... but its power was far greater than I expected.

'It's like a giant mixer. Spatial distortion is the blade caused by the mixer... If I go inside, I won't even be able to collect my bones.'

I went down to the ground only after the spatial distortion disappeared.

Human flesh and machine fragments were scattered everywhere.

"Damn it...."

I turned my head towards the direction where I heard the low curse. It was his curse, with only half of his face, the mechanical part, barely remaining.

"To think I'd die in a place like this.... It's soon.... It'll be soon that you die too."

"I wish you'd just shut up with that meaningless curse and go to hell right now."

"Hell? It's over when you die. There's no afterlife or anything. There's no God either."

"Well. That's what you think."

I approached him to finish him off. There was no need to. When I got close, his last mechanical body had become a lump of scrap metal.

-Is it over? Don't dawdle and come to the car quickly. The Droid fighter jet crashed. The London Guard and the knight are coming this way. I've erased all the traces, so we just need to leave. Ah, there are no traces left there, right?

I looked around.

There weren't many traces that could lead to me. The Blue Distortion I used turned to ash and disappeared with the wind.

"I'm going there. Start the car."

The mission was successful.
