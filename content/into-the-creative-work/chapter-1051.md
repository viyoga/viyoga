The cult leader was kneeling on the floor, praying towards a picture of the Mad God.

The picture was different from the ones the Light Church followers saw. It was a picture of a monster with tentacles. The true form of the Light Church's real Mad God.

I swung my sword at the cult leader. Blue sword energy containing red lightning flew towards the cult leader.

Uuung.

The air seemed to tremble, and a semi-transparent barrier appeared around the cult leader, blocking the sword energy.

The cult leader slowly rose from his spot. He turned to face me. He wasn't flustered, nor was he angry.

,It seems you are quite enraged, esteemed guest. Was our hospitality so lacking? If there are any followers who have wronged you, feel free to punish them."

The cult leader said leisurely.

I pointed the tip of my sword at the cult leader. Pajijijik. Red lightning pierced the air and flew towards the cult leader. This attack was also blocked by the barrier. However, I confirmed that the barrier was shaking. That barrier wasn't invincible either.

,Esteemed guest-isiyeo. I don't know what you're so angry about, but now is a crucial time. Tomorrow, we will finally have an audience with the Mad God-nim."

,Cult leader. You said you were watching me."

,Were you displeased by that? It was an unavoidable matter as it was a divine message from the Mad God-nim. Please understand."

,Since when?"

,The divine message came down about three months ago. As I said before, it's a matter of the past that doesn't matter now. We were just watching, we didn't do anything."

,Since you were watching me, you must know about my relationships."

,Of course, I know. But we only know, we haven't done anything."

,Do you know about Han Ha-rin?"

,Isn't she the younger sister of Han Ah-young, who recently became an S-Rank Hunter? And a close female friend of yours. But what's the problem? Ah, do you want to bring Han Ha-rin to the Light Church? Since she's someone you've chosen, I, of course, welcome her. I might even give her the position of guide."

,Han Ha-rin is dead."

,Oh, dear... You must be very heartbroken. If you wish, we at the Light Church will hold a requiem for her."

,Han Ha-rin died at your hands, you bastards."

Only then did the cult leader's face slightly stiffen.

,...Yes? That's unexpected. I understand why you're angry. We will find all those involved and punish them, so please calm down."

,She was sacrificed."

,Ah... I see. She must have gone into the underground corpse storage. It's a terribly smelly place."

,Disgusting. Isn't that what you did?"

,I am the cult leader, but I don't oversee everything. The one who conducts and manages the rituals in the temple is the first guide, Murat, and there is a separate person who manages the sacrifices."

,...Manages the sacrifices? You weren't just killing them randomly?"

,They are sacrifices offered to the Mad God-nim. We can't just kill them randomly. Think about offering dirty, low-quality sacrifices. Would the Mad God-nim be pleased? It would be a relief if he didn't get angry. In that sense, the Divine Might ritual is special. The sacrifices are followers full of faith in the Mad God-nim. The Mad God-nim will be moved by the sacrifices of the followers."

,You seem more enraged than I thought. I understand. But calm your anger. If Han Ha-rin's death is the problem, wouldn't it be better to revive Han Ha-rin?"

I, who was holding the sword and approaching the cult leader, stopped.

,Revive her?"

,Yes. You may think it's absurd... but if the Mad God-nim uses his power, it's possible. You can meet the Mad God-nim directly tomorrow and ask him. Just a single human life. The Mad God-nim will grant your wish."

,Forget it. I'll take care of it myself. You die here."

I swung my sword, which was covered in sword energy. The barrier seemed to resist, but then it cracked. The cult leader's shoulder was cut.

,Flash."

Before the cult leader could do anything, I swung my sword again and cut off the cult leader's neck. The cult leader's head stopped in mid-air before falling down.

,Esteemed guest-isiyeo. Has your anger subsided? I will only allow the attack on me up to this point. I think I have some responsibility in this matter. So please stop here."

The cult leader's head was floating in the air. Blood was dripping from the severed neck, but he was still alive. It was bizarre. I stabbed the cult leader's forehead with my sword. The sword pierced through the cult leader's head as if skewering it on a skewer.

,...Esteemed guest. I clearly gave you a chance. It is your will to make this choice."

Tentacles suddenly appeared from the cult leader's head and body. I lowered my eyes as I looked at the dozens of wriggling tentacles. The cult leader wasn't human. Or he was originally human, but he was no longer human.

,Either way, it doesn't matter. I will kill the cult leader."

Pajijijijijijik!

Red lightning rose and electrocuted the cult leader's head. The tentacles sticking out of the cult leader's head twitched, and a smoky smell rose. It smelled like burning meat.

,Kkeueoaaaaaaaah!"

The cult leader screamed in pain. I continued to send electricity. I wouldn't stop until the cult leader died.

Kwaang!

Suddenly, a shockwave occurred, and my body flew backward and crashed into the wall. I gritted my teeth at the pain I felt in my back and jumped up from my spot.

The cult leader had recovered his body in that short time.

,Do you see this proof of immortality? It is the power that the Mad God-nim has granted me. Ah... I will live forever thanks to the grace of the Mad God-nim."

,So you don't die even if I kill you... Perfect for blowing off some steam."

I forcibly squeezed out mana. To be honest, my body was already at its limit. I lost control of my power for a moment because I was angry after seeing Han Ha-rin's corpse in the underground ritual chamber. No, I didn't. But I don't regret it. It's already irrelevant.

,If I die from mana backlash like this... I can use Complete Recovery."

Kwareureureung!

,Stormbreaker!!"

Stormbreaker fused with Hwaryeon Bido and enveloped my body. A sword appeared in the air. Red lightning was wriggling around my body.

,Acceleration, Flash!"

I rushed at the cult leader. The cult leader didn't react. No, it's right that he couldn't react. The cult leader wasn't a Hunter. He had power, but it was power he received from the Mad God.

I swung my sword. I slashed the cult leader's body more than three times in one second. The cult leader's body regenerated as soon as it was cut. I gritted my teeth and continued to swing my sword. The sword strikes, which had gained momentum, became faster. And at the same time, I felt a pain as if my muscles were about to burst. The mana that I forcibly squeezed out was also running out. I didn't stop swinging my sword even while vomiting blood.

I stopped when my sword fell to the floor. I didn't have the strength to swing my sword anymore. My internal organs were already burned and not functioning properly. I could tell because I had experienced death several times. I'm going to die soon.

,Has your anger subsided a little?"

The cult leader recovered without any problems. A faint smile was drawn on his lips. I got angry again because it seemed like he was mocking me. The problem is that my body, which was collapsed on the floor, wouldn't move.

,You died 75 times in that short time. But you couldn't kill me. This is the great power of the Mad God-nim."

,I bet you're happy that you got the power by sucking the Mad God's Butthole."

,I would gladly suck the Mad God-nim's Butthole if it meant getting power!"

,......"

,You, the Light Church, and the Mad God. I'm going to kill you all."

,Killing the Mad God-nim... That's a very blasphemous thing to say. If someone other than you had said such a thing, I would have torn them apart and killed them right away. But since you are an esteemed guest, I will understand. The Mad God-nim will descend soon. Your punishment is something that the Mad God-nim will decide, not me."

,So, please stay still until then. Esteemed guest."

,...I already ruined the ritual chamber. It will take some time for the Mad God to descend."
