The hard ground melted and flowed like it was dissolving. And it grabbed my feet like a strong adhesive.

Purple smoke began to swirl around me. Poison gas. I stopped breathing as soon as I realized it.

Whether it was psychokinesis or not, an invisible force was pressing down on me. And that wasn't all. The temperature rose, and suddenly blood flowed from my hands.

,It's the vampires' superpowers.'

I can't hold out for long. The power of each individual superpower is weak, but with dozens manifesting simultaneously, it's quite strong.

Seogeok!

I slashed and killed one of the vampire progenitors. He didn't even scream in his dying moment, and remained expressionless. He doesn't accept death as death. Of course, I know the reason. All the vampires here are Seong Su-un. That vampire is just a finger to Seong Su-un.

,Maybe it's not even a finger, but just a fingernail.'

Even as I fought, I kept an eye on Secretary Kim. Right now, the most important thing here was Secretary Kim's safety. I absolutely couldn't bear to see Secretary Kim get killed.

Smart Secretary Kim chose to distance herself from the battle rather than helping me.

Fortunately, Seong Su-un didn't seem interested in Secretary Kim. He probably thinks that if he kills me, he can kill Secretary Kim anytime.

Kkaang!

The Super Blade I swung didn't cut through the vampire's body and bounced off. The vampire's body had turned into something like ore. I don't know what kind of ore it is, but the laser blade doesn't work on it.

,Let's see if other parts are hard too.'

It was the moment I was about to swing my sword again. For some reason, the sword felt lighter. When I rolled my eyes to the side, I saw the broken sword tang falling to the ground.

The Super Blade was a sword that relied on the cutting power of the laser blade rather than its durability. I never thought there would be something in this world that couldn't be cut with a laser blade.

,Heh heh heh. So, you don't have that fancy sword anymore?'

The vampire in front of me laughs annoyingly. I also smiled back at him.

,Heh heh. You still don't know my abilities?'

Well, he might not know. The me in this world rarely steps up to fight directly. Secretary Kim is the only one who knows my abilities well.

,Storm Breaker.'

A giant spear that appeared in the air slammed down on the vampire's head. The vampire's body, which couldn't even be scratched by the Super Blade, was shattered into pieces.

,Come.'

I reach out my hand to the Storm Breaker stuck in the ground. The Storm Breaker was disassembled into pieces. Hundreds of thousands of metal fragments flew to me and began to reassemble.

I was wearing full body armor that covered even my eyes, but my body felt much lighter. Breathing also became easier. It was thanks to the Storm Breaker blocking the superpowers that were pressing down on my body.

,Hwaryeon Bido.'

I grab the red knife that appeared in the air. The knife immediately seeped into the Storm Breaker. It fused with the Storm Breaker.

Pajijijik.

Red sparks flew from my entire body.

I spread out my palm. A sword appeared on my hand. It's the Storm Breaker's clone ability.

I gripped the sword tightly and dashed forward.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 3]

A sprint towards the vampires.

The enemies were flustered. They probably didn't expect me to run into the middle of their camp. Soon, they move in perfect order. Even though dozens of them are moving at the same time, there's not a single unnecessary movement. It was only natural. They are all one person.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Jilpungsinroe (疾風迅雷).

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 2]

I burst into their center before they could fully settle in. A shockwave containing red thunder spread in all directions, sweeping through the vampires. Those electrocuted by the Red thunder couldn't withstand it and collapsed to the ground.

Even in the midst of all that, vampires continued to appear.

,Not yet.'

,Do you think it'll end with just that?'

,This is just the beginning.'

Dozens of vampires around me actively use their superpowers to attack me. Some of them strengthened their bodies and charged. Of course, they couldn't harm me, who was protected by the Storm Breaker.

,Still, the damage is accumulating.'

The Storm Breaker is strong. But I, who am inside the Storm Breaker, am not that strong. Stamina is consumed every time I move, and mana is reduced every time I attack. As time passes, victory becomes more distant. Seong Su-un, the enemy, also knows this fact.

Seogeok!

I took down a vampire that was aiming for my back. I brought it down in one strike, but it didn’t feel right. It’s not that the sword is dull. My concentration was interrupted by random thoughts, which affected my technique.

,Okay.'

I erase the distracting thoughts.

,Just kill what you see. Then they'll die.'

Mu-nyeom-mu sang.

I killed the enemies I saw.

The only sounds I heard were the sound of blood pouring out, the sound of red thunder sparking, and the sound of bodies falling.

And at some point, I couldn't hear any sounds. Startled, I realized that there were vampire corpses scattered around me. Immediately after, my whole body began to ache. It's a sensation you can feel when you overwork your muscles to their limit.

,...Amazing. I knew you were strong, but I never thought you'd be this strong.'

I raised my head.

A total of 12 vampires were floating in the air.

They were different from the vampire progenitors I had easily killed. The atmosphere I felt was heavy.

,The vampire progenitors that kept appearing endlessly are gone.'

I roughly got a sense of it.

,Did you eat dozens of your own kind?'

,My own kind? It's more accurate to say they ate themselves.'

,The guy who went berserk before has some guts.'

,That rampage was just a process.'

I extend my trembling hand towards them. Red red thunder gathers in front of my hand and rotates clockwise.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Manroe (卍 Thunder).

Space distortion.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Extreme Art (極技) Cheongwang (天光)

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Heodo (虚道).
