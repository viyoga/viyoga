,What is your wish?

,...Are you granting wishes?

Seong Hari couldn't hide the excitement in her heart.

Yet, she felt a sense of doubt. What is the being before her? What is it that it grants wishes?

,Why do you grant wishes?

,I'm just doing what I decided to do.

,You made the Tower of Wishes, right? Why? To grant human wishes?

,This world happened to be recognized by me. Seeing humans living diligently, I wanted to grant their wishes. There's no other reason.

,......

Seong Hari realized. This person just has a different perspective on humans. Perhaps it's similar to how humans view ants.

It occurred to her that the being before her might be a god.

,If you grant my wish... I don't care if you're a god or a demon.

Seong Hari told him her wish.

,I want to see my oppa! I want to go to the real world where my oppa is!

,......

He was silent.

Because his expression wasn't visible, she couldn't know what expression he was making. It meant she couldn't know what he was thinking.

As his silence continued, Seong Hari became anxious. The worst-case scenario was imagined, and that imagination became reality.

,Impossible.

,Why?! You said you'd grant my wish!

,You should know. Because this world is fake. You're fake too, and I'm also fake. An elaborate fake created against a real backdrop. Just a fake that will disappear without a sound. You seem to be a bit different... But in the end, the essence of it being fake doesn't change.

Seong Hari felt the space shaking. No. It wasn't the space that was shaking, but her. A headache rushed in, and nausea rose.

She knows that this world, that she, is fake. But in one corner of her heart, she thought it was real because it was the world she lived in. That last hope has now been shattered. To make matters worse, there's no way to meet Seong Yujin.

Suddenly, the tower began to collapse. The space inside the tower began to shake.

,Aaaaaaaagh!

Seong Hari screamed and collapsed.

He calmly asked Seong Hari.

,Make another wish. For example, you want to put all the humans in this world under your feet, or the destruction of this world would be fine too. Originally, those are also impossible wishes, but... this world is a fake destined for destruction. I can grant it sufficiently.

With his calm words, Seong Hari regained her senses a little.

,...Since it's a fake world anyway, does that wish have any meaning?

,It's you who finds meaning in something. If you think it's meaningless, it's meaningless, and if you think it has meaning, it has meaning. I'll ask again. What is your wish?

,......

This world is fake. So, should I just make it all disappear?

It was the moment Seong Hari truly thought so. The space split, and a demon in a suit walked in. A demon Seong Hari also knew. Laplace. The Eighth Legion Commander. Seong Yujin's enemy. Blood was seeping from his shoulder. Pajijik, blue lightning was lingering on the wound, attacking him.

,An uninvited being has entered.

,Haha. Please allow this much, God of another world-isiyeo. I have a proposal for her.

,Well, I suppose it doesn't matter.

,Thank you for the generous decision, God of another world-isiyeo. If it's alright, would you tell me your name?

,I refuse. I am fake. You are not qualified to utter my name.

,I know you are connected to the real thing. Even if you are fake, you couldn't exist if the real thing didn't allow it. Because you are the only being in this universe.

,I said I refuse.

His voice was firm. Laplace immediately stepped back. The uninvited guest is himself. There was no need or reason to provoke him.

,I understand.

Laplace looked at the collapsed Seong Hari. Seong Hari gritted her teeth.

,Did you come to laugh at me?

,I've come to make you an offer you can't refuse.

,...An offer I can't refuse?

,Don't you want to become real? I'll make you real.

The demon's words were sweet. That's why she was suspicious. It's common sense that you shouldn't trust a demon's words.

,You can do what that god couldn't?

,I can. That god is fake, but I am real.

,...How are you going to make me real?

,This world is fake, but you are a bit special. You should be seen as half real.

,What does that mean?

,You have been observed at the center of the universe. Karma has accumulated. Moreover, although it's a virtual reality, you entered the real world. Although faintly, your record has been engraved in the real world. Possibilities have emerged and grown. Now, it's time to bloom. I'll help you. I'll make you real.

,I won't listen to the words of a demon...

Seong Hari's eyes wavered. A demon's words. She can't believe it. She rejects it outright. She has to reject it... .

,Don't you want to be by his side? To be by his side, you have to become real.

,The real me is already by oppa's side.

,You have to take it away. It's natural for a fake who has become real to take the place of the real thing. Besides, you have something to repay, don't you?

,I'm going to make the real...

,Yes. You can do it.

Seong Hari bit her lip. In fact, she knows. From the moment she couldn't reject the demon's offer outright, her answer was already decided. She knows, and the demon knows too.

,...What's the price? There's no way a demon who's a legion commander would help me without a price.

,Haha. You know a lot about demons. You'll find out the price after you accept my offer.

,......

,Don't worry. I won't ask for what's precious to you as the price. I promise on my name.

There was only one thing precious to her. The only real thing in a world where everything is fake.

,...Okay. If it doesn't harm oppa... I'll accept it. What do I have to do?

,Make a wish to the god. Ask for the resources of this world. You're going to devour this fake world.

,Resources?

,Even a fake world that will disappear needs energy to maintain it. The information that makes up this world, each and every resource, can all be said to be energy. You're going to have that vast energy.

Seong Hari couldn't fully understand his words. One thing was certain: she was to absorb a vast amount of energy.

,...Can I do that? I've seen many guys who didn't know their place and were ruined by gaining immense power.

,You're different from them. You have potential. I, the demon of potential, guarantee it.

Seong Hari looked at the God of another world.

,Is that your wish?

,...Yes. It's my wish.

,The wish has been granted. This fake world will collapse, and all that information will be yours.

Seong Hari felt it. Outside this tower, everything in the world was slowly collapsing. All the information that made up the fake world became energy and gathered to Seong Hari.

Laplace immediately intervened. He managed the energies gathering to Seong Hari. It would be right to reclassify and process them.

,...Can you handle this?

,I can. I already got the computing device for that earlier. Ah, the uninvited guest has arrived.

They turned their heads.

Uninvited guest.

Seong Yujin, holding a sword, appeared.

,Oppa!!

Seong Hari, who was sitting down, shouted without realizing it and jumped up.

,Hari-ya!

Seong Yujin also shouted. He ran towards Seong Hari. Just as Seong Hari was about to move, Laplace blocked her way.

,Don't confuse your priorities. What you need to do now isn't to laugh and chat with that guy. Are you going to give up the chance to become real?

The fake world began to collapse.

If she gives up here, she will simply disappear with the fake world. Seong Hari knew instinctively. Seong Yujin was running but was blown backward. Laplace had used his power.

She clenched her fist tightly and asked.

,What do I have to do?

Snap!

Laplace snapped his fingers.

Next to Seong Hari, the space split. It was a pitch-black space.

,Go inside. It's a nest for you. A nest for a fake to be reborn as real.

,Oppa is.

,I won't kill him. I swear. Well, I can't kill him right now.

,Hari-ya!!

Seong Yujin calls her. Seong Hari steeled her resolve.

,Oppa! I'll face oppa as a real person, not a fake!

Seong Hari threw herself into the space Laplace opened.

She fell into the darkness. She fell and fell and then stopped. Hot, sticky darkness enveloped her body. Seong Hari felt immense energy. A power greater than when she faced the Spirit King permeated her. It was changing her from the root.

A feeling of becoming real, not fake.

Seong Hari's consciousness sank.

,You bastard!! What did you do to Hari?!

Immediately after Seong Hari disappeared into the space, I ran to Laplace.

Laplace's power that had been repelling me had disappeared. Too easily, I grabbed Laplace's neck and pointed my sword at his solar plexus.

Laplace was relaxed.

,You're lucky. It's fortunate that the time inside the tower and outside is different. If you had come any sooner, you would have ruined everything.

,Where did you take Hari! Answer me!!

,Haha. Calm down, it's not like she's going to die or suffer. Ah, if you kill me now, she'll die too. Are you still going to kill me?

My hand trembled. I want to just kill him. But what if his words are true?

,Are you not resisting because you believe that?

,I don't have the power to resist you. Right now, I'm overwhelmed just by classifying and processing the information energy of this world. If she's not going to go wrong, I have to keep using the authority of possibility.

,You son of a bitch...!

,Calm down. It won't take long. All the conditions have already been met, so three hours should be enough. Shall we chat while watching the world collapse? It's a sight you rarely see.

The tower is slowly collapsing. What's visible beyond the tower is the collapsing sky. The finely broken sky falls like powder and then disappears.

I swung my fist at Laplace's face.

Pow! Pow pow!

His face is becoming a mess. I kept swinging my fist as if to relieve my anger.

,Stop it. You might really die. If that demon dies, that woman dies too. That's not wrong.

The being who had been silent beside me intervened. I frowned and glared at him.

,What the fuck. Who the hell are you?

,You who possess the Scales of Karma, I am not your enemy.

,Bullshit!

I wielded the Sword of Kusanagi. The blade passed exactly through his neck. But there was no change. It felt like cutting through the air.

,You still lack a lot of karma. Let me give you some advice. Keep accumulating karma. Karma will be your greatest weapon.

,This bastard is just spouting whatever he wants and being a nuisance!

I kept wielding the Sword of Kusanagi.

It was useless. I was still just cutting through the air. Even with the power of Susanoo, it was meaningless.

,Haha. He is a god of another world. One of the most honorable beings in this universe. Even if he's fake, do you think such an attack would work?

,You still have the energy to laugh?

The fist flying towards Laplace's face stopped. Laplace's condition is terrible. If I hit him one more time, he might really die. Then Seong Hari dies too. I gritted my teeth. There was nothing I could do now.

Time passed. During that time, I had to suppress the impulses of the Demon Hunter (S) while listening to Laplace's nonsense.

『Demon Hunter (S) wants to kill the Legion Commander!』

,Shut up.

The world had already collapsed. All that remained was this space where I, Laplace, and the God of another world were. This space is also slowly collapsing.

Jiiiiiiing.

The face was Seong Hari's. But her appearance was different from what I knew. She wore black armor that exposed part of her abdomen and limbs, and she held a black spear that felt like it had the power of a demon.

Her black hair had turned white, and her eyes were red as if they contained crimson.

Step, step.

She approaches with a blank expression.

,Hahahahaha! My greatest masterpiece is finally complete! New Eighth Legion Commander! I'll give you a new name! Baal! You will become the strongest demon!
