Large monsters.

Pajijijijik!

Kung. Kung. Kung!

The ogre approaches.

The typical strategy is to run away from the ogre, maintain an appropriate distance, and fight it. But Lee Sieun didn't run away.

,She's tired, but not to the point where she can't run away. She's aiming for something.'

The ogre raised its arm and swung its fist at Lee Sieun. Lee Sieun, who had been preparing in advance, dodged the attack by digging into the ogre. And she touched the ogre's torso with her hand.

Kwaaaaaang!

Lee Sieun didn't seem to have thought that far. It's not like she's stupid. She had only obtained the Reversion skill 10 minutes ago.

Peok!
