"His Grace the Duke has ordered your disposal."

A butler blindly loyal to the Filtania family approaches me.

"...Are we assassinating people with keychains these days?"

Kopan inserted the key into my handcuffs. The restraints fell from my wrists. I felt a sense of relief just from that.

"I'm not an idiot either. If I kill you, the young miss will torture me to death. His Grace the Duke must know that too. He's issuing the order knowing full well. He's telling me to take responsibility for raising you."

Kopan then inserted the key into my shackles.

"So, you're saying you'll die because of me in the end. I'm sorry to hear that. But is it okay to go against the family's orders like this? Didn't you always tell me that the family's orders should be prioritized over one's life?"

"That's for you guys, not for me. I don't want to die. The reason I'm loyal to the Filtania family is because of the money and status."

"You're so materialistic."

"Everyone's like that originally."

The shackles fell off. I got up and shook my wrists and ankles. My body felt light, considering I'd been locked up for a month. That's because it's not the ordinary body of a commoner.

"What are you planning to do now?"

"I have to run away. I've been preparing since you were dragged into the dungeon a month ago. His Grace the Duke is a cold man. I figured he'd hold me responsible for raising you."

"I don't know why you came to save me. You could have just left me and ran away."

"You're the best butler I've ever raised. You're better than any other butler. If you die here, all the time I invested in you will be gone. That's a waste."

Kopan then made a suggestion.

"Let's run away together. Someone like you can do anything well, not just be a butler."

"Thank you for saving me, but I have a master I must serve."

Kopan chuckled as if he expected that answer.

"You really are the best butler. I guess we'll have to part ways here. Since I went to the trouble of saving you, don't die."

"Yes. Please live well, Kopan-nim."

"...It's clearly a blessing, but why does it sound so unlucky?"

Kopan left. He's unlucky, but he's capable, so he'll do well wherever he goes. Well, if he wasn't capable in the first place, he wouldn't have become a butler in the Filtania family.

After escaping the dungeon, I headed to Yuria's room. I knew Filtania Castle perfectly, so avoiding the guards was a piece of cake.

I went to the balcony and tapped on the window.

She, wearing pajamas, slightly opened the curtains. Seeing me, she looked a little surprised, then opened the window.

"Eugene. How did you escape?"

"I received help from Kopan."

I briefly explained what happened in the dungeon.

She frowned and nodded.

"Mother was determined to dispose of Eugene-gun after all..."

And from now on, the Duchess will openly try to kill me.

"Eugene. Why did you come to find me? If you had run away with Kopan, you could have lived without danger."

"Young miss. Didn't I tell you? I love you, young miss."

I reflexively tried to hug Yuria, but hesitated. My body wasn't clean right now. I wasn't tortured, but I was stuck in the dungeon for a month. Even with the help of the torture uncle, there were limits to how much I could wash.

But Yuria hugged me first and kissed me.

"Young miss. Let's run away."

The purpose of this world isn't war. It's not politics either. It's love.

The title of this world is 'The Filtania Heiress and the Butler' from the start. It's a title that reeks of romance. In other words, what I have to do is achieve love in accordance with the romance.

"I will definitely make you happy. Let's leave together."

"......"

Yuria stared at me intently. Her eyes wavered.

I know.

That my position and hers are completely different. All I have is this life. On the other hand, Yuria has everything.

The position of heiress.

It's too big to throw away for just love.

"......I understand. Let's leave together. I'll be happy wherever I go if I'm with Eugene."

She started preparing to leave. She changed into clothes that were easy to move in and packed money and belongings. I quickly helped her and made a plan in my head.

The Tiltania Duchy was very large, befitting its power. Even if we rode in a carriage without stopping, it would take at least five days to leave the duchy.

'I should go west. The road is rough, so it'll be hard to track us, and it's close to the border. Once we cross the border, we can breathe a sigh of relief.'

I was regretting that I couldn't use a teleportation scroll when...

Knock, knock.

There was a knock on the door. Yuria grabbed my shoulder and pointed under the bed. I immediately crawled under the bed.

Yuria opened the door. I couldn't see who had come because of the bed.

"Young miss. Are you sleeping?"

It was a woman's voice.

"...I was getting ready to sleep. Even if I was sleeping, you would have come in anyway."

"I couldn't help it because His Grace the Duke ordered me to. Please forgive me."

She left just like that.

I got up from under the bed.

"Is that a maid?"

"Yes. She's the exclusive maid who was assigned to me after Eugene was imprisoned. Originally, she's my mother's maid. She was assigned to me not to serve me, but to monitor me. She'll probably come back around 3 a.m. to check if I'm sleeping well."

"You've been living an uncomfortable life too, young miss."

"Not as much as Eugene-gun."

She packed all the luggage she needed and hugged me. I carefully hugged her and went out onto the balcony.

"Eugene. You're going to have a hard time for a while."

"This isn't even hardship."

Yuria and I escaped from the castle.

The pursuers were on our tail within a few hours. The pursuers were more persistent and capable than expected. It was hard to completely shake them off.

「Advice: Move to the left.」

「Advice: Erase your tracks.」

「Advice: Disguise yourself as someone else and steal a horse to escape.」

The advice, which didn't display any messages when I was in the dungeon, was helping me. Thanks to it, I was able to escape safely.

On the third day.

We infiltrated a city. We needed to rest since we had been running non-stop. I'm fine, but Yuria couldn't use her original powers, so she definitely needed to rest.

The place we headed to was the Temple of Love. If we went to other temples or inns, there was a high chance we would be reported. But the Temple of Love is famous for wholeheartedly helping couples in love.

Even if they couldn't help us, they would let us rest for half a day.

And the priest of the Temple of Love smiled at us.

"I won't ask who you are. Rest as much as you want. May the blessings of love be upon you."

They noticed us and still allowed us to stay.

Yuria dozed off in the assigned room and soon fell asleep. She didn't say it, but she must have been very tired.

'Should I take a nap too?'

「Advice: Go outside the temple.」

'Do I have to go out right now?'

「Advice: Go outside the temple.」

'...Fine. I understand.'

I moved as the advice directed. It led me to a place with a lot of people. It couldn't be trying to send me to the pursuers, right? Maybe I have to get caught here to go to the next chapter.

'The end of this story might not be a happy ending. Maybe it'll end with me dying. If I think realistically, that's the most likely possibility.'

「Advice: Go into the alleyway on the right.」

I went into the alleyway.

I found an old woman wearing a drab black robe like me. She was sitting on a mat in an alleyway where people didn't come. She didn't seem like a beggar at all.

"Kihik... An interesting guest has come."

"I'm not a guest. You're not selling anything, are you, grandma?"

"Does one have to sell things? I have the ability to help you."

"That's quite an ability."

I squatted down in front of the old woman. She was an unreliable old woman, but the advice led me here. There's a high probability that she has some special ability.

"Grandma. Please help me. I really feel like I want to borrow even a cat's paw."

"Hehe. I'm a bit expensive."

"I have quite a bit of money. I can't give you all of it... but if you help me properly, I'll give you half."

I held out my money pouch. The reward I received from the academy and the money I've saved so far. It was all my wealth. Even if I gave half, I would have enough money left to settle down in a decent city.

"Kihik. You have a big hand. I'll definitely have to help you with this. It's a prepayment, so give me half first."

The old woman took out a bowl from her bosom as if she had been waiting for it and held it out. An old bowl with cracks on the surface. A perfect empty bowl for begging.

"Ah. I really feel like I'm getting scammed."

I poured half of my entire fortune into the old bowl. The sparkling gold coins filled the bowl.

"Kihikihik. The owner of this has finally appeared. Well, you know how to use it without me telling you, right?"

"...What, weren't you going to help me with magic or something?"

"Even if I'm a magician, what you need isn't magic."

"...Does it have omnipotent power or something? Like turning imagination into reality?"

"If I had that kind of ability, would I give it to you? It's just an ordinary object."

"I feel uneasy. Did the old woman use it?"

"Hey! It's a brand new, freshly made item! You don't know how amazing this thing is. If you have this, you can skip three stages!"

"It's quite disappointing for half of my entire fortune... Well, thank you."

"Kihik. If you hadn't given me half of your entire fortune, I wouldn't have given you this either. Your ungreedy heart brought you luck."

"What if I had given you my entire fortune?"

"I would have given you that as well."

"I knew I was getting scammed... Tsk. She disappeared."

I got up as well.

"...If you were going to give me this item, it didn't have to be that old woman."

I grumbled softly, looking at the object in my palm.

「Advice: Return to the Temple of Love and do what you have to do.」

•••

I forcibly woke up the sleeping Yuria. It's not good to postpone things like this. You have to do it when you can. If you mess things up by doing them slowly, you won't be able to sleep because you'll be so upset.

I led Yuria to the temple chapel.

The chapel of the Temple of Love wasn't just a place for praying to the Goddess of Love. It was often used as a wedding venue for commoners.

"Eugene. What's the important thing? Is it related to the pursuers?"

"No. I just wanted to tell you, Yuria."

I took her left hand and took out the object I bought from the old woman by giving her half of my entire fortune.

It was a ring.

"That's..."

Yuria's eyes widened in surprise. I tried to put the ring on her ring finger myself, but she grabbed her left hand. I could feel her body trembling.

"I, I can't receive it."

Tears welled up in Yuria's eyes. She looked anxious, as if she was afraid of something. It was a face and reaction I had never seen before.

"Why? Was your saying you loved me a lie?"

"There's not a single lie in those words! I... I deceived master-nim. In fact, I recognized master-nim the moment I first saw him. I had all my memories of master-nim. I also knew that if master-nim failed, if master-nim died, I would go back to the beginning! Above all... It's because of me that master-nim got caught up in this world! Because I wanted love with master-nim...!"

"I know. I noticed it halfway through and experimented for a while. You had a hard time eating the food and black tea I made."

It was during the academy that I realized she hadn't lost her memories and was acting.

I served Yuria the cookies I received from Mary without telling her. In the setting, if I made them, they should have been the best-tasting cookies. But because I didn't make them, they weren't the best-tasting, and Yuria praised them as being very delicious as usual. If she had been swallowed up by the setting like the other people, there's no way she wouldn't have felt something was off.

I suspected Yuria from then on, and after secretly doing a few tests, I realized that she was just acting.

"It wasn't bad being played by you. I've been using you too much all this time."

"Master-nim..."

I spread out each of her left fingers one by one. And I put a stylish little ring with a silver ring and a small blue jewel on her ring finger.

"I love you. Yuria."

"Eut..."

Clear, transparent tears streamed down her cheeks.
