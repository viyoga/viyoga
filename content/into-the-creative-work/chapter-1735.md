It was right after the AI's notification popped up.

The virtual reality world began to change.

The ground surged, rocks and trees flipped and reconfigured. The world itself began to change. It felt like experiencing the end of the world. Fortunately, Sung Hari and I didn't feel a single breeze.

Still, the sight was so magnificent that Sung Hari grabbed my shoulders tightly with both hands.

The NPCs nearby couldn't avoid the aftermath of the reconfiguration either. Their bodies disintegrated. Of course, no blood came out. What makes up an NPC is data, not blood. No matter how real they seemed, they were fake.

,Players bleed when they get hurt, but NPCs don't.'

I was calmly thinking about this. I don't know the details of what happened, but I'm sure I'm fucked. But didn't I summon Makina into reality in preparation for times like this?

,Makina. Can you hear me?'

-Why? Is there a problem? ...Wait. My processing speed is too fast to keep up. Talk slower.

,I'm trapped in the virtual reality world. I need to know what's causing it.'

-Oh, really? That's a big problem. I was going to hang out with Auntie in a few days.... But you're not asking me to figure out the cause, are you? I don't know how to do that.

Makina's voice was full of annoyance. She didn't seem to realize the seriousness of the situation yet.

,What's the situation outside? Move around and check. Is anyone messing with the capsule?'

-Nope. It's quiet.

,So you're saying this accident wasn't deliberately caused to trap us?'

It took time to figure out what was going on. If the AI was telling the truth, the time difference between virtual reality and reality was 50 times. It took time to grasp the situation in reality.

,Fortunately, Makina seems to be keeping up with the 50x processing speed... but that's different from moving her body.'

-Ugh. How long is this going to last?

,Are you struggling?'

-Yeah. I'm struggling in this state. Your processing speed is too fast. I wouldn't mind if the mana flowing into me increased, or if I transformed into a special machine.... But that's impossible right now.

,Why is that impossible? Just take the mana from my body.'

Among my stats, the highest was mana. A whopping A+ rank mana. I'm the best among the academy students, and even among active heroes, there are few who can match my mana rank.

Mana shortage. I was far from that word.

-Your mana and Auntie's mana are constantly being sucked into the capsule. Didn't you know?

,...I can't know the situation in reality at all. I probably wouldn't even know if someone stabbed me in the chest. That's why I summoned you into reality.'

-This isn't my fault!

,I know.'

Even with Makina, I couldn't handle it. I'm not trying to blame Makina. I didn't expect the AI to go crazy. If Kaso Academy or whatever was going to do something, I thought they'd do it to the capsule. It's more efficient that way.

-What should we do now?

,Gathering information is the top priority. And watch out so those bastards don't do anything to me and Mom. If they try to do something....'

-If they do?

,Kill me along with the capsule. Don't forget that the important thing is to smash the capsule.'

Then the connection will be cut, and I can revive in reality through complete recovery.

-Okay! Got it!

Makina answered in a bright voice. I was a little dumbfounded. She's happy to be ordered to kill her master?

,...Okay. Let's scold Makina later.'

First, we need to do what we have to do.

,The reason this happened is probably because of us. In other words, it's a trick to keep us from taking Susanoo's Magatama. That's how I should see it, right?'

Fortunately, the authentication code that Tenrai Nagisa gave me was still engraved on the back of my hand.

Find Susanoo's Magatama. I'll think about getting out of this virtual reality after that.

,If necessary, I can inform Tenrai Nagisa of the situation through Makina.'

Even as I was racking my brain, the world continued to change. The cataclysm lasted for almost 5 minutes before quietly ending.

,The medieval fantasy-like background... has changed in a subtly modern way.'

Medieval fantasy and modern times were mixed. The building styles, the NPCs' clothes, and so on. It felt like the game genre had changed in an instant, so it felt a little strange.

,I guess it goes better with this school uniform.'

The reconfigured NPCs also feel more realistic.

,Yujin-ah! Look over there! Over there! That's Korean, right?!"

Sung Hari made a fuss and pointed to one place. She didn't seem to know the seriousness of the situation. Or maybe she's acting bright on purpose because of me.

,...It's really Korean.'

Seokgun's Pub. The sign was written in Korean.

Originally, these signs were in Japanese. Because of this, Sung Hari, who can't read Japanese, didn't know what kind of store it was even if she looked at the sign. Fortunately, the interface is in Korean, so there's no big problem using the system itself.

,That's not the important thing right now. Why is there a Korean sign? Did the virtual reality really glitch because of us?'

There was too little information.

「Name: Hasegawa Sota

Affiliation: Kaso Academy 3rd Grade Class A

Level: 16

Job: Spirit Master

Sub-Job Level: 7

Sub-Job: Priest」

Fortunately, the level wasn't reset. The items I had didn't disappear either.

,Yujin-ah. Don't worry too much. It looks like there's a problem... Mom will do something about it.'

Sung Hari said in a confident tone, as if telling me to trust her. I must have been frozen with too serious a face. Considering the situation, she, who can't know the information in reality, should be more anxious than I am.

I unfroze my face and said to Sung Hari.

,I'll trust only Mom. Is that okay?'

,Of course. You know how great a hero I was.'

We started moving again.
