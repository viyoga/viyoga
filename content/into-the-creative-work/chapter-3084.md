At a time when every nation across the globe—the United States, China, Japan, and the rest—had their eyes fixed on the Tower's Transcendent, it was Russia, not America, that made contact with him first.

Anastasia Ivanovna.

A woman with blonde hair pinned up and ice-blue eyes. From the leather armor she wore to the very last strand of her hair, everything about her was immaculately ordered.

A female soldier of Russia, her true identity was that of a world-class Hunter. Andrei Morozov, the world-class Hunter publicly known as Russia's finest, was nothing more than her decoy. When the world rankings were compiled, she had deliberately submitted her decoy's name to conceal herself. Only a handful of individuals even within Russia knew who she truly was.

Anastasia met the Tower's Transcendent on the 60th floor.

"Are you the Tower's Transcendent?"

Before her stood an old man draped in a black robe. Even when she tried to peer closely at the face beneath the hood, she could barely make it out. It seemed to be a special ability of the robe itself.

"This isn't a place you stumble into by accident. Did you come looking for me?"

"Derson, the Alchemist of Hallucinations. I heard you've been in hiding after friction with the other Transcendents over spreading drugs within the Tower."

"Despite that elegant, immaculate appearance, you're a woman like a honed blade. Yes. You've found the right person. What is it you want from me?"

"My homeland, Russia, desires cooperation with you."

Derson let out a low chuckle.

"A nation, is it. Do you know? I addicted every human on my planet to the drugs I created. Every last one of them craved my drugs and gladly pressed their lips to my feet."

"...Is that a threat?"

"It's a warning. The old man before you is a man who drove an entire dimension to ruin. And still you wish to reach out your hand?"

"If it's for my homeland, Russia, I don't care whether it's drugs or anything else."

"Even if you say that, I don't know what sort of country Russia is. Is it perhaps one of those Earth nations making noise these days?"

"So you do know of Earth."

"Just because my body is here doesn't mean my eyes are blind and my ears are stopped. I'm aware that the Transcendents are mad for goods from Earth. I understand it. Things that can't be seen in the Tower are coming in, after all..."

"Russia wishes to stand higher than any other nation on Earth."

"There are people like you. Those who, despite climbing to the 60th floor, remain subservient to something like a nation. Do you know what happens when they pass the 70th floor and become Transcendents of the Tower? They break free of that worthless shackle called a nation."

"My loyalty will not waver. Do you have no intention of cooperating with Russia?"

"It's been so long since I've conversed with an intelligent being that I've gotten far too excited. Go back and... cooperation, in the end, is only possible when there's mutual benefit. What will you give me in exchange for cooperation?"

"Coins. Russia will provide an immense quantity of coins."

"Coins are the very identity of the Tower. Good. I'll cooperate. However, there is a condition. You must not provide Earth's goods to the Tower's Transcendents, and you must find and kill anyone who does."

"...You don't think we would provide goods to the Tower's Transcendents?"

"If you would, you wouldn't have come looking for me. And the most important condition is... you will oppose Hent, the Sage of Iron, and Kersha, the King of Medicine. Especially regarding Hent, the Sage of Iron—if you learn even the smallest piece of information about him, report it to me."

"You're in a hostile relationship with this person called Hent?"

"More than that. Do you wish to hear the long and tedious tale between Hent and me?"

"No. With this, you and Russia have become cooperative partners."

"Nonsense. I don't trust relationships that are mere words."

Derson drew a single sheet of paper from within his robes. It was a contract.

『National Advisor Employment Contract

Grade: Unique

A nation employs a National Advisor.

The nation shall pay the National Advisor 10% of all coins acquired.

The National Advisor shall do their utmost for the benefit of the nation.

This National Employment Contract is guaranteed by the Tower.』

"...The Tower has no shortage of extraordinary things. To think even a contract like this exists."

"If it doesn't exist, one simply makes it. The problem is that creation is no easy task, even for Transcendents. The employment contract bears my signature. The moment your nation's leader signs it, the effect activates."

Anastasia calmly examined the employment contract.

10% of the coins Russia acquires.

A full 10%.

It was as good as saying, offer up 10% of Russia's citizens. Anastasia felt a moment of doubt. Was the Transcendent before her worth this much?

"And this is a gift for a future collaborator."

A purple liquid sloshed within a transparent glass bottle.

『Derson's Enhancement Elixir

Grade: Rare

All abilities increase by 50% for 10 minutes.

As a side effect, physical aging accelerates.』

"This is...!"

Anastasia's eyes flew wide open. A 50% increase to all abilities for 10 minutes.

With this potion, floor challenges would become easier. In moments of crisis, it could serve as a hidden trump card.

The side effect of accelerated aging? In a crisis where one's life could be lost at any moment, it was no side effect at all.

"If you bring me the ingredients, I'll make as many elixirs as you need. Ah, and next time you come, bring as much information on Earth's plants as possible."

"Why are you curious about Earth's plant information?"

"Don't be foolish. So I can create drug recipes and provide them to you, obviously. I cannot go to your dimension, but I can provide the recipes."

Anastasia's pupils trembled briefly. Realizing just how dangerous the being before her was, her morality recoiled. But she thought of her homeland and discarded her morality.

"...This is a personal question, but how many floors of the Tower have you climbed?"

"The 85th floor. Average for a Transcendent. For reference, those who have climbed past the 90th floor are few enough to count on one hand, even among Transcendents."

"What about information on floor challenges above the 70th?"

"The 70th floor is..."

Zzzzzzzzt!

Noise erupted. Anastasia furrowed her brow. The noise ended the moment Derson finished speaking.

"...Did you hear that?"

"I couldn't hear it due to the sudden noise."

"The Tower censored it. It varies depending on the individual, but... for most, it's the same as what happened to you."

"Most means... there are exceptions?"

"Exceptions exist everywhere. However, even I don't know the criteria for exceptions."

"One more thing. Can a Summoner summon a Transcendent of the Tower?"

Derson burst out laughing.

"Is this a new kind of joke? You have quite the imagination."

“…….”

"It wasn't a joke?"

Anastasia explained the ability of Sex King Sung Yujin.

"Ho. An ability that summons humans. How peculiar. There are Summoners among the Transcendents, but even they cannot summon humans. Moreover, the humans he supposedly summoned—I've never heard of them either. At least among the Transcendents I know, there is no human with that kind of appearance."

"...Then what are the humans he summoned?"

"I don't know either. The Summoner himself would know."

• • •

The 22nd-floor challenge was complete.

Most of the Tower's floor challenges could be resolved through sheer force, so a single click of a summon and the clear was done. The 22nd floor required killing a mutant wolf king. The summoned Sungjigon charged forward and tore it apart.

『You have chosen a 1% increase to all stats.』

For the first time in a while, I chose the all-stats increase.

'Coins are coins, but in the later stages, this is what matters.'

Even if you can't feel it on the lower floors, as the floors climb higher, that 1% to all stats becomes enormous.

Especially once you pass the 70th floor, a 1% increase to all stats is power that can't be bought even with billions of coins.

'The world ranking rewards persist even after you become a Transcendent and nations and the world collapse. That means the Transcendents are enjoying this benefit too. And the vast majority of Transcendents are world rank number one. Either they were always number one, or they naturally became number one as all their competitors died off. For the future, I need to raise my specs.'

Of course, I'll steadily raise my coin acquisition rate as well. Maintaining summons and leveling up summoned creatures ultimately comes down to coins.

『Panacea

Grade: Legend

Cures all diseases.』

The reward this time was a single pill.

'Useless.'

For someone like me who has Complete Recovery, disease was someone else's problem. Hunters don't even fall ill easily in the first place.

'With Elena, even incurable diseases and fatal wounds can be healed.'

Elena's very existence was a higher-tier compatible replacement for this Panacea. Elena's Regression Technique could turn a fatal wound into something that was merely a fatal wound, and a congenital disability into something that was merely a disability.

'Speaking of medicine, it makes me think of Kersha, the King of Medicine. Should I use this as an excuse to meet her?'

Strike while the iron is hot—I headed to the 1st floor's common area to meet Kersha. If I couldn't meet her right away, I planned to contact her through Lupil and arrange a separate appointment.

I was in luck.

She was at her shop in the 1st floor's common area. A woman in shabby clothes wearing a cat mask.

"A rare customer after so long. Shall I call you Sex King?"

She saw right through my identity. I wasn't surprised. In the original work, she was a Transcendent who displayed exceptional insight.

"It's been a while, King of Medicine."

"So you knew after all?"

"Talggak Yeonhwa. There are few in this Tower who can handle a drug of that caliber."

"Hmm. That's true. What kind of medicine did you come looking for?"

"I came to sell medicine. Are you going to keep wearing that mask?"

"On the 1st floor, I need to wear this mask to avoid the Tower's restrictions to some extent. More importantly, the medicine?"

I held out the Panacea to her. The moment she saw the pill, her eyes went wide.

"This is a Panacea! You're selling this? Really?"

"I have a constitution that doesn't get sick."

"You seem to be under some misunderstanding. This isn't simply a medicine that cures disease. Curing incurable diseases is the bare minimum—it can even cure congenital curses that a race is born with."

"Congenital curses?"

"For example, if a vampire takes this medicine, they can walk under the sun without any restriction. On the 73rd floor, the moment a challenger enters, diseases are forcibly inflicted upon them. This can wipe out those diseases all at once too. The clear would naturally become easier. Ah, can you not hear this?"

"I can hear it."

"What?"

Kersha was more shocked than when she'd seen the Panacea.

"You, who can't even reach the 30th floor, let alone the 60th? How?"

Kersha had apparently thought this would be censored and she wouldn't hear it. But there was no censorship.

"It seems the Tower acknowledges me."

Honestly, I don't know the reason either. Even if it were censored, it wouldn't have mattered much. Through the original work, I already know the information about the 73rd floor. Even information about floors above the 90th, which even Transcendents don't know well.

"I'll tell you some interesting information. The 100th floor, the end of the Tower, is ■■■■. ■■■■■■..."

It was censored.

So thoroughly that even I, the one speaking, couldn't hear it.

Unlike the usual noise-format censorship, it felt as though the information itself was being distorted and warped so it couldn't be spoken. Even from the shape of my mouth, it would be impossible to deduce the words.

'If I use Heaven's Heart, can I avoid censorship too?'

I'm curious, but I have no intention of making an enemy of the Tower. Far from gaining anything, it seems I'd only suffer losses. Sorry to Kersha, but I never seriously intended to hand her information from the start.

Kersha was more appalled than when she'd seen the Panacea.

"...I can't hear it? Just what kind of information do you know?"

"Ah. Well, you see. ■■■■ ■■■■■ ■■■■■■■■... Oh dear... it seems the time hasn't come yet. Let's end this story here."

"...What's with that oddly irritating way of speaking?"

Kersha glared at me as if she was fed up.

“■■■■■. ■■■■■■. ■■■■■■….”

This censorship thing is oddly fun.

It feels like it's saying, "These are words you unqualified lot can't hear," and it satisfies my desire for superiority.

“■■ ■■■■■ ■■■■!!”

"Enough...!!"
