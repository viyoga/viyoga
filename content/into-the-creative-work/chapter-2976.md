The Yustia Empire’s special Agents worked solely for the Emperor.

Only a select few, including the Emperor and High Nobles, knew of their existence. Even if they died during a mission, their deaths would never be known to the public.

Most were Orphans who had received the Emperor’s Grace since childhood, and they had resolved to sacrifice their lives for the Emperor.

‘It’s been a long time since I’ve carried out a mission outside the Empire.’

Agent Marc departed for the Desert, carrying a secret imperial order. He knew there were other Agents who had received secret orders, but he didn’t know the details.

In this world, dangerous powers like Magic and supernatural abilities abound, so it’s better not to know important information. That way, there’s no risk of having information forcibly extracted.

Marc disguised himself as a desert peddler and set off.

‘My mission is one. To shake the trust of the desert dwellers in the Heavenly Demon.’

The Desert aid funds provided a convenient pretext. The Heavenly Demon had received the Desert aid funds for the Desert, yet hadn’t spent a single Fenny on the desert dwellers. That was already a confirmed fact.

‘Incitement based on facts, then. This mission should be simple.’

The Heavenly Demon was a disqualified Leader.

‘In an Empire with a figure like His Majesty the Emperor, such a thing would never happen. The desert dwellers are truly pitiful.’

His sympathy quickly vanished.

Desert dwellers were not Imperial citizens. Even if the Empire conquered the Desert, it would lose more than it gained. Such was the barrenness of the Desert. Rather, they were potential enemies who could invade the Empire. If the Desert’s population decreased due to this opportunity, it would be a benefit for the Empire.

Marc entered a desert village.

‘As expected, it’s inferior compared to the Empire.’

From the buildings to the condition of the residents. It was incomparably poor compared to the prosperous Empire. In this Desert, even a sip of water was a luxury. Marc approached the residents with a friendly smile.

“I’m a passing peddler. I have quite a bit of stock piled up, so… I’ll sell it cheap. Come take a look.”

Marc spread out the Items he had brought.

“It’s really cheap.”

“I needed a new pot anyway… This is perfect.”

“Mister. How much is this toy?”

The wary residents approached and examined the goods. The atmosphere gradually lightened.

“Everyone. Do you know? His Majesty the Emperor of the Empire has bestowed Desert aid funds upon the struggling desert dwellers in this barren land.”

“Desert aid funds? What’s that? We’ve never heard of it?”

“It is said that the Heavenly Demon, the representative of the Desert, received the Desert aid funds. A total of 30 billion Fenny. It’s a huge sum that could give at least 20,000 Fenny to each desert dweller.”

“…We’ve never heard such a thing?”

“Oh dear. It seems the rumor hasn’t reached this village yet.”

“……”

The friendly village atmosphere rapidly chilled. Marc felt dissatisfaction with the Heavenly Demon sprouting in the hearts of the villagers.

Desert aid funds. The money that should have originally gone to the desert dwellers was all taken by the Heavenly Demon. They had clearly suffered a loss.

“…The Heavenly Demon will surely use those Desert aid funds for the Desert.”

“Rumor has it that he bought a large quantity of luxury goods from the Empire and distributed them to his dancers.”

“……”

An uncomfortable and awkward silence.

‘The first operation was a success.’

Next, he would find those with the most grievances and ignite the fire. They would become a rebel army with the Empire’s support.

Afterward, Marc secretly sought out a man who looked like a warrior and was frowning deeply.

“I heard you are the bravest warrior in this village.”

“Well, that’s not wrong. But what brings a peddler-yangban to me?”

“Don’t you wish to receive the Desert aid funds?”

“Of course, I do. Who would dislike getting free Money?”

“Go and protest to the Heavenly Demon.”

“……Are you insane?”

“Listen. You are not the only one protesting. There are others like you in other villages. What if they all gather in one place to protest? No matter how much of a Heavenly Demon he is, he would have no choice but to show Respect.”

“Everyone gathers? How many people are gathering?”

“At least 1,500 people or more. Most of them are warriors.”

It was an empty promise.

Marc didn’t know the exact number of people who would gather. This was, after all, his first visit to a desert village.

“But…”

The warrior, said to be the bravest in the village, hesitated like a coward. Marc scoffed inwardly and pulled out Money, Potions, and Magic Tools from his bosom.

“This endeavor is for the great cause of the Desert. Since it’s for the Desert, my comrades and I will support you with all our might.”

“Hmm. If it’s not just me, and 1,500 desert warriors gather… it might be possible. Alright. What should I do?”

“First, quietly gather your comrades until then. When the time comes, I will call upon you. Ah, this is not rebellion. It is merely to reclaim the Desert aid funds that should have originally been paid.”

“That’s right. It’s not rebellion.”

When that day came, it would be rebellion.

Marc handed him Money, Potions, and Magic Tools under the pretext of support, then moved to the next village. He pulled a similar stunt, but the results were slightly different.

“Protest? A human mouth speaking the words of a dog. Our tribe will pretend not to have heard it. Get out of here right now!”

“I wonder where the valor of the desert warriors has gone. Does this tribe call cowards desert warriors?”

“Hmph. Stop with your cheap gambling. Gather and protest? Challenging the Heavenly Demon himself is madness. Get out immediately.”

It was a village where fear of the Heavenly Demon had permeated to the bone. Marc had no choice but to go to another village.

This village was wealthier than other desert villages. They spent Money and water lavishly.

“Desert aid funds? Ah. The Heavenly Demon extorted Money from that stupid Emperor. Hehehe.”

Marc almost cursed for a moment. It wasn’t easy to hold back insulting His Majesty the Emperor.

“The Desert aid funds are originally money that should go to the desert dwellers, but…”

“Oh, come now. The Heavenly Demon will naturally take care of how to use it in governing the Desert.”

“There are rumors that he’s using those Desert aid funds to buy luxury goods for his dancers.”

“Ah. So that’s why. No wonder he’s been sending so many things recently.”

“……Aren’t you displeased?”

“Hehehe. My daughter is one of those dancers. The things the Heavenly Demon bestows upon her, she sends to our village. And since our village has many beautiful women, many of them have become dancers. Here, this is a fruit called strawberry, sent by a dancer. It’s a rare food to eat in the Desert.”

He tasted the strawberry. It was much larger and sweeter than the strawberries cultivated in the Empire.

‘…This place is a lost cause.’

There was no dissatisfaction with the Heavenly Demon. On the contrary, they supported him.

“Peddler-yangban. Don’t look so envious. I’ll tell you the secret to becoming like our village.”

“What secret is that?”

“Have a child.”

“Huh?”

“To be precise, have a girl. Whether it’s your wife or concubine, they’ll probably be ugly, but sometimes you get lucky. If that lucky child grows up to become a dancer? That’s when your family’s fortune turns around. It takes some time, but… you have to look at life long-term, don’t you? From what I see, the Heavenly Demon is not an ordinary human but a Divine being. He’ll easily live for hundreds of years and rule this Desert.”

“…I see. That’s a good secret I’ve heard.”

“Kuhuhu. I’m telling you this because you have a good impression, peddler-yangban. It’s hard to find pretty women, so find a handsome man and adopt him. Even if the woman is ugly, if the man is handsome, there’s a slightly higher chance her daughter will be pretty.”

“……That’s truly groundbreaking and ingenious thinking. I must be off to another village now.”

“I will see you off.”

As he left the house, he heard twanging music from somewhere. A 10-year-old child was dancing to the lively music. A slightly sensual dance, with both hands behind her Head and her Hips swaying.

“She’s my youngest daughter. As you can see, isn’t her beauty quite outstanding? I’m having her practice the Zero Two Dance, an essential qualification for a dancer. The Heavenly Demon loves this dance very much.”

“…My words might sound rude, but isn’t this… selling your daughter to the Heavenly Demon?”

“Ignorant people say such things. Becoming a dancer is the most successful life a woman can have. A life where you don’t have to go through all sorts of Bullshit just to get a sip of water in this damned Desert. That girl wants such a life too.”

“……I apologize for my rudeness.”

Marc left the village. He intuited that whatever he said in this village, nothing would change.

The next day.

Marc returned to the first village he had visited.

The family of the desert warrior he had spoken with was displayed as corpses in the village center. Dismembered body parts were skewered, dripping blood. The desert warrior, his wife, and their two children. The entire family had been massacred.

“What… what happened?”

One of the villagers, a man, laughed. The man, who had been dressed in rags yesterday, wore silk clothes today. His mouth was greasy, and his entire body smelled of beef.

“Yesterday, I heard that bastard insulting Heavenly Demon-nim and reported him. I received 10 million Fenny as a reward. Hahaha!”

Something was wrong. The moment Marc tensed.

The man raised a Finger and pointed at Marc.

“It’s him! This is the peddler I spoke of yesterday! That bastard insulted Heavenly Demon-nim! I heard him clearly!”

Black-clad figures appeared in the hot Desert and surrounded Marc.

“The interrogation will be conducted at our cult headquarters. I will count to three. Kneel on the ground and raise both hands above your Head. Three, two…”

This won’t do.

If he was dragged away, it was over.

Marc pulled a dagger from his bosom. He raised his arm to swing the dagger.

Surguk.

The arm holding the dagger fell off.

“He was a traitor.”

The black-clad figures rushed at Marc. They were far more skilled martial artists than Agent Marc, and they instantly severed Marc’s limbs. The gushing blood from his limbs was stopped by applying pressure to acupoints.

“Transport him. And the informant.”

“Yes. Hehehe.”

“Take this. It’s the informant reward.”

The man, clutching a bulging money pouch, grinned from ear to ear. Then, his Waist repeatedly folded in half and straightened with astonishing speed.

“Thank you, sir! Thank you, sir!”

“Everyone listen. From now on, if anyone insults Heavenly Demon-nim, or if a suspicious fellow comes to the village and spouts strange things, report it immediately. The informant reward will be given to the first one to report.”

“……”

“Furthermore, anyone who retaliates against or insults an informant will also be punished.”

The black-clad figures carried Marc away.

The villagers, once they had completely disappeared, acted as usual.

It was just another day.

“That stupid Emperor. Kekeke.”

I sat on my Throne, scoffing at the Emperor.

Inciting the desert dwellers using the Desert aid funds as a pretext wasn’t a bad idea. If one didn’t know the Desert’s situation in detail, that is.

The desert dwellers had experienced worse things than just not receiving some money, so they weren’t about to get angry over that.

“It’s good that I can weed out undesirable elements.”

Even if a large-scale rebellion were to occur… I didn’t particularly care.

I would just kill them all.

The population decreases? Who cares. To speak extremely, even if 90% of the desert population died, it wouldn’t matter. The Desert would be maintained.

‘What’s important in Atlantis is AP. Even if the population is insufficient, AP steadily comes in.’

This wasn’t governance for the sake of governance.

What was needed was only reign.

The Demonic Cult itself was a faction solely for me.

There are those who harbor grievances against me? If I kill all those with grievances, the grievances disappear.

‘Just taking it isn’t to my liking.’

I didn’t have cards like the Empire’s Agents to use.

But I had Elena. Even though I got a few slaps on the back from Elena, she agreed to help me. Elena also didn’t view the Emperor’s behavior favorably. So, she willingly participated in messing with the Emperor.

Throughout the Empire, stories about the Desert aid funds emerged.

Not openly, but Imperial citizens harbored resentment towards the Emperor. That fact was important.

‘The Empire isn’t the Empire of old. With the exiles coming in, they learned about revolution. And also that a nation can be maintained even without an Emperor.’

For now, there won’t be a revolution or rebellion. The Desert aid funds weren’t even a big mistake by the Emperor.

‘Of course, he can’t say he was threatened. That would be an act that would tarnish the prestige of the Imperial family. Even if such rumors spread, he would deny it.’

Anyway, through this incident, the Emperor must have realized.

That the Desert, and the Demonic Cult, cannot be touched by ordinary means.

‘For the time being, the Emperor will be busy with internal cleanup. Kekeke.’
