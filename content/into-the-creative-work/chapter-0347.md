\7. Reincarnated as the Plum Blossom Swordsman in a Noble Family

347. Plum Blossom Sword Master Reborn in a Baronet Family

"I see. Then this will be quick. Your Excellency. I have some interesting information, would you like to hear it?"

At my words, Marquis Heollis's eyes lit up.

"...Information. This is intriguing. If it's information from you, who grew the Korea Trading Company into a size comparable to a giant trading company in such a short time, it can't be trivial."

"The value of information varies from person to person. Information that is more precious than a gold nugget to someone may be just a pebble on the street to another. To me, it's information that is neither a gold nugget nor a pebble. I might disappoint you, Your Excellency."

"I have a feeling that the information you're about to tell me will be like a gold nugget... no, a jewel. What kind of information is it?"

"It's nothing much. It's just that the starting point of the great war that will happen in the future is in the north."

"Hmm. I see. I'll correct myself. It's not a jewel dropped on the street, but a gold mine. That is, under the condition that your information is accurate."

If you know where the war will break out in advance, you can move quickly to gain profits. And if you calculate the aftermath of the war to some extent and maintain a good balance until the war ends, becoming the richest man in the kingdom will be a piece of cake.

"A war starting in the north... Are you perhaps talking about the northern part of our kingdom? It's certainly a brutal neighborhood, but I don't think there will be a war to be concerned about... Could you be talking about the countries further up?"

"Marquis-gakha. I think I've already given enough information."

"It's not information with any basis. I can't trust it. Or is it information that the Information Guild also knows?"

"Surely not. If the Information Guild knew, it would have already spread as a rumor, knowingly or unknowingly. And Marquis-gakha wouldn't have missed the information either."

"...It's just that your abilities are too outstanding to dismiss it as nonsense. What do you want? Membership in the Kozline Trading Company? I can definitely push for that."

"I'll end up joining the Kozline Trading Company anyway. Wasn't that the original plan?"

"...Hmm. I can't just leave the Korea Trading Company alone."

"I already said what I want."

"Your Korea Trading Company and my Hollis Trading Company working together on the war business... To be honest, securing war materials is just starting. I can't easily get materials because of the kingdom's eyes. If I openly prepare or smuggle war materials, Chancellor Helbrit will notice and grill me, as sure as the sun rises."

I knew he was talking with some bravado about war materials. It wasn't my own thought, but Yuria immediately told me through telepathy.

The market price of war materials is rising too slowly for a giant trading company to be building them up. Yuria said that if the giant Hollis Trading Company were to seriously prepare war materials, it should rise by at least 10%.

"I knew the Chancellor influenced the Kozline Trading Company... But is the interference that severe?"

"Hmm... The Korea Trading Company is located on the border, and the company has grown rapidly recently, so it's natural that you don't know much about the Chancellor... The Chancellor is more prickly, picky, and neat than you think. He's also unnecessarily perceptive, so he immediately notices any subversive movements. I'm only telling you this, but the Kozline Trading Company is also a leash that the Rapelli Kingdom puts on giant trading companies."

"...Are you allowed to say such harsh things?"

I was a little dumbfounded. He and I were strangers. We weren't close enough to badmouth high-ranking officials.

"I'm just stating the facts. Or are you going to run to Chancellor Helbrit in the royal palace and tattle on what happened today?"

"Do you think I'm crazy? More than that, should I take it that the reason you can't gather war materials is because you have to be mindful of the Chancellor?"

"That's right. It might have been different in the old days. Now that His Majesty the King's energy is weakening, Chancellor Helbrit can be said to be the real supreme power. He's even supporting the Crown Prince. Of course, his intention is to make the Crown Prince a puppet king."

"Is the Chancellor's check that severe?"

"The Chancellor is wary of civil war. The easiest time for civil war to break out is when the king is old."

"I understand. In short, the problem would be solved if there was no Chancellor."

"Why? Are you going to assassinate the Chancellor or something?"

"......"

I smiled silently and looked at Heollis. It's Heollis's joke. But looking at those eyes, it doesn't seem like just a joke.

"I've investigated the Korea Trading Company. I was curious about the secret of its success. The reason is convenient and sophisticated goods that didn't exist until now, but that alone couldn't explain the growth."

"Did you find out the other reason for the growth?"

"I found out. Merchants and nobles who interfered with the growth of the Korea Trading Company met with mysterious deaths. They were attacked by monsters, hit by carriages, and suddenly disappeared one day. The Korea Trading Company is so lucky that I suspect it's being loved by the god of death."

He knows that I ordered the assassinations. Of course, there will be no material evidence. Yuria wouldn't have left any evidence, and if she had evidence, she would have threatened me to dig up information about the Korea Trading Company's goods.

"...If the Chancellor disappears, can you cooperate with the Korea Trading Company?"

"If the Chancellor disappears, I can cooperate. But you don't know how vicious Chancellor Helbrit is. Even the god of death wouldn't be able to do anything to the Chancellor."

"That answer is enough. As for the Chancellor... wouldn't the heavens help?"

"Now, won't you tell me about the information about the north that you mentioned?"

Seeing him urge me like this, he must be really curious about the information I have. I came here with the determination to tell the information anyway, so I told him the detailed information.

"The north. Of course, I'm talking about the Hojit Kingdom and the Bischel Kingdom above the Rapelli Kingdom."

"The countries in the north. Are you perhaps saying that the Hojit Kingdom and the Bischel Kingdom will declare war on our Rapelli Kingdom? They are certainly rough countries, so it could happen, but..."

"No, Marquis-gakha. They will fight each other. A long peace has continued, but there is a deep grudge between the two countries. They will fight fiercely. And the spark of war that started from them will spread throughout the continent."

"...I can't believe it. The prince of Hojit and the princess of Bischel formed an alliance by getting married in a political marriage just 10 years ago. And you're saying they're going to war with each other?"

"The marriage will be broken soon. Because the son born to the princess of Bischel is not the prince's son."

"Wait. My ears are itching. I think I just misheard something..."

"I said it correctly."

"...Are you saying that the child of the princess of Bischel was born out of adultery? Then who is the princess's lover? Who is the real father of the royal grandson?"

"You still don't trust me."

"It's not information that's easy to believe."

"The princess of the Bischel Kingdom's own brother. That is, the current king of the Bischel Kingdom is the real father."

"......"

The Marquis opened his mouth slightly, as if in shock.

In short, what I'm saying is that the princess of the Bischel Kingdom made a child through incest and tried to make that child the king of the Hojit Kingdom.

The two kingdoms already didn't have a good relationship. If this is known, they will fight until they destroy each other.

"...Can you take responsibility for that statement?"

"Why would I take responsibility?"

"...I'm sorry. I was momentarily distracted by the shocking story. It's not your responsibility."

I didn't announce it officially, I just told the information to Marquis Heollis. And to Marquis Heollis, who knows this information, it's just someone else's kingdom's business after all.

"...If what you say is true, it's natural for a war to break out. Does the Hojit Kingdom know that fact?"

"Not yet. They're probably in the stage of suspecting."

"I can't fully believe your words."

"I understand. I would be the same if I were Marquis-gakha. However, I have given you the information. It's up to Marquis-gakha to believe it or not."

"...It's a headache. I was going to ask you about the Korea Trading Company, but... I'm worried I'll hear more shocking things later."

"There's plenty of time, Marquis-gakha. And as time passes, our relationship will get better than it is now."

I talked about this and that with Marquis Heollis after that.

"I would like to meet Lady Heollis, who will be my sister-in-law, but is she not currently at the mansion?"

"If you mean Bibi, she's already gone to Count Neviontte's banquet."

"Is that so. That's a shame."

"If you're okay with it, why don't you attend the banquet too?"

I managed my rising lip corners and pretended not to know anything.

"I would like to participate if I could, but... I wasn't invited to the banquet. Wouldn't it be a nuisance to Count Neviontte, the host of the banquet, if I went?"

"A nuisance? You can come with me. Besides, aren't you the younger brother of the invited Jent? The nobles at the banquet will welcome you."

It was possible to say that because Heollis had the real influence and power of the western nobles.

"Then I'll shamelessly participate. I'm really looking forward to meeting the nobles of the west and my sister-in-law."

•••

Marquis Heollis and I entered the banquet hall and greeted the nobles of the west. They welcomed my sudden participation. Count Neviontte, the host of the banquet, was the same. Who would criticize me when I came with Heollis, who can be said to be the real power of the west?

Except for one person.

There was one person who distorted his face as if he had chewed something bad and glared at me.

It was Jent.

He probably never dreamed that I would attend the banquet with Marquis Heollis. And he's probably imagining all sorts of ominous things in his head.

'In reality, my relationship with Marquis Heollis is only from today, but it will feel different to Jent.'

I approached Jent with a brightly smiling face.

The eyes of the surrounding nobles are focused on me. To be a little more precise, they are glancing at Yuria, who is attending me. Wearing a maid outfit, she was more beautiful than any noble wife and young lady at the banquet, and had more dignity than them.

The noble wives and young ladies will chew on Yuria later, but it's not something to worry about now.

"Nice to meet you, Jent-hyungnim. I'm meeting you here too."

"...Yeah. I didn't know you would come to the banquet. You didn't commit any rudeness to Marquis Heollis-gakha, did you?"

"Haha. You worry too much, hyungnim. I was just talking to Marquis Heollis-gakha about the company's business, and somehow I ended up attending the banquet. But hyungnim. When are you going to introduce me to the sister-in-law next to you?"

I turned my head to the side.

There was Bibi Heollis, Jent's fiancée, whose appearance I had previously confirmed through a portrait. Bright light green hair and snow-white skin. Her chest was an A cup, but she was tall like a model and had good body proportions.

I met her sapphire-colored eyes. She smiled awkwardly and greeted me.

"I'm Bibi Heollis, the eldest daughter of the Heollis family. Baron Eugene Prucus-nim."

"I'm Baron Eugene Prucus. You're very beautiful when I see you like this. Sister-in-law."

"Sister-in-law... We're not even married yet..."

"Aren't you about to marry Jent-hyungnim soon? It's okay to be called sister-in-law in advance, right? Or is this title uncomfortable for you?"

"Ah, no. Call me whatever you want. Baron-nim."

Bibi treated me awkwardly and looked at Jent. The reason is obvious. Jent must have told Bibi about me. That we are competing for the position of successor. In the process, all sorts of rumors must have come out of Jent's mouth, it's obvious without even looking.

After greeting Jent and Bibi, I headed to the other nobles. First of all, I had to establish a relationship with the nobles here.

'...But what the fuck is this guy's name?'

When I was slightly frowning because I didn't know the name of a noble, Yuria's telepathic message came.

-He is Baron Sebiroologue. The Sebiroologue territory is famous for its delicious apples.

"Baron Sebiroologue! I didn't know I would meet you here! The excellent taste of the apples from the Sebiroologue territory is also famous in my territory."

"Hahaha. The apples from our territory are indeed famous."
