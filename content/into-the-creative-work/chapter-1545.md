,Lightning, star... Malong (Demon Dragon)?'

The beggar wore a terrified expression for a moment. It seemed he saw something inside me.

Then, the beggar gritted his teeth and began to concentrate. The internal energy fight was a double-edged sword, a battle at the edge of a cliff. The moment you retreat, it's over. You'll be crippled at the very least.

I couldn't even open my mouth. The moment I did, my energy would leak out, and the beggar's internal energy would explode my body.

Pajijik, pajik!

Sparks flew from my body. I tried my best to block the beggar's internal energy, but it wasn't easy.

,The amount of internal energy I have is superior! But....'

The beggar's experience and martial art characteristics are overwhelming. I've never done this kind of internal energy fight before. And the Beggars' Sect's Kangryong Paljang (Dragon Subduing Palm), including the palm techniques, basically has the essence of Naegajungsubeop (Internal Family Heavy Hand Technique) permeated within.

,Keueueueuk.... My giheol (Qi and blood) seems to be burning.'

The internal energy rushing from the beggar was sharp. Reminiscent of a thorny vine.

,The only fortunate thing is that the other bastards can't attack me.'

Because if someone interferes in an internal energy fight, both people fighting could be in danger.

,Should I use Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art)?'

My dantian is hot. Internal injuries are accumulating little by little.

I won't use Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art). There were too many eyes watching. And my pride wouldn't allow it. I grit my teeth and operate the Noecheon Gyeol (Lightning Heaven Art). Even if I die once, I was going to beat the beggar with Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style).

The old beggar was sweating profusely, but he curled up the corners of his lips. It was a face confident of victory. In fact, that beggar bastard's internal energy was about to strangle my heart.

My heart is throbbing.

I hid my potential and waited for a chance to counterattack. The moment that bastard bursts my heart and is convinced of his victory, an opportunity will arise. Then, I'll counterattack with Complete Recovery.

,Keueueueueuk! It's coming!'

The beggar's internal energy seizes my heart. I'm holding on, but there's a limit to that too.

Kureureung.

Thunder rumbled. I was startled to realize that the sound was ringing inside me. My internal energy strengthens regardless of my will. It instantly shook off the beggar's energy that had seized my heart.

Pajik.

Sparks flew from my body. Not blue, but black sparks. A thick demonic energy permeated the lightning.

,Cheonma Shingong (Demonic martial art) was stimulated by the beggar's internal energy!'

This was close to an unconscious reaction.

The situation was reversed. The black thunder devoured the beggar's internal energy and dug into the bastard's body in reverse. The old beggar's face is dyed with astonishment. The cold sweat he was shedding soaked his top.

I didn't miss that opportunity. Black thunder had the upper hand in compatibility. I don't know what the principle is, but black thunder destroys the beggar's internal energy so easily.

Starting with the palms of our hands touching as if in prayer, the change began. The beggar's blood vessels swelled and then burst. Blood dripped down from the bastard's arm. The black thunder didn't stop there and stirred inside. It moved like a living dragon.

The dragon coiled around the beggar's heart. It seized the heart.

,Kuaaaaaaaak!'

The old beggar couldn't stand it and shouted. The shocked martial artists of the Dokgo Family rushed at me.

,Soongae! You bastard, Seomjeondo (Lightning Flame Stone)! Get off him right now!'

Dokgohan's twin swords aim for my neck. I let go of the beggar and stepped back.

,1 second. I could have killed the beggar if I had just one more second, what a shame.'

I didn't kill him, but the situation was favorable to me. The old beggar didn't just lose his life, he suffered terrible internal injuries.

What's even more disappointing is that the black thunder has disappeared.

,Uwaeeeeeeek!'

The old beggar, who had collapsed to the ground, vomits blood.

,Elder-nim!'

The other beggars of the Beggars' Sect ran to the old beggar. The old beggar kept vomiting blood, but he raised his body. He desperately holds on to his mind. Energy poured out of his body as if flames were burning.

,Elder-nim! You burned Seoncheonjigi (Innate Qi)?!'

,Listen carefully. Seomjeondo (Lightning Flame Stone) Yeomguseok (Flame Stone) is dangerous. It's dangerous now, but it will become more dangerous over time. That bastard was born with the energy of a star. Not only that, but he harbors lightning and is raising a Demon Dragon. The Demon Dragon is still young. I'm afraid of when the Demon Dragon grows up! You have to kill the Demon Dragon when it's young!'

,We will risk our lives like Elder-nim!'

The beggars of the Beggars' Sect rush at me like moths to a flame. It's a desperate charge. It was obvious that they were overdoing it.

,Are they trying to create an opening for that old beggar by sacrificing their lives?'

I couldn't understand why they were going so far. I didn't particularly want to understand either.

,Martial artists of the Dokgo Family! Help the Hyeopgaek (Knight-errant) of the Beggars' Sect!'

,Yes! Gaju (Family Head)-nim!'

Even the martial artists of the Dokgo Family rush in.

They're not as fierce as the beggars of the Beggars' Sect. They're not prepared to throw away their lives like the beggars of the Beggars' Sect.

,Whatever. If they attack, I just have to kill them.'

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Noegwang (Lightning Flash).

The light of blue lightning flashed and drew a line. The line grazed the enemies. The bodies of the Beggars' Sect beggars and the martial artists of the Dokgo Family are cut into pieces. Due to the increased blood pressure, blood fountains gushed from their bodies.

The old beggar cleverly hides his appearance behind the falling blood, using it as a curtain, and rushes in.

I once again wield the fastest slash.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Noegwang (Lightning Flash).

Kaang!

It was blocked.

Dokgohan set up his twin swords and blocked Noegwang (Lightning Flash). As a price, Dokgohan stepped back. No, he stepped aside. So that the old beggar could reach me.

The old beggar's right hand was tattered. It was the dragon that moved that right hand. To be exact, an energy in the shape of a dragon. Red blood was mixed in, making the shape more clearly visible.

,Is it something like the mysteries of Kangryong Paljang (Dragon Subduing Palm)?'

My intuition tells me. It's dangerous. If I take it head-on, I'll die.

,Even if I have Complete Recovery, I don't intend to die easily.'

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Simjeukgeom (Heart is Sword).

The sword and palm rush towards each other. No one stops, and no one can stop. The match was decided in an instant. The sword split the blood dragon along with the beggar's palm. The beggar reacted to Simjeukgeom (Heart is Sword), but didn't stop. Because he knows that the moment he stops, he loses.

,Fucking... I have to kill this bastard here... Keok!'

My sword pierced the beggar's neck. I kicked the beggar's limp body.

,Elder-nim!!'

,Seomjeondo (Lightning Flame Stone) Yeomguseok (Flame Stone)!!'

The Beggars' Sect beggars who were still alive burst into anger and rushed in. Behind them, Dokgohan with a hardened expression and the martial artists of the Dokgo Family pounced. I gripped the sword handle tightly with my left hand. As a price for using Simjeukgeom (Heart is Sword), I couldn't put strength into my right hand.

,The most troublesome bastard is dead, so my left hand is enough! I still have Complete Recovery left!'

Dokgohan of the Dokgo Family? If I don't let my guard down, I was confident I could win.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Manroe Gaehwa (Ten Thousand Lightning Bloom).

A bud of lightning rotates and blooms. It seizes the space and scatters lightning. The martial artists who touched the lightning were electrocuted and collapsed to the floor. The output wasn't enough to kill them, but suppression alone was enough.

There was something I felt as I crossed swords with the martial artists of the Dokgo Family. The fact that these bastards' morale was already at rock bottom.

,Unlike the Beggars' Sect, the Dokgo Family doesn't really have any grudges against me.'

That doesn't mean they're slacking off. The Dokgo Family is also desperate in their own way. If they can't kill me, they'll be covered in all sorts of infamy.

I deliberately show a gap during the battle. It's a kind of feint. It's an obvious gap that a master would suspect first, but there were many here who considered even that gap an opportunity. A martial artist of the Dokgo Family flies like a mosquito and tries to stab me in the side with a knife.

I used the moment to cut the martial artist's body in half even faster. Blood and guts spill out. The martial artists of the Dokgo Family hesitated, perhaps because the scene was gruesome.

,Don't stop attacking! That bastard is tired! We have to kill him here!'

Dokgohan shouted. It was true that I was tired. But I wasn't so tired that I couldn't swing my sword. Dokgohan comes from the front. The swords in both hands shine and move brilliantly.

,I can see everything.'

If I can see it, I can deal with it. I didn't counterattack Dokgohan. I only blocked his twin swords. The Beggars' Sect beggars who were rushing in like moths to a flame had already been annihilated, and the martial artists of the Dokgo Family were also dying one by one.

That became a great pressure on Dokgohan. As the Gaju (Family Head) of the Dokgo Family, there would be nothing more painful than losing the family's martial artists.

,Martial artists are all assets. It takes at least decades of time and a lot of money to raise a martial artist from the countryside.'

That pressure made Dokgohan overdo it. I won in the psychological warfare.

,It's here!'

Two twin swords forcibly entering as if widening a gap. The left is a feint, and the right is real. Dokgohan threw a gamble. It was an attack I had been waiting for. It was perfect for a counterattack.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Heodo (Empty Way).

I jump into the air as if stepping on stairs. Dokgohan panics and twists the trajectory of his sword. I once again stepped on the air and rushed towards Dokgohan.

Seogeok!

I cut off Dokgohan's left arm.

,Keueuk!'

Dokgohan stepped back.

,I can't miss it.'

I followed and tried to aim for the bastard's neck by swinging my sword, but the angle didn't come out. It would be blocked no matter how I swung my sword. The problem was that the distance between the bastard and me was too narrow.

I boldly let go of the sword in my hand. Instead, I clenched my fist and punched Dokgohan in the chin. Pajijik! Lightning permeated Dokgohan's head through his fist. Dokgohan fainted, rolling his eyes.

,I won!'

I grab Dokgohan's collapsing neck.

,Stop. Do you want to see your Gaju (Family Head) die?'

,You despicable bastard! Are you taking the Gaju (Family Head) hostage! A dirty act worthy of a Ma Gyo (Demonic Sect) member!'

,You bastards are the despicable ones. You bribed the Giru (brothel) and put San Gong Dok (Meridian Destruction Poison) in the food and alcohol, and you came in a group to kill me. Is poison and Daguri (gang beating) the spirit of the righteous sect?'

,Killing evil is justice!'

,Try killing me. Instead, this bastard in my hand will also die. If you don't need the Dokgo Gaju (Family Head)... well, you can attack.'

It didn't matter if they attacked. I had Complete Recovery. And I thought those bastards would attack. The martial artist bastards in this world that I know are bastards who stab you in the back whenever they get a chance.

But surprisingly, they didn't attack. They kept a proper distance and confronted me.

A man who appeared to be an elder-level member of the Dokgo Family walked forward.

,...I'll let you go. Instead, put down the Gaju (Family Head) right now.'

,You're trying to make a deal with me? I'm a Madu (Demon Head) that you bastards have to kill, though?'

,The safety of the Gaju (Family Head) is more important than the life of a bastard like you!'

,Keukeukeu.'

I laughed pleasantly. If that's the case, the story changes.

,I have a condition. You bastards commit suicide. Then I'll let this bastard go.'
