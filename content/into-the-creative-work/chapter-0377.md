377. God's Atlantis

「World Leap (Fake) remembers you.」

I missed Sleipnir.

I thought it was fortunate in my head. If Sleipnir had made up his mind to fight me, there was a high probability that I would have died.

'...Well, judging by the reaction of the Lord of the Sky, he would have helped me along the way.'

「The Lord of the Sky clicks his tongue briefly.」

The Lord of the Sky did not blame me. He knows it too. That I'm not strong enough to face Sleipnir yet.

Rather, what I should be worried about is not the Lord of the Sky, but Sleipnir.

Sleipnir remembered me. I attacked first, so there's no way he'd remember me in a good way.

'I've made an enemy of a troublesome bastard.'

Sleipnir has the ability to travel through all sorts of zones. I'll have to watch my back no matter what zone I go to in the future.

I took a quick look around. This is because Sleipnir might have a special reason for coming here. But no matter how much I looked around, there was nothing special. In the end, I just wasted time and returned to Iron Root Village.

•••

"The reforging of the Hwaryeon Bido is complete."

Damonseu-nim came to me. He placed the Hwaryeon Bido on the table.

The straight, red blade was no different, except that it seemed to be shining more today. The black hilt is also the same as when I saw it before.

"Has something changed?"

"A lot has changed. You'll know if you look at the essence of the sword, not the appearance."

"I don't have that much skill yet...."

I didn't have the eye of a master craftsman. No matter how much I looked at it, I couldn't feel anything strange.

"Check the information of the Hwaryeon Bido. You can see the information window differently than before."

Originally, the Hwaryeon Bido was not from the 'God's Atlantis' world, so it was impossible to bring up the information window. However, it seems that the system recognized it during this reforging process, allowing the information window to be brought up.

「Hwaryeon Bido

A sword made using the scales of a fire dragon.

It is harder than any steel, and the sharpness of the blade does not diminish.

It has the ability to strengthen attributes when equipped.

Through the reforging process, it has acquired anti-magic power.

Rank: SS」

"SS Rank?!"

"Haha. Is that what you're surprised about? Well, I was a little surprised too. If you read the information window, S rank is perfect... but it received a stage one level higher."

"Is it because of the anti-magic power? What level of anti-magic power is it?"

Anti-magic power.

A power that opposes evil and demons, slightly different from divine power. Even if the user uses demonic power, anti-magic power, unlike divine power, does not repel it.

"I brought it right after finishing the reforging, so I don't know either. Shouldn't you, the owner, test the sword's performance?"

I nodded happily. This is my weapon. As the owner, I wanted to test it myself.

I jumped up from my seat.

"Are you going to the Ironwood Forest?"

"Yes. There are banshees there, so it's the perfect environment to test anti-magic power."

"I thought you would. But do you have any more holy water?"

"Holy water?"

"The holy water you gave me. It had a better effect than regular holy water.... It was the perfect holy water for making something. I want to get a lot of that holy water if possible. Do you have any more?"

I was surprised by the effect of the holy water. I thought holy water only had the effect of glowing faintly when applied to the body.

'Holy water is 2 points per bottle... One bottle is cheap, but it’s a different story when you’re talking about dozens of bottles. And I need to collect points, so where can I afford to spend them on holy water?'

I shook my head.

"I'm sorry. All I have is what I gave you last time."

"Then can you tell me where you got that holy water?"

"I got it from a gacha."

"Ah."

Damonseu-nim nodded and understood.

In this world, you could do gacha by consuming AP at the special store.

"But that holy water was a lot of fun. I never imagined that the effect would be different each time I used a bottle."

"...It came out differently? Wasn't it just glowing faintly?"

"It did shine. I was surprised at first, but there was no harm. In addition, water suddenly came out of the sword, so I failed the reforging once. No one expected the anti-magic power effect to come out."

"Random, huh...."

I felt a little disappointed.

•••

I swung the Hwaryeon Bido at the banshee. I swung it purely using my strength, without creating sword energy or lightning.

Kkiaaaaaaak!

The banshee, hit by the sword, screamed and disappeared. It's because of the anti-magic power of the Hwaryeon Bido. It was much more effective than I thought. With this level of anti-magic power, I could understand why the Hwaryeon Bido was SS rank.

'It's not just that. Attribute enhancement has also become stronger.'

I created lightning in my left hand. It's red lightning, not blue.

Previously, I could only use Red Thunder by creating lightning through the Hwaryeon Bido. Red Thunder is powerful, but it had the disadvantage of being difficult to control.

'But this time, I can use Red Thunder and Blue Thunder as I want.'

Pajijijik.

The red lightning turned blue as I wanted. It's less powerful than Red Thunder, but I could control it almost perfectly.

'I can alternate between Red Thunder and Blue Thunder whenever I need it!'

•••

"It's hard to put on armor alone, but I designed this so that you can put it on alone. But it'll still be hard to put on alone, so ask someone else to put it on for you if possible."

With the help of Damonseu-nim and the dwarves, I put on the armor I ordered, a silver full plate armor. It was heavy and sleek as I wanted, so it looked cool.

'However, it's stuffy even with the helmet on. There's no mouth area and the eye area is small. It restricts my vision... but I can cover this with other senses.'

When I raised the face guard, my face was revealed.

I looked in the mirror and checked the information of the full body armor.

「Ermita

Armor made with all their might by the Iron Root Tribe dwarves. It is hard yet light.

It has resistance to attributes.

It is designed to be human-friendly, so there are no restrictions on movement.

There are hidden blades in the gauntlet part.

Rank: S」

"The hidden sword you wanted is made so that the blade comes out from the wrist area when you clench your fist twice quickly."

I followed Damonseu-nim's words.

Cheolkeok!

Sharp blades reaching 50cm popped out from both gauntlets.

"Oh, oh!"

An exclamation popped out at the longer than expected blades. Looking at the blades, it seemed to be a double structure.

I created mana and infused sword energy into both blades. The blue sword energy is radiant.

"Rissa Ssu!"

"What are you saying all of a sudden?!"

"It's nothing. I was so happy that I misspoke."

"I'm glad you like it."

I then received the sword and cloak. The sword had a similar appearance to a bastard sword when looking at the exterior.

「Ermita's Sword

A sword made of ironwood.

It is light and hard. It does not break easily.

Rank: B」

"This is unexpected."

Damonseu-nim's face was dark.

"We also know that the quality is not as good as the armor. If you give us just one more week, we will make it again."

"No. It's okay. It's not like B rank swords are common."

In fact, my real purpose for coming here was not the armor and sword, but the cloak.

「Ermita's Cloak

A cloak made by changing the properties of ironwood into fiber.

It has excellent durability and does not get dirty easily.

It has high cold resistance.

Rank: A」

"The cloak was made excellently!"

"It's not as good as the armor, but it has the effect you want. Even if a blizzard rages, you will feel warm if you equip this cloak."

"Oh, oh! Thank you!"

"I hope you use it well. Are you leaving right away?"

"Yes. But I want to test its performance on the way."

"It's almost instinctive to want to use new things when you get them. On your way, test it out on the Temongki in the forest outside the zone."

"Yes. I will. Do you need any separate compensation?"

"We were just carrying out Duke Balderteu's orders. And we have over 50 boxes of beer you gave us left, so what would we want? Go back safely. Oh, and please say hello to Duke Balderteu for us."

I took everything I needed.

Damonseu-nim may not know, but I had been trading with the dwarves. A trade of exchanging beer for weapons. The beer I had was hard to find anywhere, so the dwarves readily exchanged it for beer. 50 boxes of beer? What a joke. I used at least 3,000 boxes of beer here.

I made eye contact with the dwarves behind me. They looked very happy, having taken at least 70 boxes of beer per person.

"Hehehe! Come back later!"

"We'll be waiting!"

"I'm already worried about beer since you're leaving!"

"Glory to Duke Balderteu! Bless Seong Yujin!"

I parted ways with them as they saw me off.

"I will visit again someday."

How much profit can I get from the weapons I get here? Just thinking about it makes me excited.

•••

Ukkik~!

A brown monkey monster with the head of a bird, a Temongki, threw a rock at me.

A fastball at the level of a major leaguer hit my head. But the helmet didn't even dent, let alone scratch. The impact wasn't much either. It's like being hit with a toy hammer.

'Acceleration.'

I grabbed 'Ermita's Sword' and ran towards the Temongki. The Temongki on the tree was surprised and started jumping to another tree to escape.

I chase after it using mana and acceleration. There was no way I couldn't catch it.

I caught up to the Temongki and swung my sword with all my might.

Chwaaaak!

The Temongki bled and fell to the floor.

'The sharpness is not as good as the Hwaryeon Bido... but because it's heavy, the feeling of cutting monsters is felt more vividly.'

It wasn't bad.

If I wear this armor and equip this sword, there will be some restrictions when using Yeongcheonryu, but the advantage of the armor compensates for the disadvantages. When wearing armor, you don't have to worry about the small things.

'I feel like I've become a tank. It would be perfect if I just had a fancy horse.'

I hunted the Temongki. Sometimes with the gauntlet's blade, sometimes without using weapons, I beat the Temongki to death with my bare hands, only wearing armor.

The Temongki couldn't harm me at all. The Temongki's attack can't even scratch the S rank armor. It felt like I was hunting with cheat keys.

Kuung! Kung!

I heard a loud footstep sound. I turn my body back. It's a troll over 5M in size. Thick, long plate legs and a blunt head with no hair. The dark green leather is so tough that it can't be damaged by most blades.

The troll was running towards me with an excited look. It seems that the blood smell of the Temongki lured the troll.

I can't just ignore the troll now. But it's not impossible to hunt either.

'Okay. Let's kill this bastard and leave the forest.'

Cheolkeok!

A blade popped out of the gauntlet. Blue sword energy is formed on the blade and flickers.

"My life for Bozi!"

I ran towards the troll. The bastard stretched out a hand as thick as a log and tried to grab me. I stepped on the bastard's hand and ran up his arm, arriving in front of his head.

"Kaaaak!"

I stabbed the sword into both of the bastard's eyes. The bastard twisted his body and hit me. I fell to the floor, rolled once, got up, and ran towards the troll again.

The troll, who had lost his eyes, rampaged wildly. I moved more carefully, avoiding the attacks, ran towards the bastard, and stabbed the gauntlet blade into him. Because there was sword energy, I could easily cut through the bastard's tough leather.

Kung!

The bloody troll knelt on the floor. I didn't miss the opportunity and cut off the troll's neck. Even a troll with excellent regeneration could only die if his neck was cut off.

「You have acquired 110 AP.」

I raised the face guard and took a deep breath of cool air. The cold air cools down my body heat.

Hwiieeeing.

The wind blew and fluttered the red cloak.

I felt disappointed.

'A beautiful woman should be watching this style that's exploding....'
