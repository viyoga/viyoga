Yuria entered the kitchen.

The maids, busily moving about in the kitchen, frowned at Yuria. There was some territoriality, but the situation was the main issue. They were already busy as hell because of the banquet to be held tomorrow evening, and now an outsider was coming in to take up a corner of the kitchen? If Linda, the Head of Management, hadn't been there, harsh words might have been spoken.

The Head Chef was dumbfounded after hearing Linda's words.

,Linda Head of Management-nim. You're saying you're going to entrust a trainee with a dessert? Even if they're enrolled in the Maid Academy, they haven't graduated yet. Are you going to entrust work to a student who isn't a professional? They could ruin Princess-jeonha's banquet."

"It's just one dessert. And the banquet hasn't even started yet. If that tanghulu dessert is substandard... we just won't serve it at the banquet. Head Chef, I'm asking for your cooperation. The ingredients are just small fruits, sugar, corn syrup, and skewers, so it's not an unreasonable request."

"......Haa. I'll allow it for exactly 30 minutes. As Linda Head of Management also knows, the kitchen is my domain. Not even the Head of Management can trespass."

"Of course. I know that, naturally. Thank you."

Linda smiled brightly. The tense atmosphere dissipated instantly. Yuria felt that she was admired by the maids, including the Head Chef. It was like a soft charisma. It felt like she was using it well, knowing it herself.

"Okay, Yuria-ssi, the ingredients are ready. What do we do?"

"It's nothing special. If you're chefs here, you should be able to watch once and follow along."

She took a well-washed small cherry tomato and skewered it, preparing it.

She put sugar, water, and corn syrup in a pot and made syrup. When the syrup was ready, she coated the skewered fruit with the syrup. The important thing here was to make the coating thin. If the coating was too thick, it would be too sticky and the sugar taste would be too strong, which could be unpleasant. After that, she put it in ice water to harden the coating and took it out.

The completed tanghulu was smooth without a single flaw. It was so glossy that it looked like a jewel.

"Tanghulu, huh. It's the first time I've heard the name, but it's a simple dessert."

"Fruit and sugar. The taste is predictably obvious..."

"If we serve such a simple dessert, won't the guests be disappointed? Still, the appearance is jewel-like, so it's okay."

The chefs' reactions were negative. Because it wasn't anything too special.

"Let's try a sample first."

Linda brought a cherry tomato tanghulu to her mouth. She chewed the tomato without much expectation.

Wagjak!

"......!"

The thin sugar coating crunched crisply, and the juice burst out. The juice was sweeter than she had imagined. As the sugar coating melted, it mixed with the juice, maximizing the sweetness. A fun texture and the sweetness of the juice spreading in her mouth.

"It's really delicious. It would be a wanna-be dessert for people who like sweets."

Of course, Linda also liked sweets. She tasted the strawberry tanghulu next to her. The strawberry was more delicious than the cherry tomato.

At Linda's reaction, the other maids also lit up their eyes and tasted the tanghulu one by one.

"It's sweet. It's delicious. I thought the sugar would stick to my teeth... but it feels like it's mixing with the juice because it's thin."

"It feels like I'm eating junk food... but the taste is different for each fruit, so I want to eat them all."

"Ah, aah.... The snack I wanted was here?"

"The cooking process is simple, and it looks pretty... it seems like it would be okay to serve it at the banquet as one of the desserts?"

"Even though it's simple, it's a snack I haven't tasted in Neo London. It's not bad. Yuria-ssi, was it? Can you tell us more about this dish?"

The maids' tone became softer. It was the effect of sweetness. There's no woman who dislikes sweets. In [Baekhwan] too, tanghulu was a dessert that was popular among young women.

"Tanghulu is a dish from the ancient country of True China. That's why it's not known in Neo London."

The Head Chef's eyes widened.

"True China? That's an ancient country mentioned along with Great Korea and Unbelievable Japan. Good. The citizens of London are now turning their attention to ancient countries because of LK-99. If it's food from such an ancient country... the guests will be interested. I'll put tanghulu on the banquet table. The cooking method is simple, so there shouldn't be any problems. What do you think, Head of Management-nim?"

"I agree. However, if Yuria-ssi allows it."

"Tanghulu isn't a dish I developed. You can use it however you want. And... please don't reveal my name."

Yuria didn't want to gain fame just for introducing tanghulu. That was a fame that had no benefit. In the first place, she wasn't interested in fame or anything like that.

"I understand. Hehe. I feel happy for some reason today. We'll be going now."

The chefs, who finished greeting Linda, were busy making tanghulu.

Yuria and Linda went around various places in the mansion besides that.

From those in charge of cleaning to the servants who moved supplies to the warehouse. Roughly combined, there would be over 100 people. And most of them were maids. The number of butlers was small. It was a natural thing. Because the owner of this mansion was Princess-jeonha. It wasn't a place where men could come and go carelessly.

They went into the office. Linda handed over the documents that were arranged on one side of the desk.

"Since you came from the academy, you should know how to read documents, right? This is the budget and expenditure statement. Please take a look and see if there's anything strange."

"......Could this also be a test?"

"Hehe. What do you think?"

Yuria skimmed through the documents. It didn't take even a second to look at one document. Even though it seemed like she was looking roughly, all the contents written in the documents entered her head.

"Here, here, here. There's a problem with the expenses. I suspect corruption."

"...To think you'd grasp everything in less than 3 minutes. You're amazing. You must know the market prices accurately.... Do you perhaps also know the market prices for food ingredients and furniture?"

"Market analysis is also one of the evaluations at the academy."

"I know that too. Because I'm from the academy. But I remember that it's not an evaluation that grasps all markets..."

"It just happened to coincide with the market I was grasping."

"A coincidence."

Linda gave a bitter smile. She didn't think it was a coincidence at all.

"Next, please look at this document."

"...This is the list of guests coming to this banquet."

"Yes. It's obvious, but you mustn't leak it anywhere. This banquet is private, so the entry of reporters is also controlled."

"I haven't been able to ask yet, but what is the purpose of this banquet? Princess-jeonha's birthday is next year."

"It's to celebrate the development of Neo London. You know that superconductors have been commercialized, right? Since superconductors started in Neo London, the value of Neo London has increased even more. Princess-jeonha couldn't just stand still."

"...Is the Chairman of LK Company also coming?"

"You mean Eugene Meyer, the Junior Baron? Yes. The owner of the superconductor, so to speak, must come, of course. We've already received his approval. Princess-jeonha will directly praise his achievements. Unfortunately, the main character of this banquet won't be Princess-jeonha, but him."

"I see."

"Huh? Did you just smile?"

"I didn't smile."

"Aww. I think you did.... I understand. You must be feeling good because you'll be meeting a patron at the banquet."

"...So you knew after all."

"Don't feel unpleasant. We can't bring outsiders into the mansion without definite information."

"It's a natural thing, so I don't feel unpleasant."

Dugun dugun.

Unlike what she said indifferently, Yuria's heart was beating faster than usual. She was meeting Master after a long time. Just that fact alone gave her body vitality.

"Foreign companies are thoroughly excluded from the list."

"Superconductors will be supplied domestically first."

"As I see it, there's no problem with the list."

"That's right. Next is this document. It's a document related to personnel."

This was also a considerably sensitive document. As such, it would be a document that had been filtered once before coming in. Yuria silently received the test disguised as work.
