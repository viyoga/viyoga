Between tangling my body with Mi-ryeong, training at a leisurely pace, and breaking in Bing Soha, time flew by.

Four years had passed since I entered this secret realm before I even realized it.

Even as I continuously absorbed spirit creatures and kept up my training, my cultivation level refused to rise easily.

'Am I blocked?'

An invisible wall bars my way.

A wall that simple training alone cannot breach.

'I'll break through eventually.'

There was no impatience, no despair. What was bound to happen had simply happened. And there was a clue.

The Lake of Benevolence—three years had passed since the Murim Alliance Leader threw himself into it, and he still hadn't emerged. If I entered that place, something would be there.

"Mi-ryeong. You really won't go into the lake?"

"Nooo. I'm satisfied with my current training, you know. A lake where you experience human lives through hallucinations? I have no desire to do such a horrifying kind of training."

Mi-ryeong, who had been focused on her training until moments ago, answered with a languid expression. The breath that flowed from her lips was hot enough to instantly burn and melt any ordinary creature that couldn't manipulate qi.

This was because Mi-ryeong's soul was currently connected to the Mountain of Flames. She was monopolizing the Mountain of Flames and using it for her own cultivation. Unlike me, who was blocked by a wall, she had a higher cultivation level than me and was still inching forward.

"It's not like you're going into that lake for training either, dear husband."

"Right. Training's about third priority. First is the lake, second is killing the Murim Alliance Leader. Ah. The aftermath of the battle might collapse the secret realm. If you think it's dangerous, take the kids and get out of the secret realm."

If the secret realm collapsed, Mi-ryeong and my subordinates wouldn't be able to train. That was the reason I'd been dragging my feet.

Of course, four years alone wouldn't be enough.

Inside this secret realm, dozens of absolute masters at the Three Purities realm were each absorbed in their own training, yet not a single one had reached the Harmony realm.

"Understood, dear husband. Just trust me. Looking after your subordinates is also a virtuous wife's duty, after all. Hehe. So. When are you planning to enter the lake?"

"Now."

Snap.

I flicked my finger, and clouds gathered outside the pavilion's window.

"Hmm. Shall I escort you to the lake?"

"No need. It's right there if I fly. Ah, there are three years left until the truce agreement with the Murim Alliance expires... If I don't return within three years, take care of things for me."

"Leave it to me with complete confidence."

In this world, along with my master Wi-yu, Mi-ryeong was the person I could trust most.

"I'll be back."

"Have a safe trip~"

I rode the clouds and flew to the lake.

Murim Alliance martial artists were stationed in front of the lake. They had set up barriers and formations around it, controlling those who tried to enter.

In game terms, it was like a top-tier guild monopolizing an efficient hunting ground.

The moment I stepped into the formation's domain, the clouds beneath my feet scattered in an instant. An invisible pressure bore down on my qi pathways, my internal energy wavered against my will.

My body free-fell toward the ground.

Even in the midst of that, my eyes—the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil—saw straight through the formation.

'So this is the famous Zhuge Eight Trigrams Formation of the Zhuge Clan.'

The most renowned formation of the Zhuge Clan.

A formation that only the clan head and their successor among the Zhuge direct line could learn.

'A formation built on the eight trigrams, is it. So profound I can't believe a human created it.'

Zhuge Eight Trigrams Formation: Immutability.

Among the eight forms of the Zhuge Eight Trigrams Formation, the one specialized in imposing restrictions on intruders.

Its influence was strong enough to affect even the avatar of the Harmony realm, Geumje.

'I'll admit it's impressive... but it's useless on the day I use the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.'

The Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, which comprehended targets just by looking at them, could be called the natural enemy of formations and barriers.

'It's like looking at a problem with the answer key already written on it.'

As I fell toward the ground, I formed hand seals with both hands.

Seals that each formed a trigram. I rapidly formed six of the eight different seals.

The formation's restriction on me vanished as if washed away. I spun in a midair flip and landed lightly on the ground.

I scanned my surroundings once. Beyond the Zhuge Eight Trigrams Formation, multiple other formations were layered on top. There were even physical traps designed to confuse spatial perception and cleverly hidden.

'Without the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, I might have wandered around here for a while. Then gotten annoyed and laid waste to the whole area.'

With overwhelming power, one could smash formations to pieces.

With the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, there was no need. The path was visible, so I just had to walk it.

Before long, I arrived at the lake's entrance. Murim Alliance martial artists were gathered in force. There were even some elders of the Alliance. But my gaze was drawn to a single man at the center.

Outwardly, he appeared to be a man in his early to mid-thirties. His appearance was immaculate. Unlike the other martial artists armed with cold weapons like swords and spears, his hand held a white feathered fan.

He was the Murim Alliance's strategist, and the Zhuge Clan's retired patriarch—Hyeonseong, Zhuge Yun.

"They say the Thunder General, as a distant descendant of the Great Sage Equal to Heaven, was born with eyes that pierce the principles of the world... So the rumors were true. The Flower Eye Golden Pupil. No—since lightning resides in those eyes instead of fire, it would be more fitting to call them the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil."

"...Such rumors spread? Where did you hear that? In the murim, most don't even know I'm the Thunder General."

The continent was vast as hell.

Even if word travels a thousand li, it can't travel hundreds of thousands.

"The name of the Thunder General itself is spreading quietly, centered around the Imperial Palace. Most people's thinking is narrow-minded, so they find it difficult to accept that the Heavenly Demon and the Thunder General are the same person. Even if the two names are identical. I personally investigated and know of the Thunder General's achievements."

"Well, those who know, know. Since His Imperial Majesty acknowledged it, there's no problem. I am the Thunder General and the Heavenly Demon."

"Yes. For us, that is precisely the greatest problem. The one who holds His Imperial Majesty's favor is the Heavenly Demon—is that not the imperial seal of the murim? We went to considerable trouble to prevent your achievements from spreading through the murim."

"So the reason the Thunder General's name strangely never reached the murim was your doing."

"Since we couldn't block it entirely, we spread rumors that the Heavenly Demon and the Thunder General were not the same person as widely as possible. Fortunately, the Thunder General's area of activity was the northern frontlines, making it easy to block rumors. Above all, the Heavenly Demon took no measures whatsoever."

If I stepped forward myself and revealed that I was the Thunder General, all of Zhuge Yun's machinations would become meaningless.

From my perspective, I viewed the imperial military and the murim separately, so there was no reason to reveal it. Above all, if I did, there was a near-certain chance that the irritating rumor—that the Heavenly Demon is strong because he receives the Emperor's favor—would spread.

"I didn't come here to have that kind of conversation with you."

Heavenly Demon Divine Art: Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step.

I stepped forward with my right foot.

The formation trembled, and the martial artists nearby hurriedly raised their internal energy to resist.

'The formation's influence has drastically reduced the Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step's power. Should I unleash it at full force and smash the formation itself?'

"Kuh... So this is the Heavenly Demon Sovereign Step... Even under the formation's protection, it's this much... It's beyond what the history books describe."

"I don't care about your impressions. Did you really think you could stop me with rabble like this?"

"Please wait a moment. We have no intention of fighting you."

"You're trusting in that worthless truce period?"

"I am not that naive. I know that inside the secret realm, justifications and causes from outside become meaningless. We set up formations and control this place not to stop you, the Heavenly Demon. It is to prevent meaningless sacrifices on the part of the Murim Alliance. This lake is too dangerous."

"Dangerous? Does the lake attack people?"

"It doesn't attack directly, but it tempts. There is an tremendous fortuitous encounter within. Three absolute masters of the Three Purities realm, bewitched by it, leaped into this lake. The result—half a year later, they were corpses floating on the surface."

"Ho. So the Three Purities realm can't handle it. What happened to the Alliance Leader?"

"The Alliance Leader did not return. He didn't even become a corpse. Whether he crossed the Lake of Benevolence to another lake, or is still submerged within it... We have no way of knowing."

"Is it alright to blabber that so easily? Isn't that information about your Alliance Leader?"

"I know that the outcome of the war between the Heavenly Demon Cult and the Murim Alliance ultimately depends on you, the Heavenly Demon, and the Alliance Leader. If the Alliance Leader is defeated... the Murim Alliance will acknowledge defeat. That very day, the Heavenly Demon Cult will become master of the martial world. Conversely, if the Alliance Leader is victorious... the Heavenly Demon Cult, without you, will slowly crumble."

"The Nine Sects and One Clan that make up the Murim Alliance... no, it's Seven Sects now. Did all seven sects agree to this?"

"We haven't yet heard Shaolin's opinion, but does that matter?"

Shaolin alone waging war against the Heavenly Demon Cult to the bitter end was impossible—the weight class simply didn't match. In the end, Shaolin would have no choice but to follow the tide. If not, they'd close their gates.

"If you're not going to fight me, move aside."

"We will."

Zhuge Yun gestured, and the Murim Alliance martial artists stepped back in unison.

Every single one wore a gloomy expression. For a moment, I thought I'd walked into a funeral.

I approached the Lake of Benevolence. The lake's pristine surface reflected me like a mirror.

'Still, nothing visible but the lake's name. Not a single small fish inside the lake.'

Feeling a slight unease, I nonetheless stepped my leg into the lake.

I hadn't particularly applied strength to my body, yet my body, which should have floated up from buoyancy, did not rise. Even after submerging to the top of my head, it was the same. My body wouldn't float. I was still walking on the lake's bottom.

'Still, water is water. Thanks to the Water Blessing's effect, I can breathe underwater. I can feel my stamina recovering very slightly, too.'

I looked around the lakebed. In case there was a spirit creature with a special power that didn't register to my qi sense. But there was nothing on the lakebed. Eventually, I arrived at the lake's center.

'No path connecting to another place either. Release Absolute Focus.'

I, who should have been standing on the lakebed, felt my body sinking progressively downward.

Even with the Water Blessing active, my breath caught in my throat.

My consciousness grew distant.
