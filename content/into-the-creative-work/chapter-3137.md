Ingrid closes both eyes and receives the brilliant light falling from the sky.

The five sigils carved into her body blaze with fierce intensity.

'The magic is ready. I can cast Hellfire at any moment.'

Throwing Hellfire to disrupt Ingrid—that was impossible.

'It won't work. Right now, Ingrid is under the protection of causality. Even if I mobilized every means at my disposal, harming her in her current state is impossible.'

Even if it were possible, I wouldn't have attacked her at this exact moment.

Damnable curiosity keeps my gaze fixed on Ingrid.

'I haven't decided on a method yet, but I'll reach Grade 8 someday too. I'm observing for that time. If I can gain even a sliver of information, it's a net gain.'

I focused my mind and observed Ingrid.

Ingrid is ascending to the rank of Grade 8. The five sigils are pulling her body and mind up to a higher plane.

What exactly that means, I cannot comprehend. But I can feel it. A transcendent being is aiding Ingrid.

'...It's as if the world itself is screaming. As though it's trying to exclude a being that should not exist, interfering with her ascension.'

Overcoming that interference through sheer physical force alone seems impossible.

'If she can't ascend to Grade 8 through her own power alone, then isn't it impossible to reach Grade 8 through enlightenment by oneself? What even is the enlightenment needed to reach Grade 8 in the first place?'

Ritual-based ascension, I roughly understand. Using the power accumulated through the ritual, with the aid of a related transcendent, one resists the world.

'She looks anxious.'

She was like a candle flame on the verge of being snuffed out. Ingrid gritted her teeth and endured.

Had she fully succeeded in the Trial of the North Wind, she likely would have ascended to Grade 8 without much difficulty, unlike now.

The light falling from the sky fades, and the five sigils carved into her body vanish without leaving a single trace.

“…….”

I quietly swallowed dryly.

The intense presence and pressure emanating from Ingrid. Her body and mind have reached a higher plane. She has succeeded in reaching Grade 8.

"Aaaaaaaaagh!"

Ingrid screamed and collapsed to the ground. She vomits blood. The backlash of an unstable ascension—the price paid for completing an incomplete trial—has crashed down upon her.

She ascended, but fell one step short, unable to fully block the recoil.

Normally, Ingrid should have died despite succeeding. Yet Ingrid staggered to her feet.

'...Ingrid's unique skill, Gwon To Jung Rae, has activated.'

Fatal wounds disappear, physical abilities are enhanced for 3 minutes, and incoming damage is reduced by 70%. When the 3 minutes end, the accumulated damage is returned to her only to the extent that it won't kill her.

In other words, Ingrid has succeeded in her ascension.

"You bastard!!!"

Marit screamed. Her voice reverberated, ringing out. Her voice cracked. Her vocal cords must have been damaged.

"We have to kill Ingrid here!! No—if we can't kill her, we die! Three minutes! We just need to hold out for exactly three minutes!!"

Immediately after, Ingrid moved first.

She hurled my one and only battle axe at me, then sprinted toward Marit. Her speed was completely different from before. By my estimation, she'd gotten more than three times faster.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 11]

As a mage, I tried to avoid using abilities in order to refine my skill.

'Right now, I have to use an ability even if I don't want to. The speed of that flying axe is no joke.'

Dodge? Or block?

I chose the latter. I was curious about the intensity of the force loaded into that axe.

[Barrier]

A carefully calculated five-layered barrier.

The instant my thoughts returned to normal speed, five transparent walls manifested, protecting me.

KWANG KWANG KWANG KWANG!

Three barrier layers shattered at once, and the battle axe was deflected away.

'More manageable than I thought...'

KWANG KWANG KWANG KWANG!

Suddenly, a battle axe materialized in empty air and flew in from the exact same direction, the exact same position as before, slamming into the barrier. The remaining two layers break, and it aims for my head.

[Crisis Detection has activated. Remaining stacks: 6]

Flash Step, forcibly triggered.

In the slowed world, the blade of the battle axe closes in on me. I gritted my teeth and moved to the side. The axe passed by me and vanished into empty air.

'Appears out of nowhere, disappears out of nowhere. This is... an application of a unique skill.'

Grade 7 awakens a unique skill; Grade 8 applies it.

Gwon To Jung Rae.

To retreat once, then return.

'That battle axe just now appeared in empty space and vanished. And it wasn't just appearing randomly. Same form as the first, same trajectory. The second axe appeared one second later. If this were a game, you'd call it a follow-up attack.'

I understood the ability. Which meant I could counter it.

Weaving a magic formula, I check Ingrid's position. She's fighting Marit. She's taking on Marit—who's armed with a dagger and a gun—bare-handed. The blood scattering across the snowfield belonged only to Marit.

Ingrid's massive fist drove into Marit's abdomen. The first fist was blocked with a forearm, but the phantom fist that appeared one second later could not be blocked.

The instant a contract appeared beside Marit, it was torn apart and vanished. I don't know the details of the ability, but it nullified the damage.

I thrust my hand forward. Aiming precisely at Ingrid's head, I cast my spell.

A crimson-black laser shot forth from the crimson-black magic circle.

[Death Ray]

Ingrid kicked Marit's body aside with her foot and swung her fist toward the Death Ray.

The crimson-black laser couldn't overcome the barbarian warrior's fist and was torn apart.

The skin on the back of Ingrid's hand was stripped away. I saw possibility.

'She's not invincible.'

Survive three minutes?

Only twenty seconds had passed so far. Considering my condition, simply enduring won't be enough.

I could see Ingrid pausing to catch her breath.

'The aftereffects of the rank-up backlash that even Gwon To Jung Rae couldn't fully undo still linger. This is doable.'

Ingrid's gaze turns toward me.

Sharp. Her killing intent becomes a weapon, seeming to hack away at my body.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 5]

Within accelerated thought, I calculate the spell formula.

Uncharacteristically, I calculated the formula two or three times. This is my first time using this magic in actual combat. But I was certain it would succeed.

I focused my mind and exhaled.

The instant the thought acceleration ended, seven magic circles appeared around me.

The seven magic circles resonate with each other. What would normally be a complex ritual process is compressed through the resonance between the circles.

"A mage…!!"

Ingrid, who had been approaching while erupting with hatred, suddenly stopped in her tracks.

"Good instincts. If you'd come any closer, you'd have been caught up in it."

The resonating seven magic circles halt. The compressed ritual process has been completed.

If I had my way, I'd drag this out, but this magic doesn't depend on my will alone, so it was impossible.

I chant the spell.

"Summoning."

[Summoning: Kirsi]

The surrounding space begins to quake.

Space expands, contracts, and distorts.

Unfortunately, this isn't something I control directly. It's merely an automatic process for a safe summoning.

Flash.

A heavy presence makes itself felt behind my back.

I don't need to turn around to know. A massive cherry tree has appeared behind me. The cherry tree's shadow falls beneath my feet.

Wind blows, and cherry blossom petals and snowflakes scatter together.

Crackle, crackle!

Pink electricity sparked between the cherry blossom petals.

I felt a powerful will-link connect me to the phantom beast cherry tree, Kirsi.

-Is this snow? It's fascinating seeing it in person. The lightning element is abundant too…. Not bad at all.

I felt surprise from Kirsi.

At the same time, Kirsi eroded the surrounding trees. Trees of other species all became cherry trees in full bloom. The ability to temporarily make surrounding trees into her own clones.

The lightning element grew stronger by another degree.

"…Is that your trump card?"

"One of my trump cards. Honestly, it's my first time in real combat, so even I can't be sure how far I can take it."

-I can do plenty. I've got enough stored energy. I'll earn my keep, so don't worry.

"Whatever it is… I'll tear you apart along with that tree! AAAAAAAAARGH!"

She charges with a war cry. The surrounding cherry trees shook violently in unison. The pouring cherry blossom petals became lightning, filling the surroundings.

Ingrid closed the distance in an instant and thrust her fist at me. I thrust my fist out as well. The two fists collided in midair, and a shockwave burst forth.

One second later, Ingrid's fist appeared from thin air, aiming for me. I knew it was coming, so I dodged to the side.

"A mage blocking my fist?! With magic, no less?!"

"Kirsi and I are connected. Thanks to that, I can pull off things like this even without magic. But… as expected, I'm no match for an 8th-rank warrior in a straight fight."

Bang!

One of the cherry trees burst like a balloon. Kirsi had absorbed the damage I took.

I clench my left hand. A cherry-blossom-colored lightning—too bright to simply call pink—flashed. It struck Ingrid's head. The lightning exploded, burning Ingrid's entire body. Ingrid gritted her teeth and endured.

"I am blessed by lightning! You cannot kill me with thunder!"

"I disagree. I think I can kill you just fine."

I thrust my right hand into the air and clench it. Cherry-blossom-colored lightning gathered and became magic.

6th-rank lightning-element arcane magic.

[Thunder Nail]

I drive the nail of lightning straight down into Ingrid's head.

CRAAAAASH!

The destructive force is one thing, but Thunder Nail's greater purpose is to forcibly electrocute the enemy and temporarily reduce their lightning resistance. The nail of lightning didn't disappear—it lodged itself in Ingrid's head.

Ingrid, electrocuted, grimaces and throws her head back.

'A headbutt, is it.'

[Using Lightning Leap.]

Everything within a 500-meter radius is lightning. If I wanted distance, I could open it in an instant. But I moved behind her instead.

[Thunder Nail]

KZZZAAAK!

The thunder nail drove into Ingrid's flank. A spatial ambush, I reckoned, wouldn't work on her a third time.

"That's two."

"Krrgh…! You think this pathetic—!"

Ingrid launched herself, whipping out a kick. A spinning heel strike. She'd chosen counterattack over evasion.

KWAAANG!

It lands on my flank. One second later, the second kick follows, and I weather it with Kirsi's help.

[Thunder Nail]

KZZZAAAAAK!

The third thunder nail buried itself in Ingrid's left shoulder.

I felt blood trickling at the corner of my mouth. Even with Kirsi absorbing the bulk of the damage, some of my internal organs had ruptured.

"I'll just rip this damned nail out and be done with it."

Ingrid grabbed the thunder nail lodged in her flank and tried to pull it free, but lightning burst from it and she froze as if electrocuted.

"It won't be that easy. That's why I forced out a third nail. That impressive lightning resistance of yours should've dropped to merely 'a bit strong' by now. One more Thunder Break and you're dead."

"Then…! I'll kill you before you can cast another spell!"

Not a bad call.

A mage is at their most vulnerable right before casting.

But unfortunately for her, the spell was already prepared.

[Thunder Cherry Blossom]

The drifting cherry petals turned to lightning and poured down on Ingrid.

"AAAAAAGGGGGHHHH!"

Ingrid's eyes and mouth tore open wide. Her entire body must be feeling the agony of being ripped apart.

"A mage's familiar is usually linked to the mage. Either it chants spells on the mage's behalf, or it has magic unique to itself."

Weaving the formula, I stretched a hand toward the sky.

Lightning gathered in an instant and began to rotate. In an environment saturated with the lightning element, my electric magic was far more efficient than usual.

Watching the lightning spin and compress into the shape of a 卍, I glare at Ingrid. Even after taking hundreds, thousands of thunder blossoms, she doesn't fall—she thrusts her fist at me.

[Using Lightning Leap.]

In a single moment, I opened 100 meters of distance.

[Thunder Break]

A bolt more colossal than any lightning I'd ever seen slammed down onto the earth.

The thunderflash lit up heaven and earth, then vanished.

Part of the mountain range was annihilated.
