Honestly, I was a bit disappointed.

'She's so skinny that you can see her bones. It wouldn't be strange if she starved to death right away.... But her eyes are still alive.'

She was skinnier than a skinny model you can see in reality overseas. It was like looking at a patient with anorexia.

Even a beggar in the city wouldn't be this bad.

I can understand this too. This village is at a poor level. When I think about it, her mother, Nell Rins's corpse, was also very thin.

It seems like the time for secondary sexual characteristics has already passed a long time ago. I approached her while feeling distrust towards the original work.

'Her skin is white. That means she didn't mainly do farm work. Does this mean she didn't go out of the house at all?'

It's rare for commoners to have white skin. In commoner families, children work even if they haven't had their coming-of-age ceremony. From simple housework to farm work, and so on.

'Except for being too skinny, she doesn't seem to have any illnesses or injuries.'

I reached out my hand towards Yuria.

Yuria looked at my hand but didn't flinch or avoid it. She calmly accepted my hand without changing her expression. I liked that she understood her place well.

A handful of Yuria's dry, gray hair was placed on my hand. I rubbed her hair with my fingers. The gray ash on her hair fell off, revealing her original blue-black hair.

She was hiding her original hair color by covering her hair with ash.

'Blue-black hair and blue eyes. It's a characteristic of the Helbrit direct line. It's definitely Yuria Grace.'

Of course, just because someone has blue-black hair and blue eyes doesn't necessarily mean they're from the Helbrit family. This fantasy world is full of people with all sorts of hair colors. There are people with pink hair as well as purple hair.

Even in the city of Pters, where the Fruckus baronial residence is located, you could find at least 30 people with blue-black hair and blue eyes.

I plan to thoroughly hide Yuria's existence from the Plun Knights. I want to do the same to Hacent, but I can't help it.
