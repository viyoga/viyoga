"What the hell is going on!"

Lee Mun-hyeon, the head of the Korean Black Cross Society, shouted at the executives gathered in the conference room.

Lee Mun-hyeon, who usually seemed like a friendly neighborhood uncle, furrowed his brow like a demon and unleashed his anger, making the conference room silent. The seven executives lowered their heads, avoiding eye contact with Lee Mun-hyeon.

"Chief Baek of the Investigation Department!"

"...Yes, Director."

Baek Ji-wook, the chief of the Investigation Department, stepped forward.

The Korean Black Cross Society is divided into three departments: Support, Investigation, and Combat. Baek Ji-wook was the top manager of the Investigation Department.

The Investigation Department investigates vampires. They find vampires hiding somewhere or investigate anything suspected of being caused by vampires.

"You tell me! What the hell is going on!"

Baek Ji-wook barely suppressed a sigh. The atmosphere at headquarters was serious. He couldn't afford to give them any reason to nitpick.

He recalled the incident that had occurred two hours earlier and opened his mouth.

"...Around 11:10 AM today, the headquarters' commuter bus suddenly accelerated and crashed into the entrance of the headquarters. The casualties include the death of Hunter Park Gi-soo, and Hunter Jo Joo-hyeon is in critical condition and currently in a coma. The cause of the bus's sudden acceleration is still under investigation..."

"...Chief Baek of the Investigation Department, I'm warning you. If you're going to spout that kind of report, just get out of here right now!"

"...I apologize. Although there is nothing certain, we have determined that this incident was a terror attack by a vampire and have begun the investigation. There was no one on the express bus. It seems that a vampire True Ancestor used psychic powers to carry out the bus terror."

"Now you're talking properly. It's up to you guys, the Investigation Department, to find out who that vampire True Ancestor is... and what is the True Ancestor's ability?"

"We are presuming it's telekinesis."

"Telekinesis? What a joke! The bus didn't fly in, it drove in! The wheels turned. And telekinesis? Are you messing with me?"

"There is a possibility that telekinesis was used to precisely control the bus's driving."

"Okay, let's say that's true. But the bus crashed at 300 km/h. Do you think that makes sense?! The bus was going at KTX speed!"

"......We will investigate thoroughly."

"How?"

"We will summon 80% of the investigators across the country and find all the vampires hiding in Seoul."

"Not enough. Additionally, summon half of the Hunters spread across the country. And scour the nearby CCTV to find him."

Huff, huff. Lee Mun-hyeon breathed heavily. Perhaps because he had vented his anger, his ghostly face relaxed slightly.

"......Combat Department Head."

"Yes."

"Tell the Hunters to be careful. The headquarters was openly attacked, so wouldn't they attack other places? For the time being, act in teams of three."

"Understood."

"How is Jo Joo-hyeon?"

"Surgery is in progress. The doctor says the success rate is below 30%."

"......We'll have to pray to God. Assign Hunters to protect Jo Joo-hyeon nearby. And Support Department Head."

"Yes, Director."

"Contact the National Intelligence Service and request assistance. This is a declaration of war from that vampire bastard, but from Korea's perspective, it is a terror attack by a vampire, so they will actively step in."

Lee Mun-hyeon jumped up from his seat.

"I'm going to the Blue House."

He glared sharply at the assembled executives and said.

"...This attack is an unprecedented crisis in the history of the Korean Black Cross Society. You know that this is an emergency without me having to say it. Find him and kill him. If we are seen as a joke by those vampire bastards, I trust you all know what will happen to Korea..."

Lee Mun-hyeon stepped outside.

Due to the intervention of someone who was not originally planned, fate began to flow differently.

Moon Ji-hyeok scratched his head as he looked at the female police officer from the Traffic Division in front of him.

Officer Yang Jeong-min. With her neat hairstyle that came down to her collarbone, she looked very kind, but he had been meeting her every day lately. It was definitely not intentional. It was a coincidence. The problem is that the coincidence happens quite frequently, so he's worried that it might be fate.

"Mr. Moon Ji-hyeok. Why do you keep following me? Are you a stalker?"

"I'm the one who catches stalkers. I'm not following you, I'm here for work."

"What for?"

"About the major express bus crash that happened today."

"Why? Are you in charge of that case?"

"No. It's not our department's job... it's just. Something's strange. I watched the CCTV footage, and isn't it too fast for a sudden acceleration? Plus, it looks like there's no one in the driver's seat..."

On the internet, it was famous as a haunted bus. A ghost with a grudge against the company possessed the bus and crashed it. ...That's the rumor going around.

This mystery is attracting people's attention.

'...It's ridiculous, but if vampires exist in this world, there's a possibility that ghosts exist too. Plus, the atmosphere inside the police station is hush-hush about this accident.'

Even though it happened in the jurisdiction of the police station he belonged to, the police station had the same atmosphere as usual.

"Um. The Traffic Division Chief told us to ignore this, so... we don't know much about it either."

"Is that so..."

Strange.

In the past, he would have just listened and moved on since it was another department's job anyway, but now he was feeling a sense of unease.

'Maybe the government has known about vampires since a long time ago. And... if this was done by a vampire...?'

Moon Ji-hyeok decided to take the time to investigate this case personally and carefully.

"I understand. Then I'll be going now."

"Wait a minute. Eat this on your way. It's delicious."

Yang Jeong-min came closer. In that instant, the woman's dizzying scent stung his nose, startling him.

"Heok!"

"...What's wrong? Do you not like green grape candy?"

"Ah, no. I'll enjoy it. Thank you."

Moon Ji-hyeok quickly moved away from Yang Jeong-min.

'...I'm crazy. Thinking that a woman's neck looks delicious... I really feel like I've become a vampire. I need to go back to being human quickly.'

It was a desire that seemed to combine sexual desire and appetite.

Moon Ji-hyeok felt ashamed of his sudden desire and raised his hand to cover his face, then quickly lowered it, feeling a chill.

A vampire's body temperature was lower than a human's.

'How awful.'

I entered Yukseong-dong at dusk, when it was starting to get dark. Located on the outskirts of Seoul, this is a so-called shantytown. And this is the place with the worst public safety among the shantytowns.

Entertainment establishments are lined up one after another, and there are also illegal brothels operating secretly. And Yukseong-dong is a place where many gangsters and Joseonjok people live.

'A brothel... Should I go check it out?'

But I soon dropped the idea. The entertainment establishments here looked low-quality at first glance. The brothels wouldn't be any different. Going to find a prostitute would only disappoint me and waste my time.

'It's better to go somewhere else during that time.'

I entered an alleyway and found a shabby pawnshop. It was a house full of junk, and the sky-blue gate was extremely rusted. The word 'Pawnshop' was roughly written on the gate in red spray paint.

I pressed the doorbell next to it. Along with a ding sound, an old man's voice was heard.

"......Come in."

I opened the gate and went inside. In the yard, there were all sorts of junk and several container boxes. It looked more like a junkyard than a pawnshop.

I walked to a shanty house that looked like it could collapse at any moment. When I opened the door and went inside, the interior was better than I expected. Still, it was faithful to the appearance of a pawnshop, and there were quite a few decent items on display.

"You've come. It's here."

There was an old man in his 60s sitting in front of a wooden table. He was short and his face was full of wrinkles, but his eyes were sharp.

I glanced at the two men standing on either side of Kang Wook-sung. The men with pale faces were glaring at me. Silently telling me not to do anything stupid.

I walked to the table and sat across from him.

"......You're younger than I thought. How old are you?"

"21 years old. The money?"

"It's under the table."

I leaned my head to the side of the table and looked down. There was a box containing stacks of 50,000 won bills.

"It's 400 million won as promised. Where's the list?"

I put the backpack I was carrying on my back on the table. I opened the zipper and poured out the contents. Several burner accounts and a USB drive popped out.

"The money in the accounts has been laundered, and the list is on the USB. Check it out."

Kang Wook-sung gestured to the man next to him, and the man plugged the USB into the laptop on the table and checked the data.

The list is accurate.

There are a total of 913 members of the Korean Black Cross Society. Among them, 174 are professional vampire Hunters.

The list contains their names, residences, phone numbers, combat history, and other personal information.

'......Looking back, this seems to be worth more than 3 billion won.'

I quickly shook off the lingering attachment. If I had asked for 3 billion won, there was a possibility that Kang Wook-sung would not have agreed to the deal.

After about 10 minutes, the vampire said to Kang Wook-sung.

"......The list is correct. It matches the information we have and is more accurate."

Kang Wook-sung nodded.

"Take the money. The remaining 600 million will be prepared in three days."

"Oh. Thanks."

I started putting the stacks of money into my backpack. That's why I prepared a fairly sturdy 200,000 won backpack.

"How did you get this?"

"What do you think a hacker does? I got it through hacking."

"...Do you think we haven't tried hacking? The Black Cross Society uses its own network in each country. Hacking is impossible unless you break into the building and rummage through the computers."

"There's always a way. You just don't know it."

"What's the way?"

"That's my secret. Think for yourself. There are many possibilities. One of them is... a traitor from the Black Cross Society teamed up with me?"

The money was exactly 400 million won.

I zipped up my backpack and got up from my seat.

'Now that I've got the money, I need to go to Yeonghyejeong. Let's have some real fun this time.'

A smile came naturally.

'Yeonghyejeong, let's gooooo!'

I hurriedly prepared to leave the house.

"Taker. Can I ask you something?"

"Yeah. What is it?"

"What are you going to use the money for in such a hurry?"

I grinned. I thought it was probably the coolest smile of my life.

"To become king."
