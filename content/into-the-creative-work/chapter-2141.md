Dark red blood was soaking the road.

,There are some fatalities. Looks like there are some injured too."

-What are you going to do? Are you going to use your healing ability on the security team members?

,They did a good job, so I should."

To be honest, I didn't want to. Healing others was twice as hard and annoying as healing my own body. But losing experienced security team members would be too big of a loss. And with a little bit of effort, I could drastically increase the security team's loyalty.

Right now, the security team's loyalty to me was ambiguous. They were just working because I provided them with food, clothing, and shelter. In other words, it was an employment relationship. Thinking about the future, I needed soldiers who would move according to my will. The security team would be the start of that.

,I also need the support of others, so I can't just hoard my ability. It's an ability that can be used on others, so I have to use it when I need to.'

Of course, I had no intention of recklessly using my ability and being called a saint.

I'll be discriminatory.

Benefits should only go to those who have contributed. That way, there won't be any freeloaders.

I went around and healed the seriously injured. Most of them were equipped with gear, so there were few seriously injured and many lightly injured.

,T, Thank you, Chairman-nim...!"

A security team member who had been on the verge of death because of the healing ability spoke with tears streaming down their face. Their eyes were full of awe as they looked at me. It was understandable, as I had become their savior in an instant. When the urge to betray me comes in the future, they'll remember this moment.

I also healed the lightly injured who had broken ankles or minor wounds on their wrists. And I made it clear to them.

,The reason I'm healing you is because you've contributed under me. You're qualified. It's distinctly different from those slave bastards over there, or the workers at Seongak Elementary School. You are warriors! Warriors who risk their lives to protect! Therefore, you have the right to own slaves!"

,...Chairman-nim. They may be looters, but they're still human. Isn't it a bit much to make them slaves?"

If we hadn't won against those looter bastards today, you would be dead and your family would have been violated. We might have had our food stolen and been kicked out of this safe place. The young guy's social life will be a bit tough from now on.

,Let's think about that later and focus on securing the looters' assets for now. There's a lot more to do besides this."

The situation was reasonably settled.

I found another rest stop. It was dominated by Variants, so I didn't dare to go down.

I was also shocked when I passed a lake. I saw a fish the size of a 10-story building swimming below the surface. That must be a Variant too.

,Looks like there was a Variant dangerous enough to bomb the city.'

There were Players on the government's side too, so it seemed they had dealt with the dangerous Variant by any means necessary.

,What a mess.'

Mutants roamed the mountains and fields, and zombies roamed the villages. In cities with large populations, Variants were a bigger problem than zombies.

,Looking at this, Seongak Elementary School is practically heaven.'

Looking at it, there were no zombies roaming around. It seems that the cleanup of the city's interior was also somewhat complete.
