In this world, Korea is a unified Korea.

To be more precise, in this world, Korea was never divided into South and North Korea. In this world, Korea's status is within the top 10 in the world.

Because of that, in this world, Korea could travel to China, Europe, and Russia by train.

You could also go to Pyongyang and Mount Baekdu by car.

And now. Sung Hari and I came to the Eight Colors Amusement Park in Sinhung, South Hamgyong Province.

September 10, 2006.

I had been begging for a month to come on this day. It was a bit difficult to match the date, but after making various excuses and begging, I finally came today.

,Yujin-ah. Let's have fun with Mama today!"

,Yes! I want to ride a bumper car!"

,Ah. It's hot. Are you okay, Yujin-ah?"

,Yes."

She and I stood in line at the entrance of the amusement park. It was hot because of the hot sun, but it wasn't unbearable.

The bracelet on Sung Hari's wrist came into view. A silver bracelet. It's not an ordinary item. It is a bracelet that has the effect of slightly changing the perception of ordinary people. If it weren't for this bracelet, SS-class Hero Sung Hari would already be surrounded by people and in trouble.

Sung Hari, who cut a free pass and went inside, immediately frowned. A middle-aged man wearing a T-shirt and pants, Sung Han-gu, was waiting for us. Of course, we had no idea about his appearance.

,...Stalker behavior at that age... Aren't you ashamed?"

,I just happened to come here and ran into you."

,Even a passing dog wouldn't laugh at that."

He ignored Sung Hari, who was using insa, and greeted me.

,Hello. Yujin-ah. It's been a while. How have you been?"

,Yes. Grandpa."

,I'm glad he came. This will make things easier.'

In the first place, I didn't come here to enjoy the rides. How old am I to enjoy riding rides at an amusement park?

,You don't have to do that."

,Hari-ya. Isn't that more efficient?"

While Sung Hari and Sung Han-gu were having a short argument, I gave an order to Thunder Owl Mocha.

,Mocha. Fly around the sky and find a girl. She has light green hair and pointy ears. She's a half-elf. If you find her, keep an eye on her so you don't miss her.'

The Thunder Owl flies into the sky. Only Sung Hari and I can see it. Sung Hari will probably think nothing of it even if the Thunder Owl flies over the amusement park.

I had Spirit Eyes. One of the effects of Spirit Eyes is sharing the spirit's vision. If Mocha finds the elf girl, I can easily know where she is, even if I'm far away.

,Yujin-ah. This way. Don't let go of Mama's hand?"

,Yes."

I'm sorry to Sung Hari, but I'm planning to drop out when I get the chance. It's not bad for me to lead them, but if they lose me, Sung Hari and Sung Han-gu will move more actively.

Since I was small, there were only a limited number of rides I could ride. They were all so boring that I almost yawned.

,Wow! Yujin-ah! Yujin-ah! Let's ride that next! That one!"

Somehow, Sung Hari seemed more excited than me. She didn't skimp on snacks despite the amusement park's unique high prices, and she also bought useless toys. She was the type to spend money as she pleased.

Sung Han-gu was quietly following us from behind.

,Grandpa. I want to eat ice cream."

,I'll buy it right away. Wait a little."

Sung Han-gu was easily shaken off.

,Ah! Yujin-ah! There's a bear over there! Let's go take a picture! ...Yujin-ah?"

I took the opportunity to run away.

Even if she is an SS-rank Hero, she won't be able to find me in an instant in an amusement park crowded with people. It will take at least several tens of minutes.

I mixed into the crowd and ran. Someone who looked like an employee talked to me in the middle, but I ignored them.

,Activate Spirit Eyes.'

Share vision with the Thunder Owl. It was a mysterious feeling. The existing vision was not overlaid with the spirit's vision, but a new vision appeared in my head.
