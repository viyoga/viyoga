My body, sent flying behind the Spirit Gate, tumbled across the floor a few times.

"Yujin-ah!"

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim and Seong Hangu-nim rushed to me and helped me to my feet.

Looking around, the Spirit Gate had vanished, and the mana density had dropped. The drawing on the floor no longer reacted in any way.

『Skill, Spirit Contract (C-) has increased to rank (B).』

I could feel that my connection with Mocha had become stronger and more efficient. But I didn't have time to leisurely think about Spirit Contract (B).

"Seong Yujin! Do you know what you've done?! Didn't I tell you to stay still and not move?"

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim's booming shout made my ears tingle. I don't know where that old man gets such strength from.

"S-sorry."

I said, whimpering like a child.

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim took a moment to catch his breath, then softened his expression and tone, and began to nag.

"Yujin-ah. If you don't listen to this grandpa from now on, you could get hurt, so you must always pay attention. Do you know how dangerous it was just now? If you had made a mistake, you could have become a lost child in the Spirit Realm, and your mind could have collapsed."

"...Father. Didn't I tell you? Yujin-ie is too young."

"Quiet. Aren't I talking to Yujin-ie right now? You be quiet. ...No. Go get something to drink. I need to teach Yujin-ie how important an adult's words are."

"Don't overdo it. Don't overdo it. What if Yujin-ie says he won't come to the main house anymore because of this?"

"......Isn't Yujin-ie living in the main house?"

"Do you think Hari-yang will stay still? Yujin-ie has to go back in the evening. Otherwise... Hari-yang will storm into the main house and turn it into a wasteland. I'm telling you in advance, even if Hari-yang is weakened, we can't stop Hari-yang. The compatibility is the worst."

Seong Hari-yang's Spirit Devourer (S) trait was a natural enemy to spiritists.

"Aish. For Yujin-ie's future, it would be better to have him stay in the main house...."

"This is something I absolutely can't concede... Heok!"

Seong Hangu-nim's face turned dark.

"What's wrong?"

"Yujin-ie is hurt!"

My elbow got scraped when I tumbled on the floor earlier. There's blood too. About one drop.

"Why are you so surprised about something like this? Originally, children, especially boys, grow up getting hurt. You used to fall and get hurt all the time when you were young."

"That's not it... If Hari-yang finds out, she won't stay still."

"Did we hit Yujin-ie? It's just a scraped elbow."

"......Father doesn't know about Hari-yang now. Hari-yang's obsession with Yujin-ie is immense. I'll get a potion and treat it."

"Hmph. There won't be a problem. You're overreacting. Just put some ointment on it. Does it make sense to use that expensive potion for just a scraped elbow?"

"Father. Please."

"A potion is too much. Our family's ointment is good enough. The wound will disappear without a trace in a day."

"......."

Seong Hangu-nim sighed in resignation. Even so, he must be hoping that Seong Hari-yang will just let it go. Even I think it's just a slightly scraped elbow, a common injury.

But Seong Hangu-nim doesn't know how extreme Seong Hari-yang is.

To act more realistically like a child, I sometimes fall in front of Seong Hari-yang. It's a self-inflicted injury, prepared to scrape my knees or arms.

Every time, Seong Hari-yang generously uses top-grade potions that cost a fortune just to hear the price.

And that's not all. If there are fruits and medicines that are good for the body, she buys them for me no matter how much they cost. That's why she, an SS-class Hero, is short on money. Seong Hari-yang raises me with too much care.

"Yujin-ah. There's something you need to do as the first step to becoming a strong spiritist."

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim said to me. It seems like I'm finally going to do some real training.

"Okay!"

Now I'm going to learn spiritism in earnest. I wondered what I would do.

"From now on, come to the main house every day and have fun playing with the spirits."

"...Huh?"

•••

Logically speaking, making a child who is only four years old do hard training is no different from abuse.

In martial arts novels, you can often see people training in martial arts from a very young age, but this era is an era where human rights exist.

And there was a reason to play with the spirits.

For a spiritist, the most important thing is the spirit, not the mana.

'If the spirit doesn't listen, you can't control the spirit no matter how much mana you have.'

Playing with the spirits is intended to build spirit affinity and get used to the spirits. Spirit affinity is innate, but it also develops when you spend time with spirits.

"Hoo.... I have to play with spirits at this age.... I'd rather play computer games."

I squatted down in the garden.

I was annoyed.

To get through this time with automatic progression, I need to do some good deeds first. That way, next time, the avatar will play with the spirits in the same way I did.

"Kkuuk."

"Hey. Hey. What's your name?"

"Chareureuk chachareuk."

"Let's play! Let's play! Let's play!"

The spirits gathered around me. The spirits showed interest in me when I came to the main house earlier, but now it's even worse. It seems to be because I went into the Spirit Realm halfway earlier.

"Ah, noisy.... But I can't not play...."

If I skip out on this, the adults will think it's strange.

"Well, fine. Shall we play house?"

"Play house? What's that?"

"Pureureureu! Pureuk!"

Some spirits spoke human language, and some didn't. But communication between spirits was no problem. Spirits communicate with their minds, not with sounds.

"Hey, you."

I pointed to a water turtle that spoke human language.

"Huh? Me? Why?"

"We're going to play house from now on. In other words, role-playing."

"Okay. Okay. I think I saw human children do it a long time ago. I'm going to be the wife?"

"No. You're a Private. I'm a Sergeant."

"Private... What's that?"

"You'll learn as you go. It starts now."

I cleared my throat.

As a Hunter, I've never been in the military, but I've seen and heard things.

"Hey."

"Huh?"

"...Ha. Fucking hell. Hey, where did you sell your rank and name? Huh?"

I put on a serious face. The atmosphere changed drastically, and the water turtle was flustered. It seemed to be a mid-level spirit, but it had some sense since it spoke human language.

"...Uh. Uh...?"

"Oh, you're not answering? Ha, shit. I have to do this with a Private after becoming a Sergeant...."

"S-sorry."

"Sorry? You fucking...! Hey, is this your friend? Why are you talking informally!"

"I'm sorry."

"...Yo? I'm going crazy. Seriously. Hey, Mocha Sergeant."

"Kkuuk!"

Mocha answered immediately. Even saluting with one wing.

Mocha had been with me for over a year, so he was used to this. I scolded Mocha whenever I was bored. The reason Mocha listens to me so well is because he doesn't want to be scolded.

'Whether it's a human, a spirit, or a dwarf, if you scold them, something will happen.'

It was the truth of life.

"I'm going to the bathroom for a bit, so train this new recruit properly. If he's still a failure when I get back? You and I are going to have a fight today. Got it?"

"Kkuuuk!"

That's how I played military house with the spirits for several hours.

4:30 PM.

Seong Hangu-nim came out of the room and headed towards the garden. It was time to send Seong Yujin-ie to Seong Hari-yang. He wanted to feed him dinner and send him off, but the promise with Seong Hari-yang was to send Seong Yujin-ie home by 5 PM. He didn't know what would happen if he broke the promise.

"Hmm...?"

It was quiet. Originally, spirits should be wandering around outside the house, but strangely, they weren't visible. This was rare.

'Are all the spirits engrossed in playing with Yujin-ie?'

Seong Hangu-nim, who was going to the garden to find Yujin-ie, was surprised.

The many spirits were standing in rows. In front of the spirits, the water turtle that could speak human language was shouting with a strained voice.

"Is that all the voice you have?!"

"Kkaaaaaaaa!"

"Keureureureureu!"

"No, it's nottttt!"

The spirits standing in line shouted each in their own voice. The sounds were all different, but the rhythm was perfect.

"Do 10 jumping jacks. How many times?"

"Kkaaaak!"

"Ten times!"

"Huuuung!"

"15 times! Without the final slogan! Start!"

Seong Hangu-nim opened his mouth. He was able to see spirits doing jumping jacks for the first time in his life.

'...This is... the military?'

He looked around in the abnormal situation and looked for Seong Yujin-ie. He could find him without difficulty. A little further away, he was lying on the wooden floor, looking at the spirits and sucking on a juice box straw. Next to Seong Yujin-ie, a thunder owl named Mocha was fiercely glaring at the spirits.

'It's like... a general and his adjutant in the military.'

Seong Hangu-nim, who let out a hollow laugh, approached Yujin-ie.

"Yujin-ah. Can you tell this grandpa why the spirits are suddenly doing this?"

"Ah, Grandpa. We were playing house."

"Play... house? It's very different from the play house I know."

"I just taught them what I saw on TV. I'm the boss."

"I, I see."

There won't be a problem... Spirits are free beings. They don't do things they don't want to do.

"Yujin-ah. You have to go back now."

"Okay. I miss Mama."

Seong Yujin-ie jumped down from the wooden floor. And at that moment, a shout erupted from where the spirits were.

"Didn't I say not to add the final slogan!!! Do it again! 30 times! How many times?!"

"Hooooaaak!"

"Look at your voice! Is that all you have?! Get down!!!"

Seong Hangu-nim, who tried to ignore the spirits, was sweating coldly. For some reason, he had a bad feeling.

•••

Seong Hangu-nim, who had taken Seong Yujin-ie back and returned, shook his head at the sight of the spirits still playing house... no, playing military.

He had tried to stop the spirits once, but the spirits didn't even pretend to listen to Seong Hangu-nim.

Seong Hangu-nim entered Seong Myeongsaeng-nim's room.

"Father. I've taken Yujin-ie back."

"Yes. The spirits outside were strange."

"Yes. It seems that Yujin-ie taught the spirits what he saw on TV. The spirits are just caught up in a new game for a while. They'll be back to normal soon. ......Probably."

"Hmm. Spirits are fickle. More than that, I've been thinking about it. Don't you think Yujin-ie should grow up in the main house after all?"

"......Father. That's already over."

"Don't think so simply. It's for Yujin-ie's future."

"Then Father, persuade Hari-yang. I can't.... I don't have the confidence to persuade Hari-yang."

"......I'll think about it a little more."

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim's face hardened slightly. Seong Myeongsaeng-nim also had a bad relationship with Seong Hari-yang.

"Father. How are you planning to educate Yujin-ie?"

"......Before that, is Yujin-ie okay? Half of his head, even if it was only a short time of less than 30 seconds, went into the Spirit Realm and came out. Wasn't he possessed by a spirit?"

The Spirit Realm was not a place that humans could handle. Humans are shocked just by looking at the Spirit Realm. Even spiritists who have reached the realm have a very high probability of being possessed by a spirit in the Spirit Realm and having their minds broken.

"That's a relief. Yujin-ie's mental strength is beyond imagination. We can move up the education schedule a little more."

"Father!"

"I know. Don't shout. Even if I move up the schedule, I don't intend to overdo it. I'm going to leave him alone to play with the spirits for the next few months. After that, I'll slowly teach him the basics of spiritism."

"Father. We have to proceed carefully. Carefully. If Yujin-ie loses interest in spiritism... we can't force him to do it. Spiritism is all about his own will. And above all... Hari-yang won't stay still."

"I know. ...You're too conscious of that child. You don't have the dignity of a father. Dignity."

"...I couldn't even play the role of Hari-yang's father properly... how can I have dignity as a father."

"Tsk."

It was when Seong Myeongsaeng-nim clicked his tongue briefly.

Kwaaaang!

With a huge impact sound, the main house's barrier shook.

Seong Myeongsaeng-nim and Seong Hangu-nim, who were startled, rushed to the entrance where the sound came from.

The front gate was smashed by a uprooted tree that had flown in from somewhere.

"......An attack? Who is the attacker! How dare you to the Jinryeong Seongga...!!"

"Father. Calm down. There's lightning energy left in the tree. It's Hari-yang's doing."

"That child?! Why, suddenly?! Is this a declaration of war?!"

"No. Maybe... she found out about Yujin-ie's injury."

"......She threw a tree just because his elbow was scraped?"

"Didn't I tell you? Hari-yang's obsession with Yujin-ie is immense...."
