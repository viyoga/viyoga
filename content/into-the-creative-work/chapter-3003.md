Kang Ji-young boldly changed clothes right in front of me. The one left gaping at the sight was Tenrai Nagisa.

"You strip down in front of a man and you don't even have the decency to be embarrassed?"

"We're well past the stage where embarrassment is worth discussing."

"Hooh?"

Nagisa's eyes glinted, as if she'd caught onto something.

"Ji-young's body might not be on my level, but it's nothing to scoff at either. And Yujin's not the type to go blabbing about it anyway."

Sung Ha-ri was surprisingly open-minded about this sort of thing.

“…….”

The one sitting on pins and needles here was Clara. Setting me aside, the three women gathered here were all world-famous S-rank heroes, each occupying high social stations to boot.

Nagisa called out to Kang Ji-young.

"Dean Kang Ji-young. What will you do? Are you going to reveal yourself as you are? The very person whose funeral was held not long ago showing up alive and well—people are going to be plenty shocked."

"I'll make an official announcement that I was trapped in a death-like state due to a demon's trickery. If Sung Ha-ri helps, the cleanup shouldn't be a problem either."

"Dean Kang Ji-young. You are officially dead. There are considerable advantages to be reaped from that state. Isn't it a waste to show yourself at an official function right away? Take this opportunity to deal with the ones you don't like from the shadows—your political enemies, and so on."

Nagisa smiles. Despite her innocent-looking face, the atmosphere around her turns dark and murky.

Sung Ha-ri was revolted by it.

"Ugh. This sinister old hag. You haven't changed one bit, then or now."

Nagisa snorted and ignored Sung Ha-ri.

"Political enemies... I don't particularly have any. Maruhan Academy needs me."

"Well, if that's what Dean Kang Ji-young thinks, then so be it. Handle it as you see fit."

"Yes. Lady Tenrai Nagisa. Thank you for protecting my body."

"Skip the thanks. It wasn't a difficult task, and it was a request from my lord husband."

"After this, do you plan to return to Japan, Lady Nagisa?"

"Distance is of little consequence. With my ability—and my lord husband's spell scrolls—I can operate regardless of distance."

That was the advantage of spatial teleportation scrolls. I had plenty of them, so I could give Nagisa as many as she needed.

"As you know, the Demon Corps Commanders are preparing an invasion. The 3rd Corps Commander failed, but precisely because of that, the Demon Corps Commanders will launch a full-scale invasion of Earth to wash away that failure."

"The demons' pride has been thoroughly trampled, so... yes, that tracks. And it wasn't just the 3rd Corps Commander who failed, was it."

Not long ago, the 7th Corps Commander, Germadon, invaded and I killed him. Thanks to the hacking, you could say I cheesed it.

"We need to prepare for the next invasion. For reasons unknown, the demons have been targeting the Korean Peninsula. There's a high chance they'll invade the Korean Peninsula again next time. Lady Tenrai Nagisa. Please lend us your aid."

"Demons are the enemy of humanity. As a human, I will of course help."

"What, is there any need to overthink this? If they invade, we just kill them."

Sung Ha-ri said. The other two stared at her blankly. Sung Ha-ri looked back at them as if to ask what they were staring at.

"...The Demon Corps Commanders aren't that weak. If they launch a determined invasion... all of humanity will struggle to respond."

"Oh dear. Has our dear mother-in-law gotten stupid again now that she's regained her power? ...No, that's not right. She was always stupid to begin with."

"Have you lost your mind, you old hag?"

When Sung Ha-ri glared, Nagisa immediately started preparing to flee with the spatial teleportation scroll I'd given her earlier. Even Nagisa, who could be called Japan's strongest, had no intention whatsoever of fighting the current Sung Ha-ri.

Kang Ji-young didn't even spare the two a glance and asked me directly.

"Sung Yujin. Can you predict when the Demon Corps Commanders will launch their next invasion?"

"Why are you asking me that?"

"You know things others don't, don't you. Didn't you prepare for the 3rd and 7th Corps Commanders' invasions as well."

There was nothing I could do about Kang Ji-young misunderstanding me like that.

But this time was different.

I genuinely didn't know.

To begin with, things had diverged significantly from the original work. In the original, the entity known as Baal didn't exist at all, and no Demon Corps Commander had ever been annihilated like this.

'That's four Corps Commanders dead now. The 2nd, 3rd, 7th, and 8th. Four Corps Commanders remain. ...No. If you count Baal, that makes five.'

It didn't seem likely that all five Corps Commanders would invade Earth. No matter what, the constraints would become far too severe.

'Given how quiet things have been, I should assume they're preparing a determined invasion. Even I can't say for certain when they'll attack.'

What was certain was that they probably wouldn't invade later than this year. It was even possible they could invade tomorrow.

"I don't know either. But it's certain they will invade. Those demons aren't the type to fail and just back off."

"I see. So we must prepare without a set deadline. How troublesome. With so little information on the enemy, it's difficult to prepare. And the nations won't follow along if we just prepare blindly."

"Won't they do it if you tell them to?"

"...Sung Ha-ri. If it's Korea, they'll at least pretend to listen to you even if they have complaints. The problem is other countries. Do you really think America will do whatever you say?"

"America. That's a place my influence doesn't reach."

"Don't even think about forcing them into submission. There could be an actual war."

Kang Ji-young grabbed both of Sung Ha-ri's shoulders and spoke with a serious face.

"I know that much. But to go up against the Demon Corps Commanders, America's power is essential, isn't it?"

"Yes. So we need to persuade America. The problem is that it won't be easy."

"Wouldn't America actually prefer it? The demons weakening East Asia's power and all."

Unlike Kang Ji-young, I wasn't too concerned about America's involvement.

'In the face of humanity's crisis, America will eventually step up actively. That's what happened in the first run. Even if they don't, what matters is whether we can kill the Demon Corps Commanders. No matter how many rank-and-file we have, if we can't handle the Corps Commanders, there's no answer.'

A small elite force just needs to kill the Corps Commanders. The demons led by the Corps Commanders? Without the Corps Commanders as their focal point, they won't be able to invade and will have no choice but to return to the Demon Realm.

'As Kang Ji-young said, preparation is necessary. To do that, we need information. The way to find out about the current state of the Demon Realm is...'

I racked my brain a few times. I recalled the scenarios from the original Academy and went back over useful items.

'There it is.'

The Savior of the Academy has a faction composed of ability users specialized in observation.

'The Church of the Nine Eyes.'

With the power of the Church of the Nine Eyes, I should be able to observe the situation of the Demon Corps Commanders.

"Let's head out for now. We all have things we need to do."

"Wait, Yujin. Since we're all gathered like this, we should at least have lunch."

"That's true."

We tore into some dakgalbi for lunch.

If I had my way, I'd have wanted to have physical sex with Kang Ji-young, but judging by her mood, it didn't seem like something that would happen right now.

'For now, it's better to get information through the Church of the Nine Eyes first.'

• • •

I headed to the Church of the Nine Eyes' secret sanctuary.

Together with Iste, the saintess of the Church of the Nine Eyes, I met Cassandra, the first apostle and the cult leader.

Cassandra was an old woman who looked like a witch straight out of a fairy tale.

"Welcome, Saintess. And the Ninth Apostle. What has brought you to this old woman out of curiosity?"

"Apostle Cassandra. Your eyes are the Eyes of Fate, are they not."

"Yes. They are eyes that can see fate."

"With those eyes, can you also see when the Demon Corps Commanders will invade? No—you should be able to see everything, shouldn't you."

"Regrettably, the fate I can see is now only an extremely small fraction. The fate you desire, I cannot see."

"You can't see it? Why?"

Cassandra smiled. She seemed to feel joy rather than despair at the fact that she couldn't see fate.

"Ninth Apostle. It is all thanks to you. You have shattered most of the predetermined fate. Those who should have died live, and those who should have lived have died. Some received salvation, and some received punishment."

The fate she spoke of seemed to be the original work.

"Then your eyes, the Eyes of Fate, have become useless?"

"Fate does not point only to the future. I can also see the past and the present. I can still select talents suited to the Church of the Nine Eyes. Ah, though that won't be of much help to you."

"What about Iste's eyes? Can't you see the future with Iste's Primordial Eyes?"

Iste's Primordial Eyes (SS+) are eyes that see possibilities. They see possibilities, and can even partially manipulate them. They should also be able to see future possibilities.

"Why don't you ask the Saintess yourself."

I looked at Iste.

Iste shook her head.

"Among all possibilities, the hardest to see are future possibilities. And even then, I can't be certain which possibility will become reality."

So the opposite of Cassandra, who can't see, is that Iste sees too much.

"Is what the Ninth Apostle desires to fix future possibilities into certainty?"

"I'm well aware that such a thing can't be easily achieved."

Realize a desired future possibility? If that were possible, Iste would have been revered as a god rather than a saintess.

"What I want is information. At the very least, I want to know the approximate military strength of the demons."

"So it's not fixing possibilities but confirming them. It won't be easy, but... if the faithful help, the Saintess should be able to obtain the information. A ritual will be necessary."

At Cassandra's words, I looked at Iste. Iste's cooperation was needed.

"Iste."

"Yes. Yujin. I'll do my best."

Iste smiled brightly and opened the two eyes she always kept closed.

Those pupils, as if containing the universe itself—mysterious yet strangely imposing—glittered and shone.

"No. There's no need to start right away. We haven't even prepared yet."

"Just once should be possible even without preparation. If it's not a distant future possibility but a near future one, and only the Demon Corps Commanders' possibilities at that... Ah."

Drip, drip.

Suddenly, bloody tears streamed from Iste's eyes. She staggered, then spoke as if vomiting out breath.

"Two.... There are only two Corps Commanders currently alive."

"Not five?"

"Yes. Even looking at other possibilities, it's definitely two.... Cough!"

Iste vomited blood and collapsed. I hurried to her side and checked her condition. Even as I did, one corner of my mind was in turmoil.

'The other three Corps Commanders are dead? When? How?'
