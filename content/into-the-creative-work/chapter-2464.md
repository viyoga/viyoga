The architects who had been summoned to Cheonma Palace emerged with pale faces.

The order from Cheonma, the ruler of the desert, was to draw up blueprints for a magic tower. But it couldn't end with just blueprints.

,In the end, we'll be responsible for constructing the magic tower.'

If he simply wanted blueprints, he wouldn't have summoned all of us scattered throughout the desert.

They quietly exchanged glances and gathered where Cheonma's eyes couldn't reach.

,That Cheonma is a crazy bastard! Building a 1000m magic tower in 90 days? We wouldn't have enough time even if we spent over a year just on the blueprints!"

The first thing that burst out was, of course, complaints about Cheonma. Of course, this was only possible because Cheonma couldn't hear them.

,He's a crazy bastard, alright. No matter how I look at it, isn't this a Pussy? To think he'd build a tower with such a hideous shape in the center of the desert...!"

,...But we can't just refuse, can we?"

,......"

Cheonma's orders cannot be refused. If you refuse, you die. It would be fortunate if you died alone. Disaster would befall their families as well. Even if they didn't die, they would become slaves.

,When Cheonma became the ruler of the desert, we should have escaped this damn desert."

,There's no point in regretting it now. And even if we leave this desert, do you think other districts would easily accept us?"

All the districts near the desert are wary of Cheonma. They avoid doing anything that might provoke Cheonma as much as possible.

,Anyway, we have to draw up the blueprints. Fortunately, the exterior is already decided... He probably wants us to design it with a solid foundation so the tower doesn't collapse."

,That's not an easy task. A 1,000m tower? That itself is absurd. Even the Empire Magic Tower, the best magic tower in Atlantis, is only 300m tall. To build a magic tower more than three times the size of the Empire Magic Tower! Is that even possible with current Atlantis technology?! It wouldn't be easy even with 21st-century technology!"

,This is a world with gods and magic. Cheonma must have judged it possible, so he entrusted us with the blueprints, right?"

,What about the budget? Cheonma only stated the requirements and didn't say a word about the budget."

,Let's use the highest quality construction materials... Cost-effectiveness? Efficiency? Cheonma, who can be called the tyrant of the desert, doesn't want that kind of thing. He wants his achievements. Cheonma's extravagance is already well-known."

,Well... Cheonma is the ruler of 15 desert districts... The amount of AP coming in each month must be enormous."

As everyone was frowning, one of the architects suddenly muttered.

,It might not be all bad..."

,...What nonsense are you spouting?"

,Listen. Regardless of whether this magic tower is hideous or not, it's a 1,000m tall structure. Once it's built, it will become a landmark of the desert. We're creating a kind of achievement. And in Atlantis, rewards are given according to the achievements you make. There's bound to be some crumbs falling our way."

The architects' eyes sparkled.

Perhaps they could gain special powers with this opportunity. They might even catch the eye of a god and become a contractor.

,Since we have to do it anyway, we should do it properly."

A magic tower is a wizard's tower.

Even if there's a plausible tower, it's just an ordinary tower if there are no wizards.

I set out into the desert to find a wizard.

,There are quite a few magic towers in Atlantis.'

How do wizards in Atlantis become wizards?

There are several ways.

The easiest and luckiest way is to learn magic through skills or unique traits, or to contract with a magic-related god.

Studying magic books on your own or becoming a disciple of a wizard and training is also a method. However, the most common method is to enter a magic tower and learn magic.

,Magic towers are like academies. If you have money and talent, you can learn magic.'

It couldn't be helped that you had to have talent. It's not just ordinary combat skills, it's magic.

,The most famous magic tower is the Empire Magic Tower in the Justia Empire.'

As famous as it is, its power is also immense.

For me as I am now, charging into the Empire Magic Tower alone is suicide.

,The Empire Magic Tower is out. Other famous magic towers are also not to be ignored.'

Famous magic towers are often associated with other Legions or forces.

,I'll invade a moderately weak magic tower and kidnap them.'

There was already a magic tower I had set my sights on.

「You have entered District 1,999, a desolate plain.」

District 1,999 was, as the name suggests, a vast wasteland.

I took out the Cloud Control Order and flew across the desolate plain on a cloud. Before long, I discovered a large pond. I saw a 30m tower standing on the pond. In reality, the water flowing from the tower was forming the pond.

Aquari Magic Tower.

It's a magic tower that specializes in learning water-based magic.

As you can tell from it being in an uninhabited district, it's not a famous magic tower.

,Water-based magic is unpopular. Its attack power is weak.'

The value of wizards in Atlantis lies in firepower. Water-based magic, by its nature, has weak attack power, so people don't really prefer it.

,But it's different in the desert.'

Water is precious in the desert. It's common knowledge.

Even though there are oases that never dry up throughout the desert districts, water is still precious.

,Even if the water in the desert districts is polluted or disappears due to the appearance of volcanic islands, water mages can respond.'

Above all, a Pussy Tower and water go well together. A Pussy Tower dripping with water. Just imagining it is a perfect match.

I landed in front of the main gate of the magic tower. There was no entrance to the Aquari Magic Tower other than the main gate. There wasn't even a single window. But I couldn't just smash my way in recklessly. It would be a great loss if the wizard I had to kidnap was injured or killed.

Lightning Sky Style Electromagnetic Waves.

Electromagnetic waves don't work. The barrier protecting the magic tower blocks electromagnetic waves.

[The Heavenly Eye is opened.]

I could see through the interior with the Heavenly Eye. This time, it's too clear, that's the problem. I can see all the magic applied to the magic tower.

,Even though it's in such a remote place, a magic tower is still a magic tower. The interior seems wider and more complex than it appears from the outside.'

Judging by the level alone, the magic tower itself seems to be at a higher level than the wizards.

Of course, I know the reason for that.

System correction.

The system is directly correcting the Aquari Magic Tower. The same must be true for other magic towers. That's why wizards use the inefficient structure called a magic tower.

I walked confidently towards the main gate.

It was when I got about 10m close to the magic tower. A voice amplified by a loudspeaker came from the magic tower. It was a woman's voice.

-Stop. This is the Aquari Magic Tower. Unauthorized persons are not allowed to enter.

,The Aquari Magic Tower treats guests harshly."

-We have never recognized you as a guest. State your identity and purpose.

,I am Cheonma."

-...Cheonma. What purpose brings the new ruler of the western desert district here?

,I'm going to build a magic tower in the desert. But there are few wizards in the desert. I came to take a wizard."

-The wizards of our Aquari Magic Tower are only focused on research. Please look for another wizard.

,You don't know anything. The fact that I came here myself means... you have no right to refuse. I want to meet the tower master, so open the entrance."

-Ha, how arrogant. Starting a fight in front of a magic tower, of all places? Even if you are the ruler of the desert, this is a magic tower! Curse that arrogance and drown!

The surface of the Aquari Magic Tower began to glow. It glowed blue, and the magic formula was completed.

Immediately afterward, water poured down from the sky like a waterfall.

,It's just water. I was feeling refreshed anyway, so this is good. I'll take it."

I spread my arms wide.

-It's just water...? People tend to ignore water. Even though they can't live without it. Know the greatness of water!

Kwakkwakwakwa!

I smiled and took in the pouring water.

The water was cool. The water pressure was quite strong, but it wasn't enough to knock me, who was standing firm, down.

I took the opportunity to take out shampoo from my inventory and applied it to my hair. After touching my hair a few times, a lot of foam formed.

,I want to wash off the foam. Is it possible one more time?"

-Eek! Let's see how long you can afford to be so relaxed!

The water that had gathered around me began to move. It rushed at me in an instant and enveloped my whole body.

I was trapped in a water prison.

-How long can you last in a place without air? I'll enjoy your death.

Her voice sounded faint because I was underwater.

I curled up the corners of my lips.

Drown? Unfortunately, I can't drown. To be exact, I can't even if I wanted to.

With [Blessing of Water], I can breathe sufficiently even underwater. Moreover, I feel comfortable underwater. If I wanted to, I could stay underwater for days.

,Since they're going to work under me... should I play along appropriately?'

5 minutes passed. I was comfortably fiddling with my smartphone underwater. These days, smartphones are said to have waterproof functions, but my smartphone, which has the Amusement Life app installed, worked perfectly even in the deep sea or lava.

-Wh-what. Don't tell me you contracted with a god related to water?!

The Dragon Princess came to mind when I heard about the god of water. I haven't seen her recently, she's probably being lazy. She's a goddess who's a pain in the ass in many ways.

,Still, she's the Bidet Princess, so the bidet performance is definitely reliable.'

-Just because you can breathe in water doesn't mean water is safe!

A current formed in the water prison. The current rotated around me, increasing the water pressure.

,This is good timing. I got it but never had a use for it, so should I try using it once?'

One of the abilities of the Blessing of Water, the flow of water. I manipulated the current and stopped it.

-...So that kind of method doesn't work? I understand. It means you didn't come here without anything to rely on.

Dozens of blue magic circles appeared around the magic tower. Water gathered in front of the magic circles and accumulated power.

,Water cannons, huh.'

As expected, water cannons were fired. It would be a mistake to think of those as the ones in water parks just because they're called water cannons. That's magic that imparts the properties of iron to water and fires it.

,It would be fun to hit them. I'll knock them down one by one.'

I took a step forward. The water prison that had been surrounding my body shattered.

I clenched my fist. Black aura like flames rose up and enveloped my fist.

Cheonma Shingong Demonic Asura.

I began to knock down the dozens of flying water cannons with my fists.
