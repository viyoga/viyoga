Yeongcheon Ryu (Shadow Heaven Style), Byeokgye (Azure Stream).

I charged at the Blader. Or rather, I pretended to charge. I had no intention of genuinely attacking. I planned to create an opening by feinting, then exploiting the gap that appeared.

The Blader reacted. His left arm moved. The blade grazed my body. Byeokgye is a technique that uses optical illusions. He fell for it.

A gap opened in his side. Being tall, the gap was significant. Without a moment's hesitation, I lowered my upper body, dove in, and swung my sword. Silver flashed.

Noecheon Ryu (Thunder Heaven Style), Noegwang (Thunder Flash).

Kaang!

The fastest strike was blocked by the Blader's right arm. Though it was a sword, the arm, being a living creature's limb rather than metal, easily deflected the Cutlass Sword. There wasn't even a scratch. It was proof that it was far sharper than the Cutlass Sword.

The Blader raised his left arm. I stepped back, using footwork. Buuwoong. His left arm sliced through the air.

Then the Blader moved. With dazzling footwork, he instantly closed the distance, swinging both arms.

,Considering this bastard's strength... I can't block this attack.'

Noecheon Ryu (Thunder Heaven Style), Biraeshin (Flying Thunder God).

I narrowly avoided the attack. But I got a scratch on my cheek. It hadn't even touched me, but the aftereffect alone cut my cheek. Ignoring it, I went on the offensive.

Pajijik.

The Cutlass Sword glowed silver as it went for his neck. My confident stab was deflected by him tilting his head.

,Kkeukekekekeke.'

The Blader laughed. As if he was thoroughly enjoying this situation.

The Blader's arms moved chaotically. There was no order. He swung his swords according to his mood, as if swordsmanship could go to hell. It was fast and light. But that was it. Without sharp blades and immense power, the movements were not threatening at all.

I dodged the Blader's swords as if dancing. I deflected attacks I couldn't avoid with my sword.

Sweat poured down like rain. One mistake and I'd die. Next time? I don't know. Recovery wasn't infinite either. There was no guarantee that he would leave my corpse alone. There was a high probability he would eat my corpse. In the worst case, I could be eaten by him forever. Well, then a missile would come and kill him, though.

,Fucking tiring.'

My arms were heavy. Blood flowed from my palms, where the skin had peeled off from gripping the sword. My thighs felt like they would burst, and my calves were cramping.

,I'm not the only one who's tired.'

The Blader, who kept swinging his swords, was also tired. His upper body was so hot that heat haze was visible. Mutants are living things too. There are limits.

Pajijijik.

I stimulated my muscles with lightning. I pulled my slowing arms with electricity, and supported my weakening lower body with electricity. Even so, my body, pushed to its limits, faltered.

,Kuaaaaaaaaah!'

The Blader roared and swung both arms. Not missing the opening, I unleashed my final strike with all my strength.

,I can't avoid it. It's too late even if I force my legs to move.'

So I had to block it. I raised the silver-shining sword sideways.

Not deflecting, but blocking head-on. Kaaang! The Blader's arms were deflected, but the blade of the Cutlass Sword shattered. The fragments rained down.

,Kkeukeke!'

The Blader laughed, confident of his victory.

,Keke. You idiot.'

I also laughed, confident of my victory.

,Recovery.'

My ability, Recovery, wasn't just able to heal people's wounds. It also affected objects. As if time was turning back.

The broken blade was restored from the hilt. It was a sight as if a new blade was growing out.

The Blader's laughter disappeared. Horror settled in his wide eyes. He flailed his arms pathetically, trying to block my attack, but....

,Too late.'

Noecheon Ryu (Thunder Heaven Style), Noegwang (Thunder Flash).

Blue lightning flashed and beheaded the Blader. His head fell to the floor and his torso stopped abruptly. A beat later, a fountain of blood spurted from his severed neck.

His strong body fell to the floor.

I quietly caught my breath. My whole body ached. I was tired. I had a strong desire to wash up and sleep. I distanced myself from the Blader's corpse and lay down in the shade. It was a little better.

About 30 minutes later, a transport vehicle and several trucks appeared. People in black suits got out of the cars and approached the corpse.

,You've worked hard.'

Lee Yeonhee was among them. She came to me and handed me a cold bottle of water.

,I didn't expect you to come all the way here.'

,Many researchers are paying attention to the Blader's corpse. It means it's strategic material grade.'

,I have a favor to ask.'

,Yes. Do you want a part of the corpse?'

,No. Give me the data from the research on the Blader's swords. It was a very useful sword.'

,I'll try my best.'

It was an ambiguous statement. I was too tired to argue.

,You look very tired. Shall I assign someone to you?'

,No. I'll rest for a bit and go back on my own.'

,How many?! What's their armament?!'

-50 people. Their armament is... they're armed with guns, but it doesn't seem like much. I'll take care of this much on my own.

Na Chaeyoung quickly regained her composure.

Raiders.

,Raiders armed with guns... That's suspicious.'

,Ah. That's true.'

,It seems like they're a raider group sponsored by Ilwoo Group.'

The voice of Jin Chunghyun, the director of Ilwoo Group who threatened me, came to mind.

Lee Yeonhee's guess that Ilwoo Group was behind the raiders was certain.
