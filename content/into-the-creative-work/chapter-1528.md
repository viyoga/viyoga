Soryong City under lockdown for a week.

I stood before the last of the bastards: the Soryong City branch manager of the Cheonma Shingyo and his underlings. They knelt before me, begging for their lives.

,Please spare us, Daehyeop-nim! We haven't betrayed the Divine Teachings!

The branch manager shouted with desperation. I stroked my sword, looking down at him, curious how he would beg for his life.

,The Divine Teachings don't think so. Look, your names are on this list.

I showed him the list directly. Twelve names at the very end. The branch manager and his men.

,There must have been some mistake! We'll formally request an audit from the Divine Teachings! Until the audit results come out, we'll do nothing and lay down everything! So please, have mercy...!

,The audit is already over. The Divine Teachings caught you taking bribes. You were taking money to do sloppy inspections of trade ships, or letting in some things that shouldn't have been allowed in.

,That was... tradition! It's been a tradition and a ritual since long ago!

,Do you think I'm a fucking idiot?

As I unleashed my killing intent, the branch manager lowered his head sharply. I rose from my seat and approached him, sword energy swirling around the blade.

,We don't know anything! We just did as we were told!

,That's right! We did what the branch manager-nim told us to do!

,We're innocent!!

The branch manager's men screamed, terrified. It was ridiculous bullshit. I was the idiot for expecting them to come up with some interesting excuse. I beheaded the branch manager's men, one by one.

Seogeok. Seogeok. Seogeok.

Each time I swung my sword, a head fell. Not even the most skilled executioner could chop off heads so easily.

It was the moment I was about to behead the branch manager. Suddenly, he raised his head and rolled his eyes back.

,If you kill me here and now, you'll regret it!

,Regret?

I became slightly intrigued.

,Do you know who's behind me? Third Elder! Sword Demon-eolshin is behind me! If you kill me, Sword Demon-eolshin won't let it go!

I hesitated and sheathed my sword.

,What's your relationship with Sword Demon-nim?

,I'm Sword Demon-nim's subordinate. I offer money and information every quarter.

,Information?

,Soryong City is a place where many people pass through, so there's a lot of information gathered. I organize it and report it to Sword Demon-nim. I'm one of Sword Demon-nim's informants!

The branch manager spoke confidently. His acting skills were quite good. If he had been an actor in modern times, he might have become a somewhat famous supporting actor.

,Bullshit.

The sword swung. Both of the bastard's ears fell off.

,If Sword Demon was your backer, your name wouldn't have been on the list in the first place.

And he would have been acting cocky from the start, trusting in his backer before things got this far.

,As punishment for talking shit, I'll slowly boil you to death.

,Aaa... Euaaaaak!

The branch manager jumped up. Demonic energy flowed from his hands. By forcibly manipulating his inner energy, the demonic energy exploded for a moment, turning into a powerful force. Of course, it was useless. The branch manager was at the fifth level of Opening, and he couldn't beat me, who was at the tenth level of Foundation Establishment, even if he died and came back to life.

I lightly responded with a punch. The bastard's hand shattered and drooped. I subdued the branch manager with Pressure point.

,If I kill this bastard, the work I have to do here is done. All that's left is to wait until the next manager arrives.

Four more days.

I can just play around and wait.

After executing the branch manager, I headed to my usual pleasure house. Then, seeing a large ship passing by on the river, my eyes widened.

,The ship is moving? It should be under lockdown right now?

I had clearly warned the citizens. If they tried to leave the city, I would kill them all. After killing all the beggars in the city, the citizens had been cooperative.

,There are always rebels. That ship is a merchant ship. One of the merchant guilds ignored my words and moved. Trying to escape? Why?

My mind raced. The name of the merchant guild written on the flag above the ship was Ogeun Merchant Guild. I'd never heard of it before. It wasn't a famous guild.

,I haven't touched anyone except beggars and spies. There's no reason for a merchant guild to break free from my control. I'll be leaving in a few days anyway. So why....

Suddenly, I realized. They were spies who weren't on the list. They must have thought they would die too, since I was finding and executing spies, so they were attempting to escape.

,Ah, shit.

I was annoyed.

If I let them get away here, the Cheonma Shingyo would evaluate me as incompetent. I needed to receive good evaluations in order to move up. I couldn't let that ship get away.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 6]

I ran towards the ship.

I ran without thinking. I didn't use the Lightning Sound Steps of the Lightning Sky Style or the Cheonma Gunrimbo of the Cheonma Shingong. There were too many eyes watching. Since this was the territory of the Cheonma Shingyo, I needed to act like Seomjeondo Yeomguseok as thoroughly as possible.

I stepped onto the river. My feet didn't sink. I ran across the river as if I were running on land.

Deungpyeongdosu.

One of the realms of light footwork techniques, which means walking on water. Once you reach the realm of Foundation Establishment, you can do Deungpyeongdosu without much difficulty. However, it consumes a lot of inner energy, so experts don't bother using it.

I wasn't using Deungpyeongdosu. Even without light footwork techniques, I could run on water thanks to the [Blessing of Water] skill.

,I'm almost catching up!

Someone stood on the deck. He took out a bow and started shooting arrows at me. Arrows filled with inner energy rushed towards me at speeds faster than bullets.

I drew my sword and deflected the arrows. The distance to the merchant ship closed. The archer on the deck kept firing arrows. It was such a rapid fire that I wondered if he was shooting two arrows at the same time.

,But I can see it.

Since I could see it, I could deflect it.

With a single swing of my sword, three arrows were deflected. The arrows the bastard fired couldn't touch my body. The bastard changed his fighting style. He gave up rapid fire and focused his power into a single arrow.

The arrowhead on the bow glowed blue. Energy condensed to form Sword energy.

,The Sword energy is wavering like a candle in the wind. It's unstable. It feels like he's pouring everything into a single arrow.

The arrow, charged with two breaths, was fired.

I thrust my sword forward. The moment the tip of the sword collided with the arrow, I twisted my wrist. The arrow spun and bounced upwards. The archer shot another arrow. Not at me, but at the deflected arrow.

The deflected arrow changed direction and aimed for my back.

,For a bastard who hasn't even reached the realm of Foundation Establishment to display such skill. He's a talented bastard.

It might have been dangerous if I didn't have a moment of [Transience].

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 5]

[Activating the Genius's Time for 10 seconds.]

I turned my back. My feet rubbed against the surface of the river, and a huge spray of water bounced upwards. I raised my sword and thrust it forward. It collided with the arrow. Before the shockwave completely swept over my body, I moved my inner energy.

,A little inner energy manipulation is enough.

I used the shockwave in reverse as propulsion. My body flew backwards like a rocket, trying to pass the merchant ship. I twisted my waist, performed a swallow spin, shook off the propulsion, and landed on the deck. The man who had shot the arrow looked at me with a shocked expression. Judging by the blood flowing from the corner of his mouth, he seemed to have suffered internal injuries due to the reckless attack.

,It's Seomjeondo!!

,He came onto the ship with Deungpyeongdosu light footwork! His inner energy must be depleted! Kill him!

,If we don't kill Seomjeondo, we'll die!

The martial artists rushed at me, desperate to survive. I took a breath and swung my sword.

Quickly.

Even faster.

Pajijik.

I heard the sound of electricity crackling. It wasn't a sound from my ears. Inside me, the Lightning Sky Sutra was quietly reacting.

I swung my sword. Blood splattered. I swung my sword. The enemy's head flew off. I swung my sword again.

,Hmm?

I felt an anomaly as I killed the enemies.

The sword felt stuffy. It was fast, that's for sure. The fact that the enemies couldn't even react to my sword was proof of that.

,But why does it feel stuffy? It's like something is caught on the sword.

But when I looked at the sword, there was nothing caught on it. Blood splattered, but my sword wasn't so weak that it would get caught on a few drops of blood.

Strength entered my arm. The sword, which should have become faster, instead slowed down. It wasn't intentional. It was because I was trying to get rid of whatever was caught on the sword that the sword slowed down. But even the slowed-down sword couldn't be avoided by the enemies.

,It can slow down even more.

I wanted to solve what was caught on the sword first. I gave the sword more strength. The strength of my arm alone wasn't enough. I used my whole body. Even that wasn't enough. I used my inner energy as if I were exploding it.

Pajijijik.

Lightning flowed along the blade. It didn't form Sword energy. It just gave the sword strength as pure energy.

But the sword didn't get faster. It slowed down. It slowed down and slowed down until it stopped completely. It was the moment I widened my eyes in embarrassment.

[Ending the Genius's Time.]

A fountain of blood erupted.

All the enemies around me were torn to pieces and fell to the ground. I was bewildered. It was because even the enemies who I didn't remember swinging my sword at were torn to pieces and died.

,No way...!

There was an archer in front of me. The hand holding the bow was trembling. His eyes were filled with fear towards me.

,Mind is Sword... Sect Leader-nim... there's a monster here....

The archer, who had abandoned his bow, grabbed arrows in both hands and ran towards me. He was detonating his innate energy and attempting a final suicide attack.

,I think I get it.

Mind is Sword.

It's different from Mind Sword. No matter what, there was no way I could reach the realm of Mind Sword right now.

Mind is Sword is just a fragment of Mind Sword.

,It's not my arm that's wielding the sword, it's my mind that's wielding the sword.

Mind is also will.

I held the sword in my hand.

The sword didn't move at all. But the archer was split in half and died.

I looked at the tip of the sword. A drop of blood formed and then fell.

The pinnacle of quick swordplay.

A quick sword that even I, who used it, couldn't see the movement of.

,It's just like magic.

Would this work against experts of a similar level? I couldn't be sure because I hadn't tested it. And since there's no absolute power in this world, I shouldn't be careless.

,I don't know if I can use it in other worlds, but from now on, this is the Lightning Sky Style Mind is Sword.

Pyupyupyut.

The blood vessels in both arms burst, and blood splattered everywhere. It was a side effect of Mind is Sword. My arms couldn't withstand the speed of Mind is Sword.

It didn't seem like I or my other selves in other worlds would be able to use it.
