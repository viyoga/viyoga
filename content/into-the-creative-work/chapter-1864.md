,Na Baksa! (Me, Doctor!)'

I called out to Na Chaeyeong again. There was no answer.

My face hardened. Did something happen to Na Chaeyeong? No, it didn't feel like that. The siren was blaring, and the walkie-talkie was dead. Or maybe the walkie-talkie died even before that.

,I thought things were going smoothly... Did I finally get caught?'

Wiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiiing!

The siren blared loudly from the speakers on the ceiling. I frowned and glared at the ceiling. The camera was pointed directly at me.

I was about to run when I saw the chip plugged into the machine next to me. The chip was still blinking yellow.

,They said it would take one minute.'

I could wait for about a minute. Actually, it was too late to move now. From the moment they sounded the siren, those bastards had already prepared to kill me or capture me alive.

It's noisy in the hallway. It's obvious without looking. The remaining researchers are scrambling to escape.

,Should I run now? No. I need to draw aggro so Na Chaeyeong is safe. I have to attract as much attention as possible.'

So, I sat back arrogantly and stared at the camera. Of course, they wouldn't be able to see my face or expression. I'm wearing a mask, after all.

-Which organization are you with?

The siren stopped, and a middle-aged man's voice suddenly asked me.

I flipped the bird at the camera.

-America? China? Japan? Russia? No. It's too crude a method to say a national agency is involved.

About 30 seconds left?

I planned in my head.

,I'll kill all the bastards who try to stop me.'

It was a perfect plan.

-Ah, I know the answer. You must be a Player. Those crazy bastards who showed up saying this world is a game! Players each have a class, right? You're obviously an Esper. Right? Otherwise, this situation makes no sense. You were probably trying to get the combat AI and sell it to another country. But that's not going to happen. Our lab's guardians and elite special forces are on their way to you!

,Still, his tone is a bit annoying. Should I drop by the control room and kill him while I'm escaping?'

-Surrender.

That was the main point.

There was no mention of Na Chaeyeong. If I were him, I would have mentioned my colleague Na Chaeyeong. That would put more pressure on me. In other words, she hasn't been caught yet.

The yellow blinking light turned blue.

Much more than a minute had passed. I got up from the chair and swung my knife at the camera. The camera shattered and fell to the ground.

-You'll regret this.

Ignoring the voice coming from the speaker to the end, I pulled the chip out of the machine and put it in my pocket. Now, all that's left is to get out of this lab.

,...I can feel it. Eight people outside the door. Soldiers armed with guns, probably.'

Eight elite special forces soldiers are going to stop me? I don't know about the others, but there's no way they can stop me with just that.

Pajijik.

A blue current flickered from the knife and disappeared. I reached for the door.

Na Chaeyeong's expression twisted as she sensed the anomaly.

The walkie-talkie suddenly went dead. It couldn't have happened suddenly like this. Something was wrong. Fortunately, the screens on the laptop were fine. The hacking of the surveillance cameras must have been detected. But the connection was not cut off.

,Are they trying to hack back?'

She had prepared a lot for today. The laptop she was using now was one of the things she had prepared. The firewall wouldn't be easy to break through, and even if they did hack the laptop, they wouldn't be able to get any information.

,No. They don't have time to worry about this.'

The monsters on the 3rd and 5th floors were stirring. The researchers in the central control room on the 2nd floor were all running around busily. Eight armed soldiers were running to the 4th basement floor where Seong Yujin was.

,Seong Yujin. What the hell is this guy doing?'

Incredibly, Seong Yujin was sitting comfortably in a chair. Even though he noticed the anomaly, he didn't seem anxious at all. Rather, Na Chaeyeong, who was watching, was more anxious and uneasy.

,...Should I run away alone now?'

A thought that flashed through her mind. She shook her head and erased the thought. She was the one who proposed and executed this crazy plan. Seong Yujin could have refused, but he never once said not to do it.

Because they made a deal to use each other's time? Seong Yujin didn't have to do that. He had the power to kill someone like her however he wanted. Seong Yujin was considerate of her.

Na Chaeyeong stared intently at the central control room screen. It had suddenly become chaotic since a while ago. It wasn't just because Seong Yujin was discovered. There was something else.

,...Was there an important person among the people Seong Yujin killed?'

No, that wasn't important right now. What was important was that the situation wasn't entirely favorable to them.

And she had seen Seong Yujin's power with her own eyes a few days ago. He could create an opportunity. Na Chaeyeong gripped the walkie-talkie tightly and looked at the monitor. It would be crazy for her to go into the lab. Even if she helped Seong Yujin, she had to help from here.

,Show me like you did last time, you're a crazy bastard.'

And the intruder's typical knife dance began.

15 seconds. That was the time it took for the elite soldiers who even subdued monsters to be annihilated. The reason they were helplessly massacred was simple. The bullets didn't work on the opponent.

The security system is activated. The combat AI, which is still in the experimental stage, will control all the weapons in the lab to eliminate the intruder. He hopes, even though he knows it's impossible.
