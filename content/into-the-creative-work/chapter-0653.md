I got off the carriage with the knightesses and Yuria-ssi, and moved towards the place where the battle was taking place. I hid my presence first. I would first grasp the situation to some extent and then intervene. If it looked boring, I would just ignore it and pass by.

I thought it would be, at most, a battle between a merchant group and thieves. Because it's one of the most common battles in this world.

But the actual battle I saw was a battle between knights and mercenaries. Three knights were fighting against twelve mercenaries. Around them, there were already corpses of dead knights and soldiers.

The knights' condition was not good. Their faces were dark, and they were sweating profusely. Their arms and legs were trembling uneasily.

,It seems they've been poisoned."

Yuria-ssi said quietly.

When I thought about it, there was no way mercenaries could fight knights in a normal way. Moreover, the knights were fighting to protect someone.

,Young master... We'll buy you time, so please escape."

,E-Escape? You think I can escape from those bastards alone?! I need your protection!"

The young master seemed quite weak. He had an ordinary build, mysterious purple hair, and red eyes, but he was trembling like a rat.

,Young master. If you do nothing, you'll just die. And you are someone who must not die here. I'll give you a signal, so escape at the right timing. I'll prevent those bastards from chasing you for as long as possible."

The knight said with a determined look.

,Sir Berol...!"

The young master was slowly preparing to escape.

I eavesdropped on their conversation and my curiosity decreased sharply. The picture was drawn just by their words. Someone must have hired mercenaries to kill that young master.

,How boring. Let's go back."

I was about to turn around without hesitation when Floi said, looking at the mercenaries.

,My lord. Those mercenaries belong to the High Tier Mercenary Corps."

,...Really?"

,The fangs and daggers drawn on their clothes. It's the symbol of the High Tier Mercenary Corps. Unless they're deliberately trying to deceive us, they must be High Tier mercenaries."

I turned around again.

They're attacking openly without hiding their faces or affiliation? One of three things. Either they're idiots, or they're confident they can annihilate everyone, or there's someone backing them.

,If it's High Tier, the story changes. They're guys I don't like, so shall I interfere a little?"

,My lord. Are you getting involved in this?"

,Yeah. You don't like it?"

,I was just reconfirming your will. There's no further meaning. Our knightesses will just follow your will."

,Capture half alive and kill half."

,Understood."

Floi and the knightesses rushed into the battle area.

The mercenaries distorted their faces.

,Goddamn it! Retreat!"

The mercenaries quickly scattered and began to run away. It's the right decision.

,But all that does is turn 1% into 2%.'

The Golden Rose Knight Order is a knight order composed of knightesses with little experience, but their average skill is above Aura Expert Intermediate. Having received the elixir I provided and Yuria-ssi's training, they demonstrate skills beyond those of most knight orders. There's no way mercenaries can escape just by running away.

,Kuaaaaaaack!"

While listening to the screams of the dying mercenaries, I approached the young master. The poisoned knight stood in front of the young master.

,I don't know who you are, but thank you for your help."

,You don't know me?"

I narrowed my eyes. As the rumors of the succession competition in the Pruckus family spread, I also became famous. Not only nobles but even commoners recognize me right away.

,Master-nim. We are in the border area of Rapelli."

Yuria-ssi, who was next to me, said.

,Ah."

This was the border area. The area where the kingdoms of Rapelli and Valt touched. It seemed I had come this far while hunting nomads.

,Are you a Valt Kingdom noble?"

,Yes."

The knight was wary of me. The Valt Kingdom and the Rapelli Kingdom were close but not friendly. They would be at war in a few months.

,I am Baron Eugene Pruckus of the Pruckus County."

I frowned. The bastards still weren't revealing their identities.

While the knight named Berol was contemplating how to react, the young master behind him stepped forward. Trembling his legs and desperately hiding his frightened face, he said to me.

,I-I am Jerdin Hisfield of the Valt Kingdom! The eldest son of the Hisfield County!"

,Of the Hisfield Gate?"

,Y-Yes."

Hisfield Gate.

One of the fortresses of the Valt Kingdom. Located between mountain ranges, this fortress guards the center of the main road leading to the Valt capital. If the Rapelli Kingdom invades the Valt Kingdom, the Hisfield Gate is the fortress they will inevitably encounter.

,Why is the guy who should be guarding the Hisfield Gate here? Is it an invasion?"

,No! Absolutely not!"

I know it's not an invasion.

,Then why are you here? I'm also curious why you're being chased by the High Tier Mercenary Corps."

A smile spread across my face. It felt like I had found a gold coin on the ground.

It was lucky to meet these bastards here. I could easily gain merit by capturing these bastards and taking them to Count Pruckus. I could also instigate that the Valt Kingdom invaded the Rapelli Kingdom first. I could create a justification favorable to the Rapelli Kingdom.

,That's..."

,You don't have to say. However, I will have no choice but to judge as it is then."

,As it is...?"

,I will accept the Valt Kingdom's invasion as it is."

,......!"

Jerdin's face turned blue. The successor of a military family, a count's family, was too naive.

,First, I'll kill the knights and then interrogate you. If you're the eldest son of the Hisfield County, you must be the successor. I'll make you spit out a lot of information."

,Young master! We'll stop him!"

The moment Berol stepped forward and pointed his sword at me, Yuria-ssi moved. Before I knew it, the knife in her hand drew a neat trajectory. Berol's sword blade was cut in half and fell to the ground. Berol's expression was belatedly filled with astonishment. He hadn't even reacted to Yuria-ssi's attack.

,T-This can't be...! With this level of skill, at least...!"

Berol's expression twisted in despair. Nevertheless, he didn't step back.

,Stop!"

It was Jerdin who stepped forward. He jumped in front of the knight, knelt before me, and bowed his head.

,I'll tell you! I'll tell you everything, so please spare us!"

,I'll listen first. If there's even a little suspicion, I'll kill you."

And just in time, Floi brought the High Tier mercenaries. The mercenaries' faces were covered in blood. It seemed they had been beaten up for resisting.

I gave orders to Floi and Yuria-ssi.

,Start interrogating the mercenaries. I'll take care of this bastard."

Jerdin, who met my gaze, could only tremble.

•••

The interrogation proceeded easily. Jerdin was terrified, and the knights who were protecting him fainted halfway due to the poison. I left Jerdin's knights alone. I didn't care whether they died from the poison or survived.

,You were attacked by assassins on your way back to your family from the capital?"

,Yes, Baron-nim. Half of my escort troops were killed then. Thanks to their sacrifice, I managed to escape somehow, but... the attacks continued."

,Why are you here? Your family and here are quite far apart."

Just because it was a border area didn't mean it was all the same border area. This was the outermost part of the border area.

,I couldn't return to my family because I guessed who was behind the assassins. I thought there would definitely be an ambush on the way back to my family."

,Who's the mastermind?"

Jerdin clenched his fists and bit his lip. I could feel his anger. But it wasn't anger directed at me.

,...It's my younger sister."

,Oh."

A picture formed in my head.

Jerdin's younger sister was aiming for the position of head of the family.

Female nobles exist in this world. However, it is rare for a woman to formally inherit the position of successor, and in most cases, they become nobles due to unavoidable circumstances. For example, if the eldest son dies suddenly, the eldest daughter has no choice but to become a noble.

,What is Count Hisfield doing, just standing by?"

,...My father is ill and in such a critical condition that he cannot handle state affairs. That was why I was returning to my family."

,And then your younger sister revealed her ambition?"

,Yes. I don't have any evidence, but I'm sure of it."

Diona Hisfield.

That was Jerdin's younger sister's name. They were born in consecutive years, but their relationship hadn't been good since they were young.

,What about High Tier?"

,Pardon?"

,Why were the High Tier mercenaries chasing you?"

,Diona! That vicious bitch must have commissioned them to kill me!"

,The High Tier Mercenary Corps is active in the Pruckus territory of the Rapelli Kingdom. Do you think it makes sense for your younger sister to hire mercenaries from the Rapelli Kingdom?"

,B-But there's no one else who would target me except Diona."

,You might just not know it."

I didn't ask Jerdin any more questions. I lost interest. I was planning to hand Jerdin over to Count Pruckus to be recognized for my contribution, and get rid of the High Tier mercenaries by framing them appropriately.

,......"

As I closed my mouth and stared blankly at the air, Jerdin fidgeted anxiously.

,B-Baron-nim..."

,What."

,I-I was wondering what you were planning to do with me?"

,I'm going to hand you over to my father. He'll take care of your treatment. Well, he'll negotiate with the Hisfield family, and if the negotiation goes well, you'll return to the Hisfield family."

,Ah, no!"

Jerdin's face became urgent.

,If I go back like that, my position will be reduced! Above all, Diona might plot something and kill me on the way back!"

,Not my problem."

,Baron-nim!"

Jerdin crawled on his knees towards me and grabbed my leg. Jerdin was shedding tears like chicken droppings and begging me.

,Please! Please help me! I need your help, Baron-nim! I don't want to die in vain at the hands of my younger sister like this! Baron-nim!"

My face instantly soured.

Peok!

I kicked Jerdin. Jerdin rolled on the floor. Blood flowed down his nose. He crawled back on his knees and clung to my leg.

,Ah, shit."

,Baron-nim! Baron-nim! Please! Help me! Baron-nim! I'll do anything!"

Peoeok!

Jerdin flew backward and crashed into a tree. He must have received a strong impact on his back, but he crawled towards me.

,B-Baron-nim...! P-Please help me...!"

,Do you know how pathetic you look right now?"

,I-I want to live. If I go back like this, Diona will...! Baron-nim, please help me!"

Why is this guy, who was maintaining his composure until just now, suddenly going on a rampage?

,Could it be that this guy... secretly thought I would help him? And now that I'm trying to cut ties, he's begging like this.'

It seemed like this was his true self.

,You can do anything?"

,Yes! Anything! If you help me, Baron-nim, I can do anything!"

,Kill your knights with your own hands. Then I'll spare you."

,......!"

Jerdin's eyes widened at the unexpected words.

,Your reaction is slow. Didn't you hear me? If you don't want to, that's fine. I guess saying you'd do anything was a lie after all."

,N-No! I can do it! If you just spare me...!"

Jerdin got up from his seat and approached his fallen knights. He drew a sword from the knights' arms. Surprisingly, his stance was decent, as if he had learned swordsmanship.

,Baron-nim..."

,If you can't do it, that's fine."

,N-No, that's not it. Could you spare just one... Could you spare Sir Berol?"

It was an unexpected remark.

,Why?"

,Sir Berol is the most loyal knight..."

,Alright."

,Thank you!"

Jerdin didn't even wait and plunged his sword into the necks of the other two knights.

,Keoeok!"

,Keok!"

I didn't expect him to kill his knights without much hesitation.

,I did as you wished, Baron-nim!"

Jerdin threw away his sword and knelt before me. He kept looking at me, trying to read my expression.

Jerdin followed my words, but I had no intention of helping him at all.

,Okay. I'll help you."

I was planning to hand him over to Count Pruckus.

,Master-nim. The interrogation is over."

Just in time, Yuria-ssi approached.
