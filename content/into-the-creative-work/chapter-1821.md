In this world, there exist countless sects.

From one-person sects to small sects with fewer than 30 members, to sects with some scale that dominate a village, to great sects that everyone knows just by hearing their name.

Founding a sect isn't difficult. Martial arts. Even if it's a trivial martial art, if you have martial arts, you can found a sect. Even street bums can create a sect.

Even in the Demonic Cult of Heavenly Demon, sects exist. To be precise, the Demonic Cult of Heavenly Demon is a gathering of demonic people from various sects who have formed a force. It could be considered a sect alliance similar to the Martial Alliance. In other words, various small and medium-sized sects exist within the Demonic Cult of Heavenly Demon's territory.

And battles frequently occur between these sects. The reasons for the battles are diverse. Because their pride was hurt, to obtain elixirs or magical artifacts, to repay old grudges, and so on. The most common reasons are elixirs and magical artifacts.

The battle between the Scorching Blade Sect and the Silent Sword Sect was such a case.

Originally, the Scorching Blade Sect and the Silent Sword Sect were small sects in the same region and maintained a friendly relationship. Since their forces were small, it was more beneficial to divide and rule the area and stand against external enemies together than to fight over territory.

However, that friendship vanished when a monster appeared. A monster from the Five Qi Realm appeared, and the Scorching Blade Sect and the Silent Sword Sect joined forces to defeat it. It would have been fine if it had ended there, but the half-spiritual monster left behind a Water Essence Crystal as its core.

Two forces subdued the monster. One treasure was left behind by the monster.

It was absolutely impossible for one force to kindly yield to the other. They revealed their greed without hiding it and pointed their weapons at each other.

The Scorching Blade Sect with their fiercely burning blades, and the Silent Sword Sect with their heavy yet silently extending swords.

,We killed the monster! The Water Essence Crystal is ours too!

,Ridiculous! Our Silent Sword Sect's disciples sacrificed their lives to create a once-in-a-lifetime opportunity, and you snatched it away like vultures! The Water Essence Crystal is the price of our disciples' lives!

,Anyone would think only your disciples died! Are you going to ignore the sacrifices of our Scorching Blade Sect's disciples?

,You said you'd give us the monster's corpse!

,Take the corpse, which isn't even worth half of the Water Essence Crystal!

,You don't even know your place, daring to!

,I didn't like you guys from the start!

,Fine. Let's end it here!

The Heads of the family of the Scorching Blade Sect and the Silent Sword Sect screamed at each other and charged. Behind them, murderous sect members wielded their swords and blades.

Chaeng! Kang! Chaengchaengchaeng!

Hot blades and heavy swords crossed paths. The hot blades immediately retreated after touching the swords. Competing in strength with the Silent Sword Sect's swords was suicidal. The blades aimed for openings, while the swords advanced straightforwardly.

A conclusion was not easily reached.

Their skills were evenly matched. When a disciple of the Scorching Blade Sect killed a disciple of the Silent Sword Sect, a disciple of the Silent Sword Sect killed a disciple of the Scorching Blade Sect. As the battle escalated, disciples died. Nevertheless, the victory did not lean towards either side.

,Damn it. Aren't we all going to die like this?

,Did I move too hastily...?

The two Heads of the family regretted it, but blood was already flowing. Losses were accumulating.

,Now that it's come to this.

,I must kill them and obtain the Water Essence Crystal!

They gritted their teeth and swung their weapons.

Kkaaaaaang!

The blades and swords collided, creating a shockwave. They retreated to withstand the shockwave. Having moved without rest, they glared at the enemy while enjoying a brief moment of sweet respite.

,This won't do. A special decision is needed.

,Let's sacrifice the disciples. Have two disciples charge forward to create an opening for a stab.

,Since the sect has done things for them, they'll gladly sacrifice themselves.

,Disciples can be replaced. I need to eat the Water Essence Crystal and become stronger so the sect can become stronger.

The two Heads of the family had the same thought. If they could somehow kill the other Head of the family first, victory would be assured. They would consume the Water Essence Crystal to raise their realm and accept new disciples. Just as they were about to move their lips and send a telepathic message to the disciple they would sacrifice, having devised a perfect plan.

,There's something in the sky!

,Is that lightning? Lightning is supposed to fall, so why is it flying?

,Head of the family-nim! The lightning is flying this way!

The Heads of the family quickly turned their heads. As the disciples said, lightning was flying towards them. And it was quite fast. The Heads of the family, whose realms were higher than the disciples, discovered a person within the lightning.

,...That's not lightning.

,A person. They say that excellent sorcerers can transform into lightning and flames....

The two Heads of the family's complexions were not good. Even if it was a sorcerer and not a martial artist, someone who could use such magic must have at least reached the Five Qi Realm. Perhaps they were an absolute master of the Three Jeong.

Sensing an unfavorable future, they sent telepathic messages to each other.

The Head of the family of the Silent Sword Sect opened the conversation first.

-A master has appeared. If this continues, it's obvious that he will steal the Water Essence Crystal.... This isn't the time for us to be fighting each other.

-How about we scatter and run away? I'll take the Water Essence Crystal.

-You bastard, what kind of trick are you trying to pull!

-Running away with the Water Essence Crystal means taking a risk.

-Then I will gladly take the risk for you.

-.......

-.......

A moment of silence passed. The lightning was getting closer and closer. There was no time. But no one could abandon their greed. This time, the Head of the family of the Scorching Blade Sect opened the conversation first.

-That guy is a master of the Five Qi Realm.

-You can sense that?

-I'm confident in my sense of energy, if nothing else. We're on the verge of reaching the Five Qi Realm. Didn't we kill a monster of the Five Qi Realm together through combined attacks?

-That's right. So, you're saying we should kill that master together?

-Is there any other way? You don't mean to just hand over the Water Essence Crystal to that guy, do you?

-...I can't do that. A joint attack. You ambush him first.

-Since I suggested it, you ambush him first.

-Isn't ambushing your Scorching Blade Sect's specialty? The one who's good at it should do it.

-Isn't being insidious your Silent Sword Sect's thing?

The lightning that had been flying through the sky crossed the vast lake and fell to the ground. His body was flashing with electricity. Even after landing on the ground, he couldn't overcome the momentum and slid along the ground. He overcame the momentum with a Thousand-Kilogram Drop.

,His use of the Thousand-Kilogram Drop is natural. He's a master.

,That composed expression and orderly yet overwhelming aura.... He's a master that can't even be compared to monsters.

Seong Yujin's eyes scanned the crowd. The martial artists felt the hairs on their bodies stand on end. It was a familiar yet unfamiliar sensation to martial artists. A sensation of facing death. The moment they made even a slight mistake here, their lives would disappear.

,Fighting in my territory.... You've got some nerve. What are you?

,G-Great Hero! We are martial artists of the Silent Sword Sect who came to subdue a monster! We had no idea this was the Great Hero's territory!

,We are martial artists of the Scorching Blade Sect! Greetings to the Great Hero! We also didn't know this was the Great Hero's territory!

Seong Yujin chuckled and raised his aura. The martial artists staggered. The air was heavy. They wanted to collapse and surrender right there and then.

Seong Yujin belatedly discovered the monster's corpse behind them. He could feel the presence of an inner core with immense energy there.

,This isn't the kind of energy a simple monster could possess. A half-spiritual monster, huh. If it has the energy of the Five Qi Realm... it could help me break through the wall of the Three Jeong, right?

Seong Yujin grinned. He reached out his hand towards the corpse. The energy moved. The inner core within the corpse floated upwards. The Water Essence Crystal. It looked like a small crystal. He could feel the energy of water.

,Water energy doesn't blend well with other energies.

It was the moment he was about to take the Water Essence Crystal with Telekinesis.

,No!

,The Water Essence Crystal is mine!

Two Heads of the family charged forward. Seong Yujin immediately canceled the Telekinesis. He deliberately didn't use an Instant, but instead moved his lightning energy.

Lightning Heaven Flow (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

A subtle current arising from the lightning energy flowed through his entire body. He manipulated his body using this subtle current. This was the essence of the Flying Thunder God.

The blade and sword flying from the left and right, aiming for his waist and neck. He couldn't avoid them by simply moving. The best thing to do was to draw his sword and block them.

But with the Flying Thunder God, the story was different. Seong Yujin lightly kicked off the ground. His body floated upwards.

,He's not just trying to avoid it by jumping.

Pajijik.

The current grabbed his body. His body, forcibly laid down by the current, slipped between the blade and the sword.

,Unbelievable.... Is that a divine skill?!

,No matter how good a divine skill is, there's no way such movement is possible!

Seong Yujin, who had safely landed on the ground, grinned. He was satisfied with the Flying Thunder God.

,If I use the Flying Thunder God, I can move faster than simply moving my body.

The Flying Thunder God was focused on speed, so it was difficult to put strength into it. It wasn't originally made for attacking in the first place.

,This isn't enough. I need more practical experience.

And this situation was perfect for experimentation. Seong Yujin passed by the two Heads of the family and approached their disciples.

,I don't like your eyes.

Eyes that hadn't completely surrendered to Seong Yujin's aura and were looking for an opportunity. He was a yellow-sprout bastard. Seong Yujin deliberately slowly drew his Hwaryeon Bido.

The enemy gritted his teeth and raised his blade upwards. The blade was heated red-hot with heat.

Lightning Heaven Flow (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

The man widened his eyes and doubted his own eyes. Seong Yujin's body had moved without any preparation. It was so fast that it felt like he had teleported a short distance.

Hwik!

The hot blade only cut through the air.

What followed was the opponent's sneer and a red blade containing red lightning.

,Kaaaaaaak!

The moment his body was cut by the red blade, red electricity flowed in and electrocuted him. His consciousness flew away along with the sensation of his entire body being torn apart. The flown-away consciousness would never awaken again.

,Now, who should I kill next?

Seong Yujin's mocking words stimulated them. All the martial artists realized that Seong Yujin had no intention of showing mercy.

,Fight!!

,If we don't kill him, we'll die!!

The Heads of the family shouted and unleashed their fighting spirit. The martial artists, putting aside their fear, charged at Seong Yujin to survive.

Seong Yujin was relaxed even though martial artists were charging at him from all directions.

,Now this is the situation I wanted.

Even without using an Instant, he could feel the movements of all 20 people. If he could feel them, there was nothing he couldn't avoid.
