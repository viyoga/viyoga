_4. God's Atlantis

374. God's Atlantis

Teo-beok teo-beok.

Having entered the world of God's Atlantis, I walked along a rough mountain path.

Hoo-roo-roo-roo-roo!

My steps stopped. I lifted my head at the strange cry. A brown monkey with the head of a bird was standing on a tree.

,Temongki-gun.'

The monster named Temongki isn't very strong. I could easily deal with it in my current state.

Pah-jee-jik.

As I created lightning in my grasp, the startled Temongki quickly ran away. Temongki was quite cowardly for a monster. It runs away like this unless it's sure it can kill its enemy.

,Chasing after it to kill it... I'm too busy with my own path.'

It might gather its companions and try to attack me, but I'm not worried. That's because I think I can wipe out dozens of Temongkis even if they rush at me.

,When the hell is the village coming out?'

I grumbled and continued walking along the mountain path.

A few minutes later, it was the moment I felt the air suddenly become stuffy. Suddenly, a village appeared before my eyes.

「You have entered the Ironwood Forest, Area 4,523.」

Area 4,523, Ironwood Forest. I successfully arrived at my destination.

I didn't move recklessly at the entrance of the village, but looked around the village and waited for a resident to come out.

,What's with this village being such a mess?'

The buildings in the village were all unique. Some were shaped like rectangular Lego blocks, others were triangular, circular, or reminiscent of beehives, and no two houses had the same shape. The materials of the houses were also the same. Soil, wood, iron, stone, etc., were all different.

,This is... I'd believe it even if it was an amusement park.'

However, these houses have one thing in common. Each house has a chimney, and black smoke is billowing from the chimney into the sky.

,There was a barrier, so I couldn't see the smoke from the outside.'

As I was watching the black smoke gradually disappear into the sky, someone approached.

It was a dwarf wearing an iron helmet shaped like a thimble. He looked about 1M tall and had a long black beard that reached his knees. He was wearing sturdy-looking chainmail and pointed steel shoes on his feet.

My gaze was directed towards the dwarf's waist. He had a hammer hanging there that seemed impossible to even lift without the proper qualifications.

,You don't look like a peddler. Who are you? How did you find our village?"

It was a very rough voice.

,I came to the Ironroot Dwarf Village because I have a favor to ask."

I took a low profile. I wanted to enslave all these dwarves and use them as I pleased, but this wasn't the 'Baekhwan' world, and there was a separate owner of this area.

,A favor? I'm sorry to say this, but we don't listen to requests from outsiders. And we don't welcome outsiders either. We can't even give you a glass of water, so go back."

The dwarf placed his hand on the handle of the hammer hanging on his waist. It was a warning that he would use force if I didn't listen.

,Ah. Please wait a moment. I received a permit from her."

,A permit? Show it to me instead of just talking about it."

,I was just about to take it out."

I took out a letter from my pocket. It was a letter with a blue crescent moon and a sword seal stamped on it. The dwarf took his hand off the hammer handle.

,It's been a while since someone other than a merchant came."

The dwarf took the letter and carefully tore off the seal.

,I am Damons Unroot. A dwarf from the Ironroot Village. A blacksmith and a warrior."

,I am Seong Yujin, an exile."

,An exile?"

,Yes. Why?"

,It's the first time an exile has visited our village. Congratulations."

,Thank you?"

I blinked. Is that something to be congratulated for?

Damons slowly read the letter.

The permit was a letter written directly by Elena Valdert, one of the Empire Ogong, the Illusionary Duke. This area is one of the areas she controls, and you cannot trade with the Ironroot Dwarf tribe without her permission.

I asked her to get this permit. Because I had a purpose.

,...I understand. Duke-gakha wants you to make what you want. Follow me for now."

I followed Damons. Damons was heading to the place where the largest village in the village was located.

It was a house that looked like a skinny winter tree. Dozens of gaunt branches decorate it by spewing black smoke instead of leaves. The size of the house was as large as a four-story mansion.

,That's a unique and wonderful house. I've never seen a house like this before."

,My parents built this house themselves. It took about 4 months."

I opened the door of the mysterious house and went inside. A wave of heat washed over me. I reflexively took a deep breath and regretted it. The feeling of heat filling my lungs wasn't very pleasant.

Five dwarves who were working in front of the fireplace turned their attention to us.

,Damons! Who is that human?"

,Bringing a human all the way here? He must be no ordinary human."

,I don't mean to say this on our first meeting, but I have a bad feeling about this human."

The stocky dwarves showed interest in me.

If I enslaved them and used them, my life would become easier in no time, but I give up because Elena would try to kill me if I did that.

,This human had a letter from Duke-gakha. We have to make something for this human."

,Something? What something?"

,Keu keu keu. It's been a while since we've hammered to the orders of a human."

,But did he come empty-handed? Disappointing."

The dwarves' reactions weren't bad.

,Of course, I brought gifts for all of you."

I took out beer from my inventory. The common fantasy setting that dwarves like beer worked in this world as well. I made up my mind and took out 10 boxes of beer.

However, the dwarves who saw the beer I took out only tilted their heads.

,What is this?"

,It's a glass bottle I've never seen before. Is there water in the bottle? If it's water, we have enough in our village."

,It's beer. Beer. I heard you guys like beer... or was I wrong?"

,Beer? Ooooooh!"

,Ooooooh!"

The dwarves rushed to the beer boxes, grabbed a bottled beer in one hand, and immediately put it in their mouths.

,Uooooo! I can't believe such delicious beer exists! It's on a different level from the beer I've been drinking so far!"

,Kiyaaaaa! It's even cool and perfect!"

The dwarves suddenly started a drinking party. I felt uneasy. I wondered if they would pass out drunk.

But unlike my expectations, the dwarves didn't overdrink.

,Seong Yujin. Thank you. I can't believe you have such delicious beer. Where is this beer made?"

,It's German beer."

,German? Anyway, the beer tastes good."

Damons' reaction was lukewarm. It seems he just asked where the beer was from.

,But you seem to have drunk a lot, so can you make the item I want?"

,Haha! There are no weak dwarves who become incapacitated after drinking a bottle of beer! Beer is oil to us!"

,Yes? Oil?"

,Wheels turn better when they're oiled. In the same way, we move better when we're beer-oiled! Now, tell me what you want! If we have enough materials, we can even make you a gun!"

These dwarves' skills seem to be much more advanced than I thought. Come to think of it, I see some machines nearby.

,What I want is armor, a sword, and a cloak. I want the armor to be light yet strong, and the sword to have the best durability. The cloak should have the effect of blocking the cold."

,Hmm."

The dwarves who heard my words looked at each other and then put the bottle to their mouths again.

,Tsk. How boring."

,I was expecting him to ask me to make a slaughter wagon... ."

,The peddler who came last time said that guns are popular these days... was that a lie?"

The dwarves seemed disappointed.

,Armor and a sword... were you a knight? Seeing as you were sent here directly, you must be a knight trusted by Duke-gakha."

,Well... something like that."

I said vaguely. I couldn't reveal my relationship with Elena to them.

,Do you have a desired design? We'll try to meet your needs as much as possible."

,Um... first of all, I'd like it to be stylish. Heavy yet sleek, not too big... but not too small either."

,Geez. How can we do that if you say it like that? Draw a rough picture of the shape you want."

Damons handed me paper and a pencil. I took the pencil and drew the appearance of the armor and sword I wanted on the paper.

Even from an objective point of view, it was a poorly drawn picture. But it wasn't difficult to confirm the general outline or shape.

,What is this on the gauntlet? A hidden weapon?"

,It's a hidden blade. A sword that comes out of the back of the hand. Is it impossible?"

,It's not impossible. It's just that it doesn't seem very practical."

,It looks stylish, so please make it like this. I might have a chance to use it in a real battle."

,Hmm..., I understand. But this sword... the end seems blunt, is that intentional?"

,Ah, that's because I drew it wrong. Please make the sword strong and sharp."

,Is this skull decoration on the sword necessary?"

,Isn't it cool? Doesn't it give you the feeling of a supreme sword?"

,No. It only looks creepy. If you carry a sword like this, people will doubt your dignity and criticize our dwarves' tastes, so I'll take it off."

,Sure."

I nodded. In fact, the decoration of the sword wasn't that important.

,What about the cloak?"

,Please make it red."

,Alright. It'll take about a week."

Damons gathered the paper and stared at me intently.

,...Is there something you want from me? I'll give you more beer if you want."

,You'll give me more beer... I won't refuse. But do you happen to have a weapon you're using? I'd like to see it if you do."

,My weapon...?"

Why? I looked at Damons with wary eyes. I glanced around and saw that the other dwarves were also looking at me. Could it be that these guys are trying to steal my weapon?

,Ah, don't misunderstand. We don't go out of this village often, so we don't have many opportunities to see weapons made outside the village. We rarely have the chance to broaden our horizons. You don't have to show it to us if you don't want to."

,It's okay to show you. I happen to have a hobby of collecting weapons, so I have quite a few."

I took out the weapons made by the dwarves of the 'Baekhwan' world. I was going to put down the noses of the Ironroot dwarves. But the dwarves laughed when they saw the weapons I took out.

,It's well made. But it looks like it was made carelessly."

,...Carelessly?"

My face hardened. The Frost Hammer dwarf said that he made this sword with all his heart when he gave it to me.

,It's a good sword. But when we look at it, the craftsman who made this sword has the skills to make a better sword."

,Hmm. He's a pretty good craftsman, although not as good as us. But his personality doesn't seem very good."

,The handle is flimsy. There's no way the blacksmith who made this wouldn't know... ."

,He has the skills, but he doesn't have the spirit of a craftsman."

Bba-deu-deuk.

I gritted my teeth.

,Damn it. I've been treating you guys too comfortably lately, so you slave dwarves are getting out of line... I'll have to give you a proper mental education when I get back later.'

The dwarves looked at me. The sound of me grinding my teeth was a bit loud.

,...Why? Are you angry because we said too much? If you're angry, we'll apologize. We did say too much."

,It's a kind of habit. And everyone. Actually, this is just one of the swords I have. I don't even use it in real life. I'll show you the weapon I really cherish and use in real battles."

I took out the Hwaryeon Bido. I couldn't just stand by and be ignored.

As soon as the dwarves saw the Hwaryeon Bido, their eyes widened and their mouths dropped open.

,This is...!"

,I can't believe it."

,Heo-eo......"

,Amazing......"

I was satisfied to see the dwarves' surprised expressions.

,Haha. How is it? Isn't this red blade especially amazing? Every time I see it, my heart swells with pride... ."

,Seong Yujin."

Damons called me with a serious expression.

,Yes?"

,This sword has too much malice accumulated in it. It's about to turn into a Cursed sword. It looks like it's made of dragon scales... This wouldn't happen unless you slaughtered humans in a short period of time... This sword is dangerous."

,Dangerous? A sword is just a sword."

,It's difficult to grasp the condition of a sword unless you're a craftsman. Didn't you feel a headache or a sudden surge of murderous intent every time you wielded the sword? You must have also felt the urge to harm yourself."

,No. I didn't feel anything... ."
