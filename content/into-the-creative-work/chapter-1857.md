D-23.

Na Chaeyoung, a Technologist, had two basic skills.

One was disassembler creation. She could create a disassembler that could disassemble existing objects to obtain resources.

The other was crafting. If she knew and understood exactly what to craft, she could consume resources to create it. Na Chaeyoung made firearms with this crafting skill. It had the disadvantage of consuming a lot of resources and requiring her to understand and create each part of the firearm.

But the advantage was that she could make anything if she had enough resources. Even if it was something made of rare minerals.

And now, she was making a titanium knife for me to use. She was using her crafting skill to turn steel into titanium. It consumed more than 10 times the steel resources, but it was worth it.

,The structure of the knife isn't complicated, so Na Chaeyoung can easily make it if she understands titanium and has enough resources.'

Na Chaeyoung understood titanium metal in just two days. The problem was resources. Making one knife consumed more resources than I thought.

Anyway, it was finished. A straight sword, not a curved one, to my liking. I swung the knife and smiled with satisfaction.

"It's a bit light, but it's strong, which is good. Great. Since it's made of titanium, the knife's name is Titania."

"Don't use it too roughly. Even if it's a knife made of titanium, the blade will wear out."

"Then Doctor Na can repair it for me."

"Since I made it, I can repair it. But it will require additional resources."

"Speaking of resources, isn't there a way to get them in large quantities?"

"There is a way to dominate a special mine entirely, or to use a resource converter to use them efficiently... But we can't do either of those right now. The most efficient thing is the disassembler. Upgrading the disassembler will increase its efficiency."

"We don't have the resources to upgrade the disassembler."

"That's the problem."

Na Chaeyoung sighed. In the end, she couldn't do anything without resources. That was the biggest disadvantage of Technologists and Bionics. Besides, we had a mountain of things to do. We also had to start fortifying Seongak Elementary School.

I took the titanium knife and slipped away from her. Exercise is annoying and difficult, so I don't want to do it, but it's better than working.

,If I want to get stronger, I have to keep training my body. ...This is such a pain in the ass.'

I swung the titanium knife in the empty auditorium and practiced Noecheonryu. Tonight, I'm going to meet the boss of Taewang. I needed to sharpen my combat senses as much as possible.

• • •

Na Chaeyoung wore a white gown and sat in the passenger seat of the truck. She tried to ignore her pounding heart and predicted the actions of Taewang's boss. What Taewang's boss wanted was her, or more precisely, the abilities of a Technologist. Basically, her cooperation was needed, so she would be able to negotiate. She would take a loss, but... she couldn't help it. She was at a disadvantage.

Seong Yujin, who was sitting in the driver's seat, started the truck and asked Na Chaeyoung.

"Are you going to wear the gown? You always take it off when you go outside?"

"...It's a kind of jinx. It helps me concentrate when I wear it."

The truck went outside through the back gate of the school. Na Chaeyoung seemed to want to negotiate, but Seong Yujin's intuition told him that it wouldn't go well. Seong Yujin simulated the battle in his head.

He had to fight while protecting Na Chaeyoung. He might have to prioritize escaping over annihilating the enemies.

,Right now, my equipment is one pistol and a titanium knife. Just in case, two rifles are prepared in the back of the truck.'

Unfortunately, there were no bombs. Because Na Chaeyoung hadn't researched them yet. It would be helpful to have even a grenade... but he couldn't do anything about it.

They headed towards the abandoned factory in Incheon. The presence of people around them disappeared. There wasn't much light, so it felt eerie. It was a neighborhood that felt like a crime would happen.

"...I did a little research, and dozens of deaths have occurred in this neighborhood. The police announced that it was a conflict between criminal organizations, but people say that there are other secrets hidden. I heard that there are monsters."

"Is that a common urban legend?"

"It's not an urban legend. It's a kind of omen. That omen has been steadily present since 2000, when the first meteorite, the red meteorite of the beginning, fell."

"Is that also a setting from the game, Eternal Eden?"

"That's right. After that, the meteorites continued...."

The conversation that seemed to continue ended with Na Chaeyoung tightly closing her mouth. The promised abandoned factory building came into her sight. There were a few lights in the dark abandoned factory. Also, cars were lined up around the streets. Roughly, there were more than 10 cars. She had to assume that there were at least 50 gangsters around.

Seong Yujin slowed down the truck and asked Na Chaeyoung.

"Are you okay?"

"...I'm fine."

He stopped the truck. Assuming a retreat, it might be easier to abandon the truck. Someone walked towards the front of the truck. It was the man who brought us the invitation yesterday, Oh Seongsang.

"You've arrived. The boss is waiting. Please follow me as I guide you."

Seong Yujin rolled his eyes. The other gangsters were not visible. They seemed to be hiding.

,Should I spread a microcurrent like spreading my senses? I think it's possible... No. I should just save my strength. Since they're definitely hiding anyway. They won't attack until the negotiation breaks down.'

The construction site was unfinished. There were walls, but they were crude. Even then, more than half of them didn't have walls. The steel bars that formed the frame were thick and strong, so it didn't seem like they would collapse easily.

They went up to the 5th floor. It wasn't the highest floor, but the breeze was quite cool.

The center of the 5th floor.

He could see a woman in a suit sitting on a black sofa chair that looked comfortable, and a large man standing next to her. There was a table in front of the woman, and another identical chair was placed there.

The man, who was nearly 2m tall, was frowning. He had worked so hard on bulking up his upper body that the balance between his upper and lower body was similar to that of a gorilla. His hair was naturally a buzz cut.

Seong Yujin walked with Na Chaeyoung and watched Taewang's boss. She was a woman with voluminous dark brown bobbed hair parted to the side. Judging by her appearance, she looked to be in her late 30s.

She was basically a beautiful woman, but her face had many scars. The right side of her mouth was torn to her cheek, and there was a burn mark on her neck. There was a long knife scar on her left cheek, and she wore a black eye patch over her left eye. Her cheek and eye were cut together.

She was smoking a cigarette with her left hand. Seong Yujin knew from the smell that it was a marijuana cigarette. Her left hand was more brutal than her face. It had all sorts of scars, and the red burn scars were particularly severe. Her left pinky finger was missing. He couldn't tell if it was cut off or if she cut it off herself.

One thing was certain: she was a woman who had survived a battlefield.

"Doctor Na. It's hard to see your face. Please sit down."

"...I didn't expect to see you in a place like this."

"I like it here."

Na Chaeyoung sat down in the chair. Seong Yujin stood right next to her. Oh Seongsang stood to the boss's right, curling up the corners of his mouth as if he was looking forward to what would happen.

"I am Gu Taehee of Taewang. Doctor Na Chaeyoung, let me get straight to the point. Come under me. I need the power of a Technologist."

"...I have no intention of going under anyone. A life of being exploited is not in my life plan."

"It's not exploitation. I'll give you as much as you earn. Wealth or power. I'll pay you without lacking anything."

"Do you think those are what I want?"

"What do you want?"

"......I want a deal. I'll provide you with the things you need at a low price. That's the relationship I want with you."

"Ah. So you want to be on equal footing with me? Gee, you're a doctor, but have you been stuck in the lab doing research? You don't know the ways of the world. Do you think what I'm saying now looks like a deal or an offer?"

Koong.

Na Chaeyoung heard the sound of her heart dropping.

She had been nervous since she came here. She tried not to show it as much as possible, but cold sweat was flowing, and her heart was beating like crazy. She had several plans as she came here. But from the moment she arrived at the abandoned factory, her head didn't work. Her damn heart was beating so hard that her whole body felt like a heart.

,Rather....'

Should I just give in?

Wouldn't that be better? Taewang was the best gangster in Seoul. In other words, he could be said to be the best gangster in Korea. To go into that organization and work. Even if she was somewhat oppressed, her abilities would be needed, so wouldn't her safety be guaranteed?

,...If I refuse to the end here.'

A bullet would be lodged in her body. No, no. That was too generous of an imagination. She was a player. She had many uses. They wouldn't kill her on purpose, but subdue her. And she could be dragged away, kidnapped, and subjected to all sorts of terrible things.

,Ah, no! I can't think about that now!'

Her expression.

She had to maintain a blank expression. That way, she wouldn't be underestimated. She wouldn't be ignored. She could somehow maintain her expression. Because that was what she had been doing all her life. But a drop of sweat dripped down her chin, and her legs trembled.

"Doctor. Shall I tell you how to live a long life? You just need to know your place, your capacity. You have the qualities to be a manager. But you don't have the capacity to be a leader, a ruler who leads people. Do you think you can survive in a city full of chaos and death with that ambiguous mindset and control?"

"......."

Na Chaeyoung couldn't refute it. Gu Taehee saw her accurately. Her judgment was amazing.

Na Chaeyoung had little experience leading anything since she was young. She became a researcher because her parents told her that she had talent, so she chose to do it. She also played the Eternal Eden game because a friend recommended it. She also knew that a position that leads others, a position of responsibility at the highest level, was not right for her personality.

"I'm...."

She opened her lips with trembling lips.

Gu Taehee threw the marijuana cigarette on the floor and smiled. Doctor Na Chaeyoung. She thought she would be difficult, but when she saw her in person, she was nothing special. She was an ordinary person with abilities. She gets scared easily. But she has abilities and seems to have some determination, so if she manages her well, she can be very useful as an executive.

Now that she had a Technologist in her hands, she drew the next picture. According to Oh Seongsang, Technologists can use resources to create advanced weapons. Through research, they can develop advanced technology, and if they have enough resources and technology, they can even make fighter jets and tanks. So, if she came under her, she would have her start with research and develop technology as quickly as possible. Even if she could only make tanks, she would be able to gain tremendous power.

"Hey, Gorilla."

Suddenly, Seong Yujin, who had been quietly standing next to Na Chaeyoung, opened his mouth. He glared at Jihanmu, the large man standing to Gu Taehee's right, with a look full of annoyance.

"What the hell have you been doing to my woman since earlier? Are you so desperate to die?"

Jihanmu was not kind enough to just take the abuse. He frowned and growled at Seong Yujin in a low voice.

"Shut up. This is not a place for the likes of you to open your mouth. If you're a dog, act like a dog-"

The words were forcibly cut off.

The titanium knife was plunged deep into Jihanmu's neck. Before Jihanmu could move his arms and legs, the knife was drawn straight down. Blood splattered and internal organs poured out, and the knife cut off Jihanmu's neck at the moment the huge body, which had lost its center of gravity, leaned forward.

Jihanmu's head spun around in the air and fell to the floor, touching Na Chaeyoung's feet.
