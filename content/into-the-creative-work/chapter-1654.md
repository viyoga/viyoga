,Is there something blocking magic?'

I narrow my brows, staring at the golden sand soldier I just sliced a chunk out of.

Its appearance was different from that of a typical golden sand soldier.

,More like a commander unit... or rather, a warrior.'

Its entire body gleamed with gold. It wasn't a golden sand soldier, but a golden warrior.

,I need to be careful of that golden warrior.'

The force of the hail falling from the sky began to weaken little by little. The solid barrier of golden sand soldiers also loosened. Hatep revealed himself from the center.

As expected, Hatep was disguised as a golden sand soldier. As the ruler of the golden desert, he exuded a unique aura.

"The bastard we need to kill is over there! Go!"

I ordered the undead legion to attack. The undead legion burst forward as if exploding. They revealed their aggression without hiding it, as if all the battles until now had been a mere act.

"Uwaaaaaaa!"

"What the hell! Those undead bastards! Don't tell me they've been hiding their strength until now?!"

"Goddamn it! I can't die here!"

The advantage of the undead is that they have no fear of death.

The desert warriors were brave, but not as much as the undead. The undead completely disregarded their own deaths. Those already dead have no concept of death. They simply follow orders.

The desert warriors began to be pushed back by the undead legion, which was solely focused on destruction. Some of them even tried to flee. If they fell, the undead would rush in and cut off the desert warrior's breath.

The army of death scattered death in all directions.

"There is no rest for you bastards."

I grabbed the souls of the desert warriors. It was impossible to create undead from corpses, but it was possible to grab their souls.

,If necessary, I can offer the souls to the Living Corpse (Pseudo) and receive power.'

I prepared a plan for even the worst-case scenario. It didn't seem like I would lose in this battle.

"You useless bastards!"

Hatep's voice, filled with anger, echoed loudly. However, the desert warriors, who had already completely lost their will to fight, didn't even pretend to listen to Hatep's words.

「The time of disaster has ended.」

The hail falling from the sky stopped. The gathering dark clouds disappeared, and the hot sunlight began to beat down on the desert. The hail that had fallen on the sand melted at a rapid pace.

The golden sand soldiers, who had been focusing on defense, moved. Instead of advancing towards the undead legion, they chased after the fleeing desert warriors and attacked.

"Useless things! You don't deserve the name of warrior! Die! Die and become sacrifices! Oh, Master of the Oasis-isiyeo! I offer these foolish bastards! Grant me gold!"

The corpses and souls of the desert warriors who died at the hands of the golden sand soldiers disappeared, and glittering gold fell upon Hatep. Hatep scattered the received gold on the sand. The sand and gold mixed together and rose up as soldiers.

,So that's how he created the golden sand soldiers.'

Hatep knelt on the ground. He raised both hands high and shouted towards the sky.

"Oh, meaningless death-isiyeo! I offer all the deaths here! I also offer the head of Tutankhamun to you! In return, grant me power! If I am defeated, I will offer everything I have to you!"

Hatep drew upon Meaningless Death (Pseudo), even risking his own life.

Normally, it would be impossible. Unless it was a normal god. Osiris was a god of high stature. He wouldn't move for just anything.

,That would be the case normally. But right now, Osiris is hostile to me.'

Osiris accepted Hatep's offer. The energy of hell was concentrated on Hatep.

"Thank you, Meaningless Death-isiyeo!"

Hatep rose from his spot. He pointed at the undead legion and ordered the golden sand soldiers.

"Get rid of those annoying corpses!!"

The golden sand soldiers began to advance.

The undead and the golden sand soldiers collided head-on.

Those non-living beings fought without a single step back, as they had no emotions and knew no pain.

The individual power of a normal golden sand soldier was similar to that of a low-level undead. However, they fought more strategically than the low-level undead. They weren't soldiers for nothing.

Kwaaaaaang!

The sound of a shell exploding could be heard on one side of the battlefield.

It was the sound of Gula and the golden warrior fighting. The golden warrior was clearly Hatep's secret weapon.

,It's fortunate that Gula is here. If Gula wasn't here, the flow of the battle might have been unilaterally advantageous for him.'

I stopped worrying about Gula. I didn't think Gula would lose, and even if Gula died, I could revive him as long as I had the Book of the Dead. Even if that wasn't the case, I could use Again Undead. Gula is both an undead and a living human.

I watched the battle between the undead and the golden sand soldiers with a hardened expression.

,The battle is stagnant... no, it's dragging on. At this rate, the battle won't end even when night comes.'

The soldiers on both sides were steadily being consumed. Yet, the victory was not tilting to either side.

,Is it because they are soldiers who know no fear?'

They were not affected by morale. In other words, they fought like machines.

,I need to create a variable.'

I actively used Corpse Explosion. Then Hatep actively used the power of the divine seat. He created a giant wave of sand and struck the rear of the undead legion. Thousands of undead were swept away by the wave that poured down like a tsunami.

"Goddamn it. It's a big disadvantage that I can't replenish the undead because of Osiris."

I modified the intermediate undead. I removed their legs and gave them wings. The intermediate undead flew into the sky and fell into the enemy ranks.

"Corpse Explosion!!"

Kwaaaaaang!

The golden sand soldiers swept away by the bombardment disappeared.

Hatep distorted his face and took out gold from the subspace and scattered it on the ground. Golden sand soldiers sprang up like mushrooms after rain.

,As expected, he had kept gold separately.'

I winked at the Cyclops. The Cyclops, who was behind me, stepped forward.

"Blessing of the Dead."

"Impure Blessing."

"Steel Skin."

"Transcendent Power."

"Protection of the Deceased."

The liches and I poured buff magic onto the Cyclops. Originally, it was a buff that should have been given to Gula, but Gula was too far away right now.

The Cyclops's size, which was over 30 meters, nearly doubled.

"Kuaaaaaaaaaaaaa!"

The Cyclops let out a roar. The undead around it flew away and crashed into the sand.

"Shut up! Are you trying to wipe out our allies?! Your enemies are those sand dolls! Crush those sand dolls to death!"

The Cyclops moved. The desert trembled with every step it took.

The Cyclops swung its leg at the golden sand soldiers. Dozens of golden sand soldiers collapsed at once. The Cyclops chuckled and began to attack aggressively.

「The Master of the Oasis (Pseudo) descends.」

Wind blew from all directions. The wind carrying sand gathered in front of the Cyclops, forming a shape. The shape of a human with the head of a beast. Its body was made of wind and sand. The giant was not at all inferior in size to the Cyclops.

"Set has stepped up himself. Is he planning to end it here?"

Set and the Cyclops grabbed each other's hands as if in a test of strength.

Surprisingly, the Cyclops, who was covered in all sorts of buff magic, was slowly being pushed back.

,In a way, it's only natural. The Cyclops is an undead, and the opponent is a divine being.'

In addition, Set was also the god of war.

,At this rate, the Cyclops will be defeated.'

I couldn't lose the Cyclops so futilely.

"Liches. We need to strengthen the Cyclops further."

"Lord-siyeo, we have already concentrated all our magic on the Cyclops. There is no other way."

"There is one way."

My gaze turned to the low-level undead. Out of the 70,000 low-level undead, only about 30,000 were still able to move properly.

"Are you planning to use Undead Modification Technique? The size is different. Using a few hundred low-level undead as materials won't help the Cyclops."

"I'll use them all."

"...That's."

"This is the best way. You guys support me."

"...We will follow your will, Lord."

I nervously used Undead Modification Technique (SS).

The 30,000 low-level undead who were targeted paused. The bodies of the low-level undead floated into the air. Bba-deuk, ggu-deuk, ka-deu-deuk!

The bodies of the low-level undead were broken, clumped together, overlapped, and compressed into a giant mass of flesh. I felt my mana rapidly depleting.

,I've only done half of it, and it's already running out...!'

I reached out to the side and grabbed the lich's skull.

Mana Drain!

I sucked up all of the lich's mana.

"Lord-siyeo?!"

"Mana is insufficient! You sacrifice yourself!"

"Ugh...! I will gladly sacrifice myself!"

I sacrificed 20 liches to replenish my mana.

I moved my hand. The giant mass of flesh flew towards the Cyclops, who was being pushed back by Set. Undead Modification Technique (SS) was activated, and the mass of flesh permeated the Cyclops's body.

The Cyclops's size increased further. Its skin surface was covered with reddish-blue bone armor.

"Kehahahahahaha!"

The Cyclops began to overwhelm Set, letting out a thunderous laugh. Set also didn't give up and increased his body with sand and wind. However, it was not enough to defeat the Cyclops, who had been strengthened with 30,000 low-level undead. Set's body was gradually pushed back and fell backward. Thousands of golden sand soldiers caught in it turned to dust.

The Cyclops climbed onto Set's body to secure victory and took a mount position.

-Hatep! Offer me the golden sand soldiers! Quickly!

"W-What are you suddenly saying?!"

-At this rate, I'll be defeated by this corpse giant! Do you want to be defeated? Offer me the golden sand soldiers right now!

"Keuek! I will offer them!"

Hatep shouted with tears in his eyes. The golden sand soldiers nearby disintegrated and were absorbed into Set.

,Is he trying to strengthen himself in the same way I did? I can't let that happen.'

I mana drained 10 more liches. With this, I sacrificed all the liches. But there was no regret.

"Cyclops! You've worked hard until now! As a reward, I'll give you your glorious end!"

"Kuaaaaaaa?!"

The Cyclops shouted. A will to resist could be felt. As expected, the Cyclops had become too strong. I squeezed out all my mana and used black magic.

"Corpse Explosion!"

The Cyclops's giant body exploded. Golden sand soared into the sky, forming a small mushroom cloud.

「The Ruler of the Golden Desert has died.」

「You have won the special event.」
