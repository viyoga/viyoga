The God of the Sky, the King of Olympus, bestowed lightning upon humanity.

All humans were delighted, yet also felt awkward.

Because it was all so sudden.

Still, since it was lightning from the God of Gods, humans offered sacrifices in gratitude.

Zeus was the most revered among humans. At the same time, they knew he was an immense tyrant. They simply didn't dare speak ill of Zeus for fear of the lightning falling from the sky.

Even if they skipped sacrifices to other gods, they couldn't skip sacrifices to Zeus. Even cities that worshipped other gods made sure to offer sacrifices to Zeus. If they accidentally missed a sacrifice to Zeus, that city would be destroyed.

Perseus, the King of Argos, faithfully offered sacrifices of gratitude to his father, Zeus. Because his father, Zeus, wasn't the type to show leniency just because he was his son. Though he had never actually seen Zeus.

,Zeus-nim has bestowed lightning. All the temples announced the oracle simultaneously, so it's certain that lightning has been given to humans."

Perseus said to his vassals. Lightning had been given to humans, but humans didn't know how to use it. Even the most erudite scholars were at a loss for words.

It was like the past. Prometheus stole fire from Olympus and gave it to humans. Humans were grateful. And they used the fire without much thought. Fire was intuitive, after all. But lightning? How dare humans handle the lightning that strikes from the sky?

,Did the great master of the sky only give us the oracle?"

,Father gave this to me."

Perseus held something in his hand. Something transparent and round. For a moment, the vassals were captivated by the object's exotic beauty, but soon they thought rationally.

,That object must be related to lightning."

,Isn't it an object to summon and control lightning?"

,Maybe you can drop lightning to kill animals!"

,Or use lightning to punish humans!"

Only primitive methods came out of the mouths of those smart vassals. No matter how smart the scholars were, they were too ignorant about lightning. The lightning that strikes from the sky belongs only to Zeus. No one even thought of studying it.

Perseus was disappointed in his retainers, who were all spouting the same nonsense. He sighed inwardly and carefully fiddled with the object in his hand.

,If this is really a tool to handle lightning... I want to strike these unhelpful vassals with divine punishment. How do I use it? Should I shout 'Lightning'?"

But he was a king, after all, wouldn't that be too undignified?

Click!

Perseus's finger touched something, and a light came on.

,Fire?!"

Startled, Perseus almost dropped the light bulb he was holding.

The vassals who were watching opened their mouths.

,To hold fire in your hand... Your Majesty. Isn't it hot?"

,It's not hot. But is this really fire?"

,...I don't think it's fire. Zeus-nim said he would send lightning... Could this be lightning?"

,Does this look like lightning to you?"

,......"

The vassals closed their mouths again. No matter how they looked at it, that shining thing wasn't lightning. Lightning was more ferocious and terrifying.

,Hoo. I'll have to offer another sacrifice to Father. Priests, prepare the sacrifice."

Even in this situation, Perseus consistently called Zeus, whom he had never met, "Father." It was for the sake of his power.

Two days passed.

The priest announced that the preparations for the sacrifice were complete. The people gathered around the temple for the sacrifice. They seemed curious about the lightning given to humans as well.

,Oh dear. I didn't expect so many to gather."

Perseus's expression hardened.

Even if you offer a sacrifice, the gods don't always respond. Most of the time, they are ignored. Especially Zeus, who ignored almost all sacrifices except for one or two out of 100. Perseus, his own son, was only acknowledged about 5 times.

,If Father doesn't respond here... my authority will decline."

He felt burdened by the fact that the people, who usually didn't gather much for sacrifices because they were busy, had gathered so much this time.

,Damn it. I can't stop here...'

The sacrifice proceeded. He originally intended to offer only two cows, but he offered one more.

,Please. Please respond! Father!'

Was it because of his sincerity?

He felt a presence descend upon the temple.

The overwhelming presence made him gasp for breath. Perseus was in a better state. The priest sat down on the floor, trembling. His eyes were spinning, and he seemed out of his mind. Not to mention the vassals. They prostrated themselves on the floor and tightly closed their eyes. He thought he smelled urine somewhere.

The people outside the temple were the same. They instinctively knelt and worshipped Zeus. No one opened their mouth. They just worshipped.

,Father, I offer three cows! Please bestow your great wisdom upon us!"

-What are you curious about?

,We have received great grace from Father and received lightning, but we are too foolish to know how to handle it! Please bestow your great wisdom!"

-Ah, I see. I overestimated you too much. My mistake. You were ignorant, incompetent, foolish, and uncivilized beings who didn't even know the concept of electricity, let alone lightning....

Ugh.

Perseus felt something impious rising up, but he tried to control it. The opponent was a god who could destroy a city with a single gesture.

-I will bestow wisdom upon you. You need tools to handle lightning well. It would be good to treat Hephaestus well. Secure many blacksmiths as well.

Kwa-roo-roo-roong!

A bolt of lightning fell from the temple ceiling. Perseus trembled with extreme pain as if his whole body was being torn apart.

,Wh-why? Am I going to die like this?!"

Knowledge flooded into his panicked mind.

Lightning. Electricity. Generator. Battery, smartphone. Refrigerator, air conditioner, EMP....

Incomprehensible knowledge entered his mind. It was like. Even if he tried to delve deeply into the knowledge, the contents were empty, making it difficult, if not impossible, to utilize.

,...I am a king. It is the role of the blacksmith to make these things, so there is no need to know them deeply.'

Didn't Father God also say to use the blacksmiths?

Perseus shook off the pain. He wasn't just lucky to become the King of Argos.

,Father! I have a request!"

Perseus said. It was a somewhat rude way to speak to the King of the Gods. He relied on the affection of his blood relatives. At least it would be better than being a complete stranger. Wouldn't he overlook divine punishment at least once?

-A request?

Fortunately, Father God didn't seem angry.

-Hmm. Danae also asked... and Andromeda also thought well of you. I will grant you a request.

Danae was Perseus's mother. Andromeda was a princess of Ethiopia. Perseus and Andromeda were engaged, but Andromeda eventually offered her body to Zeus. It is a famous story that the Queen of Ethiopia incurred Poseidon's wrath, and the King of Ethiopia saved the country by offering his daughter to Zeus. No matter how powerful Poseidon is, he can't do anything to Zeus.

,There are those who do not believe that I am Father's son! Please give me undeniable proof that I am Father's son!"

-Are you saying you want something useful? Well, fine.

Something appeared in the air and slowly fell. Perseus spread out his hands and respectfully received it.

Perseus's eyes widened as he looked at the stick placed on his palm. The knowledge he had received contained information about this.

,Lightsaber!'

Zeus had already disappeared. He got up and pressed the button on the lightsaber.

Jiiiiiiiiing!

An energy blade soared up, emitting a blue light.

Perseus felt his chest swell as he walked out of the temple.

The people, overwhelmed by the presence of God, could not stand up and remained seated. Perseus walked in front of them and showed off the lightsaber.

,Behold! A divine object bestowed upon me, his son, by Father God, Zeus! As long as I am king, Argos will enjoy infinite glory!"

,Waaaaaaaaaaaaa! Bless King Perseus!!"

A smile spread across Perseus's face. The sound of his royal authority growing stronger pleased him.

With my and Hephaestus's power, humanity, now able to harness electricity, developed rapidly.

At first, it was creaky. It wasn't easy to educate blacksmiths and scholars. But as time passed, humans developed. Because of the help of the gods.

,Humanity has developed in the blink of an eye.'

It was no exaggeration. Because of time acceleration, time passed in units of 10 years. Heroes who had made a name for themselves in mythology died without much activity. Conversely, names that had never been heard of began to shine brilliantly.

,The Greek and Roman mythology I knew is gone.'

Now I was overflowing with power. Because all humans worshipped me.

,Air conditioners, fans, refrigerators, induction cooktops.... All the civilization that humans are enjoying now starts from electricity.'

Humans, who had become too comfortable, sometimes made foolish choices. In the old days, I would have dropped lightning and killed them all, but now I confiscated their electricity for a week. That alone was enough.

Because taking away an already convenient life was no different from sentencing them to death. Humans, who were forced to return to being primitive people because they couldn't use electricity, performed sacrifices with all their heart. They not only offered beautiful women to appease my anger, but also princesses and queens. Having tasted civilization, they didn't care about kings or anything else.

,For the same reason, Hephaestus's status has also increased.'

Civilization is by no means complete with just electricity. Hephaestus passed on his knowledge to human blacksmiths, and that soon became faith and returned to him. Now, Hephaestus has enough power to fight Poseidon 1-on-1 without losing.

,Father!"

Ares shouted and came to me. His handsome face was so distorted that it was good to see.
