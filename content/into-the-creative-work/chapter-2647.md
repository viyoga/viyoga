A colosseum created by a demon's curse.

My body began to get excited. It was similar to the feeling I get when I fight. Conversely, my mind is ice-cold.

,It can't be that only the shape of the space has changed. I saw the change in my body, so it's certain that it has a physical and mental impact.'

Unlike my body, which is being combat-ready, my mind is coldly sinking. This is probably because I'm special. Maybe it's because my Astral is special, but I'm strangely unaffected by mental influences.

,What I'm worried about is Yuria....'

Yuria, who was standing across from me, was unchanged. She looked perfectly fine, both physically and mentally. It seems like she controls her body more perfectly than I do.

,Only one person can escape from here! Only the winner can escape! I swear on my existence!'

The demon shouted. I could feel a strange power in those words. As a result, the power of the space becomes clearer.

,Is it a kind of restriction placed on himself.'

Pajijijik.

Lightning soared from my fingertips. The lightning clumped together and became elongated, taking the form of a spear. I throw the lightning spear at the demon.

[Lightning Spear]

However, the lightning spear shatters as it is blocked by an invisible wall.

,Kill the woman in front of you, not me! I told you the rules! You're a slow learner for a magician!!'

The demon doll sneers.

The surrounding space is wriggling. The curse is gradually becoming stronger.

,Master-nim. It seems that the power of the curse increases if we don't follow the rules here. I think this is probably the demon's aim.'

,I can roughly guess what that demon bastard's identity is.'

Of course, it's not Baal. Baal, also called Bael, is the highest-ranking of the 72 demons. He is also called the Demon King. There was no reason for such an existence to be playing around in a place like this.

,The 63rd-ranked demon among the 72 demons. Andras. A demon who enjoys inciting discord between people. His authority is also specialized in this area.'

The demon doll widened its eyes as if surprised. The capillaries in the demon doll's eyes burst and blood flowed down.

,You figured out my identity right away!! How did you know?!'

The corners of the demon doll's mouth are torn and smiling. He doesn't seem to care even if his identity is revealed.

,I'm glad it's not the main body. Two level 7s wouldn't have been able to face Andras's main body. Well, it doesn't make sense for such a great demon to appear in the middle of Neo London.'

Even at this moment, Andras's power is getting stronger. It's because Yuria and I aren't fighting.

,If Yuria and I fight, Andras's power will weaken. To be exact, his power is consumed while maintaining this space.'

In short, we can buy time by pretending to fight. And when Andras has weakened enough... we finish it. That's how to defeat Andras.

,Should I use my unique skill? No, even if I use my unique skill, I'm not sure I can kill that bastard for sure.'

I looked at Yuria. Yuria, who met my eyes, smiled.

,I will follow any order from Master-nim.'

,...You're saying scary things. What if I order you to die?'

,I will die. And I know that Master-nim would never give such an order.'

,Of course, I have no intention of giving such an absurd order. Even if someone impersonates me and gives such an order, ignore it.'

,Don't worry, there's no way I wouldn't recognize Master-nim.'

It was an unnecessarily embarrassing thing to say.

,Can you overcome this situation with your unique skill?'

I don't know Yuria's unique skill. I think she would tell me if I asked, but I didn't ask on purpose. Unique skills are a sensitive issue.

,...The demon's power is too strong right now. To be exact, I'd have to try it to know for sure, but... as of now, there's a high chance that even my unique skill will fail. If you order me to, I will use my unique skill right now.'

,No. Let's fight each other to reduce that bastard's power first. Think of it as a sparring match. Come to think of it, this is the first time we've sparred together.'

,I will follow Master-nim's will. Shall we start?'

,Let's start.'

I was the one who launched the first attack.

Lightning strikes her from the sky.

[Thunder Bolt]

She was not where the lightning struck. Instead, the ground was scorched and smoke rose.

,She disappeared into the shadows in an instant.'

I jumped into the sky.

[Levitation]

The power of magic floated my body.

Since she disappeared into the shadows, it's obvious that she will appear from the shadows.

,There's only my shadow above this open colosseum arena.'

As soon as I floated into the sky, Yuria appeared from within my shadow. Her mobility is on par with jumping.

Yuria held a dagger in her hand and swung it at me. A jet-black Sword energy, like a shadow, cuts through the air and rushes in.

[Psychokinesis]

I use psychokinesis magic to pull my body floating in the air. The Sword energy narrowly passed by my side.

,It's not threatening. It's not a fatal wound even if I get hit. Did she adjust her strength considering that I strengthen my body with magic.'

[Electric Field]

Electricity begins to flow over the arena. Yuria's shadow wriggles and turns round. The current was blocked by the unusually dark shadow and failed to electrocute Yuria.

I looked down at the ground with ease. Because I am in the air, she cannot quickly close the distance with shadow movement.

Move through the shadows on my body? That would be impossible.

,Perhaps there are conditions attached to shadow manipulation, such as the size or darkness of the shadow. She won't be able to handle the shadows of hair or clothes.'

Manipulate the shadows in my body to attack? There's no need to consider that from the beginning. For that to be possible, the gap between me and her would have to be significant.

[Thunder Bolt]

[Fire Ball]

[Ice Strike]

A one-sided magic bombardment pouring down from the sky.

Yuria minimized the damage from the magic shock by covering her body with shadows.

,She didn't go into the shadows, which is an easy way to avoid it? Are there restrictions or conditions on hiding in the shadows.'

While preparing the magic, I checked the demon's power and the power of the curse surrounding the area. It was definitely weaker than before.

,Since the goal is to weaken the demon's power by dragging out the battle for a long time, I should lower the tempo of the battle.'

I attacked Yuria mainly with magic that consumed little mana. Yuria avoided it without difficulty. I was amazed when I saw her movements. The Sword energy she occasionally throws is so sharp that I feel like I'll be hit right away if I'm careless.

,...Attacks that thoroughly target gaps. Yuria's fighting style is excessively efficient.'

That's why there were unexpected things to learn.

,Hmm. What is it? Why aren't these guys trying to kill each other for real? By now, even parents and children should be killing each other, right?'

The demon doll mutters, shaking its head. I ignored the demon's reaction and focused on the sparring.

It's clear that I have an overwhelming advantage. But if I'm even a little careless, I'll lose. If possible, I didn't want to lose. Especially since the opponent is Yuria. As a master, being defeated by a maid doesn't give me any dignity.

Yuria took out a cloth from the shadows and threw it into the sky. A rectangular cloth with a large area.

The cloth faces away from the sun, creating a shadow inside.

Shadow movement. Yuria appeared from within the shadow of the cloth. She reaches out to me. Shadow chains stretched out from her shadowy grip.

[Psychokinesis]

I moved my body to the side with psychokinesis to avoid the shadow chain.

Now all that's left is for Yuria to fall.

However, Yuria did not fall to the ground. She rotated her body while stepping on the empty air and swung the shadow chain like a whip.

Bang!

The impact caused by the collision with the shadow chain is offset by the barrier. The barrier was broken. The problem is that the shadow chain wrapped around my body. I gave strength to my whole body, but the shadow chain was unshaken.

Yuria pulls the shadow chain. The long chain quickly shortened and my body was pulled.

Leap.

A short teleportation upwards. I escaped from the shadow chain and immediately counterattacked Yuria with a Lightning Spear. Yuria blocks it with a shadow chain.

,Is she standing in the air by creating a foothold with shadows?'

,Yes. Because there are always shadows under my feet. It's a small trick.'

[Light]

I created a light source on the ground. The shadow foothold supporting Yuria disappeared as it received the intense light. Yuria's body falls to the ground. She landed lightly without panicking.

,It's a situation where I have no choice but to lose. You are excellent.'

,It was an overly good environment for me. There are no proper shadows here.'

,Even so, I have no way to close the distance with Master-nim. Now that the previous move has been blocked, Master-nim will be wary of even the smallest things. I lost.'

The situation would have been different if we had engaged in close combat. Knowing that, I was the first to create distance. Furthermore, the fact that the arena was open and it was difficult for shadows to form worked in my favor.

,If it were a real battle... I wouldn't have fought. There would be no reason to fight in a deliberately disadvantageous place.'

All that's left is for me to unilaterally bombard her with magic from the sky.

Avoid magic using shadows? If I use level 1 magic, Light, I can affect shadows.

In fact, this battle, no, this sparring match, has ended in my victory.

,Master-nim. May I use my unique skill one last time? I would like to show Master-nim my unique skill.'

,A battle using unique skills. It's an experience you can't often have. Let's do it.'

I went down to the ground.

Yuria looked around once and looked at her shadow.

And her shadow covered the world.

,Unique skill Dark Horizon, I call it.'

Yuria's voice was heard in the pitch-black darkness.

[Electric Detection.]

Magic-mixed electromagnetic waves spread out and confirmed Yuria's location. Just because I can't see her because of the darkness doesn't mean her existence itself has disappeared.

,Indeed... I'm trapped in the shadows. It feels like guns are pointed at me from all directions.'

[Lightning Spear]

Pajijijik.

The sound of electricity crackling rang out. But there was no lightning. I'm clearly gripping a lightning spear, but I can't see it with my eyes because there's no light.

,If it's an attack using shadows, I can just block it with a barrier. Your unique skill isn't just this, is it?'

,Yes. I can enforce laws within this dark space. For example, I can make it impossible to use magic.'

The Lightning Spear I was holding disappears. The same goes for the barrier protecting my body.

I hurriedly used magic again. There was no problem moving mana and weaving formulas... but the magic did not manifest.

,If you adjust the laws in detail, you can manipulate cause and effect or time, but it's inefficient for a strong person like Master-nim. Ah, the number of laws that can be enforced is limited to one. For reference, I am also affected by the enforced laws.'

,Is it okay to tell me everything like that? Do you perhaps have such restrictions?'

,I want Master-nim to know everything about me. I believe that will definitely happen someday.'

I could feel Yuria smiling even though I couldn't see her.

,A law that you can't use magic rather than a law that you can't handle mana... Ah. That won't work. If there's no power source called mana, the unique skill itself will be undone.'

,Yes. My unique skill is a double-edged sword. If I use it even a little wrong, it will rather cause harm to me.'

,But if the opponent is a magician like me, it's a unique skill that kills with one blow. If you take away magic from a magician, there's nothing left. I saw something good, so shall I show you too? Unique skill Absolute Star.'

The unique skill was activated with the image of the center of the universe, an eternally immortal star shining.

Mana gathers around me and sparkles like starlight. Originally, it should be starlight that should not be visible due to Yuria's Dark Horizon.

,My unique skill, Absolute Star, grants me immunity to debuffs. And it draws in and handles the power around me, including mana. I found out this time, but it seems that the mana around me is also affected by Absolute Star. Otherwise, there's no way the light would be so clearly visible.'

It was a newly discovered fact. After all, it's important to experience everything.

[Lightning Spear]

Lightning flashed around my grip and a lightning spear was created. Not only mana, but also the magic I cast is affected by Absolute Star.

The lightning illuminated not only me but also Yuria. She was touching her reddened cheeks and wearing a strange smile.

,Ah.... You are beautiful. You are indeed Master-nim. There's no way I can beat Master-nim no matter what I do.'

,The unique skill compatibility is completely in my favor... it's not something to be happy about.'

,My unique skill and Master-nim's unique skill can create perfect synergy when facing a third party. To have such compatibility even though it wasn't intentional. Would you call this a match made in heaven?'

Is that so?

Sometimes Yuria would say strange things. Like Elena is pitiful. When I asked who Elena was, she answered that she was a friend, with a strangely smug smile.

Even a perfect maid has humanity. I decided to just let it go.

,Then shall we start now?'

,Yes. Let's start. The demon's authority will not be able to be used within this space.'

The law changes.

In an instant, the colosseum collapsed and appeared in front of the mansion.

,Is it because of the demon's level? Even with Yuria's unique skill, she couldn't completely eliminate the demon's authority.'

The demon's power has weakened enough.

I threw a Lightning Spear at the demon doll.
