,Chikusho!!"

The Japanese Prime Minister shouted. The Japanese ministers gathered in the Prime Minister's office shrank back at the Prime Minister's anger.

Prime Minister.

The pinnacle of power in present-day Japan, no different from a king. If he put his mind to it, replacing the ministers here would be a piece of cake.

,Seong Su-un! You crazy bastard!!"

Hiding in the safest bunker in Tokyo in preparation for the worst-case scenario, the Prime Minister was able to see the situation outside through a monitor.

Currently, Tokyo was not in the worst-case scenario, but it was close.

Seong Su-un and the vampire unit, who willingly helped to kill Daechun Group Chairman Seong Yujin. The Prime Minister believed they would win. No matter how great Seong Yujin-ssi was, he wouldn't be able to come to Tokyo alone and fight and defeat hundreds of vampire Shinzo soldiers.

However, the Prime Minister's thoughts were wrong.

Seong Su-un pulled off an absurd troll move.

,To think he would eat over 200 vampire Shinzo!'

Having secretly experimented on vampires, he knew how amazing Seong Su-un was for eating over 200 vampires.

,But he still couldn't beat Seong Yujin!'

It wasn't just that he couldn't win. Despite having borrowed the place, he couldn't even put up a proper fight and went berserk.

Now he could see. Seong Su-un had been pushed to his limit even before the operation began.

Seong Yujin disappeared without dealing with the rampaging Seong Su-un. He abandoned him in Japan like a dog.

Seong Su-un. No, the lump of meat monster that was no longer worthy of being called Seong Su-un was swallowing the NEW Tokyo Tower and growing in size. At this rate, Tokyo was about to be destroyed by a monster.

,Prime Minister-kakha... The Japanese people are agitated. The monster's appearance is spreading through social media. Voices criticizing Prime Minister-kakha are growing louder, and there's talk of having to do a dogeza to Seong Yujin-ssi."

,Dogeza?! What did we do wrong to deserve a dogeza! The Self-Defense Forces can handle the monster! Send the Self-Defense Forces! We can raise our approval ratings by bashing Korea!"

The Korea-bashing strategy.

A time-honored political strategy used by successive Japanese Prime Ministers. The effect was outstanding.

The ministers' faces hardened.

,Have you forgotten? There's Seong Yujin-ssi in Korea."

,What does that have to do with it... You son of a bitch!"

The Prime Minister inadvertently blurted out a Korean curse word.

Currently, a 'Supreme Seong Yujin' pandemic is sweeping the world. A disease where your head explodes if you antagonize Seong Yujin-ssi. A disease that 99% of the Japanese population is infected with.

Korea is already Seong Yujin's country. If they incite the Japanese people to bash Korea, they will develop animosity towards Seong Yujin-ssi, and their heads will explode and they will die from the Supreme Seong Yujin disease.

,Let's deal with that damn monster first. Send the Self-Defense Forces."

,Fighting in downtown Tokyo is..."

,Is there any other way?! The sacrifice of the few for the sake of the many is inevitable! Issue an evacuation order and deal with the monster as quickly as possible!"

,Hai!"

The Self-Defense Forces for the sake of the Japanese Self-Defense Forces were dispatched to downtown Tokyo. Tanks raced down the streets and fighter jets streaked across the Tokyo sky.

Missiles and shells flew to kill the monster that had swallowed the NEW Tokyo Tower.

Kwaaaang! Kwang!

Explosions erupted on the monster's body. The lump of meat that had been one mass broke into small pieces and scattered in all directions. That was the beginning of the disaster.

The small pieces of meat used superpowers to cling to the Self-Defense Forces soldiers. Even if the Self-Defense Forces soldiers fired their rifles, most of the bullets were useless against the superpowers. The small pieces of meat burrowed into the Self-Defense Forces soldiers like parasites.

It was an infection. The infected Self-Defense Forces soldiers became lumps of meat that were neither vampires nor anything else. They roamed Tokyo, attacking humans. Then, at some point, they walked to the main body in the Tokyo Tower and were absorbed.

As time passed, the monster grew larger and larger, and Tokyo fell further and further into the abyss.

,This can't be happening!!"

The Prime Minister shouted with a flushed face.

A week after the monster appeared. Tokyo was on the verge of being captured by the monster. The Self-Defense Forces, which had been dispatched to kill the monster, were instead annihilated. There was no hope or anything left in Tokyo. The Japanese people tried to escape from the archipelago whenever they had the chance. Among them, many Japanese people fled to nearby Korea.

The Prime Minister glared at the bowing ministers with cold eyes and raged.

,Don't just stand there with your mouths shut! Come up with a plan! Give me a plan!"

.......

There was a plan.

To annihilate the monster without a trace with overwhelming firepower.

In other words, a nuclear bomb. Dropping a nuclear bomb on Tokyo. But Japan was sensitive to nuclear bombs. And officially, it did not possess nuclear bombs. The moment they suggested using a nuclear bomb here, they would have to take responsibility.

The Prime Minister knew this, so he was pressuring the ministers. He wanted a kamikaze to take responsibility for the aftermath of the nuclear bomb. A kamikaze full of resentment instead of honor, to die with that monster!

.......

Silence.

.......

Silence.

.......

More silence.

The heavy silence lasted for three hours. Eventually, the exhausted Prime Minister directly designated a sacrificial offering.

,Minister of Defense."

The Minister of Defense, who could be called the Minister of National Defense in Korea.

,...Yes. Prime Minister-kakha."

The Minister of Defense's voice trembled slightly.

,What is the meaning of the Self-Defense Forces?"

,...It is a self-defending unit."

,That's right. It's a self-defending unit! But right now, the Self-Defense Forces have failed to defend themselves! That damn lump of meat monster has raped Tokyo and is trying to rape all of Japan! Minister of Defense! What is your mission?!"

The Minister of Defense tightly closed his eyes. From the moment the Prime Minister pointed him out, his fate was sealed.

,To defend Japan...!"

,Minister of Defense. I ask you as Prime Minister. What must we do to deal with that monster?"

The Prime Minister's fierce momentum subsided. He asked in a soft tone. He even used the hao-che style of speech.

The Minister of Defense barely opened his reluctant lips.

,A nuclear bomb. We must annihilate the monster with a nuclear bomb."

,Are you aware that the monster is in Tokyo?"

,If we leave it as it is, Tokyo will be destroyed by the monster. And the monster will go to other cities. We must abandon Tokyo for the future of Japan."

,If the Minister of Defense says so, there's nothing we can do.... But do you have a nuclear bomb?"

,...I personally have a nuclear bomb. Please allow me to use it. I will take all responsibility."

He personally had a nuclear bomb. It was an absurd statement. He wasn't Seong Yujin-ssi, so how could an individual possess a nuclear bomb? But no one pointed it out.

A smile spread across the Prime Minister's face.

,I respect the Minister of Defense's resolve."

That day, a nuclear bomb exploded in the center of Tokyo.

A mushroom cloud billowed over Tokyo. The Minister of Defense held a press conference and announced that he was solely responsible for everything. He also committed suicide by poisoning himself with poison at midnight that day.

However, shockingly, they failed to achieve their goal. The monster was alive in the devastated ruins of the city. Even though its size was small, it was alive without being annihilated.

,It didn't die even with a nuclear bomb! This is impossible!"

The Prime Minister shouted. He received a report that the lump of meat was spreading throughout Japan. His mentality was shaken. There was no way to respond. Using another nuclear bomb? It wasn't a viable option unless they were going to burn down all of Japan.

,Damn it, damn it, damn it! At this rate... at this rate, Japan will be destroyed!!"

,Prime Minister-kakha. Please calm down. There's still time!"

,Shut up! Tell me a plan instead of spouting such nonsense!"

There were damn epidemics and monsters roaming outside. In the Prime Minister's eyes, Japan was already doomed. His approval rating had already fallen to single digits. However, due to the situation, he was not being ousted. The position of Japanese Prime Minister was a throne that was rotting and burning.

,Aaaaaaaah!"

The Prime Minister went mad. Papers and fountain pens flew through the air.

After a long fit of madness, a light bulb flashed in the Prime Minister's head.

,...Japan is doomed. That's certain. But Japan shouldn't be the only one to perish. In the first place, the vampire unit was supported by the whole world. The whole world should take responsibility."

Madness shattered the Prime Minister's common sense.

,Prime Minister...?"

,Order the Self-Defense Forces to spread pieces of the monster's lump of meat all over the world. A combo of epidemics and monsters. The whole world should taste it."

,That's impossible! No matter how secretly we proceed, we'll inevitably be caught! The whole world will criticize and antagonize Japan!"

,So are you saying we should just die alone?!? The United States has abandoned Japan! No, it's not just the United States! The whole world has abandoned Japan!"

,Prime Minister! Please calm down!"

,I won't die. Screw the epidemics and monsters!!"

The Prime Minister, screaming, ordered his secretary to bring something. The secretary brought a steel briefcase. When he opened the briefcase, a syringe containing vampire serum appeared. The Prime Minister used the syringe on himself without hesitation.

The Prime Minister's fangs elongated. His atmosphere turned cold.

,There's plenty of vampire serum. Use it too."

,Prime Minister! Are you crazy?!"

,The world is doomed. It will be doomed. To survive, you must become stronger, and becoming a vampire is the best option. You choose too. Whether to become a vampire and rule... or to become a human and be ruled."

The ministers were overwhelmed by the Prime Minister. The Prime Minister's eyes were those of a predator. A vampire who eats humans. If they defied him here, they would surely be eaten.

,...The Prime Minister is right. Japan is doomed. But if we become vampires... we can create a new Japan! I will follow the Prime Minister's will!"

The ministers all injected the serum into their bodies. They all became vampires.

Their atmosphere changed.

,Hmm. Good. I can feel the power."

,So this is the body of a vampire.... My old body is revitalized. This is... I wasn't even this good in my 20s."

,Haha. I should have become a vampire sooner."

The Prime Minister grabbed a fountain pen and threw it at the ceiling. Kwajik! The fluorescent light shattered and the lights went out. Darkness fell on the conference room.

,Let's begin the meeting for the new Japan."

,As the Prime Minister said earlier, we must spread the lump of meat all over the world."

,Before that, we all need to become Shinzo first. Let's make humans into vampires and eat them. Of course, the Prime Minister should become Shinzo first and lead us."

,There are too many humans. Will those many humans follow our vampires' instructions? The number of humans must be reduced."

,Let's use the Supreme Seong Yujin disease. Let's incite and fabricate to make humans antagonize Seong Yujin-ssi. Then their heads will explode and they'll die on their own. We can manage the surviving humans."

The plan for the new Japan's world domination began.
