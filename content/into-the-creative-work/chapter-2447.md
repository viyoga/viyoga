The ground where the Thunder Bolt struck was caved in, and clouds of dust billowed up. The two 5th-grade magic spells created synergy with each other. To be precise, Ice Fog further amplified the power of Thunder Bolt.

I glared at the dust and grit my teeth. I could feel that bastard's presence. That attack wasn't enough to kill Rahool.

,Rather than defending, the barrier he already had around him protected him.'

I glanced at the Vajra Warrior. The Vajra Warrior's body is unstable. That's because I pushed some of the burden from using magic onto the Vajra Warrior. Of course, I'm not in perfect condition either.

,If I use magic in quick succession without resting now, my mana road will definitely break down.'

When using magic with such a risk, can I definitely take care of Rahool? I can't be sure.

,I'm not cornered enough to take such a dangerous gamble. What I need to do now is hold out.'

Aisa-ssi is performing the ritual behind me. If the God of Magic doesn't do anything when the ritual is complete, we're screwed, but for now, the God of Magic has the highest chance of winning.

,If the God of Magic doesn't help... I'll have to spend my entire fortune.'

The thick dust thins out, and Rahool's silhouette is visible inside.

,Know the mercy of the heavens.'

His voice echoed.

Light pours down from the eye of the sun floating in the sky. Sunlight that burns everything to death.

Before the light reached me, the Vajra Warrior stepped forward and lifted me up, avoiding the light.

,It's not real sunlight. If it were, I wouldn't have been able to avoid it and would have been hit.'

The wasteland touched by the condensed light bursts into flames. The ground slowly disappeared. Not even turning to ashes, but complete annihilation.

The light chases me. Fortunately, its speed isn't fast, so the Vajra Warrior carrying me easily avoids it.

,What the hell is that light? It's neither magic nor sorcery.'

,If it's neither magic nor sorcery, there's only one thing it can be, right? It's the power of Akar.'

,Wasn't Akar the god of life and mutation? I didn't think he was a god who used such power.'

,And the god of mutation. Akar is a god who can do everything. That light won't stop until you die.'

Ttududuk.

Along with the sound of something being torn apart, the rocks embedded in the wasteland floated into the air. And they fly like rocks shot from a catapult. The target wasn't me, but Aisa-ssi, who was performing the ritual.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 10]

I quickly weave a formula and use magic.

[Stone Wall]

A stone wall rises from the ground, blocking the rocks that were shot.

Kwakwang! Kwang!

The Stone Wall eventually collapsed, but it blocked the rocks that were shot.

Ttududuk.

Again, rocks rise into the air from the wasteland. This time, it's targeting Aisa-ssi, not me.

[Stone Wall]

I raise a stone wall like before to block it.

I felt that his attacks were clumsy. I wondered if he was trying to conserve his strength and draw out my carelessness, but he was already exhausted.

,Even if that bastard is hiding a trump card, it doesn't seem like he intends to use it right now.'

A war of attrition continues.

He attacks, and I block. The sunlight that burns everything and annihilates it is still chasing after me.

,This isn't good.'

Stone Wall. It's a magic spell with good mana efficiency even among 3rd-grade magic spells, but right now, just using magic is putting a considerable burden on me. Moreover, I'm even operating the Vajra Warrior.

I have enough mana to cast Thunder Bolt two or three times. But my mana road will break down before that.

,I can't use Phase Shift either. The disadvantage of Phase Shift is that the magic being maintained is canceled. The Vajra Warrior who is carrying me and running will disappear.'

I have to run on my own two feet to avoid the light and protect Aisa-ssi with magic. The situation is getting worse, not better.

Tingle. Cold sweat flows. The overloaded mana road is sending a signal of pain, saying it's at its limit.

,I can still do it about once more...!'

[Stone Wall]

The ground rumbles and then stops.

The magic failed.

The rocks targeted Aisa-ssi without obstruction.

[Phase Shift]

I switch to the original mana road. The Vajra Warrior disappears, and the Vajra Club floats in the air. I tightly grip the Vajra Club. Pajijik. Naturally, electricity sparks.

,Vajra Lightning...!'

With all my might, I threw the Vajra Lightning towards the rock aiming for Aisa-ssi. The Vajra Lightning becomes lightning and flies, piercing the rock and shattering it.

There's no time to stand still. I have to avoid the sunlight falling from the sky.

Cheolkeodeok.

The shackles on my ankles prevent my movement. The shackles made of rock were connected to the ground.

,I knew you'd reveal an opening if I targeted Aisa-ssi.'

,Rahool...!'

,Having something to protect is annoying. Even if you can't protect it, nothing changes much.'

I called for the Vajra Lightning. I grabbed the Vajra Lightning that quickly returned to my hand and threw it at the shackles. Of course, the Vajra Lightning shattered the shackles. But in that gap, new shackles appeared and grabbed my legs.

,Damn it. In this case, with proper magic...!'

I use magic, enduring the damage to the mana road.

,...?!'

The movement of the mana road is smooth. It feels like something that was blocked has been released. The sunlight that was approaching me also disappeared.

The world began to darken. I briefly looked up at the sky, and the sun disappeared, and the night sky took its place. A night sky full of countless shining stars. I felt a certain presence there. A presence so great that, paradoxically, it didn't feel great. That presence is definitely looking at me.

,Did Aisa-ssi succeed in the ritual...?'

I swallowed my breath.

Mana is abundant in this space. Elements. I can feel magic.

There's no need to shatter the shackles of earth holding my legs.

I grip the Vajra Lightning and use magic. The formula was completed at a speed that couldn't be compared to usual.

,This isn't chanting magic with a formula, but rather, it feels like the formula is following after I chant the magic.'

A magic circle is drawn right in front of me. I briefly smeared blood on the blade of the Vajra Club and then placed the Vajra Club in the magic circle.

[Railgun]

The Vajra Lightning had broken through the speed of sound and reached Rahool's front.

The Vajra Lightning was struggling with the translucent earth barrier protecting Rahool. Originally, it should have lost its power and fallen, but the Vajra Lightning isn't just a bullet for the Railgun. There was also additional magic cast on the Vajra Lightning.

Pajijijijijijijik!

The Vajra Lightning emitted lightning to break through the barrier. Rahool frowned and took out a necklace from his chest.

A necklace very similar to the one Aisa-ssi had. It's a necklace of disaster.

,...To make me take out my teacher's item. I don't like it.'

Pasak!

One of the three stones breaks.

The power of the lightning emitted from the Vajra Lightning decreased. It could be seen that the power of the Vajra Lightning was rapidly decreasing.

At this point, I activated the magic I had prepared in advance.

[Blood Lightning]

Crimson lightning burst out. It was a much more powerful output than I had expected. Dozens of streaks of red lightning struck the barrier.

Pasasak!

The necklace of disaster he was holding was completely shattered into dust. The barrier and Blood Lightning disappeared at the same time, and the powerless Vajra Lightning fell to the ground.

,Finally, the thick shell has been peeled off.'

Whoa.

Sparks of lightning crackled from my exhaled breath. All the conditions were met.

,You bastard...!'

Rahool glares at me, then looks up at the night sky in surprise.

[Thunder Break]

A huge lightning bolt plunged down towards Rahool. A shockwave occurs as if a large bomb had exploded.

One more shot.

[Thunder Break]

Kwaaaaaaaang!

By the time the belated thunder began to play catch-up, the last shot had fallen.

[Thunder Break]

Pajik, pajijik....

In the place where the lightning struck, the lightning did not disappear and remained, burning the ground. I chuckled as I looked at Rahool kneeling. Rahool was alive, his whole body blackened.

,Thunder Break has the power to erase a small hill. You took three shots of that in a row and you're still alive? You have an absurd amount of toughness. Did you become the rock itself after using rock sorcery?'

One more shot.

I could do it now. It was the moment I was about to use magic again. Rahool's body cracked open, and a large eyeball popped out. The eyeball stares at me. And the world changed.

The ground pulsates and writhes as if it were alive. Flesh trees planted here and there, eyeballs embedded in the sky. Entrails crawl around, and blood gushes out. The nauseating stench is a bonus.

It was a space where even breathing was disgusting.

,...Hallucination? No. The moment I made eye contact with it, I was forcibly transferred. Is a god a god after all? It's done something absurd.'

Gulp, gulp.

A brain over 30m in diameter.

,...What are you trying to do.'

-You are bait. If that disgusting thing cherishes you... it will definitely come looking for you, even if it has to overdo it.

Kkulmeolkkulmeol.

The tentacles wriggle. It was as if they were admiring.

-Even though you are a lowly being, you don't go crazy even after seeing me. As expected of the human chosen by that thing. Won't you serve me?

,...What's the price? I'm curious what a god can give me.'

-Evolution. You will evolve in the direction you desire. If you want, you can even become a god.

I held back the laughter that was about to burst out. Becoming a god with the power of a god? Even if I somehow became a god, I would definitely be under this bastard.

,I refuse. I don't want to become a disgusting god like you.'

-Insolent thing.

It's slow.

I can respond.

Open the Astral and the mana road....

Not even the mana inside me.

Not even the mana that should be in the atmosphere.

It's as if the common sense of mana doesn't exist in this world.

A translucent barrier wrapped around my body, blocking the tentacles.

Magic. But it's not magic I used.

I can feel Akar's hatred.

I turned around. At that moment, my consciousness is forcibly pulled. I tried to refuse, but since I didn't feel any malice, I complied.

It was a half-destroyed palace. Shining stars can be seen through the gaps in the broken palace.

,The night sky.... No. Is this space?'

I looked straight ahead. I could see a woman sitting on the throne.

Blonde hair and golden eyes.

Her hair and eyes seemed to shine on their own.

She wrapped her beautiful and voluptuous body in a white cloth. Clothes that seemed to slip off with the slightest movement. It was similar to the clothing of ancient Greece.

Her neck, ears, wrists, fingers, and ankles were adorned with extravagant jewelry. It was clearly excessively adorned, but it suited her as if it were natural.

,What's the price?'

,...Didn't I say I would save you.'

,According to my calculations, it's not a good deal. And it doesn't seem like I'm the one in a hurry.'

Actually, I'm the one in a hurry.

Because if Meltes hadn't helped me, I would have died in all likelihood.

But I couldn't back down here. Meltes saving me and me saving Meltes. The difference between them would definitely be impossible to compare.

,...All magic.'

,That's a price I like. What do I have to do to save you?'

,There is no way for now. But someday, an opportunity will arise.'

,I don't like vague words.... Why are you so sure? Are you seeing the future or something?'

,I cannot see your future. That is why I can know.'

,One more thing to ask. Why did you give me a blessing?'

,I want to know.'

After finishing her answer, she took off the blue ring she was wearing on her left index finger and threw it to me. I was surprised by the sudden throw, but I managed to catch it.

,What's this?'

,You will know if you wear it.'

With her words, I returned to Akar's world again. Akar was still in front of me.

-Meltes...! The presence has disappeared.... Did you abandon your apostle and run away...!

I feel like I had a conversation with Meltes for about 2-3 minutes, but the actual time that passed was less than 10 seconds. The god in front of me didn't even notice that.

,Even among gods, there's a difference in class, huh.'

The ability to store three magic spells.

It was possible to store any magic without conditions and activate it without conditions. What's amazing is that it can even steal magic if the conditions are met.

,I received an absurd down payment.'

I also naturally came to know the three magic spells stored in the ring. I activate one of them. The ring flashed blue.

-...Are you using magic now? You dare, in my domain?

,The magic stored in this ring I received from Meltes can be activated without any conditions. Even if this place is a world without mana, where the existence of magic is not allowed.'

-Unpleasant.

The sky brightens.

Akar's consciousness and my gaze turned to the sky.

Bursting all the eyeballs embedded in the sky, very exhilaratingly.

[Meteor Strike]

-Meltes!! You son of a bitch...!!

[Dimension Warp]

=Meltes!!!
