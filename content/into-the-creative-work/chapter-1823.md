"Hoo."

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

Pajijik!

I block the yin energy trying to cling to my body with the electromagnetic barrier. The yin energy was bad news. It not only tried to steal my body temperature but also tried to penetrate my body.

'It's not ordinary yin energy. It's yin energy mixed with ghost energy.'

If my body is overtaken by yin energy, useless japgwi will target my body and try to enter. Even if I have Complete Recovery and Heavenly Mind, I can't give my body to japgwi even for a second.

'I feel like I've been wandering around for 2 hours... where is the Cheonma Shingong?'

I take out the map Baekran drew again.

The crude map depicted a withered large tree, flowing water, and something that looked like a wild dog.

'What I need to find is the flowing water between the large tree and the wild dog. She said to walk in the direction the water flows....'

I couldn't find flowing water, let alone a tree or a wild dog.

'Fucking mist.'

This thick mist was truly something else; even using the Heavenly Eye (天眼), I couldn't fully see through it. At most, it only extended my visible range.

'This must be why the Cheonma Mausoleum isn't known to the outside world and remains hidden.'

Baekran didn't tell me what to do about this mist. I don't know if she trusts me or is trying to screw me over. Personally, I hope it's the former.

'How did Cheonma or Cheon Yu-un get through this mist?'

There must be some method. A secret path passed down only to Cheonma, or a device with special power.

I grumbled inwardly while continuing to walk. Frustrated, I swung my hand in the air. A strong wind arose, pushing the mist away. It was only for a moment. The mist rushed back in, filling the surroundings again. Even using lightning to burn the mist was useless. No matter what I did, this mist wouldn't disappear.

Keong!

Suddenly, a dog bark was heard. My face lit up. This bark must be from the wild dog drawn on the map.

'Right diagonal. It came from the 1 o'clock direction.'

My senses aren't helping. These mists are interfering with my senses.

Keureureureureung.

I felt the presence of the wild dog. It was definitely within 5m, but I couldn't see it well because of the mist.

'If I can only feel its presence, then whatever.'

'It's a dog living in this kind of mist. It probably isn't an ordinary dog.'

I pinpointed the location on the map based on the dog's position. Now, I just need to find the flowing water nearby.

'The flowing water is between the withered tree and the wild dog... but I don't know the location of the withered tree.'

I couldn't tell which direction to go to find the tree. I had to move to find the tree's location. But if I moved aimlessly, I would get lost again.

'Let's pick a direction and walk in a straight line. If I'm lucky, I might find the flowing water.'

I drew the Hwaryeon Bido from my waist. I stuck the tip of the Hwaryeon Bido into the ground. Then, I dragged the knife and walked straight ahead. A line was drawn on the ground.

'Even if I go the wrong way, I can see this line and come back.'

Dragging a knife on the ground was no different from throwing it away, but the Hwaryeon Bido didn't damage its blade even when cutting through rocks. Even when it was slightly cracked when fighting the dragon, the blade hasn't been damaged since.

Keureureureureung.

'I didn't hear the dog bark behind me. Was the bark a fake?'

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

Teolseok.

'It's a monster.'

Keong! Keoeeong! Keongkeongkeong!

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Bireosin (Flying Thunder God).

If I avoided the attack, it was only natural to counterattack immediately. I held the Hwaryeon Bido in reverse grip and slashed the dog's head.

Keongkeongkeongkeongkeong!

Seogeok!

Keongkeongkeongkeong!

15 hours after entering the Cheonma Mausoleum.

I finally found the withered tree. As Baekran said, it was a tree with a height 10 times that of an adult male.

'The dog barks disappeared.'

The ghost dogs that had been constantly tormenting me vanished. The reason was probably the tree in front of me.

'It's eerie. I think this is the source of the yin energy?'

'What's important isn't the identity of this tree.'

I knew the tree's location. That was what mattered. I turned around. There was a line drawn on the ground from coming here.

'The flowing water is supposed to be around the center of this line... I didn't see it while coming here. I think I'll have to suffer again.'

Pajijik.

I felt something coming in through the electromagnetic barrier.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

Following that, other branches stretched out, targeting me.

I lightly kicked off the ground and avoided the branches. The branches looked like whips, but in reality, they were awls. The ends were very sharp, and they moved erratically, but at the decisive moment of attack, they always aimed to stab me.

'It can't be that they're only stabbing for no reason. Did they put poison on the ends of the branches?'

I suppressed the curiosity that suddenly arose and buried it underground.

'It attacked me, so I have to kill it.'

Dozens of branches stabbed towards me at high speed. It might be a ghost-possessed tree. If I used Bireosin, I could avoid them without difficulty.

'Avoiding them isn't enough.'

[Using Time Acceleration. Remaining stacks: 6]

The branches slowed down. I ran forward and swung the Hwaryeon Bido. Red lightning wriggled on the blade.

With three slashes, I cut down dozens of branches and approached the tree trunk, stabbing the Hwaryeon Bido into it.

[Time Acceleration has ended.]

'Lightning.'

Pajijijijijijijijijijik!

The tree's squirming intensified.

'It's withstanding this? Then I just need to increase the output.'

'I thought flowing water meant something like a stream.'

'This is amazing water. Can I use it as an elixir?'

'Let's save this water for later and go to where the water flows.'
