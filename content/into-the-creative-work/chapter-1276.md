I got out of the car and walked down the street.

Sophia's voice came through the wireless earphones in my ears.

-You see the house with the penguin sculpture at the entrance, right?

"Yes, I see it. You must like penguins."

-...My daughter liked them. Just go into that house. Is anyone there?

Sophia is watching my location via GPS from the car. It was dangerous for her, a regular person, to move around directly, so I'm moving alone.

I stopped walking and spread my senses. I can feel the presence of 4 people hiding inside the house and 6 people hiding outside. The enemies already know I'm here.

"There are 10 people hiding nearby. They're all watching me. About 2 of them seem to be B-class, and the rest seem to be around C-class. Should I take them down?"

-...Please avoid combat as much as possible.

"I'll try. Where's the data?"

-It's in the doghouse in the yard. There's a small drawer on the lower right side of the roof. Get the USB inside. The drawer won't be easy to see at first glance. Feel around with your palm to find it.

"There's a reason those bastards haven't found it yet. Who would've thought it was hidden in the doghouse."

-Haha. I'll take that as a compliment.

I entered Sophia's house through the front gate.

At the same time, bullets fly at me.

Noecheonryu Electromagnetic Barrier.

The bullets couldn't penetrate the electromagnetic barrier and fell to the floor.

'They shot without hesitation. Those Boshi bastards. Did they decide to go all out?'

You can't kill me with mere guns.

I approached the doghouse while maintaining my spread senses. The doghouse looked quite fancy. But it's completely empty. There's no dog.

"I don't see a pet dog. Did those bastards kill it?"

-...Rex was taken by my ex-husband.

"Ah, I see."

Sophia's voice turned gloomy. I felt like I asked something unnecessary. It seems she didn't have a good breakup with her ex-husband.

I squatted in front of the doghouse. I ran my hand along the lower right side of the roof. There was something caught on my finger.

I pulled it. The drawer opened, and the USB popped out.

"I found it."

-They couldn't find it after all. That's a relief.

"But I think I'll have to fight."

Step, step.

Two men walked towards me from my right and left. Two B-class ability users. The other 8 are hiding and waiting.

"Hey. Hand that over here."

"I promise. If you hand over the USB, we'll let you go without doing anything."

They each held a knife and an axe. Sword energy swirled around their weapons. I put the USB in my pants pocket and summoned Hwaryeon Bido.

"I'm warning you too. Back off right now. Then I'll spare your lives."

The hairy man holding the axe chuckled.

"You seem to be ignoring us because we're B-class... Do you know how many A-class ability users have died by our hands? More than 7."

"You'll die today, so it'll be 8 soon."

The skinny guy holding the knife spat out a chilling voice and charged. I raised Hwaryeon Bido, imbued with sword energy, to block the skinny guy's attack.

As if he had been waiting, the hairy man swung his axe, aiming for my neck.

I tilted my head back to dodge. The attacks of the hairy man and the skinny guy were simple.

'These guys are just distractions. The real one is...'

A bullet flies. Pajik! The electromagnetic barrier flashed and blocked the bullet. The fallen bullet rolled on the ground.

'It's the work of the 8 hiding. Judging by the fact that there was no gunshot, they must have used magic or an artifact. And...'

The bullet that fell on the ground seemed to be made of a metal other than copper. It must be a special bullet that works even on A-class Hunters.

Huuuung.

The axe and knife came at me simultaneously.

Simple and slow.

That's what I thought at that moment. The axe and knife suddenly distorted and split into dozens.

[Using an Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

I used Instant in a panic.

I reflexively analyzed their arms and postures. From my perspective, their postures were clumsy. They hadn't professionally learned martial arts.

'This isn't a technique, it's an ability. The axe and knife distorted because of... the hairy man's ability, and... the knife and axe split because of the skinny guy's ability?'

Even if they distorted and split, there was something that didn't change. That was the reach. Their attack range didn't increase.

I lightly stepped back. It became an incredibly fast backstep due to the effect of Instant, though.

"What."

"Does he also have a speed-type ability?"

I lowered my upper body and knees and brought Hwaryeon Bido to my side. Then, I imbued it with lightning energy and swung it at the enemies.

Blue sword energy split their bodies. The hairy man was separated into his upper and lower body as is, but the skinny guy was lucky enough to only have blood flowing from his side.

"Fire at once!!"

The skinny guy shouted.

Bullets poured in from all directions. I didn't react separately. There was no need to react. The electromagnetic barrier surrounding my body completely neutralized the pouring bullets.

"Damn it! I didn't hear that he's a guy who's completely immune to bullets!!"

The skinny guy swung his knife at me as if screaming. The blade splits into 3. Instead of rashly trying to block it, I stepped away, using footwork. Then, I stabbed Hwaryeon Bido into the bastard's weakness.

"Keueok!"

The skinny guy was stabbed in the chest without even being able to block it, let alone dodge.

"You can't even block such a normal attack? Were only your physical abilities and mana B-class? Your combat skills are worse than C-class. Right... You guys weren't Hunters."

If they were Hunters, they wouldn't have been caught so carelessly.

They probably didn't want to become Hunters even though they became Hunters. To become a Hunter, you must not have a criminal record. These guys were criminals even before they awakened their abilities.

'Well, they might have been guys who didn't want to do Hunter work.'

After confirming that the skinny guy had stopped breathing, I looked around. I could feel through my senses that the guys who were hiding and shooting at me were running away in a hurry.

I repeated Sophia's words in my head to not kill people as much as possible.

I reverse-summoned Hwaryeon Bido and went to Sophia.

•••

Back in the studio, Sophia sat in front of the computer and kept pounding on the keyboard. Sitting on the bed and watching her work, I quietly closed my eyes.

Kwaang!

I opened my eyes at the loud noise. I wiped the drool from the corner of my mouth with the back of my hand and turned my gaze to the source of the sound. Sophia, who had gotten up from her chair, was staring at the computer like crazy.

I looked at my smartphone. It was 11 AM.

"...Sophia. I can't say good morning. Did you work all night, perhaps?"

"...No. I slept in between. You just woke up late. Did you sleep well?"

"Yes. I slept soundly. Sophia-ssi, you don't seem to be in a good mood. Did something go wrong?"

"Jack Taylor was captured by Boshi. Because of that, Bluecade's activities have stopped."

I got out of bed and scratched my head.

"Bluecade's activities stopped just because one person was captured?"

"Because Jack Taylor was the center of Bluecade. The politicians who followed Jack Taylor decided to watch the situation. ...And I've become a journalist spouting nonsense again. Some media outlets are supporting me, but... that won't last long either. Boshi uses the media too well. And for some reason, influential figures in the political and business world are openly supporting Boshi."

"......Shouldn't you make the fact that Jack Taylor was captured public?"

"Boshi is not an easy opponent. Jack Taylor is currently officially on hiatus due to poor health. No one will believe you even if you say Jack Taylor was captured by Boshi."

"Hmm...."

"Shall I tell you something even worse? You and I have become wanted people. Our faces are spreading on the internet in real time."

Sophia pointed to the monitor.

My face and Sophia's face were posted on internet news. There's even a bounty on us. Sophia has a bounty of $50,000, and I have a bounty of $3 million.

"Isn't the bounty difference too big?"

"You're an Awakened, after all. And an ability user reaching A-class."

"Hmm. That's true."

"...You're so calm. I thought I was going crazy when I saw this."

"I don't get excited over something like this. The important thing is what we do next."

Sophia sighed deeply.

"Bluecade is planning to rescue Jack Taylor. But...."

"It's not going well, is it?"

"It's lukewarm because there's no center. It seems they're preparing a separate protest... but it won't have much effect. The current Bluecade won't be helpful."

"Then shall we two rescue Jack Taylor?"

"...No. That's difficult. Another way is...."

Sophia stared at me intently.

"There is a way. It's something that requires your consent, though."

"I said I'd help. I just hope it's not something too unreasonable."

"Do you know what Americans are enthusiastic about?"

"...Uh, well. Freedom?"

"Freedom is a given. Americans love heroes. Superheroes with superpowers."

"I know that too. The successful Hunters in the United States are called heroes separately, aren't they? I heard they're more popular than ordinary celebrities."

"That's right. So, what I'm saying is... are you willing to become a dark hero?"

"...Are you saying that seriously?"

"I'm serious. Regardless of the evaluation of me, the public opinion of you is surprisingly not bad. That's because the 3 people you killed yesterday were all mafia. And criminals with superpowers, villains. Some communities are already praising you as Jupiter."

"Jupiter?"

"Don't you know? Zeus, the chief god of Greek mythology...."

"No. I know that. What I'm curious about is why Americans are praising me."

"Dark heroes who punish criminals in a cool way are rare."

Skilled Hunters don't do dark hero work. No matter how much people want it, dark heroes are criminals after all.

There's no reason to give up a stable job and become a criminal just to gain popularity from people.

"Do we really have to do that dark hero thing?"

"We're already criminals. All that's left is to be chased by the police and bounty hunters. But if you become the perfect dark hero that Americans want... We can move American public opinion in the direction we want. It may even be possible to define Governor Boshi as evil and drag him down from the presidential seat."

"......."

I fell into thought. I weighed this and that.

First of all, it looks pretty fun.

And next is the risk... After thinking about various things, I nodded. It was worth a try.

"Let's give it a shot."
