[Devil's Clown Mask

A clown mask imbued with the power of the devil.]

I tried wearing the mask first. I thought it would be a little difficult since it was my first time, but as soon as I brought it to my face, the mask attached itself. I was startled and took the mask off, and it came off right away.

,...The devil's power is no joke.'

Putting aside a slight feeling of unease, I put the clown mask back on.

Once I put it on, I knew this mask was something else. Usually, you feel a sense of stuffiness when wearing a mask, but there was none of that. It didn't feel like I was wearing a mask at all. Even when I subtly rolled my eyes, my vision wasn't obstructed by the mask.

'Indeed. The Devil's Clown Mask.'

I faced the mirror and opened my mouth. The mouth part of the clown mask opened, revealing my mouth. When I closed my mouth, the mouth part of the clown mask closed as well. It seemed like I could even eat food with the mask on.

The same goes for the eyes. When I closed my eyes, the eye area of the clown mask closed.

Tuk tuk. I touched the mask with my fingertips. I felt the hardness unique to masks.

'Amazing.'

I reached out and grabbed the edge of the mask, trying to take it off. But it wouldn't come off.

'It won't come off because I don't want it to.'

It seemed like there was no risk of it falling off naturally.

Lastly, I touched the wriggling red wig. My hair wasn't exposed at all because of this wig. I grabbed a strand and pulled it hard, but it didn't come out. Of course, there was no pain.

'Awesome.'

I chuckled, looking at my reflection in the mirror.

The clown's mouth stretched wide.

'I changed my clothes where there were no people to avoid CCTV, and put on the mask at the right time. I stole the car on the street by hacking it. Even when I run away, there's no way I'll be tracked if I use my abilities and inventory.'

In most dramas, movies, and comics, they assume Hunters don't exist. This is because if a Hunter with powerful abilities existed, the story itself would become uninteresting. Because the Hunter would solve the case with their powerful abilities.

The absolute majority of people in reality were ordinary people, and the setting without Hunters worked better to empathize with ordinary people.

And that's incredibly fortunate for me. The fact that there are no Hunters in this world means that there is no one to stop me.

"Shall we get started?"

A man wearing an unbuttoned black suit and a clown mask with a frizzy red wig. Ordinary people would usually think of it as an event or cosplay.

She's probably calling the Black Cross Society headquarters or the vampire Hunters who are exercising upstairs.

"I'm a little busy."

Yeongcheon Ryu Practical Combat. Seogwang (Dawn).

The sword drawn from its sheath left a red trail in the air and cut off the female employee's head. Her blood splattered on my body.

Seogwang has the disadvantage of having a large preliminary motion, but if swung properly, it is a quick sword that even Hunters find difficult to react to.

'Seriously, these modern people.'

If I didn't know Yeongcheon Ryu, I might have been caught, but not now.

Yeongcheon Ryu Practical Combat Yuseon (Streamline).
