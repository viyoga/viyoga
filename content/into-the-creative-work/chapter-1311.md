The ceaselessly pouring undead were truly a sight to behold. Even though they were merely low-level undead like skeletons and zombies, the scene looked like the end of the world had arrived.

"...This isn't good."

Melissa muttered with a serious face.

"Not good? They're just skeletons and zombies, low-level undead anyway. I can wipe them out in one go. If necessary, I can use bombs."

Skeletons and zombies are just moving bones and corpses. Unlike their gruesome visuals, they aren't that strong.

"That's true for you, Master-nim. But what about others? They're objects of fear for nobles and servants who aren't specialized in combat. And there are too many of them. It will be hard for the soldiers to handle."

"The knights will be active. We're not the only ones here, are we?"

Melissa nodded at my words.

"The knights of the Archen Duchy are strong. For now, we have no choice but to trust them."

We ran down the hallway again. We opened doors and checked inside. We encountered quite a few living people.

"Young lady!"

We found three servants hiding. They were trembling in a narrow room like a storage. They brightened up as soon as they saw Melissa.

"You were hiding here. Go to the first floor. There's no point in being here separately."

"B, but...."

The servants looked out the window. Skeletons and zombies were still pouring out of the black hole in the sky.

"I know it looks dangerous. You must be scared because countless undead are appearing. The more it is like this, the more we must stick together. If you go down to the first floor, knights and soldiers will be fighting the undead. Help them."

"W, we can't do anything...!"

"I see. Then, tremble here and die to the invading undead. You don't think this place will be safe forever, do you?"

The servants looked at each other and said to Melissa.

"Young lady, can't you come down to the first floor with us...?"

"I'm sorry, but I have to resolve this situation. We can't just keep getting beaten up forever. The way down to the first floor is still safe. Go."

"Y, yes! I understand!"

The servants ran towards the first floor.

I frowned as I watched them.

"Those impudent bastards. Don't they know their place? You're too kind to the servants too."

"I learned from Master-nim? Doesn't Master-nim treat the maids kindly too?"

"Maids and servants are different."

"Well, don't get too angry. Even if it's just for a moment, since I've decided to become the master of the Archen family, they are people I have to take care of."

We moved again to find the demon contractor.

We found a corpse torn to pieces. The pieces of meat wriggled and gathered in one place. The gathered meat pulsed as if it were alive.

"Is it making undead? How gruesome."

Melissa clicked her tongue and used magic. Flames rose and burned the corpse.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion was heard. Melissa and I turned our gaze out the window. One of the annexes exploded entirely. Debris fell, and black flames soared from the debris.

A necromancer wearing a black robe stood proudly on top of the building debris.

"...There's another Master-nim over there. When did he go there?"

"This is the worst."

I muttered as I looked at a man held in the necromancer's hand. He looked like a noble. His limbs were cut off, and hundreds of black larvae wriggled on his torso. They seemed to be eating the man's flesh. The man screamed and wailed, unable to bear the pain.

"There's a magic circle, the evidence of a demon contract, drawn on his side."

"That guy won't kill the demon contractor. He'll keep him alive until he achieves his goal using black magic."

"In the end, the only option left is to fight head-on...."

The necromancer turned his head. He looked exactly where I was. The necromancer, who was staring at me with a blank face, raised the undead from the debris and headed towards the lily garden.

"It seems he doesn't intend to start a battle right away. Is he trying to be fully prepared?"

"Time is not on our side. The undead have been pouring out continuously since earlier... The key to solving this situation at once is held by him."

"Master-nim. I think we should stick together too. If Father, an Aura Master, cooperates... it would be a great help."

There are three master-level powers. With this much power, we could easily wipe out any ordinary city. But strangely, I can't quite shake the feeling that we can win against the necromancer.

"Master-nim. Let's go down to the first floor. Father will probably come to the first floor too. Maybe he's already commanding people on the first floor."

Following Melissa's suggestion, we went down to the first floor. Soldiers, knights, nobles, and servants, all the living people were gathered in the first-floor lobby. And as Melissa guessed, Duke Archen was commanding them.

Looking at the tired soldiers and knights, and the corpses scattered here and there, it seems they had already fought once. Now they are in a lull.

"Count Pruker, and Melissa. Do you know anything about the current situation?"

Duke Archen stared at me intently and asked. It's because of the necromancer. He and I look so much alike that we could be mistaken for twins, except for our skin and eye color.

"Father. I will explain. This is the work of a demon. King Dulliard is probably behind this incident."

"I heard rumors that the King had joined hands with demon contractors. I didn't believe it no matter what, but those rumors were true."

Duke Archen looked out the window.

"I tried to send the knights out. But a transparent barrier surrounded the mansion and blocked them."

"Currently, the mansion has been transformed into a maze by the power of the Mirror Demon, Birubiru. The situation will be resolved if we kill the demon or the demon contractor. The problem is that it's almost impossible to find the hidden demon... and the demon contractor is held captive by the necromancer."

"So, we have no choice but to fight the necromancer. What is the identity of that necromancer?"

This time, I stepped forward.

"It's me from a parallel world. Ah, do you know about parallel worlds?"

"I heard that theory from a wizard once."

"The Mirror Demon, Birubiru, has embodied me from a parallel world."

"Embodied? Not summoned from a parallel world?"

"Parallel worlds do not exist in this world."

I said in a confident tone. It's from the original story.

"...Then why are you saying the necromancer is from a parallel world?"

"Because Birubiru created the necromancer with the possibility of a parallel world. The necromancer is an elaborate fake. Everything from his existence to his origin is fake. But the power he possesses is real. He will have memories and experiences that are similar to mine but different."

"......Is that possible?"

"It's possible because he's a demon. The power of a demon is beyond standards. Don't judge it with human common sense."

"How many demons have joined hands with King Dulliard? If each and every demon is at this level... the rebellion will fail."

"Demons with this much power are not common. There are not many demons left who are this dangerous. We have also prepared a trump card. We will take care of the demons. The Archen Duchy should focus only on the rebellion."

The trump card was Yuria. As long as Yuria is there, I don't think we will lose.

"I understand. What are you going to do now?"

"If we want to get out, we have no choice but to fight and win against the necromancer. He knows that too, so he's acting leisurely."

I explained the plan.

The knights and soldiers will take care of the small fries, the undead. And I, Melissa, and Duke Archen will directly attack the necromancer.

"I understand."

Duke Archen nodded without saying anything.

"Father! Please take me too!!"

Berdo Archen, Duke Archen's son, jumped out. His eyes under his armor were burning fiercely. I could feel his rivalry towards Melissa.

"No."

Duke Archen said firmly.

"That necromancer is strong. He's on another level. I don't have the capacity to protect you."

"I promise I won't hold Father back! I am the successor of the Archen Duchy! Please give me a chance to punish him too!"

"The successor is Melissa. Melissa will inherit the title of Duchess when this is over. And Berdo. You are still weak. Stop the undead with the knights."

"Father! I can fight too!!"

"Anyone can fight. Distinguish between courage and recklessness. Don't overestimate your abilities."

"Father...!"

"It's an order, Berdo."

"Keuk...."

Berdo gritted his teeth. His hand pointed at Melissa.

"Why is Sister...! Why is Sister allowed and I'm not?!"

"Melissa is strong. Recognizing the strong is also a strength. In that sense, you continue to disappoint me."

Berdo was flustered by Duke Archen's deep sigh. He hurriedly looked around. The vassals and knights ignored his gaze even though they knew what he wanted. In this situation, it was too urgent to argue with Duke Archen.

Berdo was the only one who didn't know his place.

"...I will follow Father's orders."

Before moving, Duke Archen called the knight commander Harmel.

"Harmel. I'm entrusting Berdo to you."

"Leave it to me, head of the family."
