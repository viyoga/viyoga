Kung kung kung kung kung!

With both legs bitten by Soldier bugs, Emma was dragged away, her head constantly hitting the floor. Even with the power suit helmet on, the impacts weren't completely blocked, making it hard to stay conscious.

,Kueueuk...!

Barely managing to regain her senses and resist, a Soldier bug nearby attacked. Without a gun or grenades, she couldn't even put up a fight.

Hand-to-hand combat? As a soldier, she had learned the basics, but it was merely for show.

She had received enhancement surgery and wore a power suit, but that was just standard for a space marine. In the first place, she was a ship crew member, the captain's exclusive adjutant. As the elite of the elite, she never had to fight Gestalts directly.

Regular marines do simulation training almost every day, but she only did it about once a month, and they didn't simulate such horrific situations.

Stimulant? She had already injected it. And yet, this was the result.

Kung kung kung kung!

Thanks to the stimulant's effects, she could still think clearly even amidst the constant head trauma.

'I dropped my gun, and I used all my grenades. The sidearm that should be there is also gone. They threw it away while dragging me.'

Most Gestalts have low intelligence. But they can still distinguish that a gun is a weapon. They've been through it so much that they've learned guns are dangerous.

'At least the knife stored inside the power suit's thigh is intact...!'

Kill a Soldier bug with that?

That's impossible.

Unless you're a battle-hardened marine, it's hard to win against a Soldier bug in close combat. And it's even harder when you have to face multiple Soldier bugs, not just one.

'Find an opportunity to commit suicide...!'

Humans captured by the Gestalts are interrogated and then killed. Torture is, of course, included. It's better to die cleanly by suicide than to die horribly.

Rescuing comrades?

There was no room for that.

No matter how positively you think about it, charging into the hive to save one captured comrade is insane.

The ever-rational Captain Max would never make such a choice. No, no officer, including Emma, would ever choose such a crazy thing.

Kung kung kung kung kung!

The continuous, irregular impacts came to an end.

Buuuuuuung.

Emma, thrown into the air, fell to the ground. The impact struck her body. The helmet display showed the damaged areas of the power suit. The right ankle area was the most severely damaged, and the rest were minor.

,Get up, human.

A sharp voice, like scraping metal, rang out.

Emma bit her lip and stood up. She was in the middle of a vast underground chamber, surrounded by Soldier bugs.

At the center was a humanoid bug.

A humanoid bug with long antennae on its head and its body covered in hard carapace. It was a General bug, created using human general's genetic base.

'...It's my first time seeing one in person.'

The General bug can perfectly control Gestalt entities within a 300m radius with mental waves and acquire information.

Shuk.

Emma's helmet visor went up. She took out the knife stored in her thigh and tried to stab her own neck.

Peok!

,Kkeueueuk!

A sharp bone, like a spear, flew and pierced through her right forearm, where she was holding the knife.

A Spear bug, hidden among the Soldier bugs, had thrown a bone spear harder than steel.

,You were trying to commit suicide according to the records received from the Queen-nim. Give up. All you can do is answer my questions.

,Do you think... I'll talk easily?

,So, you're the rebellious type of human. That's fine. Most don't last long once their bodies are torn apart. I'll cut off both arms first, just in case you try something.

The General bug's long antennae twitched.

Two Soldier bugs jumped out and swung their blades in succession. Klang! Klang! They held out a few times thanks to the power suit, but eventually, both her arms were severed.

,Aaaaaaak!

Red blood gushed from both arms. The amount of blood gushing out decreased. The blood at the severed areas clotted quickly, stopping the bleeding. It was the ability of an enhanced human.

,What is the identity of that? Is it a variant you created?

,...That...?

Emma glared at the General bug and questioned. Thanks to the remaining effects of the stimulant, the pain was greatly reduced, and her mind was clear.

,I'm talking about the one who slaughtered dozens of my soldiers alone. What is its weakness? If you answer, I will kill you painlessly.

,Are you talking about Private Seong?

,So, Private Seong is its name. Yes. First, tell me Private Seong's weakness.

,Weakness? Are you afraid of Private Seong? So, you bugs can be afraid of a marine.

,Speak.

,You seem to be in a hurry. Why, is Private Seong coming?

It was then.

Kwang! Kwang! Kwang!

The sound of a shotgun faintly echoed from somewhere. Moreover, it was rapidly getting closer.

The Gestalts surrounding the open space began to tense up. The General bug's antennae also vibrated rapidly.

Emma widened her eyes.

'Could it be... they came to rescue me? Captain Max?! There's no way Captain Max would make that decision...!'

Kwang! Kwang! Kwang! Kwang!

As the shotgun sound grew louder, it was certain that a rescue team was coming. Emma couldn't understand it, but a smile spread across her face.

The General bug sent mental waves to the surrounding Gestalts. The Soldier bugs and Spear bugs formed ranks.

Emma was of no concern.

There was no thought of taking Emma hostage. According to past war records, the value of a single human was not particularly high. Was this human woman a special being like the Queen? That wasn't the case either. Strategically, there was no rescue value at all.

The General bug judged that Seong Yujin was coming to kill it.

Seong Yujin revealed himself.

The 10 Spear bugs on standby simultaneously launched bone spears.

But Seong Yujin was already flying in the sky. He had been running with a jetpack on his back even before the open space appeared.

,You bug bastards dare! Touch my woman?! I'll kill you all!

The flying space marine roamed the open space with a shotgun in his left hand and a vibe sword in his right.

Dozens of Soldier bugs rushed at the space marine, and the Spear bugs threw bone spears without regard for friendly fire.

But they couldn't chase the flying marine who flew through the air with a jetpack and dashed across the ceiling. In addition, grenades fell in appropriate places, splitting the air and massacring the Soldier bugs.

Kwang!

The Soldier bug's torso exploded with one shot from the shotgun.

Seogeok!

The Spear bug was split in half by the vibe sword.

The surrounding air split, and five vibe swords were ejected and repeatedly summoned back. Each time they were ejected, one Gestalt was certainly killed.

Before 10 seconds had passed, 30% of the Gestalts in the open space were dead.

The General bug's antennae moved constantly. It called for reinforcements and directed orders to its soldiers.

Once.

It only needed to block his movement once. They could swarm him all at once to stop his movement, or remove one of his limbs to disrupt his balance.

But Seong Yujin's movements were faster than the General bug's instructions. The Gestalts couldn't keep up with his speed as he flew around using the jetpack. By the time the General bug gave an order, Seong Yujin was already killing Gestalts elsewhere.

The open space was wide, but it was somewhat cramped to fly around with a jetpack. A slight mistake could lead to crashing into a wall and dying. Seong Yujin was clearly performing acrobatics. But there were no mistakes.

,Keuhahaha! I am Sexcode!

Seong Yujin flew around and scattered grenades over the heads of the Gestalts. Pung pung pung pung pung! More than 20 Soldier bugs exploded and died.

Gestalt reinforcements entered through the tunnels, but the situation didn't change. Only Seong Yujin's kill count increased.

Emma sat down on the floor and blankly watched Seong Yujin's absurd battle. Marie appeared behind Emma.

,Captain Emma-nim. It's dangerous here, so let's move back.

,Lieutenant Marie...!! Why are there only two of you? Where's the Captain-nim?

Marie grabbed Emma's torso and dragged her back, saying.

,The Captain-nim didn't come.

,......I see. So, this rescue operation wasn't the Captain-nim's order. Did you perhaps ignore the Captain-nim's orders and come in?

,Yes. That crazy bastard was going wild saying he was going to bang you, Captain-nim.

,...Huh.

Marie, having moved to a relatively safe area, began to administer first aid.

Meanwhile, the General bug belatedly realized that Seong Yujin was not killing it. Seong Yujin could have killed it from the beginning. The only reason he wasn't killing it was to kill the reinforcements gathering here.

The General bug made a quick decision. There was no chance of killing that human right now. It canceled the call for reinforcements. Instead, it would inform the Queen that there was an interesting human entity. If they could secure that human's genes, the species would evolve further.

However, the Queen would not be able to move immediately because of the succession ceremony.

Reinforcements did not come.

The open space was filled with a hill of Gestalt corpses. At the center was the General bug.

,Heok, heok...!

Seong Yujin, who had landed on the ground, took a long breath. His arms and legs trembled. He would have collapsed on the floor if it weren't for the power suit's assistance. Still, he didn't show it and moved. He couldn't let the enemy know his condition.

,Is this the end? Call more. I'll kill them all.

,We avoid meaningless attrition.

,You seem relaxed even in this situation? Let's see how long you can stay relaxed.

Seong Yujin approached the General bug and swung the vibe sword. The General bug's arms and legs were cut off. Cheolpeodeok. The General bug, fallen on the floor, only moved its antennae.

Seong Yujin's face distorted.

,Damn it. Can't it even feel pain? No, but if I keep it alive for now....

Emma shouted.

,Private Seong! Kill the General bug! The General bug can control other bugs with mental waves! If you keep it alive, our information will be transmitted to the Gestalts in real time!

,Tsk.

Seong Yujin, clicking his tongue, stepped on the General bug's head and crushed it.
