「Game Points Held: 80」

Shin Dohyeon and I decided to use the game points we'd earned this time on the random draw. Shin Dohyeon needed an ability, and I needed a tool.

,Since Dohyeon has a gun as a game tool, there must be a knife too.'

Guns are stronger than knives. It's common sense that everyone knows, but strangely, I was drawn to the knife.

"I'll do it."

First, Shin Dohyeon started the draw. She had 90 game points. She used them all for 3 draws. And the ability she got was Storage.

The ability to store things in a subspace. In game terms, it was an inventory.

After experimenting with it, Shin Dohyeon could only store things, and the size of the subspace was only about the size of a slightly large backpack.

,Enough to put a gun in.'

It takes about 1 second to put things in and take them out. Isn't 1 second the blink of an eye? Depending on how you use it, it could be a ridiculously OP ability.

Shin Dohyeon was indifferent.

"It's convenient."

Seeing her store various items in the subspace, she seems to like it.

Now it's my turn. I can do it twice. It would be perfect if a good game tool came out at once and I could enhance it once.

,Advice. When is it better to draw?'

I wonder if Advice will answer. But the answer that came back was disappointing.

&lt;Information related to game tools and ability draws cannot be known.&gt;

I had no choice but to trust my luck.

「You have drawn the Copy ability.」

「Only one ability can be selected.」

「Choose between Advice and Copy.」

Just by hearing the name, I can guess what kind of ability it is. It must be the ability to copy objects or abilities.

,Isn't that a ridiculously OP ability that manga protagonists have?'

But I wasn't drawn to it. Because to copy, there must be an object to copy. I chose Advice.

「You have acquired a die.」

A die appeared in the air. I reflexively grabbed the die. It looked like an ordinary die on the outside.

,It can't really be an ordinary die.'

Shin Dohyeon's game tool, the revolver, automatically reloads bullets every midnight. This die must also have such a special ability.

,Everyone knows how to use a die. Rolling it.'

I was about to throw the die towards the floor.

&lt;Warning. Do not throw the die now.&gt;

I stopped.

Shin Dohyeon looked at me as if something was wrong. I looked at the air.

,Can I throw it now?'

&lt;The user can only receive advice three times a day.&gt;

I couldn't hear it because I used up all the advice.

I pondered. Should I throw it? Should I not throw it? If Advice is stopping me with a warning, it could be dangerous. But hasn't the "now" that Advice is talking about already passed?

,Shibal. I can't stand it because I'm curious. Is the die going to explode or something?'

Just in case, I threw it far away, not nearby. I stood in front of Shin Dohyeon. So that I can stop Shin Dohyeon if something happens.

The die fell to the floor. And a red string suddenly appeared in the air. I reflexively grabbed it.

,Why is this suddenly here?'

I instinctively unfolded the string. It wasn't a string, but a micro bikini. The area covering the important parts was so small that I mistook it for a string.

"Ha."

Shin Dohyeon sighed. I flinched without realizing it and looked at Shin Dohyeon. She looked at me with contempt, then snatched the micro bikini away.

"Just for today."

I didn't specifically ask her to wear it. But I wanted to see her wearing the micro bikini, so I kept my mouth shut. My balls were going to be empty again tonight.

I retrieved the die before going back. The die's number was 4. I threw the die again.

&lt;The die can only be used once a day.&gt;

And after 1 hour, unfortunately, the micro bikini disappeared. But my balls were still empty.

I used Advice the next morning.

,What exactly is the die's ability?'

&lt;When the die is thrown, it brings bad luck if the number is 3 or less, and good luck if it is 4 or more. The larger and smaller the number, the greater the luck and bad luck it brings.&gt;

I still don't quite get it.

I didn't know the limits of luck and bad luck. It would be troublesome if a micro bikini suddenly appeared like yesterday.

,Can you tell me when to roll the die to get a 6?'

&lt;Roll it in 3 seconds.&gt;

,1, 2, 3.'

I dropped the die. The die that was rolling stopped. The number is 6.

A sword appeared in the air. A straight sword with an impressive red blade. It was the sword that came out when I activated Killer's Time last time. I grabbed the hilt as if snatching it. A thrilling sensation was felt through my hand.

I felt like I could cut anything. I swung the sword wildly in the air. It couldn't be more fun.

And after 1 hour, the sword disappeared. Feeling regret, I rolled the die. Nothing happened.

• • •

Around 4 PM, Yu Jin-myeong came to find me.

"I need Yu-ssi's help."

"My help?"

Yu Jin-myeong's expression was serious. It seems things aren't going as well as I thought.

"I plan to contact Peng Cheon-su's subordinate who is hesitating to betray him."

"You're contacting him directly? You're doing something dangerous."

"That's why I need Yu-ssi's help. It's enough just for Yu-ssi to be by my side. We might have to fight in an emergency... but that's unlikely."

"Wouldn't it be troublesome if our faces were known? Peng Cheon-su would try to kill us with all his might."

"We can hide our identities with makeup. And Peng Cheon-su's ability isn't to peek into memories... so our identities won't be revealed."

"Hmm. Is contact really necessary?"

"Yes. We have to give them at least minimal trust. It's too short on time to just manipulate them from behind the scenes... We have to take some risks."

I moved with Yu Jin-myeong.

We put on makeup and headed to the outskirts of Seoul. As expected of a place famous for its poor security, the atmosphere was subdued. I fiddled with the revolver in my arms. It was a revolver I borrowed from Shin Dohyeon for a while.

"This way. Please act as I said in advance."

"Okay."

We entered an old warehouse. Inside the warehouse, burly men were gathered.

"You really came."

The man sitting in the center of them, on a chair, said with admiration. He was a man wearing a wine-colored shirt and a beard. His impression was dirty.

I recalled the information about the guy I heard from Yu Jin-myeong.

Ji Byeong-su.

Peng Cheon-su's subordinate. The guy who runs the gambling den on behalf of Peng Cheon-su. At the same time, he is Peng Cheon-su's sword. A subordinate who kills the guy Peng Cheon-su tells him to kill, and kidnaps the guy he tells him to kidnap.

"Oh. You really showed up. I thought you'd be scared and wouldn't come... I'll give you a compliment."

He, who was talking to Yu Jin-myeong, looked at me. I looked at him too. There was no need to be scared. I've already committed murder. Besides, I have a revolver in my arms. Although I can only shoot 3 bullets... I can definitely kill 3 people with my skills.

"Are you the boss of Seonghae?"

Seonghae. A fake organization that Yu Jin-myeong created to contact Ji Byeong-su. The setting is that I am the boss of this organization.

"Yeah. I'm the boss of Seonghae."

"Ha. You're just a small organization, but you seem to have a lot of sass?"

"Are you any better? Stop the bullshit and get to the point."

"Oh my. You're so dignified. Should I let you know whose territory this is?"

As soon as Ji Byeong-su finished speaking, his subordinates around him picked up tools. Starting with hammers and bats, all the way to Sashimi knives.

I calmly took out the revolver from my arms and aimed it at Ji Byeong-su. Ji Byeong-su's eyes sink coldly.

"You have something dangerous. How did you get it?"

Ji Byeong-su's voice became serious. It's a gun after all. Just having this gave me respect.

"You don't need to know."

Ji Byeong-su's eyes narrowed. Ji Byeong-su is also a participant in the Devil's Game. I don't know exactly what his ability is, but if he's going to do something strange, I'm going to shoot him dead right away.

,Judging from the fact that Advice isn't warning me, it doesn't seem dangerous.'

At that time, Yu Jin-myeong intervened.

"Boss. We came to have a conversation. Ji Byeong-su-ssi. You should stop there too. As I said on the phone, we have no intention of fighting you."

"Ah, okay. Okay."

Ji Byeong-su waved his hand. His subordinates put down their tools. I also lowered the revolver.

"Is that enough? Now tell me. What is Peng Cheon-su's ability... and what are your abilities?"

"Our organization is preparing for an online gambling business. But Peng Cheon-su has a tight grip on Korean online gambling."

"Ah. That's right. I heard he's doing business in Singapore or something? I don't know much about online gambling because it's not under my jurisdiction."

"That's why we found Ji Byeong-su-ssi. Ji Byeong-su-ssi can have the domestic gambling dens. We'll be satisfied with online gambling."

"Who doesn't know that online gambling is the real deal these days? You guys are complete thugs."

Ji Byeong-su growled.

Yu Jin-myeong didn't even twitch an eyebrow.

"Peng Cheon-su's ability is the voice of the heart. He can hear the voice of the heart of the target within his sight. No, to be more precise, it would be correct to say that he can see it."

Ji Byeong-su's eyebrows twitched.

"So... he can see through people's hearts? Peng Cheon-su can?"

"That's right. Isn't Peng Cheon-su purging his subordinates these days? It's because he has that ability. Since he can see through people's hearts, it's not difficult to find traitors and rebels."

Ji Byeong-su clenched his fist tightly.

"Shibal. No wonder he's been acting up lately.... But how do you guys know that?"

"Our organization also has quite a few ability users. We also know who Peng Cheon-su's stooge here is."

"That's really Jot-gatda.... Who's the stooge?"

"Do you believe my words?"

"Let's just hear it for now. Believing it is secondary."

Yu Jin-myeong calmly pointed to the person next to Ji Byeong-su. It was a big guy holding a hand axe in one hand.

"Mun Hyeok-su-ssi. He received an order from Peng Cheon-su to monitor Ji Byeong-su-ssi. Did you report it right away by any chance?"

"...Hyeok-su-ya. Is that true?"

"No, it's not! Hyung-nim! I'm Mun Hyeok-su, Hyung-nim's right-hand man! Is that bastard trying to drive a wedge between us! Do you believe the words of that bastard who suddenly appeared? Absolutely not, Hyung-nim!"

"Haha. Then let's take out your smartphone here. There will be traces of contacting Peng Cheon-su."

"This shibal-nom!! Won't you shut up?!!"

Mun Hyeok-su, whose face turned bright red, shouted and rushed in.

I stepped forward. When I activated Super Focus, time slowed down.

,Well, I can see all the movements even without using Super Focus.'

The guy who was hit in the bullseye gets excited and pretends to rush at Yu Jin-myeong. The real purpose is to pass by Yu Jin-myeong and run away. He's probably going to ask Peng Cheon-su for help while running away.

Mun Hyeok-su passed by Yu Jin-myeong. I moved and blocked his path.

"Get out of the way!!"

An axe falling from above. I lightly dodged to the side and grabbed the guy's wrist, twisting it to take away the axe.

Peok! Peok! Peok!

I mechanically swung the axe a few times and the guy died. Blood was all over my clothes. I glanced. The corpse was a mess.

,I got a little too excited.'

The feeling of hitting someone with an axe was better than I thought. I dropped the axe on the floor and looked around. Before I knew it, I was dominating this space.
