Baek Ocheol, the Mountain King of the Four Kings Alliance, rose to his feet. His muscular body was covered in the hides of wild beasts. He killed beasts, cut their hides, and made them into clothes to wear. His lower garment was made of leopard skin, and his upper garment was made of tiger skin.

,It's about time, my brothers. Are you ready?!"

A lanky middle-aged man with a sly grin approached him. Baek Palcheol. He was Mountain King Baek Ocheol's younger brother. A large red axe was slung across his back.

,Hyung-nim. I've only prepared trustworthy guys. Even if other bastards try to interfere, these guys will stop them."

Another middle-aged man approached from the opposite side of the Mountain King. He was small and expressionless. Baek Gucheol, another of the Mountain King's younger brothers. Five small skulls floated around him. They were magical tools made by casting spells on monkey skulls.

,All preparations are perfect. If that legend is true... Mount Domyeong will reveal itself before us."

,Haha. What if the legend of Mount Domyeong is a lie after all this preparation? The little shits will be dissatisfied, won't they?"

Baek Palcheol fiddled with the axe at his waist. The Mountain King chuckled.

,Those little shits who try to climb up can just have their heads smashed as always, can't they? And that's a pointless worry. My gut is telling me. Mount Domyeong will appear."

The Mountain King snorted. He was excited.

He had been preparing for this for a long time. He stirred up the bandits to direct the attention of other forces towards them. Even if they realized his plan, they wouldn't be able to move easily because the bandits would be a nuisance. Especially since the Martial Alliance and the Demonic Cult, among other large forces, were too far away to move deliberately.

The Imperial Palace? The army? If those bastards were going to move, they would have moved already. They wouldn't move over something like this unless someone crossed the line first.

Even if other variables arose in the plan, he and his brothers would be the ones to reach the summit of Mount Domyeong in the end.

The Mountain King and his brothers went outside the mountain stronghold.

,It's time. The energy is starting to change."

Baek Gucheol said calmly, looking up at the sky. The white clouds that had been filling the sky vanished in an instant.

Light flashed high in the sky. The light fell on the mountain like a falling star. The mountain where they were standing shone dazzlingly, and they were forcibly transported outside their mountain.

A smile appeared on their faces as they were transported to the entrance of Mount Domyeong.

,See! Wasn't my gut right! The legend of Mount Domyeong was true!"

The Mountain King laughed heartily.

,Ah. I didn't expect to be transported outside... I'll have to give orders to my subordinates again."

Baek Palcheol swung his axe, cutting down a tree and leaving a message for the bandits.

,...We must be careful. The nature and ley lines are a complete mess. Even with my magic, it's hard to figure out exactly what's on Mount Domyeong. It seems like we won't be able to use teleportation or fly."

Baek Gucheol looked at the shining Mount Domyeong with a cautious gaze. Mount Domyeong was glowing faintly. It was also more than five times larger than the original mountain. The summit of Mount Domyeong was higher than the clouds.

,Hahaha! Enough with the nonsense! Let's go, our era will soon begin!"

The Mountain King burst into a hearty laugh and ascended Mount Domyeong with his brothers.

• • •

There was another group watching Mount Domyeong. They were wearing white tiger masks and black clothes. The quiet ones shared their opinions among themselves using the masks' abilities.

-Mount Domyeong has appeared. It's even more mysterious in person.

-The Mountain King has entered the mountain. We must move before he reaches the summit.

-There's no need to move hastily. The interior of Mount Domyeong is distorted and not as simple as it appears from the outside. Even if we start a little later, we can still overtake the Mountain King.

-Still, wouldn't it be better to start now?

-Wait. It's better to identify who is entering Mount Domyeong before going in. As you know, our existence must not be revealed. Avoid encounters with others as much as possible. If you inevitably encounter them, you must kill them.

Those who had been hiding around Mount Domyeong revealed themselves one by one. Most of them ran towards Mount Domyeong, panting. Then they encountered the Green Forest bandits around Mount Domyeong and engaged in battle. The pungent smell of blood already filled the air around the shining mountain.

-Ronin who happened to hear the legend of Mount Domyeong and came looking for it... They're very excited.

-They probably just see Mount Domyeong as an opportunity. They don't know what's ahead.

There were others as well. Martial artists who had prepared thoroughly and came looking for it. They engaged in a war of nerves with other forces, but they didn't attack first. Their top priority was to enter Mount Domyeong.

Ttang!

A strange bell sound rang out. Those wearing white tiger masks tensed up without realizing it.

-I feel an evil energy. It's unpleasant.

-Hundreds of Jiangshi are moving like an army. At the center of them is the old woman with the black hands.

-It's Guihon Heuksu (Ghost Soul Black Hand). That creepy old woman has come.

Guihon Heuksu was a master who didn't belong to either the Orthodox or Demonic factions. Those without affiliations are also called Muya (Wilderness).

Guihon Heuksu showed off her five hundred Jiangshi and entered Mount Domyeong. Surprisingly, as soon as that large force entered the mountain, their presence disappeared and could no longer be felt. It was as if the space itself was distorted based on the mountain.

Swaaaaaaa.

Along with the black clouds, they fell at the entrance of Mount Domyeong.

Hundred Ghosts Night Parade.

A great monster leading over a hundred monsters had appeared.

The great monster had an appearance that seemed to be a mix of dragon bones and humans, and wore armor and weapons like a general. He shone with ominous red eyes and gave orders to his subordinate monsters. Half of the monsters that made up the Hundred Ghosts Night Parade rampaged and began attacking the humans near Mount Domyeong. The other half entered Mount Domyeong with the great monster.

-That's Jeokgol Aggui (Accumulated Bone Evil Ghost) Ban Oh. He collects bones regardless of whether they are from humans or monsters. Rumor has it that he gets stronger through bones.

-There are quite a few formidable figures that can't be ignored.

-But we have the advantage. We have approximate information about Mount Domyeong.

The number of those entering Mount Domyeong noticeably decreased as the monsters appeared. This was because the monsters were quite strong. The monsters let out eerie laughs as they killed humans and tore apart their flesh and blood. It was their own feast.

-It seems like all the big shots have arrived.

-We must enter the mountain before it's too late.

-...Wait. Unordinary bastards are coming. This time it's three of them.

The three of them walked confidently. Two were men and one was a woman. The woman covered her face with a veil, but it didn't completely hide her beauty. The three of them walked forward slowly, leisurely enough to be felt.

The monsters that were eating humans hesitated. The energy they felt from them was unusual. That energy was comparable to the big shots who had entered the mountain earlier.

-I've never seen those faces before.

-The man who looks relatively weak smells like he's from the military.

The monsters who had been hesitating remembered the great monster's orders, or perhaps they believed in their numbers. The monsters rushed towards the three.

A black energy burst out from the body of the man at the front. That energy, which was extremely overbearing, was demonic energy.

The monsters, who were momentarily overwhelmed by the demonic energy, simultaneously emitted demonic energy. They resisted the demonic energy. The monsters' momentum began to rise. It seemed like the demonic energy could overwhelm the demonic energy in reverse. Until the Demon took a step.

Kung.

An intangible wave arose and swept across the world. Everything that touched it began to be forcibly crushed.

The monsters' momentum was broken in an instant. The weak monsters knelt on the ground as if their hearts had been broken.

The aftermath reached even the white tiger masks who were far away. They felt the pressure crushing their bodies.

-Keuek!

-I, is that magic?!

-No, this is....

Kung!

The second step.

All the monsters who were barely standing knelt on the ground. Not only that. The trees around him broke and the rocks shattered. The world was being crushed.

The monsters screamed and begged the man for their lives. But the man indifferently moved forward.

Kung!

The third step.

A massive demonic energy swept through the surroundings.

Monsters, humans, the world, everything exploded.

-It's the Heavenly Demon's Advent Step...! That bastard is Little Heavenly Demon Seong Yujin! Use your inner energy to protect your body!

Fortunately, the Demon did not descend. Instead, he walked through the devastated land and entered Mount Domyeong.

But the group with the white tiger masks could not rest easy.

-Heo, heok. M-my head hurts! W-what I killed back then was...!

-I, I will never, never surrender!!

They grabbed their heads. There was no physical pain. Instead, something kept coming to mind.

It was evil.

Whispering sins and inciting desires.

It was agony.

It was impossible to shake off. Even if they died, this agony would not disappear.

It was Mara.

The Demon was seeping in.

,Snap out of it!!"

The man who had taken off his white tiger mask shouted. The profound inner energy contained in his voice awakened the others.

Empty your minds! You should know how to deal with inner demons! Recite the secret of the White Spirit Mind Technique! The energy of the white tiger protects us, so these petty inner demons won't be able to harm us!

They began to recite the White Spirit Mind Technique that they had been learning and practicing since they were young. A white energy flowed out from their bodies.

The inner demons gradually retreated. Even as they disappeared, they left behind their afterimages as if mocking them. It was after a while that the white tiger masks completely eliminated the inner demons.

-W-was that the Heavenly Demon's Advent Step just now?

-It's much more terrifying than I've heard. To forcibly create inner demons...

,I don't know. As far as I know, the Heavenly Demon's Advent Step doesn't have the ability to create inner demons. It may just be inner demons that arose from being overwhelmed."

The man sighed and put the white tiger mask he had taken off back on his face.

-...Let's go. We've wasted more time than expected. If we encounter Little Heavenly Demon Seong Yujin... be prepared and engage in battle so that you don't get eaten by inner demons.
