Suddenly, I got a solo mission.

A solo mission falling to me, who hasn't even shed the newbie tag yet, was clearly strange.

I could hear the reason for this sudden solo mission from Ju Seo-hyun-ssi.

,You're being tested to see if you're qualified to be a Jaeger."

,Qualified? So, the company is testing me."

,That's Chief Kang Myung-sook's style. Just give up on the test. I'll talk to Chief Kang Myung-sook."

,You think I'd give up over something like this? But is it okay for you to tell me this?"

,It doesn't matter. It's not a test where you're given a problem and have to find the answer. The company is trying to assess your combat power. If you do well... you should be able to pass without much difficulty."

,Really?"

When I smiled, Ju Seo-hyun-ssi glared at me.

,...Please, I'm begging you, don't cause any trouble."

,I have a question."

,What?"

,Do those bastards even have the right to test and evaluate me?"

,......"

Ju Seo-hyun-ssi watched me with worried eyes before we parted ways.

,Now that I think about it, that was a cocky look. I'll have to focus on tormenting her asshole tonight.'

I drive to the location designated by the company. It was a sparsely populated area on the outskirts of Seoul. After waiting a bit at the designated location, a mission message arrived through the company app on my smartphone.

I let out a hollow laugh after reading the first line of the message.

,Kill a hidden Noble-class Vampire? Alone?'

It just seemed like they reworded "go and die."

,Even if I don't get along with the intelligence department, I haven't done anything to piss off the company, have I?'

After skimming through the entire message, I understand the details of the mission.

The Noble-class Vampire I'm supposed to face is weakened. There's also a strange phrase stating that additional support troops will arrive if I'm fighting for a while.

,So, it's a test. They'll help me if it gets dangerous, huh.'

The enemy's information was also included.

Noble-class Vampire Park Ji-woong.

A cruel vampire who enjoys attacking Inbal people and digging out their insides.

,It's presumed that he's the vampire who killed employee Seong Yujin's parents?'

A blatantly intentional phrase was written.

,Are they trying to get me riled up? Park Ji-woong's ability is sensory disruption. He attacked and killed 5 combat department employees and fled here with a right shoulder injury.'

I checked my equipment. One sniper rifle, two shotguns, two assault rifles, four pistols, five grenades, three C4s, and one Moonlight Sonata 7.

,Manageable.'

I grabbed the sword and two pistols and got out of the car. The sword felt more reliable than the pistols.

I look at the sky. The sky was dyed red because of the sunset. The sun will set soon.

,About 20 minutes? They probably want me to kill the Noble-class Vampire before the sun sets. They'll intervene as soon as the sun goes down.'

I head towards the cabin where the vampire is, looking around. Two men hidden in the rice paddies came into view. I pretended not to notice them.

,Ju Seo-hyun-ssi said that the next Jaeger is tested by two Jaegers. Those two must be the Jaegers.'

Ju Seo-hyun-ssi is a Jaeger, but she couldn't get the qualification to test me because she's my partner, which wouldn't be fair.

,Hmm. What should I do? First of all...'

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack the walkie-talkies for 3 hours.]

I hacked their walkie-talkies. I can hear their walkie-talkie conversations through the earpiece in my left ear.

-Is that guy a Jaeger candidate?

-Two pistols and a sword. His equipment is too simple. Did he learn swordsmanship from Ju Seo-hyun-ssi?

-I heard he killed over 60 vampires, including a Yana, by himself yesterday. If that's true, there shouldn't be any problems.

-He fails.

-What? Just because of his equipment?

-It's not about the equipment. Even if he only has one sword, it's fine if he uses it well like Ju Seo-hyun-ssi. Look at that guy's movements. He's moving too openly. That means he's underestimating the Noble-class Vampire.

-He seems confident.

-That's not confidence, it's arrogance. Does he think the injured Noble-class Vampire will just fight him? He needs to consider the possibility of him running away.

-Hmm. Isn't it because Park Ji-woong is his parents' enemy?

-Then he fails even more. You must always maintain composure when dealing with vampires.

-Well, let's watch for now. He's only been with the company for less than 3 months. We can teach him the minor stuff later. The important thing is his combat power.

-......

Evaluation.

I don't like being evaluated by others. If someone stronger than me evaluates me, it can't be helped. Because I'm weak, I have to accept whatever test or evaluation comes my way.

But this is different.

,Why are those bastards evaluating me when they're weaker than me?'

It's kind of pissing me off.

Kwaang!

The cabin door suddenly burst open. A gaunt vampire popped out from inside. The vampire's arms were abnormally long. There was a trace of injury on his right shoulder.

,Park Ji-woong?"

,Are you from the company? I'm so sick of you guys. But you're alone? Where's the other one?"

His voice sounded like he was gargling phlegm. Fatigue was plastered all over him. His condition seemed worse than what was written in the message.

,It's just a matter of dealing with one pathetic vampire. I'm enough on my own."

,Judging by your unfamiliar face, you're not a Jaeger... and there wasn't anyone like you among the Jaeger candidates. They sent an ordinary employee to me alone? Heh heh. Did you not do well in your company life?"

-He's talking to the vampire? He fails.

-He's his parents' enemy. He can talk for a bit. I can't hear what they're saying because they're too far away.

-If he's his enemy, he should attack as soon as he sees him. That's the only way to secure a definite victory.

,You killed my parents! I won't forgive you!"

,What the hell are you babbling with that emotionless voice...? No, wait. Your face... I think I've seen it somewhere before? I killed your parents?"

Park Ji-woong rolled his eyes as if recalling something, then grinned eerily, tearing the corners of his mouth wide.

,I remember now! The son of that big company section chief! Heh heh heh! That cocky bastard rejected my offer, so I ripped open his stomach and showed him his intestines. The screams of the bitch next to him were quite entertaining. Ah, I want to hear it again... If I rip open your stomach, I'll be able to hear a scream similar to that bitch's, right? You're his son, so your screams should be similar. Heh heh."

,You, you irreconcilable enemy! I will never forgive you! I'll kill you!"

Park Ji-woong tilts his head.

,...What's with that awkward tone and voice? You're kind of clumsy for someone who's come for revenge. Are you messing with me right now?"

,Did you already notice? You're more quick-witted than I thought."

I couldn't help it that my tone and voice were awkward.

I didn't feel the need to focus on acting for someone who was going to die anyway.

Park Ji-woong frowned after understanding my words.

,You stupid bastard. You're the one being played. Do you still not know why I bothered talking to someone like you?"

Park Ji-woong smiles and approaches. I was about to shoot my pistol, but I realized my toes were moving instead of my fingers.

,...It's Park Ji-woong's ability, sensory disruption. Did he cast his ability on me while we were talking? I didn't even notice.'

Park Ji-woong gets closer and closer.

,The first time people are hit by my ability, they try to move their arms but their necks move, or they try to move their necks but their legs move, so they can't control their bodies and they fall over. Then they get scared and beg for their lives. But you... I'll praise you for maintaining your composure."

I'm not just standing still. I was slowly moving my body here and there. I confirmed that my left toe was connected to my right middle finger.

,And my left ring finger is... Damn it. Where was it?'

I forgot.

There's not enough time to figure it all out. He's already within 5m, raising his sharp claws. I'll die if this keeps up.

,It's not like there's no way. Heavenly Heart and...'

[Activating Genius Time for 10 seconds.]

1 second.

That's how long it took to figure out the disrupted senses by slowly moving my entire body.

I threw away the pistol in my right hand and drew my sword, swinging it at Park Ji-woong. Park Ji-woong, who didn't expect me to swing a sword, reacted a beat late and tried to block the sword with his hand.

Is it because he's a Noble-class Vampire? Even though he moved a beat late, he easily grabbed the sword.

But the sword cut through his hand and severed his leg. Before the force of my swing disappeared, I cut off his arms and legs. Park Ji-woong, who lost his limbs, fell to the ground and writhed. Blood continued to flow from his severed limbs.

,Aaaaaaaaaaaagh!"

,Your screams are too loud."

,How! Your sword isn't even silver-plated!"

,Ah, this? It's a sword with great magic power, so it's especially effective against assholes like you."

I slashed Park Ji-woong's stomach with the sword.

,A vampire's insides aren't much different from a human's."

,Y-You bastard!!!"

,Shut up."

I stirred his insides with the sword. Park Ji-woong screamed continuously before dying. Thanks to the great magic power of Moonlight Sonata 7, he couldn't even last 30 seconds.

,Even though its effectiveness would have been nerfed in this world... it's still this good. It's usable.'

-Park Ji-woong is dead. Shit. That was a Noble-class Vampire? I think that bastard used his ability earlier... Did it not work on him? Hey, what do you think?

-...Hold. There's not enough data to judge right now. We can't completely judge his skills based on what he's shown us so far.

-Yana and Park Ji-woong. It's a fact that he killed two Noble-class Vampires alone.

-Didn't you just see? Even though he could have killed Park Ji-woong, he didn't kill him right away. Putting aside his skills, there's a problem with his character.

-Isn't it understandable since he's his parents' enemy?

-Anyway, it's on hold. It seems better to test him in a different way.

-Should we?

I spat on the ground.

I felt terrible listening to their walkie-talkie conversations. I picked up the pistol that had fallen on the ground and pressed the button on the walkie-talkie earpiece.

,I'm starting the reaction speed test now."

-Is that... coming from the walkie-talkie?

-Is that guy talking? That bastard, he's watching us.

The distance was about 300m.

It was a long distance to shoot with a pistol, but I was confident because I had the shooting skill.

I pulled the trigger.

The slide moved repeatedly, and the empty cartridges flew into the air.

A bullet pierced one of their heads.

,The dead bastard fails. If you're a Jaeger, you should be able to dodge a bullet. You pathetic bastard."
