I found something decent in the Yakuza's warehouse.

『Abundant New Year's Mochi

Rank: B

Slightly increases stats upon consumption.』

It was the kind of elixir item I was looking for.

There was no reason to save it, so I immediately popped it into my mouth and chewed. The mochi was quite edible.

Vitality surges through my body. I felt like my strength had slightly increased.

『Name: Seong Yujin

Strength: A Stamina: B+ Agility: B+ Durability: B Mana: A+

Traits: Spirit Eye (S) Demon Hunter (S) God of Storms (A)

Skills: Spirit Contract (A) Spirit Descent (A) Barrier (C+) Swordsmanship (B+)

Karma: Good (善) 22

I checked my status window. Unfortunately, my stats remained the same.

It wasn't that 'Abundant New Year's Mochi' had no effect. I actually felt like my physical abilities had improved slightly.

'It's just not a strong enough effect to raise my stats. All my stats are B rank or higher, so it won't change with just any elixir.'

In terms of numbers, it would be like the decimal point of my stats went up a little.

'I found the Abundant New Year's Mochi... I need to find other elixirs.'

In the Savior of the Academy game, elixirs basically only work on the first consumption. It means there's no use in eating the same elixir again.

I rummage through the warehouse.

No other useful items came out.

A C-rank katana and an A-rank swordsmanship skill did appear, but they were all things I didn't need.

[Are we going back now?]

'No way. I came all this way, it's a shame to just do this.'

[Are you going to kill that Madogeong's buddy or whatever?]

'That's on hold for a bit. I'm not confident I can kill him for sure.'

If he's a Madogeong executive, he's a force to be reckoned with even in Japan. He's not an S-class hero, but he's in the top tier of A-class. I couldn't guarantee that I could kill him with my current strength. I need to build up more power.

'Unless I can use Hwaryeon Bido completely. I can't use it because I'm recharging my power after using the domain earlier.'

Hwaryeon Bido has become more powerful, but Hwaryeon Bido is just a weapon after all. It wasn't good to rely solely on Hwaryeon Bido. I have to get stronger.

'The reason I came to Okinawa was for another purpose too. Do you know about the Mermaid's Treasure?'

[How would I know that?]

I could sense Makina's sulkiness in her voice. It seems like she just wants to go home and rest. It wasn't good to push or scold Makina at times like this. I summoned a Spirit Orb from my inventory and tossed it into the air.

Makina, who was possessing the Stormbreaker, popped out and snatched the Spirit Orb. She immediately put it in her mouth and munched on it. Her plump cheeks puffed up alternately as if she was rolling the Spirit Orb with her tongue. Then she grinned.

"Is the Spirit Orb that delicious?"

Just like cats go crazy for Churu, spirits go crazy for Spirit Orbs. I was curious, so I put a Spirit Orb in my mouth once, but it didn't taste like anything.

[Yeah! Spirit Orbs are the best! I can do anything for Spirit Orbs!]

'It's worth 20 points. Even though it came from a random draw.'

Anyway, it was easy to appease Makina.

Makina possessed the suit again. I flew to the sea off Okinawa using the jet engine.

Mermaid's Treasure.

It's not an item that directly appears in the original game. But it's definitely a real thing. I know because I heard it on the news during my first playthrough.

The news said that there's a hidden cave off the coast of Okinawa, and the Mermaid's Treasure is inside that cave. Originally, it was discovered by a Japanese A-class hero, but now I'm going to take it.

'Makina. How long can you operate underwater?'

[I'll implement a life support system, so it depends on your mana, right?]

Makina's ability to implement outdated technology, over-technology, sometimes felt so broken that it was absurd.

'Instead of my mana, you could use something else as power.'

I took out a Mana Cube from my inventory. A cube with a subtle blue light.

『Mana Cube

Rank: SS

Deploys a mana space.』

[Are you going to use that? You said it was too precious and only used it at critical moments.]

'It's perfect for training. What if I used it recklessly for combat and it broke? That would be fucking terrible.'

My thinking has changed now. I was going to use everything that was useful.

And I will have the Mana Cube in this world too.

Makina embedded the Mana Cube in the center of the suit. My lower abdomen, where the waist belt is. She had used the Mana Cube a few times, so she was uninhibited.

[It's a Mana Cube after all. The perpetual motion I've been dreaming of!]

It was true that it was a perpetual motion machine since it constantly created mana. But the problem was that the output was low, so it wasn't that versatile.

Splash!

I entered the sea off Okinawa and used Spirit Eye. Spirit Eye pierced through the darkness.

Raitenryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Waves (電磁氣波).

I spread out electromagnetic waves. But because it was underwater, the effect was much less than I expected. The search range, which should have been over 50m, was only about 10m.

'Makina. Radar.'

[Okay. I'll be able to check the terrain with radar up to 300m around.]

Radar waves spread from the suit, and a small window appeared on the user interface showing the terrain. It also showed things like fish swimming around.

'There's nothing nearby. I don't want to move... Can you move?'

[Don't use me so much!]

'One Spirit Orb.'

[I'll do everything!]

My body started moving on its own. To be exact, Makina was moving the suit.

Makina used her ability to turn the jet engine into something similar to an underwater scooter. Or, a small propeller for humans.

My body stretched forward on its own. It was quite comfortable, so I was dozing off when Manika's voice rang out.

[Wake up!!]

'...Did you find it?'

[I woke you up because I found it. This is the only underwater cave around here.]

I shake off my sleepiness and focus my eyes. There was a cave a little ways away. That place where no moonlight entered was darkness itself. But it couldn't cover my eyes.

[I can't tell what's inside. The radar doesn't work.]

'It must be connected to a dungeon.'

I went inside the cave. As I went in a little, I could see the space distorted. As expected, it was connected to a dungeon.

I entered the dungeon. No notification window appeared.

[The dungeon is underwater too. It doesn't seem like anything has changed... Ah! Mermaids!]

Mermaids appeared, holding tridents. Mermaids with human upper bodies and fish lower bodies. They were moving their mouths, but I couldn't understand because it wasn't human language. Their appearance was better than ordinary humans. Some of the female mermaids caught my eye.

'But their lower bodies are fish.'

My mood dropped when I saw their lower bodies.

I threw a punch at the attacking mermaid.

The water moved and blocked my punch. No, the flow of the punch itself changed strangely, taking away the power.

[Kyaa! The currents are strange!]

Makina made a fuss. Because the currents were moving strangely, grabbing me and moving me around.

'It's not that big of a deal. Red thunder.'

Pajijijijijik!

Discharged Red thunder electrocuted the mermaids. Perhaps because of the mermaids' characteristics, they couldn't properly resist the Red thunder.

The dead mermaids floated in the water. I went inside the cave. When I saw mermaids, I unleashed Red thunder first.

At the innermost part, I found a giant mermaid, presumably the boss monster.

Raitenryu (Thunder Heaven Style) Manrai (Swastika Thunder).

The giant mermaid was also a mermaid. It was electrocuted and died in one hit of Manrai. I got the bead behind it.

It was a gem that looked like aquamarine.

『Mermaid's Treasure

Rank: S

Can manipulate currents.

Increases stamina upon consumption.

Water spirits like it.』

[I can feel the power of spirits. The water spirit seems to like it. Are you going to contract with a water spirit with this?]

Makina asked as soon as I came out of the dungeon.

Water spirits, attracted by the Mermaid's Treasure, were already gathering around me. Most were low-level, and some mid-level spirits were occasionally seen.

A mid-level spirit that looked like a jellyfish approached me. The jellyfish spirit's thoughts were heard by me.

-Treasure. Contract. Possible.

'Get lost.'

When I unleashed Red thunder, it was horrified and ran away, creating distance.

'With something like this, I can contract with at least a high-level water spirit, but how dare a mid-level spirit like you.'

[Are you going to contract with a high-level water spirit?]

'No. I'm going to eat it.'

I had Spirit Orbs that spirits went crazy for. If I used Spirit Orbs well, I could catch a high-level spirit.

Ignoring the spirits that were following me, I summoned a space-time scroll from my inventory and tore it.

I appeared at my home in Seoul, Korea, not at Arashi Academy in Japan. I released Stormbreaker and consumed the Mermaid's Treasure.

The gem slipped down my throat and disappeared as if I was drinking very refreshing water.

『Stamina increases from B+ to A-.』

『Name: Seong Yujin

Strength: A Stamina: A- Agility: B+ Durability: B Mana: A+

Traits: Spirit Eye (S) Demon Hunter (S) God of Storms (A)

Skills: Spirit Contract (A) Spirit Descent (A) Barrier (C+) Swordsmanship (B+)

Karma: Good (善) 22

Stamina increased by one level. It was an achievement I made in less than 6 hours.

'Should I have done this sooner?'

I thought about other elixirs in my head. Things I can get now, things I can't get now, things that require other conditions to acquire, etc.

'It's harder to get as many things as I thought. I should contact Lamia and order her to get elixirs with money.'

Lamia was the woman who managed my assets. She was quite capable, perhaps because she had been educated by Yuria-ssi at one time. If I give her the things I got this time, she will dispose of them on her own.

'I've saved up money, so it's time to use it.'

I checked on Makina. She had already gone into her room and was sleeping. She acted so human that I sometimes doubted whether Makina was really a spirit.

Looking outside, the sun was starting to rise.

'I should go back to Arashi Academy in a bit. Should I eat Korean food for the first time in a while?'

But the only restaurants open at this time would probably be gukbap places.

I came outside and walked. The cool weather felt quite nice. As I passed the apartment playground, I saw familiar boys and girls.

"Yujin hyung!"

"Yujin oppa!"

The children sitting on the bench ran to me as soon as they saw me. They were children who looked to be in their early to mid-teens.

"Oh. Choi Minwoo, Choi Minha. It's been a while."

I met my son and daughter after a long time.
