He speaks in a calm yet languid voice. It's a voice I feel like I've heard somewhere before. Was it the voice of a male entertainer who specializes in radio?

,I'm glad you know. You know what I want right now, right?"

,I know you don't want green tea. But I should still ask, just in case. Would you like a cup of green tea?"

,Hand over the Fire Rat's Fur Coat."

I checked to the side without moving my eyes. I could see Okto subtly widening the distance.

,You're too hasty. That hasty personality will become a disaster someday."

,I'm saying it with good intentions."

,You've been like this since we first met. What's the reason you hate me?"

,Basically, I don't like men."

,That's quite the troublesome personality."

Tsukuyomi put his hand into his large sleeve. A red garment was visible from the end of the sleeve. It's the Fire Rat's Fur Coat.

,Stop it."

,What?"

,What you're trying to do. We can build a good relationship."

,The more I think about it, the more it seems like I'm at a loss."

「The Owner of the Sky yawns. He doesn't think you'll keep your empty promises at all.」

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

Tsukuyomi's hand holding the Fire Rat's Fur Coat moves.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

,Damn it. He's putting the Fire Rat's Fur Coat forward?'

,That sly bastard.'

He knows that the Fire Rat's Fur Coat is essential and is coming out like this.

If the Fire Rat's Fur Coat is destroyed, everything is ruined. I won't be able to enter the Moon Palace and I won't be able to mess with Hang'a.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Dragon Fist.

I use Dragon Fist with my left hand. Tsukuyomi reacted this time as well. There was no feeling that he was reacting forcibly. He dodges the attack with ease, as if he had predicted my attack from the beginning.

I opened my mouth. I pulled up Cheonma energy from my dantian and released it with a roar.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma's Roar (Heavenly Demon Roar).

,Kuaaaaaaaaah!"

,That guy doesn't get faster in an instant like my Instant.'

Tsukuyomi, floating in the sky, brandishes his hand filled with cold energy.

Chwaaaaaaaaah!

,There's a saying that there's a sea on the moon. Have you ever heard of it?"

,I think I've heard of it."

The seawater was increasing more and more. The seawater that had risen to my ankles had already risen to my knees.

I moved my legs before my lower body was completely submerged.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma's Reigning Steps.
