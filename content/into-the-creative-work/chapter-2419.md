,Is it a variant?'

The corpse wasn't normal. The skin was horribly burned, and dark blue blood vessels protruded here and there. Metallic things were sparsely mixed among the spilling entrails.

Perhaps sensing my suspicion, Na Chae-young-ssi said.

,He's an enhanced human. Looks like they implanted drugs and bio-machines.

,Bio-machines? They already made that?

,They're fast. But not perfect. Look. Even the way he looks is a mess. Probably a prototype... or something. Be careful. Enhanced humans can exert power similar to Awakened ones. Maybe even stronger.

,Is this guy not an Awakened one?

,I saw him use his ability. Awakened ones can also become enhanced humans. Well, Awakened ones have a lower suitability for enhancement, so they're not as efficient as ordinary people.

,This guy looks Japanese. I should interrogate him later.

I went outside the room. Choi Hye-jin, Irina, Yoo Seo-jeong, and Kim Ji-hee also sensed the anomaly and were preparing.

,Choi Hye-jin, Irina. You guys protect the women here.

,What? I want to fight too!

Choi Hye-jin protested. When I frowned and glared at her, she immediately cowered and lowered her gaze. It's the effect of beating her up when she doesn't listen.

,The most important people in our group, excluding me, are Doctor Na and Teacher Yoo. Protect them at all costs.

,Ah, okay.

After hearing Choi Hye-jin's subdued answer, I put on my combat helmet. The interface turned on in the display, and the map lit up, showing the locations of allies and enemies.

Of course, I don't blindly trust this minimap.

,There might be another guy with transparent abilities. The minimap is just for reference.

The real deal is electromagnetic waves.

I scatter electromagnetic waves, checking all around. Fortunately, there were no enemies nearby.

I opened the window and jumped out.

Bullets were raining down at the battlefield. Combat drones didn't hesitate to self-destruct, and the suicidal guards didn't back down. But the damage was accumulating on our side. It's a bit surprising that the drug-fueled guards are being pushed back.

,These bastards... are all Awakened enhanced humans using abilities. And they've even taken drugs.

Three enemies appeared in front of me. They quickly swapped their submachine gun magazines and aimed at me, pulling the trigger.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

I deployed the electromagnetic barrier, but the bullets pierced through. I had already moved to the side, so I dodged the bullets.

,...Rubber bullets? Did they know my ability in advance and counter it? No. If that were the case, they would have thrown grenades instead of bullets. They intend to capture me alive without killing me.

I picked up the corpse of a guard lying on the floor and used it as a shield, charging towards them.

One of the three lowered his gun and began to glare at me intensely.

My body became sluggish. My limbs didn't move as quickly as I thought they would. It's a different feeling from just becoming heavy.

,Is it an ability that slows down the target? Annoying ability.

The other two retrieved their submachine guns and pulled out pistols from their waists. An ampule was embedded in the slide of the pistol. I knew it instantly.

,Anesthetic.

If I get hit, I'm fucked.

My recovery ability activates even when I'm unconscious. Even if I die, I can automatically recover and revive. It just takes a little longer if I'm unconscious. Na Chae-young-ssi said that the abilities of Awakened ones are related to brainwaves. If the brain is completely shattered, it won't recover, and even I can die.

,No, what's important now is what happens if I get hit with the anesthetic. When I'm unconscious, the output of my ability decreases. Of course, I can't wake up instantly. The moment I lose consciousness, the kidnapping is confirmed.

If I keep getting hit with the anesthetic, I'll develop a tolerance... but it will take a considerable amount of time to build up that tolerance.

,Fortunately, I'm wearing combat gear now... so I shouldn't get hit by a stray anesthetic shot.

The surest way is to kill them before I get hit.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Swift Lightning (Rapid Wind, Swift Thunder).

Pajijijik.

Lightning runs through my entire body, stimulating it. All speeds, including thinking speed, increased. I'm still slowed down, but even so, they can't keep up with my accelerated state.

I swung my sword at the approaching bastards. Their heads floated into the air.

The guy using the slowing ability widened his eyes and tried to pull a knife from his chest. Too late. My sword was already piercing his neck.

Swift Lightning was released, and I returned to my original speed. I felt my body, which had become hot like an overloaded machine, and exhaled. My muscles also hurt. It was the aftermath of using Swift Lightning with a weak body. A kind of overclocking, you could say.

,Recovery.

I solve the burden on my body at once with recovery.

-This is the main building's front gate! Need support! There's a variant-class attacker! Five guards have been taken down! Twelve combat drones completely destroyed! Can't hold out for long! Need support!

,Variant-class? Five guards and twelve drones can hunt down most variant-class enemies. It must be a named-class among the variant-class.

The main building's front gate was close from here, so I ran without requesting information.

A monster mass was standing at the entrance of the main building's front gate. It was a 3m tall monster covered in bio-metal. A patchwork of flesh that looked like it weighed at least 1 ton.

,Isn't that a variant?

-It's a fusion ability.

Yoo Seo-jeong-ssi said. I frowned because I didn't understand her words.

-Among the abilities of Awakened ones, there is fusion. It's the ability to temporarily fuse something with oneself. Bio-machines and bio-metals are particularly suitable.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Manroe (卍 Thunder).

Lightning gathers and rotates in the shape of a swastika (卍) on my left hand. I then shot Manroe at him. The blue lightning accurately hit him.

Smoke and the smell of burning meat came from his body. But he didn't fall and looked back at me.

,Did that mass of flesh act as armor?

It's not ordinary flesh. It's bio-machines and bio-metal.

He stretched out his left hand as I had done. His thick palm split open, and a bio-metal muzzle popped out, firing bone bullets. It was slower than a bullet, so I knocked it away with my sword.

Kwang!

,Ugh.

The bone bullet was slow, but it contained tremendous power. My arm felt like it was going to break. Again, he fired a bone bullet. This time, I didn't knock it away and dodged it.

,With this much power and physique... it's hard for me to deal with him in my current state.

I reached for the drug injector worn on my left forearm. The ampule attached to the injector was X-cream melted into a liquid.

When I pressed the button on the injector, a small needle stabbed into my forearm. The drug entered my body.

Biiiiiiiiiiip.

I heard tinnitus and lost my hearing. I couldn't hear any other sounds except for the tinnitus. Other senses were more sharply activated, and my heart beat rapidly. My senses sharpened, and my thinking speed increased. His bone bullets also looked much slower than before.

After easily dodging the bone bullet, I sent electricity through the cutlass.

Uuuuuuung.

I couldn't hear the sound, but I felt the vibration.

The cutlass shone silver and became even sharper.

I rushed towards him. He reflexively swung his arm.

,As heavy as he is, he's slow. I can see everything. First, I'll cut off that annoying arm.

The sword dug into the flesh and stopped. It was too thick and tough to cut through at once. Moreover, it grabbed the sword with tremendous viscosity and wouldn't let go. His other hand came crashing down. I gave up the sword and retreated.

,Irina. Are you watching from above? Can you snipe? No, snipe him!

Biiiiiiiiiiip.

I couldn't hear the answer because of the tinnitus. But my voice was conveyed.

Irina's arrow fell on him from the 5th floor of the main building. The arrow pierced his body and exploded. Bang! Some of his flesh splattered.

,Is there a limit even with explosive arrows.

Choi Hye-jin revealed her upper body from the 5th floor and threw something. A white sword. It's a spare cutlass. As I grabbed the falling hilt and generated electricity, the sword instantly shone silver.

,Irina. It's an electric arrow.

Pak. Papapak.

Arrows were successively embedded in the nearby ground. Arrows with small batteries attached. They can also be used to electrocute the target, but that's not the purpose now.

I generate lightning. I drew electricity from the batteries attached to the arrows and gathered it on the blade. Did he sense the danger? He runs towards me, koong, koong, koong.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Lightning Gale Blade (雷罡刃).

I swung the blade of lightning, temporarily created by using a massive amount of electricity, at him.

One-hit kill.

The huge mass of flesh split in half.

They are Japanese. They probably joined hands with Jeong Woo-hyun.
