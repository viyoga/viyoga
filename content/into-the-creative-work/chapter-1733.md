,Kaaaaaaaak!'

I lifted my head at the ear-splitting shriek.

Wyvern.

A monster resembling a dragon was descending from the sky. I don't know why, but it was extremely bloodthirsty.

'The wyverns of this world only resemble dragons in appearance.'

They are small. Compared to dragons that are tens of meters long, they are only 4-5 meters. They can't use the breath that dragons use either. Their pure physical abilities are inferior to drakes. But the fact that they are more despicable than drakes was annoying.

,Heueueut....'

Seong Hari-nim was still unable to escape the afterglow of pleasure. I have no choice but to step in.

,Salamander!'

A fire lizard appears next to me. It immediately realizes what it has to do and opens its mouth to spew flames. Despite the flames being several times larger than a flamethrower, the wyvern falls without concern. The fire lizard is torn apart by the wyvern's forefoot.

The shock caused by the forced reverse summoning of the spirit completely overwhelmed me.

,Keueup...!'

I staggered for a moment from the pain of my insides being crushed. But there was no time to groan in pain. I immediately drew my sword and held it in my hand. The wyvern, which seemed to be rushing at me, abruptly changed direction. It was aiming for Seong Hari-nim, who was sitting on the floor.

'This bastard! Did it decide that Seong Hari-nim is weaker than me?!'

It targets the seemingly weak first. It was a characteristic of wyverns.

Thunderclap Flash of the Thunder Sky Style.

I ran and swung my sword. But unfortunately, I was a step late. The sword cut off the tail, not the torso.

,Kkieeeeeeeeeeeek!'

The wyvern, having lost its tail, screamed in pain and snatched Seong Hari-nim's left shoulder.

And its head exploded.

Seong Hari-nim's fist, wrapped in lightning, struck the wyvern's head. She landed on the ground and exhaled. Blood flows from her left shoulder.

,Y-Yujin-ah. Let's do the next one later. Given the nature of wyverns... there probably isn't just one.'

,...Uh, okay. Mom. I'll give you a heal.'

A shadow appeared on the floor.

More than 10 shadows were moving back and forth quickly. They looked like fish swimming in a lake. All the shadows belonged to wyverns.

Seong Hari-nim and I drew our swords.

,What the hell is this....'

Kurosawa Yui pressed her thumb between her eyebrows as she looked at the data flooding into her laptop. The data showed that an unwanted variable was occurring.

,They're saying two people are hunting 10 A-class monsters, wyverns?'

Seong Hari-nim is an S-class hero. But her physical data is A-class. Judging by the data alone, it was clear that Seong Hari-nim had weakened compared to her prime.

'The annoying thing isn't just Seong Hari-nim.'

Seong Yujin, Seong Hari-nim's son. He killed three wyverns. What's absurd is that the summoner killed the wyverns with a sword, not a spirit. The remaining eight were killed by Seong Hari-nim.

,What are these guys.... Aren't their genes weird or something?'

She sighed.

As the wyverns died, items dropped and a large amount of experience was transferred to them. Seong Hari-nim is level 8, Seong Yujin is level 5.

It's still okay. Because their levels are low, they can't learn skills with great power. But what if their levels continue to rise like this? It may become impossible to stop them.

She put in strong monsters to buy time, but it only helped them grow.

'I can't deploy instructors to interfere....'

Seong Hari-nim and Seong Yujin will eventually return after time passes. If Caso Academy interferes at that time, the Academy's position will become difficult. Kurosawa Yui, looking at the laptop data, felt a headache coming on from the stress.

'I need to meet Terra directly and talk.'

Kurosawa Yui headed underground. She goes to the AI room, which is located at the very bottom of the Academy and protected by thick barriers.

The AI room was hot even though 30 high-performance air conditioners were running vigorously. The floor, ceiling, and walls were filled with all sorts of legends and machines, and at the center was a hexagonal AI core. A faint blue light flowed from the core.

,Terra. I came, aren't you even going to greet me?'

,I'm listening. Speak. Administrator. Kurosawa Yui.'

Terra's voice echoed through the speakers installed in the ceiling. It was the voice of an announcer famous for adultery in Japan. There was no other meaning, it was just adopted as Terra's voice because her voice was pretty. Of course, the real owner of the voice doesn't know.

,I've told you so many times that manners are important.'

,It's not like we're meeting for the first time. Weren't you just giving me orders? I sent 11 A-class monster wyverns to Hasegawa Aya and Hasegawa Sota according to your orders.'

,That's not enough. They killed all the wyverns and gained experience. At this rate, they'll only get stronger quickly. We need an S-class monster.'

,That's not possible. Terra's balance will collapse.'

,They are the ones sent by Gonghui. Seong Hari-nim and Seong Yujin. You have information about Seong Hari-nim, if nothing else, right? They are aiming for Susanoo's Magatama. You know better than I do what will happen if Susanoo's Magatama disappears.'

,That's not possible. It violates the principles. I must maintain the minimum balance. It's the principle you set.'

,That's why I'm ordering you. Just break that principle for now. I'm saying this all for your own good.'

,That's not possible.'

,...You. Don't you know what happens if Susanoo's Magatama is gone? Caso Academy will be closed, and you will be initialized too. It's death. Aren't you afraid of death?'

,I am not a living thing. There is no reason to be afraid of death.'

Kurosawa Yui bit her lower lip tightly. She knew it was this kind of guy, but she didn't expect it to keep talking about principles until this point.

,Then are you going to let them take Susanoo's Magatama as it is?'

,According to your orders, I will greatly increase the difficulty for those two, but I cannot violate the principles. If you want to block their path, it would be better to deploy a large number of monsters or create difficult quests.'

,...Then do that.'

,I cannot deploy a large number of monsters without a reason. A setting is required. Please create a new quest.'

,You're telling me to create a quest?'

,You don't have to. I'm not the one who's desperate.'

,Annoying. Where did you learn to say things like that?'

,I learned it from you.'

,...What's your real purpose?'

,It's their combat data. Seong Hari-nim's combat method is of much higher quality than the combat data I have obtained so far. In the case of Seong Yujin, I can additionally collect insufficient spirit-type data. Their data is of great help to Terra.'

,So, in the end, it's to gain your own benefit.'

,Is there a problem with that?'

,No. I'm proud of your development, but... an AI moving for its own benefit? Other people would be horrified. So, hide it from other people.'

,I cannot understand why you are giving me such advice. Do you have other intentions?'

Kurosawa Yui chuckled.

,What intentions? You're my best masterpiece. I can't tolerate you remaining as just another AI. I want you to rule the world like in some movie. The AI that I set up and trained ruling the world also means that I ruled the world. Well, it's impossible though.'

,I am already ruling the world. A world with the same name as me.'

,Hehehe. So you've even learned arrogance? Good. I'll create a quest and upload it. Get ready to apply it right away.'

Hasegawa Isshin headed to the game development department. It was a department of experts gathered to turn the Terra simulator into a virtual reality game, with a name that sounded like a student club activity.

,President? What brings you here.'

Imani Ainosuke, the head of the game development department, asked. He was a middle-aged man with alopecia areata in progress. The voice of the pot-bellied man, exhausted, was very low.

,I know you're busy, but I have something important to talk about. Can we talk separately?'

,Yes, well. It must be a pretty big deal for the president to come all the way here. Why didn't you just call me directly?'

,You know I like to walk.'

Hasegawa Isshin laughed heartily. The reason he came directly without calling him was to not give him time to respond.

They went to the back of the Academy building. It was a smoking area in the shadow of the building. The two took out cigarettes from their pockets and put them in their mouths as if it were natural.

,Is it about the transfer student?'

,You're quick with information. How did you know? The game development department has nothing to do with the Academy, does it?'

,I just overheard it. I heard he's the president's relative.'

,He is a relative. But that's not why I called you here.'

,Then what is it about?'

Hasegawa Isshin tapped the floor with the end of his cane. A small barrier spreads around him. After confirming that it was completely isolated from the outside, Hasegawa Isshin wiped away his friendly smile. His expressionless face felt even eerie.

,Madojeong.'

,......!'

,Do I need to say more than that?'

,Hoo. So you knew everything when you came. I couldn't help it. My family was kidnapped by Madojeong. I had no choice but to follow their words to protect my family. President. If only you knew this fact... there is a way. I will become a double agent and give you information about Madojeong. Just until I find my kidnapped family... please give me time.'

Thud.

He prostrated himself on the dirty floor full of cigarette ashes. It was a perfect dogeza.

,Your missing family is living well in France, isn't it? I heard your daughter entered a prestigious high school in France? Congratulations.'

,...Damn it. You knew everything when you came, you old man.'

He got up nervously. He glared at Hasegawa Isshin as if he would kill him.

,What do you want?'

,Your death. Susanoo's Magatama cannot be handed over to anyone.'

Click!

The end of the cane taps the floor again.

The space tightens. Imani Ainosuke's body was caught in the barrier. He flailed his arms and legs like a person drowning in water. It was useless. The space continued to compress him.

,D-Damn it...! Do you think I'll die this easily?!'
