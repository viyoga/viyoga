"Those goddamn Joseon bastards…."

Yamahata Mazda, the Prime Minister of Japan, glared at the paper in his hand and ground his teeth.

Three hours ago, it was a declaration of war dangling from a drone that had crossed over from Korea. It had been discovered on the Niigata coast, and it had taken a full three hours before it finally reached his hands.

There was nothing to be done about it. Japan right now was pure chaos thanks to Korea's preemptive strike. It was only because Japan had been preparing for war for so long that they could even begin to regain control this quickly.

The contents of the declaration? Nothing special. It blamed Japan entirely for the cause of this war, unilaterally condemned them, and declared war—that was the whole of it.

The truth was, the contents didn't matter one bit. In an era where survival had become the paramount objective, something like a declaration of war was a triviality. There was no international body left to condemn or sanction a war started without one.

There was only one thing that had Mazda grinding his teeth.

"That a country like Korea would dare strike Japan first…!"

Mazda crumpled the Korean declaration of war and hurled it aside, then turned his eyes to the monitor mounted on the wall. On the screen was a map of the Japanese archipelago. Updated in real time, Japan's territories shifted between red and yellow.

Red meant a critical situation.

Yellow meant there was damage, but it wasn't critical.

"Missiles fell on the major cities as well, but the damage is nearly nonexistent—that's a relief. Over half the military bases were hit, though…."

Fortunately, they had anticipated Korea's missile attack, so preparations had been made—relocating critical materiel underground, and so on. On top of that, troops had been concentrated elsewhere in preparation for war, so the actual damage was far less than what appeared on screen.

Mazda tapped lightly at the map on the display.

A small window popped up, streaming live footage of the situation at that location. He felt a quiet swell of pride.

"Japan's scientific and technological prowess is not yet dead."

The screen showed the city of Tokyo. Fires burned throughout the city.

He touched the red military base zone. A burning military base appeared in real time. But beside it, armed Self-Defense Force personnel were preparing to march.

Mazda nodded gravely.

"The Yamato spirit of Japan does not break simply because a few buildings burn."

With a stern expression, he tapped the Fukuoka coastline. He had received the report that the Maritime Self-Defense Force fleet had been sunk. Regrettable, but he accepted it with composure. Everyone knew the era of the navy was over.

“…….”

The situation on the coast was horrific.

Human corpses lay scattered like garbage. At the center of it all, the wreckage of machines and the corpses of mutated creatures stood out as alien and wrong.

*'Ttanggeomi, was it? A robot that walked along the seafloor, invading all across Japan, they said.'*

The damage wasn't that severe.

The Ttanggeomi focused its attacks on military bases rather than civilian areas, and a properly armed company-sized force could handle them. Furthermore, Japan could deploy the mutated creatures they had created and tamed to counter them.

That was when it happened.

Tsushima—the Japanese territory closest to Korea—was stained black.

That meant only one thing. Five hours into the war, Tsushima had been captured. Mazda did not waver. He had anticipated that Tsushima would be the first to fall to Korea. If anything, it was slightly late.

He spoke to the secretaries waiting on standby.

"How did Tsushima fall? Is there footage?"

"Yes, sir. There is recorded footage from before the cameras on Tsushima were destroyed."

"Put it on screen."

Missiles had fallen on Tsushima as well, and the military base had been half-destroyed. Japanese soldiers scrambled to contain the damage and focused on defense. Then, from the sky, a massive transport helicopter that looked like an enormous drone appeared. It flew over Tsushima on four spinning rotors.

The transport helicopter's doors opened, and knights in power armor descended onto Tsushima. Black power armor bearing an eagle insignia. The 2nd Knight Order—the Eagles.

What followed was not war but slaughter. Ordinary soldiers were no match for the Knight Order. In less than ten minutes, the Japanese forces were annihilated.

A single knight approached and shot the camera, and the screen went dark.

"So Korea's Knight Orders—their most elite combat force—have already been deployed. From the fact that Korea is also aiming for a short decisive war, that's a relief."

No matter how devastating the preemptive strike, Japan was not a nation that would simply sit and take it.

"Detonate the Hakujitsu we secretly planted on Tsushima."

Hakujitsu was a virus created by Japan.

When an ordinary person is infected, they languish for a hundred days before dying. The symptoms resemble a cold, with the distinguishing feature being that a small amount of blood mixes with the cough.

It was a virus deliberately engineered with lowered lethality. Japan's objective was not to conquer Korea but to dominate and absorb it.

"P-Prime Minister!"

One of his secretaries burst breathlessly into the room. He furrowed his brow slightly and asked.

"What is it?"

"Th-there's a rebellion in Kyoto! The S Clone unit has risen up in revolt alongside the experimental mutated creatures!"

"What?!"

Kyoto's military base was connected to a secret research facility. Though Kyoto was a major city, it was a place under complete control. The Prime Minister had privately held plans to relocate Japan's capital to Kyoto.

"W-wait. Isn't Q in Kyoto?! Is Q safe?!"

One of the most important figures in present-day Japan. Only with Q could one become younger. Even the Prime Minister had only managed to shed three years of age through his power.

"The ringleader of the rebellion is Q! It is presumed that he used his ability to undo the brainwashing on the S Clone soldiers and the experimental mutated creatures!"

"Nonsense! Restoration abilities cannot undo brainwashing! That's already been proven through experimentation…."

Mazda's voice trailed off. Q possessed 5-star potential. He was different from the other clones. If he had deliberately pretended to be unable to undo brainwashing—

"…Was he deceiving us from the very beginning?! What were the soldiers monitoring Q doing?!"

"Missiles struck the Kyoto military base as well! It appears there was a temporary disruption in the surveillance and command systems because of it!"

"Q seized that gap to undo the S Clones' brainwashing? Unbelievable. What's the current situation in Kyoto?!"

"Th-there's camera footage!"

The secretary played the footage.

Kyoto was a complete shitshow.

S Clones roamed the city, shooting civilians without hesitation. When they found a young woman, they stripped her on the spot and raped her. The mutated creatures that had been brainwashed for experimentation also ran wild, devouring humans. The experimental mutated creatures possessed a degree of intelligence and cooperated with the S Clone unit. The mutated creatures that ran wild on pure instinct were subdued and executed by the clone unit and the other mutated creatures.

"I heard there were recent experiments to increase the intelligence of mutated creatures… I never thought it would come back to bite us like this. Oneko?! What is Oneko doing?!"

"O-Oneko is currently carrying out a war operation! I will relay the summons order!"

Mazda snapped back to his senses.

"…No, belay that. If I call Oneko back now, the war plan falls apart. The war will be extended by at least two weeks. Contact the military units near Kyoto and have them clean it up. Emphasize that Q, the ringleader of the rebellion, must be captured alive."

Even in this moment, he could not abandon his greed for youth.

"Q. What is that bastard doing right now?"

"I will find out!"

The secretary left and returned ten minutes later. Mazda felt a sense of foreboding at the pallor of the secretary's face.

"A-after checking, it appears Q departed northeast with several mutated creatures!"

"Northeast? You're saying he's coming toward Tokyo?"

Then it hit him.

Further north than Tokyo.

That an extremely dangerous individual existed in Hokkaido to the north. A monster that even Oneko's brainwashing had failed on, one that had been sealed away as the best available option. If Q's objective was to release that thing—

Mazda's face went pale, matching the secretary's complexion, as he shouted.

"Find Q and stop him! Capture is the priority, but if it's absolutely impossible, kill him! If that thing is unleashed in the middle of this war, Japan could be destroyed!"

He bellowed his orders. The startled secretaries scrambled into action.

"Prime Minister! The Korean Holy Knight Order has infiltrated Sapporo and Fukuoka! They exploited the gap after the air defense system was neutralized!"

In Japan's original war plan, the battlefield was supposed to be the Korean Peninsula. Yet somehow, only Japan was turning into a shitshow.

Mazda resisted the urge to tear his hair out.

• • •

"Your Excellency! Japan's neutralized air defense system has been restored! Also, Japan's Air Self-Defense Force has begun to move!"

Lee Yeonhee reported remotely via phone.

"So we can no longer infiltrate the Knight Orders via transport helicopter."

The Holy Knight Order that had infiltrated Japan first was the 3rd Knight Order, Silence Moon, led by Irina. They would prioritize targeting Japanese military commanders as pre-designated.

"The battlefield has shifted to the skies now. Launch the fighter jets. We must seize air superiority at all costs."

"Do not worry, Your Excellency. Korea has invested enormously in its air force for this very day, and with Dr. Na's help, we have modified our fighters to boost their performance. Barring any variables, in air power, Korea holds the advantage…."

"Why did you pause? Has a variable appeared?"

"Indeed, Your Excellency's insight is remarkable. A variable has appeared. Mutated creatures flying through the skies over Japan are attacking our forces. Judging by the mechanical equipment attached to them, they appear to be artificially created mutated creatures. In other words, biological weapons."

"They've already advanced that far? So we're at a disadvantage?"

"No. It simply means we need a little more time. Korea will seize air superiority."

"Good. I'll wait."

After gaining air superiority, I will deploy to Japan alongside the Knight Orders.

There is no need for me, the President, to move personally. Even without me stepping forward, Korea will win this war.

*'What I need right now are achievements and a myth.'*

Achievements and a myth that no one can rival.

*'The presidency is all well and good, but ultimately it's a position filled through elections. And under the Constitution of the Republic of Korea, the presidential term is five years with no re-election. I could tear up the Constitution somehow, but… rather than that, it'd be better to just change the country itself.'*

From the Republic of Korea to the Korean Empire.

Korea will become an empire.

All power will be consolidated into one, and at the very top of that power will be me.

*'The end of this war will be the beginning of the Korean Empire.'*

That was when it happened.

Lee Yeonhee's urgent voice rang out.

"Your Excellency! From the East Sea, mutated creatures led by a Grade 4 are approaching alongside Japanese forces! They will arrive within thirty minutes! Visually confirmed! An ability user or mutated creature capable of neutralizing radar is among them!"

"Have the 4th Knight Order, Atonement, the 5th Knight Order, Siren, and Army units block them. Don't forget to open the engagement with artillery bombardment."

"Gangneung is…."

"Don't fixate on a single city. It doesn't matter if we give up Gangneung. Focus on annihilating the enemy."

"Yes, Your Excellency."
