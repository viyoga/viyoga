I opened my eyes in the dead of night.

I quietly glanced at my wristwatch. 1:22 AM. Just the right time.

I carefully got up. I checked on Instructor Lexy, who was asleep next to me. She, with her bad sleeping habits, was already half-undressed. Her hands were above her head, her shirt was rolled up, and her pants were hanging precariously on her thighs.

My eyes were drawn to her small black panties. The image of Instructor Lexy's femininity that I saw a few hours ago came to mind.

,Pinkish.... No, this is not the time for this.'

I shook my head. This is an important time. I used Silence magic to eliminate the sound of my footsteps and headed towards the waterfall. Fortunately, the alarm magic was installed outside the waterfall, so there was no chance of getting caught.

I frowned when I saw the waterfall. It was very dark. It was hard to see even when I focused mana in both eyes. Especially the inside of the stream was not visible at all.

,Light.'

I used magic. A sphere of light appeared in front of me, illuminating the surroundings. I control the sphere of light with my will. The sphere of light slowly rises upward. As if they had been waiting, gnats rushed towards the sphere of light.

Seeing the shadows of the gnats running rampant, I felt the urge to burn them to death with Fire magic.

,If I had enough time, I would have.'

I wandered around near the center of the stream, not in the center. I looked down and recalled the memories from the "Dark Moon" game, looking for a special rock. I couldn't feel anything with mana detection, so I had to find it with my eyes.

,Found it.'

It's a rock that's half-submerged in the water. If you look closely at the surface, you can see faint patterns engraved on it.

Rune letters.

It is one of the magic systems that was frequently used in ancient times. In modern times, the system of formulas is well established, so rune letters, which are less efficient, are disappearing from the magic world. However, it is still hot in the fields of treasure Hunter and archeology.

,In the game, when the character got close, an activation button would automatically pop up.'

This wasn't a game, it was reality. I had to do the activation myself. I put my hand on the rock. When I carefully let mana flow, the rune letters glowed faintly.

,The fact that this is here means that the gimmicks in the game are the same.'

The secrets sleeping in this world. I know them. Feeling an indescribable sense of superiority, I continued the work.

,The second stone was on the opposite side.'

I found it easily and activated it. The third is a small rock under a tree.

,The last fourth one is behind the waterfall.'

I approached the waterfall. My clothes got wet. I just assumed it would. I can dry it with magic later.

I groped for the rock behind the waterfall with my hand. The shape of a rune letter, hollowed out on one side of the rock, was felt through the palm of my hand.

I let mana flow like I did with the other three rune letters. The rune letters glow faintly.

Kugugugugung.

The rock behind the waterfall split open to the sides like an automatic door. A space appeared that one person could enter. I released the Light magic and created a sphere of light again on my palm. The sphere of light illuminates the inside. There was a staircase leading down.

Slightly nervous, I went down.

I came down to the floor. It was blocked on all sides and I could see a hollowed-out floor. I stared intently at the floor. Triangle, square, circle, rhombus. There are a total of 4 patterns drawn.

,This is just like the game.'

As soon as I saw it, the answer came to mind. Compared to the hazy memories of Earth, the information related to the "Dark Moon" game is very clear.

,The me on Earth hated using my head. I didn't play "Dark Moon" to solve puzzles. When a puzzle came out while playing the game, I immediately searched the internet.'

That was my way of playing.

,The answer here is two triangles, two squares.'

I lifted my right foot and stepped on the triangle twice, then stepped on the square twice.

Kugugugugung.

A path leading down opened up a little further away. I solved the puzzle two more times and took my last step into the final cavity.

,The last gimmick is to light 6 torches and it's over.'

I walked around and lit 5 fixed torches. The last sixth torch is hidden. Of course, I know its location.

I tilted my head up and looked at the ceiling. A circular pattern is drawn in the center of the ceiling. That's the sixth torch.

,Fire.'

The flame reached the center of the ceiling.

Uuuuuuung.

The mana in the space fluctuates. I watched the movement of the mana and swallowed my breath. Each and every swirling mana flow was intricately set.

The walls and ceiling move with mana as power. The mana eventually gathered in the center of the ceiling. A hole was created in the center of the ceiling and two objects fell down.

It's a short sword and a red hood that look easy to hold in one hand.

,The rewards given are the same.'

I smiled with satisfaction.

The short sword is a level 4 artifact, an attribute sword. You can give the sword the attribute you want. Frankly speaking, it's not very helpful in combat. However, the fact that you can give it an attribute meant that there were many ways to use it.

,It's very helpful because attribute power is often needed when solving gimmicks.'

Of course, that was in the game. It's only level 1, but it's not really necessary for me, who can use all attribute magic.

What I was aiming for was not the short sword, but the red hood.

The Wolf's Red Hood, a level 6 artifact.

The effect of this artifact is simple. It allows you to use Transparency magic.

,The effect of Transparency magic is to deceive the character's information.'

For example, let's say there is a level 50 magician character. If you use Transparency magic, you can deceive the level, job, and even stats.

,It's possible to enter a dungeon with conditions by deceiving the character information with Transparency magic.'

It is possible to pass through spaces with job restrictions, level restrictions, and stat restrictions using Transparency magic. Of course, there are special restricted areas where Transparency magic does not work, but those only have restriction conditions different from character information.

However, what you shouldn't misunderstand is that Transparency magic only deceives information. The job and stats do not actually change.

,I can hide my level with Transparency magic.'

I stared blankly at the red hood.

This is where it gets important. I couldn't carry something like this around. I am a soldier and a slave. I cannot carry unauthorized property, and if I take it back to the unit, I will be immediately inspected.

,If I have this during the belongings inspection, it will be 100% obvious that it's an artifact.'

Moreover, it is a level 6 artifact that cannot be easily obtained even if you give billions of won.

I took a breath and pulled up mana. I start analyzing by letting mana flow into the red hood.

,I will learn the Transparency magic contained in the artifact. With my talent, it will be possible.'

No, it had to be possible.

I look at the formulas engraved inside the red hood. Complex formulas flash through my mind. I twisted my lips.

,It's different from the magic formulas I already know. Is it an illusion-type that twists perception and senses?'

To analyze it in more detail, it was necessary to disassemble the red hood's formula. Only then could I examine the formulas in the deepest parts thoroughly.

,...If I do that, I won't be able to use the artifact again. I only have one chance. Failure is the end.'

Failure once is the end. Feeling my wavering resolve, I reaffirmed my resolve.

,I can't stop here. I need Transparency magic for my goal. I believe in myself, in my talent.'

I concentrated my mind.

My mana invades deep into the red hood. Fortunately, there was no security formula, so I could examine the formula as it was without being disturbed.

Gigigigigik, gigigik.

Of course, just looking at it is not enough. I have to disassemble the overlapping formula structure and understand each and every meaning. In this process, the artifact begins to break down.

,The development method of the formula is... spinning around. It's close to a Möbius strip shape that twists the circle shape once.'

With this development method, the formula does not escape to the outside and circles around.

,...There's no need to escape. Because what you have to deceive is your own information. That's right. It's easier to maintain the magic if you develop it in the shape of a Möbius strip.'

I explored the Transparency magic formula, sweating profusely on my forehead.

When I finished, more than 2 hours had passed. Fragments of the red hood were scattered at my feet. No magical power is felt. The artifact became trash.

,Transparency. It's ambiguous to determine exactly what level it is.'

I modified some of the Transparency magic formula to suit me. I focused on hiding rather than deceiving. Thanks to that, the difficulty of the magic dropped significantly.

,Even so, it needs to be at least level 3 to use it smoothly.'

I closed my eyes.

I urge the growth of the Astral that I had stopped.

The quality of the Astral increases and expands. Without ending there, the Astral began to take root in the world. This vast world acknowledged my existence. No, I acknowledged this world.

I slowly opened my eyes. I can feel the existence of the 25 mana rods rooted in my body. The flow of mana was refreshing.

,Transparency.'

I use magic to hide my skills and level. The influence of the Astral disappears, and 7 mana rods disappear. Outwardly, it will look like level 2.

,Perfect. Even I, who cast the magic, would be deceived.'

There was a disadvantage.

The moment I use the power I hid with Transparency, the effect of Transparency is released.

,I don't plan on using level 3 magic in front of others anyway, so it's not a big disadvantage.'

I achieved my goal.

I took the attribute sword and walked towards the stairs.

,If I take the attribute sword with me, it will be taken away by the unit, so I should hide it here. I can come get it later when I have the chance.'

I came up and looked around with Light magic.

Water slimes, which weren't there until just now, are bouncing towards me, splashing their bodies. I wasn't flustered. It was something I already knew. The reason I brought the attribute sword was to deal with these guys.

I pushed mana into the attribute sword.

The attribute I want is ice.

White cold air flows through the sword.

I swung the sword at the water slime.

Hwik.

The sword cut through the air. The water slime jumped and dodged my attack. My expression contorted with annoyance.

The water slime rushed at my head. The barrier I had prepared bounced the water slime away. Other water slimes are jumping and closing the distance.

,Shibal. A slime dodged my attack? Is my swordsmanship that terrible?'

I knew that I had no talent for physical activities, but the fact that even slimes were dodging my attacks so easily made me lose motivation. The thought of learning swordsmanship disappeared far away.

I checked that my mana was connected to the attribute sword and let go of the sword handle. The sword floated in the air without falling to the floor.

Level 3 non-attribute magic, telekinesis.

I wiggled my right index finger. The attribute sword flew towards the water slime and pierced it. The cold air of the attribute sword permeated the water slime. Jjeojeong. The instantly frozen water slime shattered and fell to the floor.

,If I'm not confident in swinging the sword with my hand, I can swing the sword with magic like this. It's much easier. However, telekinesis has the worst mental power efficiency compared to other magic, so it doesn't last long... um?'

I tilted my head.

Magic is usually activated with a ratio of 2 mental power and 8 mana. However, telekinesis, which is level 3 magic, has a ratio of 9 mental power and 1 mana. Thanks to this, mana consumption is low, but mental power runs out quickly, so magicians avoid it.

,The mental power consumed decreases as the proficiency of the magic increases, but telekinesis is different.'

Telekinesis has a simple formula. First of all, willpower is more important than the formula. It is literally telekinesis (念力).

,But why am I okay even though I used telekinesis? Is telekinesis right for me? Come to think of it, I don't think I've ever lacked mental power when using other magic.'

The water slime is approaching. I put my thoughts aside and wiggled my finger. The attribute sword containing telekinesis flies through the air and cuts down the water slime.

,It's definitely twice as easy as holding the sword handle and swinging it myself.'

I changed the sword's attribute to fire (火). Flames erupted from the sword. I spun the sword through the air like a wheel. The flaming wheel turned, sending sparks flying.

,Movements that are impossible when holding the sword are possible with telekinesis.'

The rotating attribute sword mercilessly grinds the water slime. Water vapor was thick.

,This time it's wind.'

The hazy water vapor is tossed around by the blowing wind. The wind blade sliced the water slime. The water slime's liquid splattered everywhere.

,Water is not good, should I try electricity.'

Pajijijik.

A blue current flows through the sword. The attribute sword left an electrical trail in the air and dealt with all the remaining water slimes. The dead water slime became a puddle of water and soaked the floor.

I stuck the attribute sword into the floor. The electric current spread through the puddle and across the entire floor. The last one was a kind of show.

,Telekinesis and attribute sword.... It's useful?'
