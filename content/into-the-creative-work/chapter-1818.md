,Looks like you've hit a wall."

Baekran said as soon as I opened my eyes. A surge of heat flared up momentarily, but I quietly exhaled, controlling my anger. She didn't really mean to be sarcastic. She was just annoyed that I couldn't break through the wall.

,I sensed Demonic energy from you. It definitely wasn't the Demonic energy of the True Demon Sword Art. It's similar to Cheonma Shingong, yet different... Just what kind of Demonic martial art have you mastered?"

,Cheonma Shingong."

,Hmm. You don't want to answer, do you? Understood. It was a sensitive question, so I apologize."

Even if I tell her the truth, she won't believe me. That's because the Cheonma Shingong I possess is similar to, yet different from, the Cheonma Shingong she knows.

,Let me ask you something too."

,Go ahead."

,How did you break through the Wall of the Three Flowers?"

Baekran tilted her head slowly, as if she didn't understand what I was saying. Then, she exclaimed, ,Ah!'

,You're mistaken. I haven't broken through the Wall of the Three Flowers."

,I saw you circulating your Qi. Three flowers faintly appeared above your head. Isn't that proof of the Three Flowers Gather on the Crown?"

,Didn't you say they were faint? If I had truly reached the realm of the Three Flowers, the three flowers would have been clear. Right now, I'm... I'd say I'm halfway over the wall."

,So, you can cross over the realm whenever you want?"

Baekran is a regressor. Since the setting of [Cheonma's Revenge] isn't Xianxia, I can't know what realm she reached before regressing. What's certain is that she was connected to the Underworld even before her regression... What's certain is that she was also a Cheonma, so she must have reached a realm beyond the Three Flowers.

,If my lacking is filled, the Three Flowers will bloom."

,What is that lacking?"

,Who knows?"

Baekran smiled mysteriously. She didn't seem to have any intention of telling me about her weaknesses.

We approached the building that could be said to be the medium of the barrier.

,What was that?"

,What are you talking about?"

,The black doppelganger. It turned the hairpin into a black heart. Is it a magical artifact?"

,It's not a magical artifact. It's magic. It's different from sorcery. Perhaps you want to learn magic? I can teach you the basics, though."

,No thanks. I have no talent for sorcery."

,I said it's not sorcery."

,Isn't it not martial arts?"

Baekran nodded.

I completely abandoned any lingering feelings about magic.

The Cheongang Constellation System doesn't support sorcery. I'd probably not be able to cast a single spell properly even after 10 years of training. Magic probably wouldn't be much different. It's more beneficial to learn other martial arts in that time.

,If it were Qi skill, then maybe.'

That seems to fall under the martial arts category.

Kwa kwa kwa kwang!

As I swung my fist infused with Qi, the building collapsed helplessly. The barrier shook and then dissolved completely. In the distance, I could see the city lights under the dark sky.

,Ah, I didn't ask this. Where are you going and for what purpose?"

I took a small cage out of my spatial storage. Inside the cage was a Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse (萬里鼠踪) that had fallen into a catatonic state from a pressure point.

I pressed down on the small red mouse with my finger, releasing the pressure point. The Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse, awakened from its catatonic state, shook its head while squeaking and stared intently in a certain direction.

,A Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse. Were you chasing the Myriad-Li Tracking Incense?"

,I was tracking the Myriad-Li Tracking Incense at Cheon Yu-un's request. It seems some big-balled bastard broke into Cheon Yu-un's room."

,Hmph. I have something to correct. It's not a bastard, but a bitch."

,...What? Don't tell me the culprit was you? But why is the Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse pointing in a different direction?"

,I sent the clothes with the Myriad-Li Tracking Incense westward using a hawk. It seems it threw them away in a suitable place. It's worth the training."

,Why did you sneak into Cheon Yu-un's room? Was there a need to take such a risk?"

,There were two reasons. One was to find evidence that Cheon Yu-un was spreading the Reverse Demon Divine Art. But the room was clean. There was nothing. Two was to meet you. Since Cheon Yu-un probably doesn't have many trustworthy subordinates right now, I knew you'd come."

,...So, you were targeting me from the start."

,As I said before, I wanted to personally confirm the variable that is you."

As expected of a regressor. She saw right through Cheon Yu-un. I didn't feel too good. It felt like I was being played in the palm of her hand. But since she's my helper, I have to endure it.

,About the Mukjilian. You're not going to leave those bastards alone, are you?"

,It's best to deal with the Mukjilian and Cheon Yu-un together."

,...The Mukjilian has agreed to help me. I've already made a contract with the Mukjilian Lord."

,Hmph. So, the Mukjilian Lord and Cheon Yu-un have already fallen out... This could make things go even smoother. I'll try to think of it positively."

We headed towards the Yun family. Baekran confidently walked towards the chaotic gate of the Yun family. The gatekeepers, on guard, shouted.

,Stop! What brings you here at this late hour?! State your name and affiliation!"

,I am Baekran. A First-Class Imperial Guard from the Imperial family. I have come to investigate the Yun family under the order of His Majesty the Emperor, so open the gate."

Baekran took out a golden, sparkling cloth from her chest and showed it to the gatekeepers. The Emperor's seal shone brilliantly in rainbow colors.

,A First-Class Imperial Guard?!"

The gatekeeper was shocked and took a step back. But he was immediately blocked by the gate.

,I, I'll inform the elders, so please wait."

,Did I say to wait? Me, who is moving under the order of His Majesty the Emperor?"

Baekran lowered her voice as if displeased and glared. The gatekeeper's complexion turned blue.

,I've come because I know that the Yun family's head, Yun Gong-seok, has died while working with a traitor. Open the gate. If you delay even a little... I will treat you as traitors."

Traitor.

His face turns from pale to dark.

I know the reason too. The compassionate Emperor absolutely could not forgive traitors. The moment you even plot treason, any family is finished. Moreover, there was a rumor that an entire city was wiped out because of one traitor decades ago. The rumor was strangely specific...

The gatekeeper opened the gate with trembling hands. All the buildings inside the gate were lit up. It was probably because of the dead family head. These bastards probably haven't even found the family head's body yet.

,A First-Class Imperial Guard from the Imperial family has arrived!!!"

The gatekeeper shouted, putting strength into his stomach. The gatekeeper had excellent improvisation skills.

There's no way Baekran wouldn't know what I noticed. Baekran leisurely stepped inside the gate. Then, people rushed out. Some from the windows of the 4-story building, some from the end of the 1st-floor windows, some from the outhouse. They even used lightness skill to rush over and immediately prostrated themselves before Baekran.

,Yeah. You have to jump out even while taking a dump! Kyaa! This is power!'

I watch the power of authority from behind her. It was very thrilling.

One of them approached, showing politeness. His body trembled slightly as he performed the cupped fist salute.

,I am Yun Seop, the Young Lord of the Yun family."

,I am First-Class Imperial Guard Baekran. The Yun family's head, Yun Gong-seok, is suspected of treason. I've heard that the Demonic families are allied families... Have you six families plotted a rebellion?"

,Absolutely! Absolutely not! There must be some kind of misunderstanding!"

Yun Seop was startled and denied it.

,It is my job to confirm that. Send a messenger hawk to the other five families and have them gather at the Yun family within a week. You can refuse if you want. However, in that case..."

,I will send a messenger hawk right away! H, However... The Cheon family will draw a line with us."

,Isn't the Cheon family a Demonic family?"

They call themselves a Demonic Sect family (魔宗家), not a Demonic family (魔家), and do not associate with us.

,The Cheon family is the Cheonma Divine Church. I will visit them myself later... The other four families had better gather quickly. I don't like waiting."

,They will gather within three days! Baekran-nim, the wind is cold, please come inside! We will serve you!"

Baekran and I received the utmost hospitality from the Yun family. Baekran, who was left alone with me, sipped her tea and smiled.

,How is it? Things are going well, aren't they?"

,The power of a First-Class Imperial Guard is amazing."

,It's all power that comes from the Emperor. Perhaps by tomorrow, the commotion today will reach Cheon Yu-un's ears. As well as you and I being together."

,You deliberately showed me to the Yun family. Why? Cheon Yu-un will be wary."

,That way, even if Cheon Yu-un and the Cheonma Divine Church are wary of you, they won't be able to treat you carelessly. I, and the Demonic families, must be your backing for you to aim for the position of Little Cheonma."

,I see... But do I have to keep staying here?"

,Stay until tomorrow. It would be strange if you suddenly disappeared. Tomorrow, I'll tell you the location of the Cheonma Shingong, so go there. Give me the Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse."

,Why the Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse?"

,There are many uses for it. I'm not planning to receive it for free. 10 Cheonok. How is it? It's quite a good price."

,No need. It's a gift."

I handed her the Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse. There's no one who doesn't like gifts. Baekran looked back and forth between the Myriad-Li Tracing Mouse and me for a moment, then smiled and accepted it.

,Thank you. Ah, come to the Cheonma Divine Church in fifteen days. Exactly fifteen days. It can't be earlier than that, and it can't be later than that. That will be the optimal time."

Fifteen days.

Excluding travel time, it won't even be ten days. She's telling me to master the Cheonma Shingong in that time.

,Hmm. It doesn't seem impossible.'

Because I absorbed the Cheonma Shingong of [Otherworld Cheonma] in an instant. Even if I can't absorb it like that time, I have the Cheongang Constellation System.

The next day in the afternoon. I received a map from Baekran. I unfolded the map. It was at the level of doodles. She doesn't seem to have talent in this area.

The Cheonma Burial Mound (天魔陵) isn't far from here. If you go straight in this direction, you'll reach the Cheonma Burial Mound. You can find the other sites on this map. I wish you good luck.

,Finally, the Cheonma Shingong will be in my hands."

,Fifteen days. Be sure to come in fifteen days. Never be late, and never be early."

,I understand even if you don't repeat it."

,Never forget. Don't get too absorbed in the Cheonma Shingong. It's not too late to delve deeply into the Cheonma Shingong after the job is done."

,Yeah. Yeah."

I looked down at Baekran. My gaze went to her moist and soft-looking pink lips. I wanted to give her a farewell kiss, but if I did, a sword would fly.

I exchanged a short greeting with her and ran towards the Cheonma Burial Mound.
