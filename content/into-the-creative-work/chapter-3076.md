"We must absolutely join the US-led Taiwan liberation operation."

Lee Yeonhee spoke with an utterly serious expression. I understood what she was saying.

"The justification is too strong. If we don't cooperate with this, we'll be disqualified as Earthlings and become public enemies."

"Yes. That's right. China and Japan have no choice but to cooperate as well. Japan is pretending otherwise while keeping an eye on the US, and China considers Taiwan its own territory. They don't want Taiwan being trampled by alien forces either."

"Is the US asking for military support?"

Taiwan sits below Korea and Japan, beside China, above the Philippines. Other countries aside, East Asia absolutely cannot ignore this matter.

"That's part of it, but what the US wants is Hunter support. The goal is to avoid all-out war as much as possible and eliminate Taiwan's Governor Garshin as quickly as possible."

Kill the governor and the occupied nation is immediately liberated. It was information you could get with a little legwork in the Tower's public zones.

"It's the fastest, lowest-risk method. Handle it yourselves."

I spoke in a lukewarm tone. There was no particular reason for me to step in personally on this one. It wasn't as if Korea lacked S-rank Hunters, and there was also the option of Lee Yeonhee going herself.

"Would you not consider moving personally, Your Excellency?"

"Why?"

"Taiwan's President Yang Hae-wi is alive, and he has publicly declared that he will reward generously if this operation succeeds. Furthermore, we've confirmed that Taiwan currently possesses a top-grade summoning stone. The Taiwanese President told me directly."

A top-grade summoning stone. My eyes widened at the item I'd been coveting.

"So they know Korea is after the top-grade summoning stone."

"Yes. It seems information leaked when NIS agents were investigating the top-grade summoning stone."

Korea is after it.

There were plenty of parties who would jump at just that piece of information. Japan and China being prime examples.

The calculator in my head spun rapidly.

One item. Multiple interested parties. In situations like this, rewards typically differ based on contribution. It's common sense and proper order that whoever contributes the most claims the best prize.

"Korea's S-rank Hunters alone... won't be enough."

"Yes. It's embarrassing to say, but the level of Korea's S-rank Hunters is below average on a global scale. For me to participate in this operation... I'm worried about Japan. They could do something while I'm away from Korea. They might even target the Korean President directly. The worst-case scenario would be a traitor killing the President and seizing the position of head of state through a coup."

I wondered if even Japan would go that far, but then I reminded myself that Japan's current head of state, Tachibana Hayase, was no ordinary lunatic.

"Me stepping in personally means... revealing my identity to the world?"

"Even if Your Excellency doesn't move personally, it would be sufficient for Lady Yuria or Lady Elena to act. And as for Your Excellency's identity, everyone who needs to know already knows."

That was true. Japan and China were brazen enough to try killing me outright. More than anything, I had no intention of thoroughly hiding my identity. There was no one in this world, on this Earth, who could pose a threat to me. I simply didn't step forward openly because it was troublesome.

"Fine. Since a top-grade summoning stone is on the line. Let's pay Taiwan a visit."

Sending just Elena and Yuria would resolve the matter. But wouldn't it be boring being there alone?

'Might as well do some sightseeing in Taiwan while I'm at it.'

Due to the effect of my unique ability 'Character Summon,' summoned creatures near me grow stronger through a buff effect.

"By the way, how strong is this Garshin guy?"

"Taiwan's S-rank Hunter fought him one-on-one and died without lasting even a minute. He's estimated to be at least Level 70."

"He's a handful that Earth's Hunters can't handle."

That was Transcendent-level in the Tower. A low-level Transcendent, of course. Level 70 was the minimum threshold.

For reference, to receive proper treatment as a Transcendent in the Tower, you needed to be Level 80 or above, and reaching Level 85 or higher earned recognition as a strong fighter even among Transcendents.

'I can't get a clear read on exactly how strong this Garshin is. It might be tough.'

I thought it might be better to just bombard Garshin with dozens of missiles, but... Garshin was currently in Taipei, Taiwan's capital. I couldn't kill hundreds of thousands of civilians just to take out one Garshin.

'Bringing Wiyu would solve it immediately, but... I can't be an immature disciple who asks his master for help with something like this.'

The opponent was a Transcendent, so I couldn't let my guard down.

"When's the operation date?"

"Three days from now. It's taking time since Hunters from around the world are gathering. Fortunately for us, the rally point is the southern sea below Jeju Island, so we have some time."

"Is that so? First, I should replenish my forces."

"Replenish your forces, you say...?"

"Random Summon."

Lee Yeonhee's expression stiffened.

My unique ability 'Character Summon' has two types: Random Summon and Bond Summon. Bond Summon is a guaranteed summon that consumes a top-grade summoning stone. Random Summon summons a random character for a cost of 10,000 coins.

Just 10,000 coins. In theory, Random Summon could summon thousands of characters.

'The downside of Random Summon is that you never know what character will appear.'

Hadn't I already experienced this in reality?

'Levre. A cosmic entity that parasitizes planets and eventually devours them entirely. That kind of thing can be summoned in droves. And there's no proper means to control them.'

Random Summon only summons.

Among characters, there are those that cause harm just by existing.

'Even if not that extreme, there could be some who obey me immediately upon summoning but harbor different thoughts inside.'

I considered doing it in the Tower, but decided to do it here.

Because Wiyu, Elena, Yuria, and Lee Yeonhee were here.

『Using 10,000 coins to activate Random Summon. Random Summon can be attempted again in 10 days.』

'Ah right. Random Summon has a 10-day cooldown.'

A ring of light appeared before my eyes. Light gathered around the ring, forming a human shape.

I felt slightly relieved here.

'At least it's not a monster.'

The intensity of the brilliantly shining light diminished, and details formed on the human shape.

A sturdy height and thick build. Just looking at it, it was clearly a man's body.

'Fuck. Not a female character. If he's useless, immediate unsummon.'

Then the light disappeared and the face was revealed.

It was a face I knew.

『Character Seong Jigon from 'Path of Radiant Ascension' is summoned.』

The man clad in armor laughed cheerfully.

"Haha. Long time no see, Yujin."

『Name: Seong Jigon

Level: 1

Summoner: Seong Yujin.

Summon maintenance cost: 1,000 coins per 24 hours.』

"Jigon!"

I extended my hand to Seong Jigon, my brother and friend from the world of 'Path of Radiant Ascension.' Seong Jigon high-fived me as if it were the most natural thing.

"Seong Jigon! I never thought you'd be the one summoned."

"I didn't know I'd be summoned like this either. You really are special, as expected."

Seong Jigon was a man I could trust. Seeing him brought back old memories. Memories of the days when we used to fuck each other's mothers!

I immediately raised Seong Jigon's level.

『Name: Seong Jigon

Level: 60

Summoner: Seong Yujin.

Summon maintenance cost: 60,000 coins per 24 hours.』

Approximately 340 million coins were consumed. No regrets. Seong Jigon had risen to the rank of General in the world of 'Path of Radiant Ascension.' He was not weak.

"Jigon. I need you to help me out."

"Just say the word. If it's something I can do, I'll help with everything I've got."

Seong Jigon spoke with a solemn face, touching the sword at his waist. A heavy aura, like Mount Tai itself, radiated from him.

"Relax. We're not fighting right now. Let me introduce you to my women."

"Nice to meet you. I am Yuria, the master's maid and wife."

"Elena Valdert. I am Yujin's wife and a noble, but... in this world, I'm simply Yujin's wife. I never expected Yujin to have a male friend."

"I'm Lee Yeonhee. Seong Jigon, please help us for His Excellency and for Korea."

Seong Jigon greeted my women warmly, but his expression stiffened when he stood before Wiyu. He must have sensed Wiyu's essence, the overwhelming gap in their levels, even if only slightly.

"This is Wiyu. Just let that one's womanizing talk go in one ear and out the other. You've got considerable talent yourself."

"So you are Yujin's master. I've heard much about you. It is an honor to meet the Moon-Slaying Divine Maiden who cut down the moon."

"Enough with the excessive courtesy. I am not the Moon-Slaying Divine Maiden in this world."

"Lady Wiyu. May I ask for a spar sometime later?"

Seong Jigon's bond level was 7. He couldn't remember the events of this world. He probably knew that himself. Yet the reason he asked Wiyu for a spar was surely because he was a martial artist.

"A martial artist asks me for instruction—how could I refuse? Tell me when you're ready."

Wiyu was never stingy about imparting teachings.

"Lee Yeonhee. Find Jigon a decent place to stay nearby."

"Understood, Your Excellency."

No matter how close a friend Seong Jigon was, another man couldn't stay in my house besides me.

And that was what Seong Jigon wanted too. He'd want to live with his own women.

Ten minutes later, government officials in suits appeared at my house. They took Seong Jigon outside. He would learn this world's common sense from them.

And a few hours later.

I heard news that Seong Jigon had started volunteer work.

"Heh heh. As expected of Seong Jigon. An incorrigible upright-living kind of guy."

I heard he was even studying to get a caregiving certification.
