Pungdeong!

I fell into the sea of death along with the world.

I was trapped in death. All sorts of death seeped in.

If it weren't for the King of Cheonma, if I had fallen in here, I wouldn't have lasted even 3 seconds before dying. Not just my body, but my soul would have met a perfect death.

The King of Cheonma was enduring the death. It even felt like he was enjoying the situation.

,It's a parched death."

The King of Cheonma said. From the edge of the mandala he wore, it was slowly collapsing. I didn't know why the King of Cheonma was taking his time.

,These are all deaths I know."

I felt frustrated.

If I stayed like this, I would surely die.

This concept of death would kill my body and soul.

,Move, King of Cheonma! I don't know what will happen to you, a god... but I'm definitely going to die! If this continues, you won't be safe either.'

I couldn't know the details, but I could tell. This power works even on gods. Osiris's divinity is quite high. I felt anxious because even if I used Complete Recovery, it would be meaningless if I couldn't escape from here.

,Seong Yujin. Do you want to kill a god?"

The King of Cheonma asked.

I couldn't easily answer what he meant.

,Are you worried about what others think? It's okay. Even the system can't look in here. It's a special place where death and emptiness meet."

Suddenly, I had a thought. Could it be that the King of Cheonma deliberately fell here?

,Remember this power. Most gods can't resist this dense death. If you experience this death... you'll be able to kill most gods."

,...Do you want me to kill a god?'

,It's fine whether you kill them or not. I want to be able to see your journey to the end. You are the joy I've gained in tens of millions of years. I don't want you to be trapped in Atlantis."

The speed at which the mandala was breaking began to increase. The King of Cheonma, who had been lying down, pushed by death, rose to his feet.

,It's about time to get out of here."

The mandala shone and unfolded.

I knew why the King of Cheonma was so relaxed. His mandala was a concept higher than Osiris's death. The King of Cheonma was confident from the start that he could withstand death.

The mandala conversely devours death.

,The Cheonma(天魔) is a taker. Even if it's death, there's nothing he can't take."

Jiiiiiiiiiiing.

The mandala that stole death unfolded.

It was space. The King of Cheonma, sitting on countless shining stars, calmly looked down at the planet in front of him.

I knew that planet was Atlantis.

The King of Cheonma focused his eyes. The view gradually zoomed in, and Osiris standing in the desert was visible.

Osiris looked up at the King of Cheonma and murmured blankly.

,...Was there this much of a difference in power? It's absurd. Was he playing with me from the beginning?"

The mandala shone and rotated. The mandala of hell, linked to the universe, was all of creation itself.

Osiris's existence was slowly disappearing.

Osiris lost. From the moment he failed to kill the King of Cheonma with the source of death, his defeat was confirmed.

Source.

That's right. To kill a god, you have to overcome or scatter the god's source.

Osiris's source was death, and the King of Cheonma overcame that death.

Then what is the source of the King of Cheonma?

,Parched death, I can feel your agony and anguish. If you wanted to kill me, you should have killed your anguish first."

,I see. That is your source."

Osiris's existence disappeared.

It wasn't annihilation. Neither Osiris nor the King of Cheonma's main bodies are here. If you want to be precise, they all fought in the state of avatars. Even if the main body is hit, it does not disappear.

,Haha."

The King of Cheonma laughs.

His mandala has not disappeared and is still unfolded.

,King of Cheonma. The battle is over. Hand over my body to me.'

,You're saying disappointing things. I've come to the game board myself for the first time in a while. Shouldn't I enjoy what I can? First, shall we turn the sea upside down? After that, we'll test the faith of the clergy. Was it called the Justia Empire? Turning that place into hell wouldn't be bad either."

The King of Cheonma smiles brightly. When he raised his hand, the mandala of hell gathered.

A hand popped out of the mandala. The huge hand made of dirty and ugly things was not human, but demonic.

,You fucking bastard! Get out of my body right now!!"

The King of Cheonma still has a smile on his face.

,Don't get so angry. I'll only enjoy it a little. I'll make sure not to harm your companions or your woman."

,Hmm. Well, then it might be okay...'

When I thought about it, it didn't matter since I wasn't the one being harmed.

The King of Cheonma is using his power now, and the King of Cheonma is the one who will bear the aftermath. I'm just a victim who had my body taken over by the King of Cheonma.

I was the one who called the King of Cheonma... but I never thought things would turn out like this.

Even the excuse is perfect.

The hideous hand of the demon, bigger than a mountain, moved towards the planet and then stopped abruptly.

Light.

A warm and reverent light stopped the demon's arm.

The King of Cheonma raised his gaze and looked at the starting point of the light. There was something that looked like a small sun at first glance.

「The system imposes a penalty on the King of Cheonma!」

「The system imposes a penalty on the King of Cheonma!」

「The system imposes a penalty on the King of Cheonma!」

The King of Cheonma's power weakened. The mandala, which had been endlessly expanding towards the universe, lost its power and contracted.

However, the King of Cheonma didn't pay attention to the system and glared at the small sun as if he would kill it.

「The Enlightened One speaks to the King of Cheonma.」

「Stop there.」

A sacred voice spread widely throughout the universe.

,Kyaaaaaaaaak!"

The demon screamed. It was the scream of a demon with a hand bigger than a mountain. The demon's hand shook back and forth as if writhing in pain, and soon turned to ashes and disappeared.

The aftermath doesn't end there. The King of Cheonma's mandala shattered and began to disappear.

,Buddha. Do you dare to command me?"

There was no reply.

However, an overwhelming presence that could cover this universe and a sacred yet stern gaze are felt from the small sun.

,Tsk."

The King of Cheonma clicked his tongue. He raised both hands as if he had no intention of fighting.

,Okay. Okay. I lost. I'll stop here. So don't even think about waving that finger. That hurts quite a bit."

,You're giving up right here? You?'

,I'm not giving up because I want to. Buddha is not a good match for me. That crazy bastard has no anguish. I'll tell you one good thing. If you get rid of all your anguish and reach Nirvana like that guy, you can beat me. Well, it's impossible though."

I felt the presence of the King of Cheonma inside me gradually weakening. The King of Cheonma is disappearing.

Then, I became anxious instead. I could feel knowledge from the King of Cheonma, but it was very limited. And just because I have knowledge doesn't mean I can understand it. There were many things I wanted to ask the King of Cheonma.

,There's no time to answer. Ask just one question. And the other side won't allow any more than that."

,I heard you messed with the system earlier. What did you do to the system?'

Cheonma Shingong is shaking in my body. The King of Cheonma used his last strength to tamper with things so that others couldn't hear, and then answered.

,Ah, that? It's an old prank. A prank I've been playing since before Atlantis started. To tell you what it is... there are too many eyes watching. Keu keu keu keu."

The King of Cheonma's presence disappeared completely.

I regained control of my body. My hands and feet move as I please. I couldn't breathe because I was in outer space. But that's not particularly uncomfortable. Whether it's the King of Cheonma or the system, some kind of power is being applied to me.

「The Enlightened One is staring at you.」

Buddha's gaze was very unpleasant. It was an instinctive discomfort.

I strained my neck for no reason and glared at the small sun.

,It's too dazzling. Cheonan.'

[Opening the Cheonan (Sky Eye).]

I could see the being inside the small sun. It was a man sitting cross-legged. I made eye contact with the man. The man looked at me with a blank face and then disappeared.

「The system is looking at you.」

,I didn't do anything wrong. This is all the King of Cheonma's doing."

「Weren't you the one who called the King of Cheonma?」

,And that bastard took over my body. You don't think I planned all this, do you?"

「The system sighs.」

「The system sincerely hopes that you will not cause any accidents.」

,You should say that to the King of Cheonma, not me."

「The King of Cheonma will be given appropriate penalties.」

,Is he being exiled? Then I hope you'll let me contract with a new god seat. I didn't cause the accident, the King of Cheonma did. Strictly speaking, I'm also a victim. I deserve to be compensated."

「The system is speechless at your audacity.」

「The King of Cheonma will not be exiled from Atlantis. The King of Cheonma has accepted all penalties.」

,Any other ordinary god seat would have been exiled. Keu keu."

I laughed at the system.

Not all god seats are the same. There was a gap in power between them, and the weaker they were, the more disadvantages they suffered.

,What are the penalties the King of Cheonma is receiving?"

「Payment of AP and suspension. During the suspension period, you cannot exert influence on Atlantis.」

,That means I can't receive support from the King of Cheonma. It'll be quiet for a while. What about Osiris? You're not saying Osiris won't be penalized, are you?"

「Meaningless death has already been penalized.」

「He has completely given up participating in this Atlantis. 」

I would never meet Osiris again. That wasn't bad.

,Then it's time for me to be compensated."

「This incident was an accident that started with you. There will be no compensation for you.」

,Not that. Set. There will be a reward for killing the owner of the oasis (false)."

「You will acquire the Authority of the Oasis (SSS) as a reward.」

It looked like an authority that could control the oasis. With this, I would be able to perfectly dominate the desert area.

,I want something else."

「The reward has been decided. It cannot be changed.」

,The reward hasn't been given yet."

There was only one thing that hadn't been given. The place I am now is outside the Atlantis area.

「What reward do you want?」

,Send me there."

The place I pointed to was the moon.

「The Moon Hunter's eyes widen.」

「The Moon Hunter wiggles her body, expecting something.」

「The Sparkling Hunter is wailing.」

「The Sun's Adversary is paying attention.」

「The Moon Flower is horrified.」
