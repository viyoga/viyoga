Tang!

George's doppelganger holding the rifle pulled the trigger.

That one shot, more than 10 times more expensive than a missile, a magic-piercing anti-magic bullet manufactured to precisely target Eugene's head. Eugene didn't move an inch.

What moved instead of him was a violet-colored lightning. The violet lightning moved on its own and collided with the bullet.

Ting!

The bullet bounced off. It wasn't bounced off by being caught by magnetic force. It bounced off because the lightning's intensity couldn't pierce the bullet. That's an anti-magic bullet. Ordinary magic is easily pierced.

"...What the hell is that lightning...!"

Lightning. No, electricity isn't an object. It's closer to a phenomenon or energy that occurs as electrons move.

But the violet lightning blooming from that magician's fingertips is hovering around the magician as if it were alive.

"Violet lightning is lightning that possesses both energy and physical force."

"...The bullet I just shot was an anti-magic bullet. As long as it's magic, it's bound to be torn apart!"

"I know. A special bullet that forcibly deforms and destroys the formulas that make up magic. But if it doesn't directly touch the magic formula, it's no different from an ordinary bullet. With the power of an ordinary bullet, you can't touch the essence of violet lightning."

As Eugene waved his hand, the violet lightning split into four strands and fell towards George's doppelganger. It's a bit slow to call it the speed of lightning, but it's fast enough that George, a 7th class warrior, can't let his guard down.

Kwakwakwakwakwang!

The ground touched by the violet lightning was torn away. It was proof that the violet lightning itself had a considerable mass.

'If I get hit head-on, my body won't be able to withstand the physical force and will be torn apart or crushed before I get electrocuted to death.'

Uuung.

George felt his weapons and clothes being pulled. The clothes were relatively weak, but the sword he was holding in his hand was being pulled so strongly that he might drop it.

"...Metal? Is it magnetic force...!"

Iron sand in the soil, guns and bullets dropped nearby, swords, and other metal weapons, electrons, mortars, metal plates, etc., are drawn together by magnetic force and turn into lumps.

"Uwaaaaaaak!"

"Drop your weapons! Take off your clothes, take off your clothes! Even small pieces of metal are being sucked in! Get rid of the metal on your body! Belts, buttons, even implants!"

To resist magnetic force, you need to exert a force of at least 6th class or higher, or distance yourself from that violet lightning. Otherwise, you'll be sucked in and crushed to death by the metal.

Most of the mercenaries were sucked in even after taking off their weapons and clothes. Most of them, who had survived by wandering the battlefield for a long time, were cyborgs who had replaced parts of their bodies such as limbs and organs with implants. Even if they drop their weapons and take off their clothes, they can't escape the magnetic force.

'Implants are made of special metals that suppress magnetism as much as possible...! But they're still being pulled in? Does it add magnetism to the metal itself?!'

The metal monster rampages indiscriminately, smashing the mercenary's base and massacring the members unilaterally.

George attacked the metal monster with his doppelgangers. He succeeded in breaking, piercing, and cutting the metal monster's body, but... it was meaningless.

In the first place, that's neither a monster nor a robot. Since it's magnetic force itself, it doesn't matter whether the metal is cut or broken.

'Damn it. Can't I break that magic with ordinary physical attacks...!'

George knew with just one exchange. In order to save more members, it's not about dealing with that metal, but killing the magician, the caster, even one second faster.

The four Georges gripped their weapons with all their might. Mana protected the weapons. The force pulling on the violet lightning is reduced slightly, but still there.

The four Georges jump into the sky and begin the battle. Rifles spit fire and slashes fly. The barriers protecting Eugene are broken one by one by the attacks.

"My attack is not over yet."

Eugene flicks his finger.

The violet lightning that had been rampaging on the ground, attracting all kinds of metal, stood up abruptly. It looked like a huge metal caterpillar raising its head.

"If I can pull it, I can push it away too."

Pajijijijik!

At the same time as the violet lightning discharged once from the center of the metal, the clumped metal was pushed out. At a speed faster than a bullet.

George couldn't avoid it. There was too much metal to avoid.

"Damn it...!"

In the end, he is hit and pierced by the metals.

Pajijijik!

Violet lightning flashes around the magician floating under the night sky. The metals floating in the air twisted and shattered into pieces, gathering around the magician.

"Iron has been a symbol of the military since ancient times. Even now that technology has advanced, iron still dominates.... You're the natural enemy of the military."
