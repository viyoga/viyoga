Weikung blatantly hurled his axe. It was quite fast, but not impossible to dodge.

[Using a Flash (瞬間). Remaining stacks: 11]

Using Flash made even the flying axe seem slow.

,He's probably trying to get my attention.'

There was no way such an attack would work on me.

I sensed Weikung's presence. He charged at me the moment he threw the axe.

Dodge the axe, or deflect it.

Two choices were given. Whichever I chose would be according to Weikung's plan.

,I have the ability to choose a third option.'

As soon as I summoned the Stormbreaker in the form of a spear from my inventory, I threw it at the incoming axe. The axe and spear collided in mid-air. The axe had the upper hand. It was natural since it contained an aura blade. But the direction of the axe was changed, and that was enough.

I blocked Weikung's axe attack, who was running behind the thrown axe, with Hwaryeon Bido. Red and blue aura blades crossed each other. Each aura blade sparked like embers. Aura blades that could cut rocks like tofu couldn't do that to each other.

Weikung, after exchanging one blow, retreated without pushing it. He stretched out one hand, and the axe that had flown far away flew back and was caught in his hand.

,The axe itself is definitely an excellent item, but it's not magic. Did he bring the axe back using telekinesis?'

I also stretched out one hand and brought back the Stormbreaker using telekinesis.

One blow. With just one clash, I understood Weikung's fighting style. He focused more on power than technique. In this respect, he was similar to me.

"This won't be an easy fight. Don't you feel it too?"

Weikung said. The red aura surging from his axe soared. It looked like flames were burning.

"Did you get scared after just one exchange?"

"Scared? No way! I'm admiring you right now! I'm willing to die in this duel! I truly mean it!"

Weikung smiled brightly. His fighting spirit filled the space. As if saying this space was his domain.

Pajijijik.

Sparks flew from my body. Lightning energy resisted the fighting spirit.

,He's going to take over the right of occupancy of the space? Absolutely not.'

Looking at Weikung, who was laughing heartily, I made a decision. When fighting that bastard, I would fight with my skills as much as possible.

The sparkling sparks covered my body and seeped in.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning (疾风迅雷).

I took a step and ran towards Weikung. Weikung didn't avoid it and ran towards me, facing me. And then, a clash.

A shockwave exploded with a loud roar. The ground was torn apart, and dirt and stones soared. Before they fell back to the ground, a second clash followed. And then a third, a fourth.

The shock that would have caused an ordinary human's body to explode continued to occur. Weikung and I were enduring the shockwave with only the strength of our bodies. Using aura blades for defense? I couldn't do such a shameful thing.

He raised his axe. I gripped the hilt of Hwaryeon Bido tightly. Red lightning sparked from the blade, adding strength.

What followed was the same clash as before.

Aura, lightning, and fighting spirit mixed and scattered. The sight was as beautiful as a flower blooming for a moment.

"Ha, hahahahaha!"

Weikung laughed so hard his mouth tore.

"What's so fun?"

"How can I not be happy! Stop with the useless questions, Eugene Pruker! Aren't you laughing too!"

I couldn't help but be happy.

An opponent I could face with all my strength. That existence was so rare that just facing and fighting was enjoyable.

As the exchanges continued, I felt it. Weikung's and my capabilities were neither lacking nor excessive.

,Honestly, when it comes to talent, he's far above me.'

The more I fought, the more I could tell. Weikung had never learned any decent martial arts or specialized combat knowledge. He was fighting solely with experience and was on par with me, who had mastered the Thunder Sky Style.

The fact that the fighting spirit he instinctively used was compatible with the Thunder Sky Style also played a part. The lightning was blocked by the fighting spirit and couldn't even enter his body.

,But in the end, I'll win. Even if it's fun, I have to settle it. Because I'll be even more fun if I win.'

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Thunder Snake (雷蛇).

Just before Hwaryeon Bido and the axe collided, Hwaryeon Bido moved like a snake, avoiding the axe blade and crawling towards his neck. Weikung opened his mouth and accurately bit the tip of Hwaryeon Bido with his mouth. Red aura blades wriggled in his teeth.

Two axes were swung. I blocked them by placing the Stormbreaker sideways. It wasn't enough to block two with one. My body flew backward. I scratched the ground with Hwaryeon Bido to regain my balance.

I turned my gaze to check on Weikung. He wasn't there. I quickly looked up, seeing the long shadow stretched out on the ground. Weikung, floating high in the air, was falling towards me with both hands raised, holding his axes.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

Kwaaaaaang!

I avoided a direct hit by using the Flying Thunder God, but the shockwave generated by Weikung hitting the ground rushed in like a tsunami. It was different from the previous shockwaves. Fighting spirit was deliberately mixed in.

The coat, which had greatly increased my defense with magic, was torn, and my skin burst, splattering blood.

Instead of using protective aura, I gathered strength in the blade and swung it.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷閃).

The aura wrapped in red lightning flew towards Weikung. Weikung, who had just used a large technique, reacted but couldn't avoid it completely. The Thunder Flash grazed his side.

,It's an attack that wouldn't be strange to split his waist in half. Just how tough is his body?'

There was no time to be surprised. Suddenly, Weikung vomited blood. It wasn't because of the attack just now. Even though I cut his side, it wasn't a fatal wound that would cause his internal organs to spill out.

"Is it poison?"

"...It's a nasty poison. The healer said I need to rest for half a year."

"It seems Thorn deliberately released assassins."

"It's not an assassin Thorn prepared. It's just an old grudge."

I didn't ask in detail. I could roughly guess. Kairak was a country achieved through conquest. Countless blood must have been shed in the process.

I once again sent aura towards Weikung. I detonated the aura near Weikung, who was trying to avoid it.

Thunder Sky Style (Raiden-ryu) Extreme Technique (Goku-waza) Explosion Thunder (Bakushinrai).

Fragments of aura and lightning swept through Weikung's body. Weikung didn't retreat but jumped forward.

"Uooooooo!"

He charged, shouting. He looked as reckless as a mad bison, but his fighting spirit and aura blade were sharper than ever.

Weikung swung his axe. A huge slash flew, splitting the ground. I swung Hwaryeon Bido and responded with a slash. The slashes were negated and disappeared.

Weikung, who had narrowed the distance, blocked the axe he was swinging with the Stormbreaker in his left hand.

"Thank you."

"What?"

"For letting me die fighting. The poison was spreading more and more as I fought. If you hadn't properly faced me and just stalled for time, I would have self-destructed."

"Even if I fight, I'll win. There's no need to stall from the beginning."

"Haha! That's what I'm thankful for!"

Attacks without any defense followed. The equal battle tilted towards my side at some point. Weikung couldn't withstand the power and was gradually pushed back. Nevertheless, he only relied on strength to attack. His tenacity was admirable.

I felt a sense of regret. If it weren't for the poison eating away at Weikung's body, I felt like I could have fought more enjoyably.

I didn't actively use the Thunder Sky Style. There was a pleasure in fighting recklessly with strength like Weikung.

The battle, which seemed like it would last a long time, ended anticlimactically. Perhaps because of the poison, Weikung stiffened for a moment, and I didn't miss the opportunity and plunged Hwaryeon Bido into his chest. I pierced his heart for sure.

"Eugene Pruker. I acknowledge you. From now on, you are the War King of Kairak!"

Weikung died. I drew Hwaryeon Bido and looked at the enemies. His army stomped their feet and shouted in unison.

"Bless the new War King of Kairak!!"

They acknowledge me this easily?

It seemed Weikung had coordinated with the warriors before the duel. It was a good thing since there was no need to see unnecessary bloodshed.

I confidently walked towards the capital. Pain came belatedly. My skin was torn, and my whole body was covered in blood. I couldn't embarrassingly pretend it didn't hurt, so I walked with my mouth tightly shut.

• • •

I washed my body and ascended the throne of Kairak.

I didn't feel much. It wasn't my first time ascending to the throne. Around me stood armed warriors, including Harto and Sopong.

In front, a woman was kneeling. She was a beauty with healthy bronze skin and purple hair.

Her waist was slender, and her breasts and hips were full. She was tall, and her arms and legs were long. She wore a leather skirt that came down to her knees and a tank top that showed her navel. It looked somewhat vulgar, but she was a warrior before she was a princess.

"Your name is Kuka, was it?"

"Yes. New War King-isiyeo."

"I am the one who killed your father. If you want revenge, I will accept a duel. But you will become my woman instead."

Kuka didn't show hatred or fighting spirit towards me.

"I was also watching that duel. My father died as an honorable warrior, so I will acknowledge you as the War King according to my father's will."

"No wonder things are going so smoothly. He left such a will. Even so, you will become my woman. Any complaints?"

"No. Rather, it is an honor to serve a great warrior as my husband."

"I like that this country is straightforward. How great would it be if other countries were like this too. I will hold a banquet. Give wine and food to the citizens."

A warrior with a flat face stepped forward.

"War King-isiyeo, Kairak is at war. There is no room for a banquet. Please set a new policy."

I threw the axe Weikung used at the guy. The axe, filled with blue aura blade and lightning, accurately split the guy's head, causing an electric explosion. The shattered corpse of the guy dirtied the floor of the imperial court.

"I said I would hold a banquet. If there is anyone who is dissatisfied, come out."

I recovered the axe with telekinesis. The axe handle stuck to my hand. I understand why Weikung used the axe as a weapon. The feel of it when I threw it just now was amazing.

,Should I use an axe for a while?'
