Seogeok!

The fifth one was split in half and executed.

The atmosphere here was beyond quiet, it was filled with terror. Did the condemned just accept their fate without resistance? Of course not. They didn't want to die, so they grabbed guns and made a fuss, but their wild shots couldn't even scratch me. I executed those who shot at me first.

The silver blade descends, and inevitably, another human is split in half and dies. From the bisected corpses, blood and guts flow instead of the parasites that should be there.

"Next, Seo Jun-saeng."

The 11th one.

Seo Jun-saeng was a skinny man. His hair was dyed yellow with cheap dye, and his face was covered in acne. He had three piercings in his ear. He looked like a total punk, and he was bawling his eyes out.

"I, I'm not a parasite either! I swear!"

"That's what I'm trying to find out, you little shit. Come out here right now."

I pointed the cutlass, dripping with blood, at Seo Jun-saeng.

"I, I'm not! You all saw it too! The people who were executed were all humans, not parasites! It was a false accusation! If this keeps up, that bastard will kill us all! We have to kill that son of a bitch before that happens!"

Seo Jun-saeng made a decent choice. He's trying to incite the people to unite. But the situation was bad. The people were already overwhelmed by fear. They were just looking at each other, hesitating.

"Euaaaaaaaagh!"

With a scream, Seo Jun-saeng aimed a pistol at me and fired.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

The bullets that were rushing towards me fell vertically to the floor and crashed before they could even touch my body. I showed them the performance I had shown them several times before. It was once again imprinted on them that firearms don't work on me.

The reason why people can't resist me is because of this. Their greatest weapon was firearms, after all.

"Come out here, quick!"

Peok!

Choi Hye-jin approached Seo Jun-saeng and swung her bat, beating him mercilessly. Seo Jun-saeng screamed and held out until his arms and legs were broken, and Choi Hye-jin forcibly dragged him out.

I didn't kill him right away, but waited so that people could see him clearly. If you go against me, whether you're a parasite or not, this is what will happen. I'm imprinting that fact on the people.

"Starting the execution!"

The moment I stood in front of Seo Jun-saeng and raised my cutlass high. Dozens of thin, long tentacles popped out of Seo Jun-saeng's eyes and mouth, aiming for my head.

I, who had been tensing my body without letting my guard down, immediately stepped back and swung my sword to cut off the tentacles.

'Found you, parasite.'

If I were a normal human, I might have been flustered and caught off guard, but for me, who has been through thick and thin, it was nothing special. As always, I started to counterattack.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A blue flash flashed and cut off Seo Jun-saeng's neck. His head floated into the air and spun around. Tentacles popped out of the severed neck, rushing towards me as if making a final struggle.

'How disgusting.'

I split his head in half before the tentacles could reach me.

Tuk.

From the cross-section of the fallen head, a mass of tentacles that had been acting as the brain wriggled and then drooped.

People were horrified by the sight of the monster that was clearly dominating the human.

Many people had doubted that the parasitic monster was pretending to be human, even after hearing about it. But this proved that what I said was true.

"Look. I have successfully executed the parasite. The tip I received was true."

Since the parasite came out, I had the justification.

Eleven people, including the parasite, died, but in the end, the parasite did come out, didn't it? I could be proud.

Having lost its purpose, I sheathed my sword. Today's execution show is over.

"Don't be relieved that one parasite has died. We believe that there are at least five or more parasites in Saetbyeol Apartment. So, if you see someone who looks like a parasite, report it to me without delay."

I didn't say how to deal with the parasites. I don't know either. Anyway, it's not me who's in danger, it's them, so they'll figure it out.

"Oh, right. I will be managing all the food in the apartment from now on. It's because the parasites might tamper with the food. Anyone who's unhappy is highly likely to be a parasite. Anyone unhappy?"

"......"

Nearly 1,000 people were silent.

I nodded in satisfaction.

"In the afternoon, I will start individual interviews with Building 103. I trust you won't ignore me like you did yesterday."

It doesn't matter if you ignore me. However, another parasite hiding in Building 103 will be executed tomorrow.

I went into the apartment with Choi Hye-jin. I made eye contact with Lee Yebin in the middle. She was acting like a queen bee among the men. She pursed her lips and glared at me as if she wanted to kill me.

'What are you going to do if you glare at me? This apartment is mine now.'

Gather her followers and start a rebellion? Well, they saw with their own eyes that firearms don't work on me, so would they be able to start a rebellion so easily?

And even though Lee Yebin is the apartment representative, she doesn't completely control the apartment. The residents of Buildings 101 and 103 seem to follow Lee Yebin, but they are also wary of her.

'That's why I didn't kill Kang Sang-gi, the representative of Building 103. I didn't kill him to keep Lee Yebin in check.'

What if my predictions don't come true and the two of them join hands? Then they both become parasites.

A psychic who can't be hurt by guns. He was no different from the monsters roaming outside the apartment.

"Oh. Are you going to kill him, hyung-nim?!"

"Shibal. What the hell am I going to kill? But why is that bastard so eager to catch us? Only people from our building died this time!"
