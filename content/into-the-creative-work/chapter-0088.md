-Yujin. What in the world are you thinking?

Ellain's cold voice came from the crystal ball on the table. There was a hint of anger mixed in, but she maintained her composure.

I had to listen to Ellain's nagging for a while.

-Yujin Prukerus. From the moment you return to the mansion, I'm ordering you to be confined for a month.

"Yes, Mother."

I answered with a bitter smile. Confinement for a month. It was a much longer period than I had anticipated.

If I return to the mansion, I'll be living in my room for a month. Well, I can probably make excuses to go for walks.

Confinement is a light punishment. I can just rest for a month. If necessary, I can just let the time pass automatically.

-...Lastly. You did well to subdue the bandit group. You are indeed a noble of Prukerus.

"Of course, Mother. Ah, I'd like you to leave the disposal of the captured Pfrek bandits to me."

-So be it. Subduing the bandits was your accomplishment. Therefore, executing the bandits is also your right.

Ellain acknowledged my achievement.

Subduing the bandit group is enough of a noble deed. This will slightly improve my reputation.

"Thank you, Mother."

After that, we had various conversations before ending the communication.

She said she would send troops and a carriage to escort the bandits tomorrow morning. And even a carriage for me to ride in.

If everything goes as planned, I'll arrive at the mansion tomorrow afternoon. The prison that has been neglected until now will also be used.

'Hasent, when did you even pack this?'

I picked up the magic communication device on the table. This was something Hasent had packed. He's truly a flawless butler.

I then looked at Yuria, who was sitting across from me, hiding her appearance with a black cloak and cloth. In front of her was the rice porridge I had given her. She was obviously malnourished, so I couldn't feed her greasy pork belly.

"You left some. Didn't it suit your taste?"

"No. I will eat it all properly."

"I'm not telling you to force yourself to eat it, I'm just saying. Your stomach must be shrunken from starving, so it's natural that you can't eat much. But eat this."

I handed her a palm-sized container.

"What is this?"

"Super Vitamin. It's lemon flavored. It's said to contain all the nutrients a person needs, so eat one a day. Keep in mind that you must wait exactly 24 hours before taking another one."

It's something that came out of a random draw. There are 99 pills in the container.

I ate one and didn't touch it again.

I can always eat plenty of food, and I have the highest stamina stat, so I didn't need it.

I watched Yuria swallow the pill and stood up from my seat.

"I'll go get some food, so stay put."

"Yes, Master-nim."

•••

Jiggle jiggle jiggle!

The pork belly placed on the huge pot lid began to cook deliciously. A bonfire was blazing under the pot lid.

I swallowed hard, smelling the kimchi being grilled. In my opinion, kimchi grilled in pork fat was the strongest.

I glanced around. Knights and villagers, seated in various places, were watching the pot lid, swallowing their saliva like me.

The reason I had not only the knights but also the villagers attend was to gain a reputation for showing mercy to the commoners. Also, it would be to my advantage if they spread the word about the pot lid pork belly.

If rumors spread, I could sell pot lids and kimchi too!

'I wanted to let Yuria know the taste of Korean barbecue too... It's a shame.'

I couldn't force her to eat something that was obviously bad for her.

"Is it... is it ready yet?!"

A mournful voice asked me from beside me.

"Not yet. Sir Maretin. Now, let's start cutting it."

Maretin is the knight with the least experience among the six gathered here. I'm a noble, so I'm naturally excluded, and Butler Hasent has the title of Baron.

"Yes. Leave it to this Maretin."

Maretin, who spoke confidently, picked up a long piece of pork belly with tongs and began to cut it with scissors.

I clicked my tongue. It was clumsy handiwork.

'Aish. It's frustrating.'

I can understand. It's probably his first time cutting pork belly with scissors.

"...Sir Maretin. Give me the tongs and scissors. I'll show you how to cut it."

I got up from my seat. I couldn't do it sitting down because I was short. The gazes of the people sitting at the other tables were focused on me. They will watch me and follow suit.

"This is how you cut with scissors."

I held the tongs in my left hand and the scissors in my right hand.

I like to grill and eat meat, including pork belly. I not only go to restaurants to eat it, but I also grill pork belly at home from time to time.

I started cutting the meat with skilled hand movements. There was no awkwardness like Maretin.

After cutting it with scissors, I grilled the pork belly a little more.

"Alright. Now. Let's eat."

I showed them how to do it first. I picked up the well-grilled kimchi and pork belly with chopsticks and put them on a plate. After letting it cool slightly, I put it in my mouth.

I shuddered. Pork belly and kimchi are a delicious combination no matter when you eat them.

People started eating after me.

"I heard that pork belly is the belly of a pig... I never knew it was such a delicious part!"

"Is the name of this food kimchi? It's a little spicy, but it's perfect for my taste!"

"Keuk. I can't stop using my chopsticks!"

The knights picked up the pork belly with chopsticks while exclaiming. I taught them how to use chopsticks when we ate ramen last time. They weren't used to using chopsticks, so they were very clumsy.

"Young master-nim. What is that?"

"Ah. This is called ssam. It's pork belly with ssamjang on lettuce."

I put the lettuce wrap in my mouth as if to show off.

The knights hurriedly followed me, holding lettuce in one hand and starting to make wraps.

"Oh. Oooooh! Just wrapping the meat in a plant makes it taste like this!"

"This ssamjang sauce goes very well with pork belly! This ssamjang is delicious even if you eat it by itself!"

"Young master-nim! What exactly is this ssamjang?!"

"Ah. This is called Korea Magic Sauce."

"Korea Magic Sauce! So it's a sauce made with magic!"

"......Well. That's right."

I'll skip the detailed explanation.

And how much would they understand even if I explained it? The important thing is that ssamjang is delicious.

The meat on the pot was quickly emptied. The sturdy men, who were already big eaters, had even fought today. It would be strange if they ate little.

"I will grill the meat, young master-nim."

Hasent stepped forward just as I was about to grab the scissors again. There was no awkwardness in Hasent's hand movements.

"Everyone, raise your soju glasses! We can't not have a toast on this good day!"

"As expected of young master-nim!"

"I'll teach you the Korean toasting method that existed in ancient times. Follow me."

People held soju glasses in their hands and looked at me.

I raised the soju glass in my hand as high as I could and shouted.

"Gukppong!"

The knights started following me.

"Gukppong!"

"Gukppoong!"

"Gukpooooong!"

I listened to their gukppong shouts and downed the soju glass in one shot. Soju is definitely the best with pork belly.

'This is what you call isekai gukppong.'

Shudder. My body trembled with joy.

"Gukppong coin, let's gooooo!"

It was a word I shouted without realizing it.

And people followed my words without even knowing the meaning.

"Gukppong coin, let's gooooo!"

"Gukppong coin, let's goooooooo!"

"Gukppong coin, let's gooooooooooooo!"

The sound of gukppong echoed from everywhere.

After finishing the meal, I talked with the knights until late and returned to my temporary residence.

The cleanest and best house in Artel Village is where I'm staying.

When I entered the room, Yuria was sitting on a chair, asleep.

'......I'm doing this because she's mine.'

I approached her and picked up Yuria. Yuria gave me everything in exchange for revenge. And I know how to treat my things preciously.

"......."

Yuria's eyes suddenly opened wide. She seems quite sensitive.

"Close your eyes and just sleep."

I laid Yuria on the bed and came out of the room. I couldn't sleep because there was still one thing I had to do.

I came out of the house and said to the knight who was standing guard.

"Sir Maretin. You're working hard."

"Not at all, young master-nim. Aren't you sleeping?"

"I have something to say to the butler. Do you know where the butler is?"

"Butler Hasent is staying next door."

"Thank you."

I headed to the next house.

I was nervous. Hasent isn't an idiot. I have no idea what business he has with me.

"Butler. Are you inside?"

"Yes. Please come in, young master-nim."

When I opened the door and went inside, there was an old butler standing upright.

I admire his perfect posture every time I see it.

"Butler Hasent. Do you have something to say to me separately?"

"Young master Yujin. Your heart disease was cured a long time ago, wasn't it?"

I was so surprised by Hasent's words that my heart pounded.

"No. What are you talking about? As the butler also knows, my congenital heart disease is not ordinary. Even the best potions can't cure it, and famous healers and specialized healing mages have given up."

If I could have cured my heart disease, I would have done it a long time ago. With the power of the Prukerus County, I could even bring the Tower Master.

But I couldn't cure my heart disease. The first wife of Count Prukerus, who had the same heart disease as me, and the biological mother of Yujin Prukerus, Haruel Delishik, died because of heart disease. If I could have cured her heart disease, Count Prukerus would not have let her die, whom he loved.

"You had a seizure a while ago, didn't you."

"My condition has improved these days, but I still have seizures from time to time."

"Young master Yujin. You are good at talking, but you are not good at acting sick. The servants may have been fooled, but... to those with my discerning eye, your acting is clearly visible."

"......."

I was about to make an excuse, but I stopped. Hasent was already convinced. I certainly don't remember acting like I was having a seizure in front of Hasent... He must have been watching me from outside my field of vision.

I sighed.

"Yes. My heart disease is cured. One day, my heart disease suddenly got better."

"One day, suddenly?"

"I know it's hard to believe. But one day, my heart disease really got better."

"Does 'one day' mean the day your attitude changed?"

"......Yes. Well. That's right."

I felt an unnecessary sense of unease. The day my attitude changed? I don't know when he's talking about. I stayed quiet for a while to gather information when I first started this game.

"Young master-nim, have you perhaps been chosen by Serion?"

"......Huh?"

I tilted my head at the sudden, out-of-the-blue question.

Serion is the only god in this world. She is said to be the goddess who created the world and has omnipotent power. There is a cult that believes in and follows Serion, but its power is weak due to being pushed back by the Magic Tower.

Serion did not appear in the original work, so I don't know if she actually exists.

"Young master-nim, you have become noticeably more mature. Of course, there are times when you do things that I don't understand, but the knowledge you have is at a level that cannot be understood simply by reading books."

The other servants and vassals just brushed it off. They weren't very interested in me. On the other hand, Hasent must have felt something strange about me and investigated me separately.

"Young master-nim, you said you found out my identity in the study. But there is no evidence in the study that can be used to infer my identity. I would never make such an amateur mistake. The only people who know my identity are the head of the family, the Madam, and some vassals."

"......."

I scratched the back of my head with one hand. I tried to use Hasent rashly, but I got caught.

'I don't think I can kill Hasent with my skills. What should I do? Should I use the emotion controller? ...No. It's too precious to use on the old butler.'

My head became complicated.

"Young master-nim, you knew about Artel Village. More than anything, you knew that the Pfrek bandits disguised as mercenaries would attack the village. And Yuria Grace. Isn't that child a bastard of the Helbrit Duke's family?"

"......Butler. I lost. You wouldn't be convinced no matter what I said. What do you want from me, Butler?"

"One thing. Please give me a proper answer without lying."

Hasent's deep, cold gray eyes stared at me intently. I did not avoid his eyes and looked directly at him.

"Young master-nim, like the legendary prophet Nophil, have you been chosen by Serion?"

"Yes. That's right."

In the end, it's a medieval fantasy.
