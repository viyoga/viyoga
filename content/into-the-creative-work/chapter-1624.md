The pure white temple door opened.

A powerful chill that made my whole body tremble rushed out. Glancing down, the floor was frozen solid. The inside of the temple was a glacial zone itself. Exhaling caused breath to freeze instantly and fall to the ground.

I reflexively checked on Elena. She was shivering slightly. Compared to her incredible illusion magic, her physical abilities weren't that great. This level of cold could be dangerous for her.

I took off my cloak and draped it over Elena. A smile formed on her lips.

,Thanks. You're not completely devoid of manners, after all."

,If that's not enough, I can give you other clothes too."

,This cloak is enough. It doesn't seem like an ordinary cloak, though?"

,It's sturdier than other cloaks, that's for sure."

The inside of the temple wasn't as big as I'd imagined. The interior was filled with walls and pillars inscribed with unknown languages. Even without understanding the content, it felt sacred. And the chill kept flowing below my knees.

,Hey. What do the letters on the walls and pillars mean? What does it say?"

Gargal only turned his head to look at the pillars and walls. Then he shrugged.

,I'm not sure either. There are letters that demons use, but this is different. This script seems to have been created directly by the Demon King. He might have created the entire language itself."

,Created a language? Was it really necessary to go that far?"

,How would I know what's going on in the head of a demon trying to become a god? Come to think of it, the Demon King was very interested in artistic things. Ah, look over there. A painting drawn by the Demon King himself."

What Gargal pointed to was a painting hanging in the corner of the temple. It was a landscape painting depicting black mountains and an ashen sky. I'm not particularly interested in art, but I could tell it was well-drawn.

,The Demon King was unique. Usually, demons don't enjoy things like art."

,Then what do demons enjoy?"

,It varies from demon to demon. Groma, that Explosion Demon, enjoys causing explosions. I saw him playing around by detonating magical beasts before."

,What do you enjoy?"

,I enjoy doing nothing. Hmm. We've arrived."

Gargal, who had stopped, waved his arm at the empty air. Space split open, and a new sight came into view.

In the center was a being with a body made of ice. It must be a demon. Demons often have unique appearances. On either side of that being were demons trapped in ice. Including the ice demon, there were a total of nine demons.

The ice demon opened its mouth.

,Gargal. You are now betraying your own kind."

,Kaisa. Since when did demons have such a strong sense of kinship that you're talking about betrayal?"

,So you're going to side with humans? Are you going to offer your own kind to humans and try to survive alone? Gargal. That human has no intention of letting you live. He's already preparing to kill you. Can't you feel the human's malice?"

Since I seemed to have been caught already, I openly harbored murderous intent. My hand gripped the handle of the Hwaryeon Bido, waiting for the right moment to draw it.

,Look at this human. Isn't he more demonic than a demon? If I die by this human's hand... well, I guess it can't be helped. Because this human who killed me will kill even the Demon King."

,You've been consumed by revenge and your mind has become strange."

,I've been trapped for thousands of years. Even for a demon who enjoys doing nothing... try doing nothing for thousands of years. It wouldn't be strange to go crazy."

,I'll give you one last chance. If Gargal-nim sincerely apologizes, the Demon King-nim will forgive Gargal-nim as well."

,Kaisa. I already made my decision thousands of years ago."

,That's unfortunate."

Cold air surged around Kaisa. Gargal moved quickly. He used his power of severance to block the cold head-on.

,You! I'll block the cold! Quickly deal with Kaisa! Kaisa is trying to release the frozen demons! If all those demons wake up, even you won't be able to win! Move quickly!"

I drew my sword and stabbed Gargal in the back.

,Don't order me around."

,Keoeok! D-Don't do something stupid! I'm helping you right now!"

,I never asked for your help. Your role ends with the guide. Good work."

Before he could use his power to retaliate, I swung my sword upwards. The Hwaryeon Bido split Gargal's head open. Brain matter fell to the floor, instantly freezing upon contact with the cold.

,Gargal.... What a foolish end."

,Kaisa, was it? You're next."

,You're too late. The two of you can't possibly face all of us."

Kaisa spread both hands. Crack! Cracks appeared in the ice trapping the demons.

,Now, rise! The Demon King-nim's enemy is before you! Kill him!"

The ice shattered, and the demons opened their eyes.

And the demons were trapped in ice again.

,What...?"

Kaisa was bewildered. I walked towards the nearest demon and swung the Hwaryeon Bido. The demon's body, along with the ice, split in half.

,Rise! The Demon King-nim's enemy is before you! Hurry!"

Crack crack crack!

The ice shattered again.

The demons were still trapped in ice.

Having witnessed an impossible scene, Kaisa involuntarily took a step back.

Lightning Heaven Style (Lei Tian Style) Lightning Gale Blade (Lei Gang Blade).

Light flashed. Two more demons were split in half.

,W-What the hell is going on?! Rise! Hurry!"

Kaisa shouted. A storm of cold swept in all directions.

The ice shattered, and the demons seemed to move, but before they knew it, the demons were trapped in ice again.

It wasn't the feeling of turning back time. It was a sense of incongruity, as if time had been cut, the process removed, and only the result inserted.

I took advantage of the gap to kill three more demons. Now, all that remained were Kaisa and the two demons on either side.

,Cough."

Elena coughed. Blood splattered from her mouth and fell to the floor. The blood froze before it even touched the ground.

,Hmm. I'm reaching my limit."

Elena nonchalantly wiped her mouth with the back of her hand. Her complexion was as pale as a dying person, but there was no fear of death to be found.

,You! It was you...!"

,You noticed? You're more clueless than I thought."

Flutter flutter.

Blue butterflies flew gracefully throughout the space. The butterflies were completely unaffected by the cold.

,I don't know what tricks you've been playing, but if only you weren't here!"

The cold concentrated on Elena. The cold instantly began to freeze Elena's legs. Elena wasn't flustered even as she was trapped in ice.

,So this is what it feels like to be frozen alive. It's so cold that my head is spinning."

I swung the Hwaryeon Bido. I couldn't just throw away the opportunity Elena had given me.

Clang!

The ice shattered, and the demon died. Two demons remained.

,That's it! Now you're all alone. Without that woman, someone like you...."

The ice demon closed its mouth. It stared at my back with a look of horror.

Tap.

Elena walked to my side. There was no trace of illness in her, having used Complete Recovery. Her pale face was full of life.

,Yujin. I'll take care of that ice."

,Do it."

I ignored Kaisa and approached the demon trapped in ice. I stabbed the ice with a sword imbued with Aura Blade. The ice was quite hard, so it didn't go in at once. It gradually dug into the ice, piercing the demon's heart.

,Aaaaaaaagh!"

Kaisa's scream echoed through the temple. Kaisa collapsed to the floor, clutching her head with both hands and struggling.

,Don't come! Don't come!!"

The cold that filled the space disappeared. The ice demon's body began to melt. The floor was already soaked with water.

,Aaaaaaaaaaaaaagh!"

The ice demon struggled. It spewed cold air at the empty air in vain.

I approached Elena's side.

,What did you do?"

,I subtly manipulated his mind when his mental resistance dropped. Unlike solid ice, the mind is as fragile as glass. From what I can tell, that Demon King guy seems to have weakened his mind. Is it called gaslighting? He messed with his mind to make him easier to use."

The ice demon, which had been thrashing and struggling, completely melted into a puddle of water.

,You've killed all the demons. Is it over now?"

Elena asked, stretching. I looked at the ceiling. The ceiling was also engraved with unknown letters. I didn't like it.

Kwakwakwakwang!

A meteor sword fell from the sky, destroying the temple.

,Now, I have to kill the Demon King."

The Magic Stone Gate, located far away, came into view.

But the combat situation I saw was different.

The Demon King's body, Kyle, was lying on the floor, having lost his right arm, and a black dragon was rampaging madly in the air, fighting the Yurias.

,That black dragon... could it be Leosio?"

Red Dragon Leosio was gone. The black dragon had a similar size and appearance to Leosio.

The black dragon flapped its large wings, freely flying through the sky filled with corrupt mana.

,Hey! Don't just stand there, help us out!"

Sharnel shouted at me.

Just then, the black dragon inhaled. Yuria tried to block it with shadow chains, but the black dragon avoided the attack by flying through the sky.

Floi was lying unconscious on the floor. More than half of her armor was broken.

,Priscilla-nim! Please create a barrier!"

,It's too late! It's impossible to defend perfectly! Come this way! We need to minimize the defense area and focus on defense!"

Yuria was fighting head-on, and Priscilla was protecting her from behind. Focused on the battle, they hadn't even noticed that I had arrived.

I looked at Sharnel. Now that I saw it, she was dripping blood from her ears, nose, and mouth.

,Elena. Help Yuria."

,Helping is not difficult."

The black dragon spewed black breath. Elena used her power, and the breath that was falling downwards changed direction and soared upwards. The black dragon, hit by its own breath, flew through the air as if nothing had happened.

-Leosio! It's him! He's back from the Demon Realm!

,Meteor Sword."
