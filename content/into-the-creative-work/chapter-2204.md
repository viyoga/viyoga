Ambrac paused.

The generator field was the main bread and butter of the Leishen Faction. It wouldn't be an exaggeration to say that the power plants operated by the Leishen Faction fed the Leishen Faction. Of course, they had their feet in other fields as well.

,"LK Corp isn't just a company that sells superconductors. Are you planning to produce and sell small generators?"

"Yes, I'm personally interested in generators. I'd like to sell them while I'm at it."

"...I understand. If you're an electro-mage, you can't help but be interested in generators. If it's a generator that uses superconductors, you'll be able to dominate the market even if you start as a latecomer."

I stared at Ambrac. In the end, he, the successor of the faction, was the one who would make the decision. Ambrac moved his lips a few times before asking me a question.

"...Are you sure you're not interested in large generators and power plants?"

"I have no intention of competing with the Leishen Faction in the generator field. I came here to join hands with the Leishen Faction. You know the value of superconductors without me having to tell you. In the future, any product that uses electricity will have to use superconductors. I'll drop my interest in large generators and power plants."

"...I understand. In return, I'd like to receive a supply of superconductors. What's the production volume?"

"Right now, the limit is 5 tons per day. I'll give priority to the Leishen Faction for 1 ton, which is 20% of that."

"Good. Let's make a contract."

"You're decisive, -ssi."

"I can't give up when I have superconductors right in front of me. Ah, may I give you some advice?"

"I'm all ears."

"Even if you take a loss, you should bring in the Magic Association and the Assembly. It seems like it'll be difficult to protect LK Corp with just Leishen and Heitel."

"I already made appointments with the Magic Association and the Neo Round Table Assembly after this."

"I see. That was an overstep on my part."

After that, I met with the Magic Association and the Neo Round Table Assembly and signed contracts. I sold them 10% of the shares each. The Assembly wanted more shares in the company, but thanks to the Magic Association's help, I was able to sign the contract safely.

,'I'm glad I had the Magic Association and the Neo Round Table Assembly meet together.'

If I hadn't signed contracts with the Haetil Faction and the Leishen Faction in advance, I would have been ripped off for more shares. The fact that I was a high-ranking mage also helped.

The schedule for the press conference to announce the founding of LK Corp was also set.

It didn't matter if I set up an agent, but I decided to go out and announce it myself. To gain recognition, I had to stand in front of the public. To climb to a higher place, I had to make my existence known.

• • •

Many people gathered at the prepared conference venue. Of course, there were reporters, and executives from large and small companies also showed up. They were eager for the existence of superconductors and came to meet me as soon as possible.

"I'm Eugene Meyer, the CEO of LK. LK-99, which LK Corp will sell, is a room-temperature, ambient-pressure superconductor. The verification of the superconductor was conducted by the Leishen Faction. The verification result was certified as a room-temperature, ambient-pressure superconductor."

Thanks to the reputation of the Leishen Faction, no one doubted the superconductor. The companies were also quiet. Samples had already been sent to well-known companies. They must have realized that the superconductor was the real deal. And they would have tried to replicate it somehow, but they would have failed.

"I'm Hazel, a reporter from Ultimate. Did you develop LK-99 yourself, CEO-nim?"

A brown-haired woman with round glasses asked the first question.

"No. LK-99 is a legacy of Great Korea. I found traces of it in ancient ruins, and I only restored the recipe."

"Do you have any intention of disclosing the recipe for LK-99? You haven't even registered a patent."

"The recipe is mine alone. I don't think I need a patent. It seems like other reporters have a lot of questions as well. I'll give everyone a fair chance to ask questions."

I pointed to another reporter.

"I heard that the production volume of superconductors is small. Do you have any plans to maximize production by joining hands with other companies?"

"No."

"......Wouldn't you like to answer a little longer?"

"I can't give you a detailed answer to questions related to production because it involves company secrets."

I said firmly, exuding momentum.

Most of the reporters were ordinary people. They couldn't handle the energy of a high-ranking mage and shut their mouths. Some might call it oppression, but... it was difficult to prove. Momentum isn't visible.

"I heard that companies are lining up to get LK-99. What is LK Corp's sales strategy and the selling price of LK-99?"

"LK Corp doesn't just sell LK-99 indiscriminately. We will only sell to companies that meet our internal standards."

The internal standards will naturally be influenced by me and the investors. In particular, the influence of the Magic Association and the Assembly will be significant, but... this can't be helped for now. I have to bow my head to them for the time being.

"The selling price of LK-99 has not been determined yet. The selling price will be determined through appropriate negotiations."

After that, I answered the reporters diligently and successfully finished the press conference.

Most of it was glossed over... but that's what a press conference is like.

After that, I spent a busy time with no time to even blink.

I bought a small building in District B with the investment money I received and hired employees.

Because I hired the employees in a hurry, I didn't look into them in detail and only checked the highest specs possible. Most of them are probably industrial spies. I plan to weed them out later. There's no rush. I'm directly managing the core LK-special material.

I met with nobles and entrepreneurs in Neo London and signed contracts. I didn't just chase after money. Things other than money could be traded as well.

The shields of the Magic Association and the Neo Round Table Assembly were excellent, so I wasn't openly attacked. Hired mercenaries sneaking into the factory or threatening me did happen quite often, but.... I wasn't nice enough to leave the guys who were after me alone.

I captured them in reverse, tortured them to extract information, and then killed the perpetrators and their families.

After about two months, the company gradually began to stabilize.

• • •

When the world was noisy because of the appearance of LK-99, there was a particularly quiet place.

It was the Neo London Maid Academy. The Maid Academy continued its ordinary life despite the appearance of superconductors. But they didn't completely ignore LK-99. The maids of the Maid Academy are elites among elites. Understanding social trends was also one of the maids' jobs. The maids quietly analyzed social phenomena and predicted the future.

"The stock market will fluctuate. The value of electronic products that use superconductors will definitely rise."

"I heard that the Neo London Assembly has shares in LK Corp? The Assembly is very interested in the military... so the stock of military companies will rise."

"You're all wrong. Only the companies that have a contract with LK Corp will grow. The value of the other companies will eventually plummet. We need to focus on LK Corp's moves."

"LK Corp has announced its entry into the small generator market... but the other fields are quiet. There's not enough information."

"Isn't CEO Eugene Meyer a patron of Yuria-yang? Yuria-yang, do you know or have you heard any information related to LK Corp?"

All eyes were on Yuria, who was sitting quietly and reading today's newspaper. Yuria put the newspaper down on the desk. Even that simple action felt elegant, and some of the maids exclaimed.

"I'm sorry. I can't talk about information related to the company."

It was a natural thing to say, but Yuria knew all the internal information of LK Corp. Eugene often asked her for advice, so she couldn't help but know.

"...Oh dear. We've committed a discourtesy. It's natural to keep secrets.... We made a mistake because we were so excited. I'm sorry, Yuria-yang."

"It's okay. I know how you all feel."

Yuria raised her head and looked at the lights on the ceiling.

"It might be trendy to replace the lights soon."

It was an unexpected remark.

"I, I see. Lights. It's about time to replace them."

"If it's going to be a trend.... Hmm...."

"Thank you, Yuria-yang. Anyway, thank you."

Yuria smiled quietly. It was okay to give out this much information. It would be known to the public soon anyway. It's more beneficial to win their favor with these small pieces of information.

The maids, who had been talking to each other, sat down in their seats and closed their mouths as soon as it was exactly 9 a.m. The lecture hall became quiet in an instant.

Soon, the lecture hall door opened and the instructor maid appeared.

"Good morning, everyone."

"Yes. Good morning, Instructor-nim."

"I have something to tell you today. In ten days, you will each be assigned to a field practice. You will work as maids in noble families for a week. Even if it's practice, labor is labor. You will be paid. Act without shame as maids of the Academy."

The maids were of one mind. The time had come. In their hearts, they were running wild, but on the outside, they maintained their composure and dignity.

"Do you have any questions?"

At the instructor's words, the maids raised their hands like bamboo shoots after rain.

"Yes. Juliet-yang."

"Can we know in advance which noble family we'll be working for?"

"We'll let you know on the day of, for the sake of fairness. One thing is for sure, they are families with a long history. So please forgive us if you are rude. Emily-yang, ask your question."

"I heard that there's an evaluation for the field practice. What are the evaluation criteria?"

"I can't tell you the evaluation criteria. If you use what you've learned so far, your evaluation score won't be low. Effort doesn't betray you. Be confident. Line-yang. Ask your question."

"A Dark Moon will rise during the field practice period... Could monster hunting be one of the maids' jobs?"

"It could be, or it could not be. A maid's job varies depending on the noble family. Don't be afraid. Your information has already been conveyed to the noble families. They won't ask you to do impossible things. The next question is... it seems like there aren't any. Today, I will lecture on the history of Divine France. The history of baguettes is crude and boring, but it will be useful someday if you know it."
