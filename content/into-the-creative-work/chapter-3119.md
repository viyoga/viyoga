The fact that the Republic of Korea had become the Holy Korean Empire didn't dramatically change the lives of its citizens.

With the imperial proclamation and the simultaneous lifting of martial law, the lives of individual citizens actually improved.

A certain degree of freedom was granted, and markets began to revitalize.

"Oh my. Rice prices are dropping these days?"

"Rice is coming in from Japan. On top of that, some new technology has been developed, so fruits and vegetables are being mass-produced and getting cheaper too."

"Pork belly is this cheap?"

"It's cloned pork. Taste-wise, there's hardly any difference from regular pork."

Food prices began to lower and stabilize.

"This too is His Majesty the Emperor's grace."

The credit naturally went to Emperor Seong Yujin. It was indeed the Emperor's decisions that had improved the lives of imperial citizens. Though it wasn't quite at the level of the world before the D-Virus appeared.

Of course, the improvement in imperial citizens' daily lives didn't come for free. There is no effect without a cause. The burden of the prosperity enjoyed by imperial citizens was being borne by Japan.

Imperial citizens didn't pity the Japanese. This was a right they deserved to enjoy as victors of war. In fact, the Japanese themselves weren't all that dissatisfied either. The Japanese government, in preparation for war, had controlled its citizens excessively. The majority of Japanese felt that the present was better compared to those days.

"As of today, imperial citizen ranks have been announced."

The news anchor chattered on.

The ranks were divided into eight tiers total.

1st Rank: Holy Knights, general-grade and above high-ranking officials are included.

Benefits – Through the Emperor's grace, one can maintain youth.

2nd Rank: Grade 5 civil servants, imperial merit recipients, special Awakened.

Benefits – Once every 10 years, one may receive the Emperor's grace. To become younger or regain health.

3rd Rank: Ordinary imperial citizens

Benefits – May reside on the Korean Peninsula.

4th Rank: Civil servants of the Japanese territory

5th Rank: Imperial citizens of the Japanese territory

6th Rank: Criminals.

7th Rank: Felons.

8th Rank: Traitors.

"All imperial citizens of Korean origin begin at 3rd Rank. Detailed benefits by rank can be confirmed on the official website or at public institutions. As this is a policy carried out by imperial decree, all imperial citizens are to comply. Long live His Majesty the Emperor."

What drew imperial citizens' attention was the benefit of the 2nd Rank.

The Emperor's grace.

There was no one who didn't know what that was.

The Emperor's ability, Recovery, could cure incurable diseases and even grant youth.

Youth.

Even ordinary people could obtain youth if they simply put in the effort.

Of course, it wasn't easy. A Grade 5 civil servant was core personnel capable of wielding considerable influence. In military terms, it was equivalent to a field-grade officer.

"The era of civil servants has arrived!"

"Even if it's just to give my aging parents youth, I must become a Grade 5 civil servant!"

"Once every 10 years means my family can maintain youth for life!"

Imperial citizens began preparing for the civil service exam.

Becoming a merit recipient was another way to reach 2nd Rank, but most imperial citizens pretended not to see and shook their heads. It was because accumulating merit through ordinary means was impossible.

To accumulate merit, one had to be of great service to the nation, but there was no proper method.

Inventions that benefit the nation? The Empire's technology was supreme. Ordinary imperial citizens had no conditions to do anything.

The fastest way to accumulate merit was to enlist in the military and build achievements. The downside was the high probability of losing one's life before achieving anything.

"With my brain, it's impossible to break through that competition rate and become a Grade 5 civil servant. But it's unfair to grow old and die without doing anything.... I'll enlist. For His Majesty the Emperor!"

"I'm an Awakened! Please raise me to 2nd Rank! What? You say with my ability I can't become a special Awakened? Why? Are you ignoring the super-strength ability?!"

The uproar was the same in the Japanese territory of the Holy Korean Empire.

Japanese begin at 5th Rank. Japanese civil servants are 4th Rank, but the benefits weren't much different. At best, the Empire would throw them a few extra supplies.

"The Empire says it treats us as imperial citizens in words, but.... Look at those ranks. We have to be screened and enter Seoul, the capital, and even though we're the same imperials, we can't reside on the Korean Peninsula. This is blatant discrimination."

"Didn't you expect this? If this is the price of losing the war...."

"Still, the opportunity is open. If you pass the promotion exam, you can become 3rd Rank and a true imperial citizen!"

Fortunately, the promotion exam—the hope of 4th and 5th Ranks—is held regularly.

The problem was that the promotion exam wasn't easy. Native-level Korean proficiency was basic, and one had to memorize Korean history thoroughly. Becoming 3rd Rank in a short period was impossible.

"Wouldn't enlisting in the military be faster?"

"Idiot. For a 5th Rank to become a merit recipient, ordinary achievements won't cut it. And do you think there's no discrimination in the military? If you can't speak Korean, you can't even enlist. Looking at it from all angles, enlisting after becoming 3rd Rank is better."

"Hey. Did you hear that news? The Kazuma family next door reported a rebellious Japanese to the Government-General and was recognized for merit, becoming 2nd Rank. As a reward, he had an audience with His Majesty the Emperor and regained his youth."

"...I saw a young man at that house. I thought he was Kazuma's son, but it was him?!"

"There's another way to become a merit recipient right away. S-Clones scattered and hidden all over the archipelago.... Kill the illegal clones of His Majesty the Emperor that the Japanese government created, and offer their corpses to the Government-General. They say up to 6 people can share the credit.... You interested?"

Whether on the Korean Peninsula or the archipelago, vitality began to circulate throughout the entire Empire. Of course, given the times, it wasn't all peaceful. Occasionally, mutant hordes would attack cities. The national military never lost, though.

And there was a rumor circulating secretly throughout the Empire.

"There's a way to become 2nd Rank the fastest. Enter the National Service Department, one of the administrative bureaus on the imperial mainland. The problem is, they say they only select women of outstanding character and beauty. In reality, it's said that only outstanding beauty matters. Once one person enters the National Service Department... all other direct family members are also recognized as 2nd Rank. Hey. You have a beautiful daughter, don't you? Have her try."

"...I'm Japanese, though."

"The National Service Department doesn't look at other qualification criteria."

"You're telling me to sell my daughter to a place that's a brothel by name? Are you insane?"

"The National Service Department is not a brothel. It's called the National Service Department in name, but in reality, it's a department that serves only His Majesty the Emperor. In other words, entering the National Service Department is, in old-fashioned terms, the same as becoming a concubine. Hey. You're missing an arm, aren't you? If you become 2nd Rank, you can restore your arm through His Majesty's grace. And if your daughter makes a request, you might even become young again."

"...An arm and youth."

What could be gained by selling his daughter was far too tempting. Especially youth, which couldn't be bought even with money.

"......In this damned world, becoming His Majesty's concubine might actually be happier. I'll make the offer to my daughter."

The Empire was peaceful.

With His Majesty the Emperor's benevolent rule, the people did not starve, so it could truly be called an era of great peace.

• • •

My daily life as Emperor didn't change much.

The Empire's minor affairs were handled by Lee Yeonhee and other civil servants. What I had to do was set the Empire's direction.

'And bestow grace upon the sick and elderly who come once a day.'

Youth.

The fervor for it was far more tremendous than I'd imagined. People would sell even their own daughters without hesitation for the sake of youth.

'Though there are many who don't go that far. Whether they'll still be able to say that after seeing their aged selves 10 years from now is another matter.'

These days, elderly people in their 50s and above were volunteering for the military to accumulate merit.

It wasn't bad. The most important thing in this era is military power. Even an old person can pull a trigger, so they could pull their weight. If push comes to shove, they can pop drugs. Then even a 50-something can perform no less than a 20-something.

'If I had my way, I'd make them all into 20-somethings and put them to work on active duty, but.'

Handing out youth indiscriminately wasn't good.

Youth is a treasure. Treasures should rightly be given as rewards.

"Your Majesty."

Lee Yeonhee called for me.

"It's about time for your appointment."

"I see."

I rose from my seat. I don my exclusive power armor, Eternity, and head to the audience chamber.

Passing the guard knights who protect the audience chamber, I sat on the throne. The knights brought in cameras and large monitors and set them up before the throne.

7 PM sharp.

The monitor screen flickered on and a man appeared.

He was a white middle-aged man in his 50s with a slightly receding hairline.

Marcel Durand.

He was the chairman of the European Federation, the force that had occupied war-ravaged Europe.

"Pleasure to meet you, President Seong. Or should I call you Emperor now?"

Hidden in that voice was an invisible sneer. A jab asking what era this was, playing at being emperor.

I didn't bother getting angry at every little thing. Even this meeting itself was simply tiresome.

"What's your business?"

"His Majesty is impatient."

"Enough. If you cross the line any further, I'll take it as a declaration of war. I'll give Europe a nuclear shower."

Marcel's expression stiffened momentarily, then returned.

"My, my. Can't even take a joke. But could you really carry out a nuclear war? Our European Federation also has quite a few nuclear missiles in reserve."

"On my order, I can launch hundreds of nuclear missiles right now. How about you?"

"...We too can respond in emergency situations."

"Nonsense. Do you think I know nothing about your European Federation? An organization born from surviving European nations crying and eating mustard, joining hands. You may hold power above that of a president, but you're not at the level of deciding everything alone. I, on the other hand, am different. Everything in the Empire is decided by me. Even war is no exception."

"...Hah. In these turbulent times, a dictatorial state like yours might be the answer. Still, the Empire's regression is a bit much, though."

"State your business."

"An alliance. Europe has stabilized. It's time to pay attention to diplomacy. I hear the American continent has also nearly stabilized. Imperialists and corporate states like your country are preparing for a final showdown, I'm told.... You know America's latent power without me telling you. A unified America will be more powerful than the former United States. We need to join hands."

Marcel pulled out a cigarette and put it in his mouth. My eyebrow twitched. From using casual speech to me, he was treating me as an equal rather than respecting me as Emperor.

"I've heard your justification for the alliance well enough. The cause itself isn't bad. So what are the important terms of the alliance?"

"First, technology sharing. I demand that power armor technology. In exchange, our Europe will offer war technology accumulated through blood. Also, I need your ability. Give youth to me and the council members. After that, we'll discuss the occupation of India."

"Technology sharing is unnecessary. You want youth? Offer 10 European beauties, and I'll bestow youth upon one of them."

"...You're telling me to pay tribute right now?"

"Don't like it? Then let's say there's no talk of an alliance. For your reference, the Holy Korean Empire judges all nations except allies as potential hostile states. If a nuclear missile falls tonight, accept it as 'what was coming has come.'"
