District 6,700, the Kingdom of Entertainment, boasts a population comparable to that of a decent-sized nation, true to its name. A whopping 10 million people are packed into this district.

Of course, a large population wasn't necessarily a good thing. The most important thing in [Atlantis] was individual power.

The people of District 6,700 lacked the strength and experience gained from fighting monsters. District 6,700 was a prosperous place where monsters didn't appear, so there was no reason to be strong. External access was also strictly controlled by the system, so they didn't feel any danger. That was the reason why District 6,700 didn't have an army.

In other words, District 6,700 was a greenhouse, and the people in this district were flowers in a greenhouse.

,Whether they're weeds or flowers, I don't care. The important thing is that the population is 10 million. Surely there's at least one beauty among them who'll catch my eye?'

I wandered around the famous spots in District 6,700. Places with a lot of foot traffic. I planned to find a beauty who caught my eye and woo her. In other words, street casting.

But after three hours, I realized I'd been oversimplifying things.

,How is it that there isn't a single woman I like?'

A city of 10 million people. And I'd been sitting in places with a lot of foot traffic for three hours. Most of the passersby were young people, and they all had great hair and fashion sense. I also found a number of eye-catching beauties. But if you asked if they had the kind of beauty to make them celebrities... absolutely not.

,Compared to Ju Seo-hyun, they're just average Joes. If I put them in an idol group with Ju Seo-hyun, it'd be a disaster. The difference in beauty is too great, so they'd definitely be at the level of backup dancers.'

What I wanted wasn't Ju Seo-hyun's backup dancers, but Ju Seo-hyun's colleagues. Girl groups aren't a solo act, after all.

I spent another two hours wandering the streets.

It was a waste of time.

I wasted five hours.

There was no beauty on par with Ju Seo-hyun.

,...Come to think of it, there's no way other agencies would leave a beauty on Ju Seo-hyun's level alone. Ju Seo-hyun also received a contract offer from Lilith Entertainment as soon as she came here.'

District 6,700 is called the Kingdom of Entertainment.

True to its name, it's a special district where entertainers are the mainstream. There are over 100 entertainment agencies in this district alone.... They must have swept up all the decent beauties.

,I have no choice. Let's change the plan. I'll woo some moderately pretty girls and create a girl group with Ju Seo-hyun as the one-top.'

A top-class idol contracted with Lilith Entertainment? That could be achieved with Ju Seo-hyun's power alone.

,If it doesn't seem like it'll work, I can just run away.'

So, I lowered my standards and approached a moderately pretty woman.

,Hello. Could you spare a moment to talk with me?'

I approached a woman with an air of elegance and asked politely. The woman looked me up and down. She seemed to know she was pretty, and she was a bit of a bitch.

,It doesn't feel like a pick-up... Are you from an agency?'

,Ah, yes. You recognized me right away.'

,This is the 17th time.'

The woman held out her hand to me. I was taken aback. Was she asking for money? I took out my wallet from my pocket and clumsily placed some money on her hand.

,What do you think you're doing?!'

,...Weren't you asking for money?'

,Do you think I'm a beggar? I meant give me your business card. Why are you so clueless? Are you a low-level employee at the agency?'
