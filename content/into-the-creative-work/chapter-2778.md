Germadon's massive body turned to dust and crumbled. Before I was crushed to death in the dust, I unleashed lightning.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Barrier.

Most of the dust was iron dust. I used magnetism to push it away from my body. Because I was floating with magnetic force, I didn't fall even when the floor collapsed.

Feeling the power surging within me, I checked the notification window floating in the air.

『You have killed the seventh legion commander! The Demon Hunter (SS+) rejoices!』

『The Demon Hunter (SS+) rank rises to SSS.』

『Due to the influence of the Demon Hunter (SSS), physical abilities are greatly increased.』

『You have hunted a demon. Partially restores health and mana.』

『The Demon Hunter (SSS) absorbs some of the killed demon's authority.』

『Karma: Good increases by 220.』

『The contract with the Spirit King has been completed.』

Kwa-rrr-rrr!

Thunder rumbled. It was a sound coming from the ground, not the sky.

I pushed away the pouring dust and looked outside.

Seong Hari, wreathed in blue lightning, was there. She was looking up at the sky with a slightly surprised expression.

The tremendous power emanating from Seong Hari was so strong that it vibrated the surrounding land and air.

Crack! Crack!

Seong Hari was just standing there, but the road was cracking.

[Is the old Seong Hari coming back? I can't just be happy about it... I'm getting old too.]

The Spirit King's curse, which had been suppressing Seong Hari's abilities for decades, was lifted.

The Buster Clan and Son Jeong-eon, who had been surrounding Seong Hari, were watching with stiff faces. The power they felt was too extraordinary to dare attack first.

'The other heroes affiliated with the association... most of them are dead or unable to fight.'

Seong Hari was still staring at the sky with dazed eyes. The immense power that made the surrounding area tense just by existing began to subside. Seong Hari was quickly controlling her suddenly increased power.

'Just how strong has she become?'

I couldn't stand it out of curiosity. I checked Seong Hari's status window.

『Name: Seong Hari

Strength: SSS Stamina: SS Agility: SSS Durability: SS Mana: SS+

Traits: Spirit Devourer (S), Indra's Thunderbolt (SSS)

Skills: Divine Spear Unification (SSS), Spear Throw (SS), Combat Sense (SS), Purification (D+), Barrier (B)

Affection: 100

Psychology: The Spirit King's curse... is gone...!』

Kang Ji-young and I unknowingly swallowed our breaths.

Stats, traits, and skills had all suddenly increased across the board. It wasn't hard to guess the reason.

'The Spirit King's curse didn't just lower Seong Hari's stats. It was suppressing her growth, her potential!'

Seong Hari didn't just laze around while weakened by the Spirit King's curse for the past few decades.

I, who lived with her, know it well. Seong Hari had been constantly practicing with her spear. She focused on improving her skills even if her stats didn't increase. It wasn't like she lacked practical experience either.

'It's because of me. Seong Hari, who already spends a lot, would occasionally get elixirs and feed them to me.'

They were things she obtained while unofficially working as a hero.

And in the process, Seong Hari herself also took elixirs. That's why she was able to maintain A-rank hero-level stats despite the Spirit King's curse.

All those things that had accumulated, those things blocked by the Spirit King's curse dam, burst out at once.

Seong Hari completely controlled her suddenly increased power and then looked down at me. And she smiled.

,I'm glad you're safe. And thank you. Yujin-i has settled things with the Spirit King? Trust this momma with the rest."

Before I could answer, Seong Hari moved.

Kwa-rrr-rrr!

Just lightly swinging the spear in her hand caused thunder to rumble, and the Buster Clan members who were surrounding her fell lightly. The heroes, who had skills of at least A-rank or higher, couldn't even react properly. Only Son Jeong-eon barely managed to dodge by quickly bending over.

[Hari was once called the strongest hero. She was the strongest in humanity, the strongest of all time. But now she has even surpassed the power of those days. I'm relieved. At least humanity won't be destroyed.]

Son Jeong-eon gritted his teeth and created swords in the air. Over a hundred swords were created and fell like rain on Seong Hari. A flash of lightning flickered around Seong Hari. The falling swords avoided Seong Hari and stuck into the ground around her.

"This is fucking ridiculous!! This is way beyond what I expected!"

Seong Hari moved. She became a blue flash of lightning and appeared in front of Son Jeong-eon in an instant. Son Jeong-eon urgently revealed his magic power.

'That's not the Thunderbolt that Seong Hari usually uses through lightning... it's the Lightning Heaven Style Flying Thunder God...?!'

While I was in shock, Seong Hari swung her spear. Before Son Jeong-eon, who had revealed his magic power, could do anything, his head fell to the ground.

Immediately after, electromagnetic waves erupted from Seong Hari and swept through the entire city of Seoul.

Seong Hari's gaze turned to the sky. She took a spear-throwing stance and threw the lightning-wreathed spear into the sky.

A blue lightning bolt pierced the sky. It was so powerful and fast that it blew a hole through the clouds in its path. It was truly piercing the heavens.

The spear hit a satellite orbiting in low Earth orbit and caused an explosion.

-Wow! Noutroi is dead!

Noutroi.

The great demon of the 7th Legion with the authority of disguise, and a cyber demon.

'Was Noutroi's main body on the satellite? Or was the satellite itself possessed by Noutroi?'

The great demon died without even struggling. Even though he had descended to Earth and was weakened by restrictions, I didn't expect it to be this easy.

Seong Hari picked up a spear that was nearby. It was a memento of a hero affiliated with the association.

"Yujin-a. I'll go clean up for a bit."

Kwa-rrr-rrr!

By the time I heard the thunder, she had already disappeared.

Thanks to my excellent eyes, I was able to grasp Seong Hari's movements a little. She was rampaging through Seoul, slaughtering machine demons. Even if she didn't step in, the heroes would definitely win now that Germadon was dead, but she was doing that because....

'It's partly to show off her power to the world... but more than anything, it's to get used to using her power. Seong Hari looks excited too.'

In a few hours, the whole world will realize.

That the strongest hero has finally returned.

I took my eyes off her and moved to the side. When I cleared away the accumulated iron dust, I saw the highest-ranking dark spirit who had been captured by Germadon and suffered for hundreds, thousands of years.

Darkness that had taken on a human form. The form was flickering as if it was filled with noise.

'I was going to contract with the highest-ranking dark spirit... but it's too weak.'

Germadon's power plant was achieving both exploitation and maintenance at the same time.

"The spirit gate. Shall I open it for you?"

-...No. Even if I return to the spirit realm, I will disappear. Let me disappear like this. I want rest.

The dark spirit gradually began to disappear from its fingertips and toes.

-I am grateful to you for saving me from endless pain. There must be something left of me after I disappear. You take it.

The dark spirit disappeared.

What was left in its place was a black gemstone, a black diamond.

『Legacy of the Dark Spirit

Rank: SS

Contains the power of the dark spirit.

Can absorb and release darkness.

Can control darkness.』

The effect is less than expected for an SS rank. Isn't this about A or S rank level?

'The biggest advantage is that I can control darkness... but I don't know if I can use it.'

Even if I couldn't use it directly, I could give it as a gift to someone else or use it for trading, so I put it in my inventory.

Kwa-rrr-rrr! Kwa-rr!

The thunder continued to rumble.

Lightning Heaven Style Flying Thunder God.

Seong Hari became lightning and dashed through the city. Then, when she spotted an enemy, she swung her spear and annihilated them. A loud thunderclap erupted every time she swung her spear. It had been like that since the old days. When she was determined and released her power, thunder rumbled. The nickname given to her was Thunderous Roar of the Piercing Heavens.

'Yujin-i's Lightning Heaven Style is definitely more convenient in many ways.'

Seong Hari learned the Lightning Heaven Style from Seong Yujin. She naturally came into contact with the Lightning Heaven Style while training with Seong Yujin since she was young.

Unlike her spear technique, which relied half on instinct, Seong Yujin's Lightning Heaven Style was neatly organized.

'Was it called Lightning Heaven Knot? Honestly, I still don't understand more than half of it.'

Nevertheless, Seong Hari was able to use the Lightning Heaven Style thanks to her innate talent and the trait she possessed, Indra's Thunderbolt (SSS). Indra's Thunderbolt is the power to control lightning like Seong Yujin's lightning.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Wave.

Seong Hari's electromagnetic waves were more than enough to cover the entire city of Seoul. If she wanted to, she could scan the entire Korean peninsula. Maybe even more than that. She couldn't even guess where her limits were right now.

Lightning Heaven Style Gale Thunderbolt.

A technique derived from her own Thunderbolt. After using it directly, she could tell. In many ways, it was a superior version of Thunderbolt. What was impressive was its stability. Thunderbolt puts a considerable strain on the body, and it can be dangerous if concentration drops, whereas Gale Thunderbolt put less strain. That doesn't mean the effect is less.

Slash!

The machine demon died without even screaming. Before the machine demon's corpse could fall to the ground, Seong Hari slashed another machine demon 30m away.

'The Lightning Heaven Style is a way to handle lightning. If you know how to handle lightning, you can use most of the Lightning Heaven Style techniques.'

Seong Hari could also use the Lightning Heaven Style swordsmanship. However, she just didn't use it. Swords weren't her taste, and spears were several times more comfortable than swords. And she couldn't use the Lightning Heaven Style spells. Seong Hari's talent was biased to one side, so she didn't understand magic any better than Seong Yujin.

'As a mom, it's a bit embarrassing to only use my son's techniques. It seems like Yujin-i has been interested in spear techniques recently. If possible, I'd like to teach Yujin-i my techniques too... Hmm. Is this how it is in the Lightning Heaven Style?'

Lightning Heaven Style Electric Spear Form Wave Source.

Seong Hari swung her lightning-filled spear greatly. The spear accelerated due to the lightning, and the machine demon's body within range was cut off. Without stopping there, a shockwave spread out in a circle and swept through the surroundings.

Not a bad feeling.

She continued to slaughter machine demons and compile a spear technique to pass on to Seong Yujin.
