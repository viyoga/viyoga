,"Jonas Muller. He worked as a secretary for an executive at a mid-sized company called Blood Rack, which researches and combines blood. He killed his employer last night and stole the company's assets and belongings. The item is the Alchemy Blood that Blood Rack has been researching for a long time."

,"...Alchemy Blood. I've never heard of it before."

,"It's a medium that allows even mana users to use alchemy. Or so I hear."

,"The incident happened last night, and it's related to alchemy. It must have been influenced by LK Corporation's new product, Adarium."

Yuria immediately grasped the situation.

The information about Adarium, which appeared at the royal charity auction yesterday, spread around the world in an instant. As information spread that Adarium could only be handled with alchemy, not ordinary methods, the value related to alchemy rose. The personal value of alchemists, as well as stocks related to alchemy, skyrocketed.

The Alchemy Blood would be sold at the highest price right now.

,"Dean-nim. Isn't this usually the job of the London Guard or the London Knights? Looking at the documents, all the information, including the criminal's location, is available. If the London Guard steps in, they can catch him right away."

,"The location information is something I investigated and found out separately. The wanted notice for Jonas Muller will be announced in two days. We must catch Jonas Muller before that."

,"Is it to protect the reputation of the maids and butlers?"

,"A butler who betrayed his employer. This isn't just Jonas Muller's problem. It's something that harms all servants working in Neo London. We must eliminate Jonas Muller as secretly and quickly as possible."

Yuria nodded.

This was a matter of trust. If this became known, it would negatively affect the entire industry. It would harm the job changes, employment, and salaries of maids and butlers.

,'Honestly, I don't really care.'

After all, the only master Yuria will serve is one person. The servant industry? It didn't matter if it collapsed. She had no intention of changing masters and working.

Anyway, this is Maria Hilton's evaluation, isn't it?

,"Understood. Are the other targets similar people?"

,"No. This is a request and an order from the royal family."

,"...It's surprising that such an order is coming to the academy."

,"It can't be helped. The Maid Academy and the Butler Academy basically possess military force. We have no choice but to cooperate with the parliament and the royal family."

If you're aiming for Flower-class or higher, military force is essential. In reality, even Triple-class is at the level of a decent mercenary.

,"Yuria-yang. The other two people you have to eliminate are nobles. Both are barons, one is an anti-establishment activist, and the other is a spy from the Freesel Kingdom. I'll leave the method of elimination to Yuria-yang. This is also an evaluation."

,"Is there a backup plan in case I fail?"

,"Are you worried about failing? That's not like Yuria-yang. What do you think, Yuria-yang? Do you think I would have prepared for your failure?"

,"If I were Dean Maria... I think I would have prepared. Preparing for the unexpected is also a maid's duty."

,"As expected of Yuria-yang. Excellent. Here's a new ID card that I prepared just in case. It's an ID card approved by the parliament, so you won't get caught anywhere. The identity will be discarded in two days. Also, here's a card with a down payment of 10 million credits. Feel free to withdraw and use it if you need cash."

,"Thank you, Dean-nim."

The documents were perfectly memorized in her head the moment she saw them. Yuria took only what she needed and tried to leave.

,"Yuria-yang. This is your first time doing something like this, right? You might feel it's unfair to be evaluated for something you're doing for the first time. Even if you're a Level 7, the first time is always unfamiliar. But I believe you'll do well."

Maria thought that Yuria had reached Level 7 because she was lucky, just because of her talent. It was understandable. Yuria had never directly shown anything related to force. Also, her actions were far from combat.

A graduate of the Knight Training Center? There was no particularly noticeable practical experience either.

,"I will live up to your expectations."

She wouldn't know. That for Yuria, this kind of work was just a normal task. Also, that her nickname in another world was the Dark Emperor.

A plan had already been made in Yuria's head.

• • •

Jonas Muller was heading towards the western border. Even though he could have left Neo London immediately after committing the crime last night, he headed towards the border. This means he has a separate plan. Or he judged that this side was safer.

Either way, there was a high possibility that he had an accomplice.

Instead of chasing Jonas Muller right away, Yuria decided to deal with the other two targets first. She judged that it would be more efficient in terms of time.

,'The result is already determined. Dean Maria knows it too.'

Yuria was Level 7. And she didn't bother to hide her power. Maybe if it was an ambiguous power. It's more beneficial to announce the power of Level 7.

,'This isn't a difficult task that requires a Level 7 to step in.'

So, the evaluation that Maria mentioned is not about the result of the mission, but about the process. What is the best result she wants from this?

,'Quietly and quickly. If possible, without a trace.'

It wasn't a difficult task.

Yuria sneaked into the noble's mansion in broad daylight. She can't use magic in this world. But that doesn't mean she doesn't know about magic.

The barrier installed in the mansion? You can easily break it by slightly twisting the barrier formula. However, this time, she wrapped her body in shadows and entered the barrier. The barrier didn't even recognize Yuria.

Her shadow thoroughly searches the inside of the mansion and collects information in real time. That's how she found out the location of the target.

Shadow movement.

Yuria moved instantly to the shadow she had identified. It is a space movement technique that can be used because of her high understanding of space.

She appeared in the shadow behind the study. The target, a pot-bellied man, seemed busy reviewing documents.

Yuria pondered for a moment before coming to a conclusion.

,'A cerebral hemorrhage would be more natural and better than a heart attack.'

The shadow near his ear moved and entered his ear. Soon, the man opened his eyes wide and collapsed from his seat.

,"Keuheok?!"

Immediately after, the terminal inside the man's body sends some kind of signal to the London Knights headquarters. The target was a London Knights insurance subscriber. Yuria knew this but didn't touch the terminal. Doing so would make it even more unnatural.

Yuria dealt with the other target with a heart attack. Two nobles suddenly died. It's suspicious, but there's no evidence, so it will be treated as an accidental death. The upper echelons of Neo London will want that.

The next target is only Jonas Muller.

There's no reason to drag out the mission. She walked into the dark shadows. No one could see her.

• • •

Jonas Muller, wearing a butler's uniform and carrying a travel bag, headed towards the western border.

He was surprisingly calm for someone who killed his employer last night. He is a Flower-class butler. He wasn't so incompetent that he would get excited and flustered just because he killed one person. Of course, he hid the badge signifying Flower in his pocket.

The badge is good in many ways, but it attracts too much attention. He likes the badge because it has authority, but it becomes a hindrance when he needs to avoid being seen by people, like now.

He moved confidently. If he acted suspiciously because he was intimidated by committing a crime, it would attract attention instead.

The reason he was still wearing the butler's uniform? Servants themselves are common in Neo London.

You can work as a maid or butler even without a license. It's just that you won't receive as good a salary or treatment as someone with a license.

,'There are no watchful eyes. I thought at least one or two London Guards would follow me. Did I overestimate myself too much? Well, more than half of them are corrupt cops, so they wouldn't move easily.'

The closer he got to the western border, the more attention Jonas received. It can't be helped. A butler's uniform, which is common in the city, becomes harder to see as you head towards the border.

,'But I don't want to change. I'm different from those guys. I'm a Flower-class butler. An elite among elites. Even if the situation is bad, I can't wear the same clothes as those guys.'

Above all, he, who has a slight case of cleanliness, didn't want to wear dusty clothes.

He recalled the plan while riding in the carriage.

He will meet with Alphatier Pharmaceutical's agents at the western border and cross the border. After that, he will trade with blood alchemy at Alphatier headquarters. And he will prosper by gaining enormous wealth and an executive-level position at Alphatier.

,'My abilities are too good to live as a mere butler.'

He used to be satisfied with this position. High salary and social authority recognized by everyone. Lower-class nobles are conscious of him, and most businessmen are conscious of him.

But the essence is that he is a servant employed by someone.

,'With my abilities, I don't need to serve a master.'

At first, he thought about starting a business... but there was no suitable business model. No matter how mediocre a company he created, it was clear that it would be trampled on by a slightly larger company.

Mercenary work? He didn't get a servant license to do such a lowly job.

,'A large corporation executive. Even if it's not an executive, a decent manager or section chief would be nice.'

The carriage stops. He has arrived at his destination.

,"Sir. We have arrived at your destination."

At the coachman's words, he carefully got off so that dirt wouldn't get on his body. The carriage led by the mechanical horse turned around and returned to Neo London.

He got off at the western border, looked around, and clicked his tongue. Compared to the city, it's as shabby as a pioneer village. Wooden buildings are more common than concrete buildings, and the floor is not maintained, so trash is rolling around.

,'Even the air seems dirtier. I want to meet the corporate agents and leave this place as quickly as possible.'

He heads to the hideout where Alphatier corporate agents are staying. The number of people gradually decreases, and finally, no one is felt. The street itself seemed dead.

,'Is it really a company? They managed to find a place like this and use it as a hideout.'

As he headed towards the hideout, he felt uneasy. It was too quiet.

Shouldn't corporate agents be watching by now? Or is he not able to detect the presence of corporate agents? He, a Flower-class butler?

He suppressed his anxiety and entered the hideout building. It was a shabby two-story building. A building that was once used as a bar. He opened the door on the first floor, which was on the verge of breaking, and went inside.

There were more than 8 people. Sitting in chairs, standing, or leaning against the wall. Men and women in different poses. Strangely, no one was felt at all.

Among them, the one that caught his eye was a woman. A beautiful woman with blue-silver hair wearing a maid uniform. She was sitting in a chair reading a newspaper. It's today's newspaper with a picture of Count Eugene Meyer.

,"You've arrived. I've been waiting for you. Jonas Muller."

It was strange.

She's clearly speaking, but why can't I feel her at all?

Is she even human? Is she a hologram? Jonas swallowed hard and asked her.

,"Who are you? Are you an Alphatier corporate agent?"

,"Do corporate agents wear maid uniforms these days instead of suits? I didn't expect to hear such a stupid question."

Jonas gritted his teeth.

,"...These people. Did you kill them all?"

,"Yes. I wasn't originally planning to touch them... but I killed them because they said they were Alphatier."

Alphatier is the master's enemy. Therefore, she was also her enemy. Interrogation? She didn't do it. There was no way that disposable pawns would know high-level information.

Yuria got up from her seat. She carefully folded the newspaper and dropped it on the floor. The sound of the newspaper falling could not be heard. It was sucked into the shadows on the floor.

,"...A blue-silver haired maid. Right. I've heard of her. I heard there's a maid aiming for Prime-class at the Maid Academy. Indeed. It seems the Maid Academy sent you to kill me. I heard you recently became Level 7."

,"It's still a bit unpleasant for my name to be mentioned by others..."

A shadow silently soared from behind Jonas. The jet-black shadow merged into one and became a sword, aiming for Jonas's neck and falling.

Blood spurted from Jonas's neck. The blood became a sword and blocked the shadow sword. The shadow sword bounced off and disappeared.

As far as Yuria knew, Jonas's fighting style was close combat using short-term future prediction. He didn't have any abilities using blood.

,"That's the Alchemy Blood you stole. You were hiding it in your body, not in your bag."

,"It's common sense to keep important items in the safest place."

Jonas took out a black knuckle made of Adamantium from his pocket and put it on his hand.
