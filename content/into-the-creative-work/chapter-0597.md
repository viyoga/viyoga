The atmosphere of the escorts, including head escort Seok Ji-done, changed.

Seok Ji-done, who had always been so serious, became friendly to the embroiderers. He increased their break times and looked after their convenience. On the other hand, he ordered the escorts to do all sorts of chores.

Escorts treated worse than embroiderers.

Seok Ji-done openly harassed the escorts.

However, the escorts followed his orders without complaint. All three escorts wrote down Seok Ji-done's name. If they had resisted Seok Ji-done here, they could have been singled out as the one who wrote down Seok Ji-done's name.

,The moment that happens, my escort job is over. That bastard Seok Ji-done is also a Martial artist. He won't just stop at stripping me naked.'

The relationship between the three escorts was not good. They were the ones who betrayed their superior, Seok Ji-done. There was no way they would think fondly of each other as colleagues. They would probably think of each other as rivals.

The escorts also started treating the embroiderers well. They didn't give orders unless it was absolutely necessary, and they didn't nag. Occasionally, when they stopped by an inn on the way, they would treat the embroiderers to a bottle of alcohol each.

Of course, it wasn't free.

The embroiderers had to listen to the escorts' excuses and complaints. And also the voices of them slandering their colleagues.

,You guys probably don't know. How petty escort Hwang Bi-geon is. I've worked with him for almost five years, and I've never been treated well once. And I heard this in passing... escort Hwang Bi-geon is from the streets. You know, those guys. The ones who threaten innocent people with force and extort money. Don't get close to escort Hwang Bi-geon. Old habits die hard... he might be after your wealth.'

,It seems escort Yeong-tan was talking nonsense earlier. There was a big incident in the past because escort Yeong-tan lied. The head of the escort agency was merciful and didn't kick him out... he's not a pleasant guy to be around. Eighty percent of what comes out of his mouth is a lie, so there's no need to pay attention.'

The escorts were fiercely playing politics. Treating the embroiderers well was a kind of solicitation to get them on their side.

I chuckled inwardly as I watched their struggles.

,Yujin-ah. Why are the escorts suddenly acting like that?'

Innocent Seong Jigone tilted his head and asked me. I dragged him to a corner and explained the current situation.

,...Uh, you're the one who killed Go Du-mak wearing a human skin mask. But they don't even hold a grudge against you...?'

,Because I'm stronger than them. There aren't many weak people who hold a grudge against someone stronger who hurt them. They know very well that there's no chance of revenge succeeding.'

,So head escort Seok is looking for the one who wrote down his name? To get revenge?'

,That's right. He has to get revenge on the guys who are weaker than him.'

,But in reality, all the escorts wrote down head escort Seok's name....'

,The escorts don't know that fact. They probably think someone betrayed them. But what's important now isn't that. The situation has become that they can't get caught for writing down head escort Seok's name in order to survive.'

,Can't they just honestly ask for forgiveness?'

,Don't be stupid. He's the guy who tried to kill them. Would you forgive them if you were them?'

,...I wouldn't.'

,Keukeu. We just have to eat rice cakes and watch those guys. There won't be a dull moment until we go back. Well, one of them might not even make it back and die.'

,I'm a little disappointed. Weren't they colleagues who had worked together for years?'

,Jigone-ah. The Jianghu is more ruthless than you think. You can't easily trust someone just because they're a colleague. Even families stab each other in the back in the Jianghu. There aren't many relationships as good as yours and mine.'

,Eueum....'

,Keukeu. And if we stir up some discord between the escorts here, there might even be a sword fight. Should we try it?'

,Isn't stirring up discord dangerous?'

,We just have to do it carefully. If not....'

I smiled and explained my plan. Seong Jigone looked at me in shock and shook his head.

,Yujin-ah. You're a really scary guy.'

,It's something anyone could think of.'

Night fell.

The transport group set up camp as soon as it got dark and went to bed. The nights outside the city are the time for monsters, so they try not to move unless it's an emergency.

I secretly came out of the tent at night. I used the Illusiter to make myself invisible and suppressed my presence as much as possible before entering the escorts' tent and leaving a note.

,They must be on bad terms, they're not even sleeping together.'

The note said that another escort had reported you as the culprit to Seok Ji-done. A typical case of stirring up discord. But the escorts wouldn't be able to ignore this content. Because they really did write down Seok Ji-done's name.

,The atmosphere will be even more tense tomorrow.'

The atmosphere between the escorts reached its peak. They frowned and exuded killing intent even when they just made eye contact. The unrelated embroiderers were also on edge and kept their mouths shut, the atmosphere was so bad.

Head escort Seok Ji-done had no intention of mediating the relationship between the escorts.

The transport group moved forward in a tense atmosphere.

,The scenery is nice. We'll rest here.'

The transport group stopped briefly on top of a cliff.

I looked around quickly.

,This is the place from the original work! It's here!'

I took Seong Jigone to the biggest tree nearby.

,Yujin-ah. Why did you come here?'

,Because there's a lucky encounter here.'

,What?! Really?!'

Surprised, Seong Jigone looked around. But all he could see were cliffs and vegetation.

,Are you kidding me? These kinds of jokes aren't funny. I'm already having a hard time these days.'

,Why are you being so dramatic? I know you're having a hard time because you can't hold a woman anyway. And lucky encounters are usually hidden. Like this.'

I took out a dagger and stabbed it into the tree trunk. I could feel that the inside was empty. When I tore off part of the tree trunk, I saw three blooming flowers inside.

,Flowers are growing inside the tree?!'

,Mokchimhwa (Wood-Infesting Flower). Even though it looks like that, it's a spiritual medicine with the energy of fire.'

It's a spiritual medicine that blooms inside a tree, sucking up the tree's life force and spiritual energy as it grows.

,I only need one. You can have the other two.'

,C, can I really eat this?! You're the one who found it!'

,We're brothers. And you're weaker than me. If you're too weak, it'll be difficult for me. I don't want to take care of you for the rest of my life. Eat two and get stronger.'

,Yujin-ah...!'

Seong Jigone looked touched.

In this world, spiritual medicines are treasures among treasures. It's easy for an entire village to be wiped out because of a spiritual medicine, and they don't even give spiritual medicines to their own children.

,If you have these, things can get annoying, so you should take them right away. Eat them here.'

,Here? But the break time will be over soon?'

,Well. We'll probably have to stay here for about 2 hours?'

,What are you talking about?'

I pointed to the sky.

A giant bird with blue feathers was flying in the sky.

Cheonggwangjo (Blue Light Bird).

It's a spiritual creature that you can only deal with if you reach at least the Third Level of Jeong.

,We can't move recklessly with that thing in the sky. Seok Ji-done is cautious to begin with.'

,Cheonggwangjo! Isn't that monster going to attack us?!'

,Why would it? From the Cheonggwangjo's perspective, we're just pebbles rolling around. It'll pass by if we don't bother it. Hurry up and eat the Mokchimhwa and digest it.'

I sat cross-legged on the ground.

Originally, I would have used the Bright Light Ascension Art to enhance the Mokchimhwa before taking it, like the protagonist of the original work. But right now, the Bright Light Ascension Art is enhancing my weapon and armor, Stormbreaker. I can't just keep waiting because I don't know when the enhancement will end.

,I'm stronger than the original protagonist. And I can get more spiritual medicines later.'

I grabbed the Mokchimhwa and stuffed it into my mouth. I could taste the bitterness. I looked to the side and saw Seong Jigone nervously taking the Mokchimhwa.

,And I have the Cheongangseong System.'

I use the Yeongcheongi Art to control the fire energy of the Mokchimhwa. The efficiency of the Yeongcheongi Art was better than when I did it in reality.

It might be because this world is specialized in martial arts. Or it might be because of the influence of the Cheongangseong System. Personally, I think it's the latter.

After absorbing the energy of the Mokchimhwa for a while, I felt an invisible wall collapsing.

『Congratulations! You have reached the First Level of Chulji (Achieving Ambition)!』

『As a reward, you will receive 30 Cheonok (Heavenly Jade) and a Jiseongdan (Wisdom City Pill).』

I exhaled. My body was full of energy.

,I have 31 Cheonok now. I need 69 more to reach VIP 1.'

『Jiseongdan (Wisdom City Pill)

A spiritual medicine that helps you reach the level of Chulji.』

It was a spiritual medicine that wouldn't help me at all since I had already reached the level of Chulji.

,I'll give this to Seong Jigone later.'

I got up from my seat. Seong Jigone was still sitting cross-legged, diligently absorbing the energy of the spiritual medicine.

,Seong Jigone. It would be easier if this guy became useful soon.'

Seong Jigone got up from his seat just before we were about to leave. His face was full of joy.

,How is it? Did you reach a higher level?'

,Yeah! I'm now at the Eighth Level of Ipsik! It's all thanks to you! What about you, Yujin?'

,I reached Chulji.'

,Heok! Really?! Congratulations! Wow! To already be at the level of Chulji at that age! Other people would be so surprised they'd faint if they knew!'

,I'm going to keep it a secret for now. You better keep your mouth shut too.'

,Why? If you tell the sect, you can get even better support.'

,Because there are guys who will be suspicious and jealous if you're too outstanding.'

,I guess so.... Okay. I won't tell anyone, as you wish.'

The transport group set off again.
