「Entered Area 7,070, Dragon King's Palace.」

Atlantis has bonuses for being the first. Whether it's obtaining a title for the first time, or entering an area for the first time.

The fact that the first message didn't pop up meant someone had already been to this area.

,The Dragon King's Palace doesn't even appear in the original story, only in the setting book. Well, the entry requirements aren't that strict, so it's not strange that someone came here.'

Breathing underwater. This itself isn't special in Atlantis. There are items, skills, magic, etc. that allow you to breathe underwater.

Even if someone accidentally discovered this area, it doesn't mean they successfully conquered it.

,Atlantis isn't an easy place. It's even more dangerous without information. The first entrant is more likely to be dead.'

I calmly walk inside.

The Dragon King's Palace is located deep in the sea. However, it was very bright, like a shallow sea. A mysterious light, not the sun, was illuminating the area.

There were buildings made of huge corals and well-organized roads. It felt like they put well-decorated roads into the sea.

,Why are there roads? It's not like anyone's going to walk anyway.'

In the center of the area was a large, Eastern-style palace.

As I headed towards it, fish blocked my path.

"Stop, human!"

The fish spoke. Its voice clearly pierced my ears.

,Wait, am I really underwater?'

I opened my mouth.

"I... oh? My voice comes out fine."

A clear voice came from my mouth as well. Being able to speak underwater seemed to be a characteristic of this area.

"The Dragon King's Palace isn't a place for just anyone to enter! Especially not humans!"

It was cheeky for a fish, but instead of getting angry, I was amazed. How could I not be amazed when sashimi was talking?

"Can I freely roam around in the villages that aren't the Dragon King's Palace?"

"That doesn't matter. The villages are open to everyone."

"What do I have to do to enter the Dragon King's Palace?"

"You must accumulate merit. If you accumulate enough merit, you may even have an audience with the Dragon King-nim!"

In short, to enter the Dragon King's Palace, you had to be a lackey.

,There's no system notification. It's not a rule recognized by the system, but a rule made by the Dragon King.'

Unlike the rules set by the system, there's no restriction that you must follow them.

"I am Cheonma, the Supreme of the Cheonma Shingyo. I must meet the Dragon King, the Supreme of the Dragon King's Palace, so guide me."

"You stupid human! I just said it! You can only see the Dragon King-nim if you accumulate merit!"

"You stupid fish bastard. What do those petty rules mean when a Supreme meets a Supreme? You're sashimi from now on."

As soon as I summoned the Hwaryeon Bido, I sliced the fish. Fish blood spread thickly in the seawater. I frowned deeply. The thought of all the fish blood, poop, and Piss in this seawater made my skin crawl.

,Ugh. I feel like I'm going to puke.'

I immediately used the power of the Blessing of Water.

Flow of Water. I manipulated the currents around me to prevent the fish blood from coming near me.

New Water. I consumed mana to create clean water near me.

Sacrifice of Water. I consumed clean water and mana to purify the seawater around me into drinkable mineral water.

,Much better than the dirty seawater.'

It consumed quite a bit of mana, but the me in Atlantis is much stronger than the me in reality. The amount of mana is also much greater.

After reverse-summoning the Hwaryeon Bido, I walked towards the Dragon King's Palace. Walking was more comfortable, even if it was slower than swimming.

"Stop!"

"Those who are not permitted cannot enter the Dragon Palace!"

This time, a fish blocked my path. Its body was that of a sturdy human male, but since its head was a fish, it was a fish.

"How annoying. I won't say much. Move."

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Force.

Most guys feel my aura and immediately get scared and move out of the way. But the fish in front of me didn't move, instead raising their spears.

"Go back."

"If you want to see the Dragon King-nim, bring proof!"

The hands of the guys holding the spears were trembling slightly. They knew through my aura that I was not someone they could handle.

"If I were you, I would run to the Dragon King and announce the appearance of the Cheonma Supreme."

"...Our mission is to guard the entrance of the Dragon King's Palace!"

"If you harm us, the Dragon King-nim will not stand still!"

"So, you had something to rely on."

I moved forward.

Sword energy was forming on the spear tips that were stabbing towards me. They were skilled enough to dare to stand against me.

,It's too obvious. I can't even pretend to be hit.'

I reached out with both hands, grabbed the spear shafts, and stabbed them into each other's necks. The two fish didn't even let out a death cry before dying. I confidently entered the palace.

"Intruder!!"

"How dare you come here!!"

Perhaps smelling the blood, fish-men appeared from all directions. They swam freely and surrounded me from all sides. And they all rushed at me, thrusting their spears forward.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon Imperial March).

Kung!

With one stomp, I suppressed the fish's momentum. The rushing fish flinched and stopped, but soon they gathered their strength and charged again.

,Did the power of Cheonma Gunrimbo weaken because I'm underwater? Well, it's enough that I bought some time.'

In the meantime, the skill preparation was complete.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Hoicheon Maryong (Returning Heavenly Demon Dragon).

A vortex arose around me. The vortex mixed with Cheonma energy became a sharp blade itself, instantly grinding the rushing fish-men to pieces.

Their blood and flesh floated in the water. I pushed away the dirty things with my energy and moved towards the inside of the palace.

The fish that rushed in like moths to a flame disappeared at some point. Thanks to that, I was able to meet the Dragon King more easily than I thought.

The eyes of the man wearing a crown and sitting on the throne shone blue.

「The Guardian of the East (False) is glaring at you.」

「This is the domain of the Guardian of the East (False). All abilities decrease by 5%.」

"Just because you have some paltry power, do you think you're the Monkey King or something?"

His name as a god is Guardian of the East. His true name is Dragon King of the East Sea, Ogwang. He's one of the guys who had his weapons, including the Ruyi Jingu Bang, stolen by Son Ogong in Journey to the West.

"The Ruyi Jingu Bang. Is it here?"

"No. It already left the Dragon Palace a long time ago."

"Actually, I know that too. The thing I have business with isn't the Ruyi Jingu Bang, but you. Hand over your heart."

Dragon's heart.

The source of the dragon's power.

Ogwang is the Sea Dragon King. The power his heart possesses must be enormous.

"...Oh, you thunderbolt! I will show you the fury of the sea today!"

Ogwang, with blood vessels standing out on his forehead, jumps up from the throne.

At the same time, his body began to shine and transform. Into the form of a huge blue dragon that filled the wide palace to the point of breaking it.

「This is the domain of the Guardian of the East (False). All abilities decrease by 10%.」

「The dragon's aura dominates the surroundings. All creatures feel instinctive fear.」

「You have overcome the instinctive fear with your excellent mental strength.」

Absolute Mentality did Absolute Mentality things again.

I looked at the brilliantly shining blue dragon rising above the Dragon King's Palace. There was one place that particularly stood out. One of the scales on the lower part of its head, on its neck, was growing upside down.

,That's the reverse scale.'

The blue dragon, scattering divine energy, gathered energy in its mouth.

,It's a Dragon King-like aura. Just the feeling of the energy makes my skin tingle.'

If I fought him one-on-one, I would lose. I could be sure of that.

,Well, it's pretty much what I expected.'

I already knew that the owner of the Dragon King's Palace was the Dragon King of the East Sea, Ogwang. The reason I came knowing this is because I have a way.

,Even if he's a Dragon King, he's still a dragon after all.'

Among the titles I have is the title of 'Dragon Slayer'.

,Titles have unknown abilities. A slight ability. In the case of Dragon Slayer, stats slightly increase when facing a dragon. It's a slight increase that you wouldn't know unless you concentrate.'

What I'm relying on isn't the ability of a title like Dragon Slayer.

,I came alone because I'm confident I can win.'

I take out Arta's Elixir and the Golden Sword from my inventory.

「Arta's Elixir

Temporarily turns gold into a random metal.

The mutation lasts for 30 minutes.

Rank: S」

「Golden Sword

A sword made of unbreakable gold.

Rank: S」

This Arta's Elixir has a secret, which is that it's not random when it comes to the Golden Sword.

I apply the elixir to the Golden Sword.

The Golden Sword immediately turned into a silver sword.

「Notung

A sword that cuts through dragon bones and flesh.

Rank: S」

-That sword... is dangerous. Good. I won't let my guard down either!

Ogwang shines his blue eyes and uses his authority.

The pressure of the sea crushed my body. The amount of water didn't change. This is the blue dragon's authority that is only applied to me.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The pressure disappeared and I regained my freedom.

I smashed Notung with a fist imbued with Kanggi. Even if Notung was broken, the Golden Sword, which was the foundation inside, was not broken and returned to its original state.

I applied Arta's Elixir again.

「Balmung

A sword that curses dragons.

Rank: SS」

"Receive Balmung's curse."

The Golden Sword, which had become Balmung, cursed Ogwang.

Blood flowed from Ogwang's huge body floating in the palace, and he fell helplessly to the ground. He couldn't overcome the curse.

-Kkeueueueueueueuk!

I broke Balmung and applied the elixir to the Golden Sword again.

「Gram

A sword that kills dragons.

Rank: SSS」

"This heavy and cool sensation... It's been a long time. I am now the Dragon Slayer Cheonma."

-You insignificant human...!

A huge current appeared. Surprisingly, Ogwang had created a tsunami in the sea with his authority. A white foam tsunami that swept everything away.

"It's useless."

I swung Gram. The foam tsunami was cut by a huge slash and disappeared.

When I swung the sword again, the slash cut off Ogwang's tail. It cut better than I thought. Ogwang was weaker than Fafnir, who I had fought before. Or maybe I've gotten stronger since then.

Absolute difference in compatibility.

I was invincible against dragons with Gram in my hand.

"I heard dragon meat is so delicious.... If I kill you, the dragon meat will disappear... so I'll capture you alive and slowly taste your meat."

-Heok...! You crazy bastard...! I never thought I'd meet someone worse than that Monkey King! Stop! I lost! Tell me what you want!

"I want an SSS rank item related to water. Even if it's not the Ruyi Jingu Bang, there must be something else."

Originally, my goal was the Dragon King's heart.

But I belatedly remembered that Fafnir's heart, which was stronger than this guy, was SS rank. Even if I killed this guy and took his heart, it would only be SS rank.

Like when I killed Fafnir, an SSS rank item like Andvari's Ring might come out... but the possibility was low. Besides, what I want is an item related to water.

-An SSS rank item related to water? I see.... So you were aiming for my Yeouiju from the beginning. You wicked bastard...!

"...That's right. Hand it over if you don't want to die."

-Damn it. To have my treasure stolen by a human again....

The Dragon King's voice was full of sorrow. It was none of my business.

"Stop whining and quickly bring the Yeouiju."
