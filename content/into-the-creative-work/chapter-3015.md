When I arrived at the Korean Hunter Association headquarters, the staff were lined up in the lobby to greet me.

"S-Class Hunter Sung Yujin! Welcome to the Korean Hunter Association headquarters!"

The employees shouted in unison as if they'd rehearsed it, bowing their heads.

I looked at the scene and gave an awkward smile.

"Haha... I appreciate the welcome, but isn't this a bit much...?"

Of course, my inner thoughts were different.

'Yeah. This is how it should be. The youngest S-Class Hunter in history is visiting—of course they should be kissing my ass from the get-go.'

I walked across the red carpet laid on the floor toward the elevator. The staff kept their heads bowed until I got on. I liked this silence.

'Whoever came up with this welcome ceremony, I don't know, but it's excellent. Someone who knows how to treat an S-Class Hunter properly.'

Actually, I had a pretty good guess.

There were only two people who knew me well, had direct ties to the Korean Hunter Association, and could pull off something like this.

'It's either Baek Jieun or Yura. And Yura's more likely.'

Between the two, the odds heavily favored Yura.

'Baek Jieun treats me like a childhood friend, so she's comfortable with me. She doesn't feel the need to impress me.'

Yura, on the other hand?

Even though we'd gotten close recently, somewhere in her heart she feared me. She tried her hardest not to set off my temper.

'It works. Normally the Association President should've come to me, not the other way around. Since when does a fucking Association President summon the youngest S-Class Hunter?'

My mood hadn't been great before entering the headquarters, but after receiving the staff's welcome, I felt a bit better.

'Coming to visit the Association President like this every once in a while wouldn't be so bad.'

The elevator stopped. In the hallway, the President's secretaries were gathered, heads bowed. I nodded at their formal greetings and headed straight for the President's office.

Image management? Not necessary for people at the secretary level. Their mouths would stay shut. Rumors would circulate among them, sure, but that's exactly what I wanted.

'If I build a nice image, people will start treating me like a pushover, and that's a problem.'

Better to be treated like an asshole than a pushover. That way they won't bother me.

I opened the President's office door without even knocking. Association President Yoo Hyeonguk and Yura were standing, not sitting. He was in his fifties, and his posture was so rigid not a single strand of his graying hair was out of place as he bent at the waist.

"You've arrived, Hunter Sung Yujin! I've been waiting day and night since receiving your contact!"

“…….”

Yura quietly stepped back and moved to the corner. It was ninja-like speed.

I sat comfortably on the wide sofa. I leaned my back deep into the cushion and propped both feet up on the expensive table with a thud. Rude attitude? I was doing it on purpose.

"Association President. This isn't our first meeting, is it?"

"...We met a few years ago. We had a brief conversation."

"Conversation, my ass. You gave me a commendation when I was a fucking nobody. It was because of the China incident, wasn't it."

"Th-that was..."

"I know. I know. I understand. Back then I was a nobody. Looking back now it stings a little, but not enough to hold a grudge. Come on, would I really bring up something that trivial?"

I was bringing it up right now.

Still, it was true I held no grudge against the Association President. I'd never really clashed with him. It was just light small talk. An icebreaker, you could say. Even if the President was sweating buckets.

I picked up the cold can of cola sitting on the table as if it belonged there. If they were going to serve lukewarm tea or coffee, cola was better.

The President couldn't possibly have known my preferences. Which meant Yura had definitely given advice.

"Association President. Do you know Baek Jieun?"

"...You mean Manager Baek Jieun of the Management Division?"

"Yeah. She's my childhood friend. We're so close you could say we're ball-sweat close."

Baek Jieun is really good at sucking balls.

"I'll summon Manager Baek Jieun. I believe she's out in the field right now, but she should return within fifteen minutes."

"No. No need for that. If you call Jieun right now, she'll one hundred percent chew me out, so leave her alone. I'm just saying my ties to the Hunter Association run deep. Nothing special meant by it. Got it?"

"...Yes. Of course."

It meant: take good care of Baek Jieun. Fortunately, the President's head wasn't just a hat rack—he seemed to understand.

Normally I would've said it straight, but I had the dignity of an S-Class Hunter to maintain, so I'd phrased it indirectly.

'There's a reason they say position makes the man. Jieun. Your big bro looked out for you.'

This should make Baek Jieun's work a bit easier.

If her workload got heavier instead? Then the Association President had blatantly disrespected me, S-Class Hunter Sung Yujin. He might as well be saying he wanted to commit a family murder-suicide.

"The task I requested?"

"Yes. We've prepared it in cooperation with the World Hunter Association."

"I trust it's not something lame. I just became an S-Class Hunter—if I take on a mission with no impact, people will talk. They'll say I'm overhyped. And then I'll lose my shit and who knows what I'll do."

"Our Association will make sure that doesn't happen."

"And stop working Ayoung-noona to the bone. Anyone watching would think she's your subordinate. Huh?"

"Th-that's the World Hunter Association..."

"The World Hunter Association has never forced work on Hunter Han Ayoung."

Yura cut in and spoke firmly. Since it was a formal setting, she used polite speech instead of casual.

I nodded. I knew that if Han Ayoung refused, that was the end of it. The idea of an Association forcing work on an S-Class Hunter was absurd on its face.

I checked the documents the President politely handed over. They were summarized for easy reading, like quests in an online game. The minimum payout for a successful mission was over ten billion won, but it didn't catch my eye.

To an S-Class Hunter, money doesn't matter. An S-Class Hunter who obsesses over money lacks class.

"There are almost no domestic missions? And the ones that exist are all mediocre."

"Hunter Han Ayoung has completed all the high-risk missions. On top of that, Korea happens to have an unusually high number of high-rank Hunters... Even on a global scale, we're stable."

When it came to safety, the cramped little landmass was an advantage. Compared to the population, the number of Hunters was high, and their average level was among the top in the world.

'Domestic missions are too easy. Most are just dungeon clears. They look comfortable but have no impact. Since it's my first mission, even if it's a pain in the ass, I need something with impact.'

I shifted my gaze to the overseas missions.

There were almost no missions in the US or Europe. I'd heard from Yura before that the World Hunter Association prioritized managing America and Europe above all else.

'Southeast Asia—those are typically Hunter-deficient countries, so the payout rates are pretty high. They even offer artifacts as rewards.'

Hunters from Southeast Asian countries tend to abandon their homelands and naturalize in powerful nations as their rank goes up. That's because even when they clear dungeons, they often don't receive proper compensation.

'With so few A-Class or higher Hunters, even if an S-Class dungeon appears, it's hard to handle with domestic forces alone.'

So they sometimes resort to waiting for a dungeon break. When the break happens and monsters pour out, they mow them down with modern weapons. Even against S-Class boss monsters, missiles are effective.

'Depends on the boss type. S-Class boss monsters with utility traits are hard to handle with modern weapons. For dungeons containing S-Class boss monsters that must never reach Earth, the World Hunter Association steps in.'

Take, for instance, an S-Class boss monster with brainwashing abilities. A-Class or higher Hunters could resist it, but everyone else? No answer. They're just fucked.

'This is why the World Hunter Association busts its ass. It's also why they have extra-legal authority.'

If the World Hunter Association didn't exist, I'd have to step up myself. My reality going to shit isn't something I want.

'No, that's not right. The world would've gone to shit long before I even became S-Class.'

Anyway, after roughly scanning the documents, I picked one page.

"This one."

"...The subjugation of an invasive species in China."

A unique monster that appeared when a large-scale dungeon break occurred in China in the past. Pyron. The World Hunter Association classified them as one of the invasive species. Detailed information wasn't publicly known, of course.

"According to what's written here, an S-Class special entity is still growing. Shouldn't this be on the news?"

"The World Hunter Association and the Chinese government are suppressing it to prevent panic. The information will likely be released after it's subjugated."

"Because of China, right. The Chinese are deeply traumatized by Pyron right now. A special entity already at S-Class level and still growing? It wouldn't be strange if the Chinese fell into panic immediately."

"Yes. Your insight is accurate."

"Heh heh. A perfect sacrificial lamb for an S-Class Hunter's debut."

"This is a mission that Chinese S-Class Hunters will also participate in. Are you alright with that?"

"Perfect. If I find and kill the bastard before China's S-Class Hunters even get going, the impact will be even bigger. The youngest S-Class Hunter Sung Yujin, who solo-hunted a special entity! China's S-Class Hunters, reduced to useless decorations! Heugh. Just imagining it makes my mouth water."

"...Aren't you underestimating the special entity and China's S-Class Hunters a bit too much...?"

I immediately went stone-faced and stared at the President.

"Association President. Are you disrespecting me right now?"

"N-no, I'm not!"

"You must respect me. I am Sung Yujin, the youngest S-Class Hunter in the Republic of Korea. My potential is limitless—I could become the world's first SS-Class Hunter."

"Yes. I misspoke. I'll be careful going forward."

"Good. Let's do this right."

The mission starts in three days.

No need to take a plane. At the appointed time, a teleporter will show up and take me to China.

My business with the Association was done. But leaving right away felt like a waste. I'd taken the time to come all the way here, after all. I should do something.

"As an S-Class Hunter, I should go knock some sense into the Korean guilds."

"...Excuse me?"

"Association President. Summon the masters of Korea's top ten guilds. I'll give them exactly thirty minutes."

"I cannot issue an emergency summons to guild masters without justification using the President's authority... I could issue a personal summons in my name, but... they could refuse. Forget thirty minutes—they might not show up for hours."

"I'll allow you to use my name."

"Understood. I'll issue the summons to them."

The President's secretaries moved busily.

• • •

Goryeo Guild.

Number one among Korea's top ten guilds.

A guild with deep roots in Korea that had held the number one spot even when Han Ayoung was active in Suwol Guild.

Hwang Chiseung, master of Goryeo Guild and a direct descendant of the Cheonju Hwang Clan—one of the five great families that had supported Korea since ancient times—had just received a call from his old drinking buddy, Association President Yoo Hyeonguk.

"A summons. And one issued in the name of S-Class Hunter Sung Yujin, no less, when it's not even a national emergency. Ridiculous."

S-Class Hunter?

Of course he acknowledged their power. But being S-Class didn't make them invincible. They were human too. In a crisis, they die.

"Hyeonguk. Are you really being pushed around by some brat who just made S-Class?"

The reason Goryeo Guild maintained number one even when Suwol Guild was at its peak.

Simply because they were bigger? That was part of it, but the real reason was the retired S-Class Hunters behind Goryeo Guild. Retired though they were, they were beings Han Ayoung alone couldn't handle. They may have lost their prime, but they weren't weak.

Goryeo Guild had tradition, history, roots, and depth that Suwol Guild couldn't even compare to.

"I was content maintaining a good relationship with Han Ayoung—present but not overbearing. But seeing this upstart, I should crush his spirit before he grows any bigger."

It was time to teach this brat S-Class Hunter—with barely any career and nothing but luck and talent—why Goryeo Guild was Korea's greatest.

Hwang Chiseung deliberately arrived at the Korean Hunter Association headquarters a full hour later—thirty minutes past the thirty minutes specified in the summons.
