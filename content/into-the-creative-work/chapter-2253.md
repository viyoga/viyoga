As soon as I got teleported to the Eastern Continent, I understood why Priscilla-ssi said it wasn't a place for dragons. As an Aura Master, I immediately grasped the difference between the Western and Eastern Continents.

,Mana is scarcer than in the Western Continent.'

I'd estimate about 5%~10%.

Most ordinary people or knights wouldn't know. Only those sensitive to mana and mages would realize it's lacking.

Dragons? No need to even mention it. They're the creatures most sensitive to mana, so they'd feel it even more than I do. They might even have trouble breathing from the stuffiness.

,The humans of the Eastern Continent probably don't know that mana is scarcer than in the Western Continent. They were born and raised here, after all.'

I looked around. I was at the summit of an unnamed mountain. It was quite high, so I could see everything around me. I found a road. At the end of the road, I saw a city.

I focused mana into my eyes to enhance my vision and looked at the city, then whistled.

,Definitely different here in the Eastern Continent. The structure of the city and the appearance of the houses are a bit different.'

The Western Continent is based on medieval fantasy, so its culture and atmosphere reek of the Middle Ages. On the other hand, the city in the Eastern Continent looked like a mix of Western and Eastern cultures from the outside.

,I need to go there and get some information.'

I knew too little about the Eastern Continent. Fortunately, most of the Eastern Continent uses the continental common language, so there's no language barrier.

Thunderbolt Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I leaped into the air and dashed towards the city. Even while stepping through the air, I frowned. My body felt less light than when I used it in the [Bright Light Ascension] world. Plus, the amount of mana being used was considerable.

,I won't be able to use this for long.'

At the city gate, armed soldiers were on guard. If I went in head-on, there would be a squabble with the soldiers. I jumped over the wall. It wasn't even 10 meters high, so it was easy.

I landed in a dark alley. Beggars and homeless people sprawled out in the alley stared at me. They were disabled, missing arms or legs.

,What are you looking at? Get your eyes off me."

I spoke with killing intent, and the beggars and homeless people quickly averted their gazes. Even if I lacked information, I didn't want to mix words with those beggar bastards.

The main street was relatively deserted. Most of the people walking around were women. Men? They were either children or old men.

,The Eastern Continent is at war. Most of the young men must have been dragged off to the battlefield.'

I saw a man. He was huge, armed with swords and axes on his back and waist. He had a lot of hair on his face and body.

,A barbarian warrior? Is that part of the Eastern Continent's culture? Interesting.'

The man walked confidently down the street. People avoided him. If they were close, they bowed their heads in greeting, and if they were far away, they disappeared as if running away. They didn't want to be associated with the barbarian warrior.

,Now that I'm closer, I can tell. He's at the Aura Expert level. That guy is a knight in the Western Continent.'

The barbarian warrior looked at me, who was blocking his path, and his already hideous face contorted even more.

,What are you? Judging by your appearance, you don't seem to be from around here. Speak if you have something to say."

He said. He seemed to be threatening me, but his fingertips were trembling slightly. He felt my aura. I hadn't bothered to hide it. For reference, I was wearing a black fur coat. It was a bit hot, but not unbearable.

,I have some questions. You're going to have to answer them."

,...Questions? It's clear you're not from here. For a moment, I thought you were an assassin. Tell me what you want to know. I'll answer anything I know."

His attitude changed.

I tilted my head.

,You were full of killing intent just a moment ago?"

,As I said before, I initially thought you were an assassin come to kill me. The power I felt was extraordinary... I thought I was going to die here, but since you say that's not the case, there's no need to antagonize an outstanding warrior like you. Now, what do you want to know?"

The guy's voice was confident, but his gaze was directed below my eyes, not at them. I liked that he lowered his gaze on his own.

,Where is this?"

,...Hotit. You must know that... Are you testing me?"

,Cut the crap and just answer my questions. Where exactly is Hotit?"

,Where is Hotit? It's Hotit in Kairak. Its location is near the center of Kairak. It's famous for its warriors' duels, but the duels aren't being held now because all the warriors are out on the battlefield."

The Eastern Continent is currently divided in half by two warring nations. One is Thorn, which sent an envoy to the Rapelli Kingdom, and the other is Kairak.

The reason why I don't see robust men is because of the war.

,I heard Kairak has the upper hand in the war."

,That's true for now."

,...For now?"

,There's a rumor going around that Kairak's War King, Wekung, was poisoned by an assassin on the battlefield and is in critical condition. If this rumor is true, Wekung will die soon. Then Kairak will fall apart. Kairak was originally a group close to a coalition centered around Wekung anyway. There's no strong bond."

,I don't care about history... but it's probably better to know at least the basics right now. Explain the history of the Eastern Continent in the next 10 minutes."

The man explained the history as he knew it. I almost fell asleep in the middle, but I managed to listen.

,The war between Thorn and Kairak started three years ago. Originally, Thorn was the strongest nation in the Eastern Continent, but after Kairak conquered and unified other nations, it waged war on Thorn. Kairak has the upper hand in the war, but it can't be said that they've completely seized the advantage.'

I didn't even listen to the history before that. I wasn't interested.

,You're a warrior, right? Why are you here?"

,I killed my superior and was kicked out of the army. I couldn't stand being ordered around by someone weaker than me."

,The army just let you go?"

,They let me go when I said I'd kill anyone who stood in my way. I came to Hotit, a nearby city, to see if I could do mercenary work."

,Is that so? Go on your way, then."

He hesitated without moving.

,...Are you perhaps here to rule Hotit? If so... I'll help you."

,Didn't you say you killed your superior in the army? Are you waiting for a chance to kill me?"

,I killed that guy because he was weaker than me and ordered me around. You're different. You're stronger than a Great Warrior."

,Why do you want to follow me?"

,Following the strong is a warrior's instinct. ...The problem is that there's nothing to do here either."

I stroked my chin and pondered.

I still didn't know much about the Eastern Continent, so I did need a guide.

,I was originally planning to meet the King of Thorn, make him realize the difference in power, and turn him into a puppet... but conquering Kairak first wouldn't be bad either.'

Kairak is a place where the strong are respected. Whether it's politics or money, power is everything.

,I'll allow it. What's your name?"

,Hatro. What's your name, Boss?"

,Eugene Prukerus."

,That's a strange name. I can tell you're not from Kairak."

,Is there a problem?"

,The Boss is strong. There's no problem."

While heading towards the building in the center of the city, I learned about Kairak's warrior ranks.

Apprentice Warrior, General Warrior, Superior Warrior, Great Warrior.

Usually, Superior Warrior is the highest, and Great Warrior is at the Aura Master level. Hatro is a Superior Warrior. He considered me a Great Warrior.

The Warlord is the one who rules the city. He's more like a mayor than a noble.

War King was a title only the King of Kairak could use.

,We've arrived."

The Warlord's house was made of wood and was large. It was decorated with the bones of monsters and wild beasts. What should I say? It had a slight Eastern feel, but it looked more barbaric.

,Stop."

,Those without permission cannot enter."

Armed soldiers blocked my path. I was fiddling with the hilt of the Hwaryeon Bido, but Hatro stepped forward.

,Boss, I'll take care of this. This is naturally what subordinates do."

,Take care of it within a minute."

It didn't even take 30 seconds. They fell like autumn leaves before Hatro's fists. I went inside. There were no warriors. Instead, a guy who looked quite strong was sitting on the floor, drinking alcohol.

,We have an uninvited guest."

,I'll kill you and rule this city."

Seureureung.

I drew the Hwaryeon Bido from the scabbard at my waist. Blue smoke-like Sword energy formed on the red blade. The Sword energy was compressed and shone faintly like starlight.

Aura Blade, the proof of an Aura Master.

,You're at the Great Warrior level. I've lost. I'll quietly step down from this position, but I have a condition."

,A condition?"

,You wouldn't be satisfied with being the ruler of this measly city, would you? I'll follow you. Please accept me. I have about 500 warriors I can use right now. These 500 will follow you."

,There's no reason to refuse. Is a warrior not even going to fight?"

,Facing a battle with no chance of winning is not a warrior's virtue. A warrior must distinguish between courage and recklessness. Above all, I was waiting for a moment of change. You've seen Hotit, so you know. Hotit is dying because there are no men. I recently received news that the warriors sent to the battlefield were annihilated. Hotit has no future. Rather than quietly facing death... I'll bet everything on the change that is you."

,What I'm about to do is treason."

,If you succeed, a new War King will be born."

He got up from his seat and handed me alcohol.

,I'm Sopong. What's your name?"

,Eugene Prukerus."

I poured the alcohol into my mouth. It was quite strong. I swallowed it in one gulp and threw the glass behind me. The glass shattered.

,Summon the soldiers immediately. We're moving to the capital where the War King is."

,...Isn't that too hasty? Even with this much force, it's difficult to succeed in treason."

Sopong shook his head. I asked him.

,Do you have any other good ideas?"

,We conquer the surrounding cities and villages while heading to the capital, drafting warriors and gathering supplies. Long-term battles will be difficult, but short-term battles will become manageable."

,Do I have to bother with conquest activities?"

,No matter how powerful you are, you can't handle everything with strength alone. You need to build a reputation. You need to engrave your existence into people's minds to make things easier after you take the throne."

,What I want is not to rule, but to reign."

,That's why you need to make your presence known even more, isn't it?"

I had no rebuttal.
