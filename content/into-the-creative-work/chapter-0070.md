"Stop! Detective Moon!"

Moon Ji-hyuk looks at me. Soon, shock dawns on his face.

"Taker...?!"

Moon Ji-hyuk slumped down to the ground.

He seems to have received quite a mental shock knowing my true identity. Well, we've been getting along pretty well lately.

'...Well, it's not just that, his physical condition is a mess too.'

Moon Ji-hyuk's eyes were shaking violently as he looked at me.

"......That sword. Were you Jeok-gwang (Red Light)?"

I looked at Baek Sang-gi. I wondered why this bastard suddenly stepped in, but maybe it's because of me.

"That's right. I'm Jeok-gwang (Red Light)."

I answered with a smile.

According to the conversation Baek Sang-gi heard through the wiretap, he didn't seem to have any desire for the Bead of Life.

Feeding the Bead of Life to Moon Ji-hyuk and turning him back into a human. That's Baek Sang-gi's current goal.

"So, you were human after all."

I ignored Baek Sang-gi's words and approached the Flower of Life.

The most important thing right now is the Flower of Life. I have to protect the Flower of Life so that it can fully bear the four Beads of Life. Of course, the most important thing is to not let someone else take it.

I kicked Moon Ji-hyuk to create some distance. He was too close to the Flower of Life.

"Eup eup!"

Yang Jeong-min tried to escape from my arms. I put more strength into the hand covering her mouth and turned the knife on her neck sideways, bringing it closer. The cold sensation of the knife calmed Yang Jeong-min down.

Moon Ji-hyuk couldn't get up easily. Even with a vampire's recovery ability, he would need to rest for a few hours to exert his full strength.

'What I need to worry about here isn't Moon Ji-hyuk. It's Doris and Baek Sang-gi. Especially Baek Sang-gi, he feels like the most dangerous guy I've met in this world.'

Doris was rolling his eyes. He hasn't fully grasped the current situation yet.

On the other hand, Baek Sang-gi is calmly looking at me. When I kicked Moon Ji-hyuk, he didn't even glance at Moon Ji-hyuk.

'...Isn't helping Moon Ji-hyuk Baek Sang-gi's goal? Why is he looking at me like that?'

Baek Sang-gi raised his sword and aimed it at me. I frowned.

"What? Can't you see I have a hostage?"

"If I can kill you with just one hostage, it's a cheap price."

"Weren't you Moon Ji-hyuk's comrade?"

"I was impressed by the will of a young man who wasn't corrupted by the power of a vampire. But my goal is you. Jeok-gwang (Red Light). I didn't expect you to be human... but that makes you even more dangerous."

Baek Sang-gi laughs.

As the actor who played him was a male idol, he was quite handsome. But that smile is somehow eerie.

Baek Sang-gi approached me and swung his sword. I stretched out the Hwaryeon Bido, which was on Yang Jeong-min's neck, to block the sword.

Kwak!

Yang Jeong-min bit my left hand.

"Argh!"

The strength in my left hand weakened. Yang Jeong-min escaped from my arms. The bite marks are clear, showing how hard she bit.

"Ji-hyuk-ssi!"

"Aish, that girl."

I didn't have the luxury to chase after Yang Jeong-min or even complain. Baek Sang-gi had begun to swing his sword at me in earnest.

Kang! Kaang! Kang!

I frowned as I parried Baek Sang-gi's sword. He was no ordinary guy. If I tried to strike the tip of his sword and counterattack, he would invariably cut off the flow.

His physique is similar to mine. I don't think I can win by pushing him with physical strength.

'I'll end it with Byeokgye (Blue Stream).'

I stepped back from Baek Sang-gi and used Byeokgye (Blue Stream).

"As expected. An excellent killing sword."

It was blocked so easily that it was absurd.

I saw his eyes shaking. Byeokgye (Blue Stream) definitely worked. But the result was the opposite.

'Ki-sense. He felt my presence not with his eyes, but with his ki-sense.'

Immediately after, Baek Sang-gi's swordsmanship changed. The movements of the sword, which had been nothing special, moved as if they were alive. Feeling a sense of incongruity, I strongly struck his sword and stepped back.

"My swordsmanship is Seongri-geom (Neo-Confucian Sword). Originally, it's a swordsmanship for cultivating the mind and body."

I paused. I remembered what Jin Se-yeong had said before. If the opponent reveals the name of their martial art, you must also reveal yours. That's the etiquette of those who walk the path of martial arts.

Frankly speaking, I didn't understand Jin Se-yeong's words. But in this world, revealing the name of my swordsmanship isn't difficult.

"Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Sky Style)."

I glanced at Moon Ji-hyuk. Yang Jeong-min is supporting Moon Ji-hyuk and going down the mountain. It's okay. This is a deep part of Mount Seorak. At Yang Jeong-min's speed, it will take 3-4 hours to descend even if she goes on the right path.

'Yang Jeong-min doesn't know the way back because she blindly followed me. She'll probably get lost after going down a little.'

Just in case, I attached a transmitter to Yang Jeong-min's clothes. Wherever she runs away, it's in the palm of my hand.

"Are you worried about those young people?"

"I can't let them live since they saw my face. And you too."

"Haha. It's been a while since I've had a life-or-death duel with a swordsman.... How nice. The moon is bright, so it's a perfect day to die."

Baek Sang-gi's sword flies in a fluid trajectory. I tried to parry the sword and counterattack, but I was surprised. Before I knew it, Baek Sang-gi's sword was flying in the opposite direction.

'Did I miss the movement of the sword for a moment?'

I quickly respond. I grip the sword in reverse and parry Baek Sang-gi's sword. But I was a little late. Baek Sang-gi's sword cut my left forearm.

Blood is flowing, but it's not a deep wound. There's no problem moving my arm.

'I can't just keep getting hit.'

Seogwang (Dawn).

My sword draws a red trajectory, aiming for Baek Sang-gi's neck. Baek Sang-gi struck my sword from below with the end of the hilt.

"Haha."

Baek Sang-gi laughs out loud.

I couldn't laugh. I didn't think I could beat Baek Sang-gi in my current state.

Kang! Kaang! Kang!

It was a competition, I don't know how many times.

My body was full of sweat and blood. On the outside, it looks like I could collapse and die at any moment, but in reality, they are all shallow cuts.

Baek Sang-gi is also sweating. However, he didn't bleed like me. While I was cut more than 15 times, Baek Sang-gi was only cut 3 times at most.

'Damn it. He doesn't give me any room to take out a grenade or a gun from my inventory.'

I felt annoyed at my rough breathing and looked for Baek Sang-gi's opening. There was no opening.

The Seongri-geom (Neo-Confucian Sword) swordsmanship itself is not special at all compared to Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Sky Style). However, the basics are thorough to the point of vomiting. Seongri-geom (Neo-Confucian Sword) is a thorough defensive swordsmanship.

'I'm saving acceleration... but that guy isn't using his full power either. I don't think I can win even if I use acceleration... huh?'

I opened my eyes wide. Before I knew it, Doris appeared in front of the Flower of Life and reached out his hand. I moved my body faster than I thought.

Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Sky Style) Practical Technique: Biho (Flying Tiger).

Like a tiger, I run and swing the sword with both hands, putting all my weight into it.

"Kuaaaaack!"

I didn't stop there and aimed for Doris's neck. Doris used teleportation and disappeared.

"Keoeok!"

I heard Doris's scream in front of my right. When I turned my gaze there, Baek Sang-gi was behind Doris, stabbing his heart with a sword.

"H-how did you know I was coming here?!"

"I figured it out after seeing it a few times. Every time you activate your superpower, your eyes move to where you're going to teleport. In the end, teleportation can only move within your field of vision. I predicted it and hid my body in the tree. Die now, True Blood Demon."

Baek Sang-gi lowered his sword. Doris's body collapses downward. Even a True Ancestor can't survive in that situation.

"I've also defeated the True Blood Demon. Now, let's continue..."

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes.]

Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Sky Style) Practical Technique: Biho (Flying Tiger).

I increased my speed with acceleration and swung my sword greatly, putting my weight into it.

The surprise attack was almost perfect, but Baek Sang-gi reacted without difficulty and raised his sword to defend. The impact reverberated and Baek Sang-gi's posture was slightly disturbed.

I didn't miss that gap and poured out attacks.

After 10 minutes, the acceleration was released. I couldn't kill Baek Sang-gi even though I used acceleration.

"...Shit. Why isn't anything working?"

I said annoyedly, exhaling hot breath. A few minor attacks worked. But all the attacks that could be decisive were blocked.

"Jeok-gwang (Red Light). Your swordsmanship is excellent. I can tell because we exchanged swords. Your Yeongcheonryu (Shadow Sky Style) has a depth that is difficult to fathom."

Baek Sang-gi approaches me. He was as tired as I was.

"Swordsmanship is just swordsmanship. Jeok-gwang (Red Light)."

I rushed towards Baek Sang-gi once again. Baek Sang-gi moved the wrist holding the sword mysteriously, parrying my sword and striking it to the side. It's not swordsmanship or anything. It's just a trick.

Baek Sang-gi stabbed his sword into my abdomen.

"It was fun. Jeok-gwang (Red Light). Thanks to you, I was able to completely cut off my lingering attachments to this world."

"......"

Instead of falling, I took one more step forward. And I grabbed Baek Sang-gi by the collar and stabbed his chest with a sword.

I frowned.

"......Why didn't you dodge?"

He could have avoided it simply by letting go of the hilt and stepping back. But Baek Sang-gi didn't avoid it.

"I'm trying to be your companion on the road. Jeok-gwang (Red Light). I wouldn't want to be lonely in the afterlife."

I felt a surge of murderous intent at his nonchalant attitude. I wanted to swing my sword and chop him into pieces right away.

"...Ha."

I let out a short sigh, pushed him away, and pulled out the sword that was stuck in my abdomen. He fell to the ground and looked up at me.

"Thank you. Jeok-gwang (Red Light)."

What is he thankful for? I frowned, unable to understand.

"You moron."

I used Complete Recovery.

All the minor wounds on my body disappeared, and the sword hole also healed without a trace.

Baek Sang-gi's eyes widened and his mouth opened. His shocked expression was quite pleasant.

But that was only for a moment.

Baek Sang-gi soon returned to a peaceful expression and closed his eyes. There was a subtle smile on his lips.

He looked like an old man who had met a peaceful death. It was annoying.

I turned my back on Baek Sang-gi and approached the Flower of Life.

Seeing the two Beads of Life hanging on the Flower of Life, the annoying feeling instantly disappears.

'Hiya~. The color is no joke. Really.'

I stared blankly at the Flower of Life, then suddenly came to my senses and changed my clothes.

I can't touch the Bead of Life wearing dirty clothes covered in blood and sweat. Who will take responsibility if I get bad luck?

'It said that a total of 4 will be formed, right? It doesn't seem like it will take long.... I'll wait. Well.'

I sat cross-legged right in front of the Flower of Life.

I didn't let go of the Hwaryeon Bido. Because I can't 100% guarantee that there won't be variables like Baek Sang-gi.

'Ah. That's right. Where are Yang Jeong-min and Moon Ji-hyuk?'

I took out the tracker from the inventory. It looked like a square pad the size of my palm. When I turned on the tracker, I could see the location of the transmitter.

'As expected, they didn't go far.'

Moon Ji-hyuk and Yang Jeong-min were moving slowly. Judging by their appearance, they must be lost.

When you get lost in the mountains, it's best to just call 119 and ask for help.

'If it's Moon Ji-hyuk and Yang Jeong-min, there's a high chance they won't ask for help. They know that if they call people, they'll be killed by my hand.'

Even if they report it, it doesn't matter. My goal in 'Vampire Detective' is about to be achieved. I'll have lingering feelings if I think about Yeong Hye-jeong or Hera, Choi Seon-mi, but it's not enough to not give up on Vampire Detective.

I looked at the Flower of Life and recalled the battle with Baek Sang-gi. I was annoyed. But it wasn't like there was nothing to learn.

'My swordsmanship itself was better.'

But I couldn't win.

I thought about what the cause was, but it doesn't hit me. I should ask Jin Se-yeong about it later.

Time passed.

Four Beads of Life were formed on the Flower of Life.

When I pulled out the Flower of Life planted in the ground, it withered rapidly and disappeared into dust. All that was left in my hand were the four Beads of Life.

I put the four rainbow-colored Beads of Life in a plastic bag and put them in my inventory.

"Goal achieved!"

Clapping clapping!

I even clapped as a sign of self-congratulation and got up.

When I checked, Yang Jeong-min and Moon Ji-hyuk were not far away. It's a distance that I can catch up with in 30 minutes if I run with my physical abilities.

"Then. Shall we go perform the sub-mission?"

I ran through the dark mountains.

And I was able to find Yang Jeong-min walking while supporting Moon Ji-hyuk's shoulder. Moon Ji-hyuk's walk is more stable than I thought. He seems to have recovered to some extent.

I quietly go around to the side and overtake them.

"...Jeong-min-ssi. I'm okay, so just run away. My body will recover on its own over time."

"How can I abandon Ji-hyuk-ssi? I'll report it to 119 when it gets light. By then, Taker... no, Jeok-gwang (Red Light) will have run away."

I chuckled and blocked their path.

"Why would I run away?"
