KWAAAAAAAH!

The heaven-and-earth spiritual energy stirs, and a pillar of light descends from the sky.

Someone within the Secret Realm has ascended to the Three Calamities stage. I'd seen this sight more times than I could count since arriving here, so it stirred nothing in me.

'The spiritual energy inside the Secret Realm is several times richer than outside, and there are even Harmony-stage spirit creatures here. Reaching the Three Calamities stage isn't anything special.'

It could be someone from the Murim Alliance, or it could not. If the growing number of strong fighters bothered me, I could just go kill them before they matured... but why would I fear someone who'd just barely entered the Three Calamities stage?

'The future is unknowable, but the vast majority of Three Calamities practitioners never even reach the Harmony stage, let alone the mid-tier of Three Calamities.'

There was no point in paying attention to every single person who'd just stepped into the Three Calamities stage.

'The Harmony stage is a different story. If they leave this place, they're likely to become a nuisance, so anyone who reaches Harmony must be found and eliminated immediately.'

Ruthless? That's just how the martial world works.

I left the Huangbo clan's group behind and prepared to depart. Before I left, Baek Muhan approached and bowed.

"Thank you, Lord Seong. Thanks to you, I was able to gain enlightenment and achieve a breakthrough."

"Good thing you got something out of it. Otherwise, it would've looked like you just taught me your Heavenly Demon Divine Art for nothing."

"It's fine. You are my benefactor. Martial arts are just martial arts—I can teach them to you as much as you like."

"So you've let go of your attachment to martial arts?"

"Perhaps. Martial arts are just martial arts. Even two martial artists who learn the same technique achieve different results. In the end, I realized that martial arts aren't what truly matters."

"I know you feel indebted to me. You've repaid that debt with this. If you still feel obligated, let it go."

"Yes, Lord."

"If fate allows, we'll meet again. I'll be going now."

I used the Cloud-Riding Command. Clouds gathered in billowing puffs at my feet.

In my heart, I considered this meeting with Baek Muhan to be the last. I'd obtained his Heavenly Demon Divine Art, and he didn't seem likely to join the Heavenly Demon Cult like in the original 'Fallen Heavenly Demon.' I had no personal interest in him either, so there was no reason to meet again.

"Thank you, Lord."

With his farewell as the last, I rode the clouds into the sky. I'd already said my goodbyes to Huangbo Jiahui, so there was no reason to stay.

I thoroughly explored the Secret Realm, searching for spirit creatures. There were gains. I found Three Calamities-stage spirit creatures and a few enchanted artifacts. None were of high enough quality for my use, so I'd pass them down to my subordinates.

While crossing the Mountain of Gold, I encountered the current Murim Alliance Leader and former Beggar's Sect Leader, Chwi Seon'gae Wang Ji-yuk. With his half-bared white head and long white mustache as his defining features, he sat alone at the edge of the Mountain of Gold, in the center of a sheer metallic cliff. The carcass of a massive eagle lay at the base of the cliff.

My Lightning Eye Golden Pupil identified the corpse instantly. A Vajra Vulture. A Harmony-stage spirit creature. Wang Ji-yuk must have killed it and used its inner core for cultivation.

The moment Wang Ji-yuk saw me, he made no effort to hide his killing intent.

"Heavenly Demon! You dare show that disgusting face before me!"

"Show it? I've never hidden my face. Unlike you, whose head isn't exactly covered in hair."

"You're drunk on the Emperor's favor. His Majesty is not a man of endless mercy."

He was warning me.

That the Emperor would not guarantee my life.

"I know. Others talk about the Emperor's favor this and that, but in the end, I receive such treatment because I'm a talent the Emperor needs. You, on the other hand, are useless to the Emperor. Isn't that why the Beggar's Sect was annihilated at a single word from him?"

"The Beggar's Sect may have been destroyed, but its spirit has not vanished!"

"Heh heh. It's the opposite. There are no more beggars in this land. But the Beggar's Sect's martial arts remain. Did you know? The Empire's officials and people are grateful to me. Thanks to my counsel, the filthy, useless beggars are gone, and the Empire is a better place to live."

"You bastard!! Running your loose mouth carelessly! I will kill you and petition His Majesty to retract your counsel! I will restore everything to its proper order!"

"Idiot. Beggar isn't a profession."

I openly mocked Wang Ji-yuk. We stood at a set distance, sizing each other up.

Wang Ji-yuk's stage, confirmed by eye, was Harmony 5th tier. Higher than mine. But a gap like this could be overcome easily enough. If I used Heaven-Shattering Emperor, I could overwhelm him.

'Even if he's a fool, he's the Murim Alliance Leader. He must have hidden tricks.'

I wanted to confirm his tricks before using Heaven-Shattering Emperor if possible. It was a technique I couldn't use twice in a row.

More time passed.

Wang Ji-yuk didn't move a muscle.

"Not going to attack?"

"The truce between the Murim Alliance and the Heavenly Demon Cult has not yet ended."

"Good excuse. You know full well that a truce means nothing inside the Secret Realm. Heh heh."

"The Murim Alliance keeps its promises. The leader of the Demon Cult wouldn't understand."

"Yes, yes. Out of respect for the esteemed Murim Alliance Leader's face, I'll withdraw here."

The cloud carrying me drifted further from Wang Ji-yuk.

No matter how I thought about it, fighting Wang Ji-yuk here was a loss. If he and I went all out, half the Secret Realm would be destroyed.

There were still many spirit creatures I hadn't obtained, and it hadn't even been half a year since I entered the Secret Realm.

'Killing him here would feel good... but it would reduce what I can gain from the Secret Realm. Above all, I haven't even explored the lake yet.'

Was my hatred for Wang Ji-yuk strong enough to abandon the spirit creatures I could obtain? He was irritating, but not that much.

'Hmm. Still, leaving without anything would be a shame.'

Heavenly Demon Divine Art—Dragon Fist.

I thrust a fist compressed with Heavenly Demon Energy toward Wang Ji-yuk, who stood far away. The Heavenly Demon Energy flew off in the form of a black dragon.

Wang Ji-yuk's hands, which had been watching me, glowed jade-green and moved with profound intricacy. He struck the black dragon with both palms. The dragon vanished on the spot.

'So that's the famous Beggar's Sect's Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms. Truly a remarkable divine technique.'

Dragon-Subduing, for it subdues dragons.

It wasn't quite on the level of Shaolin's martial arts, but it seemed to carry some demon-subduing power as well.

• • •

Wang Ji-yuk checked his faintly trembling hands.

He'd responded to the Heavenly Demon's attack with the Eighteen Dragon-Subduing Palms, but the recoil lingered, making his hands tremble faintly. There was no real damage. Even the faint trembling faded after a short while.

'My stage was clearly higher. And yet, this much. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art. It's as powerful a demonic art as its reputation suggests.'

Wang Ji-yuk ran a simulated duel against the Heavenly Demon in his mind. He continued the imaginary battle, factoring in the Heavenly Demon's stage and the power of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art he'd just witnessed.

'...Even accounting for the lack of information on the Heavenly Demon... I cannot guarantee victory.'

He hated to admit it, but the Heavenly Demon was a genius. Never in the history of the martial world had there been a martial artist who grew strong as quickly as the Heavenly Demon.

'The gap in stage is not absolute. Perhaps by the time we leave this Secret Realm... the Heavenly Demon may surpass my stage.'

Wang Ji-yuk could be certain.

'The one who's short on time isn't the Heavenly Demon—it's me.'

He felt a sense of urgency.

The moment the Heavenly Demon surpassed his stage, there was a high chance he would ignore the truce agreement and start a war.

He pulled a small bundle from his robes. A type of spatial vessel. Only its outer appearance was slightly different. What he took from the bundle was a mat. He spread the mat in the air. It didn't fall but floated stably in the void. He stepped onto the mat. It rose into the sky.

Wang Ji-yuk took a small talisman from his robes. The communication talisman glowed.

"Strategist Zhuge. Are you listening?"

The calm voice of the Murim Alliance's Grand Strategist and the Three Calamities-stage spellmaster who held authority second only to the Alliance Leader—Hyeonseong Zhuge Yun—resonated.

—"Yes, Alliance Leader. I'm listening."

"I just encountered the Heavenly Demon."

—"Is that true?! Did a battle break out?!"

"No. We only exchanged brief greetings. He couldn't afford to abandon what he stands to gain from this Secret Realm either."

—"...How was the Heavenly Demon?"

"Setting aside his arrogant nature... he was formidable. Even though my stage was higher, I couldn't guarantee victory. If the Heavenly Demon reaches a stage comparable to mine... I will certainly be defeated. Looking at the Heavenly Demon, I could be certain. The one short on time isn't the Heavenly Demon—it's me."

—"In that case... it would be best to settle this within the Secret Realm. The Heavenly Demon's forces here are few. The best approach would be for all factions excluding the Heavenly Demon Cult to join hands and attack the Heavenly Demon."

"Haven't you confirmed that there's a Harmony-stage spellmaster at the Heavenly Demon's side? That too would be a gamble. I need a more certain method. That's why I'm currently heading to the lake."

—"There's no meaningful information on the lake yet. It's dangerous. At the very least, we should gather information first..."

"Where there's danger, there's opportunity. Strategist Zhuge. If I fail to return... beg Gongbaek Sung to take charge of the Murim Alliance. If he refuses to the end... surrender to the Demon Cult. The Heavenly Demon will die someday too... until then, lie low and endure. Living on your belly will shred your pride, but it's better than a meaningless death."

—"Alliance Leader..."

"I'm counting on you."

Wang Ji-yuk hurled himself toward the lake.

Splash.
