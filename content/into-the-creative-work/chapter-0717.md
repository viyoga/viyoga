,"If we take that thing down, we can get out of the dungeon, right? Not bad."

Seong Hari-ssi strongly stomped her feet. Kwadeuk! Maintaining her balance on the shaking floor, she took a javelin stance. Blue electricity flowed through her body. Seong Hari-ssi stared at the Machine King and quietly regulated her breathing.

The power that Seong Hari-ssi revealed overwhelmed the heroes, including me, for a moment. Just from the atmosphere, I could tell she was on a different level.

With a huge shockwave, a lightning spear flew. Tearing through the air with sheer speed alone, it reached the Machine King's body in the blink of an eye.

Kwaaaaaang!

A part of the Machine King's massive body, which was walking towards us, was smashed and staggered. It didn't fall, but a part of its body that was attacked by the spear collapsed. It was as powerful as launching a huge missile.

,"Wow, wow, wow! That ajumma is super strong!"

Makina made a fuss. It was a sight worth making a fuss over. But one strike wasn't enough. The Machine King was so big that, in terms of destruction rate, it was only about 5%.

,'In other words, it means I just need to do the same attack 19 more times....'

Seong Hari-ssi was pretending to be fine, but if you looked closely, you could tell her breathing had become rough.

,'Her stats have dropped due to the Spirit King's Curse (SS), and she must be tired from the battles so far. I don't think she can do the same attack even 5 times....'

I wasn't the only one who came to that conclusion. Rayburn approached Seong Hari-ssi and said.

,"Stop attacking mindlessly. If you become incapacitated, we're done for."

,"You're talking like you have a plan or something? Tell me."

Seong Hari-ssi raised her hand. The spear she had thrown was summoned back. It wasn't her skill, but a function of the spear itself. The spear was perfectly fine, without any damage.

,"Makina said it, didn't she? That artificial intelligence is the weakness. The Machine King is a giant moving building. Facing it head-on from the outside is foolish, and it's much better to find and destroy the artificial intelligence room from the inside."

,"That's the right idea. But how are we going to get inside?"

,"I don't have any brilliant ideas for that. We'll just have to figure it out somehow. But I've identified the location of the artificial intelligence room."

Rayburn showed Seong Hari-ssi the screen of his laptop. A cross-section of the Machine King was drawn. He must have been furiously typing on his laptop earlier to create the blueprint.

,"Wow. It's surprisingly detailed. I thought it was the original blueprint. You're pretty good, huh?"

,"If it's the soul of the Machine King, you'd know the structure, right? Are there any incorrect parts?"

,"Yeah. The structure is roughly correct. But I don't know the exact location of the artificial intelligence room either."

,"If the structure is correct, it's not hard to predict. If it's not this room, it'll be here, or here, or here."

Red dots appeared on the blueprint. Seong Hari-ssi stared intently at the blueprint and nodded.

,"I remember. Let's go. Ah, Yujin-gun, Sohee-yang, and Sara-yang, go to the back. This is an order."

She spoke with a fierce force that didn't allow for any objections.

,"O-okay, I'll do as you say, Unnie."

,"I will protect Yujin-nim even if it costs me my life!"

,"Y-yes!"

Even Makina answered with a military-like posture.

Seong Hari-ssi started running towards the Machine King with the strike team.

I watched the situation through Mocha's eyes, who was flying in the sky. The center of the strike team was, of course, Seong Hari-ssi. Having her physical abilities boosted by Flora's blessing, she showed why an S-class hero is also called the ultimate weapon. She was overwhelmingly better than other A-class heroes.

The Machine King wasn't just standing still either. It launched guided missiles and released mechanical monsters to hinder them. Ethan-gun shot down the missiles with mana arrows, and the other strike team members blocked the mechanical monsters. It was clear they were trying to conserve Seong Hari-ssi's strength as much as possible.

Someone pulled my hair. I turned my head to the side and saw Makina. I narrowed my eyes.

,"What are you doing? Why did you materialize?"

Makina was connected to me. To materialize, she needed my mana. High-level spirits or higher can materialize on their own, but it's not efficient and causes a lot of loss, so they avoid doing it if possible.

,"Huh? It just happened?"

,"......Aren't you tired?"

,"I feel a little tired, but I'm okay!"

,"......."

Materializing on her own without any effort. It was a characteristic of a Demon Spirit.

,'...Is it because I used the Demon Spirit's core?'

It was a bit unexpected, but I wasn't too worried. Makina and I were already bound by a contract. Due to the contract condition of absolute obedience, Makina couldn't betray me.

Makina seemed to be reading my expression, so she dispelled her materialization and came close to me, whispering in my ear.

,"What are you going to do? Are you just going to watch without stepping in? With your power, you can easily take down Deus. You could hack it to self-destruct, or give it an order to delete its artificial intelligence."

,"......."

Hacking is a cheat skill, but only for machines. However, it's not without its drawbacks. The cooldown is long, at 3 hours. You can only use it once per battle, so you have to use it carefully. And there was a range. My current hacking level is 20. The range is 4km. Hacking doesn't work beyond that distance.

More than anything, the more complex and sophisticated the machine, the shorter the hacking duration. Even when maintaining Makina, 15 seconds was the limit. Considering the size of the Machine King, the hacking duration was likely to be even shorter.

,'The range is fine. The Machine King is still coming this way. Even if I don't move, the distance will soon close.'

The biggest problem was the timing, and I wasn't confident that I could make the Machine King self-destruct in just a few seconds. It was better to find a more reliable method than hacking.

,'Makina. You can possess objects, right?'

,"...Oh, you know about that too?"

Another of Makina's abilities is possession. She can possess objects and mechanize them. If she possesses a machine like a computer, she can control the machine. However, it's only possible with objects.

,'You could possess the Machine King's body and interfere with it, right?'

,"This is a new ability, so I don't know much about it either. It's called possession, but I think it's similar to hacking, so I have to suppress Deus's artificial intelligence.... Deus's artificial intelligence is so high-performance that it might be a bit difficult. More than anything, Deus is too big. Your mana won't be able to handle it?"

I had forgotten about the mana problem. Makina's words were all correct. Makina is my spirit. She uses my mana to use her abilities.

,'Is there no other way? Do I have to just watch until Seong Hari-ssi destroys the Machine King?'

There's no such thing as 100% in this world. The Machine King might have hidden abilities, or Seong Hari-ssi might make a mistake. Or the strike team might hold Seong Hari-ssi back. I'm too worried about Seong Hari-ssi to just sit still and do nothing.

,'......That's it.'

I glanced at Han Sohee-yang and Sara-yang. They were too busy watching the situation to notice. I took an item out of my inventory.

It was a sticker that came out of a random draw a while ago. A sticker that was slightly different from Absolute Hypnosis.

[0.3 Scale Sticker].

It has the effect of changing the size of an object to 0.3 times its original size when attached to it.

[0.3 Scale Sticker

Attaching the sticker reduces the size and weight of an object to 30%. It returns to its original state when the sticker is removed. The sticker is disposable.

Price: 500 points

※Caution

There is a very high possibility that the shrunken object will not function properly.

Consider the space when removing the sticker.

Does not work on living creatures.]

,'...The Machine King is a building. It wouldn't be treated as a single object, would it? I can't shrink the entire size of the castle.'

But the Machine King was a machine. A machine is a collection of precise parts.

,'Makina. There's a crucial part in the Machine King's structure, right? Besides the artificial intelligence.'

,"There are a few parts that can't be missing. For example, the axis in the center of the bottom. Without that, 30% of Deus's body will be incapacitated."

,'What does that axis look like?'

,"It looks like a giant gear. Its role is to transmit power."

,'If we get rid of that, we can weaken the Machine King.'

,"Impossible. It's inside, so attacking it from the outside is impossible, and if you're inside Deus, it's better to find the artificial intelligence room."

,'That's true... No, wait a minute. That lower part. It's made of a giant plate, right?'

Below the castle, there's a steel plate with giant legs. It's not made by joining steel plates together, but made as a single piece.

,"It's to prevent soil or living things from entering from underground. They blocked the gap completely. But why is that?"

,'If that disappears or vanishes, the parts inside will pour out, right? Because the floor will disappear.'

,"That's true. But that's the most solid part of Deus's body? It won't budge with ordinary attacks. Maybe if that ajumma throws a spear with the same power as before about 3 times, she might be able to break through it?"

I called Mocha. Mocha was summoned in front of me. I materialized Mocha and gave her the 0.3 Scale Sticker in her claws.

I couldn't move because I was worried about Seong Hari-ssi's eyes, and I was slow. But if it was Mocha, it would be a different story. Mocha, who can fly freely in the sky, can move more freely than anyone else here.

Makina looked at me and Mocha as if she couldn't understand. I didn't intend to explain everything to Makina one by one.

,"Kkuuuk! Kkuk!"

Mocha, who told me to trust her, spread her wings and flew into the sky. Pajik pajik. Mocha used my mana generously and flew at full speed.

,"Yujin-gun! What are you doing now?!"

Han Sohee-yang grabbed my arm tightly to prevent me from running away and asked.

,"I asked Mocha to help. Makina said there's a good way."

,"To Makina?"

Han Sohee-yang looked around. Unlike me or Seong Hari-ssi, she can't see spirits in their ethereal state.

,'If I mention Makina, I can gloss over the question of how I did it.'

I shared my vision with Mocha.

The first thing I saw was Seong Hari-ssi. She was creating a foothold-like force field in the air, showing unbelievable mobility as she approached the Machine King. The strike team supported her with all their might.

,'She hasn't entered the Machine King's interior yet. Perfect.'

Mocha flew low as she got closer to the Machine King. Flying too high would make her an easy target for the Machine King. The Machine King fired missiles and bullets at Mocha, trying to restrain her. Mocha performed aerial acrobatics and dodged them all.

,'Mocha. You don't need to go too deep. On the outside.... Yes, attach the sticker to that part.'

The moment the sticker was attached, the Machine King's lower plate was reduced to 0.3 times its original size. The things that were holding the lower plate in place became meaningless. Even though it had shrunk, the still-large lower plate fell to the ground. And a beat later, the Machine King's internal parts fell out in droves.

The most important parts were firmly fixed, so they were fine, but in human terms, it was like peeling off the flesh and exposing the skin. The Machine King's legs no longer moved. With this, more than half of the Machine King's weapons were neutralized.

,"Goodness! How on earth did you do that?!"

Ignoring Makina's exclamation, I resummoned Mocha in front of me.

,'The distance is about 3km. Perfect.'

The strike team was flustered by the sudden luck, but struggled to not miss the opportunity. It was then. The upper part of the Machine King split open, and a giant hand appeared. It was a hand with thousands of small fingers. The fingers had all sorts of dangerous weapons attached to them, such as cannons, machine guns, blades, harpoons, and so on.

,"It's Deus's Hand! It's a kind of trump card, but to think he'd bring it out already! Zeus must be cornered!"

The strike team didn't panic. Makina had provided the strike team with all the information about Deus in advance. And Seong Hari-ssi finally entered the Machine King's interior.

The sight that unfolded before her eyes was overwhelming.

Seong Hari-ssi advanced, wielding her spear. She freely roamed the air using the force field as a foothold, wielding her spear. Every time she wielded her spear, the sound of thunder rang out. Lightning followed every movement of the spear. When the lightning flashed, the Machine King's fingers were destroyed.

The thousands of weapons didn't stop Seong Hari-ssi's advance in the slightest. Like an avatar of destruction, she simply destroyed everything and moved forward.
