In a way, it's amazing. It means it pierced through the combat helmet with just its vocal power. Of course, I didn't have the soundproofing function on.

-Master. I recommend using the soundproofing function.

"Mental abilities don't work."

-I know. I'm not doubting Master's abilities. This sound is as loud as if it's using a loudspeaker. The sound itself can have physical force.

"It's fine. It's not too bad as background music."

It's not just because I like the song, to be honest.

Even if it's not as much as sight, hearing is a great help in combat.

I can see the singing entity.

'Just looking at the face, it's quite pretty? Age in her early 20s?'

But it was a tree.

And not just an ordinary tree. A tree with a disgusting texture as if human skin had been torn off. You can't find any fresh leaves. Instead, countless blood vessels wriggle on the surface. If you look at the root of the tree, there are countless women's legs attached. Just looking at the legs, they boast quite shapely calves, which gives it a bizarre feeling.

-It is being guarded by 22 infected Variants. Intelligence is felt in the formation.

"Is that K-pop tree controlling the Variants?"

-It seems so at first glance.

The level of the escort Variants was not high. Compared to the Eodukshini I faced a while ago, they are at a normal level.

'The time has come when I can call Variants normal. That means we've gotten that much stronger. 20 is a bit much, but 10 knights can handle it. I won't be able to overwhelm them.'

I didn't think I would lose.

The base is right behind me. If necessary, I can call for support. There were many means available, such as knight reinforcements, support fire, and suicide drones.

"Are you sure that K-pop tree has intelligence? It doesn't react even when we get close. Rather, the escort Variants are on guard."

-...The probability that the K-pop tree has intelligence is decreasing. There is a possibility that there is no intelligent entity here.

"Originally, the leader gives orders from a safe place. A general can't go out with a gun like a grunt."

It's weird that I, as president, am wielding a sword and engaging in combat myself.

I can't help it since that's my tendency. And there are times when it's cheaper for me to step in directly. Like with the Eodukshini.

-I have detected a change in the formation of the escort Variants. It seems they are preparing to retreat.

"They didn't seem to be talking. How are they communicating?"

-I don't know. The possibilities are endless.

"I'll find out if I catch a few and research them. Knights, listen up. There's no need to capture them alive. Kill them for sure and leave only the corpses."

"Yes, Your Excellency."

"Lee Yeonhee-ssi?"

"...Ah, yes. Your Excellency. What did you just say?"

"You're not in good condition. Didn't you block out the outside sounds?"

Unlike Lee Yeonhee-ssi, the knights were fine.

The knights were armed with fanaticism. They have considerable resistance to mental abilities. But they are not completely immune to mental attacks like I am, who has absolute mentality. You never know what variables there will be if a battle breaks out.

Lee Yeonhee-ssi's space severing is powerful, so she can't overuse it. And if you have sensitive senses, you can't avoid space severing. Especially since Variants have better senses than most animals. Ordinary humans can't even keep up.

"Drones, focus on reconnaissance. The same goes for the guys who can request support. No, it will be easier for them. This is their territory. If they swarm too much, retreat to Seoul immediately."

Their battle is like a believer praying to God.

The tremendous sound became a shockwave and swept around. I felt blood flowing from my ears. At the same time, I felt a dizziness as if my vision was spinning.

'Did my eardrums burst?'

From a sweet ballad to an exciting yet eerie rock.

"It wasn't just attacking the mind with the song?"

'In game terms, it's a kind of area buff. The mental attack was a debuff. Now that I see it, the K-pop tree was a bard.'

But frustratingly, there was no significant attack until I reached right in front of it.

'Was it a guy with no means of attack? No wonder it's taking dozens of Variants as escorts.'

I shove the Cutlass Blade into the face of the K-pop tree. The face itself felt pretty, so it was a bit unpleasant.

'Still, this is 100% the vital point.'
