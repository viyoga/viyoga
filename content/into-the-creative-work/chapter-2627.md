LK Corporation's CEO, Count Eugene Meyer, Attacked!

That was the headline of the newspaper circulating in Neo London.

If you were to pick the hottest topic in Neo London right now, it would be the LK-99 superconductor. The interest in Eugene Meyer, the owner of the superconductor, couldn't be small. LK Corporation was also related to the future of Neo London, after all.

Those who saw the newspaper clicked their tongues in unison.

,The butler and maid betrayed him? And there was even a threesome involved? Huh, aren't butlers and maids supposed to be the epitome of trust?

,Meyer Manor is famous for its excellent salaries and benefits... They threw away their own good fortune for a moment of greed.

,In my opinion, Divine France is definitely behind this incident. Isn't France the country that's most jealous of Neo London's success? They must have been greedy for the superconductor.

,I heard Count Meyer is hiring butlers and maids. Is he still trusting them even after being betrayed?

,Honestly, it's hard to find professional personnel like butlers and maids in Neo London. But this time, you have to have a recommendation letter to apply.

,Wouldn't it be safer to just hand over the LK-99 recipe to the council? The Neo Round Table Council would use it well.

,Are you even listening to yourself? Would you sell a treasure that prints money to someone else if you had it?

,A 6th-circle battle mage. So that thick lightning that fell yesterday was a 7th-circle spell, Thunder Break... If he can use a 7th-circle spell, doesn't that make him a 7th-circle mage?

,Don't be too obsessed with ranks. Even a 5th-circle mage can do the work of a 7th-circle mage if they prepare thoroughly.

,Count Meyer's rank is a bit disappointing.

,...Do you think a 6th-circle mage is a joke?

,I'm not looking down on 6th-circle mages. But if Count Meyer was a 7th-circle mage, would those impure bastards have dared to plan an attack? Even if they did, they wouldn't have been so bold.

Neo London citizens voiced their opinions on the incident everywhere.

Fortunately, public opinion was flowing in a fairly positive direction for Eugene. Some newspapers were criticizing Eugene for unleashing a 7th-circle attack spell in Neo London, but they didn't get much of a response and were often buried.

Yuria, who was attending the Maid Academy, was also able to see the newspaper. She looked through three types of newspapers and sensed someone's artificial intervention.

,The council probably intervened and ordered them to write favorable articles.

The Neo Round Table Council and LK Corporation were more than just allies. If LK Corporation went bankrupt, it wouldn't just be a loss. The future of Neo London could be shaken. The superconductor was worth that much.

,But a betrayal by a maid and butler. It's the worst news I've ever heard in this world. How can a maid betray her master...? She has no qualities of a maid. If I had been at the mansion, I would have taken care of it before this happened....

As much as she wanted to leave the Maid Academy and return to Eugene, she had to graduate from the Maid Academy to be recognized as the best maid.

Knock knock.

Someone knocked on Yuria's dormitory door. Yuria put the newspaper down on the desk and said,

,Yes. I'm inside.

,This is Anna, your instructor. Yuria-ssi, the cooking competition hosted by the B District Hotel is just around the corner. Are you ready?

,Yes. Of course.

Yuria picked up the bag she had prepared in advance and went out the door.

Recently, she had been participating in competitions held throughout Neo London and sweeping the victories. The purpose wasn't the prize money or the goods. It was to build up her resume with competition wins. This spec would be of some help in the maid qualification exam.

• • •

It had been half a month since the betrayal incident of the servants.

Shea, along with the butlers, maids, and mercenaries, were all executed. Usually, trials take time, and if you hire a lawyer or law firm, the verdict is dragged out for at least a few months, but this time, the council intervened and it ended quickly.

The Neo Round Table Council was sending a message to others that it fully supported me.

,I never imagined that cleaning up the mess at the mansion would take longer than the trial.

I hired new butlers and maids. Unlike before, when I hired anyone with a certificate, this time I only hired butlers and maids with recommendation letters. Even so, it's hard to trust them 100%... I tried to exclude those who were desperate for money or had a bad past, like Shea, as much as possible.

Pajijik. Pajijik...!

Electricity sparked from the cherry blossom tree in the center of the garden. Electricity had been sparking from the cherry blossom tree for about an hour. The servants retreated and watched with anxious eyes. They seemed worried that the cherry blossom tree would discharge a large amount of electricity.

,I can feel more spirit energy than before. I can feel that the electricity sparking from the cherry blossom tree is being controlled. Is it finally awakening?

The flowing spirit energy solidified, and all the mana around it was absorbed into the cherry blossom tree. This caused a momentary mana vacuum. What filled that space was cherry blossom petals. Countless cherry blossom petals fell to the ground even though there was no wind.

New cherry blossom petals bloomed on the branches where the cherry blossom petals had fallen, and then fell again. The floor of the mansion garden began to be paved with pink cherry blossom petals.

I heard the servants panicking from behind.

,Don't get close! Each and every cherry blossom petal is imbued with the elemental mana of lightning!

,If you touch them because they're pretty, you'll get electrocuted!

,What about the master-nim?!

,The master-nim is fine. He's a lightning-type mage. Far from being dangerous, he'll probably feel good.

It was as one of the servants said.

This environment was by no means dangerous to me. It was full of lightning element? That was empowering me.

,I'll be able to use lightning magic more easily and powerfully in this space.

As a lightning-type mage, it's natural to have resistance to electricity.

Above all, I was the owner of the cherry blossom tree. It was a fact that even the ill-tempered cherry blossom tree acknowledged.

Cherry blossom trees began to grow everywhere in the garden. They weren't growing wildly and indiscriminately. The cherry blossom trees pushed out the flowers in the existing flower beds and grew rapidly. The garden instantly became a cherry blossom tree forest.

Pajijijijik!

The cherry blossom trees resonated. Lightning danced between the trees and formed a barrier.

The color of the electricity output from the cherry blossom trees changed to a bright pink, the color of cherry blossoms. The pink electricity moved as if stirring in all directions, then permeated me.

,It's not just the color that's changed. The moment the cherry blossom-colored lightning permeated me, my body felt lighter and my senses related to mana improved. Is this a buff-type electricity that temporarily increases physical enhancement and mana affinity?

-Haa. I'm hungry. Bring me something to eat. Preferably a spirit stone.

I could feel the will of the cherry blossom tree.

,So it wasn't unconscious all this time? It remembers the spirit stone well.

-I wasn't completely unconscious. I remember things sporadically. Especially when I eat spirit stones, my consciousness flares up. So give me some spirit stones.

,One spirit stone is worth 2kg of gold.

-Isn't that enough for my meals? The barrier I maintain at all times is worth several times that.

I couldn't logically refute it.

[Purchase a spirit stone for 200 inventory points.]

Pajijik.

The pink electricity wrapped around the spirit stone in my hand and took it away.

,Electricity with physical force... or not. Is it using it like telekinesis?

-Heuung. Good. Very good. This is the first time I've tasted something like this. Do you have any more spirit stones?

,No. It's not something you can easily get just because you have money.

I had dozens of spirit stones in my inventory, but I wasn't going to provide them indefinitely for the sake of the cherry blossom tree's habits.

-It's only natural since it's such a good thing. I can't find any information about it even if I search the internet.

,The internet? You can access the internet?

-It's easy. I just connect with radio waves.

A tree that uses the internet. If people knew, they would be surprised and feel uneasy.

,Don't tell anyone else.

-I'm not that stupid either. I don't want to attract unnecessary attention either. I don't want to be a monkey in a zoo.

The cherry blossom tree is sassy, but I believe it will do well because it's not stupid.

-More than that, give me a name soon. How long are you going to call me just cherry blossom tree?

,Do you need a name?

-Are you kidding me? Do I just look like an ordinary cherry blossom tree to you?

The cherry blossom tree's voice became venomous. Pajik pajik. Pink lightning flashed between the cherry blossom petals.

I needed time to think because it was a problem I had never thought about before.

,Giving it a name isn't difficult. The problem is that if it doesn't like the name, it's bound to cause a ruckus.

Should I call it Cherry since it's a cherry blossom tree?

,I have a feeling I'll have to replace the appliances in the mansion again if I do that.

I pondered and said.

,Kirsi. That's your name.

-What's that? It's just another word for cherry. ...Hmm. It sounds different from Cherry, so it might be pretty good?

Kirsi's will wasn't bad. It seemed to like the name quite a bit. That was fortunate. I was a little nervous because I had no talent for naming things.

,I'm glad you like it. I believe you'll do a good job. I'm going to focus on training for a while.

The power I had was largely two things.

One is magic, and the other is supernatural abilities.

Usually, even having just one supernatural ability is said to be a blessing. If you have more than two, you are said to be loved by fate.

But I have more than 10 supernatural abilities that I have realized so far.

Lightning, Marksmanship, Acceleration, Blessing of Water, Golden Semen, Heightened Sensitivity, Lightning Sky Style, Vitality Absorption, Inventory.

It doesn't end here. Recently, I realized the supernatural ability Leap while training in space magic.

I have a total of 10 supernatural abilities.

The problem is that I don't know the reason. Is it because I entered the game? Or is there some other secret in this body itself? It's even harder to guess because there's no connection between the supernatural abilities.

I can't find the answer even if I worry about it. But it was certain that I had the power. I use the given power as much as possible.

,And recently, I realized a new technique of the Lightning Sky Style.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Art (雷術) Lightning Eye Golden Gaze (雷眼金睛).

It was a technique that could be called a kind of sorcery. I had some confidence in sorcery... but I had never succeeded in Lightning Eye Golden Gaze. I had been working on this sorcery for a whole week, but there was no progress.

,I could understand most magic or sorcery at a glance, but... Lightning Eye Golden Gaze is an exception. This is not a sorcery that can be done with talent alone.

I focused only on Lightning Eye Golden Gaze for a week and then gave up.

,Maybe it's a sorcery that can only be used when you reach the realm of 7th-circle or higher.

I focused on training for a while and then went outside.

It's really time to reach 7th-circle.
