Inside a building in Seoul.

I was having a one-on-one meeting with a man. He was a middle-aged man with a neat impression. He wore glasses, and his black suit was impeccable.

The man held out a paper cup filled with coffee. I stared at the man without even looking at the coffee.

,I'm Park Hyun-woong, the director of the National Order Bureau. Thank you for cooperating with the Order Bureau so willingly. It made things easier for me."

National Order Bureau.

A South Korean government agency. It mainly handles cases related to magic.

,I'm Seong Yujin. You probably already know."

Even as I spoke, I didn't let my guard down against him. I knew Park Hyun-woong's strength because I had seen the original work, but... seeing it in a comic and experiencing it directly are different things. I wasn't sure I could beat him for sure.

,A diligent sophomore in college. His reputation isn't bad. His parents passed away in an incident two years ago... and he has no relationship with his relatives. I've investigated you in various ways... but your personality as written in the information doesn't match your current personality. More than anything... were you a magician from the start? Summoning rain clouds to suppress a forest fire... that's not the kind of power a normal magician can show."

,Does it matter if I was a magician from the start? What's important now is my choice."

Park Hyun-woong pondered for a moment and then nodded.

,How much do you know about Grimoires?"

,A magical item that allows you to use magic?"

,That's right. That's how much it's known to the public. Then do you know how Grimoires appear?"

,......."

I knew, but I kept my mouth shut. Information related to the creation of Grimoires is controlled in this world. If I knew, I would be suspected unnecessarily.

,People's wishes. Curses, some evil ritual, or someone's blessing. Unknown coincidences, natural disasters, etc. Grimoires are born for all sorts of reasons. Most Grimoires are born by chance, but... you can create them if you set your mind to it. That's why detailed information about Grimoires is controlled."

,Why are you telling me controlled information?"

,All the members of the National Order Bureau know this information. It's also information that everyone who knows knows. Seong Yujin-ssi. Come to the National Order Bureau. The National Order Bureau needs a strong magician like you."

,I will."

Park Hyun-woong's eyes widened. He quickly managed his expression and said.

,Were you planning to join the Order Bureau from the start? I've prepared quite a few things to persuade you, but... I feel a little empty. Of course, that doesn't mean I'm disappointed. Welcome to the Order Bureau."

,However, there's a condition."

The reason I joined the National Order Bureau is because the National Order Bureau is at the center of the original story. From the standpoint of aiming for a happy ending, it's better to be at the center of the story.

,I see. I'm rather relieved that there's a condition. Tell me."

,I want a commander-level position. I'm too strong to roll around with the chumps."

,I admit that you're strong. Your magic power is also unusual, and you own at least two Grimoires. However, the position of commander of the Great Magic Force can only be attained by a proven magician. Above all, the commander positions of the current seven Great Magic Forces are full."

,The reason I want a commander position is because I want independent operational rights. I don't mind creating a separate unit."

,Independent operational rights? Even commanders aren't given that much authority."

,Really? That's a bit different from my expectations. I guess I'll have to postpone joining the National Order Bureau."

,......What is your purpose?"

,Killing the Harriers."

I said firmly.

Harriers. Magic criminals who misuse magic for crime. To achieve a happy ending, it was necessary to wipe these guys out.

,I suggested it because I thought I could easily kill the Harriers if I had the power of a commander in the Great Magic Force, but... it doesn't matter. I can kill them even if I act alone."

,South Korea does not allow private sanctions."

,Then try to stop me."

,......."

Magic power flows out from Park Hyun-woong. His intention to kill my spirit first was obvious. I scoffed at him and countered with magic power. A clash of magic power. In that silent power struggle, the surrounding objects vibrate subtly.

,...I'm sorry. Let's stop here. I've taken a brief look at your skills. You seem to have the amount of magic power and magic manipulation skills suitable for a commander."

,Should I take that as a promise to give me the commander position?"

,I think I'd regret it immensely if I let you go from the National Order Bureau now. Ah, but you still have to meet the minimum requirements."

,Should I build up some achievements? I guess I can go out and catch a few Harriers and fuck them up."

,Of course, achievements are important. But what's more important than that is force. And what can be said to be the symbol of that force is... Ars Magna."

,Ars Magna? What's that?"

I asked even though I knew to avoid unnecessary suspicion.

,Take out your Grimoire. Blue Phoenix."

I took out a lighter.

,Ars Magna refers to the true power of the Grimoire. Whether you can draw it out or not is the difference between a commander and a member. Your Grimoire, Blue Phoenix, was first recorded 1,500 years ago. It is said that Blue Phoenix's Ars Magna, Azure Flame, burns everything."

,This lighter is 1,500 years old? Did they have disposable lighters back then?"

,The appearance of the Grimoire is not important. It changes depending on the era and the owner. How about it? Can you draw out Azure Flame?"

Ttiktik.

I rolled the flint of the disposable lighter with my thumb a few times, and a fire started. A red fire. A flame burning with my magic power as fuel, not gas.

,I feel like I know, but I don't know.'

Regardless of my skills, it hasn't even been two hours since I got the Grimoire. I'm even unskilled at using the power of the Grimoire. So, it's logically impossible to draw out the true power of the Grimoire or whatever.

,But if I have an extraordinary talent, the story changes.'

[Activating the time of genius for 10 seconds.]

The Grimoire and I are connected. The fact that I can handle flames is proof of that.

I trace that connection through self-observation to understand the Grimoire. I did self-observation to death in [Ascension to the Realm of Light]. Moreover, I even used the time of genius. It wasn't difficult to grasp the essence of Blue Phoenix.

It was a flame.

A flame burning blue.

Ars Magna - Azure Flame (Blue Flame)

Chikchik.

A blue flame flared up above the lighter. The consumption of magic power increased fivefold.

Park Hyun-woong laughed weakly as if he was dumbfounded.

,To think you can use Ars Magna without even a day after obtaining the Blue Phoenix. What an absurd talent. Good. You are now the commander of the 8th Division."

,There was an 8th Division?"

,The 8th Great Magic Force. It's only natural to create a new unit since we can't suddenly retire the existing commanders. I'll transfer the members of other units to the 8th Division. However, I don't intend to force the members."

,I don't really care. Ah. It doesn't matter if I scout other magicians as my subordinates, right? Since I'm the commander."

,I'll do the final screening. I'll allow it if they're not criminals and there's no major problem. The Great Magic Force is always short of talent."

I put the lighter away and tried to get up from my seat, but he stopped me with a gesture.

,Do you still have something to say?"

,Haha. How heartless. Aren't we colleagues who will work together in the future? In some ways, we can even be called comrades. I have many questions for you. Like the power to control rain."

,I don't have many questions."

,Don't be like that. Shouldn't we negotiate your salary? And aren't you curious about the Harrier you grilled nicely?"

,The salary is up to you. Is that guy alive? He had burns all over his body, including his eyes and internal organs. It wouldn't be strange if he died right away."

,The 6th Division's support skills are excellent. They can recover even if their limbs are cut off. Keep that in mind. Even if you have a fierce battle with the Harrier bastards, they can recover as long as they're alive."

I had various conversations with Park Hyun-woong. It was tedious, but I roughly talked to him.

The next day.

I received the 8th Division office at the headquarters. According to Park Hyun-woong, they hurriedly cleaned up a warehouse and made it into an office. I didn't care. I wasn't going to use the office often anyway.

He later said that he would buy another building and give it to me as the 8th Division headquarters. I didn't have high expectations.

As I was walking down the hallway towards the headquarters office, three men blocked my way. They were wearing suits, but they had a swaggering atmosphere. Among them, a muscular man with dreadlocks smiled wryly and said.

,Who is this? It's the big shot who became the commander of a new unit as soon as he joined the Order Bureau. There's a rumor that he's the director-nim's hidden son, is that true, commander-nim?"

,Who the fuck are you?"

,I'm Yoo Seok-chang, the vice-commander of the 3rd Division~ Commander of the 8th Division. The commander position was mine, but I was pushed out thanks to a rolling stone that suddenly came along. Wouldn't I feel like shit?"

,I feel even more like shit. Because a chump like you is picking a fight without knowing his place."

,What, you little shit?!"

Kwaaaang!

A huge wind grabbed the three of them and pressed them to the floor. They couldn't even react to the sudden sign.

,Did you say Yoo Sseok-chang? Don't make so much noise when you're weak."

I remember what Park Hyun-woong said yesterday. The 6th Division is so skilled that they can recover even if their limbs are cut off, right?

Wiiiiing.

I rotated the wind and tore off their limbs.

,Kkeuaaaaaaak!"

A scream echoes loudly. Everyone else must have heard it.

,Do you think the director is stupid enough to give me the commander position of the 8th Division? From now on, look at your opponent carefully before you act up."

,You, you son of a Pussy! Do you think you'll be safe after this?! My sister will tear you apart and kill you!!"

,You're begging to be killed."

Hwareureuk!

A flame rose and grilled Yoo Seok-chang nicely. I didn't kill him. Things would get bigger if I did.

,Aaaaaaaaak!"

I left his screams behind and entered the office.
