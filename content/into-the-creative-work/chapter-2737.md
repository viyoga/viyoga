The biggest reason I seized control of the Dragon Hunter Association was to monopolize the dragon's Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood.

That intention worked. No matter how high-ranking an A-class Hunter was, no one dared to oppose me. If they wanted to live, they wouldn't dare to embezzle Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood, and instead sold them to the Association. I bought the Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood at their proper value. Of course, I controlled what that proper value was.

Since I held not only power but also economic control firmly in my grasp, I could even set the price of Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood.

The problem was that Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood were dwindling as time passed. Dragons were starting to die out faster than I had anticipated.

,At least within the activity radius of the Hunters, you can hardly see Ancient Dragon-class, let alone Mature Dragon-class dragons anymore. Even Wyrm-class dragons are noticeably decreasing.,

Similarly, the number of Hunters was also decreasing. It was only natural since the job of hunting dragons itself was diminishing. Many were switching from Dragon Hunters to mercenaries. Or doing both in parallel.

,But the Association is also the one giving out mercenary requests.,

Even if dragons were to become extinct, my power wouldn't waver. Rather, my power would become even more solidified.

,The absence of dragons is the same as losing the means to become stronger.,

Unlike reality, the Hunters in this world cannot awaken naturally. To consume mana, they must consume Dragon Hearts or Dragon Heart Blood. No one among humans will emerge to threaten me.

,There are three Dragon King-class dragons left in this world. I want to find them and kill them... but there's no way to find them.,

The Dragon King-class dragons have completely hidden themselves. Even if High-thera-ssi directly intervened, there was no way to find them.

Even if I directly used the Thousand Eyes, there's no way I could see every nook and cranny of this world.

,Those Dragon Kings are so scared. Tsk tsk.,

Time passes by. I tried to find the remaining Dragon King-class dragons, but... all failed. Now, even the lingering attachment to that disappears.

,It's about time to end this world game.,

I pondered quite a bit. Not being able to meet High-thera-ssi and Gina-ssi again was quite regrettable. However, there was nothing to gain in this world except for those two. Develop this world like the [Baekhwan] world and make it a world just for me? There are not enough resources for that, and it would take too long. Also, the population of this world is less than 10% of [Baekhwan].

,And it's not like I can't meet Gina-ssi and High-thera-ssi forever.,

There are a few ways.

One of them is 'Fate,' sold in the random draw shop.

[Fate

Connections do not disappear.

Summons a target with a connection by consuming points.

Price: 1,000 points

※Caution

The summoned target consumes a certain number of points every 24 hours.

A target that has been summoned once can be summoned again after 300 days.

The target may refuse the summons.]

Connections continue even after the ending. And it can also be raised through Connection Level Up Tickets. I can use Connection Level Up Tickets later.

,Still, it's true that I won't be able to meet them for a while. But why isn't the ending popping up? It can't be that it only ends when I die, right? Is it because of the three Dragon King-class dragons that have gone into hiding?,

Anyway, I have time left.

I used that time to invest in Gigantria. To be precise, I was preparing for the situation after I finished the game. To ensure that High-thera-ssi and Gina-ssi don't suffer and that their quality of life doesn't decline, I'm pouring the minimum infrastructure into Gigantria.

Just as the work was almost finished, I received news that Gina-ssi, who had retired from being a Hunter, was pregnant. On a whim, I proposed to Gina-ssi and High-thera-ssi. Naturally, they accepted my proposal. Marriage was promised.

,Yujin-gun. The ring is complete.,

,Oh. It took three months? Unexpected. I thought you'd whip it up in a week.,

High-thera-ssi held out a silver-white ring.

,It was a matter of handling the Dragon Heart of the Deep Sea Heavenly Dragon, Calodon. No matter how much it's me, it was inevitable that it would take time.,

,You've worked hard. Thank you.,

,May I ask what you intend to use that ring for?,

,It's for a gift. I'm going to take it to another world.,

,I see.,

High-thera-ssi nodded calmly. She knew that I could travel to other worlds. She couldn't help but know since I was bringing things from other worlds so readily. Even so, I didn't tell her everything.

,If High-thera-ssi knew that this place was a world within a creation, even her mental state would be shaken. Not to mention Gina-ssi. Just because I love them doesn't mean I need to share everything.,

Share everything just because you love them, just because you're family? Just the thought of it makes me feel bad.

I had actually experienced it before. A long time ago, when my father took my onahole. I was extremely angry at the time.

,Since we're family, I can be more lenient than with strangers. But even there, there's a final line.,

Before being family, they were individuals.

If you asked me if I could die for my parents... I couldn't die. Of course, there might be people who could die for their parents. But that wasn't me.

,What's the name of the ring?,

,I haven't really thought about it. Since Calodon's Dragon Heart was the main ingredient, I'll name it Calodon.,

I check the design of the ring. A simple silver-white ring. There's nothing particularly special about it, but it's strangely shiny as if it's emitting light on its own, making it attractive.

I put the ring in my inventory and used Appraisal.

[Calodon

‣Mana Assistance

Assists in the use of mana. Efficiency increases when using mana.

‣Authority of Water

Creates and manipulates water.]

,Two abilities. A simple explanation, but that's why it's more powerful. I'll use the original and gift it to Han Ah-young-ssi.,

Before that, I need to see the ending of this world first.

,Since this isn't a quest, I don't know the exact conditions for the ending. Do I need to kill the remaining Dragon King-class dragons? Or is it my marriage with High-thera-ssi and Gina-ssi? First of all, it's certain that the death of Mir, the original protagonist, has nothing to do with it.,

I put aside the complicated problem that has no answer and beckoned to High-thera-ssi.

High-thera-ssi sat on my lap as if it were natural, with an expressionless face. She and I kissed deeply.

For the past six months, Mir had been traveling around the world under the instructions of the Hundred-Eyed Heavenly Dragon, Gigamera. Thanks to the helmet-shaped Dragon God's relic that Gigamera had given him, he could communicate with Gigamera in real-time, no matter how far away he was.

-Dragon God's relics are objects imbued with the power of the Dragon God. I don't know how the power is imbued in the objects, but they all have special abilities. Terrarisk. No, High-thera-ssi becoming human is also due to the power of the Dragon God.

,Do the relics have to be destroyed?,

-They must be. The power imbued in the relics is the power of the Dragon God... When the relics are destroyed, they will naturally return to the Dragon God. When the Dragon God's seal is released and descends upon this world. The Dragon God will have even more powerful power. Also, the seal weakens when the relics are destroyed.

Mir did so. It was a waste, but he had to do it to achieve his goal.

-The Deep Sea Heavenly Dragon, Calodon, has died. He created a deadly poison with the Dragon Hearts of the Poison Dragons and released it into the sea... Vile. But I can't deny that it's effective. I need to talk to the other two Dragon Kings.

Every time Mir heard news of Gwangroe's winning streak, he gritted his teeth and did what he had to do.

After more than half a year, only the final seal release remained.

Mir stared at a huge mural carved into a deep valley cliff. The very old mural was shining and sparkling on its own.

Three Dragon King-class dragons landed behind Mir.

The Hundred-Eyed Heavenly Dragon Gigamera, with three eyes.

The Two-Lives Heavenly Dragon Balkins, with two heads.

The Ancient Tree Heavenly Dragon Todori, shaped like a giant tree.

The last Dragon King-class dragons remaining in this world had gathered in this place.

,You've worked hard, Mir. Now it's our turn to step up.,

,You've done well for a human.,

,...Good job.,

Vast mana leaked from the dragons' bodies and seeped into the mural. Mir felt uneasy and asked.

,What are you doing now?,

,You probably know that some dragons are born naturally. The reason for that is mana. But recently, there have been almost no naturally occurring dragons. That's because Gwangroe is collecting the Dragon Hearts of dragons. Even if humans eat Dragon Hearts, the mana they absorb is practically less than 10%. Most of the rest tends to scatter into nature.,

,Gwangroe is storing the Dragon Hearts separately... so the mana in this world has become insufficient...?,

,I'm glad you understand quickly. Originally, the final seal would have been naturally released by resonating with the mana of this world. But since the overall mana of this world has decreased... the seal won't be released unless we forcibly inject mana like this.,

,Is it necessary to inject mana so rapidly? If you do this, you guys will....,

The dragons' mana injection did not stop.

,We don't have time. A long tail gets caught, as they say. Eventually, Gwangroe will find us and kill us.,

,We're sick and tired of hiding all this time. Now that Calodon has been defeated, it's almost impossible for us to beat that bastard. If that's the case, then we'll give him a piece of our minds even if we die.,

,...It's for the sake of the dragons. The Dragon God will set everything right.,

The strongest beings in this world. They were afraid of the Dragon Hunter, Gwangroe. In some ways, even more than Mir, who was human.

Their bodies, which had squeezed out mana to the limit and poured it into the mural, crumbled into dust and disappeared.

Mir, left alone, stared at the mural.

Jeojeojeojeok!

The cliff on which the mural was drawn crumbled, and golden light appeared, soaring into the sky. The world vibrates. Mana gradually begins to fill up.

Mir, overwhelmed by the oppressive presence, trembled but raised his head.

A golden dragon with a huge body over 100m tall floated proudly. The Dragon God looked at Mir and then looked around. The Dragon God sighed softly.

-The balance has been extremely disrupted.

,Dragon God! Dragon God-isiyeo! We have called you! We are...!,

-Human. I know without you saying it. The Gwangroe you speak of will soon come here. Everything will be settled.

,...Do you see the future?,

-I have seen it. But it is not the future I see now. Also, I can no longer see the future. I am weaker now than before the seal. It cannot be helped since a human is monopolizing the Dragon Hearts that should return to nature....

,Weaker than before the seal...! Are you saying you can't kill Gwangroe?!,

-You are rude. Just because I have become weaker than before the seal does not mean I cannot kill a single human, does it? I will kill Gwangroe and restore the balance. If I kill 98% of the humans and restore the sea to normal... the balance will be restored.

,98% of humans...?,

-Do not fear. You have done a great service, so you will enjoy a long life.

A startled Mir raised his voice.

,W-wait a minute! 98% of humans have to die! That's unfair! If only Gwangroe! If only Gwangroe dies, the world will return to normal!,

-Silence. I make the judgments. No one can interfere with my decisions. All that is allowed to you is to witness it. You are to quietly witness it from there.

Mir opened his mouth, but no voice came out. He could not escape because a transparent wall surrounded him.

The Dragon God slowly moved its head and observed the world.
