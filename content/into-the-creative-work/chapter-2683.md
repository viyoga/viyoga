Elena deliberately sent informants into Duke Su's territory to stir things up. The pretext was something along the lines of searching for traces of a cult.

,Duke Su must feel like he's been slapped in the face out of nowhere.'

It's quite unpleasant to have informants from another faction running rampant in one's territory. In fact, this was practically asking for a fight.

,But it's not enough to justify a war.'

The informants' public identities have nothing to do with the Valdert family. Even if they stir things up, all they do is dig up information. They're not committing crimes. If Duke Su doesn't want a war, all he can do is find fault with the informants and expel them.

Elena's goal is Duke Su's reaction. If he protests openly, she will back down and draw a line.

,I'd love to kill him right now, but I can't. But I can't leave him alone. He'll definitely have a negative impact on my wedding in some way.'

Elena deliberately met with the Emperor openly. The reason for the meeting was to report on the capture of Winter Castle and to receive the Emperor's praise. Of course, she expected that there would be behind-the-scenes discussions. Duke Su would definitely be suspicious. Just meeting with the Emperor is a form of pressure.

,He must be anxious because he attacked the Valdert family in the past.'

Elena didn't say a word to the Emperor about Duke Su. Revenge is hers. She had no intention of involving the Emperor at all.

,Come on, Duke Su. Are you just going to take it like a punching bag? The fact that you haven't protested officially means you're going to move behind the scenes. Show me what you've got.'

That way, she can directly step in and smash his bullshit, which will serve as a warning.

Some time later.

One of the Valdert family's butlers reported to Elena.

,Madam, recently, merchants have appeared in the City of Summer. It seems they're not just visiting for trade, but trying to settle down in the city."

City of Summer.

The second most populous city among the territories Elena owns. Its official name is District 3,042, Verdant Summer.

A district where early summer continues all year round.

In terms of economic scale alone, it's a much larger district than District 3,041, the Valdert Territory. Half of Valdert's budget comes from the City of Summer.

,Where are these merchants from?"

,Their origins are all different, and there was nothing particularly unusual about them. However, judging from their actions, it seems they've coordinated in advance."

Normally, she wouldn't have paid any attention to the appearance of new merchants and would have left them alone.

But this wasn't a normal situation. The entire Atlantis was suffering from the volcanic island.

,If some merchants deliberately try to mess with the City of Summer... the economy is bound to be shaken. The situation is that bad.'

The Valdert family was constantly monitoring the economy.

,We were even more vigilant because Duke Su might be up to something. Merchants appearing at this timing? It's highly likely that it's Duke Su's doing.'

Elena got up from her seat. She decided to take direct action. As she was about to leave the room, a woman appeared.

A beauty with jet-black hair and eyes contrasted by snow-white skin. It was Arel, whom Seong Yujin brought from the United States of America and who came to work for the Valdert family.

,Madam. I'd like you to confiscate the merchants' assets instead of killing them immediately."

,...Arel. Did you happen to overhear the report?"

,I check all reports before they reach you, Madam. Have you forgotten?"

,I was just a little surprised that you appeared and spoke up when I was about to take action. Is the situation so bad that we need to covet the merchants' assets?"

,Are you really asking because you don't know? The budget used for your wedding alone is more than 30% of the Valdert family's accumulated wealth. Most of the remaining assets are real estate. They're difficult assets to use urgently."

Elena hesitated slightly. Honestly, she was aware that she had invested excessively in the wedding.

,...There's a time to spend money."

,There's a time to make money."

It was advice for the Valdert family. Elena couldn't argue with the advice filled with loyalty.

,Okay. I'll try to squeeze as much money as possible out of those merchants."

• • •

Gont, one of the merchants who entered the City of Summer, looked out at the city scenery through the office window.

Despite the humid weather, everyone was wearing masks. It was because of the volcanic ash flying in the air.

In Gont's eyes, those citizens looked like slaves. Slaves who would bring money to him. At least, that's how it would be in a few months.

,Shake the economy, seize the opportunity to settle down, and suck the money dry. I don't know whose idea this plan came from, but... it's a really tempting plan. Khehehe.'

He would use the money he received from investors and the wealth he had accumulated so far to establish himself in this city. He would push out the existing merchants and unite with the merchants who shared the plan. If he succeeded, he could definitely establish himself in this city.

,I'm a little worried about Illusion Duke, but Illusion Duke isn't someone who directly interferes with the economy. He's famous for not interfering as long as you pay your taxes well.'

Things were going well.

The goods Gont sold were daily necessities such as masks and water. He sold them at 10% to 20% cheaper than the existing market price. He had an overwhelming price advantage. When other merchants reluctantly lowered their prices, Gont lowered his prices even further.

,Gont-nim! Other merchants have come to protest!"

,Ignore them. Sell more goods instead of dealing with those bastards. We need to seize the economic power of this city within a month. We can make up for the losses later."

,But is it really okay to do this? What if Illusion Duke is displeased...?"

,Haha. I've already prepared the tribute to offer to Illusion Duke. You just proceed according to the plan."

Gont was on a roll. The plan worked perfectly. The existing merchants went bankrupt, and Gont's merchant group took over their positions. Every time he saw the existing merchants closing their stores or leaving for other cities, he felt a sense of victory.

,If things continue like this... I can become one of the great merchants.'

The success of the plan was just around the corner.

Gont requested additional money from the investor, and the investor willingly sent additional investment funds.

,Gont-nim. We succeeded! We've seized the economy of this city!"

,Now even Illusion Duke won't be able to intervene recklessly. Because we're the ones who hold the economic power."

,We can't leave out alcohol on this monumental day. Let's have a drink!"

,Hahaha. That's right. We can't leave out alcohol. Pour me a drink."

Joljoljol.

Alcohol fills Gont's glass.

,Cheers!"

He toasts with his subordinates and drinks the alcohol.

The alcohol was really... like water wrung out of a rag.

,Ugh."

Gont spat out the alcohol. Instead of alcohol, there was water wrung out of a rag in the glass.

,You damn bastards! Are you playing pranks like this on this good day?!"

Gont glared at the subordinate who poured the alcohol into his glass. The subordinate's body collapses. The subordinate who fell to the floor was a pale-skinned corpse.

,...!"

Surprised, Gont jumped up from his seat. He looked at his other subordinates. They were all bloodless corpses. The commotion from just now didn't even exist.

,W-What is this?!"

Panicked, he turned his head, wanting to escape, and looked at the entrance. The door was open. A woman with short blue hair was standing there with a cane, sneering at Gont.

,Illusion Duke...?! Could it be, is all of this your doing?!"

,Don't raise your voice. Don't raise your voice."

,You can't do this! We complied with the law! We even sent people to offer you tribute! And you received the tribute! What you're doing is betrayal! It's an act of betraying the trust of merchants! Do you think other merchants will stand still?!"

,Don't use such strong words. It makes you look weak."

,Aaaaaaaaaaa!"

Gont screamed and threw himself towards the window. From the beginning, there was no way he could win against Illusion Duke in a fight. All he could do was escape from this place.

It was the moment he felt he had successfully broken through the window and escaped outside.

He was sitting in his seat.

,...I definitely... Could it be an illusion? Is it an illusion? Since when?!"

,Since when have I not been using illusions... Haha. Should I stop playing around? It's not a difficult question, so I'll answer it. One minute. One minute based on reality."

,...One minute?"

,Shatter, Kyouka Suigetsu."

Jjaenggeurang!

With the sound of glass breaking, the scenery changed.

A cramped office, subordinates standing still with blank expressions, and the tribute that he had definitely used was placed on the desk.

Gont gasped. His mind was a mess. It was hard for him to accept this reality right now. Everything up until now was an illusion? He had been in this city for dozens of days? Even now, that memory was vivid. No, maybe this isn't an illusion?

Elena ignored Gont's confusion and walked forward. Her hand reached for the tribute placed on the desk.

Gold bars.

,Is this the tribute you were going to offer me?"

,Y-Yes, Illusion Duke-gakha! I'll offer everything I have! Please, have mercy!"

,I can make as many gold bars and jewels as I want. It doesn't even take long. If I snap my fingers like this... Ta-da."

Gold bars and jewels appeared on the desk, forming a small mountain. Gont opened his mouth. Even in this situation, greed was slowly rising.

,But I try not to make tribute as much as possible. Why? Because even though it seems like a benefit in the short term, I know it's a loss in the long term. I don't want to ruin the economy with my own hands."

When she waved her hand, the tribute created by illusion disappeared.

,I looked for your investor. He's a nobleman who focuses on illegal activities, especially smuggling. Unfortunately, I couldn't find any connection to Duke Su."

,Duke Su... you say? W-We have nothing to do with Duke Su!"

,To be caught in a spider web and not even know it. It's pathetic. Of course, that's just what I'm saying. I'm not pathetic at all."

,......"

,There's something I need you to do."

,W-What do you need me to do?"

Ttak.

Elena snapped her fingers. White powder appeared on the desk.

,It's a magical powder that makes you feel good when you eat it. Go to Duke Su's territory and sell it. Of course, you won't remember me. You're just selling this magical powder at a low price of your own free will."

Elena disappeared along with the tribute.

Gont was stunned for a while, then suddenly came to his senses. His subordinates also came to their senses.

,Damn it! Clean up this powder!"

,S-Sorry. I'll clean it up right away!"

,And pack your bags quickly! We have to move diligently to sell this!"
