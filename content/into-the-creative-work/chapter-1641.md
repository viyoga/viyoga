Defeat!

Those simple two letters reached the ears of Hatep, the ruler of the Golden Desert.

Hatep trembled at the word that didn't suit him. He found it hard to bear the anger and humiliation rising from deep within.

,Say it again! You're telling me only 1,000 survived?!"

The desert warrior kneeling before Hatep spoke with a serious face.

,Oh, great ruler of the desert! We did our best! Tutankhamun used wicked magic, and the great warriors fought to the end but were defeated! Give us another chance! We must summon all the desert warriors to face Tutankhamun!"

,You goddamn bastard! What did you do so well that you're raising your voice!"

Hatep threw the staff he was holding at the warrior. The warrior, hit by the staff, bounced back and rolled away.

Hatep. He was a great warrior himself.

,Listen carefully. Summoning warriors is nothing. There are no warriors in this golden desert who dare defy my words! Maintaining an army is also easy. My wealth can feed hundreds of thousands of people for months! But you bastards ruined something that can't be easily obtained even with money! That's why I can't forgive you!"

The warrior, who had been rolling on the ground, spat out blood and looked at Hatep with awe.

,The great ruler of the Golden Desert is mourning the deaths of 2,000 warriors...!"

Hatep's expression twisted.

,This stupid bastard still doesn't get the point!"

Hatep pointed his finger at the warrior.

,Defeat! It's okay. I can accept it. I was a great warrior. I know I can't always win in battle. But running away is a different story! Hatep's army was scared of Tutankhamun and ran away! That's the rumor going around the marketplace right now!"

That was what enraged Hatep.

,Reputation! You bastards tarnished my reputation!"

Hatep suddenly rose from his golden throne. As he stomped his feet in anger, the floor trembled. His servants fell silent and bowed their heads.

,People are saying that Hatep's army was defeated by Tutankhamun and ran away in fear! Hatep the Runaway! That's the nickname you gave me!"

,A, at that time, retreat was the best option! Tutankhamun is a wicked sorcerer who commands the dead! Fighting to the death would only increase Tutankhamun's power!"

,Shut up! Enough with the excuses! You don't deserve to be called warriors!"

Hatep looked at the warrior's body. He could see golden ornaments adorning his sturdy body. It was too precious for this useless bastard to have.

,I don't need deserters in my army!"

Hatep raised his hand. Soldiers made of gold and sand appeared around him. The sand soldiers rushed at the warrior and bound his body.

,Oh, great ruler of the Golden Desert! Give us one more chance! This time, I will offer you Tutankhamun's head!"

,How can I trust someone who ran away once? You useless bastard. Fortunately, there's still a role left for you... Go to prison."

The golden sand soldiers dragged the warrior away. Hatep vented his anger by looking at the golden sand soldiers. The golden sand soldiers were excellent. They listened well, didn't get tired, and weren't afraid of death. The problem was that golden sand soldiers were expensive.

A golden sand soldier required at least 1kg of gold to create. If you used more gold, you could create a stronger golden sand soldier.

Hatep needed gold. A lot of gold.

,Hey! Collect all the gold that was given to the warriors! Also, prepare a sacrificial ritual! I will offer the warriors to the master of the oasis and get gold in return!"

,Oh, great ruler of the Golden Desert! Please reconsider! Warriors are high-quality manpower! It's too wasteful to use them as sacrificial offerings! Instead, demote them to slaves and use them!"

One of the servants shouted. His voice was filled with loyalty. But Hatep's reaction was cold.

,Do you dare defy my orders?"

,N, no, absolutely not!"

,Then follow my orders. I am absolutely right. What I need now is gold! If I have gold, I can get rid of Tutankhamun anytime!"

Hatep shouted.

He was looking at the aftermath of Tutankhamun's death, not Tutankhamun himself.

He could obtain the Golden Sand Soldier (SSS) skill as a reward for winning the special event. He used AP to get confirmation from the system. It seemed like he could get other rewards as well, but right now, all Hatep needed was the Golden Sand Soldier skill.

,With just the golden sand soldiers, I can dominate the entire western desert region of Atlantis!'

Tutankhamun wasn't that important to Hatep right now. After all, Tutankhamun would eventually fall to him.

,Hmm?"

Hatep could roughly pinpoint Tutankhamun's location. But just now, Tutankhamun had disappeared. He wouldn't have died in a sudden disaster. If Tutankhamun had died, an alert would have popped up saying that the special event had ended.

There were a few possibilities to consider.

,He either hid his presence with a special power, or he entered a special space like a dungeon. Either way, I don't like it.'

Even if he summoned an army, he couldn't set out if he didn't know where the enemy was.

,Wait. Tutankhamun is after me. That means even if I don't send an army first, he'll come looking for me someday.'

According to reports, Tutankhamun is a necromancer who commands an undead army. If given time, he will increase his undead army as much as possible before attacking.

,But an army can't grow infinitely. There must be a limit to the number of undead he can command. Above all, this special event has a time limit. Time is on my side.'

40 days left.

Hatep would win the special event if he just endured without having to kill Tutankhamun. Conversely, if Tutankhamun failed to kill Hatep within 40 days, he would lose the special event and disappear.

,It's Tutankhamun who's in a hurry, not me!'

A necromancer's strategy is obvious. He will try to increase the number of undead as much as possible before fighting.

,If situations like this, where I can't pinpoint his location, happen frequently... I'm the only one who loses out. In that case, I'd rather...!'

Hatep gathered his fighting spirit. The air trembled and vibrated. His servants quickly lowered their gazes. It was an instinctive reaction. The influence of the King of the Golden Desert (SS) and the Pharaoh (SS) had affected his servants. Right now, it was difficult for his servants to advise Hatep.

,Summon the army! We will conquer the Ant Desert in Area 5,145!"

,Oh, great ruler of the Golden Desert! Tutankhamun is growing stronger! War with other areas is premature!"

,You're wrong! Now is the perfect time. I will personally lead this conquest! I will show the power of the golden sand soldiers to the citizens! Everyone will call me the Golden King and praise me! Hehehehehe!"

Overwhelmed by Hatep's majesty, the servants looked at each other. No one could step forward. The conquest war had begun.

,After dominating the Ant Desert, we'll offer all the useless prisoners to the master of the oasis. Then we can get more gold. Keukeu."

Hatep felt that his future was shining like the sun.

,Kkyaaaaaaak!"

A large hawk screamed in the sky.

When I looked up, the hawk dropped something on me.

,It's a delivery hawk. Harmo sent it.'

What Harmo sent was related to necromancy. One was a book that allowed me to learn the skill I had requested, and the other was a skull necklace.

,Undertaker's Secretary

Allows you to learn specific black magic.

Rank: B」

,The conditions are met. Would you like to learn black magic?"

,I wouldn't have asked Harmo separately if I didn't want to learn it. Of course, I'll learn it."

As the Undertaker's Secretary disappeared in a flash of light, an alert window popped up.

,The conditions are met.」

,Acquires Undead Modification (C).」

,The rank of Undead Modification increases due to the influence of Black Magic Aptitude (SSS).」

,Acquires Undead Modification (SS).」

,Undead Modification

Allows you to modify undead.

Type: Skill

Rank: SS」

,Black Magic Aptitude. What an awesome skill.'

Black Magic Aptitude was a skill that turned me, who knew nothing about black magic, into the best black magician.

,I was originally going to get Chimera Creation.'

There are many types of black magic. Among them, chimeras are also included in black magic, but chimeras are black magic that creates living creatures, not corpses.

,Modifying living things into chimeras is a hassle. Undead Modification is several times easier.'

This time, I put on the skull necklace.

,Cursed Skull Necklace

Increases control over the undead.

Increases the stats of controlled undead by 3%.

Slightly increases the power of black magic.

Rank: A」

This could be called a must-have item for a necromancer.

,Leaving aside the A rank, the effect is good. It must be quite expensive. The support is definitely solid.'

I was satisfied.

,Shall I try using Undead Modification?'

You have to use the new skill you just got right away.

I flicked my finger. A ghoul that was nearby crawled forward. It was a pitiful ghoul with only half of its skull. Of course, what's important isn't the ghoul's skull.

,Since he's going to be used as a meat shield anyway... it's better to increase his size.'

,Uses Undead Modification (SS).」

,Simple modifications can be done without materials.」

This is because the rank of Undead Modification is high. If the rank was low, materials would have been absolutely necessary.

The ghoul's size increases by about 20%. Then, I punched the ghoul's body with my fist. The modification had an effect. It wasn't as hard as steel, but it became quite solid.

,I need materials to modify it further. The materials are...'

I selected another ghoul next to it. When I used Undead Modification (SS), the ghoul that became the material was instantly dismantled. I plastered the dismantled corpse all over the modified ghoul's body. The corpse was absorbed into the modified ghoul. The modified ghoul grew larger and larger, reaching 2.5M.

,So that's how it works. Keukeu. I got my hands on a fun skill.'
