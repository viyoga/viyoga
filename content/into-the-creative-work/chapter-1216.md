"...This is how we live. We are satisfied with our current lives!"

"Deputy Head of the family. That's just what you think. Look around you."

The Deputy Head of the family slowly looks around.

There weren't many members of the sect who rebelled against me and got excited like the Deputy Head of the family. At most, there were only four or five. The rest avoided the Deputy Head of the family's gaze and lowered their eyes.

The Black Road Sect.

It's called a sect, but in reality, it's closer to a gang. Those with some skill and a position can live well. They can live like the Deputy Head of the family, living it up. But the lives of ordinary sect members weren't so good.

They don't learn proper martial arts, and if a war breaks out with another sect, they're the first to die. They're not respected by the people around them either. Even if they somehow survive, what happens after that?

The Black Road Sect is basically a place where guys who use force gather. When they get old, they have to leave. There's no point in clinging on; they'll just die.

Savings? They spend all the money they earn on booze or prostitutes, so there's no way they'd have any.

They can do farm work? Farming is for experienced people. There's no way guys who have never farmed before can do it. Even if they try, most of them quit because it's hard and boring.

"I'll divide all the remaining assets of the Doyang Sect among you. It's not a small amount of money. You can live for five years without working. I don't care if you use that money to enter a martial arts school and learn martial arts, buy land and farm, or start a store."

The sect members looked tempted. They hadn't easily handled that much money before.

However, the Deputy Head of the family was different.

"Don't try to shake our loyalty with money! The Doyang Sect is our history!"

"Loyalty. Yeah. Loyalty is always the problem."

I grabbed the Deputy Head of the family by the scruff of his neck. I kicked his calf to break it, and I slammed my fist down on his shoulder to shatter it.

"Kkeuaaaaaak! H-Head of the family-nim!!"

"Deputy Head of the family. From now on, I'll test that precious loyalty of yours. If the loyalty you speak of truly exists, I'll hand over the position of Head of the family to you."

"Huk, huff, m-my leg...!"

"Don't whine just because your leg is broken. You were so annoying I almost killed you."

"......"

The Deputy Head of the family, sensing the murderous intent in my voice, shut his mouth. His terrified eyes were pitiful.

"As you can see, the Deputy Head of the family is in my hands. I plan to kill the Deputy Head of the family. If you want to save the Deputy Head of the family, come forward. But only those who are prepared to die in place of the Deputy Head of the family. If you show me that precious loyalty, I'll spare the Deputy Head of the family."

I looked around with fierce eyes. The sect members were busy avoiding my gaze. Even those who usually spent time with the Deputy Head of the family and acted as his henchmen were as quiet as mice.

"Deputy Head of the family. It seems the loyalty you speak of isn't here."

"H-Head of the family-nim. I was wrong...! I was out of my mind for a moment. I will follow the Head of the family-nim's will. P-Please give me one more chance...!"

"You already had your chance."

I clenched my fist. The Deputy Head of the family was close to an ordinary person, so there was no need to draw my sword. This guy had risen to the position of Deputy Head of the family with only politics and his knack for business, without any martial arts. The Doyang Sect was able to handle money to some extent thanks to the Deputy Head of the family's brain.

"I can't stand to watch this."

Eun Taemang, the former Head of the family of the Doyang Sect, stepped forward from among the sect members. He was armed with a huge executioner's blade and black armor.

"The neither-man-nor-woman should stay still as you have been."

"I can't do that. I need the Deputy Head of the family, even if no one else does. If we have the Deputy Head of the family's brain... the Doyang Sect can overcome this crisis and rise to even greater heights."

"You don't understand what I'm saying. As of today, the Doyang Sect is disbanded."

"Hahaha! Do you think the world will be as you wish!"

"......"

I ignored Eun Taemang and was about to swing my fist. I was planning to kill the Deputy Head of the family first and then kill Eun Taemang.

There was interference. Eun Taemang rushed over and swung his executioner's blade at me. I took a step back and pushed the Deputy Head of the family forward.

"Heoeok!"

The huge executioner's blade stopped right in front of the Deputy Head of the family's nose. The Deputy Head of the family was terrified and trembled.

Tatatat!

Men wearing masks and holding swords in their hands ran in from outside the training hall and surrounded me. Judging by their orderly movements, they weren't just any Tom, Dick, or Harry. They looked like they had learned martial arts and received training.

'Obviously. It must be the Baeksan Geum family.'

Eun Taemang had asked the Baeksan Geum family for help. The Baeksan Geum family had already lost influence in Baeksan Fortress due to the last Baeksan War. Currently, the Baeksan Geum family is in a situation where even the small power of the Doyang Sect is valuable, so they are helping Eun Taemang.

"Hahaha. The tables have turned! Put down the Deputy Head of the family when I'm asking nicely! And hand over the Oindan to me! Then I'll kill you neatly without torture!"

"You're being a bit too much."

I drew my sword from my waist and cut off the Deputy Head of the family's head in one swift motion. The Deputy Head of the family's head floated into the air.

Eun Taemang's face contorted.

"You goddamn bastard! Kill him immediately!!"

The guys who surrounded me rushed at me all at once. I put strength into my abdomen and knees and jumped into the sky. The power of my internal energy was tremendous. I jumped to a height of 5m in an instant.

Eun Taemang gnashed his teeth and glared at me. He was planning to attack as soon as I came down.

I released internal energy towards the soles of my feet. The Yongcheon blood point on the soles of my feet seemed to have burst due to the simple and crude use of internal energy, but I succeeded in changing direction in mid-air and rushing towards Eun Taemang.

Eun Taemang raised his executioner's blade and took a stance to counterattack. He was slow. And somehow clumsy.

'This guy. The wound he suffered during the sparring match hasn't healed yet.'

I used Challa the moment Eun Taemang swung his executioner's blade. Through the slowed-down vision, Eun Taemang's movements were clearly visible. My sword and Eun Taemang's executioner's blade collided. It was disadvantageous for me. Unlike Eun Taemang, my feet hadn't touched the ground yet.

But right now, I have completely grasped Eun Taemang. If I know it, I can use it.

I use Eun Taemang's strength to my advantage. I use the impact from his executioner's blade to spin my body and use Challa to adjust the detailed movements of my sword.

Chamgwidobeop (Slaying Ghost Sword Method) Nachal Hoeseom (Rakshasa Rotary Flash).

The sword energy carried on the sword rotated and shredded Eun Taemang's body. The reason his body wasn't torn to pieces was thanks to the black armor he was wearing. But it couldn't prevent his skin from being cut. His body was instantly covered in blood.

"......!!"

Instead of screaming, Eun Taemang chose to counterattack. I had to acknowledge his mental strength. But the counterattack was meaningless from the moment it was seen through by me.

I narrowly avoided Eun Taemang's executioner's blade, landed on the ground, and immediately jumped up again. I cut the guy's chin with the tip of my sword.

A body that jumps upwards is bound to fall down due to gravity. As I descended, I cut more than half of Eun Taemang's neck. The match was decided. Eun Taemang staggered backwards and fell straight back.

"Damn... it..."

Next, I dealt with the rest that Eun Taemang had brought. These guys were weaker than Eun Taemang. It was a bit annoying because they were clustered together, but there was no problem dealing with them alone.

Ten minutes passed and the battle was over.

My surroundings were full of corpses. I didn't bother taking off the masks of the dead guys and checking their identities. Their identities were obviously martial artists from the Baeksan Geum family, and I didn't plan to fight the Baeksan Geum family now.

'It's a bit frustrating, but if I barge into the Baeksan Geum family and cause a ruckus now, my plan will fail.'

I walked past the corpses towards the sect members who were still standing in the training hall. Their eyes were filled with awe as they looked at me.

"I'll say it again. As of today, the Doyang Sect is disbanded. Anyone who is dissatisfied, come forward."

"We will follow the Head of the family-nim's will!"

"Good. I'll divide the money as I said. Take it and get lost."

After dividing the money among the sect members, I left the Doyang Sect without any regrets. The place I was heading to wasn't the lodging, but the gates of Baeksan Fortress. If I stayed in Baeksan Fortress, I could be pursued by the Baeksan Geum family. For now, I was planning to leave Baeksan Fortress publicly and sneak into the lodging at night.

"......"

Jeok jeok.

As I was walking down the road, I turned around.

Six members of the Doyang Sect were following me. Even now that I had left Baeksan Fortress, they didn't seem to want to leave.

"What are you doing?"

The six men immediately knelt down in front of me. And they bowed their heads so deeply that their foreheads touched the ground.

"Head of the family-nim! Please take us in! We want to be with the Head of the family-nim! We swear eternal loyalty! Please take us in!"

It was obvious what their purpose was.

They wanted to learn my martial arts. To these guys, I was also a stroke of good luck, so they wouldn't want to miss it.

'This is fucking annoying.'

Annoyance surged.

Should I just kill them all? I hesitated for a moment. I had spared them with difficulty, so it was a bit much to kill them again. I had shown mercy once, so there was no reason I couldn't show it twice.

"The Doyang Sect is disbanded. I'm not the Head of the family of the Doyang Sect. You guys aren't affiliated with the Doyang Sect either. Get lost. You guys should find your own way to survive."

"Head of the family-nim...! No, Great Hero Han Su-pyeong! We'll do anything you tell us to do! We'll even return the money we received! Please take us in!"

Kung. Kung kung kung.

The guys slammed their heads on the ground. Their foreheads were torn and bleeding. They seemed to be emphasizing that they were sincere, but I didn't feel any emotion.

"Six is too many."

"......Yes?"

"Two seems like the right number."

With those words, I turned around and walked away again.

It was when I took my seventh step. I heard screams from behind. Soon, I heard shouts mixed with curses and the sound of swords clashing.

Ninety-fifth step. I felt someone behind me. When I turned around, two men covered in blood collapsed to their knees.

"Great Hero!"

"Please accept us!"

"This is really fucking annoying."

"...Great Hero. We can do anything."

"If you say you only need one person instead of two...."

The guys glanced at each other with murderous intent. They were tense, as if they would kill each other at any moment if my command was given.

It wasn't even funny.

"Ha, you fucking bastards. Please, for the love of God, know your place."

Before the guys could open their mouths, the sword drawn from my waist cut off their necks. Two heads rolled on the dirt floor.

"Seriously, you guys made me swing my sword unnecessarily."
