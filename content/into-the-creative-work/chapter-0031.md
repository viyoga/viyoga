,Noona. You said you'd support me, right? How exactly are you going to support me?",

If possible, I'd like you to support me with that amazing body of yours. I swallowed the rest of the words down.

,Wait a sec."

Contrary to my wishes, Jin Se-yeong went out of the training room and came back in about a minute.

Jin Se-yeong approached me, holding a long box in both hands.

,There are five types of support. One is free membership. Second is the provision of a personal training room. Third is personal training... in other words, individual guidance. I promise to teach you with all my heart. Fourth is practical support. Fifth is weapon support. I'll give you the knife I'm holding. How about it?"

From my perspective, these are the best possible conditions.

I'm not planning to join a guild right now, so that's beside the point. My goal is to get stronger, not to join a guild and work like a dog.

,Okay. But what's the knife you're giving me?"

My gaze went to the long box she was holding. The knife must be in there.

Jin Se-yeong carefully placed the box on the floor, took out a key, and opened the lock.

Inside the box was a reddish knife wrapped in a luxurious cloth.

The tip of the knife was beveled, there was no handguard, and the handle was a luxurious black, unlike the reddish blade.

There was a stick-like black sheath next to the knife, but my eyes were only on the red knife.

I stared blankly at the knife. Although I don't have an eye for weapons, the atmosphere tells me that this knife is not ordinary.

The iron sword I have is nothing more than trash compared to this knife.

The knife's name is Hwaryeon Bido (火鍊悲刀), and the type is Changpo Sword (菖蒲劒).

,Changpo Sword? It's a sword?"

In our Yeongcheon Style, there's no real distinction between a sword and a knife. In Yeongcheon Style, a sword generally means a long blade. We don’t worry about separating swords from knives. And because of the nature of Yeongcheon Style, we prefer daggers or light swords. This is because Yeongcheon Style’s practical techniques focus on quick kills rather than intense fighting. In this regard, the Changpo Sword is easy to handle.

,Ah."

Come to think of it, the basics of Yeongcheon Style were movements based on a one-handed sword. There are also movements that involve gripping and swinging the sword in reverse. It's definitely not a swordsmanship that involves facing each other head-on.

To freely wield the knife according to Yeongcheon Style's defense, it's better to have no handguard.

The Yeongcheon Style sword, which emphasizes speed (快) and stealth (隱), should be light, and large swords are more of a hindrance.

,If you don't like this, I'll prepare something else."

,No. No. No! That's not true! I really like it!"

,Really? That's a relief."

I slyly reached out and ran my finger along the blade. Since it's a reddish blade, I thought it might be hot, but it's very cool.

Hwaryeon Bido was created in Sijeokjang (詩的匠) in Gangwon-do.

Sijeokjang!

It's a famous smithy that I've heard of. Its skills are among the best in Korea and are known overseas.

,The blade is made of Black Iron and a Red Dragon's scale, and the handle is made by processing a Red Dragon's bone."

,Heok. Red Dragon!"

It's an SS-rank monster that turned Korea upside down 10 years ago. For reference, an SS-rank monster means it's unmeasurable.

Fortunately, all 10 major guilds cooperated to hunt the Red Dragon before it came out of the dungeon.

Don't be so surprised. This is a failure. That's why the name includes Bido (悲刀). Originally, they planned to make a knife that could control flames, but it seems they failed. Hwaryeon Bido is durable, and aside from the fact that the blade doesn't get damaged even without maintenance, it's just a normal knife.

,Th, that alone is amazing! How much would this sell for?"

,Not much. About 300 million? If you only look at the performance, 100 million is expensive, but the price went up because the material is a Red Dragon's scale."

,You're really... giving this to me?"

Jin Se-yeong nodded without hesitation.

,Yujin-ah. If you keep the conditions, that is."

I looked her straight in the eyes and said seriously.

,Noona. Hurry up and bring the contract. I'm in a hurry."

I was completely captivated by Hwaryeon Bido.

•••

Practical Combat.

This is the biggest concern for novice Hunters.

Hunters enter dungeons and hunt monsters. The problem is that dungeons are different spaces that are isolated from Earth except for the entrance. That's why crimes frequently occur within dungeons.

This is also why parties hastily formed through the Association try not to go to high-level dungeons, and the biggest reason why guilds were created is because of Hunter crimes.

Unreliable colleagues. Colleagues who stab you in the back. Colleagues who turn into robbers.

Novice Hunters with no connections or backing can only join parties hastily formed through the Association, so they hesitate to engage in practical combat.

This was a concern I also had. But now it's different. Because next to me is Jin Se-yeong, a reliable colleague and bodyguard.

Jin Se-yeong is a B-rank Hunter, and the dungeon we've entered now is an F-rank 'Ratman's Residence'. Even if the Hunters hunting nearby become robbers and attack us, Jin Se-yeong can take care of them in less than 3 minutes.

,It's your first practical combat, but don't worry. You can catch a Ratman without any problems with your skills."

Jin Se-yeong, wearing easy-to-move leather armor, said.

,I, if Noona says so, then that must be the case."

I answered, gripping the handle of Hwaryeon Bido at my waist.

I'm also wearing leather armor like her. However, I wore protectors on my shoulders and chest. Originally, I wanted to protect myself with heavy armor, but due to the nature of Yeongcheon Style, movement is important, so I had no choice but to choose light armor.

I didn't buy the armor, I borrowed it from Jin Se-yeong for a while.

,Ugh... I'm really nervous as hell."

It's not my first time entering a dungeon. I've entered a dungeon for the reason of practice and faced a goblin 1-on-1.

However, it's different then and now. Back then, there were many people around me, and goblins are the weakest monsters that even ordinary people can deal with if they're given a weapon.

I called it practical combat with bravado, but in reality, it was just practice. The real first practical combat is this moment.

,You're too nervous. Take a deep breath."

,Hoo~ha. Hoo~ha."

While taking a deep breath, I touched my smartphone instead of the knife handle. Touching my smartphone is much more reassuring than touching the knife handle.

After relieving some of my tension, I once again reached for Hwaryeon Bido at my waist.

,It reminds me of the old days. I was very nervous at first too."

,How did Noona relieve your tension?"

,I relieved some of my tension after one battle. You'll probably be the same. After doing it a few times, you'll realize that being a Hunter isn't a big deal."

The F-rank dungeon 'Ratman's Residence' is a wetland dungeon. The floor is muddy and there are lush trees and swamps everywhere.

The Ratmen that appear here are bipedal rats that freely roam the wetlands. They are 150cm tall and attack with their claws and teeth. The by-products are worth an average of 10,000 won per nail, and the magic stone is worth about 80,000 won.

We walked into the dungeon while talking.

If we saw someone else, we avoided them. Because it was an open-type dungeon that anyone could enter, we had to be aware of the location of other Hunters.

,Yujin-ah. Look over there. It's a Ratman."

I turned my head to where Jin Se-yeong was pointing. There was a black-haired Ratman about 30M away.

I could tell even from a distance. It was much bigger than a goblin. It seems to be true that three goblins can't beat one Ratman.

,I'll show you first this time too."

Jin Se-yeong walked towards the Ratman. The Ratman saw Jin Se-yeong, raised its fur, and took a fighting stance with its claws out. Most monsters try to kill people when they see them. No matter how different the skill level is, there were hardly any monsters that ran away.

,Kiyaaaaak!"

Jin Se-yeong didn't stop walking even with the Ratman's charge, and swung her knife as if passing by. The Ratman's head flew into the air and fell to the ground with red blood.

At first glance, it seems like the Ratman just came and died.

That's Yeongcheon Style's practical technique, Byeokgye (碧溪). I'm not at that level. As expected, Noona's practical techniques are incredibly skilled.

From a distance, it looks like she just passed by and cut it, but from the Ratman's perspective, Jin Se-yeong would have appeared to be far away.

The practical technique Byeokgye is a technique that blurs the opponent's sense of distance with its unique footwork.

,Magic stones are mostly in the heart area of monsters. If not the heart, they're often in the head."

Jin Se-yeong diligently packed the magic stone and claws into the bag at her waist.

What you can get from one Ratman is a magic stone and 6 claws. That's 140,000 won.

With a simple calculation, if you catch 10, you get 1.4 million won, but you have to pay 30% in taxes. If you calculate the equipment maintenance fee and dungeon entrance fee, the actual money you can use freely is about 500,000 won.

,From a Hunter's perspective, the Hunter Association and the country that take taxes are bound to be hated."

Most Hunters act in parties. The average party size is 5 people. If you catch 30 Ratmen a day, you'll earn about 300,000 won.

,This is also a high income. If you do it for ten days, you'll earn 3 million won. And if your skills increase and you catch higher-ranked monsters, earning 1 million won a day is no big deal."

It wasn't for nothing that Hunters were admired by ordinary people. But for me, who has the Amusement Life App, it's just a ridiculous amount.

If I set my mind to it and properly use the Amusement Life App to earn money, I can easily earn about 100 million won.

,Yujin-ah. There's a Ratman over there. You hunt it this time."

,...Okay."

I drew my sword and approached the Ratman. My body trembled slightly with tension. I've killed people in the game world, but this is clearly reality.

,Kiyaaaaak!"

The Ratman screamed at me and started running. I recalled Jin Se-yeong's movements that I had seen 10 minutes ago and used Byeokgye.

As I stepped on the unique footwork, the Ratman's eyes shook and looked behind me. Success. The Ratman's sense of distance was blurred, and it would think I was a few meters behind.

,Compared to Se-yeong Noona, it's a bit of a close call."

Still, I succeeded.

I passed by the Ratman and swung my knife. I accurately cut off the Ratman's neck. My strength is lacking compared to ordinary Hunters, but the sharpness of the knife covered that.

,N, not a big deal."

I muttered while looking at the Ratman's corpse. It wasn't bravado, it was genuine. The reason my words trembled was because of the excitement of succeeding in Yeongcheon Style's practical technique in real combat.

,See? It's not a big deal, right?"

,Yeah. I think I can easily kill Ratmen from now on. Noona. How far do you think I can go with my skills?"

,You can hunt lower-level E-rank monsters. But anything more than that is a bit difficult. Your Yeongcheon Style skills are certain, but your physical abilities are the problem."

Physical abilities.

Strength. Stamina. Agility is inferior to other Hunters.

I was aware of the problem myself, so I nodded readily. And her words mean that if my physical abilities are sufficient, I can easily hunt E-rank monsters.

I took the magic stone and claws from the Ratman's corpse and said to her.

,Okay. Let's keep hunting. Noona. I've got a good grasp of it."

,Okay. Since we came all the way here, we have to get our money's worth."

As soon as I saw a Ratman, I approached and killed it. I could kill it with just the basics without having to use Yeongcheon Style's practical techniques.

5 hours.

That's how long it took me to kill 24 Ratmen.

I killed almost 5 per hour, but it took much longer to find the Ratmen than the battle time.

,Only 24...."

,It can't be helped. There are many Hunters competing in open-type dungeons. What you should be careful about in open-type dungeons is not stealing monsters. 80% of the Hunter disputes that occur in dungeons are due to monster ownership."

,I've heard that too. But closed-type dungeons are a bit dangerous... Huh? Why did you stop suddenly?"

,Shh. Someone is ambushing ahead."

We were on our way back after finishing hunting. That means we have a lot of valuable by-products.

,...Bandits?"

Hunter Bandits.

As the name suggests, they are bad guys who rob Hunters. I slyly peeked out and looked at them. There were a total of 3 of them, leaning against a large tree and talking.

,Probably. We can just go around, but... then other people will get hurt. I have to take care of them."

Jin Se-yeong stepped forward, and I followed behind her.

I wasn't too worried.

This is an F-rank dungeon, and she's a B-rank Hunter. The level of the guys who are robbing here was obvious without even looking.
