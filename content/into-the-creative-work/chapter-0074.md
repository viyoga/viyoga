A quiet cafe with no customers.

I was sitting across from a man with a table between us. The man in casual clothes looked to be in his early to mid-40s.

"Really. Are you really giving me 5 million won?"

The man asked repeatedly as if he couldn't believe it.

"Why would I lie?"

I nodded and placed a white envelope of money on the table. It contained 100 50,000 won bills.

At first, the man hesitated slightly, then quickly snatched the envelope and opened the entrance to check the money.

"......It's real. I can't understand it no matter how much I think about it. Why would you want to buy something like this? Are you an employee of another broadcasting station? ...No. But it still doesn't make sense."

"Haha. I told you in advance. I'm a fan. But where's the item?"

"It's here."

He handed over a USB. I plugged the USB into the laptop in front of me.

"Thank you. PD Shin-nim."

The person sitting in front of me is a drama PD from a broadcasting station.

His name is Shin Juhong. He is the PD who produced the drama Vampire Detective.

In fact, I contacted Shin Juhong and the drama writer of Vampire Detective.

The drama writer said he couldn't come because he was busy. Well, I think he suspects me. After all, I'm someone who's giving 5 million won for the setting book of a drama that's already over. It's bound to be suspicious.

Fortunately, Shin Juhong was willing to trade.

"...You're really not going to release it on the internet, are you?"

PD Shin Juhong asked.

"No. I told you, I'm a fan? I like Vampire Detective. I wanted to keep the setting book for personal collection. If you're still worried, I'll write a contract."

"No. A contract isn't necessary. But are you really spending 5 million won on just one setting book?"

"I'm a bit impulsive. I thought I could get it quickly with about 5 million won. Thanks to that, I got it in just 2 hours. And I'm a Hunter, so I have a lot of money."

I realized something while obtaining 4 Life Orbs this time.

It's the unknown settings.

I thought that only one Life Orb was formed through the drama. Because in the drama, Moon Ji-hyuk took it with only one Life Orb formed.

But in reality, 4 were formed.

'Baek Sanggi is the same. How would I have known he was a guy with that kind of skill.'

I clicked on the laptop and looked at the document contents.

I searched for Baek Sanggi in the character settings.

In the original story, Baek Sanggi killed vampires who came from abroad 300 years ago and gained immortality by eating the Life Orb. Baek Sanggi used to belong to Geomgye, a group of vampire hunters in Joseon, and learned the Sungrigeom swordsmanship.

"......What was Baek Sanggi's original role? Looking at the settings, it doesn't seem like he's just an extra."

PD Shin Juhong looked at me as if surprised by my question.

"Baek Sanggi was supposed to play the role of the protagonist's helper. But due to story problems...."

"It's not because the actor who played Baek Sanggi was shit at acting?"

"......This is a secret, but that's right. That friend acted okay during the audition... but when he went into practice, he wasn't good at all. He had more than 50 NGs, but he didn't seem to be getting better. Ugh. Just thinking about that time...."

"Was Baek Sanggi originally going to commit suicide?"

"Yes. He was feeling weary of life. Originally, he was going to fight dozens of vampires belonging to Kantra at the end and die. The role was already decided, and the budget was tight... so I just changed some of the story."

I nodded. I heard that dramas actually change the content according to the viewers' reactions. If the viewers' reaction is good, a character who is scheduled to die may survive, and conversely, if the reaction is not good, they may quickly drop out.

I scanned through the character settings.

There were quite a few. About 40 characters were written in detail. The rest have short, simple settings written briefly as they pass by.

I switched the document and looked at the story plot. There was a lot of it, so I had no choice but to skim through it.

"There are many stories that didn't appear in the drama."

"They were discarded. They are episodes that were judged to be uninteresting or impossible."

"The Kim Chunseok episode seems interesting?"

"How could a mere detective do anything to a member of the National Assembly who controls the power of Korea?"

"...That's true."

I looked at the discarded episodes. These didn't happen even in the 'Vampire Detective' Game World.

"As expected, Vampire Detective. The setting book is also interesting."

"Thank you for enjoying our drama."

PD Shin Juhong looked at me with friendly eyes. Even though he's a bit suspicious, he's a fan who buys setting books using his own money. He can't hate it.
