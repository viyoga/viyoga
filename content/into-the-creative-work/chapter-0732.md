I hate that demon bastard. I hate things related to that demon bastard.

I agree.

The two took combat stances. Kim Cheon-woo diagonally pulled the greatsword back, and Yoo Seung-joon lowered his stance and gripped the spear shaft. It was like the posture of a beast waiting for an opportunity.

,Originally, I was going to hide it as much as possible....'

『1』

,Holy Sword Manifestation.'

Kim Cheon-woo used one of the abilities of his trait, Holy War (S).

Uuung.

The greatsword vibrated subtly. He used it as weakly as possible so that no one could notice from the outside.

Umjjeul.

Yoo Seung-joon trembled. He frowned, not guessing why he was trembling.

Kim Cheon-woo saw that clearly.

,...No. It might not be a reaction to the Holy Sword.'

Yoo Seung-joon, holding the spear, rushes forward. His agility reminded him of a weasel. Kim Cheon-woo maintained his composure as much as possible and swung the greatsword at Yoo Seung-joon. If he wasn't calm, it felt like something inside him would escape out of his body.

The spear and greatsword collided, creating a shockwave. The spear aimed for any opening in the greatsword, and the greatsword blocked the spear at all costs.

"To block all of my spear attacks with that heavy greatsword.... Indeed, you're not the vice-chairman for nothing."

"Yoo Seung-joon.... Sorry, I think I need to end this sparring as quickly as possible."

Kim Cheon-woo said with difficulty. Every time he blocked Yoo Seung-joon's attack, something kept rising up. His vision narrowed, and his body tensed up more than necessary. He wanted to end the sparring as quickly as possible because it felt like something real was about to happen.

"Do you think you can end it just because you want to? What a joke. Seong Yujin too, and you too, I don't like any of you."

"......."

"It's a shame this is inside the simulator. If it weren't for the simulator, I would have made you a cripple and made you realize your place."

Yoo Seung-joon held the spear in one hand and straightened his back.

He used his trait, Tengu's Mask (A).

A mask appeared in the air and covered Yoo Seung-joon's face. It was a Tengu mask with a long nose, wrinkled and angry red face, and thick eyebrows.

Kim Cheon-woo twisted his body to the side and raised the greatsword forward.

Kwaang!

The spear collides with the greatsword. Kim Cheon-woo's body was pushed back three steps.

Yoo Seung-joon's stats, wearing the Tengu's Mask, increased overall. Kim Cheon-woo was also briefly surprised. But just because his physical abilities increased didn't mean Yoo Seung-joon's spearmanship skills changed.

In his opinion, Yoo Seung-joon's spearmanship was mediocre. It was fast and flashy, but straightforward. It was not at a level to be wary of, as it was easily visible.

"You goddamn bastard! How long are you going to just defend!"

Yoo Seung-joon shouted. Instantly, his breathing faltered, revealing an opening. Finally, Kim Cheon-woo's greatsword counterattacked.

Surprised, Yoo Seung-joon created wind to push the greatsword away. The greatsword narrowly grazed his right shoulder.

"Keuk?! Kuaaaaak!"

Yoo Seung-joon screamed and retreated. To others, it looked like an exaggeration. He was cut by the greatsword, but it didn't hinder his combat, and only a few drops of blood flowed out. Furthermore, since the Aurora Simulator was running, there were no actual injuries on his arm.

Kim Cheon-woo's eyes coldly sank. The Holy Sword is extremely compatible with demons or Demons. Even a small cut would cause such agony and frenzy. In other words, it was proven that Yoo Seung-joon is a Demon.

"Th, thiii, thiiiiik...!"

Kim Cheon-woo gritted his teeth. His body trembled. This was the first time he had directly encountered a Demon since he was 5 years old.

I want to kill him. I want to kill him. I want to tear Yoo Seung-joon's body to pieces and kill him!

Old memories come to mind. His parents murdered by demons. Himself trembling with his eyes closed in fear and helplessness.

"I'll kill you!"

As if blowing away his memories, Kim Cheon-woo burst out all the mana he had and rushed towards Yoo Seung-joon. His large greatsword moved lightly like a rapier, attacking Yoo Seung-joon.

Yoo Seung-joon tried to push away the greatsword by extending his spear. But the spear flew far away and fell to the floor due to the greatsword's strange movements. Yoo Seung-joon, defenseless, tried to retreat, but Kim Cheon-woo didn't let him. The greatsword mercilessly butchered Yoo Seung-joon.

"Keok, kuaaak! Kuaaaaaak!"

A desperate scream echoed.

Kim Cheon-woo will go berserk as much as he wants for 3 minutes, and Yoo Seung-joon will taste hell.
