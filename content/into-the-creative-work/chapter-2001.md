First, there's Hades nearby. I saw his location with the Eye of the Sky, so I immediately moved to where Hades was.

Hades, along with his Underworld gods, was killing humans. He turned the dead humans into undead, creating an army of death. I was impressed by his hungry spirit, trying to squeeze every last drop out of the remaining humans.

"You're working hard. I feel sorry for you, so I'll listen to your last words. Spit them out."

"Zeus...!"

Hades gritted his teeth as soon as he saw me. Hades' insignificant underlings rush at me. They were such pathetic beings that even dealing with them was an insult and a mockery.

"Ahem."

Just a light cough!

The dead around me were swept away and annihilated.

"......!!"

Hades' eyes bulged out as if they would pop. Then, his face twisted miserably.

"Zeus, you crazy bastard! What the hell have you been doing in the meantime?!"

He seems to have noticed that my power has increased absurdly.

"Do I look so free that I have to explain everything to the likes of you?"

I raised my right hand. Lightning gathered. Each strand had the power to tear apart gods.

"......I thought there was hope. But it was all my delusion. Zeus. I will ask you for mercy one last time."

"How can you be so shameless? I already showed mercy before. And you're asking for mercy again? Do I look like a pushover to you?"

"I'm not asking you to spare my life. You can do whatever you want with me. But give the other gods a chance. You'll need their help to maintain order in the Underworld... It wouldn't be a bad deal for you."

"I refuse. They've already rebelled twice. Do you think I'll let them live?"

"I forced them to do it!"

"So what?"

I sneered.

These guys have already committed two rebellions. If they did it twice, how hard would it be to do it a third time?

"I can just wipe them all out and create a new god to rule the Underworld."

"If that's how it is... I have no choice!!"

Hades screamed and took something out of his chest.

A book.

A very ominous grimoire made of what looked like human skin.

I immediately threw Astrape.

Kwaaaang!

A barrier appeared, protecting Hades and his gods from Astrape. Part of the grimoire burned away.

"Where did you get that damn grimoire... Fucking Hermes, probably."

I threw Astrape with all my might. The barrier didn't break. Instead, another part of the grimoire burned.

'I'll have to hammer at it for at least a minute to make the power of that grimoire disappear.'

And a minute was enough time for Hades and his gods to do something.

"I'm sorry, everyone. The thing I feared has actually happened. Zeus has already obtained power beyond that of a creator god. He has no intention of showing mercy. All we can do is give up everything or... give everything to land a blow on Zeus."

"Don't apologize, Hades-nim!"

"If we're going to die anyway, we'll land a blow on Zeus!"

"Death to Zeus! Destruction to Olympus!"

Hades and the gods hardened their resolve. Hades, as their representative, began to read the grimoire's evil verses.

"These crazy bastards! They're trying to offer themselves to an evil god?!"

Hades didn't respond to my words.

Tentacles sprouted from the grimoire, wrapping around the gods' necks and pulling them in. All the gods were dragged into the grimoire.

Clang!

The barrier belatedly shattered, and the grimoire burst into flames. I stomped on the burning grimoire with annoyance.

Unfortunately, I couldn't tell who was connected to this grimoire. Even though I had wisdom, I didn't know everything.

I glared at the grimoire that had turned to ashes and disappeared, then turned my head and glared at the sea. A giant fish-man rose above the sea. It was the monster Dagon, who had been crouching in the deep sea. Dagon was walking towards the space battleship, koong koong. A huge tsunami rose around him, starting to engulf the land.

Sea monsters were wriggling in the tsunami. Giant squids, giant sharks, Deep Ones, and other hideous monsters' shadows could be seen.

Dagon swung his arm at the space battleship. The heavy space battleship couldn't avoid it. The barrier protecting the space battleship was torn apart powerlessly.

The space battleship lost its balance and flew far away, crashing into the ground. More than half of the space battleship was destroyed.

'With that much impact... some of the gods will probably pass out.'

That alone wouldn't kill the immortal gods. Next to Dagon, Poseidon raised his trident. A huge tsunami was about to rise and engulf the space battleship.

'Strike it down.'

Tens of millions of lightning bolts flashed, striking and piercing the sea. The current stirred through the sea, killing all kinds of sea monsters and aquatic life. Countless species living in the sea were annihilated and disappeared.

"You bastard! Zeus! How can you call yourself the chief god of this world after doing that!!"

Poseidon shouted in anger.

"Creatures can be recreated. That applies to you too, Poseidon!"

I threw an Astrape with compressed power at Poseidon. This special lightning fell from the sky, split the sea, and grazed past Poseidon. Poseidon's body burst into flames.

Poseidon above the sea didn't fall. New flesh sprouted beneath his charred skin. Poseidon shook his body as if shedding his skin, shaking off the charred skin.

Poseidon's eyes changed to something similar to Dagon's.

"Did you sleep with a deep-sea monster or something?"

"I made a terrible contract to kill you."

"So you decided to become their minion after killing me."

"......"

Poseidon shut his mouth, as if that was the correct answer.

The end of a guy who craved power without a plan was funny. Even if he succeeded in the rebellion, he wouldn't be able to enjoy the glory. He had simply entrusted himself to hatred.

'Poseidon's power comes from Dagon. I should aim for Dagon, not Poseidon.'

There was no need to fly towards Dagon. Dagon was coming to kill me. The ground and sea shook with every step he took.

'Astrape.'

A huge lightning bolt fell from the dark clouds onto Dagon's head. Dagon's skull was shattered. Blood and brain matter scattered on the ground. His wound healed in less than 3 seconds.

I stared at Dagon with narrowed eyes.

'It's effective.'

I could clearly feel that his power had weakened from that attack.

'My power isn't infinite either, but my situation is better than his. I have Complete Recovery, after all.'

Using Complete Recovery would also restore the divine power I had consumed.

"You bastard, Zeus! Where are you looking! I'm your opponent!"

Poseidon started acting up. I didn't even look at him and swung my arm. A huge lightning bolt stretched out like a whip and struck Poseidon. Poseidon, who had fallen into the sea, stood up again. He pointed his trident at me.

A tsunami rose. The tsunami, over 1 km high, was compressed and took the shape of a horse. The horse galloped across the sea, charging at me.

'He's really being annoying.'

Even though Poseidon had become stronger, he was no match for me. I could kill him in 30 seconds if I wanted to.

I dropped lightning on the charging horse. The horse dodged hundreds of lightning bolts as it galloped. I had to admit that its speed in dodging the lightning was impressive.

The horse, which had come close, lowered its head and tried to ram me. Even though it looked small, what formed that body was a tsunami over 1 km high. The mass itself was threatening.

"Heup!"

I took a breath and punched the water. The horse's body burst and became a tsunami again. However, the direction was towards Dagon and Poseidon, not me. Dagon received the tsunami without any reaction. A 1 km tsunami wasn't even knee-high for him. Poseidon was swept away by the tsunami and disappeared.

"Zeus, was it? A great one is watching you. Do you know that?"

Dagon's voice echoed throughout the entire space.

"Shut up, you fish bastard. You're just big. I'll tear you apart and kill you right now."

"The ancient ones have awakened one by one, and all of them have willingly given their lives for the great ones. Everything is as the great one wishes!"

-Kkeuaaaaaaaaak

From far away, I could hear the scream of a tremendous being. The scream of a being with power that couldn't be determined to be superior even compared to Dagon had something maddening about it in itself.

"That's the scream of the burning one."

"...He's offering himself."

"That is the will of the great one. Even the Crawling Chaos cannot defy the will of the great one. You must revere and welcome him."

Kooong! Koong! Kooong!

Dagon continued to approach. I felt hostility from him.

"You don't follow that great one?"

"I made a contract to kill you. It's a contract with my name on it, so I have to keep it."

Dagon reaches out to me. The entire sea shakes and soars into the sky.

"How dare the sea look down on the sky."

Astrape.

Electricity flows through my body. I, who had become Astrape itself, ran towards Dagon. The sea that had risen into the air blocked my path, but it was not enough to stop me, who had become lightning. I pierced through his chest. I felt that I had crushed his source.

His body slumped, and the sea that had been floating in the sky fell powerlessly.

"How boring. Nyarlathotep didn't fight properly either... but that bastard felt more persistent."

"Destruction is already close at hand. Even if I die, that fact doesn't change...."

Dagon's source disappeared.

"Zeus!!!!"

With a cry of hatred, Poseidon, who had been hiding in the sea, stabbed at me with his trident. I already knew of Poseidon's existence, so I wasn't surprised at all.

Kwaang!

Lightning struck and split Poseidon's body in half. Poseidon's end was anticlimactic.

'Now there are no more rebels. All that's left is to clean up.'

「You have completed 99% of the myth.」

「Completing the myth will complete the trial.」

'It'll be over when I return the world to normal.'
