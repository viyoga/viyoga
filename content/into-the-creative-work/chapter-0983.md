,The fifth floor trial will begin in one shijin from now. The trial is survival. Surviving for ten days. It is impossible to give up midway through the fifth floor trial. Those who want to give up, give up within one shijin from now."

Here and there, those contemplating with serious faces spoke.

The Myriad Demon Tower opens every 10 years, and repeated participation is possible as long as one is not restricted by the age limit. The problem is that the content of the next trial also changes, so there is almost no advantage to having experienced the Myriad Demon Tower once.

,...I will give up. I felt the limit of my skills, and I felt the insignificance of my talent. I'm going back to my hometown to plow the fields."

,This Myriad Demon Tower seems more difficult than I heard. I'll aim for the next one."

,I'm injured. If a battle breaks out, won't I just die like a dog? I'll give up."

There were quite a few who gave up. In terms of numbers, about 5,000 people. The abandoning ronin left like a receding tide, but there were still many entrants remaining.

,The one shijin I mentioned has passed. I will begin the fifth floor trial. Survive for ten days, no matter what."

General Baeun disappeared without looking back.

Silence descended on the fifth floor.

,Yujin-ah. What should we do?"

Seong Jigone asked. I looked around once. The atmosphere was sharp. It was an unlit powder keg. Once it explodes, we might have to face hundreds, even thousands of people.

,Check the emergency rations and drinking water. Especially the drinking water. Other than that, just stay alert."

,Okay. Food won't be a problem. Because I have the food I originally had and the emergency rations I prepared."

After a while, Seong Jigone reappeared.

,There are enough emergency rations. However, I think there will be a shortage of drinking water."

,To what extent?"

,We can easily last a week."

,Okay. I'll take care of it."

,Yujin-ah. Do you want to eat this too? It's surprisingly edible."

Seong Jigone held up a round pill. Byeokgokdan. It's a famous emergency ration in this world. It's high in calories, but the taste isn't very good.

,No thanks."

I turned my head sharply and glared at a certain man. The man, who had been eavesdropping on our conversation, brazenly walked away. The man's attire was very shabby. Dirt was smeared on his exposed skin, and his hair looked like he hadn't washed it for at least a few months.

,There's no way an ordinary beggar bastard would be here. Is he from the Beggars' Sect?'

Beggars' Sect (Gai Bang).

A sect of beggars.

The Beggars' Sect is one of the Nine Great Sects that often appears in martial arts novels. The Nine Great Sects exist in this world as well, but their reputation is not as high as in other worlds.

,...Just because he's a beggar who has mastered martial arts doesn't necessarily mean he belongs to the Beggars' Sect.'

I stopped caring about the beggar. He wasn't the only one spying on us. We had already become a target of attention in this area.

A day passed. I realized that there were more people watching us.

,These bastards. Their food and drinking water must be starting to run out. It's still okay now, but if they're cornered, they might go crazy and attack. It would be better if there was a barrier... but there's only a prairie around here. The trees have been occupied by other guys....'

Another day passed. The expression of Jin Gueon, the leader of the Cheolodan, was very serious. He also felt this chilling atmosphere.

,Seong Yujin-nim. The atmosphere is unusual. These guys might... be planning to attack. We need to prepare."

,How do we prepare?"

I asked back.

,...."

Jin Gueon closed his mouth. The surroundings were an open prairie. There was no way to do anything.

,Just stand guard well."

,Yes."

Two more days passed. A change occurred on the fifth floor. A center of gravity was created among the entrants. People gathered and formed forces. The method of forming them involved food and drinking water. A temporary alliance. No, it's closer to employment.

,They're selling some of their food to hire people to protect their food.'

They're not fools either. They know that if they fight to take away food and get seriously injured, they'll only be at a loss. This is only the fifth floor of the Myriad Demon Tower. Their minimum goal is the seventh floor, where they can obtain magical equipment.

,Yujin-ah. Isn't this dangerous? Even if it's us, there's no way to stop them if they attack all at once."

Seong Jigone fiddled with his sword hilt. Hungry bastards around us were shining their eyes fiercely. It was a touch-and-go situation.

,Shall we step up now?"

I confidently walked forward. And I shouted loudly.

,I will now sell food and drinking water! Money is of course possible, but so is exchange with goods!"

The resounding voice was enough to attract people's attention. People approached us as if they had been waiting.

,You're selling food and drinking water? Do we need to pay to buy it? We can just kill you and take it."

,That's good timing. Let's end this with a conversation. Hand over the food for free."

,Do you think you can handle all of us with your numbers?"

The bastards were arrogant. They believed in their numbers. I smirked.

,This is another innovative suicide hopeful. Didn't you see it on the first day? You watched Noejeoldan or whatever get completely destroyed by me, right?"

I raised my palm. Pajijijijik. Blue lightning danced on my palm. An impossible feat for an ordinary martial artist. The bastards' eyes shook and tension arose.

,I'm confident I can kill you all. But I'm not doing it because there's no need to. If you're not going to buy food, get out of here right now."

I threw the lightning to the ground. The lightning became a thunderbolt and soared from the ground to the sky upside down. Fear settled on the faces of the bastards who saw the pillar of lightning.

,...I will buy food. How much does it cost?"

,It's not that expensive. I have a conscience, so I'll sell it at a reasonable price. Ah, don't be disappointed if you don't have money or goods. I'll exchange it for labor too."

,You're thinking of hiring us?"

,Don't you want to?"

,Of course we do. That's what we want too. However, the term is until the end of the fifth floor trial."

More than 800 people gathered in front of me. There were no buyers of food. Everyone wanted to be hired by me, and I hired everyone. When food and drinking water became scarce, I secretly took them out of my inventory.

The atmosphere was relieved.

Because they were full and not thirsty, their bodies became comfortable. The reason to see blood disappeared.

,Yujin-ah. Were you planning this from the beginning?"

,Yeah. The fifth floor trial is about survival, not competition. There's no need to fight with swords."

,I'm glad there's no more tiring work, but isn't 800 people too many? You could have hired half of that... no, even just 500 people."

,The more lackeys, the better. Do you know where power basically comes from? It basically comes from the number of people. And...."

,And?"

,General Baeun said to survive. If I'm right, something will happen. Otherwise, the trial of surviving for ten days is too boring."

,Well, even if you only reach the level of Ipsik, you won't die from starving for ten days. You'll get weaker, though."

The fifth day of the trial began. An anomaly occurred. Demon dogs appeared at the end of the prairie. Hundreds of demon dogs, the size of tigers with gray fur, rushed in with their red eyes shining.

,The Myriad Demon Tower deliberately released the demon dogs. This is what makes it a trial.'

The martial artists were surprised and drew their swords and began to fight the demon dogs.

,Demon dogs!!"

,Don't scatter! Fight together! Demon dogs are faster than us! We have to aim for a counterattack!"

,The demon dogs' weak point is their belly! Aim for their belly!"

Most of the people here are ronin. And there is no ronin who has been active as a ronin and has never faced a demon dog. That's how common demon dogs are as monsters.

However, the number of demon dogs was excessively large. Some martial artists died.

,Yujin-ah. Shouldn't we go help?"

,It's okay. They should be able to handle demon dogs on their own. That's why I gave them food and hired them."

,Ah, shit. My hands are itching...."

,If you're really bored, go. I won't stop you."

,...I'll be back in a minute."

Seong Jigone came up to the fifth floor and focused on absorbing the spirit herbs. It's human nature to want to check the results when they appear. Seong Jigone ran towards the demon dogs and swung his sword.

,Seong Jigone's level is Chulji. He's not fighting hundreds of them alone, so he shouldn't get seriously hurt.'

I quietly watched while eating cider.

After about an hour, the situation was resolved. The martial artists sat down in their places and caught their breath. Their faces were not bright.

,They know that this is not the end, but just the beginning.'

Half of the ten days. They have to endure the remaining half.

The next day, more demon dogs appeared. More than 20 martial artists died. The next day, shadow monsters were mixed among the demon dogs. More than 40 martial artists died. And the martial artists' complaints erupted.

,Hey! Why aren't you moving? Aren't you the strongest here? If you had stepped up, the number of deaths would have been greatly reduced!"

A bandit-like bastard came out and argued with me. He wasn't alone. A bunch of riffraff were gathered around him. He was pretending to be bold, but he was terrified.

,If he was a really bold bastard, he would have come to me alone.'

I got up from my chair. That alone made the bastards flinch.

,The reason I didn't step up? I hired you to prepare for this kind of troublesome thing. If you received food and drinking water, which are more precious than anything else here, work as much as you received before complaining."

,...We just want you to step up and help us a little."

,Ah, is that so? My answer is one. I don't want to."

,Is it okay to treat us like that? If we leave, won't you be in trouble?"

,Well. I don't think I'll be in trouble. If you want to leave, leave. But do you have a place to go? Do you have food?"

,...."

,I hired you fairly. Don't forget that fact. If you understand, go back to your seats and eat."

,That's the worst. Your name is Seong Yujin, right? You have power, but you have no chivalry. You will regret this later."

,What's your name?"

,...Great Axe Warm Knight (大斧溫俠) Ha Munsoyo. I'm from Henan.

Great Axe Warm Knight. I've never heard of that moniker before. Well, just because you have a moniker doesn't necessarily mean you're a great martial artist. If you're active in the Jianghu for a few years, you'll easily get a moniker. Having a moniker is separate from being famous. And most monikers tend to be exaggerated.

,You even have chivalry attached to your moniker? You're a very great guy. But you seem to have chivalry, but no power. Don't act up too much. I don't know how you've lived so far, but chivalry without power is just a bluff."

,Many people have said those words to me so far. But most of them didn't live long."

,You must have only met idiots."

,...I will follow you in this trial. Because it's true that I'm hired for now. But I won't do that on the next floor."

Ha Munso turned around. 200 martial artists moved behind him. It was quite a sight.

,Cool...!"

Seong Jigone muttered next to me.

,Yujin-ah. I think I've finally met a true chivalrous person!"

,What true chivalrous person? That's all politics."

,Still, it's a bit unexpected. Why didn't you kill Great Axe Warm Knight right away and let him live? We have the numerical advantage. 500 people are on our side."

Tsk.

I clicked my tongue and said quietly so that only Seong Jigone could hear.

,Jigone-ah. You're misunderstanding something. The only ones on our side here are you and me. Just the two of us. The other guys are hired with food. It wouldn't be strange if they turned around and pointed their swords at us right now."

,Oh.... I understand why you didn't kill Great Axe Warm Knight. But isn't Cheolodan on our side?"

,Those bastards are just guys we're using. They'll probably stab us in the back as soon as they get the chance, right? Hey, you haven't gotten attached to Cheolodan, have you?"

,No. I can kill Cheolodan at any time. I've lived with them for the past few days, so I know now. There are almost no guys you can trust among those who aren't family."

,That's good to know. We'll abandon Cheolodan around the 7th or 8th floor. Cheolodan is only at that level. Prepare for it until then."

Seong Jigone quietly stroked his sword hilt.
