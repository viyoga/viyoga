The gang tailing Eugene discovered the purple portal and briefly held their breath.

The foreign feeling coming from the portal was extraordinary. The guys here had raided dungeons a few times, so they were confident they were experts when it came to dungeons. Even so, this was their first time seeing a dungeon like this.

"It's definitely not an ordinary dungeon."

"How did that wizard know about a dungeon like this?"

"He's at least a 5th Circle wizard. There's no way a wizard like that would come here without any information."

They cautiously approached the dungeon portal.

"I can feel it now that I'm closer. The mana concentration is enormous."

"...Earlier, it looked like the dungeon was swallowing the wizard whole. Don't get too close rashly."

They investigated and talked about the dungeon portal, eventually reaching a conclusion.

"This is a special dungeon we've never seen before. The risk level goes without saying."

"Dungeons are high risk, high return. The more dangerous, the better."

"Wait a minute. Isn't it strange? That wizard went into this dungeon alone. If he knew about the dungeon, there's no way he'd dare to go in alone, right?"

"The dungeon's condition might be the number of people. There are dungeons that get more dangerous the more people there are, right?"

"So, what are we going to do? Wait here for the wizard to come out with the dungeon rewards and then ambush him? What if the wizard fails the dungeon raid and the dungeon disappears?"

Everyone's eyes turned to the leader. The leader stroked his chin, pondered, and then came to a decision.

"We're going in. The wizard went into the dungeon alone. That means it might be less dangerous than we think. And we'll sell the dungeon information to the Raven Knights and the mercenaries for the good of all. Even if the Raven Knights are hostile, the mercenaries allied with us will protect us."

"What if those bastards collude and betray us?"

"The chances of that are low, but... we'll have no choice then. We'll just have to die. There's always risk in whatever we do. You scared, or what?"

"Ha. Scared? I could go in right now."

They prepared to enter the dungeon, wary of the portal. Among them, some were busy passing information to the Red Felshion, an adjacent area and a hostile force to the Raven Knights.

Loyalty among comrades? They had some, but not enough to beat money. They weren't going to chase dungeons for the rest of their lives, so they had to gather as much money as possible while they could. Besides, it wasn't the first time they'd sold information. They were just acting as usual.

And about 10 minutes later. They entered the dungeon portal.

Entering the dungeon portal, I sensed the darkness and used magic.

[Light]

A sphere emitting white light appeared above my head, brightly illuminating the surroundings.

The floor, walls, and ceiling were blocked by crimson flesh walls. The humid heat and slight stench of decay emanating from the flesh walls. The purple energy flowing on the surface of the flesh walls. It wasn't a pleasant place. However, the mana concentration here was ridiculously high.

I wasn't surprised. I already knew the identity of this dungeon through the original game.

I checked my body.

Absolute Polymorph had been undone and I was back to my original face.

All magic was undone as I crossed the portal. Absolute Polymorph was no exception. Since I didn't need to worry about other people's eyes in this dungeon anyway, I didn't change my appearance.

"Oh. A new wizard has come. You poor thing."

I turned my head towards the voice. A man wearing leather rags and a wooden mask was in the passageway. He held a staff with a copper-colored skin and a bird's skull embedded in it. I felt the dark mana flowing from him.

I narrowed my eyes and looked at the man. He wasn't in the original game.

"A black wizard. Are you my enemy?"

"I am the black wizard Kukuru. I am your trial opponent, but not your enemy. Welcome to the body of the Vision Possessor, Belk. Wizard."

"You're not my enemy? How can I believe that? A failure eaten by Belk."

"You seem to know the identity of this dungeon before entering. Well, I knew before I came in too. I thought I'd be okay... The result of my recklessness is as you see. First, to answer your question, I am a 6th Circle necromancer."

"I see. So you're using necromancy to control the souls enslaved to Belk. That's why you're maintaining your sanity."

"Hooh. You're quick on the uptake. I like how fast the story progresses. This is inside Belk's body. If you die here, your soul will be eaten by Belk. You won't achieve Nirvana or annihilation, but will be enslaved and have your magic stolen. Now, do you understand what I want?"

"You're saying you'll cooperate with me in exchange for liberation?"

"Being trapped here is starting to push my sanity to its limits. You've fallen into a Transcender's trap, but fortunately, the Transcender is not in their right mind. I'll guide you. I'll help you get what you want. You have nothing to lose. Isn't that a pretty attractive deal?"

I calculated in my head.

Simply looking at it, I had nothing to lose. Even with my knowledge of the original game, reality and computer games are different, so his guidance would make the dungeon raid much easier.

'If his words are lies and he's looking for the best opportunity to attack... I still have the preemptive right. Necromancers aren't particularly fast or powerful in attack.'

On the other hand, lightning magic is the pinnacle of speed and power. The difficulty of the magic itself and the mana efficiency aren't great, though.

'As long as I have Chaltana and Memorize, and Meltis's Ring, I have the preemptive right.'

The blue ring on my left index finger, Meltis's Ring, is my hidden card.

A fraudulent artifact that can store any three spells and activate them unconditionally. It can also steal magic if the conditions are met.

The currently stored spells are Resurrection, Thunder Break, and Frost Horn.

If Kukuru shows signs of betrayal, I can attack and subdue him first. I have the preemptive right.

"I have one condition."

"Greedy, like a wizard. Tell me."

"The reason you're maintaining your body here is because you're a special wizard recognized by Belk. Hand over your Arcane magic to me."

"Arcane magic, huh. Do you even know what Arcane magic is?"

Arcane. It is known as magic passed down in secret. The world understands that it is called Arcane because it is so powerful and dangerous that it must be kept hidden.

But the reality is different. Most of the Arcane magic known to the world was fake, not real.

"Arcane. I know it means the power of the Void, Arcane."

Laughter was heard from beyond Kukuru's wooden mask.

"Some say that the origin of mana began in the Void. The Void is the purest power. Arcane. Hahaha. You know a part of the truth of the world."

Arcane is dangerous, so the world has translated Arcane (Arcane) as Arcane (秘傳). Most of the Arcane magic known to the world was just an illusion.

"Real Arcane magic uses Arcane. It's so similar to mana that even the wizard themselves has difficulty distinguishing between Arcane and mana. The reason I came here includes getting real Arcane magic."

The most mysterious and special power in this world. Arcane. I also know that the power of the Dark Moon is a corrupted form of Arcane.

"Haha. Good. I'll tell you. But just because I tell you about Arcane doesn't mean you can use it all. You may not have the talent. Give up cleanly then."

Kukuru reaches out into the air. Dark mana gathers in front of his hand, drawing a crimson magic circle. I focused all my attention on that magic circle.

"To be honest, I don't know the exact principle of Arcane magic. I can't even understand how this magic is established. I had several opportunities to learn other Arcane magic during my lifetime, but this is the only one I could learn."

Even as I listened to his words, I focused on the magic circle. There was a limit to deciphering the entire formula with just the magic circle. So what I'm focusing on is something else. Dark mana? No, the Arcane hidden in the center of it.

And I felt the Arcane. That power... could only be described as mysterious.

The necromancer chanted as if putting the finishing touch on a painting.

"Death Ray."

A crimson laser with a 1m diameter shot straight out from the magic circle, penetrating the flesh wall. The laser annihilated everything in its path, leaving not even dust behind, and continued to shoot out.

The path the Death Ray pierced through. It seems to be at least 30m.

"As you can see, it can get rid of just about anything. But don't get too caught up in the power. There have been times when the Death Ray has been blocked. Even if that's not the case, it can be affected by various things."

"I know that much."

The pierced flesh wall regenerated and filled in as if nothing had happened.

I learned the magic formula for Death Ray from Kukuru.

"An incomplete magic formula. Are you not teaching it properly?"

"That's all there is to the Death Ray magic formula. Try using it once. Then you'll know."

Half in doubt, I deployed the incomplete magic formula.

A crimson magic circle is drawn in the air. Mana flowed out normally instead of dark mana like Kukuru.

The incomplete magic formula began to complete itself. I realized that Arcane had intervened and chanted.

[Death Ray]

A crimson laser shoots straight ahead. The power seems to be adjustable depending on the amount of mana used. The amount of mana consumed is fundamentally large enough to be ignored.

'Considering the difficulty of the formula and the required magical sensitivity, it's a 6th Circle spell.'

I smiled contentedly, looking at the wall that had been blown away in front of me.

"The range is limited, but I like the power."

"...You mastered Death Ray in an instant? What kind of ridiculous talent is that...."

"Thanks to you, I learned something good. Let's move on to the next thing. But... can others besides me enter this dungeon?"

"Did you have companions? Then that's unfortunate. Those who are not qualified appear elsewhere. Most of them don't make it out alive."

"They're not my companions."

"Is that so? Then you don't need to worry about them. Focus on your own work. If you fail, I won't be liberated from this hellish place and will have to stay trapped here."
