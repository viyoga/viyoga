,The disgrace of the Namgung Clan! Die!!"

Namgung Tak's blade of betrayal aimed at my back.

I wasn't flustered in the slightest. I didn't even consider it betrayal. What betrayal when I never trusted Namgung Tak in the first place?

,I didn't expect the elite martial artist of the Namgung Clan to join hands with Salwang-ssi. And that guy calls me the disgrace of the Namgung Clan? What a joke.'

Shooting Star Sword.

Dozens of Shooting Star Swords appeared in the air, aiming for Namgung Tak. Namgung Tak, who was extending his sword to my back, gritted his teeth and advanced instead of retreating. I could feel his determination to kill me no matter what.

The Shooting Star Swords pummeled Namgung Tak's body. Because Namgung Tak deployed his protective aura, he couldn't be dealt a fatal blow. Still, he suffered minor injuries. To the point where it was slightly difficult to use martial arts. In exchange, Namgung Tak got the chance to stab me in the back.

,That's what he must have thought. Leap.'

I teleported in front of Salwang-ssi, simultaneously summoning the Hwaryeon Bido and wielding it.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

The red blade struck down with lightning speed. Salwang-ssi reacted with astonishment. He blocked the blade with his left wrist.

Kkang!

The sound of metal rings out.

,Was he wearing a wrist guard under his clothes? If it can block Lightning Flash, it's a magical artifact.'

However, I had no intention of missing this opportunity. I immediately stretched out my foot and kicked Salwang-ssi in the abdomen. Salwang-ssi fell backward and rolled on the floor. Just as I was about to finish him off, Cheongun Squad, including Namgung Tak, began to form a formation around me.

,This is the Limitless Sword Formation of the Great Namgung Clan! No matter who you are, you can't escape. So Cheonma! Don't tarnish the name of the Namgung Clan and just die here!"

,You're the one who should die. I'm actually grateful that you betrayed me first. This way, I can strut around even more when I return to the Namgung Clan. Heh heh."

,You son of a bitch!!"

Dozens of Cheongun Squad members began to attack me, moving as one. I tried to block the attacks with my sword and counterattack, but... I had to give up counterattacking and just block the attack from a swordsman who suddenly popped out from the side.

,They've properly learned the sword formation. Even if I try to attack with internal energy like a palm strike... those bastards are resonating their internal energy to create an invisible barrier.'

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Myriad Lightning (卍雷)

Lightning gathers and rotates around the floor where I'm standing.

,Discharge.'

The compressed Myriad Lightning is released, and lightning spreads in all directions. However, it couldn't penetrate the sword formation. The barrier they created blocked even the lightning and suppressed my energy. I wasn't particularly surprised.

,If it could be penetrated by mere lightning, it couldn't be called the best lightning of the Namgung Clan.'

This Limitless Sword Formation was designed to face formidable enemies that couldn't be dealt with alone, so even I couldn't easily destroy it.

,The Cheonma Heavenly Steps would be the best in this situation.'

If I used Cheonma Shingong now, it would collide with the Lightning Sky Style and cause serious internal injuries. Even if I had Complete Recovery, I had no intention of recklessly launching a suicide attack.

Salwang-ssi, who had gotten up at some point, attacks me from within the sword formation. It was a very cowardly attack, befitting an assassin.

,He doesn't know much about the sword formation, but he's still trying to exploit the gaps and attack. The reputation of being the best among assassins isn't a lie.'

If it weren't for Salwang-ssi, I feel like I could do something... but it's difficult because of Salwang-ssi. Even if he's an assassin, he's an assassin who has reached the Third Realm. I couldn't ignore him.

,Even if I escape with Leap, it'll only be for a moment.'

The Leap distance is 10m. The distance was too short. With the Cheongun Squad's skills, that level of variable wouldn't even be considered a variable.

,The damage is getting worse. If I bleed any more, it'll become dangerous.'

While blocking the enemies' attacks and racking my brain, I came to a conclusion.

I took out a bottle from my inventory. It was a bottle containing the blood of Ilsumansal (One Hand Ten Thousand Killings) Dang Cheoyong, whom I had killed before. The poison master Dang Cheoyong's blood itself was poison, so I collected it. Because he died so tragically, this one bottle was all I could collect.

,Get out of the way!! It's highly poisonous!!"

The old woman shouted even before I sprayed it. Perhaps because she was an assassin closely related to poison, her nose was good.

,Did she use that good nose to hide in the latrine and target me? She's a crazy bitch who's more than just poisonous.'

But it didn't matter.

That's because the disadvantage of the sword formation is that you can't immediately undo it even if you want to. Especially when things are going so well like they are now.

I scattered the poison on the bastards. The blood of a Third Realm poison master. Because it was taken out of the inventory, it still had body temperature, as if it had just been collected, so it was very fresh blood.

Dang Cheoyong's blood melted even the barrier the bastards had created in an instant and poisoned them.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak!"

,Antidote! Antidote... Kkeueueueuk...."

The Cheongun Squad martial artists couldn't last long. There was no way martial artists of the Initial Stage or Early Stage of Origin Qi could withstand a deadly poison that even killed a Third Realm absolute master.

In addition, the poison seemed to have some kind of chemical reaction with the corpses, spreading in all directions and poisoning the Cheongun Squad members. Because the poison was weaker than when directly exposed to it, they didn't die immediately. Of course, I was no exception, so I was affected too.

,Even better.'

[Absorbed poison. Health and mana are restored.]

,Damn it! Salwang! Do you have an antidote?!"

,It's the blood of the poison master Ilsumansal! It's extremely poisonous in itself and is a deep poison! Fortunately, the poison is weakened, and Dang Cheoyong is already dead, so the deep poison is weakened, so you can control the poison by circulating your energy!"

Namgung Tak and Salwang-ssi were struggling, poisoned by the poison.

,Deep poison. That's annoying. Even if Dang Cheoyong died and it's weakened, if the deep poison remains, there's nothing I can do right now.'

I had no intention of giving Namgung Tak and Salwang-ssi time. I quickly killed the Cheongun Squad's weaklings, even using an instant. Because they were groaning from the poison, it was as simple as killing bugs.

In less than 20 seconds, Namgung Tak and Salwang-ssi remained.

,Aaaaaaak! Seong Yujin!!"

Namgung Tak rushes in with anger. He's exploding his life and exploding his internal energy. In other words, a mutual destruction.

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A red flash sliced Namgung Tak's neck. Namgung Tak's head floated into the air and slowly fell.

,Too slow to fight me head-on."

I kicked Namgung Tak's head that had fallen to the floor to finish him off.

All that's left is Salwang-ssi.

The old woman was sweating profusely and clutching a dagger. Internal energy was constantly spewing out from her body, and as a result, her white hair soared and wriggled.

She's using more internal energy than necessary to resist the poison.

,...Indeed, Ilsumansal's poison. It's weaker than the original, but it's extremely poisonous. How the hell did you kill Ilsumansal?"

,Compatibility difference? I'm pretty confident in poison."

,...That alone wouldn't explain it."

,You're deliberately stalling for time. Trying to get rid of even a little bit of the poison in the meantime?"

I formed a sword seal with my left hand. Pajijik. Blue lightning flickered from my outstretched index and middle fingers. The meaning of the seal wasn't really there. If I had to pick one, it was to increase concentration through gestures.

,...I am an assassin. I came to kill you after receiving a request. Aren't you curious who made the request? Why would I try to kill you with a martial artist from the Namgung Clan?"

,I am curious. But I've experienced too much to believe the words of the enemy as they are. Let's find that out later... I'm more curious about how long the king of assassins can last."

,...What?"

,One."

I point at the old woman with my sword fingers.

Kwang!

A bolt of lightning fell from the clear sky towards the old woman.

Although my will was mixed in, it was at a similar level to ordinary lightning in terms of power. In addition, Salwang-ssi was spewing out internal energy with all his might, so he was unharmed.

,Are you trying to make me surrender with just this attack...?!"

,Ten."

Kwakwang!

Ten bolts of lightning fell in an instant. Salwang-ssi was unharmed this time as well.

,Hundred."

Kwakwakwakwang!

A large amount of internal energy was drained, and 100 bolts of lightning fell on the old woman. The old woman wasn't unharmed. The old woman's internal energy shook violently.

I looked up at the sky. A clear sky without clouds. It would have been a little easier if there was even one dark cloud.

,It wouldn't look good to use the Cloud Summoning Amulet even now.'

I lowered my gaze again. Salwang-ssi's expression wasn't good.

,...Do you think you can get what you want by doing this?"

,Thousand."

All of my internal energy was drained, and a thousand bolts of lightning fell on the old woman. The flashes that brightly colored the world continued for a few seconds before ending. The smell of ozone was possible everywhere.

Thud.

Salwang-ssi, who had been enduring, collapsed and vomited blood.

I was impressed. Because Salwang-ssi didn't seem to have any major injuries on the outside.

,...The attack is effective, but the power is less than I thought. I didn't intend to kill him, but I didn't know he wouldn't really die. Even though my will was contained in the lightning, is it because I can't recognize the mental image?'

To be exact, I didn't really care if she died. But the old woman survived. Moreover, she's maintaining her mind even though she's exhausted.

,Heu, heuheu. True assassins have been enduring torture since ancient times. Among them is torture by electric shock. Even if you shock me with ten thousand bolts of lightning... I will never surrender."

,It's so anticlimactic when an old woman, not a beauty, says that. Ah, I have a good idea. Wait here."

Salwang-ssi can't move anymore anyway.

I took out several space-time movement scrolls. I designated the coordinates of this place for a few of them, and tore one.

That's how I called the expert on old women, Seong Jigone-nim.

,Is this the woman?"

Seong Jigone-nim, a man of great stature with broad shoulders befitting an imperial general, licked his lips as he looked at the fallen Salwang-ssi. It was the expression of a pervert who had captured prey. Salwang-ssi was startled. I could see her eyes widening.

,Yes. She's an old woman called Salwang-ssi, so it's not easy to open her mouth with torture."

,Haha. Yujin-gun told me. Women with strong spirits are surprisingly weak to affection. And he said their back doors are weak? Leave her to me."

Salwang-ssi trembled, looking at Seong Jigone-nim with eyes as if she were looking at a monster. Did she sense her future?

I imagined Salwang-ssi's future and shook my head. I didn't want to imagine it.

Seong Jigone-nim knelt down in front of Salwang-ssi. And with a kind expression, he carefully stroked the old woman's disheveled white hair.

,Becoming an assassin must not have been your intention.... Don't worry. I will not coerce you. Instead, I will only embrace you with love....

,Y, you crazy bastard! Get out of here right now! Who gave you the right to judge me?! Heueok?! Where are you touching?! Y, you! Are you trying to make fun of an old woman like me?! Just kill me!!"

,Your clear voice is very charming."

Seong Jigone-nim picked up Salwang-ssi like a princess and headed somewhere.

I didn't chase after Seong Jigone-nim.
