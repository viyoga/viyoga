The city was bustling from noon.

Citizens were running around as if they all had urgent business, and horses and carriages sped along the roads. Heavily armed police patrolled the streets, exuding an imposing atmosphere.

It was lively, but different from the usual orderly Neo London.

Of course, I know why this is. It happens every 100 days. No one in this world could not know.

And for those who have no sense of dates, a recorded voice of a famous male entertainer echoes throughout the city.

-Dear ladies and gentlemen of Neo London! That day has come again. Yes. It's the day the Dark Moon rises. Don't you feel a chill in the morning air?

A chill, huh.

That expression wasn't wrong. Neo London's mornings are always foggy, so it might feel even more so.

I needlessly tightened my coat and walked down the street. The place my steps were heading wasn't home, a store, or a factory. I was leaving this city.

-Neo London is safe. The Dark Moon has risen since the distant past. Neo London has overcome the Dark Moon until now. However, more than 100 people have gone missing every time the Dark Moon rises.

Citizens moved busily, whether he babbled about the Dark Moon or not. I'm probably the only one focusing on his words. It's not because I'm a huge fan of his, I'm just bored. I naturally listen to what he's babbling about.

-From 6 PM, all areas of Neo London will be sealed off. Citizens, go home and close your doors. I recommend going to bed early. Can't fall asleep? Go to the nearest pharmacy. Pharmacies sell sleeping pills at very cheap prices on the day the Dark Moon rises. Of course, they only sell one day's worth of sleeping pills per person. Hot couples, please bear with it tonight, no matter how hot you are. A friend of mine had sex on the day the Dark Moon rose and ended up....

Sex.

The entertainer's words were unrestrained. But no one points out the entertainer's words. In this city. No, in this world, explicit, direct, and dirty jokes were close to everyday life.

,He's probably talking about the anecdote that a woman who had sex with her husband on the day the Dark Moon rose and became pregnant gave birth to a monster.'

The genes mutated under the influence of the Dark Moon.

Born from a human uterus that received human seed, but monsters that are not human. If you know how to use the internet a little, you can get detailed information surprisingly easily.

,If you're lucky, you might have a human who has acquired factors, or a psychic, born.'

Thanks to that, there are many who deliberately have sex and try to get pregnant during the Dark Moon, aiming for a reversal in their lives. There are also quite a few scientists who deliberately give money and try experiments.

-Be sure to lock the doors. Cover the windows with plywood, and if possible, don't turn on the lights in the house. Uninvited guests may see the lights and come looking. If someone knocks on the door and you see a deceased family member, contact the London Guard immediately. Don't open the door even if an acquaintance asks you to open it. If your child's personality is extreme, give them sleeping pills and put them to sleep. Quietly, like the dead, act without anyone knowing. I'll tell you again. Today is the day the Dark Moon rises. At 6 PM....

I'm reminded of when I played the game. At first, I thought the reactions of the NPCs in the game to the Dark Moon were too exaggerated. But coming to this world, I realized that it couldn't be helped. For ordinary people, the Dark Moon was an unmanageable natural disaster. If you don't want to be swept away by a natural disaster, you have to prepare thoroughly.

,How was the last Dark Moon?'

100 days ago. I experienced my first Dark Moon in Neo London. Nothing happened. When I woke up, the black moon had gone down in the sky and the usual sun was rising.

,At that time, I was too busy adapting to Neo London, so I lived quietly at home.'

Unlike ordinary people who are simply afraid of the Dark Moon, for those who have the power to protect themselves, the Dark Moon was also a night of opportunity.

,There are quite a few things you can only get on the day the Dark Moon rises.'

That was why I was leaving Neo London today.

,What's urgent for me now is the restoration of the mana road. Maybe it's because the mana road is a mess, but it's not easy to increase the mana road.'

I've been steadily training in magic since coming to Neo London. But not a single mana road has increased.

The 16 destroyed mana roads were blocking my growth.

,I need those to go to that healer.'

Tonight, I was planning to get those necessary things.

I took a train that went out of the city. The inside of the train was deserted. But there were a few customers. Most of them were customers who smelled of blood. I was sure they were mercenaries. Of course, I don't approach them and talk to them just because they're mercenaries. Even if I talk to them, there's an eighty percent chance that I'll get cursed at.

I quietly sat in my seat and watched the changing train scenery.

Neo London Outskirts.

This area, which is farther than Zone Z, is larger than all the zones of Neo London combined.

The outskirts refer to all the territory owned by Neo London, excluding Neo London. Unlike the dazzling city, the outskirts can be said to be wild itself.

,The council has completely given up on managing the outskirts.'

It's too dangerous and vast. At the same time, there are not many resources to be gained, so it's not worth investing in. For that reason, Neo London let go of the outskirts. They do clean up from time to time, but that's really rare.

,It's a familiar place for Dark Moon players.'

Leveling, hidden exploration, hidden quests, etc. It was also an exciting place for original game players.

,I can't get everything hidden here.'

There are many things that are difficult to obtain even with my current skills. And there were no items to prepare in advance to meet the conditions. What I can do here now is limited.

,Hmm....'

I looked at the outskirts and fell into trouble.

The overall appearance of the outskirts that I knew through the game was similar, but it was very different when I went into detail.

,The original was a PC game, so it can't be helped.... It's impossible to fully implement this vast environment on a PC....'

It seemed like I would have to suffer a bit today.

I looked around and walked down the street. This place was a village formed around the train station.

,If you walk a little from the station, you'll see a pub.... Yeah. This is the same as the game.'

The pub was closed because it was morning. I found an old man lying in the alley next to the pub and approached him.

NPC Nakario.

This world isn't a game, so it's a bit strange to call him an NPC.

Ting!

A total of 20 times. I give him 2,000 credits.

In the game, if you give alms 20 times in a row, Nakario gives you a bracelet as thanks. It's not a big deal. You can get a small profit by disassembling the bracelet?

But Nakario's reaction was completely different from the one in the game.

"You bastard! Are you ignoring me because I'm a beggar?! You shitty bastard. Do you think you won't end up like me? You goddamn mercenary. Go die!"

Even if you act the same as in the game, the reaction you get is completely different.

"Do you believe the nonsense that throwing coins into the fountain brings good luck? That's bullshit that the villagers made up to rip off foreigners."
