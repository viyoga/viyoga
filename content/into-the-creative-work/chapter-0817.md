Kyle stared at the man who had entered his office.

Olond Geulbet Baron.

A man with soft blonde hair, blue eyes, and flamboyant attire. The very atmosphere he exuded screamed 'aristocrat.'

He was a nobleman active in the western corner of the Rapelli Kingdom.

Originally a declining noble, he had used this war as an opportunity to rise rapidly and begin building his reputation.

He was the nobleman who had sent Kyle a letter not long ago.

,Meeting you in person is a first, Kyle-nim. I am Olond Geulbet Baron."

,I am Kyle Prucus. The reason you came to find me is...."

,Yes, yes. I have come to serve Kyle-nim. My 5,000 soldiers will be of great help to Kyle-nim. I, too, will faithfully serve as Kyle-nim's vassal."

Geulbet Baron bowed. His composed greeting held dignity instead of servility.

Kyle couldn't understand him. Geulbet Baron was growing, building his reputation. There was no reason for him to come under Kyle-nim.

,You seem suspicious of me."

,To be honest, yes. For someone like you to suddenly become my vassal... doesn't that seem strange no matter how you look at it? Besides, I haven't even received a title yet."

,First of all, Kyle-nim. Please, speak comfortably."

,...Alright."

,In the midst of this war, titles are not important. What's more important than a title is fame, power, wealth, and martial prowess. In that respect, Kyle-nim is far superior to someone like me. After the war ends, you will obtain a title higher than a Count. No, you will inherit the Prucus County. I will make it so."

Geulbet Baron's soft yet powerful voice was perfect for gaining people's trust. However, Kyle didn't erase his suspicious gaze.

,I don't believe in loyalty without a reason. What do you want from me?"

,I want wealth and glory. Unfortunately, there is a limit to how high I can climb on my own. I know my limits best. That's why I seek wealth and glory under Kyle-nim."

,...I'm sorry, but I still can't do it. Geulbet Baron, please leave."

Even with the order to leave, Geulbet Baron didn't back down. Instead, he smiled even more leisurely.

,Kyle-nim. Don't make a decision so hastily. I know very well what Kyle-nim's current state is, and what Kyle-nim desires. Perhaps even more than Kyle-nim does."

Kyle's eyebrows twitched. If it were him normally, he would have let it pass. However, the inner demon was still present in his heart. His emotions were volatile.

,You know me well? Tell me. But if you're even a little bit wrong, I won't let it slide."

Geulbet Baron didn't even flinch.

,Kyle-nim, you currently want to destroy and kill anything that slightly bothers you, right? You're perplexed because you suddenly feel a surge of anger even though you haven't done anything, right?"

,...."

,Yes. I understand Kyle-nim. Because I was like that once too."

,You were too? How did you resolve the inner demon?"

Kyle asked urgently. Even after circulating his Qi and meditating, the inner demon didn't disappear. Kyle wanted to shake off this inner demon before it grew any larger.

,So that's what Kyle-nim calls it, an inner demon. A demon in the heart... Yes. That's a good term."

,Just answer me."

,Find out the cause of the inner demon and resolve it. Simple, isn't it?"

,...."

,As I said just now, I know what Kyle-nim wants."

,...What I want? You?"

,Yuria Grace."

Kwaang!

Kyle's fist slammed into the desk. The desk's surface shattered in the shape of a fist. Kyle's eyes twitched. The moment Yuria's name came out of Geulbet Baron's mouth, out of another man's mouth, he momentarily lost control of his anger. The inner demon was becoming more severe than before.

,Kyle-nim. Calm down."

,...What should I do?"

,The inner demon is the clash between Kyle-nim's desires and conscience. There are a whopping three solutions! Abandon your desires, or abandon your conscience. Or, achieve your desires."

,Isn't that practically two?"

,Abandoning your conscience and achieving your desires are different. If you abandon your conscience, you'll just run wild. You need to achieve your desires through the right methods, with your conscience intact. For example, if you abandon your conscience and only your desires remain, Kyle-nim will kill Eugene Prucus and forcibly take Yuria Grace. That's truly acting according to your desires."

,Tell me to kill my own flesh and blood? Are you crazy? That's the path of a demon, not a human."

Kyle growled. Geulbet Baron shrugged leisurely.

,I was just giving an example, Kyle-nim."

,...What does it mean to achieve my desires through the right methods?"

,Inherit the Prucus County. If you become the Prucus Count, it's easy to take Yuria Grace away from Eugene Prucus. After all, Eugene Prucus is a member of the Count's family, and Yuria Grace is also a maid of the Count's family."

,That's ridiculous. Yuria is married to Eugene. Even if I become the Count and call for her, Yuria won't leave Eugene's side."

,Married? Kyle-nim, you're mistaken. Eugene Prucus is unmarried. He hasn't married anyone."

Officially, that was the case. But Kyle had clearly seen it. Those two sitting in the forest, embracing each other. Those two whispering words of love.

A vein bulged on the back of Kyle's hand.

,...That's right. That's what I'm wondering too. If it were me... if I were Eugene, I would have publicly announced the marriage...."

,We can make many speculations, but the most likely speculation in my opinion is that Eugene Prucus is toying with Yuria Grace's heart. If Eugene Prucus truly loved Yuria Grace, wouldn't he have acted like Kyle-nim?"

,That's right. If it were me, I would have. If I... if I hadn't been late, Yuria would...."

,It's not too late yet. Kyle-nim can become the Prucus Count and save Yuria Grace from being toyed with by Eugene Prucus."

,...."

,Are you still hesitating? Does Kyle-nim not intend to become the Prucus Count?"

,I will become the Prucus Count. I've already decided that. But can I be sure that Yuria is being toyed with by Eugene? I saw it. Yuria... she might truly love Eugene."

,Kyle-nim, you don't know the true nature of Eugene Prucus."

,Eugene's true nature?"

,His mind is filled with nothing but women. Do you know about his mansion? There's a rumor that there are hundreds of beauties there who only serve Eugene Prucus. Furthermore, Eugene Prucus doesn't see slaves as people. There's a saying that the number of slaves executed by Eugene Prucus alone is in the tens of thousands."

Kyle couldn't deny it. Eugene, whom he had met on the battlefield, had changed so much that he doubted whether he was the Eugene he knew. He treated soldiers and slaves carelessly, and didn't care at all about the slave soldiers who were sacrificed.

,Kyle-nim. From now on, investigate Eugene Prucus's actions thoroughly. I will assist you by your side. Even though it may not seem like it, I once worked in an information guild and even rose to the position of branch manager. Uncover the true nature of Eugene Prucus, become the Prucus Count, and punish him. Save the commoners and slaves suffering in his territory. Only Kyle-nim can save Yuria Grace."

,...."

Kyle closed his eyes.

Eugene is his younger brother. If Eugene has been tainted by evil.... As a brother, he must punish Eugene. He must correct his wayward younger brother. He must save those who are suffering under Eugene, the toyed-with Yuria.

,Geulbet Baron. Do you truly swear allegiance to me?"

A smile spread across Geulbet Baron's face. He knelt down on one knee in front of Kyle without hesitation and prostrated himself.

,Of course! I swear eternal allegiance, my lord!"

,...What should I do?"

,First, you must establish discipline in the army. Kyle-nim's army is excellent, but it's too relaxed. The army doesn't act like an army. You must strengthen the internal affairs. And... I will take care of investigating the information about Eugene Prucus."

,...Dig up everything about Eugene."

,Leave it to me."

Terius had lost his vitality ever since the mercenaries of the Korea Merchant Guild had given him the box containing the crystal ball and iron ring that day.

Even when he slept, ate, and talked with his comrades, life wasn't enjoyable. Everything was bothersome and annoying. He simply repeated his daily routine regularly, without any emotion.

He wanted to die.

That thought was repeated. He was actually thinking that it might be better to just die when Kyle called him.

Terius was nervous. Kyle had definitely changed. It had been since Olond Geulbet Baron had volunteered to be Kyle's vassal. He had changed from a comfortable and friendly older brother to a charismatic ruler. The reactions of the knights and soldiers were mixed. Some liked it, saying that he had gained spirit, while others worried that he was becoming corrupted like other nobles.

Terius was closer to the latter.

,...Captain. I'm here."

,Come in."

He opened the door and went inside. Kyle and Geulbet Baron were sitting in front of the office. Terius was flustered. The atmosphere was unusual.

,Sir Terius! It's only been three days since we briefly greeted each other last time, hasn't it?"

,...Yes. Geulbet Baron-nim. Today......."

Terius cautiously watched Kyle's expression. Kyle, who had been looking at Terius with a blank face, slowly opened his mouth. His voice was dry.

,Terius. I know that you embezzled the relics of Baron Maberlot."

Kung!

Terius felt his heart sink. He was very afraid of Kyle. And he was bewildered. He himself had even considered taking his own life. But when death actually approached, his legs trembled and a suffocating fear washed over him.

Terius knelt on the floor.

,C-Captain. I'm sorry.... Yes, I needed money to save Nephia. I had no choice. Captain.... Please, forgive me just once...."

,I understand."

,...Yes?"

Terius blinked.

,The place where you sold the relics and took out the loan is the Korea Merchant Guild, right? The actual owner of the Korea Merchant Guild is my younger brother, Eugene. It was also Eugene who demanded 1 billion Ner in exchange for Nephia, a slave. Terius, you were just being toyed with by Eugene."

,...What am I supposed to do now.... There's nothing I can do."

,You don't have any thoughts of revenge?"

,What are you expecting from a crippled mercenary slave? There's no way I can take revenge."

,If you're willing, I'll help you."

,...I don't understand. He's the Captain's younger brother. Why are you helping me take revenge?"

,Eugene has gone astray. I won't correct Eugene. As a brother... I will correct Eugene. In the process, your revenge will also be achieved."

,...."

Kyle's voice was firm. Terius realized that he had made a sincere decision. Still, Terius was still skeptical. What could he do with only one left arm?

,Sir Terius. Your potential is high. The fact that you reached the level of Aura Expert at that age is proof that you are a genius."

Geulbet Baron stepped forward. He grinned and placed his hand on Terius's right shoulder. The shoulder without an arm throbbed.

,Sir Terius. I will attach an arm for you. I'll also pay off your debt."

,...Attach an arm? That's ridiculous.... I've already lost my right arm."

Terius, who had been momentarily happy, soon sighed and shook his head.

,Human arms are all similar. It doesn't necessarily have to be your arm. I know a skilled magician. You just need to allow it."

Life returned to Terius's eyes.

,...Is it also true that you will resolve the debt?"

,About 200 million is just pocket change to me."

Terius glanced at Kyle. Kyle nodded.

,Are you still hesitating? Don't you need to bring back the maid named Nephia?"

,Nephia is... not me, but him...."

,So you're going to give up?"

Terius lowered his head.

Nephia.

His hope that he had longed for for a long time.

Even if it's the wrong way, he wants to have her. He wants to keep her by his side.

Terius didn't deny his ugly emotions and raised his head.

,...I can't give up."

Terius still had the iron ring on the ring finger of his left hand.
