Kuoong. Kung. Kung.

A being that looks like a grown man enlarged more than 10 times is approaching beyond the wooden fence. A huge sound echoed from the ground each time the monster took a step.

It's a Contaminated based on an ogre. Its skin is dirty orange, and its right arm is excessively large. If you look closely, you can see something like a steel rod embedded in its right arm.

Level 4 Contaminated.

It was the bastard I had to subjugate.

,"That's one gruesome bastard, no matter how many times I see it."

Max grumbled next to me.

"There's no need for you to be next to me. Wait with the villagers at the town hall."

"Don't misunderstand. It's not that I don't trust you. I just want to see that goddamn bastard die. I swear I won't get in your way, so please don't tell me to piss off."

"......."

I couldn't be bothered to argue with him, so I decided to just let it go. It didn't matter as long as he didn't get in my way.

I focused my gaze back on the Level 4 Contaminated.

Contaminated refers to those that have been affected by waste and pollutants, and have undergone mutation due to the influence of the Dark Moon.

The biggest characteristic of Contaminated is that they don't necessarily have to be living things to become Contaminated. There are cases where corpses, waste, or scrap metal clump together to become Contaminated.

They have almost no intelligence. Even small animals are more intelligent than Contaminated. You can think of the intelligence of Contaminated as being at the level of zombies.

"When did the Level 4 Contaminated appear?"

"It first appeared three days ago. We weren't prepared at the time, so... two villagers were eaten by it. At dawn, it brazenly came and took a dump in front of the village. ...My drinking buddy was mixed in with that goddamn poop!"

I wasn't interested. I asked differently.

"How have you dealt with that bastard since then?"

"...That bastard is incredibly strong, but he's also dull and stupid. We lured him with a motorcycle. He didn't try to leave the village, so we had to circle around the village all night long. Then, when the sun rose, he went into the ground."

"Didn't you think about getting rid of him directly?"

"Why wouldn't we? Bullets, not to mention bombs, didn't work. We had no choice but to hope for help from someone else."

"So, he has tremendous strength and durability. I understand."

I got on my motorcycle and drove towards the Contaminated. Max followed behind me.

"What are you going to do?"

"First... I'll have to burn it."

[Fire Ball]

A fireball streaks across the night sky with a red tail and flies towards the Contaminated. The fireball hits the Contaminated's large body and explodes. Crimson flames engulf the Contaminated's body.

The Contaminated doesn't even scream as its body burns. It can't feel pain itself.

I narrowed my eyes and checked the condition of the Contaminated. The flames are burning, but they aren't burning the Contaminated's body at all. The night wind blew, and the flames gradually died down and disappeared.

'It's not fire resistance or magic resistance. It's just not a material that burns.'

[Torrent]

A large amount of water appeared from under my feet and flew towards the Contaminated. It soaks the Contaminated's body.

[Ice Spear]

An ice spear hit the Contaminated's chest. Cold air exploded and covered the Contaminated's body.

Jjeojeojeojeong!

In an instant, the Contaminated's body froze.

The Contaminated lifted its leg and walked forward without a care. The ice shattered and scattered in all directions. Fire doesn't work, and ice doesn't work either.

"Don't you have any more powerful magic? Magic with the power to get rid of that bastard in one shot!"

"Shut up. Don't rush me."

I used telekinesis to pull a stone rolling on the ground towards me.

[Material Conversion]

I changed the stone into metal and drew a blue magic circle. After finishing adjusting the angle, I finished activating the magic.

[Railgun]

With a deafening roar, the railgun was fired. A bullet that far exceeded the speed of sound pierced through the Contaminated's abdomen. A huge hole was created in the Contaminated's abdomen.

"Oh, oh oh!"

Max exclaimed in admiration at the meaningful hit.

On the other hand, I stared at the Contaminated with a hardened face.

"This is fucked up."

"Why? Isn't that as good as over already?"

"It didn't fall. And look closely at the wounded area."

"It's recovering...?! This is absurd...."

The Contaminated's gaping abdomen is rapidly recovering. In about 3 minutes, it will recover without leaving a single scar.

"Don't you have any more powerful magic?!"

"No."

"......Are we screwed?"

Leaving behind the dumbfounded Max, I drove my motorcycle towards the Contaminated. As the distance got closer, I could smell the stench emanating from the Contaminated. Kung kung kung. The Contaminated is approaching me.

'It's certain that it has the trait of super recovery.'

It's one of the characteristics that Contaminated have in the original game. The orthodox strategy is to use skills, items, etc. that reduce recovery power. If you don't even have those, there's only one answer.

'DPS check. If you fuck it up with overwhelming firepower before it recovers, it's over.'

The railgun worked. That alone is enough.

Three stones that had fallen on the ground soared into the air.

[Material Conversion]

The stones turn into bullets. I then completed three magic circles.

[Railgun]

Three railguns are fired simultaneously.

The dark space flashed, and the aftershock swept the ground. At the same time, the Contaminated's body was smashed and fell to the ground in pieces.

"Ooh. Amazing. Is this the magic of a Level 5 magician! I will deposit the 5 million credit commission into the promised account!"

Leaving behind the chattering Max, I rummaged through the Contaminated's corpse with the attribute sword.

"...Do you perhaps have a hobby of playing with corpses?"

"I can see traces of black magic."

"......What do you mean?"

"Look here. There are traces of a black magic circle inside the intestines."

"To my eyes, it just looks like black spots...."

"You have no eye for detail. Look at these intestines. The formulas of black magic are broken down and engraved on each and every intestine. So that the respective formulas engraved on these intestines can be compatible to complete a black magic circle."

"Even if you say that... I'm not a magician, so I don't really understand. However, I understand that a black magician is involved in this. What effect does the black magic circle have?"

"I don't know."

Max looks at me with a dumbfounded expression.

I said confidently to him.

"The intestines are severely damaged. You have to be able to interpret the black magic circle when it's intact. With this, I can only guess that there was a black magic circle."

No matter how much it's me, I couldn't tell what kind of black magic circle it was just from the traces of the black magic circle.

"Damn it. Does that mean a black magician is targeting us? Should I report it to the council and hope that support comes as soon as possible...."

"I'm interested. I'll track down the black magician."

The black magician probably created the Contaminated artificially. Judging by the fact that he roughly released the Contaminated, he must have been trying to accumulate research data. I became interested in the black magician's research data and black magic.

As a bonus, it's also an opportunity to kill the black magician and extract the Dark Hole. I can't just pass by when there's a bonus right in front of me.

"Do you know where the black magician is?"

"Didn't you say it yourself? The Contaminated came out of the ground and went back into the ground. The black magician probably set up a workshop underground."

"Isn't that dangerous?"

"It'll be dangerous."

I put my hand in my jacket pocket. I could feel the Overload Ampule. I was thinking of using the Overload Ampule in case of an emergency.

"But I'm still going."

"...Why? Why are you deliberately courting danger?"

"I'm not courting danger, I'm trying to seize an opportunity."

I was quite confident.

If it's a Level 5 or higher black magician, there's no reason to set up a workshop in a place like this. He couldn't even handle the ogre Contaminated, so he probably sent it outside.

"You go back to the village."

"...I will. It's obvious that I'll just be a burden if I follow you any further.... I'll go back and protect the villagers. And... thank you."

"There's no reason for me to receive your thanks. I'm just moving in a direction that benefits me."

"Still, thank you. I just wanted to say thank you."

Max got on his motorcycle and returned to the village.

I knelt down on one knee on the ground. I put my palm on the ground and used magic.

[Detection]

The mana wave spread out like an ultrasound. Just as I was about to stop the detection because I couldn't feel anything, I felt the presence of an underground space at the far west end. I moved immediately. I got on my motorcycle and drove west.

[Detection]

I could feel it clearly. I could feel an empty space underground. I was also trying to find the black magician's location... but I couldn't pinpoint the exact location because there was a barrier.

[Dig Out]

The ground caved in. After using the Dig Out magic three more times, I was able to enter the underground space.

The underground space was dark and quiet.

[Light]

I created a sphere of light above my head and walked straight ahead. Of course, I didn't forget to use detection magic either.

I was carefully moving forward when I stopped. An invisible wall blocked my path right in front of me.

'It's a barrier. The barrier itself is low level. At most, Level 3.'

I carefully touched the barrier with my hand. I slightly released mana and interpreted the formula of the barrier.

'Easy. If I just properly dig into the slightly twisted formula....'

Jiiiiing.

The barrier split open. I went inside the barrier.

I began to see traces of people in the passage where I couldn't see anything until now. People's footprints, trash that people seemed to have thrown away, and so on.

'He probably noticed that I invaded. He'll attack as soon as he sees me, so block it with a barrier and... after seizing the space with Electric Field....'

I was walking while imagining the battle with the black magician when I smelled blood. It wasn't fresh. There was a rotten smell mixed in. It seemed to be at least three days old.

I quickened my pace. If you accumulate combat experience against monsters and humans, you can somewhat distinguish between the smell of monster blood and human blood. And the smell of blood that I'm smelling now is human.

A cavern came out.

Looking at the books and experimental tools scattered here and there, it seemed to be the center of the black magician's workshop.

I found the black magician. He was dead on the operating table. The corpse's abdomen was empty. A monster was sitting on top of the corpse. It's a monster with bat wings, a monkey's body, and a goat's head.

It's not a monster.

It was too alien to be called a monster. It's something else.

The monster that saw me turned its goat head 180 degrees.

"Human? What is it? I feel familiarity. Are you a contractor?"

I realized what it was from those words and let out a faint groan.

"I'm the demon Bioda. Who are you?"

"I have no name to give to a demon."

"Okay. Won't you make a contract with me?"
