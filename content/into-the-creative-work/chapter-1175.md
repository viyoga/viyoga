I was eating diligently when the door of the corner store opened and a customer came in. Nakagami Risa and I craned our necks to see who it was.

It was Hagawa Ryoko and Wada Kyoshiro.

,Nakagami-senpai and Seong-sang?

Wada Kyoshiro's eyes widened. Hagawa Ryoko, standing next to him, frowned as she looked at Nakagami Risa and me.

"They're first-years. Did they come to buy something?"

"Nakagami-senpai. You're having a meal... I see. Do they sell meals here too?"

"They don't sell them. Grandma asked us to eat together. Right, Grandma?"

Nakagami Risa smiled and said to the old woman. The old woman glanced at me, then nodded.

"Well, yes. It's lonely to eat alone, so I called the students over."

Wada Kyoshiro and Hagawa Ryoko approached.

Kkorureureureuk.

A shameful sound came from Wada Kyoshiro's stomach after he saw the food. Wada Kyoshiro's face turned red.

"Are you students hungry too? Come and eat."

The old woman glanced at me and started filling a rice bowl.

"I should refuse... but it looks so delicious... I don't want to refuse."

"There's no need to refuse."

"Th, then just a little."

Wada Kyoshiro and Hagawa Ryoko joined the meal. Wada Kyoshiro ate the food, constantly exclaiming in admiration, while Hagawa Ryoko poked at her food with her chopsticks, her eyes full of suspicion.

"Wow. This is really good. It feels like I'm having a proper meal for the first time in a while."

Wada Kyoshiro seemed to be the only one without any ulterior motives at this table.

"...Um, why were Nakagami-senpai and Seong-sang here together?"

Hagawa Ryoko asked carefully.

Before I could answer, Nakagami Risa smiled, wrapped her arms around my right arm, and said.

"We decided to start dating yesterday."

"......Why?"

"Why? Because we like each other, duh. Eugene is a good man. He's perfect for me. And conversely, a woman like me is perfect for Eugene too."

"...Congratulations, senpai."

"Congratulations, senpai! Seong-sang!"

Hagawa Ryoko and Wada Kyoshiro said.

The surprising thing was Hagawa Ryoko. I thought she would be openly happy, but she was expressionless. She reached for the japchae as if she didn't care. The glass noodles slipped from her chopsticks. She tried a few more times before finally picking them up.

Wada Kyoshiro, who was somewhat full, opened his mouth to break the awkward atmosphere.

"Ah, right. I heard about Tanaka-senpai from Mori-senpai earlier.... Tanaka-senpai apparently escaped again overnight."

"Escaped?"

Nakagami Risa showed interest in his words. Tanaka Hiroshi was the guy whose hair I grabbed and slammed against the wall, killing him. I left the body in the Detention center, so the police must have taken care of it.

"Yes. Mori-senpai said he heard it from Shimada-sang... the police box chief. He said he escaped down the mountain."

"How? Wasn't that bastard locked up in the Detention center?"

"The Detention center bars were broken, so they weren't properly locked. Mori-senpai and I tried to contact Tanaka-senpai, but his phone was turned off, so we couldn't reach him. It's the same with Gonzo-senpai and Sakurai-senpai."

"Wada. Do you still call those bastards senpai?"

"Ah.... Nakagami-senpai. Those senpais' personalities aren't great, but... they wouldn't commit a crime."

"Based on what?"

"A hunch, I guess. And..."

Wada Kyoshiro hesitated, glancing at the old woman, then spoke.

"This village is a bit strange."

"Hmm."

Nakagami Risa only nodded. The look in her eyes as she looked at Wada Kyoshiro changed slightly. From looking at him like a pebble to looking at him like a slightly unusual pebble.

Uuuuuung.

Wada Kyoshiro's smartphone vibrated. I and the others had turned off our smartphones to save battery.

"It's Mori-senpai. He wants us to gather at the men's dorm by 3 PM. There's a summer festival in the village tonight...?"

Ookaruma Village's summer festival.

The Japan Festival Study Circle came to this village to investigate this festival and build up their achievements.

For Mori Masahiro, the club president, he can't miss this opportunity even in this isolated situation. The other club members are the same. Isolation is isolation, and they have to think about the situation after returning to university.

After the meal, Wada Kyoshiro and Hagawa Ryoko bought drinks and other food supplies.

"Nakagami-senpai, Seong-sang. Are you going to stay here?"

In response to Wada Kyoshiro's question, I smiled and said.

"I have something to talk about with Grandma. I'll be back before 3 PM, so don't worry."

There were more than 2 hours left until 3 PM. They left, and only the three of us remained in the corner store.

The old woman trembled. She was afraid I would harm her. It was an unnecessary worry. I had no interest in the old woman.

"Risa-senpai. We ate, so we should exercise, right?"

"Is there any exercise we can do here?"

"Sexercise is possible anywhere. If I and senpai are together."

"Pfft. Okay. I don't dislike exercising either."

She threw off her clothes enthusiastically, and we spent a hot time in front of the old woman.

"You old hag! Huddle in the corner!"

"...Yes, yes."

The old woman sat in the corner, trembling with fear.

•••

Nakagami Risa and I entered the men's dorm. Everyone was gathered even though we arrived earlier than the appointed time.

With Tanaka Hiroshi and his two cronies gone, there were 9 club members left. 4 men and 5 women. There were more women, but they were all passive.

"Good. Everyone's here."

Mori Masahiro, the club president, brought something and spread it out in front of us.

It was a map of Ookaruma Village.

"Mori-senpai. Where did you get the map?"

I asked. This village is strange. The villagers are clearly aiming for something. If what Tanaka Hiroshi said yesterday is true, their goal is related to our lives. There's no way they would give us important information like a village map.

"Ah, this? Fudo and I made it ourselves."

Fudo Jun. A third-year male student. He seemed particularly close to Mori Masahiro.

"You made it well."

"I made it by referring to digital camera footage of various parts of the village."

"But why the map? Was there a need to make it?"

"Um. I thought it would be convenient to have one."

Mori Masahiro vaguely brushed it off. Of course, that's not the reason. Mori Masahiro seems to have noticed the strange things about this village and prepared a map.

He pointed to one spot on the map.

"This is our dorm. As you can see on the map, it's far from the village entrance. It's also quite a distance from the shrine."

There was a mountain behind the dorm, and around it were fields instead of houses.

"Fudo and I explored the back earlier. There's a cliff not far away. It's quite a deep cliff, so it's impossible to get out through here."

Wada Kyoshiro raised his hand.

"Mori-senpai. It sounds like you're trying to explain how to get out... we decided to stay here until the road is repaired, right?"

"I was going to, but... this village is strange no matter how I think about it. Tanaka, Gonzo, and Sakurai were caught by the police and escaped, and now they're missing. Even if we leave out Gonzo and Sakurai, Tanaka was in no condition to escape, but he still escaped overnight."

"Did the police... do something to Tanaka-senpai?"

Wada Kyoshiro asked in a serious tone.

"...I have no evidence."

"You have suspicions, though."

"Do you remember the story I told you last time? There was a similar situation last year. A tourist who came to Ookaruma Village went missing."

At that moment, a woman raised her hand. She raised her long hand, befitting her thin body and tall height. Her long hair had no shine, and her eyes were sharp with sclera showing. Her nose was hooked, her lips were thin, and her chin was pointed. She looked sinister.

It was Minewaki Jurie, a second-year.

"Mori-senpai. About that... I checked it out...."

"You checked it out?"

"...I asked the villagers. They said no tourists came last summer."

"Minewaki. I also asked the villagers. The answers were all the same. They either denied it or avoided answering. Don't trust the villagers too much. You know the villagers are strange too, right?"

"......"

Minewaki Jurie closed her mouth. Just as Mori Masahiro was about to speak to the others again, she opened her mouth.

"...I also looked up the missing person case you mentioned on my smartphone. There were no articles related to the disappearance.... How did you know about the disappearance...?"

Everyone's eyes turned to Mori Masahiro. The atmosphere is heavy. Everyone is under a lot of stress due to the isolated situation, so they have become sensitive.

"...Haa. One of the people who went missing in this village last year was my hometown friend. The last time I talked to him was around this time. He said the village was strange. That's all I heard. I wanted to report it to the police and have them investigate, but... they didn't even listen."

An uncomfortable air surrounded them.

I was surprised when I saw Minewaki Jurie's face. She had twisted her face into a grotesque expression that looked like a ghost.

"It's all because of you!"

"...Minewaki?"

"If you hadn't brought us here, none of this would have happened! You brought us here to find your missing friend! To satisfy your own selfish desires! Tanaka, Gonzo, and Sakurai went missing because of you! It's all your fault!!"

Minewaki Jurie's screaming voice filled the living room.

Mori Masahiro closed his eyes tightly and bowed deeply. A perfect 90-degree bow.

"I'm sorry. As Minewaki said, I chose Ookaruma Village because of my missing friend. I wanted to see for myself what happened."

"You could have come alone! Why did you drag us into this?!!"

"...I was afraid. I didn't have the confidence to come here alone. So I ignored it and lived forgetting about it.... But I had the opportunity, so I chose Ookaruma Village. I thought it would be okay if I wasn't alone. ...I will take responsibility for you all. So I hope you will follow my instructions."

"Aaaaaargh! I don't trust you!!"

Minewaki Jurie threw a huge fit of hysterics and went back to the women's dorm.

Mori Masahiro couldn't raise his head even after she left.

In the end, Fudo Jun, a third-year, and Nakagami Risa stepped forward.

"I think Mori-senpai is about 20% at fault in this situation. The other 80% isn't Mori-senpai's fault. It's because of this goddamn village and the typhoon that hit all of Japan. If you want to blame someone, blame the villagers. Raise your head, Mori-senpai."

"What's important now is what we do from now on. We can sort out the rights and wrongs later. What do you guys think?"

Nakagami Risa asked the second-years and first-years.

We all defended Mori Masahiro. We may have had complaints in our hearts, but now is not the time to express them.

Mori Masahiro raised his head only after confirming everyone's opinions.

"Thank you. I'll... go to Minewaki later to apologize and persuade her."
