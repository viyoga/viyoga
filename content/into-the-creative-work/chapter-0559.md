"How can this be! I can't see fate! You've finally appeared! Ninth Apostle-isiyeo!"

I grinned.

"Yes. I am the Ninth Apostle, Cassandra. I am the end of this world, and the new beginning."

I knew the meaning of the Ninth Apostle through the original story.

The Ninth Apostle, as spoken of in the Gumok Cult, signifies the end and the beginning. The apostle who leads to a new world when the existing world ends.

The new world is a world that even the Cult Leader cannot confirm within their fate. Therefore, only someone whose fate the Cult Leader cannot see can become the Ninth Apostle.

"Cassandra Apostle."

I called her. She prefers to be called Apostle rather than Cult Leader.

"Yes. Please, speak."

"This isn't my first time. The god with nine eyes has given me a new opportunity."

"What... what are you saying? Please, tell me more."

"I cannot say."

"Is it related to the twisting of your fate?"

"That, too, I cannot say."

When scamming, there's no need to speak definitively. Mix truth and lies appropriately and let the other person guess the truth.

"......."

The old woman nodded with a stern expression. Judging by the fact that she's not attacking me, my intentions must have gotten through properly.

'Thanks to my acting trait, my success rate goes up when I scam. Keukeu.'

Cassandra has already acknowledged me as the Ninth Apostle. She called me that earlier, after all.

"Ninth Apostle-isiyeo. I acknowledge that you have undergone trials, but the other cult members do not."

"I understand. And I have no intention of running away, Cassandra Apostle. Give me trials. I will perform the trials and be formally recognized as the Ninth Apostle."

"As expected of the Ninth Apostle! What a splendid mindset!"

I have no intention of doing trials either. But if I refuse here, I'll be suspected, and things will get twisted.

'It's not like I can refuse anyway. If they make me do some shitty work, I'll just run away.'

I had no intention of doing anything for the Gumok Cult.

"The Second Apostle candidate is walking the path of trials."

"......If it's the Second Apostle, you mean that blonde child."

I pretended to know. The Second Apostle also appeared in the original story. You have to pretend to know these things right away to dispel Cassandra's suspicion.

"Yes. That child will overcome the trials and become an Apostle. That child was born with the fate of a Saintess."

"Yes. She will become the Saintess of the Gumok Cult. But why are you suddenly talking about that...?"

"Apostle of Fate-isiyeo. Your trial is to help that child."

"I don't understand Cassandra Apostle's intentions. She will complete the trials and become the Second Apostle even without my or Cassandra Apostle's help."

"Yes. I know. That is that child's fate. I want to confirm the fate of that child changing due to your intervention."

"It's not like fate will completely change just because I intervene. Rather, there's a greater possibility that it won't change."

"It will change. I am certain."

Expectation filled Cassandra's eyes. No matter what I say here, it won't get through to her. Cassandra wants to confirm me.

"I understand. I will accept the trial given to me."

"Then, Ninth Apostle-isiyeo. I will send you to that child right away. Please, grant that child a new fate..."

Cassandra gestures.

Mana gathers around me. A complex magic circle is drawn beneath my feet. I'm completely ignorant about magic, but judging by the atmosphere, it must be transfer magic.

'To do transfer magic so easily... As expected of an SSS-rank magic skill.'

Ssuuk.

I felt as if my body was being sucked into somewhere.

The Gumok Cult.

『You have entered the special dungeon, Island of Desire (Yù Dǎo).』

'Island of Desire (慾島) will test your desires and emotions.'

It was an island.

I activated Spirit Eye and looked around. The island was surrounded by the sea. Waves crashed in with the fiercely blowing wind. The waves were quite high. The sky was gloomy, as if it would pour down rain at any moment.

The island had forests, rocks, rivers... It was an island with a mixed impression. The size wasn't as big as I thought.

And there were no spirits on this island. It seems to be connected to the outside, but it's inside a dungeon.

'A special dungeon, so it's going to be troublesome.'

I scratched my cheek. Special dungeons are often different from regular dungeons from the basics.

I probably won't be able to clear the dungeon by simply killing the boss monster of the dungeon.

'Cassandra told me to find the Second Apostle candidate. She must be somewhere on this island.'

I don't know much about this dungeon, Island of Desire (Yù Dǎo). However, if I consider it in relation to the Second Apostle, I can roughly guess what kind of dungeon it is.

Just standing still won't get me anywhere. First, I moved.

'Should I go towards the peak first? I might be able to find her if I look from a high place.'

I walked along the forest path.

The wind blew, and the trees swayed. The sound of the leaves colliding with each other was pleasant to hear.

And from some point on, there was a delicious smell. It was an indescribable smell. Just a delicious smell.

My mouth watered, and the sound of my stomach rumbling echoed incessantly. My toes were already pointing towards the direction the smell was coming from.

Gulppeok. Gulppeok.

I kept swallowing my saliva. Even when I smelled Korean beef after starving for three days, it wasn't like this.

'What kind of cooking smell is this?'

The identity of the smell was, absurdly, tree fruit. A blue, unknown fruit was growing on a large tree. The shape was like an apple. The surface was so plump that it seemed like juice would burst out if I took a bite.

Kkorureureuk. Kkorureureuk.

'Ah. I can't stand it. Let's just eat one. It smells so delicious, so the taste must be amazing, right?'

As a Hunter, eating unknown fruits was something I should avoid. Because there's a possibility of poison. But I had Complete Recovery. Even if I die, I have another chance.

Tteuk.

I climbed up the tree and picked a fruit. The delicious smell gets stronger.

Wageujak!

I held the fruit and took a big bite. As I imagined, juice overflowed. The flesh was crunchy, and the juice was sweet. I devoured the fruit greedily, feeling the satisfaction of being full.

Kkorureureuk.

'One is too little.'

Fortunately, there were dozens of fruits on the tree.

Wageujak! Wageujak!

I ate the blue fruit like crazy. It was so delicious that my hand wouldn't stop.

Kkorureureuk! Kkoreureuk!

I felt something was wrong when I ate more than 10 fruits. I felt like I had eaten enough to fill more than half of my body, but I wasn't full at all.

'It's delicious, but I'm just eating it meaninglessly.'

I picked the fruit, put it in my inventory, and came down from the tree.

Even if it doesn't fill me up, the taste is the best, so I was going to eat it as a snack while walking.

'The taste is really the best. There's no way there's a taste like this anywhere else.'

It was the moment I was about to take a bite. The fruit I was holding disappeared. I tilted my head and took out another fruit from my inventory. This time, the fruit disappeared as well.

'......Could it be? Did it disappear because I got too far away from the tree?'

Should I go back to get the fruit?

'No. There's no reason to go through the trouble since I can't take it with me anyway. Let's just go.'

『You have overcome gluttony.』

My face crumpled when I saw the notification window.

I immediately understood what was happening.

Island of Desire (Yù Dǎo).

The island of desire.

This dungeon tests people's desires.

I just received the test of gluttony.

'Dare to test me as you please? I don't like it.'

I arrived at the highest peak on the island. I looked down at the island. Forests, rivers, flowers, rocks, snow, deserts... All kinds of environments were on one island.

"Found her. She was in the desert."

A child wearing a priest's robe was walking in the desert.

I watched for a while. I was going to know where the child's destination was before going to the desert now, as our paths might cross.

'What is it? Why is she spinning around in the same spot?'

It's definitely not that she's lost. This desert is only 2km at most. Even if she wanted to get lost, she couldn't.

'Is she looking for something?'

It didn't seem like she would leave the desert for a while, so I decided to go find her.

When I arrived in the desert, the child was still walking in the desert. I could see the child's appearance in more detail.

She was wearing a priest's robe that looked more luxurious than the clothes worn by the Gumok Cult members. Her golden hair was very bright and came down to her waist, swaying whenever she walked. Her skin was snow-white.

She was a child. She seemed to be about 2-3 years older than me now.

She was walking forward aimlessly with her eyes closed.

'She was closing her eyes in the original story too... Was she closing her eyes at this time too?'

I approached her. The Second Apostle candidate that Cassandra mentioned. The person I had to help was the girl right in front of me.

"Iste."

At the sound of her name, her small legs stopped. She turned her head towards me, where the sound came from.

"...Who are you?"

"The Ninth Apostle... To be exact, I'm still a candidate, but I've come to help you."

"Me?"

"Cassandra Apostle sent me here. My trial is to help your trial."

"Cult Leader-nim..."

It's not something I should say, but I felt a maturity from her that wasn't like a child. She's not an ordinary child after all.

"Ninth Apostle candidate-nim. Can you tell me your name?"

"Ah. I didn't say it. Seong Yujin. My name is Yujin. You can call me Yujin. I'm speaking in Korean, but you're not speaking in Korean."

"I am Iste Mayer. I am speaking English, and I hear Yujin-nim's words in English as well."

It was amazing, but it wasn't something to be so surprised about.

This is a special dungeon. It's possible to hear familiar languages even though you're using different languages.

"I'm sorry to say this to Yujin-nim, but I want to endure the trials alone."

"That's not something you can say. My trial is to help you. I can't give up my trial because of you."

"......Is that really Cult Leader-nim's will?"

"What benefit would I get from lying to you?"

"...I'm sorry. It was such an unexpected thing that I suspected Yujin-nim for a moment. If that is Cult Leader-nim's will, then I will accept it."
