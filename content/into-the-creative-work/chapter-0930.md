,What if I combine this clown mask with Stormbreaker?

There was no reason to hesitate, so I tried it right away.

The clown mask seeped into Stormbreaker. Stormbreaker's form began to change. What it ultimately became was armor with spikes sticking out, and the helmet transformed like a clown mask.

,...I don't like it.

It seems better to just wear the clown mask.

,There's another way... a way only possible in this world.

Spirit Summon.

I summon the spirit I have a contract with in front of me.

The mana in my body drained somewhere, and my eyes flashed, and a 60cm woman with dark blue hair and a white dress appeared. Just by her appearance, she looks like a fairy from a fairy tale.

Machina.

The machine spirit I have a contract with.

Machina rubbed her eyes and grumbled.

,What is it at this late hour...? A beautiful woman needs to keep her sleeping hours... Hurry up and finish your business and send me back.

,How dare you be so impudent.

I reached out and grabbed Machina's head and shook it up and down.

,Aaaaaaah! Ajumma! Seong Yujin is bullying me again!! Save me!!

,Mom's not here, you idiot.

,Ah, geez!!

Machina, who was getting angry, turned into a small sphere and escaped from my grasp. The sphere returned to its original form.

,Are you going to keep doing this? Just because I have a contract with you doesn't mean you can treat me carelessly.

,I let you enjoy your freedom for a while... and now you can't say anything to your master. Do you want to become canned?

Flintch.

Machina trembled.

My contract with her is unilaterally advantageous to me. She has no choice but to follow if I give an order.

,N-No, canning me is too much for just a joke. You called me because you need me, right? I'll help you with anything! What do you want me to do?

,Machina. Look at my current appearance. What do you think?

,Uh... is it cosplay?

It was the reaction I expected.

,Machina. Possess this armor.

,What? No way. I don't want to go into that weird armor.

Machina crossed her arms and turned her head away.

,Do it.

I lowered my voice and spoke threateningly. Machina distorted her face and lowered her head.

,...Okay. I'll do it. Ah, seriously. How did I end up in this situation....

Machina grumbled and possessed the armor.

One of Machina's abilities. By possessing an object, she can mechanize it.

-Oh, oh? This is a much better item than it looks! I can feel tremendous power! But why does it look like this?!

,Can you change its appearance?

-It's possible. One of the original functions of this armor is to change its appearance. That's easy. Can't you change it without my help in the first place?

I could change it.

However, I was too lazy to think about the design, so I called Machina. And I also had a desire to wear a mechanical suit.

Chwareureureuk.

The armor that covered my whole body moved quickly and changed its appearance. The body became sleek and the helmet transformed into the shape of a red devil. It was sleeker than the clown's appearance. It was like the transformation form of the main character in a tokusatsu show.

,Not bad.

-...Something's wrong with this. I was going to make it look like a Power Ranger, but why did it become a Kamen Rider? The helmet doesn't change from the devil's shape either.

I could guess why the helmet was in the shape of a red devil. It must be because of the devil's clown mask that merged with Stormbreaker.

,I can't dismantle the clown mask. It's essential to hide my identity.

Devil's clown mask.

The devil's clown mask, enhanced with the Gwangmyeong Ascension Technique, is specialized in hiding one's identity. If you take a DNA test while wearing the clown mask, the results come out strangely, and it cannot be identified with special abilities either. I already confirmed that in the [God's Atlantis] world.

,I like it, so it's fine. But are there any other functions?

-Other functions...? Like this?

A map appeared in front of my vision. In addition, it was also possible to make phone calls or surf the Internet. It was truly a super suit that combined a computer and armor.

,Good.

I used a space movement scroll to go outside the academy.
