The traitor Nakamura Jun blocked the passage. He had already drawn his katana from his waist and held it ready. The tip of his blade pointed at me. He made no effort to conceal his killing intent.

"Seong Yujin. I bear you no ill will."

"A man with no ill will wouldn't be radiating killing intent like that. You jealous of me or something?"

"Nonsense! The Shogun of the homeland wishes for you to be buried in this sea! I merely received orders from the homeland!"

"Yeah. Sure. Traitor. You keep saying 'homeland' but you're speaking Korean. Still haven't mastered Japanese yet, have you!"

"You little bastard…."

I provoked him while assessing the situation.

The tilt of the floor was different. Proof that the warship I was on was slowly sinking. This meant I'd have to pay attention to my center of gravity while fighting.

I observed Nakamura Jun. His stance with the sword looked somewhat unrefined. The belt hanging at his waist glinted with unusual brightness.

'Is that it? The Evasion Belt the government supposedly gifted him when he was in Korea.'

A Unique-grade item that allows the user to evade one dangerous attack every three days.

'That bastard's innate ability is Auto-Combat.'

As the name suggests, it automatically fights for him. Like becoming a puppet controlled by a high-performance AI, engaging in macro-driven combat. It feels like the kind of cheat skill that wouldn't be out of place on a novel protagonist.

'It really is a cheat skill. It's the ability that turned that idiot into an S-rank.'

But it wasn't an ability with only advantages.

It had a weakness.

Without combat data, the quality of Auto-Combat was poor. And that combat data required a comprehensive understanding of the target's race, weapon, environment, and more.

I pulled Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory.

"You pull a sword from thin air? A spatial ability similar to Lee Yeonhee's?"

"Similar, yeah. So your ability is Auto-Combat. How does it feel being puppeted by the System?"

"…What a pathetic ability. If you're jealous of mine, just say so. One piece of advice—you'd be better off pulling a gun than a sword. I've fought sword and blade users until I was sick of them. Even those called sword masters in Japan can't match me anymore."

"Masters? At best they're people who trained safely for twenty or thirty years."

I had honed the sword for far longer than that, dying all the while. No one could compare to me in combat experience.

"Your arrogance is astounding. Even if you dominated the world rankings up to the 20th floor, it's still only the 20th floor. You haven't even climbed to the 50th. Even if your skill is as excellent as you claim, overcoming a level gap of 40 or more is impossible. You should never have set foot on this warship from the start."

His aura shifted completely.

His killing intent diminished and his unrefined stance transformed into that of a master. Sway, sway. Even on the tilted floor, he found perfect balance at his center of gravity.

Click. Like flipping a switch, he had activated Auto-Combat. Watching his expression turn mechanical, I took my own stance.

He—no, his Auto-Combat—was wary of me. He didn't know my ability. Since it was an ability that could change the tide of battle, the Auto-Combat was on guard against it.

I moved first. Right foot forward.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流), Azure Stream (碧溪).

I mixed feints into my footwork. Creating optical illusions using angles of vision. Amateurs fall for it immediately.

His pupils flickered up and down for a moment.

'Didn't fool him. He's not just watching me with his eyes. And this space is too cramped to bait him with Azure Stream anyway.'

Even so, the narrow space worked in my favor. If it were wide open, and he went wild like a crazed monkey, it would be harder for me with my inferior physical stats.

He swung his sword. A pale white gleam faintly coated the blade. Sword aura.

My body reacted before my mind. I angled Hwaryeon Bido's blade and parried.

CHAAANG!

Sparks flew at the moment of friction. His sword glanced off to the side.

The deflection was successful. Sword aura? Even sword steel couldn't cut through my Hwaryeon Bido, so what was a bit of sword aura supposed to be?

'I actually managed to deflect without a moment's delay. The reaction speed enhancement potion is working.'

What mattered was what came next.

His sword surged forward again.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流), Gale Thunderclap (疾風迅雷).

BZZZT!

A current erupted from my spine and raced through my entire body. With my current physical stats, it put strain on my body. But without it, I couldn't keep up with his speed.

'Acceleration and a moment's delay… I don't need them.'

CHENG!

I parried and countered with a swing. The tip of my blade grazed his flank. His reaction was too agile to drive the blade in deep.

'He didn't use the Evasion Belt. So it doesn't respond to minor wounds.'

He gripped his sword with both hands. His intent was to accumulate damage even if I deflected his attacks. Draining 100% of the force was impossible no matter how skilled I was.

'He's pulling back.'

His powerfully swung blade grazed past my body. WHOOOOSH! Wind pressure stronger than a fan erupted in an instant.

'Advance again.'

It was dangerous.

If he overextended his swing, I'd have to sacrifice an arm or a leg.

'In exchange, I take his life.'

But instead of counterattacking, he retreated.

'Different from the murderous look he had earlier—he's a coward. Self-preservation is his top priority.'

It was Auto-Combat like an AI, but its operating principle was rooted in self-preservation rather than efficiency.

'Pearls before swine. The user is an idiot. He's probably thinking this: once enough data accumulates, once I tire out, he wins.'

I was indulging in a sword fight for the first time in a while, not even using Lightning Body, and this is what I get. It killed the mood.

Still, my body moved steadily. I blocked what needed blocking and countered what needed countering. Most of it was pure reaction speed that never passed through my brain.

Muscle memory? No, this is something my soul remembers.

CHAAANG!

"Damn it! My strength and speed are clearly far superior! How the hell are you holding out?!"

"Your movements are too textbook—I can see everything coming. You mix in feints here and there, but… even those are textbook."

What good was knowing how to use mana? There was no martial philosophy imbued in his blade.

'The sword's getting heavy. This should be about right.'

The moment my movements changed, he instantly retreated.

'Accumulating data before engaging means learning patterns. And when something outside the data happens, he retreats safely to observe, just like now.'

He moved exactly as I predicted. When my sword moved unpredictably, he dodged first. I used that to counter. The trajectory of my blade shifted sharply, aiming for his neck.

His belt flashed. The evasion ability had activated.

His body bent at a bizarre angle, dodging my attack.

'In this narrow passage, the evasion pattern is bound to be predictable.'

The moment his evasion ended, my blade was already piercing through his chest.

"GUUUAAGH! How?! Don't tell me your ability is seeing the future—!"

"Just prediction. Your movements are so textbook you're easier than a boss monster in a game. If you'd just trusted your physicals and charged in like a wild boar, you might've actually had a chance."

Though in that case, I would've used Lightning Body and Heaven Demon Star Sword or Diamond Lightning without holding back.

"Fuck…."

"A Japanese man's dying curse is in Korean. Heh heh."

I stripped only the belt from his corpse. It'd be a waste to throw away a Unique item.

『Evasion Belt

Grade: Unique

Automatically evades dangerous attacks.

After activation, there is a 72-hour cooldown.

Remaining cooldown: 72 hours』

'It's a lesser version of Momentary Crisis Detection. I'll give this to Lee Yeonhee. Whether she uses it herself or sells it to someone else, she'll figure it out.'

As I saw earlier, the Evasion Belt's biggest flaw is that it moves your body automatically.

You can bait the evasion movement, just like I did.

'Others might think even that is impressive, but Wiyu would say it builds bad habits and tell me to toss it immediately.'

KWAAANG! BANG!

I walked through the passage. Explosions rang out from all directions and the interior shook violently. I shifted my center of gravity back and forth, maintaining balance easily, so I never fell.

Finally, I stepped outside—and it was a battlefield. Fish-men who had crawled up onto the warship and Hunters were locked in combat, and in the sky, Chris and Garshin fought using light and seawater respectively. Garshin's ability seemed to be water manipulation.

'As expected of world-class S-ranks—flashy and powerful. Nothing compared to that traitor.'

The situation wasn't good.

The warship was gradually tilting and sinking.

"AAAAAAAAGH!"

SPLASH!

A Hunter fell into the sea with a scream.

Dozens of man-eating sharks swarmed from beneath the water and devoured the Hunter. It was like being thrown into a wood chipper, not an ocean.

'Dozens of man-eating sharks being here is strange. Is controlling fish also an innate ability? Or a racial trait?'

One thing was certain—if the warship sank completely, it was over.

In the sea, we couldn't beat them.

'Fish-men, roughly 300 or more. About 200 are watching the warship with only their heads above water. Are they waiting for it to sink?'

That's when it happened. Something wrapped in light came flying toward me from the sky.

[Using Momentary (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

I dodged to the side urgently.

KWAAAAANG!

The light that crashed into the iron wall was America's S-rank Hunter, Chris. He had the innate ability to manipulate light, and had wrapped himself in it for protection.

Thud.

Chris collapsed, bleeding. I examined him with my eyes. His eyes were open, but his body wouldn't move. His mouth wouldn't open either—only his pupils darted around.

'Paralysis poison?'

A faint green light seeped from his right hand. The green light was absorbed into him. It seemed to be healing his condition.

'A recovery-type Unique-grade glove. As expected of someone backed by America—he's got expensive gear.'

I looked straight ahead at a powerful presence.

Atop a swirling, towering pillar of seawater stood Garshin, holding a spear. A purple liquid coated the spearhead. It was the paralysis poison that had afflicted Chris. He looked at the fallen Chris and grinned.

"Strongest human on Earth. It seems the match is decided. I acknowledge it. You fought well, worthy of a great warrior of Earth."

Garshin leaped lightly onto the warship. He walked toward Chris with heavy, deliberate steps to finish him off, grinning all the while.

"However, if that's all your level amounts to, then a nation like America could be conquered with ease. The strong devour the weak, and powerful nations conquer weak ones. That's common sense in this world too, is it not? Accept it humbly. Our world is 99.9% ocean, so land resources are severely lacking—we'll cull your numbers, but we won't completely exterminate humanity."

I stepped in front of the fallen Chris.

"I can't let you kill this one here. If he dies, only China benefits."

Garshin looked me over from head to toe once.

"Hmm? A low-rank Hunter? Your loyalty toward your superior is admirable. I'll praise you for not running at the sight of me."

"What the hell are you saying, you fish bastard. The stink of fish is reaching all the way over here."

"Your manner of speech is vulgar. Is it bravado to hide your terror? It's almost pitiable."

"I'm the idiot for trying to talk to a fish. Seong Jigon. You'll do."

『Using 1,000 coins to summon Seong Jigon.』

Seong Jigon appeared in full armament and drew his sword the instant he materialized.
