The moment the claws of the Oduksi clashed with the cutlass blade, I knew it instinctively.

I can't win with strength alone. If I keep holding on, my wrist will be smashed. No, it's already half-twisted. Reflexively, I twisted the blade to deflect his claws.

The Oduksi, who had put his weight on his claws, rolled several times behind me and collided with the knights behind me.

I looked at my right wrist and clicked my tongue. It was completely wrecked. It's only this bad because I'm wearing a combat suit.

'If I didn't have the combat suit, my entire right arm would have been screwed, not just my wrist.'

While recovering my wrist, I watched them. I thought about rushing in, but decided to observe. The more information I have about that bastard, the easier it will be to catch him.

The Oduksi attacked three knights. The three knights, who could be called human weapons, were naturally not just taking it.

The knights are combat weapons hardened in death. Weapons that do not fear death confront the monster. Even if they can't see, they rely on their senses to unleash the Ultimate Martial Arts I bestowed upon them. Guns, swords, grenades. A combat method that uses everything available. Even if it means their own limbs are broken, they don't stop.

Tang, tang, tang!

The pistol didn't work. Even fired at point-blank range, it couldn't penetrate the Oduksi's skin. Still, seeing it turn red means the damage isn't completely nonexistent.

'They said those special armor-piercing bullets could kill most bastards in one shot. The power is weak, isn't it?'

Bang!

With the power of the combat suit and the effects of the drugs, he barely managed to limit some of the Oduksi's movements.

,Now's the time! Detonate all the grenades! I will self-destruct! For Seongnim!

Kwaaaang!

,Kieeeeeeek!

,It's effective.

,Even if we can't kill him, we can inflict damage.

,Don't self-destruct. Fight with your swords.

Naturally, the Oduksi had the advantage in this primitive battle.

I watch their battle and confirm the Oduksi's patterns.

The speed at which he swings his claws, the angle of his arm moving before he swings his claws, and how he balances his body, etc. I familiarize myself with the Oduksi's characteristics and patterns as much as possible.

'The most annoying thing is that tail. It's as sharp as a knife, but he uses it like a whip. Can I call it a chain sword? The problem is that it's hidden by his large body, so it's hard to see when fighting head-on.'

He swings it like a whip. But it's not as long as a whip.

'I don't know if I don't know. I knew how to use it in battle. All I have to do is adjust the distance well.'

Unlike the knights, my two eyes can see well. A plan was devised in my head.

The knights, who fought with combat suits, Zena's advice, and the power of drugs, eventually fell and died. The Oduksi, whose skin was torn here and there, unlike his hard claws, looked at me covered in blood and slowly approached.

'I'm glad he doesn't have any annoying thoughts of running away.'

There's no need to be obsessed with capturing him alive. Even just those pieces of his corpse can be resurrected.

-"Do you need support by any chance?"

I stretched out my left hand towards the charging bastard. Flashing lightning instantly rotated and compressed.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Manroe (卍 Thunder).

Bang!

An explosion rang out, but there was no expected electric shock effect. All he did was pause for a moment. I tried to go easy, but I failed. Rather, he seemed to be even more enraged and kicked off the ground and leaped.

An attack with his weight on his claws, just like before.

Kkang!

A perfect parry.

But my foot moved back slightly. I can't help it. The difference in strength was too great to withstand it with my lower body. Even if I parried perfectly, it doesn't mean there's no strain on my body.

Kkang! Kkang, kkaang, kkaang! Kkaang, kkaang!

'Looking at that bastard's claws, his solid bones must be stronger than steel. But what about his cartilage and ligaments?'

Pajijik.

I stimulate my entire body's muscles with lightning and swing my sword.

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷光).

The Oduksi has four arms, but that's just how he looks. In reality, the lower arms act as legs. This greatly reduced the bastard's mobility.

The Oduksi used his ability strongly. My vision flickered momentarily and then returned. I had been using my recovery ability continuously since earlier.

The turn has passed.

The Oduksi attacks. This time, he even used his tail. Seeing him use his tail, which he used like a hidden card, in this way, he must be cornered and agitated.

Reduced mobility. An attack that has become much simpler, only focused on strength.

On the other hand, I've dealt with a lot of bastards like this.

Now I don't even need to parry every attack. I parried some attacks while evading others. Before long, the bastard's rhythm collapses.

Even if his limbs and tail can regenerate over time, it doesn't matter. I just need to keep cutting them off before they regenerate.
