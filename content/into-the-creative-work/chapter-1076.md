From outside the wall, Li Mei was hitting the wall, opening and closing her mouth repeatedly. It looked like she was shouting something, but I couldn't hear anything. I smiled at her and waved my hand.

After 30 seconds, the space completely changed. Li Mei's figure was also gone.

「Area erosion is complete.」

「5,845th area, where heavy gazes reach.」

「All stats decrease by 20%.」

「The heavy eye smiles pleasantly.」

「The Lord of the Sky clicks his tongue.」

「The King of the Demonic Sky grumbles.」

Area erosion.

There could be several reasons, but the biggest cause of area erosion is when the domain of a God's Seat erodes the area of Atlantis.

Qing Guoli probably tried to erode the area using the power of the God's Seat called Heavy Eye.

'Qing Guoli must have made a deal with the God's Seat, Heavy Eye. To openly carry out something like this... the God's Seat Heavy Eye is no ordinary God's Seat. It must be at least a God-level.'

It's hard to handle the burden of a Hero-grade God's Seat. The system is probably busy trying to keep the God's Seat Heavy Eye in check.

'Heavy Eye. Heavy Eye.... It's a name not in the setting book... What's its true name?'

I wracked my brain, but it wasn't easy to deduce the true name just from the name of the God's Seat.

"C-Cheonma-nim!"

Someone called me. I turned my head and saw the Black Blood Alliance men who had been running away with us.

"What is it."

"Please, please send us out too! We will definitely repay your kindness!"

"It's too late. You can't get out of here now."

I reached out my hand forward. There was nothing to touch. The wall that had been blocking us was already gone. This place had already become a different space. The land was barren, and the sky was dark. To leave this place, it was necessary to conquer it.

「A special situation has occurred.」

「To escape the 5,845th area, where heavy gazes reach, you must eliminate the medium of erosion.」

「The penalty disappears. Stats return to normal.」

The original stats that had decreased by 20% returned.

Kukung, kukukukung. Buildings that were no different from ruins soar on the desolate black earth.

It seems that the system and the God's Seat Heavy Eye have negotiated appropriately. Judging from the lack of reaction from the Lord of the Sky or the King of the Demonic Sky, it seems to be at a satisfactory level.

'The system must be having a hard time too.'

Not all gods are cooperative with the system. Rather, the opposite is true. There are plenty of gods who try to troll whenever they get the chance. I almost feel sorry for the system that has to control all those bastards.

"P-Please, for fuck's sake. Please help us!"

A man fell over and grabbed one of my legs. I felt a surge of annoyance, but I held it in.

"Let go."

"I know I'm being shameless! But... we need your help! Please help us!"

The man was quick-witted. He realized that I was planning to abandon them and go somewhere else. But now that the situation had turned out like this, there was no reason for me to take these guys with me. Would taking them be helpful? What a joke. It would be enough if they just didn't get in the way.

I drew my sword and cut off the arm and head of the man who was grabbing my leg. The man died instantly on the spot.

"Aaaaaaaagh!"

"Y-You crazy bastard!!"

"Is this... betrayal!"

The others recoil in horror.

"Does this look like betrayal? From the beginning, you and I had no relationship. Don't get in my way. If you want to live, stick together and figure it out on your own."

Ignoring them as they stared at me with dumbfounded eyes, I moved to a suitable place. First, I plan to quietly wander around and observe the situation.

'I sent Li Mei to Elena, so if I hold out, Elena will come.'

I suddenly raised my head and looked at the black pillar. The black pillar was still emitting ominous waves.

"I'm hungry.... I'm hungry...!!"

Wang Xuan is chasing after me. Unlike his huge body, his speed is quite fast. I clicked my tongue and ran quickly. Wang Xuan smashed everything that blocked his path as he chased after me. Even a huge rock over 30M couldn't stop him.

As I ran away, I threw a spear at him when I had a reasonable distance. The spear pierced his body accurately, but he didn't die.

'I feel like I've repeated this shit at least 50 times.... He doesn't even get tired.'

The most annoying thing was that I couldn't kill him.

"How long do you think you can keep running away?"

Qing Guoli's voice echoed from the black pillar. That bastard knew my location.

"I think I can run away for another week?"

It's only been two days. To be honest, it was starting to get tough. That's because Wang Xuan wasn't the only one chasing me.

Kururururu.

I felt a slight vibration from the ground. I focused my senses. A black hand popped out of the ground and tried to grab my leg. I avoided them all, but if I happened to get caught, I immediately swung my sword and cut off the hand.

"I'm hungryyyyyyy!!"

Wang Xuan eats the black hands and increases his speed.

I see a cliff in front of me. Beyond that is another land. I ran towards the cliff.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

I used Cheonma Gunrimbo with all my might. I walk in the air, walking along the cliff. Wang Xuan, who was mindlessly chasing me, fell down the cliff. Kwahang! A heavy sound echoed. Looking down, Wang Xuan was wriggling.

'I've bought myself some time with this.'

Wang Xuan will get up again and chase me. I had to keep running away or conquer this area.

'The medium of erosion is obvious. It's that black pillar.'

It's not easy to approach the black pillar that emits black waves. The black waves were deadly. If you get close, your life force will be taken away.

"Haha. You bought some time. Does it feel good to be able to live a little longer? What are you doing? Aren't you going to run away?"

Qing Guoli's voice echoes again from the black pillar.

"...Qing Guoli. You've been talking a lot since yesterday. Why aren't you moving yourself?"

"You can't even handle Wang Xuan, do you think I need to move myself? The moment you get tired, Wang Xuan will devour you."

I narrowed my eyes and glared at the black pillar.

"You can't move from the black pillar. What are you doing?"

"Do I have to tell you that?"

"You better get rid of the idea that I'll just keep running away. I'm going to go and kill you."

"You'll have to kill Wang Xuan first. I'll give you a hint, appreciating your spirit. Wang Xuan can have himself killed in place of the souls of the humans he ate. Do you understand? You've killed at least 1,000 souls."

"That's not helpful."

"Haha. You don't have a shred of guilt? Good. I'll give you a more useful hint. Wang Xuan will die if you kill him 7,141 more times."

I lowered my head. Wang Xuan was clinging to the cliff, trying to climb up. But because of his heavy body, he couldn't climb the cliff and kept falling down.

'I have to kill that thing 7,141 times? Bullshit.'

A few hundred times would be one thing, but thousands of times would be no small feat. Putting aside getting tired, I don't have time for that.

I turned around and started running away. I don't even know where I'm going.

Five hours later, a new notification window popped up.

「The 5,845th area has eroded the 5,846th area.」

「The erosion rate increases.」

'Is the erosion not ending and eroding nearby areas?'

It was not a good sign.

「The dead rise.」

Undead appeared on the black land. The undead wandered around everywhere. The undead who found me revealed their hatred for the living and rushed at me.

Pajijijijik.

「Acquire 5 AP.」

「Acquire 2 AP.」

「Acquire 6 AP.」

......

At first, it was close to a neat cylinder. But now, the surface is mottled like a living creature. It looked like black blood vessels were attached to the cylinder.

'Wang Xuan hasn't been chasing me since some point. Has he completely given up on chasing me?'

Just in case, I took out a space movement scroll and tore it.

「The space movement scroll cannot be used.」

As expected.
