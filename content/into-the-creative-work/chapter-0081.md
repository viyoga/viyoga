"Yujin-gongja-nim. Is something the matter?"

I looked at the old butler standing neatly before me.

Hasent Potan.

The head butler of the Prukerus family, who holds the title of Baron.

He was in his sixties, with snow-white hair neatly swept back from his forehead. His face was full of wrinkles that couldn't be hidden, but there wasn't a single wrinkle to be found on the black butler's uniform he was wearing.

Hasent had been working as a butler for the Prukerus barony since the time of the previous head of the family. Not only the current head of the family, but none of the vassals could treat him carelessly.

'He was also an assassin who silently carried out the dirty work of the previous head of the family.'

He was the master of the now-defunct assassination group 'Fatal Sting.'

'In the original story, he's described as being incredibly strong, but he retires without much activity.'

The only reason for his retirement is that he's old. After that, he's not even mentioned in the original story.

"Hasent-jipsa-jang. I have a favor to ask."

Hasent is a neutral figure who isn't interested in the politics within the family, but I can't ignore him. If I were to displease Hasent and turn him into my enemy... there's nothing more terrible than that.

"To me, you say?"

"Yes. Actually, while reading a book in the study... I learned about Hasent-jipsa-jang's true identity."

I tensed up here. It's because Hasent might express displeasure. But, contrary to my expectations, Hasent didn't even twitch an eyebrow. He simply nodded calmly.

"I see."

It was a simple answer. I even suspected that he didn't have any emotions at all.

However, I was startled by what he said next.

"Does Yujin-gongja-nim want to become an assassin?"

"...Huh?"

"I could sense a slight assassin's aura from the way you walk. I apologize if I was mistaken."

He's not mistaken.

I've been walking around lately using the Giunbo of the Yeongcheon style. Jin Se-yeong told me that it was a way to train in everyday life. I'm thinking of quitting because the training effect doesn't seem to be that great.

"...It's true that I'm interested in assassins, but that's not why I called you, Jipsa-jang."

"Then what did you call me for?"

"Does Jipsa-jang know a lot about the underworld?"

"...Gongja-nim. The underworld isn't a good place. Is there someone you want to kill, perhaps?"

Hasent said those chilling words without changing his expression.

"No. It's not that. I personally want to use the information guild."

Hasent's eyes changed slightly at my words. It was the first time I had seen him change.

"......The information guild isn't a very good place. Just using it can lead to gossip."

I know.

It's called a guild that specializes in information. The reality is that it's just a group of criminals. If rumors spread that a noble used the information guild, that itself would be a blemish on their reputation.

'But the funny thing is that everyone secretly uses the information guild. Some nobles even sponsor the information guild.'

The conclusion is that it's fine as long as you don't get caught.

"I know. But isn't the information guild the most convenient place? Jipsa-jang. I'm too suffocated in the mansion. I also want to know information about the outside world."

"If Gongja-nim wishes, the servants will tell you stories about the outside world. There's also a way to call adventurers or mercenaries to listen to their stories."

"Those would be shallow stories without depth. I want to know stories about the deep parts of the world."

"......."

The butler looked at me. His gray eyes were like sharply honed daggers.

"......I understand. Since you want to use the information guild... I will personally go to the information guild and buy the information that Gongja-nim wants."

"You're going to move yourself, Jipsa-jang? I'd be fine with just one butler."

"Unfortunately, there are only a few butlers under me that I can trust. It's better for me to move myself."

That was a bit unexpected. But I soon nodded.

It's obvious that the butler is more trustworthy than most guys.

"However, a considerable amount of money is needed to use the information guild."

"Would this be enough?"

I took out a money pouch from my pocket. I slightly opened the pouch to show him the inside. Inside the pouch were sparkling gold and blue silver coins.

One gold coin is 500,000 Nergo, and one blue silver coin is 300,000 Nerda.

Eight gold coins. Four blue silver coins. A total of 5.2 million Nerda.

Considering that the monthly income of the middle class is about 2.5 million Ner, it's quite a large sum of money.

"That's quite a lot of money. May I ask where it came from?"

"I sold the things I invented to the knights of the Plun Knights."

I sold cigarettes and beer. Especially cigarettes were much more popular than I thought. 50,000 Ner per pack of cigarettes.

'If I convert it to real money, I sold a pack of cigarettes for about 80,000 won.'

In my eyes, it was an absurd price, but the knights bought it without any complaints. In the first place, there were no rolled cigarettes in this world, so they didn't know the appropriate price.

'I could have sold it for a higher price. But cigarettes are not only sold to knights but also to middle-class customers....'

Once you're addicted to cigarettes, there's no turning back, so they'll continue to sell. If possible, I hope they smoke 10 packs a day.

"...With this much, you should be able to get decent information. What kind of information do you want?"

"I want information about the current political situation of our Rapelli Kingdom and the Helbrit Duchy."

"The kingdom's political situation is fine, but... with this amount of money, you can only find out a small amount of information about the Helbrit Duchy. At best, only the information that is known on the surface."

"That's enough."

That's exactly what I wanted. I'm not interested in the kingdom's secret information. It won't be of much help even if I get it now. Furthermore, I know most of it through the original story.

There's no need to worry about the Magic Tower yet.

"I understand."

"Ah. And I want to find someone."

"Someone, you say?"

"I want a professional torture technician."

"......Yes?"

Hasent's eyes widened. My words seemed really unexpected.

"I want to hire a torture technician. Not as the barony, but as me personally."

"Is there a need for a torture technician...?"

"There might be."

"...This is something I have to report to the Madam."

"I'll tell my mother."

I'll get an earful, but there's no problem. Originally, noble families with some power have torture technicians.

Of course, the Prukerus barony also has a torture technician. However, they are not currently in the mansion and are living in the border city of Utrehn with the head of the family, Baron Prukerus.

"I understand. Is there anything else you want me to do?"

"I want to know the location of Artel Village and the Peupreuk Mercenary Group."

"Those are names I've never heard of."

"Artel Village is a village of runaways located in the outermost area of the Evil Garden's flood."

"A village of runaways? It seems that Doryeon-nim knows a lot about that village."

"......I only know it by name, as I've heard it in passing."

I made up an excuse. Hasent nodded without digging deeper.

"I understand. However, it seems that more money will be needed."

"How much more is needed?"

"It depends on the skill of the Peupreuk Mercenary Group."

"They're not great guys. At best, they're C-class."

"Then it seems that about 3 million Ner will be needed in addition."

"Here it is."

I immediately took out another money pouch. The money inside is 5 million Ner. Adding it to what I gave Hasent earlier, it can be said to be my entire fortune.

"3 million should be enough."

"You can keep the rest, Jipsa-jang."

I can earn as much money as I want in the future. If I can gain the butler's favor with just a few million Ner, it's very cheap.

"I can't do that. I will definitely return the balance."

It didn't work at all.

"Ah. Jipsa-jang. I heard that there's a prison near the Knights' training ground."

"There is, but... it hasn't been used for 40 years."

Originally, I was going to go alone secretly using Illusiter. But I was blocked from the entrance of the prison. The entrance was locked, and Hasent was managing the key.

"I want to take a look."

"We clean it occasionally, but it's just a formality. It's not a very hygienic place."

"I just want to take a look. The prison is the only place in the mansion that I haven't been to."

"......."

Hasent looked at me suspiciously. I just said that I wanted to have a torture technician under my command, and now I said that I wanted to go to the prison, so... it was natural to be suspicious.

"......I understand. Instead, go with a butler."

"Thank you, Jipsa-jang."

•••

"Yujin-gongja-nim. You're just going to look around. If you stay here for a long time, I'll get scolded by Hasent-nim."

A young butler said in front of the prison. He was a man who looked only around 17 years old at best. He was trembling in front of the prison, as if he was very scared.

I understand. The prison door leading to the basement is neglected and exudes a gloomy atmosphere.

"I know. I'm just going to look around and come out."

"You promised."

The young butler put the key in the door and released the lock. He pushed open the iron door of the prison with a groan. He was surprisingly strong.

I could see the stairs leading down. There was no proper light, so it was dark.

"Ugh. I came here two years ago, and nothing has changed."

The butler held a lamp and slowly went down the stairs.

Squeak squeak.

A mouse was surprised by the sudden appearance of a person and quickly ran out.

"Eek!"

The butler was startled and screamed.

I clicked my tongue briefly. Why did Hasent attach such a dimwit to me? Is he trying to screw me over? Did I do something wrong to him?

"G-Gongja-nim. It's a dangerous place, so how about holding hands?"

The butler turned to me and asked. I waved my hand to mean "don't talk nonsense and keep going."

"Go quickly."

I frowned and said with annoyance. Hold hands with a guy? I'm not crazy enough to do that.

"Ugh...."

The butler eventually made a crying face and moved forward.

The atmosphere became more gloomy as we went down. The ventilation was somewhat good, but there were many bugs and mice because it wasn't managed properly. There were especially many cockroaches and spiders crawling on the walls and floors.

Even if they clean it occasionally, they must be doing it carelessly. It's a place that's not used anyway.

'There's a prison in the territory separately in the guard or court side.'

Most criminals are taken to the guard and court prisons to be dealt with. There is no separate prison. Heinous guys are executed, and minor crimes are mostly ended with fines. If it's ambiguous to execute them, they often fall into the status of slaves.

'From the perspective of the nobles, the slave sentence is good. Labor is a valuable resource.'

The reason I came to this prison is not to look around. It's to get the ancient artifact deep inside this prison.

'Originally, it's the ancient artifact that the main character, Kyle Prukerus, gets for the first time.'

I'm going to have this and lend it to Kyle occasionally from the original story's point of view. This is a somewhat important item for Kyle.

'I have to avoid making Kyle an hopeless overpowered character.'

But if you exclude Kyle's power, there's no other use for it. Kyle has to become somewhat strong to be able to stand up to the eldest son, Jent.

'When they fight each other. I take the fisherman's profit. Keueut~! What a perfect plan!'

I imagine the situation where the Prukerus territory comes into my hands.

First of all, in the name of Baron Prukerus, I'll exercise the right of first night and sleep with all the beauties in the territory....

"Gongja-nim! It seems like you've looked around enough, shouldn't we go back?"

My happy circuit stopped. I frowned and looked at the butler disapprovingly.

"I haven't gone all the way to the end yet. Keep going."

The prison is getting dirtier as I go inside. It's evidence that the servants cleaned it roughly.

And before long. I arrived at the deepest place.

It's the prison where Kyle from the original story is trapped. It was also the largest prison among the current prisons.

The rusty iron bars were not locked and were open. It's questionable whether it can properly do the work of a prison.

'Well, Kyle from the original story is just formally trapped.'

I went inside the prison.

"Gongja-nim?!"

"No. I was wondering what it feels like to be in prison. Come in too. It's quite a strange feeling."

"Ah, no. I'll wait here."

The terrified butler shook his head. I smiled and headed towards the corner. Fortunately, it's a place where the light of the lamp doesn't reach.

'There should be a gap in the wall.'

I found it.

I reached out my small hand and put the small key in the gap into my arms.

"So this is what a prison looks like~. I roughly understand. Let's go back."
