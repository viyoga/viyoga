,Tell the 2nd Company of the 3rd Battalion to defend the sub-engine, and the 3rd Company, along with the maintenance unit, go to the main engine room to assess the status of the main engine and report back."

,Yes, Captain-nim."

In the bridge, Max ordered his adjutant, Emma. Emma hurriedly left the bridge and issued orders to the soldiers. The soldiers who received the orders moved quickly.

It was very inefficient, but there was no other choice. Since the radio was blocked, they had to use people as messengers.

Max bit his lip while looking at the information on the monitor. Cold sweat ran down his forehead. He was desperately racking his brain to respond to the situation.

,Captain-nim. The armor in the commercial area has been breached, and Gestalgs have infiltrated."

Emma, who had returned, reported.

,Lower the isolation walls in the corridor connected to the commercial area."

,The system connected to the commercial area is broken. Remote control is impossible."

,...Order the 5th Battalion to manually lower the isolation walls."

He knew that the isolation walls would only buy time. But if he didn't buy even that time, the situation would become even more desperate. The commercial area was like a crossroads, so he couldn't leave it alone.

,What happened to the messengers sent to the medical area?"

,Ten minutes have passed, but there's no news. It seems the medical area has already been occupied by those bastards."

,...Damn it. I regret not giving the marines the awakening stimulants in advance."

,According to military law, the distribution of awakening stimulants is only allowed in combat situations."

He never thought he would be ambushed by the Gestalg bastards and forced into a combat situation. Frankly, he was negligent because supply lines were rarely attacked.

,I know. I was just complaining. How many hours until we reach Planet Locke 3?"

,At the current speed, we should be able to enter the atmosphere of Planet Locke 3 within the next two hours."

,Can we connect with the outpost on Planet Locke 3?"

,We're still trying, but communication itself is impossible."

,...We need to consider several scenarios."

,What scenarios are you talking about?"

Emma asked with a stiff expression.

,The worst-case scenario is that one of the two sub-engines gets destroyed. It'll be impossible to enter Planet Locke 3 itself. There won't be enough output. In that case, I'll blow up the sub-engine and go down with the ship."

,......."

All the soldiers in the bridge were silent.

,I was a bit too extreme. If we can safely land on Planet Locke 3 and get help from the outpost, everyone can survive. Let's have hope."

One hour later.

Tang tang tang tang tang!

Gunshots rang out near the bridge. The Gestalg bugs had finally approached the bridge. Of course, there was an elite unit guarding the bridge, so they would hold out to some extent.

Max stared intently at the monitor. They were getting closer and closer to Planet Locke 3. And he could still see the space monster clinging to the Forte ship. It was a damn nuisance.

,...Since the fighters are all destroyed, there's no way to get rid of that damn thing. Even if both sub-engines are intact, it'll be impossible to land safely on the planet with that thing attached."

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The ship shook from the strong impact. Even those in the relatively safe bridge barely managed to keep their balance. Some hit their heads on the machines and bled. Max used his telekinetic powers to fix his body and Emma's body in place to maintain balance.

,What's going on?"

,Captain-nim! An explosion occurred in areas 17 through 21, and they've been blown off! We can't maintain our course!"

,There was an armory in area 19. It exploded, huh? We're screwed. Is it lucky that the sub-engines are intact...? No matter how I think about it, we can't successfully land with the ship in its current state. We're abandoning ship. Everyone, prepare to evacuate."

,...Captain-nim. We'll just die if we evacuate into space now."

,Adjutant Emma. Do you think I don't know that? We need to evacuate to Planet Locke 3, not into space. We need to evacuate while entering the atmosphere to avoid being disturbed by the Gestalgs. It might be possible using drop pods. We'll have to pray that we land near the outpost. Ah, do you have any other good ideas? If you do, I'll actively consider them. Speak freely."

,......."

Emma and the other officers were silent.

,Then everyone, arm yourselves. There are 120 elite soldiers... It's a long way to the A area drop pod bay, so not everyone will be able to get there. Be prepared."

,Understood, Captain-nim."

The soldiers gathered around Max.

,Kieeeek?!"

Tang tang tang!

I shot a bullet into the Soldier bug's head. The Soldier bug bled blue blood and collapsed. I stepped over the bug's corpse and entered the second armory.

,Originally, I should be farming in the third armory... Things don't always go according to plan."

There were more bugs than expected in the first armory, so it took longer.

,I have one hour left. I might have to revise the plan."

I went inside the second armory. The second armory mainly had heavy weapons. There was a lot of red and blue blood. First, I collected all the corpses of the space marines, as I had done so far.

,Cough. You have... amazing psychic powers."

One of the marines, who I thought was dead, spoke. Both of his legs were cut off.

,This voice... I think I've heard it before."

,Have you ever seen such a shitty bastard...? Can't you see the band on my right arm?"

,Ah. It's that instructor who got his ass kicked by me in the cafeteria last time. What are you doing here?"

His name was definitely George Johnson. His rank was captain.

A supporting character in the original story. Originally, he should be escorting the protagonist, Max, right now.

,Is it strange for a soldier to be in the operation area?"

,Was this your operation area?"

,You have no respect for your superiors. Never mind. It's my fault for expecting something from a shitty guy like you. My patrol area has been changed."

I roughly understood.

It was probably a kind of punishment. Because he fought with me and got his ass kicked. Instead of letting the matter slide, he took on a troublesome task. Or he simply fell out of Max's favor.

,Okay. Good luck."

I was going to take a lap around the armory. By then, he'd probably be dead and a corpse.

,I have a favor to ask."

,You want me to save you? I'm busy here. I'm not a medic, and the medical area has been occupied by the bugs."

,It's not that kind of favor. I know I can't survive. I also know that you have a low chance of surviving."

,Are you asking me to kill you?"

I pointed the gun at his helmet. I didn't have any particular ill feelings towards George Johnson. I didn't like him when he picked a fight with me back then, but I cleanly stabbed him, so it was over.

,If possible... please take my corpse back to Earth. I want to be buried in the soil of Earth, not in space. ...It would be even better if you killed me. I'll give you Lieutenant Colonel Tanaka's rank insignia in return."

,Who is Lieutenant Colonel Tanaka?"

,You really are a shitty guy. He's our ship's supply officer. His personal code can open all doors except the captain's office. It can also open the doors to the armory next to the armory and the garage. It looks like you came to get weapons.... If you have this, you can get permission to use most of the equipment. Don't you need it?"

,It means I don't have to bother searching other officers' corpses. Okay. I'll grant your request."

,...My will is stored in my power suit."

,I'm not sure I'll be able to return to Earth either."

,You'll be able to go. I have a feeling. ...Now, end it."

Tang tang tang!

I put his corpse in my inventory. I held Lieutenant Colonel Tanaka's rank insignia in my hand.

,I won't have to waste time because I can't open doors."

I started putting the weapons from the armory into my inventory. This armory mainly had heavy weapons.

,Oh. This is. Vibro Sword."

It was a sword that maximized the cutting power of the blade based on the principle of vibration.

It wasn't the marine's standard weapon. It was a personal weapon that could only be possessed with the permission of superiors.

,Six of them? That's quite a lot. It's much better than a knife, so of course, I'll take them."

I swept up all the ammunition too. It was only half empty, but it was more than enough for an individual.

I also went into the garage next to the armory. Unlike the relatively intact armory, it was a mess. Parts were scattered everywhere.

,Kekeke. Tanks, armored vehicles, even military vehicles, this is awesome."

I put them in my inventory.

I was worried about ownership, but it seemed like everyone who was going to die had already died, so I obtained two tanks, one armored vehicle, and four military vehicles without any ownership issues.

There might be vehicles in other garages, but this is enough for now.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The garage wall collapsed, and a giant bug with a size of 7m appeared. It was covered in a hemispherical black shell. There was a long horn in the center of its head. It was a bug that mainly charged with its defense. It was almost impossible to kill it with an assault rifle.

Even if bullets hit its body, they wouldn't reach the organs deep inside. This guy's brain and heart were located in the innermost part of its body.

Its body was covered in wounds. It seemed to have already experienced several small-scale skirmishes.

,There are no other bugs. Is it just one? I don't need to use the awakening stimulant."

The bug ran towards me as soon as it saw me. A 7m-sized armored vehicle charging at full speed.

,It's too late to dodge to the side."

I faced the bug and ran straight ahead. We were running towards each other. Even if I was wearing a power suit, I would die if I collided with it head-on.

,Its legs are thick and wide. If I try to slip underneath, I'll probably get trampled."

I stepped on the bug's horn and jumped upwards. I nonchalantly succeeded in a dangerous feat that would have killed me instantly if I had mistimed it even slightly.

I took out an attachable bomb from my inventory, attached it to the bug's wounded back shell, and jumped down.

Koong!

The bug, which had crashed into the wall, staggered for a moment and turned its body.

,It took 10 seconds."

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

The bomb exploded, and half of the bug's torso was blown away. Even if its heart and brain were intact, there was no way it could be okay with half of its body blown away. The bug scattered flesh and blue blood and collapsed on the floor.

,The location of the next third armory is... no. I've stockpiled enough weapons and ammunition. I'll skip the third armory. Instead, it's better to get a generator. It'll be convenient in many ways if I can use electricity on the ground."

I headed to the main engine room with the intention of taking a few generators. There was a power generation facility near the engine room.

,Kieeeeeeeek!"

I encountered Gestalgs along the way. I couldn't detour, so I fought with guns and swords.

I killed dozens of bugs and arrived at the main engine room with 30 minutes left.

The space monster's torso was attached to the wall of the main engine room like a wall. Thanks to that, I didn't have to worry about going out into space.

,Is that the space monster's mouth? The bugs must have infiltrated inside through there. Fortunately, no more are coming out. They must be wandering around the ship fighting."

Conversely, the main engine room was deserted. Of course, I had no intention of attacking the space monster that was acting as a wall. If that thing fell off the ship, I would be sucked out into space with it.

I tore the half-broken generators from the floor and put them in my inventory.

I was about to go back, but I saw an engine that was broken in the center of the main engine room.

,Was it called an antimatter engine?"

As the ship's main engine, it was damn big. But I could see a blue glowing mechanical sphere between the broken engines.

,I have a feeling. That must be a kind of core. It seems to be working even though there are cracks on the surface."

I had to loosen the machines that were holding the engine core in place first.

Wiiiiiiing.

I approached the engine with the vibrating Vibro Sword.

10 minutes passed.

I obtained the engine core. It didn't matter that it was cracked.

,If I put it in my inventory, it'll automatically repair itself. I don't know how long it'll take, though."

I started running to the A area drop pod bay.

Mari would be waiting. If I was lucky, I might meet Max and his group along the way.
