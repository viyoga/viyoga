If they were low-level divine seats, I would have thought, "Whatever."

The Clock Tower Killer. Jack the Ripper had good reason to run away. If he had been caught in the middle and incurred the Lord of the Sky's displeasure, it would have been a very big deal.

I scratched the back of my head. This was lucky for me. In particular, the Lord of the Sky wanting a formal contract is luckier than winning the lottery.

'...Hmm.'

First of all, a formal contract with a divine seat is unconditionally beneficial. If the opportunity arises, it's right to make a contract. That's because you can get special skills or traits by contracting with a divine seat. Also, the contracted divine seat usually provides steady support, so you can quickly become stronger in Atlantis.

Since the contract is made through the Atlantis system, it's not unfair at all. Exiles can gain power, and the more the contracted exile is active in Atlantis and exerts influence, the more divine power and AP the divine seat can obtain. Think of it as an investment concept.

However, unlike divine seats that can contract with multiple people, an exile can only contract with one divine seat, so you have to choose carefully.

'The King of Machun must be a psycho. The one I should choose here is the Lord of the Sky.'

In Atlantis, there are countless exiles who have not contracted with a divine seat. It's right to contract with the Lord of the Sky here. It's right, but....

'The Lord of the Sky. That bastard's real name is Zeus.'

The supreme god of Olympus. If you only consider the power he possesses, he is one of the top 10 gods in Atlantis.

'The problem is that he's also a psycho, though not as much as the King of Machun....'

As you can see from the mission he set to defile Ju Seo-hyeon a while ago. The Lord of the Sky is quite the pervert.

「The Lord of the Sky urges you to choose.」

"A formal contract... doesn't that mean I can only choose one of you? I don't know anything about either of you, so I don't know what to choose."

I must not be blinded by the power of the divine seat and think calmly.

The fact that they offered a formal contract right after the tutorial means that I am worth that much to them. In other words, there is a possibility that I can contract with other divine seats even in Atlantis. I am a product with excellent value, and they are the buyers. They are the ones who are desperate, not me. The initiative is on my side.

It was a husky woman's voice. Sponsoring 10,000 AP was probably to make a good impression on me.

The King of Machun is definitely generous. She's also a psycho, though.
