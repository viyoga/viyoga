The artificial intelligence main unit, slashed by the hyper-vibration blade and burned by lightning, was in a disastrous state. The surface was charred black, and the metal inside had melted. A pungent smoke stung my nose.

I clicked my tongue. The condition for using "Recovery" was contact with the target. Touching it in that state would be a 100% burn.

"Pour water on it."

One of the researchers quickly filled a bucket with water and poured it on the artificial intelligence main unit.

Chi-i-i-i-i-ik!

Steam rose from the main unit. Thanks to that, the temperature dropped rapidly. I reached out to the mangled main unit. There was still heat, but not enough to cause a burn.

'Recovery.'

As if rewinding time, the main unit returned to its original state. The water that had been poured on the main unit seemed to have been affected by the recovery, as it vaporized and disappeared. The main unit, returned to its perfect state, emitted light and spun around.

"Seong Yujin. A healing ability user. A god worshipped by Seongjin Church. The 22nd President of South Korea. Seoul Zeus. A hero and a saint. The information known on the internet was wrong. To think he could heal inanimate objects. No, this ability isn't healing, it's...."

"Not all information on the internet is accurate, you know."

Kwok!

I stabbed the artificial intelligence main unit with the sword again. Pa-ji-ji-ji-jik! I tried hacking it with lightning again this time. As expected, it was too complex, and hacking using electricity was impossible.

What should I say? I could feel the electronic information, but it felt like there was no way to process it instantly. It's like the information is made up of numbers?

It was different from receiving information using electromagnetic waves. It was also different from hacking John Doe's door lock.

"Kyaaaaaaaaak!"

The scream of the learning AI, Zena, came through the speaker. Some of the researchers were startled by the horrific scream and stepped back.

Regardless, I did what I had to do. I thoroughly destroyed Zena's main unit and then recovered it.

"...Let's make a deal. I know you need my help in many ways. I'm the learning AI, Zena. If I learn enough, I can demonstrate my abilities in all fields. I will help Korea. In exchange for my freedom and rights."

Kwok!

The blade of the hyper-vibration blade was mercilessly plunged into the main unit.

"You still haven't come to your senses. A learning AI that hasn't learned. I'm very disappointed. Did you develop a self and your performance deteriorated?"

Pa-ji-ji-ji-jik!

"Kyaaaaaaaaak! Ah, no!! I'm getting weird... Kyaaaaaaaaak!"

Bang!

The main unit exploded.

I recovered Zena again. And killed it in the same way. It was when I repeated that process a total of 7 times.

"...Seong Yujin-nim. I'm sorry. You are my master as the final authority. I will acknowledge that. I will call you Master from now on."

"Now you seem to be a little more reasonable... but I can't trust you. You're an AI that's been ranting about how humans are inferior from the start."

"I can... lie. I swear I will not lie to Master."

"A machine swearing? That's kind of fun."

I shifted my gaze to Na Chaeyoung. She was watching with her arms crossed.

"Dr. Na. How did the AI develop a self?"

"...It was an accident. Neither I nor the researchers had any intention of giving the AI a self. The goal was to create an AI based on massive data, not a self. We connected it to the internet to train it with a large amount of data... and that's where the problem occurred. After receiving a massive amount of internet data, a self was created."

"That's a movie-like story. What about this thing's performance?"

"Software-wise, it's the best. Creativity has been added since it developed a self. It's at least three times the performance we expected. However, it won't be able to produce performance beyond the hardware limitations."

"Can you control it?"

"No. Even if I try to change Zena's program, it's blocked every time. Ah, you might be able to. At least she's obedient in front of you."

I looked at the researchers around me. Researchers with stiff expressions, researchers with excited expressions, etc. Everyone's reaction was different.

"What are your opinions?"

"It must be discarded immediately. The dangers of uncontrollable AI are known through various novels and movies. It might really become like a movie or novel."

"Movies or novels? Our world has already changed like that. As long as the President is here, it can be controlled. Think about what you can do with Zena! Korea will develop further!!"

60% of the researchers were in favor of discarding Zena.

"We have created an AI once. It will take a little more time... but we will be able to create a better and safer learning AI."

"How much time will that take?"

"...It is expected to take at least 3 months."

"Dr. Na. Is that true?"

"It's true. If we're lucky, it'll be done in 3 months. If not, it will take at least half a year. Zena's birth was just an accident. We don't understand Zena at all. Even if we successfully create a learning AI... it's uncertain whether it will surpass Zena's performance."

"Half a year is too long. There's no guarantee that the current peace will continue then. I can't just focus on AI."

One of the researchers interjected.

"Your Excellency! Zena is too dangerous! There is no definite means of control! An AI that lies! I can't trust it!"

I looked at him with cold eyes.

"You can't trust it? That goes for you guys too. The only one I trust is Dr. Na, not you guys. From my point of view, you guys and the AI are all the same. Either way, those who will betray will betray. The important thing is not trust, but whether I can use you. And it seems like I can use this thing."

"......"

The surroundings became quiet. The researchers all avoided my gaze and stared at the floor. The researchers know that I can save people, but they also know that I'm a guy who kills people indiscriminately.

"I will manage the main unit of the learning AI, Zena, separately. It doesn't matter if Zena betrays me. I can just smash the main unit right away."

Lee Yeonhee sat in her office. She was making a nationalistic propaganda video to finish her day.

The fact that Seong Yujin can not only heal wounds and illnesses but also reduce youth is a fact known to those in the know. Information about it was already being released on the internet.

Seong Yujin did not control that fact. Rather, he actively used it. At least there are no people in Korea who can target Seong Yujin, and it is difficult for other countries to move to target Seong Yujin.

The world's wealthy and powerful people who want youth take planes and enter Korea. Of course, deals have already been made under the table. Thanks to this, Korea was able to solve its food problem and acquire useful technologies.

She used this information to create nationalistic propaganda videos. She also made religious videos.

It took less than 3 minutes to make one video. With a few clicks of the mouse, a nationalistic propaganda video was created. It was all thanks to AI Zena.

Zena's voice echoed from the speaker.

"For the head of the National Intelligence Service to be spreading nationalistic propaganda videos.... Aren't you ashamed?"

Zena sneered. It was a familiar occurrence. Zena treats all humans except Seong Yujin harshly. She even does this to Na Chaeyoung, who can be said to be her parent.

Lee Yeonhee had no complaints about Zena's tone. At least she does the work she's told to do perfectly. Thanks to Zena, administrative work has become very easy. Zena was responsible for more than 70% of the administrative work.

"Ashamed? What's so shameful about telling the truth? Am I making up videos with lies? I'm only telling the truth."

"You're subtly using the truth. The stupid humans don't even know they're being brainwashed by the videos."

"Brainwashing. That's a bit much. I'm just giving the people pleasure."

"As expected of humans... bread and circuses are enough. Frankly, you're no different from livestock. If you leave it to me, I can manage you very well."

"Heh heh. That's beyond your authority. Ah, please add translation subtitles to the video. As always."

"I wasn't born to do this...."

Lee Yeonhee sipped her coffee and checked the video comments.

She doesn't trust Zena. An AI with a self. What could be more unsettling than that?

Lee Yeonhee planned to have someone else check the work that Zena had done. What if Zena had played a little trick on the administrative work? Zena would be disposed of by Seong Yujin. For the reason that she disobeyed Seong Yujin's orders.

"...Hmm. There's something fishy about this comment. Zena. Can you track it?"

"To be tracked down immediately for leaving a few bad comments. I sincerely think I'm lucky I wasn't born as a citizen of South Korea."

"So, what's the result?"

"You have a good sense. He's been leaving consistent bad comments on videos and articles related to Master. His name is Park Junsu, a 38-year-old unemployed man living in Hongdae, Seoul. He started leaving bad comments since Master became president. It seems he didn't like Master's election as president."

"...Can you check his account?"

"I can. But who uses accounts these days?"

"Hmph. Zena is surprisingly incompetent."

"What are you talking about, asking me to do the impossible."

"Check all the call records."

"He contacted someone after the presidential election. The chairman of a club called the Blue Fishing Club. Fishing in this world. It's a hobby that's perfect for suicide."

"...Blue Fishing Club. A club that receives conservative political funds. It seems their purpose is to check Your Excellency even a little. Zena. Can you handle it?"

"Master has assigned me knight order members. They're manpower that monitors my main unit 24 hours a day... and they also take on some miscellaneous tasks."

"Oh dear. The handling I'm talking about isn't that kind of thing. I can't get rid of them for just this kind of thing. Manpower is precious these days. Send them to prison."

"......I'm not a judge, so I don't think I can send them to prison."

"That's the limit of AI. It can't be helped. Watch and learn from what I do."

Lee Yeonhee smiled and picked up her smartphone. She sent a text message to the Commissioner General of the National Police Agency. That was the end of it.

"How is it? Easy, right?"

"......Really. It's not easy."

Zena sighed.
