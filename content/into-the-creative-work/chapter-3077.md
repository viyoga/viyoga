Three days remained until the summoning date.

I climbed to the 20th floor. Along the way, I also confirmed Seong Jigon's power.

Seong Jigon was simply strong. Ordinary attacks couldn't leave so much as a scratch on his skin. It wasn't just his physical body that was impressive—he was equipped with swordsmanship skills to match. If an S-rank Hunter of this world fought Seong Jigon head-on, they would never win.

『Acquired the Shoes of Noon.』

Obtained as the 18th-floor ranking 1st-place reward.

The effect was becoming agile while the sun was up.

Perhaps because it was Rare-grade, it wasn't a particularly satisfying item for me. It was metal footwear, and the design was ugly besides, so I gifted it to Seong Jigon.

"This is a decent-grade law artifact. Thanks, Eugene. I'll put it to good use."

"Use it half-heartedly and toss it. How's the nursing home volunteering—you like it?"

"Haha. I'm grateful I get to serve those ladies. Want to see? These are photos we took yesterday."

Seong Jigon fumbled awkwardly with his smartphone before showing me the screen. It was a scene of a naked Seong Jigon and a white-haired old woman locked in an embrace on a bed. I unconsciously flinched backward. In the photo, Seong Jigon was fully erect, gazing lovingly at the old woman.

Shocking as the photo was, I had a sound mind, so I managed to endure it somehow. Of course, enduring it was one thing—it wasn't a photo I wanted to keep looking at, so I turned my gaze away.

"You crazy bastard... The photo is one thing, but you seduced her in a single day?"

Seducing a woman in one day isn't easy. Women you meet at clubs are mostly young, horny, and open-minded, but that was a nursing home.

"The ladies at the nursing home were all so lonely."

"Even so. Doing that, you look like you'd send them off to the afterlife mid-act."

"Haha. Of course I was careful too. And I shared my energy with them. Not just once or twice—I'm not so inconsiderate as to skip that."

So he'd apparently even shared his own energy to fuck the old women.

I was relieved that Seong Jigon seemed to be enjoying this world. He then fiddled with his smartphone and showed me a new photo.

"This thing called the internet is quite the spectacle. Look at this. You can find lewd photos so easily."

Seeing the photo, I clutched both eyes and staggered back. The photo Seong Jigon showed me was one of those commonly used as a tactical nuke!

"Kraaagh! Seong Jigon! Is this how you repay kindness with enmity?!"

"It's a good photo, what. I'm thinking of blurring the face and uploading it to the internet too. Seems there are plenty of people with the same tastes as me online."

"Can't you see they're suffering in the comments? Most of it is just insults."

Seong Jigon, being my summon, spoke fluent Korean and could even read Hangul.

"Haha. But look at the view count and upvotes. They're absurdly high. People actually like old women."

Perhaps it was his resolve that he wouldn't suffer alone.

"Fine. Use the site I'll tell you about. You'll get quite a reception there. And I'll let Lee Yeonhee know so your account doesn't get banned."

"Yeah? Thanks."

"It's nothing."

I won't be the only one suffering.

For the time being, I'd need to be careful when browsing the Korean internet.

『Acquired the Unique Ability Level Up Ticket.』

『Unique Ability Level Up Ticket

Grade: Unique

Raises the level of a Unique Ability.』

The effect of the 19th-floor challenge reward was simple. That didn't mean its value was simple. Selling it externally would net at least tens of billions.

'Because in this world, a Hunter's Unique Ability doesn't rise just by accumulating proficiency.'

I held off on using it immediately.

『Acquired the Grimoire of Twilight.』

Obtained as the 20th-floor challenge ranking 1st-place reward.

But the 20th-floor rewards didn't end there.

『Congratulations on climbing the 20th floor.』

『The Tower bestows its blessing upon you.』

『Acquired Unique Ability: Character Share (Lv.1).』

The Tower's Blessing.

Hunters receive it upon reaching levels 20, 50, and 70.

The Tower's Blessing effect is Unique Ability Awakening.

As you can see from the Unique Ability I awakened this time, it only awakens abilities related to existing Unique Abilities. In a sense, it's closer to an expansion of a Unique Ability than a new one.

『Character Share Lv.1

Consumes coins to share a character's abilities.

•Vision Share

Shares the vision of a summoned character. Consumes 10 coins per second.』

'I thought from the name that it shared characters, but it shares abilities with my characters.'

Vision Share. Utterly pathetic. From what I could tell, there was no advantage to be gained through Vision Share, yet it consumed 10 coins per second.

'It might be because the Unique Ability level is low.'

I raised the level of Character Share.

『Character Share Lv.2

Consumes coins to share a character's abilities.

•Audio-Visual Share

Shares the sight and hearing of a summoned character. Consumes 10 coins per second.』

'Now it shares sound too, huh.'

It still demanded a steep price, but the performance had clearly improved.

'If I raise the Unique Ability level further, it seems I'll be able to share abilities too. An ability that looks at the ceiling rather than the floor, is it.'

I'd have to order Lee Yeonhee to procure Unique Ability Level Up Tickets.

Should I try using it once?

I looked around. At home were Wi Yu, Elena, and Yuria.

'If I use it on Lee Yeonhee or Seong Jigon, who aren't home, I should be able to clearly feel the ability. From the looks of it, there doesn't seem to be a distance condition for sharing.'

I decided to skip Seong Jigon. There was a high chance I'd get traumatized sharing his sight and hearing.

『Sharing Lee Yeonhee's sight and hearing.』

『Consuming 10 coins per second.』

A rectangular screen appeared in my vision. Inside the screen was a corpse. Men in suits were clearing the body away.

I'd thought Vision Share meant my vision would be replaced by the target's, but it felt like watching a first-person video. This was actually better.

"...Your Excellency?"

Lee Yeonhee's voice reached my ears. Audio share. It was like listening through a speaker. Judging by Yuria and Elena's reactions, the sound seemed audible only to me.

"Seeing as you noticed the moment I used Share, did a notification pop up? Lee Yeonhee. Can you hear me?"

No answer.

There was no way Lee Yeonhee would deliberately ignore me, so it seemed my voice wasn't reaching her.

'Characters can't share my sight and hearing. It's a one-way share.'

In the screen, Lee Yeonhee removed her blood-stained gloves and operated her smartphone. Her phone's wallpaper was a drawing of a rose of Sharon and the Taegeukgi.

She dialed my number. The saved name was "Your Excellency."

Brrrrrr.

I pulled my smartphone from my pocket and answered.

"Your Excellency. Have you awakened a Unique Ability called Audio-Visual Share?"

Two of Lee Yeonhee's voices rang out. The voice coming through the smartphone was very slightly delayed.

"Yes. It seems my situation isn't visible to you?"

"Correct. Through the notification window, I could only tell that my sight and hearing were being shared."

"Isn't it unpleasant having your sight and hearing forcibly shared?"

"I am merely glad to be able to truly become Your Excellency's eyes and ears."

Whether she meant it or not, her voice was calm.

"Good to hear. But what are you doing right now? Who's the one you killed yourself?"

"A Japanese agent. He wasn't merely gathering intelligence—he was tasked with causing internal chaos in Korea. They were also planning to assassinate me, Your Excellency, and the President, so I stepped in personally to handle it. After this, I'll deal with the politicians and businessmen—traitors—who've been in contact with them."

The standoff with the Japanese Maritime Self-Defense Force in the East Sea had ended, but beneath the surface, a fierce intelligence war had apparently been raging.

"You've been through a lot."

"Not at all. It is simply my duty."

"I want Unique Ability Level Up Tickets. Are there any left in Korea?"

"There are no Unique Ability Level Up Tickets remaining in the Korean Hunter Management Bureau. They're being concentrated on A-rank Hunters with high potential to become S-rank. Korea's situation isn't good enough to let them gather dust in a warehouse. If more tickets come in later, I will offer them to Your Excellency. Your Excellency's growth is Korea's growth."

"Yes. I am Korea."

After ending the call with Lee Yeonhee, I took out the Grimoire of Twilight.

『Grimoire of Twilight

Grade: Unique

Allows learning Twilight Magic.』

'So this is that thing. The magic the original protagonist learned. It lets you learn corpse-controlling magic. The original protagonist stored corpses in subspace and took them out to use whenever needed.'

I opened the thick book with the red cover. The grimoire glowed black.

『Magic aptitude is insufficient. Cannot learn Twilight Magic.』

'Ah, right. I can't learn magic.'

It was this world's penalty. I couldn't learn any type of magic. The chance to become a necromancer had vanished, so I clicked my tongue in regret.

Beside me, Elena was looking at the grimoire with eyes full of interest.

"Elena. Want to try learning it?"

"Necromancy, is it. It's not my aptitude, but I am curious."

Elena took the Grimoire of Twilight and opened it. The grimoire glowed black, then stopped.

"It seems summons can't learn it either."

"Can't learn it with a click. Can't you learn it by reading the contents?"

"I can't even read the contents. It's a script I don't recognize."

Thanks to the Tower, the language barrier had vanished between me and my summons in this world, but this book seemed to be an exception. Elena handed the book to Yuria.

"Yuria. If it's you, you should be able to decipher the book's contents to some degree, right?"

"...It's certainly a script with regularity. Even for me, deciphering it on the spot is difficult. And even if I deciphered it perfectly, neither I nor Elena would likely be able to learn it."

"I suppose so—the magic systems are completely different, after all."

Even the same magic has different rules in each world. My summons use powers based on the laws of their own worlds, regardless of the underlying principle. In Elena's case, she freely uses illusion magic even without an Atlantis system.

"Damn. So the Grimoire of Twilight is a bust."

I considered throwing it away, then stopped.

With a click, one can learn magic. At minimum, it could earn tens of billions. Selling it for coins was fine too.

'Unless you're the original protagonist, or someone like me who can carry corpses in bulk, it won't be all that effective, though.'

I began preparing to move. Tonight, Garshin, the Governor-General who occupied Taiwan, would die, and Taiwan would be liberated.
