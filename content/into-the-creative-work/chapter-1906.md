Sparrow monsters charge towards the electrified barrier. Kwang kwang kwang! The sturdy barrier blocked the sparrow monsters' attacks. The barrier is shaking, but it won't break right away. As calculated, there's plenty of time.

"One day, I realized my power had grown stronger."

I recalled that day that happened not long ago.

It was truly a mysterious thing.

It wasn't like I had done any special training, nor was there any progress in my magic research.

I just realized that my power to control lightning had grown stronger.

Even without using magic, I could control lightning and even imbue it with special properties. My unique ability had become stronger.

If I touched this lightning with magic, it could become even stronger.

"What bullshit are you spouting? Trying to stall for time? Not gonna happen. You think that barrier will protect you forever?!"

Fireballs rise from the ground, trying to melt the barrier.

This is also within expectations. It's only natural that the impatient Gillian would use his power directly. In exchange, Gillian's arms are both burned, though.

"I'm just saying it's really mysterious. You might have experienced something like that too, who knows? Judging by your reaction, it doesn't seem like it."

I stare at the thunderbolt on my palm. The thunderbolt is in the shape of a ring, spinning around.

"Even if I try to find the cause, I can't. One thing is for sure, it's been beneficial. There's been nothing harmful about it."

I threw the completed lightning at the nearest sparrow monster. The ring shape breaks, and it leaps out like a fierce snake, electrocuting it.

[Chain Lightning]

It electrocutes one sparrow monster and absorbs its life force and mana. Originally, Chain Lightning doesn't have the ability to absorb life force, but with the property of lightning I recently realized, I granted it the ability to absorb life force.

Chain Lightning splits into five and rushes towards other sparrow monsters. Chain Lightning spreads among the monsters in an instant. Chain Lightning is a magic that demonstrates its power the more opponents there are. Even if tens of millions of people were gathered, one Chain Lightning could wipe them out within 5 minutes.

Jjaek jjaek jjaek!

The sparrow monsters are horrified and try to escape from the Chain Lightning. However, the speed of the Chain Lightning's splitting and transfer was much faster than their flapping wings.

Pajijijijijijijijik!

Having dealt with all the sparrow monsters within 1 minute, the Chain Lightning tried to move on to the next prey. Originally, it should have moved towards Gillian right away, but.

'It's too far away.'

The Chain Lightning, having lost its way, was about to disappear into the void.

That won't do. Unlike the original Chain Lightning, I can't just let go of this energy mass that has grown larger by absorbing the life force of the sparrow monsters.

As I reach out my hand, the Chain Lightning begins to gather.

"You! You were hiding your power?!!"

"Hiding my power? You just didn't recognize it."

"If that's not the case, how can a 5th circle use 6th circle magic... Is it an artifact!"

"You fool."

I change the shape of the Chain Lightning into a long spear. I remove the spell of Chain Lightning and grant it a new spell. I also take back the life force absorption property and grant it the property of extremely low temperature.

'Changing the properties of magic is something that should only be possible for a 7th circle... but thanks to my unique ability, I can use it, at least for lightning-based magic. Other magicians would be shocked if they knew this fact.'

Additionally, I imbued the lightning spear with my mana and will.

Lightning Spear. Originally, it's only a 4th circle magic, but this spear in my hand now is comparable to a 6th circle magic in terms of power.

Before the barrier completely melts from the flames, I threw the Lightning Spear. I don't think I'll miss. Even if I'm bad at everything else, I was confident in throwing and hitting.

The lightning spear pierced through Gillian's torso. It easily shattered Gillian's barrier. Following that, the lightning spear was embedded in the maple tree.

Jjeojeong!

The Lightning Spear explodes, scattering electricity and cold air in all directions. The surrounding area became an extremely low-temperature state where even the air froze. The blazing maple leaves instantly went out and became faded maple leaves.

The electricity crisscrosses through the frozen Gillian and the maple tree. As the environment temporarily falls into an extremely low-temperature state, a superconducting phenomenon occurs.

Then the electricity disappeared, and the temperature began to rise little by little. It was then that Gillian, who I didn't know was frozen or dead, screamed.

"Aaaaaaaaargh!"

It was a scream that felt even desperate.

"I'll kill you, at least!! I'll dedicate everything! My body, my soul! Kill himmm!!"

The maple tree didn't die. The flames of the maple leaves went out, and the inside of the tree was torn by electricity, but it was alive. In human terms, it would be considered seriously injured. If there's fuel, it will burn again like before.

"If there's fuel, that is."

The presence of the maple tree fades.

"What, why, why are you trying to disappear?! The contract? Keep the contract!! I'll give you my soul!!"

I landed on the ground.

I realized why Gillian, whose torso was pierced by the Lightning Spear and frozen by the extremely low temperature, was still alive and moving. It's a simple story. He modified his body into a chimera.

However, it's undeniable that death is near. Chimeras are not immortal.

"Would your body, which is already no different from a corpse, be valuable? Even if I were that tree, I don't think I would need it."

"My soul! I'll give you my soul!"

"There's something that magicians and warriors, those with power, are mistaken about. The illusion that their souls are special. Unless it's a special case, human souls are all the same. Your body would have been worth hundreds of times more than your soul. But not anymore."

"You son of a bitch!!"

Gillian clenches his fist and rushes at me. But he immediately collapses and writhes on the floor. Gillian was already at his limit.

I looked down at the fallen Gillian and prepared magic.

"I told you. I'll fry you to death with lightning."

Lightning Strike.

Lightning struck before my eyes. Gillian, struck by the lightning, trembled in pain and died. I dropped lightning two more times as if I was annoyed and kicked his head to smash it. Gillian is a chimera. It's a possibility of less than 1%, but I killed him for sure in case he might rise again.

'I'm a bit tired.'

Still, I couldn't go back right away.

Because it's time to collect the spoils of victory.

'He was going to buy a factory, so he must have money. Even if not, there will be things that can be sold for money.'

I head to Gillian's underground workshop. I checked Yuria's location with detection magic. Yuria was sitting on top of a ruined building, resting.

'It seems she's safe for now.'

I drop into the underground workshop. It was an underground workshop in a mess. There were human heads, monster arms, beast torsos, etc. They were materials for chimeras.

I wasn't interested in chimeras.

'Are there any magic books? There must be research records.'

Even if there's nothing else, there must be research records. Just like I do, magicians record their research.

'Computers are at high risk of being hacked, so they write manually on paper or in books.'

As I wandered around the workshop, I soon found a book. One is a chimera research record. Gillian had recorded everything about the chimeras he made himself.

'It started with synthesizing rats and hamsters. Then he gradually increased the size of the chimera, and about a year ago, he modified his own body into a chimera. He changed his organs little by little.'

After roughly skimming through it, I could also understand Gillian's purpose.

To synthesize creatures from the Phantasmal World into himself. That was Gillian's ultimate goal.

'He was collecting things related to Phantasmal World creatures while working as a smuggler.'

For example, the claws of a cat phantom beast or the feathers of a Flame Bird. They have value as magical materials, but they weren't very great things.

'What I like is the summoning book... but there's nothing special.'

The summoning spell was simple. If you're prepared, even a 1st circle magician could use it. The problem is contracting with the summoned being. More than 70% of summoners die in this process. Because there aren't many creatures in the Phantasmal World that are friendly to humans.

'That's why summoners need a teacher to mediate in the middle.'

The troublesome thing about summoning magic is that it's completely random when you summon it for the first time.

'I memorized the summoning spell. Not that I'm going to use it.'

Summoned beasts are not limited to Phantasmal World creatures. In theory, spirits, demons, and angels could also be summoned. But the probability is infinitely close to zero.

'It's possible to specify the summoning target by linking it with ritual magic. The problem is that, unlike the summoning spell, ritual magic is extremely complicated, and there's a lot to prepare.'

I took the book for now.

I looked around the underground workshop. There were quite a few interesting things.

'A magic tool to analyze materials. I should use this.'

In addition, there were luxury goods from foreign countries, as befitting a smuggler. From my point of view, they're stolen goods, so it's hard to sell them at the proper price, but I think I can get at least 100 million credits.

'I'll burn the drugs. If I handle this wrong, I'll be dragged to jail, so it's easier to deal with it cleanly.'

I came to the deepest part of the underground workshop.

Cash and gold bars were piled up on one side. It would be worth at least 400 million. I could say I hit the jackpot with just this. But the things next to it caught my eye.

Three high-end preservation capsules, each 1m in size.

Inside, there were different saplings. I was quite surprised to see the sapling in the first capsule. It was a tree that felt very similar to the maple tree I just saw. Of course, the presence was worlds apart, though.

'I trust my senses. All of these saplings... are trees from the Phantasmal World. How did he get them? He wouldn't have gone to the Phantasmal World himself to get them at his level....'

In the first place, if they were summoned from the Phantasmal World, they would be reverse-summoned again after a while. In other words, these were brought from the Phantasmal World in a way other than summoning.

'They're treasures. The research value is more than enough.'

I swallowed hard.

I recall the majesty of the maple tree again. Gillian, who uses the power of the maple tree, was similar to the power of a 5th circle fire magician. Although, he didn't train properly, so he couldn't use that power properly.

'If I can research and understand the tree and use its power properly....'

Even if there are no research results, I can get hundreds of millions if I sell them. The value of a tree from the Phantasmal World is that high.

'......Did Gillian perhaps try to plant this in his body?'

I don't know the answer, but I felt like he would.

I scanned the saplings in the capsules. The first one is definitely that maple tree from before. The second and third ones look a little different, so I can tell they're different kinds of trees.

Among them, the third sapling caught my eye. The reason is that it feels somewhat familiar.

'I should see it directly.'

I opened the capsule. I didn't feel any dangerous vibes from the tree right away. If it was a dangerous tree, it wouldn't have been managed so simply. I reached out my hand towards the tree.

Pajik.

As soon as my finger touched the tree, sparks flew. It was too strong to be static electricity. Surprised, I reflexively tried to control the sparks. The moment I raised my mana, it resonates with the sapling.

Pajijijik.

Lightning occurs. Surprisingly, the sapling absorbed the lightning. A change occurred in the sapling. It grew larger than before and began to bloom.

The light pink petals were a type of flower I knew well.

'Cherry blossom?'

The cherry blossoms glowed faintly.
