,I received the gift well. Now it's time to get down to business."

,...No. We prepared to escape the government's influence, not to beat it. At the time, there was tremendous animosity towards corporations, so we had no choice but to prepare. That's what Sejong City is for."

,Escaping the government's influence is trying to beat it."

This world is similar to the history of the real world, but slightly different in small parts. The existence of the Agency for National Security Planning proves it. Originally, it should have been called the National Intelligence Service, not the Agency for National Security Planning.

I don't know much about the relationship between the previous Korean government and corporations. I don't care either. What's important is whether it bothers me, helps me, or can I use it.

Sejong Businessmen Whatever? To be honest, I was thinking of cleaning it up before the presidential election. With assassinations and terrorism using the Holy Knights. Aftermath? I judged that it was much better than leaving the company intact. So I was going to do it.

,...If I dare to make an excuse, we didn't know about the Ilwoo Group's affairs either. We judged that the North Korean issue was a national matter and not something we should interfere with. Please take that into consideration."

,What good is it to have countless amounts of money if you can't use it? We don't want the power to shake the country. We just want our position to last for a long time."

Shin Godeok's eyes are burning in a way that doesn't suit his age.

I looked around and laughed.

Those with a lot of money. The status they have is by no means small. And those with high status are bound to hear information that ordinary people cannot access.

They know that I can give them youth.

,I gave youth to some generals and officers to take control of the military. The information leaked."

Well, I don't think the information will be maintained forever. No matter how much you cover it up with makeup, it's bound to show if your body suddenly becomes younger.

,Tell me straight. Do you want youth?"

Thud!

Shin Godeok immediately knelt on the floor. He looks at me and clasps his hands together.

A man who had more power than the president when the world was normal.

An old man who can't even walk without a cane.

The oldest person here knew better than anyone the fear of aging and death.

What good is it to have a lot of money? It's not like you can live for thousands of years. But what if you could live for thousands of years?

,I feel death. The Grim Reaper is always behind me. I can't see well in front because I'm old, and I can't move my body as I please. Even if I take medicine that's good for my body, expensive wild ginseng, there's no sign of improvement. Every time I go to bed, I'm afraid I won't be able to get up again. I'll do anything! ,Anything, huh? Easier said than done."

I got up from the fancy chair and approached him. I reached out to his shoulder. I can feel the emaciated bones that seem like they will break if I give them strength.

I used Recovery.

The bones become stronger and the flesh fills out. The white hair turned black. The fine wrinkles noticeably disappear.

I didn't give him complete youth. He went back from his 80s to his 50s. Shin Godeok was moved by that alone. He lowered his eyes and bowed his head. He had enjoyed and possessed a lot, so he realized the value of youth more than anyone else.

,Oh, oh, oh! You are the true savior-isiyeo!"
