"Greetings. AL-401 Earthlings. I am the Star Spirit of planet AL-401. You can call me AL."

I was in a vast space. A pure white space.

In this space where it was hard to tell the boundary between the floor and the sky, there were hundreds of people including me. Everyone was looking around with bewildered faces. The age range was 20s to 40s.

"W-What is this all of a sudden?"

"...Does anyone know what's going on?"

"Is this some kind of hidden camera prank?"

"I was definitely sleeping at home...."

'What's with all this pure white background? I'll go crazy if I stay here for too long.'

I found a man who was unusually calm in this space. A young man in his 20s wearing jeans and a black hoodie. I was convinced by his attire. He's the protagonist.

'Kang Myungjin. He has the setting of being possessed by the protagonist of a novel written by his younger sibling that he enjoyed reading.'

God's Atlantis is a web novel that was serialized on the internet. It was a pretty popular web novel.

'It's exactly the starting point of the original work. Kang Myungjin is quietly racking his brain trying to figure out the situation.'

It's not just Kang Myungjin. Some people are quietly trying to figure out the situation while watching others. They are more likely to survive the tutorial.

'I'm only here to experience mana. I'm just going to do the tutorial, so I don't need to worry about anything.'

As a secondary purpose, there is a mystical medicine hidden somewhere in the tutorial. That mystical medicine increases the amount of mana in the person who takes it.

"You have been randomly selected and banished to Atlantis. I hope you live diligently there."

It was the voice that introduced itself as AL at the beginning. A neat female voice. It wasn't Korean or anything, it was a completely unknown language, but strangely enough, I could understand it.

'It's the Atlantis common language. I should have no problem reading and writing it.'

It didn't seem like there would be many incidents due to language.

"Banished? Banished?!"

"Let me out of here. I have to go to work tomorrow."

"Why can't I make a phone call?!"

The space became noisy in an instant. Everyone was filled with anxiety and said something, it was like a bustling marketplace.

"Quiet."

With AL's one word filled with unknown power, the space became quiet in an instant. AL is not showing its appearance, but it is by no means an ordinary being.

"You may feel that this is unfair. But keep in mind. You were born on Earth as you pleased, and you have lived on Earth as you pleased. From the perspective of me, the Star Spirit of Earth, you are illegal immigrants. Nothing more, nothing less."

This is a bit of an overstatement. I know from reading the original work that Earthlings and AL, the Star Spirit of Earth, have a mutually beneficial relationship.

"You have been banished from Earth. But it doesn't mean you can't return to Earth forever. You can return to your original Earth when Atlantis is over. David Thane, whom you know well. He was also an exile, but he is a hero who returned to Earth."

David Thane.

He is an S-class Hunter from America.

A man who has gained both wealth and fame.

"Atlantis is also a land of opportunity. David Thane was not originally an Awakened. He gained power in Atlantis, and with that power, he became an American hero. There is no reason why you can't do as David Thane did."

I let out a hollow laugh. David Thane is the only one who has returned from Atlantis.

More than 10,000 people are put into Atlantis, but only one has returned.

For reference, there are no Hunters among the people here. Hunters are those who hunt monsters that appear on Earth. From the perspective of AL, the Star Spirit of Earth, Hunters are white blood cells. There is no way they would send that high-level workforce to Atlantis. It's not like there's a problem with Earth's lifespan.

"What... what are we supposed to do in that place called Atlantis?"

"You can live there as you please. I'm not asking anything of you."

"......"

Not asking. That means not expecting. Of course, AL's attitude will change due to the protagonist's actions in the middle of the novel... that's a story for the distant future.

'I can't see AL's appearance right now, but... according to the description in the original work, she's supposed to be a stunning beauty with pure white hair.'

I can tell just by listening to her voice. She must be quite a beauty.

'Doesn't violating AL, the Star Spirit of Earth, mean violating the Earth!'

Seong Yujin, the man who violated the Earth!

What a wonderful title!

"In 10 minutes. You will be leaving for the tutorial area of Atlantis. The tutorial is a kind of test. You must pass the tutorial to enter Atlantis."

"...What happens if we don't pass?"

"You die. But you don't have to worry too much. The Atlantis system will be applied to you, and you will be able to use the status window."

"Status window...? Heok! W-What, suddenly!"

The man who muttered about the status window was surprised. It seems like something appeared in front of him.

And some quick-witted people started shouting or muttering about the status window.

"This is it for me. I wish you good luck."

AL disappeared. I can't see it, but I can clearly feel that the huge presence that was in this space has disappeared.
