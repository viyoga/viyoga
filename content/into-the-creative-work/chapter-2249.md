The wealth I've accumulated so far is considerable. Mostly because I haven't spent it and just saved it.

It's not like I gathered money for the sake of gathering money. It's just that even when I earned money, there wasn't much to spend it on.

The three essential elements for human life: clothing, food, and shelter.

Clothing? Brand-name stuff is nice, but I'm not particularly obsessed with it. Although it depends on the place, I usually wear whatever's comfortable. I also don't have a hobby of collecting clothes.

Food? My palate is picky. Most high-end dishes don't satisfy my taste. Of course, there are exceptions. Like cola or chicken. Seeing that, I wonder if my taste is geared towards luxury... But even with chicken, it depends on the chicken. More than 95% of the chicken sold on the market doesn't meet my standards. Same with pizza.

The reason I prefer high-end cuisine is that most of it suits my taste. And there's no need to pour hundreds or thousands of dollars into high-end cuisine.

The dishes that Milyeong makes sufficiently satisfy my taste, and when Milyeong isn't around, I bring food from the [Baekhwan] world to eat. If I put it in my inventory, the food is preserved as is, so I can eat it when I'm hungry.

Shelter? I wasn't very interested in real estate. People tell me to invest in real estate for financial gain... I don't know. I'm not particularly worried about money even in the future.

In the old days, I was desperate for money, so I brought gold from the game world and sold it off... But now, money is piling up in my account even if I do nothing.

,How much exactly is my wealth? Would it be around 2 trillion won? No. I spent quite a bit of money last time... Hmm. I don't know.'

The revenue from 'Black River,' the game that Milyeong and I created? That hasn't been settled yet.

The way I accumulated wealth is through the sales of HB-1, a replica of Hebil's stimulant. Every time Ha Seunghee-ssi, the daughter of the Sejin Group, sells HB-1, a portion of the profits falls to me. The important thing here is that HB-1 is very expensive and sold through illegal routes all over the world.

Besides that, the money I earn from Hunter activities also accumulates because I don't spend it. I've also sold gold, jewels, and Dwarf-made weapons from other worlds on the black market quite a few times.

,Ha Seunghee disperses and circulates my assets here and there because she says a lot of taxes are taken. I have quite a few borrowed-name bank accounts.'

Tax evasion was the second reason. The real purpose is to hide my identity. I agreed to it too. If the fact that I provided Hebil's stimulant becomes known, flies will swarm from everywhere. The fact that things are going well so far is thanks to Ha Seunghee-ssi doing a good job.

,I've gathered quite a bit of money, so I should spend it.'

I got up from my seat. After roughly concealing my identity with an identity-mask, I tore a space-movement scroll.

I moved to a warehouse in America. Inside the dark warehouse, items purchased through black market dealers were piled up.

I approached a pile of sacks. Inside the sacks were full of pure white powder.

It wasn't drugs, of course.

,Even a black market dealer can't get this much drugs. Even if I could get it, there's nowhere to use it. It's dangerous because I could be tracked.'

These were all sugar.

I took out a spatial box from my pocket and put the sugar sacks in it. A spatial box is an essential item for absolute masters in the [Gwangmyeong Seungcheon] world. To be more specific, it's a space-expanding box. You have to use a spatial box to put things in your inventory and move them all at once.

Where am I taking it? To the [Baekhwan] world, of course.

,The maids also like sugar... and one of the main products of the merchant guild is sugar. Especially the nobles go crazy for it.'

Besides sugar, salt and pepper also sell very well. MSG too, of course.

Why not make it in the [Baekhwan] world?

It's not that easy.

From growing sugarcane to refining it into sugar. When I actually tried to do it, nothing was easy.

,I was blocked from the start. There's no land near my territory with an environment suitable for growing sugarcane.'

Solve it with magic or technology? It wasn't as easy as I thought. As I said, the infrastructure in the [Baekhwan] world is non-existent except for my territory.

,What's needed in Baekhwan isn't just sugar. I also need things like salt and pepper. Instead of being self-sufficient, it's easier for me to do some troublesome work.'

I also thought about developing the Baekhwan world. But to do that, I'd have to start by unifying the world, and the amount of work would increase like crazy. That's why I gave up on the country and am satisfied with the territory. Because I hate that kind of troublesome work. Besides, it would take at least decades or hundreds of years to develop it to the level I want.

,I feel like I'd screw it up a few more times if I did that... My goal in the Baekhwan world is to play around and eat.'

After taking the sugar, salt, and pepper, I went to another warehouse and started taking things. Shoes, clothes, adult products, drinks and snacks, tissues, pots, ramen, cosmetics, etc. I put an amount that's dizzying just to look at into the spatial box.

,The spatial box is already 10 full. I've put in the equivalent of 30 wagons....'

But my work isn't over yet.

In some ways, the most important things are left.

Guns and bullets. Bombs and shells, etc.

Even if there are no enemies in the [Baekhwan] world, I can't let my guard down. For the maids' armament and training, I had to bring guns and bullets.

,I bought a helicopter last time too... and to use the helicopter, I have to bring oil too... Shit. Now that I think about it, the money goes out as fast as I earn it?'

I miss the [Vampire Detective] world. When that world was running smoothly, I didn't even think about the money that was going out for supplies. It's impossible to bring a large amount of supplies from the [Eternal Eden] world because it's not in the normalization stage yet.

,I can't help it. This is an investment to live a rich and fun life in the Baekhwan world. And Yuria's childbirth isn't far off.'

It was only after an hour of hard work that I was able to put all the supplies in.

As I came out of the warehouse and breathed in the American air, three cars rushed towards me from afar. The ones who got out of the car were armed with guns and knives.

"You're a little late, aren't you?"

The broker Richard, whom I met and contracted with in America, smiled crookedly. He was the broker who filled the warehouse with goods.

"Did you come to hang out with your friends?"

"Forger. I've been a little curious since I was young, you know?"

Forger was the alias I used.

"So?"

"I was wondering where you take all those supplies every month? So I hid a hidden camera in one of the warehouses. When I looked, you had an amazing box? Those amazing boxes, hand them over to us. It's too precious a treasure for you to use alone."

"I can't believe a broker would stab me in the back. I trusted you."

"This industry is a bit like that. You see my friends here? They belong to the Black Guild 'Nanba.' They're experts in murder. If you hand over the boxes, I'll spare you."

I can guarantee it. The moment I hand over the spatial box, they'll attack right away. They're wary of the spatial box being destroyed due to an accident.

"Ah, fuck."

"Forger. Don't be too upset. Isn't life all like this? I'm telling you, if you just hand over the box, I'll spare you. You have to live to do anything, right?"

"Fucking annoying. I have to find a new American broker. I even gave you a sucker's commission, and this is how you come out? Ah, did you come out like this because I gave you a sucker's commission?"

"Forger. You don't understand the situat-"

[Using Meteor Sword.]

A meteor sword created in the air fell on the head of the burly man next to the broker Richard. The man fell, becoming a corpse. That blood smeared on Richard's cheek. Richard looked at his friend's corpse with shaking eyes.

"You're the one who doesn't understand the situation. If you wanted to kill me, you should have brought at least 10 A-class Hunters. Not trash that's at best around C-class."

"......"

"Well, if you were A-class, you wouldn't have gotten along with you in the first place."

[Using Meteor Sword.]

Several meteor swords are created in the sky. The enemies who saw it shouted.

"Shit! Run away!"

"Richard, you bastard! You said the opponent was a pushover! He's obviously A-class or higher!"

"I, I surrender! Please spare me!"

The meteor sword fell to the ground. Some avoided it, but a meteor sword was created again in the sky and fell to the ground. By putting mana into the meteor sword to increase its speed and destructive power, C-class scrubs could only be unilaterally massacred.

Richard, who was left alone, sat down on the floor.

"F, Forger... I must have been out of my mind for a moment... I'll do better in the future. I'll work without a commission. Huh? Can't you just let me off once? We've gotten along well so far."

"We got along well. Until you stabbed me in the back."

I approached Richard. I was relaxed. Richard was a black market broker, but he was an ordinary person, not an Awakened. He took out a palm-sized stick from his pocket and pointed it at me.

"Don't come! Do you know what this is? It's a magic wand that can summon lightning! It's an artifact!"

"Lightning? Interesting. Do it."

"D, die, you son of a bitch!"

A blue lightning bolt shot out from the end of the stick and flew towards me. I snatched the lightning that flew without using even a moment.

The speed and power were terrible for lightning.

"It's just a slightly dangerous electric shocker. I'll give it back to you."

I adjusted the power and threw the lightning back. If I threw it with its original power, he would definitely die.

"Kkyaaaaaaak!"

"Don't worry, you'll be alive for a few hours. There's no way I'd easily kill a guy who stabbed me in the back."

I put my foot on the head of the guy who was electrocuted and smelled like burning meat.

"I'm personally taking the trouble to electrocute you. Be grateful."

I killed Richard an hour later. I could have tortured him for a few more hours, but I lost interest in the middle, so I killed him.

,I need to find a new warehouse in another part of America. I have to find a new broker, so... that's also work.'

I scratched my head.

There were warehouses in Japan and China too. But I couldn't get them in large quantities as easily as in America. It was especially difficult to get guns in China and Japan. In the case of China, they're sensitive to supplies because they're busy cleaning up the large-scale dungeon break that happened a few years ago. And I personally don't like Chinese products.

,Should I go to the Baekhwan world for now?'
