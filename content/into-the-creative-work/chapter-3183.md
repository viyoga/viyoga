「You have entered Zone 9,141, Mahabaltma.」

「This is a Hidden Zone.」

「The cold of Mahabaltma judges sins.」

「If you endure Mahabaltma for 1 hour, you can obtain dominion over it.」

Mahabaltma was dark.

Nothing was visible except the notification windows. When I focused power into the Brain Eye Golden Focus, I could barely see about a 1-meter radius.

'Not being able to see is one problem... but my whole body feels like it's burning from the cold. The worse issue is that my mana is freezing from the inside out.'

Bzzt, crackle.

I generate brain lightning.

Blue lightning temporarily illuminated the darkness.

Ice.

I was standing on ice. And ice lotuses bloomed everywhere. The dark crimson ice lotuses, as if sculpted by someone, were living beings. Dead yet unable to die, suffering for the sins committed in life.

'...If I stay here, I'll end up like that too.'

As the lightning lit the darkness, a faint path reflected through the Brain Eye Golden Focus became visible. I dumped all my experience points into the Brain Eye Golden Focus. Thanks to having already invested a fair amount into it beforehand, I was able to level it up.

『Brain Eye Golden Focus's level has risen to 4!』

『Brain Eye Golden Focus (Lv.4 exp: 4,218/50,000)』

The Brain Eye Golden Focus was reinforced.

The path that had been faintly visible was now clearly visible.

「Using Lightning Flash (電光石火).」

I transformed my body into lightning and dashed forward. In the full-body transformation state, Mahabaltma's cold diminished, even if only slightly. Unfortunately, there was a cooldown of about 30 seconds, so I couldn't use it consecutively.

After running across the ice field for a while, I stopped before a cracked section of the ice. The path seen through the Brain Eye Golden Focus. Traces of something were pointing inward.

The thought that this might be a trap crossed my mind briefly... but not even 3 minutes had passed and I felt my fingertips freezing, so I abandoned my hesitation and dropped into the crack.

Crackle, bzzt-bzzt!

Since I was still illuminating the darkness with lightning, landing wasn't difficult.

"Even in this damned world, a dazzling thunderbolt strikes. For a moment I thought my nephew had fallen."

I turned my body toward the direction the voice came from.

There was an orange-haired young man with his limbs bound by ice chains. The long-faced youth was grinning at me.

I looked at the coat he was wearing. A coat with a golden dragon painted on a black background.

「Mischief's Trinity gazes upon you.」

"What brings you all the way here? Human."

"...The God of Deceit. Not an avatar, but the main body."

"Hm? You know me? Sorry, but I don't know much about you. Since my main body descended to Atlantis, various restrictions have arisen. The most annoying part is not knowing."

"I don't understand. Mammon too, and you too—why are you all descending to Atlantis with your main bodies now? Even if you came with your main body, you'd only be able to use a fraction of a god's authority."

「The Master of the Heavens watches.」

「The Guide of Warriors watches.」

Loki's gaze briefly turned upward before returning.

"Heh heh. Terrifying gods are watching. Unfortunately, I can't answer that question. Be grateful that I didn't lie even though I could, mortal."

Even if I pressed harder or resorted to torture, I wouldn't hear the answer I wanted.

Considering Loki's divinity, torture would be completely meaningless. Moreover, I had no time.

"Human. I can see you're dying. Release me. As reward, I'll give you this Hell's Robe I'm wearing. I will escape from this place someday. You're merely hastening what will happen anyway."

"I'll just kill you and take the Hell's Robe."

"Kill me? Do you even have the means?"

Loki chuckled and used a god's power. An intangible force spread out centered on him, seizing the space. The god's power that twists and dominates laws—Domain.

Of course, even in the midst of this, the ice chains binding Loki's limbs remained intact. Those chains were undoubtedly connected to the system.

I summoned the Flame Lotus Flying Blade from my inventory. His complexion changed instantly.

"...I see. A sword bearing a god's power. With that sword, you could kill even an avatar."

"I saved my Domain even when fighting the Hell Ogre for this moment."

It wasn't that I'd saved it specifically to kill Loki, but either way, saving the Domain back then had paid off.

"Let's negotiate."

"Kuk. I knew those words would come out immediately. Have you no pride as a god?"

"Pride isn't what matters when things have come to this. It hasn't been long since I descended to Atlantis with my main body. I don't want to go back just yet. If you release me without killing me, I'll cooperate with you for the next year. The God of Deceit himself will cooperate with you."

Loki's offer was tempting.

Looking at Norse mythology, Loki was both a troublemaker and an all-purpose problem solver. There was no need to doubt that ability. If Loki helped me, future matters would go smoothly.

"I'm not fool enough to trust the God of Deceit. Even if you cooperate with me for a year, the moment that year ends you could stab me in the back. You think I'd trust a bastard like you just for one comfortable year?"

"Heh heh. For someone saying that, you're listening to me quite well, aren't you? Do you know what your lips look like right now? They're completely blue. Now they're about to turn black. The glow in those eerie eyes of yours is fading too. Can you last another 3 minutes? Come now, the time for choosing is approaching."

Loki smiled leisurely.

I'd intended to extract meaningful information through conversation with Loki, but as expected of the God of Deceit, he wasn't easy to handle.

"If I kill you, I can definitely prevent Ragnarok."

"Ragnarok? You were worrying about just that? I'm only one of the causes of Ragnarok. Even without me, Ragnarok will happen. The Fire Giant desires to burn the world. I can help you. Release me."

Knowing Loki's capabilities, I wanted to use him even if it meant taking the risk.

Wasn't the reason that even Odin couldn't easily cast out that troublemaker Loki simply because Loki was useful?

But I shook off the temptation. A year of cooperating with Loki. I could already picture myself spending that entire time wondering when and how this bastard would stab me in the back.

"No need. I'll kill that fire giant myself. Ragnarok won't happen. I came to hell to stop it."

Domain expansion.

Starlight rose and coiled around the blade of Hwaryeon Bido. Loki let out a hollow laugh at the sight.

"...I descended in my true body for this.... The plan was ruined before it could even begin. Brother. Well, that's how it goes. Kehehe."

「The Warrior's Guide is silent.」

「The Master of the Skies snorts.」

The blade wreathed in starlight severed Loki's neck.

「You have slain the true body of the God of Mischief.」

「An impossible achievement! All stats increase by 3!」

「The Divine Throne is immortal. However, a portion of the God of Mischief's power weakens.」

Loki's flesh turned to dust and scattered. The chains that had bound his limbs dropped limply with a clatter.

I grabbed the black coat he had been wearing. My hand had no sensation, so moving it was difficult—I forced it to move using lightning and clutched the coat.

「Hell's Raiment (地獄御衣)

Ignores all penalties of the Hell Domain.

One of the Regalia of Hell.

Rank: S」

This was the reason Loki could remain unharmed despite being trapped in Mahabaliputma.

I immediately donned Hell's Raiment.

The effect was felt instantly. The cold vanished. The mana that had been freezing inside my body rapidly melted. Vitality returned to my flesh. Even if my breath froze, my lips did not.

'With this, surviving an hour here is easy.'

There was nothing here, so there was nothing to do. I pulled a comic book from my inventory and passed the time reading.

One hour passed.

「You have endured one hour at the bottom of hell.」

「You have gained dominion over the 9,141st domain, Mahabaliputma.」

「You acquire 1,000,000 AP.」

「As the ruler of Mahabaliputma, you acquire 500,000 AP every 30 days.」

「You acquire the title 'Helldiver.'」

「All stats increase by 1.」

「A hidden domain conquest completion bonus is granted.」

「Bonus 1. Random stats increase by 3.」

「Bonus 2. Two A-rank upgrade tickets.」

「Please select the bonus you desire.」

"The bonuses are stingier than I thought."

If I were only weighing cost-effectiveness, the first bonus—random stats—was the better pick. I only had two skills and traits below A-rank. Both were ambiguous as mainstays. As for stats, once they go up, it's pure gain.

'So I'll raise those two to S-rank instead.'

「Bonus 2. You have selected two A-rank upgrade tickets.」

「A-rank Upgrade Ticket.

Can be used on traits and skills of A-rank or below. When used, the rank of the trait or skill increases by one tier.

Type: Consumable

Rank: A」

I used the A-rank upgrade tickets immediately on Flash Step (A) and Marriage's Blessing (A), which I'd been using frequently as of late.

「Flash Step (電光石火)

Temporarily transforms the body into lightning to move. Immune to physical attacks.

Type: Skill

Rank: S」

Upon reaching S-rank, I could now maintain full-body transformation for over 5 seconds. The cooldown also dropped to under 10 seconds. In exchange, I instinctively knew the mana cost had increased.

「Marriage's Blessing

A blessing received from the goddess of marriage and family.

Can roughly sense the spouse's survival status and location.

Once every 100 days, can teleport without restriction to the spouse's location.

Can bear damage on behalf of the spouse.

Spouse: Elena Baldert.

Type: Skill

Rank: S」

"Hm. The performance doesn't change as dramatically as I expected. And going back is a hassle—maybe I'll just use the click."

「Using Marriage's Blessing (S).」

「The cooldown period has not yet elapsed.」

"You cannot move to a location where your spouse is present."

"...Fuck."

He'd lost all sense of time, so he'd completely forgotten when he'd last used it.
