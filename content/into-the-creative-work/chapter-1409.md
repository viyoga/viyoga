The Korea-Japan Academy Exchange is conducted in a total of 3 rounds over 2 months.

The 1st exchange match is on the 3rd day after arriving at Arashi Academy in Japan.

The 2nd exchange match is after a month has passed.

The 3rd exchange match is a week before leaving Arashi Academy.

In short, it's easy to think of it as a best-of-three series. Of course, the purpose is an exchange, so officially, victory has no meaning. But unofficially, it's accepted as a battle and game between Korea and Japan. Especially since both countries have a lot built up in various ways.
