I stopped walking and looked around.

Solid ground, heavily rising trees, and a sky full of pure white clouds. Nothing special was visible.

There was nothing special. I wondered why Ha Seunghee-ssi chose this place. I quietly watched her.

Ha Seunghee-ssi put down the bag she was carrying on her back. I heard it's an expensive artifact. The bag's capacity is much larger than it looks, and it also reduces the weight of the contents. I vaguely heard that the price exceeds 100 billion won.

I wasn't jealous of Ha Seunghee-ssi's bag at all. Because I have an inventory that is more convenient than Ha Seunghee-ssi's bag.

She rummaged through her bag and took out a glass bottle containing green liquid. HB-1. A replica of Hebil's stimulant. The appearance was no different from the original.

,Are you testing here? There's nothing here? Are you thinking of using it on the trees?

,You don't know anything. There's Bellapuric over there.

,Bella... what?

She rummaged through the bushes. She finds a weed and smiles. She carefully scraped the ground and began digging up the weed.

,Bellapuric. It's one of the medicinal herbs commonly found in dungeons. It has excellent antipyretic effects. The roots, in particular, are one of the ingredients in potions.

,...If it's a potion ingredient, it must be quite expensive.

,Yes. It usually trades for 120,000 won.

After collecting the Bellapuric, she tore the roots to shreds and scattered them everywhere. Then, she pours a bottle of HB-1 into the ground.

The shredded root fragments wriggle and regenerate. The roots go down into the ground, and the upper part becomes a stem and begins to grow. Ha Seunghee-ssi and I stepped back and watched the situation. It didn't even take a minute for the Bellapuric medicinal herb to grow.

It didn't end there. The Bellapuric's fruits fall to the ground, and the fruits that fall to the ground grow and reproduce.

After 3 minutes, the surrounding area was full of Bellapuric.

,There are about 300 plants here. I'm satisfied.

,If 120,000 won is 300...

,It's 36 million won.

,How much are you planning to sell HB-1 for?

,I'm planning to sell it for 50 million won per bottle.

,That's so expensive?

,It's cheap. Think about it. If you can get the seeds of medicinal herbs that cost more than 500,000 won each, you can get several times more profit. Well, the price of medicinal herbs will go down, though.

,What about you monopolizing HB-1 and growing and selling medicinal herbs? You can make more money that way.

,Monopolies cause problems. The biggest problem is that you end up making unnecessary enemies. I want to make as many allies as possible rather than enemies.

Ssssseuuuuueuk.

A creepy sound was heard.

Ha Seunghee-ssi and I looked around and widened our eyes. Plants other than Bellapuric, trees and bushes, began to wither and dry up.

,The side effects are strong. Was it like this on Earth too?

,...No. It wasn't this bad. Bellapuric is... safe. I need to find out if this only happens in dungeons, or if the Bellapuric medicinal herb is special.

She took out a wooden box and stored the Bellapuric. Mana is felt from the box. It seems to be a box with special effects.

,Are you taking it? It's a shame to throw it away.

,I need to research this too. The outside looks fine, but... I don't know what problems there might be inside.

We moved again. We found another medicinal herb. Like before, we used HB-1 to quickly cultivate the medicinal herb. The result was the same as with Bellapuric. The surrounding plants withered and dried up instantly.

,...I need to test it in other dungeons too. Will you help me?

,Yeah. It's not a difficult task.

I looked straight ahead.

There was a calm lake. A lake that seemed to be 1km in diameter. Pink lotus flowers floated on the surface of the water.

It was a mysterious and peaceful sight. But Ha Seunghee-ssi and I didn't approach the lake. This seemingly beautiful lake hides a D-rank monster called Fog Snake.

The Fog Snake is a snake with the head and legs of a frog. They attack with their long tongues. The tip of the tongue is sharp like an awl and has poison.

,Seonbae-nim. Do you know what happens if you spray HB-1 in the water?

,I've never sprayed it in water, so I don't know.

,If it's diluted in water, the effect decreases. If there's too much water, the effect disappears.

,Spraying HB-1 here won't have any effect then.

,That's still unknown. This is a dungeon, after all.

Ha Seunghee-ssi took out 3 bottles of HB-1 from her bag. She poured 3 bottles of HB-1 into the lake at once. The green liquid seemed to be diluting, but it was absorbed into the lotus flowers nearby. A change began. The lotus petals fall, and the lotus base spits out lotus seeds. The lotus seeds sink to the bottom of the lake and grow rapidly.

,The reaction is the same as with other medicinal herbs.

A problem occurred. The other lotus flowers around it withered and died. Not only that, but the water becomes polluted and murky. Only the lotus flowers affected by HB-1 showed off their noble appearance.

Ha Seunghee-ssi reaches out to pick a lotus flower. I pulled her by the nape of her neck. A Fog Snake jumped out of the water and stretched its tongue out into the air where Ha Seunghee-ssi's head had been. The length of the tongue was over 1m.

,Fog Snake?! I didn't even go into the lake, so why suddenly...?!

,It looks like it's pissed off because the water is polluted.

I stepped forward and pulled out Hwaryeon Bido from my waist and swung it. I cut off the Fog Snake's tongue and hit its head. I killed it easily. Fog Snakes can be dealt with easily if you avoid their surprise attacks.

,The problem is that the surprise attack is hard to avoid.

I didn't put Hwaryeon Bido back into its sheath and remained vigilant. There was no guarantee that only one would attack.

,Yujin Seonbae-nim. Please pick some lotus flowers.

,Okay. Is one enough?

,Five. I need them for research. Please pull them out with the roots.

I approached the lake and reached out my left hand. I pulled on a lotus flower. I failed to pull it out with the roots. Because the stem broke.

,Be satisfied with this.

,...What about the roots?

To pull out the roots, I had to go directly into the lake, and I didn't want to do that.

,Do it yourself then.

,...If you're going to help, help properly, won't you?

,I don't want to go into the water.

,Is that so? The hotel I booked today is rumored to have an excellent bathtub... I guess Seonbae-nim won't have a chance to get in the bathtub.

,...A bathtub play, huh. Not bad.

I put my foot into the lake. Several Fog Snakes swam and ambushed me. I swung my sword in the water. The corpses of the Fog Snakes soared upwards with the water.

,Annoying.

Pajijik.

Lightning flashed in my grasp.

,Don't use lightning. It could affect the lotus flowers.

That was a valid point.

,Okay.

I carefully dug out the lotus flowers from the roots.

,This kind of menial work is what slaves do...

It was annoying, but I couldn't help it. Ha Seunghee-ssi will service me tonight for the trouble I've gone through.

,Since she's using me like this... I'll have to see Ha Seunghee-ssi's heat dance tonight to make it worth it.

Bbi-i-i-i-i-i-ik.

I was collecting the fourth lotus flower when an annoying sound rang out in my ear.

It sounded like a flute. A flute sound that was just blown strongly without any playing.

Bbi-i-i-i-i-i-i-i-i-i-i-i-it!!!

I check the source of the sound. It's in the forest about 300m away from here.

,There are no monsters in this dungeon that make that kind of sound. Was the prior investigation wrong? If not...

Someone secretly entered the dungeon, or.

The moment the thought reached there, I threw away the lotus flower in my hand, went out of the lake, and stood next to Ha Seunghee-ssi.

,Seonbae-nim?

,...It's coming.

I pushed Ha Seunghee-ssi behind me and reached out my hand towards the front. Dozens of Fog Snakes jumped out from the front and charged.

Pajijijijijik.

I discharged the lightning gathered in my palm. Dozens of lightning streaks swept through the Fog Snakes. The electrocuted Fog Snakes fell powerlessly to the ground.

Bbi-i-i-ik, bbi-i-i-i-i-i-it!

The flute sound rang out again.

Dozens of Fog Snakes jump out of the lake. The ones with the bodies of snakes and the legs of frogs can be active on land. What's even more terrible is that they don't crawl like snakes but jump like frogs.

,...Fog Snakes are appearing every time the flute sound rings out. Are they being controlled by the flute sound?

Hunters' abilities are diverse. It wouldn't be strange to have the ability to control monsters with a flute sound. And you can think of it differently. Like a flute that controls monsters.

,It looks like they secretly entered the dungeon without us knowing.

,...Association employees were guarding the dungeon entrance.

,They must have taken advantage of a gap and entered. The level of the association employees guarding the entrance is all the same, anyway.

Pajijijijik.

I resist dozens of Fog Snakes charging at me while spraying lightning. Fortunately, they are D-rank monsters with no electrical resistance. I can kill dozens of them just by spraying lightning.

,Until my mana runs out.

Bbi-i-it! Bbi-i-i-i-i-i-it!

Cheom-beong cheom-beong cheom-beong.

Fog Snakes jump out of the lake. They are aiming exactly for Ha Seunghee-ssi and me. I ran with Ha Seunghee-ssi towards the place where the flute sound was heard.

,...Seonbae-nim. We have to kill the guy playing the flute here.

Ha Seunghee-ssi, with a cold face, said. She waved her hand towards the Fog Snakes chasing us. Her ability, invisible claws, cuts the Fog Snakes into 5 pieces.

,Is it Ha Junsoo's doing?

,No. I don't think so. Big Oppa has already missed his chance. He can't physically harm me. If he did, he would lose his position as successor forever.

,He can just do it without getting caught by the Chairman.

,There's no way Grandpa wouldn't have put surveillance on Big Oppa. Anyway, we have to kill him here. HB-1 shouldn't be known to the world yet!

,Do you have any guesses about who he is?

,...There are too many to count. The most suspicious ones are the competing companies... There are more than five companies that could do this. If you include guilds or overseas companies... It's a headache. It would be easier to catch him and interrogate him.

,You said it yourself, we can catch him and interrogate him. What's so difficult about that.

,Seonbae-nim!!

Ha Seunghee-ssi shouted. We had no choice but to stop. Because we were already surrounded by hundreds of Fog Snakes.

,It's ridiculous for them to surround us like this. The intelligence of Fog Snakes is... at the level of ordinary beasts.

,The guy playing the flute must have controlled them.

I answered indifferently and held Hwaryeon Bido in both hands. Pajijijik. Red lightning flows along the blade of Hwaryeon Bido.

Sswiiiiiiiiik!

The Fog Snakes make a sharp sound and open their mouths and stretch out their tongues all at once. The sight was like hundreds of tentacles aiming at us.

Kwak!

Red lightning seeped into the ground and then soared into the sky and spread in all directions. Hundreds of Fog Snakes were electrocuted, and the trees and bushes caught fire.

,Let's go.

,Aren't you going to collect the magic stones? Even if they're the lowest-grade D-rank magic stones... With that amount, it's like hitting the jackpot.

,I'm not interested in small change.
