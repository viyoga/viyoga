I received the fruit containing Vanilla's mana seed and examined it. I had heard rumors about it, but it was my first time seeing it in person, so it was quite fascinating. This kind of item isn't something you can easily acquire even if you have money.

As I focused my mind, I could feel the mana from the fruit. However, it was weaker than I had expected.

,This is a panacea? It's nothing compared to the panacea of the Guangming Ascendance Technique.'

It doesn't seem like it will be of much help to me right now.

"Disappointed now that you've seen it in person?"

"Yeah. I'm disappointed. I don't think it would help even if I ate it."

"Those with skills above A-rank all say that. But not B-rank and C-rank. To them, this mana seed is hope itself! A damn expensive hope, though!"

Park Jeong-gu further explains the uses of the mana seed.

Mana seeds are said to be used in various ways besides direct consumption. A prime example is the manufacturing of special potions, and there are many families or companies with special and secret recipes using mana seeds.

"In short, Vanilla's mana seed has high utility. They say even dog poop has its uses, right? How much more useful would a mana seed be, which can't even be compared to dog poop? It's got a shitton of uses!"

"Let's get to the business. Can HB-1 mass-produce mana seeds? As far as I know, HB-1 isn't very effective for special plants like panaceas."

The reason panaceas are special in the first place is because they contain mana. Even if the plant grows completely with the power of the accelerator, it's meaningless if the mana hasn't matured.

"You know quite a bit about HB-1. I thought you weren't interested?"

"Just answer the question."

"You're right. That's what's officially known. But mana seeds can be produced with HB-1 if the environmental factors are right. Did you think I would have suggested it without knowing this?"

"Is it proven?"

"According to the information, yes."

Park Jeong-gu smirks. Information was also among the items sold by the dark merchant Park Jeong-gu. So it's reliable.

"What are the conditions? Tell me in detail."

"Fertile land. Of course, not just any fertile land. It has to be land where mana is dissolved. It's even better if the mana in the atmosphere is higher."

"That's land that doesn't exist on Earth."

"It doesn't exist on Earth, but it exists in dungeons."

I narrowed my brows. The environments of dungeons vary. Most have higher mana concentrations than Earth, but conversely, some dungeons have low mana concentrations. Even if the mana concentration is high, there are many cases where the environment is frozen or made of rock, making it impossible for plants to grow.

"You're not going to tell me to find a dungeon with the right environment for Vanilla's mana seed, are you?"

"They say you should never say never... but not today."

Park Jeong-gu took out a map from the drawer. It was a map of South Korea. Several places were marked on the map.

,I thought it looked familiar... he's marked open-type dungeons.'

Open-type dungeons. Unlike closed-type dungeons, anyone can enter if they meet the qualifications. Open-type dungeons are where Hunter crimes occur most frequently. At the same time, there is much more public information than in closed-type dungeons.

,Open-type dungeons are managed without clearing them on purpose.'

In that mana stones can be obtained periodically, open-type dungeons were similar to natural resource mines. Hunters are like miners digging up mana stones.

"The markings are different for each dungeon."

"You don't even need to look at the blue markings. They're unsuitable places."

"Most of them are blue, though."

"Exactly."

Park Jeong-gu moves his finger, pointing to various places on the map.

"The places marked in red here are dungeons where mana seeds can be mass-produced. I've already finished the investigation."

"You're confident. Have you already tried it once?"

"No. It's the conclusion I came to by collecting, comparing, and calculating known information."

"I don't really trust theorists."

"It's not a theory, it's a preliminary preparation for a plan, you son of a bitch!"

"Isn't that the same thing? But you're not going to tell me to go around to all these red-marked places, are you?"

"Are you crazy? Of course not. There's an unpopular open-type dungeon. Yeah. Here in the Jeolla Province area... it's a bit far, but you have to put in the effort to do it safely."

"If I get caught?"

"Of course, they'll try to take it away. Be especially careful of getting caught by large guilds. They might sue you according to the law... or they might kill you and take it... either way, it's not good, so proceed as secretly as possible."

I looked at the mana fruit containing the mana seed. After feeling it directly, it didn't feel that great. I've lost some interest, you could say.

,But it's still something that's not easy to get... it'd be good to keep a few on hand in case I need them someday.'

And if I enhance it with the Guangming Ascendance Technique I have, it might become quite a useful item.

I took the HB-1 and the mana seed, got up from my seat, and said to Park Jeong-gu.

"Lend me a car."

"Don't you have a car? How much money do you have that you don't even have a car? Buy a car with the money you earned this time."

"I don't want to use my car for this kind of thing. If you don't like it, I'll quit here."

I hand the HB-1 and mana seed back to him. His face contorts as he realizes that I really don't have much interest.

"Goddamn it. Are you detached from money? But when I see you taking money from me, it doesn't seem like it... I really don't know what you are."

"The more money, the better. But there are many ways to make money even if it's not you."

"Ugh, shit. I guess so. Since you're an A-rank Hunter... Is there anything else you need?"

"What is it? Are you going to take care of me like a pushover? Where did the miser Park Jeong-gu I knew go?"

"Hey. I said that's worth 3 billion, right? If you add the mana seed, it's well over 3.5 billion. If you make a mistake, you're fucked, so you have to prepare as much as possible."

"Was the mana seed 500 million?"

"At the current market price."

"Then you have to get at least seven."

"If my predictions are correct, I can get over 100. You can earn 50 billion with 3.5 billion! This is a money-copying bug!"

"It's a mana seed copying bug. But half of that is mine."

"Whatever. Is there anything else you need?"

"No. I'll take care of the rest myself."

I stopped for a moment as I was about to go outside. When I looked back at Park Jeong-gu, he was frowning.

"What again?! Why?!"

"Why did you choose me for this job? You could have chosen other guys besides me."

"First, skill. You're one of the top guys among my customers. Second, greed for money. You don't seem to have greed for money even though you consistently earn it. Third, it's already been over two years since you and I have known each other. Can't I trust you a little after two years?"

"...It's so easy to earn 50 billion. You dumbass."

"Hey, you son of a bitch! If you run away with my money, I'll chase you to the end of hell and kill you!"

I left the store, leaving him raging behind me.

I had no intention of betraying Park Jeong-gu for a mere 50 billion. My reputation is something I can change by swapping out the Mask of Impunity, but there weren't many dark merchants I could trust like Park Jeong-gu.

I went to the car parked next to the store. It was a common passenger car that could be seen anywhere in South Korea.

By the time I arrived at my destination, the sun had set. Hunters usually work during the day and go home in the evening, so even an open-type dungeon would be deserted inside. Good.

,B-rank open-type dungeon, Fourth Temple.'

As expected, an association employee was guarding the dungeon entrance. Since I'm a B-rank Hunter, I can simply show my Hunter license and easily enter.

But I'm not here as Seong Yujin to do Hunter activities.

,It's better to avoid leaving a record. There's no point in my actions being known.'

I had an item for times like this.

I use the Illusitor. My body becomes transparent. For the next 30 minutes, I will be an invisible man. And 30 minutes was more than enough time.

I control my breathing and erase my presence. The Yeongcheon Style is quite helpful in times like this.

Just as I was about to move forward, I felt a presence behind me. It was a party of five.

"As expected, it's deserted if you come at night. Plus, there aren't many people here, so there won't be anyone inside the dungeon, right?"

"I don't know why we came all the way here for that comfortable hunting..."

"I'm so tired because it's night. Can't we take a nap before going in?"

They chatted and approached the association employee.

Strangely, the association employee blocked their path.

"...What brings you here?"

"What else would a Hunter come to a dungeon for? Here's my Hunter license."

"Hmm."

"If you've confirmed it, please move aside."

"You all look tired since it's night. The Fourth Temple is a dangerous place. How about taking a good nap and entering tomorrow morning?"

It was strange. Association employees guarding dungeons don't usually say this much. Their job is to check the Hunter license and let the Hunter into the dungeon. That's all. They don't care what the Hunter does inside the dungeon. That's the rule.

"Don't be nosy and move aside. We have to go to the dungeon now so we can hunt monsters because there are fewer people. We're a bit short on money, you see?"

The man who appeared to be the leader of the party frowned. His sharp voice was full of annoyance. The association employee reluctantly stepped aside.

"Confirmed. Go ahead."

"Tsk."

The party entered the dungeon.

What happened after that was also strange. As soon as the party disappeared, the association employees gathered and started talking among themselves.

"Team leader-nim. Aren't we fucked?"

"We're not the ones who are fucked, it's those guys. Don't talk nonsense and get one person ready to go in. Ah, don't run into those guys, so go in after 3 minutes."

"Yes."

After hearing the conversation, I understood.

Something is happening in this dungeon. I don't know the details, but it's some kind of illegal activity that even the association employees have been bribed for.

,Why the hell is this happening here of all places? How annoying.'

I stepped back for now.

This might be a trap. I retreated to a place where the association employees couldn't see me and called Park Jeong-woo.

-What's going on at this late hour?

"Your voice is hoarse. Were you sleeping after giving me work to do?"

-That's how much I trust you. And isn't it common sense to sleep at night?

-What are you talking about?
