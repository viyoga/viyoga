Seong Yujin. So, they're demanding my custody? The Awakened Union knows my abilities and understands the necessity of them.

,Yujin-ssi. In the current situation, you should avoid meeting with Acting President Jin Suhak directly. The enemies are probably thoroughly monitoring Jin Suhak. Among them, we can't ignore surveillance using abilities."

,Before the Awakened Union starts acting in earnest, we'll take the lead and seize public opinion first, making the Awakened Union recognized as a rebel force."

Na Chaeyoung - Are you up right now? Turn on the TV and check the news. Or check this link.

,Just asking in case... is this something that's been discussed with the government?"

At the very least, we want to meet the Awakened Seong Yujin directly and confirm that he is safe.
