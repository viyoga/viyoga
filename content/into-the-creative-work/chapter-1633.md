The districts of [God's Atlantis] basically try to maintain good relationships with the surrounding districts. It's incredibly difficult for a district to survive on its own. Therefore, they maintain good relationships with other districts as if they're engaging in diplomacy.

If the relationship with other districts deteriorates to the worst and they become isolated, life becomes difficult. It could even become a matter of survival.

But of course, there are exceptions. A prime example is the desert district to the west. The desert district is basically larger than other districts. But it's not abundant. It's barren. So, it basically doesn't receive attention from other districts.

Inter-district trade? Cases of people from other districts coming to the desert district are rare, except for merchants. Travel purposes? There might be some crazy bastards who want to travel to see the desert in the western outskirts, but not in 'District 5,146, Scorpion Desert' where I am.

So, it was very rare for other districts to contact me like this. It's been quite some time since I established the Cheonma Shin-gyo (Demonic Cult) in this desert and ruled the district, but this is the first time another district has officially approached me.

I, who had been watching the dancers' dance, slowly got up. Perhaps because I had lingering feelings, my feet wouldn't move easily. When I turned my head, the dancers looked at me and smiled.

Even if it was just a superficial smile, I couldn't help but fall for it. Because they were beautiful. Their sweat-soaked skin was firm and bouncy, and their breaths were alluring. Normally, I would have dived into the dancers like diving into heaven and spent heavenly time, but... right now, there are uninvited guests in the district.

I went out of the banquet hall with Enas.

,I think they'll probably ask for an alliance."

Enas said.

It wasn't good or bad. There wasn't much benefit to be gained from an alliance.

I entered the audience chamber. I leaned halfway back on the wide, plush chair. The maids brought wine and fruit. I fondled the maids' breasts and asses as I drank the wine and ate the fruit they handed me. I was happy right now.

,I'm sorry, Ruler of the Scorpion Desert-isiyeo. We do not show our faces to others. That is our culture and our rule."

,This is my district. Don't force your culture and rules on me. If you don't want to show your faces, get the hell out of the city as you are."

,The Ruler of the Scorpion Desert is right. If you come to another district, you must follow the laws and culture of that district."

They slowly unwrapped the cloth covering their faces.

I opened my eyes wide. Because their beauty was above average.

Distinct features, dark brown hair, and bronze skin. But what caught my eye the most were their ears. Long, slender ears. They weren't human, they were elves.

,You're not human."

,Not at all."

District 5,140, Desert Forest.

It's one of the wealthier districts in the western desert. As the name Desert Forest suggests, trees grow in the desert. I didn't expect the residents of the Desert Forest to be elves, though.

,There are many types of elves, so it's not strange."

There are elves depending on nature, such as Wood Elves, Aqua Elves, and Night Elves. It wasn't surprising that there were Desert Elves.

Five days.

This is because they have been carrying water from the oasis and storing it. The problem is that the water in the oasis is not filling up now.
