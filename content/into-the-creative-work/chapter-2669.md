I opened the glass case and took out the bracelet.

A fancy bracelet with small jewels densely embedded. It wasn't just a beautiful bracelet.

「Starway

Bracelet containing the magic of Archmage Haren.

If you have a magic (B) related trait or skill, you can learn Archmage Haren's magic arrow.

Rank: B」

Compared to the effort I put in, Starway's performance was mediocre.

I don't have any magic-related traits or skills, so I can't learn Haren's magic arrow.

,If it's a magic-related trait of B rank or higher... most wizards can't learn it.'

Elena is also a wizard. But she probably wouldn't be interested in Haren's magic arrow. Elena's power has easily surpassed even Archmage Haren's prime.

,I got the second offering.'

I put the bracelet in my inventory and look around. There were quite a few items for display. Trophies, medals, etc. Things related to the academy.

,There's nothing really worth taking.'

My business is done.

I decided to leave the Empire Academy. Of course, I'll erase or manipulate my traces with deception. Even if they feel something is wrong, they won't be able to pinpoint me as the person.

,Honestly, even though I egged them on a bit, I didn't expect things to proceed this easily. I thought they'd check the truth of the rumors... but there was none of that.'

Student Council Chairman Rowoon had plenty of time to verify the rumors. The students gathered and put pressure on him, but that wasn't the will of all the students at the Empire Academy. If Rowoon had responded calmly, I might have had a bit of trouble. But Rowoon was swept away and sided with the students.

,Even though I had prepared the means, I didn't expect them to occupy the academy right away. These guys must have wanted to do this from the start.'

If they took action, they'll have to take responsibility. Of course, I won't be part of that responsibility.

I quietly left the academy.

• • •

The president of the Empire Academy, Marquis Mersid Comen, a direct descendant of Archmage Haren, and the executives belatedly heard the news about the academy and rushed back around evening.

Originally, they planned to have dinner with the Illusion Duke and build a friendship. Even for nobles with titles, meeting the Empire Ogong, including the Illusion Duke, was a rare opportunity, so they were looking forward to it. But with the academy on the verge of collapse, they had no choice but to return early.

When he saw the academy occupied by the students, he could only open his mouth and be shocked.

Red, blue, and yellow letters and graffiti decorated the entire academy. The words written there were also absurd.

(We are resolutely opposed to the admission of commoners!)

(We will perish rather than open up!)

(The Empire Academy is a place of learning for nobles, not commoners!)

,What is this... what is this?! The school has become a mess! There's no place that's intact! How did they even manage to graffiti the 3rd and 4th floors of the building?!"

The president, venting his anger. The faculty members who had been kicked out of the main building watched him carefully.

,The students of the magic department used magic to float their bodies and graffiti with something called lacquer."

,Were you guys just watching?!"

,Hundreds of students came... we couldn't help it."

,Guards!! What were you guys doing?!"

The head of the guards stepped forward with a rotten expression.

,The opponents are students. Our job isn't to suppress students. It's also stated in the contract."

,Even so, you should have stopped them!!"

,It's beyond our authority."

The president, who was about to get angry, noticed the miserable state of the statue of Archmage Haren. The head of the statue was broken and its remains were scattered on the floor.

,No, they dare to touch our ancestor's statue...!!"

,That was also the work of a student. He seemed to be from the knight department... he was quite strong."

,The face? Did you see the face?!"

,He was wearing a mask, so I couldn't see it!"

,Wearing a mask? These crazy bastards! They acted very systematically!!"

,It looks like an impulsive act no matter how you look at it. And these days, masks are essential because of volcanic ash."

,Head of the guards! Whose side are you on?!"

,President-nim. We only do the work we're given. The academy's affairs should be resolved by the academy. We have no justification or obligation to intervene in this matter."

The president glared at the head of the guards once and turned his body towards the main building. The statue can be restored later. What's urgent now is to stop the students' protest.

He felt miserable as he looked at the main building, which had become a mess in less than a few hours.

,To turn a deep-rooted and elegant academy into a state worse than a slum... I'm very disappointed in the students' actions. Shouldn't they have talked first before protesting? A sudden protest and illegal occupation...'

Students were out at the entrance of the main building. At the center of the spirited students was the student council president, Rowoon.

Rowoon, who usually treated the president with a humble attitude, greeted the president with a brazen face.

,...Ahem. Student council president. If you had any complaints, shouldn't you have told me first before making a mess of the school? You guys are making a big mistake right now. I'll give you one last chance. Disperse immediately. And the ringleaders of this incident will be held accountable."

,President-nim. Do you really not know why we're doing this?! The academy started it first!"

,What did the academy do?"

,Admission of commoners! The academy is an educational facility for nobles! Our entire student body resolutely refuses the admission of commoners! President-nim, please withdraw the admission of commoners and leave that fact in writing!"

,Admission of commoners...? We've never even thought about or discussed the admission of commoners. Where did you hear those words and cause this situation?!"

,You've never discussed it? That's a relief then. You can announce the withdrawal of the admission of commoners at an official event!"

,I said we've never had such a discussion! Why should I withdraw a statement I never made?!"

,Anyway, please withdraw it! We won't stop protesting until you withdraw the admission of commoners! Also, please acknowledge that we will inevitably be unable to attend classes while the protest is going on..."

Student Council Chairman Rowoon spewed out his demands.

The president's face turned bright red. Withdraw something that wasn't even his statement? Isn't this an attempt to drag his face through the mud?

,...Rowoon!! I'll say it one last time! Don't regret it later and disperse immediately!"

,We've already stated our position. And you'll be the one regretting it, President-nim."

,What?"

,We've secured the president-nim's corruption documents. The president of the Empire Academy is embezzling the academy's budget. Aren't you ashamed? Withdraw the admission of commoners and resign from your position."

,What the hell are these crazy bastards saying?! You've crossed the line! Head of the guards!! What are you doing! Drag those rioters out of the academy right now!!"

The angry president shouted while pointing his finger. The head of the guards shook his head and stopped the president.

,Calm down, President-nim. The opponents are the academy's students."

,Those bastards aren't students, they're rioters!!"

Rowoon smirked at the president. Then he raised his fist high into the sky.

,We cannot tolerate the president's private school corruption! The president should resign from his position! Also, introduce a direct presidential election!"

,Withdraw! Resign! Introduce!!"

The students' voices echoed. The president went ballistic, but the head of the guards held the president's body tightly and didn't let go. The reporters who had secretly infiltrated the school, sensing the commotion, watched the scene closely.

The next day.

Dopamine called the Empire Academy was sprinkled on the Eustia Empire, which was depressed by the curse of the volcanic island.

The commoners clicked their tongues and cursed at the stupid actions of the Empire Academy students, and the Imperial family and nobles were silent for the time being.

• • •

The president, who had completely lost his mind, didn't hold back. To be exact, he couldn't hold back. If he stood by and watched the situation, the Empire Academy itself would collapse. If that happened, he would have to return the donations, and since the donations had been used and couldn't be refunded, he would go bankrupt.

,If I don't normalize the academy, I'll be ruined!'

The Imperial family and nobles didn't get involved in this situation. There was nothing to gain by getting involved, and there was no real justification.

,Using force is the worst of the worst.'

The students of the Empire Academy are nobles. Using force would mean turning hundreds or thousands of nobles into enemies.

,I'll solve it with the Imperial law.'

The students think they are the owners of the Empire Academy. But the owner of the academy was the president. The academy is the president's property.

,I can't sue all the students... so I'll identify and sue only the ringleaders. I'll have to bring some of them over to my side and induce internal strife. What's certain is that I should never forgive the ringleader, Student Council Chairman Rowoon.'

He had to put an end to it to make sure this never happened again.

The president called cleaning experts to the academy. And he calculated the cost of restoring the academy.

,...This lacquer material can't be removed with water. We need special chemical substances... but since those aren't produced in the Empire... it'll cost a lot of money."

,About how much will it cost?"

,You should expect at least 54 billion pennies."

,...54 billion...? If I ask a magician...!"

,It'll cost more than that. To completely remove the lacquer, you need the power of a high-ranking magician... even if they're high-ranking magicians, you need at least 30 of them. You know how expensive their labor costs are without me having to tell you."

,......"

,Ah, if it's the Illusion Duke, he could restore the entire academy in an instant. He's even restored a half-destroyed city with a single spell..."

,......Would the Illusion Duke grant my request?"

,Would the Illusion Duke grant your request?"

The current Illusion Duke is said to be more gentle than before, but the Illusion Duke was a terrifying being who even dealt with nobles. If you displease her, you might fall into an illusion and die anytime, anywhere. I heard that those who incurred the Illusion Duke's hostility are trapped in an infinitely continuing room.

,54 billion.... Write me an estimate."

,Are you really going to clean it? At this point, it's better to just build a new academy building."

,That might be a good idea too. Anyway, write me an estimate. The academy students will pay the cost."

The president sued 30 students, including Student Council Chairman Rowoon.

A judge was dispatched to the Empire Academy, and the protesters dispersed. The judges were not people that lower-ranking nobles could do anything about. Defy a judge? Even nobles could be sentenced to death.

Rowoon urgently convened a Hana Society meeting. But few Hana Society members attended. Only the members who had been sued attended.

,What about the general students?! What happened to the solidarity of the academy students?!"

,...They refused to attend, saying they had nothing to do with this."

,What about the president's corruption?! Can't we sue the president for corruption!"

,...The money used for corruption wasn't donations from other nobles, but money the president earned through the academy. Moreover, it was recognized as expenses used for maintaining dignity, not corruption."

,I, I'll be removed from the position of successor if I don't resolve this! No, I'll even be kicked out of the family register! A solution! Is there no solution?!"

Rowoon asked desperately. But the students gathered there only lowered their heads. Some even shed tears.

The matter had become too big to resolve. Even if their families stepped in, there was no way to repay 54 billion pennies. The nobles abandoned their children for the sake of their families. It was possible because there were brothers or cousins to replace them.

The noble society was cold.

And the Eustia Empire was not as lenient as modern Korea. Fortunately, they were nobles, so they were spared the death penalty. Although they would no longer be nobles as they were being kicked out of their families.

,This can't be.... Is my life going to be ruined like this?"

,Isn't this all because of you, Chairman-nim! Take responsibility, Chairman-nim!"

,What? Didn't you guys start it first! I just moved according to your opinions!"

Internal strife broke out.

The students passed the buck to each other, and some ran to the president and sold out their colleagues.

54 billion pennies. The president, knowing that it was obviously an amount that 30 students couldn't handle, decided to sue additional students to squeeze out as much money as possible.

,I should sue about 100 more students and get the money. It wouldn't be bad to remodel the academy at this opportunity.'

But the Imperial family and high-ranking nobles, disappointed by this situation, cut off their donations.

The president sued more students and even raised tuition fees. This fact spread widely, and the image of the Empire Academy plummeted into the gutter.

The Empire's nobles didn't hire nobles from the Empire Academy, and the commoners cursed the nobles from the Empire Academy behind their backs.

Students with a proper mind withdrew from the academy, and next year's incoming students also canceled their admission. Some nobles who had been watching for an opportunity established new academies and absorbed the students.

In short, the Empire Academy was ruined. It was ruined very miserably.

,Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaack!!"

Rowoon, who had been the student council president, screamed towards the sky. But his debt was not reduced. As a price for happily graffitiing the academy, he would live as a slave for the rest of his life, drowning in debt. They couldn't even get a loan from the Imperial Bank.

Unfortunately for them, that wasn't the end. It was belatedly revealed that Starway, which could be called the academy's treasure, had been stolen. The president naturally sued the students additionally, and the students were in a situation where they would even run away with their lives.

,Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaack!"

The academy president also screamed. It was certain that one of the students had taken the family's treasure, but he couldn't find the culprit.

Still, he was in a better situation than the students. Even though his reputation had plummeted, he still had a lot of money.

「The Gods are enjoying watching the Empire Academy situation!」

「The Lord of the Sky donates 3,000AP.」

「The King of the Devil's Heaven donates 6,666AP.」

「The Remaining Moon in the Darkness donates 2,000AP.」
