-I don't know. If I find it quickly, it won't even take a minute... but in the worst case, it could take more than 5 minutes.

The gang was rushing back to their hideout with trucks and bikes.

"It won't take long for them to arrive. Probably within 3 minutes. They seem to have just finished a job, so they'll go straight to the warehouse...."

-10 minutes.

-Stall for time, then. Use some magic or something to cause a ruckus. Buy me time to get out with the goods. You were planning to wipe them out anyway, right?

"Can you definitely secure the goods? That's the most important thing."

-Trust me.

I sighed and headed towards the bastards.

It was hard to trust Invisible Blade. But since we're working together now, I had to trust her.

The trucks stopped at the entrance. I looked down at them from the hill and opened my Astral.

[Using Ephemeral Moment. Remaining stacks: 6]

Time slowed down. Or rather, only my mind accelerated. In this slowed-down world, I selected magic and calculated the formula.

,If I want to draw attention, it's best to unleash something big.,

Huuuuuung.

Mana raced through the mana rod, turning into lightning, and the lightning became a formula that rose into the sky. The sensation was more than just electrifying. This feeling of being connected to magic was like manipulating the sky at will.

A blue lightning bolt erupted from the center of the cloudless, dry sky. The small lightning bolt gradually increased in size, now shining brightly and exuding its presence. The people on the ground noticed the anomaly and looked up at the sky.

I looked at those people and quietly recited the incantation.

[Thunder Break]

A massive lightning bolt struck the truck faster than the sound of thunder. The truck was completely destroyed, and the aftermath of the force spread around the area, creating a small crater. High-voltage current remained in the ground of the crater and did not disappear.

The power of the 6th-circle lightning magic, Thunder Break, which is a higher-level magic than the 5th-circle magic Thunder Bolt, was exactly as expected.

,If it had hit the generator over there, it could have blown up the entire hideout.,

I touched my neck. The mana rod was hot. If the 16 broken mana rods were intact, the burden on the mana rod would have been less than half of what it is now.

Kwa kwa kwa kwa kwang!

The thunder rumbled a beat later.

I was about to tell Invisible Blade that the entrance was cleared, but I stopped. A man was getting up from the crater where the current was still lingering.

"...He's still alive after taking that hit? He's no ordinary guy."

It was a man with half of his face made of machinery. The two mechanical eyes of the bastard flashed red. The bastard's gaze was directed exactly towards me.

,A man with half of his face made of machinery.... That's Prong, the boss of the Prong gang. A cyborg who's still moving even after being hit by lightning magic....,

It probably wasn't ordinary machinery. Maybe he spent all the money he made from robbery and organ trafficking just on his body.

Prong raised his right hand. His index finger pointed at me. Immediately after, a laser was fired. It was too fast to react. Still, the barrier I had put up in advance blocked the laser. The light of the laser collided with the barrier, split, and evaporated. The barrier also shook unstably and then disappeared.

,4 barriers left. He probably can't fire lasers this fast and powerful 4 times in a row. There's a limit to the output that a cyborg can have.,

-Nice. The number of people has dropped significantly.

-The dress has magic on it. It's not for combat. Some crazy bitch put high-level magic on a dress just for fashion.

"Is there anything to be surprised about? Where there's demand, there's supply. If you've got it, get out here."

"If the dress is safe, there's nothing to worry about. I'll wipe out the bastards here."

,Blue Distortion, which has the effect of distorting mana wavelengths, is an essential ingredient for mana recovery... but it's a good magic material to use as a catalyst for barriers or wards.,

Of course, I can't just use it as is. Pre-preparation is needed to control the mana distortion effect. The Blue Distortion petals I'm holding have all been pre-processed.

I scattered the petals into the air. The petals, emitting a subtle light, fluttered in the wind.

[Mana Reversal Field]

The petals fluttering in the wind scattered in all directions as if drawn by some invisible force. And a mana reversal field spread throughout this area. The movements of the enemies who entered the field noticeably slowed down.

"Whhaaaaatttttt iiiisssssss gooooiiiinnng ooonnnnnnnn? Myyyyyyy booodddddyyyyyyy iiiisssssss slooooowwwwwwww."

"You have entered my domain."

"Crush them."

They were withstanding the gravity that would have burst the whole body of an ordinary person with the power of machinery. I surveyed the number of bastards. 13 people. If you exclude the few bastards in the hideout, it can be said that they all came out.

"I have to conserve as much power as possible to deal with that bastard."
