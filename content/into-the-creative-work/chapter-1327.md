I decided to shelve all the other stuff and deal with the cracked blade of Hwaryeon Bido first. Anyway, cleaning up after the war wasn't my job, so I didn't care.

When someone's sick, you go to a doctor. When a machine breaks down, you go to an engineer. When something's wrong, you go to an expert. I went to the dwarf slave village in Tevra.

,Welcome, Count-nim! We're making weapons and armor every day as you ordered!

As soon as I showed up in the village, the dwarves prostrated themselves on the ground. It was their own way of surviving, having learned from my usual bullshit. I looked around at the Frost Hammer Tribe with a serious face and said,

,Order. Gather all the dwarves.

The dwarves spread my order throughout the village with uneasy faces. In less than ten minutes, all the dwarves gathered in the center of the village.

,Count-nim. Was there a problem with the delivered goods...?

Pesus, the chief of the Frost Hammer Tribe, said, trembling with fear.

Instead of explaining with words, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido.

,Hiiiik?!

As soon as I pulled out the knife, the dwarves let out unsightly screams and collapsed to the ground.

,P-Please spare us!

,If we made a mistake, we'll fix it right away! Please give us a chance!

,A-Are we perhaps no longer useful?

I frowned. I didn't like the dwarves acting like this. Should I kill one as an example? I seriously considered it, but then realized this wasn't the time.

,Look at this! The blade of Hwaryeon Bido is cracked!

,It's hard to see from here. Can we take a closer look?

,Sure. Look carefully. If the crack in Hwaryeon Bido gets any bigger, or if Hwaryeon Bido breaks... you'll all be slaughtered.

,Hiiiik! W-We'll look very carefully!

Pesus approached. He looked at the cracked part very carefully. His face turned serious.

,Count-nim. Can I take an even closer look?

,Sure.

Pesus reached out his hand. He wanted me to hand over Hwaryeon Bido. I was reluctant to hand Hwaryeon Bido to someone else, but I had no choice since it was to fix Hwaryeon Bido.

,Heo-eok!

As soon as Pesus received Hwaryeon Bido, he was startled and dropped Hwaryeon Bido on the ground.

,You little shit!

,Hiiik! S-Sorry!

Pesus put on the gloves hanging from his waist and held Hwaryeon Bido in his hand. Pesus's face didn't look good. It wasn't just because he dropped Hwaryeon Bido.

,You guys. Come and look at this knife.

Pesus said to the surrounding dwarves. The dwarves, while watching my reaction, couldn't hide their curiosity and slowly approached.

,Heo.... This is almost a Cursed sword. Hey, you're a Cursed sword expert. Do you know anything?

,Hmm.... I've made Cursed swords on purpose a few times, but... this is on a different level. It's naturally turning into a Cursed sword. And wasn't the material of this knife said to be Red Dragon scales and dark iron? To artificially make it a Cursed sword, you'd have to kill at least hundreds of thousands of people and accumulate grudges....

,Killed hundreds of thousands of people...?

,That's the minimum. Look at the knife. There are traces of washing away the grudges contained in the knife through reforging. But it's still like this.

,Can't you just reforge it?

,Are you crazy! Didn't you see the chief scream as soon as he grabbed the hilt? If you try to reforge it, you might die without even hammering it a few times. The best thing to do with a knife like this is to get rid of it.

The dwarf glanced at me, realizing he'd said too much. I glared at him with wide eyes. He lowered his gaze and mumbled.

,D-Destroying such a masterpiece is absolutely unacceptable. Don't you all think so?

,O-Of course.

,We shouldn't destroy a masterpiece that will be remembered in history.

They were all watching my reaction.

,Stop talking bullshit and fix Hwaryeon Bido.

The dwarves started whispering among themselves. I didn't like it, but I couldn't help it. I was the one in a hurry.

The dwarves' meeting ended. Pesus, holding Hwaryeon Bido, carefully approached me.

,Count-nim... please don't get angry and listen calmly.

,If you don't talk bullshit, I won't have any reason to get angry.

,Ahem.... T-That is....

Pesus hesitated. He looked around for help, but the dwarves were looking at Pesus with cold eyes.

,Hoo. I'll be honest. We can't fix Hwaryeon Bido with our skills.

,Are you saying you don't want to?

,N-No, absolutely not!

,I heard what you guys were saying earlier. You were talking about Hwaryeon Bido being a Cursed sword or something.

,Cursed sword. In other words, a demon sword. It refers to a weapon that has been corrupted by grudges or curses. It has special abilities... but they're usually bad.

,Just get to the point. Just get to the point.

,...Hwaryeon Bido is a knife that is undergoing demonization. The problem is that the demonization has stopped because the blade is cracked. Usually, in this case, the demonic energy... that is, things like grudges and curses, would escape and it would become ordinary metal.... But Hwaryeon Bido isn't doing that.

,So what's the reason you can't fix it?

,Because if we try to fix it, we might be crushed by the curse and grudges and die. O-Of course, we're prepared to risk our lives to fix Count-nim's Hwaryeon Bido. But if we try and die... both Hwaryeon Bido and us will face bad consequences.

,What if you get rid of the grudge or curse?

,It's already been reforged once. If we purify it in this state... Hwaryeon Bido won't be able to withstand it and will break.

,So what are you saying? It's cracked and the demonization has stopped? You're saying I should just leave it alone?

,S-Sorry! Hwaryeon Bido's durability is excellent, so it won't break right away! I think you can use it for at least 100 years!

,You little shit....

,Hiiiik!

I sighed. These incompetent dwarves were one of my sources of income. A source of income that I could suck dry for the rest of my life. If I killed them in a fit of anger, the loss would be enormous.

,Okay. I'll have to find another way. You guys go back to work.

,C-Count-nim. Looking at the state of Hwaryeon Bido, it seems like you killed a tremendous being.... Can we ask what kind of being it was?

,You can tell that too?

,Our conclusion is that it wouldn't be in that state if you killed an ordinary being....

,Hmm. I killed a Red Dragon. It was an adult.

,Heok! T-That's right. So that's why it's in that state....

Pesus and the other dwarves nodded as if they understood something.

I looked for others. I couldn't easily give up on Hwaryeon Bido here. According to Pesus, I could use it without any problems for 100 years, but.... To me, who uses the Game Life App, 100 years doesn't feel that long.

,100 years in the world of Radiant Ascension is nothing. I'll basically keep Hwaryeon Bido in my inventory, but....

First, I met Yuria. She solves most things for me. Coincidentally, Sharnel and Priscilla were also by her side. Priscilla is an Ancient Dragon, so I could ask for her advice on the state of Hwaryeon Bido.

Yuria's eyes widened when she saw the state of Hwaryeon Bido.

,This is... amazing.

,Dullibard. The grudge of that half-wit is vividly contained. Usually, that wouldn't happen.... Is it because it's a knife made of dragon scales?

,I, I don't really understand even if I look at it.

Yuria shook her head.

,I'm sorry, Master. It doesn't seem like something I can do anything about.

,If you touch this carelessly, the curse will transfer to your body. It's a material that can't even be made into a magic tool. I recommend throwing it away.

I sighed at their reactions.

Yuria and Priscilla gave up. In other words, there's no way to fix Hwaryeon Bido in this world.

But it's still too early to give up completely.

I have the Game Life App. Someday, I'll be able to get an item that can fix Hwaryeon Bido.

,If not, I can just go to a world where I can fix Hwaryeon Bido.

I looked at the blade of Hwaryeon Bido with a heavy heart. The cracked part caught my eye. It's not even 1cm, but it bothered me too much.

,......Wouldn't the crack disappear if I sanded it?

Was it just my imagination? I felt like Hwaryeon Bido trembled slightly.
