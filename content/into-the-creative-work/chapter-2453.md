I destroyed that bastard's heart, the magic stone that was his power source, and killed him.

[Telekinesis]

I took out a new body from my inventory.

This time, it wasn't a human corpse.

A monster.

It was a goblin's corpse.

,The corpse is as intact as possible, a corpse killed by burning the brain with lightning. Even if other organs are fine, the brain can't function.'

I embed the soul core into the goblin corpse's head. I put the magic stone in the heart.

Twitch.

The goblin corpse moves. The traces of the soul core and magic stone embedded in the corpse quickly disappeared. It recovered.

,One of the functions of the soul core. It uses mana to recover. ...No. Since he's already dead, it's not recovery, but restoration.'

I know the magic to create a soul core from a target. But I don't know exactly what principle the soul core is created on. It's the same even if I look at the formula.

,Ilario created this secret magic with a demon. I should assume that the power of a demon was involved.'

Not just any low-level demon. At least a high-level demon. Maybe a Demon God-class demon made a contract with Ilario.

"He speaks human words clearly. It's impossible to speak in a clear tone with the vocal structure of a goblin. Is he using mana to speak? Well, Ilario would have thought about putting this in a doll from the start.... He must be able to speak regardless of the vocal cords."

"You son of a bitch!! Turn me back to normal!!"

The bastard gets angry, shouts, and rushes at me.

Bang! Kwakwang!

"He can use his abilities even in a goblin body. Your ability is related to the soul."

[Corpse Explosion]

But the magic didn't manifest.

,The 5th-circle black magic Corpse Explosion explodes corpses that are far away, or undead made of corpses. Undead with no magic resistance can't resist. But Corpse Explosion didn't work.'

The goblin corpse was ordinary. It has no magic resistance. It means that the soul core itself has high magic resistance.

I snap my fingers. A blood-red blade appears and cuts off the goblin's limbs.

"Aaaagh! You bastard!!!!"

"If you act up one more time, I'll torture you forever."

"......"

The bastard immediately shut his mouth.

,The biggest disadvantage of Necromantic Art is that there's no way to control the created soul core. Since the magic resistance is so high that the Corpse Explosion I use doesn't work... I should assume it's impossible to control it with necromancy.'

In that sense, Necromantic Art was terrible as necromancy. There was no way to control the soul core written in the magic tome at all.

,Ilario. This crazy bastard had no intention of controlling the doll he made through Necromantic Art. Just a pervert who was satisfied with just making the doll.'

The bastard with his limbs cut off died like that. The soul core didn't have enough recovery power to regenerate the cut-off limbs.

I waited a little longer, but the bastard didn't die. The mana of the magic stone kept him alive. Even though his life was bleeding out to a critical extent.

"I, isn't it enough now? Please stop killing me...."

"There's still one left."

I took out a doll from my inventory. On the outside, it looks like an ordinary human, but the inside is filled with machines. Inside, it has a mana power source that is so sophisticated that it can't be compared to magic stones.

I put the soul core into the head of the mechanical doll.

"I, is this a human body? I can hear the sound of machines turning inside my body...."

"It's a machine."

"...But why do I feel sensations as if I'm alive?"

"It's the function of the soul core."

I snap my fingers. A blood-red blade appears and cuts off his arm.

"Kkeuaaaaagh!"

The bastard grabbed his severed arm and was in pain.

Mechanical dolls don't have a function to feel pain. Feeling pain is purely the power of the soul core.

,A perfect doll. That means a human. Since the doll itself is an imitation of a human. Not only senses and pain, but probably even desires.'

Ilario didn't think of Necromantic Art as more than a tool. His real purpose was to create souls.

,So he tried to copy the soul core to create a new soul core, creating something from nothing.'

But he failed.

The copied soul core was similar to the original. It was also a degraded version.

While I was lost in thought for a moment, the bastard moved on his own.

Bang!

He smashed his own head with his remaining hand.

The shattered soul core was visible among the shattered machine pieces.

,Once a soul core is broken, it can't be made into a soul core again.'

Ilario also gave the doll the right to commit suicide.

,I need to be careful when managing them. But this isn't impossible either. Even if they try to commit suicide, it means that I can defend the soul core.'

If it's exactly the same as a human, there will also be a desire for life. The reason why the bastard committed suicide was because he didn't want to be experimented on and suffer.

,If I coax or trade with them, it seems like it'll work somehow. If I use magic engineering well... I can create controllable subordinates.'
