Peo-eok!

The last member of the Cheolodan cut through the scarecrow's body with his sword. A feat accomplished in just one shijin (two hours).

I, who had been sitting on the floor, stood up.

,Finally, it's over. I'll get my reward and go up to the 3rd floor."

,Yes! Seong Yujin-nim!"

The reward on the 2nd floor was better than on the 1st floor. Once again, I chose the elixir without hesitation. And I chewed and swallowed the elixir right there.

I thought the other members would also choose elixirs, but surprisingly, some chose martial arts.

,Look at this Sumjin Footwork. If used well, it can be a life-saving technique."

,I chose the Sand Shark Swordplay. I saw a ronin using it last time, and it was quite a good swordsmanship."

,Hey. I'll give you money, so let me see it too."

I tilted my head. I didn't understand. It was better than the martial arts offered on the 1st floor, but the martial arts on the 2nd floor were also trash.

,Jin Gu-eon. Why did the members choose martial arts?"

,Seong Yujin-nim. We are ronin. We rarely have the opportunity to acquire even third-rate martial arts. Few of us have learned proper martial arts. Getting martial arts is not easy. So, it seems they chose martial arts for now. Also, unlike medicinal herbs, martial arts are not one-time use, so members can share them."

Martial arts were given as manuals. That meant they could be shared with the members or sold outside.

,Will those kinds of martial arts even sell?"

,They will. You can sell them for at least 30 silver nyang. The book itself is rare."

,I see."

I went up to the 3rd floor of the Manmu Tower. As soon as I entered the 3rd floor, I smelled blood.

,This is...."

The 3rd floor was a space reminiscent of the old variety show 'Departure! Dream Team'. Moving ground, arrows and sharp spears flying through the air, and a thumping ceiling, all using mechanical formations.

It was much more brutal than the variety show. The corpses were gone, but the blood on the walls was still fresh, as if it had just been spilled.

The soldier guarding the entrance to the 3rd floor glanced at us and said calmly.

,Do you see the end over there? The trial of the 3rd floor ends by moving to that end. Isn't it simple?"

,......."

The members swallowed hard, feeling nervous. Simple? It was only simple in words. For those who had to run through a path full of traps, it was not funny at all.

,Those who feel fear now, give up. More people die here than you think. The 4th floor is similar to the 3rd floor. However, it has even more dangerous mechanical formations installed."

Some members looked hesitant, as if they were considering giving up.

Jin Gu-eon stepped forward.

,Are you going to give up on just the 3rd floor? Have you forgotten why we entered the Manmu Tower?"

,Captain.... But...."

,But this and but that. Don't be scared by something like this. Look ahead carefully. There are rules. The way to avoid it is already known. Just jump and run to avoid it. I.... No, we came here ready to die. Our destination is the 10th floor, and we must pass at least the 5th floor. Because from the 5th floor, we can get real martial arts and real elixirs. If you are still afraid, run away. But, don't you dare say that you, a fleeing loser, were active in the Cheolodan. The moment you tarnish the Cheolodan's honor, I will find you and kill you."

,......."

No member ran away.

Jin Gu-eon approached me.

,Seong Yujin-nim. May I try this floor first?"

,Go ahead."

Jin Gu-eon stood in front of the mechanical formation. After checking the position of the moving floor, the flying arrows, and the spears, he gathered his inner strength and started running.

Light Body Technique? There were too many traps to use the Light Body Technique. It could rather make him trip. Jin Gu-eon ran without using the Light Body Technique. He deflected the incoming arrows and spears with his large sword.

Jin Gu-eon tried not to lose his tempo, and he reached the end while maintaining that fast tempo.

,Oooooooo!!"

The members shouted. Morale rose. Seeing Jin Gu-eon do it easily without any crisis, they gained confidence that they could do it too.

,I'm next."

I ran without looking back. Unlike Jin Gu-eon, I didn't swing my sword either. I avoided all the traps and ran to finish the trial.

,It's only 300M. It's a piece of cake.'

Seong Jigone ran after me. Seong Jigone also passed without difficulty. The members also started running one by one. Not everyone passed... Two people died. One tripped and fell into a pit, his body pierced by a spear. The other couldn't avoid a flying arrow.

The atmosphere of the Cheolodan turned cold.

The soldier said indifferently what he had to say.

,Congratulations on passing. Choose your reward and go up to the 4th floor. Those who want to give up without going up to the 4th floor, please wait over there."

And on the 4th floor, 3 more members of the Cheolodan died.

I entered the 5th floor.

Strangely, the 5th floor was made of nature. A bright sky and quietly flowing clouds, dense trees, and a refreshing meadow just by looking at it. A sorcerer with tremendous skill must have created the space.

The meadow was full of tents. The number of people who had already settled on the 5th floor was at least over 100,000.

The soldier said.

,The trial of the 5th floor will begin in four days. You are free to do whatever you want until then. You can practice martial arts, or you can rest. And I'll tell you in advance. We are not your nannies. We will not participate even if disputes arise. Solve the problems yourself."

With those words, the soldier disappeared somewhere.

I and the Cheolodan looked around. All the good spots were already taken.

,Seong Yujin-nim. What should we do? Shouldn't we take a spot near the trees in case of emergency?"

,There's already a spot."

,We can take it."

That's quite a plan.

However, that plan wasn't just thought up by Jin Gu-eon. The 5th floor was already occupied. The guys with the trees had tents that felt classy. Of course, their owners weren't ordinary either.

,It's a bit much to start a fuss from the beginning just to take a spot. Pitch a tent in a reasonably empty place. We're not going to stay here for long anyway, so there's no need to waste energy."

,Hmm. That's true. You guys. Go over there and pitch a tent first. Starting with the tents that Seong Yujin-nim and Seong Jigone-nim will use!"

Of course, it was a personal tent. Unless there were special circumstances, there was no reason for two men to share a tent.

I sat on a simple wooden chair and watched the members work their asses off.

,Sitting still in a chair while others work their asses off.... This is power, and this is flex.'

Before the tent was finished, 50 ronin rushed in and surrounded us.

,Hello. You guys look like a ronin group too, what's your name?"

I stopped Jin Gu-eon from stepping forward and got up.

,Cheolodan. What are you?"

The guy with nail marks instead of hair smiled slyly.

,Cheolodan? I've never heard of that ronin group. We are the Grotesque Sound Clan."

As soon as Jin Gu-eon and the Cheolodan members heard the name Grotesque Sound Clan, they froze. It seemed to be quite famous in the world of ronin.

,So? What business does the great Grotesque Sound Clan have with us?"

The Grotesque Sound Clan leader looked at me as if he was trying hard. I started to feel bad about the way he looked down on me. Should I show my force?

,...No. Let's listen to what he has to say.'

The Grotesque Sound Clan leader patted my shoulder with his large hand.

,We have a lot of people, right? Not only a lot, but we eat a lot too. But the Manmu Tower tells us to spend four days here? Oops, I accidentally didn't bring enough food? I'm going to starve to death? But I'm the Grotesque Sound Clan leader, right? I can't just stand still as the leader, right?"

,I understand what you're saying. I have a way. You came to the right place."

,Haha. I'm glad we're on the same page. Our Grotesque Sound Clan and Cheolodan can be friends."

,It's an easy way. What's the reason you need food? It's you! It's because you're alive! If you die, you won't need food anymore! How about it? Isn't it a perfect solution?"

,...What?"

Pajijijik.

Lightning sparked in my right fist.

,Instant.'

Before he could dodge, I swung my fist with all my might. The fist hit the guy's face accurately. Peo-eok! The guy's head exploded.

,You bastard!"

,The leader is dead!"

,Kill him!"

In an instant, the surroundings heated up and filled with murderous intent. In this brutal place, Seong Jigone moved the most agilely after me. He drew his sword and cut down two nearby Grotesque Sound Clan members in an instant.

Jin Gu-eon took care of the remaining members instead of rushing at the enemy recklessly.

,Stick together! And endure as much as possible! Seong Yujin-nim and Seong Jigone-nim will take care of those guys! We just need to endure!"

I drew the Hwaryeon Bido and sliced at the enemies in sight. There was no reason to hesitate. The other ronin were quietly watching from a distance, and the soldiers were busy watching with their arms crossed, without even getting involved.

Murder was legal in the Manmu Tower. As long as the opponent was not a soldier of the Manmu Tower.

,They're running away!"

,It's done! What are you doing, you bastards! Chase those bastards and kill them! Let the name of the Cheolodan be known!"

Jin Gu-eon shouted at the top of his lungs.

Most of the people around here are ronin. And once a ronin is looked down upon, it's over. It's better to have a reputation. That way, people around you won't pick a fight. Even if it's a bad reputation.

I chased the fleeing guys to the end and massacred them all. In addition, the Grotesque Sound Clan's luggage became ours. There was quite a lot of luggage. There was a little shortage of food, but we got 12 gold nyang, martial arts, and medicinal herbs.

I shared the medicinal herbs with Seong Jigone, and gave the martial arts to the Cheolodan.

,Thank you, Seong Yujin-nim!"

,I will not forget this grace!"

,As expected of Seong Yujin-nim. Your generosity is amazing!"

,Yujin-ah. Something is strange here."

,I took a walk around, and there's no water on the 5th floor."

I widened my eyes.

There's no water.

That was quite a serious problem. The temperature here was strangely high, and it was very dry. It's okay now, but tomorrow I'll desperately want a glass of cold water. And someone has food and water.

,There's a reason why they put us here for four days. It's not just a pure rest. This is a kind of trial of the Manmu Tower.'

The official 5th floor trial of the Manmu Tower hasn't even started yet.

,The Manmu Tower's purpose is to drop more than half of the entrants from the 5th floor.'

,Jigone. Have Jin Gu-eon set up a night watch. There will be guys doing stupid things. And make sure to secure food.... especially drinking water."

,Uh. I know. But do we really need to focus on guarding the food? If it's you...."

I raised my hand. Seong Jigone shut his mouth tightly. It's a secret that I can take out an almost infinite amount of food from my inventory.

,If the 5th floor trial is what I think it is.... It will be a good opportunity for me.'
