The 50th floor of the labyrinth was a peaceful lakeside environment.

At the center of the 50th floor was a huge lake, surrounded by smaller lakes. The ceiling looked like a bright blue sky, a cool breeze blew gently, and the land was devoid of monsters, making it quiet and peaceful.

"Excessively peaceful."

That was my impression of the 50th floor from afar. It was so peaceful that Claudia's words about a trap on the 50th floor felt like a lie.

"It's a barrier. Tata-ri and the adventurers are concealing themselves with the barrier. Tata-ri is waiting for you, Count-kakka, to go deeper."

"Indeed. I think I can feel something too. Do you know where they are?"

"They were hiding in the path between those lakes, preparing an ambush... But they probably realized I betrayed them, so their location has likely changed."

"They'll show themselves if we go. Don't let your guard down and let's advance to that big lake."
