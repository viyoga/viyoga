The day after the 1-on-1 sparring practical exam, the 1-on-1 combat exam against large monsters began.

Some students expressed dissatisfaction with the publicly conducted exam method and formally protested to the academy. The academy responded in 10 minutes.

Maruhan Academy wants students to learn even a little bit. All practical exams are conducted publicly because there are things to learn just by watching other students' battles.

The academy's response was firm. Some students, concerned that their power would be exposed to others, protested to the academy again, but it was no use. Publicize it outside the academy? Impossible. The academy's cause and reason were perfect.

However, this was an exam. If the exam proceeded in order and it was open to the students taking the exam, the students who competed later had an advantage.

This is an undeniable fact that the academy cannot refute.

The academy came out brazenly. They started the 1-on-1 monster combat exam with the ranking of the 1-on-1 sparring. In other words, it meant that I, who was ranked 1st in the 1-on-1 sparring, would take the 1-on-1 monster combat exam first.

,Good to be able to do it quickly.'

I had no complaints about this exam method. Rather, I welcome it. There's no need to wait unnecessarily. Unlike the written exam, if you finish the practical exam quickly, the academy schedule ends that day.

,It's 9 a.m. now. If I finish the exam quickly, I can relax and play.'

Jeo-beok jeo-beok.

A stern-faced supervisor walked into the arena.

"Seong Yujin. The combat time is a maximum of 2 minutes, and the large monster you will face is randomly set. The randomness of large monsters is guaranteed by the Hero Association and government agencies. Anything else you're curious about?"

I glanced at the arena control room. Officials from the Hero Association and government agencies were gathered there. The contents of this exam will be exposed to the outside of the academy knowingly or unknowingly.

"No."

"Good. I'll start right away because I'm behind schedule."

Bbi-i-i-i-ik!

The supervisor blew the whistle.

The Aurora System is activated. The space flickered for a moment, and a Twin Head Ogre was created from it. It was a large monster more than twice the size of a normal ogre. The two heads attached above the neck glared at Seong Yujin.

"Twin Head Ogre...!"

"A Twin Head Ogre that even an A-class hero has difficulty facing alone... Is Seong Yujin's luck running out here?"

"Yujin-ah, fighting!"

"I hope just a normal ogre comes out when it's my turn."

The surroundings were noisy.

Everyone expected my failure. This is because the Twin Head Ogre was the strongest of the large monsters summoned.

I ignored the reactions of the onlookers and used the Instant.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

The ogre's steps toward me slowed down. I thought in the slowed world.

,I didn't really expect this guy to come out.'

What should I do?

Should I show my full power? Or should I just fight roughly and end it so that my evaluation doesn't drop? Even if it's a Twin Head Ogre, it basically fights like an ogre, so it's not difficult to drag out the 2 minutes. Unlike these babies here, I have plenty of combat experience with monsters.

『Name: Seong Yujin

Strength: B Stamina: B Agility: B Durability: C+ Mana: A+

Traits: Spirit Eye (S) Demon Hunter (S)

Skills: Spirit Contract (A) Spirit Possession (A) Barrier (C+) Swordsmanship (B+)

Karma: Good (善) 34

I check my stats and estimate. Overall, the stats are well balanced. In particular, the mana stat is at a level that even active A-class heroes envy.

,Let's go all out. But without using a sword.'

Having made a decision, I summoned Thunder Owl Mocha.

Kwa kwa kwang!

Mocha appeared with a thunderous sound and spread its pure white wings. Blue currents flowed around it like flower petals. As it became a high-level spirit, it became flashy only on the outside.

"Kkuuk, kkuuk!"

And it possessed power stronger than its flashy appearance.

"Mocha, Spirit Possession. Cut that pig bastard's neck in one go."

"Kkuuk!"

Mocha fell toward me at a rapid speed. It enters my chest and possesses me.

Spirit Possession.

My skin turns white, and wings made of electric current crackle menacingly on my back. I spread my thunder wings and jumped into the sky. The wings flutter and I fly through the sky. The Twin Head Ogre became like a dog chasing a chicken and looked at me.

,I'm overflowing with power.'

High-level spirit.

If the highest-level spirit can be said to be a natural disaster itself, the high-level spirit possesses the power of a local god who rules over an area. In fact, most spirits who act as local gods are high-level spirits or higher.

Mocha had not been a high-level spirit for long, so its power could not be said to be complete. Nevertheless, after accepting Mocha's power, I felt like I could easily defeat a Twin Head Ogre.

Paji jiji jik.

The thunder wings emit electricity. The electricity gathers in my hand and takes the form of a spear.

I was recalling past memories.

The lightning of the 'Master of the Sky' that I briefly saw in the Atlantis of God. Recreating Astrape.

,I won't be able to follow that power exactly. But Astrape was also my skill.'

Recalling the feeling when I used Astrape, I gather lightning and create it in the form of a spear. I give the lightning substance and fix it using mana containing will. Hundreds of lines of lightning were used in this process.

,The efficiency is garbage.'

There was no other way. In the world of 'Atlantis of God', it is easy to use skills with the help of the system, but here there is almost no system assistance.

,But I'm confident in my mana pool.'

I ignore all efficiency and focus on recreating Astrape. At least the form was plausible.

,...If I throw this, will it have the same power as the lightning I saw back then? And this is just compressing lightning into the form of a spear. Zeus's lightning back then was different.'

My talent is insufficient to recreate Zeus's Astrape. I already knew I had no talent, so I have no feelings.

,If I don't have talent, I can use someone else's talent.'

The Play Life app makes that impossible thing possible.

[Activating Genius Time for 10 seconds.]

Yuria's overwhelming talent is unlocked. All senses are opened and spirituality awakens. I trembled and felt joy. This is because I instinctively knew how to handle lightning as my senses opened.

I put will into the lightning, combine the lightning into one, and refine the shape of the lightning. I finished all that in 5 seconds.

,I don't think I could do it without Genius Time. Not right now.'

I made eye contact with the Twin Head Ogre. The ogre swings both fists at me. The two heads are yelling with all their might, with angry faces. The sound of the lightning in my hand is so loud that the Twin Head Ogre's shouts are faint.

"Don't rush me. I'll kill you now."

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

Using Instant, I aimed at the Twin Head Ogre and threw the lightning spear.

Astrape flashed and fell to the ground. Astrape split between the heads of the Twin Head Ogre and fell to the ground and exploded. A lightning storm swept through the arena. A loud thunderclap echoed a beat later.

I looked down at the ground from the sky. The arena floor was completely overturned, and the Twin Head Ogre had disappeared without a trace.

"Tsk."

Even if others didn't know, I perfectly witnessed the moment the Twin Head Ogre died with the power of the Spirit Eye. Astrape did not completely split the Twin Head Ogre's body. It lost power and exploded after splitting about 70% of the Twin Head Ogre's body.

,I one-shot the Twin Head Ogre, but... I'm not very satisfied.'

I landed on the ground. Mocha came out of my body, flew high into the sky, and disappeared somewhere.

I staggered.

,...Did I use too much mana? I even feel anemic.'

I gave strength to my trembling legs. I couldn't fall down here in embarrassment. If I showed overwhelming power, I had to maintain an overwhelming posture and atmosphere.

"Seong Yujin. Good job. Go outside the arena. The score will be... announced tomorrow morning."

The supervisor tried to hide his surprise and said to me. I slowly walked out of the arena according to the supervisor's instructions.

I could hear the voices of the admiring academy students.

"...Did you see that? The Twin Head Ogre was defeated in one hit."

"Wow, lightning swept through the arena. It's a lightning storm. A lightning storm."

"Is he really an academy student? He's too strong. Even if he merged with a spirit... he's on a different level than us."

"He's the son of thunder. He's different from us from birth."

"Shouldn't he be promoted early? Thinking about attending the academy with him... Keuek. I shouldn't even dream of being number one."

"You couldn't be number one even if Seong Yujin wasn't there. Where did a 200th place dare to come from?"

"A 250th place is picking a fight. A sparring match after this?"

"Call. I would have beaten you if I hadn't made that mistake back then."

Everyone is shocked by my skills. I was satisfied and left for the arena waiting room. I was thinking of watching Lee Sieun's exam once.

•••

The next in line after me was Ryu Hana. Her opponent was an ogre, and she defeated it in 1 minute and 12 seconds. As if stimulated by me, she fought the ogre with unreasonable movements, digging into the ogre. It was fortunate that she caught it, but if she hadn't, she would have received a deduction and her score would have dropped.

Kim Cheonwoo and Choi Da-yeon also defeated large monsters. There were quite a few other students who defeated large monsters. The top-ranked students were on a different level from ordinary students.

,Lee Sieun is 19th. She received good grades in the 1-on-1 sparring. If she does well on this exam, she will be in the top 10.'

The type of large monster determined randomly was important.

,If it's a large monster like an ogre... I might have to give up on the top 10.'

I smiled when I checked the large monster that appeared in front of Lee Sieun. The opponent was a Ghost Horse. It was the best opponent for Lee Sieun.

The battle began. The Ghost Horse flew in the sky and used magic. That's why many academy students dislike Ghost Horses more than Twin Head Ogres. Especially for warriors who fight in the front, they hate Ghost Horses to the point of hating them.

,But for Lee Sieun, it's an easier opponent than a magician.'

Lee Sieun absorbed the Ghost Horse's magic and energy with Energy Drain. The magic disappeared and the Ghost Horse was furious. The furious Ghost Horse ran on the ground with the intention of trampling Lee Sieun with physical force. The distance became closer. It was a misjudgment.

Peo-eo-eo-eong!

The Ghost Horse's body exploded.

It's Lee Sieun's Reflux. The Ghost Horse, whose body is made of energy, is an easier opponent for Lee Sieun, who can use Reflux, than an orc.

,Did it end in 15 seconds? She's faster than me. After all, compatibility is important.'

The arena became noisy. The smart academy students roughly grasped Lee Sieun's abilities. Of course, that includes magicians. The magicians looked at Lee Sieun with fearful eyes.

And after a while, Lee Sieun was given the nickname Mage Genocider.
