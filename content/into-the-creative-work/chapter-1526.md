The audit of the Underworld Squad was over.

Having passed the audit without much difficulty, I immediately received a mission and left the Heavenly Demon Church.

Originally, it was rare for individual missions to be assigned in this chaotic atmosphere. It was possible because Chun Yu-Un pulled some strings.

Soyong City, a city 50 days away from the Heavenly Demon Church.

Soyong City, which was located along a river, was a glamorous city. Thanks to being located along the river, it was a city that quickly accumulated wealth through trade with other cities.

I entered the city. No one stopped me. Soyong City was a city that welcomed travelers. Perhaps because it was located in the territory of the Heavenly Demon Church, but also far away from it, it was open-minded.

,The problem is that it's too open-minded. It's easy for spies to infiltrate.'

I had three missions.

The first was to purge the Heavenly Demon Church branch located here. The corruption at this branch was too severe. So much so that I, a member of the Jeokmyeol Squad, had to come to this city myself.

The second was to find and eliminate the spies hiding in the city. I had a list given to me by the Underworld Squad in my space pocket. I just had to find the guys on the list and kill them.

The third was to blockade the city. I would manage the city until the new city manager arrived.

I first walked through the streets of the city and then stopped. There were beings that didn't fit in with the glamorous city.

They were beggars.

Clothes that were no different from rags, disheveled hair, skin covered in black grime, and a stench that made one frown. They were beggars that could be commonly seen in any city. The beggar was leaning against a wall, sitting listlessly.

The beggar was young. He looked to be in his late 20s at most. All his limbs were intact. Yet, he was living as a beggar.

As I stared intently, he made the most pitiful expression possible and begged with both hands.

,Just a penny... please give me just a penny, Daein-nim.... I'm so hungry...."

His voice sounded like he was dying. If I lowered my gaze a little more, I could see a begging bowl. There were three coins in the dented and rusted bowl.

The beggar bowed to me several times. But when I didn't react at all, he started to move, watching my reaction. He picked up the three coins that had fallen on the floor and his worn-out bowl. I followed behind the beggar.

The beggar, who was in the middle of the street, looked around and then pointed at me.

,There's a suspicious guy! A suspicious guy keeps following me! Please save me! Please!"
