Seoul is being destroyed by a natural disaster.

Naturally, the heroes stepped up. They prioritized rescuing people and attacked the materialized spirits. However, the spirits weren't easy to deal with. If they sensed danger, they would run away or call other spirits to attack together.

The peculiar thing was that it was the movement of lower-level spirits.

,Lower-level spirits are basically dumb. To put it nicely, they're instinctive. If the opponent is stronger than them? Then they run away without looking back. The only time they fight together like that is when they're being directed by someone.

Even when I look around, I don't feel the presence of a spirit master. That means they're being moved by the power of something other than a spirit master.

'At least a top-tier spirit. It would only be possible if at least five of them gathered.'

There was an even worse case. When the Spirit King directly gave the order.

"Help me! Please help me!"

A woman shouts at me. Her voice was to my liking, but her face was plain. Looking at the woman's legs, there were asphalt fragments embedded in them.

Kung. Kung. Kung.

A golem made of rock slowly walks towards the woman. It's a high-level rock spirit. It looks like it's going to stomp the woman to death with its enormous weight.

'She's not ugly, but she's plain. Plus, this is inside a dungeon. She's fake, so is there any need to save her?'

I could save the woman and rape her, but I didn't have time for that right now. I have to find Laplace and kill him.

"P, please save me!"

The woman reaches out to me. I watched silently. Kung! The high-level rock spirit stomped on the woman's body. Stomped and stomped again. I could feel hatred in that act. I approached the spirit with my Spirit Eyes activated.

The rock spirit, which had been clenching its fist at me with murderous intent, lowered its fist again.

"Human... I feel a familiar vibe from you. You're different from the humans here."

The spirit was friendly. It's thanks to my high spirit affinity.

"Why are you attacking humans?"

"Are you going to interfere?"

"I'm just asking out of curiosity. If I were going to interfere, I would have already done it."

"I see... I guess it's okay to tell you since I feel a familiarity with you. The Spirit King is furious. He is disappointed in humans and has decided to punish them. We must kill the humans as soon as possible...."

I could feel the spirit's desperate anger. This is anger felt under the influence of the Spirit King. I was sweating bullets. The Spirit King directly intervening didn't even happen in the original story.

"Why is the Spirit King angry at humans?"

"Seong Hari. That woman damaged and ate the King's sacred body."

"......."

I swallowed. I was taken aback by the unexpected name. The Seong Hari the spirit is talking about must be Yeong Hari. Is Yeong Hari alive? Not dead?

Come to think of it, this is a dungeon. It wouldn't be strange for Yeong Hari to be here. But it's likely to be a different person from the virtual reality Yeong Hari.

"You only need to attack that woman. Is there a need to attack all humans?"

"The humans, led by that woman, invaded the Spirit Realm. They tried to destroy the Spirit Realm and defy the natural order. The Spirit King was furious about that. We are the King's anger and the King's iron hammer."

The rock spirit looked around. It seems to have found another human and turns its body.

"You too, act on the King's anger. If you kill humans, the King will be pleased."

"I have something more urgent than that. Seong Hari. Where is that woman?"

"She's over there. On a high mountain. The King's limbs have moved directly... How could a human made of bones and flesh withstand that? That woman will die. That is just."

"Curse all you want."

I swung the Kusanagi Sword imbued with Susanoo's power. The rock spirit's body is cut. The large rock was divided into two. The ground shakes. I cut the remaining torso before it could use its power. The head that was left spewed murderous intent at me.

"How dare you betray me?!"

"Seong Hari is my woman. Who gave you the right to kill her? Spirit King or whatever, you have no right to touch my woman."

I cut off the rock spirit's head.

A place with mountains.

Are there only one or two mountains in Korea? Moreover, the [Savior of the Academy] world has never been divided into North and South Korea, unlike reality. The Korean War never existed at all.

'If you include the mountains in the North... there are a damn lot.'

Suddenly, I remembered. Should I meet the Seong Hari of this world? She wouldn't be the Seong Hari from the original story, and there was a possibility that she wouldn't be the Yeong Hari I know.

'Considering the last time, there's a possibility that she knows me though....'

Right now, I have to find Laplace and kill him.

'The problem is that I don't know where Laplace is.'

I paused.

I was in a situation where I couldn't do this or that. What should I do in this case? It's simple. Do what I want to do.

'I have to meet Seong Hari in the dungeon. Let's meet and get information. Then I might be able to find out about the real Seong Hari's past.'

The hint the rock spirit gave was a mountain. And the words that the King's limbs moved. If a battle took place, it wouldn't be intact, so I should look for the most dangerous mountain.

I left Seoul for now.

• • •

First, I headed to the southernmost part. Because Jirisan and Hallasan came to mind first when I thought of Korean mountains.

I used Susanoo's power. I rode the wind and flew south. Not only Seoul, but all other regions were under attack by spirits.

'I heard that China and Japan are also in chaos. Well, it's none of my business.'

Jirisan Mountain collapsed. A landslide occurred and it was a mess. It seems it will take at least a few years to clean up. It seems that a top-tier spirit used its power, but no traces of battle could be found.

Hallasan Mountain on Jeju Island exploded. Red lava flowed and volcanic ash flew in the sky. Again, no traces of battle could be found.

I looked for other mountains as well. Everything was a mess. Nothing was intact. Of course, the same goes for cities.

'Tens of millions of people will die.'

After this is over, the country of Korea will not even exist. The situation was that serious.

'I've searched all the southern mountains, but she's not there. Then what's left... is it the North.'

I entrust my body to the wind. The wind pulled my body and flew north. It was quite comfortable. It was a little cold, though.

I flew north and searched every mountain. However, there were no mountains with traces of battle. They had all been tragically destroyed by natural disasters.

Now there was only one mountain left, Baekdu Mountain.

Surprisingly, Baekdu Mountain was intact. It showed a strong appearance as if it had not been affected by natural disasters at all.

'But what is that? A tower?'

A tower stood tall in the lake at the top of Baekdu Mountain. It was a tower that appeared to be about 20 stories high, and it was magnificent and majestic.

'Why is there a tower all of a sudden? And why are there so many people here? Did they come because Baekdu Mountain is intact?'

I flew to the top of Baekdu Mountain. The lake is red. A strong smell of blood came from the lake. Looking into the lake, there were corpses everywhere. Traces of battle. It seemed like they were unilaterally attacked.

"Who are you? State your name and affiliation."

Someone who approached me from behind asked in a cold voice. It was a familiar voice. I slowly turned around.

It was a woman with dark brown hair wearing a white coat over her shoulders.

Kang Ji-young. There she was, the dean of Maruhan Academy in the original story. The side of her top was torn. Traces of blood and a bandage wrapped around her waist can be seen. Blood was seeping into the white bandage. It was clear at a glance that she was not in a normal state.

"I asked who you are."

I hesitated and opened my mouth. After all, even if she knows my real name, it won't affect anything outside the dungeon.

"Seong Yujin."

"Ah. I see. Seong Yujin. Affiliation?"

"Maruhan Academy."

"Academy? You're too strong to be a student. Are you a newly hired teacher? Why did you come here?"

"The world is a mess, does that matter?"

"...Ha. You're right, so I have nothing to say."

"What is that tower? It doesn't look like an ordinary tower."

Kang Ji-young looked at me and the tower alternately. She seemed dissatisfied with something, but then she sighed. It was a sigh of giving up, of resignation.

"Information about the tower should have been known as much as it could be... Don't you know?"

"I don't really watch the news."

"It suddenly appeared two weeks ago. There was a gate reaction right before it appeared."

"Is it a dungeon in the shape of a tower?"

"There were heroes who entered the tower first. It was a team made up of half Korean and half Chinese heroes."

This was the border area between Korea and China. It was natural for Chinese people to join.

"The team came out again in just one day. 80% of the team was annihilated. The survivors called that tower the Tower of Wishes."

"...Tower of Wishes?"

"A tower that grants the wish of the person who reaches the end of the tower. The problem is that the information was leaked from the Chinese side as well. All sorts of people flocked to it. If you look outside, you'll see people still climbing towards the tower."

"Yeah. They were coming to Baekdu Mountain. But there are no people who actually reach it."

"The spirits are blocking them. Strangely, the spirits don't come near the tower. How did you come here without being disturbed by the spirits?"

"Because I'm a spirit master."

"...A spirit master? Your name is Seong Yujin.... Are you perhaps a member of the Jinryeong family?"

I didn't answer. Kang Ji-young understood as she pleased and nodded.

'Tower of Wishes....'

Seong Hari seems to have mentioned it before.

But there is a question. If you reach the top of the tower, it grants your wish. It's a groundbreaking reward, but I don't know about its existence. I've never learned about it at the Academy either. This should have been mentioned several times in the news.

"Did Seong Hari enter the tower?"

"...Yes. 8 hours ago. We were confronting the Chinese side... when she suddenly appeared and attacked us. We couldn't stop her. We were annihilated in 5 minutes. She wasn't the Seong Hari I knew."

"You didn't die."

"Even though she became a villain, it seems she still had some old feelings left. Did you come to see Seong Hari? Give it up. No words will work on her now. Even if you're a member of the same family, she'll attack without hesitation. Especially since you're a spirit master, she will...."

Kukukung!

The tower shakes. Cracks appeared on its surface and it slowly began to collapse.

"Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaah!"

Screams.

Seong Hari's screams echoed from the top of the tower.
