『The rank of the Storm God rises to SS.』

Yamata no Orochi was definitively dead.

I descended onto the ravaged earth. The yokai that had been nearby were all caught in the blast and killed. The ones a bit further out were slain by Sun Wukong's clones.

Once the tension released, the recoil hit me. Strength drained from my body and I staggered, nearly collapsing. There's almost no physical pain. What came for me now is a vicious emptiness.

'Cheonma Shingong's Pa Cheon Hwang burns the soul. But Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel burns karma, not the soul, to gain power.'

Karma could be said to be my very existence.

[Heavenly Heart is released.]

I release the Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel and Heavenly Heart.

[Using Full Recovery.]

Karma is also a part of me. The karma consumed by the Thunder Light Kalpa Wheel was restored. The emptiness I'd felt vanished as well.

I reached my hand toward the sword embedded in the ground. Around the blade, there was something like a faint mist. More precisely, it could be called a cloud. Mist or cloud, the essence is the same.

The sword left behind by the massive Yamata no Orochi. It was such a famous tale that I couldn't not know what this sword was.

'One of Japan's Three Sacred Treasures — the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.'

I wasn't expecting much.

There was no way anything from this dungeon could be taken out, after all. It was more of a "this thing is badass, let me at least touch it" kind of feeling.

『Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi (Replica)

Rank: SS

Has the power to command clouds and wind.

Contains divine energy.

Repels evil.』

'I can check item information?'

Among the objects in this dungeon, there wasn't a single one whose item information could be checked. It was because everything was bound to the dungeon.

'...Could this be a reward the dungeon is granting me?'

A special dungeon. Even for an S-rank dungeon, the difficulty had been absurdly high.

'I was already satisfied just from the Storm God trait's rank going up... Getting a reward like this on top of it feels good.'

I gripped the hilt of the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.

Control over every cloud floating in the sky passed to me.

'Similar ability to Je-un-ryeong. When limited purely to cloud manipulation, it's inferior to Je-un-ryeong.'

Unlike Je-un-ryeong, the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi's true value lies in being a sword. A sword's purpose is to attack the opponent.

『Susanoo's Magatama reacts to the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.』

The Susanoo's Magatama, which I'd kept in my pocket rather than my inventory in order to use Susanoo's power, emitted a blue light and vibrated. In response, the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi resonated, shedding a golden glow.

Susanoo's Magatama moved on its own and seeped into the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi. It was so sudden that I was caught off guard.

『The rank of the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi rises.』

'The rank went up, so that's good? I can feel Susanoo's power from the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi now, the same power I used to feel from Susanoo's Magatama.'

I would be able to use Susanoo's power through the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi. Since the sword could perform the role of Susanoo's Magatama, there was no loss on my end.

『The Storm God (SS) reacts to the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.』

『The Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi attempts to bind itself to the Storm God (SS). Will you permit the binding?』

Unlike Susanoo's Magatama, which had been absorbed and merged on its own, this time it asked for my permission.

I deliberated. The pros and cons of binding the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi to myself were clear.

'I probably won't be able to use the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi in other worlds. Once I do this, there'll be no taking it back either. In exchange, I'll get stronger.'

I reached a conclusion. I chose the binding. For the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi's cloud-and-wind command ability, I have Je-un-ryeong. As weapons, I have Stormbreaker and Hwa-ryeon-bi-do.

'Just as was the case with Susanoo's Magatama, there's a high chance the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi won't function properly when taken to another world. Other worlds have no Susanoo, and no Storm God trait either.'

The only place where I could make use of the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi's abilities was the [Savior of the Academy] world.

'I permit the binding.'

The Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi transformed into a mysterious golden cloud. The cloud drifted slowly toward me and seeped into my body.

『The Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi is bound to the Storm God (SS).』

『The rank of the Storm God rises to SSS.』

『Storm God

Trait Rank: SSS

Commands storms.

Uses Susanoo's power.

Summons the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.』

I felt Susanoo's power take its place within me.

'Shall I give it a try?'

I summon the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi.

Mana and Susanoo's power pour into the sky. I raise my head and look up. Every cloud in the heavens except the Kinto'un Sun Wukong was riding condensed in an instant, became a sword, and plummeted to the ground.

KWAAAAAAAANG!

The shockwave from the impact swept across the ground once. I extended my hand, and the Ame no Murakumo no Tsurugi flew naturally into my grasp.

Sun Wukong, who had been munching popcorn and drinking beer on the Kinto'un, lightly stepped down to the ground and spoke.

"A divine sword. Specifically the kind best suited for cutting down nasty bastards."

I swung the Heaven-Defying Cloud Sword through the air a few times, then nodded.

"Sun Wukong. I'm grateful for your help. Thanks to you, I was able to take down Yamata no Orochi with ease."

"You would've caught it without me. How does it feel, holding the power of a god in human hands?"

"Not bad."

"That's all? You've got power that even the Jade Emperor and the great immortals of Heaven would be wary of."

"I know the source of this power isn't mine."

Within this dungeon, Susanoo's power was exceptionally strong, which was the only reason I could wield it like this. Once I left the dungeon, I wouldn't be able to display this kind of overwhelming force.

"Kehehe. You've got a clear read on yourself. That's right. The limits of a divine artifact are obvious. Most humans and gods mistake the power of a divine artifact for their own... but you don't, and that's what I like about you. If I had my way, I'd find some classy spot and share a drink or two, but..."

Sun Wukong and I looked up at the empty air. The void split open, and a blue cube emerged from within. It didn't fall to the ground—it hovered, bobbing in midair.

『Dungeon cleared. Acquiring Mana Cube.』

『Mana Cube

Rank: SS

Deploys a mana space.』

Combined with the one I'd obtained in the first run, this was my second Mana Cube.

I felt no sense of futility over having gone through all this trouble just to obtain an SS-rank item. The Mana Cube was an object with limitless potential depending on how it was used.

I grasped the Mana Cube in my hand.

Kukukukukung.

The world—the dungeon—began to collapse.

The sky and mountains visible in the distance turned into pitch-black darkness and vanished. What happened to the beings that must have existed beyond there was obvious.

"So this world wasn't real—it was a mystic realm. Kehehe. No wonder something felt off."

Sun Wukong gulped down the beer can in his hand.

"You'd noticed?"

"To some extent, after meeting you. Logically speaking, a human from thousands of years in the future suddenly showing up doesn't make sense. And the Bodhisattva's attitude was strange too. The Bodhisattva is benevolent, sure, but she treats everyone equally. Yet with you, she seemed especially... particular. The Bodhisattva knew everything. And you've probably got a whole pile of heavy shit loaded onto your shoulders. Something like the future of the world, maybe. The Bodhisattva has a soft spot for that kind of person."

"...That's unexpected. I figured once you realized the truth, you'd go on a rampage."

"That's what everyone who doesn't know me thinks. Any little thing I do, and they assume I'm throwing a fit."

"You're not angry that you were fake?"

"A little hollowed out, sure. But in the end, I'm basically a clone who didn't know he was a clone. I've made clones like that myself a few times, so I get it. A clone is just a clone. Throwing a fit won't change anything. More than anything, there was no lie in your words. In the end, the real me went to India, brought back the scriptures, fought, and became a Buddha. Just hearing that is enough for me. Thanks to you, this last drink tastes good too."

With that, Sun Wukong grinned.

"Honestly, part of me thinks it's a relief. I don't have to go on that goddamn journey to India. I mean, come on—using the Somersault Cloud, the whole thing would've taken less than a minute, but no, we had to walk on our own two feet and suffer through every tribulation."

"Kehe. Actually, I've never read Journey to the West all the way through. Honestly, there were a lot of developments that rubbed me the wrong way. Still, it was an interesting story. Especially when Tripitaka got pregnant—that was a shock."

"What? Master got pregnant?! Master is a man!!"

"Kehe. I know."

"W-who's the bastard that got Master pregnant?!"

Drink the water of the Mother-Child River and you become pregnant.

"Kehe."

I didn't answer—just laughed. Sun Wukong's face went pale. His eyes could distinguish truth from lies, but they couldn't distinguish words that didn't exist.

"D-don't tell me it's one of us? I-it's not me, right? Bajie! It's gotta be Bajie. That bastard is especially lecherous!"

"Kehehe."

"...Kehehe. In the end, it's something the real me went through. Not my problem. Ah, this is the limit now."

The darkness that had begun at the edge of the world had crept to the feet of Sun Wukong and me before we knew it.

In the pitch-black space, only Sun Wukong's and my eyes glowed—his crimson, mine flickering blue.

The darkness soon swallowed Sun Wukong's body whole.

"Farewell, hero."

The darkness consumed everything.

Splash!

I fell into the Cheonji Lake of Mount Baekdu. When I'd entered the dungeon, I'd been deep underwater; when I came out, I was above the lake's surface.

'Guess I should be glad I don't have to swim up from the bottom.'

I floated idly on the water's surface.

The lethargy from Susanoo's power draining out of me—power that had been overflowing inside the dungeon—made me not want to move a muscle.

Of course, the power hadn't vanished completely. Perhaps because the Storm God's trait had reached SSS-rank, a portion of Susanoo's power remained within me.

'Like Susanoo's Magatama, it's a rechargeable type. But the efficiency is incomparably better.'

The mana that had filled Mount Baekdu to dangerous levels had naturally all disappeared. As a result, the formless monsters of Mount Baekdu had all been annihilated. Mount Baekdu was now in a safe state.

'In a few days, it'll probably be swarming with tourists.'

[Ending the Game.]
