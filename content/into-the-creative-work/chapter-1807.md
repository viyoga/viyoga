,Even though I look like this, I pride myself on having tasted quite a few delicacies, but this dish called 'steak'? It's a unique dish that I'm trying for the first time. It would be an understatement to dismiss it as simply grilled meat, as its flavor was immense. The wine called 'grape wine' was also very good!

After finishing the meal, Baekran-ssi spat out her impressions as if excited.

,That's quite an exaggeration. From what I've heard, all the delicacies of the world gather in the Imperial family.

,That's true. All sorts of precious foods are presented to the Imperial family almost every day. They're all for His Majesty the Emperor. How could I, a mere inspector, eat those precious foods?

,You said you've tasted quite a few delicacies.

,I ate them secretly. Connections are important in the Imperial family.

Baekran-ssi said with a smile. Is she boasting about having many connections in the Imperial family? She has outstanding beauty and skills, so it would have been easy for her to make connections.

Anyway, after finishing the meal, we started walking again. Soon the sun set and it became evening, and we prepared to camp under a moderately large tree. Dinner was also deer meat. Because there was leftover deer meat from the daytime hunt. This time, I didn't even touch it and just dropped it.

We settled in a suitable place where a stream flowed. I looked around to make sure no one was there.

,Tonight's chicken.

I hadn't eaten chicken recently. It's because I ate so much of it while developing chicken in the [Dark Moon] world that I was sick of it. At that time, I vowed never to eat chicken again, but as time passed, I craved chicken like I do now.

As soon as I opened the chicken packaging, a greasy and savory smell spread. The sweet smell of the seasoning relentlessly stimulated my appetite. It was the moment I reached out for the half-and-half chicken. I felt a deep gaze from behind.

It was Baekran-ssi. She was staring intently at me.

,She wasn't there until just now.

I wasn't careless. But I didn't feel her presence approaching me at all. When I realized it, she was there.

,Her stealth skills are so outstanding that they deceive my senses. Or did she teleport?

Either way, she wasn't a woman to be taken lightly.

,Ahem. Want to eat together?

,I won't refuse.

She approached with a light step. Again, no footprints were left.

In the end, she and I sat side by side and tore into the chicken.

Basasak!

The outside was crispy and the inside was moist with juice. Baekran-ssi, who took a bite of the chicken, looked at me and the chicken alternately with surprised, rounded eyes.

,This is chicken...? It's completely different from the chicken I knew! This is also a delicacy!

She admired it repeatedly and tore into the chicken at a rapid pace. It was quite amazing to see chicken constantly going into that small mouth.

I handed her a cola in a glass. Transparent ice was floating around.

Baekran-ssi, who received the cola, tilted her head.

,That's an expensive glass. But what is this black water?

,Ah. This is called cola. It's chicken's friend.

,It's a bit unsettling, but... I'll trust you and drink it.

She took a sip of cola and widened her eyes. She stroked her neck with her other hand, as if her throat was stinging.

,Is it not good?

,...It's delicious! I was just taken aback because it's such an unfamiliar taste. A drink that hits the throat... I didn't know such a drink existed in the world!

Baekran-ssi ate cola and chicken with a cheerful expression. Only chicken bones were left at her feet. Her bone-cutting skills were so outstanding that it was hard to believe she was eating chicken for the first time.

,You're really amazing. How on earth did you discover these foods?

,I didn't discover them. I got them.

,If you don't want to talk about it, that's fine. I can't pry into things from the position of someone who got a free meal.

She washed her hands and mouth in the flowing stream. Then she tidied up her clothes. Baekran-ssi, who returned to her neat appearance, suddenly asked me with a serious expression.

,What is your ambition?

,Ambition?

,There's no way a man like you wouldn't have ambition. Is your ambition perhaps to become an elder of the Demonic Cult?

To be satisfied with just an elder? The position of elder in the Demonic Cult was not a low position. If you compare it to a fantasy world, it can be said to be a position equivalent to a considerable high-ranking noble. And the highest position that Yeom Guseok, who was born ordinary, could reach was the position of elder.

,That's right. It would be good if I could become an elder.

,I didn't say a single word like that.

,I can tell by looking at your eyes. You're not someone who can be satisfied with just the position of elder. Something more than that. Vice-Leader... Are you perhaps aiming for the position of Heavenly Demon?

,......

,The position of Heavenly Demon is not a position that you can ascend to alone. If you're really aiming for the position of Heavenly Demon... make trustworthy comrades. Even the Heavenly Demon has limitations to what they can do alone.

Heavenly Demon.

The King of the Demonic Sky. The guy who can be said to be the source of all demons. If that guy was me, what would I have done? Would I make comrades and ascend to the position of Heavenly Demon? Would that guy even acknowledge that?

I don't need comrades.

,The Heavenly Demon must reign above all demons.

,That's a domineering statement. But it's a statement that's more like the Heavenly Demon than anything else.

Baekran-ssi smiled faintly as always and walked away.

We arrived at Baeyeong City, our destination. It was a fairly large city. At the same time, it was also a city that looked peaceful.

,The Ma family is in Baeyeong City.

The six families that started the Demonic Cult together. One of them, the Yoon family, is the ruler of Baeyeong City.

,Even if the Ma family is more humble than in the past, it's still the Ma family.

I couldn't ignore the power they had accumulated over a long period of time.

,There's no point in provoking the Ma family, so I need to move quietly.

I looked around. If there were great merchants called Yoo Woon Commerce, they would definitely stand out. But I don't see anything special.

Hong Ganghu-ssi cleared his throat at my appearance of constantly looking around.

,Ahem. Didn't I tell you before? Yoo Woon Commerce doesn't do business with ordinary people. Of course, they don't do business openly. Follow me.

I followed Hong Ganghu-ssi. He moved outside the city, not inside.

,Yoo Woon Commerce sells precious things. Elixirs, magical artifacts, martial arts... all of them can be said to be treasures. And treasures attract flies.

That was the reason why Yoo Woon Commerce couldn't move openly. It was also the reason why they kept moving without settling in one place.

A small river flowed outside the city. The river wasn't very big. Hong Ganghu-ssi walked across the stepping stones made of rocks. Two Martial artists were guarding in front of the stepping stones. The two Martial artists had different clothes and even the weapons they were equipped with were different, with swords and spears. They were definitely hired mercenaries.

,Stop. This is a restricted area. Even if you're seniors, you can't enter.

The mercenaries who checked the money stepped aside.

,Welcome, guests.

Baekran-ssi, who was wearing a bamboo hat like me, admired softly.

She was admiring something else.
