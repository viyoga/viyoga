I was quite surprised to return to the city of the Cheonma Shingyo.

I thought all the Shingyo people would be hostile to me. Accordingly, I expected a battle at the city entrance. But they didn't stop me in the city. Most of the Shingyo people just went about their lives with somewhat stiff expressions.

"This is absurd. Is this also a trap?"

I scanned the surroundings at the city entrance with my Inner Eye of Golden Clarity. I couldn't see any obvious traps. The barrier protecting the city hadn't changed either.

Even before I entered the city, a group of martial artists rushed towards me. I didn't sense any hostility from them, so I just stood there and watched.

"So Cheonma-ssi! We have been waiting for So Cheonma-ssi!"

"Who are you?"

"I am Jonghu, the leader of the 3rd squad of the Yeomma Troop."

Yeomma Troop (Enma Squad)

A unit under the direct control of the Demon King with auditing authority. It's also a combat unit with power exceeding the Jeokmyeol Troop. You could say it's the best unit in the Cheonma Shingyo.

"If you're from the Yeomma Troop, you must be my enemy?"

"No! We are not following the Demon King-nim's orders!"

It wasn't a lie. That the Yeomma Troop refused the Demon King's orders?

It didn't make sense. The Yeomma Troop was under the direct control of the Demon King and wasn't a unit that followed me.

"The Demon King-nim has joined hands with a wicked group of sorcerers called the Blood Society and claimed So Cheonma-ssi's rebellion with flimsy evidence. Logically, So Cheonma-ssi's rebellion is absurd. We were suspicious of the Demon King-nim's sudden orders and independently withheld them."

"This is absurd. Can the Yeomma Troop even do this? Weren't they a unit that listened to the Demon King's orders without question?"

"A true subject corrects the faults of their lord. Our Yeomma Troop currently judges that the Demon King is either deluded by evil arts or that demonic energy has invaded his marrow and he has been consumed by demonic nature."

"Demonic nature, huh. So you guys also knew that the Demon King was in closed-door training after falling into Demonic Qi Deviation."

"...The Demon King-nim hasn't appeared in public for too long. Even when orders were issued, the Imperial Guards or the Guardians delivered them on his behalf. We couldn't help but feel something was wrong."

You know what they say, if the tail is too long, it gets caught.

'I see how things are roughly going. The Demon King moved too radically. If he had proven that he had escaped from Demonic Qi Deviation and moved calmly, the story would have been different. Bringing the Blood Society into the Shingyo was also a problem.'

I understand the Demon King's position too.

The Cheonma Shingyo is a small kingdom, and the Demon King was a king with absolute power.

'He must have thought that if he gave an order, his subordinates would unconditionally follow it. Because that's how it's been until now.'

The king orders that his rightful successor is a traitor and must be killed? What's so lacking that the successor, who will become king in time, would commit treason?

Any sane subject would think the king has gone mad, which is normal.

"How is the Shingyo's situation going?"

"Those who follow the Demon King-nim's orders and those who oppose them are divided and confronting each other."

Most of the Inner Council, except for the Yeomma Troop, are following the Demon King, while most of the Outer Council, including the Jeokmyeol Troop, are suspicious of the Demon King and are in conflict with each other.

The Inner Council mainly handles internal affairs of the Cheonma Shingyo, and the Outer Council handles external affairs. They are also called the Inner Gate and Outer Gate.

"There aren't many who sincerely follow me."

"That's..."

"It's okay. I don't expect anything from you guys. I didn't even expect it in the first place. Those who follow me are behind me now."

I pointed behind me. Thousands of Shingyo people were following me.

"The Demon Lord Four Families must have sided with the Demon King too."

"Yes."

"Then, of course, the Council of Elders also follows the Demon King."

The Demon Lord Four Families are the families of the first Demon King's disciples. They were the families that could be said to be the beginning of the Cheonma Shingyo. These four families occupy key positions in the Cheonma Shingyo, and more than half of the Council of Elders are from the Demon Lord Four Families.

I slightly turned my gaze to check Yeon Yeoha's reaction. Her father was the Sword Demon and the Third Elder. She was expressionless as always.

"Is the entire Council of Elders the enemy?"

No. The Third Elder objected to the Demon King’s orders and was imprisoned in the Demon Steel Prison Cave (魔堈獄洞).

The Demon Steel Prison Cave was the prison area of the Cheonma Shingyo. Criminals who violated the rules of the Cheonma Shingyo are imprisoned here.

'Since he's an Elder, they didn't execute him immediately for refusing orders. Well, the other Elders would have opposed immediate execution anyway. They wouldn't want to set such a precedent for themselves.'

I obtained more information afterward.

Currently, the Demon King faction, which follows the Demon King, was occupying the Inner City, and the So Cheonma faction, which does not follow the Demon King, was guarding the Demon King faction from the Outer City.

'The So Cheonma faction isn't even following me, but they're using me as a figurehead. How disgusting. I'll have to purge them after this is over.'

The Demon King is hesitant about a civil war, so they're just confronting each other, and the So Cheonma faction has no center, so they're using me as a figurehead to confront each other.

In the end, this situation will only end when the Demon King and I make a decision.

"Many are waiting for So Cheonma-ssi. I will guide you, so let's go quickly."

I'm going to the Demon Steel Prison Cave first. I'm going to rescue the imprisoned Third Elder.

I subtly gave Yeon Yeoha a thumbs up. Yeon Yeoha's eyes wavered for a moment. Yeon Yeoha calmly said.

"So Cheonma-ssi. The Third Elder's matter is not what needs to be resolved right now."

"I decide what to do. I've decided to save the Third Elder."

There's only one reason. They're my woman's parents. And looking at the state of the Cheonma Shingyo, it doesn't seem that urgent. What are they going to do if I go off on a tangent? Do they have anything to do but wait?

'I'm going to score points with Yeon Yeoha on this occasion.'

Even if Yeon Yeoha has no emotions, she knows about favors and grudges.

"Wa, wait a moment, So Cheonma-ssi!"

Jonghu, the leader of the 3rd squad of the Yeomma Troop, urgently blocked my path. I barely managed to stop myself from reflexively killing him.

"What is it? I have no business with you. You can get lost now."

The Demon Steel Prison Cave has declared neutrality in this situation! You might incur the wrath of the Prison Cave King if you go there unnecessarily!

Prison Cave King Geumgu. The manager of the Demon Steel Prison Cave and an absolute master of the Three Jeonggyeong. He's a powerhouse on the Elder level.

In the past, I wouldn't have wanted to make him an enemy either. But the situation has changed. Having reached the Harmonious Realm, I had nothing to fear. Even the Demon King will die by my hand, so is the Prison Cave King a problem?

I kicked Jonghu away.

"Don't you dare block my path."

I headed to the Demon Steel Prison Cave with the Shingyo people who followed me.

An area on the outskirts of the city with few people. The Demon Steel Prison Cave, with its steel buildings and barriers that restrict the abilities of the inmates, was deserted compared to its size.

'I killed all the prisoners.'

They all became experience points for Stormbreaker.

Even before I entered the building, a middle-aged man with a large build and scars all over his body appeared. Prison Cave King Geumgu appeared with his subordinates.

Geumgu took out his main weapon, a whip, and guarded against me.

"So Cheonma-ssi! What brings you here again?!"

"I've come to take the Third Elder. Release him."

"...I congratulate you on breaking through the wall and reaching the Harmonious Realm. But I cannot release the Third Elder. He was imprisoned by the Demon King-nim's orders."

"You are skilled at managing prisoners. I will give you a special opportunity to choose. Fight for the Demon King, or follow me."

"I hate politics, so I became the manager of the Demon Steel Prison Cave. Isn't it enough to maintain neutrality?"

"The Shingyo must be one. There is no such thing as neutrality."

Geumgu pondered for a moment and said.

"...I understand. I will follow So Cheonma-ssi."

"That's unexpected. I thought you would have loyalty to the Demon King."

"Unlike So Cheonma-ssi, the Demon King has never visited the Demon Steel Prison Cave even once. The order to imprison the Third Elder was also delivered through the Great Elder. If I have to choose one side... I will choose the side that acknowledges me, at least."

"Good. Now, bring the Third Elder."

Soon, Geumgu moved himself and brought the Third Elder. The Third Elder looked fine on the outside, but his energy was weakened. His dantian and meridians were sealed.

"So Cheonma-ssi. I didn't expect you to come to rescue me right away."

"It's to strengthen my forces."

I couldn't say that I came to get on Yeon Yeoha's good side.

"Indeed. It seems So Cheonma-ssi has already made a decision. If I and the Prison Cave King join So Cheonma-ssi, it will be a little more advantageous for So Cheonma-ssi. There is a problem. The Blood Demon has placed a seal on my body."

Yangmi, can you unseal it?

Ryu Yangmi stepped forward and examined the Sword Demon's body.

"Yes. It's a traditional Blood Society seal using blood as a medium. I can unseal it with a drop of my blood containing the power of the Blood Spirit."

"I'm counting on you."

Ryu Yangmi tore her fingertip without hesitation and took out a drop of blood. The drop of blood containing special power floated in the air and glowed faintly. Ryu Yangmi flicked her hand, and it seeped into the Sword Demon's body.

The seal disappeared, and the Sword Demon regained his energy.

"Blood Flower Ryu Yangmi... I've heard she's called the Blood Spirit Girl within the Blood Evil Star... Truly incredible skill. To解除 (haeje, unseal) a seal placed by a sorcerer of the Harmonious Realm so effortlessly!"

Unlike his admiring words, the Sword Demon's expression was as expressionless as Yeon Yeoha's.

"Was the Blood Demon in the Harmonious Realm?"

"He was in the Harmonious Realm, but I didn't feel the same overwhelming power as the Demon King."

"How does he compare to me?"

"So Cheonma-ssi is overwhelmingly stronger. So Cheonma-ssi's aura is similar to the Demon King's. Frankly, I can't guarantee which of the two, the Demon King and So Cheonma-ssi, will win."

"I will win. Follow me. Today, the Cheonma Shingyo will be reborn."

I led everyone to the place where the Inner and Outer Councils were confronting each other. To be more precise, it's the Cheonma Palace. That's the only place the Demon King could be.

'You're framing me as a traitor? Fine. I'll gladly become a traitor.'

I was planning to rebel from the start, but it felt bad to hear it directly.

As I walked down the wide-open main street, gazes were drawn to me. In the short time I went to the Demon Steel Prison Cave, the rumor that I had returned spread throughout the city. Martial artists and ordinary people alike gathered to watch. They also knew. That today is a day of history.

"So Cheonma-ssi has returned."

"An aura as heavy as a massive mountain... So the rumor that he reached the Harmonious Realm was true...!"

"It doesn't look like they're negotiating. The future of the Shingyo will be decided today."

The Shingyo people's attention is good for me. If they witness me killing the Demon King directly, they won't dare to defy me in the future and will know their place.

A huge gate blocks my path. It was the entrance to the Inner City of the Cheonma Shingyo. The So Cheonma faction Shingyo people, including the Jeokmyeol Troop Leader, opened the gate as if they had been waiting.

Inside the gate, the Demon King faction Shingyo people, including the Council of Elders, stood armed. I walked forward, receiving their sharp aura, and stopped. When I stopped, those who were walking behind me stopped together.

I raised my head and looked at the sky.

Above the Cheonma Palace.

A gaunt middle-aged man with slicked-back hair and a pale face was sitting on a luxurious chair, looking down at the ground.

That chair is the Demon King’s Sacred Seat (天魔聖座), a chair that only the Demon King can sit in.

In other words, that gaunt man is the 21st Demon King, Cheon Seongjin.

My eyes met the Demon King's.

The Demon King scanned the ground with inorganic eyes and stood up from the Demon King Sacred Seat.

The Demon King's right foot slowly rose, then came down again, stepping on the air.

Kung!

Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March). Demon King's Descent Step.

The black aura wave that started from the Demon King's foot pressed down on the entire Cheonma Shingyo.

"Keueok!"

"Keuk!"

Screams erupted from here and there.

The Shingyo people were crushed by the Demon King's Cheonma Gunrimbo, lowering their heads and bending their backs, or kneeling on the ground. The Elders were no exception. Those who had mastered demonic arts were especially susceptible to the Cheonma Gunrimbo. The only one standing tall here was me.

I circulated the Cheonma Shingong and raised my right foot.

Pajijik.

A black lightning flashed and disappeared.

My right foot stepped on the ground.

Kung!

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功), Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March). Demonic Martial Art, Demon King's Descent Step.

My Cheonma Gunrimbo overlaid the Demon King's Cheonma Gunrimbo. Those who had bowed their heads in awe of the Demon King now bowed their heads to me in awe. Some rejoiced, and some fell into demonic possession and vomited blood.

The Demon King's eyebrows twitched.

He stepped down to the ground again with Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Kung!

I had no intention of being pushed back by the Demon King.

I also stepped up into the sky with Cheonma Gunrimbo.

Kung!

The aura waves collided, the ground cracked, and cracks appeared in the sky. The Shingyo people realized that their safety was due to the Demon King's mercy and bowed their heads even further in awe.

The two Demon Kings reign over the world, and the heavens and earth cannot withstand it.

Today, one of them will die.
