Seong Hari, spear in hand, stormed into the Gumokgyo Korean branch.

Fanatics of Gumokgyo, wearing grotesque masks with nine eyes drawn on them and clad in black robes, blocked her path.

Seong Hari scrutinized them with narrowed eyes. Awakened and non-awakened were mixed in. Killing the awakened was justifiable self-defense, but killing the non-awakened could lead to serious problems. Claiming a non-awakened person was awakened was also impossible. The Hero Association could distinguish between awakened and non-awakened through corpses.

As much as she wanted to wipe them all out, she held back. She couldn't go to jail and leave Yujin-nim behind.

"Where is Yujin-nim?"

"Have you come to take the Ninth Apostle? No way! The Ninth Apostle must stay with Gumokgyo! Believers! Kill that woman!"

Pajik.

Lightning crackled from Seong Hari's spear.

Immediately after, her feet kicked off the ground. The Gumokgyo believers couldn't even follow Seong Hari's movements with their eyes.

"She disappeared... Keuek?!"

Seong Hari struck them with the spear shaft and her fists, knocking them unconscious. She didn't kill them. To save Seong Yujin, she needed information, and to verify the amount and reliability of the information, she needed to keep as many alive as possible.

"I can't see her... Eoeok!"

"S-She's like a monster!"

There was no need for Seong Han-gun or other spiritists from the Jinryeong Seong family to step in. They couldn't even follow Seong Hari's movements, let alone stop her.

Seong Han-gun, who was watching Seong Hari's movements with his arms crossed, was secretly sweating.

'She must be weakened by the Spirit King's curse... Still, she's absurdly strong.'

Seong Han-gun ordered the spiritists to clean up the situation.

"You will have to tell me everything you know."

While Seong Han-gun interrogated the subdued Gumokgyo believers, Seong Hari couldn't stay still and wandered around Gumokgyo. She couldn't let go of the hope that Seong Yujin might be hiding in a corner. Several spiritists followed behind her.

Seong Hari discovered a wooden cage in a corner of a room and quickly approached it. Because a familiar spirit was trapped inside the wooden cage.

"Moka!"

Woojikkeun.

Seong Hari smashed the wooden cage with one hand as if tearing it apart, and grabbed Moka's body with one hand.

Moka trembled. She knew that Seong Hari wouldn't kill her, but the spirit's instinct feared Seong Hari.

"Moka! You have a contract with Yujin-nim, so you know where Yujin-nim is now, right? Where is Yujin-nim?!"

"Kkuu... Kkuu...."

Moka shook her head. It meant that she didn't know where he was either. If even the contracted spirit didn't know where he was, Seong Yujin must be trapped in a special place.

Seong Hari was not disappointed. It was what she had expected. More important than that was.

"Moka. The contract hasn't been broken, right? Yujin-nim. Yujin-nim is safe, right?"

"Kkuu."

Moka nodded her head.

A sigh of relief escaped from Seong Hari's mouth. The situation that Seong Hari was most worried about was Seong Yujin being kidnapped and killed. Fortunately, the worst-case scenario was avoided.

'It's certain that Yujin-nim isn't here. But let's look around a little more. There might be a clue.'

Seong Hari wandered around. If she encountered a Gumokgyo believer, she would immediately subdue them and ask.

"Where is Yujin-nim?"

"I cannot say."

Seong Hari didn't have deep conversations with the Gumokgyo believers. She knew that she couldn't communicate with those in front of her. They were fanatics who could sacrifice their lives for their god. Even if she tortured them, they wouldn't tell her properly. She had to use other methods to find Yujin-nim.

And finally, Seong Hari arrived at the altar.

The priest, who had been kneeling quietly in front of the altar and praying, stood up.

"Uninvited guests. This place is to enshrine the god with nine eyes. Please be quiet, at least in this place."

"......Are you the owner here? I won't beat around the bush. Where is Yujin-nim?"

"The owner of this place is the god with nine eyes. He is the true owner of Gumokgyo, and we are merely servants."

The priest's mask fell to the floor with a thud. The revealed face was ordinary. Except for the third eye on his forehead.

Seong Hari gripped the spear shaft again and stepped forward, saying to the spiritists behind her.

"You guys step back."

"We will help too. Seong Hari-nim."

"I'm enough on my own. Rather, you guys will only be a hindrance."

Lightning crackled from her body as she spoke coldly. The spiritists looked at Seong Hari with pity.

"I know that you are the mother of the Ninth Apostle."

"Then tell me where Yujin-nim is. A child should be with their parents."

"Thank you for bringing the Ninth Apostle into this world. But it is the will of the god with nine eyes who leads the Ninth Apostle. Your existence is nothing but an obstacle to him. Even if I receive the Ninth Apostle's hatred, I will eliminate you here."

"You're taking a long time to say you're going to kill me."

Seong Hari threw the spear at the priest. Kwarrrrreureung! The spear aimed for the priest's chest with a thunderous sound.

The priest's third eye trembled. The direction of the spear changed, and its tip pointed towards Seong Hari.

Seong Hari simply grabbed the flying spear with her right hand.

"That eye. I don't like it."

"Hehehe. As expected, you don't understand. This eye is the eye that the god with nine eyes bestowed upon me. Even if it doesn't reach the eyes of the apostles, it is one of the greatest treasures in this world."

Kwarrrrreureung!

A spear containing lightning flew from Seong Hari's right. The same spear that Seong Hari was holding in her hand.

"......."

Seong Hari didn't panic at all and deflected the flying spear by swinging her spear. The spear flew through the air and disappeared.

Kwarrrrreureung.

This time, it flew from the back and front. From two directions. Seong Hari rotated and deflected the spear.

"I tried to reproduce your spear, but it's meaningless and difficult."

Blood dripped from the priest's third eye.

"To think that it's already at its limit after only using it 4 times... Just how strong are you?"

"It's not that I'm strong, it's that you're weak."

"This is the first time I've heard such words since becoming a priest. But I can't deny it. I can't stop you with my current self. So.... I'll have to ask that person."

He was trying to pull a trick. Seong Hari, who sensed it, moved. If throwing spears didn't work, she could move directly. She closed the distance in 1 second and thrust her spear at the priest. But an unknown force repelled her along with the spear.

"God with nine eyes. I offer everything I have. Please give me strength."

The priest's body began to pulsate and grow. The clothes he was wearing were torn off, and his skin turned blue. Dozens of eyeballs appeared all over his body.

He had clearly become a monster, not a human.

"This is crazy! A person turning into a monster...!"

"Seong Hari-nim! We've left a record! Do as you please!"

The spiritists shouted from behind her. Even if they didn't say it, Seong Hari was going to do as she pleased.

Pajijik.

Seong Hari ran and jumped into the sky. A translucent blue barrier appeared in the air. She kicked the barrier without hesitation and moved freely in the air.

The giant priest stretched out tentacles from his body and aimed for Seong Hari, but they couldn't reach Seong Hari.

Seong Hari swung her spear and slashed the priest's body. But the priest's body, which had increased in durability, was not cut in one go.

'If I can't cut it in one go, I can cut it multiple times.'

The priest's eyes followed Seong Hari's movements. But unlike his eyes, his body was too slow. Even though he could see Seong Hari's movements, he couldn't catch her or stop her. Eventually, the priest fell to the ground. The priest's dead body did not return to its original human form.

"Seong Hari-nim. We will take care of this corpse. The Hero Association will definitely want this. It will be helpful to the family."

"Okay."

The spiritists took the corpse and left. Seong Hari didn't care about the priest's corpse.

She looked around. There might be traces of finding Seong Yujin in this place.

There were no special traces except for strange patterns on the walls and floor. She leaned against the wall and let out a small sigh. She hadn't slept properly for days and had only worried about Seong Yujin, so she felt more mental fatigue than she had imagined and closed her eyes for a moment.

"Kkuuk. Kkuk!"

Seong Hari, who opened her eyes wide at the sound of Moka next to her, stared at the altar that seemed to have been made for a ritual.

A magic circle was drawn on the altar.

A teleportation magic circle. Someone was coming here through magic.

'Is it an enemy?'

Soon, a group appeared on the altar. Two people wearing masks and one child.

Her eyes, which had been showing hostility while holding a spear, widened. She ran towards the altar faster than Moka.

"Yujin-ah!"

Her running body stopped. She saw Seong Yujin's golden eyes. They were not the eyes of the son that Seong Hari knew. The eyes looking at her were dry. There was no sign of joy at all.

"......Yujin-ah?"

Seong Yujin was wearing black priestly robes. A round red jewel was embedded in his chest.

"That woman dares to call my name so carelessly."

It was a voice as dry as his eyes.

"Get rid of her."

"Yes. Ninth Apostle-nim."

The masked people on either side of Seong Yujin rushed towards Seong Hari.

I asked Cassandra, the leader of Gumokgyo, to appear with two moderately strong believers.

Cassandra agreed to cooperate with the scenario I had planned.

'If I go back and say that nothing happened, who will easily believe me? The Gumokgyo believers in the Korean branch might say strange things and make things difficult for me.'

And I was going to use this opportunity to make Seong Hari cherish me even more.

'They say that people realize something is precious only after they lose it. It's a situation that often appears in comics. Well, Seong Hari cherishes me even in normal times, though.'

In fact, there was nothing special about the plan. I was going to pretend to be brainwashed by Gumokgyo. The bead hanging on my chest was the source of the brainwashing, and the setting was that if it was destroyed, the brainwashing would be undone. What was important in this situation was my acting. And I had acting as a characteristic. I was confident in my acting.

I looked at Seong Hari fighting the two masked people. Seong Hari's skills were amazing. The speed was so fast that it was hard to follow with my own two eyes.

'Not only that. Her skill in handling the spear is amazing. It's as if the spear is a part of her body.'

Seong Hari had several opportunities to kill the masked people. But Seong Hari didn't kill the masked people. The reason was easy to guess. She didn't want to show me her committing murder.

Seong Hari was trying to subdue the masked people as much as possible.

"You pathetic things. How long are you going to disappoint me? Finish them off quickly."

One was already flying away and unconscious, and the other was struggling.

"I-I'm sorry. Apostle-nim. Please wait a moment. I'll finish them off soon... Keok?!"

The masked person, whose neck was grabbed by Seong Hari, was slammed into the floor. The knife that the masked person was holding spun around and fell towards me.

"Yujin-ah?! Dodge!!"

Seong Hari came this way and tried to grab the knife. I snatched the knife faster than her and swung it at Seong Hari.

Seong Hari raised her spear and blocked my knife. It was quite a serious attack, but it was blocked so easily that I was a little shocked.

"Yujin-ah! It's me! It's your mom! Do you recognize me?! Huh?!"

"You are my mother?"

"Yes. Yujin-ah! It seems like those guys did something to you, but it's okay. You'll be able to come back. Mom will help you."

"......I have no parents. The one who created me in this world is the god with nine eyes."

I raised my mana to strengthen my body and swung my knife. But this time again, it was still easily blocked by the spear.

"Brainwashing. Those Gumokgyo bastards brainwashed you!"

"I have never been brainwashed."
