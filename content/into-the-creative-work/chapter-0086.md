The bandits simultaneously threw nets at Nell.

"...Ugh!"

Nell was bound to get caught in the nets. There were too many nets to dodge or tear apart with her sword.

She swung her sword at the nets, trying to escape quickly. However, the nets had small, sharp thorns and sticky slime on them, making it difficult to escape even if she cut through them with her sword.

"It's over."

Pfrek said, crossing his arms. If she had been wearing armor, it might have been different, but the woman in front of him was wearing a tunic commonly worn by commoners.

The bandits shot arrows or threw stones at Nell, whose movements were restricted. Nell swung her sword desperately. However, the tangled nets wouldn't let her go easily.

It would have been different if her body had been in good condition. Her body, which had become emaciated from not eating properly for a long time, couldn't exert its full strength.

Eventually, Nell collapsed to the ground.

"Ooh! We got her!"

"Even a knight couldn't withstand this. Numbers are the answer, after all."

"Hehe. Now it's time to have some fun!"

The bandits chuckled and approached the woman. They planned to make sure she was dead before having their fun. It would be better to cut off her limbs before enjoying her... but the boss told them to kill her for sure, so they had no choice.

"I! I'll kill her first!"

A bandit holding a dagger rushed forward breathlessly. In the Pfrek bandit group, there was an unwritten rule that the one who killed the woman got to enjoy her first.

"You crazy bastard! That woman is an Aura Exper...."

Before the bandit next to him could finish speaking, the new bandit's head flew into the air.

The bandits reacted quickly. All of them grabbed their daggers and rushed in from all directions. Dozens of daggers were stabbed into her body. A dagger was also stuck in her neck, ensuring she was dead.

"...Aww. Damn it. It's no fun to fuck like this."

The bandits clicked their tongues.

A bloody body with dagger holes. And intestines spilling out from her abdomen. Even the most depraved bandits wouldn't think of violating such a gruesome corpse.

"What? Are you guys not going to do it?"

One bandit asked.

"Shibal. Look at that mess. Does it look like I'm in the mood? There are other living women, you know."

"This bitch has a better face, though. Plus, she's an Aura Expert. When else would we get to eat a woman like this? Hehe."

A bear-like man approached the corpse. He was covered in hair all over his body, including his face. He started to lower his pants in front of the gruesome corpse.

"You crazy bastard. He's really going to do it."

It was when all the bandits nearby were shaking their heads.

Tag-dak! Tag-dak!

The sound of horses running was heard. And not only that. Buried in the sound of horses running was the sound of metal clashing.

The bandits stopped what they were doing and turned their gazes to the source of the sound. The bandits' faces turned pale in an instant.

"What the fuck!"

Pfrek cursed as he saw the knights charging towards them.

"What are you doing, you idiots! Scatter and run away now!"

Pfrek shouted at the bandits again. The bandits screamed and began to scatter and run away in all directions.

'Damn it! How did the knights get here so soon? That many people and warhorses mean it's not just any ordinary knight order!'

Pfrek himself was also an Aura Expert. He had the martial prowess to become a knight, but he had never learned proper swordsmanship, and his equipment was poor compared to those wearing full-body armor. Above all, there were 20 knights at a glance.

"Don't kill them, just capture them!"

Seong Yujin-gun gave the order from behind the knights.

"That's easy."

"As you command!"

The knights replied. And so, the knights' rampage began.

It was impossible for the bandits to escape. They were knights riding warhorses. There was no comparison in terms of mobility.

"Aaaaaaah! My arm!"

The bandits fell. The knights perfectly carried out Seong Yujin-gun's order not to kill. They were true specialists in combat.

"Throw the nets! Let's do it like before!"

It was ineffective. Unless they surrounded them and threw the nets, they could simply brush them aside with their swords or dodge them. Even if they did get caught in the nets, the strength of the trained warhorses easily tore them apart.

"Arrows! Shoot arrows from afar!"

It was a futile struggle. The knights' reflexes were so sharp that they casually deflected the arrows flying at them. Even if they missed an arrow, they were wearing armor, so it had no effect at all.

"Surrender! We surrender! Please don't kill us!"

"Drop your weapons and kneel."

The knights said coldly to the bandits. The situation quickly began to be resolved.

'Damn it.'

Pfrek clenched his fist as he saw three knights approaching him. The situation was already at its worst, beyond repair. It was impossible to escape.

'The only reason they wouldn't kill us is if they plan to torture us.'

Torturing criminals was a common occurrence.

Fighting was meaningless, and escape was impossible. There was only one option left.

'I'd rather die than be tortured and prolong my life!'

Pfrek tried to stab himself in the neck with his dagger. If the sword that flew in front of him hadn't knocked his dagger away.

"I know your kind of habits very well, you know?"

It was Hent. He got off his horse and quickly ran to Pfrek, punching him in the stomach.

"Keuh-eok!"

"Give up. It's already over. Let's not make each other tired."

Pfrek raised his dagger. A bluish aura formed on the blade.

"What's that? A candle? It'll go out right away if you blow on it."

Hent chuckled and blew on it.

"You old geezer! Die!"

Hent grabbed Pfrek's arms with both hands. He twisted them 360 degrees once and then dislocated his shoulder by hitting it. He kicked and broke his knee joints with his legs.

"Oops."

Hent grabbed Pfrek's face as he tried to fall to the ground. He tore Pfrek's clothes with one hand and gagged him. This was to prevent him from biting his tongue and committing suicide.

"You dare to commit robbery in the Prūkus territory? You've underestimated the Prūkus family. You're under special management, you bastard."

Hent knocked Pfrek unconscious by hitting the back of his head and looked for Seong Yujin-gun. He found him without difficulty. Seong Yujin-gun was being guarded by the butler Hasent.

Hent kept a close eye on the butler.

"I always thought he wasn't an ordinary butler... Seeing him here, he's completely flawless."

Could he win if they fought? He couldn't be sure. That meant he had at least similar skills to himself.

"Anyway. There are so many monsters in this world."

When the Plūn Knights began to clean up the Pfrek bandit group. I got off my horse and looked around.

'It was described as a house in the most secluded part of the village, with the forest behind it.'

I found it without difficulty. I was heading towards the house when I stopped.

In front of it was the corpse of a woman caught in numerous nets.

Black hair. A sword lying a little distance away. A thin, emaciated body. Her face was beautiful.

'...Nell Lins. I guess I was too late.'

My plan went a bit wrong. I originally intended to gain favor with both Nell Lins and Yuria Grace and take them all in. According to the description in the story, Nell Lins was also an excellent female knight.

'At least it seems she didn't suffer like in the original story.'

I walked towards the house where Yuria was hiding. The sun hadn't completely set yet, so she would be hiding behind the house.

I stopped in front of the house without going inside.

I have no intention of threatening Yuria Grace. Even if I forcibly threaten her to follow me, she will end up betraying me.

I have no intention of deceiving her with sweet words either. I don't have that great of a silver tongue. Also, there's no way a fugitive like Yuria would be easily fooled.

"I am Yujin Prūkus. The third son of Count Prūkus."

First, I introduced myself.

At the same time, I glanced back. Hasent was watching me from a short distance away. I couldn't tell Hasent, who was voluntarily guarding me, to go away.

Hasent would definitely have noticed something strange about me.

'......Oh well, whatever. I'll just make something up later.'

Hasent is taciturn. Maybe he'll pretend not to know even if he hears it.

"Yuria Grace."

I called her name directly. I wanted to give off the impression that I knew her well. However, there was still no response.

Maybe she had already escaped into the forest and wasn't behind the house anymore.

...I hope that's not the case.

"I propose a deal."

All I can do is make a deal.

It's not unusual. Rather, it can be said to be the norm. The relationship between a lord and vassal is originally a transactional one. The vassal pledges loyalty, and the lord bestows a fiefdom commensurate with that loyalty.

And fortunately, I know what Yuria wants through the original story.

"I will help you with your revenge. This includes the rights to the captured bandits here, as well as revenge on those behind them."

"......."

There was no response.

Did I fail? Or was she never there to begin with?

What should I do? It would be too wasteful to lose Yuria now, given her talent. Should I just capture her and slowly brainwash her with care...? All sorts of absurd imaginations ran wild. That's when I heard the voice I had been waiting for.

"......Do you know who my revenge is against?"

It was a calmer and more pleasant female voice than I had expected.

"I know. I'm confident in my information, even if I look like this."

I said that while mouthing the words. Hasent behind me couldn't see it.

-The Helbrit Duchy.

Yuria Grace is an illegitimate child of the Helbrit Duchy.

An illegitimate child born between Duke Helbrit, who is also the kingdom's prime minister, and the female knight Nell Lins.

The Helbrit Duchy pursues cleanliness to a pathological degree, so they eliminate anything that could cause controversy in advance. This is why Nell Lins and Yuria Grace are being chased by the Duchy.

And fortunately, the Helbrit Duchy knows about Nell Lins, but they don't know Yuria's name or appearance at all.

"How do you... know so much about me?"

"I've investigated in various ways. About your bloodline. Nell Lins, a female knight who escaped from a noble family a dozen years ago.... If there are clues, it's not difficult to guess."

I pretended that I accidentally found out while investigating Nell Lins.

A moment of silence passed.

"I want to achieve revenge... entirely with my own hands."

"Of course you have to do it with your own hands. That's what revenge is."

Yuria will succeed in her revenge even without my help. That's what happened in the original story. But Yuria doesn't know the future.

The target of her revenge is the Helbrit Duchy, which holds the position of prime minister in the kingdom. Logically, it's not an opponent that a woman with nothing can handle.

If Yuria really wants revenge, she will need my help.

"The price.... What price do I have to pay you?"

"Yuria Grace. It's you. If you offer me everything you have... I will help you get revenge within 20 years. If you fail to get revenge within 20 years... you can take my life."

I said confidently. In the original story, Yuria finishes her revenge within 10 years from now. 20 years is more than enough time.

'...Hmm. Still, asking her to give me everything seems a bit too much. Should I have asked for her loyalty?'

There was something I had briefly overlooked.

"Is that all the price is?"

This is not the modern era, but a medieval fantasy world.

Human rights? They don't exist. Slavery is legal. Even commoners can have slaves if they have money.

A healthy young male slave sells for an average of 100 million Ner. Women are about half that, but it varies depending on their beauty and background.

Yuria is a commoner, but she is a fugitive. Ignoring her future value and considering her current value, she wouldn't be worth even 30 million Ner.

Objectively speaking, I'm the one who's losing out, except for the fact that Yuria is a talent who will become an Aura Master.

"Just that? I'm telling you to offer me not only your body but also your heart and soul as the price."

"If I can achieve revenge by offering this wretched existence... I will gladly offer myself."

"Then is the deal done?"

"Yes. From this moment on, everything of mine except for one thing is yours. Master-nim."

"Good. Revenge is only yours. Now I want to see your face. May I go there?"

"Yes. Master-nim."

That's perfect.

I plan to hide her appearance for now. I don't think it will happen, but it's good to be thorough when it comes to Helbrit.

Fortunately, the knights are busy cleaning up the situation and aren't paying attention to this side. They don't know about Yuria's existence. Except for one person.

"Butler Hasent."
