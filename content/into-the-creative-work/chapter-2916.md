Kim Gahoon summoned the Hightrees Guild members.

It was a weekday afternoon, and there was no dungeon raid scheduled, so he was able to gather the guild members in less than 30 minutes.

Including Kim Gahoon himself, there were 5 A-rank Hunters, 16 B-rank Hunters, and 20 C-rank Hunters. A total of 41 people.

D-rank or lower Hunters? Kim Gahoon didn't consider D-rank or lower Hunters as part of the force. In a company, they're just interns. He only hired D-rank or lower Hunters because he felt pressured by the Association, the government, and public opinion. He would cut them off, except for a few talented ones, if he had the chance.

,My younger sister, Gayoung-i, was attacked by the Black Guild. Gayoung-i wasn't an official member of Hightrees, but she helped the guild in many ways. There's no one here who hasn't received help from Gayoung-i. We're going to save Gayoung-i.

,Yes, Master.

,No matter what crazy bastards they are, we'll make them regret touching Gayoung-nim.

The Hightrees Guild members moved in their respective cars.

They had to get out of the car near Kim Gayoung's fortune-telling shop. The road was blocked. A barricade was blocking the car's entry. Next to it was a city hall mark, a police mark, and even a Hunter Association mark, indicating road construction.

,Did they impersonate not only the city hall but also the police and the Hunter Association to block the road?

Removing the barricade and entering... it felt a bit off. There were cameras nearby, and people around.

,If I act rashly, it'll be on the news. I can't damage Hightrees' image with something like this.

Even while his younger sister Kim Gayoung was suffering humiliation, he was calmly assessing the situation.

,It's only a 3-minute walk anyway. We'll go on foot. Get out.

The Hightrees Guild members got out of the car in a rush. As they were all armed Hunters, they exuded a fierce aura.

Jeobeok jeobeok.

Kim Gahoon walked in front, lost in thought.

Kim Gayoung was taken hostage. The enemy had no demands. It wasn't about money or goods.

Through the video call earlier, he felt the enemy's pure malice and murderous intent.

,Saving Gayoung-i is practically impossible. ...It's something I was prepared for. My brother and I have been doing bad things using the power of the Ancient Tree. I'll be able to save Gayoung-i with the power of the Ancient Tree. If not, I'll take thorough revenge.

The siblings had already prepared themselves for this.

They arrived at the stairs leading up to the fortune-telling shop. In front of a fancy foreign car, two B-rank Hunters from Hightrees were lying dead, their limbs and necks severed. They were the guild members assigned to Gayoung-i as gatekeepers and bodyguards.

,Shibal!

Kwang!

The guild members attacked the foreign car in front of the stairs, cursing.

Kim Gahoon didn't stop them.

It didn't take long for the foreign car to become a crumpled pile of scrap metal. But the guild members' anger wasn't relieved.

,There are bastards who messed with our guild up there. We're going to kill them. Be prepared.

The Hightrees Guild climbed the stairs, not hiding their anger and murderous intent.

Kim Gahoon, who had climbed all the stairs, was startled to see the felled Ancient Tree.

That Ancient Tree was one of the descendants of the famous Sacred Tree. Kim Gahoon and Kim Gayoung had been connected to this Ancient Tree since they were young and were able to use special powers. Kim Gahoon awakened his ability and became a Hunter, but he was still connected to the Ancient Tree.

A mysterious power of unknown origin.

,The Ancient Tree is alive.

The Ancient Tree's power had weakened, but it hadn't died, probably because the roots remained.

Deolkeong.

The door of the traditional Korean house opened, and the bastard came out.

A man in his early to mid-20s holding a red sword. Seong Yujin looked around once and threw something towards the ground of the yard.

It was Kim Gayoung, her limbs cut off, her hair torn out, and her stomach ripped open.

,O, oppa.... Save me....

Her intestines were spilling out from her torn stomach.

Even so, she was still alive. The residual lightning energy in her body, the sacred power of the Ancient Tree, was keeping Kim Gayoung alive.

,.......

The Hightrees Guild members, faced with a sight more horrific than they could have imagined, momentarily lost their anger and felt fear.

Only Kim Gahoon exploded with anger.

,I know you! S-rank Hunter candidate Seong Yujin!!

He couldn't not know him. He was famous as an S-rank Hunter candidate, and he was also related to the Han Ah-young and Han Ha-rin sisters.

,Why did you do this?!

,Are you asking because you don't know?

Eyes that seemed to be swirling with blue lightning turned towards Kim Gahoon. Kim Gahoon felt an urge to step back for a moment but bit his lip and endured.

The reason why Seong Yujin came and made Kim Gayoung look like that? He couldn't not know. He got caught giving yangbab to Han Ha-rin.

Kim Gahoon's brain was spinning rapidly.

The possibility of resolving this through conversation? None. He couldn't just let it go as her older brother and the master of the Hightrees Guild after he made Kim Gayoung look like that.

Han Ah-young isn't here. Seong Yujin is an S-rank Hunter candidate, but he's just a candidate, not an S-rank Hunter.

If he can't kill Seong Yujin here... Han Ah-young will come to kill him.

,...There's only one best option. Kill Seong Yujin and leave Korea. It's a shame about the power of the Ancient Tree, but there's no other choice. I can't live in Korea antagonizing Han Ah-young. I'll have to change my identity even if I defect overseas.

He came to a decision.

Kim Gahoon drew his sword from his waist.

,The sin of making Gayoung-i look like that...! I can never forgive....

Chwaak.

Blood splattered.

Kim Gahoon felt the bitter taste of blood entering his mouth and turned his head to the side. His colleague, an A-rank Hunter, was being sliced by Seong Yujin.

,Seong Yujin's acceleration ability...! It's so fast that I missed it for a moment?!

Kim Gahoon quickly used mana. Mana strengthened his entire body and forcibly increased his concentration. His thinking speed accelerated as he entered combat mode.

Puk.

A red sword was embedded in the chest of the A-rank Hunter behind Kim Gahoon.

All Kim Gahoon could see was the red current left behind by the passing red flash.

,This isn't the time to conserve power...!

His instincts were telling him. Now is the time to use all his power.

Dozens of vines sprouted from Kim Gahoon's entire body in an instant. It was Kim Gahoon's ability.

,The moment the vines touch the skin, they're infected with poison, and I absorb their power. If I can absorb that bastard's power, I'll win.

Chwaaaaaak.

Before the dead guild member could fall to the ground, a fountain of blood erupted from another guild member's body. Thanks to that, he was able to pinpoint the location. The problem was that he couldn't catch up by moving the vines and chasing after him.

,Too fast.

He was moving faster than Kim Gahoon's sight.

,He's taking out the other guild members first, not me. Even if I can't track his movements, I can predict them.

He sent out vines in the direction of the living guild members. It was an unavoidable sacrifice even if the guild members were attacked by the vines in the process.

,I touched him!

Seong Yujin was stepping on the vines and cutting a guild member's body in half. What was important was that any part of his body touched the vines.

,Even if it's not touching the skin, so the poison isn't effective, I can still absorb his power!

Of course, he can't absorb all of his power. The amount absorbed varies depending on the opponent's power and his own capabilities. The important thing is that the opponent becomes weaker, and he becomes stronger.

Power was flowing in through the vines.

It wasn't just absorbing mana. He was even absorbing the opponent's physical abilities.

Kim Gahoon felt joy as he felt a power that transcended his rank, but.

,Even when I absorbed the power of an A-rank raid boss monster, it wasn't this much. Could it be....

He felt fear at the power that was becoming excessively strong.

,......He's already at the level of an S-rank...?

It was too late when he realized.

Pajijijijijijik!

Red lightning traveled through the vines and into his body. Dozens of vines connected to his body burst into flames at the same time.

,Aaaaaaaaaaak!

He couldn't even scream from the pain that felt like his entire body was being torn apart. It felt like his entire body was burning from the inside out.

Teolseok.

Kim Gahoon, who had collapsed, raised his head. The surroundings were filled with blood and guts. Kim Gahoon was the only guild member alive. Seong Yujin was looking down at him with a sword in his hand. He could feel the hostility and malice. Kim Gahoon sensed that he wouldn't die as peacefully as his sister.

,O, Mother Tree-isiyeo. Please give me strength...!

He spoke as if begging towards the Ancient Tree in front of him. Sacred power flowed in through the passage connected to the Ancient Tree. The Ancient Tree was squeezing out its power and sending it to Kim Gahoon. It was the moment when Kim Gahoon felt hope and tried to get up.

Kwang!

A spear fell from the sky onto the base of the Ancient Tree. Immediately after, a huge lightning bolt fell towards the spear from a cloudless sky.

Kkiaaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!

The descendant of the Sacred Tree, the Ancient Tree, screamed spiritually and died, burned to its roots.

Kim Gahoon trembled as he felt the disappearing power.

,Plea, please kill me. Please....

What came back was the highest-grade potion being poured on his head.

That's enough.

These two bastards were the only ones who came up with the sinister plan targeting Han Ha-rin and Han Ah-young. I confirmed it through interrogation.

To put it bluntly, they judged that your value is greater than the value of the Hightrees Guild. Above all, those two crossed the line. Han Ha-rin and Han Ah-young are important figures even in the World Hunter Association. So, what are you going to do with those two?
