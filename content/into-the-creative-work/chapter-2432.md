I smirked, lips curling upward above the pool of blood.

A sense of overflowing power. This place was now my domain.

,Looking down is giving me a pain in the neck. Come down first."

I raised my hand and lowered it. The pool of blood beneath me resonated and surged. The magic's power amplified.

[Gravity]

Rahul, who had been floating in the sky, began to plummet at high speed. He stopped just before crashing into the ground. Glowing stones circled around him, floating in the air. They resembled the disaster stones Aisa-ssi had used.

[Blood Control]

Controlling blood.

Simply controlling blood, but it's a different story when that blood belongs to a Trueblood Vampire. The blood itself contains immense power.

Blood gushed from the pool, engulfing Rahul. The goal was to crush him, but... it didn't work. An invisible barrier centered around the stones blocked the blood.

,How annoying. Simply manipulating blood isn't enough to penetrate this Jiyeongjiseok (Land Shadow Record Stone)."

The ring of stones around him began to rotate. A massive wind arose, scattering the surrounding blood.

This wasn't just a phenomenon caused by the rapid movement of the stones. I realized that each and every one of those stones contained an immense amount of compressed mass.

One of the stones flew toward me. I raised a wall of blood, but it was easily pierced.

I used one of the Trueblood Vampire's powers, Mist Form. I turned my body into mist and soared into the sky. My body floated without falling. This was also a Trueblood Vampire's power.

Kwaaaaaang!

The stone struck the place where I had been, creating a massive explosion. Glancing down, I saw a crater as if a meteorite had fallen.

[Blood Spike]

Hundreds of blood spikes targeted Rahul. But once again, they failed to pierce the barrier surrounding him.

,...That barrier is too solid to be just barrier output. Is it some kind of conceptual barrier?"

,Not bad for a magician, Vampire. This barrier is like a giant rock. Harder and heavier than steel. 'Dripping water pierces stone.' But that takes thousands, tens of thousands of years. Your blood can't pierce this rock."

,What are you talking about? You're not a rock."

Cough.

Suddenly, Rahul coughed up blood. Even though it was a clone, it was made of blood and flesh. Precise clone manipulation wouldn't be possible unless the clone was similar to the original down to the cellular level.

Rahul, who had been flustered by the sudden hemoptysis, groaned as if he had realized something.

,Ignoring my barrier... a curse?"

,Just because I'm a vampire doesn't mean I can only use blood magic. Even if it's not as good as blood magic, black magic goes well with vampires. Seeing that you're only realizing this now, it seems you can't feel pain in your clone state."

Secretly taking out the Dark Hole from my inventory wasn't difficult. Using black magic secretly was even easier. Fortunately, he didn't notice.

[Pain Tulla]

A 5th-grade black magic spell that inflicts a curse, twisting the target's internal organs.

There's only one condition for the curse: making eye contact with the target.

Usually, the pain is felt immediately when cursed, so they notice it right away. If you focus, it's not difficult to lift the curse even if you're not a magician. The simpler the curse's conditions, the easier it is to lift.

I used it because he said it was a clone, and it was the right answer.

,It's a clone for use as a weapon. Is there any need to feel pain?"

,Hmm. You're right. But because of that, your internal organs are now twisted. Even if it's a clone, it won't last long, right? Three minutes at most. I'll watch you die during that time."

Even if he releases Pain Tulla, it's already too late. The golden time is gone. His internal organs are already twisted. A normal human would already be dead. The reason Rahul is still standing is because he's a clone.

,Three minutes is enough time to kill you."

Rahul spread his hands. The glowing stones that had been protecting him scattered in all directions, attacking me from every angle.

The stones were flying too fast to dodge. Their power was too strong to block.

,Mist Form.'

I used the Trueblood's power to turn my body into mist.

I could ignore attacks that used physical force. But the stones carried the術 concept of Jiyeongjiseok. Even in Mist Form, I couldn't completely disregard them. The damage built up inside me, slowly but surely.

Rahul, knowing this, didn't stop the stone bombardment. The fallen stones rose again and repeated their attacks on me.

,The power to turn your body into mist is really annoying. But all you're doing is enduring, aren't you? To think I'd be in a battle of endurance with the likes of you. I admit it, Trueblood Vampire. I'll remember you. Tell me your name before you die."

Do you think I don't know your intentions? Names are important, both magically and technically. I have no name to give to a mere clone like you, not even to the original. And a battle of endurance? I never intended to fight while enduring.

I could speak and use magic even while maintaining Mist Form.

And right now, Rahul had abandoned defense and was pouring everything into his attacks. He wanted to inflict damage on me somehow, since the clone's death was certain due to Pain Tulla. I would have done the same.

,But thanks to that, the annoying barrier disappeared, revealing a gap.'

I used the 6th-grade blood magic I had been preparing since I entered Mist Form.

[Blood Scream]

In the limited space where the pool of blood was spread, a small tsunami began to rage thousands of times. It was like a compressed storm.

Rahul was swallowed by Blood Scream, and the Jiyeongjiseok that had been targeting me lost their power and fell to the ground.

,It's not over. He's still enduring in the center of Blood Scream.'

He was enduring Blood Scream with his bare body, even though he wasn't in a normal state. I had to acknowledge his capabilities.

,But he won't be able to endure for long.'

As expected. Rahul died in less than 30 seconds. Although it was a clone, it was my victory.

I removed Mist Form and descended to the ground.

I wasn't in a normal state either. Rahul's Jiyeongjiseok had steadily damaged me as well. If it had been daytime instead of night, I would have lost.

,As a vampire, that is.'

The pool of blood that had been stagnant on the ground disappeared as if evaporating. Some of it returned to me as power, but it wasn't very satisfying.

I opened my inventory and took out a blood pack.

I opened one of the mana awakener's blood packs that I had bought for a lot of money and started drinking it.

,It tastes bad.'

Even vampires have preferred blood tastes. In my case, most blood tasted bad. But I couldn't be picky. For vampires, blood was both food and medicine.

,Taste aside, I feel a little better. Recovery is a bit slow, maybe because that stone was special.'

As I emptied one blood pack, Aisa-ssi approached me. She looked around at the messed-up surroundings and discovered Rahul's corpse.

,Oh my god. You really fought and won against Rahul-seonbae's clone?"

,I fought because I had a chance of winning."

I turned my back on her and approached Rahul's corpse.

A tattered corpse. Only the head and part of the upper body remained. That was enough. I grabbed the corpse's head and used blood magic.

[Blood Chaser]

The blood remaining in Rahul's corpse wriggled and then lost its power.

The magic didn't work.

I was going to use Blood Chaser to find the original's location... but some measures have been taken. Is this some kind of technique? It's impossible to find his location with Blood Chaser.

,I guess it failed. Well, if it's Rahul-seonbae, he would have taken some measures to prevent tracking."

,As long as we're here, he won't give up and will appear again. If you intend to stay here, tell me about him."

,Okay. Should we get out of here first? But are you okay? Your already pale complexion is even paler."

,I'm fine. Let's move."

Before leaving, Aisa-ssi took out a jade bracelet from her cleavage and handed it to me.

,Wear this. It will help you escape Rahul-seonbae's pursuit. And other people won't notice anything strange."

,I can feel an alien power. Is it the power of that great spirit?"

,Yes. A blessed jade stone processed... a bracelet of nature. Wearing it will make you blend in naturally. The effect will last about a week. Unless you deliberately cause trouble, Rahul-seonbae won't find you."

I accepted the bracelet and wore it.

To be honest, I couldn't understand the Druid's magic at all, so it was hard to trust. But there weren't many options.

Seeing Aisa-ssi so confident, the effect would definitely be there.

I wore the bracelet.

I felt a pressure weighing down on my whole body.

,...?!"

,What's wrong?!"

,What is this...? I feel like my body is being suppressed. I feel like I'm under the sunlight even though it's night."

,...Ah. The great spirit can be said to be nature itself in this world. For Druids, all nature is the great spirit... so the sun is also the great spirit, so it's the sun."

,In short, the power of the sun is contained in this bracelet?"

,Yes. Haha. It can't be helped. Please bear with it. It's better than being tracked by Rahul-seonbae, right?"

,You call that bastard seonbae so easily. That bastard tried to kill you, though?"

,That's right. But I'm used to this."

We moved away.

I was able to get information about Rahul.

Rahul.

Aisa-ssi's alumni seonbae and a 6th-grade Druid.

His specialty is cloning.

The technique he mainly uses is rock magic. Among them is the magic of Jiyeongjiseok (Land Shadow Record Stone), a technique that projects the power of the earth onto small stones.

Attack and defense. Both are excellent techniques. The power varies depending on the Druid's skill... when I saw it earlier, Rahul-seonbae's Jiyeongjiseok weighed over 10 tons each.

,...What's Rahul's purpose?"

,I don't know the details either. But I guess... transcendence. Rahul-seonbae. He's over 50 years old, even though he looks like that. He was a 6th-grade Druid when I was training to be a Druid. I heard he's been stuck at 6th grade for over 30 years. He had a huge desire for promotion back then. So, his purpose is probably... to be promoted to 7th grade."

,He fell to evil for promotion. Well, that's a common story."

It's not easy to reach 6th grade. But reaching 7th grade is even harder. Most of those who reach 6th grade die without reaching 7th grade.

,There's a saying that the reason why the strong of 6th grade go crazy is because they can't reach 7th grade.'

The art of Jiyeongjiseok (Land Shadow Record Stone). An art that channels the power of the earth into small stones. An art that is strong in both attack and defense.

Even if I don't know exactly what Rahul is doing here, as long as we're here, he'll try to find us and kill us no matter what.

,This has become annoying. I don't think I'm going back like this..."

Of course. There's always danger in reporting.

I can't do that reporting anymore, though.

,We can't do it openly. Rahul-seonbae will find out."

She smiled and raised her hand. She was wearing the same bracelet she had given me.

Do you know when I use this bracelet? When I need to do undercover reporting! Now is the time to start undercover reporting!
