Even now, skeletons and zombies continue to pour out from the black hole opened in the sky. The skeletons and zombies clumped together at one point, massing and growing larger.

Duke Archen glared at the giant zombie, already over 20m tall, and drew his sword.

,It's too big to leave to the knights and soldiers. I'll take care of this one. Go ahead and deal with that necromancer bastard. Finish him off as quickly as possible and follow me."

I looked at the giant zombie. The energy I felt from the giant zombie was comparable to Duke Archen. It's dangerous to leave this guy alone. But leaving the necromancer alone is even more dangerous.

"I'm counting on you, Duke Archen."

"Father. Don't overdo it."

We left the giant zombie to Duke Archen and headed to the Lily Garden.

Lily Garden.

As the name suggested, it was a garden where lilies bloomed profusely. Bathed in the blue moonlight, the Lily Garden was so beautiful it looked like CG.

"That's one hell of a garden."

"It's a garden conceived by my late mother. It's famous as the most beautiful garden in the Cobalt Kingdom. It's good to see it after so long. Well, it would have been more perfect if that ominous thing wasn't there."

In the center of the garden, in the shelter, sat the necromancer. Beside him, the demon contractor, tormented by the larva, was silently screaming.

What caught my eye was the necromancer's back. Behind him, a transparent glass coffin floated slightly in the air. Inside the glass coffin, a woman with long silver hair lay with her eyes closed. A woman with a beauty that didn't seem to belong to this world. There was no way I wouldn't know who she was.

"...Yuria."

The necromancer looked at me. He stood up.

Jeolgeureok, chwareureureuk, jeolgeureok, jeolgeureok.

There were numerous chains around him. Chains crawling on the ground and soaring into the sky. The starting point of those chains was the glass coffin containing Yuria.

"When I saw it earlier, it felt somehow familiar... Now I see. That's the Mistress's shadow magic. It was mixed with something else, so I was late in recognizing it."

Melissa said. She was an Aura Master and an Archmage at the same time. She was more of an expert in magic than I was.

I walked forward with Hwaryeon Bido in my left hand and Stormbreaker in my right. The necromancer struck the ground with his staff.

Kung!

Undead appear.

Two Death Knights, one Lich.

Top-tier undead that couldn't be compared to zombies and skeletons.

The Death Knights were Kyle and Claudia, and the Lich was Sharnel. I easily recognized the Lich because the robe was Sharnel's.

"Necromancer."

Normally, I would have rushed in and started fighting without a word.

That bastard is fake anyway. There's no point in having a conversation.

But now I had to ask him.

"Why is Yuria dead? Did you kill her?"

The necromancer's eyebrows twitched.

"Did I kill Yuria? Is that even a question? Would you kill Yuria?"

"Then answer the question. Why is Yuria dead?"

"I have no reason to answer you."

The necromancer pointed his staff at me. The undead attack me along with the chains.

"Master!!"

Melissa used magic. A stone wall rises from the ground, blocking the chains. It was useless. The chains pierced through the wall and flew towards me.

I dropped the weapons in both hands. Hwaryeon Bido and Stormbreaker merged into one. Then, the finely broken Stormbreaker stuck to my body and became armor.

Death Knights Kyle and Claudia charged at the same time. I charged at them with swords in both hands.

"Melissa. You take care of the Lich."

Lich Sharnel used fire magic from behind. Dozens of fireballs flew through the dark air. Melissa shot down the fireballs by throwing rocks with magic. Bang, bang, bang! Flames exploded in the air.

"You're asking for a difficult favor. That Lich is at least a high-ranking Archmage."

The Death Knights swung their swords from both sides. I trusted Stormbreaker and gave up defense.

Kaang! Kkang!

Stormbreaker didn't betray my expectations. It was slightly torn, but it withstood the Death Knights' swords.

Pajijik.

Red lightning flashed from the swords in both hands.

Thunderbolt Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

Two streaks of lightning cut through the Death Knights. Kyle's head fell off, and Claudia's upper and lower body were severed. My arms are shaking. That's because Lightning Flash wasn't originally a technique used like this.

'The path is open. It's worth the strain.'

I was about to run towards the necromancer when Kyle's headless torso blocked my path, and Claudia's upper body, without a lower body, crawled on the ground with her arms and tried to stab my back with a sword.

I ignored Claudia and passed by Kyle. Kyle's sword cut through my side. I'm safe thanks to the armor. I want to say that, but some of Kyle's sword energy penetrated inside the armor. He put the essence of Iga Jungsu Sword Technique into the sword energy.

'As expected of Kyle. His talent for swordsmanship is still there even in death.'

Ignoring the throbbing side, I glared at the necromancer in front of me.

Shadows flickered at the necromancer's feet. The shadows spread in all directions. The ground eaten by the shadows became muddy like a swamp.

"This is bullshit."

My ankle fell into the shadow swamp. It wasn't easy to move.

"What are you hesitating for!"

Melissa, who was fighting Lich Sharnel, shouted. Her magic permeated the ground. Immediately after, an explosion occurred in the ground. The shadow swamp scattered along with dirt and stones. The ground returned to its original solid state.

Moving forward, I put strength into both hands.

Thunderbolt Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning (疾风迅雷).

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 4]

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 3]

Even using Instant.

I was planning to kill that bastard in one go.

Chwareureureuk.

The chains hidden in the shadows suddenly appeared and wrapped around my ankle. I barely managed to keep my balance, almost falling forward. I glared at the chains wrapped around my ankle and swung my sword. The chains were cut, and another chain restrained the other leg.

Losing my balance and staggering, chains surged from all directions and wrapped around my body.

'...Damn it. I should have ignored the chains and swung my sword...'

I calm down my excitement. There are still means. It might even be a good thing. If I do well, I might be able to lead him to let his guard down.

'It's fortunate that I have the acting trait in times like this.'

The acting trait is surprisingly helpful in combat as well. For example, when throwing a feint. If the level is similar, you can deceive the opponent with almost a 90% probability.

"Master!!"

Melissa shouted. She tried to save me, but was hindered by Lich Sharnel. She seemed to be struggling just to deal with Sharnel.

The necromancer didn't even look at Melissa and controlled the black chains that wrapped around my body. Kwadeuk, kwadeuk! The black chains crushed and tore Stormbreaker.

"...It doesn't seem like a simple magic chain... What kind of chain is it?"

"It's the Shadow Chain Gleipnir. It's a relic used by ancient shadow mages."

"There was such a relic?"

"The world is wide. The information you know is only a part of this world."

Kwaddeudeudeuk!

The armor was torn. The necromancer used the chains to take out the smartphone in my pants pocket. He carefully grabbed my smartphone with expectant eyes.

'Now... no, not yet. He hasn't completely let his guard down yet.'

The necromancer turned on the smartphone with familiar movements. And he moved his thumb back and forth to switch screens.

I knew what that movement was. He's looking for the Pleasure Life app.

'Is the Pleasure Life app icon not visible? I didn't particularly hide it.'

His finger tapped the icon of the Pleasure Life app. Looking at his shaking eyes, he's not looking at the location of the Pleasure Life app and pressing it, but guessing the location and pressing it. However, the Pleasure Life app doesn't respond to the necromancer's finger.

"......Damn it."

It was a voice filled with terrible despair.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts 1 minute.]

The shadow chains that wrapped around my entire body are released.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 2]

The necromancer's face contorted in shock. As expected, that bastard didn't know my skills. I stabbed the necromancer's chest with the sword in my left hand, and raised the sword in my right hand high. I stopped trying to cut off that bastard's head as it was.

Because that bastard closed his eyes as if he had given up on everything.

"You. Is that really you? You're just going to die like this with just this much? If I were you, I would have struggled to the end."

"I'm fake. I had a glimmer of hope for the Pleasure Life app, but... even that hope has just collapsed. I can't save Yuria."

"...If you're going to die anyway, answer my question. Why did Yuria die?"

The necromancer opened his eyes again. He looked at me with blood-red eyes and then stepped back. He clutched his pierced chest with his hand and reached out to the glass coffin containing Yuria.

"Yuria was killed by the Demon King. Her soul was annihilated, and I barely managed to bring her body. Because her soul was annihilated, I couldn't save her even with necromancy. If I only had the power of the Pleasure Life app... I could have saved her..."

"Killed by the Demon King? Did you invade the Demon Realm?"

"Yujin Pruker. No, Seong Yujin. The original work has already been twisted. Discard the preconceived notion that the Demon King is in the Demon Realm."

"......Could it be. Is the Demon King in the human realm?"

"In my memory, the Demon King was in the human realm. I don't know the exact location either. However, there is a place I can guess."

"Where is that?"

"I don't want to tell you, but..."

The necromancer sighed as he looked at Yuria in the glass coffin.

"I'll tell you for the sake of Yuria in this world. Labyrinth City Rabbit. The Demon King is probably there. I also obtained the Shadow Chains from the lowest level of the Rabbit Labyrinth. Give the Shadow Chain Gleipnir to Yuria."

"Are there any other information? What about Priscilla or Doolivad?"

"There's no more time to tell you. There's a limit to how much I can endure... There's something I need to do before I disappear."

He raised his staff and pointed it at the sky. To be exact, he's pointing at the blue moon floating in the night sky.

Chwareureureureureureuk!

Hundreds of chains soared towards the moon from the shadows on the ground.

"I knew you were hiding there a long time ago. Pay the price for playing with me. The price is your life."

The space flickered. And the sound of something being pierced repeatedly rang out.

I activated my Heavenly Eye and looked up at the sky. A formless demon hiding in the flickering space is dying, pierced by hundreds of chains.

'Shadow Chain Gleipnir.... An item that can kill a formless demon.... It's an absurd item. I must find it and give it to Yuria.'

As the demon dies, the labyrinthization of the Archen mansion is lifted. At the same time, the necromancer's body slowly disappears, starting from his toes.

The necromancer didn't even give me a glance and opened the glass coffin. He quietly hugged Yuria's corpse tightly.

"Seong Yujin. Don't make the same mistake as me."

The necromancer disappeared with Yuria. The rampaging zombies also disappeared. Only a cool breeze blew in the quiet Lily Garden.
