Leaving the piled-up gold and treasures behind, I walked toward the books.

An old book lying among glittering treasures. It drew the eye all the more for how out of place it was.

"There's a fair amount of gold and jewels here. If I sell it all off, that's at least 1.5 billion credits. Even spirit elixirs laid out neat and tidy? Nice. Out of all the dungeons I've explored, this one's a jackpot—top three easily, just counting the rewards."

Nora's voice was trembling with excitement. I paid her no mind and picked up the book. Written on its cover were five Chinese characters.

Eight Gate Boundary Formation (八門境界陣).

From the outside, it was an ordinary book. I couldn't sense a shred of mana or any special power from it.

But I could guarantee it. The most valuable thing in this treasure vault was this book. More precisely, the knowledge contained within it.

I opened the book to check its contents.

Barrier arts.

A barrier art that used boundaries, built on a foundation of Qimen Dunjia (奇門遁甲).

I devoured the barrier arts written in the book, losing myself in them.

"Hold on, you. Aren't you going to split the treasure? We agreed to split everything we get from the dungeon fifty-fifty."

"...That's right. You split it in half."

"Hah. I guess you really are a mage. Leaving all these piled-up treasures to focus on some old book."

"You wouldn't know, not being a mage, but this isn't an ordinary book."

"I know. Eight Gate Boundary Formation. It's about barrier arts, right? You could call it a kind of grimoire."

"...You know without even looking at the contents?"

"There's no one in this world who can match my item appraisal skills. So how are we splitting the treasure? Some things can't be divided by money. Take this little Buddha statue, for example. Just keeping it at home blocks most malevolent energy—it's a totem. Put it up for auction and you'd easily get 100 million, you know?"

"...I don't care about the rest. I'm taking this book."

"I guess you're not that interested in money, huh?"

"The company brings in a fair amount. I'm not strapped for cash."

"Ah. Right. You're a chairman."

We began dividing the treasure. I took mostly gold, giving up the jewels and some of the equipment. We split the spirit elixirs in half. They weren't elixirs I needed right away. Just because something's called a spirit elixir doesn't mean it's always good for the body.

'No matter how good a spirit elixir is, it's rare for one to raise your realm through the elixir's power alone.'

If spirit elixirs were omnipotent, people would have sought only elixirs.

Still, I couldn't completely ignore them. After all, the bastards aiming for transcendence through drugs alone weren't my enemies for nothing.

KWAOOOOOOOOONG!

An explosion rang out from far away. The vibration transmitted faintly even into the treasure vault.

"Seems a big explosion went off outside. Must be the Wolfgang Mercenaries who chased us in."

It was unfortunate for the Wolfgang Mercenaries, but the treasure had gone into my and Nora's inventories and subspace. We planned to give up on the treasure we'd get from killing this dungeon's boss monster.

'Even if you kill the boss monster in a dungeon that has a treasure vault like this, it doesn't drop anything that good.'

I was satisfied just having obtained the Eight Gate Boundary Formation. I wanted to hurry outside and study it. Besides, I had to get out to receive the promised payment—Merlin's No.2, Avalonia.

"Hah."

A heavy sigh escaped Nora's lips. Her expression was stiff. The joy of obtaining treasure was nowhere to be seen.

"The path is closed."

I looked at the treasure vault's entrance.

"It's open, though."

"...The dungeon's exit has disappeared. Just a moment ago I could feel where to go... but after the explosion, I can't sense the exit anymore. The mercenaries must have used some method to erase the entrance. If not that, then..."

Nora's unique skill, Pathfinding, was the ability to find paths. If a path exists, she can find it no matter how faint. But if there is no path, she cannot find one. It wasn't an ability to create paths.

"...Blowing away a dungeon's entrance with pure physical force isn't easy. The Wolfgang Mercenaries don't seem like they'd have that kind of power. There must be another variable. For example... the dungeon boss has awakened."

"You know quite a lot about dungeons, surprisingly. That's right. Among dungeons, there are cases where the boss is sleeping. And in those cases, when the boss wakes, the dungeon entrance is sealed. To leave the dungeon, you have to defeat the boss."

"Do you know what the boss is? Sounds like a jiangshi type."

"Just because I'm me doesn't mean I can know something I've never seen."

"...We'll have to go outside and check the situation."

"The boss is at least Grade 7. Maybe even Grade 8 or higher..."

"It won't be."

"Why?"

"A hunch."

Actually, it wasn't a hunch but experience. A dungeon with a Grade 8 boss monster is far more brutal than this. That was also why Nora had chosen this dungeon. With her abilities, she'd dodge a Grade 8 dungeon's death spots like a ghost.

"Come to think of it, you seem to know more about dungeons than I do. To get out of here... it'll take at least 15 minutes."

"Too slow. I'll open the path from here."

Before Nora could even answer, I moved first.

Standing at the center of the emptied treasure vault, I raised my head. A ceiling over 10 meters high came into view. Naturally, there was nothing on the ceiling. Originally there had been luminous pearls embedded there, but even those Nora and I had neatly pocketed.

I focused my mind. Then, starting from the center of the ceiling, silver lines became visible.

Those were boundaries.

Lines that divided space from space and served as their standard.

'As expected, the barrier protecting the treasure vault was installed using the Eight Gate Boundary Formation.'

Dozens of lines came into view. There was no time to dismantle them all. Nor could I control all of them. My understanding of the Eight Gate Boundary Formation was too lacking for that. Even now, just perceiving the boundaries was making my eyes ache.

'One. I just need to handle one. Find the boundary connected to the outside.'

Finding it wasn't difficult.

There were surprisingly several boundaries connected to the outside. I pulled one of them toward me. With trembling hands, I fixed the boundary in front of me.

"Nora. Follow me. If you lose me, I'm not responsible."

I crossed the boundary before my eyes. My vision shifted in an instant. I was standing before a stone pagoda erected on a cliff. Nora walked out behind me.

"No joke. This is the spatial teleportation I've only heard about?!"

"...I merely used the barrier installed on the pagoda to move. I forcibly stitched the boundaries together. I was lucky. Lucky that I'd seen the Eight Gate Boundary Formation, and that the treasure vault was the barrier's center... If it had been outside, I couldn't have even attempted it."

"...I don't fully understand, but being able to use a barrier after seeing a grimoire once is absurd, isn't it? I've been called a genius since I was young, so I don't throw around the word talent lightly... Your talent is insane."

"I know."

"...Annoying."

Nora and I looked at the city below the pagoda. It wasn't that far away, so the city being smashed in real time was clearly visible.

The Wolfgang Mercenaries and hundreds of jiangshi were in battle. One section of the city was half-destroyed, as if it had been hit by a massive bomb.

At the center, a warrior in leather armor who appeared to be Wolfgang was facing off against a middle-aged man in a dark red qipao floating in midair.

The man's way of flying was peculiar. His black queue—his braided hair—was raised upward, spinning round and round like a helicopter. With a mustache and goatee, he stood with arms crossed, wearing a solemn expression.

"...A queue helicopter. No way...!"

"What. You know about that strange man?"

He was a boss monster that popped out with a rare probability in certain dungeons of the original game. Among players, he was counted as one of the Joat boss monsters.

"Hao Lich, Mr. Chue. The man who once ruled a region of True China."

"Hao Lich? A lich?"

"Yeah. A lich. He may look like that, but he's not a living being. We need to intervene before Wolfgang dies. If Wolfgang doesn't want to be buried in this dungeon, they'll cooperate."

"Is it that bad? With a Grade 7 like you, can't you handle him alone?"

"Mr. Chue is a dungeon boss. And the environment itself favors him."

"Even so, if you and I join forces, that's three Grade 7s. I may not specialize in combat, but I'm not weak."

"No. There's something else you need to do."

"What?"

"Liches store their life and soul separately in a life vessel. An ordinary lich carries it on their body... but I don't sense a life vessel on that one. He's probably hidden it somewhere else. While I buy time, you find it and destroy it. Finding treasure is your specialty, isn't it?"

Nora closed her eyes for a moment, then opened them. She grinned.

"I think I can find it. But it'll take some time. Will you be alright?"

"Take your time. ...Ah. Can I receive the payment in advance, by any chance? I might die, so I'd like to try using Avalonia at least once."

Nora swallowed a hollow laugh.

"...You said take your time? Well, fine. The request is almost finished anyway, and you don't seem like the type to stab me in the back."

Nora took Avalonia from her subspace and handed it to me.

A small glass orb containing a purple rose.

No.2 Avalonia.

A demon eye that even a mage cannot use without the aptitude.

I wasn't worried about the aptitude side.

'A demon eye is still magic aptitude in the end. Even if I'm strangely good at illusion-type magic, a demon eye should be different.'

Holding the demon eye, I placed it into my right eye—my dominant eye. Like falling into calm water, the demon eye entered my right eye.

My astral began reacting and connecting with the demon eye. It wasn't becoming one. A small organ was attaching itself beside my astral. A kind of auxiliary organ, perhaps.

'Feels like a small astral has formed. Does it help with spell calculations?'

I activated the demon eye immediately. Somehow it felt like turning on an auxiliary computer's power.

I could feel it.

Within my right pupil, a purple rose had bloomed in full.

By the time I'd adjusted to the demon eye and collected myself, Nora was no longer at my side. She'd left to do her own task.

"Now then. I should start with a greeting, since we've met."

A mage's style of hello-and-goodbye.

[Thunder Break]

A massive pillar of lightning crashed down on Mr. Chue, and at the same time the dark world blazed with pale lightning.

Immediately after, from within the pale lightning, a pitch-black lump of curse flew toward me.

An excellent hello-and-goodbye counter.

[Using Lightning Leap.]

With Lightning Leap, I crossed 600 meters in an instant and entered the city.

The lightning that lit the dungeon's world faded, and Hao Lich Mr. Chue, with his helicopter queue, glared at me from the air.

"I wondered what ill-mannered brat was making a ruckus... The sour stench of paocai clinging to that soul... One of Great Korea's ppangjjuppangjju, was it."

"Not paocai—kimchi. Jjangkkae jjangkkae."

"Hmph. Insolent wretch. I shall engrave the dignity of a great nation into your very bones. Abyoooo!!"

Mr. Chue pulled a pair of nunchaku from his sleeve and charged at me.
