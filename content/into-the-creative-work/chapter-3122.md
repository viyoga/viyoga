The Emperor's command had fallen.

The Holy Knight Order, Eagles, advanced into the undersea tunnel for the reconnaissance of Taiwan and the annihilation of the Arachne.

Ten knights clad in black power armor. At their vanguard, Choi Hye-jin stepped into the undersea tunnel without hesitation.

Of course, they weren't going in blindly. Running the tens of kilometers of undersea tunnel on foot posed problems of stamina and time. Beneath their feet were special transport skateboards. The board wheels rolled smoothly at speeds exceeding 200 kilometers per hour.

'To think this toy would come in handy here.'

The knights soon arrived at the site where the drone had self-destructed. A place littered with Arachne eggs and the corpse of an Arachne whose upper body had been shattered.

"Commander. Is this the Arachne? It just looks like a mutant."

"It's a mutant that evolved at the species level. Compared to pure mutants, it's weak. Most are Grade 1 mutants, I hear. Don't think of them as human—kill them all."

"Haha. Who would hesitate at an order? Even if they were human, if they're heretics, they all deserve to die. Long live His Imperial Majesty."

Choi Hye-jin and the knights disposed of the Arachne eggs without leaving a single one behind.

"Dealing with the eggs is such a pain in the ass... Can I just use a grenade?"

Choi Hye-jin looked at the knight who'd asked with a gaze dripping with contempt.

"If by some chance the undersea tunnel collapses, are you going to take responsibility?"

Honestly, even Choi Hye-jin found disposing of the eggs one by one tedious. If it were up to her heart, she'd love to sweep them all away with a grenade, but position makes the person. As the Knight Commander, she held back.

After dealing with the eggs, they began running through the tunnel again.

The path split into two forks.

Right and left.

Choi Hye-jin pondered for a moment.

"...Jena. Which way should we go?"

[The direction where Taiwan is located is to the left. The right path appears to lead toward China.]

"They dug a tunnel toward China too? Were they originally planning to attack China?"

[I cannot confirm that at present.]

The top priority was the reconnaissance of Taiwan. The knights moved down the left path. The closer they got to Taiwan, the more Arachne eggs there were. There were also adult Arachne managing the eggs.

"Knights of the Empire! Stop! Move and I shoot!"

An adult Arachne shouted, aiming its gun barrel.

The knights ignored the Arachne's words.

The Emperor had said that humans do not exchange words with insects. If the Emperor said so, that was truth itself.

Tat tat tat tat tat tat tat tat tat tat tang!

The Arachne unleashed its rifle. The bullets were larger in caliber than standard rifle rounds, but they failed to pierce the power armor and ricocheted away.

Choi Hye-jin approached the Arachne and swung a baseball bat designed for power armor.

Kwaaaaang!

The Arachne's corpse, its body burst open, flew back over 20 meters and rolled across the floor.

"Whew. That's a home run, isn't it?"

"Obviously."

The playful banter was brief. They destroyed the Arachne eggs and pressed forward.

Having killed hundreds of Arachne, they reached the end of the undersea tunnel. Choi Hye-jin kicked open the iron door blocking the exit without a moment's hesitation.

Bang!

The iron door flew off and was forced open. Beyond it, automated turrets aimed at the knights and spewed bullets.

The knights immediately fell back and dodged the bullets. There was an incline, so they didn't need to retreat far.

"Anyone bring a combat shield?"

Choi Hye-jin asked.

“…….”

There was no answer. Inside her helmet, Choi Hye-jin's expression crumpled instantly.

"You've got to be kidding me. Out of nine people, not a single one brought a shield? Are you all out of your minds?!"

"Commander, you didn't bring one either."

"I'm the commander, you little shit!"

Power armor is tough. When a tank and power armor fight, the power armor wins. However, even invincible power armor doesn't block 100% of impact. Even if the power armor withstands the shock, a portion of that shock transmits to the human inside.

Of course, the knights were enhanced humans, so they could endure most impacts and then some. Still, fighting while stupidly taking hits was an affront to a knight's pride and nothing but pointless stamina drain.

"Maknae, to the front."

"...I'm the shield?"

"Obviously, you little shit. You should've brought a shield. Why's the youngest brat holding dual blades?"

The Eagles Knight Order had free rein over their armament. Most preferred melee weapons, so Eagles knights who used guns were few even within the Order.

"Using drugs."

"Go ahead."

Drug use was also unrestricted. The maknae's eyes, having injected the drug, flashed white.

"Oh, yes. Long live His Imperial Majesty!!!!"

The maknae charged head-on and took the bullets. Behind him, the Eagles Knight Order followed and smashed the automated turrets in an instant.

The knights discovered the automated turret operators beyond the wall.

"Commander. They're not Arachne—they're human. Kill them?"

"They shot at us. They're the enemy, so kill them. They're obviously in bed with those spider bastards anyway. You're calling that a question?"

"Actually, I already killed them."

"Have you lost your mind."

It took less than an hour to finish cleanup. The knights gathered in one place.

"S! S! Yes!"

Except for the maknae, high on drugs, pointlessly slashing at the wrecked automated turrets with his blade.

"That brat looks like he cranked the drug concentration way up on his own... Quit your bullshit and get over here."

"Yes. Commander!"

The maknae ran over immediately and snapped to attention.

Even if high on drugs, a knight is a knight. Knights who held fanatical devotion to the Emperor in their hearts wouldn't lose their senses easily even when drugged.

"I'll say this once, so listen carefully. We scatter and reconnoiter Taiwan. First is finding out the current situation in Taiwan. Second is finding the mutant called the Arachne Queen or whatever it is. If you see an Arachne in the process, kill it. Don't talk to the bugs. It just wastes time. If any Taiwanese are cooperating with the Arachne, they've betrayed humanity—kill them."

"Understood. How do we handle supplies?"

"Figure it out locally."

"What if the locals won't cooperate?"

"Kill them. Refusing to cooperate with us means they don't follow His Imperial Majesty."

"Understood. For His Imperial Majesty."

"Yeah. Yeah. For His Imperial Majesty. Have you checked comms?"

"...Hm? Comms aren't working. Jena isn't responding either."

Power armor assists the wearer with artificial intelligence. In the Empire, the strong AI Jena was famous, but there were also an enormous number of lesser AIs that couldn't match Jena's performance. Since the power armor itself had a built-in AI, it functioned without issue even when communications were cut.

"They said intel on Taiwan was extremely scarce. Looks like they have signal-jamming tech. Any other problems?"

"None. Weapons are also operational."

"Operation begins. See you after we're dead."

The ten knights scattered.

Along the way, Taiwanese forces rushed in to block them, but they couldn't stop the knights executing individual combat. They never fought head-on to begin with. They avoided frontal engagements with low odds and fought only when the advantage was theirs. That was tactics.

Choi Hye-jin moved at a leisurely pace. Her assigned reconnaissance zone was Taipei City. The north of the island of Taiwan, and its capital. In other words, it was where Choi Hye-jin currently stood.

She used her jetpack to leave the military zone and flew toward the city where buildings were visible.

"Not much different from Seoul. Ah, the streets being empty is the difference."

The city streets were no different from a ghost town. Here and there on the streets, flattened corpses and blood were visible. There were cars too, but their hoods were open, or they were little more than empty husks stripped of fuel and engines.

With sea routes blocked, the value of fuel was needless to say on par with gold. No—compared to useless gold, its value was clearly greater.

Fortunately, the Empire had oil rats brought over from America. Not stopping there, all of the Empire's systems were being converted to electricity-centered rather than oil. Even the resource that powered power armor was electricity.

Walking the city streets alone, Choi Hye-jin felt gazes from all around.

'So they're watching me from inside the buildings.'

She smashed through a building wall and went inside to confirm.

"Kyaaaaaaak!"

A woman screaming, and five or six other humans in shabby clothes, trembling with terror. They weren't simply afraid of Choi Hye-jin's intrusion.

Their gazes were fixed on the ceiling. The moment Choi Hye-jin's head tilted upward, an Arachne soldier clinging to the ceiling dropped and ambushed her.

Choi Hye-jin reacted calmly. Instead of dodging, she threw her bat upward. The Arachne struck by the bat had its head burst open. Thud! The Arachne's massive body fell to the floor, spilling blood.

Choi Hye-jin approached the trembling Taiwanese.

"I am Choi Hye-jin, Knight Commander of the Eagles of the Holy Great Empire. What are you? Why are you cowering here?"

Her words were automatically translated by the AI built into the power armor and broadcast outside the helmet.

"W-we were caught and locked up by that monster. When it gets hungry, it grabs and eats us. Please, save us...!"

"That's not something I can decide. The Arachne Queen. Do you know where the boss of those monsters is?"

"I-I've heard of it. The Queen has set up in the underground of the city center—I overheard those monsters talking on the phone...!"

"Bugs use smartphones too? Well, if they have human-level intelligence, I suppose it's natural."

Their upper bodies were human. On top of that, they used guns like humans. It wasn't strange that they'd use smartphones too.

"Do you know how many of them there are?"

"Th-there are many. They're spread all across Taiwan, just not visible. The military can't even handle them!"

Taiwan's core strengths were its air force and navy. The navy was finished when the seas turned to chaos, just like Japan. The army was weak, and since the Arachne mainly operated underground, the air force couldn't bring its power to bear. After all, they couldn't bomb a city full of their own citizens because of the Arachne.

"Do you know exactly where the city center is? This is my first time in this city."

"It's over there. Go straight down that road and turn right, then..."

"Wait."

Choi Hye-jin drew the Taipei map displayed on her screen onto the floor. The paint was the warm blood of the Arachne she'd just killed.

It was a crooked map, but roughly legible. The woman pointed at a spot on the map with a trembling finger.

"H-here."

The power armor's AI confirmed the location and set a route.

"Okay. Thank you. Here's a gift."

Choi Hye-jin tossed the woman a chocolate bar she'd secretly brought along, then moved toward the city center along the set route.

'The Arachne have probably only just realized we've infiltrated.'

Thanks to the Emperor's swift decision, the Arachne lacked time to respond.

'Let's take the Queen's head before the armed ones gather in one place. Now is optimal. I can feel it in my gut. He told me to trust my gut too.'

As a rule, when the king dies, the underlings are thrown into chaos.

Arriving at the city center, Choi Hye-jin swung her bat down toward the road surface.

Kwaaaaaaaaaang!

With a thunderous roar, the road collapsed and her body dropped sharply downward.

Landing on the floor below, she could see a 5-meter Arachne Queen and ten 3-meter Arachne royal guards protecting it.

"It's been only minutes since I received word that Imperial knights had infiltrated... To think you'd already make it this far. Nesting in the capital center for symbolism and infrastructure was a mistake. Knight of the Empire. I wish to speak with the Emperor."

“…….”

Instead of answering, Choi Hye-jin gripped her white-glowing gallium baseball bat tight.
