Jjalang.

I heard the bell signaling the start of the sparring match.

"......."

I took a breath and tensed my body. Red sparks flew from the Hwaryeon Bido in my hand.

My opponent, Amatsuka Koyori, grips her spear with both hands, assuming a stance. Just like yesterday, and today, she'll likely take a defensive approach.

'Since this sparring match is ultimately for show... I'll have to drag it out for at least 5 minutes.'

What I need to aim for is Koyori's carelessness. Given the significant difference in skill between us, she'll become complacent without even realizing it.

"......."

As I stood still, Koyori eventually began to move first. She swung her spear at me. Blades of wind flew towards me.

I stepped using footwork. The wind blades that grazed past me made my bangs flutter. I approached Koyori without stopping.

Kaang! Chaeng!

Sword and spear collide. The sword is pushed back slightly, and red sparks and embers flew intensely. This confirmed it.

'This woman is holding back right now. Just as I expected, she's going to drag out the sparring match.'

She's an A-rank, called an S-rank candidate. Putting aside technique or talent, she overwhelms me from the start with her physical abilities. The reality is that if she pushes me with her physical abilities, I'll be helpless.

'It's a bit annoying, but... it's good for me. Let your guard down! The moment you do, I'll land a solid hit!'

Kaang!

Koyori put strength into her spear and pushed me away. Even as I was pushed back, I didn't take my eyes off her.

A red pentagram was drawn on the spear blade, and then flames appeared. The red flames wrapped around the spear blade and blazed.

'It's different from yesterday's sparring match. But there's no need to panic. I already know that Koyori can use Yin-Yang magic.'

It seems she's going to approach the battle in a different way than yesterday. The reason is easy to guess. She probably wants to let the audience know that yesterday's battle wasn't her full power.

'Manroe (卍 Thunder).'

I reached out and used Manroe. Lightning shot from the center of the spinning thunder in the shape of a Man (卍). Koyori didn't dodge.

"Heup!"

The flame-covered spear split the lightning.

'As expected, it doesn't work when used head-on.'

This attack wasn't entirely meaningless. I was able to see that she didn't dodge even though she could have, and instead, she blocked it.

'Koyori thinks this is more of a show than a sparring match. She's going to move more flamboyantly. She probably heard something from that politician, Shigeru.'

The possibilities are increasing. I smiled pleasantly and rushed towards Koyori.

Chaeng!

Spear and sword collided once again. Red current flowed along the spear and into Koyori, but it disappeared into the air without having any effect due to Koyori's mana.

On the other hand, her flames only made me feel heat and weren't dangerous.

'She's intentionally showing openings.'

She's inducing me to attack there. It hasn't even been a minute since the battle started. I poked at the opening to see her reaction.

Koyori's spear moved mysteriously and deflected my sword. The sight of the scattering flames mesmerized people's eyes.

'Was she trying to show this?'

She shows another opening again. She's telling me to attack there. I pretended to attack that spot and used Instant.

Her movements slowed down. I could see where the spear was moving, where her feet were stepping, and where her gaze was.

'I can't just keep getting hit...!'

Yeongcheon Style Lightning Flash.

I used the fastest attack in the Yeongcheon Style. Pajijik! Red current flashed in the trajectory of the sword.

"......!"

Koyori, startled, stepped using footwork. Sseuseuseut. Her body moved backwards. My blade barely grazed her shoulder. Her clothes were slightly torn.

I frowned as I looked at her.

'She cleverly hid her feet with her long red skirt. Is it like a moonwalk? It didn't look like she was moving backwards on the surface....'

Koyori approached me again. She became more serious than before. I just missed a golden opportunity to land a hit on her.

Kang! Chaeng! Chaeaeng!

The attacks are fiercer than before. As time passes, only minor wounds on my body are increasing.

'What is it?'

As I continued the battle, I felt a sense of incongruity. It wasn't that Koyori's attacks were wrong. It was a problem I myself was feeling.

'It's not that she's fast. I can follow her with my eyes, and I can react too. But why is it so hard to block or dodge?'

This was a problem I had been feeling for a while.

I gradually increased the speed at which I swung my sword. I thought she would also increase her speed to match my attack speed, but I was wrong. She didn't increase her speed and blocked all of my attacks. The hand holding the sword tingled.

"......Keueut."

Koyori's movements as she gripped and swung the naginata were like a dance. She rotated her body, adding weight to the spear. The long red skirt resembled flower petals fluttering in the wind.

'...Instant!'

I lowered my body deeply and aimed for the lower body. The spear blade dropped down and struck me. My body flew backwards, rolled on the floor a few times, and then stood up.

Koyori takes her stance again, but I didn't rush in.

A smile was drawn on my lips.

'I get it...!'

Something I had been agonizing over for over 100 days was finally resolved. Originally, this shouldn't have been such a drawn-out problem. If it were Yuria, she probably would have realized it in less than a day.

However, rather than the patheticness of my own lack of talent, the joy of finally realizing it dominated my whole body.

'What's more important than speed is flow!'

A continuous flow is in itself a unity of offense and defense. Koyori's martial art, which is like dancing, is exactly like that.

"......Why did you suddenly stop? Are you perhaps giving up?"

"No way. There's no way. If I was going to give up here, I wouldn't have stepped up in the first place."

I gripped the sword hilt again. Strength entered my hand.

'When I think about it, I was too cautious, aiming for Koyori's carelessness. That's not my fighting style.'

Let's die today.

No, let's go to the brink of death today. After all, I have Complete Recovery. The aftermath? I wanted to give it my all at this moment.

'Let's go.'

Bluish Sword energy appeared on the red blade. The unkempt Sword energy is sharper than usual.

Pajijijik.

Red lightning wrapped around the Sword energy. I created even more lightning. Dozens of streaks of lightning wriggled in the water that was Sword energy.

Yeongcheon Style Thunderclap Step.

With the sound of thunder, I rushed towards Koyori. Koyori narrowed her eyes and moved her shoulder diagonally to avoid the stab. Before I knew it, blue Sword energy was rippling on her spear blade as well.

I didn't stop attacking.

'Entrust myself to the flow, but I have to control that flow.'

Several attacks were exchanged. Bewilderment settled on Koyori's face. She realized that my movements had suddenly changed.

Yeongcheon Style Cold Lightning.

Blue Sword energy and Red thunder spread in all directions. Koyori held her spear upright and created wind. However, the Sword energy and Red thunder tore through the wind and injured her body.

Although I didn't inflict deep wounds, I made drops of blood splatter. I subtly smeared the falling drops of blood on my clothes.

'Alright! This achieves my goal!'

I carefully remembered the hem of my clothes stained with Koyori's blood.

Buuwoong.

"......?!"

A huge wind blew, and my body floated upwards. My body couldn't resist the wind and began to rotate. Koyori created a whirlwind with Yin-Yang magic.

My body, floating and rotating in the air, flew out of the sparring arena.

'It's okay. There's no rule that says you lose if you go outside the sparring arena.'

Outside the sparring arena, water flowed down from the waterfall, and outside of that, there was a barrier, making it impossible to get out.

I landed on the flowing water. Thanks to my skill [Blessing of Water Lv.1]'s [Water Surface Walking].

I could see Koyori making a surprised expression. She glared at me and threw the spear in her hand at me. The spear wrapped in flames flew towards me.

"Heueueup!"

I grabbed the sword hilt with both hands and swung it upwards. The trajectory of the spear changed, went up, hit the barrier ceiling, and fell down.

Pungdeong!

I hurriedly took out the spear that had fallen into the water. The flames that had been wrapped around it disappeared along with the water vapor when it fell into the water.

I gripped the spear in my right hand and took a javelin throwing stance.

Pajijijijijik!

Blue lightning swirled around the spear.

Ppudeudeudeuk!

At the moment when my bones and muscles expanded to their limit, I threw the Lightning Spear towards Koyori.

Koyori quickly formed hand seals with both hands. Since I didn't have any knowledge of Yin-Yang magic, I couldn't predict what kind of Yin-Yang magic she was using.

A huge stone wall soared from the ground in front of the thrown Lightning Spear. However, it was not enough to block the Lightning Spear. The Lightning Spear smashed the stone wall and headed towards Koyori.

A small tornado appeared in front of Koyori. The Lightning Spear, weakened by breaking the stone wall, was swept away by the tornado, rose into the air, and fell to the ground.

Koyori forms hand seals again.

Three pieces of paper flew toward me. The papers soon took the shape of people. Instead of eyes, a nose, and a mouth on their faces, the character 'Shik (式)' was written.

'Is it a Shikigami of Yin-Yang magic!'

One of them punched me with his fist, and another cut me with a sword in his left hand. The remaining one held the sword in reverse and slashed upwards from below. The Shikigami, with their terrible durability, exploded with smoke and turned into paper, falling down.

'Damn it. The next attack is coming!'

Ten arrows made of blue flames were floating above her head. The arrows flew towards me sequentially.

I ran across the water, trying to avoid the arrows. However, the arrows persistently chased me.

'Let's see if they'll chase me into the water!'

When I released Water Surface Walking, my body fell into the water. With [Blessing of Water Lv.1], I could breathe in the water and move relatively freely in the water.

The fire arrows followed me into the water, but disappeared along with the water vapor.

I swam in the water.

'It should be around here...!'

I raised my body from the water. Koyori was at a distance of less than 10M.

Yeongcheon Style Flying Tiger.

I pounced fiercely towards her.

Koyori didn't react. I sensed the organized mana emanating from her surroundings.

'It's a trap!'

It was already too late when I realized it.

A tremendous updraft occurred, blowing my body upwards. I was instantly slammed into the barrier ceiling.

"Keoeok!"

I gritted my teeth at the pain that felt like my whole body was being crushed. The updraft soon disappeared, and I kicked off the barrier ceiling and used Water Surface Walking to rise above the waterfall.

Sswaaaaaaa!

Water falls down.

I looked at Koyori below from above the waterfall. Koyori's face remained serious. When she reached out her hand, the spear flew to her.

Koyori threw the spear. Its destination was not me, but the bottom of the waterfall.

Jjeojeojeojeok!

Centered on the spear, the waterfall slowly began to freeze from the bottom up.

I gripped the sword hilt with both hands and pointed the sword tip at Koyori.

Pajijijik.

Red lightning arose one by one.

Koyori forms hand seals. Shikigami appeared around her. The pure white Shikigami in the form of eagles began to multiply.

'I can't hold out any longer. Whatever the result, I'll end it here.'

Pajijijijijik!

I drew out mana and created Red thunder. The Red thunder began to envelop not only Hwaryeon Bido, but also my body.

The sparring arena was filled with thousands of white eagle Shikigami. The fluttering sounds were threatening.

Koyori is hidden by the Shikigami and cannot be seen, but her presence is definitely felt.

'She probably won't avoid it until the end.'

The white eagles all looked at me and flapped their wings.

Jjeojeojeok!

When the cold air freezing the waterfall reached my feet, I became a red lightning bolt with the thunder of Thunderclap Step and jumped.

Yeongcheon Style Falling Lightning.

Pungpungpungpungpung! Thousands of white eagles touched by the red lightning turned into paper. The paper burned and scattered into the air as ashes.

Koyori reached out her hand. Space distortion unfolds. I tried to break through the space distortion with force, but it was not enough. I was bounced to the side and rolled on the floor.

'......I don't even have the strength to get up, and my body is throbbing and aching.'

Koyori walked over, step by step. She was already holding the spear and pointing it at me.

"...I lost."

"You worked hard."

The sparring match is over.
