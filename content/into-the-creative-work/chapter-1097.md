Lexy, who had been looking at me with a strange expression, quickly composed herself and asked.

,Why did you lower your gun? You still have a chance to shoot. Three times, in fact."

Even if I don't hit the target, she'll let me try up to three times.

,It's meaningless. The target is out of my sight, I can't see it."

To be honest, I think it's a miracle that I hit the 850M target. The 850M target looked smaller than a grain of millet. Fortunately, the shooting range was on a flat ground without obstacles and the weather was good, so I could see the target well. It would be impossible to hit an 850M target with the naked eye in actual combat.

,Well, it's hard to hit something you can't see. But what if you could see it?"

,If I could see it, I would hit it, and so would anyone else. It's easy to hit a stationary target."

,It seems like you find shooting easy. This... makes me even more interested in you."

,That was my limit just now."

Lexy approached me. She stood in front of me. I made a troubled expression. She was an instructor, and I was a slave and a test subject. If I ignored her and walked past, there would be a backlash.

,You can hit it as long as you can see it?"

,Yes."

I answered without hesitation, with conviction. Since entering the game, shooting felt very easy, as if I had gained some kind of ability.

,You can even use Telescope magic."

,...I've never heard of that magic before."

,Oh, right. I forgot you're a newbie. It's a 2nd-tier magic. It's an enhancement-type magic that improves your eyesight."

Lexy reached out her hand towards my head. I flinched for a moment, but quickly realized that she had no intention of harming me and relaxed my guard slightly.

Her mana is moving. I can feel the spell formula. The mana flows into my body. I felt discomfort from the foreign mana. I endured it because of the situation.

,...Judging by the situation, this must be Telescope magic. Let's memorize the spell formula."

2nd-tier magic. Feeling the spell formula, I can definitely tell that it's more difficult than 1st-tier magic.

,If I had five mana nodes... I think I could use it even if the casting time is a bit long?"

The spell formula gathers in my eyes. The spell formula permeated into my eyes. It's a little different from magic like Light or Fire.

,So this is body enhancement magic."

Suddenly, a new idea pops up. If I put the Light Scope spell formula on top of the Telescope spell formula, wouldn't light come out of my eyes? Of course, that was just my idea.

,If I try it and go blind, it'll be an irreversible, idiotic thing to do."

Lexy took her hand off my head. When I focus my eyes, my eyesight improves. Lexy's face feels much bigger. I could even see the small pores on her skin. Conversely, when I release my focus, my eyesight returns to normal.

,It's Telescope magic. It will last for an hour. Now, pick up the gun and check the target."

I followed her instructions.

I could see the target 900M away. The steel target stood as if it had never been hit before.

,Can you hit it?"

I answered without hesitation to Lexy's expectant voice.

,Yes."

,Then pull the trigger."

I pulled the trigger as she wanted.

Tang.

The gunshot, which I had become somewhat accustomed to, rang out and the bullet flew. The moment I thought it had flown for about 1 second, it hit the center of the target. The target moves backward. I checked the electronic display next to the shooting range. The number that was written as 900M changed to 1,000M. This is the distance to the target.

,Can you see it?"

,Yes."

,Can you do it?"

,Yes."

,Show me your true limit. If your limit really impresses me... I'll give you a reward."

,...What kind of reward are you talking about?"

,Well. There could be many things, right?"

Instructor Lexy's rank is Staff Sergeant. How far does her authority reach? I don't know, but it probably isn't very high. I focused while dismissing my expectations. I was also curious about where my limit was.

,I need to know my limits to grow."

I pulled the trigger.

An unfailing hit.

I hit targets beyond 2,000M, even up to 3,000M away. From 2,500M, I couldn't hit the center of the target, but I still hit the target anyway.

The target didn't move backward. The set limit distance was 3,000M.

,Don't lower your gun. I haven't seen your limit yet."

Lexy went to the control seat and operated a button. A new target appeared 100M away. The target moves as if to tease me. There seemed to be regularity in the movement, but there wasn't. The moving speed is about the level of a person walking.

,Start shooting."

My index finger moved. The gun muzzle spat fire. The bullet flew and hit the moving target.

,Feels good. I knew you'd hit it."

Her exclamation didn't reach my ears. I was currently extremely focused on the target that had moved backward.

I check the target. I pull the trigger. I hit the target. I check the target. I pull the trigger. I hit the target.

The machine-like cycle began to creak when the target was 1,500M away. The bullet missed and hit the ground.

,You still have two chances left. Take a deep breath and try again."

As Lexy said, I still have chances left. I calmed my mind with a deep breath and increased my concentration again.

Taang.

The target moved backward.

And I lost all my chances at 1,800M. Feeling disappointed, I lowered my gun. Lexy smiled and approached me, then hugged me. Even though my mind was an adult, my body was still in a growth phase, so I was shorter than her. My face was buried in her ample chest.

,Newbie! You're a real genius! I never thought I'd find such a genius here! If you receive professional training... you might become the best sniper in the Freisel Kingdom!"

,......"

I quietly felt Lexy's chest. Opportunities like this are rare. She let me go from her embrace about 5 seconds later.

,You can look forward to the reward."

Lexy said with a grin. Whatever it was, it was a good thing to have gained the favor of an instructor.

After that, Lexy ordered me to do free shooting and watched the other kids shoot. She corrected their postures and taught them how to shoot. Except for Number 31, everyone was about the same.

Near the end of class. My record increased slightly to 1,820M.

I received a chocolate bar from Lexy. I was feeling a bit bored, so I ate it right away. Everyone looked at me with envious eyes. Objectively, it was a normal taste, but subjectively, it was fantastic.

•••

I had dinner in the cafeteria and returned to the barracks. Personal maintenance time was given. It was a honey-like free time. The way we spent our free time was similar. Most of us study with magic books.

We are not soldiers, but slaves. If we don't prove our usefulness here, we will be disposed of. Disposal meant death.

I also took out a magic book. I opened the page I had been looking at in the morning. The page contained the Ice magic spell formula.

As I was trying to focus on the magic book, Number 212 next to me spoke to me.

,Number 211. You were really amazing today. It was like you became a completely different person after experiencing mana backflow. Especially the shooting... you even broke Number 31's record. What's your secret?"

,Secret? ...Maybe it's because of the mana backflow that Instructor Vinus-nim mentioned."

,So that's why? Unconscious enlightenment? Ah. I wonder if I can gain enlightenment if I suffer from mana backflow too?"

,Maybe, I don't really know exactly what kind of enlightenment it is either."

I vaguely glossed over it. I'm also dumbfounded when it comes to shooting or magic. It's not just a simple matter of talent blooming, there's something fishy about it.

,Is it some kind of perk given to me when I entered the game?"

Number 212 treated me with acting that was a little more familiar than yesterday. It seems like she's thinking of picking up some crumbs from me. Unfortunately, there's nothing I can do for her. She and I are both in the same situation.

I took a shower in the bathroom 1 hour before the evening roll call. The men's and women's baths were separated. Even though we are slaves and test subjects, we will become Battle Mages later, so we are treated quite well.

,Even among guinea pigs, there are expensive ones and cheap ones. We're only treated this well because we're on the more expensive side."

Other countries also do human experimentation to some extent.

,Among them, there are places that treat slaves like insects below livestock. I'm lucky that this isn't one of those places."

That doesn't mean I like this life at all. I will escape slavery and regain my freedom.

•••

It was time for martial arts.

The martial arts instructor was a young man.

Instructor Belhaga. He had a sturdy physique, and his face, hands, and arms were covered with large and small scars. He is openly watching me.

,Obviously. Instructor Lexy must have told him about me."

I wasn't used to this kind of attention, so it was burdensome. Of course, that doesn't mean it was bad.

,I mustn't forget my situation. I can be disposed of at any time. So I have to prove my abilities."

That doesn't mean I'm going to show off everything I have. I'm going to escape this place someday. The hidden capital will definitely be helpful.

,I taught you the basics of martial arts last time... so today we'll do sparring. Number 31. Number 211. Come forward."

Instructor Belhaga said with a smirk.

He designated me as Number 31's opponent. I don't know what he heard from Instructor Lexy, but Belhaga seemed to be overestimating me.

I couldn't go against the instructor, so I went forward. Number 31 approached from the front. Purple hair that swayed with every step and cool, sunken red eyes. Even her appearance was far from ordinary.

,The format is free sparring. The use of mana is prohibited. Targeting the eyes and genitals is also prohibited. It is also forbidden to bite the opponent using teeth."

I clenched my fists while listening to Instructor Belhaga's words.

My talent for magic and shooting was surprising even to myself, but martial arts were different. I was confident in martial arts. Before entering the game, I was an F-rank Hunter. Even though my rank was the lowest, I was not an ordinary person. I also had experience fighting monsters. On the other hand, my opponent is still a greenhorn without proper experience.

,Start."

Instructor Belhaga said.

Number 31 rushed in without any probing.

,I can see her movements."

Since I can see her movements, I just need to maintain my composure and respond.

Peok!

,......!!"

I was hit in the solar plexus and stepped back. I was breathless and my legs were shaking. The reaction of my body was secondary, my mind was very embarrassed.

,I definitely saw her fist. I even raised my fist to land a counter. But why did I get hit?"

Number 31's fist aims for my face. I saw it this time too. I raised my palm. I was planning to grab her wrist and perform a reverse shoulder throw.

Peoeok.

But this time too, I was hit by Number 31.

,......Damn it."

I realized what the problem was.

It was a simple problem.

My body couldn't keep up with my consciousness. Even if I tried to block the opponent's body, the reaction of my hands and legs was one beat... no, about two beats late.

The degree of physical training doesn't matter. Number 31 and I have almost the same physical abilities, and Number 211, a slave and test subject, has had a better than average body since long ago due to good food and regular exercise.

,It's the complete opposite of shooting. I have absolutely no talent for martial arts."

A fist is flying.

I know it's coming, but I couldn't avoid it.

Pak! Peok peok!

I thought while getting beaten up.

,If only I could use magic... I think the result would have been different."

It was a sparring where I couldn't even use mana, let alone magic.

,Stop."

It was stopped by Instructor Belhaga. Number 31, who had been mercilessly throwing punches and kicks, stopped immediately and distanced herself. I was beaten up throughout the sparring and it ended. My body ached all over.

,It seems like you can't do martial arts. Number 211. You lack the basics. Go hit the sandbag and train your fists."

,...Yes. Instructor-nim."

,Number 31. I know you didn't even warm up properly. Spar with Number 37."

While others improve their skills through sparring, I have become someone who has to hit a sandbag. I don't feel wronged. Rather, I think it's fortunate.

,At least I'm not being punished. Is it because I'm good at shooting that they're letting me off this much?"

I threw a punch at the sandbag. At first, I hit the sandbag with enthusiasm. However, as time went on, I only realized that I had no talent for fighting. Later, I was half-heartedly swinging my fists and thinking about magic in my head.
