An elder of the Namgung Clan, the Little Demon King of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult, and a general bestowed the name Lightning General by the Emperor. That's me.

Judging by the positions I hold, one body wouldn't be enough, but... surprisingly, I have plenty of free time.

,This isn't just any ordinary martial arts world; it's mixed with a Xianxia world. The underlings handle most of the work. The higher your position, the more you can focus on your own training.'

Unless there's something special, I don't have to step up directly.

It's the same with being a general. I wiped out all the nearby monsters last time, so there's nothing to do. Low-level monsters that occasionally appear? My subordinates take care of them. That's what the troops are there for in the first place.

,I only need to step in when monsters appear that my subordinates can't handle.'

That's why I was able to seclude myself for several years.

I went into secluded training to coincide with Milyeong-ssi entering secluded training. For the first few days, I focused on training, but then I got bored and clicked on auto-progress.

After watching about three short videos in the real world, 10 years had passed. The problem was that my level was still the same.

,Still at the eighth stage of the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown. Nothing much has changed from 10 years ago. Is this the limit of auto-progress? But there's no way I can seclude myself for 10 years in person.'

That was boring.

I didn't come to this world just to train. Auto-progress secluded training wasn't entirely meaningless. My physical abilities improved slightly. Though it doesn't seem like it will be of significant help in raising my level.

"Oh, you're out, Seobang-nim!"

Milyeong-ssi finished her secluded training before I did.

"I did train... but I didn't really gain anything. How about you?"

"I barely managed to reach the fourth stage of Harmony a while ago. It's thanks to the elixirs Seobang-nim gave me. I ate all sorts of elixirs, researched them as techniques... and luckily found a clue."

"You've been in secluded training for 10 years, are you mentally okay?"

"Huh? Ah. It's only been 10 years. What's the big deal? I'm quite used to secluded training. I've only been training since I was young. And it's not like I was completely secluded; I came out every few months."

I quietly repeated her words. Only 10 years. Unlike me, I'm reminded that she's from this world. The sense of time for cultivators who have entered martial arts or magic, unlike ordinary humans, is extraordinary.

"What about Yeoryeo and Soha?"

"They headed to the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult. That's what you had already agreed to do with them, Seobang-nim."

"I decided to have them join the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult when they reached the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown stage. A master of the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown stage is someone even the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult can't ignore, and it will be helpful when I take control of the Heavenly Demon Divine Cult as my subordinate later."

What's surprising is that they reached the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown stage in just 10 years. They both had talent, but I didn't expect this much.

I can still understand Yu Yeoryeo. She was at the tail end of the Five Viscera stage before I went into secluded training. But Danmok Soha was at the early stage of the Five Viscera. She was a genius, but not to this extent.

Milyeong-ssi answered my question.

"There are several factors. First of all, I provided elixirs generously."

"Thanks to the Cheon Gang Seong system, there are almost always elixirs left over."

"Hongye-eonni is also excellent as an alchemist. Maybe it's because Yeoryeo learned poison techniques, but she handles elixirs well and learned alchemy from Hongye-eonni."

"Yeoryeo hit the jackpot."

From Yu Yeoryeo's perspective, it was a stroke of luck. Alchemy is several times more valuable than poison techniques. That's why proper alchemists and artisans are highly favored even if their level is low.

"Wiyu-nim's help was also there. She's not stingy with her teachings, is she?"

I admit it. Wiyu-nim gave teachings even to those who weren't her disciples. Of course, she doesn't pass on her martial arts to those who aren't her disciples. She just gives light teachings in passing.

For those receiving her short teachings, it must feel like finding an oasis in the desert. Wiyu-nim is an excellent teacher.

"Yeoryeo aside, what about Soha? Soha's level was at the early stage of the Five Viscera."

"Talent is talent, but... the magic Seobang-nim cast on them. The Biikyeonri seems to have had an effect."

"The effect of Biikyeonri is sharing senses in a community of fate... Ah. Is it because of the sense sharing? Soha is sharing the senses of the high-level Yeoryeo, learning the senses of a master."

It can have a positive impact on training.

Isn't this quite a revolutionary magic?

I was tempted for a moment, but immediately shook my head. The disadvantages were more serious than the advantages.

,They're in a community of fate, and they suffer if they're more than a hundred miles apart. What if the lower-level one suddenly dies during training due to a mistake? The higher-level one also dies.'

Even if they're master and disciple, they wouldn't want to be in a community of fate. No, they can't be in a community of fate because they're master and disciple.

,A high-level magician might be able to undo the magic... but such magicians are rare. And it's not like high-level magicians don't make mistakes.'

Anyway, it was good for me that they reached that level.

I had various conversations with Milyeong-ssi and then parted ways.

The place I'm going is Yang Hongye's smithy.

The smithy, which should be emitting heat and the sound of hammers, was very quiet even when I got close.

Squeak.

When I opened the door and went inside, Yang Hongye was meditating in a lotus position. She quietly breathed in the spiritual energy of heaven and earth.

I looked at the spear in the center of the smithy. A long spear with a large, overall black blade.

I knew it as soon as I saw it. It's Stormbreaker. Even though the appearance has changed, the unique feel of Stormbreaker was still there.

I reached out to grab it, but an invisible barrier blocked my hand. I was so engrossed in Stormbreaker that I belatedly realized the existence of the barrier.

The barrier itself wasn't that great. I could tear it apart without much difficulty if I gave it my all.

"Stop. It's not finished yet."

Yang Hongye said.

"Not finished? It looks finished to me?"

"That's because you don't have an eye for it. If you want to use it in an unfinished state, go ahead. The moment you take it out of that barrier, you can't undo it."

I've endured until now. I can't ruin it at the end. I lowered my hand.

I belatedly looked closely at Yang Hongye and was surprised. Her level was at the tenth stage of the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown. She was definitely lower than me 10 years ago. Of course, just because her level is higher than mine doesn't mean she's stronger. She's an artisan.

"Did you gain some kind of enlightenment in the last 10 years?"

"This is one of the greatest masterpieces of my life. I received inspiration just from melting it with fire and hammering it. It's not wrong to say I gained enlightenment."

"Just as a martial artist trains in martial arts to raise their level, and a magician studies magic to raise their level, an artisan raises their level by creating magical artifacts."

"You know it well."

"What's the reason it's unfinished? Did you intentionally not finish it?"

"There's no way I would do that. I have failures, but I don't have unfinished works. It's because it's missing one thing to complete it. Just one thing."

"What's missing?"

"I don't know. You're the one who has to bring it. You're the owner of this magical artifact. The last thing you choose will determine the direction of this magical artifact."

Wasn't there a similar situation? When Hephaestus strengthened Hwaryeon Bido in the [God's Atlantis] world.

I rummaged through my inventory and took out an item.

Black Diamond. The legacy of the dark spirit I obtained recently.

"How about this?"

Her eyes lit up.

"An interesting item. But it's too low-grade to use as a material."

Next, I took out the heart of the Milky Way Heavenly Dragon. She, who had dragon blood mixed in her, exclaimed.

"A dragon's heart? It's not as good as a dragon pearl, but it's a great item. But this isn't it. It can't be the last one. It doesn't suit it."

I took out various things. They were rejected because they didn't match the grade or didn't suit it.

"I know you bring strange things from other worlds. But it's not like I can immediately grasp the items and use them skillfully. There are some items that I can't even understand with my own eyes. If possible, bring an item from this world. If possible, a magical artifact would be good. Magical artifacts are what I can handle best."

It made sense, so I immediately understood.

"I'm not satisfied with just any magical artifact. Do you know of any good magical artifacts?"

"What I know? I know a few. There's even a magical artifact that might suit it."

"What is it?"

"The Four Fiends. Have you heard of them?"

"Of course, I know them."

The Four Fiends are famous monsters from Chinese legends even in reality.

And in this world, they are monsters that existed in the past. Of course, they are a bit different from the Four Fiends known in reality.

"They're the great monsters that the current Emperor personally subjugated for the sake of the stability of the world. It's been so long ago that there's no trace of them now, right?"

Gunggi (窮奇), Docheol (饕餮), Dool (檮杌), Hondon (混沌). Among these four, the Emperor only recovered the bodies of Docheol and Dool. Gunggi fell into the sea while fleeing and vanished, and Hondon was ripped apart by the Emperor's hands and scattered. The Emperor tried to recover Hondon's body and soul, but the monsters had taken more than half of it, so he couldn’t retrieve it.

"I didn't know that much in detail. So?"

The monsters are said to have created a magical artifact using Hondon's corpse and soul: the Chaos Greed Wolf Sword (混沌貪狼劍). It is a magical artifact that grows stronger with each enemy it kills. They say that only Hondon's descendants can wield it. In my opinion, it's the magical artifact that fits Stormbreaker best. The problem is, I've only heard rumors, so I don't have detailed information.

"Hmm. I'll have to look into it."

"It's just that I think it's a magical artifact that suits Stormbreaker. It doesn't have to be the Chaos Greed Wolf Sword."

I smiled.

"No. I've decided on the Chaos Greed Wolf Sword."
