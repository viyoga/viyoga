"What's your plan?"

I asked Lee Yeonhee.

"The key is the coin acquisition rate. Countries with good relations with Korea see their coin acquisition rate increase. We just need to let them know this fact."

"How?"

"We sell modern weapons in the Tower. If people can kill monsters with just the twitch of a finger, even non-climbers will want to climb the Tower."

Non-climbers.

People who awakened but are too afraid to fight, so they don't climb the Tower. Most of them stay on the 1st floor. What they earn is pitifully little. Earning even 1 coin a day is considered lucky.

But what if those people are given rifles they can use in the Tower?

'Even the orcs on the 5th floor could be killed easily. And a single orc gives at least 10 coins.'

At minimum, 300 dollars a day. Kill just 10 orcs and that's 3,000 dollars.

If they can hunt monsters relatively safely while earning high income... even non-climbers won't be able to resist.

"Indeed. If non-climbers just hunt on the 10th floor, the coin acquisition rate will surge."

It's a honey pot that other countries won't be able to complain about Korea over. At the very least, it'll become difficult for them to openly express dissatisfaction with Korea.

"Yes. As a bonus, it's an opportunity to showcase the excellence of Korea's K-2 rifle. Some countries like the US and China already know that Korea uses modern weapons in the Tower. They'll probably contact us within a few hours."

"You're planning to just give the guns away?"

"Of course not. The Korean peninsula is famously resource-poor as it is—just giving them away would be pure loss. I plan to sell them at a fair price. Not too expensive, though."

"Won't selling them at a high price still work?"

"If it's too expensive, complaints will pile up. Selling in bulk at low margins is ideal. And... I plan to bring the US into this business as well. Korea's production capacity alone can't handle the demand for rifles and ammunition."

"Using the US as a shield too, then?"

"Unfortunately, Korea's weight class is insufficient in East Asia."

Even if Lee Yeonhee was a woman crazy about Korea, she wasn't oblivious to reality.

I nodded. If Lee Yeonhee's plan succeeded, it benefited me too. Perhaps I could even use this world as a stable supply world.

"And I need your help."

Lee Yeonhee's gaze turned to Wiyu, Elena, and Yuria.

"If it's something that helps Master, I'll gladly assist."

Yuria answered calmly.

Elena crossed her arms and rubbed her chin.

"My help doesn't come cheap, you know?"

"If it's money, I'll compensate you fairly. Of course, I'll also provide conveniences for you, Elena. And I heard you've recently taken up stock trading."

"It's just a hobby."

"If I come across any stock-related information, I'll provide it to you first, Elena."

"Hmm. What do you want? Stocks are just a hobby for me. My main purpose is rest. Political matters, or anything that requires running around—I refuse."

"Please use your ability to turn fantasy into reality to create recovery potions. I intend to provide them to Korean hunters first. With potions, we can reduce the hunter mortality rate to some degree."

"I see. But my power is limited. As it stands, I can barely make 100 recovery potions a day. Is that acceptable?"

"Even 100 would be a blessing. In this world, recovery potions themselves are hard to come by."

"I'll do it. But what are you planning to ask of Yuria?"

"I'd like to ask Yuria to craft magic equipment. The limits of personal firearms—bulletproof vests, body armor, rifles, grenades—are clear. To surpass those limits, we need specialized equipment tailored to each individual hunter."

"That's not difficult. However, since I must attend to Master, I cannot dedicate much time."

"Just the fact that you won't refuse is enough. Thank you."

Lee Yeonhee approached Wiyu, who was sitting on the sofa. In her hands was a luxurious bottle of Korean soju. She had figured out that Wiyu enjoyed alcohol. Of course, Wiyu never got drunk no matter how much she drank.

"Wiyu. Shall we have a drink? I'll pour for you."

"That liquor is?"

"It's soju crafted by a traditional Korean soju master. It's hard to find even with money."

"There was no need to bring something so extravagant. What is it you want to ask of me?"

"I'd like you to create a basic martial art for low-rank hunters. It doesn't need to be a powerful martial art using qi. I'm not asking for something extreme like the Extreme Fist either. Just a basic martial art is fine."

"Basic, huh. You want a martial art that doesn't compromise its direction and can be used in real combat. There are plenty of those. I'll teach you a few. Learn them yourselves and take them."

"Yes. Thank you."

I marveled at Lee Yeonhee's resourcefulness.

"Anyway, since work is done... shall we all go bowling?"

"I'll sit this out since I have important matters remaining. But I'll arrange the bowling alley for you."

We headed to the bowling alley Lee Yeonhee had arranged. There were no other customers besides us, so we could enjoy bowling comfortably.

"This is cheating!"

Elena shouted as if roaring.

Wiyu, Yuria, and I got a strike every single time we rolled the ball. We were masters with exceptional body control, so bowling being trivial was unavoidable. Elena, with her inferior physical abilities, struggled to even get a strike without magic.

"Oh my, Elena. Let me help you. You just need to put your fingers in these holes."

"I know that much!"

"That's good. Your posture breaks when you release the ball. If you just pay attention to that part, you should be able to get a good score."

"Hmm. Was that it?"

Afterward, once we returned home, I threw Elena and Yuria onto the bed.
