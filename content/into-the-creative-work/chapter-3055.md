Namhwa Noseon, the administrator of this secret realm, was dead.

The secret realm did not immediately collapse and crumble. The realm itself continued to function even without an administrator. However, the flow of spiritual energy within the realm had changed. Little by little, it was leaking out somewhere.

Even the water of the Heavenly Lake floating in the sky was slowly spilling down to the ground.

'This secret realm is both a world and a massive training ground. Namhwa Noseon was the one who regulated the flow of spiritual energy as its administrator. With Namhwa Noseon dead, the realm's regulator is gone. The realm itself will hold out for a few years, but… training here will be difficult.'

I rummaged through Namhwa Noseon's bosom. There was a spatial pouch.

'So even immortals use spatial pouches. Convenient, I'll give it that.'

However, the spatial pouch was locked with a spell. Anyone without permission couldn't open it. If someone tried to force it open, the pouch would shatter and its contents would spill out in pieces.

'A kind of simplified barrier utilizing the principles of the Six Harmonies, is it.'

The Thunder Eye Golden Pupil grasped the barrier in an instant. I extended my index finger and traced the surface of the spatial pouch. Bzzt. A small spark flew, and the barrier unraveled. I had shaken the very foundation of the barrier to dispel it.

Click.

The spatial pouch opened. I simply turned the whole thing upside down. The contents poured out all at once. I caught every falling item with Void Grasp.

'Food and clothes. Excluding the useless junk, there aren't many things worth keeping. For an immortal, he sure lived like a beggar.'

Come to think of it, it wasn't strange at all.

The Immortal Realm isn't some Peach Blossom Spring paradise where you can live without working. A Earth Immortal like Namhwa Noseon? He's only one stage above a Human Immortal. Compared to the true Great Luo Immortals of the Immortal Realm, he's nothing.

'Few useful items, but the quality is considerable.'

In particular, a spirit item caught my eye.

'The spiritual energy density is absurd. Is this a spirit item from the Immortal Realm?'

Thunder Ox Black Horn.

Bzzt, bzzk!

A horn crackling ceaselessly with golden lightning.

It was a spirit item of at least the Harmony Realm grade. Even better, it was lightning-attribute, which suited me perfectly.

'…If I consume this now, I could gain considerable training results. However, this location is far from ideal.'

For now, I decided to use the Thunder Ox Black Horn myself and placed it in my inventory.

There were other spirit items as well—a transparently glowing lotus, grapes that looked hard as amethyst.

All of them were outstanding spirit items, but none matched my attributes.

'They're spirit items of at least the Harmony Realm grade… but I don't particularly need them. There are several of each, so they don't seem as precious as the Thunder Ox Black Horn. I'll take a few to bestow upon my subordinates and present the rest to the Emperor.'

It was also true that handling subordinates with only the whip was difficult. If I looked at just a few years, the whip alone would suffice, but looking long-term, offering carrots was the right move.

Presenting gifts to the Emperor was proof of loyalty. Not genuine loyalty. What mattered was that I had offered these precious things.

Next were the items. There was one Dharma Treasure and several Dharma Tools, all related to spells.

I grasped a green orb the size of a fist. A clear, refreshing energy emanated from it.

'The Dharma Treasure, the Seonui Pearl, is for healing. Just carrying it allows recovery from most diseases and injuries without aftereffects. Excluding special incurable conditions like severed meridians, that is. It even has detoxification capabilities and curse defense effects.'

It could even restore severed limbs or a destroyed dantian. It also had the effect of purifying contaminated energy.

'It isn't specialized in any one area, but it's versatile. It's a passive-type Dharma Treasure that activates just by being carried on one's person, so even an ordinary person can use it.'

If placed in an inventory or spatial pouch, its effects couldn't be received.

'For someone like me who has Complete Recovery, it's a fairly useless Dharma Treasure.'

But a treasure is a treasure. It felt a bit wasteful to present it to the Emperor or bestow it upon subordinates.

'I might find a use for it someday, so I'll hold onto it.'

I put it in my inventory with the feeling of tossing something into a corner of the house. Even if it got forgotten and sat unused for years, decades, I didn't particularly care. It was mine now, and spoils of war at that.

'…No. Wait a moment. If I carry it while resting after sex, does stamina recover faster too? What a supremely useful Dharma Treasure.'

I picked up the book titled Taepyeong Yosul Seo and skimmed through its contents.

'Even with the Thunder Eye Golden Pupil, this is too high-level a spell to learn immediately. I'll give it to Miryeong.'

Whether she learned it directly or researched it, she'd figure it out.

The Taepyeong Yosul was a spell that unconditionally copied and used a target's spell. However, it had the drawback of the copied spell being degraded.

'On the other hand, it can copy and use unique spells that require special conditions.'

If it was Miryeong, she would put it to good use.

[Death Resistance has activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

The price of the Heaven-Defying Art had come due.

I was accustomed to death, but no matter how many times I experienced it, it felt awful.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

Instantly restored to full health, I gathered everything worth taking and descended to the ground on a cloud. With the administrator Namhwa Noseon dead, the spatial wall that had isolated the Heavenly Lake was slowly crumbling, making movement possible.

On the ground, the martial artists of the Murim Alliance were staring blankly at the sky, and when they saw me, they were struck dumb. Some bit their lips, some squeezed their eyes shut. They knew too. No, they had no choice but to know. That I had fought Wang Ji-yuk and won.

The Murim Alliance's strategist, Hyeonseong Zhuge Yun, knelt the moment I touched the ground.

"I greet the new supreme of the martial world."

Thud!

The Murim Alliance martial artists around him all dropped to their knees at once.

"You're not going to attack? I just finished a life-and-death battle. If it's me in a tired state, if all of you rushed me, wouldn't there be some chance?"

I could feel some martial artists stoking their fighting spirit. But Zhuge Yun, who could be called their representative, bowed his head even deeper. His gaze was fixed not on my face, but at my feet.

"How could we dare oppose the greatest under heaven? There is no one in the Murim Alliance who can stand against you. The victor of the Great War between Orthodox and Demonic is you. We accept defeat."

"That's not the will of the entire Murim Alliance, is it? What about the ones outside? Especially Shaolin—they won't stay quiet."

"We who are here are the core of the Murim Alliance. The majority will follow our will. Those who don't, we will persuade. If you command it, we will even accept the dissolution of the Murim Alliance. So… please, show us mercy."

"The Orthodox begging the Demonic for their lives. How pathetic."

I mocked them openly. Some martial artists who felt humiliated clenched their teeth, but… their fighting spirit didn't ignite—it snuffed out like a candle. They bowed their heads even deeper.

"We are the defeated. It is justified for the Heavenly Demon to take our lives. If you command it, we will take our own lives here and now. However… please, allow our clans and sects to survive. If you spare us from annihilation, for hundreds of years—no, for as long as the Heavenly Demon Cult reigns over this martial world—we will seal our gates."

Slaughtering them all in a fit of temper would be easy.

'But if I kill them all, the spoils I gain decrease. More than anything, I am the supreme of the martial world, not the supreme of the realm. The reality is that the martial world fills in the Empire's insufficient administrative capacity. If I kill them all, the Emperor won't be pleased.'

Having to read the Emperor's mood didn't sit well with me, but considering the benefits I received from him, I could endure it.

"I accept the Murim Alliance's surrender. However, there are conditions. First, Shaolin seals its gates."

"We thank you for your mercy. It is not annihilation, so Shaolin will accept as well."

"Second, the sects belonging to the Orthodox faction shall pay to the Heavenly Demon Cult… no. His Majesty the Emperor might take issue with that. Pay half of your taxes to the Heavenly Demon Cult. And you will periodically offer spirit items and Dharma Tools."

Zhuge Yun drew in a breath.

"…All the sects belonging to the Orthodox faction, you mean? Some sects may defect to the Demonic faction."

"Then they become enemies of the Cult."

"……Will you annihilate the Demonic faction?"

"No. If enemies disappear completely, one can't accumulate real combat experience. A suitable punching bag is necessary. Just as His Majesty the Emperor leaves the monsters of the north alone."

"The Heavenly Demon truly is a fearsome one."

"Third. I understand remnants of the Beggar's Sect remain. Handle it yourselves. If later there is a sect that sheltered the beggars of the Beggar's Sect… I will personally annihilate them."

"The Beggar's Sect has already been annihilated. The remaining Beggar's Sect members all operate under hidden identities. Even for us, uncovering their identities is difficult."

"Figure it out. Whether you're deceived by those beggar bastards, or deliberately help them, I don't care. However, when the truth is revealed later… you will bear the responsibility."

Zhuge Yun's shoulders sagged.

Responsibility.

He must have felt its weight keenly.

"I understand."

Zhuge Yun's voice trembled.

"This secret realm will collapse within a few years. I will complete my remaining training. You will take all the Orthodox martial artists and leave the realm. In a few years, on the day I finish my training and leave the realm, I will confirm your attitude and judge you. Do not forget that the Heavenly Demon is the one who reigns."

"…I will keep it in mind. Supreme of the martial world."

Leaving the deeply prostrated Zhuge Yun behind, I departed from that place.
