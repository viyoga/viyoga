[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

I'm screwed.

I died not even 10 minutes after using Full Recovery. I can't use Full Recovery. Potions won't work. Wounds that would have instantly killed me without Death Resistance can't be healed with even the highest-grade potions. Plus, potions are less effective in this world.

But it's not like there's absolutely no way. I can think of three ways right now.

Elixir, Resurrection of the Dead, Absolute Imperial Authority.

The cheapest of these is the Elixir. 15,000 points. Resurrection of the Dead is 300,000 points. Absolute Imperial Authority was an item I couldn't buy from the random draw.

,But when I think about cost-effectiveness, even using an Elixir feels like a waste.'

Blood Compass.

It was an item I'd get as a quest success reward. Price: 10,000 points. A disposable item optimized for tracking. It's only 5,000 points cheaper than an Elixir. Does that mean the Blood Compass is that good? No way. It's that the Elixir is cheap for its effect. You could say it has good cost-effectiveness.

,I didn't even have much attachment to the Blood Compass in the first place. I did this quest because the Helper System looked fun.'

I came to a conclusion.

I'm giving up on the quest. It's the first time I've failed a Game Life app quest.

It's a bit annoying, but the reward wasn't as big as I thought, so it's not that much of a waste.

[Time remaining for Death Resistance: 10 seconds]

"Seong Yujin!"

Ju Seo-hyun grabbed my body. She wasn't in a normal state either. Her right arm was shattered. Not just shattered, but thoroughly shattered. You could barely see its shape. Covered in blood, she was distorting her face, not knowing what to do.

Are those sweat or tears welling up in those eyes?

,It's probably tears.'

That's more natural. And I want to believe that. I ended up like this trying to save Ju Seo-hyun, so even if it's Ju Seo-hyun, she should at least shed tears for me.

,Should I go for something even more touching while I'm at it?'

[Time remaining for Death Resistance: 6 seconds]

"Ju Seo-hyun...! This sword... Sonata, you take it...!"

I forced myself to spit out blood as I spoke. Unlike what I was thinking, it took more than 3 seconds just to speak.

If I die, Ju Seo-hyun will probably return to Atlantis. She brought a weapon when she came, so wouldn't she take a weapon when she leaves? Even if not, if I can leave a good impression on Ju Seo-hyun, I can afford to use Moonlight Sonata 7.

"Why, why did you push yourself to save me?!"

"Just...."

I wanted to say something cool, but there wasn't time. There's only so much I can say in 1 second.

"Seong Yujin! You...!"

[Time remaining for Death Resistance: 0 seconds]

[You have died.]

Old memories come to mind.

When I first awakened to Game Life, I died in the game world once. At that time, the game ended against my will and the settlement began.

,What is it?'

But now the game hasn't ended. I was in a dead state. There's no pain. For some reason, my body feels very light. Like I have no mass at all. And I can see ahead.

"......."

Ju Seo-hyun's eyes are shaking. Sitting on the floor, she was holding my corpse with her only remaining hand.

,Astral projection...? Could it be that I've become a ghost? It's a world with vampires, werewolves, and witches... It wouldn't be strange if there were ghosts....'

[The Helper has not died.]

[Do you want to give up the quest, ignoring the Helper's wishes?]

Game Life told me the reason.

Right. This quest was different from the usual games. It seemed I could watch over Ju Seo-hyun in a ghost state.

,Let's continue the quest... I'm curious about Ju Seo-hyun's reaction.'

[The quest will continue.]

[You can adjust the progress speed.]

Ju Seo-hyun put down my corpse. She picks up Moonlight Sonata 7 with her only remaining hand and stands up.

She glared at Yoon Seo-jin, who was quietly watching from a distance.

"...I'm going to kill you."

"What a wonderful bloodlust, Butcher. But weren't you right-handed? Can you kill me with your left hand?"

"......."

Ju Seo-hyun took a step. Her body is shaking. Her right arm was shattered by the vibration, and she lost too much blood. It won't be easy to balance since she lost her arm in an instant. Likewise, her complexion wasn't good either. But her eyes, full of anger and hatred, were more ferocious than ever.

"What a perfect opportunity to turn you into a vampire."

Unlike what he said, Yoon Seo-jin didn't move his legs. He doesn't approach Ju Seo-hyun. He was clutching his side. Blood keeps coming out of his side.

,The Sonata's anti-magic is acting like a poison.'

It's so toxic that even that Vampire Lord can't easily adapt to it. Yoon Seo-jin isn't in a normal state. As Ju Seo-hyun continues to approach, Yoon Seo-jin retreated.

"Let's stop here for today. Anyone can see that I have the upper hand... but I can't guarantee everything against that Butcher. I don't gamble."

"Are you... running away?"

Ju Seo-hyun gritted her teeth. Blood droplets splattered from her mouth.

"I only fight in certain moments. That's how I became a Vampire Lord. And right now, there are too many variables. But I've confirmed that if we face each other head-on in a normal state, I'll win. And you've lost your right arm. Next time, there will be a definite conclusion. I can already see you becoming a Vampire Queen and ruling this world."

"Shut up...! I'm going to kill you right now...!"

Ju Seo-hyun runs towards Yoon Seo-jin. Her body was constantly staggering.

"Too obvious. I won't fall for that kind of act. More than anything... that sword is too dangerous."

"Yoon Seo-jin...!"

"We will meet again soon. I look forward to that day."

Yoon Seo-jin turned around and left the scene. His running body was shaking slightly, and blood continued to flow from his side.

Ju Seo-hyun collapsed to the floor. Even the sword she never let go of, fell from her grasp and collapsed on the floor.

,Ju Seo-hyun's condition is too bad. She might die.'

Sirens and gunshots can be heard in the distance. If she holds on a little longer, she'll be able to go to the hospital.

I reached out to Ju Seo-hyun, who was sitting blankly. My hand couldn't touch Ju Seo-hyun's body and passed through. All I could do now was watch.

• • •

Ju Seo-hyun, who was sitting down, stared blankly at the ground. Seong Yujin was on her mind.

Bastard.

Enemy.

Rapist.

Target of revenge.

Now that that annoying Seong Yujin was dead, she should have felt relieved. But it felt like her insides were rotting away.

,...I should have killed Seong Yujin! With my own hands! I, I should have killed him...!'

That Seong Yujin died trying to save her.

Why?

Wasn't she just a plaything to him? A sex toy, a sexual outlet, a sex slave. That's how he treated her. But he saved her by sacrificing his own life. The reason was absurd. Just because.

"Damn... bastard...."

Ju Seo-hyun wiped away the tears that were dripping down with her only remaining arm.

She couldn't be sure if the tears were flowing because she was sad, because she was in pain, or because she was frustrated. She just wanted to believe that the tears were flowing because she was frustrated.

,Why is Seong Yujin's smiling face coming to mind....'

She remembers going out and playing together on holidays. She couldn't understand why these memories were coming to mind, leaving behind other shitty memories.

"Seong Yujin.... I'll repay the debt."

He saved her.

Ju Seo-hyun didn't want to owe him anything. But Seong Yujin is already dead. There is only one way to repay the debt of life.

"I'll get revenge for you. I'll kill Yoon Seo-jin."

But it's impossible in this state. She can't use one arm. And that's very fatal. Go to the hospital? She doesn't think she'll be able to fix her completely shattered right arm. If she had a potion, she might, but she didn't have any potions.

Ju Seo-hyun reached out with her left hand. What she was holding was not a sword, but a syringe. A syringe that Yoon Seo-jin dropped the moment he was attacked. This contained Yoon Seo-jin's blood.

Become a vampire.

Then she can get power.

Ju Seo-hyun's mouth trembled. In exchange for gaining power, she has to give up being human. Giving up being human is like shattering the beliefs she has upheld in this world.

But.

Seong Yujin, who saved her, comes to her mind. At the same time, her hatred and anger towards Yoon Seo-jin also grow. Revenge dominates her body.

She was a vengeful spirit.

She had been prepared to give up anything for revenge since she was in Atlantis. She could endure becoming a monster for revenge.

She picked up the syringe and stuck it in her neck. She presses the syringe. Yoon Seo-jin's blood, the Vampire Lord's blood, spreads throughout her body through her blood vessels.

"Ah, aaaaaaaaaah...!"

Ju Seo-hyun hung her head down. Her long hair flowed down to the floor.

Her whole body is hot. The heat felt like it would burn her brain. More than anything, her senses are fluctuating. Her whole body is changing.

She instinctively moved her left hand to find the sword.

Sword.

Something she can trust more than herself. The sword was her, and she was the sword. Ju Seo-hyun tightly gripped the hilt of Moonlight Sonata 7.

The change began.

The starting point was her right arm. Her completely shattered right arm begins to regenerate. Tremendous pain accompanied the process of muscles and bones being newly aligned.

"......!"

Ju Seo-hyun gritted her teeth and endured it.

She maintains her sanity without succumbing to the pain. She remembers something she heard somewhere before. The reason why vampires become monsters is because most of them can't endure the changing process and lose their minds.

,I can't let my mind turn into a vampire too.'

She's not becoming a vampire to be consumed by power. She's becoming a vampire to use power.

The pain that had been reaching its peak disappeared at some point.

She felt the sensation of her right arm and the sensation of her elongated fangs. She's hungry. Seong Yujin's corpse comes into her sight. His body is a mess, but the blood is still fresh.

"......."

She closed her eyes tightly and opened them again. She suppresses her bloodsucking urges and gets up from her seat.

Ju Seo-hyun's hair had turned blood red.
