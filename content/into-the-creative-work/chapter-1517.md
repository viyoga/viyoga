I told Milyeong about the conversation I had with Mahobeol, the head of the Mukjilian branch. There was something that bothered me a little.

,If it's hard to find the answer, you can ask someone else.,

Even though Milyeong looks a bit dim-witted, she's actually quite smart. She's so versatile that you could call her all-around.

,So, Socheonma... was it Cheon Yu-un? He's trying to use Seobang-nim to keep that person in check. I don't have concrete evidence, but he might be trying to use him for more than just keeping him in check.,

,More than keeping him in check....,

I couldn't possibly not know what that meant.

I nodded.

And after a long time, I started training. It was direct training, not automatic.

It was a barrier inside the forest.

It wasn't a good place. There's no good land for training around here. For example, land with overflowing energy.

,Those places have already been taken over by famous sects.,

There was no choice.

It's not like I can just barge into the Jongnam Sect to do some training.

I'm grateful that Milyeong is by my side, at least. She cast a sensory art on me that helps with training. Sensory art is a technique that makes the senses more sensitive.

I clear my mind and focus on my breathing.

My senses become even more sensitive.

The soft touch of the wind on my skin begins to feel solid. The leaves falling in the wind fall slowly. Even though I wasn't using Instant, my thinking speed was accelerating due to concentration.

It wasn't anything special in this world.

There's a saying that battles between masters end in an instant. That saying is half right and half wrong. From the perspective of ordinary people, battles between masters are instantaneous, but from the perspective of masters, that instant is a ildagyeong or more.

,The reason why you can never win if the level difference is too great is because of this. The world that masters and underlings live in is different.,

I drew my sword from my waist.

Unbefitting of its nickname, Lightning Flash Sword, the sword was slow. It wasn't because my thinking was accelerated. Even from the perspective of ordinary people, they would be able to see the sword being drawn very slowly, slower than a snail crawling.

Wiyu says that quickness and slowness are one. If you realize quickness, you can know slowness, and if you realize slowness, you can know quickness. Only by realizing both can you reach the true realm of quickness.

,I don't understand at all.,

It's said to be a principle of martial arts that you should naturally realize when you reach my level. But no matter how much I pondered, I couldn't understand it.

,Quickness is about being fast. Why do I need to be slow?,

Quickness is about being fast.

Nothing else is needed.

Swing the sword quickly, kill the enemy quickly.

I just wanted my sword to be like lightning.

,But I can't ignore what a super master says.,

Wiyu was a super master among masters.

There are only a handful of people in this world who can compare to her.

,Since such a super master is my teacher, I have to do as she says.,

I swing the sword slowly.

It may look easy, but it's actually quite difficult to do yourself. The sword flies all over the place with the slightest mistake. You have to give it a lot of strength and perfectly control your muscles.

The training of slowness, which didn't really yield any results, was over.

Of course, that wasn't all there was to the training.

I plopped down on the ground. I take out a spirit pill from my inventory. It was a spirit pill made by combining the cheap elixirs that came out of the space pocket I got from killing the Black Tiger last time.

,Eating this won't suddenly raise my level, will it.,

Transcending the realm of the Five Energies. If it were possible to do it simply by taking elixirs, I would have already broken through the wall a long time ago.

Still, an elixir is an elixir.

I take it, believing that this elixir will lower the threshold of the wall even a little.

Gulp.

At the same time as the intense bitter taste disappeared, flames soared from inside my body. The hot flames spread to my fingertips and toes.

I recite the Thunder Heaven Knot and control my energy. The energy of the elixir cannot resist my will.

Pajik, pajijik.

Lightning energy burst from my entire body. The lightning energy revolves around me. It was a circulation. A circulation that was already complete.

The circulation of energy was perfect. It was too perfect, which was the problem. In order to transcend the realm, this circulation of energy must be raised to a higher level.

I deliberately disturbed the circulation. The circulation gets faster and faster. It's a supercar speeding down the highway. It's more thrilling than anything else when driving on a straight road, but the story changes when there's a corner. The lightning energy began to break away from the circulation.

The shape of the circulation is distorted. The aftermath led to internal injuries.

Blood gushes from my eyes, ears, nose, and mouth. A total of seven holes. The blood vessels are damaged. It's already beyond repair. The lightning energy that has escaped my control invades even my brain. If this happens, you'll usually die or go crazy from demonic possession.

,With Absolute Spirit, I won't go crazy.,

Bang!

My head exploded.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

[Using Complete Recovery.]

,Hoo.,

I sighed and got up. The floor was squishy with blood and brain fragments.

,I failed again.,

I pushed myself too hard, trusting in Complete Recovery. But I couldn't break through the wall. I couldn't even get a clue to break through the wall.

,I've done it several times, but this method isn't the answer.,

I trained like this every time, hoping that if I was prepared to die, I would get results. But no matter how many times I died, there were no results.

It's the same even if I use the Time of Genius.

,Well, Yuria didn't just breeze through the realms either.,

Yuria also trained steadily and got stuck in realms. Because I know how hard she worked, I don't want to belittle her efforts by calling her a genius.

,But Yuria is a genius, isn't she.,

If an ordinary person gets 1 result with 1 effort, Yuria gets 10 or more results with 1 effort.

,If Yuria were me, wouldn't it take her only a few years to break through the wall?,

I left the training ground with trivial thoughts.

Today's training was over. I didn't feel like training.

It was time for the regular report.

I sat down in a spot away from Baewol City and gazed up at the sky. A black hawk flew in. It's a low-level spirit beast called a Black Horse Hawk. The reason it has the character for horse (馬) in its name is that its body vaguely resembles a horse.

The Black Horse Hawk was a spirit beast with the ability to make its body transparent. It was also intelligent and had a homing instinct, so the Demonic Cult actively used the Black Horse Hawk.

The Black Horse Hawk flew to me and landed on the ground. The Black Horse Hawk shook its neck cynically. The pouch hanging around its neck flew to me.

I received the pouch with a question. I've met the Black Horse Hawk about 6 times so far, but this was the first time it brought something separately.

A folded piece of paper came out of the pouch. I unfold the paper to check it.

Jeong Bae, the Sakpung Ghost Sword, is hiding near Jongnam Mountain. Find him, kill him, put his head in the pouch, and send it back.

It was a mission from the Demonic Cult.

I scratched my head. It goes without saying, but I have no idea who Sakpung Ghost Sword Jeong Bae is. I've never even heard of his name or his alias.

,Judging by his alias, he's not a righteous person.,

The word ghost (鬼) is not used in the alias of a righteous martial artist.

,A heretic. Or a member of the cult.,

The latter is more likely.

Pajik.

A small spark ignited and burned the paper. The Black Horse Hawk sitting on the ground stared up at me.

,Follow me. Stay at my house for a while.,

The Black Horse Hawk nodded as if it understood me.
