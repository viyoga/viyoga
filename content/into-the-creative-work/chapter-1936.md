,Eldona!! That bastard, Eldona...!!!'

Enraged, the second brother pulled the trigger of his rifle. At the same time, I used an Instant.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 3]

Having used Instant frequently while coming up here, I now had only 3 stacks left.

Three were more than enough. I had no intention of dragging out this fight.

In the slowed-down world, I calculated the incantation while watching the bullets spewed out by the second brother. Each bullet was shining with a different color.

They were all magic bullets. And each of a different kind.

,Running a corporation must mean they're rolling in dough.'

[Gravity]

I amplified the gravity, slamming all the incoming magic bullets into the ground. Pow, pow, pow. The magic bullets exploded, flashing like fireworks.

Hwareureuk.

Flames rose from my feet. It was the third brother's magic.

,A firewall, huh.'

His incantation was crude. Since he was trying to cast the magic directly beneath my feet, I had a chance to mess with the incantation. I tampered with the incantation, twisting its direction. The flames that should have shot upwards from below my feet stretched out horizontally towards where the third brother was.

The third brother, horrified, hastily deployed a barrier to block the flames.

,Gasp! He interfered with the incantation in that short moment?!'

Regardless, I used magic towards the ceiling.

[Air Strike]

Using telekinesis, I floated my body above the broken ceiling. I looked down at the ground while flying in the sky. The incoming bullets, Sword energy, and magic were blocked by the overlapping barriers, unable to do anything.

,A magician is most secure when they have a barrier.'

If that barrier doesn't crumble even under the enemies' attacks, the outcome of the battle is as good as decided.

,The effect of the Golden Gift is better than I thought.'

Without the Golden Gift, it would have been difficult to maintain the barrier. The 50% increase in all stats was so outstanding that I felt addicted to it after experiencing it firsthand.

,I don't have Bukjayong at all either. The only downside is that it's a hard item to get.'

I looked up at the sky.

The night sky was clear without a single cloud. When I lowered my gaze, a building with over 10 floors came into view. After making a quick estimate, it seemed possible.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 2]

In the slowed-down world, I calculated the incantation and unfolded the magic circle.

Pajijik.

Cracks appeared in the sky-blue magic circle embroidered in the sky. There was a slight mistake in the incantation. The balance was slightly off.

,Did I put in too much power? I thought this was the maximum, but I can increase the output even more.'

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 1]

I corrected the magic circle. If I didn't have Instant, the magic circle would have collapsed before I could correct it.

The magic circle began to rotate slowly, emitting light. Mana resonated towards the center of the magic circle, distorting the laws of the world. Something impossible was about to happen.

,What are you trying to do?!'

Perhaps sensing that something was amiss. The first brother, Bergmann, shouted with a vein popping out of his neck. I ignored him and looked at the magic circle. I don't know how it looked to others, but it looked perfect to me.

,That bastard!! Die! Just die!!!'

The second brother fired his gun. The magic bullets pounded on the barrier. When the barrier seemed like it would break, I put up a new one. Magic bullets alone can't break through my current barrier.

,I can't believe it.... It's a magic circle that's so beautiful it's mesmerizing. I've only seen such a perfect magic circle in textbooks... I never thought I'd actually see it. It's an honor as a magician.'

The third brother seemed to have realized the difference in skill and given up on life. If he was wise, he was wise.

[Thunder Break]

A huge bolt of lightning struck down towards the building. The entire city flashed with lightning.

The building, designed to withstand several missiles, couldn't withstand a single bolt of lightning and collapsed as it was. It was only natural that everyone living inside the building died.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwang!

The sight of the building collapsing with the blue current still lingering was truly spectacular.

Feeling my energy draining away, I slowly descended to the ground.

,Would it have been less tiring if I had done this from the beginning? No. I can't say anything since I gained something.'

Above all, it was possible because I drank the Golden Gift. For the current me to use 7th-grade Thunder Break, several conditions, including the environment, had to be met. Thanks to the Golden Gift, I was able to use Thunder Break without any conditions.

-Rumen Prison Warden Leon Schneider personally executes the traitor!

That was the phrase written on the front page of the newspaper.

It was a natural reaction, of course, since I openly unleashed a large-scale electric attack magic, Thunder Break, in the city.

Public opinion was favorable to me.

It was thanks to the perception that Bergmann was a traitor. The recent ominous atmosphere in the Heist Empire was due to the traitors. Ordinary citizens shuddered at the thought of traitors.

,Even though I openly caused trouble, no one in the Imperial Army is saying anything to me. Is this the power of the 6th Corps Commander's backing....'

Anyway, the work was finished.

Rules were also established in Rumen Prison. Even if I'm not deeply involved, problems won't arise.

I took out a potion containing a vivid orange liquid from my pocket and placed it on the desk.

Flash.

Memory recovery potion.

Now that the Bergmann matter was settled, it was time to use this. As originally planned, I would restore Paul's memories to find out information about Alphatier Pharmaceuticals.

,Should I try drinking about a drop of it before that.'

My memories were not intact. I'm talking about the memories of my past life, not my current memories. Except for the memories of the original game, other memories are almost non-existent.

The artificially cut-off memories were sometimes very unpleasant. If possible, I wanted to find my memories.

,Just a drop. And it's a memory from my past life, so it probably won't be of much use.'

Still, if there was even a very slight reaction, it would be meaningful.

I put a drop of Flash in my mouth and swallowed it. It had a slightly bitter taste.

,...Nothing happened. Even if it's just a drop, there should be some kind of reaction.'

It was truly unresponsive.

Flash did not restore the memories of my past life.

,Well, if it restored the memories of my past life, it would have become an incredibly amazing item in another sense.'

I didn't have high expectations, so I wasn't too disappointed.

• • •

I fed Flash to Paul, who was trapped in the basement. I didn't feed him all of it. I left some for research purposes.

Still, the effect was sufficient.

Paul's atmosphere, whose memories were forcibly restored, changed instantly. Does a person change this much just by recalling memories? Since it's not an ordinary memory, that must be it.

,It seems like the memory has been successfully restored.'

,A drug that restores memories. How did you get it?'

,Do I have to explain that to you in detail?'

,Is it okay for you to be so prickly? I'm holding the information you want tightly in my hands?'

,Do you like prison life that much? Do you want to be stuck here for the rest of your life?'

I scanned Paul's body. His arms and legs had already been cut off. Only his torso and head were intact. Touching his torso is a bit much, so I should probably tear off his ears or eyes first.

,I should only tear off one eye. If I tear off both, he might go crazy.'

Perhaps he read my thoughts. His momentum waned. He sighed deeply and said.

,I'll tell you what you want to know. But kill me after you've heard everything.'

,That's unexpected. Don't you want to live? You know that your severed limbs can be replaced with machines.'

,No matter how I think about it, I don't think you'll let me live.'

That's correct. If this bastard leaves the prison alive, it will be difficult for me. Even if he swears to live quietly in the future, I can't trust him.

,I want a clean death. Even if I get out of prison now, I'll be living under Alphatier's surveillance. But if I keep my mouth shut, you won't leave me alone.... I'm sick of torture.'

,Isn't the torture time less than an hour a day?'

,Being in a room with nothing but the smell of Piss and Urine after cutting off my limbs is torture in itself. That's 10 hours. 100 hours. 1,000 hours, and I'm really going crazy. There's a limit to imagining things alone... Later, I can only think about wanting to die.'

,I promise to kill you cleanly. So tell me about Alphatier.'

,It's hard to open my mouth when you tell me to speak so abruptly. What exactly are you curious about?'

,Stop talking nonsense and tell me all the information about Alphatier. Except for what's known to the public.'

,There's so much that we'll be up all night.'

Paul opened his mouth.

Most of the content was something that would terrify ordinary people if they heard it. They were conducting human experiments as naturally as breathing. Experiments to turn humans into monsters, experiments to turn monsters into humans, experiments to isolate hundreds of people and stimulate their instincts, experiments to design and use a single human's life from beginning to end.

There were quite a few experiments whose purpose I couldn't understand.

,Pharmaceutical companies do those kinds of experiments? What for?'

,Alphatier believes that even mental illness can be cured with medicine. It's actually possible. But from what I've seen, their real goal is to evolve and transcend from the human species. Isn't it funny? To transcend humanity with medicine.'

,.......'

Paul said it as if he was joking. It was a reaction that he wasn't even sure about himself.

On the other hand, I judged that it was possible.

Transcendence.

It was a keyword that was treated quite importantly even in the original work.

Transcendence through magic, transcendence through physical training, transcendence through machines, etc. There are many ways to transcend. It's not strange to have transcendence through medicine.

,Alphatier's Chairman gave a speech in front of us. At that time, he said this. A new era of humanity will open, and Alphatier will be at the forefront. At that time, I thought it was just plausible bullshit to raise the morale of the employees.'

,Is it different now.'

,Looking at what those bastards are doing, it can't be helped. They're seriously trying to evolve and transcend humans.'
