Jin Sukyeol, the Chairman of Ilwoo Group, was staying in Japan but kept up with the news from Korea without missing a beat. Just because he was in Japan didn't mean his ears and eyes had disappeared.

Naturally, he heard about the horrific incident at the Busan headquarters.

His son, Vice Chairman Jin Seongman, was dead, and his grandson, Director Jin Chunghyeon, was dead. The culprit was the one who terrorized the headquarters.

Of course, he knew the terrorist's identity as well.

Seong Yujin, the representative of Seongak Elementary School.

A man currently called a hero of South Korea. His combat footage had spread worldwide and he had become famous in an instant.

In peaceful times, a hero is of no help, but in these tough times where survival is at stake, a hero has become an idol. Seong Yujin was truly Korea's idol.

This was also the reason why he couldn't officially announce the terrorist's identity. He also lacked concrete evidence.

,It's too much of a burden to touch Seong Yujin, who has already become a hero.'

The government was pushing Seong Yujin. They were putting forward the hero Seong Yujin to quell public discontent and reduce stress.

Moreover, Ilwoo Group was currently in the bad graces of the Korean government. It was only natural since they had tried to fortify and swallow Busan as they pleased.

,The plan to make Busan Ilwoo Group's territory has failed.'

The headquarters was terrorized, and Jin Seongman and Jin Chunghyeon, who were responsible for the plan, died. It was natural for Ilwoo Group's influence over Busan to decline, and with this incident, the Korean government began to keep Ilwoo Group in check.

Chairman Jin Sukyeol coldly assessed the situation. While feeling sadness and anger at the deaths of his son and grandson, he suppressed his emotions with his powerful reason. Cold reason was his strength, motto, and belief.

,I should inform the other affiliates in Korea and order them not to touch Seong Yujin.'

They will resist.

It's about the death of family, no less. His children, who are the key figures of Ilwoo Group, will shout that they must take revenge.

,Now is not the time for revenge. It's not too late to take revenge after preparations are complete.'

He made a decision. Even his children would not be able to defy his will.

Jin Sukyeol turned around.

Jin Sukyeol's villa located somewhere in Japan. Its true nature was a secret research lab created in cooperation with the Japanese government. The research topic of the lab was human evolution. Countless humans were suffering and being experimented on.

,If we can artificially manipulate human evolution... Ilwoo Group will become the master of the world.'

• • •

Currently, Son Taehyeong, a member of the sole honor guard and holding the personnel rights to select honor guard members, was walking around Seongak Elementary School, keeping an eye on people.

The first thing he looked at was the security team. They had abundant combat experience in Seongak Elementary School. With just a little training, they would surely become useful soldiers.

However, he soon excluded the security team. The security team might have loyalty, but they lacked faith. They weren't so deeply loyal that they would readily give up their lives. It felt like they worked as much as they were paid.

,The honor guard is the last barrier to protect that person. They must be able to throw away their lives like trash.'

The security team was eliminated. However, regardless of Son Taehyeong's harsh evaluation, the security team was currently in a pretty good mood.

Because of the slaves they had obtained this time.

When a slave works, the slave's owner gets the pay. In other words, security team members who owned slaves were making money without doing anything. And they could use the slaves as they pleased.

At first, the security team also felt revulsion. Even if the looters were criminals, weren't they still human? How could they treat them carelessly? The moral education and Confucian culture they had learned since childhood kept poking at their conscience.

But as time passed, and as the relationship between master and slave became clearer, their conscience became more rounded.

The starting point was the security team that had lost a colleague to the looters. Even though Seong Yujin healed the injured security team members, he did not revive the dead ones. He poured out his anger on the enslaved looters. Insults were natural, and he even used violence. The slaves could not resist.

Such scenes were seen here and there, and his other colleagues also joined him, starting to treat the slaves carelessly.

Male slaves had to work while being subjected to violence. There was only one way to escape slavery. To achieve merit and be recognized. But the opportunity to achieve merit itself was rare.

Female slaves were a bit special. Beautiful female slaves became Seong Yujin's property and their status rose from some point on. On the other hand, what if their beauty was average or below? Inevitably, they became sex slaves. It was common for them to become prostitutes and work under the orders of their master. If they were lucky, they could marry their master and escape their slave status.

In any case, these were not the talents Son Taehyeong wanted. Son Taehyeong turned his eyes elsewhere.

Those who had lost their families.

Those who had been attacked by zombies, mutants, or looters and had become disabled.

Those who were despairing in this terrible world, had lost the purpose of life, and were contemplating death.

Son Taehyeong approached them, but he didn't bring them to the honor guard right away. He had to filter out those who needed to be filtered out from among them.

Son Taehyeong gathered them. Of course, they didn't try to follow him willingly. Son Taehyeong gathered them by using money, food, and medicine as bait.

,That person is our true savior and the guardian who can protect us. We must believe in and follow that person. We must die if that person tells us to die, and live if that person tells us to live. The fact that we can be here now is all possible because of that person.'

Son Taehyeong, who was a pastor, continued to preach softly to those gathered. He sometimes changed the contents of the Bible appropriately and repeatedly had them praise that person.

They were people who had already experienced deep despair and had nowhere to turn. Faith takes root quickly in such people. Some of them already had other faiths.

,That's nonsense! I admit that Representative Seong is a great person! But Representative Seong is not a god!'

,The true savior is only Maitreya. Someday, Maitreya Buddha will appear and save this world!'

,If there was a god! Would the world be in this state?!'

Those who raised their voices and vented their anger at Son Taehyeong. The reason they participated in the meeting was solely to receive food.

,That person has not yet been reborn as a true god. But it is a predetermined future that that person will become a god and soar to the heavens.'

Son Taehyeong said. Seong Yujin, the person in question, had said nothing about himself, but Son Taehyeong believed that he would truly become a god. Because he had said so.

,Nonsense! What you're saying is no different from a Cult!'

,I'm getting tired of listening. Can I leave now?'

,Did Representative Seong tell you to brainwash us? I'm not a fool who rejects such words.'

Those who vehemently rejected faith.

Son Taehyeong smiled instead. The reason they were reacting so sensitively was because they already had faith. The same was true for those who were gritting their teeth and trying to reject religion. Persuading them was simple. All he had to do was show them evidence of God.

,I will show you the miracles of that person. Come here in two days. We will provide pork cutlet lunch boxes to those who come.'

,Gulp.'

As he tempted them with pork cutlet, the sound of swallowing saliva could be heard here and there. Even pork cutlet was hard to get these days.

And two days later, Seong Yujin appeared.

Seong Yujin did nothing. He just sat on a high chair and looked down at those gathered here. Son Taehyeong recited words of praise and offered prayers in front of him.

,Ah, Lord, here is a person who has lost his arm to a monster. Although he is one who believes in a false god, please bestow upon him gentle mercy and a holy miracle!'

A Christian who had lost his arm.

Seong Yujin quietly gestured towards him. Son Taehyeong very carefully approached Seong Yujin with him. Seong Yujin placed his hand on the head of the Christian, who was sweating profusely.

A miracle happened.

The right arm that had been lost to the mutant grew rapidly and was regenerated.

He, the person who experienced the miracle, stared at his right arm with a dazed look. The sensation of his right arm, which had been regenerated without any pain, was clear. He moved his right hand a few times and then quietly trembled and shed tears.

,Hic.... I, I dared to not recognize the Lord and acted presumptuously...! I repent! I will repent!'

The man who had experienced the miracle prostrated himself on the floor.

,I will give you a chance to repent. Follow Son Taehyeong.'

Seong Yujin said. His gaze turned to Son Taehyeong. Son Taehyeong looked around. The foolish people who had witnessed the miracle were blankly staring at Seong Yujin.

,Lord, here is the mother of a child suffering from an incurable disease. Please have mercy on this poor mother and child!'

Seong Yujin approached a seven-year-old child. Pale skin, eyes resembling dead fish, a scalp without a single strand of hair.

Seong Yujin used his ability. Life returned to her skin, and black hair grew on her scalp.

,Ah, it doesn't hurt! My illness is really cured!'

,Thank you! Thank you! Lord, thank you!'

The child was happy, and the mother bowed her head.

,Lord! Here is rotten wine! Please bestow a miracle so that we can all quench our thirst today!'

Son Taehyeong ordered the servants to bring a barrel containing rotten wine. The people confirmed with their own eyes and noses that the wine was rotten.

This was also a planned performance.

Seong Yujin used his healing ability on the rotten wine. There was no way that the ability to revive a decayed corpse and restore a broken ability would not be able to restore rotten food.

The wine was restored in his hands. A sweet fragrance spread widely instead of the rotten smell of the wine.

,.......'

Those who had witnessed the miracle closed their eyes and knelt on the floor. They prayed with faith in their hearts.

,We believe, we believe. Lord. Save us...!'
