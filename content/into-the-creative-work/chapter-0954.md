I followed Kang Ojik into the tent with the temporary waiting room sign. Besides me, seven other Hunters were seated. Among them, one Hunter caught my eye.

,Long time no see.

He was a man wearing a black coat even though summer was approaching. With his distinct features, he was the youngest of the seven Hunters.

Kang Seok-su.

A man famous as South Korea's young genius magician.

We had met and competed in a dungeon survival about two years ago.

I usually don't remember men's names or appearances well. Even if I do remember, I tend to forget them unless they're particularly impressive. The reason I didn't forget Kang Seok-su is that he frequently appears on TV and the internet, and his girlfriend was delicious.

,No, she wasn't his girlfriend back then. They were childhood friends, though. Her name is Yu Jeong-mi. I remember it clearly. She's not here.

I sat next to Kang Seok-su. There was no other seat available.

,It's been a while. What have you been up to?

,What else would a Hunter be doing? Of course, I've been fulfilling the association's requests and conquering dungeons. What about you...

,I've been doing similarly.

,It's not that, but I contacted you occasionally, and you completely ignored me. What's the reason for even blocking my number? I don't think I've done anything wrong to you?

Kang Seok-su's voice was cold. The look in his eyes wasn't friendly either. I almost hit him but held back.

,You were bothering me. I have a habit of blocking numbers when men bother me with calls. I'll unblock you since I feel sorry.

I can always block him again later.

,Huh.

Kang Seok-su, letting out a hollow laugh, shook his head.

Following that, Kang Ojik reappeared in the waiting room. He began the briefing on the B-grade dungeon.

,We have absolutely no information about this B-grade closed dungeon. It's not that we couldn't scout it, but we didn't. I think you all know the reason why.

Conquer it without information.

It meant conquering it from scratch, with absolutely nothing. I figured it was for the purpose of gaining experience, so I let it be.

,Of course, if you deem it dangerous, you can escape immediately. No one will blame you for failing. The most important thing is your lives.

He handed us a red skein of thread.

The Thread of Origin.

An item that allows for emergency escape from a closed dungeon.

,What I'm going to brief you on isn't the dungeon, but about you all. You need to cooperate with each other, but you don't have information about each other.

He explained information about me and the other Hunters. I barely got through the incredibly boring briefing. There were two women among the Hunters, but neither was an outstanding beauty enough to catch my eye.

,The briefing is over. If there are no issues, we will start conquering the dungeon in one hour.

The Hunters started chattering. They determined positions and decided on tactics based on their abilities. At the center of it was Kang Seok-su.

The meeting proceeded smoothly and ended in just 20 minutes. I fiddled with my smartphone during the remaining time.

,Han Ha-rin... no response. Did she already enter an A-grade dungeon?

Kang Seok-su approached me. He placed a canned coffee in front of me.

,Have you thought about what I said before?

,What you said before?

I narrowed my brows. I wracked my brain. No memories came to mind. Kang Seok-su sighed.

,About the guild. Didn't I suggest that we create a guild together?

,Ah, you did. You haven't made it yet?

,I'm not an A-grade Hunter yet. But I've almost filled the requirements. I'll probably take the promotion exam next month, or the month after.

,Oh. Congratulations. You'll become an A-grade Hunter faster than me.

It wasn't surprising. Kang Seok-su had been treated as a genius since he was young, and he's two years older than me.

,That offer from back then. It's still valid.

,Why me specifically?

,Your value in South Korea right now is more than you think. Your value has increased even more since you rejected Seikeol Guild's offer to join recently.

,How did you even know that? Did you investigate me?

Kang Seok-su chuckled.

,Investigate? South Korea is small. Information rolls in even if you stay still.

It's not really that information rolls in even if you stay still. It seems like he has a separate information system.

,You think I'll join your guild after rejecting Seikeol Guild? It's not like you can support me as much as Seikeol Guild can. You don't even have a guild yet. Honestly, your words sound like multi-level marketing right now.

Even with my somewhat crossing-the-line words, Kang Seok-su's face was calm.

,I understand. Compared to a giant guild, we have nothing. I know that creating a guild isn't just about ambition.

He took out his smartphone and showed it to me. It contained information about the guild.

,What is it.

,I'm going to create a guild as soon as I get promoted to A-grade. I've been preparing for it. I made the plan two years ago, and it's almost complete now.

,Seventier?

,That's the name of the guild. It's a name I chose with much deliberation. Isn't it cool?

,No. I don't really think anything of it.

&gt;

Ignoring Kang Seok-su, who was proud, I checked the guild's information.

It was different from ordinary guilds.

The Seventier Guild is run jointly by seven guild masters. It wasn't unusual for multiple guild masters to run a guild simultaneously. In fact, there are many guilds run by two guild masters together.

But seven was excessively many.

,Seven guild masters. Is this even allowed? That's too many. There's a saying that too many cooks spoil the broth.

,There's one representative. The masters determine the representative every two years through meetings and voting. Originally, I didn't intend to do this either... Look at the master information.

Five masters had already been decided. One of them was, of course, Kang Seok-su.

I was surprised when I saw the names of the five. All the promising talents who had already made a name for themselves in South Korea were included. Two of them were women. Both were beautiful and famous for their outstanding skills as Hunters.

,...How did you manage to scout them?

,They were dissatisfied with the existing top 10 guilds. The top 10 guilds in South Korea have extremely strong territoriality. Well, even so, persuading them wasn't easy.

,You even have a sponsoring company.

,I'm not thrilled about it, but it couldn't be helped. We need capital to grow quickly. However, the sponsoring company won't be able to influence the guild internally.

I read all the information about the guild. To evaluate, it was ready to be founded right away. If it grows properly, there's a good chance it could take the place of a top 10 guild.

,...With this much, there's no reason to bring me in, right?

,The goal of the Seventier Guild is to become the best guild in South Korea. That's the only way we can change the existing top 10 guilds.

The best guild.

To reach that position, one condition must be met.

,...An S-grade Hunter.

,That's right. An S-grade Hunter must exist to aim for the best guild position. And I predict that you will become an S-grade Hunter in the future. Of course, I will also become an S-grade Hunter.

Kang Seok-su spoke arrogantly. I didn't argue since the possibility wasn't completely nonexistent.

I knew what the condition for being a Seventier Guild Master was. They were all outstanding prospects, people who were said to potentially become S-grade Hunters in the future.

,...You're serious, aren't you.

,I've never not been serious. Seong Yujin. I also know that since I've been hunting without a guild, it's slowly becoming difficult starting from B-grade. It's true that it's hard to find dungeons above C-grade without the help of a guild right now. More than anything, the existence of a trustworthy comrade will be crucial.

He's right.

There were several moments of crisis while conquering C-grade dungeons. Crises that wouldn't have been a big deal if I had a trustworthy comrade.

That's why I try to enter the easiest and safest dungeons possible.

,Seong Yujin. Let's work together. If it's us, we can change the world. No, we will change it.

Kang Seok-su extended his hand to me. I pretended not to see his hand and turned my gaze away. I skimmed through the information about the Seventier Guild once more.

,...Give me time to think.

Kang Seok-su smiled and lowered his hand. He seemed satisfied just by the fact that I didn't reject him immediately.

,I can't wait long. There are only two remaining spots in Seventier.

&gt;

,It doesn't necessarily have to be seven spots, does it.

,We've decided on seven for the master positions. This will never change in the future. More than anything, Eightier is a bit weird, isn't it.

,It is weird.

•••

As the vanguard, I entered the dungeon first.

I felt my vision spin momentarily. And then, I felt a sense of incongruity.

,...It's different from usual? It feels like the dungeon itself is shaking....

I've entered dungeons hundreds of times. There's no way I could mistake that familiar sensation. This dungeon is alien.

Hwiyu uuuuuung.

A chilly wind blew. When I raised my head, I saw a gloomy sky. I blankly looked around. I was alone in a field. There was no one around. Normally, other party members should have appeared within 3 seconds.

,Do we have to conquer it separately? That's the worst. We definitely agreed to meet in the west if we got separated, right? Where's the west.

&lt;Find the Rainbow Fruit.&gt;

The dungeon informed me of the conquest condition. It was a fruit with a name I had never heard before.

&lt;You are a farmer.&gt;

&lt;If you don't do farm work for more than 3 hours a day, you will weaken.&gt;

A pitchfork and a straw hat appeared before my eyes.

,This is driving me crazy. What is this now.

I scratched my cheek. Even though closed dungeons are a bit bizarre, I had never heard of a dungeon like this.

,Should I call it a new form of closed dungeon?

It was disconcerting, but it was possible. There are dungeons like 'Hero's Whirlwind' that train Hunters.

First, I put the straw hat on my head and picked up the pitchfork.

,Do farm work? Bullshit.

I needed to see if tricks would work or not. It's not like I'll die if I don't do farm work.

,I have Complete Recovery and Heavenly Heart. Status ailments are nothing to me.

I looked around, searching for the west.

The sun rises in the east and sets in the west. So, the direction the sun sets is the west.

,If this isn't a dungeon, then that's probably the west. Well, even if that's not the west, the others will probably assume the direction the sun sets is the west, so it doesn't matter.

I threw the pitchfork away and walked towards the direction the sun was setting.

,Hey, you rascal!!

I heard an old woman's voice from behind. I turned around. A woman in her sixties was running furiously towards me. Most of her hair was gray, and her face was full of wrinkles. She only had about 6-7 teeth left, and even those were yellow and didn't look to be in good condition. She was a hag that Seong Jigone would not be able to stop his erection from if he saw her.

The hag, with the momentum of her run, smacked me on the head. It didn't hurt. It was just unpleasant. When I frowned, the hag got angry instead.

,Hans, you rascal! Are you trying to abandon the field again?! You rascal! When are you ever going to come to your senses!

,Hag. Are you crazy?

,Hag? You have no respect for your mother! You rascal!

The hag swung her fist again. The last time I got hit was because I didn't expect her to actually hit me, but this time was different. I lightly twisted my body to dodge the punch and extended my foot to trip the hag. The hag fell face-first into the damp, muddy field.

,Heok!

I put my foot on the back of the hag's head. The hag struggled to get up, flailing her arms and legs. I stomped on the hag's head a few times before slightly reducing the force.

,Ha, Hans! Why, why are you doing this...?! Help me up. Your mother is too tired....

The hag said in a tearful voice.

,Hag. I'm not Hans. My mom is definitely not a hag.

,Wh, what are you suddenly saying... Pueok!

I stomped on the hag's head again. This time, harder than before. It seemed like dirt got into the hag's ears, but I didn't care.

I took the force off my foot. The hag hurriedly lifted her head. She gasped for air with her face covered in dirt. The hag trembled and looked at me cautiously.

,Ha, Hans. Did this mother of yours do something wrong to you...?

,I said I'm not Hans. Seriously.

,Hiiik!

I stomped on her head a few more times. It didn't take long for the hag's attitude to change.

,I'm not Hans.

,Ye, yes. That's right. You are not Hans...!

,Hag. Where's the Rainbow Fruit?

I asked without much expectation.

,Rainbow Fruit...? Are you talking about the legendary Rainbow Fruit?

,Tsk. Your tone.

,Ra, Rainbow Fruit, you say?

,Yeah. Rainbow Fruit.
