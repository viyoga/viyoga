Riding in the car, I used my Spirit Eye and looked out the window. Among the winds swirling through the city, I could see a streak of foreign wind.

Jet-black wind.

It was a wind moving towards the Wind Demon Spirit. They say that Wind Spirits create wind just by existing, so it seems Demon Spirits aren't any different.

"Turn left there."

"Yes."

Sarah, who was at the wheel, answered briefly. Han Sohee-nuna, sitting in the passenger seat, kept checking on me through the rearview mirror, looking constantly anxious.

"Yujin-ah. Why don't you turn back now?"

"Sohee-nuna. No matter how much you say that, it's already too late. If I turn back here, what will happen to the people following behind?"

I gestured towards the rear of the car. There was a car chasing after the one I was in. It was a car carrying heroes to eliminate the Demon Spirit.

Fools wasting their time.

My goal is to directly kill the Demon Spirit and raise my 'Good Karma' stat, but their goal is to kill the Demon Spirit before it harms people. The Demon Spirit isn't completely corrupted and is self-destructing, so it will disappear on its own even if the heroes don't step in.

"......Yujin-ah. Just tell them the location and come back. Don't get involved in anything dangerous."

"I know, I know. The promise was just to tell them the location, right?"

I nonchalantly spat out a lie. Mocha has recovered too, and I need to land at least one hit on that Demon Spirit.

The car left the city and started driving across a flat plain. Unlike South Korea, there wasn't a single mountain in sight. It was a vast land without even a hill.

"Um, Yujin-nim. Is this the right direction?"

Sarah asked cautiously.

"You think I'm leading you in the wrong direction? You don't trust me?"

"Ah, no. It's not that, it's just a bit unexpected...."

"This is the right way. The Demon Spirit's wind is heading this way."

"......Yes."

Sarah answered, sounding unconvinced.

Seeing that lackluster reaction, it seems there's something up ahead.

'When I looked from afar, there wasn't anything special. Is something hidden?'

The car drove for 20 minutes before stopping.

"......Is it here? It certainly is windier than other places."

"It's up ahead. But Sarah-nuna. You're surprised by something else, aren't you?"

"No, it's just...."

"Is it because it's the dungeon your mom went into?"

"...You noticed?"

"I didn't know when I looked from afar, but I can tell now that I'm closer. I can see the space is distorted."

"The Spirit Eye is truly amazing."

To my eyes, the space looks distorted. It's not a proper entrance shape. That's only natural since the dungeon is closed.

'Normal dungeons don't have closed entrances. This is a special dungeon.'

I got out of the car.

A surprised Han Sohee-nuna followed me out and grabbed my arm.

"Yujin-ah. This is it, right? Let's go back now. We've done what we needed to do."

"Sohee-nuna. I'm curious about how heroes fight. Let's watch. Huh?"

"No way. Get back in the car."

"Just a little bit. Just watch a little bit."

While I was arguing with Han Sohee-nuna, the car following us stopped and the heroes got out. A total of 5 heroes. Two of them were said to be A-rank heroes. Maybe it's because it's America, but they're all wearing skintight suits. Two of them even wore masks.

"I don't see the Demon Spirit, where is it?"

The man in the black skintight suit asked me. He was the man who introduced himself as Black Stone when we first met.

"It's over there."

I pointed to the top of the dungeon with my finger. The Demon Spirit wasn't moving from there. Perhaps due to the dungeon's influence, the Demon Spirit was disappearing more slowly than when I saw it yesterday. It seems it came here seeking the most comfortable place for itself.

"This used to be the location of an S-rank dungeon.... Did the Demon Spirit sense something?"

Black Stone seemed to sense something. When he raised his hand, the heroes each took out their weapons and assumed combat stances.

"Yujin-ah! Our work is done! Let's go back!"

"Just a little bit. Just watch a little bit."

Sohee-nuna grabbed my arm and pulled, but I held my ground.

Mocha is flying in the sky. He's biding his time, waiting for my command to strike the Demon Spirit with lightning.

"Even though it's not visible, I can feel its presence. It's somewhat fortunate that it's far from the city. Let's begin. For the sake of the people, the Demon Spirit must be killed here."

Black Stone moved first. He created a spear carved from black rock from the ground and threw it at the Demon Spirit. It wasn't an ordinary spear. I could feel a slight aura similar to a spirit emanating from the spear.

'That spear could damage even spirits in their incorporeal state. Truly an A-rank hero.'

However, the spear didn't reach the Demon Spirit. A sudden, powerful whirlwind blew the rock spear away.

The Demon Spirit, which had been still, moved. The Demon Spirit looked at me and spoke.

-Go back. If you go back now, I won't do anything to you. I will soon disappear. I just want to enjoy this wind a little more before I disappear.

I heard the Demon Spirit's voice. And I wasn't the only one who heard it.

"We can't leave a dangerous guy like you near the city. Corrupted spirit.... Before you harm people, we will eliminate the seeds of unrest here."

-You don't know how precious your lives are.

Wind blew around the Demon Spirit. Dust soared upwards and swirled. I stepped back, and Han Sohee-nuna and Sarah stayed close to my side.

Han Sohee-nuna used a barrier to protect me and Sarah. She is one of the top defensive heroes in the Korean Hero Association.

"The Demon Spirit has materialized! This is our chance!"

"Wait. Spirits can't materialize, can they?"

"Spirits above a certain level can materialize with their own power. They just don't do it because it's inefficient. But Demon Spirits are different. They can materialize with high efficiency. But they can't return to the Spirit Realm. Its materialization is our chance."

The heroes kicked off the ground and jumped into the wind. They moved like clockwork, as if they had worked together many times.

But the Demon Spirit wasn't easy. It was a Demon Spirit exerting power by burning its own existence. That power is not something even A-rank heroes can easily handle.

'But it's only a matter of time. Mocha. Get ready. As soon as you see an opportunity, drop the lightning.'

I watched their battle with shining golden eyes. The most noticeable was Black Stone. Creating black rocks from the ground, he blocked all of the Demon Spirit's sharp winds. He was the center of this battle.

'Good. Right now... uh?'

One of the five heroes made a sudden move. It was a man in his early to mid-30s with an X-shaped scar on his mouth. The man with dark circles under his eyes ran towards us, not the Demon Spirit, and began to attack.

Kwaang!

His fist slammed into Han Sohee-nuna's barrier. It was already weakened from blocking the Demon Spirit's attacks. His attack cracked it. Sweat beaded on Han Sohee-nuna's forehead.

"Norden! What are you doing?!"

Sarah shouted in dismay. Her rapier was pointed directly at Norden's head.

Norden grinned. He pulled his fist back and hammered the barrier again. The cracks in the barrier grew larger and larger. Sweat beaded on Han Sohee-nuna's forehead.

"It's nothing much. Orders came from above, and I'm just following them."

"Above? Don't tell me, you're from Guirak Valley...?!"

Sarah was horrified.

"To avenge Wang Sien-nim and get rid of a troublesome brat at the same time. Die for the future of Guirak Valley."

Kwaang!

The barrier was on the verge of breaking.

I frowned. If Guirak Valley was trying to take revenge, they would have sent people with more determination than this. Guirak Valley's goal is to get rid of Norden.

"Norden! You won't get your way!"

Sarah tensed her body. She was planning to attack Norden as soon as the barrier broke.

Norden gritted his teeth and threw a punch.

Kwaaaang!

The barrier shattered, and Norden charged towards me with a fierce wind. Sarah's rapier pierced Norden's chest. But Norden's legs didn't stop.

"Even if I die. I'll take you with me. For Guirak Valley!"

"C-Crazy. What the hell is Guirak Valley!"

Sarah's rapier pierced his neck. Even this time, Norden didn't stop. His fist was pulled far back. Woooong. I could feel the flow of mana from his fist.

"Guirak Valley will bring me back to life in this world! I'm looking forward to that time!"

"Yujin-ah!"

Han Sohee-nuna rushed to hug me and protect me.

'Instant.'

Time slows down. I avoid Han Sohee-nuna and jump upwards.

'Instant. Heavenly Heart.'

I use Instant again and strengthen my body with Heavenly Heart. Thanks to the effects of Heavenly Heart, my physical abilities will skyrocket for the next minute, and no other force can stop me. That includes the fierce wind.

I dodge Norden's fist by a hair's breadth and punch him in the face.

Paaang!

"Keueok?!"

Perhaps because I contained too much mana, my right fist exploded from the impact. Blood splattered from me and him. I grabbed the forehead of Norden, who couldn't come to his senses.

'Lightning.'

Pajijijijijijijijijik!

The opponent is a B-rank hero. I won't let my guard down. Now that I've seized the opportunity, I must kill him with all my might.

"Kkeureureureureureureu!"

The blue lightning flickered so brightly that it burned Norden's entire body.

When I let go, his brain had already been burned out, and his body was on fire.

"Y-Yujin-ah!"

"Yujin-nim!"

Han Sohee-nuna and Sarah came to me and hugged me. It could be called excessive, but the scene unfolding before my eyes left them no choice. The Demon Spirit was preparing to create a giant tornado, resigned to self-destruction. If it weren't for the barrier that Han Sohee-nuna used again, I and they would have been blown into the sky.

'That bastard really has nothing but death on his mind. Mocha! Lightning!'

Lightning struck from the sky, piercing the giant wind and hitting the Demon Spirit. The Demon Spirit, struck by lightning, staggered and fell downwards.

The direction of the winds that were trying to create a tornado changed. They were all being sucked into the Demon Spirit. I clearly landed the final blow, but it feels even more dangerous.

'Shit. Did something go wrong?'

I have a feeling it's going to explode.

-This is the end. A peaceful end was not allowed for me.

The wind that was being sucked into the Demon Spirit stopped. The area around it momentarily became a vacuum.

-If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have been able to achieve my revenge. I feel gratitude. I'll give this to you.

Kwaaaaaaaaang

With a deafening roar, wind rushed in all directions. The barrier that Han Sohee-nuna had erected shook and shattered.

I fixed my eyes on the front. Something was flying towards me. Judging from Han Sohee-nuna and Sarah's expressions, it seemed like only I could see it.

I grabbed what was flying towards me with my hand. It was a translucent bead. More than half of it was stained black.

'Is this a spirit?'

Spirit Core. The source of the power that a spirit possesses, and the source of its existence.

Even if the spirit body disappears, the spirit can recover in the Spirit Realm if the Spirit Core is safe.

'But this is just an empty shell. It's like only the corpse is left. I can't feel any of the spirit's power.'

Even if I take it to the Spirit Realm, the Wind Spirit won't be able to recover. In the first place, more than half of it is corrupted, so the Spirit Realm wouldn't accept it.

'Let's think about it later. Right now, I need to think about getting out of here.'

I put the Spirit Core in my pocket.

The heroes are caught in the wind and blown far away.

"Y-Yujin-ah! This is the limit! Sarah-ssi! Hold onto Yujin tightly!"

"Yes!"

Two beauties grabbed me tightly from both sides. The barrier shattered. We thought we would be blown backwards like the other heroes. But that wasn't the case. Holding up the barrier and enduring it had actually become a poison. The wind pulled us not outwards, but inwards.

As a result of looking with the Spirit Eye, there was a crack inside.

'It doesn't make sense for a spatial rift to suddenly occur!'

Suddenly, something flashed through my mind. The place where the Demon Spirit was located was where the S-rank dungeon was located. The entrance was definitely closed, but the Demon Spirit's attack, which was no different from a suicide attack, had definitely caused a crack in the dungeon.

'Taking out the spatial movement scroll is... Damn it. It's too late.'

We were already caught in the wind and entering the dungeon. Feeling like we were falling endlessly, I raised my head. Mocha flapped his white wings and followed behind me.
