Three years had passed. The opening of the Secret Realm was close at hand.

The ten who would enter the Secret Realm had been chosen long ago.

First, myself—the master of the Heavenly Demon Cult. Miryeong, the sorceress of the Harmony Realm. Two Harmony Realm cultivators.

The Right Protector, Sword Demon Yeon Seorang. The cult's elders, Yud Yeoryeo—the Bewitching Poison Demon Consort—and Danmok Soha, the Traceless Ghost Consort. Three Unity Realm cultivators.

And five talents of the Five Qi Realm—Yeon Yeh and Seomun Soryeo among them—the future of the Heavenly Demon Cult, its rising generation.

Bringing Five Qi Realm talents along was an investment in itself. The Secret Realm's peculiar environment was immensely conducive to advancing one's cultivation. On top of that, the Secret Realm was fundamentally a place rich in spiritual energy.

'Objectively speaking, more than half the Heavenly Demon Cult's fighting strength is being committed to this Secret Realm.'

The current Heavenly Demon Cult had no successor to the Heavenly Demon.

If I died in the Secret Realm, the Heavenly Demon Cult would collapse.

'The truce with the Martial Alliance holds for another seven years. As long as no other faction makes a move, nothing much will happen for those seven years.'

The Bloodthirsty Star, an ally of the Heavenly Demon Cult, would lend aid if things came to that. Unless it were a force on the level of the Bloodthirsty Star—or the Four Kings Alliance of the unorthodox sects—none would dare look down on the Heavenly Demon Cult.

'The Four Kings Alliance of today is not the Four Kings Alliance of old. For seven years, there will be no problem. The problem is if I fail to return within seven years. The moment the truce ends, the Martial Alliance could attack.'

The Martial Alliance and the Heavenly Demon Cult, both without a supreme master. Which of the two was stronger? Originally, one would say the Heavenly Demon Cult, but… after a large-scale purge, only about thirteen years had passed. The Heavenly Demon Cult was at a disadvantage.

'Should I have made the truce thirty years instead of ten? …No. As long as I return within seven years, that's enough.'

It was too late to change the plan now. We push ahead as is.

We arrived in Henan in time for the day the Secret Realm would open. Henan was so crowded with martial artists it resembled a festival. Word that the Secret Realm would open in Henan had spread throughout the entire murim. Not only Martial Alliance members, but martial artists from the outer murim and even the unorthodox sects had gathered.

'Those Martial Alliance bastards. They deliberately gathered everyone here.'

Just by rough sense, there were tens of thousands of martial artists. Of them, several dozen were absolute masters who had reached the Unity Realm. It was the first time I learned there were this many absolute masters in the murim.

"Kkeueung. There are Buddha statues everywhere."

The Right Protector, Sword Demon Yeon Seorang, let out a groan. Just as he said, stone-carved Buddha statues stood here and there. They weren't ordinary statues either. Each held a faint trace of demon-subduing power. Those who had mastered demonic-path arts found them unpleasant just to look at. I, of course, was unaffected.

"Henan is heavily influenced by Buddhism because of Shaolin. It's a petty trick—they knew we were coming and prepared in advance. Endure it."

I told him to endure, but simply enduring is not my style.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art. Heavenly Demon Lord's Descent Step. Waveform.

I gathered power in my right foot and took a single step.

A wave of demonic energy rippled out in all directions.

Jjeojeok!

More than dozens of Buddha statues cracked and split. The feeble demon-subduing power dwelling within them perished without even managing to resist. In the process, some faint-hearted martial artists fainted on the spot. Yet they did not dare protest to me. They simply pretended not to notice and put distance between us.

The majority were wanderers belonging to no famous sect.

'They placed a limit of ten on our Heavenly Demon Cult, yet allowed those rabble in without restriction? Those Martial Alliance bastards. They're determined to provoke me. Or they have some other scheme.'

The martial artists who had distanced themselves from us whispered quietly, thinking we couldn't hear.

"So the rumor that the Heavenly Demon himself would enter this Secret Realm was true."

"Was that the famous Heavenly Demon Lord's Descent Step just now? It felt like my mind and body were being crushed. If I were even a little weaker… I'd have fainted like those others."

"The Heavenly Demon Cult. Best not to get entangled with them."

I can hear everything.

These insolent wretches daring to casually speak my name puts me in a foul mood.

'Should I raise the intensity and stomp once more.'

Just as I was about to subtly lift my right foot—

Dozens of figures dropped down before me.

They were muscular monks with shaved heads and light monk's robes. On their foreheads were red dots—the precept marks.

Nineteen in total.

'Those muscular baldies must be the famous Eighteen Arhats of Shaolin. And the old man at the center is—'

An old man with a white beard, in contrast to his shaved head. His muscular body had not withered in the least despite his age. Prayer beads were wound around his body and arms, and his eyes were hidden by extremely long white eyebrows, making his pupils barely visible.

Fist Buddha.

The abbot of Shaolin Temple and one of the supreme masters of the orthodox faction. He was also famous as a warrior monk who had mastered Shaolin's Hundred-Step Divine Fist.

'Wasn't he known to be at the upper tier of the Unity Realm? Seeing him in person, he's reached the Harmony Realm. Did he attain enlightenment recently and ascend to the Harmony Realm? Why isn't the Heavenly Demon Cult's intelligence division bringing in important information like this? Incompetent bastards.'

I could feel demon-subduing power from Fist Buddha. An aura like the natural enemy of demonic arts. Even for me, Fist Buddha was not an opponent I could simply dismiss.

"Heavenly Demon. Stop where you are."

"How unfair. What have I done? I merely walked."

"There is no one here who does not know the Heavenly Demon Lord's Descent Step, your signature technique. If your walking is the problem, then do not walk. This is Henan. A place Shaolin protects. Consider also your agreement with the Martial Alliance."

Beneath Fist Buddha's eyebrows, golden light gleamed. As he roused his inner energy, the demon-subduing power grew strong. The Heavenly Demon Divine Art was feeling displeasure.

"Well now. To think I can't even walk properly. I'll be careful."

For now, I step back.

The moment I fought Fist Buddha, it would mean immediate war with the Martial Alliance. I wouldn't even be able to enter the Secret Realm. Already, Martial Alliance members were gradually gathering around us.

At my words, Fist Buddha's aura diminished.

"Fist Buddha. Are you entering the Secret Realm as well?"

"I am not."

"I see."

'Am I fucked?'

If Fist Buddha attacked the Heavenly Demon Cult while I was gone, it would be the end. As long as the ten-year truce held, there would be no problem, but—

'Seven years from now. If the truce ends and I'm still not back, the Martial Alliance will strike the Heavenly Demon Cult first.'

The time limit I had worried about had been imposed.

"The Secret Realm will open soon. Less than an hour remains. Do not cause trouble until then. Henan is where Shaolin stands."

After that stern warning, Fist Buddha disappeared with the Eighteen Arhats.

"That baldy monk is quite arrogant. Shall I throw a curse at him?"

Miryeong, beside me, stuck out her tongue at the departing Fist Buddha.

"Forget it. Fighting Fist Buddha now would gain us nothing. Besides, he won't be as easy as you think."

I turned my head and glared at one side.

Far off, I could see the Martial Alliance Leader and his elders.

Wang Jiyuk—the current Martial Alliance Leader, and formerly the leader of the Beggar's Sect, which had been officially annihilated. At his waist, over his splendid martial robes, hung nine knots signifying the Beggar's Sect leadership. As if to say the Beggar's Sect still remained.

Our eyes met. I felt hatred in that gaze. I openly sneered at him, then turned my eyes away. I had no desire to keep looking at an old man.

I surveyed the surroundings. Famous masters were gathering one by one.

'Surprisingly few clan forces. The most famous one here is the Huangbo Clan, I suppose. Huangbo Jiahui. It's been a while.'

Huangbo Jiahui. The current head of the Huangbo Clan, which dominated Shandong Province.

In the past she was called the Shandong Jewel, but now she was called the Shandong Fist Empress. Unmarried, though.

Her eyes met mine, and she flinched in surprise before bowing her head slightly.

Incidentally, a seal called the Soul-Binding Fate Seal was placed on her lower abdomen. If she still hadn't broken it by now, her life was no different from being in the palm of my hand.

I couldn't acknowledge knowing her here, so I turned my gaze away immediately.

'Baek Muhan. That one came too.'

The protagonist of the original work, [The Fallen Heavenly Demon]. He was also the Reincarnated Heavenly Demon. Because of the redemption system the King of Hell had bestowed upon him, he couldn't do evil deeds. I'd met him before and had been ignoring him since, but it seemed he was now operating as a wanderer.

'Oh?'

There was an unusual group.

People with snow-white hair, wearing thick clothes unsuited to the warm weather.

'The North Sea Ice Palace. That white-haired woman at the center has particularly outstanding beauty. The power I sense from her is that of a Unity Realm absolute master. The North Sea Ice Palace's Little Palace Lord—Bing Soha, the Carefree Cold Woman. To think I'd see a woman so rarely seen here.'

The moniker "Carefree Cold Woman" was a bit grating, but there were no particular bad rumors about Bing Soha. Though that was partly because the North Sea Ice Palace was in a place no different from the countryside, so rumors simply didn't reach it.

'There were rumors that the Carefree Cold Woman possessed outstanding beauty.'

Those rumors were true.

Beyond her, other famous masters were visible, but none enough to draw my attention.

A little more time passed.

Kugugugugugugung.

The spiritual energy of heaven and earth began to churn.

A great roar resounded from earth and sky. Everyone watched tensely as the spiritual energy of heaven and earth surged violently. Before long, the churning energy compressed and gathered into a single point in the air, becoming one massive gate. A gate over fifty meters tall. It was the gate leading to the Secret Realm.

The first to move were the Martial Alliance's martial artists. In perfect unison, they surrounded the gate and even erected a barrier. Then they began controlling the people nearby.

"Only those with permission from the Martial Alliance or the imperial court may enter the Secret Realm. Those without permits will be punished!"

In the meantime, the Martial Alliance Leader opened the massive gate and entered the Secret Realm.

I led my group and strode confidently toward the gate. The Martial Alliance's martial artists could not block our path.
