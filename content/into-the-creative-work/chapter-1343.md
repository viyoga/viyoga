I crossed my arms and stared at the empty field.

The entire mountain disappeared, clearing my view. The sky, darkened by the Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons), was clear again, and the sun shone dazzlingly.

,This is the scene I created.'

An extraordinary power that obliterates an entire mountain!

Noecheonryu (Thunder Sky Style) Ultimate Skill, Noecheonmyeong (Thunder Sky Command).

Of course, this power was difficult to use in reality. Because my real-world specs can't keep up with my current specs.

,I don't think I'll never be able to use this technique again. If I become an S-class... I might be able to use it?'

I couldn't be sure.

But I will become stronger than S-class. It's definitely possible if I have the Game Life App.

,So I have to remember this feeling now.'

Noecheonmyeong, the ultimate skill I created, wasn't really a technique. Its power was great, but its structure was simple.

,The principle is Noecheonryu (Thunder Sky Style) Noemyeong (Thunder Cry), a technique that resonates two or more thunderbolts.'

It's not just resonating. There's a condition that you must perfectly control the thunderbolts you're resonating. Based on reality, the limit of Noemyeong is roughly six thunderbolts. If I push myself, I might be able to resonate eight thunderbolts.

But Noecheonmyeong resonates thousands of lightning bolts from the sky. Control is naturally impossible. All I can do is set the direction.

,Since there are so many thunderbolts, if I get the start right, they'll resonate on their own even if I don't control them.'

The principle of Noecheonmyeong is simple.

And there was also a simple problem.

,Energy. The problem is the energy to generate thousands of lightning bolts. And the fact that once resonance starts, it can't be reversed? That's not much of a problem.'

Can an S-class Hunter use Noecheonmyeong with their mana? I'm skeptical. The reason I could use Noecheonmyeong now was because the ghastly energy generated by the deaths of hundreds of ghosts filled the space.

,But if I reduce the resonating thunderbolts, the power won't come out properly... and the resonance might fail halfway through in the first place. Is the answer just to raise my mana stat like crazy?'

I think carefully. Noecheonmyeong is the first ultimate skill of Noecheonryu I created, so I'm attached to it.

At that moment, I saw a cloud slowly approaching from far away in the blue sky.

,Cloud... right. If I use thunderclouds, I can use Noecheonmyeong without consuming a lot of mana. But that kind of environment isn't always readily available. So, if I had the power to control clouds... no, the weather, it would be convenient... um?'

I felt something leaving my head. No, it's not just a feeling. Something black was actually leaving from between my eyebrows. It takes shape in front of me, about 5m away.

A muscular man with long black hair.

His fangs protruded from his lips like Dracula. His skin is a blackish gray. His sclera is black, and his irises are red. The eyes of a demon, as they say. He's holding a sword in his right hand. It looks exactly like the Chamgwido I'm holding.

"You, are you Dojin?"

"That's right."

"What, you could come out?"

"I've only temporarily materialized using ghastly energy. My real soul is still in your mind."

"Have you given up on your attachment to my body?"

"Don't be ridiculous. I created that body. I used the ghastly energy I collected and collected when I was trapped in that damn jar to transform myself!"

"It's true that my body has gotten a bit better. But my body is mine."

I had no intention of handing my body over to Dojin. My body is mine. I have full control. That's why Dojin materialized using ghastly energy.

"...No, wait. You materialized using my ghastly energy without my permission."

"Your ghastly energy? That's absurd. That ghastly energy is mine too."

"Bullshit."

I checked my body condition.

Ghastly energy has decreased by about 10%.

,So the ghastly energy that bastard can use is about 10%.'

Dojin swings his sword in the air. His eyes are full of fighting spirit and murderous intent as he looks at me.

"For the time being, I'll hand over the control of the body to you. But I will definitely take back my body again someday."

"So you're saying you'll keep sticking with me."

"Why? Did you expect me to leave?"

"No, I'm relieved. If you leave, I can't use ghastly energy."

Ghastly energy is the power of ghosts.

Only ghosts can use it. If Dojin disappears from my body, I'll just become a human with a good body.

,Now that I know how convenient ghastly energy is... do you think I'll let it go?'

Honestly, it's extremely unpleasant for a guy to be parasitizing my mind. But it's unavoidable if I want to use ghastly energy.

"Seong Yujin-ssi. You better be nervous. I will analyze you and become stronger inside you. And someday, when this body surpasses you... that body will be mine again."

"That'll never happen in your life."

Ha, I wonder how long that composure will last.

Dojin takes a stance.

A stance with the center of gravity slightly lowered.

It was a familiar stance to me.

It's the stance taken when using Noecheonryu (Thunder Sky Style) Noegwang (Thunder Light). Of course, Noecheonryu is a martial art that isn't greatly affected by stances once you reach a certain level, so the stance isn't that important. The important thing is that he imitated Noecheonryu.

Pajik, pajijik.

Lightning sparks from Dojin's sword. It meant he perfectly understood Noegwang.

"Well, I'll spar with you about once a month."

I was still relaxed.

Just because Dojin uses Noecheonryu doesn't mean he'll become the same as me. The performance of the body, the total amount of thunder energy. I overwhelm him in all of that. It's like Dojin, who's only level 10 in a game, is fighting me, who's level 100.

"Listen carefully. It's a one-hit kill. If you're careless, even you will suffer a fatal wound."

"Careless? You're the one who'll be careless."

Kiing.

Noecheonryu (Thunder Sky Style) Noegwang (Thunder Light).

I passed by Dojin and swung my sword. Dojin hurriedly swung his sword at me to counterattack. Two lightning trajectories intersect in the air.

The winner was naturally my Noegwang. Dojin, greatly cut, scatters blood everywhere and falls to the floor. I said without looking back.

"You're slow... even falling."

"Keueoeok... you bastard...!"

"Don't use such strong words, you look weak."

"Someday... definitely...!!"

While listening to Dojin's resentful voice, I hurriedly moved my ghastly energy. Ghastly energy is visible on my chest. There was a slight cut on Dojin.

,Shibal. How embarrassing. I can't let this be discovered.'

I used a lot of ghastly energy to increase my recovery power, and the wound disappeared without a trace.

"Anyway, as long as you're parasitizing my mind... you must absolutely follow my orders."

"...The logic of power, huh. I can't help it. I'll play along with you for the time being...."

Dojin's materialized body turned into ghastly energy and disappeared.

Shin Ojeong-ssi. This guy's reactions are quite fun. Not only that, but he doesn't cross the line.

Pajijijik.

I created lightning on my palm. Shin Ojeong-ssi's eyes widened. He repeated deep breaths and quietly muttered.

"Lightning Fairy Seong Yujin...!"

"This bastard."

He's suddenly crossing the line.

Ppak!

I listened with one ear and let it out with the other. The reason I had been exorcising ghosts was to lower my ghastly energy level to deal with the Snow Woman. But now that I've gained the power of ghosts, there's no need to bother wandering around for exorcism.

I crossed the schoolyard and immediately turned towards Seol Jiyoung-ssi's class.

"Is Jiyoung-sunbae (senior) here?"

"Jiyoung-ssi? She hasn't come yet. She usually comes to school first... she's a bit late today."

She's not late, she hasn't come.

She probably played some tricks.

I smiled coldly and turned around.

The reason I bothered to come to school and look for Seol Jiyoung-ssi was to give the Snow Woman one last chance.

,Because the Snow Woman was a beauty in the original work.'

So I gave her a chance.

If she had given up her ambition and returned to her daily life, I would have quietly let her go.

,So she's determined to try me. Well, it's obvious where she'll be.'

I left the school and headed to the abandoned hospital.

I feel much more ghastly energy than when I came last time. What does this mean?

,She's gathered all the ghosts in this neighborhood. Is it a last resistance? Ah, does she know that I dealt with the Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons)?'

Perhaps the Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons) was also the Snow Woman's doing. The Snow Woman's origin is Japan, after all.

,Maybe the Snow Woman gave the Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons) a hint, so the Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons) tried to kill me because of the prophecy.'

Well, it doesn't matter now. The Hyakki Yagyō (Night Parade of One Hundred Demons) is dead, and the Snow Woman can't handle me.

I took out the Chamgwido I had brought and entered the abandoned hospital.
