With Ban Oh and Sanwang dead, there was no one to stop me. I killed off the rest of the riffraff too. I did allow Sanwang a blow, but it wasn't a serious enough injury to die from. I did vomit blood about three times, but I was fine. Now, all that's left is to climb to the top of Domyungsan Mountain.

We approached the entrance to the summit.

Only three can ascend to the summit. There were four of us gathered here. But no problem. The Guihon Heuksu that Seong Jigone-ssi is holding is missing limbs and is out of his mind. We can just restrain him at the entrance and go up to the summit.

The five-colored clouds blocking the summit have also become so faint that it's hard to distinguish the colors now.

,Milyeong-ssi. What do you want that you came to Domyungsan Mountain? With the power you have and the strength of the Sama Clan, you can get almost anything."

Sama Milyeong looked around once and then looked up at the sky.

,Does Daehyeop-nim know about ascension?"

,Ascension. I think I've heard of it."

I looked at Seong Jigone-ssi. Seong Jigone-ssi shook his head as if he didn't know either.

Ascension. Rising into the sky. Many people, including myself, thought it was another term for achieving immortality (羽化登仙). But after researching ancient texts, I discovered that ascension and attaining immortality are different concepts. I want to understand what ascension is and how one can ascend.

,The fact that you, who came from the imperial palace's priesthood, don't know this information means it's not easily accessible."

,I don't know everything about the imperial palace just because I'm from there. There's no way I could know information that His Majesty the Emperor has forbidden."

,Ascension is information that the Emperor has forbidden?"

,Seeing as I couldn't find out, that must be the case. If not, the information has been restricted since before His Majesty the Emperor founded the nation...."

A story from before the founding of the nation.

That's probably a story from over a thousand years ago. A distant and uninteresting history story for me. I just nodded casually.

Before the five-colored clouds blocking the path completely disappeared, hidden figures jumped out.

They were people wearing White Tiger masks.

I frowned. I didn't notice them hiding at all. It means they've reached the pinnacle of stealth. Or there's another secret to their stealth.

I suddenly got annoyed. I thought it was over, but suddenly something happened.

,What the hell are you guys?"

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill). Cheonma Sword (Heavenly Demon Sword). Demon Wind.

I swung my sword. The strong energy became a black wind and flew towards the guys wearing White Tiger masks. The Demon Wind, which cuts just by touching it. It was impossible for those guys, who were at best at the level of the Ogi Merchant Group, to easily block the Demon Wind.

A golden light flickered in front of them.

The black wind touched the golden light. The direction of the black wind was forcibly turned and fell to the ground, then disappeared. As if suppressed by an invisible force.

I felt an unpleasant energy.

I glared behind the guys wearing White Tiger masks. The golden energy flickered and took on a shape.

It was Great Monk Myeonghon, sitting cross-legged in the air. A golden halo was shining brilliantly behind his head. His figure was like a Buddha statue.

I gritted my teeth. I felt irritated just looking at him. It's not exactly a mental attack or anything. It's like feeling disgusted when you see a cockroach. To be precise, it's similar to how my demonic energy reacts against Great Monk Myeonghon's energy.

,Didn't you say you wouldn't interfere in this?"

,That's what it was supposed to be, and that's what I was going to do. But, after seeing you, I changed my mind. Mara, you are extremely dangerous. You possess pure evil with no end in sight, and you've even obtained unparalleled divine skills. Moreover, haven't you received the power of the Heavenly Star, the best of all stars? I can see it. The world groaning because of you...!"

,He really thinks he's Buddha."

I operated the Cheonma Shingong and emitted demonic energy. My internal injuries worsened. It didn't matter. I have Complete Recovery left.

Golden light emanated from Great Monk Myeonghon and resisted the demonic energy. It's still an annoying energy.

,Mara, you must die here."

,Are you planning to kill me using those three masked guys? No, are they just meat shields?"

,They are the White Tiger Clan. Those who have been dedicating themselves to the world since ancient times. Even now, they are working to protect the world."

,White Tiger Clan? Ah, I remember. Do you know that the Blue Dragon Clan betrayed you?"

,Mara, don't try to deceive me with lies. The Blue Dragon Clan betrayed us? They are the ones who sacrificed themselves for the world. The protection of the Blue Dragon is guarding them. There is no betrayal or corruption."

Unlike Great Monk Myeonghon's firm words, the eyes of the guys wearing White Tiger masks, the only part of them that was revealed, were shaking like crazy. They know about the Blue Dragon Clan's betrayal.

Sama Milyeong said to me in a whisper.

,...Daehyeop-nim. Great Monk Myeonghon has mastered the Bulgwang Hangma Shingong (佛光降魔神功), one of Shaolin's ultimate techniques. As far as I know, it's the highest-ranking martial art in the Buddhist tradition and is the nemesis of demonic arts. I've heard that a former Cheonma died after being struck by the Bulgwang Hangma Shingong.

,Oh, really? That's possible. There were quite a few retards among the past Cheonmas. But I'm different from those guys."

I grabbed my sword and walked towards them. The guys wearing White Tiger masks tensed up and drew their swords.

,Little Cheonma. Please yield Domyungsan Mountain to us. As Great Monk Myeonghon said, we are working in the shadows for the peace of the world. All of this is for the peace of the world."

,Peace of the world my ass. It's not you guys who are protecting peace and order right now, it's the Emperor. You guys should clean up the Blue Dragon Clan as soon as possible."

,......Little Cheonma, everyone knows that your condition isn't perfect right now. Aren't you forcibly holding back vomiting blood even now?"

,Holding back vomiting blood? Ueeeeeeeeeek!"

I deliberately stimulated my blood points and vomited blood. Black, dead blood and clean, fresh blood mixed together and fell to the floor.

,It's true that that Bulgwang or whatever is a bit of a pain in the ass. Looking at that fake Buddha makes me feel like a disgusting bug is crawling around me."

,Gal! Demon, you will eventually repent and shed tears before Buddha!"

Great Monk Myeonghon shouted. I moved forward, taking the golden light emanating from him with my body.

I examined the appearance of the masked guys. There were almost no traces of battle. Maybe they came all the way here hand in hand with Great Monk Myeonghon from the beginning. From Great Monk Myeonghon's point of view, the strong candidates were all unappealing, like madmen, monsters, bandits, and Jiangshi necromancers.

,Those fucking bastards. Showing up at the end and trying to snatch what's mine? No way."

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功). Pacheonhwang (Breaking New Ground / Unprecedented).

I break my soul, and burn the broken soul as firewood.

My hair turns snow-white, and a huge demonic energy erupts from my body. Right now, I am a Cheonma who has surpassed the Cheonma, a Super Cheonma.

,This demonic bastard! Did you offer your soul to obtain the power to defy the heavens! But, it won't go as you wish. White Tiger Clan! Use it!"

,Daehyeop-nim!"

,Yujin-ah!"

Sama Milyeong and Seong Jigone-ssi were shocked. I spoke before they could do anything.

,I'll take care of it, so stay still. This is nothing."

As I moved forward, the guys wearing White Tiger masks flinched.

,To move even after being restrained by three Yeongbaek Stones...."

,Wh-what should we do?"

,Great Monk Myeonghon!!"

Great Monk Myeonghon, floating behind them, stared at me intently with half-closed eyes. His right hand moved slowly. At the same time as his finger went down, a golden Bulgwang came down from the sky and pressed down on my body.

Hangma Chokjiin (降魔觸地印). No demon can defeat the Bulgwang and will be defeated.

It feels like my whole body is burning.

That was all.

I unleashed all my power and moved forward.

My skin melted. Demonic energy stuck to my flesh and became my skin.

,Mara...!"

Great Monk Myeonghon's benevolent face contorted. I could feel fear through his eyes.

Cheonma Divine Skill (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

The guys wearing White Tiger masks vomited blood and collapsed. They trembled and soon met their deaths. I didn't even look at the corpses and approached Great Monk Myeonghon.

,You bastard!!"

Heavenly Demon Divine Skill (Cheonma Shingong). Heavenly Demon Divine Palm (Cheonma Sinjang).

Kwaaaaaang!

,No, it can't be! That I would be defeated by a Mara...!!"

,Die, monk."

,Working in the shadows my ass. They're just guys who live as they please."
