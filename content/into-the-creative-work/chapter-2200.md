Luke, the temporary leader of the expeditionary fleet of the Corporate Provisional Federation whose name cannot be revealed, chewed on the cigarette in his mouth.

Things had gone wrong.

Two existences that weren't in the report popped out, not just one.

One was a demon. A monster with a body of at least the 8th class. Fortunately, the demon seemed to have little intelligence compared to its powerful body that could withstand missiles without a problem.

The problem was the angel that popped out after the demon. A faceless angel with wings of lightning.

,Damn it. Even after searching through the information in my head, I don't know the origins of that demon and angel.'

Ordinary demons and angels are strongly influenced by the 72 demons and 72 angels. In other words, if you can determine their origins and find out their characteristics, you can exploit their weaknesses.

The problem is that there is no information on that angel and demon.

,The demon seems to be related to gluttony... A faceless angel. I don't know anything about that one. The strength and status of angels can be determined by the number of wings they have. But that angel's wings... are made of lightning, so it's hard to figure out how many pairs of wings it has.'
