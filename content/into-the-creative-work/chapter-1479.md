Using Full Recovery, I narrowed my brow and glared at the demon's corpse.

I tried to avoid using Full Recovery as much as possible. Because using Full Recovery would eliminate the energy of corruption that had permeated my body. That's what I thought. In reality, some of the energy of corruption did disappear.

,As soon as I used Full Recovery, the energy of corruption clung to me.'

In a way, it was obvious.

The energy of corruption looks like a curse, but its true nature is the souls of demons and the corrupted. Full Recovery is a skill that restores my body to a perfect state. It had no effect on purifying the souls of demons and the corrupted that clung to me.

,I'll return to the Sanctuary as is and purify the energy of corruption with a purification ritual to accumulate the energy of blessing. I can become stronger.'

I looked at the demon's corpse.

,I should take this too.'

It was the demon's corpse, with only its head and arms remaining. It wasn't a burdensome size. I picked up the demon's corpse and went back the way I came. I ran into paladins along the way.

,Paladin-isiyeo! The movements of the rats have suddenly slowed down, making them easier to deal with! You have slain the demon!'

,That you're holding... is a demon's corpse. It's a gruesome appearance.'

,Please return first. We will return after clearing the underground waterway.'

The paladins were enthusiastic. I nodded and said to them.

,Good work. The demon's minions are of no help to us. Burn them all as you see them.'

After that, I returned to the Sanctuary.

The Saintess was, as always, praying in front of the statue of the Goddess. She stood up when she saw me coming.

,You're always in the Sanctuary. Do you sleep in the Sanctuary too?'

,There's a room behind the Sanctuary. I take care of my sleep and meals there.'

,And the rest of the time you're praying?'

,I manage the Sanctuary. Only a minimal number of people are allowed to enter the Sanctuary... so I have to do all the minor tasks.'

,For example?'

,Replacing the candlesticks or managing the water quality of the holy water. I'm also in charge of cleaning the Sanctuary.'

The Saintess.

Compared to her position, the treatment she receives from the Order isn't as great as one might think. This is because the Saintess's role is tailored to assisting the Paladin.

I feel like she's a bird trapped in a cage, but she seems to be satisfied with her life.

,It might be much better than living a hard life outside.'

Knowing the conveniences of modern life, I can't understand her restrained life. I can't sympathize with her so carelessly.

,I see. Please perform the purification ritual.'

,Yes. Paladin-isiyeo.'

I take off my clothes and enter the sacred pond, lying down in it. The purification ritual washes away all the energy of corruption. I feel refreshed. I sat in the sacred pond for a while.

,Amelia.'

,Yes. What is it?'

,Is there any information about the next demon?'

,...Yes. Traces of a demon have been found in a small village east of Valentier.'

,I should go.'

,Right now? Please rest a little longer.'

,The thought of demons running rampant while I'm resting makes me sick. There's no time to rest.'

,I see...'

Looking at the Saintess, I'm reminded of the original game's content.

The NPC that the original game developers put the most effort into was the Saintess. The Saintess's modeling was, of course, top-notch, and her interactions were several times greater than other NPCs. In particular, there was even a favorability system.

Of course, the player can't know the Saintess's favorability. But if you talk to the Saintess often and give her a few gifts, you'll notice that her attitude becomes friendlier over time. Sometimes, she even gives the player gifts.

Her favor wasn't important because it didn't affect the main quest.

,Conversely, there's no need to maintain a tense relationship with the Saintess.'

As I was about to leave the Sanctuary, I took out a box containing several small candies from my inventory and handed it to her.

,Paladin-isiyeo. What is this?'

,It's candy.'

,Candy...?'

,It's something you eat.'

,It's a food I've never seen before. May I try one now?'

,Yes. Don't chew it, roll it around on your tongue and savor it.'

She opened the candy box. And was taken aback.

,It looks like a jewel. Is this really food?'

,Do I look like I'm lying?'

,I apologize. That was a slip of the tongue.'

She carefully picked up a candy and put it under her veil. The candy went into her mouth. Amelia's body stops. I couldn't tell what kind of expression she was making because she was wearing a veil. But seeing her stiff body, she seemed to be in shock.

,It's incredibly... sweet. This is the first time I've had such a sweet food.'

I recall the food, clothing, and shelter culture of this world. Civilization is not developing due to demons and the corrupted. It's worse than the Middle Ages.

,I don't think it's ordinary food. May I accept this?'

,It's food only for you. Don't report it to the Order and eat it alone. If the Order finds out... it'll be troublesome in many ways.'

,I understand. Thank you for giving me such food, Paladin-isiyeo.'

I left her behind and went to kill demons.
