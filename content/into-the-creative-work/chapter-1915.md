Professor Hemmington and Cliff gather the exploration team and the mercenaries.

Having heard that the ancient language written on the door had been deciphered, the members of the exploration team gathered with curiosity instead of doubt. The guard, Jin, approached them warily. It was his job to guard them, after all.

Professor Hemmington glanced at me and Cliff, then opened his mouth.

,To open that door, we need five people."

,Five people? Is that some kind of metaphor? What kind of metaphor could it be?"

,It's not a metaphor. It's literally five. You five, I mean."

Cliff throws a magic engineering bomb between them. I opened my Astral and used the barrier magic I had prepared in advance to cover Professor Hemmington and Cliff.

The exploration team members stared at the bomb. Their expressions showed curiosity instead of shock. They didn't realize what the magic engineering bomb was.

The only one who reacted was Jin. As soon as the bomb appeared, he quickly turned around and tried to get away from the spot.

Kwaaaaaang!

The bomb explodes. Its firepower was several times stronger than that of an ordinary grenade. Ordinary people were torn apart and turned into lumps of meat, and even mercenaries who had lived rough lives were fatally wounded and collapsed on the floor.

,Didn't die? Finish him off....'

It was the moment I was about to finish off the fallen Jin with magic. I sensed something. I knocked it away with telekinesis. It was a bullet. I glanced behind me and saw Josh pointing a pistol at my head. Then he put away the pistol and took out a long rifle from behind his back. He must be disappointed that the surprise attack didn't work.

,He's quick to grasp the situation.'

To be honest, I had been underestimating Josh. In this world where physical enhancement and magic coexist, the limitations of firearms are clear.

Josh bends his waist and knees, sits halfway down, and pulls the rifle trigger.

Tang! Tang! Tang!

With a heavy gunshot, bullets fly. I could feel mana from the bullets. Magic bullets.

[Magnetic]

I cast a level 3 electric-type application magic. Using magnetism, I naturally change the direction of the metal bullets. The bullets, which had circled once in the air, returned to Josh.

Josh immediately rolled on the ground to avoid the bullets and counterattacked with his pistol.

,He's wasting his time. As long as I have Magnetic, he can't kill me with bullets.'

I hesitated to use Magnetic. The bullets flying towards me were not metal. It was something silver that was cutting through the air.

,...Mana bullets? No, he compressed the wind and shot it as bullets.'

Even so, there's no way to control the wind with magnetism. I used a barrier.

Kwang! Kwang! Kwang!

The moment the bullets hit the barrier, they exploded. The barrier couldn't withstand the impact and shattered. Then, magic bullets and wind bullets mixed and flew towards me.

,If I use the fleeting moment, I can calculate and unfold the barrier's formula. But....'

If I focus on defense, I'll keep getting hit. The advantage of a gunner is that they can attack non-stop as long as they have bullets.

,I need to kill Josh as quickly as possible because I'm worried about Professor Hemmington and Cliff too.'

[Ice Wall]

Instead of a barrier, I create a wall of ice. Kwang! Kwang! Kwang! The ice wall began to break quickly, just like the barrier. Using telekinesis, I floated my body into the air and pointed at Josh with my index finger.

[Lightning Spear]

A long spear of lightning created in the air flies towards Josh. Josh pulls the trigger of his pistol to keep me in check and quickly moves out of the way. I have to give him credit for his reflexes and judgment.

,My Lightning Spear is a bit special.'

The spear of lightning caused an explosion the moment it hit the ground. Electricity spread in all directions, and the electricity touched Josh's toes. Josh, electrocuted, slumped to the ground pathetically.

An ordinary person would have died instantly from the electric shock, but Josh was only unable to move. I had to finish him off before he could move again.

,How long are you going to lie there?"

Josh spoke. He wasn't talking to me. I reflexively glanced at the critically injured Jin.

,Tsk. Did you know about me?"

The critically injured Jin staggered to his feet. His left arm was dangling, and his intestines were flowing out of his torn abdomen. No matter how I looked at it, these were injuries that wouldn't be strange even if he died right away.

,Zombie Mercenary Jin Xtum. I've heard that name before."

,Don't call me a zombie mercenary. I'm clearly a living person!"

&gt;

Jin recovered in an instant.

,Did he use a recovery-type artifact? I didn't feel any signs of magic at all. Is it an ability type? Or is there another secret?'

As a magician, my curiosity flared up, but I suppressed it. This is not the time for that. The number of mercenaries I have to deal with has increased. I need to take care of even one of them quickly.

,There's someone we need to take care of before that magic user."

Jin, who was clutching a greatsword in both hands, swung it at Cliff and Professor Hemmington. Professor Hemmington, who was far away, barely managed to avoid the attack, but Cliff was split into upper and lower halves.

,Kuaaaaak! Pro, Professor! Help me!"

Cliff reached out to Professor Hemmington, but she coldly stepped back and distanced herself.

,Eugene Meyer! What are you doing! Qu, quickly help me! Stop these guys!"

Jin was about to approach to kill Professor Hemmington. Josh suddenly got up and shouted.

,Stop! If you kill Professor Hemmington, you won't be able to get the legacy of the ancient ruins! Are you planning to go back empty-handed?!"

,Ah, that's right. We can't go back empty-handed after coming all this way. Then shall we take care of our magic user first."

Jin's murderous intent turns to me. I looked at his abdomen. A glimpse of black light and familiar mana between his intestines.

,...I see. He received dark magic surgery."

Dark magic-style human body modification. I can guarantee it. Dark magic must be engraved on his organs and bones.

,Oh, what's this? You noticed right away? Even dark magicians couldn't recognize it."

I wouldn't have recognized it if I hadn't researched dark magic.

,To what extent do you have to be to modify a human like that?'

It was certain that he was at least a master.

The desire to capture Jin alive and dissect him surged. Just observing the elaborate magic and its by-products in detail would be of great help to me.

,So, do you guys want the treasure too? Go, good. We'll divide the treasure into three equal parts. Quickly kill that magician!"

Professor Hemmington's reaction was not very surprising.

,Shut up, Professor. Shut your damn mouth before I cut off one of your arms."

Professor Hemmington shut her mouth tightly at Jin's fierce glare. He swung his greatsword in the air and approached me.

,You bastard, you magic user. I didn't like you from the first time I saw you. A guy with no business ethics, blinded by money, backstabbing a fellow mercenary? If this gets out, your reputation is over. Well, you'll die before that. Even after you die, I'll tear down your reputation."

,I don't like you either. And did you think I'd just stand by and watch? Giving a magician time is the same as asking to be killed. Did you say zombie mercenary? Let's see if you survive after taking this. Die, Jin Xtum!"

Above my head, the blue magic circle appears in the highest place. Intense sparks flew from the magic circle, which was dozens of meters in diameter. At the center of the magic circle, compressed lightning roared with the sound of thunder.

I looked at Jin and raised my hand and lowered it. Jin frowned and tried to defend himself by raising his greatsword.

[Thunder Bolt]

The huge lightning bolt fell not on Jin but on Josh.

The feint worked perfectly. Josh, who was aiming at me with his rifle and looking for an opening, had to take the Thunder Bolt without any preparation.

The impact sound covers the space, and electromagnetism fills the air. My lungs tingle when I inhale. It's giving me strength, if not to others. Is it because I'm an electric-type magician? That's also true, but it's because my ability is lightning.

,Besides, there's even a waterfall flowing. As an electric-type magician. No, as a Thunder and Cold mage, there's no better environment than this.'

The conditions have been met.

The Thunder Bolt I'll use next will be even more powerful.

,Oh, oh! Magician! I believed in you!"

Professor Hemmington made a fuss. I ignored her. I'll kill that woman in the end anyway.

,You bastard...!"

Jin's muscles swell. Knowing that the war situation is unfavorable because Josh is dead, he is using a hidden technique.

,It's a kind of dark magic. Is it the type that temporarily strengthens the body.'

He seems to want to end it quickly. But it was natural that things in the world don't go as one wishes.

Kugugugugugugung!

The entire ancient ruins vibrate. I floated my body into the air with telekinesis.

,Are you running away?!"

,You idiot. Look behind you. The door is opening."

With Josh's death, five sacrifices were offered. The malicious prank of a certain archmage was now beginning to come to an end.

,Do, does the treasure finally reveal itself!"

The 5 doors open at the same time. The inside of the door was a pitch-black space. Professor Hemmington shined a flashlight she took out of her pocket into the door, but the darkness did not disappear. Rather, it felt like it was absorbing the light.

A red dragon appeared from the open door.

,Dragon?!"

Jin was shocked, but soon let out a sigh of relief.

,What, it's just a kid. And it's not even in a normal state."

The red dragon was small. Even if it was about 2-3 times larger than a human, it was still a very young hatchling by dragon standards. Moreover, it had wounds all over its body. Its wings were torn, its legs and tail were rotten, and blood and pus were flowing from its eyes and mouth.

,Shibal. I'm swearing because I hit the jackpot. Even if it's not in good condition, a dragon is a dragon. Just selling its corpse would be enough to live comfortably for the rest of my life! Hahahahaha!"

Jin laughs. It was a sound he could make because the dragon's condition was so bad. Professor Hemmington next to him also shines her eyes. Judging from the fact that she is looking at the dragon's chest, it was certain that she was aiming for the dragon's heart.

I stretched out my hand wearing the golden bracelet towards the dragon.

,Recovery."

The golden bracelet shattered and turned into golden powder. The golden powder seeped into the dragon. The dragon's torn wings were restored, and the rotten flesh returned to its original state. Life returned to its dying eyes.

,Kuaaaaaaaa!"

The dragon let out a roar mixed with hostility.

,You crazy shibal son of a bitch!!! Are you really out of your mind, you magic user!!!"
