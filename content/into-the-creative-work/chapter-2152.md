Irina was about to go crazy these days.

It was because the residents of Block 101 were demanding she solve all the problems that had erupted.

,We need food! I fainted from hunger yesterday!",

A self-proclaimed starving ajumma shouted. She looked pretty good for someone who was starving. Since the world is what it is, she probably has emergency rations hidden in her house. Even if there was no immediate food support, she wouldn't starve right away.

,Those bastards from Block 103 are picking fights again! They must be underestimating us! We need to show those sons of bitches who's boss!",

The residents of Block 101 were burning with anger because of the incident the other day. They were clamoring for a fight whenever they thought of Block 103. But to fight Block 103 here? That just meant everyone would die. No, Seong Yujin would step in, so only Block 101 would lose out.

,Seong Yujin. That bastard is too biased towards Block 103. I heard Block 103 is wining and dining Seong Yujin... We should do it too. That's the only way we won't be discriminated against.",

In short, offer a bribe. But the people around her frowned and opposed the idea. It meant they had nothing to offer. In reality, they didn't want to give up their own things. They didn't want to take a loss.

,Parasites! We have to kill the parasites! We're all going to die from parasites at this rate!",

Some people were talking about more fundamental issues.

But there was no way to identify the parasites. They couldn't just kill people they suspected of being parasites. Voting was also suspended. Even if they suspected someone of being a parasite, they couldn't kill them.

,Irina-ssi. We can't just keep standing still like this.",

,Irina-ssi. We need food!",

,Irina-ssi! You have to make a decision! A decision!",

Irina's eyes died a little each time her name was called from here and there. She was regretting it now. If she had known it would turn out like this, she wouldn't have become the resident representative. What am I supposed to do in this situation? She couldn't think of a solution to all the problems.

She should have firmly refused the position of resident representative back then... She was too distracted by a little bit of food.

It was better when Shin Jihoon, the former representative of Block 101, was alive. Back then, she just had to quietly do what she was told.

,Everyone! You're getting too heated! Let's all calm down! Irina-ssi is also feeling troubled!",

A young man stepped forward and calmed the residents. Woo Tae-hyun. He was always helping Irina. He was close to being the vice-representative of Block 101. He was also the reason Irina became the resident representative. Woo Tae-hyun had recommended Irina for the position.

Woo Tae-hyun smiled at Irina. A friendly smile. But Irina felt burdened. Even though she drew a line, Woo Tae-hyun was trying to cross it dangerously. She knew he was a good person... but he was a bit of an annoying type for Irina.

The residents' gazes focused on her. Sweat dripped down Irina's blonde hair under her hood. She had never liked being in front of people.

,W-Well, let's just wait and see for now.",

,Just wait and see? We'll be pushovers if we stay still!",

,.......",

,Irina-ssi. You have to make a decision!",

,.......",

,My friend died! Those bastards from Block 103 shot him with a gun!!",

,.......",

People didn't want to stay still. Irina forced her mouth open.

,...Food support will be coming soon. I'll make sure the food is distributed fairly, so you won't starve to death. As for the Block 103 issue... I'll talk to Yebin-unni. I don't think it's a problem we can solve on our own. No fighting. I'll also talk to Seong Yujin-nim, who is an expert on the parasite issue.",

,No, you're just leaving everything to others.",

,Block 103 won't ignore us if we take the lead in solving the problem, you know?",

,Seong Yujin is an expert? He's just a crazy bastard.",

,Rather. How about leaving the apartment altogether? I heard Saeak Elementary School has no parasites and lots of people, so it's a good place to live....",

Before people got excited again, Woo Tae-hyun stepped in.

,Okay, okay, calm down. Let's wait as Irina said. We can hold out until the next food comes. And it's better to solve problems through dialogue. As for the parasites, we have no choice but to be careful for now.",

The meeting in Block 101 came to an end. People dispersed. Some of the men glanced at Irina. Even with a hood and mask, they could tell she was a beauty just from her exposed eyes. In particular, her dynamite figure couldn't be easily hidden even by a baggy jumper.

,Irina-ssi. You must be tired? Go in and rest. I'll take good care of the people.",

,...I'm sorry for leaving all the work to Tae-hyun-ssi.",

,It's okay. I'm helping Irina-ssi because I want to. Oh, would you like to have dinner with me tonight? I have some ramen hidden away.",

,...I'm sorry. I don't think that would be possible.",

Irina had known for a while that Woo Tae-hyun had feelings for her. But Irina had no interest in Woo Tae-hyun. No, she didn't have much interest in other people in general. Basically, the only thing that mattered to her was herself.

,Are you going to go into your room and monitor that guy again today?",

,Yes. Just in case.",

Irina parted ways with Woo Tae-hyun and went into her house. There were four locks on the front door alone. She couldn't help it. Because of her natural beauty, she got caught up in unwanted incidents. So far, there have been more than seven men who have tried to break into her house.

Irina went to the veranda window, picked up her bow, and sat down. Below, a monster cat was sunbathing and grooming itself. It was one of the mutated species circling the outskirts of Saetbyeol Apartment. Its name was Nabi. It was a cat that a cat mom, who was an apartment resident, had been raising on the street. The cat mom was eaten by the cat.

Even though it was a boring and annoying task, she continued to monitor it. If that cat came into the apartment, a major disaster would occur.

,Even though the apartment is a mess right now, it'll be sorted out soon. If they think rationally, they'll know that now is not the time to fight each other. If things get too bad, Yebin-unni will mediate. Just like she always has.",

Seong Yujin... she didn't know. He was sending her goodwill, probably because of her appearance. He might be a good person, considering he had that much power but didn't force anything on her.

,A good person... no. How many people have died since that person came?",

She wasn't resentful. Unfortunately, she wasn't the type to resent other people's deaths. She just hoped it would pass quietly.

Two hours later, Irina was summoned by Seong Yujin. Considering Seong Yujin's status within the apartment, she had no choice but to go.

Seong Yujin and Choi Hyejin were eating.

Braised short ribs.

A high-class dish that couldn't be compared to canned food or ramen. A dish that made her mouth water just from the smell.

,Choi Hyejin. Don't just eat the meat.",

,Okay. Okay.",

,I said don't just eat the meat.",

,...I'm eating potatoes too.",

,Want to eat with us? There's plenty.",

,If you watch the news, you'll see, they caught and restored the mutated species in Mokpo. Support supplies are going there as a top priority, so food support will be reduced for the time being. All the people are joining forces and blah blah blah.... This isn't my decision, it's the government's. There's nothing I can do.",

,We have to divide it as fairly as possible. Ah, what I'm eating now is from Saeak Elementary School.",

Everyone in Block 101. Even though Seong Yujin had said that earlier, Irina was so out of it that she asked again. All she had to do was go back and find out who the residents she interviewed today were. But Seong Yujin kindly showed her the list.

There were 11 interviewees from Block 101. Woo Tae-hyun was among them.

,Do you know a man named Woo Tae-hyun? He's convinced you're a parasite.",

Irina felt dizzy. Woo Tae-hyun was trying to kill her? Why? Did he want the position of resident representative? Then he could have just asked for it. She had no interest in such a burdensome position.

,I think Woo Tae-hyun and these guys have other intentions. It seems like the representative is Woo Tae-hyun... He either wants the position of resident representative... or he's trying to scare you.",

,...What would he gain by scaring me?",

,I don't know. Gaslighting?",

,...What should I do? The people in Block 101 follow Woo Tae-hyun better than me. Even if I argue something, they won't listen if Woo Tae-hyun opposes it. What... what should I do....",

Irina's body trembled. She was terrible at these political issues. The only thing that came to her mind was running away. But where should she run away to? Seong Yujin gently touched her shoulder as she panicked.

,Calm down. I'm on your side. I'll help you. I'm the manager of the vote. In the end, it's up to me whether to execute you or not. If I refuse, nothing will happen. You won't be executed.",

It was something that people from other apartments would be shocked to hear. But strangely, Irina felt relieved by his words.

,...Yujin-nim.",

The highest authority was on her side? He would take care of everything even if she didn't do anything? Irina suddenly thought Seong Yujin was very cool. She felt like she knew why they said a capable man was the best.

,Yujin-nim? We look about the same age, just call me Yujin.",

,Ah, no. I'm more comfortable with this.",

,If you're comfortable with it, then whatever. I think we've gotten close, want to have a drink? And watch some TV.",

Irina suddenly came to her senses. Seong Yujin felt like a man who was hitting on her. Wasn't this a kind of flirting?

It was hard to cut him off like she did before. Hadn't Seong Yujin said he would help her? And in the living room he was pointing to, there was a TV and cola, things she hadn't been able to see lately. And there was also a woman named Choi Hyejin here.

,J-Just a little....",

Irina sat on the sofa. The cold cola she was drinking for the first time in almost three months was tearfully electrifying. The ice cream he recommended as a dessert melted in her mouth.

Most of the news on TV was about Mokpo. Seong Yujin's words were true.
