I first donned the Ghost Cloak, suppressing and concealing Susanoo's power as much as possible.

'If I use Susanoo's power to maximize my detection ability right now, I could sense more than half of the archipelago. There's no way Yamata no Orochi, who's stronger than me, couldn't do the same.'

It was only natural that a thoroughly enraged Yamata no Orochi would fly straight at me the moment it sensed my presence.

'In a way, you could say I've fucked myself. Making it drunk and killing it like in the original is impossible now. Its guard is way too high.'

At least for now, it was impossible.

I moved quietly. If I saw a yokai, I killed it. Even without using Susanoo's power, ordinary yokai were no match for me.

Something I learned in the process: killing yokai yielded no byproducts I could collect. I couldn't put the remains of slain yokai into my inventory.

[This item has an owner.]

[Would you like to use Ownership Seizure at the cost of 15 points?]

'This kind of message doesn't pop up in normal dungeons. Must be because it's a special dungeon. This place really is unusual.'

And it wasn't just yokai remains that couldn't go into the inventory.

Even a pebble lying carelessly on the roadside couldn't be stored.

[This item has an owner.]

[Would you like to use Ownership Seizure at the cost of 1 point?]

Everything in this dungeon. Down to a single pebble or a single leaf, someone held ownership over all of it.

But it wasn't as though I gained nothing at all.

『Your influence as the God of Storms grows.』

One: the growth of the God of Storms (A+) trait.

The other: Stormbreaker's growth. Killing yokai granted experience points.

『Growth (Lv.38 exp: 2570/12,800)

‣ Absorbs a portion of the life force and soul of slain targets to gain experience.

‣ Experience can be used to strengthen abilities.

‣ Currently absorbed experience (186/13,800)』

'Looking at this, it means the yokai and humans in this dungeon aren't entirely fake.'

While I was at it, I decided to level up Stormbreaker too.

『Using Thunder Eye Golden Pupil!』

'The God of Storms trait pairs well with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil. I can use it for quite a long time.'

Thunder Eye Golden Pupil wasn't a power that radiated outward—it enhanced the user internally, so there was no risk of Yamata no Orochi sensing it and coming after me.

With Thunder Eye Golden Pupil active, tracking yokai became much easier. Quite a few yokai disguised themselves as humans, but Thunder Eye Golden Pupil saw through them instantly.

A day passed.

I killed over a thousand yokai. Word spread across Japan in an instant that Susanoo was massacring yokai. The yokai fled from me. They burrowed deep into the mountains or huddled under the protection of named Great Yokai.

Great Yokai were beings with power even gods couldn't ignore. Most gods could actually be overpowered by a Great Yokai.

While hunting yokai, if I came across a land god, I killed it without question. Killing creatures in this dungeon didn't shift my karma, so killing them for experience was the better deal.

『Your influence as the God of Storms decreases.』

That's what I thought—until this notification appeared.

'...Losing influence is no good. I should leave the land gods alone.'

Apart from land gods, humans were the same. Killing bandits or thieves was fine, but massacring common folk in large numbers reduced my influence.

'This influence thing—you could call it a kind of faith.'

As I hunted yokai like that, I gradually felt the efficiency dropping.

Perhaps because the yokai were all so similar to each other, the experience Stormbreaker gained kept shrinking.

'Time to start killing named yokai instead of small fry.'

I sought out a nearby land god.

On the surface, it appeared to be a tree yokai. Land god was closer to a kind of appointed position—yokai or spirits could become land gods too, as long as they met the conditions.

"Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto. Thy yokai hunting is a most impressive and virtuous deed, but... thou lackest mercy, and it may instead bring chaos to these lands. Please, grant us a wise decision..."

"Shut up. Save the nagging. Any yokai left around here?"

"There are none. All have departed for the underworld by thy spear and blade."

I stared hard with Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.

Perhaps because its level was low, I couldn't tell whether the land god was lying. Raising Thunder Eye Golden Pupil's level would probably fix that.

"Those eyes resemble the Monkey Immortal who runs wild on the continent."

"Have you seen that Monkey Immortal yourself?"

"I have not. I merely heard of him through other land gods, one to another."

"Ho. So that's the famous land god network I've heard about."

"Network? What does that mean?"

"It's a word from the western regions. No—in this era, it probably doesn't exist even there. What's Yamata no Orochi doing right now?"

"It rages in fury. It is wreaking havoc on nearby lands... Humans, yokai, and land gods alike are at a loss. Why didst thou attack Yamata no Orochi?"

"I thought I could kill it. And it was actually pretty effective. I saw several of Yamata no Orochi's heads knocked unconscious. What's its condition now?"

"Yamata no Orochi possesses formidable life force. It has already recovered from all its wounds."

"Just like a snake—incredible vitality. Snake wine brewed from that thing would be great for stamina."

"Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto. If Yamata no Orochi is left to rampage further, the earth shall become a hell. I know thy relations with Takamagahara are strained from past matters, but Yamata no Orochi's tyranny has reached its peak—the gods of heaven must surely be concerned as well. How about requesting aid from Takamagahara?"

Ask other gods for help? That was actually a pretty decent strategy.

But I shook my head.

"You think I can't kill a single snake? That many-headed serpent—I'll wring its neck before long. You land gods just shut up and wait. If you've got complaints, speak up. I'll personally show you my power."

"...I have no complaints. However... art thou truly unconnected to the Monkey Immortal of the continent?"

"I'm not. Ask one more useless question and I'll kill you."

"......Understood."

"Any named yokai nearby?"

"Shuten-douji resides on Mount Oe. He is a Great Yokai, and also a son of Yamata no Orochi. I hear he obtained a jar that endlessly produces Divine Brew of Bewitching Oni Poison—said to intoxicate even the mightiest being with a single sip—and holds banquets every day."

"Yamata no Orochi's son, and Divine Brew of Bewitching Oni Poison. I see. So that was the standard strategy."

The Divine Brew of Bewitching Oni Poison would probably intoxicate even Yamata no Orochi. Obtain this liquor, put Yamata no Orochi to sleep, and kill it. That must be this dungeon's standard clear method.

"Pardon?"

"Just talking to myself. Don't mind me."

I'd confirmed Shuten-douji's location yesterday while traveling across Japan. Before leaving, I spoke to the land god.

"Spread the word to every land god across the Japanese archipelago. If Yamata no Orochi flies after me, interfere with everything you've got. Any land god who doesn't cooperate, or who sides with Yamata no Orochi—I'll come collect the price later."

"...That price would be..."

Without a word, I slightly drew Hwaryeon Bido from the sheath at my waist. The land god saw a portion of the crimson blade steeped in vicious killing intent and demonic energy, and every branch on its body trembled.

"I-I understand. I shall relay it to all land gods of Japan...! B-But with our power, stopping Yamata no Orochi is difficult!"

"Who told you to fight it head-on like idiots? Use your heads. That thing has eight heads but its combined IQ is barely 80. Set traps, use dirty tricks... just do something."

"U-Understood."

"Let's do well."

I left him with that final warning and tore a Teleport Scroll. I'd set coordinates near Shuten-douji and the other two of Japan's Three Great Yokai yesterday. Flying there would take less than five minutes, but even five minutes is a hassle.

'This is exactly why I saved up Teleport Scrolls.'

Appearing near Mount Oe, I confirmed the palace where Shuten-douji resided. It was an absurdly luxurious palace, with hundreds of yokai gathered in revelry. Shuten-douji sat at the center, a sake jar tucked under his arm, swaying drunkenly.

'Thunder God's Command would be certain, but... unlike when I dropped it on Yamata no Orochi, the weather's clear. All the preparations take too damn long.'

I pulled Vaiśravaṇa's Bow from my inventory, obtained not long ago.

『Vaiśravaṇa's Bow

Rank: S

Increases arrow speed.

Increases arrow power.

Grants homing capability to arrows.』

I had a few dwarf-made arrows in my inventory, but I wasn't satisfied with them at all.

'Ordinary arrows won't cut it.'

After a moment's thought, I took out the Vajra Divine Thunder and tried nocking it on the bowstring. It was a bit awkward, but it seemed possible to fire it as an arrow substitute.

I released the Susanoo power I'd been suppressing. I crammed the power of storms into the Vajra Divine Thunder as much as possible.

Bzzt-crack, bzzzt!

Yellow lightning sparked from the Vajra Divine Thunder, and wind compressed. Power that would have shattered an ordinary arrow—the Vajra Divine Thunder withstood it effortlessly. There's a reason "Vajra" is in its name. And the Vajra Divine Thunder and the power of storms were naturally compatible.

"SUSANOOOOOOOOOOO!"

A roar filled with rage echoed faintly from far away.

The moment I released my power, Yamata no Orochi sensed it.

'I can feel it flying toward my location.'

Normally it would have arrived before me in under a minute, but the land gods had begun interfering with Yamata no Orochi. Not fighting it directly—using spells and the like to stall for time.

'The land gods. More capable than I expected.'

Thanks to them, I could leisurely pour power into the Vajra Divine Thunder to its limit.

I released the bowstring.

A sonic boom erupted as a golden line cut through the air, reaching Shuten-douji's chest in the blink of an eye and embedding itself there. I was slightly impressed that the Vajra Divine Thunder hadn't pierced through his chest.

'Physical durability that would've been a real pain in a head-on fight.'

The moment Shuten-douji's drunken, hazy eyes drifted downward and confirmed the Vajra Divine Thunder—

KWAAAAAAANG!

The power of storms stored within the Vajra Divine Thunder exploded outward.

Yellow lightning imbued with demon-subduing power through the Vajra Divine Thunder's influence burned the yokai to death in an instant, and a mighty wind swept past their bodies, scouring the entire surrounding area.

The storm raged for a few seconds, then subsided and vanished. I headed toward where Shuten-douji had been.

'You never know. The Divine Brew of Bewitching Oni Poison might have survived.'

But the jar containing the Divine Brew of Bewitching Oni Poison had been completely shattered—only its wreckage remained barely visible.

And from within the debris, Shuten-douji's head—which had been hiding—lunged for my neck.

Thunder Heaven Style: Lightning Flash.

The tip of Hwaryeon Bido, drawn like a flash of light, pierced precisely through Shuten-douji's forehead.

"H-How?"

"I knew from the start, obviously. I killed a bastard like you, but no system notification popped up—that's suspicious as hell."

Shuten-douji's head vanished.

『Your influence as the God of Storms grows.』

『The rank of God of Storms rises to S-.』

"There it is."

The storm had obliterated everything around, so there was nothing to collect.

I sheathed my blade and waited a moment. Far in the distance, I could see Yamata no Orochi flying toward me.

"SUSANOOOOOO!! I'LL FUCKING KILL YOUUUU!!"

I immediately pulled out a Teleport Scroll. I sneered at Yamata no Orochi.

"Heh heh. I killed your son. You're gonna get a lot of stress. You'll remember before long. Strong, strong."
