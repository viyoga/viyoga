「The Master of the Heavens desires honor.」

「The King of the Demon Heavens desires honor.」

In Atlantis, the higher a contractor's standing, the more the Divine Thrones benefit. One of the key metrics is influence.

In other words, the more famous my name becomes in Atlantis, the more the contracted Divine Thrones gain. The honor they seek can be understood along those same lines.

'The Master of the Heavens and the King of the Demon Heavens want me to enter the Martial Arts Tournament. And they're being pretty blatant about it. If I refuse, they might turn uncooperative for a while.'

I'd been planning to enter the tournament anyway, so it's fine. The problem is that I have two identities and only one body. Only one of them can claim the honor.

I have to choose one. And nine times out of ten, the one not chosen will throw a fit or sulk.

Choosing without justification is out of the question. I need a reason that both the Master of the Heavens and the King of the Demon Heavens can accept.

"Jesse. I'll participate under the identity of whichever side makes me the better offer."

I said, sinking deep into the chair's backrest. Insolent? So what. If they don't like it, they can just not make an offer.

The Master of the Heavens and the King of the Demon Heavens knew me well. Rather than getting angry, they dangled rewards.

「The Master of the Heavens proposes.」

「Win the Martial Arts Tournament as the Thunder Slaughter Temple, and you shall be granted 'Hero's Bloodline.'」

「The King of the Demon Heavens proposes.」

「Win the Martial Arts Tournament as the Heavenly Demon, and you shall be granted 'Avesta.'」

I leisurely checked the rewards they offered.

「Hero's Bloodline

Your bloodline inherits the power of heroes.

Those with the Hero's Bloodline possess physical abilities beyond ordinary humans and are immune to illness, living long lives.

Your descendants will bloom a portion of your abilities.

Type: Trait

Rank: SS」

First, Hero's Bloodline.

This can be seen as a trait passed down to children or descendants who share my blood.

'Physical abilities beyond ordinary humans probably won't help much, but immunity to illness and longevity isn't bad.'

And the line about my descendants blooming a portion of my abilities piqued my curiosity.

'My stats? Or traits and skills?'

「Avesta

The more evil you commit, the more Evil Karma accumulates.

Use Evil Karma to curse a target.

The more good you do, the more Good Karma accumulates.

Use Good Karma to heal a target.

As Good Karma and Evil Karma accumulate, Avesta's rank rises.

Rank: SSS」

'Now this is an interesting item.'

Evil Karma and Good Karma. Bad deeds and good deeds. If I wanted to do either, I could do plenty. Even if I didn't, just living my life without paying attention would let it accumulate on its own.

'In game terms, it's a stacking item.'

The part about the rank rising was quite intriguing.

'Just looking at rank, Avesta is higher.'

But it wasn't to my taste.

"I'll take Hero's Bloodline. Master of the Heavens, I accept your proposal."

Elena was pregnant with my child. I'd decided to gift that child a trait.

「The Master of the Heavens smiles with satisfaction.」

「The King of the Demon Heavens staggers at the unimaginable turn of events.」

Seeing the King of the Demon Heavens' message, I swallowed a hollow laugh.

'Did this bastard seriously think there was a 100% chance I'd pick Avesta?'

Does he take me for an idiot? I clicked my tongue toward the King of the Demon Heavens.

The regrettable part was that I wouldn't receive the Master of the Heavens' reward immediately. I had to win the Martial Arts Tournament. I was confident enough that I would.

"Is your choice made?"

Elena asked. She was beside me at this gathering.

"I received a proposal from the Master of the Heavens. If I win, I get Hero's Bloodline."

"Hero's Bloodline?"

I explained the trait's effects to her as she tilted her head. Elena wore a faint smile.

"For this child not yet born, it will be an irreplaceable blessing."

Elena stroked her lower belly with one hand.

She'd even quit the wine she loved so much, on the grounds that it could harm the baby.

The child's sex was still unknown.

"That's right. The only slight problem is that there's no detailed information about the Martial Arts Tournament. Do you happen to know anything?"

It was a kind of cheat, but I didn't mind at all.

"I don't. These documents only contain a rough schedule and outline. Since the System itself intervenes to run the tournament... even the Emperor probably doesn't know."

"Aren't you entering the tournament?"

If Elena were my opponent, I'd have to be nervous too. Even if I held the advantage in compatibility, I couldn't guarantee victory.

"I and the other five dukes won't be participating. Even if the System runs the tournament, the official host is the Emperor. Having the Empire's Five Heavens participate wouldn't look good. Moreover, a tournament has an audience. I don't want to reveal my full strength in a place like that."

It was a reasonable point.

Revealing your power to the world could amount to showing your strength to someone who might become an enemy.

I rose from my seat.

"Hm? Eugene. Where are you going?"

"To A-Plan Legion. I need to prepare for the tournament."

"Why go all the way there to prepare... ah."

"Yeah. If it's Kang Myungjin, there's a high chance he knows about the Martial Arts Tournament too."

• • •

Meeting Kang Myungjin wasn't difficult. A-Plan Legion was currently taking a break as well. Of course, even in the midst of that, Kang Myungjin was focused on his own training.

"I see. The Martial Arts Tournament. The conditions certainly line up."

"Conditions?"

"There won't be any events on the Atlantis continent for a while. The Atlantis continent is busy dealing with the aftermath of the volcanic island. It's a period of reorganization for what comes next, so the Divine Thrones can't easily intervene either. This is when they open a small event for the Divine Thrones' amusement."

It was the same opinion as mine.

"Calling it a small event seems like an understatement given the scale."

"The scale is large, but it's closer to a game. Tournament participants receive the System's protection and won't die. They won't suffer injuries in the tournament either. On the contrary, the experience points gained remain, so participants' skills will improve. Even if you don't win, just participating will be beneficial."

"That sounds like you're saying you'll participate too."

"Yes. There's no reason not to. Probably everyone confident in their skills will enter. Of course, the truly strong—including the Empire's Five Heavens—will mostly abstain. They don't particularly enjoy becoming clowns on stage."

Kang Myungjin will participate.

I calculated my odds of winning. If I faced him as the Thunder Slaughter Temple rather than the Heavenly Demon, could I guarantee victory?

It would be difficult, but I was confident I could win.

"If a lot of people flock to the tournament, naturally over 90% will be eliminated from the preliminaries. Tell me what the tournament involves."

“…….”

"Don't tell me you don't know either?"

"I know. I just don't want to tell a competitor."

"That's not fair. I'm the one who told you the tournament was happening."

"It's information that'll be proclaimed across the entire continent soon anyway. Knowing in advance doesn't grant much advantage."

"No. You're really not going to tell me?"

"I too am serious about this tournament. The Divine Throne I contracted with desires honor."

"Yours too?"

Thinking it over, Kang Myungjin's Divine Throne would naturally show interest in the Martial Arts Tournament.

The only Divine Thrones who wouldn't care much would be those uninterested in combat, like Endless Abundance or the Mistress of Olympus.

"Still, as a fellow member of the same legion, I'll give you some information... The preliminaries will be held over three rounds, and even you will be eliminated the moment you let your guard down. Ah, just advancing to the main bracket earns you a title."

"That's barely useful information."

"Better than knowing nothing at all, isn't it?"

He wasn't wrong.

Kang Myungjin rose from his seat and moved to leave.

"Where are you going all of a sudden?"

"To prepare for the tournament."

Kang Myungjin grinned. Uncharacteristically, he'd acted smug.

I made a sullen face, then realized my insides were boiling.

'It's fine that I don't know the future. But the thought that someone else might know it really gets my blood up.'

After a moment's deliberation, I pulled a Potion of Foresight from my inventory.

'I have two anyway. I need to drink one and check its effect.'

I was about to down one right away, then stopped.

'Wait. Drinking it as is would be a waste. Let me enhance it with the Light of Ascension first.'

The Taepyeong Sorcery Tome that was being enhanced with the Light of Ascension was already in its enhanced state. I decided to give it to Mireung later, enhanced the Potion of Foresight in the [Light of Ascension] world, and returned. I used Timeflow Spirit Guide (時流靈引), and it consumed a whopping 25 Heavenly Orbs.

'One Heavenly Orb equals 10 years of acceleration. I accelerated a total of 250 years.'

The Heavenly Orbs spent exceeded my expectations, which was a bit of a waste, but I had no regrets.

I drank the enhanced Potion of Foresight.

Gulp.

The mysterious potion slid down my throat.

「Fortune's Favor (EX) is activated.」

「The entire universe aids you!」

「Fortune's Favor (EX) will be deactivated for the time being.」

An immeasurable future pierces into my head.

I raised my chin and trembled.

I saw the future.

A failed future.

A successful future.

A successful future that comes after a failed future.

A failed future that comes after a successful future.

Every kind of future not yet arrived bubbled up like foam.

And I knew what I had to do. The enhanced Potion of Foresight didn't merely show the future.

「The Master of the Heavens widens his eyes and strokes his beard with a faintly trembling hand.」

「The King of the Demon Heavens gazes at the universe and lets out a laugh.」

I used Absolute Mind to suppress the power of foresight running wild. It would be a waste to use up all the power of foresight here.

Feeling the calmed power of foresight, I rose from my seat.

"Let's get to work for my daughters."
