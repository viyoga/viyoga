The body of General Mokdun vanished, and in the place where it had been, a single fruit remained.

The fruit, slightly larger than my fist, had ten flowers of different colors growing from its black skin.

The Lightning Eye Gold Tome revealed the fruit's name and effects.

'Ten-Flower Fruit. A spirit object brimming with the energy of life. If an ordinary person eats it, immortality is guaranteed, and they can even regain their youth. Their lifespan increases to a certain degree as well. It's the kind of spirit object that high officials would empty their entire fortunes to possess.'

The greatest advantage of this spirit object was that it would greatly aid my cultivation as a Harmony Realm practitioner.

My current level was Harmony Stage 2.

I had been cultivating steadily, but there had been no significant progress.

'Normally, the orthodox method is to cultivate with the aid of spirit herbs, spirit pills, or spirit veins. The problem is that the higher your level, the higher the grade of spirit object you require. Low-grade spirit objects don't help with cultivation and only build up resistance.'

Spirit objects that could aid a Harmony Realm practitioner couldn't be obtained even with money.

'Perhaps because General Mokdun gathered energy but couldn't release it before dying, this place is now overflowing with wood-attribute energy. Wood energy has almost no repulsion with other energies. If I eat the Ten-Flower Fruit here and focus on cultivation... I should be able to achieve meaningful results. I might even reach Harmony Stage 3.'

The wood energy filling the surroundings was slowly dispersing. Perhaps because this was a mountain of the wood attribute, the wood energy dispersed slowly. It would likely remain here for several days.

'Once I start cultivating, I won't be able to move for at least a few days. Even I can't digest all the spirit energy of the Ten-Flower Fruit in a short time. Tch. I have no choice but to give up on cultivating for now.'

A mountain of the wood attribute. And from just a portion of it, I'd obtained the Ten-Flower Fruit, an outstanding spirit object. There were surely spirit objects on other mountains as well. Even if my cultivation was urgent, I couldn't let others snatch up the spirit objects.

'The secret realm is vast, so I'll find a suitable cultivation spot. I'll postpone cultivation and gather as many spirit objects as possible.'

They weren't just for me, either. I needed to collect some for Mi-ryeong and the elders of the Heavenly Demon Cult.

Shaking off the lingering attachment that clung to my ankles, I manipulated the clouds with the Cloud-Riding Command and flew into the sky.

As I passed over a mountain of the water attribute, a massive, pure-white serpent sitting atop its peak opened its eyes wide and spat venom at me.

'Eternal Snow Flood Dragon. So it's an imugi. I didn't notice from far away. It must have had a stealth ability.'

A Harmony Realm-class spirit object attacked me without warning. It knew instinctively. That eating me would make it stronger.

'Even so, to charge in without any calculation.'

A flood dragon. In other words, since it was an imugi, its intelligence seemed low. Among spirit objects and yokai, there are those that gain power by discarding intelligence, so it wasn't strange.

I increased the speed of the clouds and dodged the transparent venom it spat. The air touched by the venom instantly froze with a crackling sound and fell to the ground.

'Venom and ice at the same time.'

I summoned the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade, gripped it in my hand, and changed direction to approach the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon. It was the moment I closed to roughly 3km. The Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's eyes flashed.

My body stopped.

Condensed cold qi had invaded my body and instantly frozen my flesh. It felt like being poisoned, but my poison resistance didn't react.

The connection with the Cloud-Riding Command was severed, and my body fell helplessly toward the ground. I coolly assessed the situation. The Lightning Eye Gold Tome shone.

'It's a divine ability it possesses. Eternal Ice Eye. A divine ability that injects cold qi into targets within a certain range and freezes them.'

It attacks simply by looking at the target, without any complex conditions.

'Even if it's not powerful enough to instantly kill someone of the same level, I can't deny it's a considerable divine ability.'

Krzzt.

Black lightning mixed with thunder qi and demon qi erupted from my entire body. My body, frozen by the cold qi, melted instantly. I landed softly on the snow piled on the ground. My legs didn't sink into the snow and stood atop it. There was no trace left on the snow.

The Eternal Snow Flood Dragon approached rapidly, as if swimming over the snow. It must have realized midway that I was unharmed, because it used its power. Thousands of icicles formed from all directions and flew at me. Icicles imbued with spirit energy. They would pierce through steel and keep going.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art, Mahura.

A black, semi-transparent barrier surrounded me. The flying icicles reflected the moment they touched the barrier and flew back in the opposite direction.

In that gap, the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon closed in before me and bit down with its fangs as if to chew me whole.

Mahura couldn't withstand its bite force and was destroyed. I tried to dodge with a leap, but.

Its pupils flashed once, cold qi surged, and froze my body. It even froze my internal energy, preventing me from using the leap. Of course, the energy of Extreme Yang began moving again in less than a second, but... it was slightly too late.

I twisted my body to avoid having my head pierced by its fangs. Instead, my left shoulder was pierced. Venom was injected, and my left arm froze. I hurriedly dodged to the side.

[You have absorbed poison. You resist the poison.]

Even with poison resistance, my frozen left arm didn't thaw. Having escaped the poison's influence, it had frozen completely—physically and conceptually. Should I use Heaven's Heart? There might be more dangerous situations ahead. It was just one arm. It wasn't a fatal wound, so I decided to leave it be.

I could feel emotion in the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's eyes. Mockery. It was sneering at me.

"You damned snake bastard."

It approached. This time, I could feel its will to finish me off for certain. It had judged that I was completely incapacitated by the poison.

'You didn't kill me and you're already certain of victory? Stupid creature.'

Pulling my fist back, I summoned the Vajra Divine Thunder before me. The Eternal Snow Flood Dragon opened its mouth wide. Aiming for the moment I disappeared from its sight, I swung my fist at the Vajra Divine Thunder from behind.

Thunder Heaven Style, Electromagnetic Cannon.

The Vajra Divine Thunder pierced through the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's palate and skull. It clearly penetrated its brain. Yet it didn't die immediately and attacked me to the end.

'It's slowed from that attack.'

I summoned the Flaming Lotus Flying Blade again, gripped it, and swung.

Thunder Heaven Style, Thunder Light.

Shhk!

The Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's head split in half.

A massive spray of blood burst from its head and splattered everywhere.

I wiped the blood off my body with my hand.

'The blood is cold as ice. The blood itself carries intense yin energy. This creature's blood is a spirit medicine in itself.'

Using Void Grasp, I collected its blood into jars. I was able to obtain about 500L. Given the creature's size, there had been much more blood, but only 500L of it contained spirit energy.

'It won't have much effect on me, but it should be quite helpful for those at the Five Qi Realm level.'

Next, I obtained the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's inner core and the Divine Well in its head. Divine Well. A well of the gods. Roughly speaking, it could be called the source of divine abilities.

'Lucky. Normally, even when you kill a spirit object with divine abilities, it's extremely rare to obtain a Divine Well.'

If I absorbed this Divine Well, I could obtain the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon's Eternal Ice Eye divine ability.

'It's a divine ability that doesn't suit me, since I wield the energy of Extreme Yang.'

For now, I stored it in my inventory. It didn't have to be me who used it.

'The most valuable thing is its inner core. Like the Ten-Flower Fruit, it should aid my cultivation.'

I looked at my left arm. Even though the Eternal Snow Flood Dragon was dead, my arm remained frozen. The cold qi lodged in my left arm showed no sign of dispersing—rather, it was trying to freeze my torso and heart. Since thunder qi and demon qi automatically blocked the cold qi, there was no major problem.

'To thaw it without Complete Recovery or Heaven's Heart, I'd need to focus on circulating energy for at least three days. Or I could get help from Mi-ryeong, who's an excellent spellcaster.'

Using Complete Recovery or Heaven's Heart felt like a waste. There was still much to do, and it wasn't a fatal wound.

'My right arm is fine. I'll move like this for the time being.'

I rode the clouds and moved again.

Afterward, I obtained three more Harmony Realm-class spirit objects. Since I could no longer sense any additional Harmony Realm-class spirit objects, I decided to focus on cultivation.

I returned to the mountain of the wood attribute. Life energy still lingered in the place where I had destroyed General Mokdun. I sat cross-legged in the spot where General Mokdun had been. My left arm was still frozen.

'I could recover instantly with Complete Recovery, but... neutralizing this vicious cold qi with my own energy is also a form of cultivation.'

This too was experience.

As I focused my mind, my cross-legged body began to float gently into the air.

I consumed the Ten-Flower Fruit. A vast energy of life entered my body. The warm, gentle energy of wood—not hot—softly melted the cold qi in my left arm and began to achieve harmony. Just watching this process of harmonization felt like cultivation.

Naturally, I had no intention of just watching. I needed to fully control this process of harmonization to become stronger.

Perhaps thanks to the effects of the Heavenly River Star system and the Heavenly Ghost Star, I fell into a state of selflessness without difficulty.

How many hours had passed?

I sensed an irritating presence.

I opened my closed eyes. There was a man approaching me. The man flinched in surprise. He was wrapped in black clothing and skillfully concealed his killing intent. His level was Three Purities Middle Stage.

"An assassin?"

"...That's right. I definitely concealed my presence perfectly... How did you notice? Is it related to those eyes?"

I had been maintaining the Lightning Eye Gold Tome continuously. This secret realm was so rich in spirit energy that maintaining the Lightning Eye Gold Tome at all times wasn't difficult.

"Who's your client?"

"Answering a question with a question? The Heavenly Demon really is rude, just as I heard."

"Is it the Martial Alliance Leader?"

"An assassin who reveals their client is below third-rate."

The assassin didn't flee and slowly approached.

"Do you think you can kill me?"

"You're talking to me, but... you're not circulating your energy. If you forcibly stop circulating energy mid-cultivation, your qi and blood will be twisted beyond repair. Especially since what you practice is a demonic art, isn't it? Even if it were a stable orthodox art, it would be dangerous, let alone a demonic art? You could die instantly. Do you think I'd miss this perfect opportunity to kill the Heavenly Demon?"

"What were you promised in exchange for killing me?"

"I was to receive a Transformation Pill to aid in advancing to the next Harmony Realm stage, three Harmony Realm-class spirit objects, ten Three Purities Realm-class spirit objects, and two Dharma treasures. Since the target is the Heavenly Demon, I should receive at least this much, shouldn't I?"

"Stupid fool."

"What?"

"If it were me, I would have demanded at least three times that."

"Ha. Where does that confidence come from? If I were you, I'd be begging for my life. Who knows, if you offered the Heavenly Demon Divine Art or a disciple's position as bait, I might waver?"

"Take a maggot like you as my disciple? I'd rather die."

Twitch.

The assassin's eyebrow moved. Like a true assassin, he controlled his emotions in an instant.

"Is that so? Now die."

The assassin, who had been approaching me without stopping even during our conversation, drew a dagger and stabbed at my solar plexus.

Krzzt!

Black lightning flashed and caught the assassin's dagger.

"You caught my blade with just internal energy...?!"

"The fact that you pressed on even after your presence was detected already makes you below third-rate."

I moved my frozen left arm.

Clang!

The cold qi that had been eating away at my left arm shattered and fell away.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art, Cruel Cold Demon Dragon.

The Heavenly Demon qi, an energy of Extreme Yang, reversed in an instant and became an energy of Extreme Yin.

My left arm, wreathed in black cold qi, grasped the assassin's head.

Jjeong!

The assassin froze solid and died.

Drip.

My qi and blood twisted, and blood flowed from my mouth.

'Cruel Cold Demon Dragon is still a bit early for me. I'm not familiar with Extreme Yin energy...'

Moreover, it was the price of forcibly stopping my energy circulation.

'It doesn't matter.'

I closed my eyes and continued cultivating.

If I die, I'll just use Complete Recovery.
