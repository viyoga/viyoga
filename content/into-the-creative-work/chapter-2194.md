,You made it all the way here. The path is so complicated that eight or nine out of ten people would get lost."

This is close to the core of Sebitseom. It's not a place you can just come to, no matter how much you smash up the terrain.

,We arrived here without much difficulty because we have an excellent guide on our side."

Woohee replied. I looked around at the corporate employees wearing the same outfits. A guide must be someone with an ability specialized in finding paths. I didn't expect them to bring such high-level talent here.

,That's a variable. I must kill the guide before things get more twisted."

Tang!

The musket in Woohee's hand spat fire. The bullet ricocheted off the ceiling and fell to the floor.

,This is a warning. If you don't want to die, open that door and tell me how to get inside."

Woohee pointed to the door behind them. A huge metal door was firmly closed. They were gathered here because they couldn't open that door.

,You can open it with a card key."

Their gazes turned to me and Orshia. Invisible Blade had entered stealth mode using the power of her cyber suit before entering this place. I faintly felt her presence. She was slowly moving behind them for an assassination.

,Tell me more details."

,...If you open the door on the right and go straight, you'll find a circular space. If you search that place, you'll be able to pick up a card key."

,Ah, thanks for the kind explanation. But seeing as you guys came here, it seems like you have a card key. Take it out."

Takeshi, holding a katana, grinned.

,We didn't bring a separate card key. That's unfortunate news for you."

Since I have a VIP resident card, I don't need a card key. If I get close, the door will open like an automatic door.

Did my words feel provocative? A cruel smile appeared on the face of Woohee, who was holding the musket.

,Oh-ho. So you're going to be uncooperative? This can't be helped. We'll have to forcibly extract cooperation. We'll take you after cutting off your limbs. Activate the mana wave device."

,Don't give orders, you pirate."

A corporate employee with a large machine on his back growled as he spoke, but diligently operated the machine.

In an instant, mana waves fluctuated around him. They were using irregular mana waves to interfere with magic casting. In this state, even a small spell requires several times more concentration.

,If you're properly prepared, killing a magician is no big deal."

Takeshi approached me with a ferocious beast-like smile.

,How long do I have to wait?"

I asked the Invisible Sword. The Invisible Sword answered in its own way.

,Kkeuaak!"

A knife was stabbed into the back of the man carrying the mana wave device. The figure of Invisible Blade wearing a fox mask was revealed. She slashed down with her knife, destroying the man and the machine at the same time. The mana waves stabilized in an instant.

,Yujin-ssi."

,It's okay, Orshia-ssi. Conserve your strength. The two of us will handle these guys."

[Electric Field]

Blue currents raced along the ground. The enemies flinched but didn't collapse. They weren't low-level enough to be taken down by just one Electric Field.

,Damn it! Takeshi! You guys deal with that goddamn bitch! We'll deal with this magician! The opponents are an electric-type magician and a space-type magician! Don't stick together, spread out! Report immediately if the space fluctuates!"

Two people jumped out in front of me. They were a man and a woman with similar faces. They seemed to be siblings. They were aiming for my right and left sides, respectively. At the same time, their faces and bodies changed. Fur grew rapidly, and their nails became long and sharp.

,Werewolves, huh? A fleeting moment."

[Using Fleeting Moment. Remaining stacks: 11]

Looking at them, I inadvertently thought of Lightning Flash Style. Evading attacks with footwork and counterattacking with daggers. That series of processes naturally came to mind.

,There's a clear difference between thinking of a way to deal with something and actually dealing with it. There's no way my body can do those complicated movements. I'll forget Lightning Flash Style."

The effect of Fleeting Moment alone was enough to respond.

I calculated the incantation in the slowed-down world and chanted.

[Reverse Gravity]

Gravity reversal. The bodies of the werewolf siblings who were attacking me lost their balance and floated upwards. They also tried to regain their balance using mana, but my magic casting was faster.

[Chain Lightning]

The lightning streak that manifested from my right hand moved and alternately electrocuted the siblings. The werewolf siblings, who were electrocuted dozens of times using each other as a medium, died amicably, emitting a burnt smell.

Then, bullets poured in from all directions. The barrier I had prepared in advance blocked the bullets.

,What kind of casting speed is that...?"

,He's definitely not an ordinary magician. Is there any information on that bastard?"

,Shut up and pull the trigger. Even if it's a level 5 or higher barrier, it's bound to be pierced if you bombard it with the latest anti-magic bullets."

For some reason, I felt the barrier was gradually being chipped away. I couldn't rely on just the barrier and hold out for long.

[Ice Fog]

I spread cold air in all directions. The surrounding temperature began to drop rapidly.

,He's also skilled in ice-type magic. Be careful. Ice-type is highly compatible with electric-type. You know what happens in extremely low-temperature conditions, right?"

Woohee said, avoiding the cold air.

,Our bodies will freeze to death before superconductivity occurs. And don't forget that you're not our teammate or leader. You're just a field collaborator."

,Aren't I doing this because you guys are fighting so frustratingly? Show us what elites are supposed to look like."

,We're going to do that even if you don't tell us."

The corporate employees, agitated by Woohee's provocation, rushed in with perfect coordination. Not simultaneously, but with a fixed difference in movement. It was a coordinated attack completed through highly advanced training, hasoseo.

,I can see everything, and all of it is caught in my senses."

[Using Fleeting Moment. Remaining stacks: 10]

Casting using the fleeting moment.

[Chain Lightning]

A lightning streak extended out. One of the enemies suddenly popped out and took the Chain Lightning with his body. He desperately tried to hold it in his body so that the lightning wouldn't transfer.

,Now!"

Kieeeing.

The knives in their hands cut through the barrier. A blue spark flew as a reaction occurred due to mana friction.

,Barrier cutter equipment, huh? The three barriers I prepared were cut at once."

In return, the knife shattered, and the man's hand was burned by the intense mana friction reaction.

Fists, swords, and bullets surged towards me, targeting my momentarily defenseless state.

[Psychokinesis]

Using psychokinesis, I took out the [Plasma Battery] from my pocket and discharged it in an instant. A blue lightning explosion engulfed the surroundings in an instant. Swords fell and bullets bounced off due to electromagnetic force, and living things were electrocuted.

Of course, it had no effect on me. All of these lightning bolts are under my control.

Kwang!

,Is that some kind of new suicide method? Can't you see the remaining lightning around me?"

,If you want to kill a monster like you, we have to take risks too. And it's not like I came empty-handed."

The lightning scattered in the air tried to penetrate her body, but as soon as it touched her coat, it slid down and disappeared.

,A suit with a discharge effect? Even so, there must be a limit, right?"

Kwang!

As she pulled the trigger of her musket, another explosion occurred, scattering the current.

,It's not a special technique or magic. Is she causing explosions with some kind of marksmanship?"

I moved my hand. According to my gesture, the current remaining in the air clustered together and covered Woohee. Woohee rotated her body and musket as if dancing, reloading and pulling the trigger at the current.

Kwang!

It only looks like a musket, but it's actually a gun closer to a shotgun.

,You're a crazy bitch."

,If you want to kill an enemy stronger than me, you have to go crazy. Farewell, magician."

[Psychokinesis]
