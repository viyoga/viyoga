As the situation ends, the warriors nearby rush over in a frenzy.

"Your Majesty! Are you alright?!"

"To think that a mage bastard would launch an attack!"

I stared indifferently at the warriors making a fuss. If they wanted to jump in, they could have done so earlier. Not after the situation is over.

"I'm tired of you guys dragging your feet. This is a royal order. All of you, charge."

"Y-Your Majesty. We are the war chiefs. We must protect Your Majesty and command the warriors...!"

Protect?

Those weaker than me are going to protect me? I remember that not a single one of them stepped forward when fighting Biifu just now, so what makes them think I'd entrust my protection to these bastards?

Annoyance surged within me. A fierce aura flowed out, threatening the warriors. The warriors quickly lowered their gaze.

I summoned an axe and hurled it at the head of the war chief who had defied me. The axe blade lodged in his skull, and lightning erupted.

Puh-ung!

The war chief's body exploded, scattering in all directions. The warriors froze instantly.

"I said it was a royal order. You have two choices. Charge, or die by my hand as traitors."

The warriors immediately turned around. They knew very well that I was the kind of guy who would kill them if they didn't like me, war chief or not.

Ppuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu!

The horn blared powerfully once more. The war chief and his warriors charged towards the castle.

I slowly walked towards the castle. Those who lagged behind me or tried to escape, taking advantage of the opportunity, had their heads split open with a thrown axe.

The warriors grit their teeth and charged towards the castle. Great warriors stepped forward, and the war chief urged the warriors on. The war accelerated rapidly. The number of warriors dying quickly increased.

Kuuuung!

The castle gate was breached. The massive gate, which seemed like it would remain closed forever, fell backward, opening the castle.

"Finally!!"

"It's time for plunder!"

"Thorn! You motherfuckers! Just die quietly!!"

The warriors cheered and entered.

Until now, the war would end once they entered the castle. Those who resisted were killed, and women were raped. Invading homes and looting treasures was basic. But this time, the situation was different.

"I'd rather die than become a slave to you barbarians!"

"Ompas-isiyeo! I will give you everything, please give me strength!"

The resistance of the Thorn Kingdom's people was fierce. Even without proper weapons like cooking utensils or wooden sticks, they desperately resisted. Normally, such resistance would be futile, but their bodies were mutating in real-time, giving the warriors a hard time.

"Did these bastards sell their souls to the devil?! They're a disgrace to humanity! Die!!"

"Damn it! I won't kill women! I said I won't kill them! Don't mutate!"

The castle instantly descended into chaos. The people of Thorn resisted to the end, and the warriors of Kairak, blinded by plunder, didn't listen to the war chief's commands.

I ignored the chaos and walked down the main street. My goal was the inner castle. I would capture the king of the Thorn Kingdom and find a way to steadily acquire Lerrit.

'Huh?'

The inner castle gate opens. Fully armed knights on horseback began to ride down the main street. Their momentum was incredibly fierce. Their gazes were solely focused on me. I could feel their determination to kill me no matter what.

'They don't seem to be in a normal state. Is it the influence of Ompas or something?'

I took out five anti-tank mines from my inventory and laid them on the main street. Then, I stepped back and watched the approaching knights.

"For Thorn!"

"We will kill the War King!"

As the distance between us closed, the knights shouted and increased their speed even more. Whether they were excited or drugged, one of the horses, greatly excited, stepped on an anti-tank mine.

Kwang!

Nearly twenty knights were caught in the explosion. Black smoke rose, and their flesh fell to the ground with a thud.

A knight who had lost his left arm emerged from the smoke. He pointed the sword in his right hand at me and charged. His blue aura blade shone even in the explosion.

I wasn't flustered at all. I already knew of his existence through my senses.

Thunder Sky Style Flying Thunder God.

Slipping away, dodging his attack by a hair's breadth, I struck his head with an axe. Already at his limit, he had no strength to block my counterattack. It was a pathetic end for an aura master.

I stepped over the aura master's corpse and headed towards the royal castle at the end of the main street.

"Euaaaaaaak!"

The soldiers and knights guarding the entrance to the royal castle fell without avoiding the blades of the Hwaryeon Bido. I killed everyone I saw and went straight to the audience chamber.

I saw a large, white door. I kicked the ornately carved door open and entered the audience chamber.

Inside the audience chamber, Hyaku, the king of Thorn, was sitting on the throne. He looked weak. He had a dying expression on his face.

"...So you've come this far."

"What are you posturing for? You've lost. Tell me how to obtain Lerrit. Then I'll spare you and your family."

"Lerrit! Is that why you joined the war in the Eastern Continent, because you coveted Lerrit?"

"Not just that."

I approached him with the Hwaryeon Bido. Hyaku was trembling, but he wasn't giving in. Cutting off his limbs would help him make future choices.

"What will you do after I surrender?"

"Of course, the Thorn Kingdom will disappear and be unified with Kairak. Continental unification. Peace will continue."

"As a duke from the Western Continent, you must know how uncivilized and barbaric Kairak is. Kairak's warrior culture will hinder the development of the Eastern Continent. Is that your goal? To hinder the development of the Eastern Continent?"

"We'll colonize the Eastern Continent. There's no plan beyond that, though?"

As long as I could stick a straw into the Eastern Continent and suck up all the honey, that was enough. I didn't care what happened to the Eastern Continent. He tilted his head at the word 'colonize'. The concept of colonization didn't exist in this world yet.

"I don't know what you mean, but... I can tell it's not a good intention."

Hyaku looked alternately at the ceiling and the floor with dying eyes, then said quietly.

"Ompas-isiyeo. He is the center of Kairak. If he dies, Kairak's morale will fall, and Kairak's warriors will start a civil war over the position of War King. Please kill him for Thorn."

"A king who doesn't even think about fighting himself and clings to a spirit."

I openly mocked him. Hyaku closed his eyes and didn't respond.

-It shall be done.

My steps stopped. It appeared in the space between me and Hyaku.

It was fire. It was also water and rock. It was human, beast, and insect. It was everything in this world.

I frowned. I couldn't define anything. My eyes hurt from looking at it. Unrelated things were changing rapidly. It was distracting. Looking at it made me annoyed.

[Opening the Celestial Eye.]

Now I can see what that thing is.

'An amorphous being. It is nothing, so it can be anything. Is that it?'

There were quite a few strange things about calling it a spirit of this world. It felt closer to a demon than a spirit.

I can perceive it. Then I can fight it. I surrounded the Hwaryeon Bido with blue strong energy.

-You saw through me? You are an excellent warrior and an excellent sage.

"An excellent sage? That's right. My intelligence is a bit outstanding."

-Hmm? I can sense stupidity in your tone. Are you not a sage?

"You little shit. Are you daring to make fun of me?!"

I approached and swung my sword. Ompas flew upwards, dodging the sword.

It was a good sign. Dodging means it's dangerous. If my attack posed no danger, it wouldn't have dodged.

'It can't ignore the aura blade, even if it ignores everything else.'

Jijijik.

Lightning rose from the center of my left hand. Waving my left hand, I scattered lightning towards Ompas. Ompas didn't dodge. It calmly accepted the current. The blue current passed through Ompas. As if there was nothing there.

"What is it?"

-I am the spirit of everything in this world. Lightning is also a part of me. It doesn't make sense for me to harm myself, does it?

"Is there no aura blade in everything in this world?"

-That's special. It's the expression of will using mana as a material by masters who have reached the extreme. In a very broad sense, you could call it a spirit.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 11]

I closed the distance in an instant and swung my sword. Ompas floated upwards as if it knew everything, dodging the sword.

I spread out my left hand. Pajijijik! Lightning soared from the center of my palm, then compressed and rotated to amplify.

Thunder Sky Style Myriad Lightning.

Lightning was fired towards Ompas. Ompas didn't dodge as if it was natural. That was its mistake. The essence of Myriad Lightning was mana. There was no reason why I couldn't imbue my will into using my mana.

-Aaaaaaak!

The electrocuted Ompas screamed and fell.

'Things are ending more easily than I thought.'

I swung the Hwaryeon Bido at the falling Ompas.

Ompas's existence suddenly changed. From an amorphous form, it transformed into a middle-aged man. He wore leather armor and held shining swords in both hands. Swords covered in aura blades. Those swords accurately pierced my gaps.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 10]

I retreated with Flying Thunder God.

"Too bad."

The man clicked his tongue and adjusted his posture. He held the swords with both hands and brought them to shoulder level, pointing the tips at me.

"Who the hell are you?"

"You should know. I am Ompas."

"Everything has changed, from your tone to your form and presence. Is that your ability?"

"Among the abilities I have, there aren't many that directly involve combat. The most efficient one is becoming the form of a human I contracted with."

Ompas lowered his stance. Bending his knees and putting strength in his ankles, he charged like a rocket.

His sword came in from below the waist, from a low position. I raised my sword to block his attack.

Chaeng!

The deflected Ompas turned his body and charged again as before. I let out a hollow laugh.

"You fight like a beast."

"This body was called Wolf Sword Jefra."

"You seem more like a son of a bitch than a wolf?"

"All those who call me a son of a bitch are dead. I'll cut your neck off like those bastards."

Ompas growled.

But what a pity. I've already figured out his fighting style.
