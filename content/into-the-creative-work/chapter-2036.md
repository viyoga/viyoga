The Korean Hero Association sent a message to all A-rank heroes.

-We have located Red Light. A-rank heroes and above, proceed to the following location and stop Red Light. Red Light has a high probability of becoming an S-rank villain in the future, so disregard whether they live or die.

Most A-rank heroes ignored the message.

Red Light was strong. Strong enough that even A-rank heroes couldn't take them lightly. It wasn't for nothing that the Association said they might become an S-rank villain. Many A-rank heroes didn't want to take the risk. Most were satisfied with their current lives.

On the other hand, A-rank heroes who wanted money and fame headed to Nampo City. Red Light, the famous dark hero these days. Just defeating them would bring money and fame. Some even had the ulterior motive that it might be easy if they teamed up with other A-rank heroes.

Rarely, there were heroes who acted with a sense of justice. Those who judged Red Light's actions as wrong.

Myeong Jia, known as Iron Storm, was one of them.

,People call Red Light a dark hero, but... they're a villain pretending to be a dark hero.'

Red Light's actions were like that. Even if she couldn't explain it in detail, she felt that they were fundamentally different from ordinary dark heroes.

Red Light kills villains. Criminals. It doesn't matter whether the criminal is an Awakened or non-Awakened. If they look like a criminal, they just kill them. They don't distinguish between the severity of the crime. They simply judge by killing.

,A murderer and a thief can't be the same. But Red Light doesn't distinguish between murderers and thieves.'

To her, that wasn't justice.

,Red Light is crazy, just like their name.'

Not just crazy, but a crazy bastard with power. Before Red Light goes even crazier, before that spearhead is aimed at innocent people, she has to subdue them.

She rode her bike and sped towards Nampo City.
