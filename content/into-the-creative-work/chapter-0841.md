Utemo's warhammer comes crashing down. I use psychokinesis to evade it, pulling my body backward with psychic power. This unconventional evasion proves effective.

Kwaang! The warhammer slams into the ground, causing the earth to cave in and fragments to fly. The sheer force is terrifying.

,Simple and ignorant,.

,A fight doesn't need to be complicated! Crush the enemy's skull! That's my kind of battle!,

,I understand,.

Utemo's fighting style is purely about power. That doesn't mean he's not agile. With such immense strength, agility naturally follows. Just seeing him keep up with my movements shows how fast he is.

,How long are you going to keep running away!,

I glance towards the city gate. Imperial soldiers are pouring in alongside the knights, engaging the dwarves in battle. With the mounted knights rampaging through the ranks, the advantage lies with the Empire.

,It doesn't look like I need to help. And I've got a rough idea of Utemo's fighting style. Shall we get down to a real fight now,.

I summon five doppelganger swords, using psychokinesis to control each one and send them flying towards him. Utemo ignores the doppelganger swords, not even giving them a glance as he advances towards me. The five swords are effortlessly blocked by the rock shield surrounding his body and bounce away.

However, instead of falling to the ground, the deflected swords surge back towards Utemo. After a few strikes, cracks appear on the rock shield, and it shatters. But a new shield immediately appears to block the swords again.

,Nothing lasts forever. If you keep hammering away, eventually it'll break. Lightning strike!,

Electricity courses through the ground. Utemo twitches, but that's all. He continues to walk steadily.

,It's minimal, but it's not entirely ineffective,.

I envelop myself in lightning and charge towards Utemo.

Sword clashes against warhammer. In a direct confrontation, the warhammer has the advantage. I twist my sword to deflect the force of the warhammer. It's impossible to completely nullify the impact. My stance falters.

,A fleeting moment!,

An opening appears. A gap in the rock shield created by the five doppelganger swords. I thrust my sword into that gap. The sword meets Utemo's shoulder armor. It doesn't seem like ordinary armor; it emits a subtle light and resists the sword.

,This sword isn't ordinary either,.

I exert more force on the hand gripping Arondight. The condensed Aura Blade pierces through the armor, and the sword touches Utemo's shoulder.

,Kheu-eup!!,

Utemo suppresses a scream. He doesn't retreat but prepares a counterattack. A rock spike appears beside me, aiming for my flank.

,You'll be the one to die first,.

I change the direction of my force, aiming for his neck instead of his shoulder. The moment Arondight is about to cut through his flesh, my body is suddenly flung backward. Utemo's rock spike hadn't even touched me.

,...What?!,

This sensation. I feel a sense of déjà vu. It feels like I've experienced this before.

,...It's similar to when the High Priestess of the Earth Mother cursed me and banished me from the land,.

Flung backward, I regain my balance using psychokinesis. The blood flowing from Utemo's shoulder stops. No, it's healed.

,Earth Mother-isiyeo, I thank you!,

,...Earth Mother? Was he a priest? He didn't look like one at all, though,.

,I am not a priest. It's just that the Earth Mother has lent me her aid. It seems the Earth Mother dislikes you quite a bit,.,

,...!,

Pressure is exerted on my body. Gravity suddenly doubles in intensity. I sense neither mana nor divine power. Nature itself is hindering me. It's difficult to move. The five doppelganger swords that were attacking Utemo have already fallen to the ground, unable to move.

,A god... is directly interfering?!,

,All the land in this world is the Earth Mother's domain,.

Utemo's eyes glow. A massive rock spike appears between him and me. The rock spike is compressed, becoming smaller. A chilling sound, as if the earth is breaking, echoes.

,Emperor-isiyeo. Do you see the light of Galatine? This light heralds your evil and promises my victory,.

The ability to amplify light when the opponent is evil, giving the wielder that much power.

It was a troublesome ability. If not for that ability, Utemo would never have been able to fight me on equal terms.

,Emperor-isiyeo! Die here!,

Utemo swings his warhammer, striking the compressed spike from behind.

,Heavenly Heart, Fleeting Moment,.

I use two skills in quick succession. Heavenly Heart frees my body from the influence of gravity, and Fleeting

Moment accelerates me. I narrowly evade the incoming rock spike and swing my sword at him.

It connects. The enhanced physical abilities from Heavenly Heart shine. I shatter all the rock shields protecting Utemo, tear through his armor, and inflict a fatal wound on his body.

,...!!,

Horrified, Utemo raises his warhammer high, intending to strike my torso. It's simple. I predicted that attack even before he lifted the warhammer. I evade Utemo's attack and aim for his neck this time.

-My dear friend!

A heavy voice, so powerful it makes the air tremble, echoes. At the same time, Utemo vanishes. My sword only cuts through empty air.

,Tsk,.

Clicking my tongue, I raise my head.

There was a dragon. And Utemo was riding on the dragon's back.

A massive body that must be at least 100 meters long. Scales that look like they're made of solid rock. Threatening horns and wings that blot out the sun. Golden eyes look down upon the city and cast magic. Rocks created in the sky fall to the ground, attacking the Imperial soldiers.

,Your Majesty! Are you unharmed?!,

,...Great General. I am fine. More importantly, do you know what that damn lizard is?,

,It is Telgmaho, the guardian dragon of Tortos, a earth dragon. The current King, Utemo, is said to have a long-standing friendship with it. ...I never imagined a dragon would appear in person to save Tortos....,

Luteon said. Dragons are originally detached from the human world. Even a guardian dragon rarely appears in person to help a country.

,No. It didn't appear to save the country or to help Utemo. It appeared to kill me. I can feel the dragon's tingling bloodlust even from here,.

,...Your Majesty. The appearance of a dragon is an unprecedented situation,.

,Do you think we should retreat?,

,...No. Retreat is impossible. The dragon's most powerful attack is its breath, but Telgmaho is currently refraining from using it,.

,It must not want to harm the dwarves. Great General, command the battlefield. Kill all the dwarves. I will slay the dragon,.

,I shall obey your command!,

I use psychokinesis to soar into the sky. The closer I get to the dragon, the more I realize how small my sword is compared to its size.

, Meteor Sword Bracelet (Liúxīng Jiàn Chuàn).,

It's a bracelet-shaped magical artifact that I stole after killing Meteor Sword Head of the family in the Guangmyeong Ascension Hall. Its original name is Meteor Sword, but I call it Meteor Sword Bracelet for convenience. It merged with Stormbreaker as soon as it was summoned. With this, the effects of Meteor Sword Bracelet, influenced by Stormbreaker, will be amplified several times over.

,First Meteor Sword,.

I summon a meteor sword from the sky. Absorbing my energy, a massive sword imbued with lightning appears in the sky and plummets vertically towards the dragon. The sword's length alone exceeds 300 meters, an ultra-large size. The dragon is horrified and frantically flaps its wings to evade.

The meteor sword falls straight to the ground and is embedded in the dwarven city. Lightning erupts from the sword, spreading in all directions. Both enemy and ally alike, those nearby are killed.

I then summon a second and third meteor sword, aiming for the dragon. The dragon performs acrobatic maneuvers, evading all the meteor swords.

-You don't stop attacking even if allies get caught up in it. You're as crazy as I've heard.

The dragon muttered in a weary tone.

,Telgmaho-isiyeo! The Emperor must be killed here!,

-I know. The Earth Mother gave me a divine message. To help you destroy the Emperor who will disrupt the order of the continent and plunge countless lives into suffering!

The dragon flies towards me.

I feel the gravity pressing down on my body again. I stop my falling body with psychokinesis.

,Damn Earth Mother! I swear on everything I have. I will definitely fuck that bitch like a dog!,

Suppressing my rising breath, I swing Arondight towards the dragon. The sword containing the Aura Blade pierces the dragon's hard scales and inflicts a wound. However, compared to the dragon's size, my sword is no more than a toothpick. This level of damage is by no means fatal.

The dragon's tail lashes out at me like a whip. I grit my teeth and endure the impact, grabbing onto the scales of the dragon's tail. I plunge my sword into the tail. Dragon's blood flowed out, but it was far from being a fatal wound.

,Meteor Sword!!,

I summon three massive swords, aiming for the dragon's front, top, and sides respectively. The dragon flew nimbly, but it couldn't evade the sword on the left.

-Kuaaaaaaak!

The dragon's left wing was pierced and began to freeze.

The aura of cold.

It's a new ability I gained after consuming the heart of the Frost Worm, which Grand Duke Lyst gave me as a birthday present.

The dragon staggers and falls. I ride stably on its tail and rush towards Utemo. Utemo also runs towards me, holding his warhammer in both hands.

Kwaaang!

Sword and warhammer collide. I slashed Utemo's right shoulder, and my body was flung off the dragon's back. The moment I stopped myself from falling in the air with psychokinesis, a rock spike that flew up from below launched my body upward.

Immediately afterward, the dragon, which was floating in the sky with magic despite its torn wings, inhaled. It was absorbing the mana of the atmosphere. And then, it exhaled a breath towards the sky, towards me.

Seething hot lava breath rises. I wrapped my body in Aura Armor.

,Meteor Sword...!!,

A massive sword containing cold energy was created behind me and fell towards the lava breath. The meteor sword seemed to be holding up, but the tip of the sword began to melt.

One meteor sword is not enough.

I then created two more meteor swords. It's not enough. I summon two more. The momentum of the lava breath decreased. Nevertheless, it succeeded in melting and destroying half of my armor. Still, thanks to the Aura Armor and the armor, my body is fine.

,Meteor Sword!!,

I summon three meteor swords flowing with cold energy. I stack two massive meteor swords to block the weakened lava breath, and the remaining one pierces through the breath and penetrates the dragon's body.

-Unbelievable...! Is this truly the power that a single human can possess!!

The dragon couldn't withstand the power of the 300-meter-long meteor sword and crashed into the foothills. I then dropped two more meteor swords. The meteor swords pierce through the dragon's neck and belly. Then, the dragon's corpse was frozen by the cold energy of the meteor swords.

I used psychokinesis to land in front of the dragon's corpse. Because Utemo was there.

,Emperor!!,

Utemo runs towards me with all his might. There is no rock shield protecting his body. Like me, Utemo is also at his limit. Instead, there was a compressed rock spike in front of him.

,Did he get angry because the dragon died? The power is enormous, but it's full of openings,.

I can easily win with this much.

I ignored my pounding heart and raised my sword. I wanted to collapse from exhaustion right away, but it's not too late to do that after killing Utemo.

It was then. Suddenly, green plant stalks rose from the ground and wrapped around my legs, waist, and shoulders, restraining me.

,That damn bitch is interfering again!!,

Utemo is right in front of me.

With less than half of my armor remaining, I released Stormbreaker. The disassembled Stormbreaker became a hexagonal shield in front of me.

Kkwang!

The rock spike collided with the shield. I created lightning and burned all the plants that restrained my body without leaving any behind.

Utemo struck the spike with his warhammer. The Stormbreaker shield shattered into pieces. But Stormbreaker had already done its job. I narrowly evaded the rock spike that was aiming for my chest. My left shoulder, which was touched by the spike, was crushed into powder.

But my sword was successfully embedded in Utemo's head.

The battle was over.

Without the dragon and Utemo, the capital of Tortos will soon fall as well.

I won.
