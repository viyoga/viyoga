The area of his right arm that was cut by the ink-like sword energy began to turn pitch black.

Information flooded in through the Mind's Eye. This was erosion. It was trying to overwrite me. If my heart or brain were eroded, I'd die. I could resist this erosion by using my inner energy. The problem was that I had to constantly use my inner energy and focus my consciousness to do so.

,It's forcing me to multitask. It's preventing me from focusing solely on the battle.'

I might be able to eliminate the erosion with a higher-level technique, but I don't have the luxury to do so right now.

It's too wasteful to use Complete Recovery or Heavenly Heart.

,Shooting Star Sword.'

A Shooting Star Sword generated in mid-air fell and severed his eroded right forearm.

Sosan Hyeongeom was quietly impressed.

,That's amazing judgment. To immediately recognize the characteristics of Black Ink and quickly make the decision to sever one's own body part is difficult even for a master. Especially if that master hasn't reached the Harmony Realm.'

Upon reaching the Harmony Realm, one could quickly regenerate severed limbs without using techniques.

,Worry about yourself instead of being impressed.'

I pointed at Sosan Hyeongeom with my remaining left hand. Hundreds of Shooting Star Swords appeared in mid-air, pointing at him and firing.

Sosan Hyeongeom drew a picture in the air with his black sword. The ink didn't fall but remained in the air. What he drew was a flowing river, and the hundreds of Shooting Star Swords were sucked into the flowing river and shattered. They couldn't even explode.

,Hyeonpil Hyeongeom (Presenting Brush Mysterious Sword). A magical artifact that temporarily materializes what is drawn with ink. A magical artifact created exclusively for Sosan Hyeongeom.'

Sosan Hyeongeom flew towards me. I gripped the Hwaryeon Bido in my left hand and clashed with him.

When I unleashed black lightning, Sosan Hyeongeom scattered black ink. As a result, the sky was as black as night despite it being daytime.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art). Cheonma Gunrimbo (Demonic Monarch's Descent Step).

I stepped on the ground in mid-air. Sosan Hyeongeom, who was in front of me, had to take the full brunt of the Cheonma Gunrimbo's shockwave. His body momentarily froze.

A once-in-a-lifetime opportunity to attack.

I unleashed the Hwaryeon Bido's domain and swung the knife. Red lightning glowed from the tip of the blade.

Ink gushed out from Sosan Hyeongeom's Hyeonpil Hyeongeom, which couldn't move, and blocked the Hwaryeon Bido's blade. However, the blade was surrounded by the domain. It cut through the ink and severed Sosan Hyeongeom's waist. His upper and lower body separated while spraying blood... Suddenly, his ink changed like ink, and his upper and lower body reattached.

He swung his sword. With a single swing, an ink dragon was drawn and rushed towards me. I barely blocked it by holding out the tattered Stormbreaker, but I couldn't withstand the force and crashed to the ground.

His whole body was soaked in ink.

Pajijijijik!

Before the ink could seep into my body, I ignited it with Black Lightning. I was about to get up again and attack Sosan Hyeongeom when a man ran up from the side.

,Little Cheonma!! Heaven is giving me a chance! I, Guju Ilgwon (Nine Pearl One Fist) Jeong Somu! I will kill you and earn merit!

A martial artist of the 9th Dan of Asceticism, half out of his mind, charged at me.

It seemed I was being severely underestimated. My right arm was cut off, I was attacked and crashed to the ground, and I was bleeding from the mouth...

,There are too many who don't know their place. Even if all my limbs were severed, would I not be able to kill a single bug.'

Inner energy and will moved.

A black lightning bolt struck the martial artist who was rushing towards me from the clear sky. The martial artist's body and soul were torn apart, burned, and disappeared.

A shadow fell over my body.

I raised my head and looked at the sky, only to see a Mount Tai drawn in ink falling to the ground.

Its sole purpose was to crush me to death.

Pajijijijik!

Black Lightning rotated and gathered in my severed right arm. It repeatedly amplified and compressed in the style of Myriad Lightning, taking the form of an arm.

I could feel sensation in my right arm made of black lightning. The sensation was an electrical signal. This lightning had completely become a part of my body.

Kkwaak.

I clenched my right hand into a fist.

I punched towards the falling black Mount Tai.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art) Dragon Fist.

The energy emitted from the black lightning fist pierced the center of Mount Tai. The form of Mount Tai collapsed, and a massive amount of ink fell to the ground like a flood. However, it wasn't as dense as Mount Tai.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Biraeshin (Flying Thunder God).

I transformed into lightning and stood in the sky, avoiding the falling flood of ink.

,Don't you have to stop it?'

I frowned at Sosan Hyeongeom's words, then realized that the direction of the flood was towards my allies. At this rate, more than half of my allied forces would be wiped out by the ink flood.

The deaths of allied soldiers were something I had prepared for. But losing more than half was not good. The Cheonma Shin-gyo's power would weaken drastically.

,You stop it too.'

,What?'

I used Shooting Star Sword.

10,000 Shooting Star Swords appeared in the sky. The tips of the swords were pointed not at Sosan Hyeongeom, but at the warriors of the Righteous Alliance. Sosan Hyeongeom distorted his face and swung his sword into the sky. In that gap, I moved to deal with the wave of ink.

Using Biraeshin, I appeared in front of the ink flood, spread my arms, grabbed the air, and twisted it.

Cheonma Shingong (Demonic Divine Art) Hwicheon Maryong (Returning Demonic Dragon) Cheonji Hwicheon (Heaven and Earth Return).

Space was inverted. The ink flood soared into the sky and was torn apart. But it didn't disappear and fell back to the ground. It was clear that it would become a flood again if left like this.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Heaven Style) Yeolroe (Fissure Lightning).

Pajijijijik!

The lightning flowing from both hands split and weaved through the ink falling from the sky, burning it.

,Keuheok!'

I couldn't hold back the reversing blood and vomited.

My blood and energy were twisted, probably because I had been continuously using Black Lightning and powerful techniques.

,Bugun (Husband)!'

Ryu Yangmi, who was nearby, ran over. She was also dripping with sweat. She looked quite exhausted. It was natural, since she had been single-handedly blocking the Blood Demon Society's sorcery hidden within the enemy forces.

,I'm okay.'

I looked at Sosan Hyeongeom, who had dealt with the 10,000 falling Shooting Star Swords. Sosan Hyeongeom also looked at me. We silently scanned each other for a while before he spoke.

,Today's battle ends here. Temporary retreat.'

,What? We haven't seized victory yet, but we haven't been defeated either.'

,Even if we win, it's meaningless if more than half of our allied forces are wiped out. This war is just the beginning.'

I had to consider the aftermath of the war.

Sosan Hyeongeom's goal wasn't necessarily to kill me. Sosan Hyeongeom's goal was victory.

We were dancing in a game set up by others, so there was no hatred that would make us abandon everything to kill the enemy.

,With that short battle just now, both I and Sosan Hyeongeom realized that the other was not an easy opponent. We are both reluctant to engage in an all-out war. He and I are thinking about the aftermath of the war.'

In fact, the enemies began to retreat. I spat the blood pooling in my mouth onto the ground and turned around, returning to the makeshift Cheonma Shin-gyo camp's tent. The Black Lightning that had been forming my right arm disappeared.

,Bugun (Husband). I can regenerate that right arm with sorcery.'

,I have a way too.'

[Using Complete Recovery.]

My right arm was regenerated without a single scar. The twisted blood and energy also returned to normal. My stamina and inner energy were also in top condition. Ryu Yangmi's eyes widened in surprise. Seeing Ryu Yangmi's surprised expression outside of the bedroom was quite enjoyable.

The executives began to gather inside the tent. Their expressions weren't very good. I listened to the executives' reports. There were no particular points except for Ryu Yangmi's report.

,I interfered with the Blood Demon Society hidden among the enemies from using large-scale evil sorcery. I felt a strong persistence from them targeting me.'

,That evil sorcery, is it enough to change the course of the war at once?'

,I can block the evil sorcery because I am here. I am the Blood Godly Woman. I know more about the Blood Demon Church's evil sorcery than anyone else, and I can use it better than anyone else.'

,The victory or defeat of this war depends on me and Sosan Hyeongeom. You guys move to preserve the Shin-gyo's forces as much as possible. The Righteous Alliance will probably do the same, so it won't be difficult. Danmok Soha.'

,Huh? Oh, yes.'

,You focus on targeting the Blood Demon Society hidden among the enemies. Show them no mercy.'

After the short meeting, I sent the people out of the tent and sat cross-legged in the center of the tent, closing my eyes.

Originally, I should have relieved my heated body with a woman's body, but the battle from just now kept flashing before my eyes, bothering me.

,If I use the Heaven-Breaking Demonic Art, I can definitely kill Sosan Hyeongeom. But... I'm gaining quite a bit from the battle with him.'

I had experienced grueling training like a real battle through my teacher, Wiyu-ssi. I had been sliced by her countless times. But training was training. It couldn't compare to a real battle.

,The Cheonma or the Martial Alliance Leader are probably watching this war. There's a high probability that the Cheonma knows about the Heaven-Breaking Demonic Art. I don't want to show my hidden card to those bastards.'

As I observed myself, I saw a wall blocking me.

I realized that this wall had become slightly thinner.

• • •

I waged war with the Righteous Alliance for a whole week.

Like handling business by meeting at a set time and place, we pointed weapons at each other and fought.

I had gotten used to it, but war was war. There were deaths every day.

I met Sosan Hyeongeom again today. We drew our weapons at the same time and revealed our killing intent, but we didn't immediately charge at each other.

Sosan Hyeongeom said.

,It poured rain last night... but now the weather is sunny again. Did you sleep well?'

,I should be asking you that. You're the one who lost an arm yesterday.'

,Ah. Those Blood Demon Society bastards are gloomy and disgusting, but their skills are quite outstanding.'

Sosan Hyeongeom exhaled. Inner energy leaked from his breath, and that inner energy became ink, swirling around him and fluctuating.

I also focused and breathed. Pajijik. Black lightning mixed with demonic energy sparked from my whole body.

,This past week. Little Cheonma, this week fighting you has been an ecstatic time as a martial artist. If I had my way, I'd want to fight not just for a week, but for a whole year.'

,With you for a whole year? You're saying terrible things.'

,I know. But we both have too much to bear to do that. Let's settle things today. To be honest, if you grow any more than this... I don't think I'll be able to win.'

,I agree with the suggestion to settle things.'

Today, black lightning and ink collided again. Today, the sky was dyed black again.

I thought as I faced Sosan Hyeongeom's sword.

Sosan Hyeongeom's Black Ink and my Black Lightning.

They were different powers, but their essence was the same in that they were influenced by the power of the mind.

His Black Ink was, how should I put it? One step faster than the Black Lightning I used. More efficient, perhaps. Unlike how I had to squeeze out my will to create Black Lightning, the Black Ink seemed to react naturally.

It was as if there was no intermediate process.

If my Black Lightning was like processing raw materials in a factory, Sosan Hyeongeom's Black Ink was like drawing directly from the raw materials.

,Does that mean it's more cumbersome because there are more processes on this side.'

It was as if he was taking the ink directly from nature and using it.

,From nature?'

Was that possible?

The essence of Black Ink was ultimately the power of the mind. The mind was something within me, which could be said to be the heart.

,Ah.'

An epiphany came suddenly.

,I was part of nature from the beginning.'

What I thought was a wall was not a wall.

It was a boundary that looked like a wall.

A boundary separating me from the world.

I crossed the boundary.

I became the world, and the world became me.

I achieved harmony with the world.
