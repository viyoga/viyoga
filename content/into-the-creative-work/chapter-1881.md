By the time we reached the 5th floor, the security team was visibly exhausted. Considering they'd taken down about 6-7 monster spiders on each floor, they'd killed over 20 monster spiders just getting to the 5th floor.

It wasn't exactly a grand achievement. In my opinion, these monster spiders weren't fully grown yet. But they're panting and puffing after only taking down 20? I know stamina drops rapidly in combat, but aren't these guys supposed to be experts, being a security team?

,And six of them got hurt.'

None of them had life-threatening injuries. I decided to fire these six from the security team. They should be grateful they're not getting expelled from Sungak Elementary School.

,Injured personnel, get first aid and then transport them out.'

Park Hae-gil said. The guy whose right hand was fucked up and was subtly ignored by the security team was displaying charisma in a real combat situation. I just watched from behind.

Gu Tae-hee came up next to me and put a cigarette in her mouth.

,You smoke?'

,Nah. Women hate the smell of cigarettes.'

,Well, I kind of like it.'

,That's not even a cigarette.'

It looks like a cigarette on the outside, but it's not. I can tell by the smell. It's a joint made of marijuana. She smelled like burning dry grass.

,I'm addicted to weed. I can't live without it.'

Maybe it was the weed, but Gu Tae-hee's body seemed a bit more relaxed. But her eyes were as sharp as ever.

,That guy, Park Hae-gil, was it?'

,Yeah. He used to be the president of PS Security. He's from a special forces unit, but I don't know which one. Don't really care.'

,He's useful. You don't find guys like that often. Especially not in this world.'

,I don't know.'

Park Hae-gil is useful? He might seem that way right now.

,Looks like you disagree with me. Can I hear your reasons?'

,Opinions can differ.'

,I'm pretty confident in my judgment of people, but I make mistakes sometimes. A guy like you wouldn't underestimate him just because you don't like him. You must have evaluated him based on criteria and information I missed. Your opinion would be helpful to me, so tell me what you think. If you really don't want to, that's fine.'

,It's not hard to talk about it. You know why that guy's right hand is fucked up? Because of his meddling. He ignored my orders and tried to save someone, and that's what happened to him.'

,...I see. That's a bit difficult.'

,And what's the point of him being a good commander here? I'd bet my hand he can't handle a single variant properly if one shows up. What's important in this era isn't leadership. It's power.'

,Power...'

,If I were to fight 100 of my subordinates, including you, who do you think would win? You guys are armed with bulletproof vests and assault rifles, and the battlefield is in your favor.'

,......'

Gu Tae-hee furrowed her brow and took a long drag of her weed. A battle between me and her must be playing out in her head.

Then, she said with a serious expression.

,That's a problem. I can't think of a way to win in a head-on fight. I don't think I could win even with a tank.'

,Head-on, huh. What about assassination?'

,Of course. You're still human, so you'll let your guard down. You have to eat, you have to take a dump, and you'll do meaningless things for entertainment. People can't help but let their guard down eventually, unless they're machines. Like this.'

Gu Tae-hee held a small knife in her left hand and pointed it at my neck. It was shorter than a finger, but it was long enough to kill a person.

I was surprised. I didn't sense any killing intent, so I didn't react. I thought she was just moving her hand to take another hit of weed. Like she said, I let my guard down.

,Even if you have monstrous strength, there are plenty of ways to kill you as long as you're human.'

,Impressive. But that's too conventional.'

,...Conventional?'

I grabbed Gu Tae-hee's hand holding the knife. Not to push it away, but to pull it in. The small blade cut into my skin. It didn't stop there; the blade cut through my arteries and veins.

The pain and foreign sensation in my neck. Even the feeling of blood draining out. It was a very Shitty feeling. But it wasn't unbearable.

Before me, Gu Tae-hee was covered in blood and in shock.

,So even a battle-hardened mob boss can be surprised like that.'

I released her hand. She hurriedly pulled out the small knife.

,What the fuck? Are you crazy?!'

,Take a good look. I'm not dead.'

I forced myself to speak, even though it was difficult. It seems my vocal cords were injured too. I focused my mind on the painful wound. Gu Tae-hee's mouth fell open. Her one good eye was wide. Reflected in her shaking pupil was the wound healing at a rapid pace.

In less than 30 seconds, the wound had completely disappeared. Only the bright red blood proved it had ever been there.

,Do you think you can kill me by crushing my heart or cutting off my head? You called me a monster. Why do you think monsters are monsters? Because common sense doesn't apply to them. Don't think too conventionally from now on.'

,I don't think there are many people like you, but...'

,Even if there aren't many, there will be some. This world is full of idiots and crazy bastards.'

,Is this a warning?'

,Well, yeah. There are always guys who try to stab you in the back when you're trying to get along. In those cases, I just show them a little bit of my power.'

Then they know to behave.

The reason Tae-wang is being so cooperative with me right now is because he thinks I'm dangerous. Rather than turning me into an enemy, he'd rather take a small loss and keep me as an ally. That's more beneficial for him. This is based on my experience.

,If you try to take too much... you'll end up like that vampire detective.'

Wasn't that what happened when I kept exploiting everyone, and the whole world rebelled? Even the South Korean government. Of course, the world was destroyed as the price for that global rebellion.

,If I want to exploit people for a long time... I need to tone down my temper.'

I wiped the blood off my neck.

,I heard that Awakened ones grow stronger as they use their abilities. Don't tell me you're hurting yourself on purpose?'

,I do it sometimes. Dr. Na doesn't seem to like it much, but that's the way to grow my abilities.'

That's how I found out that focusing on the wound helps it heal faster.

,...Why do you go that far? You're already strong enough, even without doing crazy things like that.'

,I can't let some chumps get the better of me. Those chumps started a riot earlier. The moment they think I'm weak, they'll try to take what's mine. Training is a pain, and I hate being hurt, but I hate having my stuff taken away even more.'

,...I think I understand what kind of guy you are now.'

Gu Tae-hee, having quickly regained her composure, took out a new marijuana joint and put it in her mouth. I leaned against the window. Recovering my neck earlier took quite a bit of stamina. But if I stay still like this, my stamina will recover thanks to my regeneration. Regeneration is a very helpful ability.

About three minutes passed, and Gu Tae-hee dusted off her hands.

,That should be enough of a break. Let's move.'

We headed to the 6th floor.

There were no gunshots on the 6th floor. That's because there wasn't a single monster spider. Even after searching every corner, we couldn't find any.

,It's certain that the spiders were here until just now. There are traces left behind.'

Park Hae-gil said. His face was serious.

,There's only one reason why the spiders would suddenly move. The Queen Spider summoned them. It's an all-out war.'

Gu Tae-hee clicked her tongue. Even if we're armed, an all-out war changes things. Monster spiders are fundamentally stronger than humans. If they use some of the monster spiders as meat shields and charge, it will be difficult to handle.

,We need to prepare thoroughly before we go.'

The 7th and 8th floors were also empty. The 9th floor seemed to be a breeding ground. There were eggs. Some of them even had baby spiders in them. Even the baby spiders were the size of human heads.

,If we leave them alive, they'll cause trouble later. Don't show them any mercy just because they're babies. Kill them all.'

Gu Tae-hee ordered her subordinates. They swung their maces and smashed the baby spiders and eggs.

Watching that scene, I turned on the walkie-talkie.

,Dr. Na. Are you watching?'

-...I can't watch everything. I'm busy cleaning up the mess on this end. There are too many people who participated in the rebellion, so we don't have enough prison cells. We need to make restraints separately.

,Leave that small stuff to the underlings. Do you need those baby spiders? As research subjects.'

-There might be something we can get from the byproducts of the monster spiders. But I don't think it would be that helpful. I'm a technologist, not a bionics expert. Unless it's a special material like the Queen Spider's heat-resistant spiderweb that you mentioned earlier, it's more beneficial to do technological research.

,Technological research is slow.'

-That's because I'm doing it alone. It would be great if we had more researchers, but... you know how hard it is to find trustworthy research personnel.

,Hmm. I can't help that either. Even if I forcibly bring them in and make them work, it's not like they'll do a good job if it's not just simple labor.'

I sympathized with her and quickly got to the point.

,Four combat drones. Can you support us? You've subdued all those retards, right?'

-...You want me to send combat drones all the way over there?

,It'll be quick if they fly. You've improved the batteries, so it's enough, right? We have to fight a full-scale war here. The spider bastards are definitely planning an all-out war. We can't let them control us.'

-I've sent them. They'll arrive in 3 minutes... no, 2 minutes. If the drones break down, make sure to collect the wreckage. It's several times more efficient to repair and reuse them than to make new ones.

,Thanks.'

Two minutes.

We waited a bit and then went up the stairs to the 10th floor.

It was quiet. That's why we were even more tense.

,Should I eat Bister II now? The combat drones might be effective. The suicide drones didn't work, but combat drones are different...'

-I've arrived near the building. The Queen Spider is looking up. What should I do? Attack right now?

,Wait.'

It would be more effective for the combat drones to show off their firepower if they aggro the Queen Spider. I took the lead and kicked open the door to the 10th floor office.
