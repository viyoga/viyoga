Flying through the subspace, I reached my destination and immediately reached out to tear through the dimension.

A dimensional rift occurred, and an entrance opened. Without hesitation, I entered the dimensional rift.

It was a desolate dimension. The ground was bright red, and the sky was gloomy. Clouds or a sun were nowhere to be seen. Only a few stars twinkled.

Outwardly, this world looked dead. There was no glorious past, only ashes waiting to disappear.

But through the eyes of a god, this world was alive. It had its own order and civilization. Creatures existed. Although everything was for Brahmasen.

,Brahmasen is somewhere in this dimension. I can feel his presence. But where exactly is he?

Perhaps because it was Brahmasen's territory, his presence was felt everywhere. It was hard to pinpoint Brahmasen's exact location because this wasn't Earth.

,Fortunately, this isn't a pristine dimension, so there are no restrictions. Should I use my power to scan the entire world? Or should I conserve my power?

Even without restrictions, it's hard to use power recklessly like I did on Earth.

I was pondering this and that when I saw a creature walking weakly on the ground.

It was hunched over and had a head and limbs, but it wasn't human. Its eyes were large, and its skin was like rock. All three of its fingers were long and shaped like hooks.

I transformed into lightning and landed in front of it. Surprisingly, it remained calm.

"Offspring of Brahmasen, tell me where Brahmasen is."

Thud.

It knelt on the ground. It bowed its head. It seemed accustomed to dealing with transcendent beings.

"Yujin-nim! It is an honor to meet you!"

"...You know me? Who are you?"

I'm here for the first time, though?

It raised its head and opened its mouth wide. The flesh inside its haphazardly arranged yellow teeth split, bleeding, and a small bug popped out. It was a grotesque bug that looked like a mix of a larva and a fetus.

Something came to mind as soon as I saw it.

"A subordinate of Levre."

"Yes. We have been quietly focusing on breeding and survival in Brahmasen's territory under Levre-nim's orders. But serving Yujin-nim is a duty beyond breeding and survival."

"Brahmasen hasn't noticed your existence until now? Even if other places are different, this should be the dimension where he's settled."

"Brahmasen isn't interested in most of his followers. As long as we don't openly act out... we won't attract his attention."

"Why are you here?"

"Brahmasen's territories are organically connected, except in special cases. Traveling between Brahmasen's territories isn't a big problem."

"He dominates multiple dimensions.... He's quite something. Guide me to where Brahmasen's main body is."

"Yes. I will guide you. But...."

"But? What else is there?"

"I can't be sure if the one here is really his main body."

"It is the main body. His power is spread throughout this world."

It lowered its head and guided me to where Brahmasen was.

Its pace was slow, so I grabbed it by the neck and flew in the direction it pointed.

It was a temple. A temple carved out of three giant rocky mountains. The temple was still unfinished. Tens of thousands of creatures that looked like slaves were bringing rocks and stacking them like building a pyramid, carving as they went.

"We are building the temple according to Brahmasen's orders. It's been over 500 years in your time."

"He's doing something interesting."

I realized Brahmasen's intention. It was to strengthen his power. When people prayed to me on Earth, my power grew, albeit slightly. It might not be helpful right away, but the story would change after 10, 100, or 1,000 years.

I landed in front of the temple. I felt a huge presence inside the temple.

-A moth has come without knowing its future. Or have you come to find your grave?

Brahmasen's voice echoed. The creatures that were touched by the voice all went mad and started rampaging. They rushed at me, vaguely showing hostility.

"Yujin-nim, do as you wish. We will stop those things."

Levre's subordinates were fine. In addition, those who hadn't gone mad gathered in various places and blocked the mad ones.

"Why are you guys okay?"

"To survive in Brahmasen's territory, we had to adapt to his madness first. We can adapt to any environment... even madness is no exception."

Levre's subordinates fought the crazed followers. Thanks to them, I could save even a little bit of power.

Kiiiiiiiiing.

The Thunder Light Karma Wheel behind my back rotated.

Brahmasen showed no sign of coming out of the temple even with me in front of him. So I'll bury him along with the temple.

I created blue lightning and grabbed it with my hand. I imbued this seemingly ordinary lightning with karma. The karma of Earth, of humanity, accumulated by Trinity.

,Split apart with this world.

I threw the lightning.

I heard the sound of the lightning tearing apart Brahmasen's body as it burrowed into the temple. But I didn't hear his painful groan.

The lightning continued to cut through anything that stood in its way. Now, it needed to cause a powerful explosion to create a massive impact. It had to, but... the lightning disappeared.

The ground shook, and giant pillar-like tentacles shot out from the ground. Not just one. Even as this was happening, the tentacles continued to emerge. Feeling the power of Brahmasen within the tentacles, my expression hardened.

The tentacles swept around like whips and pounded the ground like rods.

,It's dangerous. Getting hit by even one of those tentacles would be fatal.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I transformed into lightning and dodged the tentacles, flying up into the sky. The tentacles chased after me. I flew higher, towards space.

"......."

Standing in space, I was speechless as I looked at the planet.

The tentacles covered the planet. That's no longer a planet. Brahmasen. That disgusting god of madness. Itself.

A vortex formed at the center of the planet. The vortex glowed red and became the shape of an eye, looking at me.

-Consider it an honor that I'm dealing with you directly.

"...The target is big, which is good. At least I won't miss."

Kiiiiiiiiiiiiing.

The Karma Wheel rotated faster than ever. The Thunder Light generated from the Karma Wheel was brighter than the sun for a moment.

I held the Hwaryeon Bido high.

Domain Expansion.

Starlight poured out from the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido. I gathered those starlights at the tip of the blade. Red lightning surged from the tip of the blade.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Tribulation (雷劫)

I swung the sword. The red lightning burned Brahmasen's flesh and exploded.

-Ughhhhh. That hurts. How long has it been since I felt pain.... It's almost welcome.

"......Why won't you die?"

-You expect me to die from an attack of that level? Are you serious?

That level? It was an attack that used the karma of Earth and me, and even the power of the Hwaryeon Bido. It was enough power to destroy a planet.

The reason that attack didn't work was... the difference in level was immense.

The karma he had accumulated was infinitely deeper than the karma of humanity accumulated on Earth.

Kiiii....

The rotating Thunder Light Karma Wheel stopped and disappeared. The Karma Wheel runs on karma as fuel. It was natural for it to stop since there was no karma. And karma was my very existence.

I felt my power draining and disappearing.

-Is that the end? How boring.

I gritted my teeth at Brahmasen's mockery.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

The karma that had been consumed was recovered. But the karma of humanity accumulated in Trinity was not recovered. I was barely maintaining this body with only the karma I possessed.

Kiiiiiiing.

The Thunder Light Karma Wheel began to rotate, but my will to fight had already been broken. I couldn't win even if I fought. With this level of power, I wouldn't be able to inflict even a small wound on him.

I sighed and reverse-summoned the Hwaryeon Bido.

-If you have nothing more to show, then just die.

Brahmasen's tentacles reached out.

Just as I was about to struggle, I felt gazes. Those gazes became a single shackle, binding me. I knew that all those gazes were Brahmasen.

An absurd hypothesis came to mind. Perhaps even this Brahmasen in front of me wasn't the real one.

The giant tentacle touched my body. My body immediately collapsed.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

In the midst of death, I used the Regression Ticket with only my consciousness.

[Using 1-Day Regression Ticket.]

[Regressing to one day ago.]

[The Play Life App is not affected by Regression.]

Everything began to return.

"......."

Crack!

The egg in my hand broke.

Sweating coldly, I looked around and saw that I was in the kitchen. I had returned to before I cooked the fried rice.

"Hoo, shit."

Brahmasen was much stronger than I had imagined. He could probably destroy Earth in an instant if he wanted to.

,But the reason he hasn't taken action himself is... to save even a little bit of power? Or is he working on other dimensions at the same time as Earth?

What should I do from now on?

Even if it wasn't Brahmasen, there were many dangers on Earth. Right now, there's a swarm of giant locusts hiding in the crater of Mount Fuji in Japan, and the Pylons in China are up to no good.

,Even if nothing else, I'm a little greedy for the Trinity that the Chairman of the World Hunter Association has. If I had that, I could become an S-class Hunter right now.

It was impossible to secretly go and steal it. The Trinity is in the form of a tattoo on the current Chairman James's body. He wouldn't give it to me even if I went and asked. He would rather be hostile to me.

The world now is different from the world before the regression. The World Hunter Association is not powerless.

,Ah, whatever. Shit. Let's just eat fried rice. Why should I bother? There's no benefit to be gained from suffering.

The locusts and Pylons aren't causing trouble right now.

Brahmasen? Even if that bastard goes crazy and invades Earth right now, I have insurance.

The Power of Life and Death and the Absolute Imperial Power.

No matter how great Brahmasen is, he can't beat the Play Life App. It was clear from the fact that he couldn't resist the Regression Ticket.

I made and ate fried rice.

It was delicious.
