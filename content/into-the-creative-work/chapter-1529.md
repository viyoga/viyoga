New managers sent by the Cheonma Shingyo appeared in Soyong City. I handed over the management authority to them and returned to the Cheonma Shingyo.

I continued to move busily even after that. Perhaps it was due to the long peace. The inside of the Cheonma Shingyo was more rotten than I thought. Cutting away the parts that needed to be cut, and saving the parts that needed to be saved. The Cheonma Shingyo moved busily.

Fortunately, the Murim Alliance was also focusing on internal reorganization. The inside of the Murim Alliance was even more serious than that of the Cheonma Shingyo. Unlike the Cheonma Shingyo, which is a single organization, the Murim Alliance is a coalition of orthodox sects centered around the Nine Great Sects. Their internal affairs were bound to be complicated.

That's how three years passed.

During that time, there were several minor battles between the Cheonma Shingyo and the Murim Alliance. They were merely skirmishes to gauge each other's skills.

However, these minor battles accumulated, and the hatred and anger towards each other began to exceed the limit. Three years ago, even those who cried out for peace now shouted for war as their hatred grew.

I was at the Red Demise Corps headquarters. The atmosphere in the Red Demise Corps was ominous. The Red Demise Corps was one of the top combat units within the Cheonma Shingyo. Naturally, they received a lot of information.

,The Beggars' Sect. Those goddamn beggar bastards are searching for Shingyo spies with their eyes wide open, I hear. They find them and beat them to death like dogs, they say."

,The Gui Cheol Corps annihilated three combat units of the Kunlun Sect. Even the Gui Cheol Corps, which is inferior to us, is fighting on the front lines... When the hell are we going to deploy?"

,You guys. Have you heard the news about the Hwasan Sect? Those cocky flower-boy bastards publicly criticized us. Monsters wearing human skin? Parasites eating away at the world? An evil of the Jianghu that shouldn't be left alone? They spouted all sorts of bullshit, I heard."

,It's not just that. The Plum Blossom Sword Masters raided a Shingyo branch and massacred all the Shingyo people."

,Is that why the higher-ups are pissed off right now? It's a bit strange. If the higher-ups got pissed off over something like that, they would have gone crazy a long time ago."

,I heard that one of the people massacred was a member of the Four Demon Venerable Families."

The Four Demon Venerable Families.

The four families that can be said to be the beginning of the Cheonma Shingyo. The Four Demon Venerable Families are said to be the disciples of the first Cheonma, who revered the first Cheonma and founded the Cheonma Shingyo.

In the Cheonma Shingyo, the members of the Four Demon Venerable Families could be called nobles.

,A noble blood died at the hands of the Murim Alliance. The higher-ups have every right to be pissed off."

,Shouldn't we retaliate? What was it, Manjo Escort Agency? It's a huge escort agency that supports the Murim Alliance, I heard. If we attack them and plunder their supplies, we could inflict significant damage on the Murim Alliance, don't you think?"

,The Murim Alliance isn't a bunch of retards, they'll try to protect the Manjo Escort Agency with their teeth clenched. The risk is too great. It's better to safely eliminate the small sects belonging to the Murim Alliance one by one."

The gazes of the Red Demise Corps members, who were talking about how to screw over the Murim Alliance, turned to me.

,What do you think, maknae? What should we do about the Murim Alliance?"

Although it had been several years since I joined the Red Demise Corps, I was still the maknae. This was because the quota had not been filled. There were no talents worthy of joining the Red Demise Corps.

,Why don't we just wipe out the Murim Alliance territory with thunderclap bombs? We can suck the life out of the surviving civilians with the Star-Devouring Art. Selling humans to monsters is also an option."

The Red Demise Corps members were impressed by my words.

,The maknae's mind is sharp because he's young. What a novel idea."

,The vigor of youth... I had that too back in my day."

,Maknae. Your idea is good, but it's not realistic. It's difficult to obtain a large number of thunderclap bombs, and even if we had them, we shouldn't use them. If we use a large number of thunderclap bombs, the Imperial family will step in. Selling civilians to monsters is the same. It will only give the Imperial family a reason to intervene."

The Imperial family.

They were the most powerful force on this continent.

The Cheonma Shingyo and the Murim Alliance are nothing compared to the Imperial family. Murim and whatever, the one who holds the hegemony of this continent is the Emperor, no matter what anyone says.

,As long as the Imperial family exists, it's impossible to erase the Murim Alliance from this world. The Imperial family will probably step in and mediate before either side is completely destroyed. Our Shingyo must inflict as much damage as possible on the Murim Alliance before the Imperial family intervenes. So that they can't build up their strength for the next few decades... no, for hundreds of years."

All the Red Demise Corps members fell silent. Their atmosphere had become somber since the word "Imperial family" came up.

As a member of the Cheonma Shingyo, I don't like the Imperial family. However, I couldn't fully express that feeling and thought. The Imperial family was the master of this world right now.

,Commander's orders! Everyone, gather at the training grounds!"

Unit 6 Commander Oh Bong appeared and shouted. The Red Demise Corps members rose from their seats and headed to the training grounds.

The Red Demise Corps gathered at the training grounds.

Not everyone was there. Since it was a busy time, about 30% of the Red Demise Corps was missing. Still, it had been almost three years since this many people had gathered. The Red Demise Corps members, including me, sensed that this was no ordinary matter.

The Red Demise Corps Lord slowly walks in. He was solemn and serious as usual.

,It's been a while since we've received an extermination mission."

The Red Demise Corps members tensed up and listened to the Red Demise Corps Lord's words. An extermination mission. It was a mission to destroy an entire sect. To gather this many people for an extermination mission? That meant the target of the extermination was not ordinary.

,Everyone here will participate in the mission. The target of the extermination is the Hwasan Sect."

,Oh, oh!"

,The Hwasan Sect! The time has come to destroy those trash!"

The Red Demise Corps members were excited. There were no fearful people here. The Red Demise Corps was a place where those known for being rough and cruel even within the Cheonma Shingyo gathered.

,When are we leaving for the Hwasan Sect?"

,We're leaving tonight. Pack the bare minimum of supplies. We'll resupply in the cities we pass through. Outside the Cheonma Shingyo territory, we'll resupply through plunder. As you know without me having to say it, the mission is naturally confidential. Don't even tell your families."

The Red Demise Corps members looked at each other. And they uttered playful jokes.

,Are there any of us who have families?"

The Red Demise Corps members chuckled. Even those with families were the same. Most of the Red Demise Corps didn't have families. They were treated as particularly crazy even within the Cheonma Shingyo, so it wasn't easy to create new families.

,Stop with the nonsense and start preparing to leave. Anyone who's even a little late will be crushed by my hands."

The Red Demise Corps Lord turned around. The Red Demise Corps members also dispersed. They gathered in groups of three or five and chatted.

In fact, there wasn't much to prepare. The Red Demise Corps members all had spatial pouches. All they had to do was sweep in clothes, food, and other things.

For the same reason, I also had free time. I looked at Unit 4. Yeon Yeo-ha was standing at the center of Unit 4, where the women were gathered. Even among the women of Unit 4, her appearance shone like a crane among chickens.

,......."

,......."

Our eyes met.

We didn't say anything. No Red Demise Corps members were giving us their attention. No one knew about the relationship between Yeon Yeo-ha and me. Even though we were peers, not even a false rumor spread. Sword Demon, the daughter of the Third Elder, Yeon Yeo-ha, and the swordsman Yeom Gu-seok from the countryside. It was an incompatible relationship no matter who looked at it.

,Since I was fucked yesterday, is it my turn to fuck today?"

But unfortunately, I couldn't. Yeon Yeo-ha continued to move with Unit 4, and at night, all the Red Demise Corps members would leave the Cheonma Shingyo. There was no time for the two of us to be alone.

,It can't be helped. It's a waste to waste time... I should meet Cheon Yu-un."

I secretly contacted Cheon Yu-un. If he, a member of the Underworld Demon Corps, and I, a member of the Red Demise Corps, met openly, it would only spread bad rumors.

,I heard that the Red Demise Corps is attacking the Hwasan Sect."

,It's confidential, but you know it well. Does the Underworld Demon Corps know everything?"

,Even so, it's a mission to attack the Hwasan Sect, one of the Nine Great Sects. There's no way even the Underworld Demon Corps could know."

,So you found out the information in another way."

I stroked my chin. I knew it, but Cheon Yu-un was indeed a capable guy.

,How's the Mukji-ryeon situation going?"

Cheon Yu-un asked. He was more concerned about Mukji-ryeon than the Hwasan Sect.

,The Rakshasa Woman was softer on me last time. I met the Rakshasa Woman. Her hair was yellow and her eyes were red."

,That woman is not human. And she's not a good woman either. You know the Reverse Demon Divine Art, right? A demonic art that turns humans who have eaten human flesh into monsters. The Rakshasa Woman is the one who created and spread that terrible demonic art."

,Isn't it thanks to the Reverse Demon Divine Art that Mukji-ryeon was able to grow so quickly?"

,That's right. But the problem is that I can't completely control Mukji-ryeon. The Rakshasa Woman is crazy. She's a woman who will do anything for her own purposes."

,What is the Rakshasa Woman's purpose?"

,I don't know. However, that purpose will never be light."

I quietly fell into thought. The Rakshasa Woman also needs the power of Mukji-ryeon. That's why she's trying to kill Cheon Yu-un in order to completely take Mukji-ryeon into her hands.

,I knew that Cheon Yu-un's death itself wasn't the goal. I wonder if she would answer if I asked the Rakshasa Woman?"

I didn't think she would answer.

,Yeom Gu-seok. What instructions did you receive from the Rakshasa Woman?"

The question I had been waiting for came. I answered at a tempo that was neither too fast nor too slow.

,Cheonma Shingong. The Rakshasa Woman wants to know the location where the Cheonma Shingong is sleeping."

,The Cheonma Royal Tomb? Why does the Rakshasa Woman want the Cheonma Shingong?"

,I don't know either. Maybe she became interested in the Cheonma Shingong while creating the Reverse Demon Divine Art?"

,Maybe. But the Rakshasa Woman is not of the Cheonma bloodline. She should know that even if she enters the Cheonma Royal Tomb, she will only die without obtaining the Cheonma Shingong..."

Cheon Yu-un narrowed his brows. It seemed that he was very confused because of the Rakshasa Woman, who wanted unexpected information.

,What are you going to do? Isn't it a good thing if the Rakshasa Woman enters the Cheonma Royal Tomb and dies?"

,That would be the case if she went in directly. The Rakshasa Woman might be plotting to create a new Cheonma using the Cheonma bloodline. She's trying to use that guy to push me out. It's better to watch quietly for now."

Suppressing the urge to urge him to tell me right away, I asked.

,Cheonma Shingong. I've heard a lot about it, but I don't know exactly what kind of martial art it is. Can you tell me?"

,It's not difficult to tell you since everyone who's anyone knows about it."

Cheon Yu-un raised his right hand. Dark black demonic energy gathered in his grasp.

Cheonma Shingong.

It was similar to and different from the Cheonma Shingong that I possessed.

,The power to subdue all demons. The power to reign over all demons. The power to break the sky. An arrogant and absolute power. That is the Cheonma Shingong."

Looking at his Cheonma Shingong, I could roughly tell what kind of power this Cheonma Shingong had.

Power.

It's just a type of martial art that pursues overwhelming power.

,I like it."

I wanted to have this Cheonma Shingong as soon as possible.
