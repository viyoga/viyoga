E District.

If you were to compare it to Korea, it'd be like Myeongdong in Seoul.

You can see countless tourists and wealthy customers. Especially in this district, you often see nobles shopping.

What you have to be careful about is not staring at the nobles. If your eyes meet... a situation like, 'How dare a mere commoner look at me?' can actually happen. When looking at nobles, you have to glance at them as if you're not interested. It's best to not pay attention at all.

Even in Neo London's most sophisticated downtown, darkness existed. In contrast to the flashy lights, that particularly dark street is called Aira Street.

It's a street created by the descendants of Aira, who was once a hero of Neo London, as they started businesses. The nobles also respect Aira and unknowingly look out for the convenience of Aira Street. Of course, in reality, it's just that the nobles' dirty money is involved, so they don't bother touching it.

As soon as I stepped onto Aira Street, I felt gazes fixed on me from all directions. Wary eyes.

You can't shrink back from the attention here. Even if it's a bluff, you have to look strong. That's the only way to avoid troublesome fights.

,Hey, chump. You're reeking of money... are you trying to lure us on purpose?

Laughing, three black men approach. Their arms and legs were made of machinery.

,,,,,.

I raised my hand and touched my face. I never thought I'd get into a fight less than a minute after arriving on Aira Street.

'Does my face look like a chump's...? I didn't even bother hiding my strength.'

I looked around.

The guys who looked like mercenaries were looking at the black guys with pity. They recognized the power I had.

'So the guys in front of me are ignorant crazy bastards.'

I was so dumbfounded that I kept my mouth shut, and the guys, thinking I was intimidated, became even more emboldened.

,We're in a tough spot because we don't have any money right now. We had some drinks, but the bill came out ridiculously high.

,Fuck. It's because you called a woman over.

,And you didn't even get to bang her.

,Shut up, you motherfuckers. Anyway, the point is, we need money. Lend us some money. 100,000 credits will do.

Should I kill them?

I could kill them instantly.

But there were many eyes watching this way. They had no intention of helping and were watching with great interest, as if an entertaining spectacle had appeared.

'Why isn't anyone reporting this to the police?'

I was starting to doubt if this was really E District. I'd believe it even if it was X District.

'Killing is absolutely out of the question. There are too many eyes watching. As much as it sucks, this is E District. Murder is crossing the line.'

But I couldn't just ignore them and run away either.

Run away from these kinds of guys because I'm scared? My pride aside, my reputation would plummet. Who would hire a mercenary who runs away from these kinds of guys?

,What's wrong with this guy, is he retarded? Why isn't he saying anything?

,Is asking for money that difficult?

,Should I smack his head a bit? My TV gets fixed when I hit it too.

Uuuuuung.

Mana moved. A magic circle was drawn in front of my hand. The guys' eyes widened.

,What the fuck!

,He's a magician!

,Save me!!

As soon as they saw the magic circle, the guys frantically ran away. Seeing them quickly split into three directions, it seemed like they had experienced this a few times.

I retracted the magic. This is E District, and there are many eyes watching. I have to restrain myself.

,Guys with no guts. I thought I was going to have a good time killing time....

,Back in my day, we'd pull out a gun first, whether the opponent was a magician or a knight. These days....

,Bullshit. You knelt down and begged in front of a knight back then.

,You little shit. Acting like you wouldn't do the same.

The crowd that had gathered also disappeared. Perhaps because they confirmed that I was a magician, some of the wary gazes also disappeared.

,What's a spellcaster doing here?

As I was walking down the street, the owner of a shashlik shop spoke to me. He was a middle-aged man.

,Do I have to report that to you?

,I know you're in a bad mood because of what just happened... but don't be so prickly. I'm trying to help you. It's your first time here, right? Do you know the way around?

,...I'm looking for Gomez Clinic.

,A magician looking for Gomez Clinic? I see. Your mana road is fucked up, isn't it? But you're still managing to use magic. How did you end up like that?

,Don't cross the line.

,I'm just curious. If you tell me, I'll give you a free shashlik. You can just tell me roughly.

,...I ended up like this while taking down a son of a bitch.

,Personal revenge?

I nodded.

He chuckled and handed me a shashlik skewer.

,Gomez Clinic is straight down that road, and you'll see a blue pillar. From there, go right, and you'll see a building with Gomez Clinic written on it.

,I know. Did you think I came here without knowing anything? The shashlik sucks.

,Hmm. Shashlik with plum sauce isn't very good after all.... Developing a new menu is difficult.

I threw the shashlik I was holding back at him and headed towards Gomez Clinic. That guy selling shashlik is probably an informant. He was probably trying to sell my information to an information broker. I don't know how much they'd pay for that kind of information, though.

I arrived at Gomez Clinic. A woman with a bright smile was sitting at the counter.

,Welcome, customer-nim. Is this your first time at our clinic?

,I want to see Lana Gomez.

,The director-nim? I'm sorry, customer-nim. The director-nim only sees special guests.

,I'm a 5th Circle magician.

,...You're a special customer-nim. I've sent a message to the director-nim. Ah, it seems the director-nim is free. You can go up to the 4th floor.

I took the elevator to the 4th floor.

I sniffed. It smelled like a hospital. I knocked on the director's office.

,Come in.

I opened the door.

A woman was sitting at the desk. The old woman, who looked to be in her 60s, was old, but she didn't feel worn out. With a sharp impression, she adjusted her glasses and said to me.

,So the exorcist mage has arrived. Sit down.

,...Exorcist mage?

,I heard you wiped out all the ghosts in a village when the Dark Moon rose two days ago?

,The rumors have already spread....

At that time, I searched for and killed ghosts until the sun rose. At first, it was because the ones rushing at me were annoying, and from the middle, I got excited while testing my magic. That scene was captured on CCTV, and that afternoon, I received 500,000 credits in compensation from the London Guard.

,Your mana road is the problem, right? The only magicians who come to me are those kinds of guys.

,You've found me for sure.

,I'll get straight to the point. I don't have the materials for mana restoration. I have all the other materials, but the problem is Blue Distortion. It's a flower that only blooms when the Dark Moon rises, so the quantity is limited.

,The Dark Moon rose two days ago.

,Blue Distortion isn't a flower you can easily get just because the Dark Moon rose. Cultivation is also impossible. Do you think there will be many guys going around collecting flowers on the day the Dark Moon rises?

,Still, the supply is coming in little by little. The guys who came before you are waiting quietly, so just make a reservation and get a checkup. I'll contact you when your turn comes. Depending on the situation, it may take more than 3 months. If you don't like it, go to another shop.

I put my hand in my coat pocket. The air tensed up in an instant. Her hand went under the desk.

,I'm not trying to fight. I'm not planning to threaten you either.

,Then what is it?

,Blue Distortion. I have it.

I took out a box and put it on the desk. She opened the box and was shocked.

,Blue Distortion petals! You've collected this much? It looks like there are at least 5,000 petals!

,Let's make a deal. I want to receive mana restoration right away. I'll give you 500 Blue Distortion petals in return.

,You're a complete robber. You're going to get your mana road restored with only 500 petals? It costs 50 million credits to restore one mana road.

,The 500 petals are for moving up the line. I'd like to pay for the mana road restoration with the rest.

,...How many broken mana roads are there?

,16.

,A 5th Circle magician with 16 mana roads broken.... Why are you so calm? You should be grabbing my legs and begging me to save you.

,Enough with the useless talk. Is there a deal?

,This deal is a loss for me. 16 mana roads is 800 million credits. With that much, you can buy 20,000 Blue Distortion petals.

,If you can buy them, that is.

It's hard to get Blue Distortion, so I got it myself.

,...There's a condition.

,What is it?

,I want you to eliminate a gang. It's not a gang based in Neo London. They seem to be intercepting shipments on the north road of Neo London.

,Isn't that something the London Guard should handle?

,The London Guard guys are slow, and everyone knows it. I want you to take care of them and retrieve the things that were stolen from my daughter. If you don't like it... find another restorer.

Lana Gomez's expression was firm.

I sighed.

I have no choice but to accept this request. I was the one who was desperate. She is currently the most skilled and reliable restorer in Neo London.

,...I'll look into what that gang is on this side.

,There's no backing. There are about 30 gang members, and they're cyborgs.

,...Are you requesting me because I'm an electric-type magician?

,Plus, a 100% mission success rate. I judged that you weren't just anyone.

,Anyway, I'll investigate separately on this side and let you know whether I'll take the request or not.

,Good. But I can't wait long. Contact me within 3 days. Get in the diagnostic machine. Let's check your mana road condition.

Lana Gomez pointed to the diagnostic machine. It looked like a crude refrigerator. I sighed and walked towards the diagnostic machine.

,Do I have to take off my clothes too?

,I'm not interested in your naked body.

I went inside the diagnostic machine. A cold chill poured down from above.

'...You're sure it's not a refrigerator, right?'

The diagnostic machine door opened in 30 seconds. I came out and approached her, who was staring at the monitor.

A human figure was floating on a black background. The head was shining pure white, and blue lines were extending from there to the whole body.

,Is this my astral and mana road?

,Yes. The astral is in good condition... and the quality of the mana road is good too. All 34 mana roads are circulating.

,Isn't it normal for mana roads to circulate?

,That's normal. But more than half of the magicians' mana roads don't circulate. Making them circulate is one of my jobs.... Hooo. Did you get some kind of procedure? Your body is disgustingly clean.

She was impressed. It's probably because of the mana shower.

,Is it important whether it's clean or not?

,Is a magician asking me that? How absurd.

,...I learned magic on my own.

,A 5th Circle magician self-taught? I've heard it a few times, but it's my first time actually seeing it. The mana road is, as the name suggests, a road. The cleaner and smoother the road, the faster the mana can run. In that sense, all 34 of your mana roads are in top condition. It's a road that makes you want to step on the gas without thinking in a supercar.

,What about the other 16?

,They're so broken that it's hard to call them roads. Blocked, cut off, burning. You must have overworked your mana road somewhere.

,I used some strength to kill a son of a bitch. Is restoration possible?

,I've restored mana roads in worse condition than this. No problem.

,That's a relief.

I got up from my seat.

,I'll contact you within 3 days.

,Okay.

She answered without taking her eyes off the monitor.
