KWAANG!

An explosion rang out from a fair distance away. Judging the direction, it came from the dungeon entrance we'd entered through.

"Looks like Wolfgang's mercenary band has entered. I expected it, so I'm not surprised."

"Won't they chase after us?"

"I didn't leave any traces. We just need to move quietly. Still, since they're trying to kill us, I'll set up some traps."

She installed traps inside the building. Most were analog-style booby traps.

"You don't make traps using artifacts or magic?"

"Sometimes. But efficiency is surprisingly better this way. Cost-effective too. I know these kinds of traps can't deal lethal damage. This is a kind of stalling tactic."

"Stalling. So you have no intention of fighting?"

"What matters in dungeon exploration isn't combat. If there's no need to fight, not fighting is best. If you have to fight, you fight as efficiently as possible. Let's go. This way."

Nora opened a wooden window and stepped outside. She didn't jump down—she grabbed the roof above the window and pulled herself up. Her movements were fluid. Not a sound, either. Her skill was on par with a ninja's.

"Grab on."

Standing atop the roof, she extended her right hand downward.

"You think I'd need help just getting onto a roof?"

"The mages I've met couldn't even climb over a wall, let alone a roof."

"...That's probably true for most."

In this world, mages are researchers, not combatants. There's a reason the term "battle mage" exists as a separate category.

Since I'd been consistently training my body, I easily climbed onto the roof without Nora's help.

"Isn't moving along rooftops too conspicuous?"

"It's fine. Look around—the building heights aren't uniform. We get natural cover. If you stay low and move quietly, you won't be spotted. You can at least mask your presence, right?"

"Isn't that basic mercenary knowledge?"

"More than eighty percent of them don't even have that basic knowledge."

We moved from place to place. Avoiding combat while farming artifacts and materials. In fifteen minutes, we made seven hundred million.

"It's a trap. Looks like curse magic. Can you disarm it quietly?"

"If it's about skill, I've already shown you."

I glance over the curse trap she mentioned.

Scraps of cut rope lie scattered messily on the floor. On the surface, it just looks like a messy, unorganized area.

*'Those aren't ropes—they're snakes. A curse-type trap where the moment you get close, the ropes transform into snakes and bite your legs. If bitten, you'd be poisoned not by snake venom but by curse poison.'*

It was a curse trap hidden so cunningly that even I would have trouble noticing it without focus. I felt a sense of wonder at Nora for spotting it immediately.

"Can't we just go around it?"

"No. See that back door over there? We need to go through there. That's the only entrance."

That curse trap can't be dispelled through formulaic analysis like a barrier. If anything, it's closer to a hex.

[Decoy]

I cast a Tier 2 spell.

A softly glowing sphere drops onto the curse trap. The ropes transform into translucent snakes and lunged at the sphere. The snakes tangled with each other and vanished.

"You. What did you just do?"

"I tricked the curse into activating on a decoy. As you can see, the hex evaporated into thin air."

"...That's possible?"

"With an ordinary Decoy spell, no. Let's just say I used a little trick."

Even if I explained, she wouldn't understand anyway. She's not a mage.

Heading toward the back door, I paused. The back door was so small you'd have to duck your head to enter, and the door itself was enchanted with curse magic.

"So this is why you specifically hired a mage?"

"Correct. It's a door I can't open with my own strength. And it's too tempting to just give up—I want to know what's inside. Besides, hidden spaces like this usually have treasure."

I couldn't argue with that.

Treasure in a hidden place. Isn't that a cliché?

I reached my hand toward the door. A black, translucent membrane appeared and blocked my hand. It didn't feel particularly dangerous, so I ignored it and pushed my hand through.

"You. Are you okay?"

"Can't you see? I'm perfectly fine."

"Strange. Last time, the mercenary mage who came with me fell into an illusion, wailed for ten minutes, and then ran away."

"You must have hired a real idiot of a mage."

I analyzed the barrier curse and dispelled it with a formula. The curse resisted, but I crushed it with raw force.

Click!

The back door opened. Inside was a dark, narrow cave.

"I'll take the lead. Don't get more than three meters away from me."

Nora said, holding a small lantern. I felt something off about the lantern. It was an artifact.

"What happens if I fall behind?"

"You won't be able to see the lantern light, and it'll get suffocating."

A lantern that becomes invisible when you're too far away. That's a hell of an ability to replicate with magic.

I walked behind Nora, watching her back. Her outfit hadn't changed since we first met. White tank top and black leggings. A leather strap harness wrapped around her body over them. A whip at her hip, a dagger and pistol on each thigh.

*'You can see every curve of her ass—even the split.'*

My gaze kept drifting to her ass. An unavoidable instinct.

It was an ass I wanted to touch. With all that hip-up training, it had to be firm.

"You. Can you swim?"

I snapped to attention and looked ahead. Water had pooled in front of us. We'd need to swim at least several dozen meters.

"Do we need to dive down?"

"No. There is a path below, but... seeing as I'm not drawn to it, there's probably nothing useful."

"I'd rather not get into dirty water with bug carcasses floating in it."

[Levitation]

My body floats gently into the air.

I extended my hand toward Nora, whose eyes had gone wide.

"Hanging on, being carried, or piggyback. Which is your preference?"

"None of the three. Can't you just cast magic on me?"

"It's not impossible, but it means more things I have to manage. Above all, I need to conserve mana as much as possible, accounting for variables."

"If that's more efficient... I suppose there's no choice. If I'm going to do this, I'd rather have my limbs free, so being carried is better."

I wrapped my right arm around her waist. Her slim waist had a core as solid as rebar. Her chest pressed against my upper body was soft yet springy.

*'D-cup.'*

I instinctively gauged the size.

She didn't seem to be wearing a bra, but I couldn't feel her nipples. Probably nipple patches.

Without letting it show, I floated over the water. Speed slightly faster than walking. If I deliberately went slow, it would only raise suspicion.

"Flying through the sky doesn't feel bad. Mages have it convenient."

"Among your artifacts, is there one that lets you fly?"

"There is. But I can't use it consecutively, so I can't use it like this. Diving is just more efficient."

"You don't carry around a small boat or raft in your subspace?"

"You think subspace is an all-purpose warehouse? Even a small boat isn't small enough to fit in subspace. My subspace is..."

Nora's words cut off. I checked her expression—her gaze was directed downward. My gaze followed.

Beneath the water's surface, dozens of corpses had their heads tilted up, eyes wide open. They weren't moving. No life signs.

*'Jiangshi. I didn't notice because I wasn't using detection magic. Undead are no different from furniture or rocks when they're not moving.'*

If we'd gone into the water or floated a boat on the surface, we'd have had to deal with those jiangshi.

"...Wow. Dozens of water jiangshi lying in ambush. We almost had a serious problem."

"It's surprising that you, who reads traps so well, didn't spot them first."

"They're water jiangshi. Monsters made specifically for underwater ambushes. Even I can't grasp everything. Ah, left here."

Still, she found the path brilliantly.

Where we arrived, there was an elevator. It looked like a wooden elevator on the outside, but peering inside, it wasn't much different from a metal one. We touched solid ground in five minutes.

Nora didn't even flush a single cheek.

*'No reaction... Is she not a virgin? Or is that just her personality? I haven't heard any rumors of Nora Hanger having a lover.'*

Some might ask why I care so much about virginity.

Virginity is a critically important matter in magic as well.

"Hey. You. Instead of just standing there, help me with this."

Nora pointed to the lift lever inside the elevator. The lever was wrapped in yellow talismans. I went closer to examine it—it was sealed so that only authorized people could operate the lever.

*'Just flying up... impossible. It's blocked by a barrier. Disarming the barrier would take longer than operating the lever.'*

Bzzt.

A bolt of lightning seeped into the lever. The talismans wrapping it all caught fire and turned to ash.

"You just burned the talismans? Burning someone else's talismans carelessly—the backlash should be brutal."

"I burned them from the central axis first. Besides, they weren't particularly high-grade talismans."

Clank! The elevator rose when I pulled the lever. Felt like twenty meters. It stopped only after passing through four barriers.

A corridor as ornate as a luxury hotel's greeted us.

*'The treasure room is probably just past there.'*

I recalled the original game. The higher the dungeon's difficulty, the larger and more vast it was proportionally. Yet we'd made it here in only an hour.

*'It's thanks to Nora's guidance. Without her, I'd have suffered through everything instead of taking shortcuts.'*

Nora walked down the corridor with noticeably lighter steps, then stopped. She clicked her tongue and glared ahead. Dozens of statues wearing martial robes were lined up on both sides of the hallway.

"Those aren't statues—they're stone jiangshi. Get close and they'll wake up and attack."

I focused and looked at the statues. Looking carefully, I could tell they weren't statues.

"Can't we turn back?"

"No. We have to break through."

"Let me handle it. That's faster."

Step.

I moved forward. Crackle—blue lightning sparked from my fingertips.

As the distance closed, all thirty-two stone jiangshi standing like statues opened their eyes simultaneously. Their rotten fish-like eyes turned toward me. Without any greeting, the stone jiangshi lunged at me.

The magic was already prepared.

I extended my fingers, lightning rippling around them.

[Chain Lightning]

[Spell Split]

[Attribute Grant: Holy]

[Trait Grant: Attack Transfer]

The magic, enhanced with options, activated.

The golden lightning bolt from my fingertips split not into one but three branches, surging toward the stone jiangshi. The moment it touched them, it spread rapidly around them. Golden current burst ceaselessly between them.

By the time the lightning faded, the stone jiangshi's bodies had turned to ash and were falling to the floor.

"...That's not the Chain Lightning I know."

"Even if the stone attribute is strong against lightning, they're still undead. They can't help but be vulnerable to holy."

We stood before the large door at the end of the corridor.

*'Another barrier. This time it'll take time to analyze.'*

"Wait. I'll handle this one."

"...You?"

"There's a keyhole, isn't there? Then I can do it."

What Nora pulled from her subspace was a considerably large black key. Its size was completely different from the keyhole on the door.

"It's an artifact called a Skeleton Key. My most treasured possession. If there's a keyhole, it can open anything."

The large black key slid perfectly into the small keyhole. Nora turned it without hesitation.

Click!

Inside, it was filled with gold, silver, and treasures.

Among all the glittering things in the treasure vault, only a single book caught my eye.
