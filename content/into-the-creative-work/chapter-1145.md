I felt someone's presence.

I raised my head and looked towards where I felt the presence. Under the streetlight, a woman was walking towards me. A black school uniform different from the academy's, black bobbed hair barely touching her shoulders. Behind her back, something long, over 2 meters, was hanging diagonally.

,Wow. There are a lot of kids gathered here. Are you a spiritist too, oppa?

She's short. Her chest is at a modest level. Baby fat remains on her face.

I knew as soon as I saw her.

She was Seong Hari from her middle school days.

『Name: Seong Hari

Strength: B- Stamina: B Agility: A Durability: C Mana: C+

Trait: Spirit Devourer (S)

Skills: Combat Sense (A), Purification (E), Barrier (D)

Favorability: 15』

I inadvertently checked Seong Hari's status window.

I almost let out a hollow laugh as soon as I saw it. Her stats were unbelievably powerful for a middle schooler. Of course, they were paltry compared to the Seong Hari outside.

,Could I win if I fought the current Seong Hari?

The opponent was Seong Hari. The stats I could see were not everything.

,...I think I could win if I used all my abilities.

Lost in thought, I sighed. What am I doing to a middle schooler?

,Oppa. Are you mute?

Seong Hari strode closer. It's a bit much to say, but she seemed to have a lot of sass compared to her pretty face.

,...What's your business.

,You can talk. I thought you were mute because you've been looking at me with your mouth shut since earlier.

,I'm asking what your business is. Are you perhaps smitten with me?

,Are you crazy?

Seong Hari said with a straight face. I think her reaction hurt my feelings. The Seong Hari outside the dungeon is madly in love with me.

She stopped at some point as she approached. She's maintaining a distance of about 10 meters.

,Is she wary of me? But she's keeping too much distance for that?

I realized the reason belatedly. Lower spirits were gathered around me. The spirits were watching Seong Hari carefully. They looked like they would run away at any moment if she got any closer.

,I'm just curious. A spiritist I haven't seen before appeared near my house. I wouldn't be like this if you were a normal spiritist... It's the first time I've seen them follow someone so well. What's your name, oppa? My name is Seong Hari.

I pondered for a moment.

Should I tell her my name?

After all, this is a world inside a dungeon based on a parallel universe. Whatever I do here doesn't affect the outside.

,Seong Yujin.

,...Your family name is Seong? Are you perhaps from our family?

,No. It's just that we have the same family name.

,Ah. Well, I guess that could be the case. But you're not surprised even after hearing that I'm a member of the Jinryeong Seong family?

,The main house of the Jinryeong Seong family is nearby, so it's not surprising that you're a member of the Jinryeong Seong family. You probably want to see the spirits, right? You can come closer and look.

,That's true... but the spirits hate me.

,It's okay if you do this.

I reached out and grabbed the spirits nearby. I caught them easily. Perhaps because my spirit affinity is high, they didn't run away. Normally, you can't catch spirits in their spirit form, but I had the Spirit Eye.

,Th, they'll get angry if you treat them so carelessly.

,What can they do even if they get angry? Here, come closer and look.

,.......

I already knew that Seong Hari was afraid of and disliked by the spirits, but she actually liked them.

Seong Hari hesitated for a moment before coming closer. She sat next to me and stared intently at the captured spirits.

,...It's pitiful and cute how desperately they're struggling to escape.

,Why are you looking at them like it's so amazing? If you're a member of the Jinryeong Seong family, don't you see spirits every day?

,I'm a bit special. The spirits run away as soon as they see me. When I go home, the spirits hide from me. I know it can't be helped, but... it feels like I'm being ostracized, so I'm a little sad.

,.......

,I envy oppa. The spirits approach you so openly. Normally, the spirits would use their power to escape, but... they're not using their power because you're here, right? Is it perhaps related to your eyes?

I nodded.

,This is the Spirit Eye.

,Spirit Eye! I've heard of it. They say it's one of the best abilities for a spiritist... It's the first time I've actually seen it. ...Are you really not one of our family?

,If I were from the Jinryeong Seong family, you would already know me.

,I guess so. That's true. Oppa, won't you come to my house?

,...Is she really smitten with me.

,That's not it!

Seong Hari punched my shoulder with her fist. She seemed to have hit me playfully because of my absurd words, but my shoulder hurts.

,The fact that oppa is near our house means you have business with our house, right? I'll guide you. My dad and grandfather will be happy to see oppa too.

,...No. I don't have any business with the Jinryeong Seong family.

,Then why are you here?

,Just because?

,...Suddenly, you're becoming very suspicious.

Seong Hari narrowed her eyes and looked at me. I released the lower spirits I was holding. The lower spirits didn't miss the opportunity and ran far away. Seong Hari watched the escaping lower spirits with a look of lingering attachment.

,As a senior in life, I'll give you some advice. Don't be too friendly with strangers.

,I'm not a kid. And I'm not the type to be friendly with people I meet for the first time.

Seong Hari blushed as she looked at me as if I was absurd.

,It's just that... oppa doesn't feel like a stranger for some reason. What... it's hard to explain...

,Like family?

,No, no. Oppa isn't family. ...Um. You're not family, but you're kind of like family...?

,You're smitten with me.

,That's not it!

『Seong Hari's Favorability: 27』

Her favorability increased in the short time we talked.

,But there's not enough time to capture Seong Hari... Should I just pounce on her?

The important thing is whether I can completely subdue Seong Hari or not. I wasn't confident that I could completely subdue her, a middle schooler.

I sighed and stood up from my seat.

,...Are you leaving?

,Yeah. It was fun talking to you.

,Are you really not going to my house?

,I'm not interested in the Jinryeong Seong family.

,...Goodbye. Will we be able to meet again later?

,We'll meet again if we're lucky.

,What do you mean, if we're lucky.

I turned my back on Seong Hari, who was speaking discontentedly, and took a step. I'm going to find Ryu Hana. I have an idea where she is. If it's like the original work, she'll be looking for her parents in their younger days.

Kuuung.

The ground rumbled.

I stopped walking and turned around. I met Seong Hari's eyes as she stood in front of the bench. She also looked flustered.

,If it's not Seong Hari, then the cause is somewhere else.

Kuuung, Ujikkun, Kuuung.

Trees were breaking and the ground was rumbling.

Seong Hari and I simultaneously turned our heads towards the source of the commotion. A mud giant was rising from the trees behind the bench.

Seong Hari quickly created distance as the monster appeared. She took out the spear hanging diagonally on her back.

,...A middle schooler with a spear.

,I got permission from the Hero Association.

Considering the current era, it's not that strange. In the future, they'll call this era an era of upheaval or an era of chaos.

,It's annoying, should I just run away? The Jinryeong Seong family is nearby too.

This world is fake. Even if someone dies here, it won't affect the outside.

But Seong Hari keeps bothering me. Imagining the young, even childish Seong Hari dying made me feel bad. I decided to get rid of that mud giant.

But there was someone who moved before me. It was Seong Hari. She created a barrier in the air, stepped on that barrier, and charged towards the mud giant, which hadn't fully risen yet.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion was heard. It was the sound from the spear Seong Hari swung. The mud giant's right arm flew away. The mud giant swung its left arm at Seong Hari.

Seong Hari tried to twist her body in the air to avoid the mud giant's attack, but she was a bit clumsy. Part of the fist hit her body. Seong Hari flew away.

,A fleeting moment.

I ran behind Seong Hari as she flew away and caught her body. I directly acted as a cushion.

,Ugh... Oppa...?

,You were too reckless. What were you thinking charging in like that?

,Well... I've fought mud giants a few times before. If you land a big hit before they fully get up, they'll be knocked down right away... This mud giant is a bit strange.

,Is that so...?

,But aren't you going to move your hand? Do you like touching other people's boobs?

,It just happened by accident.

Without realizing it, I squeezed Seong Hari's chest tightly. Unlike the future Seong Hari, the current Seong Hari's chest was smaller than my hand, so the feeling of squeezing it was unique.

Seong Hari raised her right arm and slammed her elbow into my abdomen.

,......!

I let out a silent scream and fell away from Seong Hari. I almost threw up right there. Seong Hari glared at me with a bright red face.

,You perverted bastard. I didn't think you were like that...

,It was an accident, an accident. You already forgot that I helped you...

I stopped talking and turned my head sharply.

『The Demon Hunter (S) has detected the presence of a demon.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

The mud giant's massive body turned red, and complex black patterns floated above it.

『The Demon Hunter (S) cannot identify the demon's identity.』

『Information is insufficient.』

The mud giant is not a demon. It's just a monster that has been corrupted by the power of a demon. The real demon is probably hiding somewhere, watching this situation.

,Suddenly a demon appears... Is it a coincidence?

A demon happened to appear near me. But the demon itself doesn't show its face and attacks me using a monster. It was hard to think of it as a coincidence. My head is getting complicated.

The mud giant opened its mouth wide towards us. Dozens of stake-shaped rocks fell. Seong Hari and I stepped on our footwork and created distance from the mud giant.

Pajijik.

Lightning flashed once in my hand and disappeared. The mana that escaped from my body caused a reaction in the sky.

Kwareureureung!

A streak of blue lightning struck the top of the mud giant's head. The giant's head was slightly crushed but returned to its original state.

,Of all things, it's mud, so lightning doesn't work well.

I clicked my tongue and looked for another method.

,Oppa! Spirit! Use spirit magic!

,I can't use spirit magic.

,Huh?! Th, then summon a spirit and fight!

,I can't summon a spirit right now.

,.......

Seong Hari looked at me with suspicious eyes.

I summoned Hwaryeon Bido.

If lightning doesn't work, I can just go and slice it to death. Fortunately, I can clearly see where the mud giant's heart is.

,Acceleration.
