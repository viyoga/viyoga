I entered the cafe I had promised to meet Raffles at. It was a cafe located in an alleyway in Seoul.

As I entered the cafe, a man greeted me. The man, wearing a black mask, scanned me with his mechanical eyes and said.

"Are you Taker?"

"That's right. I'm Taker. Sorry I'm an hour late. Something important came up. But are you Anything?"

"Anything isn't me, it's the leader. You have a pistol hidden in your clothes."

"......I thought you wouldn't be able to tell from the outside. Do you have X-ray vision or something?"

"It's X-ray. You're not a cyborg. Why do you have a smartphone? Do you like those antiques?"

"Smartphones are the most perfect things, friend."

"You're a bullshit artist. Follow me. The leader is waiting."

"What's your name?"

"Jackie."

"Ah. You're a foreigner."

"I'm Korean. Jackie is a pseudonym I use. To us, names are just trivial things."

"Then I guess I'll have to work as Taker too."

I followed Jackie into the back of the cafe. There was a fairly large conference room.

A total of 8 people were waiting for me there.

"I brought him. He looks normal on the outside, and all he has is a pistol."

Jackie said. Their eyes turned to me.

Everyone is a cyborg.

There were 6 men and only 2 women. The two women were not my type at all. Not hot at all.

The man in the center approached me and said.

"Welcome. I'm Sohn Jeonghu, the leader of Raffles. I'm Anything, who talked to you. My colleagues just call me Leader."

Anything. No, Sohn Jeonghu was a man with a gentle demeanor, unlike the stiff tone of the game character. The clothes he was wearing were also casual. You could believe he was a college student.

He looked to be in his late 20s, and appeared normal on the outside.

'But in reality, he's a cyborg whose entire body, except for his brain, is mechanical.'

Sohn Jeonghu is the most dangerous guy in Raffles. He's the kind of guy who can smile and massacre children or the elderly for his own purposes.

"Taker, my real name is Seong Yujin. I'm becoming a member of Raffles from today, right?"

"It's a little suspicious. We're meeting for the first time, right? But I'm thinking of accepting you into Raffles. I'm too greedy for your abilities."

Sohn Jeonghu reached out to me. I slowly shook hands with him, but I didn't let my guard down.

'In the original work, Sohn Jeonghu is a guy who doesn't even trust his own colleagues. Even if they've been working together in Raffles for years, he'll mercilessly abandon them if they're no longer needed.'

Sohn Jeonghu is not a trustworthy guy. It's best not to get involved if possible.

I am contacting Raffles to obtain the Illusitter in the shortest amount of time. After obtaining the Illusitter, I will immediately end the game with automatic progression. I don't have much interest in this game.

Much more advanced machines than in reality? I can get that from other works too. There was no need to be obsessed with Code: XTK just because of that.

"Wait a minute, Leader. You said before that when accepting new members into Raffles, you would take our opinions into account."

It was a mohawk-styled man wearing sunglasses. His name is Mudan. He has wheels on the soles of his feet.

"...Hmm. That's right. Mudan is right. Sorry. I was a bit autocratic. Is there anyone who opposes Taker joining us? Raise your hand."

At Sohn Jeonghu's words, 3 people, including Mudan, raised their hands.

"We've decided to decide by majority vote whenever possible.... It's confirmed that Taker will become a member of our group. But we can't ignore the opinions of the minority. Mudan, Train, Hyeonji. What's the reason for your opposition?"

Mudan spoke first.

"I can't trust him. He's too suspicious. He might be a spy from another organization, or a government dog."

"That's right."

Sohn Jeonghu looked at me. He stared into my eyes and asked me.

"Are you a spy?"

"No. I'm an employee at a factory that makes machine parts."

"What's the reason you want to become a member of Raffles? If you know who we are... you should know that our work is dangerous."

"It's too much of a waste of my hacking ability if I don't do anything. I want to use my abilities to be active. I hate boring lives."

"That's not a reason to specifically choose Raffles. The government or the police would welcome your abilities. The same goes for other criminal organizations."

"I don't trust the government. There are rumors that they kidnap children and do human experiments. And other criminal organizations are boring. If I'm going to join, it has to be at least Raffles."

Sohn Jeonghu smiled at me once and then turned his gaze away. The person his gaze turned to was a large man with his shirt off, exposing his upper body. Wires were wrapped around his abdomen. It's Train.

"Train. What about you?"

"Purpose. We each have a purpose for working in Raffles. I haven't heard that guy's purpose yet."

"Taker. Did you hear that? What's your purpose?"

This was also a question I had already anticipated. There are many reasons, such as the enemy of my parents, hatred for the government, and admiration for crime. But I gave the simplest reason.

"Money. I want to make money with my hacking skills. Excluding the trivial act of robbing a bank. I think Raffles fits my purpose to some extent."

"That's right. We're a small elite group and we divide the shares accurately, so we sometimes receive contract requests and sometimes commit robberies. There's hardly any trivial work. You've come to the right place."

"......It's a little suspicious, but. Well, whatever. It seems the Leader doesn't care."

Train muttered and then turned his head away. He doesn't seem to have much interest in me.

"Hyeonji. What about you?"

"He looks weak."

A short-haired woman sitting on the desk walked towards me. Her hairstyle is half-shaved, and half of her face and both arms are mechanical.

Hyeonji stared at me as if observing me from a distance of three steps away.

"It looks like you've trained your body, but. That's all. From the outside, you're not a mutant, just a normal guy. We're a small elite group, so we need a certain level of individual combat power. What can we use a weakling like you for, who has no talent other than hacking?"

"You think I'm weak? I can kill you in 5 seconds."

"...Oh. You've got some guts?"

Hyeonji raised her right arm. A blade popped out of her wrist. Wiiiiing. A mechanical sound came from her arm. I immediately noticed. That sword is a so-called super-vibrating blade.

"You don't believe me."

"I believe you. That's why I'm asking you to show me your skills, newbie!"

"That's quite the initiation ceremony."

I said and laughed.

This situation is what I wanted. Because it can more clearly imprint my abilities on them.

[Hacking successful.]

[You can hack Hyeonji's right arm for 11 minutes.]

Hyeonji's right arm is directed not at me, but at her own neck.

"...Huh?"

Hyeonji grabbed her right wrist with her left hand in embarrassment. But her right arm didn't stop. There was a vibrating blade in front of her neck.

"Actually, it doesn't even take 5 seconds."

"No way! You can hack a cyborg? My arm isn't even connected to the network!"

"My hacking isn't the hacking you're talking about. It's a superpower. Whether it's connected to the network or blocked... I can hack any machine."

"What! That! If that's true, then cyborgs are no match for you!"

"That's right. And cyborgs are common in this world. Do you know a little about my abilities now? Don't worry about my combat power. Even without hacking, I'm not weak enough to hold you back."

I looked around. Before I knew it, the members of Raffles were surrounding me and pointing their guns at me. The atmosphere suddenly became tense.

The machines attached to their bodies are all custom-made, not mass-produced. My hacking ability greatly decreases the maintenance time as the machine becomes more complex or high-end.

'If I hack all the people here... 3 seconds? Is that about the limit?'

If it were a complete machine, I could have taken care of it. Because all I have to do is turn off the power. But they are cyborgs with only part of their bodies being mechanical. It is impossible to knock them down in 3 seconds using hacking.

"Okay. Okay. Calm down. This is just an initiation ceremony. Hyeonji started it first, right? There's no need to get serious."

Raffles' leader, Sohn Jeonghu, intervened.

He circled around me and Hyeonji, then placed both hands on my shoulders.

"Stop it. Taker. Hyeonji must have understood by now. And everyone knows about your outstanding superpowers. In terms of ability, you are more than qualified to become a member of Raffles. I... no, we really need your abilities."

I gave up the authority of Hyeonji's right arm that I had taken through hacking. Hyeonji's arm went down. She is still looking at me with disbelief in her eyes.

"Hyeonji, calm down too. You guys put your guns away. Let's welcome Taker, who became our colleague today."

"......."

No one responded to Sohn Jeonghu's jest. They lowered the guns they were pointing at me and returned to their seats.

"Hmm. The matter has been settled, but. Just in case, I'll ask. Is there anyone who doesn't recognize Taker as a member of Raffles?"

"......."

No one answered. No one raised their hand.

They saw my abilities with their own eyes. A rare and outstanding superpower that cannot be found even if you search this world.

"The atmosphere is stiff. Should we order some pizza?"

I munched on a potato pizza. The taste of pizza was no different here than in reality.

"Newbie. You eat well? Do you like pizza?"

An uncle with a goatee spoke to me next to me. His name is Yu Geuntae. He's a good-for-nothing uncle who carries something like an oxygen tank on his back.

"So-so?"

Thanks to this good-for-nothing uncle, I was able to greet the members of Raffles. But all I did was say my name.

"Okay. Listen while you eat."

Sohn Jeonghu, who had disappeared somewhere for a while, came in and said. He projected a screen onto one side of the wall with his Smart watch.

"We just received a request. The content is assassination, and the target's information is as you can see."

I looked at the screen.

There was a picture of a man with a menacing impression who looked to be in his 50s. According to the information next to him, his name is Mok Sinsoo and he is the boss of Wood God, a mafia family active in Seoul.

"The request fee is a total of 15 billion won. We received 5 billion won as a down payment, and the remaining amount will be sent after confirming the target's death."

Yu Geuntae whistled at the request fee.

"15 billion won? That's a lot!"

"Wood God is a mafia that dominates Guro-gu. And the deadline is tomorrow. They want us to kill him before tomorrow. So, I'm going to give this request to the newbie."

It was Hyeonji who protested against that.

"No. Wait a minute. Why are you suddenly giving it to the newbie? We usually get applicants first."

"It's a test of the newbie's skills. Of course, I'm not going to make him do it alone. I'm going to add 3 more people. Anyone want to do it?"

Three people raised their hands.

Yu Geuntae, Hyeonji, Ulla.

"That's perfect. Taker. How about it? Do you want to do it? The request fee is naturally divided among the four of you."

"I'd love to make money. But it doesn't seem like the money will be divided evenly?"

"That's something you guys have to discuss. If you can't come to an agreement, call me. I'll mediate."

"Well, if that's the case.... But Ulla is that person over there, right? He looks young, is he okay?"

I pointed to Ulla in the back. She is a woman of the same gender as Hyeonji.

She is a woman wearing a large gray coat and is only about 120cm tall. She is wearing a hood so deeply that her face is not visible.

Of course, I know her identity. She is in her late 30s, and like Sohn Jeonghu, her entire body except for her brain is mechanical. She looks like a young girl on the outside, so she was popular with fans of the original work.

She was definitely not my type.

"Ulla is an excellent sniper. She can snipe from as far as 6km away?"

"Ah. I see."

I said as if I wasn't impressed. I really wasn't interested in her.

According to my plan, the time I spend with these guys will not be even 30 days.

'First of all, I have to do my best in this job. If Sohn Jeonghu thinks my abilities are lacking.... He might not take me with him on that day.'
