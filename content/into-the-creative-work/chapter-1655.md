「The Ruler of the Golden Desert has died.」

「You have won the special event.」

Seeing the system notification, I smiled contentedly.

I didn't directly confirm Hatep's corpse, but the system notified me of my victory. Since the system confirmed it, Hatep must be dead.

'Well, it's impossible to survive that explosion.'

As you can see from the mushroom cloud, it's like a small nuclear explosion. Most of Hatep's golden sand soldiers were swept away, and my undead legion was also damaged as a result. About 3,000 advanced undead and less than 10,000 intermediate ones.

'It doesn't matter. I was going to clean them up after this was over anyway.'

「You acquire control of 8 districts, including the 5,144 district, the Golden Desert.」

The corners of my mouth tear.

I am given control of 8 districts, including the 5,144 district, the Golden Desert.

With this, I have control of a total of 15 districts in the western desert area alone. I have become the owner of the western desert area of Atlantis.

'I get 2.1 million AP every month from the western desert area alone. That's several times more than most nobles in the central district earn.'

However, the western desert area has not developed as much as the central district. The level of civilization is low. It's barren because it's a desert. The population is small compared to its size. It can be said that the population of the desert area has decreased by more than half due to this situation.

It is difficult to expect other income except for the fixed AP that I get.

Why not develop the district? I don't have time for that. I don't want to waste money on that either. It would be faster to invade other wealthy districts.

'The desert area should not be developed. The moment it develops to some extent, the bastards in the center will have their eyes on it.'

The reason why the desert area does not receive attention from the central district is because it is barren. Although the Golden Desert has a gold mine, it was not enough to attract the attention of the central district. The value of gold in Atlantis was not as enormous as it is on Earth.

'Special metals like mithril are more valuable than gold.'

「You are given three rewards for winning the special event.」

「First reward. Choose one of three traits and skills.」

「No Life King, Pharaoh, Black Magic Aptitude.」

「No Life King

Rules the dead.

Type: Unique Trait

Rank: SS」

「Pharaoh

King of the desert.

Stats increase in the desert.

Enemies feel intimidated.

Has high control.

Type: Trait

Rank: SS」

「Black Magic Aptitude

Has a high aptitude for black magic.

Type: Skill

Rank: SSS」

These three are the skills I got when the special event started. Every single one of them is an excellent skill. There are no skills with a rank below S. Each and every one of them is a valuable skill.

'But I have to choose one of these?'

The worry didn't last long.

First of all, No Life King.

With this unique trait, the undead I control will not rebel. Even the highest-level undead, the lich, will sacrifice itself without complaint.

'I'm not going to continue as a necromancer, so I don't need it.'

Accordingly, there is no reason to choose Black Magic Aptitude. Black Magic Aptitude (SSS) is the most outstanding and fraudulent skill among these three... but personally, black magic didn't suit me.

'In the end, the only thing I can choose is Pharaoh. Not bad. If I have this, there will be fewer guys who rebel.'

It will help me control 15 desert districts.

「You choose the trait, Pharaoh (SS).」

「No Life King (SS) and Black Magic Aptitude (SSS) are removed.」

「As Black Magic Aptitude (SSS) disappears, Undead Modification (SS) also disappears.」

「Second reward.」

「Choose one of Isis's Staff (S), Horus's Eye (A), Apophis's Poison (SS).」

The second one was not a skill but items.

Isis's Staff (S) was a staff for wizards. If I had used Black Magic Aptitude (SSS), I would have chosen Isis's Staff without looking back. Because it is a staff with abilities that any wizard would covet, regardless of the type of magic.

In other words, it was a useless item for those who are not wizards.

'Horus's Eye (A) is a unique item that is both bound and growth-type.'

If you have Horus's Eye, your senses are amplified, and you can see things you can't see.

I don't know what will happen if it grows... but growing it was also a job. I already have a lot of things to do, and I have to grow Horus's Eye (A)? Just imagining it was terrible.

And there was no guarantee that Horus's Eye (A) would be of much help to me. I have the Yuhee Life App skill called Cheonan (天眼)/Heavenly Eye, and I don't think Cheonan is any less capable than Horus's Eye.

'In the end, there's only one option left.'

「Apophis's Poison

It's a terrible poison.

Even gods will be horrified.

It is fatal to gods related to light.

Rank: SS」

In Greek mythology, it is comparable to Hydra's poison.

'No. Hydra is a minor monster that gets defeated by Heracles, but Apophis is the archenemy of the sun god Ra. Considering that, it's far more potent than Hydra's poison.'

To be honest, I didn't really like it. If the other two were even a little more useful, I wouldn't have chosen Apophis's poison.

「You choose Apophis's Poison (SS).」

A small white jar appeared in the air. I accepted the jar. The mouth of the jar is covered with paper. I slightly lift the paper to check the inside of the jar. There was a black, sticky liquid inside. I sniffed and smelled a stuffy smell.

'It's a dangerous item, so I should put it in my inventory.'

「Third reward.」

「Choose one of the Unique Trait SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket, Trait SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket, Skill SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket.」

As soon as I saw the notification window, I clenched my fist.

'The third reward was real.'

SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket. It's not something that raises you from SS to SSS, but something that instantly changes you to SSS rank, whether it's A rank or B rank. What you need to pay attention to here is the chance ticket.

'It doesn't raise you to SSS rank as soon as you use it, but it gives you a chance.'

Even this is something to be grateful for.

「You acquire the Trait SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket.」

「Trait SSS Rank Increase Chance Ticket

Grants you the chance to raise one of your traits to SSS rank.

Type: Consumable

Rank: SSS」

I chose a trait.

I have three traits. Lightning (SS), Cheonma Jiche(S), Pharaoh (SS). I decided to make Lightning (SS) SSS rank among these.

'I use the Lightning Blade state window more often than the Cheonma state window. And Cheonma Shingong is already SSS rank.'

Of course, I didn't intend to use it right away. According to the original story, the opportunity is given in the form of a quest. It's right to proceed when I have time.

'I've received all the rewards. I'm satisfied just by getting the SSS rank increase chance ticket.'

I was about to turn around when I remembered that I was still wearing Tutankhamun's golden mask.

'I've become too used to Tutankhamun's golden mask.'

It was the moment I was about to take off the golden mask. I see Gula and the golden warrior fighting.

'They're still fighting?'

Hatep is dead.

The golden sand soldier is an existence created by Hatep's skill. Now that Hatep is dead, the golden sand soldier should disappear.

'Only that golden warrior is special....'

I closed my mouth.

This is because the golden sand soldiers, except for the golden warrior fighting Gula, were still intact. Although the number had greatly decreased due to the recent attack, nearly 30,000 golden sand soldiers were still standing.

'Hatep is dead! The system confirmed it and paid the reward! But why aren't the golden sand soldiers disappearing?!'

I looked to see where Hatep was. I couldn’t see clearly because of the smoke. After thinking it over, I ordered the undead legion.

"Go! Go and see what's going on! If there are any survivors, kill them! Destroy the golden sand soldiers!"

The undead legion advanced according to my command. The action is strangely slow. This is because I don't have the No Life King (SS) unique trait and the Black Magic Aptitude (SSS) skill. Still, I'm glad they're still listening to my orders.

I noticed something strange. Unlike the golden warrior fighting Gula, the ordinary golden sand soldiers did not move even when the undead attacked.

The undead legion continued to advance, smashing the golden sand soldiers.

And it was a certain moment.

The wind blew.

The huge wind becomes a sandstorm and sweeps away the undead legion in an instant. I staggered and stepped back, using Cheonma Shingong to avoid being blown away by the strong wind, planting Moses's staff on the ground and crouching down.

「The Owner of the Oasis (False) begins a transaction with Meaningless Death (False).」

'Transaction? What transaction?'

I couldn't find the answer to the question.

This is because I have to endure so that I am not caught in the sandstorm blowing from far away. I planted Moses's staff on the ground and crouched down, using Cheonma Shingong to avoid being blown away by the strong wind.

The sandstorm, which was attracting everything as if compressing it, suddenly exploded in reverse. The dust that was blocking my vision burst out in all directions at once. I couldn't withstand this explosion either and rolled backwards, crashing into the sandy ground.

I quickly got up.

Peo-eok. Puk!

Undead were falling around me. It's the undead that was dragged into the wind. The undead, with only their heads or torsos left, trembled and stopped.

I looked straight ahead and swallowed.

Hatep was standing in the sand pit. His skin was dark green. I felt an overwhelming presence from him.

「The Owner of the Oasis (False) accepts the proposal of Meaningless Death.」

「Meaningless Death (False) absorbs the Owner of the Oasis (False).」

「Meaningless Death descends upon Meaningless Death (False).」

I check the first notification window. There is no indication of (False) after Meaningless Death. The real Osiris in the sky above is directly intervening in the current situation.

Hwi-i-i-ing.

A sand wind blew. The calmly blowing sand wind brushed past the golden sand soldiers. The golden sand soldiers collapsed, and the golden powder was carried by the wind and gathered to Hatep. Hatep slowly opened his eyes as he was hit by the golden wind.

Hatep opens his mouth.

I intuitively knew that this was not Hatep. Hatep is already dead.

"I will take away your death."

Osiris declared.
