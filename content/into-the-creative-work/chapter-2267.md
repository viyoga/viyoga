I entered the world of God's Atlantis.

Since the A-Plan Legion was on vacation, I didn't have anything to do right away. Not that it's a bad thing. There are things to do in the [God's Atlantis] world.

Sex is one of them. Spending time slapping Joo Seohyun-ssi's ass isn't a bad thing either.

But I had something to do now.

,I should buy a space-time scroll.'

Space-time scrolls are essential in Atlantis. Because the world is vast and divided into zones.

,The zones are stuck together to form a continent. And the rulers of the zones are all different.'

A zone is like a territory in the Middle Ages. Each zone is a country ruled by an individual.

The problem is when crossing zones.

Usually, you can pass by paying a toll, but if something happens, they block the border and control it.

,If there's an airplane, you can fly through the sky... but most of the transportation in this world is horses.'

The problem is that this world is damn wide. Riding a horse? When the hell are you going to get to your destination?

Therefore, those with money use space-time scrolls. Especially scrolls that can directly set coordinates are popular.

「Entered Zone 4, AP Store.」

「Welcome to the AP Store.」

「The AP Store allows you to buy items with AP or sell them for AP. Only ranked items can be sold, and they can only be sold at 30% of the market price.」

「The AP Store is only available for 2 hours a day.」

「What do you want?」

I arrived at the store after a long time.

The items sold in the store pop up in a row. There are quite a lot of items for sale. Consumables like recovery potions and space-time scrolls are refilled every certain period. However, since they are not infinitely stocked, the market price rises or falls.

My goal is a space-time scroll, but I looked around at other items first.

,You never know. There might be a jackpot item.'

There were no jackpot items.

Efficient elixirs or very special items are sold as soon as they appear in the store. Because the giant Legions are always watching the store.

There were a few items that caught my eye.

「Grape of the Evil God - 15,000,000 AP」

「Grape of the Evil God

The user will be drunk for 10 years.

Rank: B」

It's an ambiguous ability to use with the intention of harming someone. If you hate the target, you can buy highly lethal poisons and curses for a cheaper price.

,There are zones that you can only enter when you're drunk.'

It's mentioned in the original work. It was a zone called the Memory of the Drunken Immortal. I don't know the exact location, but if you have the Grape of the Evil God, you can easily conquer it.

,The original protagonist, Kang Myungjin-gun, knows. But even if he knows, he won't go.'

It was a simple reason. Efficiency. Even if you invest 15 million AP and succeed in conquering it, you won't get much in return. It means you won't even break even.

,It's too expensive. Usually, you can't even get 10,000 AP easily.'

The basic way to earn AP is by hunting monsters. When you kill a monster, you can get AP separately from the byproducts. Since you have to risk your life, the number of monster hunters is bound to be small.

Also, the gods can sponsor AP. The problem is that the gods are stingy. Even Zeus, my contracted god, is high-ranking, but he doesn't sponsor unless there's something special.

,The easiest way to earn money is through the monthly salary from ruling a zone.'

If you rule a zone, you get AP every 30 days. It's like a rent. The rent is naturally more expensive the bigger and better it is.

,Let's see. How much AP do I have now...'

「AP Owned: 21,720,808」

Twenty-one million.

It's an amount that makes my heart swell.

,I get more than 3 million AP every month. Of course, it has to be this much.'

2.1 million AP of that comes from the desert region. 15 desert zones where the Cheonma Shin-gyo is growing.

In addition to that, AP accumulated from doing various things.

I checked the price of the space-time scroll, which was my goal.

「Space-Time Scroll.

This is a space-time scroll that does not have coordinates set.

You must set the coordinates to use the scroll. You can set the coordinates by putting mana in the location.

Current Coordinates Set: None

Type: Consumable

Rank: B」

「Space-Time Scroll: 65,690 AP」

「Remaining Quantity of Space-Time Scrolls: 1,002」

「20 days until the next shipment arrives.」

I sighed as soon as I saw it. The market price has risen since the last time I saw it, when it was around 60,000 AP.

,As the level of Atlantis generally rises over time, the demand for space-time scrolls has also increased. The market price will continue to rise.'

I should have come to the store when I had the chance. I regret it a bit.

,But I probably won't come for a while. Because it's annoying. AP will eventually accumulate anyway. If I buy little by little, the market price will rise, so let's buy it all at once.'

「Purchase 300 Space-Time Scrolls.」

「19,707,000 AP will be consumed. Proceed?」

"Proceed."

With 300, I can use them freely in other worlds without worrying.

「AP Owned: 2,013,808」

300 space-time scrolls appeared in front of me. I tied them together with a string and put them in my inventory.

,The remaining 2 million AP... Should I save it? Or use it? If I save it, I can use it right away when I need it later... but that time isn't right now.'

「Space-Time Scroll: 67,010 AP」

The market price temporarily rose sharply because I bought a large quantity at once. It will stabilize over time, but I'm not that patient.

,Should I invest in zones and earn more points like others?'

After thinking about it, I just went outside the store. It was too annoying to manage and raise zones one by one.

,I should go back and tease Joo Seohyun-ssi.'

It was the moment I was about to tear a space-time scroll and return at once.

The world shook.

The ground and the sky shook at the same time. I gave strength to my lower body to prevent myself from falling.

"Euaaaaaaak!"

"Suddenly an earthquake?!"

"Get out of the building!"

The ground shook violently and began to crack. Fortunately, it didn't crack so much that people would fall in.

I looked up at the sky while maintaining my balance. It's not just my imagination. The sky is also shaking. It was certain when I saw the clouds scattering. Then, at some point, the sky also began to crack like the ground. The cracks in the sky become larger, like cracks in transparent glass.

"Look at the sky! Something. Something is happening!"

"What the hell is going on?!"

"Shit. Did a large Legion cause an accident?!"

"System! Explain! System!"

There was a universe inside the crack. A universe with countless stars twinkling. It was awe-inspiring and beautiful. People stared blankly at the crack in the sky. A strange magic was attracting people's attention.

Crack, crack!

The crack in the sky grew larger before it could recover.

The starlight in the universe within the crack began to shine more brilliantly.

,...Is that really starlight?'

It was then. Something black covered the crack. I thought the system had started working... but it wasn't.

It was a giant eyeball. A red eyeball looked down at us from within the crack. The pupil was an alien rectangle, long and horizontal like a sheep or goat.

"Aaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!"

"Stooooooop!"

"Don't loooooooook!"

People scream. They grab their heads and collapse, cover their own eyes, or harm themselves by hitting their heads on the ground. They throw off their clothes and wail, and attack their surroundings indiscriminately. They've all gone crazy, but there are degrees. Ordinary people have gone completely insane.

「Atlantis's damage has been confirmed.」

「Starting the restoration of Atlantis.」

「External influence has affected Atlantis.」

「Removing external influence.」

The crack is getting smaller.

The eyeball in the crack spun around, looking around inside, and then stopped. The place that eyeball was facing was me.

"What the fuck."

I raised my right middle finger because I didn't like being stared at. I don't know if it understood what it meant.

"Come at me if you're pissed."

It can't come at me.

Because the system is protecting me here. The crack is also getting smaller, and the guys who went crazy after making eye contact with that thing are slowly returning to normal. Even the guys who already committed suicide are going back to before they died, as if time is rewinding.

As long as the system is here, that thing can't do anything. Probably. Even if it's difficult with just the system, the other gods won't want the game to break, so they'll help.

The eyeball, which had no reaction, closed its eyes. I don't feel emotions like anger or joy. I felt politeness in that act. Then, the eye that opened again stopped observing Atlantis, turned its body, and left somewhere in the universe.

I saw it. Something black that was destroying the stars of the universe and disappearing somewhere. It didn't feel like it was destroying the stars on purpose. The stars are just being destroyed and disappearing due to the aftermath of its movement.

-Mother has left.

I was startled by the sudden voice and turned my gaze to the front.

A pharaoh with pale skin is smiling in front of me.

「??? is smiling at you.」

Black Pharaoh.

I know its identity. That body itself must be one of its avatars.

"...Crawling Chaos. It's moving very openly."

-It's okay now. Right now, the system is projecting all its capabilities to clean up the aftermath. The other gods are the same. A natural disaster has struck and disappeared, so shouldn't they clean up the aftermath?

"What the hell is going on right now?"

-I don't know. Don't try to understand natural disasters. One thing is certain, that person has left.

"......Where to?"

-The boundaries have disappeared and all restrictions have been lifted... so it probably headed outside. I don't know if it succeeded. I don't know more than that.

"Is Atlantis okay?"

-It's okay because there's a system. This is just an aftereffect. Unlike other stars, there's a system, so it can be cleaned up enough. Hmm, oh. I can't talk much either. The recovery speed is much faster than expected.

"What did you come for?"

-I came to let you know that I'm not your enemy. And since those people have disappeared... the universe will be noisy for the first time in a long time. Ah, chaos is being drawn. It's going to be fun.

The Black Pharaoh tore his mouth and smiled.

"You showed up just to spout that kind of bullshit?"

-Hehe. The fact that you and I had a conversation is important. I have high expectations for you.

The Black Pharaoh disappeared.
