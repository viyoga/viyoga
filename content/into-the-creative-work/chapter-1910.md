The old mansion I got from selling the Phantasmal World maple sapling to Baron Arpon.

I liked this old mansion. It had an old-fashioned charm that you couldn't find in other mansions. It was old. In other words, it signified tradition and history. In Neo London, that value was high.

Several maids and butlers worked at the mansion. They were people Yuria picked herself before entering the Maid Academy. Five in total. It was enough people to manage this old mansion.

,Are you awake, Master-nim? I'll prepare breakfast soon."

,No, I don't have an appetite. Skip breakfast, I'll eat lunch out."

,Yes. Understood."

I headed to the garden where I planted the Phantasmal World cherry tree. These days, my daily morning routine was checking on and tending to the cherry tree.

I headed to the center of the garden where the small cherry tree was. Its size had grown more than three times since I first saw it. And it was still growing rapidly. Its growth rate was on a completely different level from ordinary trees. And the nutrients it needed for growth were also different from ordinary trees.

,A tree that likes being fed meat, huh...."

I had been steadily catching beasts and feeding them to the cherry tree. But besides the food, there was something more important.

Mental communion.

If I didn't maintain mental communion, the cherry tree would wither quickly. I had to do it at least two to three times a week. I don't know what the mechanism is, but mental communion has a big impact on this cherry tree.

I approached the cherry tree and reached out my hand.

Pajijik.

Electricity crackled along with mana.

Cherry blossoms bloomed profusely on the tree that had been bare like a winter tree. The light pink cherry blossom petals swayed gently in the breeze.

The employees who were working stopped for a moment and stared blankly at this. It was still a small tree, but its beauty was enough to attract people's attention.

Mental communion.

At first, I was confused because I didn't know what to do. And now I'm communicating with this guy in my own way.

I open the Astral, which can be said to be the mind of a wizard.

I and the cherry tree were connected. It felt like our minds were merging into one.

,It's amazing that a tree has a mind."

It wasn't as clear a mind as a human or animal. The mind was as hazy as fog. But it definitely existed. Emotions were faint, but I could feel them.

,It's sad again today."

The cause of the sadness was Yuria. The cherry tree missed Yuria's touch. It also meant that Yuria had been taking good care of the cherry tree.

,Your master is me. Don't forget."

Pajijik.

Sparks flew between the cherry blossom petals. It was showing its will, saying it understood.

,Is there something you want?"

A clear image popped up.

Water.

Water with ice floating in it.

,Ice water, huh? Alright. I'll pour it for you."

I snapped my fingers. Ice water created by magic drenched the cherry tree. The cherry tree shook its branches.

,Too cold? You're the one who wanted it. Endure it."

It complains that I lack the meticulousness compared to Yuria. I chuckled.

According to the druid, I should be careful because I could be eaten in reverse during mental communion, but I didn't feel that dangerous at all. The cherry tree is a bit picky, but it acknowledges me.

,Can't it grow faster?"

The cherry tree's will is conveyed. It says it will grow when the time comes, so wait.

,Alright. I'll wait. But remember. There's no such thing as a free lunch in this world. Instead of me raising you, you have to cooperate with me. That's the contract."

I reaffirmed the contract I made when I first had mental communion. The cherry tree showed a sense of annoyance. Because it was what I said yesterday and the day before yesterday.

I didn't want to repeat the same words either. But the cherry tree was a tree. I had no idea how good its memory was. So I had to engrave it every day. So that it can't say anything different later.

,I am your master. I am your master. I am your master. You must trust and follow me."

I ended the mental communion, repeating it almost like brainwashing.

Then I got dressed and went to the factory. In the case of YuYu Chicken, I hired an agent, so I'm only receiving reports.

I enter the factory. There were a few magic engineering machines, but they haven't been adjusted yet. It will take some time for the factory to be operational.

I sat down at a table on one side of the factory. I picked up the magic engineering item I was researching yesterday. It was a human hand. A magic engineering hand up to the wrist.

Magic engineering can be applied to all sorts of things. That's no exception for the human body. If you have money, you can replace your hands, eyes, feet, and even internal organs with magic engineering machines.

You can easily escape the weak human body by replacing it.

Mercenaries and soldiers mainly want magic engineering bodies, but these days, everyone has at least one magic engineering body part. That's the trend.

Pajijik.

I create a faint electric current and connect it to the magic engineering hand.

,It reacts immediately to the bio-signal level current. It's a popular model these days, so there's no blockage."

The magic engineering hand crawled across the desk, using its fingers like legs, according to my will. Later, when I got used to it, I could even do tricks.

,It goes without saying that it's much stronger than a human hand... If I fix the magic circle and material, I can exert hundreds of kilograms of grip strength."

The sensation was the same as a human hand. When I held something in my hand, I felt the sensation of holding it. Because I formed artificial nerves with electricity and magic, I could feel the sensation as it was.

,Huh?"

The magic engineering hand stutters a bit. I only played with it a little roughly, but the nervous system is overloaded.

,It's not a premium product for combat, but it's already overloaded? At this rate, I won't be able to do rough exercise for even 10 minutes."

I disassembled the magic engineering hand. Various mechanical parts were intricately intertwined. I carefully detached the parts. Each part had a different manufacturer.

,They've all put security magic on each and every part."

I released the security magic. It's just cumbersome, not difficult. I can see the special magic circles intricately engraved on the parts. Each and every one of these is technology. They are classified as patents. If I use them carelessly, I'll get caught up in a patent lawsuit.

,The magic on the nervous system side is... more crude than I thought. I understand. Even in medicine, human nerves are incredibly complex."

I twisted the corners of my lips as I checked the parts on the nerve side.

,There's room for improvement, isn't there?"

Of course, I couldn't do it right now. I don't plan to make it as high-end as the premium grade. It just needs to be a little better than these parts now. Then I can successfully enter the market. Existing nerve part manufacturers? Who cares. The market is originally a competition. A place where you become obsolete if you fall behind.

,I've decided that this will be the factory's first product."

I started researching.
