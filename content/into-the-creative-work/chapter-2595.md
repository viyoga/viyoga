Yura came into my house. She looked around, craning her neck to take it all in.

,"I heard a man's room is basically dirty... It's clean. To the point where there's almost nothing except decorations. It's like this house is just for sleeping."

I looked around my house.

It was definitely too clean when I looked at it like this. But this couldn't be helped.

,'The inventory is fucking convenient, you know.'

I put everything in my inventory and live like that. If there's something I want, I take it out of the inventory, and if there's something I don't need, I throw it in the inventory. I can take things out or throw them away from my seat. When the inventory is full of trash, I can just dump it in a suitable Play world or a closed dungeon on a large scale.

,'Food is the same. If you put it in the inventory, it won't spoil. It's better than a refrigerator.'

Still, because I have feelings, I put water in the refrigerator.

Cleaning? I don't really do it, but Han Ha-rin comes and does it sometimes. And if the house gets too dirty, Han Ha-rin nags me, so I'm trying not to make it too dirty.

,"There's no problem living here. Can I get you something to drink?"

,"Green tea. Can I smoke?"

,"This is a non-smoking area."

I took a cup out of the inventory. It was a cup filled with warm green tea.

,"That's an amazing ability. It's not an ability to make green tea, is it?"

,"It's an ability to store things. In game terms, it's an inventory."

Yura took a sip of green tea and widened her eyes.

,"It's delicious...! This is the best green tea I've ever had!"

It was green tea from the Guangmyeong Ascension world. It's not an elixir, but it's a high-quality green tea that contains a little bit of spiritual energy. Milyeong made the green tea. If I made the green tea, it wouldn't taste like this.

,"I want to hear more about the World Hunter Association."

It's something related to me. It was something I couldn't avoid just because I didn't want to get involved.

But I didn't intend to just take it. If they come at me, I'll deal with them. If they define me as an enemy, I'll gladly become an enemy.

Yura explained the situation while drinking green tea.

In summary, the World Hunter Association decided to put things about me on hold and watch. They don't intend to contact me first unless I want them to. But they can't just ignore me, so Yura was dispatched as a collaborator and monitor.

,"That's bland. They're not hostile to me?"

,"The World Hunter Association isn't some kind of evil organization. There's no reason to get rid of you. It's more beneficial to build a good relationship with you."

,"But you said they're putting it on hold."

,"That's right. That's why I think it's strange too. The higher-ups rarely put problems on hold."

,"You can tell me all that?"

,"Of course not. But I decided to bet on you."

Yura continued.

,"The World Hunter Association sees your ability as the ability to travel to other worlds. They suspect that the original of HB-1, which is produced by Sejin Group, is something you brought from another world. Is that true?"

I was taken aback.

The ability to travel to other worlds. Isn't that the core ability of Play Life? The fact that the world is a fictional world is a little different, though.

,'They probably didn't find out about my ability, Play Life.'

Judging from Yura's reaction, it doesn't seem like particularly great information either. The World Hunter Association has been aware of the existence of other worlds for a while now.

,'It's not surprising. Park Suho also has the ability to travel to other worlds. There must have been abilities to travel to other worlds like Park Suho's ability before.'

I thought for a while and answered.

,"That's right. But I don't intend to explain my ability in detail."

,"Okay."

,"...But is it a problem to bring things from other worlds and sell them?"

,"HB-1 and Nectar are fine. They don't cause great harm to the world. Especially in the case of HB-1, the World Hunter Association is using it well."

From my point of view, HB-1, not to mention Nectar, is an excessively innovative product. A more magical liquid than magic that allows you to harvest fruits by simply planting plant seeds and sprinkling one drop of HB-1, which allows them to grow instantly.

,'But they say it's okay? The World Hunter Association's standards are more lenient than I thought. Well, it's not a directly dangerous item.'

I confirmed through Yura that the World Hunter Association was not hostile to me.

,"Your strength is above the average of S-class. You'd need at least two S-class to take you on."

,"Above average, huh. So there's more than that?"

,"Even among S-class, there's compatibility. Some have weaker physical abilities than C-class, but their abilities are ridiculously S-class. Those with abilities that deserve the term 'out of the ordinary'. You'll be taken down in an instant if you're careless."

I didn't get angry at Yura's somewhat provocative words.

My ability, Play Life, is already an extraordinary ability. And so is Park Suho's tattoo ability.

,"What kind of abilities specifically?"

,"I don't know the details either. Their information is confidential."

Yura's position in the World Hunter Association is not low, but it's not high either. The abilities of S-class are top secret that even Yura can't know.

,"There are quite a few S-class that aren't officially known."

,"Yeah. Because S-class each pursue different things."

The conversation ended.

I was able to get meaningful information.

,'The power of the World Hunter Association is more than I thought. I didn't intend to, but... I don't need to be enemies with the World Hunter Association.'

A sudden silence came.

We stared into each other's eyes.

The favor she showed me. Of course, I know there's no such thing as a free lunch. But I can definitely pay her back for her favor.

I took Yura's hand and headed to the bedroom. Yura didn't resist.

With this alone, I could say that I had eaten more than half of South Korea. The reason I say half is because I haven't completely taken over the provinces yet.
