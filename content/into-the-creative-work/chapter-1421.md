The students of Arashi Academy are serious about the cultural festival. They were genuinely enjoying themselves from the preparation stage.

The problem is that Japan's unique craftsmanship is displayed in this preparation process.

,Trying to do everything themselves from start to finish...'

I think they can make the sign. You can easily make a sign by bringing a suitable wooden board and painting it, or drawing a picture on paper.

,What's with making chairs and tables themselves?'

It was hard to understand.

Isn't it a waste of time to make chairs and tables yourself for a cultural festival in this day and age? I don't think academy students are good at making chairs. Chairs and tables made by companies are prettier and more valuable.

,Well, if it's a budget issue, making furniture is understandable.'

Each class was allocated a budget for the cultural festival. The budget was not a large sum of money, even as a figure of speech. It's less than 1 million won in Korean currency. The budget you receive seems to vary depending on what you do for the cultural festival... The maid &amp; butler cafe is executed with the bare minimum.

,But why are they even making the clothes themselves.'

I grumbled and went out of Arashi Academy and headed to the city. Today, my mission was to purchase materials for butler &amp; maid outfits.

Of course, I didn't come alone. I look at a woman approaching me from the distance.

Shinbo Reika.

Her refreshing green hair swayed every time she walked, as she has the blood of an elf mixed in her.

Is she really an elf? All the men around her turned their attention to her. Some men stared blankly, while others were conscious of Reika while pretending not to.

Is it because we're outside the academy? Reika was in casual clothes.

Jeans and a sky blue t-shirt.

It's nothing special, but it's a different story if you have long arms and legs and a voluptuous E-cup chest. Furthermore, Reika is also tall. Watching her walk, the city streets felt like a runway.

Reika approaches me. The envious gazes of the men around me turn to me. I curled up the corners of my lips and mocked them.

,Sorry. Did you wait long?'

Reika said. It was a clear voice.

,No. About 10 minutes? And you don't have to be sorry because you weren't late for the appointment. By the way, your clothes are pretty today.'

,R-really? I just threw something on, so I'm glad it doesn't look weird.'

It was a textbook compliment, but Reika blushed. I could feel how much experience she had with men here.

,I can give her as many of these simple compliments as she wants.'

There are hardly any women who don't like compliments. Men also like compliments. Especially when someone you like compliments you even more.

,Let's see. Reika's favorability is currently....'

『Reika's Favorability: 64』

,It's 3 points higher than when I saw her yesterday. Is it the first date bonus?'

A favorability of 64 means she's seriously thinking about dating.

,In other words, if I do my job properly... I can bang her.'

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

I make plans leisurely in a slowed-down world. The reason we met today is to buy materials for the butler &amp; maid outfits. What's important here is what happens next. I have to create a date-like atmosphere and lead her.

,Reika. Would you like to stop by a cafe for a moment? I'll buy.'

,A cafe?'

,I'm also thirsty... and we're doing a cafe at the cultural festival. We're going to a cafe to see how they operate. It would be nice if there was a maid cafe or a butler cafe, but those would be in a bigger city.'

Maid cafe? Butler cafe? Even if there are such places, I won't go. It's not a suitable place for a date.

,Okay. Let's stop by.'

,Do you go to cafes often?'

,I don't really like tea that much... I just stop by occasionally?'

,Me too.'

,...That's unexpected. I thought you'd like tea because you make black tea so well. The kids said you worked at a cafe.'

,I've never worked at a cafe. Black tea is... I like it, but not enough to go searching for cafes. What about you, coffee? Or green tea and black tea?'

,I'll have coffee, please.'

I ordered coffee and fresh juice. It's much better to drink fresh juice than to eat the cafe's tasteless coffee and tea.

,It's not like I'm really thirsty and came to the cafe.'

The important thing was to create an atmosphere.

Reika and I talked while drinking our drinks. She mainly asked about the Korean Academy, and I asked about the Yggdrasil Knights. After leaving the cafe, we walked around the streets looking for fabric.

,We don't have much of a budget. I think we'll have to go around and compare. Shinbo-ssi, are you free?'

,If it's a budget issue, there's nothing we can do. I have time.'

After that, we went around the city to find out about fabrics.

My plan was to drag out the time until dinner. Have dinner and use dessert as an excuse to wander around, then lure her to a motel because she's tired. Normally, it wouldn't work, but... Reika's favorability is currently 64. It wasn't entirely impossible.

,What should we eat for dinner? Since we're in Japan, should we eat sushi?'

I have enough money, so I thought of a high-end sushi restaurant. If I look for it, there will be one somewhere. But I'm worried that Reika might feel burdened.

In times like this, it's best to ask directly. When I turned to her, she was focused on looking at a street corner. My gaze naturally followed her. A woman with her black hair tied in a ponytail was walking busily somewhere.

,...Miyakado Mieko?'

I hardened my face. It was very rare for her, a shrine maiden of Tsukuyomi Shrine, to come outside the academy. In the original story, she is famous for not agreeing even if you give all sorts of reasons to play outside the academy together.

,She's even wearing casual clothes and is out here. Judging by her stiff expression, it doesn't seem like she came to play....'

What's going on? I'm very curious.

,Seong-ssi. I'm sorry, but I think we should part ways here today.'

Suddenly, Reika said with an apologetic expression. It was obvious what was going on. She's trying to follow Mieko. Reika and Mieko are somewhat close, so she must be worried.

,There's no reason to refuse. Forcibly clinging to her will only lower her favorability.... More than anything, I'm also curious about Mieko.'

I nodded to Reika.

,Okay. Instead, next time... buy me a drink or something.'

,You like cola, right? I'm sorry for suddenly parting ways today.'

Reika headed in the direction Mieko was going at a fast pace.

,Moka.'

I summon the thunder owl, Moka. I don't materialize her, but point to the sky with my eyes. Moka spread her large wings and soared high into the sky.

,Monitor Mieko.'

The mana in my body moved and headed to my eyes. My pupils turned golden. I activated the Spirit Eye.

My right vision changed. A view looking down from the sky. One of the abilities of the Spirit Eye is vision sharing. I can share vision with the contracted spirit.

,If I use this, tailing someone is a piece of cake. Should I approach slowly?'

I stopped walking forward. That's because Clara was visible in Moka's vision.

,Why is she here again?'

Clara, who is popular, doesn't try to go outside the academy as much as Mieko. Of course, except when she's working as a singer.

,Is it a singer activity? No. She's alone. If it were a singer activity, her manager or bodyguards would have followed her.'

Of course, she didn't come out to play. Clara's face was as serious as Mieko's. I scratched my cheek. I unconsciously imagine the choices.

-Follow Miyakado Mieko.

-Follow Clara Peyrede.

-Do nothing and return to the dormitory.

Three choices. In the original story, it would have appeared like this.

The worst thing is to do nothing.

,If I do anything, an event will occur.'

I pondered for a moment and decided to choose both.

,This isn't a game, it's reality, so there's no law that says I have to choose only one option.'

I had Moka.

,Moka. You follow Mieko.'

Reika is chasing after Mieko. Even if something happens, Reika's help can help to some extent.

On the other hand, Clara is alone. If something happens, no one can help her.

I started running in the direction Clara was going.

,She's going to the outskirts of the city. What the hell is going on?'

Clara was not visible in Moka's vision. As Moka followed Mieko, Clara disappeared from view. Mieko entered a luxury hotel. Moka also tried to enter the hotel, but I stopped her.

,Don't go in. There's a barrier protecting the hotel. Even if you're in spirit form, you'll be caught the moment you enter. Look inside the hotel through the window.'

-Kkuuung.

He had a square jaw and his impression wasn't good, even as a figure of speech. However, he gave off the atmosphere of a detective rather than a Yakuza.

I felt relieved as soon as I saw him. That's because he was a character in the original story.

,Hiragi Jiro. An information broker with a grudge against the Magic Authority. Originally, he contacts Hyodo Yuki... it's changed.'

The contents of the original story can change at any time. It's not surprising anymore. The important thing is why Mieko is meeting an information broker....

Originally, Hyodo Yuki, who should have been actively involved, was not. Originally, Mieko and Yuki join forces, but... this has also changed as I intervened.

,Still, just in case.... Moka, keep watching the hotel.'

It must be the Hemmingford family targeting Clara. This is Japan, not Korea, so Hemmingford had plenty of room to intervene.

,It seems like they're trying to resolve it through dialogue first... I should intervene in something like this. Should I hide and watch for the timing?'
