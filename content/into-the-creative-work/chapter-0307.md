A week had passed since I conquered District 601 and it became the base of the A-Plan Legion.

This week could be called the busiest week ever.

Because there were quite a few villagers, basic laws had to be established, and quick-witted merchants somehow found out and quickly came to District 601, making various proposals to the A-Plan Legion.

Among us, the one who was busiest was Kang Myungjin-nim, the master of the A-Plan Legion. He moved around to establish friendly relations with the Legions that had settled near the district.

He also attracted the attention of large Legions. This was because he conquered District 601 with the Legion's own power, without receiving any other help. The A-Plan Legion is what you'd call a super rookie. In other words, coveted talent.

,They probably want to swallow the entire Legion if possible. But fortunately for us, the large Legions nearby are wary of each other and can't move recklessly.'

Other Legion members focused on internal organization.

In my case, I had to wander outside the castle most of the time because of the work Kang Myungjin-nim assigned to me.

,I don't feel good about being exploited so much, but I can't help it... It's still early days. I have to move until the A-Plan Legion stabilizes. Then the A-Plan Legion will be able to strengthen its power faster than in the original story.'

And currently, I have entered a village near District 601 called 「District 174, Cool Summer.」

It's a village with thousands of zelkova trees. There are more trees than village buildings and people. As the name Cool Summer suggests, a cool breeze blew from somewhere.

I walked towards one side of the village. There was a long, one-story building. Judging by its appearance, it looked more like a warehouse than a place for people to live, it was so shoddily made.

I continued walking towards the building. Then, about 20 people poured out of the building.

They were all lightly dressed. Some had taken off their tops and were only wearing pants, while others were wearing ragged clothes over their bodies. However, they all had ominous weapons in their hands.

One of them stepped forward. It was a middle-aged man with a dirty beard, puffing on a cigarette. With a protruding belly, he lightly swung a large halberd in one hand and slammed it into the ground with a thud.

"What the hell are you doing, barging into our Legion headquarters?"

"Barging in? As you can see, I walked in proudly from the front, you know?"

"It says right there in front that there's a barrier, so don't come in without permission, you moron."

"Ah, that thing in front? The writing was so shitty, I thought it was a scribble drawn by a kid."

"Bullshit. Your eyes must be fucked up."

The man threw his cigarette butt at me irritably. I tilted my head. The cigarette butt brushed past my head and fell to the ground. Some of the cigarette ash touched my hair.

I brushed off the ash from my hair. It was such a small amount that it didn't even stick to my fingers, but it was still there.

,I've decided.'

Kang Myungjin-nim told me to resolve it as amicably as possible. Following that, I was going to try talking at least once.

,I'm going to kill them all.'

How can I stay still when they welcome me like this?

"Oh-ho, I see some bloodlust? Looks like you're in a really bad mood?"

"Is this how you Tyrant Legion treat your guests?"

"If you're a guest, you should follow our rules like a guest. But what are you? I've never seen you before, does anyone know this bastard?"

The master of the Tyrant Legion, Tyrant, looked around at the Legion members around him and asked. They all shook their heads.

"Never seen him before, sir?"

"Well, there are more than one or two arrogant bastards like that."

"Some guys with strong unique traits tend to act like that. You have to teach those guys a lesson with a beating."

The master turned to me again.

"Did you hear that? We don't know you. Reveal your identity and the reason you came. Depending on the reason, we might spare you."

I took out a piece of paper from my pocket. The slightly crumpled paper was a letter with violent words written on it.

"Ah, that. We sent that."

The letter consisted of six lines, which can be summarized as follows.

Hand over District 601 if you don't want to die.

"If you were from the A-Plan Legion, you should have said so in advance. Then we could have at least offered you some water. But what's your answer? When are you going to hand over District 601 to us? We'd like you to vacate it by tomorrow, but we can wait about 3 days."

"I'll give it to you in about 100 years."

"Huh?"

"Wait patiently until then. If you're really bored, you can wait in hell."

"Ooh. Looks like we've got a fight on our hands. I heard the A-Plan Legion master uses a spear... Don't you have any other comrades? Don't tell me you came alone?"

"I'm enough to deal with the likes of you on my own. Now, the hell tour service starts now. No right to refuse. Crispy on the outside! Moist on the inside!"

I crumpled the paper in my hand and put it in my pocket, then clenched both fists.

Pajijijijik.

I activate Astrape (C). A skill that grants the power of lightning to weapons. This includes fists and feet as weapons. The standard for what is considered a weapon here is what I think, so if I think of my whole body as a weapon, I might be able to grant it. However, the rank would have to be higher.

"You crazy bastard. I'll cut off your head, put it in a coffin, and send it to your master."

"You guys... well, the beasts will take care of you."

I charged towards them. I didn't feel the need to draw my sword against these guys. My bare hands are enough.

,Myriad Lightning.'

I pretended to swing my fist and shot out lightning.

Kwa-rrr-rrr-rr!

The guy who was struck by the lightning couldn't even let out a scream before dying.

Peo-uk!

I punched down with my fist, as if hammering the head of a dwarf bastard charging from the lower right. Not only that, but lightning flowed into the bastard's body, ending his life.

"You son of a bitch!"

A man who had grabbed me from behind raised a greatsword high. A faint blue Sword energy formed on the blade. I almost laughed at how crude it was.

,Myriad Lightning.'

I created Myriad Lightning under his feet where he couldn't see. A lightning bolt pierced through him and soared into the sky. A lightning strike falling from the ground to the sky.

The battlefield was a mess. The enemies were unskilled in large-scale combat, probably because they hadn't trained properly. They were getting tangled up with each other.

Of course, one of the reasons for that was that I was stirring things up like a loach. I was deliberately getting involved in complicated situations.

"Hey, you bastards! What are you doing! Why are you cowering! I told you before! Dying in battle is for cowards!"

Tyrant shouted, unable to watch any longer. But it was natural for them to be afraid.

Lightning.

I am the one who controls the lightning that falls from the sky. And the loud sound of thunder is intimidating. For people, lightning is even more terrifying than fire.

Peo-uk!

I slammed my fist into a man's jaw. The man floated up and fell backward. I stepped on the falling man's body and jumped up.

As my vision widened, I saw the enemies wandering around.

,Five Myriad Lightnings.'

Five lightning bolts shot out simultaneously, rampaging wildly.

Kwa-rrr-rrr-rrr!

"Aaaaaaah!"

Thunder and screams mingled together. I swung my fists and legs in that pandemonium.

As the battle continued, the number of enemies decreased, and the number of wounds on my body increased. No matter how good I am, it was impossible to completely avoid the enemies' attacks without weapons. However, there were no fatal injuries that would affect the battle.

"......."

In the end, all the members of the Tyrant Legion except for the master, Tyrant, were dead.

I stood in front of Tyrant, who was standing like an evil spirit. A dark green Sword energy surged fiercely like a beast in the halberd he held.

"...I heard that your A-Plan Legion is made up of rookies who have only entered Atlantis for two months. Is that false information?"

My current power is something that you can never think of as a rookie. I think so myself, so how much more would the opponent think?

"No. That's true."

"You expect me to believe that?"

"Our Legion is a bit special. Don't believe it if you don't want to."

I have no intention of explaining in detail to a guy who's going to die anyway.

Tyrant clenched the halberd with both hands. Thick arm muscles like logs twitched, and blood vessels stood out. And Tyrant's body was slowly growing.

,...Is his unique trait Gigantification?'

I shot lightning at him.

Kwa-rrr-rrr-rr!

Tyrant took the lightning head-on without dodging. But the lightning didn't paralyze him. He shone brightly with both eyes and increased his size.

,Does he have a skill related to elemental resistance? Or maybe it's a characteristic of Gigantification.'

I shot stronger lightning than before. Tyrant staggered, but I wasn't very satisfied.

,At this rate, it's just a stun gun to this bastard.'

Tyrant, who had enlarged his body to 4 meters, was running towards me. Every time he took a step, it sounded like a huge rock was moving.

I ducked my head to avoid the halberd he was swinging and burrowed into his chest.

I punched Tyrant's abs.

Peo-uk!

But he didn't writhe in pain. Instead, he lifted my body with his knee. He grabbed my right calf, which was floating in the air, with his huge hand and slammed it to the ground.

"Keo-uk!"

I felt a pain that shattered my whole body. But there was no time to writhe in pain. That's because the halberd wrapped in dark green Sword energy was falling on my head.

Kwa-ang!

I rolled to the side to avoid the halberd, bounced up, and burrowed into Tyrant again.

,Myriad Lightning!'

I squeezed out all the remaining mana and created 12 Myriad Lightnings around Tyrant. And I fired the lightning all at once.

Jijijik! Jijijijijik!

The 12 Myriad Lightnings resonated with each other and turned into one huge lightning bolt, attacking Tyrant.

"Kuaaaaaaak!"

Tyrant screamed and knelt on the floor. His eyes rolled back, and he seemed to have fainted. I didn't miss that golden opportunity and stepped on his knee and started punching his head.

After hitting his head for the seventh time, his skull finally shattered, and he died.

「Acquired 7,500 AP.」

I acquired AP, so he's definitely dead.

Tyrant's corpse began to shrink. As he died, the skill was released, and his body returned to its original size.

,Since this is an opportunity, should I search for items? Maybe he has some good stuff.'

I was about to search Tyrant's corpse first. I felt a presence from behind.

When I turned my head, 12 people were approaching me warily.

Matching light green leather coats and equipment. That alone told me that they weren't just any Legion.

Standing in front of them was a woman with platinum blonde hair. Transparently white skin and distinct features. Her breasts weren't very large, about a B cup, but her figure was slim and stylish like a model.

I looked at her ears and widened my eyes. Triangular ears, different from vampires. I knew her identity as soon as I saw them.

,A Half-elf!'

Of course, I had only heard of them, and this was the first time I had actually seen one.

"Answer the question. If you continue to remain silent, we will consider you an enemy."

They exuded an ominous aura. But it was also an organized aura. They were on a different level than the Tyrant Legion.

I don't have much energy to fight right now. My mana is at rock bottom, and I need to heal the wounds on my body. Even if I used Complete Recovery, I wasn't confident that I could fight them all at the same time and win.

Maybe all I can do is run away.

"Ah, I'm sorry. I was a bit dazed from the aftereffects of the battle. I came to District 174 because I had business with the Tyrant Legion. I'm definitely not here to fight the Reeves Legion."

Their aura diminished at my words.

"The Tyrant Legion is a Legion that stays in our district by paying a fair price. If you caused this without a reason, we will not forgive you. This is a matter of our Reeves Legion's reputation."
