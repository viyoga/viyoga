I could leisurely contemplate magic thanks to the undead that Kukuru left behind. Though half of them died after getting caught in Thunder Break, the remaining half took the brunt of the enemies' attacks.

Meat shields. It's just stalling for time, but they're keeping them from reaching me. They're decent enough as a front line.

,Damn it! They keep moving even with their heads blown off?!"

,These aren't naturally occurring undead! They'll keep moving as long as the magic remains! Since there's no one who can use holy magic... the best we can do is crush their limbs to stop them from moving!"

,Shyaaaaaaa! How dare you turn my brethren into undead!"

Unlike the mercenaries who maintained their composure even in this situation, the enraged Lizardmen broke through the undead in front and charged up the stairs. Watching them, I decided on a spell.

[Blizzard]

A 7th circle ice-type wide-area magic unfolded. Unlike the lightning-type Thunder Break, this was a spell I couldn't use until I reached the 7th circle.

Gloomy dark clouds appeared in the sky, pouring snow and cold air onto the ground.

The Lizardmen, who possessed physical abilities superior to humans but were particularly vulnerable to cold, noticeably stiffened.

,Shyaaaaa! What is this!! It hurts!"

,Shyaa...!! Move! I said don't stop...!!"

The Lizardmen moved, fueled by their rage. Watching them, I prepared a new spell.

,Feeling cold? I'll give you fire."

[Hellfire]

A blue fireball appeared and fell towards the Lizardmen.

Bang!

The blue fireball exploded, scattering blue flames in all directions. Hellfire. A blue flame that doesn't go out even without oxygen, even when doused with water, even if it's underwater, until the spell is complete, burning the Lizardmen.

The Lizardmen were engulfed in flames of over 1400 degrees Celsius. They couldn't last long and scattered as ashes.

The only Lizardman who survived was the shaman. He was at the very back, so the Hellfire didn't reach him.

,You bastard! You bastard! You bastard!!! Even if I die, I'll take you with me! Enemy of our race!!"

The Lizardman shaman shed tears of blood as he cast a spell. A terrible curse spell that used his own life as a price. Judging by the ominousness of the spell, I'd be in considerable trouble if I got hit by that. Even if I didn't die immediately, my body would be weakened by the curse and I'd eventually die.

,Kkyaaaaaa!"

The shaman screamed in agony and died. Finally, the curse was completed and flew towards me.

,That's not a physical attack, so I wouldn't be able to block it even with a barrier.'

But that's okay. Because there's a much better way than blocking it.

Reflex Mirror.

I used the ability of the artifact in the form of a hand mirror. The last curse that the shaman used, sacrificing even his own life, was easily reflected as if it were futile. However, since the target to be reflected was already dead, the curse flew towards a nearby mercenary.

,Kuaaaaaa! Kuaaaaaaa!"

The mercenary who was hit by the curse rolled around on the floor with a terrible scream. Soon, his body melted away and disappeared.

A mercenary of at least 5th circle or higher had died without resisting the curse at all.

,A curse, huh. It's not magic, but sorcery, but the essence is the same. There are curses that are used as magic too. If I dig deeper, it could be quite useful.'

That's enough with the musings. I looked at the remaining mercenaries.

The 7th circle attack magic I can use right now are Thunder Break, Blizzard, and Hellfire. These three were all I had.

,It's hard to find magic of 7th circle or higher. Still, if I throw money at the Magic Association, I'll be able to learn a few more.'

Creating magic myself is also an option. It'll take a long time, though.

"......."

The mercenaries were silent.

The murderous intent and fighting spirit towards me had faded away. They silently dealt with the undead while watching my every move. Now their goal wasn't me, but survival.

I used magic to deal with the mercenaries one by one.

[Explosion]

I blew up one of the mercenaries with explosion magic.

[Telekinesis]

An invisible force twisted a mercenary's neck.

[Torrent]

Blades of water that surged from below shredded a mercenary's body.

,When I was in the 6th circle, spamming magic to this extent would have put a strain on my mana road and left me struggling.... Now, all I feel is a slight exhaustion.'

Perhaps unable to withstand the situation where one mercenary died with each spell, one of them raised both hands and shouted.

,Surrender! I surrender! I'll compensate you later for this! Please, just spare me!"

I responded with magic.

[Lightning Spear]

A spear of lightning pierced the torso of the surrendered mercenary. He burned to death.

,If you were going to surrender now, you shouldn't have started in the first place."

,...If your cruel deeds become known to the world, it will tarnish your reputation!"

,You're all going to die here. There's no chance it'll be known to the world, so don't worry and just die. Now, what magic should I use to kill you next? Do you have any requests?"

,You crazy wizard...!"

A mercenary with a red sword dyed his whole body red and charged at me. I didn't know if it was an ability or a drug, but he had momentarily enhanced his body.

However, it wasn't very threatening to me, who had become a 7th circle mage. The moment I focused my mind a little, thought acceleration occurred and his movements appeared slow.

[Spike]

Spikes that rose from the ground pierced the body of the charging mercenary. He twitched like a bug and died.

And a dagger stabbed in from behind. I used telekinesis to grab the dagger. The old butler, who had been aiming for a surprise attack, groaned.

,You knew?"

,I've been watching you the whole time. The power I feel from you is still not much."

,...I'm not very strong. It's also true that combat isn't my specialty. But killing people isn't always about fighting. ...It seems my skills are insufficient to assassinate a 7th circle mage after all."

,An assassin who failed at assassination must die."

I reached out my hand towards him. Wind gathered and rotated in my palm.

,Your cooking was quite to my taste. I'll grant you a special last wish."

,...A life where you never know when you'll die. I've already delivered my will to my lawyer."

,Thorough, aren't you. Now, die."

[Air Strike]

A powerful blast of wind shattered the old butler's body. The remains fell in a heap below the altar.

I mechanically killed the remaining mercenaries.

Smelling the vibrating stench of blood, I looked up. Unlike before, the dungeon had regained its stability.

,How long are you going to keep watching?"

The thing that had been looking down from the sky emitted a purple light and fell to the ground.

A bronze-colored body, a wooden mask on its face. It was Kukuru.

,Amazing. Among the wizards I've seen so far, your talent is overwhelming."

,I know. It's an unprecedented talent in this world. So, what happened to Kukuru?"

,You recognized right away that I'm not Kukuru?"

I narrowed my eyes and looked at him. The fact that Kukuru's body was reconstructed was already absurd, but what was more certain was the arcane flowing from his body. Kukuru, who wasn't good at handling arcane, suddenly appears and handles arcane? That's impossible.

,Hide the arcane flowing from your body before you speak."

,You can even sense arcane? Truly a talent to covet. My mouth keeps watering...."

,Answer the question. What happened to Kukuru?"

,That cocky necromancer? I devoured him, of course. My condition wasn't good, so it was a bit dangerous... but thanks to him, I was able to come to my senses like this and appear before you. In a way, he was quite helpful to me. Because I got the chance to swallow you."

,That's good."

,Hmm?"

,The mercenaries were boring... but it seems an opponent worthy of using my Unique Ability has appeared."

,Heh. A newborn babe who has barely begun to walk the path of transcendence sees me as a test subject?"

,Transcender. I know your condition isn't normal. Just a breathing corpse. That's enough for me to give it a try."

,You little pup.... Have you forgotten that this place where you are is my stomach? Arcane Devour."

The moment the Transcender chanted, the world turned purple. Arcane was everywhere. Also, the control of mana weakened and the mana around me scattered. At this rate, it would take more time and effort than usual to construct magic.

,It's like the whole world has turned against me.'

,This is my stomach. Everything here except you is mine. Mana, arcane... even fate can be manipulated by me. Now, even the talent you possess will become mine. Be digested quietly. I'll make good use of your talent."

,If this is the best you can do right now... then I've won."

Unique Ability: Absoluteness.

Everything that tried to interfere with me disappeared.

The scattered mana was drawn to me and gathered. As the concentration increased, the mana naturally shone like blue starlight. The starlight spun around me.

The Transcender's eyes behind the wooden mask widened. He waved his hand as if he was flustered. The arcane moved. He was doing something to me, but I was fine.

,That's... your Unique Ability? A Unique Ability that doesn't even allow interference with fate...! No, even the fate I've seized is moving around you...! Are you saying you're the protagonist of this world!"

,The protagonist has to be special. In that sense... I guess I'm the protagonist."

I put my will into the starlight rotating around me. The starlight soon manifested as magic.

[Thunder Break]

A huge pillar of blue lightning fell on him. The guy who was hit by Thunder Break head-on staggered but didn't fall.

As expected of a Transcender.

Even if he's not in a normal state, he won't die from just one Thunder Break.

,But the outcome has been decided."

He lost his concentration. The arcane he had seized was released. And naturally, it gathered to me and spun around. The blue starlight of mana and the purple starlight of arcane sparkled dazzlingly.

,The arcane is overflowing. If it's now... it's possible."

[Thunder Break]

Not just one Thunder Break. I used arcane and mana at the same time to create dozens, hundreds of Thunder Breaks. An absurd miracle possible because it was manifested directly as will, not through the mana road.

Blue lightning and purple lightning dyed this world with lightning and began to tear it apart.

A scene like the end of the world unfolded.

-Kuaaaaaaa!

The Transcender's scream echoed in the world.

Even so, there was no chance of killing the Transcender. Even if he was a breathing corpse, a Transcender is a Transcender. He's not an opponent I can kill right now.

-You...! I'll remember this!!

The Transcender's intense will swept around.

I was forcibly expelled and appeared in reality.

The mercenaries who had been encamped at the dungeon entrance aimed their guns at me and pulled the trigger. But I was still using my Unique Ability.

[Telekinesis]

I used telekinesis to grab the pouring bullets and fix them in the air.

,Just die."

[Chain Lightning]

It only took 3 seconds for the 5 lightning bolts that started from my fingertips to take the lives of 30 mercenaries.
