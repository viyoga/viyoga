The Game Life app has rarely disappointed me.

However, the Game Life app's game world search system was quite disappointing.

[I Picked Up the One Ring. (Novel)]

[Lord of the Rings in Another World. (Manga)]

[Ring of Death. (Movie)]

[Hypnosis Ring. (AV)]

.......

Works related to rings popped up one after another. The common thing was that the title contained the word 'ring.'

,The One Ring? That could ruin the entire world. Even if its effects are weakened in reality, it's not the kind of thing you'd give as a gift.'

Putting aside whether the recipient would feel burdened, I'm worried about what kind of negative impact it would have on reality. In the worst case, the world could be destroyed.

,The other rings are the same.'

The Ring of Wishes is better in terms of effect. If I could, I'd like to give the Ring of Wishes as a gift.

But I don't have the points. Even if I did, it's right to avoid the Ring of Wishes.

,I already gave the Ring of Wishes to Han Ha-rin. Even if the effects are different, the design is the same. Give Han Ah-young a ring with the same design too?'

I can see the result without thinking deeply. Han Ha-rin and Han Ah-young. Both of them would feel bad.

Besides, isn't what's important in a gift the thought behind it? Even if the ring's effect is weak, the message that 'I prepared this ring just for you' is more important.

,Should I go to the Baekhwan World or the God's Atlantis World to make a ring? But it might take more time than I thought... And making a ring with the effect I want isn't easy.'

The search function of the Game Life app isn't reliable at all. I started looking directly at the list of game worlds and choosing one.

My finger, which had been moving and scrolling through the list, stopped.

[Dragon Hunter. (Manga)]

It's a manga by a Korean author, and it's quite old, being 17 years old. A manga that was completed in 32 volumes. It was quite popular back then.

The content is what you'd call a typical shonen manga. It's a manga about the protagonist becoming an S-class Dragon Hunter.

,From today's perspective, the story development is a bit frustrating.'

For modern people who are used to shorts, it doesn't have a refreshing taste. But it's still an interesting manga.

,The design of the protagonist or heroine is a bit... not great. Well, that's not what's important.'

What I'm focusing on is not the protagonist or heroine of the [Dragon Hunter] world, but the setting. And according to that setting, I can make the ring I want relatively quickly.

,While using my powers as well.'

There's no need to think too deeply. If I don't think it's for me, I can quickly watch the ending on auto-play and finish it, then choose another game world.

It's called a great aristocratic family, but according to the information in the original work, it wouldn't be strange to see it as a kingdom. If I start in the Dong family, I can get a lot.'

My purpose in this game isn't to live a life of luxury.

,I'll go with number 2, which has no stat restrictions.'

Dropped in the middle of the road, the first thing I did was examine my body.

Nothing had changed from my real body. The clothes I was wearing were still the same black tracksuit.

,A stranger who appeared from somewhere, huh? It's my real body.'

Pajijijik.

Blue electricity sparked between my fingers and disappeared. My abilities are also fine.

,Okay, then I need to make a base first, right?'

I took out a compass. I could use this to find the directions of east, west, south, and north.

"...Shibal."

Even if I know the directions, I don't know which way to go to find a city or town. The compass instantly became trash.

,I know the information about the original manga, but I don't have any information about this world itself.'

That's because I wasn't born with a setting in this world.

I am a stranger. It's no different from being transferred from reality to another world.

,Well, whatever. If I move around, I'll find a city.'

The inventory works.

I can take out means of transportation such as helicopters, drones, and bikes that I made in the [Eternal Eden] world. From these, I took out a bike.

,It's a bike modified for combat. It doesn't matter if the road is a mess.'

I felt like speeding.

I started riding the bike and speeding off. The sound was quiet. It was a characteristic of the electric motor engine.

I was starting to get tired of speeding while feeling the cold air. I spotted a carriage and horsemen approaching from the front.

,Looking at the cart behind them, it seems like a merchant group.'

The horsemen also looked like mercenaries. If they were soldiers, their equipment would be uniform, but it wasn't. The atmosphere I felt was also unique to mercenaries.

,They're being chased by something.'

It was a bipedal dinosaur. Its size was 2M, and its appearance resembled a Velociraptor. However, scales that looked like iron armor sparkled in the sunlight.

,Ah. I've definitely seen those guys in the comic book. What do they call them in this world?'

I was thinking leisurely like that when a mercenary riding a horse shouted at me.

"Turn around and run away! Seven Iron Horse Dragons are chasing us! Don't stop, run away!"

"Iron Horse Dragon. That's right. That was the name."

I got off the bike and put it in my inventory. The modified bike was something that Na Chae-young had personally modified, so it was quite rare even in the [Eternal Eden] world.

,I can roughly figure out how strong I am. And I can get information from those bastards.'

"Didn't you hear me telling you to run away? Do you want to die?!"

Ignoring the shouting mercenary, I clenched my fist.

The horse and carriage passed by me.

The Iron Horse Dragons chasing the merchant group changed their target and rushed at me. That's because their goal was to eat humans in the first place. They're probably planning to easily eat me, the human in front of them, and then chase after the carriage.

I threw an uppercut at the Iron Horse Dragon rushing at me from the front.

"S-class Punch."

Kwaang!

It hit the Iron Horse Dragon's jaw accurately, and a cannon-like sound exploded. Its massive body flew about 5m and fell to the ground. Its head was grotesquely smashed.

"Kieeeeeek!"

Even though they saw their comrade die next to them, they rushed in without hesitation.

,They're not fast. I don't even need to use acceleration. If I were to compare them to monsters in reality, they'd be around D-class.'

I quickly dealt with the 4 that were rushing at me with just my fists and legs. The remaining 2 hesitated as if they had finally come to their senses, then turned around and ran away. Their speed was similar to that of a running horse.

"Die here."

I chased after them and smashed their heads.

The merchant group that had been running away grasped the situation and slowly approached this way. A middle-aged man in wealthy clothes got out of the carriage.

"Thank you for helping us! I am Oswald, a merchant from the Flack Merchant Group! By any chance, are you a Dragon Hunter, 귀인-nim?"

"I'm Seong Yujin. An S-class Hunter... in training."

Oswald was slightly flustered, but soon smiled.

"You have the skills to hunt Iron Horse Dragons with your bare hands, yet you're in training. You must have an important story. Hunter-nim. If it's okay with you, we would like to purchase the Iron Horse Dragons that you hunted. We will calculate it at a higher price than the market price. Of course, we will also reward you for saving us."

Oswald began to be overly cautious. His voice was also slightly trembling. To him, I probably look like a war machine that can destroy a village.

"The reward is unnecessary. I'll sell you the Iron Horse Dragon corpses too. Instead, I'll accompany you until we reach the nearest city."

"...Since Hunter-nim is accompanying us, our merchant group will be safe. I am grateful. We need to organize the byproducts of the Iron Horse Dragons, so please take a moment. As you know, the Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood must be collected as soon as the dragon is killed to prevent them from spoiling. Ah, if you need the Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood, I will give them to you."

Listening to Oswald's words, the settings of [Dragon Hunter] began to come to mind one by one.

"Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood. Give me one of each."

"Yes. Hunter-nim. What are you all doing? Move quickly."

At Oswald's words, the mercenaries and employees moved. They took out butchering knives and separated the flesh, bones, and skin of the Iron Horse Dragons. Most of the internal organs were thrown away, but the heart was carefully dissected by an expert.

As I was watching with interest, Oswald carefully spoke to me.

"By any chance, is this your first time hunting Iron Horse Dragons, Hunter-nim?"

"It's my first time. You figured it out."

"I asked because you seem to be from a distant land. Would it be okay if I explained about the Iron Horse Dragon?"

"Go ahead."

"Iron Horse Dragons are commonly seen in the Central Continent, but their internal organs are useless, but their flesh is savory and sells well. The most expensive thing besides the Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood is the skin. The skin of the Iron Horse Dragon is stronger than steel, so it is also used as a material for Hunter equipment."

Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood.

It is the core setting of [Dragon Hunter].

If you eat the Dragon Heart, you can gain the dragon's abilities. You don't get it unconditionally. The body must have compatibility with the Dragon Heart. How do you know if you have compatibility? You can tell instinctively. The instinct is drawn to the Dragon Heart.

Dragon Heart Blood was the blood pooled in the Dragon Heart. It had some of the Dragon Heart's abilities, but you couldn't gain the dragon's abilities even if you drank it. Instead, if you drink it, your physical abilities and magic power will permanently increase even if you don't have compatibility. The effect is much better if you have compatibility.

However, it has little effect on those stronger than a certain level. It's only a tonic for ordinary people who haven't awakened the dragon's abilities. Dragon Heart Blood can be seen as a kind of elixir.

I stared intently at the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood that the worker handed me.

I didn't feel anything.

"What abilities can you get if you eat the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart?"

"You can gain the ability to turn your skin into steel. And if you're very lucky, you can also gain the ability to manipulate iron."

"What about Dragon Heart Blood?"

"Dragon Heart Blood mostly depends on the grade. Even though the Iron Horse Dragon is the lowest class, it's quite expensive compared to other Dragon Heart Blood because it's a material for recovery potions."

"The dragon's grade, huh? Can you tell me about it?"

"Yes. Yes. Of course."

Oswald answered without asking or questioning.

According to him, the dragon's grade is determined by the Dragon Hunter Association and divided into 4 categories.

Baby Dragon (兒龍) - Adult Dragon (成龍) - Ancient Dragon (高龍) - Dragon King (龍王).

"There's no explanation for Dragon God."

In my memory, the final boss of the original manga was a Dragon God class.

"I know that Dragon God is a being that only appears in legends...."

"Ah, that's right. I was joking."

It was a manga I saw a long time ago, so I didn't remember it well.

I thought about eating the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart, but I didn't feel anything, so I threw it into my inventory. It didn't seem like it would taste good either. Instead, I took a sip of the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart Blood.

,Damn it. It's just fishy and I don't feel anything. Could compatibility be referring to talent?'

I hoped not.
