I approached the corpse of the black wizard.

The black wizard's corpse, stabbed with thousands of fragments, resembled a hedgehog. Of course, that absolutely doesn't mean it was as cute as a hedgehog.

I looked around once. I have to take this corpse and show it to Baron Ptelgar. Just thinking about it is a pain in the ass.

,It's something that could be done by ordering someone else...,'

Suddenly, I realize that this is the first time I've killed a black wizard.

I've faced wizards a few times. I've received missions and fought them on the battlefield.

,Back then, I killed them on the battlefield. There's never been a case like now where I chased a black wizard and killed them in their abode.'

A wizard's abode is often called a workshop.

This place, filled with waste, is also clearly a black wizard's workshop. If I rummage around, something might come out. Feeling an intense curiosity, I decided to report it later.

My heart started pounding.

I was currently hiding my actual level with Transparency magic.

Superficially, I'm a 3rd class Battle Mage. I possess 25 mana rods. This alone can be considered excellent, but my actual level is 4th class, and I have 33 mana rods.

I reached 4th class half a year ago, and there has been no progress since then. I can be said to be in the early stages of 4th class. It couldn't be helped. My current living environment was not suitable for increasing my magical level. There was a severe lack of materials and information about magic, and I didn't have a workshop for personal research.

But I don't get impatient.

It's natural to experience stagnation as your level increases. How quickly and effectively you break through this stagnation period depends on the wizard's ability, talent, and luck.

,Even though I'm stuck in a place like this, it's still a 3rd class black wizard. There must be something here.'

I put on gloves, took out a knife, and rummaged through the black wizard's corpse.

It felt strangely nonchalant to be touching a corpse. I think I was a bit surprised at first. Now, I had no feelings at all.

,There are too many fragments stuck in it, which is annoying. I should have killed him more cleanly.'

I roughly pulled out the fragments stuck in the corpse and checked its pockets. Something came out of the pocket.

,...Drugs? Is it a reagent? It doesn't seem usable since it's soaked in blood.'

Besides that, things like a ballpoint pen and a stone fragment came out. The stone fragment seems to be one of the magic materials... but it didn't pique my interest.

,Hmm?'

There was a tattoo on his left shoulder. It wasn't clearly visible because of the blood, but it was definitely a symbol I knew.

I pulled out the fragments and wiped off the blood with a dirty cloth. The symbol was clearly visible. Three circles. A picture of a circle inside a circle.

,The symbol of Resistance Echoes.... Was this bastard a member of the Resistance?'

Resistance Echoes is a force that resists the class society. It is also called the Revolutionary Army. Resistance Echoes exists all over the world and mainly terrorizes countries with class systems.

Aristocrats and royalty are their arch-enemies. They believe that an equal world can be created only when the class system disappears.

,In my opinion, they're just bastards who are pissed off because they can't have it.'

Well, I didn't care what they did. As long as it doesn't concern me. And what's important now is not Resistance Echoes.

,Should I do it, or not.'

I hold the knife and contemplate.

Unlike ordinary wizards, black wizards have an organ called a 'Dark Hole'. It is a special organ that converts and stores mana into dark mana.

The ways to obtain a Dark Hole include organ transplantation, organ creation through special rituals... etc.

,In the original game, you could get a Dark Hole by killing a black wizard. It sold for quite a high price.'

If a mana awakener transplants a Dark Hole, they can use dark mana even if they are not a wizard. Then, as they gain experience and learn black magic, they become a black wizard.

,...I'll do it. It's stupid not to extract the Dark Hole after killing a black wizard. And I'm also interested in the Dark Hole.'

A special organ that converts mana into dark mana.

There will hardly be another chance to get a Dark Hole if not now.

I gripped the knife tightly and cut open the black wizard's stomach. The location of the Dark Hole differs for each black wizard. It could be under the heart, or it could be attached to the side of the kidney.

Fortunately, the Dark Hole of this black wizard was intact without any damage, thanks to being located between the small intestines. I extract the Dark Hole. Its size is about 3cm in diameter, and it looks like a black bead. I can feel dark mana from the Dark Hole.

,The Dark Hole loses its ability to convert mana into dark mana the moment it is extracted. Instead, the dark mana conversion ability can be used again if the organ is transplanted again.'

The extracted Dark Hole has the ability to store dark mana. It becomes a kind of dark mana battery.

,I have no intention of transplanting the Dark Hole. If I did, the purity of my mana would decrease, and it would not be easy to draw out the power of elemental magic.'

I looked around at the Dark Hole. I feel like I can use black magic using dark mana in this state. Of course, it's impossible right now because I don't know a single incantation of black magic.

I took out a handkerchief from my pocket and wrapped the Dark Hole. I wouldn't do such a sloppy job if it were an ordinary organ, but the Dark Hole is a special organ.

,Next is corpse disposal. I can't let them know that I stole the Dark Hole.... I have to damage it.'

Baron Ptelgar is a cunning bastard. He will definitely suspect that I stole the Dark Hole.

,Let him suspect. As long as there's no conclusive evidence.'

I draw out the slightly recovered mana and use magic. I damage the black wizard's corpse with wind magic. It wasn't a very pleasant sight.

,This is not enough. I'll have to burn the corpse additionally when my mana recovers.'

I averted my gaze from the black wizard's corpse and rummaged around. Even if it's a shabby place, it's still a black wizard's abode. There must be something.

,Found it.'

I found it after rummaging for almost 40 minutes.

One was a magic book. A magic book containing research on manifesting the 3rd class black magic, Living Bomb, as a tattoo.

,It seems he researched it himself. He was trying to sublimate the Living Bomb into arcane magic using tattoos.'

I didn't care why he was obsessed with Living Bomb, but it was quite innovative.

,Hmm. The tattoo Living Bomb was successful. He was conducting research to increase the power of the Living Bomb.'

The other thing I found was a cage made of reinforced glass. There was a rat inside the cage. It was a white rat, and a Living Bomb magic circle was drawn on its body.

,As the black wizard died, the Living Bomb magic circle became a simple tattoo with dark mana volatilized. It doesn't explode. This is just an ordinary rat with a magic circle tattoo.'

There are several types of magic circles. Typically, there are activation type and continuous type. The Living Bomb magic circle is an activation type.

I picked up the magic book. It was a book close to a research paper. That's why it was even more helpful to me.

,It's a shame that the only magic written is Living Bomb black magic. But it's still helpful.'

The incantation of black magic itself was nothing special. It feels like the incantation is a bit twisted, but it's not difficult to understand.

There is one thing special about black magic incantations. It does not react to ordinary mana and only reacts to dark mana.

,...If I can only handle dark mana, I can also use black magic.'

After rereading the magic book, I cast Fire magic and burned it. And I hold the Dark Hole in one hand and glare at it.

,Let's move the dark mana of the Dark Hole. In a way that dominates mana....'

The dark mana of the Dark Hole doesn't budge.

,...Is this not it.'

I narrowed my eyes. After trying several other methods, I opened the wizard's spirit, the Astral.

I recognize the Dark Hole with the Astral. I felt like the Dark Hole placed on the palm of my hand had become a part of me. I can feel dark mana.

There is a problem here. Mana must go through a processing process to become magic. It is to change mana into an incantation through a mana rod.

,Since the Dark Hole is outside, I can't use my mana rod. But....'

There is no rule that the process of converting mana into an incantation must be done only with a mana rod.

,There's a perfect one right in front of me.'

The magic circle drawn on the body of the rat trapped in the glass cage.

Dark mana from the Dark Hole moves and permeates the rat. The magic circle is activated. It's not just about injecting dark mana. It is activated because I fully understand the magic circle.

,I succeeded.'

I can feel that I am connected to the Living Bomb magic circle through the Dark Hole.

[Barrier]

First, I wrapped my body with a barrier.

,Explode.'

Kwaang!

The rat explodes. The glass cage shattered, and the shockwave shook the barrier. Soon, the explosion disappeared. Pieces of rat blood and entrails were scattered on the floor.

"Ha, hahahahahaha!"

Laughter burst out.

Wizards cannot use black magic. That was common sense in this world.

But just now, that common sense was broken. Ridiculously easily.

,To think that my Astral would dominate and use the Dark Hole without even transplanting it.... Is it possible for other wizards too? ...No. It's impossible. If it were possible, it would have already been known. Even in the original work, it was stated that wizards cannot use black magic.'

I chuckled and manipulated my watch to send a message to 212.

[Mission complete.]

"The battle was intense."

"Absolutely not."
