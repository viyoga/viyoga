Back in reality, I picked up my smartphone. Reflexively, I tried to check the post-ending world before realizing it was impossible since I was dead.

[Spend 3,000 points to view the post-ending world?]

Just like in the 'Paladin' world, I could pay points to see the world after my death.

'Is this world worth spending 3,000 points to check?'

I had zero lingering attachment to the 'Ground Zero' world. I'd taken what I could take, enjoyed what I could enjoy.

'The time bomb I planted in Last Ark should have gone off properly, right? That part I'm a bit curious about. Even if the bomb didn't detonate, with the shelter wrecked, they'd last a few days at most.'

I wanted to see it blown to smithereens. For my own amusement, I spent the 3,000 points.

[Viewing the post-ending world.]

I saw Vera sitting beside my corpse. My body was in a gruesome state. Looked like she'd stabbed the corpse dozens of times even after I was dead. My blood was smeared all over Vera's purple skin.

The door opened and Daren walked in. He saw the state Vera was in and let out a small sigh.

"Vera. There's no need to feel guilt. He was a bastard who deserved to die. He never saw us as human. He was a madman who saw this world as a game. Last Ark has suffered irreparable damage from this battle. Humanity is finished."

"...That bastard said 'log out' as he died."

"The man was always a lunatic. Pay it no mind."

"...That bastard's power is an ability, but it's not an ability. He called it a game's inventory? He kept pulling strange objects in and out of that unknowable space."

"That too is a type of ability. Must be related to a teleportation power."

"I knew the Market made a deal with Origin and became a mutant. He knew Last Ark's location even though I never told him, and he saw through Last Ark's entire structure and information without anyone saying a word."

"That's.... perhaps a form of precognition ability."

"You know as well as I do that no such ability exists. More than anything, does it make sense that Origin could summon something inside Last Ark?! Even if I want to deny it, that bastard shoved the evidence right in my face! This world was fake! The world ending from World War III! The world covered in radiation! Even Last Ark, humanity's last hope! It was all just a setting to entertain that bastard, the player! You, me—we're nothing but NPCs, less than a single ant crawling in that bastard's real world!"

"Vera! The man is dead! Even if this is a game world, he's already dead!"

"That bastard might be somewhere watching us, snickering. And if this is a game.... there could be a sequel. I don't want that. If my life, my emotions, are nothing but programming someone else wrote... I'd rather die."

Thk.

Vera drove the same knife that killed me into her own heart and committed suicide.

Daren reached a powerless hand toward Vera, then lowered it.

"Even if this world is inside a game, even if we're nothing but NPCs... what can we do. We can only live on, praying for God's mercy.... Even if that god is evil. I will live to the very end."

Daren went outside and tried to salvage the situation somehow. He gave a baseless speech claiming that with Origin dead, the mutants under his control would calm down.

And three hours later. The time bomb I'd secretly planted went off.

KWAAAAAANG!

Several key facilities of Last Ark were obliterated. Among them was the air conditioning system. The device that purified radiation-laced air was destroyed. Radiation flooded in.

There wasn't enough radiation neutralizer for thousands of people. Most of humanity died, and some became mutants.

Daren led a handful of humans to find the ancient facility. To endure through time using the cryogenic pods installed in the ancient facility, the nuclear bunker. Because in the far future, the Earth might be purified.

However.

[Authentication denied.]

They couldn't use the cryogenic pods because they weren't authenticated as human by the ancient facility. Several hackers tried, but the result was the same. The security related to the cryogenic pods was the highest level even within the ancient facility.

"Haha. So this is how it ends. Isn't this too cruel, God. God. Please, I hope you suffer too."

Humanity went extinct.

Yet the world of amusement did not end.

Time kept flowing. I kept the speed-up going.

The radiation was not purified. But the world adapted to the radiation.

A species that uses tools appeared.

Evolved from reptiles, they developed over time and built a brilliant civilization.

At that point I lost interest and stopped the auto-play. Instead, I checked what I'd gained this time.

What I earned as quest rewards this time.

[First Night Rights

Spend the first night with a target.

The target must be a virgin. The target naturally accepts the rights of First Night Rights in their own way.

Price: 1,000,000 points

[It's Time to Go to Bed

Look at the target and ring the bell, and the target falls into a very deep sleep.

Price: 3,000 points

The target can resist.

Oh my.]

I wanted to try First Night Rights right away. I was extremely curious how a target affected by First Night Rights would turn out.

'It's too wasteful to use it recklessly. A million points isn't chump change.'

[Available points: 43,159]

Checking my current points, the million points suddenly felt much bigger.

I decided to store it for now and checked what I'd earned as achievement rewards. The one that interested me most was the 'Single-Use Common Sense Modifier.'

[Single-Use Common Sense Modifier

Can be used at the start of amusement.

Can add or modify common sense that applies to the world of amusement.

Price: 1,000,000 points

※Warning

May conflict with the world of amusement's settings and produce unexpected results.]

'It's used at the start of amusement? Shame it can't be used in reality or in existing amusement worlds. Could I add common sense like "all women must spread their legs for me"?

Probably possible.

Looking at other creative works, even more absurd common sense modifications were possible.

[Great Destruction

Perish together with a designated target.

When Great Destruction is used, the amusement immediately ends. The destroyed amusement world cannot be re-entered by any means other than reset.

Inanimate objects or concepts can also be targets of destruction.

Price: 3,000,000 points

※Warning

Cannot be used in reality.]

"Three million points?"

More expensive than the Single-Use Common Sense Modifier.

'Is this worth that much?'

Only after reading the description once more was I surprised that inanimate objects or concepts could also be destroyed.

'Is there no limit to the range of inanimate objects or concepts? Like Earth or the universe. Could concepts like time or death be destroyed?'

I don't quite understand. If the concept of time disappears, would the world stop moving forever? If death disappears, does that mean you can live forever?

'But an ordinary body can't even last 100 years. Does it mean you don't die even when your body turns to dust? That's just another hell.'

I decided to hold onto it for now. No immediate use came to mind, and I figured I'd use it later if there was an amusement world I didn't like.

'But once used, I can't enter until I reset. Might never have a chance to use it.'

I decided to rest for today and enter the amusement world tomorrow.

[Selected: Through Infinite Subspace to the Top of the Tower.]

[Starting amusement.]

Having entered this world, I rummaged through my memories for a moment.

'Through Infinite Subspace to the Top of the Tower,' abbreviated 'Mugong Tower,' is quite a bleak world despite its title.

A tower appeared in the capital of a world nation, and when the tower collapses, the nation is annihilated. Its citizens and their assets turn to dust and vanish.

The condition for the tower's collapse is coins. Every 24 hours, coins equal to the nation's population evaporate. Hunters enter the tower, hunt monsters, and earn coins to extend the nation's survival.

A hunter's value was higher in this world than in reality. Without hunters earning coins, the nation itself would be annihilated. For reference, no nation in this world could survive more than a year. Even the United States only has 240 days. That's counting only official coins the nation tallies. Including unofficial coins, they could survive longer.

'Right now, Korea can last about 150 days or so?'

I checked this world's status window.

『Name: Sung Yujin

Age: 21

Affiliated Dimension: Earth

Affiliated Nation: Korea

Level: 13

Coins: 16,771,769

Innate Ability: Character Summon (Lv.2)

*Rank 1 buff: All abilities +9%.

*Rank 1 buff: Korea coin acquisition rate +20%.

*Summon maintenance cost: -120,000 coins per day

*Attack Detector: -50,000 coins per day』

I checked this world's status window.

I couldn't know the exact numerical values of my stats, but I could feel my physical abilities strengthening as my level rose. For reference, level equals the number of floors climbed in the tower.

'Level 13 would be an E-rank hunter by this world's standards.'

The threshold for S-rank hunter is the 61st floor.

Floor challenges can only be attempted once per day, so becoming an S-rank hunter required investing over a month of time.

I looked around.

Wiyu was sitting on the sofa, indifferently watching TV. The TV drama was a makjang drama.

A little ways off, Yuria and Elena were sitting at a table playing chess. Elena's brow was deeply furrowed as she studied the board. Yuria's expression, by contrast, was serene.

"Yuria. Can't you let me take back just one move?"

"That's the third request already. Not this time. It's about time I started preparing dinner."

"I'll help. Can't I just solve it with my illusion magic?"

"There's no care put into Elena's illusions."

"...It costs my lifespan, you know?"

"I'd rather not feed Elena's lifespan into Master's mouth. Where's the chess player gone?"

Even just eavesdropping a little, I could tell who was winning.

"I'm going to challenge the 14th floor. Anyone want to come?"

"Me!"

Elena shot up from her seat as if she'd been waiting. Her body bumped the table and the pieces on the chessboard toppled.

A shadow rippled over the chessboard and in an instant rearranged the fallen pieces back to their original positions. Yuria rose calmly.

"I'll come as well. Assisting Master is a maid's duty, after all."

I asked Wiyu.

"Master, aren't you coming?"

"Is this something that requires my power?"

"Not exactly."

It's only the 14th floor. Forget Wiyu—even without Yuria and Elena, I alone was more than enough. Still, if I wanted an overwhelming clear, having their help was better.

"Then that's settled. This is an important scene, so don't disturb me."

"Yes. Then we'll be off."

That's when it happened. I felt the sensation of space warping behind my back. It was similar to air being forcibly pushed aside, producing a faint wind.

"Your Excellency."

Lee Yeonhee walked out from within that space. Wearing a modified hanbok, she wore her usual smile that gave nothing away.

"Might I ask you to postpone the tower climb for a moment."

"Did you plant a wiretap in my house or something?"

"I would never. Even if I did plant a wiretap, wouldn't Your Excellency notice immediately? I merely sensed that Your Excellency had entered this world and came to find you."

"You can sense that?"

"To be precise, it feels like my senses suddenly float up and then sink. Since I deal with space, I was able to react sensitively."

"Well, it is something like that."

Elena nodded and agreed.

"Got it. What's the reason to postpone the tower climb?"

"Currently Korea is doing too well. If it were the United States doing well, other nations would accept it. Because it's the United States. But if it's Korea...."

"They'll be jealous. They'll find it galling. Especially nearby nations—high chance they'll kick up a fuss."

"Yes. Even in the United States, a considerable number of voices of complaint are rising."

It was only natural—the United States had lost no small amount of ranking. A 5% increase in coin acquisition rate is quite significant. Especially when the nation's survival hinges on those coins.

"I'm confident I can handle it, though."

"Making the world your enemy could cause problems for Your Excellency's comfortable lifestyle. Of course, Your Excellency wouldn't mind, but the impact on Korea's hunters would inevitably be large. In the worst case, Korea's hunters would leave Korea, and Korea's coin supply would drop."

No matter how well I do, it's impossible for me alone to earn fifty million coins a day. Even if I could, I'd have to work like a dog without rest.

'Annoying. Just abandoning Korea and working like a dog is... ah. Can't do that because of Lee Yeonhee.'

To conquer Lee Yeonhee, I had to take care of Korea.

"You have a plan, I assume?"

"Of course, Your Excellency. It won't even take a week."
