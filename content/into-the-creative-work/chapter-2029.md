The wedding went smoothly.

There wasn't really anything I did. I just looked through catalog books with Jeong Suyeon at home and decided on the things needed for the wedding. Then the servants took care of the preparations. The only thing I really cared about and acted on was when Jeong Suyeon was choosing her wedding dress.

The wedding took place at the best hotel in Korea. Jeong Suyeon and I didn't have many family, relatives, or friends to invite to the wedding, but guests flocked in from all over the world.

It was because of the influence of the Cheonil Group and my reputation as a Guardian recognized by the Angel. Thanks to that, I was able to meet high-ranking officials from various countries. Honestly, I didn't need to meet them.

The Chairman of the Cheonil Group was having a field day. He had succeeded in witch-hunting his annoying competitors in this Yeonggwanghoe incident. Because it was done in the name of the Angel, there was no backlash at all, even though he openly massacred them. That's how immense the faith in the Angel was.

Not all the competitors were dead, but the other competitors bowed their heads to the Cheonil Group because they didn't want to be witch-hunted. As long as I, with the Angel as my backer, am here, the Cheonil Group will continue to prosper.

The Angel. That bastard wasn't very interested in what was happening among humans. What he was interested in was the battle with alien monsters.

The Angel officiated the wedding according to my request, and that sacred scene was spread through the news. The Cheonil Group's and my reputation became enormous, as a bonus.

「The invasion is not far off! Prepare for the invasion war!」

When I was heading to the airport for my honeymoon after the wedding, the Angel popped up a notification window. Jeong Suyeon, who had her hand on her lower abdomen, didn't seem to see the notification window.

,What the hell, the honeymoon hasn't even started yet. But I should prepare.'

Invading another world alone. No matter how much it's me, it's bound to be a burden, but... there was a way in this world to make a person strong in an instant.

,The most representative of those is items.'

The Angel's recognition and the Cheonil Group's money.

With those, I'd be able to get almost anything. If I requested cooperation from other countries in the name of the Angel, they'd give me rare items.

,The first thing I need to get is a subspace item. Next is weapons....'

The invasion the Angel is talking about is me becoming a monster from their perspective. I was a little excited about the upcoming battle.

The original honeymoon plan was for fifteen days. The plan was to spend a week in the Maldives on a private jet and the remaining week in Europe. But life doesn't always go according to plan.

The time spent in the Maldives increased a little, and the fifteen-day honeymoon was extended to twenty days. It was because Jeong Suyeon liked the Maldives.

Dawn in a suite at a hotel in the Maldives.

I could see Jeong Suyeon standing naked by the window. She was watching the sun slowly rising over the sea.

I admired her from the bed. Her body, bathed in the rising sunlight, was beautiful. I was quietly admiring her body when she suddenly turned around. She walks confidently and gets into bed.

I hugged her body and closed my eyes, falling asleep again.

Ppuuuuuuuuuuuuuu!!

The Angel blew a horn. The horn sound announcing the war was only heard by me.

「Warrior, prepare for the invasion!」

「Human counterattack!

Humans have always been invaded. Because we lacked strength, all we could do was endure.

We endured and endured and built up strength.

Now, it's time for a counterattack. Warrior, prepare to invade. Kill all the Wolta tribe who prey on humans!

Waiting time: 30:00」

I called the servants and ordered them to bring the things I had prepared.

I put on a few items and checked the subspace pouch containing hundreds of items. I touch the Hwaryeon Bido with my hand. The preparation was finished in less than 3 minutes.

I put a drug cigarette in my mouth. The drug energy circulates throughout my body, increasing my concentration.

「The Angel's blessing is upon you.

The Angel wants victory!」

Of course, I want victory too. Defeat here meant death, and death meant quest failure.

The Angel's blessing was a kind of buff. A pretty awesome buff at that. Just in terms of physical ability alone, I became more than 3 times stronger. The amount of Force is the same. I could tell how serious the Angel was this time.

「Waiting time: 18:40」

「Waiting time: 11:07」

「Waiting time: 03:21」

The moment the waiting time became 0, my body appeared in a place with gray land. A notification window popped up in front of me.

「Survive for 5 hours or kill 500 Wolta tribe members and invade the Wolta tribe world.」

,500 of them, huh.'

There's no need to do anything separately. I can feel intense murderous intent pouring in from all directions. The Wolta tribe members flying in the sky with their wings are raising their Force.

"Nice to meet you."

I greeted them lightly. There was no reply. It's because they can't speak human language. The Wolta tribe, being half-god and half-machine, communicate with each other through communication, not vocally.

I put on the item Steel Wings. Steel wings spread wide behind my back. As soon as I rose into the sky, the Wolta tribe members opened their beaks and fired beams. They are gathering Force and sending it flying.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕) Maximum Output.

An electromagnetic barrier made of flashing lightning blocked the beams. But their attacks don't stop. They continue to attack without stopping among themselves.

,They fight like shit.'

The electromagnetic barrier cannot be maintained indefinitely. At this rate, I'll be the first to get tired in less than 15 minutes. I have to counterattack.

I spread my palm. Lightning flashed and gathered in the center of my palm. I compressed the lightning and soon detonated it. An EMP exploded and spread in all directions, sweeping through the Wolta tribe.

"Keukeukkeukkeukkeu."

"Kkieeeeeeeek."

The Wolta tribe members scream and fall. The Wolta tribe, with half of their bodies being machines, were particularly vulnerable to EMP. But I didn't knock them all down. Those who protected their bodies with Force didn't fall and endured.

Before they could use items, I took out an item from the subspace pouch first.

A small metal arrow, not even a hand span long.

Arrow of Tracking. An arrow that automatically chases the enemy for 30 minutes. The only weakness of this arrow, its durability, was overcome by strengthening it with other items on Earth.

I threw the Arrow of Tracking into the air. The arrow spun around in the air and flew towards a nearby Wolta tribe member. It instantly pierced the Wolta tribe member's head and aims for the lives of other Wolta tribe members.

I didn't entrust everything to the arrow. I also flew in the sky and swung the Hwaryeon Bido, killing the Wolta tribe members.

,EMP Shock!'

I occasionally detonated EMPs. It was their weakness, so I couldn't help but use it. The bastards had to surround their bodies with Force to prepare for the EMP while fighting me. I knew well how annoying and concentration-consuming that was.

Seogeokseogeokseogeok!

Blood splattered every time I cut them down. The red blood falling from the sky to the ground was quite beautiful.

,But these bastards are terribly bad at close combat.'

The Wolta tribe had hands and arms similar to humans, but they had no lower body. Instead, they had wings, but they had to keep moving their wings to fly in the sky. The close combat that could be done with two arms without a lower body was limited.

,Martial arts didn't develop in these bastards' world.'

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Noeseom (雷閃).

I send out Sword energy wrapped in lightning. The Wolta tribe members were startled and dodged to the side. Even without a lower body, their mobility was enormous.

,I shouldn't use Sword energy attacks.'

Dozens of Wolta tribe members gathered in one place. The bastards raised their altitude and went up. They all use Force to flap their wings. A huge wind rises and sweeps me away. There was a sharp energy similar to Sword energy mixed in the wind.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Barrier (電磁氣幕).

I blocked the sharp energy with an electromagnetic barrier, but I can't hold out for long.

,Is the wind at the level of a tornado?'

The strong wind swept away even the bastards who had fainted from the EMP. Their whole bodies were torn apart by the energy mixed in the wind. Blood is carried by the wind and swirls. I clicked my tongue briefly. The blood itself became a blade of wind.

,The Arrow of Tracking is gradually reducing the bastards one by one, but it will take time to kill them all. I can't hold out like this. It's not easy to take out a new item either.'

I have no choice. I have to use points.

[You have used Enhanced Leap.]

[2 points have been used.]

I leaped through space and moved behind the bastards.

The bastards, surprised by my disappearance, panicked and stopped flapping their wings. They look around as if looking for me, then turn around.

Too late.

Red lightning flashed from the Hwaryeon Bido and spread in all directions.

Noecheonryu (雷天流) Extreme Technique (極技) Bokjinroe (爆震雷).

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwakwang!

Red lightning caused an explosion. The explosion tore apart dozens of Wolta tribe members gathered together in an instant. The pieces of their corpses fall to the ground.

"Syatrobupiadaka!!"

One of the Wolta tribe members who was a little further away shouted at me.

I don't know what he's saying. However, judging from the fear and anger I feel from him, I think it's a curse.

"You son of a bitch."

He cursed, so I cursed too. He waved his arms and did something.

Kieeeeeeeeing!

Something tried to dig into my head but bounced right off. It's a mental attack. I was momentarily enraged and used Enhanced Leap.

[You have used Enhanced Leap.]

[1 point has been used.]

I appear right in front of him and swing my sword. Perhaps because of the aftermath of the mental attack being deflected, he didn't react properly.

"Split in half and die."

Seogeok!

After killing one, I turned around with an ominous feeling. Dozens of Wolta tribe members were holding missile launchers in their hands and aiming at me from about 3km away.

"...Missiles?"

I was a little flustered, but it's not strange. The Wolta tribe were bastards who changed half of their bodies into machines. In terms of science and technology, the Wolta tribe was more advanced than humans.

Dozens of missiles are flying. They look like guided missiles at a glance. Even if I use Enhanced Leap, there was a possibility that they would chase me.

I took out a white cloth from the subspace pouch and waved it around randomly. The white cloth rolled around me in a circle and wrapped around me.

Kwakwakwakwakwakwakwang!

The missile exploded, but I was safe inside the cloth called 'Supreme Guardian'.

,The Supreme Guardian can theoretically withstand a nuclear explosion head-on. It's a shame it's disposable, but dozens of missiles are nothing.'

Then the Supreme Guardian disappeared. The bastards with the missile launchers were panicking. No matter what, they wouldn't carry multiple missiles around.

"I'll kill you."
