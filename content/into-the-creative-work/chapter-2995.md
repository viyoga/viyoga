For the past three days, I'd been running around playing villain as Red Light.

I robbed corrupt officials of their wealth, dug up dirt on shady heroes and exposed it. I blew up buildings belonging to shitty corporations and got into car chases with cops and heroes on the streets.

They call me a dark hero, but in the end I'm just a villain who ignores the law. It was only natural that Mad Sound—Clara—got flagged for a wanted notice.

'If you go on the internet, Mad Sound has a suspiciously large fanbase.'

The reason was obvious.

It's because of that body that pairs so perfectly with those trashy outfits. Even though her face hasn't been revealed, the logic goes: if her body is that insane, her face must be gorgeous too. For the record, I agree with this claim. In my experience, girls with great bodies have pretty faces, and girls with pretty faces tend to have great bodies.

'Pretty girls know they're pretty, so they put effort into maintaining themselves.'

Clara adapted to villainy in no time. She feels guilt, but she also feels enjoyment.

'She can enjoy it because she hasn't crossed the line as a villain.'

For Clara, the Mad Sound gig is closer to a temporary escape. She believes that once the night passes, she can return to her ordinary daily life.

'Even if she hasn't crossed the line, she can't go completely back to how things were.'

There were signs of that already. She'd become a bit more aggressive in her relationship with me, you could say.

[…This is maddening. To think you'd corrupt Clara this far. No, it's not just Clara. The female students of the Academy are being corrupted because of you alone. I am seriously considering your expulsion right now.]

Kang Ji-young, visible only to me, spoke. I shrugged. There were still female students I hadn't bedded yet, so expulsion would be inconvenient, but I didn't let it show.

'She can't do it right now anyway. Even if Kang Ji-young returns to the principal's seat later, she won't be able to with Sung Ha-ri watching.'

And Kang Ji-young was already my woman.

• • •

Sung Ha-ri, having returned from conquering Chinese dungeons at the request of the Chinese government, stared blankly at the head of Yu Pil-sung, the Korean Hero Association Chairman, who was bowing at the waist before her.

The center of his head was sparse. Male pattern baldness was in progress.

"Sung Ha-ri-nim! South Korea is in chaos because of Red Light! Please help us! If you assist us, even that Red Light will be caught! We'll devise the plan! We'll even prepare bait to lure out Red Light and Mad Sound!"

"Ha."

What escaped Sung Ha-ri's lips was an irritated sigh.

Yu Pil-sung flinched. Cold sweat beaded on the smooth center of his head.

*Did I do something wrong? I've got my waist bent at exactly 90 degrees like I practiced! Is it because I didn't offer compensation?*

He couldn't bring himself to say, "As a Korean hero, shouldn't you cooperate with the Association?" Especially not when he knew Sung Ha-ri's temperament so well.

"Stop wasting your time with nonsense and focus on other villains or dungeon raids. Red Light only kills those who've done something to deserve it anyway."

"B-but the recent property damage from Red Light and Mad Sound has been astronomical."

"I told you not to."

"……Yes. Understood."

Sung Ha-ri walked past him and left.

Only after Sung Ha-ri's presence had completely vanished did Yu Pil-sung straighten his bent back. He pulled out a handkerchief and wiped the sweat from his face.

"Sung Ha-ri-nim seems lenient toward Red Light compared to other villains. Why on earth…? Is it that the bounty on Red Light doesn't interest you? Or do you approve of Red Light's actions?"

Yu Pil-sung couldn't find the answer. What was certain was that as long as Sung Ha-ri didn't permit it, the Association would never be able to go all-out to arrest or kill Red Light.

No matter how much damage piled up, it was better than making an enemy of Sung Ha-ri.

• • •

I entered the Dungeon of Malice with Clara. Having been here several times, neither I nor Clara was particularly nervous.

They say letting your guard down in a dungeon is forbidden, but I knew everything about this dungeon, including its hidden secrets. There was nothing dangerous here.

"Red Light is suspending activity for the time being. Naturally, that means his sidekick Mad Sound is also suspending activity."

"I wasn't planning to do villain stuff on my own anyway. I don't have the ability for that either. But why suddenly?"

"My mom, who went to China for work, came back to Korea."

Even for Red Light, going up against the strongest hero would be tough. In Red Light form, there's a risk of identity being exposed, so I couldn't go all-out either.

"Aha. That's an immediately convincing reason."

While making small talk, I checked Clara's affection level.

『Clara Peired's Affection: 56』

56.

One might think it hadn't risen as much as expected despite the time that had passed. But that was only natural. The higher the affection, the harder it becomes to raise further. That's common sense.

'At the Academy's Savior level, 50 or above is on the high end.'

Affection of 50 or above means she's feeling romantic emotions. Affection of 60 or above means she's seriously considering a relationship.

Of course, high affection doesn't automatically mean you can date. Even if someone feels romantic emotions, they might not realize it or might deny it. And even if they do realize their feelings, there's no guarantee they'll act on them.

'Few people make major life decisions based on emotion alone.'

Still, being able to see this affection level was quite convenient. I could tell how much someone felt for me, which made it easier to walk the line.

We arrived before the stone gate.

『Offer the music of wrath.』

Clara played on an electric guitar instead of a flute.

From the start, a passionate melody burst forth. A melody that was, quite literally, wrath itself. But as time passed, the feeling of wrath faded and became lighter. Quiet then loud, fast then slow.

An unpredictable yet strangely addictive piece.

When the performance ended, the stone gate flashed and began to open.

『Wrath unexpressed is not wrath, and wrath expressed is destruction. In the place where wrath has vanished, all things may enter.』

We passed the second stone gate and moved onward.

Once again, Mocha killed all the monsters.

The third of five stone gates blocked our path.

『Offer the music of love.』

Clara opened her flute case instead of the guitar. I asked, seeing her move without a moment's hesitation.

"Have you ever been in love?"

"Love doesn't necessarily mean romantic feelings, you know. I have family too. And there's nothing easier to compose than a song about love. Love is the cheapest emotion to work with."

Clara played one of her hit songs on the flute.

The stone gate didn't react.

Clara narrowed her eyes with determination and played other songs. Even after playing five in a row, the stone gate remained unchanged.

"Is this damn dungeon ignoring my songs?!"

Perhaps it was the influence of doing villain work. Clara had developed a habit of her speech turning sharp when she got excited.

"Clara. Are you free this weekend?"

"…I'm free. Why?"

"The theme is love. We go on a date like lovers. Like actual lovers. If you don't want to, you can refuse."

"……I won't refuse. Based on my experience so far, this dungeon seems to want something new. The problem is I'm too famous for a normal date. You know that, right?"

"Haha. Of course I know. I've got something good. Oh, how about we do a date rehearsal on the way back to the Academy today?"

"A rehearsal?"

"Let's watch a movie."

"What a cliché date. Well, fine."

Clara agreed easily.

Compared to terrorizing a Hero Association executive's home, watching a movie was yawn-inducingly easy.

Still, we went straight to a theater and watched a recently released film.

It was a bit boring. The rumors that the film industry was going downhill lately weren't for nothing, it seemed.

We had some coffee and returned to the Academy.

• • •

The next day.

On the day she was supposed to go on a date with Sung Yu-jin, Clara hadn't slept well.

'It doesn't mean anything. It's just because it's my first date with a guy.'

There were still over three hours until the meeting time. Clara washed up and started getting ready. When it came to underwear, she hesitated for a moment.

Comfortable but plain and frumpy underwear, versus flashy and sexy but slightly uncomfortable underwear. She chose the latter.

'It's not like anyone will see, but I'm a singer. This kind of thing is about mindset.'

One hour before the date.

Clara felt her heart racing. She'd never experienced this before—she couldn't blame it on the coffee she'd had earlier.

There's a saying that the heart holds the mind. That saying was accurate. No matter how much her brain denied it, her heart moved according to her feelings.

At this point, she had no choice but to admit what she'd been trying so hard to deny.

'…The fox in The Little Prince said it. If he comes at four, I'll be happy starting from three. Has he tamed me without me even knowing?'

Clara looked in the mirror. The pale face reflected there was flushed and full of life.

'No. It might be too early for that judgment. It could simply be because it's my first time. So at the end of today, after the date is over, I'll make my decision.'

What to do after making that decision—she hadn't thought that far.

Clara watched the clock, waiting nervously for the meeting time. It wasn't a bad feeling.

Fortunately, Sung Yu-jin arrived thirty minutes before the meeting time.

*He has surprisingly good manners. …No, that's just basic human decency.*

She didn't let it show as she greeted Sung Yu-jin.

• • •

I grinned as I looked at Clara.

"You look pretty today."

"Not really. Same as always. I just wore different clothes."

It was definitely casual wear. But it absolutely wasn't just the clothes that were different. From her hairstyle to her makeup, it was obvious she'd put in effort. Above all, her perfume had changed. The subtle scent wafting from her was unmistakably an expensive luxury brand.

'She's really looking forward to this.'

First, I pulled out the items I'd prepared from my inventory and handed them to her. A teleportation scroll and a ghost cloak.

"If you wear the ghost cloak, you can avoid people's eyes. I need it too, so I'm lending it to you—give it back later."

"This is a precious item. What about this scroll? You're going to use a scroll just for a date?"

"Because it's a special date. If you don't like it, tear it up and use it as a scroll back to the Academy."

“…….”

Clara stared at the scroll intently and tucked it into her pocket.

I handed her another scroll.

"Here. How about we start with your hometown in America? We'll have lunch at your favorite restaurant there."

"…How did you know my favorite restaurant?"

"There were plenty of articles about it. You did interviews too."

"That was years ago."

"So, you don't like it?"

"It's not that I don't like it."

We tore the teleportation scroll.

We used teleportation scrolls liberally throughout today's date, traveling all over the world. We saw penguins in Antarctica and polar bears in the Arctic. In Egypt, we drank a cool cocktail atop the pyramids.

"Isn't this illegal?"

"It's fine. It's fine. As long as we don't get caught."

Even if we did get caught and it became a problem, there were plenty of ways to handle it. The easiest method was using the Mom card. The most certain method was information erasure.

After that, we toured the Taj Mahal in India and saw the Colosseum in Rome, Italy.

We saw the Eiffel Tower in France and the clock tower in London, spending about ten minutes at each.

We saw elephants and lions in Africa, and I even had a running race with a kangaroo in Australia.

We had dinner in Japan and enjoyed iced Americanos at the summit of Everest.

We saw Dongting Lake in China and Mayan ruins in Mexico. In Southeast Asia, we tasted durian.

By the time we returned to the Academy, it was past midnight.

"You don't have to walk me back. It's not far. Do you think I can't find my own personal studio?"

"It's a date. Let me see it through to the end. Can I hold your hand?"

"You held it all day until I was sick of it."

"The date isn't over yet."

"……Walk me to my studio."

"Not the dorm?"

"I'm going to write a song. A new melody has been playing in my head over and over since earlier."

"Like automatic background music playing in your brain? I'm curious. What kind of melody?"

"It's not organized yet, so it's hard to put into words."

We arrived at the entrance to Clara's personal studio.

Today's date was over. I'd consumed a considerable number of teleportation scrolls. Not just the ones used today, but counting the ones used beforehand while scouting date locations, it totaled over fifty. No regrets. It was worth it.

『Clara Peired's Affection: 64』

It was definitely worth it. Her affection had risen dramatically.

'Honestly, even without seeing the affection level, I can tell Clara has feelings for me.'

It could be affection temporarily inflated by the atmosphere. There was a fair chance it would drop like a lie by tomorrow.

'Relying solely on affection level to make your move is amateur hour.'

We arrived at Clara's personal studio. I let go of her hand at the entrance.

"Clara. I had fun today."

"That's my line. A first date that's a trip around the world. No one would believe me if I told them. I probably won't forget it even decades from now."

"Heh heh. I'm honored."

Clara didn't go straight into her studio and lingered.

Wondering if she had something to say, I watched her. Soon, as if she'd made up her mind about something, she approached me.

She wrapped both arms around my neck and pulled me in, pressing her lips to mine.

My hands gently cradled Clara's lower back. Our bodies pressed closer. Her chest was crushed against me. I wanted to let my hands wander lower, but I held back desperately. It wasn't time yet.

The kiss lasted over a minute before it ended.

Her face now bright red, Clara immediately pulled away from my arms and opened the studio door.

"Don't get any weird ideas. In movies, dates end with a kiss, right? It was just the date's finale."

"In the movies I've seen, they end in bed."

She didn't answer and closed the door.

『Clara Peired's Affection: 67』

Maybe I could have gone further than a kiss.
