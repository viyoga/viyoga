Kim Jaeji stared at the radio with an anxious face. A stream of sweat flowed down his chubby cheek.

No contact.

Thirty minutes had passed since the operation began, but there was no contact whatsoever. He decided to accept reality.

,It failed.'

It failed so badly that even a failure report didn't come in.

,There's only one option left. Time to do something crazy.'

He took out another radio.

He contacted the secret nuclear research lab. He would issue a standby order so that nukes could be used at any time.

,Negotiate with Seong Yujin.'

Seong Yujin would take it as blackmail, not negotiation.

But what could he do? He would have to accept it unless he wanted to see a mushroom cloud rising over Seoul.

,It's best to maintain a good relationship with Seong Yujin in many ways. It wouldn't be good to push it to the end with nukes. I should focus on preserving my position for now.'

What about after that? He had never thought about it. As long as Seong Yujin was fine, the congressmen were nothing more than scarecrows. It would be better to borrow the power of China.

Kwang!

A loud explosion rang out. Kim Jaeji, who was sitting at his desk in his home study, was startled and jumped up.

Tang tang tang tang tang!

Gunshots rang out from downstairs. He opened the window curtains and looked outside. Drones were surrounding the mansion. Military trucks and buses were parked on the road. An entire military unit had come to get him.

,So fast... They mobilized the military in a residential area of Seoul?'

He knew that Seong Yujin had taken control of the Southern Military. But he never thought it would be to this extent.

Wasn't this a dictator wearing the mask of democracy?

Kung!

The study door opened, and armed knights rushed in. The knights stood on either side of Kim Jaeji and pointed their pistols at him. Seong Yujin entered the study leisurely with Lee Yeonhee.

,Kim Jaeji. You've been plotting something interesting.'

Seong Yujin smiled. It was a sneer. Kim Jaeji tried to twist his lips to feign composure.

,Something interesting? I don't know what you're talking about. Breaking into my house at this hour... Did things not go well in Jeju Island? Are you attacking me to cover up that failure?'

,Cut the crap. Your subordinates have told me everything. Special Forces Sari. A special forces unit that takes in promising kids from a young age and raises them. I was a little impressed by the story that could make a movie.'

,...They really told you? They are the kind of people who would commit suicide to hide information rather than beg for their lives. They were also trained to withstand torture. Even if you capture them alive, it would take at least a few days to get them to open their mouths!'

,I showed them something worse than torture. It's impossible to overcome primal fear, even if you're brainwashed.'

Kim Jaeji took a deep breath and tried to remain calm. He showed Seong Yujin the radio in his hand.

,I have nukes. If I give the order through this radio, Seoul will be a sea of fire. I won't ask for much. Let's just pretend today never happened.'

,If we pretend it never happened, will you give me the nukes?'

,Of course not. Nukes are my lifeline. Instead, I'll stay quiet for a few years.'

,Haha. You expect me to believe that? If you were in my position, would you believe your own words?'

,Believe me. It's better than Seoul being blown away.'

Seong Yujin held out his hand to Lee Yeonhee. Lee Yeonhee took out a pistol from her waist, pulled the slide to load it, and gently placed it in his hand. Seong Yujin pulled the trigger on Kim Jaeji's thigh.

,Kuaaak!'

Kim Jaeji screamed and fell to the ground.

Even without shooting skills, he was able to hit the target well at short distances with practice and experience.

,Shoot! Commander!! Shoot the nukes right now!!'

Kim Jaeji shouted into the radio. There was no reply from the radio. Seong Yujin reached out for the radio. Pajijit. A current flashed briefly and disappeared. Immediately after, the radio flew into Seong Yujin's hand.

,Can you hear me?'

-"Yes, Yujin-nim."

It was Irina who spoke from the radio.

,What's the situation?'

-"We've taken control of the nuclear research lab. We've secured 42 strategic nukes and 195 tactical nukes."

,Good job, Irina. You made things easier for me. 42 strategic nukes is a bit surprising. Leave the rest to my subordinates and return. Oh, and did you capture the guys there alive? Are they being cooperative?'

-"Yes. They surrendered after we killed the commander."

,That's good. They're engineers who can build nukes, so we should treat them well in their own way.'

Seong Yujin and Lee Yeonhee grinned.

They couldn't help but smile.

Nukes could not be made immediately even with Korea's capabilities. Not to mention Korea in its current state. In a world where the apocalypse had broken out, there was a lack of time and resources to focus on nuclear production. In the long run, nuclear defense technology was better.

If Korea acquired nuclear defense technology, it would be qualified to become the strongest country in the world. Of course, it was already a ruined world. But even in this world, the best always shines.

,I'm getting so much today. It makes me feel good. Keukeu.'

Kim Jaeji pressed down hard on the blood flowing from his thigh and begged Seong Yujin.

,S-Save me. What will the Joseon people think if I die now? There are quite a few Joseon people who are loyal to me...!'

,Yeah. That's why I've kept you alive until now. It's better for you to cooperate directly for a smooth unification. But there's a better way. The method you were trying to use.'

Seong Yujin snapped his fingers.

A man enters with the knights.

The small man was trembling with fear on his face.

,Ryangg Changho Superior...! Did you betray me?!'

,I... I want to die as a human.'

Seong Yujin gestured to Ryang Changho. Ryang Changho uses his ability. His face and small physique swell up and turn into Kim Jaeji's. The transformation took less than 5 seconds. His clothes swelled up as if they were about to burst. They didn't burst because they were elastic.

,Oh. It's amazing even after seeing it again. You can't really tell from the outside. Is the problem the atmosphere? Don't tremble.'

,Yes, yes. Your Excellency...'

Ryang Changho, who looked like Kim Jaeji, tensed his body. But he was still trembling. Seong Yujin clicked his tongue briefly.

,Looks like you need some practice time.'

It was definitely the same face, but the impression looked different because the atmosphere was different. It wasn't that important. From this moment on, Kim Jaeji would only be doing the minimum official activities.

Seong Yujin pointed his finger at the real Kim Jaeji with a pale face.

,Drag him away.'

He wouldn't kill Kim Jaeji right away because there was something he needed to know. Even if he found out everything he needed to know, he would live in a terrible place where he couldn't die.

,Save me! Save me!! I will cooperate fully! Just save my life!!'

Kim Jaeji shouted as he was dragged away by the knights. No one reacted.

Seong Yujin chuckled as he tapped the shoulder of the fake Kim Jaeji, who was still trembling.

,Let's work hard together in the future. For Korea.'

,Yes, yes. Your Excellency.'

,And do some acting practice.'

,I will work hard!'

He goes out of the mansion with Lee Yeonhee.

,Move the Southern Military to clean up the Northern Military. After that, conduct a joint operation against the Rochists.'

,Kim Jaeji's faction has been swept away this time, so it will be easy to take control of North Korea.'

,There is no North Korea in this world.'

,Oops. I was rude. The North will be stabilized.'

There, there was a mental ability mutant brought from Jeju Island, the K-Pop Tree.

Researchers including me, Na Chaeyoung, and Yoo Seojeong watched the K-Pop Tree inside the reinforced glass window. The tightly bound K-Pop Tree wriggled its body and opened its mouth. No sound could be heard. This was because the inside was completely sealed.

,Yoo-sensei. Is this okay? Problems always happen in movies.'

,It's okay. Its physical abilities are the lowest among the mutants. It can't break these restraints on its own.'

,What about that mental attack of his?'

,We've confirmed that its ability is activated through singing. There's no problem as long as you don't listen to the sound.'

,Really? You've done enough research, right.'

Yoo Seojeong gave a bitter smile.

,I stayed up all night, but... I couldn't even do it for 24 hours. There aren't any meaningful results. But Dr. Na seems to be a little different.'

,...Jena is analyzing the wavelengths that cause mental confusion. It will take time, but... we'll be able to create a machine that blocks mental wavelengths.'

Today, I will use my ability to turn the K-Pop Tree back into a human. The K-Pop Tree must have become a mutant through the black thorns, so it's most likely a human. And an Awakened one at that.

,The K-Pop Tree doesn't just have mental abilities. It can also sing songs that give strength to allies. In game terms, you could call it a supporter.'

It was a coveted talent. That's why Handless tried to recover the K-Pop Tree's corpse and revive it.

,Its abilities will be weaker than when it was a mutant, but... mutants can't be controlled, so this is the best option.'

Originally, I was going to study the K-Pop Tree for a while before turning it back into a human. Lee Yeonhee, Na Chaeyoung, and Yoo Seojeong all agreed with that opinion.

,Even if nothing else, its face is pretty. If that's its face from when it was human... I can't stand it.'

It was like being on the verge of a guaranteed gacha success.

If the gacha failed, the K-Pop Tree would live as a research lab test subject.

,My ability can't undo what I've already undone, but since there are black thorns, I can turn it back into a mutant.'

There were plenty of countermeasures.

,Open the door. I'm going in right away.'

,I'll calm the K-Pop Tree down first.'

Yoo Seojeong operated the computer in front of the lab. A metal bar appeared on the ceiling inside the lab. Electricity was discharged from the metal bar, shocking the K-Pop Tree. The mouth of the electrocuted K-Pop Tree closed.

I took the opportunity to go inside. There was a smell of burning flesh.

The K-Pop Tree looks at me. And opened its mouth to sing.

Ah, aaaa~. Aaaaaa~

I liked the sweet singing voice. I slowly approached the restrained K-Pop Tree and reached out to its trunk.

,It's time to return to being human.'
