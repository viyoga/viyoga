Listening to the footsteps approaching from the end of the hallway, I paused to think. Wouldn't this have not happened if it were night?

,...That's a meaningless assumption. According to Na Chaeyoung-ssi, this place is closed at night anyway.

Card keys can't get in, and the security devices that are turned off during the day operate at night. Therefore, the daytime when the researchers are working is safer.

I heard the sound of a door opening and closing. Fortunately, the researcher seems to have gone into a room before coming towards me.

,Should I go kill him?

After a moment of consideration, I decided to go downstairs. Killing a researcher who isn't even a combatant wouldn't make me any safer. And having killed a soldier, there wasn't much time. It would take at most 10 minutes for the soldiers to realize my presence. Depending on the situation, it could be shorter or longer.

Trusting Na Chaeyoung-ssi's words, I acted quickly. In some ways, her role was more important than mine.

I managed to get to the basement entrance and down to the basement without encountering anyone.

Bbeep!
