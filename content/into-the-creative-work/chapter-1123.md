,I want to see what you're made of. How about a duel with me?"

"......."

The chance to duel an S-class Hunter for free doesn't come often. Especially Ha Heon-shin, nicknamed 'Weirdo God', who retired from the Hunter world because he was old. But no one thinks Ha Heon-shin has gotten weaker.

Just looking at the fact that he's maintaining a middle-aged appearance in his mid-40s even though he's over 80, you can tell he's no ordinary person.

So why is he asking me for a duel? Does he really want to see my talent?

,Why does he need to see my talent?'

[Baekhwan] I am a great nobleman of the world. After thinking about it a few times, the answer came right away. No, the answer was right next to me.

,It must be because of Ha Seunghee. Does he think I'm in a relationship with Ha Seunghee?'

Perhaps Ha Heon-shin wants to see my talent, or more precisely, my limits. How far I can go.

,The chance to duel with the Weirdo God... It's not an opportunity that comes easily. Rather, I'd like to ask for a duel."

,I'm glad you think so. Strike while the iron is hot. Shall we do it right away?"

,I'd be honored, but we can't duel just anywhere."

,I have a training ground that I usually use. Let's do it there."

Ha Heon-shin gets up from his chair and walks out.

I was about to follow him when Ha Seunghee pulled on my sleeve.

,What is it?"

,Since you've decided to duel with my grandfather, do your best. Please don't disappoint him."

Ha Seunghee's eyes were complicated. I wondered what she meant, then I realized she didn't have much talent as a Hunter. It seems she disappointed Ha Heon-shin before.

,Ha Junsu doesn't have much talent as a Hunter either. He's amazing, but his martial power is at best B-class? About that level."

That's amazing enough, but considering the background of being the grandson of S-class Ha Heon-shin, it feels insignificant.

,I want to see that old geezer's surprised face."

,Could you not call my grandfather an old geezer? And you'll have to work hard to surprise him."

I shrugged at Ha Seunghee and went out of the study. A person who looked like a secretary came up and guided me and Ha Seunghee. It seems Ha Heon-shin stopped by somewhere else for a moment.

I went into a detached building next to the mansion. Now that I see it, the entire detached building was a training ground. The ceiling was high and I could feel an unusual energy from the walls.

,Is it a barrier? I don't know what kind of barrier it is, but I know it's amazing.'

Remembering that the owner of this place is one of the richest people in Korea and an S-class Hunter, this amazing training ground doesn't feel like much.

Woongseongwoongseong.

The surroundings were noisy. It was because presidents, including the Ha Seunghee and Ha Junsu siblings, had entered the training ground.

,Sorry for the many spectators. Are you perhaps uncomfortable with their gaze?"

Ha Heon-shin, who appeared wearing a dobok, asked me. His solid muscles could be seen through the dobok.

,I don't care about their gaze, but I don't really like busybodies chattering as they please."

,I'll make sure they keep their mouths shut in my name, so don't worry."

,I trust Chairman-nim's name."

Ha Heon-shin called an older man in a suit next to him.

,Secretary Choi."

,Yes. Chairman-nim."

Secretary Choi held out a box covered with cloth. A well-maintained sword exuded energy and showed its presence on the cloth.

,I prepared it because I heard you use a sword."

,It's no ordinary sword."

,It's a restoration of the hwandos that Kim Yushin used, through a special method. It's one of my treasures. I'll lend it to you."

,Ah, Kim Yushin. You mean the Ocean King. I know him well."

,...Ocean King... Are you talking about Maritime Emperor Jang Bogo?"

,Ah, I'm weak in history, so I got confused."

Pretending not to see Ha Heon-shin's awkward expression, I grabbed the sword. A cool sensation ran down my arm to my head.

,It's a good sword.'

But it's disappointing compared to Hwaryeon Bido. If you ask what's disappointing, it's hard to answer. My hand might be so used to Hwaryeon Bido that I feel that way.

,Should I take out Hwaryeon Bido now? ...There are many eyes watching, so is it better to hide it?'

I swung the sword in the air. It wasn't bad. When I moved mana, blue Sword energy formed on the blade. When I used a little Yeongcheon energy here, electricity flowed over the Sword energy.

,It's Yeongcheon-ryu's Sword energy. I knew you were Yeongcheon-ryu, but it's a new feeling to see it in person."

,Do you know Yeongcheon-ryu?"

,I had a slight connection with the previous sect leader."

Ha Heon-shin recalled memories and shook his head.

,It's a stale memory, so it's not something I'd tell you. Do you need more preparation time?"

,This is fine. Chairman-nim, don't you use a weapon?"

,My body is my weapon."

I looked at the dobok he was wearing. There was nothing special about the white dobok. It's a simple dobok. There are no accessories either. In other words, Ha Heon-shin was bare-handed without weapons or armor.

I know Ha Heon-shin is S-class, but at this point, my pride is hurt.

,I want to make sure before we duel. What happens if I seriously injure Chairman-nim? What if Chairman-nim dies in an accident...."

Hostility poured in from all directions. The hostility of the affiliates' presidents is nothing. The real thing is the momentum felt from the front, from Ha Heon-shin. The momentum, several times more intense than what I felt in the study, was pressing me.

Ha Heon-shin, who had stiffened his face, smirked. The momentum that had been pressing down on me disappeared.

,You're a bit presumptuous."

,...Did I offend you? I apologize."

,No, there's no need to apologize. It's been 10 years since I've heard such words to my face, so I'm excited."

Ha Heon-shin stepped back, putting distance between me. His eyes sharpened. The restrained momentum flowed out through his eyes.

,Try to kill me if you can. Even if I die in this duel, I won't hold you responsible in any way."

"......."

,I hate men who are all talk."

I felt a chill down my spine and gripped the sword tightly.

,Duel with an S-class or whatever. I really don't like being tested by someone.'

I raised my fighting spirit along with mana.

,Screw the consequences. If I get the chance, I'll kill him right away, old geezer.'

Ha Heon-shin, who felt my murderous intent, laughed as if it was interesting. But that smile disappeared in an instant.

,Let's begin."

As soon as he finished speaking, I used Acceleration and Instant to approach. Ha Heon-shin's reaction was just moving his eyes. I gritted my teeth. I realized that the best speed in my standards was nothing to him.

The image of a bear with an eagle's head appeared and disappeared above Ha Heon-shin's body. It's an Eagle Bear, an A-class monster.

Yeongcheon-ryu (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

The sword that had become a ray of light was caught in his hand. I never dreamed it would be blocked so easily. I felt a slight sense of emptiness.

Is that Chairman-nim's ability, Monster Plunder (怪奪)?

The ability to plunder the characteristics of monsters. Ha Heon-shin's ability was famous.

,That's right. Right now, I've additionally gained the physical abilities of the Eagle Bear. The Eagle Bear's strength and reaction speed are more amazing than you think."

Pajik.

Electricity reacted to my will.

,Seal the mana with Bongroe and cut him down. No matter how S-class he is, if he can't use mana... Keoeok!'

Ha Heon-shin, sensing the sign, grabbed the sword and threw it upwards. His body soared and collided with the ceiling. His body fell down again. Ha Heon-shin was waiting for me to fall, charging an uppercut.

Yeongcheon-ryu Ultimate Skill: Exploding Thunder.

A streak of blue lightning fell on him. The lightning exploded as soon as it touched his body.

It didn't inflict any damage. He took a step back just before the lightning fell and avoided the attack. There wasn't a single scorch mark on his white dobok.

I landed on the ground and sighed.

,I expected him to be strong, but... I didn't know it would be this much.'

I thought of Han Ah-young and compared her to him. If Han Ah-young, who had just become S-class, fought Ha Heon-shin....

,Ha Heon-shin wins nine times out of ten. Just as there is a gap even in A-class, there is a gap even in S-class.'

Before the duel started, I vaguely thought there was a 1% chance I could win. But after facing him directly, I realized there wasn't even a 0.1% chance.

,But I can't give up.'

The image of a white wolf floated above Ha Heon-shin's body. Golden eyes and a horn on his forehead. It's Lycros, an S-class monster. And Lycros's characteristic is lightning.

,...That damn old geezer.'

Lightning flew from Ha Heon-shin. It's no ordinary lightning. Lightning filled with mana. Lightning that I can't ignore either.

,Deflect it.'

I swung the sword at the lightning. The lightning stuck to the sword and tried to electrocute my body.

,The direction of using lightning was simple. It's not a technique Ha Heon-shin usually uses. He deliberately used lightning to tease me.'

The will contained in the lightning is weak. I can steal this much. Ignoring the pain from the electric shock, I swung the sword again. The deflected lightning flew towards Ha Heon-shin.

,Oho."

Ha Heon-shin exclaimed and reached out his hand. He has no intention of avoiding it at all. He intends to deflect the lightning I deflected again. I predicted that arrogance. Ha Heon-shin is qualified to do so.

And the lightning exploded.

Kwakwakwakwang!

Yeongcheon-ryu Ultimate Skill: Exploding Thunder.

,I mixed the essence of Exploding Thunder when deflecting the lightning. Unlike other ultimate skills, it has a particularly good compatibility with Exploding Thunder. Maybe it's because of the lightning attribute.'

,Keuheum. I took a hit."

After a short explosion, Ha Heon-shin coughed lightly. The white dobok was scorched, but his skin had no wounds.

,...It's a bit shocking.'

I straightened my scattered mind. There's still a chance left. Ha Heon-shin's purpose is to confirm my talent. So at least 3 minutes of the duel will continue.

I kicked off the ground and ran towards Ha Heon-shin.

•••

3 minutes passed.

,You have potential."

Ha Heon-shin said. It wasn't a compliment. What was contained in his eyes was boredom and disappointment. I didn't meet Ha Heon-shin's standards.

I breathed roughly and distanced myself. Compared to me, who was covered in sweat, Ha Heon-shin's breathing was calm.

,Let's finish up and have dinner."

Ha Heon-shin clenched his fist tightly and stepped on the ground with strength in his legs. The impact of the force became wind and swept through the training ground once.

If it were the original me, I would have given up here. Because I don't care how Ha Heon-shin evaluates me. It's not like I'll gain anything from him evaluating me highly.

,But....'

Ha Seunghee, who was in a safe place, was silently watching me.

,I promised Seunghee I'd show that old geezer's surprised face.'

I grabbed the sword with both hands and pulled up mana. I squeezed out all the mana in every corner of my body. Aftereffects? When have I ever cared about that? If necessary, I can use Complete Recovery.

Next, I raised my concentration. I looked at the sword in my hands, not Ha Heon-shin. Ha Heon-shin realized this was the last strike and was waiting.

,Heavenly Mind.'

[Heavenly Mind (天心) is activated. Lasts for 1 minute.]

My will refined the mana, the mana became Sword energy, and the Sword energy became lightning and permeated the blade.

The concentration heightened by the battle exceeded the limit and led me to a realm that I still couldn't understand.

Is the sword me?

Is the lightning me?

I don't know.

However, the existence I had to cut was clearly visible.

Yeongcheon-ryu (影天流) Ultimate Technique (極技) Heavenly Light (天光).

I became a ray of light and charged at Ha Heon-shin. I could see Ha Heon-shin's shocked expression. The image of a dark red rock monster flashed above his body. Ha Heon-shin raised both hands for the first time to create a guard.

I cut through him and passed by.

I gave strength to my staggering legs and prevented myself from falling. I didn't want to fall pathetically.

I heard the sound of liquid falling from behind. It was Ha Heon-shin's blood.

I looked back, savoring the sensation of the slash remaining on my fingertips. Ha Heon-shin, whose arms had been cut, was looking at me with a surprised expression. It wasn't a fatal injury.

,Amazing. To think you'd break through the defense of the Red Rock Turtle (赤岩龟)....
