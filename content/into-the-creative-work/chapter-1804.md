I left the Demonic Heavenly Divine Church and walked west.

Actually, I'm not sure if it's really west. I was just walking in the direction the snout of the Ten Thousand Mile Tracing Dog was pointing.

,Cheon Yu-un-nim said it was west, so it must be west.'

The direction wasn't really that important anyway.

I swept the edge of my bamboo hat with my left finger. Black robe, bamboo hat, sword at the waist. It was a look that anyone could recognize as a noble Martial artist master.

,Kyah, I'm getting drunk.'

The hand holding the sword hilt was itching. I wanted to swing the sword for no reason. And there happened to be a tree right next to me that was perfect for cutting.

,I'll cut it.'

A flash of light gleamed from the scabbard. The flash passed through the tree, drew a semicircle, and returned to the scabbard.

Cheolkeok!

Immediately after the sound from the scabbard, a line appeared on the tree trunk, and it began to slide and fall.

I wore a satisfied smile. I didn't use qi. It was the result of pure strength and swordsmanship. I in this world was much stronger than in the other world. Not to mention the body, my martial arts are aided by the Heavenly Gang Star System. I am the martial arts genius, Seong Yujin-ssi.

I saw a wild boar. It was running around like crazy, as if it was having a great time. And it had to be around me. It was as if it was threatening me to give it food. Looking closely, it wasn't an ordinary wild boar. It had a red dot on its forehead, and the energy it possessed was ferocious.

,Is it a monster?'

If it were some other weakling, I would have taken out some jerky and thrown it far away, but I wasn't going to do that.

,Annoying.'

My right hand moved to the sword hilt. The wild boar's eyes gleamed fiercely, and it charged straight at me. The wild boar's molars were as sharp as knives.

At the same time as drawing the sword, I walked forward. I cut down the wild boar in mid-air and sheathed the sword.

Cheolkeok!

The wild boar split in half and died. Blood and guts spilled out. Even if it was a monster, it wasn't like I couldn't eat it. No, rather, its corpse was even more valuable because it was a monster. There was almost nothing that the humans of this world couldn't eat.

I left the wild boar's corpse behind and walked forward.

This time, I saw a person. He was walking alone. He was walking slowly towards me from the front.

,He looks like he can use some strength.'

He was large. He wore a wide sword on his back. His hair was disheveled and his beard was thick. His clothes were dirty and stained with blood here and there.

,Is he a bandit? Or a ronin?'

Ronin and bandits both looked filthy, so it was hard to tell them apart when you encountered them in a place like this.

I put strength in my chest and raised my chin. I walked forward, exuding the aura of a master. Now, the guy overwhelmed by the aura of a master should run away with a frightened expression.

But the world didn't flow according to my wishes.

The corners of his mouth twitched. It seemed he was sneering. I could tolerate this much. I had just killed the wild boar monster, so I was feeling refreshed.

But the bastard blocked my path.

,Move.'

A voice like a beast growling rang out. I could feel condensed killing intent in that voice. He deliberately put killing intent in his voice.

,A guy who knows how to put killing intent in his voice can't recognize the aura of a master?'

I didn't hide my momentum. But I didn't reveal it recklessly either. I just acted as usual. That alone was enough. Guys who could see my capabilities would grovel on their own.

,Is he unable to grasp the strength of the opponent? No, that's not the important thing.'

He dared to block my path and even threatened me with killing intent. There were enough reasons to die.

Like when I split the wild boar, like when I cut the tree, I drew the sword from its scabbard and swung it lightly.

Kkang!

Was it because I swung it too lightly? The sword was blocked by the bastard's wide sword.

,Hahaha. You look very delicious. I'll cut you up alive and eat you.'

The bastard licked his lips with his tongue and said. I felt like shit.

,I'll kill you slowly.'

I moved the sword. Kkiiiiiiiiik! The touching swords rubbed against each other and screamed. The tip of the sword was aimed at the bastard's neck. The bastard's qi moved. A beastly, ferocious demonic energy exploded. My sword was knocked aside.

,Heuhahahahaha!'

The bastard, confident of victory, laughed loudly and struck down with his sword at my head.

I moved my qi. My consciousness accelerated momentarily. The bastard's falling sword was slow.

There's no need to use the Lightning Sky Style. I infuse qi into the sword and swing it. My sword moved later than the bastard's sword, but it reached the bastard faster.

Seogeok!

The bastard's right arm flew into the air along with the wide sword.

,Aaaaaaaagh!'

The bastard screamed and bent his upper body. The bastard's demonic energy burst out. The bastard's body began to mutate. The color turned black, and something like insect antennae grew on his head. The demonic energy I felt from the bastard turned into demonic qi like a lie.

,It didn't feel like a monster pretending to be a human. Was he originally human? It's the Reverse Demon Divine Art.'

I never thought that even such a nobody would have mastered the Reverse Demon Divine Art.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

Lightning Sky Style (雷天流) Evil Lightning (惡雷)

I used Instant before things got more troublesome. I stabbed the bastard's chest with the sword and sent a cyan lightning current through him.

,Aaaaaaaaaaagh!'

A terrible scream rang out. The bastard fell to the ground and writhed. The cyan lightning current remained maliciously inside his body.

Had about a minute passed? The bastard, who had been writhing in pain, didn't move an inch like a stone. I stepped on the bastard's corpse and walked forward.

,If even such nobodies are getting their hands on the Reverse Demon Divine Art... is someone deliberately spreading it?'

The beginning of the Reverse Demon Divine Art is Muk Jilyeon. But it's difficult to conclude that this is the work of Muk Jilyeon. The Reverse Demon Divine Art has already spread as much as it can.

,I don't know. It's not my business anyway, so who cares.'

I walked silently in the direction the Ten Thousand Mile Tracing Dog was pointing.

• • •

Around the time the sun began to set, it started to rain. The momentum was unusual, so I decided to go into an old inn nearby and rest. It was broken down here and there, but it was perfect for avoiding the rain for a while.

I gathered the wet wood I had brought from the forest in one place and created lightning.

Pajijijik.

Lightning fire rose. Whether it was wet wood or whatever, the flames blazed fiercely as I pushed through with firepower. I listened to the sound of the rain and stared blankly at the fire. It wasn't bad to spend time meaninglessly like this sometimes.

The sun had completely set. As I was chewing on jerky, I felt a presence approaching this way. Two presences in total.

,There was already a guest. There's no way anyone would live in a place that's no different from an abandoned house... I guess you came to avoid the rain too.'

It was a middle-aged swordsman and a young swordsman. They were tall and had square jaws. Judging from how similar they looked, they seemed to be family. However, I couldn't be sure of their ages just by their appearance. In this world, even if someone had the appearance of a child, they could be an old man who was hundreds of years old on the inside.

I stared intently at the middle-aged man.

He was a master. His realm was roughly middle-stage of the Five Qi. He must have a hidden move or a magical treasure, so I couldn't just look down on him because his realm was lower than mine.

,May we take shelter from the rain here for a while?'

Cheok!

The young man clasped his hands and asked politely. Fighting with a master was annoying, so I nodded.

,Do as you please.'

,Thank you. I am Hong Je-yu. The person next to me is my father.'

The middle-aged man also clasped his hands to me.

,Thank you. I am Hong Gang-hu, known as the Five Mountains Seeking Sword. What is your alias?

They sat down a little distance away from me. That distance was important. A distance where they couldn't reach me immediately even if they drew their swords. They were smiling kindly, but they were wary of me. I was a little dumbfounded. Shouldn't they be the ones being wary of me? I wasn't the uninvited guest, they were.

I chewed and swallowed the jerky before speaking.

I am Yeom Gu-seok, the Lightning Flash Blade.

,Lightning Flash Blade! I've heard of you! You're the one who recently destroyed the Dokgo Clan! They say that blade is as fast as lightning, right?'

The corners of my mouth went up. As expected, now that my reputation has increased, people recognize me right away.

,Yes. That's me. But I've never heard of the alias Five Mountains Seeking Sword.'

,We are ronin. We're not from around here.'

,With that skill, you're a ronin?'

If you have a skill level of middle-stage of the Five Qi, you can easily join and settle down in any faction. If not, you can start your own sect and live in luxury. There was no reason for a skilled person who had reached the Five Qi to work as a ronin.

,There's a story behind it.'

,What kind of story?'

,It's not a serious story. The sect I was in was just destroyed. It's a common occurrence in this world.'

,You managed to survive the destruction.'

,It didn't seem like it would work out, so I took my son and ran. Do you think I'm being cowardly?'

,I don't care.'

I answered briefly. I don't think it's cowardly. I would have done the same.

,Excuse me. Yeom Daein-nim. May I ask where you are going?'

The young man asked me.

,Why are you curious about that?'

The young man panicked and waved his hands.

,Oh, just because? No special meaning. You don't have to tell me if you don't want to.'

,I received a mission from the Divine Church and I'm going west. Don't ask what the mission is.'

The young man swallowed hard. Was it because of the words Demonic Heavenly Divine Church? He stiffened up. I asked him in return.

,Where are you guys going?'

,We are wandering aimlessly.'

Hong Gang-hu interjected and said.

,It's not just swinging a sword while holed up in a training hall that counts as training. A Martial artist must experience many things. I don't want my son to be trapped in a narrow well.'

,There's a reason why they say leaving home is suffering.'

,It was suffering even at home.'

Hong Gang-hu's eyes deepened. His son, Hong Je-yu, looked around and said.

,We are also going west. I heard that the Yuun Merchant Guild will be held in Baeyeong City in a week.'

I know about Baeyeong City too. It's one of the cities belonging to the Demonic Heavenly Divine Church.

,I've never heard of the Yuun Merchant Guild.'

,That's possible. The Yuun Merchant Guild operates somewhat secretly. They don't deal with ordinary people at all, so rumors don't spread easily.'

Hong Je-yu explained about the Yuun Merchant Guild. It's said to be a merchant guild that travels around the Jianghu. They set up shop and open a market at regular intervals.

At first, they were just a few peddlers. They sold precious spiritual medicines, and Martial artists who wanted the spiritual medicines began to follow them.

,After a few years, they became the Yuun Merchant Guild and grew so big that they were nicknamed the moving market.'

,Do they really sell spiritual medicines?'

I was suspicious because spiritual medicines were not easy to obtain.

,Yes. I went there 5 years ago and bought a spiritual medicine. The Yuun Merchant Guild sells not only spiritual medicines but also magical treasures and martial arts. It's almost a general store.'

I curled up the corners of my mouth. I felt greed rising from the depths of my heart.
