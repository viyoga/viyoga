More than 10 minutes had passed since Kashing trapped Yuria in the crack of time. In the world of the crack of time, nearly 20 years had passed.

Originally, her mind should have collapsed and she should have gone crazy. But from Kashing's point of view, Yuria's mind was fine.

,Is she really okay? Was her mind twisted from the beginning? ...Maybe she's something other than human.'

Kashing gave up on life.

His ability had a restriction that once used, he couldn't stop until the opponent's mind collapsed. Of course, this was separate from the price of the ability. He had no youth to spare.

"Becky. Run away."

"...What?"

"The operation failed. I'm going to die of old age. Go and tell that person. Yuria Grace is more than a genius... she's a monster. Embracing a monster like that is insane."

Kashing's voice became rough. His hair was already white, and wrinkles covered not only his face but his entire body. The price of aging would soon take his life.

"Okay. I'll tell that person what you said."

Kieeeeeeeng.

A sharp sound echoed from the center of the forest. Becky shut her mouth. An unknown sense of foreboding washed over her.

"No way.... She escaped from that space with her own power?"

Kashing stared at the center of the forest with horrified eyes.

There was a shadow.

Despite the dark night, a jet-black shadow flickered like a flame. It was like a dark moon floating in the sky.

The shadow eventually transformed like a flower bud and slowly bloomed. Inside was a woman wearing a maid outfit. It was Yuria Grace.

The bloomed shadow spread like ink in all directions. The wide forest was covered in shadows.

Yuria stomped the ground with her right foot, which was wearing a shoe.

Chwararararararak!

From within the shadows, thousands, tens of thousands of shadow chains soared and pierced the spirits of the forest.

Kkiaaaaaaaaak!

The dying screams of the ghosts filled the forest.

"Becky. Run away!"

Kashing shouted. Becky tried to turn around, but... her ankle was caught by a shadow chain, and she couldn't move.

Yuria appeared in front of him.

She emerged from the shadows. It was a natural space movement like flowing water. Kashing was certain. Yuria Grace was a superhuman of at least level 7 or higher. Perhaps she had been hiding her strength from the beginning.

Yuria quickly scanned Kashing and Becky.

"We've met before. Was Prince William behind this? I'd like to ask you more details, but... you're about to die."

"...Do you really have no intention of serving His Highness the Prince? If he knew your abilities... you could become one of the most powerful people in Neo London."

"I have no interest."

Yuria brandished a dagger. The jet-black shadow became a sharp Sword energy and beheaded Kashing, who was on the verge of dying of old age.

Kashing's head falls down the cliff. A shadow chain that soared from below the cliff pierced his head.

Jjeoeong.

A sound like something breaking was heard in the air.

,The way he fell down the cliff was unnatural, so I did a finishing blow... he was trying to move his companion with space movement.'

Yuria's gaze turned to Becky, who was left alone. Becky gritted her teeth, grabbed a pistol, and aimed it at Yuria's head.

"Don't move! This isn't just any bullet. It's a cursed bullet made by a high-ranking black magician sacrificing his own finger. It can't kill you, but it can make you bedridden for months."

"If I were you, I would've shot during that time."

Tang.

Becky pulled the trigger. The bullet that was fired was twice as fast as a normal bullet.

Yuria's shadow reacted even faster than that. The small shadow between her clothes, not the shadow under her feet, went forward and swallowed the bullet as it was.

Tang, tang, tang!

Even if she pulled the trigger until the magazine was empty, the result would not change. Becky dropped the empty pistol on the floor and stared blankly at Yuria. She had no next move. In the first place, she wasn't a combat specialist.

Yuria took a dagger and approached her.

"You were the maid who followed Prince William back then. I have a lot to ask. I hope you know a lot."

Yuria's dagger mercilessly cut her apart. Her torture, which surpassed the level of an expert, only gave Becky pain. Becky couldn't last even 3 minutes and spilled the information. Yuria listened to all the information and sighed.

"The information you spat out has no connection. If you look at each one individually, it seems plausible, but if you put the information together and look for connections, you can see that it has no substance."

"I, I only told the truth!"

"Yes. I know. That's why I was a little impressed. To think you'd use a method of controlling information by brainwashing yourself. Whose idea was it? You or William? Either way, that thoroughness is admirable."

Yuria knew that she couldn't undo Becky's brainwashing. Brainwashing itself is the power of the devil. You can't undo that brainwashing with ordinary methods. Yuria's dagger was accurately embedded in Becky's forehead.

Yuria turned around. Rita, Princess Elza's head bodyguard, was looking at Yuria with wary eyes. There was a sealed moonstone on her waist.

"You were hiding your strength?"

"No. I was lucky enough to awaken my ability this time. It's an ability to control shadows."

"You became level 7 as soon as you awakened your ability, and you want me to believe that?"

"Even if you don't believe it, that's the truth. Today is the day the dark moon rises, isn't it?"

Anything can happen on the day the dark moon rises.
