General Bae Un scattered his energy in all directions. Martial artists, including me, trembled for a moment at his overwhelming aura, like a tidal wave. An instinctive fear began to creep up. I circulated my inner energy, shaking off the aura that bound my body.

,As the administrator of the Myriad Demon Tower, I commend you all for passing the trial of the 10th floor and ascending to the 11th floor."

,You must be wondering who the person next to me is. Those of you who have reached the 10th floor are qualified to know this person's identity."

An old woman stepped forward. Her gait, eyes, attire-everything about her was elegant. Seong Jigone's eyes had been fixed on the old woman since earlier, unable to look away. Drool dripped from his mouth.

,It seems some of you have already guessed. I am called Neung Soryeo. I am more famously known in the world as Guwol Seonja (Nine Moon Fairy)."

Guwol Seonja. The owner of the Nine Moon Spirit Sword Technique (Guwollyeonggeombeop), the martial art offered as a reward for conquering the 20th floor of the Myriad Demon Tower, and a master who has reached the realm of Creation (Johwa), rumored to be already dead in the Jianghu.

,I faked my death. I won't reveal the reason, but if you conquer the Myriad Demon Tower, the Nine Moon Spirit Sword Technique will be given as a reward."

Neung Soryeo closed her mouth as if she had said all she needed to say. Contrary to the elegant atmosphere she exuded, her lips showed a hint of stubbornness.

I easily understood Neung Soryeo's purpose. She didn't die and placed her unique martial art in the Myriad Demon Tower? And she's watching the entrants of the Myriad Demon Tower? The answer isn't difficult to figure out.

,Neung Soryeo doesn't have any disciples. She's looking for one.'

Even if I conquer the Myriad Demon Tower, I shouldn't choose the Nine Moon Spirit Sword Technique. It's hard to believe that Guwol Seonja would willingly give away her unique martial art.

,If I have to become her disciple by force, it's better not to choose it.'

There was no reason to become the disciple of such an old woman. Of course, Seong Jigone next to me might think differently.

General Bae Un regained control of the atmosphere.

,I will give those of you who have passed the 10th floor the opportunity to work for His Majesty the Emperor. Initially, you will work as non-commissioned officers, but with the support of the military, you can receive elixirs and martial arts techniques, and the support will increase as you accumulate merits. Depending on what you do, you may even reach the same position as me."

It was a recruitment drive.

Bae Un, who seemed taciturn, was surprisingly good at talking. He emphasized that it was a stable job with support in exchange for loyalty and a monthly salary.

Starting as a non-commissioned officer and becoming a general. It's a great idea. I imagine myself becoming a general, commanding hundreds of thousands, millions of soldiers. But those aiming for the position of general aren't just these people. Those with strong backgrounds from prestigious families or sects will also be aiming for the position of general.

,Even in modern times, military life is said to be hell... and you want me to live a military life in this world where there's no concept of human rights and no one cares even if people die? That's like diving into hell.'

But the people of this world seem to think differently from me. Already, about 100 people have given up on the trials and enlisted in the military.

,Don't forget that the doors of the military are always open."

Following that, General Bae Un continued with the explanation of the 11th floor trial.

The trials became more dangerous as we ascended the floors. The number of entrants who reached the 17th floor exceeded about 300. They had all reached the Realm of Emerging Earth as a baseline, and more than 50 of them had reached the Five Energies (Ogi) like me. Having experienced the brink of death several times, their skills rose vertically with the martial arts and elixirs obtained as rewards for passing the trials.

,300 people on the 17th floor... Amazing. It's a phenomenal record. This trial must have been especially difficult... Is it the effect of the Nine Moon Spirit Sword Technique?"

,Pay attention. I'll only say this once. The terrain drawn on this map is the terrain of the 17th floor trial. The 17th floor is a free-for-all, and the starting location is determined randomly."

Only 100 out of 300 people will ascend to the 18th floor. Of course, 100 is the maximum. There will be cases where people can't find the beads.
