_6. God's Atlantis

376. God's Atlantis

"I didn't sense any mana being used... how did you defeat the banshee?"

"Did you forget what we dwarves are good at?"

"......What does drinking beer have to do with it? Was the banshee's weakness beer, perhaps?"

"......."

A brief silence fell between Damonse-nim and me. Damonse-nim then opened his mouth and laughed heartily.

"Haha. That's a funny joke. It's true that we dwarves love beer. But that's not the answer. The answer is the equipment I have. If you can't catch a banshee, why not make a weapon that lets you catch it?"

"I see. Is the curse not working because of the chainmail armor?"

"That's right. The banshee's curse isn't that strong, so just wearing this is enough to withstand it."

"There was powder left where the banshee disappeared. What is that powder?"

"As you can see, it's banshee powder. If you use this, you can make a sword sharper, or make a cloak that blocks the cold you want."

Damonse-nim shook the powder in the bottle. It was a fine blue powder.

"What happens if you eat it?"

"I don't know that."

"Hmm."

I decided to feed the banshee powder to thieves or prisoners later. It might be a drug more potent than heroin or philopon.

After that, the deeper we went into the Ironwood Forest, the more monsters attacked us. It was easier to deal with banshees flying in the air than monstrous rats crawling faster than cockroaches. The banshees weren't as agile as I thought, maybe they were being careless.

"It's easy having a warrior of your caliber with me."

"Damonse-nim is no slouch either."

"I know it's flattery, but it's nice to hear."

I didn't even go near the ironwood trees that the banshees possessed. If I saw an Ironwood Spirit, I immediately avoided it.

"Are you just leaving the Ironwood Spirits alone?"

"Eventually, the banshees will release their possession and come out of the ironwood tree on their own anyway. To defeat the Ironwood Spirit, you have to cut down the steel-like tree in one go, but where would we find the time for that?"

"Why don't you just set it on fire?"

"Do you think iron will catch fire?"

"......"

We found the white ironwood tree we were aiming for.

It was more than three times bigger than other ironwood trees and pure white.

"What should we do now?"

To my question, Damonse-nim grabbed the iron axe hanging on his back.

"Of course, we have to hack at it until the sap comes out. Then we insert a steel straw. I'll hack at the white ironwood tree, so please protect me. Monsters always get in the way whenever I'm hacking at a tree..."

It was the moment Damonse-nim was walking towards it.

*Kilkilkilkil.*

An eerie laughter was heard, and the sharp branches of the white ironwood tree were swung like whips. Damonse-nim was startled and dodged to the side, running towards me.

"Heok! What the hell!"

"Is it a banshee?"

"Normally, a banshee can't possess a white ironwood tree. It's the Banshee Queen that's possessing it."

Damonse-nim glared fiercely at the white ironwood tree. His expression was full of annoyance, but he didn't rush in recklessly.

"Today's a bust. Let's go back to the village."

"Are there no other white ironwood trees?"

"There are, but they're too far away from here. It's late, so it's better to go back to the village today and go to the place where the other white ironwood tree is tomorrow."

To think I have to go back empty-handed because of one monster... I didn't like it.

"Damonse-nim. No matter how I think about it, I don't think we should just back down after coming all the way here. Let's drag the Banshee Queen out and kill it."

"Didn't you see the branches attacking me earlier? That attack will become faster and more powerful the further inside you go. On top of that, the white ironwood tree is much harder than a normal ironwood tree. Unless the Banshee Queen comes out, we have to hack at the white ironwood tree... Ugh. It just makes me sigh."

Certainly, even I would have a hard time with that. To hack at that white ironwood tree with an axe would take several... no, at least several hours. And that's while avoiding the attacks of the Ironwood Spirit.

"Damonse-nim. Fire doesn't stick to iron, but... electricity flows through it."

I created lightning in my grasp. Damonse-nim's eyes widened and he nodded.

"Certainly... but you won't be able to burn the white ironwood tree with that."

"This isn't just any lightning. I think it will work on the Banshee Queen possessing the white ironwood tree as well."

If you kill the possessed creature, the possessing banshee dies with it. That was a characteristic of the banshees in this world.

I threw lightning at the white ironwood tree. A blue bolt struck the trunk of the white ironwood tree. Electricity spread throughout the tree.

*Pazzzzzzzzzzut!*

*Kkiaaaaaaaak!*

The lightning and the Banshee Queen's scream overlapped.

"Ooh! It's working!"

"Haha. My lightning is a whopping S rank! Do you think the Banshee Queen can withstand my lightning!"

*Pazzzzzzzzzzut!*

*Kkyyaaaaaaaak!*

Every time I threw lightning, I heard the Banshee Queen's scream. I got excited and threw lightning in succession.

The enraged Banshee Queen controlled the branches of the white ironwood tree. The branches reached out towards me, but stopped at a distance of 1M. The place where Damonse-nim and I were was out of reach of the White Ironwood Spirit's attack.

"Hahahaha! It's so much fun attacking unilaterally! This is my kind of thing!"

"Banshee Queen! You're the troublesome monster that's been bothering us! Do more! More!"

The attack continued as Damonse-nim cheered me on.

Then, as if the Banshee Queen couldn't take it anymore, she released her possession on her own and shot out of the white ironwood tree.

She was about 1.5 times bigger than a normal banshee and wore a cracked crown on her head.

*Kkiaaaaaaaak!*

The banshee screamed and ran towards us.

"Damonse-nim!"

"Don't worry!"

Damonse-nim stepped in front of me and swung his hammer. The hammer hit, but there was no sound. The Banshee Queen seemed to fly to the side, but soon changed direction and rushed towards me.

*Pajijijik!*

I created lightning in my hand. Even so, the Banshee Queen didn't back down.

*Kkiaaaaaaaak!*

She screamed in pain and clung to my body, then went *sswuk* into my body. I was protecting my body with mana, but was it because she was the Banshee Queen? I couldn't stop the possession.

「You have been possessed by the Banshee Queen.」

「Your excellent mental strength resists the Banshee Queen's possession.」

「The Banshee Queen's possession has not been lifted.」

Originally, from the moment you are possessed, you lose consciousness and your body is completely taken over by the banshee.

But right now, I was perfectly conscious. The problem was that I couldn't control my body as I pleased. And that was the same for the Banshee Queen.

"Good heavens! Possession!"

Damonse-nim was horrified and stepped away from me. It was a good decision.

"D-Damonse-nim...!"

"Heok?! Banshee Queen, you bastard! What trick are you trying to pull!"

"No! Damonse-nim! It's me! Seong Yujin! The Banshee Queen hasn't dominated my mind!"

"...Is such a thing possible?"

"Aren't you seeing it now! The Banshee Queen and I are fighting for control of the body!"

I shook my body like a patient having a seizure. My limbs moved grotesquely. When the Banshee Queen possessing my body tried to walk forward, I moved backward. When I tried to create mana, the Banshee Queen interfered.

"...I'm going crazy. What should I do?"

"That's..."

I don't know either. This was my first time being possessed, and I had never looked into possession in detail.

「The King of the Demonic Realm donates 1,000 AP.

"Do you want help? The condition is to kill one person a day for the next 100 days."」

I was annoyed by the King of the Demonic Realm's offer.

"Shut up!!"

"Why are you suddenly like this? Is my voice that loud?"

"Ah, no. I wasn't talking to Damonse-nim! The Banshee Queen is trying to seduce me without knowing her place!"

"That evil thing! Don't fall for the Banshee Queen's seduction!"

「The Lord of the Sky donates 10,000 AP.

"How long are you going to keep up this farce? You're not really being taken down by a Banshee Queen, are you?"」

"......"

I racked my brain. Judging from the Lord of the Sky's lukewarm reaction, I might already have a way to break free from the Banshee Queen's possession.

"Seong Yujin! I'll break your legs and arms first and take you to the village! We'll go back to the village and find a way to undo the possession slowly!"

"Heok! What are you talking about!"

Is this crazy dwarf out of his mind!

"There's no other way!"

"No! Wait a minute! Please wait calmly! Lightning!"

*Pajijijik!*

Electricity flows from my body. The movement of my body and mana is still being interfered with, but the lightning was activated because it was a skill. Electricity flowed through my body, but the Banshee Queen was not harmed. This is because my body itself is not harmed by lightning.

'Use Deceit (SS) to change the status window to Cheonma? No, it doesn't seem like it will work because the body isn't changing...'

Is Cheonma Shingong the answer? Maybe the Cheonma physique has resistance to possession.

'...Judging from the Lord of the Sky's reaction, there is a way! ...Ah!'

I got a grasp of it.

"Lightning Speed (Blindingly Fast)!"

I activate the skill I most recently obtained in the 'God's Atlantis' world.

My body turns into an electric body. As my body became lightning, its shape temporarily vanished.

*Kkieeek?!*

The possession was lifted. The Banshee Queen was flustered in front of me.

"Ten Thousand Thunderbolts!"

Lightning tore the Banshee Queen's body apart.

「You acquire 410 AP.」

Powder and a bracelet were dropped where the Banshee Queen disappeared.

"Amazing! To think you would kill the Banshee Queen in such a way!"

Lightning Speed was released after 3 seconds, and I returned to my original body. I picked up the bracelet and powder that had fallen on the floor.

「Banshee Queen's Powder

A high-quality material used in many places.

Has the effect of blocking the cold. Can reveal what is invisible.

Rank: B」

「Banshee Queen's Bracelet

Can only be used by women.

Depending on mana, you can fly freely in the sky for a certain period of time.

Mana+5

Rank: A」

I don't know much about the Banshee Queen's powder yet, but the Banshee Queen's bracelet was quite a good item. The ability to fly can be used in various ways, and the effect of increasing mana stats is good. Although there is a condition that only women can use it.

'I should give this as a gift... Who should I give it to?'

Damonse-nim approached. I quickly hid the bracelet behind my back.

"Don't worry. I have no intention of coveting your things. Are you okay?"

"Yes. I'm fine."

"...Can you give me the Banshee Queen's powder? I'm not asking for it for free. I'll pay you the price..."

"I'll think about it. Let's collect the white ironwood sap first."

Judging from his reaction, the Banshee Queen's powder was not a material that only blacksmiths could use. It seemed like I could sell it for a higher price in the city.

Damonse-nim asked while collecting the sap.

"You're not going to sell the Banshee Queen's powder? I'll pay you generously."

"Here, have a bottle of beer."

"Kiyaaaaaa! As expected, beer after a battle is special!"

I often visited the Ironwood Forest.

This is because it is more beneficial to train Cheonma Shingong in the cool air of the Ironwood Forest than to waste time meaninglessly in the village.

That day, I was diligently killing monster rats and banshees and training.

I sensed a presence that was different from usual.

'...What is it?'

It was a presence that felt much clearer and darker than the monster rats and banshees. I moved towards the place where I felt the presence.

A black smoke-like thing remained in the air and was gradually dissipating. I moved at a fast pace. The further I walked, the thicker the black smoke became.

And finally, I was able to find it.

It was a black horse wrapped in black smoke on its eight legs.

「World Leap (Fake) is looking at you.」

*Umjjeol.*

I paused.

World Leap. Real name Sleipnir.

A dignity that appears in various areas. You can tame it, but you have to fight and win. And even if you tame it, it will disappear immediately if it determines that you are not qualified to be its master.

In the original story, Kang Myungjin kills Sleipnir as soon as he encounters it. He had a dragon, so he had no regrets about Sleipnir.

'...Can I kill it with my current skills?'

「The Lord of the Sky donates 100,000 AP.

"It's dog food's favorite horse. Kill it."」

100,000 AP.

The Lord of the Sky was more sincere than ever.

'Oh well, whatever!'

I couldn't afford to be at odds with the Lord of the Sky here right now. I shot lightning with Ten Thousand Thunderbolts.

"Pooheeing."

Sleipnir lightly twisted its body to avoid the lightning, laughed at me, and ran towards the sky. I tried to chase it, but it was already too late. Sleipnir disappeared.

「World Leap (Fake) remembers you.」
