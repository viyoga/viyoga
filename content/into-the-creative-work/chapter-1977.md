Fafnir trembled so pitifully, it didn't suit his massive frame. Blood dripped from his wounded body.

「Gram

Sword that kills dragons.

Rank: SSS」

Gram.

An SSS-rank sword. For Atlantis's top-tier sword, its effect was excessively simple. But that's why, when it came to dragon slaying, no one could match this sword. Even Fafnir was feeling fear just by looking at Gram.

-That sword! How do you have that sword!!

And now, the Gram in my hand was even imbued with Astrape. Odin's Gram and Zeus's Astrape. Owners from different mythologies were involved.

I swung the sword towards Fafnir, who was far away. Sword energy, crackling with lightning, flew towards Fafnir. Fafnir, terrified, forcibly twisted his body to fly. It was meaningless. As if equipped with a guidance system, the sword energy also twisted, managing to slice off one of his wings.

Kwaaaang!

Fafnir fell to the ground. Due to his weight, it created an enormous roar.

"Don't even think about dodging. Gram exhibits tremendous performance if the target is a dragon. Just like right now."

Gram didn't have a sword energy guidance function or anything like that. I didn't use any special tricks either. It was just that what I swung was Gram, and the opponent was a dragon, so this happened.

Of course, just holding Gram didn't grant me absolute authority to kill all dragon species. I, who was using the sword, had to be capable to a certain extent.

I walked towards Fafnir, who had fallen to the ground and was trembling with fear. It was possible to kill Fafnir with just sword energy. The fact that I had just sliced off one of his large wings with sword energy was proof of that.

'My goal is the dragon's byproducts. So, I need to kill him as cleanly as possible.'

If I directly cut and stab his body with this sword, he would die instantly. Because this sword is a sword that kills dragons.

'I can see his reverse scale. I don't necessarily have to stab the reverse scale to kill him, but... stabbing the reverse scale would kill him more certainly.'

-Y, you bastard!

Fafnir, who had fallen to the ground, opened his mouth. He squeezed out his last bit of strength to spew out a curse breath.

Pajijijik.

I swung Gram, crackling with lightning, towards the curse breath.

Gram even sliced through the dragon's breath.

-Uwaaaaaaaak! This is unfair!!

Fafnir shouted, filled with injustice. I understand. Even I would be pissed if an enemy had a sword like that. But I'm the one holding the sword? I grinned and approached him.

It was then. Someone came running from the collapsed cave.

It was Omikos.

The blood-soaked Omikos was nothing like his usual dignified self. He just looked like an ordinary soldier craving life.

'No. He's more greedy than that.'

If he really wanted to live, he should have run in a different direction, not this way.

"The contract! Haven't you forgotten the contract?! You said you'd let me be covered in Fafnir's blood!"

"The contract has been terminated. The grim reaper chasing you is proof of that."

The grim reaper belatedly chased after Omikos. Judging from the blood flowing on that scythe, it was clear that he had been killing the other mercenaries. Perhaps Omikos was the only mercenary who survived.

"The betrayal wasn't me, it was the members!"

"And you are the leader of the mercenary group."

"I, I'll do anything! Please, let me be covered in Fafnir's blood as per the contract!"

I chuckled as I looked at the unsightly Omikos and nodded.

"Alright. Do as you please."

"Ah, ah! Thank you! I will never forget this grace!"

Omikos rushed towards the fallen Fafnir. Fafnir had no strength to resist. He could only glare at the approaching Omikos.

-You damn bug!

Omikos took off his clothes while running and became naked in an instant. Then, he stuck to Fafnir's body and smeared that blood all over himself. Fafnir was covered in wounds, so it wasn't difficult for Omikos to apply the blood.

The grim reaper approached Omikos and swung his scythe. Omikos was surprised and dodged to the side, but because he was distracted by Fafnir's blood, his reaction was slightly slow.

Seogeok!

Omikos's knee was cut.

Omikos, who had collapsed to the ground with a thud, distorted his face, then suddenly discovered something and burst into laughter.

"Ha, hahahahahahaha! I am now immortal! I will not die!"

It seemed like he had seen the system notification he wanted.

'Has he really become immortal?'

I looked back and forth between the dying Fafnir and Omikos, who was lying on the ground and laughing gleefully.

'Doesn't seem that great.'

I possessed endless wisdom now. In my eyes, the immortality gained from Fafnir's blood was nothing special.

'Omikos will have been granted dragon characteristics. Even if he's immortal, I should be able to kill him easily with Gram.'

To borrow the words of the gods, perfect immortality could never be bestowed upon a mortal.

'There is no such thing as perfection in this world. Even the gods themselves are not perfect.'

Even immortality would have its limitations. I didn't know what those limitations were, and I didn't intend to find out directly. Because I wasn't interested.

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaak!"

Omikos screamed. The grim reaper's scythe struck Omikos's back with all its might. The scythe pierced Omikos's heart accurately.

The grim reaper floating in the air tilted his skull. Because Omikos, who should have died, did not die.

Kwak! Kwak! Kwaak!

The grim reaper swung the scythe like a pickaxe, repeatedly striking Omikos's back. However, as soon as the scythe was removed, the wound on his back began to heal rapidly.

"Aaaaaak! My legs! Why aren't my legs regenerating?!"

He shouted as if he was dying from injustice.

Indeed, unlike the wound on his back, both of his shins were not regenerating.

'He lost his legs before becoming immortal. That's the only reason.'

The grim reaper continued to swing the scythe. The grim reaper I saw was a kind of machine. A machine without emotions. It was just called a grim reaper, but it was closer to an undead monster.

"Aak! Ack! Kkeuak!"

Undead and machines had something in common. That was that they didn't get tired.

'The grim reaper won't stop until the price of the contract is paid. To stop that, I have to kill the grim reaper.'

Omikos, who had lost both legs, had his movements restricted. Even if that wasn't the case, he didn't have the strength to move now.

'He doesn't seem to die from simple attacks... This is quite something. An infinite hell has been completed.'

He seemed to know that himself, as his face was full of despair.

"Yujin-nim! Help me! Just that grim reaper! Please do something about the grim reaper! Uwaaaak!"

-Kekeke. Serves you right, you stupid bug! That's the price for not knowing your place and coveting immortality!

Fafnir mocked Omikos.

Jeobeok.

As I approached Fafnir, his laughter disappeared. Fafnir, seeing Gram in my hand, seemed to have resigned himself and shouted.

-You!! Don't think this is the end! My main body will curse you!

"What do you think about him saying that?"

「The Golden Collector shakes his head.」

「The Golden Collector lowers his head and tries to sleep as if he's annoyed.」

"Keke. The main body has lost interest in this. It doesn't seem like he has any will to avenge you?"

-You crazy bastard! How can you sleep in this situation?! Curse that arrogant human!

There was no real answer from Fafnir.

In the first place, the avatars were created for amusement. The gods, who were their main bodies and originals, had no particular feelings for the avatars. The avatars were devices for the gods' amusement. The gods must have finished talking and agreed on issues related to the avatars before Atlantis even started.

-You who killed me will never die peacefully! Just like Siegfried, you too will meet a miserable end! Gold! Curse that greedy mortal!!

"All the gold you've collected so far is mine. I tasted the gold earlier... it tasted amazing. You are furious, calling me a bastard."

-Youuuuuuuuuuuuuuuu!!!!

I stabbed Gram into his reverse scale.

Kook.

Lightly.

As lightly as stabbing with a toothpick.

But the result that followed was by no means light. Gram emitted golden light. It even dyed Astrape's lightning golden and began to tear Fafnir apart.

Fafnir opened his mouth to scream, but the scream didn't come out of his mouth. Because he died before that. Fafnir was gone like that.

「You have killed an evil dragon.」

「You are granted the title 'Dragon Slayer'.」

「All stats permanently increase by 1.」

「You have obtained dominion over the 1,399th district, the Rhine River.」

「You have acquired 170,000 AP.」

「As the ruler of the Rhine River, you will acquire 40,000 AP every 30 days.」

「The Rhine River is a hidden district. You can hide or open the district.」

「If opened, trade will begin and the district will develop rapidly.」

「Once a district is opened, it cannot be hidden. Would you like to open it?」

"Open it? For whose benefit."

The district develops rapidly? The Rhine River was just one of the districts I had now. I could develop other places, so why should I bother developing this place?

'It's a district where gold comes out. Moreover, it's not a district in the backwoods. Nearby Legions can invade as much as they want if they set their minds to it.'

Hiding it, as I had done until now, was the best option. I should suck the honey myself.

'It's truly a goose that lays golden eggs.'

A district where gold comes out just by having it. I had no intention of letting others know about it.

'The Omikos Mercenary Group has been annihilated, so there's no need to worry about rumors spreading.'

No, there was still one person left.

Omikos, the leader of the mercenary group. Even now, his back was being struck by the grim reaper's scythe.

'I didn't have any particular ill feelings towards this bastard... He got greedy on his own and fell into a living hell.'

Omikos reached out to me.

"H, help me. Please...!"

"No. You broke the contract first. You have to bear the consequences."

"I didn't betray you!"

"Either way, you broke the contract. The grim reaper swinging his scythe at your back is proof of that."

"At the very least, please kill me!"

"How am I supposed to kill you when you're immortal? Just live like that."

"Uwaaaaaaaaaaaak!"
