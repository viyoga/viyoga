The world swept away by the Great Flood was silent.

At least in my world, all humans except Pandora had disappeared.

Pandora, the first woman, was pregnant with my child, but there wasn't enough time to make her the start of humanity. How long would it take for one person to become 1,000?

,1,000 people are woefully insufficient to create a myth.'

I don't know the population of ancient Greece. But to create a myth, it's obvious that you need at least hundreds of thousands of people.

,I have to create humans.'

I originally intended to wipe them out with that in mind. In the actual original history, humans were newly created.

,Did I make humans out of stones?'

There was a goddess who was an expert in this field.

I went straight to Gaia and asked her.

"Create humans."

"......Zeus. Weren't you the one who eliminated all the humans? Now you want me to create humans again?"

"You know, even if I don't, that humans have an important role. I need humans to be worshiped."

"Yeah, right. But are you going to wipe them out again if you don't like them? If that's the case, is there any need to create humans?"

"I swear on the River Styx that I will not exterminate humans."

The newly created humans will be distinctly different from the previous humans. Pandora's box has already been opened and spread to the world, so humans will not enjoy glory. Now, humans will have to make more difficult choices for survival.

"I don't trust you, but... it's a problem not having humans."

Gaia sighed and gave me stones.

"If you scatter these on the ground, humans will be created."

"It's not enough. Give me more."

"...Okay. Is this much enough?"

"Give me more."

"......Here you go."

"More."

"If there are too many humans, the human realm can become chaotic."

Gaia's face was firm.

Even though Gaia and I had a decent relationship, it wasn't exactly a great one. Gaia, in particular, was stubborn. I guess it's because she's much higher up than Zeus in terms of status, if not power.

I had no choice but to go outside. Riding the clouds, I traveled around the world and scattered the stones I received from Gaia in various places. The stones became people. They were at the level of primitive cavemen, but with the help of the gods, they would develop quickly.

This world was still the age of the gods.

• • •

「Time is accelerating.」

Various things happened. No matter who I am, I couldn't interfere in everything. Just because it's Greek mythology doesn't mean I, as Zeus, had to be involved in everything.

I look down at the ground from the sky.

I could see humans.

They build cities, enthrone kings, and worship gods.

I didn't like the fact that kingdoms, which were only the size of cities, were being built haphazardly. It felt cluttered. Humans had developed, but it seemed like it would take a very long time to develop beyond that.

It was one day.

Poseidon came to Olympus. Soaked in seawater, Poseidon sat in one of the twelve seats without a care. I narrowed my brows and glared at Poseidon.

"You reek of the sea. Can't you wash up a bit before coming?"

"Zeus, my brother. The sea is dangerous."

"What are you talking about?"

I couldn't understand him because he was talking out of the blue. I checked the sea with the eyes of the sky. The sea was the same as usual.

"I just looked at the sea. There's nothing particularly special about it."

"It doesn't look like it on the surface. The sea is different from the land. All sorts of monsters still remain."

"I gave you the Trident so you could rule the sea. Haven't you completely conquered the sea yet?"

"It's the same reason you haven't dominated all of the sky. Many gods' interests are at stake in this sea."

Unfortunately, this world wasn't just filled with the gods of Greek mythology. It was mixed with Norse mythology, Egyptian mythology, and other regional mythologies. That was the reason I couldn't dominate all of the sky. I could only dominate the sky within the realm of Greek mythology. Similarly, Hades couldn't dominate the entire underworld either.

"It's different for you. I have dominated all of the sky within my realm. What about you? Aren't you failing to dominate even the places you should be dominating?"

"Zeus. I only discovered this recently... There's a monster in the sea. It's sleeping in a very, very deep place. It's not just a simple monster. You could call it a god of the deep sea. It existed before we were born... It wouldn't be strange to consider it a primordial god."

"If it's a god, it's a god. If it's a monster, it's a monster. Decide on one."

"It is a god and also a monster."

Poseidon said seriously. It didn't seem like he was spouting nonsense.

"This god is asleep, but it's creating monsters. It's a very troublesome god."

"Is it dangerous?"

"Not really. They're weaker than me and my children. They're only slightly stronger than humans, so as long as I'm here, they won't be able to come out of the sea."

"So it's not a serious matter."

"Not for now. But I'm worried. If you see that monster, you'll understand my worries."

Clang!

Poseidon struck the floor with his Trident. Immediately, a barrier of seawater appeared. The seawater didn't splash everywhere but flowed from bottom to top and circulated. The surface of the seawater showed a video like a TV.

The very depths of the sea.

There was a giant fish-man. The fish-man was curled up in the deep sea like a fetus in its mother's womb, fast asleep.

I gasped as soon as I saw him.

,It's Dagon from the Cthulhu Mythos.'

Why is this bastard here? I was beyond surprised; I was dumbfounded.

,Was he originally here? Or did the system pull some trick?'

If the system pulled some trick?

,This might be the real purpose of the trial.'

I thought about Dagon. It's not like I know the Cthulhu Mythos in detail. However, I do know that those gods are all ridiculously strong. But Dagon didn't seem that strong somehow. If all the gods of Olympus joined forces, wouldn't they be able to kill one Dagon?

"Poseidon, have you tried attacking him?"

"Of course, I have. But my attacks didn't work. An unknown force was protecting him. I could probably inflict damage if I used force... but I didn't bother."

"Why?"

"It felt ominous. I had a bad feeling."

An ominous premonition.

I would have scoffed if a human had said that. But if the owner of the ominous premonition is Poseidon, I can't ignore it.

"It's not immediately dangerous, so let's keep an eye on it for now. But don't let your guard down."

"I think all the gods should join forces to kill him."

That was the reason Poseidon came to Olympus after so long.

"We don't have enough information. It's not too late to act after we get enough information."

There was a lack of information.

It was far too lacking.

• • •

In the real Olympus, which exists somewhere in the universe, Hades and the twelve gods were all gathered. But no one could easily open their mouths.

A variable had occurred in Seong Yujin's trial.

They had predicted that a variable would occur. Seong Yujin, who had become Zeus, had a personality that was different from ordinary people. Moreover, Seong Yujin already knew the future of the myth. It would have been stranger if a variable hadn't occurred.

It was more surprising that there weren't any major variables in the content.

"System."

Zeus broke the silence and opened his mouth.

「Lord of the Sky, speak.」

"The content of the trial has changed. Gods other than us should not interfere in the trial we set. Of course, it's absurd for there to be any trace of them. Did you dare to change the content of the trial?"

「The trial reflects all of your opinions.」

"Are you daring to play tricks?"

「I do not lie. Seong Yujin's trial was created with the power and opinions of all of you.」

Zeus swallowed a groan.

Unless the system had gone crazy, it wouldn't lie. Zeus was also present when the system was created. Zeus was one of the gods who knew the most about the system.

"It seems like there's a traitor among us."

"......"

The gods looked at each other.

There were no loud voices among them. No one uttered a suspicious word. Only a chilling silence lingered.

Nothing is permanent. The gods are no exception. Countless years were enough to change the gods.

It was Apollo who opened his mouth then.

"Can we call this betrayal? To me, it just looks like a prank. That's all I see."

Aphrodite crossed her arms. Her face was uncomfortable, and she glared.

"A prank? A foreign god, not just any god, interfered. Does that look like a prank to you? You know the heterogeneity of foreign gods very well, don't you? The thought that someone among us has joined hands with a foreign god makes me sick. Apollo. Is it you by any chance?"

"Aphrodite-nim. I will not tolerate any more insults than that."

Apollo and Aphrodite glared at each other.

Next to speak was Artemis.

"If the system that created the trial based on our power and opinions, it must know the culprit. Let's ask the system. System. Who is the traitor? Who brought another mythology into the trial?"

「I cannot tell you because I respect the rights of all divine seats. Also, my duty is to manage Atlantis. I cannot interfere in matters outside of Atlantis.」

Artemis gripped her bow. But that was all. Even she couldn't physically do anything to the system.

Ares rested his chin on his hand and said in a bothersome tone.

"It doesn't seem like a big deal. Even if a foreign god's influence entered the trial, we're not really losing anything, are we?"

It was Athena who refuted Ares.

"Our reputation is at stake. If this fact leaks out... the other gods will laugh at us. Ares. You're always short-sighted."

"You think too much unnecessarily. Have you figured out the traitor with your great wisdom?"

"......"

Wisdom and omniscience were different. No matter how wise Athena was, she didn't know all the answers. Especially when it came to matters related to gods. Athena and Ares glared at each other. The half-siblings hadn't been on good terms for a very long time.
