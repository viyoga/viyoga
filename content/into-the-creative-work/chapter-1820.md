I ran towards the Cheonma Mausoleum.

Baekran-ssi's deadline of fifteen days. Thinking about it now, it was a tight schedule. There was no guarantee that I could master the Cheonma Shingong in one go. So, to minimize travel time as much as possible, I ran to the Cheonma Mausoleum with all my might.

,Damn it. I'm not getting enough speed.'

I'm running faster than most cars. I am a master of the Fifth Realm, after all. But by my standards, it was slow.

,It's because I don't have Light Body Technique.'

A martial art that makes the body lighter. It's also called Light Body Technique or lightness skill. In this world, it could be seen as a martial art dedicated to running.

,I have space-warping scrolls and a space-warping system, so I haven't really needed it until now.'

I didn't have much reason to run around in a hurry. If I was in a hurry, I'd rather take a carriage; running around like that wasn't my style.

,I should have gotten a magical artifact for transportation.'

It wasn't like I could get a magical artifact just because I wanted one. Most magical artifacts for transportation are quite expensive. It's because this world is damn wide.

,The Lightning Sound Steps of the Lightning Heaven Style can run fast in an instant... but I can't maintain it for long.'

The Lightning Sound Steps are for short-distance running, and what I need now is a lightness skill for marathons.

,Ah, right. I have martial arts I've obtained. There must be a lightness skill among them.'

Without stopping, I took out martial arts manuals from my spatial storage.

Supreme King Divine Fist, Severance of Awakening Sword, Undying Heart Method.

I quickly scanned the three manuals I had taken out at random. Originally, with my talent, I wouldn't have been able to grasp even one of these three properly... but I was different now. Maybe it was because of my VIP Level 3 status in the Cheongang Star System, or maybe it was thanks to reaching the Fifth Realm, but just by skimming through them, I could guess the power and profundity of the martial arts.

,Among the ones that are easy to get, these are the better martial arts... but they lack depth. I wouldn't call them ascending martial arts. Especially the Supreme King Divine Fist. It's all flash and no substance; it's the worst of the bunch.'

I threw away the Supreme King Divine Fist without hesitation. It wasn't even worth one Cheonok. The others were at least worth more than one Cheonok.

,There's no lightness skill among these. Let's look at something else.'

After rummaging through my spatial storage a few more times, I found a lightness skill.

Fragrant Wind Lightness.

It was one of the top ten martial arts I had obtained from the market.

,Running like a fragrance carried on the wind...'

As you can tell from the name, it's a wind-attribute lightness skill. It's suitable for martial artists who handle wind energy, but... it wasn't like I, who was born with lightning energy, couldn't learn it at all.

,I only need it for now, so I shouldn't delve too deeply into lightness skills.'

I read the manual while running. Even though I couldn't concentrate, the contents of the manual entered my eyes easily. It felt like each phrase was being directly imprinted into my head.

,This must be the effect of the Cheongang Star System. Is it because I'm VIP Level 3? It wasn't like this at Level 2.'

I grasped the key points of Fragrant Wind Lightness. I put the manual back into my spatial storage and recited the incantations of Fragrant Wind Lightness. Lightning energy flowed through my entire body.

My body became lighter. The effect was enormous. Even with just a little force on my feet, I shot forward. The burden on my stamina with each step was greatly reduced.

,This is the basics of Light Body Technique!'

I felt good because I was lighter. But I couldn't be satisfied here. This was just the basics of lightness skill.

,Fragrant Wind Lightness is a lightness skill that utilizes wind. You have to create wind and ride on it.'

You need to be able to use wind energy as a base.

Fortunately, the wind was blowing from behind me. Even without wind energy, I could ride the wind. I entrusted my body to the blowing wind. The wind pushed my back. The important thing here was my legs. I had to move my legs in the manner of Fragrant Wind Lightness.

『Fragrant Wind Lightness is not a suitable martial art for the user.』

The Cheongang Star System warned me.

It was the first time this had happened. Maybe it was because my VIP level had increased.

,I know. I'm just experiencing it. Experiencing.'

I trusted the wind and rode it.

My legs, which had been running on the ground, were now on top of a tree. My feet stepped on thin air. No, this was similar to a bird flapping its wings. Fragrant Wind Lightness was a lightness skill for flying in the sky.

,This is fun!'

Flying in the sky was great, but I was even more pleased that I had mastered Fragrant Wind Lightness in an instant. Even though I was using Fragrant Wind Lightness in a makeshift way.

I was about to burst out laughing. My body fell downwards. It was because the wind that had been blowing from behind had changed direction.

,Crap.'

I wasn't born with wind energy, so I couldn't manipulate the wind as I wanted using ki. That was my limit.

,Can't I create a lightness skill in the style of the Lightning Heaven Style?'

I made my body lighter.

That feeling was somewhat similar to Gale Lightning.

Pajijik.

Lightning Heaven Style Gale Lightning.

I used Gale Lightning as I thought of it.

Lightning Heaven Style Empty Way.

I stepped on the void, balanced my body as it fell to the ground, stepped on the foothold created by lightning energy, and ran forward.

I immediately frowned.

,This isn't it.'

My body became lighter?

It was my mistake. Gale Lightning isn't a technique that makes the body lighter. It's a technique that strengthens the body. I was mistaken in thinking that my body had become lighter because my strength had increased. In reality, the weight of my body hadn't changed at all.

,It does get faster.'

The efficiency was terrible. The fuel consumption was excessively bad. Lightness skill is something you learn to run fast and for a long time. Gale Lightning wasn't suitable for lightness skill.

I released Gale Lightning. If I ran using Gale Lightning, I would be exhausted in less than 10 minutes.

,But I've found a clue.'

The Empty Way consumed surprisingly little lightning energy. As my proficiency increased, I could create just the right amount of foothold. The foothold didn't need to be sturdy, and if my body became lighter with lightness skill, the burden on the foothold would also decrease.

,Fragrant Wind Lightness used wind. Then I can use lightning.'

My head started spinning rapidly.

I resisted the urge to use Genius Time. I had to save it in case I needed it when learning the Cheonma Shingong.

『Assisting the user.』

,Right. This head that's spinning so fast is thanks to you.'

I thought about the characteristics of lightning. It falls. It's fast. It's a straight line.

,...Is it not a straight line?'

When you look at lightning strikes, they fall in a zigzag pattern. They don't draw a straight line. But the lightning I shoot out flies in a straight line.

,Whether it's a straight line or not, it doesn't matter. The important thing is that it extends like lightning. I am lightning.'

I utilized the principles of Light Body Technique.

The weight of my body decreased. It continued to decrease. The wind blew. My lightened body couldn't withstand the wind and soared into the sky. My body continued to become lighter.

This was dangerous.

My body being blown by the wind was nothing. As my weight disappeared, it felt like my existence was disappearing. It felt like I was emptying out. I regained my senses.

,If I'm too light, I'll be affected by air and wind. This will only backfire.'

A moderate weight that wouldn't be shaken by the wind and could receive help from the wind. Finding that weight wasn't difficult.

,What's left... no, what's lacking needs to be filled with lightning.'

Pajijik.

Lightning energy dwelled in my body. But the lightning energy had no directionality.

,Let's go forward.'

I set the direction. I stepped on the Empty Way and moved forward. It was much better than when I used Gale Lightning. This alone could be called a great lightness skill. But compared to Fragrant Wind Lightness, it was crude.

,Fragrant Wind Lightness is a fragrance carried on the wind. Then what am I?'

What should I do? I couldn't find an answer easily. Frustrated, I put strength into my legs. The lightning energy that hadn't been completely contained came out of my body and scattered in all directions. It was like lightning flying through the air.

,That's it!'

I moved my body with lightning energy. A sensation of controlling my body with minute currents instead of nerves. It felt like I had become lightning.

I imagined riding on the flying lightning.

,I already know something similar.'

Lightning Sound Steps. A footwork where thunder rumbles every time you step. Does only thunder rumble? No, a slight current remains in the place where you stepped.

,If I eliminate the thunder from the Lightning Sound Steps and use the Empty Way appropriately...'

My body became lightning and flew into the sky.

Not a footwork, but a Light Body Technique.

I gave this technique a new name.

Lightning Heaven Style Flying Thunder God.

It looked like I was flying like lightning, but in reality, I was running using the Empty Way. Because the movement of my feet was minimal, it looked like I was being shot out.

This wasn't a footwork, but a Light Body Technique.

I instantly rose above the clouds. I looked around. It was a bit difficult to breathe, but I really liked the open view.

I went higher. A height where I could see the universe. At this point, the world should look round. But the ground and sky were flat.

,The world is so damn wide that even after coming up this far, it still looks flat.'

It was too uncomfortable to breathe, so I went down.

Lightning Heaven Style Flying Thunder God.

I started running through the sky using the Flying Thunder God at a moderate height. Because of the lightning energy leaking from my body, others would see me as lightning flying through the sky.

,Hehe. The sense of speed is amazing. It can't even be compared to a motorcycle.'

I didn't know exactly how fast I was going. What was certain was that it was at least over 500 km/h.

As I was flying along, enjoying myself, I saw a battle taking place nearby. Twenty martial artists were fighting each other for their lives on a wide lakeside, so it was very easy to see. Even more so because I was running as if flying in the sky.

,Look at these bastards. Daring to fight in my territory?'

The place where my gaze reached was my territory.

I couldn't just ignore such unexpected events. It was the perfect situation for farming. Kill them all and take their Cheonok, magical artifacts, and martial arts manuals.

,Looking at them, there are no masters of the Fifth Realm; they're all scrubs. It's the perfect situation to test the Flying Thunder God.'

Besides, there was a pretty decent-looking beauty.

Kwang!

I stepped on the Empty Way and changed the direction of the Flying Thunder God. It wasn't easy because I had momentum.

Those who were trying to kill each other stopped fighting when they discovered me. I landed among them.

Tsujujujujujuju!

I tried to land coolly, but my body was pushed back due to the damn inertia.

,Ten Thousand Root Drop! Ten Thousand Root Drop! Ten Thousand Root Drop!'

I actively used the Ten Thousand Root Drop to prevent myself from crashing into a tree.
