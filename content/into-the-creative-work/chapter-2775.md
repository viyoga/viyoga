U.S. President Chester reviewed the documents on his desk. Enclosed were satellite photos.

The CIA Director, standing before him, said with a grave expression.

,Your Excellency. The city where Guileok Gokju Wang Wei was located has vanished. A massive explosion, comparable to a nuclear blast, has been observed."

,Comparable to a nuclear blast, you say. But not a nuclear bomb?"

,The form of the explosion was different from a nuclear blast. Normally, explosions of a certain scale or greater produce mushroom clouds, but no mushroom cloud was observed. However, the city has completely disappeared. CIA experts speculate that the explosion was caused by some kind of special ability or magic."

,Hmm. Not surprising if it's an S-class."

Chester shuffled through the documents.

The CIA, through satellites and special Awakened individuals, had been monitoring the battle between Guileok Gokju and Jeok-kwang. Although they couldn't observe it fully due to interference, they confirmed that Jeok-kwang used lightning and summoned spirits. Based on this, they speculated that Jeok-kwang's identity was Seong Yujin, an academy student from Korea.

Is it even possible for Seong Yujin, who isn't even a formal Hero yet, to be this strong?

,If he's that Seong Hari's son, it's possible."

Various other reasons pointed to Seong Yujin.

,He is dangerous. The CIA believes that Jeok-kwang must be eliminated as quickly as possible."

,Certainly a dangerous power. But aren't all S-classes dangerous? S-classes may have differences in ability, but they are beings with the power to destroy cities. Jeok-kwang isn't the only special one."

,Jeok-kwang is not an American. The mere fact that he is in a country other than the United States inevitably restricts America."

,He is an American. He himself has acknowledged that he will become an American. That means he is not an enemy of the United States."

,The terrorism that American has committed alone amounts to dozens of incidents. The damage amounts to hundreds of millions of dollars!"

Chester had profited tens of millions of dollars thanks to that. It was a kind of stock manipulation. Fortunately, the CIA didn't seem to know. Chester played it cool.

,There were no casualties, were there?"

,No casualties? A Hero sacrificed himself to stop Jeok-kwang, a villain died, and the inmates were all massacred! The number of those who died at Jeok-kwang's hands is in the hundreds!"

,There were no civilians among the victims. The Hero who died? If you investigate, they were all corrupt Heroes. They were in league with criminal organizations, or they were swindlers. Villains are trash that should be disposed of in the first place, and inmates are just freeloaders. Even if there was damage in the short term, it's much more beneficial in the long run. And more than 70% of Americans support Jeok-kwang. These days, the approval rating for Jeok-kwang is even higher than my own."

,Your Excellency...! Jeok-kwang is a criminal! You must not defend him!"

,Director, you're too earnest. How are you going to eliminate Jeok-kwang? Do you have a method?"

,That is..."

The Director's face crumpled.

Eliminating Jeok-kwang, who possessed force at least equivalent to an S-class, was not an easy task. Assassinate him? It was difficult to pinpoint Jeok-kwang's location. Moreover, Jeok-kwang lived in Korea, not the United States.

Overwhelm him with firepower? Jeok-kwang possessed the ability to teleport.

,...I request the assistance of the Chairman of the American Hero Association. If he, an SS-class, takes direct action and the CIA cooperates, even Jeok-kwang can be killed."

,Justin would never step up."

,......"

The CIA Director fell silent at Chester's sarcasm.

The current Chairman of the American Hero Association, Justin Hale, was not on good terms with the federal government. The federal government wanted to manage the Hero Association, and the Hero Association did not want to become the federal government's dog.

,Take a look at Justin's SNS. He's not officially supporting Jeok-kwang... but he's acknowledging the act of killing villains. Seeing how the Association is lukewarm about catching Jeok-kwang, it's clear that Justin has been influencing them. Do you think such a Justin would cooperate?"

,...If Your Excellency approves, even the Chairman will have no choice but to cooperate."

,I have heard your opinion well. Now, as President, I will order the CIA to ignore Jeok-kwang and eliminate the remnants of Guileok Gokju within China. Also, keep a close watch on the movements of the Chinese government. Also, track down the demons within the United States."

Chester said firmly. The CIA Director could not push any further. If he went any further, Chester would surely dismiss him.

,...Understood, Your Excellency."

Chester, left alone in the office, clicked his tongue loudly.

,That CIA Director doesn't know how to see the future. He will become the strongest Hero, and he is my, America's, friend."

He will eventually come to America.

Korea? In Chester's view, the present was okay, but the future was bleak. What good is being a developed country? The birth rate is the lowest. In just 20 years, Korea will be creaking.

,What was the policy the Korean government came up with again? *Chulsshan Jangryeo* Dance? HAHAHA! Amazing!"

,Understood, Seong Hari-nim."

In this world, duels between Heroes were recognized as a kind of sport.

Aurora Simulator.

A highly advanced complex magic that protects users by overlaying a virtual reality onto reality. It was possible because of this technology, which could be called a kind of magical singularity.

With the Aurora Simulator, even if Heroes fought each other to the death, they wouldn't actually die.

However, activating the Aurora Simulator cost a considerable amount of money, so it wasn't used often unless it was a special event. The exception was the Aurora Simulator installed in the Academy. It was used almost daily under the guise of safe practical training at the Academy.

The venue for this public duel with Go Young-jin was the duel arena in Sangam-dong, Seoul. It was a duel arena mainly used for international competitions.

The spectator seats, which could accommodate tens of thousands of people, were completely empty. The superficial reason was that this public duel was arranged too suddenly. In reality, I had rejected the audience.

,Because an accident is going to happen. If there are ordinary spectators, variables will arise."

Therefore, the public duel would be broadcast live on the internet.

That didn't mean there were no spectators at all.

On my side, Seong Hari and a few Association employees were seated. On Go Young-jin's side, Go Pil-du, the Buster Clan leader, and the clan members were seated. I confirmed this through the screen in the waiting room.

15 minutes until the duel begins.

Honestly, there was no possibility of me losing, but I checked my strength anyway.

,Moka and Terra haven't recovered from the aftermath of the forced reverse summoning yet. Inferra is still sulking. The only spirits I can summon are Machina and Arrest, the highest-ranking wind spirit."

I'm reluctant to summon Arrest for the duel. It can't be helped. This guy doesn't control his power, so the scale is large. If I summon him in a narrow space, there's a 100% chance I'll be harmed too.

,Even if I actively use Machina, I have to limit it to some extent so I don't look like Jeok-kwang."

Even if I don't summon spirits, there's no possibility of me losing.

I checked the equipment provided by the Association. Only equipment that meets the standards can be used in public duels. I can't use Hwaryeon Bido or Geumgang Shinroe. The same goes for other artifacts.

,They didn't tamper with my equipment. Unless Go Young-jin bribed the Association, it'll be the same."

With just Go Pil-du's backing, he can't bribe the Association. Maybe Seong Hari could.

,Machina."

,Can you do what I told you to do?"

,Yeah. You mean hacking the Aurora Simulator and deliberately turning it off at a crucial moment? I can do it. But because it's a destructive method, this Aurora Simulator will be 100% broken."

,Can't you do it without breaking it?"

,This isn't purely mechanical, it's a complex mix of magic. And honestly, it doesn't make sense that there's a machine like this with the current level of technology. Who made this?"

,I heard that Aurora, a scientist, made it decades ago."

,Decades ago? How?"

,I don't know. They probably made it with Awakened abilities."

The technology related to the Aurora Simulator was a top secret in itself. To the extent that even the Association Chairman doesn't know. There are less than 100 Aurora Simulator technicians in the world, and they are subject to special management.

The duel time was getting closer. Machina became ethereal.

[Yujin. If Go Young-jin isn't a Demon... please be lenient. Don't kill him.]

,Since it's Ji-young-ie's request, I have to grant it, of course."

Of course, there's less than a 1% chance that Go Young-jin isn't a Demon. Unless he became a Demon and suddenly became strong, there's no way he would apply for a public duel against me.

,Seong Yujin-ssi. I will guide you to the duel arena."

The Demon Hunter isn't reacting. I was a little embarrassed.

,Noutroy. Did he make a contract with that guy and hide the demon's power? If I kill him, I can be sure whether he's a Demon or not."

He wasn't a Demon? I'll have to apologize to Kang Ji-young.

-The Aurora Simulator is now activating. Both participants, please wait for a moment.
