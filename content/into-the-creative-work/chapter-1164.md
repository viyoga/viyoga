,Kyaaaaaaak!'

Nevata Noriko, a plain-looking, short-haired woman with glasses sitting in front of me, suddenly screamed and ducked her head. Everyone's eyes turned to her.

The cause of the scream was an insect. A hornet had entered the car and was buzzing around Noriko's head.

,What, what is it?!'

,A hornet?!'

,Someone catch it, quick!'

The quiet interior of the van became noisy in an instant.

The men panicked and lowered themselves as much as possible when the hornet approached. A disgusting insect, and a hornet that could even kill someone. Fear filled the eyes of both men and women.

,Shit!'

A man with bleached blonde hair and piercings in his ears cursed and swung his bag at the hornet. The hornet easily avoided the attack. The man distorted his lips and glared at the hornet. He was Tanaka Hiroshi, a famous thug in this circle and a skinny, tattooed dude.

Buung buung.

The hornet's movements became threatening. The sound of its wings became much more ferocious. The hornet flew towards the nearest human, Hagawa Ryoko.

,Ryoko!'

Instead of Hagawa Ryoko, who was trying to respond calmly, Wada Kyoshiro stepped forward. He struck the hornet with a notebook in his hand. The hornet flew back with a jolt. Nakagami Risa, who was sitting next to me, was startled and swung her smartphone to knock the hornet away. Excellent reflexes. The problem was that the hornet was flying towards me.

I flicked my middle finger and hit the hornet's body precisely. My physical abilities had dropped to the level of an average person, but this wasn't too difficult.

The hornet hit the back of the chair and fell to the floor. I stepped on the hornet and killed it. I felt the hornet's body crushing. Sighs of relief were heard from all around.

,Nakagami-senpai! I'm sorry!'

Wada Kyoshiro got up from his seat and bowed to Nakagami Risa to apologize. The hornet he had knocked away had flown towards Nakagami Risa.

Nakagami Risa glared at Wada Kyoshiro once.

,It's okay.'

Nakagami Risa, who spoke coldly, then turned her gaze to me. Her sharp eyes were now relaxed.

,I'm sorry about earlier. I was so flustered that I knocked it towards you.'

,It's alright. It can happen when you're flustered.'

,The way you flicked the hornet with your finger was unusual. Are you used to hornets?'

,Yes. I'm quite used to them.'

I answered with a smile.

In fact, I'm not used to hornets. It was the first time I'd seen a hornet since high school. But I'm not afraid of hornets at all. I've faced monsters who can kill ordinary people with one hand, so why would I be afraid of a mere hornet?

,You're a freshman, right? What's your name?'

,Seong Yujin-nim.'

,...Korean?'

,Yes. I'm Korean. Do you perhaps dislike Korea?'

,Not really. I like Korean dramas and K-pop.'

Nakagami Risa said that and started touching her smartphone. I didn't try to force the conversation to continue. All it would do is come off as hitting on her. It's an inappropriate choice for building rapport.

,It's a relief that she doesn't dislike Koreans.'

In the case of young Japanese women, there aren't many who dislike Korea. Recently, Korean dramas and music have become popular, and there are quite a few who even have a favorable impression of Korea.

,It's a different story with the guys, though.'

There are many Japanese men who look down on Korea. Not only university students, but even professors look down on Korea for the most part. Some old Japanese geezers even think that Korea is still at the level of the 80s.

I usually ignore those guys appropriately... but there are always a few who pick fights to the point where it's hard to ignore them.

,There's one here too.'

Tanaka Hiroshi. The blonde-haired thug with piercings is glaring at me. I stared back. A brief staring contest ensued. Tanaka Hiroshi was the first to look away. He slowly turned his gaze as if to say he'd get me later.

,I'll have to deal with him properly later.'

Japan, the country of ijime (bullying).

The culture of ijime is mixed into working life, so would universities be any different?

,I'm already being bullied halfway.'

It's not the kind of bullying where I'm subjected to violence, but rather being subtly ostracized and ignored... close to being an outsider. The reason I'm being bullied is because I'm Korean.

Deolkeok deolkeok.

I looked out the car window. I could see trees. No, I could only see trees.

,Even if it's a mountain village... aren't we going too far in?'

It's boring to keep looking at the same scenery. I took out my smartphone from my pocket. I turned on a game I'd never seen before and focused on it.

,It's a gacha game. There are enough game resources... Gacha, let's go.'

[Great Luck (Lv. Master) is activated.]

My skill activated and good luck occurred.

As soon as I pressed the draw button, the featured character came out, breaking through the 0.5% probability.

But I didn't feel very good.

,A skill that only activates once a day is being used on something like this... what the hell is this.'

I shook my head.

It seemed like a dud, even though I had some expectations for the skill. I gave up on my expectations for the [Great Luck] skill.

The van arrived in the village 15 minutes later.

•••

We arrived in Ookaruma Village.

It was quite a large village for a mountain village. The fields were wide and there were several modern buildings. The most noticeable thing was the shrine on the mountain at the end of the village. The shrine was the largest in this village.

,The men will stay in this building, and the women will stay in that building. The festival starts tomorrow evening, so feel free to spend your time until then.'

A large, muscular man said. His name was Mori Masahiro, with short, sporty hair and big eyes. He was the president of the Japan Festival Study Circle. He was also the one who drove the van.

I went into the accommodation. It wasn't a very big building, but it wasn't uncomfortable to live in. The problem was that there were 7 men but only 6 rooms.

,The two freshmen. I'll give you the largest room, so the two of you use the same room.'

,Yes. Mori-senpai.'

,Yes.'

Wada Kyoshiro and I answered.

In the end, it was all about seniority. The two freshmen, who had no power, couldn't disobey Mori Masahiro, a fourth-year student and the president of the circle.

,Should I just flip the table according to my temper?'

I held back.

It was too early to cause a scene. It hadn't even been an hour since I entered this world. There was too little information. I decided to stay quiet until I had gathered some information.

Wada Kyoshiro and I went into the large room on the first floor together. It was a traditional Japanese tatami room. It was spacious, but it was uncomfortable that there were no beds.

,Seong-sang. It's the first time we've met like this. I'm Wada Kyoshiro. Nice to meet you.'

Wada Kyoshiro reached out his hand to me for a handshake. A pure face without any prejudice. This bastard was definitely the protagonist of this world. I hid my reluctance and took his hand.

,I'm Seong Yujin. We're sharing a room, so let's get along.'

,Yeah. Seong-sang.'

I put down my luggage. An awkward atmosphere hung in the air. The door opened and Mori Masahiro, the president, came in.

,You two freshmen. If you have nothing to do, would you mind helping me out?'

,Mori-senpai! Yes! Of course, I'll help you!'

,...I'll help you.'

,Thank you both. The second and third-year guys aren't cooperative, so... you saved me. I'll definitely repay you for this.'

Mori Masahiro's tone sounded old-fashioned. It felt like dealing with a 꼰대 (kkondae - old-fashioned person). To be honest, I didn't want to get involved... but it was for the sake of the original story information. I mustered my patience and asked him.

,Mori-senpai. What can I help you with?'

In response to my question, Mori Masahiro took out a camera and handed it to me. I accepted the camera in a daze. I think it's called a DSLR. It's a heavy and expensive-looking camera.

,I need data to write a report. Go around the village and take pictures, and conduct interviews with the residents about the village festival. Seong, you just take good pictures. I and Wada will take care of the interviews.'

I had a feeling. He excluded me from the interview role because I'm Korean.

,Yes. Yes. I understand.'

It was actually a relief. It was an easy job where I could just press the camera shutter from a slight distance.

I followed Mori out of the accommodation and walked around the village.

After walking around for about 2 hours, I could roughly understand the structure of the village. I tried to go up to the shrine on the mountain at the end of the village, but I was stopped by the old men working in the fields around the entrance.

,Don't move!'

,Outsiders can't go up to the shrine!'

Even though they were old men, their bodies weren't frail. Maybe it was because they were trained by working in the fields, but I could see muscular bodies through their shirts. On top of that, they were holding farming tools, so just standing there was threatening.

,Why can't we? We want to worship at the shrine,'

Mori Masahiro said calmly. His large frame was threatening even when he spoke calmly. The old men swallowed their breaths as if hiding their fear and answered.

,The bonghaeng ritual is currently taking place at the shrine. It's a ceremony in preparation for tomorrow's festival.

No one can enter during the 봉행 (bonghaeng) ritual. Go up to the shrine on the day the festival ends.

Mori Masahiro stepped back.

,Ah. Is that so. We didn't know. Is it okay to take pictures of the shrine?'

,It's okay as long as you don't go up to the shrine.'

Mori Masahiro gestured to me. I took out the camera and took pictures of the shrine. The building itself was decent, so it was fun to take pictures.

Wada Kyoshiro approached the old men and started talking to them.

,Are there any shrine maidens at the shrine?'

,Shrine maidens? Of course, there are. Tomorrow night, the shrine maiden will go around the village and give blessings.'

,Can we receive blessings too?'

,Come out to the street tomorrow night. Then the shrine maiden will give you blessings on behalf of the mountain god.'

,I see. I'll definitely come out to the street tomorrow night.'

The short interview was over. We walked around the village again.

I was getting more and more annoyed with taking pictures.

,It was kind of fun at first, though.'

The sun was slowly starting to set.

,Mori-senpai. Let's go back now. There are no restaurants in this village, so we have to prepare food.'

,The women said they'd prepare it. You've worked hard. I brought some beer, so I'll share it with you.'

,Oh, beer! Thank you!'

,But let's interview one last person. Ah, this is a personal question, so you don't have to record it.'

,There's plenty of capacity, so I'll record it.'

,Okay. Thanks, Wada.'

Mori Masahiro nodded and headed towards the field. An old man working in the potato field saw us and straightened his body. And he stared at us intently.

,Senpai. Doesn't that person... feel a bit creepy?'

His body was emaciated and his eyes were sunken. His dark pupils were similar to the eyes of a corpse.

,It's rude to the elderly.'

,Ah, I'm sorry.'

,Well, I do feel a bit creepy too.'

Mori Masahiro approached the old man and started talking to him.

,Hello. I'm a student from Naio University. Would you mind answering a few questions for an interview?'

,Interview?'

,It's nothing much.'

,......'

The old man nodded. Meaning to go ahead.

,Around this time last year. I heard that 5 people went missing in Ookaruma Village. Do you perhaps know anything about them?'

,...Are you a relative of the missing people?'

,No. I'm not a relative.'

,I don't know much about that. And that's already over.'

,Over? Did the missing people return?'

,The police finished their investigation.'

The old man turned his body abruptly. He held a sharp sickle and struck the ground nervously. The sight was quite grotesque.

,Old man.'

,I have nothing to say. Don't talk to me.'

Mori Masahiro was about to talk to the old man again, but he stopped. We headed back to the accommodation.

I said to them before entering the accommodation.

,Mori-senpai. I'll stop by the convenience store for a moment and come back.'

,Huh? Do you need anything?'

,Cup noodles. I'm going to get some for a late-night snack.'

,Alright.'

I gave him the camera and went into the convenience store. They had all the necessary daily necessities. When I came out of the convenience store, the sun had completely set. Because it was a mountain village, it was even darker than the city at night. There were no streetlights installed, so it looked difficult to go back.

,There's a limit to the light from my smartphone... I should buy a flashlight while I'm here.'

I bought a flashlight and walked down the road.

I felt a gaze.

When I turned my head, I saw the old man I had seen earlier standing blankly in the field, watching me. He was holding a sharp sickle in his hand.

I ignored the creepy old man and walked down the road.

But even after walking 50m, the old man was still looking at me. I felt bad and shouted at the old man.

,Old man! Do you have something to say? If not, avert your eyes!'

,......'

The old man didn't move and just stared at me.

I frowned and changed direction, walking towards the old man.
