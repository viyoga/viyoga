I headed down to the underground workshop with Gold Smith. His workshop was spacious, white, and immaculate. From the outside, it looked less like a workshop and more like some kind of research lab.

Along one wall of the workshop, finished equipment hung on display. Hero suits and the weapons heroes wielded.

Scanning them roughly, the lowest rank among them was S-rank. Selling even one of those dozens of items would mean never worrying about money again.

"Tempting, isn't it? Sorry, but I've no intention of selling those to you."

"I've no intention of buying, either. No matter how much equipment you have, there's a limit to what you can actually use in the field."

"We think alike. I don't want my equipment rotting away in someone's storage, either."

"I heard you make equipment for S-rank heroes too."

"Eighty percent of the equipment used by active S-rank heroes came from my hands."

"I heard you made Sung Ha-ri's spear as well."

"Sung Ha-ri? Ah, you mean that woman from Korea. Was it about twenty years ago I took that commission? I don't recall the timing exactly, but I remember the request itself. She wanted a spear that would never break. Selecting the materials alone was a headache."

I pictured the spear Sung Ha-ri mainly used. Having spent time with her since childhood, seeing her spear was no difficult feat.

Sung Ha-ri didn't maintain her spear separately. Wiping it down with a cloth after use was the extent of it. And yet the blade was always sharp, without a single scratch.

"Does that spear really never break?"

"That spear was my masterpiece. 'Never' is a bit of an exaggeration, but... even if you threw that spear into the core of the sun, it would endure for hours without melting or warping."

That was the first time I learned the spear's name was Invincible. Sung Ha-ri never bothered to mention its name, and when she wasn't working, it was haphazardly tossed in a storage room. She had almost no attachment to the spear.

"I heard Gwancheon was quite fond of that spear. She probably treasures Invincible and uses it with care."

It's shoved in our house's storage room. Sung Ha-ri doesn't even carry it around unless she's working. Even when something urgent comes up, she just grabs whatever weapon is nearby.

I didn't bother telling him the truth. I had no intention of revealing my identity to him. Instead, I asked something else.

"Gwancheon doesn't wear a suit, does she?"

"She apparently doesn't like hero suits that cling tight to the body. Oriental women of the old days were all like that. It wasn't unusual."

Sung Ha-ri still looks like she's in her twenties, so even I sometimes forget her age. She belonged to a previous generation.

"You. You seem quite interested in Gwancheon?"

"We come from the same country. I've never properly fought her until now, but... the future is uncertain."

"True enough. A dark hero is, strictly speaking, a villain. Let me give you some good news. Invincible, which boasted absolute durability on Earth, is no longer absolute as of today."

Gold Smith pointed to the center of the workshop with his index finger. His finger was trembling.

"Honestly speaking, I still can't be sure whether giving you that weapon is the right call. Right after completing it, I understood how Oppenheimer felt."

Placed at the center was a massive white gun measuring a full three meters in length. It took the form of a gun, but calling it a crude pillar wouldn't have been strange either.

"Old man. You're being overly dramatic."

I walked to the center and touched the fucking huge white gun.

『World Destroyer

Rank: SSS

Can only be operated when an overwhelming power source is equipped.

Explosion and Penetration modes can be selected.』

SSS rank.

Just having the highest rank attached was satisfying enough for now.

I lifted the gun with both hands. The trigger was on the underside. One hand supporting it, the other pulling the trigger. The problem was that being fucking huge meant it weighed a ton.

'About 800kg, maybe? Not a weight that burdens me much.'

There was no separate scope. It wasn't built for precision shooting in the first place.

"Old man. Have you tested its power yourself?"

"Are you insane? That thing can't even be easily handled by an A-rank hero. To fire it, you'd have to deploy a mana cube and use maximum output. The problem is that deploying a mana cube generates pressure so intense even an A-rank hero would struggle to withstand it."

"Even if you haven't fired it, as its creator you must have a rough idea of its power. How strong are the explosion and penetration modes?"

He wasn't surprised that I knew the functions. These features had been decided through discussion between him and me from the start.

"The explosion mode has the power of a nuclear bomb capable of blowing away half of LA. A nuclear bomb without the radiation concern. The power is greater than expected, so whether handing it to you is right... I'm still doubtful."

"Surely you don't think I'd drop a nuke on a city for no reason?"

"...That sounds like you're saying you would if you had a reason."

"And the penetration mode?"

"The reason Invincible couldn't remain absolute is because of the penetration mode. Compared to the explosion mode, its range is less than one percent, with all that power concentrated. Even Invincible wouldn't withstand it."

"Good. I like it. Well done, old man."

-Huh? The structure is simple and the tech inside is nothing special, but the power is incredible? This gun makes no sense by any normal standard!

'That's what an ability is. Outstanding abilities ignore common sense.'

Gold Smith may not look it, but he's the number one equipment crafter in this world. If he couldn't make something like this, it would be more disappointing.

"The World Destroyer isn't without its flaws. Its power is tremendous, but... all of that power is pure physical force."

"Meaning it's useless against enemies that physical force doesn't work on."

"You catch on quickly. If abilities like spatial distortion interfere, you'd be helpless. A barrier that blocks physical force could stop it absurdly easily."

"That's the same for any other equipment. I'm taking this gun."

I moved to put the World Destroyer into my inventory, then paused. The mana cube mounted on the rear caught my eye.

"Hey, old man. Can this be used with a power source other than a mana cube?"

"If the specs are similar, yes. But the power source's output must be at the level of a mana cube's maximum limit. In practice, that means it's impossible with anything currently in existence besides a mana cube."

Gold Smith may not know, but I had a Nether mana cube with identical specs to a mana cube. If I used the Nether mana cube, what kind of result would come out?

'I'm curious, but it's not something I can test right now. Nor do I intend to tell Gold Smith about the Nether mana cube.'

I placed the World Destroyer into my inventory.

"Old man. You know that villains including Jamal have crawled into LA. I don't skim on commission fees. I'll wipe them out before long. And among them, Jamal especially—I'll make sure he dies."

"...I'll trust that you won't harm innocent civilians with the weapon I made."

"Trust as much as you like."

As I moved to leave the workshop, he stopped me with his voice.

"You. I hear you've been selling something called dragon hearts lately? Could I get one of those dragon hearts as well?"

"At your age, you've developed a craving for power?"

"I don't know about other things, but I am curious whether it could be used as equipment material. I'll pay the price."

I set ten Aroung-grade dragon hearts on the workshop table.

"No need for payment. You made my equipment well, so consider this a freebie."

After returning, I applied Origin to the World Destroyer and enhanced it with the Bright Ascension Diagram. Two Heavenly Jade were consumed.

『World Destroyer

Rank: SSS

Can only be operated when an overwhelming power source is equipped.

Enhanced by an unknown power.

Explosion, Penetration, and Sustained modes can be selected.』

A new mode called Sustained had been added.

A self-proclaimed villain alliance had gathered in LA.

The US government and the Hero Association weren't fools, so they were naturally monitoring the villain alliance. The air of LA was thick with the tension of impending war. Some citizens, gripped by fear, fled the city. Few considered relocating to another city permanently. Once the situation settled, they'd return as if nothing had happened.

The villain alliance didn't rampage through the city. In fact, they didn't even enter it. They set up in the outskirts of LA—no, in an industrial zone some distance from LA. With thirty thousand villains gathered, it was a small city in itself.

As villains converged on LA, heroes from other regions were also gathering there. The more combat-capable warriors assembled, the more the tension escalated.

Rumors spread that even the S-rank heroes guarding their own territories would soon make their move.

Major American broadcasters focused their coverage on the current situation.

The LA branch head of the Hero Association held talks with representatives of the villain alliance. Nothing came of it. The villain alliance still occupied the industrial zone.

That day, a fighter jet formation flew over the industrial zone three times. It was the US government's warning that they could launch a bombing run at any moment.

The villain alliance didn't seem greatly shaken, but the next day, at an official press conference they called for reporters, the alliance leader appeared.

He was a black man with short gray hair and a silver inverted-cross piercing in his ear. He wore a sharp suit, and though he wasn't particularly large, he exuded charisma. His gaze was sharp as a well-honed blade.

"I am Jamal, leader of the villain alliance. We have no intention of harming the American people, nor of fighting heroes. The only thing we want is one man—a butcher who thinks he's justice itself."

Jamal stared straight into the camera. He didn't hide his killing intent.

"Jeokgwang. I know you intend to kill us. We are gathered here. If you truly believe you are justice, that you are a hero, then come. If you don't come within three days... you are nothing but a cowardly, frightened assassin merely posing as a dark hero."
