The ritual took place in the secret church of Seongjin Elementary School.

Those who observed the ritual were the proven ones. Fanatics with unwavering faith. Most were members of the Imperial Guard. A few were priests. Some of them were bishop-level priests.

,His Holiness is entering!

One of the Imperial Guards shouted and opened the door. The secret church was bright. Light streamed in from all directions. Stained glass, I have no idea where they got it, decorated every window. In the center of the church, the ceiling was fitted with transparent glass, allowing bright light to pour in.

Around me, the standing fanatics bowed their heads, clasping their hands together in prayer. They even silenced their breathing.

,I will tell you. Open your eyes and watch carefully.

,Yes, Seong-nim.

I walked to the center of the church, stepping on the red carpet laid on the floor. At the end of the red carpet was Son Tae-hyung, the Cardinal. He was kneeling reverently in front of the freezer coffin. As I approached, Son Tae-hyung bowed his head without a word. His forehead and nose touched the floor.

,Is your wife in this coffin?

,Yes. That is correct.

Of course, I knew Son Tae-hyung's situation.

Son Tae-hyung's wife, Park Sae-jeong, was Son Tae-hyung's elementary school classmate. She was Son Tae-hyung's desk partner, and as children of that age often did, Son Tae-hyung apparently bullied his partner. It wasn't like ostracizing. All he did was draw lines on the desk and tease her. In those days, it could be seen as an expression of interest from those children.

Later, Son Tae-hyung returned from the military and met Park Sae-jeong by chance at a church, and they got married. They had one daughter.

Park Sae-jeong became a zombie on the day a super-large meteorite fell and the D virus erupted. Her daughter was next to Park Sae-jeong, and before Son Tae-hyung could do anything, she bit her daughter's shoulder. To save his daughter, Son Tae-hyung killed Park Sae-jeong. But the daughter was infected and had to be killed as well.

Son Tae-hyung placed his wife and daughter in a makeshift wooden coffin made by breaking furniture, buried them in the backyard, and erected a tombstone. He couldn't cremate them. He couldn't bear to see his wife and daughter's corpses burning. In hindsight, it was the best choice. Even I can't revive a being without a body.

Son Tae-hyung lived as he pleased after that, drifted into Seongak Elementary School, and met me.

Becoming the commander of the Imperial Guard and following me, he brought his buried wife and daughter to Seongak Elementary School and moved them to a freezer coffin. This was to prevent the corpses from rotting. Na Chae-yeong made it. The principle was not much different from a refrigerator, so it wasn't difficult to make.

,Open it.

Psh.

When Son Tae-hyung pressed the button, the lid of the freezer coffin opened, revealing the inside. Along with the cold air, the corpse of a woman was lying down. The half-rotted corpse was in pretty good condition.

,She looks ordinary.

If I told Son Tae-hyung to offer his wife and daughter to me, he would gladly do so. In fact, there were quite a few fanatics who offered their families to me. They don't shy away from it; they're actually happy.

Everyone's eyes are on me.

I didn't say anything to them. I just need to prove myself to them.

,Grab your wife and stand her up.

So that everyone here can witness a miracle.

I didn't say the rest, but the meaning must have been conveyed. Without complaint, Son Tae-hyung grabbed his wife's shoulders and stood her up.

,Hmm. It would be bland to just use recovery for this rare show.

Recovery didn't have much of an effect. So, I created lightning. Pajijik. Sparks flew around my right hand, and blue lightning gathered and wriggled.

The fanatics looked at my hand with reverent eyes. They believed the lightning I wielded was divine punishment.

They had no doubt that one day my lightning would punish all the evil in this world.

I sent the lightning into Park Sae-jeong's corpse. There was a slight musty smell.

,Park Sae-jeong, your sins are forgiven.

I just blurted it out. Son Tae-hyung and the other fanatics trembled with emotion.

,Now I will save you from death.

I placed my hand on Park Sae-jeong's head and used my recovery ability.

The rotten areas returned to normal. Then, her stopped heart began to beat again, and her pale skin regained its color. Her cold body temperature gradually rose, and breath appeared from her nose and mouth. Pareureu. Her trembling eyelids lifted.

,Ah, uh... Yeo, Yeobo?

Park Sae-jeong, who opened her eyes, looked around in confusion and then felt relieved when she saw Son Tae-hyung.

,Keuheuk, Heuheuheuk.... Thank you. Thank you, Seong-nim-isiyeo!

Son Tae-hyung hugged his wife tightly and shed tears.

Kung!

The fanatics who witnessed the miracle knelt on the spot.

,Oh, Ooooo....

,Seong-nim has saved the Cardinal's wife from death!

,Lord! Save us and lead us! We will offer everything to Seong-nim!

The fanatics cheered enthusiastically. Every single one of them cheered while shedding tears. I can guarantee it. Their faith will never waver again. Even if someone calls them fanatics and denounces them as brainwashed, they will not waver.

A dead person has been resurrected.

They witnessed the resurrection with their own eyes. How could their faith waver in such a situation?

,It's a bit of a shame to end it here.

Park Sae-jeong was already kneeling in front of me, bowing her head. Judging from her bewildered expression, she hadn't fully grasped the situation. She was just bowing her head to me, led by Son Tae-hyung. But she will soon become a fanatic as well.

Because she experienced a miracle. And because she has a daughter to revive.

,I have enough stamina, so my recovery ability must have grown.

It also helped that Park Sae-jeong's corpse was well-preserved. If her whole body had been rotten, it would have been quite difficult.

,Since I have the chance, should I bring forward one of my plans?

,Priests, remove the coffin and bring the Holy Sword and the Holy Heart Box. Cardinal, wait quietly.

The priests moved. The moment I mentioned the Holy Heart Box, they realized what I was about to do.

Realizing that a new ritual was about to begin, the other believers simply waited quietly. Park Sae-jeong was led by the priests and moved next to them. Park Sae-jeong's eyes trembled uneasily. But she was overwhelmed by the atmosphere and couldn't do anything.

A priest slowly approached, carrying a white sword and a small silver box on a tray. The white sword was made of Galchinium. It was the size of a dagger, and the blade was hard. The small box was a small freezer decorated with silver and gold.

,From now on, I will perform the Divine Ritual. I ask you, who are you?

,I am Son Tae-hyung. Just a human who wants to serve Seong-nim.

,What can you do for me?

,I can do anything.

,Can you prove your faith with death? Can you offer your heart to me?

,I will gladly prove it. I will gladly offer it!

I handed the Holy Sword to Son Tae-hyung. He held the sword in reverse grip and stabbed himself in the neck without hesitation. Blood flowed out and soaked the floor.

,Kyaaak! Yeobo!!

Park Sae-jeong, horrified, made a fuss, but a member of the Imperial Guard nearby immediately subdued her.

Son Tae-hyung carefully handed me the sword with trembling hands. I received the sword and waited for Son Tae-hyung to die.

After Son Tae-hyung was completely dead, I cut open his chest and extracted his heart. I stored the heart in the Holy Heart Box.

Ignoring the wailing Park Sae-jeong, I placed my hand on Son Tae-hyung's head. When I used recovery, the wound disappeared and his heart regenerated.

Son Tae-hyung, who regained consciousness, straightened his posture. Park Sae-jeong, who had been wailing, was startled and froze like a stone.

,You have been reborn.

,Yes, Seong-nim. Death was nothing.

,Son Tae-hyung. You have received immortality from me. You have become the first Holy Knight, so you will serve me for the rest of your life with that body.

,Everything is according to Seong-nim's will.

,The Imperial Guard will change its name to the Holy Imperial Guard. The existing members of the Imperial Guard are appointed as temporary Holy Imperial Guards. The only members of the true Holy Imperial Guard are the Holy Knights. The Holy Imperial Guard is the shield that protects me and the sword that kills my enemies.

,Everything is for Seong-nim.

I have been experimenting with my recovery ability.

As a result, I found out a few things. If a part of the body exists, I can revive a dead person with recovery as much as I want. That body part can be as small as a fingernail. I can even recover them with a single strand of hair. However, in this case, it becomes incredibly difficult for me. The burden increases dozens of times.

Then, what if a person is perfectly alive? Can I cut off a part of the body and recover the person?

The answer is no.

If the original person is alive, recovery does not work on a part of the body. In other words, it was impossible for me to copy a person with my ability.

Why is it impossible? The reason has not been clearly revealed, but I guess it has something to do with the soul. Na Chae-yeong agreed with this. She said that since this world itself is a world inside a game, it's not strange to have fantasy. The cause of superpowers itself has not yet been revealed. Na Chae-yeong's ability is also a power that cannot be explained by theory.

Anyway, I can create a limited immortality system through my recovery ability. As long as I keep Son Tae-hyung's heart separately, I can revive Son Tae-hyung even if he dies without leaving a single body part.

,The Holy Imperial Guard will become an immortal unit.

I look around.

No one doubts me. Even Park Sae-jeong, who is seeing me for the first time, is looking at me with eyes full of awe.
