Tang! Tang! Tang!

A series of single gunshots echoed from the factory.

The security team was taking care of the enemies hiding in the factory. The gunshots didn't last long. Realizing they had no chance, the enemies threw down their weapons and surrendered.

I checked the factory. From the outside, it looked like an ordinary factory. Even when I scouted it with reconnaissance drones before coming here, there weren't many signs of people.

,I didn't thoroughly scout the inside of the factory with drones... I never imagined this many people would be inside.'

What surprised me the most was that they had guns. Unlike America, where you can buy guns at the mart, getting a gun in South Korea is like finding water in the desert. It should be that way.

,There are some usable trucks and cars outside the factory. We can use the trucks... and the cars should be put into the decomposer to extract resources.'

Na Chaeyoung-ssi, the Technologist, her skills are improving day by day. She's also growing. Her capabilities are increasing, but the problem is technology. Technologists and Bionix can't just use their abilities without limit. They need to complete the technology through research.

,The current research speed is too slow. Stealing other people's technology is the best way... but that's not easy.'

I ordered some of the subjugation force to clear the area around the factory and went inside.

A wood interior factory. I naturally expected it to be full of stacked wood and dirty with sawdust and dust. The inside of the factory I saw was completely different. There was no wood at all. The machinery also looked very complex, unlike what I had expected.

,Representative-nim. There were piles of guns and bullets inside. The workers said that they started producing guns and bullets in this factory two weeks ago.'

,Ilwoo Group. Those crazy bastards. They openly remodeled a factory in Korea to make guns and bullets?'

I chuckled.

An unexpected jackpot. I needed a machine to make guns and bullets anyway. Right now, Na Chaeyoung-ssi is making the firearms for Seongak Elementary School by hand.

There was only one production line for each. It's a shame, but I should be grateful for what I have.

,Since we're going to use this machine, we need to take it carefully. I should bring the workers from Seongak Elementary School.'

In one corner of the factory, I saw prisoners kneeling with their hands and feet tied. I scanned them and clicked my tongue. Most of them were men. The women were mostly middle-aged women over 40. There were some young women, but they were ugly.

That's when it happened. The security team dragged someone from the corner of the factory. It was a middle-aged man wearing clean work clothes. Instead of being intimidated, the middle-aged man puffed up his chest and acted tough.

He seems to blindly believe in the saying that if you keep your wits about you, you can survive even in a tiger's den. In reality, you either live or die depending on the tiger's mood, though.

,What's this guy?'

,He says he's the factory manager. I brought him because I thought he might know something.'

,Good job. I was just wondering how to move the machines. It'll be easier if this bastard cooperates.'

,Do you think I'll cooperate with you looters?!'

The factory manager shouts.

Slap!

My hand struck his cheek. Blood trickled from his nose, and his lips burst.

,Ah. I slapped you without realizing it because you were being so loud. If you're going to talk, talk quietly. Huh?'

Slap!

,Keuk! W-Why this time...?'

,You have two cheeks, so you get two slaps. You don't want to cooperate with us? Okay. I respect that opinion and give you a choice. Do you want to die round? Or do you want to die square?'

The factory manager gasped. The spirit in his eyes faded. He wasn't completely subdued. It seemed like he had something he was relying on.

It's annoying to deal with him. Should I just cut off his arms and legs? I placed my hand on the hilt of my sword. Then the factory manager said urgently.

,T-This is Ilwoo Group territory! Do you think Ilwoo Group will let you get away with touching this place? Ilwoo Group will retaliate against you directly!'

,Oh. You're threatening us even in this situation? I'll praise that spirit. But you'll pay the price first.'

The sword was swung lightly. The factory manager's left arm fell to the floor with a thud.

,Aaaaaaaaargh!'

,You're loud. Next should I do the right arm... or maybe the eyes.'

,I'm sorry! I'm sorry! Please spare me! Please! I'll do whatever you tell me to!'

He knelt down, crying and begging for his life.

,If you had just knelt down and begged from the beginning instead of resisting, it would have been easy. You're so stupid.'

,I-I was just doing what the headquarters told me to do! I-I didn't have any intention of resisting!'

,From headquarters? You were in contact with Ilwoo Group. Well, the telecommunications companies aren't completely destroyed, so you can still make calls. Give me your smartphone.'

,We don't use smartphones to contact headquarters, we use military communication equipment.'

,Why?'

,It was an order from headquarters. They told us to use military communication equipment because of the risk of eavesdropping....'

,You guys are really serious. So, who did you sell the guns to?'

,I-I don't know either. Once a week, the transportation team from headquarters comes, gives us money and food, and takes the guns and bullets.'

Money.

In this world, money isn't completely meaningless. It's not like the country is completely destroyed. But it's not as important as food.

,Where is Ilwoo Group's headquarters?'

,It's in Busan.'

They come up from Busan to take the guns and bullets? In this situation? That doesn't make sense. According to the news, the highways are a mess. Cars stopped on the highway are scattered like trash, and infected people are roaming around. From Busan to Seoul? It's impossible to travel by car. Not to mention trains.

,The guns produced here are being used in Seoul.... Are they selling them to the general public? Or....'

It's definitely not military supply.

The security team brought the military communication equipment.

,Representative-nim. We received a call from Ilwoo Group headquarters. Ilwoo Group knows the current situation and requested a conversation with you, Representative-nim.'

Since an incident occurred, the factory manager immediately reported it to headquarters. I took the receiver from the security team.

,This is me.'

-...An impressive greeting. Are you Seong Yujin-ssi, the representative of Seongak Elementary School?

,Who are you?'

-I'm Jin Chunghyun, a director of Ilwoo Group. Back off from the factory and leave. Then I'll forget about this incident.

,Ah. I see. You'll forget about it. Okay. I'll back off from the factory and leave. I can't help it, I'm scared of big companies.'

It was sarcasm that anyone could recognize.

-Don't do something stupid that will turn us into enemies. South Korea is a country with gun control. You'll be punished just for having guns. Not to mention a machine that makes guns? The police and the military will kill you.

,Aren't you in South Korea?'

I heard a low laugh from the receiver.

-This is Busan.

What does that mean?

Does it mean that South Korea's power doesn't affect Busan? Or has Busan become a warlord state? There was no information.

It was impossible to grasp everything that was happening in the provinces outside of Seoul just from the news on TV.

,Did Ilwoo Group take over Busan? Are they trying to declare a corporate state?'

The world is in this state, so it wasn't impossible. Besides, Korea is very busy internally and externally right now. Just North Korea alone is a headache, and problems are constantly erupting within Korea due to infections and mutations. Ilwoo Group knows this fact well, so they can move so openly.

,These bastards know that too. That's why they're moving so openly. And they probably have connections with high-ranking officials.'

So, they're telling me to stay still if I don't want to die? Because they're stronger than me?

,Bullshit. This bastard isn't stronger than me. It's just that Ilwoo Group is bigger.'

They're in Busan? I can just go and kill them. I can ask Na Chaeyoung-ssi to put everything else aside and make a means of transportation first, right? Seoul to Busan. It's only about 300km. I can get there in a few days even if I walk.

,And this is Seoul. I don't know what you're doing, but I'm warning you to back off.'

-You're threatening me in return? How absurd. Does the fact that you're an Awakened being pushed by the media mean you can't see what's in front of you? I can kill someone like you without anyone knowing. This is my last warning. Back off from the factory. Then nothing will happen.

Just back off here? I can guarantee it. These bastards will definitely come to take care of me soon. It's not like I'm seeing guys like this for the first time.

,This is mine. The factory is mine, and the people I've captured are mine. I'll use the machines well. And you, you better not catch my eye. The moment you do, I'll show you what hell is like. Oh, and take good care of your family. Your family will die the moment they're seen.'

-You fucking bastard, how dare you!!

A sudden outburst. Was family his weak spot?

,Ah, not just your family, but all your relatives too.'

-You will definitely regret today's choice.

Click.

I hung up the receiver. There was no need to listen anymore.

I pointed to the communication equipment for the security team.

,Ignore the calls and take the equipment. The equipment itself is a good resource.'

We might be able to acquire technology just by dismantling and examining the communication equipment. And objects don't all give the same resources. The more advanced the equipment, and the stronger the creatures, the more resources you can obtain.

,What should we do with the factory manager? Execute him?'

The security team looked at the factory manager and fiddled with their guns. Executing people was one of the security team's jobs. At first, their hands trembled, but now they've gotten somewhat used to it.

,Let's keep him alive for now. We need to know how to use the machines and how to move them. Have the prisoners clean the factory. And find out about the other equipment.'

After giving the orders, I went into the office and sat in the factory manager's chair. The chair was cheap. I then took out my smartphone and contacted Lee Yeonhee-ssi, the 4th Deputy Director of the National Intelligence Service.
