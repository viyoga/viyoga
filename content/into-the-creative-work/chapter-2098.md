I arrived at the destination city.

The city had developed simply because it was close to the Imperial family.

I walked confidently toward the city entrance. I didn't hide my energy. Ordinary people felt an instinctive fear of me and kept their distance. They lowered their gaze, afraid of even making eye contact.

The soldiers guarding the city seemed to have learned some martial arts, as they recognized my energy, swallowed hard, and tensed up. I ignored the soldiers, passed them, and entered the city.

I had no intention of causing a ruckus in this city. As a city close to the Imperial family, the government troops directly managed it. In this era ruled by the Emperor, turning the government into an enemy was no different from suicide. Even the Heavenly Demon Church and the Martial Arts Alliance were wary of the Emperor.

I walked down the main street. Everywhere I went, the surroundings became quiet. It was because I wasn't hiding my energy. It wasn't that there were no gazes following me, but no one openly tried to interfere with me.

I entered a building with a sign that read 'Five Dragons Merchant Group (五龍商團).'

According to Baekran, it was one of the front businesses run by the Golden Cloth Guard.

A merchant was sitting on the first floor. Around him were boxes filled with things I couldn't identify.

,"Oh, welcome. What brings you here? Our Five Dragons Merchant Group specializes in animal hides. Are you looking for any particular hide?"

I stared intently at him. He trembled under my pressure but didn't avoid my gaze. He was quite a spirited merchant.

,"I'm looking for the hide of a yellow dragon."

I uttered the password to contact the Golden Cloth Guard. The merchant glanced around, checking his surroundings, and then said,

,"P, please come this way."

I followed the merchant into a room.

Click-clack. The merchant pressed a switch hidden in the wall seven times. The sound of metal interlocking and gears turning came from inside the wall. It was a mechanical formation.

,'The energy is spreading, cutting off the outside world. A combination of mechanical formation and magic, huh.'

There was a chair in the room, so I went and sat down.

,"There are many kinds of yellow dragon hides. Which specific hide are you looking for?"

,"I want a hide with a white flower drawn on it."

,"As it happens, that person is in the Imperial family. You should be able to meet them in about three days."

,"Three days, huh."

I didn't like it, but there was nothing I could do.

,"I'll guide you to a brothel so you can stay comfortably for three days."

,"No need. I'll wait here."

I exited the game world and started auto-progress. Three days passed in an instant.

• • •

Baekran entered the room and took off her conical hat. Her bangs fluttered. Her hair was clean and white. The back of her hair was tied up and fixed with a black hairpin.

Her soft eyes and faint smile were the same as when I last saw her. Her beautiful appearance made both men and women feel favorably toward her.

,"...You've reached the Three Flowers Realm while I haven't seen you."

,"I was already halfway to the realm when I met you last time."

,"It seems like you're getting ahead of me."

,"I just got lucky."

It was a joke.

The Three Flowers Realm wasn't simple enough to be explained away with luck. The reason she was able to get ahead of me was probably thanks to her pre-regression experience. It would have been something she had already experienced once.

,"What made you try to contact me? Did something happen to the Heavenly Demon?"

,"The Heavenly Demon is always the same. Always secluded."

,"I see. Then why did you call me? And with this kind of contact method. Do you know how surprised I was when you suddenly appeared and tried to contact me like this? What on earth...."

,"I have something to ask you. It's important."

,"Hmm. I'll answer with as much sincerity as possible... but can't you reduce your energy? Your energy is so fierce that my skin has been tingling since I entered the room."

I suppressed my energy. It felt like forcibly crushing my mood, making me feel suffocated.

,"You're getting impatient. What on earth is going on?"

I explained Milyeong's condition to her. Of course, I hid the detailed information about Milyeong.

...Indeed. Your companion, the mage, tried to enter the Harmony Realm but failed, and her mind is trapped within her mental projection... Since fire energy (火氣) continues to leak out little by little... the problem is serious.

,"Is there no way to solve it?"

,"For now, let me answer that I don't know the method."

,"......Didn't you say you studied magic to some extent?"

,"There's a big difference between knowing something and not knowing it. But just because I studied magic doesn't mean I'm a mage. Even if I can use magic, it's all basic stuff. If you only consider magic skills, I'm worse than a mage from the countryside. The mage you know is a mage skilled enough to challenge the Harmony Realm, isn't she? The depth is different. I can never catch up."

,"......."

Was seeking out Baekran the wrong choice?

I immediately started planning my next move. The first thing that came to mind was using an elixir on Milyeong. However, I wasn't sure if an elixir could cure her.

Right now, Milyeong's mind is trapped in her mental image. Would using an elixir allow her mind to escape from the mental image world? I couldn't guarantee it.

,"I know that mage is a precious person to you. But calm down. If you get too excited, even things that could work won't."

,"A precious person... I guess so. It's a bit hard to calm down. I don't have much time."

,"You have a year, don't you?"

,"A year passes quickly."

In this world, a year was nothing.

,"If you're really short on time, sealing that mage might be a solution."

,"...Seal?"

,"There are many kinds of seals. From simply imprisoning someone in an underground dungeon to high-level seals that stop space and time. Do you want a seal? I can help you."

,"I want to leave sealing as a last resort. Is there no other way?"

,"The only other option would be to find a renowned mage. The best thing would be to find a mage who has reached the Harmony Realm and ask for help."

,"Those kinds of mages aren't common. And there's no guarantee they'll help even if I find them."

,"As a second-best option, you could look for a mage in the Three Flowers Realm. Do you know the Sima Clan?"

The Sima Clan? I didn't know much about them, but I had heard the name. It was a famous place like the Zhuge Clan.

,"I've heard the name."

,"The Sima Clan is a family that consistently produces outstanding mages. They have a close relationship with the Imperial family. Most of the Sima Clan's mages work in the Imperial Palace. Probably, including the collateral lines, there are over 100 Sima clan mages working in the Imperial Palace."

,"100 people? That's an impressive family."

,"Indeed. Among them, the current head of the Sima Clan, Sima Ryeong, is from a collateral line but reached the Three Flowers Realm and took the position of head of the family. He was the Imperial High Priest but retired and is focusing on family matters."

,"Imperial High Priest? That's an impressive figure."

,"Well, even if he's the Imperial High Priest, he only presides over regular rituals... he doesn't really do anything else. So, he doesn't have as much power as others might look up to."

,"...Doesn't he manage the Imperial Palace's barriers or formations?"

,"That's a common misconception. The Imperial Palace doesn't have barriers or formations."

,"What?"

,"His Imperial Majesty resides in the Imperial Palace, so what use are those things?"

Baekran laughed, "Huhu." It was just a hunch, but it seemed like she was annoyed. I understood. Working in the Imperial Palace, filled with high-ranking officials, must be stressful for her too.

,"Anyway, according to you, the head of the Sima Clan would be helpful. I'll have to go to the Sima Clan and ask for help. ...Can I get your help?"

,"Unfortunately, I don't have any connections to the Sima Clan. The Sima Clan has deep ties with the Imperial family, so even with the power of the First Class Golden Cloth Guard, I can't treat them carelessly."

,"......Should I go and threaten them?"

,"I'll take that as a joke."

Deep ties with the Imperial family. That meant that if I messed up, I could draw the Emperor's attention.

,"How should I approach them?"

,"The best way would be to approach them through the connections of a high-ranking official. Do you know any high-ranking officials? It would be good if they were high-ranking officials related to the Imperial family."

,"Know any high-ranking officials? There's no way I would...."

I couldn't finish my sentence. Suddenly, one name came to mind.

,"It seems like there's someone who comes to mind. Who is it?"

,"...Seong Jigone."

Seong Jigone and I had been meeting regularly. Thanks to that, I knew what rank Seong Jigone held now.

Currently, Seong Jigone is a Third Rank, Senior Grade General of Manifest Martiality (昭武將軍).

,"Seong Jigone? Ah, are you talking about the General of Manifest Martiality of Hayang City? Your half-brother, I see."

Baekran said with a smile. She even knew facts that I hadn't bothered to mention. As expected, she had investigated me to some extent.

,"But a 3rd Rank is not enough, is it?"

,"Seong Jigone is young for his age. Normally, he wouldn't be at an age to reach the 3rd Rank. It's not like he's made any great achievements either."

The Empire had no enemy countries. Since the entire continent was one, it wasn't easy to gain merit through war. The only way to quickly gain merit was to subjugate monsters.

Seong Jigone hadn’t dealt with many monsters either. Still, the reason he was able to reach the high rank of 3rd Level was... thanks to the help of his teacher, Immortal Guwol (九月仙子).

Immortal Guwol, the Crown Prince's teacher. Even though that Crown Prince was dead, her power was no less than that of any high-ranking official.

,"Ah, I see. You're thinking of asking him to use Immortal Guwol's connections? Certainly, with Immortal Guwol, meeting the Sima Clan Head would be nothing. You've thought it through well. But will Seong Jigone help you?"

,"How close do you think I am with that guy? Of course, he'll help."

,"Hmm. I don't intend to criticize your half-brother... but be careful. The rumors I'm hearing aren't good."

,"The rumors aren't good?"

,"I hear he often meets the mistresses of prestigious families for the sake of power. They say he's the embodiment of ambition."

Seong Jigone wants power?

The mistresses of prestigious families would be women of a certain age. The reason Seong Jigone meets them isn't because of his ambition, but....

,"......."

I closed my mouth. It was something I didn't want to imagine.
