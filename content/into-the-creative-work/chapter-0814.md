Nephia began to organize her belongings. She left the maid uniform as is, packing clothes, paper, pens, and other items into her backpack.

,...Hey. Nephia. What's this fancy dress? Is this yours too?"

Terrius asked, pointing to a dazzling purple dress adorned with sparkling jewels. It was definitely not something a slave should own.

Nephia smiled faintly at the dress and nodded.

"It's mine. About a year ago, Master-nim... no, not Master-nim anymore. Eugene-nim gave it to me as a birthday present."

"...Eugene-nim gave it to you...? To you?"

A master celebrating a slave's birthday? It was beyond Terrius's comprehension. When he was a mercenary's slave, he never received a gift, not even on the mercenary's good days. He had received leftover food, but he never considered that a gift.

"It's not just me, other maids also receive gifts on their birthdays. Though it's rare to receive a dress like this, like I did."

"...That dress. Have you ever worn it?"

Unless you're a noble. In Terrius's mind, there was no reason for a slave to wear a dress.

Nephia frowned, openly expressing her displeasure at Terrius's words.

"It's true I was a slave. But do you have to say it like that?"

"S-Sorry. I was too insensitive."

"...There are surprisingly many occasions to wear a dress. This mansion holds a banquet every two months, or at least every three months. A banquet that even servants like me can attend. I've already worn this dress several times. It's one of my treasures."

"I see..."

Nephia carefully folded the dress and put it in her backpack. Other items that Terrius didn't recognize were neatly piled up.

"...That dagger?"

"It's for self-defense. Well-made, isn't it? Eugene-nim gave me this too, it's a dagger made by a dwarf, no less. This is also my treasure."

"A, an amazing dagger."

It was an incredibly wonderful dagger.

Terrius was on the verge of suggesting they sell the dress and dagger. Selling those would allow him to live comfortably for years. But looking at Nephia's sparkling eyes, he couldn't bring himself to say it. How could he suggest selling Nephia's most cherished treasures?

"This is my most cherished treasure."

"A ring...?"

"I received it from Master-nim not long ago. I kept it hidden in the drawer because I was afraid of losing it...."

It was a gold ring with a diamond. Nephia put the diamond ring on the ring finger of her left hand.

"Pretty, isn't it?"

"Y-Yes, pretty. But that ring... why are you wearing it on your left ring finger? Do you know the meaning of the left ring finger...?"

"Huh? Ah! I know about the customs of the North. You're talking about wearing a wedding ring on the ring finger, right? But that's just a custom of the North. It doesn't mean anything."

"...That's right."

Terrius looked at his left hand. He could see the iron ring on his ring finger. He had never thought the iron ring was strange, but today it looked very shabby.

"Nephia. The iron ring I gave you...."

"I'm keeping it safe. I always carry it with me because it's precious, you know?"

Nephia took out a necklace from her chest. The iron ring hung on the chain. He was relieved that she always kept it with her.

"...Since the iron ring is a ring, why don't you wear it on your finger? It would be safer that way. I... I'd like Nephia to wear that ring on her left ring finger... like me."

"Huh? Sorry. My fingers are bigger now than they used to be, so the iron ring doesn't fit."

"I'll resize it to fit Nephia's finger again."

"It's okay. I don't want to wear the iron ring because it's uncomfortable and painful to wear. And it's okay since I'll keep it as a necklace, right?"

"...You're right."

Nephia packed all her belongings. Terrius quickly grabbed the backpack and held it in his left hand. It was quite heavy, but he didn't show it.

"Terrius. Let me carry it."

"No. It's okay. Let me carry it."

"...Okay. I'll have dinner at the mansion and then leave. Is that okay?"

"It's okay. I have a carriage that I came in. It's close to the Puters Estate, so we can leave at night."

Nephia and Terrius left the room. They headed to the dining hall. On the way, they bumped into a few maids.

"Nephia? Why are you dressed like that...? And who's the man next to you?"

"...This is Terrius. I told you about him before, right?"

"Ah. Your childhood friend. That's right. Are you leaving the mansion?"

"Yes. I'm not a slave anymore."

"...I don't know the details, but good luck. I hope you do well there."

Terrius felt something was off. All the maids they encountered wished Nephia well and cheered her on with short greetings. Even if their words were just formalities, their eyes all pitied Nephia.

'...Is it just my imagination?'

Shouldn't they be envious of Nephia for gaining her freedom? Terrius felt uneasy for no reason.

Soon, Terrius entered the dining hall and was surprised. It was very spacious and clean. There was also a lot of food to eat. Terrius followed Nephia and filled his plate with food. He only had one arm, so he needed Nephia's help.

He was shocked after eating the food.

'Delicious!'

Just saying it was delicious was not enough. He had eaten noble cuisine with Kyle's help before, but even that was lacking compared to this.

Especially the chicken and the drink called cola were revolutionary.

"Is it good? Does it suit your taste?"

"Huh? Yeah. It's really delicious. ...Is it okay for me to eat this?"

"It's okay. We eat this every day for breakfast, lunch, and dinner. If you look around, the other maids do too."

Terrius looked around. They were all maids. And they all had beautiful appearances. They approached Nephia, exchanged a few words, and disappeared. Some maids even gave Nephia farewell gifts.

"Terrius. Try this too. It's carbonara, it's popular in the mansion these days."

"I've never had it before, but it's incredibly delicious."

"Try this steak too. Melissa-eonni likes steak."

"This is... the most delicious meat I've ever eaten."

The meal continued. Terrius's hand, which had been devouring the food, gradually slowed down, and eventually stopped altogether. He was full, but there was still a lot of food on the table.

"...Nephia. Sorry, I can't eat anymore. This food...."

"It's okay. We can just throw it away."

"...Throw it away? This much food?"

"It's not a meal if you're forcing yourself to eat when you're full, it's torture. Look over there. It's common to throw away food in this mansion."

In fact, the maids were throwing away the leftover food without hesitation. Among the food being thrown away was the steak that Terrius had praised, which commoners couldn't easily get their hands on.

A mere maid was living in luxury like a noble.

"......."

Terrius felt dizzy. Something was wrong with this mansion.

Nephia and Terrius, who had thrown away all the leftover food, left the dining hall and went straight out of the mansion. Nephia's atmosphere was quiet.

Terrius was relieved to know that she hadn't suffered like him in this mansion. At the same time, he worried if he had done something unnecessary.

'...No. I did well. If I hadn't saved Nephia, she would have continued to be raped and humiliated by Baron Prukers.'

Nephia's face as she was being held by Eugene came to mind. It wasn't the face of a woman being humiliated.

'It's an act. It must be an act to please Baron Prukers.'

They came out of the mansion's main gate. The soldiers guarding the entrance stared at them with emotionless eyes. The sun was slowly setting. The evening weather was chilly.

"Nephia. This way."

"Okay."

They got into the carriage waiting in the stable. The carriage headed to the Puters Estate. It would take half a day, so they would arrive at dawn.

The carriage started. Silence flowed between Terrius and Nephia. Terrius was flustered by the awkward atmosphere and hurriedly opened his mouth.

"N-Nephia. How have you been since that day? I... I became a mercenary's slave...."

"Terrius. You must have suffered a lot."

"You suffered too, not just me. You...."

He couldn't say, "You were raped by Eugene Prukers."

"Me? I didn't suffer much. The food was delicious, I learned to read, I had friends my age. There was nothing difficult."

"R-Really? That's a relief...."

"......."

"...I-I was appointed as a knight this time. The lord I serve is Kyle Prukers-nim, the brother of Baron Eugene Prukers. Captain Kyle doesn't have a title yet, but he's sure to become a great person. I first met Captain Kyle when I was a mercenary's slave...."

Terrius told his story in detail. It was to ease the awkward atmosphere even a little. Nephia quietly listened to him. Their conversation stopped in less than two hours.

Deolkeongdeolkeong.

Only the sound of the carriage running rang out regularly.

•••

They arrived at Terrius's house. His house was only slightly larger than a commoner's house. Compared to Nephia's room in Eugene's mansion, it wasn't even half the size.

Nephia stared blankly at the house. It was an old house that looked like it was 20 years old.

"...This is the house I received after becoming a knight this time. I originally received a better house, but... I needed money urgently...."

"I-It's because of me. It can't be helped...."

They went inside the house. There were only two rooms in total. There were no lights on the ceiling, so they had to light candles. Even with the candles lit, the room was dark.

Nephia's face turned pale. She saw a bug crawling on the floor. The sanitary condition was so bad that it wouldn't be strange if a rat popped out from an unseen corner. The smell coming from the house was also foul.

"There's a bed in the inner room. So... Nephia. If it's okay with you, let's lie down and sleep together."

Terrius said, feeling his heart pounding. His face was flushed red.

"...No. You use the bed. I'll... sleep in the chair."

"What?! S-Sorry Nephia. I think I was too inconsiderate. You use the bed! I'll sleep in the chair!"

"It's really okay. I'll sleep in the chair."

Terrius said it several times, but he couldn't break Nephia's stubbornness.

The reason Nephia stubbornly refused was because the bed was also dirty.

"Hey Terrius. Is there a refrigerator or a wardrobe?"

"There's a wardrobe, but... sorry about the refrigerator. It's an expensive item, so I don't have it yet. I'll definitely buy it for you later when I save up money."

She took her eyes off the wardrobe. The wardrobe, which was so old that it was starting to rot, was not a place where she dared to put her clothes.

"...Water? I don't see a faucet or an induction stove either?"

"You have to draw water from the well. There's water drawn in the corner. I don't know what an induction stove is... What is it?"

"...You mean a fireplace. We need to cook, don't we?"

"Ah. You can do it there. We're short on firewood. I'll wake up early tomorrow morning and take care of it."

"A shower room or a bathroom... of course, there isn't one, right?"

"Sorry. There isn't one either. I'll try to get a bathtub somehow...."

"...What about food for tomorrow morning?"

"I'll get it from the cafeteria tomorrow. I don't usually eat breakfast, but... it seems like you eat breakfast. Ah, are you going to cook yourself?"

"Sorry. I'm okay with other things, but I can't cook."

"R-Really?"

The next day came.

Nephia stared at the food on the table. Hard bread and warm soup. She took a sip of the soup. It was bland. The bread was too hard and very difficult to eat. It was basically tasteless.

"Nephia. Does the food not suit your taste?"

"...Sorry. Terrius. It's too tasteless, I can't eat it."

"Really? It can't be helped...."

"Terrius. I want to eat meat."

"...Meat? Ah, okay. I'll bring it tonight. And... take this money. Take care of lunch today."

What Terrius handed over was 8 copper coins and 4 iron coins. 8,400 Ner. It was enough money for a decent meal for a commoner.

"Where are you going today?"

"I have to inform Captain Kyle that I've returned. And there are things I have to do as a knight. I'll be back before sunset."

"...Okay."

Terrius left.

Nephia, who was left alone, thought of cleaning first. She planned to finish cleaning while the sun was up. But she couldn't see a single cleaning tool. She went into the bedroom to find cleaning tools.

She entered the bedroom and covered her nose with her hand. There was a foul smell. Terrius's clothes and belongings were scattered haphazardly. She approached the bed and was shocked. Small bugs were swarming on the yellowish blanket.

"Ew!"

Feeling nauseous, Nephia covered her mouth with her hand and hurriedly came out of the house. She didn't want to go inside.

Some thugs reached out to her, but they were defeated by her Aura Expert Intermediate level and ran away with their tails between their legs. She was stronger than Terrius.

She solved lunch with two skewers.

Nephia, who returned home, sat blankly in front of the table. Tears welled up in Nephia's eyes. This was hell.

She took out a space-time teleportation scroll from her bosom. Eugene said to stay with him for only about a week, but she didn't think she could stand it. She wanted to go back right now.
