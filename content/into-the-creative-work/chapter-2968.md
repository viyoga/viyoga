「All sectors of Mount Hua Island have been conquered.」

This wasn’t the end of everything.

It’s the endgame. There’s still a finale left.

「12 hours later, a sealed being on Mount Hua Island awakens. If you cannot stop him, all sectors of Mount Hua Island will be reset.」

Even if we fail, there’s a chance.

Although the problem is we’d have to start over from the beginning.

‘12 hours. It’s the preparation time and rest time given by the System. It’s also enough time for internal strife to break out.’

Around noon, a red dragon will appear.

I know where it will appear. The Mount Hua at the center of Mount Hua Island. The red dragon will appear from that Mount Hua, which is endlessly scattering volcanic ash across all of Atlantis.

‘If we surround the Mount Hua and unleash firepower the moment the red dragon appears… even if we can’t kill the red dragon, we can inflict significant damage. However, the problem is….’

Will the giant Legions that participated in the Mount Hua Island conquest cooperate willingly?

As giant Legions, they have some experience dealing with colossal Monsters. So, they will trust their own strength and proceed with the conquest on their own. The one who kills the red dragon, an enemy of the world, will receive the greatest credit.

‘Competition will begin. The Legions that will be most wary of… are probably the Demonic Cult, A-Plan, and Tetrix.’

There will be no instance of everyone joining hands to fight for Atlantis.

• • •

Having conquered Sector 8,866, I returned to where Kang Myungjin was. All the Legion members were gathered. Meld Norris was still there too.

Meld looked at me with a meaningful expression.

“Did you conquer this sector?”

“I did. Is there a problem?”

“…Your tone is sharp. Did you suffer an unexpected attack?”

“It’s just that I don’t particularly enjoy talking to old women.”

“Huh.”

Meld let out a hollow laugh. That was the end of it. She brushed it off as if such a provocation was nothing.

Though it was just me spouting off without thinking, provocation or not.

“I heard you’re a contractor of the Lord of the Sky. Someone I know also contracted with the Lord of the Sky. Even though they contracted with the same Divine Seat, their personalities are polar opposites.”

“There aren’t many Divine Seats who care about personality.”

“Hmm. Undeniable words. A-Plan Legion Master. I’ll be going now. We’ll meet again if there’s a chance.”

Meld, after a brief greeting to Kang Myungjin, disappeared into the forest.

‘From the moment she disappeared from sight, her presence completely vanished. As expected, she’s no ordinary old hag.’

With the uninvited guest gone, the tense atmosphere loosened.

In that situation, Kang Myungjin spoke to the members.

“If we return to Sector 8,811, all the other Legions will be gathered, and the observers will be watching us. Our objective hasn’t changed. We will fail. So, focus on survival above all else. Falling back to the rear wouldn’t be a bad idea either.”

“Myungjin Oppa talks as if he’ll move differently from us?”

“That’s right. Seong Yujin, Joo Seohyun, and I will focus on conquering the red dragon.”

“……”

Lee Minjeong closed her mouth. Dissatisfaction clouded her expression, but she couldn’t voice it carelessly. The three of us, singled out by Kang Myungjin, were the strongest in the A-Plan Legion. The members knew well that if they questioned it here, they would only be told that they were too weak.

“Let’s move. If we return too late, the other Legions will become suspicious.”

We returned to Sector 8,811.

Kang Myungjin and the other Legion Masters gathered separately for a meeting. Meanwhile, we took a Rest in the barracks we hadn’t visited in a long time.

Kang Myungjin returned in 15 minutes.

I used a Qi Barrier to prevent our conversation from being overheard by others.

“The meeting seems to have ended quickly?”

“Seong Yujin. Weren’t you expecting it? There wasn’t even a need for a meeting. No cooperation. The Legions decided to each proceed with the red dragon conquest.”

Invoking the Emperor’s name… was useless. The Emperor was probably behind this whole affair. Even if the Mount Hua Island conquest failed, after a success, it would become a minor issue and be brushed aside. What matters to others is the success or failure.

“Heavenly Demon. No, do I not need to talk to her?”

“Elena and I already finished talking before coming to Mount Hua Island. There is a way to communicate separately… but we’re not doing so for security reasons. We don’t know what tricks the Emperor might have pulled. Ah, this isn’t my opinion, it’s Elena’s.”

“Is that so. She seems quite wary of the Emperor.”

“She’d know more about the Emperor than you or I. And you know the Emperor is no ordinary person, don’t you?”

“Right. I trust you’ll do well. Rest. You’ll be in for a tough time when you wake up.”

We parted ways.

I entered Joo Seohyun’s barracks.

• • •

Little time remained until the red dragon’s appearance.

The Legions scattered. Each would target the red dragon in their own way. Joo Seohyun, Kang Myungjin, and I positioned ourselves a little distance from Mount Hua Island.

In the sky near Mount Hua Island, about 20 Mages were floating. Kang Myungjin recognized them immediately upon seeing them.

“It’s the Torma Legion. A Legion primarily composed of Mages. Is their strategy to project instantaneous firepower and subjugate the red dragon the moment it appears?”

“Sounds like a not-bad strategy. I’d even bet 100,000 AP on them failing.”

“The Bet is not valid. Everyone else, not just you, has bet on them failing.”

That was why the other Legions weren’t wary of the Torma Legion. Because they were confident it would fail from the start.

‘The Torma Legion couldn’t possibly be unaware of that atmosphere. They must have a hidden trump card.’

Will that trump card actually work?

‘It’s a situation that didn’t appear in the Original Work, so I don’t know. I’ll find out when I see it.’

Ku-gu-gu-gu-gu-gung!

The ground vibrates.

The entire Mount Hua Island began to shake. The epicenter of the earthquake was the Mount Hua, from which volcanic ash endlessly rose.

Kung!

A roar echoed from its crater.

Kung! Kung! Kung!

The sound and vibration of something colossal colliding rang out incessantly.

Everyone watching the Mount Hua from various locations could tell. Something colossal was crawling up from inside the Mount Hua.

A colossal Magic Circle was drawn around the Torma Legion’s Mages. They were preparing a grand Magic.

The black ash spewing from the Mount Hua’s crater temporarily stopped. Still, black ash remained in the sky, slowly scattering to the ground.

Kuuung!!

Finally, the thing that had crawled up the Mount Hua’s crater revealed itself.

A colossal red dragon.

It had 7 Heads, and each Head had 10 Horns growing from it.

Above its 7 Heads, a black, ominous crown floated like a halo.

「The Crown of Ruin (False) has appeared in Atlantis.」

The seven Heads of the Apocalyptic Red Dragon roared towards the world.

“——–!!!”

A grotesque scream that failed to form a sound.

At the ferocious roar that struck human instinct, Kang Myungjin and Joo Seohyun’s Bodies swayed slightly. I stood still.

‘It feels similar to Domination Step.’

It was only similar, not the same.

‘That’s too strong. No matter what I do, I don’t think I can win. It’s among the top Divine Seats. Among them, it seems comparable to a chief deity like Zeus.’

「It is not yet time for the apocalypse to begin.」

「The seven sins disappear.」

The System spoke.

It was like a declaration.

The black crown that was above the seven Heads of the Apocalyptic Red Dragon vanished without a trace.

The power of the Apocalyptic Red Dragon rapidly diminished. It wasn’t just a matter of limbs being severed. Its very essence, its Authority, vanished. In terms of power, it must have weakened by at least half.

Of course, even then, it didn’t seem possible to defeat the Apocalyptic Red Dragon.

「As the Dominator of Mount Hua Island sectors, including Sector 8,807, you can restrict the Crown of Ruin (False).」

「Would you like to restrict the Crown of Ruin (False)?」

All those who dominated the Mount Hua Island sectors promised to cooperate.

No, if we don’t restrict it, we can’t catch that thing.

“Restrict it.”

Kang Myungjin and I, who held the Domination rights to the Mount Hua Island sectors, spoke. Perhaps the Dominator of another Legion also spoke.

「All Dominators of Mount Hua Island sectors have chosen to restrict the Crown of Ruin (False).」

「The power of the Crown of Ruin (False) weakens.」

The power of the Apocalyptic Red Dragon clearly weakens. The oppressive aura emanating from its very existence vanishes, and its size shrinks by more than half. Its sharp Teeth and claws dull, and its scales lose their luster. Five of its Heads disappear, leaving only two.

That is no longer the Apocalyptic Red Dragon.

It’s just a red dragon with two Heads.

And it doesn’t end there.

“System. I’ll use the Stigma of Death on that Lizard.”

The Authority that can be used on beings within the Mount Hua Island sectors.

「The Stigma of Death has been used on the red dragon.」

「Due to the effect of the Stigma of Death, the red dragon cannot recover from injuries while on Mount Hua Island.」

Even the System affirmed that it was no longer the Apocalyptic Red Dragon.

This isn’t the end.

Just as I could conquer Sector 8,866 and use the Stigma of Death, others could also use similar Authority.

「Someone used the Stigma of Famine on the red dragon.」

「Due to the effect of the Stigma of Famine, the red dragon cannot recover mana while on Mount Hua Island.」

「Someone used the Stigma of War on the red dragon.」

「Due to the effect of the Stigma of War, the red dragon cannot use Weapons while on Mount Hua Island.」

「Someone used the Stigma of Conquest on the red dragon.」

「Due to the effect of the Stigma of Conquest, the red dragon cannot use one specific ability while on Mount Hua Island.」

「The red dragon cannot use mental attack abilities.」

I could guarantee it.

The red dragon wouldn’t be able to exert even 1% of its original power.

“Kang Myungjin. That red Lizard seems to be glaring at me, is it just my imagination?”

“It’s probably not your imagination. It’s wary of you. Probably for the same reason Biya fears you.”

Biya was a dragon contracted with Kang Myungjin.

Recently, whenever I’m around, she burrows into Kang Myungjin’s embrace and doesn’t show herself. She completely hides her presence, making it feel as if she’s not there.

The reason is probably because I am a Dragon Slayer. I even have the Title officially.

「If the red dragon is not subjugated within 6 hours, the Mount Hua Island sectors will be reset.」

As soon as the System’s notification ended, the Torma Legion’s Mages cast the grand Magic they had been preparing.

The sky, filled with black ash, split open, and a meteor from outer space plummeted rapidly towards the red dragon.

The red dragon’s two Heads gaped open towards the falling meteor. Red light condensed in their mouths. Then, red beams of light were fired. Two streams of red light accurately pierced the falling meteor. The meteor shattered into pieces without even breaking through the stratosphere.

The red dragon flapped its wing membranes and charged at the horrified Torma Legion Mages. The terrified Mages scattered and fled, but some were devoured by the red dragon.

I saw the mana that the red dragon had consumed while using its Breath attack recovering.

‘No. It’s not recovery. That bastard can’t recover mana because of the Stigma of Famine. But it doesn’t mean it can’t absorb it.’

It was an ability that appeared in the Original Work. It absorbs not only mana but also life force.

‘Breath and absorption. That’s all the abilities that bastard has now.’

I laughed.

‘Keke. No need to even take out Gram; Balmung will be more than enough.’
