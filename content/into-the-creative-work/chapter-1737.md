「Investigate the Forest of Bewilderment.

Recently, the monsters in the Forest of Bewilderment have drastically decreased. Arinna wants to know the reason. Investigate the Forest of Bewilderment and inform Arinna of the reason for the drastic decrease in monsters.

Reward: Experience, Arinna's Flower.」

"I'll let you know as soon as I get back, so please wait a moment."

「Quest accepted.」

The reason I accepted the quest was because the Forest of Bewilderment was a place I had to go anyway. It's even better to receive a quest and get items and experience at the same time.

'I probably won't be able to move on directly after the Forest of Bewilderment.'

It was clear that level restrictions would be applied, trying to hold us back as before. Even if I don't know for sure, I'll probably have to hunt in the Forest of Bewilderment for at least a week.

'And Arinna's Flower isn't just a useless item either.'

「Arinna's Flower

Prevents you from getting lost in the Forest of Bewilderment.

The effect is activated by placing it in your inventory.」

I winked at Seong Ha-ri behind me. Hesitantly approaching the NPC, she spoke in awkward Japanese.

"I, I will help you too."

"Oh my. I would be grateful if you would."

The NPC smiled and gave Seong Ha-ri the quest as well.

Terra Simulator boasts a high virtual reality implementation rate, but as a game, it has more than one or two shortcomings. The most representative of these was the absence of a community system. There is no party system, and no friend registration system. Of course, there was no function to have conversations between friends either.

'Once companions get separated, it's hard to meet again.'

So I was paying special attention to not getting separated from Seong Ha-ri. It wouldn't be strange to be together 24 hours a day.

"Mom. Did you pack potions?"

"I don't really need to pack them, they're piled up in my inventory. Are they really necessary?"

"There might be troublesome monsters ahead."

Of course, the possibility was small. We hunted easily in the previous hunting ground, so there was no way a monster that was difficult to handle would suddenly appear.

Seong Ha-ri and I went around the village, talking to NPCs about the Forest of Bewilderment. It was to receive quests.

"Do you know about the Forest of Bewilderment?"

"The Forest of Bewilderment? It's a famous forest!"

Most of them repeat the same words in this way. It couldn't be helped. It's the limit of the NPC.

At first, I even thought about violating the NPC without Seong Ha-ri knowing. But seeing the NPC's doll-like attitude, my interest completely disappeared.

"Hoo. I lost my child in the Forest of Bewilderment.... I, a hunter, underestimated the Forest of Bewilderment too much."

There are cases where the NPC says something different, spouting a story that you are not even curious about. At this time, you have to spit out keywords based on your intuition.

"Forest of Bewilderment, hunting, child, keepsake, revenge."

If one of the keywords hits, the NPC will react.

"You're going into the Forest of Bewilderment? Then could you find my dead child's belongings? At that time, my child went into the Forest of Bewilderment with a toy... I still can't find it."

"Does the monster have that toy?"

"I don't know. However, there doesn't seem to be any reason for the monster to take the toy. Will you find my child's toy?"

「Child's Keepsake.

Find the toy that the hunter's child, who went missing in the Forest of Bewilderment, used to play with and return it to the hunter.

Reward: Experience, Weapon of Bewilderment.」

'Equipment item quest! It's here!'

I held back from shouting for joy. It was difficult to obtain equipment in this virtual reality game. The things sold in stores were all trash. The only useful items are those dropped by monsters with a low probability and those given as quest rewards.

"Of course, I'll find it for you."

"Thank you."

「Quest accepted.」

"Mom. Mom, get the quest too."

"Ah, okay. What should I say?"

I told Seong Ha-ri the Japanese keywords she needed to say. After that, she and I went around the village. There were no additional quests received.

'If those bastards had shared the information, I wouldn't have had to go through this trouble.'

The students of Caso Academy had already been playing Terra Simulator for 3 years. If those guys had helped, things would have been easier. However, those guys don't even try to talk to us. Ignoring us is the least of it, and there were even guys who blatantly glared at us or picked fights.

'Anyway, they're bastards I don't like.'

「Entered the Forest of Bewilderment.」

「Status ailment Bewilderment is applied.」

「The probability of getting lost increases.」

「Detection ability is lowered.」

「There is a low probability of falling into the status ailment Confusion.」

「There is a low probability of seeing the status ailment Illusion.」

「There is a low probability of experiencing the status ailment Darkness.」

As soon as I entered, notification windows popped up one after another.

Having experienced the [God's Atlantis] world, I thought nothing of it, but Seong Ha-ri seemed quite surprised.

"Yujin-ah! My detection ability has really decreased."

Seong Ha-ri made a fuss. It was understandable. Her detection ability suddenly decreased, making her feel frustrated. It was like entering a narrow indoor space from an open meadow.

'I can only see within a radius of 10m.'

Even if I try to see beyond that, it's too dark to see.

"Mom. Be careful not to get separated. If we get separated here... you know without me having to say it, right?"

"It's okay, so don't worry about Mom."

We wandered around the Forest of Bewilderment with a relaxed mind. Even if monsters appeared, we didn't think we would be defeated at all.

We wandered around for 20 minutes, but we couldn't see a single small animal, let alone a monster.

"Yujin-ah. It doesn't seem like there will be an end to this. I didn't know when I saw it from the outside, but the forest is too wide."

"Is there any other way?"

"It would be better to destroy the forest."

It was a simple, ignorant, yet clear solution. I didn't stop Seong Ha-ri. To be honest, I didn't like this forest either.

Seong Ha-ri drew her sword. A blue aura flickered from her blade. As she concentrated and breathed, the aura became sharper. Then Seong Ha-ri swung her sword. A huge slash flew in a crescent shape, cutting down trees. More than ten trees slowly fell to the ground.

"Mom. When did you learn that skill?"

"Skill? I just swung it with force. You can do it too, Yujin."

"I can...."

To do it as quickly, accurately, and powerfully as Seong Ha-ri, I would need quite a bit of time to concentrate.

We narrowed our eyes as we looked at the fallen trees. Even though the trees had fallen, we couldn't see beyond 10m.

"It doesn't seem like this method will work."

She sighed and I shrugged. This world is just a fake, but it wasn't easy.

"We have no choice but to proceed step by step."

From some point on, I felt a gaze watching us. I turned my head towards the direction of the gaze. I could only see trees.

'Are they watching us from beyond 10m?'

It was highly likely to be a monster. And we shouldn't be careless just because it's a monster. The monsters in this world felt like they had much better artificial intelligence than the NPCs. At least in terms of combat.

I subtly checked Seong Ha-ri's reaction. I could feel that she was slightly nervous. She noticed the presence of the monster. Judging from the way her hand holding the sword hilt was twitching, it seemed like she wanted to rush out and deal with the monster right away.

"Yujin-ah. You feel it too, right?"

"Mom. Calm down. It might be a trap. Let's go slowly. Slowly. If we get separated here... you know without me saying it, right?"

"I know."

We slowly moved towards the place where we felt the gaze. Fortunately, we could still feel the gaze.

「The Forest of Bewilderment obscures your vision for 10 minutes.」

"?!"

In an instant, my vision went dark. I couldn't see anything. I panicked and flailed my arms and legs.

"Yujin-ah! Why are you suddenly like this?"

Seong Ha-ri grabbed my shoulder tightly.

"I can't see. I think I'm under a status ailment."

"That... Cure? Can't you heal it with that?"

Cure was a skill of the Priest, a support job. The effect was to remove status ailments. I used Cure on myself.

"Cure."

Some of my mana disappeared and my mind became clearer. My vision returned for a moment, but it immediately flickered out. The world was still dark.

「Cure's level is too low. Cannot remove status ailment.」

Only the notification window was clear in the dark world.

"It doesn't work."

"It's okay, Yujin-ah. Mom is here, right? Mom will protect you."

Seong Ha-ri grabbed my right hand with her left hand. She intertwined her fingers with mine and held on tightly. It had been a long time since I had held hands with her like this. What kind of expression was she making now? I couldn't tell.

"Let's go slowly."

I moved as she led me. Then Seong Ha-ri stopped. I could feel her hand trembling slightly.

I was puzzled. According to my senses, there was nothing within 10m. It was the moment I was about to call out to Seong Ha-ri.

Squeeze. Seong Ha-ri strongly gripped my hand, and at the same time, someone's voice was heard.

"Ha-ri-ssi. I like you. Please allow me to stand by your side. I swear I won't hold Ha-ri-ssi back. I will be Ha-ri-ssi's support."

It was a man's voice. He was spouting content that made me feel bad. I looked to the left where the voice was heard and used Cure.

'Suddenly hitting on someone else's woman? Let's see what kind of bastard he is! Cure!'

「Cure's level is too low. Cannot remove status ailment.」

Earlier, my vision returned for a moment, but now only the notification window popped up. It's a shame that I can't raise Cure's level because I don't have any skill points.

"You ignorant bitch. Are you showing off because you're a little strong?"

It was a venomous woman's voice. I could deeply feel the jealousy and hatred contained in that voice.

"Seong Ha-ri. There's no reason for you to leave the academy. It's not your fault. I'll talk to the headmaster, so wait a moment."

This time it was a familiar voice.

It was definitely the voice of Kang Ji-young, Seong Ha-ri's friend and the current headmaster of Maruhan Academy.

"What is your wish?"

I also heard a heavy and dignified man's voice.
