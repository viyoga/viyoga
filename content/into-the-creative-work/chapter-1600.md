-Stop.

It's not like some force grabbed me, or that my body wouldn't listen. I just stopped; that's the only way to describe it.

-In the Demon World, there was a rotating waterfall. A popular spot among demons for its beautiful scenery. One demon wanted to see that scenery forever, so he used his power. The power of stasis. That demon used his power as he pleased. He'd see a pretty bird flying and taxidermize it mid-air, or find a dying demon amusing and freeze him to prevent death, then put him in a clear glass case. To prevent chaos in the Demon World, I captured him and sealed him in the deepest underground, where even moonlight couldn't reach. You're giving me that look like, 'Why are you telling me this?' I only deliberately created an opening for that woman to appear. She hasn't shown up, perhaps she noticed my intentions.

The power of stasis.

He's using another troublesome power.

The Demon King is approaching me. I held my breath, waiting for an opportunity.

Within arm's reach. I used magic.

Thank goodness I'm a fairy who can use magic with just my will.

,Ether Magic.'

Ether wraps around my body.

It's as if I'm swimming through the earth, my whole body heavy, but I started to move. The surprised Demon King flies upward.

[Using an Instant. Remaining stacks: 6]

I follow with Instant, swinging my sword at his waist.

Kkaang!

An Aura Blade that cuts through steel like tofu was blocked. The energy flowing through the Demon King's body is negating the Aura Blade.

Thunder Sky Style Ultimate Technique: Sealing Thunder.

Pajijik.

Lightning, with the property of disrupting mana, flows along the Aura Blade to the Demon King. The energy flowing through the Demon King's body wavered unstably.

-Impressive. If I had been a little more careless, I might have suffered a fatal wound.

He waves his hand.

The stasis is released. Instead, gravity grabs my body, pulling me to the ground.

Kwang!

The impact, starting from my back, spread throughout my body. I groaned and tried to get up. It wasn't easy because of the gravity still pulling me down.

-Did you know? Among demons, there was one with the power to turn back time. It was a convenient power, one of my favorites. But recently, it disappeared, so the power is gone. That woman probably killed him. Am I wrong?

,Do you think I'll readily answer your questions?

-Calm down. I'm just trying to have a conversation with you right now.

,I have no reason to talk to you.

-Really? No reason to talk? You might need to stall for time until that woman arrives.

He noticed that Yuria isn't here.

Desperately managing my expression from hardening, I grip my sword again. It could just be a bluff.

-I could have killed you earlier. I had 17 opportunities. That woman would have noticed for sure. But she didn't show up. Unless that woman abandoned you... she must not be here.

The Demon King's words were filled with certainty. I sighed.

I'll change my approach. I decided to stall for time with conversation, also to recover from the fatigue accumulated from the aftereffects of Thunder Sky.

,You said you wanted to talk? What's your purpose?

-I can't understand why you guys are hostile towards me.

,You tried to take Yuria's body first, didn't you?

-Didn't that end in failure? And I know. You came to this labyrinth to kill me. I don't know how you found out I was here, though.

,Are you suggesting we hold hands or something?

-I will acknowledge half of this world as human territory. I will recognize you as its ruler. In return, I will rule the other half as demon land.

,What if I refuse? Are you going to kill me or something? You Demon King bastard.

-Human. I have acknowledged you, but the only reason I'm negotiating is because of that woman who follows you. I can kill you anytime I want. Watch your mouth.

,I refuse. Do you think I don't know your dirty intentions? Eventually, you'll stab me in the back. Just like you stabbed the Great Demon in the back to take the Demon King's throne!

-...You're a human who knows a lot. I see. I'll break your spirit first, then we'll talk again. I'll grant you the power of immortality. After dying about a hundred times, you'll feel like talking to me.

The gravity binding my body disappears. Immediately after, red energy flies towards me from his hand. I knew that was the power of immortality. I decided to take it. If I don't die, I can keep killing him until I kill him. Even if I can't kill him, I can buy a lot of time.

-Got you.

,I can't... use my power!

-The power of immortality doesn't exist. If there was such an excellent power, I wouldn't be going through this trouble.

,What power is this then? The power of restraint?

-It's the power of Heavenly Punishment. Once restrained, you can't escape until I release you. You are a hostage. And from now on, I will torture you. Tell me all the information about that woman, what her weaknesses are.

Tsujujujut.

His hand, made of energy, reaches out.

I had no intention of being tortured.

,Heavenly Heart.'

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The restraints are released. At the same time, the fairy's body returned to a human body. I felt a sense of alienation from the suddenly larger body.

[Using an Instant. Remaining stacks: 5]

A sword filled with Aura Blade stabs the jewel on his chest, the purplish-black gem.

Kkang!

The sword bounced off. The gem wasn't even scratched.

-I don't know how you broke free from the power of Heavenly Punishment, but... you've become weaker than when you were a fairy.

Kiiiiiiiiing!

Energy gathered in his right hand, taking the form of a sword. It was the moment he was about to swing the sword at me.

Chwareureureureuk.

The Demon King gave up his attack and retreated, dodging the shadow chains.

,These chains are....

,I'm sorry, Master-nim. I'm a little late.

,Yuria! I knew you'd come.

,Yes. Thank you for believing in me. I would like to have a leisurely conversation with Master-nim, but... I don't think we have that much time.

,You got the Shadow Chains, Gleipnir?

,Yes. It was quite a struggle, but it's worth it.

Ttak!

Yuria snapped her fingers.

Chains surged from every shadow around us. The ends of the chains were sharp like stakes.

-Stop.

The Demon King used the power of stasis. The shadow chains seemed to freeze in mid-air under its influence, but they slowly moved, then accelerated rapidly, rushing towards the Demon King.

-Annoying.

,As expected, the Demon King's power is troublesome. At this rate, even Yuria....

I couldn't see anything. It was a perfect darkness, without a trace of light. I knew this technique.

,Eternal Sky Style's Darkness!

A technique that momentarily darkens the space.

I never imagined that he could darken such a wide space so perfectly.

The darkness disappeared in just 2 seconds.

2 seconds.
