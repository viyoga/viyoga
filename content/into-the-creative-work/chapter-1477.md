,Paladin-isiyeo, welcome. I'm Amelia. As the Saintess of Valentier, I've been tasked with guiding and assisting you on behalf of the Goddess."

The Saintess of Valentier spoke in a calm voice.

Saintess.

There are as many of them as there are Paladins. They receive the Goddess's will and assist and lead the Paladins.

In the game, she was the most important NPC. She was the beginning and the end of the main quest.

Originally, the purification ritual could only be performed with the participation of multiple priests, including the bishop, but it was different for the Saintess. The Saintess, who received the role of assisting the Paladin from the Goddess, could perform the purification ritual alone.

I lie in the sacred pond. I'm completely submerged, even my head.

The souls of the demons and the corrupted clinging to my body are purified. Some of it seeped into me and became power, and the other part was sucked somewhere.

[Divinity increased by 1.]

I raise my upper body. The sacred pond splashed. I leaned against the edge of the pond and sat still. The pond, full of holy water, was very comfortable just being in it. Physical and mental fatigue are also disappearing quickly.

The Saintess came to my side. She carefully puts down a towel and new clothes. The clothes were gorgeous and luxurious, befitting the clothes worn by a Paladin.

I only moved my eyes and glanced at her face. The veil fluttered, and her face was slightly visible. It was the most beautiful face I had ever seen in this world.

My gaze returned to the front. There is a jar placed in front of the sacred pond. The inside of the jar was glowing faintly.

The aura of blessing.

The aura of corruption purified from the sacred pond is contained in that jar.

,That's why I found this place as quickly as possible."

Only the Saintess can handle the aura of blessing, and only the Paladin can become stronger with the aura of blessing.
