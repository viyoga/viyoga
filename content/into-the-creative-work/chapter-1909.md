The entrance ceremony ended, and all the guests left the Maid Academy. However, the new students remained in the academy's entrance hall.

Cold. The heat of the entrance ceremony vanished as if it were a lie.

Yuria sat up straight, straightening her back. She quietly surveyed her surroundings. There were quite a few people sitting upright like her. They were the ones who had received some level of training as servants.

There were also maids who took a rather poor posture. They were rough. They knew etiquette but had no intention of putting it into practice. Their origins were easy to guess. They were probably from mercenary backgrounds. On the other hand, there were also some maids who appeared to be from military backgrounds. And then there were the maids who looked like they didn't know anything at all. They were overly nervous.

The goal of the students here was just one.

Maid certification.

Senior, Double, Triple, Flower, Superior, Royal, Prime.

A total of seven divided qualification grades. You can receive a Senior grade certificate just by graduating from the Maid Academy without failing.

Just having this certificate eliminates any worries about getting a job. Because you can get a job at almost any company. It's not just a story of Neo London. In other countries and cities, a maid certificate is considered a spec in itself. Perhaps because of that, there were quite a few foreigners.

Triple grade is treated as the elite of the elite. Not to mention the grades above that.

Yuria's goal was Royal or Prime. The higher her grade as a servant, the higher the status of her master. However, even Yuria couldn't be sure of achieving Prime grade. Because no maid had reached Prime grade in the past few years. Perhaps there were other hidden conditions for Prime.

Knock knock.

A woman with light brown hair sitting next to her carefully poked Yuria's shoulder with her index finger.

,"What is it?"

,"Since we met like this, let's say hello. I'm Juliet. I came from outside the district."

,"I'm Yuria Grace. I'm from Neo London."

Yuria answered kindly. She had no interest in Juliet, but the sociability section might be included in the evaluation. If Yuria were the dean of the Maid Academy, she would include that in the evaluation items as well.

,"I came up to Neo London, but... is the atmosphere always this fierce?"

,"Well... I don't know much about the Maid Academy either, since I'm also a newbie."

,"Really? You don't have to use jondaetmal. We're classmates."

,"I'm comfortable with this."

,"Okay. I guess so. Let's get along well until graduation without getting expelled from the Maid Academy. But do you know what we're doing after the entrance ceremony?"

,"That's probably..."

,"Quiet!"

A drill instructor shouted loudly. The nervous students all shut their mouths and looked at the drill instructor. Yuria and Juliet were the same.

In the quiet entrance hall, the dean opened her mouth.

,"There is an etiquette score in the academy. It is an item that evaluates your etiquette. The basic score is 10 points, and points will be deducted if you use vulgar language or show frivolous behavior. If you get 0 points, you will be expelled, so please be careful. Also, the etiquette score is also included in the grade."

Everyone shut their mouths. The faces of the students who were talking using vulgar language turned pale. They were mostly from mercenary backgrounds.

,"I will inform you of today's schedule. Within 2 hours, we will finish distributing textbooks and uniforms, assigning dormitories, and conduct dining etiquette training. In the evening, we will conduct a dining etiquette evaluation test. Of course, this evaluation also affects the overall grade."

,"......!!"

Most of the maids were shocked. They didn't expect to be trained and tested from the first day of enrollment. Yuria was also surprised. But the die was already cast. It doesn't seem like the schedule will change even if they protest. Yuria recalled Neo London's dining etiquette and simulated it in her head.

,"Today, we will educate and evaluate Divine France's Easter dining etiquette. Some of you may wonder why maids have to learn Divine France's dining etiquette. Maids must respond to guests on behalf of the master when they are not present. We are educating you in preparation for that eventuality. Don't forget that maids must be perfect."

Maria Hilton, the dean of the Maid Academy, came down from the platform and left the entrance hall. Yuria unconsciously looked at her gait and nodded. It was perfect without any flaws.

,"Haa. Divine France's Easter dining etiquette? Why do we have to learn that...?"

Juliet murmured with a tearful face.

,"Divine France is close to Neo London, so the dining etiquette won't be much different."

,"Yuria, you're so relaxed. Do you already know Divine France's Easter dining etiquette?"

,"I'll know when I learn it soon."

,"Haa. I envy that relaxed personality."

Soon they received textbooks and uniforms from the drill instructor.

Juliet looked like she was about to cry right away. The textbooks received by the students were so heavy that it was difficult to even hold them, with over 30 books, and the uniforms were all maid outfits, with 7 types. For going out, morning, afternoon, exercise, cooking, cleaning, and work.

The drill instructor said while handing out the clothes.

,"Clothing management is also subject to evaluation. Please be careful as points will be deducted if some of the clothes are torn or not clean."

,"Wait a minute. This size doesn't seem to fit very well?!"

,"This is the first thing you need to solve. We will provide you with a sewing kit here. We will show you how to repair it just once, so please repair the afternoon maid outfit and gather in the lecture room."

The maids reflexively checked the clock on the wall. There was more than an hour of free time. Still, the anxiety does not go away. Most of them knew nothing about sewing.

,'Sewing in this day and age....'

,'Hehe. I've been practicing after listening to the words of seniors who have already graduated.'

,'Ah, shit. I want to quit already. I just need to get a certificate, so why sewing....'

Everyone received a sewing kit with different thoughts.

Then, they were assigned dormitory rooms.

The room was small. 3 pyeong. A room with only a bed, wardrobe, desk, and chair. Yuria unpacked her luggage first and started repairing. It didn't take even 10 minutes to repair not only the afternoon maid outfit but also all seven maid outfits she received.

Knock knock.

,"Yes."

Yuria opened the door. Juliet was holding an afternoon maid outfit in her hand and making a tearful face.

,"Juliet-yang. What's going on?"

,"Um, um. Can you help me if you're not busy? It's my first time sewing... it's not working out."

,"I'll help you. It's not difficult if you try slowly."

,"Really? Thank you! But don't you have to do it too? I'm sorry if I'm bothering you...."

,"It's okay. I'm done with my work."

Yuria didn't just do Juliet's work for her, but taught her how to do it. She also helped any maids who seemed to be in trouble besides her. It wasn't an act of kindness. This will unknowingly become a debt. They will help her when she is in trouble.

After that, Yuria received a perfect score on the Divine France Easter dining etiquette and passed.

A week has passed since the 94th class of the Maid Academy entered. The dean and instructors gathered in the conference room. A regular weekly meeting.

,"How many dropouts are there in total?"

The dean, Maria, asked calmly.

,"There are 35 people."

,"That's average."

The period with the most dropouts from the Maid Academy was the first week. Because the regulations are stricter than other places, they cannot adapt. There were mainly mercenaries who were active freely.

,"Are there any notable figures? Do you think a Superior or higher maid will come out of this class?"

,"Perhaps a Royal or higher maid may come out."

Maria's eyes lit up. It's only been a week, but the instructor is already saying this? It means that an extraordinary talent has entered. There was someone she could guess.

,"I heard that the daughter of a count family entered. Is it that child?"

Aristocratic children also enter the Maid Academy. There is no law that says you must become a maid after graduating from the Maid Academy. They are trying to obtain a maid certificate as a kind of spec.

,"No. That child is also excellent, but... it's too early to judge her potential."

,"Then who is it?"

,"It's a child named Yuria Grace. She's from the Knight Training Center, and she doesn't lack anything, not only combat but also medical knowledge, culture, etiquette, cooking, appearance, and combat power. She has a good reputation among her classmates and has received perfect scores on all four tests she has taken so far. This is the first time there has been such an outstanding student."

,"If she's from the Knight Training Center, there are no special points in her status. Is she a talent who will become a royal maid?"

,"It's too early to judge that far, but we think she may become more than that. Yuria Grace has not yet shown her limits."

Maria slowly read the documents containing Yuria's information. There was nothing wrong with it. It's not a problem that she is being sponsored by Eugene Meyer, who recently became a baronet.

,"The royal family wants excellent maids. They need talent. If she maintains her grades like this... it seems like it would be enough to become a royal maid. Can you recruit her?"

,"......"

The instructors were not sure.

,"We cannot know what Yuria Grace wants. I have had several consultations, but she does not reveal her inner thoughts."

,"In my opinion, she doesn't seem to have any particular aspirations. She said herself that her goal is to graduate from the Maid Academy and obtain a Triple or higher certificate."

,"Working in the royal family is the best career in itself, and you can also get a chance to become a noble, so would you refuse?"

,"Not all maids want to work in the royal family."

,"That's because you've never worked in the royal family, so you don't know."

,"...I admit that the royal family is great, but large corporations are better in terms of practical work."

Maria exuded momentum. The instructors who were raising their voices shut their mouths.

,"There is still plenty of time. Let's watch for now."
