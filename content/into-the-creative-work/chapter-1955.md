I was sealed.

I can't move my arms and legs. I can't even breathe. Since my body won't move, I can't use mana either. It feels like time has stopped, except for my mind.

,That bastard is mistaken that he even sealed my consciousness.'

It's a sealing technique that makes it seem like my physical time has stopped. It was natural for that bastard to be confident. But that bastard's sealing technique couldn't seal my mind. Right now, my mind was very clear, unlike my body.

,This must be thanks to Absolute Spirit.'

My mind is fine, but I can't move and I can't see. I guess it's a relief that I can at least hear.

,It's not a completely isolated seal, huh.'

That bastard didn't seal me because he was afraid of me. He sealed me to use me as a biological battery or generator for the Machine Emperor. That must be why he didn't completely isolate me. Or maybe there's another reason.

,Is the reason I can't see because I'm in a dark place? Or is it because the crystal itself doesn't transmit light?'

I tried to create lightning, but I failed. It's not just because mana isn't moving. Because it consumes stamina even without mana.

,Mana and stamina. I can't use either, so I can't use lightning.'

[The Celestial Eye is opening.]

I successfully activated the Celestial Eye. The Celestial Eye consumes mana and mental power. In other words, the Celestial Eye was being activated with mental power right now. Thanks to the Celestial Eye's effect, I could see through the crystal and see the outside. There was nothing to see since it was a machine workshop.

,It's not clear and I can't see far. There's a limit to using the Celestial Eye with just mental power.'

To be honest, I could escape right now.

Heavenly Heart and Complete Recovery.

Both are skills that can be activated instantly without needing to consume mana, stamina, or mental power. Since the seal would be considered an abnormal status, I should be able to escape immediately.

,Can I use the Game Life app? I was able to end the game last time without a smartphone, so....'

The Game Life app wasn't my smartphone's ability. It allows me to enter and enjoy all sorts of creations, so how could its power be limited to just a smartphone?

I focused, thinking of the Game Life app.

I couldn't succeed right away. I even felt like giving up. But if I gave up, there was nothing else to do. In the end, I concentrated and concentrated again. How many hours had passed? My stats appeared before my eyes.

[Seong Yujin

Level: 91

Strength: 130 Stamina: 130 Agility: 130 Intelligence: 120 Vigor: 130 Mana: 130]

,It works.'

I wasn't too surprised since I expected it.

,But it's very inconvenient. I have to keep maintaining my concentration. A smartphone is thousands of times more convenient.'

I could enter the game world even in this state.

,I'm horny. I should go to the Baekhwan world for a bit.'

[Starting the game.]

[Ending the game.]

I was quite satisfied after having some steamy sex with maids.

I was still sealed, so I couldn't move.

,I'm bored.'

There was nothing to do. Should I sleep? Or should I enter the game world?

Unlike the game world, reality couldn't be automated. Should I just use Heavenly Heart now? I had the urge, but I held back.

,I need to give that bastard a taste of his own medicine.'

Simply killing that bastard wouldn't be enough. He calls himself immortal, so it would be hard to kill him. There was no way to kill him permanently either.

,And these kinds of bastards value their plans more than their own lives.'

Ruin that bastard's plan. And ruin it at the most crucial moment.

,He probably planned it for at least 10 years.'

Creating a giant robot over 10m tall with the latest technology? Even if he replaced that technology with Yin-Yang techniques, it must have taken a lot of time and money.

,If that plan falls apart right before his eyes... Hehe. That bastard's face will be a sight to see.'

He said he would finish it within three days, so I just need to wait a little longer.

I was really looking forward to the day I would ruin that bastard's plan.

,Should I make a plan on how to ruin that bastard's plan?'

Japan was bustling.

It was because of one person's visit to Japan.

Han Ah-young.

A Hunter who recently rose to S-rank.

Han Ah-young's popularity was outstanding worldwide, and Japan was no exception. There were several reasons, but the biggest reason was Han Ah-young's appearance. Han Ah-young was more beautiful and mysterious than a celebrity. Moreover, her figure was incredibly good. Her personality was also flawless. As an S-rank, she seemed to have a sense of duty, traveling around the world to deal with dangerous dungeons and monsters.

She was different from other S-rank Hunters who stayed home because they were annoyed or didn't have enough money. In reality, it was also because of a contract she had with the Hunter Association, but ordinary people weren't interested in such trivial truths.

What mattered was the fact that the Ice Queen had visited Japan.

Even public broadcasting news covered this news, and reporters poured out this news. There were many who covered Han Ah-young's past as well.

「Why did Korean S-rank Hunter Han Ah-young decide to visit Japan without a plan?」

「Han Ah-young's dinner at a 5-star hotel was sushi!」

「Han Ah-young has visited Japan in the past!」

「Is there a possibility that Han Ah-young will naturalize in Japan? According to experts, it's quite positive!」

「Han Ah-young chatting with Japanese A-rank Hunter Kawashi with a smile! Close relationship between famous Hunters?!」

The media was excited. They poured out articles related to Han Ah-young one after another. The heat was much hotter than when a Hollywood actor visited Japan. And most of them were articles filled with goodwill. They were trying to win Han Ah-young's favor even a little bit.

Japanese people flocked to the hotel where Han Ah-young was staying. They wanted to see her in person even once, get her autograph, and take pictures. Of course, there were many people with other intentions. Just being associated with Han Ah-young in any way would attract attention.

「Han Ah-young's press conference today!」

「She came to clean up Mount Fuji for the Japanese people!」

「The Korean S-rank Hunter's favorite food is sushi!」

「She learned Japanese because she likes Japan!」

"Hehe."

Kagaya Mizuchi, the heir to Japan's Kagaya Group and a C-rank Hunter, chuckled as he looked at the articles pouring out on the internet. From the perspective of an awakened citizen like him, these articles were all exaggerated or distorted. Not severely distorted. It's just that the reporters interpreted them as they pleased.

,She likes sushi? Isn't sushi the first food most foreigners eat when they come to Japan.'

She likes sushi. Anyone could make such a statement. Just as a simple lip service.

She learned Japanese because she likes Japan? She just said a few words of greeting in Japanese. Simple sentences that can be learned in 30 seconds.

,She chatted with Kawashi? Looking at the picture, Han Ah-young's smile is obviously fake. She must have forced a conversation and spouted nonsense. Did the Japanese Association push him? It would be good to have an issue.'

Among the exaggerated articles, there was a truth.

Cleaning up Mount Fuji.

Han Ah-young declared that she came to clean up the dangerous monsters of Mount Fuji for the safety of Japan.

,Why bother?'

There are many dangerous monsters in Mount Fuji right now, but it's not urgent. The monsters that have settled in Mount Fuji only move within their own territory.

,Of course, it would be nice to clean up Mount Fuji... It seems like she has another purpose.'

She's not going to attack the dungeon near Mount Fuji either, so I don't know what her purpose is.

,Anyway, Han Ah-young is the trend right now. She has a very good image not only in Japan but also in other countries. She's especially popular in the United States.'

I don't know about her face, but her figure is definitely beyond Asian standards.

I heard that the American Hunter Association sent Han Ah-young an enormous love call. And Han Ah-young rejected it outright.

,It would be nice if she could be a model for our group. Our sales would definitely increase.'

Kagaya Mizuchi stared blankly at the picture of Han Ah-young on the screen. She was beautiful even when he looked at her again. Her smiling face shakes a man's heart. If even I'm like this, other ordinary men must be even worse.

,...If it's just a model contract, she might accept it.'

The Kagaya Group was one of Japan's top conglomerates. They could give her the best treatment in the world.

,If I meet her in person and have a business conversation... we might develop feelings for each other.'

A conglomerate and an S-rank Hunter. Isn't that a good match? Even my father, who is full of business mindset and conservative, would have no choice but to acknowledge it if the other party was Han Ah-young.

,I should ask someone to ask about her intentions... No. It would be better for me to find out her contact information and contact her directly. If I invite Japan's best sushi chef and have a conversation....'

He was imagining naturally dating Han Ah-young when he suddenly felt a terrible headache.

"Kkeueueueueueuk?!"

An excruciating pain he had never experienced before! It felt like some crazy bastard was stabbing each and every wrinkle of his brain with a knife, hammering it down, and hardening it.

He gasped for breath in the unknown pain for 10 minutes. He was trembling with fear that he might have an incurable disease.

「Make a model offer to Han Ah-young. Do not make an offer.」

A binary choice.

Kagaya Mizuchi's ability suddenly manifested.

,...Could it be. Was this headache also an effect of the ability?'

Kagaya Mizuchi swallowed hard. Why did a choice suddenly appear at this point with such excruciating pain?

,...Do not make an offer.'

「Do not make an offer.」

As if it was the correct answer, the binary choice told him the answer and disappeared.

"But if it's Han Ah-young, she could be my future wife... Kkeuaaaaaak!"

「Do not make an offer.」

"Kkeuaaaaaaaaak!"

「Do not make an offer.」

"It's not even a binary choice anymore! Kkeuaaaaaaaaak!"

After suffering for 30 minutes, he cleanly gave up on Han Ah-young. His ability, the binary choice, only tells him the correct answer for his future. There was only one reason why the binary choice was coming out like this.

,Is it that being involved with Han Ah-young will only lead to ruin....'

He had a gloomy expression. A new choice appeared. For some reason, his ability was strange today. It was appearing on its own without him activating it.

「Help Han Ah-young. Do not help.」

,...Do not help. Something big will happen if I get involved for no reason. Ignoring her is the best thing to do.'

「Help.」

Excruciating pain rushed in as if punishing a child for giving the wrong answer.

"Kkeuaaaaaak! God damn it! Act like an ability if you're an ability! Kkeuaaaaaak!"

It was the first time he had ever wanted to abandon his ability like today.
