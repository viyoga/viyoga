Beroprin, the city of the tattoo world where Park Suho reigned as its lord, was burning beneath a star-scattered night sky.

Citizens screamed and fled, or gathered at wells to fill buckets with water to fight the flames. But the fire that used the city as kindling was far too fierce to be subdued by mere buckets.

The city was collapsing. The mages were doing what they could to suppress the inferno with magic, but compared to the scale of the blaze, even their power seemed woefully insufficient.

*There's no way they just sat on their hands until the city reached this state. The fire must have spread in an instant, before anyone could even react.*

Drake, who had teleported alongside me, was gasping for breath beside me.

"Hahk, huk...! There. Bruce is over there...!"

Where his trembling fingertip pointed was the heart of the city. A mansion larger and more ornate than any other building was burning larger and more spectacularly than any other building.

KWAAAAAAAH!

As if someone had cranked up a gas burner, flames suddenly surged above the mansion. The heat radiated in all directions, intense enough that even I, standing a fair distance away, could feel it.

In front of the burning mansion, Park Suho and a tall man faced off. The man had shoulder-length brown hair, wore dress pants and a shirt. His exposed face and wrists were covered in tattoos. This was likely Bruce.

Park Suho, looking absolutely furious, was pressing Bruce with magic. Killing intent overflowed from his thoroughly contorted face.

*Putting that aside, his magical power is weaker than usual. Is it because the city is burning?*

Park Suho's power grows stronger as the city develops and becomes more formidable. Conversely, if the city weakens, his power inevitably weakens as well.

If Bruce had deliberately set the city ablaze, then he must have had an accurate understanding of Park Suho's abilities.

*The one using magic to control the fire around the mansion to some degree... that's Elisha.*

A beautiful blonde mage in a white robe was struggling with her magic. I held back the urge to run to Elisha, my woman, right then and there.

"Drake. How many tattoo abilities can Bruce use simultaneously?"

"Three. I confirmed with my own eyes that he can use three abilities at once. He might even be capable of more. The abilities he has..."

"I already know about the thirteen tattoo abilities from last time. Right now he's using fire manipulation, flight, and telepathy to read his opponent's mind."

"...You can see that from here?"

"You can roughly guess just from watching the flow of combat. What are the exact conditions for stealing a tattoo?"

"From what I know, it requires taking the target's blood and maintaining contact for thirty seconds, but... that's information he revealed himself before he betrayed us. It can't be trusted."

"Is that so."

Truthfully, that information was useless to me. I wasn't a tattoo ability user. I had no tattoo abilities for him to steal in the first place.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

Wrapping my entire body in lightning, I flew toward the mansion like a bolt from the heavens.

I appeared before the staggering Park Suho.

"Hyung!!"

Park Suho cheered as if he'd met his savior. Bruce, on the other hand, furrowed his brow as deeply as it would go.

"Thunder Heaven's Sung Yujin... I didn't expect you to show up so soon. My mistake. I should have made sure Drake was dead..."

"Not a mistake — greed. You didn't kill Drake because you coveted his ability. Am I wrong?"

“…….”

A red gleam flickered through Bruce's brown eyes. I sensed instinctively that he'd used telepathy. And he would have gleaned nothing. There was no way my mind could be pierced by such a pathetic mind-reading trick.

"Hyung! That bastard can use telepathy, so most attacks won't work on him. But he can only read surface consciousness — if you move on instinct, he can't read it. You know, that thing where the body moves before the thought. So if the two of us attack simultaneously using instinct, even he won't be able to do anything."

"Enough. I'll handle him alone. You go deal with the city. I can feel your anxiety about it all the way from here."

"...Is it that obvious?"

The city Park Suho had poured money and time into was burning. Of course he'd be anxious.

"Yeah."

"...I'll trust only you, hyung. If it gets dangerous, shout. I'll come help immediately."

"That won't be necessary."

Park Suho flew out of the mansion. He intended to secure facilities more important than the burning mansion first. Bruce didn't even spare Park Suho a glance. His gaze was fixed solely on me.

I quietly moved my mana. I didn't so much as twitch a finger, determined not to give him any information.

*Lightning.*

A blue-white bolt split the night sky and crashed down above Bruce's head.

Bruce's reaction was slow to the point of being nonexistent.

Struck by lightning, Bruce didn't let out so much as a groan. Smoke rose from his body, yet he didn't even stagger. It was as if he felt no pain at all.

"You. You couldn't sense my mana, could you?"

If he'd detected my mana moving before the lightning fell, he could have easily dodged it. Something even an A-rank hunter could do, and he couldn't.

"There was no reason to dodge. A lightning bolt of that level can't kill me."

"Is that so? Then don't dodge this either."

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 11]

Thunder Heaven Style — Myriad Thunder.

Compressed lightning shot from my palm like a bolt. This time Bruce dodged to the side. Not by sensing mana. He'd dodged by watching my attack with his own two eyes.

*His mana detection doesn't even reach half of an A-rank hunter's, yet his physical reaction speed is on par with an S-rank hunter.*

It was clearly strange.

Normally, strong individuals of A-rank or above are skilled at using mana. It's essential. If you're not proficient with mana from the start, your combat power plummets.

"Your use of mana is clumsy. Do you have little actual combat experience?"

"Mana isn't everything in combat. What matters is ability."

Bruce flicked his fingers. Sand coalesced in an instant, forming blades that flew at me.

I pulled Hwaryeon Bido from my inventory and struck down the sand blades. They lost their form and crumbled.

"The foundation of that ability is mana. Even with the same ability, using mana increases its power by at least thirty percent. You don't even know that simple fact? You really have almost no real combat experience, do you? If I hadn't intervened, Park Suho might have won in the end."

"...You're right that I don't have much combat experience. However, before absolute output of ability, all the small details become meaningless."

The flames that had been devouring and burning the mansion began gathering and coalescing in one place. A massive fire, transformed into the shape of a serpent, came surging toward me.

I leaped upward to avoid the serpent of fire. The ground where the fire serpent passed was gouged and melted into a viscous slurry. The fire serpent raised its head and lunged toward the empty air where I stood.

Bzzt, crackle.

Red lightning writhed along the blade, wrapping around it alongside blue mana.

Thunder Heaven Style — Thunder Blade.

I swung the sword from top to bottom. The blade moved silently, but the result was anything but silent. The slash bisected the fire serpent and extinguished even the remaining flames in one breath with the wind pressure. The slash didn't stop there — it flew all the way to Bruce.

Bruce protected himself with a barrier. It was useless. The slash cut through the barrier and sliced his left shoulder.

"...Kuh."

"At S-rank level, mana is essentially will itself. Techniques infused with such will rise in caliber. With similar output, they can never be blocked."

"...I'll acknowledge that your attack just now was powerful. But in the end, you couldn't kill me. That so-called will is meaningless before overwhelming power and diverse abilities...!"

"That attack wasn't meant to kill you. I just happened to damage you while blowing away the flames."

"Damage? I've taken no damage whatsoever."

His left arm, which had fallen to the ground, floated into the air and reattached itself to his shoulder. The wound, and even the sliced clothing, were restored without a trace of blood.

It wasn't healing. It was time reversal, limited to himself. One of the tattoo abilities he'd stolen after killing Selas's organization members.

I wasn't surprised, since I already knew about his ability.

"How can you use tattoo abilities so freely? From what I know, the source of tattoo abilities is the goddess Selbretina. No matter how I think about it, it's strange that the goddess would lend power to someone who kills her chosen warriors and destroys her world, let alone fail to sanction you."

"...You're neither a warrior nor a descendant of one, yet you know a great deal."

Bruce lifted his foot from the ground. Before I could even blink, he'd reached me.

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 10]

I reflexively used Flash. Even so, his movement as he raised his fist didn't slow much at all.

Mana gathered around Bruce's fist. The gathered mana doubled in an instant. This too was one of the tattoo abilities he'd stolen.

Bruce swung his fist. I deflected it with my left hand. His fist's trajectory twisted slightly, and the doubled mana discharged into empty air.

Bruce's eyes widened momentarily. His body began sliding backward. He was trying to use time reversal to return to his position one second ago and create distance.

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 9]

Stab!

Hwaryeon Bido's blade pierced through Bruce's chest. External interference broke the time reversal. His expression contorted. I spun and kicked his torso with my right foot.

BWOOM!

With a sonic boom, his body flew far into the distance.

It was a brief exchange of less than 0.1 seconds that began when he lunged at me.

Thunder Heaven Style — Flying Thunder God.

I flew through the air, chasing Bruce who'd been sent outside the city. I landed in front of him on the flat ground outside the city limits.

"I know you didn't die. A tattoo ability that grants two extra lives, wasn't it?"

Which meant I had to kill him two more times.

"The fact that my opponent knows my information... is quite troublesome."

"Your abilities? Stolen abilities, you mean."

"They're my abilities now. Those bastards couldn't use their own powers properly, but... I'm different."

Fwoooosh!

Flames erupted from his hand.

Bzzt, crackle!

Blue lightning burst from my left hand. I intended to overpower Bruce's flames with sheer output and electrocute him. Considering the mana he'd used, it should have gone exactly as planned.

Fwooooooosh!

The flames pushed back my lightning. My expression hardened as I explosively used more mana to raise the lightning's output. Just as the lightning was about to overwhelm the fire, the flames intensified and pushed the lightning back.

*...This isn't simply a matter of being outmatched in output. Those flames are...!*

[Using Leap.]

Before the flames that pushed back my lightning could reach my body, I teleported to create distance.

"You mentioned will, didn't you? Just as you said, when will is infused into an attack, its caliber changes. Ah, but this isn't my will. It's merely a very small fragment of That One."

"...Those flames just now were similar to a Domain. I thought something was off.... So you're someone who received Bramasen's power."

"You know That One's name. There was a reason That One told me to be wary of you. So the true enemy wasn't Park Suho, but you."

I ran my hand along the surface of the Stormbreaker dagger at my waist.

『Using Thunder Eye Golden Pupil.』

I can see it.

Bramasen's dark crimson power was burning, using Bruce's body as its medium. And within Bruce's eyes, I could sense a fragment of an overwhelming presence.

*Is That One looking at me through Bruce's eyes?*

Now I understood why he could use the power of tattoos despite opposing the goddess Celevretina. A god more powerful than the goddess was bestowing power upon him.

The ability to steal the power of tattoos.

That itself was proof that he'd been Bramasen's pawn from the very beginning.

[Domain Expansion]

Starlight scattered in all directions from the red blade, then stopped.

The grass blades bent by the blowing wind stopped. The dust falling downward stopped. I couldn't move my body. Not my fingers, not even my eyes.

*My thoughts haven't sped up. This is...!*

Time had stopped.
