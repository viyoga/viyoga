The shattered body of Amo was darkness itself.

Naturally. Because he was a dark spirit. His essence was darkness itself.

,Kkuuuk!

Moka roared. Azure lightning was emitted from his body, sweeping through the darkness. I shook off the darkness clinging to my body and stood up. As Amo died, the darkness was gradually disappearing.

Kales approached me. I deliberately staggered, and she hurriedly grabbed my shoulder to support me. My arm subtly brushed against her chest.

,Something... feels different than usual. Why is Moka acting like that?

,It seems he realized something from this battle.

Pajijijik. Pajik. Pajijijik.

Lightning was emitted from Moka's body without a break. Moka burned all the surrounding darkness with lightning and then flew into the sky. Moka's body flickered so brightly that it could be seen from afar.

,Do spirits also gain enlightenment?

,Spirits also have egos, you know.

,Is Moka becoming a high-level spirit now?

,I can't say for sure. But... I think Moka has accumulated enough power to become a high-level spirit. The reason he flew into the sky now is probably because he needs his own time. Believe in Moka and wait for him.

,Yeah, well. It's not like our contract is broken, so he'll be back soon.

I shared Moka's vision through the Spirit Eyes. Right now, Moka was flying through the clouds. The presence of lightning was felt around Moka. Moka was constantly getting stronger. Even if I didn't pay attention, he would reach the realm of a high-level spirit.

Kales looked at the place where Amo had been with a complicated expression. She must have felt a sense of emptiness since the high-level dark spirit she had been with for so long had disappeared like this.

,Kales-sensei....

That's that. Now was a good opportunity to comfort her and try to win her over. I slowly approached her.

,Don't be too heartbroken. How vast is this world? If you look for it, there will be other high-level dark spirits.

,Heartbroken? It's not that. I felt something strange. The Amo I know isn't usually like this. Maybe the new contractor influenced Amo. And Amo's new contractor is dangerous. I want to find out who they are if possible, but....

,Is there a way?

,No. That's the biggest problem.

Kales sighed. She stood in place and seemed to ponder for a moment before saying to me.

,There's nothing we can do here. Let's go back. I'm going to get an earful from the Dean-nim....

,Are we going back right away? We came all the way here, let's play around for a bit, shall we? It's not like we're particularly hurt.

Going back right away? No way. Now that I'm out of the Academy alone with Kales, it's the perfect opportunity to win her over.

,Originally, we were planning to do some sightseeing and then return, but... an accident happened, didn't it? I think it's better to return to the Academy right away.

Kales was firm. I knew it instinctively. No matter how much I tried to persuade her, she wouldn't easily change her mind.

,K, Kales-sensei. Can I go take care of something for a moment?

,Take care of something? Ah.... I'll wait here, so take your time.

I bought time. I moved away from Kales. I stopped at a distance where I couldn't hear her voice or feel her presence.

,Machina.

Machina was summoned with a dissatisfied face. Her swollen cheeks were covered in vegetable pieces, and her lips were stained with red sauce.

,Why does your face look like that?

,Are you asking because you don't know? It's lunchtime right now. I was eating chicken salad and rose tteokbokki, but... I couldn't even finish it because of you!

,Why are you so greedy for a spirit?

,Don't look down on spirits!

,Where's Mom?

,I was with her until just now. Send me back quickly! If I go back too late, Ajumma will eat all the tteokbokki!

,There's something you need to do. If you do just that, I'll send you back right away.

,Does it have to be now? Can't I do it after eating?

,Don't you want to eat?

,Ah, okay. I'll do it.

I raised my finger and pointed in one direction.

,If you fly this way, you'll find a car. Break the car so it can't run. Don't break it artificially, but make it look like it broke down very naturally. Can you do it?

Machina narrowed her eyes and looked at me.

,I can do it, but.... What kind of scheme are you plotting this time?

,You don't need to know. Oh, and don't tell Mom either. If she asks, just make something up.

,Okay. You have to send me back right away if I do just that.

Machina quickly flew through the trees. I shared my vision with Machina through the Spirit Eyes. In less than a minute, I found a car parked on the shoulder of the road. It was the rental car that Kales and I had come in.

-Is this the car?

'That's right. Break it.'

-Just make it so it doesn't roll, right?

'Yeah. Naturally.'

Machina picked up a nearby leaf and put it in the engine.

-It's done. Send me back. I have to eat rose tteokbokki!

'You just put in a leaf. Are you kidding me?'

-You wanted it to break down as naturally as possible. This is enough.

Machina may look like that, but she's a machine spirit. She knows more about machines than I do, so I decided to trust her for now. And if the car doesn't break down and keeps moving, I can just summon Machina again and scold her.

I reverse-summoned Machina and approached Kales.

,I'm sorry, Sensei. Did you wait long?

,Waiting long? Let's go down below.

Kales took the lead and moved forward. Perhaps because she was an elf, she easily found her way through the mountains. I found the car. It looked perfectly fine on the outside.

We got in the car, and she started the engine. The engine started and then suddenly died.

,Eung?

Kales, flustered, started the engine again. It was the same this time. The engine started and then died. She tried that action about three more times, and soon the engine completely went bad.

Kales was flustered.

,W, why is it like this?

,I, I don't know much about cars either.... Are we going to be stranded here, by any chance?

,Stranded in this day and age? What are you talking about.

Kales, who had been flustered, quickly regained her composure. It wasn't like anyone was hurt, it was just that the car had broken down. She contacted the rental car company with her smartphone.

'That's right. This is modern times.'

I clicked my tongue inwardly. South Korea's infrastructure was too good for anything lewd to happen in the stranded mountains.

,The rental car company said they'll send someone. They said it'll take about three hours... Hmm. It might be faster to walk to the nearby village instead.

,Walk? There's a village nearby?

,I saw it when we were coming earlier. It's about an hour away. It was a small village, but... I wonder if there are any taxis? If not, another way would be to ask someone else and borrow a car.

I racked my brain. Either way, the result was the same. Then wouldn't it be better to just wait in the car? Because that would give us three hours of free time.

,......Kales-sensei, what do you think would be better?

,I think it's better to move than to stay here. Can't you walk, by any chance? Should I call someone separately? If I ask the Dean-nim, we might be able to get helicopter support.

,No. Is there really a need to do that? It's not that urgent, and it's not like I'm hurt. Let's walk.

We got out of the car as is. We abandoned the car and walked down the road. Perhaps because it was such a remote area even in South Korea, there were no cars passing by on the road.

I racked my brain. There must be a way.... There must be a way I haven't thought of.

,Walking like this isn't so bad either. Right?

,Yes. It's good for exercise. But Sensei. There's something I'm curious about.

,Something you're curious about? Don't hesitate to ask.

,Earlier, Amo said that if he ate me, he would get stronger. Do spirits originally get stronger by eating people?

,You know very well that that's not the case, don't you.

,I do, but just in case.

As far as I knew, there was nothing absolute in this world.

,...Normal spirits don't get stronger by eating living things. They get stronger by absorbing the energy of nature. And the energy of nature tends to accumulate naturally.... The reason Amo made this mountain his territory was to slowly absorb the mountain's energy and get stronger. If he could get stronger by eating people, he would have already tried to eat hundreds, thousands of people.

,But Amo was convinced that he would get stronger if he ate me.

,I'm also concerned about that. Maybe Amo's other contractor did something....

Kales trailed off. Her face was really complicated.

The atmosphere is getting stiff. I changed the subject to ease the atmosphere. As the atmosphere eased, the conversation became smoother.

We arrived at the village. It was a small village. The population wouldn't even be 500 people. But it had most of the necessities. I could see elderly people working in the fields. I couldn't see any young people.

,First, let's talk to the village chief and borrow a car.

In these kinds of small villages, it was usually faster to talk directly to the village chief.

'......Is there no way? To think that I have to take a car like this to the train station and return to the Academy.... We came out of the Academy together, just the two of us... This good opportunity....'

As I walked down the village road, I noticed a guesthouse. It was run-down and small compared to the motels in the city. But the guesthouse, with its wide yard and quiet rural scenery in the background, had its own charm.

I swallowed hard. After some lewd imagination, a good idea came to mind.

,Kales-sensei.

,Hm? What is it?

,Shouldn't we stay here today and return tomorrow?

Kales tilted her head. It seemed she didn't understand what I was saying. If it were another woman, she might have gotten angry right away, but she was calm.

,What are you suddenly talking about?

I shouldn't say that my body got worse due to the aftermath of the battle. Kales, who has a sense of responsibility as a teacher, will immediately request helicopter support from the Academy Dean-nim if she knows that my condition has worsened. And Sung Hari will be contacted. Given Sung Hari's personality, she might pressure the Hero Association, so the time I can spend with Kales will be even shorter.

,It's because of Moka. Moka is near here. As Moka's contractor, I want to check Moka's growth here. And maybe if I'm not here, Moka will follow me....

,The Thunder Owl might not reach the realm of a high-level spirit...?

,Yes.

,I've never heard of that, but... it's not entirely impossible. Spirits are sensitive, after all. It's not uncommon for mid-level spirits to rise to high-level spirits....

Kales crossed her arms and stroked her own chin. She was deep in thought before opening her mouth again.

,First... let's report to the Dean-nim. We need the Dean's permission to stay overnight.

,Yes. Sensei.

Kang Ji-young would allow it. She wouldn't refuse an opportunity for an Academy student to get stronger just because the return would be delayed.

And as I predicted, Kang Ji-young allowed it. Kales and I ended up staying at the guesthouse. Of course, in separate rooms. There was no bed and no computer. But there was a wide yard and a wooden floor. Kales was satisfied.

I went to the village supermarket and bought a lot of food and came back. We ate dinner at sunset. Samgyeopsal was delicious no matter where you ate it.

Kales and I sat side by side on the wooden floor. I held up a small liquor cup. The clear soju shimmered.

,You know that Academy students are prohibited from drinking, right?

,Hey. This isn't the Academy, is it? The Academy's rules don't apply here.

,It's sophistry, but I'll acknowledge it. It's a secret to other people that we drank together.

Kales raised her glass. Cheers! The glasses clinked, making a clear sound.

The alcohol went down. The heat that passed through my throat warmed my body. She and I had a conversation with the alcohol. As the alcohol disappeared, the distance between her and me gradually narrowed.

The stars in the night sky twinkled, and the sound of insects chirping could be heard. A cool breeze blew, tickling pleasantly.

The atmosphere was created, and the time had come. I put my hand on Kales's shoulder. Her slightly loosened eyes met mine.

,Kales.

,......Yujin-ah.
