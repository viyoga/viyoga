Gracia, having been reverse-summoned, was in her bedroom.

Her castle on the 15th floor of the Tower.

The place where she had been before being summoned.

"Th-the next episode…."

She raised a trembling hand. The smartphone that should have been there was gone. The sensation of that small, rectangular metal plate was still vivid in her memory.

'I was definitely holding it in my hand.'

But it wasn't there. The reason was obvious. It wasn't hers. She hadn't been able to bring it when summoned, so she couldn't take it back when reverse-summoned either.

Gracia let out a small sigh and looked around.

Her bedroom was spacious. But there wasn't much in it. A bed, a desk, a chair, a bookshelf, a vanity, and a wardrobe. Nothing else.

'The summoning was a deviation. I've simply returned to my original daily life.'

What had she been doing at this hour?

Ah, right.

She'd been reading.

She scanned the books lined up on the bookshelf. Old books. Most were novels. She pulled one out and opened it.

She read the first chapter. Strangely, the words wouldn't register. She forced herself through it, but there was no enjoyment to be found. It was stale. More than anything, she'd already read this book over five times, so she knew the entire plot.

In the end, Gracia put the book back and perched on the edge of her bed as if collapsing onto it.

The bed against her rear felt hard.

'…The bed in that room wasn't like this.'

Everything in the bedroom felt drab. If she had her way, she'd replace it all. But from where? Inside the Tower, these things were considered top quality.

Knock knock.

The sound of a careful knock on the door.

"My lady. It's Fred."

"C-come in."

The door opened and

a dark crimson suit of full-body armor clanked its way into the room.

Fred. A servant Gracia had created with magic and her ability. He wasn't alive. He could be called a golem made from an object called a 'Madoshinkaku' obtained from the Tower. In Earth terminology, perhaps an android. At first he couldn't even speak properly, but now he was capable of thinking like a human.

"How were matters on Earth?"

"N-not bad."

"That's a relief. I shall prepare a meal. Is there any dish you desire?"

Anything's fine.

Gracia was about to say that as usual, but fell silent for a moment before opening her mouth.

"D-do you know what galbi is?"

"My apologies. It's a name I've never heard before."

"…C-can you make chicken? It's a dish where you fry a bird…."

"My lady. To obtain a chicken, I would need to procure chicken meat from the market on another common floor. As you know, I cannot move between floors on my own."

Isn't it supposed to come out with a single tap on a smartphone?

Gracia shook her head with a faint sigh.

"…I d-don't want to go outside. J-just the usual, please."

"Understood."

Fred departed.

Gracia lay down on the bed. She fidgeted with both hands, but there was nothing to do. She stared blankly at the ceiling. There were no fluorescent lights on the ceiling. The windows were covered by curtains, so the interior was dark. For her, a vampire, darkness posed no problem.

'I h-have nothing to do.'

That was the reason she had slept for five years. She had no research purpose like her friend Kersha, and since Fred handled all the household chores, she truly had nothing to do.

'I w-want to watch shorts videos. I need to watch episode 35 of The Count's Daughter of the Black Family too…. I w-want to play games too.'

She kept thinking of Earth.

'Sh-should I try imagining it…?'

Gracia focused her mind and played games in her imagination. As a Tower-dweller, she overwhelmed the enemy Tower with her superior physical and mental stats. Occasionally the game didn't go well, but that was because of incompetent junglers, not her fault.

She'd played about five imaginary games when it hit her—this was meaningless.

"My lady. I've brought your meal."

The castle had a dining hall. However, except when she had guests, Gracia took her meals in her bedroom rather than the dining hall.

Bread and soup.

“…….”

She quietly ate the bread and soup one bite at a time.

It was tasteless.

'H-how did I used to eat this…?'

She knew this was the average meal inside the Tower. Not just the Tower's people, but even the Tower's Transcendents usually ate like this. The wealthy might eat meat, but compared to Earth's meat, it was flavorless.

"I w-want to drink cola…."

"Pardon? What is cola?"

"Th-there is such a thing. It exists."

Gracia couldn't even finish half her meal.

"My lady. You seem to have no appetite. Shall I bring blood?"

The castle kept human blood stored fresh. It wasn't blood drawn from killing people. It was purchased with coins from other common floors.

"N-not today."

Afterward she headed to the bathroom. Even in the bathroom, she found herself unconsciously comparing things to Earth.

After washing, she returned to the bedroom.

With nothing to do, she lay down on the bed.

'…What should I do tomorrow?'

Something productive? She didn't want to do anything lacking interest or fun. Fred would handle all of that anyway. Go to another common floor? Now that she'd overcome the sun, should she meet people?

'…Meet people? How dreadful.'

Just imagining it made her tired. Fighting monsters would be far better.

She closed her eyes.

She wasn't particularly sleepy. As a vampire, she could stay alert for three days and nights without rest. Still, she slept because there was nothing else to do. There was also a condition that to remain in this zone, one had to sleep within a 24-hour period.

Wake up, eat the meal Fred prepared, read a book whose contents she already knew, then fall asleep.

For Gracia, life was tedious.

She had thought it was always like this.

If she hadn't met that man, if she hadn't been summoned to Earth, she would never have known another kind of life.

• • •

Several days later.

Gracia headed to the 82nd floor common zone, the plaza. It was where the Tower's Transcendents gathered.

Wearing a black dress that covered her entire body except her face and hands, she strolled through the plaza holding a black parasol.

"…Gold Blood? It really is Gold Blood. There were even rumors she'd died… She was alive. How many years has it been?"

"Wait. It's broad daylight right now. Even with a parasol, she should be weakened by the sunlight… This isn't a weakened aura."

"They say she was searching for a panacea. Looks like she found it already. She's overcome her weakness. She must be even stronger now."

"Gold Blood? Ah, that vampire? Is she strong?"

"You're the one recently acknowledged as a Transcendent, aren't you? Don't judge by appearances alone. That form is all a disguise. The Transcendents who've died at that woman's hands number over five."

"…Five of them?"

The Tower's Transcendents murmured among themselves.

Beneath the parasol, Gracia's body trembled faintly. People's gazes converged on her. It felt unpleasant. She wanted to escape from here immediately.

'Even if this is where humans with nothing better to do gather, I didn't expect this many people….'

Should she go back?

As she deliberated, Gracia firmly shook her head. She recalled the purpose for which she'd come here.

"Oh. If it isn't Gold Blood? What brings someone who's always holed up at home to a place like this?"

An old man in his seventies wearing a gray robe and sporting a pure white beard addressed her. It was Hent, the Sage of Iron.

"W-whether I come here or not is none of your business."

"Still haven't installed any social skills, I see."

"Y-you're younger than me."

"Yes, yes. Did you come looking after hearing the rumors too? Did you bring plenty of coins?"

"R-rumors…?"

"What? Didn't Kersha tell you?"

"K-Kersha? I-I came to see Kersha."

"If you want to see Kersha, you go to Kersha's residence. Why did you come here?"

"A-are you stupid? I came here because she wasn't there."

"Still trembling and watching everyone's reactions while saying exactly what you want to say. If you're going to do that, why bother caring what others think?"

Gracia raised the middle finger of her free left hand. Hent tilted his head.

"I don't know what that means, but it sure does sour the mood…."

That was when it happened. The plaza suddenly grew noisy, and the Transcendents began forming a line.

"He's here. He's early today. You line up too. No, wait."

Hent turned his head and shouted to the Transcendents gathered in the plaza.

"All of you! Yield to Gold Blood today! This one is here for the first time today!"

"First time? Hmm. I suddenly feel sorry for her. To think she didn't know about something this good until now."

"There's a per-person limit anyway…. I'll yield just for today, specially. It's absolutely not because I'm scared, okay?"

The Transcendents yielded readily. Gracia furrowed her brow, but curiosity about their attitude made her follow behind Hent.

He was a rat beastman, not a Transcendent. The moment she saw the bundle on his back, she knew he was a Tower merchant.

"Squeak-squeak?! Today I see a new face!"

"Yes. She may look like this, but she's a frightening woman. Be careful."

"Squeak! I always hold the Transcendents in reverence!"

"Of course, we know your heart. By the way, did you bring chicken?"

Chicken?

Gracia's eyes widened at the unexpected word as she looked between Hent and Rupil.

"Squeak-squeak…. My apologies. There's no chicken today either. Instead, I've brought something called a game console!"

Rupil spread open his bundle. Earth-made items appeared. Some were things Gracia recognized, and some were things she'd never seen before.

"Gold Blood. What I recommend is this. A beverage called cola. Try drinking it. You'll see a new world. I'll specially buy you one."

Hent and the other Transcendents stared at her with expectant eyes.

Gracia hesitated briefly under their gazes.

'Cola…. It's the same can I saw on Earth. Are they selling Earth's products?'

She accepted the cola Hent handed her. She deftly popped the tab of the can. Chiiiiiik! The carbonation escaped. The cola was cold.

"She opened it in one go?! This is your first time here, isn't it?! Ah, did Kersha tell you?!"

Gracia ignored Hent and the Transcendents' fuss and drank the cola. The cola she hadn't tasted in days was electrifying.

"I-it's good."

"…That's her entire reaction?"

"When I first drank cola, I felt a shock on the level of the world being created anew. I suppose a True Ancestor really is different."

"I-is there cider? That's more my taste…."

"Squeak-squeak? C-cider? What is that?"

"Ahh…. You don't know it…. Cider is…."

Gracia trailed off and closed her mouth. She hadn't drunk enough of it herself to explain what it was.

Gracia tried the things Hent recommended, eating and using them. Her reactions were lukewarm. Most of them were nothing compared to what she'd seen on Earth.

"Squeak! This is a game console! You can play a game called Tetris! It requires something called batteries…. I'll sell those separately later! Squeak squeak!"

"A game, you say. Let me try it. Ooooh! It's such a simple game, yet this fun! I could play it all day! I'm buying this!!"

Transcendents gathered around Hent. Watching him play, they kept bursting out in exclamations of amazement.

"It looks fun just watching!"

"The music is so novel too!"

"This would be good for killing time. Hey, Hent. You need to rotate that block before dropping it!"

"Kyah! The satisfaction of slotting in the long bar is no joke!"

Step by step.

Gracia backed away.

'They react this much to such a simple game? Ugh…. They're like cavemen.'

Of course, she bought a game console too. Having tried it herself, it was fun—not as fun as the Rift, but fun. It would kill time.

"Gracia?"

Gracia, who had been focused on the game console, raised her head at the familiar voice. There stood Kersha, wearing a white witch's hat and a white dress. Her purple hair swayed slightly in the blowing wind.

"How surprising. I didn't expect you to come to the plaza on your own. What's the occasion?"

"K-Kersha. I-I came to see you. Let me meet him."
