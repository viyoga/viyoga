I checked my assets. The only valuable things in sight were the laptop right in front of me and the smartphone next to the bed. What about cash? Checking the bank app, I had about 5 million won in cash. Considering I was a freshman in college, having 5 million won in pure cash was quite a lot.

,Did I get support from my family? Or was it from a part-time job?

The answer was simple. I had deposit and withdrawal records. I received 500,000 won a month from my family and earned about 1 million won from a meat restaurant part-time job.

,500,000 won a month means my family is at least upper-middle class.

I also found out about my university.

I was a freshman in the Department of Sports Science at Seoul Buyoung University. There was also a group chat, so I checked it out. I scrolled up through the chat history.

Ki Seungpal: These days, the freshmen are so lax. If you see a senior at school, bow and say hello.

Ki Seungpal: Especially Seong Yujin-ssi.

Ki Seungpal: I heard you were hitting on him?

Ki Seungpal: Are you fucking kidding me? Get your act together!

Ki Seungpal: If I hear that shit again, I won't let it slide. I'll bury your college life in the gutter, you fuck.

Ki Seungpal: Aren't you going to answer?

Seong Yujin: Yes. I'm sorry.

,Ki Seungpal? Who the hell is this bastard acting up?

It was a chat from a week ago. I knew it was said before I entered this world. But looking at the chat history made me angry. After checking a few more times, I found that Ki Seungpal had a particularly bad personality, and he had been especially on my ass for the past three months.

,Since it's not reality and just a game world... should I go find him and kill him now?

But actually doing it was a hassle.

,I'll just kill him if I run into him later. Though that's unlikely.

There were 30 days left until the zombie outbreak. There was no reason to go to university.

,I need money.

I knew the future, but stocks or the lottery were impossible. The names of the companies in this world were unfamiliar, and the original game didn't give out lottery numbers. Something might happen within 30 days.

,I think I could manipulate the stock prices? If I buy stocks and then kill the CEO of a competing company, wouldn't the stocks I bought skyrocket?

Could I make a lot of money that way?

,No matter how I think about it, robbery seems like the smartest thing to do.

I crossed my arms and pondered. I thought about what I needed for the zombie apocalypse. A woman, a place to live, food, weapons. Henchmen. These were the five things that immediately came to mind. Of course, a woman was important.

,A pretty woman is important... should I kidnap a celebrity?

Where would be a good place to live? Preferably Seoul, but... Seoul would be the most chaotic place when the zombie outbreak happens.

,Shit. Food is also a problem. Especially since I'm picky. I don't want to live eating bad food.

It would be good to have a chef. But not just any chef could satisfy my tongue.

,Henchmen are also important. It would be nice if they were talented henchmen who had awakened their abilities.

I scratched my head. I was losing motivation because there was so much to prepare. Still, I had to do it because the zombie outbreak would happen with 100% certainty in 30 days.

I got up from my seat and took out a bag from a corner of the one-room apartment. It was a sturdy hiking bag. Judging from the hiking equipment inside, I seemed to have a hobby of enjoying hiking.

,I probably won't come back to this rotten one-room apartment... let's just take what I need. The expensive laptop is a given... it would be good to have some clothes too.

The most important thing was a weapon.

I saw a baseball bat and a tennis racket in the corner. I looked around and went to the kitchen, grabbing a kitchen knife. Pajijik. A blue current flickered on the short blade of the kitchen knife.

,Ah, this cool and heavy feeling.... When it comes to weapons, it's gotta be a knife.

The traits and skills I chose when entering this world were Absolute Mind, Lightning, and Lightning Sky Style. There was no mana in this world, but there was something similar to mental energy. There was no reason not to use Lightning Sky Style if I could use Lightning.

Click.

I packed everything I needed and went out into the hallway. The doors of the one-room apartments were lined up close together on the hallway walls. It felt like a chicken coop, probably because it was an old and cheap one-room apartment. I headed to the door next door.

,Were they planning to promote it as the latest style? They all have electronic door locks installed. That'll make things easier.

I grabbed the electronic door lock and used Lightning. Pajijijik!

Ttiriririri!

The door opens.

,Electronic door locks are a piece of cake. The structure itself isn't that complicated.

I opened the door and stole anything that could be turned into money. Since it was a woman's room, there were quite a few accessories. I found underwear in a drawer under the bed. A cup. I completely lost interest. After making a mess of the one-room apartment, all I got was 23,000 won in cash.

,Damn it.

It was only natural that this was all there was. This is modern South Korea, after all.

The total amount of money I got from robbing all the empty one-room apartments was 157,000 won. Knowing that this wasn't nearly enough, I clicked my tongue and went out of the building through the window. Unlike the hallway, there were CCTVs at the building entrance. If I avoided CCTVs and black boxes as much as possible, the police wouldn't be able to track me easily.

I pulled my baseball cap down low and put on a mask, clenching my fists a few times. My current physical abilities were at the level of a good athlete, better than the average person. It was only natural since I was in the Department of Sports Science and seemed to have done various sports.

,I have abilities, but I can't use them infinitely. It's like my stamina decreases every time I use them?

Thanks to Recovery, my stamina recovered quickly if I rested for a while. And I deliberately made a wound on my hand to check the effect of Recovery. It's a passive. My stamina recovers automatically. However, that means stamina is consumed as much.

Recovery, one of the top-tier abilities. Frankly speaking, I wasn't sure if it was that good of an ability.

,It's impossible to rob a bank with my current abilities.

Even if I robbed a bank, escaping would be a problem. I didn't have a space-time warp scroll, and I didn't have any companions. Even if I stole cash, it would be hard to move it alone. If the police were determined to chase me, I would inevitably get caught.

I sighed.

Na Chaeyoung sighed.

She was one of the 300 players who had entered this world.

She didn't know why she had entered this world. She had been playing the game [Eternal Eden]. When she opened her eyes, she was in the world of the game, and there were only 30 days left until the destruction on July 30, 2022.

,Why did this happen?

[Eternal Eden] was a survival game played by 300 players. The last one to survive wins. So, if she became the last one, wouldn't she be able to return to her original world?

,...That's too optimistic.

The game had become reality.

At first, she had tried to entrust herself to the government or a company. She was confident that she could persuade them by showing them her abilities. But she gave up after seeing the fate of a player who had acted before her. She saw a player being captured by soldiers and taken to a laboratory in Gangwon-do.

What would they do to her in the lab? It was obvious. She would be subjected to all sorts of experiments. Na Chaeyoung didn't want to become a test subject.

,There are only 30 days left. At this rate, I won't be able to last long and will die.

Players have abilities according to their four classes. Her class was Technologist. In the early stages of the game, it was the class with the lowest combat ability. For her to survive, she had to rely on forces such as the government or companies. But she had openly seen a player being taken to a laboratory, so it was difficult to approach them easily.

So, she decided to create her own group, but it wasn't easy.

What she needed was a talented person. It would be even better if they were an Awakened, but even in computer games, finding an early Awakened was difficult. Besides, not all Awakened were talented. More than anything, it was hard to trust people themselves.

In the end, she hadn't been able to form a group to this day. A sigh came out on its own. She was a player, but she wasn't able to take advantage of the benefits of being a player.

,...The game and reality are different.

Still, she couldn't just stand still, so she was steadily making progress. This world was similar to Earth, but it wasn't Earth. Unlike the Korea she had originally lived in, the underworld was quite large in this world's Korea. To put it bluntly, the power of the gangsters was great.

Na Chaeyoung supplied weapons to the gangsters. Of course, firearms. Because that's where the money was. Besides, maintaining a good relationship with the gangsters would be advantageous for survival. When the zombie outbreak happened in 30 days, the gangsters would come looking for her, the weapon maker. She could negotiate then. She was preparing various things for that.

Na Chaeyoung put two pistols on the workbench into her bag. She took off her white coat. She put on bulletproof jeans and a T-shirt. She put on a jacket over the T-shirt. It was a bit hot, but she couldn't help it. She had to prepare for the worst.

Drrrreureureuk!

She opened the door to the workshop and went outside. Before she could get annoyed by the hot sun, she hurriedly got into her compact car. She started the car and looked at her house. 'Gyeongcheon Used Shop.' It was an old shop in a remote area of Gwangju. Inside that shabby shop were her treasures. A place she could never abandon now.

,Haa.

She sighed and drove up to Seoul.

The gangsters Na Chaeyoung was dealing with were the Gosan Clan, who controlled Gangdong-gu in Seoul. They mainly did loan sharking. So, they had a lot of money.

She parked her car in a suitable place and got out with her bag. Eyes gathered on her. Even though she dressed as plainly as possible, her innate beauty naturally drew attention to her. She put on a cold expression. That was to keep the men from hitting on her.

She headed down a deserted street. Her goal was the loan shark office. The deal was already done, so all she had to do was go there, hand over the guns, and receive the ghost account, and the transaction would be complete. With that money, she would buy materials and make new weapons or strengthen her base.

Tuk.

The moment she turned into the alley, she bumped shoulders with a man coming from the side. Na Chaeyoung frowned without realizing it. The man, who looked like a college student, scanned her up and down with lustful eyes and said.

,Sorry.

The man, who apologized rudely, waved his hand and walked towards the loan shark office. He glanced back at Na Chaeyoung as if he was pondering something, but Na Chaeyoung stood still and stared blankly into the air.

Because she could see something that others couldn't.
