It's already been a week since I came into the game called 'Dark Moon'.

Didn't someone say humans are creatures of adaptation? That's right. I've adapted to this life.

It's all thanks to Instructor Lexi. Rumors of her being a genius at marksmanship spread, and I've been treated well in various ways, making life easier. Even if I make a small mistake, they overlook it to some extent.

'My value has gone up. Even if I can't become a Battle Mage, they can still use me as a sniper.'

Instructor Lexi explained the importance of snipers every time she saw me. Saying that one veteran sniper makes 1,000 allied soldiers comfortable seems a bit of an exaggeration. But anyway, it seems the value of snipers is high.

Yesterday, I heard that Number 31 reached a higher level and became a Rank 2. The instructors and kids all reacted the same way, saying, "As expected of Number 31."

As Number 31 became a Rank 2, she started receiving even better treatment than before, when she was already getting special privileges.

'The difference between Rank 1 and Rank 2 is quite big, so it's not strange.'

I had no talent in martial arts or any physical activity other than shooting. Swordsmanship, spear techniques, and so on.

The shocking thing was that I was completely hopeless at swordsmanship.

'Back on Earth, I was still an F-rank Hunter who used a sword...'

Number 211, despite having the same face as me, had absolutely no athletic ability.

Ttiri-ri-ri, ttiri-ri-ri.

A cheap melody rang out from the ceiling. It was the melody announcing the next schedule. The kids sitting in the barracks went out into the hallway and lined up.

"Let's move to the mana training room."

Number 205, the head of the barracks, led us. I felt gazes as I walked down the hallway. When I glanced over, two soldiers in military uniforms holding guns were watching us as if they were monitoring us. I'm reminded again that this body's status is that of a slave and a test subject.

As soon as I entered the mana training room, I felt a dense concentration of mana. I don't know the exact principles, but the mana training room was more full of mana than other places. As the name suggests, it was a space created for mana breathing exercises.

'Number 211 experienced mana backlash here and fainted.'

And I woke up as Number 211.

I sat down in the designated spot in a lotus position. The lotus position was the most basic posture for mana breathing exercises.

I closed my eyes and focused on contemplation.

'Number 211's mana backlash was caused by breathing. I'll pay special attention to breathing and open the Astral.'

When I opened the Astral, I could feel the presence of mana clearly.

The five mana rods started working. Unlike when using magic, I draw the mana from outside into myself. More than 99% of the mana I absorb flows back out, but a tiny portion remains in my body and accumulates.

This accumulated mana becomes my asset.

"Keoeok! Keueoeok!"

I slightly opened my closed eyes at the voice that broke my concentration. A man who had taken a spot a little away from me was clutching his chest and collapsed to the floor. It was mana backlash. Soldiers rushed over and took the man to the infirmary.

'Three people have suffered mana backlash in a week. No matter what, it's strange. Did they intentionally cause mana backlash?'

As rumors about me spread, the perception of mana backlash changed a little. It was nonsense that if you gain enlightenment through mana backlash, you can reach a higher level.

'They seem to be mistaken about something. Mana backlash can only be a trigger. And even that is a trigger with a very low probability of success.'

Well, I can understand why some guys would deliberately experience mana backlash, hoping for a lucky break. It means they're that desperate.

'Our status is that of slaves and test subjects. If our performance isn't good, we'll be disposed of immediately.'

I closed my eyes again. Whether they deliberately cause mana backlash or not is someone else's business. It's not in my nature to meddle in other people's affairs, and I don't have the capacity for that.

•••

In the afternoon, instead of training in swordsmanship, I headed to the shooting range where Instructor Lexi was. The instructors recognized my talent and scheduled me to focus on marksmanship.

"Hello, Number 211. How are you feeling today?"

Lexi says with a bright smile. On the desk next to her were more than 10 firearms, starting with a pistol.

"Normal."

"You're not in bad condition, are you?"

"No."

"Today, we're going to use different firearms one by one. Surprisingly, each person has different firearms they prefer. Well, with your skill level, it probably doesn't matter which firearm you use."

I silently followed her instructions and started shooting with the pistol first. Every time I shoot, I marvel at myself. At close range, I don't even calculate the wind, and even if I pull the trigger roughly based on instinct, I hit the target.

'Is this what talent is?'

I thought talent was efficiency.

That geniuses are those who achieve maximum effect with minimal effort. Or so I thought.

'But now that I've become a genius, I see. There's no such thing as minimal effort or anything. A genius is incomprehensible.'

I erased all unhelpful thoughts from my head. I just faithfully committed to this talent I have now.

"You know what? The higher a mage's shooting skills, the better."

"...Do mages use firearms often?"

"Huh? Not that, but magic is ultimately a ranged attack. It's useless if you don't hit."

She wasn't wrong.

It's not that there are only ranged attack spells, but the image of a mage itself is that of attacking with magic from the rear. In reality, it's not much different.

'Battle Mages are a bit of an exception, but a mage's combat style is basically ranged.'

The mages in this world don't necessarily try to fight. If they have magic skills of Rank 3 or higher, they can enjoy an elite life, so why would they want to do dangerous things?

Taang. Tang. Tang.

I savor the sound of the gun as I pull the trigger. I've shot more than 300 rounds, but I'm not bored at all because I hit the target every time I shoot.

"Do you remember what I said last time?"

"...Are you talking about the reward you said you'd give me?"

"That's right. I'm asking just in case, but what kind of reward do you want to receive? Tell me honestly. It's rare for me to give a reward to someone, you know?"

"......."

Just because she told me to be honest, I had no intention of foolishly revealing my true feelings. Just because the other person is being nice to me doesn't mean I should misunderstand my current position.

"I'll take whatever you give me."

"That's a boring answer. Don't you want to go outside sometime?"

"......Will you let me go out?"

"Sorry. I don't have the authority to do that. But you'll have a chance to go outside next week."

"What's happening next week?"

"It's your first mission. No, it's better to call it a practice. You're still trainees, after all."

"I want to know the details of the practice."

"It's confidential, so I can't tell you. Actually, I wasn't even supposed to mention that there's a practice... I said it without thinking. It's a secret from everyone else."

She probably didn't say it without thinking. It probably means to be mentally prepared since there's a practice. I was about to pick up the firearm again and focus on shooting, but her hand grabbed my wrist and pulled it down.

She placed a bracelet in my hand. It was a crude metal bracelet.

"This is the reward I'm giving you. It's an artifact with a Rank 4 barrier magic on it. It's my little darling that has saved my life more than 10 times."

Artifact.

A word used to refer to an object with magic on it.

"Thank you."

I accepted the artifact without refusing. A Rank 4 barrier could be my lifeline.

"You can fail in this practice. Everyone has already confirmed your shooting skills. You won't be disposed of even if you fail."

"...I see."

"Oh, right. Instructor Vinus is eyeing you. Saying that your magic manifestation is very clean. But your shooting skills are even better than magic. Don't forget that you were born to be a sniper."

"...Yes. Thank you."

I treated Lexi without showing my true feelings.

To be honest, I wanted to become a mage more than a sniper. This body had as much magical talent as it did shooting talent.

•••

The practice is just one day away.

I plopped down on the toilet seat in the bathroom.

Everywhere I go here, other people's gazes follow me. There are other kids in the barracks, and soldiers monitor the hallways. The same goes for the shower room. There's always a manager there. The only place where I can escape other people's gazes is the bathroom.

'Even this is limited to about 15 minutes. If I stay here any longer than that, they'll barge in.'

I couldn't commit suicide even if I wanted to. The slave seal engraved on my right forearm doesn't allow suicide. It's okay to think about suicide, but if I try to act on it, the seal shines and causes pain. If I ignore the pain, I faint.

The reason I came to the bathroom wasn't to commit suicide, but to check my magic.

'Light.'

I manifest magic without incantation. The sphere of light that appeared on the palm of my hand brightly illuminated the bathroom.

'Fire.'

Flames blazed in the place where the sphere of light had been.

'Ice.'

The flames disappeared, and cold air gathered, creating ice. Holding the fist-sized ice in my hand, I frowned at the coldness and dropped the ice into the toilet bowl.

Pungdeong.

The ice quickly began to melt.

'Wind.'

The wind blew. My hair fluttered.

I skipped the earth attribute magic. I know the formula, but it's not suitable to use here.

'Water.'

Water gathered on the palm of my hand and fell down. Water and ice magic are similar but different.

'...Spark.'

Pajijijik.

Blue sparks flew on the palm of my hand.

I stared blankly at the electricity. Spark, an electric-type magic, was much more comfortable than other attribute magics like Light or Fire. Or rather, it felt familiar.

'Am I specialized in electric-type attributes? Considering that, it's not like other magic felt difficult either.'

I closed my palm. The electricity disappeared.

In that state, I closed my eyes and organized my thoughts on magic and attributes. The characteristics of the attributes I felt were like this.

The mana of light spreads in all directions, the mana of fire is hot, and the mana of ice is cold. The mana of water flows, and the mana of wind is light. The mana of earth is solid, and the mana of darkness is heavy.

'And the mana of lightning is... pure.'

It was the moment I established the attributes. The Astral, called the mage's spirit, reacted. The Astral is trying to expand rapidly. I knew that my rank was about to rise to Rank 2.

'Not yet.'

I forcibly stopped the expansion of the Astral with my will.

'If I go up to Rank 2 now, I'll stand out too much. I have to go up at least a month later.'

I forcibly stopped the rise in rank. It's usually impossible, but my magical talent made it possible. At this point, even I, who am ignorant of magic, can't help but know. My magical talent is better than my shooting.

'The path to Rank 2 isn't blocked. I can go up whenever I want.'

I turned my gaze to my palm.

The attribute I'm most comfortable with. It was definitely electricity.

Pajijik.

'Hmm?'

Electricity sparked from the palm of my hand. I was flustered. I had thought of electricity, but I hadn't activated the formula. The electricity was now flashing with only mana and will, without a formula.

'Is this... an ability?'

Several ways to utilize this ability in magic came to mind. I kept thinking about those methods as I left the bathroom.

•••

"Number 211, Number 72, Number 54, Number 105, Number 14."

Instructor Vinus called out five of us, including me.

"You are Mission 11. Under the guidance of Instructor Belhaga, eliminate the Neros gang. Listen to Instructor Belhaga for the details of the operation."

We headed to Instructor Belhaga. Instructor Belhaga briefed us on the operation in a businesslike manner.

The core of the mission was one thing.

To eliminate the Neros bandit gang hiding in the ruined city.
