Hasent arrived in a rural village far from the Pters territory, which is the center of the Prukus territory. Already, a round moon was floating in the sky instead of the sun.

Hasent quietly looked around the village from the top of a tree. It's quiet. Since the sun had set, most of the villagers had gone home and started sleeping. Unlike the city, there are not many shops that operate late.

'Yujin-gongja-nim said he is the only butcher in the village.'

Hasent moved. A black shadow wriggled under his shoe. It's magic that erases footsteps. Even if you step on a fallen leaf on the floor, there is no sound.

Hasent hid his appearance as much as possible and wandered around the inside of the village.

'...I found it. I smell a strong smell of blood here.'

It was an old shack located on the outskirts of the village. Hasent looked around the house and broke into the interior through the window.

Hasent soon frowned.

Not only the smell of blood, but also the smell of decay was strong. The house was a mess. Old and dirty clothes and cloths were hung haphazardly on the wall, and there were blood-soaked butchering tools on the table.

'Dirty bastard. He acts according to his identity!'

Even if I butchered it myself, I wouldn't want to entrust the work to such a butcher.

Hasent reached out to the shadow on the floor. The shadow rippled and a longsword slid up.

Hasent moved in the direction where steady breathing could be heard.

A man was passed out on the bed. The blanket was disheveled and his arms and legs were sticking out of the bed.

It was a strange sight.

It's the beginning of winter now. It's not enough to heat the room by burning wood in the stove, but the man is sleeping with a thin blanket and doesn't seem to feel cold at all. Rather, he is sleeping in a posture that the blanket is cumbersome.

'This dirty demon bastard!'

The sword was swung without any sound. A black aura was formed on the blade.

The man on the bed suddenly rolled to the side, avoided the sword, and stood up on the floor.

"Assassin? Who sent you?"

The man's body rippled and began to change. The whites of his eyes turned black and his pupils turned blood-red. His skin turned gray and two horns sprouted on his forehead.

"Demon bastard. You've been hiding well all this time."

"Aren't you surprised to see me?"

The man's voice cracked. It's a bizarre voice that cannot be seen as human.

"You knew I was a demon and came looking for me! How did you know?! Did the cult send you?!"

"You don't need to know. Quietly face death. Unclean thing!"

"Such a trivial thing."

The demon rushed in. Sharp claws about 20cm long grew on his hands.

The demon rushed towards Hasent. The bed in the middle collided with his body and shattered. It's an ignorant but fast rush.

'I don't know about anything else, but those claws. There's a possibility of poison.'

He is a low-level demon. He has skills above Aura Expert Intermediate. He is not an opponent to be careless about.

"Huuup!"

Hasent took a deep breath and swung his sword. He cuts off the demon's shoulder. But the demon's rush did not stop.

"I'll cut off your arms and legs and slowly find out!"

The moment the demon came close to his nose, the shadow on the floor soared like a wall and knocked the demon away. The demon flew backwards, smashed through the wooden wall, and rolled several times on the dirty dirt floor. The demon jumped up from his seat. Red blood dripped from the corner of his mouth.

"You, you're not an ordinary guy!"

The demon rolled his eyes and stepped back. He looked for a way to escape.

"Where do you think you're going."

Peok! The sword flew and pierced the demon's calf and stuck into the floor.

"Aaaagh! You human bastard!"

The demon screamed and hurriedly pulled out the sword. Hasent was already right in front of the demon. The demon's mouth was torn apart.

"Kekekeke. You stupid bastard. From the moment you came close, you were dead!"

The demon's mouth opened wide and greenish-black smoke billowed out. It's poisonous smoke. Hasent swung his hand without hesitation. The shadow blade formed on his hand cut off the demon's neck.

The demon's body collapsed on the floor. But the poisonous smoke did not stop and was coming out of the severed neck along with blood.

"Kekekeke. 20 years of effort have been wasted like this... but I'm satisfied that I was able to kill a strong human like you!"

Even though his neck was cut off, his mouth moved and a voice came out.

Hasent crossed his arms. He didn't run away and looked down at the demon with cold eyes.

"Wh, what is it. Why are you okay?!"

"Poisons of this level are all over this world."

Hasent is learning magic and swordsmanship, but his main job is still an assassin. It is natural to use poison and build up immunity to it in the body.

"Eeeeek!"

The demon gritted his teeth and made a resentful expression, then stopped moving as if he were completely dead.

"Hmm. He told me to be sure to take the demon's corpse."
