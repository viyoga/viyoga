Kang Myungjin is aiming to conquer a total of 4 areas simultaneously this time to gain dominion.

Area 6,331, Land of the Enraged Prince.

Area 6,332, Garden of the Sorrowful Queen.

Area 6,333, Castle of the Obsessed King.

Area 6,334, Tower of the Sleeping Princess.

Officially, he's targeting 3 areas, but he intends to conquer the last hidden area, 'Area 6,334,' under the guise of coincidence. The reason is to avoid suspicion. If he knows too much about the hidden area, his comrades might suspect him.

,The important thing isn't that.'

,I have to find a way. A way.'

Kang Myungjin spoke in a stiffer tone than usual. Everyone acknowledged that it was more efficient, so there were no complaints.

Following the conquest method Kang Myungjin had taught us before, we used thick cloths to cover our entire bodies, leaving only our eyes exposed.

After putting on the prepared clothes and rechecking our luggage, we finally entered Area 6,331 with Kang Myungjin's short word.

"Let's go."

It was a vast wasteland. But it was by no means an ordinary place. An ordinary wasteland wouldn't have a river of red-hot lava flowing on one side.

I could feel the suffocatingly hot air. The surrounding temperature quickly exceeded 40 degrees Celsius.

I wanted to throw away the thick cloth I was wearing. But if I did, I would feel more pain. Because this thick cloth blocks special sunlight.

"......"

Kang Myungjin stood still and took out a compass from his pocket.

「Navigator's Compass (S)」 It is a compass that points to the destination when you are lost. I got it about two months ago at Kang Myungjin's request.

I've used it a few times, but it wasn't a very good item unless it was in a situation like this.

"It's to the lower left. Let's move while forming an A formation."

A formation.

It is the most basic formation.

Kang Myungjin, Ji Youngbin, and I stand in the front line, with Joo SeoHyun and Lee Minjeong right behind us. Rillsne, Yoo Seohee, and Yoo Inha are positioned in the rear.

Rillsne is originally in charge of scouting, but she will be active in the next area.

"......"

We were quiet.

There was room to chat. Because all we were doing was walking through the wasteland without seeing any monsters. But the moment you open your mouth, hot air fills your mouth, and if you take a deep breath, you will feel the pain of your lungs burning.

"Stop. Spatial change!"

The space rippled and the scenery changed. In this area, the space changes at regular intervals. It is the opposite of spatial movement. We stay still and the space moves.

"Master! Which way should we go?!"

Ji Youngbin, a warrior who recently joined with the elf, asked. His eyes looking at Kang Myungjin showed trust and respect.

"The compass... is pointing backwards. Reorganize the formation and move again."

Area 6,331 is a trial-type area. If you hadn't learned the conquest method in advance and prepared, you could have wandered around here for days and ended up as a skinny mummy.

It would be nice if we could solve things by walking like this, but there were monsters here too.

A black stone lump suddenly rose from the wasteland.

Stone Golem.

A non-biological monster whose body must be shattered or whose core must be smashed.

However, what you need to know here is that even monsters you know may have slightly different characteristics depending on the area.

"There are a total of 6 Stone Golems. Ji Youngbin, you take the 3 on the left. Seong Yujin, take the 3 on the right. Finish them before they clump together and become more troublesome."

"Leave it to me!"

"Yes, sir."

Ji Youngbin and I took out our weapons from our waists. He has a greatsword, and I have Hwaryeon Bido. Then, I opened the cap of the water bottle, poured cold water on the weapon, and charged towards the black Stone Golems.

,I was bored from just walking, so this is good. Die!'

The knife cut through the Stone Golem's body as smoothly as tofu. This is because these guys' weakness is the cold water on the blade.

After quickly defeating the Stone Golems, I looked at Ji Youngbin. His processing speed was slower than mine. Unlike me, who moved lightly and nimbly, Ji Youngbin dealt with the Stone Golems heavily and solidly. Our combat styles were completely opposite.

"Good job, both of you."

"Haha. It's nothing!"

Unlike Ji Youngbin, who was happy with Kang Myungjin's praise, I took it for granted.
