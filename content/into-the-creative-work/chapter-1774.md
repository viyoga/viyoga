I swung the Super Blade, slicing through the liquid metal. The liquid metal seemed to lose its power, but then it reached out to me again. At the same time, the Super Blade grew heavier, and I felt pressure on my arms and legs. My vision blurred, and the metallic liquid proliferated.

The superpowers of the vampire True Ancestors.

One, one is insignificant. Even the superpowers that directly attack me can't control my body. If I include the abilities I haven't even realized yet, there are easily dozens of superpowers pressing down on me. But none of them were restricting my freedom.

,Was I always this strong?

Noecheon Gyeol (雷天結).

I chanted the gugeol, the core of the Noecheon Style. The energy containing lightning raced through my body, and I was filled with power.

I swung the Super Blade at the approaching metallic liquid. A powerful sword wind arose, blowing the metallic liquid far away.

Noecheon Style (雷天流) Noeseom (雷閃).

Falling from the sky, I swung my sword. The sword, swung more than ten times a second, became Sword energy and fell upon the enemies. The helmeted vampires couldn't avoid it and collapsed to the ground, dead.

Tuk.

I landed softly on the ground and turned my head to look around. There were still dozens of vampires left.

,Is it thanks to the clothes and helmets they're wearing that they can move so vividly even in broad daylight?

I didn't care. I was just convinced that these guys couldn't be my match.

"You little punk. Is this all you've prepared? How boring. Don't you dare go around saying you're my son."

"...Haha. How new. Did you ever think of me as your son?"

Seong Su-un said leisurely. The vampire soldiers next to him were not so relaxed. They charged at me like moths to a flame, trying to kill me. Seeing them rush in even after realizing how strong I was, I was impressed.

,Even with bombs around their necks, they're rushing in like crazy? What kind of brainwashing did you do to make them like this?

This was impressive.

Of course, it's not like I can't guess what he did. He must have been repeating brainwashing on these vampires since they were very young.

Bbeep.

I heard a signal from the vampire soldier charging at me from the front. I reflexively used Instant.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 6]

In the slowed-down world, the vampire's body swelled up and exploded. I leisurely enjoyed the sight before moving to avoid the explosive force before it touched my body.

After killing, killing, and killing the charging bastards, I arrived in front of Seong Su-un.

"Amazing. Even if you ate the Bead of Life, how did you get such power?"

Seong Su-un's composure didn't disappear.

He's still acting cocky even after I've come this far. It's too urgent of a situation to be a bluff. There's something here.

,He's the one who rebelled against me. There's no way he would have prepared something so trivial as a trap. What's his hidden trump card?

For a moment, my head threatened to become complicated. I deliberately erased all the questions.

,Whatever. Let's just kill him.

The sword containing lightning energy flashed.

Noecheon Style (雷天流) Noegwang (雷光).

The blue Sword energy cut through Seong Su-un's body. From his right shoulder to his left side. A clean diagonal line.

A red line was etched on his body. Red blood leaked from the line, and just as his torso was about to slip and fall, the wound disappeared. Only his cut clothes and the blood on the floor proved that there had been a wound.

"Complete recovery...? No, that's not it. Is that your ability as a vampire?"

He smiled, raising the corners of his mouth.

"This regenerative power is just a simple physical ability."

I narrowed my brows. I couldn't understand this creature in front of me.

"Are you even a vampire?"

"Hahaha. Now that I think about it, you were particularly uninterested in vampires. You persecuted vampires, but you never thought about researching and using them."

"What's so special about those things that I have to research them?"

"That's your arrogance. The arrogance of someone with power beyond that of a vampire! Do you know that when humans become vampires, they don't all become equally strong? There are differences between individuals. Do you know what that difference is?"

"Hmm. Now that you mention it, I am a little curious. Does a healthy guy become a strong vampire?"

Seong Su-un tapped his head with his finger.

"It's the mind. Humans with strong mental fortitude become stronger vampires. To be more specific, it's the ego. The more perfect your ego, the more powerful a vampire you can become."

"So you're saying that because your mental strength is strong, you became such a strong vampire? Now that I see it, it was all just bragging."

"But there's a limit to how strong you can become with just that. Vampires get stronger by eating other vampires. You know that, right?"

"I'm losing interest, so just get to the point. I can cut your neck off right now."

"If a normal vampire devours five of their own kind, they become a True Ancestor. Then what about more than five? What happens if you eat 50 vampires?"

"......"

I couldn't answer that question. Because I don't know.

In the original Vampire Detective, there weren't as many vampires as you might think, and cannibalism was taboo. That's because vampires were basically human. Even the original setting book didn't say what would happen if a vampire ate 50 of them.

"Did you do that experiment?"

"It was a little difficult to do it without you knowing. To tell you the result, vampires get stronger the more vampires they eat. But there was a limit. An average of 30. If you exceed the limit, you go berserk and turn into something that's not even a vampire."

"An average of 30? Then there must be some above average."

"During the experiment, there were vampires who ate up to 50. The difference in the limit is mental strength. Here's the question. How many vampires have I eaten?"

I stared at Seong Su-un and pondered. The fact that this bastard is coming out so confidently means he must have eaten more than the average number of vampires.

"...Did you eat about 50?"

"Keuhaha. You still look down on me. 250! I drank the blood of 250 True Ancestors!"

"That's a bit much. But your vampire life ends here."

Pajijik.

A laser was added to the blade of the Super Blade. The blue shining blade was beautiful.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 5]

Seogeok!

I cut off his neck. Even the feeling through the sword was perfect. The bastard's head fell.

,Cutting the body and cutting the neck. The two are clearly different.

But something's strange. Even though his head fell off, his body didn't fall and remained standing upright.

Kudeudeudeuk.

A bright red lump of flesh soared from the severed neck and took the shape of a head. A head made of meat. It was too crude in appearance to be called a real head.

"I have evolved. No matter how much it's you, you can't kill me. I will rule this world."

The bastard swung his right hand. I stepped back. His right hand exploded with a *peong*, and a new right hand sprouted. A bright red and long right hand.

,No way in hell.

While dodging the growing hand, I kept swinging my sword. The bastard's hand was chopped off and fell to the ground. But a new hand immediately sprouted.

"I, I have obtained immortality!"

"Shut up. You can't be seen as human in your current state. Have you really fallen into a monster? Just die quietly."

"Fall."

The huge sword fell as it was and struck Seong Su-un's head first.

Kwang!

It smells like burning meat. It's similar to the smell of burning beef.

,It's different from the smell of burning humans. Is it because he's a vampire?

Kkumeul.

The lump of meat moved.

Seureureureureuk.

,Is that also a type of superpower?

Pajijijijik!

I gathered lightning in my left hand and threw it at the crawling snakes. As soon as the lightning mass touched the ground, it spread in all directions and roasted the snakes golden brown.

Ossak.

Something's coming from behind.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 4]

Seogeok!

"I, I am the master of this world! I!!"

Noecheon Style (雷天流) Noeseom (雷閃).

"You little punk. Why don't you just stop?"

"Eternal life! I have obtained immortality!"

Kwang! Kwang! Kwaaaang!

,Is this the berserk state he was talking about earlier?

A flesh-colored octopus monster was raping the Tokyo Tower.

A nuclear bomb.

But it's a waste to use the nuclear bomb I have. Nuclear bombs have become difficult to obtain as the situation has reached this point. Do I really have to use my own stuff?
