Jackson is a mainland resident from District 6,700. This meant he had been living in District 6,700 since the start of the Eighth Atlantis.

District 6,700 is outwardly glamorous, but if you look inside, it's disgustingly filthy.

Jackson had experienced similar situations a few times before.

,I need to stay calm in times like these. First, back up the data to the server....'

Jackson tapped on the machine while looking out the window. Black cars stopped in front of the studio, and men in suits poured out. At the center was Chief David.

Gray hair neatly swept back, snow-white skin, sunken cheekbones, hollow eyes. The clothes he wore were, of course, a black suit. An impression reminiscent of a grim reaper.

,Damn it. They even brought thugs....'

District 6,700 is safer than other districts. At least, most people in District 6,700 think so. But if you look closely, that's also wrong. All sorts of crimes occur in District 6,700. It's just that they're not easily seen by people.

The Entertainment Kingdom earns AP every month based on popularity.

AP.

Atlantis Points.

A currency higher than the Penny, the common currency of Atlantis.

In District 6,700, you can earn AP every month just by maintaining popularity. Large agencies earn tens of thousands of AP or more per month. They're like rulers of a decent district.

AP that can be obtained relatively easily through entertainment activities. Low risk, high return. That's why the flies swarm.

,Fucking thugs....'

Most of the thugs are exiles from outside. They're guys with stronger physical abilities than ordinary humans who were lucky enough to get a ticket to District 6,700 and settle down. The only ones who can deal with the thugs are other thugs or the police in charge of maintaining security in District 6,700.

,The problem is that the cops are also dog-like bastards.'

Still, he reported it to the police with a faint hope. But it was as expected. The call was cut off before the dial tone rang twice. The police deliberately hung up.

Knock knock knock!

"Director Jackson-nim. This is David. I know you're inside. I even brought a gift for Director Jackson-nim... Please open the door."

"......!"

Jackson inhaled. His heart was racing like crazy. If it's Chief David, he might bury him today. Should he tell David everything?

,...No. If I do, that crazy bastard....'

He remembers Seong Yujin's appearance as he brutally tortured and killed Robert AD. His fingers trembled at that crazed look. Jackson was more afraid of Seong Yujin than Chief David.

"Director Jackson-nim. Are you in a dangerous situation? If you don't answer within 10 seconds... I'll forcibly open the door and go in! 10. 9. 8...."

"Stop! I'll open the door now, so don't do anything!"

"Ah, you're safe. That's a relief."

Jackson looked at his smartphone and headed for the front door. Seong Yujin read the message, but there was no reply. He couldn't tell if he was deliberately ignoring him or if he had read it and was coming here now. If he comes, it will take at least 10 minutes.

Jackson grabbed the doorknob and managed his expression. Chief David came out more peacefully than he thought. That is, he's willing to talk. He'll be able to buy time until Seong Yujin arrives.

He opened the front door. Chief David smiled faintly and bowed slightly.

"Director-nim, I'm sorry for visiting you late at night. I felt bad coming empty-handed, so I brought a gift with me. Please take a look."

"A gift?"

Jackson's gaze turned to David's back. Thugs in suits were holding boxes. And not just one or two.

"What did you bring?"

"The latest electronic products and seasonal fruits that just came out."

A gift.

If it were the usual Jackson, he would have cheered and rejoiced. But now he was as uncomfortable as if he were sitting on thorns.

•••

David, the chief of Nars Entertainment, is originally an exile who worked as a mercenary in the outer district.

He was happy to come to Atlantis. He thought that if he had his skills and brains, he could run wild in Atlantis. But the reality was different from what he thought.

Atlantis was a world of savagery.

Fists are closer than the law? What a joke. Knives were closer than the law. Power, money. It was a world where force was the best.

Good head? It's no use against a strong guy. Your head could be changed at any time with a word from a strong guy.

The reason David came to District 6,700 is because there aren't many strong guys in District 6,700. Because it's a district with many restrictions, the strong feel more frustrated than attracted to the safety of District 6,700.

,I'd rather be the head of a snake than the tail of a dragon. That's my motto.'

David looked at Jackson, who was sitting in front of him. Jackson was acting arrogant towards him, but his eyes showed an unmistakable fear.

David grinned and narrowed his eyes. He used his unique trait.

「Using Lie Detector.」

「Lie Detector

Can detect lies.

Type: Unique Trait

Rank: A 」

"The thing I asked you to do...."

"I'm doing well. Don't interfere."

「That is a lie.」

"Yes. I understand."

David's expression didn't change despite Jackson's prickly reply. But his mind was moving quickly. The thing he asked Jackson to do was a simple task of making a music video for Fovitz in a sloppy way. He just had to do it sloppily.

But it's not working out? There was something there.

David brought up another story first.

"Do you know where Robert AD is now?"

"How would I know that? That bastard disappeared without saying a word while working. He's probably sleeping at home."

「That is a lie.」

Jackson's voice trembled.

Deep fear.

He thought it was fear towards him, but... if he was really afraid of him, he wouldn't have been able to make eye contact properly. Jackson is afraid of something else.

"Director Jackson-nim. Even something trivial is fine. Please give me information about Robert AD. Are you perhaps hiding Robert AD?"

"Me hide that bastard? Why would I?"

It wasn't a lie.

Jackson is not hiding Robert.

"...Actually, I asked Robert AD to do something separately. Do you know about it?"

"I don't know. How would I know that? Don't ask me about Robert."

「That is a lie.」

"You know. I asked Robert AD to secretly film Fovitz's undressing video."

"...You made him do a dirty job. David. This has nothing to do with me."

"Director Jackson-nim, you've been afraid of something since earlier. What are you so afraid of? Is Robert AD perhaps dead?"

"......."

Jackson's pupils shook greatly.

"If not, please answer that it's not."

"I, I don't know. Robert probably took your money and disappeared. He's the kind of guy who would do that."

「That is a lie.」

"He's dead."

"I said I don't know."

「That is a lie.」

"Is it Lilith Entertainment's doing? They're coming on pretty strong this time. I knew Lilith Entertainment didn't hesitate to do dirty work, but I didn't think they would go as far as murder...."

"Fuck. I said I don't know! Get out right now if you're going to speculate!"

「That is a lie.」

"Were you threatened by Lilith Entertainment? Director-nim, let's join hands. I'll save you, Director-nim. Trust me and follow my instructions. Lilith Entertainment will never reach out to you. I promise you on my name."

David, who bowed his head to Jackson, was in a good mood at the thought of putting a leash on Jackson. Lilith Entertainment's unconventional response? He had predicted it to some extent, so it was okay. No, it was something he had been hoping for. Lilith Entertainment will falter a little because of this.

,Lilith Entertainment made a hasty move. That's their mistake. If they're going to fight in a dirty place... they're welcome. First, coax Jackson to find out the whole story....'

Thud.

A dull sound came from behind.

When David raised his head, something fell from above. It was a severed human head. The face was that of a bodyguard he knew well.

Jackson's face came into his view. Jackson's pale face was distorted with fear. The bodyguards moved before they could scream.

Thud, splat, thump.

There was no sound of battle.

Instead, he heard the sound of bodies falling. Human heads fell, and blood filled the floor. All of his bodyguards were dead. Step, step. Someone stepped through the pool of blood and approached David.

Cold sweat ran down David's neck. He felt like a sharp knife was enveloping his whole body.

"Hey."

"......."

He slowly turned his head back at the voice calling him. A man with a red knife in his hand was standing among the corpses.

"Are you David?"

"...Yes. I'm David, working at Nars Entertainment. You are...."

"How should I kill you? You messed with my woman... don't think you're going to die easily."

"......I haven't met a woman recently. There must be some misunderstanding...."

"Fovitz. You tried to film a hidden camera, you son of a bitch. That Roberts or whatever his name is told me everything."

Seong Yujin swung the Hwaryeon Bido in annoyance. The bodyguard's sedan, which was lying nearby, was neatly cut in half, spilling blood and guts.

David grabbed his trembling hands. He knew it as soon as he saw him. That's a crazy bastard who doesn't understand words. He could strongly smell the crazy bastards he had encountered about twice while living as a mercenary. One was a guy who only killed boys, and the other was a guy who enjoyed cannibalism. And all those crazy bastards were stronger than David.

"...Save, please save me. I'll do anything... I'll do anything."

David got off the chair and knelt on the floor.

"Oh. You bow right away? You're a smart guy. It's good if we can talk. I'll spare you."

「That is a lie.」

He's going to die.

The other person has no intention of sparing him at all.

He pulled himself together. He had survived facing crazy bastards twice. If he pulls himself together this time, he'll be able to survive.

"I'll really do anything. I'll give you all my assets. Just save me... just save me."

"I said I'll spare you. Look up. Let me see your face."

「That is a lie.」

David looked up.

The crazy bastards he met hated being told twice.

"It's been a long time since I've been this pissed off. I probably won't forget your face for a while. Your right arm and your left arm. Which one should I cut off first? Huh?"

"My unique trait is a lie detector. I can listen to the other person's voice and judge whether they're lying."

"...Oh. That's interesting. And?"

"I'll be your personal lie detector. Please spare me."

"I said I'll spare you?"

「That is a lie.」

"I'll bark if you tell me to bark, and I'll kneel if you tell me to kneel. I'll tell you all the corruption of Nars Entertainment. I'll definitely be useful."

"The secrets of Nars Entertainment.... I'm suddenly interested. Those celebrities there.... Can they do sexual favors?"

"Yes, they can. I'll call them right away if you want."

"I needed a useful dog anyway.... I like you. I'll spare you."

「That is a lie.」
