I was returning on a drone.

I spotted a truck speeding down an unpaved road. It was a large truck carrying a dragon over 10m in size. I could see Hunters on bikes around the truck.

,Seven Hunters. Two are roughly A-class... the rest are B-class or below.'

The group of Dragon Hunters is called a clan. It's roughly similar to a guild in the real world.

[Opening the Mind's Eye.]

I checked the surroundings. No one was visible within a 3km radius.

"That dragon is mine now."

Pajijijijik.

Lightning crackled and began to gather in my grasp.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Myriad Thunder (卍雷).

The launched lightning blasted the truck. The truck driver died instantly, and the A-class Hunter in the passenger seat jumped out, aimed a bow at me, and fired an arrow.

I deflected the arrow with Hwaryeon Bido and kicked off into the air.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I reached the A-class Hunter and swung my sword. Wind rose, pushing the A-class Hunter's body backward. The blade narrowly missed.

,Indeed. Even rotten, they're still A-class Hunters.'

I had no intention of fighting these guys for long.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant (刹那). Remaining stacks: 10]

I took a step forward and swung my sword. The A-class Hunter's head was severed and floated into the air.

Following that, I used high-speed movement with Flying Thunder God to take care of the nearby Dragon Hunters.

What remained was an A-class Dragon Hunter armed with a gunlance and shield, and wearing heavy armor.

"Why did you suddenly appear and do this? Did we do something wrong to you?"

"I coveted the dragon you had."

"...Did you really need to kill us? If you had asked, we would have given you the dragon corpse without hesitation."

"That might have been the case. But what about after? You would have run to the Dragon Hunter Association and reported this incident. And I would have become a criminal and things would have become troublesome in many ways. It's better to handle it cleanly from the start."

"To have that much power and still resort to robbery... You don't deserve to be a Dragon Hunter!"

"Are you stupid? The Hunter world is like this. There's no law in dungeons. Ah, there wasn't a dungeon here, was there."

"I won't die easily! I'll take at least one of your arms!"

Bang!

An explosion occurred at the end of the gunlance. Black smoke spread, obscuring the view. Of course, it was useless. I could see the bastard rushing in through the Mind's Eye. Even if I couldn't, I could feel all of his movements with electromagnetic waves.

I pulled the tip of Hwaryeon Bido back and took a swinging stance. Sword energy bloomed from the blade.

He seemed to be trusting in his shield and armor, but I planned to cut him down entirely the moment he approached.

"Stop!"

He shouted. Red liquid flowed out from under his helmet, as if he had vomited blood.

Kung!

His heart stopped. Not only that, but his body itself froze. An unknown force was binding my body.

,A word curse? He had that kind of ability too.'

He approached. Just before the gunlance pierced my head.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts 1 minute.]

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 9]

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷光).

A red line split the bastard's armor and body. The bastard fell to the floor with his eyes wide open.

I habitually shook Hwaryeon Bido. Not a drop of blood was on it. The speed of Thunder Flash was too fast for blood to stick to the sword. The sword energy also played a part.

"Even if I don't know anything else, word curses are a bit dangerous. A trump card used without caring about the backlash. The Hunters here aren't easy targets either."

I thought they were just pushovers, but I changed my mind.

"Next time, I should kill the A-class Hunters first."

All that was left was to collect the spoils.

I grinned.

If I killed and plundered, I wouldn't need to spend money. Rather, I could earn more money. Aside from the dragon heart, I could sell the Hunter equipment, the truck, and the dragon's byproducts.

,A perfect crime situation like this, where it's so easy to rob, is rare.'

These guys kept the most expensive dragon heart separately in a magically sealed storage box. It's a box I don't need since I have an inventory.

,I'll sell the box and appraise the dragon heart.'

,It's too big to be a Lesser Dragon. More than anything, it's a clan with two A-class Dragon Hunters. A clan of that size wouldn't bother hunting a Lesser Dragon.'

It doesn't make financial sense.

Even large guilds in the real world don't often attack C-class or lower dungeons. The money they get is too little compared to the guild's size. Even if they do attack, it's mostly to give new employees experience.

Even if this world's civilization is more advanced than I thought, it's not comparable to reality. The basics of this world are fantasy with dragons flying around.

A Dragon Hunter and other people went missing?

Ah! They died a sudden, violent death somewhere! They probably became dragon poop.
