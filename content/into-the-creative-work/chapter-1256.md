I grabbed a chair from the office and sat in the hallway. Corpses were scattered in front of me, and behind me was the way to the bathroom. Since this was a one-way street, no one could go back without my permission if I blocked it here.

I stared at Venus's head and fell into thought for a moment.

When I killed Venus, I felt a bit of relief and coolness. It's the same even now, looking at Venus's head. But it wasn't refreshing. It felt like I came out after a shower without properly drying the water on my body.

,I know the reason. It must be because my revenge isn't over yet.'

Venus is just a middle manager.

There's someone else who planned and executed the Battle Mage Project. It's probably someone with a position so high that it's embarrassing to compare them to Venus. And I know one of them.

Alpha Tear Pharmaceutical Company. The company that created the mana essence and provided it to the military. They're my target for revenge.

I couldn't just go and kill them recklessly. As I am now, if I charged at Alpha Tear, I'd just be crushed and killed like an insect.

,I need to build up my strength. To face Alpha Tear alone, I need to reach at least the 9th Circle. If I openly mess with Alpha Tear, the Freesia Kingdom will most likely step in....'

A sigh came out on its own.

I even doubted if I could end this revenge. But I couldn't just be satisfied here and end it. I will find and take revenge on the bastards involved in the Battle Mage Project.

I stared at Venus's head for a long time before closing my eyes.

As the [Overload]'s doping effect ended, extreme fatigue and pain rushed in at once. But the bigger problem was that my mana road was damaged.

Out of 50, only 34 were intact. 16 were destroyed to the point where I couldn't repair them on my own. To repair the destroyed 16 mana roads, I need to find a special healer.

,...This is serious. I can't do a long-term battle like this.'

A 5th Circle mage's mana road is at least 80 or more. But I don't even have half of that. It's because I forcibly reached the 5th Circle using drugs, and overworked my mana road with Overload.

,If I were a normal mage, I might have despaired, but I have the knowledge of the original work. I know a healer who can repair my mana road, and I also know the location of the treasures sleeping in this world and how to obtain them. It's not such a desperate situation for me. I just... need some time.'

I sighed. There's so much to do that I don't know where to start.

Tteok tteok.

I opened my eyes at the sound of footsteps. There was no one in front of me. I slowly turned my head back. There was No. 31 in her military uniform. Her purple hair was slightly wet, and her face was expressionless as usual.

To move this quickly even after being hit by sleeping gas. I quietly admired her. I guessed it was probably thanks to the witch's blood she possessed.

,You killed Instructor Venus.'

,Yeah. It was really tough. No. 31. Are you going to kill me? Honestly, I don't have the strength to fight you right now. If you're going to kill me... kill me cleanly.'

I said, looking forward again. I thought that if I were to die by No. 31 or No. 212 now, it wouldn't be so bad, even if it were other guys.

,...It reminds me of the old days. Back then. When the dark moon rose, you were the one who saved me when I went berserk and fell from the waterfall. I'll repay that debt now.'

There was a time like that.

,Should I say thank you for saving me.'

,That's not what I mean. I never intended to kill you from the beginning. What I mean by repaying the debt is... I'll take responsibility for my classmates, including No. 212.'

,......!'

Surprised, I turned to look at her. No. 31 was looking at me with her arms crossed and a haughty expression.

If it's her. If it's Irina Reltierus, the Duke's daughter, it was definitely possible. I felt a little relieved.

,Thank you. No. 31.'

,There's a condition. The Battle Mage Project. Don't announce it to the world. Bury the Battle Mage Project.'

,It's for my classmates. You don't want them to suffer from the media, do you?'

,...That includes me in those classmates.'

If the Battle Mage Project is buried, the one who benefits the most is not No. 31, but me. Because I'm a rebel who killed his superior, and I'm sure I'll be branded as a terrorist and even have a bounty on my head. The Freesia National Army won't bother to reveal the Battle Mage Project either. If they do, they can't avoid international criticism, and hyena-like human rights activists will rush in to extort money.

,Thank you. I'll try to do that if possible. I can't be sure because I'm not the one in charge of this... but the Battle Mage Project will not be announced to the world. Thank you so much, No. 31.'

,Irina.'

,Huh?'

,My name is Irina.'

She turns her body and walks towards the bathroom. I watched her disappearing back and hurriedly said.

,Irina! My name is Eugene!'

A week later.

Louis Billstein came to my hideout. For reference, he prepared the hideout where I'm staying now. It's in a remote village on the outskirts of the Freesia Kingdom.

,You look much better than when I saw you last time. I'm glad.'

,How did things go?'

,It went as Eugene-ssi wanted. We negotiated with the Freesia National Army and decided to bury the Battle Mage Project. Ah, all of Eugene-ssi's classmates have gone under the command of No. 31, Irina Reltierus. The military naturally tried to refuse, but... Duke Reltierus used his power.'

,I'm glad things worked out well. What about the pursuit of me?'

,There's no reaction yet. But we can't be relieved. There's a possibility that the Freesia Kingdom's special agents are moving secretly.'

,Yeah. I guess it's better to leave this country.'

Louis said as if he had been waiting.

,Come to our Empire. We'll give you an ID and a nice mansion. Instead, please join our company.'

,I told you that story last time. I'm not going to the Empire.'

,I still can't understand why. Isn't it a bad condition? The Freesia Kingdom can't treat the Heist Empire carelessly either.'

,Not just the Empire, but other countries can't treat it carelessly either. The Empire's military power is too strong. I'm sick of the military. Besides, isn't Kaiser purging the rebels? I have no intention of getting caught up in the purge by going to the Empire.'

Louis handed me a bag. I opened the bag and examined the contents. Seven Dark Holes in glass bottles and one bank card. A first-class ticket to Neo London. And a Neo London 3rd class citizen ID.

,I'm disappointed. But why did you choose Neo London?'

,The Haffe Republic... I killed quite a few soldiers there. As soon as I enter the Republic, they'll target me. Divine France is more peaceful than Neo London, but I hate being involved in religion. The other countries are greatly influenced by the Freesia Kingdom, so they're not safe.'

,So you chose Neo London by elimination.'

I linked the account to my watch and checked my assets.

,...Why is there only 50 million credits?'

,We spent 200 million to get the Dark Holes in that bag. Also, we spent 600 million to get the Play Road drug last time, and this time we spent 300 million to completely freeze Venus's head. Besides, after deducting Eugene-ssi's ID, train fare, hideout usage fee, etc., the remaining money is 50 million credits. It was originally 4,757 credits, but I used some generosity.'

,......Your calculation is excessively accurate.'

,Haha. As a businessman, it's a very welcome compliment. Ah, if you look at your citizen ID, I chose the last name arbitrarily.'

,Eugene Meyer. Not bad.'

,I was going to put Adolf... but it seems like Eugene-ssi doesn't like it. So I put in a safe last name.'

,If it was Adolf, I would have thrown the citizen ID at your face.'

I got up from my seat.

,Are you leaving already?'

,There's nothing to do here even if I stay longer.'

I packed my clothes and necessities into my bag. Since I didn't have many things, packing was done in 5 minutes.

,Louis. Do you believe in reincarnation?'

,Reincarnation out of the blue? I don't know much about religion or mythology.'

,It's not that. I remembered my past life in a dream, so that's why. Surprisingly, I had more than one past life.'

,Haha. Is that so. Were you a noble in your past life?'

,...How did you know? I was the son of a Count in my past life.'

,Hoo. What's the name of the family? I'll look it up.'

,Well. It becomes too blurry after I wake up from the dream.'

,It's a frivolous conversation to have before parting. If you need my help later, please contact me. I'll help you.'

,For free?'

,I have service in my dictionary, but no free stuff.'

Before leaving the door, I put on a mask on my face. I plan to take off the mask after arriving in Neo London.

,I'm going.'

,Isn't that too insincere for a farewell?'

,I don't think we're in a relationship to have a sincere greeting.'

,You're so cold. Well, go carefully. My hunch is that we'll meet again later.'

I didn't answer and waved my hand as I went outside.
