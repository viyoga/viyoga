I summoned a bottle filled with green liquid. Hebil's Accelerator.

It has an amazing effect of making plants grow instantly and bear fruit. Of course, the cost-effectiveness is good too. It can affect about 10 square meters per drop. I had over 2,000 of these Hebil's Accelerators.

,With about 100 Hebil's Accelerators, I'd have more than enough to make a forest.'

The Drought Hell is nothing now.

I dropped a drop of Hebil's Accelerator under a tree. The effect was triggered. The sapling grew rapidly. The branches grew and the leaves became lush.

But my face wasn't bright.

Normally, when you sprinkle a drop of Hebil's Accelerator, the plant should even bear fruit. But this apple tree stopped growing in the middle.

,What's the problem?'

The tree began to dry up.

I immediately realized what the problem was.

Water. There was a lack of water. I kept pouring water on the tree. As a result, I succeeded in making fruit.

The number of apples was less than I thought.

,...20. That's too few.'

I harvested the apples before they withered. Not to eat. It's to germinate the apple seeds and make new trees.

,There were gaps in bringing water in between... Is that why only 20 fruits grew?'

Bringing water from another world wasn't easy either. It's one thing if it's a small amount of water. If the amount reaches 1 ton, it becomes a big deal.

,Did I use about 30 tons of water to grow one apple tree? And that's not enough.'

To grow it properly, I think I'd need more than that, like 50 tons.

,Maybe it's because I only poured water in one place....'

I looked at the apple tree. It's been 5 minutes since I watered it. The apple tree was dying. The leaves withered and fell, absorbed into the red sand.

,If it rains, can I grow them in large quantities?'

Drought Hell.

Thinking of that name, waiting for rain to fall naturally is a foolish thing to do.

,There is a way though.'

「Flute of Torrential Rain

One-time use

Can summon torrential rain.

The torrential rain lasts for three hours.

Rank: S」

A flute that can summon torrential rain.

I can use this flute to summon torrential rain for 3 hours, then plant tree seeds and Hebil's Accelerator to create a forest.

,With Hebil's Accelerator... it's possible.'

I made a plan, but there was something missing. Apple seeds. The Flute of Torrential Rain can only be used once, for three hours, so I need to collect as many apple seeds as possible.

,Damn it. Here begins the grind.'
