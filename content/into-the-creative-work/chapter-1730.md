The environment changed in an instant. I was standing in front of a fountain in a city. The city resembled a medieval fantasy straight out of a game. It was too clean to simply call it the Middle Ages.

,Yu... Sota! You've arrived!

Seong Hari-nim was already there. She was wearing her school uniform, just like I'd seen in reality. It suited her very well.

,Aya-nuna. You were here first.

Aya-nuna. As if the word was unfamiliar, Seong Hari-nim blushed and nodded.

,Ah, yeah. They called it a tutorial, right? It ended quickly.

,It wouldn't have been too difficult for you, Nuna.

As I continued to speak, I noticed something strange. A sword scabbard was hanging at Seong Hari-nim's waist. Seong Hari-nim's main weapon was undoubtedly the spear. I had never seen her handle any other weapon.

,Nuna. That sword...? Didn't you choose the spear?

,Youjin-gun uses a sword, so I was curious what it felt like, so I chose it once. It's quite different from the spear, so it's not easy to handle.

Seong Hari-nim said with a smile. Of course, I didn't believe her 100%. With Seong Hari-nim's combat sense, she would show considerable skill even with a sword. There's also the saying, "All skills lead to one."

,What's your job?

,Samurai.

,...Samurai? Not a swordsman?

,I don't know because I didn't decide it. Who should I talk to? That notification window just set my job arbitrarily.

,I'm just calling it AI. Then what's your sub-job? You can choose your sub-job.

,Magician.

I opened my mouth at the unexpected word. Magician. It was a word that didn't suit Seong Hari-nim at all. At my reaction, Seong Hari-nim scratched her cheek awkwardly.

,I used to want to be a spirit master and a magician. I was going to be a spirit master... but I thought the spirits might hate me here too, so I decided to be a magician. Sota-ssi. What about you?

I nodded because I didn't particularly want to blame her. Besides, I wasn't in a position to judge others.

,My job is spirit master and my sub-job is priest.

,...Priest? Do you happen to have a religion you believe in?!

,No, not really. I just wanted to try it. It seems like you can do whatever you want for your sub-job.

If that weren't the case, there would be no reason to give us a choice.

,Hmm. I see. But do you know how to use magic?

,I don't know. Try shouting 'Fireball.'

,Fireball!

Seong Hari-nim shouted without hesitation. Nothing happened. Even in the embarrassing situation, Seong Hari-nim was calm. She's embarrassed to wear a school uniform, but she's not embarrassed about this. It seems.

,Hmm. It doesn't work. Is there some condition...? Youjin-ah, you try it once.

,Heal!

As expected, nothing happened.

We were racking our brains and experimenting with various things when a burly man approached us. A green name, 「Combat Instructor Harada Jun」, floated above his head.

,The transfer students are making a fuss. I'm not saying it's a bad thing. It's much better than being depressed. You can tell by the name above my head. I'm Harada Jun, a combat instructor. I'll tell you the rules. Before that, how much do you know about the Terra Simulator?

,I know it's virtual reality. Is it really possible to create this with just scientific technology?

I asked him. Harada Jun shook his head.

,It's not just scientific technology. The Terra Simulator was created using all sorts of technologies and abilities, including magic, yin-yang techniques, and superpowers. Above all, Tenrai Nagisa-nim's abilities and super artificial intelligence play the most important roles.

,Artificial intelligence alone seems like an amazing technology.

,The association provided the artificial intelligence. They said they obtained the core of the artificial intelligence from a dungeon, not that they created it directly. We just applied the core of the artificial intelligence to the Terra Simulator.

,But is this really for education? This virtual reality is like....

,Like a game? That's the intention. We deliberately made it like a game to consider accessibility for the general public.

,Can the general public also use the Terra Simulator?

,Of course, it's possible. The government and the association also have the purpose of commercializing the Terra Simulator as a game. Is that all the questions? Ask Kurokawa-sensei about technical questions, not me.

,What should we do?

Seong Hari-nim asked him. It would be troublesome if we were just left here without any instructions.

,This world, Terra, is a world created for the education of you academy students. It also includes social education. Explore this world. If you do it like a game, your level will increase. The sum of your job level and sub-job level will be your grade. It may be disadvantageous for you transfer students who are starting late... but that can't be helped. Accept it. The time difference is 1 to 5. One second in reality is five seconds here. Still, you'll be short on time, so work hard.

,If there are levels, are there also skills?

He nods at my question.

,Of course, there are. You're probably level 1 now, so there won't be any skills you can use. You can naturally learn skills as you level up. Before that... do you see that big monument over there? You can change your sub-job or learn basic skills there.

It really felt like a virtual reality game.

From some point on, Seong Hari-nim was just listening with a look that said she didn't know what was going on. Seong Hari-nim is ignorant when it comes to games. For her, games are just casual things like Tetris. RPG games? She probably doesn't even know what the word means.

,Seeing her mention Go-Stop when I suggested playing a game together last time....

She had no interest in RPG games.

,By the way, pay attention to the names above your heads. Green names mean real people like you and me. Red names are hostile targets. White names are neutral NPCs.

,Can we kill neutral NPCs and take what they have?

,You're thinking like a villain. You can't get anything even if you kill neutral NPCs. Obtain it legitimately through quests. And if you do such a thing, you'll receive penalty points. If you accumulate penalty points, you'll be disciplined by the academy, so be careful.

,Thank you for the explanation.

,If something very urgent happens, shout 'Academy, help me!' If you shout it once, the AI will help you, and if you shout it three times in a row, you'll be forcibly disconnected from Terra.

,What happens if... we die here?

,Your level decreases. Your job and sub-job levels each decrease by 1. A total of 2 levels are lost. And as I said, your level is your grade.

,What is the level of the academy's number one?

,130.

After answering all the questions, Harada Jun logged out. He didn't seem to have much interest in virtual reality.

,Sota-ssi. Did you understand what that person said?

,What about you, Nuna?

,...I didn't really understand what he was saying in the middle. Levels, quests....

,Don't worry about that. Just do what we have to do.

I looked at the back of my hand. When I thought of the cocode that the headmaster gave me, a rhombus pattern appeared on the back of my hand. One of the vertices of the rhombus shone, and then the pattern spun around. The pattern stopped and pointed in one direction.

The place we need to go has been decided. At the end of this is Susanoo's Magatama.

,Before we go, let's go to the monument that the combat instructor mentioned and learn the basic skills.

,Is that really necessary?

,You said you wanted to use magic. You can learn magic there.

,Let's go quickly. Yu.... Sota-ssi.

,You keep calling my name strangely.

,I can't help it if it doesn't stick to my tongue.

I walked to the front of the monument. I could learn the basic skills of a spirit master and the basic skills of a priest.

「Spirit Enhancement

Level: 1

Enhances the power of the spirit by 1%.」

「Heal

Level: 1

Consumes mana to restore 10% of the target's health.」

I was able to learn the skills I expected.

Seong Hari-nim also learned basic skills in the same way.

「Sword Focus

Level: 1

Increases attack power by 5% when using a sword.」

「Light

Level: 1

Consumes mana to create light.」

,Light! Sota-ssi! Look at this!

Seong Hari-nim spread out her palm and used the Light magic. When a sphere of light appeared, she smiled and rejoiced like a child.

,Mom can drop lightning too. But you're happy with that?

,I can drop lightning, but I can't make a sphere of light like this.

We moved in the direction indicated by the rhombus pattern.

A plaza appeared. There were people wearing school uniforms like me and Seong Hari-nim walking around the plaza. Their names were written in green letters above their heads.

,They're laughing and chatting. They were so quiet and bleak when they were in the classroom.... They're completely different.

A group of people approached us. A group of three men and two women. I examined their attire. They were wearing school uniforms, but the equipment they were wearing on top of them was slightly different.

,The guy wearing a robe is a magician... the guy wearing light armor over his school uniform is a swordsman.... The woman is wearing a bow and gloves.

I could infer their jobs from the equipment they were wearing.

,Transfer students.

The tallest man opened his mouth. His chin was pointed, his eyes were narrow, and his lips were thin. He had a sharp impression like a dagger. The name above his head was Kikukawa Keiichi.

We have no intention of laughing and joking with you. This is a competition. We've already formed a team, and we don't have the capacity to accept you. The same goes for the other guys.

,Ah. Is that so?

,You guys take care of yourselves. If you interfere with us, we'll kill you.

They glared at me and Seong Hari-nim menacingly and turned away and disappeared.

,It seems we're not welcome.

Seong Hari-nim smiled bitterly.

,It seems like the competition here is quite fierce. It can't be helped. If you think about it a little differently, it's actually a good thing. We have something else to do separately.

Following the direction indicated by the rhombus, we came out of the city and encountered a monster.

It was a goblin.

,I'll take care of it.

Seong Hari-nim moved lightly. But that movement was not light at all. The moment I heard the sound of the air tearing, the goblin's neck was cut off and falling to the ground.

,Terra's level doesn't affect the body.

According to what I heard from Harada Jun earlier, the body's abilities are the same as in reality. It's the same even if the level goes up. You have to constantly train your body in reality to strengthen your body in Terra.

Conversely, if the body in reality is extraordinarily strong, the body in Terra will also be extraordinarily strong.

,It doesn't seem like it will take long to find Susanoo's Magatama.
