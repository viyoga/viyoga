The King of the Rapelli Kingdom has passed away.

Unlike the death of Count Heathfield, there is no conspiracy behind the death of the Rapelli King. Count Heathfield and the Rapelli King were different in status from the start. There will be several investigations into the King's death, and the results of the investigation revealed that the Rapelli King's cause of death was old age.

The problem is that the Rapelli King died without naming an heir.

Generally speaking, it is right for the eldest son, Prince Aiden, to take the throne. But Aiden was a well-known scoundrel. If he were competent, maybe. He was incompetent. Duke Helbrit had planned to make him king and use him like a puppet, but the Helbrit family was wiped out by Yuria-ssi's hand.

Still, Aiden has supporters left. No matter what anyone says, Aiden was the eldest legitimate son, and it would be more profitable for greedy nobles if Aiden became king.

However, not all nobles are only out for their own greed. Nobles with their heads screwed on straight support Princess Eileen. She was so competent that she perfectly performed the work of a prime minister at a young age. The nobles praised that if she became queen, the Rapelli Kingdom would enjoy a golden age for the next 50 years.

,Now, the Rapelli Kingdom has no regent, and the war is ongoing.'

Therefore, it was the nobles who decided the king. Aiden and Eileen. One of the two direct royal family members can be selected to inherit the throne. But the opinions of the nobles are not united.

,It's different from the original. In the original, Aiden was favored. Because the Helbrit family chose Aiden.'

Nevertheless, it was Eileen who became king in the end. Because it was Kyle-ssi, the protagonist of the original, who chose Eileen.

"Yuria-ssi. What about the King's funeral? How are they doing the funeral?"

"It seems that the funeral will be held in the traditional way in two weeks."

"They're holding it in the traditional way even though we're at war? Those old geezers, seriously.... Kkuk kuk."

The Rapelli King's funeral is famous. They cast anti-decay magic on the coffin containing the King's body, escort the coffin, circle around the entire Rapelli Kingdom, and then cremate it.

The reason why they are holding the funeral even though the war has broken out is because the war is only happening on a small scale in the border area, but probably the political reason is bigger.

"Yuria-ssi. We have to attend the funeral. Tell Floy and prepare. We need black clothes."

"Yes. I will prepare."

Not all nobles need to attend the King's funeral. After all, as the funeral progresses, they travel around all regions, so the nobles have no choice but to attend the funeral in some sense.

,But it's a little different if you start in the funeral procession from the beginning.'

Those who aim for the central power of the Rapelli Kingdom will all participate in the funeral procession. The funeral lasts for about 100 days, and you can make an impression by holding the funeral with the next king for 100 days.

,Now that Duke Helbrit, Aiden's biggest supporter, is gone, if I do nothing and leave it alone, the nobles will probably enthrone Eileen as queen by the time the funeral is over. I have to participate and keep Eileen in check.'

Of course, I won't be able to make Aiden king with this. My goal this time is to keep Princess Eileen in check.

The first day of the funeral was gloomy. It didn't rain, but the sky was full of gray storm clouds from dawn.

The citizens of the royal capital, who had to wear dark-colored clothes during the national funeral, whispered that even the sky was mourning the death of the King.

To the people, the dead Rapelli King was a wise king. Because no major wars or major natural disasters occurred during his reign, and he was safe and sound.

,He was a king who was neither incompetent nor competent.'

If he were competent, he would have passed the throne to Eileen while he was alive, and if he were incompetent, Aiden would have already become king.

I went to Prince Aiden's room with Yuria-ssi. Prince Aiden was sitting on a chair, nervously shaking his legs.

"Baron Prucus! Why are you only coming now?! If you come to the royal capital, shouldn't you come see me!"

"Shut up."

When I coldly retorted, Aiden flinched, shut his mouth, and looked at me. Aiden knows that I wiped out the Helbrit family overnight. And he also knows that he cannot become king without me.

I sat across from him and remained silent. Aiden couldn't open his mouth while I was silent.

"Didn't the King leave a will?"

"A will.... He left one. But they were all trivial things. He didn't leave a single word about the important succession to the throne! Goddamn it! I'm the eldest son, but Father only favored Eileen. Even until the moment he died!"

"Well, calm down. It's not like Princess Eileen has become queen yet."

"Now is not the time to calm down! Do you know how many nobles follow that bitch? As far as I know, there are over 30! Among them, Duke Sunheights officially supported that bitch!"

A great noble of the South. Duke Sunheights. He was the most powerful noble among the nobles in the royal capital.

"At this rate, Eileen might take the throne after the funeral is over!"

"That won't happen, so calm down. Or do my words not sound like words to you?"

Gulp.

Aiden swallowed hard.

"I, I'm sorry. Baron. I was so nervous that I upset the Baron...."

"It's okay. Your Highness."

"Still, Baron... Eileen is dangerous. That sly bitch is plotting to push me out. How about getting rid of her right now? You can do it, can't you, Baron?"

"Your Highness. Absolute monarchy is not established just because you have taken the throne. It is a useless position if the nobles do not follow you. If Princess Eileen dies, who do you think the nobles who supported Princess Eileen will suspect?"

"If I kill them all...."

"Does Your Highness want to have a kingdom that has become a mess? There is a time for everything, so wait."

Yuria-ssi couldn't kill them all by herself either. She is strong, but there were some master-class powerhouses in the kingdom as well.

"Just wait? Is there nothing else I can do?"

Aiden said. It seems that he is getting impatient as the king has died and the throne is vacant. He didn't grieve at all at his father's death.

"Don't cause any accidents while the funeral is going on. Do you understand?"

"......I understand. Baron."

I got up from my seat. I came to him to urge him not to cause any accidents.

People lined up in front of the royal castle. Royal knights riding horses and wearing armor formed a line in the front, and behind them, splendid carriages lined up one after another. The carriage where the King's body was laid was the carriage at the very front. Behind it, Prince Aiden's carriage, Princess Rapelli's carriage, and other nobles' carriages followed one after another.

The number of nobles participating in this procession exceeded 100, and the number of armed soldiers and knights exceeded 3,000. Including the number of servants, it amounts to 5,000.

Before the procession departed, I faced Princess Eileen.

Shining blonde hair and sapphire-like blue eyes. White skin and delicate features. An outstanding figure that stands out even though she is wearing a black dress. She is a beautiful woman who could be mistaken for a sculpture rather than a person.

"Baron Prucus. You have also attended, Baron. I heard that you came to the royal capital last night... I am only seeing you now, Baron."

I looked around her. There were nobles. Not just one or two. More than half of them are mediocre, but some are nobles that I cannot ignore either. All of them are looking at me with unkind eyes.

"I am sorry. Your Highness. It was too late yesterday, and it was too early today, so I am only now greeting Your Highness."

"Was that so. Did you have a good conversation with Aiden, my brother?"

I know that I met Aiden this morning. I wasn't surprised. The royal castle is Eileen's territory. Her hands and feet, eyes and ears are scattered everywhere.

"Yes. We had a good conversation. The Prince seems to be feeling very great sadness."

"I heard that he was looking for alcohol until last night...."

"He is relieving his sadness with alcohol. It is a pity that many people seem to misunderstand the Prince."

"......."

The atmosphere cooled down. Even I knew that what I said was ridiculous. Prince Aiden always had alcohol on his lips. If he felt sadness at the King's death, he should have stayed away from alcohol.

Even though I know that, I will not take alcohol away from Aiden. It was clear that if a drunkard couldn't drink, he would cause an accident. It's better for him to just stay quiet while drinking.

"Baron Prucus. I know that you are busy with work in the border area and work related to your family. Is it okay for you to come here like this?"

What she is talking about is the heir competition.

"Thank you for your concern, but it's okay. My father is thoroughly defending the eastern part of the Rapelli Kingdom. I think it is important to protect the kingdom, but I also think it is important to commemorate His Majesty the King. Your Highness. I will apologize on behalf of my father for not being able to attend the funeral."

"It's okay. Everyone here knows that the Border Count is risking his life to defend the East."

"Ah, it seems like we will be departing soon. I will step down now. But...."

I raised my head stiffly and approached the Princess. The nobles around her were shocked. They probably don't like the fact that someone like me is trying to be on par with the Princess. But Eileen didn't even twitch an eyebrow.

"Do you have something to say to me, Baron?"

"I have business with others, not with Your Highness."

My feet turned to a noble man next to me. He was a rat-like noble.

"Baron Barmarma."

"Wh, what is it. Baron Prucus."

"Why are you here? Is the item I ordered safe?"

"The item you ordered...."

"Haha. It seems your memory has deteriorated. I am talking about the item I ordered two years ago."

"What are you talking about all of a sudden? I am not meeting with the Baron today."

"Haa...."

I sighed. This bastard had no tact. I can't entrust him with important work.

"It is related to Duke Helbrit, who is no longer here. Do I really have to mention him?"

"O, oh. Oh...."

Baron Barmarma opened his mouth blankly and trembled.

He was one of Duke Helbrit's dogs. His main job was to smuggle war materials under Duke Helbrit's orders.

"Baron Barmarma. Why are you here? Let's have a conversation."

"A, I understand."

"You understand?"

"......I understand."

Baron Barmarma lowered his head with a desperate expression. He glanced at Princess Eileen. She didn't stop me. Instead, she looked at me as if she was observing me.

I called out the names of other nobles one by one.

"Baron Reitanol. Don't you have something to talk about with me?"

"Th, that's right."

"Viscount Barkas. I have something to talk about regarding the Viscount's daughter."

"Wh, what are you talking about? I don't have a daughter!"

Viscount Barkas was startled and came behind me. He had an illegitimate child. It was a daughter, and he liked her more than his son.

"Viscount Yasprin. What happened to the money you borrowed from me last time?"

"Th, the money is invested. There is still a lot of time left before I have to pay it back!"

"I am just asking to confirm. Are you going to keep staying there?"

Viscount Yasprin looked back and forth between me and Eileen, and eventually lowered his head and came behind me.

He chose the right line. He knows that following the competent Princess Eileen will be helpful in the future. He invested half of the money he borrowed from me in Princess Eileen, and the other half in military power. However, he was a debtor and I was a creditor. And in order to extend the contract period, he had to grovel before me. Otherwise, he would go bankrupt.

I turned around. Behind me, six nobles lowered their heads and dared not raise them.

The atmosphere had changed. The nobles who had looked down on me were now looking at me with wary eyes, as if they were looking at a great noble. Princess Eileen, who had lost her supporters, looked at me with indifferent eyes.

"Your Highness. I will really step down now. The weather is cold, so please take care of your health."

"......Yes. Please be careful too, Baron."

After finishing the greetings, I walked towards my carriage. Stinging gazes are fixed on my back. My faction was born here and now. Now, even if they are great nobles, they will not be able to ignore me carelessly.
