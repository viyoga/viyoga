He was a soldier carrying a bag. I reflexively checked his rank. One five-pointed star. He was a Major, same as me. There was also a mark indicating his affiliation. 6th Corps. Even if we were the same rank, he was higher up the ladder.

I felt something was off.

,'He came alone?'

Having worked in the military, I know. Embezzling classified military items isn't something you can do alone. Especially not something worth 200 million credits.

,'Does he know something's going to happen at the exchange and that's why he came alone?'

I resisted the urge to use detection magic. Using it now could ruin the deal itself. I needed to get the item first, at least. Even if it's fake, I can't ignore the possibility that it's real.

,'In situations like this, assume the enemy is hiding somewhere.'

I finished preparing to use Barrier and an instant.

"Can I see the item?"

I asked the soldier, keeping a certain distance.

"I need to confirm this first. Is the 200 million credits ready?"

I threw the bag into the air. Using telekinesis, I floated the bag in the air and unlocked it. The bag opened, revealing the cash inside.

The soldier nodded. He opened a magically treated bag. A potion bottle fell out, accompanied by cold air.

"This is Flash, a memory recovery magic potion. It's a magic potion that's at high risk of degradation if the temperature rises, so it needs to be kept at sub-zero temperatures."

The vial contained an orange liquid. It didn't seem to freeze even at sub-zero temperatures.

,'Is it real? It's hard to tell if it's real with the naked eye since I've never seen it before.'

Was it a real deal and not a trap after all?

,'Still, I'm going to kill Kevin. Kevin is more helpful dead than alive.'

My relationship with Kevin was already strained. Even if this deal goes smoothly, I have no intention of improving my relationship with Kevin. I already promised Ahn-che the position of Deputy Warden.

,'As soon as I get Flash, I'll kill the man in front of me and Kevin.'

I handed over the money bag with telekinesis and reached for Flash. Cold air seeped into my hand. Soon, my fingers touched the potion bottle.

Jiiiiiiiiing!

"......!"

A sensation of something seeping through my fingers.

,'Dark mana?'

As soon as I felt the anomaly, I grabbed Flash and backed away.

"I've been waiting for this! Die, Leon Schneider!!!"

Kevin shouted, aiming a pistol at my back and pulling the trigger.

[Barrier]

A barrier enveloped my body, protecting me from the bullet. A rank 5 Barrier I had prepared in advance.

Kwajik!

The barrier, which shouldn't have been scratched by a bullet, cracked.

,'He's disrupting the Barrier's spell. Is that a magic bullet with a Dispel effect?'

If it hadn't been a rank 5 Barrier, it would have definitely been pierced.

I lifted my right foot and stomped hard on the ground. A magic circle flashed on the floor and disappeared.

[Spike]

Spikes rose from the ground around me, targeting Kevin and the man simultaneously. The man dodged backward as if he had been waiting for it, and Kevin also quickly retreated.

,'He's got some basic skills, as befitting someone who rose to the position of Deputy Warden.'

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

The speed of my thoughts accelerated with Instant. I reconfirmed the enemy's position and calculated the spell. There was no reason to drag out the fight.

I'll finish them off with Thunder Bolt, my most confident rank 5 lightning magic attack.

The calculation of the spell was complete, and I moved my mana. I cast using 52 mana rods simultaneously.

"Cough!"

I coughed up blood. Seven mana rods backfired, causing internal injuries. I wiped the blood from my mouth and stood up. It would take about 3 minutes to readjust the 7 mana rods. In other words, I had to re-weave the Thunder Bolt spell I had painstakingly created.

"It's already surprising that an alchemist was trying to use elemental attack magic... and he suppressed the mana backflow in less than 10 seconds.... His mana manipulation alone doesn't explain it. Just how high is his mana affinity?"

Even I don't know exactly how high my mana affinity is. It feels like it's constantly growing. And even if I did know, I had no intention of telling him.

"What kind of curse did you put on the potion?"

"So you noticed that too. But you're wrong. I've been casting the curse since a week ago. You must have been feeling unwell. Isn't that right?"

A curse.

It seemed that my recent poor condition was due to his curse. But that wasn't the shocking part.

"...I didn't realize I was cursed?"

Curses are inherently subtle. Depending on the type, the target often doesn't notice the curse until they die. In some ways, it's the perfect assassination technique.

"I admit you're an excellent magician. But that's all. There are plenty of magicians like you. Don't be arrogant."

It's clear that he's a black magician. He's openly exuding dark mana, so there's no way I wouldn't know. The important thing is which type.

"What's your name?"

"Why do you need to know my name?"

"I was impressed that you cast a curse without me noticing. I'll acknowledge you too. What's your name?"

"Niklas Kuster."

"Okay, Niklas. What kind of curse did you cast? Besides the mana backflow curse. I've already reversed that. What I'm curious about is this curse that's still causing fatigue. Thanks to you, I've been tired for the past week."

I was really curious about the curse. I need to know so I don't get hit by it again next time.

,'Curses need a medium. He must have used my hair. Kevin must have gotten my hair and given it to him.'

What I was curious about was the type of curse.

"Ha. Your bluffing is impressive. Tired? Do you think I don't know that your mind is already worn down?"

"What are you talking about? Did you cast a mental curse on me too?"

"Nonsense. That place is a prison. It was overflowing with hatred and resentment towards you. Countless vengeful spirits must have appeared in your dreams to torment you. I know better than anyone that your insides are rotting away."

Vengeful spirits.

I realized.

"You're a necromancer. You used vengeful spirits to curse me. But I've never had a nightmare."

"Are you going to deny it to the end? I wonder if you can still say that after seeing the vengeful spirits clinging to your body."

Black mana spread out from Niklas's body.

I narrowed my eyes. Things that shouldn't be visible began to appear.

Souls that couldn't find peace, wraiths. The vengeful spirits, in gruesome forms that were close to human yet not human, all stared at me.

"These are the ones who resent you. Those who were killed by you and couldn't find peace because of their deep resentment, wandering the afterlife. They are the ones who want your death and willingly offered their souls to me!"

"......Indeed. There are quite a few familiar faces."

A young child and a woman were persistently clinging to my body. I had seen those faces before. I can't forget them even if I want to. It's only been a month since I killed them. The woman and child were the ghosts of Matthias's family. In addition, the prisoners I had executed were also hating me.

"Now."

Niklas's voice rang out. The wraiths reacted in unison.

"Wraiths, unleash your resentment! Make the sinner pay the price!"

Hundreds of wraiths rushed at me. I calmly deployed a Barrier. It was useless. The incorporeal beings pierced through the Barrier and clung to my body.

"......."

My body is heavy.

That was all.

Niklas was flustered. He pointed his finger, sweating.

"W-Why can't the wraiths enter your body?!"

"Why are you asking me that? You're the expert."

"...You, don't tell me. You don't have any guilt... or even the slightest interest in those you killed?"

"They're already dead. Why should I care about them?"

"Among those you killed, there are those who committed no crime!"

"I don't kill people without a reason."

"What? Can't you see the wraiths here?! These are the proof of your sins! Among them are many who were unjustly killed by you! Look at that man! That man was mistaken for a Cabrahim gang member and killed by you!"

The battle with the Cabrahim gang. I remember it. I personally stepped in and killed the remnants because they were annoying. I also found and killed their families.

"Ah, I remember. I knew that man wasn't a gang member."

"So you're finally admitting it. That man is the innocent person you killed for no reason!"

"There was a reason for killing him."

"I'm curious to hear what kind of bullshit you're going to spew. What's the reason?"

"He was an eyesore."

He frowned at my answer. He looked at me as if he didn't understand.

"...You killed an innocent person for such a trivial reason?"

"An ant is passing by at my feet. I stepped on it because I didn't like the way it was moving. It's the same thing. I killed him because he was annoying. Ah, of course, I killed him because I was sure there would be no repercussions. Officially, he's just an unfortunate citizen who died in the process of suppressing the gang. But what does it matter?"

"...I see now that you're even more of a piece of shit than I imagined."

"It's refreshing to hear that from a necromancer."

I took out a rank 3 Dark Hole from my pocket. An organ extracted from the body of a black magician. I use all the stored dark mana to cast a spell.

[Dark Flame]

Black flames rose and clung to the wraiths.

"Kkyaaaaaaaaak!"

The wraiths screamed and fell off my body. More than half of them were annihilated in the process.

,'As expected, they're vulnerable to the same dark magic.'

Niklas hurriedly gathered the wraiths near him. He looked at me with shocked eyes.

"Alchemy, elemental magic, and now even black magic...! How are you using all of them?!"

"I just practiced a little and it worked. Alchemy and black magic are both in the category of magic, after all."

"......"

Niklas frowned.

He formed hand seals with both hands. Each time he formed a seal, the surrounding wraiths' forms distorted.

Necromancy I've never seen before.

I was curious, but this wasn't a situation where I could afford to leisurely admire it.

Pajijik!

It was the moment I was about to cast lightning magic.

Taaang!

As soon as I heard the gunshot from afar, I reflexively used Instant. A bullet was flying towards me. The point of impact was my forehead. The bullet was too big to ignore, even with the Barrier. An anti-tank round. And it's a magic bullet too. I threw my body to the side to dodge the sniper shot.
