Three weeks have passed since I joined the company.

During that time, I haven't done much office work except when writing reports. The rest of the time, I'm either resting or playing games in the office.

It might seem like a scene that employees who specialize in office work would envy, but in reality, they don't envy it at all. All they do is safely tap away at keyboards in the office, but field workers like me have to risk our lives fighting vampires every time there's a job.

If you make even a small mistake during combat, you'll be taken down by a vampire.

That's why field workers actually envy office workers more. Of course, field workers get paid more.

,'It's been three weeks since I joined. In those three weeks, four field workers have died.'

The dead were members of the combat unit. They were moving in a group of four when they were attacked by vampires in broad daylight and died.

,'It's only natural that vampires retaliate against the company. Those bastards have brains and feelings too.'

The company has been around for about 70 years. That means the history of blood it has built with vampires is long. It was only natural that vampires hated the company.

,'It's strange that we're just getting beaten. Basically, vampires are higher up on the food chain than humans.'

It's just that humans are overwhelming them now with civilization and numbers. If the number of vampires was more than half of the human population, the race that ruled the world would be vampires, not humans.

I headed to the company's first-floor cafe. Choi Sangwoo and Ji Hyunseong, who could be considered my colleagues, had already taken their seats.

"Seong Yujin-ssi! Over here!"

Ji Hyunseong waves his hand at me. I approach their table, pull out a chair, and sit down. The kiwi juice I had mentioned beforehand was already on the table. As soon as I sat down, I slurped down the kiwi juice and said.

"Delicious. They must use good kiwis here."

"Can't you even say hello before drinking juice?"

Ji Hyunseong scolded me. Coffee was placed in front of them. I didn't like coffee. Coffee made by baristas with mediocre skills is not worth drinking.

"How have you been?"

The reason I met them was twofold. One was because I had nothing to do. The other was to get information.

The original work doesn't mention specific times or dates. So, I have no choice but to keep an eye on the actions of the main and supporting characters and guess the events of the original work.

"I've been doing well. Surprisingly, Team Leader No is being very nice to me. Well, Sangwoo-ssi got into an accident and received a standby order, though."

"......"

Choi Sangwoo frowns. He exudes an atmosphere of displeasure.

Choi Sangwoo found traces of a Vampire Lord during his work and went berserk. He ignored the orders of his senior, Team Leader Choi Seonyeong, and moved on his own, almost dying. He would have died if it weren't for Choi Seonyeong. Choi Seonyeong reported the incident to the head of the Special Division, and Choi Sangwoo received a standby order.

"Yujin-ssi. How is it under Team Leader Ju Seohyeon? Is it warm?"

"I don't know if it's warm, but the work itself is easy. I've been on about 15 missions, but Team Leader Ju Seohyeon handled most of them."

Before I could do anything, Ju Seohyeon rushes towards the vampire first. Using instantaneous acceleration, Ju Seohyeon's sword couldn't be stopped even by a vampire. With a quick slash, the vampire was dead.

"Wow. So, you've been having a sweet time. But 15 times? In the last three weeks? We haven't even been out 10 times?"

"Don't you have to rest compulsorily the day after you have work?"

Ji Hyunseong was disgusted, and Choi Sangwoo asked with sparkling eyes.

"I don't know. I didn't feel the need to rest because I wasn't that tired. Team Leader Ju Seohyeon is the same."

"I'm jealous. I wish I could get that much work too...."

"Well, Team Leader Ju Seohyeon is a Jaeger, so I feel like the company is giving her a lot of work."

There was a reason why Ju Seohyeon received tremendous support from the company. She was handling an insane amount of work compared to other employees without complaint. Of course, she is compensated for her performance. I was just tagging along with Ju Seohyeon, but the performance bonus on my bank account already exceeded 100 million won.

"Can't you change partners?"

Choi Sangwoo asked. Me and Ji Hyunseong looked at him with surprised eyes.

"Do you not like Team Leader Choi Seonyeong? Aren't you guys the same werewolf hybrid?"

"It's not that I don't like her, but I should say that we don't match. I like her fighting style. She attacks vampires without mercy. But...."

Choi Sangwoo stopped talking. He seems to be thinking about something carefully. Ji Hyunseong tilted his head, not understanding Choi Sangwoo. I know through the original work that Choi Seonyeong is wary of Choi Sangwoo.

,'Choi Seonyeong has been burned by the same werewolf before. So, she didn't really like Choi Sangwoo at first either.'

That attitude must be unsatisfactory for Choi Sangwoo. It's like she doesn't trust him as a colleague.

,'It's a problem that will be solved over time.'

I continued the conversation. They mostly talked about what they had experienced during their work. In particular, Ji Hyunseong made a fuss, saying that he had been in several near-death situations. When the conversation was somewhat ripe, I asked Choi Sangwoo as if passing by.

"What do you mean you found traces of the Vampire Lord? Smell? Or did he appear in person?"

"It's a smell. The smell of that bastard... who killed my family."

"Tell me more about that bastard."

Choi Sangwoo frowned.

"...You seem to be very curious."

"Don't get the wrong idea. I'm not interested in your personal affairs. You're not the only one who wants to kill the Vampire Lord. Just like you're looking for the enemy who killed your family, I'm also looking for the enemy who killed my family."

"...The bastard who killed your family is a Vampire Lord?"

"I don't know. I didn't see it myself. Even if I ask the police, they won't tell me."

"Did you ask the Intelligence Department? If it's a vampire case, it's more reliable to ask the Intelligence Department than the police."

Choi Sangwoo was serious. It seemed to be because of the sense of kinship that his family was killed by a vampire. Of course, I had no desire for revenge on my family. A family I don't even know was killed before I came to this world. It's no different from a stranger I don't know.

"Of course, I asked the Intelligence Department too. But they didn't know much. I don't know if they really don't know, or if they don't even think about finding them. I don't have a good relationship with the Intelligence Department."

The fact that I cursed Kwak Suhyeok at the restaurant last time became cursing the entire Intelligence Department. My reputation has hit rock bottom. Thanks to Ju Seohyeon, there were no guys who openly said anything to me, but they cursed me with their eyes every time I passed by.

"Ah, that rumor. I heard about it too. You did that to Section Chief Kwak Suhyeok, who is a big shot in the Intelligence Department? You seemed fine when you were in training camp... Now that I see it, you're crazier than Choi Sangwoo."

Ji Hyunseong shook his head. Choi Sangwoo, who had been silent, frowned as if dissatisfied, glared at Ji Hyunseong for a moment, sighed, and asked me.

"It's hard to ask this question... But what was the condition of your parents' bodies?"

"...According to the police, their abdomens were dug up. Their intestines were scattered."

"It's not a Vampire Lord. That bastard doesn't kill humans like that. When he plays with humans, he cuts off their limbs and watches them until they die, and when he sucks blood, he only drinks half and throws away the rest of the body."

"...Is that so?"

"Where did your parents get attacked? Were they attacked at home?"

"They were attacked at home. A vampire broke in and dug up my parents' abdomens. There were no traces of bloodsucking."

"What were your parents' jobs?"

"Is the job important?"

"It's important. Vampires usually attack people walking on the street. It's rare for them to break into a house directly."

"My father was a department head at a large company, and my mother was a housewife."

"...A department head at a large company. You can say he has social status. In this case, it's close to planned murder."

"I heard that many high-ranking officials have been trading with vampires recently. Is it because of that?"

"Maybe. Your parents may have rejected the vampire's offer. If so, it wouldn't have been easy to reject in that situation, so they must have been great people."

"......"

"Seong Yujin-ssi. It's not the Vampire Lord's doing. But there's no reason for a Common-class to attack the house of a department head at a large company. It's not enough to make a deal. I think it's probably a Noble-class. There are less than 30 Noble-class vampires in Seoul, so if you investigate them one by one, you'll be able to find the culprit. Especially if you investigate the bastards who like digging up...."

"Thank you. I'll look into it."

I was a little embarrassed because he spoke more seriously than I thought.

,'If he's coming out this seriously, I can't just not investigate at all.'

To maintain a good relationship with Choi Sangwoo, I have to pretend to be burning with revenge.
