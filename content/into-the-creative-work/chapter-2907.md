The George Mercenary Corps didn't fall into chaos despite the sudden appearance and surprise attack of the enemy.

They were a mercenary corps that advanced on battlefields. Also, the majority were soldiers from the military. But even if they didn't fall into chaos, they couldn't help but be surprised and embarrassed.

,Is that a 7th Circle magic, Thunder Break... right?

,That power is only 7th Circle? There's no way a barrier would break so easily with a 7th Circle magic! I don't know what trick he used, but the power is at least 8th Circle!

,Has the enemy's identity been identified? One of them is definitely a witch, judging by the fact that she's riding a broom.

,We don't know the witch's identity, but we've identified the male wizard! The president of LK Corporation! Eugene Meyer!

,That combat mage? Why is he attacking us?!

Only the commander and vice-commander knew that the true purpose of the George Mercenary Corps was LK Corporation's superconductor recipe. The others in the mercenary corps knew nothing.

,We've already been attacked. Hundreds of members have just died from magic! This is war! Attack!

The mercenaries pointed their guns upwards and pulled the triggers. They activated the anti-aircraft system and even fired missiles, projecting their firepower.

The target was a 7th Circle superhuman. They had to be seen as combat weapons, not ordinary humans.

The soaring bullets and missiles stopped. As if time had stopped, they couldn't reach the target and stopped. It wasn't that the missile engines had stopped. They were still spewing flames. They were just blocked by something and couldn't move forward.

,...Is it a barrier? No, if it were a barrier, it would have bounced off or exploded. It's being held by an invisible force!

The soldiers kept firing bullets and missiles. But they still couldn't move forward, as if blocked by something.

,Could it be....

The officer felt uneasy, recalling the pillar of lightning that had just struck the base.

As far as he knew, Eugene Meyer was the president of LK Corporation and a 7th Circle mage belonging to the Raytheon School. The Raytheon School doesn't just deal with lightning. To be exact, it could be said to be a school that deals with magic related to electromagnetic force.

,Damn it! That's a magnetic field! Stop attacking!! Focus on defense immediately! Activate the emergency shield! Mages, use barriers at least!

[Magnetic Field]

The bullets and metal were caught in the invisible magnetic field like they were caught in a spider web.

Eugene twisted his right wrist. The bullets and missiles rotated 180 degrees, and their ends pointed towards the ground.

,I'll return it with a bonus.

The mage's voice, filled with mana, echoed to the ground.

[Magnetic Rail]

Pajik.

Sparks flew.

An invisible magnetic rail was formed, and bullets and missiles were loaded.

,Throw bombs to intercept!

The officer made the best judgment he could. But there wasn't enough time for the soldiers to faithfully follow that order.

The bullets and missiles were torn apart.

The defense barrier that protected the base had already been torn apart by the Thunder Break that had fallen earlier. There was no way to stop that attack.

The soldiers were pierced by bullets accelerated by the magnetic field, and their screams were buried in the explosion of the missiles.

The officer was able to survive thanks to deploying a barrier with an artifact, but the base that was revealed after the attack was devastating.

,That goddamn bastard... he guided the missiles to intensively strike important weapons in the meantime!

Still, it wasn't a total annihilation.

Hundreds more died in this attack, but soldiers remained.

,Does the George Mercenary Corps, which has advanced in war, seem like it will collapse with just two magic spells...! Prepare for the anti-magic tactics!

,No. Clean up first.

Commander George and Vice-Commander Zell walked out of the residence behind the officer's back.

,Commander-nim!

,Good work, adjutant. Clean up the surviving members as much as possible and prepare to retreat.

,Retreat! Are you saying we're going to be defeated by just one wizard?!

,It's not a defeat. We're achieving our purpose and returning.

,...Purpose?

,I'll tell you the details later. After capturing that wizard who made the first move, I don't know how he found out. I'll take care of Eugene Meyer. Zell, you take care of that witch.

The witch on the broom gestured towards the ground. That light gesture became magic. Rain of fire began to fall from the sky.

,It seems like there's a traitor among the higher-ups in Neo London.... I don't like it.

Zell took out a weapon from his back. It was a metal crossbow.

,Let's think positively. We can ask the VVIP to take responsibility for this.

George drew his sword from his waist and activated Solid.

If Astral is the spirit of a mage, Solid is the spirit of a warrior. Solid expands not to the outside of the body, but to the inside, amplifying physical abilities using mana as fuel.

George laughed. This feeling of being internally altered and evolved was always the best.

George's body and mind naturally accelerated. When he bent his knees and straightened them, he was already in the sky.

He formed mana under his feet and stepped on it, standing in the sky. It was a minor skill that any 7th Circle warrior could do.

George pointed the tip of his sword at Eugene.

,Wizard. How did you know and come here? It hasn't been many days since we came here. Which bastard gave you the information?

,It was a guess. The guys who would covet the superconductor the most in this world would be the Diroids. I expected them to use any means possible.

,Our mercenary corps has nothing to do with the Diroids.

,I'm sure you don't.

Eugene said indifferently. That kind of thing didn't matter anymore now. Either way, one of them would die tonight.

The space next to him cracked open, and a meteor sword was launched. George avoided it without difficulty. He was a superhuman who could avoid bullets raining down on the battlefield. There was no reason why he couldn't avoid a meteor sword slower than a bullet.

He narrowed the distance while avoiding the enemy's attack. George's blade flashed in the moonlight, aiming for Eugene's upper body.

[Leap]

Eugene moved to George's rear with space movement. George quickly turned around. Magic was manifested from the ring on Eugene's finger.

[Time Lock.]

George's body and mind, hit by Time Lock, stopped. Eugene used an instant to extend the time by 1 second.

[Using Instants. Remaining stacks: 10]

Two magic circles appeared on either side of George.

[Using Instants. Remaining stacks: 9]

This time above and below George's head.

[Using Instants. Remaining stacks: 8]

In front of and behind George.

,What?!

After 3 seconds, the Time Lock was released, and George was horrified to see the magic circles that occupied his six directions.

[Mana Explosion]

Kwang!

The six magic circles, condensed with mana, exploded simultaneously, causing a physical impact.

George endured it. In that short time when the magic circles exploded, he amplified Solid to the limit, focusing on physical defense. In addition, thanks to the combat suit he was wearing absorbing the damage, he was able to withstand it without suffering a fatal wound.

,The power is less than I thought. It's only this much compared to the effort of causing it in 6 directions. I should discard Mana Explosion.

,...You. Are you experimenting on me?

,There are limitations to training data. Isn't it right to extract actual combat data when you can?

,Does combat look like a joke to you?!

,No. I'm more serious than anyone.

Eugene raised his right arm above his shoulder.

[Using Instants. Remaining stacks: 7]

Pajijijik! Blue lightning wrapped around his arm, spinning around it like its center. It was intensifying the electric shock with the mysterious power of Manroe (卍雷).

Eugene lowered his arm. The lightning amplified by the blade of wind became a huge slash.

[Lightning Blade]

,......!!

George's eyes widened. The slash of wind and lightning created by the mage. It was too fast to avoid, so he tried to block it with his sword.

He was pushed back by the force.

George crashed to the ground. Even if he was a superhuman, his head rang from falling from this height. He spat out blood and looked at the sky.

Eugene's attack didn't stop. He was making a bow of lightning with one hand and pulling the string with the other. The lightning arrow on the string was rotating and compressing, aiming precisely at George.

It was definitely not an ordinary Lightning Arrow. That being said, the lightning arrow was more of a line than an arrow.

,...What Circle magic was that slash just now?

,It's a 6th Circle arcane magic.

,...Only 6th Circle? It felt like I was being hit head-on by a 7th Circle magic? What did you do to the magic?

,I have no reason to explain it to you.

,Dangerous.

George felt an instinctive sense of crisis. The moment he was hit directly by that arrow, not even his bones would be left.

Eugene released the string.

[Lightning Bow]

A blue line was fired at supersonic speed.

Unique Skill. Dog of the Battlefield.

Just before the blue line touched George's body, George's body split into 4 pieces, avoiding the arrow. The arrow that had aimed for his heart pierced the ground and exploded.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

A lightning storm arose, sweeping through the surrounding area.

,Kkeuaaaaaaak!

A mercenary corps member who was unlucky enough to be nearby was caught up in it and died.

The 4 divided chunks each became human forms in places where the lightning storm didn't reach. The 4 Georges each held different weapons. Sword, spear, rifle, shield.

,Is it a clone? An interesting Unique Skill.

The 4 Georges opened their mouths at the same time. The body was divided into 4, but his soul and mind were one.

,I can't maintain it for long, but each and every one is 7th Circle. You can never beat me alone.

,You can't maintain it for long. Good. I'll play along. I won't have sex for 24 hours.

,What the hell are you talking about all of a sudden?

[De Geas]

[Fourth Constraint. Do not have sex for 24 hours.]

Eugene's pressure rose in an instant.

,......!!

The 4 Georges stepped back in unison. An overwhelming pressure that was too intense to be called a 7th Circle mage.

,The mana that bastard has has increased tremendously...! Could that be that bastard's Unique Skill?!

Not having sex? It was a deceptive word to draw out carelessness. The truth was probably something else.

When George raised his vigilance, Eugene used magic.

[Purple Electricity(Ziden)]

Purple lightning bloomed from Eugene's fingertips.
