The next day.

I didn't get to headquarters until after lunch. Given my position, no one dared rush me to work.

When I entered the temporary office, Oh Jeonil was sitting at his desk, organizing documents. He looked perfectly fine now, without a single scar, even though he was covered in blood last night.

,You've arrived, I see. After turning Korea upside down, you leisurely come to work in the afternoon... I envy that indifference and power."

,If you're so jealous, you can be the boss too. But what's all this trash piled up on my desk?"

,Those aren't trash, but transfer requests from other Anti-Magic Unit members."

,They want to come to the 8th Unit? Why all of a sudden?"

,You really don't know, do you? The atmosphere within the Order Country is strange, all thanks to you, Daejang-nim."

,So, why?"

,Because you killed Sage of Lemegeton. Lemegeton is the worst Harrier organization in history, having overthrown European countries. Half of the members within headquarters fear Lemegeton's retaliation, and the other half revere you, Daejang-nim."

,So that's why the transfer requests have increased."

,It's not just an internal matter within the Order Country. The death of Sage has been reported in the media. The citizens are currently anxious. Turning Lemegeton into an enemy means... Korea could be in danger. Some media outlets are openly criticizing you, Daejang-nim."

,Even if I hadn't killed that bastard, Lemegeton was already targeting Korea. The drug factory is proof of that."

,That's true, but... the fear that the name Lemegeton brings is too great. Those with assets among the citizens are preparing to immigrate to the United States or Japan. If you go online right now, most of the talk is about Lemegeton."

,I don't care as long as they don't bother me."

,Even if the media is being extreme, it won't lead to anything troublesome. The Order Country is a national institution, after all. There will be pressure on the media. And you, Daejang-nim, haven't done anything wrong."

,Of course not."

I killed a few gang members who were picking a fight in the early morning and dumped them in the Pacific Ocean... but since it was a perfect crime with no bodies found, there was no need to worry about being caught. Even if I were suspected of a crime, I could just deny it and ask for proof.

I checked the documents.

,The 8th Unit will be small and elite. Too many people are noisy and troublesome to manage."

After checking the documents, I threw them all away. The members who submitted transfer requests were extras whose names and faces I had never seen. I would have accepted the transfer if they were at least supporting characters.

,Are you not accepting any more members? It might be good to have even an office worker."

,I don't accept just anyone."

,Understood, Daejang-nim. The 3rd Unit Commander has formally requested a duel with you, Daejang-nim."

,Hasn't the 3rd Unit Commander learned his lesson from the no-show last time?"

,Daejang-nim, you must accept this time. As you killed Sage, the attention is focused on you, Daejang-nim. Everyone in the unit wants to confirm your skills. In other words, it's not just a simple duel, but a place to prove the 8th Unit."

,If I refuse, rumors will spread that the 8th Unit is cowardly. Fine. I'll step on that brat from the 3rd Unit once."

It was unlikely, but if I lost the duel, I could lose my independent operation rights. Not by the Director, but by the protests of other bastards.

,The 6th Unit seems to be interested in your recovery potions, Daejang-nim."

,The 6th Unit's recovery magic should be better than recovery potions."

,Due to its nature, the 6th Unit has a small number of personnel, and most are in the rear due to their high importance. If the members carry recovery potions, the burden on the 6th Unit will be reduced, and the overall combat effectiveness of the members will increase."

,Ah, I see. Not a bad plan."

Recovery potion.

I can easily obtain them in reality. I have enough money, and Ha Seunghee, a third-generation chaebol from Sejin Group, which is involved in Hunter-related businesses, is my acquaintance.

,Even if the top-tier ones costing 1 billion won each are difficult, I can easily obtain the high-tier ones. I already have quite a few."

Recovery potions were also easily obtainable in the [Baekhwan] and [God's Atlantis] worlds. You could say they are relatively common items in fictional worlds.

,I should provide potions to the Order Country."

Potions can be obtained with money. Even though it's a waste, if the level of the Order Country rises with just one potion, my job will become easier as a result.

,I'll provide the potions. But it's not free. Make that clear."

,Since the effect is certain, you should be able to get several million won per potion."

,Just several million won?"

,...Since the 6th Unit exists, the recovery potions won't go up to tens of millions of won."

,What about mana potions? Potions that restore magic power."

,Those would go for tens of millions of won."

,You negotiate that."

,Me? I'm not good at negotiations. You, Daejang-nim, should step up to get more profit."

,If I were going to make a profit, I wouldn't have even thought about providing them to the Order Country. If I sold them to the rich, I could get at least 100 million won or more. Right?"

,Understood. Then, rather than money, I'll try to secure as many rights as possible for the 8th Unit. The 6th Unit will become our ally."

It is said that politics arise when people gather.

The Order Country was no exception.

The 6th Unit is in charge of the recovery of injured members, so even though the number of members is small, their voice is strong.

,Maintaining a good relationship with the 6th Unit will prevent political isolation."

Roughly translated, it means less troublesome work.

,Daejang-nim, a mission order has been issued from the headquarters unit. The order is to check out a suspicious abandoned building. There are rumors that a Harrier is hiding in this building."

,......"

I have independent operation rights, but I can't just refuse missions from headquarters. The 8th Unit is, after all, an Anti-Magic Unit under the Order Country.

,If you don't like it, I'll refuse. We can use Lemegeton as an excuse. If not, I'll go alone. There's no need for you, Daejang-nim, to move personally for such a thing."

,No, I'll go myself. You stay and train."

If it's an abandoned building, it must be that place from the original work. It's time to receive a new member.

• • •

The training grounds of the 3rd Anti-Magic Unit of the Order Country.

There, Director of the Order Country, Park Hyunwoong, and the commanders of each unit gathered to watch the training grounds. Of course, they are not the only ones watching the training grounds. The members of each unit are also watching from outside the barrier of the training grounds.

In the center of the training grounds, a man and a woman are looking at each other.

Yoo Minha, the commander of the 3rd Unit, whose growth stopped at the age of 8 due to a magic surge.

Seong Yujin, the commander of the 8th Unit, who recently killed Sage, an executive of Lemegeton, and became the center of attention.

This duel was also a place for Seong Yujin to prove his strength. Even though he killed Sage, few had directly confirmed his power. If Seong Yujin failed to prove his strength as the commander of the 8th Unit, the dissatisfaction of other members would surely erupt. Seong Yujin appeared too much like a parachute.

,1st Unit Commander. I didn't expect you to come either."

Park Hyunwoong said to the man next to him. Hwang Seungmin, the commander of the 1st Unit, a man in his 30s wearing a white suit, smiled and said.

,Everyone came except for the 5th and 7th Units, who have missions. It's an opportunity to see the skills of the famous 8th Unit Commander, so how could I miss it? I made time to come and see it no matter what."

In addition to them, other commanders gathered. Park Hyunwoong could not speak to all of them. Because the duel had already begun.

The start was the charge of Yoo Minha, the commander of the 3rd Unit. She increased her speed with time magic and approached in an instant.

But Seong Yujin teleported into the air and waved his hand at Yoo Minha. A large amount of fire rose from the floor of the training ground and blazed.

Yoo Minha punched the floor, detonating a shockwave to extinguish the flames.

,I didn't know you could do space teleportation magic too. It's so amazing to see it in person. I wonder how far your magical talent goes..."

Lee Sukja, the commander of the 6th Unit, received Park Hyunwoong's admiration. The 60-year-old woman, wearing a doctor's gown over her shoulders, looked at Seong Yujin with interest.

,I heard he makes potions with magic. He's a versatile friend. Especially the mana potions are so precious that I'm at a loss as to how to handle them."

,Let's worry about that later."

In the center of the training grounds, a close combat between two magicians began. Surprisingly, Seong Yujin allowed close combat without avoiding it.

Park Hyunwoong judged that Seong Yujin was arrogant. Yoo Minha, the commander of the 3rd Unit, was the strongest in close combat within the Order Country.

Yoo Minha's overwhelming amount of magic power raises her physical abilities by several levels, and a time distortion field spreads around her 1m, slowing down the enemy's attacks and speeding up her own abilities.

But his prediction was wrong. Seong Yujin overwhelmed Yoo Minha in close combat. He didn't use magic. He was simply overwhelming her with martial arts skills.

,Haha. That's my brother! I felt it when I faced Sage, but... his combat sense itself is unbelievably high. It's as if he's experienced thousands of battles."

Park Son, the commander of the 4th Unit, who is treated as an oddball within the Order Country, laughed and burst into praise.

Kwaang!

Seong Yujin's fist struck Yoo Minha's torso. Yoo Minha spat out blood and flew backward. She struggled to get up after falling to the floor.

,It's not just a punch. Did he infuse magic power to shake the inside? It seems like a technique called Bajiquan from Chinese martial arts."

As the distance widened, Seong Yujin flicked his fingers and used magic.

Dozens of wind blades shredded Yoo Minha's body. Because it was strengthened with magic power, she wasn't cut so badly that bones were visible... but small scratches gathered and blood dripped.

,Aaaaaaaah!"

Yoo Minha shouted and expanded the time distortion field to the entire training ground. But it was already too late. Her surroundings were already filled with water like a swimming pool.

Trapped in the water, she glared at Seong Yujin, who was standing on the water, and reached out her hand.

,Stop. Let's end it here. The winner is the 8th Unit Commander."

The 2nd Unit Commander pushed up his glasses with his middle finger, looking somewhat dissatisfied.

,Director-nim, if the 3rd Unit Commander had used Ars Magna, the result would have changed."

,Using Ars Magna means killing each other. And since I had already forbidden Ars Magna to both of them in advance, it's right to end it here."

,...If that's the case, then it's right to end it here. I'll be on my way. In my personal opinion, the 8th Unit Commander is too cruel and dangerous. If the Anti-Magic Unit collapses from within... the 8th Unit Commander will be at the center of it."

The 2nd Unit Commander left. Park Hyunwoong stroked his chin with a thoughtful expression.
