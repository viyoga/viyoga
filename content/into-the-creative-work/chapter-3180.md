Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Thunder Spear (卍雷槍).

I hurl the Storm Breaker, wreathed in spinning lightning.

The Storm Breaker shoots out in a dead straight line toward the enemy commander, Hell Ogre Count.

Every enemy soldier in its path was bisected and killed without exception. The lightning itself, rotating around the Storm Breaker, carried physical force. The Myriad Thunder Spear, imbued with physical force, was both a drill and a blender.

The Myriad Thunder Spear flew across the battlefield, kicking up a spray of blood, and Hell Ogre Count caught it with both hands.

Blood streamed down both his arms, but the Storm Breaker lost its force and was caught in his hands. Immediately after, the lightning compressed within the Myriad Thunder Spear exploded.

KWAAAAJJJJJIJIJIJIK!

Thousands of bolts of lightning burst out simultaneously, hammering Hell Ogre Count.

"KRAAAAAAAGH!"

Hell Ogre Count roared. It wasn't a scream. It was a kind of battle cry. Immediately after, the lightning of the Myriad Thunder Spear began to fade.

「You have heard the roar of the Hell Ogre.」

「Instinctive fear arises.」

「You do not feel fear.」

The effect of that roar reached the allied knights and soldiers. Veterans of the battlefield flinched.

I swept my gaze over them once, then spoke.

"I trust there is no coward here who would run with the enemy before his eyes."

The allies' eyes turned to me, standing at the very front. They gritted their teeth in silence.

"For the Heavenly Demon!!!"

The desert warriors bellowed to overcome the creeping terror.

"For Valdert!"

"For His Imperial Majesty!"

"For humanity!!"

Each shouted their own cause. Not a single one shouted for me. They were borrowed soldiers, so it was only natural. No — the Heavenly Demon the desert warriors spoke of was me, but... they don't even know that I am the Heavenly Demon.

"Charge."

I gave the order to Alexandra, who was at my side. Alexandra blew the war horn.

Bwooooooooooooh.

The desert warriors ran, and the knights' warhorses pounded the earth. The soldiers advanced slowly toward the enemy line, maintaining formation. Demons cast magic and demonic beasts lunged at the nearest enemies.

I looked straight ahead. Hell Ogre Count, with his five heads, hurled the Storm Breaker he'd caught back at me.

The Storm Breaker comes flying, shattering the air.

'Reverse Summon.'

[The item cannot be reverse-summoned in its current state.]

It must be because Hell Ogre Count's power is imbued in the Storm Breaker flying at me. Of course, Hell Ogre Count hasn't taken control of the Storm Breaker. That's nothing more than him brutishly slamming his strength into it and hurling it.

'Reverse summon won't work in that state. The easy path is blocked.'

I tried to change the form of the Storm Breaker connected to me. It won't take.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido and struck the incoming Storm Breaker from below upward. KAAAAAANG! Precisely speaking, I parried it. Stopping it was impossible — I only changed its direction so it flew toward the sky.

Just from that, the palm of my right hand gripping the sword split open. And I hadn't even taken it head-on.

'Hell Ogre Count's strength far surpasses my own muscle power.'

At the same time, I intuited it.

If I can't do something about Hell Ogre Count, this war will be hard. And if I hadn't joined the fight, we might have lost this war. Even if we won, the casualties would have been immense.

-If you promise to set me free, I'll tear off every one of that Hell Ogre's heads and give them to you. You will become a Hell Duke without any danger or loss.

Agares, sealed within Solomon's Ring, whispers as if tempting me.

"Shut up. You think I can't kill that one lump of meat?"

「The Master of the Heavens wishes for you to kill the monster with lightning.」

「The King of the Demon Heavens enjoys war.」

「The Lord of Mischief watches your martial prowess with keen interest.」

「The Guide of Warriors is silent.」

「The Black Serpent watches the war of hell.」

「Countless Divine Thrones observe your war.」

The Divine Thrones, silent until now, came swarming in. Combat. And war is a spectacle even the Divine Thrones can't resist. Several Divine Thrones even sent me missions directly.

I ignored all the notification windows popping up and ran toward Hell Ogre Count. Midway, I swung my sword through the air.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Flash (雷閃).

A slash mixed with lightning flew at Hell Ogre Count.

Hell Ogre Count didn't dodge — he stepped forward proudly, head-on. The slash struck his body, but all it did was scratch his thick hide.

'As expected, ordinary slashes don't work at all.'

「Using the authority of the demon sealed within Solomon's Ring.」

「Authority: Word of Command.」

"Self-harm."

An authority aimed solely at Hell Ogre Count.

Mana drains out of me in a rush, activating as the Word of Command. Formless chains of language coil around Hell Ogre Count's body and soul.

Hell Ogre Count raised his fist to smash his own head — and in that instant, the chains of language, the Word of Command, shattered.

-The Word of Command is not an omnipotent power. You use it so brutishly.

I couldn't retort to Agares's mockery. I was already within reach of Hell Ogre Count's hand. Running — within sword's reach of him.

"Human, you mean to fight me head-on? How insolent!"

"Five heads and only one mouth talking. Is there just one brain?"

"My brothers sold their souls for me!"

Hell Ogre Count stomped the ground. Just that sent a shockwave rippling outward, causing a small earthquake. The entire battlefield shook.

"Your brothers didn't sell themselves for you — you sold your brothers for yourself."

"No!"

Hell Ogre Count thrust out his fist in fury.

A straight line.

No feints, no modulation of force whatsoever.

Even if I dodge the fist, the shockwave originating from it will blow away the entire space.

「Using Lightning Speed (電光石火).」

KWAAAAAANG.

The shockwave from the fist struck the air. The aftermath sent dirt and soil flying from the ground's surface.

The effect of Lightning Speed transforms the body into lightning, granting immunity to physical attacks. After the shockwave passed, I returned to my original flesh.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Thunder Light (雷光).

Crimson lightning light sliced Hell Ogre Count's torso. Blood burst out. But it was extremely shallow. His hide was too thick to inflict a fatal wound.

"UAAAAH! It hurts!! I'm angry!!"

KWADEUDEUDEUK!

The ground he stood on split, and from his crimson skin a dark red aura slowly rose, flickering like flame. His wounds healed in an instant.

-This power. Familiar. This big brute. So he's contracted with a Phoenix.

"Die!! Die!!"

Hell Ogre charged recklessly. There was no trace of intelligence in his movements. Just simple punches thrown on sheer faith in his physical ability. The problem was that his body was so overwhelming it could tear a tank apart like paper.

He was easy to deal with. Using Lightning Speed, I ignored his attacks and struck on my own timing. The trouble was inflicting a fatal wound.

To cut his neck, the neck bones were far too thick and hard. Natural, given the neck supported five heads.

To pierce his heart, his torso was too large. No matter where I stabbed — front, back, side — the blade wouldn't reach his heart unless I buried the entire sword in.

'Even if I wound him, he regenerates immediately. Once his power runs out he won't be able to regenerate, but... there's no sign of him tiring. And he keeps charging in wildly, so there's no timing for one big hit.'

In times like this, my style would be to use the Transcendent Heavenly Demon and overwhelm with power, but there are too many eyes here — I can't use the Heavenly Demon Divine Art.

'Domain Opening is... I want to save it if I can. Once I use it, I can't use it again for a while.'

I would have used it if this were an emergency. But right now, it wasn't an emergency.

'I'll keep fighting and see when this bastard tires. And if I kill him quickly, his forces become mine too. I need to kill him as fast as possible for the profit.'

From the ring on my hand came a click of the tongue.

-Frustrating fool. Catch the fire with words.

I frowned at Agares's backseat driving.

"I was going to do that without you telling me. Stop."

「Using the authority of the demon sealed within Solomon's Ring.」

「Authority: Word of Command.」

Not on Hell Ogre Count — on the demonic authority he possesses.

The dark red flames rising from his body stopped. I drove my sword into his shoulder.

The flames must have had a body-enhancement effect too — the sword sank deeper than expected.

"Astrafe."

PAJIJIJIJIK!

Crimson lightning rode the blade and entered his body.

"Burst."

PEONG PEONG PEONG PEONG PEONG!

Lightning rampaged inside his body.

"KRAAAAAAAGH!"

Hell Ogre Count screamed in agony, thrashing. Before his flailing could hurt me, I pulled the sword out and used Lightning Speed to put distance between us.

"Striking from the inside works better than cutting from the outside."

When he quieted to catch his breath, I approached again, stabbed my sword in, and injected Astrafe — I repeated this three times.

Hell Ogre Count collapsed to the ground. The smell of burning meat reeked from his body. In the end, he'd been roasted golden all the way to his insides.
