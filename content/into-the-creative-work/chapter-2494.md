I ran forward, then stopped short.

There was a wide-open road right in front of me. To the side of the road, a little ways off, I could see hills and abandoned buildings.

My instincts were screaming. This road is dangerous. If there are people on the hills or in the abandoned buildings, they could snipe me and kill me.

It'd be better to go around towards the abandoned buildings if possible. There are quite a few trees over there that I can use for cover, and I might be able to find other weapons or supplies in the abandoned buildings.

,It'll take at least three times as long though.'

My hesitation was brief.

I went straight ahead. I ran down the wide-open road with just a pistol in my hand.

,Just because I go towards those abandoned buildings doesn't guarantee I'll get a better weapon than a pistol. Sniping? It hasn't even been three minutes since the Devil's Game started. There's no way someone could have gotten a sniper rifle and leisurely taken up a sniping position.'

In many ways, the chances of getting sniped were low.

Even so, I ran while being wary of the abandoned buildings.

I saw something moving in front of the abandoned buildings. It was too far away to see anything but a dot. But it must be a person. There's no way it could be anything else. If I spotted them, there's a good chance they spotted me too. Their viewing angle is much better than mine, after all.

I raised my pistol. I think I can hit them. Even if I miss, it doesn't really matter. Just keeping them in check will be meaningful.

,It can't be Shin Dohyun-ssi. It's not the right direction.'

Tang, tang, tang!

Three shots fired with a light heart.

The dot in front of the abandoned buildings disappeared.

「Acquired 5 game points.」

A notification window popped up like a kill log. I killed them.

,...I killed them? Is this what a gun is like? It feels completely different from killing someone with a knife.'

My body was getting excited. But not as much as when I killed someone with a knife. In a way, I might have just gotten used to killing.

Guilt? This is the Devil's Game. If I don't kill, I'll die.

I run.

A person came out of an abandoned building near the road and ran into me. It was an ordinary-looking woman in her early twenties. She had a shotgun in her hand. She was startled as soon as she saw me. That was the difference between life and death. As the woman was startled, I raised my pistol and pulled the trigger. The bullet hit the woman right between the eyes.

「Acquired 5 game points.」

,I didn't even use Super Focus... my hand moved almost reflexively.'

I picked up the shotgun from the woman's corpse. In her backpack, there were 3 servings of preserved food, bandages, and 10 shotgun shells.

,A double-barreled shotgun. I've seen it in games.'

I don't know how much a shotgun will help in an open field, but it's better than nothing. I grabbed what I needed and started running.

After running hard, I came across an abandoned building. A three-story abandoned building. It stood in front of the road, as if blocking it. If Shin Dohyun-ssi hadn't left, she would be here.

,Shin Dohyun-ssi is probably here. The first time I saw her, she was hiding under the infirmary bed.'

Having spent time with Shin Dohyun-ssi, I had a rough idea of her personality. Shin Dohyun-ssi is pretty smart, though not as much as Yoo Jinmyung-ssi. And she's a bit too cautious. If it were Shin Dohyun-ssi, she'd probably be hiding inside the building.

,There's no time limit in this game. All you have to do is survive until there are 10 people left. It's better to hide in one place and wait than to move around recklessly.'

That is, if you have enough weapons. The abandoned building isn't small, so there was a good chance there were weapons or supplies inside.

"Dohyun-ah! Are you in there?!"

I shouted loudly. I had to avoid recklessly entering the building and getting killed by Shin Dohyun-ssi.

Shin Dohyun-ssi appeared at the window on the third floor of the abandoned building.

"...Come in."

I nodded and went inside.

She was wearing a tracksuit and holding a rifle in her hand. An M16 rifle with a scope. It was a gun I knew thanks to the FPS games I enjoyed playing in middle and high school.

She was leaning against the window with her gun.

"I'm glad you came. It's better to have two people than to be alone in many ways. You keep watch at the back."

I'll follow her lead for now. The back of the building doesn't have a good sniping angle because of the trees. If I carelessly hung around the window, I'd be sniped in return.

"Dohyun-ah. Are we going to stay here?"

"This is a survival game. It's better to hide in one place than to wander around recklessly."

"Don't you think other people will think the same way?"

"......"

I know a similar online game. In the later stages of that game, the safe zone shrinks, bringing the survivors together in one place. Eventually, the survivors have no choice but to fight.

No safe zone? Then it's even more of a problem.

"That means we have to stay up for days, barely sleeping and keeping watch. More than anything, we don't have enough food to last for days."

I only have 3 servings of preserved food right now. Even if we split one serving between the two of us, it'll run out in 3 days. Our stamina will be depleted day by day, and it'll get harder and harder.

"...Are you saying we should go out and actively fight other people?"

"Rather than fighting, we should focus more on looting. Originally, looting is more important in these kinds of games."

"...Okay. I'll do as you say."

"Okay. Then let's move right away. You grabbed everything you needed, right?"

"Wait a minute. I found this... do you want to wear it?"

What Shin Dohyun-ssi brought from the corner was a green ghillie suit.

"That's great. Camouflage is important. Dohyun-ah, you wear it. I'll take the lead. If anything happens, you cover me."

We moved along a path with a lot of cover. Running into enemies here is what I wanted.

,I don't think I'll lose if I fight.'

With my unfailing accuracy, a pistol, a shotgun, and Super Focus, what do I have to fear? The only thing I had to watch out for was long-range sniping.

As I walked forward, I ran into someone. I immediately raised my shotgun. The other person also raised a pistol and aimed it at me. It was a blonde, blue-eyed foreigner. Unfortunately, Super Focus was activated late. After we were already aiming guns at each other.

,It's kind of hard to focus. It felt easier when I had a knife.'

Looking at the other person's movements, they don't seem like an ordinary person.

"Oh. I didn't expect to run into you here."

Korean came out of the foreigner's mouth. The lip sync doesn't match the voice. He's babbling in a language other than Korean, but it sounds like Korean to my ears. I wasn't too surprised.

"We can just go our separate ways. You move first."

I said. My heart was pounding. In my head, I was thinking about how to kill him. Use Super Focus, dodge, and attack. That seems like the best option.

,...Wait. This guy doesn't know about Shin Dohyun-ssi yet.'

Shin Dohyun-ssi is moving behind me, a little ways away. Since she went to the trouble of putting on the ghillie suit, it wasn't a good idea to move together. It's for situations like this.

,Shin Dohyun-ssi can snipe this foreigner in the head and kill him.'

Gunshot? The bullet is faster than the sound of the gun. By the time the gunshot rings out, he'll already be rolling on the ground.

"I want to go behind you. I'd appreciate it if you moved out of the way."

"Don't talk shit, Yankee. I was here first."

"Look, Asian friend. I admit that the situation makes us sensitive, but don't be unreasonable. How about this? Let's join hands. I'm a police officer from America. You look like you have a similar job... if we join hands, our survival rate will go up."

"Haha. What makes you think I'd trust you? You better get out of here quick. Otherwise, my partner will kill you."

The guy pauses for a moment.

"Then wouldn't that be even better? It's not like your partner has more than 10 people... if you accept me, our survival rate will go up even more. So...."

Bang.

A gunshot rang out from behind, and a bullet pierced the guy's head. A clean headshot. It was worth practicing shooting at the shooting range with Shin Dohyun-ssi all this time.

"Dohyun-ah, nice shot!"

I turned around. Shin Dohyun-ssi, who had revealed herself, was biting her lip tightly. Her eyes were shaking as if there was an earthquake.

I realized that it was the first time Shin Dohyun-ssi had killed someone directly.

"It's okay, Dohyun-ah. We had to kill him to survive."

"...Didn't we have to kill him? It might have been reassuring if we had accepted him as a partner."

"What makes you think we could trust this guy?"

"......"

"Dohyun-ah. You have to be strong. If you don't kill, you'll die. And killing... you'll get used to it after a few times."

"Is that supposed to be comforting?"

"Was it a little off?"

"...Never mind. What are we going to do?"

"We grab what we need and go on our way."

I moved while being a little more wary of my surroundings. The same situation didn't happen again.

Looting went smoothly. I also got firearms and bulletproof vests.

I gave the sniper rifle to Shin Dohyun-ssi and received an assault rifle. I also put on a bulletproof vest, bulletproof pants, and a bulletproof helmet.

"Bulletproof vest... you managed to put it on."

"What?"

"No. It's just that I thought you might not be able to wear a bulletproof vest because your chest is too big."

"Shut up."

Shin Dohyun-ssi glared at me venomously. That was all. She roughly accepts even jokes that cross the line to some extent. That was proof that Shin Dohyun-ssi and I had gotten closer.

,I should ask Jo Un-nim later to pick an auspicious day to confess.'

That's how 3 hours passed.

As I slowly started to get tired, I settled down in a suitable building. Judging from the food and ammunition we collected, we could last at least a week.

5 more hours passed, and 8 hours had passed.

-The game is starting to drag on.

-We will provide supplies every 3 hours for a faster game.

-We will also bomb certain areas at random times.

-The first supplies are dropping.

-The first bombing zone is an area with no participants.

Three bombers flew in from the left sky. Bombs fell and the ground began to burn.

"......"

"......"

Shin Dohyun-ssi and I kept our mouths shut. I wanted to kill that devil bastard.

「Game Escape Ticket - 100 GP」

Buying a game escape ticket might be the best way. The problem is that I don't have any points right now.

「Game Points Held: 45」

I was 5 game points short of strengthening Jo Un-nim.

"Dohyun-ah. I think I should use Jo Un-nim here. What do you think?"

"It's your ability. Use it if you need it."

I used it.

,Give me advice that I need right now.'

&lt;Go 3km to the right.&gt;

I moved as Jo Un-nim said.

Supplies dropped from the sky along the way. I got 2 smoke grenades, 2 hand grenades, and 2 servings of preserved food.

There was a sturdy military vehicle where Jo Un-nim told me to go. As if it were natural, the keys were in the car. There were no traces of anyone else.

,As expected of Jo Un-nim. You never disappoint me.'

I grabbed the steering wheel and Shin Dohyun-ssi got in the passenger seat. I hesitated before stepping on the accelerator.

,Where do I go now?'

&lt;Users can only receive advice twice a day.&gt;
