"Your master is me now. You have no right to refuse. Accept it."

Maybe it's because Jeonjeong died, but the Geumgang Shinroe didn't reject me like before. I obtained ownership of the Geumgang Shinroe. I could feel my and the Geumgang Shinroe's spiritualities connecting. At the same time, I could understand the power of the Geumgang Shinroe.

'A divine treasure is a divine treasure. With my current abilities, I can't fully draw out and use the power of the Geumgang Shinroe.'

The first way to use the Geumgang Shinroe: infuse it with lightning and throw it. As its name suggests, it's lightning, so even a supreme master will be caught off guard by its speed.

The second ability is magic assistance. It's limited to lightning-attribute magic, but it helps with the manifestation of any magic. It's like it handles complex formulas that I can't understand.

'Relatively simple structured magic like Lightning Restraint can be used relatively quickly with the help of the Geumgang Shinroe. Using complex magic isn't impossible. But it requires studying magic.'

The third ability is magic storage. The Geumgang Shinroe can store three lightning-attribute spells and activate them at any time just by injecting lightning energy.

Currently, the three spells stored in the Geumgang Shinroe are the Great Geumgang Guardian Art, Rain Armor Perfection, and Geumgang Eight Lightning Prison.

The Great Geumgang Guardian Art is a spell that creates a Geumgang Warrior.

Rain Armor Perfection is a spell that creates a barrier.

Geumgang Eight Lightning Prison is an excellent barrier and sealing technique. However, it requires eight skilled individuals who have mastered lightning-attribute magic.

I checked the spells and put them in and out of my inventory.

'Since I have ownership, it can be put into the inventory. There seems to be other abilities as well... This is all I can grasp for now.'

I raised my index finger. The Geumgang Shinroe came over it and began to spin around.

Another ability is that I can move the Geumgang Shinroe as I wish, like controlling a sword with energy. Of course, it consumes energy.

'It requires considerable concentration. It will take quite some time to become familiar enough to use it in battle.'

I tried to use Cheonma Shingong a little. Then the Geumgang Shinroe distanced itself from me and spun around. Even though I had ownership, it was resisting.

'It's reacting so strongly even with just a little bit of operation.... I can't use it with Cheonma Shingong.'

I suppressed the Cheonma Shingong and operated the Lightning Heaven Style. Then the Geumgang Shinroe approached me as if nothing had happened. It's quite an interesting guy.

Jeobeok jeobeok.

Three men and women approach me on the messed-up ground. It's the leader of the Eight Evils Society, Pasanakgun, and his right-hand man, Gyoak. And the Poisonous Evil Beauty, Yoo Yeoryeo. I checked Yoo Yeoryeo's condition first.

'I can see scratches... I'm glad she doesn't seem to have any internal injuries.'

Pasanakgun admired me as he looked at me.

"Amazing. To think you'd kill that damn monk. I admire that achievement and give you a chance."

"A chance? What kind of chance?"

"Hand over that divine treasure to me and join the Great Death Eight Evils Society. We just took care of the elders of the Noeum Temple. They were all internally injured, so it was relatively easy. Now this city belongs to our Great Death Eight Evils Society."

"You're full of shit. I don't go under someone weaker than me."

"...Didn't you hear? We took care of the Noeum Temple. Do you really think you can win even if you receive the combined attack of the Great Death Eight Evils Society, including me? You're alone."

"So, where are your precious subordinates right now?"

"They're just cleaning up for a bit. They'll come running as soon as I call them."

"Haven't you sensed it yet? Your internal injuries must be deeper than you thought."

"What?"

Pasanakgun was surprised and frowned, focusing on his senses. He'll feel it. The presence of the martial artists flocking to the Noeum Temple.

"Besides the Noeum Temple, don't you have more enemies?"

"Black Wind Sword Master and those trashy sects...! You were aiming for this from the start!!"

As if.

If I could have used Baekmuhan, I would have used him right away. But I can't ruin my relationship with Baekmuhan, so I left him alone for now. I didn't answer and just showed a sneer. The face of the guy who thought he'd been backstabbed is distorted with anger.

"...We can kill someone like you with just the three of us."

"Kill Gyoak and get out of here."

Before my sudden words could even finish, Yoo Yeoryeo's hand moved and stabbed Gyoak's nape with a dagger. It was a perfect fatal wound, and it was even poisoned. Gyoak died instantly and collapsed to the floor.

Pasanakgun swings his fist at Yoo Yeoryeo. But Yoo Yeoryeo had already retreated.

"Poisonous Evil Beauty!! Is this betrayal!! You ungrateful bitch!!"

"Gratitude? I joined the Great Death Eight Evils Society as a deal. I don't owe you anything. Well then, bye."

Jjieeok.

Yoo Yeoryeo tore up the space movement scroll I gave her and escaped from the scene. Pasanakgun looked around, but it was useless. Right now, she'll be with Muyeong Shintu in Nakwol Mountain, the safest place I know in this world.

"Damn it, Blood Light Demon!! You ruined everything!!"

"You guys were the ones who backstabbed first. If you were so upset, you should have gone according to plan."

"I'll kill you too, and I'll kill the Poisonous Evil Beauty too!! Keuaaaaaaaa!"

A dark red energy rises from Pasanakgun's body. Perhaps because of Gyoak's death, he's completely gone crazy, and even a Heart Demon has come. And that Heart Demon was in an out-of-control state.

"This is perfect. I needed a suitable opponent to test the Geumgang Shinroe's magic... I can kill you too, so it's killing two birds with one stone. Great Geumgang Guardian Art."

The Geumgang Shinroe began to suck in my energy. The amount is enormous. It took about 80% in an instant.

Pajijijijijijik!

A translucent substance began to form around the Geumgang Shinroe. It gradually increases in size and takes the form of a human. I climbed on top of it and watched the form.

"Hoo. I thought it would be similar to Jeonjeong's Geumgang Warrior... But it's completely different."

It did have solid muscles like the Geumgang Warrior. However, it was a woman, not a man. She wore light martial arts clothing.

Jeonjeong's Geumgang Warrior, Gwancheol Nahan General, had a golden translucent body, but my Geumgang Warrior was a blue translucent woman with breasts as big as houses. She also has hair and a face that's quite close to my taste.

And in her right hand, she holds a giant sword with a similar appearance to the Hwaryeon Bido.

"Good. I quite like it. I'll give you a name too. From now on, you are... Bojidaejangbu."

Lightning Heaven Style Lightning Flash.

He barely dodged the giant sword falling like a comb and kicked off the blade, jumping upwards. Stepping on the air, he runs to where I am in an instant.

Lightning Heaven Style Lightning Art Lightning Restraint.

Dozens of lightning chains surged from the Geumgang Shinroe in Bojidaejangbu's chest, restraining Pasanakgun.

"Keuaaaaaaaa!!"

"Shut up. Don't shout. ...Is he too crazy to hear me?"

Jjeojeok!

The lightning chains didn't last long and began to crack. It didn't matter. Unless he had a skill called Leap, he was already tied up enough.

Bojidaejangbu held out her palm towards him. Red lightning and blue lightning rotate in different directions around the center of that giant palm, resonating and merging.

Lightning Heaven Style Double Resonance Myriad Lightning Spiral.

A giant purple laser annihilated Pasanakgun's body, piercing the sky and stretching out before disappearing.

'It looks several times more powerful than Lightning Palm. A major disaster would have occurred if it had fallen to the ground.'

More surprising than that is that I used Red Thunder without going through the Hwaryeon Bido.

'I felt like it would work, so I tried it, but I didn't know it would really work.'

'Haha. Was this possible? After trying it once, there's nothing special other than needing some concentration.'

'Shibal. Can traits or skills grow without investing points?'

The past comes to mind. In my noob days, physical stats also grew depending on how I trained them. But at some point, my stats stopped increasing, so I stopped training my body moderately.

'There's no reason why skills and traits can't grow either. But why hasn't it happened until now? I've been training so hard.'

In an instant, the answer flashes through my head.

Talent.

And I've been training quite consistently until now, but... If you ask me if I've been training desperately enough to risk my life, then that's not the case.

'I have the game life app, so why train so hard? It's more fun and easier to collect points than to train risking my life. But you need to train to use your abilities skillfully.'

I roughly came to a conclusion, got rid of Bojidaejangbu, and landed on the ground. Now that the Noeum Temple and the Great Death Eight Evils Society have disappeared, it's time to clean up.
