The White Demon King, having leaped into the sky, formed a seal with his left hand and exhaled a dark breath. His dark breath became a massive block of ice, rushing towards me.

I was about to dodge it lightly when I realized the clouds had frozen, preventing movement. Even my feet were frozen in place.

,The White Demon King didn't aim his magic at my feet. He merely froze the clouds and my legs with the cold that naturally emanated from his magic.'

The reason my body isn't falling is that the clouds, frozen by the dark chill, are fixed in space. This is quite annoying.

First, I summoned the iron staff and knocked away the incoming ice chunks. I swung the iron staff with all my might. If it were ordinary ice, it would have shattered to pieces. But the black ice created by the White Demon King was best deflected to the side rather than trying to break it.

Even without directly touching my body, my hands and wrists are freezing. I could feel the cold seeping into my body, affecting my gi and blood.

Pajijijijijik!

By intentionally generating the extremely yang energy of lightning, I managed to drive out the cold, which improved the situation somewhat. I melted the frozen feet and clouds in the same way.

The White Demon King, seeing this, shouted as if it were absurd.

,You dare challenge the White Peak Immortal with just this much?!"

,I challenged because I thought I could handle it. And I still feel the same way now."

,You bastard!!"

The enraged White Demon King roared. At the same time, he exhaled. The black breath became a powerful chill, instantly stealing the temperature of the sky. The clouds floating in the sky froze and fell to the ground.

I endured by enveloping my body in lightning. Even the cold that penetrated to my bones was bearable.

I moved the clouds beneath my feet to approach the White Demon King. He calmly wielded the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 10]

I used the prepared clone technique to draw attention while leaping forward.

[Using Leap.]

The clone was struck by the sword, and from behind him, I reverse-summoned the iron staff and summoned Hwaryeon Bido to swing. The black smoke flowing from the White Demon King's mouth and body blocked my attacks.

Jjeoeoeoeong!

The black smoke, which should have been intangible, was like solid steel.

,Using monkey-like tricks!"

The bastard, having dispelled the clone, turned his body and swung his sword greatly. I hastily raised Hwaryeon Bido to block the sword strike, but I was pushed back by the force and stopped after flying backward.

,To think that Hwaryeon Bido can clash with the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword, which cuts through ordinary famous swords like tofu, without a single scratch! You have a great Dharma artifact that doesn't suit your station!"

The White Demon King's eyes gleamed with greed as he looked at Hwaryeon Bido. He seemed to have realized the greatness of Hwaryeon Bido.

,I tried to hide my true abilities and avoid using Hwaryeon Bido or Vajra Divine Lightning as much as possible, but he's not an opponent I can afford to hold back against. If I don't go all out, I'll lose.'

The White Demon King was skilled not only in magic but also in swordsmanship. He must have taken on a human-like form to learn swordsmanship in order to wield the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword.

I approached him again and engaged in battle. Each time the sword and knife clashed, strong energies shattered, and red lightning and black cold collided relentlessly to devour each other.

Kwaaang! Kkang! Kwang!

After fighting non-stop for 3 minutes, we created some distance.

,Amazing! To think that a monster who hasn't even reached the Harmonious Realm can match me! Have you just been eating and swinging your sword all your life?!"

Match. But the damage I sustained was greater. Unlike my seemingly intact exterior, my insides were shaking because I was being pushed back in terms of strength and energy. I also consumed more internal energy and stamina. It was the difference in realms. The longer it goes on, the more disadvantageous it becomes for me.

,The momentum of the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword has weakened compared to the beginning."

As if rebelling against my words, the White Demon King slashed down with his sword. A dark blue strike flew towards me.

Pajijijik!

Hwaryeon Bido emitted red lightning and cleaved the strike in two.

,What do you know to be spouting such nonsense? As you can see, the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword is perfectly fine."

,There's no need to bluff in front of me. I can clearly see the limits of that sword. That kind of massive strike can only be done about 20 more times."

,Don't you dare arbitrarily judge the limits of the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword! The Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword is like the endless sea and the vast sky! The ultimate divine sword is none other than the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword!"

,Many people are mistaken about something. The sky and the sea are not infinite. The same goes for the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword. Or, to be more precise, it's your limit."

,Shut up!! What do you know, you haven't even reached the Harmonious Realm!!"

,It is said that the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword becomes stronger the more it kills. If that were really true, you would have killed several monsters a day. The reason you don't is because the sword has reached its limit, isn't it? A sword whose limits are determined by the owner's capabilities! I can see it all!"

The White Demon King's face, flushed red and blue, turned ferocious as if I had touched his reverse scale.

Black smoke erupted from the White Demon King's body. The smoke didn't disappear but filled the space. The space froze.

,I can't avoid it. Then I'd rather attack first.'

I tried to approach by using Leap in succession.

[This is a space where you cannot Leap.]

,Damn it. Is this a space where I can't even use spatial movement itself.'

The space is filled with darkness. If it weren't for the Lightning Eye Golden Pupil, I wouldn't have been able to locate the White Demon King in the darkness.

The darkness gradually spread out, and the air and space froze the moment they touched the darkness.

,It's like this even though it's daytime... It would have been even more powerful at night.'

For reference, this was magic but not really magic. He drew out the image he had in his mind with the power of magic. Even if I were to imitate that magic, I wouldn't be able to reproduce that form and power.

,Domain Expansion."

Small starlight burst from Hwaryeon Bido. The starlight resonated with me and began to shine even in the darkness. I wore the domain around my body and flew towards the White Demon King. The domain prevented me from being frozen by the darkness.

I and the White Demon King fought.

It wasn't easy.

Even if I unleashed lightning, it was blocked by the black smoke. Even if I accelerated, he stubbornly kept up with my speed.

,The domain is not infinite. I'll risk dying once to decide the outcome.'

I recklessly rushed towards the White Demon King. The White Demon King didn't miss the small gap that appeared in the meantime. His sword slashed my shoulder. It wasn't a deep wound. Ignoring it, I thrust my left fist towards his chest. Black smoke gathered to block my fist.

The black smoke that even Hwaryeon Bido couldn't cut through.

Even with the domain around me, it's not easy to penetrate that black smoke. Even now, I can feel the repulsive force from my fist.

,But I also have a trump card.'

I slightly opened my clenched left hand. In that small space, I summoned the Vajra Divine Lightning and gripped it tightly. A bright yellow lightning bolt erupted from the tip of the Vajra club, piercing through the black smoke. Even so, it was still not enough.

『Using the light of the Heavenly Gang Star.』

'Breaking Army Star (Po Jun Star)'

The effect of the Breaking Army Star is simple.

Maximizing attack power.

A large amount of internal energy and stamina disappeared. Instead, the attack of the Vajra Divine Lightning became stronger. The yellow lightning, strengthened by the power of the star, tore through the black smoke and the cold darkness. The tip of the Vajra Divine Lightning pierced the bastard's chest.

Kwaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaaang!

An explosion of cold occurred.

With half of my body frozen, I was pushed back by the explosive force and slammed into the ground. Instead of using a tumbling technique, I wrapped my body in clouds to minimize the impact.

Pajijik.

I shook off the ice clinging to my body with lightning. Thanks to the domain, there were no internal injuries.

,But the domain doesn't seem to be working as well as I thought. Is it because the darkness he wields feels somewhat similar to the domain, even though it's slight.'

It might be a feature of the [Bright Light Ascension] world. Those who have reached the Harmonious Realm are also called Incarnations (化神).

,Red knife and Vajra club... I've heard of them. One of the human generals who recently appeared uses such weapons. Furthermore, those eyes that seem to contain lightning. Could it be that your identity is...!"

I smiled. There were quite a few Great Demons looking at me. It didn't matter. I had already decided not to hide my identity.

[Activating Heavenly Heart. Lasts for 1 minute.]

The Demonic Transformation Heart Exchange Great Art was forcibly undone, and I returned to my original appearance, the human Seong Yujin. I chuckled at the strange sense of freedom and said.

,That's right. I am Lightning General Seong Yujin!!"

I put the Vajra Divine Lightning into my inventory and operated the Cheonma Shingong.

Cheonma Divine Skill Heaven-Breaking Demolition(破天荒).

Pajijijijijijijijijijik!

Black lightning erupted from me. My hair turned white and soared upwards. My whole body was overflowing with power. It was the power of the Transcendent Cheonma.

,To shatter your own soul... Are you truly insane?!"

I ran towards the bastard who was in shock.

Kwajijijijijik!

Black lightning and black smoke collided. The black lightning was gaining the upper hand. It was slowly but surely overwhelming the black smoke.

I rushed towards the bastard and swung my knife. The White Demon King hastily raised his sword to defend.

Jjeoeoeoeong!

The White Demon King, unable to withstand the force, flew backward and slammed into the ground. In the process, two mountains were destroyed.

,Keke. This is what overwhelming power is. By the way, you're lucky. A bastard who wouldn't have been able to withstand even one blow would have died... Thanks to your excellent sword, you withstood one blow."

Looking closely at the Hwaryeon Bido in my hand, there were cracks in the blade. Even that Hwaryeon Bido couldn't withstand my current power. Of course, it will automatically repair itself over time. It will repair even faster if I put it in my inventory.

,Keheoeoeok!"

The White Demon King spat out blood and barely managed to get up. The Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword also had cracks like Hwaryeon Bido.

I stretched out my index and middle fingers and pointed at the bastard.

Black lightning rotated and gathered in front of my index finger.

Lightning Heaven Style (雷天流) Ten Thousand Lightnings (卍雷).

,This is the final Cheonma Shingong...! Demon Killing Light Cannon!!"

A black beam was fired from the compressed Ten Thousand Lightnings. The White Demon King didn't even react and was killed by having his head pierced. The beam didn't stop there but dug deep into the ground. It seemed to have dug thousands of kilometers, but there was no significant reaction. The depth of the mantle in this world seems to be extraordinary.

I lightly kicked in the air. With just that, I appeared in front of the White Demon King's corpse as if teleporting. I held the cracked Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword in my hand.

The Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword didn't feel the same overwhelming power as before.

Looking with the Lightning Eye Golden Pupil, the power of the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword is initialized when its owner dies. To rebuild its power, you just need to kill living beings, whether they are monsters or humans. The stronger the living being, the faster the power will increase.

,Since I'm going to use it as a material anyway, it doesn't matter if it doesn't have power.'

I put the Chaos Greedy Wolf Sword into my inventory and looked around.

The Great Demons were paying attention to me.
