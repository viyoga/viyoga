I knew about Bright, the leader of Luminous, and his magic from Im Hyeon-ah-ssi.

He's a sorcerer who uses light magic. I heard he mainly fights by shooting beams. There wasn't much other information. Bright hasn't moved directly much until now. Even if he does move directly, he thoroughly controls information.

,Because of the penalty, I can't recall what that bastard's Ars Magna ability is at all.'

One thing is for sure, he's not someone to be taken lightly.

Bright pointed at me with his index finger. Light gathered in front of his finger and flew towards me as a beam. I tried to block it with a wind and water shield.

It pierced through. The beam grazed past my shoulder.

,Fortunately, the speed is similar to a bullet. The power is clearly more than a bullet. However, this can't be all there is.'

I created wind blades and launched them at Bright. Bright didn't dodge. He didn't need to dodge. The light coming from his halo completely eliminated the wind blades.

,Is it like a constant barrier? It seems like a decent attack won't be enough to break through.'

I briefly considered taking out Hwaryeon Bido or Geumgang Sinroe. But I soon shook my head. I can deal with him well enough without weapons.

,I have Azure Flame. If it really comes down to it, I can just burn everything away with Azure Flame.'

Azure Flame burns everything. Magic or magical power is no exception. However, the range is short, so I need to get close.

"I think your freely moving movements are amazing. But there are things you can't avoid."

Dozens of beams were fired from behind Bright. The dozens of beams refracted in the air, filling the space around me as they approached.

Evasion is impossible. I have no choice but to block them.

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Barrier Maximum Output.

Pajijik.

The electromagnetic barrier blocked the beams being fired from all directions. The beams that had been confusing my eyes disappeared, and Bright's weakness was revealed.

,I won't miss it.'

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

I run towards Bright. Bright held out both palms towards me. Light flashed and gathered from the center of both palms.

"Have you ever been hit by a light wave? It disappears without leaving even dust behind."

An extremely thick beam of light that could blow away the studio in an instant was fired at me.

,I just need to leap.'

Leap.

I teleported 10m to the right.

At that moment, the extremely thick beam of light refracted and flew back towards me.

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Barrier Maximum Output.

It was impossible to offset it.

I was pushed back by the extremely thick beam of light, broke through the wall, and fell out of the broadcasting station building.

,Shibal. Even with maximum output, I couldn't completely block it. It feels like my whole body is burning.'

At least it wasn't a burn yet. The Anti-Magic Unit's uniform is a special outfit, so it blocked it once. If it wasn't for the uniform, I might have suffered burns all over my body.

Just before colliding with the ground, I stopped my body with wind and landed on the ground.

Bright came down directly from the broadcasting station. The light was flashing in the dark night sky, so it was noticeable. He didn't land on the ground and stopped in the air. It seems to be the effect of the halo rather than doing something with light.

"That halo, is it an ability to amplify light?"

It probably has sensing abilities too. Otherwise, it wouldn't make sense for him to react so perfectly when I used Leap.

"That's also one of the abilities this halo has."

Tang tang tang! Tang tang tang tang tang!

Gunshots rang out from all directions. The snipers from the police special forces who had surrounded the broadcasting station building all pulled the trigger at once.

It was useless. Bullets are annihilated as soon as they touch the light coming from Bright's halo.

Bright swept through the air with both hands. The beams of light created from his palms stretched out in all directions, piercing the heads of the hidden snipers.

,I knew the approximate location of the snipers with electromagnetic waves, but how did that bastard know?'

Bright's sensing ability had to be at least similar to mine.

Bright shot beams at me again. I sensed the beams with electromagnetic waves and dodged or blocked them. The beams that suddenly refracted and changed direction were quite annoying.

I tried to close the distance, but it didn't work out as I wanted. Bright didn't seem to have any intention of engaging in close combat and maintained a distance of at least 30m.

Kwakwakwang!

I dropped lightning on that bastard. It was useless. It was blocked by the light and disappeared. Unless it's an attack with considerable power, I can't break through that light and inflict damage on Bright.

"How long are you going to continue this meaningless war of attrition? The longer you drag it out, the more disadvantageous it is for you. Ars Magna basically consumes a lot of magical power. Can you maintain that state for another 3 minutes?"

"Did you know? There are grimoires that don't discriminate against their owners. This is one of them."

What Bright took out of his pocket was a blue glowing crystal ball. I felt the enormous magical power lingering in that ball.

"It's a grimoire called the Witch Orb. This grimoire's magic is to store magical power. It's a grimoire that any magician can use. 3 minutes? I can fight for another 30 minutes. How long can you fight?"

30 minutes? I have complete recovery, so I can endure that much. But I don't intend to drag this fight out that long.

I reach out my hand towards him.

Thunder Sky Style Dual Resonance Myriad Thunder Spiral.

Red thunder and Azure thunder. Two thunders rotate and resonate, changing into one force.

A purple laser stretched out towards Bright. Bright's complexion hardened in an instant, and he quickly moved to the side to dodge.

,He dodged without blocking... That means he knew something terrible would happen if he didn't dodge. It should be a technique he's seeing for the first time, right? Did he dodge by instinct? Or....'

While dodging that bastard's beams, I counterattacked with Myriad Thunder. An attack with a similar motion to Myriad Thunder Spiral. But that bastard didn't dodge.

,How about this.'

I took out a tungsten bead from my pocket and threw it into the air.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 10]

In the slowed world, I focused my concentration in an instant and laid down an electromagnetic rail in a single moment.

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Cannon.

A railgun was fired at the bastard in the air. 7 times the speed of sound. It's impossible to see and dodge. The best thing to do is to sense the magical power, feel something strange, and dodge.

,But this bastard... he moved as soon as I threw the tungsten bead into the air. Before I even laid down the electromagnetic rail.'

I curled up the corners of my lips.

"Now I get it. The real ability of that halo is to see the near future. Am I wrong?"

"That's correct. The halo shows me the near future related to me. But so what? What changes even if you know that? Your attacks will never reach me."

"It changes. There's no need to test the waters unnecessarily. You can't attack because you can see the future? I just need to land an unavoidable attack."

Jeunryeong.

I held a blue dodecagonal metal tag in my hand. The Jeunryeong, enhanced with the Light and Ascension Sword Technique, allows me to control water and wind... but its essence is to control clouds. In other words, it was about manipulating the weather as I pleased.

A large amount of mana rapidly drained away, and dark clouds appeared in the sky. The dark clouds, which filled the sky in an instant, poured down rain and flashed with lightning. Lightning swam and resonated within the dark clouds.

"You're crazy. Are you planning to blow away this entire area right now?"

"Why? Did you see a future where you die?"

"That won't happen. You'll die first before that."

Bright swung his right arm. A giant pillar of light fell from the sky towards me.

I, who was concentrating on my senses, felt an unusual flow of magical power and dodged to the side as soon as I noticed it.

Thunder Sky Style Flying Thunder God.

Bright continued to swing his arm. Pillars of light, blades of light, beams. One, each destructive attack flew in succession.

Sinkholes are created in the pillars of light, and buildings are cut down and collapse by the blades of light. The beams have a simple yet deadly power.

But with the combination of Heavenly Eye, electromagnetic waves, and Flying Thunder God, there was nothing I couldn't avoid.

Large-scale attacks like pillars of light inevitably consume a large amount of magical power, and Heavenly Eye visualizes and shows it. Pillars of light? It wasn't difficult to avoid.

Bright bit his lip. He felt impatient. The distance between me and him gradually narrowed. Bright didn't dodge. Because it becomes more difficult to avoid beam attacks as the distance gets closer. Conversely, that bastard is also in danger.

I stepped on the air and approached Bright at a rapid speed. Light flowed out from Bright's entire body and covered me.

Ars Magna, Azure Flame.

Burning the light with blue flames, I approached Bright. Bright paused and soon gave up.

"If I move from here, a large amount of lightning will fall and erase this area, and if I don't avoid it here, I'll burn to death alone... The future is fixed. Did you intentionally lead me here? You're an absurd bastard."

"If it were me, I would have avoided this spot first."

"Abandon everything and run away? I've already resigned myself to death. Running away is something I can't choose."

"Yeah. So you're going to die here."

Hwareureuk.

Blue flames rose and began to burn Bright.

Instead of screaming, Bright looked straight at me and said.

"Our battle is being broadcast live. People have also learned that you tried to destroy this entire area to kill me. You can't avoid people's criticism now. You can't be a hero."

"So what? I've never cared about that kind of thing from the beginning."

"I still can't understand exactly what your purpose is... but at least you're better than the corrupt hypocrites in this country."

Bright turned to ashes and disappeared.
