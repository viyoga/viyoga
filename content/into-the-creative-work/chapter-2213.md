Yuria followed Secretary Cynthia to the secretary's office. The office, decorated like a company office, was half empty. Judging by the items on the desks, it seemed like people were away on business trips rather than absent.

,Yuria-yang. Do you know how many businesses Her Highness the Princess runs?

,No. I don't know.

There's no way I'd know since no one told me. Of course, I never bothered to investigate separately.

,Her Highness the Princess runs 14 businesses, large and small. Of these, 6 are unofficial businesses that are not publicly known. Unfortunately, I can't tell Yuria-yang either. Unless Yuria-yang officially joins the secretary's office.

,You don't need to feel sorry. It's natural to keep secrets. Does the secretary's office directly manage the businesses?

,We don't have the manpower for that. The secretary's office is involved in the operation of the businesses according to Her Highness the Princess's wishes. In addition, Her Highness the Princess values talent and sponsors 13 artists and 75 students. Managing them is also the secretary's office's job. Also, organizing rumors related to Princess Elsa is also a job.

,...You handle most of the things related to Her Highness the Princess.

,That's right. We work for Her Highness the Princess. Loyalty is as important as ability. Can Yuria-yang work for Her Highness the Princess?

,I will work for Her Highness the Princess now.

,...Personally, that's not a very satisfying answer, but okay. Let's start with the document review.

,What criteria should I use to review the documents?

,Her Highness the Princess's time is precious. Divide the documents into two categories: those that Yuria-yang thinks require Her Highness the Princess's judgment and those that do not.

A total of 150 documents.

Yuria divided the documents in 3 minutes. Cynthia, who was watching Yuria's document work with her mouth open, quickly closed her mouth. She almost drooled.

,Ahem. You were just flipping through the documents without even looking at them....

,It's speed reading.

,...That's an enviable ability. Of course, that's if you actually checked the documents properly. Accuracy is more important than speed in this kind of work.

Cynthia checked the sorted documents. Out of 150 documents, 7 were classified as going to Her Highness the Princess.

,...That's quite different from my classification. I thought about 50 documents should be judged directly by Her Highness the Princess.

,It's my first time, so I think I made a mistake.

Rather than a mistake, the judgment criteria were different from Cynthia's.

While sorting the documents, Yuria thought of Eugene. Since Eugene doesn't do any paperwork in [Baekhwan], most of the paperwork was Yuria's responsibility. Yuria handles everything unless it's a really important matter.

,...A proposal to invite Her Highness the Princess as a guest on an entertainment show. I don't think this is a document that Her Highness the Princess needs to handle directly.

,I judged that I should submit the document since it was a proposal delivered to Her Highness the Princess.

,Her Highness the Princess's image may be tarnished by going on such a vulgar entertainment show. And since Her Highness the Princess is minimizing external activities right now, this kind of thing should be automatically cut off.

Eugene would have shown interest in entertainment shows. Because there are so many beautiful women in the entertainment industry.

,I see. I was short-sighted.

,......Why did you leave the documents related to embezzlement? These important documents should be submitted to the Princess.

300 million credit embezzlement.

In Korean currency, that's about 3 billion won. In Yuria's view, it was a small amount. Was it really necessary to bring it to the final decision maker? The job is done by ordering the subordinates to catch the embezzler, recover the embezzled amount, torture them, and kill them. The report is finished with a verbal report during tea time.

,It didn't seem like a big crime.

,...What about this new business report?

,I judged that they were doing well.

,......Why did you ignore this investment proposal?

,It's an investment that won't even recover half of the investment amount. I wonder if someone put in the proposal as a joke.

,It's an investment proposal from one of Her Highness the Princess's asset managers.

,Ah.

,Yuria-yang, you classified the documents like a plenipotentiary, not a secretary. I'll tell you the criteria, so try classifying them accordingly.

,Yes.

She classified the documents again. This time, Cynthia nodded in satisfaction.

,You're doing well now that I've told you. Next are the information-related documents. The secretary's office operates a separate intelligence department. I can't tell you the details because it's confidential.

Cynthia brought new documents.

,Those documents are...?

,These are the information coming from the intelligence department. Dozens of pieces of information are uploaded every day. You have to select the information to report to Her Highness the Princess from among these.

,Is it related this time too?

,The top priority is information related to Her Highness the Princess. Other than that, information that you should know when you judge it.

Yuria finished classifying the documents in 3 minutes.

They didn't show her really important information, but there was some pretty interesting information too. Information that would be helpful to Eugene. She was planning to pass it on to Eugene when she had the chance later.

After that, she continued to work on the documents. Yuria had to endure Cynthia's burdensome gaze.

,Yuria-ssi. Do you want to get a job at the secretary's office right away? You will work as the next chief of staff.

The daily work of the secretary's office was a battle with documents. Sifting through paperwork, creating new documents according to the Princess's orders, and delivering them to businesses.

If there is any secret deal between Princess Elsa and a politician, the secretary's office will handle the practical work. Yuria faced Princess Elsa based on the documents and shared reports. In the afternoon, she attended to Princess Elsa's needs. All she did was become a conversation partner.

,The head of the management office and the chief of staff praised Yuria-yang so much. I was really surprised, especially when Chief Cynthia was passionately advocating for you to be scouted.

,I'm glad they think highly of me.

,Yes. I'm worried about whether I'll be able to do well.

Going out on the day the Dark Moon rises is dangerous. Even ordinary monsters might mutate into some kind of monster when the Dark Moon rises.

The reason why nobles and royals, including Princess Elsa, go hunting during the Dark Moon is for propaganda. They risk their lives to get rid of monsters for the citizens! For that propaganda slogan.
