We moved towards the Black Desert.

Unfortunately, it was impossible to travel there in one go using a spatial movement scroll.

‘There are no coordinates.’

The Black Desert was a region outside my interest.

Even among the already barren desert areas, it was the most desolate. Other desert regions at least had resources like ore, but the Black Desert truly had no resources whatsoever. Wells were, of course, nonexistent, and there were no plants like cacti. There was no way to get water except by collecting the occasional rain. Merchants would sell water at exorbitant prices in the Black Desert.

‘No, there are sea resources, though.’

Since there was a sea, they filled their bellies with seafood and engaged in sea trade. But as far as I knew, there was no particular profit. Atlantis was a single continent, and the only proper nation was the Empire. Other regions also had ports. There were almost no merchants who would bring their ships to the Black Desert port, which had no specialties or anything.

‘To properly operate the Black Desert region, one would have no choice but to be mindful of the Empire.’

My current identity, the Demon King of Helten, was a criminal wanted by the Empire. Even if I dominated the Black Desert, I would lose more than I gained.

‘That’s why I left it alone until now. I didn’t even pay it any attention.’

However, they provoked me first.

I couldn’t tolerate it.

‘I’ll dominate the Black Desert, but I won’t operate it. Whether the region turns into a mess or not, AP will come in every month.’

Under the condition of not operating it at all, there would be no loss.
