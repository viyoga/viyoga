It was unexpected that Gu Taehee, the boss of Taehwang, would come in person. I didn't even know she would show up without prior notice.

I accepted Gu Taehee as a guest for now. Gu Taehee is a big shot in the underworld. Her power is immense, so I couldn't wield it as I pleased. Besides, I did rip her off last time. When you think about it, I became the owner of Seongak Elementary School thanks to Gu Taehee's help.

Surely she's not here now because the situation is urgent and she wants me to hand over Seongak Elementary School.

I met her in the reception room for now.

I was with Na Chaeyoung, and she was accompanied by two subordinates. I served them coffee. Of course, it wasn't instant coffee. Among the refugees who came to Seongak Elementary School, there was a beautiful barista, so I hired her as the main barista for the main building. The coffee wasn't to my taste.

,I looked around on the way here. Seongak Elementary School is peaceful. Without even a common zombie."

,A week ago, a troll bastard gathered zombies and killed them all. Thanks to that, it was cleaned up."

,It probably helped that this neighborhood has a smaller population than other neighborhoods. I'm jealous. Sometimes I regret not settling down in a quiet place."

Gu Taehee spoke at length. Her scarred face was weary. It was understandable. She's not just the head of any ordinary organization, but Taehwang, which can be said to be the largest organization in Korea.

The larger the power, the greater the impact of the D virus.

,Hasn't the handling of the D virus ended yet?"

,The handling of the headquarters is over. Executives and important employees were all isolated in private rooms before the D virus spread. Thanks to that, the handling itself wasn't difficult. It was just a matter of killing the guys who turned into zombies. The problem burst out on the lower end. I clearly told the executives to do it properly... but those ignorant bastards turned a deaf ear to my words."

Gu Taehee gritted her teeth as if she was frustrated, then soon sighed.

It's probably because I had a look on my face that I wasn't interested. And I really wasn't interested. Even if Gu Taehee became a zombie and died right now, I wouldn't feel anything.

,...I got carried away for a moment. Let's get to the point."

I can guess the point.

Seongak Elementary School has Na Chaeyoung, a technologist. The biggest advantage of a technologist is that they can easily make weapons. Since they're gangsters, they'll naturally be interested in firearms.

,Seong Yujin-ssi. I want to hire you as a mercenary."

The expectation was pleasantly off. No wonder Gu Taehee hasn't even glanced at Na Chaeyoung since earlier.

,A mercenary?"

,Monster spiders have nested in a building near the headquarters. At night, they crawl out and kidnap humans and zombies alike. I want you to help deal with those spiders. Of course, I'm not going to send you alone. Me and my trusted guys will support you."

,An entire building has been occupied by spiders?"

,It's a 10-story building."

,Shouldn't the state step in and handle this?"

,The military is in worse shape than is known. Most of the military units that can be operated have gone up north to check North Korea. The units in Seoul are busy protecting the guys they have. I even mobilized connections to ask a high-ranking official, but... they told us to handle it ourselves."

Gu Taehee frowned as if she was displeased. It felt like she got stuck with the grunt work.

,Why don't you just set the building on fire?"

,I tried. The spider bastards immediately put out the fire. They even threw fire extinguishers. Those bastards have the intelligence of a child. At least they have the intelligence to hunt humans."

Na Chaeyoung's face hardens.

,...Spiders with intelligence. That's dangerous. If left alone, they could become Arachne."

,Arachne?"

,It's a new race that spider monsters evolve into. Their physical abilities are one thing, but the biggest problem is that they gain intelligence beyond humans. They absorb human knowledge and create modern weapons."

Spider monsters using modern weapons. I imagined it for a moment. Hmm. How many legs does a spider have? Eight? If they attach machine guns to all eight legs and fire, would that be like eight people's worth?

,If they held swords, it would be the Eight Swords Style.'

Na Chaeyoung asked Gu Taehee as if to confirm.

,There must be a queen spider who controls all the spiders. Did you confirm it?"

,I confirmed it. She's laying eggs like a queen ant on the top floor of the building."

,Queen ants are just egg-laying machines. They're only called queens. But that queen spider is different. She'll perfectly control and dominate the spiders."

,...As expected of a player. You know a lot."

I understand the danger of spider monsters well.

,But it's not so urgent that we have to move right away, is it? It'll take time to evolve into that Arachne thing, and even if they evolve, they can't immediately absorb people's knowledge. I think there's plenty of time."

Wouldn't the military kill the spiders on their own when they stabilize later? If it seems really dangerous, they could just blow up the entire building with bombs or missiles.

I took the strength out of my body. I leaned back deeply into the back of the chair. Unless Seoul is in immediate danger. After all, the spider bastards running wild is just temporary.

,As I said before, the spider nest is in a building near the headquarters. The location of the building is also important, so I want to deal with it as soon as possible. Besides, if I dilly-dally... that high-ranking official I mentioned earlier will bother me. Unlike you guys, I'm in a hurry."

,Oh, I see. You know what? One of the things I hate is working for others."

,...I'll make sure you get a good reward."

,We have enough food and necessities. The number of people in the elementary school right now is only about 200, so we can handle it."

,This world isn't easy. There are things you can't do alone."

,Who said I'm alone? We have Doctor Na on our side."

As people gathered at Seongak Elementary School, resources were being supplied sufficiently. The method is to raid nearby empty houses or bring cars scattered on the road and disassemble them to supply resources. What Na Chaeyoung needs now is not resources but research time.

,Then how about this?"

Gu Taehee took out a small glass bottle and placed it on the desk. The glass bottle was filled with small, dark red beads. It looks like about 20 of them.

It's obviously a drug. I became interested. The shape was similar to Bister I, so I could guess the type of drug. Still, it's better to be sure, so I asked Gu Taehee.

,What is this?"

,It's Bister II. It's better than Bister I... no. You guys know it well, so there's no need to explain it."

Of course I know. Didn't I get quite a bit of honey from Bister I? Bister II is much more effective than that. If an ordinary person takes Bister II, they temporarily gain strength comparable to a superhuman.

,The problem is that the side effects are severe as the efficacy is excellent.'

First of all, blood pressure soars. You can withstand it without problems when the drug is effective, but when the drug wears off, you may faint due to high blood pressure. Also, you can permanently develop high blood pressure. Myocardial infarction can occur, and leukemia can develop.

In the worst case, you can die from internal bleeding as soon as the drug wears off. So you should take Bister II when you have a colleague or doctor present.

,I want it.'

Bister I is now losing its effectiveness and has no effect. It was because of that damn tolerance. Since I've never taken Bister II, I'll be able to exert incredible power.

,I don't need to take several Bister IIs at once. Then tolerance will develop slowly.'

Should I accept the offer?

No. This isn't the first time I've done this kind of negotiation. The fact that she readily offered a price means that she has something else prepared.

I crossed my arms and stayed still, and Gu Taehee naturally placed another medicine bottle on the desk. A bluish liquid sloshed inside the medicine bottle. It was something I'd never seen before. Na Chaeyoung admired it quietly as if she knew what it was.

,Evolution Serum! You've already made this?"

,Is this the Evolution Serum.'

I also know about Evolution Serum. I couldn't not know. Because it's the most gambling item in the [Eternal Eden] game.

Evolution Serum has a 50% chance of evolving or killing the target. The problem is that even if the evolution is successful, you don't know what kind of evolution it will be. It's literally a gamble with your life.

,...Your bionics aren't ordinary. Bister II and even Evolution Serum."

The material for Buster II is the corpse of a beast variant. In the case of Evolution Serum, it is made from the blood of an evolving entity during evolution. Evolution Serum was several times more difficult to make.

,I'll give you these as a basic. Tell me if you want anything else."

Na Chaeyoung looked at me. I stroked my chin and pretended to think for a moment before opening my mouth. Something I needed? Of course, there was. No matter how thoroughly prepared I was, there was always a shortage.

,I need construction equipment. In addition, professional construction manpower."

,...Are you thinking of expanding the elementary school?"

,As more and more people gather, the elementary school feels cramped."

,It's a bit difficult to dispatch construction manpower. However, it seems possible to hand them over to you altogether. They also want a safe place. But you have to treat them well. If you don't guarantee good treatment, no one will want to go there."

,Even if you don't say it, I'm guaranteeing good treatment for professional manpower."

I didn't like it, but even if they were men, I was giving them treatment that was no less than others if they were professional manpower. It was an unavoidable choice for the base to run well.

,Then is the contract concluded?"

,Should I write a contract?"

,Don't worry. I've prepared it."

Gu Taehee's subordinate took out the contract from the briefcase and placed it on the desk. I slightly modified the contract and signed it, and Gu Taehee stamped it.

,Signing it is neither fun nor cool. I need to get a seal.'

Maybe there will be many more times to write contracts in the future. A seal is easier and faster than a signature.

,I'll give you one more piece of information."

,I'll listen. Tell me."

,The high-ranking official I mentioned earlier is looking for those who attacked the Jeonmaeng Weapons Research Institute."

She's not probing. Gu Taehee is convinced that we are the attackers. I didn't care, but Na Chaeyoung's expression hardened slightly.

,Who is that high-ranking official?"

,I can't say. It's a condition of the contract with him."

,Okay."

I wasn't particularly scared. If they attack to retaliate, we'll retaliate too.
