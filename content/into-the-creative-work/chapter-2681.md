I came into the mirror. I came in so quickly that I lost my balance and fell to the floor. I quickly got up again and checked my surroundings.

It's a gray castle with a cold feeling. Next to me, Elena is lying face down, her head bowed.

Kang Myungjin, Lilsne, and Joo Seohyun are running towards us. I checked on Elena first.

,Elena!

,...I'm okay. There's no problem with my mind or body. I used almost 30 years of my lifespan to deal with the Thousand Year Old, but I can still live for over 70 years.

I helped Elena up. Elena doesn't refuse my touch and slowly raises her body. Her body, which was that of a child, returned to her original adult form.

,You don't look so good?

,...I have a lot to think about. About my father, and about the Thousand Year Old.

,What on earth did the Thousand Year Old do to you to be subdued in an instant?

,Thousand Year Old. I met that monster's gaze... and felt the difference in level. A huge amount of information and terror took over my mind and body in an instant.

,A huge amount of information?

,Yes. A huge amount of information that even my head can't handle. You could call it a kind of mental overload. If I, an illusionist, was like this... it's not strange for other ordinary humans to have their minds collapse and die instantly from that mental attack. If my father hadn't helped me... it would have been dangerous.

,Was it information related to the Thousand Year Old?

If so, it might be a good thing. Judging from the Thousand Year Old's monstrous form, more information was needed.

,Since it was unorganized information... I don't know. I was too busy being swept away by the information and letting it flow out. It's my own way of protecting my mind. There's almost no information left in my head now. However, I still have the feeling that it was a very old memory.

Elena sighed softly. Her complexion returns to normal. Her expression was the same as usual. An arrogant noble's expression, as if looking down on people.

,Whatever the result, we achieved our goal. There isn't no way to deal with the Thousand Year Old's mental overload. Build a stronger mental barrier, or don't meet the Thousand Year Old's gaze from the beginning, or twist and scatter the information itself with illusion. We were defeated because we didn't know, but if we know, we won't be defeated again.

I pondered for a moment, thinking of the Thousand Year Old.

The Thousand Year Old's eyes were dragon eyes. I've faced and killed dragons several times, so my eyes couldn't be wrong. But I can't conclude that the Thousand Year Old is a dragon just for that reason.

,The form the Thousand Year Old showed at the end was too monstrous to be called a dragon.

It's not like only dragons can have dragon eyes. Even Kang Myungjin is human, but he has dragon eyes. Although Kang Myungjin receives system correction.

,It's getting complicated.

There wasn't much information about the Thousand Year Old and Empire Ogong except for the Emperor and Elena in the original work and setting book.

,The Emperor and Kang Myungjin's goals are the same. There's no reason to be hostile to each other, and there's no reason to cooperate directly.

But Kang Myungjin seems to know about the Thousand Year Old.

,Lord Illusionary Duke. Are you alright?

Kang Myungjin, who had approached, asked.

,...It seems we were the last to arrive. As you can see, I don't have any major injuries. Are you all okay?

,Yes. We're fine. We successfully entered Winter Castle and dealt with the enemies nearby.

,Enemies?

Kang Myungjin pointed to the wall. There was a broken corpse under the wall. The remains of the emaciated corpse, which seemed to have starved to death, were frozen solid.

,Frozen undead. They're annoying enemies because they emit cold air, but they weren't difficult opponents. What's really dangerous here is the owner of Winter Castle, so... we were waiting for you and Eugene-gun.

,You're capable. Let's rest for a bit and then go kill the owner of this castle.

Elena took out a chair and sat down. She leaned deeply against the soft backrest and closed her eyes. She seemed to be organizing her complicated emotions. A difficult atmosphere flows.

Kang Myungjin approached me.

,Eugene-gun. Can I ask what happened?

,I was rampaging in a fake past. It's related to the Valdert family, so it's hard to explain in detail.

,I see.

I hesitated for a moment and then asked Kang Myungjin.

,Do you know about the Thousand Year Old's identity?

Was it an unexpected question? Kang Myungjin remained silent for quite a while before answering.

,Sometimes, just mentioning it can make the other person notice and become hostile. At least, I can't say it in this space.

,...Is the Thousand Year Old that powerful? Is he some kind of god?

,......

,Ah. I guess he is that powerful.

Thinking back to the Thousand Year Old's appearance at the end... it seemed like he was that powerful.

Now I'm really curious about the Thousand Year Old's identity.

I urged Kang Myungjin, but Kang Myungjin's heavy mouth didn't open easily.

,Eugene-gun. If I were to give you advice... it's best not to get involved with the Thousand Year Old and the Emperor as much as possible. They're not enemies. The Emperor sincerely wants to capture Atlantis.

I stopped asking for information about the Thousand Year Old.

,Then what about Helten?

,...Those bastards are enemies. Helten has stepped up to capture part of the volcanic island area, but their real goal isn't to capture Atlantis. But there's no need to fight them. We just need to capture Atlantis in our own way.

After that, we rested quietly. Lilsne glanced at me and Elena with a complicated expression, and Joo Seohyun glared at me as if to kill me, as always. It seems like that hostility is stronger than usual today.

Shaaaaa.

A cold wind blew into the mirror castle. That's all. The people here weren't weak enough to shiver from the cold wind.

,We've rested enough. Let's move now.

Elena said.

Kang Myungjin took the lead and moved. The destination was the audience chamber of Winter Castle.

The door to the audience chamber was not only closed but also frozen.

Kugugugung.

When we stood in front of the frozen door, it opened on its own without us even touching it. The ice attached to the door cracked and ice powder scattered.

Sitting on the frozen throne was a black knight with an emaciated face.

,The False Winter awakens.

The Black Knight of Famine opens his eyes. Blue ghostly fire blazed instead of pupils. The ghostly fire, which felt like flames, only felt cold instead of warmth.

All the windows inside the audience chamber opened and a cold wind entered.

,The Great Famine covers you. Hunger begins. The more severe the hunger, the more you lose your reason.

,Dry winter descends. Cold slowly seeps in. Your body becomes dull.

Tak.

Elena tapped the floor with her staff.

The space changed.

Sunlight poured down from the open ceiling, providing a warm heat. Thanks to that, my stiff body loosened. Elena even manipulated the cold wind with illusion.

,...Hunger can't be helped even with my illusion. Is the system directly involved? Maybe we have 10 minutes. Finish it within that time.

Lilsne shot an arrow. The Black Knight deflected the arrow with his sword. I, Kang Myungjin, and Joo Seohyun each ran towards the Black Knight in different directions.

Kang Myungjin takes the front, and I and Joo Seohyun attack the sides.

The Black Knight held a scale in his other hand. We, who had already learned the strategy through Kang Myungjin, knew the scale's ability. However, even Kang Myungjin didn't expect the Black Knight to take out the scale from the beginning.

,Eugene!!

Kang Myungjin shouted my name.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 2]

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 1]

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning (疾风迅雷).

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

,Using Lightning Speed.

I, the fastest among us, became lightning and ran to take the scale. At this distance, the lightning leap is the best, but there was no time to concentrate.

I quickly arrive in front of the Black Knight.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 0]

The Black Knight pulls the scale back and swings his sword at me at the same time.

,My body is temporarily transformed into lightning with Lightning Speed, but... the Black Knight's sword is too dangerous. If I get hit, I'll be done for. I'll abandon my left arm.

Instead, I just need to take the scale. Then I can win without variables.

Swish!

The Lightning Speed state is forcibly canceled and my left arm is cut off. My left arm falls, but no blood flows out. It froze before the blood could come out.

But my right hand didn't stop and went for the scale.

Hwareureuk.

The Black Knight's ghostly fire blazed fiercely.

,The Gaze of Winter freezes you.

My body freezes.

Not only did he take out the scale as soon as the battle started, but he also used the Gaze of Winter? The Black Knight was very determined.

,But thanks to Kang Myungjin telling me in advance, I knew.

[Activating Heavenly Mind. Lasts for 1 minute.]

My freezing body regains its freedom in an instant. Blood dripped from my severed left arm.

I roughly snatched the scale that the Black Knight was carefully holding.

,You should have held on tight if you didn't want to lose it. Ah, then you can't activate the scale?

The Black Knight didn't answer my provocation. Instead, he pulled back the sword that cut off my left arm and swung it.

That short preparation motion, it's enough time to escape.

I pulled my body back with Flying Thunder God.

The Black Knight naturally approached me and swung his sword. But the arrow shot by Lilsne hit the Black Knight's shoulder. The damage wasn't great, but it was enough to stiffen the Black Knight.

I used the gap created by Lilsne to successfully create distance.

,You did well.

Kang Myungjin said as he passed by me. Then even Joo Seohyun rushed at the Black Knight.

I smiled slyly from behind and watched the Black Knight get beaten up. Without the two variables of the scale and the Gaze of Winter, there's no way the Black Knight can win while fighting Kang Myungjin and Joo Seohyun at the same time.

,Tsk tsk. You're carelessly dropping your arm around.

Elena approached. A blue butterfly landed on my shoulder and then disappeared. Nothing happened.

,What are you doing?

,...Why isn't my illusion working? I was going to restore your arm.

,Ah. You can do it again in 1 minute.

It seems like Heavenly Mind judged Elena's Illusion Butterfly Technique as a status ailment.

1 minute later. Elena tried the Illusion Butterfly Technique again. My left arm was perfectly restored.

And the Black Knight, who had been holding out for a while, fell. The scale in my hand also disappeared.

,Area 8,840, Winter Castle has been captured.

,The temperature of Atlantis returns to normal.

,The influence of Winter Castle on the volcanic island disappears according to the ruler's wishes.

,Acquiring the title, Winter Conqueror.

,Mana stat increases by 1.

,Acquiring 10,000 AP as a capture reward.

It was decided that Kang Myungjin would have dominion over this area.

I wasn't interested in this area at all.

,It's an area with no living things, including people. There aren't even any plants. It's more barren than the desert.

At least there were people in the desert.

Winter Castle? It's just a damn big castle. There are no treasures or anything.

The area itself didn't have any particular benefits other than the AP that came out every month. Instead, the 3 items that the Black Knight left behind after his death looked quite useful.

,Should I check the information somewhere?

I'll be able to have one of the three.
