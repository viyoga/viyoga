,Boss, get your ass out here!!!"

My voice echoed.

Immediately following was a suffocating silence. Although not Martial artists, the commoners who lived adjacent to the Jianghu world knew enough to lower their gaze and shut their mouths. They acted in a way to not upset me, to be as unobtrusive as possible.

In the silence, I glared and scanned the inn. My Heavenly Eye pierced through the walls, pinpointing those who were hiding. The cook was trembling in the corner of the kitchen, and those in the inn rooms were quietly waiting for the situation to end.

,I saw the innkeeper last night. A fat, middle-aged man with a mole next to his nose.'

He wasn't there.

Even after scanning the entire inn three times, I couldn't find the innkeeper.

,...He bolted.'

This was certain. The innkeeper was a killer, just like the dead waiter. As soon as he judged that things had gone wrong, he ran.

,The cook in the kitchen seems to be an ordinary person with no involvement.'

I sat down in a chair.

I was annoyed that I had missed one, but I couldn't kill everyone here as an outlet for my anger. It was broad daylight. There were too many eyes watching.

I sat still in the chair, doing nothing. After a while, the smell of blood bothered me, so I kicked the corpse and the congee bowl into a corner.

There was no one around. When I didn't do anything, the inn guests scurried out of the inn like rats.

Kwaang!

The inn door burst open as martial artists rushed in. The character 'Sun (先)' on their uniforms stood out brightly. Around 20 martial artists poured in, surrounding me with an intimidating presence.

"......."

Unless I released the seal, I would die if I fought like this. But fortunately, they didn't seem to want to kill me.

Step, step.

Belatedly, a man entered the inn with calm steps. It was a familiar face. Sun Haedo. The head of the Sun Family. He plopped down across from me.

,To openly kill someone in Haga City's inn... Does the Sun Family look that easy to you?"

"......."

,Should I take this as a declaration of war from the Demonic Cult?"

,It's a personal matter. The dead guy wasn't a waiter, but an assassin who tried to kill me. Search the corpse's pockets. There will be a poisoned vial."

Sun Haedo gestured to his subordinates. Understanding his intention, they immediately rummaged through the waiter's corpse.

,It's here. A poison vial and a dagger. This guy is an assassin. Judging by the shape of the dagger, he seems to be an assassin from the Byungsal Group."

,...I see. This is a matter of the Jianghu world that I have no room to interfere in."

Sun Haedo got up from his seat without hesitation. He looked down at me with cold eyes.

As the head of the Sun Family, Sun Haedo had a duty to maintain the peace in Haga City. Because he was the representative of this city. He had the authority to summarily execute criminals who committed murder within the city.

However, things were different if it became a matter of the Jianghu world. If an ordinary person got caught up in a fight between Jianghu people, it would be one thing, but an assassin was treated as a criminal, neither Jianghu person nor ordinary person. Even if you openly killed an assassin, it was self-defense.

Passing by Daehyup (大侠, great hero). Is that you?

,You come out of nowhere and spout nonsense."

,Corpses were found in the alleyway. The street urchins who were playing in the alley were brutally massacred. Estimating the time of their deaths, it overlaps with the time you left the Sun Family. The location of the alleyway isn't far from the Sun Family either."

,You're saying I killed them?"

,Isn't that so?"

,What's the evidence?"

"......."

,Not even a witness?"

"......."

,You have nothing. Don't bother me with uncertain things."

Sun Haedo's face momentarily contorted, then returned to an expressionless state. It was a facial change like watching a face-changing mask performance.

,...You're quite lucky."

,I am lucky."

,I hope that luck continues to the end."

Sun Haedo left the inn with his subordinates. His subordinates explained the situation to the gathered commoners.

,Why did Sun Haedo come himself? Sending his subordinates would have been enough. Did he come to kill me?'

That couldn't be. He couldn't openly kill me because he was aware of the Demonic Cult's eyes.

,Was his goal to arrest me using this incident as a pretext? ...No. He didn't do anything for that. All he did was glare at me. ...Glared?'

I knew Sun Haedo's purpose.

He came to see if I was okay himself.

,Since he was the one who hired the assassin last night. He came to see if I was alive and how well I was doing. If I was hurt, he probably would have laughed and left.'

Tsk.

I clicked my tongue.

Sun Haedo was a Martial artist who was stepping into the realm of stubbornness. His discernment was beyond that of a master. He must have noticed that my right arm wasn't in a normal state.

,...I don't need to be impatient. I was going to use the state of my right arm as bait for him anyway.'

I spent time gathering information about Choo Jeo, and around noon, I headed to the Sun Family.

The gatekeepers, who were very nervous, glared at me.

I drew internal energy from my Dantian. The internal energy rose up and filled my lungs. I exhaled the internal energy like a dragon breathing fire and shouted.

Yeom Guseok of the Demonic Cult has arrived! Retainer of the Sun Family! Traitor of the Demonic Cult! Hwawoo Magic Sword (火羽鬼劍) Choo Jeo! I challenge you to a duel!

"......."

There was no reply.

The gatekeepers, as if they had been instructed in advance, did nothing and just stood there.

I shouted again.

,Choo Jeo! You cowardly bastard!! I respect your parents! If I were your parent, I would have smashed you to death with a rock, you disgusting son of a bitch! But your parents just abandoned you! Oh! How merciful! I believe your parents are reincarnations of Buddha!"

"......."

,Choo Jeo! You dirty traitor! I will kill you for sure!"

"......."

There was still no reply.

I spat on the front gate of the Sun Family and turned around.

•••

,Choo Jeo-gong, calm down."

Sun Haedo said urgently.

Choo Jeo was clenching his fists tightly and trembling. The fierce flames of anger burning in his chest told him to go out and kill that bastard right now.

Gaju (家主, family head)... This is the first time in decades that I've heard such abusive language...!

Choo Jeo had no tolerance for insults.

He was a Martial artist of the second level of Chulji. Having passed the wall of Chulji, he was treated as a master. He tried not to lose his dignity as a master, and if he acted with dignity first, the other party usually treated him with dignity as well. It was the first time he had been generally insulted like this.

,Originally, a scared dog barks loudly. It's proof that he's getting impatient. He's already in our hands, so there's no need to indulge in his last struggles."

,You said his right arm isn't normal. At that level, I can win even if we duel."

,That information isn't certain. Seeing him come in such a hurry... He must be confident that he can win too... He probably has a hidden card. And also, I want him to suffer and die miserably. Choo Jeo-gong, calm down your anger and wait. He will meet a miserable death in the plaza of Haga City."

,Hoo. I will trust Gaju-nim and wait. Are you still planning to use the Byungsal Group?"

,The Byungsal Group leader will move personally."

,...You hired even the leader? That must have cost a lot of money... You're amazing."

,No. He killed the Byungsal Group's assassin so miserably in the inn. Thanks to that, rumors about the Byungsal Group's assassin drowning in congee have spread throughout Haga City in an instant."

,The Byungsal Group's reputation has been tarnished."

,A killer organization is finished the moment it looks ridiculous. Well, don't worry, the Byungsal Group leader won't kill him. You're the one who will kill him."

,I will wait, hoping that time comes as soon as possible."

Choo Jeo gnashed his teeth and left for the training ground.

A few minutes after Choo Jeo left, Sun Haedo slammed his fist down, shattering the table.

,Yeom Guseok... You fucking bastard...."

Yeom Guseok provoked Choo Jeo loudly in front of the Sun Family's main gate. It was proof that Yeom Guseok was disregarding the Sun Family. Sun Haedo calmed his seething stomach, imagining Yeom Guseok dying miserably a few days later.

•••

So, Sohyeop, young hero! I'm sorry! The pavilion master has ordered that Sohyeop not be allowed into the brothel.

"......."

,I'm sorry!!"

The men belonging to the Black Faction, led by the brothel's manager, bowed to me at a 90-degree angle.

This was the third time I had been denied entry to the brothel.

The reason was the accident I caused at the inn earlier today. They didn't want to let a Jianghu person who was likely to cause trouble into the store.

I turned around without saying a word.

The Sun Family was watching me closely, and the Demonic Cult's watcher was paying attention to my movements. Causing a ruckus at the brothel without any justification would be extremely detrimental in the future.

I walked through the entertainment district, which had started its business in the evening, and thought.

,Should I go to a whorehouse for the first time in a while? It won't be as good as the high-end courtesans in the brothel, but... Still, expensive prostitutes are quite beautiful, so...'

Suddenly.

I stopped walking and grabbed the wrist of a boy passing by me. The boy, who looked to be in his early teens, was holding a sharp dagger. If I had reacted even a little later, my stomach would have been stabbed.

,...He hid his presence perfectly. How did you notice?"

The voice was that of a child, like his appearance. But there was a sense of ruthlessness in his tone and atmosphere.

,I'm glad I've been maintaining my Heavenly Eye since this afternoon.'

I was scanning my surroundings with my Heavenly Eye. It was a little tiring, but there was nothing better as a countermeasure against assassins.

,Do you think I can't kill you because you look like a child?"

I put strength into my grip, intending to crush the boy's wrist. But before that, the boy's wrist slipped out of my grasp. It was a strange sensation. It was as if the boy's wrist had suddenly turned into smoke and disappeared.

,A fleeting moment.'

Sangee Do Beop (斬鬼刀法, Demon Cutting Sword Technique) Akcham (惡斬, Evil Cut).

I cut the boy without question. A fountain of blood didn't gush out of the boy's body. Instead, the boy's figure distorted and disappeared like a phantom.

-The Byungsal Group is watching you! Fall into the fear of death and be afraid!

His telepathic message remained in my ear.

"......."

Pain surged from my right arm, which had been injured by the assassin last night. It was the price for forcing my arm to move. It was starting to bother me, so I used Complete Recovery.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

Not only did my arm recover, but the fatigue that had accumulated in my body without me knowing disappeared. Feeling much refreshed, I walked out of the city.
