,Seong Yujin is strong."

Choi Jeong-hwa pointed at Seong Hari with her chin.

Seong Hari's mood lightened compared to before.

Kwaaaaaaaang!

,...Why are you looking at me? I didn't do anything."

Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang kwakwa kwang!

,Even if he's out of the ordinary, there's a limit.... How did you educate your son? As an educator, I really want to know."

It was something that happened just 10 seconds after the 1-on-1 sparring began. At first, I was going to end it with one lightning strike... but since he was sturdy, he easily endured the lightning. When he endured the second lightning strike, I struck him with seven consecutive lightning strikes.

,Stop!"

The supervisor intervened. He quickly gestured to the staff who were waiting. They took the unconscious Ma Jin-bae to the medical room. He wouldn't have any injuries even if he was unconscious. Because the Aurora Simulator is installed in this stadium.

,Yes."

My next match is in an hour. After returning to the waiting room, I watched the next match through the installed monitor.

Lee Si-eun VS Bae Seo-na

Bae Seo-na is a magician belonging to Class 4 of the first grade. The magic she mainly uses is fire-based, and her admission ranking is 54th.

Bae Seo-na was smiling confidently at Lee Si-eun. She must have investigated Lee Si-eun in advance. In general, energy control ability is treated as a lower version of a magician, so she must be confident of victory.

,If it's an ordinary energy controller, that's true."

The supervisor blew the whistle. It was a whistle signaling the start of the sparring.

Bae Seo-na moved in a textbook manner. First, she creates distance while casting magic. When the casting is finished, she attacks immediately without hesitation. She doesn't just stand still after the attack. She continues to cast the next spell.

Lee Si-eun gestured towards the fireball flying towards her. The direction of the fireball was reversed and flew towards Bae Seo-na. Bae Seo-na, who was flustered, couldn't react quickly. That was a mistake. She was hit by the fireball she had launched, and then Lee Si-eun's lightning struck Bae Seo-na.

The battle ended simply enough to be boring.

,Bae Seo-na's carelessness was also a big factor. Well, even if she wasn't careless, Lee Si-eun would have won."

Bae Seo-na, who is a magician, can't beat Lee Si-eun. It would be a different story if she was an experienced magician.

,Seeing this, I don't have to worry about Lee Si-eun."

My next opponent was Kim Cheon-woo.

I looked around. The stadium was full of first-year students. Everyone was watching this match. A showdown between the 2nd and 5th ranked students. It was a big match that was not easy to see.

Kim Cheon-woo held a greatsword in both hands and pointed it at me.

,I know I can't beat you. But I'll do my best. I'll do my best in this sparring. So, please do your best too."

,Sure."

Bbi-iiiiiiik!

The whistle sounded.

I aimed the RPG-7 I was holding at Kim Cheon-woo and pulled the trigger. The rocket spewed fire and flew towards Kim Cheon-woo. Kim Cheon-woo set his greatsword to the side and charged forward.

Kwaaaang!

An explosion occurred. Kim Cheon-woo, who came out through the smoke of the explosion, swung his greatsword at me. The greatsword with sword energy cut through the air with a whoosh. A gap created by the large movement. I dug into that gap.

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 6]

,The faster I end the sparring, the more I stand out and the higher my score goes."

So I had no intention of dragging it out. The spear in my hand extends in a straight line towards Kim Cheon-woo's solar plexus. Then suddenly Kim Cheon-woo's body rotates. The rotation was too fast. If I stay like this, I'll get sliced by the greatsword. I quickly backed away.

,He didn't suddenly rotate. He was preparing for me to come inside... he was rotating his body from the beginning."

I clicked my tongue. Kim Cheon-woo had prepared a countermeasure against ,Instant'.

,He knew I used Instant often. Well, it's not surprising. I used Instant often while practicing."

It is difficult to avoid Instant at close range. It is even more impossible if there is a difference in skill. But if you know that I'm going to use Instant and prepare for it, the story changes. Kim Cheon-woo's response to Instant was very good. He deserves to be praised.

,But what about the second and third times?"

[Using Instant (Moment). Remaining stacks: 5]

I narrow the distance again with Instant. In the slowed-down world, Kim Cheon-woo's eyes shook. He reacted to Instant. His greatsword shook slightly and headed towards me.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 4]

I dodged the greatsword by a hair's breadth and stabbed the spear into Kim Cheon-woo's neck. Kim Cheon-woo staggered from the impact and fell to the ground.

,...You didn't even give me time to do my best."

,So you can't accept defeat?"

,It's the opposite. I lost. I admit that I can't easily beat you with my current self. But it will be different next time."

Kim Cheon-woo's eyes are burning fiercely. To be honest, it was really annoying. I even get annoyed when a guy clings to me. If Kim Cheon-woo didn't have value as a weapon for the Great Demon, I would have thoroughly trampled on him.

,...Work hard."

Kkaang!

,Her judgment is quick."

All I had to do was throw 2-3 spears in a row to prevent her from dodging.

The more she was hit by the lightning energy, the slower Ryu Hana's movements became. And all the spears in my hand disappeared.

Ttak!

,What?!"

I continued to flick my fingers as if laying down the beat for her dancing.

Ttak! Ttak ttak! Ttak ttak ttak!

With the sound, lightning flashed and 12 spears moved as if they were alive, attacking Ryu Hana.

,This works."

I was surprised even after doing it myself. Because it was quite difficult to handle magnetic force in the past.

,Is it because I have a lot of mana? Or is it simply because I've grown?"

,The source of power is you! If I can just knock you down...!"

Ttaak!

Five spears flew and hit Ryu Hana's back.

That was the end.

,I can't believe I lost like this... it's impossible...!"

,Heh heh. Try harder next time."
