[You're openly picking a fight with Kwon Sang-hoon-gun. Do you have a plan?]

Kang Ji-young-ssi asked me, holding her smartphone. I had just called Seong Hari-ssi and told her that Kwon Sang-hoon-gun was threatening me.

It wasn't exactly wrong. Kwon Sang-hoon-gun's secretary had come to me and threatened me to come to the Dean's office. I felt threatened, so it was a threat. Knowing Seong Hari-ssi's personality, she'll go straight to Kwon Sang-hoon-gun and flip everything upside down.

The reason Seong Hari-ssi had been relatively quiet until now was because Kang Ji-young-ssi, who could control Seong Hari-ssi to some extent, was the Academy Dean. An S-class Hunter and Seong Hari-ssi's friend. But Kwon Sang-hoon-gun was neither an S-class Hunter nor Seong Hari-ssi's friend.

,Plan? Me becoming the king of the Academy?'

I didn't really care whether Kwon Sang-hoon-gun became the Academy Dean or not. But as Dean, he's trying to control me? A nobody? I couldn't stand it.

[...The Academy isn't a kingdom. And if there's a king, isn't Kwon Sang-hoon-gun already the king?]

,Power comes from others. If you don't get recognition from others, you have no power or anything. If you have power, you can forcibly pull out power... but Kwon Sang-hoon-gun isn't even that.'

Kwon Sang-hoon-gun was an A-class Hunter in his younger days, but he doesn't have any notable achievements. If I had to pick one, it would be that he's worked at the Academy for 20 years. However, those alone are far from enough achievements.

On the other hand, the former Dean, Kang Ji-young-ssi, was an S-class Hunter and had many heroic achievements. Because of the comparison, Kwon Sang-hoon-gun pushed ahead with reforms.

[When you become the Academy Dean, external pressure comes from all over. I was able to withstand it... but it must have been difficult for Kwon Sang-hoon-gun. Kwon Sang-hoon-gun's reforms are also a kind of survival tactic.]

,I guess so. But the important thing is that he's come into conflict with me.'

I have no intention of assassinating Kwon Sang-hoon-gun. If I kill Kwon Sang-hoon-gun, an outsider could be appointed as Dean of the Academy. The variables become bigger. But I can't bow down to Kwon Sang-hoon-gun either.

,There are many ways to subdue Kwon Sang-hoon-gun.'

First, I need to completely control the Academy students. Haven't I already controlled them enough? Even if it looks that way for now, there was a limit to fear.

,Unless I'm the king of a country. The Academy isn't a country. I can't do overwhelming fear politics through executions.'

If Kwon Sang-hoon-gun decided to intervene, cracks could appear at any time.

"2nd year students, assemble tonight."

I said. The students in the same class nearby are startled.

"Y-Yujin-ah? Did we do something wrong?"

"It's nothing like that. Everyone assemble. Tell Ryu Hana-ssi to come too. If I tell her to come, she'll come."

• • •

Night in the park near the dormitory. The 2nd year students gathered there, hidden by a barrier. With about 280 people gathered, including transfer students, the atmosphere was a bit noisy.

Standing in front of them, I spoke with mana in my voice.

"Attention."

The students' gazes turned to me in an instant.

"The reason I gathered you tonight is because there's something I want to show you."

There's no need to beat around the bush. I took something out of my inventory.

Dragon Heart obtained from the [Dragon Hunter] world.

I have 73,842 Wyrm-class Dragon Hearts, 21,801 Dragon-class Dragon Hearts, 72 Ancient Dragon-class Dragon Hearts, and 1 Dragon King-class Dragon Heart. Among these, I take out one Wyrm-class Dragon Heart. I have Dragon Heart Blood in a similar amount.

Iron Horse Dragon's Heart.

"This heart is a kind of elixir. If someone with compatibility eats it, they can gain the ability to turn their skin into steel. If you're very lucky, you can even gain the ability to manipulate iron itself."

Everyone is surprised.

Ryu Hana-ssi, Choi Da-yeon-ssi, Kim Chun-woo-gun, Clara-ssi, and others, who are the mainstays of the 2nd year's golden generation, were no exception.

"An elixir that can give you abilities just by eating it?"

"I've never heard of it. Is it a lie?"

"No. That's real."

Some students walk forward as if possessed. You can tell they're half-crazy just by looking at their eyes. They are instinctively drawn to the Dragon Heart. These are all people who have compatibility with the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart.

"Stop."

A voice filled with mana awakened them.

"Heok...!"

"Y-Yujin-ah. Can't you give me that Dragon Heart?"

"No! I want to have it! How much do you want?!"

The students with compatibility cried out. I shook my head firmly.

"I don't sell Dragon Hearts for money. I look at loyalty and merit towards me."

Just talking isn't impactful enough.

I chose one person among those with compatibility.

"Kim I-joong-gun. You've worked hard and done well on the things I've asked you to do. This is a reward."

A male student smiles with joy and steps forward. He was a shuttle who usually listened to me without complaint.

『Name: Kim I-joong

Strength: C- Stamina: D- Agility: D Durability: C- Mana: E

Trait: Silent Step (D)

Skill: Close Combat (C), Sprint (C+)

Trust: 21 』

Trust 21. It was too low to be called loyalty.

,It doesn't matter. It's not like I wanted loyalty that came from the heart.'

The important thing is to show everyone here. That if you follow me, you can receive tremendous rewards.

"Eat it here now."

"A, alright. Thank you. Yujin-ah."

Kim I-joong-gun carefully chewed and ate the Dragon Heart. Immediately after, mana flowed out of his body. Also, his muscles seemed to be getting stronger.

"No way! I'm overflowing with power! And... Steelification!!"

Kim I-joong-gun's skin turned into steel. All the 2nd year students were shocked.

[Where did you get something like that again?!]

Kang Ji-young-ssi was also surprised.

Regardless, I checked Kim I-joong-gun's status window.

『Name: Kim I-joong

Strength: C+ Stamina: C Agility: C- Durability: B- Mana: D-

Trait: Silent Step (D), Steelification (D-), Iron Manipulation (B)

Skill: Close Combat (C), Sprint (C+)

Trust: 56 』

The stats increased far too much for just taking one Wyrm-class Dragon Heart. Leaving aside the suddenly skyrocketing trust, two traits were added.

,Not only did he get Steelification, but he also got Iron Manipulation? And it's a Dragon Heart that wasn't even buffed?'

Dragon Hearts are items from another world. Items from other worlds basically have their effects reduced.

,The Dragon Hunter world and the Savior of the Academy world are somehow a good match, so the reduced effect was small? If not, Kim I-joong-gun himself is an extremely good match for the Iron Horse Dragon's Dragon Heart.'

Originally, in the [Dragon Hunter] world, the higher the compatibility, the more the effect of the Dragon Heart was maximized.

I look at Kim I-joong-gun. Around his steelified body, the iron sand on the ground was spinning around him. It's the Iron Manipulation (B) ability.

"It's real! He really gained abilities just by eating that!"

"His physical abilities and mana have even gotten stronger!"

"It's more effective than the elixirs sold on the market!"

The students' gazes of jealousy and greed turned to Kim I-joong-gun. Kim I-joong-gun calmly accepted that gaze and raised the corners of his mouth. Unlike before, he looked full of confidence.

He was in the 200s, but with one Dragon Heart, he reached the level of the 30s. A D-class Hunter had instantly reached the level of a B-class Hunter, surpassing C-class.

,Even I'm surprised looking at it. The other students are no different.'

I took out other types of Dragon Hearts.

For today alone, I gave 30 Wyrm-class Dragon Hearts to selected students. More than half were women. It couldn't be helped that my heart went out to my sex partners.

"I have more Dragon Hearts. I will continue to give them to those who listen to me well and build merit."

Choi Da-yeon-ssi, Ryu Hana-ssi, and Kim Chun-woo-gun also looked at me with sparkling eyes. However, Clara-ssi was fiddling with her smartphone as if she wasn't interested. Clara-ssi, who is a famous singer, had almost no obsession with power compared to other students.

,I should be able to control the 1st year students in the same way.'

• • •

Go Young-jin-gun, who was ranked 1st in the 1st year entrance rankings, dropped out.

It was a pretty big deal, but the Academy was quiet because Go Young-jin-gun's influence was virtually non-existent.

The 1st year students felt fear towards me and at the same time envied the Dragon Hearts. They even engaged in loyalty competition to obtain Dragon Hearts.

What's worrying is Go Young-jin-gun's personality. With that guy's personality, he's not the type to just take it. He's the type to take revenge by moving his father or whatever.

,If he comes at me directly, I'll smash him. If he moves his father, I can move Seong Hari-ssi.'

It didn't seem like I would be harmed no matter what Go Young-jin-gun did.

,It's almost time.'

I got up from my seat and headed towards the auditorium. I'll go in through the back door and go straight onto the stage.

Today is the Academy Student Council President election day. I was the only election candidate.

,Unlike the Student Council President of the Japanese Academy next door, the Student Council President of Maruhan Academy is closer to a honorary position with no real power.'

The Student Council President of Maruhan Academy was close to an honorary position. The Student Council President is also elected by the 2nd year students and remains in office for 2 years until the 3rd year students graduate. In terms of authority alone, the Head of the Discipline Department would be stronger than the Student Council President. There was a reason why the Student Council President had no presence at all when I was in the 1st year.

,Even if there's no real power, there's one concept that's clear. The Student Council President represents the students of the Academy.'

Student representative.

That alone was enough to give the title of Student Council President sufficient value.

I went up onto the auditorium stage.

Looking ahead, the 1st and 2nd year students were sitting densely in chairs. Even the faculty members around them were watching me. There were no 3rd year students. The 3rd year students, who are graduating, don't attend these events well. The 3rd year students became busier because of Kwon Sang-hoon-gun's reforms.

I stood in front of the prepared podium and said.

"Hello. I'm Seong Yujin. I'm going to become the Student Council President of Maruhan Academy. Since time is precious, let's start the vote right away. Those who approve of my, Seong Yujin's, inauguration as Student Council President, please raise your right hand."

Cheo cheo cheok!

All the Academy students gathered in the auditorium raised their right hands.

I was elected Student Council President with 100% of the vote.

"Thank you. According to your wishes, I announce that I have become the 22nd Student Council President of Maruhan Academy as of today. Now, lower your hands."

Cheo cheo cheok!

All the students lowered their hands like knives.

I slowly looked around from the podium. Outsiders were embarrassed, and faculty members accepted it as if it were natural. Seong Hari-ssi, sitting in front, was looking at me with a proud expression. And Kwon Sang-hoon-gun's expression was clearly flustered.

Originally, the vote was supposed to be conducted by electronic voting, not a show of hands. I was the one who changed the voting method on the day, and I didn't tell Kwon Sang-hoon-gun on purpose.

Clap.

I clapped on the podium.

Clap clap clap.

When I clapped, the students started clapping all at once.

Clap clap clap clap clap clap clap clap clap clap!

Even when I stopped clapping, the students didn't stop. I listened to the applause for 3 minutes and said.

"Stop."

The applause stops all at once.

"Students, please return to your classrooms and concentrate on your studies."

The students left the auditorium like a tide.

Only I, the faculty, and some outsiders remained in the auditorium.
