,Sesco's here, you bug bastards!"

I shouted bravely at the monster spiders. I quickly scanned the number of monster spiders. There were about 20, including the queen spider, which was even bigger than I thought.

,Only 20? No way.'

There was no time to look for the rest.

The queen spider saw us and screamed.

,Kigigigigigigig!!!'

The monster spiders, except for the queen spider, charged at us all at once.

,Fire!"

As soon as I shouted, the security team and the gangsters pulled the trigger and poured out bullets. The combat drones did the same. They quickly approached the building's windows and poured down a hail of bullets. The windows were shattered by the bullets and shards of glass flew everywhere.

The light diffusely reflected on the glass flashed all around for a moment, and the sound of gunfire filled the space.

The spiders that were running at us became bullet-riddled and fell. Whitish-gray goo dripped from their bodies. With thousands of bullets pouring down at once, even monster spiders couldn't withstand it.

But the queen spider didn't fall. The bullets couldn't penetrate its rock-hard body. Still, it didn't seem to be completely unaffected, as there were scratches that were only superficial wounds.

-No way. To be so intact after taking that hail of bullets... Her skin must be harder than steel plate....

Na Chaeyoung's surprised voice came through the radio. I walked forward.

,Anyway, only the queen spider is left, so I can just kill it."

,Kigigigigik!'

The queen spider screamed. It wasn't just a threatening sound.

The ceiling seemed to shake, then suddenly collapsed and dozens of monster spiders fell.

,Seong Yujin-ssi! The elevator! The elevator doors opened and the spider bastards came out! They were ambushing in the elevator shaft!"

Gu Taehee shouted urgently. It wasn't hard to imagine the monster spiders clinging to the walls of the elevator shaft. The spiders didn't charge right away, but prioritized formation.

We were surrounded. Monster spiders occupied the front and back. They were waiting for the queen spider's command.

,Dr. Na. Were those bastards on the roof? The drones have cameras, so there's no way you wouldn't have seen them."

-I rewound the video and checked. There was a large outdoor unit on the roof... and there was a black cloth covering the inside of the unit. I thought it was a shadow, but they were hiding under the cloth.

Na Chaeyoung's voice trembled more and more. I knew how she felt. It's that feeling when someone you thought was nothing hits you in the back of the head.

,I have to admit it. That bastard was one step ahead of me. But I'm the one who wins in the end."

,Kigigigigik!'

At the same time as the queen spider shouted, the spiders charged all at once.

,Fight if you don't want to die!"

I ordered the security team and took out a Bister II from my pocket and chewed it.

-I'll provide support from this side too.

The combat drones that were flying outside charged in and attacked the monster spiders head-on. They flew through the air and supported the most endangered people.

The effect was immediate. My body heated up and I was overflowing with power. Boom boom! With the sound of my heart beating, I ran forward towards the queen spider.

A few monster spiders blocked my path as if to protect the queen spider. Pajik. The cutlass sword shone silver.

,Get out of the way!"

Thunderclap Style (雷天流) Thunder Flash (雷光).

I drew a line from top to bottom. The bisected body of the monster spider split in half. A new monster spider popped out from between them and thrust its sharp legs forward. It aimed precisely for my eyes. But I was faster than it.

Seogeok!

I raised my sword and cut off its legs and neck at once, and jumped up using its collapsing corpse as a stepping stone.

Two monster spiders rushed at me from both sides as if they had been waiting. I shifted the center of my body's weight and placed my feet on the ceiling.

Thunderclap Style (雷天流) Gale Lightning (疾风迅雷).

I temporarily strengthened my body by imbuing it with thunder.

Thunderclap Style (雷天流) Flying Thunder God (飛雷神).

I kicked off the ceiling and jumped towards them. Using the Flying Thunder God, I could move at high speed without restriction, whether in a narrow passage or in the open air.

I ignored the guys rushing at me from both sides and passed them by. I instantly approached the queen spider and swung my sword to cut off its head.

It opened its mouth. To bite me? No. It was too far away for that. Light flashed from its hideous mouth. The red light was fire.

Hwaaaaaaaa!

Red flames poured out. I couldn't avoid its unexpected superpower. I hurriedly swung my sword to cut off its head, but it sacrificed one of its legs to knock my sword away. It lost a leg, and I faced the flames head-on.

I leaned my body diagonally, put my left shoulder forward, and took a step back to create distance. The flames didn't have a long range. About 1.5m at most. Considering it came out of its mouth, I could aim for the side or back.

,Can it use it continuously?'

The queen spider approached. I couldn't move. My left arm and left leg were a mess. My left arm was charred black and wouldn't move, and my leg was less so, but it was hard to move right away. Perhaps because my left arm and left leg burned so quickly, I couldn't feel any sensation at all, including pain.

,Recover!'

I focused my mind on my left leg. I could feel a little bit of sensation, as if it was working. But it was too late. The queen spider was coming at me to finish me off.

Pajijijijijik.

Lightning surged along the blade. Even if my left arm and left leg wouldn't move, I could still use lightning.

,I'll aim for when it attacks.'

I was calculating the angle to land the best counterattack when a mace flew from afar and struck the queen spider's head. The queen spider couldn't withstand the force and fell to the side, writhing. I looked towards where the mace had come from and saw Gu Taehee. Gu Taehee, who was very excited, was tearing off the heads of monster spiders with her bare hands.

,She's tough. Did she eat a Bister II.'

The queen spider got up.

I also got up from my seat. Thanks to Gu Taehee, my left leg had regenerated. I took a stance and swung my lightning-crackling sword.

Thunderclap Style (雷天流) Lightning Flash (雷闪).

The sword energy of lightning flew and cut through the queen spider's body. It didn't go in as deep as I thought. The queen spider's skin was even harder. Maybe the mace would be more effective.

,Kiaaaaaaak!"

The queen spider pointed the end of its abdomen towards me and shot out a spider web.

,Slow.'

It was flying straight at me. There was no reason I couldn't avoid it. I simply dodged the spider web and took out a white battery from my pants pocket with my now-moving left hand. It was a special battery that Na Chaeyoung had made for me. When I squeezed the special battery tightly, it shone silver. Pajijik. Electricity sparked from the battery.

,Kigigigik!"

The spider queen shouted. The monster spiders that were fighting with the others hurriedly ran and blocked my path. The queen spider was running towards the open window. It was using the monster spiders as bait to escape on its own.

,Tsk."

I clicked my tongue and moved forward. I cut down the monster spiders and reached the window. The queen spider was climbing down the building wall.

,A spider is a spider, huh.'

I raised my silver shining sword. I didn't have the [Shooting] trait. But the target was big, and the distance wasn't that far. Only about 7-8m. Even I could hit the guy going straight down.

I threw the sword. If I missed, I was going to jump down and finish it. Fortunately, the sword pierced the queen spider's large abdomen.

The queen spider twitched and then jumped towards the ground as it was. It was determined to escape no matter what.

Pajijik.

I gathered the electricity drawn from the battery I was holding in my left hand and concentrated it in my right hand. I gave a trait to the lightning created by completely extracting electricity from the battery and paying the price with my stamina. The trait was one. Physical power.

I lightly bent my elbow to throw the lightning ball at it. I wasn't worried about hitting it. The cutlass sword was stuck in its abdomen. The cutlass sword had the property of absorbing and attracting surrounding electricity. In other words, it could conduct electricity.

I extended my elbow and threw the lightning.

Just like when I aimed at the queen spider from outside the building, the lightning drew a trajectory like a blue ray in the air and fell to the ground.

Kwaaaaaang!

An explosion erupted on the ground with the shockwave. The queen spider lay on the ground. Its large abdomen, which had been pierced, had burst open, leaving only its cephalothorax. It pathetically wriggled its three remaining legs and then stopped as if its life had run out.

Perhaps because of the explosion, the zombies that had been gathered around began to gather. The zombies began to chew and swallow the queen spider's corpse.

,Damn. It's just going to feed the zombies.'

It seemed like it would be okay since what Na Chaeyoung had asked me to bring was the queen spider's web.

,Uwaaaaaa!"

I turned my head at the scream. The monster spiders were still left and fighting back. The queen spider was dead, but the battle was not over. I picked up the mace that Gu Taehee had thrown and threw myself into the carnage to kill the monster spiders. The feel of the mace in my hand wasn't too bad.

One of Gu Taehee's subordinates also died. There were no seriously injured. Gu Taehee was lying on the floor, vomiting from the aftereffects of Bister II. Thanks to my recovery, I was fine even after the effects of the drug wore off. The problem was that I was building up a tolerance... but I decided to think about that later.

I wandered around to get the queen spider's web to give to Na Chaeyoung. Most of the things in the battle area were a mess, so I went inside to find something relatively intact.

And I found the queen spider's storage.

She had wrapped her prey in spider webs and hung them from the ceiling as cocoons.

,These must be the people the spider bastards kidnapped. There are quite a few. About 30.'

They were all dead.

I couldn't hear a single breath.

,Is this the queen spider's web? It's said to have excellent heat resistance, so I'll know if I burn it with a lighter.'

I was planning to take all the spider webs, so I held a piece of glass in my hand and tore the spider webs. The more research samples, the better.

,I guess you could call this a jackpot.'
