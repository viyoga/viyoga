The Empire has declared war on Hell.

This is different from the Volcano Island campaign. Back then, there were restrictions. This time, there are none. Troops will be conscripted and they will march into Hell.

The nobles will have to earn merit if only to protect their honor, and the Legions must earn at least some small merit if they wish to remain active within the Empire. The Legions cannot avoid this war. The moment they avoid it, they become enemies of the Empire.

The Emperor also requested cooperation from powers outside the Empire. The Heavenly Demon Cult, for instance.

'The justification is for the sake of the Atlantis continent, not the Empire. Refuse to cooperate and you become a public enemy of the world. Not that I had any intention of refusing anyway. If we don't stop Ragnarok, Atlantis is doomed.'

The Heavenly Demon Cult will march into Hell's territory. The gains from dominating a Hell zone were not insignificant.

'This time, even the Empire's Five Emperors are moving.'

It was a passage that revealed just how serious the Emperor was about this campaign.

Afterward, I met with Kang Myungjin separately.

"Kang Myungjin. What do you think about the Hell contribution for this operation? Was Ragnarok in anything you know?"

"In the original work I know, Ragnarok doesn't happen. I never predicted we'd end up attacking Hell zones this aggressively. Nor that you'd prophesy Ragnarok."

In the original work I knew, there was no Ragnarok either.

"Do you think it'll succeed?"

"If it's about stopping Ragnarok... the odds are high. That said, conquering all of Hell's zones won't be possible. Hell's zones aren't a pushover."

"Will the campaign start right away?"

"No. We start after thorough preparation. Since Hell's zones aren't a pushover, we'll need at least two months of prep. What about you? Will you move as the Heavenly Demon?"

Kang Myungjin knew that the Heavenly Demon was another one of my identities.

I had three options.

The Heavenly Demon, the A-Plan Legion, the Valdert family.

If forced to pick one, I should move as the Heavenly Demon. For the Heavenly Demon Cult to attack Hell, the Heavenly Demon—the face of the faction—must be present. The A-Plan Legion and the Valdert family have representatives other than me, so I can simply put my name on the roster. Kang Myungjin knows this, which is why he's asking.

"This time, I'll move all three."

"Will you be alright? That looks busier than my own schedule."

"I'll be fine. You and Elena just need to coordinate the schedules to some degree."

"Understood. I'll take it into consideration. I don't want to lose you as a strategic asset either. We'll need to meet with you and the Phantom Duke later to finalize the exact schedule."

And so our brief conversation ended.

• • •

The biggest problem with attacking Hell's zones is that you can't easily get in, and you can't easily get out.

The reason Elena and I fell into Hell was also because of the World Rift (僞). When leaving Hell, we had no choice but to exit through Niflheim.

Unlike back then, as considerable time has passed, several methods for entering and exiting Hell's zones have been discovered. Though they're barely at the level of sneaking through a rat hole.

'The army's too big to use workarounds. So they'll attack Niflheim first—the gateway to Hell's zones and its gatekeeper.'

That's the orthodox approach. There are other entry zones, but the only currently known one is Niflheim.

The problem is that Hell's demons know this too. Therefore, the likelihood that Hell's forces will gather at Niflheim is high.

'If that happens, humanity has no choice but to suffer. A full-scale war puts us at a disadvantage.'

In a one-on-one fight between an ordinary soldier and an ordinary demon soldier, the demon has the upper hand. And to fight in Niflheim, where we don't even hold dominion? The first battle alone will produce more casualties than expected.

On top of that, Niflheim has Hel.

'If a living being dies in Niflheim, there's a high chance they become one of Hel's soldiers—an undead.'

Fortunately, the Empire isn't stupid, so they'll take sufficient preparation time and try to avoid an all-out war in Niflheim as much as possible. Though if there's still no answer, they'll end up fighting in Niflheim anyway.

'I'm different.'

I hold dominion over the 9,371st zone—the Drought Hell—one of Hell's territories.

'A Hell Baron who rules over a Hell zone despite being human, not a demon.'

Hell zones can't be reached with teleportation scrolls even if you have the coordinates. Even though they exist on the same continent, the System's barrier blocks the boundary between Hell and the mortal world.

'The same goes for me, the rightful ruler of Hell.'

In truth, I hadn't set foot in Drought Hell even once since then. Naturally—teleportation scrolls don't work. Getting out is quite the problem too.

There was no reason to go. Nothing to gain, no beauties either. Just earning 80,000 AP every month as the price of ruling the Drought zone was profit enough.

'There is a way. The question is whether the System will interfere.'

I went to find Elena.

"...So. You're asking me to send you to the Drought zone with my illusion magic?"

"That's right. With your illusion magic, it's possible, isn't it?"

"If you're asking whether it's possible—it is. It's a place I've been to once before, and it left quite an impression on me... And with Full Recovery, there's no worry about lifespan. But I don't want to send my husband into the middle of a Hell zone. Have you forgotten how much we struggled to get out of there?"

"Elena. We need to move now. If things go as I expect, it won't be that dangerous."

"There's no guarantee things will go according to your plan. If they don't, you'll have to get out through Niflheim again."

"That's not a problem either. I'm stronger now than I was then. And if worst comes to worst, you can help me."

"...Mmm. I don't like it, but I don't see you failing. Fine. Do you want me to send you right now?"

She waved her hand through the air.

A single blue butterfly appeared and fluttered gently toward me. The butterfly, flying with elegance, touched my chest.

Regardless of my mind, my senses were thrown into disarray. Because I trusted Elena, I didn't resist.

The once-clear boundary between reality and illusion crumbled, and their positions inverted.

The illusion of spatial teleportation—something that should never exist, that merely deceives the senses—blanketed reality and became phenomenon.

When my senses returned, I was already standing on Hell's sand.

「Entering the 9,371st zone, Drought Hell.」

「The curse of Drought Hell does not apply to its ruler.」

「The ruler can control the curse of Drought Hell.」

Before my eyes was a lush forest that didn't match the name Drought Hell. These were the trees I had planted to dominate this zone.

'Every Hell zone imposes a penalty on attackers.'

Drought Hell's curse is dehydration, restricted water intake, and a random stat decrease every 24 hours. Without preparation, it's an incredibly irritating penalty.

I took the Mirror Tunnel out of my inventory.

「Mirror Tunnel

A pair of mirrors.

When activated, allows spatial movement through the mirrors.

Rank: SS」

Unlike teleportation scrolls that require stored coordinates, once installed, it can be used permanently. It was a kind of warp gate.

I poured hot water onto the mirror's surface. The sole condition for activating the Mirror Tunnel. The mirror's surface warped and connected to the other side. The mirror reflected not me, but an empty room.

The connected location was the Heavenly Demon Cult's palace.

'Connection established, at least.'

I stepped into the mirror. I emerged into the room. Instead of Drought Hell's hot, dry air, cool air welcomed me. I immediately returned to the Drought Hell zone.

'It worked! Just as I expected—teleportation scrolls are blocked, but the Mirror Tunnel isn't!'

With this, I could freely travel between Hell's zones and the mortal world.

However, I couldn't just leave the Mirror Tunnel installed in one place. It wasn't safe here. A passing demon could attack and shatter it.

'The best option is to carry it with me for safety.'

I put the Mirror Tunnel back into my inventory and moved through the Drought Hell zone.

There wasn't a single artificial structure like a castle. Most of the demonic beasts roaming Hell's zones are low-tier—low intelligence and weak. Still, they're far stronger than ordinary human soldiers.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido and stepped before the demonic beasts. A 1-meter demonic beast resembling a lizard immediately lowered its head in a posture of submission.

"So you're not attacking because I'm the ruler of this zone, and instead submitting. Hey. Roll over once."

The beast only rolled its eyes quietly, gauging my mood. It seemed less like it was refusing my command and more like it didn't understand. That's possible. Just looking at it, its brain is tiny and its intelligence seems low.

"See that tree? Attack it."

"Krrrrr."

The beast directed hostility at the tree I pointed to, then ran over and swung its claws and tail. The tree shattered and fell to the ground. The beast chewed on the fallen trunk with its mouth.

"Its strength and speed are no joke. An ordinary human would die without even resisting. Hey. Come here."

The beast ran to me.

After testing various things, the result was that the beast understands simple commands like attack, move, and stop. But if a command gets even slightly complex, it can't comprehend it. For example, a command to attack using only its tail.

"Useless except for Attack. I'll grant you the honor of becoming my AP."

I split the motionless beast in half and killed it.

「You have slain a demonic beast belonging to Hell's Drought. You do not gain AP.」

"Damn it. So AP copying doesn't work either."

I clicked my tongue and moved on.

After wandering for nearly three hours, I confirmed there wasn't a single demonic beast or demon with human-level intelligence. And I discovered the entrance to Hell's Rest Area. Since I'd opened the entrance in the past, I could go right in.

「Will you enter the main building of Hell's Rest Area?」

I entered.

「Inside Hell's Rest Area, 1 AP is consumed every 10 minutes.」

Beneath a gray sky, a city reeking of musty odor appeared. The black brick houses were gloomy, and a pale fog draped over the city.

I moved to Hell's Shop at the center of this city—dark even in broad daylight.

「The Gentlemanly Demon (僞) welcomes you.」

"Welcome. This is Hell's Shop, where everything except what we don't have is available."

A demon in the form of a middle-aged human man greeted me. Two horns sprouted from his head, and a demon's tail was visible behind his back.

"Don't give me orders."

“……?”

I walked toward him very slowly.
