,Don't you want to make a contract with me? But you must pay the price for entering my territory... The price is your life!"

Amo charged at me.

I calmly used an instant to avoid his charge. I had predicted Amo's actions to some extent. Amo's contract terms were closer to demands that he could make because he had no intention of contracting with me at all.

If he really intended to contract with me, he would have offered reasonable terms.

,Amo-nim! What are you doing?! He is my student!"

Kalles shouted.

,Just because he's your student doesn't mean he's my contractor. I miss human flesh... This guy's flesh will definitely be the best..."

Amo's long body seemed to soar into the sky, then dived straight into the darkness of the floor. It didn't even take 3 seconds for his huge body to disappear.

My golden eyes moved incessantly. I could see Amo's movements crawling in the dark.

,...What you said earlier wasn't a joke."

,Didn't I tell you I'm bad at jokes? I haven't made a joke today."

Amo's words echoed from the darkness. Kalles bit her lower lip slightly.

,...Amo-nim. Please. Just let me and my student go."

,Kalles. Stay still. I have no intention of killing you or eating you. My only purpose is to devour the blood of that traitor. Ah, if I devour him, I'll surely... become stronger. I'm looking forward to it."

Amo's body appeared from the darkness.

Behind me.

I summoned Stormbreaker in the form of a longsword in my right hand. Blue Sword energy and lightning run along the sword.

,Die, centipede."

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

A blue streak cuts through the giant centipede's body. The moment Amo's severed body was about to fall to the ground, Amo's body reattached as if time had been reversed.

,I am darkness. Do you think this kind of attack will work?"

,What a bluff."

The attack worked. The blue lightning remaining on Amo's long body is proof of that. Amo is both darkness and an existing being. And he's materialized to devour me. The attack is definitely working.

,Amo-nim!!"

Kalles ran and stood in front of me. Amo's red eyes narrowed.

,Get out of the way, Kalles. I have no intention of killing you."

,I can't understand Amo-nim's actions! Amo-nim is always calm. What is the real reason you're trying to kill Eugene?!"

,I can smell and sense the traitor from him. I've heard the story. That the traitor had a child. The traitor's bloodline must be eliminated here. This is my last warning. Get out of the way, Kalles."

I realized that the traitor Amo was talking about was Seong Hari. I remember the Spirit King saying that I was a descendant of a traitor.

,Calm down. Eugene isn't the traitor Amo-nim is talking about. This is just a simple tantrum."

,I have to take that tantrum. The traitor ate my friend. I'm trying to swallow him as revenge. As a bonus, I can become stronger. Power and revenge together. Isn't that killing two birds with one stone?"

,You've changed a lot since I last saw you. The Amo-nim I know wouldn't do this. Did you perhaps get another contractor?"

,I did get another contractor. Thanks to him, I learned a lot."

,Who did you contract with...!"

Before Kalles could finish speaking, Amo charged. Kalles's reaction was slightly slow because she didn't expect him to really attack.

,Kalles mustn't get hurt! Instant!'

I grabbed Kalles's shoulder and jumped to the side to avoid Amo's attack.

Click, click. Amo's jaws moved threateningly.

Seugeugeugeu, seugeugeugeu. Amo crawls on the floor and approaches me.

,Moka!!"

I summoned the Thunder Owl, Moka.

,Kkuuk!"

As soon as Moka was summoned, he assessed the situation and scattered lightning everywhere. Amo, who was crawling on the floor, was startled and retreated, hiding in the darkness.

I frowned. I couldn't find Amo even with my spirit eyes. He must have used a special ability comparable to my spirit eyes.

,Amo is wary of Moka.'

It's not good luck. Moka, a lightning spirit, is a top-tier intermediate spirit aiming for advanced, and has an advantage in compatibility over Amo, a darkness spirit. Amo can't take Moka lightly.

,Teacher. Can you get up? Are you hurt anywhere?"

,...There's no way I'd get hurt this easily."

Kalles got up with a gloomy expression. She looked around and bit her lower lip.

,The contract was unilaterally terminated. Amo-nim... no, Amo really intends to kill us."

,Is it possible to unilaterally terminate a contract?"

,It's possible. Because that's what the contract was like."

I don't know the details, but it seems like Amo was in a position of power in the contract.

,What do we do now?"

,Now that Amo is acting like this, there's no way to calm him down. We have to get out of the mountain before sunset."

Wieeeeeeeeng!

Something flew in with an annoying, loud noise. It was a palm-sized dark mosquito. Before I could swing my sword, Kalles reacted. She took out a dagger hidden in her thigh and quickly swung it, cutting down the mosquito. Black Sword energy flickered on the blade of the dagger.

,That's right. Kalles's dagger skills were S-rank.'

An A-class hero and former assassin. She was not a woman I could ignore.

,Let's go this way."

,Isn't that the way back?"

,If it's Amo, he would have prepared traps on the way back. There's no need to fall into a trap on purpose."

,Indeed."

I ran after her.

Amo jumped out. He was getting impatient because we weren't going to the place where he had prepared the traps. Amo aimed for Kalles's legs, not me.

Kalles jumped up and avoided Amo's first surprise attack, but was in danger due to Amo's subsequent attacks.

,Moka!! Lightning Speed!!"

Moka, covered in lightning, flew and collided with Amo's body. Amo's body floated up and hit a tree. Amo's legs twitched all at once as if trembling with anger.

,You goddamn bird...!!"

,Kkuuk, kkuuk!"

Amo wrapped his long body around Moka's body as if coiling. Surprisingly, Moka, who has quite a temper, didn't avoid it. Rather, he took the attack and released electricity in all directions as if retaliating.

Pajijijik, pajijijijik!

Hundreds of streaks of lightning illuminate the darkness.

,Teacher! Let's go!"

,...What? Like this? What about the Thunder Owl?"

,Moka is okay. I'll reverse summon him if it's dangerous."

,I'd rather fight together..."

,Go there?"

Kalles closed her mouth. It was dark where Amo and Moka were. Unintelligent dark spirits wriggle and Amo's legs twitch. Even if he has an advantage in compatibility, Moka is still an intermediate spirit. On the other hand, Amo has been dominating this area for over 200 years. In a 1-on-1 fight, Moka can barely hold on.

Kalles ran towards a place with almost no darkness. I followed her, watching Moka's situation with my spirit eyes.

,Moka. You've worked hard. Go back. You know where the spirit stone I prepared in case of emergency is, right? Eat one and recover.'

Kkuuk.

Moka's weak answer echoed in my head. Moka did well. Thanks to him, Amo suffered fatal damage. He won't be able to chase us recklessly.

,Kalles-sensei. Don't you feel something is strange?"

I watched her carefully. Her face was as hard as a rock. Sweat flowed down her smooth brown skin.

,...Amo is up to something. We can't leave the mountain without Amo's permission."

I took out my smartphone. It wasn't connected to the outside. Amo's power is interfering with the signal.

,...It seems like I can still summon spirits, so I can ask for help.'

I am connected to the spirits. I can explain the situation to Makina and get Seong Hari's help.

,That won't work. Seong Hari will make this incident public. If that happens, Kalles will be fired from her teaching position...'

Kang Ji-young's position, who recommended Kalles as a teacher, will also be affected. In many ways, there is more to lose than to gain. It's best for me and Kalles to solve this here.

,We need to change our approach."

,How?"

,From escape to resistance. It's hard to kill Amo, but... I can inflict a fatal blow. Amo wouldn't want to die either, so he'll let us out."

Kalles quietly raised one hand. Darkness gathered around her and spread in all directions.

I felt two darknesses. Kalles's darkness and Amo's darkness. Amo's darkness is superior, but Kalles's darkness is not weak either.

Kalles has activated her trait, Forest of Darkness (A+).

,I found where Amo is. He seems to be weakened because he fought the Thunder Owl. This is the best opportunity we can't miss. Eugene, I need your help. Your lightning power can inflict fatal damage on Amo. Can you do it?"

,Of course. We have to if we want to live."

,...!"

Kalles's eyes widened. I tilted my head.

,What's wrong?"

,It's nothing. I just... remembered something from the past. It was a similar situation to now."

Kalles didn't say anything more.

I quietly checked her favorability.

『Kalles's Favorability: 70』

『Kalles's Psychology: Daehyun was like this when we first met....』

I could see Kalles's psychology as her favorability reached 70.

,If her favorability is 70, it's a level where marriage is possible... In other words, if the opportunity comes, I can just knock her down...!'

Amo was curled up under a large tree, resting. Scars were visible all over his body. These were the scars he sustained while fighting Moka.

Kalles gestured to me. We planned the operation before arriving here. She would draw Amo's attention, and I would take advantage of the gap to inflict fatal damage on Amo.

Amo's antennae twitched. He must have noticed our presence, as he immediately opened his eyes and stood up.

,Did you come looking for me? Foolish things."

,The sun is up. There's really no hope if it's night, so I just made a rational choice."

,Kalles. I'll give you one last chance in light of our past relationship. Hand him over to me. Then I'm willing to contract with you again."

,...How pathetic. Are you in that bad of shape?"

,You'll need my power, won't you?"

,I don't need it. I'm an academy teacher now."

Kalles ran. At the same time, I also ran to the side, aiming for Amo. Amo seemed to have decided to deal with Kalles first, ignoring me as he moved. It was according to plan. However, the small bugs around Amo rushed at me.

Yeongcheonryu (影天流) Stream.

The sword moved naturally like flowing water. Starting with the closest bug, I cut them down, cut them down, and cut them down again. The bugs were caught in the flow of the sword and died.

The dark bugs didn't leave corpses. When cut by the blue Sword energy, they turned into darkness and disappeared.

,Instant!'

While dealing with the bugs, I suddenly turned my body and aimed for the back of Amo, who was fighting Kalles. Puk. The blade of Stormbreaker was properly embedded in Amo's back.

,You disgusting bastard...!"

,Good job! It was a surprisingly perfect surprise attack!"

Amo and Kalles's reactions were contrasting.

I took a breath and created lightning. Pajijijijik! Lightning ran through the dark centipede's body through the sword.

,Kuaaaaaaaak!"

Amo let out a scream full of pain. Dozens of his legs moved quickly, crawling into the darkness. I gritted my teeth and clung tightly to his back. Sticky darkness clung to my body. The darkness became pressure and tried to shake me off.

It was the limit.

,Moka!!"

,Kkuuuk!"

I summoned Moka, who had consumed the spirit stone and recovered more than half of his strength. Moka tore off Amo's antennae with his sharp claws and dropped lightning without a break.

,N-no...! How can I be so futile... Kuaaaaat!"

Amo tried to escape into the darkness, but Kalles intervened at the right time. She temporarily took control of the darkness, preventing Amo's escape.

Soon, Amo's body was shattered by Moka's lightning claw spikes.
