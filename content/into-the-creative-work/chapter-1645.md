Having obtained Moses' Staff and the Book of the Dead (2), I left the Ramesseum.

Knowing where the Book of the Dead (3) was, I moved without hesitation. The sight of hundreds of giant frogs, giant flies, and giant locust undead moving together was spectacular.

,There's no time to stop. Walk faster, faster."

Time was not on my side.

I knew that fact better than anyone.

,The ruler of the Golden Desert knows my location. He already sent an army once, and they were defeated by me. This time, he'll send an army twice as large. If I stay still, I'll be done for.'

I know how big the Golden Desert is. If Hatep, the ruler of the Golden Desert, decides to gather an army and march out... there's no way to win in a head-on fight right now.

,I need to increase my army as quickly as possible.'

Quantity and quality.

I need to get both.

,Walk! Don't run, walk!"

Undead don't get tired. They don't even pant after running all day. Why? Because they don't have breath to pant with!

But the undead's body is not immortal. It's made of bones and flesh. It means that the more you use it, the more its durability wears down.

,What if a joint breaks while running? That's the worst. Except for special cases like Skeleton Mages, the undead are basically meat shields. If they can't run or move on the battlefield, their usefulness decreases. At best, I can only use them as meat bombs.'

So, I need to use them as intact as possible. The undead's bodies do decay in the sunlight, but... I can't help that. I can slow down the rate of decay by using black magic.

,The bigger problem is that if I send them out haphazardly, there will be a lot of stragglers.'

Some undead are too fast, and some are too slow. If I make them run at full speed, the undead army will be divided. Dividing the forces was definitely not a good idea.

Sitting in the palanquin, I looked at the sunlight. Because I was a dead undead, I couldn't feel the heat of the sunlight. It was just a bit annoying.

,I can't do anything about the sun... so I have no choice but to endure it. I'm suddenly getting really annoyed. Should I just do auto-progress?'

I decided to postpone auto-progress for later. I had something to do now.

,I need to create high-level undead.'

Unlike the mid-level undead that I could create haphazardly, high-level undead require some effort.

,I'll make a few at first and then do auto-progress.'

I recited the high-level undead creation spell written in the Book of the Dead. Three mid-level undead nearby stopped abruptly. Their bodies were finely disassembled and clumped together to form a cocoon. The method of creation itself was similar to when I created mid-level undead.

But if you look closely, something is different. An ominous presence is felt from the cocoon along with a dark aura.

Jiiiiiiiiik!

The cocoon splits open, and a high-level undead pops out.

,Kueooooooo...."

It was a ghost.

Not just any ghost. It was a ghost that was emitting cold air. Frost formed in the place where the ghost was. Of course, the frost melted and froze repeatedly in the hot sunlight.

,A frost ghost, huh. Something's missing.'

I checked the frost ghost's abilities. It could do three things: fly, spew cold air, and shoot out cold air. Everything else was lackluster.

,Master... orders...."

,So, you can talk because you're a high-level undead. That's not bad."

I started creating new high-level undead. I can't use all the mid-level undead to create high-level undead. If I do that, the size of the army will become too small. I plan to create high-level undead while maintaining a minimum size.

Jiiiiiiiiik!

A new cocoon split open, and a zombie appeared. Not just any zombie. It was a zombie that was 3M tall, and its right arm was as disproportionately large as a tree trunk.

,Keueooo...."

,Looks like a zombie for meat shielding. Say something."

,Keueooooooo...."

Unlike the ghost, the zombie couldn't speak. It seems that not all high-level undead can speak.

,I can't shake off this ambiguous feeling.'

I was expecting a Death Knight as a high-level undead, but... it's not great.

,This one-armed undead looks slow, so... I need to modify its legs to make it run faster.'

That alone doesn't seem enough.

,Hmm.'

Just then, the frost ghost next to me caught my eye. I decided to modify the one-armed zombie using the frost ghost.

,Modifying a high-level with a high-level, so a useful one will come out.'

I use Undead Modification (SS). The frost ghost and the one-armed zombie merge. The base is the one-armed zombie. This is because the zombie had a larger mass. There are limits to Undead Modification (SS), so it's an unavoidable choice.

What was created was a cannon zombie. The large arm turned into a cannon.

,Shoot the cannon at that hill."

,Keueooo...."

The cannon zombie aimed its large cannon arm at the hill. Unlike its stupid cry, its actions were quick.

Then, cold air gathered in the cannon and was fired.

Kwaang!

A cold air shell fell on the sand dune. The shell exploded, and cold air swirled around. The sand dune instantly froze.

I opened my mouth in surprise and then smiled. It was more powerful than I had imagined.

,Cannon Zombie! I'll call you Dragoon! Your head is as stupid as a Dragoon!"

,Keueooooooo...."

Having seen the potential of high-level undead, I felt much better.

Hatep sensed the presence of Tutankhamun, who had disappeared. But he didn't tell his subordinates. According to the reports from the warriors, Tutankhamun's power was not threatening.

And he couldn't go to where Tutankhamun was in the current situation. Having chosen conquest, he was leading his army to the adjacent 5,145 area, the Ant Desert. It was the opposite direction from where Tutankhamun was.

Cheok! Cheok! Cheok!

The golden sand soldiers around Hatep march in step. Hatep's eyes sparkled as he looked at the golden sand soldiers. He desperately tried to stop the corners of his mouth from rising. If his face relaxed, he wouldn't have any dignity.

,These are soldiers!'

The golden sand soldiers don't get tired. They don't refuse orders. They don't consume food, and they don't need to sleep. They were truly ideal soldiers.

The only downside was that they didn't move proactively, but he solved that too.

,I spent a large amount of gold to create commander golden sand soldiers.'

Hatep smiled as he looked at the commander golden sand soldier walking next to him. The commander was larger and more ornate than the other golden sand soldiers.

,Compared to those guys....'

Outside the golden sand soldiers were desert warriors. The desert warriors riding camels were better off, but the desert warriors walking on two feet without camels were breathing heavily and sweating profusely.

,They need a break after marching a little, and they make a fuss asking for food more than three times a day. Compared to the golden sand soldiers, they are useless.'

Hatep, riding in a comfortable palanquin, clicked his tongue.

He planned to get rid of all the desert warriors later when the number of golden sand soldiers increased to 100,000.

,Great ruler of the Golden Desert-isiyeo! We are about to enter the Ant Desert area!"

,I know. Advance."

He finished his preparations when he advanced the army.

,I will become the ruler of the desert."

Hatep bet everything on this expedition. If he failed, he would lose everything. But Hatep was confident. He believed in the gold he had accumulated.

,Great ruler of the Golden Desert-isiyeo! We have discovered a desert tribe! The number is estimated to be around 300!"

A scout approached and reported to Hatep.

,Capture them. The more prisoners, the better. Treat the women especially well. I will offer them to the owner of the oasis. As for the men... you can kill them if they resist."

,Yes! Great ruler of the Golden Desert-isiyeo!"

The warriors of the Golden Desert advanced towards the enemies.

The battle ended quickly. Hatep's army versus 300 desert tribesmen. It was a power difference between the sun and a bonfire. The desert tribe surrendered without being able to do anything. The desert warriors shouted in victory.

At this rate, they would definitely take a break to celebrate the small victory. It was an unpleasant thing for Hatep.

,Don't stop. Our goal is to kill the ruler of the Ant Desert by today!"

At his urging, the army began to move again.
