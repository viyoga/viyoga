Odin, the Guide of Warriors and Supreme God, sat upon his throne, stroking his long beard as he observed the continent of Atlantis.

Truth be told, Odin had little interest in the eighth Atlantis continent.

When Atlantis first opened, he'd been somewhat intrigued. It was new, after all. But after it repeated a few times, boredom set in.

Various grandiose reasons had been attached to why Atlantis was started, but in his estimation, the biggest reason was just one.

'In the end, it's nothing but a gambling den for gods wagering on mortals.'

Odin, possessing wisdom, could roughly predict Atlantis's winner and its progression. In practice, his accuracy rate was quite high.

Odin had pegged Kang Myeongjin as the winner of the eighth Atlantis. However, Odin's prediction was off the mark. From some point onward, the eighth Atlantis progressed in a direction that was difficult to predict.

His fading interest surged up like flames fed with kindling.

'Something is happening centered around the eighth Atlantis. Something even my wisdom cannot grasp. Volva as well could not prophesy the distant future.'

Volva.

The witch who prophesied Ragnarok.

Even that remarkable witch could not prophesy the outcome of the eighth Atlantis.

'If Volva is like this, then Olympus's three Fates must also be unable to see the future.'

Detailed future prophecies about Atlantis were prohibited by rule, but even if one prophesied, as long as they didn't divulge it, that was that—and the penalty amounted to nothing more than a fine and expulsion from Atlantis. If one wanted to do it, it was entirely possible.

In fact, during the first and second rounds of Atlantis, there had been quite a few gods who actively used prophecy to try and gain advantage in Atlantis.

'Where Atlantis is concerned, the System approaches omnipotence. Unless one is of the Supreme God rank, it cannot be opposed.'

It couldn't be helped.

Though the condition "limited to Atlantis" was attached, the System was something most existing gods had created by expending their power.

That System had recently changed somehow.

Exactly what had changed, even Odin didn't know. There was simply a vague intuition that something had shifted. Odin did not ignore this inexplicable intuition.

"Odin."

From the darkness behind his back, a young man appeared.

He was a playful-looking youth with orange hair swept upward. With a long, narrow face, he wore a white tuxedo. A thick smile played at the corners of his mouth.

Odin asked without even turning to look at his sworn brother.

"Loki. Did you find out what I asked you to look into?"

"Yeah. On my brother's request, I risked my life and went out to the Outer Universe. If I give you just the results—nothing. Both divine temples were empty. The Void Record and the Goat. Both vanished."

"Were there traces?"

"No. Completely clean. The Void Record was always a solitary existence to begin with, but the Goat—even its offspring were all gone. At least, they're not anywhere we can perceive."

"So they vanished without a trace. And Chaos?"

"Chaos was always a bastard you never knew where he'd pop out from. He hasn't disappeared. Traces are left all over the place. Right now, he seems focused on Atlantis?"

"Besides us, who else knows that the universe's two Absolutes have disappeared?"

"Olympus? That thieving lot were sniffing around near the Outer Universe. Of course, they didn't notice me."

Odin continued stroking his beard.

"…A war may break out."

"War? Between gods?"

"The peace until now has been maintained by the existence of the two Absolutes. But those Absolutes have vanished from this universe. Loki. Can you give up hegemony over this universe?"

"Hegemony? I don't particularly care about that sort of thing. I could give it up anytime. However."

The smile vanished from Loki's face.

"I won't stand for other bastards seizing hegemony. Especially not the Olympus lot—absolutely not. If those fuckers grab hegemony over this universe, I don't even want to imagine what atrocities they'd commit."

"I think the same. Hegemony over the universe should rightfully be in our hands. However, war must be avoided. This entire universe could be reduced to ashes."

"Is that even possible? Olympus isn't the only one aiming for hegemony."

There were many gods in this universe. And many factions those gods belonged to.

"We'll have to find a point of agreement. Before that, we need to determine whether the Void Record and the Goat truly disappeared. The most certain way… would be Chaos."

"Chaos would never cooperate with us. To catch that bastard, we'd barely manage even joining hands with the Olympus side."

"There's no reason to deliberately provoke Chaos. Since Chaos is currently focused on Atlantis… perhaps the clue lies in Atlantis. For now, I will check the System logs of Atlantis."

Odin, Zeus, and the Supreme God-rank gods who had expended great power in creating the System had the authority to check the System logs.

He planned to use that authority to verify Chaos's movements.

「You do not have authority. Cannot access System logs.」

Odin's eyebrow twitched. The System had produced an error? No, that was impossible.

"…System. What kind of joke is this?"

「You do not have authority.」

"Hah. I'm not saying I'll intervene in Atlantis. I'm merely checking the logs. I promise there will be no intervention whatsoever in Atlantis."

「You do not have authority.」

"…I see. So there's something going on. System. When you were created, it was I who wrote your fundamental framework. My knowledge and power designed you. Since you are not functioning normally, I will examine you with my power."

Odin's single eye gleamed.

He extended his right hand toward the empty air. Divine power. Authority and dominion blazed at his fingertips.

「Unauthorized interference detected. Blocking.」

BOOM!

Odin's right hand burst. The fragments that had been his hand fell along with blood. From the messy wound at his wrist, foam bubbled up furiously. The foam burst pop-pop at a certain size. Accordingly, Odin's right arm grew shorter and shorter.

Odin calmly severed his right arm with his left hand. The forearm below the elbow dropped off entirely.

Loki, who had been watching, wore a far more shocked expression than Odin himself.

"Odin! This is…!"

"Yes. It's the power of Yog-Sothoth. The System has been contaminated by Yog-Sothoth."

"Wait. Speaking that name aloud is…!"

For gods, names are important. There was a reason Atlantis separately established divine throne names. Gods become entangled in causality merely by having their true names spoken. Whether they like it or not.

If one were to speak the name of an Absolute, they would receive a curse from that alone. Even Supreme God-rank beings like Odin and Loki were no exception. If one had to name an exception, it would be Chaos.

"I needed to confirm. And the confirmation is finished. There is no curse. Causality does not stir. Yog-Sothoth. Shub-Niggurath. Even speaking both names, no curse descends, and causality does not react. The universe is silent."

"…Sometimes I think you're crazier than I am. Even so, to confirm it that way. Anyway, the fact that speaking those two beings' names causes nothing means… it's certain those two are no longer in this universe. But the fact that the System possesses Yog-Sothoth's power…"

"Yes. I don't know exactly how it happened, but… in this universe, the thing closest to an Absolute is the System."

"But the System is merely a tool, isn't it?"

"What matters is who becomes the owner of that tool. Loki. Send word to Olympus. I need to have a conversation with Olympus."

The main tournament of the Martial Arts Competition is a random tournament starting from the round of 16.

The rule of the main tournament is one-on-one combat.

You win if your opponent surrenders or if you kill them. Of course, dying during the tournament doesn't mean actually dying. Death is merely elimination.

The bracket was randomly determined as soon as the third preliminary round ended yesterday. It hadn't diverged from the future I knew.

My first opponent was Ju Seohyeon.

「Beginning the Martial Arts Competition main tournament match.」

「You have been assigned to the first match.」

「Moving to the waiting room.」

「The match will begin in 5 minutes.」

The main tournament matches took place in a separate space, just like the preliminaries.

Beneath a blue sky, a neat rectangular arena at the center of a meadow.

There was an arena, but even if you left it during combat, it wasn't elimination. The arena was the entire meadow space.

Ju Seohyeon and I faced each other above the arena.

Six swords floated in the air around Ju Seohyeon. Her hand gripped the hilt at her waist. The Winter Sword. A demonic sword that uses the power of winter. The power of winter affects its user as well, but Ju Seohyeon could use it without penalty thanks to the Sword Emperor (SS) unique trait.

「The Brand of Vengeance placed upon you is activated.」

「You cannot flee while the time of vengeance is active.」

"I'll go all out."

"When have you ever not gone all out?"

“…….”

Ju Seohyeon's expression was calm.

If there was one thing different from usual, it was that she currently wasn't wearing a chastity belt.

'A shame I can't click the chastity belt dildo during combat.'

「Beginning the match start countdown.」

「3.」

「2.」

「1.」

「Begin.」

Ju Seohyeon used acceleration and leaped.

The six swords floating around her flew in from different directions. Her skill, the Igigeo Sword.

Normally, I might have struggled considerably.

'I use the power of foresight.'

Countless futures became visible.

I gripped Hwaryeonbido and took a step back. The swords that arrived faster than Ju Seohyeon grazed past my side.

One step to the right. Four swords brush past me.

One step to the left. One sword lodges in the ground where I had been.

Taking one step forward, I swung Hwaryeonbido. Ju Seohyeon's Winter Sword, entering from outside my vision, clashed against Hwaryeonbido. The power of winter flowed out, but I generated lightning to offset it.

Our eyes met in midair. Her pupils wavered. Her attack had been blocked so absurdly easily that she couldn't help but be shaken. In their usual sparring, it had never been this overwhelming.

'Sorry to say this to Ju Seohyeon, but against the current me, mind games don't work at all.'

I know everything about how she'll act. Even if she changes her movements midway, it's meaningless. Because I know that too.

Ju Seohyeon gritted her teeth. Forcing the timing forward, she prepared an unavoidable single strike, swinging all seven swords simultaneously.

Of course, I know this too.

I moved forward faster than she anticipated. The distance closed. I stole her breath. Ju Seohyeon, flustered, swung her sword to shake me off. She must have assumed I would block it. I dodged her attack by simply staying still. When her sword had fully descended, I swung Hwaryeonbido.

Squelch.

The tip of Hwaryeonbido pierced through Ju Seohyeon's heart.

"…You. You knew everything."

"You noticed. But the result is decided."

"I won't lose next time."

"I feel like I've heard that line several times already."

Ju Seohyeon and I vanished from the space.

「You have won the Martial Arts Competition main tournament round of 16 match.」
