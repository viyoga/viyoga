Pobitz's debut is D-6

There were women who were furious at the news of Pobitz's debut.

They were trainees belonging to Lilith Entertainment. They had been focusing on practice, hoping to debut for a few months at the very least, and more than three years at most, but they became like ducks in the Nakdong River because of Pobitz's appearance.

They couldn't suppress the anger rising from the depths of their hearts. This is because, usually, when an idol debuts from an entertainment company, the gap is about 3 to 5 years. In other words, their debut was pushed back, and they would have to wait another 3 to 5 years.

It would be better to leave for another entertainment company. However, it is not easy to leave a large agency like Lilith Entertainment without any regrets. There is a reason why a large agency is a large agency. The abilities they possessed were different from those of small and medium-sized entertainment companies.

So, what they chose to do was protest to the higher-ups. It was difficult to protest directly to the CEO no matter what, so they went to the A&amp;R team leader and argued.

However, contrary to expectations, they had to listen to the A&amp;R team leader's complaints.

,"Haa. I also found out about Pobitz's debut through an internet article yesterday. CEO Lilith still hasn't given us a word. I asked Director Colin... and apparently, Pobitz is made up of kids that the CEO selected and brought in from outside. I heard they're going to create a separate team and run it. I'm the team leader of the A&amp;R team, but don't you think it's too much that she doesn't even say a word to me?"

,"Team leader-nim. What about our debut?"

,"You guys? You guys will debut around next year as scheduled."

The debut schedule was not canceled. The trainees were relieved. But that didn't mean their anger was relieved.

,"What the hell are those kids? Suddenly appearing and debuting.... Are they that good?"

,"I don't know either. But they are good. You guys have seen their pictures, right? Their visuals alone are enough to make you tremble. Anyway, you guys just go about your business as usual."

The trainees trudged towards the practice room. They felt a little better than before, but anger and annoyance still remained. Then, they found Pobitz using the practice room. All four of them were practicing an intense dance song with outstanding looks.

The faces of the trainees distorted at the same time when they saw the song and dance.

,'This is one of the songs the company gave us!'

It was one of the songs they were told to practice, saying it might be used for their debut.

,'How dare they steal our song?'

The trainees, who were furious, couldn't continue to think calmly. They stormed into the practice room that Pobitz was using.

,"What are you guys?!"

,"......?"

Pobitz stopped practicing and looked at the trainees who had suddenly barged in. A total of 11 trainees crossed their arms and glared fiercely at Pobitz.
