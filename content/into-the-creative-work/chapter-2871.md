Park Joo-jung gritted his teeth.

The plan, the revenge for his son, had failed. He even received the news of the failure late.

,Failed! They are the elite of the elite from the Suma Guild! And yet, they couldn't even kill an S-class candidate whose mana was sealed?',

The money invested in them alone amounts to hundreds of billions. That hundreds of billions has now evaporated. Especially the curse that seals mana, it's hard to obtain even with money.

The loss is painful.

,...There's still a chance.'

Seeing that nothing has blown up yet, Seong Yujin is keeping his mouth shut. It was fortunate, yet unsettling.

,Does Seong Yujin want something from the Suma Guild? Or is he afraid of Han Ah-young?',

Park Joo-jung gritted his teeth.

He also knows that Han Ah-young and Seong Yujin are close. It's impossible to move Han Ah-young.

,If they failed even with the mana sealing curse... even if Joo-hyung and I move directly, we can't kill him. Revenge has realistically become impossible. Do I have to negotiate with Seong Yujin, my sworn enemy...! Heavens! Why do you give me such humiliation! I have to bow my head to the enemy who killed my son!!'

He wanted to shout at the sky with all his might.

But this is the Suma Guild headquarters. He must be careful about information leaking out.

,Soon-ryeo will be furious. I'm already dreading how to appease her....'

Park Soon-ryeo is his younger sister and Park Sun-hoo's mother. She has such a fiery personality that appeasing her won't be easy.

,...A gentleman's revenge is never too late, even after 10 years. But time isn't fair to everyone. For Seong Yujin, 10 years is... more than enough time to become an S-class.'

Park Joo-jung's face darkened.

Should he risk his life and attempt revenge? Or should he live ignoring his son's death?

,...My life isn't just my own. If I die, our family will falter. My goal is to create a prestigious family that can last for hundreds of years, like the families that make up the Goryeo Guild. I can't give up on my goal here. I'm sorry, Sun-hoo-ya. Forgive this incompetent father.'

Park Joo-jung closed his eyes tightly and took a deep breath.

Click!

The door to his private office bursts open.

Park Joo-jung frowned and looked at the entrance. It was Park Joo-hyung. His face was glistening with cold sweat.

"Joo-hyung? What's wrong?"

"B-Brother, Hunter Han Ah-young-ssi...."

"I'll tell you the rest."

Behind Park Joo-hyung, Han Ah-young and Han Ha-rin came in slowly. White hair and black hair. As expected of sisters, the similar-looking beauties entered, and the gloomy office atmosphere immediately felt brighter. The problem was that the beauties' expressions weren't good. Not good at all.

Han Ah-young didn't even hide the aura of an S-class Hunter.

Park Joo-jung felt breathless.

Because this wasn't his first time experiencing this.

Ha Heon-shin.

The distant gap he felt when he faced him when he was younger.

If they fought 100 times, he would lose 100 times.

S-classes are different in their very existence.

"Hunter Han Ah-young-ssi. What brings you here?"

Park Joo-jung tried to speak calmly, but he couldn't even stop his voice from trembling.

He and Han Ah-young weren't close enough to meet without a reason. He had a bad feeling.

"What brings me here? Do you really not know?"

Han Ah-young's voice was not just cold, but hostile.

"It seems you have complaints about the guild. If you tell me your complaints, I will take care of them."

"Ha. Did you think I wouldn't know that you sent A-class Hunters to kill Yujin-gun? I can't believe the Suma Guild is this rotten. Ha-rin-yang and I are withdrawing from the Suma Guild from now on."

So Han Ah-young found out!

"Hunter Han Ah-young! You can't do this! The contract period is still remaining! And remember what the Suma Guild has supported you with! Are you going to repay kindness with enmity like this?!"

"Repay kindness with enmity? So that's why you tried to kill Yujin-gun?"

Jjeojeong.

The mana around Han Ah-young reacts to her anger and freezes instantly. The temperature drops below 0 degrees in an instant.

Park Joo-jung's teeth chattered from the cold that penetrated to his bones. His younger brother, Park Joo-hyung, couldn't even lift his head and bowed. Han Ha-rin, behind Han Ah-young, was also trembling slightly.

"Ha."

Han Ah-young sighs. The chill in the room disappears.

"You're the ones who violated the contract first. I wish I could kill you, but... I'm not like you, so I'll do it according to the law."

Han Ah-young glared at Park Joo-jung and turned away as if she didn't want to talk anymore. Han Ha-rin glared at Park Joo-jung and Park Joo-hyung with contempt and followed her older sister out.

Park Joo-jung felt all the strength drain from his body.

If he loses the elite A-class Hunters and even Han Ah-young, an S-class Hunter, leaves... it would be difficult for the Suma Guild to maintain its position as the second-ranked in Korea. They might even become the weakest among the top 10 guilds.

"B-Brother. There's a contract, isn't there? Han Ah-young has to fulfill the contract! We've given her so much...!"

"You idiot! Do you think that damn contract will work on Han Ah-young?!"

Hold up the contract and forcibly hold onto Han Ah-young?

Forcibly holding on will only create conflict. The validity of the contract? Would that kind of thing work on an S-class Hunter? Moreover, Han Ah-young had the justification. The Korean government and the Hunter Association would all side with Han Ah-young. Not to mention public opinion.

Even if Han Ah-young killed him, the world would side with Han Ah-young. That's what an S-class Hunter is.

"What do we do now? Do you think Han Ah-young will just back down? She said she'll do it according to the law, so she'll definitely sue us!"

That Han Ah-young is suing directly? The media will swarm in. And the public officials will handle the matter with unprecedented speed as much as the attention is focused.

"God damn it! Shut your mouth! I'm thinking!"

Park Joo-jung saw himself dying in prison.

"Why are you acting like the victim?! Isn't this all because of you, brother! If you hadn't been blinded by revenge for Sun-hoo, this situation wouldn't have escalated like this!"

"You crazy bastard! Sun-hoo is your nephew! Are you saying I should have stayed still when my nephew was murdered! Don't you have any family affection?!"

"You were the one who said we should throw away what needs to be thrown away! Isn't this all caused by your mistake?! It's your problem, so you take responsibility and solve it!!"

"You disrespectful bastard! Don't you know we're already in the same boat?! Instead of yelling at me, fix the situation first!!"

"Do you think we can fix it when that Han Ah-young has left the guild?! Do you think the other top 10 guilds will just stand by! The reason they couldn't touch our Suma Guild while disliking us was because of Han Ah-young, wasn't it? We're done for now! It's all because of you, brother!"

Park Joo-hyung ran out.

"That, that, that! That ungrateful bastard! Has he forgotten who made the Suma Guild develop!"

Park Joo-jung shouted, but Park Joo-hyung didn't return.

In the chilling office, Park Joo-jung picked up his smartphone. He contacted Han Ah-young first. But she didn't answer at all, as if he was blocked.

Next, he contacted the World Hunter Association, guilds he was usually close with, politicians, judges, prosecutors, and other connections. But the voices that came back were cold.

"I heard that Hunter Han Ah-young held a press conference. I didn't know she was that kind of person. The Hunter Association will move soon. Don't run away and make things bigger."

"Oh, you. I heard that Hunter Han Ah-young left the Suma Guild. That's really unfortunate news. Hehe."

"Don't contact me. We're nothing to each other."

The connections Park Joo-jung had built up immediately turned their backs on him.

"You bastards! Do you think I fed you money for this!!"

Park Joo-jung, with his eyes rolling back, threatened them.

"Do you think I'm going to die alone?! You who ate my money are in the same boat as me! If you don't want to die with me, help me!!"

Those who had their weaknesses caught reluctantly decided to help Park Joo-jung.

Although they were afraid of the retaliation that would come later, surviving right now was their top priority. At least they bought some time. It was fortunate that the Hunter Association hadn't come to catch him yet.

However, a few hours later. An uncontrollable fire broke out.

A famous major media outlet dropped a bomb.

「The ugly truth of Suma Guild Master Park Joo-jung!

Park Joo-jung wore a mask of kindness and committed all sorts of dirty deeds. The criminal history he committed includes murder, theft, rape, tax evasion, collusion with the powerful, and so on endlessly. Some say it's slander, but the evidence clearly proves it. You can check the evidence of the crime through the link below.

.......

.......

.......」

Park Joo-jung was shocked as soon as he saw the article.

Because there was information about all the corruption that he, the Suma Guild, had committed. These were things that only the top executives of the Suma Guild knew. For example, his only younger brother, Park Joo-hyung.

"Park Joo-hyung! Could this bastard be!"

「The most sordid act among the ugly deeds committed by Park Joo-jung would be incest.

Park Joo-jung committed incest with his younger sister, Park Soon-ryeo, and gave birth to his son and nephew, Park Sun-hoo.

Park Sun-hoo recently became a monster and was subjugated by S-class Hunter candidate Seong Yujin. Enraged by this, Park Joo-jung sent 10 veteran A-class Hunters from the Suma Guild to the dungeon where Seong Yujin was, but they were annihilated in reverse.

The World Hunter Association has secured the body of Park Sun-hoo, who turned into a monster, and is conducting a genetic test to prove that Park Joo-jung is Park Sun-hoo's father.

In addition, Park Sun-hoo's monsterization is suspected to be a runaway of his ability. Park Sun-hoo, who was an A-class Hunter, had a physical mutation ability called Enhanced Wings. The entire Park family has similar abilities. In particular, Park Joo-jung has the ability to transform his upper body.

The video below is a battle video of Park Joo-jung taken a long time ago.

(A video of Park Joo-jung using his ability to deal with monsters.)

His upper body transformed like a beast. To put it nicely, it's a werewolf... but it looks very similar to the monster Lycanthrope.

Below is a picture of Park Joo-jung congratulating Park Soon-ryeo's wedding. At this time, Park Soon-ryeo was already pregnant with Park Sun-hoo. This article questions how a human can be so shameless.

.......」

"You crazy reporter bastard!!"

Park Joo-jung stood up from his seat with a face that had turned bright red.

,How did he know the secrets that even Joo-hyung doesn't know?!'

Brrrrr.

His smartphone vibrates. It was a call from Park Soon-ryeo. He didn't answer the call and hung up.

,I have to fix it.... I have to fix it...! ...From where and how?'

It was when he was feeling helpless.

Bang!

The door is smashed and Hunters rush in.

"This is Baek Ji-eun from the Korean Hunter Association. Suma Guild Master Park Joo-jung. I am placing you under arrest. We will kill you if you resist."

Park Joo-jung looked at the Hunters around Baek Ji-eun. The A-class Hunters with bitter smiles were familiar faces. They were A-class Hunters belonging to rival guilds, including the Goryeo Guild. They were enjoying Park Joo-jung's downfall.

"God damn it...."

Thud.

Park Joo-jung slumped into his chair.

There was nothing he could do.
