I took the lead and moved forward.

The mercenaries followed behind me. Somehow, the atmosphere was more chaotic than when I first saw them. They were scared shitless after seeing Fafnir in person. Fortunately, Omikos, the leader of the mercenaries, didn't show any signs of anxiety.

,Omikos. It seems like the mercenary group is scared shitless of Fafnir. Can we fight Fafnir like this?

,It's difficult right now. But it seems like Eugene-nim has a plan. I'll follow you because there's a possibility.

Omikos seemed like a calm and loyal mercenary. On the surface, anyway. There was no way to know what he was really up to.

'He must be really greedy for Fafnir's blood.'

Had we walked for about an hour? Something appeared at the edge of my vision. As I got closer, I saw it was a village.

'There's no way Fafnir would be all alone in this vast area.'

I hadn't seen any other monsters on the way here. Either there weren't any from the beginning, or Fafnir got rid of them because they bothered him. It was probably the latter. Atlantis has monsters in most areas that aren't urban.

'Fafnir seemed like a real pain in the ass.'

I cursed Fafnir inwardly and approached the village. There seemed to be an unusually large number of rocks and stones around the village. As I got closer, the answer came to me.

'It's a mining village.'

There were no mines near the village. Even without a mine, it's not strange since there could be veins of ore underground.

I confidently walked into the village. Omikos, who was next to me, was flustered.

,We can't just go in like this. The villagers will be wary.

,You seem to have a lot of experience with this.

,When you travel around the area, you often encounter small villages. Villages in these remote areas basically don't welcome strangers. They don't trust them either. It's mostly because they've been plagued by thieves, bandits, and marauders.

,What if they don't trust us?

,We'll lose if we fight. Monsters exist in this world. Even a small village has the strength to protect itself.

,When did I say I was going to fight? We need to talk about Fafnir first.

Perhaps because it was a village in a remote area, there were no guards protecting the village. I nervously entered the village. People's eyes were on me. Most of them were women doing housework. They all looked shabby.

I looked around the village for a while and slowly headed towards the center.

From afar, men came running in a hurry. They had taken off their shirts, all exposing their upper bodies. They were holding tools such as pickaxes and hammers in their hands. They were miners, so their bodies were quite good.

One of them, a middle-aged man with bulging eyes, stood in front of me as if blocking my path.

,How dare you enter our village without permission! Get out of here immediately!!

,We came to this area to kill Fafnir. We need information about Fafnir. Like weaknesses.

,You're going to kill that evil dragon with the likes of you? You must be suicide volunteers. We don't have any information about Fafnir. Get out of here.

The village men gathered more and more. There were already over 100 of them. They all showed hostility towards us. The mercenaries also became nervous. If they went crazy and attacked, we would have to fight.

,Why are you being so unfriendly? I'm just saying let's talk about Fafnir.

,Aren't you thinking about your rude attitude?

,If you keep being so uncooperative... you're just forcing me to be uncooperative. Please, don't make me a demon.

,Ha, are you threatening us now!

,This bastard doesn't understand the situation. Do you think you can win just because you have more people? I can kill you all by myself.

I was about to use mana to strengthen my body when...

Omikos suddenly stepped forward.

,You're both too excited. Eugene-nim. It seems like you've become very sensitive after meeting Fafnir earlier. Please sit here on this folding chair and rest for a while. I'll take care of this.

,I was a bit tired.

There was no need to refuse when he offered to take care of the troublesome task. I sat down on the folding chair that Omikos had set up for me. The broker said he was an inflexible mercenary... but in reality, he was overflowing with flexibility.

'Is this the experience he's gained from being a mercenary?'

,Village chief-nim. I apologize for entering the village without permission. We were flustered because we had just encountered Fafnir. As a token of apology, we'll share some of our supplies. It doesn't seem like there are many merchants coming here... don't you need some supplies?

,We do need some supplies. Do you have any fruits? Preferably fruits that grow well in this kind of land.

,I don't know about that, but we have several kinds of fruits.

,It would be nice to have some clothes too.

,We have extra clothes that we carry around. We brought a lot of things because this Fafnir raid is important.

,We don't need gifts. We may lack many things, but we're not poor. We'll pay a fair price for everything.

,Do you have pennies? Or AP?

,We don't have pennies because no merchants come here. AP is precious to us, so it's hard to use it for something like this. Instead, we have gold.

The village chief took something out of his pants pocket. It was shining yellow in the light. It was gold.

The mercenaries were surprised. They looked around and realized that this village was a place where they mined gold.

'A village that mines gold. Not bad.'

Gold has always been a guaranteed currency. The value of gold was high on Earth, and Atlantis also valued gold.

The mercenaries, who had frowned at the mention of gifts, changed their expressions when they heard that they would buy it with gold. They were all rummaging through their backpacks, trying to sell their goods. It was a business where they could make several times the profit.

「Gold Collector donates 1,000 AP.

I can't understand. Why are you trading? They're weaklings anyway. They have gold that they don't deserve, so why not just take it by force? Gold should be in the hands of the strong.」

,You can say the right thing sometimes. But why the fuck? Why did you do that earlier?

「Gold Collector donates 1,000 AP.

Trials will make you stronger.」

,Bullshit.

Even now, I grind my teeth when I see the Gold Collector.

Originally, according to my plan, I should be beating up Fafnir right now. Ganging up on him with the mercenary group.

It's not like I haven't thought about targeting another dragon instead. But the whereabouts of other dragons were ambiguous. More than anything, I had already signed the Grim Reaper's contract. It was difficult to unilaterally break the contract.

'Even if I can use Cheonsim to deal with the Grim Reaper's curse, I can't handle the Grim Reaper's pursuit right now.'

It was to strengthen Hwaryeon Bido, so I couldn't just catch any mediocre dragon.

I took out cold mineral water and soothed my burning insides.

Had about an hour passed?

The village chief, who had obtained all sorts of things and was in a good mood, began to speak.

,Our village digs tunnels to mine gold. There are gold veins everywhere, even just 50m underground.

,That's why the people of this village can use gold like water.

Omikos exclaimed in admiration. A village where gold comes out. It was indeed a name that deserved admiration.

,What's the use of that? We can't go outside the village because of Fafnir. Fafnir. That evil dragon won't let us go... Even if we have gold, there's nowhere to use it. Besides, we have to offer gold to Fafnir every week.

,We came to kill that Fafnir. Tell us Fafnir's weaknesses. If Fafnir dies, won't you be free too?

,Speak properly. If Fafnir dies, a new owner will just appear.

The mercenaries glanced at me, who was sitting apart. According to the contract, I would get all the byproducts after killing Fafnir. This area was no exception.

,There are three conditions.

,Do you really need to set conditions?

,If Fafnir finds out that I said this, do you think he'll leave our village alone? He'll harass us with all sorts of terrible things. He might demand sacrifices.

,...I'll listen to the conditions first.

,One is guaranteeing the safety of our village. Regardless of who becomes the ruler among you, guarantee the freedom and safety of our village. That arrogant bastard shouldn't become the ruler of the area.

Omikos glanced at me and then asked.

,What's the second condition?

,Return the gold that Fafnir has plundered from us. Even if it's just a part of it. We've been working for decades. Shouldn't we have some compensation?

,What's the last, third condition?

,Help us a little. A huge rock is blocking us in the underground tunnel. It's hard to break even if we use our strength. If you help us, we'll be able to break that rock. Just help us for one day. Ah, all these conditions will be guaranteed by the system. It's the authority we received from the system instead of being plundered by Fafnir.

「Transaction Agreement」

「This contract is guaranteed by the system.」

「The contract must be fulfilled.」

Omikos's face hardened.

They wanted a lot compared to just telling us information. Omikos was feeling that too.

,...We need time to discuss with our colleagues. Please wait.

,We can't wait long. We have to mine gold to offer to that goddamn Fafnir.

I led the Omikos mercenary group out of the village. There was a risk of eavesdropping in the village.

,There was a reason why the village guys were so confident. The system was giving them some convenience.

I clicked my tongue and said.

It wasn't strange. The continentals had to sacrifice themselves for the stories that the gods wanted. But what if the sacrificing continentals ruined the stories that would entertain the gods by acting like trolls? It's a headache for the system.

So, the system sometimes provides benefits to the continentals as long as the balance isn't broken. That benefit is contract guarantee, like now. It's not a benefit that directly gives power to the continentals, so they judged that there was no problem.

,What are you going to do?

Omikos asked me. They are mercenaries and I am the employer. The choice was naturally mine.

,What do you think?
