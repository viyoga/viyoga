A bloodied young man trembled before my eyes. I watched him carefully.

"Not a corrupted one. He's human."

"Is that so."

The Inquisitor took out a dagger from his bosom and stabbed the man's heart, killing him.

I asked the Inquisitor in a calm tone.

"Why did you kill him?"

"He was an impious one. He doubted the existence of the Goddess Herianne, and he was dragged here for insulting the Order. He was not corrupted, but he was a heretic. For the existence of the Order, heretics must be punished."

"I see."

"...Is that all, -imnikka?"

"What do you want from me?"

"...The previous Paladin scolded us. That one would have rebuked us."

"I am different from the previous Paladin. You bear your own sins."

"Of course. We have finished all our preparations. If it is the judgment of the Goddess Herianne, we will accept any judgment. Everything is for the Goddess-nim and the Order."

The Inquisitor continued to bring heretics. There were a total of 23 people. All but two were corrupted. I killed all the corrupted ones. The Inquisitors looked at me with respectful eyes.

"To step forward and punish the corrupted... How admirable. You are more than enough to be a role model for all of us."

I wiped the blood-stained sword with a cloth. They seem to be misunderstanding something, but the reason I directly killed the corrupted ones was all for experience points. There was no other reason. And there's no need to tell the Inquisitors that fact.

"What are you planning to do with the vermin hiding in the city?"

"Please help us. Walk through the city with us and identify the heretics and inform us. We will kidnap the heretics and bring them here."

"Is there really a need to do that? It doesn't matter if you kill them as soon as you see them. The city can be sealed off by the Holy Knights."

"There is a sacred barrier here that prevents the corrupted from exerting their power. We will interrogate them to find out the cause of the corruption."

"...Indeed. I understand. However, there is a condition."

"Speak."

"I will take charge of the execution of the corrupted. Killing those bastards is proof of my faith."

I couldn't hand over the experience points to these guys.

The Inquisitor's eyes sparkled and he let out an exclamation.

"Indeed, Paladin... You are truly an example for us."

I walked around the city with the Inquisitors. I was dressed as a traveler. Instead of armor, I wore leather clothes and pressed my cloak deep over my head to cover the Holy Eye. My job was to find the hidden corrupted and inform the Inquisitor. Then the Inquisitor recorded the corrupted, and the corrupted were kidnapped at night and dragged into the basement of the cathedral.

The Inquisitors interrogated them, and I executed the bastards who had finished their interrogation.

Ten days passed like that. Since the city of Valentier has quite a population, there were many corrupted as well.

'Thanks to the Inquisitors taking care of the small fries, I easily gained experience points.'

[Name: Eugene

Origin: Holy Knight

Level: 30

Strength: 20 Agility: 10 Stamina: 10 Divinity: 11

Skills: Holy Eye (Lv. Master), Holy Aura (Lv.1), Divine Sword (Lv.3)]

I raised 3 levels.

You can say it's only 3, but it can't be helped. The higher the level, the more experience points it requires. The accumulated blessing energy was used to strengthen the Divine Sword.

I went to the sanctuary and met the Saintess.

"I have been waiting. The elixir is complete."

"Thank you."

"I have only done what I had to do."

I received the bottle containing the blue liquid that the Saintess handed me. I drank the elixir without hesitation.

[Stamina increases by 1.]

My stats increased. It was the same as raising 1 level. I put down the empty bottle with satisfaction. There was no limit to the elixir. If I had to pick one, it would be that it requires byproducts above a certain level.

"Paladin-isiyeo, the Inquisitors have found the cause of the corruption."

"What is the cause?"

In fact, I knew the cause through the original work. The reason I didn't step forward and took a half-observational stance was to gain experience points. To face the Great Demon, I must become stronger than anyone else.

"It's rats."

"Rats?"

"Demonic rats are roaming through the underground waterways. The rats spread the energy of corruption and tempt people into the path of corruption. It is presumed that there is a demon controlling the rats in the underground waterways. Paladin-isiyeo, find and punish the demon in the underground waterways, -hasoseo."

"I understand. Killing demons is my duty and mission."

I led the Holy Knights to the underground waterways. Unlike the game, there was no need to handle everything alone.

It was dark and stinky in the underground waterways. The remains of human and animal corpses were scattered everywhere. They are the leftovers that the demon ate and threw away.

"How terrible."

"To think that a demon was hiding under the city..."

"I will never forgive it!"

The Holy Knights were burning with motivation.

Jjijjik.

The sound of a rat was heard. I and the Holy Knights stopped walking. Hundreds of red eyes in the darkness are directed at us.

We immediately took a fighting stance. The rats rush in all at once.

"The center of the underground waterways is right in front of us. The demon will be there. Don't back down and move forward."

"Yes! Paladin-isiyeo!"

"For the Goddess-nim!"

The Holy Knights shouted. They wrapped their swords in golden Holy Aura and swung them at the rats.

These rats were different from ordinary rats. The size was similar to a small dog, and the teeth were sharper than a wolf. They are rats that have received the power of the demon.

Jjijji-jjik!

The rats keep rushing in. There was no end to killing and killing.

"Paladin-isiyeo! We'll take care of this!"

"Find and punish the demon who controls the rats, -hasoseo!"

"I trust you to take care of it."

I used Sprint and ran forward. The rats gather their bodies to create a barrier. I didn't stop and put my shoulder forward.

Kwang!

I break through the wall of rats with a shoulder attack and move forward. Nevertheless, the rats blocked my way to the end. I took out an exploding holy water and threw it.

Kwakwakwang! Kwakwang!

The exploding holy water neatly cleaned up the rats, but the rats rushed in again like a wave and filled the spot.

'There's still exploding holy water left!'

I grit my teeth and throw the exploding holy water. I was able to reach the demon after using all the exploding holy water.

"Kekeke. Did you finally make it here?"

The demon was attached to the ceiling. Its head was that of a rat, and its body was like a larva cocoon. The lump of flesh hanging from the ceiling was pouring something out through a hole. Cheom-beong cheom-beong. I look at those things that fell into the waterway that was ankle-deep. It was rats. The rats wriggled and headed to where the human corpses were piled up. The newly born rats devoured the human corpses brought by the corrupted.

It's a disgusting sight. But what was even more disgusting was the pungent stench. Even if I relax a little, this stench makes me want to vomit.

The demon attached to the ceiling scanned my body with hungry eyes.

"You look delicious. The demons who have eaten Paladins say... there is no human with a more special taste than a Paladin. Especially the intestines, they say that even if three people eat and four people die, they won't know the taste?"

"Nonsense."

I leaped towards that bastard. An arm popped out of his body. The emaciated arm, like a tree branch, was over 2m long. I used the blessing of Sprint to leap once more in the air, rotating my body to avoid the bastard's attack. I kicked the ceiling and slashed the bastard before landing on the ground.

"Kuaaaaaaak!"

The bastard screams. The rats nearby began to rush towards me like crazy. I lowered my gaze to the floor. Water up to my ankles, and no allies around.

'Lightning.'

Golden current spreads out in all directions through the water. The rats that were rushing in were electrocuted and annihilated. The demon, still hanging from the ceiling, was flustered. The demon opens its mouth wide and spits out acidic liquid.

I avoided the acidic liquid and raised my left hand.

'Divine Sword.'

Golden divinity gathers in my hand and takes the form of a sword. Wielding swords in both hands, I leaped towards the bastard and swung the swords in succession.

"Keueeeeek?!"

The bastard's torso, which was hanging from the ceiling, falls down. I raised my sword for the finish.

"Do you think it will end like this!!"

The bastard's cocoon-like torso swelled up and then burst. A large amount of acidic liquid covers me. The acidic liquid melts the Stormbreaker armor and begins to melt my skin. I regained my fading mind in the smoke.

"Kelkelkel... You got hit head-on, you stupid bastard... My babies. Come here. Quickly become my flesh and blood."

The demon, with only the head of a rat and long arms like tree branches left, crawled on the floor of the underground waterway in a gruesome state. The newly born kin gather around him. The demon ate the squeaking kin. The demon's body slowly began to regenerate.

'It's not enough, it's not enough. More. I need to eat more...!'

The demon walked through the underground waterway. Since the Paladin must have melted away, he was thinking of devouring the Holy Knights who came here.

Cheom-beong.

The sound of water was heard from behind.

The demon's movement stopped abruptly.

"Where are you going?"

The demon, with wide eyes, slowly turned its head back.

A human with half of its body melted was standing proudly. The solid armor and large greatsword had melted more than half, unable to maintain their proper shape. The human was even worse. Half of his head had melted away, revealing his brain. A liquid mixed with intestines flowed from his abdomen.

It's a fatal wound.

It's strange that he's alive.

The Paladin raised the greatsword that was more than half melted. Golden light gathered on the sword's blade, replacing the broken blade.

The demon hesitated. He desperately moved his arms to get as far away from the Paladin as possible.

"Perish here."

"Hateful Paladin!! Get lost! Don't come! I said get lost...!!"

The demon's last struggle echoed in the underground waterway, but the Paladin's holy sword mercilessly cut off the demon's head.
