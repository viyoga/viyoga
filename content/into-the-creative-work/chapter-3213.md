[Using Miracle.]

“…….”

Even after using Miracle, nothing visibly happened. There wasn't even the usual flash of light.

'I definitely used it. The notification window is right there.'

I looked at Baal.

Baal's appearance hadn't changed. The same face as Seong Hari. But her hair was white, as if the colors had been inverted. Her pupils were blood-red. Dark red magical energy still billowed from the black armor she wore and the spear in her hand.

'...Did Miracle not work?'

If it failed, the next option was Absolute Imperial Authority.

'I want to save Absolute Imperial Authority, which can kill even an Outer God in one blow, for as long as possible. A power or item that turns a demon into a human. Something like that should turn up if I dig through other creative works.'

Settings where a human becomes a demon, or a demon becomes a human, are fairly common in fiction. A few works came to mind right away.

No need to rush. I could freeze the world's time by stepping outside of it.

"...This is insane. You actually became human."

Clatter.

Baal's spear dropped. The blade was so sharp it embedded itself into the floor. Baal was looking down, examining her own body this way and that.

"...I'm hungry."

Grrrrr.

The sound came from her stomach.

Seong Hari narrowed her eyes. She stared at Baal with a gaze full of suspicion.

"You, did you really become human? Your magical energy is still the same. Aren't you just pretending to be human to lure us into letting our guard down?"

Baal furrowed her brow. She glared at Seong Hari with a venomous look.

"Do you think I'm you? Pulling such a filthy trick. I am human now. The instinct to obey the Demon God, the instinct to kill other living beings—all of it is gone. In exchange, human basic desires have returned. Hunger being the most obvious one."

Baal glanced at me for no reason, then asked.

"Eugene. Do you happen to have anything to eat? I'm so hungry..."

"I do."

I pulled a Yuria handmade hamburger from my inventory and handed it to Baal. Baal hurriedly unwrapped the handmade burger and took a huge bite.

"It's warm and delicious...!"

"She eats like a beggar. Watching her is making me hungry too. Eugene. Give me one too."

"Okay."

Seong Hari ate a hamburger too. I handed them cola as a service, and they drank it without complaint.

'...If I hadn't intervened, Seong Hari might have lost and died just like the future I knew.'

'Shut up.'

Seeing Demon Hunter (SSS) throwing a fit, it seems it still treats Baal as a demon. Human yet demon. In a way, you could say she's in a state similar to a Demon Person.

'In some sense, you can't say she's become completely human. But the demonic instincts and other troublesome things are gone, so things worked out well.'

The demons of this world are nothing more than slaves and toys of the Demon God.

The reason the demon legion invades other worlds is to offer them to the Demon God. In return, demons receive power, endless lifespan, and an ageless body from the Demon God. If you become a demon by receiving the Demon God's power through the 'Baptism' first, resisting the Demon God becomes impossible.

So the root of all evil could be called the Demon God.

If I can get the drawn-in Outer God to side with me, I can restrain the Demon God from exerting power on Earth. The Demon God wouldn't likely fight another Outer God just to destroy one Earth.

The god of the Gumok Church, the god with nine eyes, came to mind first. The problem is there's no way to draw him in. He's the type who would just observe even if Earth were destroyed, without intervening. I don't understand why an entity omnipotent enough to destroy and recreate the universe if he felt like it does nothing but observe.

In a far corner of the universe where even starlight couldn't reach, the Demon God was watching over the demon legion.

To the Demon God, the demon legion was a pastime. Something to watch when there was truly nothing else to do. He had merely thrown a piece of filth—nothing to him, equivalent to dirt under a fingernail for a human—to some insects, and they rejoiced and worshipped him.

He used the demon legion that received his scraps to fight insects from another domain. There was no particular reason here either. If forced to name one, it was that the sight of insects fighting insects was somewhat entertaining.

After victory, the demon legion offered tribute to the Demon God. The Demon God found it amusing, accepted the tribute, and dropped scraps in return. That repeated.

For a human, it was like growing a plant that didn't need any intervention. A plant that grew fine on its own if left alone.

Recently, the group of insects he'd been feeding had lost. They suddenly fell into internal strife and their numbers dwindled. Since this had never happened before, the Demon God used his power to look quite deeply. Like observing the ecology of microorganisms under a microscope. It was the first time, so it was rather interesting.

Above all, one of the insects caught the Demon God's attention. An insect that became part of the demon legion without having eaten his scraps. An insect growing at an incredible speed, with a promising future. Its origin was a bit unusual, but he had no interest in the birth myths of insects.

Insect clashed with insect.

Normally, his insect would have quickly defeated and devoured the enemy's insect.

But an unknown power. A grating power that somehow felt familiar was interfering with the insects' battle.

Eventually, when the Demon God could no longer stand it and was about to intervene directly—

A shining scale appeared in his domain.

Only then did the Demon God realize that the being interfering with the insects' battle was the Scale. The one who had fought him long ago, a very strange being who threw itself into danger for mere insects.

That being called itself—

—The Punisher. Still going around harming your own existence for insects?

The shining scale, tilted to one side, transforms.

It was a form resembling the insects. Those insects called themselves humans. A being mimicking the appearance of insects. The Demon God let out a low sneer. At the same time, he felt disgust. To the Demon God, the Punisher was a lunatic with a perverse attraction to insects.

—Get lost. This is my domain.

The Demon God rose and sent a will laden with warning. That alone made the surrounding universe tremble.

The Punisher did not leave. Instead, it directed hostility toward him.

Why was the Punisher acting like this?

Unable to understand, the Demon God examined the causality. It was related to an insect.

It selects one insect and entrusts its will to it. If the insect walks righteously according to the Punisher's standards, it helps the insect and punishes the insect's enemies. If the insect deviates from the standards, it eventually punishes the insect.

From the Demon God's perspective, it was a hobby in far worse taste than his own—he merely tossed scraps to insects.

However, because it was connected to the Punisher, he couldn't examine the causality in detail.

—You want to try me? I won't let it slide like last time.

—He desires your death.

—You'd stake your annihilation for a mere insect?

The Demon God scoffed. But as the Punisher's power grew stronger, the laughter faded and tension took its place.

—What is that immense power? Why is this universe helping you?

—Because he is the center of the universe, the beginning and end of all things. He desires your complete death.

—Is there something about that insect? Fine. I'll deal with you and then find out slowly.

The battle was not long.

The Punisher's power was above the Demon God's expectations. The gap was vast.

—Kraaaaaaah! Eyes! I know you're watching! Help me! This mad thing is turning the universe into chaos!

The god with nine eyes remained silent. It merely observed.

The Demon God grew desperate.

—Punisher! I've lost! Let's talk!

—I am a system. The ending of this game has been decided, and all things shall be done according to that ending.

—Have you truly lost your mind?! Kraaaaaaah!

In the end, the Demon God who ruled a corner of the universe was annihilated.

The Punisher became the Scale once more and disappeared.

The god with nine eyes watched as always.

Everything was being carried out according to its role.
