After rejecting the Dragon Heart, Seonghari was persuaded with every possible reason until she finally relented and consumed it.

She ate the Dragon Heart of the Galaxy Heavenly Dragon right in front of me. Seonghari's brows furrowed. It was because the Dragon Heart itself tasted terrible.

Immediately after, her body began to glow softly like a star. A silvery light that appeared almost white.

Seonghari's presence grew heavier. So much so that I unconsciously took a step back from her.

Seonghari closed both eyes. She gathered her strength with steady breathing. The silver light that had only been shining on the surface of her body seeped inward. Just as I thought the silver light had vanished entirely, two pairs of wings made of silver light sprouted from her back. The wings sparkled as though they were woven from starlight.

Crackle, crackle.

Sparks flew from her body. Not the usual blue lightning—this was silver lightning.

Opening her eyes, Seonghari looked at the two pairs of silver wings extending from her back and the silver sparks leaping around her with a fascinated expression.

I also checked her status window.

『Name: Seonghari

Strength: SSS Endurance: SSS Agility: SSS Durability: SSS Mana: SSS

Traits: Spirit Devourer (S), Indra's Divine Thunder (SSS), Galaxy Heavenly Wings (SSS)

Skills: Divine Spear Unity (SSS), Spear Throw (SS+), Combat Sense (SS+), Purification (C), Force Field (A-)

Affection: 100

Psychology: With this power…』

She had successfully acquired the trait. And every single stat had been maxed out at SSS rank.

Among humanity, there was no one left who could defeat her. She was, without question, the strongest human alive.

『Galaxy Heavenly Wings (銀河天翼)

Trait Rank: SSS

Strengthens power.

The strengthened power attacks the essence.

Becomes starlight and moves to any desired location. Cannot be affected by anything during transit.』

I checked the description of Galaxy Heavenly Wings. If I were to put it in game terms, this seemed like a movement skill combined with a self-buff.

'In that it strengthens power, it's similar to Hwaryeonbi… no, maybe not. In that the strengthened power attacks the essence, could this be considered a higher-tier ability?'

I wasn't sure exactly.

Seeing is believing, as they say. This kind of thing can only be truly understood by experiencing it firsthand.

"Mom. How about a spar for old times' sake?"

"Hmm. Sure, why not. Yujin grows stronger by the day, so this time Mom might have a hard time winning?"

"You say that, but you've never once lost to me."

Ever since long ago, whenever I sparred with Seonghari, I placed certain limits on my strength. It wasn't as though I could go all out and risk killing her over a mere spar. Even setting that aside, if we fought seriously, the surroundings would be destroyed. So we had no choice but to limit our power and stick to fundamental martial arts sparring… and I had never once beaten Seonghari.

'If it's swordsmanship, I'm confident, but Seonghari's spearmanship—should I say it gets stronger in real time? No, it's more accurate to say it adapts to me.'

Just in terms of combat talent alone, she was enough to remind me of Yuria.

We faced each other in the Academy training grounds. She held a spear, and I held a sword. Silver lightning flickered along her spear.

'It's not ordinary white lightning but silver—that's actually pretty cool.'

Bzzt, crackle.

I also generated lightning. Blue lightning thrashed wildly before instantly converging and spinning into a single point at my will.

"As expected of Yujin. When it comes to handling lightning, even I can't match you."

Unlike me, Seonghari almost never used lightning directly in combat. She mainly used it to enhance her body, or to coat and reinforce her spear.

"If you applied lightning in different ways, it could help in all sorts of situations. Why don't you practice?"

"I've tried, but it doesn't work well. I suppose Mom just doesn't have talent in that direction."

Even as Seonghari spoke with a self-deprecating sigh, from my perspective as the listener, it was simply absurd.

Thunder Heaven Style (雷天流) — Myriad Lightning (卍雷).

A bolt of lightning shot out from the compressed electricity.

Seonghari, who already knew my technique, lightly swung her spear wreathed in silver lightning. As always, the lightning should have split to both sides. But the prediction was off. Instead of splitting, the lightning shattered. It shattered without even passing her spear.

"Huh?"

"Huh?"

Seonghari and I were surprised at the same time. It was a situation she hadn't predicted either.

'So that's Galaxy Heavenly Wings' power enhancement. To think it could collide head-on with formless lightning and obliterate it entirely.'

Still, we were in the middle of a spar. Seonghari collected herself and spoke to me.

"Yujin. Here I come."

Seonghari's silver wings blazed brilliantly for an instant. Then, she wrapped herself in starlight and sprinted toward me. A speed so fast that even I, who had been focused, could barely react.

[Using Flash (刹那). Remaining stacks: 11]

With Flash activated, reacting became much easier. I moved to deflect Seonghari's spear.

Her spear and body, wreathed in silver light, passed straight through me. She flew toward the wall, and just before impact, the silver wings vanished.

KWAAANG!

Seonghari couldn't overcome her inertia and crashed into the wall. Of course, the only thing that shattered was the training ground wall. Seonghari's body was fine. If the Academy Headmaster, Kang Jiyoung, saw the destroyed training ground wall, she'd start with a sigh.

"Ouch, ouch. So during transit I can't be interfered with, but conversely, I can't interfere with anything either. The conditions are pretty annoying."

"I was startled—it was like you'd become a ghost. Mom, isn't that ability totally broken?"

She dusted off the dirt and summoned her silver wings once more.

"It's not that broken. The stamina and mana consumption is pretty heavy. Keeping it active during combat would actually be a net loss."

Seonghari's eyes sparkled like a child who'd been given a new toy.

The spar continued until evening, and the result was Seonghari's victory.

Afterward, we decided to feed Dragon Hearts to Kang Jiyoung and the other S-rank heroes as well.

The only two Demon Corps Commanders remaining in the Demon Legion.

Mekin and Baal.

The two met face to face for the first time in nearly half a year.

"Baal. You've grown stronger in the few months since we last met. Are you satisfied with the reserve rations?"

The same face as Seonghari. But with white hair and clad in black metal armor, Baal immediately furrowed his brow.

"Reserve rations? Those monsters?"

"Leftovers that couldn't become demons. To me, they're worthless trash since they aren't demons, but to you with the Authority of Gluttony, it's a different story. You've grown at least a little stronger through Gluttony's Authority. Isn't that right?"

"Yeah. I did get stronger. Strong enough to kill you."

"Kehehe. Did you think I'd been sitting idle all this time? Look at the things behind me."

Baal looked behind Mekin.

Objects were piled up. From things with easily identifiable forms—swords, spears, armor—to strange monster statues and other items whose purpose couldn't be discerned. But I could sense that each and every one of those objects held extraordinary power.

"I can tell they're all treasures imbued with power. So what are you going to do with them? Surely you're not planning to use all of them? No matter how much power an object holds, an object is ultimately just an object."

"Those are offerings for me. What they contain isn't just power. They hold the life of a single being, the history of a nation, the final moments of a planet—that's what makes them all the more precious."

Baal's gaze drifted to a particular object. A statue resembling a fur-covered seal. One of the races Baal had recently killed and wiped out.

"All of those are things plundered from colony planets…."

"Correct. And Earth will become a colony planet as well. I will collect the most precious items from among what's there."

"…Humanity isn't as weak as you think. It won't be easy."

"Hehe. You still haven't shed your identity as a human. You humans seem to take pride in the civilization you've built over thousands of years, but… it's only a few thousand years at most. The Legion has been warring with and dominating all manner of planets since before your kind even discovered fire. Science? There were several planets more technologically advanced than Earth. But in the end, we won. The majority were offered to the Demon God, and some became our slaves. Earth's future won't be any different."

Baal shut his mouth. Since becoming a Demon Corps Commander, she too had learned a fair amount about the Demon Legion. It was true that the Demon Legion's history stretched far longer than the history humanity had built on Earth. Demons didn't age or die thanks to the Demon God's blessing.

"…For the past few days, I've felt a gaze once a day. A gaze so distant that it leaves you unable to do anything. Is it the Demon God's gaze?"

"Correct. It's His gaze. Since all the other Corps Commanders died and only the two of us remain… paradoxically, we've caught His interest. It's also the reason we absolutely must succeed. If we use everything the Legion has and still disappoint Him… the result waiting for us might make death seem preferable."

"What is this Demon God? Is He the source of these Authorities?"

"I don't know. Knowing more about Him wouldn't change anything. He is indeed the source of the Authorities. Demons become demons and gain Authorities through His baptism. The condition for baptism is… well, being to His liking, I suppose. Though the fact that you, a fake, became a demon is an extremely unusual case."

Baal clenched his fist tight, then released it.

"…From what I can see, a demon's Authority looks similar to a human Awakened's ability."

"Humans aren't the only special ones. Those abilities existed in other lifeforms on other planets too. From what I've seen, the more vivid a species' soul, the more individuals with abilities they produce. That damned Demon Hunter had an incredibly vivid soul as well. Enough to draw His gaze."

"I heard that Demon Hunter ultimately refused to become a demon. He's dead, so how is he being inherited?"

Mekin gestured with his chin toward behind himself.

"This universe is full of special objects. It's not uncommon for power to be inherited through objects. Even the things you call gods sometimes leave their power in objects. Though none of them can compare to the original."

"…If the Demon God creates demons and grants Authorities through His baptism, then maybe Awakened also received their abilities through someone's blessing."

"Hmm? That's not entirely without merit. This universe is so vast it's impossible to perceive, and there exist beings who rival or surpass Him. It wouldn't be strange if there were entities scattering abilities to certain lifeforms. Baal, the origin of power doesn't matter. What matters is that the final battle isn't far off."

"I know. I have no intention of losing in the final battle."

"Hehe. What you're sensing isn't just His gaze. Baal. Keep in mind that you are no longer human. No—you were never even human. You were a fake, nothing at all."

"Shut up."

"Kehehe. Now then, Baal. Use the little time remaining efficiently. Deal with the trash on the remaining colony planets."

"Someday, I'll kill you too."

Baal departed after radiating murderous intent. Mekin let the killing intent wash over him and let his eyes gleam.

*'Baal. Still a monster whose limits can't be seen. If given infinite time, she'd surpass me. Once this is over, I'll kill her before she grows stronger.'*

The center of the peaceful sky where clouds drifted broke apart, revealing a star-studded cosmos, and a massive crimson island began slowly falling toward the ground.

The demon invasion had begun.
