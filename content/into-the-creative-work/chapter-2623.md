I sat in the office chair and opened my eyes, which I had closed for a moment. It's like déjà vu. I felt an enormous sense of incongruity.

,...Again?'

Should I say that this place isn't reality right now? It feels like I've returned from a distant place. Of course, this sense of incongruity disappeared in less than a second.

,Did my brain malfunction again?'

,It's just a feeling, but I've been feeling this way every month for the past month.'

There's nothing wrong with my body even if I examine it.

,If it's not my body, is it my soul? It may be the influence of the black magic I've been researching for a long time.'

I had no intention of giving up black magic. The more power I have to wield, the better. I use black magic with the black mana of Dark Hole, an external organization, so I could prepare for a state where I can't use magic because I've used up all the mana in my body.
