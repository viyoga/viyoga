Yamata no Orochi rampaged. Even from far away, the ripples of its power could be felt.

I concealed Susanoo's power and quietly hunted the yokai. After several days passed, I could feel Yamata no Orochi's power weakening.

"Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto, is this... truly the right thing to do?! Yamata no Orochi has gone mad, and tens of thousands of lives have been crushed upon this land! Among them were dozens of land gods! Is this truly correct?!"

So noisy.

Should I kill him?

After briefly deliberating, I suppressed my killing intent. I still needed the land gods' power.

"Have you still not realized? I killed Shuten Doji and grew stronger. If I kill all the remaining Three Great Evil Spirits of Japan, I will become strong enough to slay Yamata no Orochi. I know it is difficult for you. However, leaving Yamata no Orochi unchecked is the same as discarding the future. Yamata no Orochi's savage nature will eventually devour every human on this land."

"That... is indeed correct."

"I will not tell you to face Yamata no Orochi. Stall for time, endure, run. One week is all I need. Within the next week, I will kill Yamata no Orochi."

"...I will believe in you, Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto."

"Good, you catch on quickly. Do you know the locations of any notable yokai nearby?"

I exterminated the yokai the land god informed me of as I made my way toward Mount Suzuka.

Mount Suzuka.

Dark clouds had gathered thick in the sky, thunder rumbling day and night without pause, and the mountain itself was ablaze in patches, ash drifting through the air at all hours.

Here dwelled Ootakemaru, one of the Three Great Evil Spirits of Japan.

The moment I set foot on Mount Suzuka, something massive came leaping from beyond and landed before me.

It was a red-skinned oni. Its frame was enormous at three meters, its muscles looking harder than boulders. Ootakemaru. Equipped with three treasured swords, he twisted the corners of his lips upward.

"You may be hiding your power, but at this distance I can't help but notice. It's you. The war god who killed Shuten Doji—Susanoo!"

"You know your stuff. Today I've come to take your head."

"Susanoo! I know your story! Cast out from the heavens! The younger brother of Amaterasu, hunted by that Yamata no Orochi! You want my head?! Take it if you can!!"

Ootakemaru roared and drew two of his three treasured swords.

Flames blazed up from his body, and lightning churned violently in the sky.

I drew Hwaryeon Bido from my waist and released the Susanoo power I had been suppressing.

『The God of Storms (S-) responds.』

"Wind and lightning are mine."

The wind that had been hostile to me changed direction. The lightning grew still at my will, and torrential rain poured from the dark clouds, weakening the flames that had endlessly burned Mount Suzuka.

"Indeed! So this is the God of Storms! The martial might said to be first in all the heavens! Let this body verify whether that reputation is true!!"

Ootakemaru charged. I did not dodge—I set Hwaryeon Bido sideways and caught his twin blades.

My body was pushed back.

In pure brute strength, Ootakemaru was stronger than me.

"I am the strongest oni. In a contest of pure strength, I have never lost to anyone."

"Combat isn't decided by strength alone. And yet..."

"You wonder why Yamata no Orochi stays quiet even though you've unleashed a god's power? Mount Suzuka is my domain. For a time, I can hide even Yamata no Orochi's gaze."

"Why go that far?"

"I have waited for a worthy rival like you! This battle will not be interrupted by anyone!"

"Heh heh. What a fun one you are."

Thunder Heaven Style—Soaring Tiger.

Crackle.

Wreathed in lightning across my entire body, I lunged at him like a tiger.

KAAAAAANG!

The recoil from the difference in brute strength threw my stance slightly off as I was pushed back. Ootakemaru did not miss that opening and began raining down a flurry of strikes.

I gripped Hwaryeon Bido's hilt tightly with both hands and committed to defense.

KANG! KAAAAAANG! KANG!

If I simply kept blocking, it was obvious as fire that the force would accumulate in my body. When the blade strikes clashed, I twisted my edge to deflect the force away. Ootakemaru ground his teeth and increased the speed of his barrage.

I tried to pierce through gaps in his monstrous attacks, but it was hopeless.

'I've been in sword fights more times than I can count—there's no way I'd make a mistake.'

The moment I made a mistake, my body would be sliced apart and I would die. At least in a one-on-one, unless the opponent was overwhelmingly stronger, I would not slip up.

His raging sword strikes slowed at some point, and soon an opening appeared. No matter how powerful the body, as long as it is a living thing, limits come. All the more so in a battle where life is on the line—the stress on the body surpasses imagination.

Thunder Heaven Style—Swift Wind, Sudden Thunder.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 11]

[Using Setsuna. Remaining stacks: 10]

I spent without reserve the cards I had saved for the timing that would decide victory or defeat. Speed surged in an instant.

Thunder Heaven Style—Lightning Flash.

Thunder Heaven Style's fastest sword strike flashed with crimson light and cut through Ootakemaru's flame-wreathed body. Blood burst from his body. However, I realized the wound was not deep relative to the amount of bleeding.

"Damn tough body you've got."

"You're the first to wound this body to this extent!!"

After that, a fierce battle raged for thirty minutes. Wind and lightning and flame intermingled, and Mount Suzuka was half-destroyed.

Ootakemaru, his leg tendons severed, knelt on the ground bleeding from every part of his body. With Vajra Divine Thunder driven into his heart and his left arm severed, he did not crumble even at the brink of death.

"I have lost! Truly the war god first in all the heavens! Your swordsmanship was more divine than any sorcery! I, Ootakemaru, have realized that there is a divine realm even in swordsmanship! Kill me!"

I tightened my grip on Hwaryeon Bido with hands drenched in blood from my torn palm skin, and raised it high.

"Very well."

Thunder Heaven Style—Thunder Blade.

A blade of crimson lightning beheaded him.

『As the God of Storms, your influence grows.』

『The rank of God of Storms rises to S.』

『Through the power of the God of Storms (S), Strength, Stamina, Agility, and Durability rise by one tier.』

『Name: Sung Yujin

Strength: SS- Stamina: S+ Agility: SS- Durability: S+ Mana: SS-

Traits: Spirit Eye (SSS) Demon Hunter (SSS) God of Storms (S)

Skills: Spirit Contract (S+) Spirit Command (SS) Force Field (C+) Swordsmanship (S+) Spirit Script (SS)

Karma: Good 2,968』

I felt my physical abilities and Susanoo's power grow stronger.

'If it's now, wouldn't I be able to fight Yamata no Orochi head-on and win?'

Of course, I had no intention of fighting right away. If I killed the remaining one of the Three Great Evil Spirits of Japan—the White-Faced, Golden-Furred Nine-Tailed Fox, evaluated as the strongest—I would grow even stronger.

'I should collect the experience I can collect.'

Ootakemaru's corpse and treasured swords vanished, and Mount Suzuka began to shake as if struck by an earthquake.

Ootakemaru was practically the incarnation of Mount Suzuka. Mount Suzuka too began to collapse, affected by Ootakemaru's death.

"SUSANOOOOOOOO!!"

From far away, Yamata no Orochi's rage-filled roar could be heard. By now I was used to it—it didn't faze me at all.

I took out a spatial teleportation scroll and tore it.

• • •

I met with the land god.

It was to obtain information on the White-Faced, Golden-Furred Nine-Tailed Fox—the strongest yokai in Japan aside from Yamata no Orochi. The Nine-Tailed Fox was far stronger and more cunning than Shuten Doji or Ootakemaru, so this side needed thorough preparation as well.

'In the original work, the Nine-Tailed Fox hid her identity and operated as a court lady in the Imperial Palace, but in this dungeon, she doesn't even bother hiding—she rules the Imperial Palace itself.'

There were surely traps if I went charging in blindly.

"Takehaya Susanoo, I have ill news. The White-Faced, Golden-Furred Nine-Tailed Fox and Yamata no Orochi have formed an alliance and joined forces. Yamata no Orochi has taken position beside the Imperial Palace."

It was news that made me almost spit out curses the moment I heard it.

"Is that even possible? Don't great yokai obsess over their own territories?"

"It is because rumors spread that you, my lord, were targeting the Nine-Tailed Fox. The cunning fox, fearing you, summoned Yamata no Orochi to the Imperial Palace."

"Yamata no Orochi's greatest goal would be killing me... so there's no reason for it to refuse the fox's proposal."

"Not only that—every yokai in this land is gathering at the Imperial Palace. The reason is clear. Every yokai in this land fears you."

"I understand. To those yokai, the Imperial Palace where the fox and Yamata no Orochi reside must feel like the safest place."

My plan to devour and kill the fox that had bewitched the Emperor had fallen apart.

Attacking from afar from the Imperial Palace was also difficult. The fox and Yamata no Orochi would be on high alert.

"The White-Faced, Golden-Furred Nine-Tailed Fox is a being versed in every manner of sorcery. With the power of us land gods, she is an opponent we cannot touch."

"I know. I am thinking—be quiet."

I crossed my arms and fell into thought.

The strategy that immediately came to mind was to lie low for now. The yokai would not stay gathered in one place forever. Yamata no Orochi's temper was hardly ordinary either, so as time passed, its relationship with the Nine-Tailed Fox would sour—obvious as fire.

'Stalling for time isn't good for me either. In this dungeon, I can't use spirit summoning. That means the dungeon and the real world are isolated. The flow of time must be different... but there's nothing good about staying in the dungeon for long.'

In the worst case, I might have to spend decades inside the dungeon.

'That won't do. Since they're all gathered like that, I'd love to wipe them out with a nuke, but...'

There was a high chance it wouldn't work. Yamata no Orochi looked like it would survive a direct hit from a nuclear missile, and the Nine-Tailed Fox would manage somehow with sorcery.

'As expected, the most effective thing is guerrilla warfare. Attack from afar and run. Kill the yokai little by little and grow Susanoo's power. The fact that it could take quite a long time doesn't sit well with me.'

The land god watched my expression and spoke cautiously.

"Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto, the Nine-Tailed Fox's domain is protected by a barrier. Even teleportation does not work there, so land gods like us cannot invade."

"I have no intention of ordering you to go scout, so relax. ...No, wait. If teleportation doesn't work, does that mean spatial movement doesn't work?"

"Space is sealed by the barrier. Apart from physical coming and going, invasion and escape using spatial movement are impossible."

"Damn it. Then guerrilla warfare using spatial teleportation scrolls is impossible too. Yamata no Orochi, you bastard. With an IQ of 80, you actually thought to join hands with the fox."

"How about requesting aid from Amaterasu Ookami...?"

"Shut up. I was exiled from Takamagahara. The gods of Takamagahara would never help me."

"At least try speaking to them first..."

"I said shut up."

The current Susanoo had caused havoc in the heavens, in Takamagahara, and been exiled. To receive the heavens' aid, it went without saying that I would have to go and bow my head in apology.

'It's not even something I did—why should I be the one to apologize?'

That was the reason I was reluctant to receive the heavens' help. Above all, there was no guarantee the heavens would accept my apology just because I gave one.

'If an apology would have ended it, I wouldn't have been exiled in the first place.'

Suddenly, a good idea came to mind.

"I'll borrow the power of an outside force."

"Pardon?"

The mythical age inside this dungeon was not a closed world of Japan alone. It hadn't been long since I confirmed the existence of the Korean peninsula beside it and China beside that.

"Buddha, the Jade Emperor, Jesus, Zeus—there are plenty of gods with power. Heh heh. Just you wait."

"W-wait! Takehaya Susanoo no Mikoto!!"

Ignoring the land god's call, I tore a spatial teleportation scroll.

The price for borrowing an outside force?

Who gives a damn.

As long as I can kill the White-Faced, Golden-Furred Nine-Tailed Fox and Yamata no Orochi, that's all that matters.
