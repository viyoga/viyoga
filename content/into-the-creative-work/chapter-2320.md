Kung.

I fell flat on my back. Didn't even bother with a proper landing. With my current physique, I'm not so weak that a fall from a mere 4-5m would do me in. I could probably fall from 10m without a scratch.

, "Are, are you okay?"

Namgung Hwayeon approached in a fluster. I could feel her panic at seeing me sprawled out. As I tried to get up, she offered a hand. I took it and stood.

"I'm fine. My body's pretty sturdy, you know. Don't worry about it. It's my fault for carelessly putting my hand on my senior's shoulder."

"No, I overreacted. I would've sensed you coming normally... I was just too lost in thought."

I brushed the dirt off my back and asked her.

"Still, your reaction was pretty good. Did you do judo or something?"

"I learned various martial arts from my parents since I was young. Judo was one of them."

"You seemed troubled earlier. Want to tell me about it?"

"...It's a personal matter. I can't ask you, who's unrelated, for such a favor."

"Since I'm unrelated, there might be things I can say. Don't worry, I won't tell anyone else. I'm really good at keeping secrets."

"It's okay. I appreciate you offering to listen."

Namgung Hwayeon said with a slight smile. A clear refusal. Namgung Hwayeon wasn't the type of woman to be swayed with a few words.

'The problem is her favorability. If her favorability was high, she would've come to me when she had a problem.'

Fortunately, I had the ability to see her status window. I could infer what her problem was through Namgung Hwayeon's stats.

『Name: Namgung Hwayeon

Strength: B+ Stamina: B- Agility: B Durability: B+ Mana: C+

Trait: Ssaulabi (S)

Skills: Ghost Blade (S-), Chukji (A+), Wind Blade (B)

Favorability: 38』

'Her stats have increased overall.'

It was proof that she had been training hard. Even in the original story, it was difficult to have these stats at this point in time.

'Especially the Ghost Blade's rank being S-. I think I know what event happened to Namgung Hwayeon.'

In the original game, each playable character had an awakening event. It was an event where they learned new skills or techniques after experiencing the event, becoming much stronger.

The activation conditions for this awakening event differed for each playable character. In Namgung Hwayeon's case, it was the Ghost Blade's rank becoming S-.

Namgung Hwayeon was currently experiencing the awakening event. Judging by her worrying, it was definitely the early stages of the event.

"I'll be going now. There's not much time left until dinner, so be careful not to be late."

"You've been having restless dreams lately because of the Ghost Blade, haven't you?"

Namgung Hwayeon, who was leaving, stopped and turned to look at me. Her eyes, pierced by the truth, shook violently.

"How...?!"

Of course, I knew through the original story. I also knew other minor information. Namgung Hwayeon was a playable character, so there was a lot of information released.

I pointed to my eye with my index finger. Activating the Spirit Eye, it glowed gold.

"Ghosts and spirits have similar aspects."

"I, I see."

I see my ass. They're not similar at all. Namgung Hwayeon was just an ignoramus who didn't know much about spirits and ghosts, so she fell for it.

"I think I can solve your problem, senior... Want to talk about it?"

After much deliberation, Namgung Hwayeon approached.

We sat side by side on a bench. We held drinks from a nearby vending machine in our hands.

"I have been having an old dream through the ghost dwelling in the sword. A dream of fighting and killing as a Ssaulabi who existed on this land in the past. Through this dream, I was able to gain experience by experiencing actual combat in my dreams, allowing me to become stronger quickly."

"From your perspective, senior, you could call it a lucky encounter. Isn't that a good thing?"

"I used to have these dreams about once or twice a month. It was fine then. But recently, I've been having Ssaulabi dreams every day for the past week. I know it's a dream, but... when I wake up, my bed is soaked in cold sweat, and my body is exhausted, making it difficult to go about my daily life."

"It's hard to fall asleep at night because of the dreams, too. Right?"

"......"

Namgung Hwayeon nodded.

"Can you tell me exactly what happens in the dream?"

"I become a Ssaulabi on the battlefield and fight against enemies. Even though I realize it's a dream, I have no choice but to fight. If I give up and die, it will burden my comrades... But no matter how hard I try, the result is always the same. The number of enemies is too great, and the faction I belong to eventually loses. I also die, and the dream ends."

"Hmm. As for solutions, there are two: the direct approach and the unconventional approach."

"I'm listening."

"The direct approach is to win in the dream. You don't have to kill all the enemies in the dream. It's enough to find the enemy commander and fight and win."

"Doesn't seem easy. What's the other way?"

"Eliminate the cause of the problem. In this case, the Ghost Blade. In other words, get rid of the sword you're carrying, senior."

"That's not possible. The Ghost Blade skill I have only works when I use the Ghost Blade. The Ghost Blade itself is also difficult to find a new one."

If she used an ordinary sword instead of the Ghost Blade, Namgung Hwayeon's combat power would plummet.

"Then we'll have to go with the direct approach."

"...There's no choice but to fight until I beat the enemy commander."

"You won't be able to use mana or abilities in the dream, so... pure swordsmanship skills will be important. I'll help you."

"I can't do that. It takes up too much of your time. Midterms are just around the corner, aren't they?"

"Let's do this. I'm confident in practical skills, but I'm not confident in written exams. Help me with the written exams."

Namgung Hwayeon was ranked 1st in the 2nd year. She was a superhuman who had never lost 1st place since entering the school until now, in the 2nd year. There was a reason why she was the head of the disciplinary committee.

Namgung Hwayeon shook her head at my suggestion.

"Even so, I don't want to take up your time because of my personal problems. And... it's a bit much to say this, but I don't know if even you can help me. Aren't you a spirit caster?"

If you think about it logically, that's right.

My rumors had already spread throughout the academy, but few people knew the details of my skills. I was also more famous as a spirit caster, and from Namgung Hwayeon's perspective, I was only a 1st year.

"Senior. I'm a spirit caster, but... who do you think I learned martial arts from?"

"......"

Flustered. Namgung Hwayeon's body stiffened. I could feel her fighting spirit.

To Namgung Hwayeon, Seong Hari wasn't just a respected hero. When she was young, her family was saved thanks to Seong Hari. Although Seong Hari, the person involved, didn't remember it... it was an event that had a great impact on Namgung Hwayeon's life.

"Shall we spar once? Without using mana, just pure swordsmanship skills. Honestly, I don't think I'll lose."

『Namgung Hwayeon's Favorability: 35』

Namgung Hwayeon's favorability dropped. The provocation worked properly.

"...Okay. A spar once wouldn't be bad."

• • •

Namgung Hwayeon held a wooden sword in the middle stance and glared at Seong Yujin.

Seong Yujin's stance for the spar was poor. His legs were mismatched, he held the wooden sword in one hand, and he casually rested it on his shoulder.

I knew it. Seong Yujin was openly trying to provoke her. The purpose was to disrupt her composure.

Since I knew it, there was no reason to fall for it. But seeing that relaxed face made my blood boil.

He lives with Seong Hari and receives martial arts guidance from Seong Hari because he is Seong Hari's son. It's a natural thing. Because he is Seong Hari's son. Namgung Hwayeon envied that natural thing.

'...Useless thoughts. Let's focus on the spar now.'

For my sake, and for the sake of my opponent, I had to focus on the spar.

Namgung Hwayeon took a breath and approached Seong Yujin, stepping forward. In an instant, the distance narrowed, and the wooden sword slashed through the air, rushing towards Seong Yujin's shoulder.

As if he knew in advance, Seong Yujin lightly dodged by turning his body's axis. Namgung Hwayeon didn't waver. The spar had only just begun.

As time passed, Namgung Hwayeon's composure disappeared. None of her attacks worked on Seong Yujin. Most of them were dodged, and the attacks she thought were her best were blocked powerlessly.

On the other hand, Seong Yujin's attacks? Each one was an attack that pierced the vitals. Attacks that would have hit if her reaction was just a little late.

Seong Yujin's face was calm as he attacked. He wasn't even going all out right now.

Namgung Hwayeon gritted her teeth and swung the wooden sword.

Ttaak! Ttak! Ttak!

The wooden sword and the wooden sword clashed. Her sweat also flew through the air and fell to the floor.

Namgung Hwayeon felt her sword losing its sharpness and becoming dull. At this rate, wasn't she just swinging a club instead of a sword?

Ttaaaaak!

After pushing Seong Yujin's wooden sword away once, she stepped back and took a breath. And then she rushed back to Seong Yujin.

'Not with strength, but with technique...!'

The wooden sword and the wooden sword intersected. Namgung Hwayeon twisted her wrist as if she had been waiting for it. The wooden sword rotated, shaking off Seong Yujin's wooden sword. No, it should have shaken it off.

'Why is it still attached? Huh?'

Seong Yujin raised his wooden sword. Her wooden sword was also lifted up as if it were glued on. It wasn't that she had taken the strength off the wooden sword. Rather, she was giving it more strength. But the wooden sword didn't move as she wanted.

Namgung Hwayeon, flustered, stepped back. Seong Yujin didn't miss it and stuck to her. The wooden swords that had been attached to each other fell off. But Namgung Hwayeon, whose posture had collapsed, couldn't counterattack.

Ttak! Ttak! Ttaak!

She could only somehow block Seong Yujin's fiercely continuous attacks.

Peok!

The wooden sword hit her thigh. Namgung Hwayeon had to block the enemy's attack without even feeling the pain.

Even in the midst of the chaos, she sensed the result.

'I can't win.'

Both physically and technically. The opponent was overwhelmingly superior.

'But even if it's just one counterattack...!'

It was the moment she was about to counterattack, even if it was forced. She felt an overwhelming killing intent from Seong Yujin. Namgung Hwayeon, flustered, lost her footing and sat down on the floor.

Seong Yujin raised his wooden sword. The tremendous killing intent felt from it. Namgung Hwayeon blankly stared at him. There was a monster.

A monster covered in blood and death.

Experienced a brutal battlefield where blood and guts were rampant in a dream? Compared to what's in front of me, it's nothing. That was death and destruction.

"Ah, I got excited without realizing it. ...Senior?"

Seong Yujin's killing intent disappeared. The monster returned to being a person again. Namgung Hwayeon felt all the tension in her body release.

Jureureuk.

A warm liquid flowed out of her crotch and spread in all directions.

"...Hiccup!"

It was too loose.

She, who had shown an unsightly appearance to her junior, blushed and thought.

'...I, I want to pass out.'

It was an unfulfilled wish.
