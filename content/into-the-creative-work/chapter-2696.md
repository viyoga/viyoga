I rode in the carriage with Oswald.

The carriage was a bit bumpy, but it wasn't unbearable.

Sitting across from me, Oswald began to spill information at my request.

,"A great war happened in the center a few years ago, and famous Dragon Hunters participated...."

Well, the information wasn't that helpful. Still, it was the kind of common knowledge in this world, so I decided to listen at least roughly.

I knew from experience that things would get more annoying if I didn't have this basic common sense. Of course, even if it was common sense, I didn't need to know it in detail. I could just think, 'Oh, there was a big war a few years ago,' and move on.

My hands and eyes were doing other things. I took out my smartphone and started rereading the original [Dragon Hunter] from the beginning.

,'There's no better way to get solid information than from the original.'

Honestly, it was more boring than I thought.

,'I thought it was fun back then... is it because my brain is pickled in the stimulating developments of modern times?'

I swiped through the pages, sseueuk, sseueuk.

,'The protagonist's growth is fun. But the heroine....'

She wasn't hot. I guess it's a problem with the art style. In some pages, it was hard to tell if the heroine was a man or a woman, let alone a human. I never thought I'd see art collapse in a comic.

,'They draw non-humans amazingly well. Especially dragons, they draw them so well. Does the artist hate humans or something?'

They also draw landscapes and objects well. But they uniquely draw only humans strangely.

,"You have a fascinating machine. It's true what they say about the technological prowess of the East being quite advanced. But... may I ask what you're looking at?"

,"A comic."

,"Ah. A comic. I see. I like comics too."

,"Shut up. This is an important part, so be quiet."

,"...Yes."

Oswald shut his mouth.

The art style of the original [Dragon Hunter] improved as it went on. The heroine is also drawn quite prettily. The problem is that the main heroine's presence diminishes as it goes on, so she's rarely seen.

Then, around volume 15, the sub-heroine suddenly took the main heroine's place.

,'The sub-heroine is a dragon.'

After the episode where the protagonist succeeds in hunting an Elder Dragon, the original story suddenly went off the rails. Then, somehow, it continued well until suddenly a Dragon God appeared and a rapid development began.

The ending is that the sub-heroine, who became the main heroine, joins forces to kill the Dragon God and it ends. The protagonist becomes the best Dragon Hunter and lives happily ever after.

It was a happy ending. What happened to the main heroine who became the sub-heroine? I don't know either. She didn't even appear in the last volume.

I looked at the design of the sub-heroine and questioned the author's taste.

,'Anyway, the important thing is the protagonist and the sub-heroine.'

The protagonist and the sub-heroine were at the center of the incident. It's obvious since he's the protagonist, but... the incident gradually grows, and eventually the Dragon God appears and the entire world gets caught up in it.

,"Wait. Where are we going again?"

,"We are going to Gigantria, one of the few major cities even in the center. We will arrive in two days."

In the Dragon Hunter world, all the countries were destroyed due to the great war that occurred before the start of the original story, and only the nobles remained. Especially in the center, even those nobles are gone.

,'The Dragon Hunter Association headquarters is in Gigantria. In fact, the Chairman can be said to be the owner of Gigantria.'

How do I know that?

The protagonist arrives and settles in the major city of Gigantria around volume 8.

• • •

While in the carriage, I spent time watching or playing various creations.

If I found a comic or novel boring, I would watch it roughly and then quit. This is because it is registered in the Amusement World List even if I don't watch it to the end. And novels or comics that have been discontinued are also registered. In the case of games, they are registered even if I don't see the ending.

And there were cases where webtoons that were being serialized were also registered.

,'I don't really know the criteria for registering creations in the Amusement World List. What is certain is that in the case of completed works, they are 100% registered.'

And I also learned about the Iron Demon Dragon's Dragon Heart through inventory appraisal.

[Iron Demon Dragon's Heart

This is the heart of the Iron Demon Dragon.

When consumed by a person with suitability, there is a high probability of acquiring Iron Skin.

When consumed by a person with suitability, there is a low probability of acquiring Iron Manipulation.

When consumed in reality, there is a very low probability of acquiring Iron Skin (Lv.1 skill) or Steel (Lv.1 trait). Suitability is required.

Using the original can increase the probability of acquiring skills and traits.]

,'Consuming the Dragon Heart gives you a very low chance of getting skills and traits.'

Of course, there is a condition that you must have suitability.

,'The problem is that you can't tell what suitability is even if you look at the original. What is certain is that it has an effect in other worlds as well.'

I confirmed that some of the maids in the [Baekhwan] world have suitability for the Iron Demon Dragon.

,'The Dragon Heart can turn ordinary people into Awakened ones if they meet the condition of suitability. Moreover, Dragon Heart Blood is a panacea in itself.'

It is an extremely high-demand item not only in reality but also in worlds like [Baekhwan]. I felt like I had hit the jackpot in a world I hadn't even thought deeply about.

,'It will be sold for a ridiculously high price in reality... and in the Baekhwan world, I can govern more stably by bestowing it upon the maids who follow me.'

Through experience, I know that having power and maintaining power are not easy tasks.

Humans are creatures of forgetfulness. If 10 years pass in the [Baekhwan] world, commoners could forget their fear and start a rebellion. You must have power to maintain power easily. It's good to have a lot of power to use.

And it might be needed later. I decided to collect as many Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood as possible while I was in this world.

,"Oswald."

,"Yes, Hunter-nim."

,"I'll have to use the Iron Demon Dragon's Dragon Heart and Dragon Heart Blood. I'll pay you for it."

,"Ah. I, I see. If Hunter-nim wants it, I should give it to you, of course."

,"Do you have any other Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood? If you do, I'll buy them as is."

,"I'm sorry to say that I don't have any. Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood are items that sell like hotcakes even if they are expensive.... They were all sold in the previous city."

I nodded. Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood are items that turn ordinary people into superhumans just by eating them. In this harsh world, power is directly linked to survival, power, and wealth. It's an item you'd want to have even if you had to throw away all your assets.

I didn't bother to pressure Oswald and cause friction. This guy was quite useful.

,"Contact me if you get Dragon Hearts and Dragon Heart Blood. I will buy them at a higher price than the market price. I plan to stay in Gigantria."

,"Even a Young Dragon's Dragon Heart is at least 200 million Skels. Dragon Heart Blood is cheaper than that, but it costs tens of millions of Skels."

The currency of this world is Skel. Since long ago, dragon scales have been used as currency, and it has become the common currency.

Perhaps because it's a Korean comic, the value is similar to Korean Won at a 1:1 ratio.

However, Skel's coins and bills are made of dragon scales. Therefore, the value of precious metals such as gold, silver, and copper was significantly lower than in other worlds.

,"Money is no problem. Even if I look like this, I have quite a lot."

,"I knew that Hunter-nim was wealthy. How could you not have money when you have the power to hunt an Iron Demon Dragon with just one fist?"

,"As expected, I like you because you're quick-witted. What is the current market price for 1kg of gold bullion?"

,"As I recall, it was 20 million Skels."

I was surprised. In reality, 1kg of gold is over 100 million Won.

,"Are you serious? It's only 20 million Skels?"

,"Yes. Silver is more expensive than that. 1kg of silver is close to 30 million Skels."

,"...Gold is more expensive than silver? Why?"

In reality, 1kg of silver costs about 1.5 million Won.

,"That's because silver can cause great harm to certain dragons. Typically, the White Bone Dragon and the Vampire Dragon are vulnerable to silver. I know that weapons made of silver are essential when hunting them. On the other hand, there are no dragons that are weak to gold."

,"...Hmm."

I began to understand. No matter what anyone says, the resource of this world is the dragon. Whatever it is, it was natural for the dragon to be the standard.

,'If I buy silver in bulk in reality and sell it in this world, and then buy gold and sell it back in reality, I could become rich so easily. A kind of currency arbitrage?'

Of course, I had no intention of doing that.

I was too lazy for that. Besides, I have no idea what the repercussions would be.

Above all, I have 2 trillion Won in assets that I can use in reality, so why would I do such a thing? In reality, I make money just by breathing.

,'Still, I should dispose of the gold and silver in my inventory in this world.'

If a large amount of gold and silver is released, the market price will plummet and economic problems will arise... but that's a problem for this world. It was none of my business.

,'If the price of gold and silver plummets, I can sell something else.'

What would sell well?

Fortunately, I had a capable merchant, Oswald, in front of me.

I took out a pistol from my inventory.

,"This is a Glock pistol. You can kill someone you don't like with just one finger. How much do you think you would buy this for?"

,"A pistol, I see. It's not bad for self-defense. Gun regulations have been lifted due to the aftermath of the Great War... but the size is disappointing. The bullet caliber will also be small. It's hard to even scratch a Young Dragon's scales with this kind of gun... Hmm. 20,000 Skels? Yes. I would buy it for that much."

Only 20,000 Won?

For a pistol that is hard to get even in Korea?

I was briefly disappointed, but then I remembered that the civilization level of this world was strangely developed only in terms of combat. There was a reason why Oswald wasn't that surprised even when he saw the smartphone.

After thinking for a moment, I took out something that wouldn't exist in this world.

,"T, this is...?!"

,"Ah, you don't know? It's an onahole. It's a product used for masturbation."

,"H, Hunter-nim. If you have sexual desires, you can go to a prostitute, so why use this kind of thing?"

Oswald looked at me with incomprehensible eyes.

I, who was holding a 10,000 Won new product onahole in my hand, paused for a moment and said.

,"If you sleep with prostitutes carelessly, you'll get a sexually transmitted disease."

,"These days, the medicine is so good that you don't get sexually transmitted diseases...."

,"......"

I belatedly realized that this world is a world where not only science but also magic and alchemy are developed.

If it's fantasy, shouldn't they live a little more uncivilizedly like fantasy....
