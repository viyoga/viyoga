,What a fucking bastard!!!"

Kwaang!

Jin Chunghyeon's furious fist slammed down on the desk in his office. His eyes, usually the epitome of cool reason, were bloodshot, and his lips were parted in a snarl. His waxed hair was disheveled, and his expensive suit was crumpled.

Just then, the office door burst open, and his secretary rushed in, breathless.

,Director! The headquarters building is under attack! The security team says they're using bomb drones! We need to get out of the headquarters building immediately!"

Seeing his flustered secretary, Jin Chunghyeon actually became calmer.

,Secretary Park, calm down. Have you forgotten how sturdily we built the headquarters building? It won't collapse from something like this. Besides, we just reinforced the building not too long ago. Drone bombs? It's not like a plane is crashing into it; it can withstand much more than that."

,Y-yes, that's right. There's no way the headquarters building would collapse from just this."

,Any other attacks?"

,No, there aren't. It's strange to think the military is behind this. If the military decided to punish us, it wouldn't be like this... Could it be the work of a traitor?"

,Seong Yujin. It's that bastard's doing."

,You mean Seong Yujin, the representative from Seongak Elementary School? Why would that guy, who should be in Seoul...?"

,I don't know what's going on in that crazy bastard's head. And I don't need to know. What's important is that the son of a bitch is coming down from the roof of the building. Daring to spew bullshit about killing me!!"

,D-Director, please calm down. If Seong Yujin is coming down from the roof of the building... then we can just go down first and leave the building. Surely he won't follow us all the way to the city? Busan is practically Ilwoo Group's territory."

,Leave the headquarters? Don't be ridiculous. Do you think I'd abandon the headquarters because I'm afraid of some punk like that?"

,I'm just saying this in case of an emergency. The security team has lost nearly half its members due to this bombing, but... the other half is still intact. And we also have him."

,That's right. There's no way the headquarters' power can't handle just one person. Order the head of security to kill Seong Yujin no matter what."

,Understood. You said he's coming down from the roof. I'll give the order to the head of security right now... Oh, wait. If he's coming down from above, then the Chairman's office is..."

,Have you forgotten that the Chairman-nim is in Japan?"

,I haven't forgotten. But isn't the Vice Chairman's office right below the Chairman's office? Could he be targeting...?"

The Vice Chairman.

It was Jin Chunghyeon's father. Startled, Jin Chunghyeon contacted his father on his smartphone. The signal rang once briefly, and his father answered the phone.

,Father! Come down to the lower floor immediately! A crazy bastard is targeting you!"

The urgent Jin Chunghyeon's face crumpled at the voice that followed.

-Too late. Your father's already been dismembered. Should I take a picture and send it to you?

,That fucking bastard finally did it!!"

-Finally, my ass. You and I are enemies. You tried to kill me, and now I'm trying to kill you. It's a shame that all your other family members are in different places? It would have been nice if they were all together... Anyway. You're next. I'm coming to kill you, so wait for me.

Click.

The call was disconnected.

,Aaaaaaagh!"

Jin Chunghyeon screamed and threw his smartphone. The smartphone shattered as it collided with the wall.

Jin Chunghyeon gritted his teeth and ordered his secretary.

,Secretary Park! Tell the head of security! Seong Yujin! Kill that bastard! No, capture him alive if possible! That bastard killed my father... He messed with our family! I'll never forgive him!!"

,Y-yes, I understand. I will relay the instructions to the head of security immediately."

,And take that out too!"

,When you say 'that'...?"

,The combat robot we developed! BP, was it? Deploy it immediately!"

,But BP is still in the experimental stage..."

,Deploy it!"

,Yes, yes. I understand."

,And request his cooperation as well."

Jin Chunghyeon's voice became more cautious. He was someone Jin Chunghyeon couldn't treat carelessly.

,I will follow your instructions. So please, let's go downstairs for now. The fire in the building has been mostly extinguished."

,I'll stay here."

,Pardon?"

,Me, abandon my office and run away because I'm afraid of that bastard? That's absolutely unacceptable. I'll watch that son of a bitch from my office."

Jin Chunghyeon's eyes blazed. Jin Chunghyeon's stubbornness was famous even within the Ilwoo Group. Ten years ago, that stubbornness shone brightly and earned him recognition in the Middle East. It was thanks to that achievement that he now held the position of director.

The number of people who could break Jin Chunghyeon's stubbornness was small enough to count on one hand. The secretary was not among them. The secretary quietly bowed and left the office.

I killed Jin Seongman, the Vice Chairman-nim of Ilwoo Group. I killed Jin Seongman, Jin Chunghyeon's father, as soon as I saw him without even exchanging a word. Just in time, Jin Chunghyeon called Jin Seongman, and I informed him of Jin Seongman's death. Hearing Jin Chunghyeon's enraged voice made me feel great.

,Jin Chunghyeon's office is five floors below here. Looking outside, I can already see the police gathering. Let's move quickly.'

I can't take on all of them alone. My stamina will run out, and I'll be defeated.

,Considering the time to escape, I need to kill Jin Chunghyeon within 10 minutes.'

I leave the Vice Chairman's office and head downstairs. I can't use the elevator. I used suicide drones to blow up the elevators as a priority.

I quickly descend the stairs, and security guards appear. Guys wearing bulletproof vests, rifles, and motorcycle helmets.

,State-of-the-art equipment. Their technology level is high. These guys are using equipment that even the military hasn't commercialized? There's definitely a technologist in the Ilwoo Group.'

If possible, I'd like to kill the Ilwoo Group's technologist too, but I don't have any information. How can I kill someone when I don't even know who they are or where they are?

,Terrorist!!"

,Die!!"

The security guards mercilessly sprayed bullets. I ran along the walls of the staircase, dodging the bullets and approaching them.

Seogeok!

The lightly swung blade slices through their necks. Ignoring the bodies tumbling down, I continue to descend.

Ppi bi biik!

A red light flashed in the middle of the stairs.

,A bomb?!'

Instantly recognizing the situation, I change direction and go up.

Kwaaaaaaang!

The entire staircase is blown away. If I had been even a little late, I would have been caught in the explosion and my whole body would have been torn to pieces.

I could feel the presence of people under the billowing black smoke.

Lightning Stream Electromagnetic Wave.

Under the destroyed staircase, six armed soldiers were aiming their guns upwards, waiting for me to appear. If I showed myself even a little, they would pull the trigger.

,Eat this.'

I took out a C4 from my backpack and threw it. Perhaps because the C4 was small, their reaction was slow.

Kwaaaaaaang!

The intense explosion pulverized the enemies. I jumped down towards the burning staircase. Killing the soldiers blocking my way, I arrived at the floor where Jin Chunghyeon's office was located.

As I entered the hallway, there was a robot. It was a robot with a white metal body and dozens of legs, similar in appearance to a woodlouse.

,Even though it's a robot, it's still creepy with so many legs.'

The robot's head had lenses that looked like eyes, emitting a red light.

,H-hello, c-customer-nim. T-this is a p-place where entry is p-prohibited. P-please, d-die!"

,Looks like the machine's gone crazy. Or is it still incomplete?"

,Goodbye, goodbye! Customer-nim!"

The robot moves its dozens of legs and rushes at me. It seems to be coming straight at me, but it moves back and forth like an insect, crawling on the walls. Its speed was so fast that even my eyes had difficulty tracking it.

,If I can't track it with my eyes... then I can perceive it with other senses.'

Lightning Stream Electromagnetic Wave.

Electromagnetic waves spread out, confirming the robot's location. It would be convenient to use EMP, but I decided to save it since it consumes a lot of stamina.

,Above my head.'

The robot, which had been hanging from the ceiling, falls. All of its dozens of legs had turned into sharp blades.

I hooked the falling robot with my blade and slammed it into the floor. The overturned robot bug flails, trying to flip itself over.

,N-no way. It hurts. It hurts!"

,A robot being so noisy. It hurts? Alright, I'll make it comfortable for you."

I stabbed the blade between the joints of the robot's body.

,Lightning.'

An electric current flowed through the deeply inserted galchi sword, damaging the robot's circuits.

Pung!

With the sound of something exploding, red liquid flowed down from the robot's body.

,C-c-customer-nim. The bill is, on the first floor!!"

The robot slumped down like a dead bug.

I frowned as much as I could. I felt a strange sense of unease. I moved my sword. Avoiding the armor, I cut the joints and dismantled the robot.

Inside the robot, along with complex machinery, was a brain.

It looked similar to a human brain... but its size was small.

,Is it the brain of another animal? Like a monkey or something? Did they use an animal's brain instead of AI?'

I wondered if something like this was even possible, but I recalled the characteristics of this world and nodded. It was a technology that was entirely possible.

Leaving the robot behind, I moved forward. This time, a man appeared. Dressed in black leather clothes, he stared at me emotionlessly.

,Even I wouldn't have been able to commit such an act... You're quite impressive."

Despite not having any particular equipment, he had a confident and relaxed attitude. Sensing something strange, I threw the last C4 in my backpack at him. The C4 stopped in mid-air. As if caught by something invisible.

,Psychokinesis?'

,You're carrying something dangerous. I'll take care of this."

Kwajik.

The C4 is completely disassembled and falls to the floor. He dismantled the C4 in an instant without touching the detonator.

,To be able to use psychokinesis like that... It's impossible for an ordinary Awakened. You, are you a Player?"

The four classes of Players. Lord, Esper, Technologist, Bionix. The man was definitely an Esper, one of them.

Espers may have less potential compared to other classes, but in the early stages, they are a class that uses more powerful abilities than any other class.

I thought I would encounter one someday, but I never expected to run into one here.
