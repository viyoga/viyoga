“Keo-heok…!”

Shahram vomited blood, his eyes wide open. His bloodshot eyes, as if his blood vessels would burst, glared into the void.

The Pain of being Slashed by the black blades did not disappear. Even though he was clearly Possessed by a shadow spirit and it shouldn’t have involved his real Body, he still vomited blood. It was an unbelievable occurrence.

“Just as the Body influences the Soul, the Soul also influences the Body. Stay still. Currently, your Soul is full of wounds. I will provide emergency treatment, but… you will need at least several months of recuperation once this is over.”

Shahram turned his Head to the side.

Dozens of Spellcasters were lined up, casting Spells for Shahram. Thanks to their Spells, Shahram felt the Pain quickly subside.

In their center was an old man wearing a turban. His eyes were deeply sunken, and his white beard, reaching down to his neck, was curly. He was the old man who had just spoken to him. In his left Hand, he held a wooden Staff taller than himself.

He was Grand Spellcaster Mahir, known as the Sage of the Desert.

He was the foremost Spellcaster in the desert. Although he appeared benevolent, Mahir was a ruthless man who wouldn’t hesitate to perform human sacrifice rituals.

“The assassination failed.”

“Hoo. Did Jibril-nim not prophesy that this assassination would succeed?”

“Are you insulting Jibril-nim now?”

“Calm down. I was merely surprised for a moment. I am well aware that her power is not as complete as it was when she was in the heavens. Furthermore, aren’t there many Gods in this world with a similar status to her? How did the assassination fail?”

“That bastard wasn’t asleep; he was sitting on the Bed, waiting for me. He knew I would come. Moreover, he deceived me with an illusion. …No, it’s hard to be sure if it was even an illusion.”

“Hmm. There’s no possibility of Betrayal. This assassination operation was executed urgently, and everyone here was together.”

“I don’t doubt you. That bastard also uses Spells, doesn’t he?”

“That’s right. To be precise, that bastard uses evil Spells. And it’s a method that borrows the power of an evil God. Originally, it’s a method that shouldn’t allow one to live long… but the King of Macheon’s status isn’t low, and they seem to have a good relationship. Be wary of illusions. Merely being wary reduces the chance of falling victim to an illusion by over 80%.”

“…Does that apply to the Illusionist Duke’s illusions as well?”

“Nonsense. That person is a monster among monsters, one who blurs the boundaries between illusion and reality. Naturally, they are an exception. Are you perhaps trying to assassinate the Illusionist Duke as well?”

“I merely asked. Isn’t the Illusionist Duke the most famous illusionist on this continent?”

“Among the Empire Ogong, the one I am most wary of is the Illusionist Duke. I’m not simply talking about the martial power she possesses. Because I understand Spells, I know how absurdly powerful she is.”

“Isn’t it because your disciple fell victim to the Illusionist Duke that you fear her?”

Mahir’s disciple fell victim to the Illusionist Duke four years ago. He was caught by the Illusionist Duke while spying on the Empire.

His disciple, afflicted by the illusion, mistook a passerby for a Monster and attacked them, and in a delusion, killed his own child, believing them to be an insect. In the end, he regarded his Master, Mahir, as an enemy and tried to kill him. Ultimately, Mahir killed his disciple with his own Hands. The disciple, caught in the Illusionist Duke’s illusion, was reduced to a mere Toy.

“…I can’t say that’s not part of it. But do we have time to argue with this old man now? The Demon is coming. You must make a decision as the Leader. What will you do?”

“……”

Shahram closed his eyes.

He was an exile who arrived in the early days of Atlantis.

At that time, continentals greatly shunned exiles, and there was no foundation whatsoever for them. It was a time when survival was paramount. Shahram received a revelation from Jibril.

He became an Assassin according to the revelation and contracted with the Old Man of the Mountain.

“…Everything I am now is because Jibril-nim guided me. I dedicate my everything to Jibril-nim.”

“Fanaticism, I see. But wasn’t it the Emperor who commissioned you for this task?”

“That too is Jibril-nim’s will. You, too, are here by Jibril-nim’s revelation, aren’t you?”

“Indeed. And there must be many more who have received revelations from her, like you and I. Do you not intend to go to the Empire, where she resides, and plan for the future?”

“Don’t say things you don’t mean. Will you abandon all the foundations you’ve built here?”

Mahir smiled quietly. The meaning of that seemingly benevolent smile was simple. He, too, had no intention of fleeing.

Shahram continued.

“The Heavenly Demon is growing stronger at an absurd pace. Perhaps within a few years, all the deserts of Atlantis might become the Heavenly Demon’s. For her great cause, and for our great cause, the Heavenly Demon must be killed.”

“True words. There is something we must do for that. The reason our Spellcasters cannot exert their power right now is because the Heavenly Demon possesses the power of Spells. He is not an outstanding Spellcaster. He is merely a lucky young Spellcaster who met a good God.”

Shahram looked at Mahir as if telling him to continue.

“We must ask the King of Macheon, the Heavenly Demon’s contracted Divine Seat. To not interfere in this matter.”

“A good method. Why haven’t we done it until now?”

“Evil Gods are difficult to predict in many ways. Furthermore, the King of Macheon is an evil God among evil Gods. He is a being no weaker than Shaitan. We might have to use all the sacrifices we’ve prepared.”

“If, as a result, your Spellcasters can inflict a Fatal Wound on the Heavenly Demon… it’s not a bad deal. Try it.”

Mahir did not conduct the ritual himself but had another Grand Spellcaster do it. Mahir was not a Spellcaster who conversed with Gods. Even among Spellcasters, their specialties differ slightly.

The ritual, attempted by sacrificing one of the Empire’s criminals, was successful. It connected with the King of Macheon.

“King of Macheon-isiyeo! A deal with you.”

Pang!

The Grand Spellcaster’s Body exploded and died. His blood, Flesh, and Intestines stuck upside down to the ceiling instead of the floor.

-Keu-keu-keu-keu-keu.

The King of Macheon left behind a Mockery and vanished.

Mahir stroked his beard.

“It’s a failure. Why on earth would such an evil God show such favor to a single human… I cannot understand it.”

“…The Grand Spellcaster died absurdly. Is this acceptable?”

“Don’t worry. There is a way to seal the Heavenly Demon’s Spells. According to information, the Heavenly Demon relies on a ritual tool to cast Spells… so we just need to destroy that ritual tool. However, there will be many sacrifices.”

“Do it. If we can remove the Spells from the Heavenly Demon and weaken his power even a little… that will be enough.”

“That will be handled by other Spellcasters. I will go prepare something separately.”

“Prepare?”

“This might be the Last, so I must do my best. I am going to meet the Black Pharaoh.”

• • •

Mahir muttered as he walked along the dark seashore.

“Black Pharaoh. I know you are watching.”

「The Black Pharaoh watches you.」

The Black Pharaoh reacted.

Mahir did not know the Black Pharaoh’s true identity. Although he was called the Sage of the Desert, none of the Divine Seats tried to contract with him. The reason was probably the Black Pharaoh.

“Long ago, you gave me wicked knowledge. It is thanks to you that I am who I am today.”

In his view, the Black Pharaoh was neither an evil God nor a good God.

The Black Pharaoh could not be judged by human standards of good and evil, and what the Black Pharaoh desired did not seem to be good or evil either.

“This time, too, share your knowledge with me. Of course, unlike then, I will not ask for it for free. I will offer a price.”

「The Black Pharaoh laughs.」

The deal was struck.

Wicked knowledge became an awl and pierced Mahir’s brain.

“Aaaaargh!”

Mahir clutched his Head and Screamed. Simultaneously, he used a Spell based on the wicked knowledge. His Legs moved towards the sea. He Screamed and entered the sea as if possessed.

When Mahir opened his eyes on the sandy beach, the sun was slowly rising.

Mahir had a dream. In a place with a colossal pillar, he met some entity and agreed to receive help by offering a price. He couldn’t remember what kind of entity it was, what the price was, or what kind of help it was.

The sea breeze carried a fishy smell.

• • •

We ate a hearty breakfast and entered the port City of the Black Desert.

I didn’t tell anyone else about Shahram’s assassination attempt in the early morning. Telling Kasha or Enas would only lead to reproaches. From my perspective, it was nothing significant, so I decided to let it pass. Honestly, I hadn’t even noticed Shahram’s intrusion. Shahram, the ‘Footless’ Assassin, truly had genuine assassination skills.

And anyway, Shahram would die by my Hand today.

“The City is quiet. I can feel all the citizens hiding in their homes. The soldiers are also nowhere to be seen. They are probably gathered in that palace. It seems they intend to fight to the death. How foolish.”

Enas said, Mockery in her voice, as she looked at the small palace in the center of the City.

I looked around, then towards the sea. The surging sea was more pitch-black than blue.

“It smells fishy.”

The fishy smell mixed in the sea breeze was extremely irritating.

“Huh? Isn’t a port City usually like this?”

“Haha. Enas-ssi. Even for a port City, the fishy smell isn’t usually this strong. This is clearly abnormal. There’s a possibility that Ohel has pulled another trick.”

“…For now, I don’t sense any traces of Spells.”

I raised my Hand to get their attention, then spoke.

“To the palace.”

In front of the palace, soldiers armed with bows, spears, and Swords were gathered in formation. Their numbers were in the hundreds. Although that number was small compared to the City’s size, quality is prioritized over quantity in the desert.

Their level was much higher than that of ordinary Empire soldiers.

“Baekhwadan. You’ll have to put in some effort.”

A few Baekhwadan members might die here.

The Baekhwadan members were smiling before the battle. They were trained Assassins and female warriors. Treating them like flowers would be an insult. If I were to treat them like flowers, I wouldn’t have put them in the Baekhwadan in the first place.

We did not stop. As we approached the palace, the tension heightened.

I gave the command from atop the black wolf.

“Make it clear to those bastards that I am the Master of this desert.”

“As you command!”

The Baekhwadan charged towards the enemies.
