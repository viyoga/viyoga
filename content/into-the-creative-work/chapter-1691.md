A grenade flies.

Even for a Noble-class vampire, their skin isn't as tough as steel. You can tear their flesh with a knife, and bullets will lodge in them if you shoot. However, their excellent recovery makes them as good as new in just a few seconds. Silver bullets and silver-plated knives are effective in suppressing that recovery.

,But grenades are different from other attacks.'

The explosive power inflicts significant damage on vampires. Even if an ordinary grenade doesn't kill them instantly, it inflicts pain and can temporarily incapacitate them. And if their head gets blown off or their heart explodes? Even a vampire dies.

"Block it!"

Yana shouted, giving the order. But the vampires hesitated. How could they block a flying grenade? They didn't have shields or armor. Blocking that grenade according to Yana's order was suicide. There wasn't a single vampire here who would sacrifice their life for Yana.

In the end, Yana had no choice but to step forward herself. Her right arm transforms. Her fingers disappear, and her skin becomes a hard carapace. There was a hole at the end. From there, a clump of spiderwebs spewed out, wrapping around the falling grenade.

Kwaang!

The grenade exploded. The shrapnel scatters in all directions. Shrapnel grazed Yana's face. Yana wiped the blood from her face and raged.

"Catch that bastard! Catch him, you morons! Are you just going to keep getting hit?!"

"Hah, but that guy is wearing a bomb coat! That's a real bomb coat! With that amount, it could blow away this warehouse and the surrounding area in one go!"

"Just catch him carefully so it doesn't explode! You're not seriously suggesting we run away because we're scared of one guy, are you? Huh?"

Yana's eyes gleam with anger and madness. She seemed seriously pissed. Her vampire subordinates cautiously approach me.

"Why do you think that bastard is wearing a bomb coat? It's to stop us from attacking recklessly. Even that bastard doesn't really want to die. Just be careful of the bomb coat and subdue him!"

The vampires, emboldened by Yana's words, surround me.

"Hehehe...."

I chuckled. Doesn't want to die? Suicide bombing might be a last resort for others, but not for me. Self-destruction is just a means.

I indiscriminately threw grenades at the approaching vampires.

Kwaang! Kwakwakwaang! Kwaang!

Some of the shrapnel narrowly missed my body. Fortunately, the bomb coat didn't explode. Even though it looks like this, this coat is quite sturdily designed with advanced technology from the [Vampire Detective] world. It doesn't explode easily even if you move roughly like doing acrobatics.

Kwaang! Kwangkwangkwangkwang!

The number of vampires hit by the grenades was increasing over time. Yana pointed at me and shouted.

"Overwhelm him at once! All at once!"

"He keeps throwing bombs, so it's not easy! Besides, that guy is holding a detonator in his left hand! It might be the detonator for the bomb coat!"

Four fingers on my left hand, excluding the thumb, were completely broken. Maybe it was because adrenaline was being secreted, but I didn't feel much pain. And even if my fingers are broken, it's no problem to hold a detonator.

"You useless things!"

Yana, enraged, shot spiderwebs at me.

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 6]

Having been watching Yana the whole time, I used Transience as soon as the spiderwebs flew. I leisurely checked the trajectory of the spiderwebs and moved my body to the side. The spiderwebs brushed past me.

"Kill me if you can!!"

I started running around the warehouse like a madman. I threw grenades with the safety pins pulled out and attached C4 here and there. Explosions occurred wherever I went. The warehouse began to burn fiercely.

"Catch him! Catch him!"

Yana screamed. The reason she makes her subordinates do it instead of moving herself must be because she's afraid of the bombs. Bombs are equal to everyone.

Chwak!

A spiderweb flew and grabbed my right foot.

,She shot the spiderweb right when I took my eyes off her for a moment. She's no ordinary person.'

The effect of Heavenly Heart was over, so my right foot had no choice but to be caught by the spiderweb. I tried to shake it off by putting strength in my foot, but it wasn't easy because the stickiness was extraordinary.

"I caught him! Immediately catch that bastard and restrain him!"

Yana shouted, and the vampires rushed in.

"You crazy bastard!"

"Yujun died because of you!"

"Don't even think about dying comfortably!"

I pressed the detonator without hesitation.

Kwangkwakwakwakwakwaang!

Explosions occurred all around me. The warehouse walls and roof collapsed, and crimson flames soared into the sky. Immediately after, black smoke filled the air.

I was caught in the shockwave and collapsed to the ground.

,Huh? I didn't die?'

With this level of explosion, the bomb coat should have exploded. But the bomb coat didn't explode. Besides, I only fell to the ground and am still alive and well.

I got up and realized what had happened. Around me, spiderwebs larger than me were set up like walls. The spiderwebs had blocked me.

,Yana. Looks like that bitch didn't want to die either.'

Since it's come to this, it seems better to just die. I took out the detonator from my coat pocket. It's the detonator to detonate the bombs in the coat.

,She protected me with spiderwebs, so it seems she didn't run away.... Let's die together.'

It was the moment I was about to press the detonator.

Chwak!

A spiderweb flew and restrained my right hand. I was tightly bound by the spiderweb and couldn't even twitch a finger. I couldn't press the detonator.

I was about to slam my right hand on the ground in a hurry when. Chwak! Chwak! Chwak! Spiderwebs flew in succession and restrained my body.

In front. Something fell in front of me, piercing through the black smoke. It was the corpse of a vampire whose chest had been pierced by something sharp.

"I can't believe there would be such a fool who would ignore my orders and try to run away. I need to give some mental education when accepting new subordinates."

Along with a voice like scraping metal, something heavy approached me. Its form is obscured by the smoke, but I felt like I knew what it was.

Chwak!

A spiderweb flew and wrapped around my spiderweb. My body was pulled along with the spiderweb. At the end of the spiderweb was a giant monster spider. Over 3m tall, its lower body was that of a large, round spider, and its upper body was something similar to a human.

It was the true form of the Noble-class vampire Yana. Creating and shooting sticky and hard spiderwebs was Yana's special ability.

Yana grabbed my neck and lifted me up.

"You goddamn bastard. How did you take out things from thin air? Judging by the smell and appearance, you're definitely not a vampire.... Are you perhaps from a witch's bloodline?"

Come to think of it, there were not only vampires and werewolves in this world but also witches. Because they are such a rare race, even I, who read the original work, don't know much about them.

"Keu...."

I twitched my lips. My throat was sore when I tried to speak. Of course, my eyes were sore too. More than that, my whole body ached.

,I feel like I'm going to die even though I didn't do anything.'

I realized that my physical abilities had fallen to an ordinary level.

"How pathetic. It makes me want to puke just looking at you. How did you even live with that body, vampire. Keu keu."

I sneer at Yana. Yana's body isn't normal either right now. Two of her six legs were blown off, and half of her lower body was burned.

"What are you relying on to act so cocky? That fancy bomb coat? The bomb coat won't explode. You're done for. I'm going to kill you very painfully, Juseohyun or whatever."

Puk.

One of Yana's front legs pierces through my abdomen. Like the vicious bitch she is, she doesn't stop there and moves her front leg back and forth, gouging out my insides.

"It smells delicious. Unlike your crazy mind, your body is in top condition?"

Yana licks her lips. I glared at Yana and spat out the words.

"You made a mistake. You should have run away instead of coming to catch me! That was your only chance to get out alive."

"You're about to die, but you're still full of spirit. What's the reason? You can't even twitch a finger now. Bomb coat? Can't you see my spiderwebs around you? The flames won't touch your coat! It's not me who's finished, it's you!!"

I still had a means left. It's not about summoning items from the inventory. No matter what item I summon, Yana will react faster than me and snatch the item. I couldn't move my hands right now. Even if I had a spare detonator, I couldn't press the button.

,There's no rule that says I have to detonate the bomb with a detonator.'

Tik.

Something flickered.

Yana narrowed her eyes and glared at me.

"Static electricity?"

I use lightning to generate electricity. Even if I don't have mana, I can use lightning by consuming vitality. However, as expected, static electricity wasn't enough to produce enough power.

,If I go all out, I can reach the level of a stun gun...!'

I grinned. I already knew where the fuse of the bomb coat was.

"Goodbye, you fucking bitch."

"What?"

Pajijijijijik!

In an instant, sparks flew, and everything disappeared with the explosion.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

I can't see anything because of the explosion.

Even though I don't know what happened to my body, my consciousness hasn't disappeared.

,There will be aftereffects from the explosion, so it wouldn't be good to use Complete Recovery right away.'

But if I waited too long, the warehouse might collapse. I use Complete Recovery and get crushed to death by the warehouse? That would be the worst situation.

,I can't use the Space Movement Scroll because I didn't set the coordinates for this world.... Damn it. I have to run like hell.'

I used Complete Recovery when 10 seconds had passed.

[Using Complete Recovery.]

The sensation in my body fully returned. At the same time, I felt heat. The inside of the warehouse was filled with flames. I held my breath and ran out of the warehouse.

[Using Acceleration. Lasts for 10 minutes. Remaining stacks: 5]

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 4]

The warehouse wall collapses.

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 3]

I narrowly avoid the collapsing wall by using Transience. If I didn't have Transience, I would have been crushed to death by the red-hot steel wall.

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 2]

I didn't spare Transience. If it seemed even slightly dangerous, I immediately used Transience to jump forward.

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 1]

[Using Transience. Remaining stacks: 0]

I came out of the warehouse.

The wind blowing from the sea shook my dick and disappeared.
