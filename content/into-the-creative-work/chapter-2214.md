As afternoon approached, people gathered at the hunting grounds one by one. Princess Elza and the nobles and businesspeople who followed her. And the invited reporters. Adding in the maids and butlers who escorted and attended them, and the mercenaries hired, the total came to over 300 people.

Yuria and Rita held their breath in the forest, watching the hunting grounds. Soon the sun set and the black moon rose into the sky. A moon darker than the night sky. The Dark Moon.

,There are no suspicious individuals. Let's carry out the mission. Follow me."

Yuria quietly followed Rita.

They arrived at a place with bizarre cliffs 4km away from the hunting grounds. Below them, a dense forest of trees spread out.

Kkiaaaaaaaaak!

A resounding, ghostly scream greeted them. Rita clicked her tongue, looking down below.

"This isn't good. It's being directly affected by the Dark Moon. The ghosts are swarming down there."

"Even with the Dark Moon, is it this stimulated?"

"It shouldn't be. Someone is cleverly using the power of the Dark Moon. There must be a moonstone down there."

Moonstone. A stone that fell from the moon. On days when the Dark Moon rises, this moonstone also turns black, negatively affecting its surroundings.

Naturally, moonstones are subject to special management worldwide. International law also stipulates that moonstones must be recovered and sealed. It was impossible to destroy a moonstone with force.

"Shouldn't national agencies be involved in recovering the moonstone?"

"The Congress doesn't get involved in matters outside of Neo London. Her Highness the Princess is nearby, but... because the Dark Moon is up, support can't come. It's a matter of Her Highness the Princess's reputation, so we can't avoid it. But we can't just leave a dangerous element alone. The worst could happen."

"Even if we find the moonstone, can we seal it?"

Rita took out a small string from her pocket.

"It's a magical tool with a sealing formula. With this, we can seal the moonstone. Now, let's go."

Rita hung a rope on the cliff and handed it to Yuria. They descended as if leaping, relying on a single rope.

Rita fearlessly advanced through the forest where wraiths appeared. Wraiths? When her silenced pistol spat fire, the wraiths disappeared as they were.

"How's the taste of the sacred bullets, forged and blessed with holy water by the baguettes!"

Rita, who was eliminating the dead, paused.

Suddenly, Yuria's presence disappeared. She pursed her lips and spread her senses. No sign of anyone was felt.

'She disappeared in an instant without any warning? Is it the work of ghosts? Or is it the culprit's doing? Could it be that they're targeting Yuria Grace instead of Her Highness the Princess?'

Rita's pace quickened.

• • •

The wind doesn't blow.

The sound of insects disappeared.

The clouds in the night sky don't even stir.

Yuria, who had some understanding of space-time, realized that time had stopped.

It probably wasn't that the world's time had stopped. Because Rita, who should have been in front of me, wasn't there. She carefully touched a leaf nearby. She could feel the texture of the leaf. Even if she pulled the leaf with force, it wouldn't come off. The leaf would crumple, but if she took her finger off, it would return to its original state as if nothing had happened.

'It seems like I'm trapped in the gap between time and time.'

Yuria raised her head and looked at the sky.

The ominous black moon looked down on her. Was it just her imagination? It seemed that only that ominous black moon hadn't stopped in this frozen time.

'Is the enemy coming?'

The enemy who trapped her in the gap between time and time. If their purpose was to kill her, would they have done something so troublesome? There was probably another purpose.

'Is their purpose to break my sanity?'

• • •

Above the cliff where the forest was clearly visible. At the end of it, a black man wearing a butler's uniform was sitting cross-legged. Kashing, who had his hands gathered, stared straight ahead. He saw something other than the forest in his eyes.

"Did it succeed?"

Behind Kashing, a woman with short, purple twintails, Becky, spoke. There were scars on her left arm, as if cut by a knife, while wearing a maid outfit.

"It succeeded. She's trapped in the gap between time and time. Thanks to the wraiths for buying time."

"Then, well, it's as good as over. The flow of time is different, so 1 second here is 1 million seconds there, right?"

"That's right. 1 second is 11 days. 30 seconds have passed, so 1 year has already passed in that space."

Because time was stopped, there was no chance of starving to death inside. Suicide was also impossible. Because the body doesn't change. She won't starve to death. She won't feel any physical desires, including the need for sleep.

Kashing could guarantee that even a superhuman of level 7 or higher couldn't last long in that space.

"What is that woman doing? The head of security went west. Let's take her out and brainwash her soon. Her mind isn't completely broken yet, is it?"

"She's training with a dagger. ...She's meditating now. Is she staring at the ground?"

"What are you talking about."

"The flow of time is overwhelmingly different, so it's hard to grasp. Her mind doesn't seem to be broken yet. We'll have to wait a little longer."

Becky looked at Kashing's head. Short black hair. White hair was sparsely appearing between them.

He and she were demon contractors. They can use the power of demons through a contract. But they can't just use it recklessly. There is a price to pay. In Kashing's case, he ages rapidly as he uses his power.

"Are you okay?"

"No problem. Today is the day the Dark Moon rises. My and your powers are stronger than usual."

5 minutes passed.

Kashing in his 20s had aged drastically to his 50s.

Yuria wasn't just sitting blankly. Because the time of her body had stopped, but the time of her mind hadn't. In a way, it was a lucky opportunity to train. She happened to have a dagger with her.

She swung the dagger around and soon shook her head.

'As expected, physical training is meaningless. At best, it's just to keep the senses alive.'

No matter how much she trained, her body wouldn't change.

Yuria knew she could use mana. She could swing and launch Sword energy. The problem was that it was meaningless. Even if she used mana to launch Sword energy, she couldn't cut a single leaf. Because time had stopped, there was no change. Even if she consumed mana, it would return to its original amount in the next moment.

There is no change.

This stopped world was simply forcing Yuria to mentally surrender. The enemy wanted her to go crazy.

But Yuria didn't go crazy. She couldn't go crazy. The absolute spirit she received from her master didn't allow it. Even if countless eons passed, her spirit wouldn't be worn away by even a speck.

'There is a way to escape.'

She touched her brooch.

She could escape this place with the power of concept severance, the power of the brooch gifted by Eugene. She already recognized the concept of space-time, so it was definitely possible.

But she didn't.

For her, this was an opportunity to quickly become stronger.

'Because I found something that hasn't stopped except for me.'

Her gaze turned downwards.

Shadow.

Only the shadow was reacting whenever she moved.

'The black moon hasn't stopped. It's a moon that's darker than the night sky, yet it emits light. Even if it's black, a moon is still a moon.'

Thanks to that, the environment for training was set.

Yuria sat down, crossed her legs, and focused her mind. She was thinking about Yeongcheon-style Yeongcheon Spirit Energy Art (影天氣功).

'The foundation of the Yeongcheon style is the Tai Chi. The Yeongcheon style's Lightning Flash means yang energy, and the Yeongcheon style's Dark Shadow means yin energy.'

This was also the reason why Eugene couldn't handle Dark Shadow. Eugene had the energy of extreme yang. His endless stamina must also be affected by that. Or, conversely, it may be that his yang energy is enormous because his stamina is high.

'The Grand Master who created the Yeongcheon style must have inevitably employed Tai Chi. Looking at the techniques of the Yeongcheon style, most of them are compatible with the Dark Shadow.'

Too much is as bad as too little.

If yin energy is too strong, or yang energy is too strong, problems arise. The Yeongcheon style balanced it with Tai Chi. What branched out from there was Lightning Flash. But Lightning Flash was a bonus power. The name of the Yeongcheon style itself is the Shadow's Sky, isn't it?

'My master is an exception. My master's Lightning Sky style can no longer be called the Yeongcheon style.'

Eugene's Lightning Sky style was mixed with not only the Yeongcheon style but also many other things. And it will continue to be mixed in the future.

'That doesn't mean the Yeongcheon style is weak. There is potential in the Yeongcheon style. The potential of Tai Chi. The Grand Master drastically increased the difficulty of Dark Shadow by putting the mysteries of Tai Chi into the balance of yang and yin energy, but he gave the Yeongcheon style even more potential than that. The beginning of all things. In other words, infinite possibilities.'

Paji jik.

Black lightning rises from Yuria's shadow. She frowned. The black lightning disappeared as it was.

What Yuria wanted wasn't black lightning with the power of destruction. The power to control shadows. Because she couldn't use magic due to restrictions, she tried to awaken shadow ability using the Yeongcheon style. But it didn't work as well as she thought.

Several days passed. Yuria failed repeatedly.

'My power is lacking. I'll have to dismantle the Tai Chi mysteries and limit them.'

She didn't mind cutting and modifying the Yeongcheon style. Originally, it would be crazy. Because her body wouldn't be able to withstand it. But here is a space in stopped time. She can't die even if she wants to. It's a perfect place to experiment.

It was around 247 days, 6 hours, 11 minutes, and 50 seconds after being trapped in the gap of time.

Will was conveyed from her brooch.

-I'm speechless. Do you know you're in a dangerous state right now?

Yuria doesn't overdo it unless it's related to Eugene. Because her body belongs to Eugene. Taking care of her body is her duty.
