Barbara feigned a stab with her spear, then swung it wide horizontally.

A simple trick. But the scrubs lose their lives to this simple trick. Of course, Yuria effortlessly saw through the attack and dodged it.

,Ice.'

Barbara muttered inwardly. One of the spears on Yuria's right disappeared, and cold air burst forth. The cold swept in all directions. Yuria was no exception. Yuria's body didn't freeze instantly, but the cold had the effect of numbing her muscles.

,Let's see if you can dodge this too.'

Barbara is essentially a spear master. Summoning magic? That's just a side gig. Her true skill is the spear technique she's honed since childhood.

Uuuuuung.

The spear vibrated, and mana coated it. The spear became an extension of her.

,Azure Dragon Ascendant.'

Barbara's movements were rough, but her spear was delicate. The harmony of the two created an elegant yet dangerous spear technique. But Barbara's spear didn't reach Yuria.

Just barely.

It seemed like it would reach if she went just a little further, but it didn't. That precariousness gradually disappeared over time.

It was being analyzed in real time. And it was being countered in real time.

,...What is this.'

The tip of the spear trembled slightly. Yuria didn't miss the opportunity and dove into Barbara's inner guard.

Yeongcheon Style Secret Art.

A silent strike was unleashed. Usually, when a weapon is swung, there are sounds caused by the vibration of the air. But this strike was different. It had no presence at all. Barbara was able to avoid this attack only because she could see it. A secret strike that she would have only known about after being hit if it had targeted a blind spot.

She rotated her spear. She forcibly bent its trajectory for an unconventional movement. But Yuria dodged even this, as if she had seen through it.

She hated to admit it. Her opponent had grasped her spear technique, and even her movements, in an instant.

,My 20 years have been countered in 20 seconds?'

Her face twisted. Anger surged. Talent. The difference in overwhelming talent fueled her jealousy.

,Blast.'

She used up one spear to create a shockwave. This time, too, she aimed for the back of Yuria's head, just like before. But Yuria dodged it lightly, as if she knew it was coming. Silver hair fluttered under the moonlight.

Barbara gritted her teeth.

,Let's go all the way. In the end, I'll be the one who wins.'

Barbara used Solid.

If mages have Astral, warriors have Solid. Solid is mental acceleration. A technique that accelerates the mind to forcibly enter an awakened state. A technique that almost all warriors above 3rd grade learn as a necessity.

Barbara's movements became faster, and her spear technique became sharper. Every time she swung her spear, blue flames flared up.

In the Solid state, some of one's potential and latent abilities are unlocked. This potential includes not only physical abilities but also supernatural powers and factors. The blue flames were Barbara's potential and latent abilities.

The hot heat of the blue flames burned a part of Yuria's skirt. Yuria's eyebrows twitched. Barbara smiled at that.

,Why? Getting a little nervous now? Feeling like you're going to die? Why don't you act all cocky like you did before!"

,I don't think I've ever acted cocky, though."

,That's what I'm talking about, you're being cocky!"

Four spears disappeared and became 20 arrows, which were fired at Yuria.

In front of her was Barbara in Solid state. On her sides and behind her were 20 arrows.

Yuria jumped upwards.

,You stupid bitch. I got you right where I wanted you. I knew you'd jump up to dodge. Most of those quick-moving types dodge upwards.'

Barbara, who had already anticipated this, immediately jumped up and chased after Yuria, who had jumped upwards. Her spear was wreathed in blue flames, taking the shape of a dragon.

Thwack.

,Huh?"

Knives were simultaneously lodged in Barbara's thigh, abdomen, and chest. The spear that was moving forward rapidly lost its power. Barbara fell, wondering what had happened. She could see the knife holster wrapped around Yuria's left thigh, inside her skirt.

,When did she take out the knives and throw them? I didn't see it?'

What came to mind was when she had thrust out her spear. Her vision had narrowed then because she was concentrating. But it was less than a second. Was she able to capture and utilize that?

Barbara, who had fallen to the ground, looked at Yuria's face. It was emotionless. As if this result was obvious. She was certain of it. It wasn't a coincidence or anything. She had aimed for it.

,...You monster. She didn't just counter my spear technique, she countered me.'

Living a rough life, she had traveled to many places and fought on many battlefields. In the process, she had seen quite a few so-called geniuses. Most of them couldn't even bloom their talents and crumbled and disappeared on the battlefield. This was because they didn't focus on survival like Barbara, but risked their lives for achievements.

But the geniuses who survived rose to positions that Barbara couldn't reach.

,This bitch is the most outstanding of those bastards. From the first time I saw her this afternoon, she wasn't interested in the likes of me at all and only looked at her master... She's still not looking at me now.'

It was a good opportunity to land a blow on this woman. She somehow managed to grab the dropped spear shaft, but... she didn't have the strength to wield it. Barbara died like that.

Kwaaaaaaang!

A rock fell on the abandoned building. The old walls and ceiling were easily broken, and the floor couldn't withstand the weight of the rock and collapsed.

Gillian was sitting in the underground workshop with a nonchalant expression. Because he had a barrier around his body, even the thick dust couldn't touch him.

,Ah, shit. The workshop's a mess. It'll take half a day to clean up, goddamn it."

,There won't be anything to clean up. Because you're going to die."

Hoo-oong.

Mana drained away, deploying the spell he had prepared.

[Thunder Bolt]

The compressed lightning that fell from the sky with a thunderous roar easily tore through Gillian's barrier and delivered death to his body. The bastard's body was charred black and shattered.

,Is it over?'

It was too anticlimactic for a mage with a huge workshop, but he had been hit head-on by a 5th-grade magic, Thunder Bolt. He had also confirmed the corpse with his own two eyes.

,Now that I've killed him, this workshop is mine.'

He hesitated to go down. This was because the 300 beasts around him were still growling. When the mage controlling their minds dies, the beasts return to normal. They should either fight each other according to their instincts or scatter. But they were still glaring at me.

Step. Step.

A man walked out from among the beasts. It was Gillian.

,Haha. I like that surprised look on your face."

,...Was the guy who just died a doll?"

,Did he look like a doll to you?"

A clone? No. There's no reason to create a clone in a place like this. It was hard to call it a doll either. Gillian wasn't a puppeteer, but a summoner.

,A chimera, then. The inner form doesn't matter, so you can change the outer form as you wish."

,That's right. A 5th-grade mage should naturally notice that much. It's a carefully made sacrifice, but it was worth it to have taken one Thunder Bolt. How much mana do you have left?"

,I have more than enough to spare, even if you're not worried about me."

,Then I'll have to take more."

Click.

Gillian snapped his fingers. The 300 beasts rushed at me all at once. I deployed overlapping barriers. The beasts pounded on the barriers with their claws and teeth. Watching the barriers rapidly break down, I shaped a spell.

A magic circle was instantly drawn and activated under my feet, in the air.

[Electric Field]

The electric current flowed along the ground, electrifying the beasts. Unless they were undead, no living creature could escape electrocution. Normally, the beasts' brains would be fried and they would die instantly, but the beasts were only trembling and not collapsing or fainting.

,Did you gather and modify the beasts?"

,It's perfect for experimenting. Oh, by the way, they're all successful experiments. All the failures are dead."

Gillian, who was wrapped in a barrier, answered calmly. It was impossible to break that barrier with the output of the Electric Field.

I narrowed my eyes and analyzed Gillian's barrier. Unlike his rough words and actions, he was using overlapping barriers to increase his defense.

,He's being defensive. Is it because he's a summoner?'

It would be impossible to smash those overlapping barriers with ordinary attack magic. The easiest thing to do would be to cast Thunder Bolt, but considering the casting time and other factors, I wasn't confident that I could hit him. It wasn't like I had prepared it in advance like before.

,I have no choice. Should I take care of the annoying beasts first?'

Thud!

Suddenly, some of the beasts collapsed. Dozens of them collapsed at the same time, and their bodies disappeared somewhere.

Immediately after, I felt an enormous mana flow behind Gillian. I couldn't let this go.

[Electric Whip]

In a hurry, I pulled out a whip of electricity from my palm and swung it behind Gillian. It was to interfere with his magic. The larger and more elaborate the magic, the more it is affected by physical impact.

But the beasts jumped up and blocked the Electric Whip.

[Ice Spear], [Fireball]

The magic I fired non-stop was the same. The beasts jumped up and blocked it with their bodies.

,There are different types of magic. Simple but powerful elemental magic, dark and bizarre black magic, alchemy that manipulates matter to one's liking... So, do you know what the characteristic of summoning magic is?"

,Is it contracting with creatures from other dimensions? I heard you don't even need to study spells separately. They say it's a freebie magic."

Gillian chuckled at my words. My provocation didn't work.

,That's right. If you only consider the spells, it's a freebie. The summoning spells for phantasmal beasts are all the same. But why don't mages choose freebie summoning magic?"

,I heard that managing summoned beasts is difficult."

,That's right. Those phantasmal beast bastards all demand different things. The stronger they are, the more troublesome they are, so it's quite a headache. But if you ask if it's not worth it, that's not true either."

The summoning circle was completed. I tried to interfere by sending out mana waves along the way, but it didn't work.

What appeared from the summoning circle was a tree.

A maple tree dyed red. It was a maple tree of a size that made you admire it just by looking at it. The red maple leaves were subtly shining and illuminating the surroundings.

I was momentarily captivated by its beauty. I soon noticed the anomaly. The surrounding temperature was rising.

,The common perception is that summoners can't fight directly. I'll show you directly that that's just bullshit that people who don't know anything spout."

A fireball blazed on Gillian's outstretched palm.
