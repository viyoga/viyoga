Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang!!

The monster is pounding on the entrance of the barrier. There's not a hint of intelligence in that act. Only murderous intent and instinct. That's why I could be sure it wasn't human, but a monster.

,Sama Gaju-nim. Is the barrier safe?

"......."

No answer comes back.

Has she given up on life at the monster's appearance? No. She's squeezing her eyes shut and moving her lips, concentrating. An unknown incantation flows from her mouth. She must be maintaining the barrier so it doesn't break.

Ten minutes pass, then thirty minutes pass.

Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang!

The monster is still pounding on the barrier.

The barrier didn't budge because Sama Ryung was constantly chanting.

'That monster bastard is persistent, too. Let's see what that mug looks like.'

[Opening the Eye of Heaven.]

I see through the entrance blocked by dirt and rocks.

The monster's appearance came into view. It was bigger than a bear. It walks on two feet like a human and uses two arms. Its whole body was covered in fur, and green energy flowed from its eyes. Just by its appearance, it's closer to a grotesque person than a monster.

'...It's a monster alright. Looking inside, dark energy flows instead of blood.'

I was confused whether it was a living thing or not. I couldn't judge its identity with my knowledge, so I just decided to call it a monster.

Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang!!

I quietly glared at the entrance. If the monster broke through the barrier and came in, I had to use Complete Recovery. It'd be a bit much to use it openly, so I'd have to watch Sama Ryung's reaction a bit.

Kwang kwang kwang kwang kwang!!

'Doesn't this bastard ever get tired? Pounding and acting up for three hours straight.'

More than anything else, the noise was the most annoying. I could understand why people get so worked up about noise between floors, you know?

The darkness of the world recedes. The black sky turns blue and the sun rises. The monster, which had been pounding on the barrier all night, ran away somewhere as soon as the sun rose. The restriction that had been squeezing my body also disappeared.

Sama Ryung, who had been chanting continuously while the monster was pounding on the barrier, let out a small sigh. Her face was damp with sweat.

,Sama Gaju-nim. Are you alright?

,I'm fine.

Contrary to her words, the signs of fatigue were clear.

Even for a superhuman, staying up all night for several days is tough. Especially since she spent all night today chanting to protect the barrier. It would be strange if she didn't feel tired. Unlike me, she doesn't have Complete Recovery.

,Daehyeop (Great Hero). You're soaked in sweat. It'll hinder your recovery, so let me help you.

Instead of wiping her own sweat, Sama Ryung wiped the sweat off my body with a cloth. Her hand and body are closer than usual. She was definitely exhausted.

She diligently helped me even with my bowel movements before taking care of her own body. She changes her underwear and outer clothes. Unfortunately, I couldn't see her naked body because she floated a cloth in the air to cover herself. She squatted over the chamber pot to relieve herself, cleaned up, and came to sit next to me as if nothing had happened.

,Sama Gaju-nim. This cave has been discovered by that guy. Shouldn't we leave and find a new nest?

,That... I don't think it would matter.

,What do you mean it wouldn't matter?

,This barrier is hard to find unless we're sure we're here. And I've hidden the cave entrance perfectly. The fact that other people can't find it is proof of that. But that monster immediately pounded on the barrier. It's sure from the start that we're here.

I thought of the real-life killer and survivor game. In the later part of that game, the killer gets buffs. The most powerful buff there is the one that shows the survivor's location.

'...Yesterday was the fourth night. It wouldn't be strange if the monster got new abilities like we're under a restriction. Even if one of those abilities is the ability to find the location of humans.'

In the end, the monster's goal must be to kill all the humans in this space.

In other words, it might not matter even if we leave the cave and find a new hideout. It would just be a waste of energy.

We ate after that. Of course, she was the one moving my hands. After eating, we took a proper rest and had a conversation.

Cheonma Shingong, Namgoong Clan, Manmu Tower, and so on. They were stories I experienced in this world. Surprisingly, Sama Ryung seemed to enjoy listening to my stories. On the other hand, her stories were quite boring. Most of them were monotonous stories that happened in the imperial palace.

Thanks to that, I could be sure.

Sama Ryung was a flower in a greenhouse. She had never experienced a proper threat until now. I think I know why she hasn't been able to sleep a wink until now. She can't let go of her tension even if she wants to.

Kung kung kung!

Around lunchtime, someone hit the cave entrance. The slightly relaxed atmosphere tenses up again.

,I know you're in this barrier! Open the barrier! We must join hands to deal with the monsters of the night!

A man's voice rings out. The man continues to pound on the cave entrance.

'Is that bastard trying to come into my and Sama Ryung's nest? That son of a bitch isn't even worth tearing apart and killing!'

I watched Sama Ryung's reaction. The decision-making power isn't mine, it's hers. I couldn't feel anything in her eyes.

,Sama Gaju-nim. What are you going to do?

,He's not trustworthy. And I don't think he'll be of any help to us.

Kung kung kung!

The man didn't stop pounding on the entrance.

,I'm alone! I promise I won't cause you any trouble! Open the barrier! I'm ready to hear your opinion! Let's talk! If you can't trust me, I'll show my sincerity with the Heavenly Jade I have!

He's giving Heavenly Jade? That's a bit tempting.

Wouldn't it be okay if I just took the Heavenly Jade from the man and killed him?

But Sama Ryung didn't even twitch an eyebrow and shook her head.

,How ridiculous. Trying to buy our favor with mere wealth.

Sama Ryung ignored him. She didn't even give a common answer.

The man pleaded while pounding on the barrier. We just remained silent. His frustrated tone changed from pleading to anger.

,Don't just stay silent!!!

Kung kwang!

I could feel his will to smash the barrier. Sama Ryung quietly chanted to reinforce the barrier. I could feel a powerful energy. Strong energy strikes the barrier. The barrier shook slightly, but that was it. As long as Sama Ryung has her eyes open, the barrier won't be breached.

,You goddamn bastard! I'll see how long you can stay cooped up in here! I can already picture the monster dragging you out and tearing you limb from limb!

The man left, leaving a curse. He didn't reveal who he was until the end.

And the fifth night came.

As always, the restriction covered my body.

,Huu...!

This restriction directly pressured my dantian. My demonic energy is shaking. If I forcibly circulated my internal energy in my current state, I could die from internal injuries. It was best to do nothing and stay still.

Kung.

Something is pounding on the barrier.

Kung kung kung kung kung!

A rhythmic striking sound like a machine. It's the monster. The monster came back without getting tired of it.

Sama Ryung squeezed her eyes shut and began to chant. The time of endurance had begun.

Around midnight, Sama Ryung's chanting stopped for a moment. I could see her Adam's apple moving. It was too big of a movement to just be swallowing saliva. It looked like she was forcibly swallowing something. I knew she had swallowed blood.

Was blood about to come out of her mouth? There could be several situations. Her throat might be damaged from constantly chanting... but it was more likely to be an internal injury.

Her limit was approaching.

Kwajik.

,Ah.

,Daehyeop (Great Hero). That monster was more persistent than I thought. I do have a means I've been saving... but I can't guarantee it'll be properly used in my current state. But I'll still try. I can't afford to be picky....

Just as Sama Ryung was about to get up, staggering, before I could stop her, and was about to take something out of her spatial storage....

Jjaenggeurang!

The monster suddenly smashed the barrier and rushed over, striking Sama Ryung's body. Sama Ryung's body flew back like paper, hit the wall, and fell to the floor.

'Shit!'

[Using Complete Recovery.]

My body returns to normal. Strength fills my broken right arm, and my torn left arm is regenerated. The poison that had been eating away at my legs disappeared without a trace, and my energy and blood found their place.

Cheonma Shingong. Cheonma Gunrimbo (Demonic Heavenly Demonic Art - Heavenly Demon's Advent).

Cheonma Shingong. Yonggwon (Dragon Fist).

'If one hit isn't enough, I'll hit it several times.'

'...But this is an opportunity in its own way.'
