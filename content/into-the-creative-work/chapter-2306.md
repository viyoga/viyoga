Hiding it isn't a good choice. The only places you can hide it are the golem's torso and head. The arms and legs aren't suitable because they take a lot of impact. That leaves the torso and head.

,The torso is much thicker than the head. It can protect the core relatively safely.'

Brain-Sky Style (Lei Tian Style) Myriad Lightning (Manji Lightning).

,I haven't used my body properly lately... I've got quite the touch.'

"These golems. If you think about it, they're the Empire's property, right? Elena. Is it okay to smash these?"

"It doesn't matter. The Guardian Stone's effects have changed a bit in Atlantis too. It automatically produces golems every certain amount of time. I think it makes up to about 120."

Pajijik.
