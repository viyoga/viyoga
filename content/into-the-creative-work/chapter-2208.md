Elsa met with Lydia and had a light conversation.

Elsa didn't feel anything special from Lydia. Lydia was indeed a talent hard to let go of. But there were plenty of talents like Lydia around her.

,She doesn't seem too difficult to handle though.'

Since she's a talent too good to give to others, she'll try to scout her if she gets the chance.

Next was a meeting with Yuria Grace, a talent the royal family was paying attention to.

Elsa paused when she saw Yuria entering the room. Agnes was right. Yuria's first impression was perfect. For some reason, the word "perfect maid" kept popping up in her head.

Elsa, confident in her ability to judge people, coveted Yuria. She had a feeling that if Yuria assisted her, everything would work out.

Elsa exchanged brief greetings with Yuria. Perhaps it was because she liked her first impression. She liked Yuria's short greeting too.

Elsa praised Yuria first. No one dislikes being praised as a person. To soften a stiff atmosphere, you just need to say what the other person likes to hear.

,Yuria-yang. Your grades in the Maid Academy are excellent. Even though you're taking more subjects than others, your grades are top-class. According to the recommendation letter from Dean Maria Hilton, you're the greatest genius in the history of the Maid Academy."

,That's an overstatement. There's still a lot of time left before I graduate from the Maid Academy, so it's too early to say I'm the greatest of all time."

,Too early, huh? So you admit that you're the greatest genius of all time?"

,Since Your Highness said so. I'm aiming for Prime-level. Unless I become the greatest of all time, I won't be able to reach Prime-level upon graduating from the academy."

The grades for maid certifications are Senior, Double, Triple, Flower, Superior, Royal, and Prime. Prime is the highest grade, and no maid in Neo London's history has ever reached Prime-level upon graduation.

Of course, there have been maids who reached Prime-level after graduating from the academy by taking promotion exams, but Yuria's goal was to reach Prime-level in one go.

,Yuria-yang is trying to write a new history. It's not an easy goal. That's what makes it even more meaningful. I'll cheer you on, Yuria-yang."

,Thank you, Your Highness. Your words alone give me great strength."

,Are you aware that the royal family covets you?"

,...Yes. I am aware."

,I want you too. Can't you work for me as soon as you graduate from the academy? I'll give you treatment comparable to the royal family. Also, on the day I inherit the throne... I promise you'll go to Buckingham with me too."

Here, Buckingham refers to Buckingham Palace. The place where the King of Neo London resides. Buckingham Palace is the royal family.

,I appreciate the offer, but I already have a master I must serve."

,...Are you talking about your patron, Baron Meyer?"

,Yes. He is my master."

,Even if Baron Meyer has a superconductor... I don't understand why Yuria-yang follows the Baron. You haven't known the Baron for very long. Is it because the Baron sponsored Yuria-yang? I can give you several times the Baron's sponsorship."

,Your Highness. I don't serve my master because of sponsorship. I will serve my master even without sponsorship. Because he is my master."

,...What is it then? Can't you tell me clearly?"

Yuria smiled faintly. Memories with Eugene flashed through her mind. Precious memories that she couldn't talk about in this world.

,I can't tell you."

,...I've only confirmed Yuria-yang's firm heart. Yuria-yang, this question doesn't have much meaning, so don't take it to heart. You can let it pass, or you don't have to answer. It's just a personal curiosity."

,Yes. Ask away."

,Can Yuria-yang do anything for Baron Meyer?"

,I can do anything."

,...Even if the Baron orders Yuria-yang to die?"

,I will gladly offer my death."

,...Yes. Good. It was nice meeting you. You can leave now."

,It was an honor, Your Highness."

Yuria left the room. Elsa sighed deeply. Yuria didn't waver at all, even during the conversation. That loyalty is real.

,Agnes."

,Yes, Your Highness."

,What should I do to scout Yuria-yang?"

,...You seem to like her."

,I'm confident in my ability to judge people. No, even if that wasn't the case, who wouldn't covet Yuria-yang after seeing her? I wish her loyalty was directed at me. Is there no way?"

,There isn't. I've seen many people of this type. Even if they die, even if the master of their loyalty disappears, their loyalty will not disappear."

,...I don't think the royal family will give up on Yuria. His Majesty is greedy for talent. When he was younger, he even feuded with the parliament to steal talent."

,It's not something related to us. If His Majesty has made up his mind, no one will be able to stop him."

,...Still, I don't want to give up like this. Would it be better to scout the Baron to my side instead?"

,Becoming the master of the master... That's a relatively wise decision. But the Baron is not an easy person either."

,Aside from the superconductor, he's also a Rank 6 high-ranking mage affiliated with the Magic Association. And he's said to be ruthless?"

,Yes. He personally found the mercenaries and entrepreneurs who were after the superconductor's secrets and killed their entire families. It happened in the shadows."

,That's no joke. There are many crazy people among high-ranking mages... Isn't he top-tier among them?"

,Well. I think he's average."

,Killing the enemy's family is average?"

,At least he didn't use them as test subjects. Killing a family that might become an enemy is not that surprising."

,I don't understand the world of mages. ...Does Yuria-yang know the Baron's true colors? Wouldn't she lose affection if I told her?"

,......"

Agnes looked at Elsa with a pathetic gaze. But the disrespectful look immediately disappeared.

,Your Highness."

,Yeah. Yeah. I know. I know I'm not a clean person either. That Yuria-yang's loyalty won't be shaken by mere morality. I'm just saying it out of frustration. Out of frustration."

Elsa gulped down the cold tea and asked another question.

,What's Linda's evaluation of Yuria-yang?"

,Perfect score. She says there's nothing to criticize about her work."

,Really? Makes me want her even more."

The next day, in the evening.

Countless guests flocked to the banquet hall of the Ben Nevis Dukedom.

Yuria stood behind Princess Elsa with Linda, the Dormitory Manager. Behind Princess Elsa, maids were standing in attendance. Roaming around the banquet hall and serving the guests was the job of maids with lower positions.

Elsa came to the banquet hall early to greet the guests. Among the guests who came today, there were quite a few big shots that even she, as a princess, couldn't ignore.

The guests first greeted the host, Elsa, and then scattered throughout the banquet hall, gathering in groups of twos and threes to enjoy the banquet.

At the same time, they were also looking around. As if looking for someone. No one came to this place simply to enjoy the banquet. Many came with the purpose of exchanging even a single word with big shots they couldn't easily meet in normal times and making an impression. Even those people were quite successful.

The appearance of the owner of the superconductor LK-99, which surprised the whole world. People's eyes were directed at a man walking confidently through the entrance.

Baron.

Looking only at his position, he was the lowest among the people gathered here. But no one thought that the Baron's position would last forever. He would soon receive a formal title. And that would probably be at least a Viscount or higher.

,Wasn't the production volume of the superconductor 5 tons a day?'

,If we can only get a large amount of superconductor... we can implement a magnetic levitation train.'

,A contract. I have to get a superconductor contract.'

Everyone's eyes shone. But they couldn't move rashly yet. Eugene slowly walked towards Princess Elsa.

Yuria had been blankly watching him since Eugene appeared. Was it because it had been too long? Her eyes could only see Eugene.

She wanted to run and hug him. If she opened her arms, he would smile and hug her. But she couldn't do that because of the place.

Eugene's eyes met hers. Yuria felt a jolt of electricity running through her entire body.

The two did not avoid each other's gaze. But they couldn't keep being conscious of each other. Eugene arrived in front of Princess Elsa and knelt on one knee.

,Thank you for inviting me to today's banquet, Your Highness Princess Elsa."

,It's a pleasure to meet a mage who has enhanced the reputation of Neo London like this, Baron Meyer. Or should I call you Count Meyer now?"

,...Count, you say?"

,Ah! I made a mistake... It's not something that has been announced yet. The royal family has decided to bestow the title of Count on Baron Meyer. An official letter will be sent to the Baron soon. Don't be surprised."

Eugene gave a bitter smile.

,Your Highness has given me good information. I can't just let it pass. I will present a gift to Your Highness. It's something that has been verified by the Head maid, so don't be surprised."
