The people who came to greet Yuria and Lydia were a middle-aged Head maid and three young maids following her.

The middle-aged woman walking ahead was elegant and strong at the same time. Her unwavering gait gave the impression of steel moving directly. An extraordinary dignity was felt from the three maids following behind her. Perhaps it was because the maid uniforms they were wearing were of the highest quality.

,...Eup.

Lydia shrank back, and Yuria quietly assessed them.

'The person at the very front reminds me of the Dean-nim. At least a Superior-class maid. The three following behind must be Flower-class.'

The gate opened on its own, and the middle-aged woman wearing glasses said.

,What brings you to the Bennevis Dukedom?

There was no way the Maid Academy wouldn't have asked the Bennevis Dukedom for cooperation. She's asking even though she knows everything. In the first place, it's strange for a Housekeeper-class maid to come out to greet guests.

Is this a show of power? No, it's more like a test.

Yuria slightly lifted the hem of her skirt and bowed.

,I am Yuria Grace from the Maid Academy. I have come to the Bennevis Dukedom for a field training as part of the Academy's curriculum. Here is a letter and recommendation from Maria Hilton-nim, the Dean of the Maid Academy.

,L-Lydia Metcalf, here. I also came from the Maid Academy. I also have a letter and recommendation from Maria Hilton-nim.

,...Confirmed. I am Agnes Nebal, the Head maid of the Bennevis Dukedom. The two of you can only work in the Bennevis Dukedom if you pass the interview. Follow me.

Yuria and Lydia followed Agnes. They paid attention to their steps as they were taught at the Maid Academy. They walked perfectly, as if they wouldn't spill a single drop of water even if they put a cup of water on their heads.

The interview took place in the reception room inside the mansion. Yuria and Lydia sat opposite Agnes and the three maids. In an atmosphere of pressure, Agnes opened her mouth first.

,I will deliver the letter to Her Highness the Princess. First, please introduce yourselves.

Questions poured out from Agnes.

,What values do you have as a maid?

,There are many tasks for a maid. Which task are you most confident in?

,What can you do for Her Highness the Princess?

It wasn't much different from a large corporation's interview questions.

Yuria answered appropriately. Answers that ordinary people would give. Lydia answered with a confident tone, true to her personality.

,I have listened well to your answers. I will be on my way now. The managers here will continue the interview.

Agnes left. The silence continued. Soon, Agnes' presence completely disappeared.

,Haa.

One of the three, a maid with red short hair and scars all over her face and body, relaxed her posture. She released the strength from her upright back and leaned back as if melting into the backrest.

,Agnes Head maid is good in every way, but she's too stiff. Ah, newbies. I'm Rita, the Head of Security. Ah, I'm not officially hired, so I'm not a newbie. Should I call you trainees?

The woman with cold eyes and short black hair clicked her tongue briefly as if she didn't like Rita's attitude.

,Ignore her. Her Highness Princess Elsa is generous, so she forgives some relaxation. I warn you, do not act arbitrarily in reliance on Her Highness the Princess's generosity. In particular, ignore Security Head Rita's poor attitude. I am Cynthia, the Chief Secretary.

,Hello. I'm Linda from the Management Office.

The woman sitting on the far left greeted. She had long wavy light brown hair and squinted eyes. Usually, squinting eyes give a suspicious impression, but she had a gentle atmosphere. Her figure was the best among the three. Especially her chest was full.

,In fact, this interview doesn't mean much. Just being a student at the Maid Academy means you can get a job at a decent noble family with a free pass. Ah, in your case, it's a training, so the Academy has already finished talking about it.

,Linda. You're talking too much to the trainees.

,They probably already know. What.

,That's right. You probably already know. If it wasn't for the Head maid, this formal interview wouldn't have happened.

,...You two seem to be relaxed. I have a lot of work to do afterwards. Let's get to the point. The two trainees will work as maids in the Bennevis Dukedom for the next week. The two of you will take turns working with us in the Secretariat, Management Office, and Security Office. We will check your work performance and submit a report and evaluation to the Maid Academy.

It meant that the managers were also supervisors.

,Our Secretariat assists Her Highness the Princess and carries out Her Highness the Princess's orders. Her Highness the Princess operates several corporations, and we are in charge of managing them. We also handle confidential data. Well, that's the same for other departments, but the Secretariat is especially severe. So, the two of you will have to sign a contract.

Cynthia handed over a non-disclosure agreement.

Yuria quickly read through the contract. There were no trap clauses. It was close to a standard contract.

,Wait a minute. Clause 7 here is a bit strange?

Lydia raised her eyes and protested. Yuria quickly scanned Clause 7. It was about the confidentiality period. There was nothing strange about it.

,Ah, I see. Here's a revised contract. How about you, Yuria-ssi?

,It's okay.

They had prepared a pre-amended contract? It meant that they were deliberately messing with the contract to see the reaction. Yuria and Lydia's contracts were different from each other from the beginning. Lydia's eyes fluttered in disbelief, but Cynthia nonchalantly took the signed contract.

,The Security Office's job is to protect Her Highness the Princess and the security of the Bennevis Dukedom, as the name suggests. Sometimes special missions are assigned.

,...Rita.

Cynthia glared at Rita.

,What's wrong with that? You'll find out soon enough anyway. You signed the non-disclosure agreement, too. Anyway, in the case of security, it's usually quiet, so don't be nervous. It's fucking dangerous when it's dangerous, but it's usually a honey pot compared to other departments.

Yuria and Lydia closed their mouths. They didn't particularly want to go to the Security Office.

Then Linda spoke in a soft tone.

,Ah, the Management Office is less specialized than the other two departments. The main tasks are mansion management and guest reception. There are many tasks, from the mansion's chores to the Security Office's supplies and the Secretariat's support. Especially when Her Highness the Princess holds a banquet, it gets very busy. Fortunately, Her Highness the Princess doesn't like banquets... Oh dear. There's a banquet tomorrow evening?

Linda smiled brightly. On the other hand, the other two's complexions deteriorated rapidly. It was a banquet for the heir to the throne. Royalty, as well as high-ranking nobles and other powerful people from Neo London, would flock there. The suffering of the lower ones was as good as confirmed.

Lydia's face turned pale, and Yuria was the same as usual.

,You don't have to be too anxious. The banquet preparations are already going smoothly.

It was obvious that they wouldn't be preparing a banquet hosted by the Princess at the last minute.

Cynthia cleared her throat and drew attention.

,Ahem, first, I'll let you know your schedule. Until noon today, tidy up your luggage in your accommodation, and after lunch, Lydia-yang will work with Security Head Rita. Yuria-yang will work with Management Office Head Linda. The work will not change until the end of the banquet. Management Office Head Linda will guide you to your accommodation.

,Okay~ Follow me.

The accommodation was clean and spacious like a hotel. The furniture was also luxurious.

,Please change into the maid uniform placed on the table. Since you've come to the Bennevis Dukedom, you have to wear the Bennevis uniform. Ah, I'll give you 10 minutes to tidy up your luggage. We don't have much time because we're preparing for the banquet.

The dining hall for the servants was also excellent. You could take as much of the food you wanted in a buffet style. Yuria nodded as she ate.

'The quality of the food is high. You can tell that they carefully select the ingredients.'

She felt a slight deficiency, but she ignored it. She wasn't going to work in the Dukedom forever, and if a trainee who suddenly came along pointed out the food, it would only hurt the chefs' pride. She didn't forget that she was an outsider.

'Of course, it would have been a different story if it was food that would go up to Master-nim.'

She would never allow food of this level to be served to Master-nim. For Master-nim, she could turn the Dukedom upside down as much as she wanted.

After the meal, Lydia headed to the Security Office, and Yuria followed Linda.

,First, I'll guide you through the mansion.

,Are there any places I can't enter?

,Hmm. Don't go above the 3rd floor unless there's something special. Her Highness the Princess stays on the 4th floor. The Security Office maids are guarding it, so you won't be able to go up even if you want to.

Then they moved to the annex where the banquet was scheduled to be held. The annex was decorated for the banquet, and the maids were cleaning. Linda looked around the banquet hall and checked it over and over again.

,Yuria-ssi, you've been quiet since earlier. I heard in advance that you're the top student at the Academy... You're surprisingly passive.

,All the preparations are already finished. It doesn't seem like there's anything I can interfere with.

,We decorated the banquet hall with beige flowers as the theme... Is there anything lacking in Yuria-ssi's opinion?

,It feels bright and elegant. There's nothing I can point out. I'm not qualified to point it out either.

,Hmm. I can't evaluate you if you're like this... I was originally going to make a deliberate mistake. But I can't deliberately ruin a banquet hosted by Her Highness the Princess. Even if it's during the preparation period.

,Of course.

,But I have to evaluate you. I heard that Yuria-ssi is also excellent at cooking. Can you prepare food for the guests and Her Highness the Princess who will be attending the banquet? I have to serve Her Highness the Princess with you during the banquet, so light food is fine.

,Can you tell me the list of dishes that will be served on the day of the banquet?

,Of course.

Yuria checked the paper that Linda handed her. Most of the dishes were European style. The meal side was close to perfect, so it was ambiguous to touch it. Requesting a menu change now would only be a nuisance.

,I'll add dessert. Can I order additional fruit?

,If it's fruit, same-day delivery is possible. Even if it's not, there's enough fruit prepared. Her Highness the Princess likes fruit.

Yuria was also aware of that, so she was trying to prepare a fruit dessert. You can find out that Princess Elsa likes fruit with just a little investigation.

,What kind of dessert are you going to prepare?

What Yuria was thinking of was a dessert that was recently popular among maids in the [Baekhwan] world.

,It's Tanghulu from the ancient country of True China. Have you heard of it?

,No. What is that?

,It's a dessert made by coating fruit with sugar. It's really delicious.

Sugar and fruit. It would be stranger if it wasn't delicious.

,...It sounds like food that's not good for your body.

,It's still delicious.

Yuria didn't care whether the Princess and nobles fell for Tanghulu and got diabetes.
