That inside is a different district. It's an area thickly covered with the power and presence of an unknown divine seat. Once you go in, you won't be able to get out easily.

She doesn't do stupid things like going inside recklessly. She used magic to look inside the district.

「You have been touched by the gaze of death.」

「You are outside the district.」

「The effect does not apply.」

"......Indeed."

One god came to her mind. She had already studied the divine seats through the exiles. Gods and monsters in mythology. And even things about heroes and great men of a lower rank than that.

,If it's a god related to eyes, there are several ways to attack. Reflect the gaze with a mirror, or deal with it without being noticed.'

It's not certain.

The former is too simple and is unlikely to work, and the latter is difficult from the start to not be noticed. Especially around that black pillar, it's even more so because it's flat with black ground with nothing on it.

,I can do it. I can deceive my existence with illusion.'

Our purpose.

Killing God.
