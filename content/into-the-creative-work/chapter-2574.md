Two years had passed.

The Empire's millennium anniversary banquet was just around the corner. I decided to attend with the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King.

To be precise, the Sword King butted in. Unlike the Martial Arts Alliance Lord, the Sword King wasn't easy to deal with. Moreover, he was an elder from my wife's family. I couldn't just ignore him.

,Didn't Supreme Clan Leader-nim get an invitation to the banquet either?

,Not only me, but the entire Namgung Clan wasn't invited. Probably, among the martial arts forces, you're the only one who received the Emperor's invitation. So, you can be proud of that. The Emperor basically has no interest in the martial world itself.

,To be exact, it's not me who was invited, but my teacher-nim.

,Indeed. The Fallen Moon Goddess is that great.

,Do you know much about my teacher-nim?

I asked the Sword King, my eyes shining. Wiyu didn't talk much about herself. Maybe I could get some information about Wiyu through the Sword King.

,The Namgung Clan has maintained a good relationship with the Fallen Moon Goddess-nim since long ago. I know she's great, but... I don't know much else.

The Sword King's words were somewhat disappointing.

,Hehe. You guys. I'll tell you in advance... be careful at this Imperial banquet. It's not the Emperor that's the problem. Everyone attending the banquet is bound to be someone great. If you act carelessly, you'll get into serious trouble.

,Shut up, Baek Sung. Do you think I don't know that much?

,I'll take care of it.

The Sword King and I replied.

The Martial Arts Alliance Lord's forehead was bulging as he chuckled hollowly. It was nothing special.

• • •

The Empire's capital was truly enormous.

The buildings were magnificent, and the people were swarming. There were so many people that I felt the urge to detonate a nuclear bomb.

And the Imperial Palace.

It was bigger and more splendid than any palace I had ever seen. The towering pavilions seemed to pierce the sky, and the soldiers guarding the palace were all elites. The entrance to the Imperial Palace was at least three times larger than Sungnyemun Gate.

,Hmm.

We let out a groan as we looked at the Imperial Palace. It wasn't that we were overwhelmed by the palace's splendor. Buildings are just buildings. What we were overwhelmed by was the spiritual energy vein beneath the Imperial Palace.

Dozens of spiritual energy veins were gathering around the Imperial Palace. It was no exaggeration to say that it was the center of this continent.

,I knew it from hearsay... but seeing it in person, the spiritual energy is tremendous. If I trained in a place like this, I wouldn't need any elixirs.

,If I had secluded myself for training in a place like this, I would have reached the realm of Myriad Images.

,Ahem. Training and ascension are different things.

I left the bickering pair behind and headed towards the Imperial Palace. As expected, a soldier blocked our path. I didn't want to get into an argument, so I took out the imperial edict from my inventory. An invitation stamped with the Emperor's royal seal.

The soldiers tensed up and straightened their posture.

,You are honored guests who have received the Emperor's invitation. We were waiting to escort you. We will guide you.

We entered the Imperial Palace under the guidance of the soldiers.

,The interior of the Imperial Palace... it's not just big and fancy. Formations are laid everywhere. If you move carelessly, you'll easily get lost.

We were guided to a palace to stay in during the banquet. It was a small palace, but it was still a palace. What was even more absurd was that there were over 100 of these palaces.

,This banquet will last for ten days, starting three days from now. If you need anything, just tell the palace staff.

,When will the Emperor attend the banquet? Surely he won't be attending for all ten days.

,How could we dare to know the Emperor's schedule? However, since you have the Emperor's edict, you will be able to have an audience with the Emperor. If you wish, I will summon a eunuch now.

,Even if we can't have an audience with the Emperor right away, we'll need to coordinate a schedule. Bring a eunuch.

The soldier quietly retreated. And 30 minutes later, a eunuch entered. He was a man with a smooth face, appearing to be in his early to mid-20s.

I am Ahn Juk (安竹), a eunuch serving in the Office of Royal Decrees. The Emperor has granted you an audience. Tomorrow at Misi (未時), eunuchs, including myself, will escort you to the audience hall.

The eunuch spoke cautiously. I liked that he was quick with his work.

,Understood. You may leave now.

,...There are rules of the Imperial Palace. It seems you are not familiar with the court etiquette, so I will inform you. This is necessary to have an audience with the Emperor.

I couldn't just cut him off and refuse. This was the Imperial Palace. Even a small mistake could cost me my life.

We had to learn the court etiquette until late at night.

• • •

The Emperor, sitting on the jade throne, wore a dragon robe and a myeonryugwan (a type of crown). Having lived for over a thousand years, the Emperor still looked young. No matter how you looked at him, he appeared to be only in his early to mid-30s. He gazed down at the people below the throne with a detached expression.

,We greet Your Imperial Majesty!

The Martial Arts Alliance Lord, the Sword King, and I prostrated ourselves before the Emperor. The Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King trembled slightly. Sweat trickled down their cheeks, showing how nervous they were.

I quietly rolled my eyes, feeling bewildered.

,The Emperor's aura is impressive, but not to this extent, right?

Was it because of the dozens of soldiers guarding the Emperor? No, they wouldn't be out of place as generals. Each of them was fully armored, and their cultivation was at least at the Three Flowers Gathering at the Crown. On top of that, there were four Incarnations who had reached the Harmony Realm.

,It's definitely a great pressure.

But it wasn't enough to make me tremble. Especially since the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King were martial artists who were dignified even in the face of death. I couldn't understand why they were so completely intimidated by the Emperor.

,The aura I feel from the Emperor right now is similar to Wiyu's. Is it because I'm used to it?

If not, there was something I didn't know.

,The Martial Arts Alliance Lord wanted to have a private audience with the Emperor. At this rate, it seems like he won't even be able to speak properly.

It seemed like just breathing and holding on was all they could do.

I wondered if the Martial Arts Alliance Lord would suffer a qi deviation like the Heavenly Demon did when he saw the Emperor.

,The stars are protecting you. Indeed. So you are her disciple....

Emperor Jinhyun said as if muttering to himself. Even that alone had a resonance that shook my heart.

,Is this Hyegwangsim-eo (Wisdom Light Heart Language)?

What was certain was that there was something about the Emperor.

,I feel like I've seen the future of this world. I feel good. Your name is Seong Yujin, you said. Is there anything you desire from me? Speak. I will grant it.

Suddenly, the Emperor showed favor.

The Emperor had the power and authority to grant anything. But if I misspoke here, I would incur the Emperor's wrath.

,Then things will get fucking annoying.

I had to make a wish that was trivial but not trivial.

,I dare to ask Your Imperial Majesty. Please eliminate all the beggars in the Empire!

,An unexpected request. Hmm. Beggars. They are certainly useless people. Very well. Eliminate the beggars from my country. This is an imperial decree.

,Yes, Your Majesty!

At the Emperor's command, the officials bowed their heads and paid their respects. There was no objection. From this moment on, beggars would disappear from the Empire. The Beggars' Sect, a sect of beggars? Of course, they would be annihilated.

I was inwardly impressed by the Emperor's absolute power.

,We have prepared quite a lot for this banquet. Enjoy it until the last day of the banquet.

He flicked his hand. It meant to leave. We bowed to the Emperor and quietly retreated.

,I don't know how strong the Emperor is. The difference is too great to even estimate. Considering that he's at least as skilled as Wiyu... there's no way to win right now.

I cleanly gave up on the scenario of killing the Emperor and becoming the new Emperor. Of course, I knew it wouldn't work from the start.

,Huff!

,Keuheuk. What is this...!

As soon as we left the audience hall, the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King staggered, gasping for breath. Their entire bodies were soaked in sweat.

,Unbelievable...! Is that what an Emperor is...!

,A living god is in that place! Even if I lived dozens more lives, I wouldn't be able to reach the Emperor's feet....

It seemed like the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King's mental states were shattered. I was bewildered. It was hard to understand why these old men were suddenly acting like this.

The eunuch next to us had a familiar look on his face.

,It is natural since you have seen the Emperor for the first time. Quietly calm your minds, and deeply admire the Emperor's greatness in your hearts. Also, be proud to be the Emperor's people. With the Emperor guarding the throne, we will enjoy a long glory under his reign.

The eunuch's praise of the Emperor continued until the end of the escort.

As soon as they arrived at the palace, the Martial Arts Alliance Lord and the Sword King sat cross-legged on the floor and began circulating their qi. It seemed like a struggle to shake off the inner demons in their hearts.

I sat in front of them and took out a can of cola to drink. What did those guys see in the Emperor? I couldn't help but be curious.

It wasn't until midnight that they were able to shake off their inner demons.

I first asked the Martial Arts Alliance Lord, who seemed to have aged 30 years more.

,Alliance Lord. What did you see in the Emperor?

,...I didn't see the Emperor. The Emperor saw me. It felt like everything in my heart was being dug up. And I also saw the Emperor. ...The Emperor is not human. How could one possess such a mind unless they were a god?

,So what did you see?

,I don't know. It was just distant. So distant that it felt like my world was forcibly collapsing and expanding. In front of him, I was nothing more than a bug.

The Alliance Lord's words were hard to understand. I frowned and looked at the Sword King.

,Supreme Clan Leader-nim, what did you see in the Emperor?

,I saw a sword. A single sword. His sword cut me, cut the ground, and even cut the sky. In front of his sword, my sword was nothing more than a twig. If he and I were to exchange blows... my sword wouldn't last even one exchange. I've held a sword for hundreds of years, but in front of the Emperor, those years scatter like clouds.

The Sword King, known for his swordsmanship, muttered with a look of disillusionment. Ttorureuk. He even shed tears. I felt embarrassed watching.

,If you gaze into the abyss, the abyss also gazes into you.

The Alliance Lord, who regained his senses faster than the Sword King, asked me.

,What did you see in the Emperor?

,I saw the Emperor.

,...What did you feel from the Emperor?

,I can't beat him right now.

,......What do you think the Emperor is?

,The Emperor is just the Emperor.

,.......

The Alliance Lord looked at me with complicated eyes.
