[Thunder Break]

A lightning pillar falls from the sky onto the witch. Perhaps because this is the Great Witch's space, its power was a little weaker than usual, but the surprise attack was perfect.

A 7th-circle lightning-type attack magic cast with the power of Meltes' ring before the opponent could prepare for battle.

Even if it's the same 7th circle, in this situation, it should be at least a critical hit. But the witch, revealed as the pillar of lightning disappeared leaving behind the smell of ozone, was unscathed. At best, only her clothes were scorched.

However, the color of her hair had changed. From brown to dark black. And she was wearing a white mask with a mocking expression on her face.

Putting aside the strange atmosphere emanating from her, just considering her presence, she was an 8th circle, above 7th circle.

,Hahaha. This is quite something. It stings a bit. I wonder how many hundreds of years it's been since I was struck by lightning. Young wizard, you shouldn't use things like this on people, you know."

The atmosphere had changed from before. Her voice contained an undeniable playfulness.

,Is it the influence of that mask?'

Mephistopheles. Sadly, the only information I have is that she's the witch's ancestor and the source of the witch's quirk. That's all. It can't be helped. It's not like I know everything in this world.

I don't know exactly what kind of power Mephistopheles has. It's certain that she uses the power of curses... but most demons handle the power of curses, so it couldn't be said to be special.

Mephistopheles slowly looked around. The witches trembled every time their eyes met hers. Some witches collapsed on the spot, and some witches revealed hatred and murderous intent to the point where blood vessels burst in their eyes.

,Cursed children! Cursed ones who inherited my blood! I, Mephistopheles, love you! Always! I'm always loving you! Hahahahaha!"

,Hiiiik!"

,G-Great Witch-nim! Please, let me kill that damn thing! I'll kill it even if I have to give my life!!"

The Great Witch was calm, but she didn't hide her contempt for Mephistopheles.

,Witches. Don't fall for that thing's provocation. Don't react. Watch quietly. And engrave it in your minds. Our father, our enemy. Wizard, are you just going to watch?"

,Don't rush me. The preparation for the spell is complete."

I nodded my chin towards Mephistopheles' incarnation.

[Hell Fire]

A blue fireball is fired at the demon.

The demon laughed and waved his hand in the air. A blue fireball appears and collides with the Hellfire I fired. Kwang! With an explosion, the Hellfire is nullified and disappears.

,...You cast the same magic I used. Is that your ability?"

,Haha. Hellfire isn't your magic alone, is it? Rather, isn't it one of the easiest to obtain among magic spells?"

,Don't be ridiculous. Even if it's the same magic, the power is bound to be different if the wizard is different. The wizard's individual attribute affinity, mana affinity, understanding of the spell, the qualitative burden of the mana road, the influence of the astral, etc. Above all, the characteristic of fire magic is that if the same fire-type magic collides, it's generally absorbed by the stronger one, not nullified. Hellfire, which isn't ordinary magic, was cleanly nullified and disappeared by the same Hellfire? It's impossible unless everything is the same in all fields, including the mana and spell used in the magic."

,Hahaha. Talented wizard, wizard loved by demons and angels. Let me make you a proposal."

I frowned. The words "loved by demons and angels" bothered me. He smiled and continued.

,I'll undo your curse. Do you think I can't do what the Great Witch can do? Rather, I, a demon, will do it better."

Mephistopheles' main body is a demon who has achieved transcendence comparable to the 72 demons. If such an existence steps forward directly, solving the curse of the Fallen is a piece of cake.

,I refuse. I've already made a contract with the Great Witch."

,A verbal contract? Hmm. I acknowledge it. But that simple contract has no binding force whatsoever."

,Even if that weren't the case, I wouldn't want to join hands with a twisted demon of unknown origin like you."

[Thunder Break]

I drop a pillar of lightning once again. At the same time, I used an instant.

[Using Instant (Instant). Remaining stacks: 11]

I needed to figure out how that bastard was responding to Thunder Break.

I saw the mask momentarily change into a woman's face, and the bastard's shadow turn into a mirror, pouring out Thunder Break.

That brief moment that I couldn't have captured without Instant. Thanks to that, I understood Mephistopheles' ability.

The pillar of lightning was nullified and disappeared, and his shadow and mask quickly returned to normal.

,A mirror and a mask. Is that your unique skill?"

,Oh my. You've already figured it out? What great insight."

That bastard's mana fluctuated. Spells gathered around him, and five blue fireballs appeared. He copied the Hellfire I used.

,That damn bastard. Did you save and recall my magic? A click-clack magic. It's been a while since I've felt something so unpleasant."

I clenched and unclenched my fist. Despite the rising unpleasantness and anger, my head calmly deals with the situation.

,Hellfire is the fire of hell that doesn't go out even if you pour water on it. Blocking it is the worst, and even if you dodge it, the space is limited. The best thing to do is to intercept it before it flies this way.'

I opened the inventory ejection port.

I fire the meteor swords I had prepared in advance. The swords, created with supernatural power and imbued with magic, were fired like cannonballs.

The meteor swords collide with the Hellfire and cause an explosion.

Hwareureureureureuk!

In the blue flames that scattered and burned around, the demon slowly shook his upper body. Magic, swords, guns, etc. appeared around him, preparing to attack. Among them was the meteor sword I had just fired.

,Is it possible to copy objects as well? If it's possible, how far is it possible? Also, what are the conditions for copying? One of the conditions is to see the ability directly with both eyes, right?'

He hadn't copied magic until I used magic first. Well, that's common sense, but... blindly believing in common sense in this world is a stupid thing to do.

,First, I have to withstand that attack. Leap is convenient.'

[This is a space where you cannot leap.]

,...Did that bastard do something? Or is it because it's the Great Witch's space?'

It's clear that not only Leap, but the entire space-type movement skill is blocked. I should be grateful that I can still use subspace or inventory.

[Barrier]

I overworked my mana road and ran while overlapping barrier magic. If I stay still, I'll just become a target. If I can avoid one or two attacks by moving my legs, I should avoid them.

,I'll intercept anything that looks like it'll hit me head-on with a meteor sword.'

The inventory ejection port opens around me, firing meteor swords and closing. The meteor swords intercept the attacks pouring towards me and cause explosions. The aftermath is handled by the barrier that surrounds my body.

Only after repeating this for about 30 seconds did my legs stop. A tattered barrier, rising breath, and a mana road that was slowly starting to overload.

,If I use Phase Shift, the overload on the mana road will be resolved. The problem is... will my attack work on that bastard?'

Even if I use a powerful attack magic, he'll nullify it with the exact same magic.

,If I use a different attack magic, I'll just be revealing my hand. The biggest problem is his unique skill. What if that bastard copies my unique skill?'

Honestly, I don't think he can copy even unique skills.

But I can't ignore the possibility, however small. If that bastard copies my unique skill... not only will I feel very, very dirty, but the chances of winning will disappear completely.

,Wizard. Is that all you have to show? A 7th-circle wizard who is talented but skilled in combat. If this is the case, even the Great Witch sitting over there will be disappointed in you. Take out your essence, your unique skill! Prove that you have the qualifications to kill me, Mephistopheles, as you said!"

,Shut up. I know you want to see my unique skill."

The air splits and the inventory ejection port opens. What appears is not the meteor sword that has been fired so far.

[Renting Vajra Mace for 1 hour with 3,000 inventory points.]

I use 130 million credits to fire the golden Vajra. The Vajra became golden lightning and aimed for the demon's head.

The Vajra is formed in front of the demon, but it breaks. The flustered Mephistopheles hurriedly waved his hand. The Vajra Mace stops in front of the demon.

,Time Lock magic. It's different from what I used. Was it already saved?'

The demon moved to the side. The effect of Time Lock ends, and the Vajra Mace flies and hits the ground.

,Wow. An Eastern relic? You had a very dangerous thing?"

It has a special effect of resisting Ma (魔). Thanks to that, I was able to confirm it. You can't copy powers that oppose Ma.

,I won't deny it. But what can you do? All you, a wizard, can do is use this troublesome Vajra. You can't kill me with just that."

I took a breath. According to my will, the nature of mana changes to divine mana.

Pajijijik.

The changed divine mana turned into golden light due to the influence of Barachiel's blessing.

,...A wizard handling divine mana. No matter how much you've been blessed by an angel, it's impossible... ."

,You seem very curious about my unique skill. Okay. I'll show you."

[Absolute Polymorph]

I use the power of Illusion Dream. The coat shines and enters me. The target to transform into through [Absolute Polymorph] is an angel I've transformed into before.

Golden wings sprouted on my back, and a ring-shaped halo shone and appeared above my head. It was much more powerful than when I transformed into an angel before. That's because I've grown more than I was then.

,A faceless angel? Oho! So that famous existence was you?! To think that Merlin's transcendent magic could turn a human into an angel!"

Mephistopheles stomped his feet as if excited.

I looked at him and said calmly.

,A unique skill is the essence of existence. Body, mind, soul. It's the realization of the mental image formed by all of those things influencing it. That's why a unique skill, once awakened, doesn't change. That's common sense. But there's no more idiotic thing to do than to be bound by common sense."

,Haha. You've broken away from that common sense?"

In the original work, it was possible to have multiple unique skills if you met several conditions. Well, it's closer to a trick of deriving different effects by putting one unique skill under several conditions.

,I haven't completely broken away. My mental image is firm. But my mental image is much wider and incomprehensible than you and I imagine. There's something I don't know inside me."

I guess it's probably related to supernatural power.

,Haha. I'm looking forward to it. I love irregulars very much. Now, show me!"

I pulled my hand into the air.

Divine mana gathers and realizes my mental image as a unique skill.

Unique Skill: Evil-Annihilating Execution Sword.

I grabbed the hilt of the realized Execution Sword.

The effect of the Execution Sword is one.

To realize the power and authority of the angel I know. Of course, I can't realize it completely. For now, all I can do is temporarily realize a degraded authority that isn't even half of half.

The first thing I thought of was Gabriel.

If it's Gabriel, the most famous authority, prophecy and revelation, is activated.

I saw the future. This is a prophecy.

I knew the way to that future. This was a revelation.

,...That power is Gabriel's...! I wanted an irregular, but this seems to be much more than that?!"

Mephistopheles attacks me. Dozens of attacks at least at the 6th circle level pour towards me. But nothing touches my body. According to the prophecy and revelation, I avoided what I could avoid, and I split what I couldn't avoid with the Execution Sword.

Even if it was an ordinary attack, its essence was the power of a demon. The Execution Sword, with the power of extreme compatibility, cut through the demon's attack too easily.

Having reached Mephistopheles in that way, I swung my sword towards his body.

Seogeok.

The demon's body was bisected, but Mephistopheles did not die. The demon's black blood wrapped around his body and eroded the surroundings.

,Someone's unique skill."

,Haha. What a pity. I won't die from just this much of an attack."

,I know. The reason I arrived was to confirm the location."

Pajijijijik.

Golden lightning flows through the Execution Sword.

I grabbed the hilt of the sword with both hands and raised it towards the sky.

,Barachiel. Resonate with my power."

Golden lightning soared into the starry night sky. Immediately after, a huge golden lightning bolt tore through the Great Witch's barrier and fell on Mephistopheles.

,Kuaaaaaaaaaaaaaaak!"
