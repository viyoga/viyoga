Gwangroe Seong Yujin became the Chairman of the Dragon Hunter Association. He became the ruler of the current world's humanity. If he were a human, he could be called the only emperor.

People knew Gwangroe's nature, so they predicted that a large number of people would die. But it wasn't so. Gwangroe's purge was only carried out on those related to Baek Jeongsu.

The regulations of the Hunter Association had changed, but there were no laws imposed on ordinary people. At most, it was just that beautiful women were forcibly taken away. More than half of the beautiful women welcomed it. After all, it's a tough world to live in anyway. They judged that becoming Gwangroe's woman and changing their fate was better.

Mir, the only surviving disciple of Baek Jeongsu, lived alone in the Wolf Eye Clan House. No, the clan was practically disbanded, so it was right to call it Mir's home now, not the clan house.

Mir lived quietly.

He ignored the Hunters who passed by and mocked and ridiculed him. He didn't even feel like arguing back.

,What should I do?

He had no purpose in life.

Revenge on Gwangroe, who killed his master Baek Jeongsu? It was enough as a justification. Even if he opposed Gwangroe, no one would say anything. Rather, they would nod, saying it was justified.

However, Mir didn't feel anger at Baek Jeongsu's death. Knowing Gwangroe's power, he accepted Baek Jeongsu's death as a matter of course. Baek Jeongsu, who fought Gwangroe, seemed foolish to him. Because fighting wasn't the only answer.

,There's no place for me here. Other Hunters are retiring too. I should retire too. I have money. I can live comfortably in a suitable village without an association for the rest of my life.

But Hiteira and Jina won't be by his side.

That was why Mir couldn't leave Gigantria.

Hiteira and Jina, fed up with Gwangroe's misdeeds, might return to this clan house. He had a small, invisible hope.

A few days later, someone knocked on the door of the clan house.

With a pounding heart, Mir opened the door to greet the guest. It was a Hunter, a man, not Hiteira and Jina.

He knew his face. A veteran B-class Hunter who he had been quite close to in the Hunter Association. The Hunter who had explained Do Hwang's relationships with women.

,I heard. All your women have become Gwangroe's women? Like master, like disciple.

,...Did you come here wanting to die? It's not like I can't kill you.

Mir clenched his fist. Pajijik. Yellow lightning flickered. He was confident that he could kill the old B-class Hunter in front of him with his bare hands.

Whoa. Whoa. My way of speaking is naturally vulgar, so calm down. I'm older than you, so show some respect, you guy.

,I only respect those who deserve respect. You don't deserve it.

,Haha. He's a spitting image of Do Hwang when he was young.

If you're going to bring up 사부님 (sabunim - teacher) and tell me to take revenge, get lost. I have no intention of fighting Gwangroe. I don't want a dog's death.

,There's no way. Now that Do Hwang died without even inflicting a fatal wound on Gwangroe, even the kids in the neighborhood know that there's no human who can beat Gwangroe. I can't tell you to die.

,Then what did you come for? Tell me what you want.

He put his hand in his inside pocket. Mir tensed up for a moment, but soon relaxed. If he was going to do something, he wouldn't have started with a conversation like this.

What he took out was a letter.

,It's Do Hwang's will. He asked me to give it to his disciple if he died.

,...Why did the teacher leave his will with you? He could have left it with his disciple in advance.

,He was predicting that Gwangroe would kill all of his disciples.

,Master, you also predicted that I would survive?

,You were close to Gwangroe, weren't you? Besides, you haven't been Do Hwang's disciple for long. I heard you got a guarantee from Gwangroe that he wouldn't kill you?

Gwangroe and Baek Jeongsu had such a conversation when they were alive? Even so, he didn't know that Gwangroe would keep his promise.

,Wasn't it because of Hiteira and Jina?

Anyway, he received the letter. Then he turned around without any regrets.

,I've delivered what I had to deliver. What you do with that letter is up to you now.

,Where are you going?

,Of course, I have to leave this shitty city. If Gwangroe finds out about my existence, he'll try to purge me right away. Dragons are almost extinct too. If I hide in a remote countryside, Gwangroe won't be able to find me. We won't meet again. I'll forget you, so you forget me too. Take care.

He left without regret.

Mir stared at his back disappearing into the alleyway and closed the door. Only now did he seem to know to some extent. That guy wasn't an ordinary B-class Hunter.

He sat on the sofa and checked the contents of the letter.

Will.

He thought it would contain words he couldn't usually say. But the content was too formal to be a will.

-If you want to kill Gwangroe, go west and meet the White-Eyed Heavenly Dragon Gigamera. If you go west, Gigamera will guide you. If you don't have the desire for revenge, pass this letter on to another disciple. If there are no surviving disciples... burn this will in the fire.

,......

Mir closed his eyes. Gwangroe's image came to mind. His hands and feet trembled slightly at the fear of him.

There is no one among humans who can kill Gwangroe.

But what about a Dragon King-level dragon?

There was no way that Baek Jeongsu sent this letter for no reason. There must be a way, so he sent this letter.

,...Even a Dragon King-level dragon doesn't seem like it could kill Gwangroe.

Mir quietly burned the will.

And a few days later.

Mir left for the west.

He couldn't bear the loneliness without Hiteira and Jina.

,If only Gwangroe wasn't there...! Hiteira and Jina will come back to my side...!

Loneliness drove people crazy.

Mir moved aimlessly to the west. He took a truck, and when the truck broke down, he walked to the west. After a few days, he met a dragon with three eyes.

The White-Eyed Heavenly Dragon Gigamera.

He wasn't a dragon with a hundred eyes like his nickname. But he could see farther than the combined vision of 100 eyes.

Mir opened his mouth, trying to bear the trembling of his body at Gigamera's aura.

,Are you the White-Eyed Heavenly Dragon?

-That's right. Disciple of Do Hwang.

,...The will said that if I met you, I could kill Gwangroe.

-Unfortunately, I am the weakest among the Dragon Kings. It's hard to kill Gwangroe with my power. But I know a way to kill Gwangroe. I have the power to see the other side of the world from my seat. I have observed the world with those eyes. There is no one in this world who has more knowledge than me.

,...If you can see so much... Why are you coming forward only now?

-Humans don't interest me. I heard about Gwangroe's existence from Do Hwang, but... he didn't attract my attention much. Even if I can see the end of the world, I don't necessarily have to see the end of the world. But now that I think about it, it's too dangerous to ignore him... Gwangroe is too dangerous. The extinction of dragons is just around the corner. How can I ignore it?

,Can you see what Gwangroe is doing now?

-I can if I want to. The problem is that the Dreamy Heavenly Dragon is next to him. No. Now that she's human, I should call her Hiteira. She, who controls space, can not only recognize my gaze but also track it in reverse.

Hiteira. Mir flinched at the name. Hiteira was versatile. Thinking of her, he felt a surge of anger towards Gwangroe.

,I was the one who met Hiteira first...!

He swallowed his resentment and asked Gigamera.

,Tell me how to kill Gwangroe!

-You. How much do you know about the Dragon God?

,Isn't the Dragon God a legend?

-The Dragon God exists. I have been tracking the traces of the Dragon God with my eyes for a very long time. The Dragon God values balance. So much so that he sealed himself for the sake of that balance. And that balance has already been broken by Gwangroe. How about it? Do you want to release the Dragon God's seal?

,...I'm weaker than you. But do you need my help?

-Some seals require special conditions. Like being human but having the power of a dragon. In short, I can't completely release the Dragon God's seals without your help.

,If there's anything I can do... I'll do it all.

I gathered all the beautiful women in this world.

But not even 1% of the beautiful women caught my eye. Most of the beautiful women were beautiful, but they were a bit lacking according to my standards. Of course, I could accept those beautiful women too.

,There really aren't any beauties on the level of Hiteira and Jina.

Besides that, I wielded power here and there. Since I had power, it was only natural to use it.

,There weren't many places to use it properly.

This world is a world where countries have been destroyed by the aftermath of the Great War. There wasn't much that was proper. Surprisingly, there were no food problems at all, but the overall population and quality of life didn't even match half of modern times.

,What I can get in this world is the dragon's heart and dragon's heart blood, as well as other dragon by-products.

Dragon skin, bones, and tendons are useful for Hunter equipment. Magic tools are also made with dragon's heart and dragon's heart blood as materials.

,I should start preparing to hunt the Deep Sea Heavenly Dragon Calodon soon.

There was no need to rush.

I can prepare slowly. Even if Calodon is deep in the sea, Hiteira's ability should be able to pinpoint its location.

That's how 2 months passed.

• • •

I decided that only Hiteira and I would take part in the Deep Sea Heavenly Dragon Calodon subjugation.

I wanted to summon all the Hunters to confirm the power of the Chairman and put them in a dogpile... but this was the middle of the sea. If the ship we came on broke, the Hunters would be immediately drowned. There was no answer. Hiteira and I were better off because we could fly in the sky or teleport.

Hiteira and I looked down at the wavy sea from the sky.

,Is it here?

,Yes. The sea is also part of space after all. I found it by exploring space.

[Opening the Cheonan (heavenly eye).]

Deep in the sea, a huge dragon was lying at the bottom of the sea.

Even if I launched a slash or struck lightning, it wouldn't reach that deep. But it was too dangerous for Hiteira and I to go directly into the sea. A dragon with the ability to control water. The sea is no different from inside its mouth.

,We didn't just play around for 2 months.
