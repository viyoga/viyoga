,Hello~"

Kang Su-mi greeted everyone she met as she entered the shantytown.

The elderly watched Kang Su-mi with a pleased expression. Relatively younger people simply nodded and passed by. Of course, not everyone was friendly. Some were wary.

,I don't think I've seen you kids around here before. What brings you to this neighborhood?"

,My oppa is going to be living in Seoul next month, so we came to look at houses!"

She patted my shoulder as she spoke. I smiled awkwardly and said to the old man as planned.

,I took a leave of absence because of the military, but I'm planning to go back to school next month."

,You went to the army? Your hair's pretty long for someone who did."

,I took a few months off after I was discharged."

,Hmm. I see. This neighborhood is a bit dark and has a lot of hills, but the rent is cheap, so it's not bad for living while you're going to school. Take a good look around."

He lowered his guard. He's about to leave. Kang Su-mi greeted him with a bright voice and asked.

,Yes! Thank you! Do you live here too, harabeoji?!"

,See that building over there? I live there. If you have any questions, come and ask! I've lived in this neighborhood for 30 years! 30 years!"

,Yes! I'll definitely ask if I have any questions!"

He turns his back and leaves again. Kang Su-mi pretended to wave goodbye while reflecting the old man's back in the witch's mirror. The old man is reflected as he is. The old man was human.

,Well. I figured as much.'

Vampires don't take the form of old people. Vampires who live eternal lives can adjust their appearance even at the Common level. Most maintain a young appearance in their 20s or 30s. Even older-looking faces are only middle-aged at most.

There's no reason to take on the appearance of a shabby old man. Unless there's a specific purpose.

,There's no way Kang Su-mi doesn't know this... Well, it's her job, so it's right to check everyone, even the elderly.'

Kang Su-mi took the lead and walked ahead. I followed behind her.

I looked up at the sky. The sun was starting to set.

,I found one."

Kang Su-mi said in a low voice. I lowered my gaze from the sky. Kang Su-mi was hiding by the corner of a wall, secretly watching a passerby.

,That's a vampire? He looks like an office worker?"

,Take a look for yourself."

She showed me the witch's mirror. The college student in the mirror was a monster with a muscular body and insect-like antennae.

I reflexively reached for the knife hidden in my clothes.

,Judging from the big difference between his true form and his disguised form, he's Common level.'

In a one-on-one fight, I can easily win. If I use Flash, I'm confident I can finish him off in 3 seconds.

,We're not here to fight. Don't forget our purpose is to gather information, right?"

,Of course."

I took my hand off the knife. What I really need to be wary of is Kang Su-mi, not the vampire.

,Let's tail him. We need to find out where that vampire lives and install a bug. Information is what the company needs right now."

,Right. I forgot for a second."

,Follow me carefully. If you tail him carelessly, you'll get caught."

Kang Su-mi moves.

She's pretty good at tailing, probably because she's been trained.

,It's not easy to follow a vampire who has senses superior to humans... Does she have some kind of manual or something?'

The vampire stopped by a nearby convenience store and then went into a semi-basement apartment. It seems that being a vampire doesn't mean you're not strapped for cash.

,Are we going to install the bug after going inside the house?"

,Going into that house is crazy. We'll get caught right away if we go in. In this case, we memorize the location... and install the bug near the entrance."

,Are we going to go inside later to install it?"

,We wait for the vampire to leave, then go inside and install the bug and come out. You'll be surprised when we do that job, you know? It makes my heart race. I've done it over 20 times, and my heart still beats like crazy every time."

,What if we decide there's no information to be gained?"

,The job gets passed on to the Combat or Special Ops teams. In neighborhoods with a lot of these people, the Combat team usually storms in all at once and wipes them out."

,Have you ever seen the Combat team in action?"

,About 3 times. It feels almost like a war. Armored vehicles are standard, and they even bring in helicopters."

She walked forward as she spoke. When people passed by, she switched to a different topic. At the same time, she subtly checked the vampire with the witch's mirror. If it was a vampire, she would tail them to find out where they lived and install a bug, even if it was a rough job.

,Next is over there."

,Are you sure about that place?"

What she pointed to was a fortune teller's shop in the corner of an alley. There wasn't even a sign. It just said 'Cassandra's Fortune Telling' in front of the gate.

,Even for a fortune teller's shop... How can they do business in a place like this?'

It doesn't seem like they have any intention of doing business at all.

,You think it's suspicious too, right? Since they're posing as a fortune teller's shop, let's go inside pretending to be customers."

Kang Su-mi moved confidently.

I was nervous. Maybe this is a trap that Gwak Su-hyeok prepared.

She rings the doorbell. With a beep, a woman's voice came from the intercom.

-Come in.

The gate opened.

We went inside.

There was no yard, just a short passage. A few flower pots were placed as decorations in the passage.

,It doesn't look like vampires are hiding here. Is it not a trap?'

Having been through countless traps, I had a feeling this wasn't one. But I didn't let my guard down, just in case.

We reached the end of the passage. Kang Su-mi grabbed the doorknob and said in a small voice.

,Just in case, be prepared. There might be a vampire. I'm going in."

Kang Su-mi turned the doorknob and opened the door.

There was a strong smell of incense.

The room was dark, with black curtains drawn and lit by candles. The limitations of candlelight meant the room was basically dark. It was exactly the kind of atmosphere a vampire would like.

,Hehe. Interesting people have come."

In the center of the room, a woman covered from head to toe in black cloth was sitting at a black desk. On the desk was a large crystal ball.

The only part of the woman that was visible was her eyes. The wrinkles around her eyes were impossible to hide. She was a pretty old ajumma.

,I am Cassandra. Now, come and sit here."

,Ah, yes!"

Kang Su-mi answered and walked slowly. With the woman watching, she couldn't openly take out the witch's mirror to check. If the woman was a vampire, she would attack as soon as she saw the witch's mirror.

I sat down at the desk next to Kang Su-mi.

,We are."

,It's alright. Let me guess. The two of you are... siblings, not lovers."

,Oh, how did you know?!"

Kang Su-mi made a fuss.

Well, of course. Would lovers come to a place like this on a date? Absolutely not. Besides, Kang Su-mi was even wearing a middle school uniform. It's normal to see them as brother and sister rather than lovers. Kang Su-mi probably knows that too, but she still made a fuss.

,Hmm. I see a very interesting future..."

,Really?! What kind of future?!"

,That will be 200,000 won."

The woman said firmly.

200,000 won.

I don't know much, but that seems like a pretty expensive price for a fortune teller's shop. It doesn't seem like she's doing tarot readings or writing amulets separately.

,Oppa! This person is really amazing! She knew we were siblings right away! Please, oppa!"

,Uh, well. Okay. If you want to... But I don't have 200,000 won in cash right now."

,We accept cards. Of course, bank transfers are also possible."

,Please charge it to my card."

I swiped my card. Isn't she reeking of fraud a little too obviously?

,I have received payment. Now, now, shall we steal a peek at the secrets of the heavens? First, the young lady... Crystal ball, crystal ball, show me!"

The crystal ball didn't glow! Nothing like that happened. She was just rubbing the large crystal ball with her palms. It was somewhat clumsy and crude.

,Hoh? The young lady is not her real age."

,Yes, yes?!"

Kang Su-mi was genuinely flustered. She was suddenly hit right on the mark.

,Are you perhaps born in an early year?"

,Yes! That's right! It's amazing that you could tell!"

Kang Su-mi spoke exaggeratedly, desperately trying to hide her agitation. Her hand under the desk was trembling slightly.

,Hehe. It's basic for me. Oh my, young lady, you have lived a hard life."

,H, hard?"

,You probably know that better than I do. What I see is a flame reflected in a lake. Everything except the lake is burning."

,......."

Kang Su-mi closed her mouth. She's already agitated, even though I don't know what's going on. Cassandra continued to stroke the crystal ball and speak, unconcerned by Kang Su-mi's reaction.

,Young lady. There is an obstacle in your life. The obstacle is sparkling and tempting you. It makes you do wrong things, but only leads to ruin. But fortunately, there is a benefactor who can remove the obstacle. If you follow that benefactor... Yes. A smooth life will unfold. Follow the benefactor. Then you'll be able to do almost anything."

Kang Su-mi was captivated by Cassandra's words. She was amazing. I was already listening to Cassandra's words too.

,A benefactor...? Who is that?"

,There is a saying in this country: 'The light under the lamp is the darkest.'"

,Yes?"

,Don't you understand yet? You have already met the benefactor."

,Met...? What does that mean..."

Kang Su-mi glanced at me as she said that. We're pretending to be siblings, but she and I are meeting for the first time today. If I am the benefactor that Cassandra is referring to, then she has already seen through the fact that Kang Su-mi and I are not siblings.

,You just need to trust and follow the benefactor. Then you will be able to achieve your goal."

,Ah, yes..."

,Now, let's tell the fortune of the young man. Crystal ball, crystal ball, show me!"

Cassandra rubbed the crystal ball passionately.

I stared at her with a nonchalant look. I don't know how she was able to win over Kang Su-mi, but it won't be easy with me.

,Oh my. That's really unique."

,What's unique?"

,Your life has changed recently. Or rather, should I say your life has begun? You were born recently."

,I'm over 20 years old."

,Hehe. I'm just saying what I saw. You know best what I'm talking about."

,......."

,You are a truly amazing person. So much so that I dare not judge you."

,What does that mean..."

,The crystal ball tells me. This world revolves around you. You are the center of this world."

,Yes?"

,Are you perhaps a god?"
