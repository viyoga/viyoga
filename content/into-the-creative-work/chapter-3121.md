I received a report from Lee Yeonhee that an unauthorized undersea tunnel had been discovered in a mountain in Kyushu, reported by Japanese Governor Oneko.

"An undersea tunnel. Weren't we already planning to build one connecting the Korean Peninsula and the Japanese Archipelago? And it already exists—fucking jackpot."

Tectonic shifts had brought the Korean Peninsula and the Japanese Archipelago closer together.

Before the war, if Japan had been a foreign nation, I wouldn't have even glanced at an undersea tunnel, but now Japan is unequivocally Imperial territory. I was also planning to build a maglev train as a test.

"It hasn't yet been confirmed where the undersea tunnel connects to. However, it's certain that it's a passage linked to the ocean floor."

"You haven't confirmed it? Why?"

"It was just discovered moments ago. Calling it an undersea tunnel is also speculation—the more likely possibility is that it's not an undersea tunnel but a cave dug out by a mutant."

"That makes sense."

A tunnel is, after all, a passage with an opening at the end. Even if it were an undersea tunnel connected to the Japanese Archipelago, the farthest it could reach would be Tsushima or the Korean Peninsula.

On top of that, the Archipelago had recently been experiencing frequent earthquakes from tectonic shifts. Only two weeks had passed since the tectonic activity stabilized. Digging an undersea tunnel in just two weeks? Even with the Empire's technological prowess, that's no easy feat.

'More than anything, both are my territory. Someone dug a tunnel without my permission? Do they have a death wish?'

What was even more astonishing was that the Empire hadn't noticed until now. This was not a normal situation by any measure.

"We'll begin drone reconnaissance shortly. You can monitor it in real time. Would you like to watch?"

"I've got nothing better to do. Let's see what kind of tunnel this is."

The monitor flickered on.

A crowd of people had gathered at the nameless mountain. Most were soldiers, and I could also see knights clad in black power armor. The on-site commander was Governor Oneko. Given the gravity of the situation, Oneko had stepped forward personally.

"Hm? Oneko's wearing a mask, yet she's showing up on camera."

Oneko's mask is an Outpart with information concealment abilities. When wearing that mask, she miraculously doesn't appear on camera.

"Depending on the camera's performance and shooting distance, the information concealment function seems to apply differently."

"Is that so? So it's not omnipotent."

Oneko and the soldiers saluted toward the camera. They must know I'm watching.

Choi Hyejin appeared on the footage. She was wearing a military jacket instead of power armor, crouched on the ground fiddling with her smartphone.

"Does she even know how high her position is?"

The Empire's Knight Commander is at minimum a general-grade or division-commander-grade rank. In practice, even corps commanders can't treat the Knights carelessly. From the start, the Knights are an armed force that carries out only the Emperor's orders above all else. They could be called the Emperor's private soldiers and personal guard.

"She should know. I've explained that position to her several times now."

"Choi Hyejin. She's a bit thick-headed, so she might have forgotten."

Choi Hyejin is the classic warrior type. Great body, so her head's nice and empty, you could say.

"Even so, Knight Commander Choi Hyejin has never used her power with ill intent. Ah, recently Knight Commander Choi Hyejin has been requesting the opening of a pro baseball league."

"Pro baseball. Right. She's been pestering me about it too."

I keep in regular contact with Choi Hyejin. Though it's just through a chat room. About 30% of the content is about baseball.

"Perhaps you should grant her request. As the Empire has become relatively peaceful, the citizens are craving circuses."

Bread and circuses.

The traditional method of managing the masses.

Technological advancement had roughly solved the bread part. Next comes the circus. The culture that had been halted by war needed to be set in motion again.

"Fine. Approved. Include the Japanese territories in the pro baseball league as well. Invest in soccer and other sports too."

I agree that circuses are necessary.

Shortly after, the drone began its reconnaissance. Whether it was a tunnel or simply a deep cave would soon be revealed.

The drone lit its lamps and entered. It flew stably. For reference, it wasn't being piloted by a human—the strong AI Zena was controlling it.

"The size... a truck could easily fit through."

"Yes. Even at this size, there don't appear to be any special reinforcements. At the very least, they should have erected pillars.... Wait, Zena. Can we get a closer look at the ceiling and walls?"

[Yes. Director Lee.]

The drone clung to the wall and ceiling. Viewed up close, a grayish-white substance stretched across the walls like spiderwebs. The ceiling and floor were no exception.

"That substance appears to be reinforcing the cave. Have someone collect samples. Research is needed."

[Understood.]

The drone flew deeper inside.

It flew cautiously, but there were no notable changes.

"There's nothing here. Speed it up. Ah, do you know roughly where we are?"

[Yes. Your Majesty. The drone's approximate current location is here. Beneath the seafloor.]

A map appeared on one side of the screen. Han Na and Lee Yeonhee's eyes narrowed at the drone's position.

The drone was moving sideways, not upward toward the Korean Peninsula's position in Kyushu.

"China? Have those bastards been digging a tunnel?"

"China shouldn't be in any position to afford that right now.... It seems too early to be certain."

The endpoint hadn't been confirmed yet.

The drone flew faster than before. The sound of its flight was quite loud. If anyone was there, they'd hear it and notice the drone's presence.

It doesn't matter if the drone gets destroyed. Reconnaissance drones are expendable anyway. The moment information of an attack is obtained, the recon drone has served its purpose.

The drone's direction on the map gradually angled downward.

"Taiwan? It's connected to Taiwan. They dug hundreds of kilometers of tunnel in just a few days. Lee Yeonhee. What's the current situation in Taiwan?"

"I apologize, Your Majesty. We have almost no information on Taiwan. Taiwan is currently thoroughly isolated, and all we know is that they're struggling with unmanaged mutants. Even this is information obtained through the internet, but.... Recently, there's been almost no information about Taiwan appearing online either."

The possibility of China having invaded Taiwan is... nil. China doesn't have the capacity for that right now. China itself is fractured and hasn't even stabilized yet—how could they invade Taiwan?

"It's one of two things. Either their infrastructure is destroyed, or the government is controlling information. Are there any Players in Taiwan?"

"It hasn't been confirmed. However, I once heard from Dr. Na Chaeyoung long ago. She said Players avoid Taiwan as a starting point. Aside from being an isolated island and having the advantage of rapidly developing science and technology using semiconductors, it has no major benefits, and it has the significant drawback of almost certainly being invaded by China."

At least it's certain there's no Technologist.

If there were a Technologist, there would be technological research achievements by now. The silence makes no sense otherwise.

'In all likelihood, there are no other Class Players either.'

The drone, flying at high speed, began to slow. Zena had detected something and decelerated.

'Eggs?'

Eggs larger than watermelons, coated in mucus, were clustered densely on one side of the wall. The eggs pulsated as if breathing. Made of flesh and mucous membrane, they didn't appear hard.

"Was this a mutant's cave after all?"

"It's too deep to simply be a mutant habitat. Moreover, the passage continues further in. There's a high probability this is an undersea tunnel connected to Taiwan."

"Let's crack open one of those eggs first. I'm curious what's inside."

The recon drone approached the egg. The knife mounted on the drone jutted out at the bottom of the screen and sliced the egg open.

Puk!

Along with fluid—amniotic or otherwise—that had filled the egg's interior, an immature lifeform spilled onto the floor and trembled.

It was a monster with a spider's body—eight legs—and the upper torso of a human infant that looked fragile at a glance.

"...Mutant species, Arachne."

Lee Yeonhee muttered quietly.

Arachne.

A mutant that appears in the original game as well. They live in groups and possess human-level intelligence, so they're classified as a mutant species. Naturally, Awakened exist among the Arachne as well.

"Your Majesty. The Arachne are...."

"I know. They're enemies of humanity that prey on humans."

Even if the upper torso were a stunning beauty, if the lower half is a spider's body, it doesn't get me hard.

"Listen. As Emperor of the Empire, I hereby declare."

[We're listening.]

I could feel Lee Yeonhee's eyes and Zena's sensors focusing on me.

"The Arachne are not human. They are enemies of humanity that prey on humans. The only fate for enemies of humanity is death. The Empire does not negotiate with enemies of humanity. Kill every Arachne you see on sight. Their offspring are no exception."

[We receive the Imperial command.]

The drone's knife sliced through the head of the infant Arachne writhing on the floor. The drone didn't stop there. It slashed through all the Arachne eggs and killed every Arachne offspring in sight.

Tang!

A gunshot rang out and the drone fell. Unluckily, it had been hit in the wing section. Just before crashing, the drone poured bullets toward the direction the shot came from. The bullets were blocked by a massive shield.

Having expended all its ammunition, the drone stared at the enemy through its camera.

It was an Arachne holding a massive shield in one hand and a rifle in the other. Its height exceeded 2 meters and its bulk was the size of a compact car.

It set down its gun and lifted the drone up.

"The Empire, is it. Are you watching? We wish to speak with the Empire. Emperor. Our Queen wishes to make you an offer you cannot refuse."

The male Arachne spoke in Korean. I checked the Arachne's face on the screen with an indifferent expression. It had a human head, but six eyes, and its mouth was a pair of chelicerae split down the center of the jaw that parted slightly whenever it spoke, revealing venomous fangs.

"Humans do not converse with insects."

[Self-destructing. 3, 2, 1.]

The Arachne seemed to realize something too late, its six eyes flying wide open, but it was already over. The drone exploded. No matter how much tougher an Arachne's body is compared to a human's, a bomb detonating right in its face means death.

I looked at the darkened screen and gave my order.

"I order the Eagles Knights, including Choi Hyejin, to deploy. Reconnoiter Taiwan and annihilate the Arachne."
