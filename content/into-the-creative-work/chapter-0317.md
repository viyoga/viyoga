"It's been a while, Baron Osten."

Baron Osten activated his mana upon seeing the person who came to his mansion. He had to do this, or he might get ambushed without even knowing why.

"...What brings you here, Duke Valderte-kakha?"

Elena approached with a leisurely, elegant gait and sat on the sofa, crossing her legs.

"There's no need to be so wary of me. I didn't come to fight with the Baron, nor do I have any reason to."

In contrast to Osten's stiff expression, Elena wore an arrogant smile on her face.

Baron Osten felt uncomfortable with Elena. It wasn't just because Elena was one of the Empire's Five Pillars, Empire Ogong, the Illusion Duke. It wasn't just because she was a neutral party, neither aristocratic nor imperial.

It was simply because Elena's existence itself was unsettling.

"Haven't you had a big incident recently? A riot broke out in your prized prison, and over 70% of the inmates and guards were killed or injured, I hear?"

"...Did you come here to insult me?"

"Of course not. I was just passing by and wanted to offer words of comfort."

Baron Osten didn't believe Elena's words. Passing by? What a joke. Someone of Elena's stature would rarely walk anywhere on foot.

"Is that so? Thank you for coming, but I'm fine. The restoration of the prison is progressing smoothly. We've decided to fortify the prison this time. We plan to raise the level of the guards and deploy soldiers and knights. Riots will no longer occur."

"Hmm. Isn't that a bit excessive? Or do you have some important treasure hidden in the prison?"

"For me, the prison itself is like a treasure. You know that well, don't you?"

Baron Osten's power increased depending on the number of prisoners in the prison. He was strongest when the prison was full, and now, with most of the prisoners dead or escaped, he was weaker than ever.

"Baron, you must be aware of the rumors circulating about you in society, right?"

"Are you talking about the absurd rumor that I'm raising a man-eating monster in the prison? Did you come all the way here just because of such a rumor? It seems the Illusion Duke is much more idle than I thought."

"I'm much busier than the Baron thinks. And much more capable than the Baron thinks."

"...What are you trying to say?"

"I have information that the Water Robe Duke is deeply involved in a cult."

"I don't know where you got that information, but the Water Robe Duke is not that kind of person."

"That's not for the Baron to judge."

"......Is that so? What does the Water Robe Duke's cult have to do with me?"

"Isn't the Water Robe Duke the leader of the aristocratic faction? Could there be something related to the cult in your prison? That's what I suspect."

"Did you come all the way here just for that? There are no monsters in my prison, and I have no interest in any religion, including cults. And I'm sure that's the same for the Water Robe Duke. Please leave now. I hope you understand that I'm busy with the prison. I'll formally invite you later, kakha."

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"What chance are you talking about?"

"A chance to tell me everything."

"...Ha. I have nothing to hide. How many times do I have to say it? This is frustrating."

"Is that so? Then I'll be going."

"Yes. Take care. I'm too busy to see you off. I hope you understand."

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"What chance are you talking about?"

"A chance to tell me everything."

"...Ha. I have nothing to hide. How many times do I have to say......"

Osten noticed that the conversation was repeating. He immediately lunged at Elena, who was sitting on the sofa. He was about to strike Elena's head with a fist swirling with blue aura.

Puh-ung!

Osten's body exploded.

Arms, legs, and entrails flew in all directions, and his head spun towards the ceiling.

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"What chance......"

Osten shut his mouth. Cold sweat ran down his face. The pain of his body exploding and dying that he had just felt still lingered in his mind.

"Baron. What's wrong? You don't look well."

"......"

Osten didn't open his mouth. He ignored Elena. This was probably an illusion.

'When did I fall under an illusion?'

He couldn't guess. To escape the illusion, he had to......

Puh-ung!

Osten's body exploded.

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"...Illusion Duke, when did you cast an illusion on me?"

"When? That's obvious, from the beginning."

Puh-ung!

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"......"

Osten detonated his mana. He intended to use mana to blow away the illusion cast on him. If it were an ordinary illusion, he could have dispelled it with that. However, it wasn't a very good way to break Elena's illusion.

Puh-ung!

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

Osten tried to escape the illusion. He tried to kill himself, and he tried to kill Elena in front of him. He went outside the mansion, and he tried to catch the blue butterfly attached to the ceiling.

But it was all in vain.

Puh-ung.

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

The 101st time he heard those words.

Tired of his body exploding, Osten, with tears streaming down his face, said to Elena.

"I'll tell you. I'll tell you everything. So please, get me out of this hell."

"Let's hear it first. Start talking."

"The rumors about the Water Robe Duke are true. The identity of the monster I'm raising in the prison is a priest of that cult, and every week, I capture a prisoner and offer them as a sacrifice to the cult's god."

Baron Osten revealed everything he had been hiding. He had betrayed the aristocratic faction, but he couldn't help it. He didn't want to die forever in this inescapable hell.

"I see. I understand. The Water Robe Duke was doing something quite gruesome."

"Then now...!"

Puh-ung!

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"Ah...."

Puh-ung!

"Baron Osten. I'll give you a chance."

"......"

Puh-ung!

"A cult, huh."

Elena crossed her arms and muttered quietly.

At the end of her gaze, Baron Osten was sitting blankly.

"He finally crossed the line."

Elena stood up.

Baron Osten suddenly came to his senses.

"...Are you leaving? Why did you come here anyway?"

Baron Osten seemed unaware of what had just happened.

"I just wanted to see what kind of expression the Baron would make because of the prison riot. Well, it was a more interesting expression than I thought."

"...The Illusion Duke still has bad taste, I see."

"I hear that a lot. Good work."

"I'm busy, so I won't see you off."

"Alright."

Elena left.

Osten watched her go and trembled. He couldn't understand why he was trembling.
