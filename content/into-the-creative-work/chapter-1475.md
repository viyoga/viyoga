The Fallen, revealing their true nature, rush in from all directions.

As if I'd been waiting, I gripped my greatsword and spun around. As the massive sword whirled like a top, the Fallen flinched and backed away.

,Pathetic bastards.'

They haven't been Fallen for long. Experienced Fallen would have trusted their regeneration, which is incomparably superior to humans, and charged at my sword without hesitation. And I would have struggled quite a bit.

,If they come at me like this, it's easy to deal with.'

I approach the Fallen closest to me and throw a punch. The Fallen staggers from the blow. A huge blade protrudes from the Fallen's chest, aiming for my shoulder. Ka-ang! The blade is blocked by my armor. The shoulder area of the armor is slightly torn.

,This is why you need to wear good armor.'

Buuuuuuung.

The massive greatsword bisects the bastard's body.

"Charge at him all at once and kill him!!"

"Even if he's a paladin, he's just one guy!"

"We have to kill him here! That's the only way we can live!"

"Let's kill him and have a blood festival!"

The Fallen shout. Growing desperate, the Fallen all rush at me at once. A squid-like bastard grabs my leg with a thick tentacle, a huge brute grabs my back, and nimble-looking bastards grab each of my arms.

"Kehehehe! Got him! We got him!"

"You're done for, you son of a bitch!"

"I'm going to tear out your guts while you're still alive!"

"I want the marrow! I'm going to suck the marrow out of the paladin!"

Other Fallen rush in, gleefully. Their claws and teeth relentlessly attack Stormbreaker. No matter how tough Stormbreaker is, it won't last long in this state.

,The Heavenly Heart I need most in this situation is on cooldown. And I can't use Full Recovery either.'

This is a crisis. If I stay like this, I'll die.

,But I can use Lightning.'

Pajijijijijijik!

Golden currents of electricity manifested from divine power spread in all directions. All the Fallen who were gleefully attacking me were electrocuted and trembled. Free from their grasp, I swung my greatsword. The speed at which I swung the sword was slow. This is because my physical stats aren't that overwhelming.

,There's no need to be impatient. Victory is already in my hands. I just need to take them down one by one.'

Each time I swing the greatsword, the Fallen's heads and bodies are separated.

The Fallen's death throes end. In the now-quiet street, only terrified villagers and two Fallen who hadn't charged remain.

I grip my sword and approach the remaining Fallen.

"D-don't come any closer! You monster!"

One of the Fallen ran away. It was a bastard with a broken leg, but he was running incredibly fast.

,First, I'll deal with the one who's still here.'

The remaining Fallen chose something other than running. He grabbed a nearby man and aimed his sharp claws at his neck.

"Aaaaaaaagh!"

"Shut up if you don't want to die! Paladin! You stop right there! You don't want this guy to die either, do you?! Let me escape! Then I'll spare this guy!"

My steps didn't stop.

"Stop! I said stop! Can't you hear me?! Do you have to see this guy die?!"

The Fallen's claws dug into the hostage's neck. Even so, I didn't stop.

"We do not negotiate with demons and the Fallen. We only kill them."

"S-save me! Paladin-nim! Please!"

The hostage trembled and begged for his life. I said to the hostage in an emotionless tone.

"Blame me."

"You crazy bastard!"

The Fallen's claws stab deep into the hostage's neck. A fountain of blood gushes out.

I ran, closing the distance, and swung my greatsword. Slicing through the Fallen and the hostage's body at the same time.

Blood splattered on my armor, making it sticky.

Fatigue washes over me all at once. But the work isn't over yet. I have to chase down and kill the Fallen who escaped.

,Demons and the Fallen devour humans and corrupt them. They're like a plague. If I leave him alone because he's annoying, the number of Fallen will only increase.'

Kiiiiiiing.

I activated Holy Eye. Holy Eye showed me the trail of the Fallen who had escaped. He circled around the village. He ran as if he were going to leave the village, but eventually hid inside the village.

,He's never been outside the village before. Or maybe he's trying to outsmart me by doing the opposite.'

If I didn't have Holy Eye, I would have had a hard time. I might have missed him altogether.

Clang, clang.

Making noise with my armor, I arrived at a private house. Entering the small house, I paused for a moment, doing nothing, and looked around.

Holy Eye found the hidden Fallen. He was hiding under the bed.

There was no reason to hesitate. I took my sword, approached the bed, and plunged it into the bed.

"Kkyaaaaaaaaaaak!"

The Fallen screamed horribly and jumped out of the bed. With his tattered shoulder, he splattered blood everywhere and ran out the door, sprinting through the village streets. I threw the tightly gripped greatsword at him.

The result was a hit.

He was impaled by the greatsword and fell to the ground. I approached and stomped on his head, crushing it.

,It's over. ...No. There might still be some hiding, so I should look around one more time.'

There were no more Fallen.

I decided to rest for a day and then leave the village. I climb onto my horse. There's no one around me. But I can feel the gazes. Gazes filled with fear, hoping that I will leave the village.

At that moment, an old man approached me, trembling.

"You must be the village chief. Do you have something to say?"

"Th-thank you for saving our village!"

"I was just doing my job."

"...This is a small amount of money we collected for the Order. Please, accept it."

"An offering, is it? Your money will be used to fund the killing of demons and the Fallen."

I received the money pouch.

Paladins do not refuse offerings. Half of the money received is used by themselves, and the other half is given to the Order. That was one of the Paladin's rules.

It's okay if I pocket this money. I wasn't just an ordinary paladin, but a paladin who had received the goddess's revelation.

"Restrain your desires. Demons incite human desires and corrupt them.... You must always be careful."

"Y-yes, I will keep that in mind."

The horse began to walk, clip-clop.

My destination is the Valentier Cathedral. It's quite a distance. It will take a week to get there on horseback.

,I should stop by churches and cathedrals along the way.'

The aura of corruption lingering on my body is very bothersome. And I need to replenish my holy water.

Kiiiiiiing.

I used Holy Eye from time to time. If I saw a corrupted animal, I would run over and kill it without hesitation. All of these were experience points.

•••

I stopped by a small church in a fairly large village.

The priests in the church looked aghast as soon as they saw me.

"This can't be! Such a large and dense aura of corruption...!"

"Holy water... no, the aura of corruption is too dense! A purification ritual would be more effective than holy water!"

"Inform the bishop and prepare a purification ritual!"

The priests bustled about, making a fuss.

I was about to go to the chapel, recalling the original game.

"Stop."

The church's paladins blocked my path. Dressed in full body armor, they looked at me with wary eyes.

"We know that you are an excellent paladin. But right now, your body is overflowing with impure energy. The priests are preparing a purification ritual, so please wait. And I would like you to tell us your identity. What is your name, and which church do you belong to? How did you get so much impure energy stuck to your body?"

"That's a lot of questions."

"Please understand. Demons and the Fallen have been running rampant lately, so the atmosphere in the church is as cold as ice."

"......"

Kiiiiiiiiing

I activated Holy Eye. My eyes turned blue, and a winged cross pattern appeared in the center.

The paladins were startled. They all drew their swords and planted them in the ground.

"We greet the Paladin!"

Holy Eye was one of the abilities that only paladins possessed, so it was also used to prove their identity.

"Did information arrive from the Vatican?"

"Yes. Paladin-isiyeo. Four days ago, the Vatican informed all churches and cathedrals of the existence of the Paladin!"

The paladins spoke in a tense tone. In their eyes, I could feel reverence towards me. I checked the number of paladins. Seven. This is enough.

"I'm going to the chapel. All of you, follow me."

"Paladin-isiyeo, first the purification ritual...."

"That is an order."

"...Yes. We will follow you."

I led them and strode towards the chapel. The paladins looked at me with questioning eyes. This was because I, who was visiting this church for the first time, walked straight to the chapel. I ignored their questions. It was annoying to explain, and I didn't feel the need to.

Just before opening the door to the chapel, I summoned Stormbreaker and put it on. Chwaaaaaar! From head to toe. Armored, I pushed open the door to the chapel without hesitation.

The chapel exuded a sacred atmosphere. At its center was a statue of the goddess Herianne. On both sides, statues of paladins were lined up.

I strode towards the goddess's statue. The goddess's statue was beautiful. The wings on her back were so intricately crafted that they seemed ready to move at any moment.

"Everyone, prepare for battle. And watch the statues."

I gripped the hilt of my greatsword tightly and raised it.

"Paladin-isiyeo?! What are you doing right now...?!"

The sword tip pierces the goddess's chest precisely. The statue didn't break. Instead of stone fragments, blood splattered.

"Kaaaaaaaahk!"

The goddess's statue screams. The angel's wings turn into demonic ones and flap, and the beautiful face turns into that of a wrinkled monster.

Gargoyle.

The demon of the statue.

"Paladin! You hateful dog of the goddess!"

The other statues lined up in the chapel rise up.

The paladins, realizing the situation, all drew their swords and aimed them at the demons.

"You filthy demons! How dare you hide in a place like this!"

"We cannot forgive you."

"We will kill you all!"

The paladins began to battle the gargoyles, venting their anger. I focused on the boss gargoyle in front of me. The bastard in front of me was the most dangerous. He was a bastard that I could only attempt to catch when I was at least level 30.

But the situation is different now. The surprise attack was completely successful.

,Lightning!'

Pajijijijik!

Golden lightning flows along the sword. The gargoyle, who was trying to escape by flapping its wings, screamed and staggered. I grabbed the bastard's shoulder and put strength into my sword. I have no intention of pulling out my sword until he dies. The moment I pull out my sword, he will surely run away. And after healing his wounds, he will attack again.

"Die here!"

"Paladin! I will kill you before you grow! Coming to kill me with only those eyes is your mistake and your arrogance!"

The gargoyle grabbed my armor and flew into the air.
