I stepped into the mansion.

Wheeeeeee!

A dagger came flying straight at my face. I snatched it out of the air with one hand. The dagger caught in my grip wouldn't stop vibrating—wrrrrr—like a small animal thrashing to break free.

Kkiiiiiiiik.

The aura radiating off the blade in my hand screeched against Stormbreaker's armor. Even if it couldn't hurt me right now, there was no denying this was a serious piece of hardware.

『Freyr's Dagger

Rank: S

An autonomous dagger.

Contains a fragment of ego.』

'So it really is an S-rank item. No way a conglomerate chairman got his hands on something like this through normal channels. Anything S-rank or above is damn near impossible to obtain even with money.'

It was clear Mok Tae-sun had acquired it back when he was still active as a hero.

'A dagger that fights on its own if you just leave it alone, huh. Tempting.'

Should I just toss it into the inventory like this?

[This item has an owner.]

[Would you like to use Ownership Seizure for 1,200 points?]

That was the inventory's condition. I couldn't just shove things in there that weren't mine.

'The way to gain ownership is either kill the owner, or use points to seize it. Spending points feels like a waste... I just need to kill Du Seok-man, the owner. In this state... hm?'

Wuuuuuuuuung!

The dagger's power surged even stronger.

-How long are you going to keep holding onto it? This dagger's power keeps growing. At this rate it's going to slice clean through your hand!

If I let go, it would go berserk. Judging by its behavior, it was getting stronger by the second.

'Annoying.'

I furrowed my brow.

The dagger's raw power was one thing, but I could feel the ego inside it rejecting me.

I could force it into submission if I wanted to. It would just take some time.

'For something called a fragment of ego, this ego's pretty damn strong. Don't like it.'

A weapon should know its place and shut the hell up.

"You're out."

Bzzzzzzzt!

The effects of Heaven's Heart hadn't worn off yet. Black lightning erupted from my grip. I concentrated it and crushed Freyr's Dagger in my fist.

KWAJIK!

The shattered dagger clattered to the floor.

I walked into the living room. Du Seok-man was standing there, cold sweat dripping down his face in rivulets.

"C-Crimson Light! P-please spare me! No—please spare me, sir! I'll do anything! I'll give you all my wealth! Every last bit of it! Just please let me live! I'm begging you like this!"

THUD!

Both of Du Seok-man's knees slammed into the floor. He pathetically rubbed his palms together like a beggar.

"A conglomerate chairman can't even die with dignity? Your friend Mok Tae-sun went out relatively gracefully, at least."

"A-a civilian and a hero are different! I'm not an Awakened. I'm just a rich commoner...! I-I may have run with gangs in my youth, but I've repented! I donate every year!"

"You do it for tax deductions. Who doesn't know that."

[Heaven's Heart has been deactivated.]

As Heaven's Heart released, strength drained from my body. I'd taken damage in the fight with Mok Tae-sun—my abdomen was gouged open and some of my innards were sliced—but I was using lightning qi to maintain my physical state, so I wasn't in immediate danger. Even without using Full Recovery, killing Du Seok-man posed no problem whatsoever.

"P-please, just once—show mercy! I-I'm begging you like this!"

"Can't you beg for your life in a more original way?"

I clenched my fist. Crackle. Blue lightning coiled around it.

With trembling hands, Du Seok-man pulled a strange wooden totem from his coat. It was a dull purple color overall. The patterns carved into it resembled human entrails.

"Th-this is a totem cursed with a terrible hex! If you kill me now, the curse inside will fall upon you! I'm not joking! I swear!"

[Using Flash. Remaining stacks: 6]

I used Flash to close the distance instantly and snatched the totem from him.

『Tezcatlipoca's Vengeance

Rank: SS

Leaves a death curse upon whoever harms its owner.』

Tezcatlipoca.

An item bearing the name of a god. Even though I'd taken the object, its owner was still Du Seok-man.

"Freyr's Dagger too. You've got quite the collection of rare goods. Mok Tae-sun really was something, I'll give him that."

"A-as long as I'm alive, the curse will never activate."

"But if you kill yourself, it becomes meaningless, doesn't it?"

"What?"

"I'm saying I'll torture you while you try to kill yourself."

“…….”

Du Seok-man's face went pale blue.

I didn't even bother watching his expression—I just swung my fist at his head.

PUK!

His head burst open, and the remains splattered across the floor.

『Karma: Good +27.』

"Gives more karma than most demons."

Thunder Heaven Style—Electromagnetic Wave.

I spread my qi sense throughout the mansion's interior. I could feel hidden presences. Du Seok-man's and Mok Tae-sun's families.

'So even a piece of shit like him hid his family away.'

Finding them and killing them would be easy.

'But there's no karma in it, so I'll ignore them. Du Seok-man's collection should be in the third-floor study.'

I headed to the third-floor study. I opened the door—lavish even at a glance—and a spacious room opened up. Books lined one wall, and a display case holding various objects stood on the other.

『Magical Wooden Display Case

Rank: A

Items displayed within do not deteriorate.』

'Even the display case is an item.'

There were twenty-two items inside the case. All of them A-rank or above.

'Selling what's in here could buy a building in Gangnam.'

I quickly scanned the items on display. Even if their ranks were high, most of them weren't immediately useful to me. A few caught my eye.

『Silver Cup of Antidote

Rank: A

Fill with water and drink to cure poisoning.』

『Bow of Widow Heaven

Rank: S

Increases arrow speed.

Increases arrow power.

Grants homing capability to arrows.』

『Scale of Disease

Rank: A

Heals your illness by offering another's heart.』

『Flower of Defying Heaven

Rank: S

Grows by absorbing the blood of others.

When consumed, you become as young as the youth stored within the flower.

Currently stored youth: 17 years.』

More than half the items in this collection were related to self-preservation. I could feel Du Seok-man's narcissism radiating off the shelves.

'Lots of sinister shit here. Now I understand why he gave so much karma. He must have poured everything into the Flower of Defying Heaven. Seventeen years is a considerable amount, but for Du Seok-man it wouldn't have been enough.'

These items alone weren't enough to confirm Du Seok-man had personally killed anyone. Organs and blood can be bought with money.

I pulled a rolled-up parchment from the display case. I unfurled it. Golden patterns swirled across the surface in dizzying complexity. I couldn't make out a single word of its contents.

『Chronos Melody

Rank: SS

Contains the Chronos Melody.

Only a master musician can perform it.

Attempting to perform the Chronos Melody through shortcuts results in a curse.』

I couldn't get any detailed information.

'It's SS-rank, no less. Even without knowing the details, it's worth keeping.'

I briefly recalled the original story.

In the original, Du Seok-man wasn't actually a hostile NPC. Just a quest NPC with a bad reputation. Original Du Seok-man tried to obtain the Chronos Melody through shortcuts and received the curse of aging as payment. The only thing that could break that curse was exceptional magical music.

'At the original's point in the timeline, only late-game Clara could do it.'

After Clara completes the quest and obtains the Chronos Melody, she doesn't get entangled with Du Seok-man or Mok Tae-sun.

'The Academy's Savior was set primarily in the Academy, after all. Leaving the Academy was rare to begin with.'

I put the display case and everything in it into my inventory. Du Seok-man was dead, so there was no ownership issue.

Zzzzzzzzip!

I tore a spatial teleport scroll and moved to the Academy dormitory.

[Using Full Recovery.]

'Heh heh. Everything went according to plan. Now all that's left is for Clara to master the Chronos Melody and resurrect Kang Ji-young. ...She won't fail, right?'

I brushed off the slight unease immediately. If she fails, I'll just find another way.

[Some of Du Seok-man's collection items are ominous and dangerous. Disposing of them seems wise.]

Kang Ji-young nagged as always.

After quickly clearing the dungeon, Seong Ha-ri, who happened to be at the Association, received a request from the Association President.

"Seong Ha-ri, ma'am. Chairman Du Seok-man of Cheongsong Group has requested support. It appears Crimson Light has attacked Chairman Du Seok-man's mansion."

Yoo Pil-seong, the Korean Hero Association President, spoke while carefully watching Seong Ha-ri's reaction.

"O-of course, there's no need for you to go personally, ma'am. Thanks to you, we have plenty of A-rank heroes with available capacity. We can send them as support."

"Forget it. I'll go. Don't do anything unnecessary."

"Yes, ma'am!"

Yoo Pil-seong nodded immediately. He was the type to respond lukewarmly even to the President's words, but in front of Seong Ha-ri, he couldn't maintain that insolent attitude. If he tried, he could genuinely end up dead—no joke. He knew Seong Ha-ri's temper well enough to tread carefully.

Crackle.

Blue currents sparked across Seong Ha-ri's entire body. She leapt out the window and streaked across the night sky. Du Seok-man's mansion wasn't in another city, so she arrived in an instant.

She watched Du Seok-man's mansion from the air, a short distance from the city.

Hundreds of boulders floated in the air above Du Seok-man's mansion.

'Thousand Rocks of the Heavy Heavens, was it. By the time I was active, he was already a half-retired S-rank hero.'

She'd met him in person a few times. But he hadn't been particularly impressive. When she met him, Mok Tae-sun was already well past his prime.

'Something feels off. And... Crimson Light.'

Black lightning extended from Crimson Light's body, shattering the hundreds of boulders floating in the air. Even Seong Ha-ri couldn't dismiss the power contained in that black lightning.

There wasn't a shred of hostility in her eyes as she watched Crimson Light.

'I've faced him once before, a long time ago.'

Truthfully, she'd already guessed Crimson Light's identity.

'A mother can't fail to recognize her own son. That mechanical suit is probably Machina's power.'

She didn't know why he was hiding his identity and operating as Crimson Light.

'Because he wants to do hero work? Simply because he's bored?'

The reason didn't really matter.

'He's grown so much in that short time. That's my Yujin.'

A smile curved at the corner of her lips.

To Seong Ha-ri, there was no being more important than Seong Yu-jin—not even herself.

She had her own sense of justice as a hero, but it didn't outweigh Seong Yu-jin.

If the world tried to kill Seong Yu-jin, Seong Ha-ri would kill the world first.

The mansion fell into chaos after Crimson Light departed. Seong Ha-ri slowly entered and cleaned things up.

"Cleaning up" amounted to using her power and authority to crush Cheongsong Clan so they couldn't pull any tricks. Thanks to that, the incident ended with a brief mention on the news.

The Korean public accepted it as Crimson Light doing what Crimson Light does.
