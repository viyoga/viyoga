"Recover."

Stormbreaker, which had been lying on the plain, flew quickly towards me. I injected mana into the Great Spear, changing its form into a sword.

It was a longsword with a striking blue blade. The weight was as heavy as when it was in the Great Spear state, and the blade was longer than the Hwaryeon Bido. As soon as I grabbed the hilt of the sword, I unleashed a blue aura.

"You have an interesting weapon!"

Lucman rushed towards me, as if creating an explosion with his legs. He picked up a spear along the way and swung the shaft as he approached me.

Realizing the difference in skill from the golden aura that enveloped the spear in just 0.5 seconds, I swung my sword upwards to deflect the spear.

"That's not Purrcus's swordsmanship."

"...You recognize it right away?"

"How could I not recognize it when I've seen that swordsmanship so many times? It's different from the second son's swordsmanship as well. What kind of swordsmanship it is... I'll know if I keep watching."

Lucman's spear moves. Perhaps out of consideration for me, it wasn't difficult to follow its speed with my eyes. The problem was the power behind the spear.

Kwaaaang!

"Keuk!"

Just blocking it once head-on pushed my body back about 5cm. Both hands holding the sword were numb. The power behind the spear was no joke.

Kwaang! Kwang! Kwang!

A shockwave occurred each time the sword and spear collided.

I can't win. I knew that from the start. I've barely reached the upper level of Aura Expert. It was impossible to beat Lucman, who was at the Aura Master level, from the beginning.

'But I can't just lose helplessly. Lucman wouldn't spread strange rumors either...'

I used Yeongcheon Style.

I switched to the Yeongcheon Style breathing technique and stepped with the Yeongcheon Style footwork. It's a bit awkward because I'm using a different sword than the Hwaryeon Bido, but there's no problem.

Yeongcheon Style (影天流) Lightning Snake (雷蛇).

The sword writhed like a black lightning bolt, aiming for Lucman's right shoulder.

Lucman kicked off the ground and instantly widened the distance. I stuck close to him.

Yeongcheon Style (影天流) Azure Stream (碧溪)

Aiming for the head.

"Hmm. Is the second son's swordsmanship the Plum Blossom Sword Art? It's a swordsmanship as mysterious as that."

Azure Stream didn't work, as expected. He simply tilted his head slightly, easily avoiding my attack.

Yeongcheon Style (影天流) Lightning Flash (雷光).

I unleashed the fastest sword strike in Yeongcheon Style. But this time too, it was blocked. Lucman could see all of my attacks.

"Pick up the speed a little more.... Why are you dropping your sword?"

"I lost. My mana is bottoming out, and my hands hurt."

"...You can still swing your sword, can't you?"

"Even if I swing it, I'll lose. Wouldn't it be a loss for me if I got injured?"

"Are you afraid of getting injured? You have no grit."

"Say what you want, I have no intention of continuing."

There was nothing more for me to gain by doing more than this. Experience with a great spear master? I had already felt that experience enough through the battles so far, and it was an experience I could feel elsewhere.

When I lost my will to fight, he glared at me, then sighed and released the strength in his hand holding the Great Spear.

"Hoo. You've spoiled the fun. Did you know? All of your brothers fought to their limits. The eldest even fainted. Unlike your brothers, you're not a warrior. The feeling of your swordsmanship also resembles that of assassins."

"Ah. So my brothers showed their bottom to someone who they might have a relationship with in the future."

"......You're an absurd fellow."

Maids approached me. Each of them held a towel and wiped my sweaty body. If I had continued, the towel would have been stained with red liquid.

"So you're a womanizer. You're so different from your father and brothers."

"Are you jealous?"

"......I wonder how someone like you was born into the Purrcus family."

"There doesn't need to be a great reason for someone to be born."

"You don't want to lose in a single word. I heard you have a congenital heart disease. Is your heart disease okay?"

"I haven't had a seizure recently, and the healer says it's hard to find even traces of heart disease. Marquis Petras-kakka. Now that the work is done, may I ask you something?"

"Hmm. What are you thinking of asking?"

"My evaluation. I fought as you wished, Marquis-kakka. So I deserve to hear your evaluation."

Lucman stared at me intently, then shook his head.

"You... I don't know."

"Huh?"

"Your individual martial prowess is mediocre. It's unique, but that's all. But I don't think that's all there is to it. You have wealth, and you probably have other powers as well. I think you're the most dangerous of the Purrcus family's children."

"......Is that a good evaluation?"

"It's not a bad evaluation."

I gave a troubled smile. If that evaluation turns into rumors and spreads throughout the country, my reputation will definitely drop.

"If you're not planning to leave right away, why don't you stay in my territory for a while?"

It was a polite offer.

My true feelings were that I wanted him to get the hell out of my territory right now. It wasn't welcome to have a difficult guest come.

"I'll stay for a few days. Of course, I'm not planning to stay for free. You seem interested in spears, so I'll teach you the basics of spear techniques."

"There's no need to go that far...."

"There's no need to refuse."

"......."

It didn't seem like he would easily back down no matter what I said. I had no choice but to guide him to the detached house that I had prepared for guests.

Lucman toured the Tebrah territory.

Eugene Purrcus was running the territory in a way he had never heard of before.

'Dividing the citizens into grades.... How can you have such a way of thinking?'

Citizen Grading System.

The commoners were divided into five grades. The grades were not ranks, but from the moment they were assigned a grade, they were ranks.

The higher the grade, the more benefits were given. In addition, it was commonplace for commoners of higher grades to look down on commoners of lower grades, and young women wanted to marry men of higher grades.

'The commoners in this city are desperate to raise their grades, to maintain their grades. It seems much harsher than other places.'

But there were no people fleeing to other cities. Rather, commoners were coming in from the outside. Tebrah was very innovative as it was home to the Korea Merchant Guild. It was overflowing with all sorts of strange things, and the civilization itself seemed different compared to other cities.

Most of the commoners seemed satisfied with this convenient city.

"Baron Eugene Purrcus."

"Yes, Marquis-kakka."

"Why did you implement this citizen grading system? I can't understand it."

"Marquis-kakka. Just as not all nobles are the same, not all commoners are the same. I think capable commoners should be treated better than incompetent commoners. Commoners work more passionately because they want to raise their grades and maintain their grades. As you can see from other territories, this city is probably the only one with commoners working this hard."

"Merchants, commoners, and mercenaries are giving you bribes."

"They respect me and give me gifts. As the lord, how can I refuse gifts that contain their hearts?"

"I, as an outsider, am not qualified to interfere in this city. But as a guest, let me give you some advice... Someday, something big will happen."

Lucman had seen many things as he had lived for quite a long time. Nobles who exploited their territory's people and filled their own bellies did not have good endings. They were abandoned by their children, lost everything by being deceived by someone, or were attacked by assassins.

"It's okay. This city is under my feet."

Eugene said calmly without changing his expression.

Lucman revised his evaluation of Eugene.

At first, he thought he was close to a merchant. But looking at that appearance, he was a noble. He was a noble comparable to the eldest son, Jent.

"What are you going to do about the war?"

"Pardon?"

"I'm talking about the war that's getting closer and closer. Are you still thinking of using money to support the army from behind? War is done by people. War cannot be established without weapons and food. Count Purrcus will also recognize the rear support using money as a merit."

"Even if I support supplies, I can't just stay behind. Because the attention is less than fighting head-on. I will make an undeniable contribution and become the master of Purrcus."

"I see."

Lucman taught Eugene basic spear techniques for several hours every day. But it wasn't easy.

"You have no talent for spear techniques at all. A passing soldier would handle a spear better than you! Don't just think about handling it with strength! Swing the spear more gently!"

"I told you I'm not interested in spear techniques. I like spear throwing better."

"Rumors that I'm teaching you spear techniques have already spread throughout the kingdom! My reputation is at stake with you! I'll make sure to engrave at least the basics in you! And in the future, if anyone asks about spear techniques, say you only learned the basics from me!"

Lucman stayed in Tebrah City for a month, much longer than planned. It was because he had too little talent. If he wasn't an Aura Expert, he might have had to stay for years.

'Hmm. Now that I'm about to leave the city, it feels like it'll be inconvenient. There aren't many poor people, so the security is good, and the food suits my taste.'
