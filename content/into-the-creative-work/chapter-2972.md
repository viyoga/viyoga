「The Red Dragon has been subjugated.」

「Mount Hua Island has been conquered.」

The notification of Mount Hua Island’s conquest completion would be announced to everyone in Atlantis. It was so in the Original Work. Even if it weren’t, there was no way they wouldn’t know about Mount Hua Island’s conquest completion.

‘The volcanic ash vanished all at once.’

The volcanic crater became quiet as if by a lie. It had become a dormant volcano in an instant. The volcanic ash that had drifted through the air and obscured the sky was nowhere to be seen.

The damage caused by Mount Hua Island was normalized in one go.

「You have defeated the Red Dragon and completed the conquest of Mount Hua Island. You have saved Atlantis from destruction! A wondrous Achievement!」

「You acquire the Title, Savior of Atlantis.」

「All stats increase by 5.」

A smile graced his lips. It was not common for all stats to increase by 5 at once. The conquest of Mount Hua Island was possible because the fate of Atlantis hung in the balance.

Of course, the rewards didn’t end there.

「You have subjugated the Red Dragon.」

「You acquire Domination over all areas of the Mount Hua Island region.」

「The names and characteristics of some areas within the Mount Hua Island region will be changed.」

「As the rightful Dominator of Mount Hua Island, you can banish all Outsiders within the Mount Hua Island region.」

「As the Dominator of the Mount Hua Island region, you acquire 40,000,000 AP every 30 days.」

‘Keke. Now, I won’t have to suffer from a lack of AP. Well, most of my AP will still go towards spatial movement scrolls, though.’

The convenience of spatial movement scrolls goes without saying. The problem is that the supply is woefully insufficient compared to the demand.

‘Perhaps because I’ve been sweeping up as many spatial movement scrolls as possible whenever they appear, the market price has skyrocketed.’

These days, spatial movement scrolls were hard to come by even with Money.

‘To acquire them leisurely… I might not be able to get them after Atlantis ends. So, I should get as many as I can while I still can.’

Despite being a special Item, spatial movement scrolls could be used without restriction in other worlds.

Even in reality, one could move to the Tattoo world with spatial movement scrolls. In other words, as long as the coordinates were successfully set and the dimensions were connected, there was no particular distance Limit. It would be extremely useful even in the SF worlds I might visit later.

Currently, thousands of spatial movement scrolls are in my Inventory. Someone might say it’s more than enough for a lifetime. Since I don’t use spatial movement scrolls every day, that could be true.

‘My lifetime isn’t an ordinary lifetime. Even millions wouldn’t be enough for me.’

「You can enter the 8,870th sector, Throne of Apocalypse.」

The 8,870th sector is a hidden 71st sector, granted only after completing the conquest of Mount Hua Island.

If I wish to enter the 8,870th sector from Mount Hua Island, I will be able to.

‘The Mount Hua Island conquest reward is there. Should I collect the spoils first?’

He collected the two spoils lying where the Red Dragon had been.

「Horn of Sin

A dragon’s Horn imbued with the power of Sin.

Rank: SS」

「Dragon Heart

A dragon’s Heart imbued with powerful strength.

Rank: SS」

Both are material Items.

For reference, dragon byproducts are top-tier material Items. The most common use is equipment crafting, and they can also be used as Magic materials.

‘They’d fetch a good price if I sold them. But I’m not short on Money, so I have no intention of selling them.’

For now, he put them into his Inventory.

Next, before entering the hidden sector, he spoke to the System.

“System. Banish everyone within Mount Hua Island without exception.”

「The Dominator of Mount Hua Island exercises his rightful Authority.」

「All Outsiders within Mount Hua Island are banished.」

The other Legion members who had been sprawled near him vanished all at once. The banishment was carried out immediately.

“Are the Monsters on Mount Hua Island impossible to banish?”

「Impossible.」

He hadn’t particularly expected it.

“I will enter the hidden sector.”

「Entering the 8,870th sector, Throne of Apocalypse.」

His vision suddenly shifted, and he found himself indoors.

It wasn’t dark, as gems embedded in the ceiling emitted light. Gold, silver, and treasures were piled high all around. The walls were ornate, and at its center lay a lavish black Throne. A black crown rested upon the Throne.

He paid no mind to anything else and strode purposefully towards the Throne. If he rummaged through those, he would likely find quite a few useful Items. However, the most valuable thing here was that crown.

He picked up the black crown. It was heavier than it looked.

「Crown of Apocalypse

It is bound to the first person who wears the crown.

The crown can be used for 7 minutes. After one use, the crown can be worn again after 7 days.

While wearing the crown, your status is fixed and does not change.

While wearing the crown, you can use the Authority of — once.

Rank: SSS」

While wearing the crown for 7 minutes, you become Invincible. The problem is that because your status is fixed, you cannot move. In other words, you become Invincible, immune to attacks, but simultaneously unable to attack.

He lifted the crown and placed it on his Head.

The black crown was attached to his Head only for a moment. The black crown floated gently above his Head, emitting a subtle light like a halo.

「The Crown of Apocalypse is bound.」

‘Bound, huh. A pity.’

The binding is likely related to the Atlantis System. He probably wouldn’t be able to use it in other worlds without the Atlantis System.

‘Even if there were no binding function, there’s a high chance I couldn’t use it in other worlds. With this level of ability, it would likely require System assistance to be used.’

「You can choose one of the Seven Sins and use its ability.」

「The chosen Sin cannot be changed.」

「Choose one: Pride, Greed, Gluttony, Envy, Sloth, Lust, or Wrath.」

「Pride steals the target’s Traits for 7 hours.」

「Greed steals the target’s Items for 7 hours.」

「Gluttony steals the target’s memories for 7 hours.」

「Envy steals the target’s Skills for 7 hours.」

「Sloth increases the duration for which the crown can be worn.」

「Lust Domination the body of the opposite sex for 7 hours.」

「Wrath reduces the duration for which the crown can be worn, but allows movement.」

Kang Myungjin of the Original Work used the Authority of Wrath without much Worry.

‘Instead of the crown’s duration being reduced to 1 minute, I would be able to attack enemies while Invincible.’

He would be able to unilaterally beat enemies while Invincible. Within Atlantis, it could be called a 1-minute Cheat Key.

‘It wasn’t much help when fighting a true God in the very late game.’

He didn’t need Wrath. Honestly, there were few enemies in Atlantis strong enough to warrant using the Authority of Wrath.

‘Keke. Other than Wrath… it’s decided.’

「Lust is chosen.」

「The power of Lust imbues the Crown of Apocalypse.」

The power to Dominate the body of the opposite sex, even if only for 7 hours.

As the Guardian of Pussy, it was an irresistible Authority.

‘The cooldown is one week. The sparring cycle with Joo Seohyun is also one week. If I only have the Authority of Lust… I will never lose to Joo Seohyun.’

No matter how strong Joo Seohyun becomes, she will never be able to escape my grasp. At least, not as long as she has no means to counter the Authority of Lust.

‘Keke. Thanks to the effect of the Guardian of Pussy, I already receive a bonus when dealing with women. Now that I have the Authority of Lust, I can’t lose against women.’

The moment he obtained the crown. All women in Atlantis became unable to defeat him.

He sat on the Throne with a joyful Heart.

「You have sat on the Throne of Apocalypse.」

「You possess Domination over all areas of Mount Hua Island. You can manage Mount Hua Island by consuming AP.」

「You can spread volcanic ash by consuming AP.」

「You can control Mount Hua Island by consuming AP.」

「You can adjust the laws of the Mount Hua Island region by consuming AP.」

Administrator Authority that only one who Dominates all areas of Mount Hua Island can use.

Under the condition of sufficient AP, one can even turn Mount Hua Island into their own paradise on earth.

It was a function that Kang Myungjin of the Original Work could not use. That’s because he had to consider the Imperial family’s Hint and divided the Mount Hua Island region.

‘But I, the Demon King, don’t care about the Imperial family’s Hint.’

He wouldn’t spread volcanic ash. If he did, he would turn everyone in Atlantis into an enemy.

Even for him, that would be a tough task.

‘I’ll stick to the original plan.’

「Using 1,000,000 AP, you control Mount Hua Island. Additional AP will be consumed depending on the distance.」

The colossal Mount Hua Island, located at the center of the world, began to move in the sky.

It arrived above the Imperial family in the Empire’s capital. Since it didn’t move far, no additional AP was consumed.

“I can drop Mount Hua Island to the ground, right?”

「It is possible by consuming 10,000,000 AP.」

10,000,000 AP.

At first glance, it seemed obscenely expensive.

However, recalling that 40,000,000 AP would come in every 30 days through Mount Hua Island, it wasn’t that expensive.

“Haha. The Emperor will throw a Bullshit fit.”

But he wouldn’t be able to declare war on me. The moment he declared war, Mount Hua Island would plunge into the Imperial family.

The Emperor.

The Empire, with this Mount Hua Island incident as a catalyst, once again realized the dangers of Atlantis.

They would be busy dealing with the aftermath of the volcanic ash, and after that, they would focus more on conquering Atlantis than on war.

Even if they waged war with the Demonic Cult, it would be after the conquest of Atlantis was complete.
