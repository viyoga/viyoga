Shibata chased after me relentlessly as I left the hospital.

Luring Shibata was easy. That bastard was running after me without any suspicion.

,If I hadn't eaten the Blood Pill, I would've been caught.'

Thanks to the Blood Pill strengthening my physical abilities, I was maintaining a certain distance without getting caught. I glanced back to check.

Kung! Kung kung kung kung!

It wasn't that it was bad. I do that sometimes too.

,The only one who can control that bastard... is the Miko-nim?'

I looked around. Even though the villagers could chase me, they were too frightened by Shibata and were busy running away. Shibata must be famous even among the villagers.

I went into the mountains.

Kuoong! Kwang! Kung!

Shibata's speed decreased. The distance widened and that bastard lost me.

Shibata shouted. I could hear the sound of trees breaking and falling. It seemed like he was venting his anger on the innocent trees.

,He's here.'

,Now!'

Was it instinct? Shibata glanced up at me, then raised his left arm. The sword was blocked by that bastard's left wrist.

,Since it's come to this, I'll at least take his left arm...!'

,It's too big. Is this a human body? I don't know where his heart is.'

In a hurry, I aimed for that bastard's right arm.

I swung the sword with both hands. The sword cut off half of that bastard's right arm.

That bastard crushed my leg.

"Aaaaaaah! This!"

Shibata threw me away. My body rolled around like a soccer ball. Was I lucky? Or was I unlucky? I fell straight down the cliff without being stopped by a tree.

Thud.

My body hit the ground. Thanks to the effects of the Blood Pill, the pain was minimal, but my body wouldn't move and I lost my sight.

,Shit.'

It felt like I was floating for about 5 seconds. I must have fallen at least 20m. At this height, it's impossible for my body to be intact.

What's absurd is that I'm not dead yet. My body is a mess, but my mind is clear.

,It must be because of the Blood Pill.'

I deliberately didn't use Complete Recovery. I was planning to wait for Shibata to appear and check my corpse. I'll use Complete Recovery and counterattack the moment that bastard is careless.

,It's a perfect plan. Hurry up and show up.'

But even after several tens of seconds, that bastard didn't appear. He must have thought I was dead because I fell from a high place.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

[Remaining time of Death Resistance: 15 seconds]

[Using Complete Recovery.]

I used Complete Recovery because I couldn't fail the quest by dying.

I got up with my lightened body. The effects of the Blood Pill must have disappeared as well.

I looked up at the cliff. It was too dark to measure the exact height. I felt like it was more than 20m. Can I climb up the cliff with my current body and without any equipment?

,Since I can't be sure, I'll have to go around a bit.'

I lowered my head. Finding another way wasn't easy either.

Suddenly, I felt annoyed.

I couldn't kill Shibata. Could I have killed him if I had dealt with him more calmly?

"Ssibaal!! Shibata!! I'm going to kill you! Shibata, you son of a bitch!!"

I shouted as loud as I could.
