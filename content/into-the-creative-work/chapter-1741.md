Battles became easier after Young-hari joined.

The monsters that appeared couldn't withstand a single swing of her spear and were swept away. Compared to Young-hari, the monsters were too weak.

,This won't do.'

It's good that Young-hari joined. It became unbelievably easier. But because she was wiping them all out by herself, I wasn't gaining any experience.

,There are several places here with level restrictions, like the Forest of Bewilderment.'

To avoid being held back at crucial moments, I needed to level up. For that reason, I restrained Young-hari, and Seong-hari and I moved to catch monsters.

Needless to say, Seong-hari's condition might not have been as good as usual, but the experience she had accumulated over decades wasn't going to disappear. So, Seong-hari and I steadily killed monsters and gained experience.

,What are you doing? Can't you catch them any faster?'

Young-hari sneered.

It was a sneer directed at Seong-hari, who had just caught a monster.

A vein popped out on Seong-hari's forehead.

,I caught it at the best speed I could? It was clean and efficient. If other people had seen it, they would have been amazed by my technique. Well, what would you know, you only have brute strength.'

,I would have handled it twice as fast.'

,Faster isn't always better. Do you even know what technique is?'

,What you're doing looks like a bunch of cheap tricks to me. Why are you so stubborn about using a sword when you can't even use it properly?'

The words they exchanged were harsh.

I watched quietly. I was worried that I might get caught in the crossfire if I intervened, but it was quite fun to watch.

,Parallel world... well, I don't know if it's really a parallel world, but fighting yourself... isn't this like spitting on yourself?'

Frankly speaking, Seong-hari's reaction was a bit unexpected. She's lived almost twice as long as the younger Young-hari. I thought she would use her experience to handle it smoothly.

But what I saw was nothing like smoothness. Maybe it was because she was watching me, but she didn't use swear words like "son of a bitch," but she kept calling her "bitch" and "that bitch."

,Wait a minute.'

At my words, they stopped fighting.

I grabbed my sword and stared straight ahead. The space next to the large tree shimmered, and a translucent corpse appeared. It was a ghost.

It was the first time this type of monster had appeared. I looked up at the sky. The day was coming to an end.

,Oppa. Should I take care of it?'

Pajijik. Sparks flew from Young-hari's body. If it was her, she could kill a ghost in an instant.

,No, I'll take care of it. I want to try out the new skill I got earlier.'

Even though it doesn't look like it, my secondary job is Priest.

「Holy Power

Level: 1

Consumes mana to use holy power.」

,Holy Power.'

I activate the skill. A faint golden light settled on the blade. The ghost frowned as if it didn't like the Holy Power and opened its mouth wide. A screeching sound that seemed to tear metal struck my eardrums.

「You have heard the ghost's cry. Your life force is continuously being consumed.」

Thunder Sky Style (雷天流) Flying Tiger (飛虎).

I leap up and charge forward as if flying. The ghost reacted mechanically. As I approached, it retreated, creating distance. It was within my expectations. Without panicking, I moved my feet in the air. My foot kicked off the tree next to me. The direction changed, and I flew directly at the ghost.

The tip of the sword pierced into the ghost's translucent body. The sensation was weak, but it was there. It felt similar to cutting a slightly firm jelly. I moved the sword. As I fell, I sliced through the ghost's body. The ghost disappeared with a scream.

,Ajumma. Did you see that? That's what technique is. Oppa moved the sword exquisitely in the middle to slice the ghost.'

,I knew Yu-jin would do that. Ghost-type monsters are hard to cut with a sword... but he did it. He's my son after all.'

Their compliments poured out. I hadn't done anything particularly great, so I was a bit embarrassed.

Tuk.

Something fell at my feet.

,Is it a drop item?'

Thinking nothing of it, I picked up the dropped item.

It was a cat sculpture. It was small, about the size of my palm. The sculpture was somewhat crude. The sculptor's skill didn't seem very good. There were flaws here and there. The tip of the tail was broken.

「Cat Toy

A toy that someone is desperately looking for.」

As soon as I checked the item, I remembered the quest I received from the hunter. The keepsake of a dead child. This was definitely that toy.

,So, you get it by catching ghosts.'

I put the toy in my inventory.

,Mom, Hari-ya. Let's catch some more ghosts and then go back.'

It wasn't just me who received the quest, but Seong-hari as well. She also needed this toy.

• • •

It was 2 AM when we left the Forest of Bewilderment. We got lost in the middle, so it took longer. It would have taken even longer if Young-hari hadn't been there. Undead monsters appeared more frequently at night, and thanks to Young-hari's power, we were able to handle them without difficulty.

We went to find the hunter first.

,Yu-jin-ah. It's 2 AM right now. Let's go when it's light out. Going now is too much of a nuisance.'

I shook my head at Seong-hari's words.

,Mom. The hunter is an NPC. He's not a human like us. He's just data. Just data.'

,I know that, but...'

Seong-hari gave a bitter smile. I had repeatedly told her that NPCs weren't human over the past few days, but Seong-hari couldn't treat NPCs carelessly.

Knock knock knock!

I pounded on the hunter's door with my fist. I heard a stirring inside. The hunter asked with a sleepy voice from behind the door.

,Who is it?'

,It's me. I brought the toy of your dead child.'

The door burst open. The hunter looked at the cat toy in my hand. His eyes trembled. He closed his eyes and repeatedly sighed before saying.

,...That's definitely his toy. Thank you.'

,Give me the reward.'

,I will. I have weapons that I use. Take one of them.'

,Give me a sword.'

「Hunter's Sword

A sword used by the hunter.

Attack Power +30

Attack Speed +5%

Status Ailment Bleeding Occurrence Rate +3%」

The hunter's sword looked ordinary on the outside. But the stats were satisfactory. It was the best item I had right now.

Seong-hari also handed the toy to the hunter.

,...That's definitely his toy. Thank you.'

The hunter reacted the same way he did when he received the toy from me. It was the limitation of the NPC.

,I'll take a spear.'

,A spear? Don't you use a sword?'

The hunter said, looking at the sword Seong-hari had on her waist. Since the weapon she used was different, he was asking to confirm.

,I'm better with a spear originally.'

Seong-hari said. It sounded like she was saying it to be conscious of Young-hari. Young-hari next to me smirked.

,What's going to change if you hold a spear? You can't even throw it properly with that strength.'

I'm sorry to Seong-hari, but I agreed with Young-hari's words.

Of course, I'm not doubting Seong-hari's spearmanship. In fact, her spearmanship has surpassed the level of a master.

,But the spearmanship I've seen from Seong-hari so far is...'

It was a spearmanship that relied on strength rather than technique. I wondered if Seong-hari, whose strength had weakened due to the strengthening of the Spirit King's curse for some reason, would be able to exert her proper strength.

,But it's still better than using a sword.'

Anyway, it was good that our combat power had increased.

We decided to complete the other quests in the morning. The reason was that Arinna's house was far from here.

We headed to a nearby inn. As usual, we booked one room.

,Yu-jin-ah. Wouldn't it be better to book three rooms?'

Seong-hari said, being conscious of Young-hari.

,Hmm. Should we?'

Young-hari stood with her arms crossed, as if she didn't care.

,...No. I was wrong. It's easier to keep an eye on you if we book the same room.'

,Keep an eye on? Does Ajumma keep an eye on her son? That's terrible.'

,I'm not keeping an eye on my son, but you. Who knows what you'll do to my son?!'

,Do you think I can't do anything if Ajumma is watching?!'

I stepped between them.

,We booked a big room, so let's not fight.'

What I wanted was a 3P. With two Seong-haris in front of me, it was a natural desire. The problem was that their relationship was no different from enemies.

,Even if I force a 3P in this state, it will only create resistance.'

There was a high possibility that it wouldn't be the threesome I wanted because they would be too busy keeping each other in check. It would be like turning Gukbap into separate soup and rice.

,If I try to do a 3P now, they'll only feel competitive.'

Seong-hari and Young-hari needed to become somewhat closer.

,As time passes, they'll develop some kind of attachment, even if it's hate.'

Because they are themselves.

We skip showering and prepare to sleep. Since this world is virtual reality, there was no need to shower. Even if my body gets dirty, I don't need to wash it, I can just shake it off and it becomes clean.

,Wait a minute, I'll be right back.'

Seong-hari went outside. This was unusual, so I tilted my head and followed her.

Seong-hari took out the hunter's spear in front of the inn and held it in her hand. She managed her breathing and took a stance. And then she swung the spear.

,Ah. She's finding her senses.'

She hadn't held a spear for almost 50 days. She was regaining her senses by swinging the spear like this.

The spear blade cutting through the air, the fluttering hair, the vitality felt from her firm body. Seong-hari's body was very alluring right now.

Not only that. I can see the imaginary enemy Seong-hari is facing. The imaginary enemy was herself.

,I don't know much about spearmanship, but I can tell it's threatening. She's focusing on technique rather than strength. It's Ranranru... no, it's Rannachar.'

Twisting, pulling, stabbing.

The basics and everything of spearmanship.

Seong-hari's Rannachar was as natural as flowing water.

Seueuk.

Young-hari approached next to me. She watched Seong-hari's spearmanship with her arms crossed. I thought she would say something, but she kept her mouth shut and only watched Seong-hari's spear.

,What do you think?'

,About what?'

,Mom's spearmanship.'

,...Looking at the technique itself, it's amazing. Well, it's natural since there are 20 years of time that I don't know about.'

It was a simple impression. There was no mockery or sneering. Surprisingly, Young-hari was acknowledging Seong-hari's technique.

,But I'll still win.'
