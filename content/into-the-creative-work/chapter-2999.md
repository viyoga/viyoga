Cheongsong Clan and the Cheongsong Group conglomerate.

In this world, hero clans joining hands with major corporations was a common thing.

Even in reality, guilds were often tangled up with corporations. A guild could itself be called a company. The clans of this world were no different.

In other words, the Cheongsong Clan could be called a subsidiary of the Cheongsong Group. Along the lines of Cheongsong Electronics, Cheongsong Construction, Cheongsong Chemical.

The Cheongsong Group was one of South Korea's major corporations. Though among the big players, it was on the ambiguous side. When MZ-generation Koreans thought of major corporations, Cheongsong wasn't among the names that immediately came to mind. At best, hearing the name Cheongsong, people reacted with a vague "Oh right, there's a nasty conglomerate like that too."

'About thirty years ago, they say it was a major corporation you could count on one hand.'

Now, the Cheongsong Clan had higher recognition than the Cheongsong Group. In South Korea, the Cheongsong Clan was one of the top ten hero clans you could count on one hand.

'A retired S-rank Hunter from the previous generation is with the Cheongsong Clan.'

Not as clan master, but working as a training instructor.

Mok Tae-sun, once called the Thousand Crags of the Double Heaven.

[Are you really going to kill the Cheongsong Clan—no, the chairman of the Cheongsong Group?]

Kang Ji-young, visible only to my eyes, asked even though she already knew my answer. She wanted me to take it back.

"I've already made my decision. And Du Seok-man, the chairman of the Cheongsong Group, is a bastard who deserves to die anyway."

The Cheongsong Group had once been a gangster corporation. A company that dabbled in all sorts of illegal dealings, but because of Mok Tae-sun, they never received proper punishment. These days, given the times, they didn't pull gangster shit anymore, but a few years back there'd been a controversy over workplace abuse.

[I've said this several times—you have no right to judge criminals. And your purpose isn't even judgment.]

"Whatever. To the world, I'm a dark hero."

[...Mok Tae-sun is at Chairman Du Seok-man's side. They're in-laws, and they live together. Chairman Du Seok-man has nearly been assassinated several times in the past, and because of that, he's a man who's excessively paranoid about his own safety. The Cheongsong Clan headquarters is right near the chairman's residence. If you cause a ruckus, the Cheongsong Clan will step in immediately. You might have to face a former S-rank hero and A-rank heroes all by yourself. Even if your power rivals S-rank... can you handle them as Jeok-gwang?]

"Now I see—you were worrying about me. Unnecessary worry. If I thought I'd lose, I wouldn't have started. Mok Tae-sun's days as an active S-rank were thirty years ago. Now he's just a washed-up old man."

[...I met Mok Tae-sun a few years ago. He was hale. Even retired from hero work, he's a human who never skipped his own training, kept at it consistently.]

"Oh. So he's S-rank level? As strong as you or my mom?"

[The current Ha-ri is so strong that comparing her to other S-ranks is itself an insult. If I compared him to myself... I'm stronger. If we fought ten times, I'd win nine of them.]

"Pretty good, for an assessment of a retired old man."

[That's how much the name S-rank doesn't come lightly.]

The reason Cheongsong Group Chairman Du Seok-man could commit all manner of crimes and live fat and happy was because of Mok Tae-sun's existence.

[Ask Sung Ha-ri for help. It's a job that could be finished easily and simply.]

"No."

Kill and take. And as a bonus, a chance to accumulate karma.

"I can do it, so there's no reason not to."

Zzzzzzzt!

I tore the spatial teleportation scroll.

• • •

I appeared atop a building. The night sky's wind blew in, but it didn't reach my body, clad in Storm Breaker in its armor form.

"Machina."

Machina appeared in the air.

A child's form, 60cm tall, with black hair and a dress.

"Can't we do it tomorrow? I'm tired today."

Machina, whoever she took after, was lazy as hell.

"Tch. You don't want a spirit orb?"

"Fine. I'll do it, okay."

Machina possessed Storm Breaker. The plain armor shifted into something more refined. It gave off a strong mechanical-armor feel. I fused the mana cube into the armor and used it as a power source.

-Hmm? Something feels more comfortable than before.

I looked down at the buildings below. A five-story mansion and a concrete building larger than it. Respectively, the Cheongsong Group chairman's home and the Cheongsong Clan headquarters.

Thunder Heaven Style—Electromagnetic Wave.

I scattered electromagnetic waves. Information from all directions poured into my head. Except for Chairman Du Seok-man's mansion and the Cheongsong Clan headquarters in my sight.

'A barrier, huh. Not Academy-grade, but a barrier far too extravagant for a private residence and a single individual.'

The upkeep cost must be no joke. If this were a foreign country, maybe. But this level of barrier in safe South Korea?

'Sneaking in and finishing it quietly won't be possible.'

With enough time and effort, sneaking into that barrier would be possible. I could just put Shim Eun-ji, the master of barrier destruction, to work.

'No time for that.'

I ran toward Chairman Du Seok-man's house. My body didn't fall to the ground.

Thunder Heaven Style—Void Path.

Using thunder qi, I created footholds in empty air and stood on them.

Clack.

The back of the armor opened, revealing thrusters. I drew mana from the fused mana cube and used it as fuel for the thrusters.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art—Vajra Demon Dragon.

I wrapped the Heavenly Demon qi of the Heavenly Demon Divine Art around my body, raising my physical durability to its limit. The protective qi coiling around my entire body was a bonus.

"Machina. Ready?"

-If I said I wasn't ready, would you stop?

"No. I do what I have to do. More than anything, those bastards have already noticed."

I was pouring out fierce Heavenly Demon qi in streams through the air. Any A-rank hero would have noticed my presence immediately, and then some. Didn't notice? That bastard doesn't deserve A-rank.

"I'm going."

I become a single bullet and plummet toward the ground.

In the blink of an eye, I arrived above the mansion and collided with the barrier. KWAAANG! Cracks spider across the shimmering barrier as it strains to stop my descent. The shockwave from the impact was absorbed by Stormbreaker before it could even reach my body. Inside the armor, I was as calm as if I were lying on a bed.

BUUUUUUUUUUUUM!

The thrusters on my back roar and spew massive flames.

-Let's gooooo!!

Krrrrk, krrrrk!

More cracks spread across the barrier. It teeters on the edge, looking like it could shatter with just a little more pressure. But even after ten seconds, the barrier holds, stubbornly enduring.

I raised my right fist. A portion of the Heavenly Demon Qi coiling around my body gathered into my knuckles.

Heavenly Demon Divine Art — Adamantine Demon Dragon, Reversal.

I swung my fist, channeling the accumulated damage stored within the Adamantine Demon Dragon into the strike.

SHATTER!!!

The barrier exploded into pieces.

Riding the thrusters' force, I dropped into the mansion's courtyard.

KWAAAAAANG!

A massive crater erupted in the courtyard with a thunderous boom. From its center, I slowly rose to my feet.

'My legs are stiff. Turns out landing is trickier than smashing the barrier.'

Rings of light drifted gently down around me. They were fragments of the shattered barrier. Made of mana, they glowed as if burning slowly before fading away. It was a spectacle so fantastical that even I found my gaze drawn to it for a moment.

In that brief window, heroes poured out from the Cheongsong Clan headquarters. Dozens of them surrounded me.

I swept my eyes over them once, then looked straight ahead. Two old men were walking toward me.

The pig-like man with layers of fat packed onto his belly was Du Seokman, chairman of the Cheongsong Group. He glared at me with a face like a demon, looking ready to kill.

"Jeokgwang…! You came to kill me?! I knew you'd come!"

He squeals like a pig. A character I already disliked in the original work was even more insufferable in reality.

I ignored him and shifted my gaze to the old man beside him.

Unlike Du Seokman, this white-haired man was pure muscle from head to toe. This was Mok Taesun, a former S-rank hero.

"A criminal who judges evil. I knew that once I learned of your existence, we would face each other like this someday. I just didn't know it would be today."

"If you knew, you should have run."

"Is running the end of it?"

"No. I would have chased you to the ends of the earth and killed you."

Killing him would guarantee karma. That was reason enough to pursue.

Mok Taesun nodded once, then spoke to the heroes surrounding me.

"All of you, stand down. I will handle this one myself."

"Training Instructor, sir. He's dangerous. We need to combine our strength to eliminate him."

"Clan Master. This is an old man's final request. Won't you grant it?"

"……Understood."

The heroes stepped back. But not far. Their wariness toward me was thick in the air.

'If things go south, they'll all jump in. How annoying. My plan to wipe them all out with a self-destruct when they swarmed me is ruined.'

Had Mok Taesun seen through my strategy?

"Mok Taesun! What do you think you're doing?! We should be pooling our strength to kill that bastard right now—even that wouldn't be enough!!"

"Du Seokman. You're normally cold-headed, yet you lose all reason when true crisis hits. Frankly, haven't we done plenty of things we can't be proud of? Living in wealth and glory built on the bloody tears squeezed from thousands of people…. The time has come to pay the price."

"Price? Mok Taesun! Have you lost your mind?! You're not thinking of siding with that bastard, are you?!"

"Of course not. I have to think of our grandchildren. I will kill him. These hands are already stained…. A little more blood won't change anything. You go inside."

"Grrrr…. Taesun. I'll trust only you!"

Du Seokman went into the mansion.

That bastard doesn't trust Mok Taesun. He'll probably contact the Hero Association directly. I'd bet on it.

Mok Taesun walked forward and spoke.

"Should I thank you for waiting?"

Kwoong. Kwoong. Kwoong.

The ground trembles with his every step. The earth itself resonates with him. I narrowed my eyes and checked Mok Taesun's status window.

『Name: Mok Taesun

Strength: A+ Endurance: A+ Agility: A- Durability: S+ Mana: A+

Trait: King of Rock (S+)

Skills: Rock Manipulation (S), Body Enhancement (A+), Impact Reflection (S-)

Trust: 13』

His stats were impressive enough that it was hard to believe he was old.

"No need for thanks. I've been curious about the power of an S-rank hero."

"I see. For your information, I'm betting my life."

Mok Taesun put something black into his mouth and swallowed it.

He stomped the ground. In less than a second, he closed the distance and drove his fist toward my head.

[Using Flash Step. Remaining stacks: 11]

I barely dodged it.

If it hadn't been for that split second, I wouldn't have avoided it—I'd have taken the hit square.

'There's no way this is just A-rank instantaneous speed. Even with a skill enhancement ability, it's impossible.'

I quickly put distance between myself and Mok Tae-sun, then checked his status window once more.

『Name: Mok Tae-sun

Strength: S+ Stamina: SS Agility: SS- Durability: SS+ Mana: S+

Trait: Rock King (S+)

Skills: Rock Manipulation (S), Body Enhancement (A+), Impact Reflection (S-)

Trust: 13』

For a moment I was so dumbfounded I had to ask him.

"What the hell did you eat? Give me some too."
