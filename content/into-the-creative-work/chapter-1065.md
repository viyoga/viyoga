「You have collected 15,000 life points.」

After an hour in a bloody place, I managed to collect 15,000 life points. Quite a few monsters were drawn by the smell of human blood.

「You will receive 30,000 AP as an achievement reward.」

'30,000 AP isn't a bad reward.'

It's a shame my stats didn't go up, but there's a lot I can do with 30,000 AP.

'Has time passed? The monsters drawn to the smell of blood have noticeably decreased.'

You could say all the ones nearby came and died. There was no more merit here. I looked around and moved south. There's no big reason. I moved on instinct.

'My luck stat is high, so things will work out on their own.'

This is just rough information in the setting book, so I don't know the details either. But I was confident that I wouldn't lose no matter what monster popped out. I am strong now.

I heard the sound of water. I increased the speed of my steps.

There was a wide, blue lake. It wasn't an ordinary lake. Palm trees were sticking out everywhere in the lake.

'Palm trees and a lake... It's a sight that doesn't seem to fit, but it's strangely harmonious.'

I took out my sword.

The monsters that come out of this place have one characteristic. They are all hybrid monsters mixed with two or more species. Animal and animal. Plant and plant. Or monsters that are a combination of animals and plants. Those palm trees in the lake might be some kind of monster.

'The way to check is simple.'

You just have to check. No creature wants to die easily.

I put blue Sword energy into my sword and swung it at a distant palm tree. The Sword energy flew and collided with the palm tree. The palm tree swayed greatly. A scratch appeared on the surface.

'Just a scratch with Sword energy, huh.'

It wasn't an ordinary palm tree after all.

The palm tree comes towards me. Ripples rise violently in the calm lake. It seems angry from being attacked suddenly.

What's below the palm tree is a giant blue crayfish. The crayfish, about 1.5 times bigger than me, rushes towards me, swinging its pincers.

It's a simple attack. It's not hard to avoid. I stepped back and swung my sword.

Kkaang!

The Hwaryeon Bido covered with Sword energy bounced off. There was only a small scratch on its body. It's an amazingly hard shell.

Kwareureureung.

I dropped lightning. The palm tree crayfish, struck by lightning head-on, paused for a moment. In terms of time, it's about 2 seconds. After that, it rushed at me aggressively as if it was even angrier. The lightning only scorched part of the palm tree.

'Astrape.'

Pajijijijijik.

Red lightning gathered on the blade of the Hwaryeon Bido and covered it. The blade made of only lightning shines brutally.

'In this world, using skills increases their power.'

To put it bluntly, it's compensation.

I jumped onto the crayfish. Due to the crayfish's body structure, its pincers cannot reach its back. The crayfish shook its body and tried to shake me off. I skillfully maintained my balance and stabbed the crayfish in the back with my sword.

It wasn't easy, but the blade eventually pierced the crayfish's shell and entered its body. Red lightning runs through the crayfish's body. Before long, the crayfish's body slumped, and a savory smell wafted up.

「You have gained 700 life points.」

700 life points!

It gave me more life points than any monster I've ever faced.

I looked around the lake and smiled. There are 15 palm trees in sight alone. I can easily get 10,000 points here.

'This was a gold mine. Hehe.'

•••

I went around killing any monsters I saw. There were some pretty annoying guys, but most of them knelt and died before my high stats. If I felt I lacked strength, I switched my status window to Cheonma to fight.

As I was walking through the forest, I stopped and stared at the base of a tree. There was a purple mushroom. It was a mushroom with insect legs. I pointed at the poisonous mushroom bug with my index finger. A streak of blue lightning shot towards the poisonous mushroom bug.

「You have gained 50 life points.」

「You have collected 37,000 life points.」

「You have acquired a top-grade recovery potion.」

"Lame."

As soon as I saw the recovery potion, I shook my head. It's an expensive item, but it's something you can buy as much as you want if you have money.

I swallowed the potion right there.

Top-grade potions not only restore life force, but also stamina and mana.

'Full recovery is a trump card. It's best to save it as much as possible.'

What I've collected in the past 4 hours is 37,000 life points. The goal is 50,000 points and there are 2 hours left.

'There's plenty of time.'

I need to aim for big guys, not small guys like poisonous mushroom bugs. That's how life points accumulate well.

How far had I walked through the forest?

Suddenly, I smelled something rotten. It's the smell of a corpse decomposing. I frowned at the familiar smell.

I headed towards the smell first. The monsters here are all unique. Maybe there's a monster that emits a rotten smell.

My prediction was correct. The rotten smell was the smell emitted by the monster.

'It's a patchwork.'

Patchwork zombie.

It's a zombie made by gathering and combining several corpses. It's a particularly tough zombie to deal with. Above all, there are many variations. Suddenly exploding, or emitting poisonous gas.

'This guy is huge.'

It was one of the biggest patchwork zombies I've ever seen. It's about 10M tall. In addition, patchwork zombies are usually made of human corpses, but this one was different. All kinds of animals and plants have gathered to form a single colony.

'If you sit still, it looks like a pile of corpses at first glance... But if you look closely, it's wriggling.'

I looked at the patchwork zombie and fell into thought. Can I defeat that, or not.

'I won't be able to defeat it with just a few sword strikes. I need firepower.'

Ordinary firepower won't do.

'...Wait. This is the forest of life, right? Is it okay for an undead to be here?'

I recall the setting book.

Area 4,344, Patchwork Laboratory

If you only hear the name, you might mistake it for a place that creates patchwork zombies.

But the real purpose of this patchwork laboratory is not to sew corpses together, but to synthesize living things with living things. Strange creatures are born, and sometimes strange creatures produce rare substances. The patchwork laboratory sells these rare substances. At the same time, it buys animals and plants from hunters and herbalists.

And the challenger is a setting to test the strength of synthetic creatures.

'The god (False God) who can be said to be the ruler of this place is the Patchwork King. His true name is Chimera. Chimera is a living being and despises the undead.'

Then the patchwork zombie in front of me might not be an undead, but a living creature.

'If it's a living thing, won't my lightning work?'

If things go wrong, I can just run away.

Thinking of the worst-case scenario, I dropped lightning on the patchwork.

Kwareureureung! Kwang kwang!

It wasn't just one lightning strike. Lightning continued to strike.

The patchwork struck by lightning twitches. I clicked my tongue. Even though I dropped lightning 15 times in a row, it didn't have much effect. Its body doesn't burn either.

"Geuwooooo...!"

A human face popped out of the patchwork's chest and made a strange noise. And tentacle-like things pierced through the skin of its face along with red blood. There were eyeballs at the end of the tentacles. More than 10 tentacles are watching me.

It was a sight that caused disgust. I suppressed the rising nausea and inferred its identity.

'Those tentacles.... They look just like horsehair worms.'

I think I know why the patchwork's body is wriggling.

The patchwork is just a host. The real thing is the parasite living inside it. Parasites are also living things, so it's not strange for them to be in this forest.

The patchwork golem moves. The ground shook as its huge body, over 10M tall, moved.

'What is the parasite synthesized with?'

It was a flying insect. Mosquito wings were attached to the back of the tentacle-like parasite.

The eyeball parasite flies towards me. If you look closely, sharp, thorn-like teeth were growing on the parasite's body.

'Change status window.'

「Changing status window.」

I switch to the Cheonma status window. My body is overflowing with power. When it comes to physical ability alone, the Cheonma state is better after all.

Cheonma Shingong (Heavenly Demon Divine Skill) Black Flame Demonic Dragon.

I threw the black flame that appeared in my hand at the patchwork. The black flame stuck to the patchwork's skin and began to burn.

"Geuwooooooooooooo!"

"Kkiiiiiiik!"

"Kkiiiiiiiiiiik!"

Not only the patchwork, but something else screamed. It's not the parasite. There are other creatures inside that patchwork body.

Bugs pour out of the patchwork's body. There must be over 10,000 of them. The bugs' destination was me. I covered my whole body in black flames. The black flames were hot, but bearable.

'Damn it. I don't get points even if I kill the bugs.'

Clicking my tongue inwardly, I led the patchwork towards the lake. There's no time. Maintaining the black flames consumes too much mana.

'It's the lake!'

I ran across the surface of the lake. The patchwork fearlessly chased after me. As the patchwork's body goes towards the center of the lake, the patchwork's body sinks below the lake.

The black flames did not disappear easily even when they touched the water. Part of the lake bubbled. The black flames disappeared when the patchwork was completely submerged in the water.

I looked below the surface of the water and opened my mouth.

Fish with frog legs were tearing apart the patchwork and the bugs. It was a spectacle.

「You have gained 17,487 life points.」

「You have collected 54,487 life points.」

I finally exceeded the target of 50,000 points. The patchwork gave as many points as its repulsive appearance.

Three rewards appeared in a row.

「Strength increases by 1.」

「You have acquired 40,000 AP.」

「You have acquired the Combat Recovery (A) skill.」

「Combat Recovery.

Gradually recovers life force, stamina, and mana during combat.

Type: Skill

Rank: A」

It was a skill obtained by achieving 50,000 life points. A satisfied smile spread across my face. This is what I came here to get.

'Combat Recovery. As the name suggests, it increases recovery power during combat. Fortunately, I got the skill in the Cheonma status window as planned.'

Combat sustainability increases.

At A rank, it allows you to maintain combat for more than 30 minutes even when exhausted.

'I've achieved my goal, but there's still about an hour left. Should I hunt more? If I achieve 55,000 points, it seems like I'll get additional rewards.'

I flew Sword energy and killed the fish in the lake.

「You have gained 1 life point.」

I shook my head. Even if I catch 100, I can only get 100 points.

'The efficiency is terrible. In this case, it's better to find and kill other monsters in the forest.'

I went out of the lake. As I was walking through the forest looking for monsters, I found an unexpected corpse. It was the corpse of one of the four challengers who had run away from me.

It was cut and killed by something sharp.

'It looks like he was attacked by a sword?'

The corpse was clean without any bite marks. The monsters here kill each other to eat. It doesn't make sense to kill a creature and leave its corpse intact.

'It looks like they fought among themselves.'

I felt a presence as I passed the corpse. I stared at the base of a large tree. There was someone in the unnaturally dark shadow.

"Come out. I know you're there."

I held the Hwaryeon Bido and exuded killing intent. Someone walked out of the darkness. I opened my mouth in surprise.

"You have a good sense of presence."

It was Li Mei, a beautiful woman with long pink hair and a pink cheongsam.
