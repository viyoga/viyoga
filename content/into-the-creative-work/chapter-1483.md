I hid behind a rock and looked at Sallot Castle.

Sallot Castle.

It was a castle built by humans and ruled by a noble named Sallot. But the castle was taken by demons decades ago.

The Order hadn't retaken Sallot Castle for decades. It's more accurate to say they hadn't even tried. It's understood that there are at least three demons inside Sallot Castle, and corrupted ones and undead are always wandering around.

The Order's judgment at the time was that you'd need at least 300 Holy Knights to attack Sallot Castle.

,The location of Sallot Castle isn't far from the city.'

Sallot Castle wasn't a direct threat.

So the Order left Sallot Castle alone. There was no need to poke the hornet's nest. In fact, the demons in Sallot Castle were well-behaved. Until now.

,The Order kept an eye on Sallot Castle and knew that a ritual was taking place.'

They also knew that the ritual was the resurrection of a Great Demon. Originally, the resurrection of a Great Demon would take decades, but the ritual was speeding it up.

I watched Sallot Castle for a long time. To find differences from the original game. Because that could be a variable.

,There are almost no differences. That game had details even in the trivial things.'

The security at Sallot Castle is tight. The corrupted ones guard the entrance, and the undead loiter around the outer castle. The security of the inner castle goes without saying. Especially annoying were the ghosts flying in the sky.

,There's a way. One is the underground waterway, the other is a dog hole.'

I turned around. The Holy Knights and Inquisitors waiting were awaiting my instructions. There were 500 of them. Half of them had slightly different uniforms. They were support troops from other regions besides Valentiere.

"As planned, I will infiltrate Sallot Castle. You guys will confront those bastards here and wait."

"Understood, Paladin-isiyeo."

Hearing their answer, I started to move around the castle. Going straight ahead would be insane. I would definitely be caught before long, so I had to move while hiding my body behind the rocks.

It was successful. I stuck to the west wall without being caught.

The security became even lower when I stuck to the wall. Unlike humans, demons and corrupted ones weren't meticulous. The undead had lower cognitive abilities than animals. The ghosts flying in the sky and watching the ground only directly monitored certain places. Even then, it was common to see them slacking off.

,It's an army of ragtag soldiers if you compare them to humans. The Great Demon, who can be called the leader, is gone, and the demons, who are the executives, have abandoned command and are immersed in the ritual.'

I stuck close to the wall. And I moved while hitting the wall with my foot as carefully as possible. It was a solid wall, but there was a place that felt different.

,Found it.'

I crouched down and tapped the wall. The old walls crumbled, and a dog hole was created. At first, it was a hole that only a small dog could barely enter, but when I tapped it with my fist, the hole widened and became large enough for an adult man to enter.

,There's no way demons or corrupted ones would manage a castle built by humans.'

I crawled into the castle through the dog hole.

I looked around the castle and sighed. Now it was the real beginning.

,Kill the bastards who need to be killed, and get what I need to get.'

A rotten smell vibrated.

The source of the smell was an undead sitting at the table.

Dalgeurak dalgeurak.

The zombie was eating. He held a fork in one hand and put the rotten food on the plate into his mouth. And he swallowed the food. The swallowed food couldn't pass through the esophagus and popped out of his pierced neck, falling onto the plate. The undead repeated the meal without caring. The undead's meal continued endlessly.

In front of the undead was a large birdcage. A middle-aged man was trapped in the birdcage. The clothes he was wearing were a priest's. The priest in the birdcage found me and his eyes sparkled. He looked at me as if he wanted me to save him quickly.

I suppressed my presence and approached the undead from behind. I grabbed the undead's shoulder with my gauntlet-clad hand and stabbed my sword into his back. The undead, who had no vocal cords, couldn't even scream. I used the Holy Sword to finish off the undead.

"Oh, Goddess-isiyeo! Thank you! You sent me a savior!"

"What are you doing here?"

"Can't you tell by looking? I was caught by that bastard. Now, Holy Knight-ssi, please release me."

"How long have you been trapped?"

"It's been about three months. I was on a pilgrimage... I was caught by a demon on the way. The evil demons locked me in this birdcage and watched me. They tried to corrupt me, but I endured with my steel-like faith."

"I see. That's amazing."

"You must have a purpose for infiltrating this castle. I've been trapped in this narrow birdcage for three months. I've seen and heard a lot. I will be helpful to you. Now, please open this birdcage quickly."

I approached the birdcage. I grabbed the steel bars of the birdcage with one hand and spread them apart. Kkiiiiiik! As the iron spread, the priest was startled and widened his eyes.

"Oh. That's amazing strength. I thought you would cut the bars with a sword... Keoeok?!"

A greatsword came in through the spread bars and pierced his chest. The priest looked at me as if he couldn't believe it.

"W-what are you doing? Aren't you a Holy Knight?!"

"Corrupted one, your pathetic acting doesn't work on me."

"I, I'm not a corrupted one! I haven't been corrupted! My faith is firm!"

"No, you've already been corrupted. You said you were trapped three months ago. But you don't look like someone who's been trapped for three months."

"That's... because the demons steadily gave me water and food! They were trying to use me as a sacrifice!"

"My thoughts are different. The demons were just playing with you. They must have been betting among themselves on when you would admit your corruption."

"I, I haven't been corrupted! Don't you need the information I have?!"

"I don't need information. And don't you feel strange? You're continuing to talk to me even though your heart has been pierced. You've suffered a fatal wound that would kill an ordinary human immediately!"

"T-this is the Goddess's blessing! The Goddess is protecting me."

"Really? Then I'll show you another piece of evidence."

Cheolkeok!

The helmet automatically comes off.

The priest's face is filled with shock when he sees my exposed face. His gaze was directed exactly at my eyes. The Holy Eyes were shining blue.

"P-Paladin...!"

"You can't deceive my eyes. You are a corrupted one."

"N-no! I haven't been corrupted!"

"Face reality."

I used the Holy Sword. Golden light settled on the sword. The priest's face contorted. Just before he screamed, the sword struck the priest's neck. I even cut off the priest's falling head.

,I've taken care of one for now.'

In the game, if you save this priest, he provides information about Sallot Castle. And in the end, he ambushes you at a crucial moment, which is a bit annoying.

,I already know all the information that bastard spouts anyway. There's no reason to keep him alive at all.'

I moved again.

I killed any undead or corrupted ones I saw wandering around the castle. It was possible because most of them were wandering alone. I ignored the bastards who were gathered in groups of four or five. With my current strength, I could kill them easily, but it was impossible to deal with them quietly.

I opened the door to the spire on one side of the castle and went inside. Stairs leading upwards continued. I went up, dealing with the undead sitting on the stairs one by one.

There was a locked door at the top. I checked that there was no presence in front of the door and slashed the door with my greatsword. The diagonally cut door fell over with a thud.

It was a room with nothing but a bed. It was no different from a prison. The woman wearing a stained dress was standing in front of a window blocked by bars. She spoke without turning around.

"Did you come to kill me?"

"No. I came to save you. Princess Polaris."

The princess turned her body around. She had brown hair and a disheveled appearance, but her calm eyes represented her dignity.

"...Are you a Holy Knight?"

"I am a Paladin."

I took off my helmet to show her the Holy Eyes. As the daughter of a Duke, she would have learned all sorts of knowledge. There was no way she wouldn't know about the Paladin's Holy Eyes.

"I heard that a new Paladin has appeared in Valentiere. That must be you."

"That's right."

"I didn't expect you to come to save me after half a year. How is the Austeil Dukedom?"

She asked nervously.

"It's fine."

"...What? F-fine? That can't be. If it was fine, there's no way my father would have left me like this!"

"It's too fine, that's the problem. A demon stole your face and voice and is acting like you. The Austeil Dukedom doesn't even know that you're missing."

"......"

She had a fleeting look of despair. But that was only for a moment. Soon, her face was filled with anger.

"How dare a demon mock my family.... I can never forgive it. Paladin-nim, please take me back to my family. I will repay you handsomely."

"I can't do that right now."

I explained the current situation of Sallot Castle to her. The smart her moaned after listening to my words.

"...They're conducting a ritual to resurrect the Great Demon.... The reason they're holding me is because of the ritual."

"A Paladin's bloodline, the status of a princess. To those bastards, you are a top-class sacrifice."

"A top-class sacrifice.... That doesn't sound very good."

The princess frowned. Regardless, I continued to speak.

"You were captured while transporting the Holy Spear. I need that Holy Spear."

"The Holy Spear Rotis. It's the family's treasure. Only the family's direct line can use the Holy Spear."

"I am a Paladin."

"...I see. Paladins can use the Holy Spear. That's the first time I've heard that. It's impossible to give you the Holy Spear, but I can lend it to you. But I don't have the Holy Spear right now. The demons took it."

"I know where the Holy Spear is. All you have to do is break the seal on the Holy Spear."

"Even the seal.... Paladin-nim, you know a lot."

Her wariness towards me deepened.

I was getting annoyed. The current situation was urgent. There was no time to be chatting so leisurely.

,As long as she doesn't get in the way, I don't care if she doubts me or is wary of me. As long as she doesn't get in the way.'

I turned my body away from her.

"Let's go. There's no time."
