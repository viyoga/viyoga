From a magical academic perspective, prayer could be seen as a greatly condensed ritual.

But what I did wasn't prayer or anything of the sort. I was merely pretending to pray. Faith, which is crucial for prayer? Just believing in the existence of a god or an angel doesn't automatically equate to faith. I have absolutely no reverence for angels.

,...But by assuming a praying posture and calling out Barachiel's name, I fulfilled at least the minimum ritual requirements. And for some reason, Barachiel answered. Is it because I can use divine mana without faith? Or is it the influence from when I transformed into an angel last time?

I couldn't be certain of the cause.

However, Barachiel bestowed a blessing upon me and temporarily connected with me.

An ordinary person might have been delighted to receive the favor of an angel. But I know that angels aren't necessarily benevolent beings. In this world, demons and angels are transcendents from other worlds.

"What's the reason for suddenly giving me a blessing? Did you not like that I impersonated your follower?"

Knowing that even angels, as transcendents, can't intervene as they please, I asked in an impudent tone. I was also displeased by the sudden blessing. Since it ended up being something I prayed for, I couldn't refuse it.

Barachiel's will is conveyed through the connected link.

-Become my apostle. Accept my stigmata and devote yourself to me. My sacred lightning will burn away the curse nesting in your heart without leaving a trace.

Becoming an apostle means receiving the stigmata and becoming a saint.

Barachiel may not be as famous as the Four Archangels, but in terms of power alone, he wasn't that weak.

"I refuse."

I answered without hesitation.

Accepting Barachiel's offer would mean giving up what I have and devoting myself. I had no intention of giving up what I have. Frankly speaking, it would be a loss for me.

-There is plenty of time. I permit you to speak my name in this land.

"Does that mean I can use your name in Divine France? Let me tell you in advance. My will won't change even if time passes."

There was no reply. Still, Barachiel's blessing remained.

I moved, gathering the divine mana in the form of golden lightning that enveloped my body.

• • •

The biggest difference between Divine France and Neo-London is that Divine France didn't recklessly develop its industries. Only the necessary industries are located in Divine France, and only to a minimum extent. Mainly the transportation industry.

Divine France is only called a city-state, but its size was much larger than Neo-London. And most of its land is farmland.

Farming thrives in this lush land. This is because the land of Divine France is blessed by angels. All crops, including rare medicinal herbs, grow well.

Above all, Divine France had priests who used divine magic. Because of the divine magic specialized in healing and enhancement, wealthy people with poor health came to Divine France for recuperation. In addition, divine magic is also good against demonic entities.

Most of those capable priests don't leave Divine France. There's only one reason. The effects of divine magic weaken when they leave the land of Divine France.

As I walked down the road, I spotted a wagon approaching from behind. I glanced at it. A man who looked like a peddler was sitting on the driver's seat.

[Detection]

I reconfirmed with detection magic. There was no one hiding in the wagon.

,A wagon in this era. No, considering Neo-London, is it not that strange?

The peddler stopped the wagon in front of me.

"Priest-nim. I don't know where you're going, but... I can give you a ride to the vicinity."

"I'm going to Domremy Village."

"Our destinations are the same! I'm also going to Domremy Village! Please get in! I'll take you there! It's only a 30-minute ride by wagon!"

"Then I'll be in your care."

In Divine France, priests are respected, so it was common to receive kindness from the French people. There was no reason to ignore the peddler's kindness here.

The wagon starts moving.

"Priest-nim. What order do you belong to? There's no particular insignia on your priestly robes."

"Ah. I'm a wandering priest who serves Barachiel-nim. After finishing my training at a small monastery, I recently started wandering around France to broaden my horizons."

"Barachiel! I've heard of him! The Angel of Blessings! Priest-nim, could you, perhaps, give me a blessing? Of course, I'll pay the price! Please, Priest-nim!"

"A blessing isn't anything special. It's just a temporary increase in physical abilities."

"I don't expect a great blessing! Even a simple blessing with no effect is fine! Please! Priest-nim!"

The peddler's eyes were earnest. I belatedly realized that what was important to him wasn't the effect of the blessing, but the fact that he received a blessing from me, a priest of Barachiel.

,...I don't know much about divine magic's blessing spells either. I'll cast a simple buff spell that gives a little vitality with divine mana and insist it's a blessing.

The peddler is ignorant and wouldn't know much about magic, so it'll work.

I created a seemingly impressive atmosphere, placed my hand, glowing white with divine mana, on his shoulder, and used magic.

"In the name of Barachiel, I bestow a blessing upon you."

Pajijik.

The white divine mana turned into golden lightning and permeated the simpleton's body.

"Oh, oooooh! This is the blessing of the angel Barachiel! I'm overflowing with energy all over my body!!"

The simpleton shouted and gripped the reins of the wagon. He certainly seemed more energetic than before.

I was surprised to see that.

,A normal buff spell became a real blessing. The spell I created instantly transformed into a different spell. A spell I've never seen before.... Is it Barachiel's spell? It started from the moment I spoke Barachiel's name.

Even though the spell I created had changed, I wasn't displeased. Because this is real divine magic.

,A click magic that is completed by simply uttering the name of an angel without having to calculate the spell directly...!

How convenient is this?

I wanted to use it one more time. I looked around and stared at the horse pulling the wagon. There's no rule that blessings can only be used on people.

"The horse looks tired. In the name of Barachiel, I bless you too!"

Divine Magic Click!

Golden lightning permeated the horse.

"Puhieeeeeeeeeeeeeeeing!"

The horse roared and started galloping. The simpleton, who should have stopped the horse's rampage, was even more excited.

"Haha! That's it! Pua! Run!!!"

Did I inject a combat stimulant in the name of blessing?

,This is the result of using only a small portion of divine mana based on my standards. I didn't expect it to be amplified like this. I feel like I could grasp Barachiel's spell if I do it a few more times....

We arrived in Domremy, which was supposed to take 30 minutes, in just 5 minutes.

"Thanks to Priest-nim, we were able to get here quickly! Thank you!"

"You're welcome."

I examined the simpleton. The duration of the blessing's effect is about 5 minutes. There are no side effects. The horse was a little tired, but that was natural since it was pulling the wagon and running at full speed.

I said goodbye to the simpleton and parted ways.

And I exchanged greetings with a new simpleton. It was the village chief of Domremy Village. His name was Simon d'Arc. He was a descendant of the ancient saint, Joan of Arc.

"A priest-nim has come to our village!! I couldn't be happier! I'll take you to my house! My house will be the most comfortable place to stay in this village!"

"Um. Thank you for your hospitality. Even though this is the birthplace of Saint Joan of Arc, it seems that not many priests come here."

"...These days, priests don't even know her name well. Ah, I'm Simon d'Arc, her descendant."

I knew his name through the original work. Because he was an NPC.

I accepted the simpleton's invitation. Every time I headed to his house, the people of Domremy Village subtly followed behind me.

"I'm sorry that the village is so bustling, Priest-nim. It's been almost 7 years since a priest-nim has visited this village. The villagers are also very happy."

"I understand."

I stayed in Domremy Village for a few days, helping the people with various things. Mostly by giving blessings and observing him. Thanks to Barachiel's blessing, I was able to spend my time peacefully without any seizures from the curse.

-On the next dark moon, the curse in your heart will become uncontrollably strong. You must hurry to eliminate that curse.

I know without being told.

On the day the Dark Moon rises, the connection between the curse and the Fallen will strengthen. The Fallen will surely strengthen the curse at that time and kill me.

,There's no need to be impatient. There's still more than a month left until the next Dark Moon rises.

I ignored Barachiel's words and spent more time in Simon d'Arc's place.

Every morning, I started my day by looking at the white flag of Joan of Arc hanging on the wall of Simon d'Arc's house.

,It should be happening soon. Is my contribution to the village still insufficient? Or has the system changed because it's not a game?

I was starting to get anxious too.

Maybe I need to make a new plan.

,Let's stay in this village for just three more days.

And that night.

The Holy Spirit Joan of Arc, who resided in the flag, appeared before my eyes. She was a special quest NPC that could be met if the contribution to Domremy Village was raised to 90 in the game.

Black hair and black eyes. She was tall for a woman, and she was wearing armor, but the bulge that covered her chest was particularly large. It was the appearance of the original NPC.

,Joan of Arc was originally blonde with blue eyes.... Those developers. They had no intention of fixing the historical inaccuracies.

I clicked my tongue inwardly and looked at the Holy Spirit Joan of Arc. Her body was translucent, so I could see through it.

"Follower of Barachiel, thank you. Thanks to you, Domremy Village has regained its vitality after a long time. I would like to repay you for that. Is there anything you desire?"

For a moment, I imagined Joan of Arc becoming my maid. The resurrection, which can only be used once, could be used to solve the body problem.

,No. The condition for using resurrection is that the target's corpse must be present. The Holy Spirit is not a corpse. It's impossible.

In the original game, there are three options in this scene.

Blessing, Skill, Relic.

If you choose Blessing, the total amount of divine mana increases by 10%.

If you choose Skill, you can learn one 7th-grade divine magic.

If you choose Relic, it tells you the location of the relic.

"I want a special item to protect me."

"Such wizardly greed. But no matter what your intentions are, the good deeds you have done will not disappear. I will guide you."

"You can just tell me the location."

"No. There is a high probability that you will wander around. The reason you were able to come to Domremy without wandering was because of Barachiel's blessing. Barachiel's blessing does not work on this relic."

The Holy Spirit seemed to know a lot.
