The instructor played a video. It was footage of an employee fighting a vampire.

A man wearing a baseball cap pulled low appeared in the video. It was night, after the sun had set. The man was walking slowly under the streetlights.

Taang!

With the sound of a gunshot, the man's head exploded. The man's body, collapsed on the ground, twitched and convulsed. The body swelled up. The skin was pale, and the arms and legs were long.

"A-ssei-deul! That's the real form of a vampire! The easiest way to kill a vampire is to kill it before it reveals its true form! Therefore, sniping is the best way to hunt vampires!"

The video continued.

It was a video of an employee sniping a vampire.

Taang!

Taang!

Taang!

Each time a gunshot rang out, a vampire died.

"A-ssei-deul. Don't forget that sniping is the best option. But the best-case scenario doesn't come up often. Those blood-sucking bastards know that sniping is the most dangerous thing of all. So, these guys usually insist on sticking to paths where sniping is impossible. And they act extremely sensitive in places where sniping is possible."

Vampires weren't beasts. They had human-level intelligence. They wouldn't just get sniped unilaterally.

"Next, we'll watch a video of ranged combat."

As soon as the video started, gunshots rang out. Not only the employees, but the vampires were also shooting guns like humans. The gunfight lasted for more than five minutes and ended with the vampires' victory.

"......"

The trainees were silent. They never imagined they'd be shown a video of humans losing.

"A-ssei-deul! Don't underestimate vampires! Basically, a vampire's physique is superior to a human's! Avoid frontal combat as much as possible! Keep in mind that the gunfight you just saw was the worst kind of battle! Now, we'll watch a video of optimal combat."

A two-person team of employees in suits pointed a shotgun at a passing vampire and attacked. The vampire reacted, but it couldn't be faster than the shotgun. The vampire, who lost an arm to the shotgun, revealed its true form in anger and charged at the employee holding the shotgun. The other employee behind him unloaded a rifle into the vampire's head. The vampire, with silver bullets lodged in its head, couldn't get up.

"Combat ends quickly! The best combat is like what you just saw, ending within five seconds! A-ssei-deul! If you succeed in officially joining the company, you'll work in two-person teams with a senior colleague! That's the basic setup! Throw away any arrogance that you can take on a vampire alone!"

This time too, Choi Sangwoo raised his hand.

"A-ssei number 21... It's you again. Permission to ask a question granted."

"Is a two-person team really essential? I've heard that there are employees who hunt vampires solo."

"A-ssei, you've done quite a bit of research. There are exceptions everywhere. Employees who go out hunting alone are those with veteran-level skills or higher. We call them, with reverence, Jaegers."

I knew about Jaegers from the original work. Jaegers were indeed amazing people. But even those Jaegers were still human. It was nearly impossible for even a Jaeger to hunt a Noble-class vampire alone.

"The video I'm about to show you is of one of the Jaegers from the Korean branch. It's a video to show you how amazing Jaegers are. Don't try to imitate it recklessly."

The video played.

A woman in a suit faced off against two vampires who had revealed their true forms. The woman wasn't intimidated by the vampires and drew a sword from her waist. Srrrreung. The sound of the sword being drawn was eerie.

I was surprised to see the woman in the suit. Her face wasn't clearly visible because of her long black hair, but there was no way I wouldn't recognize my woman.

,It's Ju Seo-hyun.

Ju Seohyun, wielding a single sword, danced with the vampires. Every time Ju Seohyun's sword moved, the vampires couldn't avoid it and were hit. The video ended after the vampires' arms were cut off, their legs were cut off, and their necks were cut off.

,Her physical abilities are definitely lower than when she was in God's Atlantis. But her sword has become sharper. If I were to fight the current Ju Seohyun....

I couldn't guarantee the result. My head was getting complicated for no reason.

The surroundings were quiet. The trainees were overwhelmed by Ju Seohyun's skills.

"A-ssei-deul! Stand up! It's time for practical training! Two-person teams will face off against the instructors! Focus on coordinating with your partner!"

• • •

A week had passed since I entered the training camp.

While the first week focused on theoretical education, from the second week onwards, we began to learn how to hunt vampires in earnest. And the instructors began to evaluate the trainees.

I was currently practicing sniping.

To pass, I had to successfully snipe a moving mannequin from 500m. Usually, people don't do well in the second week. But for me, who had the sniping trait, 500m was a piece of cake.

"A-ssei... You're a born sniper! Can you do 1,000m?"

"I can."

"Good! If you succeed in sniping, I'll give you the highest score!"

I put my eye to the scope. Because of the distance, the target didn't appear very large. It was smaller than a grain of rice.

,There's no way I can miss.

I pulled the trigger.

The bullet, propelled from the rifling, pierced the head of the moving mannequin.

"A-ssei! Perfect! Can you do 1,100m?!"

"It might be difficult, but I'll give it a try!"

"A spirit of challenge! Excellent!"

I stopped at 1,300m. I felt like I could snipe at 2,000m, but I was tired, so I quit. 1,300m was enough to get the highest evaluation.

After that, I received the highest scores in shooting with pistols, shotguns, assault rifles, and so on. The way the instructors looked at me changed completely. It was overwhelming.

Other trainees approached me. They asked how they could get better at shooting. I hid my annoyance and answered them sincerely.

,Social skills are also included in the evaluation score. I have to deal with them appropriately until I complete the training, even if it's annoying.

Close combat was also an evaluation subject.

Of course, I received the highest evaluation score here as well. Swords and knives were my specialty, and I handled other melee weapons without much difficulty. Except for one thing: unarmed combat.

The authority on unarmed combat wasn't me, but Choi Sangwoo.

Choi Sangwoo's tremendous physique surpassed mine. It wasn't just brute strength either. I heard he had learned boxing briefly in high school, and his punches were fierce. If I had a weapon, I would have overwhelmed him with technique, but... not in unarmed combat.

,I lost. I can't win with punches. Is it like a werewolf's instinct?

It was okay to be behind in one area.

Because, overall, being the top of the training camp was already a sure thing.

,It's going smoothly. Just like in the original work, I just need to respond well to the raid that will happen in the fourth week. I need to be more active than Choi Sangwoo.

I picked at the food on my tray. It was so bad it made me want to throw up. If there weren't any eyes watching, I would have thrown it away.

• • •

Ting!

The elevator doors opened.

Ju Seohyun, wearing a black suit, walked out of the elevator. Her steps headed towards the door marked Special Operations Team 2.

&gt;

As she opened the door and entered the office, people's gazes were fixed on her. They contained emotions such as respect, envy, and admiration. Among them, there were also some somewhat burdensome feelings mixed in. Ju Seohyun ignored their gazes and knocked on the door of the section chief's office.

"This is Deputy Ju Seohyun."

"Come in."

Section Chief Gu Hyeongjin. A middle-aged man with glasses, with circular hair loss progressing on his head. He was also Ju Seohyun's superior.

"I hunted three vampires who were hiding in Daerim-dong. The support team is handling the three vampire corpses."

"Hmm. Write it up as a report and submit it. You must be tired, so you can go home today."

"No. It won't take long, so I'll write the report now."

"Well, I appreciate that. And this is just a personal comment... You're still cold. Isn't it time you started trusting me and your colleagues?"

"I don't know what you're talking about."

Gu Hyeongjin looked into Ju Seohyun's eyes. They were so calm that they felt cold. They had worked together as colleagues for the past year, but she didn't seem to think of him or her other colleagues as colleagues.

"Hoo. Never mind. Do as you please. It would be meddling to say more. Oh, are you thinking of getting a partner soon? I know you're a Jaeger, but the higher-ups want you to take on a rookie and train them."

"No. I'm more comfortable alone. As I've said before, a partner would only hold me back."

"You're too firm. But at least take a look at the documents. I heard there are two legendary talents in this training camp. The training process hasn't ended yet, but it's safe to say that these two will be coming to the Seoul branch."

Gu Hyeongjin handed over two documents. Ju Seohyun, who had no intention of accepting a partner, was planning to quickly scan the documents and return them.

But after looking at one of the documents, Ju Seohyun's eyes widened, and she gripped the document tightly enough to crumple it. She was shaken as she looked at the identification photo at the top of the document.

Gu Hyeongjin was also surprised by Ju Seohyun's unexpected reaction.

"Why the sudden change? Do you know him?"

"......"

Ju Seohyun didn't hear Gu Hyeongjin's words. Her mind was currently very complicated.

One year ago, she was suddenly thrown from Atlantis into this place. The Atlantis system and the abilities derived from the system disappeared. At first, she wondered if the events of Atlantis were a dream, but the sword she was holding and the chastity belt under her pants told her it wasn't a dream.

Then, she ended up killing a vampire who attacked her and was scouted by the BC Company to hunt vampires. She had separately investigated Atlantis, but there were no significant results. Until now.

,Seong Yujin...!

Seong Yujin was in this world.

This world must be related to Atlantis. The proof was Seong Yujin's existence. It must be God's mischief or something.

As soon as she realized that Seong Yujin was in this world, her body became hot. This was anger. Especially the heat in her lower abdomen was probably due to the grudge of the chastity belt.

Kkadeuk.

Ju Seohyun unconsciously gritted her teeth. She wanted to meet Seong Yujin right away and demand an explanation. But she didn't know the location of the training camp. The location of the training camp was top secret, so ordinary employees couldn't know it. Even within the company, only a small number of people knew its location.

,There's no need to rush. He'll be coming to the Seoul branch in just a week.

Ju Seohyun tried to calm her excitement.

"You. You seem to have come to life. Seong Yujin. Do you know him?"

"I don't know him. Section Chief. This bastard... No, this person, I will be in charge of him."

"You? Didn't you just say you didn't need one?"

"I need him. Then I'll take that as a yes and head out."

Ju Seohyun took Seong Yujin's documents and went outside.

Section Chief Gu Hyeongjin laughed in vain. For the past year, Ju Seohyun had been the best employee, accepting any dangerous mission without refusing. Sometimes she seemed like a machine. Even when he took her to company dinners, she was always stiff.

,Seong Yujin. It seems certain that they know each other. Were they perhaps lovers? If that's the case, it'll be a disaster. I know there are more than a few men who have their eyes on Deputy Ju.

Whatever the case, Ju Seohyun's appearance, now full of life, wasn't bad.
