Ju Seo-hyeon, who killed the Vampire Lord Yoon Seo-jin, committed suicide by stabbing herself in the heart with a sword. A nuclear missile falls on her corpse.

I was next to Ju Seo-hyeon. I kept being next to her. Of course, Ju Seo-hyeon didn't notice me, though.

,'Moonlight Sonata 7 is too good to lose here. I wonder if I can recover it?'

Right now, I couldn't use most of my skills and traits.

For example, in the case of Complete Recovery, even though the cooldown time had passed, I couldn't use it in this world.

[Complete Recovery cannot be used.]

However, I could use Instant.

[Using Instant. Remaining stacks: 6]

The world slowed down, and I could move quickly. But I couldn't affect this world.

,'I should try it. Reverse Summon.'

Just before the nuclear missile fell, Moonlight Sonata 7, embedded in Ju Seo-hyeon's chest, disappeared and entered my inventory.

,'I succeeded!'

I was lucky. This sword was too good to lose here.

And the nuclear missile fell to the ground, causing a massive explosion.

It was obvious who was behind the nuclear missile. The Company. They must have seen Ju Seo-hyeon's power as a vampire and fired the nuclear missile. Honestly, if I were the Company, I wouldn't be confident in defeating Ju Seo-hyeon with any method other than a nuclear missile. Since the bomb necklace didn't work, they had to use a nuke.

Of course, that's separate from the fact that it's a pretty unpleasant thing to do.

,'Ju Seo-hyeon committed suicide, so the nuclear missile was a waste of effort.'

The person who insisted on the nuclear missile must have been Department Head Kang Myeong-suk. Because she's a woman who would do anything to kill a vampire.

Even a small-yield nuke is still a nuke. It will affect the cities and towns around here. Not to mention the victims. The countries around Romania will definitely raise hell.

,'Kang Myeong-suk will have to take responsibility for that. It's obvious without even looking. Kang Myeong-suk's finished, too.'

The Company will discipline Kang Myeong-suk. A nuclear missile is too big of an accident to hide everything. The Company might be able to manipulate public opinion, but....

,'The vampire bastards have people planted in the Company, 100%. It's obvious when you see that even Kwak Su-hyeok met Yoon Seo-jin and became a vampire.'

The vampires won't stay still. They'll expose this situation somehow, and the Company's credibility will plummet to the ground.

"......!"

Ju Seo-hyeon opened her eyes wide. She jumped up from the bed, grabbed her head, and groaned.

A slight headache and vivid memories of a dream come to mind.

She became a vampire hunter in another world. No. In that place, they called vampire hunters company employees. She joined a company specializing in vampire disposal and worked there for a year. She even got the nickname Jaeger. She met Seong Yujin.

"Ugh...."

Ju Seo-hyeon groaned.

Seong Yujin was Seong Yujin even in her dream. Who would have thought she'd be violated by Seong Yujin even in a dream?

,'...It's not just that I was violated by Seong Yujin.'

They became partners, worked together, and rested together. They lived like a cohabitating couple. But that period was only a few months.

Seong Yujin died trying to save her from the Vampire Lord. In the dream, she was sad and angry at the same time. She became a vampire to avenge Seong Yujin, and eventually, after dealing with that vampire, she committed suicide.

After dying, she seemed to have seen something falling from the sky. She remembers something cutting through the dark sky with light.

,'...Was it a meteor?'

She couldn't know exactly what it was.

She rummaged through her memories and sighed. The dream is too realistic. Was it really just a dream?

Soon, Ju Seo-hyeon frowned. With each breath, the pain lessened, and at the same time, the vivid dream was fading. It was no use desperately trying to remember the dream. Later, she'll probably just think, "I had a dream like that."

,'I feel bad.'

It wasn't about what happened with Seong Yujin in the dream. After all, it's just a dream. It doesn't matter what she and Seong Yujin did there. What she feels bad about is the disappearing memory. It felt like someone was stealing her memories.

"Heueut...."

Ju Seo-hyeon got up. Perhaps because of the dream, her body felt sluggish. She moved her body and felt that something was out of place. It was as if she was moving this body after a very long time.... It felt like something was overflowing, yet lacking.

,'When I became a vampire in the dream, I gained the ability to control blood. That feeling of controlling blood is still vivid.'

She sighed. In the end, it's just a dream. Since it's a dream, it's meaningless.

Ju Seo-hyeon checked the time. 7 AM. Was it because of the dream? She woke up later than usual. Still, she couldn't skip her usual morning training.

She picked up the sword next to her bed. It was one of her favorite swords. In the dream, it was destroyed beyond repair while fighting the Vampire Lord.

,'The sword is fine. So that was a dream after all.'

She went down to the underground training room. It was a personal training room just for her. It wasn't very spacious, but it was enough to practice swordsmanship.

Hwik!

She swings the sword.

With each swing, the memories of the dream are gradually fading. As if cutting the memories with a sword.

Ju Seo-hyeon gritted her teeth.

It's a dream.

It's just a dream.

There was no reason to be unable to concentrate like this. She doesn't try to remember the dream. But if she doesn't remember the dream... won't she forget it completely? She doesn't like that.

Cheolkeok!

The door opens.

Ju Seo-hyeon immediately frowned. There was only one person who would enter the training room without knocking like this.

"Oh. You're here again today."

It was Seong Yujin. He gives a sly smile.

"...What did you come here for?"

"What do you think I came here for?"

"You must have come with some dirty purpose."

"You don't have the right to refuse. You know? You have to beat me to refuse."

"I'll definitely win next time."

Ju Seo-hyeon said, growling. Seong Yujin only smirked, even though he felt the murderous intent in her voice.

"That's what you've been saying a hundred times."

Ju Seo-hyeon noticed that he was different from usual. Normally, he would have pounced on her like a beast in heat. She, who lost in the last battle, wouldn't be able to resist that.

"Actually."

Seong Yujin slowly approaches. Ju Seo-hyeon narrowed her brows. For a moment, the memories of the dream overlap with the current Seong Yujin.

"I got a pretty good item outside this time."

"...A pretty good item? What does that have to do with me?"

"I thought it would suit you well."

"You must have brought another strange toy."

He must have brought an adult toy. It'll probably be a disgusting-looking toy. Thinking about herself being toyed with by Seong Yujin, anger and hatred surge again. In a way, she might have to thank this Seong Yujin. Because he always reminds her of her desire for revenge in this way.

"I'll definitely kill you."

"Yeah. Yeah. A gift for you like that."

Seong Yujin takes out a sword from the air. Ju Seo-hyeon's eyes widen as if they're about to tear when she sees the sword.

Seong Yujin smirks and swings the sword dangerously. She couldn't take her eyes off that sword, which resembled moonlight.

"That sword is...."

"I worked hard to get it this time. It's a sword called Moonlight Sonata 7. It's a whopping S-rank sword."

"...Why are you giving me such a great sword?"

He scratched his head and said.

"I had a strange dream today. You came out in the dream. You and I went to a place called the Company, and we were vampire hunters. Well, it's hazy now.... In the dream, I gave you this sword at the end. This might be some kind of omen. So I'm going to give it to you."

"......."

Ju Seo-hyeon's pupils shook violently. She and Seong Yujin had the same dream. Why?

"...Maybe it's not a dream."

"What are you talking about? Here, take the sword."

Ju Seo-hyeon held the sword and trembled. Her head went down. The feeling of holding this sword.... It was similar to the feeling she felt in the dream. Was it really a dream? Was it just a dream?

Seong Yujin's hand touched Ju Seo-hyeon's cheek, who was lost in thought. Ju Seo-hyeon looked up. Seong Yujin's face approached and kissed her.

She quietly accepted the kiss. Seong Yujin's kiss was still rough. He tries to devour her like a beast. She can't just be taken advantage of. Ju Seo-hyeon also moved her mouth and resisted Seong Yujin. The sound of their tongues mixing filled the training room.

「Black Vengeance is worried about you.」

「Black Vengeance advises that you must not lose your desire for revenge!」

「Black Vengeance hopes that its advice reaches you!」

Losing my desire for revenge?

That can't be.

It's just that she's being teased by Seong Yujin because she lost the bet.

,'If I get the chance, I definitely will...!'

Ju Seo-hyeon closed her eyes, making a promise. Her body began to heat up.
