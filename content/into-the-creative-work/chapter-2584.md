As Ilgak Heuksu died, the magic protecting the city disappeared.

The Northern Army I brought, which had been besieging the city, entered as planned once the magic vanished. Perhaps it was because of Ilgak Heuksu's magic, but the city had no walls, allowing the Northern Army to enter easily. I watched from the city center.

The monsters' resistance wasn't nonexistent, but it was meaningless. From the monsters' perspective, the soldiers capable of fighting had already been drafted for the Jimang Fortress battle and were annihilated. Most of the monsters remaining in this city were just leftovers.

Even if there were monsters capable of fighting, the Northern Army were elite soldiers seasoned in the war against monsters. It's hard to even run away, let alone resist.

A massacre began.

The Northern Army killed and looted the monsters. They began to defile the decent-looking female monsters.

There weren't many beautiful female monsters. Even monsters resembling humans were rare. Just because they have two legs, two arms, a torso, and a head doesn't make them human. Most have heterogeneous appearances.

Monsters disguise themselves as humans? There was no reason to disguise themselves as humans in a monster city. If they disguised themselves as humans, they would be ostracized by the monsters. Even if they did disguise themselves, Noean Geumjeong sees through their true nature.

,Beautiful female monsters are just a fantasy,.

I sighed and took out a chair from my inventory and sat down. I quietly focused on my breathing. I had consumed quite a bit of internal energy coming here, so I needed to replenish it.

,I can't openly practice Qi circulation here. The Northern Army? They're guys I just met. I can't trust them. I'll do it in a simplified way.,

Even if it's not as efficient as practicing Qi circulation properly, I can still replenish the consumed internal energy.

"General-nim. The operation was successful."

On Gyowi came closer. In his hand was a monster's head. It was a monster with pointy teeth and small horns. When I looked at the head with Noean Geumjeong, I saw its name.

"Mugak In-gwi (Hornless Human Ghost) Eunjeok?"

"You recognized him right away. As expected of the General-nim. He's the only son of Yukgak Demon King Eunhong. He was trying to escape quietly, so I caught him, interrogated him, and killed him."

"His son was in this city? He must have been a piece of shit if I didn't even know he was here."

If he were strong, I would have found him and killed him first.

"This city seems to have been the most cherished of Yukgak Demon King's cities. I also heard information about other cities, and they are at least a hundred *ri* away. Those cities have monster armies stationed in them, so an easy battle like this one will be difficult."

"You're saying we should be satisfied with this city and retreat."

"Yes, General-nim. A lot has happened today. The soldiers are quite exhausted, even if they don't show it."

I thought for a moment and nodded. The soldiers were definitely exhausted. If we stirred up other monster cities and suffered a defeat, the achievements we've built up so far would be tarnished. It was right to retreat here.

"Understood. Once the cleanup of this city is complete, we'll retreat to Jimang Fortress. Have you found out the location of Yukgak Demon King?"

"I heard he's performing a ritual in the Yellow Sea. He says this year's famine is so bad he's borrowing the power of a god."

"The Yellow Sea? A monster is performing a ritual for Habaek? He must be confident in his ritual."

This world is different from the old days when gods could easily use their power. It's difficult to perform a proper ritual, and even if you do, the chances of a god accepting it are slim. From a god's perspective, it takes a considerable amount of power to exert influence in the Middle Realm.

"Yukgak Demon King is said to be a distant descendant of Habaek."

"If he's a descendant... he might be able to skip some of the ritual procedures."

"Yes. Especially since Habaek is a god who likes virgins. It seems he's performing the ritual by offering virgins to Habaek in the Yellow Sea as human sacrifices."

"Sacrifices... so he's talking about humans. The more beautiful they are, the more valuable they'll be as sacrifices. Tsk. Yukgak Demon King. This bastard can't be left alive. Habaek. I want to kill that bastard too if possible."

On Gyowi gasped and was startled.

"General-nim! This is a place where Habaek's influence reaches! Habaek might hear you!"

"What's he gonna do if he hears me? Let him hear it. Habaek, you son of a bitch."

"......."

On Gyowi closed his mouth, looking warily at the sky. Of course, Habaek didn't inflict any divine punishment on me.

"How long are you going to hold that head?"

"I brought it to offer it to the General-nim."

"I don't need it. Just throw it away somewhere. ...No, wait. He's Yukgak Demon King's son, so hang it on a pole and display it. Yukgak Demon King will be pissed if he sees it."

"It will also break the monsters' morale. I will do as you command, General-nim."

On Gyowi ordered his subordinates to behead Mugak In-gwi and display his head.

"You said my predecessor was General Mujin? How did General Mujin die?"

"General Mujin died in battle fighting Sagak Yeongnok on the battlefield. He liked alcohol to an excessive degree, but he was a talented man."

"General Mujin. If he was a general in the Northern Army, he must have been at least a Harmonious Realm incarnation."

"Yes, that's right. In that sense, I think you, General Noejeong, are truly amazing. Overcoming the difference in realm... is impossible with ordinary abilities."

I had the peerless divine arts of Noecheonryu and Cheonma Shingong, and the Cheongangseong system. On top of that, I had treasures like the Geumgang Shinroe, Baekjeonchang, and Unryong Armor set. I even took the Byeokgeumdan elixir, so there was no way I could lose.

"The battlefield is full of variables. Sometimes things happen that defy common sense. I saw General Mujin's last battle."

"He fought Sagak Yeongnok and got his head cut off."

The Sagak Yeongnok I saw was a deer monster. A monster with the body of a deer itself, not a human body. He didn't even have weapons like swords or spears. But his head was cut off? Of course, he could have used magic to do that.

"Ogak Damok intervened and cut off General Mujin's head."

"...Ogak Damok, you say? Something definitely felt strange. But I don't know much about Ogak Damok because there's no information about her."

"I killed Ogak Damok. She was a troublesome monster bitch who used illusions. General Mujin must have been ambushed and killed by that bitch too."

I survived because my momentary crisis detection was activated in time. General Mujin wouldn't have had the same power as me, so he must have died.

"Illusions.... This is crazy. Am I still under an illusion?"

"If you were, I would have noticed. She seems to be cautious in many ways, so she probably only used illusions during the battle to manipulate the situation."

"To think there was such a monster among the enemy... It's terrifying. I'm glad the General-nim took care of her."

The looting was slowly coming to an end.

Suddenly, the sky began to darken.

I looked up at the sky. Dark clouds covered the sky, and it began to pour.

"General Noejeong-nim?"

"It's not me."

I didn't use Jeunryeong. There was no reason to use it. That was an artificially created cloud with a different power. Since a different power was being applied, it was difficult for me to control those dark clouds.

,Did he anticipate my fighting style and make the first move?,

It's pouring rain. The rainfall is so heavy that water is already starting to pool on the ground.

I shouted, infusing my voice with internal energy.

"All troops, retreat from the city!!"

The soldiers who were looting in various parts of the city hesitated at my command. Then, the looting stopped, and they retreated out of the city. As befitting elite soldiers, they listened well to orders.

"On Gyowi. Don't get in the way here for no reason and retreat to lead the army."

"General Noejeong! Could this be...!"

"He's here. He came himself, saving me the trouble."

I got up from the chair and grabbed Geumgang Shinroe and Baekjeonchang.

Kureureung, kureureureureung....

Lightning flashed in the dark clouds. Even if the dark clouds themselves were difficult, controlling the lightning wasn't that difficult. The enemy's power was controlling the dark clouds. To be precise, it was controlling water.

I lowered my gaze slightly.

A monster with six horns was floating in the sky. The monster, with a size of 3m, had the face of a ghost. He held a spear in his right hand and a mirror in his left.

Noean Geumjeong identified the names and uses of the spear and mirror held by Yukgak Demon King.

Sagwichang (Ghost-Summoning Spear). A spear that summons ghosts.

Hwanghaegyeong (Yellow Sea Mirror). A mirror that controls water and the sea. The Hwanghaegyeong is Habaek's *beopbae*. Yukgak Demon King said he performed a ritual for Habaek... Habaek must have lent him the *beopbae*. The dark clouds pouring rain right now are also the power of the Hwanghaegyeong.

"This is an absurd situation. I only left my seat for a moment... and all my disciples are dead, my son's head has been cut off, and the people who follow me are being massacred. As a king, I failed to save my people, so my sin is deep. Since I cannot revive my dead disciples and people... I will kill you, my enemy, to soothe their spirits."

Yukgak Demon King shook the mirror in his left hand. Light flashed from the mirror. The rainwater that had been soaking the ground shot up into the sky. Mud mixed in, and the murky yellow water clumped together and flowed, becoming a giant sword that fell towards me.

I gripped Baekjeonchang. Lightning crackled and gathered at the tip of the spear.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Sky Style) Byeokpajin (Thunder Wave Suppression).

I thrust the spear towards the falling water sword. Byeokpajin severed the flow of water. The water that had formed the shape of the sword lost its power and fell to the ground.

I seized the opportunity and aimed Geumgang Shinroe at Yukgak Demon King.

Noecheonryu (Lightning Sky Style) Jeonjapho (Electromagnetic Cannon).

Geumgang Shinroe was fired. Faster than the blink of an eye, Geumgang Shinroe reached Yukgak Demon King.

Kaaang!

Yukgak Demon King deflected Geumgang Shinroe with his spear.

I frowned. Geumgang Shinroe was clearly aimed at his head. But Geumgang Shinroe's trajectory deviated slightly to the right and collided with the spear Yukgak Demon King swung, bouncing off.

I, who has the [Shooting] trait, made a mistake in aiming? That can't be.

"Did you use magic? I can't see it, so you must have used magic in advance before coming here."

Since I didn't directly see him using magic, it was difficult to immediately grasp exactly what kind of magic he used.

"I was enraged, but I didn't lose my composure. I came here prepared for battle."

"Really? Let's see how well you've prepared."

Kwareureureung!

Lightning fell from the dark clouds. The rainwater around Yukgak Demon King instantly gathered and blocked the lightning. Lightning struck relentlessly. But it couldn't penetrate the shield made of water.

I was about to use Noecheonmyeong, but I lost control of the dark clouds.

,Even using Jeunryeong is difficult. Hwanghaegyeong. That damn *beopbae* is a *beopbae*, but... Yukgak Demon King's realm is higher than mine.,

I give up on controlling the dark clouds. Instead, I'll resonate the lightning within the dark clouds. Even if I can't use Noecheonmyeong to its full extent, it will be helpful later.

"Do you think I'll just stand by and watch?"

Yukgak Demon King's mirror reflects the dark clouds. The dark clouds split into 36 equal parts. The lightning within them lost its power and disappeared.

"He covered the lightning energy with water energy and suppressed it. He could only do that because there was lightning in the dark clouds."

His skill in handling water energy is better than I thought.

,Close combat would be better in this case.,

Noecheonryu (Lightning Sky Style) Biraeshin (Flying Thunder God).

I first threw Baekjeonchang like a javelin, then wrapped my body in lightning and soared towards Yukgak Demon King. Yukgak Demon King easily deflected Baekjeonchang. I aim for that small gap. I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and gripped it, thrusting it out.

Kkaang!

It was blocked by Hwanghaegyeong. There wasn't even a scratch on the mirror's surface. It's much harder than I expected.

I was about to swing the knife again, but my body stopped. Yukgak Demon King's magic intervened.

"Let me tell you your fate. Your fate is very ominous today. Be careful of water."

The Hwanghaegyeong mirror reflects me.

,What the fuck.,

I was sucked into the mirror in an instant. There was no time to use Cheonsim.

I was in the sea.

,I was careless. I never imagined Hwanghaegyeong would have such an ability.,

Perhaps it's because my realm is low, but even with Noean Geumjeong, I couldn't fully grasp the true ability of Hwanghaegyeong, a god's *beopbae*.

,It's fortunate that I have the Blessing of Water. The way to escape from here is... below.,

I could see through Noean Geumjeong.

I swam downwards. My body feels heavy because it's a special space. I couldn't use half the strength I normally could.

,I'd like to use a space teleportation scroll if I could, but it probably won't work here since it's an isolated special space.,
