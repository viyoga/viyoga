Thwack.

Seong Hari's black spear pierced Laplace's body.

That wasn't the end of it. Laplace's existence began to be absorbed into Seong Hari at a rapid pace.

,Hahaha! That's it! Let me be eaten, Baal! Eat me and become stronger!

,Shut up.

Chwak!

Seong Hari lightly swung the spear. Laplace, who was barely holding onto the thread of life, was confirmed to die by that light motion. Laplace's existence was sucked into Seong Hari and absorbed. I could see it clearly.

It was similar to how Seong Hari looked when she absorbed a spirit.

I was bewildered. Her unique trait, Spirit Devourer (S), only works on spirits.

,...Hari-ya.

,Oppa.

Seong Hari's voice was subdued. Her red eyes were calm. It was a very different atmosphere from the her I knew.

『Demon Hunter (S) wants to kill the demon in front of you.』

『All stats temporarily increase.』

『Attack power against demons temporarily increases.』

『Demon Hunter (S) does not acknowledge demons.』

『Demon Hunter (S) wants you to make a vow.』

,Shut up.

Demons and whatnot, the one in front of me was Seong Hari. Me killing my own woman? That can never happen. I'd rather die than kill my woman.

The Demon Hunter (S) trait didn't give up. I could even sense it trying to forcibly mess with my mind to attack Seong Hari. But thanks to the Absolute Spirit effect, my mind was perfectly fine. All Demon Hunter (S) could do was whine at me.

,Hari-ya. Let's go back.

I held out my hand to her.

The world inside the dungeon was collapsing. We have to go outside the dungeon. That's the only way both I and Seong Hari can live. I had a feeling that the current Seong Hari could go outside the dungeon with me. I had that intuition.

,.......

Seong Hari stared intently at the hand I held out. Then she gave a bitter smile and shook her head.

,I'm sorry, oppa. I really want to go with you, but... I can't.

,Why?!

,Because I'm a demon now. Not just any demon, but a legion commander. You know that high-ranking demons can't just go to the Middle World.

,You're Seong Hari before you're a demon! You can go if you just accept the restrictions! Even if you're a demon, you can hide your identity and live! Let's go together, Hari-ya!

,I really want to, too. But I can't.

,Why the hell not?!

,Because that's the price of a contract with a demon. Right now, I'm not Seong Hari, but Baal. The eighth legion commander, Baal.

『Name: Baal

Strength: SSS+ Stamina: SSS Agility: SSS Durability: SSS Mana: SSS+

Trait: Indra's Thunderclap (SS)

Skills: Divine Spear Unification (S) Spear Throw (S) Combat Sense (S), Combat Recovery (A+), Barrier (A), Demonification (S)

Authority: Authority of Gluttony (SSS), Authority of Possibility (SS-)

Favorability: 92』

Her status window was visible. My face hardened. Was it because her power was enormous? Because the Spirit Devourer (S) trait disappeared and the Authority of Gluttony appeared? None of that. It was because the name written in the status window had changed.

The fact that the name in the status window had changed meant that her very foundation had changed.

I gritted my teeth. The her in front of me is not the Seong Hari I know. But still, she's my woman.

,Just ignore the contract!

,The contract is already engraved in me. I can't be with oppa right now. But... it'll be different after the contract is over. I promise. I'll be by oppa's side then.

She said with a confident voice and strode towards me. She wrapped her left arm around my neck and kissed me. We kissed like we always did. Our tongues naturally intertwined and then separated.

,So... please wait until then.

,No. I can't wait!

I hugged her body. I could feel the hardness because of the armor Seong Hari was wearing. But I couldn't let go. It was clear that if I let go like this, I wouldn't be able to see her for a long time.

,Thank you, oppa.

Seong Hari whispered in my ear.

The world collapsed.

And we disappeared from the collapsed world.

When I came to my senses, it was an empty space. She, who I was holding in my arms, disappeared like a dream. Only the small warmth remaining in my hand became a trace of her.

,Come this way.

Someone said. The voice was heard from all directions. Strangely, I knew which direction the voice was speaking from. I walked straight ahead.

,This voice is that bastard. The god of another world or something.

I looked down on him easily. After all, he was a guy who belonged to the world inside the dungeon. But looking back now, he was by no means someone to look down on.

,That bastard. He seemed strangely friendly to me.

It could have been my imagination.

The further I walked, the more the scenery changed. Darkness with nothing in it was filled with light. A small, sparkling light.

,Starlight. Is this space?

The lights gathered and formed a group. A galaxy. I felt like I'd seen it somewhere, but I couldn't remember well.

,Did I see it in a textbook?

Even after thinking about it again, I couldn't remember what the name of the galaxy was.

,You've come.

The bastard was in front of me.

A form made of something I couldn't tell whether it was light or darkness.

,Are you... the same bastard from before?

,That's right. But instead of the fake in the fake world, I am now facing you as the real thing.

,The god of another world.

,That's what they call me.

I was nervous looking at him. He has power that can't even be compared to a demon legion commander. He had power that was truly on a different level.

,Why did you call me here?

,It's a personal interest.

,Is it because of the scales or something?

,If it was just that, I wouldn't have tried to see you in person like this. It's natural for an ordinary human to have the Scales of Karma, but... when looking at the entire universe, it's not impossible. What I find amazing is your existence. I can feel another trace of karma in you. A trace of karma that shouldn't exist. Even now that I'm facing you directly, I can't believe it.

I frowned. I was annoyed that I came here because of his interest, but I was even more annoyed that I couldn't understand what he was saying at all.

,Speak clearly. What do you want to say?

,I will. By any chance, did you come from another world?

,Suddenly that conclusion comes out?

,A trace of unknown karma. Since I can't read most of it... another world. In other words, isn't it natural that it's a trace of another universe?

Did he notice the Game Life app? He's too calm to have noticed. If he had realized the existence of the Game Life app, even the noblest god would have gone crazy.

,What will you do to me if that's the case?

,I'll ask first. You don't have to answer if you don't want to.

,You're different. You don't seem like a god.

,I've never thought of myself as a god. I'm just an existence wandering this universe. If you consider me a god, then I'll be a god to you.

,I have no intention of telling you my story.

,Is that so.

,......You're not thinking of finding out by force? Like taking my head and finding out the information.

,There's no need to forcibly find out the truth. The truth isn't always beneficial. Especially the truth related to you seems dangerous.

,I might tell you if you grant my wish.

,Tell me.

,Return Seong Hari to being human.

,Are you talking about the woman who became a demon? I won't refuse your request. Manipulating causality requires a lot of power. I don't want to know the truth while using that much power.

,.......

,It seems like the story is over, so I'll send you back. I don't have any interest in humans anymore, so we won't meet again.

I could clearly see that he had lost interest from his voice.

Is this a god? I couldn't understand him. I didn't even feel like understanding him.

The moment I felt my body being sucked somewhere, I returned to the room where the Terra AI was outside the dungeon. The core of the Terra AI was in the state that Laplace had smashed it.

I looked around and then lay down on the floor. As the tension was released, all the strength in my body drained away.

Tenrai Nagisa stepped in.

,He went berserk after receiving the power of Susanoo's Magatama. It's not a strange thing. It's hard for humans to perfectly control the power of a god.

,Just try to mess with my son.

I didn't bother revealing the truth for the sake of her mental state.

,He is a mass murderer. He committed a crime, so he must be punished. Originally, it would be the death penalty, but we will end the punishment by having the association directly manage him.

In the end, the Japanese association decided to quietly bury this matter.

I returned to Arashi Academy and spent some time in the hospital room. It was a feigned illness.

,Tenrai Nagisa is sorting out the situation. If I step in here, things might get more twisted.

And quietly resting at the same time.

,You look annoyingly carefree.
