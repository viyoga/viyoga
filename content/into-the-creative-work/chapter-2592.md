Watching the four scatter, I was briefly tempted. If I used lightning here, I could easily subdue them.

,Using wind isn't familiar, so delicate control is difficult.'

I was about to leave the other riffraff and move to capture Ken, my target, when Yura grabbed my shoulder and said.

,I'll chase Ken. You go after the other three and take care of them. The others are also heinous criminals with bounties on their heads. We can't afford to miss even one.'

,Alright.'

Yura ran after Ken. Her legs were light and her speed was fast. It seemed she had learned her own version of light footwork.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Waves.

No matter how high-ranking an A-class Hunter is, unless they're specialized in detection, it's impossible for them to sense electromagnetic waves. Park Jeong-gu? I don't care about him.

I used electromagnetic waves to pinpoint the enemies' locations.

,Park Jeong-gu. You stay here quietly.'

,No, wait a minute!'

Ignoring Park Jeong-gu's words, I floated my body with wind and began to fly into the sky. Flying on the wind was awkward, but it was faster than running.

First, I caught up with the nearest guy. The guy who was running away gave up and turned around to prepare for battle.

,You fucking bastard! Do you think I'm just going to die like this? Even if I die, I'll take your eardrums with me! Be prepared to live as a deaf person for the rest of your life!'

The guy brought the flute he was holding to his mouth. A terrible, ear-splitting melody spread in all directions. But before the sound waves reached my body, I summoned Hwaryeon Bido and swung it. There was no one watching, so I didn't hesitate.

Lightning Heaven Style Lightning Flash.

The red trajectory splits the guy's head. The sound waves reached me, but my S-class body only ended up with a temporary ringing in my ears.

,He attacked so confidently, I thought it was a dangerous attack, but it was nothing.'

I unsummoned Hwaryeon Bido and flew towards the guy who had escaped in another direction.

I fought the two guys without using mana. It was a little difficult since I wasn't using mana, but the battle was a complete victory for me.

Lightning Heaven Style Electromagnetic Waves.

I checked Yura and Ken's locations and frowned. Their presence wasn't clearly felt. It was as blurry as if they were in a fog.

,Someone new has intervened. Is it Ken's reinforcements? The reason I can't feel their presence well must be because of this guy. Does he have some kind of ability to hide his presence?'

Electromagnetic waves aren't working properly. I couldn't tell exactly how strong he was.

,If Ken called for reinforcements in this situation... he must be at least an A-class.'

Yura is also an A-class. If she's facing two or more A-class opponents, she won't last long. I flew into the sky and headed to where Yura was.

It was a mountain with thick fog, which didn't match the weather or the time. Feeling that something was wrong, I stirred up the wind to blow away the fog.

The fog disappeared for a moment, but then reappeared and enveloped the mountain.

,It's definitely artificial fog.'

I knew Yura's location, so I flew quickly.

I found Yura. She was holding her broken right arm with her left hand and leaning against a tree. It wasn't a life-threatening injury.

,...Be careful. That thing is beyond standards.'

Listening to Yura's warning, I looked straight ahead. I saw Ken and a 3-meter-tall blue being. A monster with horns on its forehead. It was holding a gold-banded cudgel in one hand.

,Me. Hungry. Give me food. Quickly.'

The monster spoke clumsily. Ken, who was next to the monster, was startled and pointed at me with his index finger.

,A meal has appeared. Ao Oni. Eat that guy.'

,It's a human. Can I eat it? If I eat humans, humans get angry.'

,You can eat it. I allow it.'

Ao Oni's lips tore open, forming a gruesome smile. Ao Oni comes to me. The ground rumbles with every step.

,A monster I've never seen before. No, is it not a monster?'

I swung my hand at Ao Oni. A blade of wind filled with mana flew towards Ao Oni and collided.

,Hehe. It tickles.'

The blade of wind only left a slight scratch on Ao Oni's body and scattered. Even that scratch healed in the blink of an eye.

,Ao Oni. Most attacks don't work. His recovery is also amazing. The fog moves with every move Ao Oni makes. He doesn't exactly control the fog, but... Ao Oni's most powerful weapon is his immense strength. That's it.'

Yura said from behind. She had more information than I thought.

Thump thump.

Ao Oni, who had come close, swung his gold-banded cudgel.

[Using Ephemeral. Remaining stacks: 11]

Since there was no need to be reckless as Yura warned, I used Ephemeral. As soon as I stepped back, a powerful gust of wind rushed in and broke the surrounding trees.

,If we're just talking about strength, he's stronger than me. If I got hit head-on, I wouldn't even be able to collect my bones.'

Ao Oni wasn't slow either. Strength is speed. It doesn't make sense to have immense strength and be slow.

,I dodged it! You can't dodge it this time!'

Ao Oni swung his gold-banded cudgel again. He was swinging it lightly, but the wind pressure it generated was terrifying.

,He's not someone an A-class can handle. And this guy listens to someone weaker than him?'

There was something I didn't know.

I dodged Ao Oni's attacks while compressing and gathering wind in my palm.

[Using Ephemeral. Remaining stacks: 11]

Using Ephemeral, I broke through and slammed the compressed wind into Ao Oni's torso.

Kwaaaang!

The compressed wind exploded. Ao Oni took a few steps back from the impact. That was all.

,That attack would have killed an A-class without leaving a trace... and all it did was push him back a little?'

I'm getting a sense of things.

,It's impossible to kill Ao Oni using only the power of wind right now.'

I couldn't handle the wind like lightning. It was hard to control, and it was impossible to convert the wind's attribute.

,Cheonma Shingong. I can use it if I want to... but this is reality. There's no Heavenly Strength System or Atlantis System buff. Even if I use it, it won't be as efficient as Lightning Heaven Style. I'm a little reluctant to use it.'

Yura was behind me.

I hesitated for a moment. There was no proof that I was Seong Yujin just because I used Lightning Heaven Style.

,I can just deny it. What can they do if I say I'm not?'

So I decided to just use it.

,Aaaargh! I don't like this human! Don't dodge! Don't dodge!!!'

Ao Oni got angry because I kept dodging his attacks and started swinging his gold-banded cudgel with both hands. The ground cracked when the gold-banded cudgel hit the ground, causing a slight earthquake. The wind pressure from the gold-banded cudgel was so strong that it was a little overwhelming even for me.

But since there was nothing technical about it, it was easy to dodge the gold-banded cudgel.

[Using Ephemeral. Remaining stacks: 10]

I used Ephemeral to close the distance in an instant. The gold-banded cudgel was falling towards my head. I climbed up Ao Oni's arm and gathered strength, clenching my fist.

Lightning Heaven Style Thunder Wave Strike.

Paji jik.

My fist wrapped in lightning struck Ao Oni's head. Ao Oni's head caved in and he fell backward.

,Ao Oni!!'

Ken shouted as if in despair. This blue monster was his last hope. I narrowed my eyes and clenched my fist again.

,He's not dead. He's still alive even though his head is caved in. His brain must be shattered, but he's recovering. What an amazing endurance.'

Twitch.

Ao Oni's limbs trembled. He was already trying to recover.

I raised my right foot above Ao Oni's head.

Thunder Wave Strike isn't exactly a technique for fists. It's a technique that concentrates all the power of the body into one point and cuts off the opponent's flow. The important thing isn't the amount of power, but the subtlety of the technique. Thanks to learning it in the [Bright Light Ascension] world and being influenced by the characteristics of Lightning Heaven Style, I was able to use it perfectly with my feet.

Lightning Heaven Style Thunder Wave Strike.

Kwaang!

A shockwave erupted from my foot and swept in all directions. Even so, Ao Oni still didn't die.

,I'll keep stomping until you die.'

Paji jik.

Lightning flashed around my right foot.

Kwaang! Kwaaang! Kwaaaang!

After stomping on him several times in a row, Ao Oni finally met his death. His head was completely shattered, so even with his excellent regenerative power, he couldn't regenerate.

,It might have been easier if I had cut off his head.'

I deliberately didn't take out my sword. If I used a sword, I'd be too much like Seong Yujin.

,......'

Ken, who was watching, tried to run away like before.

,No way.'

Unlike before, I wasn't careless. I grabbed Ken, who was trying to escape, and broke his arms and legs. I even knocked him unconscious so he couldn't commit suicide.

,...Amazing. I didn't think you'd catch Ao Oni so easily.'

,Ao Oni. What's that? It's a monster I've never seen before. If we're just talking about physical specs, he's an S-class, but he acts like a moron.'

,Ao Oni. A monster that Japanese Onmyojis sealed in Shinto shrines a long time ago. A shrine where Ao Oni was sealed was stolen a few years ago... I didn't know Ken had it.'

,Was finding that shrine your goal?'

,I told you, I didn't know. Chasing the shrine is the special forces' job. I have other business with Ken.'

,I captured Ken alive as you wanted.'

,I know. I saw it with my own eyes. It was an absurd amount of strength. I'll keep my promise, so don't worry. You're not in good condition right now, so let's meet again tomorrow.'

She took a small piece of paper out of her pocket and threw it to me. I took the paper and checked it. It had a Japanese city, a hotel name, and a hotel room number written on it.

,Come there tomorrow night. I'll be waiting.'

Yura grabbed the back of the unconscious Ken's neck with her right hand, grabbed Ao Oni's arm with her left hand, and dragged them away. Her right arm was clearly broken, but she was using her arm without showing any pain.

,It's not bad to do it at a hotel. Well, she wouldn't try to screw me over, would she.'

At that time, I might have become Red Light and terrorized the World Hunter Association.

Well, that was only if Yura screwed me over.

I went back to where Park Jeong-gu was.

,There was fog in the mountains and a loud noise earlier. Was that because of you?'

,The situation is over. Yura took Ken away. I also killed the remaining executives of Kamaise.'

I didn't say anything about Ao Oni. It was annoying to explain everything. It wasn't information that Park Jeong-gu needed to know.

,Hoo. So it's resolved anyway. It was a long day.'

,What are you going to do? I'm telling you in advance, don't go into hiding.'

,I'm not going into hiding. I need to move my base first.'

,Where to?'

,I was going to go to America at first... but the existing brokers there have a strong hold. There are also quite a few Black Guilds. So I'm going to do what I've been doing. Let's meet in Korea.'

,Are you leaving right now? Did you get the money?'

,I just got it. The World Hunter Association's processing is quick.'

Park Jeong-gu chuckled and showed me a message on his smartphone. 1 billion won was deposited immediately.

,But apart from being fast, isn't 1 billion won too little?'

,Nonsense. You didn't do anything. What did you do besides stand still? I did everything.'

,......I can't argue with that. The World Hunter Association must have been watching your face and processed the work quickly. Should I split it in half?'

,No need.'

There was no reason to be attached to just 1 billion won.

Rather, I was looking forward to tomorrow night.
