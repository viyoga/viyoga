I knelt before the Emperor seated on the throne.

,Thunderbolt General, listen. Currently, a war with monsters is raging in Jimang Fortress, the northernmost and foremost frontier of the Empire. Three months ago, General Mujin, who guarded the north, was attacked and killed by monsters. Since then, the war has subsided, but the Six-Horned Demon King's monster army has invaded again. Therefore, Jimang Fortress is currently in a precarious state, on the verge of falling."

The Emperor continued.

,I issue an Imperial Edict to the Thunderbolt General. With the help of the Imperial Family High Priest, the General shall move to the north and save the Northern Army from its crisis. Furthermore, the General shall rally the Northern Army, slay the Six-Horned Demon King, and demonstrate the Empire's majesty."

,I shall obey the Imperial Edict."

I withdrew from the audience chamber. A man with a pale face and a slender physique stood in front of the audience chamber. It was Shin Yugo, the current Imperial Family High Priest. His cultivation was at the 9th stage of the Three Flowers Gathering realm.

,Thunderbolt General-nim. I will guide you to the Transfer Platform."

Shin Yugo was the Imperial Family High Priest and held a higher rank than me as a First Rank Official, so I couldn't treat him carelessly.

,Understood."

I quietly followed behind him. Shin Yugo took out a small wooden box from his robes and handed it to me. When I opened the box, there was a golden elixir inside.

,An elixir with a thunderous energy."

,It is the Byeokgeumdan (Thunderbolt Gold Elixir). His Majesty has bestowed it upon the General unofficially. It is not very helpful for cultivation, but it can temporarily grant immense thunderous energy. It will be of great help in battle."

,Since I'm receiving it unofficially, I can't even offer my thanks to His Majesty."

It was difficult to figure out the manufacturing method through the Palguk Alchemy Furnace. Milyeong was currently using the Palguk Alchemy Furnace.

,If the General accumulates merit, His Majesty will be pleased."

It didn't seem like I could acquire it later, so I wasn't too disappointed.

I asked him various questions while walking.

,Do you happen to know the head of the Sima Clan?"

,Are you referring to the previous Imperial Family High Priest? I can say that I know him. The only connection I have is when I met him during the handover."

,I don't know why the Sima Clan Head stepped down from the position of Imperial Family High Priest and retired... Can you tell me?"

If this bastard used political maneuvering to seize the position of Imperial Family High Priest, I would immediately become hostile. I was planning to stab him in the back and kill him when I got the chance. The Emperor? My woman, Sima Milyeong's, revenge was more important than that bastard.

,I understand that he stepped down from the position of Imperial Family High Priest for the sake of cultivation. He had more outstanding talent than me and was looking at higher places."

,Was there anything bad that happened to the Sima Clan Head?"

,Nothing happened. You can check the records. There's a rumor circulating that the General and Sima Clan Head-nim are particularly close... It seems to be true."

,Well, we have some connection."

I thought of Sima Milyeong. She was a woman with outstanding perseverance. At the same time, she was a capable woman. Even if I took the initiative to ask her to join me, she would probably refuse.

We arrived at the Transfer Platform. I headed towards the center of the Transfer Platform, watching the busy mages.

,Because the monsters are interfering with the transfer magic with their sorcery, you cannot transfer directly to Jimang Fortress. Therefore, the General will be transferred to Haeum Fortress, which is behind Jimang Fortress. It is located fifty li away from Jimang Fortress."

How many kilometers is fifty li? Should I ask Shin Yugo? I decided against it. He wouldn't know what kilometers were.

,Understood. Begin the transfer magic."

,Yes, General. Good luck."

I moved to Haeum Fortress through the Transfer Platform.

The scenery changes. The mages of the Imperial Palace and the splendid Transfer Platform were replaced by a slightly old Transfer Platform and three armed officers.

,General! The situation is urgent! Jimang Fortress is on the verge of falling!"

,General! The soldiers of Haeum Fortress are already supporting Jimang Fortress! It will take time to gather soldiers from other fortresses!"

,You must make a decision! General!"

,Get out of the way."

I pushed them aside and moved forward. As I exited the Transfer Platform, the cool wind of the north greeted me.

,Using the light of the Heavenly River Star."

,Heavenly Leader Star (Tiankui)."

Thunderbolt Heavenly Flow (雷天流) Thunder Art (雷術) Thunder Eye Golden Gaze (雷眼金睛).

Thunderous light dwelled in both eyes.

I naturally turned my head to the north, which I had come to know. Jimang Fortress, located over fifty li away, can be seen. It is withstanding the fierce attacks of the monster army. But it is precarious. Soon, the fortress gates and the barrier will collapse.

,Cloud Command."

Unusually dark clouds were created at my feet. I swallowed the Byeokgeumdan (Thunderbolt Gold Elixir) I had just received and headed towards Jimang Fortress on the dark clouds.

• • •

Kung!

The fortress gates shake from the monsters' attacks. The soldiers standing on the fortress walls bit their lips, held their bows, and shot arrows. But it was not enough. There were too many monsters. Even if they killed dozens of monsters, there was no sign of them decreasing.

Kung!

The fortress gates shake again. A monster with a body as large as the fortress gates was pounding on them. The monsters around it were attacking the fortress walls with their respective sorcery.

The fortress gates, the fortress walls, and even the invisible barrier installed here. Everything was shaking.

,Company Commander On! The fortress gates and the barrier are at their limit! The monster bastards will soon break through!!"

An officer shouted. Company Commander On Gaejung raised his voice and commanded the soldiers.

,Maintain the formation! There is no surrender! Thunderbolt General will soon arrive with reinforcements! Hold out until then! If we hold out, we win!!"

On Gaejung shouted words that he himself could not believe.

Thunderbolt General. A general who was appointed as the general of the Northern Army by the Emperor about three weeks ago. Nevertheless, a general who has not even shown his face in the north.

Countless rumors circulated. The Thunderbolt General was the Emperor's illegitimate child and obtained the position of general through corruption. The Emperor had decided to abandon the north.

On Gaejung did not believe such rumors. He believed in the Emperor. If the Emperor appointed him as a general, there must be a reason.

,I noticed the signs of war and sent a messenger a week ago! I even urged the sorcerers to contact the Imperial Palace directly!"

And what he heard was despairing. The Thunderbolt General was still in the Imperial Palace. Even if the Thunderbolt General came to Eumhae Fortress in the rear using transfer magic, it would take at least half a day to support Jimang Fortress.

And by that time, Jimang Fortress would already be in ruins. The soldiers and the people would fall as food for the monsters.

Kwaaaang!

With a roar, the fortress gates were destroyed. The barrier that protected the fortress also disappeared. Monsters that could fly soared over the fortress, and monsters jumped from their spots and climbed onto the fortress walls. Soldiers and monsters mixed and crossed weapons. Melee combat began.

On Gaejung glared at the monster in the rear of the monster army, beyond the broken fortress gates. It had the body of a human and the head of a wolf. It had two horns on its head.

Two-Horned Heavy Wolf (二角重狼). A disciple of the Six-Horned Demon King.

The Two-Horned Heavy Wolf knew that there was no general now and was enjoying the war. If the Two-Horned Heavy Wolf had come forward earlier, Jimang Fortress would have fallen two days ago.

,If only General Mujin were still alive, things wouldn't have been pushed back this far!"

On Gaejung gritted his teeth. He struggled to turn his gaze away. What was important now was not the Two-Horned Heavy Wolf, but the giant coming straight in. The monster, who had pounded and destroyed the fortress gates, was approaching, crushing the soldiers with its fists and feet.

The monster's cultivation was at the early stage of the Three Flowers Gathering realm. If Company Commander On Gaejung did not step forward himself, many soldiers would die. If it were just the giant monster alone, the soldiers could have subdued it sufficiently, but now there were monster soldiers rampaging around that monster.

,I'd rather fight and die than surrender!"

It was when On Gaejung was determined and grabbed his sword.

A giant thunderbolt spear that fell from the sky pierced through the torso of the giant monster and was embedded in the ground. When the giant monster, who had lost its heart, staggered and fell, the thunderbolt spear slowly swelled and exploded as it was. Hundreds of condensed thunderbolts swept through the battlefield as it was. Strangely, the aftermath only headed in the direction where the monsters were.

Not only the giant monster's corpse, but also hundreds of monsters around it were burned to death.

Kurururu....

Along with the sound of thunder coming from somewhere, the world began to darken. Humans and monsters instinctively looked up at the sky at the sudden anomaly.

The dark clouds that came from the rear of the fortress were covering the sky and multiplying. Strangely, it did not rain.

The dark clouds completely covered the sky, but the ground was not dark. Rather, it was bright.

Kwareureung, kwareureureung!

The thunderous light flashing in the dark clouds was illuminating the ground.

And they discovered it. A man standing on a small dark cloud below those enormous dark clouds.

He wore a helmet with two horns and a mountain pattern armor with a white cloud dragon engraved on it.

He held a golden vajra in his left hand and a pure white trident in his right hand.

He looked down at the battlefield with golden eyes containing thunder.

He was the Thunderbolt General.
