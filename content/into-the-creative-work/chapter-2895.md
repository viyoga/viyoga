As I got closer to the summit of Mont Blanc, I could sense the presence of witches concealing themselves with magic and flying. And then, at some point, their presence completely disappeared. It was as if they had entered an invisible world.

Those witches didn't give us much attention.

,There are fewer witches moving around than I thought?

Marta murmured.

,We're late. There's an invisible spatial barrier ahead. Can you feel it?

,...No. Not at all. What about you?

,Of course I can't feel it.

It was a spatial barrier set up by a 9th-grade witch with determination. It was impossible for me, only a 7th-grade wizard, to detect it.

,Unless I take my time to analyze it carefully. But I don't have time for that now.

We concealed our presence like the other witches and flew towards the sky.

The moment I sensed the mana density in the atmosphere changing, Marta and I were standing on the ground.

If the mana density in the environment changes drastically, most ordinary people will faint, and even those of higher grades will feel dizziness. However, I only felt a slight sense of incongruity and quickly adapted. It was thanks to my high mana affinity.

I glanced at Marta, and she was frowning. She seemed to be feeling dizzy. But that was only for a moment. She looked around, and a gasp escaped her lips.

,I thought the witches' festival would be full of scattered blood and flesh... but it's a beautiful village like something out of a fairy tale. And the houses are all so unique.

There were many houses with unique designs, such as paths neatly arranged with bricks, houses built of wood, houses shaped like wings, and houses shaped like brooms. There were many houses with sharply pointed roofs.

Looking up, there was a starry night. There was a night sky that recreated Van Gogh's 'The Starry Night'.

,The developer explained that they referenced Van Gogh's 'The Starry Night'.

There were witches setting up stalls around the streets. Witches wearing black cloaks wandered around the village. As befitting a festival, there were delicious smells and it was bustling. Of course, everyone was a woman except for me. Old people and children were all women.

,Mom! Is that person a witch too?

,...He's not a witch. He's a man. How can a man...?!

Everyone's eyes turn to me.

Witches who are born with mana from birth. Even children who are not even 10 years old can handle mana, they are demon-kin. Their sharp gazes were fixed on me.

Unlike Marta, who was nervous, I calmly accepted their gazes.

,How did a man get in here?

,Isn't there a problem with the Great Witch-nim's barrier?

,Don't doubt the Great Witch-nim. Ah, I can feel it. He has a token.

,A token? A legitimate guest? It's the first time a human has participated in Walpurgis Night as a guest.

,What stupid witch gave a token to a human man? Let's see.... Olen, Deneb, Meme...? I don't know about Olen, but if it's Deneb and Meme.... You shouldn't mess with them carelessly.

,Deneb is a crazy bitch... and Meme is scary when she's angry. And I got some help from Meme, so I'll say it on my name. Even though he's a human man, he's a guest. Don't touch him.

,Why did they give the token to a human man? Where are they?

,I don't know. Olen isn't participating in this Walpurgis Night.

,...I've never seen the purple-haired witch next to him before. Who is she? Does anyone know?

,I don't know. I've never seen her before either. Her aura is unusual. She seems to be at least a 7th grade or higher... Just what kind of witch is she? Is she a witch who has been in seclusion? The Great Witch-nim must know, right?

,It's the Great Witch-nim's barrier. There's no way the Great Witch-nim wouldn't know.

Even though they were whispering about us, few witches showed hostility. It was thanks to receiving the token of a strong witch. Witches with power above 7th grade are rare.

I walked forward. Then the witches flinched in surprise and stepped back, wary. I didn't hide the fact that I was a 7th-grade wizard.

,Wait. Isn't that man Eugene Meyer?

,Eugene Meyer? Who's that? Is he a celebrity?

,Don't you watch the news? He's the president of LK Corporation, which produces superconductors. A rising star of Neo London...!

,President? He must have a lot of money?

Hostility disappears.

That's why it's quite convenient when fame spreads. They lower their heads on their own after seeing the fame. Even wariness disappears. Even now, hostile gazes have decreased.

,So. What are we supposed to do?

Marta asks. When she scans the surroundings with a sharp look, the surprised witches turn their gazes away.

,For now, just enjoy this festival. Looking around the stalls is also a job. Witches also use credits, so buy what you need. You might be able to find magical tools or artifacts that are hard to find on the market.

,I don't need witches' stuff.

Marta said sharply.

Witches are born with black mana organs as demon-kin, but they can also use ordinary mana. So there are quite a few magical tools made with ordinary magic, not black magic.

,They're good quality magical tools, but there aren't any that are worth my attention as a 7th-grade wizard.

I stopped in front of a stall selling food.

,Ugh....

They were selling frogs skewered whole on skewers. Marta takes a step back.

,Frog candy.

,Oh. You're human, but you know it well.

The old woman who owned the stall chuckled.

Unlike the realistic frog shape, it's candy. In the original game, eating it speeds up casting speed by 20% for 10 minutes. The taste is said to be kiwi.

,I'll buy two.

,1 million credits.

Wasn't it 50,000 credits each? She's blatantly ripping me off. I stared at the old woman for a moment.

,Don't be stingy, you rich bastard.

,Just because I have a lot of money doesn't mean I have to be a sucker on purpose.

I turned away without hesitation. The old woman shrugged as if she didn't care. This kind of situation happened every time I visited a stall.

,Should I say it's like witches? They're trying to rip me off no matter what. Isn't this a kind of turf war?

,Rather than a turf war, it's probably because the word got around that I have a lot of money. They're trying to make a quick buck. Anyway, we won't see each other again.

,Ha, I still don't like witches.

As we were looking around the stalls, we found a store where witches were particularly crowded. It was a store that had brought an entire carriage.

,A bookstore?

,It's a magic tome store.

I naturally headed to the magic tome store. When I approached, the witches who were crowded there were startled and stepped back.

The owner of the store is a middle-aged witch with thick lips. Wearing a black mermaid dress, she smiles elegantly.

,An outside guest. That's rare. My store only sells one magic tome per person. What's written in the magic tome is one spell.

,What's the price?

,7th-grade magic is 100 million credits.

It's the same price as in the original game. For reference, you can learn one magic skill in the original game. An opportunity to get 7th-grade magic for only 100 million credits. It's a store you must visit when you come to Walpurgis Night.

,However, there's a condition. You can only buy magic that you can currently learn. Put your hand on the table over there and let your mana flow. The magic tomes in the store will react. My store itself is such an artifact.

,I understand. What's your name?

,Nora. I'm called the Witch of the White Book.

,There seem to be over 1,000 books in the store at a glance.

,I couldn't even collect 100 books in the old days.

I put my hand on the table and let my mana flow. Then, the books on the bookshelf in the store all rattled and floated up. Nora, who had been relaxed, and the surrounding witches' eyes widened.

,...All the magic tomes came out? That means... you have aptitude for all attributes and systems.... Ha. This is the first time I've seen such monstrous talent. There's a reason why you're called a rising star of Neo London.

,Do you have arcane magic?

,I don't deal with arcane magic. That's a bit too dangerous.

I understand. If there was arcane magic, even the same witch would show greed. I asked without much expectation.

,I don't need magic below 6th grade.

,I like that you're straightforward.

Snap.

When she snapped her fingers, the magic tomes except for 13 books were put back on the bookshelf.

,You can only choose one.

,They're manuscripts. Aren't you going to sell them all?

,No. This is my belief, and it's related to my quirk.

I quickly scanned the magic tomes. 3 books were magic I knew. Thunder Break, Hell Fire, Freezing. Besides that, I'm tempted by the representative magic of the attribute system.

But my choice was already decided before I arrived at this store.

,I'll go with this one.

7th-grade magic, Time Lock.

,Hmm. You've chosen a difficult magic. But you have the aptitude, so you can learn it if you try. Ah, you know it's not a magic for practical use, right?

,I know. It's too complicated to use in practice.

The average casting speed is more than 15 times faster than Thunder Break. It's impossible to use in practice without a special method.

In the game, it's a skill with a long cooldown that can be used with just one click.

The effect is to stop the time of the target for a short time. And because time doesn't flow, you can't directly interfere with the target. Also, the time you can stop varies depending on the opponent, and it doesn't work if the magic resistance is high.

,That target includes yourself. It can be said to be a kind of invincibility skill. But it can't block instant death skills.

Due to its special nature, time-based magic cannot be stored even with memorize magic. But I had Meltes' ring, which was far superior to memorize.

,Even if you learn Time Lock, don't be too arrogant. A power strong enough to distort space-time can forcibly shatter Time Lock.

,Okay. Thanks for the advice. Here's 100 million credits.

After paying, I moved to a secluded place with Marta.

I got a new magic, how can I stand it? I have to read it and use it right away.
