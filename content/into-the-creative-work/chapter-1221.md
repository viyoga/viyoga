Seon Family Estate.

The air in the reception room was heavy after Yeom Guseok left.

Seon Haedo, the head of the Seon Family, stared into space amidst the heavy silence. His mind replayed the image of the man who had just declared war on him and stormed out of the Seon Family Estate.

Yeom Guseok's level, as directly perceived by Seon Haedo, was Stage 2 of Chulji. And Seon Haedo's level was Stage 2 of Ogi. A completely different dimension. If Seon Haedo and Yeom Guseok fought barehanded, Seon Haedo was confident he could defeat Yeom Guseok within three exchanges. If Seon Haedo went all out from the start, it wouldn't be strange to end it within one exchange.

But the Yeom Guseok he had just seen was different. He didn't feel like he could kill him instantly, like squashing a bug.

Seon Haedo was rather overwhelmed by Yeom Guseok.

That dense murderous intent was not something an average Joe could emit. It was that of someone who had killed thousands... no, tens of thousands.

'...What's more dangerous is Yeom Guseok's Demonic Art.'

Seon Haedo clearly saw the jet-black Demonic energy flowing from Yeom Guseok's body. The moment he saw that Demonic energy, Seon Haedo felt instinctive fear.

'To feel fear and tremble at a Chulji Martial artist... how shameful.'

He thought Yeom Guseok's Demonic Art was special.

The Demonic Art of Demonic Arts. A bizarre martial art that was hard to even call a martial art. For example, a Demonic Art that allows one to grow stronger quickly by eating human flesh.

'Either way, that bastard is dangerous. He's a Heavenly Demon Church member who has reached the Chulji realm despite not having completed the Ipmaso. His talent is real, no matter what anyone says. If time passes... the Seon Family Estate could really be in danger.'

Sakjugulgeun (Stump Digging and Root Excavation)

Those four characters wouldn't leave Seon Haedo's head.

He breathed deeply in the empty reception room for a while to calm his trembling body, then shouted outside.

"Is anyone there?!"

"Yes, Gaju-nim!"

The escort warrior’s resounding reply came back. In reality, he was more like someone who helped with tasks rather than an escort warrior. In modern terms, he was a personal secretary.

"I need to meet with Chujeo-gong and talk. Bring Chujeo-gong to the reception room right away."

"Yes, Gaju-nim!"

Not long after, the reception room door opened and a young man entered. This man, who looked to be just past his coming-of-age ceremony, was Chujeo, Yeom Guseok's mission target.

Don't be fooled by appearances and underestimate him. Chujeo was actually nearly 90 years old. He was keeping his youthful look with plastic surgery that alters his appearance. Of course, plastic surgery isn't immortality. It's just a type of technique (sulbeop) that changes how you look. As time goes by, he still ages, so he needs to have plastic surgery from time to time if he wants to stay young.

For reference, Martial artists who have reached a certain level cannot be judged by their appearance alone.

There are many different ways to stay youthful in this world. A well-known one is sorcery, and there are also martial arts techniques that prevent aging. Seeking youth through mystical elixirs is another method.

And there are those who naturally stay youthful without any magical techniques, martial arts, or elixirs. This is because a master's body reflects the state of the mind. If the mind appears young, the body will also be influenced and retain its youth.

However, as time passes, not only the body but also the mind ages. That was the way of the world. Maintaining a young mind while living for decades or hundreds of years was difficult even for enlightened monks.

"Sit down."

Chujeo sat in the chair that Yeom Guseok had sat in. He looked behind Seon Haedo. There was a wall stained with green tea. Below it, shattered pieces of a teacup were scattered.

"You've already heard that someone came to the Seon Family Estate, haven't you?"

"I heard that a Heavenly Demon Church Martial artist came to kill me. And I heard that Gaju-nim drove him away. Thank you."

"The Seon Family Estate doesn't easily cast out guests or retainers. You've worked hard for the Seon Family Estate; do you think I would discard you like an old shoe?"

"This Chujeo feels deeply indebted to Gaju-nim! I will repay this favor with my life!"

"Indeed, my choice was not wrong."

Seon Haedo, who had been smiling contentedly, soon erased his expression and said.

"But the matter is not over. That bastard will try to kill you. He's from Ipmaso, and the mission he received is to kill you."

"...From Ipmaso. Considering the timing, it must be the third test. He has to return to Ipmaso, so if I just hold out for about ten days, it will be over."

"Holding out won't do."

Seon Haedo said firmly. At his serious and determined voice, Chujeo quietly waited for him to speak.

"That bastard is not ordinary. If we leave him alone like this, not only you but also the Seon Family Estate could be in danger. Sakjugulgeun... we must finish this while he's staying in Haga City. I want to step in and kill him myself, but... I am the head of the Seon Family Estate. If I step in directly, we might have to fight a life-or-death battle with the Heavenly Demon Church."

"I will follow Gaju's will. I will step in and kill him myself."

"It will be difficult for you alone."

"Please trust me. I want to prove my sincerity to Gaju."

"I trust you. But you know better than I that the Heavenly Demon Church is not an easy opponent, right? That bastard is the Heavenly Demon Church's dagger. Sharper than anything, more powerful than anything."

"......Hoo. That's right. The Heavenly Demon Church. I underestimated those bastards for a moment. Gaju, lend me the Seongeongdae."

Seongeongdae (Vanguard Unit).

The best military unit of the Seon Family Estate, consisting of 30 Martial artists. The elite of the elite. Seongeongdae members have at least Stage 5 of Ipsik.

"I'm sorry, but the Seon Family Estate cannot directly intervene in this matter. Please understand my position."

"...I understand. Even for the Seon Family Estate, it would be burdensome... But what other way is there...."

"Chujeo-gong. Listen to my plan. I'm thinking of using the Byeongsaldan."

Byeongsaldan (竝殺團) is a Salmun (殺問) that operates in and around Haga City. In other words, it is an assassin group. They make a living by killing people for money.

"Will the Byeongsaldan take the request? Even if those bastards are crazy about money, they wouldn't be stupid enough to turn the Heavenly Demon Church into an enemy."

"That bastard's situation is special right now. He received a mission from the Heavenly Demon Church, but he can't use the name of the Heavenly Demon Church."

"...The third test of Ipmaso...!"

"That's right. And I'm not planning to use those bastards to kill him. You have to be the one to kill him. The Byeongsaldan will torment him. Very severely."

"......So, my job is to kill him in a weakened state. Because if I kill him, the Heavenly Demon Church won't be able to directly say anything to the Seon Family Estate."

Seon Haedo took a brief breath. He distorted his face and feigned distress.

"......My heart is torn to pieces having to say this to you, but... once this is over...."

"You don't need to make such a painful expression. I will kill him and leave Haga City. Thank you for taking me in all this time."

Chujeo understood his situation.

Now that the Heavenly Demon Church had found him, he couldn't stay at the Seon Family Estate any longer. He praised Seon Haedo in words, but in reality, he couldn't trust Seon Haedo.

Seon Haedo was the head of the Seon Family Estate. Contrary to what the common people believed, he was not a righteous and honorable Daehyeop (大俠). If the Heavenly Demon Church officially demanded Chujeo, Seon Haedo would not hesitate to hand Chujeo over to them. If they ordered him to kill Chujeo, he would do it.

For Chujeo, the best option was to leave while maintaining a good relationship with the Seon Family Estate.

"Where are you planning to go after this is over?"

"I'm planning to go south, where the Heavenly Demon Church has no influence. I was planning to leave from the beginning."

"Have you saved enough money?"

"Thanks to you, I've saved a lot of money, but... it's a bit short."

"Don't worry. I'll give you plenty of travel expenses."

"Thank you, Daehyeop! I will never forget this favor!"

"Hoo. I have a lot to do. I'll get up now."

I enjoyed boating in Boiru in the afternoon and went to another Giru at night. It was a city I hadn't visited in a long time. I was curious about what the other Giru was like. I had to experience the Ginyo of the other Giru at least once.

I got a recommendation for another Giru from Boiru. The Giru in Haga City maintain cooperation rather than competing with each other. In modern terms, it was a corporate cartel. Of course, it was illegal in modern times, but not so much in this world. There were few who cracked down on it.

'In the first place, this world is not a world where the emperor manages the city by employing officials... but a world where the city is managed using Murim sects. A cartel between stores is just a minor thing.'

It wasn't that the emperor was incompetent. It was quite the opposite. The emperor was competent. The reason he implemented this management method was because his territory, the continent, was too vast. There were over thousands of cities in this continent, and the population was in the tens of billions.

Compared to that enormous scale, the level of civilization was close to the Middle Ages. Occasionally, over-technology appeared, but that was really rare.

'This is a world without computers. It's impossible for the emperor to manage the entire continent alone. Well, it's not like the emperor created this system of using Murim sects.'

Originally, Murim sects had been managing the regions. The emperor unified the continent with overwhelming force and brought the Murim under his control. The Murim had to follow the emperor's laws and pay taxes to the emperor.

I was walking down the street thinking about this and that when I stopped. Today was a day when the moon didn't rise. It was dark all around. Not even the sound of insects could be heard. I could see the lights coming from the Giru far away, but it was too far.

"......"

Swaeeeeek.

A dull-glowing dagger flies from the darkness. It's hard to see because it's mixed in with the darkness. But the sound of tearing through the wind revealed its location.

I drew my sword from my waist and knocked the dagger upwards. The dagger spun in the air and fell to the ground. I kicked the fallen dagger as if to vent my anger and stared straight into the darkness.

[Opening the Cheonan (Heavenly Eye).]

I can see the bastards hiding in the darkness.

They are all hiding their faces with black clothes and masks. But there's something the bastards don't know. Cheonan allows for X-ray vision unless it's blocked by 술법 (sulbeop) or something. I check their faces. They are all faces I don't know.

'There are only six I can see. Their skill at hiding their presence is excellent. They're not Martial artists from the Seon Family Estate, but assassins.'

It's obvious who hired the assassins and sent them to me. Seon Haedo.

'You can't step in directly because of the Heavenly Demon Church, so you're coming out like this?'

I gripped the sword hilt tightly. Thinking of Seon Haedo made me angry. Fortunately, there was an object to vent my anger on right in front of me.

'I'll kill them all...!'

I ran towards the assassins.
