,"Are you saying you summoned my inner demons?! That's impossible!"

The Demonic One shouted as if having a seizure.

At that, I raised my right foot. The Demonic One flinched and stepped back.

I didn't step with the Cheonma Gunrimbo. Instead, I controlled my breathing.

Feeling embarrassed, the Demonic One blushed and forcibly raised himself up. He was about to unleash his killing intent and use his power. An overwhelming power that even I couldn't ignore.

However.

,"Kulk...!"

The Demonic One vomited blood.

His momentum faltered. With inner demons taking hold, his will couldn't properly manifest. Even with overwhelming power, he couldn't use it properly. Rather, it gnawed at his own lifeblood and spirit.

The Demonic One urgently waved his hand in the air. The Cheonma Seongjwa, which had been far away, flew over and supported his body. The Demonic One's complexion improved slightly.

,"The effect is good. To think he'd be this vulnerable to inner demons even though his cultivation is higher than mine. So, he never completely overcame his inner demons from the start?"

,"Damn it.... What the hell did you do to the Cheonma Gunrimbo? The Cheonma Gunrimbo doesn't have the power to forcibly awaken inner demons...!"

,"My Cheonma Gunrimbo does."

The Demonic One widened his eyes and moved his power. Blood streamed from his mouth and nose. He was enduring the twisting of his lifeblood and forcibly using his power.

He held his sword while sitting on the Cheonma Seongjwa. His immense demonic energy gathered in the Cheonma Seonggeom. Even the Cheonma Seonggeom's ability to manipulate mass was combined.

He was trying to use the secret technique of Cheonma Shingong, the Mageop (魔劫) Muryang (無量).

If that one sword were swung, it would be a strike capable of collapsing Mount Tai.

I stepped with Jingak.

Cheonma Shingong (天魔神功) Cheonma Gunrimbo (Heavenly Demon March).

Kung!

The shockwave of the Cheonma Gunrimbo shook the space and stimulated the Demonic One's inner demons.

,"Keoeok!"

The Demonic One couldn't withstand it and vomited blood. His hand fell weakly, and the Cheonma Seonggeom fell to the ground. The Demonic One's immense power gradually disappeared. If it weren't for the Cheonma Seongjwa, he would have fallen disgracefully to the ground.

The Demonic One's face looked decades older in an instant.

I approached that Demonic One.

,"The Cheonma-nim is in danger!! Kill the traitor, So Cheonma!!"

,"How dare you interfere with a sacred duel! Stop them!!"

A battle began on the ground, where they were focused on us. The elders, including the Great Elder who had confirmed the Demonic One's defeat, moved. Those who followed me moved to stop them.

,"Surrender! So Cheonma-nim has already won! So Cheonma-nim is the new Cheonma, the leader, the supreme one!"

,"Remember So Cheonma's cold-blooded nature! Do you think he, who even killed the leader, will forgive us?! If we don't kill So Cheonma, we will die!"

The Sword Demon, who had reached the end of the Samjeong, clashed with the Great Elder. Starting with him, a full-scale civil war erupted. If it weren't for the Sword Demon, my side would have been at a disadvantage.

I walked towards the Demonic One again. The Demonic One tried to move his trembling hand but stopped. He leaned deeply against the back of the Cheonma Seongjwa.

,"I've lost. Seong Yujin-ssi. You are the 22nd Cheonma."

,"Just because you acknowledge me doesn't mean I won't kill you. Only by killing you can all the problems of the Cheonma Shin-gyo be resolved."

,"I know. I have no intention of begging you for my life. I have no intention of living while fighting against these damn inner demons. Kill me."

I raised the Hwaryeon Bido. Pajijijik! Black lightning coiled around the blade.

Before I swung the knife, I asked him.

,"You must have a last resort. Why aren't you using it?"

Are you talking about the Pateonhwang (破天荒)? The Pateonhwang is not a technique that can be used carelessly. To use it, you need a Dharma treasure that will instead destroy your soul. The current Cheonma Shin-gyo doesn't have such a Dharma treasure.

I didn't use that Pateonhwang either, being wary of it.

If the Cheonma were to use the Pateonhwang at the last moment, the only way to counter it would be for me to use the Pateonhwang as well.

,"You're just going to die without even struggling? Like this?"

,"...You're mistaken about something. Ah, that's right. You don't know much about the origin of the Cheonma Shingong. The Cheonma Shingong is not a martial art created by humans. The first Cheonma was just lucky enough to learn the Cheonma Shingong. Do you know about reincarnation? All beings in this world fall into hell and are judged. Those who have learned the Cheonma Shingong must settle their karma after death. The Pateonhwang frees oneself from the pain of reincarnation by destroying one's own soul."

,"...So it's for suicide to avoid falling into hell, not for battle."

,"That's right. You could say it's the last mercy of the one who created the Cheonma Shingong. But among the past Cheonmas, there are only a handful who used the Pateonhwang at the last moment."

,"So why aren't you using the Pateonhwang?"

The Demonic One laughed softly. Then he vomited blood again.

,"You, who won't die even after using the Pateonhwang by some means, don't know. Eternal annihilation. How terrifying it is to have everything end. Even if you fall into hell, even if you settle all your karma in pain, it is human instinct to want to seize another chance."

I looked at the Demonic One with new eyes. I couldn't understand the Demonic One. There was a huge gap between me and him called Yuhee. I didn't particularly want to understand him either.

,"Surprisingly, the Blood Demon hasn't shown up until the end. Where is that bastard?"

,"That bastard is a coward. He's outside the Cheonma Shin-gyo. He's nearby, so kill me and go find him and kill him. He has killed tens of thousands of lives and is storing that life force. He's probably performing a human sacrifice right now. If you leave him alone, he will become a future threat to the Cheonma Shin-gyo."

,"The Blood Demon will follow you to hell."

The Demonic One quietly closed his eyes. His complexion was much more comfortable than before.

,"...The inner demons disappear only after accepting the end."

Seogeok!

I cut off the Demonic One's head.

The Demonic One's body fell to the ground and then suddenly flew away as if drawn in a certain direction.

Through the Noean Geumjeong, I knew that the Blood Demon was taking the Demonic One's corpse with magic.

,"Although I killed him, it's the Demonic One's corpse! Blood Demon! It's not something a bastard like you can covet!"

I shouted and flew in the direction the corpse was flying. My body creaked from the aftermath of the battle.

,'Blood Demon! As soon as I see you, I'll go into the Cho Cheonma state and subdue you!'

After that, I'll clean up the Cheonma Shin-gyo.

I, who was following the Demonic One's corpse, stopped in the air.

It wasn't just because I found the Blood Demon, who had killed his subordinates and disciples. The Blood Demon was holding up a splendid scepter and performing a sacrificial ritual.

,"Oh great being of hell! I offer 330,000 human souls and the corpse of the Cheonma! Kill my enemy!!"

The Blood Demon threw a jar from the altar. From the broken jar, 330,000 human souls were sacrificed to the underworld and disappeared.

Soon, a small hole appeared. A passage connected to the underworld. What popped out from there was a red-skinned right hand. I knew through the Noean Geumjeong that it was Chiwoo's hand.

,'Why is Chiwoo's right hand popping out from the underworld? Where did the main body go? It doesn't make sense for a bastard like the Blood Demon to summon Chiwoo....'

Chiwoo's right hand grabbed the Demonic One's headless corpse. Power settled in the Demonic One's corpse. The skin turned red, and a blood-colored right hand regenerated from the severed neck. It was a terribly grotesque power. But I could feel a power that could rule the world.

,"Hahahahahahaha! I succeeded!! You bastard, So Cheonma! I won't let you die peacefully! I'll make you taste all the pain!!"

The Blood Demon shouted, laughing heartily.

I remained silent.

There was no need to move directly. I felt the presence watching this place.

,"You stupid bastard. You've crossed the line."

I looked at the sky. Golden light gathered above the clouds and fell to the ground as it was.

A pillar of golden light crushed Chiwoo's right hand. The world was dyed golden, and everything that touched the golden pillar disappeared.

Gwangjeong Myeolgak (光井滅却).

I shuddered at the Emperor's power.

The Demonic One's corpse and the Dharma treasure, the Golden Thread Clothes, disappeared, but Chiwoo's right hand did not disappear. A being that has escaped death cannot be killed even by the Emperor's power.

-Kill that bastard.

The Emperor ordered.

He passed the final blow to me. It couldn't be helped. I'm the only one who can kill that thing in the middle realm.

『Using the light of Cheongangseong.』

Cheonsalseong (Heavenly Destruction Star)

The pillar of light falling from the sky disappeared. I approached Chiwoo's right hand, feeling the remaining heat. I executed death on the wriggling thing like a bug. Chiwoo's right hand disappeared.

I turned my head towards the Blood Demon. The bastard on the altar was trembling all over, looking back and forth between me and the sky.

,"T, the Emperor is moving directly...!"

I let out a burst of laughter, seeing that he had already lost his will to fight.

,"Your Majesty. I have quite a bit built up with this bastard. I'll kill him for sure, so please leave this bastard's disposal to me."

-...I permit it.

The Emperor's gaze disappeared.

I ran to the Blood Demon and swung my sword. The Blood Demon's legs were cut off.

,"Kuaaaaaak!!"

,"You can't even dodge it even though I didn't swing with all my might? What a waste of the Johwa Gyeong level. Well, it must be a level obtained through offerings in nine out of ten cases. Since your sin of touching my woman is deep, don't even think about dying easily."

,"N, not yet...!"

The Blood Demon tried to take out a Dharma artifact from his chest. My sword was faster. The Blood Demon, whose limbs were all cut off, didn't give up. Blood light shone from his eyes. I punched him in the head before he could use his power.

,"I can see everything you're trying to do to use your power."

,"Kuaaaak! You bastard!!!"

,"Let's get hit a little first."

I started beating the Blood Demon with divine excitement.

For Seong Yujin's growth.

Tak.
