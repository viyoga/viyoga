Eugene Meyer hosted a banquet.

The reason was to commemorate the disposal of the Droid's Shredder Bugs.

In Neo London, where Droids were considered mortal enemies, this was more than enough reason. He sent invitations to all the bigwigs of Neo London. He was entitled to do so.

Of course, the bigwigs of Neo London don't attend banquets just because they receive an invitation. Proportionally, more people politely declined the invitation. Some declined to keep Eugene Meyer, who was recently rising in popularity, in check, but there were also unavoidable circumstances.

In the case of Neo London's Queen, Victoria VI, she originally intended to decline under the pretext of poor health. It wasn't to keep Eugene Meyer in check. She doesn't move for just anything, let alone Eugene Meyer. She is the face of Neo London, so she refrains as much as possible.

But this time, she couldn't resist.

The first reason was that the name Eugene Meyer came to her ears several times a day, the second was that he was the master of Yuria Grace, who was likely to become a Prime-class Head maid, and the third was that Eugene Meyer was already eyeing the 8th class.

The carriage carrying the Queen drove through the streets of Neo London and arrived at Eugene Meyer's mansion.

What could be seen from the entrance was cherry blossoms in full bloom. Whenever a cool breeze blew, cherry blossom petals scattered in the wind. The Queen exclaimed in admiration.

,It's beautiful. Isn't it not the season for cherry blossoms to bloom?"

The black-haired Head maid, Florence Palmer, the only Prime-class Head maid and 8th class in this era, stood by the Queen's side with her usual expressionless face, looking around.

,That cherry tree is a Phantasmal World plant. It contains the power of lightning in itself. I heard it's in full bloom 365 days a year. The pink petals are very luscious. For your information, I'm also pink."

The Queen ignored the Head maid's unnecessarily added words. It's common for those who have stepped into the realm of superhumans to have a screw loose in one corner of their heads. Florence was in the top 10%.

The Queen looked straight ahead. Count Eugene Meyer and Yuria Grace were there.

,It's an honor to have you accept our invitation, Your Majesty."

,You got rid of the Droid's Shredder Bugs. You took care of the troublesome guy who smashed six Neo London outposts, so how could I, as Queen, stay still? I feel grateful to you, and I've prepared a personal gift as well. Florence."

Florence stepped forward. She took out a neatly packaged box from her subspace pocket. Yuria received it, and Eugene expressed his gratitude.

,Thank you, Your Majesty. May I ask what's inside the box?"

,Open it and see."

When the Queen smiled and urged him, Eugene opened the box. Inside the box was a folded golden silk. The silk shimmered subtly in the sunlight.

,This is... Sun Silk."

One of the rarest fabrics in this world. If you make clothes with this fabric, it will automatically repair itself unless it is completely torn, and the wearer will not be affected by cold or heat and will not get sick.

,I knew you'd recognize it right away. The royal family also has a hard time getting their hands on this precious item, so use it well. The dress I'm wearing now is also made of Sun Silk. I'll also let you know about a seamstress who can handle Sun Silk."

,Thank you, Your Majesty."

Eugene served the Queen with all his heart.

• • •

I guided the Queen to one of the private rooms I had prepared. Only then did I let out a sigh. It was beyond my expectations that the Queen would accept the invitation.

,Yuria. Thanks to you, we got through it well. I never thought the Queen would come as you predicted."

,I didn't completely predict it. I just prepared to be able to respond to any situation."

,Easy to say. Most people can't do that. It seems the Queen came without contacting the other royals separately."

Victoria VI is preparing for abdication. The problem is that the successor has not yet been decided and is in competition. Because of this, the Queen was avoiding contact with the royal family.

It was then. A Head maid was approaching quickly in the hallway.

,Master-nim. Duke Bennevis and Duke Snowden have arrived almost simultaneously. They parked their carriages from different directions."

The 1st and 2nd in line to the throne, the Princess and the Prince.

They got out of their carriages from different directions? They are candidates for the next King. As the host, it is natural to greet them.

,...This is fucked up. Depending on who I go out to greet, rumors will spread in the political world that I support someone. Is that what the Princess and the Prince are aiming for?"

,More than 90% of the time, that's the case. Master-nim is intentionally keeping a distance from the royal family... They must be desperate. Currently, the most influential nobleman in Neo London is Master-nim."

Considering not only the future value of superconductors but also my power, it can't be helped.

Personally, I wasn't very interested, and the Queen also asked me not to get involved. If I get involved in the succession war... I will turn the current Queen into an enemy.

Even if Victoria VI is a setting sun, what I need now is not the future but the present. My judgment is that I should focus on the present rather than the future.

,...Send a Head maid to each of them. Tell them I'm busy serving Her Majesty the Queen right now."

,Yes, Master-nim."

The Head maid left.

,That's a wise choice."

I heard a voice from behind. When I turned around, it was the royal Head maid Florence, not Yuria.

I was surprised that I didn't feel her presence at all. Did even Yuria miss it? When I glanced at Yuria, her face was calm, so it was hard to guess what she was thinking.

,Florence-ssi. Is there something you need?"

,No. I don't have anything to do... I came to see if I could help the Count-nim. Ah, I don't do lewd services."

,...Thank you for your words, but it's okay. I'll get a bad reputation if I use Florence-ssi, who is a guest. And isn't Florence-ssi Her Majesty the Queen's bodyguard?"

,If Her Majesty the Queen is assassinated... it's not my fault, it's the host's fault."

,That won't happen."

,Yes. I'm just kidding. Her Majesty the Queen's bodyguard is no problem."

Is it okay for the Queen's Head maid to make such jokes? I held back from saying something. The other person is the royal Head maid and an 8th class. Even ordinary nobles have no choice but to be at her mercy. Above all, she seemed to have a friendship-like relationship with the Queen.

,I need something to drink. I'd like to choose it myself. May I ask you to guide me?"

,Yuria. Please guide the guest."

,Yes, Master-nim."

Yuria guided Florence.

I envied Deneb and Invisible Blade, who were stuck in the break room as trainee Head maids.

One of the Head maids hurriedly approached me.

,Master-nim. There's a commotion in the banquet hall. The guests want a duel."

,A duel? What for?"

,I don't know the details, but it seems to be a love affair."

,Ha."

It's obvious. The other nobles will be egging them on with giggles instead of stopping them. That's what I would have done if I were there.

,But I'm the host of this banquet. If I leave it alone, my reputation will be tarnished. What if I allow the duel and someone gets hurt? People will blame me. They'll say, 'Why didn't you stop it when you were the host?'

I couldn't leave it to the Head maids. They're not Yuria-level, who can't be ignored even by nobles.

,Annoying. That's why I don't even hold simple social parties. Tsk.'

I headed to the banquet hall at a fast pace.

Florence watched Yuria's walking posture, who would soon be her successor. There was nothing to criticize. She was more noble than a noble.

,Yuria-ssi, are you satisfied working at the Meyer family?"

,Yes. I am satisfied."

,Her Majesty the Queen thinks very highly of you. Have you ever thought about working for the royal family?"

,My answer is always the same. I am Eugene-nim's Head maid."

,Please don't take this the wrong way. I've investigated Yuria-ssi and Count Meyer-nim. There are rumors that you've gone beyond a master-servant relationship. Is Count Meyer-nim that good at nighttime activities?"

Yuria's legs stopped. Florence also stopped and looked at her. Florence obviously provoked Yuria on purpose. There was no malice. It was just to test her reaction. Even she would have a hard time figuring out Yuria if she didn't do this.

,By any chance, is Florence-ssi a virgin?"

Yuria scanned Florence's body up and down. Florence reflexively resisted the urge to cover her chest with her hands. She had an E-cup chest that could be called a large chest, but it was smaller than Yuria's chest. She had no choice but to admit it.

,...My skin is very firm, but even so, I'm more than 20 years older than Yuria-ssi."

,So you're a virgin."

,Keeping one's chastity is a virtue for women. Does my chastity matter?"

,It matters. Personally, I think it's amazing that Florence-ssi, who is more than 20 years older than me, has kept her chastity. Ah, you asked about Master-nim's nighttime activities, right? Hmm. Florence-ssi is a virgin, so you won't understand even if I tell you."

,......"

Yuria started walking again. Florence frowned. She was definitely more than 20 years younger than her, but why did she feel like she was older?

,I expected it, but... my successor won't be easy.'

Florence sighed inwardly.

The banquet lasted until late at night. Fortunately, the guests gradually left. It was fortunate that the mansion was in the center of Neo London. Their residences were 10 to 30 minutes away by carriage, so it was easy to get back.

,The royals left when the Queen left. Did the Queen take the royals with her?'

When the royals left, the nobles also started to leave.

Still, there were some clueless nobles who stayed and drank, but... I could just call their servants and kick them out.

,In the first place, I announced that the banquet would only last until 10 p.m., so there's no problem.'

There won't be any rumors that it was a boring party later. It's a banquet attended by bigwigs including the Queen, so there's no way that would happen.

As I was taking a breather after seeing off the bigwig nobles, a knight riding a mechanical horse descended from the night sky.

,Oh dear. It seems I'm too late."

The armored man took off his helmet. A middle-aged face was revealed. He smiled as if showing off his healthy teeth.

,Sir Pucksibal. I didn't expect you to come."

London's best swordsman. A member of the Neo Round Table. 9th class. A knight standing before transcendence.

,Haha. I couldn't refuse an invitation from a promising young nobleman who's making a name for himself in Neo London these days. I came as quickly as I could... but I ended up being late. I'm sorry."

,It's okay. Would you like a glass of wine?"

,I can't ask you to treat me as a guest when it's late. I'll look forward to the next time."

Pucksibal got back on his mechanical horse.

,There's someone among the members of the Neo Round Table who hates you very much. Be careful."

Pucksibal left.

It seems he came to deliver that last message.

,That's a relief.'

At least Pucksibal, who came to tell me, won't be that member.
