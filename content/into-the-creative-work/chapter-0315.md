Dinner at the Austen Prison wasn't much different from breakfast: bland soup, hard bread. Just a slice of apple was added. It was an apple on the verge of rotting, but it tasted better than the soup and bread.

After dinner, there was free time until lights out.

I sat blankly on the prison floor, needing to redesign my plan.

,If Owen, that bastard, had abandoned his comrades and run away with me, there would have been no need to rework the plan....'

My plan was to smash through the prison walls, escape with Owen at full speed, get out of the prison area, and use a teleportation scroll. If I could just use the teleportation scroll, the prison's pursuit would end.

,Sending a knight order to chase after two prisoners is too much. It's not like we committed heinous crimes like treason....'

The mission itself wasn't difficult. If Owen cooperated, that is.

,Shibal. Annoying. Should I just kill him?'

First of all, I can't use the teleportation scroll. Simple reason: I didn't have enough teleportation scrolls. I have 10 million AP, but I only prepared enough for Owen and me.

,I do have a few more, but the coordinates aren't set, so it's as good as having none.'

In other words, to take Owen's comrades with me, the existing plan wouldn't work.

,If I don't use the teleportation scroll, I'll be chased by the knight order... I have no choice.'

The only way is a riot.

•••

Tang! Tangtang!

While I was lying on the floor, dozing off with a weary body, a guard came to my cell and banged on the bars.

"Prisoner 756487."

I raise my body at the sound of him calling me.

The guard, instead of his usual annoyed face, stared at me with a serious expression and opened the cell door.

"Come out."

"...What's going on all of a sudden?"

"I said come out."

"......"

The atmosphere was unusual, so I decided to follow the guard's words for now. The guard put handcuffs on my wrists and pushed my back.

"Go forward."

"......"

I moved as the guard said, racking my brain.

,Did I do something wrong?'

I couldn't think of anything, no matter how much I thought about it.

I hadn't caused any trouble. I didn't pick fights with the guards. I did break the limbs of one prisoner in the exercise yard, but all the guards who were there at the time were bribed by me. It's already over.

,...Did any of the prisoners report my abilities to the guards? Or are the guards just being greedy?'

I glanced at the guard behind me and erased that thought. There was a hint of pity in the guard's eyes as he looked at me.

I sighed as if frustrated and asked the guard.

"What's going on? Can't you tell me?"

"What kind of trouble did you cause before you came here?"

"I came in for rape. Yesterday."

"Ah. That's right."

The guard's pity disappeared. He seemed to remember that I was a criminal bastard he couldn't associate with.

"It's your karma, so deal with it yourself."

"Huh?"

"It's not left, it's right. Do you have any last words?"

"Last words...? Are you talking about a will?"

"You might die. If you have any last words for your family or friends, I'll pass them on. Consider it a reward for the money you gave me during exercise time today."

"......No."

"Okay."

The place the guard took me was another building. A building that reeked of blood, unlike the others.

I walked down the hallway, looking around. It was made of iron doors instead of iron bars, and it was very quiet. I could see the prisoner trapped there through the feeding port on the iron door.

,Is this solitary confinement.'

I knew this was a building where prisoners who caused trouble came to be punished. The terrible smell of blood must be the traces of torture.

"We've arrived. Door number 3. Go in."

Following the guard's words, I pushed the solid iron door that had no feeding port. The slightly open iron door opened with an unpleasant creaking sound.

As soon as the door opened, the guard pushed my back and slammed the iron door shut. Clang! The door was locked from the outside.

Inside the iron door, there were torture tools such as torture chairs, bonfires, water, thorns, and knives, and four men were waiting for me.

Three of them wore swords at their waists. They looked like mercenaries. And the other one was someone I knew.

"It's only been a day. You have no idea how much I've been looking forward to this time!"

"......Why are you here?"

"Why am I here? Of course, for revenge, Rohan!"

Rohan.

It's the alias I used right before I was caught and brought to prison.

And the man in front of me was Koum, the merchant, the husband of the woman I raped.

"I told you! Don't think it's over! I'll spend all my money to kill you!"

The three mercenaries slowly approached me.

"Ha. You bribed the warden well. Did you spend all your fortune?"

"It only cost 10% of my entire fortune. That's your worth! I won't let you die comfortably! Repay the sin of raping my wife and die!"

The mercenaries grabbed my body. They grabbed my shoulders, arms, and back, trying to drag me to the torture chair.

"...Huh?"

"What?"

"Shibal. Aren't prisoners supposed to wear ability-sealing shackles?"

The mercenaries were flustered when I didn't move at all.

"What are you doing! Move quickly! You got paid, so do your job!"

Ting!

I put strength in my wrists and pulled to release the handcuffs. The mercenaries were flustered, then distanced themselves from me and raised their swords.

"You know, I don't like men touching my body. Especially when it's sweaty bastards like you."

Pajijijik.

Pure white lightning crackled from my body and covered the mercenaries. The mercenaries couldn't resist and were electrocuted to death.

"Uh, uh..."

Koum was flustered and stepped back. He probably didn't know I was this strong. There's no reason to deliberately turn myself in and go to prison with this kind of power.

"Who's torturing who to death?"

"C, calm down and listen to me... Aaaaaaack!"

Pajijijijik!

I controlled the lightning so it wouldn't kill him and let it flow through Koum's body.

"I'm not very good at torture... but since I have time, I'll play with you."

"Aaaaaaaack!"

The torture chamber was filled with screams.

After torturing Koum to death for nearly two hours, I decided to start taking action and stood in front of the iron door.

This iron door will probably open around tomorrow morning.

,Should I break it?'

That's not bad either, but there's something I want to experiment with.

,Lightning Flash.'

Lightning Embodiment (電身化). My body turned into lightning. In that form, I ran toward the iron door and passed through it without any problem.

,It's as I thought.'

My body became lightning itself, and it was possible because the iron door was a conductive metal.

,But what happens if I can't pass through and my body returns to normal in the middle? Do I get stuck and die?'

That would be terrible. So I decided not to use it this way unless I was sure I could move safely.

First, I found all the guards on duty nearby, killed them, and stole the keys to the solitary cells.

I was going to release the guys in solitary confinement. These guys, who were not only locked up in solitary confinement but also tortured, must be full of hatred for the guards.

I give them freedom and weapons.

"It's time for a riot. Keukeukeu."

•••

Bang! Kwang! Bang!

Explosions continued to occur in the prison. The bombs I had planted in various places during exercise time began to explode.

"It's a riot! The prisoners are rioting... Keueok?!"

Tang!

One of the guards, who was shouting towards his fellow guards, fell to the floor with a finger-sized hole in his head.

It's a rifle.

I removed the shackles of the prisoners in solitary confinement and gave them rifles and iron bar cutters. The prisoners killed the guards and destroyed the prison to gather their comrades.

For the record, I also cut through the prison bars with a sword. It was too easy for me, who can use Sword energy, to cut through iron bars.

"I came to pick you up as promised, Owen."

"S, surely you didn't cause the riot that's happening now?"

Owen shouted in shock. The other prisoners were busy escaping through the cut bars. Even if they didn't want to join the riot, they had to escape if they didn't want to die because the building was on fire.

Tang! Tangtangtangtang!

Gunshots rang out continuously.

"It's easier to divert attention by causing a riot. The warden and the guards will be building a base separately."

"What about the knight order?!"

"The walls throughout the prison have collapsed. The prisoners will all escape in different directions. The knight order's forces will also be dispersed, making it easier to escape."

"C, crazy...! Are you using the prisoners as sacrifices to help us escape?!"

"Using them? I just gave the prisoners a chance."

"......You're being reckless. Is it okay to do it this way?"

"Why are you so worried? What's important to us isn't the lives of the prisoners."

"......Ah. Right. That's right. I must have been in prison for too long, I almost forgot which organization we belong to."

"Where are your comrades?"

"They're nearby. Hey guys! Gather around!"

Owen shouted. Then four prisoners gathered. I looked at them and sighed. They all looked weak.

I used their Sword energy to smash their shackles.

"...What are you guys even doing here?"

"I received a request for investigation. Sorry, I'll explain in detail later. Isn't escaping the priority right now?"

"That's true. Come this way."

"Where are we going? People are going that way?"

"Looking at you guys, it seems difficult to escape openly. So I'll move as secretly as possible."

I started to escape with Owen and his group.

Tangtangtangtangtang!

Gunshots continued to ring out.

"Warden! Tell the warden to come out! I'm going to kill that bastard!"

The shouts of excited prisoners echoed throughout the prison.

I successfully escaped from the Austen Prison in District 1,067.

The guys who caused the riot inside the prison without escaping will be easily subdued by the knight order and tortured to death. I did give them rifles, but the knights basically use Sword energy and wear ranked armor. The knight's armor is too hard to penetrate with guns.

To compare it to modern weapons, the knight is like a tank.

,On the other hand, the prisoners only have rifles.'

「Voice Buried in the Wind

Rank: A」

"Is that perhaps an item with communication abilities?"

"That's right."

"Mirror! You're contacting that executive, Mirror!"

"Go away."

"Can't I listen with you? I'm so curious about what kind of conversation you're having with the executive!"

"Suit yourself."

I answered nonchalantly. Elena never told me not to contact her in front of others. Also, I have a feeling that if I refuse Owen, he'll annoyingly stick to me.

I brought the conch shell to my lips and said.

"Mirror. Mirror. Can you talk now? It's about the mission."

The signal must have gone to the conch shell that Elena has now. If I don't get an answer within 1 minute, I'll assume she's in a situation where she can't answer and move separately.

About 20 seconds later, a voice came from the conch shell.

A neat woman's voice. It's Elena.

-I didn't think it would be that difficult... To contact me without coming to the designated place... Did you finish the job successfully?

"A variable occurred."

-A variable?

I explain the current situation.

It's not that Owen was caught, but he deliberately entered the prison with his comrades for another reason. And what I did to get Owen and his comrades out.

-Aha! Hahahahaha! You blew up part of the prison with bombs and gave the prisoners guns to cause a riot? I never imagined it! So, is this how you handle things? How fun. I like the fact that Baron Austen's face will be crumpled for a while the most.

Elena laughed pleasantly.

Looking at Owen next to me, he probably never imagined that the executive would have this kind of personality and tone.

-The reaction is a bit slow. Is there someone next to you?
