,sulbeop, Taoism, sorcery, umyangsul.... It's true that the types are divided when you delve into magic, but nowadays, any phenomena caused by artificially manipulating mana are generally referred to as magic. In other words, your sulbeop is also magic.

,Yes. You are an excellent magician."

,My self-introduction was late. I am Kim Ji-hak, the director of the Magic Foundation of the Association. I am in charge of all magic incidents that occur in Korea. No tracking at all?

Judging by the fact that only she had an ashtray in front of her, the conference room seemed to be a non-smoking area, but no one said anything to her.

She said in a stiff tone. It was a rough voice that scratched at my ears. Smoke was billowing from her mouth.

One of only five S-class Hunters in Korea, including Han Ah-young.

I gauged her skills. Can I win if I fight? I'm not sure.

Those intelligence guys... they're not doing their jobs properly."

I think I know why she's called Hell Tiger. Her temperament is incredibly fierce. Moreover, looking at the ashtray, it's already piled high with cigarettes. It reminds me of the saying, "When tigers used to smoke."

Ttak.

Kim Ji-hak, the director of the Magic Foundation, and Hell Tiger Lee Yeon-sook.
