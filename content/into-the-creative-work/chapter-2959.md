If this didn’t work, I planned to spend AP to buy a bunch of recovery-related Items. If there really was no other way, I’d even use an Elixir.

‘It’s not just an ordinary regeneration gene, but a super-regeneration gene. It should have some effect.’

Indeed, it wasn’t long before the effect appeared. Parts of her blackened half-Body began to recover little by little.

“It really is working?!”

“Does it hurt?”

“It feels ticklish. Ah, I’m hungry. Extremely hungry, at that.”

Kkorureureuk. Kkorureureuk!

A mighty sound came from Kasha’s stomach. It didn’t stop there. Her Body was visibly thinning in real-time. She instantly transformed into a state of malnutrition.

“Bring me anything to eat!”

As Kasha screamed irritably, the members ran outside.

Seeing Kasha’s teardrop-shaped D-cup Breasts shrinking, I was horrified and pulled out a hamburger and cola from my Inventory. Her Breasts and Ass Flesh can’t shrink!

She devoured the hamburger and cola like a glutton. It didn’t even take 5 seconds to eat one hamburger. Even though it was carbonated cola, she drank 500ml in just 3 seconds.

“More! Give me more!”

She consumed 120 hamburgers and 60 liters of cola until she fully recovered. She recovered her blackened half-Body, and fortunately, her physique returned to normal.

‘Is she recovering quickly by consuming calories? The calorie consumption seems quite severe.’

Since she can’t fight while eating, it feels a bit ambiguous. Even if she can eat after the battle… it seems she loses strength during recovery.

‘It looks like she can’t control the super-regeneration.’

Super-regeneration could be a double-edged Sword. Regenerating the Body during battle and consuming nutrients? It was the same as saying she would lose strength.

‘Still, recovering from 1 or 2 Fatal Wounds shouldn’t be a problem.’

Objectively, it was a good ability, but to me, who possesses complete recovery, it felt like a piece of Scum.

‘Complete recovery has no cost except for a 12-hour cooldown. Even if I die, I recover.’

Even if my Heart is pierced, my Head explodes, and my entire Body is ground to dust, I recover. On the other hand, super-regeneration? Can one recover from instant death? It was too dangerous to experiment.

“Ahhh. I feel alive. How to put it. All the old scars are gone… it feels like being reborn?”

Kasha got off the Bed and moved her Body around. There seemed to be no particular side effects.

“Thank you, Heavenly Demon. And Ersia. You don’t need to be disheartened. I wasn’t injured because of you.”

“…Older Sister Kasha was injured while Embracing me, wasn’t she?”

“That’s overthinking. I just got hit while fighting frantically. It’s a common occurrence.”
