Jjieeeeeeek.

With the sound of paper tearing, the space splits. We took a fighting stance and tensed up at the sudden situation.

The person who came out of the split space was a familiar woman.

Arinna.

The NPC who gave us the quest.

"You saved the Forest of Bewilderment. Thank you. You really are adventurers."

The atmosphere she exudes is very different. She looks ordinary, but there's no way a woman who splits space and appears can be ordinary. The sense of incongruity that comes from that difference.

"What are you?"

"I'm the owner of this forest. The villagers call me the Master of Bewilderment. Even though they don't know who I really am."

Arinna laughed softly and gestured. The aura of death that had been filling the cave disappeared without a trace.

Cheujeut.

My wrist tingles. When I glance at it, the rhombus pattern engraved on my wrist was reacting. Sung Hari's was the same.

,It doesn't seem like the NPC has turned into a monster... Is this a set event?'

She wasn't an ordinary opponent. I couldn't guarantee that we could win if we fought.

「You have completed the 'Save the Forest of Bewilderment' quest.」

「Your level has increased.」

"Me? You could say I'm a woman who received a great calling."

"A great calling? From who?"

"I don't know either. I just know what I have to do. Personally, I'm grateful to you. Because I regained my strength thanks to you guys taking care of Saenggangsi."

"Are you going to fight us?"

"No. I didn't come here to fight. I came to express my gratitude. And to inform you of the danger that is engulfing this world. And I want you to eliminate that danger. Of course, I'm not asking you to do it for free. Adventurers move when there's proper compensation, right?"

Arinna gestures in the air.

Jjieeeeeeek.

The space tears and a white comma-shaped jade appears in the gap.

The pattern on my wrist, the authentication code given by Tenrai Nagisa, shines and points to Arinna. That was Susanoo's comma-shaped jade.

Sung Hari rushed at Arinna. It was a surprise attack that even I didn't expect. Sung Hari reached out to snatch Susanoo's comma-shaped jade. But she failed, blocked by an invisible wall.

I sensed it. There's no way to break through that with ordinary methods.

,In game terms, it's an indestructible object. An area that players can't touch.'

In terms of the [God's Atlantis] world, it could be said to be direct intervention by the system.

Arinna didn't even look at Sung Hari and stared intently at me. I acknowledged that Arinna wasn't just a simple NPC. Seeing her take out Susanoo's comma-shaped jade, her true identity is....

"Are you Terra?"

"You could see it that way. To be exact, a sub-artificial intelligence, um. You could say I'm a clone of Terra."

"The fact that you openly showed us Susanoo's comma-shaped jade means... you want something."

"Yes. The current Terra Simulator is being invaded by an unknown virus."

"The end of the world? You didn't set it up on purpose, did you?"

"Why would I deliberately set up something that doesn't help me at all? The end of the world. Please get rid of the virus. As compensation, I'll give you Susanoo's comma-shaped jade."

「Salvation

Overcome the end of the world and save this world.

Reward: Susanoo's Comma-Shaped Jade」

"...Why are you appearing in this way and making us an offer?"

If it was the artificial intelligence Terra, there was no need to bother appearing like this.

She could just put up a notification window and explain it as if she were talking.

"The main body is being invaded by the virus. What I can do is limited."

"What about Susanoo's comma-shaped jade? It's strange that you have that important object in the first place."

"The main body created me, a sub-artificial intelligence, only to manage Susanoo's comma-shaped jade. That means the main body values Susanoo's comma-shaped jade that much. What's so strange about that?"

I can't trust Arinna. How can I trust someone who suddenly appears and offers a deal?

,Even if it's suspicious, I have no choice but to accept the deal.'

I can't take Susanoo's comma-shaped jade. Even if Sung Hari tried to attack her by surprise, it didn't work. Young Hari is glaring at Arinna and trying to read her, but the probability of success is low. I grabbed Young Hari's shoulder so she wouldn't do anything unnecessary.

"Okay. I'll accept. Where's the virus we need to deal with?"

"I don't know either. I haven't fully grasped its true nature yet. But fortunately, there's a clue."

「The Secret of the Dead Village.

There is a dead village at the end of the Forest of Bewilderment. Uncover the secrets of that place.

Reward: Experience, Boots of Coldness, End of the World Quest.」

A quest window appeared in front of me.

Sung Hari-deul just tilted their heads. But as soon as I saw it, I frowned.

Chain quest.

It smells like an epic chain quest.

,I'm going to be running around like crazy, aren't I?'

But there was no other way.

"I'll take the quest. It's certain that I'll receive Susanoo's comma-shaped jade after the job is done, right?"

「You have accepted the quest.」

"Of course. The Terra Simulator has no problems even without Susanoo's comma-shaped jade."

Is that really true? If so, why did she even create a sub-artificial intelligence to manage it? If Susanoo's comma-shaped jade isn't needed, shouldn't she just hide it deep in the virtual reality?

I didn't voice the rising questions. I don't think she'll answer properly even if I ask. And right now, it's advantageous to pretend to obediently follow Arinna's words. Even if I fight, I can't take it away.

,If only hacking wasn't on cooldown...'

It couldn't be helped, even if it was a shame.

"Then I'll see you later."

Arinna, who was done with her business, smiled and walked into the torn space.

The inside of the cave was quiet as if nothing had happened.
