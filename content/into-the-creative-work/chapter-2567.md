I decided to return to Nakwol Mountain and take the Taegeuk Hondan.

As the saying goes, important training should be done in the most familiar and safest place. For me, the safest place was none other than Nakwol Mountain.

The Demonic Cult? I don't trust it. The Supreme One there isn't me, but the Heavenly Demon. There were hardly any people I could call my own in the Demonic Cult.

,Nakwol Mountain also has spiritual veins flowing through it, so it's perfect for training.'

I sat cross-legged in the center of the training ground and took out the Taegeuk Hondan, putting it in my mouth. I swallowed the hard pill without chewing. Before it even reached my stomach, the pill melted instantly, and the condensed spiritual energy burst out as if decompressing.

The spiritual energy, imbued with the profound power of Taegeuk, naturally stimulated and attempted to fuse the brain energy and demonic energy. At this time, the mental image I recognized, the *shimjok*, was helpful.

Inside me, energies began to swirl and fuse as if in a storm. I simply focused, felt it, and fell into a state of ecstasy.

How much time had passed? Having absorbed all the spiritual energy of the Taegeuk Hondan, I slightly opened my mouth and exhaled. Some of my inner strength flowed out and dissipated into the air.

I open my eyes. A blue light flashed and disappeared. My *giheol* and body were further strengthened.

,I've finally reached the realm of Samjeong 5th Dan.'

I was satisfied for the moment. Of course, I have no intention of being satisfied here.

,I don't even know when I'll be able to advance to the next stage.'

Advancing through spiritual medicines like now was only possible when the body and mind were ready. That preparation process is training, experience, and enlightenment.

Feeling the profound inner strength surging within me, I rise from my seat. Looking outside, the sun is rising. I tore a space-movement scroll and teleported to Tongbisi.

As soon as I entered the *giru*, I met the Martial Arts Alliance Leader. That bastard, pretending to be a Wudang Taoist and not holding any *ginyeo* in his arms, was sitting in the first-floor restaurant, holding his ground. As soon as he saw me, he approached.

"Your *giun* is even stronger than it was three days ago. Congratulations on your achievement. Are you going to the Imperial Capital now?"

"I'm going to Anhui Province."

"Anhui Province? To the Namgung Clan? That's unexpected. Didn't you kill Namgung Sega's Cheongundaedo?"

"...Those Cheongundae bastards first joined hands with Salwang and tried to kill me. If you think about it, those bastards are the traitors of the Namgung Clan."

"The Namgung Clan and the Demonic Cult. Which is more important to you?"

"Both are important."

"If you had to choose only one?"

"Choose only one? Then I won't choose either."

"Hehe. I expected it... It seems that power isn't that important to you."

My women are more important than power. But that could be a weakness, so I didn't say it. I don't trust the Martial Arts Alliance Leader.

"Having power is convenient. There's no particular value beyond that."

The most important thing in this world was individual strength. If you have overwhelming power, you can make any force kneel. It's already been proven. The Emperor is like that.

"Are you leaving right away, perhaps?"

"Now that training is over, I have to unwind. I'm leaving tomorrow."

And the next day, in the afternoon.

I left the *giru*. The Martial Arts Alliance Leader, who had been waiting, approached me.

"It reminds me of the old days. There was a time when I wandered the *gangho* like you do now. I traveled with like-minded friends and merchants I met by chance."

"Traveling my ass. I'm flying right away. Follow me if you can."

I summoned the Jeunryeong from my inventory. The dodecagonal azure metal badge was in my hand. When I activated my inner strength, a cloud was created in front of me.

I climbed onto the cloud. The cloud floated into the air.

"Amazing. It doesn't seem like a *sulbeop*, is it the power of a *beopgi*? But are you riding alone? Would it kill you to give me a ride?"

"I have no intention of riding with an old man. Run. You're the Martial Arts Alliance Leader, you can do that much, right?"

"Hehe. In the old days, I would have bought suffering for the sake of training... But now that I'm old, my knees hurt."

The Martial Arts Alliance Leader suddenly rolled up his left sleeve. There was a crane-shaped tattoo on his left forearm.

The Martial Arts Alliance Leader and a tattoo. I thought it was a bad combination. A mysterious light flowed from his tattoo, and it transformed into a giant blue crane with eight wings.

"Blue Crane. It's been a while. I didn't call you for battle. I have a destination, so take me there."

The giant crane nodded gracefully. The Martial Arts Alliance Leader climbed onto the crane.

As I flew into the sky on the cloud, the Blue Crane followed, flapping its wings. I looked at the Blue Crane with curious eyes.

"A *yeongmul*. How did you summon and control a *yeongmul*? You're not a *sulbeopsa*, are you?"

"Do you know that there are multiple worlds in this universe?"

"I know. They are divided into several, such as the Immortal Realm, the Underworld, and the Material Realm."

"The Material Realm. This world we are in is also called the Middle Realm because it is between other worlds. Do you know which world is closest to our world?"

"...The Immortal Realm?"

"The Spirit Beast Realm. It is also called the *yeongmul* Realm. There are no humans there, only *yeongmul*. It is said that *yeongmul* are born there and train to become immortals. It is much smaller than the Middle Realm, so it is said that there are not many resources. So, sometimes *yeongmul* from the *yeongmul* Realm come down to the Middle Realm for training or to obtain goods."

"Is that crane a *yeongmul* that came down from the *yeongmul* Realm? Judging from the *giun* I feel, it's at the Ogi level."

"It's a young one. It's not even 100 years old."

"Not even 100 years old and already at the Ogi level?"

Unless you're a genius, you usually have to train for more than 120 years to reach the Ogi realm. Reaching the Ogi realm before 100 years of training? That means you're a genius.

"*Yeongmul* in the *yeongmul* Realm are born in nature. They usually start at the Chulji realm."

"Starting at Chulji as soon as you're born is too OP."

"Hehe. There are differences even among *yeongmul*. It is said that dragons are born very rarely, and they start at the Samjeong realm."

"In other words, their very nature is different."

The Martial Arts Alliance Leader knew a lot. Most of it was useless and uninteresting anecdotes, so I just let it flow in one ear and out the other.

It was when the boring time continued.

There was a change in the circulating *cheonjiyounggi*. I and the Martial Arts Alliance Leader stopped in the sky and looked around the world.

"...The *yeonggi* of the sky is fixed, and the *yeonggi* of the earth is gathering in one place... It's not a good sign. Someone is preparing for ascension."

"Ascension? Is this a sign of ascension? Was a *mansang* powerhouse near here?"

"Hmm? You don't seem to know much about ascension."

"The ascension realm is after the *mansang* realm, right?"

"Wrong. That's a common misconception. Didn't your teacher, the Heavenly Demon, teach you?"

"The Heavenly Demon is not my teacher."

I had never received detailed teachings about the realms from Wiyu. Wiyu's teachings were mainly focused on martial arts. It's not like I became her disciple from a time when I knew nothing. She didn't bother to teach me what she knew.

,The realm settings I know are from the original Bright Light Ascension. Have the realms changed as they've mixed with other worlds?'

I listened to the Martial Arts Alliance Leader for now.

"If you cross the wall of the Harmony Realm, you can choose between *mansang* and ascension. Usually, they choose ascension. *Mansang* only adds 500 years to your lifespan, but... By ascending and being reborn as an immortal, the limits of your lifespan disappear, and you go up to the Immortal Realm to enjoy eternal life. There are quite a few people in this world who turn their backs on the secular world and only train for ascension."

"If you reach *mansang*, can you never ascend again?"

"That's not true. It is said that you can ascend if you perform a ritual in time for the time that comes once every 100 years. The problem is that you can fail. So, wouldn't most *suseonja* aim for ascension?"

The saying that ascension is a trap, which I had heard somewhere, comes to mind. Surely the Emperor's teacher said that.

The *yeonggi* in the sky began to churn. The *yeonggi* soon turned red. There was a bloody smell in the sky.

"Oh, dear. The *yeonggi* has a strong bloody smell. Usually, in this case, there is a high possibility that the ascender is a *yokai*."

Neocheonryu (雷天流) Electromagnetic Wave (電磁氣波).

I tried to find the ascender, but the fluctuating *cheonjiyounggi* interfered. It was too wide here in the mountainous area to look with Cheonan.

"...You. You just did something."

"If they're trying to ascend, it's a great opportunity to ambush them. If it's a *yokai*, I can get its *naedan*, so I'll find it and kill it."

I felt *gipa* from the Blue Crane. The *gipa* was directed at the Martial Arts Alliance Leader. I guess he's doing something similar to telepathy.

"The Blue Crane says that the *yokai* trying to ascend right now is a White Tiger. You can have the White Tiger's *naedan*, but I'll take the rest of the White Tiger's corpse, including its skin. How about it?"

"You're giving up the *naedan*? The most precious thing? What's the catch?"

"Hehe. You don't know much, do you? The White Tiger's skin goes without saying, and its claws, whiskers, and eyes are very good materials for making *beopgi*. Above all, the White Tiger's meat itself is a *yeongyak* that strengthens the *gigol*, so it is very helpful for young disciples who are just starting to learn martial arts."

The Martial Arts Alliance Leader swallows hard. It seems he had killed and eaten a White Tiger before.

"I'll do that. But how do you know where the White Tiger bastard is?"

"You can find out by going down to the ground and looking at the flow of the *yeongmaek*. And if it's a *yokai* of this level, it must have ordered its disciples to protect it. If you catch those disciples and threaten them, you may be able to easily find out the White Tiger's location."

"So, we have to go down first."

We went down to the ground.

It was an opportunity to gain a free profit. I couldn't resist.
