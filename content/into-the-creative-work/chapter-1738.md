I knew it instinctively. The voices I'm hearing right now are all hallucinations. And they're all related to Seong Hari. Feeling her hand trembling in mine, I couldn't help but realize the truth, even if I didn't want to.

,Just like I'm experiencing a status abnormality that's making me unable to see, Seong Hari-nim is also experiencing a status abnormality that's causing hallucinations to appear. Are all the voices I'm hearing coming from these hallucinations?

If that's the case, it makes sense. It's just a bit surprising that all those hallucinations are related to Seong Hari's past.

'Hallucinations materializing Seong Hari's past... that means the AI knows Seong Hari's past. Does it also have the ability to read players' pasts?'

My head is getting complicated. I also have things I'm hiding from Seong Hari. I'd rather not have those things exposed through hallucinations.

,You broke your promise. Traitor.

Hmph. Seong Hari is startled. Her agitation is transmitted to me.

,Mom?

,...It's nothing. Just a normal hallucination. A slightly nasty one.

,But Mom, your hand is shaking?

,I didn't expect to see hallucinations here. I'm fine now. All the hallucinations have disappeared.

I could sense that she didn't want to talk about the hallucinations anymore.

I quietly chose my words. Various questions are popping up in my head. But she won't answer all of them even if I ask. At most, one or two. And I can't even be sure of that. Seong Hari is reluctant to talk about her past.

Therefore, I asked Seong Hari the most innocuous question among the ones that came to mind.

,What's your wish, Mom?

,...Suddenly?

,One of the hallucinations asked earlier. What your wish is. Who was that person?

,...It's a secret. I can't see them anymore anyway. Ah, you asked what my wish is, right? I want Yujin to be happy. That's my wish. I can do anything for that.

Her voice was filled with firm resolve.

,Were all the people who appeared as hallucinations people you know, Mom?

,Hallucinations are just hallucinations. It doesn't matter anymore.

Seong Hari said firmly. It was like drawing a line. Even if I asked about the hallucinations, she wouldn't answer in detail.

I walked forward as Seong Hari led me.

,Go back.

Something appeared and spoke. It was a voice like scraping metal. I can't tell if it's a man or a woman.

What's certain is that it's not a hallucination. Unlike hallucinations, I can vividly feel its presence. It was definitely the owner of the gaze that had been watching us from earlier.

,Hey, I feel like I've seen you somewhere before. Do you know me?

Seong Hari asked. There was hostility in her voice.

Suddenly, I realized that it was babbling in Korean.

'The default language in this world is Japanese. Even the NPCs use Japanese, but the monster is babbling in Korean? What the hell is going on?'

No matter how wide I open my eyes, my vision is pitch black. I can't see anything. Not being able to see is more frustrating than I thought.

,This place is our grave. If you don't turn back, I'll take it as you wanting to join us.

,That's creepy. You're not human, are you? Why are you pretending to be human? Do you want to be human so badly?

,I understand your intentions.

Their conversation didn't mesh well.

Kudeuk, Kadeudeuk.

Something is happening. The sound of trees breaking, crawling sounds, the sound of the ground shaking.

'It's not just the sounds. The air is stuffy. There's something huge in front of us. It doesn't seem like just one...'

I can't see even when I use Spirit Eyes.

The same goes for Heavenly Eyes. When I use Heavenly Eyes, I see reality, not virtual reality. This virtual reality world has many restrictions on me.

,Yujin-ah! Enemies all around! Burn them all with Salamander!

,Salamander!!

I summoned Salamander as Seong Hari said. Even though I can't see, I could feel Salamander's presence.

The fire lizard, sensing my will, spews fire in all directions.

The effect wasn't great. Whether it's because some strange power is acting on the forest, the trees themselves don't burn well. I could feel enemies approaching through the flames.

,I'm going to let go of your hand for a moment.

Seong Hari let go of my hand, which she had been holding, and dashed forward.

Kung! Kuung! Kung!

Enemies are approaching. 10 of them. No, it seems like there are 13. Each of them has a massive body. I have no idea what kind of monsters they are. They are quietly emitting hostility and murderous intent.

,Bless!

I used Bless, one of the Priest's skills, on Seong Hari. It was a skill that slightly increases the target's stats for a certain period of time. It was possible because her presence was clear even though I couldn't see.

,Thank you, Yujin-ah!

Kwang!

An explosion resounds.

I felt the presence of an enemy approaching me from behind. I drew my sword from my waist.

Kwaang!

It's not me. Seong Hari instantly came to my side and slashed the enemy.

Kwaang!

I knew what the roar was. It wasn't Seong Hari's attack, but the sound of her kicking off the ground and leaping. Every time Seong Hari leaps, the presence of an enemy disappears one by one. It's overwhelming. I don't even need to do anything.

,Traitor. That power isn't yours.

,I can't forgive you.

,We trusted you!

Voices resound. They were all voices blaming and resenting Seong Hari. I realized that the hallucinations I thought had disappeared had reappeared.

,That's scary. It shouldn't exist.

Salamander. The fire lizard, who always answered with monosyllables, spoke in a voice full of fear.

The fire lizard arbitrarily takes my mana and spews fire at Seong Hari.

,What the fuck! Is Salamander crazy?!

I reach out my hand towards the fire lizard. The fire lizard flew upwards, avoiding my hand, and spewed fire at Seong Hari.

,This bastard... why is it acting up again?! Stop it right now!

,Understand, Master. The traitor is over there. We must kill our natural enemy. The Spirit King is hoping for that.

,I said stop!

The fire lizard didn't listen to my command.

I gritted my teeth. I forcibly severed the connection with the fire lizard. The aftermath became a shock and covered me. A twisting pain covers my body.

,Heal.

I use Heal on myself to recover. The twisting pain in my body has improved a bit. I gripped the hilt of my sword. I can fight even without a spirit.

,It's okay, it's all over!

Kwareureung!

Thunder rumbled from Seong Hari. Before I could do anything, the enemies quickly disappeared.

When the thunder stopped, the enemy was gone.

,It's over.

Seong Hari came over and patted my shoulder as if it was nothing.

「You have regained your sight.」

Finally, after 10 minutes, my vision returned.

The surroundings were devastated. The trees were all smashed and lying on the ground, and there were small craters and holes here and there on the ground. The enemies' corpses were slowly turning gray and disappearing.

,...Undead?

It was a massive undead. A lump of meat made by clumping corpses together.

,I didn't know it was an undead because it didn't smell rotten.

There were burn marks. The blackened ground is the work of the fire lizard.

,I told you Mom is okay, right?

,...Are you really okay? You're sweating a lot.

,I'm a little tired because I used my strength after a long time. The enemies weren't ordinary.

Seong Hari smiles. Then she staggered.

,Mom!

,......Ah, sorry. I suddenly lost strength in my body.

,What the hell is going on? There's no way Mom would get tired from just this much. What's going on?

Seong Hari gave a bitter smile at my question.

,It seems like the curse has gotten stronger.

The Spirit King's curse.

That curse that takes away Seong Hari's power has suddenly gotten stronger. It was something I couldn't understand.

'This isn't reality, it's virtual reality. But the curse is affecting her?'

I firmly grabbed the staggering Seong Hari and pulled her towards me. Seong Hari obediently leaned against my body.

,Don't worry. I'll be fine if I rest for a bit.

Seong Hari smiles even while sweating profusely. I laid her down on the floor so that she, who looked pitiful, could rest.

A chilling wind blew.

You could say it's a wind that embraces death.

I looked at the source of the wind.

There was a man wearing black clothes. The man's face, standing powerlessly, was pale. He spoke to me and Seong Hari.

,We cannot allow our grave to be defiled. You must pay the price.

The ground shook slightly, and the undead came up. It's different from the undead with the massive body from before. The body was normal, but the arms and legs were stiff. There was an amulet attached to the head.

'Is it a Jiangshi.'

There are many types of Jiangshi. Blood Jiangshi, Iron Jiangshi, Living Jiangshi, etc. The difference in power varies greatly depending on the type. Unfortunately, I don't know how to distinguish Jiangshi.

,Yujin-ah....

Seong Hari tries to raise her body by leaning on her sword. I used Heal on her. It was useless. The curse that binds her body doesn't loosen at all. I even used Cure. Cure was also ineffective.

,I'll buy you some time, so run away.

,...In that condition?

,Mom is fine. It's just this much.

I sighed and shook my head.

,You're trembling and talking too much. Besides, this is the Forest of Bewilderment. It's hard to run away. Let's just fight together and die.

Fortunately, the good thing is that even if you die, you don't die completely. When you die, your level and sub-level each go down by one, and you revive nearby. There was no need to fear death in this world.

,Aren't you going to listen to Mom?

Seong Hari said in a stern voice. I looked at her with a dumbfounded look for a moment.

,Mom. Have you forgotten that this is virtual reality?

,Huh? Ah, that's right. I did.

,You don't die even if you die. Only your level drops.

,......That's right. Dying with my son.... It feels strange.

,Of course, I don't intend to just die.

I gripped my sword and took a fighting stance. I don't summon spirits. Even if I summon a spirit, there was a high possibility that it would attack Seong Hari like before.

Pajijik.

Lightning energy (雷氣) surged, and sparks flew off the blade. I finished getting ready to use the Lightning Sky Style.

The Jiangshi came closer, hopping. I knew instinctively that they were formidable opponents.

'I'm really going to die here.'

It was the moment I was concentrating my mind on the tip of the sword.

Suddenly, a spear flew from somewhere and pierced between the Jiangshi, creating a shockwave.

Kwaaaaaaaaang!

The Jiangshi, caught in the shockwave, couldn't withstand it and were shattered in an instant.
