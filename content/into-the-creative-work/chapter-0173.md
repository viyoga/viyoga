「Samsunggano (Fake) has discovered you.」

A heavily armed man of large build riding a 3M tall red horse. He has bird feathers on his helmet and a spear in his hand. A spear with blades on both sides of the spearhead. A very famous weapon in China.

'Samsunggano. Meaning a slave with three surnames.'

A red horse and a Halberd. It's not hard to guess who that man is with just these clues.

The most outstanding warrior in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

Lü Bu Fengxian.

The man with the strongest martial prowess in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms.

'No need to be scared. That's a fake.'

There is an avatar of a God's Seat in Atlantis. However, the avatar does not have as much power as the real God's Seat. It is restricted by the system.

I glanced at Kang Myungjin next to me. He is looking at Lü Bu, who is slowly approaching us, with a stiff expression. He must be thinking about how to escape in his head.

'In the original story, Kang Myungjin eventually defeats Lü Bu and gets the Halberd.'

But not right now.

I quietly said to Kang Myungjin.

"...Don't you think we can defeat him if we fight together?"

"...That's Lü Bu Fengxian, the strongest warrior in the Romance of the Three Kingdoms. Even if he's a fake and his power is weakened by the system, he's still Lü Bu Fengxian. It's better to run away."

Kang Myungjin was too scared.

'...Hasn't he really grasped how strong he is yet?'

I can understand. Before possessing the protagonist, he was just an ordinary person, not even a Hunter. The guys he faced in the tutorial were just small fry. He has a vague fear because he knows about the God's Seat.

'If he defeats Lü Bu, Kang Myungjin will gain some confidence. I should just let that guy handle it alone.'

The Halberd that you can get by defeating Lü Bu? I don't know how to handle a spear. It's not such an amazing weapon that I have to use it.

"...It's better to run away to the sides at the same time. If you go down the mountain, you'll see a village. If you get lost, you can find the village by heading towards the highest mountain."

"Even if it's that Lü Bu, isn't he just a normal human in the end?"

"He's not a normal human, he's a God's Seat. I'll give you a signal, so run away to the left at the right timing."

Kung. Kung. Kung!

Lü Bu, who had approached, said to us.

"Are you exiles? You look weaker than I thought. Hmm. It doesn't matter. I am Lü Bu. I have been given the mission to kill the exiles who entered Nakilsan. It is your destiny to have encountered me."

Lü Bu wields his Halberd.

Kaang!

Kang Myungjin, holding a spear, blocked the Halberd and shouted.

"Now! Run not towards the road, but towards the trees!"

I started running to the left, and Kang Myungjin, who had knocked away the spear, ran to the right.

I glanced back. Lü Bu is standing leisurely, looking at us alternately.

"...I've decided."

Then he chases after me. The great Red Hare moves. It seems like he's running slowly, but he's at a similar level to me, who is running at full speed.

'...Shibal. Why me? In the original story, he chased after Kang Myungjin.'

In the original story, Kang Myungjin came here alone, so there was only one guy to chase.

'The reason why he told me to run towards the trees is because the Red Hare can't run properly in the densely wooded mountains.'

If this was a plain instead of a mountain, I would have been caught in less than a minute.

'According to the setting, Lü Bu is strongest when he's riding the Red Hare and can fully demonstrate the Red Hare's abilities.'

When fighting Lü Bu, you have to pull him down from the Red Hare, or fight in a place that restricts the Red Hare. This is because Lü Bu's physical abilities also decrease when he gets off the Red Hare.

I just turned my head and looked back.

The Red Hare is still chasing me. Lü Bu is holding the bow on his back and putting an arrow on the bowstring.

I used a tree as a shield to block the flying arrow.

Just being hit doesn't suit my personality.

'Manroe.'

I shot out lightning.

Lü Bu swung his Halberd and knocked away the lightning.

'He just knocks away the lightning flying straight at him.'

I thought it would make him hesitate for even a second, but it wasn't at all. I decided to just focus on running away.

Kwaang! Kwajik! Kwang!

It was noisy behind me.

It's the sound of Lü Bu, who couldn't catch me, angrily swinging his Halberd and smashing the surrounding trees.

"Stop! If you are also a man, face me fairly!"

"Are you the one to say that while riding a horse!"

"Okay. If I get off the Red Hare, will you fight me fairly?!"

Lü Bu would actually get off the Red Hare. In the original story, he got off the Red Hare due to Kang Myungjin's provocation and died fighting 1v1.

"I'll fight you if you get off the Red Hare!"

Lü Bu jumped off the Red Hare and landed on the ground. And he pointed his Halberd at me.

"Here. I'm down. Come!"

"He really got off. You crazy bastard. Kekeke."

I laughed at Lü Bu and ran away with all my might. I was going to take this opportunity to create some distance. Fight? I never intended to from the beginning. That guy will fight Kang Myungjin and die in a few hours.

"Stop there!"

Lü Bu hurriedly got on the Red Hare and started chasing me.

"Who would stop when you tell them to stop!"

「The Owner of the Sky frowns.」

The Owner of the Sky doesn't seem to like this situation very much.

'It doesn't matter. The formal contract has already been made anyway.'

Also, Zeus would definitely like it when I have a relationship with a woman.

Just because you've made a contract with a God's Seat doesn't necessarily mean they're on the same side as the exile. Like the King of Maccheon, they may try to trick the contractor.

I turned around.

Lü Bu, riding the Red Hare, couldn't approach this way from the village boundary. He glared at me once and then turned the Red Hare around and disappeared into the mountains.

'Lü Bu is being treated as a monster in this area.'

The safety zone only prevents monster attacks and invasions. It does not prevent human attacks.

"Two exiles have come...."

"What day is it today?"

"Maybe we'll be able to go to another area too."

The continentals living in the uncaptured area have basic information and knowledge about Atlantis, but they cannot go to other areas.

The secret of the continentals. One of them is that most of them originally died in another world and were given life again in Atlantis by the power of God.

This is a fact that is naturally revealed in the original story. Those who have influence in Atlantis probably already know.

'From their point of view, it's a honey deal. They died and were reborn.'

I looked around and shouted confidently.

"I am Seong Yujin! Hasn't a man with short hair come to the village? He's an exile like me!"

Among the people, an old man came out, tapping the ground with a cane. He has a white beard on his chin and a hunched back. He seems to be the oldest and the representative of the people here.

"Welcome to Nakil Village. If you're looking for an exile, he came here 10 minutes ago."

"Is that so? Where is that guy?"

"He went to that alley."

"Thank you."

"Have you come to capture this area?"

"No. We just happened to enter this area. And we're too weak to capture the area."

"...Is that so?"

The old man said with a disappointed look.

'I understand. You must have been frustrated being trapped here for 12 years. And looking at the appearance of the people around you, it doesn't seem like they're well-off either.'

Kang Myungjin and I know the capture conditions for this area.

Lü Bu is just a named monster in the game.

To capture this area, you have to defeat the Adversary of the Sun on the top of Nakilsan, the high mountain behind the village.

When Kang Myungjin achieves his goal, he will immediately go to the First Area, the Starting Church, through the mirror. And after some time has passed, he will come back here and capture it.
