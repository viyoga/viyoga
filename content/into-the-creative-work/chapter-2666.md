,"I can't just steal it. That Starway bracelet is an offering to the goddess. The process is important. This much spectacle should satisfy them. There are actually many gods expecting it."

「The King of the Demonic Sky is sparkling with anticipation.」

,"...Hmm. It would be better to revise the plan a little. From a broad perspective, it will work. The Imperial Academy may look decent on the surface, but if you look closely, there are quite a few problems. However, take out this phrase."

Elena pointed to a piece of paper that said Democratic Empire.

,"Why? Democratic Empire. It sounds good."

,"The Empire is a country of emperors and nobles. Democracy? It's the most guarded ideology. And the Imperial Academy is an educational institution for noble children. The moment you utter democracy, you'll become an enemy."

We decided to revise the detailed plan a bit. It was easier because Elena directly helped.

Then, suddenly, I became curious.

,"Why does only the nobility attend the Imperial Academy? Usually, other world's fantasy academies have commoners mixed in, right? What is it? Is it to build connections?"

There are many academy stories where the main character is a commoner.

,"If you want to build connections, it's more effective to hold social parties. Why would you send your child to the academy for four years? Of course, it's for education. A noble's child is a noble. But just because they were born a noble doesn't mean they know everything from the start."

,"You can educate them at home."

,"Nobles are busy. Especially lower-ranking nobles with little or no title. They don't have enough time or expertise to directly educate their children. Above all, hiring a renowned tutor requires money and connections. Therefore, the children of nobles without wealth and connections often didn't receive education and were often stupid."

I thought of the nobles of the Justia Empire. There were many nobles in the Justia Empire. Knights and wizards, and even administrative officials, were considered quasi-nobles and entered the nobility. Of course, they were nothing compared to the real nobles with titles.

,"In the very old days, the children of lower-ranking nobles were put into practical work without education. As a result, the Empire was shaken for a short time. When they were about to revise the hereditary system because the perception of lower-ranking nobles deteriorated, the Archmage Haren, who was from a lower-ranking noble family, stepped up and founded the Imperial Academy."

,"The name is Imperial, but it has nothing to do with the Imperial family?"

,"The only academy in the Empire approved by the Imperial family. It's just a name given because the academy was only in the Empire. If you think about it, it has nothing to do with the Imperial family. Students at the Imperial Academy pay money to the academy to receive education. The money is not small, so quite a few students take out loans."

The Imperial Academy was a school, no, more like an academy, where anyone could attend if they were a noble and paid money.

Still, it's definitely a place that can't be ignored since the children of nobles gather there.

A blonde man with glasses leisurely took a sip of coffee.

It was Rowoon Grab, the student council chairman of the Imperial Academy. As the heir to the Grab baronetcy, he liked the silence of the student council president's office.

But the silence was soon shattered.

Kwaang!

The student council door was roughly opened, and a female student appeared. It was Milla Kopel, the vice-president with green pigtails and a plump figure. Her body, from the Knight Department, was all muscular.

,"Chairman-nim! It's a big problem!"

,"A big problem? How big does it have to be for you to come out so ungracefully? Milla. I told you not to act like a commoner."

,"Chairman-nim. Are you insulting me by calling me a commoner right now?!"

Milla's face instantly contorted. Rowoon flinched in surprise. It felt like a tiger's face was appearing next to Milla's face.

,"I'm just saying. How could the best female knight of the Imperial Academy be a commoner? Haha."

,"Watch your words. I almost applied for a duel with you, Chairman-nim."

,"...Um. I'll be careful. So... what's the big problem that's making you come out like this?"

,"The Imperial Academy is trying to open up!"

,"Open up?"

,"Due to the Imperial Academy's operational problems, they're saying they're going to accept commoners as students next year!"

Rowoon opened his eyes wide and jumped up from his seat.

,"Accept commoners as students in the historic Imperial Academy?! Is the academy crazy?!"

,"The number of students at the academy has been steadily decreasing for a while now, hasn't it?"

,"That's because this world has become Atlantis. The Empire's birth rate is also at an all-time low."

,"And the Exiles are active in Atlantis. There's also talk of bringing them into the Empire.... Similarly, there's talk of the Empire making talented commoners into nobles to embrace them! The beginning of that is our Imperial Academy!"

,"What?! Ah, no. Wait. I haven't heard such a thing officially. Is that reliable information?"

,"Where there's smoke, there's fire, isn't it? The rumor has already spread among the students!"

The rumor has already spread among the students?

Rowoon felt a sense of crisis.

If he doesn't step up and do something here, the students will definitely ignore him. Besides, opening the school to commoners? Absolutely not. Nobles taking classes and studying with commoners? Just imagining it is horrifying.

,"...What's the atmosphere like among the students right now?"

,"Look out the window."

He quickly turned his head. Outside the window, a group of students was gathered. They all looked angry and their expressions were not good. The atmosphere was also serious.

,"...I can't just stand by.

Tadadadak!

One person rushed in from outside. It was a small, brown-haired man. It's Persson, the student council secretary.

,"Chairman-nim! The students are now demanding the temporary creation of a Hana Association regarding this academy opening incident!"

,"Hana Association? What's that again."

,"A committee where all students unite to oppose the academy's tyranny! It refers to an organization temporarily formed for this incident!"

,"I see. Are there any students who want to become the chairman of the Hana Association?"

,"Shouldn't the chairman-nim take on such a task, of course?!"

Rowoon felt a slight chill in his head. If things don't go well, isn't he the only one who's going to take the blame?

,"I'm busy leading the student council. It's difficult. It would be better to call someone else. Vice-chairman. How about you try it this time?"

Vice-chairman Milla immediately shook her head.

,"I know myself. It's beyond my capabilities. The only one who's fit for that position is the chairman-nim. If you successfully complete this task... all the students will respect the chairman-nim."

,"Hmm."

Rowoon pondered repeatedly.

The leader of an organization is dangerous. If something goes wrong, the leader will be held responsible. But conversely, if the organization succeeds, they can get the biggest reward. High risk. High return.

,'Is the academy crazy enough to fight the students seriously? If the students protest as a group, the academy staff will back down appropriately. The owners of the academy are the students. An academy without students is not an academy.'

He decided.

He decided to wear a new hat to get a bigger reward.

,"I understand. I've decided to become the chairman of the Hana Association. But even if it's a temporary organization, I can't run it alone. I need members to form the Hana Association. They must be people who influence the academy. So... as the student council chairman, I'm issuing an emergency summons. I'm calling the representative students from the Knight Department, Magic Department, and Administration Department, and the representative students from the clubs."

,"Yes! Chairman-nim!"

It didn't even take 30 minutes for the summons to be issued and everyone to gather. Things were going too well, too quickly.

,'It's the first time I've issued an emergency summons... and everyone attended. This is what a student council chairman is.'

Rowoon trembled, feeling the sweet taste of power.

,"...Ahem. You've probably heard how things are going on your way here. The academy is trying to accept commoners."

,"Absolutely not."

,"We must stop that at all costs. It's ruining the academy's traditions."

The gathered students spoke in unison. If they refuse here, they will be branded as traitors by the students gathered outside.

,"Yes. I also agree. The Hana Association is a temporary organization to counter this. The Hana Association is a position represented by students, and I need your help. There are various positions needed... so I'd like you to take on the positions."

,"......"

The representative students were silent. They were busy looking at each other.

Then one person raised their hand and stepped forward. It was a black-haired student he had never seen before. The student council chairman felt a sense of déjà vu for a moment, but he thought nothing of it. Just because he's the student council chairman doesn't mean he knows the information of all the students.

,"Who are you?"

,"I'm Jin Corona, the chairman of the tennis club. The students are demanding a quick response. So let's just decide the positions by drawing lots."

,"No. We can't decide such an important matter by drawing lots...."

,"Isn't there meaning in us gathering like this anyway? The academy will eventually accept our request and yield. The owner of the academy is the students, so how dare the faculty members oppose us."

,"Hmm. That's true. If there are no objections, let's decide the positions by drawing lots."
