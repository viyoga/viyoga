Inside the smartphone screen, a hexagonal box with a [?] mark was shaking and emitting light.

And an ice pack fell from the screen.

[Ice Pack

It's a cool ice pack.

Price: 1 Point]

"......"

I was silent.

My precious 1 point transformed into a small ice pack that wasn't even worth 5,000 won. It was infuriating, but sadly, I didn't even have the energy to get angry right now.

I spent 200 points on random draws for an hour.

I broke even.

I got over 10 Spirit Orbs and a panacea worth over 3,000 points. Although it was useless to me since I had Complete Recovery.

At first, I was angry.

How could I not be angry when I invested points and only got junk items? I cursed when I got sculptures or kitchen tools that had no effect for 30 points in a row.

I cursed the land, cursed the sky, cursed the Game Life app, cursed the country. Anyway, I cursed everything that came to mind.

But time passed and now it's now. I can't even curse anymore. The anxiety that I might not get a decent skill even after using all my points is soaring.

'Please, just give me any skill!'

At some point, my heart was shouting that.

There's also the option to stop the random draws here.

But if I stop now, what about the points that have been used so far? They're as good as being thrown away without any meaning.

'I've already broken even. I won't lose anything if I stop here. My head knows that, but....'

It's a loss if I don't get a skill. I kept thinking that.

[Skill Cooldown Reset Ticket

Resets the cooldown of a skill.

Price: 2,000 Points

※Caution

In the case of consumed stacks, only 1 will be charged.]

My depressed heart wavered for a moment. That was it.

Skill cooldown. It's a good item. Even though it's not that useful to me.

'The skill with the longest cooldown among the skills I have is Complete Recovery.'

Cooldown of 12 hours.

But I don't feel like it's that long either. It's not like I use Complete Recovery every day, and I don't think there will be many situations where I need to use it continuously after using it once.

'But it's better than nothing. It'll be useful as a last resort.'

I pressed my smartphone again and used the random draw.

I used 300 points.

Skill? It didn't come out. No other special items came out either.

'It's okay. It's okay. I've only used 300 points. I have more than 600 points left.'

My index finger pressing the smartphone screen was trembling.

And.

Exactly the 336th draw.

[You have acquired the skill, Random Summon!]

I opened my eyes wide and read the skill's description.

[Random Summon Lv. 1

‣Random Summon

Consumes 500 points to randomly summon a character from a creation.

‣Confirmed Summon

Consumes 1,000 points to summon a character from a creation.

However, only characters that have already been summoned once can be summoned.

Characters available for confirmed summon: -

※The summoned character's stats are the same as the summoner's stats.

※The duration of the summoned character is random.

※The summon cannot be canceled. It will be unsummoned upon death.

※The cooldown for Random Summon and Confirmed Summon is shared.

Cooldown: 120 days]

"......This is."

It's a good skill. To be able to summon a character from a creation into reality. How fraudulent is this skill if you only listen to it?

In some ways, it's similar to Affinity Summon. However, it seems that skills are not shared like Affinity Summon.

The disadvantages are that it eats up points and the cooldown is incredibly long.

'In the case of skills, the cooldown in reality and the game world are separate.'

In the case of Affinity Summon, the cooldown only flows in reality. On the other hand, separate skills flow separately. I've already confirmed it with Complete Recovery. Even if it's on cooldown in reality, it can be used in the game world. If both worlds are on cooldown, it cannot be used.

'If the avatar uses a skill during automatic progress and I enter the game world, I have to wait for the cooldown.'

Well, there are disadvantages, but in some ways, it was also an advantage.

'More than that, what I'm most concerned about is....'

The reaction of the summoned character. Since it's randomly summoned, I don't know which character will be summoned. It could be a good guy, or it could be a bad guy.

'If it's a character with a bad personality, they might try to kill me instead.'

The skill information says that the summon cannot be canceled. To cancel the summon, you have to kill the character.

'What if a guy with the ability to destroy the world is summoned?'

Then do I have to die?

'......Seeing that they have the same stats as me, it's highly unlikely that they'll be able to do that.'

I tapped the floor with my fingertips.

First of all, I want to try it out since I have a new skill. I need to use it once to know exactly what kind of ability it is.

'Test it safely in the game world? No. It's an opportunity to summon a character from a creation into reality. It's more meaningful in reality.'

Don't just think negatively, think positively.

A beautiful woman from a creation comes into reality. If the stats are the same, the success rate of rape will be low. There's also a way to seduce them. Or forcibly induce heat using the remaining aphrodisiac.

'Committing it in the game world is different from committing it in the real world. Hehehe.'

Above all, I have the ability to make up for mistakes.

30-day Regression Ticket.

Since there are no restrictions that it can only be used in the game world, it will also be effective in reality.

'I can earn 500 points anytime! Start Summon!'

[Starting Random Summon by consuming 500 points.]

Light burst out from the smartphone and took a shape. The light soon disappeared and a creature was visible.

What appeared in front of me was not the beauty I wanted.

It was a beast standing on the floor with four feet.

A fox.

It was a white fox with two tails. The fox looked at me with wary black eyes.

'Shibal. This is fucked up. I didn't expect a non-human character to come out. What creation is this character from....... Huh?'

I was distracted by the sudden appearance of the fox and didn't check the notification window on the smartphone screen.

[Milyeong (美靈) from the world of Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo has been summoned.]

[The Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo game world is deactivated.]

[The summon duration is 33 days.]

'Who the hell is Milyeong?!'

I desperately racked my brain.

Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo has a huge original volume, so it takes time to figure out each character. And it may not be a character from the original work.

'...If they're from Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo in my game world, they might be from another work. The Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo world is mixed with other worlds.'

The fox looked around. It seems to be amazed by the modern room it's seeing for the first time. And then it looks at me again. The fox and I had a staring contest.

'I shouldn't ignore it just because it's a fox. It'll have the same stats as me.'

Two tails.

It was definitely not an ordinary fox.

Suddenly. Something flashed through my mind. Come to think of it, there was a fox episode in the original Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo.

"......Milyeong. I am your master. Why? Because I summoned you."

I spoke in the language of the Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo world.

"Keureureureureu."

The fox raised its tail and bared its fangs as if it understood me. The teeth were too clean and even for an animal's teeth.

"Don't pretend to be a beast. I know you can talk. Two tails. You're definitely not an ordinary fox. Are you a yokai? Or a spirit beast?"

"......."

The fox stared at me silently and then opened its mouth.

"I'm a yokai. Why, are you scared of me?"

What came out of the fox's mouth was a woman's voice. It was also a stimulating and sexy voice that shook a man's guard.

'It's a female. I don't have to kill it.'

I relaxed a little. The fact that it answered meant that it didn't intend to start a fight right away. Since it was the first character I summoned, I wanted to resolve things through conversation.

"That's a lie. I don't feel any evil yokai energy from you. You're a spirit beast, aren't you?"

"Ha. It's ridiculous how you humans distinguish between yokai and spirit beasts. It's not like yokai only do bad things, and it's not like spirit beasts only do good things."

"I know."

"......."

The fox looked at me suspiciously when I answered immediately.

If it's not a spirit beast and not a yokai, there's a possibility that it's a human. Maybe it transformed its body into a fox using magic or spells.

"Milyeong. I am your summoner."

"Heung. I know that too. I also know that your name is Seong Yujin and that I can only be here for 33 days. But so what?"

"I said I'm your master."

"Stop talking nonsense. You only summoned me, you didn't become my master. And I have no intention of serving you as my master at all. To be honest, you're too weak. If I had my original power, you would be kneeling in front of me begging for your life."

"......."

Things don't seem to be going as well as I thought.

I immediately changed the direction of the conversation.

"Sorry. It's my first time summoning, so I guess I was mistaken."

I bowed politely and apologized.

It's probably not a bluff that the fox in front of me has more power than me in its original form. The Gwangmyeong Seungcheondo world is basically much higher level than reality.

"Heung. You're surprisingly good at figuring out your place? But you lack sincerity. Kneel down and apologize. Wouldn't that make your sincerity more felt?"

Thud.

I knelt down.

Does it hurt my pride to kneel before this beast?

I could sense it. This fox is probably a real woman. Even if it's not, it can turn into a woman.

'If it's a cliché, it'll be a stunning beauty.'

The reason I'm kneeling now is to gain momentum.

"I'm sorry."

"I'll forgive you."

A simple answer came back.

I sat cross-legged.

"I won't say I'm your master. But it's also true that I summoned you. So I hope you'll cooperate to some extent."

"...Well, I agreed to be summoned, so I don't intend to be hostile to the end. I'm also curious about other worlds. There's nothing I can't cooperate with. But."

"But?"

"Give me some of your life force. You have a life force that can't even be compared to ordinary humans. Even most spirit beasts wouldn't have as much life force as you."

"You're saying I have to pay life force as a price for cooperation. Okay."

"......You answered so easily? Do you even know what life force is? It's the source of life. If you don't have life force, you'll get sick and die."

"That's probably the case when too much life force is extracted. Doesn't it usually recover after a few days?"

"Heung. It seems you know something. Don't bother refusing. I'll take the price before we cooperate."

The fox slightly opened its mouth. A pure white energy from my body was sucked into its mouth. It was definitely not mana.

At first, it was okay. I felt something leaving my body, but it felt refreshing.
