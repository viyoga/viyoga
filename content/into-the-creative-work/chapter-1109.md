Before entering the Rumel Forest, Lexi Instructor-nim and I went to the 105th Unit to receive support for equipment and emergency rations.

The 105th Unit is a unit that guards the Rumel Forest, one of the border areas.

It's a battalion-level unit, so it's not a large unit. The Rumel Forest was a remote place even among the border areas, and it was almost abandoned for various reasons.

I quietly rolled my eyes and looked around the 105th Unit. It was different from the 667th Unit I belonged to. The exterior looks like a plausible barracks, but if you look closely, you can see various outdated things.

,...The 667th Unit uniquely has a lot of the latest equipment. It's natural since the financial power invested in the unit is different.'

The 667th Unit was created for the 'Battle Mage Project'. The project is already on a successful track, so it is receiving a lot of attention from the higher-ups. In addition, a large corporation is also involved, so there was no way that funds would be lacking.

'The military discipline is also a mess.'

Even though someone came to the unit, the soldiers of the 105th Unit were wandering around and working half-heartedly. Their faces are full of annoyance at the scorching sunlight and the tediousness of the day's work.

'I've been to units with this kind of atmosphere a few times because of missions.'

In this case, it's a demoted unit in nine out of ten cases.

A unit that has been demoted after causing an accident during military service. Even though this is the front line, the front line is still the front line. Even if this place is devastated by enemy bombing, there is a rear unit not far away that can be restored immediately, and there are no important cities or facilities nearby.

The unit members also know that fact, so there is no military discipline or motivation.

'It's none of my business.'

Lexi Instructor-nim and I checked the equipment we received. The condition of the equipment was excellent. There were no problems whatsoever.

We checked the equipment and immediately prepared to leave. It was a creepy unit. Since they are guys who have been demoted for causing accidents, there is a high possibility that they will cause another accident. There was no point in staying here for long.

,Do you like the equipment? I chose only A-grade items from what we have in the unit.'

A middle-aged man with a mechanical right arm approached. A mechanical prosthetic hand. In this world, it was common to modify parts of the body with machines, so it was not surprising.

The middle-aged man is Major Baugh, the person in charge of the 105th Unit. I felt some intention in him coming out to see us off in person.

,Yes. It's nice because it's well maintained. I'll make sure to tell the higher-ups that I received good equipment.'

Lexi Instructor-nim saw through Major Baugh's intentions and said. Major Baugh smiled and nodded.

,I'm glad I could be of help to the famous Panther of the Ross Unit.'

Panther of the Ross Unit.

It was Lexi Instructor-nim's nickname. I don't know much about the name, but I know that the Ross Unit is a fairly famous special unit. And I also know that Lexi Instructor-nim's rank was originally a lieutenant, but she was demoted.

Lexi Instructor-nim's expression hardened for a moment. She didn't really like talking about old things.

,...I didn't know Major-nim knew me. Did my rumors spread this far?

,Just because I'm stuck in a remote corner doesn't mean my ears are closed. What's a little surprising is that you're still active. If you killed a superior officer, shouldn't you be in a military prison at the very least?

Major Baugh said with a smirk.

I knew that Lexi Instructor-nim had caused an accident and was demoted... Did she really kill a superior officer? I was a little surprised by the unexpected fact.

Lexi Instructor-nim shrugged nonchalantly.

,I have the ability, unlike some people.'

Major Baugh narrowed his eyes. His dry lips trembled slightly. He hesitated whether to speak or not, but then he smiled.

,...Good luck with your mission.'

,Yes. Thank you. I'll finish the mission soon and see you again.'

,Be careful. The Rumel Forest is not an easy place.'

He turned around and went into the barracks. As soon as his figure disappeared from view, Lexi Instructor-nim's face contorted.

,Incompetent old man. He's just scratching at my nerves for no reason.'

She spat on the floor and grabbed my shoulder.

,Let's go. We'll probably have to start shooting from the beginning.'

It didn't take 30 minutes for me to understand what Lexi Instructor-nim meant.

After entering the Rumel Forest, someone started following us. Lexi Instructor-nim and I were not flustered. We went deeper into the forest, as if to lure the pursuers in.

I wiped the sweat flowing down my forehead. The Rumel Forest was a jungle in reality, even though it was just a forest in name. The hot sunlight and suffocating moisture, the insects that can be seen with just a slight turn of the head, and the plants that obstruct the view. I expected it, but it's a really annoying environment.

,Lexi-nuna. The guys following us from behind... are they from the 105th Unit?

,That's right. You're getting good at it since you've been around for a while, huh?

,If we're being followed less than an hour after entering the forest, then of course it's only them. Their purpose is....'

,It's me.'

She said as if it was natural. But her eyes were not calm at all. Restrained murderous intent was in her pupils.

I looked at her again. Pink short hair, skin that was unbelievably white and soft for a veteran soldier, full breasts and a slender waist. She had an outstanding beauty that was rarely seen.

,Your gaze is lecherous. You're not thinking strange things, are you?

,You're the one who's thinking strange things, nuna.'

,It's natural for those guys to be after me. It's happened a few times while working in the military. Of course, I sent them all to the afterlife, though.'

,...Those guys will be after nuna's body too, but their top priority will be the equipment we have.'

The equipment we received from the 105th Unit was all A-grade. Among them were expensive, state-of-the-art equipment.

Those guys are planning to kill us and take this equipment. If we are killed by them, it will be treated as a missing person due to mission failure, and the equipment will be officially lost in the Rumel Forest. It's a great way to make money.

,That's not all. Major Baugh. I could smell something fishy coming from that guy. He wants to get rid of us just in case.'

,...Just in case, what?

,In case we find evidence of his corruption in this forest and report it to the higher-ups? This is just suspicion without any evidence yet..., but he's probably involved in smuggling. That's the only way to make money in a place like this.'

For Major Baugh, who has a shady background, the best scenario is for us to die in the Rumel Forest and fail the mission.

,...What should we do with the guys following us? They're getting closer.'

,Are you seriously asking because you don't know?

,It's not that, it's just that you have the decision-making power, nuna.'

Just because Lexi Instructor-nim is nice to me doesn't mean I should mistake the difference in our status.

,That's right. Well, we have to get rid of anything that gets in the way of the mission. It's not just us who can have accidents, it's them too.'

,What's the method?

,Figure it out yourself. This much is easy, right?

It seemed that Lexi Instructor-nim had decided to leave it to me without moving herself. She often left work to me because of her experience, so it was a familiar situation.

I looked around.

First of all, sniping is impossible. There are too many obstructive elements. Dense trees and unknown plants are blocking the view.

Meet the guys directly and fight? It's not bad, but it's inefficient.

,I'll use trap magic.'

,Trap magic? Are you talking about the military arcane magic that I know?

,Yes. It's Air Trap.'

Air Trap, a level 2 arcane magic, is the military arcane magic of the Prisel Army. It is one of the military arcane magics that Venus Instructor-nim taught me before.

Air Trap is, in simple terms, an invisible magic trap. A magic that sets conditions and installs it in the air, on walls, or on the ground.

'The amount of time I can maintain Air Trap without a medium at my current level is about 3 hours. That's enough time.'

The disadvantage of Air Trap is that it is easily caught by those who are sensitive to mana.

'I've been researching and modifying Air Trap for the past few hours, so they won't notice unless they're a level 2 mage. There are no level 2 mages among the guys chasing us.'

There was no reason for high-level manpower like mages to rot in a demoted unit.

'The way those guys are chasing us is not magical. It's a method of chasing the traces left on the ground. There's a ranger with reasonably good skills.'

It was convenient that there was no need to predict the guys' path separately.

Open Astral and activate the Mana Road. Mana flows through the 18 Mana Roads and turns into a formula. I smiled as I felt the mana flowing without obstruction.

'I've been busy for the past 5 months. Researching magic, enduring mana essence....'

Having mana essence injected into my body is the worst, but I can't deny that I'm growing rapidly thanks to it.

'The limit is 17 Air Traps with my current mana capacity. ...10 should be enough.'

I moved with Lexi Instructor-nim and installed Air Traps.
