I deflected the Sword energy flying towards me with Hwaryeon Bido. This Sword energy was identical in form and power to the Sword energy I had just unleashed.

I wasn't surprised or flustered. I recognized it as the ability of Feedback.

Feedback's ability is, simply put, the ability to return things. It's different from reflection. I hadn't even thrown the Sword energy at Feedback, yet it was fed back and flew towards me.

,Kkeuaaaaaaaak! Doctor!! Doctor!!!"

Ma Young-jae, the Governor of Gangwon Province who lost both legs, screamed. Feedback shook his head, looking at him.

,Governor-nim. Please be quiet. Didn't I rush over like this? If you go to the hospital now, your true identity will be exposed. Can't you reattach your legs later? Haha."

Feedback laughed cheerfully. A faded gem-like stone fell from Ma Young-jae's pocket. He wouldn't carry a real stone in his pocket, so it must be an item. An item that summoned Feedback to this place.

,However, I'm not sure how the situation is unfolding. Why did the youngest of the Cheonil Group target the Governor of Gangwon Province? This is blatant terrorism. Do you think you can challenge the public power of the Republic of Korea and get away with it?"

,Are your eyes messed up? That monster is the Governor of Gangwon Province? Does that really look like a human to you?"

Feedback glanced at Ma Young-jae. Then he said shamelessly.

,Don't make fun of people's appearances! He may be ugly, but he's human!"

,Try to insist on something that makes sense. No matter how I look at it, it's a different species. Ah, Feedback. Are you of the same species as that thing?"

,No, I'm human!"

Feedback said firmly. I wasn't stupid enough to believe those words so easily. I didn't care whether those words were true or not.

I put a drug cigarette in my mouth and took a deep drag. Feeling my concentration increase, I charged towards Feedback.

Hwaryeon Bido drew a red trajectory. Aiming for Feedback's neck. Kill him when I can.

Kkaang!

An identical blade to Hwaryeon Bido popped out of thin air and deflected my attack.

,He can not only reproduce Sword energy but also the blade of Hwaryeon Bido. The speed and power I wield, even the force within it is the same. The only difference is the direction. He can control the direction as he wants.'

Broker Kang Eun-sook briefed me about Feedback before the operation began. Because Feedback is the guardian closest to Ma Young-jae. Feedback can feed back attacks within 10m of himself. It doesn't matter whether it's a close-range or long-range attack.

Feedback's condition is recognition. He can only return attacks that he recognizes. In other words, I just need to launch an attack that he can't recognize.

,It's not as easy as it sounds.'

Feedback was a top-tier guardian active in Gangwon Province. Having been active as a guardian for over 10 years, his combat power was on a different level from the average Joe.

I approach Feedback without wielding my sword. Since I didn't attack, Feedback couldn't feed back any attacks. I approached the guy who was just standing there blankly.

I didn't attack right away. If I attacked while he was clearly watching, he would use his ability and attack me in reverse.

,Haha. It seems I've been underestimated. People underestimate and ignore my strength because I work in Gangwon Province, not Seoul. I'm not in Gangwon Province because I'm too weak to go to Seoul. I'll prove that to you too."

Feedback's right fist flies towards me. It's a feint. The real one is a kick. I jumped to the side and avoided his kick. My fist reflexively went forward. The guy activated his ability. A fist identical to my fist falls above my head.

,Even my fist is a target of his ability.'

It's a pretty annoying ability. I caught the fist with my palm and dropped lightning. Feedback didn't avoid the lightning and took it head-on. He was fine even after being struck by lightning. The only damage was the ends of his hair being singed.

,He recognized the lightning. He protected his body with force before getting hit. Even so, there's too little damage. Is the clothes he's wearing an item?'

Feedback smirked.

,I'll return it."

Lightning erupted from his body and rushed towards me. I swung my sword to deflect the lightning, and as if waiting for it, a sword materialized in the air and attacked me.

If I attack, my attack becomes the enemy's attack and flies towards me, and if I deflect that attack, another attack becomes another attack and flies towards me. I felt like I was falling deeper and deeper into a swamp. It's a very shitty battle method.

Feedback's strategy is an attack that he can't recognize? It's easy to say. His senses are already sharpened to a razor's edge.

,Ah, shit. This is so annoying."

Suddenly, irritation surged. Do I have to fight him while using my brain? I'm not Jung Soo-yeon. Jung Soo-yeon fights using her brain to make the most of her abilities. Because she has many abilities, fighting recklessly is very inefficient.

But me?

Since when have I fought using my brain?

,Haha. Retreating like this is also a method. But you and the Cheonil Group will have to pay the price later. You don't think the South Korean government will stand still after the Governor of Gangwon Province is attacked, do you?"

Certainly, even the Cheonil Group couldn't cover it up. It was no different from directly challenging the national public power.

,When did I say I was running away?"

,Are you saying you're going to die now? Hmm. I respect your choice!"

Feedback was still smiling. He didn't doubt his victory in the slightest. I was pissed off, so I immediately took action.

I move forward without breaking the chain of attacks. The blade of Hwaryeon Bido materializes in the air and falls on my shoulder. I swing my sword to deflect it. This time, a blade materialized from the side aims for my side. I tried to avoid it, but I was a little late. I ignored it and moved forward.

,Is this some kind of self-abandonment? Charging in with your life on the line... More than half of the guys I've faced have made that choice. And they all died."

Cheolkeok.

A blade popped out of Feedback's sleeve and shot towards me. The distance is too close to avoid it. I swung my sword to deflect it. Because he also recognized that as an attack, a blade materialized in the air and fell on me. Blood spurted from my left shoulder. I ignored it. After all, the distance has narrowed.

The smile disappeared from Feedback's determined face. The serious guy throws or swings daggers that he had hidden all over his body. I didn't counterattack those. I avoided them if I could, and if I couldn't, I only protected my vitals. Even with daggers stuck in my arms, legs, and torso, I steadily moved forward.

,This crazy bastard...."

Feedback's composure disappeared, and I could see the fear he was feeling.

He stepped back. I approached him even faster. When he retreats one step, I advance two steps.

The distance was reached.

The distance where the sword reaches.

I swung my sword without hesitation. His ability was activated. My attack materialized in the air and blocked my attack.

,You're already bleeding a lot! If I just hold on, it's my victory!"

,If you can hold on, that is."

I continue the attack.

I didn't use Lightning Flash on purpose. Because an attack that is too powerful is more dangerous when it comes back.

Attacks collide with attacks. Blood splatters, my vision is blurred, and a gap is created in him. Without hesitation, I stabbed my sword into that gap.

,Keuaaaaaaaak!"

The guy, whose side was cut, screamed and aimed for my neck, stabbing a dagger. Blocking the dagger with my shoulder, I stabbed Hwaryeon Bido into his body.

Pook pook pook!

Pook pook pook!

I and he swung our swords and daggers like crazy, filled with hatred. From some point on, Feedback only used his ability defensively.

Feedback stabbed my body with daggers to survive, and I just stabbed him with my sword to kill him.

Blood splattered.

He used his ability to defend only his vitals no matter what. On the other hand, I attacked everything, including the vitals. I was at a disadvantage because of what I had suffered before narrowing the distance.

Thud.

I was the one who fell first. I bled too much.

,Ha, haha. I was scared for a moment. You're the first guy like you. A guy who's more like a monster than a monster... A crazy bastard is not crazy for no reason."

The guy who regained his composure laughed. He stabbed a dagger into my heart as if to confirm the kill.

[Death Resistance has been activated. You will not die for the next 15 seconds.]

,W, well done, Feedback! Now call 119! My leg! My leg is cut off!"

,Governor-nim. Calm down. Aren't there more enemies left? Besides, you don't seem to be in that much danger. But weren't you really human?"

,I, I'll explain that later."

,Well, it doesn't matter if you don't explain. What's important between us is sincerity, isn't it? I just got a new girlfriend, so I need some money. She likes buildings a bit...."

,I understand, so call 119! No, bring a recovery-type ability user!"

,Hmm. He'll be here soon. It'll take 15 minutes, but that's fast."

[Using Full Recovery.]

When I silently got up, Feedback's eyes, who belatedly noticed, widened as much as possible. He had finished confirming the kill, and then I suddenly got up without a single wound, so it was understandable.

Lightning Flash Style Lightning Flash (雷光).

The sword became a ray of light and cut Feedback's thick neck.

Seogeok!

His head falls.

,This is the correct way to use Full Recovery."

Checking my body condition, which is at its best, I look at Ma Young-jae, who is lying face down. The guy lying on the floor looked up at me and trembled.

,Let's go to a good place."

I grab Ma Young-jae by the scruff of the neck and leave the mansion. The other guys? They'll get out on their own. Feedback, who could be said to be the most dangerous, died at my hands, so escaping should be easy.

,I have to kill Noh Kyung-hak too... I don't have enough time. Let's aim for the next opportunity.'

I arrived at the designated safe house. Kang Eun-sook was waiting at the safe house. Kang Eun-sook widened her eyes as if surprised to see me.

,I can't believe you're the first to arrive. Amazing."

,I brought a gift too."

,That's a gift I like."

A cold smile spread across Kang Eun-sook's face as she saw the legless Ma Young-jae.
