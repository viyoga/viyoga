,"Hmm. It seems like things have gotten quite interesting."

At the sudden voice, Sung Hari and I abruptly raised our heads. An old man in a suit, Hasegawa Isshin, was floating in the air. He looked down at us with a relaxed expression.

I was taken aback by his unexpected appearance. Why is Hasegawa Isshin here? Was he trapped in the Terra Simulator like us?

Just as my mind was about to get complicated, Sung Hari, who was next to me, raised her spear and aimed it at Hasegawa Isshin.

,"Did you orchestrate this situation?"

,"No. This situation is because a spy from the Demonic Sect summoned a demon.... Oh, dear. It's too long to explain. And I don't have time for that. Let me be clear. I didn't orchestrate this."

,"Then why did you appear before us? You don't exactly seem to have good intentions."

,"First, let me politely ask. Would you give up the Magatama of Susanoo? The Terra Simulator absolutely needs the Magatama of Susanoo. Please cooperate for the sake of humanity's future."

,"You've come to the wrong person. You should be asking Tenrai Nagisa, not us."

,"I'll persuade my *sunbae-nim*. You two, please stop. Haven't you been here too long? You need to return to reality."

My brow furrows. Hasegawa Isshin's tone was gentle, but the meaning within was a threat. If you want to get back to reality, listen to me. In other words, he's saying that if we don't follow his orders, we won't be able to return to reality.

,'What's he so confident about? The AI? No, it's a bluff!'

He probably can't control the rampaging artificial intelligence, Terra. If he could, he wouldn't need to show up in person like this; he would have just trapped us in the virtual reality and attacked our real bodies in reality.

,"I know that woman's personality well, and she's not soft enough to be swayed by your persuasion. She'll probably just get angry that things didn't go her way."

Sung Hari said. She didn't seem to have any intention of joining hands with Hasegawa Isshin. Of course, I feel the same way as her. Tenrai Nagisa is too pretty to listen to the requests of an old man like Hasegawa Isshin. And I haven't even gotten to bang Tenrai Nagisa yet, so I can't afford to be on bad terms with her.

,"Hmm. You know my *sunbae-nim* well. Then I'll make another offer. I'll give you money, so give up the Magatama of Susanoo."

,"You probably know that the Demonic Sect is after the Magatama of Susanoo, right? Can you handle the Demonic Sect?"

,"My *sunbae-nim* doesn't know what she's talking about. Where is safer than virtual reality? Even if the entire Demonic Sect attacks, they won't be able to take the Magatama of Susanoo."

,"Isn't the artificial intelligence going crazy because of the Demonic Sect? That's what you said earlier."

,"That's because the summoned demon... Haa. Never mind. You've already made up your minds. No matter what I say, it'll be like casting pearls before swine. I'll stop you by force."

Mana made of lines spreads in all directions around Hasegawa Isshin. It was a type of power I had never seen before, so I muttered without realizing it.

,"Magic?"

Sung Hari corrected me.

,"No, it's barrier magic. Barriers are usually for defense, but they can also be used for offense, depending on how you use them. In that case, it's best to find the weakest point in the barrier and attack it intensively. An even more certain method is to defeat the barrier magic user."

I took out a dagger from my inventory and threw it at Hasegawa Isshin. The dagger shot out like lightning. It actually contained lightning energy.

Ting!

The dagger was blocked by an invisible wall surrounding Hasegawa Isshin's body and bounced off. That's also a barrier.

,"You've already surpassed the level of a student. You could debut as an active duty player."

,"Bullshit. And if you think that killing us will give you the Magatama of Susanoo, you're sorely mistaken. We're players, and we revive countless times."

,"Haha. You're misunderstanding something. Kill you? No. My goal is to stop you."

,"What?"

,"Arinna's quest. Did you really think that only you could receive the quest?"

「Salvation

Overcome the end and save this world.

Reward: Magatama of Susanoo」

This is the quest I received from Arinna. If I end the apocalypse, I can receive the Magatama of Susanoo as a reward. But we're not the only ones who received this quest?

To end the apocalypse, I have to eliminate the source of the apocalypse. And the source of the apocalypse is Young Hari.

,"No way."

,"The source of the apocalypse. I don't know how it happened, but Sung Hari. It's your doppelganger."

,"...You're going to kill Hari while stopping us? Do you think you can do that alone?!"

,"Don't you see that the source of the apocalypse isn't coming here? I'm not alone."

I, who had been focusing on Hasegawa Isshin, turned my head sharply to find Young Hari.

Far away. In a distance where I couldn't see unless I amplified my vision with mana in my eyes, she was fighting men and women wearing black uniforms.

,'Kaso Academy students!'

I used Spirit Eyes. My vision became stronger, and I could see what was happening. At least dozens of students were fighting Young Hari. Surprisingly, they were fighting Young Hari on equal terms.

,"Those guys got that strong? No way!"

,"You underestimate our academy students too much. Well, they wouldn't be a match in reality, but this is virtual reality. They're receiving Terra's assistance. Above all, our students are familiar with the Terra Simulator."

An academy student is hit by Young Hari's attack and dies. And they revive not far away. Even the revival point has changed. The Terra artificial intelligence is also our enemy. At this rate, even Young Hari will soon reach her limit.

,"You bastard...!"

Young Hari dies. Young Hari is a bit different from us. She's a player, but she's not a player. Moreover, she's designated as the source of the apocalypse. There's no guarantee that she'll revive like a player.

I gritted my teeth. I can't let Young Hari die. I have to take her and get out of here somehow. I can think about the rest later.

,"Mom!"

I poured all the buffs I could use on Sung Hari. The strengthened Sung Hari leaps toward Hasegawa Isshin, who is floating in the sky.

,"Even if it's like this... to fight against the Thunderous Star of Gwancheon. What an honor."

Hasegawa Isshin leisurely waved his staff. A line of light made of barriers flies toward Sung Hari. Sung Hari used the force field as a stepping stone to dodge in mid-air. The line of light that passed by her exploded, causing a shockwave.

Sung Hari falls. She didn't just fall. Even in the midst of that, she threw her spear in retaliation.

Kaang!

The spear bounces off. Sung Hari clicks her tongue. She bounces her body and leaps again to continue the battle. Hasegawa Isshin responded with ease.

,"Twenty barriers have already been created. For barriers to form so easily... Terra's assistance is amazing."

Sung Hari spurs on and continues the battle.

Hasegawa Isshin looks relaxed on the outside, but he's focusing all his attention on Sung Hari. Relatively, I was free. He's looking down on me too much.

,'Let's recall what Sung Hari said. Find the weak point and destroy the barrier.'

The Spirit Eyes I have were a great help in this situation.

When I used Spirit Eyes, I could see the flow of mana. Whether it was magic or barrier magic, its source was mana, so if I used Spirit Eyes, I could see the hidden barrier with my own eyes.

,'I'm going crazy. I can't understand it even when I see it.'

The barrier was made of lines and shapes. At first glance, it was beautiful. Except that it was dominating the space and restricting me and Sung Hari.

,'The pressure of the barrier is getting stronger. I have to do something.'

I reached out to the barrier. I felt a strong repulsive force and immediately pulled my hand back. If I had tried to put it in deeper, my arm would have been crushed.

,'I can't approach it recklessly.'

I looked around and examined the barrier. I found a strange spot.

The part where the barriers met. It was somehow clumsy compared to other places. Should I call it a seam?

I reach out my hand. I feel the repulsive force again.

,'The repulsive force is much weaker than before. I can break it.'

Of course, I can't break it with pure brute force alone.

Pajijik.

I covered my hand with lightning energy and grabbed the seam of the barrier.

Jjeoeoeoeong!

The two barriers simultaneously crumple. The space fluctuated, and the barrier disappeared, reducing the pressure.

Hasegawa Isshin turned his head sharply and looked at me.

,"How did you break the barrier...!"

He was incredibly surprised, his eyes wide with shock. I was annoyed by that reaction. How much had he been looking down on me?

I moved to the next weak point.

,"Those unique eyes.... I see. You can visualize the barrier! I should have taken you down first."

,"Do you think I'll just stand by?"

Sung Hari intervened and blocked Hasegawa Isshin. I ran and broke the barriers one by one. The barriers were regenerating, but not as fast as I was breaking them.

,"Stop him!!"

The barriers move. The barriers that had been intensively marking Sung Hari began to pour down on me like a waterfall. The pressure of the barriers crushes my body. Some barriers became sharp needles and tried to penetrate my body.

Pajijik, Pajik!

Thunder Sky Style Electromagnetic Barrier.

I cover my body with a barrier to block the barriers.

,'This isn't good.'

It's hard to move my body because of the barriers that are checking me.

,'A way....'

I need to disperse the power of the barriers directed at me. Sung Hari's help? She was already doing a good job. The fact that I can move even this much is thanks to Sung Hari successfully drawing Hasegawa Isshin's attention.

I glanced at Sung Hari. She was fighting Hasegawa Isshin some distance away. The important thing is that the distance has widened.

,'I should use that skill I've been sealing. I'll use all the remaining skill points.'

「Spirit Summoning

Level: 20

Summons a spirit.」

「Spirit Enhancement

Level: 20

Enhances the spirit's power by 20%.」

,"Anything is fine! Come out!"

I used Spirit Summoning. The mana inside me rushes out.

Hwiiiiiiing.

The wind blew. The wind gathers in one place and takes the shape of a human. A translucent, muscular man. The spirit, with a body over 3m tall, looked down at me from the air and said.

,"Contractor, I am Yuperis. Is there something you wish to ask of me?"

An overwhelming presence that made my whole body tremble.

This guy was definitely a high-ranking wind spirit.
