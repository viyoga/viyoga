Pajijijijik.

Roughly, it was a sequence of strong-weak-weak-medium-strong-medium-strong-strong. Anyway, after going through this short process, I opened my palm.

Uuuuung.

The metal, now a superconductor, floated above my palm.

Thanks to this recipe given by the Lord of the Sky, Super America's technology was able to develop even faster. With the help of a great scientist, Super America reached a kind of singularity.

Looking out the hatchery window, I could see skyscrapers densely packed together, with cars flying between them.

Even though I provided adequate rest, she ignored me and worked until she died. It was a time of war and development. There was so much to do, so she pushed herself too hard.

Even if I had tried to stop her, she wouldn't have listened. Arel was a woman who lived and died for her work. I'd never seen a woman so obsessed with work that she overworked herself to death twice.

,Aside from me, she was the highest authority in Super America, so the underlings couldn't stop Arel.'

From various pieces of information I gathered, Park Si-woo was a reincarnator. In his previous life, he lived as an adventurer in Atlantis before dying. He suddenly remembered his past life as a child and started a revolution.

It's a bit random, but he has a unique trait called Unfortunate Fate (SS), so it's not entirely nonsensical.

Judging by how quiet the system is, he must have used legal methods. Just like how the Lord of the Sky consumed Faith Points to tell me the superconductor recipe. The leader of the Uruk civilization might have used a power I don't know about.

,He might have already been sanctioned by the system for interfering too much.'

The important thing is that Uruk has already been destroyed.

Currently, there are only 3 civilization leaders left, including me.

I am the victor, and Lucas, one of the losers, is imprisoned and has become a pregnancy machine like Park Si-woo. But as a leader, he didn't die.

,Time skips for leaders except for turns. But there's a trick to this too. If the leader's life is in danger, time doesn't skip.'

In other words, if you torture them, time won't skip and they have to spend the full time. Even so, you can't torture them all day, but they would have suffered for decades. And will continue to suffer.

,You bastards. If you tried to kill me, you should pay the price. I'll find you and kill you even after the district conquest is over and I return to Atlantis.'

The leader of Zhonghua abandoned his civilization and ran away. Even though the East Continent is Shitty, he tenaciously hides and survives.

I thought about showering the East Continent with nuclear missiles and turning it to ash... but I decided to refrain. The East Continent is now a testing ground and a stress-relieving scarecrow. If the East Continent completely disappeared, Super America's internal affairs could become chaotic. In the worst case, a civil war would break out.

,There are 60 years left until the year 500. I must avoid civil war at all costs.'

I know why the leader of Zhonghua is hiding.

He must be expecting Tiamat to appear in the year 500.

,I don't think Tiamat will be anything special either.'

Super America has developed this far in just 440 years. This is probably something even the system didn't expect. I hope Tiamat has a powerful force. Because that would be fun.

Knock knock knock.

The door opened and the secretary came in. She was holding a rectangular bag in her hand. The secretary carefully placed the bag on the table.

"Your Excellency. This is a nuclear bomb that has been lightened as much as possible. The power is 20kt. There are no major advantages except for its portability."

From what I've roughly gathered, Fat Man had a power of 21kt. This small bag has a similar power to Fat Man.

For reference, the Tsar Bomba is said to be 50Mt. An explosive force equivalent to 50 million tons of TNT.

,I wanted a Tsar Bomba level... but they say that's difficult even with current technology.'

I tried to put the nuclear briefcase in my inventory, but the nuclear briefcase doesn't leave my hand.

「The conquest is in progress. You cannot take items.」

The system's intervention.

It would be a lie to say I didn't expect it at all. Nuclear bombs could disrupt the balance of Atlantis.

"Haa...."

Because it's true that I was looking forward to this moment.

Wasn't the reason I entered the [Eternal Eden] world to get my hands on a nuclear briefcase like this? It would have been much easier if I could have future technology, including nuclear briefcases, here.

,If I don't like it, I can just detonate nuclear bombs. It's fun just imagining it.'

But the system doesn't allow it.

"Shitty system...."
